## 样本1：短司法解释 - 破坏公用电信设施（第1条）

**中文原文：**
> 采用截断通信线路、损毁通信设备或者删除、修改、增加电信网计算机信息系统中存储、处理、传输的数据和应用程序等手段，故意破坏正在使用的公用电信设施，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第一百二十四条规定的"危害公共安全"，依照刑法第一百二十四条第一条的规定，以破坏公用电信设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：

**英文翻译：**
> Article 1 Where any person intentionally damages a public telecommunications facility in service by severing communication lines, damaging communication equipment, or deleting, modifying, or adding data or application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in a telecommunications network computer system, and such act falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute "endangering public security" as provided for in Article 124 of the Criminal Law...

**审校要点：**
- [ ] "正在使用的公用电信设施" → "public telecommunications facility in service" ✓
- [ ] "危害公共安全" → "endangering public security" ✓
- [ ] "处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑" → "sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years" ✓
- [ ] 是否引用了《刑法》？→ "Criminal Law" ✓（需确认是否应为完整标题）
