# 中华人民共和国特种设备安全法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2013-06-29  
**生效日期**：2014-01-01

> （2013年6月29日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　生产、经营、使用 第一节　一般规定 第二节　生　　产 第三节　经　　营 第四节　使　　用 第三章　检验、检测 第四章　监督管理 第五章　事故应急救援与调查处理 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强特种设备安全工作，预防特种设备事故，保障人身和财产安全，促进经济社会发展，制定本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强特种设备安全工作，预防特种设备事故，保障人身和财产安全，促进经济社会发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening the safety of special equipment, preventing accidents involving special equipment, ensuring personal and property safety, and promoting economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　特种设备的生产（包括设计、制造、安装、改造、修理）、经营、使用、检验、检测和特种设备安全的监督管理，适用本法。  
本法所称特种设备，是指对人身和财产安全有较大危险性的锅炉、压力容器（含气瓶）、压力管道、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施、场（厂）内专用机动车辆，以及法律、行政法规规定适用本法的其他特种设备。  
国家对特种设备实行目录管理。特种设备目录由国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门制定，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the production (including design, manufacture, installation, modification, repair), operation, use, inspection, testing of special equipment, and the supervision and administration of special equipment safety.  
Special equipment as mentioned in this Law refers to boilers, pressure vessels (including gas cylinders), pressure pipelines, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, large-scale amusement devices, special-purpose motor vehicles in factories (premises), and other special equipment for which the application of this Law is provided for by laws or administrative regulations, that have a relatively high danger to personal and property safety.  
The State applies a catalogue management system to special equipment. The catalogue of special equipment shall be formulated by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council and shall be executed upon approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　特种设备安全工作应当坚持安全第一、预防为主、节能环保、综合治理的原则。

**Article 3** The work on special equipment safety shall adhere to the principles of safety first, prevention foremost, energy conservation and environmental protection, and comprehensive governance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对特种设备的生产、经营、使用，实施分类的、全过程的安全监督管理。

**Article 4** The State implements classified and full-process safety supervision and administration over the production, operation, and use of special equipment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门对全国特种设备安全实施监督管理。县级以上地方各级人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门对本行政区域内特种设备安全实施监督管理。

**Article 5** The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council shall implement supervision and administration over national special equipment safety. The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of local people's governments at or above the county level shall implement supervision and administration over special equipment safety within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院和地方各级人民政府应当加强对特种设备安全工作的领导，督促各有关部门依法履行监督管理职责。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当建立协调机制，及时协调、解决特种设备安全监督管理中存在的问题。

**Article 6** The State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over the work on special equipment safety and urge relevant departments to perform their supervision and administration duties according to law.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish coordination mechanisms to timely coordinate and solve problems existing in the supervision and administration of special equipment safety.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　特种设备生产、经营、使用单位应当遵守本法和其他有关法律、法规，建立、健全特种设备安全和节能责任制度，加强特种设备安全和节能管理，确保特种设备生产、经营、使用安全，符合节能要求。

**Article 7** Entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment shall comply with this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, establish and improve responsibility systems for special equipment safety and energy conservation, strengthen management of special equipment safety and energy conservation, and ensure the safety of production, operation, and use of special equipment while meeting energy conservation requirements.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　特种设备生产、经营、使用、检验、检测应当遵守有关特种设备安全技术规范及相关标准。  
特种设备安全技术规范由国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门制定。

**Article 8** The production, operation, use, inspection, and testing of special equipment shall comply with relevant special equipment safety technical specifications and relevant standards.  
Special equipment safety technical specifications shall be formulated by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　特种设备行业协会应当加强行业自律，推进行业诚信体系建设，提高特种设备安全管理水平。

**Article 9** Trade associations for special equipment shall strengthen industry self-discipline, promote the construction of an industry integrity system, and improve the level of special equipment safety management.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持有关特种设备安全的科学技术研究，鼓励先进技术和先进管理方法的推广应用，对做出突出贡献的单位和个人给予奖励。

**Article 10** The State supports scientific and technological research concerning special equipment safety, encourages the promotion and application of advanced technologies and advanced management methods, and gives rewards to entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当加强特种设备安全宣传教育，普及特种设备安全知识，增强社会公众的特种设备安全意识。

**Article 11** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall strengthen publicity and education on special equipment safety, popularize knowledge on special equipment safety, and enhance the public's awareness of special equipment safety.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位和个人有权向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门和有关部门举报涉及特种设备安全的违法行为，接到举报的部门应当及时处理。

**Article 12** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report illegal acts involving special equipment safety to the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety and relevant departments, and the department receiving the report shall handle it in a timely manner.

### 第二章　生产、经营、使用

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　特种设备生产、经营、使用单位及其主要负责人对其生产、经营、使用的特种设备安全负责。  
特种设备生产、经营、使用单位应当按照国家有关规定配备特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员，并对其进行必要的安全教育和技能培训。

**Article 13** Entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment and their principal persons-in-charge shall be responsible for the safety of the special equipment they produce, operate, or use.  
Entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, equip themselves with special equipment safety管理人员, testing personnel, and operators, and shall provide them with necessary safety education and skill training.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员应当按照国家有关规定取得相应资格，方可从事相关工作。特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员应当严格执行安全技术规范和管理制度，保证特种设备安全。

**Article 14** Special equipment safety managers, testing personnel, and operators shall obtain corresponding qualifications in accordance with relevant State regulations before they may engage in relevant work. Special equipment safety managers, testing personnel, and operators shall strictly implement safety technical specifications and management systems to ensure the safety of special equipment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　特种设备生产、经营、使用单位对其生产、经营、使用的特种设备应当进行自行检测和维护保养，对国家规定实行检验的特种设备应当及时申报并接受检验。

**Article 15** Entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment shall conduct self-inspection and maintenance of the special equipment they produce, operate, or use, and shall promptly declare and undergo inspection for special equipment for which inspection is required by State regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　特种设备采用新材料、新技术、新工艺，与安全技术规范的要求不一致，或者安全技术规范未作要求、可能对安全性能有重大影响的，应当向国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门申报，由国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门及时委托安全技术咨询机构或者相关专业机构进行技术评审，评审结果经国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门批准，方可投入生产、使用。  
国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当将允许使用的新材料、新技术、新工艺的有关技术要求，及时纳入安全技术规范。

**Article 16** Where new materials, new technologies, or new processes are adopted for special equipment, which are inconsistent with the requirements of safety technical specifications, or for which safety technical specifications have no requirements but which may have a significant impact on safety performance, a declaration shall be submitted to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council, which shall promptly entrust a safety technical advisory institution or a relevant professional institution to conduct a technical review. The reviewed result shall be approved by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council before the special equipment may be put into production or use.  
The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council shall promptly incorporate the relevant technical requirements for the new materials, new technologies, or new processes that are allowed to be used into safety technical specifications.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励投保特种设备安全责任保险。

**Article 17** The State encourages the purchase of insurance for special equipment safety liability.

#### 第二节　生产

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家按照分类监督管理的原则对特种设备生产实行许可制度。特种设备生产单位应当具备下列条件，并经负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门许可，方可从事生产活动：  
（一）有与生产相适应的专业技术人员；  
（二）有与生产相适应的设备、设施和工作场所；  
（三）有健全的质量保证、安全管理和岗位责任等制度。

**Article 18** The State applies a licensing system to the production of special equipment based on the principle of classified supervision and administration. A special equipment production entity shall meet the following conditions and shall obtain a license from the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety before it may engage in production activities:  
(1) having professional technicians appropriate for the production;  
(2) having equipment, facilities, and workplaces appropriate for the production;  
(3) having sound quality assurance, safety management, and post responsibility systems.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　特种设备生产单位应当保证特种设备生产符合安全技术规范及相关标准的要求，对其生产的特种设备的安全性能负责。不得生产不符合安全性能要求和能效指标以及国家明令淘汰的特种设备。

**Article 19** A special equipment production entity shall ensure that the production of special equipment meets the requirements of safety technical specifications and relevant standards, and shall be responsible for the safety performance of the special equipment it produces. It shall not produce special equipment that does not meet safety performance requirements and energy efficiency indicators, or that is expressly eliminated by the State.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　锅炉、气瓶、氧舱、客运索道、大型游乐设施的设计文件，应当经负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门核准的检验机构鉴定，方可用于制造。  
特种设备产品、部件或者试制的特种设备新产品、新部件以及特种设备采用的新材料，按照安全技术规范的要求需要通过型式试验进行安全性验证的，应当经负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门核准的检验机构进行型式试验。

**Article 20** The design documents of boilers, gas cylinders, oxygen chambers, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement devices shall be verified by an inspection institution approved by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety before they may be used for manufacture.  
For special equipment products, components, or trial-produced new special equipment products, new components, or new materials adopted for special equipment, if type testing is required for safety verification in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, type testing shall be conducted by an inspection institution approved by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　特种设备出厂时，应当随附安全技术规范要求的设计文件、产品质量合格证明、安装及使用维护保养说明、监督检验证明等相关技术资料和文件，并在特种设备显著位置设置产品铭牌、安全警示标志及其说明。

**Article 21** When special equipment is delivered from the factory, it shall be accompanied by relevant technical data and documents such as design documents required by safety technical specifications, product quality certificates, installation, use and maintenance instructions, and supervision inspection certificates, and a product nameplate, safety warning signs and their descriptions shall be placed in a conspicuous position on the special equipment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　电梯的安装、改造、修理，必须由电梯制造单位或者其委托的依照本法取得相应许可的单位进行。电梯制造单位委托其他单位进行电梯安装、改造、修理的，应当对其安装、改造、修理进行安全指导和监控，并按照安全技术规范的要求进行校验和调试。电梯制造单位对电梯安全性能负责。

**Article 22** The installation, modification, or repair of an elevator must be carried out by the elevator manufacturer or a unit that has obtained corresponding permits pursuant to this Law as entrusted by the manufacturer. Where an elevator manufacturer entrusts another unit to install, modify, or repair an elevator, it shall provide safety guidance and monitoring for the installation, modification, or repair, and shall carry out calibration and commissioning in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications. The elevator manufacturer shall be responsible for the safety performance of the elevator.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　特种设备安装、改造、修理的施工单位应当在施工前将拟进行的特种设备安装、改造、修理情况书面告知直辖市或者设区的市级人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门。

**Article 23** The construction entity for the installation, modification, or repair of special equipment shall, before construction, notify in writing the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government of a municipality directly under the Central Government or of a districted city of the intended installation, modification, or repair of the special equipment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　特种设备安装、改造、修理竣工后，安装、改造、修理的施工单位应当在验收后三十日内将相关技术资料和文件移交特种设备使用单位。特种设备使用单位应当将其存入该特种设备的安全技术档案。

**Article 24** After the completion of the installation, modification, or repair of special equipment, the construction entity for the installation, modification, or repair shall, within 30 days after acceptance, hand over the relevant technical data and documents to the special equipment user. The special equipment user shall store them in the safety technical file of the special equipment.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　锅炉、压力容器、压力管道元件等特种设备的制造过程和锅炉、压力容器、压力管道、电梯、起重机械、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安装、改造、重大修理过程，应当经特种设备检验机构按照安全技术规范的要求进行监督检验；未经监督检验或者监督检验不合格的，不得出厂或者交付使用。

**Article 25** The manufacturing process of special equipment such as boilers, pressure vessels, and pressure pipeline components, and the installation, modification, and major repair processes of boilers, pressure vessels, pressure pipelines, elevators, hoisting machinery, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement devices shall be subject to supervision inspection by a special equipment inspection institution in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications; without supervision inspection or where the supervision inspection is not passed, the special equipment shall not be delivered from the factory or put into use.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立缺陷特种设备召回制度。因生产原因造成特种设备存在危及安全的同一性缺陷的，特种设备生产单位应当立即停止生产，主动召回。  
国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门发现特种设备存在应当召回而未召回的情形时，应当责令特种设备生产单位召回。

**Article 26** The State establishes a recall system for defective special equipment. Where a special equipment has identical defects that endanger safety due to production reasons, the special equipment production entity shall immediately stop production and take the initiative to recall it.  
Where the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council finds that a special equipment that should be recalled has not been recalled, it shall order the special equipment production entity to recall it.

#### 第三节　经营

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　特种设备销售单位销售的特种设备，应当符合安全技术规范及相关标准的要求，其设计文件、产品质量合格证明、安装及使用维护保养说明、监督检验证明等相关技术资料和文件应当齐全。  
特种设备销售单位应当建立特种设备检查验收和销售记录制度。  
禁止销售未取得许可生产的特种设备，未经检验和检验不合格的特种设备，或者国家明令淘汰和已经报废的特种设备。

**Article 27** The special equipment sold by a special equipment seller shall meet the requirements of safety technical specifications and relevant standards, and its relevant technical data and documents such as design documents, product quality certificates, installation, use and maintenance instructions, and supervision inspection certificates shall be complete.  
A special equipment seller shall establish a system for inspection, acceptance, and sales records of special equipment.  
It shall be prohibited to sell special equipment produced without a license, special equipment that has not been inspected or has failed inspection, or special equipment that is expressly eliminated or has been scrapped by the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　特种设备出租单位不得出租未取得许可生产的特种设备或者国家明令淘汰和已经报废的特种设备，以及未按照安全技术规范的要求进行维护保养和未经检验或者检验不合格的特种设备。

**Article 28** A special equipment lessor shall not lease special equipment produced without a license, special equipment expressly eliminated or scrapped by the State, or special equipment that has not been maintained in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, or that has not been inspected or has failed inspection.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　特种设备在出租期间的使用管理和维护保养义务由特种设备出租单位承担，法律另有规定或者当事人另有约定的除外。

**Article 29** The obligations of use management and maintenance of special equipment during the lease period shall be borne by the special equipment lessor, unless otherwise provided by law or agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　进口的特种设备应当符合我国安全技术规范的要求，并经检验合格；需要取得我国特种设备生产许可的，应当取得许可。  
进口特种设备随附的技术资料和文件应当符合[本法第二十一条](#art-21)的规定，其安装及使用维护保养说明、产品铭牌、安全警示标志及其说明应当采用中文。  
特种设备的进出口检验，应当遵守有关进出口商品检验的法律、行政法规。

**Article 30** Imported special equipment shall meet the requirements of China's safety technical specifications and shall pass inspection; where a China special equipment production license is required, such license shall be obtained.  
The technical data and documents accompanying imported special equipment shall comply with the provisions of Article 21 of this Law, and the installation, use and maintenance instructions, product nameplate, safety warning signs and their descriptions shall be in Chinese.  
The import and export inspection of special equipment shall comply with the laws and administrative regulations on the inspection of import and export commodities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　进口特种设备，应当向进口地负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门履行提前告知义务。

**Article 31** When importing special equipment, the importer shall fulfill the obligation of advance notification to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety at the place of import.

#### 第四节　使用

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　特种设备使用单位应当使用取得许可生产并经检验合格的特种设备。  
禁止使用国家明令淘汰和已经报废的特种设备。

**Article 32** A special equipment user shall use special equipment that has been produced with a license and has passed inspection.  
It shall be prohibited to use special equipment expressly eliminated or scrapped by the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　特种设备使用单位应当在特种设备投入使用前或者投入使用后三十日内，向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门办理使用登记，取得使用登记证书。登记标志应当置于该特种设备的显著位置。

**Article 33** A special equipment user shall, before putting the special equipment into use or within 30 days after putting it into use, register the use with the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety and obtain a use registration certificate. The registration mark shall be placed in a conspicuous position on the special equipment.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　特种设备使用单位应当建立岗位责任、隐患治理、应急救援等安全管理制度，制定操作规程，保证特种设备安全运行。

**Article 34** A special equipment user shall establish safety management systems such as post responsibility, hazard rectification, and emergency rescue, and formulate operating procedures to ensure the safe operation of the special equipment.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　特种设备使用单位应当建立特种设备安全技术档案。安全技术档案应当包括以下内容：  
（一）特种设备的设计文件、产品质量合格证明、安装及使用维护保养说明、监督检验证明等相关技术资料和文件；  
（二）特种设备的定期检验和定期自行检查记录；  
（三）特种设备的日常使用状况记录；  
（四）特种设备及其附属仪器仪表的维护保养记录；  
（五）特种设备的运行故障和事故记录。

**Article 35** A special equipment user shall establish a safety technical file for the special equipment. The safety technical file shall include the following:  
(1) relevant technical data and documents such as design documents, product quality certificates, installation, use and maintenance instructions, and supervision inspection certificates of the special equipment;  
(2) records of periodic inspection and periodic self-inspection of the special equipment;  
(3) records of daily use conditions of the special equipment;  
(4) maintenance records of the special equipment and its ancillary instruments and meters;  
(5) records of operational failures and accidents of the special equipment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施等为公众提供服务的特种设备的运营使用单位，应当对特种设备的使用安全负责，设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职的特种设备安全管理人员；其他特种设备使用单位，应当根据情况设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职、兼职的特种设备安全管理人员。

**Article 36** Operating users of special equipment that provide services to the public, such as elevators, passenger ropeways, and large-scale amusement devices, shall be responsible for the safety of the use of the special equipment and shall set up a special equipment safety management institution or be equipped with full-time special equipment safety managers; other special equipment users shall, depending on the circumstances, set up a special equipment safety management institution or be equipped with full-time or part-time special equipment safety managers.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　特种设备的使用应当具有规定的安全距离、安全防护措施。  
与特种设备安全相关的建筑物、附属设施，应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 37** The use of special equipment shall have the specified safety distances and safety protective measures.  
Buildings and ancillary facilities related to the safety of special equipment shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　特种设备属于共有的，共有人可以委托物业服务单位或者其他管理人管理特种设备，受托人履行本法规定的特种设备使用单位的义务，承担相应责任。共有人未委托的，由共有人或者实际管理人履行管理义务，承担相应责任。

**Article 38** Where special equipment is co-owned, the co-owners may entrust a property service unit or other manager to manage the special equipment. The trustee shall perform the obligations of a special equipment user as provided for in this Law and bear corresponding liability. Where the co-owners do not entrust, the co-owners or the actual manager shall perform the management obligations and bear corresponding liability.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　特种设备使用单位应当对其使用的特种设备进行经常性维护保养和定期自行检查，并作出记录。  
特种设备使用单位应当对其使用的特种设备的安全附件、安全保护装置进行定期校验、检修，并作出记录。

**Article 39** A special equipment user shall carry out regular maintenance and periodic self-inspection of the special equipment it uses and shall make records.  
A special equipment user shall periodically calibrate and repair the safety accessories and safety protection devices of the special equipment it uses and shall make records.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　特种设备使用单位应当按照安全技术规范的要求，在检验合格有效期届满前一个月向特种设备检验机构提出定期检验要求。  
特种设备检验机构接到定期检验要求后，应当按照安全技术规范的要求及时进行安全性能检验。特种设备使用单位应当将定期检验标志置于该特种设备的显著位置。  
未经定期检验或者检验不合格的特种设备，不得继续使用。

**Article 40** A special equipment user shall, in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, submit a request for periodic inspection to a special equipment inspection institution one month before the expiry of the validity period of the inspection.  
Upon receiving a request for periodic inspection, the special equipment inspection institution shall promptly carry out a safety performance inspection in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications. The special equipment user shall place the periodic inspection mark in a conspicuous position on the special equipment.  
Special equipment that has not undergone periodic inspection or has failed periodic inspection shall not continue to be used.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　特种设备安全管理人员应当对特种设备使用状况进行经常性检查，发现问题应当立即处理；情况紧急时，可以决定停止使用特种设备并及时报告本单位有关负责人。  
特种设备作业人员在作业过程中发现事故隐患或者其他不安全因素，应当立即向特种设备安全管理人员和单位有关负责人报告；特种设备运行不正常时，特种设备作业人员应当按照操作规程采取有效措施保证安全。

**Article 41** A special equipment safety manager shall conduct regular inspections of the use conditions of the special equipment and shall immediately deal with any problems found; in an emergency, the manager may decide to stop using the special equipment and promptly report to the relevant person-in-charge of the entity.  
Where an operator of special equipment discovers a potential accident hazard or other unsafe factor during the operation, the operator shall immediately report to the special equipment safety manager and the relevant person-in-charge of the entity; where the special equipment operates abnormally, the operator shall take effective measures in accordance with the operating procedures to ensure safety.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　特种设备出现故障或者发生异常情况，特种设备使用单位应当对其进行全面检查，消除事故隐患，方可继续使用。

**Article 42** Where a special equipment malfunctions or has an abnormal condition, the special equipment user shall conduct a comprehensive inspection of it and eliminate the potential accident hazard before it may continue to be used.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　客运索道、大型游乐设施在每日投入使用前，其运营使用单位应当进行试运行和例行安全检查，并对安全附件和安全保护装置进行检查确认。  
电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位应当将电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全使用说明、安全注意事项和警示标志置于易于为乘客注意的显著位置。  
公众乘坐或者操作电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施，应当遵守安全使用说明和安全注意事项的要求，服从有关工作人员的管理和指挥；遇有运行不正常时，应当按照安全指引，有序撤离。

**Article 43** Before a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement device is put into use each day, the operating user shall conduct a trial run and routine safety inspection, and shall check and confirm the safety accessories and safety protection devices.  
The operating user of an elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement device shall place the safety use instructions, safety precautions, and warning signs of the elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement device in a conspicuous position easily noticeable by passengers.  
The public riding or operating an elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement device shall comply with the requirements of the safety use instructions and safety precautions, and shall obey the management and direction of relevant staff; in case of abnormal operation, they shall evacuate in an orderly manner in accordance with safety guidelines.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　锅炉使用单位应当按照安全技术规范的要求进行锅炉水（介）质处理，并接受特种设备检验机构的定期检验。  
从事锅炉清洗，应当按照安全技术规范的要求进行，并接受特种设备检验机构的监督检验。

**Article 44** A boiler user shall carry out boiler water (medium) treatment in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications and shall accept periodic inspection by a special equipment inspection institution.  
The cleaning of boilers shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications and shall be subject to supervision inspection by a special equipment inspection institution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　电梯的维护保养应当由电梯制造单位或者依照本法取得许可的安装、改造、修理单位进行。  
电梯的维护保养单位应当在维护保养中严格执行安全技术规范的要求，保证其维护保养的电梯的安全性能，并负责落实现场安全防护措施，保证施工安全。  
电梯的维护保养单位应当对其维护保养的电梯的安全性能负责；接到故障通知后，应当立即赶赴现场，并采取必要的应急救援措施。

**Article 45** The maintenance of an elevator shall be carried out by the elevator manufacturer or by an installation, modification, or repair unit that has obtained a license pursuant to this Law.  
The elevator maintenance unit shall strictly implement the requirements of safety technical specifications during maintenance, ensure the safety performance of the elevator it maintains, and be responsible for implementing on-site safety protection measures to ensure construction safety.  
The elevator maintenance unit shall be responsible for the safety performance of the elevator it maintains; upon receiving a fault notice, it shall immediately rush to the scene and take necessary emergency rescue measures.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　电梯投入使用后，电梯制造单位应当对其制造的电梯的安全运行情况进行跟踪调查和了解，对电梯的维护保养单位或者使用单位在维护保养和安全运行方面存在的问题，提出改进建议，并提供必要的技术帮助；发现电梯存在严重事故隐患时，应当及时告知电梯使用单位，并向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告。电梯制造单位对调查和了解的情况，应当作出记录。

**Article 46** After an elevator is put into use, the elevator manufacturer shall follow up and investigate the safe operation of the elevator it manufactures, propose improvement suggestions for problems existing in the maintenance and safe operation of the elevator by the maintenance unit or user, and provide necessary technical assistance; where a serious potential accident hazard is found in the elevator, the manufacturer shall promptly notify the elevator user and report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety. The elevator manufacturer shall make records of the investigation and understanding.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　特种设备进行改造、修理，按照规定需要变更使用登记的，应当办理变更登记，方可继续使用。

**Article 47** Where the modification or repair of special equipment requires a change of use registration as prescribed, the change registration shall be completed before it may continue to be used.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　特种设备存在严重事故隐患，无改造、修理价值，或者达到安全技术规范规定的其他报废条件的，特种设备使用单位应当依法履行报废义务，采取必要措施消除该特种设备的使用功能，并向原登记的负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门办理使用登记证书注销手续。  
前款规定报废条件以外的特种设备，达到设计使用年限可以继续使用的，应当按照安全技术规范的要求通过检验或者安全评估，并办理使用登记证书变更，方可继续使用。允许继续使用的，应当采取加强检验、检测和维护保养等措施，确保使用安全。

**Article 48** Where a special equipment has a serious potential accident hazard, has no value for modification or repair, or meets other scrapping conditions specified in safety technical specifications, the special equipment user shall fulfill the scrapping obligation according to law, take necessary measures to eliminate the use function of the special equipment, and go through the cancellation procedures for the use registration certificate with the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety that originally registered it.  
For special equipment other than those meeting the scrapping conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, if it has reached the design service life but may continue to be used, it shall pass inspection or safety assessment in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, and the change of the use registration certificate shall be completed before it may continue to be used. Where continued use is allowed, measures such as strengthening inspection, testing, and maintenance shall be taken to ensure safe use.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　移动式压力容器、气瓶充装单位，应当具备下列条件，并经负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门许可，方可从事充装活动：  
（一）有与充装和管理相适应的管理人员和技术人员；  
（二）有与充装和管理相适应的充装设备、检测手段、场地厂房、器具、安全设施；  
（三）有健全的充装管理制度、责任制度、处理措施。  
充装单位应当建立充装前后的检查、记录制度，禁止对不符合安全技术规范要求的移动式压力容器和气瓶进行充装。  
气瓶充装单位应当向气体使用者提供符合安全技术规范要求的气瓶，对气体使用者进行气瓶安全使用指导，并按照安全技术规范的要求办理气瓶使用登记，及时申报定期检验。

**Article 49** A filling unit for mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders shall meet the following conditions and shall obtain a license from the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety before it may engage in filling activities:  
(1) having management personnel and technical personnel appropriate for filling and management;  
(2) having filling equipment, testing means, site, plant, apparatus, and safety facilities appropriate for filling and management;  
(3) having sound filling management systems, responsibility systems, and handling measures.  
A filling unit shall establish a system for inspection and recording before and after filling, and shall be prohibited from filling mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders that do not meet the requirements of safety technical specifications.  
A gas cylinder filling unit shall provide gas users with gas cylinders that meet the requirements of safety technical specifications, provide guidance on safe use of gas cylinders to gas users, and handle gas cylinder use registration in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, and promptly declare periodic inspection.

### 第三章　检验、检测

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　从事本法规定的监督检验、定期检验的特种设备检验机构，以及为特种设备生产、经营、使用提供检测服务的特种设备检测机构，应当具备下列条件，并经负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门核准，方可从事检验、检测工作：  
（一）有与检验、检测工作相适应的检验、检测人员；  
（二）有与检验、检测工作相适应的检验、检测仪器和设备；  
（三）有健全的检验、检测管理制度和责任制度。

**Article 50** Special equipment inspection institutions engaged in supervision inspection and periodic inspection as provided for in this Law, and special equipment testing institutions providing testing services for the production, operation, or use of special equipment, shall meet the following conditions and shall be approved by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety before they may engage in inspection and testing work:  
(1) having inspection and testing personnel appropriate for the inspection and testing work;  
(2) having inspection and testing instruments and equipment appropriate for the inspection and testing work;  
(3) having sound inspection and testing management systems and responsibility systems.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　特种设备检验、检测机构的检验、检测人员应当经考核，取得检验、检测人员资格，方可从事检验、检测工作。  
特种设备检验、检测机构的检验、检测人员不得同时在两个以上检验、检测机构中执业；变更执业机构的，应当依法办理变更手续。

**Article 51** Inspection and testing personnel of special equipment inspection and testing institutions shall pass assessment and obtain inspection and testing personnel qualifications before they may engage in inspection and testing work.  
Inspection and testing personnel of special equipment inspection and testing institutions shall not concurrently practice in two or more inspection and testing institutions; where they change their practicing institutions, they shall go through the change procedures according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　特种设备检验、检测工作应当遵守法律、行政法规的规定，并按照安全技术规范的要求进行。  
特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员应当依法为特种设备生产、经营、使用单位提供安全、可靠、便捷、诚信的检验、检测服务。

**Article 52** Inspection and testing work for special equipment shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications.  
Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and their inspection and testing personnel shall provide safe, reliable, convenient, and honest inspection and testing services for entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员应当客观、公正、及时地出具检验、检测报告，并对检验、检测结果和鉴定结论负责。  
特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员在检验、检测中发现特种设备存在严重事故隐患时，应当及时告知相关单位，并立即向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告。  
负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当组织对特种设备检验、检测机构的检验、检测结果和鉴定结论进行监督抽查，但应当防止重复抽查。监督抽查结果应当向社会公布。

**Article 53** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and their inspection and testing personnel shall objectively, fairly, and timely issue inspection and testing reports, and shall be responsible for the inspection and testing results and expert evaluation conclusions.  
Where a special equipment inspection or testing institution or its inspection or testing personnel finds a serious potential accident hazard in a special equipment during inspection or testing, it shall promptly notify the relevant entity and immediately report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety.  
The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall organize random supervision inspections of the inspection and testing results and expert evaluation conclusions of special equipment inspection and testing institutions, but shall avoid repeated inspections. The results of random supervision inspections shall be made public.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　特种设备生产、经营、使用单位应当按照安全技术规范的要求向特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员提供特种设备相关资料和必要的检验、检测条件，并对资料的真实性负责。

**Article 54** Entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment shall, in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, provide special equipment inspection and testing institutions and their inspection and testing personnel with relevant data and necessary inspection and testing conditions for special equipment, and shall be responsible for the authenticity of the data.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员对检验、检测过程中知悉的商业秘密，负有保密义务。  
特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员不得从事有关特种设备的生产、经营活动，不得推荐或者监制、监销特种设备。

**Article 55** Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and their inspection and testing personnel shall have a duty of confidentiality regarding trade secrets learned during inspection and testing.  
Special equipment inspection and testing institutions and their inspection and testing personnel shall not engage in the production or operation of special equipment, and shall not recommend, supervise the production, or supervise the sale of special equipment.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　特种设备检验机构及其检验人员利用检验工作故意刁难特种设备生产、经营、使用单位的，特种设备生产、经营、使用单位有权向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门投诉，接到投诉的部门应当及时进行调查处理。

**Article 56** Where a special equipment inspection institution or its inspection personnel deliberately creates difficulties for entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment by taking advantage of inspection work, the entity that produces, operates, or uses special equipment shall have the right to complain to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety, and the department receiving the complaint shall promptly conduct an investigation and handle it.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门依照本法规定，对特种设备生产、经营、使用单位和检验、检测机构实施监督检查。  
负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当对学校、幼儿园以及医院、车站、客运码头、商场、体育场馆、展览馆、公园等公众聚集场所的特种设备，实施重点安全监督检查。

**Article 57** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, conduct supervision inspections on entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment and on inspection and testing institutions.  
The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall implement key safety supervision inspections on special equipment in public gathering places such as schools, kindergartens, hospitals, railway stations, passenger terminals, shopping malls, stadiums, exhibition halls, and parks.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门实施本法规定的许可工作，应当依照本法和其他有关法律、行政法规规定的条件和程序以及安全技术规范的要求进行审查；不符合规定的，不得许可。

**Article 58** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety, when carrying out licensing work provided for in this Law, shall conduct examination in accordance with the conditions, procedures, and requirements of safety technical specifications provided for in this Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations; where the requirements are not met, no license shall be granted.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门在办理本法规定的许可时，其受理、审查、许可的程序必须公开，并应当自受理申请之日起三十日内，作出许可或者不予许可的决定；不予许可的，应当书面向申请人说明理由。

**Article 59** When handling licensing provided for in this Law, the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall make their procedures for acceptance, examination, and licensing public, and shall make a decision to grant or not to grant the license within 30 days from the date of accepting the application; where the license is not granted, they shall state the reasons in writing to the applicant.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门对依法办理使用登记的特种设备应当建立完整的监督管理档案和信息查询系统；对达到报废条件的特种设备，应当及时督促特种设备使用单位依法履行报废义务。

**Article 60** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall establish complete supervision and administration files and information inquiry systems for special equipment for which use registration has been handled according to law; for special equipment that has met the scrapping conditions, they shall promptly urge the special equipment user to fulfill the scrapping obligation according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门在依法履行监督检查职责时，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）进入现场进行检查，向特种设备生产、经营、使用单位和检验、检测机构的主要负责人和其他有关人员调查、了解有关情况；  
（二）根据举报或者取得的涉嫌违法证据，查阅、复制特种设备生产、经营、使用单位和检验、检测机构的有关合同、发票、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
（三）对有证据表明不符合安全技术规范要求或者存在严重事故隐患的特种设备实施查封、扣押；  
（四）对流入市场的达到报废条件或者已经报废的特种设备实施查封、扣押；  
（五）对违反本法规定的行为作出行政处罚决定。

**Article 61** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety may exercise the following powers when performing supervision and inspection duties according to law:  
(1) entering the site for inspection, and investigating and understanding relevant circumstances from the principal persons-in-charge and other relevant personnel of entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment and inspection and testing institutions;  
(2) based on reports or obtained evidence of suspected illegal acts, consulting and copying relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials of entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment and inspection and testing institutions;  
(3) sealing up or seizing special equipment that is evidenced to not meet requirements of safety technical specifications or to have serious potential accident hazards;  
(4) sealing up or seizing special equipment that has entered the market and meets scrapping conditions or has been scrapped;  
(5) making decisions on administrative penalties for acts violating the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门在依法履行职责过程中，发现违反本法规定和安全技术规范要求的行为或者特种设备存在事故隐患时，应当以书面形式发出特种设备安全监察指令，责令有关单位及时采取措施予以改正或者消除事故隐患。紧急情况下要求有关单位采取紧急处置措施的，应当随后补发特种设备安全监察指令。

**Article 62** Where, in the course of performing duties according to law, the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety discover acts violating the provisions of this Law or the requirements of safety technical specifications, or find that a special equipment has a potential accident hazard, they shall issue a special equipment safety supervision directive in writing, ordering the relevant entity to take timely measures to correct or eliminate the potential accident hazard. In an emergency, where the relevant entity is required to take emergency disposal measures, a special equipment safety supervision directive shall be subsequently issued retroactively.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门在依法履行职责过程中，发现重大违法行为或者特种设备存在严重事故隐患时，应当责令有关单位立即停止违法行为、采取措施消除事故隐患，并及时向上级负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告。接到报告的负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当采取必要措施，及时予以处理。  
对违法行为、严重事故隐患的处理需要当地人民政府和有关部门的支持、配合时，负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当报告当地人民政府，并通知其他有关部门。当地人民政府和其他有关部门应当采取必要措施，及时予以处理。

**Article 63** Where, in the course of performing duties according to law, the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety discover a major illegal act or a serious potential accident hazard in a special equipment, they shall order the relevant entity to immediately stop the illegal act and take measures to eliminate the potential accident hazard, and shall promptly report to the superior department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety. The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety that receives the report shall take necessary measures to handle the matter promptly.  
Where the handling of an illegal act or serious potential accident hazard requires the support and cooperation of the local people's government and relevant departments, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall report to the local people's government and notify other relevant departments. The local people's government and other relevant departments shall take necessary measures to handle the matter promptly.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　地方各级人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门不得要求已经依照本法规定在其他地方取得许可的特种设备生产单位重复取得许可，不得要求对已经依照本法规定在其他地方检验合格的特种设备重复进行检验。

**Article 64** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of local people's governments at all levels shall not require a special equipment production entity that has already obtained a license in another place pursuant to this Law to obtain a duplicate license, and shall not require a special equipment that has already passed inspection in another place pursuant to this Law to undergo duplicate inspection.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门的安全监察人员应当熟悉相关法律、法规，具有相应的专业知识和工作经验，取得特种设备安全行政执法证件。  
特种设备安全监察人员应当忠于职守、坚持原则、秉公执法。  
负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门实施安全监督检查时，应当有二名以上特种设备安全监察人员参加，并出示有效的特种设备安全行政执法证件。

**Article 65** Safety inspectors of the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall be familiar with relevant laws and regulations, have corresponding professional knowledge and work experience, and obtain special equipment safety administrative law enforcement certificates.  
Special equipment safety inspectors shall be devoted to their duties, adhere to principles, and enforce the law impartially.  
When carrying out safety supervision inspections, the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall have at least two special equipment safety inspectors present and shall present valid special equipment safety administrative law enforcement certificates.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门对特种设备生产、经营、使用单位和检验、检测机构实施监督检查，应当对每次监督检查的内容、发现的问题及处理情况作出记录，并由参加监督检查的特种设备安全监察人员和被检查单位的有关负责人签字后归档。被检查单位的有关负责人拒绝签字的，特种设备安全监察人员应当将情况记录在案。

**Article 66** When the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety conduct supervision inspections on entities that produce, operate, or use special equipment and on inspection and testing institutions, they shall make records of the contents of each supervision inspection, the problems found, and the handling situation, and the records shall be signed by the special equipment safety inspectors participating in the supervision inspection and the relevant person-in-charge of the inspected entity and then filed. Where the relevant person-in-charge of the inspected entity refuses to sign, the special equipment safety inspector shall record the situation in the file.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门及其工作人员不得推荐或者监制、监销特种设备；对履行职责过程中知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 67** The departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety and their staff shall not recommend, supervise the production, or supervise the sale of special equipment; they shall have a duty of confidentiality regarding trade secrets learned in the course of performing their duties.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当定期向社会公布特种设备安全总体状况。

**Article 68** The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council and the departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regularly make public the overall safety status of special equipment.

### 第五章　事故应急救援与调查处理

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当依法组织制定特种设备重特大事故应急预案，报国务院批准后纳入国家突发事件应急预案体系。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府及其负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当依法组织制定本行政区域内特种设备事故应急预案，建立或者纳入相应的应急处置与救援体系。  
特种设备使用单位应当制定特种设备事故应急专项预案，并定期进行应急演练。

**Article 69** The department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council shall organize the formulation of an emergency plan for major and extraordinary accidents involving special equipment according to law, and upon approval by the State Council, incorporate it into the national emergency plan system for emergencies.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their departments responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall organize the formulation of emergency plans for special equipment accidents within their respective administrative regions according to law, and establish or incorporate them into the corresponding emergency response and rescue systems.  
Special equipment users shall formulate special emergency plans for special equipment accidents and conduct emergency drills regularly.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　特种设备发生事故后，事故发生单位应当按照应急预案采取措施，组织抢救，防止事故扩大，减少人员伤亡和财产损失，保护事故现场和有关证据，并及时向事故发生地县级以上人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门和有关部门报告。  
县级以上人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门接到事故报告，应当尽快核实情况，立即向本级人民政府报告，并按照规定逐级上报。必要时，负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门可以越级上报事故情况。对特别重大事故、重大事故，国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门应当立即报告国务院并通报国务院安全生产监督管理部门等有关部门。  
与事故相关的单位和人员不得迟报、谎报或者瞒报事故情况，不得隐匿、毁灭有关证据或者故意破坏事故现场。

**Article 70** After a special equipment accident occurs, the entity where the accident occurred shall take measures in accordance with the emergency plan, organize rescue, prevent the accident from expanding, reduce casualties and property losses, protect the accident scene and relevant evidence, and promptly report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government at or above the county level at the place where the accident occurred and to relevant departments.  
Upon receiving an accident report, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government at or above the county level shall verify the situation as soon as possible, immediately report to the people's government at the same level, and report level by level in accordance with regulations. When necessary, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety may report the accident situation by skipping levels. For particularly major accidents and major accidents, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council shall immediately report to the State Council and notify the department of emergency management under the State Council and other relevant departments.  
Entities and personnel related to the accident shall not delay reporting, make false reports, or conceal the accident situation, and shall not conceal, destroy relevant evidence, or intentionally damage the accident scene.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　事故发生地人民政府接到事故报告，应当依法启动应急预案，采取应急处置措施，组织应急救援。

**Article 71** Upon receiving an accident report, the people's government at the place where the accident occurred shall activate the emergency plan according to law, take emergency response measures, and organize emergency rescue.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　特种设备发生特别重大事故，由国务院或者国务院授权有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
发生重大事故，由国务院负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
发生较大事故，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
发生一般事故，由设区的市级人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门会同有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
事故调查组应当依法、独立、公正开展调查，提出事故调查报告。

**Article 72** Where a particularly major special equipment accident occurs, the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council shall organize an accident investigation team to conduct an investigation.  
Where a major accident occurs, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety under the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, organize an accident investigation team to conduct an investigation.  
Where a relatively large accident occurs, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, together with relevant departments, organize an accident investigation team to conduct an investigation.  
Where a general accident occurs, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government at the level of a districted city shall, together with relevant departments, organize an accident investigation team to conduct an investigation.  
The accident investigation team shall conduct an investigation according to law, independently and impartially, and submit an accident investigation report.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　组织事故调查的部门应当将事故调查报告报本级人民政府，并报上一级人民政府负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门备案。有关部门和单位应当依照法律、行政法规的规定，追究事故责任单位和人员的责任。  
事故责任单位应当依法落实整改措施，预防同类事故发生。事故造成损害的，事故责任单位应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 73** The department that organizes the accident investigation shall submit the accident investigation report to the people's government at the same level and report it to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety of the people's government at the next higher level for the record. Relevant departments and entities shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, investigate the liability of the entity and personnel responsible for the accident.  
The entity responsible for the accident shall implement corrective measures according to law to prevent the occurrence of similar accidents. Where the accident causes damage, the entity responsible for the accident shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本法规定，未经许可从事特种设备生产活动的，责令停止生产，没收违法制造的特种设备，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；已经实施安装、改造、修理的，责令恢复原状或者责令限期由取得许可的单位重新安装、改造、修理。

**Article 74** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an entity engages in special equipment production activities without a license, it shall be ordered to stop production, the illegally manufactured special equipment shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if installation, modification, or repair has already been carried out, it shall be ordered to restore the original condition or be ordered to have a unit that has obtained a license re-install, re-modify, or re-repair within a specified period.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，特种设备的设计文件未经鉴定，擅自用于制造的，责令改正，没收违法制造的特种设备，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 75** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the design documents of special equipment are used for manufacture without verification, the entity shall be ordered to correct, the illegally manufactured special equipment shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本法规定，未进行型式试验的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款。

**Article 76** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, type testing is not carried out, the entity shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，特种设备出厂时，未按照安全技术规范的要求随附相关技术资料和文件的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止制造、销售，处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 77** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, when special equipment is delivered from the factory, the relevant technical data and documents are not attached in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications, the entity shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop manufacturing and selling, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，特种设备安装、改造、修理的施工单位在施工前未书面告知负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门即行施工的，或者在验收后三十日内未将相关技术资料和文件移交特种设备使用单位的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 78** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the construction entity for the installation, modification, or repair of special equipment starts construction without notifying in writing the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety before construction, or fails to transfer the relevant technical data and documents to the special equipment user within 30 days after acceptance, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，特种设备的制造、安装、改造、重大修理以及锅炉清洗过程，未经监督检验的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销生产许可证。

**Article 79** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the manufacturing, installation, modification, major repair, or boiler cleaning process of special equipment is not subject to supervision inspection, the entity shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the production license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，电梯制造单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未按照安全技术规范的要求对电梯进行校验、调试的；  
（二）对电梯的安全运行情况进行跟踪调查和了解时，发现存在严重事故隐患，未及时告知电梯使用单位并向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告的。

**Article 80** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an elevator manufacturer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to calibrate or commission the elevator in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications;  
(2) when following up and investigating the safe operation of the elevator, finding a serious potential accident hazard but failing to promptly notify the elevator user and report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，特种设备生产单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止生产，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销生产许可证：  
（一）不再具备生产条件、生产许可证已经过期或者超出许可范围生产的；  
（二）明知特种设备存在同一性缺陷，未立即停止生产并召回的。  
违反本法规定，特种设备生产单位生产、销售、交付国家明令淘汰的特种设备的，责令停止生产、销售，没收违法生产、销售、交付的特种设备，处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
特种设备生产单位涂改、倒卖、出租、出借生产许可证的，责令停止生产，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销生产许可证。

**Article 81** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment production entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop production, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the production license shall be revoked:  
(1) no longer having production conditions, the production license has expired, or producing beyond the scope of the license;  
(2) knowing that the special equipment has identical defects, failing to immediately stop production and recall it.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment production entity produces, sells, or delivers special equipment expressly eliminated by the State, it shall be ordered to stop production and selling, the illegally produced, sold, or delivered special equipment shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where a special equipment production entity alters, sells, leases, or lends its production license, it shall be ordered to stop production, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the production license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，特种设备经营单位有下列行为之一的，责令停止经营，没收违法经营的特种设备，处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）销售、出租未取得许可生产，未经检验或者检验不合格的特种设备的；  
（二）销售、出租国家明令淘汰、已经报废的特种设备，或者未按照安全技术规范的要求进行维护保养的特种设备的。  
违反本法规定，特种设备销售单位未建立检查验收和销售记录制度，或者进口特种设备未履行提前告知义务的，责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。  
特种设备生产单位销售、交付未经检验或者检验不合格的特种设备的，依照本条第一款规定处罚；情节严重的，吊销生产许可证。

**Article 82** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment operating entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to stop operation, the illegally operated special equipment shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) selling or leasing special equipment that has not obtained a license for production, has not been inspected, or has failed inspection;  
(2) selling or leasing special equipment expressly eliminated or scrapped by the State, or special equipment that has not been maintained in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment seller fails to establish a system for inspection, acceptance, and sales records, or an imported special equipment fails to fulfill the obligation of advance notification, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a special equipment production entity sells or delivers special equipment that has not been inspected or has failed inspection, it shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph; if the circumstances are serious, the production license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反本法规定，特种设备使用单位有下列行为之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止使用有关特种设备，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款：  
（一）使用特种设备未按照规定办理使用登记的；  
（二）未建立特种设备安全技术档案或者安全技术档案不符合规定要求，或者未依法设置使用登记标志、定期检验标志的；  
（三）未对其使用的特种设备进行经常性维护保养和定期自行检查，或者未对其使用的特种设备的安全附件、安全保护装置进行定期校验、检修，并作出记录的；  
（四）未按照安全技术规范的要求及时申报并接受检验的；  
（五）未按照安全技术规范的要求进行锅炉水（介）质处理的；  
（六）未制定特种设备事故应急专项预案的。

**Article 83** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment user commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop using the relevant special equipment, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using special equipment without completing use registration in accordance with regulations;  
(2) failing to establish a safety technical file for the special equipment, or the safety technical file does not meet the prescribed requirements, or failing to place use registration marks or periodic inspection marks according to law;  
(3) failing to carry out regular maintenance and periodic self-inspection of the special equipment it uses, or failing to periodically calibrate or repair the safety accessories and safety protection devices of the special equipment it uses, and failing to make records;  
(4) failing to promptly declare and undergo inspection in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications;  
(5) failing to carry out boiler water (medium) treatment in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications;  
(6) failing to formulate a special emergency plan for special equipment accidents.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，特种设备使用单位有下列行为之一的，责令停止使用有关特种设备，处三万元以上三十万元以下罚款：  
（一）使用未取得许可生产，未经检验或者检验不合格的特种设备，或者国家明令淘汰、已经报废的特种设备的；  
（二）特种设备出现故障或者发生异常情况，未对其进行全面检查、消除事故隐患，继续使用的；  
（三）特种设备存在严重事故隐患，无改造、修理价值，或者达到安全技术规范规定的其他报废条件，未依法履行报废义务，并办理使用登记证书注销手续的。

**Article 84** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment user commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to stop using the relevant special equipment, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using special equipment that has not obtained a license for production, has not been inspected, or has failed inspection, or special equipment expressly eliminated or scrapped by the State;  
(2) where the special equipment malfunctions or has an abnormal condition, failing to conduct a comprehensive inspection and eliminate the potential accident hazard, and continuing to use it;  
(3) where the special equipment has a serious potential accident hazard, has no value for modification or repair, or meets other scrapping conditions specified in safety technical specifications, failing to fulfill the scrapping obligation according to law and complete the cancellation procedures for the use registration certificate.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，移动式压力容器、气瓶充装单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销充装许可证：  
（一）未按照规定实施充装前后的检查、记录制度的；  
（二）对不符合安全技术规范要求的移动式压力容器和气瓶进行充装的。  
违反本法规定，未经许可，擅自从事移动式压力容器或者气瓶充装活动的，予以取缔，没收违法充装的气瓶，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 85** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a filling unit for mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the filling license shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to implement the system for inspection and recording before and after filling in accordance with regulations;  
(2) filling mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders that do not meet the requirements of safety technical specifications.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an entity engages in the filling of mobile pressure vessels or gas cylinders without a license, it shall be banned, the illegally filled gas cylinders shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，特种设备生产、经营、使用单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止使用有关特种设备或者停产停业整顿，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款：  
（一）未配备具有相应资格的特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员的；  
（二）使用未取得相应资格的人员从事特种设备安全管理、检测和作业的；  
（三）未对特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员进行安全教育和技能培训的。

**Article 86** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment production, operation, or use entity falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop using the relevant special equipment or to stop production for rectification, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to equip itself with special equipment safety managers, testing personnel, and operators with corresponding qualifications;  
(2) using personnel without corresponding qualifications to engage in special equipment safety management, testing, or operation;  
(3) failing to provide safety education and skill training for special equipment safety managers, testing personnel, and operators.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的运营使用单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停止使用有关特种设备或者停产停业整顿，处二万元以上十万元以下罚款：  
（一）未设置特种设备安全管理机构或者配备专职的特种设备安全管理人员的；  
（二）客运索道、大型游乐设施每日投入使用前，未进行试运行和例行安全检查，未对安全附件和安全保护装置进行检查确认的；  
（三）未将电梯、客运索道、大型游乐设施的安全使用说明、安全注意事项和警示标志置于易于为乘客注意的显著位置的。

**Article 87** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an operating user of an elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement device falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop using the relevant special equipment or to stop production for rectification, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) failing to set up a special equipment safety management institution or to be equipped with full-time special equipment safety managers;  
(2) before a passenger ropeway or large-scale amusement device is put into use each day, failing to conduct a trial run and routine safety inspection, and failing to check and confirm the safety accessories and safety protection devices;  
(3) failing to place the safety use instructions, safety precautions, and warning signs of the elevator, passenger ropeway, or large-scale amusement device in a conspicuous position easily noticeable by passengers.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，未经许可，擅自从事电梯维护保养的，责令停止违法行为，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
电梯的维护保养单位未按照本法规定以及安全技术规范的要求，进行电梯维护保养的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 88** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an entity engages in elevator maintenance without a license, it shall be ordered to stop the illegal act, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where an elevator maintenance unit fails to carry out elevator maintenance in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the requirements of safety technical specifications, it shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　发生特种设备事故，有下列情形之一的，对单位处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款；对主要负责人处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；主要负责人属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分：  
（一）发生特种设备事故时，不立即组织抢救或者在事故调查处理期间擅离职守或者逃匿的；  
（二）对特种设备事故迟报、谎报或者瞒报的。

**Article 89** Where a special equipment accident occurs and any of the following circumstances exists, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity; a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the principal person-in-charge; if the principal person-in-charge is a functionary of the State, he or she shall also be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) when a special equipment accident occurs, failing to immediately organize rescue, or leaving the post without authorization or absconding during the accident investigation and handling;  
(2) delaying reporting, making false reports, or concealing the special equipment accident.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　发生事故，对负有责任的单位除要求其依法承担相应的赔偿等责任外，依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）发生一般事故，处十万元以上二十万元以下罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故，处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故，处五十万元以上二百万元以下罚款。

**Article 90** For an accident, the entity responsible shall, in addition to bearing corresponding compensation liability according to law, be fined in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for a general accident, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan;  
(2) for a relatively large accident, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan;  
(3) for a major accident, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　对事故发生负有责任的单位的主要负责人未依法履行职责或者负有领导责任的，依照下列规定处以罚款；属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分：  
（一）发生一般事故，处上一年年收入百分之三十的罚款；  
（二）发生较大事故，处上一年年收入百分之四十的罚款；  
（三）发生重大事故，处上一年年收入百分之六十的罚款。

**Article 91** Where the principal person-in-charge of an entity responsible for an accident fails to perform his or her duties according to law or bears leadership responsibility, he or she shall be fined in accordance with the following provisions; if he or she is a functionary of the State, he or she shall also be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) for a general accident, a fine of 30 percent of his or her annual income in the previous year;  
(2) for a relatively large accident, a fine of 40 percent of his or her annual income in the previous year;  
(3) for a major accident, a fine of 60 percent of his or her annual income in the previous year.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　违反本法规定，特种设备安全管理人员、检测人员和作业人员不履行岗位职责，违反操作规程和有关安全规章制度，造成事故的，吊销相关人员的资格。

**Article 92** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment safety manager, testing personnel, or operator fails to perform his or her post duties, violates operating procedures or relevant safety rules and regulations, and causes an accident, his or her qualifications shall be revoked.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　违反本法规定，特种设备检验、检测机构及其检验、检测人员有下列行为之一的，责令改正，对机构处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销机构资质和有关人员的资格：  
（一）未经核准或者超出核准范围、使用未取得相应资格的人员从事检验、检测的；  
（二）未按照安全技术规范的要求进行检验、检测的；  
（三）出具虚假的检验、检测结果和鉴定结论或者检验、检测结果和鉴定结论严重失实的；  
（四）发现特种设备存在严重事故隐患，未及时告知相关单位，并立即向负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告的；  
（五）泄露检验、检测过程中知悉的商业秘密的；  
（六）从事有关特种设备的生产、经营活动的；  
（七）推荐或者监制、监销特种设备的；  
（八）利用检验工作故意刁难相关单位的。  
违反本法规定，特种设备检验、检测机构的检验、检测人员同时在两个以上检验、检测机构中执业的，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销其资格。

**Article 93** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment inspection or testing institution or its inspection or testing personnel commits any of the following acts, the institution shall be ordered to correct, fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and the directly responsible person-in-charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the institution's qualification and the relevant personnel's qualifications shall be revoked:  
(1) engaging in inspection or testing without approval or beyond the approved scope, or using personnel who have not obtained corresponding qualifications for inspection or testing;  
(2) failing to carry out inspection or testing in accordance with the requirements of safety technical specifications;  
(3) issuing false inspection or testing results and expert evaluation conclusions, or inspection or testing results and expert evaluation conclusions that are seriously inaccurate;  
(4) finding a serious potential accident hazard in special equipment but failing to promptly notify the relevant entity and immediately report to the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety;  
(5) disclosing trade secrets learned during inspection or testing;  
(6) engaging in the production or operation of special equipment;  
(7) recommending, supervising the production, or supervising the sale of special equipment;  
(8) deliberately creating difficulties for relevant entities by taking advantage of inspection work.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an inspection or testing personnel of a special equipment inspection or testing institution concurrently practices in two or more inspection or testing institutions, he or she shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, his or her qualifications shall be revoked.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由上级机关责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）未依照法律、行政法规规定的条件、程序实施许可的；  
（二）发现未经许可擅自从事特种设备的生产、使用或者检验、检测活动不予取缔或者不依法予以处理的；  
（三）发现特种设备生产单位不再具备本法规定的条件而不吊销其许可证，或者发现特种设备生产、经营、使用违法行为不予查处的；  
（四）发现特种设备检验、检测机构不再具备本法规定的条件而不撤销其核准，或者对其出具虚假的检验、检测结果和鉴定结论或者检验、检测结果和鉴定结论严重失实的行为不予查处的；  
（五）发现违反本法规定和安全技术规范要求的行为或者特种设备存在事故隐患，不立即处理的；  
（六）发现重大违法行为或者特种设备存在严重事故隐患，未及时向上级负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门报告，或者接到报告的负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门不立即处理的；  
（七）要求已经依照本法规定在其他地方取得许可的特种设备生产单位重复取得许可，或者要求对已经依照本法规定在其他地方检验合格的特种设备重复进行检验的；  
（八）推荐或者监制、监销特种设备的；  
（九）泄露履行职责过程中知悉的商业秘密的；  
（十）接到特种设备事故报告未立即向本级人民政府报告，并按照规定上报的；  
（十一）迟报、漏报、谎报或者瞒报事故的；  
（十二）妨碍事故救援或者事故调查处理的；  
（十三）其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。

**Article 94** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety or its staff commits any of the following acts, the superior organ shall order it to correct; the directly responsible person-in-charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be given a sanction according to law:  
(1) failing to implement licensing in accordance with the conditions and procedures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations;  
(2) discovering that an entity engages in the production, use, inspection, or testing of special equipment without a license but failing to ban it or failing to deal with it according to law;  
(3) discovering that a special equipment production entity no longer meets the conditions prescribed in this Law but failing to revoke its license, or discovering illegal acts in the production, operation, or use of special equipment but failing to investigate and punish them;  
(4) discovering that a special equipment inspection or testing institution no longer meets the conditions prescribed in this Law but failing to revoke its approval, or failing to investigate and punish its act of issuing false inspection or testing results and expert evaluation conclusions or inspection or testing results and expert evaluation conclusions that are seriously inaccurate;  
(5) discovering acts violating the provisions of this Law or the requirements of safety technical specifications, or finding that a special equipment has a potential accident hazard, but failing to deal with it immediately;  
(6) discovering a major illegal act or a serious potential accident hazard in special equipment but failing to promptly report to the superior department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety, or the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety that receives the report failing to deal with it immediately;  
(7) requiring a special equipment production entity that has already obtained a license in another place pursuant to this Law to obtain a duplicate license, or requiring a special equipment that has already passed inspection in another place pursuant to this Law to undergo duplicate inspection;  
(8) recommending, supervising the production, or supervising the sale of special equipment;  
(9) disclosing trade secrets learned in the course of performing duties;  
(10) receiving a special equipment accident report but failing to immediately report to the people's government at the same level and report it in accordance with regulations;  
(11) delaying reporting, omitting reporting, making false reports, or concealing an accident;  
(12) obstructing accident rescue or accident investigation and handling;  
(13) other acts of abusing powers, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，特种设备生产、经营、使用单位或者检验、检测机构拒不接受负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门依法实施的监督检查的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，责令停产停业整顿，处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款。  
特种设备生产、经营、使用单位擅自动用、调换、转移、损毁被查封、扣押的特种设备或者其主要部件的，责令改正，处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销生产许可证，注销特种设备使用登记证书。

**Article 95** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a special equipment production, operation, or use entity or an inspection or testing institution refuses to accept supervision inspections carried out by the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety according to law, it shall be ordered to correct within a specified period; if it fails to correct within the specified period, it shall be ordered to stop production for rectification, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a special equipment production, operation, or use entity, without authorization, uses, exchanges, transfers, or damages a sealed-up or seized special equipment or its main components, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the production license shall be revoked and the special equipment use registration certificate shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　违反本法规定，被依法吊销许可证的，自吊销许可证之日起三年内，负责特种设备安全监督管理的部门不予受理其新的许可申请。

**Article 96** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a license is revoked according to law, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of special equipment safety shall not accept a new license application from the entity within three years from the date of revocation of the license.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　违反本法规定，造成人身、财产损害的，依法承担民事责任。  
违反本法规定，应当承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金，其财产不足以同时支付时，先承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 97** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, personal or property damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an entity is liable for civil compensation and also liable for fines or penal fines, and its property is insufficient to pay all concurrently, it shall first bear civil compensation liability.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 98** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an act constitutes a violation of public security administration, public security administration punishment shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　特种设备行政许可、检验的收费，依照法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 99** The fees for special equipment administrative licensing and inspection shall be charged in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　军事装备、核设施、航空航天器使用的特种设备安全的监督管理不适用本法。  
铁路机车、海上设施和船舶、矿山井下使用的特种设备以及民用机场专用设备安全的监督管理，房屋建筑工地、市政工程工地用起重机械和场（厂）内专用机动车辆的安装、使用的监督管理，由有关部门依照本法和其他有关法律的规定实施。

**Article 100** This Law shall not apply to the supervision and administration of special equipment safety used in military equipment, nuclear facilities, or aerospace vehicles.  
The supervision and administration of the safety of special equipment used in railway locomotives, offshore facilities and ships, mining underground equipment, and special equipment for civil airports, and the installation and use of hoisting machinery and special-purpose motor vehicles in factories (premises) at construction sites of buildings and municipal engineering works, shall be implemented by relevant departments in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　本法自2014年1月1日起施行。

**Article 101** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2014.
