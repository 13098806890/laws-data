# 中华人民共和国公共图书馆法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-26  
**生效日期**：2018-10-26

> （2017年11月4日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十次会议通过　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　设　　立 第三章　运　　行 第四章　服　　务 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了促进公共图书馆事业发展，发挥公共图书馆功能，保障公民基本文化权益，提高公民科学文化素质和社会文明程度，传承人类文明，坚定文化自信，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称公共图书馆，是指向社会公众免费开放，收集、整理、保存文献信息并提供查询、借阅及相关服务，开展社会教育的公共文化设施。 前款规定的文献信息包括图书报刊、音像制品、缩微制品、数字资源等。 第三条　公共图书馆是社会主义公共文化服务体系的重要组成部分，应当将推动、引导、服务全民阅读作为重要任务。 公共图书馆应当坚持社会主义先进文化前进方向，坚持以人民为中心，坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领，传承发展中华优秀传统文化，继承革命文化，发展社会主义先进文化。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将公共图书馆事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，将公共图书馆建设纳入城乡规划和土地利用总体规划，加大对政府设立的公共图书馆的投入，将所需经费列入本级政府预算，并及时、足额拨付。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进公共图书馆事业发展，发挥公共图书馆功能，保障公民基本文化权益，提高公民科学文化素质和社会文明程度，传承人类文明，坚定文化自信，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of promoting the development of public libraries, giving full play to the functions of public libraries, protecting the basic cultural rights and interests of citizens, improving the scientific and cultural quality of citizens and the degree of social civility, inheriting human civilization, and strengthening cultural confidence.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称公共图书馆，是指向社会公众免费开放，收集、整理、保存文献信息并提供查询、借阅及相关服务，开展社会教育的公共文化设施。  
前款规定的文献信息包括图书报刊、音像制品、缩微制品、数字资源等。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "public library" means a public cultural facility that is open to the public free of charge, collects, organizes, preserves document information, provides inquiry, borrowing and related services, and carries out social education.  
The document information mentioned in the preceding paragraph includes books, newspapers and periodicals, audio-visual products, microform products, digital resources, and the like.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公共图书馆是社会主义公共文化服务体系的重要组成部分，应当将推动、引导、服务全民阅读作为重要任务。  
公共图书馆应当坚持社会主义先进文化前进方向，坚持以人民为中心，坚持以社会主义核心价值观为引领，传承发展中华优秀传统文化，继承革命文化，发展社会主义先进文化。

**Article 3** A public library is an important component of the socialist public cultural service system, and shall take promoting, guiding, and serving nationwide reading as an important task.  
A public library shall adhere to the direction of advanced socialist culture, adhere to a people-centered approach, adhere to the guidance of socialist core values, inherit and develop the excellent traditional Chinese culture, inherit the revolutionary culture, and develop advanced socialist culture.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将公共图书馆事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，将公共图书馆建设纳入城乡规划和土地利用总体规划，加大对政府设立的公共图书馆的投入，将所需经费列入本级政府预算，并及时、足额拨付。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织自筹资金设立公共图书馆。县级以上人民政府应当积极调动社会力量参与公共图书馆建设，并按照国家有关规定给予政策扶持。

**Article 4** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the public library cause into the national economic and social development plan at the corresponding level, incorporate the construction of public libraries into the urban and rural planning and the overall plan for land use, increase investment in public libraries established by the government, include the necessary funding in the government budget at the corresponding level, and allocate the funds in a timely and full manner.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to raise funds on their own to establish public libraries. The people's governments at or above the county level shall actively mobilize social forces to participate in the construction of public libraries, and provide policy support in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院文化主管部门负责全国公共图书馆的管理工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责与公共图书馆管理有关的工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门负责本行政区域内公共图书馆的管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责本行政区域内与公共图书馆管理有关的工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of culture of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of public libraries nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the work related to the administration of public libraries within the scope of their respective duties.  
The competent departments of culture of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of public libraries within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work related to the administration of public libraries within their respective administrative regions and within the scope of their respective duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法向公共图书馆捐赠，并依法给予税收优惠。  
境外自然人、法人和其他组织可以依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，通过捐赠方式参与境内公共图书馆建设。

**Article 6** The State encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to make donations to public libraries according to law, and grants tax preferences according to law.  
Overseas natural persons, legal persons, and other organizations may participate in the construction of public libraries within the territory through donations in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家扶持革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区和贫困地区公共图书馆事业的发展。

**Article 7** The State supports the development of public libraries in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持发挥科技在公共图书馆建设、管理和服务中的作用，推动运用现代信息技术和传播技术，提高公共图书馆的服务效能。

**Article 8** The State encourages and supports giving play to the role of science and technology in the construction, management, and service of public libraries, and promotes the use of modern information technology and communication technology to improve the service efficiency of public libraries.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持在公共图书馆领域开展国际交流与合作。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports international exchanges and cooperation in the field of public libraries.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公共图书馆应当遵守有关知识产权保护的法律、行政法规规定，依法保护和使用文献信息。  
馆藏文献信息属于文物、档案或者国家秘密的，公共图书馆应当遵守有关文物保护、档案管理或者保守国家秘密的法律、行政法规规定。

**Article 10** A public library shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the protection of intellectual property rights, and protect and use document information according to law.  
Where the document information in the collection falls under cultural relics, archives, or State secrets, the public library shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the protection of cultural relics, the administration of archives, or the protection of State secrets.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公共图书馆行业组织应当依法制定行业规范，加强行业自律，维护会员合法权益，指导、督促会员提高服务质量。

**Article 11** Trade organizations of public libraries shall formulate industry norms according to law, strengthen industry self-discipline, protect the lawful rights and interests of their members, and guide and urge their members to improve the quality of services.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在公共图书馆事业发展中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 12** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of the public library cause shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　设立

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家建立覆盖城乡、便捷实用的公共图书馆服务网络。公共图书馆服务网络建设坚持政府主导，鼓励社会参与。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域内人口数量、人口分布、环境和交通条件等因素，因地制宜确定公共图书馆的数量、规模、结构和分布，加强固定馆舍和流动服务设施、自助服务设施建设。

**Article 13** The State establishes a public library service network covering urban and rural areas that is convenient and practical. The construction of the public library service network shall adhere to government leadership and encourage social participation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on factors such as the population size, population distribution, environment, and traffic conditions within their respective administrative regions, determine the number, scale, structure, and distribution of public libraries according to local conditions, and strengthen the construction of fixed library buildings, mobile service facilities, and self-service facilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府应当设立公共图书馆。  
地方人民政府应当充分利用乡镇（街道）和村（社区）的综合服务设施设立图书室，服务城乡居民。

**Article 14** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish public libraries.  
Local people's governments shall make full use of the comprehensive service facilities of townships (sub-districts) and villages (communities) to set up library rooms to serve urban and rural residents.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　设立公共图书馆应当具备下列条件：  
（一）章程；  
（二）固定的馆址；  
（三）与其功能相适应的馆舍面积、阅览座席、文献信息和设施设备；  
（四）与其功能、馆藏规模等相适应的工作人员；  
（五）必要的办馆资金和稳定的运行经费来源；  
（六）安全保障设施、制度及应急预案。

**Article 15** The establishment of a public library shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having articles of association;  
(2) Having a fixed library address;  
(3) Having a floor area, reading seats, document information, and facilities and equipment commensurate with its functions;  
(4) Having staff commensurate with its functions, collection size, and other factors;  
(5) Having necessary funds for running the library and stable sources of operating funds; and  
(6) Having safety and security facilities, systems, and emergency response plans.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公共图书馆章程应当包括名称、馆址、办馆宗旨、业务范围、管理制度及有关规则、终止程序和剩余财产的处理方案等事项。

**Article 16** The articles of association of a public library shall include matters such as its name, address, purpose of running the library, scope of business, management systems and relevant rules, termination procedures, and the plan for disposing of remaining property.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公共图书馆的设立、变更、终止应当按照国家有关规定办理登记手续。

**Article 17** The establishment, modification, or termination of a public library shall go through registration formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门应当在其网站上及时公布本行政区域内公共图书馆的名称、馆址、联系方式、馆藏文献信息概况、主要服务内容和方式等信息。

**Article 18** The competent departments of culture of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall promptly publish on their websites information such as the names, addresses, contact information, overview of collected document information, main service contents, and methods of public libraries within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　政府设立的公共图书馆馆长应当具备相应的文化水平、专业知识和组织管理能力。  
公共图书馆应当根据其功能、馆藏规模、馆舍面积、服务范围及服务人口等因素配备相应的工作人员。公共图书馆工作人员应当具备相应的专业知识与技能，其中专业技术人员可以按照国家有关规定评定专业技术职称。

**Article 19** The director of a public library established by the government shall have the corresponding cultural level, professional knowledge, and organizational management capabilities.  
A public library shall be staffed with corresponding personnel based on factors such as its functions, collection size, floor area, service scope, and population served. Staff members of a public library shall have the corresponding professional knowledge and skills, among whom professional and technical personnel may be evaluated for professional and technical titles in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公共图书馆可以以捐赠者姓名、名称命名文献信息专藏或者专题活动。  
公民、法人和其他组织设立的公共图书馆，可以以捐赠者的姓名、名称命名公共图书馆、公共图书馆馆舍或者其他设施。  
以捐赠者姓名、名称命名应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定，符合国家利益和社会公共利益，遵循公序良俗。

**Article 20** A public library may name a dedicated collection of document information or a thematic activity after the name of a donor.  
A public library established by citizens, legal persons, or other organizations may name the public library, its buildings, or other facilities after the name of a donor.  
Naming after the name of a donor shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be in the national interest and social public interest, and follow public order and good customs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公共图书馆终止的，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理其剩余财产。

**Article 21** Upon the termination of a public library, its remaining property shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家设立国家图书馆，主要承担国家文献信息战略保存、国家书目和联合目录编制、为国家立法和决策服务、组织全国古籍保护、开展图书馆发展研究和国际交流、为其他图书馆提供业务指导和技术支持等职能。国家图书馆同时具有本法规定的公共图书馆的功能。

**Article 22** The State establishes the National Library, which mainly undertakes functions such as the strategic preservation of national document information, compilation of the national bibliography and union catalogues, serving national legislation and decision-making, organizing the national protection of ancient books, conducting research on library development and international exchanges, and providing professional guidance and technical support to other libraries. The National Library also has the functions of a public library as stipulated in this Law.

### 第三章　运行

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家推动公共图书馆建立健全法人治理结构，吸收有关方面代表、专业人士和社会公众参与管理。

**Article 23** The State promotes public libraries to establish and improve a legal person governance structure, and to involve representatives from relevant sectors, professionals, and the public in management.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公共图书馆应当根据办馆宗旨和服务对象的需求，广泛收集文献信息；政府设立的公共图书馆还应当系统收集地方文献信息，保存和传承地方文化。  
文献信息的收集应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 24** A public library shall extensively collect document information in accordance with its purpose of running the library and the needs of its service targets; a public library established by the government shall also systematically collect local document information to preserve and inherit local culture.  
The collection of document information shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公共图书馆可以通过采购、接受交存或者捐赠等合法方式收集文献信息。

**Article 25** A public library may collect document information through lawful means such as procurement, acceptance of deposits or donations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　出版单位应当按照国家有关规定向国家图书馆和所在地省级公共图书馆交存正式出版物。

**Article 26** A publishing entity shall deposit official publications with the National Library and the provincial-level public library at its location in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公共图书馆应当按照国家公布的标准、规范对馆藏文献信息进行整理，建立馆藏文献信息目录，并依法通过其网站或者其他方式向社会公开。

**Article 27** A public library shall organize the document information in its collection in accordance with the standards and norms published by the State, establish a catalogue of collected document information, and make it public through its website or other means according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公共图书馆应当妥善保存馆藏文献信息，不得随意处置；确需处置的，应当遵守国务院文化主管部门有关处置文献信息的规定。  
公共图书馆应当配备防火、防盗等设施，并按照国家有关规定和标准对古籍和其他珍贵、易损文献信息采取专门的保护措施，确保安全。

**Article 28** A public library shall properly preserve the document information in its collection and shall not dispose of it at will; where disposal is truly necessary, it shall comply with the provisions of the competent department of culture of the State Council on the disposal of document information.  
A public library shall be equipped with fire prevention, anti-theft, and other facilities, and shall take special protection measures for ancient books and other precious and fragile document information in accordance with the relevant provisions and standards of the State to ensure safety.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公共图书馆应当定期对其设施设备进行检查维护，确保正常运行。  
公共图书馆的设施设备场地不得用于与其服务无关的商业经营活动。

**Article 29** A public library shall regularly inspect and maintain its facilities and equipment to ensure normal operation.  
The facilities, equipment, and premises of a public library shall not be used for commercial activities unrelated to its services.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公共图书馆应当加强馆际交流与合作。国家支持公共图书馆开展联合采购、联合编目、联合服务，实现文献信息的共建共享，促进文献信息的有效利用。

**Article 30** A public library shall strengthen inter-library exchanges and cooperation. The State supports public libraries in carrying out joint procurement, joint cataloguing, and joint services to achieve the co-construction and sharing of document information, and to promote the effective use of document information.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级人民政府应当因地制宜建立符合当地特点的以县级公共图书馆为总馆，乡镇（街道）综合文化站、村（社区）图书室等为分馆或者基层服务点的总分馆制，完善数字化、网络化服务体系和配送体系，实现通借通还，促进公共图书馆服务向城乡基层延伸。总馆应当加强对分馆和基层服务点的业务指导。

**Article 31** The people's governments at the county level shall, according to local conditions, establish a central-branch library system suited to local characteristics, with the county-level public library as the central library and the comprehensive cultural stations of townships (sub-districts), library rooms of villages (communities), etc. as branch libraries or grassroots service points; improve the digital and networked service system and the distribution system; realize inter-library borrowing and returning; and promote the extension of public library services to the grassroots in urban and rural areas. The central library shall strengthen professional guidance to branch libraries and grassroots service points.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公共图书馆馆藏文献信息属于档案、文物的，公共图书馆可以与档案馆、博物馆、纪念馆等单位相互交换重复件、复制件或者目录，联合举办展览，共同编辑出版有关史料或者进行史料研究。

**Article 32** Where the document information in the collection of a public library falls under archives or cultural relics, the public library may exchange duplicates, copies, or catalogues with archives, museums, memorial halls, and other units, jointly hold exhibitions, and jointly compile, publish or conduct research on relevant historical materials.

### 第四章　服务

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　公共图书馆应当按照平等、开放、共享的要求向社会公众提供服务。  
公共图书馆应当免费向社会公众提供下列服务：  
（一）文献信息查询、借阅；  
（二）阅览室、自习室等公共空间设施场地开放；  
（三）公益性讲座、阅读推广、培训、展览；  
（四）国家规定的其他免费服务项目。

**Article 33** A public library shall provide services to the public in accordance with the requirements of equality, openness, and sharing.  
A public library shall provide the following services to the public free of charge:  
(1) Inquiry and borrowing of document information;  
(2) Opening of public space facilities such as reading rooms and self-study rooms;  
(3) Public-interest lectures, reading promotion, training, and exhibitions; and  
(4) Other free service items prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　政府设立的公共图书馆应当设置少年儿童阅览区域，根据少年儿童的特点配备相应的专业人员，开展面向少年儿童的阅读指导和社会教育活动，并为学校开展有关课外活动提供支持。有条件的地区可以单独设立少年儿童图书馆。  
政府设立的公共图书馆应当考虑老年人、残疾人等群体的特点，积极创造条件，提供适合其需要的文献信息、无障碍设施设备和服务等。

**Article 34** A public library established by the government shall set up a reading area for children and adolescents, equip corresponding professional personnel according to the characteristics of children and adolescents, carry out reading guidance and social education activities for children and adolescents, and provide support for schools to carry out relevant extracurricular activities. Regions with the conditions may set up separate children's libraries.  
A public library established by the government shall consider the characteristics of groups such as the elderly and persons with disabilities, actively create conditions, and provide document information, barrier-free facilities and equipment, services, etc. suitable for their needs.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　政府设立的公共图书馆应当根据自身条件，为国家机关制定法律、法规、政策和开展有关问题研究，提供文献信息和相关咨询服务。

**Article 35** A public library established by the government shall, based on its own conditions, provide document information and relevant consulting services for State organs in formulating laws, regulations, and policies and conducting research on related issues.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公共图书馆应当通过开展阅读指导、读书交流、演讲诵读、图书互换共享等活动，推广全民阅读。

**Article 36** A public library shall promote nationwide reading by carrying out activities such as reading guidance, book exchange, speech recitation, and book swapping and sharing.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公共图书馆向社会公众提供文献信息，应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得向未成年人提供内容不适宜的文献信息。  
公共图书馆不得从事或者允许其他组织、个人在馆内从事危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益和其他违反法律法规的活动。

**Article 37** A public library, when providing document information to the public, shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, and shall not provide document information with inappropriate content to minors.  
A public library shall not engage in, or allow other organizations or individuals to engage in, on its premises, activities that endanger national security, harm the public interest, or otherwise violate laws and regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　公共图书馆应当通过其网站或者其他方式向社会公告本馆的服务内容、开放时间、借阅规则等；因故闭馆或者更改开放时间的，除遇不可抗力外，应当提前公告。  
公共图书馆在公休日应当开放，在国家法定节假日应当有开放时间。

**Article 38** A public library shall announce to the public through its website or other means its service contents, opening hours, borrowing rules, and the like; if it closes or changes its opening hours for some reason, it shall announce the change in advance, except in the case of force majeure.  
A public library shall be open on public holidays and shall have opening hours on statutory holidays.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　政府设立的公共图书馆应当通过流动服务设施、自助服务设施等为社会公众提供便捷服务。

**Article 39** A public library established by the government shall provide convenient services to the public through mobile service facilities, self-service facilities, and the like.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家构建标准统一、互联互通的公共图书馆数字服务网络，支持数字阅读产品开发和数字资源保存技术研究，推动公共图书馆利用数字化、网络化技术向社会公众提供便捷服务。  
政府设立的公共图书馆应当加强数字资源建设、配备相应的设施设备，建立线上线下相结合的文献信息共享平台，为社会公众提供优质服务。

**Article 40** The State establishes a unified standard and interconnected public library digital service network, supports the development of digital reading products and research on digital resource preservation technologies, and promotes public libraries to use digital and networked technologies to provide convenient services to the public.  
A public library established by the government shall strengthen the construction of digital resources, equip corresponding facilities and equipment, and establish a document information sharing platform combining online and offline services to provide quality services to the public.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　政府设立的公共图书馆应当加强馆内古籍的保护，根据自身条件采用数字化、影印或者缩微技术等推进古籍的整理、出版和研究利用，并通过巡回展览、公益性讲座、善本再造、创意产品开发等方式，加强古籍宣传，传承发展中华优秀传统文化。

**Article 41** A public library established by the government shall strengthen the protection of ancient books in its collection, adopt digitalization, photocopying, or microform technologies, etc. according to its own conditions to promote the collation, publication, research, and utilization of ancient books, and strengthen the promotion of ancient books and inherit and develop the excellent traditional Chinese culture through mobile exhibitions, public-interest lectures, reproduction of rare editions, creative product development, and other means.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　公共图书馆应当改善服务条件、提高服务水平，定期公告服务开展情况，听取读者意见，建立投诉渠道，完善反馈机制，接受社会监督。

**Article 42** A public library shall improve service conditions and service levels, regularly announce the service development situation, listen to readers' opinions, establish complaint channels, improve feedback mechanisms, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　公共图书馆应当妥善保护读者的个人信息、借阅信息以及其他可能涉及读者隐私的信息，不得出售或者以其他方式非法向他人提供。

**Article 43** A public library shall properly protect readers' personal information, borrowing information, and other information that may involve readers' privacy, and shall not sell or otherwise illegally provide such information to others.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　读者应当遵守公共图书馆的相关规定，自觉维护公共图书馆秩序，爱护公共图书馆的文献信息、设施设备，合法利用文献信息；借阅文献信息的，应当按照规定时限归还。  
对破坏公共图书馆文献信息、设施设备，或者扰乱公共图书馆秩序的，公共图书馆工作人员有权予以劝阻、制止；经劝阻、制止无效的，公共图书馆可以停止为其提供服务。

**Article 44** Readers shall comply with the relevant provisions of public libraries, consciously maintain the order of public libraries, take care of the document information, facilities, and equipment of public libraries, and use document information lawfully; those who borrow document information shall return it within the prescribed time limit.  
If a reader damages the document information, facilities, or equipment of a public library, or disrupts the order of a public library, the staff of the public library shall have the right to dissuade and stop such conduct; if the dissuasion or stopping is ineffective, the public library may cease to provide services to such person.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家采取政府购买服务等措施，对公民、法人和其他组织设立的公共图书馆提供服务给予扶持。

**Article 45** The State adopts measures such as government procurement of services to support the provision of services by public libraries established by citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家鼓励公民参与公共图书馆志愿服务。县级以上人民政府文化主管部门应当对公共图书馆志愿服务给予必要的指导和支持。

**Article 46** The State encourages citizens to participate in volunteer services for public libraries. The competent departments of culture of the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary guidance and support for volunteer services in public libraries.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院文化主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门应当制定公共图书馆服务规范，对公共图书馆的服务质量和水平进行考核。考核应当吸收社会公众参与。考核结果应当向社会公布，并作为对公共图书馆给予补贴或者奖励等的依据。

**Article 47** The competent department of culture of the State Council and the competent departments of culture of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate public library service standards, and assess the service quality and level of public libraries. The assessment shall involve the participation of the public. The assessment results shall be made public and shall serve as the basis for granting subsidies or rewards to public libraries.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家支持公共图书馆加强与学校图书馆、科研机构图书馆以及其他类型图书馆的交流与合作，开展联合服务。  
国家支持学校图书馆、科研机构图书馆以及其他类型图书馆向社会公众开放。

**Article 48** The State supports public libraries in strengthening exchanges and cooperation with school libraries, scientific research institution libraries, and other types of libraries, and in carrying out joint services.  
The State supports school libraries, scientific research institution libraries, and other types of libraries in opening to the public.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公共图书馆从事或者允许其他组织、个人在馆内从事危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益活动的，由文化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得；情节严重的，可以责令停业整顿、关闭；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究法律责任。

**Article 49** If a public library engages in, or allows other organizations or individuals to engage in, on its premises, activities that endanger national security or harm the public interest, the competent department of culture shall order it to make corrections and confiscate its illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, it may order it to suspend business for rectification or close down; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible personnel shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　公共图书馆及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由文化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得：  
（一）违规处置文献信息；  
（二）出售或者以其他方式非法向他人提供读者的个人信息、借阅信息以及其他可能涉及读者隐私的信息；  
（三）向社会公众提供文献信息违反有关法律、行政法规的规定，或者向未成年人提供内容不适宜的文献信息；  
（四）将设施设备场地用于与公共图书馆服务无关的商业经营活动；  
（五）其他不履行本法规定的公共图书馆服务要求的行为。  
公共图书馆及其工作人员对应当免费提供的服务收费或者变相收费的，由价格主管部门依照前款规定给予处罚。  
公共图书馆及其工作人员有前两款规定行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究法律责任。

**Article 50** If a public library or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts, the competent department of culture shall order it to make corrections and confiscate its illegal gains:  
(1) Disposing of document information in violation of regulations;  
(2) Selling or otherwise illegally providing to others readers' personal information, borrowing information, or other information that may involve readers' privacy;  
(3) Providing document information to the public in violation of the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, or providing document information with inappropriate content to minors;  
(4) Using facilities, equipment, or premises for commercial activities unrelated to public library services; or  
(5) Other acts of failing to fulfill the public library service requirements stipulated in this Law.  
If a public library or any of its staff members charges fees or imposes disguised charges for services that shall be provided free of charge, the competent department of price shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
If a public library or any of its staff members commits any act prescribed in the preceding two paragraphs, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible personnel shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　出版单位未按照国家有关规定交存正式出版物的，由出版主管部门依照有关出版管理的法律、行政法规规定给予处罚。

**Article 51** A publishing unit that fails to deposit official publications in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State shall be punished by the publishing regulatory authority in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on publishing management.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　文化主管部门或者其他有关部门及其工作人员在公共图书馆管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 52** Where the competent cultural department or other relevant departments and their staff abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractices for personal gain in the management of public libraries, the persons directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　损坏公共图书馆的文献信息、设施设备或者未按照规定时限归还所借文献信息，造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 53** Where a person damages the documents and information, facilities and equipment of a public library, or fails to return borrowed documents and information within the prescribed time limit, thereby causing property losses or other damages, he or she shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本法自2018年1月1日起施行。

**Article 55** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2018.
