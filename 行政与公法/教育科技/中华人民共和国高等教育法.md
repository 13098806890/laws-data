# 中华人民共和国高等教育法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-12-29  
**生效日期**：2018-12-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展高等教育事业，实施科教兴国战略，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，根据宪法和教育法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution and the Education Law for the purpose of developing higher education, implementing the strategy of rejuvenating the country through science and education, and promoting socialist material and cultural progress.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事高等教育活动，适用本法。  
本法所称高等教育，是指在完成高级中等教育基础上实施的教育。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities of higher education conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purpose of this Law, higher education refers to education provided on the basis of completion of senior secondary education.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论为指导，遵循宪法确定的基本原则，发展社会主义的高等教育事业。

**Article 3** The State shall adhere to the guidance of Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought and Deng Xiaoping Theory, follow the basic principles established by the Constitution, and develop socialist higher education.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　高等教育必须贯彻国家的教育方针，为社会主义现代化建设服务、为人民服务，与生产劳动和社会实践相结合，使受教育者成为德、智、体、美等方面全面发展的社会主义建设者和接班人。

**Article 4** Higher education must implement the State's educational policy, serve socialist modernization and the people, and combine with productive labor and social practice, so as to cultivate the educated to become socialist builders and successors who are well developed morally, intellectually, physically, and aesthetically.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　高等教育的任务是培养具有社会责任感、创新精神和实践能力的高级专门人才，发展科学技术文化，促进社会主义现代化建设。

**Article 5** The task of higher education is to cultivate high-level specialized personnel with a sense of social responsibility, innovative spirit and practical ability, to develop science, technology and culture, and to promote socialist modernization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家根据经济建设和社会发展的需要，制定高等教育发展规划，举办高等学校，并采取多种形式积极发展高等教育事业。  
国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织和公民等社会力量依法举办高等学校，参与和支持高等教育事业的改革和发展。

**Article 6** The State shall formulate plans for the development of higher education, establish higher education institutions, and actively develop higher education in various forms according to the needs of economic construction and social development.  
The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, social groups, other social organizations and citizens to establish higher education institutions according to law, and to participate in and support the reform and development of higher education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家按照社会主义现代化建设和发展社会主义市场经济的需要，根据不同类型、不同层次高等学校的实际，推进高等教育体制改革和高等教育教学改革，优化高等教育结构和资源配置，提高高等教育的质量和效益。

**Article 7** The State shall, in accordance with the needs of socialist modernization and the development of a socialist market economy, and based on the actual conditions of different types and levels of higher education institutions, promote the reform of the higher education system and the reform of higher education teaching, optimize the structure and resource allocation of higher education, and improve the quality and efficiency of higher education.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家根据少数民族的特点和需要，帮助和支持少数民族地区发展高等教育事业，为少数民族培养高级专门人才。

**Article 8** The State shall, in light of the characteristics and needs of ethnic minorities, assist and support the development of higher education in ethnic minority areas, and cultivate high-level specialized personnel for ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公民依法享有接受高等教育的权利。  
国家采取措施，帮助少数民族学生和经济困难的学生接受高等教育。  
高等学校必须招收符合国家规定的录取标准的残疾学生入学，不得因其残疾而拒绝招收。

**Article 9** Citizens shall enjoy the right to receive higher education according to law.  
The State shall take measures to assist students from ethnic minorities and students with financial difficulties in receiving higher education.  
Higher education institutions must admit disabled students who meet the admission standards prescribed by the State, and shall not refuse admission on account of their disability.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家依法保障高等学校中的科学研究、文学艺术创作和其他文化活动的自由。  
在高等学校中从事科学研究、文学艺术创作和其他文化活动，应当遵守法律。

**Article 10** The State shall protect, according to law, the freedom of scientific research, literary and artistic creation, and other cultural activities in higher education institutions.  
Those who engage in scientific research, literary and artistic creation and other cultural activities in higher education institutions shall abide by the law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　高等学校应当面向社会，依法自主办学，实行民主管理。

**Article 11** Higher education institutions shall face society, run independently according to law, and practice democratic management.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励高等学校之间、高等学校与科学研究机构以及企业事业组织之间开展协作，实行优势互补，提高教育资源的使用效益。  
国家鼓励和支持高等教育事业的国际交流与合作。

**Article 12** The State encourages cooperation among higher education institutions, and between higher education institutions and scientific research institutions, enterprises and public institutions, so as to complement each other's advantages and improve the utilization efficiency of educational resources.  
The State encourages and supports international exchanges and cooperation in higher education.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院统一领导和管理全国高等教育事业。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府统筹协调本行政区域内的高等教育事业，管理主要为地方培养人才和国务院授权管理的高等学校。

**Article 13** The State Council shall exercise unified leadership and administration over the national higher education.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall coordinate the higher education within their respective administrative regions and administer higher education institutions mainly for cultivating local talents and those authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院教育行政部门主管全国高等教育工作，管理由国务院确定的主要为全国培养人才的高等学校。国务院其他有关部门在国务院规定的职责范围内，负责有关的高等教育工作。

**Article 14** The education administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the national higher education work and administer the higher education institutions determined by the State Council to mainly cultivate talents for the whole country. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for related higher education work within the scope of their duties prescribed by the State Council.

### 第二章　高等教育基本制度

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　高等教育包括学历教育和非学历教育。  
高等教育采用全日制和非全日制教育形式。  
国家支持采用广播、电视、函授及其他远程教育方式实施高等教育。

**Article 15** Higher education includes degree-conferring education and non-degree education.  
Higher education shall adopt full-time and part-time forms.  
The State supports the implementation of higher education through radio, television, correspondence and other distance education methods.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　高等学历教育分为专科教育、本科教育和研究生教育。  
高等学历教育应当符合下列学业标准：  
（一）专科教育应当使学生掌握本专业必备的基础理论、专门知识，具有从事本专业实际工作的基本技能和初步能力；  
（二）本科教育应当使学生比较系统地掌握本学科、专业必需的基础理论、基本知识，掌握本专业必要的基本技能、方法和相关知识，具有从事本专业实际工作和研究工作的初步能力；  
（三）硕士研究生教育应当使学生掌握本学科坚实的基础理论、系统的专业知识，掌握相应的技能、方法和相关知识，具有从事本专业实际工作和科学研究工作的能力。博士研究生教育应当使学生掌握本学科坚实宽广的基础理论、系统深入的专业知识、相应的技能和方法，具有独立从事本学科创造性科学研究工作和实际工作的能力。

**Article 16** Higher degree-conferring education shall be divided into specialized (short-cycle) education, undergraduate education and graduate education.  
Higher degree-conferring education shall meet the following academic standards:  
(1) Specialized education shall enable students to master the basic theories and specialized knowledge necessary for the specialty, and to possess the basic skills and preliminary ability to engage in practical work in the specialty;  
(2) Undergraduate education shall enable students to systematically master the basic theories and basic knowledge necessary for the discipline and specialty, to master the necessary basic skills, methods and related knowledge of the specialty, and to possess the preliminary ability to engage in practical work and research work in the specialty;  
(3) Master's degree education shall enable students to master solid basic theories and systematic professional knowledge of the discipline, to master corresponding skills, methods and related knowledge, and to possess the ability to engage in practical work and scientific research in the specialty. Doctoral degree education shall enable students to master solid and broad basic theories, systematic and in-depth professional knowledge, corresponding skills and methods of the discipline, and to possess the ability to independently engage in creative scientific research and practical work in the discipline.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　专科教育的基本修业年限为二至三年，本科教育的基本修业年限为四至五年，硕士研究生教育的基本修业年限为二至三年，博士研究生教育的基本修业年限为三至四年。非全日制高等学历教育的修业年限应当适当延长。高等学校根据实际需要，可以对本学校的修业年限作出调整。

**Article 17** The basic length of schooling for specialized education shall be two to three years, for undergraduate education four to five years, for master's degree education two to three years, and for doctoral degree education three to four years. The length of schooling for part-time higher degree-conferring education shall be appropriately extended. Higher education institutions may adjust their length of schooling according to actual needs.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　高等教育由高等学校和其他高等教育机构实施。  
大学、独立设置的学院主要实施本科及本科以上教育。高等专科学校实施专科教育。经国务院教育行政部门批准，科学研究机构可以承担研究生教育的任务。  
其他高等教育机构实施非学历高等教育。

**Article 18** Higher education shall be provided by higher education institutions and other institutions of higher learning.  
Universities and independently established colleges shall mainly provide undergraduate and above-level education. Higher specialized schools shall provide specialized education. With the approval of the education administrative department of the State Council, scientific research institutions may undertake the task of graduate education.  
Other institutions of higher learning shall provide non-degree higher education.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　高级中等教育毕业或者具有同等学力的，经考试合格，由实施相应学历教育的高等学校录取，取得专科生或者本科生入学资格。  
本科毕业或者具有同等学力的，经考试合格，由实施相应学历教育的高等学校或者经批准承担研究生教育任务的科学研究机构录取，取得硕士研究生入学资格。  
硕士研究生毕业或者具有同等学力的，经考试合格，由实施相应学历教育的高等学校或者经批准承担研究生教育任务的科学研究机构录取，取得博士研究生入学资格。  
允许特定学科和专业的本科毕业生直接取得博士研究生入学资格，具体办法由国务院教育行政部门规定。

**Article 19** Persons who have graduated from senior secondary education or have equivalent educational level, upon passing the examinations, may be admitted by higher education institutions providing corresponding degree-conferring education, and shall obtain the qualification for admission as specialized (short-cycle) or undergraduate students.  
Persons who have graduated from undergraduate education or have equivalent educational level, upon passing the examinations, may be admitted by higher education institutions providing corresponding degree-conferring education or by scientific research institutions approved to undertake graduate education tasks, and shall obtain the qualification for admission as master's degree students.  
Persons who have graduated from master's degree education or have equivalent educational level, upon passing the examinations, may be admitted by higher education institutions providing corresponding degree-conferring education or by scientific research institutions approved to undertake graduate education tasks, and shall obtain the qualification for admission as doctoral degree students.  
Graduates from certain disciplines and specialties may be directly admitted to doctoral degree programs as prescribed. The specific measures shall be formulated by the education administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　接受高等学历教育的学生，由所在高等学校或者经批准承担研究生教育任务的科学研究机构根据其修业年限、学业成绩等，按照国家有关规定，发给相应的学历证书或者其他学业证书。  
接受非学历高等教育的学生，由所在高等学校或者其他高等教育机构发给相应的结业证书。结业证书应当载明修业年限和学业内容。

**Article 20** Students receiving higher degree-conferring education shall, by the higher education institution or the scientific research institution approved to undertake graduate education tasks where they study, based on their length of schooling and academic performance, and in accordance with relevant State regulations, be issued corresponding degree certificates or other academic certificates.  
Students receiving non-degree higher education shall, by the higher education institution or other institution of higher learning where they study, be issued corresponding completion certificates. The completion certificate shall state the length of schooling and the content of study.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家实行高等教育自学考试制度，经考试合格的，发给相应的学历证书或者其他学业证书。

**Article 21** The State shall implement a system of higher education self-study examinations. Persons who pass the examinations shall be issued corresponding degree certificates or other academic certificates.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家实行学位制度。学位分为学士、硕士和博士。  
公民通过接受高等教育或者自学，其学业水平达到国家规定的学位标准，可以向学位授予单位申请授予相应的学位。

**Article 22** The State shall implement a system of academic degrees. Degrees shall be divided into bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees.  
Citizens who, through receiving higher education or self-study, attain the academic level meeting the State-prescribed degree standards may apply to the degree-conferring unit for the conferment of corresponding degrees.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　高等学校和其他高等教育机构应当根据社会需要和自身办学条件，承担实施继续教育的工作。

**Article 23** Higher education institutions and other institutions of higher learning shall, according to social needs and their own conditions for running schools, undertake the work of providing continuing education.

### 第三章　高等学校的设立

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　设立高等学校，应当符合国家高等教育发展规划，符合国家利益和社会公共利益。

**Article 24** The establishment of a higher education institution shall conform to the national plan for higher education development and shall serve the national interests and the public interests of society.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　设立高等学校，应当具备教育法规定的基本条件。  
大学或者独立设置的学院还应当具有较强的教学、科学研究力量，较高的教学、科学研究水平和相应规模，能够实施本科及本科以上教育。大学还必须设有三个以上国家规定的学科门类为主要学科。设立高等学校的具体标准由国务院制定。  
设立其他高等教育机构的具体标准，由国务院授权的有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国务院规定的原则制定。

**Article 25** The establishment of a higher education institution shall meet the basic conditions prescribed by the Education Law.  
A university or an independently established college shall also have relatively strong teaching and scientific research forces, relatively high teaching and scientific research levels and corresponding scale, and be capable of providing undergraduate and above-level education. A university must have no fewer than three disciplines prescribed by the State as its main disciplines. The specific standards for establishing higher education institutions shall be formulated by the State Council.  
The specific standards for establishing other institutions of higher learning shall be formulated by the relevant departments authorized by the State Council or by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government according to the principles prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　设立高等学校，应当根据其层次、类型、所设学科类别、规模、教学和科学研究水平，使用相应的名称。

**Article 26** The establishment of a higher education institution shall use a corresponding name according to its level, type, categories of disciplines offered, scale, and level of teaching and scientific research.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请设立高等学校的，应当向审批机关提交下列材料：  
（一）申办报告；  
（二）可行性论证材料；  
（三）章程；  
（四）审批机关依照本法规定要求提供的其他材料。

**Article 27** An applicant for the establishment of a higher education institution shall submit the following materials to the examining and approving authority:  
(1) A sponsoring report;  
(2) Feasibility study materials;  
(3) The charter;  
(4) Other materials required by the examining and approving authority in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　高等学校的章程应当规定以下事项：  
（一）学校名称、校址；  
（二）办学宗旨；  
（三）办学规模；  
（四）学科门类的设置；  
（五）教育形式；  
（六）内部管理体制；  
（七）经费来源、财产和财务制度；  
（八）举办者与学校之间的权利、义务；  
（九）章程修改程序；  
（十）其他必须由章程规定的事项。

**Article 28** The charter of a higher education institution shall prescribe the following matters:  
(1) The name and location of the institution;  
(2) The aim of running the institution;  
(3) The scale of the institution;  
(4) The setting of disciplines;  
(5) The forms of education;  
(6) The internal management system;  
(7) The sources of funds, property and financial system;  
(8) The rights and obligations between the sponsor and the institution;  
(9) The procedure for amending the charter;  
(10) Other matters that must be prescribed by the charter.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　设立实施本科及以上教育的高等学校，由国务院教育行政部门审批；设立实施专科教育的高等学校，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批，报国务院教育行政部门备案；设立其他高等教育机构，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门审批。审批设立高等学校和其他高等教育机构应当遵守国家有关规定。  
审批设立高等学校，应当委托由专家组成的评议机构评议。  
高等学校和其他高等教育机构分立、合并、终止，变更名称、类别和其他重要事项，由本条第一款规定的审批机关审批；修改章程，应当根据管理权限，报国务院教育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门核准。

**Article 29** The establishment of a higher education institution providing undergraduate and above-level education shall be examined and approved by the education administrative department of the State Council; the establishment of a higher education institution providing specialized education shall be examined and approved by the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and be reported to the education administrative department of the State Council for the record; the establishment of other institutions of higher learning shall be examined and approved by the education administrative department of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. The examination and approval of the establishment of higher education institutions and other institutions of higher learning shall comply with relevant State regulations.  
Where the establishment of a higher education institution is examined and approved, it shall be evaluated by an evaluation body composed of experts.  
The division, merger, termination, change of name, category or other important matters of a higher education institution or other institution of higher learning shall be examined and approved by the examining and approving authority prescribed in the first paragraph of this Article; the amendment of the charter shall be submitted to the education administrative department of the State Council or the education administrative department of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval according to the administration authority.

### 第四章　高等学校的组织和活动

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　高等学校自批准设立之日起取得法人资格。高等学校的校长为高等学校的法定代表人。  
高等学校在民事活动中依法享有民事权利，承担民事责任。

**Article 30** A higher education institution shall obtain the status of a legal person from the date of approval of its establishment. The president of a higher education institution shall be the legal representative of the institution.  
A higher education institution shall enjoy civil rights and bear civil liability according to law in civil activities.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　高等学校应当以培养人才为中心，开展教学、科学研究和社会服务，保证教育教学质量达到国家规定的标准。

**Article 31** Higher education institutions shall take the cultivation of talents as their central task, carry out teaching, scientific research and social services, and ensure that the quality of education and teaching meets the standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　高等学校根据社会需求、办学条件和国家核定的办学规模，制定招生方案，自主调节系科招生比例。

**Article 32** Higher education institutions shall, according to social needs, their conditions for running schools and the scale of the institution approved by the State, formulate enrollment plans and independently adjust the enrollment ratio of departments and specialties.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　高等学校依法自主设置和调整学科、专业。

**Article 33** Higher education institutions shall, according to law, independently establish and adjust disciplines and specialties.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　高等学校根据教学需要，自主制定教学计划、选编教材、组织实施教学活动。

**Article 34** Higher education institutions shall, according to teaching needs, independently formulate teaching plans, select and compile teaching materials, and organize and implement teaching activities.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　高等学校根据自身条件，自主开展科学研究、技术开发和社会服务。  
国家鼓励高等学校同企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织在科学研究、技术开发和推广等方面进行多种形式的合作。  
国家支持具备条件的高等学校成为国家科学研究基地。

**Article 35** Higher education institutions shall, according to their own conditions, independently conduct scientific research, technological development and social services.  
The State encourages higher education institutions to engage in various forms of cooperation with enterprises, public institutions, social groups and other social organizations in scientific research, technological development and promotion.  
The State supports qualified higher education institutions in becoming national scientific research bases.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　高等学校按照国家有关规定，自主开展与境外高等学校之间的科学技术文化交流与合作。

**Article 36** Higher education institutions shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, independently conduct scientific, technological and cultural exchanges and cooperation with higher education institutions outside the territory.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　高等学校根据实际需要和精简、效能的原则，自主确定教学、科学研究、行政职能部门等内部组织机构的设置和人员配备；按照国家有关规定，评聘教师和其他专业技术人员的职务，调整津贴及工资分配。

**Article 37** Higher education institutions shall, according to actual needs and the principles of simplification and efficiency, independently determine the establishment of internal organizational structures such as teaching, scientific research and administrative departments, and the staffing; and, in accordance with relevant State regulations, evaluate and appoint the positions of teachers and other professional and technical personnel, and adjust allowances and salary distribution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　高等学校对举办者提供的财产、国家财政性资助、受捐赠财产依法自主管理和使用。  
高等学校不得将用于教学和科学研究活动的财产挪作他用。

**Article 38** Higher education institutions shall, according to law, independently manage and use the property provided by the sponsors, the financial aid from the State, and the property donated.  
Higher education institutions shall not misappropriate property used for teaching and scientific research activities for other purposes.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家举办的高等学校实行中国共产党高等学校基层委员会领导下的校长负责制。中国共产党高等学校基层委员会按照中国共产党章程和有关规定，统一领导学校工作，支持校长独立负责地行使职权，其领导职责主要是：执行中国共产党的路线、方针、政策，坚持社会主义办学方向，领导学校的思想政治工作和德育工作，讨论决定学校内部组织机构的设置和内部组织机构负责人的人选，讨论决定学校的改革、发展和基本管理制度等重大事项，保证以培养人才为中心的各项任务的完成。  
社会力量举办的高等学校的内部管理体制按照国家有关社会力量办学的规定确定。

**Article 39** Higher education institutions established by the State shall practice a system under which the president assumes full responsibility under the leadership of the basic-level committee of the Communist Party of China in the institution. The basic-level committee of the Communist Party of China in the institution shall, in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China and relevant regulations, exercise unified leadership over the work of the institution, support the president to independently and responsibly exercise his functions and powers. Its leadership duties mainly include: implementing the line, principles and policies of the Communist Party of China, adhering to the socialist orientation in running the institution, leading ideological and political work and moral education in the institution, discussing and deciding on the establishment of internal organizational structures and the candidates for persons in charge of internal organizational structures, discussing and deciding on major matters such as the reform, development and basic management systems of the institution, and ensuring the completion of various tasks centered on cultivating talents.  
The internal management system of higher education institutions established by social forces shall be determined in accordance with the State provisions on running schools by social forces.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　高等学校的校长，由符合教育法规定的任职条件的公民担任。高等学校的校长、副校长按照国家有关规定任免。

**Article 40** The president of a higher education institution shall be held by a citizen who meets the qualifications prescribed by the Education Law. The president and vice-presidents of a higher education institution shall be appointed and removed in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　高等学校的校长全面负责本学校的教学、科学研究和其他行政管理工作，行使下列职权：  
（一）拟订发展规划，制定具体规章制度和年度工作计划并组织实施；  
（二）组织教学活动、科学研究和思想品德教育；  
（三）拟订内部组织机构的设置方案，推荐副校长人选，任免内部组织机构的负责人；  
（四）聘任与解聘教师以及内部其他工作人员，对学生进行学籍管理并实施奖励或者处分；  
（五）拟订和执行年度经费预算方案，保护和管理校产，维护学校的合法权益；  
（六）章程规定的其他职权。  
高等学校的校长主持校长办公会议或者校务会议，处理前款规定的有关事项。

**Article 41** The president of a higher education institution shall be fully responsible for the teaching, scientific research and other administrative work of the institution, and shall exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) To draft development plans, formulate specific rules and regulations and annual work plans, and organize their implementation;  
(2) To organize teaching activities, scientific research and ideological and moral education;  
(3) To draft plans for the establishment of internal organizational structures, recommend candidates for vice-presidents, and appoint and remove persons in charge of internal organizational structures;  
(4) To appoint and dismiss teachers and other internal staff, manage students' registration status, and give rewards or impose sanctions on students;  
(5) To draft and implement annual budget plans, protect and manage school property, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the institution;  
(6) Other functions and powers prescribed by the charter.  
The president of a higher education institution shall preside over meetings of the president or meetings of the institution's affairs to handle the relevant matters prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　高等学校设立学术委员会，履行下列职责：  
（一）审议学科建设、专业设置，教学、科学研究计划方案；  
（二）评定教学、科学研究成果；  
（三）调查、处理学术纠纷；  
（四）调查、认定学术不端行为；  
（五）按照章程审议、决定有关学术发展、学术评价、学术规范的其他事项。

**Article 42** A higher education institution shall establish an academic committee to perform the following duties:  
(1) To deliberate on discipline construction, specialty setting, and teaching and scientific research plans and programs;  
(2) To evaluate teaching and scientific research achievements;  
(3) To investigate and handle academic disputes;  
(4) To investigate and identify academic misconduct;  
(5) To deliberate on and decide, in accordance with the charter, other matters concerning academic development, academic evaluation and academic norms.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　高等学校通过以教师为主体的教职工代表大会等组织形式，依法保障教职工参与民主管理和监督，维护教职工合法权益。

**Article 43** Higher education institutions shall, through organizational forms such as the congress of faculty and staff with teachers as the main body, safeguard according to law the participation of faculty and staff in democratic management and supervision, and protect the lawful rights and interests of faculty and staff.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　高等学校应当建立本学校办学水平、教育质量的评价制度，及时公开相关信息，接受社会监督。  
教育行政部门负责组织专家或者委托第三方专业机构对高等学校的办学水平、效益和教育质量进行评估。评估结果应当向社会公开。

**Article 44** Higher education institutions shall establish a system for evaluating the level of running the institution and the quality of education, disclose relevant information in a timely manner, and accept social supervision.  
The education administrative department shall be responsible for organizing experts or entrusting third-party professional institutions to evaluate the level, efficiency and quality of education of higher education institutions. The evaluation results shall be made public to society.

### 第五章　高等学校教师和其他教育工作者

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　高等学校的教师及其他教育工作者享有法律规定的权利，履行法律规定的义务，忠诚于人民的教育事业。

**Article 45** Teachers and other educators in higher education institutions shall enjoy the rights prescribed by law, perform the obligations prescribed by law, and be loyal to the people's educational cause.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　高等学校实行教师资格制度。中国公民凡遵守宪法和法律，热爱教育事业，具有良好的思想品德，具备研究生或者大学本科毕业学历，有相应的教育教学能力，经认定合格，可以取得高等学校教师资格。不具备研究生或者大学本科毕业学历的公民，学有所长，通过国家教师资格考试，经认定合格，也可以取得高等学校教师资格。

**Article 46** Higher education institutions shall practice a system of teacher qualification. Any Chinese citizen who abides by the Constitution and the law, loves the educational cause, has good moral character, possesses the educational background of graduate or undergraduate studies, has corresponding teaching ability, and is determined to be qualified, may obtain the qualification for a teacher in a higher education institution. A citizen who does not possess the educational background of graduate or undergraduate studies but has expertise in a certain field, upon passing the national teacher qualification examination and being determined to be qualified, may also obtain the qualification for a teacher in a higher education institution.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　高等学校实行教师职务制度。高等学校教师职务根据学校所承担的教学、科学研究等任务的需要设置。教师职务设助教、讲师、副教授、教授。  
高等学校的教师取得前款规定的职务应当具备下列基本条件：  
（一）取得高等学校教师资格；  
（二）系统地掌握本学科的基础理论；  
（三）具备相应职务的教育教学能力和科学研究能力；  
（四）承担相应职务的课程和规定课时的教学任务。  
教授、副教授除应当具备以上基本任职条件外，还应当对本学科具有系统而坚实的基础理论和比较丰富的教学、科学研究经验，教学成绩显著，论文或者著作达到较高水平或者有突出的教学、科学研究成果。  
高等学校教师职务的具体任职条件由国务院规定。

**Article 47** Higher education institutions shall practice a system of teachers' professional titles. The professional titles of teachers in higher education institutions shall be set according to the needs of teaching, scientific research and other tasks undertaken by the institution. The professional titles of teachers shall include teaching assistant, lecturer, associate professor and professor.  
Teachers in higher education institutions who obtain the professional titles prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall meet the following basic conditions:  
(1) Having obtained the qualification for a teacher in a higher education institution;  
(2) Systematically mastering the basic theories of the discipline;  
(3) Possessing the teaching ability and scientific research ability corresponding to the professional title;  
(4) Undertaking the courses and the prescribed class hours of teaching tasks corresponding to the professional title.  
In addition to the above basic conditions, a professor or associate professor shall also have systematic and solid basic theories of the discipline and relatively rich experience in teaching and scientific research, remarkable teaching achievements, and papers or works reaching a relatively high level or outstanding teaching and scientific research achievements.  
The specific conditions for the professional titles of teachers in higher education institutions shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　高等学校实行教师聘任制。教师经评定具备任职条件的，由高等学校按照教师职务的职责、条件和任期聘任。  
高等学校的教师的聘任，应当遵循双方平等自愿的原则，由高等学校校长与受聘教师签订聘任合同。

**Article 48** Higher education institutions shall practice a system of teacher employment. Where a teacher is evaluated as meeting the qualifications, the higher education institution shall employ him or her according to the duties, conditions and term of the professional title.  
The employment of teachers in higher education institutions shall follow the principle of equality and voluntariness between both parties, and the president of the higher education institution shall sign an employment contract with the employed teacher.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　高等学校的管理人员，实行教育职员制度。高等学校的教学辅助人员及其他专业技术人员，实行专业技术职务聘任制度。

**Article 49** The administrative staff of higher education institutions shall practice a system of educational staff. The teaching assistants and other professional and technical personnel of higher education institutions shall practice a system of professional and technical post employment.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家保护高等学校教师及其他教育工作者的合法权益，采取措施改善高等学校教师及其他教育工作者的工作条件和生活条件。

**Article 50** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of teachers and other educators in higher education institutions, and take measures to improve the working and living conditions of teachers and other educators in higher education institutions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　高等学校应当为教师参加培训、开展科学研究和进行学术交流提供便利条件。  
高等学校应当对教师、管理人员和教学辅助人员及其他专业技术人员的思想政治表现、职业道德、业务水平和工作实绩进行考核，考核结果作为聘任或者解聘、晋升、奖励或者处分的依据。

**Article 51** Institutions of higher education shall provide convenient conditions for teachers to participate in training, conduct scientific research, and engage in academic exchanges.  
Institutions of higher education shall assess teachers, administrative staff, teaching assistants, and other professional technical personnel in respect of their ideological and political performance, professional ethics, professional competence, and work achievements. The assessment results shall serve as the basis for appointment or dismissal, promotion, reward, or disciplinary action.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　高等学校的教师、管理人员和教学辅助人员及其他专业技术人员，应当以教学和培养人才为中心做好本职工作。

**Article 52** Teachers, administrative staff, teaching assistants, and other professional technical personnel of institutions of higher education shall focus on teaching and talent cultivation in performing their duties.

### 第六章　高等学校的学生

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　高等学校的学生应当遵守法律、法规，遵守学生行为规范和学校的各项管理制度，尊敬师长，刻苦学习，增强体质，树立爱国主义、集体主义和社会主义思想，努力学习马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论，具有良好的思想品德，掌握较高的科学文化知识和专业技能。  
高等学校学生的合法权益，受法律保护。

**Article 53** Students of institutions of higher education shall abide by laws and regulations, comply with student conduct norms and the various management systems of the school, respect teachers, study diligently, strengthen physical fitness, foster the ideas of patriotism, collectivism, and socialism, study Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, and Deng Xiaoping Theory earnestly, maintain good ideological and moral character, and acquire high-level scientific and cultural knowledge and professional skills.  
The lawful rights and interests of students of institutions of higher education are protected by law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　高等学校的学生应当按照国家规定缴纳学费。  
家庭经济困难的学生，可以申请补助或者减免学费。

**Article 54** Students of institutions of higher education shall pay tuition fees in accordance with state regulations.  
Students with financial difficulties may apply for subsidies or tuition waivers or reductions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家设立奖学金，并鼓励高等学校、企业事业组织、社会团体以及其他社会组织和个人按照国家有关规定设立各种形式的奖学金，对品学兼优的学生、国家规定的专业的学生以及到国家规定的地区工作的学生给予奖励。  
国家设立高等学校学生勤工助学基金和贷学金，并鼓励高等学校、企业事业组织、社会团体以及其他社会组织和个人设立各种形式的助学金，对家庭经济困难的学生提供帮助。  
获得贷学金及助学金的学生，应当履行相应的义务。

**Article 55** The State establishes scholarships and encourages institutions of higher education, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other social organizations and individuals to establish various forms of scholarships in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, to reward students with excellent character and academic performance, students studying in specialties designated by the State, and students who work in areas designated by the State after graduation.  
The State establishes work-study funds and student loans for students of institutions of higher education and encourages institutions of higher education, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other social organizations and individuals to establish various forms of grants to provide assistance to students with financial difficulties.  
Students who receive student loans and grants shall fulfill corresponding obligations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　高等学校的学生在课余时间可以参加社会服务和勤工助学活动，但不得影响学业任务的完成。  
高等学校应当对学生的社会服务和勤工助学活动给予鼓励和支持，并进行引导和管理。

**Article 56** Students of institutions of higher education may participate in social services and work-study activities during their spare time, provided that such activities do not affect the completion of their academic tasks.  
Institutions of higher education shall encourage and support students' social service and work-study activities and provide guidance and management.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　高等学校的学生，可以在校内组织学生团体。学生团体在法律、法规规定的范围内活动，服从学校的领导和管理。

**Article 57** Students of institutions of higher education may organize student associations within the school. Student associations shall operate within the scope prescribed by laws and regulations and shall be subject to the leadership and management of the school.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　高等学校的学生思想品德合格，在规定的修业年限内学完规定的课程，成绩合格或者修满相应的学分，准予毕业。

**Article 58** A student of an institution of higher education whose ideological and moral character is deemed qualified, who has completed the prescribed courses within the prescribed duration of study, and whose grades are satisfactory or who has accumulated the required credits, shall be permitted to graduate.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　高等学校应当为毕业生、结业生提供就业指导和服务。  
国家鼓励高等学校毕业生到边远、艰苦地区工作。

**Article 59** Institutions of higher education shall provide employment guidance and services to graduates and completers.  
The State encourages graduates of institutions of higher education to work in remote and hardship areas.

### 第七章　高等教育投入和条件保障

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　高等教育实行以举办者投入为主、受教育者合理分担培养成本、高等学校多种渠道筹措经费的机制。  
国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照[教育法第五十六条](../../行政与公法/教育科技/中华人民共和国教育法.md#art-56)的规定，保证国家举办的高等教育的经费逐步增长。  
国家鼓励企业事业组织、社会团体及其他社会组织和个人向高等教育投入。

**Article 60** Higher education shall implement a mechanism whereby the sponsors assume the primary responsibility for funding, recipients of education reasonably share the training costs, and institutions of higher education raise funds through multiple channels.  
The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 56 of the Education Law, ensure a gradual increase in funding for higher education sponsored by the State.  
The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other social organizations and individuals to invest in higher education.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　高等学校的举办者应当保证稳定的办学经费来源，不得抽回其投入的办学资金。

**Article 61** The sponsors of institutions of higher education shall ensure stable sources of funding for school operation and shall not withdraw the funds they have invested.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国务院教育行政部门会同国务院其他有关部门根据在校学生年人均教育成本，规定高等学校年经费开支标准和筹措的基本原则；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门会同有关部门制订本行政区域内高等学校年经费开支标准和筹措办法，作为举办者和高等学校筹措办学经费的基本依据。

**Article 62** The administrative department for education under the State Council shall, together with other relevant departments under the State Council, prescribe the standards for annual expenditure of institutions of higher education and the basic principles for fund-raising based on the annual per-student educational cost; the administrative departments for education of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, together with relevant departments, formulate the standards for annual expenditure of institutions of higher education within their respective administrative regions and the methods for fund-raising, which shall serve as the basic basis for sponsors and institutions of higher education to raise funds for school operation.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家对高等学校进口图书资料、教学科研设备以及校办产业实行优惠政策。高等学校所办产业或者转让知识产权以及其他科学技术成果获得的收益，用于高等学校办学。

**Article 63** The State applies preferential policies to institutions of higher education in respect of the import of books, materials, teaching and research equipment, and school-run industries. The income derived from school-run industries or from the transfer of intellectual property rights and other scientific and technological achievements shall be used for the operation of institutions of higher education.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　高等学校收取的学费应当按照国家有关规定管理和使用，其他任何组织和个人不得挪用。

**Article 64** The tuition fees collected by institutions of higher education shall be managed and used in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and no other organization or individual shall misappropriate them.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　高等学校应当依法建立、健全财务管理制度，合理使用、严格管理教育经费，提高教育投资效益。  
高等学校的财务活动应当依法接受监督。

**Article 65** Institutions of higher education shall establish and improve financial management systems in accordance with law, use educational funds rationally and manage them strictly, and improve the efficiency of educational investment.  
The financial activities of institutions of higher education shall be subject to supervision in accordance with law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对高等教育活动中违反教育法规定的，依照教育法的有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 66** Any violation of the provisions of the Education Law in higher education activities shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Education Law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　中国境外个人符合国家规定的条件并办理有关手续后，可以进入中国境内高等学校学习、研究、进行学术交流或者任教，其合法权益受国家保护。

**Article 67** Individuals from outside China who meet the conditions prescribed by the State and have gone through relevant formalities may enter institutions of higher education within the territory of China for study, research, academic exchanges, or teaching, and their lawful rights and interests shall be protected by the State.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法所称高等学校是指大学、独立设置的学院和高等专科学校，其中包括高等职业学校和成人高等学校。  
本法所称其他高等教育机构是指除高等学校和经批准承担研究生教育任务的科学研究机构以外的从事高等教育活动的组织。  
本法有关高等学校的规定适用于其他高等教育机构和经批准承担研究生教育任务的科学研究机构，但是对高等学校专门适用的规定除外。

**Article 68** For the purposes of this Law, institutions of higher education refer to universities, independently established colleges, and specialized higher education schools, including higher vocational schools and adult higher education schools.  
For the purposes of this Law, other higher education institutions refer to organizations engaged in higher education activities other than institutions of higher education and scientific research institutions approved to undertake postgraduate education.  
The provisions of this Law concerning institutions of higher education shall apply to other higher education institutions and scientific research institutions approved to undertake postgraduate education, except for those provisions specifically applicable to institutions of higher education.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　本法自1999年1月1日起施行。

**Article 69** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1999.
