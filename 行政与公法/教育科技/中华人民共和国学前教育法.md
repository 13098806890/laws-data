# 中华人民共和国学前教育法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-11-08  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （2024年11月8日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　学前儿童 第三章　幼儿园 第四章　教职工 第五章　保育教育 第六章　投入保障 第七章　监督管理 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障适龄儿童接受学前教育，规范学前教育实施，促进学前教育普及普惠安全优质发展，提高全民族素质，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内实施学前教育，适用本法。 本法所称学前教育，是指由幼儿园等学前教育机构对三周岁到入小学前的儿童（以下称学前儿童）实施的保育和教育。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障适龄儿童接受学前教育，规范学前教育实施，促进学前教育普及普惠安全优质发展，提高全民族素质，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution to ensure that school-age children receive preschool education, regulate the implementation of preschool education, promote the universal, inclusive, safe, and high-quality development of preschool education, and improve the quality of the entire nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内实施学前教育，适用本法。  
本法所称学前教育，是指由幼儿园等学前教育机构对三周岁到入小学前的儿童（以下称学前儿童）实施的保育和教育。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the implementation of preschool education within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Preschool education as mentioned in this Law means the childcare and education provided by kindergartens and other preschool education institutions to children aged three years until they enter primary school (hereinafter referred to as "preschool children").

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行学前教育制度。  
学前教育是国民教育体系的组成部分，是重要的社会公益事业。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a preschool education system.  
Preschool education is a component of the national education system and an important public welfare undertaking.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　学前教育应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持社会主义办学方向，贯彻国家的教育方针。  
学前教育应当落实立德树人根本任务，培育社会主义核心价值观，继承和弘扬中华优秀传统文化、革命文化、社会主义先进文化，培育中华民族共同体意识，为培养德智体美劳全面发展的社会主义建设者和接班人奠定基础。

**Article 4** Preschool education shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, persist in the socialist direction of running schools, and implement the State's educational policies.  
Preschool education shall fulfill the fundamental task of fostering virtue and nurturing people, cultivate the core socialist values, inherit and promote the excellent traditional Chinese culture, revolutionary culture, and advanced socialist culture, cultivate the sense of community of the Chinese nation, and lay the foundation for cultivating socialist builders and successors who are well-developed morally, intellectually, physically, aesthetically, and labor-wise.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立健全学前教育保障机制。  
发展学前教育坚持政府主导，以政府举办为主，大力发展普惠性学前教育，鼓励、引导和规范社会力量参与。

**Article 5** The State shall establish and improve the preschool education guarantee mechanism.  
The development of preschool education shall adhere to government leadership, with government-run institutions as the mainstay, vigorously develop inclusive preschool education, and encourage, guide, and regulate the participation of social forces.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家推进普及学前教育，构建覆盖城乡、布局合理、公益普惠、安全优质的学前教育公共服务体系。  
各级人民政府应当依法履行职责，合理配置资源，缩小城乡之间、区域之间学前教育发展差距，为适龄儿童接受学前教育提供条件和支持。  
国家采取措施，倾斜支持农村地区、革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区和欠发达地区发展学前教育事业；保障适龄的家庭经济困难儿童、孤儿、残疾儿童和农村留守儿童等接受普惠性学前教育。

**Article 6** The State shall promote the universalization of preschool education and build a public service system for preschool education that covers urban and rural areas, has a rational layout, is public welfare and inclusive, and is safe and high-quality.  
People's governments at all levels shall perform their duties according to law, allocate resources rationally, narrow the development gap between urban and rural areas and between regions in preschool education, and provide conditions and support for school-age children to receive preschool education.  
The State shall take measures to provide targeted support for the development of preschool education in rural areas, old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and underdeveloped regions; and ensure that school-age children from families with financial difficulties, orphans, disabled children, and left-behind children in rural areas, among others, receive inclusive preschool education.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全社会应当为适龄儿童接受学前教育、健康快乐成长创造良好环境。

**Article 7** The whole society shall create a favorable environment for school-age children to receive preschool education and grow up healthily and happily.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院领导全国学前教育工作。  
省级人民政府和设区的市级人民政府统筹本行政区域内学前教育工作，健全投入机制，明确分担责任，制定政策并组织实施。  
县级人民政府对本行政区域内学前教育发展负主体责任，负责制定本地学前教育发展规划，统筹幼儿园建设、运行，加强公办幼儿园教师配备补充和工资待遇保障，对幼儿园进行监督管理。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当支持本辖区内学前教育发展。

**Article 8** The State Council shall lead the national preschool education work.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities divided into districts shall coordinate the preschool education work within their respective administrative regions, improve the investment mechanism, clarify shared responsibilities, formulate policies, and organize their implementation.  
The people's governments of counties shall bear the main responsibility for the development of preschool education within their respective administrative regions, be responsible for formulating local preschool education development plans, coordinating the construction and operation of kindergartens, strengthening the staffing and supplementation of public kindergarten teachers and the guarantee of their wages and benefits, and supervising and managing kindergartens.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall support the development of preschool education within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府教育行政部门负责学前教育管理和业务指导工作，配备相应的管理和教研人员。县级以上人民政府卫生健康行政部门、疾病预防控制部门按照职责分工负责监督指导幼儿园卫生保健工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责学前教育管理工作，履行规划制定、资源配置、经费投入、人员配备、待遇保障、幼儿园登记等方面的责任，依法加强对幼儿园举办、教职工配备、收费行为、经费使用、财务管理、安全保卫、食品安全等方面的监管。

**Article 9** The education administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration and professional guidance of preschool education, and shall allocate corresponding administrative and teaching research personnel. The health administrative departments and disease prevention and control departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, be responsible for supervising and guiding the health care work in kindergartens.  
Other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, be responsible for the administration of preschool education, fulfill responsibilities in aspects such as planning formulation, resource allocation, funding input, staffing, benefit guarantee, and kindergarten registration, and shall, according to law, strengthen supervision over the establishment of kindergartens, staffing of faculty and staff, fee charging practices, fund use, financial management, security, and food safety, among others.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持学前教育、儿童发展、特殊教育方面的科学研究，推广研究成果，宣传、普及科学的教育理念和方法。

**Article 10** The State shall encourage and support scientific research in preschool education, child development, and special education; promote research results; and publicize and popularize scientific educational concepts and methods.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励创作、出版、制作和传播有利于学前儿童健康成长的图书、玩具、音乐作品、音像制品等。

**Article 11** The State shall encourage the creation, publication, production, and dissemination of books, toys, musical works, audio-visual products, etc., that are conducive to the healthy growth of preschool children.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在学前教育工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in preschool education shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　学前儿童

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　学前儿童享有生命安全和身心健康、得到尊重和保护照料、依法平等接受学前教育等权利。  
学前教育应当坚持最有利于学前儿童的原则，给予学前儿童特殊、优先保护。

**Article 13** Preschool children shall enjoy the rights to life safety and physical and mental health, to be respected, protected, and cared for, and to receive preschool education equally according to law.  
Preschool education shall adhere to the principle of the best interests of preschool children and provide special and priority protection to preschool children.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　实施学前教育应当从学前儿童身心发展特点和利益出发，尊重学前儿童人格尊严，倾听、了解学前儿童的意见，平等对待每一个学前儿童，鼓励、引导学前儿童参与家庭、社会和文化生活，促进学前儿童获得全面发展。

**Article 14** The implementation of preschool education shall proceed from the characteristics and interests of preschool children's physical and mental development, respect the personal dignity of preschool children, listen to and understand the opinions of preschool children, treat every preschool child equally, encourage and guide preschool children to participate in family, social, and cultural life, and promote the comprehensive development of preschool children.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，推动适龄儿童在其父母或者其他监护人的工作或者居住的地区方便就近接受学前教育。  
学前儿童入幼儿园接受学前教育，除必要的身体健康检查外，幼儿园不得对其组织任何形式的考试或者测试。  
学前儿童因特异体质、特定疾病等有特殊需求的，父母或者其他监护人应当及时告知幼儿园，幼儿园应当予以特殊照顾。

**Article 15** Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to facilitate school-age children to receive preschool education conveniently and nearby in the area where their parents or other guardians work or reside.  
When preschool children enter kindergartens to receive preschool education, the kindergartens shall not organize any form of examination or test for them, except for necessary physical health checks.  
If preschool children have special needs due to special physical constitutions, specific diseases, etc., their parents or other guardians shall promptly inform the kindergarten, and the kindergarten shall provide special care.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　父母或者其他监护人应当依法履行抚养与教育儿童的义务，为适龄儿童接受学前教育提供必要条件。  
父母或者其他监护人应当尊重学前儿童身心发展规律和年龄特点，创造良好家庭环境，促进学前儿童健康成长。

**Article 16** Parents or other guardians shall, according to law, fulfill their obligations to raise and educate children and provide necessary conditions for school-age children to receive preschool education.  
Parents or other guardians shall respect the laws of physical and mental development and age characteristics of preschool children, create a good family environment, and promote the healthy growth of preschool children.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　普惠性幼儿园应当接收能够适应幼儿园生活的残疾儿童入园，并为其提供帮助和便利。  
父母或者其他监护人与幼儿园就残疾儿童入园发生争议的，县级人民政府教育行政部门应当会同卫生健康行政部门等单位组织对残疾儿童的身体状况、接受教育和适应幼儿园生活能力等进行全面评估，并妥善解决。

**Article 17** Inclusive kindergartens shall accept disabled children who are able to adapt to kindergarten life and shall provide them with help and convenience.  
If a dispute arises between parents or other guardians and a kindergarten regarding the admission of a disabled child, the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall, together with the health administrative department and other units, organize a comprehensive assessment of the disabled child's physical condition, ability to receive education and adapt to kindergarten life, and shall properly resolve the matter.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　青少年宫、儿童活动中心、图书馆、博物馆、文化馆、美术馆、科技馆、纪念馆、体育场馆等公共文化服务机构和爱国主义教育基地应当提供适合学前儿童身心发展的公益性教育服务，并按照有关规定对学前儿童免费开放。

**Article 18** Public cultural service institutions such as youth palaces, children's activity centers, libraries, museums, cultural centers, art galleries, science and technology museums, memorial halls, and sports venues, as well as patriotic education bases, shall provide public welfare educational services suitable for the physical and mental development of preschool children, and shall be open to preschool children free of charge in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位和个人不得组织学前儿童参与违背学前儿童身心发展规律或者与年龄特点不符的商业性活动、竞赛类活动和其他活动。

**Article 19** No unit or individual shall organize preschool children to participate in commercial activities, competitions, or other activities that violate the laws of physical and mental development of preschool children or are inconsistent with their age characteristics.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　面向学前儿童的图书、玩具、音像制品、电子产品、网络教育产品和服务等，应当符合学前儿童身心发展规律和年龄特点。  
家庭和幼儿园应当教育学前儿童正确合理使用网络和电子产品，控制其使用时间。

**Article 20** Books, toys, audio-visual products, electronic products, online education products and services, etc., targeted at preschool children shall comply with the laws of physical and mental development and age characteristics of preschool children.  
Families and kindergartens shall educate preschool children to use the internet and electronic products correctly and reasonably, and shall control their usage time.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　学前儿童的名誉、隐私和其他合法权益受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。  
幼儿园及其教职工等单位和个人收集、使用、提供、公开或者以其他方式处理学前儿童个人信息，应当取得其父母或者其他监护人的同意，遵守有关法律法规的规定。  
涉及学前儿童的新闻报道应当客观、审慎和适度。

**Article 21** The reputation, privacy, and other lawful rights and interests of preschool children shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual shall infringe upon them.  
When kindergartens, their staff, and other units or individuals collect, use, provide, disclose, or otherwise process the personal information of preschool children, they shall obtain the consent of their parents or other guardians and comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
News reports involving preschool children shall be objective, prudent, and moderate.

### 第三章　幼儿园

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹当前和长远，根据人口变化和城镇化发展趋势，科学规划和配置学前教育资源，有效满足需求，避免浪费资源。

**Article 22** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take into account both the present and the long term, scientifically plan and allocate preschool education resources in light of population changes and urbanization trends, effectively meet demand, and avoid waste of resources.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，扩大普惠性学前教育资源供给，提高学前教育质量。  
公办幼儿园和普惠性民办幼儿园为普惠性幼儿园，应当按照有关规定提供普惠性学前教育服务。

**Article 23** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to expand the supply of inclusive preschool education resources and improve the quality of preschool education.  
Public kindergartens and inclusive private kindergartens are inclusive kindergartens and shall provide inclusive preschool education services in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级人民政府应当利用财政性经费或者国有资产等举办或者支持举办公办幼儿园。  
各级人民政府依法积极扶持和规范社会力量举办普惠性民办幼儿园。  
普惠性民办幼儿园接受政府扶持，收费实行政府指导价管理。非营利性民办幼儿园可以向县级人民政府教育行政部门申请认定为普惠性民办幼儿园，认定标准由省级人民政府或者其授权的设区的市级人民政府制定。

**Article 24** People's governments at all levels shall, using financial funds or state-owned assets, etc., establish or support the establishment of public kindergartens.  
People's governments at all levels shall, according to law, actively support and regulate the establishment of inclusive private kindergartens by social forces.  
Inclusive private kindergartens shall accept government support, and their fees shall be subject to government-guided price management. Non-profit private kindergartens may apply to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level for recognition as inclusive private kindergartens; the recognition criteria shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or by the people's government of a city divided into cities authorized by it.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当以县级行政区划为单位制定幼儿园布局规划，将普惠性幼儿园建设纳入城乡公共管理和公共服务设施统一规划，并按照非营利性教育用地性质依法以划拨等方式供地，不得擅自改变用途。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，结合本地实际，在幼儿园布局规划中合理确定普惠性幼儿园覆盖率。

**Article 25** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate kindergarten layout plans on the basis of county-level administrative divisions, incorporate the construction of inclusive kindergartens into the unified planning of urban and rural public management and public service facilities, and shall allocate land by means of allocation, etc., according to law, as non-profit educational land, and shall not change the purpose without authorization.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and in light of local conditions, reasonably determine the coverage rate of inclusive kindergartens in the kindergarten layout plans.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　新建居住区等应当按照幼儿园布局规划等相关规划和标准配套建设幼儿园。配套幼儿园应当与首期建设的居住区同步规划、同步设计、同步建设、同步验收、同步交付使用。建设单位应当按照有关规定将配套幼儿园作为公共服务设施移交地方人民政府，用于举办普惠性幼儿园。  
现有普惠性幼儿园不能满足本区域适龄儿童入园需求的，县级人民政府应当通过新建、扩建以及利用公共设施改建等方式统筹解决。

**Article 26** New residential areas, etc., shall be equipped with kindergartens in accordance with relevant plans and standards such as kindergarten layout plans. Supporting kindergartens shall be planned, designed, constructed, accepted, and delivered for use simultaneously with the first phase of the residential area. The construction unit shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, transfer the supporting kindergarten as a public service facility to the local people's government for use in establishing inclusive kindergartens.  
If the existing inclusive kindergartens cannot meet the demand for school-age children in the area to enter kindergartens, the people's government at the county level shall make overall arrangements through new construction, expansion, and renovation of public facilities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　地方各级人民政府应当构建以公办幼儿园为主的农村学前教育公共服务体系，保障农村适龄儿童接受普惠性学前教育。  
县级人民政府教育行政部门可以委托乡镇中心幼儿园对本乡镇其他幼儿园开展业务指导等工作。

**Article 27** Local people's governments at all levels shall establish a rural preschool education public service system with public kindergartens as the mainstay, and ensure that rural school-age children receive inclusive preschool education.  
The education administrative department of the people's government at the county level may entrust the township central kindergarten to carry out professional guidance and other work for other kindergartens in the township.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本区域内残疾儿童的数量、分布状况和残疾类别，统筹实施多种形式的学前特殊教育，推进融合教育，推动特殊教育学校和有条件的儿童福利机构、残疾儿童康复机构增设学前部或者附设幼儿园。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the number, distribution, and categories of disabilities of disabled children in their region, coordinate the implementation of various forms of preschool special education, promote inclusive education, and encourage special education schools and qualified child welfare institutions and rehabilitation institutions for disabled children to add preschool departments or affiliated kindergartens.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　设立幼儿园，应当具备下列基本条件：  
（一）有组织机构和章程；  
（二）有符合规定的幼儿园园长、教师、保育员、卫生保健人员、安全保卫人员和其他工作人员；  
（三）符合规定的选址要求，设置在安全区域内；  
（四）符合规定的规模和班额标准；  
（五）有符合规定的园舍、卫生室或者保健室、安全设施设备及户外场地；  
（六）有必备的办学资金和稳定的经费来源；  
（七）卫生评价合格；  
（八）法律法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 29** The establishment of a kindergarten shall meet the following basic conditions:  
(1) having an organizational structure and articles of association;  
(2) having a kindergarten principal, teachers, childcare workers, health care personnel, security personnel, and other staff members that meet the requirements;  
(3) meeting the required site selection requirements and being located in a safe area;  
(4) meeting the required standards for scale and class size;  
(5) having premises, a clinic or health care room, safety facilities and equipment, and outdoor space that meet the requirements;  
(6) having necessary operating funds and a stable source of funding;  
(7) having passed health evaluation;  
(8) other conditions provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　设立幼儿园经县级人民政府教育行政部门依法审批、取得办学许可证后，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定进行相应法人登记。

**Article 30** After the establishment of a kindergarten is examined and approved by the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level according to law and a school license is obtained, the corresponding legal person registration shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　幼儿园变更、终止的，应当按照有关规定提前向县级人民政府教育行政部门报告并向社会公告，依法办理相关手续，妥善安置在园儿童。

**Article 31** If a kindergarten is altered or terminated, it shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, report in advance to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level and make a public announcement, handle relevant procedures according to law, and properly arrange for the children in the kindergarten.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　学前教育机构中的中国共产党基层组织，按照中国共产党章程开展党的活动，加强党的建设。  
公办幼儿园的基层党组织统一领导幼儿园工作，支持园长依法行使职权。民办幼儿园的内部管理体制按照国家有关民办教育的规定确定。

**Article 32** The grassroots organizations of the Communist Party of China in preschool education institutions shall carry out Party activities in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China and strengthen Party building.  
The grassroots Party organizations in public kindergartens shall uniformly lead the work of the kindergartens and support the principals in exercising their powers according to law. The internal management system of private kindergartens shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on private education.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　幼儿园应当保障教职工依法参与民主管理和监督。  
幼儿园应当设立家长委员会，家长委员会可以对幼儿园重大事项决策和关系学前儿童切身利益的事项提出意见和建议，对幼儿园保育教育工作和日常管理进行监督。

**Article 33** Kindergartens shall ensure that faculty and staff participate in democratic management and supervision according to law.  
Kindergartens shall establish parent committees. The parent committees may offer opinions and suggestions on the decision-making of major matters of the kindergarten and matters concerning the vital interests of preschool children, and supervise the childcare and education work and daily management of the kindergarten.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　任何单位和个人不得利用财政性经费、国有资产、集体资产或者捐赠资产举办或者参与举办营利性民办幼儿园。  
公办幼儿园不得转制为民办幼儿园。公办幼儿园不得举办或者参与举办营利性民办幼儿园和其他教育机构。  
以中外合作方式设立幼儿园，应当符合外商投资和中外合作办学有关法律法规的规定。

**Article 34** No unit or individual may use financial funds, state-owned assets, collective assets, or donated assets to establish or participate in the establishment of for-profit private kindergartens.  
Public kindergartens shall not be converted into private kindergartens. Public kindergartens shall not establish or participate in the establishment of for-profit private kindergartens or other educational institutions.  
The establishment of kindergartens through Sino-foreign cooperation shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on foreign investment and Sino-foreign cooperative education.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　社会资本不得通过兼并收购等方式控制公办幼儿园、非营利性民办幼儿园。  
幼儿园不得直接或者间接作为企业资产在境内外上市。上市公司不得通过股票市场融资投资营利性民办幼儿园，不得通过发行股份或者支付现金等方式购买营利性民办幼儿园资产。

**Article 35** Social capital shall not control public kindergartens or non-profit private kindergartens through mergers, acquisitions, or other means.  
Kindergartens shall not be directly or indirectly listed as enterprise assets domestically or abroad. Listed companies shall not invest in for-profit private kindergartens through stock market financing, nor shall they purchase the assets of for-profit private kindergartens through issuing shares, paying cash, or other means.

### 第四章　教职工

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　幼儿园教师应当爱护儿童，具备优良品德和专业能力，为人师表，忠诚于人民的教育事业。  
全社会应当尊重幼儿园教师。

**Article 36** Kindergarten teachers shall care for children, possess excellent moral character and professional competence, set an example by their conduct, and be loyal to the people's educational cause.  
The whole society shall respect kindergarten teachers.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　担任幼儿园教师应当取得幼儿园教师资格；已取得其他教师资格并经县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门组织的学前教育专业培训合格的，可以在幼儿园任教。

**Article 37** To serve as a kindergarten teacher, one shall obtain a kindergarten teacher qualification; those who have obtained other teacher qualifications and have passed the preschool education professional training organized by the education administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level may teach in kindergartens.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　幼儿园园长由其举办者或者决策机构依法任命或者聘任，并报县级人民政府教育行政部门备案。  
幼儿园园长应当具有[本法第三十七条](#art-37)规定的教师资格、大学专科以上学历、五年以上幼儿园教师或者幼儿园管理工作经历。  
国家推行幼儿园园长职级制。幼儿园园长应当参加县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门组织的园长岗位培训。

**Article 38** The kindergarten principal shall be appointed or employed according to law by the sponsor or decision-making body, and shall be reported to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level for record.  
The kindergarten principal shall have the teacher qualification prescribed in Article 37 of this Law, an associate degree or above, and more than five years' experience as a kindergarten teacher or in kindergarten management.  
The State shall promote the rank system for kindergarten principals. Kindergarten principals shall participate in the principal position training organized by the education administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　保育员应当具有国家规定的学历，并经过幼儿保育职业培训。  
卫生保健人员包括医师、护士和保健员，医师、护士应当取得相应执业资格，保健员应当具有国家规定的学历，并经过卫生保健专业知识培训。  
幼儿园其他工作人员的任职资格条件，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 39** Childcare workers shall have the educational background prescribed by the State and shall have received vocational training in childcare.  
Health care personnel include doctors, nurses, and health workers. Doctors and nurses shall obtain corresponding practice qualifications, and health workers shall have the educational background prescribed by the State and shall have received professional training in health care knowledge.  
The qualifications for other staff members of kindergartens shall be implemented in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　幼儿园教师职务（职称）分为初级、中级和高级。  
幼儿园教师职务（职称）评审标准应当符合学前教育的专业特点和要求。  
幼儿园卫生保健人员中的医师、护士纳入卫生专业技术人员职称系列，由人力资源社会保障、卫生健康行政部门组织评审。

**Article 40** The professional titles (technical positions) of kindergarten teachers are divided into junior, intermediate, and senior levels.  
The evaluation criteria for the professional titles (technical positions) of kindergarten teachers shall conform to the professional characteristics and requirements of preschool education.  
Doctors and nurses among the health care personnel of kindergartens shall be included in the professional title series of health professionals, and their evaluation shall be organized by the human resources and social security administrative departments and health administrative departments.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院教育行政部门会同有关部门制定幼儿园教职工配备标准。地方各级人民政府及有关部门按照相关标准保障公办幼儿园及时补充教师，并应当优先满足农村地区、革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区和欠发达地区公办幼儿园的需要。幼儿园及其举办者应当按照相关标准配足配齐教师和其他工作人员。

**Article 41** The education administrative department of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, formulate standards for the staffing of kindergarten faculty and staff. Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall, in accordance with relevant standards, ensure that public kindergartens are timely supplemented with teachers, and shall give priority to meeting the needs of public kindergartens in rural areas, old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and underdeveloped regions. Kindergartens and their sponsors shall, in accordance with relevant standards, fully equip teachers and other staff members.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　幼儿园园长、教师、保育员、卫生保健人员、安全保卫人员和其他工作人员应当遵守法律法规和职业道德规范，尊重、爱护和平等对待学前儿童，不断提高专业素养。

**Article 42** Kindergarten principals, teachers, childcare workers, health care personnel, security personnel, and other staff members shall abide by laws, regulations, and professional ethics; respect, care for, and treat preschool children equally; and continuously improve their professional competence.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　幼儿园应当与教职工依法签订聘用合同或者劳动合同，并将合同信息报县级人民政府教育行政部门备案。

**Article 43** Kindergartens shall, according to law, sign employment contracts or labor contracts with their faculty and staff, and shall report the contract information to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level for record.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　幼儿园聘任（聘用）园长、教师、保育员、卫生保健人员、安全保卫人员和其他工作人员时，应当向教育、公安等有关部门查询应聘者是否具有虐待、性侵害、性骚扰、拐卖、暴力伤害、吸毒、赌博等违法犯罪记录；发现其有前述行为记录，或者有酗酒、严重违反师德师风行为等其他可能危害儿童身心安全情形的，不得聘任（聘用）。  
幼儿园发现在岗人员有前款规定可能危害儿童身心安全情形的，应当立即停止其工作，依法与其解除聘用合同或者劳动合同，并向县级人民政府教育行政部门进行报告；县级人民政府教育行政部门可以将其纳入从业禁止人员名单。  
有本条第一款规定可能危害儿童身心安全情形的个人不得举办幼儿园；已经举办的，应当依法变更举办者。

**Article 44** When kindergartens employ (hire) principals, teachers, childcare workers, health care personnel, security personnel, and other staff members, they shall inquire with the education, public security, and other relevant departments whether the applicant has any record of illegal or criminal acts such as abuse, sexual assault, sexual harassment, abduction, violent injury, drug use, gambling, etc.; if it is found that the person has a record of the aforementioned acts, or has other circumstances that may endanger the physical and mental safety of children, such as alcoholism or serious violation of teacher ethics, the person shall not be employed (hired).  
If a kindergarten discovers that an on-duty staff member falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph that may endanger the physical and mental safety of children, it shall immediately stop the person from working, terminate the employment contract or labor contract according to law, and report to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level; the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level may include the person in the list of persons prohibited from engaging in the profession.  
A person with the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article that may endanger the physical and mental safety of children shall not establish a kindergarten; if a kindergarten has already been established, the sponsor shall be changed according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　幼儿园应当关注教职工的身体、心理状况。幼儿园园长、教师、保育员、卫生保健人员、安全保卫人员和其他工作人员，应当在入职前和入职后每年进行健康检查。

**Article 45** Kindergartens shall pay attention to the physical and psychological conditions of their faculty and staff. Kindergarten principals, teachers, childcare workers, health care personnel, security personnel, and other staff members shall undergo health check-ups before taking up their posts and annually thereafter.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　幼儿园及其举办者应当按照国家规定保障教师和其他工作人员的工资福利，依法缴纳社会保险费，改善工作和生活条件，实行同工同酬。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将公办幼儿园教师工资纳入财政保障范围，统筹工资收入政策和经费支出渠道，确保教师工资及时足额发放。民办幼儿园可以参考当地公办幼儿园同类教师工资收入水平合理确定教师薪酬标准，依法保障教师工资待遇。

**Article 46** Kindergartens and their sponsors shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, guarantee the wages and benefits of teachers and other staff members, pay social insurance premiums according to law, improve working and living conditions, and implement equal pay for equal work.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall include the wages of public kindergarten teachers in the scope of financial guarantee, coordinate wage income policies and funding channels, and ensure the timely and full payment of teachers' wages. Private kindergartens may reasonably determine teachers' salary standards by referring to the income levels of similar teachers in local public kindergartens, and shall guarantee teachers' wages and benefits according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　幼儿园教师在职称评定、岗位聘任（聘用）等方面享有与中小学教师同等的待遇。  
符合条件的幼儿园教师按照有关规定享受艰苦边远地区津贴、乡镇工作补贴等津贴、补贴。  
承担特殊教育任务的幼儿园教师按照有关规定享受特殊教育津贴。

**Article 47** Kindergarten teachers shall enjoy the same treatment as primary and secondary school teachers in terms of professional title evaluation, position appointment (employment), etc.  
Kindergarten teachers who meet the conditions shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, enjoy subsidies and allowances such as hardship and remote area allowances and township work subsidies.  
Kindergarten teachers who undertake special education tasks shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, enjoy special education allowances.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院教育行政部门应当制定高等学校学前教育专业设置标准、质量保证标准和课程教学标准体系，组织实施学前教育专业质量认证，建立培养质量保障机制。  
省级人民政府应当根据普及学前教育的需要，制定学前教育师资培养规划，支持高等学校设立学前教育专业，合理确定培养规模，提高培养层次和培养质量。  
制定公费师范生培养计划，应当根据学前教育发展需要专项安排学前教育专业培养计划。

**Article 48** The education administrative department of the State Council shall formulate standards for the establishment of preschool education specialties in higher education institutions, quality assurance standards, and a curriculum teaching standard system; organize and implement quality certification for preschool education specialties; and establish a training quality assurance mechanism.  
People's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the need to universalize preschool education, formulate plans for the training of preschool education teachers, support higher education institutions in establishing preschool education specialties, reasonably determine the training scale, and improve the training level and quality.  
When formulating plans for the training of publicly-funded (normal) students, special arrangements shall be made for preschool education specialty training plans based on the development needs of preschool education.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府教育、卫生健康等有关部门应当按照职责分工制定幼儿园园长、教师、保育员、卫生保健人员等工作人员培训规划，建立培训支持服务体系，开展多种形式的专业培训。

**Article 49** The education, health, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, formulate training plans for kindergarten principals, teachers, childcare workers, health care personnel, and other staff members; establish a training support service system; and carry out various forms of professional training.

### 第五章　保育教育

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　幼儿园应当坚持保育和教育相结合的原则，面向全体学前儿童，关注个体差异，注重良好习惯养成，创造适宜的生活和活动环境，有益于学前儿童身心健康发展。

**Article 50** Kindergartens shall adhere to the principle of combining childcare and education, cater to all preschool children, pay attention to individual differences, emphasize the cultivation of good habits, and create a suitable living and activity environment that is conducive to the healthy physical and mental development of preschool children.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　幼儿园应当把保护学前儿童安全放在首位，对学前儿童在园期间的人身安全负有保护责任。  
幼儿园应当落实安全责任制相关规定，建立健全安全管理制度和安全责任制度，完善安全措施和应急反应机制，按照标准配备安全保卫人员，及时排查和消除火灾等各类安全隐患。幼儿园使用校车的，应当符合校车安全管理相关规定，保护学前儿童安全。  
幼儿园应当按照国家有关规定投保校方责任保险。

**Article 51** : Kindergartens shall prioritize the protection of preschool children's safety and bear the responsibility for protecting the personal safety of preschool children during their stay in the kindergarten.  
Kindergartens shall implement relevant provisions of the safety responsibility system, establish and improve safety management systems and safety responsibility systems, improve safety measures and emergency response mechanisms, equip security personnel in accordance with standards, and promptly investigate and eliminate various safety hazards such as fires. Where kindergartens use school buses, they shall comply with relevant provisions on school bus safety management to protect the safety of preschool children.  
Kindergartens shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, take out liability insurance for the school's responsibility.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　幼儿园发现学前儿童受到侵害、疑似受到侵害或者面临其他危险情形的，应当立即采取保护措施，并向公安、教育等有关部门报告。  
幼儿园发生突发事件等紧急情况，应当优先保护学前儿童人身安全，立即采取紧急救助和避险措施，并及时向有关部门报告。  
发生前两款情形的，幼儿园应当及时通知学前儿童父母或者其他监护人。

**Article 52** : Where a kindergarten discovers that a preschool child has been harmed, is suspected of being harmed, or faces other dangerous situations, it shall immediately take protective measures and report to the public security, education, and other relevant departments.  
In the event of emergencies such as sudden incidents in a kindergarten, it shall prioritize the protection of preschool children's personal safety, immediately take emergency rescue and hazard avoidance measures, and promptly report to the relevant departments.  
In the circumstances described in the preceding two paragraphs, the kindergarten shall promptly notify the parents or other guardians of the preschool child.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　幼儿园应当建立科学合理的一日生活制度，保证户外活动时间，做好儿童营养膳食、体格锻炼、全日健康观察、食品安全、卫生与消毒、传染病预防与控制、常见病预防等卫生保健管理工作，加强健康教育。

**Article 53** : Kindergartens shall establish a scientific and reasonable daily routine, ensure outdoor activity time, and properly carry out health care management such as children's nutritional meals, physical exercise, full-day health observation, food safety, hygiene and disinfection, prevention and control of infectious diseases, and prevention of common diseases, and strengthen health education.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　招收残疾儿童的幼儿园应当配备必要的康复设施、设备和专业康复人员，或者与其他具有康复设施、设备和专业康复人员的特殊教育机构、康复机构合作，根据残疾儿童实际情况开展保育教育。

**Article 54** : Kindergartens that enroll children with disabilities shall be equipped with necessary rehabilitation facilities, equipment, and professional rehabilitation personnel, or cooperate with other special education institutions or rehabilitation institutions that have rehabilitation facilities, equipment, and professional rehabilitation personnel, and carry out childcare education based on the actual conditions of children with disabilities.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院教育行政部门制定幼儿园教育指导纲要和学前儿童学习与发展指南，地方各级人民政府教育行政部门依据职责组织实施，加强学前教育教学研究和业务指导。  
幼儿园应当按照国家有关规定，科学实施符合学前儿童身心发展规律和年龄特点的保育和教育活动，不得组织学前儿童参与商业性活动。

**Article 55** : The education administrative department of the State Council shall formulate the kindergarten education guidance outline and the learning and development guidelines for preschool children. The education administrative departments of local people's governments at all levels shall organize implementation in accordance with their duties, and strengthen preschool education teaching research and professional guidance.  
Kindergartens shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, scientifically implement childcare and education activities that conform to the laws of physical and mental development and age characteristics of preschool children, and shall not organize preschool children to participate in commercial activities.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　幼儿园应当以学前儿童的生活为基础，以游戏为基本活动，发展素质教育，最大限度支持学前儿童通过亲近自然、实际操作、亲身体验等方式探索学习，促进学前儿童养成良好的品德、行为习惯、安全和劳动意识，健全人格、强健体魄，在健康、语言、社会、科学、艺术等各方面协调发展。  
幼儿园应当以国家通用语言文字为基本保育教育语言文字，加强学前儿童普通话教育，提高学前儿童说普通话的能力。

**Article 56** : Kindergartens shall take the life of preschool children as the foundation, take play as the basic activity, develop quality education, and provide maximum support for preschool children to explore and learn through ways such as getting close to nature, hands-on operation, and personal experience, so as to promote preschool children to develop good morals, behavioral habits, safety and labor awareness, sound personality, strong physique, and coordinated development in aspects such as health, language, society, science, and art.  
Kindergartens shall take the national common language and script as the basic language and script for childcare education, strengthen Putonghua education for preschool children, and improve their ability to speak Putonghua.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　幼儿园应当配备符合相关标准的玩教具和幼儿图书。  
在幼儿园推行使用的课程教学类资源应当经依法审定，具体办法由国务院教育行政部门制定。  
幼儿园应当充分利用家庭、社区的教育资源，拓展学前儿童生活和学习空间。

**Article 57** : Kindergartens shall be equipped with play and teaching aids and children's books that meet relevant standards.  
Course teaching resources promoted for use in kindergartens shall be subject to examination and approval according to law, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the education administrative department of the State Council.  
Kindergartens shall make full use of the educational resources of families and communities to expand the living and learning space of preschool children.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　幼儿园应当主动与父母或者其他监护人交流学前儿童身心发展状况，指导家庭科学育儿。  
父母或者其他监护人应当积极配合、支持幼儿园开展保育和教育活动。

**Article 58** : Kindergartens shall proactively communicate with parents or other guardians about the physical and mental development status of preschool children, and guide families in scientific child-rearing.  
Parents or other guardians shall actively cooperate with and support kindergartens in carrying out childcare and education activities.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　幼儿园与小学应当互相衔接配合，共同帮助儿童做好入学准备和入学适应。  
幼儿园不得采用小学化的教育方式，不得教授小学阶段的课程，防止保育和教育活动小学化。小学坚持按照课程标准零起点教学。  
校外培训机构等其他任何机构不得对学前儿童开展半日制或者全日制培训，不得教授学前儿童小学阶段的课程。

**Article 59** : Kindergartens and primary schools shall coordinate and cooperate with each other, and jointly help children prepare for and adapt to school.  
Kindergartens shall not adopt primary-school-oriented education methods, nor teach primary school level courses, to prevent the primary-school orientation of childcare and education activities. Primary schools shall insist on zero-starting-point teaching in accordance with curriculum standards.  
No off-campus training institutions or any other institutions shall provide half-day or full-day training to preschool children, nor teach preschool children primary school level courses.

### 第六章　投入保障

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　学前教育实行政府投入为主、家庭合理负担保育教育成本、多渠道筹措经费的投入机制。  
各级人民政府应当优化教育财政投入支出结构，加大学前教育财政投入，确保财政性学前教育经费在同级财政性教育经费中占合理比例，保障学前教育事业发展。

**Article 60** : Preschool education shall implement an investment mechanism with government investment as the mainstay, families reasonably bearing the costs of childcare education, and funds raised through multiple channels.  
People's governments at all levels shall optimize the structure of educational financial input and expenditure, increase financial input in preschool education, ensure that fiscal funds for preschool education account for a reasonable proportion of fiscal education funds at the same level, and guarantee the development of preschool education.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　学前教育财政补助经费按照中央与地方财政事权和支出责任划分原则，分别列入中央和地方各级预算。中央财政通过转移支付对地方统筹给予支持。省级人民政府应当建立本行政区域内各级人民政府财政补助经费分担机制。

**Article 61** : Financial subsidy funds for preschool education shall, in accordance with the principle of dividing fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities between the central and local governments, be included in the budgets of the central and local governments at all levels respectively. The central finance shall provide support to local governments through transfer payments in a coordinated manner. The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall establish a mechanism for sharing financial subsidy funds among people's governments at all levels within its administrative region.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国务院和省级人民政府统筹安排学前教育资金，重点扶持农村地区、革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区和欠发达地区发展学前教育。

**Article 62** : The State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall make overall arrangements for preschool education funds, and shall give priority support to the development of preschool education in rural areas, old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and underdeveloped areas.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　地方各级人民政府应当科学核定普惠性幼儿园办园成本，以提供普惠性学前教育服务为衡量标准，统筹制定财政补助和收费政策，合理确定分担比例。  
省级人民政府制定并落实公办幼儿园生均财政拨款标准或者生均公用经费标准，以及普惠性民办幼儿园生均财政补助标准。其中，残疾学前儿童的相关标准应当考虑保育教育和康复需要适当提高。  
有条件的地方逐步推进实施免费学前教育，降低家庭保育教育成本。

**Article 63** : Local people's governments at all levels shall scientifically verify the operating costs of inclusive kindergartens, take the provision of inclusive preschool education services as the measurement standard, formulate financial subsidy and fee policies in a coordinated manner, and reasonably determine the sharing ratio.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate and implement the per-student financial allocation standard or per-student public funds standard for public kindergartens, as well as the per-student financial subsidy standard for inclusive private kindergartens. Among these, the relevant standards for preschool children with disabilities shall be appropriately increased taking into account the needs of childcare education and rehabilitation.  
Where conditions permit, the implementation of free preschool education shall be gradually promoted to reduce the cost of family childcare education.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　地方各级人民政府应当通过财政补助、购买服务、减免租金、培训教师、教研指导等多种方式，支持普惠性民办幼儿园发展。

**Article 64** : Local people's governments at all levels shall support the development of inclusive private kindergartens through various means such as financial subsidies, purchase of services, rent reduction or exemption, teacher training, and teaching research guidance.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国家建立学前教育资助制度，为家庭经济困难的适龄儿童等接受普惠性学前教育提供资助。

**Article 65** : The State shall establish a preschool education financial aid system to provide assistance for school-age children from families with economic difficulties to receive inclusive preschool education.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家鼓励自然人、法人和非法人组织通过捐赠、志愿服务等方式支持学前教育事业。

**Article 66** : The State encourages natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to support the preschool education cause through donations, volunteer services, and other means.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当建立健全幼儿园安全风险防控体系，强化幼儿园周边治安管理和巡逻防控工作，加强对幼儿园安全保卫的监督指导，督促幼儿园加强安全防范建设，及时排查和消除安全隐患，依法保障学前儿童与幼儿园安全。  
禁止在幼儿园内及周边区域建设或者设置有危险、有污染的建筑物和设施设备。

**Article 67** : People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish and improve the kindergarten safety risk prevention and control system, strengthen public security management and patrol and prevention work around kindergartens, strengthen supervision and guidance on kindergarten security protection, urge kindergartens to strengthen safety prevention construction, promptly investigate and eliminate safety hazards, and ensure the safety of preschool children and kindergartens according to law.  
It shall be prohibited to construct or install buildings, facilities, and equipment that are dangerous or polluting within kindergartens and in surrounding areas.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　省级人民政府或者其授权的设区的市级人民政府根据办园成本、经济发展水平和群众承受能力等因素，合理确定公办幼儿园和非营利性民办幼儿园的收费标准，并建立定期调整机制。  
县级以上地方人民政府及有关部门应当加强对幼儿园收费的监管，必要时可以对收费实行市场调节价的营利性民办幼儿园开展成本调查，引导合理收费，遏制过高收费。

**Article 68** : The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or a city divided into districts authorized by it, shall, based on factors such as the cost of running kindergartens, the level of economic development, and the affordability of the public, reasonably determine the fee standards for public kindergartens and non-profit private kindergartens, and establish a regular adjustment mechanism.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant departments shall strengthen supervision over kindergarten fees, and may, when necessary, conduct cost investigations on for-profit private kindergartens whose fees are subject to market-regulated prices, guide reasonable fee collection, and curb excessively high fees.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　幼儿园收取的费用应当主要用于保育和教育活动、保障教职工待遇、促进教职工发展和改善办园条件。学前儿童伙食费应当专款专用。  
幼儿园应当执行收费公示制度，收费项目和标准、服务内容、退费规则等应当向家长公示，接受社会监督。  
幼儿园不得违反有关规定收取费用，不得向学前儿童及其家长组织征订教学材料，推销或者变相推销商品、服务等。

**Article 69** : Fees collected by kindergartens shall be mainly used for childcare and education activities, ensuring the treatment of teaching and administrative staff, promoting the development of teaching and administrative staff, and improving the conditions for running the kindergarten. Meal fees for preschool children shall be used for designated purposes only.  
Kindergartens shall implement a fee disclosure system, and the items and standards of fees, service contents, and refund rules shall be disclosed to parents and subject to public supervision.  
Kindergartens shall not collect fees in violation of relevant regulations, nor shall they organize the subscription of teaching materials for preschool children and their parents, or sell or sell in disguised form goods or services, etc.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　幼儿园应当依法建立健全财务、会计及资产管理制度，严格经费管理，合理使用经费，提高经费使用效益。  
幼儿园应当按照有关规定实行财务公开，接受社会监督。县级以上人民政府教育等有关部门应当加强对公办幼儿园的审计。民办幼儿园每年应当依法进行审计，并向县级人民政府教育行政部门提交经审计的财务会计报告。

**Article 70** : Kindergartens shall, according to law, establish and improve financial, accounting, and asset management systems, strictly manage funds, use funds reasonably, and improve the efficiency of fund use.  
Kindergartens shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, implement financial disclosure and accept public supervision. Education and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the audit of public kindergartens. Private kindergartens shall conduct audits annually according to law and submit audited financial accounting reports to the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当建立健全学前教育经费预算管理和审计监督制度。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、挪用学前教育经费，不得向幼儿园非法收取或者摊派费用。

**Article 71** : People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish and improve the preschool education fund budget management and audit supervision systems.  
No unit or individual may encroach upon or misappropriate preschool education funds, nor illegally collect or apportion fees from kindergartens.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　县级人民政府教育行政部门应当建立健全各类幼儿园基本信息备案及公示制度，利用互联网等方式定期向社会公布并更新政府学前教育财政投入、幼儿园规划举办等方面信息，以及各类幼儿园的教师和其他工作人员的资质和配备、招生、经费收支、收费标准、保育教育质量等方面信息。

**Article 72** : The education administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall establish and improve the filing and disclosure system for basic information of various types of kindergartens, and use the internet and other means to regularly publish and update information to the public on government financial input in preschool education, kindergarten planning and establishment, as well as information on the qualifications and staffing of teachers and other staff of various types of kindergartens, enrollment, revenue and expenditure, fee standards, and quality of childcare education.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　县级以上人民政府教育督导机构对学前教育工作执行法律法规情况、保育教育工作等进行督导。督导报告应当定期向社会公开。

**Article 73** : The education supervision institutions of people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise the implementation of laws and regulations in preschool education work, childcare and education work, etc. Supervision reports shall be regularly disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　国务院教育行政部门制定幼儿园保育教育质量评估指南。省级人民政府教育行政部门应当完善幼儿园质量评估标准，健全幼儿园质量评估监测体系，将各类幼儿园纳入质量评估范畴，并向社会公布评估结果。

**Article 74** : The education administrative department of the State Council shall formulate the kindergarten childcare education quality assessment guide. The education administrative departments of people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall improve kindergarten quality assessment standards, improve the kindergarten quality assessment and monitoring system, include all types of kindergartens in the scope of quality assessment, and publish assessment results to the public.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　地方各级人民政府及有关部门有下列情形之一的，由上级机关或者有关部门按照职责分工责令限期改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未按照规定制定、调整幼儿园布局规划，或者未按照规定提供普惠性幼儿园建设用地；  
（二）未按照规定规划居住区配套幼儿园，或者未将新建居住区配套幼儿园举办为普惠性幼儿园；  
（三）利用财政性经费、国有资产、集体资产或者捐赠资产举办或者参与举办营利性民办幼儿园，或者改变、变相改变公办幼儿园性质；  
（四）未按照规定制定并落实公办幼儿园生均财政拨款标准或者生均公用经费标准、普惠性民办幼儿园生均财政补助标准；  
（五）其他未依法履行学前教育管理和保障职责的情形。

**Article 75** : Where local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments fall under any of the following circumstances, the superior organs or relevant departments shall, according to their division of duties, order correction within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the leading personnel responsible and the directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to formulate or adjust the kindergarten layout plan in accordance with the provisions, or failing to provide land for the construction of inclusive kindergartens in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to plan supporting kindergartens in residential areas in accordance with the provisions, or failing to establish new residential area supporting kindergartens as inclusive kindergartens;  
(3) using fiscal funds, state-owned assets, collective assets, or donated assets to establish or participate in establishing for-profit private kindergartens, or changing or changing in disguised form the nature of public kindergartens;  
(4) failing to formulate and implement the per-student financial allocation standard or per-student public funds standard for public kindergartens and the per-student financial subsidy standard for inclusive private kindergartens in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) other circumstances where the duties of preschool education management and guarantee are not performed according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　地方各级人民政府及教育等有关部门的工作人员违反本法规定，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 76** : Where staff members of local people's governments at all levels and education and other relevant departments violate the provisions of this Law by abusing their powers, neglecting their duties, or engaging in irregularities for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　居住区建设单位未按照规定建设、移交配套幼儿园，或者改变配套幼儿园土地用途的，由县级以上地方人民政府自然资源、住房和城乡建设、教育等有关部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，依法给予处罚。

**Article 77** : Where a residential area construction unit fails to construct or transfer supporting kindergartens in accordance with the provisions, or changes the land use purpose of supporting kindergartens, the natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, education, and other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their division of duties, order correction within a time limit and impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　擅自举办幼儿园或者招收学前儿童实施半日制、全日制培训的，由县级人民政府教育等有关部门依照《中华人民共和国教育法》、《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法》的规定予以处理；对非法举办幼儿园的单位和个人，根据情节轻重，五至十年内不受理其举办幼儿园或者其他教育机构的申请。

**Article 78** : Where anyone establishes a kindergarten without authorization or enrolls preschool children to provide half-day or full-day training, the education and other relevant departments of the people's government at the county level shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the Education Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Promotion of Privately-run Schools; for units and individuals that illegally establish kindergartens, depending on the severity of the circumstances, their applications to establish kindergartens or other educational institutions shall not be accepted for five to ten years.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　幼儿园有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府教育等有关部门按照职责分工责令限期改正，并予以警告；有违法所得的，退还所收费用后没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停止招生、吊销办学许可证：  
（一）组织入园考试或者测试；  
（二）因管理疏忽或者放任发生体罚或者变相体罚、歧视、侮辱、虐待、性侵害等危害学前儿童身心安全的行为；  
（三）未依法加强安全防范建设、履行安全保障责任，或者未依法履行卫生保健责任；  
（四）使用未经审定的课程教学类资源；  
（五）采用小学化的教育方式或者教授小学阶段的课程；  
（六）开展与学前儿童身心发展规律、年龄特点不符的活动，或者组织学前儿童参与商业性活动；  
（七）未按照规定配备幼儿园教师或者其他工作人员；  
（八）违反规定收取费用；  
（九）克扣、挪用学前儿童伙食费。  
依照前款规定被吊销办学许可证的幼儿园，应当妥善安置在园儿童。

**Article 79** : Where a kindergarten falls under any of the following circumstances, the education and other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their division of duties, order correction within a time limit and issue a warning; if there are illegal gains, the fees collected shall be refunded and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the kindergarten shall be ordered to suspend enrollment and its school-running license shall be revoked:  
(1) organizing entrance examinations or tests;  
(2) due to management negligence or connivance, the occurrence of corporal punishment or disguised corporal punishment, discrimination, humiliation, abuse, sexual assault, or other acts that endanger the physical and mental safety of preschool children;  
(3) failing to strengthen safety prevention construction or fulfill safety guarantee responsibilities according to law, or failing to fulfill health care responsibilities according to law;  
(4) using course teaching resources that have not been examined and approved;  
(5) adopting primary-school-oriented education methods or teaching primary school level courses;  
(6) carrying out activities that do not conform to the laws of physical and mental development and age characteristics of preschool children, or organizing preschool children to participate in commercial activities;  
(7) failing to equip kindergarten teachers or other staff in accordance with the provisions;  
(8) collecting fees in violation of regulations;  
(9) embezzling or misappropriating meal fees for preschool children.  
Where a kindergarten's school-running license is revoked in accordance with the preceding paragraph, it shall properly arrange for the children in the kindergarten.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　幼儿园教师或者其他工作人员有下列情形之一的，由所在幼儿园或者县级人民政府教育等有关部门根据情节轻重，依法给予当事人、幼儿园负责人处分，解除聘用合同或者劳动合同；由县级人民政府教育行政部门禁止其一定期限内直至终身从事学前教育工作或者举办幼儿园；情节严重的，吊销其资格证书：  
（一）体罚或者变相体罚儿童；  
（二）歧视、侮辱、虐待、性侵害儿童；  
（三）违反职业道德规范或者危害儿童身心安全，造成不良后果。

**Article 80** : Where a kindergarten teacher or other staff member falls under any of the following circumstances, the kindergarten where he or she works or the education and other relevant departments of the people's government at the county level shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose sanctions on the person concerned and the person in charge of the kindergarten according to law, and terminate the employment contract or labor contract; the education administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall prohibit him or her from engaging in preschool education work or establishing kindergartens for a certain period or even for life; if the circumstances are serious, his or her qualification certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) corporal punishment or disguised corporal punishment of children;  
(2) discrimination, humiliation, abuse, or sexual assault of children;  
(3) violation of professional ethics norms or endangering the physical and mental safety of children, causing adverse consequences.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　在学前教育活动中违反本法规定的行为，本法未规定法律责任，《中华人民共和国教育法》、《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》、《中华人民共和国劳动法》等法律、行政法规有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 81** : Any act that violates the provisions of this Law in preschool education activities, for which this Law does not provide legal liability, but which is provided for in laws and administrative regulations such as the Education Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Minors, and the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, shall be dealt with in accordance with those provisions.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，侵害学前儿童、幼儿园、教职工合法权益，造成人身损害或者财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** : Any violation of the provisions of this Law that infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of preschool children, kindergartens, or teaching and administrative staff, causing personal injury or property loss, shall bear civil liability according to law; if it constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　小学、特殊教育学校、儿童福利机构、残疾儿童康复机构等附设的幼儿班等学前教育机构适用本法有关规定。  
军队幼儿园的管理，依照本法和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 83** : Preschool education institutions such as preschool classes attached to primary schools, special education schools, child welfare institutions, and rehabilitation institutions for children with disabilities shall be subject to the relevant provisions of this Law.  
The management of kindergartens of the armed forces shall be carried out in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　鼓励有条件的幼儿园开设托班，提供托育服务。  
幼儿园提供托育服务的，依照有关法律法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 84** : Kindergartens with conditions are encouraged to set up nursery classes and provide nursery services.  
Where kindergartens provide nursery services, they shall comply with relevant laws, regulations, and state provisions.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法自2025年6月1日起施行。

**Article 85** : This Law shall come into force on June 1, 2025.
