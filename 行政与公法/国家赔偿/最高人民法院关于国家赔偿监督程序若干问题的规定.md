# 最高人民法院关于国家赔偿监督程序若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕9号  
**公布日期**：2017-02-27  
**生效日期**：2017-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于国家赔偿监督程序若干问题的规定》已于2017年2月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1711次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年5月1日起施行。 2017年4月20日 最高人民法院 关于国家赔偿监督程序若干问题的规定 法释〔2017〕9号 为了保障赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关的申诉权，规范国家赔偿监督程序，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》及有关法律规定，结合国家赔偿工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　依照国家赔偿法第三十条的规定，有下列情形之一的，适用本规定予以处理： （一）赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关认为赔偿委员会生效决定确有错误，向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉的； （二）赔偿委员会生效决定违反国家赔偿法规定，经本院院长决定或者上级人民法院指令重新审理，以及上级人民法院决定直接审理的； （三）最高人民检察院对各级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，上级人民检察院对下级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，发现违反国家赔偿法规定，向同级人民法院赔偿委员会提出重新审查意见的。 行政赔偿案件的审判监督依照行政诉讼法的相关规定执行。 第二条　赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关对赔偿委员会生效决定，认为确有错误的，可以向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉。申诉审查期间，不停止生效决定的执行。 第三条　赔偿委员会决定生效后，赔偿请求人死亡或者其主体资格终止的，其权利义务承继者可以依法提出申诉。 赔偿请求人死亡，依法享有继承权的同一顺序继承人有数人时，其中一人或者部分人申诉的，申诉效力及于全体；但是申请撤回申诉或者放弃赔偿请求的，效力不及于未明确表示撤回申诉或者放弃赔偿请求的其他继承人。 赔偿义务机关被撤销或者职权变更的，继续行使其职权的机关可以依法提出申诉。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　依照[国家赔偿法第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)的规定，有下列情形之一的，适用本规定予以处理：  
（一）赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关认为赔偿委员会生效决定确有错误，向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉的；  
（二）赔偿委员会生效决定违反国家赔偿法规定，经本院院长决定或者上级人民法院指令重新审理，以及上级人民法院决定直接审理的；  
（三）最高人民检察院对各级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，上级人民检察院对下级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，发现违反国家赔偿法规定，向同级人民法院赔偿委员会提出重新审查意见的。  
行政赔偿案件的审判监督依照行政诉讼法的相关规定执行。

**Article 1** In accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, these Provisions shall apply to the handling of any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where a claimant for compensation or an obligor for compensation believes that an effective decision of the compensation commission is indeed erroneous and files a petition with the compensation commission of the People's Court at the next higher level;  
(2) where an effective decision of the compensation commission violates the provisions of the State Compensation Law, and the president of the court decides to retry the case or the higher People's Court directs a retrial, or the higher People's Court decides to directly adjudicate the case;  
(3) where the Supreme People's Procuratorate discovers that an effective decision of the compensation commission of a People's Court at any level, or a higher People's Procuratorate discovers that an effective decision of the compensation commission of a lower People's Court, violates the provisions of the State Compensation Law, and submits an opinion on retrial review to the compensation commission of the People's Court at the same level.  
The trial supervision of administrative compensation cases shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关对赔偿委员会生效决定，认为确有错误的，可以向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉。申诉审查期间，不停止生效决定的执行。

**Article 2** If a claimant for compensation or an obligor for compensation believes that an effective decision of the compensation commission is indeed erroneous, it may file a petition with the compensation commission of the People's Court at the next higher level. During the period of petition review, the enforcement of the effective decision shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿委员会决定生效后，赔偿请求人死亡或者其主体资格终止的，其权利义务承继者可以依法提出申诉。  
赔偿请求人死亡，依法享有继承权的同一顺序继承人有数人时，其中一人或者部分人申诉的，申诉效力及于全体；但是申请撤回申诉或者放弃赔偿请求的，效力不及于未明确表示撤回申诉或者放弃赔偿请求的其他继承人。  
赔偿义务机关被撤销或者职权变更的，继续行使其职权的机关可以依法提出申诉。

**Article 3** After an effective decision of the compensation commission is made, if the claimant for compensation dies or its legal personality terminates, the successor of its rights and obligations may file a petition according to law.  
Where there are multiple heirs of the same order who are entitled to succession according to law after the death of the claimant for compensation, if one or part of them file a petition, the effect of the petition shall extend to all; however, if an application for withdrawal of the petition or waiver of the compensation claim is made, the effect shall not extend to other heirs who have not clearly expressed withdrawal of the petition or waiver of the compensation claim.  
Where the obligor for compensation is dissolved or its functions and powers are changed, the organ that continues to exercise its functions and powers may file a petition according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　赔偿请求人、法定代理人可以委托一至二人作为代理人代为申诉。申诉代理人的范围包括：  
（一）律师、基层法律服务工作者；  
（二）赔偿请求人的近亲属或者工作人员；  
（三）赔偿请求人所在社区、单位以及有关社会团体推荐的公民。  
赔偿义务机关可以委托本机关工作人员、法律顾问、律师一至二人代为申诉。

**Article 4** A claimant for compensation or a legal representative may appoint one or two persons as agents to act in the petition. The scope of petition agents includes:  
(1) lawyers and grassroots legal service workers;  
(2) close relatives or staff members of the claimant for compensation;  
(3) citizens recommended by the community, unit, or relevant social organization where the claimant for compensation is located.  
An obligor for compensation may appoint one or two of its own staff members, legal advisors, or lawyers as agents to act in the petition.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关申诉，应当提交以下材料：  
（一）申诉状。申诉状应当写明申诉人和被申诉人的基本信息，申诉的法定事由，以及具体的请求、事实和理由；书写申诉状确有困难的，可以口头申诉，由人民法院记入笔录。  
（二）身份证明及授权文书。赔偿请求人申诉的，自然人应当提交身份证明，法人或者其他组织应当提交营业执照、组织机构代码证书、法定代表人或者主要负责人身份证明；赔偿义务机关申诉的，应当提交法定代表人或者主要负责人身份证明；委托他人申诉的，应当提交授权委托书和代理人身份证明。  
（三）法律文书。即赔偿义务机关、复议机关及赔偿委员会作出的决定书等法律文书。  
（四）其他相关材料。以有新的证据证明原决定认定的事实确有错误为由提出申诉的，应当同时提交相关证据材料。  
申诉材料不符合前款规定的，人民法院应当一次性告知申诉人需要补正的全部内容及补正期限。补正期限一般为十五日，最长不超过一个月。申诉人对必要材料拒绝补正或者未能在规定期限内补正的，不予审查。收到申诉材料的时间自人民法院收到补正后的材料之日起计算。

**Article 5** A claimant for compensation or an obligor for compensation filing a petition shall submit the following materials:  
(1) A petition in writing. The petition shall state the basic information of the petitioner and the respondent, the statutory grounds for the petition, and the specific claims, facts, and reasons; if it is truly difficult to write a petition, an oral petition may be made, which shall be recorded in the minutes by the People's Court.  
(2) Identity certificate and authorization documents. Where a claimant for compensation files a petition, a natural person shall submit an identity certificate, and a legal person or other organization shall submit a business license, an organization code certificate, and an identity certificate of the legal representative or the principal responsible person; where an obligor for compensation files a petition, it shall submit the identity certificate of its legal representative or principal responsible person; where another person is entrusted to file the petition, a power of attorney and the agent's identity certificate shall be submitted.  
(3) Legal instruments, namely the decision letters and other legal instruments made by the obligor for compensation, the reconsideration organ, and the compensation commission.  
(4) Other relevant materials. If a petition is filed on the ground that there is new evidence proving that the facts determined in the original decision are indeed erroneous, the relevant evidentiary materials shall be submitted at the same time.  
If the petition materials do not comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall inform the petitioner at one time of all the contents to be supplemented and the time limit for supplementation. The time limit for supplementation shall generally be fifteen days, and shall not exceed one month at most. If the petitioner refuses to supplement the necessary materials or fails to do so within the prescribed time limit, the petition shall not be reviewed. The time for receiving the petition materials shall be calculated from the date when the People's Court receives the supplemented materials.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申诉符合下列条件的，人民法院应当在收到申诉材料之日起七日内予以立案：  
（一）申诉人具备本规定的主体资格；  
（二）受理申诉的人民法院是作出生效决定的人民法院的上一级人民法院；  
（三）提交的材料符合[本规定第五条](#art-5)的要求。  
申诉不符合上述规定的，人民法院不予受理并应当及时告知申诉人。

**Article 6** If a petition meets the following conditions, the People's Court shall place it on file within seven days from the date of receiving the petition materials:  
(1) The petitioner has the subject-matter qualification under these Provisions;  
(2) The People's Court accepting the petition is the People's Court at the next higher level of the People's Court that made the effective decision;  
(3) The submitted materials meet the requirements of Article 5 of these Provisions.  
If the petition does not meet the above conditions, the People's Court shall not accept it and shall promptly inform the petitioner.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关申诉，有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予受理：  
（一）赔偿委员会驳回申诉后，申诉人再次提出申诉的；  
（二）赔偿请求人对作为赔偿义务机关的人民法院作出的决定不服，未在法定期限内向其上一级人民法院赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，在赔偿义务机关的决定发生法律效力后直接向人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉的；  
（三）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关对最高人民法院赔偿委员会作出的决定不服提出申诉的；  
（四）赔偿请求人对行使侦查、检察职权的机关以及看守所主管机关、监狱管理机关作出的决定，未在法定期限内向其上一级机关申请复议，或者申请复议后复议机关逾期未作出决定或者复议机关已作出复议决定，但赔偿请求人未在法定期限内向复议机关所在地的同级人民法院赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，在赔偿义务机关、复议机关的相关决定生效后直接向人民法院赔偿委员会申诉的。

**Article 7** Where a claimant for compensation or an obligor for compensation files a petition under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not accept it:  
(1) The petitioner files a petition again after the compensation commission has dismissed the previous petition;  
(2) The claimant for compensation, dissatisfied with the decision made by the People's Court acting as the obligor for compensation, fails to apply to the compensation commission of the People's Court at the next higher level for a compensation decision within the statutory time limit, and directly files a petition with the compensation commission of the People's Court after the decision of the obligor for compensation takes legal effect;  
(3) The claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation, dissatisfied with a decision made by the compensation commission of the Supreme People's Court, files a petition;  
(4) The claimant for compensation, with respect to a decision made by an organ exercising the powers of investigation or prosecution, or by the competent authority of a detention house or a prison administration authority, fails to apply for reconsideration to its higher-level organ within the statutory time limit, or after applying for reconsideration, the reconsideration organ fails to make a decision within the time limit or has made a reconsideration decision, but the claimant for compensation fails to apply to the compensation commission of the People's Court at the same level as the reconsideration organ for a compensation decision within the statutory time limit, and directly files a petition with the compensation commission of the People's Court after the relevant decisions of the obligor for compensation or the reconsideration organ take effect.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　赔偿委员会对于立案受理的申诉案件，应当着重围绕申诉人的申诉事由进行审查。必要时，应当对原决定认定的事实、证据和适用法律进行全面审查。

**Article 8** The compensation commission shall, for a petition case that has been placed on file and accepted, focus its review on the grounds for the petition raised by the petitioner. Where necessary, it shall conduct a comprehensive review of the facts, evidence, and application of law in the original decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　赔偿委员会审查申诉案件采取书面审查的方式，根据需要可以听取申诉人和被申诉人的陈述和申辩。

**Article 9** The compensation commission shall review petition cases by means of documentary examination, and may, as needed, hear the statements and arguments of the petitioner and the respondent.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　赔偿委员会审查申诉案件，一般应当在三个月内作出处理，至迟不得超过六个月。有特殊情况需要延长的，由本院院长批准。

**Article 10** The compensation commission shall generally render a decision on a petition case within three months, and shall not exceed six months at the latest. If extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it shall be approved by the president of the court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一的，应当决定重新审理：  
（一）有新的证据，足以推翻原决定的；  
（二）原决定认定的基本事实缺乏证据证明的；  
（三）原决定认定事实的主要证据是伪造的；  
（四）原决定适用法律确有错误的；  
（五）原决定遗漏赔偿请求，且确实违反国家赔偿法规定的；  
（六）据以作出原决定的法律文书被撤销或者变更的；  
（七）审判人员在审理该案时有贪污受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁判行为的；  
（八）原审理程序违反法律规定，可能影响公正审理的。

**Article 11** A decision to retry the case shall be made under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) There is new evidence sufficient to overturn the original decision;  
(2) The basic facts found in the original decision lack evidentiary support;  
(3) The main evidence for the facts found in the original decision is forged;  
(4) The application of law in the original decision is indeed erroneous;  
(5) The original decision omitted a compensation claim and indeed violates the provisions of the State Compensation Law;  
(6) The legal instrument on which the original decision was based has been revoked or amended;  
(7) The adjudicative personnel engaged in acts of embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, or perverting the law in adjudicating the case;  
(8) The original adjudicative procedure violated legal provisions, potentially affecting fair adjudication.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申诉人在申诉阶段提供新的证据，应当说明逾期提供的理由。  
申诉人提供的新的证据，能够证明原决定认定的基本事实或者处理结果错误的，应当认定为[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一项规定的情形。

**Article 12** Where a petitioner provides new evidence at the petition stage, it shall state the reasons for the late submission.  
If the new evidence provided by the petitioner can prove that the basic facts found in the original decision or the outcome of the decision is erroneous, it shall be deemed as the circumstance provided in Article 11(1) of these Provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　赔偿委员会经审查，对申诉人的申诉按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）申诉人主张的重新审理事由成立，且符合国家赔偿法和本规定的申诉条件的，决定重新审理。重新审理包括上级人民法院赔偿委员会直接审理或者指令原审人民法院赔偿委员会重新审理。  
（二）申诉人主张的重新审理事由不成立，或者不符合国家赔偿法和本规定的申诉条件的，书面驳回申诉。  
（三）原决定不予受理或者驳回赔偿申请错误的，撤销原决定，指令原审人民法院赔偿委员会依法审理。

**Article 13** After review, the compensation commission shall handle the petitioner's petition according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the grounds for retrial asserted by the petitioner are established and meet the petition conditions under the State Compensation Law and these Provisions, a decision to retry the case shall be made. Retrial includes direct adjudication by the compensation commission of the higher People's Court or direction to the compensation commission of the original trial People's Court to retry the case.  
(2) If the grounds for retrial asserted by the petitioner are not established, or do not meet the petition conditions under the State Compensation Law and these Provisions, the petition shall be dismissed in writing.  
(3) If the original decision to reject the case or dismiss the compensation application is erroneous, the original decision shall be revoked, and the compensation commission of the original trial People's Court shall be directed to conduct adjudication according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院院长发现本院赔偿委员会生效决定违反国家赔偿法规定，认为需要重新审理的，应当提交审判委员会讨论决定。  
最高人民法院对各级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，上级人民法院对下级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，发现违反国家赔偿法规定的，有权决定直接审理或者指令下级人民法院赔偿委员会重新审理。

**Article 14** Where the president of a People's Court discovers that an effective decision of the compensation commission of that court violates the provisions of the State Compensation Law and considers that a retrial is necessary, the matter shall be submitted to the adjudicative committee for discussion and decision.  
Where the Supreme People's Court, with respect to effective decisions of the compensation commissions of People's Courts at all levels, or a higher People's Court, with respect to effective decisions of the compensation commission of a lower People's Court, discovers that such decisions violate the provisions of the State Compensation Law, it has the power to decide to directly adjudicate the case or to direct the compensation commission of the lower People's Court to retry the case.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　最高人民检察院对各级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，上级人民检察院对下级人民法院赔偿委员会生效决定，向同级人民法院赔偿委员会提出重新审查意见的，同级人民法院赔偿委员会应当决定直接审理，并将决定书送达提出意见的人民检察院。

**Article 15** Where the Supreme People's Procuratorate, with respect to effective decisions of the compensation commissions of People's Courts at all levels, or a higher People's Procuratorate, with respect to effective decisions of the compensation commission of a lower People's Court, submits an opinion on retrial review to the compensation commission of the People's Court at the same level, the compensation commission of the People's Court at the same level shall decide to directly adjudicate the case and shall serve the decision letter on the People's Procuratorate that submitted the opinion.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　赔偿委员会重新审理案件，适用国家赔偿法和相关司法解释关于赔偿委员会审理程序的规定；本规定依据国家赔偿法和相关法律对重新审理程序有特别规定的，适用本规定。  
原审人民法院赔偿委员会重新审理案件，应当另行指定审判人员。

**Article 16** Where the compensation commission retries a case, the provisions of the State Compensation Law and relevant judicial interpretations concerning the adjudicative procedure of the compensation commission shall apply; where these Provisions have special provisions on the retrial procedure in accordance with the State Compensation Law and relevant laws, these Provisions shall apply.  
Where the compensation commission of the original trial People's Court retries a case, it shall designate different adjudicative personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　决定重新审理的案件，可以根据案件情形中止原决定的执行。

**Article 17** For a case decided to be retried, the enforcement of the original decision may be suspended depending on the circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　赔偿委员会重新审理案件，采取书面审理的方式，必要时可以向有关单位和人员调查情况、收集证据，听取申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关的陈述和申辩。有[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一项、第三项情形，或者赔偿委员会认为确有必要的，可以组织申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关公开质证。  
对于人民检察院提出意见的案件，赔偿委员会组织质证时应当通知提出意见的人民检察院派员出席。

**Article 18** Where the compensation commission retries a case, it shall adopt the method of documentary adjudication, and may, when necessary, investigate circumstances and collect evidence from relevant entities and personnel, and hear the statements and arguments of the petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation. Where there are circumstances under Article 11(1) or (3) of these Provisions, or where the compensation commission considers it truly necessary, it may organize public cross-examination involving the petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation.  
For a case in which a People's Procuratorate has submitted an opinion, when the compensation commission organizes cross-examination, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate that submitted the opinion to send a representative to attend.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　赔偿委员会重新审理案件，应当对原决定认定的事实、证据和适用法律进行全面审理。

**Article 19** Where the compensation commission retries a case, it shall conduct a comprehensive adjudication of the facts, evidence, and application of law in the original decision.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　赔偿委员会重新审理的案件，应当在两个月内依法作出决定。

**Article 20** A case retried by the compensation commission shall be decided according to law within two months.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　案件经重新审理后，应当根据下列情形分别处理：  
（一）原决定认定事实清楚、适用法律正确的，应当维持原决定；  
（二）原决定认定事实、适用法律虽有瑕疵，但决定结果正确的，应当在决定中纠正瑕疵后予以维持；  
（三）原决定认定事实、适用法律错误，导致决定结果错误的，应当撤销、变更、重新作出决定；  
（四）原决定违反国家赔偿法规定，对不符合案件受理条件的赔偿申请进行实体处理的，应当撤销原决定，驳回赔偿申请；  
（五）申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关经协商达成协议的，赔偿委员会依法审查并确认后，应当撤销原决定，根据协议作出新决定。

**Article 21** After a case is retried, it shall be handled according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the facts found in the original decision are clear and the application of law is correct, the original decision shall be affirmed;  
(2) If the facts found or the application of law in the original decision has defects, but the result of the decision is correct, the defects shall be corrected in the decision and the original decision shall be affirmed;  
(3) If the facts found or the application of law in the original decision is erroneous, resulting in an erroneous result of the decision, the original decision shall be revoked, modified, or a new decision shall be made;  
(4) If the original decision violates the provisions of the State Compensation Law by conducting substantive handling of a compensation application that does not meet the case acceptance conditions, the original decision shall be revoked and the compensation application shall be dismissed;  
(5) If the petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation reach an agreement through consultation, after the compensation commission reviews and confirms the agreement according to law, it shall revoke the original decision and make a new decision based on the agreement.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　赔偿委员会重新审理后作出的决定，应当及时送达申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和提出意见的人民检察院。

**Article 22** The decision made by the compensation commission after a retrial shall be promptly served on the petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation, and on the People's Procuratorate that submitted the opinion.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在申诉审查或者重新审理期间，有下列情形之一的，赔偿委员会应当决定中止审查或者审理：  
（一）申诉人、被申诉人或者原赔偿请求人、原赔偿义务机关死亡或者终止，尚未确定权利义务承继者的；  
（二）申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人丧失行为能力，尚未确定法定代理人的；  
（三）宣告无罪的案件，人民法院决定再审或者人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的；  
（四）申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关因不可抗拒的事由，在法定审限内不能参加案件处理的；  
（五）其他应当中止的情形。  
中止的原因消除后，赔偿委员会应当及时恢复审查或者审理，并通知申诉人、被申诉人或者赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和提出意见的人民检察院。

**Article 23** During the period of petition review or retrial, under any of the following circumstances, the compensation commission shall decide to suspend the review or adjudication:  
(1) The petitioner, the respondent, or the original claimant for compensation or the original obligor for compensation dies or terminates, and the successor of rights and obligations has not yet been determined;  
(2) The petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation loses capacity for civil conduct, and a legal representative has not yet been determined;  
(3) In a case where an acquittal judgment has been made, the People's Court decides to retry the case or the People's Procuratorate files a protest under the trial supervision procedure;  
(4) The petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation is unable to participate in the case proceedings within the statutory adjudication period due to force majeure;  
(5) Other circumstances where suspension should occur.  
After the cause for suspension is eliminated, the compensation commission shall promptly resume the review or adjudication and notify the petitioner, the respondent, or the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation, and the People's Procuratorate that submitted the opinion.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在申诉审查期间，有下列情形之一的，赔偿委员会应当决定终结审查：  
（一）申诉人死亡或者终止，无权利义务承继者或者权利义务承继者声明放弃申诉的；  
（二）据以申请赔偿的撤销案件决定、不起诉决定或者无罪判决被撤销的；  
（三）其他应当终结的情形。  
在重新审理期间，有上述情形或者人民检察院撤回意见的，赔偿委员会应当决定终结审理。

**Article 24** During the period of petition review, under any of the following circumstances, the compensation commission shall decide to terminate the review:  
(1) The petitioner dies or terminates, and there is no successor of rights and obligations, or the successor of rights and obligations declares waiver of the petition;  
(2) The decision to dismiss the case, the decision not to prosecute, or the acquittal judgment on which the compensation application was based is revoked;  
(3) Other circumstances where termination should occur.  
During the period of retrial, if any of the above circumstances occurs or if the People's Procuratorate withdraws its opinion, the compensation commission shall decide to terminate the adjudication.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　申诉人在申诉审查或者重新审理期间申请撤回申诉的，赔偿委员会应当依法审查并作出是否准许的决定。  
赔偿委员会准许撤回申诉后，申诉人又重复申诉的，不予受理，但有[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一项、第三项、第六项、第七项规定情形，自知道或者应当知道该情形之日起六个月内提出的除外。

**Article 25** Where a petitioner applies to withdraw the petition during the period of petition review or retrial, the compensation commission shall review the application according to law and make a decision on whether to grant permission.  
After the compensation commission grants permission to withdraw the petition, if the petitioner files a repeated petition, it shall not be accepted, except where there are circumstances under Article 11(1), (3), (6), or (7) of these Provisions and the petition is filed within six months from the date when the petitioner knows or should have known of such circumstances.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　赔偿请求人在重新审理期间申请撤回赔偿申请的，赔偿委员会应当依法审查并作出是否准许的决定。准许撤回赔偿申请的，应当一并撤销原决定。  
赔偿委员会准许撤回赔偿申请的决定送达后，赔偿请求人又重复申请国家赔偿的，不予受理。

**Article 26** Where a claimant for compensation applies to withdraw the compensation application during the period of retrial, the compensation commission shall review the application according to law and make a decision on whether to grant permission. If permission to withdraw the compensation application is granted, the original decision shall be revoked at the same time.  
After the decision of the compensation commission to grant permission to withdraw the compensation application is served, if the claimant for compensation files a repeated application for state compensation, it shall not be accepted.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本规定自2017 年 5月 1日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释和规范性文件，与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 27** These Provisions shall come into force as of May 1, 2017. Where any judicial interpretations and normative documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
