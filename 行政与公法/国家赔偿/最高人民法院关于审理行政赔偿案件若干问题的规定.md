# 最高人民法院关于审理行政赔偿案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕10号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-06  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理行政赔偿案件若干问题的规定》已于2021年12月6日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1855次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年5月1日起施行。 2022年3月20日 最高人民法院 关于审理行政赔偿案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2022〕10号 为保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，监督行政机关依法履行行政赔偿义务，确保人民法院公正、及时审理行政赔偿案件，实质化解行政赔偿争议，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法）《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》（以下简称国家赔偿法）等法律规定，结合行政审判工作实际，制定本规定。 一、受案范围 第一条　国家赔偿法第三条、第四条规定的“其他违法行为”包括以下情形： （一）不履行法定职责行为； （二）行政机关及其工作人员在履行行政职责过程中作出的不产生法律效果，但事实上损害公民、法人或者其他组织人身权、财产权等合法权益的行为。 第二条　依据行政诉讼法第一条、第十二条第一款第十二项和国家赔偿法第二条规定，公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关及其工作人员违法行使行政职权对其劳动权、相邻权等合法权益造成人身、财产损害的，可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼。 第三条　赔偿请求人不服赔偿义务机关下列行为的，可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼： （一）确定赔偿方式、项目、数额的行政赔偿决定； （二）不予赔偿决定； （三）逾期不作出赔偿决定；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[国家赔偿法第三条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-3)、第四条规定的“其他违法行为”包括以下情形：  
（一）不履行法定职责行为；  
（二）行政机关及其工作人员在履行行政职责过程中作出的不产生法律效果，但事实上损害公民、法人或者其他组织人身权、财产权等合法权益的行为。

**Article 1** The "other illegal acts" specified in Article 3 and Article 4 of the State Compensation Law include the following circumstances:  
(1) Acts of failing to perform statutory duties;  
(2) Acts performed by administrative organs and their staff in the course of performing administrative duties that do not produce legal effects but actually damage the personal rights, property rights and other lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons or other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　依据[行政诉讼法第一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-1)、第十二条第一款第十二项和[国家赔偿法第二条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-2)规定，公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关及其工作人员违法行使行政职权对其劳动权、相邻权等合法权益造成人身、财产损害的，可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼。

**Article 2** Pursuant to the provisions of Article 1, subparagraph (12) of the first paragraph of Article 12 of the Administrative Procedure Law and Article 2 of the State Compensation Law, if a citizen, legal person or other organization believes that an administrative organ and its staff have illegally exercised administrative powers and caused personal or property damage to their lawful rights and interests such as the right to labor and the right of neighboring relationship, they may, according to law, initiate an administrative compensation action.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿请求人不服赔偿义务机关下列行为的，可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼：  
（一）确定赔偿方式、项目、数额的行政赔偿决定；  
（二）不予赔偿决定；  
（三）逾期不作出赔偿决定；  
（四）其他有关行政赔偿的行为。

**Article 3** If a compensation claimant refuses to accept the following acts of the organ liable for compensation, they may, according to law, initiate an administrative compensation action:  
(1) An administrative compensation decision determining the method, items and amount of compensation;  
(2) A decision of non-compensation;  
(3) Failure to make a compensation decision within the statutory time limit;  
(4) Other acts relating to administrative compensation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　法律规定由行政机关最终裁决的行政行为被确认违法后，赔偿请求人可以单独提起行政赔偿诉讼。

**Article 4** After an administrative act that is subject to final adjudication by an administrative organ according to law is confirmed as illegal, the compensation claimant may separately initiate an administrative compensation action.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民、法人或者其他组织认为国防、外交等国家行为或者行政机关制定发布行政法规、规章或者具有普遍约束力的决定、命令侵犯其合法权益造成损害，向人民法院提起行政赔偿诉讼的，不属于人民法院行政赔偿诉讼的受案范围。  
二、诉讼当事人

**Article 5** If a citizen, legal person or other organization believes that national acts such as national defense and foreign affairs, or administrative regulations, rules, or decisions and orders with universal binding force formulated and issued by administrative organs infringe upon their lawful rights and interests and cause damage, and initiates an administrative compensation action with a People's Court, such action shall not fall within the scope of acceptance of administrative compensation actions of the People's Courts.  
II. Litigation Parties

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公民、法人或者其他组织一并提起行政赔偿诉讼中的当事人地位，按照其在行政诉讼中的地位确定，行政诉讼与行政赔偿诉讼当事人不一致的除外。

**Article 6** The status of the parties in an administrative compensation action initiated together with an administrative action shall be determined according to their status in the administrative action, unless the parties in the administrative action and the administrative compensation action are inconsistent.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　受害的公民死亡，其继承人和其他有扶养关系的人可以提起行政赔偿诉讼，并提供该公民死亡证明、赔偿请求人与死亡公民之间的关系证明。  
受害的公民死亡，支付受害公民医疗费、丧葬费等合理费用的人可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼。  
有权提起行政赔偿诉讼的法人或者其他组织分立、合并、终止，承受其权利的法人或者其他组织可以依法提起行政赔偿诉讼。

**Article 7** If the injured citizen dies, his or her successors and other persons who have a dependency relationship with him or her may initiate an administrative compensation action, and shall provide the death certificate of the citizen and the proof of the relationship between the compensation claimant and the deceased citizen.  
If the injured citizen dies, a person who has paid reasonable expenses such as medical expenses and funeral expenses for the injured citizen may, according to law, initiate an administrative compensation action.  
If a legal person or other organization entitled to initiate an administrative compensation action is divided, merged, or terminated, the legal person or other organization that succeeds to its rights may, according to law, initiate an administrative compensation action.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　两个以上行政机关共同实施侵权行政行为造成损害的，共同侵权行政机关为共同被告。赔偿请求人坚持对其中一个或者几个侵权机关提起行政赔偿诉讼，以被起诉的机关为被告，未被起诉的机关追加为第三人。

**Article 8** If two or more administrative organs jointly commit a tortious administrative act causing damage, the jointly tortious administrative organs shall be co-defendants. If the compensation claimant insists on initiating an administrative compensation action against only one or several of the tortious organs, the sued organ shall be the defendant, and the unsued organ shall be added as a third party.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　原行政行为造成赔偿请求人损害，复议决定加重损害的，复议机关与原行政行为机关为共同被告。赔偿请求人坚持对作出原行政行为机关或者复议机关提起行政赔偿诉讼，以被起诉的机关为被告，未被起诉的机关追加为第三人。

**Article 9** If the original administrative act causes damage to the compensation claimant, and the reconsideration decision aggravates the damage, the reconsideration organ and the organ that made the original administrative act shall be co-defendants. If the compensation claimant insists on initiating an administrative compensation action against either the organ that made the original administrative act or the reconsideration organ, the sued organ shall be the defendant, and the unsued organ shall be added as a third party.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　行政机关依据[行政诉讼法第九十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-97)的规定申请人民法院强制执行其行政行为，因据以强制执行的行政行为违法而发生行政赔偿诉讼的，申请强制执行的行政机关为被告。  
三、证据

**Article 10** If an administrative organ applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution of its administrative act pursuant to the provisions of Article 97 of the Administrative Procedure Law, and an administrative compensation action arises because the administrative act based on which compulsory execution is sought is illegal, the administrative organ that applied for compulsory execution shall be the defendant.  
III. Evidence

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政赔偿诉讼中，原告应当对行政行为造成的损害提供证据；因被告的原因导致原告无法举证的，由被告承担举证责任。  
人民法院对于原告主张的生产和生活所必需物品的合理损失，应当予以支持；对于原告提出的超出生产和生活所必需的其他贵重物品、现金损失，可以结合案件相关证据予以认定。

**Article 11** In an administrative compensation action, the plaintiff shall provide evidence for the damage caused by the administrative act; if, due to the defendant's reasons, the plaintiff is unable to adduce evidence, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.  
The People's Court shall support the reasonable losses of articles necessary for production and livelihood claimed by the plaintiff; for other valuable articles and cash losses claimed by the plaintiff that exceed what is necessary for production and livelihood, the People's Court may determine them in light of the relevant evidence in the case.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　原告主张其被限制人身自由期间受到身体伤害，被告否认相关损害事实或者损害与违法行政行为存在因果关系的，被告应当提供相应的证据证明。  
四、起诉与受理

**Article 12** If the plaintiff claims that he or she suffered bodily harm during the period of restriction of personal freedom, and the defendant denies the facts of the relevant damage or the causal relationship between the damage and the illegal administrative act, the defendant shall provide corresponding evidence to prove.  
IV. Prosecution and Acceptance

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　行政行为未被确认为违法，公民、法人或者其他组织提起行政赔偿诉讼的，人民法院应当视为提起行政诉讼时一并提起行政赔偿诉讼。  
行政行为已被确认为违法，并符合下列条件的，公民、法人或者其他组织可以单独提起行政赔偿诉讼：  
（一）原告具有行政赔偿请求资格；  
（二）有明确的被告；  
（三）有具体的赔偿请求和受损害的事实根据；  
（四）赔偿义务机关已先行处理或者超过法定期限不予处理；  
（五）属于人民法院行政赔偿诉讼的受案范围和受诉人民法院管辖；  
（六）在法律规定的起诉期限内提起诉讼。

**Article 13** If an administrative act has not been confirmed as illegal, and a citizen, legal person or other organization initiates an administrative compensation action, the People's Court shall deem that the administrative compensation action is initiated together with the administrative action.  
If an administrative act has been confirmed as illegal and meets the following conditions, a citizen, legal person or other organization may separately initiate an administrative compensation action:  
(1) The plaintiff has the qualification to claim administrative compensation;  
(2) There is a specific defendant;  
(3) There are specific compensation claims and factual basis for the damage;  
(4) The organ liable for compensation has already handled the matter first or has failed to handle it beyond the statutory time limit;  
(5) It falls within the scope of acceptance of administrative compensation actions of the People's Courts and the jurisdiction of the court receiving the action;  
(6) The action is initiated within the time limit for prosecution prescribed by law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　原告提起行政诉讼时未一并提起行政赔偿诉讼，人民法院审查认为可能存在行政赔偿的，应当告知原告可以一并提起行政赔偿诉讼。  
原告在第一审庭审终结前提起行政赔偿诉讼，符合起诉条件的，人民法院应当依法受理；原告在第一审庭审终结后、宣判前提起行政赔偿诉讼的，是否准许由人民法院决定。  
原告在第二审程序或者再审程序中提出行政赔偿请求的，人民法院可以组织各方调解；调解不成的，告知其另行起诉。

**Article 14** If the plaintiff does not initiate an administrative compensation action together with the administrative action, and the People's Court upon examination considers that administrative compensation may exist, it shall inform the plaintiff that the plaintiff may initiate an administrative compensation action together.  
If the plaintiff initiates an administrative compensation action before the conclusion of the court hearing of first instance and meets the conditions for prosecution, the People's Court shall accept it according to law; if the plaintiff initiates an administrative compensation action after the conclusion of the court hearing of first instance but before the pronouncement of judgment, whether to permit it shall be decided by the People's Court.  
If the plaintiff raises an administrative compensation claim during the second instance proceedings or retrial proceedings, the People's Court may organize mediation among the parties; if mediation fails, it shall inform the plaintiff to initiate a separate action.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公民、法人或者其他组织应当自知道或者应当知道行政行为侵犯其合法权益之日起两年内，向赔偿义务机关申请行政赔偿。赔偿义务机关在收到赔偿申请之日起两个月内未作出赔偿决定的，公民、法人或者其他组织可以依照行政诉讼法有关规定提起行政赔偿诉讼。

**Article 15** A citizen, legal person or other organization shall, within two years from the date on which they know or should have known that the administrative act infringes upon their lawful rights and interests, apply to the organ liable for compensation for administrative compensation. If the organ liable for compensation fails to make a compensation decision within two months from the date of receipt of the compensation application, the citizen, legal person or other organization may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law, initiate an administrative compensation action.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公民、法人或者其他组织提起行政诉讼时一并请求行政赔偿的，适用行政诉讼法有关起诉期限的规定。

**Article 16** If a citizen, legal person or other organization requests administrative compensation together with the initiation of an administrative action, the provisions on the time limit for prosecution in the Administrative Procedure Law shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公民、法人或者其他组织仅对行政复议决定中的行政赔偿部分有异议，自复议决定书送达之日起十五日内提起行政赔偿诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
行政机关作出有赔偿内容的行政复议决定时，未告知公民、法人或者其他组织起诉期限的，起诉期限从公民、法人或者其他组织知道或者应当知道起诉期限之日起计算，但从知道或者应当知道行政复议决定内容之日起最长不得超过一年。

**Article 17** If a citizen, legal person or other organization has objections only to the administrative compensation part of an administrative reconsideration decision, and initiates an administrative compensation action within fifteen days from the date of service of the reconsideration decision document, the People's Court shall accept it according to law.  
If, when making an administrative reconsideration decision that contains compensation content, the administrative organ fails to inform the citizen, legal person or other organization of the time limit for prosecution, the time limit for prosecution shall be calculated from the date on which the citizen, legal person or other organization knows or should have known the time limit for prosecution, but the maximum period from the date on which they know or should have known the content of the administrative reconsideration decision shall not exceed one year.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行政行为被有权机关依照法定程序撤销、变更、确认违法或无效，或者实施行政行为的行政机关工作人员因该行为被生效法律文书或监察机关政务处分确认为渎职、滥用职权的，属于本规定所称的行政行为被确认为违法的情形。

**Article 18** If an administrative act is revoked, changed, or confirmed as illegal or invalid by a competent authority in accordance with statutory procedures, or if an administrative organ staff member who implemented the administrative act is confirmed as having engaged in malfeasance or abuse of power by an effective legal instrument or a disciplinary action of the supervisory authority, such circumstances shall be considered as the administrative act being confirmed as illegal as referred to in these Provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公民、法人或者其他组织一并提起行政赔偿诉讼，人民法院经审查认为行政诉讼不符合起诉条件的，对一并提起的行政赔偿诉讼，裁定不予立案；已经立案的，裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 19** If a citizen, legal person or other organization initiates an administrative compensation action together with an administrative action, and the People's Court upon examination considers that the administrative action does not meet the conditions for prosecution, it shall rule that the administrative compensation action initiated together is not to be placed on file; if it has already been placed on file, it shall rule to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在涉及行政许可、登记、征收、征用和行政机关对民事争议所作的裁决的行政案件中，原告提起行政赔偿诉讼的同时，有关当事人申请一并解决相关民事争议的，人民法院可以一并审理。  
五、审理和判决

**Article 20** In an administrative case involving administrative license, registration, expropriation, requisition, and rulings made by administrative organs on civil disputes, if the plaintiff initiates an administrative compensation action and at the same time the relevant parties apply for the joint resolution of related civil disputes, the People's Court may hear them together.  
V. Trial and Judgment

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　两个以上行政机关共同实施违法行政行为，或者行政机关及其工作人员与第三人恶意串通作出的违法行政行为，造成公民、法人或者其他组织人身权、财产权等合法权益实际损害的，应当承担连带赔偿责任。  
一方承担连带赔偿责任后，对于超出其应当承担部分，可以向其他连带责任人追偿。

**Article 21** If two or more administrative organs jointly implement an illegal administrative act, or an administrative organ and its staff maliciously collude with a third party to make an illegal administrative act, causing actual damage to the personal rights, property rights and other lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person or other organization, they shall bear joint and several compensation liability.  
After one party bears joint and several compensation liability, it may claim recourse against the other jointly and severally liable persons for the portion exceeding its own share.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　两个以上行政机关分别实施违法行政行为造成同一损害，每个行政机关的违法行为都足以造成全部损害的，各个行政机关承担连带赔偿责任。  
两个以上行政机关分别实施违法行政行为造成同一损害的，人民法院应当根据其违法行政行为在损害发生和结果中的作用大小，确定各自承担相应的行政赔偿责任；难以确定责任大小的，平均承担责任。

**Article 22** If two or more administrative organs separately implement illegal administrative acts causing the same damage, and each administrative organ's illegal act is sufficient to cause the entire damage, each administrative organ shall bear joint and several compensation liability.  
If two or more administrative organs separately implement illegal administrative acts causing the same damage, the People's Court shall, based on the role of each illegal administrative act in the occurrence and outcome of the damage, determine the respective administrative compensation liability; if the proportion of liability is difficult to determine, they shall bear liability equally.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　由于第三人提供虚假材料，导致行政机关作出的行政行为违法，造成公民、法人或者其他组织损害的，人民法院应当根据违法行政行为在损害发生和结果中的作用大小，确定行政机关承担相应的行政赔偿责任；行政机关已经尽到审慎审查义务的，不承担行政赔偿责任。

**Article 23** If, due to a third party providing false materials, an administrative act made by an administrative organ is illegal, causing damage to a citizen, legal person or other organization, the People's Court shall, based on the role of the illegal administrative act in the occurrence and outcome of the damage, determine that the administrative organ bears corresponding administrative compensation liability; if the administrative organ has fulfilled its duty of careful review, it shall not bear administrative compensation liability.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　由于第三人行为造成公民、法人或者其他组织损害的，应当由第三人依法承担侵权赔偿责任；第三人赔偿不足、无力承担赔偿责任或者下落不明，行政机关又未尽保护、监管、救助等法定义务的，人民法院应当根据行政机关未尽法定义务在损害发生和结果中的作用大小，确定其承担相应的行政赔偿责任。

**Article 24** If damage is caused to a citizen, legal person or other organization by the act of a third party, the third party shall bear tort compensation liability according to law; if the third party's compensation is insufficient, is unable to bear compensation liability, or is whereabouts unknown, and the administrative organ has failed to fulfill its statutory duties of protection, supervision, rescue, etc., the People's Court shall, based on the role of the administrative organ's failure to fulfill its statutory duties in the occurrence and outcome of the damage, determine that it bears corresponding administrative compensation liability.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　由于不可抗力等客观原因造成公民、法人或者其他组织损害，行政机关不依法履行、拖延履行法定义务导致未能及时止损或者损害扩大的，人民法院应当根据行政机关不依法履行、拖延履行法定义务行为在损害发生和结果中的作用大小，确定其承担相应的行政赔偿责任。

**Article 25** If damage is caused to a citizen, legal person or other organization due to objective reasons such as force majeure, and the administrative organ's failure to perform its statutory duties according to law or delay in performing such duties results in failure to stop losses in time or expansion of damage, the People's Court shall, based on the role of the administrative organ's failure to perform or delay in performing its statutory duties in the occurrence and outcome of the damage, determine that it bears corresponding administrative compensation liability.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有下列情形之一的，属于[国家赔偿法第三十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-35)规定的“造成严重后果”：  
（一）受害人被非法限制人身自由超过六个月；  
（二）受害人经鉴定为轻伤以上或者残疾；  
（三）受害人经诊断、鉴定为精神障碍或者精神残疾，且与违法行政行为存在关联；  
（四）受害人名誉、荣誉、家庭、职业、教育等方面遭受严重损害，且与违法行政行为存在关联。  
有下列情形之一的，可以认定为后果特别严重：  
（一）受害人被限制人身自由十年以上；  
（二）受害人死亡；  
（三）受害人经鉴定为重伤或者残疾一至四级，且生活不能自理；  
（四）受害人经诊断、鉴定为严重精神障碍或者精神残疾一至二级，生活不能自理，且与违法行政行为存在关联。

**Article 26** The following circumstances shall be considered as 'causing serious consequences' as provided in Article 35 of the State Compensation Law:  
(1) The victim is illegally restricted in personal freedom for more than six months;  
(2) The victim is determined through expert evaluation to have minor injuries or above, or to be disabled;  
(3) The victim is diagnosed or evaluated as having a mental disorder or mental disability, and there is a connection with the illegal administrative act;  
(4) The victim suffers serious damage in reputation, honor, family, occupation, education, etc., and there is a connection with the illegal administrative act.  
The following circumstances may be deemed as particularly serious consequences:  
(1) The victim is restricted in personal freedom for more than ten years;  
(2) The victim dies;  
(3) The victim is determined through expert evaluation to have serious injuries or disability of level one to four, and is unable to take care of themselves;  
(4) The victim is diagnosed or evaluated as having a serious mental disorder or mental disability of level one to two, is unable to take care of themselves, and there is a connection with the illegal administrative act.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违法行政行为造成公民、法人或者其他组织财产损害，不能返还财产或者恢复原状的，按照损害发生时该财产的市场价格计算损失。市场价格无法确定，或者该价格不足以弥补公民、法人或者其他组织损失的，可以采用其他合理方式计算。  
违法征收征用土地、房屋，人民法院判决给予被征收人的行政赔偿，不得少于被征收人依法应当获得的安置补偿权益。

**Article 27** If an illegal administrative act causes damage to the property of a citizen, legal person or other organization, and the property cannot be returned or restored to its original state, the loss shall be calculated according to the market price of the property at the time of the damage. If the market price cannot be determined, or if such price is insufficient to compensate the loss of the citizen, legal person or other organization, other reasonable methods may be adopted for calculation.  
In case of illegal expropriation or requisition of land or houses, the administrative compensation awarded by the People's Court to the person whose property is expropriated shall not be less than the resettlement compensation rights that the person should have obtained according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　下列损失属于[国家赔偿法第三十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-36)第六项规定的“停产停业期间必要的经常性费用开支”：  
（一）必要留守职工的工资；  
（二）必须缴纳的税款、社会保险费；  
（三）应当缴纳的水电费、保管费、仓储费、承包费；  
（四）合理的房屋场地租金、设备租金、设备折旧费；  
（五）维系停产停业期间运营所需的其他基本开支。

**Article 28** The following losses shall be considered as 'necessary recurring expenses during the period of cessation of production and business operations' as provided in subparagraph (6) of Article 36 of the State Compensation Law:  
(1) Wages of necessary on-site employees;  
(2) Taxes and social insurance premiums that must be paid;  
(3) Utility fees, storage fees, warehousing fees, and contracting fees that should be paid;  
(4) Reasonable rent for premises and equipment, and equipment depreciation expenses;  
(5) Other basic expenses necessary to maintain operations during the period of cessation of production and business operations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　下列损失属于[国家赔偿法第三十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-36)第八项规定的“直接损失”：  
（一）存款利息、贷款利息、现金利息；  
（二）机动车停运期间的营运损失；  
（三）通过行政补偿程序依法应当获得的奖励、补贴等；  
（四）对财产造成的其他实际损失。

**Article 29** The following losses shall be considered as 'direct losses' as provided in subparagraph (8) of Article 36 of the State Compensation Law:  
(1) Interest on deposits, loans, and cash;  
(2) Operating losses during the period when motor vehicles are out of service;  
(3) Rewards, subsidies, etc., that should have been obtained according to law through administrative compensation procedures;  
(4) Other actual losses caused to property.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被告有[国家赔偿法第三条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-3)规定情形之一，致人精神损害的，人民法院应当判决其在违法行政行为影响的范围内，为受害人消除影响、恢复名誉、赔礼道歉；消除影响、恢复名誉和赔礼道歉的履行方式，可以双方协商，协商不成的，人民法院应当责令被告以适当的方式履行。造成严重后果的，应当判决支付相应的精神损害抚慰金。

**Article 30** If the defendant is in one of the circumstances specified in Article 3 of the State Compensation Law and causes moral damage to a person, the People's Court shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to, within the scope of influence of the illegal administrative act, eliminate the impact, restore the victim's reputation, and make an apology. The methods of fulfilling the elimination of impact, restoration of reputation, and apology may be negotiated by both parties; if negotiation fails, the People's Court shall order the defendant to fulfill them in an appropriate manner. If serious consequences are caused, the court shall render a judgment ordering payment of corresponding moral damage consolation payments.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院经过审理认为被告对公民、法人或者其他组织造成财产损害的，判决被告限期返还财产、恢复原状；无法返还财产、恢复原状的，判决被告限期支付赔偿金和相应的利息损失。  
人民法院审理行政赔偿案件，可以对行政机关赔偿的方式、项目、标准等予以明确，赔偿内容确定的，应当作出具有赔偿金额等给付内容的判决；行政赔偿决定对赔偿数额的确定确有错误的，人民法院判决予以变更。

**Article 31** If after trial the People's Court finds that the defendant has caused property damage to a citizen, legal person or other organization, it shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to return the property and restore it to its original state within a time limit; if the property cannot be returned or restored, it shall render a judgment ordering the defendant to pay compensation and corresponding interest losses within a time limit.  
When trying an administrative compensation case, the People's Court may clarify the methods, items, standards, etc., of compensation by the administrative organ; if the compensation content is determined, it shall render a judgment containing payment content such as the amount of compensation; if the administrative compensation decision is indeed erroneous in determining the amount of compensation, the People's Court shall render a judgment to modify it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院判决驳回原告的行政赔偿请求：  
（一）原告主张的损害没有事实根据的；  
（二）原告主张的损害与违法行政行为没有因果关系的；  
（三）原告的损失已经通过行政补偿等其他途径获得充分救济的；  
（四）原告请求行政赔偿的理由不能成立的其他情形。  
六、其他

**Article 32** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall render a judgment to dismiss the plaintiff's administrative compensation claim:  
(1) The damage claimed by the plaintiff has no factual basis;  
(2) The damage claimed by the plaintiff has no causal relationship with the illegal administrative act;  
(3) The plaintiff's losses have already been fully remedied through other channels such as administrative compensation;  
(4) Other circumstances where the grounds for the plaintiff's request for administrative compensation are untenable.  
VI. Miscellaneous

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本规定自2022年5月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理行政赔偿案件若干问题的规定》（法发〔1997〕10号）同时废止。  
本规定实施前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 33** These Provisions shall come into force as of May 1, 2022. The Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Trial of Administrative Compensation Cases (Fa Fa [1997] No. 10) shall be abolished at the same time.  
If any judicial interpretation issued by this Court before the implementation of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
