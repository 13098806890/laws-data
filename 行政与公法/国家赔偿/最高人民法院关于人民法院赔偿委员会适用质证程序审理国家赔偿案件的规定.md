# 最高人民法院关于人民法院赔偿委员会适用质证程序审理国家赔偿案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕27号  
**公布日期**：2013-12-16  
**生效日期**：2014-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院赔偿委员会适用质证程序审理国家赔偿案件的规定》已于2013年12月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1600次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年3月1日起施行。 2013年12月19日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院赔偿委员会适用质证程序 审理国家赔偿案件的规定 法释〔2013〕27号 为规范人民法院赔偿委员会（以下简称赔偿委员会）适用质证程序审理国家赔偿案件，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》等有关法律规定，结合国家赔偿工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条   赔偿委员会根据国家赔偿法第二十七条的规定，听取赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关的陈述和申辩，进行质证的，适用本规定。 第二条   有下列情形之一，经书面审理不能解决的，赔偿委员会可以组织赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关进行质证： （一）对侵权事实、损害后果及因果关系有争议的； （二）对是否属于国家赔偿法第十九条规定的国家不承担赔偿责任的情形有争议的； （三）对赔偿方式、赔偿项目或者赔偿数额有争议的； （四）赔偿委员会认为应当质证的其他情形。 第三条   除涉及国家秘密、个人隐私或者法律另有规定的以外，质证应当公开进行。 赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关申请不公开质证，对方同意的，赔偿委员会可以不公开质证。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   赔偿委员会根据[国家赔偿法第二十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-27)的规定，听取赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关的陈述和申辩，进行质证的，适用本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply where the compensation committee, in accordance with Article 27 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, hears the statements and arguments of the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation and conducts cross-examination.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   有下列情形之一，经书面审理不能解决的，赔偿委员会可以组织赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关进行质证：  
（一）对侵权事实、损害后果及因果关系有争议的；  
（二）对是否属于[国家赔偿法第十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-19)规定的国家不承担赔偿责任的情形有争议的；  
（三）对赔偿方式、赔偿项目或者赔偿数额有争议的；  
（四）赔偿委员会认为应当质证的其他情形。

**Article 2** Where any of the following circumstances exists and the matter cannot be resolved through written adjudication, the compensation committee may organize the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation to conduct cross-examination:  
(1) There is a dispute over the facts of infringement, the consequences of damage, and the causal relationship;  
(2) There is a dispute over whether the circumstances fall under those for which the State does not bear compensation liability as prescribed in Article 19 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) There is a dispute over the method, items, or amount of compensation;  
(4) Other circumstances for which the compensation committee deems cross-examination necessary.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   除涉及国家秘密、个人隐私或者法律另有规定的以外，质证应当公开进行。  
赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关申请不公开质证，对方同意的，赔偿委员会可以不公开质证。

**Article 3** Unless it involves State secrets, individual privacy, or otherwise provided by law, cross-examination shall be conducted openly.  
Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation applies for non-public cross-examination and the other party agrees, the compensation committee may conduct cross-examination in private.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关在质证活动中的法律地位平等，有权委托代理人，提出回避申请，提供证据，申请查阅、复制本案质证材料，进行陈述、质询、申辩，并应当依法行使质证权利，遵守质证秩序。

**Article 4** The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation shall have equal legal status in cross-examination activities, and shall have the right to appoint authorized agents, apply for withdrawal, provide evidence, apply to inspect or copy the cross-examination materials of the case, make statements, conduct inquiries, and present arguments, and shall exercise their cross-examination rights according to law and observe the order of cross-examination.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关对其主张的有利于自己的事实负举证责任，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。  
没有证据或者证据不足以证明其事实主张的，由负有举证责任的一方承担不利后果。

**Article 5** The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the facts favorable to themselves that they assert, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretation.  
Where there is no evidence or the evidence is insufficient to prove the alleged facts, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall bear the adverse consequences.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   下列事实需要证明的，由赔偿义务机关负举证责任：  
（一）赔偿义务机关行为的合法性；  
（二）赔偿义务机关无过错；  
（三）因赔偿义务机关过错致使赔偿请求人不能证明的待证事实；  
（四）赔偿义务机关行为与被羁押人在羁押期间死亡或者丧失行为能力不存在因果关系。

**Article 6** The following facts that require proof shall be borne by the obligor for compensation:  
(1) The legality of the act of the obligor for compensation;  
(2) The obligor for compensation has no fault;  
(3) The fact to be proved that the claimant for compensation cannot prove due to the fault of the obligor for compensation;  
(4) There is no causal relationship between the act of the obligor for compensation and the death or loss of capacity of the person in custody during the period of custody.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   下列情形，由赔偿义务机关负举证责任：  
（一）属于法定免责情形；  
（二）赔偿请求超过法定时效；  
（三）具有其他抗辩事由。

**Article 7** Under the following circumstances, the obligor for compensation shall bear the burden of adducing evidence:  
(1) The circumstances fall under statutory exemption from liability;  
(2) The claim for compensation exceeds the statutory limitation;  
(3) There are other grounds for defense.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   赔偿委员会认为必要时，可以通知复议机关参加质证，由复议机关对其作出复议决定的事实和法律依据进行说明。

**Article 8** Where the compensation committee deems it necessary, it may notify the reconsideration organ to participate in the cross-examination, and the reconsideration organ shall explain the facts and legal basis on which it made the reconsideration decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   赔偿请求人可以在举证期限内申请赔偿委员会调取下列证据：  
（一）由国家有关部门保存，赔偿请求人及其委托代理人无权查阅调取的证据；  
（二）涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的证据；  
（三）赔偿请求人及其委托代理人因客观原因不能自行收集的其他证据。  
赔偿请求人申请赔偿委员会调取证据，应当提供具体线索。

**Article 9** The claimant for compensation may, within the time limit for adducing evidence, apply to the compensation committee to obtain the following evidence:  
(1) Evidence kept by a relevant State authority that the claimant for compensation and its authorized agent have no right to inspect or obtain;  
(2) Evidence involving State secrets, trade secrets, or individual privacy;  
(3) Other evidence that the claimant for compensation and its authorized agent cannot collect on their own due to objective reasons.  
Where the claimant for compensation applies to the compensation committee to obtain evidence, it shall provide specific leads.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   赔偿委员会有权要求赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关提供或者补充证据。  
涉及国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的事实，或者涉及依职权追加质证参加人、中止审理、终结审理、回避等程序性事项的，赔偿委员会可以向有关单位和人员调查情况、收集证据。

**Article 10** The compensation committee shall have the authority to require the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation to provide or supplement evidence.  
Where the facts involve national interests, social public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, or where procedural matters such as adding participants to cross-examination, suspending adjudication, terminating adjudication, or withdrawal are involved by virtue of office, the compensation committee may investigate the circumstances and collect evidence from relevant entities and individuals.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关应当在收到受理案件通知书之日起十日内提供证据。赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关确因客观事由不能在该期限内提供证据的，赔偿委员会可以根据其申请适当延长举证期限。  
赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关无正当理由逾期提供证据的，应当承担相应的不利后果。

**Article 11** The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation shall provide evidence within ten days from the date of receipt of the notice of case acceptance. Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation is genuinely unable to provide evidence within that time limit due to objective reasons, the compensation committee may, upon its application, appropriately extend the time limit for adducing evidence.  
Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation provides evidence late without justifiable reasons, it shall bear corresponding adverse consequences.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   对于证据较多或者疑难复杂的案件，赔偿委员会可以组织赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关在质证前交换证据，明确争议焦点，并将交换证据的情况记录在卷。  
赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关在证据交换过程中没有争议并记录在卷的证据，经审判员在质证中说明后，可以作为认定案件事实的依据。

**Article 12** For cases with voluminous evidence or of a complicated nature, the compensation committee may organize the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation to exchange evidence before cross-examination, clarify the focus of disputes, and record the exchange of evidence on file.  
Evidence that is not disputed during the evidence exchange process and is recorded on file may, after being explained by the adjudicator during cross-examination, serve as the basis for determining the facts of the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   赔偿委员会应当指定审判员组织质证，并在质证三日前通知赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和其他质证参与人。必要时，赔偿委员会可以通知赔偿义务机关实施原职权行为的工作人员或者其他利害关系人到场接受询问。  
赔偿委员会决定公开质证的，应当在质证三日前公告案由，赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关的名称，以及质证的时间、地点。

**Article 13** The compensation committee shall designate an adjudicator to organize the cross-examination and notify the claimant for compensation, the obligor for compensation, and other participants in the cross-examination three days before the cross-examination. Where necessary, the compensation committee may notify the staff members who performed the original official act of the obligor for compensation or other interested persons to be present to accept inquiries.  
Where the compensation committee decides to conduct cross-examination openly, it shall announce the cause of action, the names of the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation, as well as the time and place of cross-examination three days before the cross-examination.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   适用质证程序审理国家赔偿案件，未经质证的证据不得作为认定案件事实的依据，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 14** In the adjudication of a State compensation case using cross-examination procedures, evidence that has not been cross-examined shall not be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关应围绕证据的关联性、真实性、合法性，针对证据有无证明力以及证明力大小，进行质证。

**Article 15** The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation shall conduct cross-examination focusing on the relevance, authenticity, and legality of the evidence, and regarding whether the evidence has probative force and the extent of such probative force.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   质证开始前，由书记员查明质证参与人是否到场，宣布质证纪律。  
质证开始时，由主持质证的审判员核对赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关，宣布案由，宣布审判员、书记员名单，向赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关告知质证权利义务以及询问是否申请回避。

**Article 16** Before the commencement of cross-examination, the court clerk shall ascertain whether the participants are present and announce the cross-examination discipline.  
At the commencement of cross-examination, the adjudicator presiding over the cross-examination shall verify the identity of the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation, announce the cause of action, announce the list of adjudicators and court clerks, inform the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation of their rights and obligations in cross-examination, and inquire whether they apply for withdrawal.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   质证一般按照下列顺序进行：  
（一）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关分别陈述，复议机关进行说明；  
（二）审判员归纳争议焦点；  
（三）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关分别出示证据，发表意见；  
（四）询问参加质证的证人、鉴定人、勘验人；  
（五）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关就争议的事项进行质询和辩论；  
（六）审判员宣布赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关认识一致的事实和证据；  
（七）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关最后陈述意见。

**Article 17** Cross-examination shall generally proceed in the following order:  
(1) The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation each make statements, and the reconsideration organ gives explanations;  
(2) The adjudicator summarizes the focus of disputes;  
(3) The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation each present evidence and state their opinions;  
(4) Questions are put to the witnesses, expert evaluators, and inspectors participating in the cross-examination;  
(5) The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation conduct inquiries and debates on the matters in dispute;  
(6) The adjudicator announces the facts and evidence on which the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation have reached a consensus;  
(7) The claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation make final statements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条   赔偿委员会根据赔偿请求人申请调取的证据，作为赔偿请求人提供的证据进行质证。  
赔偿委员会依照职权调取的证据应当在质证时出示，并就调取该证据的情况予以说明，听取赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关的意见。

**Article 18** Evidence obtained by the compensation committee upon the application of the claimant for compensation shall be cross-examined as evidence provided by the claimant for compensation.  
Evidence obtained ex officio by the compensation committee shall be presented during cross-examination, and an explanation of the circumstances under which such evidence was obtained shall be given, and the opinions of the claimant for compensation and the obligor for compensation shall be heard.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条   赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关对对方主张的不利于自己的事实，在质证中明确表示承认的，对方无需举证；既未表示承认也未否认，经审判员询问并释明法律后果后，其仍不作明确表示的，视为对该项事实的承认。  
赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关委托代理人参加质证的，代理人在代理权限范围内的承认视为被代理人的承认，但参加质证的赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关当场明确表示反对的除外；代理人超出代理权限范围的承认，参加质证的赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关当场不作否认表示的，视为被代理人的承认。  
上述承认违反法律禁止性规定，或者损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益的，不发生自认的效力。

**Article 19** Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation explicitly admits, during cross-examination, the facts unfavorable to itself that are asserted by the other party, the other party shall not be required to adduce evidence; where it neither admits nor denies, and after the adjudicator inquires and explains the legal consequences, it still fails to make an explicit statement, it shall be deemed as an admission of the fact.  
Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation appoints an authorized agent to participate in cross-examination, admissions made by the agent within the scope of authority shall be deemed as admissions by the principal, except where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation participating in cross-examination explicitly objects on the spot; where admissions made by the agent exceed the scope of authority, if the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation participating in cross-examination does not deny them on the spot, it shall be deemed as admissions by the principal.  
If the admissions mentioned above violate the prohibitive provisions of laws or harm national interests, social public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, they shall not be effective as self-admissions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条   下列事实无需举证证明：  
（一）自然规律以及定理、定律；  
（二）众所周知的事实；  
（三）根据法律规定推定的事实；  
（四）已经依法证明的事实；  
（五）根据日常生活经验法则推定的事实。  
前款（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项，赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关有相反证据否定其真实性的除外。

**Article 20** The following facts do not require proof by adducing evidence:  
(1) Natural laws, theorems, and laws;  
(2) Facts that are common knowledge;  
(3) Facts presumed by legal provisions;  
(4) Facts that have been proved according to law;  
(5) Facts presumed based on rules of daily life experience.  
With respect to items (2), (3), (4), and (5) of the preceding paragraph, unless the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation has contrary evidence to negate their truthfulness.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条   有证据证明赔偿义务机关持有证据无正当理由拒不提供的，赔偿委员会可以就待证事实作出有利于赔偿请求人的推定。

**Article 21** Where there is evidence that the obligor for compensation holds evidence and refuses to provide it without justifiable reasons, the compensation committee may make an inference regarding the fact to be proved that is favorable to the claimant for compensation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条   赔偿委员会应当依据法律规定，遵照法定程序，全面客观地审核证据，运用逻辑推理和日常生活经验，对证据的证明力进行独立、综合的审查判断。

**Article 22** The compensation committee shall, in accordance with legal provisions and statutory procedures, examine the evidence comprehensively and objectively, and using logical reasoning and daily life experience, independently and comprehensively review and determine the probative force of the evidence.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条   书记员应当将质证的全部活动记入笔录。质证笔录由赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和其他质证参与人核对无误或者补正后签名或者盖章。拒绝签名或者盖章的，应当记明情况附卷，由审判员和书记员签名。  
具备条件的，赔偿委员会可以对质证活动进行全程同步录音录像。

**Article 23** The court clerk shall record all activities of the cross-examination in the record. The cross-examination record shall be signed or sealed by the claimant for compensation, the obligor for compensation, and other participants after they verify that it is correct or after corrections are made. Where a party refuses to sign or seal, the circumstances shall be noted and attached to the file, and the adjudicator and court clerk shall sign it.  
Where conditions permit, the compensation committee may make a synchronous audio and video recording of the entire cross-examination process.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条   赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关经通知无正当理由拒不参加质证或者未经许可中途退出质证的，视为放弃质证，赔偿委员会可以综合全案情况和对方意见认定案件事实。

**Article 24** Where the claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation, after being notified, refuses without justifiable reasons to participate in cross-examination or withdraws from cross-examination midway without permission, it shall be deemed as waiving cross-examination, and the compensation committee may determine the facts of the case based on the overall circumstances of the case and the opinions of the other party.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条   有下列情形之一的，可以延期质证：  
（一）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关因不可抗拒的事由不能参加质证的；  
（二）赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关临时提出回避申请，是否回避的决定不能在短时间内作出的；  
（三）需要通知新的证人到场，调取新的证据，重新鉴定、勘验，或者补充调查的；  
（四）其他应当延期的情形。

**Article 25** Cross-examination may be adjourned under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation is unable to participate in cross-examination due to force majeure;  
(2) The claimant for compensation or the obligor for compensation raises an application for withdrawal on the spot, and a decision on whether to allow withdrawal cannot be made within a short time;  
(3) It is necessary to notify new witnesses to be present, obtain new evidence, conduct new expert evaluations or inspections, or conduct supplementary investigations;  
(4) Other circumstances where an adjournment is appropriate.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条   本规定自2014年3月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 26** These Provisions shall come into force on March 1, 2014.  
Where any judicial interpretation issued by the Supreme People's Court before these Provisions come into force is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
