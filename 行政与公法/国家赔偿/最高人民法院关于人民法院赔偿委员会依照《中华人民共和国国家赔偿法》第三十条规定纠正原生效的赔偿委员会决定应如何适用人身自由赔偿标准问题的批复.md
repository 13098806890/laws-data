[《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)规定  
  
纠正原生效的赔偿委员会决定应如何适用  
  
人身自由赔偿标准问题的批复  
  
法释〔2014〕7号  
  
吉林、山东、河南省高级人民法院：  
  
关于人民法院赔偿委员会在赔偿申诉监督程序中如何适用人身自由赔偿标准问题，经研究，批复如下：  
  
人民法院赔偿委员会依照[《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)规定纠正原生效的赔偿委员会决定时，原决定的错误系漏算部分侵犯人身自由天数的，应在维持原决定支付的人身自由赔偿金的同时，就漏算天数按照重新审查或者直接审查后作出决定时的上年度国家职工日平均工资标准计算相应的人身自由赔偿金；原决定的错误系未支持人身自由赔偿请求的，按照重新审查或者直接审查后作出决定时的上年度国家职工日平均工资标准计算人身自由赔偿金。

**Article 1** June 30, 2014  
Supreme People's Court  
Reply of the Supreme People's Court on How to Apply the Standard of Compensation for Personal Freedom When Correcting a Previously Effective Decision of the Compensation Commission in Accordance with Article 30 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China  
Interpretation No. 7 [2014] of the Supreme People's Court  
To the Higher People's Courts of Jilin, Shandong, and Henan Provinces:  
Regarding the issue of how to apply the standard of compensation for personal freedom by the compensation commission of a People's Court in the supervision procedure for compensation petitions, upon study, the reply is as follows:  
When the compensation commission of a People's Court, in accordance with Article 30 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, corrects the previously effective decision of the compensation commission, if the error of the original decision consists in the undercounting of some days of infringement of personal freedom, the compensation for personal freedom for the undercounted days shall be calculated in accordance with the average daily wage of the national employees in the previous year at the time when the decision is made after re-examination or direct review, while maintaining the compensation for personal freedom paid under the original decision; if the error of the original decision consists in failing to support the claim for compensation for personal freedom, the compensation for personal freedom shall be calculated in accordance with the average daily wage of the national employees in the previous year at the time when the decision is made after re-examination or direct review.
