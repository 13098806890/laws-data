### 最高人民法院关于公安机关不履行法定行政职责是否承担行政赔偿责任问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于公安机关不履行法定行政职责  
  
是否承担行政赔偿责任问题的批复  
  
（2001年6月26日最高人民法院审判委员会第1182次会议通过，自2001年7月22日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕23号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔 2000〕198号《关于公安机关不履行法定职责是否承担行政赔偿责任的问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
由于公安机关不履行法定行政职责，致使公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益遭受损害的，应当承担行政赔偿责任。在确定赔偿的数额时，应当考虑该不履行法定职责的行为在损害发生过程和结果中所起的作用等因素。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2001] No. 23  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
We have received your Petition No. [2000] 198, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether Public Security Organs Shall Assume Administrative Compensation Liability for Failure to Perform Statutory Duties." Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a public security organ fails to perform its statutory administrative duties, thereby causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, it shall assume administrative compensation liability. When determining the amount of compensation, factors such as the role played by the act of failing to perform statutory duties in the process and consequences of the damage shall be considered.  
This is the reply.
