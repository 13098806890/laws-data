# 最高人民法院关于人民法院赔偿委员会审理国家赔偿案件程序的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕6号  
**公布日期**：2011-02-28

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院赔偿委员会审理国家赔偿案件程序的规定》已于2011年2月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1513次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年3月22日施行。 2011年3月17日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院赔偿委员会 审理国家赔偿案件程序的规定 法释〔2011〕6号 根据2010年4月29日修正的《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》（以下简称国家赔偿法），结合国家赔偿工作实际，对人民法院赔偿委员会（以下简称赔偿委员会）审理国家赔偿案件的程序作如下规定： 第一条  赔偿请求人向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，应当递交赔偿申请书一式四份。赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以口头申请。口头提出申请的，人民法院应当填写《申请赔偿登记表》，由赔偿请求人签名或者盖章。 第二条  赔偿请求人向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，应当提供以下法律文书和证明材料： （一）赔偿义务机关作出的决定书； （二）复议机关作出的复议决定书，但赔偿义务机关是人民法院的除外； （三）赔偿义务机关或者复议机关逾期未作出决定的，应当提供赔偿义务机关对赔偿申请的收讫凭证等相关证明材料； （四）行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关在赔偿申请所涉案件的刑事诉讼程序、民事诉讼程序、行政诉讼程序、执行程序中作出的法律文书； （五）赔偿义务机关职权行为侵犯赔偿请求人合法权益造成损害的证明材料； （六）证明赔偿申请符合申请条件的其他材料。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  赔偿请求人向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，应当递交赔偿申请书一式四份。赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以口头申请。口头提出申请的，人民法院应当填写《申请赔偿登记表》，由赔偿请求人签名或者盖章。

**Article 1** A compensation claimant applying to the Compensation Committee for a compensation decision shall submit a written application for compensation in quadruplicate. If it is truly difficult for the compensation claimant to write the application, an oral application may be made. If an oral application is made, the People's Court shall fill out the Application Registration Form for Compensation, which shall be signed or sealed by the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  赔偿请求人向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定，应当提供以下法律文书和证明材料：  
（一）赔偿义务机关作出的决定书；  
（二）复议机关作出的复议决定书，但赔偿义务机关是人民法院的除外；  
（三）赔偿义务机关或者复议机关逾期未作出决定的，应当提供赔偿义务机关对赔偿申请的收讫凭证等相关证明材料；  
（四）行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关在赔偿申请所涉案件的刑事诉讼程序、民事诉讼程序、行政诉讼程序、执行程序中作出的法律文书；  
（五）赔偿义务机关职权行为侵犯赔偿请求人合法权益造成损害的证明材料；  
（六）证明赔偿申请符合申请条件的其他材料。

**Article 2** A compensation claimant applying to the Compensation Committee for a compensation decision shall provide the following legal documents and evidentiary materials:  
(1) The decision document made by the compensatory obligation organ;  
(2) The reconsideration decision document made by the reconsideration organ, except where the compensatory obligation organ is a People's Court;  
(3) If the compensatory obligation organ or the reconsideration organ fails to make a decision within the time limit, the compensation claimant shall provide relevant evidentiary materials such as the receipt voucher of the compensation application issued by the compensatory obligation organ;  
(4) The legal documents made by the organs exercising the functions of investigation, prosecution, and adjudication in the criminal action, civil action, administrative action, and enforcement proceedings of the case involved in the compensation application;  
(5) Evidentiary materials proving that the acts within the functions of the compensatory obligation organ have infringed upon the lawful rights and interests of the compensation claimant and caused damage;  
(6) Other materials proving that the compensation application meets the conditions for application.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  赔偿委员会收到赔偿申请，经审查认为符合申请条件的，应当在七日内立案，并通知赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和复议机关；认为不符合申请条件的，应当在七日内决定不予受理；立案后发现不符合申请条件的，决定驳回申请。  
前款规定的期限，自赔偿委员会收到赔偿申请之日起计算。申请材料不齐全的，赔偿委员会应当在五日内一次性告知赔偿请求人需要补正的全部内容，收到赔偿申请的时间应当自赔偿委员会收到补正材料之日起计算。

**Article 3** Upon receiving a compensation application, if after examination the Compensation Committee finds that it meets the conditions for application, it shall open a case within seven days and notify the compensation claimant, the compensatory obligation organ, and the reconsideration organ; if it finds that it does not meet the conditions for application, it shall decide to reject the application within seven days; if it finds after opening the case that it does not meet the conditions for application, it shall decide to dismiss the application.  
The time limit specified in the preceding paragraph shall be calculated from the date on which the Compensation Committee receives the compensation application. If the application materials are incomplete, the Compensation Committee shall, within five days, inform the compensation claimant of all the contents that need to be supplemented at one time, and the date of receipt of the compensation application shall be calculated from the date on which the Compensation Committee receives the supplemental materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  赔偿委员会应当在立案之日起五日内将赔偿申请书副本或者《申请赔偿登记表》副本送达赔偿义务机关和复议机关。

**Article 4** The Compensation Committee shall, within five days from the date of opening the case, serve a copy of the written application for compensation or a copy of the Application Registration Form for Compensation on the compensatory obligation organ and the reconsideration organ.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  赔偿请求人可以委托一至二人作为代理人。律师、提出申请的公民的近亲属、有关的社会团体或者所在单位推荐的人、经赔偿委员会许可的其他公民，都可以被委托为代理人。  
赔偿义务机关、复议机关可以委托本机关工作人员一至二人作为代理人。

**Article 5** A compensation claimant may entrust one or two persons as agents. Lawyers, near relatives of the citizen filing the application, persons recommended by a relevant social organization or the claimant's work unit, and other citizens permitted by the Compensation Committee may be entrusted as agents.  
The compensatory obligation organ and the reconsideration organ may entrust one or two of their own staff members as agents.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关、复议机关委托他人代理，应当向赔偿委员会提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。  
授权委托书应当载明委托事项和权限。代理人代为承认、放弃、变更赔偿请求，应当有委托人的特别授权。

**Article 6** Where a compensation claimant, compensatory obligation organ, or reconsideration organ entrusts another person to act as an agent, it shall submit to the Compensation Committee a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal.  
The power of attorney shall specify the matters and scope of the entrustment. Where an agent admits, waives, or modifies a compensation claim on behalf of the principal, special authorization from the principal shall be obtained.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件，应当指定一名审判员负责具体承办。  
负责具体承办赔偿案件的审判员应当查清事实并写出审理报告，提请赔偿委员会讨论决定。  
赔偿委员会作赔偿决定，必须有三名以上审判员参加，按照少数服从多数的原则作出决定。

**Article 7** When hearing a compensation case, the Compensation Committee shall designate one judge to be responsible for handling the case.  
The judge responsible for handling the compensation case shall ascertain the facts, write a hearing report, and submit it to the Compensation Committee for discussion and decision.  
When the Compensation Committee makes a compensation decision, at least three judges shall participate, and the decision shall be made in accordance with the principle of majority rule.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  审判人员有下列情形之一的，应当回避，赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关有权以书面或者口头方式申请其回避：  
（一）是本案赔偿请求人的近亲属；  
（二）是本案代理人的近亲属；  
（三）与本案有利害关系；  
（四）与本案有其他关系，可能影响对案件公正审理的。  
前款规定，适用于书记员、翻译人员、鉴定人、勘验人。

**Article 8** A judge shall withdraw from the case in any of the following circumstances, and the compensation claimant and the compensatory obligation organ have the right to apply for his or her withdrawal in writing or orally:  
(1) Being a near relative of the compensation claimant in the case;  
(2) Being a near relative of the agent in the case;  
(3) Having an interest in the case;  
(4) Having other relations with the case that may affect the fair adjudication of the case.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to court clerks, interpreters, expert evaluators, and inspectors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件，可以组织赔偿义务机关与赔偿请求人就赔偿方式、赔偿项目和赔偿数额依照国家赔偿法第四章的规定进行协商。

**Article 9** When hearing a compensation case, the Compensation Committee may organize the compensatory obligation organ and the compensation claimant to consult on the method of compensation, items of compensation, and amount of compensation in accordance with the provisions of Chapter IV of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  组织协商应当遵循自愿和合法的原则。赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关一方或者双方不愿协商，或者协商不成的，赔偿委员会应当及时作出决定。

**Article 10** Organizing consultation shall follow the principles of voluntariness and legality. If either or both of the compensation claimant and the compensatory obligation organ are unwilling to consult, or if consultation fails, the Compensation Committee shall make a decision promptly.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关经协商达成协议的，赔偿委员会审查确认后应当制作国家赔偿决定书。

**Article 11** If the compensation claimant and the compensatory obligation organ reach an agreement through consultation, the Compensation Committee shall, after examination and confirmation, make a written decision on state compensation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关对自己提出的主张或者反驳对方主张所依据的事实有责任提供证据加以证明。有[国家赔偿法第二十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-26)第二款规定情形的，应当由赔偿义务机关提供证据。  
没有证据或者证据不足以证明其事实主张的，由负有举证责任的一方承担不利后果。

**Article 12** The compensation claimant and the compensatory obligation organ each have the responsibility to provide evidence to prove the facts on which their own claims or rebuttals of the other party's claims are based. Where the circumstances provided in paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China exist, the compensatory obligation organ shall provide evidence.  
If there is no evidence or the evidence is insufficient to prove the factual claim, the party bearing the burden of adducing evidence shall bear the adverse consequences.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  赔偿义务机关对其职权行为的合法性负有举证责任。  
赔偿请求人可以提供证明职权行为违法的证据，但不因此免除赔偿义务机关对其职权行为合法性的举证责任。

**Article 13** The compensatory obligation organ bears the burden of adducing evidence for the legality of its acts within its functions.  
The compensation claimant may provide evidence proving that the acts within functions are illegal, but this shall not exempt the compensatory obligation organ from its burden of adducing evidence for the legality of its acts within its functions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  有下列情形之一的，赔偿委员会可以组织赔偿请求人和赔偿义务机关进行质证：  
（一）对侵权事实、损害后果及因果关系争议较大的；  
（二）对是否属于[国家赔偿法第十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-19)规定的国家不承担赔偿责任的情形争议较大的；  
（三）对赔偿方式、赔偿项目或者赔偿数额争议较大的；  
（四）赔偿委员会认为应当质证的其他情形。

**Article 14** In any of the following circumstances, the Compensation Committee may organize cross-examination between the compensation claimant and the compensatory obligation organ:  
(1) There is a significant dispute over the facts of infringement, the consequences of damage, and the causal relationship;  
(2) There is a significant dispute over whether the circumstances fall under the circumstances where the state does not bear compensation liability as provided in Article 19 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) There is a significant dispute over the method of compensation, items of compensation, or amount of compensation;  
(4) Other circumstances in which the Compensation Committee considers that cross-examination should be conducted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  赔偿委员会认为重大、疑难的案件，应报请院长提交审判委员会讨论决定。审判委员会的决定，赔偿委员会应当执行。

**Article 15** For a case that the Compensation Committee considers significant or difficult, it shall report to the President for submission to the Trial Committee for discussion and decision. The Compensation Committee shall implement the decision of the Trial Committee.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  赔偿委员会作出决定前，赔偿请求人撤回赔偿申请的，赔偿委员会应当依法审查并作出是否准许的决定。

**Article 16** If, before the Compensation Committee makes a decision, the compensation claimant withdraws the compensation application, the Compensation Committee shall examine the withdrawal according to law and make a decision on whether to permit it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  有下列情形之一的，赔偿委员会应当决定中止审理：  
（一）赔偿请求人死亡，需要等待其继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属表明是否参加赔偿案件处理的；  
（二）赔偿请求人丧失行为能力，尚未确定法定代理人的；  
（三）作为赔偿请求人的法人或者其他组织终止，尚未确定权利义务承受人的；  
（四）赔偿请求人因不可抗拒的事由，在法定审限内不能参加赔偿案件处理的；  
（五）宣告无罪的案件，人民法院决定再审或者人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的；  
（六）应当中止审理的其他情形。  
中止审理的原因消除后，赔偿委员会应当及时恢复审理，并通知赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和复议机关。

**Article 17** The Compensation Committee shall decide to suspend the hearing in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The compensation claimant dies and it is necessary to wait for his or her heirs and other relatives with maintenance relationships to indicate whether to participate in the handling of the compensation case;  
(2) The compensation claimant loses capacity for conduct and no legal representative has been designated;  
(3) The legal person or other organization acting as the compensation claimant is terminated and no successor of rights and obligations has been determined;  
(4) The compensation claimant is unable to participate in the handling of the compensation case within the statutory trial period due to force majeure;  
(5) In a case where an acquittal has been pronounced, the People's Court decides to retry the case or the People's Procuratorate lodges a protest in accordance with the trial supervision procedure;  
(6) Other circumstances in which the hearing should be suspended.  
After the cause for suspension is eliminated, the Compensation Committee shall promptly resume the hearing and notify the compensation claimant, the compensatory obligation organ, and the reconsideration organ.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  有下列情形之一的，赔偿委员会应当决定终结审理：  
（一）赔偿请求人死亡，没有继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属或者赔偿请求人的继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属放弃要求赔偿权利的；  
（二）作为赔偿请求人的法人或者其他组织终止后，其权利义务承受人放弃要求赔偿权利的；  
（三）赔偿请求人据以申请赔偿的撤销案件决定、不起诉决定或者无罪判决被撤销的；  
（四）应当终结审理的其他情形。

**Article 18** The Compensation Committee shall decide to terminate the hearing in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The compensation claimant dies and there is no heir or other relative with a maintenance relationship, or the heirs and other relatives with maintenance relationships of the compensation claimant waive the right to claim compensation;  
(2) After the legal person or other organization acting as the compensation claimant is terminated, the successor of its rights and obligations waives the right to claim compensation;  
(3) The decision to close the case, decision not to prosecute, or judgment of acquittal on which the compensation claimant bases his or her application for compensation is revoked;  
(4) Other circumstances in which the hearing should be terminated.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件应当按照下列情形，分别作出决定：  
（一）赔偿义务机关的决定或者复议机关的复议决定认定事实清楚，适用法律正确的，依法予以维持；  
（二）赔偿义务机关的决定、复议机关的复议决定认定事实清楚，但适用法律错误的，依法重新决定；  
（三）赔偿义务机关的决定、复议机关的复议决定认定事实不清、证据不足的，查清事实后依法重新决定；  
（四）赔偿义务机关、复议机关逾期未作决定的，查清事实后依法作出决定。

**Article 19** When hearing a compensation case, the Compensation Committee shall make a decision respectively according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the decision of the compensatory obligation organ or the reconsideration decision of the reconsideration organ has clear findings of fact and correct application of law, it shall be affirmed according to law;  
(2) If the decision of the compensatory obligation organ or the reconsideration decision of the reconsideration organ has clear findings of fact but erroneous application of law, a new decision shall be made according to law;  
(3) If the decision of the compensatory obligation organ or the reconsideration decision of the reconsideration organ has unclear findings of fact or insufficient evidence, a new decision shall be made according to law after the facts are clarified;  
(4) If the compensatory obligation organ or the reconsideration organ fails to make a decision within the time limit, a decision shall be made according to law after the facts are clarified.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件作出决定，应当制作国家赔偿决定书，加盖人民法院印章。

**Article 20** When the Compensation Committee hears a compensation case and makes a decision, it shall prepare a written decision on state compensation and affix the seal of the People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  国家赔偿决定书应当载明以下事项：  
（一）赔偿请求人的基本情况，赔偿义务机关、复议机关的名称及其法定代表人；  
（二）赔偿请求人申请事项及理由，赔偿义务机关的决定、复议机关的复议决定情况；  
（三）赔偿委员会认定的事实及依据；  
（四）决定的理由及法律依据；  
（五）决定内容。

**Article 21** A written decision on state compensation shall specify the following matters:  
(1) Basic information of the compensation claimant, the names and legal representatives of the compensatory obligation organ and the reconsideration organ;  
(2) The matters and grounds of the compensation claimant's application, and the content of the decision of the compensatory obligation organ and the reconsideration decision of the reconsideration organ;  
(3) The facts found by the Compensation Committee and the basis therefor;  
(4) The reasons for the decision and the legal basis;  
(5) The content of the decision.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  赔偿委员会作出的决定应当分别送达赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和复议机关。

**Article 22** The decision made by the Compensation Committee shall be served separately on the compensation claimant, the compensatory obligation organ, and the reconsideration organ.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  人民法院办理本院为赔偿义务机关的国家赔偿案件参照本规定。

**Article 23** Where a People's Court handles a state compensation case in which it itself is the compensatory obligation organ, it shall refer to these Provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  自本规定公布之日起，《人民法院赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件程序的暂行规定》即行废止；本规定施行前本院发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 24** As of the date of promulgation of these Provisions, the Interim Provisions of the People's Court Compensation Committee on Procedures for Hearing Compensation Cases shall be abolished; if any judicial interpretation issued by the People's Court before the coming into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
