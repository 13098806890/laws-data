# 最高人民法院关于审理国家赔偿案件确定精神损害赔偿责任适用法律若干问题的解释

### 正文

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  决定为受害人消除影响、恢复名誉或者赔礼道歉的，应当载入决定主文。  
赔偿义务机关在决定作出前已为受害人消除影响、恢复名誉或者赔礼道歉，或者原侵权案件的纠正被媒体广泛报道，客观上已经起到消除影响、恢复名誉作用，且符合本解释规定的，可以在决定书中予以说明。

**Article 6** Where a decision is made to eliminate the effects, restore the reputation, or make an apology for the victim, it shall be recorded in the main body of the decision.  
If, before the decision is made, the compensation obligor has eliminated the effects, restored the reputation, or made an apology for the victim, or the correction of the original tort case has been widely reported by the media, which has objectively served to eliminate the effects and restore the reputation, and such measures comply with the provisions of this Interpretation, an explanation may be given in the decision document.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[国家赔偿法第三十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-35)规定的“造成严重后果”：  
（一）无罪或者终止追究刑事责任的人被羁押六个月以上；  
（二）受害人经鉴定为轻伤以上或者残疾；  
（三）受害人经诊断、鉴定为精神障碍或者精神残疾，且与侵权行为存在关联；  
（四）受害人名誉、荣誉、家庭、职业、教育等方面遭受严重损害，且与侵权行为存在关联。  
受害人无罪被羁押十年以上；受害人死亡；受害人经鉴定为重伤或者残疾一至四级，且生活不能自理；受害人经诊断、鉴定为严重精神障碍或者精神残疾一至二级，生活不能自理，且与侵权行为存在关联的，可以认定为后果特别严重。

**Article 7** Under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined that “serious consequences have been caused” as stipulated in Article 35 of the State Compensation Law:  
(1) A person who is innocent or for whom criminal liability has been terminated has been held in custody for more than six months;  
(2) The victim is determined through expert evaluation to have sustained minor injury or above, or disability;  
(3) The victim is diagnosed or determined through expert evaluation to have a mental disorder or mental disability, and there is a connection with the tortious act;  
(4) The victim suffers serious damage in terms of reputation, honor, family, occupation, education, etc., and there is a connection with the tortious act.  
Where an innocent victim is held in custody for more than ten years; the victim dies; the victim is determined through expert evaluation to have sustained serious injury or disability of Grade 1 to 4 and is unable to take care of themselves; or the victim is diagnosed or determined through expert evaluation to have a severe mental disorder or mental disability of Grade 1 to 2, is unable to take care of themselves, and there is a connection with the tortious act, it may be determined that the consequences are particularly serious.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  致人精神损害，造成严重后果的，精神损害抚慰金一般应当在[国家赔偿法第三十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-33)、第三十四条规定的人身自由赔偿金、生命健康赔偿金总额的百分之五十以下（包括本数）酌定；后果特别严重，或者虽然不具有[本解释第七条](#art-7)第二款规定情形，但是确有证据证明前述标准不足以抚慰的，可以在百分之五十以上酌定。

**Article 8** Where mental damage is caused to a person, resulting in serious consequences, the mental damage consolation payment shall generally be determined at a level not exceeding 50% (inclusive of that figure) of the total amount of compensation for personal freedom and compensation for life and health as specified in Articles 33 and 34 of the State Compensation Law. If the consequences are particularly serious, or although the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of Article 7 of this Interpretation do not exist, there is credible evidence proving that the aforementioned standard is insufficient to provide consolation, the amount may be determined at a level exceeding 50%.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  精神损害抚慰金的具体数额，应当在兼顾社会发展整体水平的同时，参考下列因素合理确定：  
（一）精神受到损害以及造成严重后果的情况；  
（二）侵权行为的目的、手段、方式等具体情节；  
（三）侵权机关及其工作人员的违法、过错程度、原因力比例；  
（四）原错判罪名、刑罚轻重、羁押时间；  
（五）受害人的职业、影响范围；  
（六）纠错的事由以及过程；  
（七）其他应当考虑的因素。

**Article 9** The specific amount of the mental damage consolation payment shall be reasonably determined by taking into account the overall level of social development and referring to the following factors:  
(1) The circumstances of the mental damage suffered and the serious consequences caused;  
(2) The specific details such as the purpose, means, and method of the tortious act;  
(3) The degree of illegality and fault of the tortfeasor organ and its staff, and the proportion of causation;  
(4) The original wrongfully convicted charge, the severity of the penalty, and the duration of custody;  
(5) The occupation of the victim and the scope of impact;  
(6) The reason and process for correcting the error;  
(7) Other factors that shall be considered.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  精神损害抚慰金的数额一般不少于一千元；数额在一千元以上的，以千为计数单位。  
赔偿请求人请求的精神损害抚慰金少于一千元，且其请求事由符合本解释规定的造成严重后果情形，经释明不予变更的，按照其请求数额支付。

**Article 10** The amount of the mental damage consolation payment shall generally be no less than 1,000 yuan; if the amount is above 1,000 yuan, it shall be denominated in units of one thousand.  
If the mental damage consolation payment requested by the claimant is less than 1,000 yuan, and the grounds for the claim meet the circumstances of causing serious consequences as stipulated in this Interpretation, and the claimant does not change the request after explanation, the payment shall be made according to the requested amount.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  受害人对损害事实和后果的发生或者扩大有过错的，可以根据其过错程度减少或者不予支付精神损害抚慰金。

**Article 11** If the victim is at fault for the occurrence or aggravation of the damage facts and consequences, the mental damage consolation payment may be reduced or not paid based on the degree of fault.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  决定中载明的支付精神损害抚慰金及其他责任承担方式，赔偿义务机关应当履行。

**Article 12** The compensation obligor shall fulfill the payment of mental damage consolation payment and other methods of assuming liability as specified in the decision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  人民法院审理[国家赔偿法第三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-38)所涉侵犯公民人身权的国家赔偿案件，以及作为赔偿义务机关审查处理国家赔偿案件，涉及精神损害赔偿的，参照本解释规定。

**Article 13** When the People's Court hears state compensation cases involving infringement of a citizen's personal rights as covered by Article 38 of the State Compensation Law, and when acting as the compensation obligor to review and handle state compensation cases, if mental damage compensation is involved, the provisions of this Interpretation shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  本解释自2021年4月1日起施行。本解释施行前的其他有关规定与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 14** This Interpretation shall come into force as of April 1, 2021. Any other relevant provisions that are inconsistent with this Interpretation and were in effect before the implementation of this Interpretation shall be superseded by this Interpretation.
