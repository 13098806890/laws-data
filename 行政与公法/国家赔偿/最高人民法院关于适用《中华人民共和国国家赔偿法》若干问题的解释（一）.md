# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕4号  
**公布日期**：2011-02-14

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国国家赔偿法〉若干问题的解释（一）》已于2011年2月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1511次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年3月18日施行。 2011年2月28日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》 若干问题的解释（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  国家机关及其工作人员行使职权侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益的行为发生在2010年12月1日以后，或者发生在2010年12月1日以前、持续至2010年12月1日以后的，适用修正的国家赔偿法。

**Article 1** Where the act of a State organ or its personnel in exercising functions and powers infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of a citizen, legal person or other organization occurs after December 1, 2010, or occurs before December 1, 2010 but continues until after December 1, 2010, the amended State Compensation Law shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  国家机关及其工作人员行使职权侵犯公民、法人和其他组织合法权益的行为发生在2010年12月1日以前的，适用修正前的国家赔偿法，但有下列情形之一的，适用修正的国家赔偿法：  
（一）2010年12月1日以前已经受理赔偿请求人的赔偿请求但尚未作出生效赔偿决定的；  
（二）赔偿请求人在2010年12月1日以后提出赔偿请求的。

**Article 2** Where the act of a State organ or its personnel in exercising functions and powers infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of a citizen, legal person or other organization occurs before December 1, 2010, the State Compensation Law before amendment shall apply; however, under any of the following circumstances, the amended State Compensation Law shall apply:  
(1) A compensation request from a compensation claimant has been accepted before December 1, 2010, but no effective compensation decision has yet been made;  
(2) The compensation claimant submits a compensation request after December 1, 2010.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院对2010年12月1日以前已经受理但尚未审结的国家赔偿确认案件，应当继续审理。

**Article 3** A People's Court shall continue to hear a case of confirmation of state compensation that was accepted before December 1, 2010 but has not yet been concluded.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  公民、法人和其他组织对行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关以及看守所、监狱管理机关在2010年12月1日以前作出并已发生法律效力的不予确认职务行为违法的法律文书不服，未依据修正前的国家赔偿法规定提出申诉并经有权机关作出侵权确认结论，直接向人民法院赔偿委员会申请赔偿的，不予受理。

**Article 4** Where a citizen, legal person or other organization refuses to accept a legally effective legal document made before December 1, 2010 by an organ exercising the functions of investigation, prosecution or adjudication, or by a detention house or prison administration organ, which does not confirm that an official act is illegal, and such person or organization, without filing a petition pursuant to the provisions of the State Compensation Law before amendment and without obtaining a conclusion of confirmation of infringement from the competent organ, directly applies for compensation to the Compensation Committee of a People's Court, the application shall not be accepted.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  公民、法人和其他组织对在2010年12月1日以前发生法律效力的赔偿决定不服提出申诉的，人民法院审查处理时适用修正前的国家赔偿法；但是仅就修正的国家赔偿法增加的赔偿项目及标准提出申诉的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 5** Where a citizen, legal person or other organization files a petition against a compensation decision that became legally effective before December 1, 2010, a People's Court shall apply the State Compensation Law before amendment when reviewing and handling the petition; however, if the petition is only on the grounds of the compensation items and standards newly added by the amended State Compensation Law, the People's Court shall not accept such petition.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院审查发现2010年12月1日以前发生法律效力的确认裁定、赔偿决定确有错误应当重新审查处理的，适用修正前的国家赔偿法。

**Article 6** Where a People's Court upon review finds that a confirmation ruling or a compensation decision that became legally effective before December 1, 2010 is indeed erroneous and shall be reviewed and handled anew, the State Compensation Law before amendment shall apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  赔偿请求人认为行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关以及看守所、监狱管理机关及其工作人员在行使职权时有修正的[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)第（一）、（二）、（三）项、第十八条规定情形的，应当在刑事诉讼程序终结后提出赔偿请求，但下列情形除外：  
（一）赔偿请求人有证据证明其与尚未终结的刑事案件无关的；  
（二）刑事案件被害人依据[刑事诉讼法第一百九十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-198)的规定，以财产未返还或者认为返还的财产受到损害而要求赔偿的。

**Article 7** Where a compensation claimant considers that an organ exercising the functions of investigation, prosecution or adjudication, or a detention house or prison administration organ, or its personnel, has committed an act falling under the circumstances specified in Article 17, subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3), and Article 18 of the amended State Compensation Law in exercising its functions, the compensation claimant shall submit a compensation request after the conclusion of the criminal proceedings, except in the following circumstances:  
(1) The compensation claimant has evidence proving that he or she is unrelated to the criminal case that has not yet been concluded;  
(2) The victim in a criminal case, pursuant to the provisions of Article 198 of the Criminal Procedure Law, claims compensation on the ground that property has not been returned or that the returned property has been damaged.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  赔偿请求人认为人民法院有修正的[国家赔偿法第三十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-38)规定情形的，应当在民事、行政诉讼程序或者执行程序终结后提出赔偿请求，但人民法院已依法撤销对妨害诉讼采取的强制措施的情形除外。

**Article 8** Where a compensation claimant considers that a People's Court has committed an act falling under the circumstances specified in Article 38 of the amended State Compensation Law, the compensation claimant shall submit a compensation request after the conclusion of the civil or administrative proceedings or enforcement proceedings, except where the People's Court has already revoked in accordance with law the compulsory measures taken against obstruction of proceedings.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  赔偿请求人或者赔偿义务机关认为人民法院赔偿委员会作出的赔偿决定存在错误，依法向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会提出申诉的，不停止赔偿决定的执行；但人民法院赔偿委员会依据修正的[国家赔偿法第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)的规定决定重新审查的，可以决定中止原赔偿决定的执行。

**Article 9** Where a compensation claimant or an organ liable for compensation considers that a compensation decision made by the Compensation Committee of a People's Court is erroneous and files a petition to the Compensation Committee of the People's Court at the next higher level according to law, the enforcement of the compensation decision shall not be suspended; however, if the Compensation Committee of the People's Court decides to conduct a new review pursuant to Article 30 of the amended State Compensation Law, it may decide to suspend the enforcement of the original compensation decision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  人民检察院依据修正的[国家赔偿法第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-30)第三款的规定，对人民法院赔偿委员会在2010年12月1日以后作出的赔偿决定提出意见的，同级人民法院赔偿委员会应当决定重新审查，并可以决定中止原赔偿决定的执行。

**Article 10** Where a People's Procuratorate presents an opinion, pursuant to the provisions of Article 30, paragraph 3 of the amended State Compensation Law, on a compensation decision made by the Compensation Committee of a People's Court after December 1, 2010, the Compensation Committee of the People's Court at the same level shall decide to conduct a new review and may decide to suspend the enforcement of the original compensation decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  本解释自公布之日起施行。

**Article 11** This Interpretation shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
