# 机关团体建设楼堂馆所管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-05  
**生效日期**：2017-12-01

> (2017年8月18日国务院第182次常务会议通过　2017年10月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第688号公布　自2017年12月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了严格控制机关、团体建设楼堂馆所，厉行节约、反对浪费，制定本条例。 第二条　机关、团体建设楼堂馆所，适用本条例。 本条例所称建设，是指新建、扩建、改建、购置；所称楼堂馆所，是指办公用房以及培训中心等各类具有住宿、会议、餐饮等接待功能的场所和设施。 第三条　建设办公用房应当严格履行审批程序，严格执行建设标准。未经批准，不得建设办公用房。 禁止以技术业务用房等名义建设办公用房或者违反规定在技术业务用房中设置办公用房。 第四条　建设办公用房应当遵循朴素、实用、安全、节能的原则。 国务院发展改革部门会同国务院住房城乡建设部门、财政部门制定办公用房建设标准，并向社会公布。 第五条　机关、团体不得建设培训中心等各类具有住宿、会议、餐饮等接待功能的场所和设施。 第二章　项目审批 第六条　建设办公用房的，应当向负责项目审批的机关(以下简称审批机关)报送项目建议书、可行性研究报告、初步设计；购置办公用房的，不报送初步设计。 根据办公用房项目的具体情况，经审批机关同意，项目建议书、可行性研究报告、初步设计可以合并编制报送。 第七条　办公用房项目的审批机关及其审批权限，按照国家有关规定执行。 第八条　审批机关应当严格审核办公用房项目，按照建设标准核定建设内容、建设规模和投资概算。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了严格控制机关、团体建设楼堂馆所，厉行节约、反对浪费，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strictly controlling the construction of office buildings and guest houses by state organs and organizations, practicing economy, and opposing waste.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　机关、团体建设楼堂馆所，适用本条例。  
本条例所称建设，是指新建、扩建、改建、购置；所称楼堂馆所，是指办公用房以及培训中心等各类具有住宿、会议、餐饮等接待功能的场所和设施。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the construction of office buildings and guest houses by state organs and organizations.  
The term "construction" as used in these Regulations means new construction, expansion, reconstruction, and purchase; the term "office buildings and guest houses" means office premises as well as various types of places and facilities with reception functions such as accommodation, meeting, and catering, including training centers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　建设办公用房应当严格履行审批程序，严格执行建设标准。未经批准，不得建设办公用房。  
禁止以技术业务用房等名义建设办公用房或者违反规定在技术业务用房中设置办公用房。

**Article 3** The construction of office premises shall strictly undergo the approval procedures and strictly comply with the construction standards. Without approval, no office premises shall be constructed.  
It shall be prohibited to construct office premises in the name of technical and business rooms or to set up office premises in technical and business rooms in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　建设办公用房应当遵循朴素、实用、安全、节能的原则。  
国务院发展改革部门会同国务院住房城乡建设部门、财政部门制定办公用房建设标准，并向社会公布。

**Article 4** The construction of office premises shall follow the principles of simplicity, practicality, safety, and energy efficiency.  
The development and reform department of the State Council shall, jointly with the housing and urban-rural construction department of the State Council and the finance department, formulate construction standards for office premises and make them public.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　机关、团体不得建设培训中心等各类具有住宿、会议、餐饮等接待功能的场所和设施。

**Article 5** State organs and organizations shall not construct various types of places and facilities with reception functions such as accommodation, meeting, and catering, including training centers.

### 第二章　项目审批

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　建设办公用房的，应当向负责项目审批的机关(以下简称审批机关)报送项目建议书、可行性研究报告、初步设计；购置办公用房的，不报送初步设计。  
根据办公用房项目的具体情况，经审批机关同意，项目建议书、可行性研究报告、初步设计可以合并编制报送。

**Article 6** For the construction of office premises, the project proposal, feasibility study report, and preliminary design shall be submitted to the authority responsible for project approval (hereinafter referred to as the approval authority); for the purchase of office premises, the preliminary design shall not be submitted.  
Depending on the specific circumstances of the office premises project, with the consent of the approval authority, the project proposal, feasibility study report, and preliminary design may be compiled and submitted in combination.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　办公用房项目的审批机关及其审批权限，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 7** The approval authority for office premises projects and its approval authority shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　审批机关应当严格审核办公用房项目，按照建设标准核定建设内容、建设规模和投资概算。

**Article 8** The approval authority shall strictly review office premises projects and verify the construction content, construction scale, and investment estimate in accordance with the construction standards.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　办公用房项目有下列情形之一的，审批机关不得批准：  
(一)属于禁止建设办公用房的情形；  
(二)建设的必要性不充分；  
(三)建设资金来源不符合规定；  
(四)建设内容、建设规模等不符合建设标准；  
(五)其他不得批准的情形。

**Article 9** The approval authority shall not approve an office premises project under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It falls within the circumstances where the construction of office premises is prohibited;  
(2) The necessity for construction is insufficient;  
(3) The source of construction funds does not comply with provisions;  
(4) The construction content, construction scale, etc., do not comply with the construction standards;  
(5) Other circumstances where approval shall not be granted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对未经批准的办公用房项目，不得办理规划、用地、施工等相关手续，不得安排预算、拨付资金。

**Article 10** For office premises projects that have not been approved, the relevant procedures such as planning, land use, and construction shall not be handled, and no budget shall be arranged or funds shall be allocated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　办公用房项目应当按照审批机关核定的建设内容、建设规模和投资概算进行设计、施工。因国家政策调整、价格上涨、地质条件发生重大变化等原因确需增加投资概算的，应当经原审批机关批准。

**Article 11** Office premises projects shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the construction content, construction scale, and investment estimate verified by the approval authority. Where it is truly necessary to increase the investment estimate due to adjustments in national policies, price increases, major changes in geological conditions, or other reasons, approval from the original approval authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　审批机关应当完善审批工作流程，建立健全审批工作责任制和内部监督机制。

**Article 12** The approval authority shall improve the approval workflow and establish and improve the responsibility system for approval work and the internal supervision mechanism.

### 第三章　建设资金

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　办公用房项目的建设资金由预算资金安排。  
办公用房项目的建设资金来源不得有下列情形：  
(一)挪用各类专项资金；  
(二)向单位、个人摊派；  
(三)向银行等金融机构借款；  
(四)接受赞助或者捐赠；  
(五)其他违反规定的情形。

**Article 13** The construction funds for office premises projects shall be arranged from budgetary funds.  
The source of construction funds for office premises projects shall not have any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Misappropriation of various special funds;  
(2) Apportionment to units or individuals;  
(3) Borrowing from financial institutions such as banks;  
(4) Acceptance of sponsorship or donations;  
(5) Other circumstances in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　办公用房项目的建设资金按照国库集中支付制度的有关规定支付。

**Article 14** The construction funds for office premises projects shall be paid in accordance with the relevant provisions of the centralized treasury payment system.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　办公用房项目竣工后，建设单位应当按照规定及时组织编报竣工财务决算，并及时办理固定资产入账手续。

**Article 15** After the completion of an office premises project, the construction entity shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly organize the preparation and submission of the final financial accounts for the completed project, and promptly handle the procedures for recording fixed assets.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审批机关、财政部门、审计机关、监察机关(以下统称监督检查机关)应当按照各自的职责，加强对建设楼堂馆所活动的监督检查和绩效评价。  
监督检查机关应当密切配合，建立信息共享机制，并根据实际需要开展联合监督检查、专项监督检查。

**Article 16** The approval authority, finance department, audit authority, and supervisory authority (hereinafter collectively referred to as the supervision and inspection authorities) shall, in accordance with their respective functions, strengthen the supervision, inspection, and performance evaluation of activities related to the construction of office buildings and guest houses.  
The supervision and inspection authorities shall closely cooperate, establish an information sharing mechanism, and carry out joint supervision and inspection and special supervision and inspection as needed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　监督检查机关对建设楼堂馆所活动实施监督检查，有权要求建设单位以及其他相关单位提供有关文件和资料，向有关人员了解情况，进行现场核查。建设单位以及其他相关单位对监督检查应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 17** When carrying out supervision and inspection of activities related to the construction of office buildings and guest houses, the supervision and inspection authorities shall have the right to require the construction entity and other relevant units to provide relevant documents and materials, inquire about the situation from relevant personnel, and conduct on-site verification. The construction entity and other relevant units shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection and shall not refuse or obstruct it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　建设单位应当按照有关规定加强办公用房项目档案管理，将项目审批和实施过程中的有关文件、资料存档备查。

**Article 18** The construction entity shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, strengthen the archives management of office premises projects, and archive the relevant documents and materials in the process of project approval and implementation for future reference.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　除依法应当保密的情形外，下列信息应当通过政府网站等便于公众知晓的方式公开：  
(一)办公用房项目审批情况，包括建设单位名称、批准的理由以及建设内容、建设规模、投资概算等；增加投资概算的，还应当公开增加投资概算的情况和理由。  
(二)监督检查情况，包括发现的违法建设楼堂馆所的单位名称、基本事实以及处理结果等。

**Article 19** Except for circumstances that shall be kept confidential according to law, the following information shall be disclosed through government websites or other means that are convenient for the public to know:  
(1) The approval status of office premises projects, including the name of the construction entity, the reasons for approval, and the construction content, construction scale, investment estimate, etc.; where the investment estimate is increased, the circumstances and reasons for the increase shall also be disclosed.  
(2) The supervision and inspection status, including the names of units that have illegally constructed office buildings and guest houses, the basic facts, and the results of handling.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对违反本条例规定的行为，任何单位和个人有权向监督检查机关举报。  
监督检查机关应当通过公开举报电话和邮箱、在政府网站设置举报专栏等方式，接受单位和个人的举报，并及时依法处理。

**Article 20** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the supervision and inspection authorities any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations.  
The supervision and inspection authorities shall accept reports from units and individuals by means such as making public the reporting telephone number and email address, setting up a reporting column on the government website, and shall handle them promptly according to law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　机关、团体有下列情形之一的，根据具体情况责令停止相关建设活动或者改正，对所涉楼堂馆所予以收缴、拍卖或者责令限期腾退，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)建设培训中心等各类具有住宿、会议、餐饮等接待功能的场所和设施；  
(二)未经批准建设办公用房；  
(三)以技术业务用房等名义建设办公用房，或者违反规定在技术业务用房中设置办公用房；  
(四)擅自改变建设内容、扩大建设规模，或者擅自增加投资概算；  
(五)办公用房项目建设资金来源不符合规定。

**Article 21** Where a state organ or organization is under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to stop the relevant construction activities or make corrections according to the specific circumstances, the office buildings and guest houses involved shall be confiscated, auctioned, or ordered to be vacated within a time limit, and the leading persons and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Constructing various types of places and facilities with reception functions such as accommodation, meeting, and catering, including training centers;  
(2) Constructing office premises without approval;  
(3) Constructing office premises in the name of technical and business rooms, or setting up office premises in technical and business rooms in violation of regulations;  
(4) Unauthorized change of construction content, expansion of construction scale, or unauthorized increase of investment estimate;  
(5) The source of construction funds for the office premises project does not comply with the provisions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列情形之一的，责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)超越审批权限审批办公用房项目；  
(二)对不符合规定的办公用房项目予以批准；  
(三)对未经批准的办公用房项目办理规划、用地、施工等相关手续，或者安排预算、拨付资金；  
(四)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的情形。

**Article 22** Under any of the following circumstances, corrections shall be ordered, and the leading persons and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Approving office premises projects beyond the approval authority;  
(2) Approving office premises projects that do not comply with the provisions;  
(3) Handling relevant procedures such as planning, land use, and construction for unapproved office premises projects, or arranging budgets or allocating funds;  
(4) Other circumstances of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　建设单位未按照规定将办公用房项目审批和实施过程中的有关文件、资料存档备查，或者转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃有关文件、资料的，责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 23** Where the construction entity fails to archive the relevant documents and materials in the process of project approval and implementation for future reference in accordance with the provisions, or transfers, conceals, alters, or destroys the relevant documents and materials, corrections shall be ordered, and the leading persons and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)至第二十三条规定的处分，由监察机关或者其他有关机关按照管理权限实施，其他处理措施由审批机关实施。

**Article 24** The sanctions provided for in Articles 21 to 23 of these Regulations shall be implemented by the supervisory authority or other relevant authorities according to their management authority, and other handling measures shall be implemented by the approval authority.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例所称团体，是指工会、共青团、妇联等人民团体。

**Article 26** The term "organizations" as used in these Regulations means people's organizations such as trade unions, the Communist Youth League, and the Women's Federation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　财政给予经费保障的事业单位和人民团体以外的其他团体建设楼堂馆所，参照适用本条例。

**Article 27** Where public institutions that receive financial guarantees for funding and other organizations other than people's organizations construct office buildings and guest houses, these Regulations shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　机关、人民团体、财政给予经费保障的事业单位和其他团体维修办公用房，应当严格履行审批程序，执行维修标准。禁止进行超标准装修或者配置超标准的设施。  
违反前款规定的，根据具体情况责令停止维修活动或者改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 28** The repair of office premises by state organs, people's organizations, public institutions that receive financial guarantees for funding, and other organizations shall strictly undergo the approval procedures and comply with the repair standards. It shall be prohibited to carry out decoration or allocate facilities that exceed the standards.  
In case of violation of the preceding paragraph, the repair activities shall be ordered to stop or corrections shall be made according to the specific circumstances, and the leading persons and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　财政给予经费补助的事业单位和人民团体以外的其他团体建设、维修楼堂馆所的管理办法，由国务院发展改革部门会同国务院财政部门、住房城乡建设部门等有关部门以及有关机关事务管理部门按照确有必要、严格控制的原则制定。  
国有企业建设、维修楼堂馆所的管理办法，由国务院发展改革部门会同国务院国有资产监督管理机构、财政部门、住房城乡建设部门等有关部门，按照前款规定的原则制定。

**Article 29** The administrative measures for the construction and repair of office buildings and guest houses by public institutions that receive financial subsidies and other organizations other than people's organizations shall be formulated by the development and reform department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council, the housing and urban-rural construction department, and other relevant departments as well as the relevant government office administration authorities, in accordance with the principle of genuine necessity and strict control.  
The administrative measures for the construction and repair of office buildings and guest houses by state-owned enterprises shall be formulated by the development and reform department of the State Council jointly with the state-owned assets supervision and administration authority of the State Council, the finance department, the housing and urban-rural construction department, and other relevant departments, in accordance with the principle set out in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　军队单位建设、维修楼堂馆所，按照军队的有关规定执行。

**Article 30** The construction and repair of office buildings and guest houses by military units shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the military.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自2017年12月1日起施行。国务院1988年9月22日发布施行的《楼堂馆所建设管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2017. The Provisional Regulations on the Administration of the Construction of Office Buildings and Guest Houses promulgated and implemented by the State Council on September 22, 1988 shall be abolished at the same time.
