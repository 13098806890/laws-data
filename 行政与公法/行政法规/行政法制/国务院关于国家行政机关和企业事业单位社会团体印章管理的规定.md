# 国务院关于国家行政机关和企业事业单位社会团体印章管理的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1999-10-31

> (1999年10月31日国务院发布) 1993年国务院印发的《国务院关于国家行政机关和企业事业单位印章的规定》(国发〔1993〕21号)，对于规范和加强国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体印章的管理工作，起到了重要的作用。但是，随着政府机构的变化，有些条款已不再适用。为进一步规范和加强国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体印章管理，现对国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体印章的制发、收缴和管理规定如下： 一、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体的印章为圆形，中央刊国徽或五角星。 二、国务院的印章，直径6厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图一)，由国务院自制。 三、各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院办公厅、国务院各部委的印章，直径5厘米，中央刊国徽、国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图二)，由国务院制发。 四、国务院直属机构、办事机构的印章，正部级单位的直径5厘米，副部级单位的直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图三)，由国务院制发。 五、国务院直属事业单位的印章，正部级单位的直径5厘米，副部级单位的直径4.5厘米，经国家机构编制管理部门认定具有行政职能的单位的印章中央刊国徽，没有行政职能的单位的印章中央刊五角星，国徽或五角星外刊单位名称，自左而右环行(图四)，由国务院制发。 六、国务院议事协调机构和临时机构的印章，直径5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图五)，由国务院制发。 七、国务院部委管理的国家局的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图六)，由国务院制发。 八、国务院部委的外事司(局)的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图七)，由国务院制发。 国务院部门的内设机构和所属事业单位，法定名称中冠“中华人民共和国”或“国家”的单位的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊单位名称，自左而右环行(图八)，由国务院制发。 九、自治州、市、县级(县、自治县、县级市、旗、自治旗、特区、林区，下同)和市辖区人民政府的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图九)，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制发。 十、地区(盟)行政公署的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十)，由省、自治区人民政府制发。 十一、乡(镇)人民政府的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十一)，由县级人民政府制发。 十二、驻外国的大使馆、领事馆的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十二)，由外交部制发。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体的印章为圆形，中央刊国徽或五角星。

**Article 1** 1. Seals of state administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations shall be circular, with the national emblem or a five-pointed star engraved in the center.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、国务院的印章，直径6厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图一)，由国务院自制。

**Article 2** 2. The seal of the State Council shall be 6 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 1), and shall be made by the State Council itself.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院办公厅、国务院各部委的印章，直径5厘米，中央刊国徽、国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图二)，由国务院制发。

**Article 3** 3. The seals of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and of the General Office of the State Council and the ministries and commissions of the State Council shall be 5 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 2), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、国务院直属机构、办事机构的印章，正部级单位的直径5厘米，副部级单位的直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图三)，由国务院制发。

**Article 4** 4. For seals of organs directly under the State Council and administrative offices, those of units at the full ministerial level shall be 5 cm in diameter, and those of units at the deputy ministerial level shall be 4.5 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 3), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、国务院直属事业单位的印章，正部级单位的直径5厘米，副部级单位的直径4.5厘米，经国家机构编制管理部门认定具有行政职能的单位的印章中央刊国徽，没有行政职能的单位的印章中央刊五角星，国徽或五角星外刊单位名称，自左而右环行(图四)，由国务院制发。

**Article 5** 5. For seals of institutions directly under the State Council, those of units at the full ministerial level shall be 5 cm in diameter, and those of units at the deputy ministerial level shall be 4.5 cm in diameter. For units that are recognized by the state institution establishment administration department as having administrative functions, the national emblem shall be engraved in the center of the seal; for units without administrative functions, a five-pointed star shall be engraved in the center. The name of the unit shall be engraved outside the national emblem or five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 4), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、国务院议事协调机构和临时机构的印章，直径5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图五)，由国务院制发。

**Article 6** 6. The seals of the State Council's consultative coordination bodies and ad hoc bodies shall be 5 cm in diameter, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 5), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、国务院部委管理的国家局的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图六)，由国务院制发。

**Article 7** 7. The seals of state bureaus administered by the ministries and commissions of the State Council shall be 4.5 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 6), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、国务院部委的外事司(局)的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图七)，由国务院制发。  
国务院部门的内设机构和所属事业单位，法定名称中冠“中华人民共和国”或“国家”的单位的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊单位名称，自左而右环行(图八)，由国务院制发。

**Article 8** 8. The seals of the foreign affairs departments (bureaus) under the ministries and commissions of the State Council shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 7), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.  
The seals of internal bodies and subordinate institutions of the departments of the State Council, as well as units whose legal names are prefixed with 'People's Republic of China' or 'State,' shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the unit engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 8), and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、自治州、市、县级(县、自治县、县级市、旗、自治旗、特区、林区，下同)和市辖区人民政府的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图九)，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制发。

**Article 9** 9. The seals of the people's governments of autonomous prefectures, cities, counties (including counties, autonomous counties, county-level cities, banners, autonomous banners, special zones, and forest districts, hereinafter the same), and of municipal districts shall be 4.5 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 9), and shall be made and issued by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、地区(盟)行政公署的印章，直径4.5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十)，由省、自治区人民政府制发。

**Article 10** 10. The seals of administrative offices of prefectures (leagues) shall be 4.5 cm in diameter, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 10), and shall be made and issued by the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、乡(镇)人民政府的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十一)，由县级人民政府制发。

**Article 11** 11. The seals of the people's governments of townships (towns) shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 11), and shall be made and issued by the people's governments at the county level.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、驻外国的大使馆、领事馆的印章，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行(图十二)，由外交部制发。

**Article 12** 12. The seals of embassies and consulates stationed abroad shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 12), and shall be made and issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、国家行政机关内设机构或直属单位的印章，直径不得大于4.5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊单位名称，自左而右环行或者名称前段自左而右环行、后段自左而右横排(图十三)，分别由国务院各部门和地方各级国家行政机关制发。

**Article 13** 13. The seals of internal bodies or units directly under state administrative organs shall not exceed 4.5 cm in diameter, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the unit engraved outside the five-pointed star, either running left to right in a circular pattern, or with the first part running left to right in a circular pattern and the latter part arranged horizontally from left to right (Figure 13). They shall be made and issued respectively by the departments of the State Council and state administrative organs at various local levels.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、企业事业单位、社会团体的印章，直径不得大于4.5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊单位名称，自左而右环行(图十四)。制发办法由公安部会同有关部门另行制定。

**Article 14** 14. The seals of enterprises, institutions, and social organizations shall not exceed 4.5 cm in diameter, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the unit engraved outside the five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern (Figure 14). The measures for making and issuing such seals shall be separately formulated by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with relevant departments.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体印章所刊名称，应为法定名称。如名称字数过多不易刻制，可以采用规范化简称。地区(盟)行政公署的印章，冠省(自治区)的名称。自治州、市、县级人民政府的印章，不冠省(自治区、直辖市)的名称。市辖区人民政府的印章冠市的名称，乡(镇)人民政府的印章，冠县级行政区域的名称。

**Article 15** 15. The name engraved on the seals of state administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations shall be the legal name. If the name is too long to be easily engraved, a standardized abbreviation may be used. The seals of administrative offices of prefectures (leagues) shall be prefixed with the name of the province (autonomous region). The seals of the people's governments of autonomous prefectures, cities, and counties shall not be prefixed with the name of the province (autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government). The seals of the people's governments of municipal districts shall be prefixed with the name of the city, and the seals of the people's governments of townships (towns) shall be prefixed with the name of the county-level administrative area.

<a id="art-16"></a>十六、实行民族区域自治的地方人民政府的印章，可以并刊汉字和相应的民族文字。

**Article 16** 16. The seals of the people's governments of areas implementing regional ethnic autonomy may concurrently display Chinese characters and the corresponding ethnic script.

<a id="art-17"></a>十七、印章所刊汉字，应当使用国务院公布的简化字，字体为宋体。

**Article 17** 17. The Chinese characters engraved on seals shall be simplified Chinese characters as promulgated by the State Council, in Song typeface.

<a id="art-18"></a>十八、印章的质料，由制发机关根据实际需要确定。

**Article 18** 18. The material of seals shall be determined by the issuing organ based on actual needs.

<a id="art-19"></a>十九、各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院各部委、各直属机构印制文件时使用的套印印章、印模，其规格、式样与正式印章等同，由国务院制发。

**Article 19** 19. The overprint seals and seal impressions used by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and by the ministries and commissions and organs directly under the State Council when printing documents, shall have specifications and patterns equivalent to the official seals, and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>二十、国务院有关部委外事用的火漆印，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行，由国务院制发。

**Article 20** 20. The wax seals used for foreign affairs by the relevant ministries and commissions of the State Council shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern, and shall be made and issued by the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>二十一、国务院的钢印，直径4.2厘米，中央刊国徽，国徽外刊机关名称，自左而右环行，由国务院自制。  
地方外事机构、驻外使领馆钢印的规格、式样，由外交部制定。  
其他确需使用钢印的单位，其钢印直径不得大于4.2厘米，不得小于3.5厘米，中央刊五角星，五角星外刊单位名称，自左而右环行，报经其印章制发机关批准后刻制。

**Article 21** 21. The steel seal of the State Council shall be 4.2 cm in diameter, with the national emblem engraved in the center, and the name of the organ engraved outside the national emblem, running left to right in a circular pattern, and shall be made by the State Council itself.  
The specifications and patterns of steel seals used by local foreign affairs agencies and embassies and consulates abroad shall be formulated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.  
For other units that indeed need to use steel seals, the diameter of their steel seals shall not exceed 4.2 cm and shall not be less than 3.5 cm, with a five-pointed star engraved in the center, and the name of the unit engraved outside the five-pointed star, running left to right in a circular pattern. They shall be engraved after reporting to and obtaining approval from the organ that issues their seals.

<a id="art-22"></a>二十二、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体的其他专用印章(包括经济合同章、财务专用章等)，在名称、式样上应与单位正式印章有所区别，经本单位领导批准后可以刻制。

**Article 22** 22. Other special-purpose seals (including economic contract seals, financial seals, etc.) of state administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations shall differ in name and pattern from the unit's official seal, and may be engraved after approval by the leadership of the unit.

<a id="art-23"></a>二十三、印章制发机关应规范和加强印章制发的管理，严格办理程序和审批手续。国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体刻制印章，应到当地公安机关指定的刻章单位刻制。

**Article 23** 23. The seal issuing organs shall standardize and strengthen the management of seal issuance, and strictly follow procedures and approval formalities. State administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations that engrave seals shall go to the engraving units designated by the local public security organs for engraving.

<a id="art-24"></a>二十四、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体的印章，如因单位撤销、名称改变或换用新印章而停止使用时，应及时送交印章制发机关封存或销毁，或者按公安部会同有关部门另行制定的规定处理。

**Article 24** 24. If the seals of state administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations are discontinued due to the dissolution of the unit, change of name, or replacement with a new seal, they shall be promptly delivered to the seal issuing organ for sealing up or destruction, or be handled in accordance with the provisions separately formulated by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with relevant departments.

<a id="art-25"></a>二十五、国家行政机关和企业事业单位、社会团体必须建立健全印章管理制度，加强用印管理，严格审批手续。未经本单位领导批准，不得擅自使用单位印章。

**Article 25** 25. State administrative organs, enterprises, institutions, and social organizations must establish and improve a seal management system, strengthen the management of seal usage, and strictly follow approval formalities. Without approval from the leadership of the unit, the unit's seal shall not be used without authorization.

<a id="art-26"></a>二十六、对伪造印章或使用伪造印章者，要依照国家有关法规查处。如发现伪造印章或使用伪造印章者，应及时向公安机关或印章所刊名称单位举报。具体的印章社会治安管理办法，由公安部会同有关部门制定。

**Article 26** 26. Those who forge seals or use forged seals shall be investigated and punished in accordance with relevant state laws and regulations. If any forgery of seals or use of forged seals is discovered, it shall be promptly reported to the public security organ or the unit whose name is engraved on the seal. The specific measures for the social security management of seals shall be formulated by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with relevant departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>二十七、过去有关印章管理的规定，如有与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
附件：印章规格式样(略)

**Article 27** 27. If any previous provisions on seal management are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
