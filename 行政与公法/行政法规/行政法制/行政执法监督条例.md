# 行政执法监督条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-12-17  
**生效日期**：2026-02-01

> 第六条　县级以上人民政府司法行政部门是本级人民政府的行政执法监督机构，代表本级人民政府承担行政执法监督具体事务，负责实施行政执法监督工作，定期向本级人民政府报告行政执法监督工作情况。 设在乡镇（街道）的司法所协助县级人民政府行政执法监督机构依法开展行政执法监督工作。 第七条　行政执法监督机构承担下列职责： （一）指导监督行政执法工作，组织落实行政执法责任制和责任追究制度； （二）协调行政执法体制改革有关法治事项，协调行政执法争议； （三）组织推动行政执法规范化、正规化、专业化、数字化建设； （四）统筹实施行政执法主体、行政执法人员、行政执法行为、行政执法保障等方面的管理制度； （五）法律、法规规定的其他职责。 第八条　行政执法监督机构应当严格依法履行监督职责，不得以监督代替行政执法，不得滥用职权干预行政执法，避免增加行政执法机关负担。 第二章　监督范围 第九条　行政执法监督机构对行政执法机关贯彻落实党中央、国务院关于行政执法工作的重大决策部署，落实行政执法各项制度，依法开展行政执法等进行监督。 第十条　行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法主体资格的管理，加强对行政执法人员的监督，健全行政执法人员资格、证件管理制度，监督行政执法机关做好行政执法人员准入和退出工作。 第十一条　行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法行为的监督，督促行政执法机关提升行政执法质效，依法开展行政许可、行政处罚、行政强制、行政检查、行政征收征用、行政给付等工作。 行政执法监督机构对行政执法下列情形进行监督： （一）行政执法的主体、人员是否具有合法资格；

### 正文

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　行政执法监督机构承担下列职责：  
（一）指导监督行政执法工作，组织落实行政执法责任制和责任追究制度；  
（二）协调行政执法体制改革有关法治事项，协调行政执法争议；  
（三）组织推动行政执法规范化、正规化、专业化、数字化建设；  
（四）统筹实施行政执法主体、行政执法人员、行政执法行为、行政执法保障等方面的管理制度；  
（五）法律、法规规定的其他职责。

**Article 7** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall assume the following functions and responsibilities:  
(1) guide and supervise administrative law enforcement work, organize the implementation of the administrative law enforcement responsibility system and the accountability system;  
(2) coordinate legal matters related to the reform of the administrative law enforcement system, and coordinate administrative law enforcement disputes;  
(3) organize and promote the standardization, regularization, specialization, and digitalization of administrative law enforcement;  
(4) coordinate the implementation of management systems regarding administrative law enforcement subjects, administrative law enforcement personnel, administrative law enforcement acts, and administrative law enforcement guarantees;  
(5) other functions and responsibilities provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行政执法监督机构应当严格依法履行监督职责，不得以监督代替行政执法，不得滥用职权干预行政执法，避免增加行政执法机关负担。

**Article 8** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strictly perform its supervision duties according to law, and shall not replace administrative law enforcement with supervision, nor abuse its powers to interfere with administrative law enforcement, and shall avoid increasing the burden on administrative law enforcement organs.

### 第二章　监督范围

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政执法监督机构对行政执法机关贯彻落实党中央、国务院关于行政执法工作的重大决策部署，落实行政执法各项制度，依法开展行政执法等进行监督。

**Article 9** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall supervise whether administrative law enforcement organs implement major decisions and arrangements of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council concerning administrative law enforcement work, implement various administrative law enforcement systems, and carry out administrative law enforcement according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法主体资格的管理，加强对行政执法人员的监督，健全行政执法人员资格、证件管理制度，监督行政执法机关做好行政执法人员准入和退出工作。

**Article 10** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strengthen the management of the qualifications of administrative law enforcement subjects, strengthen supervision over administrative law enforcement personnel, improve the management system for the qualifications and certificates of administrative law enforcement personnel, and supervise administrative law enforcement organs in properly carrying out the admission and removal of administrative law enforcement personnel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法行为的监督，督促行政执法机关提升行政执法质效，依法开展行政许可、行政处罚、行政强制、行政检查、行政征收征用、行政给付等工作。  
行政执法监督机构对行政执法下列情形进行监督：  
（一）行政执法的主体、人员是否具有合法资格；  
（二）行政执法程序是否合法；  
（三）行政执法决定是否违法或者明显不当；  
（四）行政执法过程中是否存在有案不立、推诿扯皮、以罚代管、违规异地执法、趋利性执法以及乱收费、乱罚款、乱检查、乱查封等执法不作为、乱作为行为；  
（五）行政执法过程中是否存在简单粗暴等不文明行为；  
（六）行政执法人员是否规范使用证件、标志标识及执法装备，是否按规定着制式服装；  
（七）其他影响行政执法合法性、适当性的情形。

**Article 11** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strengthen supervision over administrative law enforcement acts, urge administrative law enforcement organs to improve the quality and efficiency of administrative law enforcement, and carry out administrative licensing, administrative penalties, administrative compulsion, administrative inspection, administrative levy and requisition, administrative payments, and other work according to law.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall supervise the following circumstances of administrative law enforcement:  
(1) whether the subject and personnel of administrative law enforcement have legal qualifications;  
(2) whether the administrative law enforcement procedure is lawful;  
(3) whether the administrative law enforcement decision is illegal or manifestly inappropriate;  
(4) whether there exist acts of nonfeasance or malfeasance such as failure to file a case when there should be one, shifting responsibilities, replacing management with fines, illegal extraterritorial law enforcement, profit-driven law enforcement, arbitrary charges, arbitrary fines, arbitrary inspections, and arbitrary seizures;  
(5) whether there exist uncivilized acts such as being summary or rough during administrative law enforcement;  
(6) whether administrative law enforcement personnel properly use certificates, signs, logos, and law enforcement equipment, and whether they wear prescribed uniforms according to regulations;  
(7) other circumstances affecting the legality and appropriateness of administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行政执法监督机构对行政执法机关按照国家有关规定落实下列行政执法制度情况进行监督：  
（一）行政执法人员资格、证件管理制度；  
（二）行政执法公示、执法全过程记录、重大执法决定法制审核等行政执法程序制度；  
（三）行政裁量权基准等行政执法标准制度；  
（四）行政执法事项清单管理、行政执法责任确定、行政执法状况评议、行政执法责任追究等相关制度；  
（五）行政执法与刑事司法衔接制度；  
（六）行政执法体制改革中法治建设相关制度；  
（七）与推进全国统一大市场建设相关的行政执法制度；  
（八）其他行政执法制度。

**Article 12** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall supervise the implementation by administrative law enforcement organs of the following administrative law enforcement systems in accordance with relevant state provisions:  
(1) the management system for the qualifications and certificates of administrative law enforcement personnel;  
(2) the administrative law enforcement procedure systems such as the publication of administrative law enforcement, full recording of the law enforcement process, and legal review of major law enforcement decisions;  
(3) the administrative law enforcement standard systems such as the benchmark for administrative discretion;  
(4) the systems related to the list management of administrative law enforcement matters, determination of administrative law enforcement responsibilities, evaluation of the status of administrative law enforcement, and accountability for administrative law enforcement;  
(5) the system for the connection between administrative law enforcement and criminal justice;  
(6) the systems related to the construction of the rule of law in the reform of the administrative law enforcement system;  
(7) the administrative law enforcement systems related to promoting the construction of a unified national market;  
(8) other administrative law enforcement systems.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　行政执法监督机构可以对本级行政执法机关之间涉及行政执法事项清单管理、案件管辖以及跨领域、跨区域行政执法等方面的争议进行协调；经协调不能取得一致意见的，行政执法监督机构应当提出处理意见，按程序报人民政府决定。

**Article 13** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency may coordinate disputes between administrative law enforcement organs at the same level concerning the list management of administrative law enforcement matters, case jurisdiction, and cross-field or cross-regional administrative law enforcement; if consensus cannot be reached through coordination, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall propose handling opinions and submit them to the people's government for decision in accordance with procedures.

### 第三章　监督方式

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　行政执法监督机构根据工作需要，综合运用日常监督、重点监督、专项监督等方式，对行政执法工作进行全方位、全流程、常态化、长效化监督。

**Article 14** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall, based on work needs, comprehensively use methods such as daily supervision, key supervision, and special supervision to carry out all-round, whole-process, normalized, and long-term supervision of administrative law enforcement work.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　行政执法监督机构可以采取法律法规执行情况评估、执法资格确认、执法案卷评查、执法质效评议等方式，对行政执法工作进行日常监督。

**Article 15** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency may adopt methods such as evaluation of the implementation of laws and regulations, confirmation of law enforcement qualifications, review of law enforcement case files, and assessment of law enforcement quality and efficiency to carry out daily supervision of administrative law enforcement work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　行政执法监督机构可以采取问卷调查、个别访谈、实地调研等方式，对行政执法机关执行法律法规情况进行评估。

**Article 16** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency may adopt methods such as questionnaire surveys, individual interviews, and field research to evaluate the implementation of laws and regulations by administrative law enforcement organs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　行政执法监督机构对行政执法主体资格进行确认，对经确认有行政执法主体资格的，按程序向社会公示。  
行政执法监督机构按照规定对行政执法人员资格进行审核，对符合法定条件并通过行政执法资格考试的，制发行政执法证件，确认行政执法人员资格。

**Article 17** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall confirm the qualifications of administrative law enforcement subjects, and for those confirmed to have the qualifications of an administrative law enforcement subject, it shall publicize them to the public in accordance with procedures.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall review the qualifications of administrative law enforcement personnel in accordance with regulations, and for those who meet the statutory conditions and pass the administrative law enforcement qualification examination, it shall issue administrative law enforcement certificates and confirm their qualifications as administrative law enforcement personnel.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行政执法监督机构通过执法案卷评查检查行政执法决定是否合法，是否与违法行为的事实、性质、情节以及社会危害程度相当，以及行政执法文书是否规范，证据是否真实、完整。

**Article 18** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall, through review of law enforcement case files, examine whether the administrative law enforcement decision is lawful, whether it is commensurate with the facts, nature, circumstances, and degree of social harm of the illegal act, whether the administrative law enforcement documents are standardized, and whether the evidence is authentic and complete.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　行政执法监督机构结合行政执法机关的执法特点和具体情况制定评议方案、标准，对行政执法机关的执法质量、执法效果等进行评议。评议标准、过程、结果应当以适当方式公开。

**Article 19** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall, taking into account the law enforcement characteristics and specific circumstances of the administrative law enforcement organs, formulate evaluation plans and standards, and evaluate the law enforcement quality and law enforcement effectiveness of the administrative law enforcement organs. The evaluation standards, process, and results shall be disclosed in an appropriate manner.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行政执法监督机构对企业和群众反映强烈、产生重大社会影响的典型性、代表性行政执法突出问题进行重点监督。  
行政执法监督机构对通过涉企行政执法诉求沟通机制、政务服务便民热线等渠道反映的行政执法问题线索及时进行研判，确定重点监督事项。  
重点监督可以采取挂牌督办、提级监督等方式。

**Article 20** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall conduct key supervision over typical and representative prominent administrative law enforcement issues that have drawn strong complaints from enterprises and the public and have had a major social impact.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall promptly study and assess leads on administrative law enforcement problems reflected through channels such as the communication mechanism for enterprise-related administrative law enforcement appeals and government service convenience hotlines, and determine matters for key supervision.  
Key supervision may adopt methods such as supervision with a listed case or supervision at a higher level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　省级以上人民政府行政执法监督机构根据党中央、国务院决策部署，可以结合人大代表建议、政协提案、监察建议、司法建议、检察建议、行政复议建议等反映的行政执法问题，对关系经济社会发展大局、人民群众切身利益的特定领域、特定问题开展专项监督。  
开展专项监督应当制定工作方案，明确专项监督的责任部门、监督重点、进度安排和工作要求等，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 21** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency of a people's government at or above the provincial level may, based on the decisions and arrangements of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council and taking into account the administrative law enforcement problems reflected in suggestions from people's congress deputies, proposals from the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, supervision suggestions, judicial suggestions, procuratorial suggestions, and administrative reconsideration suggestions, conduct special supervision over specific fields or specific issues that concern the overall situation of economic and social development and the vital interests of the people.  
To carry out special supervision, a work plan shall be formulated, specifying the responsible departments, supervision priorities, schedule, and work requirements, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　开展行政执法监督可以采取下列措施：  
（一）要求行政执法机关自查、说明情况；  
（二）开展检查、访谈、暗访；  
（三）组织座谈、听证、统计、评估；  
（四）调阅、复制与监督事项有关的资料；  
（五）约谈行政执法机关负责人或者相关责任人；  
（六）其他必要的措施。

**Article 22** The following measures may be taken to carry out administrative law enforcement supervision:  
(1) require the administrative law enforcement organ to conduct self-inspection and explain the situation;  
(2) carry out inspections, interviews, and unannounced visits;  
(3) organize seminars, hearings, statistics, and evaluations;  
(4) access and copy materials related to the supervision matters;  
(5) interview the person in charge of the administrative law enforcement organ or the responsible person;  
(6) other necessary measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　行政执法监督机构根据工作需要，可以邀请人大代表、政协委员、行业代表、专家学者、新闻工作者等参加行政执法监督活动。

**Article 23** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency may, based on work needs, invite deputies to people's congresses, members of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, industry representatives, experts and scholars, journalists, and others to participate in administrative law enforcement supervision activities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　行政执法监督机构应当通过开展政策解读、答复有关问题、发布典型案例等方式，加强对行政执法工作的指导，促进行政执法机关和行政执法人员依法履行职责。  
行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法普遍性、规律性问题的研究，总结解决行政执法问题的经验，提出关于改进行政执法工作、完善相关行政执法制度的建议。

**Article 24** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strengthen guidance on administrative law enforcement work by carrying out policy interpretation, answering relevant questions, publishing typical cases, and other means, and promote the performance of duties according to law by administrative law enforcement organs and administrative law enforcement personnel.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strengthen research on universal and regular problems of administrative law enforcement, summarize experience in solving administrative law enforcement problems, and put forward suggestions on improving administrative law enforcement work and perfecting relevant administrative law enforcement systems.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　行政执法监督机构依法履行监督职责时，行政执法机关应当予以配合，任何单位、个人不得干扰、拒绝和阻挠。

**Article 25** When the administrative law enforcement supervision agency performs its supervision duties according to law, the administrative law enforcement organ shall cooperate, and no unit or individual shall interfere, refuse, or obstruct.

### 第四章　监督处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　行政执法监督机构对行政执法监督中发现的问题，根据不同情形制发行政执法监督督办函、行政执法监督意见书或者报请本级人民政府制发行政执法监督决定书等督促有关行政执法机关予以纠正。  
行政执法监督机构对发现的问题能够当场纠正的，应当督促有关行政执法机关立即纠正。

**Article 26** For problems discovered in administrative law enforcement supervision, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall, depending on the circumstances, issue a supervision follow-up letter for administrative law enforcement, a supervision opinion letter for administrative law enforcement, or submit a request to the people's government at the same level to issue a supervision decision for administrative law enforcement, or take other actions to urge the relevant administrative law enforcement organ to make corrections.  
If a problem discovered can be corrected on the spot, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall urge the relevant administrative law enforcement organ to make immediate corrections.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对行政执法机关应当履行而未履行行政执法职责的，行政执法监督机构可以制发行政执法监督督办函，督促其依法履行职责。  
行政执法机关应当按照行政执法监督督办函的要求及时履行行政执法职责，并在规定时限内向行政执法监督机构报送纠正情况。

**Article 27** Where an administrative law enforcement organ should perform but fails to perform its administrative law enforcement duties, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency may issue a supervision follow-up letter for administrative law enforcement to urge it to perform its duties according to law.  
The administrative law enforcement organ shall timely perform its administrative law enforcement duties in accordance with the requirements of the supervision follow-up letter and submit a report on the correction status to the administrative law enforcement supervision agency within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对行政执法监督工作中查明的违法或者明显不当的行政执法问题，行政执法监督机构可以制发行政执法监督意见书，监督有关行政执法机关予以纠正。  
行政执法机关应当按照行政执法监督意见书的要求及时纠正，并在规定时限内向行政执法监督机构报送纠正情况。

**Article 28** For illegal or manifestly inappropriate administrative law enforcement problems identified in administrative law enforcement supervision work, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency may issue a supervision opinion letter for administrative law enforcement to supervise the relevant administrative law enforcement organ to make corrections.  
The administrative law enforcement organ shall timely make corrections in accordance with the requirements of the supervision opinion letter and submit a report on the correction status to the administrative law enforcement supervision agency within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对涉及重大问题或者行政执法机关未按照行政执法监督督办函要求履职、未按照行政执法监督意见书要求纠正的，由行政执法监督机构提出处理建议，报本级人民政府决定；本级人民政府决定纠正的，制发行政执法监督决定书予以纠正。

**Article 29** For matters involving major issues or where an administrative law enforcement organ fails to perform duties as required by the supervision follow-up letter or fails to make corrections as required by the supervision opinion letter, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall propose handling suggestions and submit them to the people's government at the same level for decision; if the people's government at the same level decides to make corrections, it shall issue a supervision decision for administrative law enforcement to make corrections.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　行政执法机关对行政执法监督机构作出的处理结果有异议的，可以向其提出并说明理由，行政执法监督机构应当及时处理。

**Article 30** Where an administrative law enforcement organ has objections to the handling result made by the administrative law enforcement supervision agency, it may raise the objection and state the reasons, and the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall promptly handle the matter.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　行政执法监督机构发现行政执法机关拒不落实行政执法制度或者行政执法行为存在突出问题的，可以在一定范围内进行通报或者向社会公布。

**Article 31** Where the administrative law enforcement supervision agency discovers that an administrative law enforcement organ refuses to implement an administrative law enforcement system or that there are prominent problems in an administrative law enforcement act, it may circulate a notice within a certain scope or make the information public to the society.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　行政执法监督机构在履行监督职责过程中，发现行政执法人员存在违法或者明显不当情形的，综合考虑主客观原因、后果、纠正情况等因素，提出对其作出批评教育、离岗教育、调离执法岗位、取消执法资格等处理的建议，由有权机关依法处理。

**Article 32** During the performance of supervision duties, if the administrative law enforcement supervision agency discovers that an administrative law enforcement personnel has committed an illegal or manifestly inappropriate act, it shall, after comprehensively considering factors such as subjective and objective reasons, consequences, and the status of correction, propose handling suggestions such as criticism and education, off-duty education, removal from the law enforcement post, or revocation of law enforcement qualifications, and the competent organ shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　行政执法监督应当加强与监察监督的贯通协同，健全信息共享和线索移送机制，按照规定程序向监察机关移送行政执法人员违反本条例规定或者涉嫌贪污贿赂、失职渎职等职务违法或者职务犯罪的问题线索。  
行政执法监督应当加强与政府督查、行政复议等的协调衔接，建立健全工作沟通和信息共享机制，提高监督质效，形成监督合力。  
对符合行政复议、行政诉讼受理条件的行政争议，行政执法监督机构应当引导当事人依法申请行政复议、提起行政诉讼；对已经进入行政复议、行政诉讼程序的行政争议，行政执法监督机构不重复监督。

**Article 33** Administrative law enforcement supervision shall strengthen integration and coordination with supervision supervision, improve the mechanisms for information sharing and lead transfer, and transfer to the supervisory organ, in accordance with prescribed procedures, leads on administrative law enforcement personnel who violate the provisions of these Regulations or who are suspected of duty-related illegal or criminal acts such as corruption, bribery, dereliction of duty, or neglect of duty.  
Administrative law enforcement supervision shall strengthen coordination and connection with government supervision, administrative reconsideration, and other mechanisms, establish and improve mechanisms for work communication and information sharing, enhance the quality and efficiency of supervision, and form a synergy of supervision.  
For administrative disputes that meet the conditions for acceptance of administrative reconsideration or administrative action, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall guide the party to apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law; for administrative disputes that have already entered the administrative reconsideration or administrative action procedure, the administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall not conduct duplicate supervision.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各级人民政府应当加强行政执法监督结果运用，并将行政执法监督结果作为法治政府建设成效评价的重要内容。

**Article 34** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the application of the results of administrative law enforcement supervision, and shall take the results of administrative law enforcement supervision as an important part of the evaluation of the effectiveness of the construction of a law-based government.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家统筹加强行政执法监督队伍建设，配备与行政执法监督工作任务相适应的行政执法监督人员。  
行政执法监督机构应当加强对行政执法监督人员的教育培训，提高其政治能力和业务能力。

**Article 35** The State shall make overall plans to strengthen the building of the contingent of administrative law enforcement supervision personnel, and assign administrative law enforcement supervision personnel commensurate with the tasks of administrative law enforcement supervision.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency shall strengthen the education and training of administrative law enforcement supervision personnel to improve their political competence and professional competence.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院行政执法监督机构应当研究制定行政执法规范化建设标准，督促行政执法机关加强行政执法规范化建设。

**Article 36** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency of the State Council shall research and formulate standards for the standardization of administrative law enforcement, and urge administrative law enforcement organs to strengthen the standardization of administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院行政执法监督机构应当提升全国行政执法监督信息一体化水平，对相关行政执法行为信息进行归集，运用大数据、云计算、人工智能等对行政执法过程中存在的问题进行快速预警，实现精准、高效、实时监督。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府行政执法监督机构应当组织建设本行政区域的行政执法监督信息系统，加强数据共享，提高行政执法监督效能。

**Article 37** The administrative law enforcement supervision agency of the State Council shall enhance the level of integration of national administrative law enforcement supervision information, collect information on relevant administrative law enforcement acts, and use big data, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and other technologies to provide rapid early warning of problems in the administrative law enforcement process, achieving precise, efficient, and real-time supervision.  
The administrative law enforcement supervision agency of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the construction of an administrative law enforcement supervision information system for its administrative region, strengthen data sharing, and improve the efficiency of administrative law enforcement supervision.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府应当加强行政执法监督工作保障，将行政执法监督工作所需经费列入本级预算。

**Article 38** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the guarantee for administrative law enforcement supervision work, and shall include the funds needed for administrative law enforcement supervision work in the budgets at the same level.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　行政执法监督机构不依法履职的，由本级人民政府或者上级行政执法监督机构责令限期改正；拒不改正的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 39** Where an administrative law enforcement supervision agency fails to perform its duties according to law, the people's government at the same level or the administrative law enforcement supervision agency at a higher level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　行政执法监督人员在行政执法监督中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where an administrative law enforcement supervision personnel abuses his or her powers, neglects his or her duties, or engages in graft in administrative law enforcement supervision, he or she shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　拒绝、阻挠行政执法监督人员开展监督，故意扰乱行政执法监督工作秩序的，依法给予处分。

**Article 41** Anyone who refuses or obstructs administrative law enforcement supervision personnel from carrying out supervision, or deliberately disrupts the order of administrative law enforcement supervision work, shall be given sanctions according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府部门对本部门所属机构、派出机构行政执法工作的监督检查，实行垂直管理的部门或者实行双重领导并且以上级部门领导为主的部门对所属机构、下级部门行政执法工作的监督检查，参照本条例有关规定执行。  
县级以上地方人民政府依法对设立在本行政区域内实行垂直管理或者实行双重领导并且以上级部门领导为主的行政执法机关遵守和执行法律和政策情况进行监督，将发现的问题及时告知其上一级主管部门。  
设区的市级以上人民政府部门在本级人民政府行政执法监督机构的指导下，依照有关法律规定对下级人民政府相应部门的行政执法工作进行督促指导。

**Article 42** The supervision and inspection by a department of a people's government at or above the county level over the administrative law enforcement work of its subordinate organs and dispatched organs, and the supervision and inspection by a department under vertical management or a department under dual leadership with the leadership of the higher department as the mainstay over the administrative law enforcement work of its subordinate organs and lower-level departments, shall be carried out with reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.  
A local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise the compliance with and implementation of laws and policies by administrative law enforcement organs established within its administrative region that are under vertical management or under dual leadership with the leadership of the higher department as the mainstay, and shall promptly inform the competent department at the next higher level of problems discovered.  
A department of a people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall, under the guidance of the administrative law enforcement supervision agency of the people's government at the same level and in accordance with relevant legal provisions, supervise and guide the administrative law enforcement work of the corresponding department of the lower-level people's government.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　行政执法监督工作中涉及行政执法人员管理、教育培训、行为规范等方面的制度，由国务院行政执法监督机构会同国务院有关部门另行制定。

**Article 43** The systems concerning the management, education and training, and code of conduct of administrative law enforcement personnel in administrative law enforcement supervision work shall be separately formulated by the administrative law enforcement supervision agency of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2026年2月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2026.
