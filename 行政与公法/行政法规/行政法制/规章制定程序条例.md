# 规章制定程序条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-12-22  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

> (2001年11月16日中华人民共和国国务院令第322号公布　根据2017年12月22日《国务院关于修改〈规章制定程序条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范规章制定程序，保证规章质量，根据立法法的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　规章的立项、起草、审查、决定、公布、解释，适用本条例。 违反本条例规定制定的规章无效。 第三条　制定规章，应当贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，遵循立法法确定的立法原则，符合宪法、法律、行政法规和其他上位法的规定。 没有法律或者国务院的行政法规、决定、命令的依据，部门规章不得设定减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务的规范，不得增加本部门的权力或者减少本部门的法定职责。没有法律、行政法规、地方性法规的依据，地方政府规章不得设定减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务的规范。 第四条　制定政治方面法律的配套规章，应当按照有关规定及时报告党中央或者同级党委(党组)。 制定重大经济社会方面的规章，应当按照有关规定及时报告同级党委(党组)。 第五条　制定规章，应当切实保障公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，在规定其应当履行的义务的同时，应当规定其相应的权利和保障权利实现的途径。 制定规章，应当体现行政机关的职权与责任相统一的原则，在赋予有关行政机关必要的职权的同时，应当规定其行使职权的条件、程序和应承担的责任。 第六条　制定规章，应当体现全面深化改革精神，科学规范行政行为，促进政府职能向宏观调控、市场监管、社会管理、公共服务、环境保护等方面转变。 制定规章，应当符合精简、统一、效能的原则，相同或者相近的职能应当规定由一个行政机关承担，简化行政管理手续。 第七条　规章的名称一般称“规定”、“办法”，但不得称“条例”。 第八条　规章用语应当准确、简洁，条文内容应当明确、具体，具有可操作性。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范规章制定程序，保证规章质量，根据立法法的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Legislation Law for the purpose of regulating the rule-making procedures and ensuring the quality of rules.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　规章的立项、起草、审查、决定、公布、解释，适用本条例。  
违反本条例规定制定的规章无效。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the initiation, drafting, review, decision, promulgation, and interpretation of rules.  
Rules formulated in violation of these Regulations shall be null and void.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　制定规章，应当贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，遵循立法法确定的立法原则，符合宪法、法律、行政法规和其他上位法的规定。  
没有法律或者国务院的行政法规、决定、命令的依据，部门规章不得设定减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务的规范，不得增加本部门的权力或者减少本部门的法定职责。没有法律、行政法规、地方性法规的依据，地方政府规章不得设定减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务的规范。

**Article 3** In formulating rules, the Party's line, principles, policies, and decisions shall be implemented, the legislative principles established by the Legislation Law shall be followed, and the provisions of the Constitution, laws, administrative regulations, and other higher-level legal norms shall be complied with.  
Without the basis of laws or the administrative regulations, decisions, or orders of the State Council, departmental rules shall not establish norms that reduce the rights of or increase the obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, nor shall they increase the powers of the department or reduce its statutory duties. Without the basis of laws, administrative regulations, or local regulations, local government rules shall not establish norms that reduce the rights of or increase the obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　制定政治方面法律的配套规章，应当按照有关规定及时报告党中央或者同级党委(党组)。  
制定重大经济社会方面的规章，应当按照有关规定及时报告同级党委(党组)。

**Article 4** In formulating supporting rules for laws regarding political matters, reports shall be made in a timely manner to the Central Committee of the Party or the Party committee (Party group) at the same level in accordance with relevant provisions.  
In formulating rules regarding major economic and social matters, reports shall be made in a timely manner to the Party committee (Party group) at the same level in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　制定规章，应当切实保障公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，在规定其应当履行的义务的同时，应当规定其相应的权利和保障权利实现的途径。  
制定规章，应当体现行政机关的职权与责任相统一的原则，在赋予有关行政机关必要的职权的同时，应当规定其行使职权的条件、程序和应承担的责任。

**Article 5** In formulating rules, the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall be effectively protected, and while stipulating the obligations they shall perform, corresponding rights and the means to ensure the realization of such rights shall also be provided.  
In formulating rules, the principle of unifying the functions and powers of administrative organs with their responsibilities shall be reflected, and while granting the relevant administrative organs necessary functions and powers, the conditions, procedures, and responsibilities for exercising such functions and powers shall be stipulated.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　制定规章，应当体现全面深化改革精神，科学规范行政行为，促进政府职能向宏观调控、市场监管、社会管理、公共服务、环境保护等方面转变。  
制定规章，应当符合精简、统一、效能的原则，相同或者相近的职能应当规定由一个行政机关承担，简化行政管理手续。

**Article 6** In formulating rules, the spirit of comprehensively deepening reform shall be reflected, administrative acts shall be scientifically regulated, and the transformation of government functions towards macroeconomic regulation, market supervision, social management, public services, environmental protection, and other aspects shall be promoted.  
In formulating rules, the principles of simplification, unity, and efficiency shall be complied with, identical or similar functions shall be stipulated to be undertaken by one administrative organ, and administrative procedures shall be simplified.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　规章的名称一般称“规定”、“办法”，但不得称“条例”。

**Article 7** The names of rules shall generally be "provisions" or "measures", but shall not be called "regulations".

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　规章用语应当准确、简洁，条文内容应当明确、具体，具有可操作性。  
法律、法规已经明确规定的内容，规章原则上不作重复规定。  
除内容复杂的外，规章一般不分章、节。

**Article 8** The wording of rules shall be precise and concise, and the content of articles shall be clear, specific, and operable.  
In principle, rules shall not duplicate provisions already clearly specified in laws and regulations.  
Unless the content is complex, rules shall generally not be divided into chapters or sections.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　涉及国务院两个以上部门职权范围的事项，制定行政法规条件尚不成熟，需要制定规章的，国务院有关部门应当联合制定规章。  
有前款规定情形的，国务院有关部门单独制定的规章无效。

**Article 9** Where a matter involves the scope of functions and powers of two or more departments of the State Council, and the conditions for formulating administrative regulations are not yet mature, making it necessary to formulate rules, the relevant departments of the State Council shall jointly formulate rules.  
In the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, rules formulated separately by a relevant department of the State Council shall be null and void.

### 第二章　立项

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院部门内设机构或者其他机构认为需要制定部门规章的，应当向该部门报请立项。  
省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府所属工作部门或者下级人民政府认为需要制定地方政府规章的，应当向该省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市、自治州的人民政府报请立项。  
国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府，可以向社会公开征集规章制定项目建议。

**Article 10** Where an internal organ or other institution of a department of the State Council considers it necessary to formulate departmental rules, it shall submit a request for project initiation to the said department.  
Where a subordinate working department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, a city divided into districts, or an autonomous prefecture, or a lower-level people's government considers it necessary to formulate local government rules, it shall submit a request for project initiation to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, city divided into districts, or autonomous prefecture.  
Departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures may publicly solicit proposals for rule-making projects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　报送制定规章的立项申请，应当对制定规章的必要性、所要解决的主要问题、拟确立的主要制度等作出说明。

**Article 11** An application for project initiation for formulating rules shall include explanations on the necessity of formulating the rules, the main problems to be solved, the main systems to be established, and other matters.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院部门法制机构，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府法制机构(以下简称法制机构)，应当对制定规章的立项申请和公开征集的规章制定项目建议进行评估论证，拟订本部门、本级人民政府年度规章制定工作计划，报本部门、本级人民政府批准后向社会公布。  
年度规章制定工作计划应当明确规章的名称、起草单位、完成时间等。

**Article 12** The legal affairs department of a department of the State Council, and the legal affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, a city divided into districts, or an autonomous prefecture (hereinafter referred to as the legal affairs department), shall evaluate and review the applications for project initiation for formulating rules and the publicly solicited proposals for rule-making projects, prepare the annual rule-making work plan of the department or the people's government at the same level, and, after submission to and approval by the department or the people's government at the same level, publish it to the public.  
The annual rule-making work plan shall specify the name of the rules, the drafting unit, the completion time, and other matters.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府，应当加强对执行年度规章制定工作计划的领导。对列入年度规章制定工作计划的项目，承担起草工作的单位应当抓紧工作，按照要求上报本部门或者本级人民政府决定。  
法制机构应当及时跟踪了解本部门、本级人民政府年度规章制定工作计划执行情况，加强组织协调和督促指导。  
年度规章制定工作计划在执行中，可以根据实际情况予以调整，对拟增加的规章项目应当进行补充论证。

**Article 13** Departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall strengthen leadership over the implementation of the annual rule-making work plan. For projects included in the annual rule-making work plan, the unit undertaking the drafting work shall expedite its efforts and submit the matter to the department or the people's government at the same level for decision in accordance with the requirements.  
The legal affairs department shall promptly track and understand the implementation of the annual rule-making work plan of the department or the people's government at the same level, and strengthen organizational coordination, supervision, and guidance.  
The annual rule-making work plan may be adjusted according to actual circumstances during implementation, and supplementary review shall be conducted for proposed additional rule projects.

### 第三章　起草

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　部门规章由国务院部门组织起草，地方政府规章由省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府组织起草。  
国务院部门可以确定规章由其一个或者几个内设机构或者其他机构具体负责起草工作，也可以确定由其法制机构起草或者组织起草。  
省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府可以确定规章由其一个部门或者几个部门具体负责起草工作，也可以确定由其法制机构起草或者组织起草。

**Article 14** Departmental rules shall be organized for drafting by a department of the State Council, and local government rules shall be organized for drafting by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, a city divided into districts, or an autonomous prefecture.  
A department of the State Council may designate one or several of its internal organs or other institutions to be specifically responsible for the drafting of rules, or it may designate its legal affairs department to draft or organize the drafting.  
The people's government of a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, a city divided into districts, or an autonomous prefecture may designate one or several of its departments to be specifically responsible for the drafting of rules, or it may designate its legal affairs department to draft or organize the drafting.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　起草规章，应当深入调查研究，总结实践经验，广泛听取有关机关、组织和公民的意见。听取意见可以采取书面征求意见、座谈会、论证会、听证会等多种形式。  
起草规章，除依法需要保密的外，应当将规章草案及其说明等向社会公布，征求意见。向社会公布征求意见的期限一般不少于30日。  
起草专业性较强的规章，可以吸收相关领域的专家参与起草工作，或者委托有关专家、教学科研单位、社会组织起草。

**Article 15** In drafting rules, thorough investigation and research shall be conducted, practical experience shall be summarized, and opinions of relevant organs, organizations, and citizens shall be extensively solicited. Opinions may be solicited through various forms such as written solicitation, symposia, discussion meetings, and hearings.  
In drafting rules, unless confidentiality is required by law, the draft rules and their explanations shall be published to the public for comments. The period for public comments shall generally be no less than 30 days.  
In drafting rules with high professional requirements, experts in relevant fields may be involved in the drafting work, or the drafting may be entrusted to relevant experts, teaching and research institutions, or social organizations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　起草规章，涉及社会公众普遍关注的热点难点问题和经济社会发展遇到的突出矛盾，减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务，对社会公众有重要影响等重大利益调整事项的，起草单位应当进行论证咨询，广泛听取有关方面的意见。  
起草的规章涉及重大利益调整或者存在重大意见分歧，对公民、法人或者其他组织的权利义务有较大影响，人民群众普遍关注，需要进行听证的，起草单位应当举行听证会听取意见。听证会依照下列程序组织：  
(一)听证会公开举行，起草单位应当在举行听证会的30日前公布听证会的时间、地点和内容；  
(二)参加听证会的有关机关、组织和公民对起草的规章，有权提问和发表意见；  
(三)听证会应当制作笔录，如实记录发言人的主要观点和理由；  
(四)起草单位应当认真研究听证会反映的各种意见，起草的规章在报送审查时，应当说明对听证会意见的处理情况及其理由。

**Article 16** Where the drafting of rules involves hot and difficult issues of common public concern, prominent contradictions encountered in economic and social development, reduction of rights of or increase of obligations of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, or matters of significant interest adjustment that have a major impact on the public, the drafting unit shall conduct demonstration and consultation and extensively solicit opinions from relevant parties.  
Where the drafted rules involve significant interest adjustment or major differences of opinion, have a considerable impact on the rights and obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, are of common public concern, and require a hearing, the drafting unit shall hold a hearing to solicit opinions. The hearing shall be organized in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The hearing shall be held publicly, and the drafting unit shall announce the time, place, and content of the hearing at least 30 days before the hearing is held;  
(2) Relevant organs, organizations, and citizens participating in the hearing shall have the right to ask questions and express opinions on the drafted rules;  
(3) Minutes of the hearing shall be made, faithfully recording the main viewpoints and reasons of the speakers;  
(4) The drafting unit shall carefully study the various opinions reflected in the hearing, and when submitting the drafted rules for review, it shall explain the handling of the opinions from the hearing and the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　起草部门规章，涉及国务院其他部门的职责或者与国务院其他部门关系紧密的，起草单位应当充分征求国务院其他部门的意见。  
起草地方政府规章，涉及本级人民政府其他部门的职责或者与其他部门关系紧密的，起草单位应当充分征求其他部门的意见。起草单位与其他部门有不同意见的，应当充分协商；经过充分协商不能取得一致意见的，起草单位应当在上报规章草案送审稿(以下简称规章送审稿)时说明情况和理由。

**Article 17** In drafting departmental rules that involve the functions of other departments of the State Council or are closely related to other departments of the State Council, the drafting unit shall fully solicit the opinions of such other departments.  
In drafting local government rules that involve the functions of other departments of the people's government at the same level or are closely related to other departments, the drafting unit shall fully solicit the opinions of such other departments. Where the drafting unit and other departments have differing opinions, full consultation shall be conducted; if no consensus can be reached after full consultation, the drafting unit shall explain the circumstances and reasons when submitting the draft rules for review (hereinafter referred to as the draft rules for review).

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　起草单位应当将规章送审稿及其说明、对规章送审稿主要问题的不同意见和其他有关材料按规定报送审查。  
报送审查的规章送审稿，应当由起草单位主要负责人签署；几个起草单位共同起草的规章送审稿，应当由该几个起草单位主要负责人共同签署。  
规章送审稿的说明应当对制定规章的必要性、规定的主要措施、有关方面的意见及其协调处理情况等作出说明。  
有关材料主要包括所规范领域的实际情况和相关数据、实践中存在的主要问题、汇总的意见、听证会笔录、调研报告、国内外有关立法资料等。

**Article 18** The drafting unit shall submit the draft rules for review, their explanations, different opinions on the main issues of the draft rules for review, and other relevant materials for review in accordance with the provisions.  
The draft rules for review submitted for review shall be signed by the principal responsible person of the drafting unit; where the draft rules for review are jointly drafted by several drafting units, they shall be jointly signed by the principal responsible persons of those drafting units.  
The explanations of the draft rules for review shall include explanations on the necessity of formulating the rules, the main measures provided for, the opinions of relevant parties and their coordination and handling, and other matters.  
The relevant materials mainly include the actual situation and relevant data of the regulated field, the main problems existing in practice, the summaries of opinions, the minutes of hearings, investigation reports, relevant domestic and foreign legislative materials, etc.

### 第四章　审查

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　规章送审稿由法制机构负责统一审查。法制机构主要从以下方面对送审稿进行审查：  
(一)是否符合[本条例第三条](#art-3)、第四条、第五条、第六条的规定；  
(二)是否符合社会主义核心价值观的要求；  
(三)是否与有关规章协调、衔接；  
(四)是否正确处理有关机关、组织和公民对规章送审稿主要问题的意见；  
(五)是否符合立法技术要求；  
(六)需要审查的其他内容。

**Article 19** The legal affairs department shall be responsible for the unified review of the draft rules for review. The legal affairs department shall mainly review the draft from the following aspects:  
(1) Whether it complies with the provisions of Articles 3, 4, 5, and 6 of these Regulations;  
(2) Whether it meets the requirements of the core values of socialism;  
(3) Whether it is coordinated and aligned with relevant rules;  
(4) Whether the opinions of relevant organs, organizations, and citizens on the main issues of the draft rules for review have been properly addressed;  
(5) Whether it meets the technical requirements of legislation;  
(6) Other content requiring review.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　规章送审稿有下列情形之一的，法制机构可以缓办或者退回起草单位：  
(一)制定规章的基本条件尚不成熟或者发生重大变化的；  
(二)有关机构或者部门对规章送审稿规定的主要制度存在较大争议，起草单位未与有关机构或者部门充分协商的；  
(三)未按照本条例有关规定公开征求意见的；  
(四)上报送审稿不符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的。

**Article 20** Where the draft rules for review fall under any of the following circumstances, the legal affairs department may postpone the processing or return the draft to the drafting unit:  
(1) The basic conditions for formulating the rules are not yet mature or have undergone significant changes;  
(2) There are substantial disputes among relevant institutions or departments over the main systems stipulated in the draft rules for review, and the drafting unit has not fully consulted with such institutions or departments;  
(3) Public comments have not been solicited in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) The submitted draft for review does not comply with the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　法制机构应当将规章送审稿或者规章送审稿涉及的主要问题发送有关机关、组织和专家征求意见。  
法制机构可以将规章送审稿或者修改稿及其说明等向社会公布，征求意见。向社会公布征求意见的期限一般不少于30日。

**Article 21** The legal affairs department shall send the draft rules for review or the main issues involved in the draft rules for review to relevant organs, organizations, and experts for comments.  
The legal affairs department may publish the draft rules for review or the revised draft and their explanations to the public for comments. The period for public comments shall generally be no less than 30 days.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　法制机构应当就规章送审稿涉及的主要问题，深入基层进行实地调查研究，听取基层有关机关、组织和公民的意见。

**Article 22** The legal affairs department shall conduct in-depth field investigations and research at the grassroots level regarding the main issues involved in the draft rules for review, and solicit opinions from relevant grassroots organs, organizations, and citizens.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　规章送审稿涉及重大利益调整的，法制机构应当进行论证咨询，广泛听取有关方面的意见。论证咨询可以采取座谈会、论证会、听证会、委托研究等多种形式。  
规章送审稿涉及重大利益调整或者存在重大意见分歧，对公民、法人或者其他组织的权利义务有较大影响，人民群众普遍关注，起草单位在起草过程中未举行听证会的，法制机构经本部门或者本级人民政府批准，可以举行听证会。举行听证会的，应当依照[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定的程序组织。

**Article 23** Where the draft rules for review involve significant interest adjustment, the legal affairs department shall conduct demonstration and consultation and extensively solicit opinions from relevant parties. Demonstration and consultation may take various forms such as symposia, discussion meetings, hearings, and commissioned research.  
Where the draft rules for review involve significant interest adjustment or major differences of opinion, have a considerable impact on the rights and obligations of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, are of common public concern, and the drafting unit did not hold a hearing during the drafting process, the legal affairs department, upon approval by the department or the people's government at the same level, may hold a hearing. Where a hearing is held, it shall be organized in accordance with the procedures provided in Article 16 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有关机构或者部门对规章送审稿涉及的主要措施、管理体制、权限分工等问题有不同意见的，法制机构应当进行协调，力求达成一致意见。对有较大争议的重要立法事项，法制机构可以委托有关专家、教学科研单位、社会组织进行评估。  
经过充分协调不能达成一致意见的，法制机构应当将主要问题、有关机构或者部门的意见和法制机构的意见及时报本部门或者本级人民政府领导协调，或者报本部门或者本级人民政府决定。

**Article 24** Where relevant institutions or departments have differing opinions on issues such as the main measures, management system, and division of authority involved in the draft rules for review, the legal affairs department shall conduct coordination to strive to reach consensus. For important legislative matters with substantial controversy, the legal affairs department may entrust relevant experts, teaching and research institutions, or social organizations to conduct an evaluation.  
If no consensus can be reached after full coordination, the legal affairs department shall promptly report the main issues, the opinions of the relevant institutions or departments, and its own opinions to the leadership of the department or the people's government at the same level for coordination, or submit them to the department or the people's government at the same level for decision.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　法制机构应当认真研究各方面的意见，与起草单位协商后，对规章送审稿进行修改，形成规章草案和对草案的说明。说明应当包括制定规章拟解决的主要问题、确立的主要措施以及与有关部门的协调情况等。  
规章草案和说明由法制机构主要负责人签署，提出提请本部门或者本级人民政府有关会议审议的建议。

**Article 25** The legal affairs department shall carefully study opinions from all sides, and after consulting with the drafting unit, revise the draft rules for review to form a draft rule and an explanation of the draft. The explanation shall include the main problems to be solved by formulating the rules, the main measures established, and the coordination with relevant departments, among other matters.  
The draft rule and the explanation shall be signed by the principal responsible person of the legal affairs department, who shall put forward a proposal for submission to the relevant meeting of the department or the people's government at the same level for deliberation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　法制机构起草或者组织起草的规章草案，由法制机构主要负责人签署，提出提请本部门或者本级人民政府有关会议审议的建议。

**Article 26** Where the draft rule is drafted or organized for drafting by the legal affairs department, it shall be signed by the principal responsible person of the legal affairs department, who shall put forward a proposal for submission to the relevant meeting of the department or the people's government at the same level for deliberation.

### 第五章　决定和公布

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　部门规章应当经部务会议或者委员会会议决定。  
地方政府规章应当经政府常务会议或者全体会议决定。

**Article 27** Departmental rules shall be decided by a ministerial meeting or a committee meeting.  
Local government rules shall be decided by the executive meeting of the government or the plenary meeting of the government.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　审议规章草案时，由法制机构作说明，也可以由起草单位作说明。

**Article 28** When deliberating the draft rule, the legal affairs department shall provide explanations, or the drafting unit may provide explanations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　法制机构应当根据有关会议审议意见对规章草案进行修改，形成草案修改稿，报请本部门首长或者省长、自治区主席、市长、自治州州长签署命令予以公布。

**Article 29** The legal affairs department shall revise the draft rule in accordance with the deliberation opinions of the relevant meeting, form a revised draft, and submit it to the head of the department or the governor of the province, the chairman of the autonomous region, the mayor of the municipality, or the prefect of the autonomous prefecture for signing an order for promulgation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公布规章的命令应当载明该规章的制定机关、序号、规章名称、通过日期、施行日期、部门首长或者省长、自治区主席、市长、自治州州长署名以及公布日期。  
部门联合规章由联合制定的部门首长共同署名公布，使用主办机关的命令序号。

**Article 30** The order promulgating rules shall specify the enacting organ, serial number, title of the rules, date of adoption, date of entry into force, signature of the head of the department or the governor of the province, the chairman of the autonomous region, the mayor of the municipality, or the prefect of the autonomous prefecture, and the date of promulgation.  
Joint departmental rules shall be signed and promulgated jointly by the heads of the jointly formulating departments, using the serial number of the order of the leading organ.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　部门规章签署公布后，及时在国务院公报或者部门公报和中国政府法制信息网以及在全国范围内发行的报纸上刊载。  
地方政府规章签署公布后，及时在本级人民政府公报和中国政府法制信息网以及在本行政区域范围内发行的报纸上刊载。  
在国务院公报或者部门公报和地方人民政府公报上刊登的规章文本为标准文本。

**Article 31** After departmental rules are signed and promulgated, they shall be promptly published in the State Council Bulletin or the departmental bulletin, the China Government Legal Information Network, and newspapers distributed nationwide.  
After local government rules are signed and promulgated, they shall be promptly published in the bulletin of the people's government at the same level, the China Government Legal Information Network, and newspapers distributed within the administrative region.  
The text of rules published in the State Council Bulletin or the departmental bulletin and the local people's government bulletin shall be the standard text.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　规章应当自公布之日起30日后施行；但是，涉及国家安全、外汇汇率、货币政策的确定以及公布后不立即施行将有碍规章施行的，可以自公布之日起施行。

**Article 32** Rules shall become effective 30 days after the date of promulgation; however, where the rules involve national security, foreign exchange rates, or determination of monetary policy, or where failure to take effect immediately after promulgation would impede the implementation of the rules, they may become effective on the date of promulgation.

### 第六章　解释与备案

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　规章解释权属于规章制定机关。  
规章有下列情形之一的，由制定机关解释：  
(一)规章的规定需要进一步明确具体含义的；  
(二)规章制定后出现新的情况，需要明确适用规章依据的。  
规章解释由规章制定机关的法制机构参照规章送审稿审查程序提出意见，报请制定机关批准后公布。  
规章的解释同规章具有同等效力。

**Article 33** The power to interpret rules belongs to the enacting organ.  
Rules shall be interpreted by the enacting organ under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The provisions of the rules need to further clarify their specific meaning;  
(2) New circumstances arise after the formulation of the rules, requiring clarification of the basis for applying the rules.  
The interpretation of rules shall be proposed by the legal affairs department of the enacting organ with reference to the review procedure for draft rules for review, and shall be published after being approved by the enacting organ.  
The interpretation of rules shall have the same effect as the rules themselves.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　规章应当自公布之日起30日内，由法制机构依照立法法和《法规规章备案条例》的规定向有关机关备案。

**Article 34** Within 30 days from the date of promulgation, the rules shall be submitted by the legal affairs department to the relevant organ for record in accordance with the Legislation Law and the Regulations on the Filing of Regulations and Rules.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织、公民认为规章同法律、行政法规相抵触的，可以向国务院书面提出审查的建议，由国务院法制机构研究并提出处理意见，按照规定程序处理。  
国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织、公民认为设区的市、自治州的人民政府规章同法律、行政法规相抵触或者违反其他上位法的规定的，也可以向本省、自治区人民政府书面提出审查的建议，由省、自治区人民政府法制机构研究并提出处理意见，按照规定程序处理。

**Article 35** Where state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, or citizens consider that rules conflict with laws or administrative regulations, they may submit a written proposal for review to the State Council, and the legal affairs department of the State Council shall study and propose opinions for handling, which shall be handled in accordance with the prescribed procedures.  
Where state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, or citizens consider that rules of the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture conflict with laws or administrative regulations or violate the provisions of other higher-level legal norms, they may also submit a written proposal for review to the people's government of the province or autonomous region, and the legal affairs department of the people's government of the province or autonomous region shall study and propose opinions for handling, which shall be handled in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　依法不具有规章制定权的县级以上地方人民政府制定、发布具有普遍约束力的决定、命令，参照本条例规定的程序执行。

**Article 36** Where a local people's government at or above the county level that does not have the power to formulate rules according to law formulates and issues decisions or orders with general binding force, the procedures provided in these Regulations shall be followed mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府，应当根据全面深化改革、经济社会发展需要以及上位法规定，及时组织开展规章清理工作。对不适应全面深化改革和经济社会发展要求、不符合上位法规定的规章，应当及时修改或者废止。

**Article 37** Departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures shall, in light of comprehensively deepening reform, the needs of economic and social development, and the provisions of higher-level legal norms, promptly organize and carry out the work of reviewing rules. Rules that are not adapted to the requirements of comprehensively deepening reform and economic and social development, or that do not comply with the provisions of higher-level legal norms, shall be promptly amended or abolished.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院部门，省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州的人民政府，可以组织对有关规章或者规章中的有关规定进行立法后评估，并把评估结果作为修改、废止有关规章的重要参考。

**Article 38** Departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities divided into districts, and autonomous prefectures may organize post-legislative evaluation of relevant rules or relevant provisions therein, and shall take the evaluation results as an important reference for amending or abolishing relevant rules.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　规章的修改、废止程序适用本条例的有关规定。  
规章修改、废止后，应当及时公布。

**Article 39** The procedures for amending and abolishing rules shall be subject to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.  
After rules are amended or abolished, they shall be promptly published.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　编辑出版正式版本、民族文版、外文版本的规章汇编，由法制机构依照《法规汇编编辑出版管理规定》的有关规定执行。

**Article 40** The editing and publishing of official, ethnic languages, and foreign language versions of the compilation of rules shall be carried out by the legal affairs department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Provisions on the Administration of the Editing and Publishing of Collections of Laws and Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
