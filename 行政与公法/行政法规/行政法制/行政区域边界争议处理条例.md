# 行政区域边界争议处理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1989-02-03  
**生效日期**：1989-02-03

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了妥善处理行政区域边界争议，以利于安定团结，保障社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the proper handling of disputes over administrative area boundaries, in order to facilitate stability and unity, and to ensure the smooth progress of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称的边界争议是指省、自治区、直辖市之间，自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区之间，乡、民族乡、镇之间，双方人民政府对毗邻行政区域界线的争议。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "boundary disputes" refer to disputes between the people's governments of two parties concerning the boundaries of adjacent administrative areas, such as between provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; between autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities, and municipal districts; and between townships, ethnic townships, and towns.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　处理因行政区域界线不明确而发生的边界争议，应当按照有利于各族人民的团结，有利于国家的统一管理，有利于保护、开发和利用自然资源的原则，由争议双方人民政府从实际情况出发，兼顾当地双方群众的生产和生活，实事求是，互谅互让地协商解决。经争议双方协商未达成协议的，由争议双方的上级人民政府决定。必要时，可以按照行政区划管理的权限，通过变更行政区域的方法解决。  
解决边界争议，必须明确划定争议地区的行政区域界线。

**Article 3** Boundary disputes arising from unclear administrative area boundaries shall be resolved through consultation between the people's governments of the two parties, in accordance with the principles of benefiting the unity of all ethnic groups, facilitating the unified administration of the State, and protecting, developing and utilizing natural resources, proceeding from the actual situation, taking into account the production and livelihood of the local people of both parties, seeking truth from facts, and making mutual understanding and mutual concessions. Where no agreement is reached through consultation between the two parties, the matter shall be decided by the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties. When necessary, the dispute may be resolved by changing the administrative area in accordance with the authority of administrative division management.  
In resolving a boundary dispute, the administrative area boundary of the disputed area must be clearly delimited.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下列已明确划定或者核定的行政区域界线，必须严格遵守：  
(一)根据行政区划管理的权限，上级人民政府在确定行政区划时明确划定的界线；  
(二)由双方人民政府或者双方的上级人民政府明确划定的争议地区的界线；  
(三)发生边界争议之前，由双方人民政府核定一致的界线。

**Article 4** The following administrative area boundaries that have been clearly delimited or verified must be strictly observed:  
(1) Boundaries clearly delimited by the people's government at a higher level when determining administrative divisions in accordance with the authority of administrative division management;  
(2) Boundaries of the disputed area clearly delimited by the people's governments of both parties or the people's government at a higher level of both parties;  
(3) Boundaries verified as consistent by the people's governments of both parties before the occurrence of a boundary dispute.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　争议双方人民政府的负责人，必须对国家和人民负责，顾全大局，及时解决边界争议，不得推诿和拖延。

**Article 5** The responsible persons of the people's governments of the two disputing parties must be responsible to the State and the people, take the overall situation into consideration, and resolve boundary disputes in a timely manner, and shall not shirk or delay.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民政部是国务院处理边界争议的主管部门。  
县级以上的地方各级人民政府的民政部门是本级人民政府处理边界争议的主管部门。

**Article 6** The Ministry of Civil Affairs shall be the competent department of the State Council for handling boundary disputes.  
The civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be the competent departments of the respective people's governments for handling boundary disputes.

### 第二章　处理依据

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列文件和材料，作为处理边界争议的依据：  
(一)国务院(含政务院及其授权的主管部门)批准的行政区划文件或者边界线地图；  
(二)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准的不涉及毗邻省、自治区、直辖市的行政区划文件或者边界线地图；  
(三)争议双方的上级人民政府(含军政委员会、人民行政公署)解决边界争议的文件和所附边界线地图；  
(四)争议双方人民政府解决边界争议的协议和所附边界线地图；  
(五)发生边界争议之前，经双方人民政府核定一致的边界线文件或者盖章的边界线地图。

**Article 7** The following documents and materials shall serve as the basis for handling boundary disputes:  
(1) Documents on administrative divisions or boundary maps approved by the State Council (including the Government Administration Council and its authorized competent departments);  
(2) Documents on administrative divisions or boundary maps approved by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government that do not involve adjacent provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) Documents and attached boundary maps of the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties (including military and political committees, people's administrative offices) resolving the boundary dispute;  
(4) Agreements and attached boundary maps of the people's governments of the two disputing parties resolving the boundary dispute;  
(5) Documents on boundary lines or stamped boundary maps verified as consistent by the people's governments of both parties before the occurrence of the boundary dispute.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　解放以后直至发生边界争议之前的下列文件和材料，作为处理边界争议的参考：  
(一)根据有关法律的规定，确定自然资源权属时核发的证书；  
(二)有关人民政府在争议地区行使行政管辖的文件和材料；  
(三)争议双方的上级人民政府及其所属部门，或者争议双方人民政府及其所属部门，开发争议地区自然资源的决定或者协议；  
(四)根据有关政策的规定，确定土地权属的材料。

**Article 8** The following documents and materials from after the liberation up to before the occurrence of the boundary dispute shall serve as reference for handling boundary disputes:  
(1) Certificates issued when determining the ownership of natural resources in accordance with relevant laws;  
(2) Documents and materials of the relevant people's governments exercising administrative jurisdiction in the disputed area;  
(3) Decisions or agreements of the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties and its subordinate departments, or the people's governments of the two disputing parties and their subordinate departments, on developing natural resources in the disputed area;  
(4) Materials on determining land ownership in accordance with relevant policies.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[本条例第七条](#art-7)、第八条规定以外的任何文件和材料，均不作为处理边界争议的依据和参考。

**Article 9** No documents or materials other than those specified in Articles 7 and 8 of these Regulations shall serve as the basis or reference for handling boundary disputes.

### 第三章　处理程序

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　边界争议发生后，争议双方人民政府必须采取有效措施防止事态扩大。任何一方都不得往争议地区迁移居民，不得在争议地区设置政权组织，不准破坏自然资源。  
严禁聚众闹事、械斗伤人，严禁抢夺和破坏国家、集体和个人的财产。发生群众纠纷时，争议双方人民政府必须立即派人到现场调查处理，并报告争议双方的上一级人民政府。

**Article 10** After a boundary dispute arises, the people's governments of the two disputing parties must take effective measures to prevent the situation from escalating. Neither party may move residents into the disputed area, establish political organizations in the disputed area, or destroy natural resources.  
Gathering crowds to create disturbances, armed fights causing injuries, and seizing or destroying State, collective or individual property are strictly prohibited. When a mass dispute occurs, the people's governments of the two disputing parties must immediately send personnel to the scene to investigate and handle the matter, and report to the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市之间的边界争议，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府协商解决；经协商未达成协议的，双方应当将各自的解决方案并附边界线地形图，报国务院处理。  
国务院受理的省、自治区、直辖市之间的边界争议，由民政部会同国务院有关部门调解；经调解未达成协议的，由民政部会同国务院有关部门提出解决方案，报国务院决定。

**Article 11** Boundary disputes between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be resolved through consultation between the relevant people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; if no agreement is reached through consultation, the two parties shall submit their respective solutions along with a topographic map of the boundary line to the State Council for handling.  
For boundary disputes between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government accepted by the State Council, the Ministry of Civil Affairs shall conduct mediation jointly with relevant departments of the State Council; if no agreement is reached through mediation, the Ministry of Civil Affairs shall, jointly with relevant departments of the State Council, propose a solution and submit it to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　省、自治区、直辖市境内的边界争议，由争议双方人民政府协商解决；经协商未达成协议的，双方应当将各自的解决方案并附边界线地形图，报双方的上一级人民政府处理。  
争议双方的上一级人民政府受理的边界争议，由其民政部门会同有关部门调解；经调解未达成协议的，由民政部门会同有关部门提出解决方案，报本级人民政府决定。

**Article 12** Boundary disputes within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be resolved through consultation between the people's governments of the two disputing parties; if no agreement is reached through consultation, the two parties shall submit their respective solutions along with a topographic map of the boundary line to the people's government at the next higher level of the two parties for handling.  
For boundary disputes accepted by the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties, the civil affairs department of that government shall conduct mediation jointly with relevant departments; if no agreement is reached through mediation, the civil affairs department shall, jointly with relevant departments, propose a solution and submit it to the people's government at the same level for decision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经双方人民政府协商解决的边界争议，由双方人民政府的代表在边界协议和所附边界线地形图上签字。

**Article 13** For a boundary dispute resolved through consultation between the people's governments of the two parties, the representatives of the people's governments of the two parties shall sign the boundary agreement and the attached topographic map of the boundary line.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　争议双方人民政府达成的边界协议，或者争议双方的上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，凡不涉及自然村隶属关系变更的，自边界协议签字或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定下达之日起生效。  
争议双方人民政府达成的边界协议，或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，凡涉及自然村隶属关系变更的，必须按照《国务院关于行政区管理的规定》中有关行政区域界线变更的审批权限和程序办理。

**Article 14** A boundary agreement reached by the people's governments of the two disputing parties, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties on resolving a boundary dispute, that does not involve a change in the subordinate relationship of natural villages shall take effect from the date of signing of the boundary agreement or the date of issuance of the decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving the boundary dispute.  
A boundary agreement reached by the people's governments of the two disputing parties, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving a boundary dispute, that involves a change in the subordinate relationship of natural villages must be handled in accordance with the provisions on the approval authority and procedures for changes of administrative area boundaries in the Regulations of the State Council on the Administration of Administrative Divisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　争议双方人民政府达成的边界协议，或者争议双方的上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定生效后，由争议双方人民政府联合实地勘测边界线，标绘大比例尺的边界线地形图。  
实地勘测的边界线地形图，经双方人民政府盖章后，代替边界协议或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定所附的边界线地形图。

**Article 15** After a boundary agreement reached by the people's governments of the two disputing parties, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties on resolving a boundary dispute, takes effect, the people's governments of the two disputing parties shall jointly conduct on-site survey and mapping of the boundary line, and draw a large-scale topographic map of the boundary line.  
The on-site surveyed and mapped topographic map of the boundary line, after being stamped by the people's governments of both parties, shall replace the topographic map of the boundary line attached to the boundary agreement or the decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving the boundary dispute.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方人民政府处理的边界争议，必须履行备案手续。争议双方人民政府达成的边界协议，由双方人民政府联合上报备案；争议双方的上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，由作出决定的人民政府上报备案。上报备案时，应当附实地勘测的边界线地形图。  
省、自治区、直辖市之间的边界协议，上报国务院备案。  
自治州、自治县的边界协议或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，逐级上报国务院备案。  
县、市、市辖区的边界协议或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，逐级上报民政部备案。  
乡、民族乡、镇的边界协议或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，逐级上报本省(自治区、直辖市)人民政府备案。

**Article 16** For a boundary dispute handled by a local people's government, the record-filing formalities must be fulfilled. A boundary agreement reached by the people's governments of the two disputing parties shall be jointly submitted by the two people's governments for record-filing; a decision of the people's government at the next higher level of the two disputing parties on resolving a boundary dispute shall be submitted for record-filing by the people's government that made the decision. When submitting for record-filing, the on-site surveyed and mapped topographic map of the boundary line shall be attached.  
A boundary agreement between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be submitted to the State Council for record-filing.  
A boundary agreement of an autonomous prefecture or autonomous county, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving a boundary dispute, shall be submitted level by level to the State Council for record-filing.  
A boundary agreement of a county, city, or municipal district, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving a boundary dispute, shall be submitted level by level to the Ministry of Civil Affairs for record-filing.  
A boundary agreement of a township, ethnic township, or town, or a decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving a boundary dispute, shall be submitted level by level to the people's government of the province (autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government) for record-filing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　边界争议解决后，争议双方人民政府必须认真执行边界协议或者上级人民政府解决边界争议的决定，向有关地区的群众公布正式划定的行政区域界线，教育当地干部和群众严格遵守。

**Article 17** After a boundary dispute is resolved, the people's governments of the two disputing parties must diligently implement the boundary agreement or the decision of the people's government at the next higher level on resolving the boundary dispute, announce the formally delimited administrative area boundary to the masses in the relevant areas, and educate the local cadres and masses to strictly observe it.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　争议双方人民政府的负责人，违反本条例的规定，玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受较大损失的，应当给予行政处分；造成重大损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where a responsible person of the people's government of either disputing party, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, neglects his or her duty, causing relatively large losses to public property, the State, or the interests of the people, an administrative sanction shall be imposed; if the losses are heavy and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反[本条例第十条](#art-10)的规定，情节较重的，对直接责任人员和其他肇事者，分别给予行政处分、治安管理处罚；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where the provisions of Article 10 of these Regulations are violated, and the circumstances are relatively serious, the directly responsible persons and other offenders shall be given administrative sanctions or public security penalties respectively; if the circumstances are serious and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行政区域边界划定后，违反本条例的规定越界侵权造成损害的，当事一方可以向有管辖权的人民法院起诉。

**Article 20** Where, after the administrative area boundary is delimited, a party violates the provisions of these Regulations by crossing the boundary and infringing upon rights, causing damage, the other party may file a prosecution with the People's Court having jurisdiction.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本条例由民政部负责解释。

**Article 21** The Ministry of Civil Affairs shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1981年5月30日国务院发布的《行政区域边界争议处理办法》同时废止。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The "Measures for Handling Disputes over Administrative Area Boundaries" promulgated by the State Council on May 30, 1981 shall be abolished simultaneously.
