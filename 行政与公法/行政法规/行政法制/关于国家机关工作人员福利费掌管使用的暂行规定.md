# 关于国家机关工作人员福利费掌管使用的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1957-05-22

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、工作人员的福利费标准，根据各级机关工作人员工资水平和城乡生活水平的不同情况，另作具体规定。

**Article 1** I. The standards for welfare funds for staff shall be otherwise specified in detail based on the different circumstances of the salary levels of staff in organs at all levels and the urban and rural living standards.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、中央机关工作人员的福利费，经过国务院人事局作必要的调剂以后，由各机关的人事工作部门掌管使用；地方机关工作人员的福利费，经过各省、自治区、直辖市人民委员会的人事局(处)在本省、区、市辖区内作必要的调剂以后，由县级以上各级的人事工作部门统一掌管使用。  
工作人员福利费年终结余，一律不再上缴，但不能挪作别用。

**Article 2** II. The welfare funds for staff of central organs shall, after necessary adjustments by the Personnel Bureau of the State Council, be managed and used by the personnel work departments of each organ; the welfare funds for staff of local organs shall, after necessary adjustments by the personnel bureaus (divisions) of the people's committees of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within their respective provincial, regional, or municipal jurisdictions, be uniformly managed and used by the personnel work departments at or above the county level.  
The year-end surplus of welfare funds for staff shall in no case be turned over to the higher authorities, but shall not be diverted to other uses.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、福利费的使用范围，包括：  
(一)解决工作人员的家属生活费困难；  
(二)解决工作人员的家属患病医药费困难；  
(三)解决工作人员的家属死亡埋葬费困难；  
(四)解决工作人员的其他特殊困难；  
(五)补助集体福利事业费用。

**Article 3** III. The scope of use of welfare funds shall include:  
(1) solving difficulties in the living expenses of the family members of staff;  
(2) solving difficulties in the medical expenses of the family members of staff due to illness;  
(3) solving difficulties in the burial expenses of the family members of staff due to death;  
(4) solving other special difficulties of staff; and  
(5) subsidizing the expenses of collective welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、工作人员的工资收入和其他经济收入，负担本人生活费(职务不同，生活费用应有所区别)和他(她)们家属生活费(一般根据家属居住地的人民生活水平确定)有困难的，或者因家属治病医药费、家属死亡埋葬费有困难的，以及有其他特殊困难的，都应该根据“困难大的多补助，困难小的少补助”的原则，酌予定期或者临时补助。  
福利费解决了工作人员各项困难，还有结余的时候，对集体福利所需费用，可以适当地给予补助。

**Article 4** IV. Where staff members have difficulties in meeting their own living expenses (which shall vary according to positions) and the living expenses of their family members (generally determined according to the people's living standards in the place where the family members reside) from their salary income and other economic income, or have difficulties due to medical expenses for family members' illness, burial expenses for family members' death, or have other special difficulties, they shall be granted periodic or temporary subsidies as appropriate in accordance with the principle of 'greater subsidies for greater difficulties and smaller subsidies for smaller difficulties.'  
When welfare funds still have a surplus after solving various difficulties of staff, they may appropriately subsidize the expenses needed for collective welfare.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、工作人员的各项困难补助，由本人申请，经过本机关福利组织评议以后，商请本机关人事工作部门确定。

**Article 5** V. Various difficulty subsidies for staff shall be applied for by the staff member himself/herself, and after evaluation by the welfare organization of the organ, shall be determined upon consultation with the personnel work department of the organ.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、各省、自治区、直辖市人民委员会可以根据本规定的精神，结合当地具体情况作具体的规定。

**Article 6** VI. The people's committees of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the spirit of these Provisions and in light of the specific local circumstances, make specific provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、本规定自一九五七年六月一日起实行。原中央人民政府政务院于一九五四年三月所发《关于各级人民政府工作人员福利费掌管使用办法的通知》和本院一九五四年十二月所发《关于各级人民政府工作人员福利费掌管使用问题的补充通知》都同时废止。

**Article 7** VII. These Provisions shall come into effect as of June 1, 1957. The Notice on the Measures for the Management and Use of Welfare Funds for Staff of People's Governments at All Levels issued by the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government in March 1954 and the Supplementary Notice on the Management and Use of Welfare Funds for Staff of People's Governments at All Levels issued by the State Council in December 1954 shall be abolished simultaneously.
