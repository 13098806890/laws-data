# 国务院关于口岸开放的若干规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1985-09-18  
**生效日期**：1985-09-18

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、本规定所指口岸是供人员、货物和交通工具出入国境的港口、机场、车站、通道等。口岸分为一类口岸和二类口岸。一类口岸是指由国务院批准开放的口岸(包括中央管理的口岸和由省、自治区、直辖市管理的部分口岸)；二类口岸是指由省级人民政府批准开放并管理的口岸。

**Article 1** I. The ports referred to in these Provisions are ports, airports, railway stations, and passages for the entry and exit of persons, goods, and means of transport. Ports are classified into Category I ports and Category II ports. Category I ports refer to ports approved for opening by the State Council (including ports under central management and some ports under the management of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government); Category II ports refer to ports approved for opening and managed by the provincial people's governments.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、口岸的开放和关闭，由国务院或省级人民政府审批后公布执行。

**Article 2** II. The opening and closing of ports shall be subject to approval by the State Council or the provincial people's government, and shall be announced for enforcement thereafter.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、凡开放口岸，应根据需要设立边防检查、海关、港务监督、卫生检疫、动植物检疫、商品检验等检查检验机构，以及国家规定的其他口岸机构。

**Article 3** III. For any open port, inspection and examination agencies such as border inspection, customs, port superintendency administration, health quarantine, animal and plant quarantine, and commodity inspection, as well as other port agencies as prescribed by the state, shall be established as needed.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、两类口岸的具体划分：  
(一) 以下为一类口岸：  
1．对外国籍船舶、飞机、车辆等交通工具开放的海、陆、空客货口岸；  
2．只允许我国籍船舶、飞机、车辆出入国境的海、陆、空客货口岸；  
3．允许外国籍船舶进出我国领海内的海面交货点。  
(二) 以下为二类口岸：  
1．依靠其他口岸派人前往办理出入境检查检验手续的国轮外贸运输装卸点、起运点、交货点；  
2．同毗邻国家地方政府之间进行边境小额贸易和人员往来的口岸；  
3．只限边境居民通行的出入境口岸。

**Article 4** IV. The specific classification of the two categories of ports:  
(I) The following are Category I ports:  
1. Seaport, land, and airport passenger and cargo ports open to means of transport such as foreign vessels, aircraft, and vehicles;  
2. Seaport, land, and airport passenger and cargo ports that only allow Chinese vessels, aircraft, and vehicles to enter or leave the country;  
3. Seaborne delivery points within China's territorial sea that allow foreign vessels to enter and leave.  
(II) The following are Category II ports:  
1. Loading and unloading points, departure points, and delivery points for foreign trade transport by Chinese vessels that rely on other ports to send personnel to handle entry-exit inspection and examination formalities;  
2. Ports for small-scale border trade and personnel exchanges between local governments of neighboring countries;  
3. Entry-exit ports restricted to border residents only.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、报批程序：  
(一) 一类口岸：由有关部(局)或港口、码头、车站、机场和通道所在地的省级人民政府会商大军区后，报请国务院批准，同时抄送国务院口岸领导小组、总参谋部和有关主管部门。  
(二) 二类口岸：由口岸所在地的人民政府征得当地大军区和海军的同意，并会商口岸检查检验等有关单位后，报请省级人民政府批准。批文同时送国务院口岸领导小组和有关主管部门备案。

**Article 5** V. Approval application procedures:  
(I) Category I ports: The relevant ministry (bureau) or the provincial people's government of the location of the port, dock, railway station, airport, or passage shall, after consultation with the major military command, submit a report to the State Council for approval, and simultaneously send a copy to the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council, the General Staff Headquarters, and the relevant competent departments.  
(II) Category II ports: The people's government of the location of the port, having obtained the consent of the local major military command and the navy, and after consultation with the port inspection and examination and other relevant units, shall submit a report to the provincial people's government for approval. The approval document shall simultaneously be sent to the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council and the relevant competent departments for record.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、报批开放口岸应附具下列资料：  
(一) 对口岸开放进行的可行性研究报告，以及口岸的基本条件、近三年客货运量、经济效益和发展前景的资料。  
(二) 根据客货运输任务提出的有关检查检验单位、口岸办公室、中国银行等机构设置和人员编制方案。  
(三) 检查检验场地和办公、生活设施等规划，以及投资预算和资金来源。

**Article 6** VI. The following materials shall be attached to the application for approval of port opening:  
(I) A feasibility study report on the port opening, and materials on the basic conditions of the port, passenger and cargo volumes in the recent three years, economic benefits, and development prospects.  
(II) Plans for the establishment and staffing of relevant inspection and examination units, port offices, Bank of China, and other institutions proposed in light of passenger and cargo transport tasks.  
(III) Plans for inspection and examination sites and office and living facilities, as well as investment budgets and sources of funds.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、对外开放前的验收：  
(一) 新开放的口岸，在开放前必须对其交通安全设施、通信设施、联检场地、检查检验等单位的机构设置和人员配备，以及办公、生活设施等进行验收。验收合格后，才能宣布开放。  
(二) 一类口岸，由国务院口岸领导小组办公室负责组织验收；二类口岸，由所在省、自治区、直辖市口岸办公室或其他主管口岸工作的部门负责组织验收。

**Article 7** VII. Inspection and acceptance before opening to foreign countries:  
(I) For a newly opened port, its traffic safety facilities, communication facilities, joint inspection sites, the establishment and staffing of inspection and examination units, and office and living facilities must be inspected and accepted before opening. Only after passing the acceptance inspection may the opening be announced.  
(II) For Category I ports, the Office of the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the inspection and acceptance; for Category II ports, the port office of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or other department in charge of port affairs shall be responsible for organizing the inspection and acceptance.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、临时进出我国非开放区域的审批权限：  
(一) 临时从我国非开放的港口或沿海水域进出的中、外国籍船舶，由交通部审批，并报国务院口岸领导小组备案。报批前应征得军事主管部门和当地人民政府以及有关检查检验单位的同意，并安排好检查检验工作。  
(二) 临时从我国非开放机场起降的中、外国籍民用飞机，由中国民用航空局征得军事主管部门同意后审批，非民用飞机由军事主管部门审批，并报国务院口岸领导小组备案。报批前应征得当地人民政府和有关检查检验部门的同意，并安排好检查检验工作。  
(三) 临时从我国非开放的陆地边界区域进出境的中、外国籍车辆和人员，由省级人民政府审批。报批前应征得当地省军区和公安部门的同意，并安排好检查检验工作。

**Article 8** VIII. Examination and approval authority for temporary entry into or exit from non-open areas of China:  
(I) Chinese and foreign vessels temporarily entering or leaving non-open ports or coastal waters of China shall be subject to examination and approval by the Ministry of Communications, and shall be reported to the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council for record. Before submitting for approval, the consent of the competent military department, the local people's government, and the relevant inspection and examination units shall be obtained, and the inspection and examination work shall be properly arranged.  
(II) Chinese and foreign civil aircraft temporarily taking off or landing at non-open airports of China shall be subject to examination and approval by the Civil Aviation Administration of China after obtaining the consent of the competent military department; non-civil aircraft shall be subject to examination and approval by the competent military department, and shall be reported to the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council for record. Before submitting for approval, the consent of the local people's government and the relevant inspection and examination departments shall be obtained, and the inspection and examination work shall be properly arranged.  
(III) Chinese and foreign vehicles and persons temporarily entering or leaving China through non-open land border areas shall be subject to examination and approval by the provincial people's government. Before submitting for approval, the consent of the local provincial military command and the public security department shall be obtained, and the inspection and examination work shall be properly arranged.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、口岸开放应有计划地进行，按隶属关系分别列入国家或地方口岸开放计划。国务院有关部门和省、自治区、直辖市应将口岸开放计划(草案)，于计划年度前两个月报国务院口岸领导小组，并抄报国家计委、劳动人事部和检查检验单位的有关主管部门。

**Article 9** IX. The opening of ports shall be carried out in a planned manner and shall be included in the national or local port opening plans according to their affiliation. The relevant departments of the State Council and the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall submit the port opening plan (draft) to the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council two months before the planning year, and simultaneously report a copy to the State Planning Commission, the Ministry of Labor and Personnel, and the relevant competent departments of the inspection and examination units.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、开放口岸检查检验设施建设资金来源：  
(一) 中央管理的口岸，由中央负责解决；地方管理的口岸，由地方负责解决。  
(二) 国家新建开放的港口、码头、车站和机场(含军用改为军民合用的机场)等口岸建设项目(包括利用外资和中外合资项目)，以及老口岸新建作业区和经济开发区的新港区等项目，所需联检场地应与港口、码头、车站、机场等主体工程统一规划。所需投资包括在主体工程之内。检查检验单位办公、生活土建设施(包括宿舍)的投资，由口岸建设项目的主管部门组织有关单位研究，统一汇总报国家计委审批。批准后，投资划拨给口岸所在地的省、自治区、直辖市，由地方统一规划，统一设计施工。军用改建为军民合用机场的口岸项目，应事先征得空军或海军同意，如在机场内建设，建设单位可提出要求，由空军或海军统一规划。  
(三) 各部(局)直属的原有港口、码头、车站和机场需要对外开放时，所需联检场地，原则上要利用原有建筑设施。如确需扩建、新建，应由港口、码头、车站和机场的主管部门投资建设。检查检验单位的办公、生活土建设施(包括宿舍)的投资，原则上由各自主管部门解决。对确有困难的，国家或地方给予适当补助，由地方统一建设，投资交地方包干使用。  
(四) 地方新开口岸，所需联检场地和检查检验单位的办公、生活土建设施(包括宿舍)，由地方统一投资，统一建设。  
(五) 国际海员俱乐部的建设规划和投资来源，比照(二)、(三)、(四)项规定解决。  
(六) 检查检验单位所需的交通工具、仪器设备等，由各自主管部门解决。  
(七) 联检场地内，划给检查检验单位的办公和业务用房(包括水、电、市内电话)，应由港口、码头、车站和机场(包括军民合用的机场)的经营单位免费提供。

**Article 10** X. Sources of funds for the construction of inspection and examination facilities at open ports:  
(I) For ports under central management, the central government shall be responsible for funding; for ports under local management, the local government shall be responsible for funding.  
(II) For port construction projects (including projects utilizing foreign investment and Sino-foreign joint ventures) such as newly opened ports, docks, railway stations, and airports (including military airports converted to joint military-civilian use), as well as new operation areas of old ports and new port areas of economic development zones, the required joint inspection sites shall be planned uniformly with the main projects such as ports, docks, railway stations, and airports. The required investment shall be included in the main projects. The investment in office and living civil engineering facilities (including dormitories) for inspection and examination units shall be studied by the competent department of the port construction project in conjunction with relevant units, compiled and submitted to the State Planning Commission for approval. After approval, the investment shall be allocated to the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the port is located, and the local government shall carry out unified planning, design, and construction. For port projects involving military airports converted to joint military-civilian use, the consent of the Air Force or Navy shall be obtained in advance; if construction is to be carried out within the airport, the construction entity may put forward requirements, and the Air Force or Navy shall carry out unified planning.  
(III) When existing ports, docks, railway stations, and airports directly under various ministries (bureaus) need to be opened to foreign countries, the required joint inspection sites shall, in principle, utilize existing buildings and facilities. If expansion or new construction is truly necessary, the competent department of the port, dock, railway station, or airport shall invest in the construction. The investment in office and living civil engineering facilities (including dormitories) for inspection and examination units shall, in principle, be resolved by their respective competent departments. Where genuine difficulties exist, the state or local government shall provide appropriate subsidies, and the local government shall carry out unified construction, with the investment being used by the local government under a lump-sum contract.  
(IV) For newly opened local ports, the required joint inspection sites and office and living civil engineering facilities (including dormitories) for inspection and examination units shall be invested in and built uniformly by the local government.  
(V) The construction plan and sources of investment for international seamen's clubs shall be resolved by reference to the provisions in items (II), (III), and (IV).  
(VI) The means of transport, instruments, and equipment required by inspection and examination units shall be provided by their respective competent departments.  
(VII) Within the joint inspection sites, the office and operational premises (including water, electricity, and local telephone) allocated to inspection and examination units shall be provided free of charge by the operating entities of the ports, docks, railway stations, and airports (including airports for joint military-civilian use).

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、本规定由国务院口岸领导小组办公室负责解释。

**Article 11** XI. These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Office of the Leading Group for Port Affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 12** XII. These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
