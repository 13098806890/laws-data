# 国务院行政机构设置和编制管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1997-08-03  
**生效日期**：1997-08-03

> (1997年8月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第227号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范国务院行政机构的设置，加强编制管理，提高行政效率，根据宪法和国务院组织法，制定本条例。 第二条　国务院行政机构设置和编制管理应当适应国家政治、经济、社会发展的需要，遵循精简、统一、高效的原则。 第三条　国务院根据宪法和国务院组织法的规定，行使国务院行政机构设置和编制管理职权。 国务院机构编制管理机关在国务院领导下，负责国务院行政机构设置和编制管理的具体工作。 第二章　机构设置管理 第四条　国务院行政机构的设置以职能的科学配置为基础，做到职能明确、分工合理、机构精简，有利于提高行政效能。 国务院根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，适应社会主义市场经济体制的要求，适时调整国务院行政机构；但是，在一届政府任期内，国务院组成部门应当保持相对稳定。 第五条　国务院行政机构的设立、撤销或者合并，由国务院机构编制管理机关事先组织有关部门和专家进行评估和论证。 第六条　国务院行政机构根据职能分为国务院办公厅、国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构、国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构和国务院议事协调机构。 国务院办公厅协助国务院领导处理国务院日常工作。 国务院组成部门依法分别履行国务院基本的行政管理职能。国务院组成部门包括各部、各委员会、中国人民银行和审计署。 国务院直属机构主管国务院的某项专门业务，具有独立的行政管理职能。 国务院办事机构协助国务院总理办理专门事项，不具有独立的行政管理职能。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范国务院行政机构的设置，加强编制管理，提高行政效率，根据宪法和国务院组织法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution and the Organic Law of the State Council for the purpose of regulating the establishment of the administrative organs of the State Council, strengthening staffing management, and improving administrative efficiency.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院行政机构设置和编制管理应当适应国家政治、经济、社会发展的需要，遵循精简、统一、高效的原则。

**Article 2** The establishment of administrative organs of the State Council and the management of their staffing shall meet the needs of the country's political, economic and social development and follow the principles of simplification, unification and efficiency.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院根据宪法和国务院组织法的规定，行使国务院行政机构设置和编制管理职权。  
国务院机构编制管理机关在国务院领导下，负责国务院行政机构设置和编制管理的具体工作。

**Article 3** The State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution and the Organic Law of the State Council, exercise the powers of establishing administrative organs and managing their staffing.  
Under the leadership of the State Council, the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management shall be responsible for the specific work concerning the establishment of administrative organs of the State Council and the management of their staffing.

### 第二章　机构设置管理

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院行政机构的设置以职能的科学配置为基础，做到职能明确、分工合理、机构精简，有利于提高行政效能。  
国务院根据国民经济和社会发展的需要，适应社会主义市场经济体制的要求，适时调整国务院行政机构；但是，在一届政府任期内，国务院组成部门应当保持相对稳定。

**Article 4** The establishment of administrative organs of the State Council shall be based on the scientific allocation of functions, so as to achieve clear functions, reasonable division of labor, and streamlined organs, thus facilitating the improvement of administrative effectiveness.  
The State Council shall, in accordance with the needs of national economic and social development and the requirements of the socialist market economy system, make timely adjustments to its administrative organs; however, within the term of one government, the constituent departments of the State Council shall remain relatively stable.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院行政机构的设立、撤销或者合并，由国务院机构编制管理机关事先组织有关部门和专家进行评估和论证。

**Article 5** The establishment, dissolution or merger of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be subject to evaluation and demonstration organized in advance by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management in conjunction with the relevant departments and experts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院行政机构根据职能分为国务院办公厅、国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构、国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构和国务院议事协调机构。  
国务院办公厅协助国务院领导处理国务院日常工作。  
国务院组成部门依法分别履行国务院基本的行政管理职能。国务院组成部门包括各部、各委员会、中国人民银行和审计署。  
国务院直属机构主管国务院的某项专门业务，具有独立的行政管理职能。  
国务院办事机构协助国务院总理办理专门事项，不具有独立的行政管理职能。  
国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构由国务院组成部门管理，主管特定业务，行使行政管理职能。  
国务院议事协调机构承担跨国务院行政机构的重要业务工作的组织协调任务。国务院议事协调机构议定的事项，经国务院同意，由有关的行政机构按照各自的职责负责办理。在特殊或者紧急的情况下，经国务院同意，国务院议事协调机构可以规定临时性的行政管理措施。

**Article 6** The administrative organs of the State Council shall, according to their functions, be divided into the General Office of the State Council, the constituent departments of the State Council, the organs directly under the State Council, the offices of the State Council, the state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council, and the deliberation and coordination organs of the State Council.  
The General Office of the State Council shall assist the leaders of the State Council in handling the routine work of the State Council.  
The constituent departments of the State Council shall respectively perform the basic administrative management functions of the State Council according to law. The constituent departments of the State Council include the various ministries and commissions, the People's Bank of China and the National Audit Office.  
The organs directly under the State Council shall be in charge of specific specialized businesses of the State Council and have independent administrative management functions.  
The offices of the State Council shall assist the Premier of the State Council in handling specialized matters and do not have independent administrative management functions.  
The state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council shall be managed by the constituent departments of the State Council, be in charge of specific businesses, and exercise administrative management functions.  
The deliberation and coordination organs of the State Council shall undertake the tasks of organizing and coordinating important cross-administrative-organ work of the State Council. Matters decided by the deliberation and coordination organs of the State Council shall, with the consent of the State Council, be handled by the relevant administrative organs according to their respective functions. In special or emergency circumstances, with the consent of the State Council, the deliberation and coordination organs of the State Council may formulate temporary administrative management measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　依照国务院组织法的规定，国务院设立办公厅。  
国务院组成部门的设立、撤销或者合并由国务院机构编制管理机关提出方案，经国务院常务会议讨论通过后，由国务院总理提请全国人民代表大会决定；在全国人民代表大会闭会期间，提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。

**Article 7** In accordance with the provisions of the Organic Law of the State Council, the State Council shall establish the General Office.  
The establishment, dissolution or merger of a constituent department of the State Council shall be proposed by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management, submitted to the State Council executive meeting for discussion and approval, and then submitted by the Premier of the State Council to the National People's Congress for decision; during the recess of the National People's Congress, it shall be submitted to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for decision.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构和国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构的设立、撤销或者合并由国务院机构编制管理机关提出方案，报国务院决定。

**Article 8** The establishment, dissolution or merger of the organs directly under the State Council, the offices of the State Council and the state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council shall be proposed by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management and submitted to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设立国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构和国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构的方案，应当包括下列事项：  
(一)设立机构的必要性和可行性；  
(二)机构的类型、名称和职能；  
(三)司级内设机构的名称和职能；  
(四)与业务相近的国务院行政机构职能的划分；  
(五)机构的编制。  
撤销或者合并前款所列机构的方案，应当包括下列事项：  
(一)撤销或者合并的理由；  
(二)撤销或者合并机构后职能的消失、转移情况；  
(三)撤销或者合并机构后编制的调整和人员的分流。

**Article 9** A plan for the establishment of a constituent department of the State Council, an organ directly under the State Council, an office of the State Council, or a state administrative organ administered by a constituent department of the State Council shall include the following matters:  
(1) the necessity and feasibility of establishing the organ;  
(2) the type, name and functions of the organ;  
(3) the names and functions of its internal bodies at the department level;  
(4) the division of functions with administrative organs of the State Council that have similar businesses;  
(5) the staffing of the organ.  
A plan for the dissolution or merger of any of the organs mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall include the following matters:  
(1) the reasons for dissolution or merger;  
(2) the disappearance or transfer of functions after dissolution or merger;  
(3) the adjustment of staffing and the redeployment of personnel after dissolution or merger.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设立国务院议事协调机构，应当严格控制；可以交由现有机构承担职能的或者由现有机构进行协调可以解决问题的，不另设立议事协调机构。  
设立国务院议事协调机构，应当明确规定承担办事职能的具体工作部门；为处理一定时期内某项特定工作设立的议事协调机构，还应当明确规定其撤销的条件或者撤销的期限。

**Article 10** The establishment of a deliberation and coordination organ of the State Council shall be strictly controlled; if the functions can be undertaken by an existing organ or if the problems can be solved through coordination by an existing organ, no separate deliberation and coordination organ shall be established.  
For the establishment of a deliberation and coordination organ of the State Council, the specific working department that undertakes the office functions shall be clearly defined; for a deliberation and coordination organ established to handle a specific task within a certain period, the conditions for its dissolution or the time limit for its dissolution shall also be clearly defined.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院议事协调机构的设立、撤销或者合并，由国务院机构编制管理机关提出方案，报国务院决定。

**Article 11** The establishment, dissolution or merger of a deliberation and coordination organ of the State Council shall be proposed by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management and submitted to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院行政机构设立后，需要对职能进行调整的，由国务院机构编制管理机关提出方案，报国务院决定。

**Article 12** After an administrative organ of the State Council is established, if its functions need to be adjusted, the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management shall propose a plan and submit it to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院办公厅、国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构在职能分解的基础上设立司、处两级内设机构；国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构根据工作需要可以设立司、处两级内设机构，也可以只设立处级内设机构。

**Article 13** The General Office of the State Council, the constituent departments of the State Council, the organs directly under the State Council, and the offices of the State Council shall, on the basis of functional decomposition, establish internal bodies at the department level and the division level; the state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council may, according to work needs, establish internal bodies at both the department and division levels, or may establish only internal bodies at the division level.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院行政机构的司级内设机构的增设、撤销或者合并，经国务院机构编制管理机关审核方案，报国务院批准。  
国务院行政机构的处级内设机构的设立、撤销或者合并，由国务院行政机构根据国家有关规定决定，按年度报国务院机构编制管理机关备案。

**Article 14** The addition, dissolution or merger of an internal body at the department level of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be subject to review of the plan by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The establishment, dissolution or merger of an internal body at the division level of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be decided by the administrative organ of the State Council in accordance with the relevant state provisions, and shall be reported to the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management for record on an annual basis.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　增设国务院行政机构的司级内设机构的方案，应当包括下列事项：  
(一)增设机构的必要性；  
(二)增设机构的名称和职能；  
(三)与业务相近的司级内设机构职能的划分。  
撤销或者合并前款所列机构的方案，应当包括下列事项：  
(一)撤销或者合并机构的理由；  
(二)撤销或者合并机构后职能的消失、转移情况；  
(三)撤销或者合并机构后编制的调整。

**Article 15** A plan for the addition of an internal body at the department level of an administrative organ of the State Council shall include the following matters:  
(1) the necessity of adding the body;  
(2) the name and functions of the added body;  
(3) the division of functions with internal bodies at the department level that have similar businesses.  
A plan for the dissolution or merger of any of the bodies mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall include the following matters:  
(1) the reasons for dissolution or merger;  
(2) the disappearance or transfer of functions after dissolution or merger;  
(3) the adjustment of staffing after dissolution or merger.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院行政机构及其司级内设机构的名称应当规范、明确，并与该机构的类型和职能相称。  
国务院行政机构及其司级内设机构不得擅自变更名称。

**Article 16** The names of the administrative organs of the State Council and their internal bodies at the department level shall be standardized and clear, and shall be commensurate with the type and functions of such organs or bodies.  
The administrative organs of the State Council and their internal bodies at the department level may not change their names without authorization.

### 第三章　编制管理

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院行政机构的编制依据职能配置和职位分类，按照精简的原则确定。  
前款所称编制，包括人员的数量定额和领导职数。

**Article 17** The staffing of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be determined on the basis of function allocation and position classification, in accordance with the principle of simplification.  
The staffing referred to in the preceding paragraph includes the fixed number of personnel and the number of leading positions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院行政机构的编制在国务院行政机构设立时确定。  
国务院行政机构的编制方案，应当包括下列事项：  
(一)机构人员定额和人员结构比例；  
(二)机构领导职数和司级内设机构领导职数。

**Article 18** The staffing of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be determined at the time of its establishment.  
A plan for the staffing of an administrative organ of the State Council shall include the following matters:  
(1) the fixed number of personnel of the organ and the proportion of personnel structure;  
(2) the number of leading positions of the organ and the number of leading positions of internal bodies at the department level.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院行政机构增加或者减少编制，由国务院机构编制管理机关审核方案，报国务院批准。

**Article 19** An increase or decrease in the staffing of an administrative organ of the State Council shall be subject to review of the plan by the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院议事协调机构不单独确定编制，所需要的编制由承担具体工作的国务院行政机构解决。

**Article 20** Deliberation and coordination organs of the State Council shall not have their staffing determined separately; the staffing needed shall be provided by the administrative organ of the State Council that undertakes the specific work.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院办公厅、国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构和国务院办事机构的领导职数，按照国务院组织法的规定确定。国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构的领导职数，参照国务院组织法关于国务院直属机构领导职数的规定确定。  
国务院办公厅、国务院组成部门、国务院直属机构、国务院办事机构的司级内设机构的领导职数为一正二副；国务院组成部门管理的国家行政机构的司级内设机构的领导职数根据工作需要为一正二副或者一正一副。  
国务院行政机构的处级内设机构的领导职数，按照国家有关规定确定。

**Article 21** The number of leading positions in the General Office of the State Council, the constituent departments of the State Council, the organs directly under the State Council, and the offices of the State Council shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the Organic Law of the State Council. The number of leading positions in the state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council shall be determined by reference to the provisions of the Organic Law of the State Council on the number of leading positions in the organs directly under the State Council.  
The number of leading positions in the internal bodies at the department level of the General Office of the State Council, the constituent departments of the State Council, the organs directly under the State Council, and the offices of the State Council shall be one principal and two deputies; the number of leading positions in the internal bodies at the department level of the state administrative organs administered by the constituent departments of the State Council shall, according to work needs, be one principal and two deputies or one principal and one deputy.  
The number of leading positions in the internal bodies at the division level of the administrative organs of the State Council shall be determined in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院机构编制管理机关应当对国务院行政机构的机构设置和编制执行情况进行监督检查。  
国务院行政机构应当每年向国务院机构编制管理机关提供其机构设置和编制管理情况的报告。

**Article 22** The State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management shall supervise and inspect the establishment of administrative organs of the State Council and the implementation of staffing.  
The administrative organs of the State Council shall provide each year a report on the status of their establishment and staffing management to the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院行政机构违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院机构编制管理机关责令限期纠正；逾期不纠正的，由国务院机构编制管理机关建议国务院或者国务院有关部门对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
(一)擅自设立司级内设机构的；  
(二)擅自扩大职能的；  
(三)擅自变更机构名称的；  
(四)擅自超过核定的编制使用工作人员的；  
(五)有违反机构设置和编制管理法律、行政法规的其他行为的。

**Article 23** Where an administrative organ of the State Council violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management shall order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the State Council's organ for administrative organization and staffing management shall propose to the State Council or the relevant department of the State Council that administrative sanctions be imposed according to law on the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible:  
(1) establishing an internal body at the department level without authorization;  
(2) expanding functions without authorization;  
(3) changing the name of an organ without authorization;  
(4) employing personnel in excess of the approved staffing without authorization;  
(5) having other activities that violate the laws and administrative regulations on organ establishment and staffing management.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方各级人民政府行政机构的设置和编制管理办法另行制定。  
国务院行政机构不得干预地方各级人民政府的行政机构设置和编制管理工作，不得要求地方各级人民政府设立与其业务对口的行政机构。

**Article 24** Measures for the management of the establishment and staffing of administrative organs of local people's governments at various levels shall be formulated separately.  
Administrative organs of the State Council may not interfere in the management of the establishment and staffing of administrative organs of local people's governments at various levels, and may not require local people's governments at various levels to establish administrative organs corresponding to their businesses.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 25** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
