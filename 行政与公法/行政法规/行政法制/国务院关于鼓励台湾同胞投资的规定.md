# 国务院关于鼓励台湾同胞投资的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1988-07-02  
**生效日期**：1988-07-03

> (1988年6月25日国务院第10次常务会议通过　1988年7月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第7号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为促进大陆和台湾地区的经济技术交流，以利于祖国海峡两岸的共同繁荣，鼓励台湾的公司、企业和个人(以下统称台湾投资者)在大陆投资，制定本规定。 第二条　台湾投资者可以在大陆各省、自治区、直辖市、经济特区投资。 鼓励台湾投资者到海南省以及福建、广东、浙江等省沿海地带划定的岛屿和地区从事土地开发经营。 第三条　台湾投资者在大陆可以下列形式进行投资： (一)举办台湾投资者拥有全部资本的企业； (二)举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业； (三)开展补偿贸易、来料加工装配、合作生产； (四)购买企业的股票和债券； (五)购置房产； (六)依法取得土地使用权，开发经营； (七)法律、法规允许的其他投资形式。 第四条　台湾投资者可以在大陆的工业、农业、服务业以及其他符合社会和经济发展方向的行业投资。台湾投资者可以从各地方人民政府有关部门公布的项目中选择投资项目，也可以自行提出投资项目意向，向拟投资地区对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关申请。 国家鼓励台湾投资者投资举办产品出口企业和先进技术企业，并给予相应的优惠待遇。 第五条　台湾投资者在大陆投资举办拥有全部资本的企业、合资经营企业和合作经营企业(以下统称台胞投资企业)，除适用本规定外，参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定，享受相应的外商投资企业待遇。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为促进大陆和台湾地区的经济技术交流，以利于祖国海峡两岸的共同繁荣，鼓励台湾的公司、企业和个人(以下统称台湾投资者)在大陆投资，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to promote economic and technological exchanges between the mainland and the Taiwan region, to facilitate the common prosperity of the two sides of the Taiwan Strait, and to encourage companies, enterprises, and individuals of Taiwan (hereinafter collectively referred to as Taiwan investors) to invest in the mainland.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　台湾投资者可以在大陆各省、自治区、直辖市、经济特区投资。  
鼓励台湾投资者到海南省以及福建、广东、浙江等省沿海地带划定的岛屿和地区从事土地开发经营。

**Article 2** Taiwan investors may invest in all provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and special economic zones of the mainland.  
Taiwan investors are encouraged to engage in land development and operation in designated islands and areas in the coastal zones of Hainan Province and Fujian, Guangdong, Zhejiang, and other provinces.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　台湾投资者在大陆可以下列形式进行投资：  
(一)举办台湾投资者拥有全部资本的企业；  
(二)举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业；  
(三)开展补偿贸易、来料加工装配、合作生产；  
(四)购买企业的股票和债券；  
(五)购置房产；  
(六)依法取得土地使用权，开发经营；  
(七)法律、法规允许的其他投资形式。

**Article 3** Taiwan investors may make investments in the mainland in the following forms:  
(1) Establishing enterprises with all capital owned by Taiwan investors;  
(2) Establishing equity joint ventures and contractual joint ventures;  
(3) Conducting compensation trade, processing and assembling with supplied materials, and cooperative production;  
(4) Purchasing stocks and bonds of enterprises;  
(5) Purchasing real estate;  
(6) Obtaining the right to the use of land according to law for development and operation;  
(7) Other forms of investment permitted by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　台湾投资者可以在大陆的工业、农业、服务业以及其他符合社会和经济发展方向的行业投资。台湾投资者可以从各地方人民政府有关部门公布的项目中选择投资项目，也可以自行提出投资项目意向，向拟投资地区对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关申请。  
国家鼓励台湾投资者投资举办产品出口企业和先进技术企业，并给予相应的优惠待遇。

**Article 4** Taiwan investors may invest in industries such as industry, agriculture, service industry, and other sectors that are in line with the direction of social and economic development. Taiwan investors may select investment projects from those published by the relevant departments of the local people's governments, or may propose investment project intentions on their own, and apply to the department of foreign economic relations and trade of the intended investment area or the examination and approval authority designated by the local people's government.  
The State encourages Taiwan investors to invest in establishing export-oriented enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises, and shall grant them corresponding preferential treatment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　台湾投资者在大陆投资举办拥有全部资本的企业、合资经营企业和合作经营企业(以下统称台胞投资企业)，除适用本规定外，参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定，享受相应的外商投资企业待遇。  
台湾投资者在大陆进行其他形式的投资，以及在大陆没有设立营业机构而有来源于大陆的股息、利息、租金、特许权使用费和其他所得，除适用本规定外，也可以参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定。

**Article 5** For enterprises with all capital owned by Taiwan investors, equity joint ventures, and contractual joint ventures (hereinafter collectively referred to as Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises) invested and established by Taiwan investors in the mainland, in addition to the application of these Provisions, the provisions of the relevant national laws and regulations concerning foreign-related economic affairs shall be applied mutatis mutandis, and they shall enjoy the corresponding treatment for foreign-invested enterprises.  
For other forms of investment made by Taiwan investors in the mainland, and for dividends, interest, rent, royalties, and other income derived from the mainland without having established a business establishment in the mainland, in addition to the application of these Provisions, the provisions of the relevant national laws and regulations concerning foreign-related economic affairs may also be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　台湾投资者可以用可自由兑换货币、机器设备或者其他实物、工业产权、专有技术等作为投资。

**Article 6** Taiwan investors may use convertible currencies, machinery and equipment or other tangible assets, industrial property rights, know-how, etc. as investment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　台湾投资者在大陆的投资、购置的资产、工业产权、投资所得利润和其他合法权益受国家法律保护，并可以依法转让和继承。  
台湾投资者在大陆的活动应当遵守国家的法律、法规。

**Article 7** The investments, purchased assets, industrial property rights, profits derived from investment, and other lawful rights and interests of Taiwan investors in the mainland shall be protected by the laws of the State, and may be transferred and succeeded according to law.  
The activities of Taiwan investors in the mainland shall comply with the laws and regulations of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家对台湾投资者的投资和其他资产不实行国有化。

**Article 8** The State shall not nationalize the investments and other assets of Taiwan investors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家根据社会公共利益的需要，对台胞投资企业实行征收时，依照法律程序进行并给予相应的补偿。

**Article 9** Where the State, in the need of social public interest, expropriates a Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise, it shall do so in accordance with legal procedures and provide corresponding compensation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　台湾投资者投资获得的合法利润，其他合法收入和清算后的资金，可以依法汇往境外。

**Article 10** The lawful profits obtained from investment by Taiwan investors, other lawful income, and funds after liquidation may be remitted abroad according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　台胞投资企业在其投资总额内进口本企业所需的机器设备、生产用车辆和办公设备，以及台胞个人在企业工作期间运进自用的、合理数量的生活用品和交通工具，免缴进口关税、工商统一税，免领进口许可证。  
台胞投资企业进口用于生产出口产品的原材料、燃料、散件、零部件、元器件、配套件，免缴进口关税、工商统一税，免领进口许可证，由海关实行监管。上述进口料件，如用于在大陆销售的产品，应当按照国家规定补办进口手续，并照章补税。  
台胞投资企业生产的出口产品，除国家限制出口的外，免缴出口关税和工商统一税。

**Article 11** For a Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise, machinery and equipment, vehicles for production, and office equipment required for the enterprise and imported within its total investment amount, as well as a reasonable quantity of daily necessities and means of transport imported for personal use by Taiwan compatriots during their employment in the enterprise, shall be exempted from import customs duties and consolidated industrial and commercial tax, and no import license shall be required.  
For raw materials, fuel, semi-finished products, parts, components, and auxiliary materials imported by a Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise for use in producing export products, they shall be exempted from import customs duties and consolidated industrial and commercial tax, and no import license shall be required, and they shall be under the supervision of the customs. If the aforesaid imported materials and parts are used in products sold in the mainland, the enterprise shall go through the formalities for import retrospectively and pay the tax according to the regulations.  
Export products manufactured by a Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise, except those for which export is restricted by the State, shall be exempted from export customs duties and consolidated industrial and commercial tax.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　台胞投资企业可以向大陆的金融机构借款，也可以向境外的金融机构借款，并可以本企业资产和权益抵押、担保。

**Article 12** A Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise may borrow loans from financial institutions in the mainland, and may also borrow loans from financial institutions outside the mainland, and may mortgage and guarantee its assets and rights and interests.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　台湾投资者拥有全部资本的企业，经营期限由投资者自行确定；合资经营企业和合作经营企业，经营期限由合资或者合作各方协商确定，也可以不规定经营期限。

**Article 13** The term of operation of an enterprise with all capital owned by Taiwan investors shall be determined by the investor itself; the term of operation of an equity joint venture or a contractual joint venture shall be determined through consultation by the parties to the joint venture or cooperation, or may be not stipulated.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　合资经营企业董事会的组成和董事长的委派、合作经营企业董事会或者联合管理机构的组成和董事长或者联合管理机构主任的委派，可以参照出资比例或者合作条件由合资或者合作各方协商决定。

**Article 14** The composition of the board of directors and the appointment of the chairman of an equity joint venture, and the composition of the board of directors or the joint management body and the appointment of the chairman or the director of the joint management body of a contractual joint venture, may be decided through consultation by the parties to the joint venture or cooperation with reference to the proportion of capital contributions or the conditions of cooperation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　台胞投资企业依照经批准的合同、章程进行经营管理活动。企业的经营管理自主权不受干涉。

**Article 15** A Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise shall carry out its operation and management activities in accordance with the approved contract and articles of association. The autonomy of the enterprise in operation and management shall not be interfered with.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在大陆投资的台胞个人以及台胞投资企业从境外聘请的技术和管理人员，可以申请办理多次入出境的证件。

**Article 16** Individual Taiwan compatriots investing in the mainland and the technical and managerial personnel engaged from outside the mainland by a Taiwan compatriot investment enterprise may apply for multiple-entry and exit documents.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　台湾投资者在大陆投资可以委托大陆的亲友为其代理人。代理人应当持有具有法律效力的委托书。

**Article 17** A Taiwan investor investing in the mainland may entrust his or her relatives or friends in the mainland as agents. The agent shall hold a legally effective power of attorney.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在台胞投资企业集中的地区，台湾投资者可以向当地人民政府申请成立台商协会。

**Article 18** In areas where Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises are concentrated, Taiwan investors may apply to the local people's government for the establishment of a Taiwan business association.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　台湾投资者在大陆投资举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业，由大陆的合资、合作方负责申请；举办台湾投资者拥有全部资本的企业，由台湾投资者直接申请或者委托在大陆的亲友、咨询服务机构等代为申请。台湾投资者投资举办企业的申请，由当地对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关统一受理。  
台胞投资企业的审批，按照国务院规定的权限办理。各级对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关应当在收到全部申请文件之日起45天内决定批准或者不批准。  
申请人应当在收到批准证书之日起30天内，按照有关登记管理办法，向工商行政管理机关申请登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 19** Where a Taiwan investor invests in establishing an equity joint venture or a contractual joint venture in the mainland, the mainland party to the joint venture or cooperation shall be responsible for the application. Where a Taiwan investor invests in establishing an enterprise with all capital owned by Taiwan investors, the Taiwan investor shall directly apply or entrust his or her relatives or friends in the mainland, consulting service institutions, etc. to apply on its behalf. The application for an enterprise invested by a Taiwan investor shall be accepted uniformly by the local department of foreign economic relations and trade or the examination and approval authority designated by the local people's government.  
The examination and approval of Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall be handled within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council. The departments of foreign economic relations and trade at all levels or the examination and approval authorities designated by the local people's governments shall, within 45 days from the date of receiving all application documents, decide on approval or disapproval.  
The applicant shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the certificate of approval, apply to the administrative authorities for industry and commerce for registration and obtain a business license in accordance with the relevant registration management measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　台湾投资者在大陆投资因履行合同发生的或者与合同有关的争议，当事人应当尽可能通过协商或者调解解决。  
当事人不愿协商、调解的，或者协商、调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，提交大陆或者香港的仲裁机构仲裁。  
当事人没有在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 20** Disputes arising from the performance of a contract or relating to the contract in the investment made by a Taiwan investor in the mainland shall be resolved by the parties through consultation or mediation as far as possible.  
If the parties are unwilling to consult or mediate, or if consultation or mediation fails, they may submit the dispute to an arbitration institution in the mainland or Hong Kong for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards.  
If the parties have not included an arbitration clause in the contract and have not reached a written arbitration agreement afterwards, they may prosecute with the People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定由对外经济贸易部负责解释。

**Article 21** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
