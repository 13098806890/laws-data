# 国务院关于禁止在市场经济活动中实行地区封锁的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：2011-01-08

> (2001年4月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第303号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系，禁止市场经济活动中的地区封锁行为，破除地方保护，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制定本规定。 第二条　各级人民政府及其所属部门负有消除地区封锁、保护公平竞争的责任，应当为建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系创造良好的环境和条件。 第三条　禁止各种形式的地区封锁行为。 禁止任何单位或者个人违反法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，以任何方式阻挠、干预外地产品或者工程建设类服务(以下简称服务)进入本地市场，或者对阻挠、干预外地产品或者服务进入本地市场的行为纵容、包庇，限制公平竞争。 第四条　地方各级人民政府及其所属部门(包括被授权或者委托行使行政权的组织，下同)不得违反法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，实行下列地区封锁行为： (一)以任何方式限定、变相限定单位或者个人只能经营、购买、使用本地生产的产品或者只能接受本地企业、指定企业、其他经济组织或者个人提供的服务； (二)在道路、车站、港口、航空港或者本行政区域边界设置关卡，阻碍外地产品进入或者本地产品运出； (三)对外地产品或者服务设定歧视性收费项目、规定歧视性价格，或者实行歧视性收费标准； (四)对外地产品或者服务采取与本地同类产品或者服务不同的技术要求、检验标准，或者对外地产品或者服务采取重复检验、重复认证等歧视性技术措施，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场； (五)采取专门针对外地产品或者服务的专营、专卖、审批、许可等手段，实行歧视性待遇，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场； (六)通过设定歧视性资质要求、评审标准或者不依法发布信息等方式限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人参加本地的招投标活动； (七)以采取同本地企业、其他经济组织或者个人不平等的待遇等方式，限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地投资或者设立分支机构，或者对外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地的投资或者设立的分支机构实行歧视性待遇，侵害其合法权益； (八)实行地区封锁的其他行为。 第五条　任何地方不得制定实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的规定，妨碍建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系，损害公平竞争环境。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系，禁止市场经济活动中的地区封锁行为，破除地方保护，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of establishing and improving a national unified, fair competition, standardized and orderly market system, prohibiting regional blockade acts in market economic activities, breaking down local protectionism, and maintaining the order of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府及其所属部门负有消除地区封锁、保护公平竞争的责任，应当为建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系创造良好的环境和条件。

**Article 2** People's governments at all levels and their subordinate departments bear the responsibility of eliminating regional blockade and protecting fair competition, and shall create a favorable environment and conditions for establishing and improving a national unified, fair competition, standardized and orderly market system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　禁止各种形式的地区封锁行为。  
禁止任何单位或者个人违反法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，以任何方式阻挠、干预外地产品或者工程建设类服务(以下简称服务)进入本地市场，或者对阻挠、干预外地产品或者服务进入本地市场的行为纵容、包庇，限制公平竞争。

**Article 3** Regional blockade acts in any form are prohibited.  
No unit or individual shall, in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council, obstruct or interfere in any way with the entry of products from other regions or engineering construction services (hereinafter referred to as services) into the local market, or condone or cover up acts that obstruct or interfere with the entry of products or services from other regions into the local market, thereby restricting fair competition.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民政府及其所属部门(包括被授权或者委托行使行政权的组织，下同)不得违反法律、行政法规和国务院的规定，实行下列地区封锁行为：  
(一)以任何方式限定、变相限定单位或者个人只能经营、购买、使用本地生产的产品或者只能接受本地企业、指定企业、其他经济组织或者个人提供的服务；  
(二)在道路、车站、港口、航空港或者本行政区域边界设置关卡，阻碍外地产品进入或者本地产品运出；  
(三)对外地产品或者服务设定歧视性收费项目、规定歧视性价格，或者实行歧视性收费标准；  
(四)对外地产品或者服务采取与本地同类产品或者服务不同的技术要求、检验标准，或者对外地产品或者服务采取重复检验、重复认证等歧视性技术措施，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场；  
(五)采取专门针对外地产品或者服务的专营、专卖、审批、许可等手段，实行歧视性待遇，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场；  
(六)通过设定歧视性资质要求、评审标准或者不依法发布信息等方式限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人参加本地的招投标活动；  
(七)以采取同本地企业、其他经济组织或者个人不平等的待遇等方式，限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地投资或者设立分支机构，或者对外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地的投资或者设立的分支机构实行歧视性待遇，侵害其合法权益；  
(八)实行地区封锁的其他行为。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at all levels and their subordinate departments (including organizations authorized or entrusted to exercise administrative powers, the same below) shall not, in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council, implement any of the following regional blockade acts:  
(1) limiting or disguisedly limiting, in any manner, units or individuals to only operate, purchase, or use products produced locally, or to only accept services provided by local enterprises, designated enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals;  
(2) setting up checkpoints on roads, at railway stations, ports, airports, or at the boundaries of their administrative regions to obstruct the entry of products from other regions or the outward shipment of local products;  
(3) establishing discriminatory fee items, prescribing discriminatory prices, or implementing discriminatory fee standards for products or services from other regions;  
(4) adopting technical requirements or inspection standards for products or services from other regions that are different from those for similar local products or services, or adopting discriminatory technical measures such as repeated inspections or repeated certifications for products or services from other regions, thereby restricting the entry of such products or services into the local market;  
(5) adopting means such as exclusive dealing, monopoly, examination and approval, or licensing specifically targeting products or services from other regions, implementing discriminatory treatment, and restricting the entry of such products or services into the local market;  
(6) restricting or excluding enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals from other regions from participating in local bid invitations or tendering activities by setting discriminatory qualification requirements, evaluation criteria, or failing to publish information according to law;  
(7) restricting or excluding enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals from other regions from investing or establishing branches locally by adopting unequal treatment compared to local enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals, or implementing discriminatory treatment against the investments or branches of such entities from other regions, thereby infringing on their legitimate rights and interests;  
(8) other acts that constitute regional blockade.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何地方不得制定实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的规定，妨碍建立和完善全国统一、公平竞争、规范有序的市场体系，损害公平竞争环境。

**Article 5** No locality shall formulate provisions that implement regional blockade or contain content of regional blockade, thereby hindering the establishment and improvement of a national unified, fair competition, standardized and orderly market system and impairing the environment of fair competition.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　地方各级人民政府所属部门的规定属于实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的，由本级人民政府改变或者撤销；本级人民政府不予改变或者撤销的，由上一级人民政府改变或者撤销。

**Article 6** Where the provisions of the subordinate departments of a local people's government at any level implement regional blockade or contain content of regional blockade, such provisions shall be changed or revoked by the people's government at the same level; if the people's government at the same level fails to change or revoke them, they shall be changed or revoked by the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　省、自治区、直辖市以下地方各级人民政府的规定属于实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的，由上一级人民政府改变或者撤销；上一级人民政府不予改变或者撤销的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府改变或者撤销。

**Article 7** Where the provisions of local people's governments below the level of province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government implement regional blockade or contain content of regional blockade, such provisions shall be changed or revoked by the people's government at the next higher level; if the people's government at the next higher level fails to change or revoke them, they shall be changed or revoked by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定属于实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的，由国务院改变或者撤销。

**Article 8** Where the provisions of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government implement regional blockade or contain content of regional blockade, such provisions shall be changed or revoked by the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级人民政府或者其所属部门设置地区封锁的规定或者含有地区封锁内容的规定，是以国务院所属部门不适当的规定为依据的，由国务院改变或者撤销该部门不适当的规定。

**Article 9** Where the provisions of a local people's government at any level or its subordinate departments that implement regional blockade or contain content of regional blockade are based on inappropriate provisions of a department under the State Council, the State Council shall change or revoke such inappropriate provisions of that department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　以任何方式限定、变相限定单位或者个人只能经营、购买、使用本地生产的产品或者只能接受本地企业、指定企业、其他经济组织或者个人提供的服务的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门查处，撤销限定措施。

**Article 10** Where any person, in any manner, limits or disguisedly limits units or individuals to only operate, purchase, or use products produced locally or to only accept services provided by local enterprises, designated enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent department of economic and trade and the administrative department for industry and commerce to investigate and handle the case, and shall revoke the restrictive measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在道路、车站、港口、航空港或者在本行政区域边界设置关卡，阻碍外地产品进入和本地产品运出的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织经济贸易管理部门、公安部门和交通部门查处，撤销关卡。

**Article 11** Where any person sets up checkpoints on roads, at railway stations, ports, airports, or at the boundaries of its administrative region, thereby obstructing the entry of products from other regions or the outward shipment of local products, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent department of economic and trade, the public security department, and the transportation department to investigate and handle the case, and shall revoke the checkpoints.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对外地产品或者服务设定歧视性收费项目、规定歧视性价格，或者实行歧视性收费标准的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织财政部门和价格部门查处，撤销歧视性收费项目、价格或者收费标准。

**Article 12** Where any person establishes discriminatory fee items, prescribes discriminatory prices, or implements discriminatory fee standards for products or services from other regions, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the financial department and the price department to investigate and handle the case, and shall revoke the discriminatory fee items, prices, or fee standards.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对外地产品或者服务采取和本地同类产品或者服务不同的技术要求、检验标准，或者对外地产品或者服务采取重复检验、重复认证等歧视性技术措施，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织质量技术监督部门查处，撤销歧视性技术措施。

**Article 13** Where any person adopts technical requirements or inspection standards for products or services from other regions that are different from those for similar local products or services, or adopts discriminatory technical measures such as repeated inspections or repeated certifications for products or services from other regions, thereby restricting the entry of such products or services into the local market, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the quality and technical supervision department to investigate and handle the case, and shall revoke the discriminatory technical measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　采取专门针对外地产品或者服务的专营、专卖、审批、许可等手段，实行歧视性待遇，限制外地产品或者服务进入本地市场的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门、质量技术监督部门和其他有关主管部门查处，撤销歧视性待遇。

**Article 14** Where any person adopts means such as exclusive dealing, monopoly, examination and approval, or licensing specifically targeting products or services from other regions, implements discriminatory treatment, and restricts the entry of such products or services into the local market, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent department of economic and trade, the administrative department for industry and commerce, the quality and technical supervision department, and other relevant competent departments to investigate and handle the case, and shall revoke the discriminatory treatment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　通过设定歧视性资质要求、评审标准或者不依法发布信息等方式，限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人参加本地的招投标活动的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织有关主管部门查处，消除障碍。

**Article 15** Where any person restricts or excludes enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals from other regions from participating in local bid invitations or tendering activities by setting discriminatory qualification requirements, evaluation criteria, or failing to publish information according to law, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the relevant competent departments to investigate and handle the case, and shall eliminate the obstacles.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　以采取同本地企业、其他经济组织或者个人不平等的待遇等方式，限制或者排斥外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地投资或者设立分支机构，或者对外地企业、其他经济组织或者个人在本地的投资或者设立的分支机构实行歧视性待遇的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门查处，消除障碍。

**Article 16** Where any person restricts or excludes enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals from other regions from investing or establishing branches locally by adopting unequal treatment compared to local enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals, or implements discriminatory treatment against the investments or branches of such entities from other regions, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent department of economic and trade and the administrative department for industry and commerce to investigate and handle the case, and shall eliminate the obstacles.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　实行[本规定第四条](#art-4)第(一)项至第(七)项所列行为以外的其他地区封锁行为的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门、质量技术监督部门和其他有关主管部门查处，消除地区封锁。

**Article 17** Where any person implements any other regional blockade act other than those listed in subparagraphs (1) through (7) of Article 4 of these Provisions, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent department of economic and trade, the administrative department for industry and commerce, the quality and technical supervision department, and other relevant competent departments to investigate and handle the case, and shall eliminate the regional blockade.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照[本规定第十条](#art-10)至第十七条的规定组织所属有关部门对地区封锁行为进行查处，处理决定由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府作出；必要时，国务院经济贸易管理部门、国务院工商行政管理部门、国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者国务院其他有关部门可以对涉及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的地区封锁行为进行查处。  
地方各级人民政府及其所属部门不得以任何名义、方式阻挠、干预依照本规定对地区封锁行为进行的查处工作。

**Article 18** The people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 10 through 17 of these Provisions, organize its relevant subordinate departments to investigate and handle regional blockade acts, and the decision on handling shall be made by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; when necessary, the competent department of economic and trade of the State Council, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council, or other relevant departments of the State Council may investigate and handle regional blockade acts involving the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Local people's governments at all levels and their subordinate departments shall not, in any name or manner, obstruct or interfere with the investigation and handling of regional blockade acts conducted in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地区封锁行为属于根据地方人民政府或者其所属部门的规定实行的，除依照[本规定第十条](#art-10)至第十七条的规定查处、消除地区封锁外，并应当依照[本规定第六条](#art-6)至第九条的规定，对有关规定予以改变或者撤销。

**Article 19** Where a regional blockade act is implemented pursuant to the provisions of a local people's government or its subordinate departments, in addition to investigating, handling, and eliminating the regional blockade in accordance with the provisions of Articles 10 through 17 of these Provisions, the relevant provisions shall also be changed or revoked in accordance with the provisions of Articles 6 through 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　任何单位和个人均有权对地区封锁行为进行抵制，并向有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门、质量技术监督部门或者其他有关部门直至国务院经济贸易管理部门、国务院工商行政管理部门、国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者国务院其他有关部门检举。  
有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其经济贸易管理部门、工商行政管理部门、质量技术监督部门或者其他有关部门接到检举后，应当自接到检举之日起5个工作日内，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府责成有关地方人民政府在30个工作日内调查、处理完毕，或者由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府在30个工作日内依照本规定直接调查、处理完毕；特殊情况下，调查、处理时间可以适当延长，但延长的时间不得超过30个工作日。  
国务院经济贸易管理部门、国务院工商行政管理部门、国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者国务院其他有关部门接到检举后，应当在5个工作日内，将检举材料转送有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府。  
接受检举的政府、部门应当为检举人保密。对检举有功的单位和个人，应当给予奖励。

**Article 20** Any unit or individual shall have the right to resist regional blockade acts and to report them to the relevant people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or to its competent department of economic and trade, administrative department for industry and commerce, quality and technical supervision department, or other relevant departments, or even to the competent department of economic and trade of the State Council, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council, or other relevant departments of the State Council.  
After receiving a report, the relevant people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or its competent department of economic and trade, administrative department for industry and commerce, quality and technical supervision department, or other relevant departments shall, within 5 working days from the date of receiving the report, have the people's government of the relevant locality complete investigation and handling within 30 working days, or the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall directly investigate and complete handling within 30 working days in accordance with these Provisions; under special circumstances, the time for investigation and handling may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed 30 working days.  
After receiving a report, the competent department of economic and trade of the State Council, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council, or other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within 5 working days, transfer the reporting materials to the relevant people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The government or department that accepts the report shall keep the confidentiality of the whistleblower. Units and individuals that have rendered meritorious service in reporting shall be given rewards.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对地方人民政府或者其所属部门违反本规定，实行地区封锁的，纵容、包庇地区封锁的，或者阻挠、干预查处地区封锁的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府给予通报批评；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府违反本规定，实行地区封锁的，纵容、包庇地区封锁的，或者阻挠、干预查处地区封锁的，由国务院给予通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，按照法定程序，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where a local people's government or any department thereof violates these Provisions by imposing regional blockades, conniving at or covering up regional blockades, or obstructing or interfering with the investigation and handling of regional blockades, the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall circulate a notice of criticism; where a people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government violates these Provisions by imposing regional blockades, conniving at or covering up regional blockades, or obstructing or interfering with the investigation and handling of regional blockades, the State Council shall circulate a notice of criticism. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall, in accordance with the statutory procedures and depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地方人民政府或者其所属部门违反本规定，制定实行地区封锁或者含有地区封锁内容的规定的，除依照[本规定第六条](#art-6)至第九条的规定对有关规定予以改变或者撤销外，对该地方人民政府或者其所属部门的主要负责人和签署该规定的负责人，按照法定程序，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 22** Where a local people's government or any department thereof violates these Provisions by formulating provisions imposing regional blockades or containing regional blockade content, in addition to changing or revoking the relevant provisions in accordance with Articles 6 through 9 of these Provisions, the principal responsible person of such local people's government or department and the responsible person who signed the said provisions shall, in accordance with the statutory procedures and depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　接到检举地区封锁行为的政府或者有关部门，不在规定期限内进行调查、处理或者泄露检举人情况的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，按照法定程序，根据情节轻重，给予降级、撤职直至开除公职的行政处分。

**Article 23** Where the government or a relevant department that receives a report of regional blockade conduct fails to investigate and handle it within the prescribed time limit, or divulges the informant's information, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall, in accordance with the statutory procedures and depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or even dismissal from public employment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　采取暴力、威胁等手段，欺行霸市、强买强卖，阻碍外地产品或者服务进入本地市场，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
经营单位有前款规定行为的，并由工商行政管理部门依法对该经营单位予以处罚，直至责令停产停业、予以查封并吊销其营业执照。

**Article 24** Where a person uses violence, threats, or other means to dominate the market, engage in forcible buying and selling, or impede the entry of products or services from other localities into the local market, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a business unit commits any act specified in the preceding paragraph, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, according to law, impose penalties on the said business unit, up to and including ordering it to suspend production or business, sealing up its premises, and revoking its business license.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　地方人民政府或者其所属部门滥用行政权力，实行地区封锁所收取的费用及其他不正当收入，应当返还有关企业、其他经济组织或者个人；无法返还的，由上一级人民政府财政部门予以收缴。

**Article 25** Fees and other improper incomes collected by a local people's government or any department thereof through the abuse of administrative power to impose regional blockades shall be returned to the relevant enterprises, other economic organizations, or individuals; if return is impossible, the finance department of the people's government at the next higher level shall confiscate them.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　地方人民政府或者其所属部门的工作人员对检举地区封锁行为的单位或者个人进行报复陷害的，按照法定程序，根据情节轻重，给予降级、撤职直至开除公职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where a staff member of a local people's government or any department thereof retaliates against or frames up any unit or individual that reports regional blockade conduct, the staff member shall, in accordance with the statutory procedures and depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or even dismissal from public employment; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　监察机关依照行政监察法的规定，对行政机关及其工作人员的地区封锁行为实施监察。

**Article 27** Supervisory organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Supervision Law, exercise supervision over regional blockade conduct of administrative organs and their staff members.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本规定自公布之日起施行。  
自本规定施行之日起，地方各级人民政府及其所属部门的规定与本规定相抵触或者部分相抵触的，全部或者相抵触的部分自行失效。  
依据宪法和有关法律关于地方性法规不得同宪法、法律和行政法规相抵触的规定，自本规定施行之日起，地方性法规同本规定相抵触的，应当执行本规定。

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.  
As of the date on which these Provisions come into force, where provisions of local people's governments at all levels and their departments contravene or partially contravene these Provisions, such provisions shall wholly or partially cease to have effect automatically.  
Pursuant to the provisions of the Constitution and relevant laws that local regulations shall not contravene the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations, as of the date on which these Provisions come into force, where local regulations contravene these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
