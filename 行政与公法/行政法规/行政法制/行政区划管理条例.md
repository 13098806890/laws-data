# 行政区划管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-10-10  
**生效日期**：2019-01-01

> (2017年11月22日国务院第193次常务会议通过　2018年10月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第704号公布　自2019年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强行政区划的管理，根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法》、《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　行政区划管理工作应当加强党的领导，加强顶层规划。行政区划应当保持总体稳定，必须变更时，应当本着有利于社会主义现代化建设、有利于推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化、有利于行政管理、有利于民族团结、有利于巩固国防的原则，坚持与国家发展战略和经济社会发展水平相适应、注重城乡统筹和区域协调、推进城乡发展一体化、促进人与自然和谐发展的方针，制订变更方案，逐级上报审批。行政区划的重大调整应当及时报告党中央。 第三条　行政区划的设立、撤销以及变更隶属关系或者行政区域界线时，应当考虑经济发展、资源环境、人文历史、地形地貌、治理能力等情况；变更人民政府驻地时，应当优化资源配置、便于提供公共服务；变更行政区划名称时，应当体现当地历史、文化和地理特征。 第四条　国务院民政部门负责全国行政区划的具体管理工作。国务院其他有关部门按照各自职责做好全国行政区划相关的管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域行政区划的具体管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照各自职责做好本行政区域行政区划相关的管理工作。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对行政区划管理工作的领导，将行政区划工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，将行政区划的管理工作经费纳入预算。 第六条　省、自治区、直辖市的设立、撤销、更名，报全国人民代表大会批准。 第七条　下列行政区划的变更由国务院审批： (一)省、自治区、直辖市的行政区域界线的变更，人民政府驻地的迁移，简称、排列顺序的变更； (二)自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的设立、撤销、更名和隶属关系的变更以及自治州、自治县、设区的市人民政府驻地的迁移； (三)自治州、自治县的行政区域界线的变更，县、市、市辖区的行政区域界线的重大变更； (四)凡涉及海岸线、海岛、边疆要地、湖泊、重要资源地区及特殊情况地区的隶属关系或者行政区域界线的变更。 第八条　县、市、市辖区的部分行政区域界线的变更，县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府驻地的迁移，国务院授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批；批准变更时，同时报送国务院备案。 第九条　乡、民族乡、镇的设立、撤销、更名，行政区域界线的变更，人民政府驻地的迁移，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强行政区划的管理，根据《中华人民共和国宪法》和《中华人民共和国地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法》、《中华人民共和国民族区域自治法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of administrative divisions, in accordance with the Constitution of the People's Republic of China, the Organic Law of the Local People's Congresses and Local People's Governments of the People's Republic of China, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on Regional Ethnic Autonomy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　行政区划管理工作应当加强党的领导，加强顶层规划。行政区划应当保持总体稳定，必须变更时，应当本着有利于社会主义现代化建设、有利于推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化、有利于行政管理、有利于民族团结、有利于巩固国防的原则，坚持与国家发展战略和经济社会发展水平相适应、注重城乡统筹和区域协调、推进城乡发展一体化、促进人与自然和谐发展的方针，制订变更方案，逐级上报审批。行政区划的重大调整应当及时报告党中央。

**Article 2** The administration of administrative divisions shall strengthen Party leadership and top-level planning. Administrative divisions shall maintain overall stability, and when alteration is necessary, the principles of being conducive to socialist modernization, to the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity, to administrative management, to ethnic unity, and to national defense consolidation shall be adhered to, and the policies of being compatible with the national development strategy and the level of economic and social development, balancing urban and rural development and regional coordination, advancing the integration of urban and rural development, and promoting the harmonious development of humans and nature shall be upheld. Alteration plans shall be formulated and submitted level by level for approval. Major adjustments of administrative divisions shall be reported to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China in a timely manner.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　行政区划的设立、撤销以及变更隶属关系或者行政区域界线时，应当考虑经济发展、资源环境、人文历史、地形地貌、治理能力等情况；变更人民政府驻地时，应当优化资源配置、便于提供公共服务；变更行政区划名称时，应当体现当地历史、文化和地理特征。

**Article 3** When establishing, abolishing, or altering the subordination relationship or the boundaries of administrative divisions, consideration shall be given to such factors as economic development, resources and environment, humanities and history, topography and landforms, and governance capacity; when altering the seat of a people's government, the allocation of resources shall be optimized and the provision of public services shall be facilitated; when altering the name of an administrative division, the local historical, cultural, and geographical characteristics shall be reflected.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院民政部门负责全国行政区划的具体管理工作。国务院其他有关部门按照各自职责做好全国行政区划相关的管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域行政区划的具体管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照各自职责做好本行政区域行政区划相关的管理工作。

**Article 4** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for the specific management of administrative divisions nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall perform the management work related to administrative divisions nationwide in accordance with their respective functions.  
The civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the specific management of administrative divisions within their respective administrative areas. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall perform the management work related to administrative divisions within their respective administrative areas in accordance with their respective functions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对行政区划管理工作的领导，将行政区划工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，将行政区划的管理工作经费纳入预算。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over the management of administrative divisions, incorporate the work on administrative divisions into the national economic and social development plan, and include the funds for the management of administrative divisions in the budget.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　省、自治区、直辖市的设立、撤销、更名，报全国人民代表大会批准。

**Article 6** The establishment, abolition, or renaming of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be submitted to the National People's Congress for approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列行政区划的变更由国务院审批：  
(一)省、自治区、直辖市的行政区域界线的变更，人民政府驻地的迁移，简称、排列顺序的变更；  
(二)自治州、县、自治县、市、市辖区的设立、撤销、更名和隶属关系的变更以及自治州、自治县、设区的市人民政府驻地的迁移；  
(三)自治州、自治县的行政区域界线的变更，县、市、市辖区的行政区域界线的重大变更；  
(四)凡涉及海岸线、海岛、边疆要地、湖泊、重要资源地区及特殊情况地区的隶属关系或者行政区域界线的变更。

**Article 7** The following alterations of administrative divisions shall be examined and approved by the State Council:  
(1) alteration of the administrative boundary lines of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, relocation of the seat of a people's government, and alteration of the abbreviation or order;  
(2) establishment, abolition, renaming, and alteration of the subordination relationship of autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties, cities, and municipal districts, and relocation of the seat of the people's government of an autonomous prefecture, autonomous county, or city divided into districts;  
(3) alteration of the administrative boundary lines of autonomous prefectures and autonomous counties, and major alterations of the administrative boundary lines of counties, cities, and municipal districts;  
(4) any alteration involving the subordination relationship or administrative boundary lines of areas that include coastlines, islands, border areas of strategic importance, lakes, important resource areas, and special circumstances areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县、市、市辖区的部分行政区域界线的变更，县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府驻地的迁移，国务院授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批；批准变更时，同时报送国务院备案。

**Article 8** The alteration of part of the administrative boundary lines of counties, cities, and municipal districts, and the relocation of the seat of the people's government of a county, a city not divided into districts, or a municipal district, shall be examined and approved by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government as authorized by the State Council; when approving the alteration, it shall be simultaneously submitted to the State Council for record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　乡、民族乡、镇的设立、撤销、更名，行政区域界线的变更，人民政府驻地的迁移，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批。

**Article 9** The establishment, abolition, renaming of townships, ethnic townships, and towns, alteration of administrative boundary lines, and relocation of the seat of a people's government, shall be examined and approved by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依照法律、国家有关规定设立的地方人民政府的派出机关的撤销、更名、驻地迁移、管辖范围的确定和变更，由批准设立该派出机关的人民政府审批。

**Article 10** The abolition, renaming, relocation of the seat, and determination and alteration of the scope of jurisdiction of an agency dispatched by a local people's government established in accordance with laws and relevant provisions of the State shall be examined and approved by the people's government that approved the establishment of such agency.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　市、市辖区的设立标准，由国务院民政部门会同国务院其他有关部门拟订，报国务院批准。  
镇、街道的设立标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门会同本级人民政府其他有关部门拟订，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准；批准设立标准时，同时报送国务院备案。

**Article 11** The standards for establishing cities and municipal districts shall be drafted by the civil affairs department of the State Council in conjunction with other relevant departments of the State Council, and submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The standards for establishing towns and subdistricts shall be drafted by the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in conjunction with other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, and submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval; when approving the establishment standards, they shall be simultaneously submitted to the State Council for record.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　依照本条例报送国务院备案的事项，径送国务院民政部门。

**Article 12** Matters submitted to the State Council for record in accordance with these Regulations shall be sent directly to the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请变更行政区划向上级人民政府提交的材料应当包括：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)与行政区划变更有关的历史、地理、民族、经济、人口、资源环境、行政区域面积和隶属关系的基本情况；  
(三)风险评估报告；  
(四)专家论证报告；  
(五)征求社会公众等意见的情况；  
(六)变更前的行政区划图和变更方案示意图；  
(七)国务院民政部门规定应当提交的其他材料。

**Article 13** The materials submitted to the people's government at a higher level when applying for alteration of an administrative division shall include:  
(1) an application letter;  
(2) basic information on history, geography, ethnicity, economy, population, resources and environment, area of the administrative division, and subordination relationship related to the alteration of the administrative division;  
(3) a risk assessment report;  
(4) an expert demonstration report;  
(5) the status of solicitation of opinions from the public and others;  
(6) a map of the administrative division before alteration and a schematic diagram of the alteration plan;  
(7) other materials as prescribed by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府民政部门在承办行政区划变更的工作时，应当根据情况分别征求有关机构编制部门和本级人民政府的外事、发展改革、民族、财政、自然资源、住房城乡建设、城乡规划等有关部门的意见；在承办民族自治地方的行政区划变更的工作时，应当同民族自治地方的自治机关和有关民族的代表充分协商。

**Article 14** When undertaking the work of altering an administrative division, the civil affairs department of a people's government at or above the county level shall, according to circumstances, seek opinions from the relevant organizational establishment department and from the departments of foreign affairs, development and reform, ethnic affairs, finance, natural resources, housing and urban-rural construction, urban and rural planning, and other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level; when undertaking the alteration of an administrative division in an ethnic autonomous area, it shall fully consult with the autonomous organs of the ethnic autonomous area and representatives of the relevant ethnic groups.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有关地方人民政府应当自审批机关批准行政区划变更之日起12个月内完成变更；情况复杂，12个月内不能完成变更的，经审批机关批准，可以延长6个月；完成变更时，同时向审批机关报告。

**Article 15** The relevant local people's government shall complete the alteration within 12 months from the date of approval by the approving authority; if the circumstances are complex and the alteration cannot be completed within 12 months, the period may be extended by 6 months with the approval of the approving authority; upon completion of the alteration, it shall be reported to the approving authority at the same time.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　行政区划变更后，应当依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定勘定行政区域界线，并更新行政区划图。

**Article 16** After the alteration of an administrative division, the administrative boundary lines shall be surveyed and determined in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State, and the administrative division map shall be updated.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　行政区划变更后，需要变更行政区划代码的，由民政部门于1个月内确定、公布其行政区划代码。

**Article 17** After the alteration of an administrative division, if the administrative division code needs to be altered, the civil affairs department shall determine and publish the administrative division code within one month.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　行政区划变更后，有关地方人民政府应当向社会公告。

**Article 18** After the alteration of an administrative division, the relevant local people's government shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院民政部门应当建立行政区划管理的信息系统。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当及时向国务院民政部门报送本行政区域行政区划变更的信息。

**Article 19** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall establish an information system for the management of administrative divisions.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall promptly submit to the civil affairs department of the State Council the information on alterations of administrative divisions within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府民政部门，应当加强对行政区划档案的管理。  
行政区划管理中形成的请示、报告、图表、批准文件以及与行政区划管理工作有关的材料，应当依法整理归档，妥善保管。具体办法由国务院民政部门会同国家档案行政管理部门制定。

**Article 20** The civil affairs departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of archives of administrative divisions.  
Requests for instructions, reports, charts and diagrams, approval documents, and other materials related to the administration of administrative divisions generated in the process of administrative division management shall be arranged and filed according to law, and properly preserved. Specific measures shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council in conjunction with the state archives administrative department.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　上级人民政府应当加强对下级人民政府行政区划管理工作的监督、指导。

**Article 21** People's governments at higher levels shall strengthen the supervision and guidance over the administration of administrative divisions by people's governments at lower levels.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定，未及时完成行政区划变更、备案、信息报送的，由上一级人民政府责令限期完成。

**Article 22** Where a person fails to complete in a timely manner the alteration of an administrative division, the record, or the submission of information, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the people's government at the next higher level shall order completion within a time limit.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，擅自变更行政区划的，由上一级人民政府责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 23** Where a person alters an administrative division without authorization, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the people's government at the next higher level shall order correction; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，在行政区划变更过程中弄虚作假的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where a person engages in fraud in the course of altering an administrative division, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家工作人员在行政区划的管理工作中，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where a state functionary abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the administration of administrative divisions, a sanction shall be given according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院民政部门可以依据本条例的规定，制定具体实施办法。

**Article 26** The civil affairs department of the State Council may, pursuant to these Regulations, formulate specific implementation measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2019年1月1日起施行。1985年1月15日国务院发布的《国务院关于行政区划管理的规定》同时废止。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2019. The Regulations of the State Council on the Administration of Administrative Divisions, promulgated by the State Council on January 15, 1985, shall be simultaneously abolished.
