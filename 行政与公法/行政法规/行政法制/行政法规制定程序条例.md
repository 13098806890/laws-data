# 行政法规制定程序条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-12-22  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

> (2001年11月16日中华人民共和国国务院令第321号公布　根据2017年12月22日《国务院关于修改〈行政法规制定程序条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范行政法规制定程序，保证行政法规质量，根据宪法、立法法和国务院组织法的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　行政法规的立项、起草、审查、决定、公布、解释，适用本条例。 第三条　制定行政法规，应当贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，符合宪法和法律的规定，遵循立法法确定的立法原则。 第四条　制定政治方面法律的配套行政法规，应当按照有关规定及时报告党中央。 制定经济、文化、社会、生态文明等方面重大体制和重大政策调整的重要行政法规，应当将行政法规草案或者行政法规草案涉及的重大问题按照有关规定及时报告党中央。 第五条　行政法规的名称一般称“条例”，也可以称“规定”、“办法”等。国务院根据全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的授权决定制定的行政法规，称“暂行条例”或者“暂行规定”。 国务院各部门和地方人民政府制定的规章不得称“条例”。 第六条　行政法规应当备而不繁，逻辑严密，条文明确、具体，用语准确、简洁，具有可操作性。 行政法规根据内容需要，可以分章、节、条、款、项、目。章、节、条的序号用中文数字依次表述，款不编序号，项的序号用中文数字加括号依次表述，目的序号用阿拉伯数字依次表述。 第二章　立项 第七条　国务院于每年年初编制本年度的立法工作计划。 第八条　国务院有关部门认为需要制定行政法规的，应当于国务院编制年度立法工作计划前，向国务院报请立项。 国务院有关部门报送的行政法规立项申请，应当说明立法项目所要解决的主要问题、依据的党的路线方针政策和决策部署，以及拟确立的主要制度。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范行政法规制定程序，保证行政法规质量，根据宪法、立法法和国务院组织法的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Constitution, the Legislation Law and the Organic Law of the State Council for the purpose of regulating the formulation procedures for administrative regulations and ensuring the quality of administrative regulations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　行政法规的立项、起草、审查、决定、公布、解释，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the initiation, drafting, review, decision, promulgation and interpretation of administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　制定行政法规，应当贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，符合宪法和法律的规定，遵循立法法确定的立法原则。

**Article 3** In formulating administrative regulations, the Party's line, principles, policies and decisions and deployments shall be implemented and carried out, the provisions of the Constitution and laws shall be complied with, and the legislative principles established in the Legislation Law shall be followed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　制定政治方面法律的配套行政法规，应当按照有关规定及时报告党中央。  
制定经济、文化、社会、生态文明等方面重大体制和重大政策调整的重要行政法规，应当将行政法规草案或者行政法规草案涉及的重大问题按照有关规定及时报告党中央。

**Article 4** Where supporting administrative regulations for laws in the political field are formulated, they shall be reported to the Central Committee of the Party in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Where important administrative regulations involving major institutional and policy adjustments in the economic, cultural, social and ecological civilization fields are formulated, the draft administrative regulations or the major issues involved in the draft administrative regulations shall be reported to the Central Committee of the Party in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行政法规的名称一般称“条例”，也可以称“规定”、“办法”等。国务院根据全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会的授权决定制定的行政法规，称“暂行条例”或者“暂行规定”。  
国务院各部门和地方人民政府制定的规章不得称“条例”。

**Article 5** The title of administrative regulations shall generally be "Regulations", and may also be "Provisions" or "Measures", etc. Administrative regulations formulated by the State Council pursuant to the authorization decisions of the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee shall be called "Interim Regulations" or "Interim Provisions".  
Rules formulated by various departments of the State Council and local people's governments shall not be called "Regulations".

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行政法规应当备而不繁，逻辑严密，条文明确、具体，用语准确、简洁，具有可操作性。  
行政法规根据内容需要，可以分章、节、条、款、项、目。章、节、条的序号用中文数字依次表述，款不编序号，项的序号用中文数字加括号依次表述，目的序号用阿拉伯数字依次表述。

**Article 6** Administrative regulations shall be comprehensive yet not cumbersome, logically rigorous, clear and specific in articles, accurate and concise in wording, and shall be operable.  
Administrative regulations may, according to the needs of their content, be divided into chapters, sections, articles, paragraphs, subparagraphs and items. The serial numbers of chapters, sections and articles shall be expressed in sequence in Chinese numerals; paragraphs shall not be numbered; the serial numbers of subparagraphs shall be expressed in sequence in Chinese numerals enclosed in parentheses; and the serial numbers of items shall be expressed in sequence in Arabic numerals.

### 第二章　立项

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院于每年年初编制本年度的立法工作计划。

**Article 7** The State Council shall formulate the annual legislative work plan at the beginning of each year.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院有关部门认为需要制定行政法规的，应当于国务院编制年度立法工作计划前，向国务院报请立项。  
国务院有关部门报送的行政法规立项申请，应当说明立法项目所要解决的主要问题、依据的党的路线方针政策和决策部署，以及拟确立的主要制度。  
国务院法制机构应当向社会公开征集行政法规制定项目建议。

**Article 8** Where relevant departments of the State Council deem it necessary to formulate administrative regulations, they shall submit a proposal for project initiation to the State Council before the State Council formulates the annual legislative work plan.  
The application for project initiation of administrative regulations submitted by the relevant departments of the State Council shall specify the main problems to be solved by the legislative project, the Party's line, principles, policies, decisions and deployments on which it is based, and the main systems to be established.  
The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall solicit suggestions on projects for formulating administrative regulations from the public.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院法制机构应当根据国家总体工作部署，对行政法规立项申请和公开征集的行政法规制定项目建议进行评估论证，突出重点，统筹兼顾，拟订国务院年度立法工作计划，报党中央、国务院批准后向社会公布。  
列入国务院年度立法工作计划的行政法规项目应当符合下列要求：  
(一)贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，适应改革、发展、稳定的需要；  
(二)有关的改革实践经验基本成熟；  
(三)所要解决的问题属于国务院职权范围并需要国务院制定行政法规的事项。

**Article 9** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall, in light of the overall national work arrangements, assess and demonstrate the applications for project initiation of administrative regulations and the publicly solicited suggestions on projects for formulating administrative regulations, highlight the key points, make overall plans, draft the annual legislative work plan of the State Council, and after reporting it to the Central Committee of the Party and the State Council for approval, publish it to the public.  
The administrative regulations projects included in the annual legislative work plan of the State Council shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) Implementing and carrying out the Party's line, principles, policies, decisions and deployments, and meeting the needs of reform, development and stability;  
(2) The relevant reform practical experience is basically mature;  
(3) The problems to be solved fall within the functions and powers of the State Council and require the State Council to formulate administrative regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对列入国务院年度立法工作计划的行政法规项目，承担起草任务的部门应当抓紧工作，按照要求上报国务院；上报国务院前，应当与国务院法制机构沟通。  
国务院法制机构应当及时跟踪了解国务院各部门落实国务院年度立法工作计划的情况，加强组织协调和督促指导。  
国务院年度立法工作计划在执行中可以根据实际情况予以调整。

**Article 10** For the administrative regulations projects included in the annual legislative work plan of the State Council, the department undertaking the drafting work shall work hard, and submit the draft to the State Council in accordance with the requirements; before submitting it to the State Council, it shall communicate with the legislative affairs institution of the State Council.  
The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall timely track and understand the implementation of the annual legislative work plan of the State Council by various departments of the State Council, and strengthen organization, coordination, supervision and guidance.  
The annual legislative work plan of the State Council may be adjusted during implementation according to the actual circumstances.

### 第三章　起草

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政法规由国务院组织起草。国务院年度立法工作计划确定行政法规由国务院的一个部门或者几个部门具体负责起草工作，也可以确定由国务院法制机构起草或者组织起草。

**Article 11** Administrative regulations shall be drafted under the organization of the State Council. The annual legislative work plan of the State Council shall determine that one or several departments of the State Council shall be specifically responsible for the drafting work, or may also determine that the legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall draft or organize the drafting.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　起草行政法规，应当符合[本条例第三条](#art-3)、第四条的规定，并符合下列要求：  
(一)弘扬社会主义核心价值观；  
(二)体现全面深化改革精神，科学规范行政行为，促进政府职能向宏观调控、市场监管、社会管理、公共服务、环境保护等方面转变；  
(三)符合精简、统一、效能的原则，相同或者相近的职能规定由一个行政机关承担，简化行政管理手续；  
(四)切实保障公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，在规定其应当履行的义务的同时，应当规定其相应的权利和保障权利实现的途径；  
(五)体现行政机关的职权与责任相统一的原则，在赋予有关行政机关必要的职权的同时，应当规定其行使职权的条件、程序和应承担的责任。

**Article 12** Drafting of administrative regulations shall comply with the provisions of Articles 3 and 4 of these Regulations, and shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) Promoting the core socialist values;  
(2) Reflecting the spirit of comprehensively deepening reform, scientifically regulating administrative acts, and promoting the transformation of government functions towards macro-control, market supervision, social management, public services and environmental protection, etc.;  
(3) Complying with the principles of simplification, unity and efficiency, stipulating that the same or similar functions shall be performed by one administrative organ, and simplifying administrative procedures;  
(4) Effectively protecting the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, legal persons and other organizations, and while stipulating the obligations they shall perform, also stipulating their corresponding rights and the ways to ensure the realization of rights;  
(5) Reflecting the principle of unifying the functions and powers of administrative organs with their responsibilities, and while granting necessary functions and powers to the relevant administrative organs, stipulating the conditions, procedures and responsibilities for exercising such functions and powers.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　起草行政法规，起草部门应当深入调查研究，总结实践经验，广泛听取有关机关、组织和公民的意见。涉及社会公众普遍关注的热点难点问题和经济社会发展遇到的突出矛盾，减损公民、法人和其他组织权利或者增加其义务，对社会公众有重要影响等重大利益调整事项的，应当进行论证咨询。听取意见可以采取召开座谈会、论证会、听证会等多种形式。  
起草行政法规，起草部门应当将行政法规草案及其说明等向社会公布，征求意见，但是经国务院决定不公布的除外。向社会公布征求意见的期限一般不少于30日。  
起草专业性较强的行政法规，起草部门可以吸收相关领域的专家参与起草工作，或者委托有关专家、教学科研单位、社会组织起草。

**Article 13** When drafting administrative regulations, the drafting department shall conduct in-depth investigation and research, summarize practical experience, and extensively solicit opinions from relevant organs, organizations and citizens. For matters involving major interest adjustments such as hot and difficult issues of general public concern, prominent contradictions encountered in economic and social development, derogation of rights or increase of obligations of citizens, legal persons and other organizations, and having important impacts on the general public, demonstration and consultation shall be conducted. Opinions may be solicited by various means such as holding symposiums, demonstration meetings, and hearings.  
When drafting administrative regulations, the drafting department shall publish the draft administrative regulations and their explanation, etc., to the public for comments, unless the State Council decides not to publish them. The period for soliciting comments from the public shall generally be not less than 30 days.  
For drafting administrative regulations with strong professionalism, the drafting department may involve experts in relevant fields in the drafting work, or entrust relevant experts, teaching and research units, or social organizations with the drafting.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　起草行政法规，起草部门应当就涉及其他部门的职责或者与其他部门关系紧密的规定，与有关部门充分协商，涉及部门职责分工、行政许可、财政支持、税收优惠政策的，应当征得机构编制、财政、税务等相关部门同意。

**Article 14** When drafting administrative regulations, the drafting department shall fully consult with the relevant departments on provisions involving the functions and duties of other departments or having close relationships with other departments. If it involves division of functions and duties among departments, administrative licenses, financial support, or tax preferential policies, the consent of the relevant departments such as those for institutional establishment, finance and taxation shall be obtained.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　起草行政法规，起草部门应当对涉及有关管理体制、方针政策等需要国务院决策的重大问题提出解决方案，报国务院决定。

**Article 15** When drafting administrative regulations, the drafting department shall propose solutions for major issues involving the relevant management system, principles and policies, etc., that require decision by the State Council, and report them to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　起草部门向国务院报送的行政法规草案送审稿(以下简称行政法规送审稿)，应当由起草部门主要负责人签署。  
起草行政法规，涉及几个部门共同职责需要共同起草的，应当共同起草，达成一致意见后联合报送行政法规送审稿。几个部门共同起草的行政法规送审稿，应当由该几个部门主要负责人共同签署。

**Article 16** The draft administrative regulation submitted by the drafting department to the State Council for review (hereinafter referred to as the draft for review of the administrative regulation) shall be signed by the principal responsible person of the drafting department.  
Where the drafting of an administrative regulation involves the joint functions and duties of several departments and requires joint drafting, they shall draft jointly, and after reaching a consensus, jointly submit the draft for review of the administrative regulation. The draft for review of the administrative regulation jointly drafted by several departments shall be jointly signed by the principal responsible persons of those departments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　起草部门将行政法规送审稿报送国务院审查时，应当一并报送行政法规送审稿的说明和有关材料。  
行政法规送审稿的说明应当对立法的必要性，主要思路，确立的主要制度，征求有关机关、组织和公民意见的情况，各方面对送审稿主要问题的不同意见及其协调处理情况，拟设定、取消或者调整行政许可、行政强制的情况等作出说明。有关材料主要包括所规范领域的实际情况和相关数据、实践中存在的主要问题、国内外的有关立法资料、调研报告、考察报告等。

**Article 17** When submitting the draft for review of the administrative regulation to the State Council for examination, the drafting department shall also submit the explanation on the draft for review and relevant materials.  
The explanation on the draft for review of the administrative regulation shall explain the necessity of legislation, the main ideas, the main systems established, the circumstances of soliciting opinions from relevant organs, organizations and citizens, the different opinions on the main issues of the draft for review from various quarters and the coordinated handling thereof, and the situation of intending to establish, cancel or adjust administrative licenses and administrative compulsion, etc. The relevant materials mainly include the actual situation and relevant data in the regulated field, the main problems existing in practice, relevant legislative materials at home and abroad, research reports, investigation reports, etc.

### 第四章　审查

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　报送国务院的行政法规送审稿，由国务院法制机构负责审查。  
国务院法制机构主要从以下方面对行政法规送审稿进行审查：  
(一)是否严格贯彻落实党的路线方针政策和决策部署，是否符合宪法和法律的规定，是否遵循立法法确定的立法原则；  
(二)是否符合[本条例第十二条](#art-12)的要求；  
(三)是否与有关行政法规协调、衔接；  
(四)是否正确处理有关机关、组织和公民对送审稿主要问题的意见；  
(五)其他需要审查的内容。

**Article 18** The draft for review of the administrative regulation submitted to the State Council shall be examined by the legislative affairs institution of the State Council.  
The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall examine the draft for review of the administrative regulation mainly from the following aspects:  
(1) Whether it strictly implements and carries out the Party's line, principles, policies, decisions and deployments, whether it complies with the provisions of the Constitution and laws, and whether it follows the legislative principles established in the Legislation Law;  
(2) Whether it meets the requirements of Article 12 of these Regulations;  
(3) Whether it is coordinated and aligned with relevant administrative regulations;  
(4) Whether it correctly handles the opinions of relevant organs, organizations and citizens on the main issues of the draft for review;  
(5) Other contents that need to be examined.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　行政法规送审稿有下列情形之一的，国务院法制机构可以缓办或者退回起草部门：  
(一)制定行政法规的基本条件尚不成熟或者发生重大变化的；  
(二)有关部门对送审稿规定的主要制度存在较大争议，起草部门未征得机构编制、财政、税务等相关部门同意的；  
(三)未按照本条例有关规定公开征求意见的；  
(四)上报送审稿不符合[本条例第十五条](#art-15)、第十六条、第十七条规定的。

**Article 19** Where the draft for review of the administrative regulation falls under any of the following circumstances, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council may defer the processing or return it to the drafting department:  
(1) The basic conditions for formulating the administrative regulation are not yet mature or have undergone major changes;  
(2) The relevant departments have major disputes over the main systems stipulated in the draft for review, and the drafting department has not obtained the consent of the relevant departments such as those for institutional establishment, finance and taxation;  
(3) The opinions have not been publicly solicited in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) The submitted draft for review does not comply with the provisions of Articles 15, 16 and 17 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院法制机构应当将行政法规送审稿或者行政法规送审稿涉及的主要问题发送国务院有关部门、地方人民政府、有关组织和专家等各方面征求意见。国务院有关部门、地方人民政府应当在规定期限内反馈书面意见，并加盖本单位或者本单位办公厅(室)印章。  
国务院法制机构可以将行政法规送审稿或者修改稿及其说明等向社会公布，征求意见。向社会公布征求意见的期限一般不少于30日。

**Article 20** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall send the draft for review of the administrative regulation or the main issues involved therein to the relevant departments of the State Council, local people's governments, relevant organizations, experts and other parties for comments. The relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments shall provide written comments within the prescribed time limit, and affix the seal of their own organ or its general office (office).  
The legislative affairs institution of the State Council may publish the draft for review of the administrative regulation or the revised draft and its explanation, etc., to the public for comments. The period for soliciting comments from the public shall generally be not less than 30 days.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院法制机构应当就行政法规送审稿涉及的主要问题，深入基层进行实地调查研究，听取基层有关机关、组织和公民的意见。

**Article 21** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall conduct on-site investigation and research at the grassroots level on the main issues involved in the draft for review of the administrative regulation, and solicit opinions from grassroots relevant organs, organizations and citizens.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　行政法规送审稿涉及重大利益调整的，国务院法制机构应当进行论证咨询，广泛听取有关方面的意见。论证咨询可以采取座谈会、论证会、听证会、委托研究等多种形式。  
行政法规送审稿涉及重大利益调整或者存在重大意见分歧，对公民、法人或者其他组织的权利义务有较大影响，人民群众普遍关注的，国务院法制机构可以举行听证会，听取有关机关、组织和公民的意见。

**Article 22** Where the draft for review of the administrative regulation involves major interest adjustments, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall conduct demonstration and consultation, and extensively solicit opinions from relevant parties. Demonstration and consultation may be conducted by various means such as symposiums, demonstration meetings, hearings, and commissioned research.  
If the draft for review of the administrative regulation involves major interest adjustments or there are major differences of opinion, which has a significant impact on the rights and obligations of citizens, legal persons or other organizations, and is of general public concern, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council may hold hearings to solicit opinions from relevant organs, organizations and citizens.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院有关部门对行政法规送审稿涉及的主要制度、方针政策、管理体制、权限分工等有不同意见的，国务院法制机构应当进行协调，力求达成一致意见。对有较大争议的重要立法事项，国务院法制机构可以委托有关专家、教学科研单位、社会组织进行评估。  
经过充分协调不能达成一致意见的，国务院法制机构、起草部门应当将争议的主要问题、有关部门的意见以及国务院法制机构的意见及时报国务院领导协调，或者报国务院决定。

**Article 23** Where the relevant departments of the State Council have different opinions on the main systems, principles and policies, management system, division of powers, etc., involved in the draft for review of the administrative regulation, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall coordinate to strive to reach a consensus. For important legislative matters with significant disputes, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council may entrust relevant experts, teaching and research units, or social organizations to conduct evaluation.  
Where a consensus cannot be reached after full coordination, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council and the drafting department shall promptly report the main issues in dispute, the opinions of the relevant departments, and the opinions of the legislative affairs institution of the State Council to the leaders of the State Council for coordination, or report to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院法制机构应当认真研究各方面的意见，与起草部门协商后，对行政法规送审稿进行修改，形成行政法规草案和对草案的说明。

**Article 24** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall carefully study the opinions from all quarters, and after consulting with the drafting department, revise the draft for review of the administrative regulation to form the draft administrative regulation and the explanation on the draft.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　行政法规草案由国务院法制机构主要负责人提出提请国务院常务会议审议的建议；对调整范围单一、各方面意见一致或者依据法律制定的配套行政法规草案，可以采取传批方式，由国务院法制机构直接提请国务院审批。

**Article 25** The principal responsible person of the legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall propose a recommendation to submit the draft administrative regulation to the State Council executive meeting for deliberation; for draft administrative regulations with a narrow scope of adjustment, with consensus among all parties, or which are supporting regulations formulated pursuant to laws, the method of circulation for approval may be adopted, and the legislative affairs institution of the State Council may directly submit the draft to the State Council for approval.

### 第五章　决定与公布

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　行政法规草案由国务院常务会议审议，或者由国务院审批。  
国务院常务会议审议行政法规草案时，由国务院法制机构或者起草部门作说明。

**Article 26** The draft administrative regulation shall be deliberated at the State Council executive meeting, or be examined and approved by the State Council.  
When the State Council executive meeting deliberates the draft administrative regulation, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council or the drafting department shall make explanations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院法制机构应当根据国务院对行政法规草案的审议意见，对行政法规草案进行修改，形成草案修改稿，报请总理签署国务院令公布施行。  
签署公布行政法规的国务院令载明该行政法规的施行日期。

**Article 27** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall, based on the State Council's deliberation opinions on the draft administrative regulation, revise the draft administrative regulation to form a revised draft, and submit it to the Premier for signing an order of the State Council for promulgation and implementation.  
The order of the State Council signing and promulgating the administrative regulation shall specify the implementation date of the administrative regulation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　行政法规签署公布后，及时在国务院公报和中国政府法制信息网以及在全国范围内发行的报纸上刊载。国务院法制机构应当及时汇编出版行政法规的国家正式版本。  
在国务院公报上刊登的行政法规文本为标准文本。

**Article 28** After the administrative regulation is signed and promulgated, it shall be published promptly in the Bulletin of the State Council, on the Chinese Government Legal Information Website, and in newspapers distributed nationwide. The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall promptly compile and publish the official national version of the administrative regulation.  
The text of the administrative regulation published in the Bulletin of the State Council shall be the standard text.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行政法规应当自公布之日起30日后施行；但是，涉及国家安全、外汇汇率、货币政策的确定以及公布后不立即施行将有碍行政法规施行的，可以自公布之日起施行。

**Article 29** Administrative regulations shall come into force 30 days after the date of promulgation; however, those involving state security, foreign exchange rates, the determination of monetary policies, or where failure to come into force immediately after promulgation would hinder the implementation of the administrative regulation, may come into force on the date of promulgation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　行政法规在公布后的30日内由国务院办公厅报全国人民代表大会常务委员会备案。

**Article 30** Within 30 days after promulgation, the administrative regulation shall be submitted by the General Office of the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for recordation.

### 第六章　行政法规解释

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　行政法规有下列情形之一的，由国务院解释：  
(一)行政法规的规定需要进一步明确具体含义的；  
(二)行政法规制定后出现新的情况，需要明确适用行政法规依据的。  
国务院法制机构研究拟订行政法规解释草案，报国务院同意后，由国务院公布或者由国务院授权国务院有关部门公布。  
行政法规的解释与行政法规具有同等效力。

**Article 31** An administrative regulation shall be interpreted by the State Council under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The provisions of the administrative regulation need further clarification of their specific meanings;  
(2) New circumstances emerge after the formulation of the administrative regulation, and the basis for applying the administrative regulation needs to be clarified.  
The legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall study and draft the interpretation draft of the administrative regulation, and after reporting it to the State Council for approval, the interpretation shall be promulgated by the State Council or by the relevant department of the State Council authorized by the State Council.  
The interpretation of the administrative regulation shall have the same effect as the administrative regulation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院各部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以向国务院提出行政法规解释要求。

**Article 32** Various departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may submit requests for interpretation of administrative regulations to the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对属于行政工作中具体应用行政法规的问题，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府法制机构以及国务院有关部门法制机构请求国务院法制机构解释的，国务院法制机构可以研究答复；其中涉及重大问题的，由国务院法制机构提出意见，报国务院同意后答复。

**Article 33** For issues concerning the specific application of administrative regulations in administrative work, where the legislative affairs institutions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government or the legislative affairs institutions of relevant departments of the State Council request an interpretation from the legislative affairs institution of the State Council, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council may study and reply; where major issues are involved, the legislative affairs institution of the State Council shall propose an opinion, and after reporting it to the State Council for approval, shall give a reply.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　拟订国务院提请全国人民代表大会或者全国人民代表大会常务委员会审议的法律草案，参照本条例的有关规定办理。

**Article 34** The drafting of legislative bills to be submitted by the State Council to the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee for deliberation shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院可以根据全面深化改革、经济社会发展需要，就行政管理等领域的特定事项，决定在一定期限内在部分地方暂时调整或者暂时停止适用行政法规的部分规定。

**Article 35** The State Council may, in light of the needs of comprehensively deepening reform and economic and social development, decide, on specific matters in the field of administrative management and other fields, to temporarily adjust or temporarily suspend the application of some provisions of administrative regulations in some localities within a certain period.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院法制机构或者国务院有关部门应当根据全面深化改革、经济社会发展需要以及上位法规定，及时组织开展行政法规清理工作。对不适应全面深化改革和经济社会发展要求、不符合上位法规定的行政法规，应当及时修改或者废止。

**Article 36** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council shall, in light of the needs of comprehensively deepening reform, economic and social development and the provisions of superior laws, promptly organize and carry out the cleanup of administrative regulations. Administrative regulations that do not meet the requirements of comprehensively deepening reform and economic and social development, or that do not comply with the provisions of superior laws, shall be promptly revised or abolished.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院法制机构或者国务院有关部门可以组织对有关行政法规或者行政法规中的有关规定进行立法后评估，并把评估结果作为修改、废止有关行政法规的重要参考。

**Article 37** The legislative affairs institution of the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council may organize post-legislation evaluation of relevant administrative regulations or relevant provisions of administrative regulations, and use the evaluation results as important references for revising or abolishing the relevant administrative regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　行政法规的修改、废止程序适用本条例的有关规定。  
行政法规修改、废止后，应当及时公布。

**Article 38** The procedures for the revision and abolition of administrative regulations shall be governed by the relevant provisions of these Regulations.  
After the revision or abolition of an administrative regulation, it shall be promptly promulgated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　行政法规的外文正式译本和民族语言文本，由国务院法制机构审定。

**Article 39** The official foreign language translations and ethnic language versions of administrative regulations shall be examined and approved by the legislative affairs institution of the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。1987年4月21日国务院批准、国务院办公厅发布的《行政法规制定程序暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002. The Interim Regulations on Procedures for Formulating Administrative Regulations approved by the State Council on April 21, 1987 and promulgated by the General Office of the State Council shall be abolished simultaneously.
