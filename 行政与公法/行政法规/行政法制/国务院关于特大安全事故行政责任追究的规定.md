# 国务院关于特大安全事故行政责任追究的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2001-04-21  
**生效日期**：2001-04-21

> (2001年4月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第302号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　为了有效地防范特大安全事故的发生，严肃追究特大安全事故的行政责任，保障人民群众生命、财产安全，制定本规定。 第二条　地方人民政府主要领导人和政府有关部门正职负责人对下列特大安全事故的防范、发生，依照法律、行政法规和本规定的规定有失职、渎职情形或者负有领导责任的，依照本规定给予行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任： (一)特大火灾事故； (二)特大交通安全事故； (三)特大建筑质量安全事故； (四)民用爆炸物品和化学危险品特大安全事故； (五)煤矿和其他矿山特大安全事故； (六)锅炉、压力容器、压力管道和特种设备特大安全事故； (七)其他特大安全事故。 地方人民政府和政府有关部门对特大安全事故的防范、发生直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，比照本规定给予行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 特大安全事故肇事单位和个人的刑事处罚、行政处罚和民事责任，依照有关法律、法规和规章的规定执行。 第三条　特大安全事故的具体标准，按照国家有关规定执行。 第四条　地方各级人民政府及政府有关部门应当依照有关法律、法规和规章的规定，采取行政措施，对本地区实施安全监督管理，保障本地区人民群众生命、财产安全，对本地区或者职责范围内防范特大安全事故的发生、特大安全事故发生后的迅速和妥善处理负责。 第五条　地方各级人民政府应当每个季度至少召开一次防范特大安全事故工作会议，由政府主要领导人或者政府主要领导人委托政府分管领导人召集有关部门正职负责人参加，分析、布置、督促、检查本地区防范特大安全事故的工作。会议应当作出决定并形成纪要，会议确定的各项防范措施必须严格实施。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了有效地防范特大安全事故的发生，严肃追究特大安全事故的行政责任，保障人民群众生命、财产安全，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of effectively preventing the occurrence of extraordinarily serious safety accidents, seriously investigating the administrative liability for extraordinarily serious safety accidents, and ensuring the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地方人民政府主要领导人和政府有关部门正职负责人对下列特大安全事故的防范、发生，依照法律、行政法规和本规定的规定有失职、渎职情形或者负有领导责任的，依照本规定给予行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)特大火灾事故；  
(二)特大交通安全事故；  
(三)特大建筑质量安全事故；  
(四)民用爆炸物品和化学危险品特大安全事故；  
(五)煤矿和其他矿山特大安全事故；  
(六)锅炉、压力容器、压力管道和特种设备特大安全事故；  
(七)其他特大安全事故。  
地方人民政府和政府有关部门对特大安全事故的防范、发生直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，比照本规定给予行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
特大安全事故肇事单位和个人的刑事处罚、行政处罚和民事责任，依照有关法律、法规和规章的规定执行。

**Article 2** Where the principal leading persons of local people's governments and the principal responsible persons of relevant government departments, in respect of the prevention and occurrence of the following extraordinarily serious safety accidents, are negligent, derelict of duty, or bear leading liability in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and these Provisions, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to these Provisions; if their acts constitute the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) extraordinarily serious fire accidents;  
(2) extraordinarily serious traffic safety accidents;  
(3) extraordinarily serious construction quality safety accidents;  
(4) extraordinarily serious safety accidents involving civil explosives and chemical dangerous goods;  
(5) extraordinarily serious safety accidents in coal mines and other mines;  
(6) extraordinarily serious safety accidents involving boilers, pressure vessels, pressure pipelines and special equipment;  
(7) other extraordinarily serious safety accidents.  
The persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible for the prevention and occurrence of extraordinarily serious safety accidents in local people's governments and relevant government departments shall be given administrative sanctions by reference to these Provisions; if their acts constitute the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Criminal penalties, administrative penalties and civil liability for units and individuals that cause extraordinarily serious safety accidents shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations and rules.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　特大安全事故的具体标准，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 3** The specific standards for extraordinarily serious safety accidents shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民政府及政府有关部门应当依照有关法律、法规和规章的规定，采取行政措施，对本地区实施安全监督管理，保障本地区人民群众生命、财产安全，对本地区或者职责范围内防范特大安全事故的发生、特大安全事故发生后的迅速和妥善处理负责。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations and rules, take administrative measures to implement safety supervision and management within their respective regions, ensure the safety of people's lives and property within their respective regions, and be responsible for preventing the occurrence of extraordinarily serious safety accidents within their respective regions or within the scope of their duties, and for the prompt and proper handling after the occurrence of such accidents.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民政府应当每个季度至少召开一次防范特大安全事故工作会议，由政府主要领导人或者政府主要领导人委托政府分管领导人召集有关部门正职负责人参加，分析、布置、督促、检查本地区防范特大安全事故的工作。会议应当作出决定并形成纪要，会议确定的各项防范措施必须严格实施。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at all levels shall hold at least one working meeting on the prevention of extraordinarily serious safety accidents each quarter, which shall be convened by the principal leading persons of the government or, upon entrustment by the principal leading persons, by the leading persons in charge of the relevant work, and attended by the principal responsible persons of relevant departments, to analyze, arrange, supervise and inspect the work of preventing extraordinarily serious safety accidents within their respective regions. Decisions shall be made at the meeting and minutes shall be prepared, and the various preventive measures determined at the meeting must be strictly implemented.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　市(地、州)、县(市、区)人民政府应当组织有关部门按照职责分工对本地区容易发生特大安全事故的单位、设施和场所安全事故的防范明确责任、采取措施，并组织有关部门对上述单位、设施和场所进行严格检查。

**Article 6** People's governments of cities (prefectures, leagues), counties (cities, districts) shall organize relevant departments to, according to the division of duties, clarify responsibilities and take measures for the prevention of safety accidents in units, facilities and places within their respective regions that are prone to extraordinarily serious safety accidents, and shall organize relevant departments to conduct strict inspections of the said units, facilities and places.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　市(地、州)、县(市、区)人民政府必须制定本地区特大安全事故应急处理预案。本地区特大安全事故应急处理预案经政府主要领导人签署后，报上一级人民政府备案。

**Article 7** People's governments of cities (prefectures, leagues), counties (cities, districts) must formulate emergency response plans for extraordinarily serious safety accidents within their respective regions. Such emergency response plans shall be signed by the principal leading persons of the government and then submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for recordation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　市(地、州)、县(市、区)人民政府应当组织有关部门对[本规定第二条](#art-2)所列各类特大安全事故的隐患进行查处；发现特大安全事故隐患的，责令立即排除；特大安全事故隐患排除前或者排除过程中，无法保证安全的，责令暂时停产、停业或者停止使用。法律、行政法规对查处机关另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 8** People's governments of cities (prefectures, leagues), counties (cities, districts) shall organize relevant departments to investigate and deal with the potential hazards of all types of extraordinarily serious safety accidents listed in Article 2 of these Provisions; if any potential hazard of an extraordinarily serious safety accident is discovered, it shall be ordered to be eliminated immediately; if safety cannot be ensured before or during the elimination of such potential hazard, a temporary suspension of production, business operation or use shall be ordered. Where laws or administrative regulations have other provisions on the investigating and dealing authorities, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　市(地、州)、县(市、区)人民政府及其有关部门对本地区存在的特大安全事故隐患，超出其管辖或者职责范围的，应当立即向有管辖权或者负有职责的上级人民政府或者政府有关部门报告；情况紧急的，可以立即采取包括责令暂时停产、停业在内的紧急措施，同时报告；有关上级人民政府或者政府有关部门接到报告后，应当立即组织查处。

**Article 9** Where the people's governments of cities (prefectures, leagues), counties (cities, districts) and their relevant departments find that potential hazards of extraordinarily serious safety accidents existing within their respective regions are beyond their jurisdiction or scope of duties, they shall immediately report to the people's government at the next higher level or relevant government departments that have jurisdiction or bear the responsibility; in urgent circumstances, they may immediately take emergency measures including ordering a temporary suspension of production or business operation, and simultaneously make a report; after receiving such a report, the relevant people's government at the next higher level or relevant government department shall immediately organize an investigation and handling.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中小学校对学生进行劳动技能教育以及组织学生参加公益劳动等社会实践活动，必须确保学生安全。严禁以任何形式、名义组织学生从事接触易燃、易爆、有毒、有害等危险品的劳动或者其他危险性劳动。严禁将学校场地出租作为从事易燃、易爆、有毒、有害等危险品的生产、经营场所。  
中小学校违反前款规定的，按照学校隶属关系，对县(市、区)、乡(镇)人民政府主要领导人和县(市、区)人民政府教育行政部门正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予记过、降级直至撤职的行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
中小学校违反本条第一款规定的，对校长给予撤职的行政处分，对直接组织者给予开除公职的行政处分；构成非法制造爆炸物罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** Primary and secondary schools must ensure the safety of students when providing labor skill education or organizing students to participate in social practice activities such as public welfare labor. It is strictly prohibited to organize students, in any form or under any pretext, to engage in labor involving contact with flammable, explosive, toxic, hazardous or other dangerous goods, or any other dangerous labor. It is strictly prohibited to lease school premises as places for the production or operation of flammable, explosive, toxic, hazardous or other dangerous goods.  
Where a primary or secondary school violates the preceding paragraph, depending on the school's affiliation, the principal leading persons of the people's government of the county (city, district) or township (town) and the principal responsible person of the education administrative department of the people's government of the county (city, district) shall be given administrative sanctions ranging from a demerit, demotion to dismissal, depending on the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a primary or secondary school violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article, the principal of the school shall be given the administrative sanction of dismissal, and the person directly organizing the activity shall be given the administrative sanction of removal from public employment; if the act constitutes the crime of illegally manufacturing explosives or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依法对涉及安全生产事项负责行政审批(包括批准、核准、许可、注册、认证、颁发证照、竣工验收等，下同)的政府部门或者机构，必须严格依照法律、法规和规章规定的安全条件和程序进行审查；不符合法律、法规和规章规定的安全条件的，不得批准；不符合法律、法规和规章规定的安全条件，弄虚作假，骗取批准或者勾结串通行政审批工作人员取得批准的，负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构除必须立即撤销原批准外，应当对弄虚作假骗取批准或者勾结串通行政审批工作人员的当事人依法给予行政处罚；构成行贿罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构违反前款规定，对不符合法律、法规和规章规定的安全条件予以批准的，对部门或者机构的正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予降级、撤职直至开除公职的行政处分；与当事人勾结串通的，应当开除公职；构成受贿罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** Government departments or agencies responsible for administrative review and approval (including approval, verification, licensing, registration, certification, issuance of permits, completion acceptance, etc., hereinafter the same) in matters involving production safety must conduct examinations strictly in accordance with the safety conditions and procedures prescribed by laws, regulations and rules; where the safety conditions prescribed by laws, regulations and rules are not met, approval shall not be granted; where the safety conditions prescribed by laws, regulations and rules are not met but, by resorting to fraud, approval is obtained through deception or through collusion with the administrative review and approval staff, the government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval must immediately revoke the original approval and shall, in accordance with law, impose administrative penalties on the party that resorted to fraud to obtain approval or colluded with the administrative review and approval staff; if the act constitutes the crime of bribery or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph by granting approval for matters that do not meet the safety conditions prescribed by laws, regulations and rules, the principal responsible person of the department or agency shall be given administrative sanctions ranging from demotion, dismissal to removal from public employment, depending on the severity of the circumstances; where such person colludes with a party, he or she shall be removed from public employment; if the act constitutes the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对依照[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一款的规定取得批准的单位和个人，负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构必须对其实施严格监督检查；发现其不再具备安全条件的，必须立即撤销原批准。  
负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构违反前款规定，不对取得批准的单位和个人实施严格监督检查，或者发现其不再具备安全条件而不立即撤销原批准的，对部门或者机构的正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成受贿罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 12** With respect to units and individuals that have obtained approval in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Provisions, the government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval must conduct rigorous supervision and inspection over them; if it is discovered that they no longer meet the safety conditions, the original approval must be immediately revoked.  
Where a government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph by failing to conduct rigorous supervision and inspection over the approved units and individuals, or failing to immediately revoke the original approval upon discovering that they no longer meet the safety conditions, the principal responsible person of the department or agency shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or dismissal, depending on the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对未依法取得批准，擅自从事有关活动的，负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构发现或者接到举报后，应当立即予以查封、取缔，并依法给予行政处罚；属于经营单位的，由工商行政管理部门依法相应吊销营业执照。  
负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构违反前款规定，对发现或者举报的未依法取得批准而擅自从事有关活动的，不予查封、取缔、不依法给予行政处罚，工商行政管理部门不予吊销营业执照的，对部门或者机构的正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成受贿罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where any unit or individual engages in relevant activities without obtaining approval according to law, the government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval shall, upon discovery or receipt of a report, immediately seal up and ban the activity, and impose administrative penalties according to law; if it is a business unit, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall accordingly revoke its business license according to law.  
Where a government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph by failing to seal up and ban activities discovered or reported as being engaged in without obtaining approval according to law, failing to impose administrative penalties according to law, or the administrative department for industry and commerce fails to revoke the business license, the principal responsible person of the department or agency shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or dismissal, depending on the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　市(地、州)、县(市、区)人民政府依照本规定应当履行职责而未履行，或者未按照规定的职责和程序履行，本地区发生特大安全事故的，对政府主要领导人，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
负责行政审批的政府部门或者机构、负责安全监督管理的政府有关部门，未依照本规定履行职责，发生特大安全事故的，对部门或者机构的正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予撤职或者开除公职的行政处分；构成玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** Where the people's government of a city (prefecture, league) or county (city, district) fails to perform the duties it is required to perform under these Provisions, or fails to perform them in accordance with the prescribed duties and procedures, and an extraordinarily serious safety accident occurs within its region, the principal leading person of the government shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or dismissal, depending on the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes the crime of neglect of duty, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a government department or agency responsible for administrative review and approval, or a relevant government department responsible for safety supervision and management fails to perform its duties under these Provisions, and an extraordinarily serious safety accident occurs, the principal responsible person of the department or agency shall be given administrative sanctions of dismissal or removal from public employment, depending on the severity of the circumstances; if the act constitutes the crime of neglect of duty or any other crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发生特大安全事故，社会影响特别恶劣或者性质特别严重的，由国务院对负有领导责任的省长、自治区主席、直辖市市长和国务院有关部门正职负责人给予行政处分。

**Article 15** Where an extraordinarily serious safety accident occurs and its social impact is particularly adverse or its nature is particularly serious, the State Council shall impose administrative sanctions on the governors of provinces, chairpersons of autonomous regions, mayors of municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the principal responsible persons of relevant departments of the State Council who bear leading liability.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　特大安全事故发生后，有关县(市、区)、市(地、州)和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府及政府有关部门应当按照国家规定的程序和时限立即上报，不得隐瞒不报、谎报或者拖延报告，并应当配合、协助事故调查，不得以任何方式阻碍、干涉事故调查。  
特大安全事故发生后，有关地方人民政府及政府有关部门违反前款规定的，对政府主要领导人和政府部门正职负责人给予降级的行政处分。

**Article 16** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily serious safety accident, the relevant people's governments of counties (cities, districts), cities (prefectures, leagues), provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and relevant government departments, shall immediately report the accident to higher authorities in accordance with the procedures and time limits prescribed by the State, and shall not conceal, make false reports, or delay the report, and shall cooperate with and assist in the accident investigation, and shall not obstruct or interfere with the accident investigation in any way.  
After the occurrence of an extraordinarily serious safety accident, where the relevant local people's government and relevant government departments violate the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the principal leading persons of the government and the principal responsible persons of the government departments shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　特大安全事故发生后，有关地方人民政府应当迅速组织救助，有关部门应当服从指挥、调度，参加或者配合救助，将事故损失降到最低限度。

**Article 17** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily serious safety accident, the relevant local people's government shall promptly organize rescue operations, and the relevant departments shall obey commands and dispatch, participate in or cooperate with rescue operations, so as to minimize the losses from the accident.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　特大安全事故发生后，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照国家有关规定迅速、如实发布事故消息。

**Article 18** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily serious safety accident, the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, promptly and truthfully release information about the accident.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　特大安全事故发生后，按照国家有关规定组织调查组对事故进行调查。事故调查工作应当自事故发生之日起60日内完成，并由调查组提出调查报告；遇有特殊情况的，经调查组提出并报国家安全生产监督管理机构批准后，可以适当延长时间。调查报告应当包括依照本规定对有关责任人员追究行政责任或者其他法律责任的意见。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当自调查报告提交之日起30日内，对有关责任人员作出处理决定；必要时，国务院可以对特大安全事故的有关责任人员作出处理决定。

**Article 19** After the occurrence of an extraordinarily serious safety accident, an investigation team shall be organized according to the relevant provisions of the State to investigate the accident. The accident investigation shall be completed within 60 days from the date of the accident, and the investigation team shall submit an investigation report; in special circumstances, upon the proposal of the investigation team and approval by the State administration of work safety supervision, the time limit may be appropriately extended. The investigation report shall include opinions on the investigation of administrative liability or other legal liability of the relevant responsible persons in accordance with these Provisions.  
The people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of submission of the investigation report, make a decision on handling the relevant responsible persons; when necessary, the State Council may make a decision on handling the relevant responsible persons in relation to the extraordinarily serious safety accident.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方人民政府或者政府部门阻挠、干涉对特大安全事故有关责任人员追究行政责任的，对该地方人民政府主要领导人或者政府部门正职负责人，根据情节轻重，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 20** Where a local people's government or a government department obstructs or interferes with the investigation of administrative liability of the persons responsible for an extraordinarily serious safety accident, the principal leading person of the local people's government or the principal responsible person of the government department shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion or dismissal, depending on the severity of the circumstances.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　任何单位和个人均有权向有关地方人民政府或者政府部门报告特大安全事故隐患，有权向上级人民政府或者政府部门举报地方人民政府或者政府部门不履行安全监督管理职责或者不按照规定履行职责的情况。接到报告或者举报的有关人民政府或者政府部门，应当立即组织对事故隐患进行查处，或者对举报的不履行、不按照规定履行安全监督管理职责的情况进行调查处理。

**Article 21** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report potential hazards of extraordinarily serious safety accidents to the relevant local people's government or government department, and shall have the right to report to the people's government at the next higher level or a government department any failure of a local people's government or government department to perform its safety supervision and management duties or to perform its duties in accordance with the relevant provisions. The relevant people's government or government department that receives such a report shall immediately organize an investigation and handling of the potential hazard, or investigate and handle the reported failure to perform or to perform in accordance with the provisions of the safety supervision and management duties.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　监察机关依照行政监察法的规定，对地方各级人民政府和政府部门及其工作人员履行安全监督管理职责实施监察。

**Article 22** The supervisory organs shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Supervision Law, exercise supervision over the performance of safety supervision and management duties by local people's governments at all levels, government departments and their staff.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对特大安全事故以外的其他安全事故的防范、发生追究行政责任的办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参照本规定制定。

**Article 23** The measures for investigating administrative liability for the prevention and occurrence of safety accidents other than extraordinarily serious safety accidents shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 24** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
