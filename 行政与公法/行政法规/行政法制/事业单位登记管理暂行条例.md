# 事业单位登记管理暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2004-06-27

> (1998年10月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第252号发布　根据2004年6月27日《国务院关于修改〈事业单位登记管理暂行条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范事业单位登记管理，保障事业单位的合法权益，发挥事业单位在社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设中的作用，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称事业单位，是指国家为了社会公益目的，由国家机关举办或者其他组织利用国有资产举办的，从事教育、科技、文化、卫生等活动的社会服务组织。 事业单位依法举办的营利性经营组织，必须实行独立核算，依照国家有关公司、企业等经营组织的法律、法规登记管理。 第三条　事业单位经县级以上各级人民政府及其有关主管部门(以下统称审批机关)批准成立后，应当依照本条例的规定登记或者备案。 事业单位应当具备法人条件。 第四条　事业单位应当遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策。 第五条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关所属的事业单位登记管理机构(以下简称登记管理机关)负责实施事业单位的登记管理工作。县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当加强对登记管理机关的事业单位登记管理工作的监督检查。 事业单位实行分级登记管理。分级登记管理的具体办法由国务院机构编制管理机关规定。 法律、行政法规对事业单位的监督管理另有规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。 第二章　登记 第六条　申请事业单位法人登记，应当具备下列条件： (一)经审批机关批准设立； (二)有自己的名称、组织机构和场所；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范事业单位登记管理，保障事业单位的合法权益，发挥事业单位在社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设中的作用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating the registration and administration of public institutions, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of public institutions, and giving play to the role of public institutions in the socialist material and spiritual civilization construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称事业单位，是指国家为了社会公益目的，由国家机关举办或者其他组织利用国有资产举办的，从事教育、科技、文化、卫生等活动的社会服务组织。  
事业单位依法举办的营利性经营组织，必须实行独立核算，依照国家有关公司、企业等经营组织的法律、法规登记管理。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, 'public institutions' refer to social service organizations engaged in activities such as education, science and technology, culture, and health, which are established by state organs or by other organizations using state-owned assets for the purpose of social welfare.  
A for-profit business organization established by a public institution according to law must implement independent accounting and shall be registered and administered in accordance with the laws and regulations of the State concerning business organizations such as companies and enterprises.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　事业单位经县级以上各级人民政府及其有关主管部门(以下统称审批机关)批准成立后，应当依照本条例的规定登记或者备案。  
事业单位应当具备法人条件。

**Article 3** After a public institution is established upon approval by the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant competent departments (hereinafter collectively referred to as the approving authorities), it shall register or file a record in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
A public institution shall meet the conditions for being a legal person.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　事业单位应当遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策。

**Article 4** A public institution shall abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, and state policies.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关所属的事业单位登记管理机构(以下简称登记管理机关)负责实施事业单位的登记管理工作。县级以上各级人民政府机构编制管理机关应当加强对登记管理机关的事业单位登记管理工作的监督检查。  
事业单位实行分级登记管理。分级登记管理的具体办法由国务院机构编制管理机关规定。  
法律、行政法规对事业单位的监督管理另有规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 5** The public institution registration administration organ (hereinafter referred to as the registration administration organ) under the establishment management organ of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for implementing the registration administration of public institutions. The establishment management organs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the registration administration work of the registration administration organs regarding public institutions.  
Public institutions shall be subject to graded registration administration. Specific measures for graded registration administration shall be prescribed by the establishment management organ of the State Council.  
Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the supervision and administration of public institutions, the provisions of such laws and administrative regulations shall prevail.

### 第二章　登记

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请事业单位法人登记，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)经审批机关批准设立；  
(二)有自己的名称、组织机构和场所；  
(三)有与其业务活动相适应的从业人员；  
(四)有与其业务活动相适应的经费来源；  
(五)能够独立承担民事责任。

**Article 6** To apply for legal person registration of a public institution, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) The establishment has been approved by the approving authority;  
(2) It has its own name, organizational structure, and premises;  
(3) It has employees commensurate with its business activities;  
(4) It has funding sources commensurate with its business activities;  
(5) It is able to independently bear civil liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请事业单位法人登记，应当向登记管理机关提交下列文件：  
(一)登记申请书；  
(二)审批机关的批准文件；  
(三)场所使用权证明；  
(四)经费来源证明；  
(五)其他有关证明文件。

**Article 7** To apply for legal person registration of a public institution, the following documents shall be submitted to the registration administration organ:  
(1) A registration application;  
(2) The approval document of the approving authority;  
(3) A certificate of the right to use the premises;  
(4) A certificate of funding sources;  
(5) Other relevant certifying documents.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　登记管理机关应当自收到登记申请书之日起30日内依照本条例的规定进行审查，作出准予登记或者不予登记的决定。准予登记的，发给《事业单位法人证书》；不予登记的，应当说明理由。  
事业单位法人登记事项包括：名称、住所、宗旨和业务范围、法定代表人、经费来源(开办资金)等情况。

**Article 8** The registration administration organ shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the registration application, conduct examination in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and make a decision on whether to grant registration. If registration is granted, a 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution' shall be issued; if registration is not granted, the reasons shall be stated.  
The matters for legal person registration of a public institution include: name, domicile, purpose and business scope, legal representative, funding sources (start-up capital), and other such information.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经登记的事业单位，凭《事业单位法人证书》刻制印章，申请开立银行账户。事业单位应当将印章式样报登记管理机关备案。

**Article 9** A registered public institution shall, on the basis of its 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution', make seals and apply to open a bank account. The public institution shall report the seal impressions to the registration administration organ for record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　事业单位的登记事项需要变更的，应当向登记管理机关办理变更登记。

**Article 10** If the registration matters of a public institution need to be changed, it shall undergo change registration with the registration administration organ.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　法律规定具备法人条件、自批准设立之日起即取得法人资格的事业单位，或者法律、其他行政法规规定具备法人条件、经有关主管部门依法审核或者登记，已经取得相应的执业许可证书的事业单位，不再办理事业单位法人登记，由有关主管部门按照分级登记管理的规定向登记管理机关备案。  
县级以上各级人民政府设立的直属事业单位直接向登记管理机关备案。

**Article 11** A public institution that meets the conditions for being a legal person as prescribed by law and obtains the status of a legal person from the date of approval of its establishment, or a public institution that meets the conditions for being a legal person as prescribed by law or other administrative regulations and has obtained the corresponding practice license certificate after examination or registration by the relevant competent department according to law, shall not undergo legal person registration of a public institution; instead, the relevant competent department shall file a record with the registration administration organ in accordance with the provisions on graded registration administration.  
A public institution directly under the people's government at or above the county level shall directly file a record with the registration administration organ.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　事业单位备案的事项，除[本条例第八条](#art-8)第二款所列事项外，还应当包括执业许可证明文件或者设立批准文件。  
对备案的事业单位，登记管理机关应当自收到备案文件之日起30日内发给《事业单位法人证书》。

**Article 12** For the matters to be filed by a public institution, in addition to the matters listed in paragraph 2 of Article 8 of these Regulations, the practice license certificate document or the establishment approval document shall also be included.  
For a public institution that files a record, the registration administration organ shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the filing documents, issue a 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution'.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　事业单位被撤销、解散的，应当向登记管理机关办理注销登记或者注销备案。  
事业单位办理注销登记前，应当在审批机关指导下成立清算组织，完成清算工作。  
事业单位应当自清算结束之日起15日内，向登记管理机关办理注销登记。事业单位办理注销登记，应当提交撤销或者解散该事业单位的文件和清算报告；登记管理机关收缴《事业单位法人证书》和印章。

**Article 13** If a public institution is revoked or dissolved, it shall undergo cancellation registration or cancellation of record with the registration administration organ.  
Before undergoing cancellation registration, the public institution shall establish a liquidation organization under the guidance of the approving authority and complete the liquidation work.  
The public institution shall, within 15 days from the date of completion of liquidation, undergo cancellation registration with the registration administration organ. To undergo cancellation registration, the public institution shall submit the document of revocation or dissolution of the public institution and the liquidation report; the registration administration organ shall take back the 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution' and seals.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　事业单位的登记、备案或者变更名称、住所以及注销登记或者注销备案，由登记管理机关予以公告。

**Article 14** The registration, record, change of name or domicile, cancellation registration or cancellation of record of a public institution shall be announced by the registration administration organ.

### 第三章　监督管理

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　事业单位开展活动，按照国家有关规定取得的合法收入，必须用于符合其宗旨和业务范围的活动。  
事业单位接受捐赠、资助，必须符合事业单位的宗旨和业务范围，必须根据与捐赠人、资助人约定的期限、方式和合法用途使用。

**Article 15** The legitimate income obtained by a public institution in carrying out activities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State must be used for activities consistent with its purpose and business scope.  
The acceptance of donations and grants by a public institution must be consistent with the purpose and business scope of the public institution, and must be used in accordance with the term, method, and lawful purpose agreed with the donor or grantor.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　事业单位必须执行国家有关财务、价格等管理制度，接受财税、审计部门的监督。

**Article 16** A public institution must implement the state's management systems concerning finance, pricing, etc., and accept supervision from the finance, tax, and audit departments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　事业单位应当于每年3月31日前分别向登记管理机关和审批机关报送上一年度执行本条例情况的报告。

**Article 17** A public institution shall, before March 31 each year, submit to the registration administration organ and the approving authority respectively a report on the implementation of these Regulations in the preceding year.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　事业单位未按照本条例规定办理登记的，由登记管理机关责令限期补办登记手续；逾期不补办的，由登记管理机关建议对该事业单位的负责人和其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 18** If a public institution fails to undergo registration in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the registration administration organ shall order it to go through the registration formalities within a time limit; if it fails to do so within the time limit, the registration administration organ shall recommend that the person in charge of the public institution and other directly responsible persons be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　事业单位有下列情形之一的，由登记管理机关给予警告，责令限期改正；情节严重的，经审批机关同意，予以撤销登记，收缴《事业单位法人证书》和印章：  
(一)不按照本条例的规定办理变更登记、注销登记的；  
(二)涂改、出租、出借《事业单位法人证书》或者出租、出借印章的；  
(三)违反规定接受、使用捐赠、资助的。  
事业单位违反法律、其他法规的，由有关机关依法处理。

**Article 19** If a public institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the registration administration organ shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, upon the consent of the approving authority, its registration shall be revoked, and its 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution' and seals shall be taken back:  
(1) It fails to undergo change registration or cancellation registration in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) It alters, leases, or lends out the 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution', or leases or lends out its seals;  
(3) It accepts or uses donations or grants in violation of regulations.  
If a public institution violates laws or other regulations, it shall be dealt with by the relevant organ according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　登记管理机关的工作人员在事业单位登记管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 20** If a staff member of the registration administration organ, in the course of registration administration of public institutions, abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　《事业单位法人证书》的式样由国务院机构编制管理机关制定。

**Article 21** The form of the 'Legal Person Certificate of Public Institution' shall be prescribed by the establishment management organ of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例实行前已经成立的事业单位，应当自本条例实行之日起1年内依照本条例有关规定办理登记或者备案手续。

**Article 22** A public institution that has been established before these Regulations come into force shall, within one year from the date these Regulations come into force, undergo registration or record formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
