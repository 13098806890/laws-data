# 行政区域界线管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2002-05-13  
**生效日期**：2002-07-01

> (2002年5月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第353号公布　自2002年7月1日起施行) 第一条　为了巩固行政区域界线勘定成果，加强行政区域界线管理，维护行政区域界线附近地区稳定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称行政区域界线，是指国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准的行政区域毗邻的各有关人民政府行使行政区域管辖权的分界线。 地方各级人民政府必须严格执行行政区域界线批准文件和行政区域界线协议书的各项规定，维护行政区域界线的严肃性、稳定性。任何组织或者个人不得擅自变更行政区域界线。 第三条　国务院民政部门负责全国行政区域界线管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域界线管理工作。 第四条　行政区域界线勘定后，应当以通告和行政区域界线详图予以公布。 省、自治区、直辖市之间的行政区域界线由国务院民政部门公布，由毗邻的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府共同管理。省、自治区、直辖市范围内的行政区域界线由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公布，由毗邻的自治州、县(自治县)、市、市辖区人民政府共同管理。 第五条　行政区域界线的实地位置，以界桩以及作为行政区域界线标志的河流、沟渠、道路等线状地物和行政区域界线协议书中明确规定作为指示行政区域界线走向的其他标志物标定。 第六条　任何组织或者个人不得擅自移动或者损坏界桩。非法移动界桩的，其行为无效。 行政区域界线毗邻的各有关人民政府应当按照行政区域界线协议书的规定，对界桩进行分工管理。对损坏的界桩，由分工管理该界桩的一方在毗邻方在场的情况下修复。 因建设、开发等原因需要移动或者增设界桩的，行政区域界线毗邻的各有关人民政府应当协商一致，共同测绘，增补档案资料，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。 第七条　行政区域界线毗邻的任何一方不得擅自改变作为行政区域界线标志的河流、沟渠、道路等线状地物；因自然原因或者其他原因改变的，应当保持行政区域界线协议书划定的界线位置不变，行政区域界线协议书中另有约定的除外。 第八条　行政区域界线协议书中明确规定作为指示行政区域界线走向的其他标志物，应当维持原貌。因自然原因或者其他原因使标志物发生变化的，有关县级以上人民政府民政部门应当组织修测，确定新的标志物，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。 第九条　依照《国务院关于行政区划管理的规定》经批准变更行政区域界线的，毗邻的各有关人民政府应当按照勘界测绘技术规范进行测绘，埋设界桩，签订协议书，并将协议书报批准变更该行政区域界线的机关备案。 第十条　生产、建设用地需要横跨行政区域界线的，应当事先征得毗邻的各有关人民政府同意，分别办理审批手续，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了巩固行政区域界线勘定成果，加强行政区域界线管理，维护行政区域界线附近地区稳定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of consolidating the achievements in the delimitation of administrative boundary lines, strengthening the management of administrative boundary lines, and maintaining the stability of areas adjacent to administrative boundary lines.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称行政区域界线，是指国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准的行政区域毗邻的各有关人民政府行使行政区域管辖权的分界线。  
地方各级人民政府必须严格执行行政区域界线批准文件和行政区域界线协议书的各项规定，维护行政区域界线的严肃性、稳定性。任何组织或者个人不得擅自变更行政区域界线。

**Article 2** The term "administrative boundary lines" as used in these Regulations refers to the dividing lines approved by the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, within which the relevant people's governments adjacent to each other exercise their jurisdiction over administrative regions.  
Local people's governments at all levels must strictly implement all provisions of the documents approving administrative boundary lines and the agreements on administrative boundary lines, and safeguard the seriousness and stability of administrative boundary lines. No organization or individual may alter administrative boundary lines without authorization.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院民政部门负责全国行政区域界线管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域界线管理工作。

**Article 3** The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of administrative boundary lines nationwide. The civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of administrative boundary lines within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　行政区域界线勘定后，应当以通告和行政区域界线详图予以公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市之间的行政区域界线由国务院民政部门公布，由毗邻的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府共同管理。省、自治区、直辖市范围内的行政区域界线由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公布，由毗邻的自治州、县(自治县)、市、市辖区人民政府共同管理。

**Article 4** After the delimitation of administrative boundary lines, they shall be published by means of a notice and a detailed map of the administrative boundary lines.  
The administrative boundary lines between provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be published by the civil affairs department of the State Council and shall be jointly managed by the people's governments of the adjacent provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The administrative boundary lines within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be published by the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and shall be jointly managed by the people's governments of the adjacent autonomous prefectures, counties (autonomous counties), cities, and municipal districts.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行政区域界线的实地位置，以界桩以及作为行政区域界线标志的河流、沟渠、道路等线状地物和行政区域界线协议书中明确规定作为指示行政区域界线走向的其他标志物标定。

**Article 5** The on-site location of administrative boundary lines shall be demarcated by boundary markers and linear features such as rivers, ditches, roads that serve as symbols of administrative boundary lines, and other markers clearly stipulated in the agreements on administrative boundary lines as indicators of the direction of administrative boundary lines.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何组织或者个人不得擅自移动或者损坏界桩。非法移动界桩的，其行为无效。  
行政区域界线毗邻的各有关人民政府应当按照行政区域界线协议书的规定，对界桩进行分工管理。对损坏的界桩，由分工管理该界桩的一方在毗邻方在场的情况下修复。  
因建设、开发等原因需要移动或者增设界桩的，行政区域界线毗邻的各有关人民政府应当协商一致，共同测绘，增补档案资料，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。

**Article 6** No organization or individual may move or damage boundary markers without authorization. Any illegal moving of boundary markers shall be void.  
The relevant people's governments adjacent to administrative boundary lines shall, in accordance with the provisions of the agreements on administrative boundary lines, divide the management responsibilities for boundary markers. A damaged boundary marker shall be repaired by the party responsible for its management in the presence of the adjacent party.  
Where it is necessary to move or add boundary markers due to construction, development, or other reasons, the relevant people's governments adjacent to the administrative boundary lines shall reach consensus through consultation, conduct joint surveying and mapping, supplement the archival materials, and report to the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines for the record.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　行政区域界线毗邻的任何一方不得擅自改变作为行政区域界线标志的河流、沟渠、道路等线状地物；因自然原因或者其他原因改变的，应当保持行政区域界线协议书划定的界线位置不变，行政区域界线协议书中另有约定的除外。

**Article 7** Neither party adjacent to an administrative boundary line may alter the linear features such as rivers, ditches, and roads that serve as symbols of the administrative boundary line without authorization; if such features are changed due to natural or other reasons, the location of the boundary line as delineated in the agreement on the administrative boundary line shall remain unchanged, unless otherwise agreed in the agreement on the administrative boundary line.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行政区域界线协议书中明确规定作为指示行政区域界线走向的其他标志物，应当维持原貌。因自然原因或者其他原因使标志物发生变化的，有关县级以上人民政府民政部门应当组织修测，确定新的标志物，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。

**Article 8** Other markers clearly stipulated in the agreements on administrative boundary lines as indicators of the direction of the boundary lines shall be maintained in their original condition. If the markers change due to natural or other reasons, the relevant civil affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize re-surveying, determine new markers, and report to the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines for the record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　依照《国务院关于行政区划管理的规定》经批准变更行政区域界线的，毗邻的各有关人民政府应当按照勘界测绘技术规范进行测绘，埋设界桩，签订协议书，并将协议书报批准变更该行政区域界线的机关备案。

**Article 9** Where the administrative boundary lines are changed upon approval in accordance with the Provisions of the State Council on the Administration of Administrative Divisions, all relevant adjacent people's governments shall, in accordance with the technical specifications for boundary delimitation surveying and mapping, conduct surveying and mapping, bury boundary markers, sign an agreement, and submit the agreement to the authority that approved the change of the administrative boundary lines for the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　生产、建设用地需要横跨行政区域界线的，应当事先征得毗邻的各有关人民政府同意，分别办理审批手续，并报该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。

**Article 10** Where production or construction land needs to span an administrative boundary line, consent shall be obtained in advance from all relevant adjacent people's governments, the examination and approval procedures shall be handled separately, and the matter shall be reported to the approving authority of the administrative boundary line for the record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政区域界线勘定确认属于某一行政区域但不与该行政区域相连的地域或者由一方使用管理但位于毗邻行政区域内的地域，其使用管理按照各有关人民政府签订的行政区域界线协议书有关规定或者该行政区域界线的批准机关的决定执行。

**Article 11** The use and management of an area which, as confirmed by the delimitation of administrative boundary lines, belongs to an administrative region but is not connected to that region, or an area which is used and managed by one party but is located in an adjacent administrative region, shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the agreement on administrative boundary lines signed by all relevant people's governments or the decision of the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行政区域界线毗邻的县级以上地方各级人民政府应当建立行政区域界线联合检查制度，每5年联合检查一次。遇有影响行政区域界线实地走向的自然灾害、河流改道、道路变化等特殊情况，由行政区域界线毗邻的各有关人民政府共同对行政区域界线的特定地段随时安排联合检查。联合检查的结果，由参加检查的各地方人民政府共同报送该行政区域界线的批准机关备案。

**Article 12** Local people's governments at or above the county level adjacent to administrative boundary lines shall establish a joint inspection system for administrative boundary lines, and conduct a joint inspection once every five years. In the event of special circumstances such as natural disasters, river course changes, road changes, etc., that affect the on-site direction of the administrative boundary lines, all relevant people's governments adjacent to the administrative boundary lines shall arrange a joint inspection of the specific sections of the administrative boundary lines at any time. The results of the joint inspection shall be jointly submitted by all participating local people's governments to the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines for the record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　勘定行政区域界线以及行政区域界线管理中形成的协议书、工作图、界线标志记录、备案材料、批准文件以及其他与勘界记录有关的材料，应当按照有关档案管理的法律、行政法规的规定立卷归档，妥善保管。

**Article 13** The agreements, working maps, records of boundary markers, filing materials, approval documents, and other materials related to boundary delimitation records formed during the delimitation and management of administrative boundary lines shall be filed and properly preserved in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on archives management.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　行政区域界线详图是反映县级以上行政区域界线标准画法的国家专题地图。任何涉及行政区域界线的地图，其行政区域界线画法一律以行政区域界线详图为准绘制。  
国务院民政部门负责编制省、自治区、直辖市行政区域界线详图；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门负责编制本行政区域内的行政区域界线详图。

**Article 14** The detailed map of administrative boundary lines is a national thematic map reflecting the standard representation of administrative boundary lines at or above the county level. For any map involving administrative boundary lines, the representation of the administrative boundary lines shall be drawn in accordance with the detailed map of administrative boundary lines.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for compiling the detailed maps of administrative boundary lines for provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the civil affairs departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for compiling the detailed maps of administrative boundary lines within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　因对行政区域界线实地位置认定不一致引发的争议，由该行政区域界线的批准机关依照该行政区域界线协议书的有关规定处理。

**Article 15** Disputes arising from inconsistencies in the determination of the on-site location of administrative boundary lines shall be handled by the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines in accordance with the relevant provisions of the agreement on the administrative boundary lines.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　违反本条例的规定，有关国家机关工作人员在行政区域界线管理中有下列行为之一的，根据不同情节，依法给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分；致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不履行行政区域界线批准文件和行政区域界线协议书规定的义务，或者不执行行政区域界线的批准机关的决定的；  
(二)不依法公布批准的行政区域界线的；  
(三)擅自移动、改变行政区域界线标志，或者命令、指使他人擅自移动、改变行政区域界线标志，或者发现他人擅自移动、改变行政区域界线标志不予制止的；  
(四)毗邻方未在场时，擅自维修行政区域界线标志的。

**Article 16** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a functionary of a relevant state organ commits any of the following acts in the administration of administrative boundary lines, he or she shall, depending on the circumstances, be given an administrative sanction of recording a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office according to law; if such act causes heavy losses to public property, the State, or the interests of the people, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power and the crime of dereliction of duty:  
(1) failing to perform the obligations stipulated in the documents approving administrative boundary lines or the agreements on administrative boundary lines, or failing to implement the decisions of the approving authority of the administrative boundary lines;  
(2) failing to publish the approved administrative boundary lines according to law;  
(3) moving or altering the symbols of administrative boundary lines without authorization, or ordering or instructing others to do so, or failing to stop others from moving or altering such symbols without authorization upon discovery;  
(4) repairing the symbols of administrative boundary lines without authorization when the adjacent party is not present.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反本条例的规定，故意损毁或者擅自移动界桩或者其他行政区域界线标志物的，应当支付修复标志物的费用，并由所在地负责管理该行政区域界线标志的人民政府民政部门处1000元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，并依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 17** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person intentionally damages or moves boundary markers or other symbols of administrative boundary lines without authorization, he or she shall pay the costs of repairing the symbols, and shall be imposed a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan by the civil affairs department of the local people's government responsible for managing the symbols of the administrative boundary lines; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall also be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本条例的规定，擅自编制行政区域界线详图，或者绘制的地图的行政区域界线的画法与行政区域界线详图的画法不一致的，由有关人民政府民政部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法编制的行政区域界线详图和违法所得，并处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 18** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person compiles a detailed map of administrative boundary lines without authorization, or draws a map in which the representation of administrative boundary lines is inconsistent with that in the detailed map of administrative boundary lines, the civil affairs department of the relevant people's government shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally compiled detailed map of administrative boundary lines and the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　乡、民族乡、镇行政区域界线的管理，参照本条例的有关规定执行。

**Article 19** The administration of administrative boundary lines of townships, ethnic townships, and towns shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本条例自2002年7月1日起施行。

**Article 20** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2002.
