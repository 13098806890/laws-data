# 全国年节及纪念日放假办法

**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-11-10

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为统一全国年节及纪念日的假期，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of unifying the holidays for national festivals and commemorative days.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　全体公民放假的节日：  
（一）元旦，放假1天（1月1日）；  
（二）春节，放假4天（农历除夕、正月初一至初三）；  
（三）清明节，放假1天（农历清明当日）；  
（四）劳动节，放假2天（5月1日、2日）；  
（五）端午节，放假1天（农历端午当日）；  
（六）中秋节，放假1天（农历中秋当日）；  
（七）国庆节，放假3天（10月1日至3日）。

**Article 2** Festivals during which all citizens shall have a holiday:  
(1) New Year's Day, 1 day off (January 1);  
(2) Spring Festival, 4 days off (Lunar New Year's Eve, and the first to third days of the first lunar month);  
(3) Qingming Festival, 1 day off (the day of Qingming in the lunar calendar);  
(4) Labor Day, 2 days off (May 1 and 2);  
(5) Dragon Boat Festival, 1 day off (the day of the Dragon Boat Festival in the lunar calendar);  
(6) Mid-Autumn Festival, 1 day off (the day of the Mid-Autumn Festival in the lunar calendar);  
(7) National Day, 3 days off (October 1 to 3).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　部分公民放假的节日及纪念日：  
（一）妇女节（3月8日），妇女放假半天；  
（二）青年节（5月4日），14周岁以上的青年放假半天；  
（三）儿童节（6月1日），不满14周岁的少年儿童放假1天；  
（四）中国人民解放军建军纪念日（8月1日），现役军人放假半天。

**Article 3** Festivals and commemorative days during which part of the citizens shall have a holiday:  
(1) International Women's Day (March 8), women shall have a half-day off;  
(2) Youth Day (May 4), youth aged 14 or above shall have a half-day off;  
(3) Children's Day (June 1), children under 14 years old shall have 1 day off;  
(4) Army Day of the Chinese People's Liberation Army (August 1), active duty servicemen shall have a half-day off.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　少数民族习惯的节日，由各少数民族聚居地区的地方人民政府，按照各该民族习惯，规定放假日期。

**Article 4** Festivals of ethnic minority customs shall be prescribed by the local people's governments in areas where ethnic minorities live in concentrated communities, in accordance with the customs of the respective ethnic groups, the dates of the holidays.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　二七纪念日、五卅纪念日、七七抗战纪念日、九三抗战胜利纪念日、九一八纪念日、教师节、护士节、记者节、植树节等其他节日、纪念日，均不放假。

**Article 5** The February 7 Memorial Day, May 30 Memorial Day, July 7 War of Resistance Memorial Day, September 3 Victory Day of the War of Resistance, September 18 Memorial Day, Teachers' Day, Nurses' Day, Journalists' Day, Arbor Day, and other festivals and commemorative days shall not be given holidays.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全体公民放假的假日，如果适逢周六、周日，应当在工作日补假。部分公民放假的假日，如果适逢周六、周日，则不补假。

**Article 6** If a holiday during which all citizens shall have a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, compensatory leave shall be granted on a working day. If a holiday during which part of the citizens shall have a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, no compensatory leave shall be granted.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全体公民放假的假日，可合理安排统一放假调休，结合落实带薪年休假等制度，实际形成较长假期。除个别特殊情形外，法定节假日假期前后连续工作一般不超过6天。

**Article 7** Holidays during which all citizens shall have a holiday may be reasonably arranged for unified days off and adjusted, in combination with the implementation of systems such as paid annual leave, to actually form longer holidays. Except for individual special circumstances, consecutive workdays before and after statutory holidays shall generally not exceed six days.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本办法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 8** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
