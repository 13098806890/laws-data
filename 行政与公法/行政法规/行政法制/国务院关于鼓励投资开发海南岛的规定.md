# 国务院关于鼓励投资开发海南岛的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1988-05-04  
**生效日期**：1988-05-04

> (1988年5月4日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了吸收境内外投资，加快海南岛的开发建设，特制定本规定。 第二条　国家对海南经济特区实行更加灵活开放的经济政策，授予海南省人民政府更大的自主权。 第三条　国家鼓励境内外的企业、其他经济组织或者个人(以下简称投资者)投资开发海南岛，兴办各项经济和社会事业。 第四条　国家依法保护投资者的合法权益，对投资者的资产不实行国有化和征收，在特殊情况下，为社会公共利益的需要，对投资者的资产可以依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。 投资者必须遵守中国的法律、法规。 第五条　投资者可以下列方式在海南岛投资经营： (一)投资举办中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业、外资企业(以下简称外商投资企业)以及法律允许的其他类型的企业。各类企业的经营期限，由投资各方在合同中约定或者经有关主管部门批准； (二)购买股票、债券等有价证券； (三)购买、参股经营或者承包、租赁经营企业； (四)采用其他国际上通行的投资方式投资经营，开展经济技术合作和交流。 第六条　海南岛国家所有的土地实行有偿使用。 海南省人民政府可依法将国家所有土地的使用权有偿出让给投资者，土地使用权出让一次签约的期限根据不同行业和项目的具体情况确定，最长期限为七十年；期满后需要继续使用的，经批准期限可以延长。 投资者可以依照国家有关规定将取得的土地使用权有偿转让。 第七条　海南岛的矿藏资源依法实行有偿开采。国家规定的特定矿藏资源开采应报经国家主管部门批准，其他矿藏资源开采，由海南省人民政府批准。允许投资者以合资经营、合作经营和独资经营的方式进行勘探开采。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了吸收境内外投资，加快海南岛的开发建设，特制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of attracting domestic and foreign investment and accelerating the development and construction of Hainan Island.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对海南经济特区实行更加灵活开放的经济政策，授予海南省人民政府更大的自主权。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a more flexible and open economic policy for the Hainan Special Economic Zone and shall grant greater autonomous power to the People's Government of Hainan Province.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励境内外的企业、其他经济组织或者个人(以下简称投资者)投资开发海南岛，兴办各项经济和社会事业。

**Article 3** The State shall encourage enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals (hereinafter referred to as investors) within and outside China to invest in the development of Hainan Island and to establish various economic and social undertakings.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家依法保护投资者的合法权益，对投资者的资产不实行国有化和征收，在特殊情况下，为社会公共利益的需要，对投资者的资产可以依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。  
投资者必须遵守中国的法律、法规。

**Article 4** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of investors according to law, and shall not nationalize or expropriate the assets of investors. Under special circumstances, for the needs of social public interest, the assets of investors may be expropriated in accordance with legal procedures, and corresponding compensation shall be given.  
Investors must abide by the laws and regulations of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　投资者可以下列方式在海南岛投资经营：  
(一)投资举办中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业、外资企业(以下简称外商投资企业)以及法律允许的其他类型的企业。各类企业的经营期限，由投资各方在合同中约定或者经有关主管部门批准；  
(二)购买股票、债券等有价证券；  
(三)购买、参股经营或者承包、租赁经营企业；  
(四)采用其他国际上通行的投资方式投资经营，开展经济技术合作和交流。

**Article 5** Investors may invest and operate on Hainan Island in the following ways:  
(1) Investing in and establishing Chinese-foreign equity joint ventures, Chinese-foreign contractual joint ventures, wholly foreign-owned enterprises (hereinafter referred to as foreign-invested enterprises) and other types of enterprises permitted by law. The term of operation of each type of enterprise shall be stipulated in the contract by the investing parties or approved by the competent authorities;  
(2) Purchasing stocks, bonds and other negotiable securities;  
(3) Purchasing enterprises, or participating in their operation through equity participation, or contracting or leasing enterprises;  
(4) Adopting other internationally accepted investment methods to invest and operate, and carrying out economic and technical cooperation and exchanges.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海南岛国家所有的土地实行有偿使用。  
海南省人民政府可依法将国家所有土地的使用权有偿出让给投资者，土地使用权出让一次签约的期限根据不同行业和项目的具体情况确定，最长期限为七十年；期满后需要继续使用的，经批准期限可以延长。  
投资者可以依照国家有关规定将取得的土地使用权有偿转让。

**Article 6** The state-owned land on Hainan Island shall be used on a compensated basis.  
The People's Government of Hainan Province may, according to law, grant the right to use state-owned land to investors for a fee. The term of a one-time contract for the grant of land use right shall be determined according to the specific conditions of different industries and projects, with the maximum term being seventy years; if continued use is required after the expiration of the term, the term may be extended upon approval.  
Investors may, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, transfer the acquired land use right for a fee.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海南岛的矿藏资源依法实行有偿开采。国家规定的特定矿藏资源开采应报经国家主管部门批准，其他矿藏资源开采，由海南省人民政府批准。允许投资者以合资经营、合作经营和独资经营的方式进行勘探开采。

**Article 7** The mineral resources on Hainan Island shall be exploited on a compensated basis according to law. The exploitation of specific mineral resources as prescribed by the State shall be reported to and approved by the competent departments of the State; the exploitation of other mineral resources shall be approved by the People's Government of Hainan Province. Investors shall be permitted to conduct exploration and exploitation by means of joint ventures, cooperative ventures or wholly owned enterprises.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　投资者可以合资、合作方式在海南岛投资从事港口、码头、机场、公路、铁路、电站、煤矿、水利等基础设施建设，也可以独资经营专用设施，并可依照国家有关规定投资经营与上述设施相关联的各类企业和服务事业，实行综合经营。

**Article 8** Investors may invest, by means of joint ventures or cooperative ventures, in the construction of infrastructure such as ports, wharves, airports, highways, railways, power stations, coal mines and water conservancy projects on Hainan Island, and may also operate special facilities as wholly owned enterprises, and may, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, invest in and operate various enterprises and service undertakings connected with the aforesaid facilities, engaging in comprehensive operation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　根据经济发展的需要，经中国人民银行批准，可以在海南岛设立外资银行、中外合资银行或者其他金融机构。

**Article 9** Where necessary for economic development, foreign banks, Chinese-foreign joint venture banks or other financial institutions may be established on Hainan Island upon approval by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在海南岛投资兴办各项经济和社会事业，由海南省人民政府审查批准。但投资范围和投资总额超过国家授权海南省人民政府审批范围的，应当按照国家规定程序报批。

**Article 10** The establishment of various economic and social undertakings on Hainan Island through investment shall be subject to examination and approval by the People's Government of Hainan Province. However, if the scope of investment and the total amount of investment exceed the limits authorized by the State for the People's Government of Hainan Province to approve, the matter shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　获准举办的企业作为投资进口的建设物资、生产设备和管理设备，为生产经营进口的原材料、零配件、包装材料和其他物料以及自用的交通工具、办公用品，由海南省人民政府自行审批。

**Article 11** The import of construction materials, production equipment and management equipment as investment by enterprises approved for establishment, and the import of raw materials, spare parts, packaging materials and other materials for production and operation, as well as means of transport and office supplies for their own use, shall be subject to approval by the People's Government of Hainan Province on its own.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在海南岛举办的企业(国家银行和保险公司除外)，从事生产、经营所得和其他所得，均按15%的税率征收企业所得税，另按应纳税额附征10%的地方所得税。其中：  
(一)从事港口、码头、机场、公路、铁路、电站、煤矿、水利等基础设施开发经营的企业和从事农业开发经营的企业，经营期限在十五年以上的，从开始获利的年度起，第一年至第五年免征所得税，第六年至第十年减半征收所得税；  
(二)从事工业、交通运输业等生产性行业的企业经营期限在十年以上的，从开始获利的年度起，第一年和第二年免征所得税，第三年至第五年减半征收所得税，其中被海南省人民政府确认为先进技术企业的，第六年至第八年减半征收所得税；  
(三)从事工业、农业等生产性行业的企业，在按照规定减免企业所得税期满后，凡当年企业出口产品产值达到当年企业产品产值70%以上的，当年可以减按10%的税率缴纳企业所得税；  
(四)从事服务性行业的企业，投资总额超过五百万美元或者二千万人民币，经营期限在十年以上的，从开始获利的年度起，第一年免征所得税，第二年和第三年减半征收所得税。  
对海南岛内的企业征收的地方所得税，需要给予减征或者免征所得税优惠的，由海南省人民政府决定。

**Article 12** For enterprises (excluding state banks and insurance companies) established on Hainan Island, enterprise income tax shall be levied at a rate of 15% on income from production, operation and other sources, and local income tax at 10% of the tax payable shall be additionally levied. Among them:  
(1) For enterprises engaged in the development and operation of infrastructure such as ports, wharves, airports, highways, railways, power stations, coal mines and water conservancy projects, and enterprises engaged in agricultural development and operation, with an operation term of fifteen years or more, income tax shall be exempted from the first to the fifth profit-making year, and shall be levied at half the rate from the sixth to the tenth year;  
(2) For enterprises engaged in productive industries such as industry and transportation, with an operation term of ten years or more, income tax shall be exempted for the first and second profit-making years, and shall be levied at half the rate from the third to the fifth year; among them, those recognized as technologically advanced enterprises by the People's Government of Hainan Province shall have income tax levied at half the rate from the sixth to the eighth year;  
(3) For enterprises engaged in productive industries such as industry and agriculture, after the expiration of the period of reduction or exemption of enterprise income tax as stipulated, if the value of export products of the enterprise in the current year reaches 70% or more of the total value of products of the enterprise in that year, the enterprise may pay enterprise income tax at a reduced rate of 10% for that year;  
(4) For enterprises engaged in service industries, with a total investment exceeding US$5 million or RMB 20 million, and an operation term of ten years or more, income tax shall be exempted for the first profit-making year, and shall be levied at half the rate for the second and third years.  
Whether any reduction or exemption of local income tax levied on enterprises on Hainan Island shall be granted shall be decided by the People's Government of Hainan Province.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　境外投资者在海南岛内没有设立机构而有来源于海南岛的股息、利息、租金、特许权使用费和其他所得，除依法免征所得税者外，均按10%的税率征收所得税。需要给予减征或者免征所得税优惠的，由海南省人民政府决定。

**Article 13** Where foreign investors have no establishment on Hainan Island but have income from dividends, interest, rent, royalties and other income derived from Hainan Island, income tax shall be levied at a rate of 10%, except for income exempted from income tax according to law. Whether any reduction or exemption of income tax shall be granted shall be decided by the People's Government of Hainan Province.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在海南岛举办的外商投资企业和外商持有25%以上股份的企业均享有进出口经营权，其他企业经海南省人民政府批准也可以享有进出口经营权，进口本企业生产、经营必需的货物，出口本企业的产品。

**Article 14** Foreign-invested enterprises established on Hainan Island and enterprises in which foreign investors hold 25% or more of the shares shall have the right to engage in import and export; other enterprises may also have such right upon approval by the People's Government of Hainan Province, and may import goods necessary for their production and operation and export their own products.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　海南岛内的企业进口本企业建设和生产所必需的机器设备、原材料、零配件、交通运输工具和其他物料，以及办公用品，均免征关税、产品税或增值税。  
海南岛内的企业进口供岛内市场销售的货物，减半征收关税、产品税或增值税。

**Article 15** Enterprises on Hainan Island shall be exempted from customs duties, product tax or value-added tax on the import of machinery, equipment, raw materials, spare parts, means of transport and other materials necessary for their construction and production, as well as office supplies.  
Enterprises on Hainan Island shall enjoy a 50% reduction of customs duties, product tax or value-added tax on the import of goods for sale on the island market.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励海南岛内的企业生产的产品出口。对企业生产的出口产品免征出口关税，除原油、成品油和国家另有规定的少数产品外，退还已征的产品税或增值税。

**Article 16** The State shall encourage the export of products manufactured by enterprises on Hainan Island. Export duties shall be exempted on export products manufactured by such enterprises, and, except for crude oil, refined oil and a few products otherwise prescribed by the State, the product tax or value-added tax already levied shall be refunded.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海南岛内的企业生产的产品在岛内市场销售的，除矿物汕、烟、酒和海南省人民政府规定的其他少数产品减半征收产品税或增值税外，其余免征产品税或增值税。含有进口料件的，按照第十五条的规定，免征或者补征进口料件的关税、产品税或增值税。  
企业生产的产品销往境内其他地区，除国家限制进口的产品需按国家有关规定审批外，其余产品均可自主销售，但应照章征收产品税或增值税；含有进口料件的照章补征进口料件的关税、产品税或增值税。  
海南岛内的外商投资企业的产品内销，符合国家以产顶进办法规定的，可以申请以产顶进。

**Article 17** For products manufactured by enterprises on Hainan Island and sold on the island market, except for mineral oil, tobacco, alcohol and a few other products prescribed by the People's Government of Hainan Province, which shall be subject to a 50% reduction of product tax or value-added tax, the remainder shall be exempted from product tax or value-added tax. Where imported materials or parts are used, the customs duties, product tax or value-added tax on such imported materials or parts shall be exempted or levied retroactively in accordance with the provisions of Article 15.  
For products manufactured by enterprises and sold to other regions within China, except for products whose import is restricted by the State and which require approval in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, all other products may be sold independently, but product tax or value-added tax shall be levied according to regulations; where imported materials or parts are used, the customs duties, product tax or value-added tax on such imported materials or parts shall be levied retroactively according to regulations.  
Where the products of foreign-invested enterprises on Hainan Island are sold domestically and meet the requirements of the State's regulations on substituting domestic products for imported products, an application may be made for such substitution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　海南岛内的企业出口产品和从事其他经营活动取得的外汇收入，均可保留现汇，按当地中国人民银行的规定管理。  
企业可以在海南岛或者境内其他地区的外汇调剂市场调剂外汇余缺、平衡收支。

**Article 18** Enterprises on Hainan Island may retain all foreign exchange income from export of products and from other business activities in cash, and shall manage such foreign exchange in accordance with the regulations of the local People's Bank of China.  
Enterprises may adjust foreign exchange surplus or deficit and balance their income and expenditure on the foreign exchange swap market on Hainan Island or in other regions within China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　境外投资者从在海南岛投资举办的企业获得的利润，可以从企业的外汇存款帐户自由汇往境外，免缴汇出额的所得税。  
境外投资者将从海南岛内的企业获得的利润，在境内再投资，期限不少于五年的，退还其再投资部分已缴纳所得税税款的40%；如果投资用于海南岛内的基础设施建设和农业开发企业、产品出口企业和先进技术企业，全部退还其再投资部分已缴纳的所得税税款。  
境内投资者从海南岛内的企业获得的利润，可以自由汇往境内其他地区。汇往境内其他地区的利润，从开始获利的年度起十年内不再补缴所得税。

**Article 19** Profits obtained by foreign investors from enterprises in which they have invested on Hainan Island may be freely remitted abroad from the foreign exchange deposit accounts of the enterprises, and shall be exempted from income tax on the remitted amount.  
Where foreign investors reinvest in China the profits obtained from enterprises on Hainan Island for a term of not less than five years, 40% of the income tax already paid on the reinvested portion shall be refunded; if the reinvestment is made in infrastructure construction and agricultural development enterprises, product export enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises on Hainan Island, all of the income tax already paid on the reinvested portion shall be refunded.  
Profits obtained by domestic investors from enterprises on Hainan Island may be freely remitted to other regions within China. For profits remitted to other regions within China, no additional income tax shall be levied within ten years from the first profit-making year.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　凡与我国有外交关系或者官方贸易往来的国家或地区的外国人，在海南岛洽谈投资、贸易，进行经济技术交流、探亲、旅游，停留时间不超过十五天的，可临时在海口或三亚口岸办理入境签证手续；如有正当理由需要延长在海南岛内的停留期限或者转往境内其他地区，可按有关规定申请办理签证延期或加签手续。  
在海南岛常驻的外国人、投资举办企业或者参加开发建设工作的外国人及其随行眷属，海南省人民政府有关部门可根据其申请，签发前往海南岛的多次入境签证。

**Article 20** Foreigners from countries or regions that have diplomatic relations or official trade relations with China, who come to Hainan Island for investment negotiations, trade, economic and technological exchanges, family visits, or tourism, and whose stay does not exceed fifteen days, may temporarily go through visa formalities upon entry at Haikou or Sanya port; if there are legitimate reasons requiring an extension of the stay on Hainan Island or travel to other regions within China, they may apply for visa extension or endorsement in accordance with the relevant regulations.  
For foreigners permanently residing on Hainan Island, foreigners investing in or establishing enterprises, or participating in development and construction work on Hainan Island, and their accompanying family members, the relevant departments of the People's Government of Hainan Province may, upon application, issue multiple-entry visas to Hainan Island.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　香港、澳门、台湾同胞和华侨，凡持有国务院主管部门及其授权机关签发的有效护照或其他有效证件，前往海南岛及转往境内其他地区或者出境，无需办理签证。台湾同胞可以直接在海南岛的口岸申领《台湾同胞旅行证明》。  
海南省的国内单位向境外派出经济、贸易、旅游机构，到境外举办企业，其人员出国，除国家另有规定者外，授权海南省人民政府审批。

**Article 21** Compatriots from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan and overseas Chinese who hold valid passports or other valid documents issued by the competent departments of the State Council or their authorized organs may travel to Hainan Island, transfer to other regions within China, or exit, without the need to obtain visas. Compatriots from Taiwan may directly apply for a "Travel Permit for Compatriots from Taiwan" at the ports on Hainan Island.  
Where domestic units in Hainan Province establish economic, trade or tourism institutions abroad or establish enterprises abroad, and their personnel go abroad, the People's Government of Hainan Province is authorized to approve such matters, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定未尽事项，海南省人民政府可按照国家有关经济特区的规定办理。  
本规定的实施办法，由国务院有关主管部门会同海南省人民政府制定。

**Article 22** Matters not covered by these Provisions may be handled by the People's Government of Hainan Province in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State on special economic zones.  
The implementing measures for these Provisions shall be formulated by the relevant competent departments of the State Council jointly with the People's Government of Hainan Province.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
