# 国务院关于鼓励华侨和香港澳门同胞投资的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1990-08-18  
**生效日期**：1990-08-18

> (1990年8月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第64号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为促进我国经济发展，鼓励华侨和香港澳门同胞(以下统称华侨、港澳投资者)在境内投资，制定本规定。 第二条　华侨、港澳投资者可以在境内各省、自治区、直辖市、经济特区投资。 鼓励华侨、港澳投资者依照国家有关规定从事土地开发经营。 第三条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内可以下列形式进行投资： (一)举办华侨、港澳投资者拥有全部资本的企业； (二)举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业； (三)开展补偿贸易、来料加工装配、合作生产； (四)购买企业的股票和债券； (五)购置房产； (六)依法取得土地使用权，开发经营； (七)法律、法规允许的其他投资形式。 第四条　华侨、港澳投资者可以在境内的工业、农业、服务业以及其他符合社会和经济发展方向的行业投资。华侨、港澳投资者可以从各地方人民政府有关部门公布的项目中选择投资项目，也可以自行提出投资项目意向，向拟投资地区对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关申请。 国家鼓励华侨、港澳投资者投资举办产品出口企业和先进技术企业，并给予相应的优惠待遇。 第五条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内投资举办拥有全部资本的企业、合资经营企业和合作经营企业(以下统称华侨、港澳同胞投资企业)，除适用本规定外，参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定，享受相应的外商投资企业待遇。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为促进我国经济发展，鼓励华侨和香港澳门同胞(以下统称华侨、港澳投资者)在境内投资，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to promote China's economic development and to encourage overseas Chinese and compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao (hereinafter collectively referred to as overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors) to invest within China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　华侨、港澳投资者可以在境内各省、自治区、直辖市、经济特区投资。  
鼓励华侨、港澳投资者依照国家有关规定从事土地开发经营。

**Article 2** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may invest in the various provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and special economic zones within China.  
Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors are encouraged to engage in land development and operation in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内可以下列形式进行投资：  
(一)举办华侨、港澳投资者拥有全部资本的企业；  
(二)举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业；  
(三)开展补偿贸易、来料加工装配、合作生产；  
(四)购买企业的股票和债券；  
(五)购置房产；  
(六)依法取得土地使用权，开发经营；  
(七)法律、法规允许的其他投资形式。

**Article 3** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may invest within China in the following forms:  
(1) establishing enterprises with all capital invested by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors;  
(2) establishing equity joint ventures and contractual joint ventures;  
(3) undertaking compensation trade, processing with supplied materials and assembling, and cooperative production;  
(4) purchasing stocks and bonds of enterprises;  
(5) purchasing real property;  
(6) obtaining the right to the use of land according to law and carrying out development and operation; and  
(7) other forms of investment permitted by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　华侨、港澳投资者可以在境内的工业、农业、服务业以及其他符合社会和经济发展方向的行业投资。华侨、港澳投资者可以从各地方人民政府有关部门公布的项目中选择投资项目，也可以自行提出投资项目意向，向拟投资地区对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关申请。  
国家鼓励华侨、港澳投资者投资举办产品出口企业和先进技术企业，并给予相应的优惠待遇。

**Article 4** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may invest in industry, agriculture, service industries and other sectors that are in line with the direction of social and economic development within China. Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may select investment projects from those published by the relevant departments of the local people's governments, or may propose their own investment project intentions and apply to the department of foreign economic relations and trade of the area in which they intend to invest or the examination and approval authority designated by the local people's government.  
The State encourages overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors to invest in establishing export-oriented enterprises and technologically advanced enterprises, and shall grant them corresponding preferential treatment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内投资举办拥有全部资本的企业、合资经营企业和合作经营企业(以下统称华侨、港澳同胞投资企业)，除适用本规定外，参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定，享受相应的外商投资企业待遇。  
华侨、港澳投资者在境内进行其他形式的投资，以及在境内没有设立营业机构而有来源于境内的股息、利息、租金、特许权使用费和其他所得，除适用本规定外，也可以参照执行国家有关涉外经济法律、法规的规定。

**Article 5** Where overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors invest within China in establishing enterprises with all capital invested by them, equity joint ventures, and contractual joint ventures (hereinafter collectively referred to as enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots), in addition to the application of these Provisions, the provisions of the relevant state laws and regulations concerning foreign economic relations shall be applied mutatis mutandis, and they shall enjoy the corresponding treatment for enterprises with foreign investment.  
Where overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors make other forms of investment within China, and where they have no business establishment within China but have dividends, interest, rent, royalties and other income derived from within China, in addition to the application of these Provisions, the provisions of the relevant state laws and regulations concerning foreign economic relations may also be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　华侨、港澳投资者可以用可自由兑换货币、机器设备或者其他实物、工业产权、专有技术等作为投资。

**Article 6** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may invest with convertible foreign currencies, machinery and equipment or other tangible assets, industrial property rights, proprietary technology, and the like.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内的投资、购置的资产、工业产权、投资所得利润和其他合法权益受国家法律保护，并可以依法转让和继承。  
华侨、港澳投资者在境内的活动应当遵守国家的法律、法规。

**Article 7** The investments, purchased assets, industrial property rights, profits derived from investments and other lawful rights and interests of overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors within China shall be protected by the state laws, and may be transferred and inherited according to law.  
Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors shall abide by the state laws and regulations in their activities within China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家对华侨、港澳投资者的投资和其他资产不实行国有化。

**Article 8** The State shall not nationalize the investments and other assets of overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家根据社会公共利益的需要，对华侨、港澳同胞投资企业实行征收时，依照法律程序进行并给予相应的补偿。

**Article 9** Where the State, in the interest of social public interest, expropriates an enterprise with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots, it shall proceed in accordance with legal procedures and grant appropriate compensation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　华侨、港澳投资者投资获得的合法利润，其他合法收入和清算后的资金，可以依法汇往境外。

**Article 10** The lawful profits, other lawful income and funds after liquidation obtained by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors from their investments may be remitted abroad according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　华侨、港澳同胞投资企业在其投资总额内进口本企业所需的机器设备、生产用车辆和办公设备，以及华侨、港澳同胞个人在企业工作期间运进自用的、合理数量的生活用品和交通工具，免缴进口关税、工商统一税，免领进口许可证。  
华侨、港澳同胞投资企业进口用于生产出口产品的原材料、燃料、散件、零部件、元器件、配套件，免缴进口关税、工商统一税，免领进口许可证，由海关实行监管。上述进口料件，如用于在境内销售的产品，应当按照国家规定补办进口手续，并照章补税。  
华侨、港澳同胞投资企业生产的出口产品，除国家限制出口的外，免缴出口关税和工商统一税。

**Article 11** Enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots shall, within their total investment, be exempted from import customs duties and the consolidated industrial and commercial tax and shall be exempted from import license requirements for importing machinery and equipment, production vehicles and office equipment required by the enterprise, as well as for importing reasonable quantities of daily necessities and means of transport for personal use by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots during their work in the enterprise.  
Enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots shall be exempted from import customs duties and the consolidated industrial and commercial tax and shall be exempted from import license requirements for importing raw materials, fuel, CKD components, spare parts, components, and auxiliary parts used for producing export products, and such imports shall be subject to customs supervision. If the above-mentioned imported materials and parts are used in products sold within China, the import formalities shall be gone through retroactively and the taxes shall be paid according to the relevant state provisions.  
Export products produced by enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots, except those restricted by the State for export, shall be exempted from export customs duties and the consolidated industrial and commercial tax.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　华侨、港澳同胞投资企业可以向境内的金融机构借款，也可以向境外的金融机构借款，并可以本企业资产和权益抵押、担保。

**Article 12** Enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots may borrow funds from financial institutions within China and may also borrow funds from financial institutions outside China, and may use the assets and rights and interests of the enterprise as mortgage or guarantee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　华侨、港澳投资者拥有全部资本的企业，经营期限由投资者自行确定；合资经营企业和合作经营企业，经营期限由合资或者合作各方协商确定，也可以不规定经营期限。

**Article 13** For enterprises with all capital invested by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors, the term of operation shall be determined by the investors on their own; for equity joint ventures and contractual joint ventures, the term of operation shall be determined through negotiation by the parties to the joint venture or cooperation, or the term of operation may not be specified.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　合资经营企业董事会的组成和董事长的委派、合作经营企业董事会或者联合管理机构的组成和董事长或者联合管理机构主任的委派，可以参照出资比例或者合作条件，由合资或者合作各方协商决定。

**Article 14** The composition of the board of directors and the appointment of the chairman of an equity joint venture, and the composition of the board of directors or the joint management body and the appointment of the chairman or the director of the joint management body of a contractual joint venture, may be determined through consultation by the parties to the joint venture or cooperation by reference to the investment ratio or the cooperative conditions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　华侨、港澳同胞投资企业依照经批准的合同、章程进行经营管理活动。企业的经营管理自主权不受干涉。

**Article 15** Enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots shall conduct their business management activities in accordance with the approved contracts and articles of association. The autonomous right of business management of the enterprise shall not be interfered with.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在境内投资的华侨、港澳同胞个人以及华侨、港澳同胞投资企业从境外聘请的技术和管理人员，可以申请办理多次入出境的证件。

**Article 16** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots who invest within China, as well as technical and managerial personnel employed from abroad by enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots, may apply for multiple entry and exit documents.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内投资可以委托境内的亲友为其代理人。代理人应当持有具有法律效力的委托书。

**Article 17** Overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors investing within China may entrust their relatives or friends within China as their agents. The agent shall hold a legally valid power of attorney.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在华侨、港澳同胞投资企业集中的地区，华侨、港澳投资者可以向当地人民政府申请成立华侨、港澳投资者协会。

**Article 18** In areas where enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots are concentrated, overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors may apply to the local people's government for the establishment of an association of overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内投资举办合资经营企业、合作经营企业，由境内的合资、合作方负责申请；举办华侨、港澳投资者拥有全部资本的企业，由华侨、港澳投资者直接申请或者委托在境内的亲友、咨询服务机构等代为申请。华侨、港澳投资者投资举办企业的申请，由当地对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关统一受理。  
华侨、港澳同胞投资企业的审批，按照国务院规定的权限办理。各级对外经济贸易部门或者地方人民政府指定的审批机关应当在收到全部申请文件之日起四十五天内决定批准或者不批准。  
申请人应当在收到批准证书之日起三十天内，按照有关登记管理办法，向工商行政管理机关申请登记，领取营业执照。

**Article 19** Where overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors invest within China in establishing equity joint ventures or contractual joint ventures, the application shall be made by the Chinese party to the joint venture or cooperation; where they establish enterprises with all capital invested by them, the application shall be made directly by the overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors or by their relatives, friends, consulting service agencies, etc. within China on their behalf. Applications for the establishment of enterprises by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors shall be accepted uniformly by the local department of foreign economic relations and trade or the examination and approval authority designated by the local people's government.  
The examination and approval of enterprises with investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao compatriots shall be handled within the limits of authority prescribed by the State Council. The department of foreign economic relations and trade at various levels or the examination and approval authority designated by the local people's government shall, within forty-five days from the date of receipt of all application documents, decide whether to approve or disapprove.  
The applicant shall, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the certificate of approval, apply for registration with the administrative authority for industry and commerce in accordance with the relevant registration management measures and obtain a business license.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　华侨、港澳投资者在境内投资因履行合同发生的或者与合同有关的争议，当事人应当尽可能通过协商或者调解解决。  
当事人不愿协商、调解的，或者协商、调解不成的，可以依据合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，提交境内或者其他仲裁机构仲裁。  
当事人没有在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又没有达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院起诉。

**Article 20** Disputes arising from the performance of a contract or relating to a contract in respect of investment by overseas Chinese and Hong Kong/Macao investors within China shall be settled by the parties as far as possible through consultation or mediation.  
Where the parties are unwilling to engage in consultation or mediation, or where consultation or mediation fails, the dispute may be submitted to an arbitration institution within China or another arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards.  
Where the parties have not included an arbitration clause in the contract and have not entered into a written arbitration agreement afterwards, they may bring a prosecution before a People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定由对外经济贸易部负责解释。

**Article 21** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations and Trade.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
