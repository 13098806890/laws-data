# 政府参事工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-11-02  
**生效日期**：2010-01-01

> (2009年10月28日国务院第85次常务会议通过　2009年11月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第565号公布　自2010年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范和加强政府参事工作，保障参事依法履行职责，发挥参事参政议政、建言献策、咨询国是、民主监督、统战联谊的作用，制定本条例。 第二条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市以及设区的市人民政府设立的参事工作机构，主管本级人民政府的参事工作。上级人民政府参事工作机构指导下级人民政府参事工作机构的工作。 第三条　参事实行聘任制。国务院参事由国务院总理聘任，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参事分别由省长、自治区主席、市长聘任，设区的市人民政府参事由市长聘任。 第四条　参事主要从民主党派成员和无党派人士中聘任，也可以从中国共产党党员专家学者中聘任。 第五条　参事应当符合下列条件： (一)拥护中华人民共和国宪法； (二)具有一定的代表性、较大的社会影响和较高的知名度； (三)具有较强的参政咨询能力； (四)具备履行职责的身体条件和意愿。 参事的首聘年龄不得低于55周岁，不得高于65周岁。参事任职的最高年龄不得超过70周岁。 第六条　聘任参事应当依照下列程序： (一)本级人民政府领导人员和各有关单位向参事工作机构推荐参事人选； (二)参事工作机构会同有关部门根据本条例第五条规定的条件，研究确定考察人选并进行考察； (三)参事工作机构与有关部门根据考察情况研究确定参事拟聘人选，报本级人民政府审定；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和加强政府参事工作，保障参事依法履行职责，发挥参事参政议政、建言献策、咨询国是、民主监督、统战联谊的作用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating and strengthening the work of government counselors, ensuring that counselors perform their duties according to law, and giving play to the role of counselors in participating in the deliberation and administration of state affairs, offering suggestions and recommendations, consulting on national affairs, exercising democratic supervision, and conducting united front and friendship activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市以及设区的市人民政府设立的参事工作机构，主管本级人民政府的参事工作。上级人民政府参事工作机构指导下级人民政府参事工作机构的工作。

**Article 2** The counselor work institutions established by the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities divided into districts shall be in charge of the counselor work of the people's government at the same level. The counselor work institution of a higher-level people's government shall guide the work of the counselor work institution of a lower-level people's government.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　参事实行聘任制。国务院参事由国务院总理聘任，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参事分别由省长、自治区主席、市长聘任，设区的市人民政府参事由市长聘任。

**Article 3** Counselors shall be appointed under an appointment system. Counselors of the State Council shall be appointed by the Premier of the State Council; counselors of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be appointed by the governor, chairman of the autonomous region, or mayor, respectively; and counselors of the people's governments of cities divided into districts shall be appointed by the mayor.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　参事主要从民主党派成员和无党派人士中聘任，也可以从中国共产党党员专家学者中聘任。

**Article 4** Counselors shall be mainly appointed from among members of democratic parties and persons without party affiliation, and may also be appointed from among experts and scholars who are members of the Communist Party of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　参事应当符合下列条件：  
(一)拥护中华人民共和国宪法；  
(二)具有一定的代表性、较大的社会影响和较高的知名度；  
(三)具有较强的参政咨询能力；  
(四)具备履行职责的身体条件和意愿。  
参事的首聘年龄不得低于55周岁，不得高于65周岁。参事任职的最高年龄不得超过70周岁。

**Article 5** A counselor shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) support the Constitution of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) have certain representativeness, considerable social influence, and high public visibility;  
(3) have strong ability to participate in political affairs and provide consultation;  
(4) have the physical condition and willingness to perform duties.  
The age at initial appointment of a counselor shall not be lower than 55 years old and shall not be higher than 65 years old. The maximum age for a counselor to hold office shall not exceed 70 years old.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　聘任参事应当依照下列程序：  
(一)本级人民政府领导人员和各有关单位向参事工作机构推荐参事人选；  
(二)参事工作机构会同有关部门根据[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定的条件，研究确定考察人选并进行考察；  
(三)参事工作机构与有关部门根据考察情况研究确定参事拟聘人选，报本级人民政府审定；  
(四)本级人民政府主要负责人签发聘书。

**Article 6** The appointment of a counselor shall be carried out in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) the leading persons of the people's government at the same level and relevant units recommend candidates for counselor to the counselor work institution;  
(2) the counselor work institution, together with relevant departments, shall, in accordance with the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations, study and determine the candidates for assessment and conduct assessment;  
(3) the counselor work institution and relevant departments shall, based on the assessment results, study and determine the proposed candidates for appointment as counselor, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for examination and approval;  
(4) the principal responsible person of the people's government at the same level shall issue the letter of appointment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　参事每届任期不超过5年。政府工作需要且符合[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定条件的，可以续聘。

**Article 7** The term of office of a counselor shall not exceed five years. A counselor may be reappointed if the work of the government so requires and if the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations are met.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　参事在任期内不再符合[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定的条件或者不能继续履行职责的，参事工作机构应当及时报本级人民政府主要负责人予以解聘。

**Article 8** If a counselor no longer meets the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations or is unable to continue performing duties during the term of office, the counselor work institution shall promptly report to the principal responsible person of the people's government at the same level for dismissal.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　参事在任期内可以向参事工作机构申请离任。参事申请离任的，由参事工作机构报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 9** A counselor may apply to the counselor work institution for resignation during the term of office. If a counselor applies for resignation, the counselor work institution shall submit the application to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　参事履行下列职责：  
(一)围绕本级人民政府的中心工作开展调查研究，了解、反映社情民意；  
(二)对本级人民政府的工作进行监督，提出意见、建议和批评；  
(三)对有关法律文件草案、本级人民政府工作报告稿和其他重要文件草案提出修改意见和建议；  
(四)参加爱国统一战线工作；  
(五)承担本级人民政府交办的其他工作。

**Article 10** A counselor shall perform the following duties:  
(1) conduct investigation and research focusing on the central work of the people's government at the same level, and learn about and reflect social conditions and public opinion;  
(2) supervise the work of the people's government at the same level, and offer opinions, suggestions, and criticism;  
(3) put forward revision opinions and suggestions on draft legal documents, the draft work report of the people's government at the same level, and other important draft documents;  
(4) participate in the work of the patriotic united front;  
(5) undertake other work assigned by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　参事享有下列权利：  
(一)直接向本级人民政府领导人员反映情况，提出意见和建议；  
(二)根据履行职责的需要，向本级和下级人民政府有关部门了解情况；  
(三)应邀参加本级人民政府有关会议；  
(四)享受国家规定的工作待遇。

**Article 11** A counselor shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) directly report situations and put forward opinions and suggestions to the leading persons of the people's government at the same level;  
(2) as required for performing duties, learn about relevant information from the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and of lower-level people's governments;  
(3) attend relevant meetings of the people's government at the same level upon invitation;  
(4) enjoy the working benefits prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　参事应当履行工作职责，遵守工作纪律，保守国家秘密，遵守廉政规定，不得利用职务之便谋取不正当利益。

**Article 12** A counselor shall perform work duties, observe work discipline, keep state secrets, comply with integrity rules, and shall not take advantage of his or her position to seek improper gains.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立参事工作机构的人民政府应当为本级人民政府参事工作提供经费保障，为参事依法履行职责提供便利条件。

**Article 13** The people's government that establishes a counselor work institution shall provide financial guarantee for the counselor work of the people's government at the same level, and provide convenient conditions for counselors to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设立参事工作机构的人民政府应当根据科学民主依法决策的需要，安排本级人民政府参事开展参政咨询工作的重点任务，定期向参事通报政府重大决策、工作部署和经济社会发展情况，采取座谈会、论证会等方式直接听取参事的意见和建议，接受参事对政府工作的民主监督。  
设立参事工作机构的人民政府有关部门和下级人民政府应当支持、配合参事的工作，及时办理参事建议并向参事工作机构反馈办理情况。

**Article 14** The people's government that establishes a counselor work institution shall, in light of the need for scientific, democratic, and law-based decision-making, arrange key tasks for counselors at the same level to carry out political participation and consultation work, regularly brief counselors on major government decisions, work arrangements, and economic and social development, directly solicit opinions and suggestions from counselors by means of symposiums, argumentation meetings, etc., and accept counselors' democratic supervision over government work.  
The relevant departments of the people's government that establishes a counselor work institution and lower-level people's governments shall support and cooperate with the work of counselors, promptly handle counselors' suggestions, and provide feedback to the counselor work institution on the handling status.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　参事工作机构负责对本级人民政府参事的组织管理和服务，履行下列职责：  
(一)组织参事学习党和国家的方针政策；  
(二)组织参事围绕本级人民政府的中心工作开展调查研究；  
(三)组织参事开展同外国政府咨询机构的交流与合作；  
(四)组织参事参加爱国统一战线工作；  
(五)为参事依法履行职责提供保障和服务；  
(六)对参事履行职责的情况进行考评；  
(七)承办本级人民政府交办的其他事项。

**Article 15** The counselor work institution shall be responsible for the organization, management, and service of counselors of the people's government at the same level, and shall perform the following duties:  
(1) organize counselors to study the guidelines and policies of the Party and the state;  
(2) organize counselors to conduct investigation and research focusing on the central work of the people's government at the same level;  
(3) organize counselors to carry out exchanges and cooperation with foreign government advisory bodies;  
(4) organize counselors to participate in the work of the patriotic united front;  
(5) provide guarantee and services for counselors to perform duties according to law;  
(6) evaluate the performance of counselors in performing duties;  
(7) undertake other matters assigned by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　参事的人事关系所在单位应当为参事履行职责提供必要的工作条件，落实国家规定的待遇。

**Article 16** The entity where a counselor's personnel relationship is maintained shall provide necessary working conditions for the counselor to perform duties, and implement the benefits prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　参事的人事关系在党政机关、事业单位的，其编制管理按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 17** If the personnel relationship of a counselor is maintained in a Party or government organ or a public institution, the management of its staffing shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　参事在参事工作中成绩显著、为政府科学民主依法决策做出突出贡献的，由本级人民政府给予表彰奖励。

**Article 18** If a counselor achieves outstanding results in counselor work and makes remarkable contributions to the scientific, democratic, and law-based decision-making of the government, the people's government at the same level shall give commendation and reward.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　参事不依法履行职责或者从事与参事身份不符的活动、造成不良后果的，参事工作机构应当报本级人民政府主要负责人予以辞聘。

**Article 19** If a counselor fails to perform duties according to law or engages in activities inconsistent with the status of a counselor, causing adverse consequences, the counselor work institution shall report to the principal responsible person of the people's government at the same level for discharge.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本条例自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 20** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2010.
