# 储蓄管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1993-03-01

> (1992年12月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第107号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了发展储蓄事业，保护储户的合法权益，加强储蓄管理，制定本条例。 第二条　凡在中国境内办理储蓄业务的储蓄机构和参加储蓄的个人，必须遵守本条例的规定。 第三条　本条例所称储蓄是指个人将属于其所有的人民币或者外币存入储蓄机构，储蓄机构开具存折或者存单作为凭证，个人凭存折或者存单可以支取存款本金和利息，储蓄机构依照规定支付存款本金和利息的活动。 任何单位和个人不得将公款以个人名义转为储蓄存款。 第四条　本条例所称储蓄机构是指经中国人民银行或其分支机构批准，各银行、信用合作社办理储蓄业务的机构，以及邮政企业依法办理储蓄业务的机构。 第五条　国家保护个人合法储蓄存款的所有权及其他合法权益，鼓励个人参加储蓄。 储蓄机构办理储蓄业务，必须遵循“存款自愿，取款自由，存款有息，为储户保密”的原则。 第六条　中国人民银行负责全国储蓄管理工作。 中国人民银行及其分支机构负责储蓄机构和储蓄业务的审批，协调、仲裁有关储蓄机构之间在储蓄业务方面的争议，监督、稽核储蓄机构的业务工作，纠正和处罚违反国家储蓄法律、法规和政策的行为。 第七条　中国人民银行经国务院批准，可以采取适当措施稳定储蓄，保护储户利益。 第八条　除储蓄机构外，任何单位和个人不得办理储蓄业务。 第二章　储蓄机构 第九条　储蓄机构的设置，应当遵循统一规划，方便群众，注重实效，确保安全的原则。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展储蓄事业，保护储户的合法权益，加强储蓄管理，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of developing the savings business, protecting the lawful rights and interests of depositors, and strengthening savings management.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡在中国境内办理储蓄业务的储蓄机构和参加储蓄的个人，必须遵守本条例的规定。

**Article 2** All savings institutions handling savings business within the territory of China and individuals participating in savings must abide by the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称储蓄是指个人将属于其所有的人民币或者外币存入储蓄机构，储蓄机构开具存折或者存单作为凭证，个人凭存折或者存单可以支取存款本金和利息，储蓄机构依照规定支付存款本金和利息的活动。  
任何单位和个人不得将公款以个人名义转为储蓄存款。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, 'savings' means the activity in which an individual deposits Renminbi or foreign currency owned by him or her with a savings institution, the savings institution issues a passbook or certificate of deposit as evidence, the individual may withdraw the principal and interest of the deposit by presenting the passbook or certificate of deposit, and the savings institution pays the principal and interest of the deposit in accordance with regulations.  
No unit or individual shall convert public funds into savings deposits in the name of an individual.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本条例所称储蓄机构是指经中国人民银行或其分支机构批准，各银行、信用合作社办理储蓄业务的机构，以及邮政企业依法办理储蓄业务的机构。

**Article 4** For the purpose of these Regulations, 'savings institution' means an institution of a bank or a credit cooperative that handles savings business with the approval of the People's Bank of China or its branch, as well as an institution of a postal enterprise that handles savings business according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家保护个人合法储蓄存款的所有权及其他合法权益，鼓励个人参加储蓄。  
储蓄机构办理储蓄业务，必须遵循“存款自愿，取款自由，存款有息，为储户保密”的原则。

**Article 5** The State shall protect the ownership and other lawful rights and interests of individuals' lawful savings deposits, and encourage individuals to participate in savings.  
When handling savings business, a savings institution must follow the principles of 'voluntary deposit, free withdrawal, interest payment on deposits, and confidentiality for depositors'.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中国人民银行负责全国储蓄管理工作。  
中国人民银行及其分支机构负责储蓄机构和储蓄业务的审批，协调、仲裁有关储蓄机构之间在储蓄业务方面的争议，监督、稽核储蓄机构的业务工作，纠正和处罚违反国家储蓄法律、法规和政策的行为。

**Article 6** The People's Bank of China shall be responsible for the management of savings throughout the country.  
The People's Bank of China and its branches shall be responsible for the approval of savings institutions and savings businesses, coordinating and arbitrating disputes among savings institutions relating to savings businesses, supervising and auditing the business operations of savings institutions, and correcting and punishing acts that violate State laws, regulations and policies on savings.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国人民银行经国务院批准，可以采取适当措施稳定储蓄，保护储户利益。

**Article 7** The People's Bank of China may, with the approval of the State Council, take appropriate measures to stabilize savings and protect the interests of depositors.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　除储蓄机构外，任何单位和个人不得办理储蓄业务。

**Article 8** No unit or individual shall handle savings business except for savings institutions.

### 第二章　储蓄机构

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　储蓄机构的设置，应当遵循统一规划，方便群众，注重实效，确保安全的原则。

**Article 9** The establishment of a savings institution shall follow the principles of unified planning, convenience for the masses, emphasis on practical results, and ensuring safety.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　储蓄机构的设置，应当按照国家有关规定报中国人民银行或其分支机构批准，并申领《经营金融业务许可证》，但国家法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 10** The establishment of a savings institution shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, be submitted to the People's Bank of China or its branch for approval, and a 'Permit for Operating Financial Business' shall be applied for, except as otherwise provided by State laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　储蓄机构的设置必须具备下列条件：  
(一)有机构名称、组织机构和营业场所；  
(二)熟悉储蓄业务的工作人员不少于四人；  
(三)有必要的安全防范设备。

**Article 11** The establishment of a savings institution must meet the following conditions:  
(1) There shall be an institution name, organizational structure, and business premises;  
(2) The number of staff members familiar with savings business shall be no less than four;  
(3) There shall be necessary safety and security equipment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经当地中国人民银行分支机构批准，储蓄机构可以设立储蓄代办点。储蓄代办点的管理办法，由中国人民银行规定。

**Article 12** With the approval of the local branch of the People's Bank of China, a savings institution may establish savings agent offices. The administrative measures for savings agent offices shall be prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　储蓄机构应当按照规定时间营业，不得擅自停业或者缩短营业时间。

**Article 13** A savings institution shall operate during the prescribed hours, and shall not suspend business or shorten business hours without authorization.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　储蓄机构应当保证储蓄存款本金和利息的支付，不得违反规定拒绝支付储蓄存款本金和利息。

**Article 14** A savings institution shall guarantee the payment of principal and interest on savings deposits, and shall not refuse to pay principal and interest on savings deposits in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　储蓄机构不得使用不正当手段吸收储蓄存款。

**Article 15** A savings institution shall not use improper means to attract savings deposits.

### 第三章　储蓄业务

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　储蓄机构可以办理下列人民币储蓄业务：  
(一)活期储蓄存款；  
(二)整存整取定期储蓄存款；  
(三)零存整取定期储蓄存款；  
(四)存本取息定期储蓄存款；  
(五)整存零取定期储蓄存款；  
(六)定活两便储蓄存款；  
(七)华侨(人民币)整存整取定期储蓄存款；  
(八)经中国人民银行批准开办的其他种类的储蓄存款。

**Article 16** A savings institution may handle the following Renminbi savings businesses:  
(1) Current savings deposits;  
(2) Fixed-term savings deposits by lump-sum deposit and withdrawal;  
(3) Fixed-term savings deposits by installment deposit and lump-sum withdrawal;  
(4) Fixed-term savings deposits by deposit of principal and withdrawal of interest;  
(5) Fixed-term savings deposits by lump-sum deposit and installment withdrawal;  
(6) Current and fixed-term savings deposits;  
(7) Fixed-term savings deposits by lump-sum deposit and withdrawal for overseas Chinese (Renminbi);  
(8) Other types of savings deposits approved by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经外汇管理部门批准，储蓄机构可以办理下列外币储蓄业务：  
(一)活期储蓄存款；  
(二)整存整取定期储蓄存款；  
(三)经中国人民银行批准开办的其他种类的外币储蓄存款。  
办理外币储蓄业务，存款本金和利息应当用外币支付。

**Article 17** With the approval of the foreign exchange administration department, a savings institution may handle the following foreign currency savings businesses:  
(1) Current savings deposits;  
(2) Fixed-term savings deposits by lump-sum deposit and withdrawal;  
(3) Other types of foreign currency savings deposits approved by the People's Bank of China.  
When handling foreign currency savings business, the principal and interest of deposits shall be paid in foreign currency.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　储蓄机构办理定期储蓄存款时，根据储户的意愿，可以同时为储户办理定期储蓄存款到期自动转存业务。

**Article 18** When handling fixed-term savings deposits, a savings institution may, at the request of the depositor, simultaneously handle automatic renewal of the fixed-term savings deposit upon maturity.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　根据国家住房改革的有关政策和实际需要，经当地中国人民银行分支机构批准，储蓄机构可以办理个人住房储蓄业务。

**Article 19** In accordance with relevant policies and actual needs of the national housing reform, and with the approval of the local branch of the People's Bank of China, a savings institution may handle personal housing savings business.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经中国人民银行或其分支机构批准，储蓄机构可以办理下列金融业务：  
(一)发售和兑付以居民个人为发行对象的国库券、金融债券、企业债券等有价证券；  
(二)个人定期储蓄存款存单小额抵押贷款业务；  
(三)其他金融业务。

**Article 20** With the approval of the People's Bank of China or its branch, a savings institution may handle the following financial businesses:  
(1) Issuing and redeeming negotiable securities such as treasury bonds, financial bonds, and corporate bonds that are issued to individual residents;  
(2) Small-amount mortgage loan business against certificates of personal fixed-term savings deposits;  
(3) Other financial businesses.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　储蓄机构可以办理代发工资和代收房租、水电费等服务性业务。

**Article 21** A savings institution may handle service businesses such as acting as agent for salary payment and collection of rent, water and electricity fees.

### 第四章　储蓄存款利率和计息

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　储蓄存款利率由中国人民银行拟订，经国务院批准后公布，或者由国务院授权中国人民银行制定、公布。

**Article 22** The interest rates on savings deposits shall be proposed by the People's Bank of China, and published after approval by the State Council, or shall be formulated and published by the People's Bank of China as authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　储蓄机构必须挂牌公告储蓄存款利率，不得擅自变动。

**Article 23** A savings institution must publicly announce the interest rates on savings deposits by displaying them, and shall not alter them without authorization.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　未到期的定期储蓄存款，全部提前支取的，按支取日挂牌公告的活期储蓄存款利率计付利息；部分提前支取的，提前支取的部分按支取日挂牌公告的活期储蓄存款利率计付利息，其余部分到期时按存单开户日挂牌公告的定期储蓄存款利率计付利息。

**Article 24** Where a fixed-term savings deposit which has not yet matured is withdrawn in full before maturity, interest shall be calculated and paid at the current savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the date of withdrawal; where it is partially withdrawn before maturity, interest on the partially withdrawn portion shall be calculated and paid at the current savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the date of withdrawal, and interest on the remaining portion shall be calculated and paid at the fixed-term savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the date of deposit of the certificate of deposit upon maturity.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　逾期支取的定期储蓄存款，其超过原定存期的部分，除约定自动转存的外，按支取日挂牌公告的活期储蓄存款利率计付利息。

**Article 25** For overdue fixed-term savings deposits, interest on the portion exceeding the original fixed term shall be calculated and paid at the current savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the date of withdrawal, unless automatic renewal has been agreed upon.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　定期储蓄存款在存期内遇有利率调整，按存单开户日挂牌公告的相应的定期储蓄存款利率计付利息。

**Article 26** Where the interest rate is adjusted during the deposit term of a fixed-term savings deposit, interest shall be calculated and paid at the corresponding fixed-term savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the date of deposit of the certificate of deposit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　活期储蓄存款在存入期间遇有利率调整，按结息日挂牌公告的活期储蓄存款利率计付利息。全部支取活期储蓄存款，按清户日挂牌公告的活期储蓄存款利率计付利息。

**Article 27** Where the interest rate is adjusted during the deposit period of a current savings deposit, interest shall be calculated and paid at the current savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the interest settlement date. Where a current savings deposit is withdrawn in full, interest shall be calculated and paid at the current savings deposit interest rate publicly announced on the account closure date.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　储户认为储蓄存款利息支付有错误时，有权向经办的储蓄机构申请复核；经办的储蓄机构应当及时受理、复核。

**Article 28** Where a depositor believes that the payment of interest on a savings deposit is erroneous, the depositor shall have the right to apply to the savings institution handling the business for re-checking; the savings institution handling the business shall promptly accept and re-check the application.

### 第五章　提前支取、挂失、查询和过户

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　未到期的定期储蓄存款，储户提前支取的，必须持存单和存款人的身份证明办理；代储户支取的，代支取人还必须持其身份证明。

**Article 29** Where a depositor withdraws a fixed-term savings deposit before maturity, the depositor must present the certificate of deposit and the identity certificate of the depositor for handling; where a person withdraws on behalf of the depositor, the person acting as agent must also present his or her identity certificate.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　存单、存折分为记名式和不记名式。记名式的存单、存折可以挂失，不记名式的存单、存折不能挂失。

**Article 30** Savings deposit certificates and passbooks are divided into registered and unregistered types. Registered certificates and passbooks may be reported lost, while unregistered certificates and passbooks cannot be reported lost.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　储户遗失存单、存折或者预留印鉴的印章的，必须立即持本人身份证明，并提供储户的姓名、开户时间、储蓄种类、金额、账号及住址等有关情况，向其开户的储蓄机构书面申请挂失。在特殊情况下，储户可以用口头或者函电形式申请挂失，但必须在5天内补办书面申请挂失手续。  
储蓄机构受理挂失后，必须立即停止支付该储蓄存款；受理挂失前该储蓄存款已被他人支取的，储蓄机构不负赔偿责任。

**Article 31** Where a depositor loses a savings deposit certificate, passbook, or the seal of a reserved specimen impression, the depositor must immediately present his or her identity certificate, provide relevant information such as the depositor's name, account opening date, type of savings, amount, account number, and address, and apply in writing to the savings institution with which the account is opened for reporting the loss. Under special circumstances, the depositor may apply to report the loss orally or by letter or telegram, but must complete the formalities for a written application for reporting the loss within five days.  
After accepting the report of loss, the savings institution must immediately stop payment of the savings deposit; if the savings deposit has already been withdrawn by another person before the report of loss is accepted, the savings institution shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　储蓄机构及其工作人员对储户的储蓄情况负有保密责任。  
储蓄机构不代任何单位和个人查询、冻结或者划拨储蓄存款，国家法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 32** A savings institution and its staff members shall have the duty of confidentiality regarding the savings information of depositors.  
A savings institution shall not inquire into, freeze, or transfer savings deposits on behalf of any unit or individual, except as otherwise provided by State laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　储蓄存款的所有权发生争议，涉及办理过户的，储蓄机构依据人民法院发生法律效力的判决书、裁定书或者调解书办理过户手续。

**Article 33** Where a dispute arises over the ownership of a savings deposit and involves the transfer of ownership, the savings institution shall handle the transfer procedures pursuant to a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document of a People's Court.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的单位和个人，由中国人民银行或其分支机构责令其纠正，并可以根据情节轻重处以罚款、停业整顿、吊销《经营金融业务许可证》；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)擅自开办储蓄业务的；  
(二)擅自设置储蓄机构的；  
(三)储蓄机构擅自开办新的储蓄种类的；  
(四)储蓄机构擅自办理本条例规定以外的其他金融业务的；  
(五)擅自停业或者缩短营业时间的；  
(六)储蓄机构采取不正当手段吸收储蓄存款的；  
(七)违反国家利率规定，擅自变动储蓄存款利率的；  
(八)泄露储户储蓄情况或者未经法定程序代为查询、冻结、划拨储蓄存款的；  
(九)其他违反国家储蓄法律、法规和政策的。  
违反[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的，依照国家有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 34** Any unit or individual that violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the People's Bank of China or its branch to make corrections, and may be punished by a fine, suspension of business for rectification, or revocation of the 'Permit for Operating Financial Business' depending on the seriousness of the circumstances; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Handling savings business without authorization;  
(2) Establishing a savings institution without authorization;  
(3) A savings institution opening a new type of savings without authorization;  
(4) A savings institution handling financial businesses other than those provided for in these Regulations without authorization;  
(5) Suspending business or shortening business hours without authorization;  
(6) A savings institution using improper means to attract savings deposits;  
(7) Violating State regulations on interest rates and altering savings deposit interest rates without authorization;  
(8) Disclosing a depositor's savings information or inquiring into, freezing, or transferring savings deposits without legal procedures;  
(9) Other acts that violate State laws, regulations and policies on savings.  
Any violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 3 of these Regulations shall be punished in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对处罚决定不服的，当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国行政复议法》的规定申请复议。对复议决定不服的，当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 35** Where a party is dissatisfied with a punishment decision, the party may apply for reconsideration in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China. Where the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may initiate prosecution with a People's Court in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　复议申请人逾期不起诉又不履行复议决定的，依照《中华人民共和国行政复议法》的规定执行。

**Article 36** Where the applicant for reconsideration neither initiates prosecution within the time limit nor performs the reconsideration decision, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　储蓄机构违反国家有关规定，侵犯储户合法权益，造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 37** Where a savings institution violates relevant State regulations, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a depositor, and causes losses, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例施行前的定期储蓄存款，在原定存期内，依照本条例施行前国家有关规定办理计息事宜。

**Article 38** For fixed-term savings deposits existing before the coming into force of these Regulations, interest calculation matters within the original fixed term shall be handled in accordance with relevant State regulations in force before the coming into force of these Regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例由中国人民银行负责解释，实施细则由中国人民银行制定。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the People's Bank of China, and the implementing rules shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自1993年3月1日起施行。1980年5月28日中国人民银行发布的《中国人民银行储蓄存款章程》同时废止。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 1993. The 'Savings Deposit Regulations of the People's Bank of China' issued by the People's Bank of China on May 28, 1980 shall be abolished simultaneously.
