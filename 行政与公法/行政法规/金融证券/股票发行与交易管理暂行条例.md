# 股票发行与交易管理暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1993-04-22  
**生效日期**：1993-04-22

> (1993年4月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第112号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了适应发展社会主义市场经济的需要，建立和发展全国统一、高效的股票市场，保护投资者的合法权益和社会公共利益，促进国民经济的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事股票发行、交易及其相关活动，必须遵守本条例。 本条例关于股票的规定适用于具有股票性质、功能的证券。 第三条　股票的发行与交易，应当遵循公开、公平和诚实信用的原则。 第四条　股票的发行与交易，应当维护社会主义公有制的主体地位，保障国有资产不受侵害。 第五条　国务院证券委员会(以下简称“证券委”)是全国证券市场的主管机构，依照法律、法规的规定对全国证券市场进行统一管理。中国证券监督管理委员会(以下简称“证监会”)是证券委的监督管理执行机构，依照法律、法规的规定对证券发行与交易的具体活动进行管理和监督。 第六条　人民币特种股票发行与交易的具体办法另行制定。 境内企业直接或者间接到境外发行股票、将其股票在境外交易，必须经证券委审批，具体办法另行制定。 第二章　股票的发行 第七条　股票发行人必须是具有股票发行资格的股份有限公司。 前款所称股份有限公司，包括已经成立的股份有限公司和经批准拟成立的股份有限公司。 第八条　设立股份有限公司申请公开发行股票，应当符合下列条件： (一)其生产经营符合国家产业政策；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了适应发展社会主义市场经济的需要，建立和发展全国统一、高效的股票市场，保护投资者的合法权益和社会公共利益，促进国民经济的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to meet the needs of developing the socialist market economy, to establish and develop a nationwide unified and efficient stock market, to protect the legitimate rights and interests of investors and the public interest of society, and to promote the development of the national economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事股票发行、交易及其相关活动，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例关于股票的规定适用于具有股票性质、功能的证券。

**Article 2** Anyone engaging in the issuance, trading and related activities of stocks within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.  
The provisions of these Regulations regarding stocks shall apply to securities that have the nature and function of stocks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　股票的发行与交易，应当遵循公开、公平和诚实信用的原则。

**Article 3** The issuance and trading of stocks shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, and good faith.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　股票的发行与交易，应当维护社会主义公有制的主体地位，保障国有资产不受侵害。

**Article 4** The issuance and trading of stocks shall maintain the dominant position of socialist public ownership and ensure that state-owned assets are not infringed upon.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院证券委员会(以下简称“证券委”)是全国证券市场的主管机构，依照法律、法规的规定对全国证券市场进行统一管理。中国证券监督管理委员会(以下简称“证监会”)是证券委的监督管理执行机构，依照法律、法规的规定对证券发行与交易的具体活动进行管理和监督。

**Article 5** The Securities Committee of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the "Securities Committee") is the competent authority for the national securities market and shall conduct unified management of the national securities market in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations. The China Securities Regulatory Commission (hereinafter referred to as the "CSRC") is the supervisory and administrative executive body of the Securities Committee and shall manage and supervise the specific activities of securities issuance and trading in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民币特种股票发行与交易的具体办法另行制定。  
境内企业直接或者间接到境外发行股票、将其股票在境外交易，必须经证券委审批，具体办法另行制定。

**Article 6** Specific measures for the issuance and trading of Renminbi special stocks shall be formulated separately.  
Domestic enterprises that directly or indirectly issue stocks abroad or trade their stocks abroad must obtain approval from the Securities Committee, and specific measures shall be formulated separately.

### 第二章　股票的发行

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　股票发行人必须是具有股票发行资格的股份有限公司。  
前款所称股份有限公司，包括已经成立的股份有限公司和经批准拟成立的股份有限公司。

**Article 7** An issuer of stocks must be a company limited by shares that is qualified to issue stocks.  
The company limited by shares referred to in the preceding paragraph includes an established company limited by shares and a company limited by shares approved to be established.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立股份有限公司申请公开发行股票，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)其生产经营符合国家产业政策；  
(二)其发行的普通股限于一种，同股同权；  
(三)发起人认购的股本数额不少于公司拟发行的股本总额的35%；  
(四)在公司拟发行的股本总额中，发起人认购的部分不少于人民币三千万元，但是国家另有规定的除外；  
(五)向社会公众发行的部分不少于公司拟发行的股本总额的25%，其中公司职工认购的股本数额不得超过拟向社会公众发行的股本总额的10%；公司拟发行的股本总额超过人民币四亿元的，证监会按照规定可以酌情降低向社会公众发行的部分的比例，但是最低不少于公司拟发行的股本总额的10%；  
(六)发起人在近三年内没有重大违法行为；  
(七)证券委规定的其他条件。

**Article 8** To apply for the public issuance of stocks upon the establishment of a company limited by shares, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Its production and operation conform to the national industrial policies;  
(2) The ordinary shares it issues are limited to one class, with the same rights for the same shares;  
(3) The amount of share capital subscribed by the promoters is not less than 35% of the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company;  
(4) In the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company, the portion subscribed by the promoters is not less than RMB 30 million, unless otherwise provided by the State;  
(5) The portion issued to the public is not less than 25% of the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company, among which the amount of share capital subscribed by the company's employees shall not exceed 10% of the total share capital proposed to be issued to the public; if the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company exceeds RMB 400 million, the CSRC may, in accordance with regulations, appropriately reduce the proportion of the portion issued to the public, but the minimum shall not be less than 10% of the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company;  
(6) The promoters have no major illegal acts in the past three years;  
(7) Other conditions prescribed by the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　原有企业改组设立股份有限公司申请公开发行股票，除应当符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)所列条件外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)发行前一年末，净资产在总资产中所占比例不低于30%，无形资产在净资产中所占比例不高于20%，但是证券委另有规定的除外；  
(二)近三年连续盈利。  
国有企业改组设立股份有限公司公开发行股票的，国家拥有的股份在公司拟发行的股本总额中所占的比例由国务院或者国务院授权的部门规定。

**Article 9** Where an existing enterprise is restructured to establish a company limited by shares and applies for public issuance of stocks, in addition to meeting the conditions set forth in Article 8 of these Regulations, the following conditions shall also be met:  
(1) At the end of the year prior to issuance, the proportion of net assets in total assets is not less than 30%, and the proportion of intangible assets in net assets is not higher than 20%, unless otherwise provided by the Securities Committee;  
(2) It has been profitable for the past three consecutive years.  
Where a state-owned enterprise is restructured to establish a company limited by shares for public issuance of stocks, the proportion of state-owned shares in the total share capital proposed to be issued by the company shall be prescribed by the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　股份有限公司增资申请公开发行股票，除应当符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)和第九条所列条件外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)前一次公开发行股票所得资金的使用与其招股说明书所述的用途相符，并且资金使用效益良好；  
(二)距前一次公开发行股票的时间不少于十二个月；  
(三)从前一次公开发行股票到本次申请期间没有重大违法行为；  
(四)证券委规定的其他条件。

**Article 10** Where a company limited by shares increases its capital and applies for public issuance of stocks, in addition to meeting the conditions set forth in Articles 8 and 9 of these Regulations, the following conditions shall also be met:  
(1) The use of the funds obtained from the previous public issuance of stocks is consistent with the purposes stated in the prospectus, and the use of funds has achieved good results;  
(2) The time interval from the previous public issuance of stocks is not less than twelve months;  
(3) There have been no major illegal acts during the period from the previous public issuance of stocks to this application;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　定向募集公司申请公开发行股票，除应当符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)和第九条所列条件外，还应当符合下列条件：  
(一)定向募集所得资金的使用与其招股说明书所述的用途相符，并且资金使用效益良好；  
(二)距最近一次定向募集股份的时间不少于十二个月；  
(三)从最近一次定向募集到本次公开发行期间没有重大违法行为；  
(四)内部职工股权证按照规定范围发放，并且已交国家指定的证券机构集中托管；  
(五)证券委规定的其他条件。

**Article 11** Where a company that raised funds through private placement applies for public issuance of stocks, in addition to meeting the conditions set forth in Articles 8 and 9 of these Regulations, the following conditions shall also be met:  
(1) The use of the funds obtained from the private placement is consistent with the purposes stated in the prospectus, and the use of funds has achieved good results;  
(2) The time interval from the most recent private placement of shares is not less than twelve months;  
(3) There have been no major illegal acts during the period from the most recent private placement to this public issuance;  
(4) The internal employee stock certificates have been issued within the prescribed scope and have been deposited for centralized custody with a securities institution designated by the State;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请公开发行股票，按照下列程序办理：  
(一)申请人聘请会计师事务所、资产评估机构、律师事务所等专业性机构，对其资信、资产、财务状况进行审定、评估和就有关事项出具法律意见书后，按照隶属关系，分别向省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市人民政府(以下简称“地方政府”)或者中央企业主管部门提出公开发行股票的申请；  
(二)在国家下达的发行规模内，地方政府对地方企业的发行申请进行审批，中央企业主管部门在与申请人所在地地方政府协商后对中央企业的发行申请进行审批；地方政府、中央企业主管部门应当自收到发行申请之日起三十个工作日内作出审批决定，并抄报证券委；  
(三)被批准的发行申请，送证监会复审；证监会应当自收到复审申请之日起二十个工作日内出具复审意见书，并将复审意见书抄报证券委；经证监会复审同意的，申请人应当向证券交易所上市委员会提出申请，经上市委员会同意接受上市，方可发行股票。

**Article 12** An application for public issuance of stocks shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The applicant shall engage professional institutions such as accounting firms, asset appraisal institutions, and law firms to examine, appraise its creditworthiness, assets, and financial status, and to issue legal opinions on relevant matters. Thereafter, according to the subordinate relationship, the applicant shall submit the application for public issuance of stocks to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or city with independent planning status (hereinafter referred to as the "local government") or to the competent department of central enterprises;  
(2) Within the issuance scale assigned by the State, the local government shall examine and approve the issuance application of local enterprises, and the competent department of central enterprises shall, after consulting with the local government where the applicant is located, examine and approve the issuance application of central enterprises. The local government or the competent department of central enterprises shall make a decision on examination and approval within thirty working days from the date of receiving the issuance application, and shall send a copy to the Securities Committee;  
(3) The approved issuance application shall be submitted to the CSRC for review; the CSRC shall issue a review opinion within twenty working days from the date of receiving the review application, and shall send a copy of the review opinion to the Securities Committee. If the CSRC approves upon review, the applicant shall submit an application to the listing committee of the stock exchange, and only after the listing committee agrees to accept the listing may the stocks be issued.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请公开发行股票，应当向地方政府或者中央企业主管部门报送下列文件：  
(一)申请报告；  
(二)发起人会议或者股东大会同意公开发行股票的决议；  
(三)批准设立股份有限公司的文件；  
(四)工商行政管理部门颁发的股份有限公司营业执照或者股份有限公司筹建登记证明；  
(五)公司章程或者公司章程草案；  
(六)招股说明书；  
(七)资金运用的可行性报告；需要国家提供资金或者其他条件的固定资产投资项目，还应当提供国家有关部门同意固定资产投资立项的批准文件；  
(八)经会计师事务所审计的公司近三年或者成立以来的财务报告和由二名以上注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的审计报告；  
(九)经二名以上律师及其所在事务所就有关事项签字、盖章的法律意见书；  
(十)经二名以上专业评估人员及其所在机构签字、盖章的资产评估报告，经二名以上注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的验资报告；涉及国有资产的，还应当提供国有资产管理部门出具的确认文件；  
(十一)股票发行承销方案和承销协议；  
(十二)地方政府或者中央企业主管部门要求报送的其他文件。

**Article 13** To apply for public issuance of stocks, the following documents shall be submitted to the local government or the competent department of central enterprises:  
(1) Application report;  
(2) Resolution of the promoters' meeting or the shareholders' general meeting approving the public issuance of stocks;  
(3) Document approving the establishment of the company limited by shares;  
(4) Business license of the company limited by shares or registration certificate for the preparation of the company limited by shares issued by the administrative department for industry and commerce;  
(5) Articles of association or draft articles of association of the company;  
(6) Prospectus;  
(7) Feasibility report on the use of funds; for fixed asset investment projects requiring state funding or other conditions, the approval document from the relevant state department for the establishment of the fixed asset investment project shall also be provided;  
(8) Financial reports of the company for the past three years or since its establishment audited by an accounting firm, and audit reports signed and sealed by two or more certified public accountants and their firms;  
(9) Legal opinions signed and sealed by two or more lawyers and their firms on relevant matters;  
(10) Asset appraisal reports signed and sealed by two or more professional appraisers and their institutions, and capital verification reports signed and sealed by two or more certified public accountants and their firms; if state-owned assets are involved, a confirmation document issued by the state-owned assets management department shall also be provided;  
(11) Underwriting plan and underwriting agreement for the stock issuance;  
(12) Other documents required by the local government or the competent department of central enterprises.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被批准的发行申请送证监会复审时，除应当报送[本条例第十三条](#art-13)所列文件外，还应当报送下列文件：  
(一)地方政府或者中央企业主管部门批准发行申请的文件；  
(二)证监会要求报送的其他文件。

**Article 14** When the approved issuance application is submitted to the CSRC for review, in addition to the documents listed in Article 13 of these Regulations, the following documents shall also be submitted:  
(1) The document from the local government or the competent department of central enterprises approving the issuance application;  
(2) Other documents required by the CSRC.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[本条例第十三条](#art-13)所称招股说明书应当按照证监会规定的格式制作，并载明下列事项：  
(一)公司的名称、住所；  
(二)发起人、发行人简况；  
(三)筹资的目的；  
(四)公司现有股本总额，本次发行的股票种类、总额，每股的面值、售价，发行前的每股净资产值和发行结束后每股预期净资产值，发行费用和佣金；  
(五)初次发行的发起人认购股本的情况、股权结构及验资证明；  
(六)承销机构的名称、承销方式与承销数量；  
(七)发行的对象、时间、地点及股票认购和股款缴纳的方式；  
(八)所筹资金的运用计划及收益、风险预测；  
(九)公司近期发展规划和经注册会计师审核并出具审核意见的公司下一年的盈利预测文件；  
(十)重要的合同；  
(十一)涉及公司的重大诉讼事项；  
(十二)公司董事、监事名单及其简历；  
(十三)近三年或者成立以来的生产经营状况和有关业务发展的基本情况；  
(十四)经会计师事务所审计的公司近三年或者成立以来的财务报告和由二名以上注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的审计报告。  
(十五)增资发行的公司前次公开发行股票所筹资金的运用情况；  
(十六)证监会要求载明的其他事项。

**Article 15** The prospectus referred to in Article 13 of these Regulations shall be prepared in accordance with the format prescribed by the CSRC and shall contain the following particulars:  
(1) The name and domicile of the company;  
(2) Brief information on the promoters and the issuer;  
(3) The purpose of the fund-raising;  
(4) The existing total share capital of the company, the types and total amount of shares to be issued in this issuance, the par value and offering price per share, the net asset value per share before issuance and the expected net asset value per share after issuance, issuance expenses and commissions;  
(5) The circumstances of the promoters' subscription of share capital in the initial issuance, the shareholding structure, and the capital verification certificate;  
(6) The name of the underwriting institution, the method of underwriting, and the quantity to be underwritten;  
(7) The targets, time, place, and methods of stock subscription and payment of share capital;  
(8) The plan for the use of the funds raised and the forecast of income and risks;  
(9) The company's recent development plan and the document of the company's profit forecast for the next year reviewed by a certified public accountant with a review opinion;  
(10) Important contracts;  
(11) Major litigation matters involving the company;  
(12) The list and resumes of the company's directors and supervisors;  
(13) The production and operation status and basic information on the relevant business development in the past three years or since the establishment;  
(14) Financial reports of the company for the past three years or since its establishment audited by an accounting firm, and audit reports signed and sealed by two or more certified public accountants and their firms;  
(15) The use of funds raised from the previous public issuance of stocks by the company for capital increase issuance;  
(16) Other matters required by the CSRC to be stated.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　招股说明书的封面应当载明：“发行人保证招股说明书的内容真实、准确、完整。政府及国家证券管理部门对本次发行所作出的任何决定，均不表明其对发行人所发行的股票的价值或者投资人的收益作出实质性判断或者保证。”

**Article 16** The cover of the prospectus shall state: "The issuer guarantees that the content of the prospectus is true, accurate, and complete. Any decision made by the government or the national securities regulatory authorities regarding this issuance shall not be deemed as a substantive judgment or guarantee on the value of the shares issued by the issuer or the income of investors."

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　全体发起人或者董事以及主承销商应当在招股说明书上签字，保证招股说明书没有虚假、严重误导性陈述或者重大遗漏，并保证对其承担连带责任。

**Article 17** All promoters or directors and the lead underwriter shall sign the prospectus, guaranteeing that the prospectus contains no false, seriously misleading statements or material omissions, and guaranteeing that they shall bear joint and several liability therefor.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　为发行人出具文件的注册会计师及其所在事务所、专业评估人员及其所在机构、律师及其所在事务所，在履行职责时，应当按照本行业公认的业务标准和道德规范，对其出具文件内容的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查和验证。

**Article 18** The certified public accountants and their firms, professional appraisers and their institutions, and lawyers and their firms that issue documents for the issuer shall, when performing their duties, examine and verify the truthfulness, accuracy, and completeness of the contents of the documents they issue in accordance with the generally recognized professional standards and ethical norms of their respective industries.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在获准公开发行股票前，任何人不得以任何形式泄露招股说明书的内容。在获准公开发行股票后，发行人应当在承销期开始前二个至五个工作日期间公布招股说明书。  
发行人应当向认购人提供招股说明书。证券承销机构应当将招股说明书备置于营业场所，并有义务提醒认购人阅读招股说明书。  
招股说明书的有效期为六个月，自招股说明书签署完毕之日起计算。招股说明书失效后，股票发行必须立即停止。

**Article 19** Before obtaining approval for public issuance of stocks, no person shall disclose the content of the prospectus in any form. After obtaining approval for public issuance of stocks, the issuer shall publish the prospectus during the period of two to five working days before the commencement of the underwriting period.  
The issuer shall provide the prospectus to subscribers. The securities underwriting institution shall keep the prospectus available at its business premises and shall have the obligation to remind subscribers to read the prospectus.  
The prospectus shall be valid for six months, calculated from the date when the prospectus is fully signed. After the prospectus ceases to have effect, the issuance of stocks must be immediately suspended.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公开发行的股票应当由证券经营机构承销。承销包括包销和代销两种方式。  
发行人应当与证券经营机构签署承销协议。承销协议应当载明下列事项：  
(一)当事人的名称、住所及法定代表人的姓名；  
(二)承销方式；  
(三)承销股票的种类、数量、金额及发行价格；  
(四)承销期及起止日期；  
(五)承销付款的日期及方式；  
(六)承销费用的计算、支付方式和日期；  
(七)违约责任；  
(八)其他需要约定的事项。  
证券经营机构收取承销费用的原则，由证监会确定。

**Article 20** Publicly issued stocks shall be underwritten by securities operating institutions. Underwriting includes firm commitment underwriting and best efforts underwriting.  
The issuer shall enter into an underwriting agreement with the securities operating institution. The underwriting agreement shall contain the following particulars:  
(1) The names, domiciles, and names of the legal representatives of the parties;  
(2) The method of underwriting;  
(3) The types, quantity, amount, and offering price of the shares to be underwritten;  
(4) The underwriting period and its commencement and expiration dates;  
(5) The date and method of payment for underwriting;  
(6) The calculation, payment method, and date of underwriting fees;  
(7) Liability for breach of contract;  
(8) Other matters that need to be agreed upon.  
The principles for the collection of underwriting fees by securities operating institutions shall be determined by the CSRC.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　证券经营机构承销股票，应当对招股说明书和其他有关宣传材料的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查；发现含有虚假、严重误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，不得发出要约邀请或者要约；已经发出的，应当立即停止销售活动，并采取相应的补救措施。

**Article 21** . A securities trading institution underwriting shares shall verify the truthfulness, accuracy and completeness of the prospectus and other relevant promotional materials; if it finds any false, seriously misleading statements or material omissions, it shall not issue an invitation for offer or an offer; if already issued, it shall immediately cease sales activities and take appropriate remedial measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　拟公开发行股票的面值总额超过人民币三千万元或者预期销售总金额超过人民币五千万元的，应当由承销团承销。  
承销团由二个以上承销机构组成。主承销商由发行人按照公平竞争的原则，通过竞标或者协商的方式确定。主承销商应当与其他承销商签署承销团协议。

**Article 22** . Where the total face value of shares to be publicly issued exceeds RMB 30 million yuan or the total expected sales amount exceeds RMB 50 million yuan, the shares shall be underwritten by an underwriting syndicate.  
The underwriting syndicate shall consist of two or more underwriting institutions. The lead underwriter shall be determined by the issuer through competitive bidding or negotiation in accordance with the principle of fair competition. The lead underwriter shall sign an underwriting syndicate agreement with other underwriters.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　拟公开发行股票的面值总额超过人民币一亿元或者预期销售总金额超过人民币一亿五千万元的，承销团中的外地承销机构的数目以及总承销量中在外地销售的数量，应当占合理的比例。  
前款所称外地是指发行人所在的省、自治区、直辖市以外的地区。

**Article 23** . Where the total face value of shares to be publicly issued exceeds RMB 100 million yuan or the total expected sales amount exceeds RMB 150 million yuan, the number of non-local underwriting institutions in the underwriting syndicate and the quantity of shares sold in non-local areas out of the total underwriting volume shall account for a reasonable proportion.  
For the purpose of the preceding paragraph, 'non-local areas' mean areas other than the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the issuer is located.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　承销期不得少于十日，不得超过九十日。  
在承销期内，承销机构应当尽力向认购人出售其所承销的股票，不得为本机构保留所承销的股票。  
承销期满后，尚未售出的股票按照承销协议约定的包销或者代销方式分别处理。

**Article 24** . The underwriting period shall not be less than ten days nor exceed ninety days.  
During the underwriting period, the underwriting institution shall use its best efforts to sell the shares it underwrites to subscribers and shall not retain the underwritten shares for itself.  
After the expiration of the underwriting period, unsold shares shall be handled respectively in accordance with the firm commitment or best efforts underwriting methods agreed upon in the underwriting agreement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　承销机构或者其委托机构向社会发放股票认购申请表，不得收取高于认购申请表印制和发放成本的费用，并不得限制认购申请表发放数量。  
认购数量超过拟公开发行的总量时，承销机构应当按照公平原则，采用按比例配售、按比例累退配售或者抽签等方式销售股票。采用抽签方式时，承销机构应当在规定的日期，在公证机关监督下，按照规定的程序，对所有股票认购申请表进行公开抽签，并对中签者销售股票。  
除承销机构或者其委托机构外，任何单位和个人不得发放、转售股票认购申请表。

**Article 25** . When distributing share subscription application forms to the public, an underwriting institution or its entrusted institution shall not charge fees exceeding the cost of printing and distributing the subscription application forms, and shall not restrict the quantity of subscription application forms distributed.  
Where the subscription quantity exceeds the total amount to be publicly issued, the underwriting institution shall sell shares by means such as proportional allocation, proportional regressive allocation, or drawing lots in accordance with the principle of fairness. Where drawing lots is adopted, the underwriting institution shall, on the specified date, under the supervision of a notary public and in accordance with the prescribed procedures, publicly draw lots for all share subscription application forms, and sell shares to the successful draw winners.  
No unit or individual other than the underwriting institution or its entrusted institution may distribute or resell share subscription application forms.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　承销机构应当在承销期满后的十五个工作日内向证监会提交承销情况的书面报告。

**Article 26** . The underwriting institution shall, within fifteen working days after the expiration of the underwriting period, submit a written report on the underwriting situation to the Securities Regulatory Commission.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　证券经营机构在承销期结束后，将其持有的发行人的股票向发行人以外的社会公众作出要约邀请、要约或者销售，应当经证监会批准，按照规定的程序办理。

**Article 27** . Where a securities trading institution, after the conclusion of the underwriting period, makes an invitation for offer, offer or sale to the public other than the issuer of the shares it holds, it shall obtain the approval of the Securities Regulatory Commission and handle the matter in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　发行人用新股票换回其已经发行在外的股票，并且这种交换无直接或者间接的费用发生的，不适用本章规定。

**Article 28** . Where an issuer exchanges new shares for shares that have already been issued and outstanding, and no direct or indirect expenses are incurred in such exchange, the provisions of this Chapter shall not apply.

### 第三章　股票的交易

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　股票交易必须在经证券委批准可以进行股票交易的证券交易场所进行。

**Article 29** . Share trading must be conducted at a securities trading venue approved by the Securities Committee to carry out share trading.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　股份有限公司申请其股票在证券交易所交易，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)其股票已经公开发行；  
(二)发行后的股本总额不少于人民币五千万元；  
(三)持有面值人民币一千元以上的个人股东人数不少于一千人，个人持有的股票面值总额不少于人民币一千万元；  
(四)公司有最近三年连续盈利的记录；原有企业改组设立股份有限公司的，原企业有最近三年连续盈利的记录，但是新设立的股份有限公司除外；  
(五)证券委规定的其他条件。

**Article 30** . A joint stock limited company applying for trading of its shares on a stock exchange shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Its shares have been publicly issued;  
(2) The total share capital after issuance is not less than RMB 50 million yuan;  
(3) The number of individual shareholders each holding shares with a face value of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan is not less than 1,000 persons, and the total face value of shares held by individuals is not less than RMB 10 million yuan;  
(4) The company has a record of continuous profitability for the most recent three years; for a company reorganized from an existing enterprise into a joint stock limited company, the original enterprise has a record of continuous profitability for the most recent three years, except for a newly established joint stock limited company;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公开发行股票符合前条规定条件的股份有限公司，申请其股票在证券交易所交易，应当向证券交易所的上市委员会提出申请；上市委员会应当自收到申请之日起二十个工作日内作出审批，确定具体上市时间。审批文件报证监会备案，并抄报证券委。

**Article 31** . A joint stock limited company whose public issuance of shares meets the conditions specified in the preceding Article, when applying for trading of its shares on a stock exchange, shall submit an application to the listing committee of the stock exchange; the listing committee shall, within twenty working days from the date of receipt of the application, make an examination and approval decision and determine the specific listing date. The examination and approval documents shall be submitted to the Securities Regulatory Commission for record, and a copy shall be submitted to the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　股份有限公司申请其股票在证券交易所交易，应当向证券交易所的上市委员会送交下列文件：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)公司登记注册文件；  
(三)股票公开发行的批准文件；  
(四)经会计师事务所审计的公司近三年或者成立以来的财务报告和由二名以上的注册会计师及其所在事务所签字、盖章的审计报告；  
(五)证券交易所会员的推荐书；  
(六)最近一次的招股说明书；  
(七)证券交易所要求的其他文件。

**Article 32** . A joint stock limited company applying for trading of its shares on a stock exchange shall submit the following documents to the listing committee of the stock exchange:  
(1) An application;  
(2) The company's registration and incorporation documents;  
(3) The approval documents for the public issuance of shares;  
(4) The financial reports of the company for the most recent three years or since its establishment, audited by an accounting firm, and the audit report signed and sealed by two or more certified public accountants and their firm;  
(5) A recommendation letter from a member of the stock exchange;  
(6) The most recent prospectus;  
(7) Other documents required by the stock exchange.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　股票获准在证券交易所交易后，上市公司应当公布上市公告并将[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)所列文件予以公开。

**Article 33** . After the shares are approved for trading on a stock exchange, the listed company shall publish a listing announcement and make public the documents listed in Article 32 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　上市公告的内容，除应当包括[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的招股说明书的主要内容外，还应当包括下列事项：  
(一)股票获准在证券交易所交易的日期和批准文号；  
(二)股票发行情况、股权结构和最大的十名股东的名单及持股数额；  
(三)公司创立大会或者股东大会同意公司股票在证券交易所交易的决议；  
(四)董事、监事和高级管理人员简历及其持有本公司证券的情况；  
(五)公司近三年或者成立以来的经营业绩和财务状况以及下一年的盈利预测文件；  
(六)证券交易所要求载明的其他事项。

**Article 34** . The contents of the listing announcement shall, in addition to including the main contents of the prospectus as specified in Article 15 of these Regulations, also include the following matters:  
(1) The date and approval document number for the approval of the shares for trading on the stock exchange;  
(2) The share issuance situation, shareholding structure, and the list of the ten largest shareholders and their shareholding amounts;  
(3) The resolution of the company's inaugural meeting or shareholders' general meeting approving the trading of the company's shares on the stock exchange;  
(4) The resumes of the directors, supervisors and senior officers and their holdings of securities of the company;  
(5) The company's business performance and financial condition for the most recent three years or since its establishment, and the profit forecast document for the next year;  
(6) Other matters required to be stated by the stock exchange.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　为上市公司出具文件的注册会计师及其所在事务所、专业评估人员及其所在机构、律师及其所在事务所，在履行职责时，应当按照本行业公认的业务标准和道德规范，对其出具文件内容的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查和验证。

**Article 35** . Certified public accountants and their firms, professional appraisers and their institutions, and lawyers and their firms that issue documents for a listed company shall, when performing their duties, verify and confirm the truthfulness, accuracy and completeness of the contents of the documents they issue in accordance with the professional standards and ethics recognized in their respective industries.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家拥有的股份的转让必须经国家有关部门批准，具体办法另行规定。  
国家拥有的股份的转让，不得损害国家拥有的股份的权益。

**Article 36** . The transfer of shares owned by the State must be approved by the relevant State authorities, and specific measures shall be formulated separately.  
The transfer of shares owned by the State shall not harm the rights and interests of shares owned by the State.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　证券交易场所，证券保管、清算、过户、登记机构和证券经营机构，应当保证外地委托人与本地委托人享有同等待遇，不得歧视或者限制外地委托人。

**Article 37** . Securities trading venues, securities custody, clearing, transfer, and registration institutions, and securities trading institutions shall ensure that non-local principals enjoy equal treatment with local principals, and shall not discriminate against or restrict non-local principals.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　股份有限公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员和持有公司5%以上有表决权股份的法人股东，将其所持有的公司股票在买入后六个月内卖出或者在卖出后六个月内买入，由此获得的利润归公司所有。  
前款规定适用于持有公司5%以上有表决权股份的法人股东的董事、监事和高级管理人员。

**Article 38** . Directors, supervisors, senior officers of a joint stock limited company and legal person shareholders holding 5% or more of the voting shares of the company who sell the company's shares held by them within six months after purchase, or repurchase within six months after sale, the profits derived therefrom shall belong to the company.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to the directors, supervisors and senior officers of legal person shareholders holding 5% or more of the voting shares of the company.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　证券业从业人员、证券业管理人员和国家规定禁止买卖股票的其他人员，不得直接或者间接持有、买卖股票，但是买卖经批准发行的投资基金证券除外。

**Article 39** . Securities industry professionals, securities industry administrators, and other persons prohibited by State regulations from trading shares shall not directly or indirectly hold or trade shares, except for trading in approved investment fund securities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　为股票发行出具审计报告、资产评估报告、法律意见书等文件的有关专业人员，在该股票承销期内和期满后六个月内，不得购买或者持有该股票。  
为上市公司出具审计报告、资产评估报告、法律意见书等文件的有关专业人员，在其审计报告、资产评估报告、法律意见书等文件成为公开信息前，不得购买或者持有该公司的股票；成为公开信息后的五个工作日内，也不得购买该公司的股票。

**Article 40** . Relevant professionals who issue audit reports, asset appraisal reports, legal opinions, and other documents for a share issuance shall not purchase or hold such shares during the underwriting period and within six months after its expiration.  
Relevant professionals who issue audit reports, asset appraisal reports, legal opinions, and other documents for a listed company shall not purchase or hold the company's shares before such audit reports, asset appraisal reports, legal opinions, and other documents become public information; nor shall they purchase the company's shares within five working days after such documents become public information.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　未依照国家有关规定经过批准，股份有限公司不得购回其发行在外的股票。

**Article 41** A company limited by shares shall not repurchase its outstanding shares without approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　未经证券委批准，任何人不得对股票及其指数的期权、期货进行交易。

**Article 42** Without approval of the Securities Commission, no person shall trade in options or futures of stocks or stock indices.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　任何金融机构不得为股票交易提供贷款。

**Article 43** No financial institution shall provide loans for stock trading.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　证券经营机构不得将客户的股票借与他人或者作为担保物。

**Article 44** A securities operation institution shall not lend a client's stocks to others or use them as collateral.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　经批准从事证券自营、代理和投资基金管理业务中二项以上业务的证券经营机构，应当将不同业务的经营人员、资金、账目分开。

**Article 45** A securities operation institution that engages in two or more of the businesses of securities proprietary trading, agency trading, and investment fund management with approval shall separate the operating personnel, funds, and accounts of different businesses.

### 第四章　上市公司的收购

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　任何个人不得持有一个上市公司5‰以上的发行在外的普通股；超过的部分，由公司在征得证监会同意后，按照原买入价格和市场价格中较低的一种价格收购。但是，因公司发行在外的普通股总量减少，致使个人持有该公司5‰以上发行在外的普通股的，超过的部分在合理期限内不予收购。  
外国和香港、澳门、台湾地区的个人持有的公司发行的人民币特种股票和在境外发行的股票，不受前款规定的5‰的限制。

**Article 46** No individual shall hold more than 5‰ of the outstanding ordinary shares of a listed company; the excess portion shall be purchased by the company, with the consent of the Securities Regulatory Commission, at the lower of the original purchase price and the market price. However, where the reduction of the total amount of outstanding ordinary shares of the company results in an individual holding more than 5‰ of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company, the excess portion shall not be purchased within a reasonable period.  
The Renminbi special shares issued by the company held by individuals from foreign countries and the regions of Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan, and shares issued outside China, shall not be subject to the 5‰ limit provided in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　任何法人直接或者间接持有一个上市公司发行在外的普通股达到5%时，应当自该事实发生之日起三个工作日内，向该公司、证券交易场所和证监会作出书面报告并公告。但是，因公司发行在外的普通股总量减少，致使法人持有该公司5%以上发行在外的普通股的，在合理期限内不受上述限制。  
任何法人持有一个上市公司5%以上的发行在外的普通股后，其持有该种股票的增减变化每达到该种股票发行在外总额的2%时，应当自该事实发生之日起三个工作日内，向该公司、证券交易场所和证监会作出书面报告并公告。  
法人在依照前两款规定作出报告并公告之日起二个工作日内和作出报告前，不得再行直接或者间接买入或者卖出该种股票。

**Article 47** When a legal person directly or indirectly holds 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of a listed company, it shall, within three working days from the date on which such fact occurs, make a written report to the company, the securities exchange venue, and the Securities Regulatory Commission and announce it. However, where the reduction of the total amount of outstanding ordinary shares of the company results in a legal person holding more than 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company, the above restriction shall not apply within a reasonable period.  
After any legal person holds more than 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of a listed company, each time the increase or decrease in its holdings of such shares reaches 2% of the total amount of such outstanding shares, it shall, within three working days from the date on which such fact occurs, make a written report to the company, the securities exchange venue, and the Securities Regulatory Commission and announce it.  
A legal person shall not directly or indirectly buy or sell such shares within two working days after making the report and announcement in accordance with the preceding two paragraphs and before making the report.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　发起人以外的任何法人直接或者间接持有一个上市公司发行在外的普通股达到30%时，应当自该事实发生之日起四十五个工作日内，向该公司所有股票持有人发出收购要约，按照下列价格中较高的一种价格，以货币付款方式购买股票：  
(一)在收购要约发出前十二个月内收购要约人购买该种股票所支付的最高价格；  
(二)在收购要约发出前三十个工作日内该种股票的平均市场价格。  
前款持有人发出收购要约前，不得再行购买该种股票。

**Article 48** When any legal person other than the promoters directly or indirectly holds 30% of the outstanding ordinary shares of a listed company, it shall, within forty-five working days from the date on which such fact occurs, issue a takeover offer to all shareholders of the company to purchase the shares by cash payment at the higher of the following prices:  
(1) the highest price paid by the offeror for purchasing such shares within the twelve months before the issuance of the takeover offer;  
(2) the average market price of such shares within the thirty working days before the issuance of the takeover offer.  
The holder mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall not purchase such shares again before issuing the takeover offer.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　收购要约人在发出收购要约前应当向证监会作出有关收购的书面报告；在发出收购要约的同时应当向受要约人、证券交易场所提供本身情况的说明和与该要约有关的全部信息，并保证材料真实、准确、完整，不产生误导。  
收购要约的有效期不得少于三十个工作日，自收购要约发出之日起计算。自收购要约发出之日起三十个工作日内，收购要约人不得撤回其收购要约。

**Article 49** An offeror shall make a written report on the takeover to the Securities Regulatory Commission before issuing a takeover offer; at the same time as issuing the takeover offer, it shall provide the offerees and the securities exchange venue with a description of its own situation and all information related to the offer, and guarantee that the materials are true, accurate, and complete and not misleading.  
The validity period of a takeover offer shall not be less than thirty working days, calculated from the date of issuance of the takeover offer. Within thirty working days from the date of issuance of the takeover offer, the offeror shall not withdraw its takeover offer.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　收购要约的全部条件适用于同种股票的所有持有人。

**Article 50** All conditions of a takeover offer shall apply to all holders of the same type of shares.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　收购要约期满，收购要约人持有的普通股未达到该公司发行在外的普通股总数的5%的，为收购失败；收购要约人除发出新的收购要约外，其以后每年购买的该公司发行在外的普通股，不得超过该公司发行在外的普通股总数的5%。  
收购要约期满，收购要约人持有的普通股达到该公司发行在外的普通股总数的75%以上的，该公司应当在证券交易所终止交易。  
收购要约人要约购买股票的总数低于预受要约的总数时，收购要约人应当按照比例从所有预受收购要约的受要约人中购买该股票。  
收购要约期满，收购要约人持有的股票达到该公司股票总数的90%时，其余股东有权以同等条件向收购要约人强制出售其股票。

**Article 51** Upon expiration of a takeover offer, if the ordinary shares held by the offeror do not reach 5% of the total number of outstanding ordinary shares of the company, the takeover fails; except that the offeror may issue a new takeover offer, its purchase of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company in each subsequent year shall not exceed 5% of the total number of outstanding ordinary shares of the company.  
Upon expiration of a takeover offer, if the ordinary shares held by the offeror reach 75% or more of the total number of outstanding ordinary shares of the company, the company shall terminate trading on the stock exchange.  
Where the total number of shares that the offeror offers to purchase is lower than the total number of shares that have been accepted by the offerees, the offeror shall purchase the shares from all offerees who have accepted the offer on a pro rata basis.  
Upon expiration of a takeover offer, if the shares held by the offeror reach 90% of the total number of shares of the company, the remaining shareholders shall have the right to compulsorily sell their shares to the offeror on the same conditions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　收购要约发出后，主要要约条件改变的，收购要约人应当立即通知所有受要约人。通知可以采用新闻发布会、登报或者其他传播形式。  
收购要约人在要约期内及要约期满后三十个工作日内，不得以要约规定以外的任何条件购买该种股票。  
预受收购要约的受要约人有权在收购要约失效前撤回对该要约的预受。

**Article 52** After a takeover offer is issued, if the main conditions of the offer change, the offeror shall immediately notify all offerees. The notice may be given by press conference, newspaper publication, or other forms of dissemination.  
The offeror shall not purchase such shares on any conditions other than those specified in the offer during the offer period and within thirty working days after the expiration of the offer period.  
Offerees who have accepted a takeover offer shall have the right to withdraw their acceptance of the offer before the offer lapses.

### 第五章　保管、清算和过户

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　股票发行采取记名式。发行人可以发行簿记券式股票，也可以发行实物券式股票。簿记券式股票名册应当由证监会指定的机构保管。实物券式股票集中保管的，也应当由证监会指定的机构保管。

**Article 53** Shares shall be issued in registered form. Issuers may issue book-entry shares or physical share certificates. The register of book-entry shares shall be kept in custody by an institution designated by the Securities Regulatory Commission. If physical share certificates are kept in centralized custody, they shall also be kept in custody by an institution designated by the Securities Regulatory Commission.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　未经股票持有人的书面同意，股票保管机构不得将该持有人的股票借与他人或者作为担保物。

**Article 54** Without the written consent of the shareholder, a share custodian institution shall not lend the shareholder's shares to others or use them as collateral.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　证券清算机构应当根据方便、安全、公平的原则，制定股票清算、交割的业务规则和内部管理规则。  
证券清算机构应当按照公平的原则接纳会员。

**Article 55** A securities clearing institution shall formulate business rules and internal management rules for the clearing and settlement of shares in accordance with the principles of convenience, safety, and fairness.  
A securities clearing institution shall admit members on the principle of fairness.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　证券保管、清算、过户、登记机构应当接受证监会监管。

**Article 56** Securities custodians, clearing institutions, transfer agents, and registration institutions shall be subject to the supervision of the Securities Regulatory Commission.

### 第六章　上市公司的信息披露

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　上市公司应当向证监会、证券交易场所提供下列文件：  
(一)在每个会计年度的前六个月结束后六十日内提交中期报告；  
(二)在每个会计年度结束后一百二十日内提交经注册会计师审计的年度报告。  
中期报告和年度报告应当符合国家的会计制度和证监会的有关规定，由上市公司授权的董事或者经理签字，并由上市公司盖章。

**Article 57** A listed company shall provide the following documents to the Securities Regulatory Commission and the securities exchange venue:  
(1) an interim report submitted within sixty days after the end of the first six months of each fiscal year;  
(2) an annual report submitted within one hundred and twenty days after the end of each fiscal year, audited by certified public accountants.  
The interim report and the annual report shall conform to the national accounting system and the relevant provisions of the Securities Regulatory Commission, be signed by an authorized director or manager of the listed company, and be sealed by the listed company.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　[本条例第五十七条](#art-57)所列中期报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)公司财务报告；  
(二)公司管理部门对公司财务状况和经营成果的分析；  
(三)涉及公司的重大诉讼事项；  
(四)公司发行在外股票的变动情况；  
(五)公司提交给有表决权的股东审议的重要事项；  
(六)证监会要求载明的其他内容。

**Article 58** The interim report referred to in Article 57 of these Regulations shall include the following contents:  
(1) the financial report of the company;  
(2) the analysis of the company's financial condition and operating results by the company's management;  
(3) major litigation matters involving the company;  
(4) changes in the company's outstanding shares;  
(5) important matters submitted by the company to the shareholders entitled to vote for consideration;  
(6) other contents required by the Securities Regulatory Commission.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　[本条例第五十七条](#art-57)所列年度报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)公司简况；  
(二)公司的主要产品或者主要服务项目简况；  
(三)公司所在行业简况；  
(四)公司所拥有的重要的工厂、矿山、房地产等财产简况；  
(五)公司发行在外股票的情况，包括持有公司5%以上发行在外普通股的股东的名单及前十名最大的股东的名单；  
(六)公司股东数量；  
(七)公司董事、监事和高级管理人员简况、持股情况和报酬；  
(八)公司及其关联人一览表和简况；  
(九)公司近三年或者成立以来的财务信息摘要；  
(十)公司管理部门对公司财务状况和经营成果的分析；  
(十一)公司发行在外债券的变动情况；  
(十二)涉及公司的重大诉讼事项；  
(十三)经注册会计师审计的公司最近二个年度的比较财务报告及其附表、注释；该上市公司为控股公司的，还应当包括最近二个年度的比较合并财务报告；  
(十四)证监会要求载明的其他内容。

**Article 59** The annual report referred to in Article 57 of these Regulations shall include the following contents:  
(1) a brief introduction to the company;  
(2) a brief introduction to the company's main products or main services;  
(3) a brief introduction to the industry in which the company operates;  
(4) a brief introduction to the important plants, mines, real estate and other properties owned by the company;  
(5) information on the company's outstanding shares, including the list of shareholders holding more than 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company and the list of the top ten largest shareholders;  
(6) the number of shareholders of the company;  
(7) a brief introduction to the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the company, their shareholdings and remuneration;  
(8) a list and brief introduction to the company and its related persons;  
(9) a summary of financial information of the company for the latest three years or since its establishment;  
(10) the analysis of the company's financial condition and operating results by the company's management;  
(11) changes in the company's outstanding bonds;  
(12) major litigation matters involving the company;  
(13) comparative financial reports for the latest two fiscal years of the company audited by certified public accountants, together with their schedules and notes; if the listed company is a holding company, it shall also include comparative consolidated financial reports for the latest two fiscal years;  
(14) other contents required by the Securities Regulatory Commission.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　发生可能对上市公司股票的市场价格产生较大影响、而投资人尚未得知的重大事件时，上市公司应当立即将有关该重大事件的报告提交证券交易场所和证监会，并向社会公布，说明事件的实质。但是，上市公司有充分理由认为向社会公布该重大事件会损害上市公司的利益，且不公布也不会导致股票市场价格重大变动的，经证券交易场所同意，可以不予公布。  
前款所称重大事件包括下列情况：  
(一)公司订立重要合同，该合同可能对公司的资产、负债、权益和经营成果中的一项或者多项产生显著影响；  
(二)公司的经营政策或者经营项目发生重大变化；  
(三)公司发生重大的投资行为或者购置金额较大的长期资产的行为；  
(四)公司发生重大债务；  
(五)公司未能归还到期重大债务的违约情况；  
(六)公司发生重大经营性或者非经营性亏损；  
(七)公司资产遭受重大损失；  
(八)公司生产经营环境发生重要变化；  
(九)新颁布的法律、法规、政策、规章等，可能对公司的经营有显著影响；  
(十)董事长、30%以上的董事或者总经理发生变动；  
(十一)持有公司5%以上的发行在外的普通股的股东，其持有该种股票的增减变化每达到该种股票发行在外总额的2%以上的事实；  
(十二)涉及公司的重大诉讼事项；  
(十三)公司进入清算、破产状态。

**Article 60** When a major event occurs that may have a significant impact on the market price of a listed company's shares and that investors have not yet learned of, the listed company shall immediately submit a report on such major event to the securities exchange venue and the Securities Regulatory Commission and announce it to the public, explaining the essence of the event. However, if the listed company has sufficient reason to believe that disclosing the major event to the public would harm the interests of the listed company and that non-disclosure would not cause major fluctuations in the stock market price, it may, with the consent of the securities exchange venue, choose not to disclose.  
"Major events" referred to in the preceding paragraph include the following circumstances:  
(1) entering into an important contract by the company, which may have a significant effect on one or more of the company's assets, liabilities, equity, and operating results;  
(2) significant changes in the company's business policies or business projects;  
(3) major investment actions by the company or actions to purchase long-term assets with relatively large amounts;  
(4) incurrence of major debts by the company;  
(5) default by the company on major debts due;  
(6) major operating or non-operating losses suffered by the company;  
(7) major losses of the company's assets;  
(8) important changes in the company's production and operation environment;  
(9) newly promulgated laws, regulations, policies, rules, etc., which may have a significant impact on the company's operations;  
(10) changes in the chairman of the board, more than 30% of the directors, or the general manager;  
(11) the fact that a shareholder holding more than 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company has an increase or decrease in its holdings of such shares that reaches 2% or more of the total amount of such outstanding shares;  
(12) major litigation matters involving the company;  
(13) the company enters into a state of liquidation or bankruptcy.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　在任何公共传播媒介中出现的消息可能对上市公司股票的市场价格产生误导性影响时，该公司知悉后应当立即对该消息作出公开澄清。

**Article 61** Where any information appearing in any public communication medium may have a misleading effect on the market price of shares of a listed company, the company shall, immediately after becoming aware of such information, make a public clarification thereof.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　上市公司的董事、监事和高级管理人员持有该公司普通股的，应当向证监会、证券交易场所和该公司报告其持股情况；持股情况发生变化的，应当自该变化发生之日起十个工作日内向证监会、证券交易场所和该公司作出报告。  
前款所列人员在辞职或者离职后六个月内负有依照本条规定作出报告的义务。

**Article 62** Where directors, supervisors and senior officers of a listed company hold ordinary shares of the company, they shall report their shareholding status to the CSRC, the securities trading venue and the company; if the shareholding status changes, they shall make a report to the CSRC, the securities trading venue and the company within ten working days from the date on which such change occurs.  
The persons listed in the preceding paragraph shall have the obligation to make a report in accordance with this Article within six months after resignation or departure from office.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　上市公司应当将要求公布的信息刊登在证监会指定的全国性报刊上。  
上市公司在依照前款规定公布信息的同时，可以在证券交易场所指定的地方报刊上公布有关信息。

**Article 63** A listed company shall publish the information required to be disclosed in the national newspapers and periodicals designated by the CSRC.  
While publishing information in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, a listed company may also publish the relevant information in local newspapers and periodicals designated by the securities trading venue.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　证监会应当将上市公司及其董事、监事、高级管理人员和持有公司5%以上的发行在外的普通股的股东所提交的报告、公告及其他文件及时向社会公开，供投资人查阅。  
证监会要求披露的全部信息均为公开信息，但是下列信息除外：  
(一) 法律、法规予以保护并允许不予披露的商业秘密；  
(二) 证监会在调查违法行为过程中获得的非公开信息和文件；  
(三) 根据有关法律、法规规定可以不予披露的其他信息和文件。

**Article 64** The CSRC shall promptly make public the reports, announcements and other documents submitted by a listed company, its directors, supervisors, senior officers, and shareholders holding more than 5% of the outstanding ordinary shares of the company, for inspection by investors.  
All information required to be disclosed by the CSRC shall be public information, except for the following information:  
(1) trade secrets protected by laws and regulations and permitted to be undisclosed;  
(2) non-public information and documents obtained by the CSRC in the course of investigating illegal acts;  
(3) other information and documents that may be undisclosed in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　股票持有人可以授权他人代理行使其同意权或者投票权。但是，任何人在征集二十五人以上的同意权或者投票权时，应当遵守证监会有关信息披露和作出报告的规定。

**Article 65** A shareholder may authorize another person to exercise its consent rights or voting rights on its behalf. However, any person soliciting consent rights or voting rights from more than twenty-five persons shall comply with the CSRC's provisions on information disclosure and reporting.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　上市公司除应当向证监会、证券交易场所提交本章规定的报告、公告、信息及文件外，还应当按照证券交易场所的规定提交有关报告、公告、信息及文件，并向所有股东公开。

**Article 66** In addition to submitting the reports, announcements, information and documents prescribed in this Chapter to the CSRC and the securities trading venue, a listed company shall also submit relevant reports, announcements, information and documents in accordance with the rules of the securities trading venue, and make them public to all shareholders.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　[本条例第五十七条](#art-57)至第六十五条的规定，适用于已经公开发行股票，其股票并未在证券交易场所交易的股份有限公司。

**Article 67** The provisions of Articles 57 through 65 of these Regulations shall apply to joint stock companies limited that have already publicly issued shares but whose shares are not traded on a securities trading venue.

### 第七章　调查和处罚

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　对违反本条例规定的单位和个人，证监会有权进行调查或者会同国家有关部门进行调查；重大的案件，由证券委组织调查。

**Article 68** With respect to units and individuals that violate the provisions of these Regulations, the CSRC shall have the authority to conduct investigations or conduct investigations jointly with relevant state departments; for major cases, the Securities Committee shall organize the investigation.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　证监会可以对证券经营机构的业务活动进行检查。

**Article 69** The CSRC may inspect the business activities of securities operating institutions.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　股份有限公司违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，根据不同情况，单处或者并处警告、责令退还非法所筹股款、没收非法所得、罚款；情节严重的，停止其发行股票资格：  
(一)未经批准发行或者变相发行股票的；  
(二)以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获准发行股票或者获准其股票在证券交易场所交易的；  
(三)未按照规定方式、范围发行股票，或者在招股说明书失效后销售股票的；  
(四)未经批准购回其发行在外的股票的。  
对前款所列行为负有直接责任的股份有限公司的董事、监事和高级管理人员，给予警告或者处以三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 70** Where a joint stock company limited violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, depending on the circumstances, it shall be given a warning, ordered to return the illegally raised share funds, confiscated of illegal gains, and/or fined; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification to issue shares shall be suspended:  
(1) issuing shares or issuing shares in a disguised form without approval;  
(2) obtaining approval to issue shares or to have its shares traded on a securities trading venue by deception or other improper means;  
(3) failing to issue shares in the prescribed manner or scope, or selling shares after the prospectus has ceased to have effect;  
(4) repurchasing its outstanding shares without approval.  
Directors, supervisors and senior officers of the joint stock company limited who are directly responsible for the acts listed in the preceding paragraph shall be given a warning or fined not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 300,000.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　证券经营机构违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，根据不同情况，单处或者并处警告、没收非法获取的股票和其他非法所得、罚款；情节严重的，限制、暂停其证券经营业务或者撤销其证券经营业务许可：  
(一)未按照规定的时间、程序、方式承销股票的；  
(二)未按照规定发放股票认购申请表的；  
(三)将客户的股票借与他人或者作为担保物的；  
(四)收取不合理的佣金和其他费用的；  
(五)以客户的名义为本机构买卖股票的；  
(六)挪用客户保证金的；  
(七)在代理客户买卖股票活动中，与客户分享股票交易的利润或者分担股票交易的损失，或者向客户提供避免损失的保证的；  
(八)为股票交易提供融资的。  
对前款所列行为负有责任的证券经营机构的主管人员和直接责任人员，给予警告或者处以三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Where a securities operating institution violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, depending on the circumstances, it shall be given a warning, confiscated of illegally obtained shares and other illegal gains, and/or fined; if the circumstances are serious, its securities business shall be restricted or suspended, or its securities business permit shall be revoked:  
(1) underwriting shares not in accordance with the prescribed time, procedures or manner;  
(2) failing to distribute share subscription application forms in accordance with the prescribed manner;  
(3) lending clients' shares to others or using them as collateral;  
(4) charging unreasonable commissions or other fees;  
(5) buying or selling shares in the name of clients for the institution's own account;  
(6) misappropriating clients' margin deposits;  
(7) in the activity of buying or selling shares as agent for clients, sharing profits or losses from share transactions with clients, or providing clients with guarantees against losses;  
(8) providing financing for share transactions.  
Persons in charge and persons directly responsible of the securities operating institution who are responsible for the acts listed in the preceding paragraph shall be given a warning or fined not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 300,000.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　内幕人员和以不正当手段获取内幕信息的其他人员违反本条例规定，泄露内幕信息、根据内幕信息买卖股票或者向他人提出买卖股票的建议的，根据不同情况，没收非法获取的股票和其他非法所得，并处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。  
证券业从业人员、证券业管理人员和国家规定禁止买卖股票的其他人员违反本条例规定，直接或者间接持有、买卖股票的，除责令限期出售其持有的股票外，根据不同情况，单处或者并处警告、没收非法所得、五千元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 72** Where an insider or any other person who has obtained inside information by improper means violates the provisions of these Regulations by disclosing inside information, buying or selling shares on the basis of inside information, or making recommendations to others to buy or sell shares, depending on the circumstances, the illegally obtained shares and other illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed.  
Where a securities industry practitioner, a securities industry administrator, or any other person prohibited by state regulations from buying or selling shares violates the provisions of these Regulations by directly or indirectly holding or buying or selling shares, in addition to being ordered to sell the shares held within a specified time limit, depending on the circumstances, they shall be given a warning, confiscated of illegal gains, and/or fined not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 50,000.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　会计师事务所、资产评估机构和律师事务所违反本条例规定，出具的文件有虚假、严重误导性内容或者有重大遗漏的，根据不同情况，单处或者并处警告、没收非法所得、罚款；情节严重的，暂停其从事证券业务或者撤销其从事证券业务许可。  
对前款所列行为负有直接责任的注册会计师、专业评估人员和律师，给予警告或者处以三万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，撤销其从事证券业务的资格。

**Article 73** Where an accounting firm, an asset appraisal institution or a law firm violates the provisions of these Regulations by issuing documents with false, seriously misleading contents or material omissions, depending on the circumstances, it shall be given a warning, confiscated of illegal gains, and/or fined; if the circumstances are serious, its engagement in securities business shall be suspended or its permit to engage in securities business shall be revoked.  
Certified public accountants, professional appraisers and lawyers who are directly responsible for the acts listed in the preceding paragraph shall be given a warning or fined not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 300,000; if the circumstances are serious, their qualification to engage in securities business shall be revoked.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　任何单位和个人违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，根据不同情况，单处或者并处警告、没收非法获取的股票和其他非法所得、罚款：  
(一)在证券委批准可以进行股票交易的证券交易场所之外进行股票交易的；  
(二)在股票发行、交易过程中，作出虚假、严重误导性陈述或者遗漏重大信息的；  
(三)通过合谋或者集中资金操纵股票市场价格，或者以散布谣言等手段影响股票发行、交易的；  
(四)为制造股票的虚假价格与他人串通，不转移股票的所有权或者实际控制，虚买虚卖的；  
(五)出售或者要约出售其并不持有的股票，扰乱股票市场秩序的；  
(六)利用职权或者其他不正当手段，索取或者强行买卖股票，或者协助他人买卖股票的；  
(七)未经批准对股票及其指数的期权、期货进行交易的；  
(八)未按照规定履行有关文件和信息的报告、公开、公布义务的；  
(九)伪造、篡改或者销毁与股票发行、交易有关的业务记录、财务账簿等文件的；  
(十)其他非法从事股票发行、交易及其相关活动的。  
股份有限公司有前款所列行为，情节严重的，可以停止其发行股票的资格；证券经营机构有前款所列行为，情节严重的，可以限制、暂停其证券经营业务或者撤销其证券经营业务许可。

**Article 74** Where any unit or individual violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, depending on the circumstances, it shall be given a warning, confiscated of illegally obtained shares and other illegal gains, and/or fined:  
(1) conducting share transactions outside the securities trading venues approved by the Securities Committee for share trading;  
(2) making false, seriously misleading statements or omitting material information in the process of share issuance or trading;  
(3) manipulating the market price of shares through collusion or concentration of funds, or affecting share issuance or trading by spreading rumors or other means;  
(4) colluding with others to create a false price of shares by fictitious buying and selling without transferring the ownership or actual control of the shares;  
(5) selling or offering to sell shares that the seller does not hold, thereby disrupting the order of the share market;  
(6) using official powers or other improper means to demand or forcefully buy or sell shares, or assisting others in buying or selling shares;  
(7) trading in options or futures on shares or their indices without approval;  
(8) failing to fulfill the obligations of reporting, disclosure or publication of relevant documents and information in accordance with the provisions;  
(9) forging, tampering with or destroying business records, financial books and other documents related to share issuance or trading;  
(10) other illegal activities in share issuance, trading and related activities.  
Where a joint stock company limited commits an act listed in the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, its qualification to issue shares may be suspended; where a securities operating institution commits an act listed in the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, its securities business may be restricted or suspended, or its securities business permit may be revoked.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　[本条例第七十条](#art-70)、第七十一条、第七十二条、第七十四条规定的处罚，由证券委指定的机构决定；重大的案件的处罚，报证券委决定。[本条例第七十三条](#art-73)规定的处罚，由有关部门在各自的职权范围内决定。

**Article 75** Penalties provided for in Articles 70, 71, 72 and 74 of these Regulations shall be decided by the institution designated by the Securities Committee; penalties for major cases shall be reported to the Securities Committee for decision. Penalties provided for in Article 73 of these Regulations shall be decided by the relevant departments within their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　上市公司和证券交易所或者其他证券业自律性管理组织的会员及其工作人员违反本条例规定，除依照本条例规定给予行政处罚外，由证券交易所或者其他证券业自律性管理组织根据章程或者自律准则给予制裁。

**Article 76** Where a listed company, a stock exchange or any other self-regulatory management organization of the securities industry, or their members or staff, violate the provisions of these Regulations, in addition to being given administrative penalties in accordance with these Regulations, sanctions shall be imposed by the stock exchange or other self-regulatory management organization of the securities industry in accordance with its articles of association or self-regulatory rules.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本条例规定，给他人造成损失的，应当依法承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 77** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes loss to others shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第八章　争议的仲裁

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　与股票的发行或者交易有关的争议，当事人可以按照协议的约定向仲裁机构申请调解、仲裁。

**Article 79** For disputes related to the issuance or trading of shares, the parties may apply to an arbitration institution for mediation or arbitration in accordance with the provisions of the agreement.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　证券经营机构之间以及证券经营机构与证券交易场所之间因股票的发行或者交易引起的争议，应当由证券委批准设立或者指定的仲裁机构调解、仲裁。

**Article 80** Disputes arising between securities operating institutions or between securities operating institutions and securities trading venues due to the issuance or trading of shares shall be mediated or arbitrated by an arbitration institution approved or designated by the Securities Committee.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)“股票”是指股份有限公司发行的、表示其股东按其持有的股份享受权益和承担义务的可转让的书面凭证。  
“簿记券式股票”是指发行人按照证监会规定的统一格式制作的、记载股东权益的书面名册。  
“实物券式股票”是指发行人在证监会指定的印制机构统一印制的书面股票。  
(二)“发行在外的普通股”是指公司库存以外的普通股。  
(三)“公开发行”是指发行人通过证券经营机构向发行人以外的社会公众就发行人的股票作出的要约邀请、要约或者销售行为。  
(四)“承销”是指证券经营机构依照协议包销或者代销发行人所发行股票的行为。  
(五)“承销机构”是指以包销或者代销方式为发行人销售股票的证券经营机构。  
(六)“包销”是指承销机构在发行期结束后将未售出的股票全部买下的承销方式。  
(七)“代销”是指承销机构代理发售股票，在发行期结束后，将未售出的股票全部退还给发行人或者包销人的承销方式。  
(八)“公布”是指将本条例规定应当予以披露的文件刊载在证监会指定的报刊上的行为。  
(九)“公开”是指将本条例规定应当予以披露的文件备置于发行人及其证券承销机构的营业地和证监会，供投资人查阅的行为。  
(十)“要约”是指向特定人或者非特定人发出购买或者销售某种股票的口头的或者书面的意思表示。  
(十一)“要约邀请”是指建议他人向自己发出要约的意思表示。  
(十二)“预受”是指受要约人同意接受要约的初步意思表示，在要约期满前不构成承诺。  
(十三)“上市公司”是指其股票获准在证券交易场所交易的股份有限公司。  
(十四)“内幕人员”是指任何由于持有发行人的股票，或者在发行人或者与发行人有密切联系的企业中担任董事、监事、高级管理人员，或者由于其会员地位、管理地位、监督地位和职业地位，或者作为雇员、专业顾问履行职务，能够接触或者获取内幕信息的人员。  
(十五)“内幕信息”是指有关发行人、证券经营机构、有收购意图的法人、证券监督管理机构、证券业自律性管理组织以及与其有密切联系的人员所知悉的尚未公开的可能影响股票市场价格的重大信息。  
(十六)“证券交易场所”是指经批准设立的、进行证券交易的证券交易所和证券交易报价系统。  
(十七)“证券业管理人员”是指证券管理部门和证券业自律性管理组织的工作人员。  
(十八)“证券业从业人员”是指从事证券发行、交易及其他相关业务的机构的工作人员。

**Article 81** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Stock" means a transferable written certificate issued by a joint stock limited company, representing that its shareholders enjoy rights and assume obligations based on the shares they hold.  
"Book-entry stock" means a written register made by the issuer in a uniform format prescribed by the CSRC, recording the rights and interests of shareholders.  
"Physical stock" means a written stock uniformly printed by the issuer through a printing institution designated by the CSRC.  
(2) "Outstanding common stock" means common stock other than that held in the company's treasury.  
(3) "Public offering" means an invitation for offer, an offer, or a sale of the issuer's stock made by the issuer through a securities operating institution to the public other than the issuer.  
(4) "Underwriting" means the act of a securities operating institution purchasing on a firm commitment basis or selling on a best efforts basis the stock issued by the issuer in accordance with an agreement.  
(5) "Underwriter" means a securities operating institution that sells the issuer's stock through firm commitment underwriting or best efforts underwriting.  
(6) "Firm commitment underwriting" means an underwriting method whereby the underwriter purchases all unsold stock after the end of the offering period.  
(7) "Best efforts underwriting" means an underwriting method whereby the underwriter acts as an agent to sell the stock, and returns all unsold stock to the issuer or the firm commitment underwriter after the end of the offering period.  
(8) "Publication" means the act of publishing the documents required to be disclosed under these Regulations in a newspaper designated by the CSRC.  
(9) "Disclosure" means the act of making available the documents required to be disclosed under these Regulations at the business premises of the issuer and its underwriter, and at the CSRC, for inspection by investors.  
(10) "Offer" means an oral or written expression of intention to purchase or sell a certain type of stock addressed to a specific person or unspecified persons.  
(11) "Invitation for offer" means an expression of intention suggesting that another person make an offer to oneself.  
(12) "Preliminary acceptance" means a preliminary expression of intention by the offeree to accept the offer, which does not constitute acceptance before the expiration of the offer period.  
(13) "Listed company" means a joint stock limited company whose stock has been approved for trading on a securities trading venue.  
(14) "Insider" means any person who, due to holding stock of the issuer, or serving as a director, supervisor, or senior officer in the issuer or an enterprise closely related to the issuer, or due to their membership, management, supervision, or professional position, or as an employee or professional advisor performing duties, has access to or obtains inside information.  
(15) "Inside information" means material information that is not yet public, known to the issuer, securities operating institution, a legal person with acquisition intentions, securities regulatory authority, securities industry self-regulatory organization, or persons closely related to them, that may affect the market price of stock.  
(16) "Securities trading venue" means a stock exchange and a securities trading quotation system established with approval for securities trading.  
(17) "Securities industry administrative personnel" means the staff of securities regulatory departments and securities industry self-regulatory organizations.  
(18) "Securities industry practitioners" means the staff of institutions engaged in securities issuance, trading, and other related businesses.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　证券经营机构和证券交易所的管理规定，另行制定。  
公司内部职工持股不适用本条例。

**Article 82** The administrative regulations on securities operating institutions and stock exchanges shall be formulated separately.  
The provisions on employee stock ownership within a company shall not apply to these Regulations.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本条例由证券委负责解释。

**Article 83** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the Securities Committee.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 84** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
