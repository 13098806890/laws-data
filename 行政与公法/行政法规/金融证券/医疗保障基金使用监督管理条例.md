# 医疗保障基金使用监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-01-15  
**生效日期**：2021-05-01

> (2020年12月9日国务院第117次常务会议通过　2021年1月15日中华人民共和国国务院令第735号公布　自2021年5月1日起施行) 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强医疗保障基金使用监督管理，保障基金安全，促进基金有效使用，维护公民医疗保障合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国社会保险法》和其他有关法律规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内基本医疗保险（含生育保险）基金、医疗救助基金等医疗保障基金使用及其监督管理。 第三条　医疗保障基金使用坚持以人民健康为中心，保障水平与经济社会发展水平相适应，遵循合法、安全、公开、便民的原则。 第四条　医疗保障基金使用监督管理实行政府监管、社会监督、行业自律和个人守信相结合。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作的领导，建立健全医疗保障基金使用监督管理机制和基金监督管理执法体制，加强医疗保障基金使用监督管理能力建设，为医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作提供保障。 第六条　国务院医疗保障行政部门主管全国的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府医疗保障行政部门负责本行政区域的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。 第七条　国家鼓励和支持新闻媒体开展医疗保障法律、法规和医疗保障知识的公益宣传，并对医疗保障基金使用行为进行舆论监督。有关医疗保障的宣传报道应当真实、公正。 县级以上人民政府及其医疗保障等行政部门应当通过书面征求意见、召开座谈会等方式，听取人大代表、政协委员、参保人员代表等对医疗保障基金使用的意见，畅通社会监督渠道，鼓励和支持社会各方面参与对医疗保障基金使用的监督。 医疗机构、药品经营单位（以下统称医药机构）等单位和医药卫生行业协会应当加强行业自律，规范医药服务行为，促进行业规范和自我约束，引导依法、合理使用医疗保障基金。 第二章　基金使用 第八条　医疗保障基金使用应当符合国家规定的支付范围。 医疗保障基金支付范围由国务院医疗保障行政部门依法组织制定。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照国家规定的权限和程序，补充制定本行政区域内医疗保障基金支付的具体项目和标准，并报国务院医疗保障行政部门备案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强医疗保障基金使用监督管理，保障基金安全，促进基金有效使用，维护公民医疗保障合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国社会保险法》和其他有关法律规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Social Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations, for the purposes of strengthening the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, ensuring the safety of the fund, promoting the effective use of the fund, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens in medical security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内基本医疗保险（含生育保险）基金、医疗救助基金等医疗保障基金使用及其监督管理。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the use of medical security funds, including basic medical insurance (including maternity insurance) funds, medical assistance funds, etc., and the supervision and administration thereof within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　医疗保障基金使用坚持以人民健康为中心，保障水平与经济社会发展水平相适应，遵循合法、安全、公开、便民的原则。

**Article 3** The use of the medical security fund shall adhere to the principle of people's health as the center, the level of protection shall be compatible with the level of economic and social development, and shall follow the principles of legality, safety, openness, and convenience.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　医疗保障基金使用监督管理实行政府监管、社会监督、行业自律和个人守信相结合。

**Article 4** The supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund shall combine government regulation, social supervision, industry self-discipline, and individual integrity.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作的领导，建立健全医疗保障基金使用监督管理机制和基金监督管理执法体制，加强医疗保障基金使用监督管理能力建设，为医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作提供保障。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, establish and improve the supervision and administration mechanism for the use of the medical security fund and the law enforcement system for fund supervision and administration, strengthen the capacity building of supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, and provide guarantees for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院医疗保障行政部门主管全国的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府医疗保障行政部门负责本行政区域的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。

**Article 6** The medical security administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund.  
The medical security administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund within its administrative region. Other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和支持新闻媒体开展医疗保障法律、法规和医疗保障知识的公益宣传，并对医疗保障基金使用行为进行舆论监督。有关医疗保障的宣传报道应当真实、公正。  
县级以上人民政府及其医疗保障等行政部门应当通过书面征求意见、召开座谈会等方式，听取人大代表、政协委员、参保人员代表等对医疗保障基金使用的意见，畅通社会监督渠道，鼓励和支持社会各方面参与对医疗保障基金使用的监督。  
医疗机构、药品经营单位（以下统称医药机构）等单位和医药卫生行业协会应当加强行业自律，规范医药服务行为，促进行业规范和自我约束，引导依法、合理使用医疗保障基金。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports news media to carry out public welfare publicity of medical security laws, regulations, and knowledge, and to exercise public opinion supervision over the use of the medical security fund. Relevant publicity reports on medical security shall be truthful and fair.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their medical security and other administrative departments shall, by means of soliciting opinions in writing, holding forums, etc., solicit opinions from deputies to the people's congresses, members of the political consultative conferences, representatives of insured persons, etc. on the use of the medical security fund, smooth channels for social supervision, and encourage and support all sectors of society to participate in the supervision of the use of the medical security fund.  
Medical institutions, drug business units (hereinafter collectively referred to as medical institutions), and other units, as well as medical and health industry associations, shall strengthen industry self-discipline, standardize medical service conduct, promote industry norms and self-restraint, and guide the lawful and reasonable use of the medical security fund.

### 第二章　基金使用

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　医疗保障基金使用应当符合国家规定的支付范围。  
医疗保障基金支付范围由国务院医疗保障行政部门依法组织制定。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照国家规定的权限和程序，补充制定本行政区域内医疗保障基金支付的具体项目和标准，并报国务院医疗保障行政部门备案。

**Article 8** The use of the medical security fund shall conform to the payment scope prescribed by the State.  
The payment scope of the medical security fund shall be organized and formulated by the medical security administrative department of the State Council according to law. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the authority and procedures prescribed by the State, supplement and formulate specific items and standards for payment of the medical security fund within their respective administrative regions, and shall report them to the medical security administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家建立健全全国统一的医疗保障经办管理体系，提供标准化、规范化的医疗保障经办服务，实现省、市、县、乡镇（街道）、村（社区）全覆盖。

**Article 9** The State shall establish and improve a nationwide unified medical security handling management system, provide standardized and normalized medical security handling services, and achieve full coverage of provinces, cities, counties, townships (subdistricts), and villages (communities).

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　医疗保障经办机构应当建立健全业务、财务、安全和风险管理制度，做好服务协议管理、费用监控、基金拨付、待遇审核及支付等工作，并定期向社会公开医疗保障基金的收入、支出、结余等情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 10** Medical security handling agencies shall establish and improve business, financial, security, and risk management systems, properly carry out service agreement management, cost monitoring, fund allocation, benefit review and payment, and other work, and regularly disclose to the public the revenue, expenditure, balance, etc., of the medical security fund, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　医疗保障经办机构应当与定点医药机构建立集体谈判协商机制，合理确定定点医药机构的医疗保障基金预算金额和拨付时限，并根据保障公众健康需求和管理服务的需要，与定点医药机构协商签订服务协议，规范医药服务行为，明确违反服务协议的行为及其责任。  
医疗保障经办机构应当及时向社会公布签订服务协议的定点医药机构名单。  
医疗保障行政部门应当加强对服务协议订立、履行等情况的监督。

**Article 11** Medical security handling agencies shall establish a collective negotiation mechanism with designated medical institutions, reasonably determine the budget amount and allocation time limit of the medical security fund for designated medical institutions, and, based on the needs of protecting public health and management services, negotiate and sign service agreements with designated medical institutions, standardize medical service conduct, and specify the acts that violate the service agreement and the liabilities therefor.  
Medical security handling agencies shall promptly make public the list of designated medical institutions that have signed service agreements.  
The medical security administrative department shall strengthen supervision over the conclusion and performance of service agreements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　医疗保障经办机构应当按照服务协议的约定，及时结算和拨付医疗保障基金。  
定点医药机构应当按照规定提供医药服务，提高服务质量，合理使用医疗保障基金，维护公民健康权益。

**Article 12** Medical security handling agencies shall settle and allocate the medical security fund in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of the service agreement.  
Designated medical institutions shall provide medical services in accordance with the provisions, improve service quality, use the medical security fund reasonably, and protect the health rights and interests of citizens.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　定点医药机构违反服务协议的，医疗保障经办机构可以督促其履行服务协议，按照服务协议约定暂停或者不予拨付费用、追回违规费用、中止相关责任人员或者所在部门涉及医疗保障基金使用的医药服务，直至解除服务协议；定点医药机构及其相关责任人员有权进行陈述、申辩。  
医疗保障经办机构违反服务协议的，定点医药机构有权要求纠正或者提请医疗保障行政部门协调处理、督促整改，也可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 13** Where a designated medical institution violates the service agreement, the medical security handling agency may urge it to perform the service agreement, suspend or refuse to allocate fees in accordance with the service agreement, recover the fees in violation of the agreement, suspend the medical services involving the use of the medical security fund by the relevant responsible personnel or the department to which they belong, until the service agreement is terminated; the designated medical institution and its relevant responsible personnel shall have the right to make statements and defenses.  
Where a medical security handling agency violates the service agreement, the designated medical institution shall have the right to request correction or to refer the matter to the medical security administrative department for coordination and handling and urge rectification, and may also apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　定点医药机构应当建立医疗保障基金使用内部管理制度，由专门机构或者人员负责医疗保障基金使用管理工作，建立健全考核评价体系。  
定点医药机构应当组织开展医疗保障基金相关制度、政策的培训，定期检查本单位医疗保障基金使用情况，及时纠正医疗保障基金使用不规范的行为。

**Article 14** Designated medical institutions shall establish an internal management system for the use of the medical security fund, designate a special institution or person to be responsible for the management of the use of the medical security fund, and establish and improve the assessment and evaluation system.  
Designated medical institutions shall organize and carry out training on the systems and policies related to the medical security fund, regularly inspect the use of the medical security fund in their own units, and promptly correct irregularities in the use of the medical security fund.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　定点医药机构及其工作人员应当执行实名就医和购药管理规定，核验参保人员医疗保障凭证，按照诊疗规范提供合理、必要的医药服务，向参保人员如实出具费用单据和相关资料，不得分解住院、挂床住院，不得违反诊疗规范过度诊疗、过度检查、分解处方、超量开药、重复开药，不得重复收费、超标准收费、分解项目收费，不得串换药品、医用耗材、诊疗项目和服务设施，不得诱导、协助他人冒名或者虚假就医、购药。  
定点医药机构应当确保医疗保障基金支付的费用符合规定的支付范围；除急诊、抢救等特殊情形外，提供医疗保障基金支付范围以外的医药服务的，应当经参保人员或者其近亲属、监护人同意。

**Article 15** Designated medical institutions and their staff shall implement the management provisions on real-name medical treatment and drug purchase, verify the medical security credentials of insured persons, provide reasonable and necessary medical services in accordance with diagnosis and treatment standards, issue expense documents and relevant materials truthfully to insured persons, and shall not split hospitalization, fake hospitalization, engage in excessive diagnosis and treatment, excessive examination, split prescriptions, over-prescribe medication, duplicate medication, duplicate fees, over-standard fees, split-item fees, swap drugs, medical consumables, diagnosis and treatment items and service facilities, or induce or assist others to obtain medical treatment or purchase drugs in the name of another person or by false means.  
Designated medical institutions shall ensure that the expenses paid by the medical security fund fall within the prescribed payment scope; except for special circumstances such as emergency treatment and rescue, the provision of medical services outside the payment scope of the medical security fund shall require the consent of the insured person or his/her close relatives or guardians.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　定点医药机构应当按照规定保管财务账目、会计凭证、处方、病历、治疗检查记录、费用明细、药品和医用耗材出入库记录等资料，及时通过医疗保障信息系统全面准确传送医疗保障基金使用有关数据，向医疗保障行政部门报告医疗保障基金使用监督管理所需信息，向社会公开医药费用、费用结构等信息，接受社会监督。

**Article 16** Designated medical institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions, keep financial accounts, accounting vouchers, prescriptions, medical records, treatment and examination records, expense details, records of incoming and outgoing drugs and medical consumables, and other materials, timely transmit all data related to the use of the medical security fund through the medical security information system comprehensively and accurately, report to the medical security administrative department the information required for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, disclose to the public information such as medical expenses and cost structure, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　参保人员应当持本人医疗保障凭证就医、购药，并主动出示接受查验。参保人员有权要求定点医药机构如实出具费用单据和相关资料。  
参保人员应当妥善保管本人医疗保障凭证，防止他人冒名使用。因特殊原因需要委托他人代为购药的，应当提供委托人和受托人的身份证明。  
参保人员应当按照规定享受医疗保障待遇，不得重复享受。  
参保人员有权要求医疗保障经办机构提供医疗保障咨询服务，对医疗保障基金的使用提出改进建议。

**Article 17** Insured persons shall present their own medical security credentials for medical treatment and drug purchase, and shall proactively show them for inspection. Insured persons shall have the right to request designated medical institutions to issue expense documents and relevant materials truthfully.  
Insured persons shall properly keep their own medical security credentials to prevent others from using them under their names. Where it is necessary to entrust another person to purchase drugs due to special reasons, the identity certificates of the principal and the agent shall be provided.  
Insured persons shall enjoy medical security benefits in accordance with the provisions and shall not enjoy duplicate benefits.  
Insured persons shall have the right to request medical security handling agencies to provide medical security consulting services and to propose suggestions for improvement on the use of the medical security fund.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在医疗保障基金使用过程中，医疗保障等行政部门、医疗保障经办机构、定点医药机构及其工作人员不得收受贿赂或者取得其他非法收入。

**Article 18** In the process of using the medical security fund, the medical security and other administrative departments, medical security handling agencies, designated medical institutions, and their staff shall not accept bribes or obtain other illegal income.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　参保人员不得利用其享受医疗保障待遇的机会转卖药品，接受返还现金、实物或者获得其他非法利益。  
定点医药机构不得为参保人员利用其享受医疗保障待遇的机会转卖药品，接受返还现金、实物或者获得其他非法利益提供便利。

**Article 19** Insured persons shall not use their opportunity to enjoy medical security benefits to resell drugs, accept cash refunds, goods, or obtain other illegal benefits.  
Designated medical institutions shall not facilitate insured persons in using their opportunity to enjoy medical security benefits to resell drugs, accept cash refunds, goods, or obtain other illegal benefits.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗保障经办机构、定点医药机构等单位及其工作人员和参保人员等人员不得通过伪造、变造、隐匿、涂改、销毁医学文书、医学证明、会计凭证、电子信息等有关资料，或者虚构医药服务项目等方式，骗取医疗保障基金。

**Article 20** Medical security handling agencies, designated medical institutions, and other units and their staff, as well as insured persons and other persons, shall not defraud the medical security fund by means of forging, altering, concealing, tampering with, or destroying relevant materials such as medical documents, medical certificates, accounting vouchers, electronic information, or fabricating medical service items.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　医疗保障基金专款专用，任何组织和个人不得侵占或者挪用。

**Article 21** The medical security fund shall be used for its specific purposes, and no organization or individual shall misappropriate or divert it.

### 第三章　监督管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　医疗保障、卫生健康、中医药、市场监督管理、财政、审计、公安等部门应当分工协作、相互配合，建立沟通协调、案件移送等机制，共同做好医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作。  
医疗保障行政部门应当加强对纳入医疗保障基金支付范围的医疗服务行为和医疗费用的监督，规范医疗保障经办业务，依法查处违法使用医疗保障基金的行为。

**Article 22** The departments of medical security, health, traditional Chinese medicine, market regulation, finance, audit, and public security shall divide work, cooperate with each other, establish mechanisms for communication and coordination, case transfer, etc., and jointly carry out the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund.  
The medical security administrative department shall strengthen the supervision over medical service conduct and medical expenses that are included in the payment scope of the medical security fund, standardize medical security handling operations, and investigate and punish illegal uses of the medical security fund according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院医疗保障行政部门负责制定服务协议管理办法，规范、简化、优化医药机构定点申请、专业评估、协商谈判程序，制作并定期修订服务协议范本。  
国务院医疗保障行政部门制定服务协议管理办法，应当听取有关部门、医药机构、行业协会、社会公众、专家等方面意见。

**Article 23** The medical security administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the service agreement management measures, standardizing, simplifying, and optimizing the procedures for application for designation, professional evaluation, and negotiation of medical institutions, and formulating and regularly revising the service agreement templates.  
When formulating the service agreement management measures, the medical security administrative department of the State Council shall solicit opinions from relevant departments, medical institutions, industry associations, the public, experts, etc.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗保障行政部门应当加强与有关部门的信息交换和共享，创新监督管理方式，推广使用信息技术，建立全国统一、高效、兼容、便捷、安全的医疗保障信息系统，实施大数据实时动态智能监控，并加强共享数据使用全过程管理，确保共享数据安全。

**Article 24** The medical security administrative department shall strengthen information exchange and sharing with relevant departments, innovate supervision and administration methods, promote the use of information technology, establish a nationwide unified, efficient, compatible, convenient, and secure medical security information system, implement real-time dynamic intelligent monitoring of big data, and strengthen the whole-process management of the use of shared data to ensure the security of shared data.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　医疗保障行政部门应当根据医疗保障基金风险评估、举报投诉线索、医疗保障数据监控等因素，确定检查重点，组织开展专项检查。

**Article 25** The medical security administrative department shall, based on factors such as medical security fund risk assessment, tip-offs and complaints, and medical security data monitoring, determine the focus of inspections and organize special inspections.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　医疗保障行政部门可以会同卫生健康、中医药、市场监督管理、财政、公安等部门开展联合检查。  
对跨区域的医疗保障基金使用行为，由共同的上一级医疗保障行政部门指定的医疗保障行政部门检查。

**Article 26** The medical security administrative department may, together with the departments of health, traditional Chinese medicine, market regulation, finance, and public security, conduct joint inspections.  
For cross-regional use of the medical security fund, inspections shall be carried out by the medical security administrative department designated by the common higher-level medical security administrative department.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　医疗保障行政部门实施监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入现场检查；  
（二）询问有关人员；  
（三）要求被检查对象提供与检查事项相关的文件资料，并作出解释和说明；  
（四）采取记录、录音、录像、照相或者复制等方式收集有关情况和资料；  
（五）对可能被转移、隐匿或者灭失的资料等予以封存；  
（六）聘请符合条件的会计师事务所等第三方机构和专业人员协助开展检查；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 27** When conducting supervision and inspection, the medical security administrative department may take the following measures:  
(1) Entering the site for inspection;  
(2) Questioning relevant persons;  
(3) Requiring the inspected party to provide documents and materials related to the inspection matter and to make explanations and clarifications;  
(4) Collecting relevant information and materials by means of recording, audio recording, video recording, photography, or reproduction;  
(5) Sealing up materials that may be transferred, concealed, or destroyed;  
(6) Engaging qualified accounting firms and other third-party institutions and professionals to assist in the inspection;  
(7) Other measures provided for by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　医疗保障行政部门可以依法委托符合法定条件的组织开展医疗保障行政执法工作。

**Article 28** The medical security administrative department may, according to law, entrust organizations meeting the statutory conditions to carry out medical security administrative law enforcement work.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　开展医疗保障基金使用监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并且应当出示执法证件。  
医疗保障行政部门进行监督检查时，被检查对象应当予以配合，如实提供相关资料和信息，不得拒绝、阻碍检查或者谎报、瞒报。

**Article 29** When carrying out supervision and inspection of the use of the medical security fund, there shall be no fewer than two inspectors, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials.  
When the medical security administrative department conducts supervision and inspection, the inspected party shall cooperate, provide relevant materials and information truthfully, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection or make false or concealed reports.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　定点医药机构涉嫌骗取医疗保障基金支出的，在调查期间，医疗保障行政部门可以采取增加监督检查频次、加强费用监控等措施，防止损失扩大。定点医药机构拒不配合调查的，经医疗保障行政部门主要负责人批准，医疗保障行政部门可以要求医疗保障经办机构暂停医疗保障基金结算。经调查，属于骗取医疗保障基金支出的，依照[本条例第四十条](#art-40)的规定处理；不属于骗取医疗保障基金支出的，按照规定结算。  
参保人员涉嫌骗取医疗保障基金支出且拒不配合调查的，医疗保障行政部门可以要求医疗保障经办机构暂停医疗费用联网结算。暂停联网结算期间发生的医疗费用，由参保人员全额垫付。经调查，属于骗取医疗保障基金支出的，依照[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)的规定处理；不属于骗取医疗保障基金支出的，按照规定结算。

**Article 30** Where a designated medical institution is suspected of defrauding the medical security fund of expenditures, during the investigation period, the medical security administrative department may take measures such as increasing the frequency of supervision and inspection and strengthening cost monitoring to prevent the expansion of losses. Where the designated medical institution refuses to cooperate with the investigation, with the approval of the principal person in charge of the medical security administrative department, the medical security administrative department may require the medical security handling agency to suspend the settlement of the medical security fund. If after investigation it is determined to be a fraudulent claim for medical security fund expenditures, it shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 40 of these Regulations; if it is not a fraudulent claim, settlement shall be made in accordance with the provisions.  
Where an insured person is suspected of defrauding the medical security fund of expenditures and refuses to cooperate with the investigation, the medical security administrative department may require the medical security handling agency to suspend the online settlement of medical expenses. Medical expenses incurred during the period of suspension of online settlement shall be fully paid in advance by the insured person. If after investigation it is determined to be a fraudulent claim for medical security fund expenditures, it shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 41 of these Regulations; if it is not a fraudulent claim, settlement shall be made in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　医疗保障行政部门对违反本条例的行为作出行政处罚或者行政处理决定前，应当听取当事人的陈述、申辩；作出行政处罚或者行政处理决定，应当告知当事人依法享有申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的权利。

**Article 31** Before making a decision on administrative penalty or administrative disposition for a violation of these Regulations, the medical security administrative department shall hear the statements and defenses of the party; when making a decision on administrative penalty or administrative disposition, it shall inform the party of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration or file an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医疗保障等行政部门、医疗保障经办机构、会计师事务所等机构及其工作人员，不得将工作中获取、知悉的被调查对象资料或者相关信息用于医疗保障基金使用监督管理以外的其他目的，不得泄露、篡改、毁损、非法向他人提供当事人的个人信息和商业秘密。

**Article 32** The medical security and other administrative departments, medical security handling agencies, accounting firms, and other institutions and their staff shall not use the materials or relevant information of the investigated party obtained or learned in their work for purposes other than the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, and shall not disclose, alter, destroy, or illegally provide to others the personal information and trade secrets of the party.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院医疗保障行政部门应当建立定点医药机构、人员等信用管理制度，根据信用评价等级分级分类监督管理，将日常监督检查结果、行政处罚结果等情况纳入全国信用信息共享平台和其他相关信息公示系统，按照国家有关规定实施惩戒。

**Article 33** The medical security administrative department of the State Council shall establish a credit management system for designated medical institutions and personnel, conduct graded and classified supervision and administration based on credit evaluation ratings, incorporate the results of routine supervision and inspection, administrative penalty results, etc., into the national credit information sharing platform and other relevant information disclosure systems, and impose sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　医疗保障行政部门应当定期向社会公布医疗保障基金使用监督检查结果，加大对医疗保障基金使用违法案件的曝光力度，接受社会监督。

**Article 34** The medical security administrative department shall regularly disclose to the public the results of supervision and inspection of the use of the medical security fund, increase the exposure of illegal cases involving the use of the medical security fund, and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　任何组织和个人有权对侵害医疗保障基金的违法违规行为进行举报、投诉。  
医疗保障行政部门应当畅通举报投诉渠道，依法及时处理有关举报投诉，并对举报人的信息保密。对查证属实的举报，按照国家有关规定给予举报人奖励。

**Article 35** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report and complain about illegal or irregular acts that infringe upon the medical security fund.  
The medical security administrative department shall smooth the channels for reporting and complaints, handle relevant reports and complaints in a timely manner according to law, and keep the informant's information confidential. For reports verified to be true, the informant shall be rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　医疗保障经办机构有下列情形之一的，由医疗保障行政部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未建立健全业务、财务、安全和风险管理制度；  
（二）未履行服务协议管理、费用监控、基金拨付、待遇审核及支付等职责；  
（三）未定期向社会公开医疗保障基金的收入、支出、结余等情况。

**Article 36** Where a medical security handling agency falls under any of the following circumstances, the medical security administrative department shall order it to make corrections, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to establish and improve business, financial, security, and risk management systems;  
(2) Failing to perform the duties of service agreement management, cost monitoring, fund allocation, benefit review and payment, etc.;  
(3) Failing to regularly disclose to the public the revenue, expenditure, balance, etc., of the medical security fund.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　医疗保障经办机构通过伪造、变造、隐匿、涂改、销毁医学文书、医学证明、会计凭证、电子信息等有关资料或者虚构医药服务项目等方式，骗取医疗保障基金支出的，由医疗保障行政部门责令退回，处骗取金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 37** Where a medical security handling agency defrauds the medical security fund of expenditures by means of forging, altering, concealing, tampering with, or destroying relevant materials such as medical documents, medical certificates, accounting vouchers, electronic information, or fabricating medical service items, the medical security administrative department shall order the return of the funds and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the amount defrauded, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　定点医药机构有下列情形之一的，由医疗保障行政部门责令改正，并可以约谈有关负责人；造成医疗保障基金损失的，责令退回，处造成损失金额1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，责令定点医药机构暂停相关责任部门6个月以上1年以下涉及医疗保障基金使用的医药服务；违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法处理：  
（一）分解住院、挂床住院；  
（二）违反诊疗规范过度诊疗、过度检查、分解处方、超量开药、重复开药或者提供其他不必要的医药服务；  
（三）重复收费、超标准收费、分解项目收费；  
（四）串换药品、医用耗材、诊疗项目和服务设施；  
（五）为参保人员利用其享受医疗保障待遇的机会转卖药品，接受返还现金、实物或者获得其他非法利益提供便利；  
（六）将不属于医疗保障基金支付范围的医药费用纳入医疗保障基金结算；  
（七）造成医疗保障基金损失的其他违法行为。

**Article 38** Where a designated medical institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the medical security administrative department shall order it to make corrections and may also interview the relevant responsible person; if losses are caused to the medical security fund, it shall order the return of the losses and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the amount of the losses; if it refuses to make corrections or causes serious consequences, it shall order the designated medical institution to suspend the medical services involving the use of the medical security fund in the relevant responsible department for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year; if it violates other laws or administrative regulations, the relevant competent department shall deal with it according to law:  
(1) Splitting hospitalization or fake hospitalization;  
(2) Engaging in excessive diagnosis and treatment, excessive examination, splitting prescriptions, over-prescribing medication, duplicate medication, or providing other unnecessary medical services in violation of diagnosis and treatment standards;  
(3) Duplicate fees, over-standard fees, or split-item fees;  
(4) Swapping drugs, medical consumables, diagnosis and treatment items, and service facilities;  
(5) Facilitating insured persons in using their opportunity to enjoy medical security benefits to resell drugs, accept cash refunds, goods, or obtain other illegal benefits;  
(6) Including medical expenses not within the payment scope of the medical security fund into the settlement of the medical security fund;  
(7) Other illegal acts causing losses to the medical security fund.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　定点医药机构有下列情形之一的，由医疗保障行政部门责令改正，并可以约谈有关负责人；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法处理：  
（一）未建立医疗保障基金使用内部管理制度，或者没有专门机构或者人员负责医疗保障基金使用管理工作；  
（二）未按照规定保管财务账目、会计凭证、处方、病历、治疗检查记录、费用明细、药品和医用耗材出入库记录等资料；  
（三）未按照规定通过医疗保障信息系统传送医疗保障基金使用有关数据；  
（四）未按照规定向医疗保障行政部门报告医疗保障基金使用监督管理所需信息；  
（五）未按照规定向社会公开医药费用、费用结构等信息；  
（六）除急诊、抢救等特殊情形外，未经参保人员或者其近亲属、监护人同意提供医疗保障基金支付范围以外的医药服务；  
（七）拒绝医疗保障等行政部门监督检查或者提供虚假情况。

**Article 39** Where a designated medical institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the medical security administrative department shall order it to make corrections and may also interview the relevant responsible person; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it violates other laws or administrative regulations, the relevant competent department shall deal with it according to law:  
(1) Failing to establish an internal management system for the use of the medical security fund, or failing to have a special institution or person responsible for the management of the use of the medical security fund;  
(2) Failing to keep financial accounts, accounting vouchers, prescriptions, medical records, treatment and examination records, expense details, records of incoming and outgoing drugs and medical consumables, and other materials in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) Failing to transmit data related to the use of the medical security fund through the medical security information system in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) Failing to report to the medical security administrative department the information required for the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Failing to disclose to the public information such as medical expenses and cost structure in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) Except for special circumstances such as emergency treatment and rescue, providing medical services outside the payment scope of the medical security fund without the consent of the insured person or his/her close relatives or guardians;  
(7) Refusing supervision and inspection by the medical security or other administrative departments or providing false information.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　定点医药机构通过下列方式骗取医疗保障基金支出的，由医疗保障行政部门责令退回，处骗取金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；责令定点医药机构暂停相关责任部门6个月以上1年以下涉及医疗保障基金使用的医药服务，直至由医疗保障经办机构解除服务协议；有执业资格的，由有关主管部门依法吊销执业资格：  
（一）诱导、协助他人冒名或者虚假就医、购药，提供虚假证明材料，或者串通他人虚开费用单据；  
（二）伪造、变造、隐匿、涂改、销毁医学文书、医学证明、会计凭证、电子信息等有关资料；  
（三）虚构医药服务项目；  
（四）其他骗取医疗保障基金支出的行为。  
定点医药机构以骗取医疗保障基金为目的，实施了[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)规定行为之一，造成医疗保障基金损失的，按照本条规定处理。

**Article 40** Where a designated medical institution defrauds the medical security fund of expenditures by any of the following means, the medical security administrative department shall order the return of the funds and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the amount defrauded; order the designated medical institution to suspend the medical services involving the use of the medical security fund in the relevant responsible department for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year, until the medical security handling agency terminates the service agreement; if it has a practice qualification, the relevant competent department shall revoke the practice qualification according to law:  
(1) Inducing or assisting others to obtain medical treatment or purchase drugs in the name of another person or by false means, providing false certification materials, or colluding with others to issue false expense documents;  
(2) Forging, altering, concealing, tampering with, or destroying relevant materials such as medical documents, medical certificates, accounting vouchers, electronic information;  
(3) Fabricating medical service items;  
(4) Other acts of defrauding the medical security fund of expenditures.  
Where a designated medical institution, for the purpose of defrauding the medical security fund, commits any of the acts specified in Article 38 of these Regulations and causes losses to the medical security fund, it shall be dealt with in accordance with this Article.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　个人有下列情形之一的，由医疗保障行政部门责令改正；造成医疗保障基金损失的，责令退回；属于参保人员的，暂停其医疗费用联网结算3个月至12个月：  
（一）将本人的医疗保障凭证交由他人冒名使用；  
（二）重复享受医疗保障待遇；  
（三）利用享受医疗保障待遇的机会转卖药品，接受返还现金、实物或者获得其他非法利益。  
个人以骗取医疗保障基金为目的，实施了前款规定行为之一，造成医疗保障基金损失的；或者使用他人医疗保障凭证冒名就医、购药的；或者通过伪造、变造、隐匿、涂改、销毁医学文书、医学证明、会计凭证、电子信息等有关资料或者虚构医药服务项目等方式，骗取医疗保障基金支出的，除依照前款规定处理外，还应当由医疗保障行政部门处骗取金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where an individual falls under any of the following circumstances, the medical security administrative department shall order him/her to make corrections; if losses are caused to the medical security fund, it shall order the return of the losses; if the individual is an insured person, his/her online settlement of medical expenses shall be suspended for a period of not less than three months but not more than twelve months:  
(1) Allowing another person to use his/her medical security credentials in the name of the holder;  
(2) Enjoying duplicate medical security benefits;  
(3) Using the opportunity to enjoy medical security benefits to resell drugs, accept cash refunds, goods, or obtain other illegal benefits.  
Where an individual, for the purpose of defrauding the medical security fund, commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph and causes losses to the medical security fund; or uses another person's medical security credentials to obtain medical treatment or purchase drugs under that person's name; or defrauds the medical security fund of expenditures by means of forging, altering, concealing, tampering with, or destroying relevant materials such as medical documents, medical certificates, accounting vouchers, electronic information, or fabricating medical service items, in addition to being dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the amount defrauded shall also be imposed by the medical security administrative department.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　医疗保障等行政部门、医疗保障经办机构、定点医药机构及其工作人员收受贿赂或者取得其他非法收入的，没收违法所得，对有关责任人员依法给予处分；违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法处理。

**Article 42** Where the medical security and other administrative departments, medical security handling agencies, designated medical institutions, or their staff accept bribes or obtain other illegal income, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and the relevant responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if they violate other laws or administrative regulations, the relevant competent department shall deal with them according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　定点医药机构违反本条例规定，造成医疗保障基金重大损失或者其他严重不良社会影响的，其法定代表人或者主要负责人5年内禁止从事定点医药机构管理活动，由有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 43** Where a designated medical institution violates the provisions of these Regulations, causing significant losses to the medical security fund or other serious adverse social impacts, its legal representative or principal person in charge shall be prohibited from engaging in the management activities of designated medical institutions for five years, and the relevant departments shall give sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，侵占、挪用医疗保障基金的，由医疗保障等行政部门责令追回；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 44** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, misappropriates or diverts the medical security fund, the medical security and other administrative departments shall order the recovery of the fund; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　退回的基金退回原医疗保障基金财政专户；罚款、没收的违法所得依法上缴国库。

**Article 45** The returned funds shall be returned to the original financial account of the medical security fund; fines and confiscated illegal gains shall be turned over to the State treasury according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　医疗保障等行政部门、医疗保障经办机构、会计师事务所等机构及其工作人员，泄露、篡改、毁损、非法向他人提供个人信息、商业秘密的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法处理。

**Article 46** Where the medical security and other administrative departments, medical security handling agencies, accounting firms, and other institutions and their staff disclose, alter, destroy, or illegally provide personal information or trade secrets to others, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if they violate other laws or administrative regulations, the relevant competent department shall deal with them according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　医疗保障等行政部门工作人员在医疗保障基金使用监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 47** Where staff members of the medical security and other administrative departments abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the supervision and administration of the use of the medical security fund, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，给有关单位或者个人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 48** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations constitutes a public security administration violation, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations causes losses to a relevant unit or individual, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　职工大额医疗费用补助、公务员医疗补助等医疗保障资金使用的监督管理，参照本条例执行。  
居民大病保险资金的使用按照国家有关规定执行，医疗保障行政部门应当加强监督。

**Article 49** The supervision and administration of the use of medical security funds such as employee large-amount medical expense subsidies and civil servant medical subsidies shall be carried out by reference to these Regulations.  
The use of resident major illness insurance funds shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the medical security administrative department shall strengthen supervision.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例自2021年5月1日起施行。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2021.
