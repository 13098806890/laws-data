# 期货交易管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2007-04-15

> (2007年3月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第489号公布　根据2012年10月24日《国务院关于修改〈期货交易管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范期货交易行为，加强对期货交易的监督管理，维护期货市场秩序，防范风险，保护期货交易各方的合法权益和社会公共利益，促进期货市场积极稳妥发展，制定本条例。 第二条　任何单位和个人从事期货交易及其相关活动，应当遵守本条例。 本条例所称期货交易，是指采用公开的集中交易方式或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的其他方式进行的以期货合约或者期权合约为交易标的的交易活动。 本条例所称期货合约，是指期货交易场所统一制定的、规定在将来某一特定的时间和地点交割一定数量标的物的标准化合约。期货合约包括商品期货合约和金融期货合约及其他期货合约。 本条例所称期权合约，是指期货交易场所统一制定的、规定买方有权在将来某一时间以特定价格买入或者卖出约定标的物(包括期货合约)的标准化合约。 第三条　从事期货交易活动，应当遵循公开、公平、公正和诚实信用的原则。禁止欺诈、内幕交易和操纵期货交易价格等违法行为。 第四条　期货交易应当在依照本条例第六条第一款规定设立的期货交易所、国务院批准的或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的其他期货交易场所进行。 禁止在前款规定的期货交易场所之外进行期货交易。 第五条　国务院期货监督管理机构对期货市场实行集中统一的监督管理。 国务院期货监督管理机构派出机构依照本条例的有关规定和国务院期货监督管理机构的授权，履行监督管理职责。 第二章　期货交易所 第六条　设立期货交易所，由国务院期货监督管理机构审批。 未经国务院批准或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准，任何单位或者个人不得设立期货交易场所或者以任何形式组织期货交易及其相关活动。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范期货交易行为，加强对期货交易的监督管理，维护期货市场秩序，防范风险，保护期货交易各方的合法权益和社会公共利益，促进期货市场积极稳妥发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating futures trading activities, strengthening the supervision and administration of futures trading, maintaining the order of the futures market, preventing risks, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of all parties involved in futures trading and the public interest, and promoting the sound and steady development of the futures market.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　任何单位和个人从事期货交易及其相关活动，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称期货交易，是指采用公开的集中交易方式或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的其他方式进行的以期货合约或者期权合约为交易标的的交易活动。  
本条例所称期货合约，是指期货交易场所统一制定的、规定在将来某一特定的时间和地点交割一定数量标的物的标准化合约。期货合约包括商品期货合约和金融期货合约及其他期货合约。  
本条例所称期权合约，是指期货交易场所统一制定的、规定买方有权在将来某一时间以特定价格买入或者卖出约定标的物(包括期货合约)的标准化合约。

**Article 2** Any unit or individual engaged in futures trading and related activities shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "futures trading" means trading activities conducted by means of public centralized trading or other means approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, with futures contracts or options contracts as the trading targets.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "futures contract" means a standardized contract uniformly formulated by a futures trading venue, which stipulates the delivery of a specified quantity of underlying subject matter at a specified future time and place. Futures contracts include commodity futures contracts, financial futures contracts, and other futures contracts.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "options contract" means a standardized contract uniformly formulated by a futures trading venue, which stipulates that the buyer has the right to buy or sell the agreed underlying subject matter (including futures contracts) at a specific price at a future date.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事期货交易活动，应当遵循公开、公平、公正和诚实信用的原则。禁止欺诈、内幕交易和操纵期货交易价格等违法行为。

**Article 3** Activities involving futures trading shall adhere to the principles of openness, fairness, justice, and good faith. Fraud, insider trading, manipulation of futures trading prices, and other illegal acts are prohibited.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　期货交易应当在依照[本条例第六条](#art-6)第一款规定设立的期货交易所、国务院批准的或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准的其他期货交易场所进行。  
禁止在前款规定的期货交易场所之外进行期货交易。

**Article 4** Futures trading shall be conducted at a futures exchange established in accordance with the provisions of Article 6, paragraph 1 of these Regulations, or at other futures trading venues approved by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Futures trading outside the futures trading venues specified in the preceding paragraph is prohibited.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院期货监督管理机构对期货市场实行集中统一的监督管理。  
国务院期货监督管理机构派出机构依照本条例的有关规定和国务院期货监督管理机构的授权，履行监督管理职责。

**Article 5** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall exercise centralized and unified supervision and administration over the futures market.  
The dispatched agencies of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall perform their supervisory and administrative duties in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations and the authorization of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第二章　期货交易所

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设立期货交易所，由国务院期货监督管理机构审批。  
未经国务院批准或者国务院期货监督管理机构批准，任何单位或者个人不得设立期货交易场所或者以任何形式组织期货交易及其相关活动。

**Article 6** The establishment of a futures exchange shall be subject to the examination and approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Without approval by the State Council or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, no unit or individual may establish a futures trading venue or organize futures trading and related activities in any form.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　期货交易所不以营利为目的，按照其章程的规定实行自律管理。期货交易所以其全部财产承担民事责任。期货交易所的负责人由国务院期货监督管理机构任免。  
期货交易所的管理办法由国务院期货监督管理机构制定。

**Article 7** A futures exchange shall not be for profit-making purposes and shall exercise self-discipline management in accordance with its articles of association. A futures exchange shall bear civil liability with all its property. The persons in charge of a futures exchange shall be appointed and removed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The administrative measures for futures exchanges shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　期货交易所会员应当是在中华人民共和国境内登记注册的企业法人或者其他经济组织。  
期货交易所可以实行会员分级结算制度。实行会员分级结算制度的期货交易所会员由结算会员和非结算会员组成。

**Article 8** Members of a futures exchange shall be enterprise legal persons or other economic organizations registered within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
A futures exchange may implement a tiered membership settlement system. Under a tiered membership settlement system, members of a futures exchange shall consist of clearing members and non-clearing members.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　有[《中华人民共和国公司法》第一百四十六条](../../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-146)规定的情形或者下列情形之一的，不得担任期货交易所的负责人、财务会计人员：  
(一)因违法行为或者违纪行为被解除职务的期货交易所、证券交易所、证券登记结算机构的负责人，或者期货公司、证券公司的董事、监事、高级管理人员，以及国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他人员，自被解除职务之日起未逾5年；  
(二)因违法行为或者违纪行为被撤销资格的律师、注册会计师或者投资咨询机构、财务顾问机构、资信评级机构、资产评估机构、验证机构的专业人员，自被撤销资格之日起未逾5年。

**Article 9** A person who falls under the circumstances specified in Article 146 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China or under any of the following circumstances shall not serve as a person in charge or a financial accounting personnel of a futures exchange:  
(1) A person who has been removed from his/her position as a person in charge of a futures exchange, stock exchange, or securities depository and clearing institution, or as a director, supervisor, or senior officer of a futures company or securities company, or as other persons specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, due to illegal or disciplinary acts, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of removal;  
(2) A lawyer, certified public accountant, or professional of an investment consulting institution, financial advisory institution, credit rating institution, asset appraisal institution, or verification institution whose qualification has been revoked due to illegal or disciplinary acts, where less than five years have elapsed since the date of revocation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　期货交易所应当依照本条例和国务院期货监督管理机构的规定，建立、健全各项规章制度，加强对交易活动的风险控制和对会员以及交易所工作人员的监督管理。期货交易所履行下列职责：  
(一)提供交易的场所、设施和服务；  
(二)设计合约，安排合约上市；  
(三)组织并监督交易、结算和交割；  
(四)为期货交易提供集中履约担保；  
(五)按照章程和交易规则对会员进行监督管理；  
(六)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他职责。  
期货交易所不得直接或者间接参与期货交易。未经国务院期货监督管理机构审核并报国务院批准，期货交易所不得从事信托投资、股票投资、非自用不动产投资等与其职责无关的业务。

**Article 10** A futures exchange shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, establish and improve various rules and regulations, strengthen risk control over trading activities, and enhance supervision and administration over members and exchange staff. A futures exchange shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Providing trading venues, facilities, and services;  
(2) Designing contracts and arranging for the listing of contracts;  
(3) Organizing and supervising trading, clearing, and delivery;  
(4) Providing centralized performance guarantee for futures trading;  
(5) Supervising and administering members in accordance with the articles of association and trading rules;  
(6) Other duties specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures exchange shall not directly or indirectly participate in futures trading. Without review by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and approval by the State Council, a futures exchange shall not engage in trust investment, stock investment, non-self-use real estate investment, or other businesses unrelated to its duties.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　期货交易所应当按照国家有关规定建立、健全下列风险管理制度：  
(一)保证金制度；  
(二)当日无负债结算制度；  
(三)涨跌停板制度；  
(四)持仓限额和大户持仓报告制度；  
(五)风险准备金制度；  
(六)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他风险管理制度。  
实行会员分级结算制度的期货交易所，还应当建立、健全结算担保金制度。

**Article 11** A futures exchange shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish and improve the following risk management systems:  
(1) A margin system;  
(2) A same-day no-debt settlement system;  
(3) A price limit system;  
(4) A position limit and large position reporting system;  
(5) A risk reserve system;  
(6) Other risk management systems specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures exchange that implements a tiered membership settlement system shall also establish and improve a settlement guarantee fund system.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当期货市场出现异常情况时，期货交易所可以按照其章程规定的权限和程序，决定采取下列紧急措施，并应当立即报告国务院期货监督管理机构：  
(一)提高保证金；  
(二)调整涨跌停板幅度；  
(三)限制会员或者客户的最大持仓量；  
(四)暂时停止交易；  
(五)采取其他紧急措施。  
前款所称异常情况，是指在交易中发生操纵期货交易价格的行为或者发生不可抗拒的突发事件以及国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他情形。  
异常情况消失后，期货交易所应当及时取消紧急措施。

**Article 12** When abnormal circumstances arise in the futures market, a futures exchange may, in accordance with the authority and procedures specified in its articles of association, decide to take the following emergency measures and shall immediately report to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council:  
(1) Increasing margins;  
(2) Adjusting the price limit range;  
(3) Restricting the maximum position of members or clients;  
(4) Temporarily suspending trading;  
(5) Taking other emergency measures.  
The term "abnormal circumstances" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the occurrence of acts manipulating futures trading prices, force majeure events, or other circumstances specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
After the abnormal circumstances disappear, the futures exchange shall promptly cancel the emergency measures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　期货交易所办理下列事项，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准：  
(一)制定或者修改章程、交易规则；  
(二)上市、中止、取消或者恢复交易品种；  
(三)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他事项。  
国务院期货监督管理机构批准期货交易所上市新的交易品种，应当征求国务院有关部门的意见。

**Article 13** A futures exchange shall obtain approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for handling the following matters:  
(1) Formulating or amending its articles of association or trading rules;  
(2) Listing, suspending, canceling, or resuming trading varieties;  
(3) Other matters specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
When approving a futures exchange to list new trading varieties, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall solicit opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　期货交易所的所得收益按照国家有关规定管理和使用，但应当首先用于保证期货交易场所、设施的运行和改善。

**Article 14** The income of a futures exchange shall be managed and used in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, but shall first be used to ensure the operation and improvement of the futures trading venues and facilities.

### 第三章　期货公司

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　期货公司是依照《中华人民共和国公司法》和本条例规定设立的经营期货业务的金融机构。设立期货公司，应当在公司登记机关登记注册，并经国务院期货监督管理机构批准。  
未经国务院期货监督管理机构批准，任何单位或者个人不得设立或者变相设立期货公司，经营期货业务。

**Article 15** A futures company is a financial institution established in accordance with the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations to operate futures business. To establish a futures company, it shall be registered with the company registration authority and obtain approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Without approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, no unit or individual may establish or establish in disguised form a futures company to operate futures business.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请设立期货公司，应当符合《中华人民共和国公司法》的规定，并具备下列条件：  
(一)注册资本最低限额为人民币3000万元；  
(二)董事、监事、高级管理人员具备任职条件，从业人员具有期货从业资格；  
(三)有符合法律、行政法规规定的公司章程；  
(四)主要股东以及实际控制人具有持续盈利能力，信誉良好，最近3年无重大违法违规记录；  
(五)有合格的经营场所和业务设施；  
(六)有健全的风险管理和内部控制制度；  
(七)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他条件。  
国务院期货监督管理机构根据审慎监管原则和各项业务的风险程度，可以提高注册资本最低限额。注册资本应当是实缴资本。股东应当以货币或者期货公司经营必需的非货币财产出资，货币出资比例不得低于85%。  
国务院期货监督管理机构应当在受理期货公司设立申请之日起6个月内，根据审慎监管原则进行审查，作出批准或者不批准的决定。  
未经国务院期货监督管理机构批准，任何单位和个人不得委托或者接受他人委托持有或者管理期货公司的股权。

**Article 16** An application to establish a futures company shall meet the requirements of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China and satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The minimum registered capital is RMB 30 million yuan;  
(2) The directors, supervisors, and senior officers meet the qualifications for their positions, and the practitioners have futures practitioner qualifications;  
(3) Having the company's articles of association that comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations;  
(4) The major shareholders and actual controllers have sustained profitability, good reputation, and no record of major illegal or disciplinary violations in the recent three years;  
(5) Having qualified business premises and facilities;  
(6) Having sound risk management and internal control systems;  
(7) Other conditions specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may raise the minimum registered capital based on the prudent supervision principle and the risk level of each business. The registered capital shall be paid-in capital. Shareholders shall make capital contributions in currency or non-monetary property essential for the futures company's operations, and the proportion of currency contributions shall not be less than 85%.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, within six months from the date of receiving the application for establishing a futures company, examine it in accordance with the prudent supervision principle and make a decision on approval or disapproval.  
Without approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, no unit or individual may entrust or accept the entrustment of others to hold or manage the equity of a futures company.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　期货公司业务实行许可制度，由国务院期货监督管理机构按照其商品期货、金融期货业务种类颁发许可证。期货公司除申请经营境内期货经纪业务外，还可以申请经营境外期货经纪、期货投资咨询以及国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他期货业务。  
期货公司不得从事与期货业务无关的活动，法律、行政法规或者国务院期货监督管理机构另有规定的除外。  
期货公司不得从事或者变相从事期货自营业务。  
期货公司不得为其股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人提供融资，不得对外担保。

**Article 17** The business of a futures company shall be subject to a licensing system, and the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall issue licenses according to the types of its commodity futures and financial futures businesses. In addition to applying to engage in domestic futures brokerage business, a futures company may also apply to engage in overseas futures brokerage, futures investment consulting, and other futures businesses specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures company shall not engage in activities unrelated to futures business, except as otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
A futures company shall not engage in or engage in disguised form in futures proprietary trading.  
A futures company shall not provide financing to its shareholders, actual controllers, or other related persons, and shall not provide external guarantees.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　期货公司从事经纪业务，接受客户委托，以自己的名义为客户进行期货交易，交易结果由客户承担。

**Article 18** A futures company engaging in brokerage business shall accept clients' entrustment, conduct futures trading on behalf of clients in its own name, and the trading results shall be borne by the clients.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　期货公司办理下列事项，应当经国务院期货监督管理机构批准：  
(一)合并、分立、停业、解散或者破产；  
(二)变更业务范围；  
(三)变更注册资本且调整股权结构；  
(四)新增持有5%以上股权的股东或者控股股东发生变化；  
(五)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他事项。  
前款第三项、第五项所列事项，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出批准或者不批准的决定；前款所列其他事项，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自受理申请之日起2个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定。

**Article 19** A futures company shall obtain approval from the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for handling the following matters:  
(1) Merger, division, suspension of business, dissolution, or bankruptcy;  
(2) Change of business scope;  
(3) Change of registered capital and adjustment of equity structure;  
(4) Addition of shareholders holding more than 5% of the equity, or change of controlling shareholders;  
(5) Other matters specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
With respect to matters listed in items (3) and (5) of the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within 20 days from the date of receiving the application; with respect to other matters listed in the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within two months from the date of receiving the application.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　期货公司或者其分支机构有[《中华人民共和国行政许可法》第七十条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政许可法.md#art-70)规定的情形或者下列情形之一的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当依法办理期货业务许可证注销手续：  
(一)营业执照被公司登记机关依法注销；  
(二)成立后无正当理由超过3个月未开始营业，或者开业后无正当理由停业连续3个月以上；  
(三)主动提出注销申请；  
(四)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他情形。  
期货公司在注销期货业务许可证前，应当结清相关期货业务，并依法返还客户的保证金和其他资产。期货公司分支机构在注销经营许可证前，应当终止经营活动，妥善处理客户资产。

**Article 20** Where a futures company or its branch office falls under the circumstances specified in Article 70 of the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China or under any of the following circumstances, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall handle the cancellation of the futures business license in accordance with the law:  
(1) The business license has been cancelled by the company registration authority in accordance with the law;  
(2) After establishment, without justifiable reasons, it has not commenced business for more than three months, or after starting business, without justifiable reasons, it has suspended business for three consecutive months or more;  
(3) It proactively applies for cancellation;  
(4) Other circumstances specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Before the cancellation of the futures business license, the futures company shall settle the relevant futures business and return the clients' margins and other assets in accordance with the law. Before the cancellation of the business license of a branch office, the branch office shall terminate its business activities and properly handle the clients' assets.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　期货公司应当建立、健全并严格执行业务管理规则、风险管理制度，遵守信息披露制度，保障客户保证金的存管安全，按照期货交易所的规定，向期货交易所报告大户名单、交易情况。

**Article 21** A futures company shall establish, improve, and strictly implement business management rules and risk management systems, comply with the information disclosure system, ensure the safe custody of clients' margins, and report the list of large holders and trading conditions to the futures exchange in accordance with the provisions of the futures exchange.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　其他期货经营机构从事期货投资咨询业务，应当遵守国务院期货监督管理机构的规定。

**Article 22** Other futures business institutions engaging in futures investment consulting business shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第四章　期货交易基本规则

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在期货交易所进行期货交易的，应当是期货交易所会员。  
符合规定条件的境外机构，可以在期货交易所从事特定品种的期货交易。具体办法由国务院期货监督管理机构制定。

**Article 23** Those who conduct futures trading at a futures exchange shall be members of the futures exchange.  
Overseas institutions that meet the prescribed conditions may engage in futures trading of specific varieties at a futures exchange. The specific measures shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　期货公司接受客户委托为其进行期货交易，应当事先向客户出示风险说明书，经客户签字确认后，与客户签订书面合同。期货公司不得未经客户委托或者不按照客户委托内容，擅自进行期货交易。  
期货公司不得向客户作获利保证；不得在经纪业务中与客户约定分享利益或者共担风险。

**Article 24** When a futures company accepts a client's entrustment to conduct futures trading on his/her behalf, it shall present a risk disclosure statement to the client in advance, obtain the client's signature for confirmation, and then enter into a written contract with the client. A futures company shall not conduct futures trading without the client's entrustment or deviate from the content of the client's entrustment.  
A futures company shall not make profit guarantees to clients; it shall not agree with clients to share profits or bear risks in brokerage business.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　下列单位和个人不得从事期货交易，期货公司不得接受其委托为其进行期货交易：  
(一)国家机关和事业单位；  
(二)国务院期货监督管理机构、期货交易所、期货保证金安全存管监控机构和期货业协会的工作人员；  
(三)证券、期货市场禁止进入者；  
(四)未能提供开户证明材料的单位和个人；  
(五)国务院期货监督管理机构规定不得从事期货交易的其他单位和个人。

**Article 25** The following units and individuals shall not engage in futures trading, and futures companies shall not accept their entrustment to conduct futures trading on their behalf:  
(1) State organs and public institutions;  
(2) Staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, futures exchanges, futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions, and the futures industry association;  
(3) Persons banned from entering the securities and futures markets;  
(4) Units and individuals that fail to provide account opening certification materials;  
(5) Other units and individuals specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council as not allowed to engage in futures trading.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　客户可以通过书面、电话、互联网或者国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他方式，向期货公司下达交易指令。客户的交易指令应当明确、全面。  
期货公司不得隐瞒重要事项或者使用其他不正当手段诱骗客户发出交易指令。

**Article 26** Clients may send trading orders to futures companies in writing, by telephone, through the Internet, or by other means specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council. The clients' trading orders shall be clear and complete.  
A futures company shall not conceal important matters or use other improper means to induce clients to issue trading orders.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　期货交易所应当及时公布上市品种合约的成交量、成交价、持仓量、最高价与最低价、开盘价与收盘价和其他应当公布的即时行情，并保证即时行情的真实、准确。期货交易所不得发布价格预测信息。  
未经期货交易所许可，任何单位和个人不得发布期货交易即时行情。

**Article 27** A futures exchange shall promptly publish the trading volume, trading price, open interest, highest price and lowest price, opening price and closing price of listed variety contracts, and other real-time quotations that shall be published, and shall ensure the truthfulness and accuracy of the real-time quotations. A futures exchange shall not release price forecast information.  
Without the permission of the futures exchange, no unit or individual may release real-time quotations of futures trading.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　期货交易应当严格执行保证金制度。期货交易所向会员、期货公司向客户收取的保证金，不得低于国务院期货监督管理机构、期货交易所规定的标准，并应当与自有资金分开，专户存放。  
期货交易所向会员收取的保证金，属于会员所有，除用于会员的交易结算外，严禁挪作他用。  
期货公司向客户收取的保证金，属于客户所有，除下列可划转的情形外，严禁挪作他用：  
(一)依据客户的要求支付可用资金；  
(二)为客户交存保证金，支付手续费、税款；  
(三)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他情形。

**Article 28** The margin system shall be strictly implemented in futures trading. The margins collected by a futures exchange from members and by a futures company from clients shall not be lower than the standards prescribed by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and the futures exchange, and shall be separated from self-owned funds and kept in special accounts.  
Margins collected by a futures exchange from members belong to the members and shall not be misappropriated for any other purposes except for the settlement of the members' transactions.  
Margins collected by a futures company from clients belong to the clients and shall not be misappropriated for any other purposes except in the following circumstances where transfers may be made:  
(1) Paying available funds upon the client's request;  
(2) Depositing margins on behalf of the client, and paying handling fees and taxes;  
(3) Other circumstances specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　期货公司应当为每一个客户单独开立专门账户、设置交易编码，不得混码交易。

**Article 29** A futures company shall open a separate special account and set up a trading code for each client, and shall not conduct trading with mixed codes.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　期货公司经营期货经纪业务又同时经营其他期货业务的，应当严格执行业务分离和资金分离制度，不得混合操作。

**Article 30** If a futures company operates both futures brokerage business and other futures businesses, it shall strictly implement the system of business separation and fund separation, and shall not mix operations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　期货交易所、期货公司、非期货公司结算会员应当按照国务院期货监督管理机构、财政部门的规定提取、管理和使用风险准备金，不得挪用。

**Article 31** Futures exchanges, futures companies, and non-futures-company clearing members shall set aside, manage, and use risk reserves in accordance with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and the finance department, and shall not misappropriate them.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　期货交易的收费项目、收费标准和管理办法由国务院有关主管部门统一制定并公布。

**Article 32** The fee items, fee rates, and administrative measures for futures trading shall be uniformly formulated and published by the relevant competent authorities of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　期货交易的结算，由期货交易所统一组织进行。  
期货交易所实行当日无负债结算制度。期货交易所应当在当日及时将结算结果通知会员。  
期货公司根据期货交易所的结算结果对客户进行结算，并应当将结算结果按照与客户约定的方式及时通知客户。客户应当及时查询并妥善处理自己的交易持仓。

**Article 33** The clearing of futures trading shall be uniformly organized by the futures exchange.  
A futures exchange shall implement a same-day no-debt settlement system. The futures exchange shall promptly notify members of the settlement results on the same day.  
A futures company shall settle with clients based on the settlement results of the futures exchange, and shall promptly notify clients of the settlement results in the manner agreed with the clients. Clients shall promptly inquire about and properly handle their own trading positions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　期货交易所会员的保证金不足时，应当及时追加保证金或者自行平仓。会员未在期货交易所规定的时间内追加保证金或者自行平仓的，期货交易所应当将该会员的合约强行平仓，强行平仓的有关费用和发生的损失由该会员承担。  
客户保证金不足时，应当及时追加保证金或者自行平仓。客户未在期货公司规定的时间内及时追加保证金或者自行平仓的，期货公司应当将该客户的合约强行平仓，强行平仓的有关费用和发生的损失由该客户承担。

**Article 34** When a member of a futures exchange has insufficient margin, it shall promptly add margin or close out positions on its own. If the member fails to add margin or close out positions on its own within the time limit prescribed by the futures exchange, the futures exchange shall forcibly close out the member's contracts, and the relevant expenses and losses incurred from the forced close-out shall be borne by the member.  
When a client has insufficient margin, the client shall promptly add margin or close out positions on its own. If the client fails to promptly add margin or close out positions within the time limit prescribed by the futures company, the futures company shall forcibly close out the client's contracts, and the relevant expenses and losses incurred from the forced close-out shall be borne by the client.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　期货交易的交割，由期货交易所统一组织进行。  
交割仓库由期货交易所指定。期货交易所不得限制实物交割总量，并应当与交割仓库签订协议，明确双方的权利和义务。交割仓库不得有下列行为：  
(一)出具虚假仓单；  
(二)违反期货交易所业务规则，限制交割商品的入库、出库；  
(三)泄露与期货交易有关的商业秘密；  
(四)违反国家有关规定参与期货交易；  
(五)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他行为。

**Article 35** Delivery of futures trading shall be uniformly organized by the futures exchange.  
Delivery warehouses shall be designated by the futures exchange. The futures exchange shall not restrict the total volume of physical delivery and shall enter into an agreement with the delivery warehouse specifying the rights and obligations of both parties. A delivery warehouse shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Issuing false warehouse receipts;  
(2) Violating the business rules of the futures exchange by restricting the entry or exit of delivery commodities;  
(3) Disclosing trade secrets related to futures trading;  
(4) Participating in futures trading in violation of relevant state provisions;  
(5) Other acts specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　会员在期货交易中违约的，期货交易所先以该会员的保证金承担违约责任；保证金不足的，期货交易所应当以风险准备金和自有资金代为承担违约责任，并由此取得对该会员的相应追偿权。  
客户在期货交易中违约的，期货公司先以该客户的保证金承担违约责任；保证金不足的，期货公司应当以风险准备金和自有资金代为承担违约责任，并由此取得对该客户的相应追偿权。

**Article 36** If a member defaults in futures trading, the futures exchange shall first bear the liability for breach of contract with the member's margin; if the margin is insufficient, the futures exchange shall bear the liability for breach of contract on behalf of the member using its risk reserve and self-owned funds, and thereby acquire the corresponding right of recourse against the member.  
If a client defaults in futures trading, the futures company shall first bear the liability for breach of contract with the client's margin; if the margin is insufficient, the futures company shall bear the liability for breach of contract on behalf of the client using its risk reserve and self-owned funds, and thereby acquire the corresponding right of recourse against the client.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实行会员分级结算制度的期货交易所，应当向结算会员收取结算担保金。期货交易所只对结算会员结算，收取和追收保证金，以结算担保金、风险准备金、自有资金代为承担违约责任，以及采取其他相关措施；对非结算会员的结算、收取和追收保证金、代为承担违约责任，以及采取其他相关措施，由结算会员执行。

**Article 37** A futures exchange that implements a tiered membership settlement system shall collect settlement guarantee funds from clearing members. The futures exchange shall only settle with clearing members, collect and demand margins, bear the liability for breach of contract on behalf of parties using the settlement guarantee funds, risk reserves, and self-owned funds, and take other related measures; settlement with non-clearing members, collection and demand of margins, assumption of liability for breach of contract on behalf of parties, and other related measures shall be executed by clearing members.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　期货交易所、期货公司和非期货公司结算会员应当保证期货交易、结算、交割资料的完整和安全。

**Article 38** Futures exchanges, futures companies, and non-futures-company clearing members shall ensure the completeness and security of futures trading, clearing, and delivery records.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　任何单位或者个人不得编造、传播有关期货交易的虚假信息，不得恶意串通、联手买卖或者以其他方式操纵期货交易价格。

**Article 39** No unit or individual may fabricate or disseminate false information about futures trading, or maliciously collude, act in concert to buy or sell, or otherwise manipulate futures trading prices.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　任何单位或者个人不得违规使用信贷资金、财政资金进行期货交易。  
银行业金融机构从事期货交易融资或者担保业务的资格，由国务院银行业监督管理机构批准。

**Article 40** No unit or individual may use credit funds or fiscal funds in violation of regulations for futures trading.  
The qualification of banking financial institutions to engage in futures trading financing or guarantee business shall be approved by the banking regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国有以及国有控股企业进行境内外期货交易，应当遵循套期保值的原则，严格遵守国务院国有资产监督管理机构以及其他有关部门关于企业以国有资产进入期货市场的有关规定。

**Article 41** State-owned enterprises and state-controlled enterprises conducting domestic and overseas futures trading shall follow the principle of hedging and strictly comply with the relevant provisions of the state-owned assets regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments regarding enterprises using state-owned assets to enter the futures market.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　境外期货项下购汇、结汇以及外汇收支，应当符合国家外汇管理有关规定。  
境内单位或者个人从事境外期货交易的办法，由国务院期货监督管理机构会同国务院商务主管部门、国有资产监督管理机构、银行业监督管理机构、外汇管理部门等有关部门制订，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 42** The purchase, sale, and receipt and payment of foreign exchange under overseas futures shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state foreign exchange administration.  
The measures for domestic units or individuals to engage in overseas futures trading shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council together with the competent department of commerce of the State Council, the state-owned assets regulatory authority, the banking regulatory authority, the foreign exchange administration department, and other relevant departments, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

### 第五章　期货业协会

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　期货业协会是期货业的自律性组织，是社会团体法人。  
期货公司以及其他专门从事期货经营的机构应当加入期货业协会，并缴纳会员费。

**Article 43** The futures industry association is a self-regulatory organization of the futures industry and is a social organization legal person.  
Futures companies and other institutions specializing in futures operations shall join the futures industry association and pay membership fees.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　期货业协会的权力机构为全体会员组成的会员大会。  
期货业协会的章程由会员大会制定，并报国务院期货监督管理机构备案。  
期货业协会设理事会。理事会成员按照章程的规定选举产生。

**Article 44** The governing body of the futures industry association is the general assembly of all members.  
The articles of association of the futures industry association shall be formulated by the general assembly and submitted to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation.  
The futures industry association shall have a council. The members of the council shall be elected in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　期货业协会履行下列职责：  
(一)教育和组织会员遵守期货法律法规和政策；  
(二)制定会员应当遵守的行业自律性规则，监督、检查会员行为，对违反协会章程和自律性规则的，按照规定给予纪律处分；  
(三)负责期货从业人员资格的认定、管理以及撤销工作；  
(四)受理客户与期货业务有关的投诉，对会员之间、会员与客户之间发生的纠纷进行调解；  
(五)依法维护会员的合法权益，向国务院期货监督管理机构反映会员的建议和要求；  
(六)组织期货从业人员的业务培训，开展会员间的业务交流；  
(七)组织会员就期货业的发展、运作以及有关内容进行研究；  
(八)期货业协会章程规定的其他职责。  
期货业协会的业务活动应当接受国务院期货监督管理机构的指导和监督。

**Article 45** The futures industry association shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Educating and organizing members to comply with futures laws, regulations, and policies;  
(2) Formulating industry self-regulatory rules that members shall follow, supervising and inspecting members' conduct, and imposing disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the regulations for violations of the association's articles of association and self-regulatory rules;  
(3) Being responsible for the recognition, management, and revocation of futures practitioner qualifications;  
(4) Receiving complaints from clients related to futures business, and mediating disputes between members or between members and clients;  
(5) Safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of members in accordance with the law, and reporting members' suggestions and requests to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(6) Organizing professional training for futures practitioners and facilitating business exchanges among members;  
(7) Organizing members to conduct research on the development, operation, and related aspects of the futures industry;  
(8) Other duties specified in the articles of association of the futures industry association.  
The business activities of the futures industry association shall be subject to the guidance and supervision of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院期货监督管理机构对期货市场实施监督管理，依法履行下列职责：  
(一)制定有关期货市场监督管理的规章、规则，并依法行使审批权；  
(二)对品种的上市、交易、结算、交割等期货交易及其相关活动，进行监督管理；  
(三)对期货交易所、期货公司及其他期货经营机构、非期货公司结算会员、期货保证金安全存管监控机构、期货保证金存管银行、交割仓库等市场相关参与者的期货业务活动，进行监督管理；  
(四)制定期货从业人员的资格标准和管理办法，并监督实施；  
(五)监督检查期货交易的信息公开情况；  
(六)对期货业协会的活动进行指导和监督；  
(七)对违反期货市场监督管理法律、行政法规的行为进行查处；  
(八)开展与期货市场监督管理有关的国际交流、合作活动；  
(九)法律、行政法规规定的其他职责。

**Article 46** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council exercises supervision and administration over the futures market and performs the following duties in accordance with the law:  
(1) Formulating rules and regulations concerning the supervision and administration of the futures market, and exercising examination and approval powers in accordance with the law;  
(2) Supervising and administering futures trading and related activities such as the listing, trading, clearing, and delivery of varieties;  
(3) Supervising and administering the futures business activities of market participants including futures exchanges, futures companies and other futures business institutions, non-futures-company clearing members, futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions, futures margin custodian banks, and delivery warehouses;  
(4) Formulating qualification standards and management measures for futures practitioners and supervising their implementation;  
(5) Supervising and inspecting the information disclosure of futures trading;  
(6) Guiding and supervising the activities of the futures industry association;  
(7) Investigating and punishing acts that violate laws and administrative regulations on futures market supervision and administration;  
(8) Conducting international exchanges and cooperation related to futures market supervision and administration;  
(9) Other duties specified by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)对期货交易所、期货公司及其他期货经营机构、非期货公司结算会员、期货保证金安全存管监控机构和交割仓库进行现场检查；  
(二)进入涉嫌违法行为发生场所调查取证；  
(三)询问当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人，要求其对与被调查事件有关的事项作出说明；  
(四)查阅、复制与被调查事件有关的财产权登记等资料；  
(五)查阅、复制当事人和与被调查事件有关的单位和个人的期货交易记录、财务会计资料以及其他相关文件和资料；对可能被转移、隐匿或者毁损的文件和资料，可以予以封存；  
(六)查询与被调查事件有关的单位的保证金账户和银行账户；  
(七)在调查操纵期货交易价格、内幕交易等重大期货违法行为时，经国务院期货监督管理机构主要负责人批准，可以限制被调查事件当事人的期货交易，但限制的时间不得超过15个交易日；案情复杂的，可以延长至30个交易日；  
(八)法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。

**Article 47** When performing its duties in accordance with the law, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may take the following measures:  
(1) Conducting on-site inspections of futures exchanges, futures companies and other futures business institutions, non-futures-company clearing members, futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions, and delivery warehouses;  
(2) Entering the places where suspected illegal acts occur to investigate and collect evidence;  
(3) Inquiring of parties and units or individuals related to the matter under investigation, and requiring them to provide explanations on matters related to the investigation;  
(4) Inspecting and copying materials such as property right registration records related to the matter under investigation;  
(5) Inspecting and copying the futures trading records, financial accounting materials, and other related documents and materials of the parties and units or individuals related to the matter under investigation; documents and materials that may be transferred, concealed, or destroyed may be sealed up;  
(6) Querying the margin accounts and bank accounts of units related to the matter under investigation;  
(7) When investigating major futures illegal acts such as manipulation of futures trading prices or insider trading, upon approval by the principal responsible person of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, restricting the futures trading of the parties involved in the matter under investigation, provided that the restriction period shall not exceed 15 trading days; where the case is complicated, it may be extended to 30 trading days;  
(8) Other measures specified by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　期货交易所、期货公司及其他期货经营机构、期货保证金安全存管监控机构，应当向国务院期货监督管理机构报送财务会计报告、业务资料和其他有关资料。  
对期货公司及其他期货经营机构报送的年度报告，国务院期货监督管理机构应当指定专人进行审核，并制作审核报告。审核人员应当在审核报告上签字。审核中发现问题的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当及时采取相应措施。  
必要时，国务院期货监督管理机构可以要求非期货公司结算会员、交割仓库，以及期货公司股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人报送相关资料。

**Article 48** Futures exchanges, futures companies and other futures business institutions, and futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions shall submit financial accounting reports, business materials, and other related materials to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
With respect to the annual reports submitted by futures companies and other futures business institutions, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall designate specialized persons to review them and prepare review reports. The reviewers shall sign the review reports. If problems are discovered during the review, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall promptly take corresponding measures.  
When necessary, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may require non-futures-company clearing members, delivery warehouses, and shareholders, actual controllers, or other related persons of futures companies to submit relevant materials.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院期货监督管理机构依法履行职责，进行监督检查或者调查时，被检查、调查的单位和个人应当配合，如实提供有关文件和资料，不得拒绝、阻碍和隐瞒；其他有关部门和单位应当给予支持和配合。

**Article 49** When the futures regulatory authority of the State Council performs its duties in accordance with the law and conducts supervision and inspection or investigation, the units and individuals being inspected or investigated shall cooperate, truthfully provide relevant documents and materials, and shall not refuse, obstruct, or conceal; other relevant departments and units shall provide support and cooperation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家根据期货市场发展的需要，设立期货投资者保障基金。  
期货投资者保障基金的筹集、管理和使用的具体办法，由国务院期货监督管理机构会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 50** The State shall establish a futures investor protection fund based on the needs of the development of the futures market.  
The specific measures for the raising, management, and use of the futures investor protection fund shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council together with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院期货监督管理机构应当建立、健全保证金安全存管监控制度，设立期货保证金安全存管监控机构。  
客户和期货交易所、期货公司及其他期货经营机构、非期货公司结算会员以及期货保证金存管银行，应当遵守国务院期货监督管理机构有关保证金安全存管监控的规定。

**Article 51** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish and improve a margin safe custody monitoring system and establish a futures margin safe custody monitoring institution.  
Clients, futures exchanges, futures companies and other futures business institutions, non-futures-company clearing members, and futures margin custodian banks shall comply with the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on margin safe custody monitoring.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　期货保证金安全存管监控机构依照有关规定对保证金安全实施监控，进行每日稽核，发现问题应当立即报告国务院期货监督管理机构。国务院期货监督管理机构应当根据不同情况，依照本条例有关规定及时处理。

**Article 52** The futures margin safe custody monitoring institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, monitor the safety of margins, conduct daily audits, and promptly report any discovered problems to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council. The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall, based on the different circumstances, handle the matter promptly in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院期货监督管理机构对期货交易所和期货保证金安全存管监控机构的董事、监事、高级管理人员，实行资格管理制度。

**Article 53** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall implement a qualification management system for the directors, supervisors, and senior officers of futures exchanges and futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院期货监督管理机构应当制定期货公司持续性经营规则，对期货公司的净资本与净资产的比例，净资本与境内期货经纪、境外期货经纪等业务规模的比例，流动资产与流动负债的比例等风险监管指标作出规定；对期货公司及其分支机构的经营条件、风险管理、内部控制、保证金存管、关联交易等方面提出要求。

**Article 54** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall formulate continuing operation rules for futures companies, and stipulate risk supervision indicators such as the ratio of net capital to net assets, the ratio of net capital to the scale of domestic futures brokerage, overseas futures brokerage, and other businesses, and the ratio of current assets to current liabilities; it shall set requirements for futures companies and their branches regarding operating conditions, risk management, internal control, margin custody, and related party transactions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　期货公司及其分支机构不符合持续性经营规则或者出现经营风险的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以对期货公司及其董事、监事和高级管理人员采取谈话、提示、记入信用记录等监管措施或者责令期货公司限期整改，并对其整改情况进行检查验收。  
期货公司逾期未改正，其行为严重危及期货公司的稳健运行、损害客户合法权益，或者涉嫌严重违法违规正在被国务院期货监督管理机构调查的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以区别情形，对其采取下列措施：  
(一)限制或者暂停部分期货业务；  
(二)停止批准新增业务；  
(三)限制分配红利，限制向董事、监事、高级管理人员支付报酬、提供福利；  
(四)限制转让财产或者在财产上设定其他权利；  
(五)责令更换董事、监事、高级管理人员或者有关业务部门、分支机构的负责人员，或者限制其权利；  
(六)限制期货公司自有资金或者风险准备金的调拨和使用；  
(七)责令控股股东转让股权或者限制有关股东行使股东权利。  
对经过整改符合有关法律、行政法规规定以及持续性经营规则要求的期货公司，国务院期货监督管理机构应当自验收完毕之日起3日内解除对其采取的有关措施。  
对经过整改仍未达到持续性经营规则要求，严重影响正常经营的期货公司，国务院期货监督管理机构有权撤销其部分或者全部期货业务许可、关闭其分支机构。

**Article 55** If a futures company or its branch fails to comply with the continuing operation rules or incurs operational risks, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may take supervisory measures such as conducting talks, issuing warnings, or recording in credit records against the futures company and its directors, supervisors, and senior officers, or order the futures company to rectify within a prescribed time limit, and inspect and accept the rectification.  
If the futures company fails to rectify within the prescribed time limit, and its conduct seriously endangers the sound operation of the futures company, harms the legitimate rights and interests of clients, or is suspected of serious illegal or disciplinary acts and is under investigation by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may, depending on the circumstances, take the following measures against it:  
(1) Restrict or suspend part of the futures business;  
(2) Cease approving new business;  
(3) Restrict the distribution of dividends, restrict the payment of remuneration and provision of benefits to directors, supervisors, and senior officers;  
(4) Restrict the transfer of property or the creation of other rights on property;  
(5) Order the replacement of directors, supervisors, senior officers, or responsible persons of relevant business departments or branches, or restrict their rights;  
(6) Restrict the transfer and use of the futures company's self-owned funds or risk reserves;  
(7) Order the controlling shareholder to transfer equity or restrict the relevant shareholders from exercising their shareholder rights.  
For a futures company that, after rectification, complies with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and continuing operation rules, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall lift the measures taken against it within three days from the date of completion of the acceptance inspection.  
For a futures company that, after rectification, still fails to meet the continuing operation rules and seriously affects normal operations, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council has the right to revoke part or all of its futures business licenses and close its branches.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　期货公司违法经营或者出现重大风险，严重危害期货市场秩序、损害客户利益的，国务院期货监督管理机构可以对该期货公司采取责令停业整顿、指定其他机构托管或者接管等监管措施。经国务院期货监督管理机构批准，可以对该期货公司直接负责的董事、监事、高级管理人员和其他直接责任人员采取以下措施：  
(一)通知出境管理机关依法阻止其出境；  
(二)申请司法机关禁止其转移、转让或者以其他方式处分财产，或者在财产上设定其他权利。

**Article 56** If a futures company operates illegally or incurs major risks, seriously endangering the order of the futures market and harming the interests of clients, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may take supervisory measures such as ordering the company to suspend operations for rectification, designating another institution to take over or manage the company. With the approval of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, the following measures may be taken against the directors, supervisors, senior officers, and other persons directly responsible of the futures company:  
(1) Notifying the exit control authority to prevent them from leaving the country in accordance with the law;  
(2) Applying to the judicial authorities to prohibit them from transferring, assigning, or otherwise disposing of property, or creating other rights on property.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　期货公司的股东有虚假出资或者抽逃出资行为的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当责令其限期改正，并可责令其转让所持期货公司的股权。  
在股东按照前款要求改正违法行为、转让所持期货公司的股权前，国务院期货监督管理机构可以限制其股东权利。

**Article 57** If a shareholder of a futures company makes false capital contributions or withdraws capital contributions, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit and may order it to transfer the equity it holds in the futures company.  
Before the shareholder rectifies the illegal act and transfers the equity it holds in the futures company in accordance with the requirements of the preceding paragraph, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may restrict its shareholder rights.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　当期货市场出现异常情况时，国务院期货监督管理机构可以采取必要的风险处置措施。

**Article 58** When abnormal circumstances arise in the futures market, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council may take necessary risk disposal measures.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　期货公司的交易软件、结算软件，应当满足期货公司审慎经营和风险管理以及国务院期货监督管理机构有关保证金安全存管监控规定的要求。期货公司的交易软件、结算软件不符合要求的，国务院期货监督管理机构有权要求期货公司予以改进或者更换。  
国务院期货监督管理机构可以要求期货公司的交易软件、结算软件的供应商提供该软件的相关资料，供应商应当予以配合。国务院期货监督管理机构对供应商提供的相关资料负有保密义务。

**Article 59** The trading software and clearing software of a futures company shall meet the requirements of prudent operation and risk management of the futures company and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on margin safe custody monitoring. If the trading software or clearing software of a futures company does not meet the requirements, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council has the right to require the futures company to improve or replace it.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may require the supplier of the trading software or clearing software of a futures company to provide relevant materials of the software, and the supplier shall cooperate. The futures regulatory authority of the State Council has a duty to maintain the confidentiality of the relevant materials provided by the supplier.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　期货公司涉及重大诉讼、仲裁，或者股权被冻结或者用于担保，以及发生其他重大事件时，期货公司及其相关股东、实际控制人应当自该事件发生之日起5日内向国务院期货监督管理机构提交书面报告。

**Article 60** When a futures company is involved in major litigation or arbitration, or its equity is frozen or used as guarantee, or other major events occur, the futures company and its relevant shareholders and actual controllers shall submit a written report to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council within five days from the date of the occurrence of the event.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等中介服务机构向期货交易所和期货公司等市场相关参与者提供相关服务时，应当遵守期货法律、行政法规以及国家有关规定，并按照国务院期货监督管理机构的要求提供相关资料。

**Article 61** When providing relevant services to market participants such as futures exchanges and futures companies, intermediary service institutions such as accounting firms, law firms, and asset appraisal institutions shall comply with futures laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, and provide relevant materials as required by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国务院期货监督管理机构应当与有关部门建立监督管理的信息共享和协调配合机制。  
国务院期货监督管理机构可以和其他国家或者地区的期货监督管理机构建立监督管理合作机制，实施跨境监督管理。

**Article 62** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall establish information sharing and coordination mechanisms with relevant departments for supervision and administration.  
The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may establish supervisory cooperation mechanisms with futures regulatory authorities of other countries or regions to implement cross-border supervision and administration.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国务院期货监督管理机构、期货交易所、期货保证金安全存管监控机构和期货保证金存管银行等相关单位的工作人员，应当忠于职守，依法办事，公正廉洁，保守国家秘密和有关当事人的商业秘密，不得利用职务便利牟取不正当的利益。

**Article 63** Staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, futures exchanges, futures margin safe custody monitoring institutions, futures margin custodian banks, and other relevant units shall be devoted to their duties, act in accordance with the law, be fair and honest, keep state secrets and trade secrets of the relevant parties, and shall not take advantage of their positions to seek improper gains.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　期货交易所、非期货公司结算会员有下列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得：  
(一)违反规定接纳会员的；  
(二)违反规定收取手续费的；  
(三)违反规定使用、分配收益的；  
(四)不按照规定公布即时行情的，或者发布价格预测信息的；  
(五)不按照规定向国务院期货监督管理机构履行报告义务的；  
(六)不按照规定向国务院期货监督管理机构报送有关文件、资料的；  
(七)不按照规定建立、健全结算担保金制度的；  
(八)不按照规定提取、管理和使用风险准备金的；  
(九)违反国务院期货监督管理机构有关保证金安全存管监控规定的；  
(十)限制会员实物交割总量的；  
(十一)任用不具备资格的期货从业人员的；  
(十二)违反国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他行为。  
有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予纪律处分，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
有本条第一款第二项所列行为的，应当责令退还多收取的手续费。  
期货保证金安全存管监控机构有本条第一款第五项、第六项、第九项、第十一项、第十二项所列行为的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚、处分。期货保证金存管银行有本条第一款第九项、第十二项所列行为的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚、处分。

**Article 64** If a futures exchange or a non-futures-company clearing member commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, and have its illegal gains confiscated:  
(1) Admitting members in violation of regulations;  
(2) Charging handling fees in violation of regulations;  
(3) Using or distributing income in violation of regulations;  
(4) Failing to publish real-time quotations in accordance with regulations, or releasing price forecast information;  
(5) Failing to perform the reporting obligation to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with regulations;  
(6) Failing to submit relevant documents and materials to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with regulations;  
(7) Failing to establish or improve a settlement guarantee fund system in accordance with regulations;  
(8) Failing to set aside, manage, or use risk reserves in accordance with regulations;  
(9) Violating the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on margin safe custody monitoring;  
(10) Restricting the total volume of physical delivery of members;  
(11) Employing futures practitioners without proper qualifications;  
(12) Other acts violating the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph is committed, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.  
If an act listed in item (2) of the first paragraph of this Article is committed, the excess handling fees collected shall be ordered to be refunded.  
If a futures margin safe custody monitoring institution commits any of the acts listed in items (5), (6), (9), (11), or (12) of the first paragraph, it shall be punished and sanctioned in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article. If a futures margin custodian bank commits any of the acts listed in items (9) or (12) of the first paragraph, it shall be punished and sanctioned in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　期货交易所有下列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)未经批准，擅自办理[本条例第十三条](#art-13)所列事项的；  
(二)允许会员在保证金不足的情况下进行期货交易的；  
(三)直接或者间接参与期货交易，或者违反规定从事与其职责无关的业务的；  
(四)违反规定收取保证金，或者挪用保证金的；  
(五)伪造、涂改或者不按照规定保存期货交易、结算、交割资料的；  
(六)未建立或者未执行当日无负债结算、涨跌停板、持仓限额和大户持仓报告制度的；  
(七)拒绝或者妨碍国务院期货监督管理机构监督检查的；  
(八)违反国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他行为。  
有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予纪律处分，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
非期货公司结算会员有本条第一款第二项、第四项至第八项所列行为之一的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚、处分。  
期货保证金安全存管监控机构有本条第一款第三项、第七项、第八项所列行为的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚、处分。

**Article 65** If a futures exchange commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification:  
(1) Handling matters listed in Article 13 of these Regulations without authorization;  
(2) Allowing members to conduct futures trading when margins are insufficient;  
(3) Directly or indirectly participating in futures trading, or engaging in businesses unrelated to its duties in violation of regulations;  
(4) Collecting margins in violation of regulations, or misappropriating margins;  
(5) Forging, altering, or failing to preserve futures trading, clearing, and delivery records in accordance with regulations;  
(6) Failing to establish or implement the same-day no-debt settlement, price limit, position limit, and large position reporting systems;  
(7) Refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(8) Other acts violating the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph is committed, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.  
If a non-futures-company clearing member commits any of the acts listed in items (2) or (4) through (8) of the first paragraph, it shall be punished and sanctioned in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.  
If a futures margin safe custody monitoring institution commits any of the acts listed in items (3), (7), or (8) of the first paragraph, it shall be punished and sanctioned in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　期货公司有下列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销期货业务许可证：  
(一)接受不符合规定条件的单位或者个人委托的；  
(二)允许客户在保证金不足的情况下进行期货交易的；  
(三)未经批准，擅自办理[本条例第十九条](#art-19)所列事项的；  
(四)违反规定从事与期货业务无关的活动的；  
(五)从事或者变相从事期货自营业务的；  
(六)为其股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人提供融资，或者对外担保的；  
(七)违反国务院期货监督管理机构有关保证金安全存管监控规定的；  
(八)不按照规定向国务院期货监督管理机构履行报告义务或者报送有关文件、资料的；  
(九)交易软件、结算软件不符合期货公司审慎经营和风险管理以及国务院期货监督管理机构有关保证金安全存管监控规定的要求的；  
(十)不按照规定提取、管理和使用风险准备金的；  
(十一)伪造、涂改或者不按照规定保存期货交易、结算、交割资料的；  
(十二)任用不具备资格的期货从业人员的；  
(十三)伪造、变造、出租、出借、买卖期货业务许可证或者经营许可证的；  
(十四)进行混码交易的；  
(十五)拒绝或者妨碍国务院期货监督管理机构监督检查的；  
(十六)违反国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他行为。  
期货公司有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂停或者撤销期货从业人员资格。  
期货公司之外的其他期货经营机构有本条第一款第八项、第十二项、第十三项、第十五项、第十六项所列行为的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚。  
期货公司的股东、实际控制人或者其他关联人未经批准擅自委托他人或者接受他人委托持有或者管理期货公司股权的，拒不配合国务院期货监督管理机构的检查，拒不按照规定履行报告义务、提供有关信息和资料，或者报送、提供的信息和资料有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚。

**Article 66** If a futures company commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or have its futures business license revoked:  
(1) Accepting entrustment from units or individuals that do not meet the prescribed conditions;  
(2) Allowing clients to conduct futures trading when margins are insufficient;  
(3) Handling matters listed in Article 19 of these Regulations without authorization;  
(4) Engaging in activities unrelated to futures business in violation of regulations;  
(5) Engaging in or engaging in disguised form in futures proprietary trading;  
(6) Providing financing to its shareholders, actual controllers, or other related persons, or providing external guarantees;  
(7) Violating the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on margin safe custody monitoring;  
(8) Failing to perform the reporting obligation or submit relevant documents and materials to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with regulations;  
(9) The trading software or clearing software does not meet the requirements of prudent operation and risk management of the futures company and the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council on margin safe custody monitoring;  
(10) Failing to set aside, manage, or use risk reserves in accordance with regulations;  
(11) Forging, altering, or failing to preserve futures trading, clearing, and delivery records in accordance with regulations;  
(12) Employing futures practitioners without proper qualifications;  
(13) Forging, altering, leasing, lending, or buying or selling futures business licenses or operating licenses;  
(14) Conducting trading with mixed codes;  
(15) Refusing or obstructing supervision and inspection by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council;  
(16) Other acts violating the provisions of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If a futures company commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, their futures practitioner qualifications shall be suspended or revoked.  
If any futures business institution other than a futures company commits any of the acts listed in items (8), (12), (13), (15), or (16) of the first paragraph, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.  
If a shareholder, actual controller, or other related person of a futures company, without authorization, entrusts others or accepts entrustment from others to hold or manage the equity of the futures company, refuses to cooperate with the inspection of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, refuses to perform the reporting obligation or provide relevant information and materials in accordance with regulations, or the information and materials submitted or provided contain false records, misleading statements, or material omissions, such person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　期货公司有下列欺诈客户行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销期货业务许可证：  
(一)向客户作获利保证或者不按照规定向客户出示风险说明书的；  
(二)在经纪业务中与客户约定分享利益、共担风险的；  
(三)不按照规定接受客户委托或者不按照客户委托内容擅自进行期货交易的；  
(四)隐瞒重要事项或者使用其他不正当手段，诱骗客户发出交易指令的；  
(五)向客户提供虚假成交回报的；  
(六)未将客户交易指令下达到期货交易所的；  
(七)挪用客户保证金的；  
(八)不按照规定在期货保证金存管银行开立保证金账户，或者违规划转客户保证金的；  
(九)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他欺诈客户的行为。  
期货公司有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂停或者撤销期货从业人员资格。  
任何单位或者个人编造并且传播有关期货交易的虚假信息，扰乱期货交易市场的，依照本条第一款、第二款的规定处罚。

**Article 67** If a futures company commits any of the following acts of defrauding clients, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or have its futures business license revoked:  
(1) Making profit guarantees to clients or failing to present a risk disclosure statement to clients in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Agreeing with clients to share profits or bear risks in brokerage business;  
(3) Failing to accept clients' entrustment in accordance with regulations or conducting futures trading without following the content of the clients' entrustment;  
(4) Concealing important matters or using other improper means to induce clients to issue trading orders;  
(5) Providing false transaction confirmations to clients;  
(6) Failing to transmit clients' trading orders to the futures exchange;  
(7) Misappropriating clients' margins;  
(8) Failing to open margin accounts at a futures margin custodian bank in accordance with regulations, or transferring clients' margins in violation of regulations;  
(9) Other acts of defrauding clients specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If a futures company commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, their futures practitioner qualifications shall be suspended or revoked.  
If any unit or individual fabricates and disseminates false information about futures trading, disrupting the futures trading market, such unit or individual shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　期货公司及其他期货经营机构、非期货公司结算会员、期货保证金存管银行提供虚假申请文件或者采取其他欺诈手段隐瞒重要事实骗取期货业务许可的，撤销其期货业务许可，没收违法所得。

**Article 68** If a futures company or other futures business institution, a non-futures-company clearing member, or a futures margin custodian bank provides false application documents or uses other fraudulent means to conceal material facts in order to obtain a futures business license, its futures business license shall be revoked and its illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　期货交易内幕信息的知情人或者非法获取期货交易内幕信息的人，在对期货交易价格有重大影响的信息尚未公开前，利用内幕信息从事期货交易，或者向他人泄露内幕信息，使他人利用内幕信息进行期货交易的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。单位从事内幕交易的，还应当对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处3万元以上30万元以下的罚款。  
国务院期货监督管理机构、期货交易所和期货保证金安全存管监控机构的工作人员进行内幕交易的，从重处罚。

**Article 69** If a person who is in the know of inside information on futures trading or who illegally obtains inside information on futures trading, before the information that may have a significant impact on futures trading prices is made public, uses the inside information to engage in futures trading, or discloses the inside information to others, causing others to use the inside information for futures trading, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed. If a unit engages in insider trading, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall also be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan.  
If a staff member of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, a futures exchange, or a futures margin safe custody monitoring institution engages in insider trading, a heavier punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　任何单位或者个人有下列行为之一，操纵期货交易价格的，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款：  
(一)单独或者合谋，集中资金优势、持仓优势或者利用信息优势联合或者连续买卖合约，操纵期货交易价格的；  
(二)蓄意串通，按事先约定的时间、价格和方式相互进行期货交易，影响期货交易价格或者期货交易量的；  
(三)以自己为交易对象，自买自卖，影响期货交易价格或者期货交易量的；  
(四)为影响期货市场行情囤积现货的；  
(五)国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他操纵期货交易价格的行为。  
单位有前款所列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 70** If any unit or individual commits any of the following acts to manipulate futures trading prices, it shall be ordered to rectify, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Individually or in collusion, concentrating capital advantages, position advantages, or using information advantages to jointly or continuously buy or sell contracts, manipulating futures trading prices;  
(2) Deliberately colluding to conduct futures transactions with each other at pre-arranged times, prices, and manners, affecting futures trading prices or volumes;  
(3) Trading with oneself as the counterparty, buying and selling the same contracts, affecting futures trading prices or volumes;  
(4) Hoarding physical commodities to influence futures market trends;  
(5) Other acts of manipulating futures trading prices specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.  
If a unit commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　交割仓库有[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)第二款所列行为之一的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令期货交易所暂停或者取消其交割仓库资格。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** If a delivery warehouse commits any of the acts listed in Article 35, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, it shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the futures exchange shall be ordered to suspend or revoke its qualification as a delivery warehouse. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国有以及国有控股企业违反本条例和国务院国有资产监督管理机构以及其他有关部门关于企业以国有资产进入期货市场的有关规定进行期货交易，或者单位、个人违规使用信贷资金、财政资金进行期货交易的，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满10万元的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分。

**Article 72** If a state-owned enterprise or a state-controlled enterprise conducts futures trading in violation of these Regulations and the relevant provisions of the state-owned assets regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments regarding enterprises using state-owned assets to enter the futures market, or if a unit or individual uses credit funds or fiscal funds in violation of regulations for futures trading, a warning shall be given, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from demotion to dismissal.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　境内单位或者个人违反规定从事境外期货交易的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满20万元的，并处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，暂停其境外期货交易。对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 73** If a domestic unit or individual engages in overseas futures trading in violation of regulations, it shall be ordered to rectify, given a warning, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, its overseas futures trading shall be suspended. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　非法设立期货交易场所或者以其他形式组织期货交易活动的，由所在地县级以上地方人民政府予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
非法设立期货公司及其他期货经营机构，或者擅自从事期货业务的，予以取缔，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不满20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 74** If a futures trading venue is illegally established or futures trading activities are organized in other forms, the local people's government at or above the county level shall ban it, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.  
If a futures company or other futures business institution is illegally established, or if futures business is engaged in without authorization, it shall be banned, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　期货公司的交易软件、结算软件供应商拒不配合国务院期货监督管理机构调查，或者未按照规定向国务院期货监督管理机构提供相关软件资料，或者提供的软件资料有虚假、重大遗漏的，责令改正，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 75** If the supplier of trading software or clearing software of a futures company refuses to cooperate with the investigation of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, or fails to provide relevant software materials to the futures regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with regulations, or the provided software materials contain falsehoods or material omissions, it shall be ordered to rectify and be fined not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　会计师事务所、律师事务所、资产评估机构等中介服务机构未勤勉尽责，所出具的文件有虚假记载、误导性陈述或者重大遗漏的，责令改正，没收业务收入，暂停或者撤销相关业务许可，并处业务收入1倍以上5倍以下的罚款。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告，并处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 76** If an intermediary service institution such as an accounting firm, law firm, or asset appraisal institution fails to exercise due diligence, and the documents it issues contain false records, misleading statements, or material omissions, it shall be ordered to rectify, have its business income confiscated, have its relevant business license suspended or revoked, and be fined not less than one time but not more than five times the business income. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning and be fined not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　任何单位或者个人违反本条例规定，情节严重的，由国务院期货监督管理机构宣布该个人、该单位或者该单位的直接责任人员为期货市场禁止进入者。

**Article 77** If any unit or individual violates the provisions of these Regulations and the circumstances are serious, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall declare such individual, such unit, or the directly responsible persons of such unit as persons banned from entering the futures market.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国务院期货监督管理机构、期货交易所、期货保证金安全存管监控机构和期货保证金存管银行等相关单位的工作人员，泄露知悉的国家秘密或者会员、客户商业秘密，或者徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、滥用职权、收受贿赂的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 78** If a staff member of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council, a futures exchange, a futures margin safe custody monitoring institution, a futures margin custodian bank, or other relevant unit discloses state secrets or trade secrets of members or clients that he/she knows, or engages in favoritism, dereliction of duty, abuse of power, or acceptance of bribes, he/she shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 79** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　对本条例规定的违法行为的行政处罚，除本条例已有规定的外，由国务院期货监督管理机构决定；涉及其他有关部门法定职权的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当会同其他有关部门处理；属于其他有关部门法定职权的，国务院期货监督管理机构应当移交其他有关部门处理。

**Article 80** Administrative penalties for illegal acts specified in these Regulations shall, unless otherwise provided in these Regulations, be decided by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council; if the matter involves the statutory functions and powers of other relevant departments, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall handle it jointly with such other departments; if the matter falls within the statutory functions and powers of other relevant departments, the futures regulatory authority of the State Council shall transfer it to such other departments for handling.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)商品期货合约，是指以农产品、工业品、能源和其他商品及其相关指数产品为标的物的期货合约。  
(二)金融期货合约，是指以有价证券、利率、汇率等金融产品及其相关指数产品为标的物的期货合约。  
(三)保证金，是指期货交易者按照规定交纳的资金或者提交的价值稳定、流动性强的标准仓单、国债等有价证券，用于结算和保证履约。  
(四)结算，是指根据期货交易所公布的结算价格对交易双方的交易结果进行的资金清算和划转。  
(五)交割，是指合约到期时，按照期货交易所的规则和程序，交易双方通过该合约所载标的物所有权的转移，或者按照规定结算价格进行现金差价结算，了结到期未平仓合约的过程。  
(六)平仓，是指期货交易者买入或者卖出与其所持合约的品种、数量和交割月份相同但交易方向相反的合约，了结期货交易的行为。  
(七)持仓量，是指期货交易者所持有的未平仓合约的数量。  
(八)持仓限额，是指期货交易所对期货交易者的持仓量规定的最高数额。  
(九)标准仓单，是指交割仓库开具并经期货交易所认定的标准化提货凭证。  
(十)涨跌停板，是指合约在1个交易日中的交易价格不得高于或者低于规定的涨跌幅度，超出该涨跌幅度的报价将被视为无效，不能成交。  
(十一)内幕信息，是指可能对期货交易价格产生重大影响的尚未公开的信息，包括：国务院期货监督管理机构以及其他相关部门制定的对期货交易价格可能发生重大影响的政策，期货交易所作出的可能对期货交易价格发生重大影响的决定，期货交易所会员、客户的资金和交易动向以及国务院期货监督管理机构认定的对期货交易价格有显著影响的其他重要信息。  
(十二)内幕信息的知情人员，是指由于其管理地位、监督地位或者职业地位，或者作为雇员、专业顾问履行职务，能够接触或者获得内幕信息的人员，包括：期货交易所的管理人员以及其他由于任职可获取内幕信息的从业人员，国务院期货监督管理机构和其他有关部门的工作人员以及国务院期货监督管理机构规定的其他人员。

**Article 81** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings specified below:  
(1) "Commodity futures contract" means a futures contract with agricultural products, industrial products, energy, and other commodities and their related index products as the underlying subject matter.  
(2) "Financial futures contract" means a futures contract with negotiable securities, interest rates, exchange rates, and other financial products and their related index products as the underlying subject matter.  
(3) "Margin" means the funds paid by a futures trader in accordance with the regulations or the standard warehouse receipts, treasury bonds, and other negotiable securities with stable value and strong liquidity submitted by a futures trader for clearing and performance guarantee.  
(4) "Clearing" means the fund liquidation and transfer of the trading results of the two parties to a transaction based on the clearing price published by the futures exchange.  
(5) "Delivery" means the process of closing out open contracts that expire, by the transfer of ownership of the underlying subject matter specified in the contract between the two parties to the transaction in accordance with the rules and procedures of the futures exchange, or by cash settlement of the difference at the prescribed clearing price.  
(6) "Close-out" means the act of a futures trader buying or selling a contract that is identical in variety, quantity, and delivery month to the contract held by the trader but opposite in trading direction, thereby closing out the futures trading position.  
(7) "Open interest" means the number of open contracts held by a futures trader.  
(8) "Position limit" means the maximum amount of open positions prescribed by the futures exchange for futures traders.  
(9) "Standard warehouse receipt" means a standardized delivery document issued by a delivery warehouse and recognized by the futures exchange.  
(10) "Price limit" means that the trading price of a contract in a single trading day shall not exceed or fall below the prescribed upward or downward range; quotations outside such range shall be deemed invalid and cannot be executed.  
(11) "Inside information" means unpublished information that may have a significant impact on futures trading prices, including: policies formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments that may have a significant impact on futures trading prices; decisions made by the futures exchange that may have a significant impact on futures trading prices; the capital and trading trends of members and clients of the futures exchange; and other important information recognized by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council as having a significant impact on futures trading prices.  
(12) "Persons in the know of inside information" means persons who, by virtue of their management, supervisory, or professional positions, or as employees or professional advisors performing duties, have access to or are able to obtain inside information, including: management personnel of the futures exchange and other practitioners who can obtain inside information because of their positions; staff members of the futures regulatory authority of the State Council and other relevant departments; and other persons specified by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国务院期货监督管理机构可以批准设立期货专门结算机构，专门履行期货交易所的结算以及相关职责，并承担相应法律责任。

**Article 82** The futures regulatory authority of the State Council may approve the establishment of a specialized futures clearing institution to specifically perform the clearing and related duties of the futures exchange and bear corresponding legal liability.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　境外机构在境内设立、收购或者参股期货经营机构，以及境外期货经营机构在境内设立分支机构(含代表处)的管理办法，由国务院期货监督管理机构会同国务院商务主管部门、外汇管理部门等有关部门制订，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 83** The administrative measures for overseas institutions to establish, acquire, or hold equity in futures business institutions within China, and for overseas futures business institutions to establish branches (including representative offices) within China, shall be formulated by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council together with the competent department of commerce of the State Council, the foreign exchange administration department, and other relevant departments, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　在期货交易所之外的国务院期货监督管理机构批准的交易场所进行的期货交易，依照本条例的有关规定执行。

**Article 84** Futures trading conducted at trading venues approved by the futures regulatory authority of the State Council other than futures exchanges shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　不属于期货交易的商品或者金融产品的其他交易活动，由国家有关部门监督管理，不适用本条例。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../刑事/检察院/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../刑事/检察院/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-5" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第5条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 85** Other trading activities of commodities or financial products that do not constitute futures trading shall be supervised and administered by the relevant state departments and shall not be subject to these Regulations.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　本条例自2007年4月15日起施行。1999年6月2日国务院发布的《期货交易管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 86** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 15, 2007. The Interim Regulations on the Administration of Futures Trading promulgated by the State Council on June 2, 1999 shall be simultaneously abolished.
