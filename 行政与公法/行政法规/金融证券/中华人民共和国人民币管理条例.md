# 中华人民共和国人民币管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19  
**生效日期**：2000-05-01

> (2000年2月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第280号发布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对人民币的管理，维护人民币的信誉，稳定金融秩序，根据《中华人民共和国中国人民银行法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Bank of China for the purpose of strengthening the administration of Renminbi, maintaining the credibility of Renminbi, and stabilizing financial order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称人民币，是指中国人民银行依法发行的货币，包括纸币和硬币。  
从事人民币的设计、印制、发行、流通和回收等活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Renminbi as mentioned in these Regulations refers to the currency issued by the People's Bank of China according to law, including banknotes and coins.  
Any entity or individual that engages in the design, printing, issuance, circulation, and recovery of Renminbi shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国的法定货币是人民币。以人民币支付中华人民共和国境内的一切公共的和私人的债务，任何单位和个人不得拒收。

**Article 3** Renminbi is the legal tender of the People's Republic of China. For payment of all public and private debts within the territory of the People's Republic of China in Renminbi, no entity or individual may refuse to accept it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民币的单位为元，人民币辅币单位为角、分。1元等于10角，1角等于10分。  
人民币依其面额支付。

**Article 4** The unit of Renminbi is yuan, and the subsidiary units of Renminbi are jiao and fen. One yuan equals 10 jiao, and one jiao equals 10 fen.  
Renminbi shall be paid according to its face value.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中国人民银行是国家管理人民币的主管机关，负责本条例的组织实施。

**Article 5** The People's Bank of China is the competent authority in charge of the administration of Renminbi and shall be responsible for the organization and implementation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人都应当爱护人民币。禁止损害人民币和妨碍人民币流通。

**Article 6** All entities and individuals shall protect Renminbi. It is prohibited to damage Renminbi or hinder the circulation of Renminbi.

### 第二章　设计和印制

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　新版人民币由中国人民银行组织设计，报国务院批准。

**Article 7** The design of new versions of Renminbi shall be organized by the People's Bank of China and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民币由中国人民银行指定的专门企业印制。

**Article 8** Renminbi shall be printed by specialized enterprises designated by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　印制人民币的企业应当按照中国人民银行制定的人民币质量标准和印制计划印制人民币。

**Article 9** An enterprise printing Renminbi shall print Renminbi in accordance with the quality standards and printing plans for Renminbi formulated by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　印制人民币的企业应当将合格的人民币产品全部解缴中国人民银行人民币发行库，将不合格的人民币产品按照中国人民银行的规定全部销毁。

**Article 10** An enterprise printing Renminbi shall deliver all qualified Renminbi products to the Renminbi issuance vault of the People's Bank of China, and shall destroy all unqualified Renminbi products in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　印制人民币的原版、原模使用完毕后，由中国人民银行封存。

**Article 11** The original plates and original molds used for printing Renminbi shall, after use, be sealed and stored by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　印制人民币的特殊材料、技术、工艺、专用设备等重要事项属于国家秘密。印制人民币的企业和有关人员应当保守国家秘密；未经中国人民银行批准，任何单位和个人不得对外提供。

**Article 12** The special materials, technologies, techniques, and dedicated equipment for printing Renminbi, and other important matters, constitute State secrets. The enterprises printing Renminbi and relevant personnel shall keep State secrets; without the approval of the People's Bank of China, no entity or individual may disclose them to the outside.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　除中国人民银行指定的印制人民币的企业外，任何单位和个人不得研制、仿制、引进、销售、购买和使用印制人民币所特有的防伪材料、防伪技术、防伪工艺和专用设备。有关管理办法由中国人民银行另行制定。

**Article 13** Except for the enterprises designated by the People's Bank of China for printing Renminbi, no entity or individual may develop, imitate, introduce, sell, purchase, or use the anti-counterfeiting materials, anti-counterfeiting technologies, anti-counterfeiting techniques, and dedicated equipment peculiar to printing Renminbi. The relevant administrative measures shall be formulated separately by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民币样币是检验人民币印制质量和鉴别人民币真伪的标准样本，由印制人民币的企业按照中国人民银行的规定印制。人民币样币上应当加印“样币”字样。

**Article 14** Renminbi sample coins are standard samples used for inspecting the printing quality of Renminbi and for authenticating Renminbi, and shall be printed by enterprises printing Renminbi in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China. The specimen character "样币" (sample coin) shall be overprinted on Renminbi sample coins.

### 第三章　发行和回收

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民币由中国人民银行统一发行。

**Article 15** Renminbi shall be uniformly issued by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中国人民银行发行新版人民币，应当报国务院批准。  
中国人民银行应当将新版人民币的发行时间、面额、图案、式样、规格、主色调、主要特征等予以公告。  
中国人民银行不得在新版人民币发行公告发布前将新版人民币支付给金融机构。

**Article 16** The People's Bank of China shall, when issuing a new version of Renminbi, submit a report to the State Council for approval.  
The People's Bank of China shall make a public announcement of the issue date, face value, design, pattern, specifications, main color tone, and main features of the new version of Renminbi.  
The People's Bank of China may not deliver the new version of Renminbi to financial institutions before the public announcement of the issuance of the new version of Renminbi.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　因防伪或者其他原因，需要改变人民币的印制材料、技术或者工艺的，由中国人民银行决定。  
中国人民银行应当将改版后的人民币的发行时间、面额、主要特征等予以公告。  
中国人民银行不得在改版人民币发行公告发布前将改版人民币支付给金融机构。

**Article 17** If it is necessary to change the printing materials, technologies, or techniques of Renminbi due to anti-counterfeiting or other reasons, the decision shall be made by the People's Bank of China.  
The People's Bank of China shall make a public announcement of the issue date, face value, and main features of the revised version of Renminbi.  
The People's Bank of China may not deliver the revised version of Renminbi to financial institutions before the public announcement of the issuance of the revised version of Renminbi.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　中国人民银行可以根据需要发行纪念币。  
纪念币是具有特定主题的限量发行的人民币，包括普通纪念币和贵金属纪念币。

**Article 18** The People's Bank of China may issue commemorative coins as needed.  
Commemorative coins are Renminbi issued in limited quantities with specific themes, including ordinary commemorative coins and precious metal commemorative coins.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　纪念币的主题、面额、图案、材质、式样、规格、发行数量、发行时间等由中国人民银行确定；但是，纪念币的主题涉及重大政治、历史题材的，应当报国务院批准。  
中国人民银行应当将纪念币的主题、面额、图案、材质、式样、规格、发行数量、发行时间等予以公告。  
中国人民银行不得在纪念币发行公告发布前将纪念币支付给金融机构。

**Article 19** The theme, face value, design, material, pattern, specifications, issue quantity, and issue date of commemorative coins shall be determined by the People's Bank of China; provided that where the theme of a commemorative coin involves major political or historical subject matter, it shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The People's Bank of China shall make a public announcement of the theme, face value, design, material, pattern, specifications, issue quantity, and issue date of the commemorative coins.  
The People's Bank of China may not deliver commemorative coins to financial institutions before the public announcement of the issuance of the commemorative coins.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中国人民银行设立人民币发行库，在其分支机构设立分支库，负责保管人民币发行基金。各级人民币发行库主任由同级中国人民银行行长担任。  
人民币发行基金是中国人民银行人民币发行库保存的未进入流通的人民币。  
人民币发行基金的调拨，应当按照中国人民银行的规定办理。任何单位和个人不得违反规定动用人民币发行基金，不得干扰、阻碍人民币发行基金的调拨。

**Article 20** The People's Bank of China shall establish a Renminbi issuance vault and set up branch vaults in its branches, which shall be responsible for the safekeeping of the Renminbi issuance fund. The director of each level of Renminbi issuance vault shall be the president of the People's Bank of China at the corresponding level.  
The Renminbi issuance fund is the Renminbi kept in the Renminbi issuance vault of the People's Bank of China that has not entered into circulation.  
The allocation of the Renminbi issuance fund shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China. No entity or individual may, in violation of the provisions, use the Renminbi issuance fund, or interfere with or obstruct the allocation of the Renminbi issuance fund.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　特定版别的人民币的停止流通，应当报国务院批准，并由中国人民银行公告。  
办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当按照中国人民银行的规定，收兑停止流通的人民币，并将其交存当地中国人民银行。  
中国人民银行不得将停止流通的人民币支付给金融机构，金融机构不得将停止流通的人民币对外支付。

**Article 21** The discontinuation of circulation of specific versions of Renminbi shall be reported to the State Council for approval and shall be announced by the People's Bank of China.  
A financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall, in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China, collect and convert Renminbi that have been discontinued from circulation, and shall deposit them with the local People's Bank of China.  
The People's Bank of China shall not pay Renminbi that have been discontinued from circulation to financial institutions, and financial institutions shall not make outward payments of Renminbi that have been discontinued from circulation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当按照中国人民银行的规定，无偿为公众兑换残缺、污损的人民币，挑剔残缺、污损的人民币，并将其交存当地中国人民银行。  
中国人民银行不得将残缺、污损的人民币支付给金融机构，金融机构不得将残缺、污损的人民币对外支付。

**Article 22** A financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall, in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China, exchange for the public, free of charge, mutilated or soiled Renminbi, sort out mutilated or soiled Renminbi, and deposit them with the local People's Bank of China.  
The People's Bank of China shall not pay mutilated or soiled Renminbi to financial institutions, and financial institutions shall not make outward payments of mutilated or soiled Renminbi.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　停止流通的人民币和残缺、污损的人民币，由中国人民银行负责回收、销毁。具体办法由中国人民银行制定。

**Article 23** Renminbi that have been discontinued from circulation and mutilated or soiled Renminbi shall be recovered and destroyed by the People's Bank of China. The specific measures shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China.

### 第四章　流通和保护

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当根据合理需要的原则，办理人民币券别调剂业务。

**Article 24** A financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall, in accordance with the principle of reasonable need, conduct the business of adjusting Renminbi denominations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　禁止非法买卖流通人民币。  
纪念币的买卖，应当遵守中国人民银行的有关规定。

**Article 25** It is prohibited to illegally buy or sell Renminbi in circulation.  
The purchase and sale of commemorative coins shall comply with the relevant provisions of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　禁止下列损害人民币的行为：  
(一)故意毁损人民币；  
(二)制作、仿制、买卖人民币图样；  
(三)未经中国人民银行批准，在宣传品、出版物或者其他商品上使用人民币图样；  
(四)中国人民银行规定的其他损害人民币的行为。  
前款人民币图样包括放大、缩小和同样大小的人民币图样。

**Article 26** The following acts that damage Renminbi are prohibited:  
(1) intentionally damaging Renminbi;  
(2) manufacturing, imitating, or trading in Renminbi designs;  
(3) using Renminbi designs on promotional materials, publications, or other goods without the approval of the People's Bank of China;  
(4) other acts damaging Renminbi as prescribed by the People's Bank of China.  
The Renminbi designs referred to in the preceding paragraph include enlarged, reduced, and same-size Renminbi designs.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民币样币禁止流通。  
人民币样币的管理办法，由中国人民银行制定。

**Article 27** Renminbi specimen notes are prohibited from circulation.  
The administrative measures for Renminbi specimen notes shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人不得印制、发售代币票券，以代替人民币在市场上流通。

**Article 28** No unit or individual may print or sell token coupons to replace Renminbi in circulation in the market.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　中国公民出入境、外国人入出境携带人民币实行限额管理制度，具体限额由中国人民银行规定。

**Article 29** A quota management system shall be implemented for the carrying of Renminbi by Chinese citizens entering or leaving the country and by foreigners entering or exiting the country. The specific quota shall be prescribed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　禁止伪造、变造人民币。禁止出售、购买伪造、变造的人民币。禁止走私、运输、持有、使用伪造、变造的人民币。

**Article 30** It is prohibited to counterfeit or alter Renminbi. It is prohibited to sell or purchase counterfeit or altered Renminbi. It is prohibited to smuggle, transport, possess, or use counterfeit or altered Renminbi.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　单位和个人持有伪造、变造的人民币的，应当及时上交中国人民银行、公安机关或者办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构；发现他人持有伪造、变造的人民币的，应当立即向公安机关报告。

**Article 31** Any unit or individual that possesses counterfeit or altered Renminbi shall promptly turn them over to the People's Bank of China, a public security organ, or a financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses; anyone who discovers another person possessing counterfeit or altered Renminbi shall immediately report to a public security organ.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　中国人民银行、公安机关发现伪造、变造的人民币，应当予以没收，加盖“假币”字样的戳记，并登记造册；持有人对公安机关没收的人民币的真伪有异议的，可以向中国人民银行申请鉴定。  
公安机关应当将没收的伪造、变造的人民币解缴当地中国人民银行。

**Article 32** When the People's Bank of China or a public security organ discovers counterfeit or altered Renminbi, it shall confiscate them, stamp them with a mark bearing the words "counterfeit currency", and register them in a ledger; if the holder disputes the authenticity of the Renminbi confiscated by the public security organ, he may apply to the People's Bank of China for expert evaluation.  
The public security organ shall deliver the confiscated counterfeit or altered Renminbi to the local People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构发现伪造、变造的人民币，数量较多、有新版的伪造人民币或者有其他制造贩卖伪造、变造的人民币线索的，应当立即报告公安机关；数量较少的，由该金融机构两名以上工作人员当面予以收缴，加盖“假币”字样的戳记，登记造册，向持有人出具中国人民银行统一印制的收缴凭证，并告知持有人可以向中国人民银行或者向中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构申请鉴定。对伪造、变造的人民币收缴及鉴定的具体办法，由中国人民银行制定。  
办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当将收缴的伪造、变造的人民币解缴当地中国人民银行。

**Article 33** When a financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses discovers counterfeit or altered Renminbi, if the quantity is relatively large, there are new versions of counterfeit Renminbi, or there are other leads concerning the manufacture or sale of counterfeit or altered Renminbi, it shall immediately report to a public security organ; if the quantity is relatively small, the financial institution shall, in the presence of the holder, have the Renminbi confiscated by two or more of its staff members, stamp them with a mark bearing the words "counterfeit currency", register them in a ledger, issue a confiscation certificate uniformly printed by the People's Bank of China to the holder, and inform the holder that he may apply to the People's Bank of China or to the business institution of a wholly state-owned commercial bank authorized by the People's Bank of China for expert evaluation. The specific measures for the confiscation and expert evaluation of counterfeit or altered Renminbi shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China.  
The financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall deliver the confiscated counterfeit or altered Renminbi to the local People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　中国人民银行和中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构应当无偿提供鉴定人民币真伪的服务。  
对盖有“假币”字样戳记的人民币，经鉴定为真币的，由中国人民银行或者中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构按照面额予以兑换；经鉴定为假币的，由中国人民银行或者中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构予以没收。  
中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构应当将没收的伪造、变造的人民币解缴当地中国人民银行。

**Article 34** The People's Bank of China and the business institutions of wholly state-owned commercial banks authorized by the People's Bank of China shall provide, free of charge, the service of authenticating Renminbi.  
For Renminbi stamped with a mark bearing the words "counterfeit currency", if they are determined to be genuine after expert evaluation, the People's Bank of China or the business institution of a wholly state-owned commercial bank authorized by the People's Bank of China shall exchange them at face value; if they are determined to be counterfeit after expert evaluation, the People's Bank of China or the business institution of a wholly state-owned commercial bank authorized by the People's Bank of China shall confiscate them.  
The business institution of a wholly state-owned commercial bank authorized by the People's Bank of China shall deliver the confiscated counterfeit or altered Renminbi to the local People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当采取有效措施，防止以伪造、变造的人民币对外支付。  
办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构应当在营业场所无偿提供鉴别人民币真伪的服务。

**Article 35** A financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall take effective measures to prevent making outward payments with counterfeit or altered Renminbi.  
A financial institution handling Renminbi deposit and withdrawal businesses shall provide, free of charge, the service of identifying the authenticity of Renminbi at its business premises.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　伪造、变造的人民币由中国人民银行统一销毁。

**Article 36** Counterfeit or altered Renminbi shall be uniformly destroyed by the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民币反假鉴别仪应当按照国家规定标准生产。  
人民币反假鉴别仪国家标准，由中国人民银行会同有关部门制定，并协助组织实施。

**Article 37** Renminbi anti-counterfeit authentication instruments shall be manufactured in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State.  
The national standards for Renminbi anti-counterfeit authentication instruments shall be formulated by the People's Bank of China in conjunction with relevant departments, and it shall assist in organizing the implementation thereof.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　人民币有下列情形之一的，不得流通：  
(一)不能兑换的残缺、污损的人民币；  
(二)停止流通的人民币。

**Article 38** Renminbi that fall under any of the following circumstances shall not be put into circulation:  
(1) mutilated or soiled Renminbi that cannot be exchanged;  
(2) Renminbi that have been discontinued from circulation.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　印制人民币的企业和有关人员有下列情形之一的，由中国人民银行给予警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予纪律处分：  
(一)未按照中国人民银行制定的人民币质量标准和印制计划印制人民币的；  
(二)未将合格的人民币产品全部解缴中国人民银行人民币发行库的；  
(三)未按照中国人民银行的规定将不合格的人民币产品全部销毁的；  
(四)未经中国人民银行批准，擅自对外提供印制人民币的特殊材料、技术、工艺或者专用设备等国家秘密的。

**Article 39** Where an enterprise printing Renminbi or any relevant person falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Bank of China shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall be imposed; and disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) failing to print Renminbi in accordance with the quality standards for Renminbi and the printing plan formulated by the People's Bank of China;  
(2) failing to deliver all qualified Renminbi products to the Renminbi issue vault of the People's Bank of China;  
(3) failing to destroy all unqualified Renminbi products in accordance with the provisions of the People's Bank of China;  
(4) without the approval of the People's Bank of China, providing to outsiders the special materials, technologies, processes, or special equipment used for printing Renminbi, which constitute State secrets.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定的，由工商行政管理机关和其他有关行政执法机关给予警告，没收违法所得和非法财物，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 13 of these Regulations shall be given a warning by the administrative authority for industry and commerce and other relevant administrative law enforcement authorities, and his illegal gains and illegal property shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)第二款、第三款和第二十二条规定的，由中国人民银行给予警告，并处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 41** . Financial institutions that handle RMB deposit and withdrawal business, if violating the provisions of Article 21, paragraphs 2 and 3, and Article 22 of these Regulations, shall be given a warning by the People's Bank of China and shall also be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　故意毁损人民币的，由公安机关给予警告，并处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** . Whoever intentionally defaces RMB shall be given a warning by the public security organ and shall also be fined not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条第一款第二项和第四项规定的，由工商行政管理机关和其他有关行政执法机关给予警告，没收违法所得和非法财物，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处1000元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
工商行政管理机关和其他有关行政执法机关应当销毁非法使用的人民币图样。

**Article 43** . Whoever violates the provisions of Article 25, Article 26, paragraph 1, items 2 and 4 of these Regulations shall be given a warning by the administrative authority for industry and commerce and other relevant administrative law enforcement authorities, and his illegal gains and illegal property shall be confiscated, and he shall also be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the amount of his illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, he shall be fined not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
The administrative authority for industry and commerce and other relevant administrative law enforcement authorities shall destroy the illegally used designs of RMB.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　办理人民币存取款业务的金融机构、中国人民银行授权的国有独资商业银行的业务机构违反[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)、第三十四条和第三十五条规定的，由中国人民银行给予警告，并处1000元以上5万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 44** . Where financial institutions that handle RMB deposit and withdrawal business, and the business offices of wholly State-owned commercial banks authorized by the People's Bank of China, violate the provisions of Article 33, Article 34 and Article 35 of these Regulations, the People's Bank of China shall give them a warning and shall also impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中国人民银行、公安机关、工商行政管理机关及其工作人员违反本条例有关规定的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 45** . Where the People's Bank of China, the public security organs, the administrative authorities for industry and commerce and their staff members violate the relevant provisions of these Regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)第三款、第二十六条第一款第三项、第二十八条和第三十条规定的，依照《中华人民共和国中国人民银行法》的有关规定予以处罚；其中，违反[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** . Whoever violates the provisions of Article 20, paragraph 3, Article 26, paragraph 1, item 3, Article 28 and Article 30 of these Regulations shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Bank of China; where the violation of Article 30 of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自2000年5月1日起施行。

**Article 47** . These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2000.
