# 直销管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2005-12-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为规范直销行为，加强对直销活动的监管，防止欺诈，保护消费者的合法权益和社会公共利益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating direct selling activities, strengthening the supervision over direct selling activities, preventing fraud, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consumers and the public interest.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事直销活动，应当遵守本条例。  
直销产品的范围由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院工商行政管理部门根据直销业的发展状况和消费者的需求确定、公布。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in direct selling activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall abide by these Regulations.  
The scope of direct selling products shall be determined and announced by the competent commerce department of the State Council jointly with the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council based on the development status of the direct selling industry and the needs of consumers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称直销，是指直销企业招募直销员，由直销员在固定营业场所之外直接向最终消费者(以下简称消费者)推销产品的经销方式。  
本条例所称直销企业，是指依照本条例规定经批准采取直销方式销售产品的企业。  
本条例所称直销员，是指在固定营业场所之外将产品直接推销给消费者的人员。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'direct selling' means a distribution method whereby a direct selling enterprise recruits direct sellers, and the direct sellers promote products directly to end consumers (hereinafter referred to as consumers) outside fixed business premises.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'direct selling enterprise' means an enterprise that has been approved to sell products by means of direct selling in accordance with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'direct seller' means a person who directly promotes products to consumers outside fixed business premises.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在中华人民共和国境内设立的企业(以下简称企业)，可以依照本条例规定申请成为以直销方式销售本企业生产的产品以及其母公司、控股公司生产产品的直销企业。  
直销企业可以依法取得贸易权和分销权。

**Article 4** An enterprise established within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as an enterprise) may, in accordance with these Regulations, apply to become a direct selling enterprise that sells products manufactured by itself as well as products manufactured by its parent company or holding company by means of direct selling.  
A direct selling enterprise may obtain trading rights and distribution rights according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　直销企业及其直销员从事直销活动，不得有欺骗、误导等宣传和推销行为。

**Article 5** A direct selling enterprise and its direct sellers, when engaging in direct selling activities, shall not engage in any propaganda or promotion activities such as deception or misleading.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院商务主管部门和工商行政管理部门依照其职责分工和本条例规定，负责对直销企业和直销员及其直销活动实施监督管理。

**Article 6** The competent commerce department and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties and the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the supervision and administration of direct selling enterprises, direct sellers, and their direct selling activities.

### 第二章　直销企业及其分支机构的设立和变更

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请成为直销企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)投资者具有良好的商业信誉，在提出申请前连续5年没有重大违法经营记录；外国投资者还应当有3年以上在中国境外从事直销活动的经验；  
(二)实缴注册资本不低于人民币8000万元；  
(三)依照本条例规定在指定银行足额缴纳了保证金；  
(四)依照规定建立了信息报备和披露制度。

**Article 7** To apply to become a direct selling enterprise, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) The investor has a good commercial reputation and has no record of material illegal business operations in the five consecutive years preceding the submission of the application; a foreign investor shall also have experience in engaging in direct selling activities outside China for more than three years;  
(2) The paid-in registered capital is not less than RMB 80 million;  
(3) The full amount of the deposit has been paid into a designated bank in accordance with these Regulations;  
(4) An information reporting and disclosure system has been established in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请成为直销企业应当填写申请表，并提交下列申请文件、资料：  
(一)符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定条件的证明材料；  
(二)企业章程，属于中外合资、合作企业的，还应当提供合资或者合作企业合同；  
(三)市场计划报告书，包括依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定拟定的经当地县级以上人民政府认可的从事直销活动地区的服务网点方案；  
(四)符合国家标准的产品说明；  
(五)拟与直销员签订的推销合同样本；  
(六)会计师事务所出具的验资报告；  
(七)企业与指定银行达成的同意依照本条例规定使用保证金的协议。

**Article 8** An applicant for becoming a direct selling enterprise shall fill in an application form and submit the following application documents and materials:  
(1) Evidentiary materials demonstrating that the conditions set forth in Article 7 of these Regulations are met;  
(2) The enterprise's articles of association; where it is a Sino-foreign equity joint venture or contractual joint venture, the joint venture contract or cooperative enterprise contract shall also be provided;  
(3) A market plan report, including a service network plan for the areas where direct selling activities are to be conducted, drafted in accordance with Article 10 of these Regulations and approved by the local people's government at or above the county level;  
(4) Product descriptions that comply with national standards;  
(5) A sample of the promotion contract to be signed with direct sellers;  
(6) A capital verification report issued by an accounting firm;  
(7) An agreement between the enterprise and the designated bank consenting to the use of the deposit in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请人应当通过所在地省、自治区、直辖市商务主管部门向国务院商务主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市商务主管部门应当自收到申请文件、资料之日起7日内，将申请文件、资料报送国务院商务主管部门。国务院商务主管部门应当自收到全部申请文件、资料之日起90日内，经征求国务院工商行政管理部门的意见，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，由国务院商务主管部门颁发直销经营许可证。  
申请人持国务院商务主管部门颁发的直销经营许可证，依法向工商行政管理部门申请变更登记。  
国务院商务主管部门审查颁发直销经营许可证，应当考虑国家安全、社会公共利益和直销业发展状况等因素。

**Article 9** An applicant shall submit an application to the competent commerce department of the State Council through the competent commerce department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The competent commerce department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within seven days from the date of receiving the application documents and materials, forward the application documents and materials to the competent commerce department of the State Council. The competent commerce department of the State Council shall, within 90 days from the date of receiving all application documents and materials, after seeking the opinions of the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approved, the competent commerce department of the State Council shall issue a direct selling business license.  
The applicant shall, with the direct selling business license issued by the competent commerce department of the State Council, apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce for registration of modification according to law.  
When examining and issuing the direct selling business license, the competent commerce department of the State Council shall take into account factors such as national security, social public interest, and the development status of the direct selling industry.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　直销企业从事直销活动，必须在拟从事直销活动的省、自治区、直辖市设立负责该行政区域内直销业务的分支机构(以下简称分支机构)。  
直销企业在其从事直销活动的地区应当建立便于并满足消费者、直销员了解产品价格、退换货及企业依法提供其他服务的服务网点。服务网点的设立应当符合当地县级以上人民政府的要求。  
直销企业申请设立分支机构，应当提供符合前款规定条件的证明文件和资料，并应当依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)第一款规定的程序提出申请。获得批准后，依法向工商行政管理部门办理登记。

**Article 10** A direct selling enterprise that engages in direct selling activities must establish a branch (hereinafter referred to as a branch) responsible for the direct selling business within the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it intends to conduct direct selling activities.  
A direct selling enterprise shall, in the areas where it conducts direct selling activities, establish service outlets that facilitate and satisfy consumers' and direct sellers' need to understand product prices, returns and exchanges, and other services provided by the enterprise according to law. The establishment of service outlets shall comply with the requirements of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
When a direct selling enterprise applies to establish a branch, it shall provide evidentiary documents and materials that meet the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, and shall submit an application in accordance with the procedures set forth in Article 9, paragraph 1 of these Regulations. After obtaining approval, it shall go through registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　直销企业有关[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一项、第二项、第三项、第五项、第六项、第七项所列内容发生重大变更的，应当依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)第一款规定的程序报国务院商务主管部门批准。

**Article 11** If a direct selling enterprise undergoes a material change concerning the contents listed in items (1), (2), (3), (5), (6), and (7) of Article 8 of these Regulations, it shall report to the competent commerce department of the State Council for approval in accordance with the procedures set forth in Article 9, paragraph 1 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院商务主管部门应当将直销企业及其分支机构的名单在政府网站上公布，并及时进行更新。

**Article 12** The competent commerce department of the State Council shall publish the list of direct selling enterprises and their branches on the government website and shall update it in a timely manner.

### 第三章　直销员的招募和培训

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　直销企业及其分支机构可以招募直销员。直销企业及其分支机构以外的任何单位和个人不得招募直销员。  
直销员的合法推销活动不以无照经营查处。

**Article 13** A direct selling enterprise and its branches may recruit direct sellers. No entity or individual other than a direct selling enterprise and its branches may recruit direct sellers.  
The lawful promotion activities of a direct seller shall not be investigated and punished as unlicensed business operations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　直销企业及其分支机构不得发布宣传直销员销售报酬的广告，不得以缴纳费用或者购买商品作为成为直销员的条件。

**Article 14** A direct selling enterprise and its branches shall not publish advertisements promoting the sales remuneration of direct sellers, and shall not make the payment of fees or the purchase of goods a condition for becoming a direct seller.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　直销企业及其分支机构不得招募下列人员为直销员：  
(一)未满18周岁的人员；  
(二)无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的人员；  
(三)全日制在校学生；  
(四)教师、医务人员、公务员和现役军人；  
(五)直销企业的正式员工；  
(六)境外人员；  
(七)法律、行政法规规定不得从事兼职的人员。

**Article 15** A direct selling enterprise and its branches shall not recruit the following persons as direct sellers:  
(1) Persons under the age of 18;  
(2) Persons who have no civil capacity or have limited civil capacity;  
(3) Full-time students;  
(4) Teachers, medical personnel, public servants, and active military personnel;  
(5) Regular employees of the direct selling enterprise;  
(6) Persons from outside the territory of China;  
(7) Persons who are not allowed to engage in part-time jobs as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　直销企业及其分支机构招募直销员应当与其签订推销合同，并保证直销员只在其一个分支机构所在的省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内已设立服务网点的地区开展直销活动。未与直销企业或者其分支机构签订推销合同的人员，不得以任何方式从事直销活动。

**Article 16** When a direct selling enterprise and its branches recruit direct sellers, they shall sign a promotion contract with them and ensure that the direct sellers only conduct direct selling activities in the areas within the administrative region of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where one of its branches is located and where service outlets have been established. Persons who have not signed a promotion contract with a direct selling enterprise or its branches shall not engage in direct selling activities in any manner.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　直销员自签订推销合同之日起60日内可以随时解除推销合同；60日后，直销员解除推销合同应当提前15日通知直销企业。

**Article 17** A direct seller may rescind the promotion contract at any time within 60 days from the date of signing the promotion contract; after 60 days, the direct seller shall notify the direct selling enterprise 15 days in advance before rescinding the promotion contract.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　直销企业应当对拟招募的直销员进行业务培训和考试，考试合格后由直销企业颁发直销员证。未取得直销员证，任何人不得从事直销活动。  
直销企业进行直销员业务培训和考试，不得收取任何费用。  
直销企业以外的单位和个人，不得以任何名义组织直销员业务培训。

**Article 18** A direct selling enterprise shall provide business training and conduct examinations for the direct sellers it intends to recruit, and shall issue a direct seller certificate to those who pass the examination. No person shall engage in direct selling activities without obtaining a direct seller certificate.  
A direct selling enterprise shall not charge any fees for providing business training and conducting examinations for direct sellers.  
No entity or individual other than a direct selling enterprise shall organize business training for direct sellers in any name.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对直销员进行业务培训的授课人员应当是直销企业的正式员工，并符合下列条件：  
(一)在本企业工作1年以上；  
(二)具有高等教育本科以上学历和相关的法律、市场营销专业知识；  
(三)无因故意犯罪受刑事处罚的记录；  
(四)无重大违法经营记录。  
直销企业应当向符合前款规定的授课人员颁发直销培训员证，并将取得直销培训员证的人员名单报国务院商务主管部门备案。国务院商务主管部门应当将取得直销培训员证的人员名单，在政府网站上公布。  
境外人员不得从事直销员业务培训。

**Article 19** The instructors who provide business training to direct sellers shall be regular employees of the direct selling enterprise and shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having worked in the enterprise for more than one year;  
(2) Having a bachelor's degree or above in higher education and relevant expertise in law and marketing;  
(3) Having no record of criminal punishment for intentional crimes;  
(4) Having no record of material illegal business operations.  
A direct selling enterprise shall issue a direct selling trainer certificate to instructors who meet the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, and shall report the list of persons who have obtained the direct selling trainer certificate to the competent commerce department of the State Council for record-filing. The competent commerce department of the State Council shall publish the list of persons who have obtained the direct selling trainer certificate on the government website.  
Persons from outside the territory of China shall not provide business training to direct sellers.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　直销企业颁发的直销员证、直销培训员证应当依照国务院商务主管部门规定的式样印制。

**Article 20** The direct seller certificate and direct selling trainer certificate issued by a direct selling enterprise shall be printed in accordance with the format prescribed by the competent commerce department of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　直销企业应当对直销员业务培训的合法性、培训秩序和培训场所的安全负责。  
直销企业及其直销培训员应当对直销员业务培训授课内容的合法性负责。  
直销员业务培训的具体管理办法由国务院商务主管部门、国务院工商行政管理部门会同有关部门另行制定。

**Article 21** A direct selling enterprise shall be responsible for the legality, training order and safety of the training premises of business training for direct sellers.  
A direct selling enterprise and its direct selling trainers shall be responsible for the legality of the teaching content of business training for direct sellers.  
The specific administrative measures for business training for direct sellers shall be separately formulated by the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.

### 第四章　直销活动

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　直销员向消费者推销产品，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)出示直销员证和推销合同；  
(二)未经消费者同意，不得进入消费者住所强行推销产品，消费者要求其停止推销活动的，应当立即停止，并离开消费者住所；  
(三)成交前，向消费者详细介绍本企业的退货制度；  
(四)成交后，向消费者提供发票和由直销企业出具的含有退货制度、直销企业当地服务网点地址和电话号码等内容的售货凭证。

**Article 22** When a direct seller promotes products to consumers, he shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) present his direct seller certificate and promotion contract;  
(2) without the consent of the consumer, he may not enter the consumer's residence to forcibly promote products; if the consumer requests him to stop the promotion activities, he shall immediately stop and leave the consumer's residence;  
(3) before concluding a transaction, he shall introduce in detail to the consumer the return system of his enterprise;  
(4) after concluding a transaction, he shall provide the consumer with an invoice and a sales voucher issued by the direct selling enterprise, which contains the return system, the address and telephone number of the local service outlet of the direct selling enterprise, etc.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　直销企业应当在直销产品上标明产品价格，该价格与服务网点展示的产品价格应当一致。直销员必须按照标明的价格向消费者推销产品。

**Article 23** A direct selling enterprise shall indicate the product price on its direct selling products, and such price shall be consistent with the product price displayed at the service outlet. A direct seller must promote products to consumers at the indicated price.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　直销企业至少应当按月支付直销员报酬。直销企业支付给直销员的报酬只能按照直销员本人直接向消费者销售产品的收入计算，报酬总额(包括佣金、奖金、各种形式的奖励以及其他经济利益等)不得超过直销员本人直接向消费者销售产品收入的30%。

**Article 24** A direct selling enterprise shall pay remuneration to direct sellers at least once a month. The remuneration paid by a direct selling enterprise to a direct seller may only be calculated based on the income derived by the direct seller himself from directly selling products to consumers. The total amount of remuneration (including commissions, bonuses, various forms of rewards and other economic benefits, etc.) shall not exceed 30% of the income derived by the direct seller himself from directly selling products to consumers.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　直销企业应当建立并实行完善的换货和退货制度。  
消费者自购买直销产品之日起30日内，产品未开封的，可以凭直销企业开具的发票或者售货凭证向直销企业及其分支机构、所在地的服务网点或者推销产品的直销员办理换货和退货；直销企业及其分支机构、所在地的服务网点和直销员应当自消费者提出换货或者退货要求之日起7日内，按照发票或者售货凭证标明的价款办理换货和退货。  
直销员自购买直销产品之日起30日内，产品未开封的，可以凭直销企业开具的发票或者售货凭证向直销企业及其分支机构或者所在地的服务网点办理换货和退货；直销企业及其分支机构和所在地的服务网点应当自直销员提出换货或者退货要求之日起7日内，按照发票或者售货凭证标明的价款办理换货和退货。  
不属于前两款规定情形，消费者、直销员要求换货和退货的，直销企业及其分支机构、所在地的服务网点和直销员应当依照有关法律法规的规定或者合同的约定，办理换货和退货。

**Article 25** A direct selling enterprise shall establish and implement a complete system for exchange and return of goods.  
Within 30 days from the date of purchase of a direct selling product, if the product has not been opened, a consumer may, by presenting the invoice or sales voucher issued by the direct selling enterprise, apply for exchange or return of goods to the direct selling enterprise and its branches, the local service outlet, or the direct seller who promoted the product; the direct selling enterprise and its branches, the local service outlet and the direct seller shall, within 7 days from the date on which the consumer makes the request for exchange or return of goods, handle the exchange or return of goods according to the price indicated on the invoice or sales voucher.  
Within 30 days from the date of purchase of a direct selling product, if the product has not been opened, a direct seller may, by presenting the invoice or sales voucher issued by the direct selling enterprise, apply for exchange or return of goods to the direct selling enterprise and its branches or the local service outlet; the direct selling enterprise and its branches and the local service outlet shall, within 7 days from the date on which the direct seller makes the request for exchange or return of goods, handle the exchange or return of goods according to the price indicated on the invoice or sales voucher.  
If the circumstances do not fall under the preceding two paragraphs and a consumer or direct seller requests exchange or return of goods, the direct selling enterprise and its branches, the local service outlet and the direct seller shall handle the exchange or return of goods in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations or the contract.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　直销企业与直销员、直销企业及其直销员与消费者因换货或者退货发生纠纷的，由前者承担举证责任。

**Article 26** If a dispute arises between a direct selling enterprise and a direct seller, or between a direct selling enterprise and its direct seller and a consumer, concerning exchange or return of goods, the former shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　直销企业对其直销员的直销行为承担连带责任，能够证明直销员的直销行为与本企业无关的除外。

**Article 27** A direct selling enterprise shall bear joint and several liability for the direct selling activities of its direct sellers, unless it can prove that the direct selling activities of the direct seller are unrelated to the enterprise.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　直销企业应当依照国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门的规定，建立并实行完备的信息报备和披露制度。  
直销企业信息报备和披露的内容、方式及相关要求，由国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门另行规定。

**Article 28** A direct selling enterprise shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, establish and implement a complete system for information reporting and disclosure.  
The content, methods and relevant requirements for information reporting and disclosure by direct selling enterprises shall be separately prescribed by the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council.

### 第五章　保证金

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　直销企业应当在国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门共同指定的银行开设专门账户，存入保证金。  
保证金的数额在直销企业设立时为人民币2000万元；直销企业运营后，保证金应当按月进行调整，其数额应当保持在直销企业上一个月直销产品销售收入15%的水平，但最高不超过人民币1亿元，最低不少于人民币2000万元。保证金的利息属于直销企业。

**Article 29** A direct selling enterprise shall open a special account and deposit a guarantee fund in a bank jointly designated by the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council.  
The amount of the guarantee fund shall be RMB 20 million at the time of establishment of the direct selling enterprise; after the direct selling enterprise commences operations, the guarantee fund shall be adjusted on a monthly basis, and its amount shall be maintained at the level of 15% of the direct selling enterprise's direct selling product sales revenue of the previous month, but shall not exceed RMB 100 million and shall not be less than RMB 20 million. The interest on the guarantee fund shall belong to the direct selling enterprise.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　出现下列情形之一，国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门共同决定，可以使用保证金：  
(一)无正当理由，直销企业不向直销员支付报酬，或者不向直销员、消费者支付退货款的；  
(二)直销企业发生停业、合并、解散、转让、破产等情况，无力向直销员支付报酬或者无力向直销员和消费者支付退货款的；  
(三)因直销产品问题给消费者造成损失，依法应当进行赔偿，直销企业无正当理由拒绝赔偿或者无力赔偿的。

**Article 30** If any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council may jointly decide to use the guarantee fund:  
(1) without justifiable reasons, the direct selling enterprise does not pay remuneration to direct sellers, or does not pay the return price to direct sellers or consumers;  
(2) the direct selling enterprise encounters circumstances such as business suspension, merger, dissolution, transfer, or bankruptcy, and is unable to pay remuneration to direct sellers or is unable to pay the return price to direct sellers and consumers;  
(3) the direct selling product causes losses to consumers, and compensation shall be made according to law, but the direct selling enterprise refuses to compensate without justifiable reasons or is unable to compensate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　保证金依照[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定使用后，直销企业应当在1个月内将保证金的数额补足到[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定的水平。

**Article 31** After the guarantee fund is used in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of these Regulations, the direct selling enterprise shall, within one month, replenish the amount of the guarantee fund to the level prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 29 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　直销企业不得以保证金对外担保或者违反本条例规定用于清偿债务。

**Article 32** A direct selling enterprise shall not use the guarantee fund to provide security to external parties or to settle debts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　直销企业不再从事直销活动的，凭国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门出具的凭证，可以向银行取回保证金。

**Article 33** If a direct selling enterprise no longer engages in direct selling activities, it may, by presenting the certificate issued by the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council, retrieve the guarantee fund from the bank.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院商务主管部门和国务院工商行政管理部门共同负责保证金的日常监管工作。  
保证金存缴、使用的具体管理办法由国务院商务主管部门、国务院工商行政管理部门会同有关部门另行制定。

**Article 34** The competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council shall jointly be responsible for the daily supervision and management of the guarantee fund.  
The specific administrative measures for the deposit, payment and use of the guarantee fund shall be separately formulated by the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　工商行政管理部门负责对直销企业和直销员及其直销活动实施日常的监督管理。工商行政管理部门可以采取下列措施进行现场检查：  
(一)进入相关企业进行检查；  
(二)要求相关企业提供有关文件、资料和证明材料；  
(三)询问当事人、利害关系人和其他有关人员，并要求其提供有关材料；  
(四)查阅、复制、查封、扣押相关企业与直销活动有关的材料和非法财物；  
(五)检查有关人员的直销培训员证、直销员证等证件。  
工商行政管理部门依照前款规定进行现场检查时，检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示合法证件；实施查封、扣押的，必须经县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人批准。

**Article 35** The administrative department for industry and commerce shall be responsible for the daily supervision and administration of direct selling enterprises and direct sellers and their direct selling activities. The administrative department for industry and commerce may take the following measures to conduct on-site inspections:  
(1) enter relevant enterprises for inspection;  
(2) require relevant enterprises to provide relevant documents, materials and evidentiary materials;  
(3) interrogate the parties, interested parties and other relevant persons, and require them to provide relevant materials;  
(4) inspect, copy, seal up and detain the materials and illegal property of relevant enterprises related to direct selling activities;  
(5) inspect the direct selling trainer certificates, direct seller certificates and other certificates of relevant persons.  
When the administrative department for industry and commerce conducts an on-site inspection in accordance with the preceding paragraph, there shall be no fewer than two inspectors, and they shall present their legal credentials; when sealing up or detaining, the approval of the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall be obtained.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　工商行政管理部门实施日常监督管理，发现有关企业有涉嫌违反本条例行为的，经县级以上工商行政管理部门主要负责人批准，可以责令其暂时停止有关的经营活动。

**Article 36** If, in the course of daily supervision and administration, the administrative department for industry and commerce discovers that a relevant enterprise is suspected of violating the provisions of these Regulations, it may, upon approval by the principal responsible person of the administrative department for industry and commerce at or above the county level, order the enterprise to temporarily suspend the relevant business activities.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　工商行政管理部门应当设立并公布举报电话，接受对违反本条例行为的举报和投诉，并及时进行调查处理。  
工商行政管理部门应当为举报人保密；对举报有功人员，应当依照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 37** The administrative department for industry and commerce shall establish and publicize a report telephone number to accept reports and complaints regarding violations of these Regulations, and shall promptly investigate and handle such reports.  
The administrative department for industry and commerce shall keep the identity of the reporter confidential; and shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, give rewards to persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对直销企业和直销员及其直销活动实施监督管理的有关部门及其工作人员，对不符合本条例规定条件的申请予以许可或者不依照本条例规定履行监督管理职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。对不符合本条例规定条件的申请予以的许可，由作出许可决定的有关部门撤销。

**Article 38** If the relevant departments and their staff members that implement supervision and administration over direct selling enterprises and direct sellers and their direct selling activities grant a permit to an application that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, or fail to perform their supervision and administration duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law. The permit granted to an application that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations shall be revoked by the relevant department that made the decision to grant the permit.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反[本条例第九条](#art-9)和第十条规定，未经批准从事直销活动的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收直销产品和违法销售收入，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款，并依法予以取缔；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** If a party engages in direct selling activities without approval in violation of the provisions of Articles 9 and 10 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the direct selling products and illegal sales revenue, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 300,000; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 300,000 but not more than RMB 500,000, and shall ban it in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　申请人通过欺骗、贿赂等手段取得[本条例第九条](#art-9)和第十条设定的许可的，由工商行政管理部门没收直销产品和违法销售收入，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款，由国务院商务主管部门撤销其相应的许可，申请人不得再提出申请；情节严重的，处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款，并依法予以取缔；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** If an applicant obtains a permit established under Articles 9 and 10 of these Regulations by means of fraud, bribery, or other such means, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall confiscate the direct selling products and illegal sales revenue, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 300,000; the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the corresponding permit, and the applicant shall not submit another application; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 300,000 but not more than RMB 500,000, and shall ban it in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　直销企业违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，处3万元以上30万元以下的罚款；对不再符合直销经营许可条件的，由国务院商务主管部门吊销其直销经营许可证。

**Article 41** Where a direct selling enterprise violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; and if it no longer meets the conditions for a direct selling business license, the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke its direct selling business license.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　直销企业违反规定，超出直销产品范围从事直销经营活动的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收直销产品和违法销售收入，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款，由工商行政管理部门吊销有违法经营行为的直销企业分支机构的营业执照直至由国务院商务主管部门吊销直销企业的直销经营许可证。

**Article 42** Where a direct selling enterprise violates the provisions and engages in direct selling activities beyond the scope of direct selling products, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the direct selling products and illegal sales revenue, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license of the branch of the direct selling enterprise that has engaged in illegal business operations, or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the direct selling business license of the direct selling enterprise.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　直销企业及其直销员违反本条例规定，有欺骗、误导等宣传和推销行为的，对直销企业，由工商行政管理部门处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，由工商行政管理部门吊销有违法经营行为的直销企业分支机构的营业执照直至由国务院商务主管部门吊销直销企业的直销经营许可证。对直销员，由工商行政管理部门处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令直销企业撤销其直销员资格。

**Article 43** Where a direct selling enterprise or its direct seller violates the provisions of these Regulations by engaging in deceptive or misleading publicity or sales activities, with respect to the direct selling enterprise, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license of the branch of the direct selling enterprise that has engaged in illegal business operations, or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the direct selling business license of the direct selling enterprise. With respect to the direct seller, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the direct selling enterprise to revoke his qualification as a direct seller.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　直销企业及其分支机构违反本条例规定招募直销员的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，由工商行政管理部门吊销有违法经营行为的直销企业分支机构的营业执照直至由国务院商务主管部门吊销直销企业的直销经营许可证。

**Article 44** Where a direct selling enterprise or its branch violates the provisions of these Regulations in recruiting direct sellers, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license of the branch of the direct selling enterprise that has engaged in illegal business operations, or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the direct selling business license of the direct selling enterprise.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，未取得直销员证从事直销活动的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收直销产品和违法销售收入，可以处2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engages in direct selling activities without obtaining a direct seller certificate, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order him to make corrections, confiscate the direct selling products and illegal sales revenue, and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　直销企业进行直销员业务培训违反本条例规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，由工商行政管理部门吊销有违法经营行为的直销企业分支机构的营业执照直至由国务院商务主管部门吊销直销企业的直销经营许可证；对授课人员，由工商行政管理部门处5万元以下的罚款，是直销培训员的，责令直销企业撤销其直销培训员资格。  
直销企业以外的单位和个人组织直销员业务培训的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Where a direct selling enterprise conducts business training for direct sellers in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license of the branch of the direct selling enterprise that has engaged in illegal business operations, or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the direct selling business license of the direct selling enterprise; with respect to the lecturer, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan, and if he is a direct selling trainer, it shall order the direct selling enterprise to revoke his qualification as a direct selling trainer.  
Where any entity or individual other than a direct selling enterprise organizes business training for direct sellers, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　直销员违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定的，由工商行政管理部门没收违法销售收入，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令直销企业撤销其直销员资格，并对直销企业处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where a direct seller violates the provisions of Article 22 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall confiscate the illegal sales revenue and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the direct selling enterprise to revoke his qualification as a direct seller, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the direct selling enterprise.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　直销企业违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定的，依照价格法的有关规定处理。

**Article 48** Where a direct selling enterprise violates the provisions of Article 23 of these Regulations, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Price Law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　直销企业违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)和第二十五条规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款，由工商行政管理部门吊销有违法经营行为的直销企业分支机构的营业执照直至由国务院商务主管部门吊销直销企业的直销经营许可证。

**Article 49** Where a direct selling enterprise violates the provisions of Articles 24 and 25 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license of the branch of the direct selling enterprise that has engaged in illegal business operations, or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke the direct selling business license of the direct selling enterprise.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　直销企业未依照有关规定进行信息报备和披露的，由工商行政管理部门责令限期改正，处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，由国务院商务主管部门吊销其直销经营许可证。

**Article 50** Where a direct selling enterprise fails to report and disclose information in accordance with the relevant provisions, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke its direct selling business license.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　直销企业违反本条例第五章有关规定的，由工商行政管理部门责令限期改正，处10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，由国务院商务主管部门吊销其直销经营许可证。

**Article 51** Where a direct selling enterprise violates the relevant provisions of Chapter V of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan, and the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall revoke its direct selling business license.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例的违法行为同时违反《禁止传销条例》的，依照《禁止传销条例》有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 52** Where an illegal act in violation of these Regulations also violates the Regulations on Prohibiting Pyramid Schemes, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on Prohibiting Pyramid Schemes.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　直销企业拟成立直销企业协会等社团组织，应当经国务院商务主管部门批准，凭批准文件依法申请登记。

**Article 53** Where a direct selling enterprise intends to establish a social organization such as a direct selling enterprise association, it shall obtain the approval of the competent department of commerce of the State Council, and apply for registration according to law on the strength of the approval document.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的投资者在境内投资建立直销企业，开展直销活动的，参照本条例有关外国投资者的规定办理。

**Article 54** Where investors from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region invest in establishing direct selling enterprises within the territory and carry out direct selling activities, the provisions of these Regulations concerning foreign investors shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2005年12月1日起施行。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2005.
