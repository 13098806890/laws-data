# 基金会管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2004-03-08  
**生效日期**：2004-06-01

> (2004年2月11日国务院第39次常务会议通过　2004年3月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第400号公布　自2004年6月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范基金会的组织和活动，维护基金会、捐赠人和受益人的合法权益，促进社会力量参与公益事业，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称基金会，是指利用自然人、法人或者其他组织捐赠的财产，以从事公益事业为目的，按照本条例的规定成立的非营利性法人。 第三条　基金会分为面向公众募捐的基金会(以下简称公募基金会)和不得面向公众募捐的基金会(以下简称非公募基金会)。公募基金会按照募捐的地域范围，分为全国性公募基金会和地方性公募基金会。 第四条　基金会必须遵守宪法、法律、法规、规章和国家政策，不得危害国家安全、统一和民族团结，不得违背社会公德。 第五条　基金会依照章程从事公益活动，应当遵循公开、透明的原则。 第六条　国务院民政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门是基金会的登记管理机关。 国务院民政部门负责下列基金会、基金会代表机构的登记管理工作： (一)全国性公募基金会； (二)拟由非内地居民担任法定代表人的基金会； (三)原始基金超过2000万元，发起人向国务院民政部门提出设立申请的非公募基金会； (四)境外基金会在中国内地设立的代表机构。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域内地方性公募基金会和不属于前款规定情况的非公募基金会的登记管理工作。 第七条　国务院有关部门或者国务院授权的组织，是国务院民政部门登记的基金会、境外基金会代表机构的业务主管单位。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范基金会的组织和活动，维护基金会、捐赠人和受益人的合法权益，促进社会力量参与公益事业，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating the organization and activities of foundations, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of foundations, donors and beneficiaries, and promoting the participation of social forces in public welfare undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称基金会，是指利用自然人、法人或者其他组织捐赠的财产，以从事公益事业为目的，按照本条例的规定成立的非营利性法人。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "foundation" means a non-profit legal person established in accordance with these Regulations for the purpose of engaging in public welfare undertakings by using property donated by natural persons, legal persons or other organizations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基金会分为面向公众募捐的基金会(以下简称公募基金会)和不得面向公众募捐的基金会(以下简称非公募基金会)。公募基金会按照募捐的地域范围，分为全国性公募基金会和地方性公募基金会。

**Article 3** Foundations are divided into foundations that may raise funds from the public (hereinafter referred to as public fundraising foundations) and foundations that shall not raise funds from the public (hereinafter referred to as non-public fundraising foundations). Public fundraising foundations are divided into national public fundraising foundations and local public fundraising foundations according to the geographical scope of fundraising.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　基金会必须遵守宪法、法律、法规、规章和国家政策，不得危害国家安全、统一和民族团结，不得违背社会公德。

**Article 4** A foundation must abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, rules and national policies, and shall not jeopardize national security, unity or ethnic solidarity, and shall not violate social ethics.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　基金会依照章程从事公益活动，应当遵循公开、透明的原则。

**Article 5** A foundation that engages in public welfare activities in accordance with its articles of association shall follow the principles of openness and transparency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院民政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门是基金会的登记管理机关。  
国务院民政部门负责下列基金会、基金会代表机构的登记管理工作：  
(一)全国性公募基金会；  
(二)拟由非内地居民担任法定代表人的基金会；  
(三)原始基金超过2000万元，发起人向国务院民政部门提出设立申请的非公募基金会；  
(四)境外基金会在中国内地设立的代表机构。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门负责本行政区域内地方性公募基金会和不属于前款规定情况的非公募基金会的登记管理工作。

**Article 6** The civil affairs department of the State Council and the civil affairs departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be the registration authorities for foundations.  
The civil affairs department of the State Council shall be responsible for the registration administration of the following foundations and representative offices of foundations:  
(1) national public fundraising foundations;  
(2) foundations whose proposed legal representatives are non-Mainland residents;  
(3) non-public fundraising foundations with initial funds exceeding RMB 20 million yuan, whose founders have applied for establishment to the civil affairs department of the State Council; and  
(4) representative offices established in Mainland China by overseas foundations.  
The civil affairs departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the registration administration of local public fundraising foundations and non-public fundraising foundations not falling under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院有关部门或者国务院授权的组织，是国务院民政部门登记的基金会、境外基金会代表机构的业务主管单位。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府授权的组织，是省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门登记的基金会的业务主管单位。

**Article 7** The relevant departments of the State Council or the organizations authorized by the State Council shall be the business supervisory units for foundations registered with the civil affairs department of the State Council and representative offices of overseas foundations.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government or the organizations authorized by such governments shall be the business supervisory units for foundations registered with the civil affairs departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第二章　设立、变更和注销

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立基金会，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)为特定的公益目的而设立；  
(二)全国性公募基金会的原始基金不低于800万元人民币，地方性公募基金会的原始基金不低于400万元人民币，非公募基金会的原始基金不低于200万元人民币；原始基金必须为到账货币资金；  
(三)有规范的名称、章程、组织机构以及与其开展活动相适应的专职工作人员；  
(四)有固定的住所；  
(五)能够独立承担民事责任。

**Article 8** The establishment of a foundation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) it is established for a specific public welfare purpose;  
(2) the initial fund of a national public fundraising foundation shall be no less than RMB 8 million yuan, that of a local public fundraising foundation shall be no less than RMB 4 million yuan, and that of a non-public fundraising foundation shall be no less than RMB 2 million yuan; the initial fund must be monetary funds received into the account;  
(3) it has a standardized name, articles of association, organizational structure and full-time staff appropriate for carrying out its activities;  
(4) it has a fixed domicile; and  
(5) it is capable of independently bearing civil liability.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立基金会，申请人应当向登记管理机关提交下列文件：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)章程草案；  
(三)验资证明和住所证明；  
(四)理事名单、身份证明以及拟任理事长、副理事长、秘书长简历；  
(五)业务主管单位同意设立的文件。

**Article 9** To apply for the establishment of a foundation, the applicant shall submit the following documents to the registration authority:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) a draft of the articles of association;  
(3) a certificate of capital verification and a certificate of domicile;  
(4) a list of directors, identity certificates, and resumes of the proposed chairperson, vice chairpersons and secretary-general;  
(5) the document of consent to the establishment from the business supervisory unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　基金会章程必须明确基金会的公益性质，不得规定使特定自然人、法人或者其他组织受益的内容。  
基金会章程应当载明下列事项：  
(一)名称及住所；  
(二)设立宗旨和公益活动的业务范围；  
(三)原始基金数额；  
(四)理事会的组成、职权和议事规则，理事的资格、产生程序和任期；  
(五)法定代表人的职责；  
(六)监事的职责、资格、产生程序和任期；  
(七)财务会计报告的编制、审定制度；  
(八)财产的管理、使用制度；  
(九)基金会的终止条件、程序和终止后财产的处理。

**Article 10** The articles of association of a foundation must clearly specify the public welfare nature of the foundation and shall not contain content that benefits specific natural persons, legal persons or other organizations.  
The articles of association of a foundation shall include the following:  
(1) name and domicile;  
(2) purpose of establishment and business scope of public welfare activities;  
(3) amount of initial fund;  
(4) composition, functions and powers, and rules of procedure of the council, and the qualifications, selection procedures and terms of office of directors;  
(5) duties of the legal representative;  
(6) duties, qualifications, selection procedures and terms of office of supervisors;  
(7) systems for preparation and review of financial accounting reports;  
(8) systems for management and use of property;  
(9) conditions and procedures for termination of the foundation, and the disposal of property after termination.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　登记管理机关应当自收到[本条例第九条](#art-9)所列全部有效文件之日起60日内，作出准予或者不予登记的决定。准予登记的，发给《基金会法人登记证书》；不予登记的，应当书面说明理由。  
基金会设立登记的事项包括：名称、住所、类型、宗旨、公益活动的业务范围、原始基金数额和法定代表人。

**Article 11** The registration authority shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of all valid documents listed in Article 9 of these Regulations, make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove the registration. If registration is approved, the "Foundation Legal Person Registration Certificate" shall be issued; if registration is not approved, reasons shall be given in writing.  
The matters for establishing registration of a foundation include: name, domicile, type, purpose, business scope of public welfare activities, amount of initial fund and legal representative.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　基金会拟设立分支机构、代表机构的，应当向原登记管理机关提出登记申请，并提交拟设机构的名称、住所和负责人等情况的文件。  
登记管理机关应当自收到前款所列全部有效文件之日起60日内作出准予或者不予登记的决定。准予登记的，发给《基金会分支(代表)机构登记证书》；不予登记的，应当书面说明理由。  
基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构设立登记的事项包括：名称、住所、公益活动的业务范围和负责人。  
基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构依据基金会的授权开展活动，不具有法人资格。

**Article 12** If a foundation intends to establish a branch or representative office, it shall submit an application for registration to the original registration authority, and submit documents on the name, domicile and responsible person of the proposed institution.  
The registration authority shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of all valid documents listed in the preceding paragraph, make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove the registration. If registration is approved, the "Foundation Branch (Representative) Office Registration Certificate" shall be issued; if registration is not approved, reasons shall be given in writing.  
The matters for establishing registration of a branch office or representative office of a foundation include: name, domicile, business scope of public welfare activities and responsible person.  
A branch office or representative office of a foundation shall carry out activities under the authorization of the foundation and shall not have the status of a legal person.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　境外基金会在中国内地设立代表机构，应当经有关业务主管单位同意后，向登记管理机关提交下列文件：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)基金会在境外依法登记成立的证明和基金会章程；  
(三)拟设代表机构负责人身份证明及简历；  
(四)住所证明；  
(五)业务主管单位同意在中国内地设立代表机构的文件。  
登记管理机关应当自收到前款所列全部有效文件之日起60日内，作出准予或者不予登记的决定。准予登记的，发给《境外基金会代表机构登记证书》；不予登记的，应当书面说明理由。  
境外基金会代表机构设立登记的事项包括：名称、住所、公益活动的业务范围和负责人。  
境外基金会代表机构应当从事符合中国公益事业性质的公益活动。境外基金会对其在中国内地代表机构的民事行为，依照中国法律承担民事责任。

**Article 13** An overseas foundation that establishes a representative office in Mainland China shall, after obtaining the consent of the relevant business supervisory unit, submit the following documents to the registration authority:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) certification of the foundation's lawful establishment outside China and its articles of association;  
(3) identity certificate and resume of the proposed responsible person of the representative office;  
(4) certificate of domicile;  
(5) the document of consent from the business supervisory unit for the establishment of the representative office in Mainland China.  
The registration authority shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of all valid documents listed in the preceding paragraph, make a decision on whether to approve or disapprove the registration. If registration is approved, the "Overseas Foundation Representative Office Registration Certificate" shall be issued; if registration is not approved, reasons shall be given in writing.  
The matters for establishing registration of an overseas foundation representative office include: name, domicile, business scope of public welfare activities and responsible person.  
An overseas foundation representative office shall engage in public welfare activities that conform to the nature of China's public welfare undertakings. An overseas foundation shall bear civil liability for the civil acts of its representative office in Mainland China in accordance with Chinese laws.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　基金会、境外基金会代表机构依照本条例登记后，应当依法办理税务登记。  
基金会、境外基金会代表机构，凭登记证书依法申请组织机构代码、刻制印章、开立银行账户。  
基金会、境外基金会代表机构应当将组织机构代码、印章式样、银行账号以及税务登记证件复印件报登记管理机关备案。

**Article 14** After registration in accordance with these Regulations, a foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall undergo tax registration in accordance with law.  
A foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall, on the strength of its registration certificate, apply for an organization code, engrave a seal, and open a bank account in accordance with law.  
A foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall file for record with the registration authority its organization code, specimen of seal, bank account number and a copy of its tax registration certificate.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　基金会、基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构和境外基金会代表机构的登记事项需要变更的，应当向登记管理机关申请变更登记。  
基金会修改章程，应当征得其业务主管单位的同意，并报登记管理机关核准。

**Article 15** If the registered items of a foundation, a branch office of a foundation, a representative office of a foundation or an overseas foundation representative office need to be changed, an application for change of registration shall be made to the registration authority.  
If a foundation amends its articles of association, it shall obtain the consent of its business supervisory unit and submit it to the registration authority for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　基金会、境外基金会代表机构有下列情形之一的，应当向登记管理机关申请注销登记：  
(一)按照章程规定终止的；  
(二)无法按照章程规定的宗旨继续从事公益活动的；  
(三)由于其他原因终止的。

**Article 16** A foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall apply to the registration authority for deregistration under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it terminates in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association;  
(2) it is unable to continue engaging in public welfare activities in accordance with the purpose stated in its articles of association; or  
(3) it terminates for other reasons.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　基金会撤销其分支机构、代表机构的，应当向登记管理机关办理分支机构、代表机构的注销登记。  
基金会注销的，其分支机构、代表机构同时注销。

**Article 17** If a foundation dissolves its branch office or representative office, it shall undergo deregistration of such branch office or representative office with the registration authority.  
If a foundation is deregistered, its branch offices and representative offices shall be deregistered simultaneously.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　基金会在办理注销登记前，应当在登记管理机关、业务主管单位的指导下成立清算组织，完成清算工作。  
基金会应当自清算结束之日起15日内向登记管理机关办理注销登记；在清算期间不得开展清算以外的活动。

**Article 18** Before undergoing deregistration, a foundation shall establish a liquidation organization under the guidance of the registration authority and the business supervisory unit to complete the liquidation.  
A foundation shall undergo deregistration with the registration authority within 15 days from the date of completion of liquidation; during the liquidation period, it shall not engage in any activities other than those related to liquidation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　基金会、基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构以及境外基金会代表机构的设立、变更、注销登记，由登记管理机关向社会公告。

**Article 19** The establishment, change and deregistration of foundations, branch offices of foundations, representative offices of foundations and overseas foundation representative offices shall be publicly announced by the registration authority.

### 第三章　组织机构

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　基金会设理事会，理事为5人至25人，理事任期由章程规定，但每届任期不得超过5年。理事任期届满，连选可以连任。  
用私人财产设立的非公募基金会，相互间有近亲属关系的基金会理事，总数不得超过理事总人数的1/3；其他基金会，具有近亲属关系的不得同时在理事会任职。  
在基金会领取报酬的理事不得超过理事总人数的1/3。  
理事会设理事长、副理事长和秘书长，从理事中选举产生，理事长是基金会的法定代表人。

**Article 20** A foundation shall have a council, with the number of directors being 5 to 25. The term of office of directors shall be specified in the articles of association, but each term shall not exceed 5 years. Directors may be re-elected upon expiration of their terms.  
For a non-public fundraising foundation established with private property, the total number of directors who have close kinship with each other shall not exceed 1/3 of the total number of directors; for other foundations, persons with close kinship shall not serve concurrently on the council.  
The number of directors who receive remuneration from the foundation shall not exceed 1/3 of the total number of directors.  
The council shall have a chairperson, vice chairpersons and a secretary-general, who shall be elected from among the directors. The chairperson shall be the legal representative of the foundation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　理事会是基金会的决策机构，依法行使章程规定的职权。  
理事会每年至少召开2次会议。理事会会议须有2/3以上理事出席方能召开；理事会决议须经出席理事过半数通过方为有效。  
下列重要事项的决议，须经出席理事表决，2/3以上通过方为有效：  
(一)章程的修改；  
(二)选举或者罢免理事长、副理事长、秘书长；  
(三)章程规定的重大募捐、投资活动；  
(四)基金会的分立、合并。  
理事会会议应当制作会议记录，并由出席理事审阅、签名。

**Article 21** The council shall be the decision-making body of the foundation, exercising the functions and powers specified in the articles of association in accordance with law.  
The council shall hold at least 2 meetings each year. A council meeting may be convened only if more than 2/3 of the directors are present; a resolution of the council shall be valid only if it is approved by more than half of the directors present.  
Resolutions on the following important matters shall be valid only if they are approved by more than 2/3 of the directors present after voting:  
(1) amendment of the articles of association;  
(2) election or removal of the chairperson, vice chairpersons and secretary-general;  
(3) major fundraising and investment activities stipulated in the articles of association; and  
(4) division or merger of the foundation.  
Minutes of council meetings shall be prepared and reviewed and signed by the directors present.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　基金会设监事。监事任期与理事任期相同。理事、理事的近亲属和基金会财会人员不得兼任监事。  
监事依照章程规定的程序检查基金会财务和会计资料，监督理事会遵守法律和章程的情况。  
监事列席理事会会议，有权向理事会提出质询和建议，并应当向登记管理机关、业务主管单位以及税务、会计主管部门反映情况。

**Article 22** A foundation shall have supervisors. The term of office of supervisors shall be the same as that of directors. Directors, close relatives of directors, and financial and accounting personnel of the foundation shall not serve concurrently as supervisors.  
Supervisors shall inspect the financial and accounting records of the foundation in accordance with the procedures specified in the articles of association, and supervise the council's compliance with laws and the articles of association.  
Supervisors shall attend council meetings as non-voting participants, have the right to raise questions and make suggestions to the council, and shall report the situation to the registration authority, the business supervisory unit, and the tax and accounting regulatory authorities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　基金会理事长、副理事长和秘书长不得由现职国家工作人员兼任。基金会的法定代表人，不得同时担任其他组织的法定代表人。公募基金会和原始基金来自中国内地的非公募基金会的法定代表人，应当由内地居民担任。  
因犯罪被判处管制、拘役或者有期徒刑，刑期执行完毕之日起未逾5年的，因犯罪被判处剥夺政治权利正在执行期间或者曾经被判处剥夺政治权利的，以及曾在因违法被撤销登记的基金会担任理事长、副理事长或者秘书长，且对该基金会的违法行为负有个人责任，自该基金会被撤销之日起未逾5年的，不得担任基金会的理事长、副理事长或者秘书长。  
基金会理事遇有个人利益与基金会利益关联时，不得参与相关事宜的决策；基金会理事、监事及其近亲属不得与其所在的基金会有任何交易行为。  
监事和未在基金会担任专职工作的理事不得从基金会获取报酬。

**Article 23** The chairperson, vice chairpersons and secretary-general of a foundation shall not be concurrently held by current state functionaries. The legal representative of a foundation shall not concurrently serve as the legal representative of another organization. The legal representative of a public fundraising foundation or a non-public fundraising foundation whose initial funds come from Mainland China shall be a Mainland resident.  
A person who: has been sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment for a crime, and less than 5 years have elapsed since the completion of the sentence; has been sentenced to deprivation of political rights for a crime and is still serving such sentence or has been so sentenced in the past; or has served as chairperson, vice chairperson or secretary-general of a foundation that was deregistered for illegal acts and bears personal responsibility for such illegal acts, and less than 5 years have elapsed since the date of such deregistration, shall not serve as chairperson, vice chairperson or secretary-general of a foundation.  
A director of a foundation who has a conflict of personal interest with the interest of the foundation shall not participate in decision-making on the relevant matters; directors, supervisors and their close relatives of a foundation shall not engage in any transaction with the foundation.  
Supervisors and directors who do not hold full-time positions in the foundation shall not receive remuneration from the foundation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　担任基金会理事长、副理事长或者秘书长的香港居民、澳门居民、台湾居民、外国人以及境外基金会代表机构的负责人，每年在中国内地居留时间不得少于3个月。

**Article 24** Hong Kong residents, Macao residents, Taiwan residents, foreigners who serve as chairperson, vice chairperson or secretary-general of a foundation, as well as the responsible person of an overseas foundation representative office, shall stay in Mainland China for no less than 3 months each year.

### 第四章　财产的管理和使用

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　基金会组织募捐、接受捐赠，应当符合章程规定的宗旨和公益活动的业务范围。境外基金会代表机构不得在中国境内组织募捐、接受捐赠。  
公募基金会组织募捐，应当向社会公布募得资金后拟开展的公益活动和资金的详细使用计划。

**Article 25** A foundation that organizes fundraising or accepts donations shall conform to the purpose and business scope of public welfare activities as specified in its articles of association. An overseas foundation representative office shall not organize fundraising or accept donations within the territory of China.  
A public fundraising foundation that organizes fundraising shall publicly announce the public welfare activities to be carried out with the funds raised and the detailed plan for the use of funds.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　基金会及其捐赠人、受益人依照法律、行政法规的规定享受税收优惠。

**Article 26** Foundations, their donors and beneficiaries shall enjoy tax preferences in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　基金会的财产及其他收入受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得私分、侵占、挪用。  
基金会应当根据章程规定的宗旨和公益活动的业务范围使用其财产；捐赠协议明确了具体使用方式的捐赠，根据捐赠协议的约定使用。  
接受捐赠的物资无法用于符合其宗旨的用途时，基金会可以依法拍卖或者变卖，所得收入用于捐赠目的。

**Article 27** The property and other income of a foundation shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may privately distribute, encroach upon or misappropriate them.  
A foundation shall use its property in accordance with the purpose and business scope of public welfare activities as specified in its articles of association; for donations for which the donation agreement specifies the specific method of use, the property shall be used in accordance with the terms of the donation agreement.  
If the materials accepted as donations cannot be used for purposes consistent with the foundation's purpose, the foundation may auction or sell them in accordance with law, and the proceeds shall be used for the purpose of the donation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　基金会应当按照合法、安全、有效的原则实现基金的保值、增值。

**Article 28** A foundation shall preserve and increase the value of its funds in accordance with the principles of legality, safety and effectiveness.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公募基金会每年用于从事章程规定的公益事业支出，不得低于上一年总收入的70%；非公募基金会每年用于从事章程规定的公益事业支出，不得低于上一年基金余额的8%。  
基金会工作人员工资福利和行政办公支出不得超过当年总支出的10%。

**Article 29** A public fundraising foundation shall spend no less than 70% of its total revenue of the previous year on public welfare undertakings as specified in its articles of association each year; a non-public fundraising foundation shall spend no less than 8% of its fund balance of the previous year on such undertakings each year.  
The staff salaries, welfare and administrative office expenses of a foundation shall not exceed 10% of its total expenditure for the current year.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　基金会开展公益资助项目，应当向社会公布所开展的公益资助项目种类以及申请、评审程序。

**Article 30** When carrying out public welfare funding projects, a foundation shall publicly announce the types of public welfare funding projects to be carried out and the application and review procedures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　基金会可以与受助人签订协议，约定资助方式、资助数额以及资金用途和使用方式。  
基金会有权对资助的使用情况进行监督。受助人未按协议约定使用资助或者有其他违反协议情形的，基金会有权解除资助协议。

**Article 31** A foundation may enter into an agreement with the recipient to specify the method and amount of funding, as well as the purpose and manner of use of the funds.  
A foundation shall have the right to supervise the use of the funding. If the recipient fails to use the funding in accordance with the terms of the agreement or otherwise violates the agreement, the foundation shall have the right to terminate the funding agreement.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　基金会应当执行国家统一的会计制度，依法进行会计核算、建立健全内部会计监督制度。

**Article 32** A foundation shall implement the unified national accounting system, conduct accounting in accordance with law, and establish and improve an internal accounting supervision system.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　基金会注销后的剩余财产应当按照章程的规定用于公益目的；无法按照章程规定处理的，由登记管理机关组织捐赠给与该基金会性质、宗旨相同的社会公益组织，并向社会公告。

**Article 33** The remaining property of a foundation after deregistration shall be used for public welfare purposes in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association; if it cannot be disposed of in accordance with the articles of association, the registration authority shall organize its donation to a social welfare organization with the same nature and purpose as the foundation, and make a public announcement.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　基金会登记管理机关履行下列监督管理职责：  
(一)对基金会、境外基金会代表机构实施年度检查；  
(二)对基金会、境外基金会代表机构依照本条例及其章程开展活动的情况进行日常监督管理；  
(三)对基金会、境外基金会代表机构违反本条例的行为依法进行处罚。

**Article 34** The registration authority for foundations shall perform the following supervisory and administrative duties:  
(1) conducting annual inspections of foundations and overseas foundation representative offices;  
(2) carrying out day-to-day supervision and administration of the activities of foundations and overseas foundation representative offices in accordance with these Regulations and their articles of association; and  
(3) imposing penalties in accordance with law for violations of these Regulations by foundations and overseas foundation representative offices.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　基金会业务主管单位履行下列监督管理职责：  
(一)指导、监督基金会、境外基金会代表机构依据法律和章程开展公益活动；  
(二)负责基金会、境外基金会代表机构年度检查的初审；  
(三)配合登记管理机关、其他执法部门查处基金会、境外基金会代表机构的违法行为。

**Article 35** The business supervisory unit of a foundation shall perform the following supervisory and administrative duties:  
(1) guiding and supervising foundations and overseas foundation representative offices in carrying out public welfare activities in accordance with laws and their articles of association;  
(2) being responsible for the preliminary review of the annual inspection of foundations and overseas foundation representative offices; and  
(3) cooperating with the registration authority and other law enforcement departments in investigating and dealing with violations by foundations and overseas foundation representative offices.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　基金会、境外基金会代表机构应当于每年3月31日前向登记管理机关报送上一年度工作报告，接受年度检查。年度工作报告在报送登记管理机关前应当经业务主管单位审查同意。  
年度工作报告应当包括：财务会计报告、注册会计师审计报告，开展募捐、接受捐赠、提供资助等活动的情况以及人员和机构的变动情况等。

**Article 36** A foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall, before March 31 of each year, submit a work report for the previous year to the registration authority for annual inspection. The annual work report shall be reviewed and approved by the business supervisory unit before being submitted to the registration authority.  
The annual work report shall include: the financial accounting report, the audit report of a certified public accountant, the situation of fundraising, acceptance of donations, provision of funding and other activities, as well as changes in personnel and organizational structure, etc.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　基金会应当接受税务、会计主管部门依法实施的税务监督和会计监督。  
基金会在换届和更换法定代表人之前，应当进行财务审计。

**Article 37** A foundation shall accept tax supervision and accounting supervision implemented by the tax and accounting regulatory authorities in accordance with law.  
A foundation shall undergo a financial audit before the change of term or the replacement of its legal representative.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　基金会、境外基金会代表机构应当在通过登记管理机关的年度检查后，将年度工作报告在登记管理机关指定的媒体上公布，接受社会公众的查询、监督。

**Article 38** After passing the annual inspection by the registration authority, a foundation or an overseas foundation representative office shall publish the annual work report on the media designated by the registration authority to accept public inquiry and supervision.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　捐赠人有权向基金会查询捐赠财产的使用、管理情况，并提出意见和建议。对于捐赠人的查询，基金会应当及时如实答复。  
基金会违反捐赠协议使用捐赠财产的，捐赠人有权要求基金会遵守捐赠协议或者向人民法院申请撤销捐赠行为、解除捐赠协议。

**Article 39** A donor shall have the right to inquire of the foundation about the use and management of the donated property, and to raise opinions and suggestions. The foundation shall reply to the donor's inquiry promptly and truthfully.  
If a foundation uses the donated property in violation of the donation agreement, the donor shall have the right to demand that the foundation comply with the donation agreement, or to apply to the People's Court for revocation of the donation act and termination of the donation agreement.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　未经登记或者被撤销登记后以基金会、基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构或者境外基金会代表机构名义开展活动的，由登记管理机关予以取缔，没收非法财产并向社会公告。

**Article 40** Any person who, without registration or after registration has been revoked, carries out activities in the name of a foundation, branch office of a foundation, representative office of a foundation or overseas foundation representative office shall be banned by the registration authority, its illegal property shall be confiscated, and a public announcement shall be made.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　基金会、基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构或者境外基金会代表机构有下列情形之一的，登记管理机关应当撤销登记：  
(一)在申请登记时弄虚作假骗取登记的，或者自取得登记证书之日起12个月内未按章程规定开展活动的；  
(二)符合注销条件，不按照本条例的规定办理注销登记仍继续开展活动的。

**Article 41** The registration authority shall revoke the registration of a foundation, branch office of a foundation, representative office of a foundation or overseas foundation representative office under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) it obtained registration by fraud through practicing deception in the application for registration, or it fails to carry out activities in accordance with its articles of association within 12 months from the date of obtaining the registration certificate; or  
(2) it meets the conditions for deregistration but continues to carry out activities without undergoing deregistration in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　基金会、基金会分支机构、基金会代表机构或者境外基金会代表机构有下列情形之一的，由登记管理机关给予警告、责令停止活动；情节严重的，可以撤销登记：  
(一)未按照章程规定的宗旨和公益活动的业务范围进行活动的；  
(二)在填制会计凭证、登记会计账簿、编制财务会计报告中弄虚作假的；  
(三)不按照规定办理变更登记的；  
(四)未按照本条例的规定完成公益事业支出额度的；  
(五)未按照本条例的规定接受年度检查，或者年度检查不合格的；  
(六)不履行信息公布义务或者公布虚假信息的。  
基金会、境外基金会代表机构有前款所列行为的，登记管理机关应当提请税务机关责令补交违法行为存续期间所享受的税收减免。

**Article 42** A foundation, branch office of a foundation, representative office of a foundation or overseas foundation representative office shall, under any of the following circumstances, be given a warning and ordered to cease activities by the registration authority; if the circumstances are serious, its registration may be revoked:  
(1) it carries out activities not in conformity with the purpose and business scope of public welfare activities as specified in its articles of association;  
(2) it practices deception in the preparation of accounting vouchers, recording of accounting books, or preparation of financial accounting reports;  
(3) it fails to undergo change registration in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) it fails to meet the mandated expenditure quotas for public welfare undertakings in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) it fails to undergo annual inspection in accordance with these Regulations, or fails to pass the annual inspection; or  
(6) it fails to fulfill the obligation to disclose information or discloses false information.  
If a foundation or overseas foundation representative office commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the registration authority shall request the tax authority to order it to make up the tax reductions or exemptions it enjoyed during the period of the illegal act.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　基金会理事会违反本条例和章程规定决策不当，致使基金会遭受财产损失的，参与决策的理事应当承担相应的赔偿责任。  
基金会理事、监事以及专职工作人员私分、侵占、挪用基金会财产的，应当退还非法占用的财产；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** If the council of a foundation makes improper decisions in violation of these Regulations and the articles of association, thereby causing property losses to the foundation, the directors who participated in the decision-making shall bear corresponding compensation liability.  
If directors, supervisors or full-time staff of a foundation privately distribute, encroach upon or misappropriate the property of the foundation, they shall return the illegally occupied property; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　基金会、境外基金会代表机构被责令停止活动的，由登记管理机关封存其登记证书、印章和财务凭证。

**Article 44** If a foundation or an overseas foundation representative office is ordered to cease activities, the registration authority shall seal up its registration certificate, seal and financial vouchers.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　登记管理机关、业务主管单位工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 45** If a staff member of the registration authority or the business supervisory unit abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in favoritism and malpractice, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, an administrative sanction or disciplinary sanction shall be imposed in accordance with law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例所称境外基金会，是指在外国以及中华人民共和国香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区合法成立的基金会。

**Article 46** For the purposes of these Regulations, "overseas foundation" means a foundation lawfully established in a foreign country or in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region or the Taiwan region of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　基金会设立申请书、基金会年度工作报告的格式以及基金会章程范本，由国务院民政部门制订。

**Article 47** The formats of the application for establishment of a foundation, the annual work report of a foundation and the model articles of association of a foundation shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2004年6月1日起施行，1988年9月27日国务院发布的《基金会管理办法》同时废止。  
本条例施行前已经设立的基金会、境外基金会代表机构，应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内，按照本条例的规定申请换发登记证书。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2004, and the "Measures for the Administration of Foundations" promulgated by the State Council on September 27, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.  
Foundations and overseas foundation representative offices that have been established prior to the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within 6 months from the date of such coming into force, apply for replacement of their registration certificates in accordance with these Regulations.
