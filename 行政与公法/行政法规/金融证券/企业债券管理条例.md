# 企业债券管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1993年8月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第121号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对企业债券的管理，引导资金的合理流向，有效利用社会闲散资金，保护投资者的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内具有法人资格的企业(以下简称企业)在境内发行的债券。但是，金融债券和外币债券除外。 除前款规定的企业外，任何单位和个人不得发行企业债券。 第三条　企业进行有偿筹集资金活动，必须通过公开发行企业债券的形式进行。但是，法律和国务院另有规定的除外。 第四条　发行和购买企业债券应当遵循自愿、互利、有偿的原则。 第二章　企业债券 第五条　本条例所称企业债券，是指企业依照法定程序发行、约定在一定期限内还本付息的有价证券。 第六条　企业债券的票面应当载明下列内容： (一)企业的名称、住所； (二)企业债券的面额； (三)企业债券的利率； (四)还本期限和方式； (五)利息的支付方式；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对企业债券的管理，引导资金的合理流向，有效利用社会闲散资金，保护投资者的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of enterprise bonds, guiding the rational flow of funds, effectively utilizing idle funds in society, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of investors.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内具有法人资格的企业(以下简称企业)在境内发行的债券。但是，金融债券和外币债券除外。  
除前款规定的企业外，任何单位和个人不得发行企业债券。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to bonds issued within the territory of the People's Republic of China by enterprises with the status of legal persons (hereinafter referred to as enterprises) within the territory. However, financial bonds and bonds denominated in foreign currencies shall be excluded.  
No unit or individual, other than the enterprises specified in the preceding paragraph, may issue enterprise bonds.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　企业进行有偿筹集资金活动，必须通过公开发行企业债券的形式进行。但是，法律和国务院另有规定的除外。

**Article 3** An enterprise that engages in activities of raising funds for consideration must do so by publicly issuing enterprise bonds, unless otherwise provided by laws or the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　发行和购买企业债券应当遵循自愿、互利、有偿的原则。

**Article 4** The issuance and purchase of enterprise bonds shall follow the principles of voluntariness, mutual benefit, and compensation.

### 第二章　企业债券

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本条例所称企业债券，是指企业依照法定程序发行、约定在一定期限内还本付息的有价证券。

**Article 5** For the purpose of these Regulations, "enterprise bonds" shall refer to negotiable securities issued by enterprises in accordance with the statutory procedures, with a commitment to repay the principal and pay interest within a specified period.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业债券的票面应当载明下列内容：  
(一)企业的名称、住所；  
(二)企业债券的面额；  
(三)企业债券的利率；  
(四)还本期限和方式；  
(五)利息的支付方式；  
(六)企业债券发行日期和编号；  
(七)企业的印记和企业法定代表人的签章；  
(八)审批机关批准发行的文号、日期。

**Article 6** The face of an enterprise bond shall specify the following:  
(1) The name and domicile of the enterprise;  
(2) The face value of the enterprise bond;  
(3) The interest rate of the enterprise bond;  
(4) The term and method of repayment of principal;  
(5) The method of payment of interest;  
(6) The date of issuance and serial number of the enterprise bond;  
(7) The seal of the enterprise and the signature or seal of the legal representative of the enterprise;  
(8) The document number and date of the approval for issuance by the approving authority.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业债券持有人有权按照约定期限取得利息、收回本金，但是无权参与企业的经营管理。

**Article 7** A holder of an enterprise bond shall have the right to receive interest and recover the principal within the agreed term, but shall have no right to participate in the operation and management of the enterprise.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业债券持有人对企业的经营状况不承担责任。

**Article 8** A holder of an enterprise bond shall bear no liability for the business condition of the enterprise.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业债券可以转让、抵押和继承。

**Article 9** Enterprise bonds may be transferred, mortgaged, and inherited.

### 第三章　企业债券的管理

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家计划委员会会同中国人民银行、财政部、国务院证券委员会拟订全国企业债券发行的年度规模和规模内的各项指标，报国务院批准后，下达各省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市人民政府和国务院有关部门执行。  
未经国务院同意，任何地方、部门不得擅自突破企业债券发行的年度规模，并不得擅自调整年度规模内的各项指标。

**Article 10** The State Planning Commission shall, in conjunction with the People's Bank of China, the Ministry of Finance, and the Securities Commission of the State Council, draw up the annual national quotas for enterprise bond issuance and the various targets within such quotas, and shall, after approval by the State Council, transmit them to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities separately listed in the state plan, as well as to the relevant departments of the State Council for enforcement.  
Without the consent of the State Council, no locality or department may arbitrarily exceed the annual national quotas for enterprise bond issuance, nor may it arbitrarily adjust the various targets within the annual quotas.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业发行企业债券必须按照本条例的规定进行审批；未经批准的，不得擅自发行和变相发行企业债券。  
中央企业发行企业债券，由中国人民银行会同国家计划委员会审批；地方企业发行企业债券，由中国人民银行省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市分行会同同级计划主管部门审批。

**Article 11** An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds must undergo examination and approval in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; without approval, it may not arbitrarily issue enterprise bonds, nor issue them in a disguised form.  
The issuance of enterprise bonds by central enterprises shall be subject to examination and approval by the People's Bank of China in conjunction with the State Planning Commission; the issuance of enterprise bonds by local enterprises shall be subject to examination and approval by the branches of the People's Bank of China in provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, or cities separately listed in the state plan in conjunction with the competent planning departments at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　企业发行企业债券必须符合下列条件：  
(一)企业规模达到国家规定的要求；  
(二)企业财务会计制度符合国家规定；  
(三)具有偿债能力；  
(四)企业经济效益良好，发行企业债券前连续3年盈利；  
(五)所筹资金用途符合国家产业政策。

**Article 12** An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds must meet the following conditions:  
(1) The scale of the enterprise reaches the requirements set by the state;  
(2) The financial accounting system of the enterprise complies with state provisions;  
(3) The enterprise has the ability to repay debts;  
(4) The enterprise has good economic results and has been profitable for three consecutive years prior to the issuance of enterprise bonds;  
(5) The use of the funds raised conforms to the state industrial policy.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业发行企业债券应当制订发行章程。  
发行章程应当包括下列内容：  
(一)企业的名称、住所、经营范围、法定代表人；  
(二)企业近3年的生产经营状况和有关业务发展的基本情况；  
(三)财务报告；  
(四)企业自有资产净值；  
(五)筹集资金的用途；  
(六)效益预测；  
(七)发行对象、时间、期限、方式；  
(八)债券的种类及期限；  
(九)债券的利率；  
(十)债券总面额；  
(十一)还本付息方式；  
(十二)审批机关要求载明的其他事项。

**Article 13** An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds shall formulate an issuance prospectus.  
The issuance prospectus shall include the following:  
(1) The name, domicile, business scope, and legal representative of the enterprise;  
(2) The production and operation status of the enterprise in the recent three years and the basic situation concerning business development;  
(3) Financial reports;  
(4) The net value of the enterprise's own assets;  
(5) The purpose of the funds raised;  
(6) Forecast of benefits;  
(7) The target subscribers, time, term, and method of issuance;  
(8) The types and terms of the bonds;  
(9) The interest rate of the bonds;  
(10) The total face value of the bonds;  
(11) The method of repayment of principal and payment of interest;  
(12) Other matters required to be specified by the approving authority.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业申请发行企业债券，应当向审批机关报送下列文件：  
(一)发行企业债券的申请书；  
(二)营业执照；  
(三)发行章程；  
(四)经会计师事务所审计的企业近3年的财务报告；  
(五)审批机关要求提供的其他材料。  
企业发行企业债券用于固定资产投资，按照国家有关规定需要经有关部门审批的，还应当报送有关部门的审批文件。

**Article 14** An enterprise applying to issue enterprise bonds shall submit the following documents to the approving authority:  
(1) An application for issuance of enterprise bonds;  
(2) The business license;  
(3) The issuance prospectus;  
(4) The financial reports of the enterprise for the recent three years audited by an accounting firm;  
(5) Other materials required to be provided by the approving authority.  
Where an enterprise issues enterprise bonds for investment in fixed assets and, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, requires approval from the relevant departments, it shall also submit the approval documents from such departments.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业发行企业债券应当公布经审批机关批准的发行章程。  
企业发行企业债券，可以向经认可的债券评信机构申请信用评级。

**Article 15** An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds shall publish the issuance prospectus approved by the approving authority.  
An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds may apply for a credit rating from a recognized bond credit rating institution.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业发行企业债券的总面额不得大于该企业的自有资产净值。

**Article 16** The total face value of enterprise bonds issued by an enterprise shall not exceed the net value of the enterprise's own assets.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业发行企业债券用于固定资产投资的，依照国家有关固定资产投资的规定办理。

**Article 17** Where an enterprise issues enterprise bonds for investment in fixed assets, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant state provisions on investment in fixed assets.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　企业债券的利率不得高于银行相同期限居民储蓄定期存款利率的40%。

**Article 18** The interest rate of enterprise bonds shall not exceed 40% of the interest rate on bank time deposits of the same term for household savings.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何单位不得以下列资金购买企业债券：  
(一)财政预算拨款；  
(二)银行贷款；  
(三)国家规定不得用于购买企业债券的其他资金。  
办理储蓄业务的机构不得将所吸收的储蓄存款用于购买企业债券。

**Article 19** No unit may use the following funds to purchase enterprise bonds:  
(1) Fiscal budget appropriations;  
(2) Bank loans;  
(3) Other funds that the state has provided may not be used to purchase enterprise bonds.  
An institution handling savings business may not use the savings deposits it has absorbed to purchase enterprise bonds.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业发行企业债券所筹资金应当按照审批机关批准的用途，用于本企业的生产经营。  
企业发行企业债券所筹资金不得用于房地产买卖、股票买卖和期货交易等与本企业生产经营无关的风险性投资。

**Article 20** The funds raised by an enterprise through the issuance of enterprise bonds shall be used for the production and operation of the enterprise in accordance with the purpose approved by the approving authority.  
The funds raised by an enterprise through the issuance of enterprise bonds may not be used for risky investments unrelated to the enterprise's own production and operation, such as real estate trading, stock trading, and futures trading.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　企业发行企业债券，应当由证券经营机构承销。  
证券经营机构承销企业债券，应当对发行债券的企业的发行章程和其他有关文件的真实性、准确性、完整性进行核查。

**Article 21** An enterprise that issues enterprise bonds shall have them underwritten by a securities operating institution.  
A securities operating institution that underwrites enterprise bonds shall verify the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the issuance prospectus and other relevant documents of the enterprise issuing the bonds.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业债券的转让，应当在经批准的可以进行债券交易的场所进行。

**Article 22** The transfer of enterprise bonds shall be conducted at the venues approved for bond trading.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　非证券经营机构和个人不得经营企业债券的承销和转让业务。

**Article 23** Non-securities operating institutions and individuals may not engage in the underwriting or transfer of enterprise bonds.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　单位和个人所得的企业债券利息收入，按照国家规定纳税。

**Article 24** The interest income derived from enterprise bonds by units and individuals shall be taxed in accordance with state provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　中国人民银行及其分支机构和国家证券监督管理机构，依照规定的职责，负责对企业债券的发行和交易活动，进行监督检查。

**Article 25** The People's Bank of China and its branches, as well as the state securities regulatory authority, shall, in accordance with their prescribed functions and duties, be responsible for supervising and inspecting the issuance and trading activities of enterprise bonds.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　未经批准发行或者变相发行企业债券的，以及未通过证券经营机构发行企业债券的，责令停止发行活动，退还非法所筹资金，处以相当于非法所筹资金金额5%以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Where enterprise bonds are issued without approval or in a disguised form, or where enterprise bonds are issued without going through a securities operating institution, the issuer shall be ordered to cease the issuance activities, refund the illegally raised funds, and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the amount of the illegally raised funds.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　超过批准数额发行企业债券的，责令退还超额发行部分或者核减相当于超额发行金额的贷款额度，处以相当于超额发行部分5%以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where enterprise bonds are issued in excess of the approved amount, the issuer shall be ordered to refund the excess portion or reduce the loan quota by an amount equivalent to the excess issuance, and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the excess portion.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　超过[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的最高利率发行企业债券的，责令改正，处以相当于所筹资金金额5%以下的罚款。

**Article 28** Where enterprise bonds are issued at an interest rate exceeding the maximum rate specified in Article 18 of these Regulations, the issuer shall be ordered to make corrections and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the amount of funds raised.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　用财政预算拨款、银行贷款或者国家规定不得用于购买企业债券的其他资金购买企业债券的，以及办理储蓄业务的机构用所吸收的储蓄存款购买企业债券的，责令收回该资金，处以相当于所购买企业债券金额5%以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where fiscal budget appropriations, bank loans, or other funds that the state has provided may not be used to purchase enterprise bonds are used to purchase enterprise bonds, or where an institution handling savings business uses the savings deposits it has absorbed to purchase enterprise bonds, the entity shall be ordered to recover such funds and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the amount of enterprise bonds purchased.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未按批准用途使用发行企业债券所筹资金的，责令改正，没收其违反批准用途使用资金所获收益，并处以相当于违法使用资金金额5%以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Where the funds raised through the issuance of enterprise bonds are used for purposes other than those approved, the entity shall be ordered to make corrections, confiscate the gains obtained from using the funds in violation of the approved purposes, and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the amount of funds illegally used.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　非证券经营机构和个人经营企业债券的承销或者转让业务的，责令停止非法经营，没收非法所得，并处以承销或者转让企业债券金额5%以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Where a non-securities operating institution or an individual engages in the underwriting or transfer of enterprise bonds, the entity or individual shall be ordered to cease the illegal operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and be imposed a fine of not more than 5% of the amount of enterprise bonds underwritten or transferred.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十七条、第二十八条、第二十九条、第三十条、第三十一条规定的处罚，由中国人民银行及其分支机构决定。

**Article 32** The penalties provided in Articles 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, and 31 of these Regulations shall be determined by the People's Bank of China or its branches.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对有[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十七条、第二十八条、第二十九条、第三十条、第三十一条所列违法行为的单位的法定代表人和直接责任人员，由中国人民银行及其分支机构给予警告或者处以1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where a unit commits any of the illegal acts listed in Articles 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, and 31 of these Regulations, the legal representative and the persons directly responsible of such unit shall be given a warning or impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan by the People's Bank of China or its branches; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　地方审批机关违反本条例规定，批准发行企业债券的，责令改正，给予通报批评，根据情况相应核减该地方企业债券的发行规模。

**Article 34** Where a local approving authority, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, approves the issuance of enterprise bonds, it shall be ordered to make corrections, given a notice of criticism, and the local enterprise bond issuance quota shall be correspondingly reduced according to the circumstances.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　企业债券监督管理机关的工作人员玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where a staff member of the supervisory and administrative authority for enterprise bonds neglects duties or engages in malpractice for personal gain, such staff member shall be given administrative sanctions; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　发行企业债券的企业违反本条例规定，给他人造成损失的，应当依法承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 36** Where an enterprise that issues enterprise bonds violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes losses to others, it shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　企业发行短期融资券，按照中国人民银行有关规定执行。

**Article 37** The issuance of short-term financing bonds by enterprises shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例由中国人民银行会同国家计划委员会解释。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the People's Bank of China in conjunction with the State Planning Commission.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1987年3月27日国务院发布的《企业债券管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Regulations on the Administration of Enterprise Bonds promulgated by the State Council on March 27, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously.
