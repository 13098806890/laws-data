# 棉花质量监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2017-10-07

> (2001年8月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第314号公布　根据2006年7月4日《国务院关于修改〈棉花质量监督管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对棉花质量的监督管理，维护棉花市场秩序，保护棉花交易各方的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen supervision and administration over the quality of cotton, maintain the order of the cotton market, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of all parties involved in cotton trading.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　棉花经营者(含棉花收购者、加工者、销售者、承储者，下同)从事棉花经营活动，棉花质量监督机构对棉花质量实施监督管理，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Cotton business operators (including cotton purchasers, processors, sellers, and storers, the same below) shall comply with these Regulations when engaging in cotton business activities, and cotton quality supervision organizations shall implement supervision and administration over cotton quality.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　棉花经营者应当建立、健全棉花质量内部管理制度，严格实施岗位质量规范、质量责任及相应的考核办法。

**Article 3** Cotton business operators shall establish and improve internal management systems for cotton quality, and strictly implement post quality specifications, quality responsibilities, and corresponding assessment methods.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院质量监督检验检疫部门主管全国棉花质量监督工作，由其所属的中国纤维检验机构负责组织实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府质量监督部门负责本行政区域内棉花质量监督工作。设有专业纤维检验机构的地方，由专业纤维检验机构在其管辖范围内对棉花质量实施监督；没有设立专业纤维检验机构的地方，由质量监督部门在其管辖范围内对棉花质量实施监督(专业纤维检验机构和地方质量监督部门并列使用时，统称棉花质量监督机构)。

**Article 4** The quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department under the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision over cotton quality nationwide, and the China Fiber Inspection Institution under it shall be responsible for organized implementation.  
The quality supervision departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for cotton quality supervision within their respective administrative regions. In places where professional fiber inspection institutions are established, such institutions shall supervise cotton quality within their jurisdiction; in places where no professional fiber inspection institution is established, the quality supervision departments shall supervise cotton quality within their jurisdiction (when professional fiber inspection institutions and local quality supervision departments are used together, they are collectively referred to as cotton quality supervision organizations).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民政府及其工作人员不得包庇、纵容本地区的棉花质量违法行为，或者阻挠、干预棉花质量监督机构依法对棉花收购、加工、销售、承储中违反本条例规定的行为进行查处。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at all levels and their staff members shall not shield or connive at illegal acts concerning cotton quality in their regions, or obstruct or interfere with cotton quality supervision organizations in investigating and dealing with acts violating the provisions of these Regulations in cotton purchasing, processing, selling, and storing according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人对棉花质量违法行为，均有权检举。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any illegal act concerning cotton quality.

### 第二章　棉花质量义务

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　棉花经营者收购棉花，应当建立、健全棉花收购质量检查验收制度，具备品级实物标准和棉花质量检验所必备的设备、工具。  
棉花经营者收购棉花时，应当按照国家标准和技术规范，排除异性纤维和其他有害物质后确定所收购棉花的类别、等级、数量；所收购的棉花超出国家规定水分标准的，应当进行晾晒、烘干等技术处理，保证棉花质量。  
棉花经营者应当分类别、分等级置放所收购的棉花。

**Article 7** When purchasing cotton, cotton business operators shall establish and improve a quality inspection and acceptance system for cotton purchase, and be equipped with grade physical standards and necessary equipment and tools for cotton quality inspection.  
When purchasing cotton, cotton business operators shall, in accordance with national standards and technical specifications, determine the category, grade, and quantity of the cotton purchased after removing foreign fibers and other harmful substances; if the moisture content of the purchased cotton exceeds the national standard, they shall carry out technical treatments such as drying and baking to ensure cotton quality.  
Cotton business operators shall place the purchased cotton by category and grade.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　棉花经营者加工棉花，必须符合下列要求：  
(一)按照国家标准，对所加工棉花中的异性纤维和其他有害物质进行分拣，并予以排除；  
(二)按照国家标准，对棉花分等级加工，并对加工后的棉花进行包装并标注标识，标识应当与棉花质量相符；  
(三)按照国家标准，将加工后的棉花成包组批放置。  
棉花经营者不得使用国家明令禁止的皮辊机、轧花机、打包机以及其他棉花加工设备加工棉花。

**Article 8** When processing cotton, cotton business operators must meet the following requirements:  
(1) sort and remove foreign fibers and other harmful substances in the processed cotton in accordance with national standards;  
(2) process cotton by grade in accordance with national standards, package the processed cotton and affix marks, which shall be consistent with the cotton quality;  
(3) place the processed cotton into bales and lots in accordance with national standards.  
Cotton business operators shall not use roller gins, ginning machines, baling machines, or other cotton processing equipment explicitly prohibited by the State to process cotton.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　棉花经营者销售棉花，必须符合下列要求：  
(一)每批棉花附有质量凭证；  
(二)棉花包装、标识符合国家标准；  
(三)棉花类别、等级、重量与质量凭证、标识相符；  
(四)经公证检验的棉花，附有公证检验证书，其中国家储备棉还应当粘贴公证检验标志。

**Article 9** When selling cotton, cotton business operators must meet the following requirements:  
(1) each batch of cotton shall be accompanied by a quality certificate;  
(2) the cotton packaging and marks comply with national standards;  
(3) the category, grade, and weight of the cotton are consistent with the quality certificate and marks;  
(4) cotton that has undergone notarized inspection shall be accompanied by a notarized inspection certificate, and the national reserve cotton therein shall also have a notarized inspection mark affixed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　棉花经营者承储国家储备棉，应当建立、健全棉花入库、出库质量检查验收制度，保证入库、出库的国家储备棉的类别、等级、数量与公证检验证书、公证检验标志相符。  
棉花经营者承储国家储备棉，应当按照国家规定维护、保养承储设施，保证国家储备棉质量免受人为因素造成的质量变异。  
棉花经营者不得将未经棉花质量公证检验的棉花作为国家储备棉入库、出库。  
政府机关及其工作人员，不得强令棉花经营者将未经棉花质量公证检验的棉花作为国家储备棉入库、出库。

**Article 10** When storing national reserve cotton, cotton business operators shall establish and improve quality inspection and acceptance systems for cotton warehousing and outbound, and ensure that the category, grade, and quantity of the national reserve cotton warehoused and outbound are consistent with the notarized inspection certificate and notarized inspection mark.  
When storing national reserve cotton, cotton business operators shall maintain and upkeep warehousing facilities in accordance with national regulations to ensure that the quality of national reserve cotton is not subject to quality variations caused by human factors.  
Cotton business operators shall not warehouse or outbound cotton that has not undergone cotton quality notarized inspection as national reserve cotton.  
Government agencies and their staff members shall not compel cotton business operators to warehouse or outbound cotton that has not undergone cotton quality notarized inspection as national reserve cotton.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　棉花经营者收购、加工、销售、承储棉花，不得伪造、变造、冒用棉花质量凭证、标识、公证检验证书、公证检验标志。

**Article 11** When purchasing, processing, selling, and storing cotton, cotton business operators shall not forge, alter, or fraudulently use cotton quality certificates, marks, notarized inspection certificates, or notarized inspection marks.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　严禁棉花经营者在收购、加工、销售、承储等棉花经营活动中掺杂掺假、以次充好、以假充真。

**Article 12** Cotton business operators are strictly prohibited from adulterating, passing off inferior products as good ones, or passing off fake products as genuine ones in cotton business activities such as purchasing, processing, selling, and storing.

### 第三章　棉花质量监督

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家实行棉花质量公证检验制度。  
前款所称棉花质量公证检验，是指专业纤维检验机构按照国家标准和技术规范，对棉花的质量、数量进行检验并出具公证检验证书的活动。

**Article 13** The State implements a cotton quality notarized inspection system.  
The term "cotton quality notarized inspection" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to the activity in which a professional fiber inspection institution inspects the quality and quantity of cotton and issues a notarized inspection certificate in accordance with national standards and technical specifications.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　棉花经营者向用棉企业销售棉花，交易任何一方在棉花交易结算前，可以委托专业纤维检验机构对所交易的棉花进行公证检验；经公证检验后，由专业纤维检验机构出具棉花质量公证检验证书，作为棉花质量、数量的依据。

**Article 14** When a cotton business operator sells cotton to a cotton-using enterprise, either party may, before settlement of the cotton transaction, entrust a professional fiber inspection institution to conduct notarized inspection on the cotton traded; after the notarized inspection, the professional fiber inspection institution shall issue a cotton quality notarized inspection certificate, which shall serve as the basis for the quality and quantity of the cotton.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家储备棉的入库、出库，必须经棉花质量公证检验；经公证检验后，由专业纤维检验机构出具棉花质量公证检验证书，作为国家财政支付存储国家储备棉所需费用的依据。  
经公证检验的国家储备棉，由专业纤维检验机构粘贴中国纤维检验机构统一规定的公证检验标志。

**Article 15** The warehousing and outbound of national reserve cotton must undergo cotton quality notarized inspection; after the notarized inspection, the professional fiber inspection institution shall issue a cotton quality notarized inspection certificate, which shall serve as the basis for the State to pay the expenses required for storing the national reserve cotton.  
For the national reserve cotton that has undergone notarized inspection, the professional fiber inspection institution shall affix the notarized inspection mark uniformly prescribed by the China Fiber Inspection Institution.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专业纤维检验机构进行棉花质量公证检验，必须执行国家标准及其检验方法、技术规范和时间要求，保证客观、公正、及时。专业纤维检验机构出具的棉花质量公证检验证书应当真实、客观地反映棉花的质量、数量。  
棉花质量公证检验证书的内容应当包括：产品名称、送检(委托)单位、批号、包数、检验依据、检验结果、检验单位、检验人员等内容。  
棉花质量公证检验证书的格式由国务院质量监督检验检疫部门规定。

**Article 16** When conducting cotton quality notarized inspection, a professional fiber inspection institution must implement national standards and their inspection methods, technical specifications, and time requirements, and ensure objectivity, fairness, and timeliness. The cotton quality notarized inspection certificate issued by the professional fiber inspection institution shall truthfully and objectively reflect the quality and quantity of the cotton.  
The content of the cotton quality notarized inspection certificate shall include: product name, unit submitting for inspection (entrusting unit), batch number, number of bales, inspection basis, inspection results, inspection unit, and inspection personnel.  
The format of the cotton quality notarized inspection certificate shall be prescribed by the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　专业纤维检验机构实施棉花质量公证检验不得收取费用，所需检验费用按照国家有关规定列支。

**Article 17** A professional fiber inspection institution shall not charge fees for carrying out cotton quality notarized inspection, and the necessary inspection expenses shall be listed and paid in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院质量监督检验检疫部门在全国范围内对经棉花质量公证检验的棉花组织实施监督抽验，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府质量监督部门在本行政区域内对经棉花质量公证检验的棉花组织实施监督抽验。  
监督抽验的内容是：棉花质量公证检验证书和公证检验标志是否与实物相符；专业纤维检验机构实施的棉花质量公证检验是否客观、公正、及时。  
监督抽验所需样品从公证检验的留样中随机抽取，并应当自抽取样品之日起10日内作出检验结论。

**Article 18** The quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department under the State Council shall organize and implement supervisory spot inspections on cotton that has undergone cotton quality notarized inspection nationwide, and the quality supervision departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize and implement supervisory spot inspections on cotton that has undergone cotton quality notarized inspection within their respective administrative regions.  
The contents of supervisory spot inspections are: whether the cotton quality notarized inspection certificate and notarized inspection mark are consistent with the physical cotton; whether the cotton quality notarized inspection conducted by the professional fiber inspection institution is objective, fair, and timely.  
Samples required for supervisory spot inspections shall be randomly drawn from the retained samples of the notarized inspection, and the inspection conclusion shall be made within 10 days from the date of drawing the samples.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　棉花质量监督机构对棉花质量公证检验以外的棉花，可以在棉花收购、加工、销售、承储的现场实施监督检查。  
监督检查的内容是：棉花质量、数量和包装是否符合国家标准；棉花标识以及质量凭证是否与实物相符。

**Article 19** For cotton other than that subject to cotton quality notarized inspection, cotton quality supervision organizations may conduct on-site supervision and inspection at the places of cotton purchasing, processing, selling, and storing.  
The contents of supervision and inspection are: whether the cotton quality, quantity, and packaging comply with national standards; whether the cotton marks and quality certificates are consistent with the physical cotton.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　棉花质量监督机构在实施棉花质量监督检查过程中，根据违法嫌疑证据或者举报，对涉嫌违反本条例规定的行为进行查处时，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)对涉嫌从事违反本条例的经营活动的场所实施现场检查；  
(二)向棉花经营单位的有关人员调查、了解与涉嫌从事违反本条例的经营活动有关的情况；  
(三)查阅、复制与棉花经营有关的合同、单据、账簿以及其他资料；  
(四)对涉嫌掺杂掺假、以次充好、以假充真或者其他有严重质量问题的棉花以及专门用于生产掺杂掺假、以次充好、以假充真的棉花的设备、工具予以查封或者扣押。

**Article 20** When conducting cotton quality supervision and inspection, a cotton quality supervision organization may, based on evidence of suspected violations or reports, exercise the following powers when investigating and dealing with acts suspected of violating these Regulations:  
(1) conduct on-site inspection of the premises suspected of engaging in business activities violating these Regulations;  
(2) investigate and learn from the relevant personnel of the cotton business unit about the circumstances related to the suspected business activities violating these Regulations;  
(3) consult and duplicate contracts, documents, account books, and other materials related to cotton business;  
(4) seal up or seize cotton suspected of being adulterated, passing off inferior products as good ones, passing off fake products as genuine ones, or having other serious quality problems, as well as equipment and tools specially used for producing adulterated, inferior, or fake cotton.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　棉花质量监督机构根据监督检查的需要，可以对棉花质量进行检验；检验所需样品按照国家有关标准，从收购、加工、销售、储备的棉花中随机抽取，并应当自抽取检验样品之日起3日内作出检验结论。  
依照前款规定进行的检验不得收取费用，所需检验费用按照国家有关规定列支。

**Article 21** A cotton quality supervision institution may, as required by supervision and inspection, carry out inspection on cotton quality; the samples required for inspection shall be randomly taken from cotton acquired, processed, sold or stored in accordance with the relevant national standards, and the inspection conclusion shall be made within three days from the date of taking the inspection samples.  
No fee shall be charged for the inspection carried out in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, and the necessary inspection expenses shall be disbursed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　棉花经营者、用棉企业对依照本条例进行的棉花质量公证检验和棉花质量监督检查中实施检验的结果有异议的，可以自收到检验结果之日起5日内向省、自治区、直辖市的棉花质量监督机构或者中国纤维检验机构申请复检；省、自治区、直辖市的棉花质量监督机构或者中国纤维检验机构应当自收到申请之日起7日内作出复检结论，并告知申请人。棉花经营者、用棉企业对复检结论仍有异议的，可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 22** Where a cotton business operator or cotton user has any objection to the result of the notarization inspection of cotton quality or the inspection carried out during cotton quality supervision inspection conducted in accordance with these Regulations, it may, within five days from the date of receiving the inspection result, apply for a re-inspection to the cotton quality supervision institution of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government or the China Fiber Inspection Institution; the said institution shall make a re-inspection conclusion within seven days from the date of receiving the application and notify the applicant thereof. Where the cotton business operator or cotton user still has any objection to the re-inspection conclusion, it may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经国务院质量监督检验检疫部门认可的其他纤维检验机构，可以受委托从事棉花质量检验业务。具体办法由国务院质量监督检验检疫部门会同国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 23** Other fiber inspection institutions recognized by the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council may, upon entrustment, engage in cotton quality inspection business. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　棉花经营者收购棉花，违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)第二款、第三款的规定，不按照国家标准和技术规范排除异性纤维和其他有害物质后确定所收购棉花的类别、等级、数量，或者对所收购的超出国家规定水分标准的棉花不进行技术处理，或者对所收购的棉花不分类别、等级置放的，由棉花质量监督机构责令改正，可以处3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 24** Where a cotton business operator, when acquiring cotton, violates the provisions of the second and third paragraphs of Article 7 of these Regulations by failing to determine the type, grade and quantity of the acquired cotton after removing foreign fibers and other harmful substances in accordance with the national standards and technical specifications, or failing to technically treat the acquired cotton that exceeds the national standard for moisture content, or failing to place the acquired cotton by type and grade, the cotton quality supervision institution shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　棉花经营者加工棉花，违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一款的规定，不按照国家标准分拣、排除异性纤维和其他有害物质，不按照国家标准对棉花分等级加工、进行包装并标注标识，或者不按照国家标准成包组批放置的，由棉花质量监督机构责令改正，并可以根据情节轻重，处10万元以下的罚款。  
棉花经营者加工棉花，违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第二款的规定，使用国家明令禁止的棉花加工设备的，由棉花质量监督机构没收并监督销毁禁止的棉花加工设备，并处非法设备实际价值2倍以上10倍以下的罚款。

**Article 25** Where a cotton business operator, when processing cotton, violates the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 8 of these Regulations by failing to sort and remove foreign fibers and other harmful substances in accordance with the national standards, or failing to process, package and mark cotton by grade in accordance with the national standards, or failing to make bales and group batches for placement in accordance with the national standards, the cotton quality supervision institution shall order it to make corrections and may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.  
Where a cotton business operator, when processing cotton, violates the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 8 of these Regulations by using cotton processing equipment explicitly prohibited by the State, the cotton quality supervision institution shall confiscate and supervise the destruction of the prohibited cotton processing equipment, and shall concurrently impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the actual value of the illegal equipment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　棉花经营者销售棉花，违反[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定，销售的棉花没有质量凭证，或者其包装、标识不符合国家标准，或者质量凭证、标识与实物不符，或者经公证检验的棉花没有公证检验证书、国家储备棉没有粘贴公证检验标志的，由棉花质量监督机构责令改正，并可以根据情节轻重，处10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Where a cotton business operator, when selling cotton, violates the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations by selling cotton without a quality certificate, or having packaging or markings that do not conform to the national standards, or having quality certificates or markings that do not correspond to the actual goods, or selling cotton that has undergone notarization inspection without a notarization inspection certificate, or having national reserve cotton without a notarization inspection label affixed, the cotton quality supervision institution shall order it to make corrections and may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　棉花经营者承储国家储备棉，违反[本条例第十条](#art-10)第一款、第二款、第三款的规定，未建立棉花入库、出库质量检查验收制度，或者入库、出库的国家储备棉实物与公证检验证书、标志不符，或者不按照国家规定维护、保养承储设施致使国家储备棉质量变异，或者将未经公证检验的棉花作为国家储备棉入库、出库的，由棉花质量监督机构责令改正，可以处10万元以下的罚款；造成重大损失的，对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级以上的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Where a cotton business operator storing national reserve cotton violates the provisions of the first, second and third paragraphs of Article 10 of these Regulations by failing to establish a system for quality inspection and acceptance of cotton entering and leaving the warehouse, or having the actual national reserve cotton entering or leaving the warehouse inconsistent with the notarization inspection certificate or mark, or failing to maintain and service the storage facilities in accordance with the national provisions, thereby causing deterioration of the quality of the national reserve cotton, or storing or releasing cotton that has not undergone notarization inspection as national reserve cotton, the cotton quality supervision institution shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; where major losses are caused, disciplinary sanctions of demotion or above shall be imposed on the person in charge and other persons directly responsible; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　棉花经营者隐匿、转移、损毁被棉花质量监督机构查封、扣押的物品的，由棉花质量监督机构处被隐匿、转移、损毁物品货值金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where a cotton business operator conceals, transfers or damages the articles sealed up or seized by the cotton quality supervision institution, the said institution shall impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the articles concealed, transferred or damaged; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　棉花经营者违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定，伪造、变造、冒用棉花质量凭证、标识、公证检验证书、公证检验标志的，由棉花质量监督机构处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，移送工商行政管理机关吊销营业执照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Where a cotton business operator violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations by forging, altering or fraudulently using cotton quality certificates, markings, notarization inspection certificates or notarization inspection labels, the cotton quality supervision institution shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be transferred to the administrative authority for industry and commerce for revocation of its business license; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　棉花经营者违反[本条例第十二条](#art-12)的规定，在棉花经营活动中掺杂掺假、以次充好、以假充真，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由棉花质量监督机构没收掺杂掺假、以次充好、以假充真的棉花和违法所得，处违法货值金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款，并移送工商行政管理机关依法吊销营业执照。

**Article 30** Where a cotton business operator violates the provisions of Article 12 of these Regulations by adulterating, passing off inferior cotton as superior, or passing off counterfeit cotton as genuine in cotton business activities, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the cotton quality supervision institution shall confiscate the adulterated, inferior or counterfeit cotton and the illegal income, impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the illegal goods, and transfer the case to the administrative authority for industry and commerce for revocation of its business license according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　专业纤维检验机构违反[本条例第十六条](#art-16)的规定，不执行国家标准及其检验方法、技术规范或者时间要求，或者出具的棉花质量公证检验证书不真实、不客观的，由国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者地方质量监督部门责令改正；对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 31** Where a specialized fiber inspection institution violates the provisions of Article 16 of these Regulations by failing to implement the national standards and their inspection methods, technical specifications or time requirements, or issuing a cotton quality notarization inspection certificate that is not truthful or objective, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council or the local quality supervision department shall order it to make corrections; and shall impose disciplinary sanctions of demotion or removal from office on the person in charge and other persons directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　专业纤维检验机构违反[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定收取公证检验费用的，由国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者地方质量监督部门责令退回所收取的公证检验费用；对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予记大过或者降级的行政处分。

**Article 32** Where a specialized fiber inspection institution collects notarization inspection fees in violation of the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council or the local quality supervision department shall order it to refund the collected notarization inspection fees; and shall impose disciplinary sanctions of a serious demerit or demotion on the person in charge and other persons directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　专业纤维检验机构未实施公证检验而编造、出具公证检验证书或者粘贴公证检验标志，弄虚作假的，由国务院质量监督检验检疫部门或者地方质量监督部门对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where a specialized fiber inspection institution, without having carried out notarization inspection, fabricates and issues a notarization inspection certificate or affixes a notarization inspection label, thereby engaging in fraud, the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council or the local quality supervision department shall impose disciplinary sanctions of demotion or removal from office on the person in charge and other persons directly responsible according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　政府机关及其工作人员违反[本条例第十条](#art-10)第四款的规定，强令将未经公证检验的棉花作为国家储备棉入库、出库的，对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 34** Where a government organ or its staff member violates the provisions of the fourth paragraph of Article 10 of these Regulations by ordering that cotton which has not undergone notarization inspection be stored or released as national reserve cotton, disciplinary sanctions of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed on the person in charge and other persons directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　政府机关及其工作人员包庇、纵容本地区的棉花质量违法行为，或者阻挠、干预棉花质量监督机构依法对违反本条例的行为进行查处的，依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where a government organ or its staff member harbors or connives at illegal cotton quality activities in its region, or obstructs or interferes with the cotton quality supervision institution in investigating and dealing with acts violating these Regulations according to law, disciplinary sanctions of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)、第三十条规定的棉花货值金额按照违法收购、加工、销售的棉花的牌价或者结算票据计算；没有牌价或者结算票据的，按照同类棉花市场价格计算。

**Article 36** The value of cotton goods as mentioned in Articles 28 and 30 of these Regulations shall be calculated on the basis of the listed price or the settlement receipt of the cotton illegally acquired, processed or sold; where there is no listed price or settlement receipt, it shall be calculated on the basis of the market price of cotton of the same kind.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离，收缴的罚款必须全部上缴国库。

**Article 37** The administrative penalty of a fine imposed in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations, with the separation of the fine decision and the fine collection, and all fines collected shall be turned over to the State treasury in full.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　毛、绒、茧丝、麻类纤维的质量监督管理，比照本条例执行。

**Article 38** The quality supervision and administration of hair, down, silk cocoon and bast fibers shall be implemented by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
