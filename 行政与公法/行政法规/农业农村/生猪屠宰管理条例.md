# 生猪屠宰管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-06-25  
**生效日期**：2021-08-01

> （1997年12月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第238号公布　2008年5月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第525号第一次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　2021年6月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第742号第四次修订） 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强生猪屠宰管理，保证生猪产品质量安全，保障人民身体健康，制定本条例。 第二条　国家实行生猪定点屠宰、集中检疫制度。 除农村地区个人自宰自食的不实行定点屠宰外，任何单位和个人未经定点不得从事生猪屠宰活动。 在边远和交通不便的农村地区，可以设置仅限于向本地市场供应生猪产品的小型生猪屠宰场点，具体管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。 第三条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国生猪屠宰的行业管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域内生猪屠宰活动的监督管理。 县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内负责生猪屠宰活动的相关管理工作。 第四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对生猪屠宰监督管理工作的领导，及时协调、解决生猪屠宰监督管理工作中的重大问题。 乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当加强生猪定点屠宰的宣传教育，协助做好生猪屠宰监督管理工作。 第五条　国家鼓励生猪养殖、屠宰、加工、配送、销售一体化发展，推行标准化屠宰，支持建设冷链流通和配送体系。 第六条　国家根据生猪定点屠宰厂（场）的规模、生产和技术条件以及质量安全管理状况，推行生猪定点屠宰厂（场）分级管理制度，鼓励、引导、扶持生猪定点屠宰厂（场）改善生产和技术条件，加强质量安全管理，提高生猪产品质量安全水平。生猪定点屠宰厂（场）分级管理的具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。 第七条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当建立生猪定点屠宰厂（场）信用档案，记录日常监督检查结果、违法行为查处等情况，并依法向社会公示。 第二章　生猪定点屠宰 第八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门会同生态环境主管部门以及其他有关部门，按照科学布局、集中屠宰、有利流通、方便群众的原则，结合生猪养殖、动物疫病防控和生猪产品消费实际情况制订生猪屠宰行业发展规划，报本级人民政府批准后实施。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强生猪屠宰管理，保证生猪产品质量安全，保障人民身体健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of pig slaughtering, ensure the quality and safety of pig products, and protect the health of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行生猪定点屠宰、集中检疫制度。  
除农村地区个人自宰自食的不实行定点屠宰外，任何单位和个人未经定点不得从事生猪屠宰活动。  
在边远和交通不便的农村地区，可以设置仅限于向本地市场供应生猪产品的小型生猪屠宰场点，具体管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a system of designated pig slaughtering and centralized quarantine.  
Except for personal slaughtering for self-consumption in rural areas where designated slaughtering is not required, no unit or individual may engage in pig slaughtering activities without a designated site.  
In remote rural areas with inconvenient transportation, small-scale pig slaughtering sites that are limited to supplying pig products to the local market may be established. The specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国生猪屠宰的行业管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责本行政区域内生猪屠宰活动的监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内负责生猪屠宰活动的相关管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of the pig slaughtering industry nationwide. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pig slaughtering activities within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the relevant administrative work of pig slaughtering activities within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对生猪屠宰监督管理工作的领导，及时协调、解决生猪屠宰监督管理工作中的重大问题。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当加强生猪定点屠宰的宣传教育，协助做好生猪屠宰监督管理工作。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over the supervision and administration of pig slaughtering, and shall promptly coordinate and resolve major issues in the supervision and administration of pig slaughtering.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall strengthen publicity and education on designated pig slaughtering, and assist in the supervision and administration of pig slaughtering.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励生猪养殖、屠宰、加工、配送、销售一体化发展，推行标准化屠宰，支持建设冷链流通和配送体系。

**Article 5** The State shall encourage the integrated development of pig breeding, slaughtering, processing, distribution, and sales, promote standardized slaughtering, and support the construction of cold chain circulation and distribution systems.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家根据生猪定点屠宰厂（场）的规模、生产和技术条件以及质量安全管理状况，推行生猪定点屠宰厂（场）分级管理制度，鼓励、引导、扶持生猪定点屠宰厂（场）改善生产和技术条件，加强质量安全管理，提高生猪产品质量安全水平。生猪定点屠宰厂（场）分级管理的具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 6** The State shall, based on the scale, production and technical conditions, and quality and safety management status of designated pig slaughterhouses (plants), implement a graded management system for designated pig slaughterhouses (plants), and shall encourage, guide, and support designated pig slaughterhouses (plants) to improve their production and technical conditions, strengthen quality and safety management, and raise the quality and safety level of pig products. The specific measures for graded management of designated pig slaughterhouses (plants) shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当建立生猪定点屠宰厂（场）信用档案，记录日常监督检查结果、违法行为查处等情况，并依法向社会公示。

**Article 7** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish credit files for designated pig slaughterhouses (plants), record the results of routine supervision and inspection, investigations and punishments of illegal acts, and other matters, and shall publicize such information to the public according to law.

### 第二章　生猪定点屠宰

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门会同生态环境主管部门以及其他有关部门，按照科学布局、集中屠宰、有利流通、方便群众的原则，结合生猪养殖、动物疫病防控和生猪产品消费实际情况制订生猪屠宰行业发展规划，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
生猪屠宰行业发展规划应当包括发展目标、屠宰厂（场）设置、政策措施等内容。

**Article 8** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with the competent department of ecological environment and other relevant departments, formulate a development plan for the pig slaughtering industry in accordance with the principles of scientific layout, centralized slaughtering, facilitation of circulation, and convenience for the people, taking into account the actual conditions of pig breeding, animal disease prevention and control, and consumption of pig products, and shall submit the plan to the people's government at the same level for approval before implementation.  
The development plan for the pig slaughtering industry shall include development objectives, the establishment of slaughterhouses (plants), policy measures, and other contents.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）由设区的市级人民政府根据生猪屠宰行业发展规划，组织农业农村、生态环境主管部门以及其他有关部门，依照本条例规定的条件进行审查，经征求省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门的意见确定，并颁发生猪定点屠宰证书和生猪定点屠宰标志牌。  
生猪定点屠宰证书应当载明屠宰厂（场）名称、生产地址和法定代表人（负责人）等事项。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）变更生产地址的，应当依照本条例的规定，重新申请生猪定点屠宰证书；变更屠宰厂（场）名称、法定代表人（负责人）的，应当在市场监督管理部门办理变更登记手续后15个工作日内，向原发证机关办理变更生猪定点屠宰证书。  
设区的市级人民政府应当将其确定的生猪定点屠宰厂（场）名单及时向社会公布，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。

**Article 9** A designated pig slaughterhouse (plant) shall be determined by the people's government of a city divided into districts, in accordance with the development plan for the pig slaughtering industry. The said government shall organize the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, ecological environment, and other relevant departments to conduct a review in accordance with the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, and shall seek the opinions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and thereafter issue the designated pig slaughtering certificate and the designated pig slaughtering signboard.  
The designated pig slaughtering certificate shall state the name of the slaughterhouse (plant), its production address, and the legal representative (person in charge), among other matters.  
If a designated pig slaughterhouse (plant) changes its production address, it shall re-apply for the designated pig slaughtering certificate in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations. If it changes its name or legal representative (person in charge), it shall, within 15 working days after completing the change registration formalities with the market supervision and administration department, apply to the original issuing authority for a change of the designated pig slaughtering certificate.  
The people's government of a city divided into districts shall promptly make public the list of designated pig slaughterhouses (plants) it has determined, and shall report it to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当将生猪定点屠宰标志牌悬挂于厂（场）区的显著位置。  
生猪定点屠宰证书和生猪定点屠宰标志牌不得出借、转让。任何单位和个人不得冒用或者使用伪造的生猪定点屠宰证书和生猪定点屠宰标志牌。

**Article 10** A designated pig slaughterhouse (plant) shall hang the designated pig slaughtering signboard at a conspicuous place in its premises.  
The designated pig slaughtering certificate and the designated pig slaughtering signboard shall not be lent or transferred. No unit or individual may falsely use or use forged designated pig slaughtering certificates and designated pig slaughtering signboards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与屠宰规模相适应、水质符合国家规定标准的水源条件；  
（二）有符合国家规定要求的待宰间、屠宰间、急宰间、检验室以及生猪屠宰设备和运载工具；  
（三）有依法取得健康证明的屠宰技术人员；  
（四）有经考核合格的兽医卫生检验人员；  
（五）有符合国家规定要求的检验设备、消毒设施以及符合环境保护要求的污染防治设施；  
（六）有病害生猪及生猪产品无害化处理设施或者无害化处理委托协议；  
（七）依法取得动物防疫条件合格证。

**Article 11** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) have water source conditions that are commensurate with the slaughter scale and whose water quality meets the standards prescribed by the state;  
(2) have ante-mortem rooms, slaughter rooms, emergency slaughter rooms, inspection rooms, as well as pig slaughter equipment and transport vehicles that meet the requirements prescribed by the state;  
(3) have slaughter technical personnel who have obtained health certificates according to law;  
(4) have veterinary health inspection personnel who have passed the assessment;  
(5) have inspection equipment and disinfection facilities that meet the requirements prescribed by the state, and pollution prevention and control facilities that meet the requirements for environmental protection;  
(6) have facilities for harmless treatment of diseased pigs and pig products, or an agreement for entrusting harmless treatment;  
(7) have obtained the Animal Epidemic Prevention Conditions Certificate according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）屠宰的生猪，应当依法经动物卫生监督机构检疫合格，并附有检疫证明。

**Article 12** Pigs slaughtered by a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall be subject to quarantine by an animal health supervision institution according to law and be accompanied by a quarantine certificate.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当建立生猪进厂（场）查验登记制度。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当依法查验检疫证明等文件，利用信息化手段核实相关信息，如实记录屠宰生猪的来源、数量、检疫证明号和供货者名称、地址、联系方式等内容，并保存相关凭证。发现伪造、变造检疫证明的，应当及时报告农业农村主管部门。发生动物疫情时，还应当查验、记录运输车辆基本情况。记录、凭证保存期限不得少于2年。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）接受委托屠宰的，应当与委托人签订委托屠宰协议，明确生猪产品质量安全责任。委托屠宰协议自协议期满后保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 13** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall establish a system for inspection and registration of pigs entering the factory (site).  
A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall inspect documents such as quarantine certificates according to law, verify the relevant information by means of informatization, truthfully record the source, quantity, quarantine certificate number of the slaughtered pigs, and the name, address, and contact information of the supplier, and retain the relevant vouchers. If a forged or altered quarantine certificate is discovered, it shall promptly report to the competent agricultural and rural department. When an animal epidemic occurs, it shall also inspect and record the basic information of the transport vehicle. The retention period for records and vouchers shall not be less than 2 years.  
If a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) accepts an entrustment for slaughter, it shall enter into an entrustment slaughter agreement with the principal, specifying the responsibility for the quality and safety of pig products. The retention period for the entrustment slaughter agreement after its expiration shall not be less than 2 years.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）屠宰生猪，应当遵守国家规定的操作规程、技术要求和生猪屠宰质量管理规范，并严格执行消毒技术规范。发生动物疫情时，应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定，开展动物疫病检测，做好动物疫情排查和报告。

**Article 14** When slaughtering pigs, a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall comply with the operating procedures, technical requirements, and quality management norms for pig slaughter prescribed by the state, and shall strictly implement the disinfection technical specifications. When an animal epidemic occurs, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural and rural department of the State Council, carry out animal disease testing, and conduct animal epidemic investigation and reporting.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当建立严格的肉品品质检验管理制度。肉品品质检验应当遵守生猪屠宰肉品品质检验规程，与生猪屠宰同步进行，并如实记录检验结果。检验结果记录保存期限不得少于2年。  
经肉品品质检验合格的生猪产品，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当加盖肉品品质检验合格验讫印章，附具肉品品质检验合格证。未经肉品品质检验或者经肉品品质检验不合格的生猪产品，不得出厂（场）。经检验不合格的生猪产品，应当在兽医卫生检验人员的监督下，按照国家有关规定处理，并如实记录处理情况；处理情况记录保存期限不得少于2年。  
生猪屠宰肉品品质检验规程由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 15** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall establish a strict meat quality inspection management system. The meat quality inspection shall comply with the meat quality inspection procedures for pig slaughter, be conducted synchronously with pig slaughter, and the inspection results shall be truthfully recorded. The retention period for records of inspection results shall not be less than 2 years.  
For pig products that pass the meat quality inspection, the designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall affix a meat quality inspection qualified stamp and attach a meat quality inspection certificate. Pig products that have not undergone meat quality inspection or have failed meat quality inspection shall not leave the factory (site). Pig products that fail the inspection shall, under the supervision of veterinary health inspection personnel, be disposed of in accordance with relevant state provisions, and the disposal situation shall be truthfully recorded; the retention period for records of the disposal situation shall not be less than 2 years.  
The meat quality inspection procedures for pig slaughter shall be formulated by the competent agricultural and rural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生猪屠宰的检疫及其监督，依照动物防疫法和国务院的有关规定执行。县级以上地方人民政府按照本级政府职责，将生猪、生猪产品的检疫和监督管理所需经费纳入本级预算。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当按照规定足额配备农业农村主管部门任命的兽医，由其监督生猪定点屠宰厂（场）依法查验检疫证明等文件。  
农业农村主管部门任命的兽医对屠宰的生猪实施检疫。检疫合格的，出具检疫证明、加施检疫标志，并在检疫证明、检疫标志上签字或者盖章，对检疫结论负责。未经检疫或者经检疫不合格的生猪产品，不得出厂（场）。经检疫不合格的生猪及生猪产品，应当在农业农村主管部门的监督下，按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 16** The quarantine of pig slaughter and its supervision shall be carried out in accordance with the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council. A local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its duties according to the level of government, incorporate the funds needed for quarantine and supervision management of pigs and pig products into its own budget.  
The competent agricultural and rural department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to the provisions, fully staff veterinarians appointed by the competent agricultural and rural department, and they shall supervise the designated pig slaughterhouse (site) in inspecting documents such as quarantine certificates according to law.  
The veterinarians appointed by the competent agricultural and rural department shall carry out quarantine on the pigs being slaughtered. If the quarantine is qualified, they shall issue a quarantine certificate, affix a quarantine mark, and sign or seal on the quarantine certificate and quarantine mark, and be responsible for the quarantine conclusion. Pig products that have not undergone quarantine or have failed quarantine shall not leave the factory (site). Pigs and pig products that fail quarantine shall, under the supervision of the competent agricultural and rural department, be disposed of in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当建立生猪产品出厂（场）记录制度，如实记录出厂（场）生猪产品的名称、规格、数量、检疫证明号、肉品品质检验合格证号、屠宰日期、出厂（场）日期以及购货者名称、地址、联系方式等内容，并保存相关凭证。记录、凭证保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 17** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall establish a record system for pig products leaving the factory (site), truthfully record the name, specification, quantity, quarantine certificate number, meat quality inspection certificate number, slaughter date, date of leaving the factory (site), and the name, address, and contact information of the purchaser, and retain the relevant vouchers. The retention period for records and vouchers shall not be less than 2 years.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）对其生产的生猪产品质量安全负责，发现其生产的生猪产品不符合食品安全标准、有证据证明可能危害人体健康、染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当立即停止屠宰，报告农业农村主管部门，通知销售者或者委托人，召回已经销售的生猪产品，并记录通知和召回情况。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）应当对召回的生猪产品采取无害化处理等措施，防止其再次流入市场。

**Article 18** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the pig products it produces. If it discovers that the pig products it produces do not meet food safety standards, there is evidence that they may harm human health, they are infected or suspected of being infected with epidemic disease, it shall immediately stop slaughter, report to the competent agricultural and rural department, notify the seller or the client, recall the pig products that have already been sold, and record the notification and recall situation.  
The designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall take measures such as harmless treatment on the recalled pig products to prevent them from re-entering the market.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）对病害生猪及生猪产品进行无害化处理的费用和损失，由地方各级人民政府结合本地实际予以适当补贴。

**Article 19** The expenses and losses incurred by a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) for carrying out harmless treatment on diseased pigs and pig products shall be appropriately subsidized by local people's governments at all levels in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　严禁生猪定点屠宰厂（场）以及其他任何单位和个人对生猪、生猪产品注水或者注入其他物质。  
严禁生猪定点屠宰厂（场）屠宰注水或者注入其他物质的生猪。

**Article 20** It is strictly prohibited for a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) and any other unit or individual to inject water or inject other substances into pigs or pig products.  
It is strictly prohibited for a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) to slaughter pigs that have been injected with water or other substances.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）对未能及时出厂（场）的生猪产品，应当采取冷冻或者冷藏等必要措施予以储存。

**Article 21** A designated pig slaughterhouse (site) shall take necessary measures such as freezing or refrigeration to store pig products that cannot be delivered from the site in a timely manner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　严禁任何单位和个人为未经定点违法从事生猪屠宰活动的单位和个人提供生猪屠宰场所或者生猪产品储存设施，严禁为对生猪、生猪产品注水或者注入其他物质的单位和个人提供场所。

**Article 22** No unit or individual may provide premises for pig slaughtering or storage facilities for pig products to any unit or individual that illegally engages in pig slaughtering activities without designation, nor may any unit or individual provide premises to any unit or individual that injects water or other substances into pigs or pig products.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事生猪产品销售、肉食品生产加工的单位和个人以及餐饮服务经营者、集中用餐单位生产经营的生猪产品，必须是生猪定点屠宰厂（场）经检疫和肉品品质检验合格的生猪产品。

**Article 23** The pig products used by units and individuals engaged in the sale of pig products, the production and processing of meat food, as well as by catering service providers and centralized dining units in their production and operation, must be pig products that have passed quarantine and meat quality inspection at a designated pig slaughterhouse (site).

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地方人民政府及其有关部门不得限制外地生猪定点屠宰厂（场）经检疫和肉品品质检验合格的生猪产品进入本地市场。

**Article 24** Local people's governments and their relevant departments shall not restrict the entry of pig products that have passed quarantine and meat quality inspection at designated pig slaughterhouses (sites) from other regions into the local market.

### 第三章　监督管理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家实行生猪屠宰质量安全风险监测制度。国务院农业农村主管部门负责组织制定国家生猪屠宰质量安全风险监测计划，对生猪屠宰环节的风险因素进行监测。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据国家生猪屠宰质量安全风险监测计划，结合本行政区域实际情况，制定本行政区域生猪屠宰质量安全风险监测方案并组织实施，同时报国务院农业农村主管部门备案。

**Article 25** The State shall implement a quality and safety risk monitoring system for pig slaughtering. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the formulation of a national quality and safety risk monitoring plan for pig slaughtering and monitoring risk factors in the pig slaughtering process.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national quality and safety risk monitoring plan for pig slaughtering and in light of the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, formulate and organize the implementation of quality and safety risk monitoring plans for pig slaughtering within their administrative regions, and simultaneously report them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当根据生猪屠宰质量安全风险监测结果和国务院农业农村主管部门的规定，加强对生猪定点屠宰厂（场）质量安全管理状况的监督检查。

**Article 26** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the results of quality and safety risk monitoring for pig slaughtering and the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, strengthen supervision and inspection of the quality and safety management status of designated pig slaughterhouses (sites).

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农业农村主管部门应当依照本条例的规定严格履行职责，加强对生猪屠宰活动的日常监督检查，建立健全随机抽查机制。  
农业农村主管部门依法进行监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生猪屠宰等有关场所实施现场检查；  
（二）向有关单位和个人了解情况；  
（三）查阅、复制有关记录、票据以及其他资料；  
（四）查封与违法生猪屠宰活动有关的场所、设施，扣押与违法生猪屠宰活动有关的生猪、生猪产品以及屠宰工具和设备。  
农业农村主管部门进行监督检查时，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示执法证件。  
对农业农村主管部门依法进行的监督检查，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 27** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs shall strictly perform their duties in accordance with these Regulations, strengthen daily supervision and inspection of pig slaughtering activities, and establish and improve a random inspection mechanism.  
When conducting supervision and inspection according to law, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs may take the following measures:  
(1) Entering sites such as pig slaughtering premises to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Inquiring about the situation from relevant units and individuals;  
(3) Reviewing and copying relevant records, invoices, and other materials;  
(4) Sealing up premises and facilities related to illegal pig slaughtering activities, and seizing pigs, pig products, slaughtering tools, and equipment related to illegal pig slaughtering activities.  
When the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs conduct supervision and inspection, there shall be no fewer than two inspectors, and they shall produce their law enforcement credentials.  
Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted according to law by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and shall not refuse or obstruct such inspection.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　农业农村主管部门应当建立举报制度，公布举报电话、信箱或者电子邮箱，受理对违反本条例规定行为的举报，并及时依法处理。

**Article 28** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs shall establish a reporting system, publish a reporting telephone number, mailbox, or email address, accept reports on acts violating these Regulations, and handle them in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农业农村主管部门发现生猪屠宰涉嫌犯罪的，应当按照有关规定及时将案件移送同级公安机关。  
公安机关在生猪屠宰相关犯罪案件侦查过程中认为没有犯罪事实或者犯罪事实显著轻微，不需要追究刑事责任的，应当及时将案件移送同级农业农村主管部门。公安机关在侦查过程中，需要农业农村主管部门给予检验、认定等协助的，农业农村主管部门应当给予协助。

**Article 29** Where the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs discovers that a pig slaughtering act is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall timely transfer the case to the public security organ at the same level in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Where the public security organ, during the investigation of a criminal case related to pig slaughtering, considers that there is no criminal fact or that the criminal fact is obviously minor and does not require the investigation of criminal liability, it shall timely transfer the case to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at the same level. Where the public security organ needs assistance from the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs in inspection, determination, or other matters during the investigation, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs shall provide such assistance.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农业农村主管部门在监督检查中发现生猪定点屠宰厂（场）不再具备本条例规定条件的，应当责令停业整顿，并限期整改；逾期仍达不到本条例规定条件的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌。

**Article 30** Where the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs discovers during supervision and inspection that a designated pig slaughterhouse (site) no longer meets the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, it shall order the suspension of business for rectification and set a time limit for correction; if the conditions prescribed in these Regulations are still not met upon expiration of the time limit, the people's government of a city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and recover the designated pig slaughtering signboard.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，未经定点从事生猪屠宰活动的，由农业农村主管部门责令关闭，没收生猪、生猪产品、屠宰工具和设备以及违法所得；货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款。  
冒用或者使用伪造的生猪定点屠宰证书或者生猪定点屠宰标志牌的，依照前款的规定处罚。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）出借、转让生猪定点屠宰证书或者生猪定点屠宰标志牌的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌；有违法所得的，由农业农村主管部门没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Any person who, in violation of these Regulations, engages in pig slaughtering activities without being designated, shall be ordered by the competent agricultural and rural department to close down, and the pigs, pig products, slaughtering tools and equipment, and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 Yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 Yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed.  
Any person who fraudulently uses or uses a forged designated pig slaughtering certificate or designated pig slaughtering signboard shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) lends or transfers the designated pig slaughtering certificate or designated pig slaughtering signboard, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard; if there is any illegal income, the competent agricultural and rural department shall confiscate the illegal income and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）有下列情形之一的，由农业农村主管部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌：  
（一）未按照规定建立并遵守生猪进厂（场）查验登记制度、生猪产品出厂（场）记录制度的；  
（二）未按照规定签订、保存委托屠宰协议的；  
（三）屠宰生猪不遵守国家规定的操作规程、技术要求和生猪屠宰质量管理规范以及消毒技术规范的；  
（四）未按照规定建立并遵守肉品品质检验制度的；  
（五）对经肉品品质检验不合格的生猪产品未按照国家有关规定处理并如实记录处理情况的。  
发生动物疫情时，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）未按照规定开展动物疫病检测的，由农业农村主管部门责令停业整顿，并处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌。

**Article 32** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) commits any of the following acts in violation of these Regulations, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to suspend operation for rectification, impose a fine of not less than 5,000 Yuan but not more than 50,000 Yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 Yuan but not more than 50,000 Yuan on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard:  
(1) failing to establish and comply with the system of inspection and registration of pigs entering the plant (site) and the system of recording pig products leaving the plant (site) in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to conclude or preserve the entrusted slaughtering agreement in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) slaughtering pigs without complying with the operating procedures, technical requirements, quality management standards for pig slaughtering, and disinfection technical standards prescribed by the State;  
(4) failing to establish and comply with the meat quality inspection system in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) failing to handle pig products that fail the meat quality inspection in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and to truthfully record the handling situation.  
Where an animal epidemic occurs, and a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) fails to carry out animal disease testing in accordance with the provisions, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to suspend operation for rectification, impose a fine of not less than 5,000 Yuan but not more than 50,000 Yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 Yuan but not more than 50,000 Yuan on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）出厂（场）未经肉品品质检验或者经肉品品质检验不合格的生猪产品的，由农业农村主管部门责令停业整顿，没收生猪产品和违法所得；货值金额不足1万元的，并处10万元以上15万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额15倍以上30倍以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌，并可以由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的规定，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5日以上15日以下拘留。

**Article 33** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) ships out pig products that have not undergone meat quality inspection or have failed the meat quality inspection in violation of these Regulations, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to suspend operation for rectification, and confiscate the pig products and illegal gains; if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 Yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 Yuan but not more than 150,000 Yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 Yuan or more, a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed; a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be imposed on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard, and the public security organ may, in accordance with the provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, impose a detention of not less than 5 days but not more than 15 days on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　生猪定点屠宰厂（场）依照本条例规定应当召回生猪产品而不召回的，由农业农村主管部门责令召回，停止屠宰；拒不召回或者拒不停止屠宰的，责令停业整顿，没收生猪产品和违法所得；货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌。  
委托人拒不执行召回规定的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 34** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) shall recall pig products in accordance with these Regulations but fails to do so, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to recall the products and stop slaughtering; if it refuses to recall or refuses to stop slaughtering, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to suspend operation for rectification, confiscate the pig products and illegal gains; if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 Yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 Yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed; a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be imposed on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard.  
Where the client refuses to comply with the recall provisions, the client shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）、其他单位和个人对生猪、生猪产品注水或者注入其他物质的，由农业农村主管部门没收注水或者注入其他物质的生猪、生猪产品、注水工具和设备以及违法所得；货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；对生猪定点屠宰厂（场）或者其他单位的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。注入其他物质的，还可以由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的规定，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5日以上15日以下拘留。  
生猪定点屠宰厂（场）对生猪、生猪产品注水或者注入其他物质的，除依照前款规定处罚外，还应当由农业农村主管部门责令停业整顿；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌。

**Article 35** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site), other entity, or individual injects water or other substances into pigs or pig products in violation of these Regulations, the competent agricultural and rural department shall confiscate the pigs or pig products that have been injected with water or other substances, the injection tools and equipment, and the illegal gains; if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 Yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 Yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed; a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons of the designated pig slaughtering plant (site) or other entity. If other substances are injected, the public security organ may, in accordance with the provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, impose a detention of not less than 5 days but not more than 15 days on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons.  
Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) injects water or other substances into pigs or pig products, in addition to being punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the competent agricultural and rural department shall also order it to suspend operation for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）屠宰注水或者注入其他物质的生猪的，由农业农村主管部门责令停业整顿，没收注水或者注入其他物质的生猪、生猪产品和违法所得；货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由设区的市级人民政府吊销生猪定点屠宰证书，收回生猪定点屠宰标志牌。

**Article 36** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) slaughters pigs that have been injected with water or other substances in violation of these Regulations, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to suspend operation for rectification, confiscate the pigs that have been injected with water or other substances, the pig products, and the illegal gains; if the value of the goods is less than 10,000 Yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 Yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall be concurrently imposed; a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan shall be imposed on its directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the people's government of the city divided into districts shall revoke the designated pig slaughtering certificate and take back the designated pig slaughtering signboard.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，为未经定点违法从事生猪屠宰活动的单位和个人提供生猪屠宰场所或者生猪产品储存设施，或者为对生猪、生猪产品注水或者注入其他物质的单位和个人提供场所的，由农业农村主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Where any person, in violation of these Regulations, provides a pig slaughtering premises or pig product storage facilities for entities or individuals that illegally engage in pig slaughtering activities without being designated, or provides premises for entities or individuals that inject water or other substances into pigs or pig products, the competent agricultural and rural department shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 Yuan but not more than 100,000 Yuan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，生猪定点屠宰厂（场）被吊销生猪定点屠宰证书的，其法定代表人（负责人）、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员自处罚决定作出之日起5年内不得申请生猪定点屠宰证书或者从事生猪屠宰管理活动；因食品安全犯罪被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的，终身不得从事生猪屠宰管理活动。

**Article 38** Where a designated pig slaughtering plant (site) has its designated pig slaughtering certificate revoked in violation of these Regulations, its legal representative (person in charge), directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons shall not apply for a designated pig slaughtering certificate or engage in pig slaughtering management activities within 5 years from the date of the decision on punishment; if sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty for a food safety crime, they shall be prohibited from engaging in pig slaughtering management activities for life.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　农业农村主管部门和其他有关部门的工作人员在生猪屠宰监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 39** Where a staff member of the competent agricultural and rural department or other relevant department abuses power, derelicts duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the supervision and administration of pig slaughtering, if the act does not constitute a crime, the staff member shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例规定的货值金额按照同类检疫合格及肉品品质检验合格的生猪、生猪产品的市场价格计算。

**Article 40** The value of goods as mentioned in these Regulations shall be calculated based on the market price of the same kind of pigs and pig products that have passed quarantine and meat quality inspection.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Anyone who violates these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定实行定点屠宰的其他动物的屠宰管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市根据本地区的实际情况，参照本条例制定。

**Article 42** The administrative measures for slaughter of other animals subject to designated slaughter as determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the actual conditions of their respective regions, with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例所称生猪产品，是指生猪屠宰后未经加工的胴体、肉、脂、脏器、血液、骨、头、蹄、皮。

**Article 43** For the purposes of these Regulations, "pig products" refer to the carcass, meat, fat, viscera, blood, bones, head, trotters, and skin of pigs after slaughter without further processing.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　生猪定点屠宰证书、生猪定点屠宰标志牌以及肉品品质检验合格验讫印章和肉品品质检验合格证的式样，由国务院农业农村主管部门统一规定。

**Article 44** The formats of the pig designated slaughter certificate, the pig designated slaughter signboard, and the seal for qualified meat quality inspection and the certificate for qualified meat quality inspection shall be uniformly prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2021年8月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2021.
