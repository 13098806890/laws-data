# 粮食流通管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-02-15  
**生效日期**：2021-04-15

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护粮食生产者的积极性，促进粮食生产，维护经营者、消费者的合法权益，保障国家粮食安全，维护粮食流通秩序，根据有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with relevant laws for the purpose of protecting the enthusiasm of grain producers, promoting grain production, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of operators and consumers, ensuring national grain security, and maintaining the order of grain circulation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事粮食的收购、销售、储存、运输、加工、进出口等经营活动（以下统称粮食经营活动），应当遵守本条例。  
前款所称粮食，是指小麦、稻谷、玉米、杂粮及其成品粮。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in business activities such as the purchase, sale, storage, transportation, processing, import and export of grain (hereinafter collectively referred to as grain business activities) within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
The term "grain" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to wheat, rice, corn, miscellaneous grains, and their processed products.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励多种所有制市场主体从事粮食经营活动，促进公平竞争。依法从事的粮食经营活动受国家法律保护。严禁以非法手段阻碍粮食自由流通。  
国有粮食企业应当转变经营机制，提高市场竞争能力，在粮食流通中发挥主渠道作用，带头执行国家粮食政策。

**Article 3** The State encourages market entities under various forms of ownership to engage in grain business activities and promotes fair competition. Grain business activities conducted according to law shall be protected by State laws. It is strictly prohibited to obstruct the free circulation of grain by illegal means.  
State-owned grain enterprises shall transform their operating mechanisms, enhance their market competitiveness, play a leading role in grain circulation, and take the lead in implementing the State's grain policies.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　粮食价格主要由市场供求形成。  
国家加强粮食流通管理，增强对粮食市场的调控能力。

**Article 4** The price of grain shall be primarily formed by market supply and demand.  
The State shall strengthen the management of grain circulation and enhance its capacity to regulate the grain market.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　粮食经营活动应当遵循自愿、公平、诚信的原则，不得损害粮食生产者、消费者的合法权益，不得损害国家利益和社会公共利益，并采取有效措施，防止和减少粮食损失浪费。

**Article 5** Grain business activities shall follow the principles of voluntariness, fairness, and good faith, and shall not harm the legitimate rights and interests of grain producers and consumers, nor harm the national interests and public interests; effective measures shall be taken to prevent and reduce grain loss and waste.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食和储备行政管理部门负责全国粮食的总量平衡、宏观调控和重要粮食品种的结构调整以及粮食流通的中长期规划。国家粮食和储备行政管理部门负责粮食流通的行政管理、行业指导，监督有关粮食流通的法律、法规、政策及各项规章制度的执行。  
国务院市场监督管理、卫生健康等部门在各自的职责范围内负责与粮食流通有关的工作。

**Article 6** The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain and reserve administrative department shall be responsible for the overall balance of grain, macro-control and structural adjustment of important grain varieties, as well as the medium- and long-term planning of grain circulation nationwide. The national grain and reserve administrative department shall be responsible for the administrative management and industry guidance of grain circulation, and shall supervise the implementation of laws, regulations, policies and various rules related to grain circulation.  
The market supervision and administration, health and other departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the work related to grain circulation within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　省、自治区、直辖市应当落实粮食安全党政同责，完善粮食安全省长责任制，承担保障本行政区域粮食安全的主体责任，在国家宏观调控下，负责本行政区域粮食的总量平衡和地方储备粮等的管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门负责本行政区域粮食流通的行政管理、行业指导；县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理、卫生健康等部门在各自的职责范围内负责与粮食流通有关的工作。

**Article 7** Provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall implement the joint responsibility of the Party and government for grain security, improve the responsibility system of provincial governors for grain security, assume the primary responsibility for ensuring grain security in their respective administrative regions, and under the macro-control of the State, be responsible for the overall balance of grain and the management of local grain reserves, etc. in their administrative regions.  
The grain and reserve administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administrative management and industry guidance of grain circulation in their respective administrative regions; the market supervision and administration, health and other departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work related to grain circulation within their respective functions and duties.

### 第二章　粮食经营

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　粮食经营者，是指从事粮食收购、销售、储存、运输、加工、进出口等经营活动的自然人、法人和非法人组织。

**Article 8** "Grain business operator" means a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that engages in business activities such as the purchase, sale, storage, transportation, processing, import and export of grain.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事粮食收购的经营者（以下简称粮食收购者），应当具备与其收购粮食品种、数量相适应的能力。  
从事粮食收购的企业（以下简称粮食收购企业），应当向收购地的县级人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门备案企业名称、地址、负责人以及仓储设施等信息，备案内容发生变化的，应当及时变更备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门应当加强粮食收购管理和服务，规范粮食收购活动。具体管理办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 9** An operator engaged in the purchase of grain (hereinafter referred to as "grain purchaser") shall have the capability commensurate with the varieties and quantities of grain to be purchased.  
An enterprise engaged in the purchase of grain (hereinafter referred to as "grain purchase enterprise") shall file with the grain and reserve administrative department of the people's government at the county level in the place of purchase information such as the enterprise name, address, person in charge and storage facilities; where the filed information changes, it shall update the filing promptly.  
The grain and reserve administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management and services for grain purchase and regulate grain purchase activities. Specific management measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　粮食收购者收购粮食，应当告知售粮者或者在收购场所公示粮食的品种、质量标准和收购价格。

**Article 10** When purchasing grain, a grain purchaser shall inform the seller or publicly display at the purchase site the varieties, quality standards and purchase prices of the grain.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　粮食收购者收购粮食，应当执行国家粮食质量标准，按质论价，不得损害农民和其他粮食生产者的利益；应当及时向售粮者支付售粮款，不得拖欠；不得接受任何组织或者个人的委托代扣、代缴任何税、费和其他款项。  
粮食收购者收购粮食，应当按照国家有关规定进行质量安全检验，确保粮食质量安全。对不符合食品安全标准的粮食，应当作为非食用用途单独储存。

**Article 11** When purchasing grain, a grain purchaser shall implement the national grain quality standards, set prices according to quality, and shall not harm the interests of farmers and other grain producers; shall promptly pay the grain seller the grain proceeds, and shall not delay payment; shall not accept entrustment from any organization or individual to withhold or collect any taxes, fees or other sums on their behalf.  
When purchasing grain, a grain purchaser shall conduct quality and safety inspection in accordance with the relevant State provisions to ensure grain quality and safety. Grain that does not meet food safety standards shall be stored separately for non-food purposes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　粮食收购企业应当向收购地的县级人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门定期报告粮食收购数量等有关情况。  
跨省收购粮食，应当向收购地和粮食收购企业所在地的县级人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门定期报告粮食收购数量等有关情况。

**Article 12** A grain purchase enterprise shall periodically report to the grain and reserve administrative department of the people's government at the county level in the place of purchase information such as the quantity of grain purchased.  
When purchasing grain across provinces, the enterprise shall periodically report information such as the quantity of grain purchased to the grain and reserve administrative departments of the people's governments at the county level in both the place of purchase and the place where the grain purchase enterprise is located.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　粮食收购者、从事粮食储存的企业（以下简称粮食储存企业）使用的仓储设施，应当符合粮食储存有关标准和技术规范以及安全生产法律、法规的要求，具有与储存品种、规模、周期等相适应的仓储条件，减少粮食储存损耗。  
粮食不得与可能对粮食产生污染的有毒有害物质混存，储存粮食不得使用国家禁止使用的化学药剂或者超量使用化学药剂。

**Article 13** The storage facilities used by grain purchasers and enterprises engaged in grain storage (hereinafter referred to as "grain storage enterprises") shall meet the requirements of relevant standards and technical specifications for grain storage as well as work safety laws and regulations, and shall have storage conditions commensurate with the varieties, scale, and cycle of storage, so as to reduce grain storage losses.  
Grain shall not be stored together with toxic or harmful substances that may cause contamination to the grain; no chemical agents prohibited by the State or chemical agents in excess of the prescribed dosage shall be used for grain storage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　运输粮食应当严格执行国家粮食运输的技术规范，减少粮食运输损耗。不得使用被污染的运输工具或者包装材料运输粮食，不得与有毒有害物质混装运输。

**Article 14** The transportation of grain shall strictly implement the national technical specifications for grain transportation to reduce grain transportation losses. No contaminated means of transport or packaging materials shall be used to transport grain, and grain shall not be mixed with toxic or harmful substances during transportation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　从事粮食的食品生产，应当符合食品安全法律、法规和标准规定的条件和要求，对其生产食品的安全负责。  
国家鼓励粮食经营者提高成品粮出品率和副产物综合利用率。

**Article 15** Any person engaged in the food production of grain shall meet the conditions and requirements prescribed by food safety laws, regulations and standards, and shall be responsible for the safety of the food produced.  
The State encourages grain business operators to increase the yield of processed grain and the comprehensive utilization rate of by-products.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　销售粮食应当严格执行国家粮食质量等有关标准，不得短斤少两、掺杂使假、以次充好，不得囤积居奇、垄断或者操纵粮食价格、欺行霸市。

**Article 16** The sale of grain shall strictly implement the national grain quality and other relevant standards; no short weight, adulteration, passing off inferior as superior, hoarding, monopolizing or manipulating grain prices, or dominating the market shall be allowed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　粮食储存期间，应当定期进行粮食品质检验，粮食品质达到轻度不宜存时应当及时出库。  
建立粮食销售出库质量安全检验制度。正常储存年限内的粮食，在出库前应当由粮食储存企业自行或者委托粮食质量安全检验机构进行质量安全检验；超过正常储存年限的粮食，储存期间使用储粮药剂未满安全间隔期的粮食，以及色泽、气味异常的粮食，在出库前应当由粮食质量安全检验机构进行质量安全检验。未经质量安全检验的粮食不得销售出库。

**Article 17** During grain storage, grain quality shall be inspected periodically; when the grain quality reaches a level that is slightly unsuitable for storage, it shall be taken out of storage promptly.  
A quality and safety inspection system for grain sales and release from storage shall be established. For grain within the normal storage period, before release from storage, the grain storage enterprise shall conduct quality and safety inspection by itself or entrust a grain quality and safety inspection institution to do so; for grain beyond the normal storage period, grain for which the safety interval of chemical agents used during storage has not expired, and grain with abnormal color or odor, the quality and safety inspection shall be conducted by a grain quality and safety inspection institution before release from storage. No grain that has not undergone quality and safety inspection shall be sold or released from storage.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　粮食收购者、粮食储存企业不得将下列粮食作为食用用途销售出库：  
（一）真菌毒素、农药残留、重金属等污染物质以及其他危害人体健康的物质含量超过食品安全标准限量的；  
（二）霉变或者色泽、气味异常的；  
（三）储存期间使用储粮药剂未满安全间隔期的；  
（四）被包装材料、容器、运输工具等污染的；  
（五）其他法律、法规或者国家有关规定明确不得作为食用用途销售的。

**Article 18** Grain purchasers and grain storage enterprises shall not sell or release from storage the following grain for food purposes:  
(1) Grain in which the content of contaminants such as mycotoxins, pesticide residues, heavy metals and other substances harmful to human health exceeds the limits specified in food safety standards;  
(2) Grain that is moldy or has abnormal color or odor;  
(3) Grain for which the safety interval of chemical agents used during storage has not expired;  
(4) Grain contaminated by packaging materials, containers, means of transport, etc.;  
(5) Other grain that is explicitly prohibited from being sold for food purposes by other laws, regulations or relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事粮食收购、加工、销售的规模以上经营者，应当按照所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，执行特定情况下的粮食库存量。

**Article 19** Operators above a certain scale engaged in grain purchase, processing and sale shall, in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located, implement the grain inventory level under specific circumstances.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　粮食经营者从事政策性粮食经营活动，应当严格遵守国家有关规定，不得有下列行为：  
（一）虚报粮食收储数量；  
（二）通过以陈顶新、以次充好、低收高转、虚假购销、虚假轮换、违规倒卖等方式，套取粮食价差和财政补贴，骗取信贷资金；  
（三）挤占、挪用、克扣财政补贴、信贷资金；  
（四）以政策性粮食为债务作担保或者清偿债务；  
（五）利用政策性粮食进行除政府委托的政策性任务以外的其他商业经营；  
（六）在政策性粮食出库时掺杂使假、以次充好、调换标的物，拒不执行出库指令或者阻挠出库；  
（七）购买国家限定用途的政策性粮食，违规倒卖或者不按照规定用途处置；  
（八）擅自动用政策性粮食；  
（九）其他违反国家政策性粮食经营管理规定的行为。

**Article 20** A grain business operator engaged in policy-related grain business activities shall strictly comply with the relevant State provisions and shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) Falsely reporting the quantity of grain purchased and stored;  
(2) Obtaining grain price differences and fiscal subsidies, or defrauding credit funds, by means such as passing off old grain as new, passing off inferior as superior, purchasing at low prices and transferring at high prices, making false purchases and sales, making false rotations, or illegally reselling;  
(3) Misappropriating, diverting or withholding fiscal subsidies or credit funds;  
(4) Using policy-related grain as a guarantee for debts or to settle debts;  
(5) Using policy-related grain for commercial operations other than policy-related tasks entrusted by the government;  
(6) When releasing policy-related grain from storage, adulterating, passing off inferior as superior, substituting subject matter, refusing to execute release instructions or obstructing release;  
(7) Purchasing policy-related grain with restricted use by the State, illegally reselling it or disposing of it not in accordance with the prescribed use;  
(8) Unauthorized use of policy-related grain;  
(9) Other acts violating the State provisions on the management of policy-related grain business.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国有粮食企业应当积极收购粮食，并做好政策性粮食购销工作，服从和服务于国家宏观调控。

**Article 21** State-owned grain enterprises shall actively purchase grain, and shall properly carry out the purchase and sale of policy-related grain, subjecting to and serving the macro-control of the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对符合贷款条件的粮食收购者，银行应当按照国家有关规定及时提供收购贷款。  
中国农业发展银行应当保证中央和地方储备粮以及其他政策性粮食的信贷资金需要，对国有粮食企业、大型粮食产业化龙头企业和其他粮食企业，按企业的风险承受能力提供信贷资金支持。  
政策性粮食收购资金应当专款专用，封闭运行。

**Article 22** For grain purchasers that meet the loan conditions, banks shall promptly provide purchase loans in accordance with the relevant State provisions.  
The Agricultural Development Bank of China shall ensure the credit fund needs for central and local grain reserves and other policy-related grain, and shall provide credit fund support to State-owned grain enterprises, large leading grain industrialization enterprises and other grain enterprises according to their risk-bearing capacity.  
The funds for policy-related grain purchase shall be used exclusively for specified purposes and shall be operated in a closed loop.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　所有从事粮食收购、销售、储存、加工的经营者以及饲料、工业用粮企业，应当建立粮食经营台账，并向所在地的县级人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门报送粮食购进、销售、储存等基本数据和有关情况。粮食经营台账的保存期限不得少于3年。粮食经营者报送的基本数据和有关情况涉及商业秘密的，粮食和储备行政管理部门负有保密义务。  
国家粮食流通统计依照《中华人民共和国统计法》的有关规定执行。

**Article 23** All operators engaged in grain purchase, sale, storage and processing, as well as feed and industrial grain-using enterprises, shall establish grain business ledgers, and shall submit to the grain and reserve administrative department of the people's government at the county level where they are located the basic data and relevant information on grain purchase, sale, storage, etc. The retention period of grain business ledgers shall not be less than 3 years. Where the basic data and relevant information submitted by the grain business operator involve trade secrets, the grain and reserve administrative department shall have a duty of confidentiality.  
The national grain circulation statistics shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门应当建立粮食经营者信用档案，记录日常监督检查结果、违法行为查处情况，并依法向社会公示。  
粮食行业协会以及中介组织应当加强行业自律，在维护粮食市场秩序方面发挥监督和协调作用。

**Article 24** The grain and reserve administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall establish credit files for grain business operators, record the results of routine supervision and inspection, and the investigation and punishment of illegal acts, and shall publicize them to the public according to law.  
Grain industry associations and intermediary organizations shall strengthen industry self-discipline and play a supervisory and coordinating role in maintaining the order of the grain market.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家鼓励和支持开发、推广应用先进的粮食储存、运输、加工和信息化技术，开展珍惜和节约粮食宣传教育。  
县级以上人民政府粮食和储备行政管理部门应当加强对粮食经营者的指导和服务，引导粮食经营者节约粮食、降低粮食损失损耗。

**Article 25** The State encourages and supports the development, promotion and application of advanced technologies for grain storage, transportation, processing and informatization, and carries out publicity and education on cherishing and conserving grain.  
The grain and reserve administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen guidance and services for grain business operators, guiding them to conserve grain and reduce grain losses and waste.

### 第三章　宏观调控

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家采取政策性粮食购销、粮食进出口等多种经济手段和必要的行政手段，加强对粮食市场的调控，保持全国粮食供求总量基本平衡和市场基本稳定。

**Article 26** The State shall adopt various economic means such as policy-related grain purchase and sale, grain import and export, and necessary administrative means to strengthen the regulation of the grain market, and maintain the basic balance of total grain supply and demand and basic market stability nationwide.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家实行中央和地方分级粮食储备制度。粮食储备用于调节粮食供求、稳定粮食市场，以及应对重大自然灾害或者其他突发事件等情况。  
政策性粮食的采购和销售，原则上通过规范的粮食交易中心公开进行，也可以通过国家规定的其他方式进行。

**Article 27** The State implements a system of grain reserves at the central and local levels. Grain reserves shall be used for regulating grain supply and demand, stabilizing the grain market, and responding to major natural disasters or other emergencies.  
The procurement and sale of policy-related grain shall, in principle, be conducted openly through standardized grain trading centers, or may also be conducted through other methods prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院和地方人民政府建立健全粮食风险基金制度。粮食风险基金主要用于支持粮食储备、稳定粮食市场等。  
国务院和地方人民政府财政部门负责粮食风险基金的监督管理，确保专款专用。

**Article 28** The State Council and local people's governments shall establish and improve the grain risk fund system. The grain risk fund shall be mainly used for supporting grain reserves, stabilizing the grain market, etc.  
The financial departments of the State Council and local people's governments shall be responsible for the supervision and management of the grain risk fund to ensure that it is used exclusively for its specified purposes.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　为保障市场供应、保护种粮农民利益，必要时可由国务院根据粮食安全形势，结合财政状况，决定对重点粮食品种在粮食主产区实行政策性收储。  
当粮食价格显著上涨或者有可能显著上涨时，国务院和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以按照《中华人民共和国价格法》的规定，采取价格干预措施。

**Article 29** To ensure market supply and protect the interests of grain farmers, when necessary, the State Council may, based on the grain security situation and in light of the financial situation, decide to implement policy-related purchase and storage for key grain varieties in major grain-producing areas.  
When grain prices rise significantly or are likely to rise significantly, the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may take price intervention measures in accordance with the provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食和储备行政管理部门会同国务院农业农村、统计、市场监督管理等部门负责粮食市场供求形势的监测和预警分析，健全监测和预警体系，完善粮食供需抽查制度，发布粮食生产、消费、价格、质量等信息。

**Article 30** The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain and reserve administrative department shall, together with the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, statistics, market supervision and administration of the State Council, be responsible for monitoring and early warning analysis of the grain market supply and demand situation, improving the monitoring and early warning system, improving the grain supply and demand spot-check system, and releasing information on grain production, consumption, prices, quality, etc.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励粮食主产区和主销区以多种形式建立稳定的产销关系，鼓励培育生产、收购、储存、加工、销售一体化的粮食企业，支持建设粮食生产、加工、物流基地或者园区，加强对政府储备粮油仓储物流设施的保护，鼓励发展订单农业。在执行政策性收储时国家给予必要的经济优惠，并在粮食运输方面给予优先安排。

**Article 31** The State encourages major grain-producing areas and major grain-consuming areas to establish stable production-marketing relationships in various forms, encourages the cultivation of grain enterprises integrating production, purchase, storage, processing and sales, supports the construction of grain production, processing and logistics bases or parks, strengthens the protection of government grain and oil reserve storage and logistics facilities, and encourages the development of contract farming. When implementing policy-related purchase and storage, the State shall provide necessary economic preferences and give priority arrangements in grain transportation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在重大自然灾害、重大疫情或者其他突发事件引起粮食市场供求异常波动时，国家实施粮食应急机制。

**Article 32** When major natural disasters, major epidemics or other emergencies cause abnormal fluctuations in grain market supply and demand, the State shall implement the grain emergency mechanism.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立突发事件的粮食应急体系。国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食和储备行政管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定全国的粮食应急预案，报请国务院批准。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地区的实际情况，制定本行政区域的粮食应急预案。

**Article 33** The State shall establish a grain emergency response system for emergencies. The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain and reserve administrative department shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national grain emergency response plan and submit it to the State Council for approval. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate grain emergency response plans for their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　启动全国的粮食应急预案，由国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食和储备行政管理部门提出建议，报国务院批准后实施。  
启动省、自治区、直辖市的粮食应急预案，由省、自治区、直辖市发展改革部门及粮食和储备行政管理部门提出建议，报本级人民政府决定，并向国务院报告。  
设区的市级、县级人民政府粮食应急预案的制定和启动，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定。

**Article 34** The initiation of the national grain emergency response plan shall be proposed by the development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain and reserve administrative department, and implemented after reporting to the State Council for approval.  
The initiation of the grain emergency response plan of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be proposed by the development and reform department and the grain and reserve administrative department of that province, autonomous region or municipality, reported to the people's government at the same level for decision, and reported to the State Council.  
The formulation and initiation of grain emergency response plans by people's governments at the city with districts level and county level shall be decided by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　粮食应急预案启动后，粮食经营者必须按照国家要求承担应急任务，服从国家的统一安排和调度，保证应急的需要。

**Article 35** After the grain emergency response plan is initiated, grain business operators must undertake emergency tasks in accordance with the State requirements, subject to the unified arrangements and dispatch of the State, and ensure the needs of the emergency response.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家鼓励发展粮食产业经济，提高优质粮食供给水平，鼓励粮食产业化龙头企业提供安全优质的粮食产品。

**Article 36** The State encourages the development of the grain industry economy, improves the supply level of high-quality grain, and encourages leading grain industrialization enterprises to provide safe and high-quality grain products.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家建立健全粮食流通质量安全风险监测体系。国务院卫生健康、市场监督管理以及国家粮食和储备行政管理等部门，分别按照职责组织实施全国粮食流通质量安全风险监测；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康、市场监督管理、粮食和储备行政管理等部门，分别按照职责组织实施本行政区域的粮食流通质量安全风险监测。

**Article 37** The State shall establish and improve the grain circulation quality and safety risk monitoring system. The health, market supervision and administration, and national grain and reserve administrative departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions and duties, organize and implement the national grain circulation quality and safety risk monitoring; the health, market supervision and administration, grain and reserve administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their respective functions and duties, organize and implement the grain circulation quality and safety risk monitoring within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　粮食和储备行政管理部门依照本条例对粮食经营者从事粮食收购、储存、运输活动和政策性粮食的购销活动，以及执行国家粮食流通统计制度的情况进行监督检查。  
粮食和储备行政管理部门在监督检查过程中，可以进入粮食经营者经营场所，查阅有关资料、凭证；检查粮食数量、质量和储存安全情况；检查粮食仓储设施、设备是否符合有关标准和技术规范；向有关单位和人员调查了解相关情况；查封、扣押非法收购或者不符合国家粮食质量安全标准的粮食，用于违法经营或者被污染的工具、设备以及有关账簿资料；查封违法从事粮食经营活动的场所。

**Article 38** The grain and reserve administrative departments shall, in accordance with these Regulations, conduct supervision and inspection on grain business operators' activities of grain purchase, storage, and transportation, their activities of policy-related grain purchase and sale, as well as their compliance with the national grain circulation statistics system.  
In the process of supervision and inspection, the grain and reserve administrative departments may enter the business premises of grain business operators, consult relevant materials and vouchers; inspect the quantity, quality and storage safety of grain; inspect whether the grain storage facilities and equipment meet the relevant standards and technical specifications; investigate and learn about relevant information from relevant units and individuals; seal up or seize grain that is illegally purchased or does not meet the national grain quality and safety standards, tools and equipment used for illegal operations or contaminated, and relevant account books and materials; and seal up premises illegally used for grain business activities.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　市场监督管理部门依照有关法律、法规的规定，对粮食经营活动中的扰乱市场秩序行为、违法交易行为以及价格违法行为进行监督检查。

**Article 39** The market supervision and administration departments shall, in accordance with relevant laws and regulations, conduct supervision and inspection on acts of disrupting market order, illegal trading acts, and illegal pricing acts in grain business activities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强本行政区域粮食污染监控，建立健全被污染粮食收购处置长效机制，发现区域性粮食污染的，应当及时采取处置措施。  
被污染粮食处置办法由国家粮食和储备行政管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 40** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the monitoring of grain contamination in their respective administrative regions, establish and improve a long-term mechanism for the purchase and disposal of contaminated grain, and upon discovery of regional grain contamination, shall promptly take disposal measures.  
The measures for the disposal of contaminated grain shall be formulated by the national grain and reserve administrative department together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何单位和个人有权对违反本条例规定的行为向有关部门检举。有关部门应当为检举人保密，并依法及时处理。

**Article 41** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the relevant department any act violating the provisions of these Regulations. The relevant department shall keep the whistleblower confidential and shall handle the matter promptly according to law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，粮食和储备行政管理部门和其他有关部门不依法履行粮食流通管理和监督职责的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the grain and reserve administrative departments and other relevant departments fail to perform their duties of grain circulation management and supervision according to law, the leading personnel in charge and the directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　粮食收购企业未按照规定备案或者提供虚假备案信息的，由粮食和储备行政管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，处2万元以上5万元以下罚款。

**Article 43** Where a grain purchase enterprise fails to file in accordance with the provisions or provides false filing information, the grain and reserve administrative department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　粮食收购者有未按照规定告知、公示粮食收购价格或者收购粮食压级压价，垄断或者操纵价格等价格违法行为的，由市场监督管理部门依照《中华人民共和国价格法》、《中华人民共和国反垄断法》的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 44** Where a grain purchaser commits price-related illegal acts such as failing to inform or publicize grain purchase prices in accordance with the provisions, suppressing grades and prices when purchasing grain, or monopolizing or manipulating prices, the market supervision and administration department shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Price Law of the People's Republic of China and the Anti-Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　有下列情形之一的，由粮食和储备行政管理部门责令改正，给予警告，可以并处20万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处20万元以上50万元以下罚款：  
（一）粮食收购者未执行国家粮食质量标准；  
（二）粮食收购者未及时向售粮者支付售粮款；  
（三）粮食收购者违反本条例规定代扣、代缴税、费和其他款项；  
（四）粮食收购者收购粮食，未按照国家有关规定进行质量安全检验，或者对不符合食品安全标准的粮食未作为非食用用途单独储存；  
（五）从事粮食收购、销售、储存、加工的粮食经营者以及饲料、工业用粮企业未建立粮食经营台账，或者未按照规定报送粮食基本数据和有关情况；  
（六）粮食储存企业未按照规定进行粮食销售出库质量安全检验。

**Article 45** Under any of the following circumstances, the grain and reserve administrative department shall order corrections, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a concurrent fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) A grain purchaser fails to implement the national grain quality standards;  
(2) A grain purchaser fails to promptly pay the grain seller the grain proceeds;  
(3) A grain purchaser, in violation of these Regulations, withholds or collects taxes, fees and other sums on behalf of others;  
(4) A grain purchaser, when purchasing grain, fails to conduct quality and safety inspection in accordance with the relevant State provisions, or fails to store grain that does not meet food safety standards separately for non-food purposes;  
(5) Grain business operators engaged in grain purchase, sale, storage and processing, as well as feed and industrial grain-using enterprises, fail to establish grain business ledgers or fail to submit basic grain data and relevant information in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) A grain storage enterprise fails to conduct quality and safety inspection for grain sold and released from storage in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　粮食收购者、粮食储存企业未按照本条例规定使用仓储设施、运输工具的，由粮食和储备行政管理等部门按照职责责令改正，给予警告；被污染的粮食不得非法销售、加工。

**Article 46** Where a grain purchaser or grain storage enterprise fails to use storage facilities or means of transport in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the grain and reserve administrative department and other relevant departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, order corrections and give a warning; the contaminated grain shall not be illegally sold or processed.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　粮食收购者、粮食储存企业将下列粮食作为食用用途销售出库的，由粮食和储备行政管理部门没收违法所得；违法销售出库的粮食货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额1倍以上5倍以下罚款：  
（一）真菌毒素、农药残留、重金属等污染物质以及其他危害人体健康的物质含量超过食品安全标准限量的；  
（二）霉变或者色泽、气味异常的；  
（三）储存期间使用储粮药剂未满安全间隔期的；  
（四）被包装材料、容器、运输工具等污染的；  
（五）其他法律、法规或者国家有关规定明确不得作为食用用途销售的。

**Article 47** Where a grain purchaser or grain storage enterprise sells and releases from storage the following grain for food purposes, the grain and reserve administrative department shall confiscate the illegal gains; if the value of the grain illegally sold and released from storage is less than 10,000 yuan, a concurrent fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a concurrent fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the grain shall be imposed:  
(1) Grain in which the content of contaminants such as mycotoxins, pesticide residues, heavy metals and other substances harmful to human health exceeds the limits specified in food safety standards;  
(2) Grain that is moldy or has abnormal color or odor;  
(3) Grain for which the safety interval of chemical agents used during storage has not expired;  
(4) Grain contaminated by packaging materials, containers, means of transport, etc.;  
(5) Other grain that is explicitly prohibited from being sold for food purposes by other laws, regulations or relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　从事粮食的食品生产，不符合食品安全法律、法规和标准规定的条件和要求的，由市场监督管理部门依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》、《中华人民共和国食品安全法实施条例》等有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 48** Where any person engaged in the food production of grain fails to meet the conditions and requirements prescribed by food safety laws, regulations and standards, the market supervision and administration department shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on the Implementation of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　从事政策性粮食经营活动，有下列情形之一的，由粮食和储备行政管理部门责令改正，给予警告，没收违法所得，并处50万元以上200万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处200万元以上500万元以下罚款：  
（一）虚报粮食收储数量；  
（二）通过以陈顶新、以次充好、低收高转、虚假购销、虚假轮换、违规倒卖等方式，套取粮食价差和财政补贴，骗取信贷资金；  
（三）挤占、挪用、克扣财政补贴、信贷资金；  
（四）以政策性粮食为债务作担保或者清偿债务；  
（五）利用政策性粮食进行除政府委托的政策性任务以外的其他商业经营；  
（六）在政策性粮食出库时掺杂使假、以次充好、调换标的物，拒不执行出库指令或者阻挠出库；  
（七）购买国家限定用途的政策性粮食，违规倒卖或者不按照规定用途处置；  
（八）擅自动用政策性粮食；  
（九）其他违反国家政策性粮食经营管理规定的行为。  
粮食应急预案启动后，不按照国家要求承担应急任务，不服从国家的统一安排和调度的，依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 49** Where any person engaged in policy-related grain business activities commits any of the following circumstances, the grain and reserve administrative department shall order corrections, give a warning, confiscate illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a concurrent fine of not less than 2,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Falsely reporting the quantity of grain purchased and stored;  
(2) Obtaining grain price differences and fiscal subsidies, or defrauding credit funds, by means such as passing off old grain as new, passing off inferior as superior, purchasing at low prices and transferring at high prices, making false purchases and sales, making false rotations, or illegally reselling;  
(3) Misappropriating, diverting or withholding fiscal subsidies or credit funds;  
(4) Using policy-related grain as a guarantee for debts or to settle debts;  
(5) Using policy-related grain for commercial operations other than policy-related tasks entrusted by the government;  
(6) When releasing policy-related grain from storage, adulterating, passing off inferior as superior, substituting subject matter, refusing to execute release instructions or obstructing release;  
(7) Purchasing policy-related grain with restricted use by the State, illegally reselling it or disposing of it not in accordance with the prescribed use;  
(8) Unauthorized use of policy-related grain;  
(9) Other acts violating the State provisions on the management of policy-related grain business.  
Where, after the grain emergency response plan is initiated, any person fails to undertake emergency tasks in accordance with the State requirements or fails to subject to the unified arrangements and dispatch of the State, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对粮食经营活动中的扰乱市场秩序、违法交易等行为，由市场监督管理部门依照有关法律、法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 50** Acts of disrupting market order, illegal trading, etc. in grain business activities shall be punished by the market supervision and administration department in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　从事粮食经营活动的企业有违反本条例规定的违法情形且情节严重的，对其法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本企业取得收入的1倍以上10倍以下罚款。

**Article 51** Where an enterprise engaged in grain business activities has illegal circumstances in violation of these Regulations and the circumstances are serious, its legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than one time but not more than ten times the income obtained from the enterprise in the previous year.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，阻碍粮食自由流通的，依照《国务院关于禁止在市场经济活动中实行地区封锁的规定》给予处罚。

**Article 52** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, obstructs the free circulation of grain shall be punished in accordance with the Provisions of the State Council on Prohibiting Regional Blockades in Market Economic Activities.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Where any violation of these Regulations constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
粮食收购，是指向种粮农民、其他粮食生产者或者粮食经纪人、农民专业合作社等批量购买粮食的活动。  
粮食加工，是指通过处理将原粮转化成半成品粮、成品粮以及其他食用或者非食用产品的活动。  
政策性粮食，是指政府指定或者委托粮食经营者购买、储存、加工、销售，并给予财政、金融等方面政策性支持的粮食，包括但不限于政府储备粮。  
粮食经纪人，是指以个人或者家庭为经营主体，直接向种粮农民、其他粮食生产者、农民专业合作社批量购买粮食的经营者。  
技术规范，是指尚未制定国家标准、行业标准，国家粮食和储备行政管理部门根据监督管理工作需要制定的补充技术要求。

**Article 54** For the purposes of these Regulations, the meanings of the following terms are:  
Grain purchase means the activity of purchasing grain in bulk from grain-growing farmers, other grain producers, or grain brokers, farmers' professional cooperatives, etc.  
Grain processing means the activity of converting raw grain into semi-finished grain, finished grain, and other edible or non-edible products through processing.  
Policy-based grain means grain that the government designates or entrusts grain operators to purchase, store, process, and sell, and to which policy support in finance, banking, etc. is granted, including but not limited to government reserve grain.  
Grain broker means an operator who, with an individual or household as the business entity, directly purchases grain in bulk from grain-growing farmers, other grain producers, and farmers' professional cooperatives.  
Technical specification means supplementary technical requirements formulated by the National Food and Strategic Reserves Administration according to the needs of supervision and management, where no national standard or industry standard has been developed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　大豆、油料和食用植物油的收购、销售、储存、运输、加工、进出口等经营活动，适用[本条例除第九条](#art-9)第二款以外的规定。  
粮食进出口的管理，依照有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 55** The provisions of these Regulations, with the exception of paragraph 2 of Article 9, shall apply to the business activities such as purchase, sale, storage, transportation, processing, import and export of soybeans, oil plants, and edible vegetable oils.  
The administration of grain import and export shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本条例自2021年4月15日起施行。

**Article 56** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 15, 2021.
