# 植物检疫条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07

> (1983年1月3日国务院发布　根据1992年5月13日《国务院关于修改〈植物检疫条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一条　为了防止危害植物的危险性病、虫、杂草传播蔓延，保护农业、林业生产安全，制定本条例。 第二条　国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门主管全国的植物检疫工作，各省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门主管本地区的植物检疫工作。 第三条　县级以上地方各级农业主管部门、林业主管部门所属的植物检疫机构，负责执行国家的植物检疫任务。 植物检疫人员进入车站、机场、港口、仓库以及其他有关场所执行植物检疫任务，应穿着检疫制服和佩带检疫标志。 第四条　凡局部地区发生的危险性大、能随植物及其产品传播的病、虫、杂草，应定为植物检疫对象。农业、林业植物检疫对象和应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单，由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。各省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门可以根据本地区的需要，制定本省、自治区、直辖市的补充名单，并报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门备案。 第五条　局部地区发生植物检疫对象的，应划为疫区，采取封锁、消灭措施，防止植物检疫对象传出；发生地区已比较普遍的，则应将未发生地区划为保护区，防止植物检疫对象传入。 疫区应根据植物检疫对象的传播情况、当地的地理环境、交通状况以及采取封锁、消灭措施的需要来划定，其范围应严格控制。 在发生疫情的地区，植物检疫机构可以派人参加当地的道路联合检查站或者木材检查站；发生特大疫情时，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以设立植物检疫检查站，开展植物检疫工作。 第六条　疫区和保护区的划定，由省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门提出，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门备案。 疫区和保护区的范围涉及两省、自治区、直辖市以上的，由有关省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门共同提出，报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门批准后划定。 疫区、保护区的改变和撤销的程序，与划定时同。 第七条　调运植物和植物产品，属于下列情况的，必须经过检疫： (一)列入应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单的，运出发生疫情的县级行政区域之前，必须经过检疫； (二)凡种子、苗木和其他繁殖材料，不论是否列入应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单和运往何地，在调运之前，都必须经过检疫。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防止危害植物的危险性病、虫、杂草传播蔓延，保护农业、林业生产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of preventing the spread and dissemination of dangerous diseases, insects, and weeds that harm plants, and protecting the safety of agricultural and forestry production.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门主管全国的植物检疫工作，各省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门主管本地区的植物检疫工作。

**Article 2** The agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council shall be in charge of the plant quarantine work of the whole country, and the agriculture administrative departments and forestry administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be in charge of the plant quarantine work of their respective regions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上地方各级农业主管部门、林业主管部门所属的植物检疫机构，负责执行国家的植物检疫任务。  
植物检疫人员进入车站、机场、港口、仓库以及其他有关场所执行植物检疫任务，应穿着检疫制服和佩带检疫标志。

**Article 3** The plant quarantine agencies affiliated with the agriculture administrative departments and forestry administrative departments at or above the county level shall be responsible for carrying out the national plant quarantine tasks.  
Plant quarantine personnel, when entering stations, airports, ports, warehouses or other relevant places to carry out plant quarantine tasks, shall wear quarantine uniforms and display quarantine badges.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　凡局部地区发生的危险性大、能随植物及其产品传播的病、虫、杂草，应定为植物检疫对象。农业、林业植物检疫对象和应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单，由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。各省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门可以根据本地区的需要，制定本省、自治区、直辖市的补充名单，并报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门备案。

**Article 4** Any diseases, insects, or weeds that occur in local areas, are highly dangerous, and can be spread with plants or plant products shall be designated as plant quarantine objects. The lists of agricultural and forestry plant quarantine objects and plants and plant products subject to quarantine shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council. The agriculture administrative departments and forestry administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the needs of their respective regions, formulate supplementary lists for their own provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall report them to the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council for archival purposes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　局部地区发生植物检疫对象的，应划为疫区，采取封锁、消灭措施，防止植物检疫对象传出；发生地区已比较普遍的，则应将未发生地区划为保护区，防止植物检疫对象传入。  
疫区应根据植物检疫对象的传播情况、当地的地理环境、交通状况以及采取封锁、消灭措施的需要来划定，其范围应严格控制。  
在发生疫情的地区，植物检疫机构可以派人参加当地的道路联合检查站或者木材检查站；发生特大疫情时，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以设立植物检疫检查站，开展植物检疫工作。

**Article 5** Where plant quarantine objects occur in local areas, such areas shall be designated as epidemic areas, and blockade and eradication measures shall be taken to prevent the plant quarantine objects from spreading out; where the areas of occurrence are already relatively extensive, the areas where the objects have not occurred shall be designated as protected areas to prevent the plant quarantine objects from being introduced.  
Epidemic areas shall be delimited in accordance with the transmission situation of the plant quarantine objects, the local geographical environment, traffic conditions, and the need to take blockade and eradication measures, and the scope thereof shall be strictly controlled.  
In areas where epidemic situations occur, the plant quarantine agency may send personnel to participate in local joint road inspection stations or timber inspection stations; where a major epidemic situation occurs, with the approval of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, a plant quarantine inspection station may be established to carry out plant quarantine work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　疫区和保护区的划定，由省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门提出，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门备案。  
疫区和保护区的范围涉及两省、自治区、直辖市以上的，由有关省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门共同提出，报国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门批准后划定。  
疫区、保护区的改变和撤销的程序，与划定时同。

**Article 6** The designation of epidemic areas and protected areas shall be proposed by the agriculture administrative departments and forestry administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, reported to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for approval, and reported to the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council for archival purposes.  
Where the scope of an epidemic area or protected area involves two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall be jointly proposed by the agriculture administrative departments and forestry administrative departments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be designated after being reported to and approved by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.  
The procedures for changing and abolishing an epidemic area or protected area shall be the same as those for designation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　调运植物和植物产品，属于下列情况的，必须经过检疫：  
(一)列入应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单的，运出发生疫情的县级行政区域之前，必须经过检疫；  
(二)凡种子、苗木和其他繁殖材料，不论是否列入应施检疫的植物、植物产品名单和运往何地，在调运之前，都必须经过检疫。

**Article 7** The transportation of plants and plant products under any of the following circumstances must undergo quarantine:  
(1) Where they are included in the list of plants and plant products subject to quarantine, quarantine must be undergone before being transported out of the county-level administrative region where an epidemic situation occurs;  
(2) All seeds, seedlings, and other propagation materials, regardless of whether they are included in the list of plants and plant products subject to quarantine and the place to which they are transported, must undergo quarantine before transportation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　按照[本条例第七条](#art-7)的规定必须检疫的植物和植物产品，经检疫未发现植物检疫对象的，发给植物检疫证书。发现有植物检疫对象，但能彻底消毒处理的，托运人应按植物检疫机构的要求，在指定地点作消毒处理，经检查合格后发给植物检疫证书；无法消毒处理的，应停止调运。  
植物检疫证书的格式由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。  
对可能被植物检疫对象污染的包装材料、运载工具、场地、仓库等，也应实施检疫。如已被污染，托运人应按植物检疫机构的要求处理。  
因实施检疫需要的车船停留、货物搬运、开拆、取样、储存、消毒处理等费用，由托运人负责。

**Article 8** For plants and plant products that must undergo quarantine in accordance with Article 7 of these Regulations, if no plant quarantine objects are found after quarantine, a plant quarantine certificate shall be issued. If plant quarantine objects are found but can be thoroughly disinfected, the consignor shall, in accordance with the requirements of the plant quarantine agency, carry out disinfection at a designated place, and a plant quarantine certificate shall be issued after inspection passes; if disinfection cannot be carried out, transportation shall be stopped.  
The format of the plant quarantine certificate shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.  
Quarantine shall also be carried out on packaging materials, means of transport, premises, warehouses, etc., that may be contaminated by plant quarantine objects. If they have been contaminated, the consignor shall handle them in accordance with the requirements of the plant quarantine agency.  
Expenses for vehicle or vessel detention, cargo handling, unpacking, sampling, storage, disinfection, etc., necessary for implementing quarantine shall be borne by the consignor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　按照[本条例第七条](#art-7)的规定必须检疫的植物和植物产品，交通运输部门和邮政部门一律凭植物检疫证书承运或收寄。植物检疫证书应随货运寄。具体办法由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门会同铁道、交通、民航、邮政部门制定。

**Article 9** For plants and plant products that must undergo quarantine in accordance with Article 7 of these Regulations, the transportation and postal departments shall, in all cases, accept for carriage or acceptance only upon presentation of the plant quarantine certificate. The plant quarantine certificate shall be sent together with the goods. The specific measures shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council jointly with the railway, communications, civil aviation, and postal departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　省、自治区、直辖市间调运[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定必须经过检疫的植物和植物产品的，调入单位必须事先征得所在地的省、自治区、直辖市植物检疫机构同意，并向调出单位提出检疫要求；调出单位必须根据该检疫要求向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市植物检疫机构申请检疫。对调入的植物和植物产品，调入单位所在地的省、自治区、直辖市的植物检疫机构应当查验检疫证书，必要时可以复检。  
省、自治区、直辖市内调运植物和植物产品的检疫办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 10** Where plants and plant products that must undergo quarantine as provided for in Article 7 of these Regulations are transported between provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the receiving unit must first obtain the consent of the plant quarantine agency of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and shall present quarantine requirements to the dispatching unit; the dispatching unit must, on the basis of such quarantine requirements, apply for quarantine to the plant quarantine agency of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. For the plants and plant products received, the plant quarantine agency of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the receiving unit is located shall examine the quarantine certificate, and may re-inspect if necessary.  
The quarantine measures for the transportation of plants and plant products within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　种子、苗木和其他繁殖材料的繁育单位，必须有计划地建立无植物检疫对象的种苗繁育基地、母树林基地。试验、推广的种子、苗木和其他繁殖材料，不得带有植物检疫对象。植物检疫机构应实施产地检疫。

**Article 11** Breeding units of seeds, seedlings, and other propagation materials shall, in a planned manner, establish bases for breeding seeds and seedlings and mother tree forests free from plant quarantine objects. Seeds, seedlings, and other propagation materials for experiment and popularization shall not carry plant quarantine objects. The plant quarantine agency shall implement origin quarantine.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从国外引进种子、苗木，引进单位应当向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市植物检疫机构提出申请，办理检疫审批手续。但是，国务院有关部门所属的在京单位从国外引进种子、苗木，应当向国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门所属的植物检疫机构提出申请，办理检疫审批手续。具体办法由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。  
从国外引进、可能潜伏有危险性病、虫的种子、苗木和其他繁殖材料，必须隔离试种，植物检疫机构应进行调查、观察和检疫，证明确实不带危险性病、虫的，方可分散种植。

**Article 12** Where seeds or seedlings are introduced from abroad, the introducing unit shall submit an application to the plant quarantine agency of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and go through the quarantine approval procedures. However, units in Beijing under the relevant departments of the State Council that introduce seeds or seedlings from abroad shall submit an application to the plant quarantine agency under the agriculture administrative department or the forestry administrative department of the State Council, and go through the quarantine approval procedures. The specific measures shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.  
Seeds, seedlings, and other propagation materials introduced from abroad that may harbor dangerous diseases or insects must be isolated for trial planting. The plant quarantine agency shall conduct investigation, observation, and quarantine. Only after it is proven that they are indeed free from dangerous diseases or insects may they be planted in a scattered manner.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农林院校和试验研究单位对植物检疫对象的研究，不得在检疫对象的非疫区进行。因教学、科研确需在非疫区进行时，应当遵守国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门的规定。

**Article 13** The research on plant quarantine objects by agricultural and forestry colleges and experimental research units shall not be carried out in the non-epidemic areas of the quarantine objects. Where it is truly necessary for teaching or scientific research to be conducted in non-epidemic areas, the provisions of the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council shall be complied with.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　植物检疫机构对于新发现的检疫对象和其他危险性病、虫、杂草，必须及时查清情况，立即报告省、自治区、直辖市农业主管部门、林业主管部门，采取措施，彻底消灭，并报告国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门。

**Article 14** For newly discovered quarantine objects and other dangerous diseases, insects, and weeds, the plant quarantine agency must promptly ascertain the situation, immediately report to the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, take measures to completely eradicate them, and report to the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　疫情由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门发布。

**Article 15** Epidemic situations shall be announced by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　按照[本条例第五条](#art-5)第一款和第十四条的规定，进行疫情调查和采取消灭措施所需的紧急防治费和补助费，由省、自治区、直辖市在每年的植物保护费、森林保护费或者国营农场生产费中安排。特大疫情的防治费，国家酌情给予补助。

**Article 16** The emergency prevention and control expenses and subsidies necessary for conducting epidemic investigation and taking eradication measures in accordance with the provisions of Article 5, paragraph 1, and Article 14 of these Regulations shall be arranged by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government out of their annual plant protection funds, forest protection funds, or state farm production funds. For the prevention and control expenses of major epidemic situations, the State may grant subsidies as it deems appropriate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在植物检疫工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 17** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in plant quarantine work shall be given awards by the people's governments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列行为之一的，植物检疫机构应当责令纠正，可以处以罚款；造成损失的，应当负责赔偿；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例规定办理植物检疫证书或者在报检过程中弄虚作假的；  
(二)伪造、涂改、买卖、转让植物检疫单证、印章、标志、封识的；  
(三)未依照本条例规定调运、隔离试种或者生产应施检疫的植物、植物产品的；  
(四)违反本条例规定，擅自开拆植物、植物产品包装，调换植物、植物产品，或者擅自改变植物、植物产品的规定用途的；  
(五)违反本条例规定，引起疫情扩散的。  
有前款第(一)、(二)、(三)、(四)项所列情形之一，尚不构成犯罪的，植物检疫机构可以没收非法所得。  
对违反本条例规定调运的植物和植物产品，植物检疫机构有权予以封存、没收、销毁或者责令改变用途。销毁所需费用由责任人承担。

**Article 18** Where any of the following acts is committed, the plant quarantine agency shall order rectification and may impose a fine; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made; if a crime is constituted, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to apply for a plant quarantine certificate in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or resorting to fraud in the reporting process;  
(2) forging, altering, trading, or transferring plant quarantine documents, seals, marks, or seals;  
(3) failing to transport, isolate for trial planting, or produce plants or plant products subject to quarantine in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) violating the provisions of these Regulations, opening the packaging of plants or plant products without authorization, replacing plants or plant products, or changing the prescribed use of plants or plant products without authorization;  
(5) violating the provisions of these Regulations, causing the spread of an epidemic situation.  
Where the circumstances listed in items (1), (2), (3), or (4) of the preceding paragraph exist but do not constitute a crime, the plant quarantine agency may confiscate the illegal gains.  
With respect to plants and plant products transported in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the plant quarantine agency shall have the authority to seal up, confiscate, destroy, or order a change of use. The expenses necessary for destruction shall be borne by the person responsible.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　植物检疫人员在植物检疫工作中，交通运输部门和邮政部门有关工作人员在植物、植物产品的运输、邮寄工作中，徇私舞弊、玩忽职守的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where plant quarantine personnel in plant quarantine work, or relevant staff members of transportation departments or postal departments in the transport or mailing of plants or plant products, engage in illegal practices for personal gain or neglect their duties, they shall be given disciplinary sanctions by the unit to which they belong or the competent authority at a higher level; if a crime is constituted, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人对植物检疫机构的行政处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知书之日起十五日内，向作出行政处罚决定的植物检疫机构的上级机构申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定书之日起十五日内向人民法院提起诉讼。当事人逾期不申请复议或者不起诉又不履行行政处罚决定的，植物检疫机构可以申请人民法院强制执行或者依法强制执行。

**Article 20** Where a party disagrees with an administrative penalty decision made by a plant quarantine agency, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the agency superior to the plant quarantine agency that made the administrative penalty decision; if it disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, institute a prosecution in a People's Court. Where the party fails to apply for reconsideration or institute a prosecution within the time limit and fails to comply with the administrative penalty decision, the plant quarantine agency may apply to a People's Court for compulsory enforcement or enforce according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　植物检疫机构执行检疫任务可以收取检疫费，具体办法由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。

**Article 21** The plant quarantine agency may collect quarantine fees when performing quarantine tasks. The specific measures shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　进出口植物的检疫，按照《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》的规定执行。

**Article 22** The quarantine of plants for import and export shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例的实施细则由国务院农业主管部门、林业主管部门制定。各省、自治区、直辖市可根据本条例及其实施细则，结合当地具体情况，制定实施办法。

**Article 23** The detailed implementation rules of these Regulations shall be formulated by the agriculture administrative department and the forestry administrative department under the State Council. Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of their local specific conditions, formulate implementation measures in accordance with these Regulations and the detailed implementation rules.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。国务院批准，农业部一九五七年十二月四日发布的《国内植物检疫试行办法》同时废止。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Measures for Domestic Plant Quarantine approved by the State Council and promulgated by the Ministry of Agriculture on December 4, 1957, shall be abolished simultaneously.
