# 农民承担费用和劳务管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1991-12-07  
**生效日期**：1991-12-07

> (1991年11月5日国务院第92次常务会议通过　1991年12月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第92号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了减轻农民负担，保护农民的合法权益，调动农民的生产积极性，促进农村经济持续稳定协调发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称农民承担的费用和劳务，是指农民除缴纳税金，完成国家农产品定购任务外，依照法律、法规所承担的村(包括村民小组，下同)提留、乡(包括镇，下同)统筹费、劳务(农村义务工和劳动积累工)以及其他费用。 向国家缴纳税金，完成国家农产品定购任务，承担前款规定的各项费用和劳务，是农民应尽的义务。除此以外要求农民无偿提供任何财力、物力和劳务的，均为非法行为，农民有权拒绝。 第三条　国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国农民承担费用和劳务(以下简称农民负担)的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的农民负担监督管理工作。 乡人民政府主管本乡的农民负担监督管理工作，日常工作由乡农村经济经营管理部门负责。 第四条　各级农民负担监督管理部门负责检查有关农民负担管理的法律、法规和政策的执行情况；会同有关主管部门审核涉及农民负担的文件；协助有关机关处理涉及农民负担的案件；培训农民负担监督管理工作人员。 第五条　国家鼓励和支持发展农村集体经济，提倡主要依靠集体经营增加的收入，兴办农村社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设事业。 第二章　村提留、乡统筹费、劳务的标准和使用范围 第六条　农民直接向集体经济组织缴纳的村提留和乡统筹费(不含乡村集体所有制企业缴纳的利润)，以乡为单位，以国家统计局批准、农业部制定的农村经济收益分配统计报表和计算方法统计的数字为依据，不得超过上一年农民人均纯收入的5%。对经济发达的地区，经省、自治区、直辖市批准，可以适当提高提取比例。 乡统筹费的最高限额由省、自治区、直辖市确定。 第七条　村提留包括公积金、公益金和管理费： (一)公积金，用于农田水利基本建设、植树造林、购置生产性固定资产和兴办集体企业。 (二)公益金，用于五保户供养、特别困难户补助、合作医疗保健以及其他集体福利事业。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了减轻农民负担，保护农民的合法权益，调动农民的生产积极性，促进农村经济持续稳定协调发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated with a view to reducing the burden on peasants, protecting their legitimate rights and interests, mobilizing their initiative in production, and promoting the sustained, stable and coordinated development of the rural economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称农民承担的费用和劳务，是指农民除缴纳税金，完成国家农产品定购任务外，依照法律、法规所承担的村(包括村民小组，下同)提留、乡(包括镇，下同)统筹费、劳务(农村义务工和劳动积累工)以及其他费用。  
向国家缴纳税金，完成国家农产品定购任务，承担前款规定的各项费用和劳务，是农民应尽的义务。除此以外要求农民无偿提供任何财力、物力和劳务的，均为非法行为，农民有权拒绝。

**Article 2** "Fees and services borne by peasants" as mentioned in these Regulations means the village (including villagers' groups, the same below) retainage, township (including towns, the same below) overall planning fees, services (compulsory rural labour and labour accumulation work) and other fees borne by peasants in accordance with laws and regulations, in addition to paying taxes and fulfilling the state's task of purchasing agricultural products according to plan.  
Paying taxes to the state, fulfilling the state's task of purchasing agricultural products according to plan, and bearing the various fees and services specified in the preceding paragraph are the obligations of peasants. Any demand on peasants to provide financial or material resources or services free of charge beyond these obligations shall be illegal acts, and peasants shall have the right to refuse.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国农民承担费用和劳务(以下简称农民负担)的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府农业行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的农民负担监督管理工作。  
乡人民政府主管本乡的农民负担监督管理工作，日常工作由乡农村经济经营管理部门负责。

**Article 3** The administrative department for agriculture under the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of fees and services borne by peasants (hereinafter referred to as peasants' burden) throughout the country. The administrative departments for agriculture of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of peasants' burden within their respective administrative areas.  
The township people's government shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of peasants' burden in its township, and the routine work thereof shall be the responsibility of the township rural economic operation and management department.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级农民负担监督管理部门负责检查有关农民负担管理的法律、法规和政策的执行情况；会同有关主管部门审核涉及农民负担的文件；协助有关机关处理涉及农民负担的案件；培训农民负担监督管理工作人员。

**Article 4** The supervision and administration departments for peasants' burden at all levels shall be responsible for inspecting the implementation of laws, regulations and policies concerning the management of peasants' burden; jointly with the relevant competent departments, examining and verifying documents concerning peasants' burden; assisting the relevant organs in handling cases concerning peasants' burden; and training personnel for the supervision and administration of peasants' burden.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励和支持发展农村集体经济，提倡主要依靠集体经营增加的收入，兴办农村社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设事业。

**Article 5** The state encourages and supports the development of the rural collective economy, and advocates the use of income primarily derived from collective operations to undertake undertakings for the socialist material and cultural progress in rural areas.

### 第二章　村提留、乡统筹费、劳务的标准和使用范围

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　农民直接向集体经济组织缴纳的村提留和乡统筹费(不含乡村集体所有制企业缴纳的利润)，以乡为单位，以国家统计局批准、农业部制定的农村经济收益分配统计报表和计算方法统计的数字为依据，不得超过上一年农民人均纯收入的5%。对经济发达的地区，经省、自治区、直辖市批准，可以适当提高提取比例。  
乡统筹费的最高限额由省、自治区、直辖市确定。

**Article 6** The village retainage and township overall planning fees paid directly by peasants to collective economic organizations (excluding profits paid by rural collective-owned enterprises) shall, taking the township as a unit and based on the figures compiled in accordance with the statistical statements and calculation methods for the distribution of rural economic income approved by the National Bureau of Statistics and formulated by the Ministry of Agriculture, not exceed 5% of the per capita net income of peasants in the preceding year. For economically developed areas, with the approval of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, the proportion to be collected may be appropriately increased.  
The maximum limit of township overall planning fees shall be determined by the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　村提留包括公积金、公益金和管理费：  
(一)公积金，用于农田水利基本建设、植树造林、购置生产性固定资产和兴办集体企业。  
(二)公益金，用于五保户供养、特别困难户补助、合作医疗保健以及其他集体福利事业。  
(三)管理费，用于村干部报酬和管理开支。  
村干部报酬实行定额补助和误工补贴两种形式。具体定额补助人数、标准和误工补贴办法，由乡人民政府根据村规模、经济发展水平和实际工作需要制定，报县级人民政府农民负担监督管理部门备案。

**Article 7** Village retainage includes the accumulation fund, the public welfare fund and the management fee:  
(1) The accumulation fund shall be used for capital construction of farmland and water conservancy, afforestation, purchase of productive fixed assets and establishment of collective enterprises.  
(2) The public welfare fund shall be used for the support of households enjoying the five guarantees (food, clothing, medical care, housing and burial expenses), subsidies for households with special difficulties, cooperative medical care and other collective welfare undertakings.  
(3) The management fee shall be used for the remuneration of village cadres and administrative expenses.  
The remuneration of village cadres shall be in two forms: fixed subsidies and compensation for lost working time. The specific number of persons entitled to fixed subsidies, the standards therefor and the method of compensation for lost working time shall be determined by the township people's government in light of the size of the village, the level of economic development and the actual needs of work, and shall be reported to the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden under the county people's government for the record.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　乡统筹费用于安排乡村两级办学、计划生育、优抚、民兵训练、修建乡村道路等民办公助事业。  
乡统筹费可以用于五保户供养。五保户供养从乡统筹费中列支的，不得在村提留中重复列支。

**Article 8** Township overall planning fees shall be used for arranging such undertakings run by the people and subsidized by the state as education at the village and township levels, family planning, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs and servicemen, militia training and construction of village and township roads.  
Township overall planning fees may be used for supporting households enjoying the five guarantees. Where such support is funded from township overall planning fees, it shall not be charged again from village retainage.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　乡统筹费内的乡村两级办学经费(即农村教育事业费附加)用于本乡范围内乡村两级的民办教育事业。  
乡村两级办学经费在乡统筹费内所占比例，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育主管部门提出，经同级农民负担监督管理部门审核，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并报国务院农业行政主管部门和教育主管部门备案。

**Article 9** The funds for school operation at the village and township levels within the township overall planning fees (namely, the additional rural educational expenses) shall be used for the run-by-the-people educational undertakings at the village and township levels within the township.  
The proportion of the funds for school operation at the village and township levels within the township overall planning fees shall be proposed by the competent educational department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, examined and verified by the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden at the same level, submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval, and reported to the administrative department for agriculture and the competent educational department under the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　农村义务工，主要用于植树造林、防汛、公路建勤、修缮校舍等。按标准工日计算，每个农村劳动力每年承担五至十个农村义务工。  
因抢险救灾，需要增加农村义务工的，由当地人民政府统筹安排。

**Article 10** Compulsory rural labour shall be mainly used for afforestation, flood prevention, road construction and maintenance, repair of school buildings, etc. Calculated in standard work-days, each rural labourer shall undertake five to ten compulsory rural labour days each year.  
Where additional compulsory rural labour is needed due to emergency rescue and disaster relief, it shall be arranged by the local people's government in a unified manner.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　劳动积累工，主要用于农田水利基本建设和植树造林。按标准工日计算，每个农村劳动力每年承担十至二十个劳动积累工。有条件的地方，经县级以上人民政府批准，可以适当增加。劳动积累工应当主要在农闲期间使用。

**Article 11** Labour accumulation work shall be mainly used for capital construction of farmland and water conservancy and for afforestation. Calculated in standard work-days, each rural labourer shall undertake ten to twenty labour accumulation work-days each year. In areas where conditions permit, with the approval of the people's government at or above the county level, the number may be appropriately increased. Labour accumulation work shall be mainly performed during the slack farming seasons.

### 第三章　村提留、乡统筹费、劳务的提取和管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　村提留和乡统筹费主要按农民从事的产业和经济收入承担。  
承包耕地的农民按其承包的耕地面积或者劳动力向其所属的集体经济组织缴纳村提留和乡统筹费。  
经营个体工商业和私营企业的，应在税后按经营所在地规定的提取比例，缴纳村提留和乡统筹费，但不计算在[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定的限额比例之内。

**Article 12** Village retainage and township overall planning fees shall be borne primarily in accordance with the industry engaged in and the economic income of the peasants.  
Peasants contracting cultivated land shall pay village retainage and township overall planning fees to the collective economic organization to which they belong on the basis of the area of cultivated land contracted or the number of labourers.  
Operators of individual industrial and commercial businesses and private enterprises shall, after paying taxes, pay village retainage and township overall planning fees at the proportion stipulated by the place of operation, but such fees shall not be counted within the proportional limit specified in Article 6 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对收入水平在本村平均线以下的革命烈军属、伤残军人、失去劳动能力的复员退伍军人和特别困难户，经村集体经济组织成员大会或者成员代表会议讨论评定，适当减免村提留。

**Article 13** For family members of revolutionary martyrs and servicemen, disabled servicemen, demobilized and retired servicemen who have lost the ability to work, and households with special difficulties whose income level is below the average line of the village, after discussion and evaluation by the general meeting of members of the village collective economic organization or the meeting of representatives of members, the village retainage may be appropriately reduced or exempted.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　乡人民政府评定的贫困村，经村集体经济组织提出申请，乡农民负担监督管理部门审核、乡人民政府同意，报乡人民代表大会审议通过，可以适当核减乡统筹费。

**Article 14** For a poor village evaluated by the township people's government, upon application by the village collective economic organization, examination and verification by the township supervision and administration department for peasants' burden, consent by the township people's government, and submission to the township people's congress for deliberation and approval, the township overall planning fees may be appropriately reduced.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农村义务工和劳动积累工以出劳为主，本人要求以资代劳的，须经村集体经济组织批准。  
对因病或者伤残不能承担农村义务工、劳动积累工的，经村集体经济组织成员大会或者成员代表会议讨论通过，可以减免。

**Article 15** Compulsory rural labour and labour accumulation work shall be mainly performed by personal labour. Where a person requests to substitute monetary payment for labour, approval from the village collective economic organization shall be required.  
For those who are unable to undertake compulsory rural labour or labour accumulation work due to illness or disability, after discussion and adoption by the general meeting of members of the village collective economic organization or the meeting of representatives of members, such labour may be reduced or exempted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　村提留和乡统筹费，实行全年统算统收制度，由村集体经济组织和乡人民政府组织收取。

**Article 16** Village retainage and township overall planning fees shall be subject to a system of unified calculation and collection for the whole year, and shall be collected by the village collective economic organization and the township people's government.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　村提留，由村集体经济组织每年年底作出当年决算方案并提出下一年度预算方案，经村集体经济组织成员大会或者成员代表会议讨论通过，报乡人民政府备案。讨论通过的预、决算方案，应当张榜公布，接受群众监督。  
村民委员会应当对村提留的收取和使用实施监督。

**Article 17** For village retainage, the village collective economic organization shall, at the end of each year, prepare a final account plan for the current year and propose a budget plan for the next year. After discussion and adoption by the general meeting of members of the village collective economic organization or the meeting of representatives of members, the plans shall be reported to the township people's government for the record. The budget and final account plans so adopted shall be posted for public scrutiny and subject to supervision by the masses.  
The villagers committee shall exercise supervision over the collection and use of village retainage.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　乡统筹费，由乡人民政府商乡集体经济组织每年年底作出当年决算方案并编制下一年度预算方案，经乡人民代表大会审议通过后，连同本乡范围内的村提留预算方案，一并报县级人民政府农民负担监督管理部门备案。讨论通过后的乡统筹费预、决算方案，应当张榜公布，接受群众监督。

**Article 18** For township overall planning fees, the township people's government shall, after consulting the township collective economic organization, prepare a final account plan for the current year and compile a budget plan for the next year at the end of each year. After deliberation and adoption by the township people's congress, these plans, together with the budget plan for village retainage within the township, shall be reported to the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden under the county people's government for the record. The budget and final account plans for township overall planning fees so adopted shall be posted for public scrutiny and subject to supervision by the masses.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　乡统筹费属于集体经济组织范围内全体农民所有，主要用于本乡民办公助事业，不得混淆和改变乡统筹费的集体资金性质和用途。

**Article 19** Township overall planning fees shall be owned by all peasants within the scope of the collective economic organization, and shall be mainly used for undertakings run by the people and subsidized by the state in the township. The nature of collective funds and the use of township overall planning fees shall not be confused or altered.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对村提留和乡统筹费，应当实行严格的财务管理制度。集体经济组织应当对村提留和乡统筹费的使用实行内部审计监督制度。

**Article 20** A strict financial management system shall be implemented for village retainage and township overall planning fees. The collective economic organization shall implement an internal audit and supervision system for the use of village retainage and township overall planning fees.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农村义务工和劳动积累工，由乡人民政府商村集体经济组织提出用工计划，经乡人民代表大会审议通过后执行，年终由村集体经济组织张榜公布用工情况，接受群众监督。

**Article 21** For compulsory rural labour and labour accumulation work, the township people's government shall, after consulting the village collective economic organization, put forward a plan for the use of labour. After deliberation and adoption by the township people's congress, the plan shall be implemented. At the end of the year, the village collective economic organization shall post the situation of labour use for public scrutiny and subject to supervision by the masses.

### 第四章　其他项目的监督管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　面向农民的行政事业性收费，其项目设置、标准的制定和调整，须经省、自治区、直辖市以上人民政府财政、物价主管部门会同农民负担监督管理部门批准，重要项目须经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 22** For administrative and institutional fees to be charged to peasants, the establishment of items and the formulation and adjustment of standards must be approved by the competent departments of finance and price under the people's government at or above the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, jointly with the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden; important items must be approved by the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　向农民集资，必须在法律、法规和国务院有关政策允许的范围内进行，并遵循自愿、适度、出资者受益、资金定向使用的原则。集资项目的设置和范围的确定，须经省、自治区、直辖市以上人民政府计划主管部门会同财政主管部门、农民负担监督管理部门批准，重要项目须经国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 23** Fundraising from peasants must be conducted within the scope permitted by laws, regulations and relevant policies of the State Council, and shall follow the principles of voluntariness, appropriateness, benefit to the contributors and targeted use of funds. The establishment of fundraising items and the determination of their scope must be approved by the competent department of planning under the people's government at or above the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, jointly with the competent department of finance and the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden; important items must be approved by the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在农村建立各种基金，须经国务院财政主管部门会同农民负担监督管理部门和有关主管部门批准，重要项目须经国务院批准。

**Article 24** The establishment of various funds in rural areas must be approved by the competent department of finance under the State Council, jointly with the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden and the relevant competent departments; important items must be approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　向农民发放牌照、证件和簿册，必须依照法律、法规的规定或者经省、自治区、直辖市以上人民政府批准。  
向农民发放牌照、证件和簿册，只准收取工本费。

**Article 25** The issuance of licenses, certificates and booklets to peasants must be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations or approved by the people's government at or above the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For the issuance of licenses, certificates and booklets to peasants, only the cost of production may be charged.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　向农民发行有价证券、报刊和书籍，应当遵循自愿原则，任何单位不得摊派。

**Article 26** The issuance of securities, newspapers, periodicals and books to peasants shall follow the principle of voluntariness, and no unit shall impose apportionment.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　组织农民参加保险，应当遵守法律、法规的规定。

**Article 27** Organizing peasants to participate in insurance shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　严禁非法对农民罚款和没收财物。

**Article 28** Imposing fines on peasants and confiscating their property illegally shall be strictly prohibited.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家机关工作人员在农村执行公务，所需经费不得向农民和集体经济组织摊派。

**Article 29** Where state organ personnel perform official duties in rural areas, the necessary expenses shall not be apportioned to peasants or collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　企业、事业单位为农民和集体经济组织提供经济、技术、劳务、信息等服务，应当遵循自愿原则。收取服务费用，应当按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 30** Where enterprises and institutions provide such services as economic, technical, labour and information services to peasants and collective economic organizations, they shall follow the principle of voluntariness. The collection of service fees shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何行政机关、事业单位在农村设置机构或者配备人员，所需经费不得向农民和集体经济组织摊派。

**Article 31** Where any administrative organ or institution establishes an organ or assigns personnel in rural areas, the necessary expenses shall not be apportioned to peasants or collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　任何单位和个人都有权举报违反本条例的行为。农民负担监督管理部门和有关主管部门接到举报后，必须及时查处或者提请同级人民政府依法处理。

**Article 32** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act violating these Regulations. Upon receiving a report, the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden or the relevant competent department must promptly investigate and handle the matter or submit it to the people's government at the same level for handling according to law.

### 第五章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有下列事迹之一的单位和个人，可以由人民政府给予表彰或者奖励：  
(一)严格执行本条例规定，切实减轻农民负担的；  
(二)在农民负担监督管理工作中，认真履行本条例规定职责，成绩显著的；  
(三)检举、揭发向农民乱收费、乱集资、乱罚款和各种摊派行为，有突出贡献的。

**Article 33** Units or individuals that have any of the following achievements may be commended or rewarded by the people's government:  
(1) strictly implementing the provisions of these Regulations and effectively reducing the burden on peasants;  
(2) conscientiously performing the duties specified in these Regulations in the supervision and administration of peasants' burden and achieving outstanding results;  
(3) making outstanding contributions in reporting and exposing acts of arbitrary collection of fees, arbitrary fundraising, arbitrary fines and various apportionments from peasants.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定设置的收费、集资和基金项目，由农民负担监督管理部门或者有关部门报请同级人民政府予以撤销。

**Article 34** Where any charge, fundraising or fund item is established in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden or the relevant department shall submit it to the people's government at the same level for cancellation.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定向农民和集体经济组织收费、集资和进行各种摊派的，由农民负担监督管理部门或者有关部门报请同级人民政府责令如数退还非法收取的款物。

**Article 35** Where any unit collects fees from or conducts fundraising or various apportionments to peasants or collective economic organizations in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden or the relevant department shall submit it to the people's government at the same level for ordering the full return of the illegally collected money or goods.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例和国务院有关规定增加的农村义务工和劳动积累工，经乡农民负担监督管理部门核实，由乡人民政府在下一年度用工计划中扣减，或者由用工单位按标准工日给予农民出工补贴。

**Article 36** Where the amount of compulsory rural labour or labour accumulation work is increased in violation of these Regulations or the relevant provisions of the State Council, after verification by the township supervision and administration department for peasants' burden, the township people's government shall deduct such amount from the labour use plan for the next year, or the employing unit shall give peasants a subsidy for their labour at the standard work-day rate.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对违反本条例规定的单位负责人和直接责任人员，由农民负担监督管理部门提请上述人员所在单位或者有关主管机关给予行政处分。

**Article 37** For persons in charge of units and persons directly responsible who violate the provisions of these Regulations, the supervision and administration department for peasants' burden shall submit a request to the unit to which the above-mentioned persons belong or the competent authority concerned for imposing administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对检举、揭发、控告和抵制向农民乱收费、乱集资、乱罚款和进行各种摊派的单位和人员打击报复，属于违反《中华人民共和国行政监察条例》的，由行政监察机关依法处理；属于违反《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》的，由公安机关依法处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where any unit or person that reports, exposes, accuses or resists arbitrary collection of fees, arbitrary fundraising, arbitrary fines and various apportionments from peasants is subjected to retaliation, if such act constitutes a violation of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Supervision, it shall be handled by the administrative supervision organ according to law; if it constitutes a violation of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Penalties for Public Security, it shall be punished by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　各省、自治区、直辖市可以根据本条例，结合本地的实际情况，制定实施细则。

**Article 39** The provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Regulations and in light of their local actual conditions, formulate implementing rules.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例由国务院农业行政主管部门负责解释，并组织实施。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the administrative department for agriculture under the State Council, which shall also be responsible for their implementation.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
