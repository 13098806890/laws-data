# 中央储备粮管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2016-02-06

> (2003年8月15日中华人民共和国国务院令第388号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对中央储备粮的管理，保证中央储备粮数量真实、质量良好和储存安全，保护农民利益，维护粮食市场稳定，有效发挥中央储备粮在国家宏观调控中的作用，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称中央储备粮，是指中央政府储备的用于调节全国粮食供求总量，稳定粮食市场，以及应对重大自然灾害或者其他突发事件等情况的粮食和食用油。 第三条　从事和参与中央储备粮经营管理、监督活动的单位和个人，必须遵守本条例。 第四条　国家实行中央储备粮垂直管理体制，地方各级人民政府及有关部门应当对中央储备粮的垂直管理给予支持和协助。 第五条　中央储备粮的管理应当严格制度、严格管理、严格责任，确保中央储备粮数量真实、质量良好和储存安全，确保中央储备粮储得进、管得好、调得动、用得上并节约成本、费用。 未经国务院批准，任何单位和个人不得擅自动用中央储备粮。 第六条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门负责拟订中央储备粮规模总量、总体布局和动用的宏观调控意见，对中央储备粮管理进行指导和协调；国家粮食行政管理部门负责中央储备粮的行政管理，对中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全实施监督检查。 第七条　国务院财政部门负责安排中央储备粮的贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴，并保证及时、足额拨付；负责对中央储备粮有关财务执行情况实施监督检查。 第八条　中国储备粮管理总公司具体负责中央储备粮的经营管理，并对中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全负责。 中国储备粮管理总公司依照国家有关中央储备粮管理的行政法规、规章、国家标准和技术规范，建立、健全中央储备粮各项业务管理制度，并报国家粮食行政管理部门备案。 第九条　中国农业发展银行负责按照国家有关规定，及时、足额安排中央储备粮所需贷款，并对发放的中央储备粮贷款实施信贷监管。 第十条　任何单位和个人不得以任何方式骗取、挤占、截留、挪用中央储备粮贷款或者贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴。 第十一条　任何单位和个人不得破坏中央储备粮的仓储设施，不得偷盗、哄抢或者损毁中央储备粮。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对中央储备粮的管理，保证中央储备粮数量真实、质量良好和储存安全，保护农民利益，维护粮食市场稳定，有效发挥中央储备粮在国家宏观调控中的作用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of central grain reserves, ensuring the authenticity of quantity, good quality and storage safety of central grain reserves, protecting the interests of farmers, maintaining stability of the grain market, and effectively playing the role of central grain reserves in the national macro-control.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称中央储备粮，是指中央政府储备的用于调节全国粮食供求总量，稳定粮食市场，以及应对重大自然灾害或者其他突发事件等情况的粮食和食用油。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "central grain reserves" means grain and edible oil reserved by the Central Government for regulating the total supply and demand of grain nationwide, stabilizing the grain market, and responding to major natural disasters or other emergencies.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事和参与中央储备粮经营管理、监督活动的单位和个人，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 3** All entities and individuals that engage in or participate in the operation, management and supervision of central grain reserves must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行中央储备粮垂直管理体制，地方各级人民政府及有关部门应当对中央储备粮的垂直管理给予支持和协助。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a vertical management system for central grain reserves. Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall provide support and assistance for the vertical management of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中央储备粮的管理应当严格制度、严格管理、严格责任，确保中央储备粮数量真实、质量良好和储存安全，确保中央储备粮储得进、管得好、调得动、用得上并节约成本、费用。  
未经国务院批准，任何单位和个人不得擅自动用中央储备粮。

**Article 5** The management of central grain reserves shall be subject to strict systems, strict management and strict accountability, so as to ensure the authenticity of quantity, good quality and storage safety of central grain reserves, and ensure that central grain reserves can be stored, managed, transferred and used effectively and economically.  
Without approval of the State Council, no entity or individual may unauthorizedly use the central grain reserves.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门负责拟订中央储备粮规模总量、总体布局和动用的宏观调控意见，对中央储备粮管理进行指导和协调；国家粮食行政管理部门负责中央储备粮的行政管理，对中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全实施监督检查。

**Article 6** The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department shall, together with the finance department of the State Council, be responsible for drafting opinions on the total quantity, overall layout and use of central grain reserves for macro-control purposes, and for directing and coordinating the administration of central grain reserves; the national grain administrative department shall be responsible for the administrative management of central grain reserves, and for supervisory inspection of the quantity, quality and storage safety of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院财政部门负责安排中央储备粮的贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴，并保证及时、足额拨付；负责对中央储备粮有关财务执行情况实施监督检查。

**Article 7** The finance department of the State Council shall be responsible for arranging financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses for central grain reserves, and for ensuring timely and full appropriation; and shall be responsible for supervisory inspection of the financial execution related to central grain reserves.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中国储备粮管理总公司具体负责中央储备粮的经营管理，并对中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全负责。  
中国储备粮管理总公司依照国家有关中央储备粮管理的行政法规、规章、国家标准和技术规范，建立、健全中央储备粮各项业务管理制度，并报国家粮食行政管理部门备案。

**Article 8** China Grain Reserves Corporation shall be specifically responsible for the operation and management of central grain reserves, and shall be responsible for the quantity, quality and storage safety of central grain reserves.  
China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations, rules, national standards and technical specifications of the State on the administration of central grain reserves, establish and improve all business management systems for central grain reserves, and report them to the national grain administrative department for recordation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国农业发展银行负责按照国家有关规定，及时、足额安排中央储备粮所需贷款，并对发放的中央储备粮贷款实施信贷监管。

**Article 9** Agricultural Development Bank of China shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, arrange timely and full loans required for central grain reserves, and shall carry out credit supervision over the loans granted for central grain reserves.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　任何单位和个人不得以任何方式骗取、挤占、截留、挪用中央储备粮贷款或者贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴。

**Article 10** No entity or individual may, by any means, defraud, embezzle, intercept or misappropriate loans for central grain reserves or financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人不得破坏中央储备粮的仓储设施，不得偷盗、哄抢或者损毁中央储备粮。  
中央储备粮储存地的地方人民政府对破坏中央储备粮仓储设施，偷盗、哄抢或者损毁中央储备粮的违法行为，应当及时组织有关部门予以制止、查处。

**Article 11** No entity or individual may damage the storage facilities of central grain reserves, or steal, loot or destroy central grain reserves.  
The local people's government at the place where central grain reserves are stored shall, in a timely manner, organize relevant departments to stop and investigate and deal with illegal acts of damaging storage facilities of central grain reserves, or stealing, looting or destroying central grain reserves.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位和个人对中央储备粮经营管理中的违法行为，均有权向国家粮食行政管理部门等有关部门举报。国家粮食行政管理部门等有关部门接到举报后，应当及时查处；举报事项的处理属于其他部门职责范围的，应当及时移送其他部门处理。

**Article 12** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report to the national grain administrative department and other relevant departments any illegal acts in the operation and management of central grain reserves. Upon receipt of such a report, the national grain administrative department and other relevant departments shall promptly investigate and handle the matter; if the handling of the reported matters falls within the functions of another department, they shall promptly transfer the matter to that other department for handling.

### 第二章　中央储备粮的计划

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中央储备粮的储存规模、品种和总体布局方案，由国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门，根据国家宏观调控需要和财政承受能力提出，报国务院批准。

**Article 13** The plan for the storage size, variety and overall layout of central grain reserves shall be proposed by the development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department, together with the finance department of the State Council, based on the needs of national macro-control and financial affordability, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中央储备粮的收购、销售计划，由国家粮食行政管理部门根据国务院批准的中央储备粮储存规模、品种和总体布局方案提出建议，经国务院发展改革部门、国务院财政部门审核同意后，由国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门和中国农业发展银行共同下达中国储备粮管理总公司。

**Article 14** The plans for the purchase and sale of central grain reserves shall be proposed by the national grain administrative department in accordance with the plan for the storage size, variety and overall layout of central grain reserves approved by the State Council; after examination and approval by the development and reform department of the State Council and the finance department of the State Council, they shall be jointly issued to China Grain Reserves Corporation by the development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department, together with the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中国储备粮管理总公司根据中央储备粮的收购、销售计划，具体组织实施中央储备粮的收购、销售。

**Article 15** China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, in accordance with the plans for the purchase and sale of central grain reserves, specifically organize the implementation of the purchase and sale of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中央储备粮实行均衡轮换制度，每年轮换的数量一般为中央储备粮储存总量的20%至30%。  
中国储备粮管理总公司应当根据中央储备粮的品质情况和入库年限，提出中央储备粮年度轮换的数量、品种和分地区计划，报国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门和中国农业发展银行批准。中国储备粮管理总公司在年度轮换计划内根据粮食市场供求状况，具体组织实施中央储备粮的轮换。

**Article 16** Central grain reserves shall be subject to a system of balanced rotation. The quantity of rotation each year shall generally be 20% to 30% of the total storage of central grain reserves.  
China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, based on the quality and storage years of central grain reserves, propose the quantity, variety and region-specific plan for the annual rotation of central grain reserves, and submit it to the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China for approval. China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, within the annual rotation plan and according to the supply and demand situation of grain market, specifically organize the implementation of the rotation of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中国储备粮管理总公司应当将中央储备粮收购、销售、年度轮换计划的具体执行情况，及时报国务院发展改革部门、国家粮食行政管理部门和国务院财政部门备案，并抄送中国农业发展银行。

**Article 17** China Grain Reserves Corporation shall promptly report the specific implementation status of the purchase, sale and annual rotation plans for central grain reserves to the development and reform department of the State Council, the national grain administrative department and the finance department of the State Council for recordation, and shall send a copy to Agricultural Development Bank of China.

### 第三章　中央储备粮的储存

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　中国储备粮管理总公司直属企业为专门储存中央储备粮的企业。  
中央储备粮也可以依照本条例的规定由具备条件的其他企业代储。

**Article 18** The enterprises directly under China Grain Reserves Corporation shall be enterprises specialized in storing central grain reserves.  
Central grain reserves may also be stored on behalf of others by other enterprises that meet the requirements in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　代储中央储备粮的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)仓库容量达到国家规定的规模，仓库条件符合国家标准和技术规范的要求；  
(二)具有与粮食储存功能、仓型、进出粮方式、粮食品种、储粮周期等相适应的仓储设备；  
(三)具有符合国家标准的中央储备粮质量等级检测仪器和场所，具备检测中央储备粮储存期间仓库内温度、水分、害虫密度的条件；  
(四)具有经过专业培训的粮油保管员、粮油质量检验员等管理技术人员；  
(五)经营管理和信誉良好，并无严重违法经营记录。  
选择代储中央储备粮的企业，应当遵循有利于中央储备粮的合理布局，有利于中央储备粮的集中管理和监督，有利于降低中央储备粮成本、费用的原则。

**Article 19** An enterprise that stores central grain reserves on behalf of others shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) its warehouse capacity meets the size prescribed by the State, and the warehouse conditions meet the requirements of national standards and technical specifications;  
(2) it possesses storage equipment compatible with the grain storage function, warehouse type, grain intake and outtake methods, grain variety, storage cycle, etc.;  
(3) it possesses testing instruments and premises for the quality grade of central grain reserves that meet national standards, and has the conditions for testing temperature, moisture content and pest density in the warehouse during storage of central grain reserves;  
(4) it has management and technical personnel such as grain and oil custodians and grain and oil quality inspectors who have received professional training;  
(5) it has good operation management and reputation, and has no record of serious illegal business operations.  
The selection of enterprises for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others shall follow the principles of facilitating the rational layout of central grain reserves, facilitating the centralized management and supervision of central grain reserves, and facilitating the reduction of costs and expenses of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　具备[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定代储条件的企业，经国家粮食行政管理部门审核同意，取得代储中央储备粮的资格。  
企业代储中央储备粮的资格认定办法，由国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门，并征求中国农业发展银行和中国储备粮管理总公司的意见制定。

**Article 20** An enterprise that meets the conditions for storage on behalf of others as prescribed in Article 19 of these Regulations may, upon examination and approval by the national grain administrative department, obtain the qualification for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others.  
The measures for determining the qualification of enterprises for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others shall be formulated by the national grain administrative department, together with the finance department of the State Council, after soliciting opinions from Agricultural Development Bank of China and China Grain Reserves Corporation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　中国储备粮管理总公司负责从取得代储中央储备粮资格的企业中，根据中央储备粮的总体布局方案择优选定中央储备粮代储企业，报国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门和中国农业发展银行备案，并抄送当地粮食行政管理部门。  
中国储备粮管理总公司应当与中央储备粮代储企业签订合同，明确双方的权利、义务和违约责任等事项。  
中央储备粮代储企业不得将中央储备粮轮换业务与其他业务混合经营。

**Article 21** China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, from among the enterprises that have obtained the qualification for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others, select the enterprises for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others according to the overall layout plan for central grain reserves, and shall report to the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China for recordation, and send a copy to the local grain administrative department.  
China Grain Reserves Corporation shall conclude contracts with the enterprises storing central grain reserves on behalf of others, specifying the rights, obligations and liabilities for breach of contract of both parties.  
An enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others may not mix the rotation business of central grain reserves with other businesses.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中国储备粮管理总公司直属企业、中央储备粮代储企业(以下统称承储企业)储存中央储备粮，应当严格执行国家有关中央储备粮管理的行政法规、规章、国家标准和技术规范，以及中国储备粮管理总公司依照有关行政法规、规章、国家标准和技术规范制定的各项业务管理制度。

**Article 22** The enterprises directly under China Grain Reserves Corporation and the enterprises storing central grain reserves on behalf of others (hereinafter collectively referred to as "storage enterprises") shall strictly implement the relevant administrative regulations, rules, national standards and technical specifications of the State on the administration of central grain reserves, as well as the various business management systems formulated by China Grain Reserves Corporation in accordance with the relevant administrative regulations, rules, national standards and technical specifications.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承储企业必须保证入库的中央储备粮达到收购、轮换计划规定的质量等级，并符合国家规定的质量标准。

**Article 23** A storage enterprise must ensure that the central grain reserves stored in the warehouse meet the quality grades specified in the purchase and rotation plans and conform to the quality standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　承储企业应当对中央储备粮实行专仓储存、专人保管、专账记载，保证中央储备粮账账相符、账实相符、质量良好、储存安全。

**Article 24** A storage enterprise shall store central grain reserves in dedicated warehouses, assign dedicated personnel for custody, and keep dedicated accounts, so as to ensure that the accounts of central grain reserves are consistent with each other and with the actual storage, that the quality is good, and that storage is safe.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　承储企业不得虚报、瞒报中央储备粮的数量，不得在中央储备粮中掺杂掺假、以次充好，不得擅自串换中央储备粮的品种、变更中央储备粮的储存地点，不得因延误轮换或者管理不善造成中央储备粮陈化、霉变。

**Article 25** A storage enterprise may not falsely report or conceal the quantity of central grain reserves, may not adulterate or substitute inferior commodities for superior ones in central grain reserves, may not unauthorizedly change the variety of central grain reserves or alter the storage location of central grain reserves, and may not cause the ageing or mildew of central grain reserves due to delayed rotation or poor management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　承储企业不得以低价购进高价入账、高价售出低价入账、以旧粮顶替新粮、虚增入库成本等手段套取差价，骗取中央储备粮贷款和贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴。

**Article 26** A storage enterprise may not, by means of purchasing at a low price but recording at a high price, selling at a high price but recording at a low price, substituting old grain for new grain, or falsely increasing storage cost, obtain price differences, or defraud loans for central grain reserves and financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　承储企业应当建立、健全中央储备粮的防火、防盗、防洪等安全管理制度，并配备必要的安全防护设施。  
地方各级人民政府应当支持本行政区域内的承储企业做好中央储备粮的安全管理工作。

**Article 27** A storage enterprise shall establish and improve safety management systems for central grain reserves such as fire prevention, theft prevention, and flood prevention, and shall be equipped with necessary safety protection facilities.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall support the storage enterprises within their respective administrative regions in carrying out the safety management of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　承储企业应当对中央储备粮的储存管理状况进行经常性检查；发现中央储备粮数量、质量和储存安全等方面的问题，应当及时处理；不能处理的，承储企业的主要负责人必须及时报告中国储备粮管理总公司或者其分支机构。

**Article 28** A storage enterprise shall conduct regular inspections of the storage management status of central grain reserves; if any problem concerning the quantity, quality or storage safety of central grain reserves is found, it shall be promptly dealt with; if it cannot be dealt with, the principal person in charge of the storage enterprise must promptly report to China Grain Reserves Corporation or its branch.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　承储企业应当在轮换计划规定的时间内完成中央储备粮的轮换。  
中央储备粮的轮换应当遵循有利于保证中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全，保持粮食市场稳定，防止造成市场粮价剧烈波动，节约成本、提高效率的原则。  
中央储备粮轮换的具体管理办法，由国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门，并征求中国农业发展银行和中国储备粮管理总公司的意见制定。

**Article 29** A storage enterprise shall complete the rotation of central grain reserves within the time limit specified in the rotation plan.  
The rotation of central grain reserves shall follow the principles of ensuring the quantity, quality and storage safety of central grain reserves, maintaining stability of the grain market, preventing drastic fluctuations in market grain prices, and saving costs and improving efficiency.  
The specific management measures for the rotation of central grain reserves shall be formulated by the development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department, together with the finance department of the State Council, after soliciting opinions from Agricultural Development Bank of China and China Grain Reserves Corporation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　中央储备粮的收购、销售、轮换原则上应当通过规范的粮食批发市场公开进行，也可以通过国家规定的其他方式进行。

**Article 30** The purchase, sale and rotation of central grain reserves shall, in principle, be conducted openly through standardized grain wholesale markets, or may be conducted through other means prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　承储企业不得以中央储备粮对外进行担保或者对外清偿债务。  
承储企业依法被撤销、解散或者破产的，其储存的中央储备粮由中国储备粮管理总公司负责调出另储。

**Article 31** A storage enterprise may not use central grain reserves as security for external guarantees or for repaying external debts.  
If a storage enterprise is lawfully dissolved, disbanded or declared bankrupt, China Grain Reserves Corporation shall be responsible for transferring the central grain reserves stored by it to another storage facility.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　中央储备粮的管理费用补贴实行定额包干，由国务院财政部门拨付给中国储备粮管理总公司；中国储备粮管理总公司按照国务院财政部门的有关规定，通过中国农业发展银行补贴专户，及时、足额拨付到承储企业。中国储备粮管理总公司在中央储备粮管理费用补贴包干总额内，可以根据不同储存条件和实际费用水平，适当调整不同地区、不同品种、不同承储企业的管理费用补贴标准；但同一地区、同一品种、储存条件基本相同的承储企业的管理费用补贴标准原则上应当一致。  
中央储备粮的贷款利息实行据实补贴，由国务院财政部门拨付。

**Article 32** The subsidy for administrative expenses of central grain reserves shall be subject to a fixed lump-sum system, and shall be appropriated by the finance department of the State Council to China Grain Reserves Corporation; China Grain Reserves Corporation shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the finance department of the State Council, appropriate the subsidy promptly and in full to the storage enterprises through the dedicated subsidy account with Agricultural Development Bank of China. Within the total amount of the lump-sum subsidy for administrative expenses of central grain reserves, China Grain Reserves Corporation may appropriately adjust the administrative expense subsidy standards for different regions, different varieties and different storage enterprises according to different storage conditions and actual cost levels; however, the administrative expense subsidy standards for storage enterprises with basically the same storage conditions in the same region and for the same variety shall, in principle, be consistent.  
The loan interest for central grain reserves shall be subsidized based on actual expenditure, and shall be appropriated by the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　中央储备粮贷款实行贷款与粮食库存值增减挂钩和专户管理、专款专用。  
承储企业应当在中国农业发展银行开立基本账户，并接受中国农业发展银行的信贷监管。  
中国储备粮管理总公司应当创造条件，逐步实行中央储备粮贷款统借统还。

**Article 33** Loans for central grain reserves shall be linked to the increase or decrease of the value of grain inventory, and shall be subject to dedicated account management and dedicated use.  
A storage enterprise shall open a basic account with Agricultural Development Bank of China and accept the credit supervision of Agricultural Development Bank of China.  
China Grain Reserves Corporation shall create conditions to gradually implement unified borrowing and repayment of loans for central grain reserves.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　中央储备粮的入库成本由国务院财政部门负责核定。中央储备粮的入库成本一经核定，中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构和承储企业必须遵照执行。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自更改中央储备粮入库成本。

**Article 34** The storage cost of central grain reserves shall be verified and determined by the finance department of the State Council. Once verified and determined, China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches and the storage enterprises must comply with it.  
No entity or individual may unauthorizedly change the storage cost of central grain reserves.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家建立中央储备粮损失、损耗处理制度，及时处理所发生的损失、损耗。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国家粮食行政管理部门，并征求中国储备粮管理总公司和中国农业发展银行的意见制定。

**Article 35** The State shall establish a system for handling losses and waste of central grain reserves, and shall promptly handle any losses and waste that occur. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council, together with the national grain administrative department, after soliciting opinions from China Grain Reserves Corporation and Agricultural Development Bank of China.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　中国储备粮管理总公司应当定期统计、分析中央储备粮的储存管理情况，并将统计、分析情况报送国务院发展改革部门、国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门及中国农业发展银行。

**Article 36** China Grain Reserves Corporation shall regularly compile statistics and analyze the storage management status of central grain reserves, and shall submit the statistical and analysis results to the development and reform department of the State Council, the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China.

### 第四章　中央储备粮的动用

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门，应当完善中央储备粮的动用预警机制，加强对需要动用中央储备粮情况的监测，适时提出动用中央储备粮的建议。

**Article 37** The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department shall improve the early warning mechanism for the use of central grain reserves, strengthen monitoring of situations requiring the use of central grain reserves, and timely propose suggestions for using central grain reserves.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　出现下列情况之一的，可以动用中央储备粮：  
(一)全国或者部分地区粮食明显供不应求或者市场价格异常波动；  
(二)发生重大自然灾害或者其他突发事件需要动用中央储备粮；  
(三)国务院认为需要动用中央储备粮的其他情形。

**Article 38** Central grain reserves may be used under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) there is a clear shortage of supply or abnormal fluctuation of market prices in the whole country or in some regions;  
(2) major natural disasters or other emergencies occur that require the use of central grain reserves;  
(3) other circumstances under which the State Council considers it necessary to use central grain reserves.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　动用中央储备粮，由国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门会同国务院财政部门提出动用方案，报国务院批准。动用方案应当包括动用中央储备粮的品种、数量、质量、价格、使用安排、运输保障等内容。

**Article 39** For the use of central grain reserves, the development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department shall, together with the finance department of the State Council, propose a plan for use, and submit it to the State Council for approval. The plan for use shall include the variety, quantity, quality, price, arrangements for use, transportation guarantee, and other relevant contents of the central grain reserves to be used.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院发展改革部门及国家粮食行政管理部门，根据国务院批准的中央储备粮动用方案下达动用命令，由中国储备粮管理总公司具体组织实施。  
紧急情况下，国务院直接决定动用中央储备粮并下达动用命令。  
国务院有关部门和有关地方人民政府对中央储备粮动用命令的实施，应当给予支持、配合。

**Article 40** The development and reform department of the State Council and the national grain administrative department shall, based on the plan for use of central grain reserves approved by the State Council, issue a use order, and China Grain Reserves Corporation shall specifically organize the implementation.  
In emergency circumstances, the State Council shall directly decide to use central grain reserves and issue a use order.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant local people's governments shall provide support and cooperation for the implementation of the use order for central grain reserves.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何单位和个人不得拒绝执行或者擅自改变中央储备粮动用命令。

**Article 41** No entity or individual may refuse to execute or unauthorizedly alter the use order for central grain reserves.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门按照各自职责，依法对中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构、承储企业执行本条例及有关粮食法规的情况，进行监督检查。在监督检查过程中，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)进入承储企业检查中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全；  
(二)向有关单位和人员了解中央储备粮收购、销售、轮换计划及动用命令的执行情况；  
(三)调阅中央储备粮经营管理的有关资料、凭证；  
(四)对违法行为，依法予以处理。

**Article 42** The national grain administrative department and the finance department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and according to law, conduct supervisory inspection over the implementation of these Regulations and relevant grain laws and regulations by China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches, and the storage enterprises. In the course of supervisory inspection, they may exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) entering into storage enterprises to inspect the quantity, quality and storage safety of central grain reserves;  
(2) inquiring of relevant entities and persons about the implementation of the purchase, sale and rotation plans and the use order for central grain reserves;  
(3) consulting relevant materials and vouchers for the operation and management of central grain reserves;  
(4) handling illegal acts according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门在监督检查中，发现中央储备粮数量、质量、储存安全等方面存在问题，应当责成中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构、承储企业立即予以纠正或者处理；发现中央储备粮代储企业不再具备代储条件，国家粮食行政管理部门应当取消其代储资格；发现中国储备粮管理总公司直属企业存在不适于储存中央储备粮的情况，国家粮食行政管理部门应当责成中国储备粮管理总公司对有关直属企业限期整改。

**Article 43** If during supervisory inspection, the national grain administrative department and the finance department of the State Council find problems in the quantity, quality or storage safety of central grain reserves, they shall instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches, and the storage enterprises to immediately rectify or handle them; if they find that an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others no longer meets the conditions for storage on behalf of others, the national grain administrative department shall revoke its qualification for storage on behalf of others; if they find that an enterprise directly under China Grain Reserves Corporation is not suitable for storing central grain reserves, the national grain administrative department shall instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order the relevant directly-affiliated enterprise to rectify within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门的监督检查人员应当将监督检查情况作出书面记录，并由监督检查人员和被检查单位的负责人签字。被检查单位的负责人拒绝签字的，监督检查人员应当将有关情况记录在案。

**Article 44** The supervisory inspection personnel of the national grain administrative department and the finance department of the State Council shall make written records of the supervisory inspection, and such records shall be signed by the supervisory inspection personnel and the person in charge of the inspected entity. If the person in charge of the inspected entity refuses to sign, the supervisory inspection personnel shall record the relevant circumstances in the file.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　审计机关依照审计法规定的职权和程序，对有关中央储备粮的财务收支情况实施审计监督；发现问题，应当及时予以处理。

**Article 45** Audit authorities shall, in accordance with the functions and procedures prescribed by the Audit Law, carry out audit supervision over the financial revenues and expenditures related to central grain reserves; if problems are found, they shall promptly handle them.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构、承储企业，对国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门、审计机关的监督检查人员依法履行职责，应当予以配合。  
任何单位和个人不得拒绝、阻挠、干涉国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门、审计机关的监督检查人员依法履行监督检查职责。

**Article 46** China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches, and the storage enterprises shall cooperate with the supervisory inspection personnel of the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and the audit authorities in performing their duties according to law.  
No entity or individual may refuse, obstruct or interfere with the supervisory inspection personnel of the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and the audit authorities in performing their supervisory inspection duties according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构应当加强对中央储备粮的经营管理和检查，对中央储备粮的数量、质量存在的问题，应当及时予以纠正；对危及中央储备粮储存安全的重大问题，应当立即采取有效措施予以处理，并报告国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门及中国农业发展银行。

**Article 47** China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches shall strengthen the operation management and inspection of central grain reserves, and shall promptly rectify any problems in the quantity or quality of central grain reserves; with respect to major problems endangering the storage safety of central grain reserves, they shall immediately take effective measures to deal with them, and report to the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　中国农业发展银行应当按照资金封闭管理的规定，加强对中央储备粮贷款的信贷监管。中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构、承储企业对中国农业发展银行依法进行的信贷监管，应当予以配合，并及时提供有关资料和情况。

**Article 48** Agricultural Development Bank of China shall, in accordance with the provisions on closed management of funds, strengthen the credit supervision over loans for central grain reserves. China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches, and the storage enterprises shall cooperate with Agricultural Development Bank of China in carrying out credit supervision according to law, and shall promptly provide relevant materials and information.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家机关工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，给予警告直至撤职的行政处分；情节严重的，给予降级直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不及时下达中央储备粮收购、销售及年度轮换计划的；  
(二)给予不具备代储条件的企业代储中央储备粮资格，或者发现中央储备粮代储企业不再具备代储条件不及时取消其代储资格的；  
(三)发现中国储备粮管理总公司直属企业存在不适于储存中央储备粮的情况不责成中国储备粮管理总公司对其限期整改的；  
(四)接到举报、发现违法行为不及时查处的。

**Article 49** Where any functionary of a State organ violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, he shall be given an administrative sanction ranging from a warning to removal from office; if the circumstances are serious, he shall be given an administrative sanction ranging from demotion to dismissal; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to issue in a timely manner the plans for purchase, sale and annual rotation of central grain reserves;  
(2) granting the qualification for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others to an enterprise that does not meet the conditions for storage on behalf of others, or failing to revoke the qualification for storage on behalf of others in a timely manner upon discovering that an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others no longer meets the conditions;  
(3) upon discovering that an enterprise directly under China Grain Reserves Corporation is not suitable for storing central grain reserves, failing to instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit;  
(4) upon receiving a report or discovering an illegal act, failing to investigate and handle it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国家粮食行政管理部门责令改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，责成中国储备粮管理总公司给予警告直至撤职的纪律处分；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒不组织实施或者擅自改变中央储备粮收购、销售、年度轮换计划及动用命令的；  
(二)选择未取得代储中央储备粮资格的企业代储中央储备粮的；  
(三)发现中央储备粮的数量、质量存在问题不及时纠正，或者发现危及中央储备粮储存安全的重大问题，不立即采取有效措施处理并按照规定报告的；  
(四)拒绝、阻挠、干涉国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门、审计机关的监督检查人员依法履行监督检查职责的。

**Article 50** Where China Grain Reserves Corporation or any of its branches violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the national grain administrative department shall order it to make corrections; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be instructed by China Grain Reserves Corporation to be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from a warning to removal from office; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from demotion to dismissal; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) refusing to organize the implementation of or unauthorizedly altering the plans for purchase, sale and annual rotation of central grain reserves or the use order;  
(2) selecting an enterprise that has not obtained the qualification for storing central grain reserves on behalf of others to store central grain reserves on behalf of others;  
(3) upon discovering problems in the quantity or quality of central grain reserves, failing to correct them in a timely manner, or upon discovering major problems endangering the storage safety of central grain reserves, failing to immediately take effective measures to deal with them and report them as required;  
(4) refusing, obstructing or interfering with the supervisory inspection personnel of the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council or the audit authorities in performing their supervisory inspection duties according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　承储企业违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国家粮食行政管理部门责成中国储备粮管理总公司对其限期改正；情节严重的，对中央储备粮代储企业，还应当取消其代储资格；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)入库的中央储备粮不符合质量等级和国家标准要求的；  
(二)对中央储备粮未实行专仓储存、专人保管、专账记载，中央储备粮账账不符、账实不符的；  
(三)发现中央储备粮的数量、质量和储存安全等方面的问题不及时处理，或者处理不了不及时报告的；  
(四)拒绝、阻挠、干涉国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门、审计机关的监督检查人员或者中国储备粮管理总公司的检查人员依法履行职责的。

**Article 51** Where a storage enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the national grain administrative department shall instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if the circumstances are serious, with respect to an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others, its qualification for storage on behalf of others shall also be revoked; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from a warning to dismissal; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) the central grain reserves stored in the warehouse do not meet the quality grade and national standard requirements;  
(2) failing to store central grain reserves in dedicated warehouses, assign dedicated personnel for custody, and keep dedicated accounts, or the accounts of central grain reserves are inconsistent with each other or with the actual storage;  
(3) upon discovering problems in the quantity, quality or storage safety of central grain reserves, failing to deal with them in a timely manner, or failing to report in a timely manner if they cannot be dealt with;  
(4) refusing, obstructing or interfering with the supervisory inspection personnel of the national grain administrative department, the finance department of the State Council, the audit authorities or the inspection personnel of China Grain Reserves Corporation in performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　承储企业违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国家粮食行政管理部门责成中国储备粮管理总公司对其限期改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分；对其他直接责任人员给予警告直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；对中央储备粮代储企业，取消其代储资格：  
(一)虚报、瞒报中央储备粮数量的；  
(二)在中央储备粮中掺杂掺假、以次充好的；  
(三)擅自串换中央储备粮的品种、变更中央储备粮储存地点的；  
(四)造成中央储备粮陈化、霉变的；  
(五)拒不执行或者擅自改变中央储备粮收购、销售、轮换计划和动用命令的；  
(六)擅自动用中央储备粮的；  
(七)以中央储备粮对外进行担保或者清偿债务的。

**Article 52** Where a storage enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the national grain administrative department shall instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from demotion to dismissal; other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from a warning to dismissal; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; with respect to an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others, its qualification for storage on behalf of others shall be revoked:  
(1) falsely reporting or concealing the quantity of central grain reserves;  
(2) adulterating or substituting inferior commodities for superior ones in central grain reserves;  
(3) unauthorizedly changing the variety of central grain reserves or altering the storage location of central grain reserves;  
(4) causing the ageing or mildew of central grain reserves;  
(5) refusing to execute or unauthorizedly altering the plans for purchase, sale and rotation of central grain reserves and the use order;  
(6) unauthorizedly using central grain reserves;  
(7) providing security or repaying debts with central grain reserves.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　承储企业违反本条例规定，以低价购进高价入账、高价售出低价入账、以旧粮顶替新粮、虚增入库成本等手段套取差价，骗取中央储备粮贷款和贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴的，由国家粮食行政管理部门、国务院财政部门按照各自职责责成中国储备粮管理总公司对其限期改正，并责令退回骗取的中央储备粮贷款和贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员给予降级直至开除的纪律处分；对其他直接责任人员给予警告直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；对中央储备粮代储企业，取消其代储资格。

**Article 53** Where a storage enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations and, by means of purchasing at a low price but recording at a high price, selling at a high price but recording at a low price, substituting old grain for new grain, or falsely increasing storage cost, obtains price differences or defrauds loans for central grain reserves and financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses, the national grain administrative department and the finance department of the State Council shall, within their respective functions, instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit, and order it to return the defrauded loans for central grain reserves and financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from demotion to dismissal; other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from a warning to dismissal; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; with respect to an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others, its qualification for storage on behalf of others shall be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　中央储备粮代储企业将中央储备粮轮换业务与其他业务混合经营的，由国家粮食行政管理部门责成中国储备粮管理总公司对其限期改正；对直接负责的主管人员给予警告直至降级的纪律处分；造成中央储备粮损失的，对直接负责的主管人员给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分，并取消其代储资格。

**Article 54** Where an enterprise storing central grain reserves on behalf of others mixes the rotation business of central grain reserves with other businesses, the national grain administrative department shall instruct China Grain Reserves Corporation to order it to rectify within a prescribed time limit; the persons directly in charge shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from a warning to demotion; if losses are caused to central grain reserves, the persons directly in charge shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from removal from office to dismissal, and its qualification for storage on behalf of others shall be revoked.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，挤占、截留、挪用中央储备粮贷款或者贷款利息、管理费用等财政补贴，或者擅自更改中央储备粮入库成本的，由国务院财政部门、中国农业发展银行按照各自职责责令改正或者给予信贷制裁；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予撤职直至开除的纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where anyone violates the provisions of these Regulations by embezzling, intercepting or misappropriating loans for central grain reserves or financial subsidies such as loan interest and administrative expenses, or unauthorizedly changing the storage cost of central grain reserves, the finance department of the State Council and Agricultural Development Bank of China shall, within their respective functions, order correction or impose credit sanctions; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions ranging from removal from office to dismissal according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家机关和中国农业发展银行的工作人员违反本条例规定，滥用职权、徇私舞弊或者玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予降级直至开除的行政处分或者纪律处分。

**Article 56** Where any functionary of a State organ or Agricultural Development Bank of China violates the provisions of these Regulations by abusing power, engaging in malpractices for personal gain or neglecting duties, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction or disciplinary sanction ranging from demotion to dismissal shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，破坏中央储备粮仓储设施，偷盗、哄抢、损毁中央储备粮，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚；造成财产损失的，依法承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 57** Where anyone violates the provisions of these Regulations by damaging storage facilities of central grain reserves, or stealing, looting or destroying central grain reserves, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if property losses are caused, civil compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例规定的对国家机关工作人员的行政处分，依照《中华人民共和国公务员法》的规定执行；对中国储备粮管理总公司及其分支机构、承储企业、中国农业发展银行工作人员的纪律处分，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 58** The administrative sanctions against functionaries of State organs provided for in these Regulations shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Civil Servants; the disciplinary sanctions against functionaries of China Grain Reserves Corporation and its branches, storage enterprises, and Agricultural Development Bank of China shall be executed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　地方储备粮的管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市参照本条例制定。

**Article 59** The measures for the administration of local grain reserves shall be formulated by the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 60** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
