# 农药管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （1997年5月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第216号发布　根据2001年11月29日《国务院关于修改〈农药管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　2017年2月8日国务院第164次常务会议修订通过　根据2022年3月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强农药管理，保证农药质量，保障农产品质量安全和人畜安全，保护农业、林业生产和生态环境，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称农药，是指用于预防、控制危害农业、林业的病、虫、草、鼠和其他有害生物以及有目的地调节植物、昆虫生长的化学合成或者来源于生物、其他天然物质的一种物质或者几种物质的混合物及其制剂。 前款规定的农药包括用于不同目的、场所的下列各类： （一）预防、控制危害农业、林业的病、虫（包括昆虫、蜱、螨）、草、鼠、软体动物和其他有害生物； （二）预防、控制仓储以及加工场所的病、虫、鼠和其他有害生物； （三）调节植物、昆虫生长； （四）农业、林业产品防腐或者保鲜； （五）预防、控制蚊、蝇、蜚蠊、鼠和其他有害生物； （六）预防、控制危害河流堤坝、铁路、码头、机场、建筑物和其他场所的有害生物。 第三条　国务院农业主管部门负责全国的农药监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门负责本行政区域的农药监督管理工作。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的农药监督管理工作。 第四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对农药监督管理工作的组织领导，将农药监督管理经费列入本级政府预算，保障农药监督管理工作的开展。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强农药管理，保证农药质量，保障农产品质量安全和人畜安全，保护农业、林业生产和生态环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening pesticide administration, ensuring pesticide quality, safeguarding the quality and safety of agricultural products and the safety of humans and animals, and protecting agricultural and forestry production and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称农药，是指用于预防、控制危害农业、林业的病、虫、草、鼠和其他有害生物以及有目的地调节植物、昆虫生长的化学合成或者来源于生物、其他天然物质的一种物质或者几种物质的混合物及其制剂。  
前款规定的农药包括用于不同目的、场所的下列各类：  
（一）预防、控制危害农业、林业的病、虫（包括昆虫、蜱、螨）、草、鼠、软体动物和其他有害生物；  
（二）预防、控制仓储以及加工场所的病、虫、鼠和其他有害生物；  
（三）调节植物、昆虫生长；  
（四）农业、林业产品防腐或者保鲜；  
（五）预防、控制蚊、蝇、蜚蠊、鼠和其他有害生物；  
（六）预防、控制危害河流堤坝、铁路、码头、机场、建筑物和其他场所的有害生物。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "pesticides" refers to substances, mixtures thereof and their preparations that are chemically synthesized or derived from organisms or other natural substances, and which are used for preventing or controlling diseases, insects, weeds, rodents, and other harmful organisms that damage agriculture and forestry, or for the purpose of regulating the growth of plants and insects.  
Pesticides as prescribed in the preceding paragraph include the following categories used for different purposes and places:  
(1) Preventing or controlling diseases, insects (including insects, ticks, mites), weeds, rodents, mollusks, and other harmful organisms that damage agriculture and forestry;  
(2) Preventing or controlling diseases, insects, rodents, and other harmful organisms in storage and processing places;  
(3) Regulating the growth of plants and insects;  
(4) Preserving or keeping fresh agricultural and forestry products;  
(5) Preventing or controlling mosquitoes, flies, cockroaches, rodents, and other harmful organisms;  
(6) Preventing or controlling harmful organisms that damage river dikes, railways, docks, airports, buildings, and other places.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业主管部门负责全国的农药监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门负责本行政区域的农药监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的农药监督管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent agricultural department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pesticides nationwide.  
The competent agricultural departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of pesticides within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the related supervision and administration of pesticides within the scope of their respective duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对农药监督管理工作的组织领导，将农药监督管理经费列入本级政府预算，保障农药监督管理工作的开展。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen organizational leadership over the supervision and administration of pesticides, include the funds for the supervision and administration of pesticides in their respective government budgets, and ensure the implementation of the supervision and administration of pesticides.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　农药生产企业、农药经营者应当对其生产、经营的农药的安全性、有效性负责，自觉接受政府监管和社会监督。  
农药生产企业、农药经营者应当加强行业自律，规范生产、经营行为。

**Article 5** Pesticide manufacturing enterprises and pesticide business operators shall be responsible for the safety and efficacy of the pesticides they manufacture and operate, and shall consciously accept government regulation and public supervision.  
Pesticide manufacturing enterprises and pesticide business operators shall strengthen industry self-discipline and regulate their manufacturing and business practices.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持研制、生产、使用安全、高效、经济的农药，推进农药专业化使用，促进农药产业升级。  
对在农药研制、推广和监督管理等工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰或者奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports the research, development, manufacture and use of safe, highly effective and economical pesticides, promotes the professional use of pesticides, and facilitates the upgrading of the pesticide industry.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the research, development, extension, supervision and administration of pesticides shall be commended or rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　农药登记

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家实行农药登记制度。农药生产企业、向中国出口农药的企业应当依照本条例的规定申请农药登记，新农药研制者可以依照本条例的规定申请农药登记。  
国务院农业主管部门所属的负责农药检定工作的机构负责农药登记具体工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门所属的负责农药检定工作的机构协助做好本行政区域的农药登记具体工作。

**Article 7** The State shall implement a pesticide registration system. Pesticide manufacturing enterprises and enterprises exporting pesticides to China shall apply for pesticide registration in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; developers of new pesticides may apply for pesticide registration in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
The institution responsible for pesticide inspection work affiliated with the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall be responsible for the specific work of pesticide registration. The institutions responsible for pesticide inspection work affiliated with the competent agricultural departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall assist in carrying out the specific work of pesticide registration within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院农业主管部门组织成立农药登记评审委员会，负责农药登记评审。  
农药登记评审委员会由下列人员组成：  
（一）国务院农业、林业、卫生、环境保护、粮食、工业行业管理、安全生产监督管理等有关部门和供销合作总社等单位推荐的农药产品化学、药效、毒理、残留、环境、质量标准和检测等方面的专家；  
（二）国家食品安全风险评估专家委员会的有关专家；  
（三）国务院农业、林业、卫生、环境保护、粮食、工业行业管理、安全生产监督管理等有关部门和供销合作总社等单位的代表。  
农药登记评审规则由国务院农业主管部门制定。

**Article 8** The competent agricultural department of the State Council shall organize the establishment of a Pesticide Registration Evaluation Committee, which shall be responsible for the evaluation of pesticide registrations.  
The Pesticide Registration Evaluation Committee shall be composed of the following persons:  
(1) Experts in the fields of pesticide product chemistry, efficacy, toxicology, residues, environment, quality standards and testing recommended by relevant departments under the State Council, such as agriculture, forestry, health, environmental protection, grain, industrial administration, and work safety supervision and administration, and the All China Federation of Supply and Marketing Cooperatives;  
(2) Relevant experts from the National Food Safety Risk Assessment Expert Committee;  
(3) Representatives from relevant departments under the State Council, such as agriculture, forestry, health, environmental protection, grain, industrial administration, and work safety supervision and administration, and the All China Federation of Supply and Marketing Cooperatives.  
The evaluation rules for pesticide registration shall be formulated by the competent agricultural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请农药登记的，应当进行登记试验。  
农药的登记试验应当报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门备案。

**Article 9** An applicant for pesticide registration shall conduct registration trials.  
Registration trials of pesticides shall be reported to the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located for filing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　登记试验应当由国务院农业主管部门认定的登记试验单位按照国务院农业主管部门的规定进行。  
与已取得中国农药登记的农药组成成分、使用范围和使用方法相同的农药，免予残留、环境试验，但已取得中国农药登记的农药依照[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定在登记资料保护期内的，应当经农药登记证持有人授权同意。  
登记试验单位应当对登记试验报告的真实性负责。

**Article 10** Registration trials shall be conducted by registration trial units recognized by the competent agricultural department of the State Council in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council.  
For pesticides that are identical in composition, scope of use and method of use to pesticides that have already obtained registration in China, residue and environmental trials shall be exempted; provided that if the pesticides that have already obtained registration in China are within the protection period of registration data as provided for in Article 15 of these Regulations, the authorization and consent of the holder of the pesticide registration certificate shall be obtained.  
Registration trial units shall be responsible for the authenticity of their registration trial reports.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　登记试验结束后，申请人应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门提出农药登记申请，并提交登记试验报告、标签样张和农药产品质量标准及其检验方法等申请资料；申请新农药登记的，还应当提供农药标准品。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内提出初审意见，并报送国务院农业主管部门。  
向中国出口农药的企业申请农药登记的，应当持本条第一款规定的资料、农药标准品以及在有关国家（地区）登记、使用的证明材料，向国务院农业主管部门提出申请。

**Article 11** After the completion of registration trials, an applicant shall submit an application for pesticide registration to the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located, and provide the registration trial report, label sample, and pesticide product quality standards and their testing methods, among other application materials; for an application for registration of a new pesticide, the applicant shall also provide a pesticide reference standard.  
The competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, provide a preliminary review opinion and submit it to the competent agricultural department of the State Council.  
An enterprise that exports pesticides to China and applies for pesticide registration shall submit an application to the competent agricultural department of the State Council with the materials specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the pesticide reference standard, and evidence of registration and use in the relevant country (region).

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院农业主管部门受理申请或者收到省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门报送的申请资料后，应当组织审查和登记评审，并自收到评审意见之日起20个工作日内作出审批决定，符合条件的，核发农药登记证；不符合条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 12** After accepting an application or receiving the application materials submitted by the competent agricultural department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall organize review and registration evaluation, and shall, within 20 working days from the date of receiving the evaluation opinion, make a decision on approval. If the conditions are met, it shall issue a pesticide registration certificate; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农药登记证应当载明农药名称、剂型、有效成分及其含量、毒性、使用范围、使用方法和剂量、登记证持有人、登记证号以及有效期等事项。  
农药登记证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续生产农药或者向中国出口农药的，农药登记证持有人应当在有效期届满90日前向国务院农业主管部门申请延续。  
农药登记证载明事项发生变化的，农药登记证持有人应当按照国务院农业主管部门的规定申请变更农药登记证。  
国务院农业主管部门应当及时公告农药登记证核发、延续、变更情况以及有关的农药产品质量标准号、残留限量规定、检验方法、经核准的标签等信息。

**Article 13** A pesticide registration certificate shall state the pesticide name, formulation, active ingredients and their content, toxicity, scope of use, method and dosage of use, holder of the certificate, certificate number, and validity period, among other matters.  
The validity period of a pesticide registration certificate shall be five years. If, upon expiration of the validity period, it is necessary to continue manufacturing pesticides or exporting pesticides to China, the holder of the pesticide registration certificate shall apply to the competent agricultural department of the State Council for renewal 90 days before the expiration of the validity period.  
If the matters stated in the pesticide registration certificate change, the holder of the pesticide registration certificate shall apply for modification of the pesticide registration certificate in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council.  
The competent agricultural department of the State Council shall promptly announce information such as the issuance, renewal, and modification of pesticide registration certificates, as well as the relevant pesticide product quality standard numbers, residue limit provisions, testing methods, and approved labels.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　新农药研制者可以转让其已取得登记的新农药的登记资料；农药生产企业可以向具有相应生产能力的农药生产企业转让其已取得登记的农药的登记资料。

**Article 14** A developer of a new pesticide may transfer the registration data of the new pesticide for which it has obtained registration; a pesticide manufacturing enterprise may transfer the registration data of the pesticide for which it has obtained registration to a pesticide manufacturing enterprise with corresponding manufacturing capacity.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对取得首次登记的、含有新化合物的农药的申请人提交的其自己所取得且未披露的试验数据和其他数据实施保护。  
自登记之日起6年内，对其他申请人未经已取得登记的申请人同意，使用前款规定的数据申请农药登记的，登记机关不予登记；但是，其他申请人提交其自己所取得的数据的除外。  
除下列情况外，登记机关不得披露本条第一款规定的数据：  
（一）公共利益需要；  
（二）已采取措施确保该类信息不会被不正当地进行商业使用。

**Article 15** The State shall protect the test data and other data obtained and not disclosed by an applicant for a pesticide containing a new compound that has obtained its first registration.  
Within six years from the date of registration, if another applicant uses the data specified in the preceding paragraph to apply for pesticide registration without the consent of the applicant who has obtained the registration, the registration authority shall not grant the registration; except where the other applicant submits data obtained by itself.  
The registration authority shall not disclose the data specified in the first paragraph of this Article except under the following circumstances:  
(1) Required by the public interest;  
(2) Measures have been taken to ensure that such information will not be improperly used for commercial purposes.

### 第三章　农药生产

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农药生产应当符合国家产业政策。国家鼓励和支持农药生产企业采用先进技术和先进管理规范，提高农药的安全性、有效性。

**Article 16** The manufacture of pesticides shall comply with national industrial policies. The State encourages and supports pesticide manufacturing enterprises in adopting advanced technologies and advanced management standards to improve the safety and efficacy of pesticides.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家实行农药生产许可制度。农药生产企业应当具备下列条件，并按照国务院农业主管部门的规定向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门申请农药生产许可证：  
（一）有与所申请生产农药相适应的技术人员；  
（二）有与所申请生产农药相适应的厂房、设施；  
（三）有对所申请生产农药进行质量管理和质量检验的人员、仪器和设备；  
（四）有保证所申请生产农药质量的规章制度。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内作出审批决定，必要时应当进行实地核查。符合条件的，核发农药生产许可证；不符合条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
安全生产、环境保护等法律、行政法规对企业生产条件有其他规定的，农药生产企业还应当遵守其规定。

**Article 17** The State shall implement a pesticide manufacturing license system. A pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall meet the following conditions and apply for a pesticide manufacturing license to the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council:  
(1) Having technical personnel suitable for the manufacture of the applied pesticide;  
(2) Having factory buildings and facilities suitable for the manufacture of the applied pesticide;  
(3) Having personnel, instruments and equipment for quality management and quality testing of the applied pesticide;  
(4) Having rules and regulations to ensure the quality of the applied pesticide.  
The competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on approval, and shall conduct an on-site verification if necessary. If the conditions are met, it shall issue a pesticide manufacturing license; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Where other laws or administrative regulations on work safety, environmental protection, etc., have other provisions on the manufacturing conditions of an enterprise, the pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall also comply with those provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农药生产许可证应当载明农药生产企业名称、住所、法定代表人（负责人）、生产范围、生产地址以及有效期等事项。  
农药生产许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续生产农药的，农药生产企业应当在有效期届满90日前向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门申请延续。  
农药生产许可证载明事项发生变化的，农药生产企业应当按照国务院农业主管部门的规定申请变更农药生产许可证。

**Article 18** A pesticide manufacturing license shall state the name, domicile, legal representative (person in charge), scope of manufacture, manufacturing address, and validity period of the pesticide manufacturing enterprise, among other matters.  
The validity period of a pesticide manufacturing license shall be five years. If, upon expiration of the validity period, it is necessary to continue manufacturing pesticides, the pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall apply to the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for renewal 90 days before the expiration of the validity period.  
If the matters stated in the pesticide manufacturing license change, the pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall apply for modification of the pesticide manufacturing license in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　委托加工、分装农药的，委托人应当取得相应的农药登记证，受托人应当取得农药生产许可证。  
委托人应当对委托加工、分装的农药质量负责。

**Article 19** Where a pesticide is manufactured under contract or repackaged, the principal shall have obtained the corresponding pesticide registration certificate, and the contractor shall have obtained a pesticide manufacturing license.  
The principal shall be responsible for the quality of the pesticides manufactured under contract or repackaged.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　农药生产企业采购原材料，应当查验产品质量检验合格证和有关许可证明文件，不得采购、使用未依法附具产品质量检验合格证、未依法取得有关许可证明文件的原材料。  
农药生产企业应当建立原材料进货记录制度，如实记录原材料的名称、有关许可证明文件编号、规格、数量、供货人名称及其联系方式、进货日期等内容。原材料进货记录应当保存2年以上。

**Article 20** When purchasing raw materials, a pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall check the product quality inspection certificate and relevant license documents, and shall not purchase or use raw materials that are not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate as required by law or that have not obtained the relevant license documents as required by law.  
A pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall establish a raw material procurement recording system, truthfully recording the name of the raw material, the number of the relevant license document, specifications, quantity, name and contact information of the supplier, date of purchase, and other contents. The raw material procurement records shall be kept for at least two years.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　农药生产企业应当严格按照产品质量标准进行生产，确保农药产品与登记农药一致。农药出厂销售，应当经质量检验合格并附具产品质量检验合格证。  
农药生产企业应当建立农药出厂销售记录制度，如实记录农药的名称、规格、数量、生产日期和批号、产品质量检验信息、购货人名称及其联系方式、销售日期等内容。农药出厂销售记录应当保存2年以上。

**Article 21** A pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall strictly produce in accordance with the product quality standards to ensure that the pesticide products are consistent with the registered pesticides. Pesticides leaving the factory for sale shall pass quality inspection and be accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate.  
A pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall establish a pesticide ex-factory sales recording system, truthfully recording the name, specifications, quantity, date of manufacture and batch number, product quality inspection information, name and contact information of the purchaser, date of sale, and other contents. The pesticide ex-factory sales records shall be kept for at least two years.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　农药包装应当符合国家有关规定，并印制或者贴有标签。国家鼓励农药生产企业使用可回收的农药包装材料。  
农药标签应当按照国务院农业主管部门的规定，以中文标注农药的名称、剂型、有效成分及其含量、毒性及其标识、使用范围、使用方法和剂量、使用技术要求和注意事项、生产日期、可追溯电子信息码等内容。  
剧毒、高毒农药以及使用技术要求严格的其他农药等限制使用农药的标签还应当标注“限制使用”字样，并注明使用的特别限制和特殊要求。用于食用农产品的农药的标签还应当标注安全间隔期。

**Article 22** The packaging of pesticides shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State and shall be printed with or affixed with a label. The State encourages pesticide manufacturing enterprises to use recyclable pesticide packaging materials.  
The pesticide label shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council, indicate in Chinese the name of the pesticide, formulation, active ingredients and their content, toxicity and its identification, scope of use, method and dosage of use, technical requirements and precautions for use, date of manufacture, traceable electronic information code, and other contents.  
The labels of restricted-use pesticides, such as acutely toxic and highly toxic pesticides and other pesticides with strict technical requirements for use, shall also bear the words "Restricted Use" and specify the particular restrictions and special requirements for use. The labels of pesticides used for edible agricultural products shall also indicate the pre-harvest interval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农药生产企业不得擅自改变经核准的农药的标签内容，不得在农药的标签中标注虚假、误导使用者的内容。  
农药包装过小，标签不能标注全部内容的，应当同时附具说明书，说明书的内容应当与经核准的标签内容一致。

**Article 23** A pesticide manufacturing enterprise shall not alter the approved label contents of a pesticide without authorization, and shall not mark false or misleading contents on the pesticide label.  
If the pesticide packaging is too small to display all the label contents, an instruction sheet shall be attached, and the contents of the instruction sheet shall be consistent with the approved label contents.

### 第四章　农药经营

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家实行农药经营许可制度，但经营卫生用农药的除外。农药经营者应当具备下列条件，并按照国务院农业主管部门的规定向县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门申请农药经营许可证：  
（一）有具备农药和病虫害防治专业知识，熟悉农药管理规定，能够指导安全合理使用农药的经营人员；  
（二）有与其他商品以及饮用水水源、生活区域等有效隔离的营业场所和仓储场所，并配备与所申请经营农药相适应的防护设施；  
（三）有与所申请经营农药相适应的质量管理、台账记录、安全防护、应急处置、仓储管理等制度。  
经营限制使用农药的，还应当配备相应的用药指导和病虫害防治专业技术人员，并按照所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门的规定实行定点经营。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内作出审批决定。符合条件的，核发农药经营许可证；不符合条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 24** The State shall implement a pesticide business license system, except for those operating sanitary pesticides. A pesticide business operator shall meet the following conditions and apply for a pesticide business license to the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council:  
(1) Having business personnel with professional knowledge of pesticides and pest control, familiarity with pesticide management regulations, and the ability to guide the safe and rational use of pesticides;  
(2) Having business premises and storage premises effectively separated from other commodities, sources of drinking water, residential areas, etc., and equipped with protective facilities suitable for the applied pesticide business;  
(3) Having systems for quality management, ledger records, safety protection, emergency response, and storage management suitable for the applied pesticide business.  
Those operating restricted-use pesticides shall also be equipped with corresponding technical personnel for medication guidance and pest control, and shall conduct business at designated points in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall make a decision on approval within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, it shall issue a pesticide business license; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant in writing and explain the reasons.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　农药经营许可证应当载明农药经营者名称、住所、负责人、经营范围以及有效期等事项。  
农药经营许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要继续经营农药的，农药经营者应当在有效期届满90日前向发证机关申请延续。  
农药经营许可证载明事项发生变化的，农药经营者应当按照国务院农业主管部门的规定申请变更农药经营许可证。  
取得农药经营许可证的农药经营者设立分支机构的，应当依法申请变更农药经营许可证，并向分支机构所在地县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门备案，其分支机构免予办理农药经营许可证。农药经营者应当对其分支机构的经营活动负责。

**Article 25** A pesticide business license shall state the name, domicile, person in charge, scope of business, and validity period of the pesticide business operator, among other matters.  
The validity period of a pesticide business license shall be five years. If, upon expiration of the validity period, it is necessary to continue operating pesticides, the pesticide business operator shall apply to the issuing authority for renewal 90 days before the expiration of the validity period.  
If the matters stated in the pesticide business license change, the pesticide business operator shall apply for modification of the pesticide business license in accordance with the provisions of the competent agricultural department of the State Council.  
A pesticide business operator that has obtained a pesticide business license and establishes a branch shall apply for modification of the pesticide business license in accordance with the law, and report to the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the branch is located for filing; the branch is exempted from obtaining a pesticide business license. The pesticide business operator shall be responsible for the business activities of its branches.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农药经营者采购农药应当查验产品包装、标签、产品质量检验合格证以及有关许可证明文件，不得向未取得农药生产许可证的农药生产企业或者未取得农药经营许可证的其他农药经营者采购农药。  
农药经营者应当建立采购台账，如实记录农药的名称、有关许可证明文件编号、规格、数量、生产企业和供货人名称及其联系方式、进货日期等内容。采购台账应当保存2年以上。

**Article 26** When purchasing pesticides, a pesticide business operator shall check the product packaging, label, product quality inspection certificate, and relevant license documents, and shall not purchase pesticides from a pesticide manufacturing enterprise that has not obtained a pesticide manufacturing license or from another pesticide business operator that has not obtained a pesticide business license.  
A pesticide business operator shall establish a procurement ledger, truthfully recording the name of the pesticide, the number of the relevant license document, specifications, quantity, name and contact information of the manufacturer and supplier, date of purchase, and other contents. The procurement ledger shall be kept for at least two years.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农药经营者应当建立销售台账，如实记录销售农药的名称、规格、数量、生产企业、购买人、销售日期等内容。销售台账应当保存2年以上。  
农药经营者应当向购买人询问病虫害发生情况并科学推荐农药，必要时应当实地查看病虫害发生情况，并正确说明农药的使用范围、使用方法和剂量、使用技术要求和注意事项，不得误导购买人。  
经营卫生用农药的，不适用本条第一款、第二款的规定。

**Article 27** A pesticide business operator shall establish a sales ledger, truthfully recording the name, specifications, quantity, manufacturing enterprise, purchaser, date of sale, and other contents of the pesticides sold. The sales ledger shall be kept for at least two years.  
A pesticide business operator shall inquire with the purchaser about the occurrence of pests and diseases and scientifically recommend pesticides; if necessary, it shall inspect the occurrence of pests and diseases on site, and correctly explain the scope of use, method and dosage of use, technical requirements and precautions for use of the pesticide, and shall not mislead the purchaser.  
For operators of sanitary pesticides, the provisions of the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall not apply.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　农药经营者不得加工、分装农药，不得在农药中添加任何物质，不得采购、销售包装和标签不符合规定，未附具产品质量检验合格证，未取得有关许可证明文件的农药。  
经营卫生用农药的，应当将卫生用农药与其他商品分柜销售；经营其他农药的，不得在农药经营场所内经营食品、食用农产品、饲料等。

**Article 28** A pesticide business operator shall not process or repackage pesticides, shall not add any substance to pesticides, and shall not purchase or sell pesticides whose packaging and labels do not comply with regulations, that are not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate, or that have not obtained relevant license documents.  
Operators of sanitary pesticides shall sell sanitary pesticides in separate counters from other commodities; operators of other pesticides shall not operate food, edible agricultural products, feed, etc. within the pesticide business premises.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　境外企业不得直接在中国销售农药。境外企业在中国销售农药的，应当依法在中国设立销售机构或者委托符合条件的中国代理机构销售。  
向中国出口的农药应当附具中文标签、说明书，符合产品质量标准，并经出入境检验检疫部门依法检验合格。禁止进口未取得农药登记证的农药。  
办理农药进出口海关申报手续，应当按照海关总署的规定出示相关证明文件。

**Article 29** An overseas enterprise shall not directly sell pesticides in China. An overseas enterprise that sells pesticides in China shall establish a sales institution in China in accordance with the law or entrust a qualified Chinese agency to sell on its behalf.  
Pesticides exported to China shall be accompanied by Chinese labels and instructions, comply with product quality standards, and be inspected and tested by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine department in accordance with the law before being deemed qualified. The import of pesticides that have not obtained a pesticide registration certificate shall be prohibited.  
When going through customs declaration procedures for the import or export of pesticides, the relevant certification documents shall be presented in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs.

### 第五章　农药使用

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门应当加强农药使用指导、服务工作，建立健全农药安全、合理使用制度，并按照预防为主、综合防治的要求，组织推广农药科学使用技术，规范农药使用行为。林业、粮食、卫生等部门应当加强对林业、储粮、卫生用农药安全、合理使用的技术指导，环境保护主管部门应当加强对农药使用过程中环境保护和污染防治的技术指导。

**Article 30** The competent agricultural departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the guidance and service for pesticide use, establish and improve the system for safe and rational use of pesticides, and, in accordance with the requirements of prevention first and integrated control, organize the promotion of scientific pesticide use techniques and regulate pesticide use behaviors. The forestry, grain, and health departments shall strengthen technical guidance for the safe and rational use of pesticides in forestry, grain storage, and sanitary purposes, and the competent environmental protection departments shall strengthen technical guidance for environmental protection and pollution prevention in the process of pesticide use.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级人民政府农业主管部门应当组织植物保护、农业技术推广等机构向农药使用者提供免费技术培训，提高农药安全、合理使用水平。  
国家鼓励农业科研单位、有关学校、农民专业合作社、供销合作社、农业社会化服务组织和专业人员为农药使用者提供技术服务。

**Article 31** The competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall organize plant protection, agricultural technology extension, and other institutions to provide free technical training to pesticide users to improve the level of safe and rational use of pesticides.  
The State encourages agricultural research institutes, relevant schools, specialized farmer cooperatives, supply and marketing cooperatives, agricultural socialized service organizations, and professionals to provide technical services to pesticide users.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家通过推广生物防治、物理防治、先进施药器械等措施，逐步减少农药使用量。  
县级人民政府应当制定并组织实施本行政区域的农药减量计划；对实施农药减量计划、自愿减少农药使用量的农药使用者，给予鼓励和扶持。  
县级人民政府农业主管部门应当鼓励和扶持设立专业化病虫害防治服务组织，并对专业化病虫害防治和限制使用农药的配药、用药进行指导、规范和管理，提高病虫害防治水平。  
县级人民政府农业主管部门应当指导农药使用者有计划地轮换使用农药，减缓危害农业、林业的病、虫、草、鼠和其他有害生物的抗药性。  
乡、镇人民政府应当协助开展农药使用指导、服务工作。

**Article 32** The State shall gradually reduce the amount of pesticides used by promoting biological control, physical control, advanced application equipment, and other measures.  
The county people's government shall formulate and organize the implementation of a pesticide reduction plan for its administrative region; it shall provide encouragement and support to pesticide users who implement the pesticide reduction plan and voluntarily reduce the amount of pesticides used.  
The competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall encourage and support the establishment of specialized pest control service organizations, and shall guide, regulate, and manage specialized pest control and the preparation and application of restricted-use pesticides to improve the level of pest control.  
The competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall guide pesticide users to rotate the use of pesticides in a planned manner to slow down the resistance of diseases, insects, weeds, rodents, and other harmful organisms that damage agriculture and forestry.  
Township and town people's governments shall assist in carrying out the work of pesticide use guidance and service.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　农药使用者应当遵守国家有关农药安全、合理使用制度，妥善保管农药，并在配药、用药过程中采取必要的防护措施，避免发生农药使用事故。  
限制使用农药的经营者应当为农药使用者提供用药指导，并逐步提供统一用药服务。

**Article 33** Pesticide users shall comply with the state system for safe and rational use of pesticides, properly store pesticides, and take necessary protective measures during the preparation and application of pesticides to avoid pesticide use accidents.  
Operators of restricted-use pesticides shall provide medication guidance to pesticide users and gradually provide unified medication services.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　农药使用者应当严格按照农药的标签标注的使用范围、使用方法和剂量、使用技术要求和注意事项使用农药，不得扩大使用范围、加大用药剂量或者改变使用方法。  
农药使用者不得使用禁用的农药。  
标签标注安全间隔期的农药，在农产品收获前应当按照安全间隔期的要求停止使用。  
剧毒、高毒农药不得用于防治卫生害虫，不得用于蔬菜、瓜果、茶叶、菌类、中草药材的生产，不得用于水生植物的病虫害防治。

**Article 34** Pesticide users shall strictly use pesticides in accordance with the scope of use, method and dosage of use, technical requirements and precautions indicated on the pesticide label, and shall not expand the scope of use, increase the dosage, or change the method of use.  
Pesticide users shall not use prohibited pesticides.  
For pesticides with a pre-harvest interval indicated on the label, use shall be stopped before the harvest of agricultural products in accordance with the requirements of the pre-harvest interval.  
Acutely toxic and highly toxic pesticides shall not be used for the prevention and control of sanitary pests, shall not be used in the production of vegetables, melons and fruits, tea, edible fungi, Chinese herbal medicines, and shall not be used for the prevention and control of pests and diseases of aquatic plants.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农药使用者应当保护环境，保护有益生物和珍稀物种，不得在饮用水水源保护区、河道内丢弃农药、农药包装物或者清洗施药器械。  
严禁在饮用水水源保护区内使用农药，严禁使用农药毒鱼、虾、鸟、兽等。

**Article 35** Pesticide users shall protect the environment, protect beneficial organisms and rare species, and shall not discard pesticides, pesticide packaging materials, or clean application equipment in protected areas of drinking water sources or river courses.  
The use of pesticides within protected areas of drinking water sources shall be strictly prohibited, and the use of pesticides to poison fish, shrimp, birds, animals, etc. shall be strictly prohibited.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　农产品生产企业、食品和食用农产品仓储企业、专业化病虫害防治服务组织和从事农产品生产的农民专业合作社等应当建立农药使用记录，如实记录使用农药的时间、地点、对象以及农药名称、用量、生产企业等。农药使用记录应当保存2年以上。  
国家鼓励其他农药使用者建立农药使用记录。

**Article 36** Agricultural product production enterprises, food and edible agricultural product storage enterprises, specialized pest control service organizations, and specialized farmer cooperatives engaged in agricultural product production shall establish pesticide use records, truthfully recording the time, place, target, and name, dosage, and manufacturing enterprise of the pesticides used, among other information. The pesticide use records shall be kept for at least two years.  
The State encourages other pesticide users to establish pesticide use records.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励农药使用者妥善收集农药包装物等废弃物；农药生产企业、农药经营者应当回收农药废弃物，防止农药污染环境和农药中毒事故的发生。具体办法由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院农业主管部门、国务院财政部门等部门制定。

**Article 37** The State encourages pesticide users to properly collect pesticide packaging waste and other wastes; pesticide manufacturing enterprises and pesticide business operators shall recycle pesticide wastes to prevent environmental pollution and pesticide poisoning accidents. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent environmental protection department of the State Council jointly with the competent agricultural department of the State Council, the finance department of the State Council, and other departments.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发生农药使用事故，农药使用者、农药生产企业、农药经营者和其他有关人员应当及时报告当地农业主管部门。  
接到报告的农业主管部门应当立即采取措施，防止事故扩大，同时通知有关部门采取相应措施。造成农药中毒事故的，由农业主管部门和公安机关依照职责权限组织调查处理，卫生主管部门应当按照国家有关规定立即对受到伤害的人员组织医疗救治；造成环境污染事故的，由环境保护等有关部门依法组织调查处理；造成储粮药剂使用事故和农作物药害事故的，分别由粮食、农业等部门组织技术鉴定和调查处理。

**Article 38** In the event of a pesticide use accident, pesticide users, pesticide manufacturing enterprises, pesticide business operators, and other relevant persons shall promptly report to the local competent agricultural department.  
The competent agricultural department that receives the report shall immediately take measures to prevent the accident from expanding, and simultaneously notify the relevant departments to take corresponding measures. If the accident causes pesticide poisoning, the competent agricultural department and the public security organ shall organize investigation and handling in accordance with their respective functions and powers, and the competent health department shall immediately organize medical treatment for the injured persons in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if the accident causes environmental pollution, the competent environmental protection department and other relevant departments shall organize investigation and handling in accordance with the law; if the accident involves grain storage agent use or crop phytotoxicity, the grain department and the agricultural department, respectively, shall organize technical appraisal and investigation and handling.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　因防治突发重大病虫害等紧急需要，国务院农业主管部门可以决定临时生产、使用规定数量的未取得登记或者禁用、限制使用的农药，必要时应当会同国务院对外贸易主管部门决定临时限制出口或者临时进口规定数量、品种的农药。  
前款规定的农药，应当在使用地县级人民政府农业主管部门的监督和指导下使用。

**Article 39** For urgent needs such as the prevention and control of sudden major pests and diseases, the competent agricultural department of the State Council may decide to temporarily manufacture and use a specified quantity of pesticides that have not been registered or are prohibited or restricted for use; when necessary, it shall, jointly with the competent foreign trade department of the State Council, decide to temporarily restrict export or temporarily import a specified quantity and variety of pesticides.  
The pesticides specified in the preceding paragraph shall be used under the supervision and guidance of the competent agricultural department of the county people's government where they are used.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门应当定期调查统计农药生产、销售、使用情况，并及时通报本级人民政府有关部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门应当建立农药生产、经营诚信档案并予以公布；发现违法生产、经营农药的行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法移送公安机关查处。

**Article 40** The competent agricultural departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall regularly investigate and compile statistics on the manufacture, sale, and use of pesticides, and promptly notify the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level.  
The competent agricultural departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish credit files for pesticide manufacture and business and make them public; if they discover that illegal acts of manufacturing or operating pesticides are suspected of constituting a crime, they shall transfer the case to the public security organ for handling in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门履行农药监督管理职责，可以依法采取下列措施：  
（一）进入农药生产、经营、使用场所实施现场检查；  
（二）对生产、经营、使用的农药实施抽查检测；  
（三）向有关人员调查了解有关情况；  
（四）查阅、复制合同、票据、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
（五）查封、扣押违法生产、经营、使用的农药，以及用于违法生产、经营、使用农药的工具、设备、原材料等；  
（六）查封违法生产、经营、使用农药的场所。

**Article 41** In performing the duties of supervision and administration of pesticides, the competent agricultural departments of the people's governments at or above the county level may take the following measures in accordance with the law:  
(1) Entering premises where pesticides are manufactured, operated, or used to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Conducting random sampling and testing of pesticides being manufactured, operated, or used;  
(3) Investigating and inquiring about relevant information from relevant persons;  
(4) Consulting and copying contracts, vouchers, account books, and other relevant materials;  
(5) Sealing up or seizing pesticides illegally manufactured, operated, or used, as well as tools, equipment, and raw materials used for illegally manufacturing, operating, or using pesticides;  
(6) Sealing up the premises where pesticides are illegally manufactured, operated, or used.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家建立农药召回制度。农药生产企业发现其生产的农药对农业、林业、人畜安全、农产品质量安全、生态环境等有严重危害或者较大风险的，应当立即停止生产，通知有关经营者和使用者，向所在地农业主管部门报告，主动召回产品，并记录通知和召回情况。  
农药经营者发现其经营的农药有前款规定的情形的，应当立即停止销售，通知有关生产企业、供货人和购买人，向所在地农业主管部门报告，并记录停止销售和通知情况。  
农药使用者发现其使用的农药有本条第一款规定的情形的，应当立即停止使用，通知经营者，并向所在地农业主管部门报告。

**Article 42** The State shall establish a pesticide recall system. If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise discovers that the pesticides it manufactures pose a serious hazard or significant risk to agriculture, forestry, human and animal safety, agricultural product quality and safety, or the ecological environment, it shall immediately stop manufacturing, notify the relevant business operators and users, report to the local competent agricultural department, voluntarily recall the products, and record the notification and recall situation.  
If a pesticide business operator discovers that the pesticides it operates fall under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall immediately stop selling, notify the relevant manufacturing enterprises, suppliers, and purchasers, report to the local competent agricultural department, and record the cessation of sales and the notifications.  
If a pesticide user discovers that the pesticides he uses fall under the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of this Article, he shall immediately stop using them, notify the business operator, and report to the local competent agricultural department.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院农业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门应当组织负责农药检定工作的机构、植物保护机构对已登记农药的安全性和有效性进行监测。  
发现已登记农药对农业、林业、人畜安全、农产品质量安全、生态环境等有严重危害或者较大风险的，国务院农业主管部门应当组织农药登记评审委员会进行评审，根据评审结果撤销、变更相应的农药登记证，必要时应当决定禁用或者限制使用并予以公告。

**Article 43** The competent agricultural department of the State Council and the competent agricultural departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize the institutions responsible for pesticide inspection and plant protection institutions to monitor the safety and efficacy of registered pesticides.  
If it is discovered that a registered pesticide poses a serious hazard or significant risk to agriculture, forestry, human and animal safety, agricultural product quality and safety, or the ecological environment, the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall organize the Pesticide Registration Evaluation Committee to conduct an evaluation, and based on the evaluation results, shall revoke or modify the corresponding pesticide registration certificate; when necessary, it shall decide to prohibit or restrict the use of such pesticide and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　有下列情形之一的，认定为假农药：  
（一）以非农药冒充农药；  
（二）以此种农药冒充他种农药；  
（三）农药所含有效成分种类与农药的标签、说明书标注的有效成分不符。  
禁用的农药，未依法取得农药登记证而生产、进口的农药，以及未附具标签的农药，按照假农药处理。

**Article 44** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as counterfeit pesticides:  
(1) Passing off non-pesticides as pesticides;  
(2) Passing off one type of pesticide as another type of pesticide;  
(3) The types of active ingredients contained in a pesticide do not match the active ingredients indicated on the pesticide label or instruction sheet.  
Prohibited pesticides, pesticides manufactured or imported without having obtained a pesticide registration certificate according to law, and pesticides without accompanying labels shall be treated as counterfeit pesticides.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　有下列情形之一的，认定为劣质农药：  
（一）不符合农药产品质量标准；  
（二）混有导致药害等有害成分。  
超过农药质量保证期的农药，按照劣质农药处理。

**Article 45** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as substandard pesticides:  
(1) Failing to meet the pesticide product quality standards;  
(2) Being mixed with harmful ingredients that cause phytotoxicity, etc.  
Pesticides that have exceeded their quality guarantee period shall be treated as substandard pesticides.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　假农药、劣质农药和回收的农药废弃物等应当交由具有危险废物经营资质的单位集中处置，处置费用由相应的农药生产企业、农药经营者承担；农药生产企业、农药经营者不明确的，处置费用由所在地县级人民政府财政列支。

**Article 46** Counterfeit pesticides, substandard pesticides, and recovered pesticide waste shall be handed over to units with hazardous waste operation qualifications for centralized disposal; the disposal costs shall be borne by the corresponding pesticide manufacturing enterprises and pesticide business operators; if the pesticide manufacturing enterprise or pesticide business operator is unclear, the disposal costs shall be covered by the finance of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　禁止伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借农药登记证、农药生产许可证、农药经营许可证等许可证明文件。

**Article 47** The forgery, alteration, transfer, leasing, or lending of license documents such as pesticide registration certificates, pesticide manufacturing licenses, and pesticide business licenses shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门及其工作人员和负责农药检定工作的机构及其工作人员，不得参与农药生产、经营活动。

**Article 48** The competent agricultural departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and their staff, as well as the institutions responsible for pesticide inspection and their staff, shall not participate in the manufacture or business of pesticides.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府农业主管部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府责令改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分；负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不履行监督管理职责，所辖行政区域的违法农药生产、经营活动造成重大损失或者恶劣社会影响；  
（二）对不符合条件的申请人准予许可或者对符合条件的申请人拒不准予许可；  
（三）参与农药生产、经营活动；  
（四）有其他徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守行为。

**Article 49** Where the competent agricultural departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and their staff commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level shall order correction; the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions in accordance with the law; if the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of supervision and administration, resulting in major losses or adverse social impact due to illegal pesticide manufacturing or business activities within the administrative region under their jurisdiction;  
(2) Granting permission to applicants who do not meet the conditions or refusing to grant permission to applicants who meet the conditions;  
(3) Participating in the manufacture or business of pesticides;  
(4) Engaging in other acts of favoritism, abuse of power, or dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　农药登记评审委员会组成人员在农药登记评审中谋取不正当利益的，由国务院农业主管部门从农药登记评审委员会除名；属于国家工作人员的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** If a member of the Pesticide Registration Evaluation Committee seeks improper gains in pesticide registration evaluation, the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall remove such member from the Pesticide Registration Evaluation Committee; if the member is a state functionary, sanctions shall be given in accordance with the law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　登记试验单位出具虚假登记试验报告的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业主管部门没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；由国务院农业主管部门从登记试验单位中除名，5年内不再受理其登记试验单位认定申请；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** If a registration trial unit issues a false registration trial report, the competent agricultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall remove such unit from the list of registration trial units and shall not accept its application for recognition as a registration trial unit within five years; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　未取得农药生产许可证生产农药或者生产假农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令停止生产，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等，违法生产的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证和相应的农药登记证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
取得农药生产许可证的农药生产企业不再符合规定条件继续生产农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令限期整改；逾期拒不整改或者整改后仍不符合规定条件的，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证。  
农药生产企业生产劣质农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令停止生产，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等，违法生产的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；情节严重的，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证和相应的农药登记证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
委托未取得农药生产许可证的受托人加工、分装农药，或者委托加工、分装假农药、劣质农药的，对委托人和受托人均依照本条第一款、第三款的规定处罚。

**Article 52** If a person manufactures pesticides without having obtained a pesticide manufacturing license or manufactures counterfeit pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of manufacturing, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally manufactured products, and the tools, equipment, raw materials, etc. used for the illegal manufacturing; if the value of the illegally manufactured products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license and the corresponding pesticide registration certificate; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.  
If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise that has obtained a pesticide manufacturing license no longer meets the prescribed conditions and continues to manufacture pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit; if it refuses to rectify after the time limit expires or still fails to meet the prescribed conditions after rectification, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license.  
If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise manufactures substandard pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of manufacturing, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally manufactured products, and the tools, equipment, raw materials, etc. used for the illegal manufacturing; if the value of the illegally manufactured products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license and the corresponding pesticide registration certificate; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.  
If a person entrusts a contractor that has not obtained a pesticide manufacturing license to process or repackage pesticides, or entrusts the processing or repackaging of counterfeit or substandard pesticides, both the principal and the contractor shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the first and third paragraphs of this Article.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　农药生产企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得、违法生产的产品和用于违法生产的原材料等，违法生产的产品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上2万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证和相应的农药登记证：  
（一）采购、使用未依法附具产品质量检验合格证、未依法取得有关许可证明文件的原材料；  
（二）出厂销售未经质量检验合格并附具产品质量检验合格证的农药；  
（三）生产的农药包装、标签、说明书不符合规定；  
（四）不召回依法应当召回的农药。

**Article 53** If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally manufactured products, and the raw materials used for the illegal manufacturing; if the value of the illegally manufactured products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; if the enterprise refuses to correct or the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license and the corresponding pesticide registration certificate:  
(1) Purchasing or using raw materials that are not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate as required by law or that have not obtained relevant license documents as required by law;  
(2) Selling pesticides leaving the factory that have not passed quality inspection and are not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate;  
(3) The packaging, label, or instructions of the manufactured pesticides do not comply with the regulations;  
(4) Failing to recall pesticides that should be recalled in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　农药生产企业不执行原材料进货、农药出厂销售记录制度，或者不履行农药废弃物回收义务的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证和相应的农药登记证。

**Article 54** If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise fails to implement the systems for recording raw material procurement and ex-factory pesticide sales, or fails to fulfill the obligation of recycling pesticide waste, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the enterprise refuses to correct or the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license and the corresponding pesticide registration certificate.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　农药经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令停止经营，没收违法所得、违法经营的农药和用于违法经营的工具、设备等，违法经营的农药货值金额不足1万元的，并处5000元以上5万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）违反本条例规定，未取得农药经营许可证经营农药；  
（二）经营假农药；  
（三）在农药中添加物质。  
有前款第二项、第三项规定的行为，情节严重的，还应当由发证机关吊销农药经营许可证。  
取得农药经营许可证的农药经营者不再符合规定条件继续经营农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令限期整改；逾期拒不整改或者整改后仍不符合规定条件的，由发证机关吊销农药经营许可证。

**Article 55** If a pesticide business operator commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of business, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally operated pesticides, and the tools, equipment, etc. used for the illegal business; if the value of the illegally operated pesticides is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law:  
(1) Operating pesticides without having obtained a pesticide business license in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Operating counterfeit pesticides;  
(3) Adding substances to pesticides.  
If the acts specified in items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph are serious, the issuing authority shall also revoke the pesticide business license.  
If a pesticide business operator that has obtained a pesticide business license no longer meets the prescribed conditions and continues to operate pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit; if it refuses to rectify after the time limit expires or still fails to meet the prescribed conditions after rectification, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide business license.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　农药经营者经营劣质农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令停止经营，没收违法所得、违法经营的农药和用于违法经营的工具、设备等，违法经营的农药货值金额不足1万元的，并处2000元以上2万元以下罚款，货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额2倍以上5倍以下罚款；情节严重的，由发证机关吊销农药经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** If a pesticide business operator operates substandard pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of business, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally operated pesticides, and the tools, equipment, etc. used for the illegal business; if the value of the illegally operated pesticides is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide business license; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　农药经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得和违法经营的农药，并处5000元以上5万元以下罚款；拒不改正或者情节严重的，由发证机关吊销农药经营许可证：  
（一）设立分支机构未依法变更农药经营许可证，或者未向分支机构所在地县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门备案；  
（二）向未取得农药生产许可证的农药生产企业或者未取得农药经营许可证的其他农药经营者采购农药；  
（三）采购、销售未附具产品质量检验合格证或者包装、标签不符合规定的农药；  
（四）不停止销售依法应当召回的农药。

**Article 57** If a pesticide business operator commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally operated pesticides, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the operator refuses to correct or the circumstances are serious, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide business license:  
(1) Establishing a branch without having modified the pesticide business license as required by law, or failing to report to the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the branch is located for filing;  
(2) Purchasing pesticides from a pesticide manufacturing enterprise that has not obtained a pesticide manufacturing license or from another pesticide business operator that has not obtained a pesticide business license;  
(3) Purchasing or selling pesticides that are not accompanied by a product quality inspection certificate or whose packaging or labels do not comply with the regulations;  
(4) Failing to stop selling pesticides that should be recalled in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　农药经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款，并由发证机关吊销农药经营许可证：  
（一）不执行农药采购台账、销售台账制度；  
（二）在卫生用农药以外的农药经营场所内经营食品、食用农产品、饲料等；  
（三）未将卫生用农药与其他商品分柜销售；  
（四）不履行农药废弃物回收义务。

**Article 58** If a pesticide business operator commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if the operator refuses to correct or the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide business license:  
(1) Failing to implement the systems for pesticide procurement ledgers and sales ledgers;  
(2) Operating food, edible agricultural products, feed, etc. within the business premises where pesticides other than sanitary pesticides are operated;  
(3) Failing to sell sanitary pesticides in separate counters from other commodities;  
(4) Failing to fulfill the obligation of recycling pesticide waste.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　境外企业直接在中国销售农药的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业主管部门责令停止销售，没收违法所得、违法经营的农药和用于违法经营的工具、设备等，违法经营的农药货值金额不足5万元的，并处5万元以上50万元以下罚款，货值金额5万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下罚款，由发证机关吊销农药登记证。  
取得农药登记证的境外企业向中国出口劣质农药情节严重或者出口假农药的，由国务院农业主管部门吊销相应的农药登记证。

**Article 59** If an overseas enterprise directly sells pesticides in China, the competent agricultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of sales, confiscate the illegal gains, the illegally operated pesticides, and the tools, equipment, etc. used for the illegal business; if the value of the illegally operated pesticides is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the products shall also be imposed; the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide registration certificate.  
If an overseas enterprise that has obtained a pesticide registration certificate exports substandard pesticides to China under serious circumstances or exports counterfeit pesticides, the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall revoke the corresponding pesticide registration certificate.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　农药使用者有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府农业主管部门责令改正，农药使用者为农产品生产企业、食品和食用农产品仓储企业、专业化病虫害防治服务组织和从事农产品生产的农民专业合作社等单位的，处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，农药使用者为个人的，处1万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不按照农药的标签标注的使用范围、使用方法和剂量、使用技术要求和注意事项、安全间隔期使用农药；  
（二）使用禁用的农药；  
（三）将剧毒、高毒农药用于防治卫生害虫，用于蔬菜、瓜果、茶叶、菌类、中草药材生产或者用于水生植物的病虫害防治；  
（四）在饮用水水源保护区内使用农药；  
（五）使用农药毒鱼、虾、鸟、兽等；  
（六）在饮用水水源保护区、河道内丢弃农药、农药包装物或者清洗施药器械。  
有前款第二项规定的行为的，县级人民政府农业主管部门还应当没收禁用的农药。

**Article 60** If a pesticide user commits any of the following acts, the competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall order correction; if the pesticide user is a unit such as an agricultural product production enterprise, a food and edible agricultural product storage enterprise, a specialized pest control service organization, or a specialized farmer cooperative engaged in agricultural product production, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the pesticide user is an individual, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law:  
(1) Failing to use pesticides in accordance with the scope of use, method and dosage of use, technical requirements and precautions, and pre-harvest interval indicated on the pesticide label;  
(2) Using prohibited pesticides;  
(3) Using acutely toxic or highly toxic pesticides for the prevention and control of sanitary pests, for the production of vegetables, melons and fruits, tea, edible fungi, Chinese herbal medicines, or for the prevention and control of pests and diseases of aquatic plants;  
(4) Using pesticides within protected areas of drinking water sources;  
(5) Using pesticides to poison fish, shrimp, birds, animals, etc.;  
(6) Discarding pesticides, pesticide packaging materials, or cleaning application equipment in protected areas of drinking water sources or river courses.  
Where the act specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph is committed, the competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall also confiscate the prohibited pesticides.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　农产品生产企业、食品和食用农产品仓储企业、专业化病虫害防治服务组织和从事农产品生产的农民专业合作社等不执行农药使用记录制度的，由县级人民政府农业主管部门责令改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，处2000元以上2万元以下罚款。

**Article 61** If an agricultural product production enterprise, a food and edible agricultural product storage enterprise, a specialized pest control service organization, or a specialized farmer cooperative engaged in agricultural product production fails to implement the pesticide use recording system, the competent agricultural department of the county people's government shall order correction; if the unit refuses to correct or the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借农药登记证、农药生产许可证、农药经营许可证等许可证明文件的，由发证机关收缴或者予以吊销，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where any person forges, alters, transfers, leases, or lends license documents such as a pesticide registration certificate, a pesticide manufacturing license, or a pesticide business license, the issuing authority shall confiscate or revoke such documents, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　未取得农药生产许可证生产农药，未取得农药经营许可证经营农药，或者被吊销农药登记证、农药生产许可证、农药经营许可证的，其直接负责的主管人员10年内不得从事农药生产、经营活动。  
农药生产企业、农药经营者招用前款规定的人员从事农药生产、经营活动的，由发证机关吊销农药生产许可证、农药经营许可证。  
被吊销农药登记证的，国务院农业主管部门5年内不再受理其农药登记申请。

**Article 63** Where a person manufactures pesticides without having obtained a pesticide manufacturing license, operates pesticides without having obtained a pesticide business license, or has had its pesticide registration certificate, pesticide manufacturing license, or pesticide business license revoked, the directly responsible person in charge shall not engage in pesticide manufacturing or business activities for a period of ten years.  
If a pesticide manufacturing enterprise or a pesticide business operator employs a person specified in the preceding paragraph to engage in pesticide manufacturing or business activities, the issuing authority shall revoke the pesticide manufacturing license or pesticide business license.  
Where a pesticide registration certificate is revoked, the competent agricultural department of the State Council shall not accept its application for pesticide registration within five years.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　生产、经营的农药造成农药使用者人身、财产损害的，农药使用者可以向农药生产企业要求赔偿，也可以向农药经营者要求赔偿。属于农药生产企业责任的，农药经营者赔偿后有权向农药生产企业追偿；属于农药经营者责任的，农药生产企业赔偿后有权向农药经营者追偿。

**Article 64** If the manufacture or operation of pesticides causes personal injury or property damage to a pesticide user, the pesticide user may claim compensation from the pesticide manufacturing enterprise or from the pesticide business operator. If the liability lies with the pesticide manufacturing enterprise, the pesticide business operator, after paying compensation, has the right to seek recourse from the pesticide manufacturing enterprise; if the liability lies with the pesticide business operator, the pesticide manufacturing enterprise, after paying compensation, has the right to seek recourse from the pesticide business operator.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　申请农药登记的，申请人应当按照自愿有偿的原则，与登记试验单位协商确定登记试验费用。

**Article 65** An applicant for pesticide registration shall, on the principle of voluntariness and compensation, negotiate with the registration trial unit to determine the fees for registration trials.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本条例自2017年6月1日起施行。

**Article 66** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2017.
