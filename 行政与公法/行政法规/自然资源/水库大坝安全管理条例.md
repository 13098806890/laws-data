# 水库大坝安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19

> (1991年3月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第77号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强水库大坝安全管理，保障人民生命财产和社会主义建设的安全，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内坝高15米以上或者库容100万立方米以上的水库大坝(以下简称大坝)。大坝包括永久性挡水建筑物以及与其配合运用的泄洪、输水和过船建筑物等。 坝高15米以下、10米以上或者库容100万立方米以下、10万立方米以上，对重要城镇、交通干线、重要军事设施、工矿区安全有潜在危险的大坝，其安全管理参照本条例执行。 第三条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关主管部门对全国的大坝安全实施监督。县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同有关主管部门对本行政区域内的大坝安全实施监督。 各级水利、能源、建设、交通、农业等有关部门，是其所管辖的大坝的主管部门。 第四条　各级人民政府及其大坝主管部门对其所管辖的大坝的安全实行行政领导负责制。 第五条　大坝的建设和管理应当贯彻安全第一的方针。 第六条　任何单位和个人都有保护大坝安全的义务。 第二章　大坝建设 第七条　兴建大坝必须符合由国务院水行政主管部门会同有关大坝主管部门制定的大坝安全技术标准。 第八条　兴建大坝必须进行工程设计。大坝的工程设计必须由具有相应资格证书的单位承担。 大坝的工程设计应当包括工程观测、通信、动力、照明、交通、消防等管理设施的设计。 第九条　大坝施工必须由具有相应资格证书的单位承担。大坝施工单位必须按照施工承包合同规定的设计文件、图纸要求和有关技术标准进行施工。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强水库大坝安全管理，保障人民生命财产和社会主义建设的安全，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the safety management of reservoir dams and ensuring the safety of people's lives and property and the safety of socialist construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内坝高15米以上或者库容100万立方米以上的水库大坝(以下简称大坝)。大坝包括永久性挡水建筑物以及与其配合运用的泄洪、输水和过船建筑物等。  
坝高15米以下、10米以上或者库容100万立方米以下、10万立方米以上，对重要城镇、交通干线、重要军事设施、工矿区安全有潜在危险的大坝，其安全管理参照本条例执行。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to reservoir dams within the territory of the People's Republic of China with a dam height of 15 meters or more or a storage capacity of 1 million cubic meters or more (hereinafter referred to as dams). Dams include permanent water-retaining structures and flood discharge, water conveyance, and navigation structures that are used in conjunction with them.  
For dams with a dam height of less than 15 meters but not less than 10 meters, or a storage capacity of less than 1 million cubic meters but not less than 100,000 cubic meters, which pose potential danger to the safety of important towns, transportation arteries, important military installations, or industrial and mining areas, their safety management shall be implemented with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关主管部门对全国的大坝安全实施监督。县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同有关主管部门对本行政区域内的大坝安全实施监督。  
各级水利、能源、建设、交通、农业等有关部门，是其所管辖的大坝的主管部门。

**Article 3** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant competent departments of the State Council, supervise the safety of dams nationwide. The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant competent departments, supervise the safety of dams within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments at all levels, such as water conservancy, energy, construction, transportation, and agriculture, shall be the competent departments of the dams under their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府及其大坝主管部门对其所管辖的大坝的安全实行行政领导负责制。

**Article 4** The people's governments at all levels and their competent departments for dams shall implement a system of administrative leadership responsibility for the safety of the dams under their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　大坝的建设和管理应当贯彻安全第一的方针。

**Article 5** The construction and management of dams shall adhere to the policy of safety first.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人都有保护大坝安全的义务。

**Article 6** Every unit and individual shall have the obligation to protect the safety of dams.

### 第二章　大坝建设

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　兴建大坝必须符合由国务院水行政主管部门会同有关大坝主管部门制定的大坝安全技术标准。

**Article 7** The construction of dams must comply with the dam safety technical standards formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council together with the relevant competent departments for dams.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　兴建大坝必须进行工程设计。大坝的工程设计必须由具有相应资格证书的单位承担。  
大坝的工程设计应当包括工程观测、通信、动力、照明、交通、消防等管理设施的设计。

**Article 8** The construction of dams must require engineering design. The engineering design of dams must be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification certificates.  
The engineering design of dams shall include the design of management facilities such as engineering observation, communications, power, lighting, transportation, and fire protection.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　大坝施工必须由具有相应资格证书的单位承担。大坝施工单位必须按照施工承包合同规定的设计文件、图纸要求和有关技术标准进行施工。  
建设单位和设计单位应当派驻代表，对施工质量进行监督检查。质量不符合设计要求的，必须返工或者采取补救措施。

**Article 9** The construction of dams must be undertaken by units with corresponding qualification certificates. The dam construction unit must carry out construction in accordance with the design documents, drawing requirements, and relevant technical standards stipulated in the construction contract.  
The construction unit and the design unit shall station representatives to supervise and inspect the construction quality. If the quality does not meet the design requirements, rework must be carried out or remedial measures must be taken.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　兴建大坝时，建设单位应当按照批准的设计，提请县级以上人民政府依照国家规定划定管理和保护范围，树立标志。  
已建大坝尚未划定管理和保护范围的，大坝主管部门应当根据安全管理的需要，提请县级以上人民政府划定。

**Article 10** When constructing a dam, the construction unit shall, in accordance with the approved design, request the people's government at or above the county level to delineate the management and protection scope and set up markers in accordance with national regulations.  
If the management and protection scope of an existing dam has not been delineated, the competent department for dams shall, based on the needs of safety management, request the people's government at or above the county level to delineate it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　大坝开工后，大坝主管部门应当组建大坝管理单位，由其按照工程基本建设验收规程参与质量检查以及大坝分部、分项验收和蓄水验收工作。  
大坝竣工后，建设单位应当申请大坝主管部门组织验收。

**Article 11** After a dam commences construction, the competent department of dam shall establish a dam management entity, which shall, in accordance with the procedures for acceptance of basic construction projects, participate in quality inspection and the acceptance of dam parts, items, and impoundment.  
After the dam is completed, the construction entity shall apply to the competent department of dam to organize acceptance.

### 第三章　大坝管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　大坝及其设施受国家保护，任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁坏。大坝管理单位应当加强大坝的安全保卫工作。

**Article 12** Dams and their facilities shall be protected by the State. No unit or individual may encroach upon or damage them. The dam management entity shall strengthen the security protection work of the dam.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　禁止在大坝管理和保护范围内进行爆破、打井、采石、采矿、挖沙、取土、修坟等危害大坝安全的活动。

**Article 13** Activities that endanger the safety of dams, such as blasting, well digging, quarrying, mining, sand digging, soil extraction, and tomb building, shall be prohibited within the management and protection scope of dams.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　非大坝管理人员不得操作大坝的泄洪闸门、输水闸门以及其他设施，大坝管理人员操作时应当遵守有关的规章制度。禁止任何单位和个人干扰大坝的正常管理工作。

**Article 14** Persons other than dam management personnel may not operate the flood discharge gates, water conveyance gates, and other facilities of a dam. Dam management personnel shall observe relevant rules and regulations when operating. No unit or individual may interfere with the normal management work of the dam.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止在大坝的集水区域内乱伐林木、陡坡开荒等导致水库淤积的活动。禁止在库区内围垦和进行采石、取土等危及山体的活动。

**Article 15** Activities causing reservoir sedimentation, such as indiscriminate logging of trees and reclamation on steep slopes, shall be prohibited within the catchment area of a dam. Activities such as reclamation, quarrying, and soil extraction that endanger the mountain body shall be prohibited within the reservoir area.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　大坝坝顶确需兼做公路的，须经科学论证和县级以上地方人民政府大坝主管部门批准，并采取相应的安全维护措施。

**Article 16** Where the dam crest indeed needs to be used concurrently as a highway, it shall be subject to scientific demonstration and approval by the competent department of dam of the local people's government at or above the county level, and corresponding safety maintenance measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止在坝体修建码头、渠道、堆放杂物、晾晒粮草。在大坝管理和保护范围内修建码头、鱼塘的，须经大坝主管部门批准，并与坝脚和泄水、输水建筑物保持一定距离，不得影响大坝安全、工程管理和抢险工作。

**Article 17** It shall be prohibited to construct wharves, channels, pile up sundries, or dry grain and grass on the dam body. Where wharves or fish ponds are constructed within the management and protection scope of a dam, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of dam, and a certain distance shall be maintained from the dam toe and the spillway and conveyance structures, and such construction may not affect dam safety, project management, or emergency rescue work.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　大坝主管部门应当配备具有相应业务水平的大坝安全管理人员。  
大坝管理单位应当建立、健全安全管理规章制度。

**Article 18** The competent department of dam shall equip with dam safety management personnel with corresponding professional competence.  
The dam management entity shall establish and improve safety management rules and regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　大坝管理单位必须按照有关技术标准，对大坝进行安全监测和检查；对监测资料应当及时整理分析，随时掌握大坝运行状况。发现异常现象和不安全因素时，大坝管理单位应当立即报告大坝主管部门，及时采取措施。

**Article 19** The dam management entity must, in accordance with relevant technical standards, conduct safety monitoring and inspection of the dam; it shall promptly organize and analyze monitoring data to keep abreast of the dam's operating status at all times. When abnormal phenomena or unsafe factors are discovered, the dam management entity shall immediately report to the competent department of dam and take timely measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　大坝管理单位必须做好大坝的养护修理工作，保证大坝和闸门启闭设备完好。

**Article 20** The dam management entity must carry out maintenance and repair work of the dam to ensure that the dam and the gate opening and closing equipment are in good condition.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　大坝的运行，必须在保证安全的前提下，发挥综合效益。大坝管理单位应当根据批准的计划和大坝主管部门的指令进行水库的调度运用。  
在汛期，综合利用的水库，其调度运用必须服从防汛指挥机构的统一指挥；以发电为主的水库，其汛限水位以上的防洪库容及其洪水调度运用，必须服从防汛指挥机构的统一指挥。  
任何单位和个人不得非法干预水库的调度运用。

**Article 21** The operation of a dam shall achieve comprehensive benefits under the premise of ensuring safety. The dam management unit shall carry out the scheduling and operation of the reservoir in accordance with the approved plan and the instructions of the dam competent authority.  
During the flood season, for reservoirs with comprehensive utilization, their scheduling and operation shall be subject to the unified command of the flood control command authority; for reservoirs mainly for power generation, the flood control storage capacity above the flood limit water level and the flood regulation thereof shall be subject to the unified command of the flood control command authority.  
No unit or individual may illegally interfere with the scheduling and operation of the reservoir.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　大坝主管部门应当建立大坝定期安全检查、鉴定制度。  
汛前、汛后，以及暴风、暴雨、特大洪水或者强烈地震发生后，大坝主管部门应当组织对其所管辖的大坝的安全进行检查。

**Article 22** The dam competent authority shall establish a system for regular safety inspection and expert evaluation of dams.  
Before and after the flood season, and after the occurrence of storms, rainstorms, extraordinary floods or strong earthquakes, the dam competent authority shall organize safety inspections of the dams under its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　大坝主管部门对其所管辖的大坝应当按期注册登记，建立技术档案。大坝注册登记办法由国务院水行政主管部门会同有关主管部门制定。

**Article 23** The dam competent authority shall register the dams under its jurisdiction on schedule and establish technical archives. The measures for dam registration shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant competent authorities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　大坝管理单位和有关部门应当做好防汛抢险物料的准备和气象水情预报，并保证水情传递、报警以及大坝管理单位与大坝主管部门、上级防汛指挥机构之间联系通畅。

**Article 24** The dam management unit and relevant departments shall make preparations for flood control and emergency rescue materials and meteorological and hydrological forecasts, and shall ensure the transmission of hydrological information, alarm, and smooth communication between the dam management unit and the dam competent authority and the superior flood control command authority.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　大坝出现险情征兆时，大坝管理单位应当立即报告大坝主管部门和上级防汛指挥机构，并采取抢救措施；有垮坝危险时，应当采取一切措施向预计的垮坝淹没地区发出警报，做好转移工作。

**Article 25** When signs of danger appear on a dam, the dam management unit shall immediately report to the dam competent authority and the superior flood control command authority, and shall take rescue measures; when there is a danger of dam breach, it shall take all measures to issue an alarm to the expected inundated area of the dam breach and make proper arrangements for evacuation.

### 第四章　险坝处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对尚未达到设计洪水标准、抗震设防标准或者有严重质量缺陷的险坝，大坝主管部门应当组织有关单位进行分类，采取除险加固等措施，或者废弃重建。  
在险坝加固前，大坝管理单位应当制定保坝应急措施；经论证必须改变原设计运行方式的，应当报请大坝主管部门审批。

**Article 26** For dangerous dams that have not yet reached the design flood standard or the seismic fortification standard or have serious quality defects, the dam competent authority shall organize relevant units to classify them and take measures such as danger removal and reinforcement, or abandon and rebuild.  
Before reinforcement of a dangerous dam, the dam management unit shall formulate emergency measures for dam protection; if it is demonstrated that the original design operation mode must be changed, an application shall be submitted to the dam competent authority for examination and approval.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　大坝主管部门应当对其所管辖的需要加固的险坝制定加固计划，限期消除危险；有关人民政府应当优先安排所需资金和物料。  
险坝加固必须由具有相应设计资格证书的单位作出加固设计，经审批后组织实施。险坝加固竣工后，由大坝主管部门组织验收。

**Article 27** The dam competent authority shall formulate reinforcement plans for the dangerous dams under its jurisdiction that need reinforcement, and eliminate the danger within a time limit; the relevant people's government shall give priority to allocating the necessary funds and materials.  
The reinforcement of a dangerous dam must be designed by a unit with corresponding design qualification certificates, and shall be organized and implemented after examination and approval. After the completion of the reinforcement of a dangerous dam, the dam competent authority shall organize the acceptance check.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　大坝主管部门应当组织有关单位，对险坝可能出现的垮坝方式、淹没范围作出预估，并制定应急方案，报防汛指挥机构批准。

**Article 28** The dam competent authority shall organize relevant units to make a pre-estimate of the possible dam breach mode and inundation scope of a dangerous dam, and formulate emergency plans, which shall be submitted to the flood control command authority for approval.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由大坝主管部门责令其停止违法行为，赔偿损失，采取补救措施，可以并处罚款；应当给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)毁坏大坝或者其观测、通信、动力、照明、交通、消防等管理设施的；  
(二)在大坝管理和保护范围内进行爆破、打井、采石、采矿、取土、挖沙、修坟等危害大坝安全活动的；  
(三)擅自操作大坝的泄洪闸门、输水闸门以及其他设施，破坏大坝正常运行的；  
(四)在库区内围垦的；  
(五)在坝体修建码头、渠道或者堆放杂物、晾晒粮草的；  
(六)擅自在大坝管理和保护范围内修建码头、鱼塘的。

**Article 29** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the dam competent authority shall order the cessation of the illegal act, compensation for losses, and adoption of remedial measures, and may also impose a fine; if a public security penalty shall be imposed, the public security organ shall impose the penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Destroying a dam or its management facilities such as observation, communication, power, lighting, transportation, and fire-fighting facilities;  
(2) Carrying out blasting, well drilling, stone quarrying, mining, soil extraction, sand dredging, tomb building, or other activities harmful to dam safety within the dam management and protection area;  
(3) Operating the flood discharge gates, water delivery gates, or other facilities of the dam without authorization, thereby disrupting the normal operation of the dam;  
(4) Reclaiming land from the reservoir area;  
(5) Building docks or channels on the dam body, or stacking sundries, or drying grain and grass on the dam body;  
(6) Building docks or fish ponds within the dam management and protection area without authorization.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　盗窃或者抢夺大坝工程设施、器材的，依照刑法规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 30** Whoever steals or forcibly seizes dam engineering facilities or equipment shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　由于勘测设计失误、施工质量低劣、调度运用不当以及滥用职权，玩忽职守，导致大坝事故的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关对责任人员给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Where a dam accident is caused by survey and design errors, poor construction quality, improper operation and management, or abuse of power and dereliction of duty, the unit to which it belongs or the competent authority at a higher level shall impose administrative sanctions on the responsible personnel; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起15日内，向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，直接向人民法院起诉。当事人逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。  
对治安管理处罚不服的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定办理。

**Article 32** If a party is dissatisfied with a decision on an administrative penalty, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level of the organ that made the penalty decision; if dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, bring a prosecution to the People's Court. The party may also, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, directly bring a prosecution to the People's Court. If the party fails to apply for reconsideration or bring a prosecution to the People's Court within the time limit and also fails to comply with the penalty decision, the organ that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.  
If dissatisfied with a public security administration punishment, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院有关部门和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施细则。

**Article 33** The relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementation rules in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
