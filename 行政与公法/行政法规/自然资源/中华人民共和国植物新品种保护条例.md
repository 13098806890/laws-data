# 中华人民共和国植物新品种保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-04-29  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （1997年3月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第213号公布　根据2013年1月31日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国植物新品种保护条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　2025年4月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第807号第三次修订）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护植物新品种权，鼓励培育和推广应用植物新品种，促进农业、林草业的发展，根据《中华人民共和国种子法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Seed Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of protecting rights to new varieties of plants, encouraging the breeding and application of new plant varieties, and promoting the development of agriculture, forestry and grassland industries.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称植物新品种，是指经过人工选育或者对发现的野生植物加以改良，具备新颖性、特异性、一致性、稳定性和适当命名的植物品种。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, a "new variety of plant" means a variety of plant that has been artificially bred or improved from a discovered wild plant, and which possesses novelty, distinctness, uniformity, stability, and an appropriate denomination.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　植物新品种保护工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻党和国家知识产权战略部署，促进育种创新，推动种业高质量发展。

**Article 3** The protection of new varieties of plants shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the national intellectual property strategy, promote breeding innovation, and facilitate high-quality development of the seed industry.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门按照职责分工负责全国植物新品种保护工作，开展植物新品种权申请的受理和审查，并对符合本条例规定的植物新品种授予植物新品种权（以下称品种权）；健全植物新品种测试体系，完善繁殖材料保藏管理，加强植物新品种保护宣传培训和相关技术研究。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依照各自职责，负责本行政区域内的植物新品种保护工作。

**Article 4** The agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the protection of new varieties of plants nationwide, accept and examine applications for rights to new varieties of plants, and grant rights to new varieties of plants (hereinafter referred to as variety rights) to new plant varieties that meet the requirements of these Regulations; establish a sound testing system for new plant varieties, improve the management of preservation of reproductive materials, and strengthen publicity, training and relevant technical research on the protection of new varieties of plants.  
The agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the protection of new varieties of plants within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对在植物新品种培育和推广应用等工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 5** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the breeding and application of new plant varieties shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生产、经营和推广被授予品种权的植物新品种（以下称授权品种），应当符合有关种子管理的法律法规的规定。

**Article 6** The production, marketing, and promotion of new plant varieties for which variety rights have been granted (hereinafter referred to as authorized varieties) shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on seed management.

### 第二章　品种权的内容和归属

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　品种权所有人（以下称品种权人）对其授权品种，享有排他的独占权。除法律和本条例另有规定外，任何单位或者个人未经品种权人许可，不得对该授权品种的繁殖材料，实施下列行为：  
（一）生产、繁殖和为繁殖而进行处理；  
（二）许诺销售、销售；  
（三）进口、出口；  
（四）为实施本款第一项至第三项行为进行储存。  
实施前款规定的行为，涉及由未经许可使用授权品种的繁殖材料而获得的收获材料的，应当得到品种权人的许可；但是，品种权人对繁殖材料已有合理机会行使其权利的除外。  
对下列品种实施第一款、第二款规定的行为，应当得到授权品种的品种权人的许可：  
（一）授权品种的实质性派生品种，但该授权品种本身不是实质性派生品种；  
（二）与授权品种相比，不具备[本条例第十六条](#art-16)规定的明显区别的品种；  
（三）为商业目的重复使用授权品种进行生产或者繁殖的另一品种。

**Article 7** The owner of variety rights (hereinafter referred to as the variety rights holder) shall enjoy the exclusive right over its authorized variety. Unless otherwise provided by law or these Regulations, no unit or individual may, without the permission of the variety rights holder, carry out the following acts with respect to the reproductive material of the authorized variety:  
(1) producing, propagating, and treating for propagation;  
(2) offering for sale, selling;  
(3) importing, exporting;  
(4) storing for the purpose of carrying out the acts specified in items (1) to (3) of this paragraph.  
Where the acts specified in the preceding paragraph involve harvested material obtained through unauthorized use of the reproductive material of the authorized variety, permission from the variety rights holder shall be obtained; however, this shall not apply if the variety rights holder has already had a reasonable opportunity to exercise its rights over the reproductive material.  
For the following varieties, the acts specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 shall require the permission of the variety rights holder of the authorized variety:  
(1) an essentially derived variety of the authorized variety, unless the authorized variety itself is not an essentially derived variety;  
(2) a variety that, compared with the authorized variety, does not possess the clear distinctness required by Article 16 of these Regulations;  
(3) another variety that repeatedly uses the authorized variety for commercial purposes in production or propagation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家分步实施实质性派生品种制度，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门以目录形式确定具体实施范围，报国务院批准后公布施行。  
实质性派生品种主要依据分子检测、表型测试结果判定，必要时综合考虑育种方法、选育过程、亲缘关系等因素。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当制定实质性派生品种判定指南，确定适用范围、检测和测试方法、判定阈值、技术流程等，并明确检测、测试机构应当具备的条件。  
国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门组建由育种、检测、测试、管理及法律等方面专家组成的专家库，为实施实质性派生品种制度提供专业支持。

**Article 8** The State shall implement the system for essentially derived varieties in a phased manner. The agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council shall determine the specific scope of implementation in the form of a catalogue, and submit it to the State Council for approval before publication and implementation.  
An essentially derived variety shall be determined mainly based on the results of molecular detection and phenotypic testing, and where necessary, factors such as breeding method, selection process, and genetic relationship shall be taken into comprehensive consideration. The agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council shall formulate guidelines for determining essentially derived varieties, specifying the scope of application, detection and testing methods, determination thresholds, technical procedures, etc., and clarify the conditions that detection and testing institutions shall meet.  
The agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council shall establish an expert database composed of experts in breeding, detection, testing, management, and law to provide professional support for the implementation of the system for essentially derived varieties.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　执行本单位的任务或者主要是利用本单位的物质技术条件所完成的职务育种，品种权的申请权属于该单位；非职务育种，品种权的申请权属于完成育种的个人。利用本单位的物质技术条件所完成的育种，单位与完成育种的个人对品种权的申请权有合同约定的，从其约定。  
委托育种或者合作育种，当事人可以在合同中约定品种权的申请权归属；没有合同约定的，品种权的申请权属于受委托完成或者共同完成育种的单位或者个人。  
申请被批准后，品种权属于申请人。

**Article 9** For a service breeding accomplished by executing the tasks of the employing unit or mainly by using the material and technical conditions of the unit, the right to apply for variety rights shall belong to the unit; for a non-service breeding, the right to apply for variety rights shall belong to the individual who accomplished the breeding. Where a breeding is accomplished by using the material and technical conditions of the unit, if there is a contract between the unit and the individual who accomplished the breeding regarding the right to apply for variety rights, such contract shall prevail.  
For commissioned breeding or cooperative breeding, the parties may stipulate the ownership of the right to apply for variety rights in the contract; if there is no contractual stipulation, the right to apply for variety rights shall belong to the unit or individual that was commissioned to accomplish the breeding or that jointly accomplished the breeding.  
After the application is approved, the variety rights shall belong to the applicant.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一个植物新品种只能授予一项品种权。两个以上的申请人分别就同一个植物新品种申请品种权的，品种权授予最先申请的人；同时申请的，品种权授予最先完成该植物新品种育种的人。

**Article 10** Only one variety right may be granted for one new variety of plant. Where two or more applicants separately apply for variety rights for the same new variety of plant, the variety right shall be granted to the person who first filed the application; if the applications are filed simultaneously, the variety right shall be granted to the person who first accomplished the breeding of the new plant variety.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　品种权的申请权和品种权可以依法转让。  
中国的单位或者个人就其在境内培育的植物新品种向外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织转让申请权或者品种权的，应当经国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门批准。  
转让申请权或者品种权的，当事人应当订立书面合同，并向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门登记，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门予以公告。转让自登记之日起生效。  
以品种权出质的，由出质人和质权人共同向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门办理出质登记，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门予以公告。

**Article 11** The right to apply for variety rights and the variety rights themselves may be transferred according to law.  
Where a Chinese unit or individual transfers the right to apply for variety rights or the variety rights for a new plant variety bred within the territory of China to a foreigner, foreign enterprise, or other foreign organization, the transfer shall be approved by the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council.  
Where the right to apply for variety rights or the variety rights are transferred, the parties shall conclude a written contract and register with the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council, which shall publish an announcement. The transfer shall take effect from the date of registration.  
Where a variety right is pledged, the pledgor and the pledgee shall jointly apply to the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council for pledge registration, which shall be announced by such departments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在下列情况下使用授权品种的，可以不经品种权人许可，不向其支付使用费，但是不得侵犯品种权人依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规享有的其他权利：  
（一）利用授权品种进行育种及其他科研活动；  
（二）农民自繁自用授权品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 12** In the following circumstances, the use of an authorized variety may be done without the permission of the variety rights holder and without paying royalties, provided that other rights enjoyed by the variety rights holder under these Regulations and relevant laws and administrative regulations are not infringed:  
(1) using the authorized variety for breeding and other scientific research activities;  
(2) a farmer propagating and using the reproductive material of the authorized variety for his own use.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　为了国家利益或者社会公共利益，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门可以作出实施品种权强制许可的决定，并予以登记和公告。  
取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人应当付给品种权人合理的使用费，其数额由双方商定；双方不能达成协议的，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门裁决。  
品种权人对强制许可决定不服的，品种权人和取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人对强制许可使用费的裁决不服的，可以依法提起诉讼。  
取得实施强制许可的单位或者个人不享有独占的实施权，并且无权允许他人实施。

**Article 13** For the sake of national interest or public interest, the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council may make a decision on compulsory licensing for the implementation of variety rights, and shall register and announce such decision.  
A unit or individual that obtains a compulsory license shall pay a reasonable royalty to the variety rights holder, the amount of which shall be negotiated between the parties; if the parties fail to reach an agreement, the royalty shall be determined by the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council.  
If the variety rights holder is dissatisfied with the compulsory licensing decision, or if the variety rights holder or the unit or individual obtaining the compulsory license is dissatisfied with the ruling on the compulsory licensing royalty, an action may be brought in accordance with law.  
A unit or individual that obtains a compulsory license shall not enjoy the exclusive right of implementation and shall have no right to authorize others to implement it.

### 第三章　授予品种权的条件

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请品种权的植物新品种应当属于国家植物品种保护名录中列举的植物的属或者种。植物品种保护名录由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门确定和公布。  
对违反法律，危害社会公共利益、生态环境的植物新品种，不授予品种权。

**Article 14** A new plant variety for which a variety right is applied for shall belong to a genus or species of plants listed in the national catalogue of protected plant varieties. The catalogue of protected plant varieties shall be determined and published by the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council.  
No variety right shall be granted for a new plant variety that violates the law or endangers social public interests or the ecological environment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　授予品种权的植物新品种应当具备新颖性。新颖性，是指申请品种权的植物新品种在申请日前该品种繁殖材料、收获材料未被销售、推广，或者经申请权人自行或者同意销售、推广该品种繁殖材料、收获材料，在中国境内未超过1年；在境外，木本、藤本植物品种未超过6年，其他植物品种未超过4年。  
2016年1月1日《中华人民共和国种子法》施行后新列入植物品种保护名录的植物的属或者种，从名录公布之日起1年内提出品种权申请的，申请日前在中国境内销售、推广该品种繁殖材料、收获材料未超过4年的，具备新颖性。  
除销售、推广行为丧失新颖性外，下列情形视为已丧失新颖性：  
（一）品种经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依据播种面积确认已经形成事实扩散的；  
（二）农作物品种已审定或者登记2年以上未申请植物新品种权的。

**Article 15** A new plant variety for which a variety right is granted shall possess novelty. Novelty means that, prior to the filing date of the application, the reproductive material or harvested material of the new plant variety has not been sold or promoted, or if sold or promoted by the applicant or with the applicant's consent, such sale or promotion has not exceeded one year within China; outside China, for woody and vine plant varieties, it has not exceeded six years, and for other plant varieties, it has not exceeded four years.  
For a genus or species of plants newly added to the catalogue of protected plant varieties after the Seed Law of the People's Republic of China came into force on January 1, 2016, if an application for variety rights is filed within one year from the date of publication of the catalogue, and the sale or promotion of the reproductive material or harvested material of the variety within China before the filing date has not exceeded four years, it shall be considered to possess novelty.  
In addition to loss of novelty through sale or promotion, the following circumstances shall be deemed as loss of novelty:  
(1) the variety has been confirmed by the agricultural or forestry grassland competent department of the provincial people's government, based on the area sown, to have already been practically disseminated;  
(2) a crop variety that has been examined and approved or registered for two years or more without applying for new plant variety rights.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　授予品种权的植物新品种应当具备特异性。特异性，是指一个植物品种有一个以上性状明显区别于已知品种。

**Article 16** A new plant variety for which a variety right is granted shall possess distinctness. Distinctness means that a plant variety clearly differs from any known variety in one or more characteristics.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　授予品种权的植物新品种应当具备一致性。一致性，是指一个植物品种的特性，除可预期的自然变异外，群体内个体间相关的特征或者特性表现一致。

**Article 17** A new plant variety for which a variety right is granted shall possess uniformity. Uniformity means that, subject to the variation that may be expected from the particular features of its propagation, the relevant characteristics of the variety are sufficiently uniform among its individual plants.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　授予品种权的植物新品种应当具备稳定性。稳定性，是指一个植物品种经过反复繁殖后或者在特定繁殖周期结束时，其主要性状保持不变。

**Article 18** A new plant variety for which a variety right is granted shall possess stability. Stability means that the essential characteristics of a plant variety remain unchanged after repeated propagation or at the end of a particular cycle of propagation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　授予品种权的植物新品种应当具备适当的名称，并与相同或者相近的植物属或者种中已知品种的名称相区别。该名称经授权后即为该植物新品种的通用名称。不论授权品种的保护期是否届满，销售、推广该授权品种应当使用其授权的名称。  
下列名称不得用于品种命名：  
（一）仅以数字表示的；  
（二）违反社会公德的；  
（三）对植物新品种的特征、特性或者育种者身份等容易引起误解的；  
（四）损害他人在先权利的；  
（五）法律、行政法规以及国家规定禁止的其他情形。  
同一植物品种在申请新品种保护和品种审定、品种登记、销售、推广时只能使用同一个名称。

**Article 19** A new plant variety for which a variety right is granted shall have an appropriate denomination, which shall be distinguishable from the denominations of known varieties of the same or closely related genus or species. Once authorized, such denomination shall become the generic name of the new plant variety. Regardless of whether the protection period of the authorized variety has expired, the authorized variety shall be sold and promoted using its authorized denomination.  
The following denominations shall not be used for naming a variety:  
(1) those consisting solely of numerals;  
(2) those contrary to social morality;  
(3) those liable to mislead as to the characteristics, properties, or identity of the breeder of the new plant variety;  
(4) those infringing upon prior rights of others;  
(5) other circumstances prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and State regulations.  
The same plant variety may only use one and the same denomination when applying for new variety protection and for variety examination and approval, variety registration, sale, and promotion.

### 第四章　品种权的申请和受理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中国的单位或者个人申请品种权的，可以直接或者委托代理机构向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门提出申请。  
中国的单位或者个人申请品种权的植物新品种涉及国家安全或者重大利益需要保密的，应当按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 20** A Chinese unit or individual that applies for variety rights may file an application directly or through an agency with the agricultural and forestry grassland competent departments of the State Council.  
If a new plant variety for which a Chinese unit or individual applies for variety rights involves national security or major interests and needs to be kept confidential, the application shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织在中国申请品种权的，应当按其所属国和中华人民共和国签订的协议或者共同参加的国际条约办理，或者根据互惠原则，依照本条例办理。  
在中国没有经常居所或者营业场所的外国人、外国企业或者外国其他组织，向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门提出品种权申请的，应当委托在中国依法设立的代理机构办理。

**Article 21** Where a foreigner, foreign enterprise, or other foreign organization files an application for a variety right in China, the application shall be handled in accordance with the agreement concluded between the country to which it belongs and the People's Republic of China or the international treaty to which both countries are party, or in accordance with these Regulations on the basis of the principle of reciprocity.  
Where a foreigner, foreign enterprise, or other foreign organization that has no habitual residence or business premises in China files an application for a variety right with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council, it shall entrust an agency lawfully established in China to handle the application.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请品种权的，应当向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门提交符合规定格式要求的申请文件。  
申请文件应当使用中文书写。

**Article 22** For an application for a variety right, the applicant shall submit to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council the application documents that conform to the prescribed format requirements.  
The application documents shall be written in Chinese.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门收到品种权申请文件之日为申请日；申请文件是邮寄的，以寄出的邮戳日为申请日。

**Article 23** The date on which the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council receive the application documents for a variety right shall be the date of filing; if the application documents are sent by mail, the postmark date of sending shall be the date of filing.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请人自在外国第一次提出品种权申请之日起12个月内，又在中国就该植物新品种提出品种权申请的，依照该外国同中华人民共和国签订的协议或者共同参加的国际条约，或者根据相互承认优先权的原则，可以享有优先权。  
申请人要求优先权的，应当在申请时提出书面说明，并在3个月内提交经原受理机关确认的第一次提出的品种权申请文件的副本；未依照本条例规定提出书面说明或者提交申请文件副本的，视为未要求优先权。

**Article 24** Where, within 12 months from the date on which the applicant first files an application for a variety right in a foreign country, the applicant files an application for a variety right for the same new plant variety in China, the applicant may enjoy the right of priority in accordance with the agreement concluded between that foreign country and the People's Republic of China or the international treaty to which both countries are party, or on the basis of the principle of mutual recognition of priority.  
Where the applicant claims priority, it shall submit a written statement at the time of filing the application and, within three months, submit a copy of the documents of the first application for a variety right confirmed by the original acceptance authority; if the written statement is not submitted or the copy of the application documents is not submitted in accordance with these Regulations, the claim for priority shall be deemed not to have been made.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对符合[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当予以受理，明确申请日、给予申请号，并自收到申请之日起1个月内通知申请人缴纳申请费。  
对不符合或者经修改仍不符合[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门不予受理，并通知申请人。

**Article 25** For an application for a variety right that conforms to the provisions of Article 22 of these Regulations, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall accept it, specify the date of filing, assign an application number, and notify the applicant to pay the application fee within one month from the date of receiving the application.  
For an application for a variety right that does not conform to, or after amendment still does not conform to, the provisions of Article 22 of these Regulations, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall not accept it and shall notify the applicant.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请人可以在品种权授予前修改或者撤回品种权申请。

**Article 26** The applicant may amend or withdraw the application for a variety right before the variety right is granted.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　任何单位或者个人将在中国境内培育的植物新品种向境外申请品种权的，应当向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门登记；向境外提供繁殖材料的，应当遵守《中华人民共和国种子法》关于向境外提供种质资源的规定。

**Article 27** Any entity or individual that files an application for a variety right abroad for a new plant variety cultivated within the territory of China shall register with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council; where reproductive material is provided abroad, it shall comply with the provisions of the Seed Law of the People's Republic of China on providing germplasm resources abroad.

### 第五章　品种权的审查与批准

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　申请人缴纳申请费后，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门对品种权申请的下列内容进行初步审查：  
（一）是否属于植物品种保护名录列举的植物属或者种的范围；  
（二）是否符合[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)的规定；  
（三）是否符合新颖性的规定；  
（四）植物新品种的命名是否适当。

**Article 28** After the applicant pays the application fee, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall conduct a preliminary examination of the following contents of the application for a variety right:  
(1) whether it falls within the scope of the plant genera or species listed in the catalog of protected plant varieties;  
(2) whether it conforms to the provisions of Article 21 of these Regulations;  
(3) whether it conforms to the provisions on novelty;  
(4) whether the denomination of the new plant variety is appropriate.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当自受理品种权申请之日起3个月内完成初步审查；情况复杂的，可以延长3个月。对经初步审查合格的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门予以公告，并通知申请人在3个月内缴纳审查费。  
对经初步审查不合格的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当通知申请人在3个月内陈述意见或者予以修正。逾期未答复的，品种权申请视为撤回；修正后仍然不合格的，驳回申请。

**Article 29** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall complete the preliminary examination within three months from the date of accepting the application for a variety right; if the circumstances are complex, the time limit may be extended by three months. For an application for a variety right that passes the preliminary examination, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall make an announcement and notify the applicant to pay the examination fee within three months.  
For an application for a variety right that fails the preliminary examination, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall notify the applicant to present arguments or make corrections within three months. If no response is given within the time limit, the application for a variety right shall be deemed to be withdrawn; if it still fails after correction, the application shall be rejected.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请人按照规定缴纳审查费后，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门对品种权申请的特异性、一致性和稳定性进行实质审查。  
申请人未按照规定缴纳审查费的，品种权申请视为撤回。

**Article 30** After the applicant pays the examination fee according to the provisions, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall conduct a substantive examination of the distinctness, uniformity, and stability of the application for a variety right.  
If the applicant fails to pay the examination fee according to the provisions, the application for a variety right shall be deemed to be withdrawn.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门主要依据申请文件和其他有关书面材料进行实质审查。国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门认为必要时，可以委托指定的测试机构进行测试或者考察业已完成的种植或者其他试验的结果。  
因审查需要，申请人应当根据国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门的要求提供必要的资料和该植物新品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 31** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall mainly conduct the substantive examination based on the application documents and other relevant written materials. When the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council deem it necessary, they may entrust a designated testing agency to conduct tests or inspect the results of completed cultivation or other experiments.  
For the needs of the examination, the applicant shall provide necessary materials and the reproductive material of the new plant variety as required by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对经实质审查符合本条例规定的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当作出授予品种权的决定，颁发品种权证书，并予以登记和公告，品种权自授权公告之日起生效。  
对经实质审查不符合本条例规定的品种权申请，国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门予以驳回，并通知申请人。

**Article 32** For an application for a variety right that, after substantive examination, conforms to the provisions of these Regulations, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall make a decision to grant the variety right, issue a variety right certificate, and register and announce it. The variety right shall take effect from the date of the announcement of grant.  
For an application for a variety right that, after substantive examination, does not conform to the provisions of these Regulations, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall reject it and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门设立植物新品种复审委员会（以下称复审委员会）。  
对驳回品种权申请的决定不服的，申请人可以自收到通知之日起3个月内，向复审委员会请求复审。复审请求符合规定的，复审委员会应当自收到之日起6个月内作出决定，并通知申请人。依法需要检测、测试鉴定的，所需时间不计算在规定期限内。  
申请人对复审委员会的复审决定不服的，可以依法提起诉讼。  
复审的具体规定由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门制定。

**Article 33** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall establish a Plant Variety Reexamination Committee (hereinafter referred to as the Reexamination Committee).  
If the applicant is dissatisfied with the decision to reject the application for a variety right, it may, within three months from the date of receiving the notice, request reexamination from the Reexamination Committee. If the reexamination request conforms to the provisions, the Reexamination Committee shall make a decision within six months from the date of receiving the request and notify the applicant. If testing or expert evaluation is required according to law, the time needed shall not be counted within the prescribed time limit.  
If the applicant is dissatisfied with the reexamination decision of the Reexamination Committee, it may institute legal proceedings according to law.  
Specific provisions on reexamination shall be formulated by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　品种权被授予后，在自初步审查合格公告之日起至被授予品种权之日止的期间，任何单位或者个人违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的，品种权人享有追偿的权利。

**Article 34** After the variety right is granted, during the period from the date of announcement of passing the preliminary examination to the date of grant of the variety right, if any entity or individual violates the provisions of Article 7 of these Regulations, the variety right holder shall have the right to claim compensation.

### 第六章　品种权的期限、终止和无效

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　品种权的保护期限，自授权公告之日起，木本、藤本植物为25年，其他植物为20年。

**Article 35** The term of protection of a variety right shall be 25 years for woody and vine plants, and 20 years for other plants, starting from the date of the announcement of grant.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　品种权人应当自被授予品种权的当年开始缴纳年费，并且按照国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门的要求提供用于检测、测试的该授权品种的繁殖材料。

**Article 36** The variety right holder shall start paying annual fees from the year in which the variety right is granted, and shall provide reproductive material of the granted variety for testing and examination as required by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有下列情形之一的，品种权在其保护期限届满前终止：  
（一）品种权人以书面声明放弃品种权的；  
（二）品种权人未按照规定缴纳年费的；  
（三）品种权人未按照国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门的要求提供检测、测试所需的该授权品种的繁殖材料的；  
（四）经检测、测试该授权品种不再符合被授予品种权时的特征和特性的。  
品种权的终止，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门登记和公告。

**Article 37** A variety right shall terminate before the expiration of its term of protection under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the variety right holder makes a written statement renouncing the variety right;  
(2) the variety right holder fails to pay the annual fee according to the provisions;  
(3) the variety right holder fails to provide the reproductive material of the granted variety required for testing and examination as required by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council;  
(4) after testing and examination, the granted variety no longer conforms to the characteristics and properties at the time of grant.  
The termination of a variety right shall be registered and announced by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　自公告授予品种权之日起，复审委员会可以依据职权或者依据任何单位或者个人的书面请求，对不符合[本条例第十五条](#art-15)至第十八条规定的，宣告品种权无效；对不符合[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定的，责令更名，拒不更名的，宣告品种权无效。宣告品种权无效或者更名的决定，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门登记和公告，并由复审委员会通知当事人。  
当事人对复审委员会的无效宣告决定不服的，可以依法提起诉讼。

**Article 38** From the date of the announcement of grant of the variety right, the Reexamination Committee may, ex officio or upon written request of any entity or individual, declare the variety right invalid if it does not conform to the provisions of Articles 15 to 18 of these Regulations; if it does not conform to the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations, it shall order a change of denomination, and if there is refusal to change, declare the variety right invalid. The decision to declare the variety right invalid or to change the denomination shall be registered and announced by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council, and the Reexamination Committee shall notify the parties.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the decision on invalidation of the Reexamination Committee, it may institute legal proceedings according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　被宣告无效的品种权视为自始不存在。  
宣告品种权无效的决定，对在宣告前人民法院作出并已执行的植物新品种侵权的判决、调解书，县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门作出并已执行的植物新品种侵权处理决定，以及已经履行的品种权实施许可合同和品种权转让合同，不具有追溯力。但是，因品种权人的恶意给他人造成损失的，应当给予合理赔偿。  
依照前款规定不返还植物新品种侵权赔偿金、品种权使用费、品种权转让费，明显违反公平原则的，应当全部或者部分返还。

**Article 39** A variety right that has been declared invalid shall be deemed never to have existed.  
The decision to declare a variety right invalid shall not have retroactive effect on judgments or mediation documents on new plant variety infringement that have been made by a People's Court and enforced before the declaration, on decisions on new plant variety infringement that have been made by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland at or above the county level and enforced before the declaration, or on variety right exploitation license contracts and variety right assignment contracts that have been performed. However, if loss is caused to others due to the malicious intent of the variety right holder, reasonable compensation shall be given.  
If the failure to return compensation for new plant variety infringement, variety right royalties, or variety right assignment fees in accordance with the preceding paragraph obviously violates the principle of fairness, the whole or part thereof shall be returned.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人因不可抗力而延误本条例规定的期限或者国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门指定的期限，导致其权利丧失的，自障碍消除之日起2个月内且自期限届满之日起2年内，可以向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门说明理由，并附具有关证明文件，请求恢复其权利。  
当事人因其他正当理由而延误本条例规定的期限或者国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门指定的期限，导致其权利丧失的，可以自收到国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门通知之日起2个月内向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门说明理由，请求恢复其权利；但是，延误复审请求期限的，可以自复审请求期限届满之日起2个月内向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门请求恢复其权利。  
当事人请求延长国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门指定期限的，应当在期限届满前，向国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门说明理由并办理有关手续。  
本条第一款和第二款的规定不适用[本条例第十五条](#art-15)、第二十四条、第三十五条规定的期限。

**Article 40** If a party misses a time limit prescribed in these Regulations or specified by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council due to force majeure, resulting in the loss of its rights, it may, within two months from the date of removal of the obstacle and within two years from the date of expiration of the time limit, explain the reasons to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council and attach relevant supporting documents, and request restoration of its rights.  
If a party misses a time limit prescribed in these Regulations or specified by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council due to other legitimate reasons, resulting in the loss of its rights, it may, within two months from the date of receiving the notice from the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council, explain the reasons to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council and request restoration of its rights; however, if the time limit for requesting reexamination is missed, it may request restoration of its rights from the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council within two months from the expiration of the time limit for requesting reexamination.  
If a party requests an extension of a time limit specified by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council, it shall, before the expiration of the time limit, explain the reasons to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council and go through the relevant procedures.  
The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall not apply to the time limits prescribed in Articles 15, 24, and 35 of these Regulations.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定，有侵犯品种权行为的，由当事人协商解决。不愿协商或者协商不成的，品种权人或者利害关系人可以请求县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依据各自的职权进行处理，也可以依法提起诉讼。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依据各自的职权，根据当事人自愿的原则，对侵犯品种权所造成的损害赔偿可以进行调解。调解达成协议的，当事人应当履行；当事人不履行协议或者调解未达成协议的，品种权人或者利害关系人可以依法提起诉讼。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门处理侵犯品种权案件时，为了维护社会公共利益，责令侵权人停止侵权行为，没收违法所得和植物品种繁殖材料；货值金额不足5万元的，并处1万元以上25万元以下罚款；货值金额5万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款。

**Article 41** Where a violation of Article 7 of these Regulations constitutes an act of infringement of variety rights, the parties shall negotiate for settlement. If negotiation is not desired or fails, the variety rights holder or interested party may request the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level to handle the matter in accordance with their respective functions and powers, or may initiate a lawsuit according to law.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level, in accordance with their respective functions and powers and based on the principle of voluntary participation of the parties, may conduct mediation on the compensation for damages caused by infringement of variety rights. Where a mediation agreement is reached, the parties shall perform it; where a party fails to perform the agreement or mediation fails to reach an agreement, the variety rights holder or interested party may initiate a lawsuit according to law.  
When handling cases of infringement of variety rights, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level shall, for the purpose of safeguarding public interest, order the infringer to cease the infringing act, and confiscate illegal gains and plant variety propagating materials; where the value of the goods is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; where the value of the goods is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　假冒授权品种的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门责令停止假冒行为，没收违法所得和植物品种繁殖材料；货值金额不足5万元的，并处1万元以上25万元以下罚款；货值金额5万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a party counterfeits authorized varieties, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the counterfeiting act, confiscate illegal gains and plant variety propagating materials; where the value of the goods is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; where the value of the goods is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门在查处侵犯品种权案件和假冒授权品种案件时，有权依法采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生产经营场所进行现场检查；  
（二）对植物品种的繁殖材料、收获材料进行取样测试、试验或者检验；  
（三）查阅、复制与涉嫌违法行为有关的合同、票据、账簿、生产经营档案及其他有关资料；  
（四）查封、扣押有证据证明是侵犯品种权或者假冒授权品种的植物品种繁殖材料，以及用于侵犯品种权或者假冒授权品种的工具、设备及运输工具等；  
（五）查封从事侵犯品种权或者假冒授权品种活动的场所。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门依法行使前款规定的职权时，当事人应当协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 43** When investigating and handling cases of infringement of variety rights and cases of counterfeiting authorized varieties, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures according to law:  
(1) Entering the premises of production and operation for on-site inspection;  
(2) Conducting sampling, testing, experimentation or inspection of plant variety propagating materials and harvested materials;  
(3) Reviewing and copying contracts, bills, account books, production and operation archives and other relevant materials related to the suspected illegal acts;  
(4) Sealing up or seizing plant variety propagating materials that have evidence proving to be infringing variety rights or counterfeiting authorized varieties, as well as tools, equipment, and means of transport used for infringing variety rights or counterfeiting authorized varieties;  
(5) Sealing up premises where activities of infringing variety rights or counterfeiting authorized varieties are conducted.  
When the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level exercise the functions and powers provided in the preceding paragraph according to law, the parties shall provide assistance and cooperation, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人就品种权的申请权和品种权的权属发生争议的，可以依法提起诉讼。

**Article 44** Where a party disputes the right to apply for a variety right or the ownership of a variety right, it may initiate a lawsuit according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　对不知道是侵犯品种权的繁殖材料、收获材料实施下列行为，能够证明有合法来源的，不承担赔偿责任：  
（一）为他人繁殖而进行处理；  
（二）许诺销售、销售；  
（三）为实施前两项行为进行储存。

**Article 45** Where a person, without knowing that the propagating materials or harvested materials infringe a variety right, commits any of the following acts, and can prove the legitimate source, it shall not bear compensation liability:  
(1) Handling for propagation for others;  
(2) Offering for sale or selling;  
(3) Storing for the purpose of carrying out the acts mentioned in items (1) and (2).

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门及有关部门工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者索取、收受贿赂的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where staff members of the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level and relevant departments abuse their powers, neglect their duties, engage in malpractice for personal gain, or extort or accept bribes, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在品种权申请过程中存在欺骗、隐瞒、伪造等不诚信行为的，由国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门按照国家有关规定记入相关主体信用记录，并向社会公布。

**Article 47** Where there are dishonest acts such as deception, concealment, or forgery in the process of applying for a variety right, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall record such acts in the credit records of the relevant entities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and publish them to the public.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
（一）繁殖材料是指可用于繁殖的植物整株或者部分，包括籽粒、果实、根、茎、苗、芽、叶、花等；  
（二）收获材料是指经过种植后获得的植物整株或者部分。

**Article 48** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Propagating materials" mean whole plants or parts thereof that can be used for propagation, including seeds, fruits, roots, stems, seedlings, buds, leaves, flowers, etc.;  
(2) "Harvested materials" mean whole plants or parts thereof obtained after planting.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自2025年6月1日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2025.
