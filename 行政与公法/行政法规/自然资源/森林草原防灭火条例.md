# 森林草原防灭火条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-11-16  
**生效日期**：2026-01-01

### 正文

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强森林草原防灭火宣传教育，组织经常性的森林草原防灭火宣传活动，普及森林草原防灭火知识，提高公民的森林草原防火意识和安全意识，为森林草原防灭火工作营造良好社会氛围。

**Article 11** The people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall strengthen publicity and education on forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing, organize regular publicity activities on forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing, popularize knowledge on forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing, enhance citizens' awareness of forest and grassland fire prevention and safety, and create a favorable social atmosphere for forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家鼓励通过保险等形式分散森林草原火灾风险，提高林业和草原防灾减灾能力和灾后恢复重建能力。

**Article 12** The State encourages dispersing forest and grassland fire risks through insurance and other forms, and improving the forestry and grassland disaster prevention and mitigation capabilities and post-disaster recovery and reconstruction capabilities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对在森林草原防灭火工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 13** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　森林草原火灾的预防

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院林业草原主管部门应当制定森林草原火险区划等级标准。省级人民政府林业草原主管部门应当按照森林草原火险区划等级标准，以县为单位确定本行政区域的森林草原火险区划等级，报本级森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）和国务院林业草原主管部门备案，并及时向社会公布。

**Article 14** The competent forestry and grassland department of the State Council shall establish the forest and grassland fire risk zoning classification standards. The competent forestry and grassland department of the provincial people's government shall, in accordance with the forest and grassland fire risk zoning classification standards, determine the forest and grassland fire risk zoning classification within its administrative region on a county basis, report to the forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing command organization (mechanism) at the same level and the competent forestry and grassland department of the State Council for the record, and promptly make it public.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院林业草原主管部门会同国务院应急管理部门等有关部门，在充分衔接相关规划的基础上，根据全国森林草原火险区划等级和实际工作需要，编制全国森林草原火灾防治方案，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准后组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门会同同级应急管理部门等有关部门，根据全国森林草原火灾防治方案，结合本地实际，编制本行政区域的森林草原火灾防治方案，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 15** The competent forestry and grassland department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the emergency management department of the State Council and other relevant departments, on the basis of fully linking up with relevant plans, and in accordance with the national forest and grassland fire risk zoning classification and actual work needs, formulate a national forest and grassland fire prevention and control plan, and organize its implementation after reporting to the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council for approval.  
The competent forestry and grassland department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the emergency management department and other relevant departments at the same level, formulate a forest and grassland fire prevention and control plan for its administrative region based on the national forest and grassland fire prevention and control plan and in light of local realities, and organize its implementation after reporting to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院应急管理部门会同国务院林业草原主管部门等有关部门按照有关规定编制国家森林草原火灾应急预案，报国务院批准。  
县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门会同同级林业草原主管部门等有关部门按照有关规定编制森林草原火灾应急预案，报本级人民政府批准，并报上一级人民政府应急管理部门备案。  
县级人民政府应当组织乡级人民政府、街道办事处根据森林草原火灾应急预案制定森林草原火灾应急处置方案；居民委员会、村民委员会应当按照森林草原火灾应急预案和森林草原火灾应急处置方案的规定，协助做好森林草原火灾应急处置工作。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当定期组织开展森林草原火灾应急预案的演练。

**Article 16** The emergency management department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the competent forestry and grassland department of the State Council and other relevant departments, formulate a national forest and grassland fire emergency plan in accordance with the relevant provisions, and report it to the State Council for approval.  
The emergency management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the competent forestry and grassland department and other relevant departments at the same level, formulate a forest and grassland fire emergency plan in accordance with the relevant provisions, report it to the people's government at the same level for approval, and report it to the emergency management department of the people's government at the next higher level for the record.  
The people's government at the county level shall organize the people's government at the township level and sub-district offices to formulate forest and grassland fire emergency response plans based on the forest and grassland fire emergency plan; residents' committees and villagers' committees shall, in accordance with the provisions of the forest and grassland fire emergency plan and the forest and grassland fire emergency response plan, assist in the emergency response work for forest and grassland fires.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant departments thereof shall regularly organize drills of the forest and grassland fire emergency plan.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　森林草原火灾应急预案应当包括下列内容：  
（一）主要任务；  
（二）组织指挥体系；  
（三）处置力量；  
（四）预警和信息报告；  
（五）应急响应；  
（六）综合保障；  
（七）后期处置。

**Article 17** The forest and grassland fire emergency plan shall include the following contents:  
(1) main tasks;  
(2) organizational command system;  
(3) disposal forces;  
(4) early warning and information reporting;  
(5) emergency response;  
(6) comprehensive support;  
(7) post-event disposal.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家综合性消防救援队伍承担国家规定的森林草原火灾扑救以及预防相关工作。  
地方森林草原消防队伍由县级以上地方人民政府或者规模较大的森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位根据实际需要建立，相关人员应当取得国家规定的职业资格，具体办法由国务院应急管理部门、林业草原主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
地方半专业（兼职）森林草原消防队伍由乡级人民政府、街道办事处和森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位根据实际需要建立。  
国家引导和规范社会力量有序参与森林草原防灭火工作。  
县级以上人民政府按照规定及时调配力量开展森林草原防灭火工作。

**Article 18** The national comprehensive fire rescue teams shall undertake forest and grassland fire fighting and related prevention work as prescribed by the State.  
Local forest and grassland fire fighting teams shall be established by local people's governments at or above the county level or large-scale forest, tree, woodland, and grassland operating units based on actual needs, and the relevant personnel shall obtain the professional qualifications prescribed by the State; the specific measures shall be formulated by the emergency management department and the competent forestry and grassland department of the State Council in conjunction with relevant departments.  
Local semi-professional (part-time) forest and grassland fire fighting teams shall be established by people's governments at the township level, sub-district offices, and forest, tree, woodland, and grassland operating units based on actual needs.  
The State guides and regulates social forces to participate in forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing work in an orderly manner.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly deploy forces to carry out forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing work.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各类森林草原防灭火队伍应当建立防灭火安全制度，按照有关标准配备个人防护装备，定期进行培训和演练，并组织扑救安全教育和安全避险演练，建立联防联训联战机制，加强衔接配合。

**Article 19** All types of forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing teams shall establish fire prevention and extinguishing safety systems, be equipped with personal protective equipment in accordance with relevant standards, conduct regular training and drills, and organize firefighting safety education and safety evacuation drills, establish joint prevention, joint training, and joint combat mechanisms, and strengthen coordination and cooperation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人在其经营区域内承担森林草原防灭火责任，应当建立森林草原防灭火责任制，划定森林草原防灭火责任区，确定森林草原防灭火责任人，并配备森林草原防灭火设施设备，定期排查并及时消除火灾隐患。

**Article 20** Forest, tree, woodland, and grassland operating units and individuals shall assume the responsibility for forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing within their operating areas, shall establish a forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing responsibility system, delineate forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing responsibility zones, designate forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing responsible persons, be equipped with forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing facilities and equipment, and regularly inspect and promptly eliminate fire hazards.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承担电力、通信线路和石油天然气管道的森林草原防灭火责任的经营单位，应当在森林草原火灾危险地段和危险时段，采取相应防灭火措施，排查和消除火灾隐患，并组织开展防火巡护。  
铁路、公路的经营管理单位应当负责本单位经营管理的林地、草原的防灭火工作，并配合县级以上地方人民政府做好铁路、公路沿线森林草原火灾危险地段和危险时段的防灭火工作。  
农牧场、工矿等企业事业单位以及旅游区、开发区经营管理机构，应当负责其经营管理区域内的森林草原防灭火工作，并配合有关单位做好周边地区的防灭火工作。

**Article 21** Operating units that bear the responsibility for forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing for power, communication lines and oil and gas pipelines shall, in forest and grassland fire-danger sections and fire-danger periods, take corresponding fire prevention and extinguishing measures, investigate and eliminate fire hazards, and organize and conduct fire patrols.  
The operating and management units of railways and highways shall be responsible for the fire prevention and extinguishing work of the forest land and grassland under their own operation and management, and shall cooperate with the local people's governments at or above the county level in carrying out the fire prevention and extinguishing work in forest and grassland fire-danger sections and fire-danger periods along the railways and highways.  
Enterprises, public institutions such as farms, industrial and mining enterprises, as well as the operating and management agencies of tourist areas and development zones, shall be responsible for the forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing work within their operating and management areas, and shall cooperate with relevant units in carrying out the fire prevention and extinguishing work in the surrounding areas.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　专职或者兼职护林员、草管员负责巡护森林草原，发现火情应当及时处理并按照有关规定报告，做好防火宣传，并协助有关部门调查森林草原火灾案件。

**Article 22** Full-time or part-time forest rangers and grassland rangers shall be responsible for patrolling forests and grasslands, shall promptly handle and report any fire situation found in accordance with relevant provisions, carry out fire prevention publicity, and assist relevant departments in investigating forest and grassland fire cases.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，加强防火道、隔离带、通信基站、应急广播、瞭望塔、应急水源（储水）等基础设施建设，推动防灭火装备转型升级，建立健全森林草原防灭火物资储备保障体系，及时补充更新防灭火物资。

**Article 23** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, strengthen the construction of infrastructure such as firebreaks, fire barrier belts, communication base stations, emergency broadcasting, watchtowers, and emergency water sources (water storage), promote the transformation and upgrading of fire prevention and extinguishing equipment, establish and improve a forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing material reserve guarantee system, and promptly replenish and update fire prevention and extinguishing materials.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院和省级人民政府应当根据森林草原防灭火实际需要，开展航空消防建设，充分利用卫星遥感技术和现有军用、民用航空基础设施，建立相关单位参与的森林草原防灭火航空协作机制。

**Article 24** The State Council and the provincial people's governments shall, according to the actual needs of forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing, carry out aviation firefighting construction, make full use of satellite remote sensing technologies and existing military and civil aviation infrastructure, and establish a forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing aviation coordination mechanism with the participation of relevant units.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域内森林草原资源分布状况和森林草原火灾发生规律，划定森林草原防火区，规定森林草原防火期，并向社会公布。  
防火期内，县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门和森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人，应当根据森林草原火险气象等级预报和预警信息，在防火区内采取相应的火灾预防和应急准备措施。

**Article 25** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the distribution of forest and grassland resources within their respective administrative regions and the patterns of forest and grassland fires, delimit forest and grassland fire prevention zones, prescribe forest and grassland fire prevention periods, and announce them to the public.  
During the fire prevention period, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, as well as forest, tree, forest land, and grassland operating units and individuals, shall, based on the forest and grassland fire risk meteorological grade forecasts and early warning information, take corresponding fire prevention and emergency preparedness measures within the fire prevention zones.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在防火区内开办农牧场、工矿等企业事业单位，设立旅游区或者新建开发区的，其森林草原防灭火设施应当与该建设项目同步规划、同步设计、同步施工、同步验收；在林区成片造林、在天然草原成片种草的，应当同时配套建设森林草原防灭火设施。森林草原防灭火设施应当由建设单位负责组织验收，验收合格后方可投入使用。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当加强对建设单位验收活动和验收结果的监督核查。  
森林草原防灭火设施验收标准由省级人民政府组织有关部门制定。

**Article 26** Where enterprises, public institutions such as farms, industrial and mining enterprises are established, tourist areas are set up, or development zones are newly built within fire prevention zones, their forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing facilities shall be planned, designed, constructed, and accepted simultaneously with the construction project; where afforestation is carried out in patches in forest areas or grass planting is carried out in patches on natural grasslands, supporting forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing facilities shall be constructed at the same time. The forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing facilities shall be organized for acceptance inspection by the construction unit, and may be put into use only after passing the acceptance inspection. The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the construction unit's acceptance inspection activities and results.  
The acceptance inspection standards for forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing facilities shall be formulated by the provincial people's governments with the organization of relevant departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　防火期内，禁止在防火区内野外用火。因防治病虫鼠害、冻害等特殊情况确需野外用火的，应当经县级人民政府批准，并按照要求采取防火措施，严防失火；需要在防火区内进行非军事实弹演习、爆破等活动的，应当经县级以上地方人民政府批准，并采取必要的防火措施。  
防火期内，中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织进入防火区进行实弹演习、爆破等活动，处置突发事件和执行其他紧急任务的，应当事前采取必要的防火措施，并通报县级以上地方人民政府。  
任何人不得在防火区内丢弃火种。

**Article 27** During the fire prevention period, outdoor fires are prohibited within the fire prevention zones. Where outdoor fires are truly necessary for special circumstances such as the prevention and control of diseases, pests, rodents, frost damage, etc., the matter shall be approved by the county people's government, and fire prevention measures shall be taken as required to strictly prevent fire outbreaks; where activities such as non-military live ammunition exercises, blasting, etc. are required within the fire prevention zones, the matter shall be approved by the local people's government at or above the county level, and necessary fire prevention measures shall be taken.  
During the fire prevention period, where the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organization enter fire prevention zones to conduct live ammunition exercises, blasting, and other activities, handle emergencies, and perform other urgent tasks, they shall take necessary fire prevention measures in advance and notify the local people's governments at or above the county level.  
No person shall discard any fire source within the fire prevention zones.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　防火期内，森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人应当在防火区内设置防火警示宣传标志或者设备，并向进入其经营区域的人员告知防火注意事项。  
防火期内，进入防火区的各种机动车辆和机械设备应当按照规定安装防火装置、配备灭火器材。在防火区内野外作业的机械设备，应当采取防火措施；作业人员应当遵守防火安全操作规程，防止失火。

**Article 28** During the fire prevention period, forest, tree, forest land, and grassland operating units and individuals shall set up fire warning publicity signs or equipment within the fire prevention zones, and inform persons entering their operating areas of fire prevention precautions.  
During the fire prevention period, all types of motor vehicles and mechanical equipment entering the fire prevention zones shall install fire prevention devices and be equipped with fire extinguishing equipment in accordance with regulations. Mechanical equipment used for outdoor operations within the fire prevention zones shall take fire prevention measures; the operators shall comply with fire safety operating procedures to prevent fire outbreaks.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　防火期内，县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门、国务院确定的重点国有林区的管理机构可以设立临时性的防火检查站，结合实际对进入防火区的车辆和人员进行防火登记、检查，开展针对性防灭火宣传教育。

**Article 29** During the fire prevention period, the forestry and grassland competent departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and the management agencies of key state-owned forest areas as determined by the State Council may set up temporary fire prevention checkpoints, conduct fire prevention registration and inspection of vehicles and persons entering the fire prevention zones based on actual conditions, and carry out targeted fire prevention and extinguishing publicity and education.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　防火期内，预报有高温、干旱、大风等高火险天气的，县级以上地方人民政府应当在防火区内划定森林草原高火险区，规定森林草原高火险期。高火险期内，必要时县级以上地方人民政府可以发布命令，严禁一切野外用火；对可能引起森林草原火灾的居民生活用火应当严格管理。

**Article 30** During the fire prevention period, where high fire risk weather such as high temperature, drought, strong winds, etc. is forecast, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall delimit forest and grassland high fire risk areas within the fire prevention zones and prescribe forest and grassland high fire risk periods. During the high fire risk period, when necessary, the local people's governments at or above the county level may issue orders strictly prohibiting all outdoor fires; domestic fires that may cause forest and grassland fires shall be strictly managed.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　高火险期内进入高火险区的，应当经县级以上地方人民政府批准，严格按照批准的时间、地点、范围活动，并接受县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门和同级相关部门的监督管理。

**Article 31** Any person entering a high fire risk zone during a high fire risk period shall obtain approval from the local people's government at or above the county level, shall strictly carry out activities in accordance with the approved time, location, and scope, and shall accept supervision and management by the competent forestry and grassland department of the local people's government at or above the county level and relevant departments at the same level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立森林草原火险分级预警制度，按照森林草原火险的紧急程度、发展势态和可能造成的危害程度分为一级、二级、三级和四级，分别用红色、橙色、黄色和蓝色标示，一级为最高级别。  
县级以上人民政府应急管理部门应当会同同级林业草原主管部门、气象主管机构等，加强森林草原火险监测预警技术研究，综合运用现代化火灾感知方式和多渠道信息加强火险监测预警，建立联合会商机制，及时制作发布森林草原火险预警信息。  
气象主管机构应当加强森林草原火险气象等级预报能力建设，依法提供森林草原火险气象等级预报服务。广播、电视、报刊以及网络服务提供者、电信运营商应当及时、准确、无偿播发或者刊载森林草原火险气象等级预报和预警信息。  
森林草原火险预警信息发布后，县级以上地方人民政府应当根据预警级别依法采取相应措施。

**Article 32** The State shall establish a graded forest and grassland fire risk warning system, under which such risks shall be classified into Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, and Level 4 according to the urgency, development trend, and potential harm of the forest and grassland fire risk, and shall be indicated by red, orange, yellow, and blue respectively, with Level 1 being the highest level.  
The emergency management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the competent forestry and grassland department and the meteorological authority at the same level, strengthen research on forest and grassland fire risk monitoring and early warning technologies, comprehensively use modern fire perception methods and multi-channel information to enhance fire risk monitoring and early warning, establish a joint consultation mechanism, and promptly produce and release forest and grassland fire risk warning information.  
The meteorological authority shall strengthen the capacity for forecasting forest and grassland fire risk meteorological levels, and provide forest and grassland fire risk meteorological level forecast services according to law. Radio, television, newspapers, periodicals, network service providers, and telecommunications operators shall promptly, accurately, and free of charge broadcast or publish forest and grassland fire risk meteorological level forecasts and warning information.  
After the forest and grassland fire risk warning information is released, the local people's government at or above the county level shall take corresponding measures according to law on the basis of the warning level.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上人民政府应急管理部门、林业草原主管部门等有关部门应当按照职责分工对防火区内有关单位的森林草原防灭火组织建设、责任制落实、设施建设等情况进行检查。必要时，县级以上人民政府可以组织相关部门联合检查。检查中发现森林草原火灾隐患，应当及时下达森林草原火灾隐患整改通知书，责令限期消除隐患。被检查单位应当积极配合，不得阻挠、妨碍检查活动。

**Article 33** The emergency management department, the competent forestry and grassland department, and other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, inspect the organization building, implementation of the responsibility system, facility construction, and other aspects of forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression of relevant entities within the fire prevention zone. When necessary, the people's government at or above the county level may organize joint inspections by relevant departments. If forest and grassland fire hazards are found during inspection, a notice of rectification of forest and grassland fire hazards shall be issued promptly, ordering the hazards to be eliminated within a specified time limit. The entity being inspected shall cooperate actively and shall not obstruct or hinder the inspection activities.

### 第三章　森林草原火灾的扑救

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当公布森林草原火警电话等报警方式，建立森林草原防灭火值班制度。任何人发现森林草原火情，应当立即报警。  
森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人对其经营区域内发生的火情，应当立即向当地人民政府或者森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）报告并采取有效处置措施。  
接到报警、报告的当地人民政府或者森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）应当立即派人赶赴现场调查核实，采取相应的处置措施，并按照有关规定逐级报告上级人民政府和森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制），必要时可以越级上报。具备条件的，应当进行网络直报或者自动速报。

**Article 34** The local people's government at or above the county level shall publish the forest and grassland fire alarm telephone number and other means of reporting, and establish a duty system for forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression. Any person who discovers a forest or grassland fire shall immediately report it.  
The operator and individuals of forests, trees, forestland, and grasslands shall immediately report any fire occurring within their operational area to the local people's government or the forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression command organization (mechanism), and take effective measures to handle it.  
Upon receiving the alarm or report, the local people's government or the forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression command organization (mechanism) shall immediately dispatch personnel to the scene for investigation and verification, take corresponding response measures, and report step by step to the higher-level people's government and the forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression command organization (mechanism) in accordance with relevant regulations; when necessary, it may skip levels in reporting. Where conditions permit, direct online reporting or automatic quick reporting shall be carried out.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　发生下列森林草原火情，省级人民政府森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）应当立即报告国家森林草原防灭火指挥部，由国家森林草原防灭火指挥部按照规定报告国务院，并及时通报国务院有关部门和军队有关单位：  
（一）过火森林面积超过100公顷的森林火情，或者过火草原面积超过5000公顷的草原火情；  
（二）造成3人以上死亡或者10人以上重伤的森林草原火情；  
（三）威胁居民区或者重要设施的森林草原火情；  
（四）火场距国界或者实际控制线5公里以内，并对我国或者相邻国家和地区森林草原资源构成威胁的森林草原火情；  
（五）经研判需要报告的其他森林草原火情。  
省级人民政府直接向国务院报告的森林草原火情信息，应当同时抄送国家森林草原防灭火指挥部，国家森林草原防灭火指挥部根据需要及时通报有关单位。

**Article 35** In the event of any of the following forest or grassland fires, the provincial-level people's government's forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression command organization (mechanism) shall immediately report to the National Forest and Grassland Fire Prevention and Suppression Headquarters, which shall report to the State Council in accordance with regulations, and promptly notify the relevant departments of the State Council and relevant military units:  
(1) a forest fire with an burned area exceeding 100 hectares, or a grassland fire with an burned area exceeding 5,000 hectares;  
(2) a forest or grassland fire resulting in three or more deaths or ten or more serious injuries;  
(3) a forest or grassland fire threatening residential areas or important facilities;  
(4) a forest or grassland fire with the fire site within five kilometers of the national border or the actual control line, and threatening the forest and grassland resources of China or neighboring countries or regions;  
(5) other forest or grassland fires that require reporting after assessment.  
Where a provincial-level people's government directly reports forest or grassland fire information to the State Council, a copy shall be sent to the National Forest and Grassland Fire Prevention and Suppression Headquarters at the same time, and the National Forest and Grassland Fire Prevention and Suppression Headquarters shall promptly notify relevant units as needed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　接到火情报告后，地方各级人民政府应当立即按照应急预案开展处置工作。  
国家森林草原防灭火指挥部根据森林草原火灾应对工作需要，适时启动应急响应，组织应急处置。

**Article 36** Upon receiving fire reports, local people's governments at all levels shall immediately carry out response work in accordance with the emergency plan.  
The National Forest and Grassland Fire Prevention and Suppression Headquarters shall, based on the needs of forest and grassland fire response, initiate an emergency response in a timely manner and organize emergency response operations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　发生森林草原火灾，县级以上人民政府有关部门和单位在本级人民政府的统一领导下，按照本级森林草原火灾应急预案分工，履行扑救职责。

**Article 37** In the event of a forest or grassland fire, the relevant departments and units of the people's government at or above the county level shall, under the unified leadership of the people's government at the same level, perform their firefighting duties in accordance with the division of responsibilities under the forest and grassland fire emergency plan at the same level.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　扑救森林草原火灾应当根据需要及时设立火场前线指挥部，设立时机、级别、规模等由县级以上地方人民政府确定。火场前线指挥部由县级以上地方人民政府负责人担任总指挥，同时应当设置专业指挥。各类参与火灾扑救的救援力量应当接受火场前线指挥部的统一指挥。  
火场前线指挥部应当在核实火灾准确位置、范围以及风力、风向、火势的基础上，根据火灾现场天气、地理等条件，合理确定扑救方案、划分扑救地段、确定扑救责任人，科学防范和处置险情。军事设施、核设施、危险化学品生产储存设施设备、油气管道、铁路线路等重要目标物和重大危险源受到火灾威胁时，应当在专业人员指导下消除威胁、科学施救。

**Article 38** In fighting a forest or grassland fire, a fire-site frontline command post shall be established in a timely manner as needed, and the timing, level, scale, etc. thereof shall be determined by the local people's government at or above the county level. The fire-site frontline command post shall have a person in charge of the local people's government at or above the county level serving as the commander-in-chief, and a professional commander shall also be appointed. All rescue forces participating in firefighting shall accept unified command from the fire-site frontline command post.  
The fire-site frontline command post shall, on the basis of verifying the exact location, scope, wind force, wind direction, and fire intensity, and in light of the weather, geographical conditions, and other factors at the fire scene, reasonably determine the firefighting plan, divide the firefighting sectors, designate the persons responsible for firefighting, and scientifically prevent and handle dangers. When important targets such as military facilities, nuclear facilities, hazardous chemical production and storage facilities and equipment, oil and gas pipelines, railway lines, and other major hazard sources are threatened by fire, the threat shall be eliminated under the guidance of professionals, and rescue shall be carried out scientifically.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　扑救森林草原火灾，应当及时解救、疏散、撤离受火灾威胁的群众，并做好火灾扑救人员的管理和安全防护，保障扑救人员安全，尽最大可能避免人员伤亡。  
扑救森林草原火灾，应当以各类森林草原防灭火队伍为主，必要时可以组织干部群众协助扑救，但不得安排未经相关专业培训的人员直接灭火，不得动员残疾人、孕妇和未成年人以及其他不适宜的人员参加灭火。

**Article 39** In fighting a forest or grassland fire, persons threatened by the fire shall be promptly rescued, evacuated, and withdrawn, and the management and safety protection of firefighting personnel shall be ensured to guarantee the safety of firefighting personnel and, to the greatest extent possible, avoid casualties.  
In fighting a forest or grassland fire, various types of forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression teams shall be the main force; when necessary, cadres and the masses may be organized to assist in firefighting, but persons without relevant professional training shall not be arranged to directly fight the fire, and disabled persons, pregnant women, minors, and other unsuitable persons shall not be mobilized to participate in firefighting.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　扑救森林草原火灾时，有关地方人民政府应当立即就地就近组织地方森林草原消防队伍、国家综合性消防救援队伍等力量参与扑救。  
在执行森林草原防灭火任务时，国家综合性消防救援队伍力量调动按照有关规定执行。国家综合性消防救援队伍执行森林草原火灾扑救任务时，其现场最高指挥员进入火场前线指挥部，负责专业指挥工作，参与决策和组织现场指挥。

**Article 40** When fighting a forest or grassland fire, the relevant local people's government shall immediately organize local forest and grassland fire brigades, the national comprehensive fire rescue team, and other forces to participate in firefighting from the nearest available locations.  
When carrying out forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression tasks, the mobilization of forces of the national comprehensive fire rescue team shall be conducted in accordance with relevant regulations. When the national comprehensive fire rescue team performs forest and grassland firefighting tasks, its highest-ranking on-site commander shall enter the fire-site frontline command post, take charge of professional command work, and participate in decision-making and on-site command organization.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　因扑救森林草原火灾需要，县级以上人民政府可以决定采取开设防火隔离带、清除障碍物、应急取水、局部交通管制等应急措施。

**Article 41** Where necessary for fighting forest and grassland fires, people's governments at or above the county level may decide to take emergency measures such as opening firebreaks, removing obstacles, emergency water intake, and local traffic control.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家实行森林草原火灾信息统一发布制度。森林草原火灾信息由县级以上人民政府应急管理部门向社会发布。特别重大、重大森林草原火灾信息，由省级人民政府发布，必要时由国家森林草原防灭火指挥部或者国务院应急管理部门发布。  
县级以上人民政府应急管理部门应当通过多种方式及时、准确向社会发布森林草原火灾和应对工作情况，回应社会关切。任何单位和个人不得编造、故意传播有关森林草原火灾和火灾应对等的虚假信息。

**Article 42** The State shall implement a unified release system for forest and grassland fire information. Information on forest and grassland fires shall be released to the public by the emergency management departments of the people's governments at or above the county level. Information on exceptionally serious and major forest and grassland fires shall be released by the provincial people's government, or when necessary, by the National Forest and Grassland Fire Prevention and Extinguishing Headquarters or the emergency management department of the State Council.  
The emergency management departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall release information on forest and grassland fires and response work to the public in a timely and accurate manner through various means, and respond to public concerns. No unit or individual may fabricate or intentionally disseminate false information concerning forest and grassland fires and fire response.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　森林草原火灾明火扑灭后，扑救队伍应当对火灾现场进行全面检查，清理余火。当地人民政府应当组织足够人员看守火场，按照规定经检查验收合格后，方可撤出看守人员。

**Article 43** After the open flames of a forest or grassland fire are extinguished, the firefighting team shall conduct a comprehensive inspection of the fire scene and clean up residual fires. The local people's government shall organize sufficient personnel to guard the fire scene, and only after the inspection and acceptance have been completed in accordance with the provisions may the guarding personnel be withdrawn.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府应当建立工作机制，因扑救森林草原火灾需要，可以征用场地、物资、设备和交通运输工具等。灭火工作结束后，应当及时返还，并依法给予公平、合理的补偿。

**Article 44** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a working mechanism, and may requisition sites, materials, equipment, and means of transportation, among others, as needed for fighting forest and grassland fires. After the firefighting work is completed, such requisitioned items shall be returned in a timely manner, and fair and reasonable compensation shall be given according to law.

### 第四章　灾后处置

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　根据受害森林草原面积和伤亡人数，森林草原火灾分为一般、较大、重大和特别重大四个等级。  
森林火灾分为：  
（一）一般森林火灾：受害森林面积0.067公顷以上10公顷以下的，或者死亡1人以上3人以下的，或者重伤1人以上10人以下的；  
（二）较大森林火灾：受害森林面积10公顷以上100公顷以下的，或者死亡3人以上10人以下的，或者重伤10人以上50人以下的；  
（三）重大森林火灾：受害森林面积100公顷以上1000公顷以下的，或者死亡10人以上30人以下的，或者重伤50人以上100人以下的；  
（四）特别重大森林火灾：受害森林面积1000公顷以上的，或者死亡30人以上的，或者重伤100人以上的。  
草原火灾分为：  
（一）一般草原火灾：受害草原面积10公顷以上1000公顷以下的，或者死亡1人以上3人以下的，或者重伤1人以上10人以下的；  
（二）较大草原火灾：受害草原面积1000公顷以上5000公顷以下的，或者死亡3人以上10人以下的，或者重伤10人以上50人以下的；  
（三）重大草原火灾：受害草原面积5000公顷以上8000公顷以下的，或者死亡10人以上30人以下的，或者重伤50人以上100人以下的；  
（四）特别重大草原火灾：受害草原面积8000公顷以上的，或者死亡30人以上的，或者重伤100人以上的。  
本条所称“以上”包括本数，“以下”不包括本数。

**Article 45** According to the area of forest or grassland damaged and the number of casualties, forest and grassland fires are classified into four levels: ordinary, relatively serious, serious, and exceptionally serious.  
Forest fires are classified into:  
(1) Ordinary forest fires: where the damaged forest area is 0.067 hectares or more but less than 10 hectares, or where 1 or more but less than 3 persons are killed, or where 1 or more but less than 10 persons are seriously injured;  
(2) Relatively serious forest fires: where the damaged forest area is 10 hectares or more but less than 100 hectares, or where 3 or more but less than 10 persons are killed, or where 10 or more but less than 50 persons are seriously injured;  
(3) Serious forest fires: where the damaged forest area is 100 hectares or more but less than 1,000 hectares, or where 10 or more but less than 30 persons are killed, or where 50 or more but less than 100 persons are seriously injured;  
(4) Exceptionally serious forest fires: where the damaged forest area is 1,000 hectares or more, or where 30 or more persons are killed, or where 100 or more persons are seriously injured.  
Grassland fires are classified into:  
(1) Ordinary grassland fires: where the damaged grassland area is 10 hectares or more but less than 1,000 hectares, or where 1 or more but less than 3 persons are killed, or where 1 or more but less than 10 persons are seriously injured;  
(2) Relatively serious grassland fires: where the damaged grassland area is 1,000 hectares or more but less than 5,000 hectares, or where 3 or more but less than 10 persons are killed, or where 10 or more but less than 50 persons are seriously injured;  
(3) Serious grassland fires: where the damaged grassland area is 5,000 hectares or more but less than 8,000 hectares, or where 10 or more but less than 30 persons are killed, or where 50 or more but less than 100 persons are seriously injured;  
(4) Exceptionally serious grassland fires: where the damaged grassland area is 8,000 hectares or more, or where 30 or more persons are killed, or where 100 or more persons are seriously injured.  
For the purposes of this Article, “or more” includes the given number, and “less than” does not include the given number.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门及时对森林草原火灾发生原因、受害面积和蓄积、人员伤亡、其他经济损失、生态环境影响以及应急救援处置等情况进行调查评估，并形成调查评估报告。森林草原火灾有责任单位、责任人的，当地人民政府应当根据调查评估报告确定火灾责任单位、责任人，并依法处理。  
森林草原火灾调查评估工作按照国家有关规定执行。森林草原火灾损失评估标准，由国务院林业草原主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 46** The people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to investigate and assess in a timely manner the causes of forest and grassland fires, the damaged area and volume, casualties, other economic losses, ecological and environmental impacts, and emergency rescue and response, and shall produce an investigation and assessment report. Where there are responsible units or responsible persons for the forest or grassland fire, the local people's government shall, based on the investigation and assessment report, determine the responsible units and responsible persons for the fire and handle the matter according to law.  
The investigation and assessment of forest and grassland fires shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The standards for assessing losses from forest and grassland fires shall be formulated by the competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　森林草原火灾等级根据调查评估报告确定，发生在灌木林地、疏林地、未成林造林地、苗圃地等林地起火未造成林木受害，或者受害森林面积不足0.067公顷，或者受害草原面积不足10公顷，并且未造成人员死亡、重伤的，不统计为火灾。  
县级以上地方人民政府森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）应当按照国家有关规定对森林草原火灾情况进行统计，报上级人民政府森林草原防灭火指挥机构（机制）和本级人民政府统计机构，并及时通报本级人民政府有关部门。  
森林草原火灾统计调查项目和统计调查制度由国务院应急管理部门会同有关部门依法制定。

**Article 47** The level of forest and grassland fires shall be determined based on the investigation and assessment report. If a fire occurs on shrubland, open forest land, immature forest land, nursery land, or other forest land without causing damage to trees, or if the damaged forest area is less than 0.067 hectares, or the damaged grassland area is less than 10 hectares, and no death or serious injury is caused, such fire shall not be counted as a fire.  
The forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing command institutions (mechanisms) of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, compile statistics on forest and grassland fires, report them to the forest and grassland fire prevention and extinguishing command institutions (mechanisms) of the people's government at the next higher level and the statistical institutions of the people's government at the same level, and promptly notify the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level.  
The statistical survey items and statistical survey system for forest and grassland fires shall be formulated by the emergency management department of the State Council jointly with relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　对因扑救森林草原火灾负伤、致残或者死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予医疗救治、抚恤优待；符合烈士评定条件的，按照国家有关规定评定为烈士。  
参加森林草原火灾扑救的人员按照国家有关规定享受人身保险等相应保险待遇。  
用人单位应当保证其职工在参加森林草原火灾扑救期间的工资待遇和福利不变。

**Article 48** Persons who are injured, disabled, or die as a result of fighting forest and grassland fires shall be given medical treatment, pension, and preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if they meet the conditions for being recognized as a martyr, they shall be recognized as a martyr in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Persons participating in the fighting of forest and grassland fires shall enjoy corresponding insurance benefits such as personal accident insurance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Employers shall ensure that the wages, benefits, and welfare of their employees remain unchanged during their participation in fighting forest and grassland fires.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　参加森林草原火灾扑救的人员的有关补贴补助以及扑救森林草原火灾所发生的其他费用，按照省级人民政府规定的标准，由火灾责任单位或者责任人支付；起火原因不清的，由起火单位支付；以上费用可以由当地人民政府先行垫付。

**Article 49** Subsidies and allowances for persons participating in the fighting of forest and grassland fires and other expenses incurred in fighting forest and grassland fires shall be paid by the responsible unit or the responsible person for the fire according to the standards prescribed by the provincial people's government; if the cause of the fire is unclear, they shall be paid by the unit where the fire originated; the above expenses may be advanced by the local people's government.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　森林草原火灾发生后，森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人应当及时采取更新造林等措施，恢复火烧迹地森林植被和林业、牧业生产条件，当地人民政府应当为森林草原资源的灾后恢复提供必要支持。

**Article 50** After a forest or grassland fire occurs, the operators and individuals of forests, trees, forest land, and grassland shall promptly take measures such as reforestation to restore forest vegetation on the burned area and the conditions for forestry and animal husbandry production. The local people's government shall provide necessary support for the post-disaster restoration of forest and grassland resources.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，地方各级人民政府，县级以上地方人民政府应急管理部门、林业草原主管部门或者其他有关部门及其工作人员，有下列行为之一的，由其上级行政机关责令改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未按照有关规定编制森林草原火灾应急预案，或者未定期组织开展森林草原火灾应急预案的演练；  
（二）发现森林草原火灾隐患未及时下达森林草原火灾隐患整改通知书；  
（三）对不符合森林草原防灭火要求的野外用火予以批准，或者对不符合森林草原防灭火要求的非军事实弹演习、爆破等活动予以批准；  
（四）瞒报、谎报或者故意拖延报告森林草原火情；  
（五）在预警信息发布后未依法采取相应措施；  
（六）未及时采取相应的处置措施或者处置不力；  
（七）在森林草原火灾调查评估中弄虚作假；  
（八）不依法履行职责的其他行为。

**Article 51** Where, in violation of these Regulations, local people's governments at all levels, the emergency management departments, forestry and grassland authorities of local people's governments at or above the county level, or other relevant departments and their staff members commit any of the following acts, the administrative organ at the next higher level shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the leading persons with responsibility and the directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to formulate forest and grassland fire emergency plans in accordance with relevant provisions, or failing to organize drills of forest and grassland fire emergency plans on a regular basis;  
(2) Upon discovering hidden dangers of forest and grassland fires, failing to issue a notice of correction of such hidden dangers in a timely manner;  
(3) Approving outdoor fire use that does not meet the requirements for forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression, or approving activities such as live-fire exercises not involving military activities or blasting that do not meet such requirements;  
(4) Concealing, falsely reporting, or intentionally delaying the reporting of forest and grassland fire conditions;  
(5) After the issuance of early warning information, failing to take corresponding measures according to law;  
(6) Failing to take timely corresponding response measures or taking inadequate response measures;  
(7) Engaging in fraud in the investigation and assessment of forest and grassland fires;  
(8) Other acts of failing to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位或者个人未履行森林草原防灭火责任的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门、应急管理部门按照职责分工责令改正，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下罚款，对个人处1000元以上1万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，对单位处5万元以上20万元以下罚款，对个人处2000元以上2万元以下罚款。

**Article 52** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an entity or individual operating forests, trees, forest land, or grassland fails to fulfill its or his/her responsibility for forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression, the forestry and grassland authority or the emergency management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of duties, order it/him/her to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the entity, or a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the individual; if it/he/she refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, or a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the individual.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，防火区内的有关单位或者个人拒绝接受森林草原防灭火检查，或者接到森林草原火灾隐患整改通知书逾期不消除火灾隐患的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门、应急管理部门按照职责分工责令改正，对单位处1万元以上2万元以下罚款，对个人处300元以上3000元以下罚款。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a relevant entity or individual within a fire prevention zone refuses to accept a forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression inspection, or, upon receiving a notice of correction of hidden dangers of forest and grassland fires, fails to eliminate the hidden dangers within the time limit, the forestry and grassland authority or the emergency management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of duties, order it/him/her to make corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the entity, or a fine of not less than 300 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan on the individual.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，防火期内未经批准擅自在防火区内野外用火的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门责令停止违法行为，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下罚款，对个人处300元以上5000元以下罚款。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of these Regulations, during a fire prevention period, a person without approval uses fire outdoors in a fire prevention zone without authorization, the forestry and grassland authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the entity, or a fine of not less than 300 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan on the individual.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，防火期内未经批准在防火区内进行非军事实弹演习、爆破等活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门责令停止违法行为，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of these Regulations, during a fire prevention period, a person without authorization conducts activities such as live-fire exercises not involving military activities or blasting in a fire prevention zone without approval, the forestry and grassland authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门责令改正，对单位处5000元以上2万元以下罚款，对个人处300元以上3000元以下罚款：  
（一）防火期内，森林、林木、林地、草原经营单位和个人未在防火区内设置防火警示宣传标志或者设备，或者经批准的野外用火未按照要求采取防火措施；  
（二）防火期内，进入防火区的机动车辆和机械设备未按照规定安装防火装置、配备灭火器材；  
（三）防火期内，在防火区内野外作业的机械设备未采取防火措施或者从事野外作业的机械设备作业人员不遵守防火安全操作规程；  
（四）进入防火区的车辆和人员拒绝接受防火登记、检查；  
（五）高火险期内，未经批准擅自进入高火险区活动或者未严格按照批准的时间、地点、范围活动；  
（六）在防火区内丢弃火种；  
（七）破坏森林草原防灭火设施设备；  
（八）在防火区内开办农牧场、工矿等企业事业单位，设立旅游区，新建开发区，或者在林区成片造林、在天然草原成片种草未同时配套建设森林草原防灭火设施，或者新建设森林草原防灭火设施未经验收合格投入使用。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the forestry and grassland authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the entity, or a fine of not less than 300 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan on the individual:  
(1) During a fire prevention period, an entity or individual operating forests, trees, forest land, or grassland fails to set up fire prevention warning signs or equipment in the fire prevention zone, or fails to take fire prevention measures as required for approved outdoor fire use;  
(2) During a fire prevention period, motor vehicles and mechanical equipment entering the fire prevention zone fail to be installed with fire prevention devices or equipped with fire extinguishing equipment as required;  
(3) During a fire prevention period, mechanical equipment used for field operations in the fire prevention zone fails to take fire prevention measures, or the operators of such mechanical equipment fail to comply with fire safety operation procedures;  
(4) Vehicles or persons entering the fire prevention zone refuse to accept fire prevention registration or inspection;  
(5) During a high fire risk period, a person without authorization enters a high fire risk zone for activities, or fails to strictly conduct activities in accordance with the approved time, place, and scope;  
(6) Discarding fire sources in the fire prevention zone;  
(7) Damaging forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression facilities or equipment;  
(8) Establishing enterprises or institutions such as farms, pastures, industrial or mining enterprises, setting up tourist areas, developing new development zones within the fire prevention zone, or carrying out contiguous afforestation in forest areas or contiguous grass planting on natural grasslands without simultaneously building supporting forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression facilities, or putting newly built forest and grassland fire prevention and suppression facilities into use without passing acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　编造并传播有关森林草原火灾和火灾应对等的虚假信息，或者明知是虚假信息而进行传播的，依法予以处罚。

**Article 57** Any person who fabricates and disseminates false information concerning forest and grassland fires and fire response, or disseminates such information knowing it to be false, shall be punished according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　森林草原火灾有责任单位和责任人的，县级以上人民政府林业草原主管部门可以责令其补种树木、恢复植被。

**Article 58** Where there is a responsible entity and a responsible person for a forest and grassland fire, the forestry and grassland authority of the people's government at or above the county level may order them to replant trees and restore vegetation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　森林草原消防车辆按照国家规定享受应急救援车辆相关政策，涉及车辆编制核定和配备的，按照党政机关特种专业技术用车有关规定办理。  
森林草原消防车辆应当按照规定喷涂标志图案，安装报警器、标志灯具。

**Article 60** Forest and grassland firefighting vehicles shall, in accordance with state provisions, enjoy the relevant policies for emergency rescue vehicles; where the verification and allocation of vehicle quotas are involved, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on special technical vehicles for Party and government organs.  
Forest and grassland firefighting vehicles shall, in accordance with the provisions, have emblem patterns painted on them and be equipped with sirens and signal lamps.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　在中华人民共和国边境地区发生的森林草原火灾，按照中华人民共和国政府与有关国家政府签订的有关协定或者部门间合作机制开展扑救工作；没有协定或者机制的，由中华人民共和国政府和有关国家政府协商办理。

**Article 61** For forest and grassland fires occurring in the border areas of the People's Republic of China, firefighting operations shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant agreements signed by the government of the People's Republic of China and the governments of the relevant countries, or in accordance with inter-departmental cooperation mechanisms; where there is no agreement or mechanism, the matter shall be handled through consultation between the government of the People's Republic of China and the governments of the relevant countries.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　对设在林牧区的军事设施、军工设施的森林草原防灭火工作，以及军事区域森林草原防灭火基础设施建设、设施设备配备工作，军事法规、规章另有规定的，从其规定。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵组织执行森林草原火灾扑救任务的，依照有关法律法规的规定执行。

**Article 62** Where military regulations or rules provide otherwise for the forest and grassland fire prevention and firefighting work of military installations and defense industry facilities located in forest and pastoral areas, as well as for the construction of forest and grassland fire prevention and firefighting infrastructure and the equipping of facilities and equipment in military areas, such provisions shall prevail.  
Where the People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force, and the militia organizations perform tasks of fighting forest and grassland fires, they shall do so in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例自2026年1月1日起施行。《森林防火条例》和《草原防火条例》同时废止。

**Article 63** These Regulations shall come into force on January 1, 2026. The Regulations on Forest Fire Prevention and the Regulations on Grassland Fire Prevention shall be abolished simultaneously.
