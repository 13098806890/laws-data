### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国森林法》(以下简称森林法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Forest Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　森林资源，包括森林、林木、林地以及依托森林、林木、林地生存的野生动物、植物和微生物。  
森林，包括乔木林和竹林。  
林木，包括树木和竹子。  
林地，包括郁闭度0.2以上的乔木林地以及竹林地、灌木林地、疏林地、采伐迹地、火烧迹地、未成林造林地、苗圃地和县级以上人民政府规划的宜林地。

**Article 2** Forest resources include forests, trees, forest land, and wild animals, plants and microorganisms that rely on forests, trees and forest land for survival.  
Forests include arbor forests and bamboo forests.  
Trees include trees and bamboos.  
Forest land includes arbor forest land with a canopy density of 0.2 or more, as well as bamboo forest land, shrub land, sparse forest land, cutover land, burned land, immature forest plantation, nursery land, and land suitable for forest designated by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家依法实行森林、林木和林地登记发证制度。依法登记的森林、林木和林地的所有权、使用权受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。  
森林、林木和林地的权属证书式样由国务院林业主管部门规定。

**Article 3** The State shall, according to law, implement a system of registration and certification for forests, trees and forest land. The ownership and right of use of forests, trees and forest land registered according to law shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon them.  
The forms of the certificates of title for forests, trees and forest land shall be prescribed by the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　依法使用的国家所有的森林、林木和林地，按照下列规定登记：  
(一)使用国务院确定的国家所有的重点林区(以下简称重点林区)的森林、林木和林地的单位，应当向国务院林业主管部门提出登记申请，由国务院林业主管部门登记造册，核发证书，确认森林、林木和林地使用权以及由使用者所有的林木所有权；  
(二)使用国家所有的跨行政区域的森林、林木和林地的单位和个人，应当向共同的上一级人民政府林业主管部门提出登记申请，由该人民政府登记造册，核发证书，确认森林、林木和林地使用权以及由使用者所有的林木所有权；  
(三)使用国家所有的其他森林、林木和林地的单位和个人，应当向县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门提出登记申请，由县级以上地方人民政府登记造册，核发证书，确认森林、林木和林地使用权以及由使用者所有的林木所有权。  
未确定使用权的国家所有的森林、林木和林地，由县级以上人民政府登记造册，负责保护管理。

**Article 4** The State-owned forests, trees and forest land that are used according to law shall be registered in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Units that use the State-owned key forest areas (hereinafter referred to as key forest areas) designated by the State Council shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the State Council, and the competent forestry department of the State Council shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the right to use the forests, trees and forest land and the ownership of trees owned by the user;  
(2) Units and individuals that use cross-administrative region State-owned forests, trees and forest land shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the people's government at the next higher level that is common to them, and such people's government shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the right to use the forests, trees and forest land and the ownership of trees owned by the user;  
(3) Units and individuals that use other State-owned forests, trees and forest land shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and such people's government at or above the county level shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the right to use the forests, trees and forest land and the ownership of trees owned by the user.  
State-owned forests, trees and forest land for which the right of use has not been determined shall be registered and compiled in a register by the people's government at or above the county level, which shall be responsible for their protection and management.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　集体所有的森林、林木和林地，由所有者向所在地的县级人民政府林业主管部门提出登记申请，由该县级人民政府登记造册，核发证书，确认所有权。  
单位和个人所有的林木，由所有者向所在地的县级人民政府林业主管部门提出登记申请，由该县级人民政府登记造册，核发证书，确认林木所有权。  
使用集体所有的森林、林木和林地的单位和个人，应当向所在地的县级人民政府林业主管部门提出登记申请，由该县级人民政府登记造册，核发证书，确认森林、林木和林地使用权。

**Article 5** For collectively owned forests, trees and forest land, the owner shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the county people's government at the place where the property is located, and such county people's government shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the ownership.  
For trees owned by units or individuals, the owner shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the county people's government at the place where the property is located, and such county people's government shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the ownership of the trees.  
Units and individuals that use collectively owned forests, trees and forest land shall submit an application for registration to the competent forestry department of the county people's government at the place where the property is located, and such county people's government shall register and compile a register, issue a certificate, and confirm the right to use the forests, trees and forest land.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　改变森林、林木和林地所有权、使用权的，应当依法办理变更登记手续。

**Article 6** Where the ownership or right of use of forests, trees and forest land is changed, the procedures for modification registration shall be handled according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当建立森林、林木和林地权属管理档案。

**Article 7** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall establish archives for the management of the ownership of forests, trees and forest land.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家重点防护林和特种用途林，由国务院林业主管部门提出意见，报国务院批准公布；地方重点防护林和特种用途林，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门提出意见，报本级人民政府批准公布；其他防护林、用材林、特种用途林以及经济林、薪炭林，由县级人民政府林业主管部门根据国家关于林种划分的规定和本级人民政府的部署组织划定，报本级人民政府批准公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的重点防护林和特种用途林的面积，不得少于本行政区域森林总面积的30%。  
经批准公布的林种改变为其他林种的，应当报原批准公布机关批准。

**Article 8** For State key protection forests and special-purpose forests, the competent forestry department of the State Council shall put forward opinions and submit them to the State Council for approval and publication; for local key protection forests and special-purpose forests, the competent forestry department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall put forward opinions and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval and publication; for other protection forests, commercial forests, special-purpose forests, as well as economic forests and fuel forests, the competent forestry department of the county people's government shall organize the delimitation in accordance with the State provisions on forest type classification and the deployment of the people's government at the same level, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval and publication.  
The area of key protection forests and special-purpose forests within the administrative region of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall not be less than 30% of the total forest area of that administrative region.  
Where an approved and published forest type is changed to another forest type, approval from the original approving and publishing authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　依照[森林法第八条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国森林法.md#art-8)第一款第(五)项规定提取的资金，必须专门用于营造坑木、造纸等用材林，不得挪作他用。审计机关和林业主管部门应当加强监督。

**Article 9** Funds extracted in accordance with the provisions of item (5) of the first paragraph of Article 8 of the Forest Law shall be used exclusively for the establishment of commercial forests such as pit timber and papermaking timber, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes. Audit authorities and forestry departments shall strengthen supervision.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院林业主管部门向重点林区派驻的森林资源监督机构，应当加强对重点林区内森林资源保护管理的监督检查。

**Article 10** The forest resource supervision bodies stationed by the competent forestry department of the State Council in the key forest areas shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the protection and management of forest resources in the key forest areas.

### 第二章　森林经营管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院林业主管部门应当定期监测全国森林资源消长和森林生态环境变化的情况。  
重点林区森林资源调查、建立档案和编制森林经营方案等项工作，由国务院林业主管部门组织实施；其他森林资源调查、建立档案和编制森林经营方案等项工作，由县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门组织实施。

**Article 11** The competent forestry department of the State Council shall regularly monitor the nationwide growth and decline of forest resources and changes in the forest ecological environment.  
The work of forest resource survey, establishment of archives, and compilation of forest management plans in key forest areas shall be organized and implemented by the competent forestry department of the State Council; the work of forest resource survey, establishment of archives, and compilation of forest management plans in other forest areas shall be organized and implemented by the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　制定林业长远规划，应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)保护生态环境和促进经济的可持续发展；  
(二)以现有的森林资源为基础；  
(三)与土地利用总体规划、水土保持规划、城市规划、村庄和集镇规划相协调。

**Article 12** The formulation of long-term forestry plans shall follow the following principles:  
(1) protecting the ecological environment and promoting sustainable economic development;  
(2) based on existing forest resources;  
(3) coordinating with the overall land use plan, soil and water conservation plan, urban plan, and village and town plan.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　林业长远规划应当包括下列内容：  
(一)林业发展目标；  
(二)林种比例；  
(三)林地保护利用规划；  
(四)植树造林规划。

**Article 13** Long-term forestry plans shall include the following contents:  
(1) forestry development objectives;  
(2) forest type proportions;  
(3) forest land protection and utilization plans;  
(4) afforestation plans.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　全国林业长远规划由国务院林业主管部门会同其他有关部门编制，报国务院批准后施行。  
地方各级林业长远规划由县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门会同其他有关部门编制，报本级人民政府批准后施行。  
下级林业长远规划应当根据上一级林业长远规划编制。  
林业长远规划的调整、修改，应当报经原批准机关批准。

**Article 14** The national long-term forestry plan shall be formulated by the competent forestry department of the State Council jointly with other relevant departments, and shall come into force after being submitted to the State Council for approval.  
Long-term forestry plans at various local levels shall be formulated by the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level jointly with other relevant departments, and shall come into force after being submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.  
Long-term forestry plans at lower levels shall be formulated in accordance with the long-term forestry plan at the next higher level.  
Adjustments or amendments to long-term forestry plans shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家依法保护森林、林木和林地经营者的合法权益。任何单位和个人不得侵占经营者依法所有的林木和使用的林地。  
用材林、经济林和薪炭林的经营者，依法享有经营权、收益权和其他合法权益。  
防护林和特种用途林的经营者，有获得森林生态效益补偿的权利。

**Article 15** The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of operators of forests, trees and forest land according to law. No unit or individual may encroach upon the trees lawfully owned by operators or the forest land used by them.  
Operators of commercial forests, economic forests and fuel forests shall enjoy the right of operation, the right to proceeds and other lawful rights and interests according to law.  
Operators of protection forests and special-purpose forests shall have the right to obtain compensation for forest ecological benefits.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　勘查、开采矿藏和修建道路、水利、电力、通讯等工程，需要占用或者征收、征用林地的，必须遵守下列规定：  
(一)用地单位应当向县级以上人民政府林业主管部门提出用地申请，经审核同意后，按照国家规定的标准预交森林植被恢复费，领取使用林地审核同意书。用地单位凭使用林地审核同意书依法办理建设用地审批手续。占用或者征收、征用林地未经林业主管部门审核同意的，土地行政主管部门不得受理建设用地申请。  
(二)占用或者征收、征用防护林林地或者特种用途林林地面积10公顷以上的，用材林、经济林、薪炭林林地及其采伐迹地面积35公顷以上的，其他林地面积70公顷以上的，由国务院林业主管部门审核；占用或者征收、征用林地面积低于上述规定数量的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门审核。占用或者征收、征用重点林区的林地的，由国务院林业主管部门审核。  
(三)用地单位需要采伐已经批准占用或者征收、征用的林地上的林木时，应当向林地所在地的县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门或者国务院林业主管部门申请林木采伐许可证。  
(四)占用或者征收、征用林地未被批准的，有关林业主管部门应当自接到不予批准通知之日起7日内将收取的森林植被恢复费如数退还。

**Article 16** Where exploration and mining of mineral deposits or construction of roads, water conservancy, electric power, communications and other projects requires the occupation, or expropriation or requisition, of forest land, the following provisions must be complied with:  
(1) The land-using unit shall submit a land use application to the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level. After examination and approval, it shall pay the forest vegetation restoration fee in advance in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, and obtain the approval letter for the use of forest land. The land-using unit shall handle the construction land approval procedures according to law by presenting the approval letter for the use of forest land. Where the occupation, or expropriation or requisition, of forest land has not been examined and approved by the competent forestry department, the land administration department shall not accept the construction land application.  
(2) Where the area of protection forest land or special-purpose forest land occupied, or expropriated or requisitioned, is 10 hectares or more; the area of commercial forest land, economic forest land, fuel forest land and their cutover land occupied, or expropriated or requisitioned, is 35 hectares or more; or the area of other forest land occupied, or expropriated or requisitioned, is 70 hectares or more, the examination shall be conducted by the competent forestry department of the State Council. Where the area of forest land occupied, or expropriated or requisitioned, is less than the above-mentioned quantities, the examination shall be conducted by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. Where forest land in key forest areas is occupied, or expropriated or requisitioned, the examination shall be conducted by the competent forestry department of the State Council.  
(3) Where a land-using unit needs to fell trees on the forest land that has been approved for occupation, or expropriation or requisition, it shall apply for a tree felling permit to the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the forest land is located, or to the competent forestry department of the State Council.  
(4) Where the occupation, or expropriation or requisition, of forest land is not approved, the relevant competent forestry department shall, within 7 days from the date of receiving the notice of disapproval, return the collected forest vegetation restoration fee in full.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　需要临时占用林地的，应当经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门批准。  
临时占用林地的期限不得超过两年，并不得在临时占用的林地上修筑永久性建筑物；占用期满后，用地单位必须恢复林业生产条件。

**Article 17** Where temporary occupation of forest land is required, approval from the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained.  
The period of temporary occupation of forest land shall not exceed two years, and no permanent structures may be built on the temporarily occupied forest land; after the expiration of the occupation period, the land-using unit must restore the forestry production conditions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　森林经营单位在所经营的林地范围内修筑直接为林业生产服务的工程设施，需要占用林地的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门批准；修筑其他工程设施，需要将林地转为非林业建设用地的，必须依法办理建设用地审批手续。  
前款所称直接为林业生产服务的工程设施是指：  
(一)培育、生产种子、苗木的设施；  
(二)贮存种子、苗木、木材的设施；  
(三)集材道、运材道；  
(四)林业科研、试验、示范基地；  
(五)野生动植物保护、护林、森林病虫害防治、森林防火、木材检疫的设施；  
(六)供水、供电、供热、供气、通讯基础设施。

**Article 18** Where a forest management unit needs to occupy forest land within the scope of the forest land it manages for the construction of engineering facilities directly serving forestry production, approval from the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained; where other engineering facilities are to be constructed and forest land needs to be converted into non-forestry construction land, the construction land approval procedures must be handled according to law.  
The term "engineering facilities directly serving forestry production" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means:  
(1) facilities for cultivating and producing seeds and seedlings;  
(2) facilities for storing seeds, seedlings and timber;  
(3) skid roads and haul roads;  
(4) forestry scientific research, experiment and demonstration bases;  
(5) facilities for wildlife protection, forest protection, forest pest control, forest fire prevention, and timber quarantine;  
(6) infrastructure for water supply, electricity supply, heat supply, gas supply, and communications.

### 第三章　森林保护

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当根据森林病虫害测报中心和测报点对测报对象的调查和监测情况，定期发布长期、中期、短期森林病虫害预报，并及时提出防治方案。  
森林经营者应当选用良种，营造混交林，实行科学育林，提高防御森林病虫害的能力。  
发生森林病虫害时，有关部门、森林经营者应当采取综合防治措施，及时进行除治。  
发生严重森林病虫害时，当地人民政府应当采取紧急除治措施，防止蔓延，消除隐患。

**Article 19** The competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the investigation and monitoring of the objects of forecast by the forest pest forecasting centers and stations, regularly issue long-term, medium-term and short-term forest pest forecasts, and promptly put forward prevention and control plans.  
Forest operators shall select improved varieties, establish mixed forests, practice scientific forest management, and improve their ability to prevent and control forest pests.  
When forest pests occur, relevant departments and forest operators shall adopt comprehensive control measures and promptly carry out eradication.  
When severe forest pests occur, the local people's government shall take emergency eradication measures to prevent the spread and eliminate the hazards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院林业主管部门负责确定全国林木种苗检疫对象。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门根据本地区的需要，可以确定本省、自治区、直辖市的林木种苗补充检疫对象，报国务院林业主管部门备案。

**Article 20** The competent forestry department of the State Council shall be responsible for determining the quarantine objects of national tree seeds and seedlings. The competent forestry department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on the needs of the region, determine supplementary quarantine objects for tree seeds and seedlings of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and report them to the competent forestry department of the State Council for archiving.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止毁林开垦、毁林采种和违反操作技术规程采脂、挖笋、掘根、剥树皮及过度修枝的毁林行为。

**Article 21** Acts of destroying forests by clearing land for cultivation, destroying forests by collecting seeds, and collecting resin, digging bamboo shoots, digging roots, peeling bark, and excessive pruning in violation of operational technical regulations are prohibited.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　25度以上的坡地应当用于植树、种草。25度以上的坡耕地应当按照当地人民政府制定的规划，逐步退耕，植树和种草。

**Article 22** Sloping land with a gradient of 25 degrees or more shall be used for tree-planting and grass-planting. Sloping farmland with a gradient of 25 degrees or more shall be gradually returned to tree-planting and grass-planting in accordance with the plan formulated by the local people's government.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　发生森林火灾时，当地人民政府必须立即组织军民扑救；有关部门应当积极做好扑救火灾物资的供应、运输和通讯、医疗等工作。

**Article 23** When a forest fire occurs, the local people's government must immediately organize the military and civilians to fight the fire; relevant departments shall actively do a good job in supplying, transporting, and communicating materials for fire fighting, as well as medical work.

### 第四章　植树造林

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　森林法所称森林覆盖率，是指以行政区域为单位森林面积与土地面积的百分比。森林面积，包括郁闭度0.2以上的乔木林地面积和竹林地面积、国家特别规定的灌木林地面积、农田林网以及村旁、路旁、水旁、宅旁林木的覆盖面积。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国务院确定的森林覆盖率奋斗目标，确定本行政区域森林覆盖率的奋斗目标，并组织实施。

**Article 24** The term "forest coverage rate" as used in the Forest Law means the percentage of forest area to land area within an administrative region. Forest area includes the area of arbor forest land with a canopy density of 0.2 or more and the area of bamboo forest land, the area of shrub forest land specially designated by the State, and the coverage area of farmland shelterbelts and trees beside villages, roads, water bodies and houses.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the forest coverage rate target determined by the State Council, determine the forest coverage rate target for its own administrative region and organize its implementation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　植树造林应当遵守造林技术规程，实行科学造林，提高林木的成活率。  
县级人民政府对本行政区域内当年造林的情况应当组织检查验收，除国家特别规定的干旱、半干旱地区外，成活率不足85%的，不得计入年度造林完成面积。

**Article 25** Afforestation shall comply with afforestation technical regulations, adopt scientific afforestation methods, and improve the survival rate of trees.  
The county people's government shall organize inspection and acceptance of the afforestation situation in the current year within its administrative region. Except for arid and semi-arid areas specially designated by the State, where the survival rate is less than 85%, it shall not be counted in the annual afforestation completed area.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家对造林绿化实行部门和单位负责制。  
铁路公路两旁、江河两岸、湖泊水库周围，各有关主管单位是造林绿化的责任单位。工矿区，机关、学校用地，部队营区以及农场、牧场、渔场经营地区，各该单位是造林绿化的责任单位。  
责任单位的造林绿化任务，由所在地的县级人民政府下达责任通知书，予以确认。

**Article 26** The State implements a system of responsibility of departments and units for afforestation and greening.  
The relevant competent units are the responsible units for afforestation and greening along both sides of railways and highways, both sides of rivers, and around lakes and reservoirs. In industrial and mining areas, land used by government organs and schools, military camps, and areas managed by farms, pastures and fisheries, each such unit is the responsible unit for afforestation and greening.  
The afforestation tasks of the responsible units shall be confirmed by the county people's government at the place where the unit is located through the issuance of a notice of responsibility.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家保护承包造林者依法享有的林木所有权和其他合法权益。未经发包方和承包方协商一致，不得随意变更或者解除承包造林合同。

**Article 27** The State shall protect the lawful ownership of trees and other lawful rights and interests enjoyed by contract afforestation operators according to law. Without consensus between the contracting party and the contractor, the contract for afforestation by contract may not be arbitrarily changed or terminated.

### 第五章　森林采伐

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家所有的森林和林木以国有林业企业事业单位、农场、厂矿为单位，集体所有的森林和林木、个人所有的林木以县为单位，制定年森林采伐限额，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门汇总、平衡，经本级人民政府审核后，报国务院批准；其中，重点林区的年森林采伐限额，由国务院林业主管部门报国务院批准。  
国务院批准的年森林采伐限额，每5年核定一次。

**Article 28** The annual forest felling quota shall be formulated with State-owned forestry enterprises and institutions, farms, factories and mines as units for State-owned forests and trees, and with counties as units for collectively owned forests and trees and individually owned trees, and shall be aggregated and balanced by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and after examination by the people's government at the same level, submitted to the State Council for approval; among them, the annual forest felling quota for key forest areas shall be submitted by the competent forestry department of the State Council to the State Council for approval.  
The annual forest felling quotas approved by the State Council shall be verified once every five years.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　采伐森林、林木作为商品销售的，必须纳入国家年度木材生产计划；但是，农村居民采伐自留山上个人所有的薪炭林和自留地、房前屋后个人所有的零星林木除外。

**Article 29** Where forests and trees are felled for sale as commodities, they must be included in the national annual timber production plan; however, this does not apply to the felling of fuel forests individually owned on private hills by rural residents and scattered trees individually owned on private plots and in front of and behind houses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请林木采伐许可证，除应当提交申请采伐林木的所有权证书或者使用权证书外，还应当按照下列规定提交其他有关证明文件：  
(一)国有林业企业事业单位还应当提交采伐区调查设计文件和上年度采伐更新验收证明；  
(二)其他单位还应当提交包括采伐林木的目的、地点、林种、林况、面积、蓄积量、方式和更新措施等内容的文件；  
(三)个人还应当提交包括采伐林木的地点、面积、树种、株数、蓄积量、更新时间等内容的文件。  
因扑救森林火灾、防洪抢险等紧急情况需要采伐林木的，组织抢险的单位或者部门应当自紧急情况结束之日起30日内，将采伐林木的情况报告当地县级以上人民政府林业主管部门。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/森林采伐更新管理办法.md#art-5" title="被《森林采伐更新管理办法》第5条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** To apply for a tree felling permit, in addition to submitting the certificate of ownership or certificate of right to use of the trees to be felled, the following relevant supporting documents shall be submitted in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) State-owned forestry enterprises and institutions shall also submit the survey and design documents of the felling area and the acceptance certificate of the previous year's felling and regeneration;  
(2) Other units shall also submit documents including the purpose, location, forest type, stand condition, area, standing stock volume, method, and regeneration measures for felling the trees;  
(3) Individuals shall also submit documents including the location, area, tree species, number of trees, standing stock volume, and regeneration time for felling the trees.  
Where trees need to be felled in emergency situations such as fighting forest fires or flood control and rescue, the unit or department organizing the rescue shall, within 30 days from the date of termination of the emergency situation, report the situation of the felled trees to the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有下列情形之一的，不得核发林木采伐许可证：  
(一)防护林和特种用途林进行非抚育或者非更新性质的采伐的，或者采伐封山育林期、封山育林区内的林木的；  
(二)上年度采伐后未完成更新造林任务的；  
(三)上年度发生重大滥伐案件、森林火灾或者大面积严重森林病虫害，未采取预防和改进措施的。  
林木采伐许可证的式样由国务院林业主管部门规定，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门印制。

**Article 31** Tree felling permits shall not be issued in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) felling of protection forests and special-purpose forests for non-tending or non-regeneration purposes, or felling of trees in a mountain closure period or a mountain closure area;  
(2) where the regeneration afforestation task was not completed after the previous year's felling;  
(3) where major illegal felling cases, forest fires, or large-scale severe forest pests occurred in the previous year and no preventive or improvement measures were taken.  
The form of the tree felling permit shall be prescribed by the competent forestry department of the State Council and printed by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　除森林法已有明确规定的外，林木采伐许可证按照下列规定权限核发：  
(一)县属国有林场，由所在地的县级人民政府林业主管部门核发；  
(二)省、自治区、直辖市和设区的市、自治州所属的国有林业企业事业单位、其他国有企业事业单位，由所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门核发；  
(三)重点林区的国有林业企业事业单位，由国务院林业主管部门核发。

**Article 32** Except as otherwise expressly provided for in the Forest Law, tree felling permits shall be issued in accordance with the following authority:  
(1) For county-owned state forest farms, the permit shall be issued by the competent forestry department of the local county people's government;  
(2) For state-owned forestry enterprises and institutions and other state-owned enterprises and institutions under the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and under the city divided into districts or autonomous prefecture, the permit shall be issued by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(3) For state-owned forestry enterprises and institutions in key forest areas, the permit shall be issued by the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　利用外资营造的用材林达到一定规模需要采伐的，应当在国务院批准的年森林采伐限额内，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门批准，实行采伐限额单列。

**Article 33** Where commercial forests established with foreign investment reach a certain scale and need to be felled, the felling shall be approved by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within the annual forest felling quota approved by the State Council, and a separate felling quota column shall be implemented.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　木材收购单位和个人不得收购没有林木采伐许可证或者其他合法来源证明的木材。  
前款所称木材，是指原木、锯材、竹材、木片和省、自治区、直辖市规定的其他木材。

**Article 34** Timber purchasing units and individuals shall not purchase timber without a tree felling permit or other legally valid source certificate.  
The term "timber" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means logs, sawn timber, bamboo wood, wood chips, and other timber prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从林区运出非国家统一调拨的木材，必须持有县级以上人民政府林业主管部门核发的木材运输证。  
重点林区的木材运输证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门核发；其他木材运输证，由县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门核发。  
木材运输证自木材起运点到终点全程有效，必须随货同行。没有木材运输证的，承运单位和个人不得承运。  
木材运输证的式样由国务院林业主管部门规定。

**Article 35** Timber transported out of a forest area that is not subject to unified State allocation must be accompanied by a timber transport certificate issued by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level.  
Timber transport certificates for key forest areas shall be issued by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; other timber transport certificates shall be issued by the competent forestry department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
A timber transport certificate shall be valid for the entire journey from the point of origin to the destination and must accompany the goods. Without a timber transport certificate, carriers and individuals shall not transport the timber.  
The form of the timber transport certificate shall be prescribed by the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　申请木材运输证，应当提交下列证明文件：  
(一)林木采伐许可证或者其他合法来源证明；  
(二)检疫证明；  
(三)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业主管部门规定的其他文件。  
符合前款条件的，受理木材运输证申请的县级以上人民政府林业主管部门应当自接到申请之日起3日内发给木材运输证。  
依法发放的木材运输证所准运的木材运输总量，不得超过当地年度木材生产计划规定可以运出销售的木材总量。

**Article 36** To apply for a timber transport certificate, the following supporting documents shall be submitted:  
(1) a tree felling permit or other legally valid source certificate;  
(2) a quarantine certificate;  
(3) other documents prescribed by the competent forestry department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
If the conditions in the preceding paragraph are met, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level that accepted the application for a timber transport certificate shall, within 3 days from the date of receiving the application, issue a timber transport certificate.  
The total volume of timber permitted for transport under the timber transport certificates issued according to law shall not exceed the total volume of timber that may be transported and sold as provided for in the local annual timber production plan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准在林区设立的木材检查站，负责检查木材运输；无证运输木材的，木材检查站应当予以制止，可以暂扣无证运输的木材，并立即报请县级以上人民政府林业主管部门依法处理。

**Article 37** Timber inspection stations established in forest areas with the approval of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for inspecting timber transport; where timber is transported without a certificate, the timber inspection station shall stop it, may temporarily detain the timber transported without a certificate, and immediately report to the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level for handling according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　盗伐森林或者其他林木，以立木材积计算不足0.5立方米或者幼树不足20株的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令补种盗伐株数10倍的树木，没收盗伐的林木或者变卖所得，并处盗伐林木价值3倍至5倍的罚款。  
盗伐森林或者其他林木，以立木材积计算0.5立方米以上或者幼树20株以上的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令补种盗伐株数10倍的树木，没收盗伐的林木或者变卖所得，并处盗伐林木价值5倍至10倍的罚款。

**Article 38** Where forest or other trees are illegally felled, and the standing stock volume calculated is less than 0.5 cubic meters or the number of saplings is less than 20, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the replanting of 10 times the number of trees illegally felled, confiscate the illegally felled trees or the proceeds from their sale, and impose a fine of not less than 3 times but not more than 5 times the value of the illegally felled trees.  
Where forest or other trees are illegally felled, and the standing stock volume calculated is 0.5 cubic meters or more or the number of saplings is 20 or more, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the replanting of 10 times the number of trees illegally felled, confiscate the illegally felled trees or the proceeds from their sale, and impose a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the value of the illegally felled trees.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　滥伐森林或者其他林木，以立木材积计算不足2立方米或者幼树不足50株的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令补种滥伐株数5倍的树木，并处滥伐林木价值2倍至3倍的罚款。  
滥伐森林或者其他林木，以立木材积计算2立方米以上或者幼树50株以上的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令补种滥伐株数5倍的树木，并处滥伐林木价值3倍至5倍的罚款。  
超过木材生产计划采伐森林或者其他林木的，依照前两款规定处罚。

**Article 39** Where forest or other trees are excessively felled, and the standing stock volume calculated is less than 2 cubic meters or the number of saplings is less than 50, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the replanting of 5 times the number of trees excessively felled, and impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 3 times the value of the excessively felled trees.  
Where forest or other trees are excessively felled, and the standing stock volume calculated is 2 cubic meters or more or the number of saplings is 50 or more, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the replanting of 5 times the number of trees excessively felled, and impose a fine of not less than 3 times but not more than 5 times the value of the excessively felled trees.  
Where forest or other trees are felled in excess of the timber production plan, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，收购没有林木采伐许可证或者其他合法来源证明的木材的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收非法经营的木材和违法所得，并处违法所得2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, timber without a tree felling permit or other legally valid source certificate is purchased, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegally traded timber and the illegal income, and impose a fine of not more than 2 times the illegal income.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，毁林采种或者违反操作技术规程采脂、挖笋、掘根、剥树皮及过度修枝，致使森林、林木受到毁坏的，依法赔偿损失，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，补种毁坏株数1倍至3倍的树木，可以处毁坏林木价值1倍至5倍的罚款；拒不补种树木或者补种不符合国家有关规定的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门组织代为补种，所需费用由违法者支付。  
违反森林法和本条例规定，擅自开垦林地，致使森林、林木受到毁坏的，依照[森林法第四十四条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国森林法.md#art-44)的规定予以处罚；对森林、林木未造成毁坏或者被开垦的林地上没有森林、林木的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状，可以处非法开垦林地每平方米10元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, acts such as destroying forests by collecting seeds, or collecting resin, digging bamboo shoots, digging roots, peeling bark, or excessive pruning in violation of operational technical regulations cause damage to forests or trees, compensation for losses shall be paid according to law, and the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, order the replanting of 1 to 3 times the number of damaged trees, and may impose a fine of not less than 1 time but not more than 5 times the value of the damaged trees; if the replanting is refused or does not comply with the relevant State provisions, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize the replanting on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of the Forest Law and these Regulations, forest land is opened up without authorization, causing damage to forests or trees, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of the Forest Law; where no damage is caused to forests or trees, or there are no forests or trees on the opened-up forest land, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, order restoration to the original condition within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 10 yuan per square meter of the illegally opened-up forest land.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期完成造林任务；逾期未完成的，可以处应完成而未完成造林任务所需费用2倍以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分：  
(一)连续两年未完成更新造林任务的；  
(二)当年更新造林面积未达到应更新造林面积50%的；  
(三)除国家特别规定的干旱、半干旱地区外，更新造林当年成活率未达到85%的；  
(四)植树造林责任单位未按照所在地县级人民政府的要求按时完成造林任务的。

**Article 42** In any of the following circumstances, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the completion of the afforestation task within a specified time limit; if the task is not completed within the specified time limit, a fine of not more than 2 times the cost required for the unfinished afforestation task may be imposed; and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to complete the regeneration afforestation task for two consecutive years;  
(2) the area of regeneration afforestation in the current year is less than 50% of the area that should be regenerated;  
(3) except for arid and semi-arid areas specially designated by the State, the survival rate of regeneration afforestation in the current year is less than 85%;  
(4) the responsible unit for afforestation fails to complete the afforestation task on time in accordance with the requirements of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　未经县级以上人民政府林业主管部门审核同意，擅自改变林地用途的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期恢复原状，并处非法改变用途林地每平方米10元至30元的罚款。  
临时占用林地，逾期不归还的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 43** Where, without examination and approval by the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level, the use of forest land is changed without authorization, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order restoration to the original condition within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 10 yuan but not more than 30 yuan per square meter of the forest land whose use has been illegally changed.  
Where forest land is temporarily occupied and not returned after the expiration of the period, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　无木材运输证运输木材的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收非法运输的木材，对货主可以并处非法运输木材价款30%以下的罚款。  
运输的木材数量超出木材运输证所准运的运输数量的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收超出部分的木材；运输的木材树种、材种、规格与木材运输证规定不符又无正当理由的，没收其不相符部分的木材。  
使用伪造、涂改的木材运输证运输木材的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收非法运输的木材，并处没收木材价款10%至50%的罚款。  
承运无木材运输证的木材的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门没收运费，并处运费1倍至3倍的罚款。

**Article 44** Where timber is transported without a timber transport certificate, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegally transported timber, and may also impose a fine of not more than 30% of the price of the illegally transported timber on the owner of the goods.  
Where the quantity of timber transported exceeds the quantity permitted under the timber transport certificate, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the excess timber; where the tree species, timber type or specifications of the timber transported do not conform to those stated in the timber transport certificate and no justifiable reason is given, the timber that does not conform shall be confiscated.  
Where timber is transported using a forged or altered timber transport certificate, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegally transported timber and impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 50% of the confiscated timber price.  
Where a carrier transports timber without a timber transport certificate, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the freight and impose a fine of not less than 1 time but not more than 3 times the freight.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　擅自移动或者毁坏林业服务标志的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门责令限期恢复原状；逾期不恢复原状的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门代为恢复，所需费用由违法者支付。

**Article 45** Where forestry service signs are moved or damaged without authorization, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order restoration to the original condition within a specified time limit; if restoration is not made within the specified time limit, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall restore them on behalf of the violator, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，未经批准，擅自将防护林和特种用途林改变为其他林种的，由县级以上人民政府林业主管部门收回经营者所获取的森林生态效益补偿，并处所获取森林生态效益补偿3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, without approval, protection forests or special-purpose forests are changed to other forest types, the competent forestry department of the people's government at or above the county level shall recover the forest ecological benefit compensation obtained by the operator and impose a fine of not more than 3 times the amount of such compensation obtained.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例中县级以上地方人民政府林业主管部门职责权限的划分，由国务院林业主管部门具体规定。

**Article 47** The division of functions and powers of the competent forestry departments of local people's governments at or above the county level mentioned in these Regulations shall be specifically prescribed by the competent forestry department of the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1986年4月28日国务院批准、1986年5月10日林业部发布的《中华人民共和国森林法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China, approved by the State Council on April 28, 1986, and promulgated by the Ministry of Forestry on May 10, 1986, shall be simultaneously abolished.
