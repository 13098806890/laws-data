# 地震预报管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1998-12-17  
**生效日期**：1998-12-17

> (1998年12月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第255号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对地震预报的管理，规范发布地震预报行为，根据《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事地震预报活动，必须遵守本条例。 第三条　地震预报包括下列类型： (一)地震长期预报，是指对未来10年内可能发生破坏性地震的地域的预报； (二)地震中期预报，是指对未来一二年内可能发生破坏性地震的地域和强度的预报； (三)地震短期预报，是指对3个月内将要发生地震的时间、地点、震级的预报； (四)临震预报，是指对10日内将要发生地震的时间、地点、震级的预报。 第四条　国家鼓励和扶持地震预报的科学技术研究，提高地震预报水平。 对在地震预报工作中做出突出贡献或者显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。 第二章　地震预报意见的形成 第五条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构，应当加强地震预测工作。 第六条　任何单位和个人根据地震观测资料和研究结果提出的地震预测意见，应当向所在地或者所预测地区的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构书面报告，也可以直接向国务院地震工作主管部门书面报告，不得向社会散布。 任何单位和个人不得向国(境)外提出地震预测意见；但是，以长期、中期地震活动趋势研究成果进行学术交流的除外。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对地震预报的管理，规范发布地震预报行为，根据《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protecting Against and Mitigating Earthquake Disasters for the purposes of strengthening the management of earthquake prediction and regulating the acts of issuing earthquake predictions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事地震预报活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Anyone who engages in earthquake prediction activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地震预报包括下列类型：  
(一)地震长期预报，是指对未来10年内可能发生破坏性地震的地域的预报；  
(二)地震中期预报，是指对未来一二年内可能发生破坏性地震的地域和强度的预报；  
(三)地震短期预报，是指对3个月内将要发生地震的时间、地点、震级的预报；  
(四)临震预报，是指对10日内将要发生地震的时间、地点、震级的预报。

**Article 3** Earthquake predictions include the following types:  
(1) Long-term earthquake prediction means the prediction of areas where destructive earthquakes may occur within the next 10 years;  
(2) Medium-term earthquake prediction means the prediction of areas and intensities where destructive earthquakes may occur within the next one to two years;  
(3) Short-term earthquake prediction means the prediction of the time, location, and magnitude of earthquakes that will occur within the next three months;  
(4) Imminent earthquake prediction means the prediction of the time, location, and magnitude of earthquakes that will occur within the next 10 days.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励和扶持地震预报的科学技术研究，提高地震预报水平。  
对在地震预报工作中做出突出贡献或者显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 4** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on earthquake prediction to improve the level of earthquake prediction.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions or remarkable achievements in earthquake prediction work shall be given rewards.

### 第二章　地震预报意见的形成

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构，应当加强地震预测工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen earthquake prediction work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人根据地震观测资料和研究结果提出的地震预测意见，应当向所在地或者所预测地区的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构书面报告，也可以直接向国务院地震工作主管部门书面报告，不得向社会散布。  
任何单位和个人不得向国(境)外提出地震预测意见；但是，以长期、中期地震活动趋势研究成果进行学术交流的除外。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual that puts forward earthquake prediction opinions based on earthquake observation data and research results shall submit a written report to the institution responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the local people's government at or above the county level at the location or of the predicted area, and may also directly submit a written report to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council, and shall not disseminate such opinions to the public.  
No unit or individual may submit earthquake prediction opinions to any country (region) outside China; except for academic exchanges conducted with research results on long-term and medium-term earthquake activity trends.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人观察到与地震有关的异常现象时，应当及时向所在地的县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构报告。

**Article 7** When any unit or individual observes abnormal phenomena related to earthquakes, it or he shall promptly report to the institution responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the local people's government at or above the county level at the location.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院地震工作主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构应当组织召开地震震情会商会，对各种地震预测意见和与地震有关的异常现象进行综合分析研究，形成地震预报意见。  
市、县人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构可以组织召开地震震情会商会，形成地震预报意见，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构报告。

**Article 8** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize and convene earthquake situation consultation meetings to conduct comprehensive analysis and research on various earthquake prediction opinions and abnormal phenomena related to earthquakes, and form earthquake prediction opinions.  
The institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's governments of cities and counties may organize and convene earthquake situation consultation meetings to form earthquake prediction opinions and report to the institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　地震预报意见的评审

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地震预报意见实行评审制度。评审包括下列内容：  
(一)地震预报意见的科学性、可能性；  
(二)地震预报的发布形式；  
(三)地震预报发布后可能产生的社会、经济影响。

**Article 9** A review system shall be implemented for earthquake prediction opinions. The review includes the following contents:  
(1) The scientific nature and possibility of the earthquake prediction opinion;  
(2) The form of issuance of the earthquake prediction;  
(3) The social and economic impacts that may arise after the issuance of the earthquake prediction.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院地震工作主管部门应当组织有关专家，对下列地震预报意见进行评审，并将评审结果报国务院：  
(一)全国地震震情会商会形成的地震预报意见；  
(二)省、自治区、直辖市地震震情会商会形成的可能发生严重破坏性地震的地震预报意见。

**Article 10** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall organize relevant experts to review the following earthquake prediction opinions and report the review results to the State Council:  
(1) Earthquake prediction opinions formed at national earthquake situation consultation meetings;  
(2) Earthquake prediction opinions formed at earthquake situation consultation meetings in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that indicate the possibility of severe destructive earthquakes.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构应当组织有关专家，对下列地震预报意见进行评审，并将评审结果向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院地震工作主管部门报告：  
(一)本省、自治区、直辖市地震震情会商会形成的地震预报意见；  
(二)市、县地震震情会商会形成的地震预报意见。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构，对可能发生严重破坏性地震的地震预报意见，应当先报经国务院地震工作主管部门评审后，再向本级人民政府报告。

**Article 11** The institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize relevant experts to review the following earthquake prediction opinions and report the review results to the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council:  
(1) Earthquake prediction opinions formed at earthquake situation consultation meetings within the respective province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(2) Earthquake prediction opinions formed at earthquake situation consultation meetings in cities and counties.  
The institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, with respect to earthquake prediction opinions that indicate the possibility of severe destructive earthquakes, first submit them to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council for review before reporting to the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构，在震情跟踪会商中，根据明显临震异常形成的临震预报意见，在紧急情况下，可以不经评审，直接报本级人民政府，并报国务院地震工作主管部门。

**Article 12** Where, during earthquake situation tracking consultation, an imminent earthquake prediction opinion is formed based on obvious imminent earthquake anomalies, the institution responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, under emergency circumstances, directly report to the people's government at the same level without undergoing review, and also report to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何单位和个人不得向社会散布地震预报意见及其评审结果。

**Article 13** No unit or individual may disseminate earthquake prediction opinions or the review results thereof to the public.

### 第四章　地震预报的发布

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对地震预报实行统一发布制度。  
全国性的地震长期预报和地震中期预报，由国务院发布。  
省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的地震长期预报、地震中期预报、地震短期预报和临震预报，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府发布。  
新闻媒体刊登或者播发地震预报消息，必须依照本条例的规定，以国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府发布的地震预报为准。

**Article 14** The State shall implement a unified issuance system for earthquake predictions.  
National long-term earthquake predictions and medium-term earthquake predictions shall be issued by the State Council.  
Long-term earthquake predictions, medium-term earthquake predictions, short-term earthquake predictions, and imminent earthquake predictions within the administrative regions of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be issued by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Where news media publish or broadcast earthquake prediction information, they must, in accordance with these Regulations, take as the standard the earthquake predictions issued by the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　已经发布地震短期预报的地区，如果发现明显临震异常，在紧急情况下，当地市、县人民政府可以发布48小时之内的临震预报，并同时向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府及其负责管理地震工作的机构和国务院地震工作主管部门报告。

**Article 15** In areas where a short-term earthquake prediction has been issued, if obvious imminent earthquake anomalies are discovered, under emergency circumstances, the local city or county people's government may issue an imminent earthquake prediction within 48 hours, and shall simultaneously report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and its institution responsible for the administration of earthquake work, and to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地震短期预报和临震预报在发布预报的时域、地域内有效。预报期内未发生地震的，原发布机关应当做出撤销或者延期的决定，向社会公布，并妥善处理善后事宜。

**Article 16** Short-term earthquake predictions and imminent earthquake predictions shall be valid within the time and area of the issued prediction. If no earthquake occurs within the prediction period, the original issuing authority shall make a decision to cancel or extend the prediction, announce it to the public, and properly handle the aftermath.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　发生地震谣言，扰乱社会正常秩序时，国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构应当采取措施，迅速予以澄清，其他有关部门应当给予配合、协助。

**Article 17** When earthquake rumors occur and disrupt normal social order, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the institutions responsible for the administration of earthquake work of local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to promptly clarify the situation, and other relevant departments shall provide cooperation and assistance.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事地震工作的专业人员违反本条例规定，擅自向社会散布地震预测意见、地震预报意见及其评审结果的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 18** Where professionals engaged in earthquake work violate these Regulations and disseminate earthquake prediction opinions, earthquake forecast opinions, or the review results thereof to the public without authorization, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本条例规定，制造地震谣言，扰乱社会正常秩序的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 19** Anyone who violates these Regulations, fabricates earthquake rumors, and disrupts normal social order shall be given a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，向国(境)外提出地震预测意见的，由国务院地震工作主管部门给予警告，并可以由其所在单位根据造成的不同后果依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 20** Anyone who violates these Regulations and submits earthquake prediction opinions to any country (region) outside China shall be given a warning by the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council, and may be given disciplinary sanctions according to law by his or her unit based on the different consequences caused.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事地震工作的国家工作人员玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 21** Where a state functionary engaged in earthquake work neglects his or her duty and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　震后地震趋势判定公告的权限和程序，由国务院地震工作主管部门制定。

**Article 22** The authority and procedures for the announcement of post-earthquake earthquake trend judgments shall be formulated by the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　北京市的地震短期预报和临震预报，由国务院地震工作主管部门和北京市人民政府负责管理地震工作的机构，组织召开地震震情会商会，提出地震预报意见，经国务院地震工作主管部门组织评审后，报国务院批准，由北京市人民政府发布。

**Article 23** For short-term earthquake predictions and imminent earthquake predictions in Beijing, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the institution responsible for the administration of earthquake work of the Beijing Municipal People's Government shall organize and convene earthquake situation consultation meetings, put forward earthquake prediction opinions, submit them to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council for organization of review, then report to the State Council for approval, and the Beijing Municipal People's Government shall issue them.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1988年6月7日国务院批准、1988年8月9日国家地震局发布的《发布地震预报的规定》同时废止。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisions on the Issuance of Earthquake Predictions approved by the State Council on June 7, 1988 and promulgated by the State Seismological Bureau on August 9, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.
