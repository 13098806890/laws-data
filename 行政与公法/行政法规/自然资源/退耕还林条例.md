# 退耕还林条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2003-01-20

> (2002年12月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第367号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范退耕还林活动，保护退耕还林者的合法权益，巩固退耕还林成果，优化农村产业结构，改善生态环境，制定本条例。 第二条　国务院批准规划范围内的退耕还林活动，适用本条例。 第三条　各级人民政府应当严格执行“退耕还林、封山绿化、以粮代赈、个体承包”的政策措施。 第四条　退耕还林必须坚持生态优先。退耕还林应当与调整农村产业结构、发展农村经济，防治水土流失、保护和建设基本农田、提高粮食单产，加强农村能源建设，实施生态移民相结合。 第五条　退耕还林应当遵循下列原则： (一)统筹规划、分步实施、突出重点、注重实效； (二)政策引导和农民自愿退耕相结合，谁退耕、谁造林、谁经营、谁受益； (三)遵循自然规律，因地制宜，宜林则林，宜草则草，综合治理； (四)建设与保护并重，防止边治理边破坏； (五)逐步改善退耕还林者的生活条件。 第六条　国务院西部开发工作机构负责退耕还林工作的综合协调，组织有关部门研究制定退耕还林有关政策、办法，组织和协调退耕还林总体规划的落实；国务院林业行政主管部门负责编制退耕还林总体规划、年度计划，主管全国退耕还林的实施工作，负责退耕还林工作的指导和监督检查；国务院发展计划部门会同有关部门负责退耕还林总体规划的审核、计划的汇总、基建年度计划的编制和综合平衡；国务院财政主管部门负责退耕还林中央财政补助资金的安排和监督管理；国务院农业行政主管部门负责已垦草场的退耕还草以及天然草场的恢复和建设有关规划、计划的编制，以及技术指导和监督检查；国务院水行政主管部门负责退耕还林还草地区小流域治理、水土保持等相关工作的技术指导和监督检查；国务院粮食行政管理部门负责粮源的协调和调剂工作。 县级以上地方人民政府林业、计划、财政、农业、水利、粮食等部门在本级人民政府的统一领导下，按照本条例和规定的职责分工，负责退耕还林的有关工作。 第七条　国家对退耕还林实行省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责制。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织有关部门采取措施，保证退耕还林中央补助资金的专款专用，组织落实补助粮食的调运和供应，加强退耕还林的复查工作，按期完成国家下达的退耕还林任务，并逐级落实目标责任，签订责任书，实现退耕还林目标。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范退耕还林活动，保护退耕还林者的合法权益，巩固退耕还林成果，优化农村产业结构，改善生态环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating the activities of returning farmland to forest, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of those engaged in such activities, consolidating the achievements of returning farmland to forest, optimizing the rural industrial structure, and improving the ecological environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院批准规划范围内的退耕还林活动，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to activities of returning farmland to forest within the scope of the plans approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民政府应当严格执行“退耕还林、封山绿化、以粮代赈、个体承包”的政策措施。

**Article 3** People's governments at all levels shall strictly implement the policy measures of “returning farmland to forest, closing hillsides to facilitate afforestation, providing grain relief, and individual contracting”.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　退耕还林必须坚持生态优先。退耕还林应当与调整农村产业结构、发展农村经济，防治水土流失、保护和建设基本农田、提高粮食单产，加强农村能源建设，实施生态移民相结合。

**Article 4** Returning farmland to forest must adhere to the principle of ecological priority. The conversion of farmland to forest shall be combined with the adjustment of the rural industrial structure, the development of the rural economy, the prevention and control of water and soil erosion, the protection and construction of basic farmland, the increase of per-unit grain yield, the strengthening of rural energy construction, and the implementation of ecological migration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　退耕还林应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)统筹规划、分步实施、突出重点、注重实效；  
(二)政策引导和农民自愿退耕相结合，谁退耕、谁造林、谁经营、谁受益；  
(三)遵循自然规律，因地制宜，宜林则林，宜草则草，综合治理；  
(四)建设与保护并重，防止边治理边破坏；  
(五)逐步改善退耕还林者的生活条件。

**Article 5** The conversion of farmland to forest shall follow the following principles:  
(1) making overall plans, implementing step by step, highlighting key points, and emphasizing practical results;  
(2) combining policy guidance with farmers' voluntary conversion of farmland, and whoever converts farmland shall plant trees, manage them, and benefit from them;  
(3) following laws of nature, taking measures suitable to local conditions, planting trees where suitable for trees and grass where suitable for grass, and carrying out comprehensive treatment;  
(4) attaching equal importance to construction and protection, preventing destruction while carrying out treatment; and  
(5) gradually improving the living conditions of those engaged in returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院西部开发工作机构负责退耕还林工作的综合协调，组织有关部门研究制定退耕还林有关政策、办法，组织和协调退耕还林总体规划的落实；国务院林业行政主管部门负责编制退耕还林总体规划、年度计划，主管全国退耕还林的实施工作，负责退耕还林工作的指导和监督检查；国务院发展计划部门会同有关部门负责退耕还林总体规划的审核、计划的汇总、基建年度计划的编制和综合平衡；国务院财政主管部门负责退耕还林中央财政补助资金的安排和监督管理；国务院农业行政主管部门负责已垦草场的退耕还草以及天然草场的恢复和建设有关规划、计划的编制，以及技术指导和监督检查；国务院水行政主管部门负责退耕还林还草地区小流域治理、水土保持等相关工作的技术指导和监督检查；国务院粮食行政管理部门负责粮源的协调和调剂工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府林业、计划、财政、农业、水利、粮食等部门在本级人民政府的统一领导下，按照本条例和规定的职责分工，负责退耕还林的有关工作。

**Article 6** The working organ of the State Council for the development of the western region shall be responsible for comprehensive coordination of the work of returning farmland to forest, organizing relevant departments to study and formulate relevant policies and measures, and organizing and coordinating the implementation of the overall plan for returning farmland to forest. The competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the overall plan and annual plans for returning farmland to forest, supervising the implementation of the work of returning farmland to forest nationwide, and providing guidance and supervision and inspection for the work. The competent department of development planning under the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, be responsible for the examination of the overall plan for returning farmland to forest, the compilation of plans, the formulation of annual capital construction plans, and comprehensive balancing. The competent financial department under the State Council shall be responsible for the arrangement and supervision and management of the central financial subsidy funds for returning farmland to forest. The competent administrative department of agriculture under the State Council shall be responsible for the formulation of relevant plans and programs for returning reclaimed grasslands to grassland and for the restoration and construction of natural grasslands, as well as for technical guidance and supervision and inspection. The competent administrative department of water resources under the State Council shall be responsible for technical guidance and supervision and inspection of work related to small watershed management and water and soil conservation in areas where farmland is being returned to forest or grassland. The competent administrative department of grain under the State Council shall be responsible for the coordination and adjustment of grain sources.  
The departments of forestry, planning, finance, agriculture, water resources, and grain of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, under the unified leadership of the people's government at the corresponding level and in accordance with these Regulations and the prescribed division of responsibilities, be responsible for the relevant work of returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对退耕还林实行省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责制。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织有关部门采取措施，保证退耕还林中央补助资金的专款专用，组织落实补助粮食的调运和供应，加强退耕还林的复查工作，按期完成国家下达的退耕还林任务，并逐级落实目标责任，签订责任书，实现退耕还林目标。

**Article 7** The State shall implement a system of responsibility of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for returning farmland to forest. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize relevant departments to take measures to ensure that the central subsidy funds for returning farmland to forest are used for specified purposes only, to organize the delivery and supply of subsidized grain, to strengthen the review of the work of returning farmland to forest, to complete the tasks of returning farmland to forest assigned by the State on schedule, and to gradually assign target responsibilities layer by layer, sign responsibility documents, and achieve the goals of returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　退耕还林实行目标责任制。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门应当与退耕还林工程项目负责人和技术负责人签订责任书，明确其应当承担的责任。

**Article 8** A target responsibility system shall be implemented for returning farmland to forest.  
Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall sign responsibility documents with the project directors and technical directors of the projects of returning farmland to forest, specifying the responsibilities they shall assume.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持退耕还林应用技术的研究和推广，提高退耕还林科学技术水平。

**Article 9** The State shall support the research and promotion of applied technologies for returning farmland to forest, in order to raise the scientific and technological level of such work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府应当组织开展退耕还林活动的宣传教育，增强公民的生态建设和保护意识。  
在退耕还林工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels shall organize and carry out publicity and education on the activities of returning farmland to forest, enhancing citizens' awareness of ecological construction and protection.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of returning farmland to forest shall be commended and rewarded by the relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人都有权检举、控告破坏退耕还林的行为。  
有关人民政府及其有关部门接到检举、控告后，应当及时处理。

**Article 11** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or accuse any act that damages the work of returning farmland to forest.  
Upon receiving such reports or accusations, the relevant people's government and its relevant departments shall handle them in a timely manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级审计机关应当加强对退耕还林资金和粮食补助使用情况的审计监督。

**Article 12** Audit institutions at all levels shall strengthen audit supervision over the use of funds and grain subsidies for returning farmland to forest.

### 第二章　规划和计划

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　退耕还林应当统筹规划。  
退耕还林总体规划由国务院林业行政主管部门编制，经国务院西部开发工作机构协调、国务院发展计划部门审核后，报国务院批准实施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门根据退耕还林总体规划会同有关部门编制本行政区域的退耕还林规划，经本级人民政府批准，报国务院有关部门备案。

**Article 13** The conversion of farmland to forest shall be carried out on the basis of overall planning.  
The overall plan for returning farmland to forest shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council, coordinated by the working organ of the State Council for the development of the western region, and examined by the competent department of development planning under the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The competent administrative department of forestry under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the overall plan for returning farmland to forest and jointly with relevant departments, formulate a plan for returning farmland to forest for its own administrative region, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the corresponding level for approval and to the relevant departments of the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　退耕还林规划应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)范围、布局和重点；  
(二)年限、目标和任务；  
(三)投资测算和资金来源；  
(四)效益分析和评价；  
(五)保障措施。

**Article 14** A plan for returning farmland to forest shall include the following main contents:  
(1) scope, layout, and key areas;  
(2) time limits, objectives, and tasks;  
(3) investment estimates and sources of funds;  
(4) benefit analysis and evaluation; and  
(5) safeguard measures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　下列耕地应当纳入退耕还林规划，并根据生态建设需要和国家财力有计划地实施退耕还林：  
(一)水土流失严重的；  
(二)沙化、盐碱化、石漠化严重的；  
(三)生态地位重要、粮食产量低而不稳的。  
江河源头及其两侧、湖库周围的陡坡耕地以及水土流失和风沙危害严重等生态地位重要区域的耕地，应当在退耕还林规划中优先安排。

**Article 15** The following types of farmland shall be included in the plan for returning farmland to forest, and the conversion of farmland to forest shall be implemented in a planned manner according to the needs of ecological construction and the financial capacity of the State:  
(1) farmland with severe water and soil erosion;  
(2) farmland with severe desertification, salinization, or stony desertification;  
(3) farmland that is located in ecologically important areas and has low and unstable grain yields.  
Steep-slope farmland at the sources of rivers and on both sides thereof, as well as around lakes and reservoirs, and farmland in ecologically important areas such as those with severe water and soil erosion and damage from wind and sand, shall be given priority in the plan for returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　基本农田保护范围内的耕地和生产条件较好、实际粮食产量超过国家退耕还林补助粮食标准并且不会造成水土流失的耕地，不得纳入退耕还林规划；但是，因生态建设特殊需要，经国务院批准并依照有关法律、行政法规规定的程序调整基本农田保护范围后，可以纳入退耕还林规划。  
制定退耕还林规划时，应当考虑退耕农民长期的生计需要。

**Article 16** Farmland within the scope of basic farmland protection and farmland that has good production conditions, whose actual grain yield exceeds the State standard for subsidized grain for returning farmland to forest, and which does not cause water and soil erosion, shall not be included in the plan for returning farmland to forest; however, if special needs for ecological construction so require, after obtaining the approval of the State Council and adjusting the scope of basic farmland protection in accordance with the procedures prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations, such farmland may be included in the plan for returning farmland to forest.  
When formulating a plan for returning farmland to forest, the long-term livelihood needs of the farmers whose farmland is converted shall be taken into consideration.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　退耕还林规划应当与国民经济和社会发展规划、农村经济发展总体规划、土地利用总体规划相衔接，与环境保护、水土保持、防沙治沙等规划相协调。

**Article 17** The plan for returning farmland to forest shall be linked with the national economic and social development plan, the overall plan for rural economic development, and the overall plan for land utilization, and shall be coordinated with plans for environmental protection, water and soil conservation, and desertification prevention and control.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　退耕还林必须依照经批准的规划进行。未经原批准机关同意，不得擅自调整退耕还林规划。

**Article 18** The conversion of farmland to forest must be carried out in accordance with the approved plan. Without the consent of the original approving authority, no adjustment shall be made to the plan for returning farmland to forest without authorization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门根据退耕还林规划，会同有关部门编制本行政区域下一年度退耕还林计划建议，由本级人民政府发展计划部门审核，并经本级人民政府批准后，于每年8月31日前报国务院西部开发工作机构、林业、发展计划等有关部门。国务院林业行政主管部门汇总编制全国退耕还林年度计划建议，经国务院西部开发工作机构协调，国务院发展计划部门审核和综合平衡，报国务院批准后，由国务院发展计划部门会同有关部门于10月31日前联合下达。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府发展计划部门会同有关部门根据全国退耕还林年度计划，于11月30日前将本行政区域下一年度退耕还林计划分解下达到有关县(市)人民政府，并将分解下达情况报国务院有关部门备案。

**Article 19** The competent administrative department of forestry under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the plan for returning farmland to forest and jointly with relevant departments, formulate a proposed plan for returning farmland to forest for the following year for its own administrative region. Such proposed plan shall be examined by the competent department of development planning of the people's government at the corresponding level, and after approval by the people's government at the corresponding level, shall be submitted before August 31 of each year to the working organ of the State Council for the development of the western region and the relevant departments such as forestry and development planning. The competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council shall compile a national proposed annual plan for returning farmland to forest, which, after coordination by the working organ of the State Council for the development of the western region and examination and comprehensive balancing by the competent department of development planning under the State Council, shall be submitted to the State Council for approval, and thereafter the competent department of development planning under the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, issue the plan in a unified manner before October 31.  
The competent department of development planning under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with relevant departments and in accordance with the national annual plan for returning farmland to forest, break down and assign the plan for returning farmland to forest for the following year for its own administrative region to the relevant people's governments of counties (cities) before November 30, and shall report the breakdown and assignment to the relevant departments of the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门根据国家下达的下一年度退耕还林计划，会同有关部门编制本行政区域内的年度退耕还林实施方案，报本级人民政府批准实施。  
县级人民政府林业行政主管部门可以根据批准后的省级退耕还林年度实施方案，编制本行政区域内的退耕还林年度实施方案，报本级人民政府批准后实施，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门备案。

**Article 20** The competent administrative department of forestry under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the next year's plan for returning farmland to forest assigned by the State and jointly with relevant departments, formulate an annual implementation plan for returning farmland to forest for its own administrative region, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the corresponding level for approval before implementation.  
The competent administrative department of forestry under a people's government at the county level may, based on the approved provincial annual implementation plan for returning farmland to forest, formulate an annual implementation plan for returning farmland to forest for its own administrative region, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the corresponding level for approval before implementation, and shall be reported to the competent administrative department of forestry under the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for the record.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　年度退耕还林实施方案，应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)退耕还林的具体范围；  
(二)生态林与经济林比例；  
(三)树种选择和植被配置方式；  
(四)造林模式；  
(五)种苗供应方式；  
(六)植被管护和配套保障措施；  
(七)项目和技术负责人。

**Article 21** An annual implementation plan for returning farmland to forest shall include the following main contents:  
(1) the specific scope of the farmland to be returned to forest;  
(2) the proportion of ecological forest to economic forest;  
(3) the selection of tree species and the configuration of vegetation;  
(4) the afforestation model;  
(5) the mode of supply of seedlings;  
(6) vegetation management and supporting safeguard measures; and  
(7) the project director and technical director.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级人民政府林业行政主管部门应当根据年度退耕还林实施方案组织专业人员或者有资质的设计单位编制乡镇作业设计，把实施方案确定的内容落实到具体地块和土地承包经营权人。  
编制作业设计时，干旱、半干旱地区应当以种植耐旱灌木(草)、恢复原有植被为主；以间作方式植树种草的，应当间作多年生植物，主要林木的初植密度应当符合国家规定的标准。

**Article 22** The competent administrative department of forestry under a people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the annual implementation plan for returning farmland to forest, organize professionals or qualified design institutes to prepare township operational designs, assigning the contents determined in the implementation plan to specific plots of land and holders of land contractual management rights.  
When preparing operational designs, in arid and semi-arid areas, the main focus shall be on planting drought-tolerant shrubs (grass) and restoring the original vegetation; where intercropping is used for planting trees and grass, perennial plants shall be intercropped, and the initial planting density of the main forest trees shall meet the standards prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　退耕土地还林营造的生态林面积，以县为单位核算，不得低于退耕土地还林面积的80%。  
退耕还林营造的生态林，由县级以上地方人民政府林业行政主管部门根据国务院林业行政主管部门制定的标准认定。

**Article 23** The area of ecological forest created by converting farmland to forest shall be calculated on a county basis and shall not be less than 80% of the area of farmland converted to forest.  
The ecological forest created by returning farmland to forest shall be recognized by the competent administrative department of forestry of a local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the standards established by the competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council.

### 第三章　造林、管护与检查验收

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级人民政府或者其委托的乡级人民政府应当与有退耕还林任务的土地承包经营权人签订退耕还林合同。  
退耕还林合同应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)退耕土地还林范围、面积和宜林荒山荒地造林范围、面积；  
(二)按照作业设计确定的退耕还林方式；  
(三)造林成活率及其保存率；  
(四)管护责任；  
(五)资金和粮食的补助标准、期限和给付方式；  
(六)技术指导、技术服务的方式和内容；  
(七)种苗来源和供应方式；  
(八)违约责任；  
(九)合同履行期限。  
退耕还林合同的内容不得与本条例以及国家其他有关退耕还林的规定相抵触。

**Article 24** The people's government at the county level or the township people's government entrusted by it shall sign a contract for returning farmland to forest with the holders of land contractual management rights who have tasks of converting farmland to forest.  
A contract for returning farmland to forest shall include the following main contents:  
(1) the scope and area of farmland to be returned to forest and the scope and area of barren mountains and wasteland suitable for afforestation;  
(2) the method of converting farmland to forest determined in accordance with the operational design;  
(3) the survival rate of afforestation and its preservation rate;  
(4) management and protection responsibilities;  
(5) the standards, duration, and method of payment for subsidies of funds and grain;  
(6) the method and content of technical guidance and technical services;  
(7) the source and supply method of seedlings;  
(8) liability for breach of contract; and  
(9) the term of performance of the contract.  
The contents of a contract for returning farmland to forest shall not conflict with these Regulations or other relevant State provisions on returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　退耕还林需要的种苗，可以由县级人民政府根据本地区实际组织集中采购，也可以由退耕还林者自行采购。集中采购的，应当征求退耕还林者的意见，并采用公开竞价方式，签订书面合同，超过国家种苗造林补助费标准的，不得向退耕还林者强行收取超出部分的费用。  
任何单位和个人不得为退耕还林者指定种苗供应商。  
禁止垄断经营种苗和哄抬种苗价格。

**Article 25** The seedlings needed for returning farmland to forest may be purchased centrally by the people's government at the county level in light of local conditions, or may be purchased by the persons engaged in returning farmland to forest themselves. Where central procurement is adopted, the opinions of such persons shall be solicited, public bidding shall be used, a written contract shall be signed, and where the cost exceeds the State standard for afforestation subsidy for seedlings, no unit or individual may forcibly charge the excess amount to the persons engaged in returning farmland to forest.  
No unit or individual may designate the supplier of seedlings for persons engaged in returning farmland to forest.  
Monopoly in the seedling business and price gouging of seedlings shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　退耕还林所用种苗应当就地培育、就近调剂，优先选用乡土树种和抗逆性强树种的良种壮苗。

**Article 26** The seedlings used for returning farmland to forest shall be cultivated locally and allocated nearby, and priority shall be given to the use of fine and strong seedlings of native tree species and tree species with strong stress resistance.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　林业、农业行政主管部门应当加强种苗培育的技术指导和服务的管理工作，保证种苗质量。  
销售、供应的退耕还林种苗应当经县级人民政府林业、农业行政主管部门检验合格，并附具标签和质量检验合格证；跨县调运的，还应当依法取得检疫合格证。

**Article 27** The competent administrative departments of forestry and agriculture shall strengthen technical guidance for seedling cultivation and management of services to ensure the quality of seedlings.  
The seedlings sold and supplied for returning farmland to forest shall be inspected and qualified by the competent administrative departments of forestry and agriculture of the people's government at the county level, and shall be accompanied by labels and certificates of quality inspection; where they are transported across counties, a certificate of quarantine shall also be obtained according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据本行政区域的退耕还林规划，加强种苗生产与采种基地的建设。  
国家鼓励企业和个人采取多种形式培育种苗，开展产业化经营。

**Article 28** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the plan for returning farmland to forest for their respective administrative regions, strengthen the construction of bases for seedling production and seed collection.  
The State shall encourage enterprises and individuals to cultivate seedlings through various forms and to carry out industrial operation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　退耕还林者应当按照作业设计和合同的要求植树种草。  
禁止林粮间作和破坏原有林草植被的行为。

**Article 29** Persons engaged in returning farmland to forest shall plant trees and grass in accordance with the requirements of the operational design and the contract.  
The interplanting of trees and grain crops and any act that damages the original forest and grass vegetation shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　退耕还林者在享受资金和粮食补助期间，应当按照作业设计和合同的要求在宜林荒山荒地造林。

**Article 30** Persons engaged in returning farmland to forest, during the period when they enjoy subsidies in funds and grain, shall carry out afforestation on barren mountains and wasteland suitable for afforestation in accordance with the requirements of the operational design and the contract.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级人民政府应当建立退耕还林植被管护制度，落实管护责任。  
退耕还林者应当履行管护义务。  
禁止在退耕还林项目实施范围内复耕和从事滥采、乱挖等破坏地表植被的活动。

**Article 31** The people's government at the county level shall establish a system for the management and protection of vegetation in areas where farmland has been returned to forest, and shall assign responsibilities for management and protection.  
Persons engaged in returning farmland to forest shall perform their obligations of management and protection.  
Re-cultivation and activities such as excessive gathering and arbitrary digging that destroy surface vegetation within the scope of implementation of projects for returning farmland to forest shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当组织技术推广单位或者技术人员，为退耕还林提供技术指导和技术服务。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall organize technology extension units or technical personnel to provide technical guidance and technical services for returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级人民政府林业行政主管部门应当按照国务院林业行政主管部门制定的检查验收标准和办法，对退耕还林建设项目进行检查验收，经验收合格的，方可发给验收合格证明。

**Article 33** The competent administrative department of forestry under a people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the inspection and acceptance standards and methods formulated by the competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council, carry out inspection and acceptance of construction projects of returning farmland to forest. Only upon acceptance as qualified may a certificate of acceptance be issued.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当对县级退耕还林检查验收结果进行复查，并根据复查结果对县级人民政府和有关责任人员进行奖惩。  
国务院林业行政主管部门应当对省级复查结果进行核查，并将核查结果上报国务院。

**Article 34** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall conduct re-inspection of the inspection and acceptance results at the county level regarding returning farmland to forest, and shall, based on the results of such re-inspection, impose rewards or penalties on the people's governments at the county level and the responsible persons concerned.  
The competent administrative department of forestry under the State Council shall verify the results of the provincial re-inspection and report the verification results to the State Council.

### 第四章　资金和粮食补助

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家按照核定的退耕还林实际面积，向土地承包经营权人提供补助粮食、种苗造林补助费和生活补助费。具体补助标准和补助年限按照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 35** The State shall, on the basis of the verified actual area of farmland returned to forest, provide subsidized grain, seedling afforestation subsidies, and living subsidies to the holders of land contractual management rights. The specific subsidy standards and subsidy duration shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　尚未承包到户和休耕的坡耕地退耕还林的，以及纳入退耕还林规划的宜林荒山荒地造林，只享受种苗造林补助费。

**Article 36** Where sloping farmland that has not yet been contracted to households or is left fallow is converted to forest, and where afforestation is carried out on barren mountains and wasteland suitable for afforestation included in the plan for returning farmland to forest, only seedling afforestation subsidies shall be enjoyed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　种苗造林补助费和生活补助费由国务院计划、财政、林业部门按照有关规定及时下达、核拨。

**Article 37** The seedling afforestation subsidies and living subsidies shall be timely issued and allocated by the departments of planning, finance, and forestry under the State Council in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　补助粮食应当就近调运，减少供应环节，降低供应成本。粮食补助费按照国家有关政策处理。  
粮食调运费用由地方财政承担，不得向供应补助粮食的企业和退耕还林者分摊。

**Article 38** The subsidized grain shall be transported locally, with fewer supply links and lower supply costs. The grain subsidy expenses shall be handled in accordance with the relevant policies of the State.  
The expenses for the transportation of grain shall be borne by the local finance and shall not be apportioned to the enterprises supplying the subsidized grain or to the persons engaged in returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据当地口粮消费习惯和农作物种植习惯以及当地粮食库存实际情况合理确定补助粮食的品种。  
补助粮食必须达到国家规定的质量标准。不符合国家质量标准的，不得供应给退耕还林者。

**Article 39** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall reasonably determine the types of subsidized grain on the basis of local grain consumption habits, crop cultivation habits, and the actual situation of local grain stocks.  
The subsidized grain must meet the quality standards prescribed by the State. Grain that fails to meet the State quality standards shall not be supplied to persons engaged in returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　退耕土地还林的第一年，该年度补助粮食可以分两次兑付，每次兑付的数量由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。  
从退耕土地还林第二年起，在规定的补助期限内，县级人民政府应当组织有关部门和单位及时向持有验收合格证明的退耕还林者一次兑付该年度补助粮食。

**Article 40** In the first year of conversion of farmland to forest, the subsidized grain for that year may be paid in two installments, with the amount of each payment to be determined by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
From the second year of conversion of farmland to forest onwards, within the prescribed subsidy period, the people's government at the county level shall organize relevant departments and units to promptly pay the annual subsidized grain in a single lump sum to persons engaged in returning farmland to forest who hold a certificate of acceptance.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　兑付的补助粮食，不得折算成现金或者代金券。供应补助粮食的企业不得回购退耕还林补助粮食。

**Article 41** The subsidized grain paid out shall not be converted into cash or vouchers. The enterprises supplying the subsidized grain shall not repurchase the subsidized grain for returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　种苗造林补助费应当用于种苗采购，节余部分可以用于造林补助和封育管护。  
退耕还林者自行采购种苗的，县级人民政府或者其委托的乡级人民政府应当在退耕还林合同生效时一次付清种苗造林补助费。  
集中采购种苗的，退耕还林验收合格后，种苗采购单位应当与退耕还林者结算种苗造林补助费。

**Article 42** The seedling afforestation subsidies shall be used for the procurement of seedlings, and any surplus may be used for afforestation subsidies and fencing and management.  
Where persons engaged in returning farmland to forest purchase seedlings themselves, the people's government at the county level or the township people's government entrusted by it shall pay the full amount of the seedling afforestation subsidies in a single lump sum upon the taking effect of the contract for returning farmland to forest.  
Where seedlings are procured centrally, after the project of returning farmland to forest has been accepted as qualified, the seedling procurement unit shall settle the seedling afforestation subsidies with the persons engaged in returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　退耕土地还林后，在规定的补助期限内，县级人民政府应当组织有关部门及时向持有验收合格证明的退耕还林者一次付清该年度生活补助费。

**Article 43** After farmland has been converted to forest, within the prescribed subsidy period, the people's government at the county level shall organize relevant departments to promptly pay the full amount of the annual living subsidy in a single lump sum to persons engaged in returning farmland to forest who hold a certificate of acceptance.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　退耕还林资金实行专户存储、专款专用，任何单位和个人不得挤占、截留、挪用和克扣。  
任何单位和个人不得弄虚作假、虚报冒领补助资金和粮食。

**Article 44** Funds for returning farmland to forest shall be stored in special accounts and used for their specified purposes only; no unit or individual may divert, withhold, misappropriate, or embezzle such funds.  
No unit or individual may engage in fraud, make false reports, or fraudulently obtain subsidies in the form of funds or grain.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　退耕还林所需前期工作和科技支撑等费用，国家按照退耕还林基本建设投资的一定比例给予补助，由国务院发展计划部门根据工程情况在年度计划中安排。  
退耕还林地方所需检查验收、兑付等费用，由地方财政承担。中央有关部门所需核查等费用，由中央财政承担。

**Article 45** The State shall grant subsidies for expenses such as preliminary work and scientific and technological support needed for returning farmland to forest, at a certain proportion of the capital construction investment for such projects, which shall be arranged by the competent department of development planning under the State Council in the annual plan according to the project situation.  
Expenses needed by localities for inspection, acceptance, payment, etc. for returning farmland to forest shall be borne by the local finance. Expenses needed by relevant central departments for verification, etc. shall be borne by the central finance.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　实施退耕还林的乡(镇)、村应当建立退耕还林公示制度，将退耕还林者的退耕还林面积、造林树种、成活率以及资金和粮食补助发放等情况进行公示。

**Article 46** Townships (towns) and villages implementing the conversion of farmland to forest shall establish a publicity system for returning farmland to forest, and shall publicize the area of farmland converted to forest by each person engaged in such activity, the tree species planted, the survival rate, and the distribution of subsidies in funds and grain.

### 第五章　其他保障措施

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家保护退耕还林者享有退耕土地上的林木(草)所有权。自行退耕还林的，土地承包经营权人享有退耕土地上的林木(草)所有权；委托他人还林或者与他人合作还林的，退耕土地上的林木(草)所有权由合同约定。  
退耕土地还林后，由县级以上人民政府依照森林法、草原法的有关规定发放林(草)权属证书，确认所有权和使用权，并依法办理土地变更登记手续。土地承包经营合同应当作相应调整。

**Article 47** The State shall protect the ownership of trees (grass) on the converted land enjoyed by persons engaged in returning farmland to forest. Where a person converts farmland to forest on its own, the holder of the land contractual management right shall enjoy the ownership of trees (grass) on the converted land; where a person entrusts another person or cooperates with another person to carry out the conversion, the ownership of trees (grass) on the converted land shall be determined by the contract.  
After farmland is converted to forest, the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Forest Law and the Grassland Law, issue certificates of title to the forest (grass), confirming the ownership and the right of use, and shall handle the procedures for registration of modification of the land in accordance with the law. The land contractual management contract shall be adjusted accordingly.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　退耕土地还林后的承包经营权期限可以延长到70年。承包经营权到期后，土地承包经营权人可以依照有关法律、法规的规定继续承包。  
退耕还林土地和荒山荒地造林后的承包经营权可以依法继承、转让。

**Article 48** The term of the contractual management right after farmland has been converted to forest may be extended to 70 years. After the term of the contractual management right expires, the holder of the land contractual management right may continue to contract in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
The contractual management right over land converted to forest and over barren mountains and wasteland after afforestation may be inherited or transferred according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　退耕还林者按照国家有关规定享受税收优惠，其中退耕还林(草)所取得的农业特产收入，依照国家规定免征农业特产税。  
退耕还林的县(市)农业税收因灾减收部分，由上级财政以转移支付的方式给予适当补助；确有困难的，经国务院批准，由中央财政以转移支付的方式给予适当补助。

**Article 49** Persons engaged in returning farmland to forest shall enjoy tax preferences in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. Among them, the income from special agricultural products obtained from returning farmland to forest (or grassland) shall be exempted from the special agricultural product tax in accordance with the State provisions.  
For counties (cities) engaged in returning farmland to forest, where the agricultural tax revenue decreases due to disasters, the higher-level finance shall provide appropriate subsidies by means of transfer payments; if there are genuine difficulties, subject to the approval of the State Council, the central finance shall provide appropriate subsidies by means of transfer payments.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　资金和粮食补助期满后，在不破坏整体生态功能的前提下，经有关主管部门批准，退耕还林者可以依法对其所有的林木进行采伐。

**Article 50** After the period of subsidies in funds and grain expires, provided that the overall ecological functions are not damaged, and with the approval of the relevant competent departments, persons engaged in returning farmland to forest may, in accordance with the law, fell the trees owned by them.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　地方各级人民政府应当加强基本农田和农业基础设施建设，增加投入，改良土壤，改造坡耕地，提高地力和单位粮食产量，解决退耕还林者的长期口粮需求。

**Article 51** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the construction of basic farmland and agricultural infrastructure, increase investment, improve soil, transform sloping farmland, enhance soil fertility and per-unit grain yield, and meet the long-term rations needs of those who have returned farmland to forest.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地方各级人民政府应当根据实际情况加强沼气、小水电、太阳能、风能等农村能源建设，解决退耕还林者对能源的需求。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in light of actual conditions, strengthen the construction of rural energy sources such as methane, small hydro-power, solar energy, and wind energy, and meet the energy needs of those who have returned farmland to forest.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　地方各级人民政府应当调整农村产业结构，扶持龙头企业，发展支柱产业，开辟就业门路，增加农民收入，加快小城镇建设，促进农业人口逐步向城镇转移。

**Article 53** Local people's governments at all levels shall adjust the rural industrial structure, support leading enterprises, develop pillar industries, create employment opportunities, increase farmers' income, accelerate the construction of small towns, and promote the gradual transfer of the agricultural population to urban areas.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家鼓励在退耕还林过程中实行生态移民，并对生态移民农户的生产、生活设施给予适当补助。

**Article 54** The State encourages ecological migration in the process of returning farmland to forest, and shall grant appropriate subsidies to the production and living facilities of ecological migrant households.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　退耕还林后，有关地方人民政府应当采取封山禁牧、舍饲圈养等措施，保护退耕还林成果。

**Article 55** After the return of farmland to forest, the relevant local people's governments shall adopt measures such as mountain closure and grazing prohibition, and stall feeding to protect the achievements of returning farmland to forest.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　退耕还林应当与扶贫开发、农业综合开发和水土保持等政策措施相结合，对不同性质的项目资金应当在专款专用的前提下统筹安排，提高资金使用效益。

**Article 56** Returning farmland to forest shall be combined with policy measures such as poverty alleviation and development, comprehensive agricultural development, and water and soil conservation. Funds for projects of different natures shall be arranged in a coordinated manner under the premise of being used for designated purposes, so as to improve the efficiency of fund utilization.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家工作人员在退耕还林活动中违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，依照刑法关于贪污罪、受贿罪、挪用公款罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分：  
(一)挤占、截留、挪用退耕还林资金或者克扣补助粮食的；  
(二)弄虚作假、虚报冒领补助资金和粮食的；  
(三)利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处的。  
国家工作人员以外的其他人员有前款第(二)项行为的，依照刑法关于诈骗罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府林业行政主管部门责令退回所冒领的补助资金和粮食，处以冒领资金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 57** Where any state functionary, in the activities of returning farmland to forest, violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits one of the following acts, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of embezzlement, the crime of taking bribes, the crime of misappropriating public funds, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) embezzling, intercepting, or misappropriating funds for returning farmland to forest, or withholding subsidized grain;  
(2) practicing fraud, making false reports, or fraudulently obtaining subsidized funds and grain;  
(3) taking advantage of his/her position to accept property or other benefits from others.  
Where any person other than a state functionary commits the act specified in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of fraud or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the fraudulently obtained subsidized funds and grain, and impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the amount of the funds fraudulently obtained.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家机关工作人员在退耕还林活动中违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由其所在单位或者上一级主管部门责令限期改正，退还分摊的和多收取的费用，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分：  
(一)未及时处理有关破坏退耕还林活动的检举、控告的；  
(二)向供应补助粮食的企业和退耕还林者分摊粮食调运费用的；  
(三)不及时向持有验收合格证明的退耕还林者发放补助粮食和生活补助费的；  
(四)在退耕还林合同生效时，对自行采购种苗的退耕还林者未一次付清种苗造林补助费的；  
(五)集中采购种苗的，在退耕还林验收合格后，未与退耕还林者结算种苗造林补助费的；  
(六)集中采购的种苗不合格的；  
(七)集中采购种苗的，向退耕还林者强行收取超出国家规定种苗造林补助费标准的种苗费的；  
(八)为退耕还林者指定种苗供应商的；  
(九)批准粮食企业向退耕还林者供应不符合国家质量标准的补助粮食或者将补助粮食折算成现金、代金券支付的；  
(十)其他不依照本条例规定履行职责的。

**Article 58** Where any functionary of a state organ, in the activities of returning farmland to forest, violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits one of the following acts, the unit to which he/she belongs or the competent authority at the next higher level shall order him/her to make corrections within a specified time limit and refund the apportioned and overcharged fees; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) failing to handle in a timely manner reports or accusations concerning activities that destroy the return of farmland to forest;  
(2) apportioning grain transportation costs to enterprises supplying subsidized grain or to persons returning farmland to forest;  
(3) failing to distribute subsidized grain and living subsidies in a timely manner to persons returning farmland to forest who hold certificates of acceptance;  
(4) failing to pay a lump sum of seedling and afforestation subsidies to persons returning farmland to forest who purchase seedlings on their own when the contract for returning farmland to forest takes effect;  
(5) where seedlings are purchased centrally, failing to settle the seedling and afforestation subsidies with the persons returning farmland to forest after the acceptance of returned farmland to forest;  
(6) where the seedlings purchased centrally are substandard;  
(7) where seedlings are purchased centrally, forcibly charging seedling fees that exceed the standard for seedling and afforestation subsidies prescribed by the State from persons returning farmland to forest;  
(8) designating seedling suppliers for persons returning farmland to forest;  
(9) approving grain enterprises to supply subsidized grain that does not meet the national quality standards to persons returning farmland to forest, or to convert the subsidized grain into cash or vouchers for payment;  
(10) other failures to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　采用不正当手段垄断种苗市场，或者哄抬种苗价格的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪、强迫交易罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由工商行政管理机关依照反不正当竞争法的规定处理；反不正当竞争法未作规定的，由工商行政管理机关处以非法经营额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 59** Where any person monopolizes the seedling market by illegitimate means or drives up seedling prices, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation, the crime of forced transaction, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, the administrative authority for industry and commerce shall handle the matter in accordance with the Anti-Unfair Competition Law; if the Anti-Unfair Competition Law does not provide for such act, the administrative authority for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal turnover.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　销售、供应未经检验合格的种苗或者未附具标签、质量检验合格证、检疫合格证的种苗的，依照刑法关于生产、销售伪劣种子罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府林业、农业行政主管部门或者工商行政管理机关依照种子法的规定处理；种子法未作规定的，由县级以上人民政府林业、农业行政主管部门依据职权处以非法经营额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where any person sells or supplies seedlings that have not passed inspection or are not accompanied by labels, certificates of quality inspection, or certificates of quarantine, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of producing or selling fake or substandard seeds or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, the competent forestry or agricultural administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the administrative authority for industry and commerce shall handle the matter in accordance with the Seed Law; if the Seed Law does not provide for such act, the competent forestry or agricultural administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its functions and powers, impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal turnover.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　供应补助粮食的企业向退耕还林者供应不符合国家质量标准的补助粮食的，由县级以上人民政府粮食行政管理部门责令限期改正，可以处非法供应的补助粮食数量乘以标准口粮单价1倍以下的罚款。  
供应补助粮食的企业将补助粮食折算成现金或者代金券支付的，或者回购补助粮食的，由县级以上人民政府粮食行政管理部门责令限期改正，可以处折算现金额、代金券额或者回购粮食价款1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 61** Where an enterprise supplying subsidized grain supplies subsidized grain that does not meet the national quality standards to persons returning farmland to forest, the competent grain administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and may impose a fine of not more than 1 time the product of the quantity of illegally supplied subsidized grain multiplied by the unit price of standard rations.  
Where an enterprise supplying subsidized grain converts the subsidized grain into cash or vouchers for payment, or buys back the subsidized grain, the competent grain administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and may impose a fine of not more than 1 time the amount of cash converted, the amount of vouchers, or the price of the repurchased grain.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　退耕还林者擅自复耕，或者林粮间作、在退耕还林项目实施范围内从事滥采、乱挖等破坏地表植被的活动的，依照刑法关于非法占用农用地罪、滥伐林木罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府林业、农业、水利行政主管部门依照森林法、草原法、水土保持法的规定处罚。

**Article 62** Where a person who has returned farmland to forest, without authorization, resumes cultivation, or practices intercropping of trees and grain, or engages in activities such as indiscriminate picking and digging that destroy surface vegetation within the scope of the project for returning farmland to forest, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegally occupying agricultural land, the crime of indiscriminately felling trees, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a criminal offense, the competent forestry, agricultural, or water conservancy administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with the Forest Law, the Grassland Law, and the Water and Soil Conservation Law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　已垦草场退耕还草和天然草场恢复与建设的具体实施，依照草原法和国务院有关规定执行。  
退耕还林还草地区小流域治理、水土保持等相关工作的具体实施，依照水土保持法和国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 63** The specific implementation of returning reclaimed grassland to grassland and the restoration and construction of natural grasslands shall be carried out in accordance with the Grassland Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
The specific implementation of related work such as small watershed management and water and soil conservation in areas where farmland is returned to forest or grassland shall be carried out in accordance with the Water and Soil Conservation Law and the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国务院批准的规划范围外的土地，地方各级人民政府决定实施退耕还林的，不享受本条例规定的中央政策补助。

**Article 64** Where local people's governments at all levels decide to implement the return of farmland to forest on land outside the planning scope approved by the State Council, such return shall not enjoy the central policy subsidies prescribed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本条例自2003年1月20日起施行。

**Article 65** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 20, 2003.
