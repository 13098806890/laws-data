# 地质资料管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2002-07-01

> (2002年3月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第349号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强对地质资料的管理，充分发挥地质资料的作用，保护地质资料汇交人的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　地质资料的汇交、保管和利用，适用本条例。 本条例所称地质资料，是指在地质工作中形成的文字、图表、声像、电磁介质等形式的原始地质资料、成果地质资料和岩矿芯、各类标本、光薄片、样品等实物地质资料。 第三条　国务院地质矿产主管部门负责全国地质资料汇交、保管、利用的监督管理。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门负责本行政区域内地质资料汇交、保管、利用的监督管理。 第四条　国务院地质矿产主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门的地质资料馆(以下简称地质资料馆)以及受国务院地质矿产主管部门委托的地质资料保管单位(以下简称地质资料保管单位)承担地质资料的保管和提供利用工作。 第五条　国家建立地质资料信息系统。 第六条　在地质资料管理工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，由国务院地质矿产主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门给予奖励。 第二章　地质资料的汇交 第七条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事矿产资源勘查开发的探矿权人或者采矿权人，为地质资料汇交人。 在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事前款规定以外地质工作项目的，其出资人为地质资料汇交人；但是，由国家出资的，承担有关地质工作项目的单位为地质资料汇交人。 第八条　国家对地质资料实行统一汇交制度。 地质资料汇交人应当按照本条例附件规定的范围汇交地质资料。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强对地质资料的管理，充分发挥地质资料的作用，保护地质资料汇交人的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the management of geological data, fully utilizing the role of geological data, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of persons who submit geological data.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　地质资料的汇交、保管和利用，适用本条例。  
本条例所称地质资料，是指在地质工作中形成的文字、图表、声像、电磁介质等形式的原始地质资料、成果地质资料和岩矿芯、各类标本、光薄片、样品等实物地质资料。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the submission, preservation and use of geological data.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "geological data" means original geological data, results geological data in forms such as texts, charts, audio-visual materials, and electromagnetic media, as well as physical geological data such as rock and mineral cores, various specimens, thin sections and samples, which are formed in geological work.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院地质矿产主管部门负责全国地质资料汇交、保管、利用的监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门负责本行政区域内地质资料汇交、保管、利用的监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the submission, preservation and use of geological data nationwide.  
The competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the submission, preservation and use of geological data within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院地质矿产主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门的地质资料馆(以下简称地质资料馆)以及受国务院地质矿产主管部门委托的地质资料保管单位(以下简称地质资料保管单位)承担地质资料的保管和提供利用工作。

**Article 4** The geological data archives of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and of the competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as geological data archives), as well as units entrusted by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council with the preservation of geological data (hereinafter referred to as geological data preservation units), shall undertake the work of preserving geological data and making them available for use.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立地质资料信息系统。

**Article 5** The State shall establish a geological data information system.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在地质资料管理工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，由国务院地质矿产主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门给予奖励。

**Article 6** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the work of geological data management shall be rewarded by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council or the competent departments of geology and mineral resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第二章　地质资料的汇交

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事矿产资源勘查开发的探矿权人或者采矿权人，为地质资料汇交人。  
在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事前款规定以外地质工作项目的，其出资人为地质资料汇交人；但是，由国家出资的，承担有关地质工作项目的单位为地质资料汇交人。

**Article 7** A holder of exploration right or mining right who engages in mineral resources exploration and development within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction shall be a person who submits geological data.  
For any person who engages in a geological work project other than those specified in the preceding paragraph within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction, the investor shall be the person who submits geological data; however, if the investment is made by the State, the unit undertaking the relevant geological work project shall be the person who submits geological data.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家对地质资料实行统一汇交制度。  
地质资料汇交人应当按照本条例附件规定的范围汇交地质资料。  
除成果地质资料、国家规定需要汇交的原始地质资料和实物地质资料外，其他的原始地质资料和实物地质资料只需汇交目录。国家规定需要汇交的原始地质资料和实物地质资料细目，由国务院地质矿产主管部门商国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a unified submission system for geological data.  
A person who submits geological data shall submit geological data within the scope specified in the Appendices to these Regulations.  
Except for results geological data, and original geological data and physical geological data that the State prescribes shall be submitted, it shall only be necessary to submit catalogs for other original geological data and physical geological data. The detailed list of original geological data and physical geological data that the State prescribes shall be submitted shall be formulated by the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本条例附件规定的下列地质资料，由地质资料汇交人向国务院地质矿产主管部门汇交：  
(一)石油、天然气、煤层气和放射性矿产的地质资料；  
(二)海洋地质资料；  
(三)国务院地质矿产主管部门规定应当向其汇交的其他地质资料。  
前款规定以外的地质资料，由地质资料汇交人向地质工作项目所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府地质矿产主管部门汇交。

**Article 9** The following geological data specified in the Appendices to these Regulations shall be submitted by the person who submits geological data to the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council:  
(1) geological data on petroleum, natural gas, coalbed methane and radioactive minerals;  
(2) marine geological data;  
(3) other geological data that the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council prescribes shall be submitted to it.  
Geological data other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall be submitted by the person who submits geological data to the competent department of geology and mineral resources of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the geological work project is located.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地质资料汇交人应当按照下列规定的期限汇交地质资料：  
(一)探矿权人应当在勘查许可证有效期届满的30日前汇交。  
(二)除下列情形外，采矿权人应当在采矿许可证有效期届满的90日前汇交：  
1．属于阶段性关闭矿井的，自关闭之日起180日内汇交；  
2．采矿权人开发矿产资源时，发现新矿体、新矿种或者矿产资源储量发生重大变化的，自开发勘探工作结束之日起180日内汇交。  
(三)因违反探矿权、采矿权管理规定，被吊销勘查许可证或者采矿许可证的，自处罚决定生效之日起15日内汇交。  
(四)工程建设项目地质资料，自该项目竣工验收之日起180日内汇交。  
(五)其他的地质资料，自地质工作项目结束之日起180日内汇交。

**Article 10** A person who submits geological data shall submit geological data within the following time limits:  
(1) A holder of exploration right shall submit within 30 days before the expiration of the validity period of the exploration license.  
(2) Except under the following circumstances, a holder of mining right shall submit within 90 days before the expiration of the validity period of the mining license:  
1. where a mine is closed by stages, it shall submit within 180 days from the date of closure;  
2. where a holder of mining right discovers a new orebody or a new type of mineral, or a material change occurs in the reserves of mineral resources during the development of mineral resources, it shall submit within 180 days from the date of completion of the development exploration work.  
(3) Where an exploration license or a mining license is revoked due to violation of the provisions governing exploration right or mining right, it shall submit within 15 days from the date on which the decision on punishment takes effect.  
(4) Geological data of an engineering construction project shall be submitted within 180 days from the date of completion acceptance of the project.  
(5) Other geological data shall be submitted within 180 days from the date of completion of the geological work project.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因不可抗力，地质资料汇交人不能按照[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的期限汇交地质资料的，应当将造成延期汇交地质资料的不可抗力事实书面告知负责接收地质资料的地质矿产主管部门。

**Article 11** Where a person who submits geological data is unable to submit geological data within the time limit prescribed in Article 10 of these Regulations due to force majeure, it shall inform in writing the competent department of geology and mineral resources responsible for receiving geological data of the fact of force majeure causing the delay in submission.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　汇交的地质资料，应当符合国务院地质矿产主管部门的有关规定及国家有关技术标准。  
任何单位和个人不得伪造地质资料，不得在地质资料汇交中弄虚作假。

**Article 12** The geological data submitted shall conform to the relevant provisions of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council and the relevant national technical standards.  
No unit or individual may forge geological data or practice fraud in the submission of geological data.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　汇交的地质资料，经验收合格后，由负责接收地质资料的地质矿产主管部门出具地质资料汇交凭证，并按照国务院地质矿产主管部门的规定及时移交地质资料馆或者地质资料保管单位。

**Article 13** After the geological data submitted have been checked and accepted, the competent department of geology and mineral resources responsible for receiving geological data shall issue a certificate of submission of geological data, and shall promptly transfer the data to the geological data archive or geological data preservation unit in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

### 第三章　地质资料的保管和利用

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地质资料馆和地质资料保管单位，应当建立地质资料的整理、保管制度，配置保存、防护、安全等必要设施，配备专业技术人员，保障地质资料的完整和安全。

**Article 14** Geological data archives and geological data preservation units shall establish a system for the arrangement and preservation of geological data, equip themselves with necessary facilities for storage, protection and security, and assign professional technical personnel to ensure the integrity and safety of geological data.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　探矿权人、采矿权人汇交的地质资料，自勘查许可证、采矿许可证有效期届满之日起30日内，由地质资料馆或者地质资料保管单位予以公开；勘查许可证、采矿许可证获准延期的，自延续期届满之日起30日内，由地质资料馆或者地质资料保管单位予以公开。  
前款规定以外的地质资料，自汇交之日起90日内，由地质资料馆或者地质资料保管单位予以公开。需要保护的，由接收地质资料的单位按照国务院地质矿产主管部门的规定予以保护。

**Article 15** Geological data submitted by a holder of exploration right or a holder of mining right shall be made public by the geological data archive or geological data preservation unit within 30 days from the date of expiration of the validity period of the exploration license or mining license; where the exploration license or mining license is approved for extension, it shall be made public within 30 days from the date of expiration of the extension period.  
Geological data other than those specified in the preceding paragraph shall be made public by the geological data archive or geological data preservation unit within 90 days from the date of submission. If protection is required, the unit receiving the geological data shall provide protection in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　涉及国家秘密或者著作权的地质资料的保护、公开和利用，按照保守国家秘密法、著作权法的有关规定执行。

**Article 16** The protection, disclosure and use of geological data involving State secrets or copyright shall be governed by the relevant provisions of the Law on Guarding State Secrets and the Copyright Law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　保护期内的地质资料，只公开资料目录。但是，汇交人书面同意提前公开其汇交的地质资料的，自其同意之日起，由地质资料馆或者地质资料保管单位予以公开。

**Article 17** For geological data within the protection period, only the catalog of the data shall be made public. However, if the person who submits the data agrees in writing to make the geological data submitted by it public ahead of time, it shall be made public by the geological data archive or geological data preservation unit from the date of such consent.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　保护期内的地质资料可以有偿利用，具体方式由利用人与地质资料汇交人协商确定。但是，利用保护期内国家出资勘查、开发取得的地质资料的，按照国务院地质矿产主管部门的规定执行。  
因救灾等公共利益需要，政府及其有关部门可以无偿利用保护期内的地质资料。

**Article 18** Geological data within the protection period may be used on a compensated basis, and the specific method shall be determined through consultation between the user and the person who submitted the geological data. However, where geological data obtained from exploration and development funded by the State within the protection period are used, the provisions of the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall be followed.  
Where required by public interests such as disaster relief, the government and its relevant departments may use geological data within the protection period free of charge.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地质资料的利用人应当按照规定利用地质资料，不得损毁、散失地质资料。  
地质资料馆和地质资料保管单位应当按照规定管理地质资料，不得非法披露、提供利用保护期内的地质资料或者封锁公开的地质资料。

**Article 19** A user of geological data shall use geological data in accordance with the provisions, and shall not damage or cause the loss of geological data.  
Geological data archives and geological data preservation units shall manage geological data in accordance with the provisions, and shall not illegally disclose or provide for use geological data within the protection period, or block publicly available geological data.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　未依照本条例规定的期限汇交地质资料的，由负责接收地质资料的地质矿产主管部门责令限期汇交；逾期不汇交的，处1万元以上5万元以下罚款，并予以通报，自发布通报之日起至逾期未汇交的资料全部汇交之日止，该汇交人不得申请新的探矿权、采矿权，不得承担国家出资的地质工作项目。

**Article 20** Where geological data are not submitted within the time limit prescribed in these Regulations, the competent department of geology and mineral resources responsible for receiving geological data shall order the person to submit within a specified time limit; if the person fails to submit within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and a circular shall be issued. From the date of issuance of the circular until the date on which all overdue data are submitted, the person shall not apply for any new exploration right or mining right, nor undertake any geological work project funded by the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　伪造地质资料或者在地质资料汇交中弄虚作假的，由负责接收地质资料的地质矿产主管部门没收、销毁地质资料，责令限期改正，处10万元罚款；逾期不改正的，通知原发证机关吊销其勘查许可证、采矿许可证或者取消其承担该地质工作项目的资格，自处罚决定生效之日起2年内，该汇交人不得申请新的探矿权、采矿权，不得承担国家出资的地质工作项目。

**Article 21** Where geological data are forged or fraud is practiced in the submission of geological data, the competent department of geology and mineral resources responsible for receiving geological data shall confiscate and destroy the geological data, order correction within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of 100,000 yuan; if correction is not made within the time limit, it shall notify the original issuing authority to revoke its exploration license or mining license, or cancel its qualification to undertake the geological work project. Within two years from the date on which the decision on punishment takes effect, the person shall not apply for any new exploration right or mining right, nor undertake any geological work project funded by the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　地质矿产主管部门、地质资料馆、地质资料保管单位违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；造成损失的，依法予以赔偿：  
(一)非法披露、提供利用保护期内的地质资料的；  
(二)封锁地质资料，限制他人查阅、利用公开的地质资料的；  
(三)不按照规定管理地质资料，造成地质资料损毁、散失的。  
地质资料利用人损毁、散失地质资料的，依法予以赔偿。

**Article 22** Where the competent department of geology and mineral resources, the geological data archive or the geological data preservation unit, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law:  
(1) illegally disclosing or providing for use geological data within the protection period;  
(2) blocking geological data and restricting others from consulting or using publicly available geological data;  
(3) failing to manage geological data in accordance with the provisions, resulting in damage or loss of geological data.  
Where a user of geological data damages or causes the loss of geological data, it shall make compensation according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　非法披露、提供利用保密的地质资料的，依照保守国家秘密法的规定予以处罚。

**Article 23** Where classified geological data are illegally disclosed or provided for use, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Guarding State Secrets.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例施行前，汇交人按照规定应当汇交而没有汇交的地质资料，由国务院地质矿产主管部门组织清查后，按照本条例汇交、保管和提供利用。

**Article 24** For geological data that, prior to the coming into force of these Regulations, a person who submits geological data should have submitted but has not submitted in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of geology and mineral resources under the State Council shall organize an inventory, and such data shall be submitted, preserved and made available for use in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　由国家出资在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域以外从事地质工作所取得的地质资料的汇交，参照本条例执行。

**Article 25** The submission of geological data obtained from geological work funded by the State and conducted outside the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction shall be governed by these Regulations by reference.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自2002年7月1日起施行。1988年5月20日国务院批准、原地质矿产部发布的《全国地质资料汇交管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2002. The Measures for the Administration of Geological Data Submission Nationwide, approved by the State Council on May 20, 1988 and promulgated by the former Ministry of Geology and Mineral Resources, shall be abolished simultaneously.
