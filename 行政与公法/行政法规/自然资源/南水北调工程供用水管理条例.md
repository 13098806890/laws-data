# 南水北调工程供用水管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2014-02-16  
**生效日期**：2014-02-16

> (2014年1月22日国务院第37次常务会议通过　2014年2月16日中华人民共和国国务院令第647号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强南水北调工程的供用水管理，充分发挥南水北调工程的经济效益、社会效益和生态效益，制定本条例。 第二条　南水北调东线工程、中线工程的供用水管理，适用本条例。 第三条　南水北调工程的供用水管理遵循先节水后调水、先治污后通水、先环保后用水的原则，坚持全程管理、统筹兼顾、权责明晰、严格保护，确保调度合理、水质合格、用水节约、设施安全。 第四条　国务院水行政主管部门负责南水北调工程的水量调度、运行管理工作，国务院环境保护主管部门负责南水北调工程的水污染防治工作，国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内，负责南水北调工程供用水的有关工作。 第五条　南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域、受水区县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内南水北调工程供用水的有关工作，并将南水北调工程的水质保障、用水管理纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。 国家对南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域的产业结构调整、生态环境保护予以支持，确保南水北调工程供用水安全。 第六条　国务院确定的南水北调工程管理单位具体负责南水北调工程的运行和保护工作。 南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府确定的单位具体负责本行政区域内南水北调配套工程的运行和保护工作。 第二章　水量调度 第七条　南水北调工程水量调度遵循节水为先、适度从紧的原则，统筹协调水源地、受水区和调水下游区域用水，加强生态环境保护。 南水北调工程水量调度以国务院批准的多年平均调水量和受水区省、直辖市水量分配指标为基本依据。 第八条　南水北调东线工程水量调度年度为每年10月1日至次年9月30日；南水北调中线工程水量调度年度为每年11月1日至次年10月31日。 第九条　淮河水利委员会商长江水利委员会提出南水北调东线工程年度可调水量，于每年9月15日前报送国务院水行政主管部门，并抄送有关省人民政府和南水北调工程管理单位。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强南水北调工程的供用水管理，充分发挥南水北调工程的经济效益、社会效益和生态效益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of water supply and use of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, and to fully leverage its economic, social, and ecological benefits.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　南水北调东线工程、中线工程的供用水管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the management of water supply and use of the eastern route and central route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　南水北调工程的供用水管理遵循先节水后调水、先治污后通水、先环保后用水的原则，坚持全程管理、统筹兼顾、权责明晰、严格保护，确保调度合理、水质合格、用水节约、设施安全。

**Article 3** The management of water supply and use of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall follow the principles of water conservation prior to water diversion, pollution control prior to water delivery, environmental protection prior to water use, and shall adhere to whole-process management, overall planning, clear rights and responsibilities, and strict protection, to ensure rational dispatch, qualified water quality, economical water use, and facility safety.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院水行政主管部门负责南水北调工程的水量调度、运行管理工作，国务院环境保护主管部门负责南水北调工程的水污染防治工作，国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内，负责南水北调工程供用水的有关工作。

**Article 4** The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the water quantity dispatch and operation management of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project; the environmental protection department of the State Council shall be responsible for the prevention and control of water pollution of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project; and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions, be responsible for the work related to the water supply and use of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域、受水区县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内南水北调工程供用水的有关工作，并将南水北调工程的水质保障、用水管理纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国家对南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域的产业结构调整、生态环境保护予以支持，确保南水北调工程供用水安全。

**Article 5** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the water source areas, the areas along the water diversion route, and the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be responsible for the work related to the water supply and use of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project within their respective administrative regions, and shall incorporate the water quality guarantee and water use management of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project into the national economic and social development plan.  
The State shall support the adjustment of industrial structure and ecological environmental protection in the water source areas and the areas along the water diversion route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, so as to ensure the safety of water supply and use of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院确定的南水北调工程管理单位具体负责南水北调工程的运行和保护工作。  
南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府确定的单位具体负责本行政区域内南水北调配套工程的运行和保护工作。

**Article 6** The entity designated by the State Council for the management of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be specifically responsible for the operation and protection of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.  
The entity designated by the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving area of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be specifically responsible for the operation and protection of the supporting facilities of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　水量调度

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　南水北调工程水量调度遵循节水为先、适度从紧的原则，统筹协调水源地、受水区和调水下游区域用水，加强生态环境保护。  
南水北调工程水量调度以国务院批准的多年平均调水量和受水区省、直辖市水量分配指标为基本依据。

**Article 7** The water quantity dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall follow the principles of prioritizing water conservation and moderate tightness, shall coordinate the water use of the water source areas, the water-receiving areas, and the downstream areas of the water diversion, and shall strengthen ecological environmental protection.  
The water quantity dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be based on the multi-year average water diversion volume approved by the State Council and the water quantity allocation indicators for the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　南水北调东线工程水量调度年度为每年10月1日至次年9月30日；南水北调中线工程水量调度年度为每年11月1日至次年10月31日。

**Article 8** The water quantity dispatch year for the eastern route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be from October 1 of each year to September 30 of the following year; the water quantity dispatch year for the central route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be from November 1 of each year to October 31 of the following year.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　淮河水利委员会商长江水利委员会提出南水北调东线工程年度可调水量，于每年9月15日前报送国务院水行政主管部门，并抄送有关省人民政府和南水北调工程管理单位。  
长江水利委员会提出南水北调中线工程年度可调水量，于每年10月15日前报送国务院水行政主管部门，并抄送有关省、直辖市人民政府和南水北调工程管理单位。

**Article 9** The Huaihe River Water Resources Commission shall, after consulting the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission, submit the annual adjustable water volume for the eastern route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project to the water administrative department of the State Council before September 15 of each year, and shall copy it to the relevant provincial people's governments and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.  
The Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall submit the annual adjustable water volume for the central route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project to the water administrative department of the State Council before October 15 of each year, and shall copy it to the relevant provincial and municipal people's governments and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门根据年度可调水量提出年度用水计划建议。属于南水北调东线工程受水区的于每年9月20日前、属于南水北调中线工程受水区的于每年10月20日前，报送国务院水行政主管部门，并抄送有关流域管理机构和南水北调工程管理单位。  
年度用水计划建议应当包括年度引水总量建议和月引水量建议。

**Article 10** The water administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall, based on the annual adjustable water volume, propose annual water use plans. For those belonging to the water-receiving areas of the eastern route, they shall submit the proposals before September 20 of each year; for those belonging to the water-receiving areas of the central route, they shall submit the proposals before October 20 of each year, to the water administrative department of the State Council, and shall copy them to the relevant basin management agencies and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.  
The annual water use plan proposals shall include proposals for the total annual water diversion volume and proposals for the monthly water diversion volume.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院水行政主管部门综合平衡年度可调水量和南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市年度用水计划建议，按照国务院批准的受水区省、直辖市水量分配指标的比例，制订南水北调工程年度水量调度计划，征求国务院有关部门意见后，在水量调度年度开始前下达有关省、直辖市人民政府和南水北调工程管理单位。

**Article 11** The water administrative department of the State Council shall comprehensively balance the annual adjustable water volume and the annual water use plan proposals of the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, formulate the annual water dispatch plan of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project in accordance with the proportions of the water allocation indicators for the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the receiving areas approved by the State Council, and after soliciting opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council, issue the plan to the relevant provincial and municipal people's governments and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project before the start of the water dispatch year.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　南水北调工程管理单位根据年度水量调度计划制订月水量调度方案，涉及到航运的，应当与交通运输主管部门协商，协商不一致的，由县级以上人民政府决定；雨情、水情出现重大变化，月水量调度方案无法实施的，应当及时进行调整并报告国务院水行政主管部门。

**Article 12** The management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall formulate a monthly water dispatch plan based on the annual water dispatch plan. If it involves navigation, it shall consult with the competent department of transportation; if consultation fails to reach an agreement, the decision shall be made by the people's government at or above the county level. If significant changes occur in rainfall and water conditions, making the monthly water dispatch plan unenforceable, the plan shall be adjusted in a timely manner and reported to the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　南水北调工程供水实行由基本水价和计量水价构成的两部制水价，具体供水价格由国务院价格主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
水费应当及时、足额缴纳，专项用于南水北调工程运行维护和偿还贷款。

**Article 13** The water supply of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall implement a two-part water price consisting of a basic water price and a metered water price. The specific water supply price shall be formulated by the competent department of pricing of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The water fee shall be paid in full and on time, and shall be used exclusively for the operation and maintenance of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project and for loan repayment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府授权的部门或者单位应当与南水北调工程管理单位签订供水合同。供水合同应当包括年度供水量、供水水质、交水断面、交水方式、水价、水费缴纳时间和方式、违约责任等。

**Article 14** The department or entity authorized by the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government in the receiving area of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall enter into a water supply contract with the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. The water supply contract shall include items such as the annual water supply volume, water supply quality, water delivery cross-section, water delivery method, water price, time and method of water fee payment, liability for breach of contract, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　水量调度年度内南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市用水需求出现重大变化，需要转让年度水量调度计划分配的水量的，由有关省、直辖市人民政府授权的部门或者单位协商签订转让协议，确定转让价格，并将转让协议报送国务院水行政主管部门，抄送南水北调工程管理单位；国务院水行政主管部门和南水北调工程管理单位应当相应调整年度水量调度计划和月水量调度方案。

**Article 15** If, within a water dispatch year, the water demand of a province or municipality directly under the Central Government in the receiving area of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project undergoes a significant change, and it is necessary to transfer the water volume allocated in the annual water dispatch plan, the department or entity authorized by the relevant provincial or municipal people's government shall negotiate and enter into a transfer agreement, determine the transfer price, and submit the transfer agreement to the water administrative department of the State Council, with a copy to the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. The water administrative department of the State Council and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall correspondingly adjust the annual water dispatch plan and the monthly water dispatch plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和有关省、直辖市人民政府以及南水北调工程管理单位编制南水北调工程水量调度应急预案，报国务院批准。  
南水北调工程水源地和受水区省、直辖市人民政府有关部门、有关流域管理机构以及南水北调工程管理单位应当根据南水北调工程水量调度应急预案，制定相应的应急预案。

**Article 16** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, the relevant provincial and municipal people's governments, and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, formulate an emergency plan for water dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The relevant departments of the provincial and municipal people's governments in the water source areas and receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the relevant river basin management institutions, and the management entity of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall, based on the emergency plan for water dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, formulate corresponding emergency plans.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　南水北调工程水量调度应急预案应当针对重大洪涝灾害、干旱灾害、生态破坏事故、水污染事故、工程安全事故等突发事件，规定应急管理工作的组织指挥体系与职责、预防与预警机制、处置程序、应急保障措施以及事后恢复与重建措施等内容。  
国务院或者国务院授权的部门宣布启动南水北调工程水量调度应急预案后，可以依法采取下列应急处置措施：  
(一)临时限制取水、用水、排水；  
(二)统一调度有关河道的水工程；  
(三)征用治污、供水等所需设施；  
(四)封闭通航河道。

**Article 17** The emergency plan for water dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall, in response to emergencies such as major flood disasters, drought disasters, ecological damage accidents, water pollution accidents, and engineering safety accidents, stipulate the organizational command system and responsibilities for emergency management, prevention and early warning mechanisms, handling procedures, emergency safeguard measures, and post-event recovery and reconstruction measures.  
After the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council announces the activation of the emergency plan for water dispatch of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the following emergency response measures may be taken according to law:  
(1) temporarily restrict water intake, water use, and drainage;  
(2) uniformly dispatch water projects in relevant rivers;  
(3) requisition facilities needed for pollution treatment, water supply, etc.;  
(4) close navigable river courses.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院水行政主管部门、环境保护主管部门按照职责组织对南水北调工程省界交水断面、东线工程取水口、丹江口水库的水量、水质进行监测。  
国务院水行政主管部门、环境保护主管部门按照职责定期向社会公布南水北调工程供用水水量、水质信息，并建立水量、水质信息共享机制。

**Article 18** The water administrative department and the environmental protection department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize monitoring of the water volume and water quality at the provincial boundary water delivery cross-sections of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the water intake of the Eastern Route Project, and the Danjiangkou Reservoir.  
The water administrative department and the environmental protection department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, regularly disclose to the public information on the water volume and water quality supplied and used by the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, and establish a sharing mechanism for water volume and water quality information.

### 第三章　水质保障

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　南水北调工程水质保障实行县级以上地方人民政府目标责任制和考核评价制度。  
南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强工业、城镇、农业和农村、船舶等水污染防治，建设防护林等生态隔离保护带，确保供水安全。  
依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，对南水北调工程水源地实行水环境生态保护补偿。

**Article 19** The guarantee of water quality in the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for local people's governments at or above the county level.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in the water source areas and areas along the water diversion route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall strengthen water pollution prevention and control from industry, urban areas, agriculture, rural areas, ships, etc., construct shelterbelts and other ecological isolation protection belts, and ensure the safety of water supply.  
In accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, ecological protection compensation for the water environment shall be implemented in the water source areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地实行重点水污染物排放总量控制制度。  
南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地省人民政府应当将国务院确定的重点水污染物排放总量控制指标逐级分解下达到有关市、县人民政府，由市、县人民政府分解落实到水污染物排放单位。

**Article 20** The Eastern Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project along the water diversion route areas and the water source area of the Central Route Project shall implement a total discharge volume control system for key water pollutants.  
The provincial people's governments in the water diversion route areas of the Eastern Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project and the water source area of the Central Route Project shall break down and assign the total discharge volume control indicators for key water pollutants determined by the State Council to the relevant city and county people's governments at each level, and the city and county people's governments shall further break down and assign them to water pollutant discharge units.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域禁止建设不符合国家产业政策、不能实现水污染物稳定达标排放的建设项目。现有的落后生产技术、工艺、设备等，由当地省人民政府组织淘汰。  
南水北调中线工程水源地禁止建设增加污染物排放总量的建设项目。

**Article 21** Construction projects that do not conform to the national industrial policies or cannot achieve stable discharge of water pollutants up to the standards shall be prohibited in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. The existing outdated production technologies, processes, equipment, etc. shall be eliminated by the people's government of the relevant province.  
Construction projects that increase the total amount of pollutant discharge shall be prohibited in the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地的水污染物排放单位，应当配套建设与其排放量相适应的治理设施；重点水污染物排放单位应当按照有关规定安装自动监测设备。  
南水北调东线工程干线、中线工程总干渠禁止设置排污口。

**Article 22** Water pollutant discharge entities in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route and the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall construct supporting treatment facilities commensurate with their discharge volume; key water pollutant discharge entities shall install automatic monitoring equipment in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Setting up sewage outlets shall be prohibited in the main line of the Eastern Route and the main canal of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地县级以上地方人民政府所在城镇排放的污水，应当经过集中处理，实现达标排放。  
南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地县级以上地方人民政府应当合理规划、建设污水集中处理设施和配套管网，并组织收集、无害化处理城乡生活垃圾，避免污染水环境。  
南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地的畜禽养殖场、养殖小区，应当按照国家有关规定对畜禽粪便、废水等进行无害化处理和资源化利用。

**Article 23** Sewage discharged from towns where the local people's governments at or above the county level are located in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route and the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be subject to centralized treatment and achieve discharge up to the standards.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route and the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall reasonably plan and construct centralized sewage treatment facilities and supporting pipeline networks, and organize the collection and harmless treatment of urban and rural domestic waste to avoid polluting the water environment.  
Livestock and poultry farms and breeding communities in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route and the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, conduct harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, wastewater, etc.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域和中线工程水源地县级人民政府应当根据水源保护的需要，划定禁止或者限制采伐、开垦区域。  
南水北调东线工程调水沿线区域、中线工程水源地、中线工程总干渠沿线区域应当规划种植生态防护林；生态地位重要的水域应当采取建设人工湿地等措施，提高水体自净能力。

**Article 24** The people's government at the county level in the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route and the water source area of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall, based on the needs of water source protection, delimit areas where felling or reclamation is prohibited or restricted.  
In the areas along the water diversion route of the Eastern Route, the water source area of the Central Route, and the areas along the main canal of the Central Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, ecological shelter forests shall be planned and planted; for water areas of important ecological status, measures such as constructing artificial wetlands shall be taken to improve the self-purification capacity of the water body.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　南水北调东线工程取水口、丹江口水库、中线工程总干渠需要划定饮用水水源保护区的，依照《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定划定，实行严格保护。

**Article 25** Where it is necessary to delimit drinking water source protection zones at the water intake of the Eastern Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the Danjiangkou Reservoir, and the main canal of the Central Route, such delimit shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Prevention and Control of Water Pollution, and strict protection shall be implemented.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　丹江口水库库区和洪泽湖、骆马湖、南四湖、东平湖湖区应当按照水功能区和南水北调工程水质保障的要求，由当地省人民政府组织逐步拆除现有的网箱养殖、围网养殖设施，严格控制人工养殖的规模、品种和密度。对因清理水产养殖设施导致转产转业的农民，当地县级以上地方人民政府应当给予补贴和扶持，并通过劳动技能培训、纳入社会保障体系等方式，保障其基本生活。  
丹江口水库库区和洪泽湖、骆马湖、南四湖、东平湖湖区禁止餐饮等经营活动。

**Article 26** In the reservoir area of the Danjiangkou Reservoir and the lake areas of Hongze Lake, Luoma Lake, Nansi Lake, and Dongping Lake, the local people's government of the relevant province shall organize the gradual removal of existing cage culture and net enclosure culture facilities in accordance with the requirements of water function zones and water quality assurance for the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, and strictly control the scale, species, and density of artificial aquaculture. For farmers who have to switch to other industries due to the removal of aquaculture facilities, the local people's government at or above the county level shall grant subsidies and support, and ensure their basic living through labor skill training, inclusion in the social security system, and other means.  
Catering and other business activities shall be prohibited in the reservoir area of the Danjiangkou Reservoir and the lake areas of Hongze Lake, Luoma Lake, Nansi Lake, and Dongping Lake.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　南水北调东线工程干线规划通航河道、丹江口水库及其上游通航河道应当科学规划建设港口、码头等航运设施，港口、码头应当配备与其吞吐能力相适应的船舶污染物接收、处理设备。现有的港口、码头不能达到水环境保护要求的，由当地省人民政府组织治理或者关闭。  
在前款规定河道航行的船舶应当按照要求进行技术改造，实现污染物船内封闭、收集上岸，不向水体排放；达不到要求的船舶和运输危险废物、危险化学品的船舶，不得进入上述河道，有关船闸管理单位不得放行。

**Article 27** For the planned navigable river channels of the main line of the Eastern Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the Danjiangkou Reservoir and its upstream navigable channels, ports, wharves and other navigation facilities shall be scientifically planned and constructed, and ports and wharves shall be equipped with ship pollutant reception and treatment facilities commensurate with their throughput capacity. Where existing ports and wharves cannot meet the requirements for water environmental protection, the local people's government of the relevant province shall organize treatment or closure.  
Ships navigating in the river channels specified in the preceding paragraph shall undergo technical transformation as required to achieve containment of pollutants on board, collection ashore, and no discharge into the water body; ships that fail to meet the requirements and ships transporting hazardous waste or hazardous chemicals shall not enter the said river channels, and the relevant lock management units shall not allow passage.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　建设穿越、跨越、邻接南水北调工程输水河道的桥梁、公路、石油天然气管道、雨污水管道等工程设施的，其建设、管理单位应当设置警示标志，并采取有效措施，防范工程建设或者交通事故、管道泄漏等带来的安全风险。

**Article 28** Where engineering facilities such as bridges, highways, oil and natural gas pipelines, and rainwater and sewage pipelines are constructed crossing, spanning, or adjoining the water conveyance channels of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the construction and management units shall set up warning signs and take effective measures to prevent safety risks arising from engineering construction, traffic accidents, pipeline leaks, etc.

### 第四章　用水管理

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　南水北调工程受水区县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹配置南水北调工程供水和当地水资源，逐步替代超采的地下水，严格控制地下水开发利用，改善水生态环境。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at or above the county level in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall coordinate the allocation of water supply from the South-to-North Water Diversion Project and local water resources, gradually replace over-exploited groundwater, strictly control the development and utilization of groundwater, and improve the water ecological environment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　南水北调工程受水区县级以上地方人民政府应当以南水北调工程供水替代不适合作为饮用水水源的当地水源，并逐步退还因缺水挤占的农业用水和生态环境用水。

**Article 30** Local people's governments at or above the county level in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall replace local water sources that are unsuitable as drinking water sources with water supply from the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, and gradually restore agricultural water and ecological environment water that have been occupied due to water shortage.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　南水北调工程受水区县级以上地方人民政府应当对本行政区域的年度用水实行总量控制，加强用水定额管理，推广节水技术、设备和设施，提高用水效率和效益。  
南水北调工程受水区县级以上地方人民政府应当鼓励、引导农民和农业生产经营组织调整农业种植结构，因地制宜减少高耗水作物种植比例，推行节水灌溉方式，促进节水农业发展。

**Article 31** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall implement total volume control over the annual water use in their respective administrative regions, strengthen the management of water use quotas, promote water-saving technologies, equipment and facilities, and improve water use efficiency and effectiveness.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall encourage and guide farmers and agricultural production and operation organizations to adjust the agricultural planting structure, reduce the proportion of high water-consuming crops according to local conditions, promote water-saving irrigation methods, and facilitate the development of water-saving agriculture.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府应当制订并公布本行政区域内禁止、限制类建设项目名录，淘汰、限制高耗水、高污染的建设项目。

**Article 32** The people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall formulate and publish a catalogue of prohibited and restricted construction projects within their respective administrative regions, and eliminate and restrict high water-consuming and high-polluting construction projects.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府应当将国务院批准的地下水压采总体方案确定的地下水开采总量控制指标和地下水压采目标，逐级分解下达到有关市、县人民政府，并组织编制本行政区域的地下水限制开采方案和年度计划，报国务院水行政主管部门、国土资源主管部门备案。

**Article 33** The people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall break down and assign, level by level, the total groundwater extraction volume control indicators and groundwater reduction extraction targets determined in the overall plan for groundwater reduction extraction approved by the State Council to the relevant people's governments of cities and counties, and shall organize the formulation of groundwater restricted extraction plans and annual plans for their respective administrative regions, and submit them to the competent water administrative department and the competent land and resources department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　南水北调工程受水区内地下水超采区禁止新增地下水取用水量。具备水源替代条件的地下水超采区，应当划定为地下水禁采区，禁止取用地下水。  
南水北调工程受水区禁止新增开采深层承压水。

**Article 34** In the groundwater overdraft areas within the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, new groundwater extraction or use volumes are prohibited. Groundwater overdraft areas that have conditions for water source substitution shall be designated as groundwater extraction prohibition areas, where groundwater extraction is prohibited.  
New extraction of deep confined groundwater is prohibited in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　南水北调工程受水区省、直辖市人民政府应当统筹考虑南水北调工程供水价格与当地地表水、地下水等各种水源的水资源费和供水价格，鼓励充分利用南水北调工程供水，促进水资源合理配置。

**Article 35** The people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall make overall plans taking into account the water supply price of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project and the water resource fees and water supply prices of various water sources such as local surface water and groundwater, and shall encourage the full utilization of water supplied by the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, thereby promoting the rational allocation of water resources.

### 第五章　工程设施管理和保护

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域、受水区县级以上地方人民政府应当做好工程设施安全保护有关工作，防范和制止危害南水北调工程设施安全的行为。

**Article 36** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the water source areas, the areas along the water diversion route, and the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall perform well the work related to the safety protection of the project facilities, and shall prevent and stop acts that endanger the safety of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project facilities.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　南水北调工程管理单位应当建立、健全安全生产责任制，加强对南水北调工程设施的监测、检查、巡查、维修和养护，配备必要的人员和设备，定期进行应急演练，确保工程安全运行，并及时组织清理管理范围内水域、滩地的垃圾。

**Article 37** The management units of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall establish and improve the responsibility system for work safety, strengthen the monitoring, inspection, patrol, maintenance and upkeep of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project facilities, equip necessary personnel and equipment, conduct regular emergency drills to ensure the safe operation of the project, and timely organize the cleanup of garbage in the waters and shoals within the management scope.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　南水北调工程应当依法划定管理范围和保护范围。  
南水北调东线工程的管理范围和保护范围，由工程所在地的省人民政府组织划定；其中，省际工程的管理范围和保护范围，由国务院水行政主管部门或者其授权的流域管理机构商有关省人民政府组织划定。  
丹江口水库、南水北调中线工程总干渠的管理范围和保护范围，由国务院水行政主管部门或者其授权的流域管理机构商有关省、直辖市人民政府组织划定。

**Article 38** The management scope and protection scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be delimited according to law.  
The management scope and protection scope of the Eastern Route Project of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be delimited by the people's government of the province where the project is located; among them, the management scope and protection scope of inter-provincial projects shall be delimited by the competent water administrative department of the State Council or its authorized river basin management agency in consultation with the relevant provincial people's governments.  
The management scope and protection scope of the Danjiangkou Reservoir and the main canal of the Middle Route Project of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be delimited by the competent water administrative department of the State Council or its authorized river basin management agency in consultation with the relevant people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　南水北调工程管理范围按照国务院批准的设计文件划定。  
南水北调工程管理单位应当在工程管理范围边界和地下工程位置上方地面设立界桩、界碑等保护标志，并设立必要的安全隔离设施对工程进行保护。未经南水北调工程管理单位同意，任何人不得进入设置安全隔离设施的区域。  
南水北调工程管理范围内的土地不得转作其他用途，任何单位和个人不得侵占；管理范围内禁止擅自从事与工程管理无关的活动。

**Article 39** The management scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be delimited in accordance with the design documents approved by the State Council.  
The management units of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall set up boundary markers, boundary tablets and other protection signs at the boundaries of the project management scope and on the ground above the locations of underground works, and shall set up necessary safety isolation facilities to protect the project. Without the consent of the management units of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, no person may enter the areas where safety isolation facilities are installed.  
The land within the management scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall not be converted to other uses, and no unit or individual may occupy it; within the management scope, activities unrelated to project management are prohibited without authorization.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　南水北调工程保护范围按照下列原则划定并予以公告：  
(一)东线明渠输水工程为从堤防背水侧的护堤地边线向外延伸至50米以内的区域，中线明渠输水工程为从管理范围边线向外延伸至200米以内的区域；  
(二)暗涵、隧洞、管道等地下输水工程为工程设施上方地面以及从其边线向外延伸至50米以内的区域；  
(三)倒虹吸、渡槽、暗渠等交叉工程为从管理范围边线向交叉河道上游延伸至不少于500米不超过1000米、向交叉河道下游延伸至不少于1000米不超过3000米以内的区域；  
(四)泵站、水闸、管理站、取水口等其他工程设施为从管理范围边线向外延伸至不少于50米不超过200米以内的区域。

**Article 40** The protection scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be delimited and announced in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) For the open channel water conveyance project of the Eastern Route, it shall be the area extending outward from the outer edge of the levee berm on the landside of the dike to within 50 meters; for the open channel water conveyance project of the Middle Route, it shall be the area extending outward from the boundary line of the management scope to within 200 meters;  
(2) For underground water conveyance projects such as culverts, tunnels and pipelines, it shall be the ground surface above the project facilities and the area extending outward from their boundary lines to within 50 meters;  
(3) For crossing projects such as inverted siphons, aqueducts and buried channels, it shall be the area extending upstream of the crossing channel from the boundary line of the management scope to not less than 500 meters but not more than 1,000 meters, and extending downstream of the crossing channel to not less than 1,000 meters but not more than 3,000 meters;  
(4) For other project facilities such as pumping stations, sluice gates, management stations and water intakes, it shall be the area extending outward from the boundary line of the management scope to not less than 50 meters but not more than 200 meters.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　南水北调工程管理单位应当在工程沿线路口、村庄等地段设置安全警示标志；有关地方人民政府主管部门应当按照有关规定，在交叉桥梁入口处设置限制质量、轴重、速度、高度、宽度等标志，并采取相应的工程防范措施。

**Article 41** The South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity shall set up safety warning signs at locations such as intersections and villages along the project route; the competent departments of the relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, set up signs restricting mass, axle load, speed, height, width, etc., at the entrances of cross bridges, and take corresponding engineering precautionary measures.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　禁止危害南水北调工程设施的下列行为：  
(一)侵占、损毁输水河道(渠道、管道)、水库、堤防、护岸；  
(二)在地下输水管道、堤坝上方地面种植深根植物或者修建鱼池等储水设施、堆放超重物品；  
(三)移动、覆盖、涂改、损毁标志物；  
(四)侵占、损毁或者擅自使用、操作专用输电线路设施、专用通信线路、闸门等设施；  
(五)侵占、损毁交通、通信、水文水质监测等其他设施。  
禁止擅自从南水北调工程取用水资源。

**Article 42** The following acts that endanger the facilities of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project are prohibited:  
(1) Encroaching upon or damaging water conveyance channels (canals, pipelines), reservoirs, dikes, or revetments;  
(2) Planting deep-rooted plants on the ground above underground water conveyance pipelines or dams, or constructing fish ponds or other water storage facilities, or stacking overweight articles;  
(3) Moving, covering, altering, or damaging markers;  
(4) Encroaching upon, damaging, or without authorization using or operating dedicated power transmission line facilities, dedicated communication lines, gates, or other facilities;  
(5) Encroaching upon or damaging other facilities such as transportation, communication, hydrological and water quality monitoring facilities.  
It is prohibited to withdraw water from the South-to-North Water Diversion Project without authorization.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　禁止在南水北调工程保护范围内实施影响工程运行、危害工程安全和供水安全的爆破、打井、采矿、取土、采石、采砂、钻探、建房、建坟、挖塘、挖沟等行为。

**Article 43** Within the protection scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, acts such as blasting, well drilling, mining, earth taking, stone quarrying, sand excavation, drilling, building construction, tomb building, pond digging, ditch digging, etc., that affect project operation or endanger project safety or water supply safety are prohibited.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在南水北调工程管理范围和保护范围内建设桥梁、码头、公路、铁路、地铁、船闸、管道、缆线、取水、排水等工程设施，按照国家规定的基本建设程序报请审批、核准时，审批、核准单位应当征求南水北调工程管理单位对拟建工程设施建设方案的意见。  
前款规定的建设项目在施工、维护、检修前，应当通报南水北调工程管理单位，施工、维护、检修过程中不得影响南水北调工程设施安全和正常运行。

**Article 44** When submitting for approval or verification, in accordance with the basic construction procedures specified by the State, projects such as bridges, wharves, highways, railways, subways, ship locks, pipelines, cables, water intakes, drainage facilities, etc., to be constructed within the management scope and protection scope of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the approving or verifying authority shall solicit opinions from the South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity on the proposed construction plan.  
Before construction, maintenance, or overhaul of a construction project as specified in the preceding paragraph, the project entity shall notify the South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity, and the construction, maintenance, or overhaul process shall not affect the safety and normal operation of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project facilities.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　在汛期，南水北调工程管理单位应当加强巡查，发现险情立即采取抢修等措施，并及时向有关防汛抗旱指挥机构报告。

**Article 45** During the flood season, the South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity shall strengthen patrols, immediately take emergency repair measures upon discovering dangerous situations, and promptly report to the relevant flood control and drought relief headquarters.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　南水北调工程管理单位应当加强南水北调工程设施的安全保护，制定安全保护方案，建立健全安全保护责任制，加强安全保护设施的建设、维护，落实治安防范措施，及时排除隐患。  
南水北调工程重要水域、重要设施需要派出中国人民武装警察部队守卫或者抢险救援的，依照《中华人民共和国人民武装警察法》和国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 46** The South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity shall strengthen the safety protection of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project facilities, formulate a safety protection plan, establish and improve a safety protection responsibility system, strengthen the construction and maintenance of safety protection facilities, implement public security prevention measures, and promptly eliminate hidden dangers.  
If the important water areas or important facilities of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project require the dispatch of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force for guard or emergency rescue, this shall be executed in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Armed Police Force and relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　在紧急情况下，南水北调工程管理单位因工程抢修需要取土占地或者使用有关设施的，有关单位和个人应当予以配合。南水北调工程管理单位应当于事后恢复原状；造成损失的，应当依法予以补偿。

**Article 47** In an emergency, if the South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity needs to take earth or occupy land, or use relevant facilities for emergency repair of the project, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate. The South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity shall restore the original condition afterwards; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　行政机关及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由主管机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不及时制订下达或者不执行年度水量调度计划的；  
(二)不编制或者不执行水量调度应急预案的；  
(三)不编制或者不执行南水北调工程受水区地下水限制开采方案的；  
(四)不履行水量、水质监测职责的；  
(五)不履行本条例规定的其他职责的。

**Article 48** If an administrative organ or its staff member violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the competent authority or supervisory authority shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to formulate and issue in a timely manner, or failing to implement, the annual water quantity dispatch plan;  
(2) Failing to formulate or failing to implement the water quantity dispatch emergency plan;  
(3) Failing to formulate or failing to implement the groundwater restricted extraction plan in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project;  
(4) Failing to perform the duties of water quantity and water quality monitoring;  
(5) Failing to perform other duties as provided in these Regulations.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　南水北调工程管理单位及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由主管机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)虚假填报或者篡改工程运行情况等资料的；  
(二)不执行年度水量调度计划或者水量调度应急预案的；  
(三)不及时制订或者不执行月水量调度方案的；  
(四)对工程设施疏于监测、检查、巡查、维修、养护，不落实安全生产责任制，影响工程安全、供水安全的；  
(五)不履行本条例规定的其他职责的。

**Article 49** If the South-to-North Water Diversion Project management entity or its staff member violates these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the competent authority or supervisory authority shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; if the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable personnel constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Falsely filling in or tampering with project operation data or other materials;  
(2) Failing to implement the annual water quantity dispatch plan or the water quantity dispatch emergency plan;  
(3) Failing to formulate in a timely manner or failing to implement the monthly water quantity dispatch plan;  
(4) Neglecting the monitoring, inspection, patrol, repair, and maintenance of project facilities, failing to implement the safety production responsibility system, affecting project safety or water supply safety;  
(5) Failing to perform other duties as provided in these Regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，实施排放水污染物等危害南水北调工程水质安全行为的，依照《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，在南水北调工程受水区地下水禁采区取用地下水、在受水区地下水超采区新增地下水取用水量、在受水区新增开采深层承压水，或者擅自从南水北调工程取用水资源的，依照《中华人民共和国水法》的规定处理。

**Article 50** Any person who violates these Regulations by committing acts such as discharging water pollutants that endanger the water quality safety of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be dealt with in accordance with the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Any person who violates these Regulations by extracting groundwater from the prohibited extraction area in the water-receiving areas of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, increasing groundwater extraction in the groundwater over-extraction area in the water-receiving areas, newly exploiting deep confined groundwater in the water-receiving areas, or without authorization withdrawing water from the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, shall be dealt with in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，运输危险废物、危险化学品的船舶进入南水北调东线工程干线规划通航河道、丹江口水库及其上游通航河道的，由县级以上地方人民政府负责海事、渔业工作的行政主管部门按照职责权限予以扣押，对危险废物、危险化学品采取卸载等措施，所需费用由违法行为人承担；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** : Where a vessel transporting hazardous wastes or hazardous chemicals enters the planned navigable channels of the main line of the Eastern Route Project of the South-to-North Water Diversion, the Danjiangkou Reservoir, or the navigable channels upstream thereof in violation of these Regulations, the competent administrative department in charge of maritime or fishery affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its functions and powers, detain the vessel, take measures such as unloading the hazardous wastes or hazardous chemicals, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，建设穿越、跨越、邻接南水北调工程输水河道的桥梁、公路、石油天然气管道、雨污水管道等工程设施，未采取有效措施，危害南水北调工程安全和供水安全的，由建设项目审批、核准单位责令采取补救措施；在补救措施落实前，暂停工程设施建设。

**Article 52** : Where a project facility such as a bridge, highway, oil or natural gas pipeline, or rainwater or sewage pipeline is constructed crossing, spanning, or adjacent to a water conveyance channel of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project in violation of these Regulations, and no effective measures are taken, thereby endangering the safety of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project and the safety of water supply, the unit that has approved or verified the construction project shall order remedial measures to be taken; before the remedial measures are implemented, construction of the project facility shall be suspended.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，侵占、损毁、危害南水北调工程设施，或者在南水北调工程保护范围内实施影响工程运行、危害工程安全和供水安全的行为的，依照《中华人民共和国水法》的规定处理；《中华人民共和国水法》未作规定的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构按照职责权限，责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施；造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** : Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, encroaches upon, damages, or endangers the facilities of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, or commits any act within the protected area of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project that affects the project's operation or endangers the project's safety and the safety of water supply, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China; if there is no provision in the Water Law of the People's Republic of China, the competent water administrative department or the river basin management authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its functions and powers, order the cessation of the illegal act and require remedial measures to be taken within a time limit; if losses are caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　南水北调工程水源地、调水沿线区域有下列情形之一的，暂停审批有关行政区域除污染减排和生态恢复项目外所有建设项目的环境影响评价文件：  
(一)在东线工程干线、中线工程总干渠设置排污口的；  
(二)排污超过重点水污染物排放总量控制指标的；  
(三)违法批准建设污染水环境的建设项目造成重大水污染事故等严重后果，未落实补救措施的。

**Article 54** : Where any of the following circumstances exists in the water source areas or the areas along the water diversion route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, the examination and approval of environmental impact assessment documents for all construction projects in the relevant administrative regions, except for pollution reduction and ecological restoration projects, shall be suspended:  
(1) A sewage outlet is set up in the main line of the Eastern Route Project or the main canal of the Middle Route Project;  
(2) The discharge of pollutants exceeds the total discharge control target for key water pollutants;  
(3) A major water pollution accident or other serious consequences are caused by the illegal approval of a construction project that pollutes the water environment, and no remedial measures have been implemented.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　南水北调东线工程，指从江苏省扬州市附近的长江干流引水，调水到江苏省北部和山东省等地的主体工程。  
南水北调中线工程，指从丹江口水库引水，调水到河南省、河北省、北京市、天津市的主体工程。  
南水北调配套工程，指东线工程、中线工程分水口门以下，配置、调度分配给本行政区域使用的南水北调供水的工程。

**Article 55** : The Eastern Route Project of the South-to-North Water Diversion refers to the main project that diverts water from the mainstream of the Yangtze River near Yangzhou City, Jiangsu Province, to northern Jiangsu Province and Shandong Province.  
The Middle Route Project of the South-to-North Water Diversion refers to the main project that diverts water from the Danjiangkou Reservoir to Henan Province, Hebei Province, Beijing Municipality, and Tianjin Municipality.  
The supporting projects of the South-to-North Water Diversion refer to the projects below the water diversion outlet gates of the Eastern Route Project and the Middle Route Project, which allocate and dispatch the water supplied by the South-to-North Water Diversion for use in the relevant administrative region.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 56** : These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
