# 长江三峡水利枢纽安全保卫条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：经济法  
**公布日期**：2013-09-09  
**生效日期**：2013-10-01

> (2013年7月12日国务院第16次常务会议通过　2013年9月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第640号公布　自2013年10月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强长江三峡水利枢纽安全保卫工作，维护长江三峡水利枢纽的安全和秩序，制定本条例。 第二条　国家设立长江三峡水利枢纽(以下简称三峡枢纽)安全保卫区，依照本条例的规定对三峡枢纽实施保护。 第三条　三峡枢纽安全保卫区的范围包括三峡枢纽及其周边特定区域，分为陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区、空域安全保卫区。 陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区实行分区安全保卫制度，具体范围的划定和调整，由湖北省人民政府确定并公布。 空域安全保卫区为陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区上空的低空空域。 第四条　三峡枢纽安全保卫工作坚持预防与应急处置相结合、专门机关管理与人民群众参与相结合、安全保卫与经济社会发展并重的原则。 第五条　国家统一领导三峡枢纽安全保卫工作。国务院公安、交通运输、水行政等部门和三峡枢纽运行管理单位依照法律、行政法规和国务院确定的职责分工负责三峡枢纽安全保卫有关工作。湖北省人民政府、宜昌市人民政府对三峡枢纽安全保卫工作实行属地管理。 第六条　任何组织或者个人都不得危害三峡枢纽的安全，发现危害三峡枢纽安全的行为和隐患应当立即报告。 对保护三峡枢纽安全作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰奖励。 第七条　三峡枢纽安全保卫工作所需经费，由有关人民政府和单位按照职责分工和规定的经费负担体制予以保障。 第二章　陆域安全保卫 第八条　陆域安全保卫区划分为限制区、控制区、核心区。各区的周边界线应当设置实物屏障或者警示标志，各区的出入口和重点部位应当配备警戒岗哨或者技术防范设施。 第九条　限制区是限制无关车辆接近三峡枢纽的区域。车辆凭限制区通行证方可进入限制区。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强长江三峡水利枢纽安全保卫工作，维护长江三峡水利枢纽的安全和秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the security and protection of the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River, and maintaining the safety and order of the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家设立长江三峡水利枢纽(以下简称三峡枢纽)安全保卫区，依照本条例的规定对三峡枢纽实施保护。

**Article 2** The State establishes a security and protection zone for the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River (hereinafter referred to as the Three Gorges Project), and shall protect the Three Gorges Project in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　三峡枢纽安全保卫区的范围包括三峡枢纽及其周边特定区域，分为陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区、空域安全保卫区。  
陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区实行分区安全保卫制度，具体范围的划定和调整，由湖北省人民政府确定并公布。  
空域安全保卫区为陆域安全保卫区、水域安全保卫区上空的低空空域。

**Article 3** The scope of the security and protection zone of the Three Gorges Project includes the Three Gorges Project and its surrounding specific areas, and is divided into a land security and protection zone, a water security and protection zone, and an air security and protection zone.  
A zoned security and protection system shall be implemented in the land security and protection zone and the water security and protection zone, and the delimitation and adjustment of the specific scope shall be determined and announced by the People's Government of Hubei Province.  
The air security and protection zone is the low-altitude airspace above the land security and protection zone and the water security and protection zone.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　三峡枢纽安全保卫工作坚持预防与应急处置相结合、专门机关管理与人民群众参与相结合、安全保卫与经济社会发展并重的原则。

**Article 4** The security and protection work of the Three Gorges Project shall adhere to the principles of combining prevention with emergency response, combining specialized agency management with participation of the masses, and giving equal importance to security and protection and economic and social development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家统一领导三峡枢纽安全保卫工作。国务院公安、交通运输、水行政等部门和三峡枢纽运行管理单位依照法律、行政法规和国务院确定的职责分工负责三峡枢纽安全保卫有关工作。湖北省人民政府、宜昌市人民政府对三峡枢纽安全保卫工作实行属地管理。

**Article 5** The State shall exercise unified leadership over the security and protection work of the Three Gorges Project. The public security, transport, water administration and other departments under the State Council and the operation and management entity of the Three Gorges Project shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and the division of responsibilities determined by the State Council, be responsible for the relevant work concerning the security and protection of the Three Gorges Project. The People's Government of Hubei Province and the People's Government of Yichang City shall exercise territorial management over the security and protection work of the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何组织或者个人都不得危害三峡枢纽的安全，发现危害三峡枢纽安全的行为和隐患应当立即报告。  
对保护三峡枢纽安全作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰奖励。

**Article 6** No organization or individual may endanger the safety of the Three Gorges Project. Any act or hidden danger that endangers the safety of the Three Gorges Project shall be reported immediately.  
Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to the protection of the safety of the Three Gorges Project shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　三峡枢纽安全保卫工作所需经费，由有关人民政府和单位按照职责分工和规定的经费负担体制予以保障。

**Article 7** The funds required for the security and protection work of the Three Gorges Project shall be guaranteed by the relevant people's governments and entities in accordance with the division of responsibilities and the prescribed funding burden system.

### 第二章　陆域安全保卫

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　陆域安全保卫区划分为限制区、控制区、核心区。各区的周边界线应当设置实物屏障或者警示标志，各区的出入口和重点部位应当配备警戒岗哨或者技术防范设施。

**Article 8** The land security and protection zone is divided into a restricted area, a control area, and a core area. Physical barriers or warning signs shall be set up at the boundaries of each area, and guard posts or technical prevention facilities shall be equipped at the entrances and exits and key parts of each area.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　限制区是限制无关车辆接近三峡枢纽的区域。车辆凭限制区通行证方可进入限制区。  
人员可以徒步进出限制区。但是，根据三峡枢纽安全保卫工作的需要，三峡枢纽运行管理单位可以临时限制人员通行。

**Article 9** The restricted area is an area where irrelevant vehicles are restricted from approaching the Three Gorges Project. Vehicles may enter the restricted area only with a restricted area pass.  
Personnel may enter and exit the restricted area on foot. However, the operation and management entity of the Three Gorges Project may temporarily restrict the passage of personnel as required by the security and protection work of the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　控制区是限制无关车辆和人员接近三峡枢纽，并在紧急情况时提供有效缓冲和防护的区域。车辆和人员凭控制区通行证并按规定接受安全检查方可进入控制区。

**Article 10** The control area is an area that restricts irrelevant vehicles and personnel from approaching the Three Gorges Project, and provides effective buffer and protection in case of emergency. Vehicles and personnel may enter the control area only with a control area pass and after undergoing security checks as prescribed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　核心区是实行封闭式管理的区域。核心区实施下列安全保卫措施：  
(一)出入口和重点部位由人民武装警察部队设置岗哨，车辆、人员凭核心区通行证、接受安全检查并确认资格身份方可通行；  
(二)物资凭有效的物资转场、外运、内运申请单通行，必要时在出入口对所有通行物资进行危险物品检测；  
(三)配备覆盖全区的视频监控等技术防范设施，实施全天候安全巡查。

**Article 11** The core area is an area under closed management. The following security and protection measures shall be implemented in the core area:  
(1) The People's Armed Police Force shall set up guards at the entrances and exits and key parts; vehicles and personnel may pass only with a core area pass, after undergoing security checks and having their identity confirmed;  
(2) Materials may pass only with valid applications for material transfer, outward transport, or inward transport; where necessary, dangerous goods detection shall be conducted on all passing materials at the entrances and exits;  
(3) Video surveillance and other technical prevention facilities covering the whole area shall be equipped, and round-the-clock security patrols shall be carried out.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进入陆域安全保卫区的车辆、人员的通行证件，由三峡枢纽运行管理单位核发和管理，并报公安机关备案。

**Article 12** The passes for vehicles and personnel entering the land security and protection zone shall be issued and managed by the operation and management entity of the Three Gorges Project, and shall be reported to the public security organ for record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　严禁非法携带枪支、弹药、国家规定的管制器具或者非法运输危险物品进入陆域安全保卫区。  
因施工作业等需要运输危险物品进入陆域安全保卫区的，应当经三峡枢纽运行管理单位同意，并依法报公安机关批准。

**Article 13** It is strictly prohibited to illegally carry firearms, ammunition, or controlled instruments prescribed by the State, or illegally transport dangerous articles into the land security and protection zone.  
If it is necessary to transport dangerous articles into the land security and protection zone for construction or other operations, the consent of the operation and management entity of the Three Gorges Project shall be obtained, and the matter shall be submitted to the public security organ for approval according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在陆域安全保卫区活动的人员不得有下列行为：  
(一)超越通行证限定的范围活动；  
(二)通过设有禁止通行标志的区域；  
(三)攀爬、钻越、移动、损毁实物屏障或者警示标志；  
(四)扰乱管理秩序和危害设施安全的其他行为。

**Article 14** Personnel active in the land security and protection zone shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Exceeding the scope limited by the pass;  
(2) Passing through areas marked with no-entry signs;  
(3) Climbing, burrowing under, moving, or damaging physical barriers or warning signs;  
(4) Other acts that disturb the management order or endanger the safety of facilities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　未持有效的通行证件或者拒绝接受安全检查的车辆、人员，禁止进入陆域安全保卫区。安全保卫人员对非法进入或者违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)、第十四条规定的车辆、人员，应当立即制止；对不服从管理的，应当立即依法予以控制并移送公安机关处理。

**Article 15** Vehicles and personnel that do not hold valid passes or refuse to undergo security checks are prohibited from entering the land security and protection zone. Security and protection personnel shall immediately stop vehicles and personnel that illegally enter or violate the provisions of Articles 13 and 14 of these Regulations; if they refuse to comply with management, they shall be immediately controlled according to law and transferred to the public security organ for handling.

### 第三章　水域安全保卫

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　水域安全保卫区划分为管制区、通航区、禁航区。各区的周边界线应当设置警示标志，配备警戒岗哨或者技术防范设施。

**Article 16** The water security and protection zone is divided into a control area, a navigation area, and a prohibited navigation area. Warning signs shall be set up at the boundaries of each area, and guard posts or technical prevention facilities shall be equipped.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　管制区是对拟通过三峡枢纽通航建筑物(以下称过闸)的船舶进行安全检查、防止未经批准通过的船舶接近三峡枢纽的缓冲水域。进入管制区的船舶，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)过闸船舶应当按照国务院交通运输部门规定的程序和要求，提前向三峡通航管理机构报告；  
(二)不得运输国务院交通运输部门规定禁止过闸的危险物品；运输其他危险物品过闸的，应当向三峡通航管理机构申报，不得伪报、瞒报；  
(三)不得进行容易引发火灾、爆炸事故的检修作业；  
(四)不得在集泊期间上下除本船船员以外的其他人员或者装卸物品；  
(五)不得抢航、追越或者有其他扰乱水上交通秩序的行为。

**Article 17** The control area is a buffer water area for conducting security inspections on vessels that intend to pass through the navigation structures of the Three Gorges Project (hereinafter referred to as passing the lock), and preventing vessels that have not been approved from approaching the Three Gorges Project. Vessels entering the control area shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Vessels passing the lock shall report to the Three Gorges navigation administration authority in advance in accordance with the procedures and requirements prescribed by the transport department of the State Council;  
(2) No vessels shall transport dangerous articles that are prohibited by the transport department of the State Council from passing the lock; those transporting other dangerous articles through the lock shall declare to the Three Gorges navigation administration authority, and shall not make false declarations or conceal them;  
(3) No repair operations that may easily cause fire or explosion accidents shall be carried out;  
(4) During the period of berthing, no persons other than the crew of the vessel may board or disembark, and no goods may be loaded or unloaded;  
(5) No overtaking or other acts that disturb the order of water traffic shall be committed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　通航区是由三峡通航管理机构统一指挥调度过闸船舶进出引航道和通航建筑物的特定区域。  
船舶应当按照调度指令有序进出引航道和通航建筑物，不得在通航区内擅自停泊，不得抢航、追越或者有其他扰乱水上交通秩序的行为。过闸期间船员、乘客不得擅自离开本船，不得攀爬通航建筑物。

**Article 18** The navigation area is a specific area where the Three Gorges navigation administration authority shall uniformly direct and dispatch vessels passing the lock to enter and exit the approach channel and the navigation structures.  
Vessels shall enter and exit the approach channel and the navigation structures in an orderly manner in accordance with the dispatching instructions, and shall not berth without authorization in the navigation area, or commit overtaking or other acts that disturb the order of water traffic. During the period of passing the lock, crew members and passengers shall not leave their vessels without authorization, and shall not climb the navigation structures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　禁航区是禁止船舶和人员进入的水域。除公务执法船舶以及持有三峡枢纽运行管理单位签发的作业任务书和三峡通航管理机构签发的施工作业许可证的船舶外，任何船舶和人员不得进入禁航区。  
禁航区应当设置人民武装警察部队岗哨。

**Article 19** The prohibited navigation area is a water area where vessels and personnel are prohibited from entering. With the exception of official law enforcement vessels and vessels holding a work assignment letter issued by the operation and management entity of the Three Gorges Project and a construction work permit issued by the Three Gorges navigation administration authority, no vessel or personnel may enter the prohibited navigation area.  
Guards of the People's Armed Police Force shall be set up in the prohibited navigation area.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对因机械故障等原因失去控制有可能进入水域安全保卫区的船舶，三峡通航管理机构应当立即采取措施使其远离。  
对违反规定进入管制区、通航区的船舶，公安机关、三峡通航管理机构应当立即制止并将其带离。  
对违反规定进入禁航区的船舶，人民武装警察部队执勤人员应当立即进行拦截并责令驶离；对拒绝驶离的，应当立即依法予以控制并移送公安机关处理。

**Article 20** For a vessel that has lost control due to mechanical failure or other reasons and may enter the water security and protection zone, the Three Gorges navigation administration authority shall immediately take measures to keep it away.  
For a vessel that enters the control area or navigation area in violation of the provisions, the public security organ and the Three Gorges navigation administration authority shall immediately stop it and have it removed.  
For a vessel that enters the prohibited navigation area in violation of the provisions, the personnel on duty of the People's Armed Police Force shall immediately intercept it and order it to leave; if it refuses to leave, it shall be immediately controlled according to law and transferred to the public security organ for handling.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　运输危险物品过闸的船舶，应当按照规定的方式、时间过闸，不得与客运船舶同一闸次通过，除核定船员和押运人员外，禁止其他人员随船。

**Article 21** A vessel transporting dangerous substances through the ship lock shall pass through the lock in accordance with the prescribed manner and time, and shall not pass through the same lockage as passenger vessels. Except for authorized crew members and escorts, no other persons shall be allowed on board.

### 第四章　空域安全保卫

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在空域安全保卫区飞行的航空器，应当严格按照飞行管制部门批准的计划飞行。

**Article 22** An aircraft flying in the airspace security zone shall strictly fly in accordance with the plan approved by the flight control authority.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止在空域安全保卫区进行风筝、孔明灯、热气球、飞艇、动力伞、滑翔伞、三角翼、无人机、轻型直升机、航模等升放或者飞行活动。

**Article 23** Launching or flying activities such as kites, sky lanterns, hot air balloons, airships, powered paragliders, paragliders, delta wings, unmanned aerial vehicles, light helicopters, and model aircraft are prohibited in the airspace security zone.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　负有三峡枢纽安全保卫职责的部门和单位，应当加强对空域安全保卫区安全情况的动态监控，发现违反三峡枢纽空域安全保卫规定的升放或者飞行活动，应当立即协调有关部门查明情况，依法采取必要的处置措施。

**Article 24** The departments and units responsible for the security protection of the Three Gorges Project shall strengthen dynamic monitoring of the security situation in the airspace security zone. If they discover any launching or flying activities that violate the regulations on airspace security protection of the Three Gorges Project, they shall immediately coordinate with relevant departments to ascertain the situation and take necessary disposal measures according to law.

### 第五章　安全保卫职责

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院确定的机构负责沟通协调和研究解决三峡枢纽安全保卫工作中的重大事项。  
湖北省人民政府负责组织建立由长江流域各省、直辖市人民政府以及国务院有关部门和单位参加的三峡枢纽安全保卫指挥系统，制定三峡枢纽安全保卫方案，并做好信息沟通和汇总研判工作。  
宜昌市人民政府负责组织建立由三峡枢纽运行管理单位、三峡通航管理机构、公安机关、人民武装警察部队等组成的三峡枢纽安全保卫指挥平台，统一管理三峡枢纽安全保卫工作。  
三峡枢纽运行管理单位应当加强与三峡安全保卫区内其他机构、单位的沟通协调，并依法提供必要的保障和业务指导。

**Article 25** The institution designated by the State Council shall be responsible for communication, coordination, research, and resolution of major issues in the security protection work of the Three Gorges Project.  
The People's Government of Hubei Province shall be responsible for organizing the establishment of a security protection command system for the Three Gorges Project, which includes the people's governments of the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government in the Yangtze River Basin, as well as relevant departments and units under the State Council; formulating the security protection plan for the Three Gorges Project; and performing information communication, aggregation, and assessment work.  
The People's Government of Yichang City shall be responsible for organizing the establishment of a security protection command platform for the Three Gorges Project, composed of the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project, the Three Gorges navigation administration agency, public security organs, and the People's Armed Police Force, and for managing the security protection work of the Three Gorges Project in a unified manner.  
The operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project shall strengthen communication and coordination with other institutions and units within the Three Gorges security zone, and provide necessary support and professional guidance according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　宜昌市人民政府应当会同三峡枢纽运行管理单位加强三峡枢纽安全保卫区及其周边地区社会管理综合治理，落实治安联防制度，维护社会秩序，防范针对三峡枢纽的破坏活动。

**Article 26** The People's Government of Yichang City shall, together with the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project, strengthen comprehensive social management in the security zone of the Three Gorges Project and its surrounding areas, implement the system of joint public security prevention, maintain social order, and prevent destructive activities targeting the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　负有三峡枢纽安全保卫职责的地方人民政府应当组织开展经常性的安全宣传教育，提高社会公众安全防范意识和能力。  
三峡枢纽周边地区的村民委员会、居民委员会、企业事业单位应当根据所在地人民政府的要求，结合各自的实际情况，开展三峡枢纽安全保卫和应急救援知识的宣传普及活动。

**Article 27** The local people's governments responsible for the security protection of the Three Gorges Project shall organize and carry out regular safety publicity and education to enhance the safety awareness and capabilities of the public.  
The villagers' committees, residents' committees, enterprises, and institutions in the areas surrounding the Three Gorges Project shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and in light of their respective actual conditions, carry out publicity and popularization activities on knowledge of security protection and emergency rescue of the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　三峡枢纽运行管理单位是安全生产、治安保卫重点单位，应当依法落实安全生产、治安保卫重点单位职责，建立安全运行监测体系；对重要部位、特种设备进行重点排查梳理，加强对重要岗位工作人员的背景审查及其身份核对。  
湖北省人民政府、宜昌市人民政府应当定期对三峡枢纽运行管理单位依法履行上述职责的情况进行监督检查，发现问题及时督促改正。

**Article 28** The operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project is a key unit for work safety and public security protection, and shall, according to law, fulfill the duties of a key unit for work safety and public security protection, establish a safety operation monitoring system; conduct key inspections and sorting of important parts and special equipment, and strengthen background checks and identity verification of staff in key positions.  
The People's Government of Hubei Province and the People's Government of Yichang City shall regularly supervise and inspect the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project's performance of the aforesaid duties according to law, and promptly urge rectification upon discovery of problems.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　三峡枢纽运行管理单位应当加强计算机信息系统安全保护工作，依法落实信息安全等级保护制度和技术标准，建立安全管理制度、完善技术保护措施，做好通报预警、安全监测、应急处置等工作，发现危害计算机信息系统安全的情况应当立即向公安机关报告。  
公安机关应当加强对三峡枢纽运行管理单位计算机信息系统安全保护工作的监督、检查、指导，发现影响计算机信息系统安全的情况应当及时通知三峡枢纽运行管理单位采取安全保护措施。

**Article 29** The operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project shall strengthen the security protection of computer information systems, implement the information security level protection system and technical standards according to law, establish security management systems and improve technical protection measures, and properly perform tasks such as notification and early warning, security monitoring, and emergency response. If any situation endangering the security of computer information systems is discovered, it shall immediately report to the public security organ.  
The public security organs shall strengthen the supervision, inspection, and guidance of the security protection work of the computer information systems of the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project. If any situation affecting the security of computer information systems is discovered, they shall promptly notify the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project to take security protection measures.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　三峡枢纽运行管理单位是消防安全重点单位，应当依法落实消防安全重点单位职责，配备消防设施和消防器材，建立消防安全制度和操作规程。  
湖北省人民政府、宜昌市人民政府应当针对三峡枢纽安全保卫工作加强公安消防队、专职消防队建设，及时组织消除火灾隐患。

**Article 30** The operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project is a key unit for fire safety, and shall, according to law, fulfill the duties of a key unit for fire safety, equip fire protection facilities and fire-fighting equipment, and establish fire safety systems and operating procedures.  
The People's Government of Hubei Province and the People's Government of Yichang City shall, with respect to the security protection work of the Three Gorges Project, strengthen the construction of public security fire brigades and full-time fire brigades, and promptly organize the elimination of fire hazards.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在三峡枢纽安全保卫区范围内设立或者调整游览项目，应当由三峡枢纽运行管理单位制定游览项目实施方案，报湖北省人民政府审批。湖北省人民政府应当组织有关部门和单位对设立或者调整的游览项目进行安全风险评估。三峡枢纽运行管理单位应当按照批准的游览项目实施方案组织游览活动，不得擅自扩大范围、增设游览项目。  
根据三峡枢纽安全保卫、生产运行、防汛抗旱、水资源调度等需要，三峡枢纽运行管理单位可以会同有关部门控制进入游览区域的游客人数、暂停部分或者全部游览项目，并将相关信息及时予以公布。

**Article 31** To establish or adjust a sightseeing project within the security zone of the Three Gorges Project, the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project shall formulate an implementation plan for the sightseeing project and submit it to the People's Government of Hubei Province for approval. The People's Government of Hubei Province shall organize relevant departments and units to conduct a safety risk assessment of the established or adjusted sightseeing project. The operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project shall organize the sightseeing activities in accordance with the approved implementation plan and shall not expand the scope or add sightseeing projects without authorization.  
When necessary for the security protection, production operation, flood control and drought relief, or water resource dispatch of the Three Gorges Project, the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project may, together with relevant departments, control the number of tourists entering the sightseeing area, suspend part or all of the sightseeing projects, and promptly publish the relevant information.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　长江流域各港口、码头应当建立由经营管理单位法定代表人负责的安全保卫制度，消除拟进入三峡枢纽安全保卫区的船舶的安全隐患。  
拟进入三峡枢纽安全保卫区的船舶应当建立由船舶所有人、经营人负责的安全保卫制度，依法对船员、乘客和货物进行安全检查和登记。

**Article 32** Each port and dock in the Yangtze River Basin shall establish a security protection system under the responsibility of the legal representative of the operation management unit to eliminate safety hazards of vessels intending to enter the security zone of the Three Gorges Project.  
A vessel intending to enter the security zone of the Three Gorges Project shall establish a security protection system under the responsibility of the vessel owner and operator, and shall conduct safety inspections and registration of crew members, passengers, and cargo according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　三峡通航管理机构应当依照本条例和国务院交通运输部门制定的三峡枢纽通航安全管理规定，对拟过闸的船舶进行安全检查、航运调度指挥。

**Article 33** The Three Gorges navigation administration agency shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the safety management regulations for navigation through the Three Gorges Project formulated by the transportation department of the State Council, conduct safety inspections and navigation dispatch and command for vessels intending to pass through the ship lock.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　负有三峡枢纽安全保卫职责的地方人民政府、部门和单位，应当按照《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定，制定各类突发事件的应急预案，组织培训应急救援专业力量，定期开展联合演练；三峡枢纽运行管理单位应当对三峡枢纽安全保卫区内发生的突发事件进行先期处置。

**Article 34** The local people's governments, departments, and units responsible for the security protection of the Three Gorges Project shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China, formulate emergency response plans for various types of emergencies, organize and train professional emergency rescue forces, and regularly conduct joint drills; the operation management unit of the Three Gorges Project shall conduct initial response to emergencies occurring within the security zone of the Three Gorges Project.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关责令改正，予以警告，对单位可以处5万元以下罚款，对个人可以处1000元以下罚款：  
(一)违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)的规定，非法运输危险物品进入陆域安全保卫区的；  
(二)违反[本条例第十四条](#art-14)的规定，扰乱陆域安全保卫区管理秩序或者危害设施安全的；  
(三)违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定，非法进入陆域安全保卫区的；  
(四)违反[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定，人员非法进入禁航区的；  
(五)违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)的规定，非法进行升放活动的。

**Article 35** Any of the following acts shall be ordered by the public security organ to make corrections and given a warning; a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual:  
(1) Violating the provisions of Article 13 of these Regulations, illegally transporting dangerous substances into the land security zone;  
(2) Violating the provisions of Article 14 of these Regulations, disrupting the management order of the land security zone or endangering the safety of facilities;  
(3) Violating the provisions of Article 15 of these Regulations, illegally entering the land security zone;  
(4) Violating the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations, illegally entering the prohibited navigation area by persons;  
(5) Violating the provisions of Article 23 of these Regulations, illegally conducting launching activities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有下列行为之一的，由交通运输部门责令改正，予以警告，对单位可以处10万元以下的罚款，对个人可以处2000元以下罚款：  
(一)违反[本条例第十七条](#art-17)、第十八条的规定，非法进入管制区、通航区，或者进入后不服从管理的；  
(二)违反[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)的规定，非法运输危险物品过闸的；  
(三)违反[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定，导致进入三峡枢纽安全保卫区的船舶存在安全隐患的。

**Article 36** Any of the following acts shall be ordered by the transportation department to make corrections and given a warning; a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual:  
(1) Violating the provisions of Articles 17 and 18 of these Regulations, illegally entering the control zone or navigation zone, or failing to comply with management after entry;  
(2) Violating the provisions of Article 21 of these Regulations, illegally transporting dangerous substances through the ship lock;  
(3) Violating the provisions of Article 32 of these Regulations, resulting in safety hazards for vessels entering the security zone of the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　负有三峡枢纽安全保卫职责的地方人民政府、部门和单位及其工作人员违反本条例规定，不依法履行三峡枢纽安全保卫职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 37** If a local people's government, department, or unit responsible for the security protection of the Three Gorges Project, or any of its staff members, violates the provisions of these Regulations and fails to perform the duties of security protection of the Three Gorges Project according to law, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，同时违反其他法律、行政法规规定的，由有关行政管理部门依照其他法律、行政法规的规定从重处罚。

**Article 38** If a violation of these Regulations also violates other laws or administrative regulations, the relevant administrative department shall impose a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of such other laws or administrative regulations.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　涉及三峡枢纽水库大坝安全、企业事业单位内部治安保卫、内河交通安全管理、电力设施保护等事项，本条例未作规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 39** Matters concerning the safety of the dam of the Three Gorges Project reservoir, internal public security protection of enterprises and institutions, inland river traffic safety management, and protection of electrical power facilities, which are not provided for in these Regulations, shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　葛洲坝水利枢纽的安全保卫规定，由湖北省人民政府参照本条例制定。

**Article 40** The security protection regulations for the Gezhouba Water Control Project shall be formulated by the People's Government of Hubei Province by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自2013年10月1日起施行。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force on October 1, 2013.
