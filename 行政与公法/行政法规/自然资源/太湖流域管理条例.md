# 太湖流域管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-09-07  
**生效日期**：2011-11-01

> (2011年8月24日国务院第169次常务会议通过　2011年9月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第604号公布　自2011年11月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强太湖流域水资源保护和水污染防治，保障防汛抗旱以及生活、生产和生态用水安全，改善太湖流域生态环境，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称太湖流域，包括江苏省、浙江省、上海市(以下称两省一市)长江以南，钱塘江以北，天目山、茅山流域分水岭以东的区域。 第三条　太湖流域管理应当遵循全面规划、统筹兼顾、保护优先、兴利除害、综合治理、科学发展的原则。 第四条　太湖流域实行流域管理与行政区域管理相结合的管理体制。 国家建立健全太湖流域管理协调机制，统筹协调太湖流域管理中的重大事项。 第五条　国务院水行政、环境保护等部门依照法律、行政法规规定和国务院确定的职责分工，负责太湖流域管理的有关工作。 国务院水行政主管部门设立的太湖流域管理机构(以下简称太湖流域管理机构)在管辖范围内，行使法律、行政法规规定的和国务院水行政主管部门授予的监督管理职责。 太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门依照法律、法规规定，负责本行政区域内有关的太湖流域管理工作。 第六条　国家对太湖流域水资源保护和水污染防治实行地方人民政府目标责任制与考核评价制度。 太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当将水资源保护、水污染防治、防汛抗旱、水域和岸线保护以及生活、生产和生态用水安全等纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，调整经济结构，优化产业布局，严格限制高耗水和高污染的建设项目。 第二章　饮用水安全 第七条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当合理确定饮用水水源地，并依照《中华人民共和国水法》、《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定划定饮用水水源保护区，保障饮用水供应和水质安全。 第八条　禁止在太湖流域饮用水水源保护区内设置排污口、有毒有害物品仓库以及垃圾场；已经设置的，当地县级人民政府应当责令拆除或者关闭。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强太湖流域水资源保护和水污染防治，保障防汛抗旱以及生活、生产和生态用水安全，改善太湖流域生态环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening water resources protection and water pollution prevention and control in the Taihu Lake Basin, ensuring flood control, drought relief, and the safety of domestic, productive, and ecological water use, and improving the ecological environment of the Taihu Lake Basin.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称太湖流域，包括江苏省、浙江省、上海市(以下称两省一市)长江以南，钱塘江以北，天目山、茅山流域分水岭以东的区域。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, the Taihu Lake Basin comprises the area south of the Yangtze River, north of the Qiantang River, and east of the watersheds of the Tianmu Mountain and Maoshan Mountain Basins within the territories of Jiangsu Province, Zhejiang Province, and Shanghai Municipality (hereinafter referred to as the two provinces and one municipality).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　太湖流域管理应当遵循全面规划、统筹兼顾、保护优先、兴利除害、综合治理、科学发展的原则。

**Article 3** The management of the Taihu Lake Basin shall follow the principles of comprehensive planning, overall consideration, giving priority to protection, promoting benefits and eliminating hazards, integrated management, and scientific development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　太湖流域实行流域管理与行政区域管理相结合的管理体制。  
国家建立健全太湖流域管理协调机制，统筹协调太湖流域管理中的重大事项。

**Article 4** The Taihu Lake Basin shall apply a management system combining basin management with administrative regional management.  
The State shall establish and improve a coordination mechanism for the management of the Taihu Lake Basin, to make overall plans and coordinate major issues in the management of the Taihu Lake Basin.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院水行政、环境保护等部门依照法律、行政法规规定和国务院确定的职责分工，负责太湖流域管理的有关工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门设立的太湖流域管理机构(以下简称太湖流域管理机构)在管辖范围内，行使法律、行政法规规定的和国务院水行政主管部门授予的监督管理职责。  
太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门依照法律、法规规定，负责本行政区域内有关的太湖流域管理工作。

**Article 5** The water administration, environmental protection, and other departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the division of duties determined by the State Council, be responsible for the relevant work of the management of the Taihu Lake Basin.  
The Taihu Lake Basin Authority (hereinafter referred to as the Taihu Lake Basin Authority), established by the competent water administration department of the State Council, shall, within its jurisdiction, exercise the supervisory and management functions provided for by laws and administrative regulations and as delegated by the competent water administration department of the State Council.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, in accordance with laws and regulations, be responsible for the relevant management work of the Taihu Lake Basin within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家对太湖流域水资源保护和水污染防治实行地方人民政府目标责任制与考核评价制度。  
太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当将水资源保护、水污染防治、防汛抗旱、水域和岸线保护以及生活、生产和生态用水安全等纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，调整经济结构，优化产业布局，严格限制高耗水和高污染的建设项目。

**Article 6** The State shall apply a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for local people's governments regarding water resources protection and water pollution prevention and control in the Taihu Lake Basin.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall incorporate water resources protection, water pollution prevention and control, flood control and drought relief, water area and shoreline protection, and the safety of domestic, productive, and ecological water use into their national economic and social development plans, adjust economic structure, optimize industrial layout, and strictly restrict construction projects with high water consumption and high pollution.

### 第二章　饮用水安全

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当合理确定饮用水水源地，并依照《中华人民共和国水法》、《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定划定饮用水水源保护区，保障饮用水供应和水质安全。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall rationally determine drinking water source areas, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution, delineate drinking water source protection zones, to ensure the supply and water quality safety of drinking water.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　禁止在太湖流域饮用水水源保护区内设置排污口、有毒有害物品仓库以及垃圾场；已经设置的，当地县级人民政府应当责令拆除或者关闭。

**Article 8** It shall be prohibited to set up sewage outlets, warehouses for toxic and hazardous substances, or garbage dumps within drinking water source protection zones in the Taihu Lake Basin. Where such facilities have already been set up, the local people's government at the county level shall order their removal or closure.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　太湖流域县级人民政府应当建立饮用水水源保护区日常巡查制度，并在饮用水水源一级保护区设置水质、水量自动监测设施。

**Article 9** People's governments at the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall establish a routine inspection system for drinking water source protection zones, and shall install automatic monitoring facilities for water quality and quantity in Class I drinking water source protection zones.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照水源互补、科学调度的原则，合理规划、建设应急备用水源和跨行政区域的联合供水项目。按照规划供水范围的正常用水量计算，应急备用水源应当具备不少于7天的供水能力。  
太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府供水主管部门应当根据生活饮用水国家标准的要求，编制供水设施技术改造规划，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, in accordance with the principles of source complementarity and scientific regulation, rationally plan and construct emergency backup water sources and joint water supply projects across administrative regions. Calculated based on the normal water consumption within the planned water supply scope, the emergency backup water source shall have a water supply capacity of not less than seven days.  
The competent water supply departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, in accordance with the requirements of national standards for drinking water, prepare technical renovation plans for water supply facilities, submit them to the people's governments at the same level for approval, and then organize their implementation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织水行政、环境保护、住房和城乡建设等部门制定本行政区域的供水安全应急预案。有关部门应当根据本行政区域的供水安全应急预案制定实施方案。  
太湖流域供水单位应当根据本行政区域的供水安全应急预案，制定相应的应急工作方案，并报供水主管部门备案。

**Article 11** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall organize the water administration, environmental protection, housing and urban-rural development, and other relevant departments to formulate emergency response plans for water supply safety within their respective administrative regions. Relevant departments shall formulate implementing plans based on the emergency response plans for water supply safety within their respective administrative regions.  
Water supply units within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, based on the emergency response plans for water supply safety within their respective administrative regions, formulate corresponding emergency work plans, and submit them to the competent water supply departments for recordation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　供水安全应急预案应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)应急备用水源和应急供水设施；  
(二)监测、预警、信息报告和处理；  
(三)组织指挥体系和应急响应机制；  
(四)应急备用水源启用方案或者应急调水方案；  
(五)资金、物资、技术等保障措施。

**Article 12** An emergency response plan for water supply safety shall include the following main contents:  
(1) Emergency backup water sources and emergency water supply facilities;  
(2) Monitoring, early warning, information reporting and processing;  
(3) Organizational command system and emergency response mechanism;  
(4) Plan for activation of emergency backup water sources or emergency water diversion plan;  
(5) Safeguard measures such as funds, materials, and technology.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　太湖流域市、县人民政府应当组织对饮用水水源、供水设施以及居民用水点的水质进行实时监测；在蓝藻暴发等特殊时段，应当增加监测次数和监测点，及时掌握水质状况。  
太湖流域市、县人民政府发现饮用水水源、供水设施以及居民用水点的水质异常，可能影响供水安全的，应当立即采取预防、控制措施，并及时向社会发布预警信息。

**Article 13** People's governments at the city and county levels within the Taihu Lake Basin shall organize real-time monitoring of water quality at drinking water sources, water supply facilities, and residential water consumption points. During special periods such as cyanobacteria blooms, they shall increase the frequency of monitoring and the number of monitoring points to timely grasp water quality conditions.  
If people's governments at the city and county levels within the Taihu Lake Basin find abnormal water quality at drinking water sources, water supply facilities, or residential water consumption points that may affect water supply safety, they shall immediately take preventive and control measures, and promptly issue early warning information to the public.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发生供水安全事故，太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当立即按照规定程序上报，并根据供水安全事故的严重程度和影响范围，按照职责权限启动相应的供水安全应急预案，优先保障居民生活饮用水。  
发生供水安全事故，需要实施跨流域或者跨省、直辖市行政区域水资源应急调度的，由太湖流域管理机构对太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河的水工程下达调度指令。  
防汛抗旱期间发生供水安全事故，需要实施水资源应急调度的，由太湖流域防汛抗旱指挥机构、太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构下达调度指令。

**Article 14** In the event of a water supply safety accident, local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall immediately report it in accordance with the prescribed procedures, and shall, based on the severity and scope of impact of the water supply safety accident, activate the corresponding emergency response plan for water supply safety in accordance with their functions and powers, giving priority to ensuring drinking water for residents.  
If, due to a water supply safety accident, an emergency water resources transfer across basins or across provincial or municipal administrative regions is necessary, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall issue transfer instructions to the water projects on Taihu Lake, Taipu River, Xinmeng River, and Wangyu River.  
If, during flood control or drought relief periods, a water supply safety accident occurs and an emergency water resources transfer is necessary, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the Taihu Lake Basin or of the local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall issue transfer instructions.

### 第三章　水资源保护

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　太湖流域水资源配置与调度，应当首先满足居民生活用水，兼顾生产、生态用水以及航运等需要，维持太湖合理水位，促进水体循环，提高太湖流域水环境容量。  
太湖流域水资源配置与调度，应当遵循统一实施、分级负责的原则，协调总量控制与水位控制的关系。

**Article 15** The allocation and regulation of water resources in the Taihu Lake Basin shall first satisfy the water use for residents' living, take into account the needs for production, ecological water use, and navigation, maintain a reasonable water level in Taihu Lake, promote water circulation, and enhance the water environment capacity of the Taihu Lake Basin.  
The allocation and regulation of water resources in the Taihu Lake Basin shall follow the principles of unified implementation and hierarchical responsibility, and coordinate the relationship between total quantity control and water level control.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　太湖流域管理机构应当商两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门，根据太湖流域综合规划制订水资源调度方案，报国务院水行政主管部门批准后组织两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门统一实施。  
水资源调度方案批准前，太湖流域水资源调度按照国务院水行政主管部门批准的引江济太调度方案以及有关年度调度计划执行。  
地方人民政府、太湖流域管理机构和水工程管理单位主要负责人应当对水资源调度方案和调度指令的执行负责。

**Article 16** The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, after consulting with the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, formulate a water resources regulation plan based on the comprehensive plan for the Taihu Lake Basin, submit it to the competent water administration department of the State Council for approval, and then organize the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality to implement it uniformly.  
Before the water resources regulation plan is approved, the water resources regulation in the Taihu Lake Basin shall be executed in accordance with the regulation plan for diverting water from the Yangtze River to Taihu Lake approved by the competent water administration department of the State Council and the relevant annual regulation plans.  
The principal responsible persons of local people's governments, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority, and water project management units shall be responsible for the implementation of water resources regulation plans and regulation instructions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　太浦河太浦闸、泵站，新孟河江边枢纽、运河立交枢纽，望虞河望亭、常熟水利枢纽，由太湖流域管理机构下达调度指令。  
国务院水行政主管部门规定的对流域水资源配置影响较大的水工程，由太湖流域管理机构商当地省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门下达调度指令。  
太湖流域其他水工程，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责权限下达调度指令。  
下达调度指令应当以水资源调度方案为基本依据，并综合考虑实时水情、雨情等情况。

**Article 17** Regulation instructions for the Taipu Gate and pumping station on the Taipu River, the Jiangbian Hub and Canal Overpass Hub on the Xinmeng River, and the Wangting and Changshu hydro-junctions on the Wangyu River shall be issued by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority.  
Regulation instructions for water projects specified by the competent water administration department of the State Council that have a significant impact on the allocation of water resources in the basin shall be issued by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority after consulting with the competent water administration departments of the local provincial or municipal people's governments.  
Regulation instructions for other water projects within the Taihu Lake Basin shall be issued by the competent water administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with their functions and powers.  
Regulation instructions shall be issued based on the water resources regulation plan, taking into account real-time hydrological and rainfall conditions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河实行取水总量控制制度。两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门应当于每年2月1日前将上一年度取水总量控制情况和本年度取水计划建议报太湖流域管理机构。太湖流域管理机构应当根据取水总量控制指标，结合年度预测来水量，于每年2月25日前向两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门下达年度取水计划。  
太湖流域管理机构应当对太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河取水总量控制情况进行实时监控。对取水总量已经达到或者超过取水总量控制指标的，不得批准建设项目新增取水。

**Article 18** A system of total water withdrawal control shall be applied to Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River. The competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, before February 1 of each year, report the total water withdrawal control situation of the previous year and the water withdrawal plan proposal for the current year to the Taihu Lake Basin Authority. The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, based on the total water withdrawal control targets and taking into account the forecasted annual water inflow, issue the annual water withdrawal plan to the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality before February 25 of each year.  
The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall conduct real-time monitoring of the total water withdrawal control for Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River. Where the total water withdrawal has reached or exceeded the total water withdrawal control target, no new water withdrawal for construction projects may be approved.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院环境保护等部门和两省一市人民政府，按照流域综合规划、水资源保护规划和经济社会发展要求，拟定太湖流域水功能区划，报国务院批准。  
太湖流域水功能区划未涉及的太湖流域其他水域的水功能区划，由两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级环境保护等部门拟定，征求太湖流域管理机构意见后，由本级人民政府批准并报国务院水行政、环境保护主管部门备案。  
调整经批准的水功能区划，应当经原批准机关或者其授权的机关批准。

**Article 19** The competent water administration department of the State Council shall, together with the environmental protection department of the State Council and other relevant departments and the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, formulate the water function zoning of the Taihu Lake Basin in accordance with the comprehensive basin plan, the water resources protection plan, and the requirements of economic and social development, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
For the water function zoning of other waters within the Taihu Lake Basin not covered by the water function zoning of the Taihu Lake Basin, the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, together with the environmental protection departments and other relevant departments at the same level, formulate such zoning, seek the opinions of the Taihu Lake Basin Authority, and then submit it to the people's governments at the same level for approval and to the competent water administration and environmental protection departments of the State Council for recordation.  
Any adjustment to an approved water function zoning shall be approved by the original approving authority or the authority authorized thereby.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　太湖流域的养殖、航运、旅游等涉及水资源开发利用的规划，应当遵守经批准的水功能区划。  
在太湖流域湖泊、河道从事生产建设和其他开发利用活动的，应当符合水功能区保护要求；其中在太湖从事生产建设和其他开发利用活动的，有关主管部门在办理批准手续前，应当就其是否符合水功能区保护要求征求太湖流域管理机构的意见。

**Article 20** Plans involving the development and utilization of water resources, such as those for aquaculture, shipping, and tourism in the Taihu Lake Basin, shall comply with the approved water function zoning.  
Any person engaging in production, construction, or other development and utilization activities in lakes or rivers within the Taihu Lake Basin shall meet the protection requirements of the water function zoning. Where such activities are carried out in Taihu Lake, the relevant competent department shall, before going through the approval formalities, seek the opinions of the Taihu Lake Basin Authority on whether they meet the protection requirements of the water function zoning.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和太湖流域管理机构应当加强对水功能区保护情况的监督检查，定期公布水资源状况；发现水功能区未达到水质目标的，应当及时报告有关人民政府采取治理措施，并向环境保护主管部门通报。  
主要入太湖河道控制断面未达到水质目标的，在不影响防洪安全的前提下，太湖流域管理机构应当通报有关地方人民政府关闭其入湖口门并组织治理。

**Article 21** The competent water administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin and the Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the protection of water function zones, and regularly publish the status of water resources. If they find that a water function zone fails to meet the water quality target, they shall promptly report to the relevant people's government to take remedial measures, and notify the competent environmental protection department.  
If a control section of a major river entering Taihu Lake fails to meet the water quality target, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, provided that flood control safety is not affected, notify the relevant local people's government to close the entrance gate of that river into the lake and organize remedial measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照太湖流域综合规划和太湖流域水环境综合治理总体方案等要求，组织采取环保型清淤措施，对太湖流域湖泊、河道进行生态疏浚，并对清理的淤泥进行无害化处理。

**Article 22** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, in accordance with the requirements of the comprehensive plan for the Taihu Lake Basin, the overall plan for comprehensive water environment management of the Taihu Lake Basin, and other requirements, organize the adoption of environmentally friendly dredging measures, carry out ecological dredging of lakes and rivers in the Taihu Lake Basin, and conduct harmless treatment of the dredged sludge.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强用水定额管理，采取有效措施，降低用水消耗，提高用水效率，并鼓励回用再生水和综合利用雨水、海水、微咸水。  
需要取水的新建、改建、扩建建设项目，应当在水资源论证报告书中按照行业用水定额要求明确节约用水措施，并配套建设节约用水设施。节约用水设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产。

**Article 23** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall strengthen the management of water use quotas, take effective measures to reduce water consumption and increase water use efficiency, and encourage the reuse of reclaimed water and the comprehensive utilization of rainwater, seawater, and brackish water.  
New construction, renovation, or expansion projects requiring water withdrawal shall specify water-saving measures in their water resources demonstration reports in accordance with the requirements of industry water use quotas, and shall construct water-saving facilities as supporting facilities. Water-saving facilities shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家将太湖流域承压地下水作为应急和战略储备水源，禁止任何单位和个人开采，但是供水安全事故应急用水除外。

**Article 24** The State shall designate confined groundwater in the Taihu Lake Basin as an emergency and strategic reserve water source, and shall prohibit any unit or individual from exploiting it, except for emergency water use in water supply safety accidents.

### 第四章　水污染防治

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　太湖流域实行重点水污染物排放总量控制制度。  
太湖流域管理机构应当组织两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门，根据水功能区对水质的要求和水体的自然净化能力，核定太湖流域湖泊、河道纳污能力，向两省一市人民政府环境保护主管部门提出限制排污总量意见。  
两省一市人民政府环境保护主管部门应当按照太湖流域水环境综合治理总体方案、太湖流域水污染防治规划等确定的水质目标和有关要求，充分考虑限制排污总量意见，制订重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划，经国务院环境保护主管部门审核同意，报两省一市人民政府批准并公告。  
两省一市人民政府应当将重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划确定的控制指标分解下达到太湖流域各市、县。市、县人民政府应当将控制指标分解落实到排污单位。

**Article 25** A system of total discharge control for key water pollutants shall be applied in the Taihu Lake Basin.  
The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall organize the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality to, based on the water quality requirements of the water function zones and the natural purification capacity of the water bodies, verify the pollutant receiving capacity of lakes and rivers in the Taihu Lake Basin, and submit opinions on the total discharge limits to the competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality.  
The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, in accordance with the water quality targets and relevant requirements set in the overall plan for comprehensive water environment management of the Taihu Lake Basin, the plan for water pollution prevention and control of the Taihu Lake Basin, and other documents, give full consideration to the opinions on total discharge limits, formulate plans for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants, submit them to the competent environmental protection department of the State Council for review and approval, and then submit them to the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality for approval and public announcement.  
The people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall break down and assign the control targets determined in the plans for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants to the cities and counties within the Taihu Lake Basin. The people's governments at the city and county levels shall allocate and assign the control targets to the pollutant discharging units.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　两省一市人民政府环境保护主管部门应当根据水污染防治工作需要，制订本行政区域其他水污染物排放总量控制指标，经国务院环境保护主管部门审核，报本级人民政府批准，并由两省一市人民政府抄送国务院环境保护、水行政主管部门。

**Article 26** The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, based on the needs of water pollution prevention and control, formulate total discharge control targets for other water pollutants in their respective administrative regions, submit them to the competent environmental protection department of the State Council for review, then submit them to the people's governments at the same level for approval, and the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall send copies thereof to the competent environmental protection and water administration departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院环境保护主管部门可以根据太湖流域水污染防治和优化产业结构、调整产业布局的需要，制定水污染物特别排放限值，并商两省一市人民政府确定和公布在太湖流域执行水污染物特别排放限值的具体地域范围和时限。

**Article 27** The competent environmental protection department of the State Council may, based on the needs of water pollution prevention and control in the Taihu Lake Basin and the optimization of industrial structure and adjustment of industrial layout, formulate special discharge limits for water pollutants, and, after consulting with the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, determine and publish the specific geographical scope and time limit for implementing special discharge limits for water pollutants in the Taihu Lake Basin.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　排污单位排放水污染物，不得超过经核定的水污染物排放总量，并应当按照规定设置便于检查、采样的规范化排污口，悬挂标志牌；不得私设暗管或者采取其他规避监管的方式排放水污染物。  
禁止在太湖流域设置不符合国家产业政策和水环境综合治理要求的造纸、制革、酒精、淀粉、冶金、酿造、印染、电镀等排放水污染物的生产项目，现有的生产项目不能实现达标排放的，应当依法关闭。  
在太湖流域新设企业应当符合国家规定的清洁生产要求，现有的企业尚未达到清洁生产要求的，应当按照清洁生产规划要求进行技术改造，两省一市人民政府应当加强监督检查。

**Article 28** Pollutant discharging units shall not discharge water pollutants in excess of the verified total discharge amount of water pollutants, and shall, in accordance with the provisions, set up standardized sewage outlets that facilitate inspection and sampling, and hang signboards; they shall not install concealed pipes or take other measures to evade supervision in discharging water pollutants.  
It shall be prohibited to set up in the Taihu Lake Basin production projects that discharge water pollutants, such as papermaking, leather tanning, alcohol, starch, metallurgy, brewing, printing and dyeing, and electroplating, which do not conform to the national industrial policies and the requirements of comprehensive water environment management. Existing production projects that cannot achieve discharge standards shall be closed according to law.  
Newly established enterprises in the Taihu Lake Basin shall meet the national requirements for cleaner production. Existing enterprises that have not yet met the cleaner production requirements shall carry out technical renovation in accordance with the requirements of the cleaner production plan, and the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall strengthen supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　新孟河、望虞河以外的其他主要入太湖河道，自河口1万米上溯至5万米河道岸线内及其岸线两侧各1000米范围内，禁止下列行为：  
(一)新建、扩建化工、医药生产项目；  
(二)新建、扩建污水集中处理设施排污口以外的排污口；  
(三)扩大水产养殖规模。

**Article 29** Within the riverine shoreline from 10,000 meters upstream to 50,000 meters upstream from the estuary of other major rivers entering Taihu Lake, other than the Xinmeng River and the Wangyu River, and within 1,000 meters on each side of such shoreline, the following acts shall be prohibited:  
(1) Constructing new or expanding chemical or pharmaceutical production projects;  
(2) Constructing new or expanding sewage outlets other than those of centralized sewage treatment facilities;  
(3) Expanding the scale of aquaculture.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　太湖岸线内和岸线周边5000米范围内，淀山湖岸线内和岸线周边2000米范围内，太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线内和岸线两侧各1000米范围内，其他主要入太湖河道自河口上溯至1万米河道岸线内及其岸线两侧各1000米范围内，禁止下列行为：  
(一)设置剧毒物质、危险化学品的贮存、输送设施和废物回收场、垃圾场；  
(二)设置水上餐饮经营设施；  
(三)新建、扩建高尔夫球场；  
(四)新建、扩建畜禽养殖场；  
(五)新建、扩建向水体排放污染物的建设项目；  
(六)[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)规定的行为。  
已经设置前款第一项、第二项规定设施的，当地县级人民政府应当责令拆除或者关闭。

**Article 30** Within the shoreline of Taihu Lake and within 5,000 meters around such shoreline, within the shoreline of Dianshan Lake and within 2,000 meters around such shoreline, within the shoreline of the Taipu River, Xinmeng River, and Wangyu River and within 1,000 meters on each side of such shoreline, and within the riverine shoreline from the estuary to 10,000 meters upstream of other major rivers entering Taihu Lake and within 1,000 meters on each side of such shoreline, the following acts shall be prohibited:  
(1) Setting up storage or transportation facilities for highly toxic substances or dangerous chemicals, and waste recycling yards or garbage dumps;  
(2) Setting up facilities for water catering operations;  
(3) Constructing new or expanding golf courses;  
(4) Constructing new or expanding livestock and poultry farms;  
(5) Constructing new or expanding construction projects that discharge pollutants into water bodies;  
(6) The acts specified in Article 29 of these Regulations.  
Where facilities specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph have already been set up, the local people's government at the county level shall order their removal or closure.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当推广测土配方施肥、精准施肥、生物防治病虫害等先进适用的农业生产技术，实施农药、化肥减施工程，减少化肥、农药使用量，发展绿色生态农业，开展清洁小流域建设，有效控制农业面源污染。

**Article 31** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall promote advanced and applicable agricultural production technologies such as soil testing and formulated fertilization, precision fertilization, and biological pest control, implement projects to reduce the application of pesticides and chemical fertilizers, reduce the usage of chemical fertilizers and pesticides, develop green ecological agriculture, carry out the construction of clean small watersheds, and effectively control agricultural non-point source pollution.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　两省一市人民政府应当加强对太湖流域水产养殖的管理，合理确定水产养殖规模和布局，推广循环水养殖、不投饵料养殖等生态养殖技术，减少水产养殖污染。  
国家逐步淘汰太湖围网养殖。江苏省、浙江省人民政府渔业行政主管部门应当按照统一规划、分步实施、合理补偿的原则，组织清理在太湖设置的围网养殖设施。

**Article 32** The people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall strengthen the management of aquaculture in the Taihu Lake Basin, rationally determine the scale and layout of aquaculture, promote ecological breeding technologies such as recirculating aquaculture and non-feeding aquaculture, and reduce pollution from aquaculture.  
The State shall gradually phase out net-cage aquaculture in Taihu Lake. The competent fishery administrative departments of the people's governments of Jiangsu Province and Zhejiang Province shall, in accordance with the principles of unified planning, phased implementation, and reasonable compensation, organize the clearance of net-cage aquaculture facilities set up in Taihu Lake.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　太湖流域的畜禽养殖场、养殖专业合作社、养殖小区应当对畜禽粪便、废水进行无害化处理，实现污水达标排放；达到两省一市人民政府规定规模的，应当配套建设沼气池、发酵池等畜禽粪便、废水综合利用或者无害化处理设施，并保证其正常运转。

**Article 33** Livestock and poultry farms, specialized farming cooperatives, and farming communities in the Taihu Lake Basin shall conduct harmless treatment of livestock and poultry manure and wastewater to achieve discharge of sewage up to standards. Where they reach the scale prescribed by the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, they shall construct supporting facilities such as biogas digesters and fermentation tanks for the comprehensive utilization or harmless treatment of livestock and poultry manure and wastewater, and ensure their normal operation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当合理规划建设公共污水管网和污水集中处理设施，实现雨水、污水分流。自本条例施行之日起5年内，太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府所在城镇和重点建制镇的生活污水应当全部纳入公共污水管网并经污水集中处理设施处理。  
太湖流域县级人民政府应当为本行政区域内的农村居民点配备污水、垃圾收集设施，并对收集的污水、垃圾进行集中处理。

**Article 34** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall rationally plan and construct public sewage networks and centralized sewage treatment facilities to achieve separation of rainwater and sewage. Within five years from the date these Regulations come into force, all domestic sewage from towns where local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin are located and from key organized towns shall be incorporated into the public sewage network and treated by centralized sewage treatment facilities.  
People's governments at the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall equip rural residential areas within their respective administrative regions with sewage and garbage collection facilities, and conduct centralized treatment of the collected sewage and garbage.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　太湖流域新建污水集中处理设施，应当符合脱氮除磷深度处理要求；现有的污水集中处理设施不符合脱氮除磷深度处理要求的，当地市、县人民政府应当自本条例施行之日起1年内组织进行技术改造。  
太湖流域市、县人民政府应当统筹规划建设污泥处理设施，并指导污水集中处理单位对处理污水产生的污泥等废弃物进行无害化处理，避免二次污染。  
国家鼓励污水集中处理单位配套建设再生水利用设施。

**Article 35** Newly constructed centralized sewage treatment facilities in the Taihu Lake Basin shall meet the requirements for advanced treatment of nitrogen and phosphorus removal. Where existing centralized sewage treatment facilities fail to meet the requirements for advanced treatment of nitrogen and phosphorus removal, the local people's governments at the city or county level shall organize technical renovation within one year from the date these Regulations come into force.  
People's governments at the city and county levels within the Taihu Lake Basin shall make overall plans for the construction of sludge treatment facilities, and guide centralized sewage treatment units to conduct harmless treatment of waste such as sludge generated from sewage treatment, so as to avoid secondary pollution.  
The State encourages centralized sewage treatment units to construct supporting facilities for reclaimed water utilization.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在太湖流域航行的船舶应当按照要求配备污水、废油、垃圾、粪便等污染物、废弃物收集设施。未持有合法有效的防止水域环境污染证书、文书的船舶，不得在太湖流域航行。运输剧毒物质、危险化学品的船舶，不得进入太湖。  
太湖流域各港口、码头、装卸站和船舶修造厂应当配备船舶污染物、废弃物接收设施和必要的水污染应急设施，并接受当地港口管理部门和环境保护主管部门的监督。  
太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府和有关海事管理机构应当建立健全船舶水污染事故应急制度，在船舶水污染事故发生后立即采取应急处置措施。

**Article 36** Vessels navigating in the Taihu Lake Basin shall, as required, be equipped with facilities for collecting pollutants and waste such as sewage, waste oil, garbage, and excrement. Vessels that do not hold valid certificates and documents for the prevention of environmental pollution of waters shall not navigate in the Taihu Lake Basin. Vessels transporting highly toxic substances or dangerous chemicals shall not enter Taihu Lake.  
All ports, wharves, loading and unloading stations, and ship repair yards in the Taihu Lake Basin shall be equipped with reception facilities for ship pollutants and waste and necessary water pollution emergency facilities, and shall be subject to supervision by the local port administration department and the competent environmental protection department.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin and the relevant maritime administration authorities shall establish and improve emergency response systems for ship water pollution accidents, and take emergency response measures immediately after the occurrence of a ship water pollution accident.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　太湖流域县级人民政府应当组建专业打捞队伍，负责当地重点水域蓝藻等有害藻类的打捞。打捞的蓝藻等有害藻类应当运送至指定的场所进行无害化处理。  
国家鼓励运用技术成熟、安全可靠的方法对蓝藻等有害藻类进行生态防治。

**Article 37** People's governments at the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall organize professional salvage teams to be responsible for salvaging harmful algae such as cyanobacteria in key local waters. The salvaged harmful algae such as cyanobacteria shall be transported to designated sites for harmless treatment.  
The State encourages the use of technically mature, safe, and reliable methods for ecological prevention and control of harmful algae such as cyanobacteria.

### 第五章　防汛抗旱与水域、岸线保护

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　太湖流域防汛抗旱指挥机构在国家防汛抗旱指挥机构的领导下，统一组织、指挥、指导、协调和监督太湖流域防汛抗旱工作，其具体工作由太湖流域管理机构承担。

**Article 38** The flood control and drought relief headquarters of the Taihu Lake Basin shall, under the leadership of the national flood control and drought relief headquarters, uniformly organize, command, direct, coordinate, and supervise the flood control and drought relief work in the Taihu Lake Basin. Its specific work shall be undertaken by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　太湖流域管理机构应当会同两省一市人民政府，制订太湖流域洪水调度方案，报国家防汛抗旱指挥机构批准。太湖流域洪水调度方案是太湖流域防汛调度的基本依据。  
太湖流域发生超标准洪水或者特大干旱灾害，由太湖流域防汛抗旱指挥机构组织两省一市人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构提出处理意见，报国家防汛抗旱指挥机构批准后执行。

**Article 39** The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, together with the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, formulate a flood regulation plan for the Taihu Lake Basin, and submit it to the national flood control and drought relief headquarters for approval. The flood regulation plan for the Taihu Lake Basin shall be the basic basis for flood regulation in the Taihu Lake Basin.  
Where a flood exceeding the design standard or an extraordinary drought disaster occurs in the Taihu Lake Basin, the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the Taihu Lake Basin shall organize the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality to propose handling opinions, and submit them to the national flood control and drought relief headquarters for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　太浦河太浦闸、泵站，新孟河江边枢纽、运河立交枢纽，望虞河望亭、常熟水利枢纽以及国家防汛抗旱指挥机构规定的对流域防汛抗旱影响较大的水工程的防汛抗旱调度指令，由太湖流域防汛抗旱指挥机构下达。  
太湖流域其他水工程的防汛抗旱调度指令，由太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构按照职责权限下达。

**Article 40** Flood control and drought relief regulation instructions for the Taipu Gate and pumping station on the Taipu River, the Jiangbian Hub and Canal Overpass Hub on the Xinmeng River, the Wangting and Changshu hydro-junctions on the Wangyu River, and the water projects specified by the national flood control and drought relief headquarters that have a significant impact on flood control and drought relief in the basin shall be issued by the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the Taihu Lake Basin.  
Flood control and drought relief regulation instructions for other water projects in the Taihu Lake Basin shall be issued by the flood control and drought relief headquarters of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin in accordance with their functions and powers.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　太湖水位以及与调度有关的其他水文测验数据，以国家基本水文测站的测验数据为准；未设立国家基本水文测站的，以太湖流域管理机构确认的水文测验数据为准。

**Article 41** The water level of Taihu Lake and other hydrological test data related to regulation shall be based on the test data from the national basic hydrological stations. Where no national basic hydrological station has been established, the data shall be based on the hydrological test data confirmed by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　太湖流域管理机构应当组织两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级交通运输主管部门，根据防汛抗旱和水域保护需要制订岸线利用管理规划，经征求两省一市人民政府国土资源、环境保护、城乡规划等部门意见，报国务院水行政主管部门审核并由其报国务院批准。岸线利用管理规划应当明确太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线划定、利用和管理等要求。  
太湖流域县级人民政府应当按照岸线利用管理规划，组织划定太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线，设置界标，并报太湖流域管理机构备案。

**Article 42** The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall organize the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, together with the competent transportation departments at the same level, to formulate shoreline utilization management plans based on the needs of flood control, drought relief, and water area protection. After soliciting opinions from the land and resources, environmental protection, urban and rural planning, and other relevant departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, such plans shall be submitted to the competent water administration department of the State Council for review and then to the State Council for approval. The shoreline utilization management plans shall specify the requirements for the delimitation, utilization, and management of the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River.  
People's governments at the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, in accordance with the shoreline utilization management plans, organize the delimitation of the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River, set up boundary markers, and report to the Taihu Lake Basin Authority for recordation.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线内兴建建设项目，应当符合太湖流域综合规划和岸线利用管理规划，不得缩小水域面积，不得降低行洪和调蓄能力，不得擅自改变水域、滩地使用性质；无法避免缩小水域面积、降低行洪和调蓄能力的，应当同时兴建等效替代工程或者采取其他功能补救措施。

**Article 43** Construction projects built within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River shall comply with the comprehensive plan for the Taihu Lake Basin and the shoreline utilization management plans, shall not reduce the water area, shall not diminish flood discharge and storage capacity, and shall not change the use nature of water areas or tidal flats without authorization. If it is unavoidable to reduce the water area or diminish flood discharge and storage capacity, equivalent alternative projects shall be constructed simultaneously or other functional remedial measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　需要临时占用太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线内水域、滩地的，应当经太湖流域管理机构同意，并依法办理有关手续。临时占用水域、滩地的期限不得超过2年。  
临时占用期限届满，临时占用人应当及时恢复水域、滩地原状；临时占用水域、滩地给当地居民生产等造成损失的，应当依法予以补偿。

**Article 44** Any person who needs to temporarily occupy water areas or tidal flats within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, and the Wangyu River shall obtain the consent of the Taihu Lake Basin Authority and go through the relevant procedures according to law. The period of temporary occupation of water areas or tidal flats shall not exceed two years.  
Upon the expiry of the temporary occupation period, the temporary occupant shall promptly restore the water areas or tidal flats to their original condition. If the temporary occupation of water areas or tidal flats causes losses to the production of local residents, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　太湖流域圩区建设、治理应当符合流域防洪要求，合理控制圩区标准，统筹安排圩区外排水河道规模，严格控制联圩并圩，禁止将湖荡等大面积水域圈入圩内，禁止缩小圩外水域面积。  
两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门应当编制圩区建设、治理方案，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河以及两省一市行政区域边界河道的圩区建设、治理方案在批准前，应当征得太湖流域管理机构同意。

**Article 45** The construction and management of polder areas in the Taihu Lake Basin shall meet the flood control requirements of the basin, reasonably control the standards of polder areas, make overall arrangements for the scale of drainage channels outside the polder areas, strictly control the merging of polders, prohibit enclosing large water areas such as lakes into polders, and prohibit reducing the water area outside the polders.  
The competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall prepare plans for the construction and management of polder areas, submit them to the people's governments at the same level for approval, and then organize their implementation. The plans for the construction and management of polder areas for Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, the Wangyu River, and the boundary rivers between the administrative regions of the two provinces and one municipality shall, before approval, obtain the consent of the Taihu Lake Basin Authority.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　禁止在太湖岸线内圈圩或者围湖造地；已经建成的圈圩不得加高、加宽圩堤，已经围湖所造的土地不得垫高土地地面。  
两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同同级国土资源等部门，自本条例施行之日起2年内编制太湖岸线内已经建成的圈圩和已经围湖所造土地清理工作方案，报国务院水行政主管部门和两省一市人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 46** It shall be prohibited to enclose polders within the shoreline of Taihu Lake or to reclaim land by enclosing the lake. For polders already constructed, the embankments shall not be heightened or widened; for land already reclaimed by enclosing the lake, the ground surface shall not be raised.  
The competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, together with the land and resources departments and other relevant departments at the same level, within two years from the date these Regulations come into force, prepare work plans for clearing the polders already constructed within the shoreline of Taihu Lake and the land already reclaimed by enclosing the lake, submit them to the competent water administration department of the State Council and the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality for approval, and then organize their implementation.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施保护和改善太湖生态环境，在太湖岸线周边500米范围内，饮用水水源保护区周边1500米范围内和主要入太湖河道岸线两侧各200米范围内，合理建设生态防护林。

**Article 47** Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin and their relevant departments shall take measures to protect and improve the ecological environment of Taihu Lake, and shall rationally construct ecological shelterbelts within 500 meters around the shoreline of Taihu Lake, within 1,500 meters around drinking water source protection zones, and within 200 meters on each side of the shorelines of major rivers entering Taihu Lake.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府林业、水行政、环境保护、农业等部门应当开展综合治理，保护湿地，促进生态恢复。  
两省一市人民政府渔业行政主管部门应当根据太湖流域水生生物资源状况、重要渔业资源繁殖规律和水产种质资源保护需要，开展水生生物资源增殖放流，实行禁渔区和禁渔期制度，并划定水产种质资源保护区。

**Article 48** The forestry, water administration, environmental protection, agriculture, and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall carry out comprehensive management, protect wetlands, and promote ecological restoration.  
The competent fishery administrative departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall, based on the status of aquatic biological resources in the Taihu Lake Basin, the breeding patterns of important fishery resources, and the needs for protecting aquatic germplasm resources, carry out augmentation and release of aquatic biological resources, implement a closed fishing area and closed fishing season system, and designate aquatic germplasm resource protection zones.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　上游地区未完成重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划、行政区域边界断面水质未达到阶段水质目标的，应当对下游地区予以补偿；上游地区完成重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划、行政区域边界断面水质达到阶段水质目标的，下游地区应当对上游地区予以补偿。补偿通过财政转移支付方式或者有关地方人民政府协商确定的其他方式支付。具体办法由国务院财政、环境保护主管部门会同两省一市人民政府制定。

**Article 49** Where an upstream area fails to complete the plan for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants, or the water quality at the boundary section of the administrative region fails to meet the phased water quality target, the upstream area shall provide compensation to the downstream area. Where the upstream area completes the plan for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants and the water quality at the boundary section of the administrative region meets the phased water quality target, the downstream area shall provide compensation to the upstream area. Compensation shall be paid through fiscal transfer payments or other methods determined through consultation by the relevant local people's governments. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent finance and environmental protection departments of the State Council together with the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　排放污水的单位和个人，应当按照规定缴纳污水处理费。通过公共供水设施供水的，污水处理费和水费一并收取；使用自备水源的，污水处理费和水资源费一并收取。污水处理费应当纳入地方财政预算管理，专项用于污水集中处理设施的建设和运行。污水处理费不能补偿污水集中处理单位正常运营成本的，当地县级人民政府应当给予适当补贴。

**Article 50** Units and individuals that discharge sewage shall pay sewage treatment fees in accordance with the provisions. Where water is supplied through public water supply facilities, sewage treatment fees and water charges shall be collected together. Where self-supplied water sources are used, sewage treatment fees and water resources fees shall be collected together. Sewage treatment fees shall be included in the local fiscal budget management, and shall be used exclusively for the construction and operation of centralized sewage treatment facilities. If sewage treatment fees cannot compensate for the normal operation costs of centralized sewage treatment units, the local people's government at the county level shall provide appropriate subsidies.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　对为减少水污染物排放自愿关闭、搬迁、转产以及进行技术改造的企业，两省一市人民政府应当通过财政、信贷、政府采购等措施予以鼓励和扶持。  
国家鼓励太湖流域排放水污染物的企业投保环境污染责任保险，具体办法由国务院环境保护主管部门会同国务院保险监督管理机构制定。

**Article 51** With respect to enterprises that voluntarily close, relocate, change their product, or carry out technical renovation for the purpose of reducing water pollutant discharge, the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall provide encouragement and support through fiscal, credit, government procurement, and other measures.  
The State encourages enterprises discharging water pollutants in the Taihu Lake Basin to take out environmental pollution liability insurance. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent environmental protection department of the State Council together with the insurance regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对因清理水产养殖、畜禽养殖，实施退田还湖、退渔还湖等导致转产转业的农民，当地县级人民政府应当给予补贴和扶持，并通过劳动技能培训、纳入社会保障体系等方式，保障其基本生活。  
对因实施农药、化肥减施工程等导致收入减少或者支出增加的农民，当地县级人民政府应当给予补贴。

**Article 52** With respect to farmers who are forced to change their production or occupation due to the clearance of aquaculture or livestock and poultry farming, or the implementation of returning farmland to lakes or returning fishing grounds to lakes, the local people's government at the county level shall provide subsidies and support, and shall ensure their basic livelihood through means such as labor skill training and inclusion in the social security system.  
With respect to farmers whose income is reduced or expenses are increased due to the implementation of projects to reduce pesticide and chemical fertilizer application, the local people's government at the county level shall provide subsidies.

### 第七章　监测与监督

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院发展改革、环境保护、水行政、住房和城乡建设等部门应当按照国务院有关规定，对两省一市人民政府水资源保护和水污染防治目标责任执行情况进行年度考核，并将考核结果报国务院。  
太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府应当对下一级人民政府水资源保护和水污染防治目标责任执行情况进行年度考核。

**Article 53** The National Development and Reform Commission, the competent environmental protection department, the competent water administration department, the competent housing and urban-rural construction department, and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council, conduct an annual assessment of the implementation of the target responsibilities for water resources protection and water pollution prevention and control by the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, and report the assessment results to the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall conduct an annual assessment of the implementation of the target responsibilities for water resources protection and water pollution prevention and control by the people's governments at the next lower level.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家按照统一规划布局、统一标准方法、统一信息发布的要求，建立太湖流域监测体系和信息共享机制。  
太湖流域管理机构应当商两省一市人民政府环境保护、水行政主管部门和气象主管机构等，建立统一的太湖流域监测信息共享平台。  
两省一市人民政府环境保护主管部门负责本行政区域的水环境质量监测和污染源监督性监测。太湖流域管理机构和两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门负责水文水资源监测；太湖流域管理机构负责两省一市行政区域边界水域和主要入太湖河道控制断面的水环境质量监测，以及太湖流域重点水功能区和引江济太调水的水质监测。  
太湖流域水环境质量信息由两省一市人民政府环境保护主管部门按照职责权限发布。太湖流域水文水资源信息由太湖流域管理机构会同两省一市人民政府水行政主管部门统一发布；发布水文水资源信息涉及水环境质量的内容，应当与环境保护主管部门协商一致。太湖流域年度监测报告由国务院环境保护、水行政主管部门共同发布，必要时也可以授权太湖流域管理机构发布。

**Article 54** The State shall, in accordance with the requirements of unified planning and layout, unified standards and methods, and unified information release, establish a monitoring system and an information sharing mechanism for the Taihu Lake Basin.  
The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, after consulting with the competent environmental protection and water administration departments and the meteorological authorities of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality, establish a unified monitoring information sharing platform for the Taihu Lake Basin.  
The competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall be responsible for water environment quality monitoring and supervision-oriented monitoring of pollution sources within their respective administrative regions. The Taihu Lake Basin Authority and the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality shall be responsible for hydrology and water resources monitoring. The Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall be responsible for water environment quality monitoring in the boundary waters between the administrative regions of the two provinces and one municipality and at the control sections of major rivers entering Taihu Lake, as well as water quality monitoring in key water function zones in the Taihu Lake Basin and for the water diversion from the Yangtze River to Taihu Lake.  
Information on water environment quality in the Taihu Lake Basin shall be released by the competent environmental protection departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality in accordance with their functions and powers. Information on hydrology and water resources in the Taihu Lake Basin shall be released uniformly by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority together with the competent water administration departments of the people's governments of the two provinces and one municipality. Where the release of hydrology and water resources information involves content related to water environment quality, it shall be coordinated and agreed with the competent environmental protection department. The annual monitoring report on the Taihu Lake Basin shall be jointly released by the competent environmental protection and water administration departments of the State Council, and may, when necessary, be released by the Taihu Lake Basin Authority upon authorization.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　有下列情形之一的，有关部门应当暂停办理两省一市相关行政区域或者主要入太湖河道沿线区域可能产生污染的建设项目的审批、核准以及环境影响评价、取水许可和排污口设置审查等手续，并通报有关地方人民政府采取治理措施：  
(一)未完成重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划，行政区域边界断面、主要入太湖河道控制断面未达到阶段水质目标的；  
(二)未完成本条例规定的违法设施拆除、关闭任务的；  
(三)因违法批准新建、扩建污染水环境的生产项目造成供水安全事故等严重后果的。

**Article 55** Under any of the following circumstances, the relevant departments shall suspend the handling of procedures such as the approval, verification, environmental impact assessment, water withdrawal permit, and examination of sewage outlet setting for construction projects that may cause pollution in the relevant administrative regions of the two provinces and one municipality or in the areas along the major rivers entering Taihu Lake, and shall notify the relevant local people's governments to take remedial measures:  
(1) The plan for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants has not been completed, and the water quality at the boundary section of the administrative region or at the control section of a major river entering Taihu Lake fails to meet the phased water quality target;  
(2) The task of removing or closing illegal facilities as required by these Regulations has not been completed;  
(3) Serious consequences such as water supply safety accidents have occurred due to the illegal approval of new construction or expansion of production projects that pollute the water environment.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　太湖流域管理机构和太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当对设置在太湖流域湖泊、河道的排污口进行核查登记，建立监督管理档案，对污染严重和违法设置的排污口，依照《中华人民共和国水法》、《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定处理。

**Article 56** The Taihu Lake Basin Authority and the competent water administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall check and register the sewage outlets set up in lakes and rivers within the Taihu Lake Basin, establish supervision and management files, and deal with sewage outlets that cause serious pollution or are illegally set up in accordance with the provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门应当会同有关部门，加强对重点水污染物排放总量削减和控制计划落实情况的监督检查，并按照职责权限定期向社会公布。  
国务院环境保护主管部门应当定期开展太湖流域水污染调查和评估。

**Article 57** The competent environmental protection departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall, together with relevant departments, strengthen supervision and inspection of the implementation of plans for total discharge reduction and control of key water pollutants, and shall regularly make public the relevant information in accordance with their functions and powers.  
The competent environmental protection department of the State Council shall regularly carry out water pollution investigations and assessments in the Taihu Lake Basin.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政、环境保护、渔业、交通运输、住房和城乡建设等部门和太湖流域管理机构，应当依照本条例和相关法律、法规的规定，加强对太湖开发、利用、保护、治理的监督检查，发现违法行为，应当通报有关部门进行查处，必要时可以直接通报有关地方人民政府进行查处。

**Article 58** The water administration, environmental protection, fishery, transportation, housing and urban-rural construction, and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin and the Taihu Lake Basin Authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and relevant laws and regulations, strengthen supervision and inspection of the development, utilization, protection, and management of Taihu Lake. If they discover any illegal act, they shall notify the relevant department to investigate and deal with it, and may, when necessary, directly notify the relevant local people's government to investigate and deal with it.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不履行供水安全监测、报告、预警职责，或者发生供水安全事故后不及时采取应急措施的；  
(二)不履行水污染物排放总量削减、控制职责，或者不依法责令拆除、关闭违法设施的；  
(三)不履行本条例规定的其他职责的。

**Article 59** Where a local people's government at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin or its staff member violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of monitoring, reporting, or early warning of water supply safety, or failing to take emergency measures in a timely manner after the occurrence of a water supply safety accident;  
(2) Failing to perform the duties of reducing and controlling total discharge of water pollutants, or failing to order the removal or closure of illegal facilities according to law;  
(3) Failing to perform other duties provided for in these Regulations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府水行政、环境保护、住房和城乡建设等部门及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府责令改正，通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不组织实施供水设施技术改造的；  
(二)不执行取水总量控制制度的；  
(三)不履行监测职责或者发布虚假监测信息的；  
(四)不组织清理太湖岸线内的圈圩、围湖造地和太湖围网养殖设施的；  
(五)不履行本条例规定的其他职责的。

**Article 60** Where the water administration, environmental protection, housing and urban-rural construction, and other relevant departments of a people's government at or above the county level or their staff members violate the provisions of these Regulations and commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level shall order correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give sanctions to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to organize the implementation of technical renovation of water supply facilities;  
(2) Failing to implement the system of total water withdrawal control;  
(3) Failing to perform monitoring duties or releasing false monitoring information;  
(4) Failing to organize the clearance of polders within the shoreline of Taihu Lake, land reclamation by enclosing the lake, and net-cage aquaculture facilities in Taihu Lake;  
(5) Failing to perform other duties provided for in these Regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　太湖流域管理机构及其工作人员违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由国务院水行政主管部门责令改正，通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不履行水资源调度职责的；  
(二)不履行水功能区、排污口管理职责的；  
(三)不组织制订水资源调度方案、岸线利用管理规划的；  
(四)不履行监测职责的；  
(五)不履行本条例规定的其他职责的。

**Article 61** Where the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or its staff members violate the provisions of these Regulations and commit any of the following acts, the competent water administration department of the State Council shall order correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give sanctions to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of water resources regulation;  
(2) Failing to perform the duties of managing water function zones and sewage outlets;  
(3) Failing to organize the formulation of water resources regulation plans or shoreline utilization management plans;  
(4) Failing to perform monitoring duties;  
(5) Failing to perform other duties provided for in these Regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　太湖流域水工程管理单位违反本条例规定，拒不服从调度的，由太湖流域管理机构或者水行政主管部门按照职责权限责令改正，通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where a water project management unit in the Taihu Lake Basin violates the provisions of these Regulations and refuses to comply with regulation, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or the competent water administration department shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, order correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give sanctions to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　排污单位违反本条例规定，排放水污染物超过经核定的水污染物排放总量，或者在已经确定执行太湖流域水污染物特别排放限值的地域范围、时限内排放水污染物超过水污染物特别排放限值的，依照[《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》第七十四条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国水污染防治法.md#art-74)的规定处罚。

**Article 63** Where a pollutant discharging unit violates the provisions of these Regulations by discharging water pollutants in excess of the verified total discharge amount of water pollutants, or by discharging water pollutants in excess of the special discharge limits for water pollutants within the geographical scope and time limit that have been determined for implementing the special discharge limits for water pollutants in the Taihu Lake Basin, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 74 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例规定，在太湖、淀山湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河和其他主要入太湖河道岸线内以及岸线周边、两侧保护范围内新建、扩建化工、医药生产项目，或者设置剧毒物质、危险化学品的贮存、输送设施，或者设置废物回收场、垃圾场、水上餐饮经营设施的，由太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门责令改正，处20万元以上50万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，由太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门依法强制执行，所需费用由违法行为人承担；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，在太湖、淀山湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河和其他主要入太湖河道岸线内以及岸线周边、两侧保护范围内新建、扩建高尔夫球场的，由太湖流域县级以上地方人民政府责令停止建设或者关闭。

**Article 64** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, constructs new or expands chemical or pharmaceutical production projects, or sets up storage or transportation facilities for highly toxic substances or dangerous chemicals, or sets up waste recycling yards, garbage dumps, or facilities for water catering operations within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, Dianshan Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, the Wangyu River, or other major rivers entering Taihu Lake, or within the protected areas around or on both sides of such shorelines, the competent environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the person refuses to correct, the competent environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall enforce the decision according to law, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, constructs new or expands golf courses within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, Dianshan Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, the Wangyu River, or other major rivers entering Taihu Lake, or within the protected areas around or on both sides of such shorelines, the local people's government at or above the county level within the Taihu Lake Basin shall order the cessation of construction or closure.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例规定，运输剧毒物质、危险化学品的船舶进入太湖的，由交通运输主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where a vessel transporting highly toxic substances or dangerous chemicals enters Taihu Lake in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent transportation department shall order correction, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and, if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the vessel refuses to correct, the department shall order suspension of production and business for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例规定，在太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线内兴建不符合岸线利用管理规划的建设项目，或者不依法兴建等效替代工程、采取其他功能补救措施的，由太湖流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责权限责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，由太湖流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责权限依法强制执行，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 66** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, constructs a construction project within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, or the Wangyu River that does not conform to the shoreline utilization management plan, or fails to construct an equivalent alternative project or take other functional remedial measures according to law, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or the competent water administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the person refuses to correct, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or the competent water administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, enforce the decision according to law, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由太湖流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责权限责令改正，对单位处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，对个人处1万元以上3万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，由太湖流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责权限依法强制执行，所需费用由违法行为人承担：  
(一)擅自占用太湖、太浦河、新孟河、望虞河岸线内水域、滩地或者临时占用期满不及时恢复原状的；  
(二)在太湖岸线内圈圩，加高、加宽已经建成圈圩的圩堤，或者垫高已经围湖所造土地地面的；  
(三)在太湖从事不符合水功能区保护要求的开发利用活动的。  
违反本条例规定，在太湖岸线内围湖造地的，依照[《中华人民共和国水法》第六十六条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国水法.md#art-66)的规定处罚。

**Article 67** Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or the competent water administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on a unit, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan on an individual; if the person refuses to correct, the Taihu Lake Basin Authority or the competent water administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and powers, enforce the decision according to law, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator:  
(1) Unauthorized occupation of water areas or tidal flats within the shorelines of Taihu Lake, the Taipu River, the Xinmeng River, or the Wangyu River, or failure to restore them to their original condition in a timely manner upon expiry of the temporary occupation period;  
(2) Enclosing polders within the shoreline of Taihu Lake, heightening or widening the embankments of polders already constructed, or raising the ground surface of land already reclaimed by enclosing the lake;  
(3) Engaging in development and utilization activities in Taihu Lake that do not meet the protection requirements of the water function zoning.  
Where any person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, reclaims land by enclosing the lake within the shoreline of Taihu Lake, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 66 of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例所称主要入太湖河道控制断面，包括望虞河、大溪港、梁溪河、直湖港、武进港、太滆运河、漕桥河、殷村港、社渎港、官渎港、洪巷港、陈东港、大浦港、乌溪港、大港河、夹浦港、合溪新港、长兴港、杨家浦港、旄儿港、苕溪、大钱港的入太湖控制断面。

**Article 68** For the purpose of these Regulations, the control sections of major rivers entering Taihu Lake include the control sections of the Wangyu River, Daxi Port, Liangxi River, Zhihu Port, Wujin Port, Taige Canal, Caoqiao River, Yincun Port, Shedu Port, Guandu Port, Hongxiang Port, Chendong Port, Dapu Port, Wuxi Port, Dagang River, Jiapu Port, Hexin New Port, Changxing Port, Yangjiapu Port, Mao'er Port, Tiaoxi, and Daqian Port entering Taihu Lake.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　两省一市可以根据水环境综合治理需要，制定严于国家规定的产业准入条件和水污染防治标准。

**Article 69** The two provinces and one municipality may, based on the needs of comprehensive water environment management, formulate industrial access conditions and water pollution prevention and control standards that are stricter than those prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本条例自2011年11月1日起施行。

**Article 70** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2011.
