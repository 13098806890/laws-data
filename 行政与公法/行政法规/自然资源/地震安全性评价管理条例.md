# 地震安全性评价管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对地震安全性评价的管理，防御与减轻地震灾害，保护人民生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protecting Against and Mitigating Earthquake Disasters for the purpose of strengthening the administration of seismic safety evaluation, defending against and mitigating earthquake disasters, and protecting the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事地震安全性评价活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any entity that engages in seismic safety evaluation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，依照《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》和本条例的规定，需要进行地震安全性评价的，必须严格执行国家地震安全性评价的技术规范，确保地震安全性评价的质量。

**Article 3** For a construction project that is newly built, expanded or reconstructed and is subject to seismic safety evaluation in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protecting Against and Mitigating Earthquake Disasters and these Regulations, the technical specifications of the state for seismic safety evaluation must be strictly complied with to ensure the quality of seismic safety evaluation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院地震工作主管部门负责全国的地震安全性评价的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构负责本行政区域内的地震安全性评价的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of seismic safety evaluation nationwide.  
The department or institution responsible for earthquake affairs under the people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of seismic safety evaluation within its respective administrative region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励、扶持有关地震安全性评价的科技研究，推广应用先进的科技成果，提高地震安全性评价的科技水平。

**Article 5** The state shall encourage and support scientific and technological research related to seismic safety evaluation, promote and apply advanced scientific and technological achievements, and improve the scientific and technological level of seismic safety evaluation.

### 第二章　地震安全性评价单位

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事地震安全性评价的单位应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与从事地震安全性评价相适应的地震学、地震地质学、工程地震学方面的专业技术人员；  
(二)有从事地震安全性评价的技术条件。

**Article 6** A unit engaged in seismic safety evaluation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having professional and technical personnel in seismology, seismic geology, and engineering seismology commensurate with the seismic safety evaluation work;  
(2) having the technical conditions for seismic safety evaluation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　禁止地震安全性评价单位以其他地震安全性评价单位的名义承揽地震安全性评价业务。禁止地震安全性评价单位允许其他单位以本单位的名义承揽地震安全性评价业务。

**Article 7** It is prohibited for a seismic safety evaluation unit to undertake seismic safety evaluation business in the name of another seismic safety evaluation unit. It is also prohibited for a seismic safety evaluation unit to permit any other unit to undertake seismic safety evaluation business in its own name.

### 第三章　地震安全性评价的范围和要求

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下列建设工程必须进行地震安全性评价：  
(一)国家重大建设工程；  
(二)受地震破坏后可能引发水灾、火灾、爆炸、剧毒或者强腐蚀性物质大量泄露或者其他严重次生灾害的建设工程，包括水库大坝、堤防和贮油、贮气、贮存易燃易爆、剧毒或者强腐蚀性物质的设施以及其他可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程；  
(三)受地震破坏后可能引发放射性污染的核电站和核设施建设工程；  
(四)省、自治区、直辖市认为对本行政区域有重大价值或者有重大影响的其他建设工程。

**Article 8** The following construction projects must undergo seismic safety evaluation:  
(1) major state construction projects;  
(2) construction projects that, if damaged by an earthquake, may cause floods, fires, explosions, massive leakage of highly toxic or highly corrosive substances, or other serious secondary disasters, including reservoir dams, dikes, facilities for storing oil, gas, inflammable, explosive, highly toxic or highly corrosive substances, and other construction projects that may cause serious secondary disasters;  
(3) construction projects of nuclear power plants and nuclear facilities that, if damaged by an earthquake, may cause radioactive contamination;  
(4) other construction projects deemed by a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to have significant value or significant impact on its administrative region.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地震安全性评价单位对建设工程进行地震安全性评价后，应当编制该建设工程的地震安全性评价报告。  
地震安全性评价报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)工程概况和地震安全性评价的技术要求；  
(二)地震活动环境评价；  
(三)地震地质构造评价；  
(四)设防烈度或者设计地震动参数；  
(五)地震地质灾害评价；  
(六)其他有关技术资料。

**Article 9** After performing seismic safety evaluation on a construction project, the seismic safety evaluation unit shall prepare a seismic safety evaluation report for the construction project.  
The seismic safety evaluation report shall include the following contents:  
(1) project overview and technical requirements for seismic safety evaluation;  
(2) evaluation of the seismic activity environment;  
(3) evaluation of the seismogeological structure;  
(4) fortification intensity or design ground motion parameters;  
(5) evaluation of seismic geological disasters;  
(6) other relevant technical data.

### 第四章　地震安全性评价报告的审定

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院地震工作主管部门负责下列地震安全性评价报告的审定：  
(一)国家重大建设工程；  
(二)跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域的建设工程；  
(三)核电站和核设施建设工程。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构负责除前款规定以外的建设工程地震安全性评价报告的审定。

**Article 10** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the examination and approval of the seismic safety evaluation reports for the following construction projects:  
(1) major state construction projects;  
(2) construction projects spanning the administrative regions of two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) construction projects of nuclear power plants and nuclear facilities.  
The department or institution responsible for earthquake affairs under the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the examination and approval of the seismic safety evaluation reports for construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院地震工作主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当自收到地震安全性评价报告之日起15日内进行审定，确定建设工程的抗震设防要求。

**Article 11** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the earthquake safety evaluation report, conduct examination and verification, and determine the requirements for seismic fortification of construction projects.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院地震工作主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，在确定建设工程抗震设防要求后，应当以书面形式通知建设单位，并告知建设工程所在地的市、县人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构应当将其确定的建设工程抗震设防要求报国务院地震工作主管部门备案。

**Article 12** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, after determining the requirements for seismic fortification of construction projects, notify the construction entity in writing, and inform the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of the city or county where the construction project is located.  
The departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall submit the determined requirements for seismic fortification of construction projects to the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council for recordation.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府负责项目审批的部门，应当将抗震设防要求纳入建设工程可行性研究报告的审查内容。对可行性研究报告中未包含抗震设防要求的项目，不予批准。

**Article 13** The departments responsible for project approval under the people's governments at or above the county level shall include the requirements for seismic fortification in the review contents of the feasibility study report of construction projects. Projects whose feasibility study reports do not contain the requirements for seismic fortification shall not be approved.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院建设行政主管部门和国务院铁路、交通、民用航空、水利和其他有关专业主管部门制定的抗震设计规范，应当明确规定按照抗震设防要求进行抗震设计的方法和措施。

**Article 14** The seismic design codes formulated by the competent administrative department of construction under the State Council and the competent departments of railway, transportation, civil aviation, water conservancy, and other relevant specialized departments under the State Council shall clearly specify the methods and measures for seismic design in accordance with the requirements for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　建设工程设计单位应当按照抗震设防要求和抗震设计规范，进行抗震设计。

**Article 15** The design entities of construction projects shall conduct seismic design in accordance with the requirements for seismic fortification and the seismic design codes.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院地震工作主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构，应当会同有关专业主管部门，加强对地震安全性评价工作的监督检查。

**Article 16** The competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council and the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the relevant specialized departments, strengthen the supervision and inspection of earthquake safety evaluation work.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反本条例的规定，地震安全性评价单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构依据职权，责令改正，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)以其他地震安全性评价单位的名义承揽地震安全性评价业务的；  
(二)允许其他单位以本单位名义承揽地震安全性评价业务的。

**Article 17** Where a seismic safety evaluation entity violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, pursuant to their authority, order rectification, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) soliciting seismic safety evaluation business in the name of another seismic safety evaluation entity;  
(2) permitting another entity to solicit seismic safety evaluation business in its own name.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本条例的规定，国务院地震工作主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构不履行审定地震安全性评价报告职责，国务院地震工作主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府负责管理地震工作的部门或者机构不履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依法追究有关责任人的刑事责任；没有造成严重后果，尚不构成犯罪的，对部门或者机构负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职的处分。

**Article 18** Where the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fail to perform their duties of examining and verifying seismic safety evaluation reports, or the competent department of earthquake affairs under the State Council or the departments or agencies responsible for earthquake administration under the local people's governments at or above the county level fail to perform their supervision and administration duties, or fail to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered, thereby causing heavy losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people, criminal liability of the persons responsible shall be investigated according to law; if no serious consequences are caused and the act does not constitute a crime, persons in charge who are liable and other directly responsible persons of the department or agency shall be given demotion or dismissal from posts.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 19** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
