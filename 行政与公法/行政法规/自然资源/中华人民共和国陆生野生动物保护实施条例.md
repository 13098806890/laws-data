### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国野生动物保护法》(以下简称《野生动物保护法》)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Wildlife Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Wildlife Protection Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称陆生野生动物，是指依法受保护的珍贵、濒危、有益的和有重要经济、科学研究价值的陆生野生动物(以下简称野生动物)；所称野生动物产品，是指陆生野生动物的任何部分及其衍生物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, terrestrial wildlife refers to precious, endangered, beneficial and terrestrial wildlife of important economic or scientific research value that are protected according to law (hereinafter referred to as wildlife); wildlife products refer to any part of terrestrial wildlife and derivatives thereof.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院林业行政主管部门主管全国陆生野生动物管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门主管本行政区域内陆生野生动物管理工作。自治州、县和市人民政府陆生野生动物管理工作的行政主管部门，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 3** The administrative department for forestry under the State Council shall be in charge of the administration of terrestrial wildlife nationwide.  
The administrative department for forestry of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be in charge of the administration of terrestrial wildlife within its administrative region. The competent administrative department for the administration of terrestrial wildlife of an autonomous prefecture, county, or city shall be determined by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上各级人民政府有关主管部门应当鼓励、支持有关科研、教学单位开展野生动物科学研究工作。

**Article 4** The relevant competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall encourage and support relevant scientific research and educational institutions in conducting scientific research on wildlife.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　野生动物行政主管部门有权对《野生动物保护法》和本条例的实施情况进行监督检查，被检查的单位和个人应当给予配合。

**Article 5** The competent administrative department for wildlife shall have the right to supervise and inspect the implementation of the Wildlife Protection Law and these Regulations, and the entities and individuals being inspected shall cooperate.

### 第二章　野生动物保护

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当开展保护野生动物的宣传教育，可以确定适当时间为保护野生动物宣传月、爱鸟周等，提高公民保护野生动物的意识。

**Article 6** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out publicity and education on wildlife protection, and may designate appropriate periods as Wildlife Protection Publicity Month, Bird Week, etc., to enhance citizens' awareness of wildlife protection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院林业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门，应当定期组织野生动物资源调查，建立资源档案，为制定野生动物资源保护发展方案、制定和调整国家和地方重点保护野生动物名录提供依据。  
野生动物资源普查每十年进行一次。

**Article 7** The administrative department for forestry under the State Council and the administrative departments for forestry of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regularly organize surveys of wildlife resources, establish resource archives, and provide a basis for formulating wildlife resource protection and development plans and for formulating and adjusting the lists of key protected wildlife at the national and local levels.  
A general survey of wildlife resources shall be conducted once every ten years.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上各级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门，应当组织社会各方面力量，采取生物技术措施和工程技术措施，维护和改善野生动物生存环境，保护和发展野生动物资源。  
禁止任何单位和个人破坏国家和地方重点保护野生动物的生息繁衍场所和生存条件。

**Article 8** The competent administrative departments for wildlife of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize social forces from all aspects, adopt biological and engineering measures, maintain and improve the living environment of wildlife, and protect and develop wildlife resources.  
No entity or individual shall destroy the habitats and living conditions of key protected wildlife at the national and local levels.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人发现受伤、病弱、饥饿、受困、迷途的国家和地方重点保护野生动物时，应当及时报告当地野生动物行政主管部门，由其采取救护措施；也可以就近送具备救护条件的单位救护。救护单位应当立即报告野生动物行政主管部门，并按照国务院林业行政主管部门的规定办理。

**Article 9** Any entity or individual that finds injured, sick, weak, hungry, stranded, or lost key protected wildlife at the national or local level shall promptly report to the local competent administrative department for wildlife, which shall take rescue measures; it may also be sent to a nearby entity with rescue capabilities for rescue. The rescue entity shall immediately report to the competent administrative department for wildlife and handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department for forestry under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有关单位和个人对国家和地方重点保护野生动物可能造成的危害，应当采取防范措施。因保护国家和地方重点保护野生动物受到损失的，可以向当地人民政府野生动物行政主管部门提出补偿要求。经调查属实并确实需要补偿的，由当地人民政府按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 10** Relevant entities and individuals shall take precautionary measures against possible harm caused by key protected wildlife at the national and local levels. If losses are incurred due to the protection of key protected wildlife at the national and local levels, they may submit a claim for compensation to the competent administrative department for wildlife of the local people's government. If the claim is verified as true and compensation is indeed necessary, the local people's government shall provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　野生动物猎捕管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　禁止猎捕、杀害国家重点保护野生动物。  
有下列情形之一，需要猎捕国家重点保护野生动物的，必须申请特许猎捕证：  
(一)为进行野生动物科学考察、资源调查，必须猎捕的；  
(二)为驯养繁殖国家重点保护野生动物，必须从野外获取种源的；  
(三)为承担省级以上科学研究项目或者国家医药生产任务，必须从野外获取国家重点保护野生动物的；  
(四)为宣传、普及野生动物知识或者教学、展览的需要，必须从野外获取国家重点保护野生动物的；  
(五)因国事活动的需要，必须从野外获取国家重点保护野生动物的；  
(六)为调控国家重点保护野生动物种群数量和结构，经科学论证必须猎捕的；  
(七)因其他特殊情况，必须捕捉、猎捕国家重点保护野生动物的。

**Article 11** It is prohibited to hunt, capture, or kill wildlife under special state protection.  
Where there is any of the following circumstances requiring the hunting or capturing of wildlife under special state protection, a special hunting and capturing permit must be applied for:  
(1) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for conducting scientific surveys or resource investigations of wildlife;  
(2) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for obtaining wild sources for domestication and breeding of wildlife under special state protection;  
(3) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for undertaking scientific research projects at or above the provincial level or national pharmaceutical production tasks;  
(4) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for promoting and popularizing knowledge of wildlife or for teaching or exhibition purposes;  
(5) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for the needs of state affairs;  
(6) when such hunting or capturing is necessary for regulating the population quantity and structure of wildlife under special state protection, as demonstrated by scientific justification; and  
(7) when such hunting or capturing is necessary due to other special circumstances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请特许猎捕证的程序如下：  
(一)需要捕捉国家一级保护野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地和捕捉地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门签署的意见，向国务院林业行政主管部门申请特许猎捕证；  
(二)需要在本省、自治区、直辖市猎捕国家二级保护野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地的县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门签署的意见，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门申请特许猎捕证；  
(三)需要跨省、自治区、直辖市猎捕国家二级保护野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门签署的意见，向猎捕地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门申请特许猎捕证。  
动物园需要申请捕捉国家一级保护野生动物的，在向国务院林业行政主管部门申请特许猎捕证前，须经国务院建设行政主管部门审核同意；需要申请捕捉国家二级保护野生动物的，在向申请人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门申请特许猎捕证前，须经同级政府建设行政主管部门审核同意。  
负责核发特许猎捕证的部门接到申请后，应当在3个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定。

**Article 12** The procedures for applying for a special hunting and capturing permit are as follows:  
(1) If the capturing of wildlife under Class I state protection is needed, the applicant must attach the opinions signed by the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located and where the capturing is to take place, and apply to the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council for a special hunting and capturing permit;  
(2) If the hunting of wildlife under Class II state protection within the same province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government is needed, the applicant must attach the opinions signed by the competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level where the applicant is located, and apply to the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for a special hunting and capturing permit;  
(3) If the hunting of wildlife under Class II state protection across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government is needed, the applicant must attach the opinions signed by the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located, and apply to the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government of the hunting location for a special hunting and capturing permit.  
Where a zoo needs to apply for the capturing of wildlife under Class I state protection, before applying to the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council for a special hunting and capturing permit, it must first obtain the examination and consent of the competent administrative department for construction of the State Council; where it needs to apply for the capturing of wildlife under Class II state protection, before applying to the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located, it must first obtain the examination and consent of the competent administrative department for construction of the same level of government.  
The department responsible for issuing the special hunting and capturing permit shall, after receiving the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval within three months.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，不予发放特许猎捕证：  
(一)申请猎捕者有条件以合法的非猎捕方式获得国家重点保护野生动物的种源、产品或者达到所需目的的；  
(二)猎捕申请不符合国家有关规定或者申请使用的猎捕工具、方法以及猎捕时间、地点不当的；  
(三)根据野生动物资源现状不宜捕捉、猎捕的。

**Article 13** A special hunting and capturing permit shall not be issued under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The applicant for hunting or capturing is able to obtain the sources, products or achieve the desired purpose of wildlife under special state protection through legal non-hunting or non-capturing means;  
(2) The application for hunting or capturing does not comply with the relevant state provisions, or the hunting or capturing tools, methods, time or locations applied for are inappropriate; or  
(3) It is not suitable for capturing or hunting according to the current status of wildlife resources.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　取得特许猎捕证的单位和个人，必须按照特许猎捕证规定的种类、数量、地点、期限、工具和方法进行猎捕，防止误伤野生动物或者破坏其生存环境。猎捕作业完成后，应当在10日内向猎捕地的县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门申请查验。  
县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门对在本行政区域内猎捕国家重点保护野生动物的活动，应当进行监督检查，并及时向批准猎捕的机关报告监督检查结果。

**Article 14** Units and individuals that have obtained a special hunting and capturing permit must carry out hunting or capturing in accordance with the species, quantity, location, time limit, tools and methods specified in the permit, and prevent accidental injury to wildlife or damage to their living environment. After the hunting or capturing operation is completed, they shall apply to the competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level of the hunting location for inspection within 10 days.  
The competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level shall supervise and inspect the activities of hunting or capturing wildlife under special state protection within its administrative region, and shall promptly report the results of the supervision and inspection to the authority that approved the hunting or capturing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　猎捕非国家重点保护野生动物的，必须持有狩猎证，并按照狩猎证规定的种类、数量、地点、期限、工具和方法进行猎捕。  
狩猎证由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门按照国务院林业行政主管部门的规定印制，县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门或者其授权的单位核发。  
狩猎证每年验证1次。

**Article 15** Those who hunt or capture wildlife not under special state protection must hold a hunting permit and carry out hunting or capturing in accordance with the species, quantity, location, time limit, tools and methods specified in the hunting permit.  
The hunting permit shall be printed by the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council, and shall be issued by the competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level or its authorized unit.  
The hunting permit shall be subject to annual verification.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门，应当根据本行政区域内非国家重点保护野生动物的资源现状，确定狩猎动物种类，并实行年度猎捕量限额管理。狩猎动物种类和年度猎捕量限额，由县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门按照保护资源、永续利用的原则提出，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门批准，报国务院林业行政主管部门备案。

**Article 16** The competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the current resource status of wildlife not under special state protection within its administrative region, determine the species that may be hunted, and implement annual quota management for the hunting quantity. The species that may be hunted and the annual hunting quantity quota shall be proposed by the competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level in accordance with the principles of resource conservation and sustainable utilization, approved by the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and reported to the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council for record.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方各级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门应当组织狩猎者有计划地开展狩猎活动。  
在适合狩猎的区域建立固定狩猎场所的，必须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 17** The competent administrative departments for wildlife of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize hunters to carry out hunting activities in a planned manner.  
Where a fixed hunting ground is to be established in an area suitable for hunting, it must be approved by the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止使用军用武器、汽枪、毒药、炸药、地枪、排铳、非人为直接操作并危害人畜安全的狩猎装置、夜间照明行猎、歼灭性围猎、火攻、烟熏以及县级以上各级人民政府或者其野生动物行政主管部门规定禁止使用的其他狩猎工具和方法狩猎。

**Article 18** It is prohibited to use military weapons, air guns, poisons, explosives, ground guns, shotguns, hunting devices that are not directly operated manually and endanger the safety of humans and livestock, night hunting by illumination, indiscriminate encircling hunting, fire attacks, smoke smoking, and other hunting tools and methods that are prohibited by the people's governments at or above the county level or their competent administrative departments for wildlife.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国人在中国境内对国家重点保护野生动物进行野外考察、标本采集或者在野外拍摄电影、录像的，必须向国家重点保护野生动物所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门提出申请，经其审核后，报国务院林业行政主管部门或者其授权的单位批准。

**Article 19** Where foreigners conduct field investigations, collect specimens, or shoot films or videos in the wild of wildlife under special state protection within the territory of China, they must submit an application to the competent administrative department for forestry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the wildlife under special state protection is located, and after examination by that department, the application shall be submitted to the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council or its authorized unit for approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　外国人在中国境内狩猎，必须在国务院林业行政主管部门批准的对外国人开放的狩猎场所内进行，并遵守中国有关法律、法规的规定。

**Article 20** Where foreigners hunt within the territory of China, they must do so at hunting grounds open to foreigners approved by the competent administrative department for forestry of the State Council, and comply with the provisions of relevant Chinese laws and regulations.

### 第四章　野生动物驯养繁殖管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　驯养繁殖国家重点保护野生动物的，应当持有驯养繁殖许可证。  
国务院林业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门可以根据实际情况和工作需要，委托同级有关部门审批或者核发国家重点保护野生动物驯养繁殖许可证。动物园驯养繁殖国家重点保护野生动物的，林业行政主管部门可以委托同级建设行政主管部门核发驯养繁殖许可证。  
驯养繁殖许可证由国务院林业行政主管部门印制。

**Article 21** Anyone who domesticates and breeds wildlife under special state protection shall hold a domestication and breeding permit.  
The competent forestry administrative department of the State Council and the competent forestry administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual circumstances and work needs, entrust the relevant departments at the same level to examine and approve or issue domestication and breeding permits for wildlife under special state protection. Where a zoo domesticates and breeds wildlife under special state protection, the competent forestry administrative department may entrust the competent construction administrative department at the same level to issue the domestication and breeding permit.  
The domestication and breeding permit shall be printed by the competent forestry administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从国外或者外省、自治区、直辖市引进野生动物进行驯养繁殖的，应当采取适当措施，防止其逃至野外；需要将其放生于野外的，放生单位应当向所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门提出申请，经省级以上人民政府林业行政主管部门指定的科研机构进行科学论证后，报国务院林业行政主管部门或者其授权的单位批准。  
擅自将引进的野生动物放生于野外或者因管理不当使其逃至野外的，由野生动物行政主管部门责令限期捕回或者采取其他补救措施。

**Article 22** Anyone who introduces wildlife from abroad or from other provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government for domestication and breeding shall take appropriate measures to prevent the escape of such wildlife into the wild; if the wildlife needs to be released into the wild, the releasing entity shall file an application with the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and after scientific demonstration by a scientific research institution designated by the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, the matter shall be reported to the competent forestry administrative department of the State Council or the unit authorized thereby for approval.  
Where the introduced wildlife is released into the wild without authorization or escapes into the wild due to improper management, the competent administrative department for wildlife shall order the party to recapture the wildlife within a prescribed time limit or take other remedial measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从国外引进的珍贵、濒危野生动物，经国务院林业行政主管部门核准，可以视为国家重点保护野生动物；从国外引进的其他野生动物，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门核准，可以视为地方重点保护野生动物。

**Article 23** Precious and endangered wildlife introduced from abroad may, upon verification by the competent forestry administrative department of the State Council, be deemed as wildlife under special state protection; other wildlife introduced from abroad may, upon verification by the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, be deemed as wildlife under special local protection.

### 第五章　野生动物经营利用管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　收购驯养繁殖的国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品的单位，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门商有关部门提出，经同级人民政府或者其授权的单位批准，凭批准文件向工商行政管理部门申请登记注册。  
依照前款规定经核准登记的单位，不得收购未经批准出售的国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品。

**Article 24** Entities that purchase wildlife under special state protection that is domesticated and bred, or products thereof, shall be proposed by the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in consultation with the relevant departments, and after approval by the people's government at the same level or the unit authorized thereby, shall apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce for registration on the strength of the approval document.  
Entities that have been approved and registered in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not purchase wildlife under special state protection, or products thereof, that have not been approved for sale.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营利用非国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品的，应当向工商行政管理部门申请登记注册。

**Article 25** Anyone who operates or utilizes wildlife not under special state protection, or products thereof, shall apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce for registration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　禁止在集贸市场出售、收购国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品。  
持有狩猎证的单位和个人需要出售依法获得的非国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品的，应当按照狩猎证规定的种类、数量向经核准登记的单位出售，或者在当地人民政府有关部门指定的集贸市场出售。

**Article 26** It is prohibited to sell or purchase wildlife under special state protection, or products thereof, in a market.  
Entities and individuals that hold a hunting permit and need to sell wildlife not under special state protection, or products thereof, legally obtained, shall sell such wildlife or products to approved and registered entities, in accordance with the types and quantities specified in the hunting permit, or sell them in a market designated by the relevant department of the local people's government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上各级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门和工商行政管理部门，应当对野生动物或者其产品的经营利用建立监督检查制度，加强对经营利用野生动物或者其产品的监督管理。  
对进入集贸市场的野生动物或者其产品，由工商行政管理部门进行监督管理；在集贸市场以外经营野生动物或者其产品，由野生动物行政主管部门、工商行政管理部门或者其授权的单位进行监督管理。

**Article 27** The competent administrative departments for wildlife and the administrative departments for industry and commerce of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a supervision and inspection system for the operation and utilization of wildlife or products thereof, and shall strengthen the supervision and management of the operation and utilization of wildlife or products thereof.  
Wildlife or products thereof that enter a market shall be subject to supervision and management by the administrative department for industry and commerce; the operation of wildlife or products thereof outside a market shall be subject to supervision and management by the competent administrative department for wildlife, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or the unit authorized thereby.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　运输、携带国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品出县境的，应当凭特许猎捕证、驯养繁殖许可证，向县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门提出申请，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门或者其授权的单位批准。动物园之间因繁殖动物，需要运输国家重点保护野生动物的，可以由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门授权同级建设行政主管部门审批。

**Article 28** Anyone who transports or carries wildlife under special state protection, or products thereof, out of a county's territory shall file an application with the competent administrative department for wildlife of the people's government at the county level, on the strength of the special hunting permit or the domestication and breeding permit, and the matter shall be reported to the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the unit authorized thereby, for approval. Where a zoo needs to transport wildlife under special state protection for the purpose of breeding animals between zoos, the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may authorize the competent construction administrative department at the same level to examine and approve.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　出口国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品的，以及进出口中国参加的国际公约所限制进出口的野生动物或者其产品的，必须经进出口单位或者个人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业行政主管部门审核，报国务院林业行政主管部门或者国务院批准；属于贸易性进出口活动的，必须由具有有关商品进出口权的单位承担。  
动物园因交换动物需要进出口前款所称野生动物的，国务院林业行政主管部门批准前或者国务院林业行政主管部门报请国务院批准前，应当经国务院建设行政主管部门审核同意。

**Article 29** To export wildlife under special state protection or products thereof, or to import or export wildlife, or products thereof, that are restricted from import or export by international conventions to which China is a party, the matter must be examined by the competent forestry administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the importer or exporter or individual is located, and then reported to the competent forestry administrative department of the State Council or the State Council for approval; for import or export activities of a commercial nature, such activities must be undertaken by entities with the relevant commodity import and export rights.  
Where a zoo needs to import or export the wildlife referred to in the preceding paragraph for the purpose of exchanging animals, the competent forestry administrative department of the State Council, before approval or before submitting the matter to the State Council for approval, shall obtain the examination and consent of the competent construction administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　利用野生动物或者其产品举办出国展览等活动的经济收益，主要用于野生动物保护事业。

**Article 30** The economic gains obtained from activities such as organizing overseas exhibitions using wildlife or products thereof shall be primarily used for the cause of wildlife protection.

### 第六章　奖励和惩罚

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有下列事迹之一的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府或者其野生动物行政主管部门给予奖励：  
(一)在野生动物资源调查、保护管理、宣传教育、开发利用方面有突出贡献的；  
(二)严格执行野生动物保护法规，成绩显著的；  
(三)拯救、保护和驯养繁殖珍贵、濒危野生动物取得显著成效的；  
(四)发现违反野生动物保护法规行为，及时制止或者检举有功的；  
(五)在查处破坏野生动物资源案件中有重要贡献的；  
(六)在野生动物科学研究中取得重大成果或者在应用推广科研成果中取得显著效益的；  
(七)在基层从事野生动物保护管理工作五年以上并取得显著成绩的；  
(八)在野生动物保护管理工作中有其他特殊贡献的。

**Article 31** Entities and individuals that have performed any of the following deeds shall be rewarded by the people's government at or above the county level or its wildlife administrative department:  
(1) Making outstanding contributions to wildlife resource investigation, protection and management, publicity and education, development and utilization;  
(2) Strictly implementing wildlife protection laws and regulations and achieving remarkable results;  
(3) Achieving significant results in rescuing, protecting, domesticating and breeding rare or endangered wildlife;  
(4) Timely stopping or reporting with merit any act violating wildlife protection laws and regulations;  
(5) Making important contributions in investigating and handling cases of destroying wildlife resources;  
(6) Making major achievements in wildlife scientific research or obtaining remarkable benefits in applying and promoting scientific research results;  
(7) Engaging in grassroots wildlife protection and management work for more than five years and achieving remarkable results;  
(8) Making other special contributions in wildlife protection and management.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　非法捕杀国家重点保护野生动物的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；情节显著轻微危害不大的，或者犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，由野生动物行政主管部门没收猎获物、猎捕工具和违法所得，吊销特许猎捕证，并处以相当于猎获物价值10倍以下的罚款，没有猎获物的处1万元以下罚款。

**Article 32** Whoever illegally hunts or kills wildlife under special state protection shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great, or where the criminal circumstances are minor and no criminal punishment is necessary, the wildlife administrative department shall confiscate the catch, hunting tools, and illegal gains, revoke the special hunting license, and impose a fine of not more than 10 times the value of the catch, or if there is no catch, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反野生动物保护法规，在禁猎区、禁猎期或者使用禁用的工具、方法猎捕非国家重点保护野生动物，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十二条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-32)的规定处以罚款的，按照下列规定执行：  
(一)有猎获物的，处以相当于猎获物价值8倍以下的罚款；  
(二)没有猎获物的，处2000元以下罚款。

**Article 33** Where a person violates wildlife protection laws and regulations by hunting or catching non-state-specially-protected wildlife in a prohibited hunting area, during a prohibited hunting period, or using prohibited tools or methods, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 32 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) If there is a catch, a fine of not more than 8 times the value of the catch shall be imposed;  
(2) If there is no catch, a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反野生动物保护法规，未取得狩猎证或者未按照狩猎证规定猎捕非国家重点保护野生动物，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十三条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-33)的规定处以罚款的，按照下列规定执行：  
(一)有猎获物的，处以相当于猎获物价值5倍以下的罚款；  
(二)没有猎获物的，处1000元以下罚款。

**Article 34** Where a person violates wildlife protection laws and regulations by hunting or catching non-state-specially-protected wildlife without a hunting license or not in accordance with the provisions of the hunting license, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 33 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) If there is a catch, a fine of not more than 5 times the value of the catch shall be imposed;  
(2) If there is no catch, a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反野生动物保护法规，在自然保护区、禁猎区破坏国家或者地方重点保护野生动物主要生息繁衍场所，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十四条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-34)的规定处以罚款的，按照相当于恢复原状所需费用3倍以下的标准执行。  
在自然保护区、禁猎区破坏非国家或者地方重点保护野生动物主要生息繁衍场所的，由野生动物行政主管部门责令停止破坏行为，限期恢复原状，并处以恢复原状所需费用2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 35** Where a person violates wildlife protection laws and regulations by destroying major habitats and breeding grounds of wildlife under special state or local protection in nature reserves or prohibited hunting areas, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 34 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine shall be executed at a standard of not more than 3 times the cost necessary to restore the original condition.  
Where a person destroys major habitats and breeding grounds of wildlife not under special state or local protection in nature reserves or prohibited hunting areas, the wildlife administrative department shall order the cessation of the destructive act, restore the original condition within a time limit, and impose a fine of not more than 2 times the cost necessary to restore the original condition.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反野生动物保护法规，出售、收购、运输、携带国家或者地方重点保护野生动物或者其产品的，由工商行政管理部门或者其授权的野生动物行政主管部门没收实物和违法所得，可以并处相当于实物价值10倍以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where a person violates wildlife protection laws and regulations by selling, purchasing, transporting, or carrying wildlife under special state or local protection or their products, the administrative department for industry and commerce or the wildlife administrative department authorized thereby shall confiscate the items and illegal gains, and may additionally impose a fine of not more than 10 times the value of the items.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　伪造、倒卖、转让狩猎证或者驯养繁殖许可证，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-37)的规定处以罚款的，按照5000元以下的标准执行。伪造、倒卖、转让特许猎捕证或者允许进出口证明书，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-37)的规定处以罚款的，按照5万元以下的标准执行。

**Article 37** Where a person forges, scalps, or transfers a hunting license or a domestication and breeding license, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 37 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine shall be executed at a standard of not more than 5,000 yuan. Where a person forges, scalps, or transfers a special hunting license or a permit for import and export, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 37 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine shall be executed at a standard of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反野生动物保护法规，未取得驯养繁殖许可证或者超越驯养繁殖许可证规定范围驯养繁殖国家重点保护野生动物的，由野生动物行政主管部门没收违法所得，处3000元以下罚款，可以并处没收野生动物、吊销驯养繁殖许可证。

**Article 38** Where a person violates wildlife protection laws and regulations by domesticating or breeding wildlife under special state protection without a domestication and breeding license or beyond the scope specified in such license, the wildlife administrative department shall confiscate illegal gains, impose a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan, and may additionally confiscate the wildlife and revoke the domestication and breeding license.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　外国人未经批准在中国境内对国家重点保护野生动物进行野外考察、标本采集或者在野外拍摄电影、录像的，由野生动物行政主管部门没收考察、拍摄的资料以及所获标本，可以并处5万元以下罚款。

**Article 39** Where a foreigner, without approval, conducts field surveys, collects specimens, or films or videotapes wildlife under special state protection in the wild within the territory of China, the wildlife administrative department shall confiscate the survey and filming materials and the specimens obtained, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪，应当给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍野生动物行政管理人员依法执行职务的；  
(二)偷窃、哄抢或者故意损坏野生动物保护仪器设备或者设施的；  
(三)偷窃、哄抢、抢夺非国家重点保护野生动物或者其产品的；  
(四)未经批准猎捕少量非国家重点保护野生动物的。

**Article 40** Where a person commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime but shall be subject to public security administration punishment, the public security organ shall impose punishment in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Refusing or obstructing wildlife administrative personnel from performing their duties according to law;  
(2) Stealing, forcibly taking, or intentionally damaging wildlife protection instruments, equipment, or facilities;  
(3) Stealing, forcibly taking, or seizing non-state-specially-protected wildlife or their products;  
(4) Hunting or catching a small amount of non-state-specially-protected wildlife without approval.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反野生动物保护法规，被责令限期捕回而不捕的，被责令限期恢复原状而不恢复的，野生动物行政主管部门或者其授权的单位可以代为捕回或者恢复原状，由被责令限期捕回者或者被责令限期恢复原状者承担全部捕回或者恢复原状所需的费用。

**Article 41** Any person who violates the wildlife protection laws and regulations, is ordered to recapture within a time limit but fails to do so, or is ordered to restore the original condition within a time limit but fails to do so, the wildlife administrative department or its authorized unit may recapture or restore the original condition on behalf of such person, and the person who is ordered to recapture or restore the original condition shall bear all expenses incurred in such recapture or restoration.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反野生动物保护法规，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Any person who violates the wildlife protection laws and regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　依照野生动物保护法规没收的实物，按照国务院林业行政主管部门的规定处理。

**Article 43** The physical objects confiscated in accordance with the wildlife protection laws and regulations shall be disposed of pursuant to the provisions of the forestry administrative department of the State Council.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例由国务院林业行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the forestry administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
