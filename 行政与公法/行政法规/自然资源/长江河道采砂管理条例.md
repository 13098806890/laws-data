# 长江河道采砂管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强长江河道采砂管理，维护长江河势稳定，保障防洪和通航安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of sand mining in the Yangtze River channel, maintain the stability of the Yangtze River regime, and ensure flood control and navigation safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在长江宜宾以下干流河道内从事开采砂石（以下简称长江采砂）及其管理活动的，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in sand and gravel mining in the main stream channel of the Yangtze River below Yibin (hereinafter referred to as Yangtze River sand mining) and related management activities shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院水行政主管部门及其所属的长江水利委员会应当加强对长江采砂的统一管理和监督检查，并做好有关组织、协调和指导工作。  
长江采砂管理，实行地方人民政府行政首长负责制。沿江县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对本行政区域内长江采砂活动的管理，做好长江采砂的组织、协调和监督检查工作。  
沿江县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门依照本条例的规定，具体负责本行政区域内长江采砂的管理和监督检查工作。  
国务院交通行政主管部门所属的长江航务管理局负责长江航道管理工作，长江海事机构负责长江交通安全的监督管理工作。公安部门负责长江水上治安管理工作，依法打击长江采砂活动中的犯罪行为。

**Article 3** The water administrative department of the State Council and the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission under it shall strengthen the unified management and supervision of Yangtze River sand mining, and shall properly carry out relevant organization, coordination and guidance work.  
  
The management of Yangtze River sand mining shall implement the administrative head responsibility system of local people's governments. The local people's governments at or above the county level along the river shall strengthen the management of Yangtze River sand mining activities within their respective administrative regions, and shall properly carry out the organization, coordination and supervision and inspection of Yangtze River sand mining.  
  
The water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level along the river shall, in accordance with these Regulations, be specifically responsible for the management and supervision of Yangtze River sand mining within their respective administrative regions.  
  
The Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs under the transportation administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the management of the Yangtze River waterway, and the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency shall be responsible for the supervision and management of Yangtze River traffic safety. The public security department shall be responsible for the public security management of the Yangtze River waters, and shall combat criminal activities in Yangtze River sand mining according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对长江采砂实行统一规划制度。  
长江采砂规划由长江水利委员会会同四川省、湖北省、湖南省、江西省、安徽省、江苏省和重庆市、上海市人民政府水行政主管部门编制，经征求长江航务管理局和长江海事机构意见后，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。国务院水行政主管部门批准前，应当征求国务院交通行政主管部门的意见。  
长江采砂规划一经批准，必须严格执行；确需修改时，应当依照前款规定批准。  
长江采砂规划批准实施前，长江水利委员会可以会同沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门、长江航务管理局和长江海事机构确定禁采区和禁采期，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a unified planning system for Yangtze River sand mining.  
  
The Yangtze River sand mining plan shall be prepared by the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission jointly with the water administrative departments of the people's governments of Sichuan Province, Hubei Province, Hunan Province, Jiangxi Province, Anhui Province, Jiangsu Province, Chongqing Municipality and Shanghai Municipality, and after soliciting opinions from the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs and the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency, shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval. Before approval by the water administrative department of the State Council, it shall solicit opinions from the transportation administrative department of the State Council.  
  
Once the Yangtze River sand mining plan is approved, it must be strictly implemented; if modification is truly necessary, it shall be approved in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
  
Before the Yangtze River sand mining plan is approved and implemented, the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission may, jointly with the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river, the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs, and the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency, determine the prohibited mining areas and prohibited mining periods, and shall submit them to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　长江采砂规划应当充分考虑长江防洪安全和通航安全的要求，符合长江流域综合规划和长江防洪、河道整治以及航道整治等专业规划。

**Article 5** The Yangtze River sand mining plan shall fully consider the requirements of Yangtze River flood control safety and navigation safety, and shall conform to the comprehensive plan of the Yangtze River basin and specialized plans such as Yangtze River flood control, river regulation and waterway regulation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　长江采砂规划应当包括下列内容：  
（一）禁采区和可采区；  
（二）禁采期和可采期；  
（三）年度采砂控制总量；  
（四）可采区内采砂船只的控制数量。

**Article 6** The Yangtze River sand mining plan shall include the following:  
(1) prohibited mining areas and permissible mining areas;  
(2) prohibited mining periods and permissible mining periods;  
(3) the annual total control volume of sand mining;  
(4) the control number of sand mining vessels in permissible mining areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门根据长江采砂规划，可以拟订本行政区域内长江采砂规划实施方案，报本级人民政府批准后实施，并报长江水利委员会、长江航务管理局备案。  
沿江省、直辖市人民政府应当将长江采砂规划确定的禁采区和禁采期予以公告。  
沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门可以根据本行政区域内长江的水情、工情、汛情、航道变迁和管理等需要，在长江采砂规划确定的禁采区、禁采期外增加禁采范围、延长禁采期限，报本级人民政府决定后公告。

**Article 7** The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river may, based on the Yangtze River sand mining plan, formulate implementation plans for the Yangtze River sand mining plan within their respective administrative regions, which shall be implemented after approval by the people's government at the same level, and shall be filed with the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission and the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs.  
  
The people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river shall publish the prohibited mining areas and prohibited mining periods determined in the Yangtze River sand mining plan.  
  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river may, based on the needs of the Yangtze River's hydrological conditions, engineering conditions, flood conditions, waterway changes and management within their respective administrative regions, increase the scope of prohibition or extend the prohibition periods beyond the prohibited mining areas and prohibited mining periods determined in the Yangtze River sand mining plan, and shall publish them after decision by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　长江水利委员会和沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当加强对长江采砂规划实施情况的监督检查。

**Article 8** The Yangtze River Water Resources Commission and the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the implementation of the Yangtze River sand mining plan.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对长江采砂实行采砂许可制度。  
河道采砂许可证由沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门审批发放；属于省际边界重点河段的，经有关省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门签署意见后，由长江水利委员会审批发放；涉及航道的，审批发放前应当征求长江航务管理局和长江海事机构的意见。省际边界重点河段的范围由国务院水行政主管部门划定。  
河道采砂许可证式样由国务院水行政主管部门规定，由沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门和长江水利委员会印制。

**Article 9** The State shall implement a sand mining permit system for Yangtze River sand mining.  
  
The river channel sand mining permit shall be examined, approved and issued by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government along the river; if it belongs to a key river section at an inter-provincial boundary, after the water administrative department of the relevant province or municipality directly under the Central Government signs its opinion, it shall be examined, approved and issued by the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission; if it involves a waterway, the opinions of the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs and the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency shall be solicited before examination, approval and issuance. The scope of the key river sections at inter-provincial boundaries shall be delimited by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
  
The format of the river channel sand mining permit shall be prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council, and shall be printed by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river and the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　从事长江采砂活动的单位和个人应当向沿江市、县人民政府水行政主管部门提出申请；符合下列条件的，由长江水利委员会或者沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定，审批发放河道采砂许可证：  
（一）符合长江采砂规划确定的可采区和可采期的要求；  
（二）符合年度采砂控制总量的要求；  
（三）符合规定的作业方式；  
（四）符合采砂船只数量的控制要求；  
（五）采砂船舶、船员证书齐全；  
（六）有符合要求的采砂设备和采砂技术人员；  
（七）长江水利委员会或者沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门规定的其他条件。  
市、县人民政府水行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起10日内签署意见后，报送沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门审批；属于省际边界重点河段的，经有关省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门签署意见后，报送长江水利委员会审批。长江水利委员会或者沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内予以审批；不予批准的，应当在作出不予批准决定之日起7日内通知申请人，并说明理由。

**Article 10** Any unit or individual engaged in Yangtze River sand mining activities shall submit an application to the water administrative department of the people's government of the city or county along the river; if the following conditions are met, the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission or the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government along the river shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations, examine, approve and issue a river channel sand mining permit:  
(1) meeting the requirements of permissible mining areas and permissible mining periods determined in the Yangtze River sand mining plan;  
(2) meeting the requirements of the annual total control volume of sand mining;  
(3) meeting the prescribed operation methods;  
(4) meeting the control requirements for the number of sand mining vessels;  
(5) having complete certificates for the sand mining vessel and crew;  
(6) having sand mining equipment and sand mining technical personnel that meet the requirements;  
(7) other conditions prescribed by the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission or the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government along the river.  
  
The water administrative department of the people's government of the city or county shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the application, sign its opinion and submit it to the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government along the river for examination and approval; if it belongs to a key river section at an inter-provincial boundary, after the water administrative department of the relevant province or municipality directly under the Central Government signs its opinion, it shall be submitted to the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission for examination and approval. The Yangtze River Water Resources Commission or the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government along the river shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, conduct examination and approval; if approval is not granted, the applicant shall be notified within 7 days from the date of the decision not to approve, and the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　河道采砂许可证应当载明船主姓名（名称）、船名、船号和开采的性质、种类、地点、时限以及作业方式、弃料处理方式、许可证的有效期限等有关事项和内容。

**Article 11** The river channel sand mining permit shall state the name (title) of the vessel owner, vessel name, vessel number, and the nature, type, location, time limit of mining, as well as the operation method, waste material disposal method, validity period of the permit, and other relevant matters and contents.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从事长江采砂活动的单位和个人应当按照河道采砂许可证的规定进行开采。有关县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和长江水利委员会应当按照职责划分对其加强监督检查。  
从事长江采砂活动的单位和个人需要改变河道采砂许可证规定的事项和内容的，应当重新办理河道采砂许可证。  
禁止伪造、涂改或者买卖、出租、出借或者以其他方式转让河道采砂许可证。

**Article 12** Any unit or individual engaged in Yangtze River sand mining activities shall conduct mining in accordance with the provisions of the river channel sand mining permit. The relevant water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level and the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall, according to their respective functions and duties, strengthen supervision and inspection thereof.  
  
If a unit or individual engaged in Yangtze River sand mining activities needs to change the matters and contents stated in the river channel sand mining permit, it shall re-apply for a river channel sand mining permit.  
  
It is prohibited to forge, alter, buy, sell, lease, lend, or transfer the river channel sand mining permit by other means.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　为保障航道畅通和航行安全，采砂作业应当服从通航要求，并设立明显标志。

**Article 13** To ensure the unimpeded flow of the waterway and navigation safety, sand mining operations shall comply with navigation requirements and shall set up conspicuous signs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　长江水利委员会和沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门年审批采砂总量不得超过规划确定的年度采砂控制总量。  
沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当在每年1月31日前将上一年度的长江采砂审批发证情况和实施情况，报长江水利委员会备案。

**Article 14** The total annual sand mining volume approved by the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission and the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river shall not exceed the annual control volume of sand mining determined in the plan.  
  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river shall, before January 31 of each year, report the previous year's approval and permit issuance situation and implementation situation of Yangtze River sand mining to the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission for filing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　沿江县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门因整修长江堤防进行吹填固基或者整治长江河道采砂的，应当经本省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门审查，并报长江水利委员会批准；长江航务管理局因整治长江航道采砂的，应当事先征求长江水利委员会的意见。  
因吹填造地从事采砂活动的单位和个人，应当依法申请河道采砂许可证。

**Article 15** If the water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level along the river conducts hydraulic fill for foundation consolidation due to the repair of the Yangtze River embankment or conducts sand mining for the regulation of the Yangtze River channel, it shall be examined by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission for approval; if the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs conducts sand mining for the regulation of the Yangtze River waterway, it shall solicit the opinion of the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission in advance.  
  
Any unit or individual engaged in sand mining activities for the purpose of hydraulic fill for land reclamation shall apply for a river channel sand mining permit according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　除按照河道采砂许可证规定的期限在可采区作业外，采砂船舶应当集中停放在沿江县级人民政府指定的地点，并由采砂船舶所有者或者使用者负责管护。无正当理由，不得擅自离开指定的地点。

**Article 16** Except for operations in permissible mining areas within the period specified in the river channel sand mining permit, sand mining vessels shall be concentrated and moored at locations designated by the people's government at the county level along the river, and shall be managed and maintained by the owner or user of the sand mining vessel. Without justifiable reasons, they shall not leave the designated locations without authorization.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止运输、收购、销售未取得河道采砂许可证的单位、个人开采的长江河道砂石。  
长江水利委员会应当会同沿江省、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门及有关部门、长江航务管理局、长江海事机构等单位建立统一的长江河道采砂管理信息平台，推进实施长江河道砂石开采、运输、收购、销售全过程追溯。

**Article 17** It is prohibited to transport, purchase, or sell sand and gravel from the Yangtze River channel mined by units or individuals that have not obtained a river channel sand mining permit.  
  
The Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall, jointly with the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces and municipalities directly under the Central Government along the river and relevant departments, the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs, the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency, and other units, establish a unified information platform for the management of sand mining in the Yangtze River channel, and promote the implementation of whole-process traceability of sand and gravel mining, transportation, purchase, and sale in the Yangtze River channel.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反本条例规定，未办理河道采砂许可证，擅自在长江采砂的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者长江水利委员会依据职权，责令停止违法行为，没收违法开采的砂石和违法所得以及采砂船舶和挖掘机械等作业设备、工具，并处违法开采的砂石货值金额2倍以上20倍以下的罚款；货值金额不足10万元的，并处20万元以上200万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a person has not applied for a river channel sand mining permit and has engaged in sand mining in the Yangtze River without authorization, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall, according to their functions and duties, order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally mined sand and gravel and illegal gains, as well as the sand mining vessel and excavation machinery and other operation equipment and tools, and impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 20 times the value of the illegally mined sand and gravel; if the value is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本条例规定，采砂单位、个人未按照河道采砂许可证规定的要求采砂的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者长江水利委员会依据职权，责令停止违法行为，没收违法开采的砂石和违法所得，并处违法开采的砂石货值金额1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；情节严重或者在禁采区、禁采期采砂的，没收违法开采的砂石和违法所得以及采砂船舶和挖掘机械等作业设备、工具，吊销河道采砂许可证，并处违法开采的砂石货值金额2倍以上20倍以下的罚款，货值金额不足10万元的，并处20万元以上200万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a sand mining unit or individual has not mined sand in accordance with the requirements specified in the river channel sand mining permit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall, according to their functions and duties, order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally mined sand and gravel and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 1 time but not more than 2 times the value of the illegally mined sand and gravel; if the circumstances are serious or sand mining has been conducted in prohibited mining areas or during prohibited mining periods, the illegally mined sand and gravel and illegal gains, as well as the sand mining vessel and excavation machinery and other operation equipment and tools, shall be confiscated, the river channel sand mining permit shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 20 times the value of the illegally mined sand and gravel shall be imposed; if the value is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，运输、收购、销售未取得河道采砂许可证的单位、个人开采的长江河道砂石的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门、长江水利委员会、有关海事管理机构以及县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门依据职权，责令停止违法行为，没收违法运输、收购、销售的砂石和违法所得，并处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处20万元以上200万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a person has transported, purchased, or sold sand and gravel from the Yangtze River channel mined by units or individuals that have not obtained a river channel sand mining permit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission, the relevant maritime administrative agency, and other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally transported, purchased, or sold sand and gravel and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　违反本条例规定，采砂船舶未在指定地点集中停放或者无正当理由擅自离开指定地点的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停靠在指定地点，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，予以强行转移至指定地点。

**Article 21** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a sand mining vessel has not been concentrated and moored at a designated location or has left the designated location without authorization without justifiable reasons, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to moor at the designated location and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if it refuses to rectify, it shall be forcibly transferred to the designated location.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　伪造、变造、转让、出租、出借河道采砂许可证，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者长江水利委员会依据职权予以吊销或者收缴，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 22** Where a person forges, alters, transfers, leases, or lends a river channel sand mining permit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission shall, according to their functions and duties, revoke or confiscate it, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在长江航道内非法采砂影响通航安全的，由长江航务管理局、长江海事机构依照《中华人民共和国内河交通安全管理条例》和《中华人民共和国航道管理条例》等规定给予处罚。

**Article 23** Where a person illegally mines sand in the Yangtze River waterway, affecting navigation safety, the Changjiang River Administration of Navigational Affairs and the Yangtze River maritime administrative agency shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Inland River Traffic Safety and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Waterways.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　依照本条例规定应当给予行政处罚，而有关水行政主管部门不给予行政处罚的，由上级人民政府水行政主管部门责令其作出行政处罚决定或者直接给予行政处罚；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 24** Where, according to these Regulations, an administrative penalty should be imposed, but the relevant water administrative department does not impose it, the water administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make a decision on administrative penalty or directly impose the administrative penalty; the person in charge with responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依照本条例实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离，所收取的罚款必须全部上缴国库。

**Article 25** An administrative penalty of a fine imposed in accordance with these Regulations shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, implement the separation of the decision on fines and the collection of fines, and all fines collected must be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有下列行为之一，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不执行已批准的长江采砂规划、擅自修改长江采砂规划或者违反长江采砂规划组织采砂的；  
（二）不按照规定审批发放河道采砂许可证或者其他批准文件的；  
（三）不履行本条例规定的监督检查职责，造成长江采砂秩序混乱或者造成重大责任事故的。

**Article 26** Where any person commits one of the following acts, the person in charge with responsibility and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to implement the approved Yangtze River sand mining plan, modifying the Yangtze River sand mining plan without authorization, or organizing sand mining in violation of the plan;  
(2) failing to examine, approve, and issue river channel sand mining permits or other approval documents in accordance with the regulations;  
(3) failing to perform the duties of supervision and inspection prescribed in these Regulations, resulting in disorder in Yangtze River sand mining or causing a major liability accident.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
