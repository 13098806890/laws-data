# 大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-04-14  
**生效日期**：2006-09-01

> (2006年7月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第471号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2017年4月14日《国务院关于修改〈大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置条例〉的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了做好大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置工作，维护移民合法权益，保障工程建设的顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　大中型水利水电工程的征地补偿和移民安置，适用本条例。 第三条　国家实行开发性移民方针，采取前期补偿、补助与后期扶持相结合的办法，使移民生活达到或者超过原有水平。 第四条　大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置应当遵循下列原则： (一)以人为本，保障移民的合法权益，满足移民生存与发展的需求； (二)顾全大局，服从国家整体安排，兼顾国家、集体、个人利益； (三)节约利用土地，合理规划工程占地，控制移民规模； (四)可持续发展，与资源综合开发利用、生态环境保护相协调； (五)因地制宜，统筹规划。 第五条　移民安置工作实行政府领导、分级负责、县为基础、项目法人参与的管理体制。 国务院水利水电工程移民行政管理机构(以下简称国务院移民管理机构)负责全国大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的管理和监督。 县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的组织和领导；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的移民管理机构，负责本行政区域内大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的管理和监督。 第二章　移民安置规划

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置工作，维护移民合法权益，保障工程建设的顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of properly carrying out land acquisition compensation and resettlement for the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of resettlers, and ensuring the smooth progress of project construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　大中型水利水电工程的征地补偿和移民安置，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to land acquisition compensation and resettlement for large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行开发性移民方针，采取前期补偿、补助与后期扶持相结合的办法，使移民生活达到或者超过原有水平。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a resettlement policy of development-oriented approach, adopting a method combining early-stage compensation and subsidies with later-stage support, so that the living standards of resettlers shall reach or exceed their original levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)以人为本，保障移民的合法权益，满足移民生存与发展的需求；  
(二)顾全大局，服从国家整体安排，兼顾国家、集体、个人利益；  
(三)节约利用土地，合理规划工程占地，控制移民规模；  
(四)可持续发展，与资源综合开发利用、生态环境保护相协调；  
(五)因地制宜，统筹规划。

**Article 4** Land acquisition compensation and resettlement for the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects shall follow the following principles:  
(1) People-oriented, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of resettlers, and meeting the needs of resettlers for survival and development;  
(2) Taking the overall situation into consideration, subjecting to the overall arrangements of the State, and balancing the interests of the State, collectives, and individuals;  
(3) Economically utilizing land, reasonably planning the land occupied by the project, and controlling the scale of resettlement;  
(4) Sustainable development, coordinating with comprehensive development and utilization of resources and ecological environmental protection;  
(5) Adapting measures to local conditions and making overall plans.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　移民安置工作实行政府领导、分级负责、县为基础、项目法人参与的管理体制。  
国务院水利水电工程移民行政管理机构(以下简称国务院移民管理机构)负责全国大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的管理和监督。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的组织和领导；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的移民管理机构，负责本行政区域内大中型水利水电工程移民安置工作的管理和监督。

**Article 5** The resettlement work shall adopt a management system featuring government leadership, hierarchical responsibility, county-level foundation, and participation of the project legal person.  
The administrative department for water conservancy and hydropower project resettlement under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council) shall be responsible for the management and supervision of resettlement work for large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects nationwide.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing and leading the resettlement work for large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects within their respective administrative regions; the resettlement administration departments designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the management and supervision of resettlement work for large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　移民安置规划

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　已经成立项目法人的大中型水利水电工程，由项目法人编制移民安置规划大纲，按照审批权限报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院移民管理机构审批；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院移民管理机构在审批前应当征求移民区和移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府的意见。  
没有成立项目法人的大中型水利水电工程，项目主管部门应当会同移民区和移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府编制移民安置规划大纲，按照审批权限报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院移民管理机构审批。

**Article 6** For large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects for which a project legal person has been established, the project legal person shall prepare the resettlement planning outline and, according to the authority for approval, submit it to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council for approval; before approval, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council shall solicit opinions from the local people's governments at or above the county level in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area.  
For large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects for which no project legal person has been established, the project competent department shall, jointly with the local people's governments at or above the county level in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area, prepare the resettlement planning outline and, according to the authority for approval, submit it to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　移民安置规划大纲应当根据工程占地和淹没区实物调查结果以及移民区、移民安置区经济社会情况和资源环境承载能力编制。  
工程占地和淹没区实物调查，由项目主管部门或者项目法人会同工程占地和淹没区所在地的地方人民政府实施；实物调查应当全面准确，调查结果经调查者和被调查者签字认可并公示后，由有关地方人民政府签署意见。实物调查工作开始前，工程占地和淹没区所在地的省级人民政府应当发布通告，禁止在工程占地和淹没区新增建设项目和迁入人口，并对实物调查工作作出安排。

**Article 7** The resettlement planning outline shall be prepared based on the results of physical inventory surveys of the project area and inundation area, as well as the economic and social conditions and the carrying capacity of resources and the environment in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area.  
The physical inventory surveys of the project area and inundation area shall be carried out by the project competent department or the project legal person jointly with the local people's government of the place where the project area and inundation area are located; the physical inventory surveys shall be comprehensive and accurate, and after the survey results are signed and confirmed by the surveyors and the surveyed parties and made public, the relevant local people's government shall issue its comments. Before the commencement of the physical inventory surveys, the provincial people's government of the place where the project area and inundation area are located shall issue a notice, prohibiting the addition of new construction projects and the immigration of population into the project area and inundation area, and making arrangements for the physical inventory surveys.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　移民安置规划大纲应当主要包括移民安置的任务、去向、标准和农村移民生产安置方式以及移民生活水平评价和搬迁后生活水平预测、水库移民后期扶持政策、淹没线以上受影响范围的划定原则、移民安置规划编制原则等内容。

**Article 8** The resettlement planning outline shall mainly include the tasks, destinations, and standards of resettlement, the production resettlement methods for rural resettlers, the evaluation of resettlers' living standards and the prediction of living standards after relocation, the later-stage support policies for reservoir resettlers, the delimitation principles for the affected area above the inundation line, and the principles for preparing the resettlement plan.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　编制移民安置规划大纲应当广泛听取移民和移民安置区居民的意见；必要时，应当采取听证的方式。  
经批准的移民安置规划大纲是编制移民安置规划的基本依据，应当严格执行，不得随意调整或者修改；确需调整或者修改的，应当报原批准机关批准。

**Article 9** In preparing the resettlement planning outline, opinions of resettlers and residents of the resettlement area shall be extensively heard; where necessary, hearings shall be held.  
The approved resettlement planning outline shall be the basic basis for preparing the resettlement plan, and shall be strictly implemented without arbitrary adjustment or modification; where adjustment or modification is truly necessary, approval from the original approving authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　已经成立项目法人的，由项目法人根据经批准的移民安置规划大纲编制移民安置规划；没有成立项目法人的，项目主管部门应当会同移民区和移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府，根据经批准的移民安置规划大纲编制移民安置规划。  
大中型水利水电工程的移民安置规划，按照审批权限经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府移民管理机构或者国务院移民管理机构审核后，由项目法人或者项目主管部门报项目审批或者核准部门，与可行性研究报告或者项目申请报告一并审批或者核准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府移民管理机构或者国务院移民管理机构审核移民安置规划，应当征求本级人民政府有关部门以及移民区和移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府的意见。

**Article 10** Where a project legal person has been established, the project legal person shall prepare the resettlement plan in accordance with the approved resettlement planning outline; where no project legal person has been established, the project competent department shall, jointly with the local people's governments at or above the county level in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area, prepare the resettlement plan in accordance with the approved resettlement planning outline.  
The resettlement plan for a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project shall, after being reviewed by the resettlement administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council according to the authority for approval, be submitted by the project legal person or the project competent department to the project approval or verification department for approval or verification together with the feasibility study report or the project application report.  
When reviewing the resettlement plan, the resettlement administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council shall solicit opinions from the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and from the local people's governments at or above the county level in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　编制移民安置规划应当以资源环境承载能力为基础，遵循本地安置与异地安置、集中安置与分散安置、政府安置与移民自找门路安置相结合的原则。  
编制移民安置规划应当尊重少数民族的生产、生活方式和风俗习惯。  
移民安置规划应当与国民经济和社会发展规划以及土地利用总体规划、城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划相衔接。

**Article 11** The resettlement plan shall be prepared on the basis of the carrying capacity of resources and the environment, and shall follow the principles of combining local resettlement with relocation resettlement, centralized resettlement with decentralized resettlement, and government-arranged resettlement with resettlers' self-arranged resettlement.  
The preparation of the resettlement plan shall respect the production and living methods and customs of ethnic minorities.  
The resettlement plan shall be coordinated with the national economic and social development plan, the overall land use plan, the overall urban plan, and the village and town plans.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　移民安置规划应当对农村移民安置、城(集)镇迁建、工矿企业迁建、专项设施迁建或者复建、防护工程建设、水库水域开发利用、水库移民后期扶持措施、征地补偿和移民安置资金概(估)算等作出安排。  
对淹没线以上受影响范围内因水库蓄水造成的居民生产、生活困难问题，应当纳入移民安置规划，按照经济合理的原则，妥善处理。

**Article 12** The resettlement plan shall make arrangements for such matters as the resettlement of rural resettlers, the relocation of cities (towns), the relocation of industrial and mining enterprises, the relocation or reconstruction of special facilities, the construction of protective works, the development and utilization of reservoir areas, later-stage support measures for reservoir resettlers, and the budget (estimate) of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement.  
The difficulties in production and living of residents within the affected area above the inundation line caused by reservoir impoundment shall be included in the resettlement plan and properly dealt with in accordance with the principle of economic rationality.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对农村移民安置进行规划，应当坚持以农业生产安置为主，遵循因地制宜、有利生产、方便生活、保护生态的原则，合理规划农村移民安置点；有条件的地方，可以结合小城镇建设进行。  
农村移民安置后，应当使移民拥有与移民安置区居民基本相当的土地等农业生产资料。

**Article 13** In planning the resettlement of rural resettlers, the principle of mainly arranging resettlement through agricultural production shall be adhered to, and the principles of adapting to local conditions, facilitating production, making life convenient, and protecting the ecology shall be followed, with reasonable planning of resettlement sites for rural resettlers; where conditions permit, this may be carried out in conjunction with the construction of small towns.  
After rural resettlers are resettled, they shall have agricultural means of production such as land that are substantially equivalent to those of residents in the resettlement area.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对城(集)镇移民安置进行规划，应当以城(集)镇现状为基础，节约用地，合理布局。  
工矿企业的迁建，应当符合国家的产业政策，结合技术改造和结构调整进行；对技术落后、浪费资源、产品质量低劣、污染严重、不具备安全生产条件的企业，应当依法关闭。

**Article 14** In planning the resettlement of cities (towns), the current situation of the cities (towns) shall be taken as the basis, with economical use of land and rational layout.  
The relocation of industrial and mining enterprises shall comply with the State's industrial policies and be carried out in conjunction with technological transformation and structural adjustment; enterprises with backward technology, waste of resources, low product quality, serious pollution, or lack of conditions for safe production shall be closed down according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　编制移民安置规划应当广泛听取移民和移民安置区居民的意见；必要时，应当采取听证的方式。  
经批准的移民安置规划是组织实施移民安置工作的基本依据，应当严格执行，不得随意调整或者修改；确需调整或者修改的，应当依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)的规定重新报批。  
未编制移民安置规划或者移民安置规划未经审核的大中型水利水电工程建设项目，有关部门不得批准或者核准其建设，不得为其办理用地等有关手续。

**Article 15** In preparing the resettlement plan, opinions of resettlers and residents of the resettlement area shall be extensively heard; where necessary, hearings shall be held.  
The approved resettlement plan shall be the basic basis for organizing and implementing the resettlement work, and shall be strictly implemented without arbitrary adjustment or modification; where adjustment or modification is truly necessary, a new submission and approval shall be made in accordance with the provisions of Article 10 of these Regulations.  
For construction projects of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects that have not prepared a resettlement plan or whose resettlement plan has not been reviewed, the relevant departments shall not approve or authorize their construction, and shall not handle relevant procedures such as land use for them.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　征地补偿和移民安置资金、依法应当缴纳的耕地占用税和耕地开垦费以及依照国务院有关规定缴纳的森林植被恢复费等应当列入大中型水利水电工程概算。  
征地补偿和移民安置资金包括土地补偿费、安置补助费，农村居民点迁建、城(集)镇迁建、工矿企业迁建以及专项设施迁建或者复建补偿费(含有关地上附着物补偿费)，移民个人财产补偿费(含地上附着物和青苗补偿费)和搬迁费，库底清理费，淹没区文物保护费和国家规定的其他费用。

**Article 16** The funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement, the farmland occupation tax and farmland reclamation fees payable according to law, and the forest vegetation restoration fees payable in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council shall be included in the budget of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects.  
The funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement include: land compensation fees, resettlement subsidies, compensation fees for the relocation of rural settlements, cities (towns), industrial and mining enterprises, and special facilities or their reconstruction (including compensation fees for relevant above-ground attachments), compensation fees for resettlers' personal property (including compensation fees for above-ground attachments and young crops) and relocation expenses, reservoir bed clearance fees,文物保护 fees for the inundation area, and other fees prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　农村移民集中安置的农村居民点、城(集)镇、工矿企业以及专项设施等基础设施的迁建或者复建选址，应当依法做好环境影响评价、水文地质与工程地质勘察、地质灾害防治和地质灾害危险性评估。

**Article 17** For the site selection for the relocation or reconstruction of rural settlements where rural resettlers are centrally resettled, cities (towns), industrial and mining enterprises, and special facilities, environmental impact assessment, hydrogeological and engineering geological surveys, geological disaster prevention, and geological hazard risk assessment shall be carried out according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对淹没区内的居民点、耕地等，具备防护条件的，应当在经济合理的前提下，采取修建防护工程等防护措施，减少淹没损失。  
防护工程的建设费用由项目法人承担，运行管理费用由大中型水利水电工程管理单位负责。

**Article 18** For residential areas, farmland, and other features within the inundation area, where conditions for protection exist, protective measures such as constructing protective works shall be taken under the premise of economic rationality to reduce inundation losses.  
The construction costs of protective works shall be borne by the project legal person, and the operation and management costs shall be borne by the management unit of the large or medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower project.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对工程占地和淹没区内的文物，应当查清分布，确认保护价值，坚持保护为主、抢救第一的方针，实行重点保护、重点发掘。

**Article 19** For cultural relics within the project area and inundation area, their distribution shall be ascertained, their protection value confirmed, and the policy of placing protection first and rescue first shall be adhered to, with key protection and key excavation carried out.

### 第三章　征地补偿

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　依法批准的流域规划中确定的大中型水利水电工程建设项目的用地，应当纳入项目所在地的土地利用总体规划。  
大中型水利水电工程建设项目核准或者可行性研究报告批准后，项目用地应当列入土地利用年度计划。  
属于国家重点扶持的水利、能源基础设施的大中型水利水电工程建设项目，其用地可以以划拨方式取得。

**Article 20** The land for construction projects of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects as determined in the river basin plan approved according to law shall be included in the overall land use plan of the place where the project is located.  
After a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower construction project is authorized or its feasibility study report is approved, the project land shall be included in the annual land use plan.  
For large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower construction projects that belong to water conservancy and energy infrastructure supported by the State as a priority, the land may be obtained through allocation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　大中型水利水电工程建设项目用地，应当依法申请并办理审批手续，实行一次报批、分期征收，按期支付征地补偿费。  
对于应急的防洪、治涝等工程，经有批准权的人民政府决定，可以先行使用土地，事后补办用地手续。

**Article 21** Land for construction projects of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects shall be applied for and undergo approval procedures according to law, with a system of one-time application for approval, phased expropriation, and timely payment of land acquisition compensation fees.  
For emergency flood control, waterlogging control, and other projects, upon the decision of the people's government with approval authority, the land may be used first, and the land use procedures shall be completed afterwards.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　大中型水利水电工程建设征收土地的土地补偿费和安置补助费，实行与铁路等基础设施项目用地同等补偿标准，按照被征收土地所在省、自治区、直辖市规定的标准执行。  
被征收土地上的零星树木、青苗等补偿标准，按照被征收土地所在省、自治区、直辖市规定的标准执行。  
被征收土地上的附着建筑物按照其原规模、原标准或者恢复原功能的原则补偿；对补偿费用不足以修建基本用房的贫困移民，应当给予适当补助。  
使用其他单位或者个人依法使用的国有耕地，参照征收耕地的补偿标准给予补偿；使用未确定给单位或者个人使用的国有未利用地，不予补偿。  
移民远迁后，在水库周边淹没线以上属于移民个人所有的零星树木、房屋等应当分别依照本条第二款、第三款规定的标准给予补偿。

**Article 22** The land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies for land expropriated for the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects shall adopt the same compensation standards as those for land used for railways and other infrastructure projects, and shall be implemented in accordance with the standards prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the expropriated land is located.  
The compensation standards for scattered trees, young crops, and other items on the expropriated land shall be implemented in accordance with the standards prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the expropriated land is located.  
The attached buildings on the expropriated land shall be compensated in accordance with the principle of their original scale, original standard, or restoration of original function; for impoverished resettlers whose compensation fees are insufficient to build basic housing, appropriate subsidies shall be given.  
For the use of State-owned farmland lawfully used by other units or individuals, compensation shall be given by reference to the compensation standards for expropriation of farmland; for the use of State-owned unused land not designated for use by any unit or individual, no compensation shall be given.  
After resettlers move far away, the scattered trees, houses, and other property owned by resettlers above the inundation line around the reservoir shall be compensated in accordance with the standards prescribed respectively in paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　大中型水利水电工程建设临时用地，由县级以上人民政府土地主管部门批准。

**Article 23** Temporary land use for the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects shall be approved by the competent land department of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　工矿企业和交通、电力、电信、广播电视等专项设施以及中小学的迁建或者复建，应当按照其原规模、原标准或者恢复原功能的原则补偿。

**Article 24** The relocation or reconstruction of industrial and mining enterprises, special facilities such as transportation, electric power, telecommunications, radio and television, and primary and secondary schools shall be compensated in accordance with the principle of their original scale, original standard, or restoration of original function.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　大中型水利水电工程建设占用耕地的，应当执行占补平衡的规定。为安置移民开垦的耕地、因大中型水利水电工程建设而进行土地整理新增的耕地、工程施工新造的耕地可以抵扣或者折抵建设占用耕地的数量。  
大中型水利水电工程建设占用25度以上坡耕地的，不计入需要补充耕地的范围。

**Article 25** Where the construction of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project occupies farmland, the provisions on the balance of occupation and compensation shall be implemented. The farmland reclaimed for resettling migrants, the newly increased farmland from land consolidation due to the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects, and the farmland newly created by project construction may be deducted or offset against the amount of farmland occupied by the construction.  
Where the construction of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project occupies sloping farmland with a gradient of 25 degrees or more, such occupation shall not be counted within the scope of farmland that needs to be supplemented.

### 第四章　移民安置

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　移民区和移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府负责移民安置规划的组织实施。

**Article 26** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of the resettlement plan.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　大中型水利水电工程开工前，项目法人应当根据经批准的移民安置规划，与移民区和移民安置区所在的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者市、县人民政府签订移民安置协议；签订协议的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者市人民政府，可以与下一级有移民或者移民安置任务的人民政府签订移民安置协议。

**Article 27** Before the commencement of construction of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project, the project legal person shall, in accordance with the approved resettlement plan, sign a resettlement agreement with the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the people's government of the city or county, where the resettlement source area and the resettlement area are located; the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the people's government of the city that signs the agreement may sign resettlement agreements with the people's governments at the next lower level that have resettlement tasks.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　项目法人应当根据大中型水利水电工程建设的要求和移民安置规划，在每年汛期结束后60日内，向与其签订移民安置协议的地方人民政府提出下年度移民安置计划建议；签订移民安置协议的地方人民政府，应当根据移民安置规划和项目法人的年度移民安置计划建议，在与项目法人充分协商的基础上，组织编制并下达本行政区域的下年度移民安置年度计划。

**Article 28** The project legal person shall, in accordance with the requirements of the construction of the large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project and the resettlement plan, within 60 days after the end of the flood season each year, submit a proposal for the next year's resettlement plan to the local people's government with which it signed the resettlement agreement; the local people's government that signed the resettlement agreement shall, on the basis of full consultation with the project legal person, organize the preparation and issuance of the annual resettlement plan for the next year within its administrative region in accordance with the resettlement plan and the project legal person's annual resettlement plan proposal.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　项目法人应当根据移民安置年度计划，按照移民安置实施进度将征地补偿和移民安置资金支付给与其签订移民安置协议的地方人民政府。

**Article 29** The project legal person shall, in accordance with the annual resettlement plan and the implementation progress of resettlement, pay the funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement to the local people's government with which it signed the resettlement agreement.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农村移民在本县通过新开发土地或者调剂土地集中安置的，县级人民政府应当将土地补偿费、安置补助费和集体财产补偿费直接全额兑付给该村集体经济组织或者村民委员会。  
农村移民分散安置到本县内其他村集体经济组织或者村民委员会的，应当由移民安置村集体经济组织或者村民委员会与县级人民政府签订协议，按照协议安排移民的生产和生活。

**Article 30** Where rural resettlers are centrally resettled within the same county through newly developed land or adjusted land, the county people's government shall directly pay in full the land compensation fees, resettlement subsidies, and collective property compensation fees to the village collective economic organization or the villagers' committee of that village.  
Where rural resettlers are scattered and resettled in other village collective economic organizations or villagers' committees within the same county, the resettlement village collective economic organization or the villagers' committee shall sign an agreement with the county people's government, and arrange the production and living of the resettlers in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　农村移民在本省行政区域内其他县安置的，与项目法人签订移民安置协议的地方人民政府，应当及时将相应的征地补偿和移民安置资金交给移民安置区县级人民政府，用于安排移民的生产和生活。  
农村移民跨省安置的，项目法人应当及时将相应的征地补偿和移民安置资金交给移民安置区省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，用于安排移民的生产和生活。

**Article 31** Where rural resettlers are resettled in other counties within the same province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the local people's government that signed the resettlement agreement with the project legal person shall promptly deliver the corresponding funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement to the county people's government of the resettlement area for use in arranging the production and living of the resettlers.  
Where rural resettlers are resettled across provinces, the project legal person shall promptly deliver the corresponding funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government of the resettlement area for use in arranging the production and living of the resettlers.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　搬迁费以及移民个人房屋和附属建筑物、个人所有的零星树木、青苗、农副业设施等个人财产补偿费，由移民区县级人民政府直接全额兑付给移民。

**Article 32** The relocation expenses and compensation fees for resettlers' personal property, such as their houses and auxiliary buildings, individually owned scattered trees, young crops, and agricultural and sideline facilities, shall be directly paid in full by the county people's government of the resettlement source area to the resettlers.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　移民自愿投亲靠友的，应当由本人向移民区县级人民政府提出申请，并提交接收地县级人民政府出具的接收证明；移民区县级人民政府确认其具有土地等农业生产资料后，应当与接收地县级人民政府和移民共同签订协议，将土地补偿费、安置补助费交给接收地县级人民政府，统筹安排移民的生产和生活，将个人财产补偿费和搬迁费发给移民个人。

**Article 33** Where resettlers voluntarily choose to move to relatives or friends, they shall submit an application to the county people's government of the resettlement source area and provide an acceptance certificate issued by the county people's government of the receiving place; after confirming that the resettlers have agricultural means of production such as land, the county people's government of the resettlement source area shall sign an agreement jointly with the county people's government of the receiving place and the resettlers, deliver the land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies to the county people's government of the receiving place for overall planning of the resettlers' production and living, and issue the personal property compensation fees and relocation expenses to the resettlers individually.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　城(集)镇迁建、工矿企业迁建、专项设施迁建或者复建补偿费，由移民区县级以上地方人民政府交给当地人民政府或者有关单位。因扩大规模、提高标准增加的费用，由有关地方人民政府或者有关单位自行解决。

**Article 34** The compensation fees for the relocation of cities (towns), relocation of industrial and mining enterprises, and relocation or reconstruction of special facilities shall be delivered by the local people's government at or above the county level in the resettlement source area to the local people's government or relevant units. The increased expenses due to expansion of scale or raising of standards shall be solved by the relevant local people's government or relevant units themselves.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农村移民集中安置的农村居民点应当按照经批准的移民安置规划确定的规模和标准迁建。  
农村移民集中安置的农村居民点的道路、供水、供电等基础设施，由乡(镇)、村统一组织建设。  
农村移民住房，应当由移民自主建造。有关地方人民政府或者村民委员会应当统一规划宅基地，但不得强行规定建房标准。

**Article 35** Rural settlements where rural resettlers are centrally resettled shall be relocated in accordance with the scale and standards determined in the approved resettlement plan.  
The infrastructure such as roads, water supply, and power supply for rural settlements where rural resettlers are centrally resettled shall be uniformly organized and constructed by townships (towns) and villages.  
The housing of rural resettlers shall be built independently by the resettlers themselves. The relevant local people's government or the villagers' committee shall uniformly plan the residential land, but shall not forcibly prescribe housing construction standards.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　农村移民安置用地应当依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国农村土地承包法》办理有关手续。

**Article 36** The land use for resettlement of rural resettlers shall undergo relevant procedures in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Rural Land Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　移民安置达到阶段性目标和移民安置工作完毕后，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院移民管理机构应当组织有关单位进行验收；移民安置未经验收或者验收不合格的，不得对大中型水利水电工程进行阶段性验收和竣工验收。

**Article 37** After the resettlement work reaches phased objectives and upon completion of the resettlement work, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council shall organize relevant units to carry out acceptance inspection; where the resettlement work has not been inspected or has failed the inspection, no phased acceptance or final acceptance of the large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project shall be conducted.

### 第五章　后期扶持

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府应当编制水库移民后期扶持规划，报上一级人民政府或者其移民管理机构批准后实施。  
编制水库移民后期扶持规划应当广泛听取移民的意见；必要时，应当采取听证的方式。  
经批准的水库移民后期扶持规划是水库移民后期扶持工作的基本依据，应当严格执行，不得随意调整或者修改；确需调整或者修改的，应当报原批准机关批准。  
未编制水库移民后期扶持规划或者水库移民后期扶持规划未经批准，有关单位不得拨付水库移民后期扶持资金。

**Article 38** The local people's government at or above the county level in the resettlement area shall prepare a plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, and submit it to the people's government at the next higher level or its resettlement administration department for approval before implementation.  
In preparing the plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, opinions of resettlers shall be extensively heard; where necessary, hearings shall be held.  
The approved plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers shall be the basic basis for the later-stage support work for reservoir resettlers, and shall be strictly implemented without arbitrary adjustment or modification; where adjustment or modification is truly necessary, approval from the original approving authority shall be obtained.  
Where no plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers has been prepared or the plan has not been approved, relevant units shall not allocate funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　水库移民后期扶持规划应当包括后期扶持的范围、期限、具体措施和预期达到的目标等内容。水库移民安置区县级以上地方人民政府应当采取建立责任制等有效措施，做好后期扶持规划的落实工作。

**Article 39** The plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers shall include such contents as the scope, duration, specific measures, and expected objectives of later-stage support. The local people's government at or above the county level in the reservoir resettlement area shall adopt effective measures such as establishing a responsibility system to ensure the implementation of the later-stage support plan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　水库移民后期扶持资金应当按照水库移民后期扶持规划，主要作为生产生活补助发放给移民个人；必要时可以实行项目扶持，用于解决移民村生产生活中存在的突出问题，或者采取生产生活补助和项目扶持相结合的方式。具体扶持标准、期限和资金的筹集、使用管理依照国务院有关规定执行。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国家规定的原则，结合本行政区域实际情况，制定水库移民后期扶持具体实施办法，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 40** The funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers shall, in accordance with the plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, be mainly distributed to individual resettlers as production and living subsidies; where necessary, project support may be implemented to solve prominent problems in production and living in resettlers' villages, or a combination of production and living subsidies and project support may be adopted. The specific support standards, duration, and the raising, use, and management of funds shall be in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the principles prescribed by the State and in light of the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, formulate specific implementation measures for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级人民政府应当加强移民安置区的交通、能源、水利、环保、通信、文化、教育、卫生、广播电视等基础设施建设，扶持移民安置区发展。  
移民安置区地方人民政府应当将水库移民后期扶持纳入本级人民政府国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 41** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the construction of infrastructure such as transportation, energy, water conservancy, environmental protection, telecommunications, culture, education, health, and radio and television in the resettlement area, and support the development of the resettlement area.  
The local people's government of the resettlement area shall incorporate the later-stage support for reservoir resettlers into the national economic and social development plan of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家在移民安置区和大中型水利水电工程受益地区兴办的生产建设项目，应当优先吸收符合条件的移民就业。

**Article 42** The production and construction projects initiated by the State in the resettlement area and the areas benefiting from large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects shall give priority to employing eligible resettlers.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　大中型水利水电工程建成后形成的水面和水库消落区土地属于国家所有，由该工程管理单位负责管理，并可以在服从水库统一调度和保证工程安全、符合水土保持和水质保护要求的前提下，通过当地县级人民政府优先安排给当地农村移民使用。

**Article 43** The water surface and the land in the reservoir drawdown area formed after the completion of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project shall be owned by the State, and shall be managed by the project management unit; and, subject to compliance with the unified operation of the reservoir, ensuring project safety, and meeting the requirements of soil and water conservation and water quality protection, such land may, through the local county people's government, be given priority to local rural resettlers for use.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家在安排基本农田和水利建设资金时，应当对移民安置区所在县优先予以扶持。

**Article 44** When allocating funds for basic farmland and water conservancy construction, the State shall give priority support to the county where the resettlement area is located.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强对移民的科学文化知识和实用技术的培训，加强法制宣传教育，提高移民素质，增强移民就业能力。

**Article 45** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall strengthen the training of resettlers in scientific and cultural knowledge and practical skills, strengthen legal education, improve the quality of resettlers, and enhance their employability.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　大中型水利水电工程受益地区的各级地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照优势互补、互惠互利、长期合作、共同发展的原则，采取多种形式对移民安置区给予支持。

**Article 46** The local people's governments at all levels in the areas benefiting from large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the principles of complementary advantages, mutual benefit, long-term cooperation, and common development, adopt various forms to provide support to the resettlement area.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家对移民安置和水库移民后期扶持实行全过程监督。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院移民管理机构应当加强对移民安置和水库移民后期扶持的监督，发现问题应当及时采取措施。

**Article 47** The State shall exercise supervision over the entire process of resettlement and later-stage support for reservoir resettlers. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the Resettlement Administration Department of the State Council shall strengthen supervision over resettlement and later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, and shall take timely measures upon discovery of problems.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家对征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金的拨付、使用和管理实行稽察制度，对拨付、使用和管理征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金的有关地方人民政府及其有关部门的负责人依法实行任期经济责任审计。

**Article 48** The State shall implement an inspection and audit system for the allocation, use, and management of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement, and funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, and shall carry out tenure economic responsibility audits according to law on the persons in charge of the relevant local people's governments and their relevant departments that allocate, use, and manage the funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and the funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对下级人民政府及其财政、发展改革、移民等有关部门或者机构拨付、使用和管理征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金的监督。  
县级以上地方人民政府或者其移民管理机构应当加强对征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金的管理，定期向上一级人民政府或者其移民管理机构报告并向项目法人通报有关资金拨付、使用和管理情况。

**Article 49** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the allocation, use, and management of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers by the people's governments at lower levels and their relevant departments or institutions, such as finance, development and reform, and resettlement.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level or their resettlement administration departments shall strengthen the management of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, regularly report to the people's governments at the next higher level or their resettlement administration departments, and notify the project legal person of the relevant allocation, use, and management of the funds.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级审计、监察机关应当依法加强对征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金拨付、使用和管理情况的审计和监察。  
县级以上人民政府财政部门应当加强对征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金拨付、使用和管理情况的监督。  
审计、监察机关和财政部门进行审计、监察和监督时，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，及时提供有关资料。

**Article 50** The audit and supervisory organs at all levels shall, according to law, strengthen the audit and supervision of the allocation, use, and management of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers.  
The finance departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the allocation, use, and management of funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers.  
When the audit and supervisory organs and the finance departments carry out audits, supervision, and oversight, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and provide relevant materials in a timely manner.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家对移民安置实行全过程监督评估。签订移民安置协议的地方人民政府和项目法人应当采取招标的方式，共同委托移民安置监督评估单位对移民搬迁进度、移民安置质量、移民资金的拨付和使用情况以及移民生活水平的恢复情况进行监督评估；被委托方应当将监督评估的情况及时向委托方报告。

**Article 51** The State shall implement supervision and evaluation over the entire process of resettlement. The local people's government that signed the resettlement agreement and the project legal person shall, through bidding, jointly entrust a resettlement supervision and evaluation unit to supervise and evaluate the progress of resettlement relocation, the quality of resettlement, the allocation and use of resettlement funds, and the restoration of resettlers' living standards; the entrusted party shall promptly report the supervision and evaluation results to the entrusting party.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　征地补偿和移民安置资金应当专户存储、专账核算，存储期间的孳息，应当纳入征地补偿和移民安置资金，不得挪作他用。

**Article 52** Funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement shall be deposited in a dedicated account and accounted for in a special ledger, and the interest accrued during the deposit period shall be included in the funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　移民区和移民安置区县级人民政府，应当以村为单位将大中型水利水电工程征收的土地数量、土地种类和实物调查结果、补偿范围、补偿标准和金额以及安置方案等向群众公布。群众提出异议的，县级人民政府应当及时核查，并对统计调查结果不准确的事项进行改正；经核查无误的，应当及时向群众解释。  
有移民安置任务的乡(镇)、村应当建立健全征地补偿和移民安置资金的财务管理制度，并将征地补偿和移民安置资金收支情况张榜公布，接受群众监督；土地补偿费和集体财产补偿费的使用方案应当经村民会议或者村民代表会议讨论通过。  
移民安置区乡(镇)人民政府、村(居)民委员会应当采取有效措施帮助移民适应当地的生产、生活，及时调处矛盾纠纷。

**Article 53** The county people's governments in the resettlement source area and the resettlement area shall, on a village basis, make public the quantity and types of land expropriated for large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydropower projects, the results of physical inventory surveys, the scope of compensation, the compensation standards and amounts, and the resettlement plan. Where the masses raise objections, the county people's government shall promptly verify and correct any inaccuracies in the statistical survey results; where the verification shows no errors, it shall promptly explain to the masses.  
Townships (towns) and villages with resettlement tasks shall establish and improve the financial management system for funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement, and shall publicly post the revenues and expenditures of the funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement for public supervision; the plan for the use of land compensation fees and collective property compensation fees shall be discussed and adopted by the villagers' meeting or the villagers' representative meeting.  
The township (town) people's government and the village (neighborhood) committee in the resettlement area shall take effective measures to help resettlers adapt to local production and living, and promptly mediate and resolve conflicts and disputes.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府或者其移民管理机构以及项目法人应当建立移民工作档案，并按照国家有关规定进行管理。

**Article 54** The local people's governments at or above the county level or their resettlement administration departments, as well as the project legal person, shall establish resettlement work archives and manage them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家切实维护移民的合法权益。  
在征地补偿和移民安置过程中，移民认为其合法权益受到侵害的，可以依法向县级以上人民政府或者其移民管理机构反映，县级以上人民政府或者其移民管理机构应当对移民反映的问题进行核实并妥善解决。移民也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。  
移民安置后，移民与移民安置区当地居民享有同等的权利，承担同等的义务。

**Article 55** The State shall earnestly safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of resettlers.  
During the process of land acquisition compensation and resettlement, where resettlers believe that their legitimate rights and interests have been infringed upon, they may report to the people's government at or above the county level or its resettlement administration department according to law, and the people's government at or above the county level or its resettlement administration department shall verify the issues reported by resettlers and properly resolve them. Resettlers may also file a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law.  
After resettlement, resettlers shall enjoy equal rights and assume equal obligations with the local residents of the resettlement area.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　按照移民安置规划必须搬迁的移民，无正当理由不得拖延搬迁或者拒迁。已经安置的移民不得返迁。

**Article 56** Resettlers who must relocate in accordance with the resettlement plan shall not delay or refuse to relocate without justifiable reasons. Resettlers who have been resettled shall not move back.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，有关地方人民政府、移民管理机构、项目审批部门及其他有关部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；造成严重后果，有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反规定批准移民安置规划大纲、移民安置规划或者水库移民后期扶持规划的；  
(二)违反规定批准或者核准未编制移民安置规划或者移民安置规划未经审核的大中型水利水电工程建设项目的；  
(三)移民安置未经验收或者验收不合格而对大中型水利水电工程进行阶段性验收或者竣工验收的；  
(四)未编制水库移民后期扶持规划，有关单位拨付水库移民后期扶持资金的；  
(五)移民安置管理、监督和组织实施过程中发现违法行为不予查处的；  
(六)在移民安置过程中发现问题不及时处理，造成严重后果以及有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守等违法行为的。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the relevant local people's government, resettlement administration department, project approval department, or other relevant department commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if serious consequences are caused and the responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Approving the resettlement planning outline, resettlement plan, or plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers in violation of regulations;  
(2) Approving or authorizing a construction project of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project that has not prepared a resettlement plan or whose resettlement plan has not been reviewed, in violation of regulations;  
(3) Conducting phased acceptance or final acceptance of a large or medium-sized water conservancy or hydropower project when the resettlement work has not been accepted or has failed acceptance;  
(4) Allocating funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers by relevant units when no plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers has been prepared;  
(5) Failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered during the management, supervision, and organized implementation of resettlement;  
(6) Failing to deal with problems discovered during the resettlement process in a timely manner, causing serious consequences, or committing other illegal acts such as abuse of power or dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，项目主管部门或者有关地方人民政府及其有关部门调整或者修改移民安置规划大纲、移民安置规划或者水库移民后期扶持规划的，由批准该规划大纲、规划的有关人民政府或者其有关部门、机构责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；造成重大损失，有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，项目法人调整或者修改移民安置规划大纲、移民安置规划的，由批准该规划大纲、规划的有关人民政府或者其有关部门、机构责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成重大损失，有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the project competent department or the relevant local people's government or its relevant department adjusts or modifies the resettlement planning outline, resettlement plan, or plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, the relevant people's government or its relevant department or institution that approved the planning outline or plan shall order correction, and the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if heavy losses are caused and the responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, the project legal person adjusts or modifies the resettlement planning outline or resettlement plan, the relevant people's government or its relevant department or institution that approved the planning outline or plan shall order correction, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan on the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons; if heavy losses are caused and the responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，在编制移民安置规划大纲、移民安置规划、水库移民后期扶持规划，或者进行实物调查、移民安置监督评估中弄虚作假的，由批准该规划大纲、规划的有关人民政府或者其有关部门、机构责令改正，对有关单位处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of these Regulations, fraudulent acts are committed in the preparation of the resettlement planning outline, resettlement plan, or plan for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, or in conducting physical inventory surveys or resettlement supervision and evaluation, the relevant people's government or its relevant department or institution that approved the planning outline or plan shall order correction, impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan on the relevant units; impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan on the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons; if losses are caused to others, liability for compensation shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，侵占、截留、挪用征地补偿和移民安置资金、水库移民后期扶持资金的，责令退赔，并处侵占、截留、挪用资金额3倍以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究有关责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any unit or individual embezzles, intercepts, or misappropriates funds for land acquisition compensation and resettlement or funds for later-stage support for reservoir resettlers, the funds shall be ordered to be returned or compensated, and a fine of not more than three times the amount of funds embezzled, intercepted, or misappropriated shall be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, the criminal liability of the relevant responsible persons shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，拖延搬迁或者拒迁的，当地人民政府或者其移民管理机构可以申请人民法院强制执行；违反治安管理法律、法规的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究有关责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any resettler delays or refuses to relocate, the local people's government or its resettlement administration department may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution; if the provisions of laws and regulations on public security administration are violated, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, the criminal liability of the relevant responsible persons shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　长江三峡工程的移民工作，依照《长江三峡工程建设移民条例》执行。  
南水北调工程的征地补偿和移民安置工作，依照本条例执行。但是，南水北调工程中线、东线一期工程的移民安置规划的编制审批，依照国务院的规定执行。

**Article 62** The resettlement work for the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River shall be carried out in accordance with the Regulations on Resettlement for the Construction of the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River.  
The land acquisition compensation and resettlement work for the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be carried out in accordance with these Regulations. However, the preparation and approval of the resettlement plan for the middle route and the first phase of the eastern route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例自2006年9月1日起施行。1991年2月15日国务院发布的《大中型水利水电工程建设征地补偿和移民安置条例》同时废止。

**Article 63** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2006. The Regulations on Land Acquisition Compensation and Resettlement for the Construction of Large and Medium-sized Water Conservancy and Hydropower Projects promulgated by the State Council on February 15, 1991 shall be abolished at the same time.
