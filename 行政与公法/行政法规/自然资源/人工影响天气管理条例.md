# 人工影响天气管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-03-27  
**生效日期**：2020-03-27

> (2002年3月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第348号公布　根据2020年3月27日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强对人工影响天气工作的管理，防御和减轻气象灾害，根据《中华人民共和国气象法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事人工影响天气活动，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　本条例所称人工影响天气，是指为避免或者减轻气象灾害，合理利用气候资源，在适当条件下通过科技手段对局部大气的物理、化学过程进行人工影响，实现增雨雪、防雹、消雨、消雾、防霜等目的的活动。 第四条　人工影响天气工作按照作业规模和影响范围，在作业地县级以上地方人民政府的领导和协调下，由气象主管机构组织实施和指导管理。 第五条　开展人工影响天气工作，应当制定人工影响天气工作计划。人工影响天气工作计划由有关地方气象主管机构商同级有关部门编制，报本级人民政府批准后实施。 按照有关人民政府批准的人工影响天气工作计划开展的人工影响天气工作属于公益性事业，所需经费列入该级人民政府的财政预算。 第六条　组织实施人工影响天气作业，应当具备适宜的天气气候条件，充分考虑当地防灾减灾的需要和作业效果。 第七条　国家鼓励和支持人工影响天气科学技术研究，推广使用先进技术。 县级以上地方人民政府应当组织专家对人工影响天气作业的效果进行评估，并根据评估结果，对提供决策依据的有关单位给予奖惩。 第八条　人工影响天气的作业地点，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构根据当地气候特点、地理条件，依照《中华人民共和国民用航空法》、《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》的有关规定，会同有关飞行管制部门确定。 第九条　从事人工影响天气作业的单位，应当符合省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构规定的条件。 第十条　人工影响天气作业单位应当按照国务院气象主管机构制定的人工影响天气作业人员培训标准对从事人工影响天气作业的人员进行岗前培训。人工影响天气作业人员应当掌握相关作业规范和操作规程后，方可实施人工影响天气作业。 利用高射炮、火箭发射装置从事人工影响天气作业的人员名单，由所在地的气象主管机构抄送当地公安机关备案。 第十一条　利用高射炮、火箭发射装置实施人工影响天气作业，由作业地的县级以上地方气象主管机构向有关飞行管制部门申请空域和作业时限。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对人工影响天气工作的管理，防御和减轻气象灾害，根据《中华人民共和国气象法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Meteorological Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the administration of weather modification activities, and preventing and mitigating meteorological disasters.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事人工影响天气活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any entity or individual that engages in weather modification activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称人工影响天气，是指为避免或者减轻气象灾害，合理利用气候资源，在适当条件下通过科技手段对局部大气的物理、化学过程进行人工影响，实现增雨雪、防雹、消雨、消雾、防霜等目的的活动。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, "weather modification" means activities that, in order to avoid or mitigate meteorological disasters and rationally utilize climate resources, artificially influence the physical and chemical processes of the local atmosphere through scientific and technological means under appropriate conditions, so as to achieve the purposes of increasing precipitation or snowfall, preventing hail, dissipating rain, dissipating fog, preventing frost, etc.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人工影响天气工作按照作业规模和影响范围，在作业地县级以上地方人民政府的领导和协调下，由气象主管机构组织实施和指导管理。

**Article 4** Weather modification work shall, based on the scale and scope of influence of the operations, be organized, implemented, directed and managed by the competent meteorological authorities under the leadership and coordination of the local people's government at or above the county level in the operation area.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　开展人工影响天气工作，应当制定人工影响天气工作计划。人工影响天气工作计划由有关地方气象主管机构商同级有关部门编制，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
按照有关人民政府批准的人工影响天气工作计划开展的人工影响天气工作属于公益性事业，所需经费列入该级人民政府的财政预算。

**Article 5** To carry out weather modification work, a weather modification work plan shall be formulated. The weather modification work plan shall be drawn up by the relevant local competent meteorological authority in consultation with the relevant departments at the same level, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval before implementation.  
Weather modification work carried out in accordance with the weather modification work plan approved by the relevant people's government shall be a public welfare undertaking, and the necessary funds shall be included in the financial budget of that people's government.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　组织实施人工影响天气作业，应当具备适宜的天气气候条件，充分考虑当地防灾减灾的需要和作业效果。

**Article 6** The organization and implementation of weather modification operations shall be carried out under suitable weather and climatic conditions, with full consideration given to the local needs for disaster prevention and mitigation and the effects of the operations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励和支持人工影响天气科学技术研究，推广使用先进技术。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织专家对人工影响天气作业的效果进行评估，并根据评估结果，对提供决策依据的有关单位给予奖惩。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on weather modification, and promotes the use of advanced technologies.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize experts to evaluate the effects of weather modification operations, and, based on the evaluation results, reward or punish the relevant entities that have provided decision-making basis.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人工影响天气的作业地点，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构根据当地气候特点、地理条件，依照《中华人民共和国民用航空法》、《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》的有关规定，会同有关飞行管制部门确定。

**Article 8** The operation sites for weather modification shall be determined by the competent meteorological authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, in conjunction with the relevant flight control departments, in accordance with the local climatic characteristics and geographical conditions, and in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China and the Basic Rules for Flights of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事人工影响天气作业的单位，应当符合省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构规定的条件。

**Article 9** The entities engaged in weather modification operations shall meet the conditions prescribed by the competent meteorological authorities of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人工影响天气作业单位应当按照国务院气象主管机构制定的人工影响天气作业人员培训标准对从事人工影响天气作业的人员进行岗前培训。人工影响天气作业人员应当掌握相关作业规范和操作规程后，方可实施人工影响天气作业。  
利用高射炮、火箭发射装置从事人工影响天气作业的人员名单，由所在地的气象主管机构抄送当地公安机关备案。

**Article 10** The weather modification operation entities shall, in accordance with the training standards for weather modification operators formulated by the competent meteorological authority of the State Council, provide pre-service training for personnel engaged in weather modification operations. Weather modification operators may not carry out weather modification operations until they have mastered the relevant operation specifications and procedures.  
The list of personnel who engage in weather modification operations using anti-aircraft guns or rocket launchers shall be copied by the local competent meteorological authority to the local public security organ for the record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　利用高射炮、火箭发射装置实施人工影响天气作业，由作业地的县级以上地方气象主管机构向有关飞行管制部门申请空域和作业时限。  
利用飞机实施人工影响天气作业，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构向有关飞行管制部门申请空域和作业时限；所需飞机由军队或者民航部门按照供需双方协商确定的方式提供；机场管理机构及有关单位应当根据人工影响天气工作计划做好保障工作。  
有关飞行管制部门接到申请后，应当及时作出决定并通知申请人。

**Article 11** Where anti-aircraft guns or rocket launchers are used to conduct weather modification operations, the local meteorological authority at or above the county level of the operation site shall apply to the relevant flight control department for the airspace and operation time limit.  
Where aircraft are used to conduct weather modification operations, the meteorological authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall apply to the relevant flight control department for the airspace and operation time limit; the required aircraft shall be provided by the military or civil aviation department in accordance with the method agreed upon by the supply and demand parties through consultation; the airport management authority and relevant entities shall, in accordance with the weather modification work plan, provide necessary support.  
The relevant flight control department shall, upon receiving the application, make a decision promptly and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　实施人工影响天气作业，必须在批准的空域和作业时限内，严格按照国务院气象主管机构规定的作业规范和操作规程进行，并接受县级以上地方气象主管机构的指挥、管理和监督，确保作业安全。  
实施人工影响天气作业，作业地的气象主管机构应当根据具体情况提前公告，并通知当地公安机关做好安全保卫工作。

**Article 12** Weather modification operations shall be conducted strictly within the approved airspace and operation time limit, in accordance with the operation specifications and procedures prescribed by the meteorological authority under the State Council, and shall be subject to the command, management, and supervision of the local meteorological authority at or above the county level to ensure operational safety.  
When conducting weather modification operations, the meteorological authority of the operation site shall make a public announcement in advance based on the specific circumstances and shall notify the local public security organs to carry out security and protection work.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　作业地气象台站应当及时无偿提供实施人工影响天气作业所需的气象探测资料、情报、预报。  
农业农村、水利、自然资源、应急管理、林业和草原等有关部门应当及时无偿提供实施人工影响天气作业所需的灾情、水文、火情等资料。

**Article 13** The meteorological stations in the operation area shall provide, in a timely manner and free of charge, the meteorological observation data, information, and forecasts required for weather modification operations.  
The relevant departments of agriculture and rural affairs, water resources, natural resources, emergency management, forestry and grassland, etc., shall provide, in a timely manner and free of charge, the disaster, hydrological, fire, and other data required for weather modification operations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　需要跨省、自治区、直辖市实施人工影响天气作业的，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府协商确定；协商不成的，由国务院气象主管机构商有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 14** Where weather modification operations need to be conducted across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the relevant people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall determine the matter through consultation; if consultation fails, the meteorological authority under the State Council shall determine the matter in consultation with the relevant people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　实施人工影响天气作业使用的火箭发射装置、炮弹、火箭弹，由国务院气象主管机构和有关部门共同指定的企业按照国家有关强制性技术标准和要求组织生产。  
因作业需要采购前款规定设备的，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构按照国家有关政府采购的规定组织采购。

**Article 15** The rocket launchers, projectiles, and rockets used in weather modification operations shall be produced by enterprises jointly designated by the meteorological authority under the State Council and relevant departments in accordance with the relevant national mandatory technical standards and requirements.  
Where equipment specified in the preceding paragraph needs to be purchased for operational needs, the meteorological authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall organize the procurement in accordance with the relevant national provisions on government procurement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　运输、存储人工影响天气作业使用的高射炮、火箭发射装置、炮弹、火箭弹，应当遵守国家有关武器装备、爆炸物品管理的法律、法规。实施人工影响天气作业使用的炮弹、火箭弹，由军队、当地人民武装部协助存储；需要调运的，由有关部门依照国家有关武器装备、爆炸物品管理的法律、法规的规定办理手续。

**Article 16** The transportation and storage of anti-aircraft guns, rocket launchers, projectiles, and rockets used in weather modification operations shall comply with the laws and regulations of the State on the management of weapons, equipment, and explosives. The projectiles and rockets used in weather modification operations shall be stored with the assistance of the military and the local people's armed forces departments; if they need to be transferred, the relevant departments shall go through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of the State's laws and regulations on the management of weapons, equipment, and explosives.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　实施人工影响天气作业使用的高射炮、火箭发射装置，由省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构组织年检；年检不合格的，应当立即进行检修，经检修仍达不到规定的技术标准和要求的，予以报废。

**Article 17** The anti-aircraft guns and rocket launchers used in weather modification operations shall be subject to annual inspection organized by the meteorological authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; those failing the annual inspection shall be immediately repaired; if they still fail to meet the prescribed technical standards and requirements after repair, they shall be scrapped.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止下列行为：  
(一)将人工影响天气作业设备转让给非人工影响天气作业单位或者个人；  
(二)将人工影响天气作业设备用于与人工影响天气无关的活动；  
(三)使用年检不合格、超过有效期或者报废的人工影响天气作业设备。  
人工影响天气作业单位之间转让人工影响天气作业设备的，应当自转让之日起三十日内向有关省、自治区、直辖市气象主管机构备案。

**Article 18** The following acts are prohibited:  
(1) transferring weather modification operation equipment to non-weather modification operation entities or individuals;  
(2) using weather modification operation equipment for activities unrelated to weather modification;  
(3) using weather modification operation equipment that has failed annual inspection, exceeded its validity period, or been scrapped.  
Where weather modification operation equipment is transferred between weather modification operation entities, the transferor shall file a record with the relevant meteorological authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within thirty days from the date of transfer.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一，造成严重后果的，依照刑法关于危险物品肇事罪、重大责任事故罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由有关气象主管机构按照管理权限责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，禁止从事人工影响天气作业；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)违反人工影响天气作业规范或者操作规程的；  
(二)未按照批准的空域和作业时限实施人工影响天气作业的；  
(三)将人工影响天气作业设备转让给非人工影响天气作业单位或者个人的；  
(四)人工影响天气作业单位之间转让人工影响天气作业设备，未按照规定备案的；  
(五)将人工影响天气作业设备用于与人工影响天气无关的活动的。

**Article 19** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, if causing serious consequences, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of causing a hazardous substances accident, the crime of major liability accident, or other crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the relevant meteorological authority shall, within its administrative authority, order correction and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be prohibited from engaging in weather modification operations; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) violating weather modification operation specifications or procedures;  
(2) conducting weather modification operations not in accordance with the approved airspace and operation time limit;  
(3) transferring weather modification operation equipment to non-weather modification operation entities or individuals;  
(4) transferring weather modification operation equipment between weather modification operation entities without filing a record as required;  
(5) using weather modification operation equipment for activities unrelated to weather modification.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，组织实施人工影响天气作业，造成特大安全事故的，对有关主管机构的负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照《国务院关于特大安全事故行政责任追究的规定》处理。

**Article 20** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the organization and implementation of weather modification operations cause a particularly serious safety accident, the persons in charge of the relevant competent authority, the directly responsible supervisors, and other directly responsible persons shall be dealt with in accordance with the Provisions of the State Council on the Investigation of Administrative Liability for Particularly Serious Safety Accidents.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　为军事目的从事人工影响天气活动的具体管理办法，由中央军事委员会制定。

**Article 21** The specific management measures for engaging in weather modification activities for military purposes shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自2002年5月1日起施行。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2002.
