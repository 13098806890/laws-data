# 取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2006-04-15

> (2006年2月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第460号公布　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强水资源管理和保护，促进水资源的节约与合理开发利用，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称取水，是指利用取水工程或者设施直接从江河、湖泊或者地下取用水资源。 取用水资源的单位和个人，除本条例第四条规定的情形外，都应当申请领取取水许可证，并缴纳水资源费。 本条例所称取水工程或者设施，是指闸、坝、渠道、人工河道、虹吸管、水泵、水井以及水电站等。 第三条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门按照分级管理权限，负责取水许可制度的组织实施和监督管理。 国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构(以下简称流域管理机构)，依照本条例规定和国务院水行政主管部门授权，负责所管辖范围内取水许可制度的组织实施和监督管理。 县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、财政部门和价格主管部门依照本条例规定和管理权限，负责水资源费的征收、管理和监督。 第四条　下列情形不需要申请领取取水许可证： (一)农村集体经济组织及其成员使用本集体经济组织的水塘、水库中的水的； (二)家庭生活和零星散养、圈养畜禽饮用等少量取水的； (三)为保障矿井等地下工程施工安全和生产安全必须进行临时应急取(排)水的； (四)为消除对公共安全或者公共利益的危害临时应急取水的； (五)为农业抗旱和维护生态与环境必须临时应急取水的。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强水资源管理和保护，促进水资源的节约与合理开发利用，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the management and protection of water resources, and promoting the conservation and rational development and utilization of water resources.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称取水，是指利用取水工程或者设施直接从江河、湖泊或者地下取用水资源。  
取用水资源的单位和个人，除[本条例第四条](#art-4)规定的情形外，都应当申请领取取水许可证，并缴纳水资源费。  
本条例所称取水工程或者设施，是指闸、坝、渠道、人工河道、虹吸管、水泵、水井以及水电站等。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'water abstraction' means the drawing of water resources directly from rivers, lakes, or underground by means of water abstraction projects or facilities.  
Any unit or individual that abstracts water resources shall, except under the circumstances prescribed in Article 4 of these Regulations, apply for and obtain a water abstraction permit and pay water resources fees.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'water abstraction projects or facilities' means sluice gates, dams, channels, artificial rivers, siphons, pumps, wells, and hydropower stations, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门按照分级管理权限，负责取水许可制度的组织实施和监督管理。  
国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构(以下简称流域管理机构)，依照本条例规定和国务院水行政主管部门授权，负责所管辖范围内取水许可制度的组织实施和监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、财政部门和价格主管部门依照本条例规定和管理权限，负责水资源费的征收、管理和监督。

**Article 3** The water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their hierarchical management authority, be responsible for the organization, implementation, and supervision of the water abstraction permit system.  
The basin management agencies established by the water administrative department of the State Council in important rivers and lakes designated by the State (hereinafter referred to as 'basin management agencies') shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and the authorization of the water administrative department of the State Council, be responsible for the organization, implementation, and supervision of the water abstraction permit system within their jurisdiction.  
The water administrative departments, finance departments, and price authorities of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their management authority, be responsible for the collection, management, and supervision of water resources fees.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　下列情形不需要申请领取取水许可证：  
(一)农村集体经济组织及其成员使用本集体经济组织的水塘、水库中的水的；  
(二)家庭生活和零星散养、圈养畜禽饮用等少量取水的；  
(三)为保障矿井等地下工程施工安全和生产安全必须进行临时应急取(排)水的；  
(四)为消除对公共安全或者公共利益的危害临时应急取水的；  
(五)为农业抗旱和维护生态与环境必须临时应急取水的。  
前款第(二)项规定的少量取水的限额，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定；第(三)项、第(四)项规定的取水，应当及时报县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构备案；第(五)项规定的取水，应当经县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构同意。

**Article 4** No application for a water abstraction permit is required in the following circumstances:  
(1) A rural collective economic organization or its members use water from ponds or reservoirs belonging to the said collective economic organization;  
(2) Small amounts of water are abstracted for domestic use and drinking by scatter-feeding or pen-feeding livestock and poultry;  
(3) Temporary emergency abstraction (or discharge) of water must be carried out to ensure the safety of underground engineering construction such as mines and production safety;  
(4) Temporary emergency abstraction of water is carried out to eliminate hazards to public safety or public interests;  
(5) Temporary emergency abstraction of water must be carried out for agricultural drought relief and maintenance of ecology and environment.  
The limit for small amounts of water abstraction as provided in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; water abstraction under subparagraphs (3) and (4) of the preceding paragraph shall be promptly reported to the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the basin management agency for record; water abstraction under subparagraph (5) of the preceding paragraph shall be subject to the consent of the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the basin management agency.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　取水许可应当首先满足城乡居民生活用水，并兼顾农业、工业、生态与环境用水以及航运等需要。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以依照本条例规定的职责权限，在同一流域或者区域内，根据实际情况对前款各项用水规定具体的先后顺序。

**Article 5** The water abstraction permit shall first meet the domestic water needs of urban and rural residents, with due consideration given to agricultural, industrial, ecological and environmental water uses, as well as navigation needs.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with their functions and powers prescribed in these Regulations, prescribe the specific order of priority for the various types of water use mentioned in the preceding paragraph within the same basin or region based on actual circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　实施取水许可必须符合水资源综合规划、流域综合规划、水中长期供求规划和水功能区划，遵守依照《中华人民共和国水法》规定批准的水量分配方案；尚未制定水量分配方案的，应当遵守有关地方人民政府间签订的协议。

**Article 6** The implementation of water abstraction permits must conform to the comprehensive water resources plan, the comprehensive basin plan, the medium- and long-term water supply and demand plan, and the water function zoning, and comply with the water allocation scheme approved in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China; where no water allocation scheme has been formulated, it shall comply with the agreements concluded between relevant local people's governments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实施取水许可应当坚持地表水与地下水统筹考虑，开源与节流相结合、节流优先的原则，实行总量控制与定额管理相结合。  
流域内批准取水的总耗水量不得超过本流域水资源可利用量。  
行政区域内批准取水的总水量，不得超过流域管理机构或者上一级水行政主管部门下达的可供本行政区域取用的水量；其中，批准取用地下水的总水量，不得超过本行政区域地下水可开采量，并应当符合地下水开发利用规划的要求。制定地下水开发利用规划应当征求国土资源主管部门的意见。

**Article 7** The implementation of water abstraction permits shall adhere to the principle of overall consideration of surface water and groundwater, the combination of source development and conservation with priority given to conservation, and the integration of gross control and quota management.  
The total amount of water consumed for approved abstractions within a basin shall not exceed the available amount of water resources in that basin.  
The total amount of water approved for abstraction within an administrative region shall not exceed the amount of water that the basin management agency or the water administrative department at the next higher level has allocated for abstraction in that administrative region; and the total amount of approved groundwater abstraction shall not exceed the exploitable amount of groundwater in that administrative region and shall comply with the requirements of the groundwater development and utilization plan. When formulating a groundwater development and utilization plan, the opinions of the competent department of land and resources shall be solicited.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　取水许可和水资源费征收管理制度的实施应当遵循公开、公平、公正、高效和便民的原则。

**Article 8** The implementation of the water abstraction permit and water resources fee collection management systems shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, impartiality, efficiency, and convenience for the people.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都有节约和保护水资源的义务。  
对节约和保护水资源有突出贡献的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** All units and individuals have the obligation to conserve and protect water resources.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to water conservation and protection shall be commended and rewarded by the people's governments at or above the county level.

### 第二章　取水的申请和受理

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请取水的单位或者个人(以下简称申请人)，应当向具有审批权限的审批机关提出申请。申请利用多种水源，且各种水源的取水许可审批机关不同的，应当向其中最高一级审批机关提出申请。  
取水许可权限属于流域管理机构的，应当向取水口所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门，应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内提出意见，并连同全部申请材料转报流域管理机构；流域管理机构收到后，应当依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)的规定作出处理。

**Article 10** The unit or individual applying for water abstraction (hereinafter referred to as the 'applicant') shall submit an application to the examination and approval authority with the authority to examine and approve. If the application involves multiple water sources and the authorities responsible for examining and approving water abstraction permits for each water source are different, the application shall be submitted to the highest authority among them.  
If the authority over water abstraction permits belongs to a basin management agency, the application shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the intake is located. The water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 20 working days from the date of receiving the application, provide its opinions and forward all application materials to the basin management agency; upon receipt, the basin management agency shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请取水应当提交下列材料：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)与第三者利害关系的相关说明；  
(三)属于备案项目的，提供有关备案材料；  
(四)国务院水行政主管部门规定的其他材料。  
建设项目需要取水的，申请人还应当提交建设项目水资源论证报告书。论证报告书应当包括取水水源、用水合理性以及对生态与环境的影响等内容。

**Article 11** To apply for water abstraction, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) an application form;  
(2) relevant explanations regarding the interests of third parties;  
(3) if the project is subject to recordation, the relevant recordation materials;  
(4) other materials prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
If a construction project requires water abstraction, the applicant shall also submit a water resources demonstration report for the construction project. The demonstration report shall include the water source, the rationality of water use, and the impact on ecology and environment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请书应当包括下列事项：  
(一)申请人的名称(姓名)、地址；  
(二)申请理由；  
(三)取水的起始时间及期限；  
(四)取水目的、取水量、年内各月的用水量等；  
(五)水源及取水地点；  
(六)取水方式、计量方式和节水措施；  
(七)退水地点和退水中所含主要污染物以及污水处理措施；  
(八)国务院水行政主管部门规定的其他事项。

**Article 12** The application form shall include the following particulars:  
(1) the name (or title) and address of the applicant;  
(2) the reasons for the application;  
(3) the starting time and term of water abstraction;  
(4) the purpose of water abstraction, the amount of water to be abstracted, the amount of water to be used in each month of the year, etc.;  
(5) the water source and the location of water abstraction;  
(6) the method of water abstraction, the metering method, and water conservation measures;  
(7) the location of water discharge, the main pollutants contained in the discharged water, and the wastewater treatment measures;  
(8) other matters prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构，应当自收到取水申请之日起5个工作日内对申请材料进行审查，并根据下列不同情形分别作出处理：  
(一)申请材料齐全、符合法定形式、属于本机关受理范围的，予以受理；  
(二)提交的材料不完备或者申请书内容填注不明的，通知申请人补正；  
(三)不属于本机关受理范围的，告知申请人向有受理权限的机关提出申请。

**Article 13** The water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the basin management agency shall, within 5 working days from the date of receiving the water abstraction application, examine the application materials and handle them respectively in accordance with the following different circumstances:  
(1) if the application materials are complete, conform to the statutory form, and fall within the scope of acceptance of the authority, the application shall be accepted;  
(2) if the submitted materials are incomplete or the contents of the application form are unclear, the applicant shall be notified to make corrections;  
(3) if the application does not fall within the scope of acceptance of the authority, the applicant shall be informed to submit the application to the authority with acceptance jurisdiction.

### 第三章　取水许可的审查和决定

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　取水许可实行分级审批。  
下列取水由流域管理机构审批：  
(一)长江、黄河、淮河、海河、滦河、珠江、松花江、辽河、金沙江、汉江的干流和太湖以及其他跨省、自治区、直辖市河流、湖泊的指定河段限额以上的取水；  
(二)国际跨界河流的指定河段和国际边界河流限额以上的取水；  
(三)省际边界河流、湖泊限额以上的取水；  
(四)跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域的取水；  
(五)由国务院或者国务院投资主管部门审批、核准的大型建设项目的取水；  
(六)流域管理机构直接管理的河道(河段)、湖泊内的取水。  
前款所称的指定河段和限额以及流域管理机构直接管理的河道(河段)、湖泊，由国务院水行政主管部门规定。  
其他取水由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的审批权限审批。

**Article 14** Water abstraction permits shall be examined and approved at different levels.  
The following water abstractions shall be examined and approved by the basin management agencies:  
(1) water abstractions above the quota from the main streams of the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Huaihe River, Haihe River, Luanhe River, Zhujiang River, Songhuajiang River, Liaohe River, Jinshajiang River, Hanjiang River, and Taihu Lake, as well as from designated reaches of other rivers and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) water abstractions above the quota from designated reaches of international transboundary rivers and international boundary rivers;  
(3) water abstractions above the quota from rivers and lakes on provincial boundaries;  
(4) water abstractions across the administrative regions of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) water abstractions for large construction projects examined and approved by the State Council or the competent investment department of the State Council;  
(6) water abstractions from watercourses (reaches) and lakes directly managed by the basin management agencies.  
The designated reaches, quotas, and the watercourses (reaches) and lakes directly managed by the basin management agencies as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
Other water abstractions shall be examined and approved by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the examination and approval authority prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　批准的水量分配方案或者签订的协议是确定流域与行政区域取水许可总量控制的依据。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市的江河、湖泊，尚未制定水量分配方案或者尚未签订协议的，有关省、自治区、直辖市的取水许可总量控制指标，由流域管理机构根据流域水资源条件，依据水资源综合规划、流域综合规划和水中长期供求规划，结合各省、自治区、直辖市取水现状及供需情况，商有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门提出，报国务院水行政主管部门批准；设区的市、县(市)行政区域的取水许可总量控制指标，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门依据本省、自治区、直辖市取水许可总量控制指标，结合各地取水现状及供需情况制定，并报流域管理机构备案。

**Article 15** The approved water allocation scheme or the concluded agreement shall be the basis for determining the total amount control of water abstraction permits in basins and administrative regions.  
For rivers and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, if no water allocation scheme has been formulated or no agreement has been concluded, the total amount control indicators for water abstraction permits for the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be proposed by the basin management agency in consultation with the water administrative departments of the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, based on the water resources conditions of the basin, the comprehensive water resources plan, the comprehensive basin plan, and the medium- and long-term water supply and demand plan, and taking into account the current status of water abstraction and supply-demand situations, and then submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval; the total amount control indicators for water abstraction permits for the administrative regions of cities with districts and counties (cities) shall be formulated by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, based on the total amount control indicators for water abstraction permits of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and taking into account the local water abstraction status and supply-demand situations, and shall be reported to the basin management agency for record.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　按照行业用水定额核定的用水量是取水量审批的主要依据。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门和质量监督检验管理部门对本行政区域行业用水定额的制定负责指导并组织实施。  
尚未制定本行政区域行业用水定额的，可以参照国务院有关行业主管部门制定的行业用水定额执行。

**Article 16** The amount of water use verified according to the water use quota for the industry shall be the main basis for approval of the amount of water abstraction.  
The water administrative departments and the quality supervision and inspection departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for guiding and organizing the formulation of water use quotas for industries within their respective administrative regions.  
If no water use quotas for industries have been formulated for an administrative region, the water use quotas for industries formulated by the relevant competent department under the State Council may be implemented with reference.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　审批机关受理取水申请后，应当对取水申请材料进行全面审查，并综合考虑取水可能对水资源的节约保护和经济社会发展带来的影响，决定是否批准取水申请。

**Article 17** After accepting a water abstraction application, the examination and approval authority shall conduct a comprehensive review of the application materials and, taking into account the possible impact of the water abstraction on water resource conservation, protection, and socioeconomic development, decide whether to approve the water abstraction application.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　审批机关认为取水涉及社会公共利益需要听证的，应当向社会公告，并举行听证。  
取水涉及申请人与他人之间重大利害关系的，审批机关在作出是否批准取水申请的决定前，应当告知申请人、利害关系人。申请人、利害关系人要求听证的，审批机关应当组织听证。  
因取水申请引起争议或者诉讼的，审批机关应当书面通知申请人中止审批程序；争议解决或者诉讼终止后，恢复审批程序。

**Article 18** If the examination and approval authority considers that the water abstraction involves a public interest requiring a hearing, it shall make a public announcement and hold a hearing.  
If the water abstraction involves a significant interest relationship between the applicant and another person, the examination and approval authority shall, before deciding whether to approve the water abstraction application, inform the applicant and the interested party. If the applicant or the interested party requests a hearing, the examination and approval authority shall organize a hearing.  
If a dispute or lawsuit arises due to the water abstraction application, the examination and approval authority shall notify the applicant in writing to suspend the examination and approval procedure; the procedure shall resume after the dispute is resolved or the lawsuit is terminated.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　审批机关应当自受理取水申请之日起45个工作日内决定批准或者不批准。决定批准的，应当同时签发取水申请批准文件。  
对取用城市规划区地下水的取水申请，审批机关应当征求城市建设主管部门的意见，城市建设主管部门应当自收到征求意见材料之日起5个工作日内提出意见并转送取水审批机关。  
本条第一款规定的审批期限，不包括举行听证和征求有关部门意见所需的时间。

**Article 19** The examination and approval authority shall decide to approve or disapprove the water abstraction application within 45 working days from the date of accepting the application. If it decides to approve, it shall simultaneously issue a document approving the water abstraction application.  
For an application to abstract groundwater in an urban planning area, the examination and approval authority shall solicit the opinions of the competent department of urban construction. The competent department of urban construction shall, within 5 working days from the date of receiving the materials for opinion solicitation, provide its opinions and forward them to the water abstraction examination and approval authority.  
The time required for holding hearings and soliciting opinions from relevant authorities shall not be included in the period for examination and approval as prescribed in the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，审批机关不予批准，并在作出不批准的决定时，书面告知申请人不批准的理由和依据：  
(一)在地下水禁采区取用地下水的；  
(二)在取水许可总量已经达到取水许可控制总量的地区增加取水量的；  
(三)可能对水功能区水域使用功能造成重大损害的；  
(四)取水、退水布局不合理的；  
(五)城市公共供水管网能够满足用水需要时，建设项目自备取水设施取用地下水的；  
(六)可能对第三者或者社会公共利益产生重大损害的；  
(七)属于备案项目，未报送备案的；  
(八)法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。  
审批的取水量不得超过取水工程或者设施设计的取水量。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, the examination and approval authority shall not approve the application and shall, when making the decision of disapproval, inform the applicant in writing of the reasons and basis for disapproval:  
(1) groundwater is to be abstracted in an area where groundwater abstraction is prohibited;  
(2) the amount of water abstraction is to be increased in an area where the total amount of water abstraction permits has already reached the control total;  
(3) significant damage may be caused to the use function of water bodies in a water function zone;  
(4) the layout of water abstraction and discharge is unreasonable;  
(5) when the urban public water supply network can meet water needs, a construction project uses self-provided water abstraction facilities to abstract groundwater;  
(6) significant damage may be caused to third parties or the public interest;  
(7) the project is subject to recordation but has not been recorded;  
(8) other circumstances as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The approved amount of water abstraction shall not exceed the designed abstraction capacity of the water abstraction project or facility.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　取水申请经审批机关批准，申请人方可兴建取水工程或者设施。

**Article 21** Only after the water abstraction application is approved by the examination and approval authority may the applicant commence construction of water abstraction projects or facilities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　取水申请批准后3年内，取水工程或者设施未开工建设，或者需由国家审批、核准的建设项目未取得国家审批、核准的，取水申请批准文件自行失效。  
建设项目中取水事项有较大变更的，建设单位应当重新进行建设项目水资源论证，并重新申请取水。

**Article 22** If, within three years after the approval of the water abstraction application, the construction of the water abstraction project or facility has not commenced, or if a construction project requiring examination and approval by the State has not obtained such examination and approval, the document approving the water abstraction application shall automatically cease to have effect.  
If there is a significant change in the water abstraction matters in a construction project, the construction unit shall conduct a new water resources demonstration for the construction project and reapply for water abstraction.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　取水工程或者设施竣工后，申请人应当按照国务院水行政主管部门的规定，向取水审批机关报送取水工程或者设施试运行情况等相关材料；经验收合格的，由审批机关核发取水许可证。  
直接利用已有的取水工程或者设施取水的，经审批机关审查合格，发给取水许可证。  
审批机关应当将发放取水许可证的情况及时通知取水口所在地县级人民政府水行政主管部门，并定期对取水许可证的发放情况予以公告。

**Article 23** After the water abstraction project or facility is completed, the applicant shall, in accordance with the provisions of the water administrative department of the State Council, submit to the water abstraction examination and approval authority the relevant materials such as the trial operation of the water abstraction project or facility; if the project passes the inspection, the examination and approval authority shall issue a water abstraction permit.  
If water is abstracted directly using an existing water abstraction project or facility, upon examination and approval by the authority, a water abstraction permit shall be issued.  
The examination and approval authority shall timely notify the water administrative department of the people's government at the county level where the intake is located of the issuance of water abstraction permits, and shall regularly publish the information on the issuance of water abstraction permits.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　取水许可证应当包括下列内容：  
(一)取水单位或者个人的名称(姓名)；  
(二)取水期限；  
(三)取水量和取水用途；  
(四)水源类型；  
(五)取水、退水地点及退水方式、退水量。  
前款第(三)项规定的取水量是在江河、湖泊、地下水多年平均水量情况下允许的取水单位或者个人的最大取水量。  
取水许可证由国务院水行政主管部门统一制作，审批机关核发取水许可证只能收取工本费。

**Article 24** A water abstraction permit shall include the following contents:  
(1) the name (or title) of the unit or individual abstracting water;  
(2) the term of water abstraction;  
(3) the amount of water abstraction and the purpose of water use;  
(4) the type of water source;  
(5) the locations of water abstraction and discharge, the method of discharge, and the amount of discharge.  
The amount of water abstraction specified in subparagraph (3) of the preceding paragraph is the maximum amount of water that the unit or individual abstracting water may abstract under the condition of average annual water volume in rivers, lakes, or groundwater.  
Water abstraction permits shall be uniformly produced by the water administrative department of the State Council; the examination and approval authority may only charge the cost of production when issuing water abstraction permits.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　取水许可证有效期限一般为5年，最长不超过10年。有效期届满，需要延续的，取水单位或者个人应当在有效期届满45日前向原审批机关提出申请，原审批机关应当在有效期届满前，作出是否延续的决定。

**Article 25** The validity period of a water abstraction permit is generally five years, and shall not exceed ten years. If an extension is needed upon expiration, the unit or individual abstracting water shall, within 45 days before the expiration of the validity period, file an application with the original examination and approval authority, and the original examination and approval authority shall, before the expiration of the validity period, decide whether to extend the permit.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　取水单位或者个人要求变更取水许可证载明的事项的，应当依照本条例的规定向原审批机关申请，经原审批机关批准，办理有关变更手续。

**Article 26** If a unit or individual abstracting water requests a change to the matters indicated in the water abstraction permit, it shall apply to the original examination and approval authority in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, and upon approval by the original examination and approval authority, shall go through the relevant modification procedures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　依法获得取水权的单位或者个人，通过调整产品和产业结构、改革工艺、节水等措施节约水资源的，在取水许可的有效期和取水限额内，经原审批机关批准，可以依法有偿转让其节约的水资源，并到原审批机关办理取水权变更手续。具体办法由国务院水行政主管部门制定。

**Article 27** If a unit or individual that has obtained a water abstraction right according to law conserves water resources through measures such as adjusting product and industrial structures, reforming processes, and water conservation, it may, within the validity period and the water abstraction limit of the water abstraction permit and with the approval of the original examination and approval authority, transfer the conserved water resources for a fee according to law, and shall go through the procedures for the modification of the water abstraction right with the original examination and approval authority. Specific measures shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council.

### 第四章　水资源费的征收和使用管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　取水单位或者个人应当缴纳水资源费。  
取水单位或者个人应当按照经批准的年度取水计划取水。超计划或者超定额取水的，对超计划或者超定额部分累进收取水资源费。  
水资源费征收标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门会同同级财政部门、水行政主管部门制定，报本级人民政府批准，并报国务院价格主管部门、财政部门和水行政主管部门备案。其中，由流域管理机构审批取水的中央直属和跨省、自治区、直辖市水利工程的水资源费征收标准，由国务院价格主管部门会同国务院财政部门、水行政主管部门制定。

**Article 28** Units or individuals abstracting water shall pay water resources fees.  
Units or individuals abstracting water shall abstract water in accordance with the approved annual water abstraction plan. If water is abstracted in excess of the plan or quota, water resources fees shall be charged on a progressive basis for the excess portion.  
The collection standards for water resources fees shall be formulated by the price authority of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the finance department and the water administrative department at the same level, submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval, and reported to the price authority, the finance department, and the water administrative department under the State Council for record. Among these, the collection standards for water resources fees for water conservancy projects directly under the central government and those across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, for which water abstraction permits are examined and approved by basin management agencies, shall be formulated by the price authority under the State Council jointly with the finance department and the water administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　制定水资源费征收标准，应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)促进水资源的合理开发、利用、节约和保护；  
(二)与当地水资源条件和经济社会发展水平相适应；  
(三)统筹地表水和地下水的合理开发利用，防止地下水过量开采；  
(四)充分考虑不同产业和行业的差别。

**Article 29** The formulation of collection standards for water resources fees shall follow the following principles:  
(1) promoting the rational development, utilization, conservation, and protection of water resources;  
(2) being compatible with local water resources conditions and the level of socioeconomic development;  
(3) coordinating the rational development and utilization of surface water and groundwater to prevent overexploitation of groundwater;  
(4) giving full consideration to the differences among different industries and sectors.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　各级地方人民政府应当采取措施，提高农业用水效率，发展节水型农业。  
农业生产取水的水资源费征收标准应当根据当地水资源条件、农村经济发展状况和促进农业节约用水需要制定。农业生产取水的水资源费征收标准应当低于其他用水的水资源费征收标准，粮食作物的水资源费征收标准应当低于经济作物的水资源费征收标准。农业生产取水的水资源费征收的步骤和范围由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 30** Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to improve agricultural water use efficiency and develop water-saving agriculture.  
The collection standards for water resources fees for agricultural production water abstraction shall be formulated based on local water resources conditions, rural economic development status, and the need to promote agricultural water conservation. The collection standards for water resources fees for agricultural production water abstraction shall be lower than those for other types of water use, and the collection standards for water resources fees for grain crops shall be lower than those for cash crops. The steps and scope for collection of water resources fees for agricultural production water abstraction shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　水资源费由取水审批机关负责征收；其中，流域管理机构审批的，水资源费由取水口所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门代为征收。

**Article 31** Water resources fees shall be collected by the water abstraction examination and approval authority; where the examination and approval is carried out by a basin management agency, the water resources fees shall be collected on its behalf by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the intake is located.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　水资源费缴纳数额根据取水口所在地水资源费征收标准和实际取水量确定。  
水力发电用水和火力发电贯流式冷却用水可以根据取水口所在地水资源费征收标准和实际发电量确定缴纳数额。

**Article 32** The amount of water resources fees payable shall be determined based on the collection standards for water resources fees at the location of the intake and the actual amount of water abstracted.  
The amount payable for water used for hydropower generation and for once-through cooling water for thermal power generation may be determined based on the collection standards for water resources fees at the location of the intake and the actual amount of electricity generated.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　取水审批机关确定水资源费缴纳数额后，应当向取水单位或者个人送达水资源费缴纳通知单，取水单位或者个人应当自收到缴纳通知单之日起7日内办理缴纳手续。  
直接从江河、湖泊或者地下取用水资源从事农业生产的，对超过省、自治区、直辖市规定的农业生产用水限额部分的水资源，由取水单位或者个人根据取水口所在地水资源费征收标准和实际取水量缴纳水资源费；符合规定的农业生产用水限额的取水，不缴纳水资源费。取用供水工程的水从事农业生产的，由用水单位或者个人按照实际用水量向供水工程单位缴纳水费，由供水工程单位统一缴纳水资源费；水资源费计入供水成本。  
为了公共利益需要，按照国家批准的跨行政区域水量分配方案实施的临时应急调水，由调入区域的取用水的单位或者个人，根据所在地水资源费征收标准和实际取水量缴纳水资源费。

**Article 33** After determining the amount of water resources fees payable, the water abstraction examination and approval authority shall deliver a notice of payment of water resources fees to the unit or individual abstracting water, and the unit or individual abstracting water shall, within 7 days from the date of receiving the payment notice, handle the payment procedures.  
For water abstracted directly from rivers, lakes, or underground for agricultural production, water resources fees shall be paid by the unit or individual abstracting water based on the collection standards at the location of the intake and the actual amount of water abstracted for the portion exceeding the agricultural water use quota prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; no water resources fees shall be paid for water abstraction within the prescribed agricultural water use quota. For water abstracted from water supply projects for agricultural production, the water user or individual shall pay water fees to the water supply project unit based on the actual amount of water used, and the water supply project unit shall uniformly pay the water resources fees; the water resources fees shall be included in the cost of water supply.  
For temporary emergency water diversion implemented in accordance with a trans-administrative regional water allocation plan approved by the State for the purpose of public interest, the units or individuals abstracting or using water in the receiving area shall pay water resources fees based on the collection standards at their location and the actual amount of water abstracted or used.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　取水单位或者个人因特殊困难不能按期缴纳水资源费的，可以自收到水资源费缴纳通知单之日起7日内向发出缴纳通知单的水行政主管部门申请缓缴；发出缴纳通知单的水行政主管部门应当自收到缓缴申请之日起5个工作日内作出书面决定并通知申请人；期满未作决定的，视为同意。水资源费的缓缴期限最长不得超过90日。

**Article 34** If a unit or individual abstracting water is unable to pay water resources fees on time due to special difficulties, it may, within 7 days from the date of receiving the payment notice, apply to the water administrative department that issued the payment notice for deferred payment; the water administrative department that issued the payment notice shall, within 5 working days from the date of receiving the application for deferred payment, make a written decision and notify the applicant; if no decision is made within the time limit, it shall be deemed as consent. The period of deferred payment for water resources fees shall not exceed 90 days at the maximum.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　征收的水资源费应当按照国务院财政部门的规定分别解缴中央和地方国库。因筹集水利工程基金，国务院对水资源费的提取、解缴另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 35** The collected water resources fees shall be turned over to the central and local treasuries respectively in accordance with the provisions of the finance department of the State Council. If the State Council has other provisions on the withholding and turnover of water resources fees for raising water conservancy project funds, the provisions thereof shall prevail.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　征收的水资源费应当全额纳入财政预算，由财政部门按照批准的部门财政预算统筹安排，主要用于水资源的节约、保护和管理，也可以用于水资源的合理开发。

**Article 36** The collected water resources fees shall be fully included in the fiscal budget, and shall be arranged comprehensively by the finance department in accordance with the approved departmental fiscal budgets, primarily for the conservation, protection, and management of water resources, and may also be used for the rational development of water resources.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得截留、侵占或者挪用水资源费。  
审计机关应当加强对水资源费使用和管理的审计监督。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may withhold, misappropriate, or divert water resources fees.  
Audit authorities shall strengthen audit supervision over the use and management of water resources fees.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构应当依照本条例规定，加强对取水许可制度实施的监督管理。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、财政部门和价格主管部门应当加强对水资源费征收、使用情况的监督管理。

**Article 38** The water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level or the basin management agencies shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, strengthen the supervision and management of the implementation of the water abstraction permit system.  
The water administrative departments, finance departments, and price authorities of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and management of the collection and use of water resources fees.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　年度水量分配方案和年度取水计划是年度取水总量控制的依据，应当根据批准的水量分配方案或者签订的协议，结合实际用水状况、行业用水定额、下一年度预测来水量等制定。  
国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的流域年度水量分配方案和年度取水计划，由流域管理机构会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门制定。  
县级以上各地方行政区域的年度水量分配方案和年度取水计划，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门根据上一级地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构下达的年度水量分配方案和年度取水计划制定。

**Article 39** The annual water allocation scheme and the annual water abstraction plan shall be the basis for the annual total amount control of water abstraction, and shall be formulated in accordance with the approved water allocation scheme or the concluded agreement, taking into account the actual water use situation, water use quotas for industries, and the forecasted water inflow for the next year, etc.  
The basin annual water allocation scheme and annual water abstraction plan for important rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be formulated by the basin management agency jointly with the water administrative departments of the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The annual water allocation scheme and annual water abstraction plan for each local administrative region at or above the county level shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the annual water allocation scheme and annual water abstraction plan transmitted by the water administrative department of the local people's government at the next higher level or the basin management agency.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　取水审批机关依照本地区下一年度取水计划、取水单位或者个人提出的下一年度取水计划建议，按照统筹协调、综合平衡、留有余地的原则，向取水单位或者个人下达下一年度取水计划。  
取水单位或者个人因特殊原因需要调整年度取水计划的，应当经原审批机关同意。

**Article 40** The water abstraction examination and approval authority shall, based on the next year's water abstraction plan for its region and the next year's water abstraction plan proposals submitted by the units or individuals abstracting water, and in accordance with the principle of overall coordination, comprehensive balance, and leaving some margin, issue the next year's water abstraction plan to the units or individuals abstracting water.  
If a unit or individual abstracting water needs to adjust its annual water abstraction plan due to special reasons, the adjustment shall be subject to the consent of the original examination and approval authority.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列情形之一的，审批机关可以对取水单位或者个人的年度取水量予以限制：  
(一)因自然原因，水资源不能满足本地区正常供水的；  
(二)取水、退水对水功能区水域使用功能、生态与环境造成严重影响的；  
(三)地下水严重超采或者因地下水开采引起地面沉降等地质灾害的；  
(四)出现需要限制取水量的其他特殊情况的。  
发生重大旱情时，审批机关可以对取水单位或者个人的取水量予以紧急限制。

**Article 41** In any of the following circumstances, the examination and approval authority may impose restrictions on the annual amount of water abstraction of a unit or individual abstracting water:  
(1) due to natural causes, water resources are insufficient to meet normal water supply in the region;  
(2) water abstraction or discharge has caused a serious impact on the use function of water bodies in water function zones, ecology, and the environment;  
(3) groundwater is seriously overexploited or the exploitation of groundwater has caused geological hazards such as land subsidence;  
(4) other special circumstances arise that require restrictions on the amount of water abstraction.  
When a major drought occurs, the examination and approval authority may impose emergency restrictions on the amount of water abstraction of a unit or individual abstracting water.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　取水单位或者个人应当在每年的12月31日前向审批机关报送本年度的取水情况和下一年度取水计划建议。  
审批机关应当按年度将取用地下水的情况抄送同级国土资源主管部门，将取用城市规划区地下水的情况抄送同级城市建设主管部门。  
审批机关依照[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)第一款的规定，需要对取水单位或者个人的年度取水量予以限制的，应当在采取限制措施前及时书面通知取水单位或者个人。

**Article 42** A unit or individual abstracting water shall, before December 31 of each year, submit to the examination and approval authority a report on its water abstraction for the current year and a proposal for the next year's water abstraction plan.  
The examination and approval authority shall, on an annual basis, send a copy of the information on groundwater abstraction to the competent department of land and resources at the same level, and send a copy of the information on groundwater abstraction in the urban planning area to the competent department of urban construction at the same level.  
If the examination and approval authority needs to impose restrictions on the annual amount of water abstraction of a unit or individual abstracting water in accordance with the provisions of Article 41(1) of these Regulations, it shall timely notify the unit or individual in writing before taking the restriction measures.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　取水单位或者个人应当依照国家技术标准安装计量设施，保证计量设施正常运行，并按照规定填报取水统计报表。

**Article 43** Units or individuals abstracting water shall install metering facilities in accordance with national technical standards, ensure the normal operation of the metering facilities, and fill in water abstraction statistical reports in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　连续停止取水满2年的，由原审批机关注销取水许可证。由于不可抗力或者进行重大技术改造等原因造成停止取水满2年的，经原审批机关同意，可以保留取水许可证。

**Article 44** If water abstraction has been continuously suspended for two years, the water abstraction permit shall be cancelled by the original examination and approval authority. If the suspension for two years is caused by force majeure or major technological transformation, the water abstraction permit may be retained with the consent of the original examination and approval authority.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构在进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查单位或者个人提供有关文件、证照、资料；  
(二)要求被检查单位或者个人就执行本条例的有关问题作出说明；  
(三)进入被检查单位或者个人的生产场所进行调查；  
(四)责令被检查单位或者个人停止违反本条例的行为，履行法定义务。  
监督检查人员在进行监督检查时，应当出示合法有效的行政执法证件。有关单位和个人对监督检查工作应当给予配合，不得拒绝或者阻碍监督检查人员依法执行公务。

**Article 45** When conducting supervision and inspection, the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level or the basin management agencies shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) to require the unit or individual under inspection to provide relevant documents, licenses, and materials;  
(2) to require the unit or individual under inspection to provide explanations on relevant issues concerning the implementation of these Regulations;  
(3) to enter the production premises of the unit or individual under inspection to conduct investigations;  
(4) to order the unit or individual under inspection to cease acts that violate these Regulations and to perform legal obligations.  
When conducting supervision and inspection, the inspectors shall present valid law enforcement credentials. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection work and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspectors from performing their official duties according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当按照国务院水行政主管部门的规定，及时向上一级水行政主管部门或者所在流域的流域管理机构报送本行政区域上一年度取水许可证发放情况。  
流域管理机构应当按照国务院水行政主管部门的规定，及时向国务院水行政主管部门报送其上一年度取水许可证发放情况，并同时抄送取水口所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门。  
上一级水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构发现越权审批、取水许可证核准的总取水量超过水量分配方案或者协议规定的数量、年度实际取水总量超过下达的年度水量分配方案和年度取水计划的，应当及时要求有关水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构纠正。

**Article 46** The water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the water administrative department of the State Council, timely submit to the water administrative department at the next higher level or the basin management agency in whose basin they are located a report on the issuance of water abstraction permits in their respective administrative regions for the preceding year.  
The basin management agencies shall, in accordance with the provisions of the water administrative department of the State Council, timely submit to the water administrative department of the State Council a report on the issuance of water abstraction permits for the preceding year, and shall simultaneously send a copy thereof to the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the intake is located.  
If the water administrative department at the next higher level or the basin management agency discovers that there have been ultra vires approvals, that the total amount of water abstraction approved in water abstraction permits exceeds the amount specified in the water allocation scheme or agreement, or that the total actual annual water abstraction exceeds the annual water allocation scheme and annual water abstraction plan issued, it shall promptly require the relevant water administrative department or basin management agency to make corrections.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门、流域管理机构或者其他有关部门及其工作人员，有下列行为之一的，由其上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对符合法定条件的取水申请不予受理或者不在法定期限内批准的；  
(二)对不符合法定条件的申请人签发取水申请批准文件或者发放取水许可证的；  
(三)违反审批权限签发取水申请批准文件或者发放取水许可证的；  
(四)不按照规定征收水资源费，或者对不符合缓缴条件而批准缓缴水资源费的；  
(五)侵占、截留、挪用水资源费的；  
(六)不履行监督职责，发现违法行为不予查处的；  
(七)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的行为。  
前款第(五)项规定的被侵占、截留、挪用的水资源费，应当依法予以追缴。

**Article 47** If any of the following acts is committed by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level, the basin management agencies, or other relevant departments or their staff members, the administrative organ at the next higher level or the supervisory organ shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to accept a water abstraction application that meets the statutory conditions or failing to approve it within the statutory time limit;  
(2) issuing a document approving a water abstraction application or issuing a water abstraction permit to an applicant who does not meet the statutory conditions;  
(3) issuing a document approving a water abstraction application or issuing a water abstraction permit in violation of the approval authority;  
(4) failing to collect water resources fees in accordance with regulations, or approving deferred payment of water resources fees for applicants who do not meet the conditions for deferred payment;  
(5) misappropriating, withholding, or diverting water resources fees;  
(6) failing to perform supervision duties and failing to investigate illegal acts discovered;  
(7) other acts of abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.  
The water resources fees that have been misappropriated, withheld, or diverted as provided in subparagraph (5) of the preceding paragraph shall be recovered according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　未经批准擅自取水，或者未依照批准的取水许可规定条件取水的，依照[《中华人民共和国水法》第六十九条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国水法.md#art-69)规定处罚；给他人造成妨碍或者损失的，应当排除妨碍、赔偿损失。

**Article 48** Anyone who abstracts water without approval or abstracts water not in accordance with the conditions prescribed in the approved water abstraction permit shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 69 of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China; if nuisance or damage is caused to others, the nuisance shall be eliminated and the damage shall be compensated.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　未取得取水申请批准文件擅自建设取水工程或者设施的，责令停止违法行为，限期补办有关手续；逾期不补办或者补办未被批准的，责令限期拆除或者封闭其取水工程或者设施；逾期不拆除或者不封闭其取水工程或者设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构组织拆除或者封闭，所需费用由违法行为人承担，可以处5万元以下罚款。

**Article 49** If anyone constructs a water abstraction project or facility without having obtained the document approving the water abstraction application, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, and the relevant procedures shall be completed within a time limit; if the procedures are not completed within the time limit or the application for completion is not approved, the water abstraction project or facility shall be ordered to be dismantled or sealed within a time limit; if the project or facility is not dismantled or sealed within the time limit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the basin management agency shall organize its dismantlement or sealing, with the expenses borne by the violator, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　申请人隐瞒有关情况或者提供虚假材料骗取取水申请批准文件或者取水许可证的，取水申请批准文件或者取水许可证无效，对申请人给予警告，责令其限期补缴应当缴纳的水资源费，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** If an applicant obtains a document approving a water abstraction application or a water abstraction permit by concealing relevant information or providing false materials, the document or permit shall be invalid, the applicant shall be given a warning, ordered to make up the water resources fees payable within a time limit, and fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　拒不执行审批机关作出的取水量限制决定，或者未经批准擅自转让取水权的，责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；逾期拒不改正或者情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 51** If anyone refuses to implement the decision on restriction of water abstraction made by the examination and approval authority, or transfers the water abstraction right without approval, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease and corrections shall be made within a time limit, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the party refuses to make corrections within the time limit or if the circumstances are serious, the water abstraction permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　有下列行为之一的，责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处5000元以上2万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证：  
(一)不按照规定报送年度取水情况的；  
(二)拒绝接受监督检查或者弄虚作假的；  
(三)退水水质达不到规定要求的。

**Article 52** If any of the following acts is committed, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease and corrections shall be made within a time limit, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the water abstraction permit shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to submit annual water abstraction information in accordance with regulations;  
(2) refusing to accept supervision and inspection, or resorting to deception;  
(3) the quality of discharged water fails to meet the prescribed requirements.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　未安装计量设施的，责令限期安装，并按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量和水资源费征收标准计征水资源费，处5000元以上2万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。  
计量设施不合格或者运行不正常的，责令限期更换或者修复；逾期不更换或者不修复的，按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量和水资源费征收标准计征水资源费，可以处1万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 53** If no metering facilities are installed, the party shall be ordered to install them within a time limit, and water resources fees shall be assessed based on the amount of water calculated at the maximum daily abstraction capacity and the collection standards for water resources fees, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the water abstraction permit shall be revoked.  
If the metering facilities are substandard or operate abnormally, the party shall be ordered to replace or repair them within a time limit; if the replacement or repair is not made within the time limit, water resources fees shall be assessed based on the amount of water calculated at the maximum daily abstraction capacity and the collection standards for water resources fees, and a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the water abstraction permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　取水单位或者个人拒不缴纳、拖延缴纳或者拖欠水资源费的，依照[《中华人民共和国水法》第七十条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国水法.md#art-70)规定处罚。

**Article 54** If a unit or individual abstracting water refuses to pay, delays payment, or defaults on payment of water resources fees, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 70 of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对违反规定征收水资源费、取水许可证照费的，由价格主管部门依法予以行政处罚。

**Article 55** If anyone collects water resources fees or fees for water abstraction permit licenses in violation of regulations, the price authority shall impose an administrative penalty according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　伪造、涂改、冒用取水申请批准文件、取水许可证的，责令改正，没收违法所得和非法财物，并处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** If anyone forges, alters, or fraudulently uses a document approving a water abstraction application or a water abstraction permit, the party shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains and illegal property shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构按照规定的权限决定。

**Article 57** The administrative penalties provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the water administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level or the basin management agencies within their prescribed authority.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2006年4月15日起施行。1993年8月1日国务院发布的《取水许可制度实施办法》同时废止。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 15, 2006. The Measures for the Implementation of the Water Abstraction Permit System promulgated by the State Council on August 1, 1993 shall be abolished simultaneously.
