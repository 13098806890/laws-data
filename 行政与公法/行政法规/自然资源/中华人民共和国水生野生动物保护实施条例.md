**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2013-12-07

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国野生动物保护法》(以下简称《野生动物保护法》)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Wildlife (hereinafter referred to as the Wildlife Protection Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称水生野生动物，是指珍贵、濒危的水生野生动物；所称水生野生动物产品，是指珍贵、濒危的水生野生动物的任何部分及其衍生物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term "aquatic wildlife" means rare and endangered aquatic wildlife; the term "aquatic wildlife products" means any parts of rare and endangered aquatic wildlife and derivatives thereof.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院渔业行政主管部门主管全国水生野生动物管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府渔业行政主管部门主管本行政区域内水生野生动物管理工作。  
《野生动物保护法》和本条例规定的渔业行政主管部门的行政处罚权，可以由其所属的渔政监督管理机构行使。

**Article 3** The competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the management of aquatic wildlife nationwide.  
The competent fisheries administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the management of aquatic wildlife within their respective administrative regions.  
The administrative penalty power of the competent fisheries administrative departments as specified in the Wildlife Protection Law and these Regulations may be exercised by the fishery administration and supervision institutions under them.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上各级人民政府及其有关主管部门应当鼓励、支持有关科研单位、教学单位开展水生野生动物科学研究工作。

**Article 4** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant competent departments shall encourage and support relevant scientific research units and teaching units to carry out scientific research on aquatic wildlife.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　渔业行政主管部门及其所属的渔政监督管理机构，有权对《野生动物保护法》和本条例的实施情况进行监督检查，被检查的单位和个人应当给予配合。

**Article 5** The competent fisheries administrative department and the fishery administration and supervision institutions under it shall have the power to supervise and inspect the implementation of the Wildlife Protection Law and these Regulations, and the entities and individuals being inspected shall cooperate.

### 第二章　水生野生动物保护

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院渔业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门，应当定期组织水生野生动物资源调查，建立资源档案，为制定水生野生动物资源保护发展规划、制定和调整国家和地方重点保护水生野生动物名录提供依据。

**Article 6** The competent fisheries administrative department of the State Council and the competent fisheries administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regularly organize surveys of aquatic wildlife resources, establish resource archives, and provide a basis for formulating development plans for the protection of aquatic wildlife resources and for formulating and adjusting the lists of key protected aquatic wildlife at the national and local levels.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　渔业行政主管部门应当组织社会各方面力量，采取有效措施，维护和改善水生野生动物的生存环境，保护和增殖水生野生动物资源。  
禁止任何单位和个人破坏国家重点保护的和地方重点保护的水生野生动物生息繁衍的水域、场所和生存条件。

**Article 7** The competent fisheries administrative department shall organize all sectors of society to take effective measures to maintain and improve the living environment of aquatic wildlife, and to protect and increase aquatic wildlife resources.  
It shall be prohibited for any entity or individual to destroy the waters, sites, and living conditions for the habitation and reproduction of aquatic wildlife under key national protection and key local protection.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人对侵占或者破坏水生野生动物资源的行为，有权向当地渔业行政主管部门或者其所属的渔政监督管理机构检举和控告。

**Article 8** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report and accuse any act of encroaching upon or destroying aquatic wildlife resources to the local competent fisheries administrative department or the fishery administration and supervision institution under it.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人发现受伤、搁浅和因误入港湾、河汊而被困的水生野生动物时，应当及时报告当地渔业行政主管部门或者其所属的渔政监督管理机构，由其采取紧急救护措施；也可以要求附近具备救护条件的单位采取紧急救护措施，并报告渔业行政主管部门。已经死亡的水生野生动物，由渔业行政主管部门妥善处理。  
捕捞作业时误捕水生野生动物的，应当立即无条件放生。

**Article 9** Any entity or individual that discovers any aquatic wildlife that is injured, stranded, or trapped due to straying into harbors or river branches shall promptly report to the local competent fisheries administrative department or the fishery administration and supervision institution under it, which shall take emergency rescue measures; such entity or individual may also request any nearby entity with rescue conditions to take emergency rescue measures and report to the competent fisheries administrative department. Dead aquatic wildlife shall be properly disposed of by the competent fisheries administrative department.  
Where aquatic wildlife is accidentally caught during fishing operations, it shall be released immediately and unconditionally.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　因保护国家重点保护的和地方重点保护的水生野生动物受到损失的，可以向当地人民政府渔业行政主管部门提出补偿要求。经调查属实并确实需要补偿的，由当地人民政府按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 10** Any person who suffers losses due to the protection of aquatic wildlife under key national protection and key local protection may file a claim for compensation with the competent fisheries administrative department of the local people's government. If the claim is verified and compensation is indeed necessary, the local people's government shall grant compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院渔业行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，应当在国家重点保护的和地方重点保护的水生野生动物的主要生息繁衍的地区和水域，划定水生野生动物自然保护区，加强对国家和地方重点保护水生野生动物及其生存环境的保护管理，具体办法由国务院另行规定。

**Article 11** The fishery administrative department of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in the main breeding and habitat areas and waters of aquatic wildlife under key national and local protection, designate aquatic wildlife nature reserves to strengthen the protection and management of aquatic wildlife under key national and local protection and their living environment. Specific measures shall be separately formulated by the State Council.

### 第三章　水生野生动物管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　禁止捕捉、杀害国家重点保护的水生野生动物。  
有下列情形之一，确需捕捉国家重点保护的水生野生动物的，必须申请特许捕捉证：  
(一)为进行水生野生动物科学考察、资源调查，必须捕捉的；  
(二)为驯养繁殖国家重点保护的水生野生动物，必须从自然水域或者场所获取种源的；  
(三)为承担省级以上科学研究项目或者国家医药生产任务，必须从自然水域或者场所获取国家重点保护的水生野生动物的；  
(四)为宣传、普及水生野生动物知识或者教学、展览的需要，必须从自然水域或者场所获取国家重点保护的水生野生动物的；  
(五)因其他特殊情况，必须捕捉的。

**Article 12** It is prohibited to capture or kill aquatic wildlife under key national protection.  
In any of the following circumstances where it is truly necessary to capture aquatic wildlife under key national protection, a special capture permit must be applied for:  
(1) capture is necessary for conducting scientific surveys or resource investigations of aquatic wildlife;  
(2) it is necessary to obtain source species from natural waters or sites for the domestication and breeding of aquatic wildlife under key national protection;  
(3) it is necessary to obtain aquatic wildlife under key national protection from natural waters or sites for undertaking scientific research projects at or above the provincial level or national pharmaceutical production tasks;  
(4) it is necessary to obtain aquatic wildlife under key national protection from natural waters or sites for the purposes of publicity, popularization of knowledge about aquatic wildlife, or teaching or exhibition;  
(5) capture is necessary due to other special circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请特许捕捉证的程序：  
(一)需要捕捉国家一级保护水生野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地和捕捉地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门签署的意见，向国务院渔业行政主管部门申请特许捕捉证；  
(二)需要在本省、自治区、直辖市捕捉国家二级保护水生野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地的县级人民政府渔业行政主管部门签署的意见，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门申请特许捕捉证；  
(三)需要跨省、自治区、直辖市捕捉国家二级保护水生野生动物的，必须附具申请人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门签署的意见，向捕捉地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门申请特许捕捉证。  
动物园申请捕捉国家一级保护水生野生动物的，在向国务院渔业行政主管部门申请特许捕捉证前，须经国务院建设行政主管部门审核同意；申请捕捉国家二级保护水生野生动物的，在向申请人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门申请特许捕捉证前，须经同级人民政府建设行政主管部门审核同意。  
负责核发特许捕捉证的部门接到申请后，应当自接到申请之日起3个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定。

**Article 13** The procedures for applying for a special capture permit:  
(1) If it is necessary to capture aquatic wildlife under Class I national protection, the applicant must submit the application together with the opinions signed by the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where both the applicant is located and the capture will take place, and apply to the fishery administrative department of the State Council for a special capture permit;  
(2) If it is necessary to capture aquatic wildlife under Class II national protection within the same province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the applicant must submit the application together with the opinions signed by the fishery administrative department of the people's government at the county level where the applicant is located, and apply to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for a special capture permit;  
(3) If it is necessary to capture aquatic wildlife under Class II national protection across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the applicant must submit the application together with the opinions signed by the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located, and apply to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the capture will take place for a special capture permit.  
If a zoo applies to capture aquatic wildlife under Class I national protection, it must obtain the examination and consent of the construction administrative department of the State Council before applying to the fishery administrative department of the State Council for a special capture permit; if it applies to capture aquatic wildlife under Class II national protection, it must obtain the examination and consent of the construction administrative department of the people's government at the same level before applying to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located for a special capture permit.  
The department responsible for issuing the special capture permit shall, upon receipt of the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove within three months from the date of receipt.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　有下列情形之一的，不予发放特许捕捉证：  
(一)申请人有条件以合法的非捕捉方式获得国家重点保护的水生野生动物的种源、产品或者达到其目的的；  
(二)捕捉申请不符合国家有关规定，或者申请使用的捕捉工具、方法以及捕捉时间、地点不当的；  
(三)根据水生野生动物资源现状不宜捕捉的。

**Article 14** A special capture permit shall not be issued in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The applicant has the conditions to obtain source species, products, or achieve the purpose of aquatic wildlife under key national protection through lawful non-capture means;  
(2) The capture application does not comply with relevant state regulations, or the capture tools, methods, time, or location applied for are inappropriate;  
(3) Capture is not advisable based on the current status of aquatic wildlife resources.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　取得特许捕捉证的单位和个人，必须按照特许捕捉证规定的种类、数量、地点、期限、工具和方法进行捕捉，防止误伤水生野生动物或者破坏其生存环境。捕捉作业完成后，应当及时向捕捉地的县级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者其所属的渔政监督管理机构申请查验。  
县级人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者其所属的渔政监督管理机构对在本行政区域内捕捉国家重点保护的水生野生动物的活动，应当进行监督检查，并及时向批准捕捉的部门报告监督检查结果。

**Article 15** Entities and individuals that have obtained a special capture permit must carry out capture in accordance with the species, quantity, location, time limit, tools, and methods specified in the permit, and shall prevent accidental injury to aquatic wildlife or damage to their living environment. Upon completion of capture operations, they shall promptly apply for inspection to the fishery administrative department of the people's government at the county level where the capture took place or its subordinate fishery supervision and management institution.  
The fishery administrative department of the people's government at the county level or its subordinate fishery supervision and management institution shall supervise and inspect the activities of capturing aquatic wildlife under key national protection within its administrative region, and shall promptly report the results of supervision and inspection to the department that approved the capture.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国人在中国境内进行有关水生野生动物科学考察、标本采集、拍摄电影、录像等活动的，必须经国家重点保护的水生野生动物所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 16** Foreigners conducting activities such as scientific surveys, specimen collection, film or video recording related to aquatic wildlife within the territory of China must obtain approval from the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the aquatic wildlife under key national protection is located.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　驯养繁殖国家一级保护水生野生动物的，应当持有国务院渔业行政主管部门核发的驯养繁殖许可证；驯养繁殖国家二级保护水生野生动物的，应当持有省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门核发的驯养繁殖许可证。  
动物园驯养繁殖国家重点保护的水生野生动物的，渔业行政主管部门可以委托同级建设行政主管部门核发驯养繁殖许可证。

**Article 17** Those who domesticate and breed aquatic wildlife under Class I national protection shall hold a domestication and breeding license issued by the fishery administrative department of the State Council; those who domesticate and breed aquatic wildlife under Class II national protection shall hold a domestication and breeding license issued by the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
If a zoo domesticates and breeds aquatic wildlife under key national protection, the fishery administrative department may entrust the construction administrative department at the same level to issue the domestication and breeding license.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止出售、收购国家重点保护的水生野生动物或者其产品。因科学研究、驯养繁殖、展览等特殊情况，需要出售、收购、利用国家一级保护水生野生动物或者其产品的，必须向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门提出申请，经其签署意见后，报国务院渔业行政主管部门批准；需要出售、收购、利用国家二级保护水生野生动物或者其产品的，必须向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门提出申请，并经其批准。

**Article 18** It is prohibited to sell, purchase, or utilize aquatic wildlife under key national protection or their products. If due to special circumstances such as scientific research, domestication and breeding, or exhibition, it is necessary to sell, purchase, or utilize aquatic wildlife under Class I national protection or their products, an application must be submitted to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and after its signed opinion, it shall be reported to the fishery administrative department of the State Council for approval; if it is necessary to sell, purchase, or utilize aquatic wildlife under Class II national protection or their products, an application must be submitted to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall obtain its approval.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上各级人民政府渔业行政主管部门和工商行政管理部门，应当对水生野生动物或者其产品的经营利用建立监督检查制度，加强对经营利用水生野生动物或者其产品的监督管理。  
对进入集贸市场的水生野生动物或者其产品，由工商行政管理部门进行监督管理，渔业行政主管部门给予协助；在集贸市场以外经营水生野生动物或者其产品，由渔业行政主管部门、工商行政管理部门或者其授权的单位进行监督管理。

**Article 19** The fishery administrative departments and administrative departments for industry and commerce of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a supervision and inspection system for the business and utilization of aquatic wildlife or their products, and strengthen the supervision and management of such business and utilization.  
For aquatic wildlife or their products entering a fair trade market, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall conduct supervision and management, and the fishery administrative department shall provide assistance; for the business of aquatic wildlife or their products outside the fair trade market, the fishery administrative department, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or their authorized entities shall conduct supervision and management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　运输、携带国家重点保护的水生野生动物或者其产品出县境的，应当凭特许捕捉证或者驯养繁殖许可证，向县级人民政府渔业行政主管部门提出申请，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门或者其授权的单位批准。动物园之间因繁殖动物，需要运输国家重点保护的水生野生动物的，可以由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门授权同级建设行政主管部门审批。

**Article 20** For transporting or carrying aquatic wildlife under key national protection or their products out of a county, the person shall, by presenting the special capture permit or domestication and breeding license, submit an application to the fishery administrative department of the people's government at the county level, and report it to the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or its authorized entity for approval. If zoos need to transport aquatic wildlife under key national protection for breeding purposes, the fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may authorize the construction administrative department at the same level to examine and approve.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　交通、铁路、民航和邮政企业对没有合法运输证明的水生野生动物或者其产品，应当及时通知有关主管部门处理，不得承运、收寄。

**Article 21** Transportation, railway, civil aviation and postal enterprises shall, upon discovering aquatic wild animals or their products without lawful transport certificates, promptly notify the relevant competent authorities for handling, and shall not transport or accept for delivery.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从国外引进水生野生动物的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门提出申请，经省级以上人民政府渔业行政主管部门指定的科研机构进行科学论证后，报国务院渔业行政主管部门批准。

**Article 22** Anyone introducing aquatic wild animals from abroad shall file an application with the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. After scientific demonstration by a scientific research institution designated by the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, the matter shall be submitted to the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出口国家重点保护的水生野生动物或者其产品的，进出口中国参加的国际公约所限制进出口的水生野生动物或者其产品的，必须经进出口单位或者个人所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府渔业行政主管部门审核，报国务院渔业行政主管部门批准；属于贸易性进出口活动的，必须由具有有关商品进出口权的单位承担。  
动物园因交换动物需要进出口前款所称水生野生动物的，在国务院渔业行政主管部门批准前，应当经国务院建设行政主管部门审核同意。

**Article 23** Anyone exporting aquatic wild animals or their products under key national protection, or importing or exporting aquatic wild animals or their products which are subject to import/export restrictions under international conventions to which China is a party, must be examined by the competent fishery administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the importing/exporting unit or individual is located, and then submitted to the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council for approval. For import/export activities of a commercial nature, they must be undertaken by units with the relevant commodity import/export rights.  
If a zoo needs to import or export the aquatic wild animals referred to in the preceding paragraph for the purpose of exchanging animals, the matter shall, before approval by the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council, be examined and consented to by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　利用水生野生动物或者其产品举办展览等活动的经济收益，主要用于水生野生动物保护事业。

**Article 24** The economic benefits obtained from holding exhibitions or other activities using aquatic wild animals or their products shall be primarily used for the cause of aquatic wild animal protection.

### 第四章　奖励和惩罚

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列事迹之一的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府或者其渔业行政主管部门给予奖励：  
(一)在水生野生动物资源调查、保护管理、宣传教育、开发利用方面有突出贡献的；  
(二)严格执行野生动物保护法规，成绩显著的；  
(三)拯救、保护和驯养繁殖水生野生动物取得显著成效的；  
(四)发现违反水生野生动物保护法律、法规的行为，及时制止或者检举有功的；  
(五)在查处破坏水生野生动物资源案件中作出重要贡献的；  
(六)在水生野生动物科学研究中取得重大成果或者在应用推广有关的科研成果中取得显著效益的；  
(七)在基层从事水生野生动物保护管理工作5年以上并取得显著成绩的；  
(八)在水生野生动物保护管理工作中有其他特殊贡献的。

**Article 25** Units or individuals with one of the following deeds shall be rewarded by the people's government at or above the county level or its competent fishery administrative department:  
(1) Having made outstanding contributions in the investigation, protection and management, publicity and education, development and utilization of aquatic wild animal resources;  
(2) Having strictly implemented the laws and regulations on wildlife protection and achieved notable results;  
(3) Having achieved remarkable results in rescuing, protecting, and domestically breeding aquatic wild animals;  
(4) Having promptly stopped or reported violations of laws and regulations on aquatic wild animal protection and meritoriously served;  
(5) Having made important contributions in investigating and handling cases damaging aquatic wild animal resources;  
(6) Having achieved significant results in scientific research on aquatic wild animals or in the application and promotion of relevant scientific research achievements;  
(7) Having been engaged in grassroots aquatic wild animal protection and management work for more than five years and achieved notable results;  
(8) Having made other special contributions in the work of aquatic wild animal protection and management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　非法捕杀国家重点保护的水生野生动物的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；情节显著轻微危害不大的，或者犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，由渔业行政主管部门没收捕获物、捕捉工具和违法所得，吊销特许捕捉证，并处以相当于捕获物价值10倍以下的罚款，没有捕获物的处以1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Anyone illegally catching or killing aquatic wild animals under key national protection shall be investigated for criminal liability according to the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law. If the circumstances are obviously minor and harm is not great, or if the criminal circumstances are minor and no criminal punishment is necessary, the competent fishery administrative department shall confiscate the catch, capturing tools and illegal gains, revoke the special catching permit, and impose a fine of not more than 10 times the value of the catch. If there is no catch, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反野生动物保护法律、法规，在水生野生动物自然保护区破坏国家重点保护的或者地方重点保护的水生野生动物主要生息繁衍场所，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十四条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-34)的规定处以罚款的，罚款幅度为恢复原状所需费用的3倍以下。

**Article 27** Where anyone violates the laws and regulations on wildlife protection by destroying the main habitats and breeding grounds of aquatic wild animals under key national or local protection in aquatic wild animal nature reserves, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 34 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine range shall be not more than three times the cost necessary for restoration to the original state.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反野生动物保护法律、法规，出售、收购、运输、携带国家重点保护的或者地方重点保护的水生野生动物或者其产品的，由工商行政管理部门或者其授权的渔业行政主管部门没收实物和违法所得，可以并处相当于实物价值10倍以下的罚款。

**Article 28** Anyone who, in violation of the laws and regulations on wildlife protection, sells, purchases, transports, or carries aquatic wild animals or their products under key national or local protection shall have the items and illegal gains confiscated by the administrative department for industry and commerce or the competent fishery administrative department authorized by it, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 10 times the value of the items.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　伪造、倒卖、转让驯养繁殖许可证，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-37)的规定处以罚款的，罚款幅度为5000元以下。伪造、倒卖、转让特许捕捉证或者允许进出口证明书，依照[《野生动物保护法》第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国野生动物保护法.md#art-37)的规定处以罚款的，罚款幅度为5万元以下。

**Article 29** Where anyone forges, resells, or transfers a domestication and breeding license, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 37 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine range shall be not more than 5,000 yuan. Where anyone forges, resells, or transfers a special catching permit or an import/export permit certificate, and a fine is imposed in accordance with Article 37 of the Wildlife Protection Law, the fine range shall be not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反野生动物保护法规，未取得驯养繁殖许可证或者超越驯养繁殖许可证规定范围，驯养繁殖国家重点保护的水生野生动物的，由渔业行政主管部门没收违法所得，处3000元以下的罚款，可以并处没收水生野生动物、吊销驯养繁殖许可证。

**Article 30** Anyone who, in violation of the regulations on wildlife protection, domesticates or breeds aquatic wild animals under key national protection without obtaining a domestication and breeding license or beyond the scope specified in the license, shall have the illegal gains confiscated by the competent fishery administrative department and shall be imposed a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan, and may also be ordered to confiscate the aquatic wild animals and have the domestication and breeding license revoked.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　外国人未经批准在中国境内对国家重点保护的水生野生动物进行科学考察、标本采集、拍摄电影、录像的，由渔业行政主管部门没收考察、拍摄的资料以及所获标本，可以并处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Where a foreigner, without approval, conducts scientific research, collects specimens, shoots films, or makes video recordings of aquatic wildlife under special state protection within the territory of China, the competent fishery administrative department shall confiscate the research and filming materials as well as the specimens obtained, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan concurrently.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪，应当给予治安管理处罚的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定予以处罚：  
(一)拒绝、阻碍渔政检查人员依法执行职务的；  
(二)偷窃、哄抢或者故意损坏野生动物保护仪器设备或者设施的。

**Article 32** Any person who commits any of the following acts, if the act does not constitute a crime and a public security administration penalty shall be imposed, shall be punished by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Penalties:  
(1) refusing or obstructing a fishery inspection officer from performing his duties according to law; or  
(2) stealing, grabbing, or intentionally damaging instruments, equipment, or facilities for wildlife protection.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　依照野生动物保护法规的规定没收的实物，按照国务院渔业行政主管部门的有关规定处理。

**Article 33** The physical objects confiscated in accordance with the provisions of the wildlife protection laws and regulations shall be disposed of in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例由国务院渔业行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the competent fishery administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
