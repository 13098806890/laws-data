# 地下水管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-10-21  
**生效日期**：2021-12-01

> 第十一条　县级以上人民政府应当组织水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门开展地下水状况调查评价工作。调查评价成果是编制地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划以及管理地下水的重要依据。调查评价成果应当依法向社会公布。 第十二条　县级以上人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门根据地下水状况调查评价成果，统筹考虑经济社会发展需要、地下水资源状况、污染防治等因素，编制本级地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划，依法履行征求意见、论证评估等程序后向社会公布。 地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划是节约、保护、利用、修复治理地下水的基本依据。地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划应当服从水资源综合规划和环境保护规划。 第十三条　国民经济和社会发展规划以及国土空间规划等相关规划的编制、重大建设项目的布局，应当与地下水资源条件和地下水保护要求相适应，并进行科学论证。 第十四条　编制工业、农业、市政、能源、矿产资源开发等专项规划，涉及地下水的内容，应当与地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划相衔接。 第十五条　国家建立地下水储备制度。国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源、发展改革等主管部门，对地下水储备工作进行指导、协调和监督检查。 县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府自然资源、发展改革等主管部门，根据本行政区域内地下水条件、气候状况和水资源储备需要，制定动用地下水储备预案并报本级人民政府批准。 除特殊干旱年份以及发生重大突发事件外，不得动用地下水储备。 第三章　节约与保护 第十六条　国家实行地下水取水总量控制制度。国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院自然资源主管部门，根据各省、自治区、直辖市地下水可开采量和地表水水资源状况，制定并下达各省、自治区、直辖市地下水取水总量控制指标。 第十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门，根据国家下达的地下水取水总量控制指标，制定本行政区域内县级以上行政区域的地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准后下达实施，并报国务院水行政主管部门或者其授权的流域管理机构备案。 第十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门制定本行政区域内地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标时，涉及省际边界区域且属于同一水文地质单元的，应当与相邻省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门协商确定。协商不成的，由国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门确定。 第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据地下水取水总量控制指标、地下水水位控制指标和国家相关技术标准，合理确定本行政区域内地下水取水工程布局。 第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当根据本行政区域内地下水取水总量控制指标、地下水水位控制指标以及科学分析测算的地下水需求量和用水结构，制定地下水年度取水计划，对本行政区域内的年度取用地下水实行总量控制，并报上一级人民政府水行政主管部门备案。 第二十一条　取用地下水的单位和个人应当遵守取水总量控制和定额管理要求，使用先进节约用水技术、工艺和设备，采取循环用水、综合利用及废水处理回用等措施，实施技术改造，降低用水消耗。

### 正文

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门根据地下水状况调查评价成果，统筹考虑经济社会发展需要、地下水资源状况、污染防治等因素，编制本级地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划，依法履行征求意见、论证评估等程序后向社会公布。  
地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划是节约、保护、利用、修复治理地下水的基本依据。地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划应当服从水资源综合规划和环境保护规划。

**Article 12** The competent departments of water administration, natural resources, ecological environment, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the results of investigation and assessment of groundwater conditions, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as economic and social development needs, groundwater resource conditions, and pollution prevention and control, prepare plans for groundwater protection and utilization, pollution prevention and control, and other related plans at the corresponding level, and after performing procedures such as soliciting opinions and conducting demonstration and evaluation according to law, shall release them to the public.  
Plans for groundwater protection and utilization, pollution prevention and control, and other related plans shall be the basic basis for the conservation, protection, utilization, restoration and remediation of groundwater. Such plans shall be subordinate to the comprehensive water resources plan and the environmental protection plan.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国民经济和社会发展规划以及国土空间规划等相关规划的编制、重大建设项目的布局，应当与地下水资源条件和地下水保护要求相适应，并进行科学论证。

**Article 13** The preparation of relevant plans such as the national economic and social development plan and the territorial spatial plan, as well as the layout of major construction projects, shall be compatible with the groundwater resource conditions and groundwater protection requirements, and shall undergo scientific demonstration.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　编制工业、农业、市政、能源、矿产资源开发等专项规划，涉及地下水的内容，应当与地下水保护利用和污染防治等规划相衔接。

**Article 14** The preparation of special plans for industry, agriculture, municipal affairs, energy, mineral resources development, and other fields, insofar as they involve groundwater content, shall be coordinated with plans for groundwater protection and utilization, pollution prevention and control, and other related plans.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家建立地下水储备制度。国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源、发展改革等主管部门，对地下水储备工作进行指导、协调和监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府自然资源、发展改革等主管部门，根据本行政区域内地下水条件、气候状况和水资源储备需要，制定动用地下水储备预案并报本级人民政府批准。  
除特殊干旱年份以及发生重大突发事件外，不得动用地下水储备。

**Article 15** The State shall establish a groundwater reserve system. The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent departments of natural resources, development and reform, and other relevant departments of the State Council, provide guidance, coordination, and supervision and inspection on groundwater reserve work.  
The competent water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the competent departments of natural resources, development and reform, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, formulate plans for using groundwater reserves based on the groundwater conditions, climatic conditions, and the need for water resource reserves within their respective administrative regions, and submit such plans to the people's government at the same level for approval.  
Except in years of exceptional drought and in the event of major emergencies, groundwater reserves shall not be used.

### 第三章　节约与保护

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实行地下水取水总量控制制度。国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院自然资源主管部门，根据各省、自治区、直辖市地下水可开采量和地表水水资源状况，制定并下达各省、自治区、直辖市地下水取水总量控制指标。

**Article 16** The State shall implement a total groundwater withdrawal volume control system. The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, based on the exploitable groundwater volume and the surface water resource conditions of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, formulate and issue total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets for each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门，根据国家下达的地下水取水总量控制指标，制定本行政区域内县级以上行政区域的地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准后下达实施，并报国务院水行政主管部门或者其授权的流域管理机构备案。

**Article 17** The competent water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, based on the total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets issued by the State, formulate total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets and groundwater level control targets for each administrative division at or above the county level within its administrative region, and after approval by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, shall issue them for implementation and submit them to the competent water administrative department of the State Council or its authorized river basin management authority for filing.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门制定本行政区域内地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标时，涉及省际边界区域且属于同一水文地质单元的，应当与相邻省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门协商确定。协商不成的，由国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门确定。

**Article 18** When the competent water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government formulates total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets and groundwater level control targets within its administrative region, if such targets involve inter-provincial boundary areas that belong to the same hydrogeological unit, the department shall consult with the competent water administrative departments of the people's governments of the adjacent provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government to determine them. If consultation fails, the matters shall be determined by the competent water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据地下水取水总量控制指标、地下水水位控制指标和国家相关技术标准，合理确定本行政区域内地下水取水工程布局。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets, groundwater level control targets, and relevant national technical standards, rationally determine the layout of groundwater withdrawal projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当根据本行政区域内地下水取水总量控制指标、地下水水位控制指标以及科学分析测算的地下水需求量和用水结构，制定地下水年度取水计划，对本行政区域内的年度取用地下水实行总量控制，并报上一级人民政府水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 20** The competent water administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the total groundwater withdrawal volume control targets, groundwater level control targets, and the scientifically analyzed and calculated groundwater demand and water use structure within its administrative region, formulate an annual groundwater withdrawal plan, implement total volume control over the annual groundwater withdrawal within its administrative region, and submit the plan to the competent water administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level for filing.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　取用地下水的单位和个人应当遵守取水总量控制和定额管理要求，使用先进节约用水技术、工艺和设备，采取循环用水、综合利用及废水处理回用等措施，实施技术改造，降低用水消耗。  
对下列工艺、设备和产品，应当在规定的期限内停止生产、销售、进口或者使用：  
（一）列入淘汰落后的、耗水量高的工艺、设备和产品名录的；  
（二）列入限期禁止采用的严重污染水环境的工艺名录和限期禁止生产、销售、进口、使用的严重污染水环境的设备名录的。

**Article 21** All units and individuals that withdraw groundwater shall comply with the requirements of total withdrawal volume control and quota management, use advanced water-saving technologies, processes and equipment, adopt measures such as circulating water use, comprehensive utilization, and wastewater treatment and reuse, carry out technological transformation, and reduce water consumption.  
The production, sale, import or use of the following processes, equipment and products shall cease within the prescribed period:  
(1) those listed in the catalog of outdated, backward, and high water-consumption processes, equipment and products;  
(2) those listed in the catalog of processes that are severely polluting to the water environment and prohibited to be adopted within a time limit, and the catalog of equipment that are severely polluting to the water environment and prohibited to be produced, sold, imported or used within a time limit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　新建、改建、扩建地下水取水工程，应当同时安装计量设施。已有地下水取水工程未安装计量设施的，应当按照县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门规定的期限安装。  
单位和个人取用地下水量达到取水规模以上的，应当安装地下水取水在线计量设施，并将计量数据实时传输到有管理权限的水行政主管部门。取水规模由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门制定、公布，并报国务院水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 22** : For new construction, renovation, or expansion of groundwater extraction projects, metering facilities shall be installed simultaneously. For existing groundwater extraction projects without metering facilities, they shall be installed within the time limit prescribed by the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
Where the amount of groundwater extracted by a unit or individual reaches or exceeds the extraction scale, online metering facilities for groundwater extraction shall be installed, and the metering data shall be transmitted in real time to the water administrative department with competence. The extraction scale shall be formulated and announced by the water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the water administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　以地下水为灌溉水源的地区，县级以上地方人民政府应当采取保障建设投入、加大对企业信贷支持力度、建立健全基层水利服务体系等措施，鼓励发展节水农业，推广应用喷灌、微灌、管道输水灌溉、渠道防渗输水灌溉等节水灌溉技术，以及先进的农机、农艺和生物技术等，提高农业用水效率，节约农业用水。

**Article 23** : In areas where groundwater serves as the irrigation water source, the local people's government at or above the county level shall take measures such as ensuring construction investment, increasing credit support to enterprises, and establishing and improving grass-roots water conservancy service systems, to encourage the development of water-saving agriculture, promote the application of water-saving irrigation technologies such as sprinkler irrigation, micro-irrigation, pipeline water conveyance irrigation, canal seepage control irrigation, as well as advanced agricultural machinery, agronomic and biological technologies, so as to improve agricultural water use efficiency and save agricultural water.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院根据国民经济和社会发展需要，对取用地下水的单位和个人试点征收水资源税。地下水水资源税根据当地地下水资源状况、取用水类型和经济发展等情况实行差别税率，合理提高征收标准。征收水资源税的，停止征收水资源费。  
尚未试点征收水资源税的省、自治区、直辖市，对同一类型取用水，地下水的水资源费征收标准应当高于地表水的标准，地下水超采区的水资源费征收标准应当高于非超采区的标准，地下水严重超采区的水资源费征收标准应当大幅高于非超采区的标准。

**Article 24** : The State Council shall, based on the needs of national economic and social development, pilot the levy of a water resource tax on units and individuals that extract groundwater. The groundwater water resource tax shall apply differential tax rates based on local groundwater resource conditions, types of water extraction and use, and economic development, and the levy standard shall be reasonably increased. Where the water resource tax is levied, the collection of water resource fees shall be discontinued.  
For provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government that have not yet piloted the water resource tax, for the same type of water extraction and use, the groundwater water resource fee collection standard shall be higher than that for surface water; the water resource fee collection standard for groundwater overexploitation areas shall be higher than that for non-overexploitation areas; and the water resource fee collection standard for severely overexploited groundwater areas shall be substantially higher than that for non-overexploitation areas.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一的，对取用地下水的取水许可申请不予批准：  
（一）不符合地下水取水总量控制、地下水水位控制要求；  
（二）不符合限制开采区取用水规定；  
（三）不符合行业用水定额和节水规定；  
（四）不符合强制性国家标准；  
（五）水资源紧缺或者生态脆弱地区新建、改建、扩建高耗水项目；  
（六）违反法律、法规的规定开垦种植而取用地下水。

**Article 25** : An application for a water extraction license for groundwater extraction shall not be approved in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) It does not meet the requirements for total groundwater extraction control and groundwater level control;  
(2) It does not comply with the regulations on water extraction and use in restricted extraction areas;  
(3) It does not conform to the industry water use quotas and water-saving regulations;  
(4) It does not meet the mandatory national standards;  
(5) New construction, renovation, or expansion of high water-consumption projects in water-scarce or ecologically fragile areas;  
(6) Extraction of groundwater for reclamation and planting in violation of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　建设单位和个人应当采取措施防止地下工程建设对地下水补给、径流、排泄等造成重大不利影响。对开挖达到一定深度或者达到一定排水规模的地下工程，建设单位和个人应当于工程开工前，将工程建设方案和防止对地下水产生不利影响的措施方案报有管理权限的水行政主管部门备案。开挖深度和排水规模由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定、公布。

**Article 26** : Construction entities and individuals shall take measures to prevent underground engineering construction from causing significant adverse impacts on groundwater recharge, runoff, discharge, etc. For underground engineering where the excavation depth reaches a certain level or the drainage scale reaches a certain extent, the construction entity or individual shall, before the commencement of the project, submit the construction plan and the plan of measures to prevent adverse impacts on groundwater to the water administrative department with competence for recordation. The excavation depth and drainage scale shall be formulated and announced by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　除下列情形外，禁止开采难以更新的地下水：  
（一）应急供水取水；  
（二）无替代水源地区的居民生活用水；  
（三）为开展地下水监测、勘探、试验少量取水。  
已经开采的，除前款规定的情形外，有关县级以上地方人民政府应当采取禁止开采、限制开采措施，逐步实现全面禁止开采；前款规定的情形消除后，应当立即停止取用地下水。

**Article 27** : Except in the following circumstances, the extraction of difficult-to-renew groundwater is prohibited:  
(1) Emergency water supply extraction;  
(2) Residential domestic water use in areas without alternative water sources;  
(3) Small-scale water extraction for groundwater monitoring, exploration, or testing.  
Where extraction has already occurred, except for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall take measures such as prohibiting or restricting extraction, gradually achieving a complete prohibition; after the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph cease to exist, the extraction of groundwater shall be immediately discontinued.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强地下水水源补给保护，充分利用自然条件补充地下水，有效涵养地下水水源。  
城乡建设应当统筹地下水水源涵养和回补需要，按照海绵城市建设的要求，推广海绵型建筑、道路、广场、公园、绿地等，逐步完善滞渗蓄排等相结合的雨洪水收集利用系统。河流、湖泊整治应当兼顾地下水水源涵养，加强水体自然形态保护和修复。  
城市人民政府应当因地制宜采取有效措施，推广节水型生活用水器具，鼓励使用再生水，提高用水效率。

**Article 28** : Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the protection of groundwater source recharge, fully utilize natural conditions to replenish groundwater, and effectively conserve groundwater sources.  
Urban and rural construction shall coordinate the needs for groundwater source conservation and recharge, and, in accordance with the requirements of sponge city construction, promote sponge-type buildings, roads, squares, parks, green spaces, etc., and gradually improve the rainwater and floodwater collection and utilization system that combines retention, infiltration, storage, and drainage. River and lake remediation shall also take into account groundwater source conservation and strengthen the protection and restoration of natural water body forms.  
City people's governments shall take effective measures according to local conditions, promote water-saving domestic water fixtures, encourage the use of reclaimed water, and improve water use efficiency.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据地下水水源条件和需要，建设应急备用饮用水水源，制定应急预案，确保需要时正常使用。  
应急备用地下水水源结束应急使用后，应当立即停止取水。

**Article 29** : Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on groundwater source conditions and needs, construct emergency standby drinking water sources and formulate emergency plans to ensure normal use when needed.  
After the emergency use of an emergency standby groundwater source ends, water extraction shall be immediately discontinued.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有关县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门编制重要泉域保护方案，明确保护范围、保护措施，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
对已经干涸但具有重要历史文化和生态价值的泉域，具备条件的，应当采取措施予以恢复。

**Article 30** : The water administrative department of the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, prepare a protection plan for important spring areas, specifying the scope of protection and protection measures, and submit it to the people's government at the same level for approval before implementation.  
For spring areas that have dried up but have important historical, cultural, and ecological value, where conditions permit, measures shall be taken to restore them.

### 第四章　超采治理

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源主管部门根据地下水状况调查评价成果，组织划定全国地下水超采区，并依法向社会公布。

**Article 31** : The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, and based on the results of groundwater condition investigation and assessment, organize the delineation of national groundwater overexploitation areas and announce them to the public according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府自然资源等主管部门，统筹考虑地下水超采区划定、地下水利用情况以及地质环境条件等因素，组织划定本行政区域内地下水禁止开采区、限制开采区，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准后公布，并报国务院水行政主管部门备案。  
地下水禁止开采区、限制开采区划定后，确需调整的，应当按照原划定程序进行调整。

**Article 32** The water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in conjunction with the natural resources department and other relevant departments of the same level of people's government, taking into consideration factors such as the delineation of groundwater over-exploitation areas, the utilization of groundwater, and geological environment conditions, organize the delineation of groundwater prohibited extraction zones and groundwater restricted extraction zones within its administrative region, and after approval by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, promulgate them and submit them to the water administrative department of the State Council for recordation.  
After the delineation of groundwater prohibited extraction zones and groundwater restricted extraction zones, if adjustment is truly necessary, the adjustment shall be carried out in accordance with the original delineation procedures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有下列情形之一的，应当划为地下水禁止开采区：  
（一）已发生严重的地面沉降、地裂缝、海（咸）水入侵、植被退化等地质灾害或者生态损害的区域；  
（二）地下水超采区内公共供水管网覆盖或者通过替代水源已经解决供水需求的区域；  
（三）法律、法规规定禁止开采地下水的其他区域。

**Article 33** A groundwater prohibited extraction zone shall be designated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Areas where serious geological disasters or ecological damage such as land subsidence, ground fissures, seawater (saline water) intrusion, and vegetation degradation have occurred;  
(2) Areas within groundwater over-exploitation areas that are covered by the public water supply pipeline network or where the water supply demand has already been met through alternative water sources;  
(3) Other areas where groundwater extraction is prohibited by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　有下列情形之一的，应当划为地下水限制开采区：  
（一）地下水开采量接近可开采量的区域；  
（二）开采地下水可能引发地质灾害或者生态损害的区域；  
（三）法律、法规规定限制开采地下水的其他区域。

**Article 34** A groundwater restricted extraction zone shall be designated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Areas where the volume of groundwater extraction is close to the extractable volume;  
(2) Areas where groundwater extraction may cause geological disasters or ecological damage;  
(3) Other areas where groundwater extraction is restricted by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　除下列情形外，在地下水禁止开采区内禁止取用地下水：  
（一）为保障地下工程施工安全和生产安全必须进行临时应急取（排）水；  
（二）为消除对公共安全或者公共利益的危害临时应急取水；  
（三）为开展地下水监测、勘探、试验少量取水。  
除前款规定的情形外，在地下水限制开采区内禁止新增取用地下水，并逐步削减地下水取水量；前款规定的情形消除后，应当立即停止取用地下水。

**Article 35** Except for the following circumstances, groundwater extraction shall be prohibited in groundwater prohibited extraction zones:  
(1) Temporary emergency extraction (or drainage) of water that must be conducted to ensure the safety of underground engineering construction and production safety;  
(2) Temporary emergency extraction of water to eliminate hazards to public safety or public interests;  
(3) A small quantity of water extraction for conducting groundwater monitoring, exploration, or testing.  
Except for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, new groundwater extraction shall be prohibited in groundwater restricted extraction zones, and the volume of groundwater extraction shall be gradually reduced; after the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph are eliminated, groundwater extraction shall be immediately ceased.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门，编制本行政区域地下水超采综合治理方案，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准后，报国务院水行政主管部门备案。  
地下水超采综合治理方案应当明确治理目标、治理措施、保障措施等内容。

**Article 36** The water administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the same level of people's government, formulate a comprehensive groundwater over-exploitation governance plan for its administrative region, and after approval by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, submit it to the water administrative department of the State Council for recordation.  
The comprehensive groundwater over-exploitation governance plan shall specify such contents as governance objectives, governance measures, and safeguard measures.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地下水超采区的县级以上地方人民政府应当加强节水型社会建设，通过加大海绵城市建设力度、调整种植结构、推广节水农业、加强工业节水、实施河湖地下水回补等措施，逐步实现地下水采补平衡。  
国家在替代水源供给、公共供水管网建设、产业结构调整等方面，加大对地下水超采区地方人民政府的支持力度。

**Article 37** The local people's governments at or above the county level in groundwater over-exploitation areas shall strengthen the construction of a water-saving society, and through measures such as increasing the intensity of sponge city construction, adjusting planting structures, promoting water-saving agriculture, strengthening industrial water conservation, and implementing groundwater recharge from rivers and lakes, gradually achieve a balance between groundwater extraction and recharge.  
The State shall increase support to the local people's governments of groundwater over-exploitation areas in such aspects as the supply of alternative water sources, construction of public water supply pipeline networks, and industrial structure adjustment.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　有关县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府自然资源主管部门加强对海（咸）水入侵的监测和预防。已经出现海（咸）水入侵的地区，应当采取综合治理措施。

**Article 38** The water administrative department of the relevant local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the natural resources department of the same level of people's government, strengthen the monitoring and prevention of seawater (saline water) intrusion. In areas where seawater (saline water) intrusion has already occurred, comprehensive governance measures shall be taken.

### 第五章　污染防治

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院水行政、自然资源等主管部门，指导全国地下水污染防治重点区划定工作。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同本级人民政府水行政、自然资源等主管部门，根据本行政区域内地下水污染防治需要，划定地下水污染防治重点区。

**Article 39** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the water administrative department, natural resources department, and other relevant departments of the State Council, guide the work of delineating key areas for groundwater pollution prevention and control nationwide. The competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in conjunction with the water administrative department, natural resources department, and other relevant departments of the same level of people's government, based on the needs of groundwater pollution prevention and control within its administrative region, delineate key areas for groundwater pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　禁止下列污染或者可能污染地下水的行为：  
（一）利用渗井、渗坑、裂隙、溶洞以及私设暗管等逃避监管的方式排放水污染物；  
（二）利用岩层孔隙、裂隙、溶洞、废弃矿坑等贮存石化原料及产品、农药、危险废物、城镇污水处理设施产生的污泥和处理后的污泥或者其他有毒有害物质；  
（三）利用无防渗漏措施的沟渠、坑塘等输送或者贮存含有毒污染物的废水、含病原体的污水和其他废弃物；  
（四）法律、法规禁止的其他污染或者可能污染地下水的行为。

**Article 40** The following acts that pollute or may pollute groundwater are prohibited:  
(1) Discharging water pollutants by means of using seepage wells, seepage pits, fissures, karst caves, and privately installed hidden pipes and other ways to evade supervision;  
(2) Using rock pores, fissures, karst caves, abandoned mine pits, etc. to store petrochemical raw materials and products, pesticides, hazardous wastes, sludge and treated sludge generated from urban sewage treatment facilities, or other toxic and hazardous substances;  
(3) Using ditches, pits, ponds, etc. without anti-leakage measures to transport or store wastewater containing toxic pollutants, sewage containing pathogens, and other wastes;  
(4) Other acts that pollute or may pollute groundwater prohibited by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　企业事业单位和其他生产经营者应当采取下列措施，防止地下水污染：  
（一）兴建地下工程设施或者进行地下勘探、采矿等活动，依法编制的环境影响评价文件中，应当包括地下水污染防治的内容，并采取防护性措施；  
（二）化学品生产企业以及工业集聚区、矿山开采区、尾矿库、危险废物处置场、垃圾填埋场等的运营、管理单位，应当采取防渗漏等措施，并建设地下水水质监测井进行监测；  
（三）加油站等的地下油罐应当使用双层罐或者采取建造防渗池等其他有效措施，并进行防渗漏监测；  
（四）存放可溶性剧毒废渣的场所，应当采取防水、防渗漏、防流失的措施；  
（五）法律、法规规定应当采取的其他防止地下水污染的措施。  
根据前款第二项规定的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者排放有毒有害物质情况，地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定，商有关部门确定并公布地下水污染防治重点排污单位名录。地下水污染防治重点排污单位应当依法安装水污染物排放自动监测设备，与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网，并保证监测设备正常运行。

**Article 41** Enterprises, public institutions, and other producers and operators shall take the following measures to prevent groundwater pollution:  
(1) When constructing underground engineering facilities or conducting activities such as underground exploration or mining, the environmental impact assessment documents prepared according to law shall include the content of groundwater pollution prevention and control, and protective measures shall be taken;  
(2) Chemical production enterprises, and the operation and management units of industrial agglomeration areas, mining areas, tailings ponds, hazardous waste disposal sites, landfills, etc., shall take anti-leakage and other measures, and construct groundwater quality monitoring wells for monitoring;  
(3) Underground oil tanks at gas stations, etc., shall use double-layer tanks or take other effective measures such as building anti-seepage pools, and conduct anti-leakage monitoring;  
(4) Premises where soluble highly toxic waste residues are stored shall take measures for waterproofing, anti-leakage, and anti-loss;  
(5) Other measures to prevent groundwater pollution that shall be taken as provided by laws or regulations.  
Based on the discharge of toxic and hazardous substances by the enterprises, public institutions, and other producers and operators specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, the competent department of ecological environment of the local people's government shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council, consult with relevant departments to determine and publish the list of key sewage-discharging units for groundwater pollution prevention and control. Key sewage-discharging units for groundwater pollution prevention and control shall, according to law, install automatic monitoring equipment for water pollutant discharge, connect to the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment, and ensure the normal operation of the monitoring equipment.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在泉域保护范围以及岩溶强发育、存在较多落水洞和岩溶漏斗的区域内，不得新建、改建、扩建可能造成地下水污染的建设项目。

**Article 42** Within the spring basin protection zones and areas with strong karst development, numerous sinkholes, and karst depressions, no construction, renovation, or expansion of construction projects that may cause groundwater pollution shall be undertaken.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　多层含水层开采、回灌地下水应当防止串层污染。  
多层地下水的含水层水质差异大的，应当分层开采；对已受污染的潜水和承压水，不得混合开采。  
已经造成地下水串层污染的，应当按照封填井技术要求限期回填串层开采井，并对造成的地下水污染进行治理和修复。  
人工回灌补给地下水，应当符合相关的水质标准，不得使地下水水质恶化。

**Article 43** Extraction and artificial recharge of groundwater in multi-layer aquifers shall prevent cross-contamination between layers.  
Where there is significant difference in water quality among aquifers of multi-layer groundwater, extraction shall be conducted by layers; for already contaminated phreatic water and confined water, mixed extraction shall not be permitted.  
Where cross-contamination of groundwater between layers has already occurred, the cross-layer extraction wells shall be sealed and backfilled within a prescribed time limit in accordance with the technical requirements for well sealing and backfilling, and the resulting groundwater pollution shall be treated and remediated.  
Artificial recharge to replenish groundwater shall meet relevant water quality standards and shall not deteriorate the groundwater quality.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人应当科学、合理使用农药、肥料等农业投入品，农田灌溉用水应当符合相关水质标准，防止地下水污染。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强农药、肥料等农业投入品使用指导和技术服务，鼓励和引导农业生产经营者等有关单位和个人合理使用农药、肥料等农业投入品，防止地下水污染。

**Article 44** Agricultural producers and operators and other relevant entities and individuals shall use agricultural inputs such as pesticides and fertilizers in a scientific and reasonable manner, and irrigation water for farmland shall comply with relevant water quality standards to prevent groundwater pollution.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall strengthen guidance and technical services for the use of agricultural inputs such as pesticides and fertilizers, and encourage and guide agricultural producers and operators and other relevant entities and individuals to use agricultural inputs such as pesticides and fertilizers in a reasonable manner to prevent groundwater pollution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　依照《中华人民共和国土壤污染防治法》的有关规定，安全利用类和严格管控类农用地地块的土壤污染影响或者可能影响地下水安全的，制定防治污染的方案时，应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。  
污染物含量超过土壤污染风险管控标准的建设用地地块，编制土壤污染风险评估报告时，应当包括地下水是否受到污染的内容；列入风险管控和修复名录的建设用地地块，采取的风险管控措施中应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。  
对需要实施修复的农用地地块，以及列入风险管控和修复名录的建设用地地块，修复方案中应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。

**Article 45** In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Soil Pollution, where soil pollution in agricultural land plots for safe use and strict control affects or may affect the safety of groundwater, the plan for pollution prevention and control shall include measures for groundwater pollution prevention and control.  
For construction land plots where the pollutant content exceeds the soil pollution risk control standards, the soil pollution risk assessment report shall include the content on whether groundwater is polluted; for construction land plots included in the list for risk control and remediation, the risk control measures adopted shall include measures for groundwater pollution prevention and control.  
For agricultural land plots requiring remediation, and for construction land plots included in the list for risk control and remediation, the remediation plan shall include measures for groundwater pollution prevention and control.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门应当依照职责加强监督管理，完善协作配合机制。  
国务院水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门建立统一的国家地下水监测站网和地下水监测信息共享机制，对地下水进行动态监测。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门根据需要完善地下水监测工作体系，加强地下水监测。

**Article 46** The competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecological environment of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their duties, strengthen supervision and management, and improve coordination and cooperation mechanisms.  
The competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecological environment under the State Council shall establish a unified national groundwater monitoring station network and a groundwater monitoring information sharing mechanism to conduct dynamic monitoring of groundwater.  
The competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecological environment of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as needed, improve the groundwater monitoring work system and strengthen groundwater monitoring.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁坏或者擅自移动地下水监测设施设备及其标志。  
新建、改建、扩建建设工程应当避开地下水监测设施设备；确实无法避开、需要拆除地下水监测设施设备的，应当由县级以上人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门按照有关技术要求组织迁建，迁建费用由建设单位承担。  
任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造地下水监测数据。

**Article 47** No entity or individual shall occupy, damage, or remove without authorization groundwater monitoring facilities and equipment and their signs.  
For new construction, renovation, or expansion of construction projects, groundwater monitoring facilities and equipment shall be avoided; where it is truly impossible to avoid and removal of groundwater monitoring facilities and equipment is necessary, the competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize relocation in accordance with relevant technical requirements, and the relocation costs shall be borne by the construction entity.  
No entity or individual shall tamper with or falsify groundwater monitoring data.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　建设地下水取水工程的单位和个人，应当在申请取水许可时附具地下水取水工程建设方案，并按照取水许可批准文件的要求，自行或者委托具有相应专业技术能力的单位进行施工。施工单位不得承揽应当取得但未取得取水许可的地下水取水工程。  
以监测、勘探为目的的地下水取水工程，不需要申请取水许可，建设单位应当于施工前报有管辖权的水行政主管部门备案。  
地下水取水工程的所有权人负责工程的安全管理。

**Article 48** Entities and individuals constructing groundwater intake works shall, when applying for a water intake permit, attach the construction plan for the groundwater intake works, and carry out the construction by themselves or entrust a unit with appropriate professional technical capability in accordance with the requirements of the water intake permit approval document. The construction unit shall not undertake any groundwater intake works for which a water intake permit should have been obtained but has not been obtained.  
For groundwater intake works for the purpose of monitoring or exploration, no water intake permit is required; the construction entity shall, before construction, report to the water administrative department with jurisdiction for recordation.  
The owner of groundwater intake works shall be responsible for the safety management of the works.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当对本行政区域内的地下水取水工程登记造册，建立监督管理制度。  
报废的矿井、钻井、地下水取水工程，或者未建成、已完成勘探任务、依法应当停止取水的地下水取水工程，应当由工程所有权人或者管理单位实施封井或者回填；所有权人或者管理单位应当将其封井或者回填情况告知县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门;无法确定所有权人或者管理单位的，由县级以上地方人民政府或者其授权的部门负责组织实施封井或者回填。  
实施封井或者回填，应当符合国家有关技术标准。

**Article 49** The water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall register and establish a supervision and management system for groundwater intake works within its administrative area.  
For abandoned mines, wells, or groundwater intake works, or for groundwater intake works that are unfinished, have completed exploration tasks, or are required by law to cease water intake, the owner or management unit of the works shall seal or backfill the well; the owner or management unit shall inform the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level of the sealing or backfilling status; where the owner or management unit cannot be determined, the local people's government at or above the county level or the department authorized by it shall be responsible for organizing and implementing the sealing or backfilling.  
The sealing or backfilling shall comply with relevant national technical standards.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当组织水行政、自然资源、生态环境等主管部门，划定集中式地下水饮用水水源地并公布名录，定期组织开展地下水饮用水水源地安全评估。

**Article 50** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecological environment to delineate centralized groundwater drinking water sources and publish a directory, and regularly organize safety assessments of groundwater drinking water sources.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府自然资源等主管部门，根据水文地质条件和地下水保护要求，划定需要取水的地热能开发利用项目的禁止和限制取水范围。  
禁止在集中式地下水饮用水水源地建设需要取水的地热能开发利用项目。禁止抽取难以更新的地下水用于需要取水的地热能开发利用项目。  
建设需要取水的地热能开发利用项目，应当对取水和回灌进行计量，实行同一含水层等量取水和回灌，不得对地下水造成污染。达到取水规模以上的，应当安装取水和回灌在线计量设施，并将计量数据实时传输到有管理权限的水行政主管部门。取水规模由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门制定、公布。  
对不符合本条第一款、第二款、第三款规定的已建需要取水的地热能开发利用项目，取水单位和个人应当按照水行政主管部门的规定限期整改，整改不合格的，予以关闭。

**Article 51** The water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the competent departments of natural resources and other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, delineate the prohibited and restricted water intake areas for geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake, based on hydrogeological conditions and groundwater protection requirements.  
It is prohibited to construct geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake within centralized groundwater drinking water sources. It is prohibited to extract non-renewable groundwater for geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake.  
For the construction of geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake, the water intake and recharge shall be metered, and an equal quantity of water shall be extracted from and recharged into the same aquifer, and no groundwater pollution shall be caused. For those exceeding a certain scale of water intake, online metering facilities for water intake and recharge shall be installed, and the metering data shall be transmitted in real time to the water administrative department with management authority. The scale of water intake shall be formulated and published by the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For existing geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake and do not comply with the provisions of the first, second, and third paragraphs of this Article, the water intake entities and individuals shall rectify within a prescribed time limit in accordance with the provisions of the water administrative department; if rectification fails to meet the requirements, the projects shall be closed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　矿产资源开采、地下工程建设疏干排水量达到规模的，应当依法申请取水许可，安装排水计量设施，定期向取水许可审批机关报送疏干排水量和地下水水位状况。疏干排水量规模由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定、公布。  
为保障矿井等地下工程施工安全和生产安全必须进行临时应急取（排）水的，不需要申请取水许可。取（排）水单位和个人应当于临时应急取（排）水结束后5个工作日内，向有管理权限的县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门备案。  
矿产资源开采、地下工程建设疏干排水应当优先利用，无法利用的应当达标排放。

**Article 52** Where the drainage volume from dewatering for mineral resources exploitation and underground engineering construction reaches a certain scale, a water-taking permit shall be applied for according to law, drainage metering facilities shall be installed, and the drainage volume and groundwater level status shall be regularly reported to the water-taking permit approval authority. The scale of drainage volume shall be formulated and announced by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For temporary emergency water-taking (drainage) necessary to ensure the construction safety and production safety of underground projects such as mines, no water-taking permit shall be applied for. The unit or individual that takes (drains) water shall, within 5 working days after the completion of the temporary emergency water-taking (drainage), file a record with the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level that has jurisdiction.  
Dewatering drainage from mineral resources exploitation and underground engineering construction shall be prioritized for utilization; where it cannot be utilized, it shall be discharged up to the standard.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府水行政、生态环境等主管部门应当建立从事地下水节约、保护、利用活动的单位和个人的诚信档案，记录日常监督检查结果、违法行为查处等情况，并依法向社会公示。

**Article 53** The competent departments of water administration, ecology and environment, and other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall establish credit files for units and individuals engaged in groundwater conservation, protection, and utilization activities, record the results of routine supervision and inspection, investigation and punishment of illegal acts, and shall publicly disclose such information to the society according to law.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府，县级以上人民政府水行政、生态环境、自然资源主管部门和其他负有地下水监督管理职责的部门有下列行为之一的，由上级机关责令改正，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未采取有效措施导致本行政区域内地下水超采范围扩大，或者地下水污染状况未得到改善甚至恶化；  
（二）未完成本行政区域内地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标；  
（三）对地下水水位低于控制水位未采取相关措施；  
（四）发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的检举后未予查处；  
（五）有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊等违法行为。

**Article 54** Where a local people's government at or above the county level, or the competent departments of water administration, ecology and environment, natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level, or other departments responsible for groundwater supervision and administration commit any of the following acts, the superior authority shall order correction, and shall impose a sanction on the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons according to law:  
(1) failing to take effective measures, resulting in the expansion of the scope of groundwater over-extraction within their administrative region, or the failure to improve or even worsening of groundwater pollution;  
(2) failing to meet the control targets for total groundwater extraction volume and groundwater level within their administrative region;  
(3) failing to take relevant measures when the groundwater level falls below the control level;  
(4) failing to investigate and punish illegal acts upon discovering them or receiving reports of such acts; or  
(5) other illegal acts such as abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，未经批准擅自取用地下水，或者利用渗井、渗坑、裂隙、溶洞以及私设暗管等逃避监管的方式排放水污染物等违法行为，依照《中华人民共和国水法》、《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》、《中华人民共和国土壤污染防治法》、《取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例》等法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 55** Any illegal act in violation of these Regulations, such as unauthorized taking of groundwater without approval, or discharging water pollutants by means of seepage wells, seepage pits, fissures, karst caves, or secretly installed pipes to evade supervision, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China, the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the Soil Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Administration of Water-Taking Permits and Collection of Water Resources Fees, and other laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　地下水取水工程未安装计量设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期安装，并按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量计征相关费用，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。  
计量设施不合格或者运行不正常的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期更换或者修复；逾期不更换或者不修复的，按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量计征相关费用，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 56** Where a groundwater extraction project is not equipped with metering facilities, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order installation within a specified period, and shall levy relevant fees based on the water volume calculated according to the maximum daily extraction capacity, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the water-taking permit shall be revoked.  
Where metering facilities are substandard or malfunctioning, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order replacement or repair within a specified period; if replacement or repair is not carried out within the time limit, the relevant fees shall be levied based on the water volume calculated according to the maximum daily extraction capacity, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the water-taking permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　地下工程建设对地下水补给、径流、排泄等造成重大不利影响的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期采取措施消除不利影响，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；逾期不采取措施消除不利影响的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门组织采取措施消除不利影响，所需费用由违法行为人承担。  
地下工程建设应当于开工前将工程建设方案和防止对地下水产生不利影响的措施方案备案而未备案的，或者矿产资源开采、地下工程建设疏干排水应当定期报送疏干排水量和地下水水位状况而未报送的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期补报；逾期不补报的，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 57** Where underground engineering construction causes significant adverse impacts on groundwater recharge, runoff, discharge, etc., the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the adoption of measures to eliminate the adverse impacts within a specified period, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if measures are not taken to eliminate the adverse impacts within the time limit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the adoption of measures to eliminate the adverse impacts, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Where the construction plan and the plan for measures to prevent adverse impacts on groundwater should have been filed for record before the commencement of underground engineering construction but were not filed, or where the dewatering drainage volume and groundwater level status should have been periodically reported for mineral resources exploitation or underground engineering construction but were not reported, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the making of a supplementary report within a specified period; if the supplementary report is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　报废的矿井、钻井、地下水取水工程，或者未建成、已完成勘探任务、依法应当停止取水的地下水取水工程，未按照规定封井或者回填的，由县级以上地方人民政府或者其授权的部门责令封井或者回填，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；不具备封井或者回填能力的，由县级以上地方人民政府或者其授权的部门组织封井或者回填，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 58** Where an abandoned mine, well, or groundwater extraction project, or a groundwater extraction project that has not been completed, has completed exploration tasks, or should have stopped water extraction according to law, is not sealed or backfilled as required, the local people's government at or above the county level or the department authorized by it shall order sealing or backfilling and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the violator is not capable of sealing or backfilling, the local people's government at or above the county level or the department authorized by it shall organize the sealing or backfilling, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　利用岩层孔隙、裂隙、溶洞、废弃矿坑等贮存石化原料及产品、农药、危险废物或者其他有毒有害物质的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门责令限期改正，处10万元以上100万元以下罚款。  
利用岩层孔隙、裂隙、溶洞、废弃矿坑等贮存城镇污水处理设施产生的污泥和处理后的污泥的，由县级以上地方人民政府城镇排水主管部门责令限期改正，处20万元以上200万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处200万元以上500万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5万元以上50万元以下罚款。  
在泉域保护范围以及岩溶强发育、存在较多落水洞和岩溶漏斗的区域内，新建、改建、扩建造成地下水污染的建设项目的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门处10万元以上50万元以下罚款，并报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令拆除或者关闭。

**Article 59** Where rock pores, fissures, karst caves, abandoned mine pits, etc. are used to store petrochemical raw materials and products, pesticides, hazardous wastes, or other toxic and harmful substances, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government shall order correction within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.  
Where rock pores, fissures, karst caves, abandoned mine pits, etc. are used to store sludge and treated sludge from urban sewage treatment facilities, the competent department of urban drainage of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 2,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons.  
Where a construction project that causes groundwater pollution is newly constructed, reconstructed, or expanded within the protected area of a spring field or within an area with strong karst development and numerous sinkholes and karst funnels, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government shall impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and shall, upon approval by the people's government with approval authority, order demolition or closure.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　侵占、毁坏或者擅自移动地下水监测设施设备及其标志的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；逾期不采取补救措施的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政、自然资源、生态环境主管部门组织补救，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 60** Whoever encroaches upon, damages, or without authorization moves groundwater monitoring facilities, equipment, or their signs shall be ordered by the competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecology and environment of the local people's government at or above the county level to stop the illegal act and take remedial measures within a specified period, and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, the competent departments of water administration, natural resources, and ecology and environment of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize remedial measures, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　以监测、勘探为目的的地下水取水工程在施工前应当备案而未备案的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令限期补办备案手续；逾期不补办备案手续的，责令限期封井或者回填，处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；逾期不封井或者回填的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门组织封井或者回填，所需费用由违法行为人承担。

**Article 61** Where a groundwater extraction project for monitoring or exploration purposes should have been filed for record before construction but was not filed, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the procedures for filing for record to be completed within a specified period; if the filing procedures are not completed within the time limit, an order shall be given to seal the well or backfill within a specified period, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the well is not sealed or backfilled within the time limit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the sealing or backfilling, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Whoever violates these Regulations and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given a public security penalty by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例下列用语含义是：  
地下水取水工程，是指地下水取水井及其配套设施，包括水井、集水廊道、集水池、渗渠、注水井以及需要取水的地热能开发利用项目的取水井和回灌井等。  
地下水超采区，是指地下水实际开采量超过可开采量，引起地下水水位持续下降、引发生态损害和地质灾害的区域。  
难以更新的地下水，是指与大气降水和地表水体没有密切水力联系，无法补给或者补给非常缓慢的地下水。

**Article 63** For the purpose of these Regulations, the meanings of the following terms are:  
Groundwater extraction project means a groundwater intake well and its supporting facilities, including water wells, catchment galleries, catchment basins, infiltration galleries, injection wells, and water intake wells and recharge wells of geothermal energy development and utilization projects that require water intake, among others.  
Groundwater overdraft area means an area where the actual extraction of groundwater exceeds the extractable amount, causing a continuous decline of the groundwater level and resulting in ecological damage and geological disasters.  
Groundwater that is difficult to replenish means groundwater that has no close hydraulic connection with atmospheric precipitation and surface water bodies, and cannot be replenished or is replenished very slowly.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2021年12月1日起施行。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2021.
