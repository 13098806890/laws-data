# 中华人民共和国自然保护区条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2026-02-03  
**生效日期**：2026-03-15

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了高质量推进自然保护区建设，加强对自然保护区的保护和管理，全面推进生态文明和美丽中国建设，加快推进人与自然和谐共生的现代化，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to promote the construction of nature reserves in a high-quality manner, strengthen the protection and management of nature reserves, comprehensively advance ecological civilization and the building of a Beautiful China, and accelerate the modernization of harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域设立自然保护区、开展自然保护区保护和管理及相关活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称自然保护区，是指由国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准设立，以保护典型的自然生态系统、珍稀濒危野生动植物物种的天然集中分布区、有特殊意义的自然遗迹等为主要目的，实现自然资源科学保护和合理利用的特定陆地和海洋区域。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the establishment of nature reserves, the carrying out of protection and management of nature reserves and related activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, a "nature reserve" refers to a specific terrestrial and marine area established with the approval of the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, with the main purposes of protecting typical natural ecosystems, natural concentrated distribution areas of rare and endangered wild animal and plant species, and natural relics of special significance, and achieving scientific protection and rational utilization of natural resources.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　自然保护区建设应当贯彻党和国家路线方针政策、决策部署，践行绿水青山就是金山银山理念，坚持以人民为中心的发展思想，坚持生态保护第一、统筹保护和发展，坚持因地制宜、突出特色，构建政府主导、多方参与、社会共享的机制，实现生态保护、绿色发展、民生改善相统一。

**Article 3** The construction of nature reserves shall implement the lines, principles, policies, decisions, and deployments of the Party and the state, practice the concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets, adhere to the people-centered development philosophy, uphold the priority of ecological protection and the coordination of protection and development, adhere to adapting measures to local conditions and highlighting characteristics, and establish a mechanism led by the government, participated in by multiple parties, and shared by society, so as to achieve the unification of ecological protection, green development, and improvement of people's livelihoods.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和自然保护区所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对自然保护区建设的统筹协调，完善支持自然保护区建设的政策措施，将自然保护区建设纳入国民经济和社会发展相关规划。

**Article 4** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the location of the nature reserve shall strengthen the overall coordination of nature reserve construction, improve policy measures to support nature reserve construction, and incorporate the construction of nature reserves into relevant plans for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立多元化自然保护区资金保障制度。县级以上人民政府应当按照财政事权与支出责任划分原则将自然保护区管理工作所需经费列入本级预算。  
国家鼓励通过设立基金、捐赠、资助等方式，为自然保护区建设提供支持。

**Article 5** The state shall establish a diversified funding guarantee system for nature reserves. The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the funds required for the management of nature reserves in their respective budgets in accordance with the principle of division of fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities.  
The state encourages the provision of support for nature reserve construction through means such as establishing funds, donations, and subsidies.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院林业草原主管部门负责全国自然保护区的监督管理工作。国务院自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村、交通运输等有关部门在各自职责范围内负责自然保护区的有关监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府林业草原主管部门负责本行政区域内自然保护区的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责自然保护区的有关监督管理工作。  
按照规定设立的各自然保护区管理机构依照本条例和规定的职责，负责各该自然保护区的保护和管理工作。

**Article 6** The competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and management of nature reserves nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council for natural resources, ecological environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs, and transportation shall be responsible for the relevant supervision and management of nature reserves within their respective functions.  
The competent department of forestry and grassland under the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and management of nature reserves within its administrative region. Other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the relevant supervision and management of nature reserves within their respective functions.  
The management agencies of each nature reserve established according to law shall be responsible for the protection and management of that nature reserve in accordance with these Regulations and the prescribed functions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院林业草原主管部门和有关部门按照职责建立健全自然保护区相关技术规范和标准体系，依法组织制定并适时修订自然保护区相关技术规范和标准。

**Article 7** The competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council and the relevant departments shall, according to their functions, establish and improve the relevant technical specifications and standards system for nature reserves, and organize the formulation and timely revision of relevant technical specifications and standards for nature reserves according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持自然保护区相关科学技术研究开发和应用推广，加强自然保护区相关专业人才培养，强化科技创新对自然保护区建设的支撑作用。

**Article 8** The state shall encourage and support the research and development, application, and promotion of science and technology related to nature reserves, strengthen the training of professional personnel in nature reserves, and enhance the supporting role of scientific and technological innovation in the construction of nature reserves.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院林业草原主管部门和有关部门、自然保护区所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、自然保护区管理机构应当组织开展多种形式的自然保护区保护宣传教育活动。  
新闻媒体应当开展自然保护区保护公益宣传，依法进行舆论监督。

**Article 9** The competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council and the relevant departments, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the location of the nature reserve and their relevant departments, and the management agencies of the nature reserve shall organize and carry out various forms of publicity and education activities for the protection of nature reserves.  
News media shall conduct public welfare publicity for the protection of nature reserves and carry out oversight by public opinion according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家通过多种方式，支持和促进自然保护区领域的国际交流合作。

**Article 10** The state shall support and promote international exchanges and cooperation in the field of nature reserves through various means.

### 第二章　自然保护区的设立

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设立自然保护区应当具有下列条件之一：  
（一）典型的自然地理区域、自然生态系统区域以及已经遭受破坏但经保护和修复能够恢复的同类自然生态系统区域；  
（二）珍稀濒危野生动植物物种的天然集中分布区；  
（三）具有特殊保护价值的海域、海岸、岛屿、湿地、内陆水域、森林、草原和荒漠；  
（四）具有重大科学文化价值的地质剖面、地质构造、古生物化石集中分布区、地貌景观、地质灾变遗迹等自然遗迹；  
（五）需要予以特殊保护的其他自然区域。  
设立自然保护区，应当充分听取有关方面意见，妥善处理与当地经济社会发展和居民生产生活的关系。

**Article 11** The establishment of a nature reserve shall meet one of the following conditions:  
(1) Typical natural geographical areas, natural ecosystem areas, and similar natural ecosystem areas that have been damaged but can be restored through protection and restoration;  
(2) Natural concentrated distribution areas of rare and endangered wild animal and plant species;  
(3) Sea areas, coasts, islands, wetlands, inland waters, forests, grasslands, and deserts with special protection value;  
(4) Natural relics such as geological sections, geological structures, concentrated distribution areas of paleontological fossils, landform landscapes, and geological disaster relics with significant scientific and cultural value;  
(5) Other natural areas requiring special protection.  
When establishing a nature reserve, opinions from relevant parties shall be fully solicited, and the relationship with local economic and social development and the production and living of residents shall be properly handled.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　自然保护区分为国家级自然保护区和省级自然保护区。  
在国内外有典型意义、在科学上有重大国际影响或者有特殊科学研究价值的自然保护区，以及涉及国家海洋权益的自然保护区，列为国家级自然保护区。其他自然保护区列为省级自然保护区。

**Article 12** Nature reserves are divided into national nature reserves and provincial nature reserves.  
Nature reserves that have typical significance at home and abroad, have significant international scientific influence or have special scientific research value, and nature reserves involving national marine rights and interests shall be classified as national nature reserves. Other nature reserves shall be classified as provincial nature reserves.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　拟设立国家级自然保护区的，由拟设立的自然保护区所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者国务院有关部门提出申请，由国务院林业草原主管部门会同有关部门组织评审后，提出审批建议，报国务院批准。  
拟设立省级自然保护区的，由拟设立的自然保护区所在地设区的市、县级人民政府提出申请，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门会同有关部门组织评审后，提出审批建议，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并抄送国务院林业草原主管部门和有关部门。  
拟设立的自然保护区跨两个以上行政区域的，除由国务院有关部门提出申请的外，由有关行政区域人民政府协商一致后提出申请，按照本条第一款或者第二款规定的程序审批。

**Article 13** For a proposed national nature reserve, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed nature reserve is located, or the relevant department of the State Council shall submit an application. After evaluation organized by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments, an approval recommendation shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
For a proposed provincial nature reserve, the people's government of the city divided into districts or the county where the proposed nature reserve is located shall submit an application. After evaluation organized by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the relevant departments, an approval recommendation shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval, with a copy sent to the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council and the relevant departments.  
If a proposed nature reserve spans two or more administrative regions, except in cases where the application is submitted by a relevant department of the State Council, the application shall be submitted after the people's governments of the relevant administrative regions have reached consensus through consultation, and the approval shall be processed in accordance with the procedures specified in the first or second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家级自然保护区以所在地地名加“国家级自然保护区”命名；省级自然保护区以所在地地名加“省级自然保护区”命名。有特殊保护对象的自然保护区，可以在所在地地名后加特殊保护对象的名称。

**Article 14** A national nature reserve shall be named by adding "National Nature Reserve" to the name of the place where it is located; a provincial nature reserve shall be named by adding "Provincial Nature Reserve" to the name of the place where it is located. For a nature reserve with special protection objects, the name of the special protection object may be added after the name of the place where it is located.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自然保护区区域范围的划定应当统筹考虑自然生态系统等保护对象分布区域的完整性、管理可行性和周边经济社会发展的需要，坚持实事求是、科学合理，经过充分调查和科学论证。  
自然保护区区域范围内不再保留自然公园类自然保护地。自然保护区全部划入国家公园区域范围的，不再保留；部分划入的，未划入部分经科学评估并按照规定程序批准，予以整合或者撤销。

**Article 15** The delineation of the boundaries of a nature reserve shall take into account the integrity of the distribution areas of protected objects such as natural ecosystems, the feasibility of management, and the needs of surrounding economic and social development, adhere to the principles of seeking truth from facts and being scientific and reasonable, and be carried out after sufficient investigation and scientific demonstration.  
No natural park-type protected areas shall be retained within the boundaries of a nature reserve. If a nature reserve is entirely included within the boundaries of a national park, it shall no longer be retained; if it is partially included, the part not included shall be integrated or revoked after scientific assessment and approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　自然保护区划分为核心保护区和一般控制区，实行分区管控。  
自然保护区的下列区域应当划为核心保护区：  
（一）自然生态系统保存完整或者生态脆弱需要休养生息的区域；  
（二）珍稀濒危野生动植物物种的关键分布区域以及生态廊道重要节点；  
（三）重要自然遗迹的集中分布区域；  
（四）其他需要重点保护的区域。  
核心保护区以外的区域划为一般控制区。

**Article 16** A nature reserve shall be divided into a core protection zone and a general control zone, and shall be subject to zoning control.  
The following areas of a nature reserve shall be designated as the core protection zone:  
(1) Areas where natural ecosystems are well-preserved or ecologically fragile and require rest and restoration;  
(2) Key distribution areas of rare and endangered wild animal and plant species and important nodes of ecological corridors;  
(3) Concentrated distribution areas of important natural relics;  
(4) Other areas requiring key protection.  
Areas outside the core protection zone shall be designated as the general control zone.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　设立自然保护区后，自然保护区所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照有关规定及时组织完成自然保护区勘界；涉及国家海洋权益的自然保护区，由国务院有关部门组织完成勘界。自然保护区管理机构应当根据保护和管理需要及时设置自然保护区界碑界桩、电子标识或者电子围栏等界线标志。  
禁止损毁、涂改、遮挡或者擅自拆除、移动自然保护区界线标志。

**Article 17** After the establishment of a nature reserve, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the nature reserve is located shall promptly organize the completion of boundary delimitation of the nature reserve according to relevant regulations; for a nature reserve involving national marine rights and interests, the relevant department of the State Council shall organize the completion of boundary delimitation. The management agency of the nature reserve shall, based on the needs of protection and management, promptly set up boundary markers, electronic markers, or electronic fences and other boundary signs for the nature reserve.  
It is prohibited to damage, alter, obscure, or remove or move boundary signs of a nature reserve without authorization.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家级自然保护区、省级自然保护区的名称、区域范围、管控分区分别由国务院和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府在批准设立时一并批复，并分别由国务院林业草原主管部门和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门依法公布。  
国务院林业草原主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门应当与有关部门共享自然保护区区域范围、管控分区坐标等信息。

**Article 18** The name, boundary scope, and control zoning of a national nature reserve and a provincial nature reserve shall be approved together by the State Council and the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located respectively at the time of approval of establishment, and shall be published according to law by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council and the competent department of forestry and grassland of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government respectively.  
The competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council and the competent departments of forestry and grassland of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall share information such as the coordinates of the boundary scope and control zoning of nature reserves with relevant departments.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因自然保护区主要保护对象生存环境发生重大改变，确需调整自然保护区区域范围的，应当按照自然保护区设立的程序，经原批准设立该自然保护区的人民政府批准。自然保护区变更名称，按照前述程序办理。  
国家级自然保护区、省级自然保护区调整管控分区，应当分别经国务院林业草原主管部门和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门会同同级有关部门组织评审后批准。  
自然保护区设立后，原则上不得撤销；因特殊情形失去保护价值的，经原批准设立该自然保护区的人民政府批准，可以撤销。

**Article 19** If, due to major changes in the living environment of the main protected objects of a nature reserve, it is necessary to adjust the boundary scope of the nature reserve, the adjustment shall be approved by the people's government that originally approved the establishment of the nature reserve in accordance with the procedure for establishing a nature reserve. A change of name of a nature reserve shall be handled in accordance with the aforementioned procedures.  
An adjustment of the control zoning of a national nature reserve or a provincial nature reserve shall be approved after evaluation organized by the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council or the competent department of forestry and grassland of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the relevant departments at the same level, respectively.  
After a nature reserve is established, it shall not be revoked in principle; if it loses its protection value due to special circumstances, it may be revoked with the approval of the people's government that originally approved its establishment.

### 第三章　自然保护区的保护和管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家坚持山水林田湖草沙一体化保护，按照自然生态系统特性和内在规律，对自然保护区实行整体保护、系统修复、综合治理。

**Article 20** The state shall adhere to the integrated protection of mountains, waters, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands, and deserts, and shall implement overall protection, systematic restoration, and comprehensive management of nature reserves in accordance with the characteristics and inherent laws of natural ecosystems.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　自然保护区管理机构在综合科学考察和专项调查的基础上，根据需要组织编制所管理自然保护区的相关规划（以下称自然保护区规划），明确保护和管理的目标任务、保护对象、管理要求、保护措施等事项，并对原有居民生产生活活动等作出安排。组织编制自然保护区规划，应当广泛听取有关方面意见、深入论证。  
国家级自然保护区规划经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门审查同意后，报国务院林业草原主管部门批准实施；涉及国家海洋权益的自然保护区规划，经国务院有关部门审查同意后，报国务院林业草原主管部门批准实施。省级自然保护区规划报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门批准实施。规划审批前，应当征求同级有关部门意见。  
自然保护区规划应当与国民经济和社会发展规划、国土空间总体规划等相衔接，并纳入国土空间规划实施监督信息系统，接受统一监管。自然保护区规划应当依法公开。

**Article 21** On the basis of comprehensive scientific surveys and special investigations, the management authority of a nature reserve shall, as necessary, organize the formulation of relevant plans for the nature reserve under its management (hereinafter referred to as nature reserve plans), specifying the objectives and tasks, protection objects, management requirements, protection measures and other matters for protection and management, and make arrangements for the production and living activities of original residents, among others. In organizing the formulation of nature reserve plans, opinions from relevant parties shall be extensively solicited and in-depth demonstration shall be conducted.  
Plans for national nature reserves shall, after examination and approval by the competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality where they are located, be submitted to the competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council for approval and implementation; plans for nature reserves involving national maritime rights and interests shall, after examination and approval by the relevant departments of the State Council, be submitted to the competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council for approval and implementation. Plans for provincial nature reserves shall be submitted to the competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality where they are located for approval and implementation. Before approval of plans, opinions of relevant departments at the same level shall be solicited.  
Nature reserve plans shall be coordinated with the national economic and social development plan, the overall territorial space planning, among others, and be incorporated into the territorial space planning implementation supervision information system, and be subject to unified supervision and regulation. Nature reserve plans shall be made public according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　自然保护区管理机构应当依据法律、法规、国家有关规定和自然保护区规划，健全完善自然保护区保护和管理的规章制度，加强自然保护区日常保护和管理工作。  
自然保护区区域内的单位、个人以及进入自然保护区的人员，应当遵守法律、法规、自然保护区保护和管理的规章制度，接受自然保护区管理机构的管理。

**Article 22** The management authority of a nature reserve shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant provisions of the State and nature reserve plans, improve and perfect the rules and regulations for the protection and management of the nature reserve, and strengthen the daily protection and management work of the nature reserve.  
Units, individuals within the area of a nature reserve and persons entering the nature reserve shall abide by laws, regulations, and the rules and regulations for the protection and management of the nature reserve, and accept the management of the management authority of the nature reserve.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对自然保护区区域内的自然资源，按照国家自然资源确权登记有关规定进行确权登记，确权登记时应当将自然保护区作为独立登记单元。

**Article 23** Natural resources within the area of a nature reserve shall be registered for confirmation of rights in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on confirmation and registration of natural resources, and when such registration is made, the nature reserve shall be taken as an independent registration unit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家加强自然保护区监测网络体系建设，充分发挥各类监测站点的作用，运用信息化手段加强监测数据集成分析、共享和综合应用，全面掌握自然生态系统构成、分布、动态变化和生物多样性状况以及生态环境质量、人为活动干扰情况，及时评估和预警生态风险。

**Article 24** The State shall strengthen the construction of a monitoring network system for nature reserves, give full play to the role of various monitoring stations, use information technology means to strengthen the integrated analysis, sharing and comprehensive application of monitoring data, comprehensively grasp the composition, distribution, dynamic changes of natural ecosystems, the status of biodiversity, the quality of the ecological environment and the situation of human activity interference, and promptly assess and give early warning of ecological risks.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然保护区区域内受损自然生态系统修复、生态廊道连通、重要栖息地恢复等应当坚持自然恢复为主；确有必要开展种群调控、树种更新等人工修复活动的，应当充分听取有关方面意见，按照有关规定制定科学合理的修复方案并依法实施。  
自然保护区管理机构应当采取措施，加强对外来物种入侵的防范和应对，维护生态系统安全，提高生态系统质量。

**Article 25** Restoration of damaged natural ecosystems, connection of ecological corridors, restoration of important habitats within the area of a nature reserve shall adhere to the principle of giving priority to natural restoration; where it is truly necessary to carry out artificial restoration activities such as population regulation and tree species renewal, opinions from relevant parties shall be fully solicited, and scientific and reasonable restoration plans shall be formulated in accordance with relevant provisions and implemented according to law.  
The management authority of a nature reserve shall take measures to strengthen the prevention and response to the invasion of alien species, maintain the safety of the ecosystem and improve the quality of the ecosystem.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　除下列活动外，自然保护区核心保护区内禁止人为活动：  
（一）为保护自然保护区开展的调查监测、生态修复、管护巡护等活动，科研观测、基础测绘、文物和其他文化遗产保护、防灾减灾、应急救援活动，以及国家机关依法履行执法职责确需开展的活动；  
（二）原有居民必要的生产生活活动，以及确需保留、无法避让的已有重要基础设施的运行、维护、改造；  
（三）必须且无法避让、以生态环境无害化方式穿越地下、水下或者空中的线性基础设施建设；  
（四）为维护国家安全、实施国家重大战略确需开展的活动，以及无法避让的国家重大项目建设；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定或者国务院批准的其他活动。

**Article 26** Except for the following activities, human activities are prohibited in the core protection area of a nature reserve:  
(1) Activities such as survey and monitoring, ecological restoration, management and patrol conducted for the protection of the nature reserve, scientific research observation, basic surveying and mapping, protection of cultural relics and other cultural heritage, disaster prevention and reduction, emergency rescue activities, and activities truly necessary for State organs to perform their law enforcement duties;  
(2) Necessary production and living activities of original residents, and the operation, maintenance and renovation of existing important infrastructure that truly needs to be retained and cannot be avoided;  
(3) Construction of linear infrastructure that is necessary, cannot be avoided, and passes through underground, underwater or air in an ecologically harmless manner;  
(4) Activities truly necessary for maintaining national security and implementing major national strategies, and construction of major national projects that cannot be avoided;  
(5) Other activities prescribed by laws, administrative regulations or approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　自然保护区一般控制区内仅允许开展下列人为活动：  
（一）核心保护区允许开展的活动；  
（二）符合国土空间规划且无法避让的重要基础设施的建设、运行和维护；  
（三）古生物化石调查发掘，基础地质调查，战略性矿产资源远景调查和规定范围内的战略性矿产资源勘查；  
（四）珍稀濒危野生动植物的野化、繁殖，非破坏性的标本采集活动；  
（五）与自然保护区保护目标一致的人工商品林抚育、树种更新等森林经营活动；  
（六）科普宣传、生态旅游、教育文化体育等公共服务活动；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定或者国务院批准的其他活动。

**Article 27** Only the following human activities are allowed in the general control area of a nature reserve:  
(1) Activities allowed in the core protection area;  
(2) Construction, operation and maintenance of important infrastructure that conforms to the territorial space planning and cannot be avoided;  
(3) Investigation and excavation of paleontological fossils, basic geological survey, prospective survey of strategic mineral resources and exploration of strategic mineral resources within the prescribed scope;  
(4) Reintroduction and reproduction of rare and endangered wild animals and plants, non-destructive specimen collection activities;  
(5) Forest management activities such as tending of artificial commercial forests and tree species renewal consistent with the protection objectives of the nature reserve;  
(6) Public service activities such as science popularization, eco-tourism, education, culture and sports;  
(7) Other activities prescribed by laws, administrative regulations or approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对下列情形的自然保护区，经科学论证，在确保主要保护对象安全和不损害生态功能的前提下，可以实行差别化管控措施：  
（一）主要保护对象为自然遗迹的自然保护区，可以在核心保护区建设必要的防护、陈列、展示等设施，开展古生物化石调查发掘以及适度的科普宣传、生态旅游、教育文化体育等公共服务活动；  
（二）主要保护对象位于地下的自然保护区，其核心保护区地上部分可以按照一般控制区保护和管理；  
（三）自然生态过程、主要保护对象生息繁衍具有明显季节性变化规律的自然保护区，可以实行季节性差别管控措施。

**Article 28** For nature reserves in the following circumstances, after scientific demonstration and on the premise of ensuring the safety of the main protection objects and not damaging the ecological functions, differentiated control measures may be implemented:  
(1) For a nature reserve whose main protection objects are natural relics, necessary facilities such as protection, display and exhibition may be constructed in the core protection area, and activities such as paleontological fossil investigation and excavation, and appropriate public service activities such as science popularization, eco-tourism, education, culture and sports may be carried out;  
(2) For a nature reserve whose main protection objects are underground, the above-ground part of the core protection area may be protected and managed in accordance with the general control area;  
(3) For a nature reserve whose natural ecological process and the breeding and reproduction of main protection objects have obvious seasonal variation patterns, seasonal differentiated control measures may be implemented.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　自然保护区区域内原有居民的生产生活活动，应当以不超出现有规模和利用强度、合理改善生产生活条件为前提。  
根据保护和管理需要，自然保护区区域内原有居民确有必要迁出的，由有关地方人民政府妥善安置。

**Article 29** The production and living activities of original residents within the area of a nature reserve shall be on the premise of not exceeding the existing scale and utilization intensity and reasonably improving production and living conditions.  
Where it is truly necessary to relocate original residents within the area of a nature reserve according to the needs of protection and management, the relevant local people's government shall properly resettle them.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未经批准，外国人不得在自然保护区区域内从事标本采集等活动。

**Article 30** Without approval, foreigners shall not engage in activities such as specimen collection within the area of a nature reserve.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　在自然保护区开展[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十七条、第二十八条规定的活动，依法需要办理相关手续的，有关单位和个人应当依法办理；涉及修筑设施的，还应当经国务院林业草原主管部门或者所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府林业草原主管部门同意，但原有居民必要的生产生活活动、原有线性基础设施的运行维护活动以及自然保护区管理机构依据自然保护区规划修筑管护巡护、科研观测、科普宣传、防灾减灾等设施的除外。  
在自然保护区开展调查监测、科研观测、标本采集的单位和个人，应当按照自然保护区管理机构的要求，提交相关活动成果。在自然保护区开展[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十七条、第二十八条规定的活动，应当采取必要措施，避免或者减少对自然生态系统、生态廊道、重要栖息地以及自然和人文景观的不利影响。自然保护区管理机构应当根据实际情况和需要，明确开展相关活动的边界、强度以及应当采取的保护性措施等。

**Article 31** Where activities specified in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of these Regulations are carried out in a nature reserve and relevant procedures are required to be handled according to law, the relevant units and individuals shall handle them according to law; if construction of facilities is involved, consent from the competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council or the competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality where it is located shall also be obtained, except for necessary production and living activities of original residents, operation and maintenance activities of existing linear infrastructure, and construction of facilities such as management and patrol, scientific research observation, science popularization, disaster prevention and reduction by the management authority of the nature reserve in accordance with the nature reserve plan.  
Units and individuals that carry out survey and monitoring, scientific research observation, and specimen collection in a nature reserve shall, in accordance with the requirements of the management authority of the nature reserve, submit the results of relevant activities. Where activities specified in Articles 26, 27 and 28 of these Regulations are carried out in a nature reserve, necessary measures shall be taken to avoid or reduce adverse effects on natural ecosystems, ecological corridors, important habitats, and natural and cultural landscapes. The management authority of the nature reserve shall, based on the actual situation and needs, specify the boundaries, intensity and protective measures to be taken for carrying out relevant activities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　自然保护区管理机构应当建立日常巡护制度，合理配备巡护人员和巡护装备，加强巡护站点建设。巡护人员应当观察和记录主要保护对象生境状况及其变化情况，对违反自然保护区保护和管理规定的行为予以劝阻、制止并及时报告。

**Article 32** The management authority of a nature reserve shall establish a daily patrol system, reasonably equip patrol personnel and patrol equipment, and strengthen the construction of patrol stations. Patrol personnel shall observe and record the habitat status of the main protection objects and their changes, and shall dissuade, stop and promptly report any act that violates the provisions on protection and management of the nature reserve.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　自然保护区管理机构应当制定自然保护区突发事件应急预案，加强与所在地人民政府有关部门的协作配合，做好防灾减灾、安全管理和应急保障相关工作，提升应急保障能力。

**Article 33** The management authority of a nature reserve shall formulate an emergency response plan for the nature reserve, strengthen coordination and cooperation with the relevant departments of the local people's government, do a good job in disaster prevention and reduction, safety management and emergency support related work, and enhance the emergency support capability.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　自然保护区管理机构应当在确保生态得到保护的前提下，完善自然保护区公共服务体系，提升公共服务功能。自然保护区一般控制区可以划定适当区域，设置必要的辅助设施设备，为开展相关科学研究、科普宣传、生态旅游、教育文化体育等公共服务活动提供支持。  
自然保护区管理机构应当加强自然保护区访客管理和服务，合理确定访客容量，明确访客行为规范，按照国家有关规定提供必要的无障碍服务，完善访客安全保障和紧急救助等相关机制。  
自然保护区区域内不得开设与保护目标不一致的参观、旅游等项目。

**Article 34** The management authority of a nature reserve shall, on the premise of ensuring ecological protection, improve the public service system of the nature reserve and enhance the public service function. In the general control area of a nature reserve, appropriate areas may be designated and necessary auxiliary facilities and equipment may be set up to provide support for carrying out public service activities such as relevant scientific research, science popularization, eco-tourism, education, culture and sports.  
The management authority of a nature reserve shall strengthen the management and services for visitors to the nature reserve, reasonably determine the visitor capacity, specify the code of conduct for visitors, provide necessary barrier-free services in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and improve related mechanisms such as visitor safety protection and emergency rescue.  
No visits, tourism or other projects inconsistent with the protection objectives shall be opened within the area of a nature reserve.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府林业草原主管部门，自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村等有关部门，以及自然保护区管理机构，应当依据职责加强对自然保护区保护情况的监督检查，依法查处违法行为。  
海警机构依照《中华人民共和国海警法》的规定，在职责范围内对自然保护区区域内海岸线向海一侧的保护情况进行监督检查，依法查处违法行为。  
监督检查人员开展监督检查，应当主动出示执法证件。被检查单位和个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。  
公安机关可以根据需要在自然保护区设置工作站点，开展生态警务工作。  
有关国家机关依法在自然保护区履行法定职责的，自然保护区管理机构应当提供必要便利。

**Article 35** The competent department of forestry and grassland of the people's government at or above the county level, the relevant departments of natural resources, ecological environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs, among others, and the management authority of a nature reserve shall, according to their functions and duties, strengthen the supervision and inspection of the protection of the nature reserve and investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law.  
The coast guard agency shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Coast Guard Law of the People's Republic of China, supervise and inspect the protection of the seaward side of the coastline within the area of a nature reserve within its functions and duties, and investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law.  
Inspectors conducting supervision and inspection shall proactively present their law enforcement credentials. The units and individuals under inspection shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
Public security organs may, as needed, set up work stations within a nature reserve to carry out ecological police work.  
Where relevant State organs perform statutory duties in a nature reserve according to law, the management authority of the nature reserve shall provide necessary convenience.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对违反自然保护区保护和管理规定的行为，任何单位和个人有权向自然保护区管理机构或者有关部门举报，接到举报的自然保护区管理机构和有关部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 36** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act that violates the provisions on protection and management of a nature reserve to the management authority of the nature reserve or the relevant department, and the management authority or the relevant department that receives the report shall handle it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　自然保护区管理机构应当依法及时公开自然保护区保护和管理等工作情况，自觉接受社会监督和舆论监督。

**Article 37** The management authority of a nature reserve shall promptly make public the work of protection and management of the nature reserve according to law, and consciously accept social supervision and oversight by public opinion.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　自然保护区管理机构、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、国务院有关部门工作人员在自然保护区保护和管理等工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 38** Where functionaries of the management authority of a nature reserve, the local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments, and the relevant departments of the State Council abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the protection and management of a nature reserve, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　损毁、涂改、遮挡或者擅自拆除、移动自然保护区界线标志的，由自然保护区管理机构责令停止违法行为、采取补救措施，可以处2万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
在自然保护区开展调查监测、科研观测、标本采集的单位和个人，未向自然保护区管理机构提交相关活动成果的，由自然保护区管理机构责令改正，可以处5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Anyone who damages, alters, obscures, or without authorization dismantles or removes boundary markers of a nature reserve shall be ordered by the management authority of the nature reserve to cease the illegal act and take remedial measures, and may be fined not more than 20,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
Where units and individuals that carry out survey and monitoring, scientific research observation, and specimen collection in a nature reserve fail to submit the results of relevant activities to the management authority of the nature reserve, the management authority of the nature reserve shall order them to make corrections, and may impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　在自然保护区核心保护区内开展[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定以外的活动的，由县级以上人民政府林业草原、自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村等有关部门按照职责分工责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处10万元以下的罚款；属于违法修筑设施或者进行工程建设的，责令限期拆除、恢复原状，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Anyone who carries out activities other than those specified in Article 26 of these Regulations in the core protection area of a nature reserve shall be ordered by the relevant departments of forestry and grassland, natural resources, ecological environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level, according to their division of functions and duties, to cease the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains, and may be fined not more than 100,000 yuan; if it involves illegal construction of facilities or engineering construction, the person shall be ordered to dismantle within a time limit and restore the original condition, and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if ecological damage is caused, the person shall be ordered to restore within a time limit or take other remedial measures, and be fined not less than 1,000,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在自然保护区一般控制区内开展[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定以外的活动的，由县级以上人民政府林业草原、自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村等有关部门按照职责分工责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以处5万元以下的罚款；属于违法修筑设施或者进行工程建设的，责令限期拆除、恢复原状，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where an activity other than that specified in Article 27 of these Regulations is carried out in the general control area of a nature reserve, the relevant departments of forestry and grassland, natural resources, ecological environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if an illegal construction of facilities or engineering construction is involved, the offender shall be ordered to demolish within a specified period and restore the original state, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if ecological damage is caused, the offender shall be ordered to restore within a specified period or take other remedial measures, and a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　自然保护区区域内原有居民违反[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)第一款规定开展生产生活活动的，由自然保护区管理机构责令改正，根据情节轻重可以处2000元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where an original resident in a nature reserve area carries out production or living activities in violation of paragraph 1 of Article 29 of these Regulations, the nature reserve management authority shall order correction, and may, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在自然保护区区域内开展[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第二十七条、第二十八条规定的活动，未采取必要措施避免或者减少对自然生态系统、生态廊道、重要栖息地以及自然和人文景观的不利影响的，由县级以上人民政府林业草原、自然资源、生态环境、水行政、农业农村等有关部门按照职责分工责令改正，可以处10万元以下的罚款；造成生态破坏的，责令限期修复或者采取其他补救措施，相关活动在核心保护区开展的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，在一般控制区开展的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where an activity specified in Article 26, 27 or 28 of these Regulations is carried out in a nature reserve area without taking necessary measures to avoid or reduce adverse impacts on the natural ecosystem, ecological corridors, important habitats, and natural and cultural landscapes, the relevant departments of forestry and grassland, natural resources, ecological environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order correction, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if ecological damage is caused, the offender shall be ordered to restore within a specified period or take other remedial measures; if the relevant activity is carried out in a core protection area, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it is carried out in a general control area, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　[本条例第四十条](#art-40)、第四十一条、第四十三条规定的违法行为，发生在自然保护区区域内海岸线向海一侧的，海警机构在职责范围内依法实施处罚。  
对[本条例第四十条](#art-40)、第四十一条、第四十三条规定的违法行为，有关行政法规规定的罚款数额高于本条例规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 44** Where an illegal act as specified in Article 40, 41 or 43 of these Regulations occurs on the seaward side of the coastline within a nature reserve area, the coast guard authority shall, within its scope of duties, impose penalties according to law.  
For an illegal act as specified in Article 40, 41 or 43 of these Regulations, if the amount of fine prescribed by relevant administrative regulations is higher than that prescribed in these Regulations, the former shall apply.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　自然保护区区域内的单位、个人以及进入自然保护区的人员不服从自然保护区管理机构管理的，自然保护区管理机构有权予以劝阻、制止；构成扰乱公共秩序等违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 45** Where a unit or individual within a nature reserve area or a person entering a nature reserve fails to comply with the management of the nature reserve management authority, the nature reserve management authority shall have the right to dissuade and stop such conduct; if the conduct constitutes a disturbance of public order or other act violating public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，破坏自然资源或者污染环境、破坏生态的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, destroys natural resources or pollutes the environment or damages the ecology, shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　涉及国家海洋权益的自然保护区，由国务院林业草原主管部门会同有关部门根据本条例制定具体管理办法。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例，制定实施办法。

**Article 47** For a nature reserve involving national maritime rights and interests, the competent department of forestry and grassland under the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, formulate specific management measures in accordance with these Regulations.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate implementation measures.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2026年3月15日起施行。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 15, 2026.
