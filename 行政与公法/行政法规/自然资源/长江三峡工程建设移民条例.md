# 长江三峡工程建设移民条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：2001-03-01

> (2001年2月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第299号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了做好三峡工程建设移民工作，维护移民合法权益，保障三峡工程建设，促进三峡库区经济和社会发展，制定本条例。 第二条　三峡工程建设移民，适用本条例。 第三条　三峡工程建设，实行开发性移民方针，统筹使用移民资金，合理开发资源，保护生态环境，妥善安置移民，使移民的生产、生活达到或者超过原有水平，为三峡库区经济和社会发展创造条件。 第四条　三峡工程建设移民工作应当与三峡库区建设、沿江地区对外开放、水土保持和环境保护相结合。 第五条　三峡工程建设移民，实行国家扶持、各方支援与自力更生相结合的原则，采取前期补偿、补助与后期生产扶持相结合的方针，兼顾国家、集体和个人的利益。 三峡工程淹没区、移民安置区所在地的人民政府和群众应当顾全大局，服从国家统筹安排，正确处理移民搬迁和经济发展的关系。 第六条　三峡工程建设移民，实行移民任务和移民资金包干的原则。 第七条　国家对三峡工程建设移民依法给予补偿。具体补偿标准由国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构会同国务院有关部门组织测算、拟订，报国务院批准后执行。 第八条　三峡工程建设移民工作实行统一领导、分省(直辖市)负责、以县为基础的管理体制。 国务院三峡工程建设委员会是三峡工程建设移民工作的领导决策机构。 国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构负责三峡工程建设移民工作。 湖北省、重庆市人民政府负责本行政区域内三峡工程建设移民工作，并设立三峡工程建设移民管理机构。 三峡工程淹没区和移民安置区所在地的市、县、区人民政府负责本行政区域内三峡工程建设移民工作，并可以根据需要设立三峡工程建设移民管理机构。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了做好三峡工程建设移民工作，维护移民合法权益，保障三峡工程建设，促进三峡库区经济和社会发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of properly carrying out the resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the resettled persons, ensuring the construction of the Three Gorges Project, and promoting the economic and social development of the Three Gorges Reservoir Area.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　三峡工程建设移民，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　三峡工程建设，实行开发性移民方针，统筹使用移民资金，合理开发资源，保护生态环境，妥善安置移民，使移民的生产、生活达到或者超过原有水平，为三峡库区经济和社会发展创造条件。

**Article 3** The construction of the Three Gorges Project shall implement the policy of development-oriented resettlement, make overall use of resettlement funds, rationally develop resources, protect the ecological environment, properly resettle the resettled persons, ensure that the production and living standards of the resettled persons reach or exceed the original levels, and create conditions for the economic and social development of the Three Gorges Reservoir Area.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　三峡工程建设移民工作应当与三峡库区建设、沿江地区对外开放、水土保持和环境保护相结合。

**Article 4** The resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall be combined with the construction of the Three Gorges Reservoir Area, the opening-up of the areas along the river, water and soil conservation, and environmental protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　三峡工程建设移民，实行国家扶持、各方支援与自力更生相结合的原则，采取前期补偿、补助与后期生产扶持相结合的方针，兼顾国家、集体和个人的利益。  
三峡工程淹没区、移民安置区所在地的人民政府和群众应当顾全大局，服从国家统筹安排，正确处理移民搬迁和经济发展的关系。

**Article 5** The resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall implement the principle of combining state support, assistance from all parties, and self-reliance, adopt the policy of combining preliminary compensation and subsidies with later-stage production support, and balance the interests of the state, the collective, and individuals.  
The people's governments and the masses in the areas where the Three Gorges Project inundation area and resettlement area are located shall take the overall situation into consideration, submit to the state's overall arrangements, and correctly handle the relationship between the relocation of resettled persons and economic development.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　三峡工程建设移民，实行移民任务和移民资金包干的原则。

**Article 6** The resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall implement the principle of lump-sum contracting for resettlement tasks and resettlement funds.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对三峡工程建设移民依法给予补偿。具体补偿标准由国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构会同国务院有关部门组织测算、拟订，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 7** The state shall grant compensation according to law to the resettled persons for the construction of the Three Gorges Project. The specific compensation standards shall be calculated and formulated by the resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　三峡工程建设移民工作实行统一领导、分省(直辖市)负责、以县为基础的管理体制。  
国务院三峡工程建设委员会是三峡工程建设移民工作的领导决策机构。  
国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构负责三峡工程建设移民工作。  
湖北省、重庆市人民政府负责本行政区域内三峡工程建设移民工作，并设立三峡工程建设移民管理机构。  
三峡工程淹没区和移民安置区所在地的市、县、区人民政府负责本行政区域内三峡工程建设移民工作，并可以根据需要设立三峡工程建设移民管理机构。

**Article 8** The resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall implement a management system of unified leadership, responsibility of provinces (municipalities directly under the Central Government), and county-level basis.  
The Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council is the leading decision-making body for the resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project.  
The resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council shall be responsible for the resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project.  
The people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality shall be responsible for the resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project within their respective administrative regions, and shall establish resettlement administrations for the construction of the Three Gorges Project.  
The people's governments of cities, counties, and districts where the Three Gorges Project inundation area and resettlement area are located shall be responsible for the resettlement work for the construction of the Three Gorges Project within their respective administrative regions, and may establish resettlement administrations for the construction of the Three Gorges Project as needed.

### 第二章　移民安置

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　三峡工程建设移民安置，应当编制移民安置规划。移民安置规划应当与土地利用总体规划相衔接。  
水利部长江水利委员会会同湖北省、重庆市人民政府，负责编制《长江三峡工程水库淹没处理及移民安置规划大纲》(以下简称《规划大纲》)，报国务院三峡工程建设委员会审批。  
湖北省、重庆市人民政府应当按照《规划大纲》，负责组织本行政区域内有关市、县、区人民政府编制并批准有关市、县、区的移民安置规划，并分别汇总编制本省、直辖市的移民安置规划，报国务院三峡工程建设委员会备案。  
国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构应当加强对移民安置规划实施情况的监督。

**Article 9** Resettlement plans shall be formulated for the resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project. The resettlement plans shall be consistent with the overall land use plans.  
The Changjiang Water Resources Commission of the Ministry of Water Resources, jointly with the people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality, shall be responsible for formulating the 'Outline of Reservoir Inundation Treatment and Resettlement Plan for the Three Gorges Project on the Yangtze River' (hereinafter referred to as the 'Plan Outline'), and submit it to the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council for approval.  
The people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality shall, in accordance with the Plan Outline, be responsible for organizing the relevant people's governments of cities, counties, and districts within their respective administrative regions to formulate and approve the resettlement plans for those cities, counties, and districts, and respectively compile the resettlement plans for their own province or municipality, and submit them to the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council for record.  
The resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council shall strengthen supervision over the implementation of the resettlement plans.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经批准的移民安置规划应当严格执行，不得随意调整或者修改；确需调整或者修改的，应当按照原审批程序报批。

**Article 10** The approved resettlement plans shall be strictly implemented, and shall not be adjusted or modified at will; if adjustment or modification is truly necessary, approval shall be applied for in accordance with the original examination and approval procedures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　三峡工程建设用地按照批准的规划，一次审批，分期划拨，并依法办理土地权属变更登记手续。  
三峡工程建设移民迁建用地应当严格控制规模，并依据土地利用总体规划和土地利用年度计划，分批次逐级上报省级以上人民政府依法办理农用地转用和土地征收手续。移民迁建用地不得转让，不得用于非移民项目。

**Article 11** The land used for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall be approved once according to the approved plan, allocated in phases, and the registration procedures for change of land ownership shall be handled according to law.  
The scale of land for relocation and reconstruction of the resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall be strictly controlled. In accordance with the overall land use plan and the annual land use plan, the procedures for conversion of agricultural land and land acquisition shall be handled in batches and reported level by level to the people's government at or above the provincial level according to law. The land for relocation and reconstruction of resettled persons shall not be transferred or used for non-resettlement projects.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因三峡工程建设和移民迁建，土地被全部征收并安置在第二产业、第三产业或者自谋职业的农村移民，经本人同意，由有关县、区人民政府批准，可以转为非农业户口。

**Article 12** Rural resettled persons whose land has been fully requisitioned due to the construction of the Three Gorges Project and the relocation and reconstruction of resettled persons, and who are resettled in secondary industry, tertiary industry, or self-employment, may, with their consent and upon approval by the relevant county or district people's government, change their household registration to non-agricultural status.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　移民安置地的有关地方人民政府应当合理调整土地，鼓励移民在安置地发展优质、高效、高产农业和生态农业；有条件的地方，可以通过发展第二产业、第三产业安置移民。

**Article 13** The relevant local people's governments in the resettlement area shall rationally adjust land, and encourage resettled persons to develop high-quality, high-efficiency, high-yield agriculture and ecological agriculture in the resettlement area; where conditions permit, resettled persons may be resettled through the development of secondary and tertiary industries.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　三峡工程建设移民安置实行就地安置与异地安置、集中安置与分散安置、政府安置与移民自找门路安置相结合。移民首先在本县、区安置；本县、区安置不了的，由湖北省、重庆市人民政府在本行政区域内其他市、县、区安置；湖北省、重庆市安置不了的，在其他省、自治区、直辖市安置。

**Article 14** The resettlement for the construction of the Three Gorges Project shall combine on-site resettlement with off-site resettlement, centralized resettlement with decentralized resettlement, and government resettlement with resettlement through the resettled persons' own arrangements. Resettled persons shall first be resettled in their own county or district; if resettlement cannot be achieved in their own county or district, they shall be resettled by the people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality in other cities, counties, or districts within their respective administrative regions; if resettlement cannot be achieved in Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality, they shall be resettled in other provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农村移民需要安置到本县、区其他农村集体经济组织的，由该农村集体经济组织与县、区人民政府移民管理机构或者负责移民管理工作的部门签订协议，并按照协议安排移民的生产、生活。

**Article 15** If rural resettled persons need to be resettled in other rural collective economic organizations in the same county or district, the rural collective economic organization shall enter into an agreement with the resettlement administration of the county or district people's government or the department responsible for resettlement management, and shall arrange the production and living of the resettled persons in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　移民在本县、区安置不了，需要在湖北省、重庆市行政区域内其他市、县、区安置的，由迁出地和安置地的市、县、区人民政府签订协议，办理有关手续。  
移民需要在湖北省、重庆市以外的地区安置的，分别由湖北省、重庆市人民政府与安置地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府签订协议，办理有关手续。

**Article 16** If resettled persons cannot be resettled in their own county or district and need to be resettled in other cities, counties, or districts within the administrative regions of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality, the people's governments of the cities, counties, or districts of the place of relocation and the place of resettlement shall enter into an agreement and handle the relevant procedures.  
If resettled persons need to be resettled in areas outside Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality, the people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality shall respectively enter into agreements with the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government of the resettlement area and handle the relevant procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　三峡工程受益地区和有条件的省、自治区、直辖市及其市、县、区应当接收政府组织外迁和投亲靠友自主外迁的三峡库区农村移民，并及时办理有关手续，统一安排移民的生产、生活。  
投亲靠友自主外迁的三峡库区农村移民，应当持有迁出地的县、区人民政府出具的证明。

**Article 17** The regions benefiting from the Three Gorges Project and the qualified provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and their cities, counties, and districts, shall accept rural resettled persons from the Three Gorges Reservoir Area who are relocated by government organization or who relocate themselves by joining relatives or friends, and shall handle the relevant procedures in a timely manner, and uniformly arrange the production and living of the resettled persons.  
The rural resettled persons from the Three Gorges Reservoir Area who relocate themselves by joining relatives or friends shall hold a certificate issued by the county or district people's government of the place of relocation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农村居民点迁建应当按照移民安置规划，依法编制新居民点建设规划。编制新居民点建设规划，应当因地制宜，有利生产，方便生活。  
新建居民点的道路、供水、供电等基础设施，由乡(镇)、村统一组织施工。  
房屋拆迁补偿资金按照农村房屋补偿标准包干到户，由移民用于住房建设。  
移民建造住房，可以分户建造，也可以按照自愿原则统一建造。有关地方人民政府以及村民委员会不得强行规定建房标准。

**Article 18** The relocation and reconstruction of rural residential areas shall prepare construction plans for new residential areas according to law in accordance with the resettlement plans. The preparation of construction plans for new residential areas shall adapt to local conditions, be conducive to production, and facilitate living.  
Infrastructure such as roads, water supply, and power supply for new residential areas shall be constructed uniformly by the township (town) or village.  
Compensation funds for house demolition shall be contracted to each household according to the rural house compensation standards, and shall be used by the resettled persons for housing construction.  
Resettled persons may build houses separately or may build them uniformly on a voluntary basis. The relevant local people's governments and villagers' committees shall not impose building standards by force.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　城镇迁建，应当按照移民安置规划，依法编制迁建区详细规划，并确定需要迁建的公共建筑和各项基础设施的具体位置。  
城镇公共建筑和各项基础设施迁建补偿资金实行包干管理，其数额按照实际淹没损失和适当发展的原则核定。  
城镇迁建中单位和居民搬迁的补偿资金实行包干管理，其数额按照实际淹没损失核定。

**Article 19** The relocation and reconstruction of towns shall prepare detailed plans for the relocation area according to law in accordance with the resettlement plans, and determine the specific locations of public buildings and various infrastructure facilities that need to be relocated and reconstructed.  
The compensation funds for the relocation and reconstruction of public buildings and various infrastructure facilities in towns shall be managed on a lump-sum basis, and the amount shall be determined according to the principle of actual inundation losses and appropriate development.  
The compensation funds for the relocation of units and residents in the relocation and reconstruction of towns shall be managed on a lump-sum basis, and the amount shall be determined according to the actual inundation losses.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　需要迁建的城镇应当提前建设基础设施。  
对自筹资金或者使用非移民资金提前搬迁的单位和居民，有关地方人民政府不得减少其应得的移民资金数额。

**Article 20** The towns that need to be relocated and reconstructed shall construct infrastructure in advance.  
For units and residents that relocate ahead of schedule by self-raised funds or using non-resettlement funds, the relevant local people's governments shall not reduce the amount of resettlement funds to which they are entitled.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有关地方人民政府应当根据国家产业政策，结合技术改造，对需要搬迁的工矿企业进行统筹规划和结构调整。产品质量好、有市场的企业，可以通过对口支援，与名优企业合作、合资，把企业的搬迁与企业的重组结合起来；技术落后、浪费资源、产品质量低劣、污染严重的企业，应当依法实行兼并、破产或者关闭。  
有关地方人民政府应当妥善安排破产、关闭企业职工和离退休人员的基本生活，做好再就业和社会养老保险工作。  
工矿企业搬迁补偿资金实行包干管理，其数额按照实际淹没损失的重置价格核定。

**Article 21** The relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the national industrial policy and in combination with technological transformation, make overall plans and structural adjustments for the industrial and mining enterprises that need to be relocated. Enterprises with good product quality and market may, through paired assistance, cooperate or form joint ventures with well-known and high-quality enterprises, combining the relocation of enterprises with their restructuring; enterprises with backward technology, waste of resources, poor product quality, and serious pollution shall be merged, bankrupted, or closed down according to law.  
The relevant local people's governments shall properly arrange the basic living of employees and retirees of bankrupt or closed enterprises, and do a good job of reemployment and social old-age insurance.  
The compensation funds for the relocation of industrial and mining enterprises shall be managed on a lump-sum basis, and the amount shall be determined according to the replacement cost of actual inundation losses.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　因三峡工程蓄水被淹没的公路、桥梁、港口、码头、水利工程、电力设施、电信线路、广播电视等基础设施和文物古迹需要复建的，应当根据复建规划，按照经济合理的原则，预先在淹没线以上复建。复建补偿资金实行包干管理，其数额按照原规模、原标准或者为恢复原功能所需投资核定。

**Article 22** If infrastructure such as highways, bridges, ports, wharves, water conservancy projects, power facilities, telecommunication lines, radio and television, and cultural relics and historic sites that are inundated due to the impoundment of the Three Gorges Project need to be reconstructed, they shall be reconstructed above the inundation line in advance in accordance with the reconstruction plan and the principle of economic rationality. The compensation funds for reconstruction shall be managed on a lump-sum basis, and the amount shall be determined according to the original scale, original standards, or the investment required to restore the original functions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　城镇迁建单位、工矿企业和居民的搬迁以及基础设施的复建，因扩大规模和提高标准超过包干资金的部分，分别由有关地方人民政府、有关单位、居民自行解决。

**Article 23** The portions exceeding the lump-sum funds due to expanded scale and raised standards in the relocation of units, industrial and mining enterprises, and residents in towns, as well as the reconstruction of infrastructure, shall be resolved respectively by the relevant local people's governments, relevant units, and residents themselves.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　移民工程建设应当做好项目前期论证工作。城镇、农村居民点、工矿企业、基础设施的选址和迁建，应当做好水文地质、工程地质勘察、地质灾害防治勘查和地质灾害危险性评估。

**Article 24** The construction of resettlement projects shall carry out preliminary project demonstration work. For the site selection and relocation of towns, rural residential areas, industrial and mining enterprises, and infrastructure, hydrogeological and engineering geological surveys, geological disaster prevention surveys, and geological disaster risk assessments shall be conducted.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　移民工程建设应当履行基本建设程序，严格执行国务院2000年1月发布的《建设工程质量管理条例》规定的各项制度，确保建设工程质量。  
移民工程建设施工，应当保护生态环境，防止植被破坏和水土流失。

**Article 25** The construction of resettlement projects shall go through the capital construction procedures, strictly implement the various systems stipulated in the 'Regulations on the Administration of Construction Project Quality' promulgated by the State Council in January 2000, and ensure the quality of construction projects.  
The construction of resettlement projects shall protect the ecological environment, prevent vegetation damage and water and soil erosion.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　安置移民生产，严禁开垦25度以上的坡地；已经开垦的，应当按照规划退耕还林还草。对已经开垦的25度以下的坡地，应当因地制宜，采取“坡改梯”措施，实行山水林田路综合规划治理。

**Article 26** In arranging production for resettled persons, it is strictly prohibited to reclaim slopes with a gradient of 25 degrees or more; land that has already been reclaimed shall be returned to forest or grassland according to the plan. For slopes with a gradient of less than 25 degrees that have been reclaimed, measures shall be taken to convert slopes into terraced fields according to local conditions, and comprehensive planning and management of mountains, waters, forests, farmlands, and roads shall be implemented.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　三峡工程淹没区的林木，在淹没前已经达到采伐利用标准的，经依法批准后，林木所有者可以采伐、销售；不能采伐利用的，淹没后按照《规划大纲》的规定给予补偿。

**Article 27** For the forests in the inundation area of the Three Gorges Project that have reached the standard for felling and utilization before inundation, the forest owners may, after approval according to law, fell and sell them; for those that cannot be felled and utilized, compensation shall be given after inundation in accordance with the provisions of the Plan Outline.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　三峡工程建设，应当按照“保护为主、抢救第一”和“重点保护、重点发掘”的原则，做好文物抢救、保护工作。

**Article 28** The construction of the Three Gorges Project shall, in accordance with the principles of 'protection first, rescue first' and 'key protection, key excavation', do a good job of rescuing and protecting cultural relics.

### 第三章　淹没区、安置区的管理

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有关地方人民政府应当加强对三峡工程淹没区基本建设的管理。任何单位和个人不得在淹没线以下擅自新建、扩建和改建项目。违反《国务院办公厅关于严格控制三峡工程坝区和库区淹没线以下区域人口增长和基本建设的通知》的规定，在1992年4月4日后建设的项目，按照违章建筑处理。

**Article 29** The relevant local people's governments shall strengthen the management of capital construction in the inundation area of the Three Gorges Project. No unit or individual may, without authorization, construct, expand, or rebuild projects below the inundation line. Projects constructed after April 4, 1992, in violation of the provisions of the 'Notice of the General Office of the State Council on Strictly Controlling Population Growth and Capital Construction in the Dam Area and the Area Below the Inundation Line of the Reservoir of the Three Gorges Project', shall be treated as illegal buildings.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　三峡库区有关公安机关应当加强对淹没区的户籍管理，严格控制非淹没区人口迁入淹没区。1992年4月4日后，按照《国务院办公厅关于严格控制三峡工程坝区和库区淹没线以下区域人口增长和基本建设的通知》的规定允许迁入的人口，经县级以上人民政府公安机关批准入户的，由国家负责搬迁安置；因其他原因擅自迁入的人口，国家不负责搬迁安置。  
三峡库区各级地方人民政府和有关单位应当加强计划生育管理，控制人口增长，保证库区的人口出生率不超过湖北省、重庆市的规定。  
本条第一款所称允许迁入的人口，是指因出生、婚嫁、工作调动、军人转业退伍和高等院校、中等专业技术学校毕业分配以及刑满释放等迁入的人口。

**Article 30** The relevant public security organs in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area shall strengthen the household registration management in the inundation area, and strictly control the migration of population from non-inundation areas into the inundation area. After April 4, 1992, for the population allowed to migrate in accordance with the provisions of the 'Notice of the General Office of the State Council on Strictly Controlling Population Growth and Capital Construction in the Dam Area and the Area Below the Inundation Line of the Reservoir of the Three Gorges Project', if their household registration is approved by the public security organ of the people's government at or above the county level, the state shall be responsible for their relocation and resettlement; for the population that migrates in without authorization for other reasons, the state shall not be responsible for their relocation and resettlement.  
The local people's governments at all levels and relevant units in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area shall strengthen the management of family planning, control population growth, and ensure that the birth rate in the reservoir area does not exceed the provisions of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality.  
The population allowed to migrate referred to in the first paragraph of this Article means the population migrating in due to birth, marriage, job transfer, military personnel's job transfer and demobilization, assignment of graduates from institutions of higher learning and secondary specialized technical schools, and release from prison.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　按照移民安置规划必须搬迁的单位和移民，不得拒绝搬迁或者拖延搬迁；已经搬迁并得到补偿和安置的，应当及时办理补偿销号手续，并不得返迁或者要求再次补偿。  
按照移民安置规划已经搬迁的单位和移民，其搬迁前使用的土地及其附着物由当地县级人民政府依法处理。

**Article 31** Units and resettled persons that must be relocated in accordance with the resettlement plans shall not refuse to relocate or delay relocation; those that have already relocated and received compensation and resettlement shall promptly go through the procedures for cancellation of compensation and shall not move back or request compensation again.  
For units and resettled persons that have already relocated in accordance with the resettlement plans, the land and its attachments used before relocation shall be dealt with by the local county people's government according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　三峡水库消落区的土地属于国家所有，由三峡水利枢纽管理单位负责管理，可以通过当地县级人民政府优先安排给当地农村移民使用；但是，不得影响水库安全、防洪、发电和生态环境保护。因蓄水给使用该土地的移民造成损失的，国家不予补偿。

**Article 32** The land in the drawdown area of the Three Gorges Reservoir belongs to the state, and shall be managed by the management unit of the Three Gorges Hydro Project. It may be given priority to local rural resettled persons for use through the local county people's government; however, it shall not affect the safety, flood control, power generation, and ecological environmental protection of the reservoir. If losses are caused to the resettled persons using the land due to impoundment, the state shall not compensate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有关地方人民政府应当对三峡工程移民档案加强管理，确保档案完整、准确和安全。

**Article 33** The relevant local people's governments shall strengthen the management of the resettlement archives of the Three Gorges Project to ensure the completeness, accuracy, and safety of the archives.

### 第四章　移民资金使用的管理和监督

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　移民资金实行静态控制，动态管理。除价格指数变动、国家政策调整和发生不可抗力外，不再增加移民资金。

**Article 34** The resettlement funds shall be subject to static control and dynamic management. Except for changes in price indices, adjustment of national policies, and occurrence of force majeure, the resettlement funds shall not be increased.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　移民资金年度计划应当纳入国家年度投资计划。  
国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构根据经批准的三峡工程移民安置规划，组织编制移民资金年度计划，报国务院审批。  
县级以上地方人民政府移民管理机构或者负责移民管理工作的部门组织编制本行政区域的移民资金年度项目计划，经本级人民政府审核同意后报上一级人民政府移民管理机构审批。  
经批准的移民资金年度项目计划，不得擅自调整；确需调整的，应当报原审批机关批准。

**Article 35** The annual plan for resettlement funds shall be included in the national annual investment plan.  
The resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council shall, based on the approved resettlement plans for the Three Gorges Project, organize the preparation of the annual plan for resettlement funds, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The resettlement administrations or the departments responsible for resettlement management of local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the preparation of the annual project plan for resettlement funds within their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the resettlement administration of the people's government at the next higher level for approval after being examined and approved by the people's government at the same level.  
The approved annual project plan for resettlement funds shall not be adjusted without authorization; if adjustment is truly necessary, it shall be submitted to the original approving authority for approval.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　移民资金安排应当突出重点，保证移民安置进度与枢纽工程建设进度相适应。  
移民资金由有关地方人民政府按照移民安置规划安排使用。  
有移民安置任务的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据国家移民资金投资包干方案，将移民资金拨付到县级人民政府和有关单位，由县级人民政府和有关单位将移民资金具体落实到各类移民投资项目。

**Article 36** The allocation of resettlement funds shall prioritize key points and ensure that the progress of resettlement is compatible with the progress of the construction of the key project.  
The resettlement funds shall be arranged and used by the relevant local people's governments in accordance with the resettlement plans.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have resettlement tasks shall, in accordance with the state's lump-sum investment plan for resettlement funds, allocate the resettlement funds to the county people's governments and relevant units, and the county people's governments and relevant units shall specifically allocate the resettlement funds to various resettlement investment projects.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　移民资金应当在国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府移民管理机构指定的银行专户存储、专账核算。国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的移民资金管理部门应当按照包干方案、移民资金年度项目计划和进度及时拨付移民资金。

**Article 37** The resettlement funds shall be deposited in a special account and accounted for in a special ledger at a bank designated by the resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council or the resettlement administration of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The department for resettlement funds management designated by the State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall allocate the resettlement funds in a timely manner in accordance with the lump-sum plan, the annual project plan for resettlement funds, and the progress.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　移民资金应当用于下列项目：  
(一)农村移民安置补偿；  
(二)城镇迁建补偿；  
(三)工矿企业迁建补偿；  
(四)基础设施项目复建；  
(五)环境保护；  
(六)国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构规定的与移民有关的其他项目。  
任何部门、单位和个人不得挤占、截留和挪用移民资金。

**Article 38** Resettlement funds shall be used for the following projects:  
(1) Compensation for resettlement of rural resettled persons;  
(2) Compensation for relocation and reconstruction of towns;  
(3) Compensation for relocation and reconstruction of industrial and mining enterprises;  
(4) Reconstruction of infrastructure projects;  
(5) Environmental protection;  
(6) Other projects related to resettlement as stipulated by the resettlement administration of the Three Gorges Project Construction Committee of the State Council.  
No department, unit, or individual may divert, intercept, or misappropriate resettlement funds.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　移民资金存储期间的孳息，应当纳入移民资金，不得挪作他用。

**Article 39** The interest accrued during the deposit of resettlement funds shall be included in the resettlement funds and shall not be used for other purposes.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　有关地方人民政府设立的城镇迁建工程建设指挥部(管委会)不是一级财务核算单位，移民项目资金不得经其转拨。

**Article 40** The headquarters (management committees) for the construction of town relocation and reconstruction projects established by the relevant local people's governments are not first-level financial accounting units, and resettlement project funds shall not be transferred through them.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家对移民资金的管理、拨付和安排使用实行稽察制度，对管理、拨付和安排使用移民资金的有关地方人民政府及其有关部门、机构的负责人实行任期经济责任审计制度。

**Article 41** The state shall implement an inspection system for the management, allocation, and use of resettlement funds, and shall implement a term economic responsibility audit system for the persons in charge of the relevant local people's governments and their relevant departments and institutions that manage, allocate, and use resettlement funds.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对下级人民政府及其有关部门管理、拨付和安排使用移民资金情况的监督。  
各级人民政府移民管理机构或者负责移民管理工作的部门应当加强内部审计和监察，定期向本级人民政府、上级主管部门报告移民资金年度项目计划执行情况、移民资金拨付和使用情况。

**Article 42** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the management, allocation, and use of resettlement funds by the people's governments at lower levels and their relevant departments.  
The resettlement administrations or the departments responsible for resettlement management of the people's governments at all levels shall strengthen internal audit and supervision, and regularly report to the people's government at the same level and the competent department at the next higher level on the implementation of the annual project plan for resettlement funds and the allocation and use of resettlement funds.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有移民任务的乡(镇)、村应当建立健全财务管理制度，乡(镇)、村移民资金的使用情况应当张榜公布，接受群众监督。

**Article 43** Townships (towns) and villages with resettlement tasks shall establish and improve financial management systems, and the use of resettlement funds in townships (towns) and villages shall be publicly posted to accept supervision by the masses.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　各级审计机关和监察、财政部门应当加强对移民资金管理、拨付和安排使用的审计和监察、监督，依法履行国家有关法律、法规赋予的职责。  
审计机关和监察、财政部门进行审计和监察、监督时，有关单位和个人应当予以配合，及时提供有关资料。

**Article 44** The audit organs, supervision departments, and financial departments at all levels shall strengthen the audit, supervision, and oversight of the management, allocation, and use of resettlement funds, and perform their duties conferred by relevant national laws and regulations according to law.  
When the audit organs, supervision departments, and financial departments conduct audits, supervision, and oversight, the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and provide relevant materials in a timely manner.

### 第五章　扶持措施

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家从三峡电站的电价收入中提取一定资金设立三峡库区移民后期扶持基金，分配给湖北省、重庆市和接收外迁移民的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，用于移民的后期扶持。具体办法由财政部会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 45** The state shall allocate a certain amount of funds from the electricity price income of the Three Gorges Power Station to establish a post-relocation support fund for resettled persons in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area, which shall be distributed to the people's governments of Hubei Province, Chongqing Municipality, and the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government that receive out-migrated resettled persons, for the post-relocation support of resettled persons. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　三峡电站投产后缴纳的税款依法留给地方的部分，分配给湖北省、重庆市人民政府，用于支持三峡库区建设和生态环境保护。具体办法由财政部会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 46** The portion of taxes paid after the Three Gorges Power Station is put into operation that is retained by the local government according to law shall be distributed to the people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality for supporting the construction of the Three Gorges Reservoir Area and ecological environmental protection. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　农村移民建房占用耕地，免征耕地占用税。三峡工程坝区和淹没区建设占用耕地，按照应纳税额的40%征收耕地占用税；城镇、企业事业单位搬迁和基础设施复建占用耕地，按照国家有关规定缴纳耕地占用税。缴纳的耕地占用税全部用于三峡库区农村移民安置。

**Article 47** The occupation of arable land for the construction of houses by rural resettled persons shall be exempt from arable land occupation tax. The occupation of arable land for construction in the dam area and inundation area of the Three Gorges Project shall be subject to arable land occupation tax at 40% of the tax payable; the occupation of arable land for the relocation of towns, enterprises, and institutions and the reconstruction of infrastructure shall pay arable land occupation tax in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state. All the arable land occupation tax paid shall be used for the resettlement of rural resettled persons in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　三峡电站投产后，应当优先安排三峡库区用电。

**Article 48** After the Three Gorges Power Station is put into operation, priority shall be given to arranging electricity use for the Three Gorges Reservoir Area.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家将三峡库区有水电资源条件的受淹县、区列为农村水电初级电气化县，予以扶持。

**Article 49** The state shall designate the inundated counties and districts with hydropower resource conditions in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area as primary rural hydropower electrification counties and provide support.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家将三峡库区具备一定条件的受淹县、区优先列入生态农业试点示范县，予以扶持，并优先安排基本农田及水利专项资金，用于移民安置区农田水利建设。

**Article 50** The state shall give priority to designating the inundated counties and districts with certain conditions in the Three Gorges Reservoir Area as pilot and demonstration counties for ecological agriculture, provide support, and give priority to arranging special funds for basic farmland and water conservancy for the construction of farmland water conservancy in the resettlement area.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院有关部门和湖北省、重庆市人民政府及其有关部门在安排建设项目、分配资金时，对三峡库区有关县、区应当优先照顾。

**Article 51** The relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of Hubei Province and Chongqing Municipality and their relevant departments shall give priority to the relevant counties and districts of the Three Gorges Reservoir area when arranging construction projects and allocating funds.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国务院有关部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市应当按照优势互补、互惠互利、长期合作、共同发展的原则，采取多种形式鼓励名优企业到三峡库区投资建厂，并从教育、文化、科技、人才、管理、信息、资金、物资等方面对口支援三峡库区移民。

**Article 52** The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the principles of complementary advantages, mutual benefit, long-term cooperation, and common development, adopt various forms to encourage famous and excellent enterprises to invest and establish factories in the Three Gorges Reservoir area, and provide counterpart support to the resettlement in the Three Gorges Reservoir area in terms of education, culture, science and technology, talents, management, information, funds, and materials.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家在三峡库区和三峡工程受益地区安排的建设项目，应当优先吸收符合条件的移民就业。

**Article 53** Construction projects arranged by the State in the Three Gorges Reservoir area and areas benefiting from the Three Gorges Project shall give priority to employing qualified resettled persons.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家对专门为安置农村移民开发的土地和新办的企业，依法减免农业税、农业特产农业税、企业所得税。

**Article 54** The State shall, according to law, exempt the agricultural tax, agricultural specialty tax on agricultural products, and enterprise income tax on land specifically developed for the resettlement of rural migrants and newly established enterprises.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，未经批准，擅自调整、修改移民安置规划和移民资金年度项目计划的，由规划、计划的审批机关责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 55** Where anyone violates these Regulations and, without approval, adjusts or modifies the resettlement plan or the annual project plan of resettlement funds without authorization, the approval authority of the plan or project plan shall order correction within a specified time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，擅自将移民迁建用地的使用权转让或者用于非移民项目的，由县级以上人民政府土地行政主管部门会同同级移民管理机构依据职责，责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得l倍以上3倍以下的罚款。没收的违法所得和收缴的罚款，全部纳入移民资金，用于移民迁建。

**Article 56** Where anyone violates these Regulations and transfers the right to use land for resettlement and relocation or uses it for non-resettlement projects without authorization, the land administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the resettlement administrative agency at the same level, in accordance with their functions and duties, order correction within a specified time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains. The confiscated illegal gains and the collected fines shall all be incorporated into the resettlement funds and used for resettlement and relocation.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，在淹没线以下擅自新建、扩建和改建项目的，由县级以上人民政府移民管理机构依据职责，责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状，可以处5万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 57** Where anyone violates these Regulations and constructs, expands, or renovates projects below the inundation line without authorization, the resettlement administrative agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order cessation of the illegal act, restore the original state within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，在移民搬迁和安置过程中，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府移民管理机构会同同级有关部门依据职责，责令限期改正，给予警告；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以处罚：  
(一)拒绝搬迁或者拖延搬迁的；  
(二)按照规定标准已获得安置补偿，搬迁后又擅自返迁的；  
(三)按照规定标准获得安置补偿后，无理要求再次补偿的。

**Article 58** Where anyone violates these Regulations and commits one of the following acts during the process of resettlement and relocation, the resettlement administrative agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, in accordance with their functions and duties, order correction within a specified time limit and give a warning; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law:  
(1) Refusing to relocate or delaying relocation;  
(2) Having obtained resettlement compensation in accordance with the prescribed standards, but moving back without authorization after relocation;  
(3) Having obtained resettlement compensation in accordance with the prescribed standards, but unreasonably demanding compensation again.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关审计机关、财政部门依照审计、财政法律、法规的规定予以处罚；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)将移民资金用于非移民项目、偿还非移民债务和平衡地方财政预算的；  
(二)利用移民资金进行融资、投资和提供担保的；  
(三)购买股票、债券和其他有价证券的；  
(四)利用其他方式挪用移民资金的。

**Article 59** Where anyone violates these Regulations and commits one of the following acts, the relevant auditing authority or financial department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of auditing and financial laws and regulations; administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Using resettlement funds for non-resettlement projects, repaying non-resettlement debts, or balancing local fiscal budgets;  
(2) Using resettlement funds for financing, investment, or providing guarantees;  
(3) Purchasing stocks, bonds, and other marketable securities;  
(4) Misappropriating resettlement funds by other means.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，在国务院三峡工程建设委员会移民管理机构或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府移民管理机构指定的银行之外的金融机构存储移民资金的，由县级以上人民政府移民管理机构按照职责分工，责令限期改正，给予警告；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where anyone violates these Regulations and deposits resettlement funds in a financial institution other than the bank designated by the resettlement administrative agency of the State Council Three Gorges Project Construction Committee or the resettlement administrative agency of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the resettlement administrative agency of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to the division of functions and duties, order correction within a specified time limit and give a warning; administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains shall be imposed.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，挤占、截留移民资金的，由有关审计机关、财政部门依法予以追缴，可以处挤占、截留移民资金数额1倍以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 61** Where anyone violates these Regulations and squeezes or intercepts resettlement funds, the relevant auditing authority or financial department shall recover the funds according to law and may impose a fine of not more than one time the amount of resettlement funds squeezed or intercepted; administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　在移民工程建设中，破坏植被和生态环境，造成水土流失的，依照环境保护法和水土保持法的有关规定处罚。

**Article 62** Where, in the construction of resettlement projects, vegetation and the ecological environment are damaged, causing soil erosion, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Environmental Protection Law and the Water and Soil Conservation Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家机关工作人员在移民工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 63** Where state functionaries, in the course of resettlement work, neglect their duties, abuse their power, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2001年3月1日起施行。1993年8月19日国务院公布施行的《长江三峡工程建设移民条例》同时废止。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2001. The Regulations on the Resettlement of the Three Gorges Project Construction promulgated and came into force by the State Council on August 19, 1993 shall be abolished simultaneously.
