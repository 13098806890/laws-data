# 黄河水量调度条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2006-07-24  
**生效日期**：2006-08-01

> (2006年7月5日国务院第142次常务会议通过　2006年7月24日中华人民共和国国务院令第472号公布　自2006年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强黄河水量的统一调度，实现黄河水资源的可持续利用，促进黄河流域及相关地区经济社会发展和生态环境的改善，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。 第二条　黄河流域的青海省、四川省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、陕西省、山西省、河南省、山东省，以及国务院批准取用黄河水的河北省、天津市(以下称十一省区市)的黄河水量调度和管理，适用本条例。 第三条　国家对黄河水量实行统一调度，遵循总量控制、断面流量控制、分级管理、分级负责的原则。 实施黄河水量调度，应当首先满足城乡居民生活用水的需要，合理安排农业、工业、生态环境用水，防止黄河断流。 第四条　黄河水量调度计划、调度方案和调度指令的执行，实行地方人民政府行政首长负责制和黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构以及水库主管部门或者单位主要领导负责制。 第五条　国务院水行政主管部门和国务院发展改革主管部门负责组织、协调、监督、指导黄河水量调度工作。 黄河水利委员会依照本条例的规定负责黄河水量调度的组织实施和监督检查工作。 有关县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和黄河水利委员会所属管理机构，依照本条例的规定负责所辖范围内黄河水量调度的实施和监督检查工作。 第六条　在黄河水量调度工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由有关县级以上人民政府或者有关部门给予奖励。 第二章　水量分配 第七条　黄河水量分配方案，由黄河水利委员会商十一省区市人民政府制订，经国务院发展改革主管部门和国务院水行政主管部门审查，报国务院批准。 国务院批准的黄河水量分配方案，是黄河水量调度的依据，有关地方人民政府和黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构必须执行。 第八条　制订黄河水量分配方案，应当遵循下列原则：

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强黄河水量的统一调度，实现黄河水资源的可持续利用，促进黄河流域及相关地区经济社会发展和生态环境的改善，根据《中华人民共和国水法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Water Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of strengthening the unified regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River, realizing the sustainable utilization of the water resources of the Yellow River, and promoting the economic and social development and the improvement of the ecological environment in the Yellow River Basin and relevant areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　黄河流域的青海省、四川省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、陕西省、山西省、河南省、山东省，以及国务院批准取用黄河水的河北省、天津市(以下称十一省区市)的黄河水量调度和管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the regulation and management of the water volume of the Yellow River in Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Gansu Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Shaanxi Province, Shanxi Province, Henan Province, Shandong Province, and Hebei Province and Tianjin Municipality that are approved by the State Council to divert water from the Yellow River (hereinafter referred to as the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对黄河水量实行统一调度，遵循总量控制、断面流量控制、分级管理、分级负责的原则。  
实施黄河水量调度，应当首先满足城乡居民生活用水的需要，合理安排农业、工业、生态环境用水，防止黄河断流。

**Article 3** The State shall exercise unified regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River, adhering to the principles of total amount control, cross-section flow control, graded management, and graded responsibility.  
In implementing the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River, priority shall be given to meeting the needs of urban and rural residents for domestic water use, and water use for agriculture, industry, and ecological environment shall be arranged rationally to prevent the drying up of the Yellow River.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　黄河水量调度计划、调度方案和调度指令的执行，实行地方人民政府行政首长负责制和黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构以及水库主管部门或者单位主要领导负责制。

**Article 4** The enforcement of the regulation plan, regulation scheme and regulation directives for the water volume of the Yellow River shall be subject to the responsibility system of the chief executive of the local people's government and the responsibility system of the main leaders of the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs, as well as the competent department or unit of the reservoir.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院水行政主管部门和国务院发展改革主管部门负责组织、协调、监督、指导黄河水量调度工作。  
黄河水利委员会依照本条例的规定负责黄河水量调度的组织实施和监督检查工作。  
有关县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和黄河水利委员会所属管理机构，依照本条例的规定负责所辖范围内黄河水量调度的实施和监督检查工作。

**Article 5** The water administrative department of the State Council and the competent department for development and reform of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, supervising and guiding the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River.  
The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the organization, implementation, supervision and inspection of the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River.  
The water administrative departments of the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level and the administrative organs subordinate to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the implementation, supervision and inspection of the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在黄河水量调度工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由有关县级以上人民政府或者有关部门给予奖励。

**Article 6** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of regulating the water volume of the Yellow River shall be rewarded by the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level or the relevant departments.

### 第二章　水量分配

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　黄河水量分配方案，由黄河水利委员会商十一省区市人民政府制订，经国务院发展改革主管部门和国务院水行政主管部门审查，报国务院批准。  
国务院批准的黄河水量分配方案，是黄河水量调度的依据，有关地方人民政府和黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构必须执行。

**Article 7** The scheme for allocation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall be formulated by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission after consulting the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval after being examined by the competent department for development and reform of the State Council and the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The scheme for allocation of the water volume of the Yellow River approved by the State Council shall be the basis for the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River, and the relevant local people's governments and the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs must implement it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　制订黄河水量分配方案，应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)依据流域规划和水中长期供求规划；  
(二)坚持计划用水、节约用水；  
(三)充分考虑黄河流域水资源条件，取用水现状、供需情况及发展趋势，发挥黄河水资源的综合效益；  
(四)统筹兼顾生活、生产、生态环境用水；  
(五)正确处理上下游、左右岸的关系；  
(六)科学确定河道输沙入海水量和可供水量。  
前款所称可供水量，是指在黄河流域干、支流多年平均天然年径流量中，除必需的河道输沙入海水量外，可供城乡居民生活、农业、工业及河道外生态环境用水的最大水量。

**Article 8** The formulation of the scheme for allocation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall follow the following principles:  
(1) be based on the basin plan and the medium and long-term water supply and demand plan;  
(2) adhere to planned water use and water conservation;  
(3) give full consideration to the water resource conditions of the Yellow River Basin, the current situation of water diversion and use, supply and demand and development trends, and bring into play the comprehensive benefits of the water resources of the Yellow River;  
(4) make overall plans and take all factors into consideration for domestic, production, and ecological environment water use;  
(5) properly handle the relationships between the upper and lower reaches and between the left and right banks;  
(6) scientifically determine the water volume for sediment transport to the sea in the river channel and the available water volume.  
The 'available water volume' referred to in the preceding paragraph means, among the multi-year average natural annual runoff of the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River Basin, the maximum water volume available for domestic use by urban and rural residents, agriculture, industry, and ecological environment use outside the river channel, except for the necessary water volume for sediment transport to the sea in the river channel.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　黄河水量分配方案需要调整的，应当由黄河水利委员会商十一省区市人民政府提出方案，经国务院发展改革主管部门和国务院水行政主管部门审查，报国务院批准。

**Article 9** Where the scheme for allocation of the water volume of the Yellow River needs to be adjusted, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, after consulting the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, propose a scheme, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval after being examined by the competent department for development and reform of the State Council and the water administrative department of the State Council.

### 第三章　水量调度

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　黄河水量调度实行年度水量调度计划与月、旬水量调度方案和实时调度指令相结合的调度方式。  
黄河水量调度年度为当年7月1日至次年6月30日。

**Article 10** The regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall adopt a regulation method combining the annual regulation plan with monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and real-time regulation directives.  
The regulation year for the water volume of the Yellow River shall be from July 1 of the current year to June 30 of the following year.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　黄河干、支流的年度和月用水计划建议与水库运行计划建议，由十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门和河南、山东黄河河务局以及水库管理单位，按照调度管理权限和规定的时间向黄河水利委员会申报。河南、山东黄河河务局申报黄河干流的用水计划建议时，应当商河南省、山东省人民政府水行政主管部门。

**Article 11** The proposals for annual and monthly water use plans and for reservoir operation plans of the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River shall be submitted to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus, and the reservoir management units, in accordance with the regulation management authority and the prescribed time. When the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus submit the proposals for water use plans for the main stream of the Yellow River, they shall consult the water administrative departments of the people's governments of Henan Province and Shandong Province.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　年度水量调度计划由黄河水利委员会商十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门和河南、山东黄河河务局以及水库管理单位制订，报国务院水行政主管部门批准并下达，同时抄送国务院发展改革主管部门。  
经批准的年度水量调度计划，是确定月、旬水量调度方案和年度黄河干、支流用水量控制指标的依据。年度水量调度计划应当纳入本级国民经济和社会发展年度计划。

**Article 12** The annual regulation plan shall be formulated by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission after consulting the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus, and the reservoir management units, and shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval and issuance, with a copy to the competent department for development and reform of the State Council.  
The approved annual regulation plan shall be the basis for determining the monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and the annual control indicators for water use in the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River. The annual regulation plan shall be incorporated into the annual plan for national economic and social development at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　年度水量调度计划，应当依据经批准的黄河水量分配方案和年度预测来水量、水库蓄水量，按照同比例丰增枯减、多年调节水库蓄丰补枯的原则，在综合平衡申报的年度用水计划建议和水库运行计划建议的基础上制订。

**Article 13** The annual regulation plan shall be formulated on the basis of comprehensively balancing the submitted proposals for annual water use plans and reservoir operation plans, in accordance with the approved scheme for allocation of the water volume of the Yellow River, the forecasted annual incoming water volume, and the reservoir storage volume, and following the principles of increasing water volume in wet years and decreasing in dry years in the same proportion, and storing water in wet years and replenishing in dry years for multi-year regulation reservoirs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　黄河水利委员会应当根据经批准的年度水量调度计划和申报的月用水计划建议、水库运行计划建议，制订并下达月水量调度方案；用水高峰时，应当根据需要制订并下达旬水量调度方案。

**Article 14** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, in accordance with the approved annual regulation plan and the submitted proposals for monthly water use plans and reservoir operation plans, formulate and issue monthly regulation schemes; during peak water use periods, it shall, as needed, formulate and issue ten-day regulation schemes.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　黄河水利委员会根据实时水情、雨情、旱情、墒情、水库蓄水量及用水情况，可以对已下达的月、旬水量调度方案作出调整，下达实时调度指令。

**Article 15** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission may, based on real-time water conditions, rainfall conditions, drought conditions, soil moisture conditions, reservoir storage volume and water use situation, make adjustments to the issued monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and issue real-time regulation directives.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　青海省、四川省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、陕西省、山西省境内黄河干、支流的水量，分别由各省级人民政府水行政主管部门负责调度；河南省、山东省境内黄河干流的水量，分别由河南、山东黄河河务局负责调度，支流的水量，分别由河南省、山东省人民政府水行政主管部门负责调度；调入河北省、天津市的黄河水量，分别由河北省、天津市人民政府水行政主管部门负责调度。  
市、县级人民政府水行政主管部门和黄河水利委员会所属管理机构，负责所辖范围内分配水量的调度。  
实施黄河水量调度，必须遵守经批准的年度水量调度计划和下达的月、旬水量调度方案以及实时调度指令。

**Article 16** The water volume of the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River within the territories of Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Gansu Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Shaanxi Province, and Shanxi Province shall be regulated respectively by the water administrative departments of the respective provincial people's governments; the water volume of the main stream of the Yellow River within the territories of Henan Province and Shandong Province shall be regulated respectively by the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus, and the water volume of the tributaries shall be regulated respectively by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of Henan Province and Shandong Province; the water volume of the Yellow River diverted to Hebei Province and Tianjin Municipality shall be regulated respectively by the water administrative departments of the people's governments of Hebei Province and Tianjin Municipality.  
The water administrative departments of the people's governments at the city and county levels and the administrative organs subordinate to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall be responsible for regulating the allocated water volume within their respective jurisdictions.  
The implementation of the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River must comply with the approved annual regulation plan and the issued monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and the real-time regulation directives.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　龙羊峡、刘家峡、万家寨、三门峡、小浪底、西霞院、故县、东平湖等水库，由黄河水利委员会组织实施水量调度，下达月、旬水量调度方案及实时调度指令；必要时，黄河水利委员会可以对大峡、沙坡头、青铜峡、三盛公、陆浑等水库组织实施水量调度，下达实时调度指令。  
水库主管部门或者单位具体负责实施所辖水库的水量调度，并按照水量调度指令做好发电计划的安排。

**Article 17** For the reservoirs such as Longyangxia, Liujiaxia, Wanjiazhai, Sanmenxia, Xiaolangdi, Xixiayuan, Guxian, and Dongping Lake, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall organize the implementation of the regulation of water volume, and issue monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and real-time regulation directives; when necessary, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission may organize the implementation of the regulation of water volume for the reservoirs such as Daxia, Shapotou, Qingtongxia, Sanshenggong, and Luhun, and issue real-time regulation directives.  
The competent department or unit of the reservoir shall be specifically responsible for implementing the regulation of the water volume of the reservoirs under its jurisdiction, and shall make arrangements for power generation plans in accordance with the regulation directives.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　黄河水量调度实行水文断面流量控制。黄河干流水文断面的流量控制指标，由黄河水利委员会规定；重要支流水文断面及其流量控制指标，由黄河水利委员会会同黄河流域有关省、自治区人民政府水行政主管部门规定。  
青海省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、河南省、山东省人民政府，分别负责并确保循化、下河沿、石嘴山、头道拐、高村、利津水文断面的下泄流量符合规定的控制指标；陕西省和山西省人民政府共同负责并确保潼关水文断面的下泄流量符合规定的控制指标。  
龙羊峡、刘家峡、万家寨、三门峡、小浪底水库的主管部门或者单位，分别负责并确保贵德、小川、万家寨、三门峡、小浪底水文断面的出库流量符合规定的控制指标。

**Article 18** The regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall implement flow control at hydrological cross-sections. The flow control indicators at the hydrological cross-sections of the main stream of the Yellow River shall be prescribed by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission; the hydrological cross-sections of important tributaries and their flow control indicators shall be prescribed by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission jointly with the water administrative departments of the relevant provinces and autonomous regions in the Yellow River Basin.  
The people's governments of Qinghai Province, Gansu Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Henan Province, and Shandong Province shall respectively be responsible for and ensure that the discharge flows at the Xunhua, Xiaheyan, Shizuishan, Toudaoguai, Gaocun, and Lijin hydrological cross-sections comply with the prescribed control indicators; the people's governments of Shaanxi Province and Shanxi Province shall jointly be responsible for and ensure that the discharge flow at the Tongguan hydrological cross-section complies with the prescribed control indicators.  
The competent departments or units of the Longyangxia, Liujiaxia, Wanjiazhai, Sanmenxia, and Xiaolangdi reservoirs shall respectively be responsible for and ensure that the outflow flows at the Guide, Xiaochuan, Wanjiazhai, Sanmenxia, and Xiaolangdi hydrological cross-sections comply with the prescribed control indicators.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　黄河干、支流省际或者重要控制断面和出库流量控制断面的下泄流量以国家设立的水文站监测数据为依据。对水文监测数据有争议的，以黄河水利委员会确认的水文监测数据为准。

**Article 19** The discharge flows at inter-provincial or important control cross-sections and at outflow flow control cross-sections of the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River shall be based on the monitoring data from hydrological stations established by the State. In case of any dispute over the hydrological monitoring data, the hydrological monitoring data confirmed by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall prevail.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　需要在年度水量调度计划外使用其他省、自治区、直辖市计划内水量分配指标的，应当向黄河水利委员会提出申请，由黄河水利委员会组织有关各方在协商一致的基础上提出方案，报国务院水行政主管部门批准后组织实施。

**Article 20** Where it is necessary to use the planned water allocation indicators of other provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities outside the annual regulation plan, an application shall be submitted to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission, and the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall organize the relevant parties to propose a scheme on the basis of consensus reached through consultation, which shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for approval before being organized and implemented.

### 第四章　应急调度

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　出现严重干旱、省际或者重要控制断面流量降至预警流量、水库运行故障、重大水污染事故等情况，可能造成供水危机、黄河断流时，黄河水利委员会应当组织实施应急调度。

**Article 21** Where severe drought, a drop of flow at an inter-provincial or important control cross-section to the early warning flow, a malfunction of reservoir operation, a major water pollution accident, or any other situation occurs, which may cause a water supply crisis or the drying up of the Yellow River, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall organize and implement emergency regulation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　黄河水利委员会应当商十一省区市人民政府以及水库主管部门或者单位，制订旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案，经国务院水行政主管部门审查，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准。

**Article 22** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, after consulting the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities and the competent departments or units of reservoirs, formulate a preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions, which shall be submitted to the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council for approval after being examined by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门和河南、山东黄河河务局以及水库管理单位，应当根据经批准的旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案，制订实施方案，并抄送黄河水利委员会。

**Article 23** The water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus, and the reservoir management units shall, in accordance with the approved preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions, formulate implementation plans, and submit copies to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　出现旱情紧急情况时，经国务院水行政主管部门同意，由黄河水利委员会组织实施旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案，并及时调整取水及水库出库流量控制指标；必要时，可以对黄河流域有关省、自治区主要取水口实行直接调度。  
县级以上地方人民政府、水库管理单位应当按照旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案及其实施方案，合理安排用水计划，确保省际或者重要控制断面和出库流量控制断面的下泄流量符合规定的控制指标。

**Article 24** When a drought emergency occurs, with the consent of the water administrative department of the State Council, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall organize the implementation of the preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions, and shall timely adjust the control indicators for water diversion and reservoir outflow flow; when necessary, it may implement direct regulation of major water intakes in the relevant provinces and autonomous regions in the Yellow River Basin.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and reservoir management units shall, in accordance with the preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions and its implementation plan, rationally arrange water use plans, and ensure that the discharge flows at inter-provincial or important control cross-sections and outflow flow control cross-sections comply with the prescribed control indicators.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出现旱情紧急情况时，十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门和河南、山东黄河河务局以及水库管理单位，应当每日向黄河水利委员会报送取(退)水及水库蓄(泄)水情况。

**Article 25** When a drought emergency occurs, the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus, and the reservoir management units shall submit daily reports to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission on the conditions of water diversion (return) and reservoir storage (discharge).

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　出现省际或者重要控制断面流量降至预警流量、水库运行故障以及重大水污染事故等情况时，黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构、有关省级人民政府及其水行政主管部门和环境保护主管部门以及水库管理单位，应当根据需要，按照规定的权限和职责，及时采取压减取水量直至关闭取水口、实施水库应急泄流方案、加强水文监测、对排污企业实行限产或者停产等处置措施，有关部门和单位必须服从。  
省际或者重要控制断面的预警流量，由黄河水利委员会确定。

**Article 26** Where a drop of flow at an inter-provincial or important control cross-section to the early warning flow, a malfunction of reservoir operation, a major water pollution accident, or any other situation occurs, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs, the relevant provincial people's governments and their water administrative departments and environmental protection departments, and the reservoir management units shall, as needed, in accordance with the prescribed authority and duties, promptly take such disposal measures as reducing water diversion volume up to closing the water intake, implementing the reservoir emergency discharge plan, strengthening hydrological monitoring, and imposing production restriction or suspension on pollutant-discharging enterprises, and the relevant departments and units must comply.  
The early warning flows at inter-provincial or important control cross-sections shall be determined by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　实施应急调度，需要动用水库死库容的，由黄河水利委员会商有关水库主管部门或者单位，制订动用水库死库容的水量调度方案，经国务院水行政主管部门审查，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准实施。

**Article 27** Where the implementation of emergency regulation requires the use of the dead storage of a reservoir, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall, after consulting the relevant competent department or unit of the reservoir, formulate a regulation scheme for using the dead storage of the reservoir, which shall be submitted to the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council for approval and implementation after being examined by the water administrative department of the State Council.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构和县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当加强对所辖范围内水量调度执行情况的监督检查。

**Article 28** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs and the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the implementation of the regulation of water volume within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门和河南、山东黄河河务局，应当按照国务院水行政主管部门规定的时间，向黄河水利委员会报送所辖范围内取(退)水量报表。

**Article 29** The water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities, and the Henan and Shandong Yellow River Bureaus shall, within the time prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council, submit to the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission the statements of water diversion (return) volume within their respective jurisdictions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　黄河水量调度文书格式，由黄河水利委员会编制、公布，并报国务院水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 30** The format of the documents for the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall be compiled and published by the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission, and shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　黄河水利委员会应当定期将黄河水量调度执行情况向十一省区市人民政府水行政主管部门以及水库主管部门或者单位通报，并及时向社会公告。

**Article 31** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall periodically notify the water administrative departments of the people's governments of the eleven provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities and the competent departments or units of reservoirs of the implementation of the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River, and shall timely make public announcements.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构、县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门，应当在各自的职责范围内实施巡回监督检查，在用水高峰时对主要取(退)水口实施重点监督检查，在特殊情况下对有关河段、水库、主要取(退)水口进行驻守监督检查；发现重点污染物排放总量超过控制指标或者水体严重污染时，应当及时通报有关人民政府环境保护主管部门。

**Article 32** The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs, and the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, carry out patrol supervision and inspection, carry out key supervision and inspection of major water intakes (outfalls) during peak water use periods, and carry out stationing supervision and inspection of relevant river sections, reservoirs, and major water intakes (outfalls) under special circumstances; where the total discharge of key pollutants exceeds the control indicators or the water body is seriously polluted, they shall promptly notify the environmental protection department of the relevant people's government.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构、县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门实施监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查单位提供有关文件和资料，进行查阅或者复制；  
(二)要求被检查单位就执行本条例的有关问题进行说明；  
(三)进入被检查单位生产场所进行现场检查；  
(四)对取(退)水量进行现场监测；  
(五)责令被检查单位纠正违反本条例的行为。

**Article 33** When carrying out supervision and inspection, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs, and the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) require the unit under inspection to provide relevant documents and materials, and to consult or reproduce them;  
(2) require the unit under inspection to explain relevant issues concerning the implementation of these Regulations;  
(3) enter the production premises of the unit under inspection to conduct on-site inspection;  
(4) conduct on-site monitoring of the water diversion (return) volume;  
(5) order the unit under inspection to correct any act violating these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　监督检查人员在履行监督检查职责时，应当向被检查单位或者个人出示执法证件，被检查单位或者个人应当接受和配合监督检查工作，不得拒绝或者妨碍监督检查人员依法执行公务。

**Article 34** When performing their duties of supervision and inspection, the inspectors shall present their law enforcement credentials to the unit or individual under inspection, and the unit or individual under inspection shall accept and cooperate with the supervision and inspection work, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspectors from performing their official duties according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其上级主管部门、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
(一)不制订年度水量调度计划的；  
(二)不及时下达月、旬水量调度方案的；  
(三)不制订旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案及其实施方案和动用水库死库容水量调度方案的。

**Article 35** In case of any of the following acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law by their superior competent departments, units, or supervisory authorities:  
(1) failing to formulate the annual regulation plan;  
(2) failing to issue monthly and ten-day regulation schemes in a timely manner;  
(3) failing to formulate the preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions and its implementation plan and the regulation scheme for using the dead storage of the reservoir.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其上级主管部门、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分；造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不执行年度水量调度计划和下达的月、旬水量调度方案以及实时调度指令的；  
(二)不执行旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案及其实施方案、水量调度应急处置措施和动用水库死库容水量调度方案的；  
(三)不履行监督检查职责或者发现违法行为不予查处的；  
(四)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守等违法行为。

**Article 36** In case of any of the following acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law by their superior competent departments, units, or supervisory authorities; if serious consequences are caused and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to implement the annual regulation plan and the issued monthly and ten-day regulation schemes and real-time regulation directives;  
(2) failing to implement the preliminary regulation plan under drought emergency conditions and its implementation plan, the emergency response measures for regulation, and the regulation scheme for using the dead storage of the reservoir;  
(3) failing to perform the duties of supervision and inspection or failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered;  
(4) other illegal acts such as abuse of power and dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　省际或者重要控制断面下泄流量不符合规定的控制指标的，由黄河水利委员会予以通报，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，按照控制断面下泄流量的缺水量，在下一调度时段加倍扣除；对控制断面下游水量调度产生严重影响或者造成其他严重后果的，本年度不再新增该省、自治区的取水工程项目。对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其上级主管部门、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分。

**Article 37** Where the discharge flow at an inter-provincial or important control cross-section fails to meet the prescribed control indicator, the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission shall circulate a notice and order correction within a prescribed time limit; if correction is not made within the time limit, the shortfall in the discharge flow at the control cross-section shall be deducted double in the next regulation period; if causing serious impact on the regulation of water volume downstream of the control cross-section or any other serious consequences, no new water diversion project in that province or autonomous region shall be added in the current year. The responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law by their superior competent departments, units, or supervisory authorities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　水库出库流量控制断面的下泄流量不符合规定的控制指标，对控制断面下游水量调度产生严重影响的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其上级主管部门、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分。

**Article 38** Where the discharge flow at the outflow flow control cross-section of a reservoir fails to meet the prescribed control indicator, causing serious impact on the regulation of water volume downstream of the control cross-section, the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law by their superior competent departments, units, or supervisory authorities.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，有关用水单位或者水库管理单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河水利委员会及其所属管理机构按照管理权限，责令停止违法行为，给予警告，限期采取补救措施，并处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，由其上级主管部门、单位或者监察机关依法给予处分：  
(一)虚假填报或者篡改上报的水文监测数据、取用水量数据或者水库运行情况等资料的；  
(二)水库管理单位不执行水量调度方案和实时调度指令的；  
(三)超计划取用水的。

**Article 39** In case of any of the following acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations by the relevant water user or reservoir management unit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission and its subordinate administrative organs shall, in accordance with the management authority, order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, order remedial measures to be taken within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; the responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law by their superior competent departments, units, or supervisory authorities:  
(1) falsely filling in or altering the submitted data such as hydrological monitoring data, water diversion and use volume data, or reservoir operation conditions;  
(2) the reservoir management unit fails to implement the regulation scheme and real-time regulation directives;  
(3) diverting or using water exceeding the plan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)妨碍、阻挠监督检查人员或者取用水工程管理人员依法执行公务的；  
(二)在水量调度中煽动群众闹事的。

**Article 40** In case of any of the following acts in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the public security organ shall impose public security penalties according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) obstructing or hindering the inspectors or the management personnel of water diversion and use projects from performing their official duties according to law;  
(2) inciting the masses to create disturbances in the regulation of water volume.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　黄河水量调度中，有关用水计划建议和水库运行计划建议申报时间，年度水量调度计划制订、下达时间，月、旬水量调度方案下达时间，取(退)水水量报表报送时间等，由国务院水行政主管部门规定。

**Article 41** The time for submitting the proposals for water use plans and reservoir operation plans, the time for formulating and issuing the annual regulation plan, the time for issuing the monthly and ten-day regulation schemes, and the time for submitting the statements of water diversion (return) volume in the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River shall be prescribed by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在黄河水量调度中涉及水资源保护、防洪、防凌和水污染防治的，依照《中华人民共和国水法》、《中华人民共和国防洪法》和《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的有关规定执行。

**Article 42** Where the regulation of the water volume of the Yellow River involves water resources protection, flood control, ice prevention, and water pollution prevention, the relevant provisions of the Water Law of the People's Republic of China, the Flood Control Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例自2006年8月1日起施行。

**Article 43** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2006.
