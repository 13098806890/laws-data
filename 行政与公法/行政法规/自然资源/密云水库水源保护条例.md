### 正文

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　密云水库流域地方人民政府应当组织开展密云水库流域生态保护修复，采取下列措施恢复和增强水体自净能力，减少入河入库污染物对密云水库水质的影响：  
（一）建设水源涵养林；  
（二）在水库周边建设库滨带；  
（三）在水库入库口建设生态廊道；  
（四）依托现有排水沟渠建设植草过滤带；  
（五）科学规范增殖放流；  
（六）其他生态保护修复措施。  
密云水库流域生态保护修复涉及的重大工程，依法纳入国家级、省级生态保护修复重大工程规划。

**Article 12** The local people's governments in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall organize and carry out ecological protection and restoration of the Miyun Reservoir basin, and take the following measures to restore and enhance the water body's self-purification capacity, and reduce the impact of pollutants entering rivers and reservoirs on the water quality of Miyun Reservoir:  
(1) construct water conservation forests;  
(2) construct reservoir bank zones around the reservoir;  
(3) construct ecological corridors at the reservoir inlets;  
(4) construct grass-planted filtration belts by relying on existing drainage ditches;  
(5) scientifically and normatively carry out stock enhancement and release;  
(6) other ecological protection and restoration measures.  
Major projects involving ecological protection and restoration of the Miyun Reservoir basin shall, according to law, be incorporated into the national and provincial plans for major ecological protection and restoration projects.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在密云水库流域新建、改建、扩建建设项目，应当依法履行建设项目水土流失防治责任，采取有效措施加强水土流失防治。

**Article 13** For new construction, renovation, or expansion of construction projects in the Miyun Reservoir basin, the responsibility for soil erosion prevention and control of construction projects shall, according to law, be fulfilled, and effective measures shall be taken to strengthen soil erosion prevention and control.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在密云水库管理范围以及潮河、白河干流河道管理范围内，禁止实施下列行为：  
（一）违法建设建筑物、构筑物；  
（二）未经处理达标、直接排放污水；  
（三）施用化肥农药；  
（四）采砂或者堆放矿渣、石渣、煤灰、垃圾等固体废物；  
（五）网箱养殖；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定禁止实施的其他行为。

**Article 14** Within the management scope of Miyun Reservoir and the management scope of the main river channels of the Chaohe River and Baihe River, the following acts are prohibited:  
(1) illegally constructing buildings or structures;  
(2) directly discharging sewage without treatment up to standards;  
(3) applying chemical fertilizers and pesticides;  
(4) sand mining or stockpiling solid wastes such as slag, stone chips, coal ash, and garbage;  
(5) cage culture;  
(6) other acts prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在密云水库管理范围以及潮河、白河干流河道管理范围外100米范围内，除生态修复设施、水工程设施、公用基础设施以及北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府确定的不造成水污染的必要项目外，禁止新建、扩建其他建设项目。  
前款规定区域内严格控制化肥、农药施用，具体办法由北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府制定。

**Article 15** Within the area of 100 meters outside the management scope of Miyun Reservoir and the management scope of the main river channels of the Chaohe River and Baihe River, except for ecological restoration facilities, water engineering facilities, public infrastructure, and necessary projects determined by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province that do not cause water pollution, no other construction projects shall be newly built or expanded.  
Within the area specified in the preceding paragraph, the application of chemical fertilizers and pesticides shall be strictly controlled, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在密云水库管理范围以及潮河、白河干流河道管理范围外1000米范围内，禁止新建、扩建尾矿库、化工园区和化工项目。  
根据密云水库水源保护需要，北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府可以确定在前款规定区域内禁止或者限制新建、扩建的其他建设项目。

**Article 16** Within the area of 1,000 meters outside the management scope of Miyun Reservoir and the management scope of the main river channels of the Chaohe River and Baihe River, no new tailings ponds, chemical industry parks, or chemical projects shall be newly built or expanded.  
According to the needs for water source protection of Miyun Reservoir, the People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province may determine other construction projects to be prohibited or restricted from being newly built or expanded within the area specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[本条例第十四条](#art-14)、第十五条、第十六条规定的区域与依法划定的饮用水水源保护区重合的部分，其保护措施依照有关法律、行政法规关于饮用水水源保护区的规定执行。

**Article 17** Where the areas specified in Articles 14, 15, and 16 of these Regulations overlap with the legally delineated drinking water source protection zones, the protection measures shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on drinking water source protection zones.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府应当健全完善水量水质监测网络，建立断面考核机制和监测信息共享机制。  
潮河、白河干流和重要支流水量水质的省界监测断面、监测指标、监测标准等事项，由北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府共同确定。省界监测断面设有国家水文站点、水质监测站点的，可以直接采用其监测数据。

**Article 18** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall improve and perfect the water quantity and quality monitoring network, and establish cross-section assessment mechanisms and monitoring information sharing mechanisms.  
Matters such as the provincial boundary monitoring sections, monitoring indicators, and monitoring standards for water quantity and quality of the main streams and important tributaries of the Chaohe River and Baihe River shall be jointly determined by the People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province. Where national hydrological stations or water quality monitoring stations are located at the provincial boundary monitoring sections, their monitoring data may be directly used.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府生态环境、水行政、自然资源、农业农村等主管部门应当按照职责分工加强日常监管，并建立健全部门联合执法和信息共享机制，及时查处违反密云水库水源保护有关规定的违法行为。  
北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府应当推进密云水库水源保护区域联合执法，推动执法标准统一。

**Article 19** The competent departments of ecology and environment, water administration, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, and other departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, strengthen daily supervision, and establish and improve mechanisms for joint departmental enforcement and information sharing, and promptly investigate and handle illegal acts in violation of the relevant provisions on water source protection of Miyun Reservoir.  
The People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province shall promote joint enforcement in the Miyun Reservoir water source protection area and promote the unification of enforcement standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　密云水库流域各级河长应当认真履行职责，加强对河道环境的日常巡查管护，及时推动整改发现的河道环境问题。

**Article 20** The river chiefs at all levels in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall earnestly perform their duties, strengthen daily patrol and management of the river environment, and promptly promote the rectification of discovered river environmental issues.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、乡镇人民政府应当采取多种方式，加强对水源保护、绿色发展以及有关法律、法规的宣传教育。

**Article 21** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin, their relevant departments, and the township people's governments shall adopt various methods to strengthen the publicity and education on water source protection, green development, and relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院有关部门和相关地方人民政府加大对密云水库流域绿色发展的支持力度。  
国务院有关部门应当结合各自职责，加强对密云水库流域绿色发展的指导，并在公共资源配置、绿色产业布局、就业促进等方面依法依规给予支持。  
鼓励、支持金融机构按照市场化、法治化原则提供与密云水库流域绿色发展相适应的金融服务，引导社会资本依法依规参与密云水库流域绿色发展。

**Article 22** The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant local people's governments shall increase support for the green development of the Miyun Reservoir basin.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in light of their respective functions and duties, strengthen guidance on the green development of the Miyun Reservoir basin, and provide support in accordance with laws and regulations in such aspects as allocation of public resources, layout of green industries, and employment promotion.  
Financial institutions are encouraged and supported to provide financial services compatible with the green development of the Miyun Reservoir basin in accordance with market-oriented and rule-of-law principles, and social capital is guided to participate in the green development of the Miyun Reservoir basin in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府应当制定专项政策，明确支持和促进密云水库流域绿色发展的具体措施。

**Article 23** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall formulate special policies to specify concrete measures for supporting and promoting the green development of the Miyun Reservoir basin.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府应当就密云水库水源保护协商建立地区间横向生态保护补偿机制，综合考虑密云水库水源保护现状、保护成本、保护成效以及地区经济社会发展水平、财政承受能力等因素，合理确定、动态调整补偿标准。  
北京市人民政府、河北省人民政府统筹使用不同渠道生态保护补偿资金，对密云水库水源保护实施财政纵向补偿。中央财政按照生态保护补偿制度有关规定予以支持。  
鼓励充分发挥市场机制在密云水库流域生态保护补偿中的作用，推进生态保护补偿市场化发展。

**Article 24** The People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province shall negotiate to establish a horizontal ecological protection compensation mechanism among regions for the water source protection of Miyun Reservoir, and comprehensively consider factors such as the current status of water source protection, protection costs, protection effectiveness, as well as the level of regional economic and social development and fiscal affordability, to reasonably determine and dynamically adjust the compensation standards.  
The People's Government of Beijing Municipality and the People's Government of Hebei Province shall coordinate the use of ecological protection compensation funds from different channels to implement fiscal vertical compensation for the water source protection of Miyun Reservoir. The central finance shall provide support in accordance with the relevant provisions of the ecological protection compensation system.  
The full play of the market mechanism in the ecological protection compensation of the Miyun Reservoir basin is encouraged, and the market-oriented development of ecological protection compensation shall be promoted.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　密云水库流域地方人民政府及其有关部门在密云水库水源保护工作中不履行或者不正确履行职责的，依照法律、法规和国家有关规定追究责任，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where the local people's governments in the Miyun Reservoir basin and their relevant departments fail to perform or incorrectly perform their duties in the water source protection of Miyun Reservoir, they shall be held accountable in accordance with laws, regulations, and relevant state provisions, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例第十条第二款、第十三条、第十四条规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。有关法律、行政法规对[本条例第十四条](#art-14)第三项、第五项行为未作处罚规定的，由密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府生态环境、水行政、农业农村等主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为；情节严重的，对单位处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对个人处500元以上2000元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 26** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 10, paragraph 2, Article 13, or Article 14 of these Regulations shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations. Where relevant laws and administrative regulations do not provide for punishment for the acts specified in Article 14, items (3) and (5) of these Regulations, the competent departments of ecology and environment, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs, and other departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order cessation of the illegal act; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第一款、第十六条规定的，由密云水库流域县级以上地方人民政府生态环境、水行政、自然资源等主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 15, paragraph 1, or Article 16 of these Regulations shall, by the competent departments of ecology and environment, water administration, natural resources, and other departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Miyun Reservoir basin in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be ordered to cease the illegal act, restore the original condition within a time limit, and be imposed a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　密云水库水源保护及相关活动除应当遵守本条例外，还应当遵守生态环境保护、防灾减灾救灾等有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 28** In addition to complying with these Regulations, the water source protection of Miyun Reservoir and related activities shall also comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on ecological environment protection, disaster prevention, mitigation, and relief.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自2025年12月1日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2025.
