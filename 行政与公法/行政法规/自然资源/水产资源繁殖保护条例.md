### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据中华人民共和国宪法第六条：“矿藏，水流，国有的森林、荒地和其他海陆资源，都属于全民所有”和第十一条：“国家保护环境和自然资源，防治污染和其他公害”的精神，为了繁殖保护水产资源，发展水产事业，以适应社会主义现代化建设的需要，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the spirit of Article 6 of the Constitution of the People's Republic of China, which provides that "mineral deposits, waters, state-owned forests, wasteland and other marine and land resources are owned by the whole people," and Article 11, which provides that "the state protects the environment and natural resources, and prevents and controls pollution and other public hazards," for the purpose of conserving and protecting aquatic resources and developing the aquaculture industry to meet the needs of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡是有经济价值的水生动物和植物的亲体、幼体、卵子、孢子等，以及赖以繁殖成长的水域环境，都按本条例的规定加以保护。

**Article 2** All parent bodies, larvae, eggs, spores, and other forms of aquatic animals and plants of economic value, as well as the aquatic environment on which they depend for reproduction and growth, shall be protected in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家水产总局、各海区渔业指挥部和地方各级革命委员会，应当加强对水产资源繁殖保护工作的组织领导，充分发动和依靠群众，认真贯彻执行本条例。

**Article 3** The National Aquatic Products Administration, the regional fishery headquarters, and the local revolutionary committees at all levels shall strengthen the organizational leadership over the conservation and protection of aquatic resources, fully mobilize and rely on the masses, and earnestly implement these Regulations.

### 第二章　保护对象和采捕原则

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对下列重要或名贵的水生动物和植物应当重点加以保护。  
(一)鱼类  
海水鱼：带鱼、大黄鱼、小黄鱼、兰圆鲹、沙丁鱼、太平洋鲱鱼、鳓鱼、真鲷、黑鲷、二长棘鲷、红笛鲷、梭鱼、鲆、鲽、鳎、石斑鱼、鳕鱼、狗母鱼、金线鱼、鲳鱼、鱼、白姑鱼、黄姑鱼、鲐鱼、马鲛、海鳗。  
淡水鱼：鲤鱼、青鱼、草鱼、鲢鱼、鳙鱼、鲌鱼、红鳍鲌鱼、鲮鱼、鲫鱼、鲥鱼、鳜鱼、鲂鱼、鳊鱼、鲑鱼、长江鲟、中华鲟、白鲟、青海湖裸鲤、鲚鱼、银鱼、河鳗、黄鳝、鲴鱼。  
(二)虾蟹类  
对虾、毛虾、青虾、鹰爪虾、中华绒螯蟹、梭子蟹、青蟹。  
(三)贝类  
鲍鱼、蛏、蚶、牡蛎、西施舌、扇贝、江瑶、文蛤、杂色蛤、翡翠贻贝、紫贻贝、厚壳贻贝、珍珠贝、河蚌。  
(四)海藻类  
紫菜、裙带菜、石花菜、江篱、海带、麒麟菜。  
(五)淡水食用水生植物类  
连藕、菱角、芡实。  
(六)其它  
白鳍豚、鲸、大鲵、海龟、玳瑁、海参、乌贼、鱿鱼、乌龟、鳖。  
各省、自治区、直辖市革命委员会可以根据本地的水产资源情况，对重点保护对象，作必要的增减。

**Article 4** The following important or valuable aquatic animals and plants shall be given priority protection.  
(1) Fish  
Marine fish: hairtail, large yellow croaker, small yellow croaker, blue round scad, sardine, Pacific herring, Chinese herring, red sea bream, black sea bream, two-line spine bream, red snapper, mullet, flounder, halibut, tonguefish, grouper, cod, lizardfish, golden thread, pomfret, Chinese mackerel, white croaker, yellow croaker, chub mackerel, Spanish mackerel, sea eel.  
Freshwater fish: common carp, black carp, grass carp, silver carp, bighead carp, whitefish, redfin whitefish, mud carp, crucian carp, Chinese herring, mandarin fish, blunt-snout bream, bream, salmon, Yangtze sturgeon, Chinese sturgeon, white sturgeon, Qinghai Lake naked carp, Chinese grenadier anchovy, icefish, river eel, yellow eel, Chinese siniperca.  
(2) Shrimps and crabs  
Penaeus shrimp, Acetes shrimp, freshwater shrimp, sergestid shrimp, Chinese mitten crab, swimming crab, mud crab.  
(3) Shellfish  
Abalone, razor clam, blood clam, oyster, donax, scallop, pen shell, carpet shell, variegated clam, green mussel, blue mussel, thick-shell mussel, pearl oyster, freshwater mussel.  
(4) Marine algae  
Laver, wakame, agarophyte, Gracilaria, kelp, Eucheuma.  
(5) Freshwater edible aquatic plants  
Lotus root, water caltrop, fox nut.  
(6) Others  
Yangtze finless porpoise, whale, giant salamander, sea turtle, hawksbill turtle, sea cucumber, cuttlefish, squid, tortoise, soft-shelled turtle.  
The revolutionary committees of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the local aquatic resource situation, make necessary additions or deletions to the protected species.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　水生动物的可捕标准，应当以达到性成熟为原则。对各种捕捞对象应当规定具体的可捕标准(长度或重量)和渔获物中小于可捕标准部分的最大比重。捕捞时应当保留足够数量的亲体，使资源能够稳定增长。  
各种经济藻类和淡水食用水生植物，应当待其长成后方得采收，并注意留种、留株，合理轮采。

**Article 5** The catchable standard for aquatic animals shall be based on the principle of reaching sexual maturity. Specific catchable standards (length or weight) and the maximum proportion of catches below such standards shall be prescribed for various target species. During fishing, a sufficient number of parent stock shall be retained to ensure the stable growth of the resource.  
Various economic algae and freshwater edible aquatic plants shall be harvested only after they have fully grown, and care shall be taken to preserve seeds and parent plants, and to adopt a reasonable rotation of harvesting.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各地应当因地制宜采取各种措施，如改良水域条件、人工投放苗种、投放鱼巢、灌江纳苗、营救幼鱼、移植驯化、消除敌害、引种栽植等，增殖水产资源。

**Article 6** All localities shall adopt various measures tailored to local conditions, such as improving water area conditions, artificially releasing fry and fingerlings, placing fish nests, diverting river water to capture fry, rescuing juvenile fish, transplanting and acclimatizing species, eliminating predators, and introducing and planting species, to enhance aquatic resources.

### 第三章　禁渔区和禁渔期

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对某些重要鱼虾贝类产卵场、越冬场和幼体索饵场，应当合理规定禁渔区、禁渔期，分别不同情况，禁止全部作业，或限制作业的种类和某些作业的渔具数量。

**Article 7** For certain important spawning grounds, overwintering grounds, and feeding grounds of fish, shrimp, and shellfish, closed areas and closed seasons shall be reasonably prescribed. Depending on the circumstances, either all operations shall be prohibited, or specific types of operations and the number of fishing gear for certain operations shall be restricted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　凡是鱼、蟹等产卵洄游通道的江河，不得遮断河面拦捕，应当留出一定宽度的通道，以保证足够数量的亲体上溯或降河产卵繁殖。更不准在闸口拦捕鱼、蟹幼体和产卵洄游的亲体，必要时应当规定禁渔期。因养殖生产需要而捕捞鱼苗、蟹苗者，应当经省、自治区、直辖市水产部门批准，在指定水域和时间内作业。

**Article 8** In rivers that serve as spawning migration routes for fish, crabs, and the like, it shall not be permitted to block the river surface and intercept the catch. A passage of sufficient width shall be left to ensure that an adequate number of parent fish can ascend or descend the river to spawn and reproduce. It shall also be prohibited to intercept and catch juvenile fish, crabs, or parent stocks during their spawning migration at sluice gates. If necessary, closed seasons shall be prescribed. Those who need to catch fish fry or crab larvae for aquaculture production shall obtain approval from the aquatic product department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall operate only in designated waters and during designated periods.

### 第四章　渔具和渔法

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各种主要渔具，应当按不同捕捞对象，分别规定最小网眼(箔眼)尺寸。其中机轮拖网、围网和机帆船拖网的最小网眼尺寸，由国家水产总局规定。  
禁止制造或出售不合规定的渔具。

**Article 9** For various major fishing gear, the minimum mesh size (or netting size) shall be prescribed separately according to different target species. The minimum mesh size for mechanical trawls, purse seines, and motorized sailing vessel trawls shall be prescribed by the National Aquatic Products Administration.  
It shall be prohibited to manufacture or sell non-compliant fishing gear.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　现有危害资源的渔具、渔法，应当根据其危害资源的程度，区别对待。对危害资源较轻的，应当有计划、有步骤地予以改进。对严重危害资源的，应当加以禁止或限期淘汰，在没有完全淘汰之前，应当适当地限制其作业场所和时间。  
捕捞小型成熟鱼、虾的小眼网具，只准在指定的水域和时间内作业。

**Article 10** Existing fishing gear and methods that are harmful to resources shall be treated differently according to the extent of the harm. Those that cause minor harm to resources shall be improved in a planned and step-by-step manner. Those that cause serious harm to resources shall be prohibited or phased out within a specified period. Before they are completely phased out, the fishing grounds and time of their use shall be appropriately restricted.  
Small-mesh nets used for catching small mature fish and shrimp shall be permitted only in designated waters and during designated periods.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　严禁炸鱼、毒鱼、滥用电力捕鱼以及进行敲䑩作业等严重损害水产资源的行为。

**Article 11** It shall be strictly prohibited to engage in acts that severely damage aquatic resources, such as using explosives to catch fish, using poisons to catch fish, abusing electric fishing methods, and operating the "knocking" fishing method.

### 第五章　水域环境的维护

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　禁止向渔业水域排弃有害水产资源的污水、油类、油性混合物等污染物质和废弃物。各工矿企业必须严格执行国家颁发的《工业“三废”排放试行标准》、《放射防护规定》和其他有关规定。  
因卫生防疫或驱除病虫害等，需要向渔业水域投注药物时，应当兼顾到水产资源的繁殖保护。农村浸麻应当集中在指定的水域中进行。

**Article 12** It shall be prohibited to discharge into fishery waters polluting substances and wastes such as sewage, oil, and oily mixtures that are harmful to aquatic resources. All industrial and mining enterprises must strictly implement the "Provisional Standards for the Discharge of Industrial "Three Wastes"" issued by the state, the "Regulations on Radiation Protection" and other relevant provisions.  
When it is necessary to inject drugs into fishery waters for health and epidemic prevention or to control pests and diseases, the conservation and protection of aquatic resources shall be taken into account. The retting of hemp in rural areas shall be concentrated in designated waters.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　修建水利工程，要注意保护渔业水域环境。在鱼、蟹等洄游通道筑坝，要相应地建造过鱼设施。已建成的水利工程，凡阻碍鱼、蟹等洄游和产卵的，由水产部门和水利管理部门协商，在许可的水位、水量、水质的条件下，适时开闸纳苗或捕苗移殖。  
围垦海涂、湖滩，要在不损害水产资源的条件下，统筹安排，有计划地进行。

**Article 13** When constructing water conservancy projects, attention shall be paid to protecting the fishery water environment. Where dams are built on migration routes of fish, crabs, etc., corresponding fish passage facilities shall be constructed. For existing water conservancy projects that obstruct the migration and spawning of fish, crabs, etc., the aquatic product department and the water conservancy management department shall negotiate, and under the conditions of permissible water level, water volume, and water quality, shall timely open sluice gates to allow fry to enter or capture fry for transplantation.  
Reclamation of tidal flats and lake beaches shall be carried out under conditions that do not harm aquatic resources, with overall planning and in a planned manner.

### 第六章　奖惩

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对贯彻执行本条例有成绩的单位或个人，国家水产总局、各海区渔业指挥部和地方各级革命委员会应当酌情给予表扬或适当的物质奖励。

**Article 14** The National Aquatic Products Administration, the regional fishery headquarters, and the local revolutionary committees at all levels shall, as appropriate, give commendations or appropriate material rewards to units or individuals that have achieved outstanding results in implementing these Regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对违反本条例的，应当视情节轻重给予批评教育，或赔偿损失、没收渔获、没收渔具、罚款等处分。凡干部带头怂恿违反本条例的，要追究责任，必要时给予行政或纪律处分。对严重损害资源造成重大破坏的，或抗拒管理，行凶打人的，要追究刑事责任。对坏人的破坏活动要坚决打击，依法惩处。

**Article 15** Those who violate these Regulations shall, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, be given criticism and education, or be subject to such penalties as compensation for losses, confiscation of catches, confiscation of fishing gear, or fines. Where any cadre takes the lead in instigating violations of these Regulations, his or her liability shall be investigated, and administrative or disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed if necessary. Where there is serious damage to resources causing major destruction, or resistance to management or assault causing injury, criminal liability shall be investigated. The sabotage activities of bad elements shall be resolutely combated and punished according to law.

### 第七章　组织领导和职责

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　全国水产资源繁殖保护工作由国家水产总局管理，有关部门配合。地方各级革命委员会应当指定水产行政部门和其他有关部门具体负责本条例的贯彻执行，并可以根据需要设置渔政管理机构。各海区渔业指挥部和省、自治区、直辖市应当配备渔政船只。  
有些海湾、湖泊、江河、水库等水域，也可以根据需要，经省、自治区、直辖市革命委员会批准，设立水产资源繁殖保护管理机构或群众性的管理委员会。

**Article 16** The national conservation and protection of aquatic resources shall be managed by the National Aquatic Products Administration, with the cooperation of relevant departments. Local revolutionary committees at all levels shall designate the aquatic product administrative departments and other relevant departments to be specifically responsible for the implementation of these Regulations, and may establish fishery administration agencies as necessary. The regional fishery headquarters and provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be equipped with fishery administration vessels.  
For certain bays, lakes, rivers, reservoirs, and other waters, if necessary, with the approval of the revolutionary committee of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, management agencies for the conservation and protection of aquatic resources or mass management committees may be established.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各级水产行政部门及其渔政管理机构，应当切实加强对水产资源繁殖保护工作的管理，建立渔业许可证制度，核定渔船、渔具发展数量和作业类型，进行渔船登记，加强监督检查，保障对水产资源的合理利用。  
水产科研部门应当将资源调查、资源保护和改进渔具、渔法的研究工作列为一项重要任务，及时提出水产资源繁殖保护的建议，并为制定实施细则提供科学依据。

**Article 17** The aquatic product administrative departments at all levels and their fishery administration agencies shall effectively strengthen the management of the conservation and protection of aquatic resources, establish a fishery license system, determine the number of fishing vessels and fishing gear to be developed and the types of operations, register fishing vessels, strengthen supervision and inspection, and ensure the rational utilization of aquatic resources.  
Aquatic product research departments shall list the research tasks of resource surveys, resource protection, and improvement of fishing gear and methods as an important mission, promptly put forward suggestions for the conservation and protection of aquatic resources, and provide scientific basis for formulating implementing rules.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　凡是跨越本省、自治区、直辖市水域进行渔业生产的，必须遵守当地水产资源繁殖保护的有关具体规定。  
因科学研究工作需要，从事与本条例和当地有关水产资源繁殖保护的规定有抵触的活动，必须事先报经省、自治区、直辖市水产行政部门批准。

**Article 18** Anyone who engages in fishery production across the waters of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government must comply with the relevant specific provisions on local conservation and protection of aquatic resources.  
Where activities that conflict with these Regulations and local provisions on the conservation and protection of aquatic resources are necessary for scientific research work, prior approval must be obtained from the aquatic product administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　地方各级革命委员会应当根据本条例的规定，结合本地区的具体情况，制定实施细则，报上一级领导机关备案。

**Article 19** Local revolutionary committees at all levels shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and in light of the specific local conditions, formulate implementing rules and report them to the next higher leading authority for filing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本条例自颁布之日起施行。

**Article 20** These Regulations shall come into force on the date of their promulgation.
