# 中华人民共和国濒危野生动植物进出口管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2006-09-01

> (2006年4月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第465号公布　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一条　为了加强对濒危野生动植物及其产品的进出口管理，保护和合理利用野生动植物资源，履行《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》(以下简称公约)，制定本条例。 第二条　进口或者出口公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，应当遵守本条例。 出口国家重点保护的野生动植物及其产品，依照本条例有关出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的规定办理。 第三条　国务院林业、农业(渔业)主管部门(以下称国务院野生动植物主管部门)，按照职责分工主管全国濒危野生动植物及其产品的进出口管理工作，并做好与履行公约有关的工作。 国务院其他有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内负责做好相关工作。 第四条　国家濒危物种进出口管理机构代表中国政府履行公约，依照本条例的规定对经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准出口的国家重点保护的野生动植物及其产品、批准进口或者出口的公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，核发允许进出口证明书。 第五条　国家濒危物种进出口科学机构依照本条例，组织陆生野生动物、水生野生动物和野生植物等方面的专家，从事有关濒危野生动植物及其产品进出口的科学咨询工作。 第六条　禁止进口或者出口公约禁止以商业贸易为目的进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，因科学研究、驯养繁殖、人工培育、文化交流等特殊情况，需要进口或者出口的，应当经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准；按照有关规定由国务院批准的，应当报经国务院批准。 禁止出口未定名的或者新发现并有重要价值的野生动植物及其产品以及国务院或者国务院野生动植物主管部门禁止出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品。 第七条　进口或者出口公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，出口国务院或者国务院野生动植物主管部门限制出口的野生动植物及其产品，应当经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准。 第八条　进口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，必须具备下列条件： (一)对濒危野生动植物及其产品的使用符合国家有关规定； (二)具有有效控制措施并符合生态安全要求； (三)申请人提供的材料真实有效；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对濒危野生动植物及其产品的进出口管理，保护和合理利用野生动植物资源，履行《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》(以下简称公约)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the import and export management of endangered wild animals and plants and their products, protecting and rationally utilizing wild animal and plant resources, and fulfilling the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (hereinafter referred to as the Convention).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进口或者出口公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，应当遵守本条例。  
出口国家重点保护的野生动植物及其产品，依照本条例有关出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的规定办理。

**Article 2** The import or export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products whose import and export are restricted by the Convention shall comply with these Regulations.  
The export of wild animals and plants and their products under key national protection shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations concerning the export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院林业、农业(渔业)主管部门(以下称国务院野生动植物主管部门)，按照职责分工主管全国濒危野生动植物及其产品的进出口管理工作，并做好与履行公约有关的工作。  
国务院其他有关部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内负责做好相关工作。

**Article 3** The competent departments of forestry, agriculture (fisheries) under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council) shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, take charge of the import and export administration of endangered wild animals and plants and their products nationwide, and shall properly handle the work relating to the implementation of the Convention.  
Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the relevant work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家濒危物种进出口管理机构代表中国政府履行公约，依照本条例的规定对经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准出口的国家重点保护的野生动植物及其产品、批准进口或者出口的公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，核发允许进出口证明书。

**Article 4** The National Administration for the Import and Export of Endangered Species shall, on behalf of the Chinese Government, implement the Convention, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, issue permits for the import or export of the following: wild animals and plants under key national protection and their products that have been approved for export by the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council; and endangered wild animals and plants and their products whose import and export are restricted by the Convention and have been approved for import or export.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家濒危物种进出口科学机构依照本条例，组织陆生野生动物、水生野生动物和野生植物等方面的专家，从事有关濒危野生动植物及其产品进出口的科学咨询工作。

**Article 5** The National Scientific Authority on Import and Export of Endangered Species shall, in accordance with these Regulations, organize experts in terrestrial wild animals, aquatic wild animals, and wild plants to engage in scientific consultation work concerning the import and export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　禁止进口或者出口公约禁止以商业贸易为目的进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，因科学研究、驯养繁殖、人工培育、文化交流等特殊情况，需要进口或者出口的，应当经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准；按照有关规定由国务院批准的，应当报经国务院批准。  
禁止出口未定名的或者新发现并有重要价值的野生动植物及其产品以及国务院或者国务院野生动植物主管部门禁止出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品。

**Article 6** The import or export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products whose import and export for commercial trade are prohibited by the Convention shall be prohibited, except that where such import or export is necessary due to special circumstances such as scientific research, domestication and breeding, artificial cultivation, or cultural exchange, approval shall be obtained from the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council; where approval by the State Council is required under relevant provisions, it shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The export of wild animals and plants and their products that have not been named or are newly discovered and of significant value, as well as endangered wild animals and plants and their products whose export is prohibited by the State Council or the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council, shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　进口或者出口公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品，出口国务院或者国务院野生动植物主管部门限制出口的野生动植物及其产品，应当经国务院野生动植物主管部门批准。

**Article 7** The import or export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products whose import and export are restricted by the Convention, and the export of wild animals and plants and their products whose export is restricted by the State Council or the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council, shall be subject to the approval of the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　进口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)对濒危野生动植物及其产品的使用符合国家有关规定；  
(二)具有有效控制措施并符合生态安全要求；  
(三)申请人提供的材料真实有效；  
(四)国务院野生动植物主管部门公示的其他条件。

**Article 8** The import of endangered wild animals and plants and their products shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The use of endangered wild animals and plants and their products complies with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) Effective control measures are in place and the requirements for ecological safety are met;  
(3) The materials submitted by the applicant are authentic and valid;  
(4) Other conditions as publicized by the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)符合生态安全要求和公共利益；  
(二)来源合法；  
(三)申请人提供的材料真实有效；  
(四)不属于国务院或者国务院野生动植物主管部门禁止出口的；  
(五)国务院野生动植物主管部门公示的其他条件。

**Article 9** The export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) The requirements for ecological safety and public interests are met;  
(2) The source is lawful;  
(3) The materials submitted by the applicant are authentic and valid;  
(4) The items are not prohibited from export by the State Council or the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council;  
(5) Other conditions as publicized by the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，申请人应当按照管理权限，向其所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业(渔业)主管部门提出申请，或者向国务院林业主管部门提出申请，并提交下列材料：  
(一)进口或者出口合同；  
(二)濒危野生动植物及其产品的名称、种类、数量和用途；  
(三)活体濒危野生动物装运设施的说明资料；  
(四)国务院野生动植物主管部门公示的其他应当提交的材料。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业(渔业)主管部门应当自收到申请之日起10个工作日内签署意见，并将全部申请材料转报国务院农业(渔业)主管部门。

**Article 10** For the import or export of endangered wild animals and plants and their products, the applicant shall, in accordance with the administrative competence, submit an application to the competent department of agriculture (fisheries) of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located, or to the competent department of forestry under the State Council, and shall submit the following materials:  
(1) The import or export contract;  
(2) The name, type, quantity and purpose of use of the endangered wild animals and plants and their products;  
(3) The explanatory materials on the shipping facilities for living endangered wild animals;  
(4) Other materials that shall be submitted as publicized by the competent departments of wild animals and plants under the State Council.  
The competent department of agriculture (fisheries) of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 10 working days from the date of receipt of the application, sign its comments and forward all the application materials to the competent department of agriculture (fisheries) under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院野生动植物主管部门应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定，并书面通知申请人。在20个工作日内不能作出决定的，经本行政机关负责人批准，可以延长10个工作日，延长的期限和理由应当通知申请人。

**Article 11** The competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove, and shall notify the applicant in writing. If a decision cannot be made within 20 working days, upon approval of the head of the administrative department, the period may be extended by 10 working days, and the extended period and reasons therefor shall be notified to the applicant.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请人取得国务院野生动植物主管部门的进出口批准文件后，应当在批准文件规定的有效期内，向国家濒危物种进出口管理机构申请核发允许进出口证明书。  
申请核发允许进出口证明书时应当提交下列材料：  
(一)允许进出口证明书申请表；  
(二)进出口批准文件；  
(三)进口或者出口合同。  
进口公约限制进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品的，申请人还应当提交出口国(地区)濒危物种进出口管理机构核发的允许出口证明材料；出口公约禁止以商业贸易为目的进出口的濒危野生动植物及其产品的，申请人还应当提交进口国(地区)濒危物种进出口管理机构核发的允许进口证明材料；进口的濒危野生动植物及其产品再出口时，申请人还应当提交海关进口货物报关单和海关签注的允许进口证明书。

**Article 12** After obtaining the import or export approval document from the competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council, the applicant shall, within the validity period specified in the approval document, apply to the national endangered species import and export management authority for the issuance of a permit for import and export.  
When applying for the issuance of a permit for import and export, the applicant shall submit the following materials:  
(1) an application form for the permit for import and export;  
(2) the import or export approval document;  
(3) the import or export contract.  
If importing endangered wild animals or plants and their products whose import and export are restricted by the Convention, the applicant shall also submit the export permit certificate issued by the endangered species import and export management authority of the exporting country (region); if exporting endangered wild animals or plants and their products whose import and export are prohibited for the purpose of commercial trade by the Convention, the applicant shall also submit the import permit certificate issued by the endangered species import and export management authority of the importing country (region); if re-exporting imported endangered wild animals or plants and their products, the applicant shall also submit the customs import goods declaration form and the import permit certificate endorsed by the customs.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家濒危物种进出口管理机构应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内，作出审核决定。对申请材料齐全、符合本条例规定和公约要求的，应当核发允许进出口证明书；对不予核发允许进出口证明书的，应当书面通知申请人和国务院野生动植物主管部门并说明理由。在20个工作日内不能作出决定的，经本机构负责人批准，可以延长10个工作日，延长的期限和理由应当通知申请人。  
国家濒危物种进出口管理机构在审核时，对申请材料不符合要求的，应当在5个工作日内一次性通知申请人需要补正的全部内容。

**Article 13** The national endangered species import and export management authority shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application, make an examination decision. Where the application materials are complete and comply with the provisions of these Regulations and the requirements of the Convention, it shall issue a permit for import and export; where it decides not to issue a permit for import and export, it shall notify the applicant and the competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council in writing and state the reasons. If a decision cannot be made within 20 working days, upon approval of the head of the authority, the period may be extended by 10 working days, and the extended period and reasons therefor shall be notified to the applicant.  
When conducting the examination, if the application materials do not meet the requirements, the national endangered species import and export management authority shall, within 5 working days, notify the applicant at one time of all the contents that need to be supplemented or corrected.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家濒危物种进出口管理机构在核发允许进出口证明书时，需要咨询国家濒危物种进出口科学机构的意见，或者需要向境外相关机构核实允许进出口证明材料等有关内容的，应当自收到申请之日起5个工作日内，将有关材料送国家濒危物种进出口科学机构咨询意见或者向境外相关机构核实有关内容。咨询意见、核实内容所需时间不计入核发允许进出口证明书工作日之内。

**Article 14** Where, when issuing a permit for import and export, the national endangered species import and export management authority needs to consult the national endangered species import and export scientific institution for opinions, or needs to verify with overseas relevant authorities the contents of the permit certificate and other related materials, it shall, within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the application, send the relevant materials to the national endangered species import and export scientific institution for opinions or verify the relevant contents with the overseas relevant authorities. The time needed for obtaining opinions or verifying contents shall not be counted in the working days for issuing the permit for import and export.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院野生动植物主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动植物主管部门以及国家濒危物种进出口管理机构，在审批濒危野生动植物及其产品进出口时，除收取国家规定的费用外，不得收取其他费用。

**Article 15** When examining and approving the import or export of endangered wild animals or plants and their products, the competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council, the competent departments of wildlife administration of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the national endangered species import and export management authority shall not collect any fees other than those prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　因进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品对野生动植物资源、生态安全造成或者可能造成严重危害和影响的，由国务院野生动植物主管部门提出临时禁止或者限制濒危野生动植物及其产品进出口的措施，报国务院批准后执行。

**Article 16** Where the import or export of endangered wild animals or plants and their products causes or may cause serious harm or impact to wildlife resources or ecological security, the competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council shall propose measures for temporarily prohibiting or restricting the import or export of endangered wild animals or plants and their products, and shall submit such measures to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从不属于任何国家管辖的海域获得的濒危野生动植物及其产品，进入中国领域的，参照本条例有关进口的规定管理。

**Article 17** Endangered wild animals or plants and their products obtained from sea areas not under the jurisdiction of any country that enter the territory of China shall be managed by reference to the provisions of these Regulations on import.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　进口濒危野生动植物及其产品涉及外来物种管理的，出口濒危野生动植物及其产品涉及种质资源管理的，应当遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 18** Where the import of endangered wild animals or plants and their products involves the management of alien species, or the export of endangered wild animals or plants and their products involves the management of germplasm resources, the relevant provisions of the State shall be complied with.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，应当在国务院野生动植物主管部门会同海关总署指定并经国务院批准的口岸进行。

**Article 19** The import or export of endangered wild animals or plants and their products shall be conducted at ports designated by the competent department of wildlife administration under the State Council jointly with the General Administration of Customs and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，应当按照允许进出口证明书规定的种类、数量、口岸、期限完成进出口活动。

**Article 20** Those importing or exporting endangered wild animals or plants and their products shall complete the import or export activities in accordance with the species, quantity, port, and time limit specified in the permit for import and export.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，应当向海关提交允许进出口证明书，接受海关监管，并自海关放行之日起30日内，将海关验讫的允许进出口证明书副本交国家濒危物种进出口管理机构备案。  
过境、转运和通运的濒危野生动植物及其产品，自入境起至出境前由海关监管。  
进出保税区、出口加工区等海关特定监管区域和保税场所的濒危野生动植物及其产品，应当接受海关监管，并按照海关总署和国家濒危物种进出口管理机构的规定办理进出口手续。  
进口或者出口濒危野生动植物及其产品的，应当凭允许进出口证明书向海关报检，并接受检验检疫。

**Article 21** Any person importing or exporting endangered wild animals and plants and their products shall submit a permit for import and export to the Customs, accept Customs supervision, and within 30 days from the date of Customs release, submit a copy of the permit endorsed by the Customs to the State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species for recordation.  
Endangered wild animals and plants and their products in transit, transshipment, or through transport shall be subject to Customs supervision from entry to exit.  
Endangered wild animals and plants and their products entering or leaving specific Customs supervision areas such as bonded zones and export processing zones and bonded premises shall accept Customs supervision and go through import and export formalities in accordance with the provisions of the General Administration of Customs and the State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species.  
Any person importing or exporting endangered wild animals and plants and their products shall declare to the Customs for inspection with the permit for import and export and accept inspection and quarantine.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家濒危物种进出口管理机构应当将核发允许进出口证明书的有关资料和濒危野生动植物及其产品年度进出口情况，及时抄送国务院野生动植物主管部门及其他有关主管部门。

**Article 22** The State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species shall promptly send a copy of the relevant information on issuing permits for import and export and the annual import and export situation of endangered wild animals and plants and their products to the competent department of wild animals and plants of the State Council and other relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　进出口批准文件由国务院野生动植物主管部门组织统一印制；允许进出口证明书及申请表由国家濒危物种进出口管理机构组织统一印制。

**Article 23** The import and export approval documents shall be uniformly printed under the organization of the competent department of wild animals and plants of the State Council; the permits for import and export and application forms shall be uniformly printed under the organization of the State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　野生动植物主管部门、国家濒危物种进出口管理机构的工作人员，利用职务上的便利收取他人财物或者谋取其他利益，不依照本条例的规定批准进出口、核发允许进出口证明书，情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 24** Any staff member of the competent department of wild animals and plants or the State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species who, taking advantage of his or her position, accepts property from others or seeks other benefits, and fails to approve import and export or issue permits for import and export in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the circumstances do not constitute a crime, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家濒危物种进出口科学机构的工作人员，利用职务上的便利收取他人财物或者谋取其他利益，出具虚假意见，情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 25** Any staff member of the scientific institution for import and export of endangered species of the State who, taking advantage of his or her position, accepts property from others or seeks other benefits, and issues false opinions, if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the circumstances do not constitute a crime, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　非法进口、出口或者以其他方式走私濒危野生动植物及其产品的，由海关依照海关法的有关规定予以处罚；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
罚没的实物移交野生动植物主管部门依法处理；罚没的实物依法需要实施检疫的，经检疫合格后，予以处理。罚没的实物需要返还原出口国(地区)的，应当由野生动植物主管部门移交国家濒危物种进出口管理机构依照公约规定处理。

**Article 26** Any person who illegally imports, exports or smuggles endangered wild animals and plants and their products by other means shall be punished by the Customs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
The confiscated items shall be handed over to the competent department of wild animals and plants for disposal according to law; if the confiscated items need to be quarantined according to law, they shall be disposed of after passing the quarantine. If the confiscated items need to be returned to the original exporting country (region), they shall be handed over by the competent department of wild animals and plants to the State Administration for Import and Export of Endangered Species for disposal in accordance with the provisions of the Convention.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　伪造、倒卖或者转让进出口批准文件或者允许进出口证明书的，由野生动植物主管部门或者市场监督管理部门按照职责分工依法予以处罚；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Any person who forges, scalps, or transfers import and export approval documents or permits for import and export shall be punished by the competent department of wild animals and plants or the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties according to law; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自2006年9月1日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force on September 1, 2006.
