# 野生药材资源保护管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1987-10-30  
**生效日期**：1987-12-01

> (1987年10月30日国务院发布　自1987年12月1日起施行) 第一条　为保护和合理利用野生药材资源，适应人民医疗保健事业的需要，特制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内采猎、经营野生药材的任何单位或个人，除国家另有规定外，都必须遵守本条例。 第三条　国家对野生药材资源实行保护、采猎相结合的原则，并创造条件开展人工种养。 第四条　国家重点保护的野生药材物种分为三级： 一级：濒临灭绝状态的稀有珍贵野生药材物种(以下简称一级保护野生药材物种)； 二级：分布区域缩小、资源处于衰竭状态的重要野生药材物种(以下简称二级保护野生药材物种)； 三级：资源严重减少的主要常用野生药材物种(以下简称三级保护野生药材物种)。 第五条　国家重点保护的野生药材物种名录，由国家医药管理部门会同国务院野生动物、植物管理部门制定。 在国家重点保护的野生药材物种名录之外，需要增加的野生药材保护物种，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定并抄送国家医药管理部门备案。 第六条　禁止采猎一级保护野生药材物种。 第七条　采猎、收购二、三级保护野生药材物种的，必须按照批准的计划执行。该计划由县以上(含县，下同)医药管理部门(含当地人民政府授权管理该项工作的有关部门，下同)会同同级野生动物、植物管理部门制定，报上一级医药管理部门批准。 第八条　采猎二、三级保护野生药材物种的，不得在禁止采猎区、禁止采猎期进行采猎，不得使用禁用工具进行采猎。 前款关于禁止采猎区、禁止采猎期和禁止使用的工具，由县以上医药管理部门会同同级野生动物、植物管理部门确定。 第九条　采猎二、三级保护野生药材物种的，必须持有采药证。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保护和合理利用野生药材资源，适应人民医疗保健事业的需要，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of protecting and rationally utilizing wild medicinal material resources and meeting the needs of the people's medical and health care undertakings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内采猎、经营野生药材的任何单位或个人，除国家另有规定外，都必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any unit or individual that collects, hunts, or deals in wild medicinal materials within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations, unless otherwise provided by the State.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对野生药材资源实行保护、采猎相结合的原则，并创造条件开展人工种养。

**Article 3** The State applies the principle of combining protection with collection and hunting of wild medicinal material resources, and shall create conditions for artificial cultivation and breeding.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家重点保护的野生药材物种分为三级：  
一级：濒临灭绝状态的稀有珍贵野生药材物种(以下简称一级保护野生药材物种)；  
二级：分布区域缩小、资源处于衰竭状态的重要野生药材物种(以下简称二级保护野生药材物种)；  
三级：资源严重减少的主要常用野生药材物种(以下简称三级保护野生药材物种)。

**Article 4** The wild medicinal material species under special State protection are classified into three grades:  
Grade I: rare and precious wild medicinal material species on the verge of extinction (hereinafter referred to as Grade I protected wild medicinal material species);  
Grade II: important wild medicinal material species whose distribution areas are shrinking and resources are in a state of depletion (hereinafter referred to as Grade II protected wild medicinal material species);  
Grade III: major commonly used wild medicinal material species whose resources are seriously reduced (hereinafter referred to as Grade III protected wild medicinal material species).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家重点保护的野生药材物种名录，由国家医药管理部门会同国务院野生动物、植物管理部门制定。  
在国家重点保护的野生药材物种名录之外，需要增加的野生药材保护物种，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定并抄送国家医药管理部门备案。

**Article 5** The list of wild medicinal material species under special State protection shall be formulated by the State pharmaceutical administration in conjunction with the wildlife and plant administration departments of the State Council.  
For wild medicinal material protection species that need to be added beyond the list of wild medicinal material species under special State protection, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate and submit them to the State pharmaceutical administration for record.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　禁止采猎一级保护野生药材物种。

**Article 6** The collection and hunting of Grade I protected wild medicinal material species are prohibited.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　采猎、收购二、三级保护野生药材物种的，必须按照批准的计划执行。该计划由县以上(含县，下同)医药管理部门(含当地人民政府授权管理该项工作的有关部门，下同)会同同级野生动物、植物管理部门制定，报上一级医药管理部门批准。

**Article 7** Whoever collects, hunts, or purchases Grade II or Grade III protected wild medicinal material species must carry out such activities in accordance with the approved plan. Such plan shall be formulated by the pharmaceutical administration at or above the county level (including the county level, the same below) (including the relevant department authorized by the local people's government to administer such work, the same below) in conjunction with the wildlife and plant administration departments at the same level, and shall be submitted to the pharmaceutical administration at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　采猎二、三级保护野生药材物种的，不得在禁止采猎区、禁止采猎期进行采猎，不得使用禁用工具进行采猎。  
前款关于禁止采猎区、禁止采猎期和禁止使用的工具，由县以上医药管理部门会同同级野生动物、植物管理部门确定。

**Article 8** Whoever collects or hunts Grade II or Grade III protected wild medicinal material species shall not carry out collection or hunting in prohibited areas or during prohibited periods, and shall not use prohibited tools for collection or hunting.  
The prohibited areas, prohibited periods, and prohibited tools referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be determined by the pharmaceutical administration at or above the county level in conjunction with the wildlife and plant administration departments at the same level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　采猎二、三级保护野生药材物种的，必须持有采药证。  
取得采药证后，需要进行采伐或狩猎的，必须分别向有关部门申请采伐证或狩猎证。

**Article 9** Whoever collects or hunts Grade II or Grade III protected wild medicinal material species must hold a collection permit.  
After obtaining the collection permit, if logging or hunting is required, the person must apply to the relevant departments for a logging permit or a hunting permit respectively.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　采药证的格式由国家医药管理部门确定。采药证由县以上医药管理部门会同同级野生动物、植物管理部门核发。  
采伐证或狩猎证的核发，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 10** The format of the collection permit shall be determined by the State pharmaceutical administration. The collection permit shall be issued by the pharmaceutical administration at or above the county level in conjunction with the wildlife and plant administration departments at the same level.  
The issuance of logging permits or hunting permits shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建立国家或地方野生药材资源保护区，需经国务院或县以上地方人民政府批准。  
在国家或地方自然保护区内建立野生药材资源保护区，必须征得国家或地方自然保护区主管部门的同意。

**Article 11** The establishment of a national or local wild medicinal material resource reserve requires the approval of the State Council or the local people's government at or above the county level.  
If a wild medicinal material resource reserve is to be established within a national or local nature reserve, the consent of the competent department of national or local nature reserves must be obtained.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　进入野生药材资源保护区从事科研、教学、旅游等活动的，必须经该保护区管理部门批准。进入设在国家或地方自然保护区范围内野生药材资源保护区的，还须征得该自然保护区主管部门的同意。

**Article 12** Whoever enters a wild medicinal material resource reserve to engage in scientific research, teaching, tourism, or other activities must obtain the approval of the administrative department of that reserve. If the person enters a wild medicinal material resource reserve located within a national or local nature reserve, the consent of the competent department of that nature reserve shall also be obtained.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　一级保护野生药材物种属于自然淘汰的，其药用部分由各级药材公司负责经营管理，但不得出口。

**Article 13** If Grade I protected wild medicinal material species are naturally eliminated, their medicinal parts shall be managed and operated by medicinal material companies at various levels, but shall not be exported.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　二、三级保护野生药材物种属于国家计划管理的品种，由中国药材公司统一经营管理；其余品种由产地县药材公司或其委托单位按照计划收购。

**Article 14** Grade II and Grade III protected wild medicinal material species that are subject to State planned management shall be uniformly managed and operated by the China Medicinal Materials Corporation; the remaining species shall be purchased by the county medicinal material company of the producing area or its entrusted entity according to the plan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　二、三级保护野生药材物种的药用部分，除国家另有规定外，实行限量出口。  
实行限量出口和出口许可证制度的品种，由国家医药管理部门会同国务院有关部门确定。

**Article 15** The medicinal parts of Grade II and Grade III protected wild medicinal material species shall be subject to restricted export, unless otherwise provided by the State.  
The varieties subject to restricted export and the export license system shall be determined by the State pharmaceutical administration in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　野生药材的规格、等级标准，由国家医药管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 16** The specifications and grade standards for wild medicinal materials shall be formulated by the State pharmaceutical administration in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对保护野生药材资源作出显著成绩的单位或个人，由各级医药管理部门会同同级有关部门给予精神鼓励或一次性物质奖励。

**Article 17** Units or individuals that have made outstanding achievements in protecting wild medicinal material resources shall be given spiritual encouragement or one-time material rewards by the pharmaceutical administrations at various levels in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)、第七条、第八条、第九条规定的，由当地县以上医药管理部门会同同级有关部门没收其非法采猎的野生药材及使用工具，并处以罚款。

**Article 18** Whoever violates the provisions of Articles 6, 7, 8, and 9 of these Regulations shall have his illegally collected or hunted wild medicinal materials and the tools used confiscated by the local pharmaceutical administration at or above the county level in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level, and shall also be imposed a fine.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反[本条例第十二条](#art-12)规定的，当地县以上医药管理部门和自然保护区主管部门有权制止；造成损失的，必须承担赔偿责任。

**Article 19** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 12 of these Regulations may be stopped by the local pharmaceutical administration at or above the county level and the competent department of nature reserves; if losses are caused, the violator must bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)、第十四条、第十五条规定的，由工商行政管理部门或有关部门没收其野生药材和全部违法所得，并处以罚款。

**Article 20** Whoever violates the provisions of Articles 13, 14, and 15 of these Regulations shall have his wild medicinal materials and all illegal gains confiscated by the administrative department for industry and commerce or relevant departments, and shall also be imposed a fine.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　保护野生药材资源管理部门工作人员徇私舞弊的，由所在单位或上级管理部门给予行政处分；造成野生药材资源损失的，必须承担赔偿责任。

**Article 21** If a staff member of the administrative department for the protection of wild medicinal material resources engages in malpractice for personal gain, he shall be given an administrative sanction by his unit or the higher-level administrative department; if losses of wild medicinal material resources are caused, he must bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚决定书之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉；期满不起诉又不执行的，作出行政处罚决定的部门可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 22** If a party refuses to accept the decision on administrative penalty, he may file a prosecution with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the decision on penalty; if he neither files a prosecution nor complies with the decision within the time limit, the department that made the decision on administrative penalty may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　破坏野生药材资源情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** If the destruction of wild medicinal material resources is of a serious nature and constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施细则。

**Article 24** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing rules in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例由国家医药管理局负责解释。

**Article 25** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Pharmaceutical Administration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自1987年12月1日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 1987.
