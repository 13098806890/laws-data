# 古树名木保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-01-25  
**生效日期**：2025-03-15

> 国家鼓励通过认捐、认养等多种形式资助古树名木保护事业。 第七条　国家鼓励、支持古树名木保护科学研究和先进技术推广应用，提高古树名木保护管理水平。 国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式，组织开展古树名木保护宣传教育，传承古树名木文化，增强全社会的古树名木保护意识，营造保护古树名木的良好社会氛围。 第八条　对古树按照下列标准实行分级保护： （一）树龄500年以上的，实行一级保护； （二）树龄300年以上不满500年的，实行二级保护； （三）树龄100年以上不满300年的，实行三级保护。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际情况，可以决定对城市内树龄300年以上不满500年的古树实行一级保护、树龄100年以上不满300年的古树实行二级保护。 对名木均实行一级保护，不受树龄限制。 第九条　全国绿化委员会每10年组织开展一次全国古树名木资源普查。普查间隔期内，县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门适时开展古树名木资源补充调查。在普查、补充调查以及古树名木认定等工作中，县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门可以组织开展古树名木鉴定。 国家鼓励单位、个人提供古树名木资源信息。 第十条　县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门根据古树名木资源普查、补充调查结果认定古树名木，报本级人民政府批准后依法公布。 实行一级保护的古树和名木由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府古树名木主管部门认定，实行二级保护、三级保护的古树分别由设区的市级、县级人民政府古树名木主管部门认定。 对在一定区域内集中生长形成的古树群体，可以实行整体保护，并按照涉及古树名木的最高保护等级，由相应的地方人民政府古树名木主管部门认定为古树群，报本级人民政府批准后依法公布。 古树名木的认定和公布实行动态管理。

### 正文

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励、支持古树名木保护科学研究和先进技术推广应用，提高古树名木保护管理水平。  
国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式，组织开展古树名木保护宣传教育，传承古树名木文化，增强全社会的古树名木保护意识，营造保护古树名木的良好社会氛围。

**Article 7** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on the protection of ancient and notable trees and the promotion and application of advanced technologies, so as to improve the level of protection and management of ancient and notable trees.  
The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in various forms, organize and carry out publicity and education on the protection of ancient and notable trees, inherit the culture of ancient and notable trees, enhance the awareness of the whole society on the protection of ancient and notable trees, and create a favorable social atmosphere for the protection of ancient and notable trees.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对古树按照下列标准实行分级保护：  
（一）树龄500年以上的，实行一级保护；  
（二）树龄300年以上不满500年的，实行二级保护；  
（三）树龄100年以上不满300年的，实行三级保护。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际情况，可以决定对城市内树龄300年以上不满500年的古树实行一级保护、树龄100年以上不满300年的古树实行二级保护。  
对名木均实行一级保护，不受树龄限制。

**Article 8** Ancient trees shall be protected by grades according to the following standards:  
(1) Those with a tree age of more than 500 years shall be subject to Grade I protection;  
(2) Those with a tree age of more than 300 years but less than 500 years shall be subject to Grade II protection;  
(3) Those with a tree age of more than 100 years but less than 300 years shall be subject to Grade III protection.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on actual circumstances, decide to apply Grade I protection to ancient trees within cities with a tree age of more than 300 years but less than 500 years, and Grade II protection to ancient trees with a tree age of more than 100 years but less than 300 years.  
All notable trees shall be subject to Grade I protection, without being limited by tree age.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国绿化委员会每10年组织开展一次全国古树名木资源普查。普查间隔期内，县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门适时开展古树名木资源补充调查。在普查、补充调查以及古树名木认定等工作中，县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门可以组织开展古树名木鉴定。  
国家鼓励单位、个人提供古树名木资源信息。

**Article 9** The National Greening Commission shall organize a national survey of ancient and notable tree resources every 10 years. During the interval between surveys, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, at appropriate times, carry out supplementary surveys of ancient and notable tree resources. In the work of surveys, supplementary surveys, and the identification of ancient and notable trees, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level may organize expert evaluations of ancient and notable trees.  
The State encourages entities and individuals to provide information on ancient and notable tree resources.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门根据古树名木资源普查、补充调查结果认定古树名木，报本级人民政府批准后依法公布。  
实行一级保护的古树和名木由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府古树名木主管部门认定，实行二级保护、三级保护的古树分别由设区的市级、县级人民政府古树名木主管部门认定。  
对在一定区域内集中生长形成的古树群体，可以实行整体保护，并按照涉及古树名木的最高保护等级，由相应的地方人民政府古树名木主管部门认定为古树群，报本级人民政府批准后依法公布。  
古树名木的认定和公布实行动态管理。  
古树名木认定和分级保护管理的具体办法由国务院古树名木主管部门规定。

**Article 10** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the results of the surveys and supplementary surveys of ancient and notable tree resources, identify ancient and notable trees and, after reporting to and being approved by the people's government at the same level, announce them according to law.  
Ancient trees subject to Grade I protection and notable trees shall be identified by the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; ancient trees subject to Grade II protection and Grade III protection shall be identified respectively by the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government of a city divided into districts and of the people's government at the county level.  
For groups of ancient trees formed by concentrated growth in a certain area, overall protection may be implemented, and in accordance with the highest protection grade of the ancient and notable trees involved, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the corresponding local people's government shall identify them as ancient tree groups and, after reporting to and being approved by the people's government at the same level, announce them according to law.  
The identification and announcement of ancient and notable trees shall be subject to dynamic management.  
The specific measures for the identification and graded protection management of ancient and notable trees shall be prescribed by the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门应当建立古树名木档案，并按照有关规范和要求，对古树名木设立保护标志，建设相应的保护设施，采取相应的保护措施。  
县级人民政府应当按照国务院古树名木主管部门的规定，划定古树名木保护范围，并依法公布。  
古树名木保护范围划定后，应当及时纳入国土空间规划实施监督信息系统。  
因生态保护等公共利益需要，造成古树名木相关权利人合法权益受到损害的，县级以上地方人民政府应当依法给予补偿；涉及属于集体所有、私人所有的古树名木的，县级以上地方人民政府可以采取措施合理照顾有关单位、个人的利益。

**Article 11** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall establish archives for ancient and notable trees and, in accordance with relevant specifications and requirements, set up protection signs for ancient and notable trees, construct corresponding protection facilities, and take corresponding protection measures.  
The people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council, delimit the protection scope of ancient and notable trees and announce it according to law.  
After the protection scope of ancient and notable trees is delimited, it shall be incorporated into the supervision information system for the implementation of territorial space planning in a timely manner.  
Where the legitimate rights and interests of the relevant right holders of ancient and notable trees are damaged due to the needs of public interests such as ecological protection, the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide compensation according to law; where ancient and notable trees owned by collectives or individuals are involved, the local people's government at or above the county level may take measures to reasonably take into account the interests of the relevant entities and individuals.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级人民政府古树名木主管部门应当按照有利于加强古树名木日常养护的原则，明确有关单位、个人作为古树名木的日常养护责任人，与日常养护责任人签订日常养护协议，根据古树名木权属情况、保护等级、养护状况、养护费用等，明确双方权利义务。  
对在古树名木保护管理工作中作出突出贡献的单位、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 12** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the principle of facilitating the strengthening of daily maintenance of ancient and notable trees, designate the relevant entities and individuals as the daily maintenance duty holders of ancient and notable trees, sign daily maintenance agreements with the daily maintenance duty holders, and specify the rights and obligations of both parties based on the ownership, protection grade, maintenance condition, maintenance costs, etc., of the ancient and notable trees.  
Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection and management of ancient and notable trees shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级人民政府古树名木主管部门应当加强对日常养护责任人的技术指导和培训，提高日常养护责任人的科学养护能力。日常养护责任人应当严格履行养护义务，对损害古树名木及其生长环境的行为应当予以制止。  
日常养护责任人发现古树名木遭受损害或者出现生长异常等情形的，应当及时报告县级人民政府古树名木主管部门，县级人民政府古树名木主管部门应当及时组织现场调查，并对所涉古树名木采取救治、复壮等措施。

**Article 13** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level shall strengthen technical guidance and training for daily maintenance duty holders to improve their scientific maintenance capabilities. Daily maintenance duty holders shall strictly perform their maintenance obligations and shall stop any act that damages ancient and notable trees or their growing environment.  
Where a daily maintenance duty holder discovers that an ancient or notable tree has been damaged or has abnormal growth, he or she shall promptly report to the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level, and the said department shall promptly organize an on-site investigation and take measures such as treatment and rejuvenation for the ancient or notable tree concerned.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　禁止采伐古树名木。  
因重大植物疫情防控、抢险救灾等特殊紧急情形，依法采取应急处置措施采伐古树名木的，县级人民政府有关部门应当及时通知县级人民政府古树名木主管部门。县级人民政府古树名木主管部门应当在应急处置工作结束后30日内将有关情况逐级报告至省、自治区、直辖市人民政府古树名木主管部门。涉及实行一级保护的古树和名木的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府古树名木主管部门应当报告国务院古树名木主管部门。

**Article 14** It shall be prohibited to fell ancient or notable trees.  
Where, due to special emergency circumstances such as major plant epidemic prevention and control, emergency rescue and disaster relief, emergency response measures are taken according to law to fell ancient or notable trees, the relevant department of the people's government at the county level shall promptly notify the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level. The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level shall, within 30 days after the conclusion of the emergency response work, report the relevant information level by level to the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. Where ancient trees subject to Grade I protection or notable trees are involved, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall report to the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　古树名木原则上实行原地保护，不得移植。有下列情形之一，确需移植古树名木的，建设单位应当提出移植申请并提交相应的移植和养护方案，按照涉及古树名木的最高保护等级，经认定该古树名木的县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门审核并报本级人民政府批准：  
（一）国家能源、交通、水利、军事设施等重点建设项目选址确实无法避让古树名木；  
（二）省、自治区、直辖市能源、交通、水利等重点建设项目选址确实无法避让实行二级保护、三级保护的古树；  
（三）其他基础设施建设项目选址确实无法避让实行三级保护的古树。  
古树名木的生长状况可能危害公众生命安全，采取修剪、支撑等防护措施仍无法消除安全隐患的，由县级人民政府古树名木主管部门提出相应的移植和养护方案，按照管理权限报有关人民政府批准。

**Article 15** Ancient and notable trees shall, in principle, be protected in situ and shall not be transplanted. Where, under any of the following circumstances, it is indeed necessary to transplant ancient or notable trees, the construction entity shall submit an application for transplantation and a corresponding transplantation and maintenance plan, and, according to the highest protection grade of the ancient or notable trees involved, the said application and plan shall be examined by the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level that identified the ancient or notable trees and submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval:  
(1) The site selection for key construction projects such as national energy, transportation, water conservancy, or military facilities cannot really avoid ancient or notable trees;  
(2) The site selection for key construction projects such as energy, transportation, or water conservancy of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government cannot really avoid ancient trees subject to Grade II or Grade III protection;  
(3) The site selection for other infrastructure construction projects cannot really avoid ancient trees subject to Grade III protection.  
Where the growth condition of an ancient or notable tree may endanger public safety and the safety hazard cannot be eliminated even after taking protective measures such as pruning or bracing, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at the county level shall propose a corresponding transplantation and maintenance plan and, in accordance with the administrative authority, submit it to the relevant people's government for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　移植古树名木应当就近实施。  
古树名木移植，属于[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第一款规定情形的，移植费用和移植后5年内的养护费用由建设单位承担；属于[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第二款规定情形的，移植费用和移植后5年内的养护费用由有关县级人民政府承担。

**Article 16** The transplantation of ancient or notable trees shall be carried out nearby.  
If the transplantation of an ancient or notable tree falls under the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 15 of these Regulations, the transplantation costs and the maintenance costs within five years after transplantation shall be borne by the construction entity; if it falls under the circumstances specified in the second paragraph of Article 15 of these Regulations, the transplantation costs and the maintenance costs within five years after transplantation shall be borne by the relevant people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　任何单位、个人不得实施下列损害古树名木及其生长环境的行为：  
（一）买卖、运输、加工非法采伐、移植的古树名木；  
（二）挖根、剥损树皮、过度修剪枝干；  
（三）向古树名木灌注有毒有害物质；  
（四）在古树名木保护范围内铺设非通透性硬化地面、使用明火、堆放重物、倾倒易燃易爆物品或者有毒有害物质；  
（五）在古树名木上刻划、架设线缆、缠绕或者悬挂物体等，攀爬古树名木；  
（六）破坏古树名木保护设施、保护标志；  
（七）其他损害古树名木及其生长环境的行为。

**Article 17** No entity or individual may commit any of the following acts that damage ancient or notable trees or their growing environment:  
(1) Buying, selling, transporting, or processing illegally felled or transplanted ancient or notable trees;  
(2) Digging roots, stripping bark, or excessively pruning branches;  
(3) Pouring toxic or harmful substances into ancient or notable trees;  
(4) Laying impermeable hardened ground, using open flames, piling heavy objects, dumping flammable or explosive articles, or toxic or harmful substances within the protection scope of ancient or notable trees;  
(5) Carving, installing cables, winding or hanging objects on ancient or notable trees, or climbing them;  
(6) Damaging the protection facilities or protection signs of ancient or notable trees;  
(7) Other acts that damage ancient or notable trees or their growing environment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　建设项目选址、建设，应当避开古树名木保护范围；因特殊情况不能避开，确需在古树名木保护范围内新建、扩建建筑物、构筑物或者铺设管线的，应当在施工前报告县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门，并按照要求采取必要的工程措施，尽可能减轻对古树名木及其生长环境的损害。  
古树名木保护范围内已有的建筑物、构筑物、管线等，不符合古树名木保护要求的，县级人民政府应当有计划地组织治理。  
古树名木保护与文物保护相关时，由县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门和文物主管部门共同制定保护措施。

**Article 18** The site selection and construction of construction projects shall avoid the protection scope of ancient and notable trees; where, due to special circumstances, it is impossible to avoid the said scope and it is indeed necessary to construct new buildings or structures or lay pipelines within the protection scope of ancient or notable trees, a report shall be made to the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level before construction, and necessary engineering measures shall be taken as required to minimize the damage to ancient or notable trees and their growing environment.  
Where existing buildings, structures, pipelines, etc., within the protection scope of ancient or notable trees do not meet the protection requirements for ancient or notable trees, the people's government at the county level shall organize the planned treatment thereof.  
Where the protection of ancient and notable trees is related to the protection of cultural relics, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level and the competent department of cultural relics shall jointly formulate protection measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在不损害古树名木及其生长环境的前提下，可以开展下列活动：  
（一）合理利用古树名木资源开展保护技术、遗传育种、生物学等科学研究，推广应用科研成果；  
（二）结合历史文化名镇名村、历史文化街区、传统村落以及传统节庆、民俗，挖掘古树名木的历史和文化价值；  
（三）开展科普宣教，促进古树名木资源与生态旅游融合发展；  
（四）古树名木属于传统经济树种的，相关权利人依法开展必要的生产经营活动。

**Article 19** The following activities may be carried out on the premise that no damage is caused to ancient or notable trees or their growing environment:  
(1) Rationally utilizing the resources of ancient and notable trees to conduct scientific research on protection technologies, genetic breeding, biology, etc., and promoting and applying the research results;  
(2) Combining with famous historical and cultural towns and villages, historical and cultural blocks, traditional villages, and traditional festivals and folk customs, to explore the historical and cultural value of ancient and notable trees;  
(3) Carrying out science popularization and education to promote the integrated development of ancient and notable tree resources and ecotourism;  
(4) Where ancient or notable trees are traditional economic tree species, the relevant right holders may carry out necessary production and operation activities according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　古树名木死亡的，由认定该古树名木的县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门组织确认、查明原因并提出处置意见报本级人民政府；具有原地保留价值的，予以保留，并根据实际情况采取必要的保护措施。

**Article 20** Where an ancient or notable tree dies, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level that identified the tree shall organize confirmation, ascertain the cause, and propose a disposal opinion to be reported to the people's government at the same level; if the tree has value in being retained in situ, it shall be retained, and necessary protection measures shall be taken according to the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门应当建立古树名木分级保护巡查制度，根据古树名木的保护等级、分布地域、长势情况等确定巡查周期，定期开展巡查。  
对位于偏远地区和交通不便地区的古树名木，县级以上人民政府古树名木主管部门可以利用大数据、卫星遥感、无人机等技术手段开展动态监控。  
国家鼓励单位、个人举报、制止损害古树名木及其生长环境的行为。

**Article 21** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall establish a graded protection inspection system for ancient and notable trees, determine the inspection cycle based on the protection grade, distribution area, growth condition, etc., of ancient and notable trees, and carry out regular inspections.  
For ancient and notable trees located in remote areas or areas with inconvenient transportation, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at or above the county level may use technical means such as big data, satellite remote sensing, and drones to carry out dynamic monitoring.  
The State encourages entities and individuals to report or stop acts that damage ancient or notable trees or their growing environment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院古树名木主管部门应当建立古树名木保护管理信息平台。  
县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门应当及时将本行政区域内的古树名木数据信息归集至古树名木保护管理信息平台。

**Article 22** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council shall establish an information platform for the protection and management of ancient and notable trees.  
The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in a timely manner, collect the data and information on ancient and notable trees within its administrative region into the information platform for the protection and management of ancient and notable trees.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府古树名木主管部门依照本条例规定，按照职责分工对古树名木保护管理等活动进行监督检查，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）询问被检查单位、个人，要求其对与监督检查事项有关的情况作出说明；  
（二）进行现场检查；  
（三）查阅、复制有关文件、资料，对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存；  
（四）查封、扣押涉嫌违法活动的场所、设施或者财物。  
县级以上人民政府古树名木主管部门依法进行监督检查，有关单位、个人应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 23** The competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with these Regulations and within its respective functions and duties, conduct supervision and inspection over activities such as the protection and management of ancient and notable trees, and shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) Inquiring the entities and individuals under inspection, requiring them to make explanations on matters related to the supervision and inspection;  
(2) Conducting on-site inspections;  
(3) Consulting and copying relevant documents and materials, and sealing up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, concealed, or tampered with;  
(4) Seizing or impounding places, facilities, or property suspected of being used in illegal activities.  
Where the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the people's government at or above the county level conducts supervision and inspection according to law, the relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct such inspection.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对古树名木保护管理工作不力、问题突出的地区，国务院古树名木主管部门可以约谈有关地方人民政府及其有关部门主要负责人，要求其采取措施及时整改。  
古树名木保护管理情况，应当纳入领导干部自然资源资产离任审计。

**Article 24** For regions where the protection and management of ancient and notable trees are ineffective and problems are prominent, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council may hold talks with the principal responsible persons of the relevant local people's governments and their relevant departments, requiring them to take measures to rectify the situation in a timely manner.  
The protection and management of ancient and notable trees shall be included in the audit of natural resource assets of leading officials upon their departure from office.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院古树名木主管部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其古树名木主管部门、其他有关部门的工作人员在古树名木保护管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 25** Where any staff member of the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the State Council, the local people's government at or above the county level and its competent department of ancient and notable trees, or other relevant departments abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the protection and management of ancient and notable trees, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，采伐、移植古树名木的，由县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门责令停止违法行为、采取补救措施，没收违法所得，并依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）涉及实行一级保护的古树和名木的，处每株50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；  
（二）涉及实行二级保护的古树的，处每株20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；  
（三）涉及实行三级保护的古树的，处每株10万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例规定，损害古树名木及其生长环境，造成古树名木死亡的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 26** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, fells or transplants an ancient or notable tree, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, take remedial measures, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine according to the following provisions:  
(1) If ancient trees subject to Grade I protection or notable trees are involved, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan per tree;  
(2) If ancient trees subject to Grade II protection are involved, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan per tree;  
(3) If ancient trees subject to Grade III protection are involved, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan per tree.  
Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, damages an ancient or notable tree or its growing environment, thereby causing the death of the ancient or notable tree, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　有[本条例第十七条](#art-17)第一项、第二项、第三项、第四项规定行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门责令停止违法行为、采取补救措施，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不停止违法行为、不采取补救措施或者造成严重后果的，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。  
有[本条例第十七条](#art-17)第五项、第六项、第七项规定行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门责令停止违法行为、采取补救措施，没收违法所得，可以并处1万元以下的罚款；拒不停止违法行为、不采取补救措施或者造成严重后果的，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where any act specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), or (4) of Article 17 of these Regulations is committed, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, take remedial measures, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the illegal act is not ceased, remedial measures are not taken, or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.  
Where any act specified in subparagraphs (5), (6), or (7) of Article 17 of these Regulations is committed, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, take remedial measures, confiscate the illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the illegal act is not ceased, remedial measures are not taken, or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)第一款规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府古树名木主管部门责令停止违法行为、限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，没收违法所得；拒不停止违法行为、逾期不恢复原状、不采取其他补救措施或者造成严重后果的，依照下列规定处以罚款：  
（一）涉及实行一级保护的古树和名木的，处每株20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；  
（二）涉及实行二级保护的古树的，处每株10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；  
（三）涉及实行三级保护的古树的，处每株5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 28** Where anyone violates the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 18 of these Regulations, the competent department of ancient and notable trees of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, restore the original state within a specified time limit or take other remedial measures, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal act is not ceased, the original state is not restored within the time limit, other remedial measures are not taken, or serious consequences are caused, a fine shall be imposed according to the following provisions:  
(1) If ancient trees subject to Grade I protection or notable trees are involved, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan per tree;  
(2) If ancient trees subject to Grade II protection are involved, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan per tree;  
(3) If ancient trees subject to Grade III protection are involved, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan per tree.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, causes loss to another person, he or she shall bear compensation liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2025年3月15日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 15, 2025.
