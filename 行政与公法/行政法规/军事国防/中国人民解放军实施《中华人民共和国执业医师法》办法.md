# 中国人民解放军实施《中华人民共和国执业医师法》办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2000-09-14  
**生效日期**：2000-09-14

> (2000年9月14日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第290号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国执业医师法》(以下简称《执业医师法》)的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　军队的医师工作由中国人民解放军总政治部、总后勤部主管。 军队各级政治机关、后勤(联勤)机关按照职责分工，负责本级医师的管理工作。 本办法所称医师，包括执业医师和执业助理医师。 第三条　军队人员具有《执业医师法》第九条、第十条规定的条件的，可以参加医师资格考试。 军队参加医师资格考试的人员，应当在规定的时间内向所在单位报名，填写军队人员医师资格考试报名表。经军队团级以上单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构医务部门和政治部门审核符合条件的，由团级以上单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构的医务部门，到所在地县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门集体办理报名手续，并组织参加医师资格考试。 军队参加医师资格考试人员的实践技能考试，由总部、军兵种、军区以及其他相当等级的单位(以下简称军区级单位)政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门组织实施。 第四条　军队人员的医师资格考试成绩和有关考试信息由国务院卫生行政部门通报总后勤部卫生部。 总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部依据国务院卫生行政部门确定的医师资格考试合格分数线和军队人员实践技能考试成绩，确定军队参加医师资格考试成绩合格的人员名单，并通知参加考试人员所在的军区级单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门。 军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门依据总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部通知的名单，为医师资格考试成绩合格的军队人员核发国务院卫生行政部门规定的医师资格证书。 第五条　取得医师资格的军队医师，可以向所在军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门申请医师执业注册。 申请医师执业注册的军队医师应当填写军队医师执业注册申请表，由团级以上单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构的医务部门逐级上报至军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门，由军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门共同审核。 军队医疗、预防、保健机构可以为本机构中的医师集体办理注册手续。 除有《执业医师法》第十五条规定的情形外，军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内准予注册，并由军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门发给总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部统一印制的军队医师执业证书。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国执业医师法》(以下简称《执业医师法》)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Licensed Doctors (hereinafter referred to as the Licensed Doctors Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　军队的医师工作由中国人民解放军总政治部、总后勤部主管。  
军队各级政治机关、后勤(联勤)机关按照职责分工，负责本级医师的管理工作。  
本办法所称医师，包括执业医师和执业助理医师。

**Article 2** The work of doctors in the military shall be under the charge of the General Political Department and the General Logistics Department of the People's Liberation Army.  
The political organs and logistics (joint logistics) organs at all levels of the military shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the administration of doctors at their corresponding levels.  
The term "doctors" as used in these Measures includes licensed doctors and licensed assistant doctors.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军队人员具有《执业医师法》第九条、第十条规定的条件的，可以参加医师资格考试。  
军队参加医师资格考试的人员，应当在规定的时间内向所在单位报名，填写军队人员医师资格考试报名表。经军队团级以上单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构医务部门和政治部门审核符合条件的，由团级以上单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构的医务部门，到所在地县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门集体办理报名手续，并组织参加医师资格考试。  
军队参加医师资格考试人员的实践技能考试，由总部、军兵种、军区以及其他相当等级的单位(以下简称军区级单位)政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门组织实施。

**Article 3** Military personnel who meet the conditions specified in Articles 9 and 10 of the Licensed Doctors Law may take the Physician Qualification Examination.  
Military personnel who take the Physician Qualification Examination shall register with their units within the prescribed time limit and fill in the registration form for the Physician Qualification Examination for military personnel. After examination and verification by the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments at or above the regiment level, or by the medical affairs departments and political departments of medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions at or above the regiment level, and if they are found to meet the conditions, the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments at or above the regiment level or the medical affairs departments of medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions at or above the regiment level shall collectively handle the registration procedures with the health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level in the locality, and organize them to take the Physician Qualification Examination.  
The practical skills examination for military personnel taking the Physician Qualification Examination shall be organized and implemented by the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of the headquarters, branches of the armed forces, military regions, and other units of equivalent level (hereinafter referred to as military region-level units).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　军队人员的医师资格考试成绩和有关考试信息由国务院卫生行政部门通报总后勤部卫生部。  
总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部依据国务院卫生行政部门确定的医师资格考试合格分数线和军队人员实践技能考试成绩，确定军队参加医师资格考试成绩合格的人员名单，并通知参加考试人员所在的军区级单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门。  
军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门依据总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部通知的名单，为医师资格考试成绩合格的军队人员核发国务院卫生行政部门规定的医师资格证书。

**Article 4** The examination results and relevant examination information of military personnel for the Physician Qualification Examination shall be notified by the health administrative department of the State Council to the Health Department of the General Logistics Department.  
The Cadre Department of the General Political Department and the Health Department of the General Logistics Department shall, based on the passing score line for the Physician Qualification Examination determined by the health administrative department of the State Council and the practical skills examination results of military personnel, determine the list of military personnel who have passed the Physician Qualification Examination, and notify the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of the military region-level units to which the examinees belong.  
The logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units shall, based on the list notified by the Cadre Department of the General Political Department and the Health Department of the General Logistics Department, issue the Physician Qualification Certificates specified by the health administrative department of the State Council to the military personnel who have passed the Physician Qualification Examination.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　取得医师资格的军队医师，可以向所在军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门申请医师执业注册。  
申请医师执业注册的军队医师应当填写军队医师执业注册申请表，由团级以上单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构的医务部门逐级上报至军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门，由军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门共同审核。  
军队医疗、预防、保健机构可以为本机构中的医师集体办理注册手续。  
除有《执业医师法》第十五条规定的情形外，军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内准予注册，并由军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门发给总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部统一印制的军队医师执业证书。  
经审核不符合注册条件不予注册的，受理注册的军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门应当书面通知申请人所在单位的后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构的医务部门，并说明理由。

**Article 5** Military doctors who have obtained the qualification as doctors may apply to the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of the military region-level units to which they belong for registration of medical practice.  
Military doctors applying for registration of medical practice shall fill in the application form for registration of medical practice for military doctors, which shall be reported level by level by the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments at or above the regiment level or the medical affairs departments of medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions at or above the regiment level to the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units, and shall be jointly examined and verified by the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of the military region-level units.  
Military medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions may collectively handle the registration procedures for doctors within their institutions.  
Except for the circumstances specified in Article 15 of the Licensed Doctors Law, the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units shall approve the registration within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, and the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units shall issue the Military Doctor Practice Certificate uniformly printed by the Cadre Department of the General Political Department and the Health Department of the General Logistics Department.  
If the registration is not approved because the conditions are not met after examination, the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the military region-level unit that accepted the registration shall notify in writing the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the unit to which the applicant belongs or the medical affairs department of the medical treatment, prevention, or health care institution at or above the regiment level, and shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　未经执业注册取得军队医师执业证书的，不得在军队从事医师执业活动。  
军队有任免权的单位对未取得军队医师执业证书的人员，不得任命卫生专业技术职务。

**Article 6** No person who has not obtained a Military Doctor Practice Certificate through registration of medical practice may engage in medical practice activities in the military.  
Units with appointment and removal authority in the military shall not appoint any person who has not obtained a Military Doctor Practice Certificate to a health professional technical post.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军队医师注册后有《执业医师法》第十六条所列情形之一或者下列情形之一的，所在单位应当在30日内报告准予注册的机关，由该机关注销注册并收回军队医师执业证书：  
(一)受开除军籍或者除名处分的；  
(二)转业、复员以及离休、退休后移交地方人民政府安置，不再继续从事医疗、预防、保健业务的；  
(三)有军队规定不宜从事医疗、预防、保健业务的其他情形的。

**Article 7** Where a military doctor, after registration, falls under any of the circumstances listed in Article 16 of the Licensed Doctors Law or any of the following circumstances, the unit to which he belongs shall, within 30 days, report to the authority that approved the registration, and such authority shall cancel the registration and retrieve the Military Doctor Practice Certificate:  
(1) Being punished by expulsion from military service or removal from the military roster;  
(2) Being transferred to civilian work, demobilized, or after retirement or honorable retirement, being placed by the local people's government, and no longer continuing to engage in medical treatment, prevention, or health care services;  
(3) Having other circumstances as prescribed by the military that render him unsuitable to engage in medical treatment, prevention, or health care services.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军队医师在军区级单位内变动执业类别和执业范围的，应当到准予注册的机关办理变更注册手续。  
军队医师在军区级单位之间变动执业地点、执业类别和执业范围的，应当持原准予注册机关出具的证明，在新的单位依照[本办法第五条](#art-5)的规定申请办理变更注册手续。

**Article 8** Where a military doctor changes his category of practice or scope of practice within a military region-level unit, he shall go through the change of registration procedures with the authority that approved the registration.  
Where a military doctor changes his place of practice, category of practice, or scope of practice between military region-level units, he shall, with the certificate issued by the original authority that approved the registration, apply for change of registration procedures at the new unit in accordance with Article 5 of these Measures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军队医师转业、复员以及离休、退休后由地方人民政府安置并继续从事医疗、预防、保健业务的，应当交回军队医师执业证书，并持军队原准予注册的机关出具的证明，到所在地县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门申请办理变更注册手续，领取医师执业证书。

**Article 9** Where a military doctor, after being transferred to civilian work, demobilized, or retired or honorably retired, is placed by the local people's government and continues to engage in medical treatment, prevention, or health care services, he shall return the Military Doctor Practice Certificate, and with the certificate issued by the original military authority that approved the registration, apply for change of registration procedures with the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level in the locality, and receive a Doctor Practice Certificate.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方医师入伍后继续从事医疗、预防、保健业务的，应当交回医师执业证书，并持地方原准予注册的机关出具的证明，到所在军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门申请办理变更注册手续，领取军队医师执业证书。

**Article 10** Where a local doctor, after joining the military, continues to engage in medical treatment, prevention, or health care services, he shall return the Doctor Practice Certificate, and with the certificate issued by the original local authority that approved the registration, apply for change of registration procedures with the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the military region-level unit to which he belongs, and receive a Military Doctor Practice Certificate.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军区级单位政治机关干部部门和后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门应当于每年十二月底以前向总政治部干部部、总后勤部卫生部报告当年军队医师准予注册、注销注册和变更注册的有关情况。

**Article 11** The political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units shall, before the end of December each year, report to the Cadre Department of the General Political Department and the Health Department of the General Logistics Department the relevant information on the approvals of registration, cancellations of registration, and changes of registration of military doctors for that year.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军队医师除应当履行《执业医师法》第二十二条规定的义务外，还应当履行下列义务：  
(一)服从命令，听从指挥；  
(二)履行军队医师职责，尽心尽责地为部队服务，为伤病员服务；  
(三)学习平时、战时卫生勤务和卫生防疫知识，提高战伤救治技术水平，完成平时、战时卫生勤务保障任务；  
(四)指导部队开展战伤救治训练，对军队人员进行健康教育。

**Article 12** In addition to fulfilling the obligations specified in Article 22 of the Licensed Doctors Law, military doctors shall also fulfill the following obligations:  
(1) Obey orders and follow directions;  
(2) Fulfill the duties of a military doctor, and serve the troops and the sick and wounded with utmost dedication and responsibility;  
(3) Study peacetime and wartime health service and health epidemic prevention knowledge, improve technical levels of war wound treatment, and complete peacetime and wartime health service support tasks;  
(4) Guide the troops in conducting war wound treatment training and provide health education to military personnel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军队医师不得有下列行为：  
(一)拒诊、拒治伤病员；  
(二)向伤病员或者其家属推销药品、医疗保健器械；  
(三)开展以牟利为目的的私人诊疗活动；  
(四)以军队医师身份做医疗广告；  
(五)损害军队形象或者伤病员利益的其他行为。

**Article 13** Military doctors shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) Refusing to diagnose or treat the sick and wounded;  
(2) Promoting drugs or medical health care devices to the sick and wounded or their family members;  
(3) Carrying out private diagnosis and treatment activities for profit-making purposes;  
(4) Making medical advertisements using their identity as military doctors;  
(5) Other acts that harm the image of the military or the interests of the sick and wounded.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军队医师的考核和培训由军区级单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门，按照《执业医师法》和军队的有关规定组织实施。

**Article 14** The assessment and training of military doctors shall be organized and implemented by the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units in accordance with the Licensed Doctors Law and the relevant provisions of the military.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军队医师有下列情形之一的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予表彰或者奖励：  
(一)在执业活动中医德高尚，为部队服务、为伤病员服务，事迹突出的；  
(二)热爱临床工作，医术精湛，完成医疗救治任务，成绩突出的；  
(三)在战场救护中不怕流血牺牲，完成任务出色的；  
(四)遇有自然灾害、传染病流行、突发重大伤亡事故及其他严重威胁军队人员和人民生命健康的紧急情况时，救死扶伤表现突出的；  
(五)长期在边远、艰苦的基层单位或者条件艰苦的岗位努力工作，做出显著贡献的；  
(六)对医学科学技术研究有重大突破的；  
(七)有国家和军队规定应当予以表彰或者奖励的其他情形的。

**Article 15** Where a military doctor falls under any of the following circumstances, he shall be commended or rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army:  
(1) Having noble medical ethics in practice activities, serving the troops and the sick and wounded, with outstanding deeds;  
(2) Being devoted to clinical work, having excellent medical skills, completing medical treatment tasks with outstanding achievements;  
(3) Being unafraid of bloodshed and sacrifice in battlefield rescue, and completing tasks excellently;  
(4) When encountering natural disasters, epidemics of infectious diseases, sudden major casualty accidents, and other emergency situations that seriously threaten the lives and health of military personnel and the people, demonstrating outstanding performance in saving lives and healing the wounded;  
(5) Having worked hard for a long time in remote, difficult grassroots units or in positions with harsh conditions, making significant contributions;  
(6) Having made major breakthroughs in medical science and technology research;  
(7) Having other circumstances that shall be commended or rewarded as prescribed by the State and the military.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　军队人员以不正当手段取得医师执业证书的，由军队发给证书的部门予以吊销；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分。

**Article 16** Where a military person obtains a Doctor Practice Certificate by improper means, the department that issued the certificate shall revoke it; the supervisory personnel directly responsible and other directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军队医师有《执业医师法》第三十七条所列行为之一或者下列行为之一的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分，军队团级以上单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门并可以责令暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动；情节严重的，吊销执业证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒诊、拒治伤病员的；  
(二)利用职务之便，索取、收受伤病员财物，向伤病员或者其家属推销药品和医疗保健器械，以及牟取其他不正当利益的；  
(三)擅自开展行医活动，利用媒体做医疗广告，造成恶劣影响的；  
(四)发生自然灾害、传染病流行、突发重大伤亡事故及其他严重威胁军队人员和人民生命健康的紧急情况时，不服从命令的；  
(五)违反国家和军队规定，给伤病员造成其他严重损害的。  
军队医师在医疗、预防、保健工作中造成事故的，依照法律或者国家和军队的有关规定处理。

**Article 17** Where a military doctor commits any of the acts listed in Article 37 of the Licensed Doctors Law or any of the following acts, he shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, and the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the military unit at or above the regiment level may order him to suspend practice for not less than six months but not more than one year; if the circumstances are serious, his practice certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing to diagnose or treat the sick and wounded;  
(2) Taking advantage of his position to extort or accept property from the sick and wounded, promoting drugs or medical health care devices to the sick and wounded or their family members, or seeking other improper benefits;  
(3) Carrying out medical practice activities without authorization, or making medical advertisements through the media, causing adverse effects;  
(4) When natural disasters, epidemics of infectious diseases, sudden major casualty accidents, or other emergency situations that seriously threaten the lives and health of military personnel and the people occur, disobeying orders;  
(5) Violating State or military regulations and causing other serious harm to the sick and wounded.  
Where a military doctor causes an accident in the course of medical treatment, prevention, or health care work, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the law or the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军队人员非医师行医的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，给予处分；给患者造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where a military person practices medicine without being a doctor, he shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army; if damage is caused to a patient, he shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　1998年6月26日《执业医师法》公布前按照国家和军队有关规定取得卫生专业技术职务的军队人员，由所在团级以上单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门或者团级以上医疗、预防、保健机构医务部门和政治部门报军区级单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门认定，取得相应的医师资格。其中仍在军队医疗、预防、保健机构或者岗位从事医疗、预防、保健业务的医师，依照《执业医师法》和本办法的有关规定，由所在团级单位集体核报军区级单位政治机关干部部门、后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门，予以注册并发给军队医师执业证书。

**Article 19** For military personnel who obtained health professional technical titles in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military before the promulgation of the Licensed Doctors Law on June 26, 1998, their units' political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments at or above the regiment level, or the medical affairs departments and political departments of medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions at or above the regiment level, shall report to the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units for confirmation, and they shall obtain the corresponding doctor qualifications. Among them, doctors who are still engaged in medical treatment, prevention, or health care services in military medical treatment, prevention, or health care institutions or posts, shall, in accordance with the Licensed Doctors Law and the relevant provisions of these Measures, be collectively reported and verified by the regiment-level units to which they belong to the political organs' cadre departments and logistics (joint logistics) organs' health departments of military region-level units, and shall be registered and issued with Military Doctor Practice Certificates.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在军队基层单位从事预防、保健和一般医疗服务的卫生员，由总后勤部另行制定管理办法。

**Article 20** Administrative measures for health workers who engage in prevention, health care, and general medical services in grassroots units of the military shall be separately formulated by the General Logistics Department.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军队医疗、预防、保健机构聘用地方医师，必须经军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门审核。所聘用人员必须依法取得医师资格，经聘用单位出具证明，到原执业注册的卫生行政部门办理变更执业注册手续，交回原医师执业证书，由军区级单位后勤(联勤)机关卫生部门办理执业注册手续。  
未经批准擅自聘用地方医师或者非医师行医的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的有关规定，对聘用单位负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予处分。所聘用的地方非医师由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门依法处罚；给患者造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where a military medical treatment, prevention, or health care institution employs a local doctor, it must obtain the examination and verification of the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the military region-level unit. The employed person must have obtained the doctor qualification according to law. With the certificate issued by the employing unit, he shall go through the change of practice registration procedures with the health administrative department of the original practice registration, return the original Doctor Practice Certificate, and the logistics (joint logistics) organs' health department of the military region-level unit shall handle the practice registration procedures.  
Where a local doctor is employed without approval, or a non-doctor practices medicine, the supervisory personnel directly responsible and other directly responsible personnel of the employing unit shall be given sanctions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The employed local non-doctor shall be punished by the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level according to law; if damage is caused to the patient, he shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　中国人民武装警察部队的医师工作适用本办法。

**Article 22** These Measures shall apply to the work of doctors of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本办法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
