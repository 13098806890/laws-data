# 军服管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-01-13  
**生效日期**：2009-03-01

> (2009年1月13日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第547号公布　自2009年3月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强军服管理，维护军服的专用性和严肃性，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称军服，是指中国人民解放军现行装备的制式服装及其标志服饰。 第三条　军服的制式由中央军事委员会批准。 军服由军队军需主管部门负责监制。 第四条　生产军服、军服专用材料的企业(以下称军服承制企业)应当具备生产军服、军服专用材料必需的条件和能力，具有质量保证体系和良好资信，并符合军队军需主管部门规定的其他条件。 申请生产军服、军服专用材料的企业，经军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构查验，具备前款规定条件的，列入军服承制企业备选名录。 军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构根据军服、军服专用材料生产任务，从军服承制企业备选名录中择优确定军服承制企业，与其签订军服、军服专用材料生产合同。 第五条　军服承制企业应当严格履行军服、军服专用材料生产合同，按照合同约定的时间、品种、数量完成军服、军服专用材料生产任务，执行军服生产技术规范。 军服承制企业不得转让军服、军服专用材料生产合同或者军服生产技术规范，也不得委托其他企业生产军服、军服专用材料。 军服承制企业的工作人员不得泄露军服专用材料生产技术，不得泄露军服、军服专用材料生产数量、接收单位等涉及国家秘密的信息。 第六条　军服、军服专用材料生产中的试制品，经军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构检验合格的，作为制成品接收；军服、军服专用材料生产中的残次品，未经改制、染色等处理的，不得销售或者以其他方式转让；军服生产中剩余的军服专用材料，应当按照军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构的要求，妥善保管或者移交。 第七条　承运军服、军服专用材料的企业，应当具备货物运输资质。军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构应当与其签订运输合同。承运企业应当严格履行运输合同，承运企业工作人员不得泄露运输的军服、军服专用材料的数量、接收单位等涉及国家秘密的信息。 第八条　军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构应当对军服承制企业生产军服、军服专用材料的情况进行检查，并向国务院工商行政管理部门通报军服、军服专用材料生产合同签订和履行的情况。 第九条　现役军人以及依照法律、法规和军队有关规定可以穿着军服的人员，应当依照有关规定穿着军服。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强军服管理，维护军服的专用性和严肃性，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of military uniforms and maintain the specificity and solemnity of military uniforms.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称军服，是指中国人民解放军现行装备的制式服装及其标志服饰。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "military uniforms" means the standard uniforms and their insignia currently in service with the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　军服的制式由中央军事委员会批准。  
军服由军队军需主管部门负责监制。

**Article 3** The designs of military uniforms shall be approved by the Central Military Commission.  
The military uniforms shall be manufactured under the supervision of the competent military logistics department.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产军服、军服专用材料的企业(以下称军服承制企业)应当具备生产军服、军服专用材料必需的条件和能力，具有质量保证体系和良好资信，并符合军队军需主管部门规定的其他条件。  
申请生产军服、军服专用材料的企业，经军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构查验，具备前款规定条件的，列入军服承制企业备选名录。  
军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构根据军服、军服专用材料生产任务，从军服承制企业备选名录中择优确定军服承制企业，与其签订军服、军服专用材料生产合同。

**Article 4** Enterprises that produce military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms (hereinafter referred to as military uniform contracting enterprises) shall possess the necessary conditions and capabilities for producing military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, have a quality assurance system and good credit standing, and meet other conditions prescribed by the competent military logistics department.  
Enterprises applying to produce military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, after being inspected by the competent military logistics department or its authorized institution and meeting the conditions prescribed in the preceding paragraph, shall be included in the alternative list of military uniform contracting enterprises.  
The competent military logistics department or its authorized institution shall, based on the production tasks of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, select the best military uniform contracting enterprises from the alternative list and sign production contracts for military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms with them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军服承制企业应当严格履行军服、军服专用材料生产合同，按照合同约定的时间、品种、数量完成军服、军服专用材料生产任务，执行军服生产技术规范。  
军服承制企业不得转让军服、军服专用材料生产合同或者军服生产技术规范，也不得委托其他企业生产军服、军服专用材料。  
军服承制企业的工作人员不得泄露军服专用材料生产技术，不得泄露军服、军服专用材料生产数量、接收单位等涉及国家秘密的信息。

**Article 5** A military uniform contracting enterprise shall strictly perform the production contract for military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, complete the production tasks of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms within the time, variety, and quantity stipulated in the contract, and implement the technical specifications for military uniform production.  
A military uniform contracting enterprise shall not transfer the production contract for military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms or the technical specifications for military uniform production, nor shall it entrust any other enterprise to produce military uniforms or special materials for military uniforms.  
The staff members of a military uniform contracting enterprise shall not disclose the production technology of special materials for military uniforms, nor shall they disclose information involving State secrets such as the production quantity of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, or the receiving units.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　军服、军服专用材料生产中的试制品，经军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构检验合格的，作为制成品接收；军服、军服专用材料生产中的残次品，未经改制、染色等处理的，不得销售或者以其他方式转让；军服生产中剩余的军服专用材料，应当按照军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构的要求，妥善保管或者移交。

**Article 6** Trial products in the production of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, after passing inspection by the competent military logistics department or its authorized institution, shall be accepted as finished products. Defective products in the production of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, unless reworked, dyed, or otherwise treated, shall not be sold or transferred by other means. The remaining special materials for military uniforms from the production of military uniforms shall be properly kept or handed over in accordance with the requirements of the competent military logistics department or its authorized institution.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承运军服、军服专用材料的企业，应当具备货物运输资质。军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构应当与其签订运输合同。承运企业应当严格履行运输合同，承运企业工作人员不得泄露运输的军服、军服专用材料的数量、接收单位等涉及国家秘密的信息。

**Article 7** Enterprises that transport military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms shall possess the qualifications for cargo transportation. The competent military logistics department or its authorized institution shall sign a transport contract with such enterprises. The transporting enterprise shall strictly perform the transport contract, and the staff members of the transporting enterprise shall not disclose information involving State secrets such as the quantity of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms being transported, or the receiving units.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军队军需主管部门或者其授权的机构应当对军服承制企业生产军服、军服专用材料的情况进行检查，并向国务院工商行政管理部门通报军服、军服专用材料生产合同签订和履行的情况。

**Article 8** The competent military logistics department or its authorized institution shall inspect the production of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms by military uniform contracting enterprises, and shall notify the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council of the signing and performance of production contracts for military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　现役军人以及依照法律、法规和军队有关规定可以穿着军服的人员，应当依照有关规定穿着军服。  
军队警备执勤人员应当加强检查、纠察，及时纠正违法穿着军服的行为。  
影视制作和文艺演出单位的演艺人员因扮演军人角色需要穿着军服的，应当遵守军队关于军服穿着的规定，不得损害军队和军人形象。非拍摄、演出时不得穿着军服。

**Article 9** Active-duty servicemen and other persons who may wear military uniforms in accordance with laws, regulations, and relevant military provisions shall wear military uniforms in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Personnel on military police duty shall strengthen inspections and patrols and promptly correct illegal wearing of military uniforms.  
Actors in film and television production and theatrical performance units who need to wear military uniforms for playing the role of servicemen shall comply with the military regulations on wearing military uniforms and shall not harm the image of the armed forces and servicemen. They shall not wear military uniforms when not filming or performing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　禁止买卖、出租或者擅自出借、赠送军服。  
禁止使用军服和中国人民解放军曾经装备的制式服装从事经营活动。  
禁止以“军需”、“军服”、“军品”等用语招揽顾客。

**Article 10** It is prohibited to buy, sell, rent, or without authorization lend or give military uniforms as gifts.  
It is prohibited to use military uniforms and standard uniforms that were once in service with the People's Liberation Army to engage in business activities.  
It is prohibited to solicit customers by using such terms as "military supplies," "military uniforms," or "military products."

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的制式服装及其标志服饰，应当与军服有明显区别。  
禁止生产、销售、购买和使用仿照军服样式、颜色制作的足以使公众视为军服的仿制品。

**Article 11** The standard uniforms and their insignia of state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other entities shall be clearly distinguishable from military uniforms.  
It is prohibited to produce, sell, purchase, or use imitations made in the style and color of military uniforms that are sufficient to cause the public to regard them as military uniforms.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由工商行政管理部门没收违法物品和违法所得，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；违法经营数额巨大的，吊销营业执照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)非法生产军服、军服专用材料的；  
(二)买卖军服、军服专用材料的；  
(三)生产、销售军服仿制品的。  
工商行政管理部门发现涉嫌非法生产、销售军服或者军服仿制品的行为时，可以查封、扣押涉嫌物品。

**Article 12** Any person who violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances shall be subject to confiscation of illegal articles and illegal gains by the administrative department for industry and commerce, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if the amount of illegal business is huge, the business license shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) illegally producing military uniforms or special materials for military uniforms;  
(2) buying or selling military uniforms or special materials for military uniforms;  
(3) producing or selling imitations of military uniforms.  
When the administrative department for industry and commerce discovers suspected illegal production or sale of military uniforms or imitations of military uniforms, it may seal up or detain the suspected articles.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军服承制企业违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿：  
(一)转让军服、军服专用材料生产合同或者生产技术规范，或者委托其他企业生产军服、军服专用材料的；  
(二)销售或者以其他方式转让未经改制、染色等处理的军服、军服专用材料残次品的；  
(三)未将军服生产中剩余的军服专用材料妥善保管、移交的。  
具有前款规定情形之一的，军队军需主管部门应当将其从军服承制企业备选名录中除名，并不得再列入军服承制企业备选名录。

**Article 13** If a military uniform contracting enterprise violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification:  
(1) transferring the production contract or production technology specifications for military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms, or entrusting other enterprises to produce military uniforms or special materials for military uniforms;  
(2) selling or transferring by other means defective products of military uniforms or special materials for military uniforms that have not been reworked, dyed, or otherwise treated;  
(3) failing to properly keep or hand over the remaining special materials for military uniforms from the production of military uniforms.  
If any of the circumstances provided for in the preceding paragraph occurs, the competent military logistics department shall remove such enterprise from the alternative list of military uniform contracting enterprises and shall not include it again in such list.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军服承制企业的工作人员泄露军服专用材料生产技术，或者军服承制、承运企业的工作人员泄露军服、军服专用材料生产、运输数量以及接收单位等涉及国家秘密的信息，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** If a staff member of a military uniform contracting enterprise discloses the production technology of special materials for military uniforms, or if a staff member of a military uniform contracting or transporting enterprise discloses information involving State secrets such as the production or transportation quantity of military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms or the receiving units, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反本条例规定，使用军服和中国人民解放军曾经装备的制式服装从事经营活动，或者以“军需”、“军服”、“军品”等用语招揽顾客的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法物品和违法所得，并处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 15** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, uses military uniforms and standard uniforms that were once in service with the People's Liberation Army to engage in business activities, or solicits customers by using such terms as "military supplies," "military uniforms," or "military products," shall be ordered to make corrections by the administrative department for industry and commerce, with his illegal articles and illegal gains confiscated, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan imposed; if he refuses to make corrections, he shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　穿着军服或者军服仿制品冒充军人招摇撞骗的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 16** Any person who wears military uniforms or imitations of military uniforms to impersonate a serviceman for swindling and cheating shall be subject to public security administration penalties by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公务员和现役军人在军服管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
现役军人出租或者擅自出借、赠送军服的，依照《中国人民解放军纪律条令》的规定给予处分。

**Article 17** Any public servant or active-duty serviceman who abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of military uniform management shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
An active-duty serviceman who rents out or without authorization lends or gives military uniforms as gifts shall be given sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the Discipline Regulations of the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对公安机关、工商行政管理部门查获的军服仿制品的认定存在争议的，由省军区(卫戍区、警备区)或者军分区(警备区)军需主管部门鉴定。  
公安机关、工商行政管理部门依法没收的军服、军服专用材料，应当移交省军区(卫戍区、警备区)或者军分区(警备区)军需主管部门；依法没收的军服仿制品，应当按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 18** If there is a dispute over the identification of imitations of military uniforms seized by the public security organs or the administrative departments for industry and commerce, the competent logistics departments of the provincial military commands (garrison commands or garrison forces) or military sub-commands (garrison forces) shall conduct an expert evaluation.  
The military uniforms and special materials for military uniforms lawfully confiscated by the public security organs or the administrative departments for industry and commerce shall be handed over to the competent logistics departments of the provincial military commands (garrison commands or garrison forces) or military sub-commands (garrison forces). The imitations of military uniforms lawfully confiscated shall be disposed of in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中国人民武装警察部队现行装备的制式服装及其标志服饰的管理，参照本条例执行。

**Article 19** The management of the standard uniforms and their insignia currently in service with the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be implemented by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本条例自2009年3月1日起施行。

**Article 20** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2009.
