### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范文职人员的管理，保障文职人员合法权益，建设德才兼备的高素质、专业化文职人员队伍，促进军事人员现代化建设，根据《中华人民共和国国防法》等有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the National Defense Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws for the purposes of regulating the administration of civilian personnel, protecting the lawful rights and interests of civilian personnel, building a contingent of civilian personnel with both integrity and professional competence and of high quality and specialty, and promoting the modernization of military personnel.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称文职人员，是指在军队编制岗位依法履行职责的非服兵役人员，是军队人员的组成部分，依法享有国家工作人员相应的权利、履行相应的义务。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, civilian personnel refer to personnel who are not on active service but perform duties according to law in authorized posts of the armed forces, and are a component of military personnel, enjoying the corresponding rights of State functionaries and performing corresponding obligations according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　文职人员管理坚持中国共产党的绝对领导，深入贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻军委主席负责制，落实新时代党的组织路线，坚持党管干部、党管人才，坚持人才是第一资源，坚持公开、平等、竞争、择优，依照法定的权限、条件、标准和程序进行。

**Article 3** The administration of civilian personnel shall adhere to the absolute leadership of the Communist Party of China, thoroughly implement Xi Jinping's thinking on strengthening the military, implement the system of the Chairman of the Central Military Commission assuming overall responsibility, implement the Party's organizational line for the new era, uphold the principle of the Party supervising officials and talent, adhere to the concept that talent is the primary resource, uphold the principles of openness, equality, competition, and merit selection, and be conducted in accordance with statutory authority, conditions, standards, and procedures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　文职人员主要编配在军民通用、非直接参与作战，且专业性、保障性、稳定性较强的岗位，按照岗位性质分为管理类文职人员、专业技术类文职人员、专业技能类文职人员。管理类文职人员和专业技术类文职人员是党的干部队伍的重要组成部分。

**Article 4** Civilian personnel are mainly assigned to posts that are both military and civilian in nature, not directly involved in combat, and with strong specialty, support, and stability. According to the nature of the posts, civilian personnel are classified into civilian personnel in management, civilian personnel in specialized technology, and civilian personnel in specialized skill. Civilian personnel in management and civilian personnel in specialized technology are an important component of the Party's cadre ranks.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军队建立与军人、公务员、事业单位工作人员相独立的文职人员政策制度体系。文职人员政策制度应当体现军事职业特点，构建完善的管理、保障机制。  
军队对文职人员实行分类分级管理，提高管理效能和科学化水平。

**Article 5** The armed forces shall establish a policy and institution system for civilian personnel that is independent of that for military personnel, civil servants, and personnel of public institutions. The policies and institutions for civilian personnel shall reflect the characteristics of military professions, and shall establish comprehensive management and safeguard mechanisms.  
The armed forces shall implement classified and hierarchical administration of civilian personnel to enhance administrative efficiency and scientific level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中央军事委员会统一领导全军文职人员管理工作，中央军事委员会政治工作部负责组织指导全军文职人员管理工作。团级以上单位的政治工作部门在党委领导下，负责本单位的文职人员管理工作。  
中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关、军队有关单位应当按照职责分工，做好文职人员的招录聘用、教育培训、户籍管理、社会保障、人力资源管理、抚恤优待、退休管理等工作，为文职人员提供公共服务和便利。

**Article 6** The Central Military Commission shall exercise unified leadership over the administration of civilian personnel throughout the armed forces, and the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the administration of civilian personnel throughout the armed forces. The political work departments of units at the regiment level and above, under the leadership of the Party committees, shall be responsible for the administration of civilian personnel in their respective units.  
The relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State, relevant local organs, and relevant military units shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly carry out the recruitment and employment, education and training, household registration management, social insurance, human resources management, pension and preferential treatment, and retirement management of civilian personnel, and provide public services and conveniences for civilian personnel.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家和军队依法保障文职人员享有与其身份属性、职业特点、职责使命和所作贡献相称的地位和权益，鼓励文职人员长期稳定地为国防和军队建设服务。  
军队有关单位会同中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关建立文职人员联合工作机制，协调做好跨军地文职人员管理有关工作。

**Article 7** The State and the armed forces shall, according to law, ensure that civilian personnel enjoy a status and rights commensurate with their identity attributes, occupational characteristics, duties and missions, and contributions, and encourage civilian personnel to serve national defense and military construction in a long-term and stable manner.  
Relevant military units shall, jointly with the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State and relevant local organs, establish a joint working mechanism for civilian personnel to coordinate the work related to the administration of civilian personnel across military and civilian spheres.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在文职人员管理工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the administration of civilian personnel shall be given commendations and awards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

### 第二章　基本条件、职责、义务和权利

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　文职人员应当具备下列基本条件：  
（一）具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）年满18周岁；  
（三）符合军队招录聘用文职人员的政治条件；  
（四）志愿服务国防和军队建设；  
（五）符合岗位要求的文化程度、专业水平和工作能力；  
（六）具有正常履行职责的身体条件和心理素质；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 9** Civilian personnel shall possess the following basic conditions:  
(1) Having the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) Having reached the age of 18;  
(3) Meeting the political conditions for recruitment and employment of civilian personnel by the armed forces;  
(4) Volunteering to serve national defense and military construction;  
(5) Meeting the educational level, professional level, and work ability required by the post;  
(6) Having the physical conditions and psychological quality to perform duties normally;  
(7) Other conditions provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　文职人员主要履行下列职责：  
（一）根据所任岗位，从事行政事务等管理工作，教育教学、科学研究、工程技术、医疗卫生等专业技术工作，操作维护、勤务保障等专业技能工作；  
（二）根据需要，参加军事训练和战备值勤；  
（三）根据需要，在作战和有作战背景的军事行动中承担支援保障任务，以及参加非战争军事行动；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他职责。

**Article 10** Civilian personnel shall mainly perform the following duties:  
(1) According to the post held, engaging in administrative affairs and other management work, education and teaching, scientific research, engineering technology, medical and health care and other specialized technical work, operation and maintenance, service support and other specialized skill work;  
(2) Participating in military training and combat readiness duty as needed;  
(3) Undertaking support and safeguard tasks in combat and military operations with combat background, and participating in non-war military operations as needed;  
(4) Other duties provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　文职人员应当履行下列义务：  
（一）忠于中国共产党，忠于社会主义，忠于祖国，忠于人民，努力为国防和军队建设服务；  
（二）遵守宪法、法律、法规和军队有关规章制度；  
（三）服从命令，听从指挥，遵守纪律，保守秘密，发扬军队优良传统，维护军队良好形象；  
（四）认真履职尽责，团结协作，勤奋敬业，努力提高工作质量和效率；  
（五）学习和掌握履行职责所需要的科学文化、专业知识和技术技能，提高职业能力；  
（六）清正廉洁，公道正派，恪守职业道德，模范遵守社会公德、家庭美德；  
（七）根据需要，依法转服现役；  
（八）法律、法规规定的其他义务。

**Article 11** Civilian personnel shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) Being loyal to the Communist Party of China, loyal to socialism, loyal to the motherland, loyal to the people, and striving to serve national defense and military construction;  
(2) Complying with the Constitution, laws, regulations, and relevant rules and regulations of the armed forces;  
(3) Obeying orders, following commands, observing discipline, keeping secrets, carrying forward the fine traditions of the armed forces, and maintaining a good image of the armed forces;  
(4) Conscientiously performing duties and responsibilities, uniting and cooperating, being diligent and dedicated, and striving to improve work quality and efficiency;  
(5) Learning and mastering the scientific culture, professional knowledge, and technical skills necessary for performing duties, and improving professional competence;  
(6) Being upright and honest, fair and decent, abiding by professional ethics, and exemplary in observing social ethics and family virtues;  
(7) Transferring to active service according to law as needed;  
(8) Other obligations provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　文职人员享有下列权利：  
（一）获得勋章、荣誉称号、奖励、表彰以及纪念章等；  
（二）获得工资报酬，享受相应的福利待遇、抚恤优待和社会保障；  
（三）获得履行职责应当具有的工作条件和劳动保护；  
（四）参加培训；  
（五）非因法定事由、非经法定程序，不被免职、降职（级）、辞退、终止或者解除聘用合同、处分等；  
（六）申请辞职或者解除聘用合同，申请人事争议处理，提出申诉和控告；  
（七）法律、法规规定的其他权利。

**Article 12** Civilian personnel shall enjoy the following rights:  
(1) Receiving medals, honorary titles, awards, commendations, and commemorative badges;  
(2) Receiving remuneration for work and enjoying corresponding welfare benefits, pension and preferential treatment, and social insurance;  
(3) Obtaining working conditions and labor protection necessary for performing duties;  
(4) Participating in training;  
(5) Not being removed from office, demoted (downgraded), dismissed, having the employment contract terminated or rescinded, or subjected to disciplinary action without statutory reasons or statutory procedures;  
(6) Applying for resignation or rescission of the employment contract, applying for handling of personnel disputes, and filing petitions and accusations;  
(7) Other rights provided by laws and regulations.

### 第三章　岗位设置与级别

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军队建立文职人员岗位管理制度。  
军队根据职责任务、人员编制设定文职人员岗位，明确岗位类别、岗位职务层级、岗位等级。  
文职人员岗位类别，分为管理岗位、专业技术岗位、专业技能岗位。根据岗位特点和管理需要，可以划分若干具体类别。  
文职人员岗位职务层级，在管理岗位和专业技术岗位设置。  
文职人员岗位等级，根据岗位类别，分为文员等级、专业技术岗位等级、专业技能岗位等级。

**Article 13** The armed forces shall establish a post management system for civilian personnel.  
The armed forces shall set up civilian personnel posts according to duties, mission and personnel establishment, and specify the type of post, post function rank, and post grade.  
The types of civilian personnel posts are classified into management posts, specialized technical posts, and specialized skill posts. According to the characteristics of the posts and management needs, several specific categories may be further divided.  
Civilian personnel post function ranks are set in management posts and specialized technical posts.  
Civilian personnel post grades, according to the type of post, are classified into staff rank, specialized technical post grade, and specialized skill post grade.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　管理类文职人员实行岗位职务层级与文员等级并行制度。  
担任领导职务的管理类文职人员的岗位职务层级，由高到低分为七个层级，即军队文职部级副职、军队文职局级正职、军队文职局级副职、军队文职处级正职、军队文职处级副职、军队文职科级正职、军队文职科级副职。  
文员等级在军队文职局级以下设置，由高到低分为十二个等级，即一级文员至十二级文员。  
军队文职局级以下岗位职务层级对应的最低文员等级是：  
（一）军队文职局级正职：一级文员；  
（二）军队文职局级副职：二级文员；  
（三）军队文职处级正职：四级文员；  
（四）军队文职处级副职：六级文员；  
（五）军队文职科级正职：八级文员；  
（六）军队文职科级副职：十级文员。

**Article 14** Management civilian personnel shall be subject to a system where post function rank and staff rank operate in parallel.  
The post function ranks of management civilian personnel holding leadership positions, from high to low, are divided into seven levels: Deputy Ministerial (military civilian personnel), Director-General (military civilian personnel), Deputy Director-General (military civilian personnel), Division Director (military civilian personnel), Deputy Division Director (military civilian personnel), Section Chief (military civilian personnel), and Deputy Section Chief (military civilian personnel).  
Staff ranks are set at the level of Director-General (military civilian personnel) and below, divided into twelve levels from high to low, i.e., from Grade 1 Staff to Grade 12 Staff.  
The minimum staff rank corresponding to post function ranks at the Director-General level and below is:  
(1) Director-General (military civilian personnel): Grade 1 Staff;  
(2) Deputy Director-General (military civilian personnel): Grade 2 Staff;  
(3) Division Director (military civilian personnel): Grade 4 Staff;  
(4) Deputy Division Director (military civilian personnel): Grade 6 Staff;  
(5) Section Chief (military civilian personnel): Grade 8 Staff;  
(6) Deputy Section Chief (military civilian personnel): Grade 10 Staff.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　专业技术类文职人员实行岗位职务层级和专业技术岗位等级管理制度。  
专业技术类文职人员岗位职务层级的设置和管理，按照军队有关规定执行。  
专业技术类文职人员的专业技术岗位等级，由高到低分为十三个等级，即专业技术一级至十三级。

**Article 15** Specialized technical civilian personnel shall be subject to a system of post function rank and specialized technical post grade management.  
The establishment and management of post function ranks for specialized technical civilian personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.  
The specialized technical post grades for specialized technical civilian personnel, from high to low, are divided into thirteen levels, i.e., Grade 1 to Grade 13 of specialized technical posts.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专业技能类文职人员岗位分为技术工岗位和普通工岗位。  
技术工岗位文职人员实行专业技能岗位等级管理制度，由高到低分为五个等级，即专业技能一级至五级；普通工岗位文职人员不分等级。

**Article 16** The posts for specialized skill civilian personnel are divided into skilled worker posts and ordinary worker posts.  
Civilian personnel in skilled worker posts shall be subject to a specialized skill post grade management system, divided into five grades from high to low, i.e., Grade 1 to Grade 5 of specialized skill; civilian personnel in ordinary worker posts are not graded.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　文职人员实行级别管理制度。  
文职人员级别，根据所任岗位职务层级、岗位等级及其德才表现、工作实绩和资历确定。  
文职人员级别的设置和管理，以及与岗位职务层级、岗位等级的对应关系，按照军队有关规定执行。  
文职人员的岗位职务层级、岗位等级与级别是确定文职人员工资以及其他待遇的主要依据。

**Article 17** Civilian personnel shall be subject to a grade management system.  
The grade of civilian personnel shall be determined according to the post function rank, post grade held, and their performance in terms of integrity and ability, work achievements, and seniority.  
The establishment and management of civilian personnel grades, and the corresponding relationship with post function ranks and post grades, shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.  
The post function rank, post grade, and grade of civilian personnel shall be the main basis for determining their salary and other benefits.

### 第四章　招录聘用

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军队实行公开招考、直接引进、专项招录相结合的文职人员招录聘用制度。  
公开招考，适用于新招录聘用七级文员、专业技术八级、专业技能三级以下和普通工岗位的文职人员。  
直接引进，适用于选拔高层次人才和特殊专业人才。  
专项招录，适用于从退役军人等特定群体中招录聘用文职人员。

**Article 18** The armed forces shall implement a system for the recruitment and employment of civilian personnel combining open examinations, direct introduction, and special recruitment.  
Open examinations apply to the recruitment and employment of civilian personnel for positions at Grade 7 Staff, Grade 8 specialized technical, Grade 3 specialized skill and below, and ordinary worker posts.  
Direct introduction applies to the selection of high-level talent and special professional talent.  
Special recruitment applies to the recruitment and employment of civilian personnel from specific groups such as retired military personnel.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　新招录聘用的文职人员，除应当具备[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的基本条件以外，还应当具备军队规定的拟任岗位有关资格条件。其中，文职人员首次招录聘用的最高年龄分别为：  
（一）军队文职局级副职、二级文员以上岗位，以及专业技术七级以上岗位的，50周岁；  
（二）军队文职处级正职至军队文职科级正职、三级文员至八级文员、专业技术八级至专业技术十级岗位，以及专业技能二级以上岗位的，45周岁；  
（三）军队文职科级副职、九级文员至十二级文员、专业技术十一级至专业技术十三级岗位，以及专业技能三级以下和普通工岗位的，35周岁。  
根据军队建设和执行任务需要，可以按照军队有关规定适当放宽文职人员招录聘用的最高年龄限制等条件。  
文职人员岗位应当优先招录聘用符合条件的退役军人。

**Article 19** Newly recruited and employed civilian personnel shall, in addition to meeting the basic conditions provided in Article 9 of these Regulations, also meet the relevant qualification conditions for the proposed post as prescribed by the armed forces. Among them, the maximum age for the first-time recruitment and employment of civilian personnel is:  
(1) For posts at the level of Deputy Director-General (military civilian personnel), Grade 2 Staff and above, and Grade 7 specialized technical posts and above: 50 years old;  
(2) For posts from Division Director (military civilian personnel) to Section Chief (military civilian personnel), from Grade 3 Staff to Grade 8 Staff, from Grade 8 to Grade 10 specialized technical posts, and Grade 2 specialized skill posts and above: 45 years old;  
(3) For posts at the level of Deputy Section Chief (military civilian personnel), from Grade 9 Staff to Grade 12 Staff, from Grade 11 to Grade 13 specialized technical posts, and Grade 3 specialized skill posts and below and ordinary worker posts: 35 years old.  
Where necessary for military construction and mission execution, the maximum age limit and other conditions for the recruitment and employment of civilian personnel may be appropriately relaxed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.  
Posts for civilian personnel shall give priority to the recruitment and employment of qualified retired military personnel.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公开招考文职人员，一般按照制定计划、发布信息、资格审查、统一笔试、面试、体格检查、政治考核、结果公示、审批备案的程序进行。  
直接引进和专项招录文职人员的程序，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 20** Open examinations for civilian personnel shall generally be conducted in accordance with the procedures of formulating plans, releasing information, qualifications review, unified written examinations, interviews, physical examinations, political assessment, public announcement of results, and approval and filing.  
The procedures for direct introduction and special recruitment of civilian personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　招录聘用军队文职部级副职和专业技术三级以上岗位文职人员，由中央军事委员会审批。招录聘用其他管理类文职人员和专业技术类文职人员，由中央军事委员会机关部委、中央军事委员会直属机构、战区、军兵种、中央军事委员会直属单位审批。招录聘用专业技能类文职人员，由师级以上单位审批。

**Article 21** The recruitment and employment of civilian personnel for posts at the level of Deputy Ministerial (military civilian personnel) and Grade 3 specialized technical posts and above shall be subject to approval by the Central Military Commission. The recruitment and employment of other management civilian personnel and specialized technical civilian personnel shall be subject to approval by the departments and commissions of the Central Military Commission organs, directly subordinate organs of the Central Military Commission, theater commands, services and arms, and units directly under the Central Military Commission. The recruitment and employment of specialized skill civilian personnel shall be subject to approval by units at the division level and above.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　新招录聘用的文职人员按照军队有关规定实行试用期。试用期满考核合格的，按照规定任职定级；考核不合格的或者试用期内本人自愿放弃的，取消录用。

**Article 22** Newly recruited and employed civilian personnel shall undergo a probationary period in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces. Those who pass the assessment at the end of the probationary period shall be appointed and graded in accordance with the provisions; those who fail the assessment or voluntarily withdraw during the probationary period shall have their employment cancelled.

### 第五章　培训

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　军队根据文职人员履行职责、改善知识结构和提高职业能力需要，对文职人员实施分类分级培训。  
文职人员的培训，坚持军队院校教育、部队训练实践、军事职业教育相结合。

**Article 23** The armed forces shall implement classified and hierarchical training for civilian personnel based on the needs for performing duties, improving knowledge structure, and enhancing professional competence.  
The training of civilian personnel shall adhere to the combination of military academy education, military unit training practice, and military vocational education.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　文职人员的培训，分为初任培训、晋升培训、岗位培训。  
对新招录聘用的文职人员应当进行初任培训，使其具备适应岗位必备的军政素质和基本业务能力。  
对拟晋升岗位职务层级的文职人员应当进行晋升培训，提高其政治能力、管理能力和专业能力。  
根据岗位特点和工作需要，应当对文职人员进行岗位培训，提高其履行职责能力。

**Article 24** The training of civilian personnel is divided into initial training, promotion training, and post training.  
Initial training shall be provided to newly recruited and employed civilian personnel to enable them to acquire the essential military-political quality and basic professional competence to adapt to the post.  
Promotion training shall be provided to civilian personnel who are to be promoted in post function rank, to improve their political ability, management ability, and professional ability.  
Post training shall be provided to civilian personnel according to the characteristics of the post and work needs, to improve their ability to perform duties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　文职人员培训纳入军队人员培训体系统一组织实施。  
军队可以利用国家和社会资源，对文职人员进行培训。中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关应当积极支持军队开展文职人员培训工作。

**Article 25** The training of civilian personnel shall be incorporated into the military personnel training system for unified organization and implementation.  
The armed forces may utilize national and social resources to train civilian personnel. The relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State and relevant local organs shall actively support the armed forces in carrying out civilian personnel training.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　军队根据工作需要，可以安排文职人员参加学历升级教育，选派文职人员参加有关学习培训。

**Article 26** The armed forces may, according to work needs, arrange for civilian personnel to participate in education for upgrading their academic qualifications, and select and send civilian personnel to attend relevant study and training.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　用人单位应当对文职人员培训情况进行登记，并归入文职人员人事档案。  
培训情况作为文职人员资格评定、考核、任用等的依据之一。

**Article 27** The employing unit shall record the training situation of civilian personnel and file it into the personnel files of civilian personnel.  
The training situation shall serve as one of the bases for qualification assessment, evaluation, appointment, etc. of civilian personnel.

### 第六章　考核

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　文职人员实行分类分级考核。  
文职人员的考核，应当全面考核文职人员的政治品质、专业能力、担当精神、工作实绩、廉洁自律等情况。

**Article 28** Civilian personnel shall be subject to classified and hierarchical assessment.  
The assessment of civilian personnel shall comprehensively assess their political quality, professional competence, spirit of responsibility, work performance, integrity and self-discipline, and other aspects.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　文职人员的考核，分为年度考核、聘（任）期考核、专项考核。  
年度考核，主要考核文职人员年度履行职责的总体情况。  
聘（任）期考核，主要考核文职人员在一个聘（任）期内的总体情况。  
专项考核，主要考核拟任用文职人员的总体情况，以及文职人员执行任务、参加教育培训、试用期表现等情况。

**Article 29** The assessment of civilian personnel is divided into annual assessment, employment (appointment) term assessment, and special assessment.  
Annual assessment mainly assesses the overall performance of civilian personnel in performing their duties during the year.  
Employment (appointment) term assessment mainly assesses the overall performance of civilian personnel during one employment (appointment) term.  
Special assessment mainly assesses the overall situation of civilian personnel to be appointed, as well as their performance in executing tasks, participating in education and training, and probationary period.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　文职人员的考核工作，由用人单位或者其上级单位按照军队有关规定组织实施。

**Article 30** The assessment of civilian personnel shall be organized and implemented by the employing unit or its superior unit in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　文职人员的考核，应当形成考核报告、评语、鉴定或者等次等结果。其中，文职人员年度考核结果，分为优秀、称职、基本称职、不称职四个等次；聘（任）期考核和专项考核，根据需要明确考核结果等次。  
考核结果作为文职人员任用、工资待遇确定、奖惩实施、续聘竞聘和辞退解聘等的主要依据。

**Article 31** The assessment of civilian personnel shall produce results such as assessment reports, comments, evaluations, or grades. Among them, the annual assessment results of civilian personnel are divided into four grades: excellent, competent, basically competent, and incompetent; for employment (appointment) term assessment and special assessment, the grades of assessment results shall be specified as needed.  
The assessment results shall serve as the main basis for the appointment, determination of salary and benefits, implementation of rewards and punishments, renewal of employment or competitive employment, and dismissal or termination of employment of civilian personnel.

### 第七章　任用

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　文职人员的任用，包括岗位职务任免、岗位职务层级升降，以及岗位等级和级别的确定与调整。

**Article 32** The appointment of civilian personnel includes the appointment and removal of posts, the promotion and demotion of post function ranks, and the determination and adjustment of post grades and grades.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　文职人员实行委任制和聘用制相结合的任用方式。对实行聘用制的文职人员，用人单位应当与其签订聘用合同。  
担任领导职务的管理类文职人员实行任期制。  
文职人员的任用条件、权限和办理程序，以及聘用合同管理和领导职务任期管理的具体办法，由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 33** Civilian personnel shall be subject to an appointment system combining appointment by order and employment contract. For civilian personnel under the employment contract system, the employing unit shall sign an employment contract with them.  
Management civilian personnel holding leadership positions shall be subject to a term system.  
The conditions, authority, and procedures for the appointment of civilian personnel, as well as the specific measures for the management of employment contracts and the term management of leadership positions, shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对不适宜或者不胜任现岗位的文职人员，用人单位应当调整其岗位，并重新确定其岗位职务层级、岗位等级和级别。  
文职人员调整任职、辞职、被辞退、终止和解除聘用合同、退休、岗位编制撤销，以及受到开除处分的，原职务自行免除；因其他情形需要免职的，按照军队有关规定执行。

**Article 34** For civilian personnel who are unsuitable or incompetent for their current posts, the employing unit shall adjust their posts and re-determine their post function rank, post grade, and grade.  
Where a civilian personnel has a change in appointment, resigns, is dismissed, terminates or rescinds the employment contract, retires, has the post establishment cancelled, or is subject to expulsion, the original post shall be automatically removed; where removal is required for other reasons, it shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　文职人员职称、职业资格和职业技能等级的取得，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。  
文职人员在招录聘用前取得的职称、职业资格和职业技能等级，用人单位应当予以认可。  
文职人员退出军队后，在军队工作期间取得的职称、职业资格和职业技能等级仍然有效。

**Article 35** The acquisition of professional titles, vocational qualifications, and vocational skill grades by civilian personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
The professional titles, vocational qualifications, and vocational skill grades obtained by civilian personnel before recruitment and employment shall be recognized by the employing unit.  
After civilian personnel leave the armed forces, the professional titles, vocational qualifications, and vocational skill grades obtained during their service in the armed forces shall remain valid.

### 第八章　交流

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　文职人员在用人单位本专业领域岗位长期稳定工作，根据需要可以组织交流。

**Article 36** Civilian personnel who work stably and for a long period in posts within the professional field of the employing unit may be organized for exchange as needed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　文职人员的交流分为军队内部交流和跨军地交流两种方式，以军队内部交流为主。  
文职人员交流，应当具备拟任岗位资格条件，且相应岗位编制有空缺。

**Article 37** The exchange of civilian personnel is divided into two methods: internal exchange within the armed forces and cross-military-civilian exchange, with internal exchange within the armed forces as the main method.  
The exchange of civilian personnel shall meet the qualification conditions for the proposed post, and there shall be a vacancy in the corresponding post establishment.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　符合下列情形之一的，文职人员可以在军队内部交流：  
（一）因执行任务需要充实力量的；  
（二）本单位无合适人选且不宜通过招录聘用补充的；  
（三）改善队伍结构需要调整任职的；  
（四）任期届满需要调整任职，或者按照规定需要任职回避的；  
（五）法律、法规等有关规定明确的其他情形。

**Article 38** Civilian personnel may be exchanged within the armed forces under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where it is necessary to strengthen strength due to mission execution;  
(2) Where the unit has no suitable candidate and it is not appropriate to supplement through recruitment and employment;  
(3) Where adjustment of appointment is needed to improve the structure of the contingent;  
(4) Where adjustment of appointment is needed due to expiration of term, or where post avoidance is required according to provisions;  
(5) Other circumstances clearly provided by laws, regulations, and other relevant provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　因国家重大战略以及重大工程、重大项目、重大任务急需干部或者紧缺专业技术人才的，军队有关单位可以根据具体需求，按照干部管理权限商中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关、国有企业和事业单位选调干部或者专业技术人才到文职人员岗位工作。  
根据工作需要，中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关、国有企业和事业单位可以按照干部管理权限，根据国家和军队有关规定，通过“一事一议”方式选调文职人员到有关单位工作。

**Article 39** Where there is an urgent need for cadres or scarce specialized technical talent due to major national strategies, major projects, major programs, or major tasks, relevant military units may, according to specific needs and in accordance with the cadre management authority, consult with the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State, relevant local organs, State-owned enterprises, and public institutions to select and transfer cadres or specialized technical talent to work in civilian personnel posts.  
According to work needs, the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State, relevant local organs, State-owned enterprises, and public institutions may, in accordance with the cadre management authority and the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces, select and transfer civilian personnel to work in relevant units through a "case-by-case" approach.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　文职人员有下列情形之一的，除[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一项规定情形外，不得交流：  
（一）招录聘用后工作未满2年的；  
（二）工作特别需要、暂无合适接替人选的；  
（三）因涉嫌违纪违法正在接受纪律审查、监察调查，或者涉嫌犯罪，司法程序尚未终结的；  
（四）法律、法规规定其他不得交流的情形。

**Article 40** Civilian personnel under any of the following circumstances shall not be exchanged, except for the circumstances provided in item (1) of Article 38 of these Regulations:  
(1) Those who have worked for less than 2 years after recruitment and employment;  
(2) Those whose work is particularly needed and for whom there is no suitable replacement for the time being;  
(3) Those who are under disciplinary review or supervisory investigation due to suspected violation of discipline or law, or who are suspected of committing a crime and the judicial procedures have not yet been concluded;  
(4) Other circumstances where exchange is not allowed as provided by laws and regulations.

### 第九章　教育管理

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　用人单位应当根据军队有关规定，结合文职人员身份属性和岗位职责，坚持统分结合、注重效能原则，坚持尊重激励与监督约束并重，做好文职人员的教育管理工作，营造干事创业的良好环境。

**Article 41** The employing unit shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces, taking into account the identity attributes and post responsibilities of civilian personnel, adhere to the principle of combining centralized and decentralized management with a focus on efficiency, adhere to the equal emphasis on respect and incentive and supervision and restraint, and properly carry out the education and management of civilian personnel, creating a favorable environment for work and entrepreneurship.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　用人单位应当加强文职人员队伍的思想政治建设，引导文职人员投身强军兴军实践，培养政治合格、业务熟练、敢于担当、积极作为、恪尽职守、遵规守纪的职业操守，培育热爱军队、服务国防的职业认同。

**Article 42** The employing unit shall strengthen the ideological and political construction of the civilian personnel contingent, guide civilian personnel to devote themselves to the practice of strengthening and revitalizing the armed forces, cultivate professional ethics of being politically qualified, professionally proficient, daring to take responsibility, active in action, conscientious in duties, and compliant with rules and discipline, and foster professional identification with loving the armed forces and serving national defense.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　用人单位应当加强对文职人员的安全管理和保密教育，对涉密岗位文职人员，按照国家和军队有关规定进行管理。  
文职人员因公、因私出国（境）的管理，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 43** The employing unit shall strengthen safety management and confidentiality education for civilian personnel, and manage civilian personnel in posts involving classified information in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
The management of civilian personnel traveling abroad (including to Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan) for official or private purposes shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　文职人员可以按照军队有关规定，参加军地本专业领域学术组织，以及社会团体的组织及其活动。军队鼓励支持从事专业技术工作的文职人员参加国家和地方的人才工程计划、军民科技协同创新等活动。  
从事专业技术工作的文职人员，在履行好岗位职责、完成本职工作的前提下，经批准可以到军队以外单位兼职。

**Article 44** Civilian personnel may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces, participate in academic organizations in their professional field within the military and civilian spheres, as well as social organizations and their activities. The armed forces encourage and support civilian personnel engaged in specialized technical work to participate in national and local talent project plans, military-civilian science and technology collaborative innovation, and other activities.  
Civilian personnel engaged in specialized technical work may, on the premise of properly performing their post duties and completing their own work, and with approval, take part-time jobs in units outside the armed forces.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军队建立文职人员宣誓制度。  
文职人员应当严格遵守军队内务管理有关规定。  
文职人员服装的制式及其标志服饰由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 45** The armed forces shall establish an oath-taking system for civilian personnel.  
Civilian personnel shall strictly abide by the relevant provisions of the armed forces on internal affairs management.  
The style and insignia of civilian personnel uniforms shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对在国防和军队建设中取得突出成绩、为国家和人民作出突出贡献的文职人员，按照国家和军队有关规定给予勋章、荣誉称号、奖励、表彰以及纪念章等。  
文职人员可以按照国家和军队有关规定接受地方人民政府、群团组织和社会组织，以及国际组织和其他国家、军队给予的荣誉。

**Article 46** Civilian personnel who have achieved outstanding results in national defense and military construction and made outstanding contributions to the State and the people shall be awarded medals, honorary titles, awards, commendations, and commemorative badges in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
Civilian personnel may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces, accept honors conferred by local people's governments, mass organizations and social organizations, as well as international organizations and other countries and armed forces.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　文职人员与用人单位发生的人事争议，按照国家和军队有关规定依法处理。  
文职人员对涉及本人的考核结果、辞职辞退、处分决定等不服的，可以申请复核、提出申诉。  
文职人员认为用人单位及有关人员侵犯其合法权益的，可以依法提出控告。  
对文职人员的复核申请、申诉或者控告，军队有关单位应当及时受理。

**Article 47** Personnel disputes arising between civilian personnel and the employing unit shall be handled according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
Where civilian personnel disagree with the assessment results, resignation or dismissal decisions, or disciplinary action decisions concerning themselves, they may apply for review or file a petition.  
Where civilian personnel believe that the employing unit or relevant personnel have infringed upon their lawful rights and interests, they may file an accusation according to law.  
Relevant military units shall promptly accept the review applications, petitions, or accusations of civilian personnel.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　文职人员招录聘用、考核、任用、奖惩、人事争议处理等工作，实行回避制度。

**Article 48** A recusal system shall be implemented in the work of recruitment and employment, assessment, appointment, rewards and punishments, and handling of personnel disputes for civilian personnel.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　文职人员的人事档案，按照军队有关规定进行管理。

**Article 49** The personnel files of civilian personnel shall be managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　军队用人单位按照国家有关规定，进行组织机构登记。

**Article 50** Military employing units shall register their organizational structures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第十章　待遇保障

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　军队建立与国家机关事业单位工作人员待遇政策相衔接、体现“优才优待、优绩优奖”激励导向的文职人员待遇保障体系。

**Article 51** The armed forces shall establish a treatment guarantee system for civilian personnel that is aligned with the treatment policies for staff of State organs and public institutions and reflects the incentive orientation of "favorable treatment for outstanding talent, and awards for outstanding performance".

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　文职人员依法享受相应的政治待遇、工作待遇和生活待遇。  
文职人员的政治待遇，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。  
文职人员在军队工作期间根据所任岗位职务层级和岗位等级等，享受军队规定的办公用房、公务用车等工作待遇；免职、退出军队的，调整或者取消相应的工作待遇。

**Article 52** Civilian personnel shall enjoy corresponding political treatment, work treatment, and living treatment according to law.  
The political treatment of civilian personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
During their service in the armed forces, civilian personnel shall, according to the post function rank and post grade they hold, enjoy work treatment such as office space and official vehicles as prescribed by the armed forces; where they are removed from office or leave the armed forces, the corresponding work treatment shall be adjusted or cancelled.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　军队建立统一的文职人员工资制度。文职人员工资包括基本工资、津贴、补贴等。  
在军队技术密集型单位，可以实行文职人员绩效工资。

**Article 53** The armed forces shall establish a unified salary system for civilian personnel. The salary of civilian personnel includes basic salary, allowances, subsidies, etc.  
In military units with intensive technology, performance-based pay may be implemented for civilian personnel.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　用人单位及其文职人员应当按照国家有关规定参加社会保险，依法缴纳社会保险费。  
军队根据国家有关规定，为文职人员建立补充保障。

**Article 54** The employing unit and its civilian personnel shall participate in social insurance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and pay social insurance premiums according to law.  
The armed forces shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish supplementary safeguards for civilian personnel.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　文职人员享受国家和军队规定的社会化、货币化住房保障政策。  
用人单位及其文职人员应当按照规定缴存、使用住房公积金，缴存的住房公积金由所在地住房公积金管理中心统一管理。  
文职人员可以按照军队有关规定租住军队集体宿舍或者公寓住房。

**Article 55** Civilian personnel shall enjoy the socialized and monetized housing security policies prescribed by the State and the armed forces.  
The employing unit and its civilian personnel shall, in accordance with the provisions, contribute to and use the housing provident fund. The contributed housing provident fund shall be managed uniformly by the housing provident fund management center at the place of location.  
Civilian personnel may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces, rent military collective dormitories or apartment housing.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　文职人员享受国家和军队规定的医疗补助和医疗保健政策。文职人员在作战和有作战背景的军事行动中承担支援保障任务，以及参加非战争军事行动期间，实行军队免费医疗。

**Article 56** Civilian personnel shall enjoy the medical subsidies and medical care policies prescribed by the State and the armed forces. During the period when civilian personnel undertake support and safeguard tasks in combat and military operations with combat background, and participate in non-war military operations, free military medical care shall be implemented.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　文职人员的抚恤优待，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。  
文职人员因在作战和有作战背景的军事行动中承担支援保障任务，参加非战争军事行动以及军级以上单位批准且列入军事训练计划的军事训练造成伤亡的，其抚恤优待参照有关军人抚恤优待的规定执行。

**Article 57** The pension and preferential treatment for civilian personnel shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.  
Where civilian personnel suffer casualties due to undertaking support and safeguard tasks in combat and military operations with combat background, participating in non-war military operations, and military training approved by units at the military level or above and included in the military training plan, their pension and preferential treatment shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions on pension and preferential treatment for military personnel.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　文职人员按照军队有关规定享受探亲休假、交通补助、看望慰问、困难救济和子女入托等福利待遇。

**Article 58** Civilian personnel shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces, enjoy welfare benefits such as family visit leave, transportation subsidies, visits and condolences, hardship relief, and children's nursery care.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　文职人员办理落户，以及配偶子女随迁等，按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 59** The settlement of household registration for civilian personnel, as well as the relocation of their spouses and children, shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对文职人员中的高层次人才和特殊专业人才，按照国家和军队有关规定给予相关优惠待遇。对符合规定条件的，军队可以实行年薪制、协议工资、项目工资等市场化薪酬制度。  
军队可以为文职人员岗位重要人才购买相关保险。文职人员可以享受科技成果转化收益。

**Article 60** High-level talent and special professional talent among civilian personnel shall be granted relevant preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces. For those meeting the prescribed conditions, the armed forces may implement market-based compensation systems such as annual salary system, negotiated salary, and project salary.  
The armed forces may purchase relevant insurance for important talent in civilian personnel posts. Civilian personnel may enjoy income from the commercialization of scientific and technological achievements.

### 第十一章　退出

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　实行委任制的文职人员辞职，或者被用人单位辞退的；实行聘用制的文职人员解除、终止聘用合同，或者用人单位解除、终止聘用合同的，按照军队有关规定执行。

**Article 61** Where a civilian personnel under the appointment by order system resigns or is dismissed by the employing unit; or where a civilian personnel under the employment contract system rescinds or terminates the employment contract, or the employing unit rescinds or terminates the employment contract, such matters shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　文职人员因用人单位精简整编等原因需要退出军队的，由军队有关单位会同中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关根据不同情形按照有关政策予以妥善安排。

**Article 62** Where civilian personnel need to leave the armed forces due to reasons such as downsizing and reorganization of the employing unit, relevant military units shall, jointly with the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State and relevant local organs, make proper arrangements according to different circumstances and in accordance with relevant policies.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　用人单位可以依法辞退文职人员或者单方面解除聘用合同。  
有下列情形之一的，用人单位不得辞退文职人员或者单方面解除聘用合同：  
（一）因公（工）负伤或者患职业病，经劳动能力鉴定机构鉴定为一级至六级伤残的；  
（二）患病或者负伤，在规定的医疗期内的；  
（三）女性文职人员在孕期、产期、哺乳期内的；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 63** The employing unit may, according to law, dismiss a civilian personnel or unilaterally rescind the employment contract.  
Under any of the following circumstances, the employing unit shall not dismiss a civilian personnel or unilaterally rescind the employment contract:  
(1) Those who are injured or disabled due to official duties (work) or suffer from occupational diseases, and have been assessed by a labor capability appraisal institution as having a disability level from Grade 1 to Grade 6;  
(2) Those who are ill or injured and are within the prescribed medical treatment period;  
(3) Female civilian personnel who are in pregnancy, childbirth, or nursing period;  
(4) Other circumstances provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　文职人员可以依法辞职或者单方面解除聘用合同。  
有下列情形之一的，文职人员不得辞职或者单方面解除聘用合同：  
（一）未满军队规定最低工作年限的；  
（二）国家发布动员令或者宣布进入战争状态时；  
（三）部队受领作战任务或者遭敌突然袭击时；  
（四）在作战和有作战背景的军事行动中承担支援保障任务，参加非战争军事行动以及军级以上单位批准且列入军事训练计划的军事训练期间；  
（五）在涉及核心、重要军事秘密等特殊岗位任职或者离开上述岗位不满军队规定的脱密期限的；  
（六）正在接受审计、纪律审查、监察调查，或者涉嫌犯罪，司法程序尚未终结的；  
（七）法律、法规规定或者聘用合同约定的其他情形。

**Article 64** Civilian personnel may, according to law, resign or unilaterally rescind the employment contract.  
Under any of the following circumstances, civilian personnel shall not resign or unilaterally rescind the employment contract:  
(1) Those who have not completed the minimum working period prescribed by the armed forces;  
(2) When the State issues a mobilization order or declares a state of war;  
(3) When the unit receives a combat mission or is subjected to a surprise attack by the enemy;  
(4) During the period of undertaking support and safeguard tasks in combat and military operations with combat background, participating in non-war military operations, and military training approved by units at the military level or above and included in the military training plan;  
(5) Those who are serving in special posts involving core or important military secrets, or who have left such posts but have not yet completed the declassification period prescribed by the armed forces;  
(6) Those who are under audit, disciplinary review, or supervisory investigation, or who are suspected of committing a crime and the judicial procedures have not yet been concluded;  
(7) Other circumstances provided by laws and regulations or agreed upon in the employment contract.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　文职人员符合国家和军队规定退休条件的，应当退休。  
文职人员退休后，享受国家和军队规定的相应待遇。各级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门牵头承担退休文职人员服务管理工作，人力资源社会保障、医保等相关部门和街道（乡镇）、社区（村）按照职责分工做好相关工作。

**Article 65** Where a civilian personnel meets the retirement conditions prescribed by the State and the armed forces, they shall retire.  
After retirement, civilian personnel shall enjoy the corresponding benefits prescribed by the State and the armed forces. The competent departments for retired military personnel affairs of the people's governments at all levels shall take the lead in undertaking the service and management of retired civilian personnel, and the relevant departments such as human resources and social security, medical insurance, and subdistricts (townships), communities (villages) shall properly carry out the relevant work in accordance with their respective duties.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　文职人员退出军队的，由军队有关单位按照任用权限审批。

**Article 66** Where a civilian personnel leaves the armed forces, the relevant military unit shall examine and approve the matter in accordance with the appointment authority.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　文职人员自退出军队之日起，与用人单位的人事关系即行终止。用人单位应当按照国家和军队有关规定，及时办理文职人员人事档案、社会保险、住房公积金等关系转移的相关手续。  
符合国家和军队规定的补偿情形的，用人单位应当给予文职人员经济补偿。  
文职人员退出军队后，从业限制和脱密期管理按照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 67** The personnel relationship between a civilian personnel and the employing unit shall be terminated as of the date the civilian personnel leaves the armed forces. The employing unit shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces, promptly handle the procedures for the transfer of the civilian personnel's personnel files, social insurance, housing provident fund, and other relevant relationships.  
Where the circumstances for compensation as prescribed by the State and the armed forces are met, the employing unit shall provide economic compensation to the civilian personnel.  
After civilian personnel leave the armed forces, the restrictions on employment and the management of the declassification period shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces.

### 第十二章　法律责任

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　军队有关单位及其工作人员，在文职人员管理工作中违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由其所在单位或者上级单位给予通报批评，责令限期改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不按照编制限额、资格条件、规定程序进行文职人员招录聘用的；  
（二）在招录聘用等工作中发生泄露试题、违反考场纪律以及其他严重影响公开、公正行为的；  
（三）不按照规定进行文职人员培训、考核、任用、交流、回避、奖惩以及办理退出的；  
（四）违反规定调整文职人员工资、福利、保险待遇标准的；  
（五）不按照规定受理和处理文职人员申诉、控告的；  
（六）违反本条例规定的其他情形。

**Article 68** Where a relevant military unit or its staff member, in the administration of civilian personnel, violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the unit or its superior unit shall give a notice of criticism and order correction within a specified time limit; the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who are liable shall be given disciplinary action according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Not recruiting and employing civilian personnel in accordance with the authorized strength, qualification conditions, and prescribed procedures;  
(2) Engaging in acts such as leaking examination questions, violating examination discipline, or other acts seriously affecting openness and fairness in the recruitment and employment process;  
(3) Not carrying out training, assessment, appointment, exchange, recusal, rewards and punishments, and handling of departure for civilian personnel in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) Adjusting the standards of salary, benefits, and insurance treatment for civilian personnel in violation of the provisions;  
(5) Not accepting and handling the petitions and accusations of civilian personnel in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) Other circumstances violating the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　中央和国家有关机关、地方有关机关及其工作人员，在文职人员管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** Where the relevant organs of the Central Committee and the State, relevant local organs, or their staff members, abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the administration of civilian personnel, the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who are liable shall be given disciplinary action according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　文职人员违纪违法、失职失责的，按照规定给予处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
文职人员违反国家和军队有关规定或者聘用合同约定，给用人单位造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 70** Where a civilian personnel violates discipline or law, or is derelict of duty or fails in responsibility, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a civilian personnel violates the relevant provisions of the State and the armed forces or the terms of the employment contract, causing losses to the employing unit, they shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　文职人员辞职或者被辞退、解除聘用合同，且存在严重违约失信行为的，或者被军队开除的，不得再次进入军队工作。

**Article 71** Where a civilian personnel resigns or is dismissed, or the employment contract is rescinded, and there is serious breach of contract or dishonesty, or is expelled from the armed forces, such personnel shall not re-enter the armed forces for work.

### 第十三章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　对军队建设急需的高层次人才和特殊专业人才，可以在文职人员岗位设置、人事管理和待遇保障等方面采取特殊措施，具体办法由中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 72** For high-level talent and special professional talent urgently needed for military construction, special measures may be taken in terms of post setting, personnel management, and treatment guarantee for civilian personnel. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国家和军队对深化国防和军队改革期间现役军人转改的文职人员另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 73** Where the State and the armed forces have other provisions on civilian personnel who are transferred from active service to civilian personnel during the period of deepening national defense and military reform, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
（一）用人单位，是指与文职人员建立人事关系的军队团级以上建制单位；  
（二）聘用制，是指以签订聘用合同的形式确定用人单位与文职人员基本人事关系的用人方式；  
（三）委任制，是指不签订聘用合同、以直接任用的形式确定用人单位与文职人员基本人事关系的用人方式。

**Article 74** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the following meanings:  
(1) "Employing unit" means a military unit at the regiment level or above that establishes a personnel relationship with a civilian personnel;  
(2) "Employment contract system" means a mode of employment whereby the basic personnel relationship between the employing unit and a civilian personnel is established by signing an employment contract;  
(3) "Appointment by order system" means a mode of employment whereby the basic personnel relationship between the employing unit and a civilian personnel is established by direct appointment without signing an employment contract.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　中国人民武装警察部队文职人员，适用本条例。

**Article 75** These Regulations shall apply to civilian personnel of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　本条例自2023年1月1日起施行。

**Article 76** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2023.
