# 军工关键设备设施管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-06-24  
**生效日期**：2011-10-01

> (2011年6月24日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第598号公布　自2011年10月1日起施行) 第一条　为了保持和提高国防科研生产能力，加强军工关键设备设施的管理，保障军工关键设备设施的安全、完整和有效使用，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称军工关键设备设施，是指直接用于武器装备科研生产的重要的实验设施、工艺设备、试验及测试设备等专用的军工设备设施。 军工关键设备设施的目录，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门会同军队武器装备主管部门、国务院国有资产监督管理机构和国务院有关部门制定。 第三条　国家对军工关键设备设施实行登记管理，对使用国家财政资金购建的用于武器装备总体、关键分系统、核心配套产品科研生产的军工关键设备设施的处置实行审批管理。 第四条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门会同国务院有关部门依照本条例规定，对全国军工关键设备设施进行管理。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门会同同级有关部门依照本条例规定，对有关军工关键设备设施进行管理。 第五条　军工关键设备设施管理，应当遵循严格责任、分工负责、方便有效的原则。 第六条　占有、使用军工关键设备设施的企业、事业单位(以下简称企业、事业单位)及其工作人员，负责军工关键设备设施管理的部门、单位及其工作人员，对知悉的国家秘密和商业秘密负有保密义务。 第七条　中央管理的企业负责办理所属单位军工关键设备设施的登记。国务院教育主管部门负责办理所属高等学校军工关键设备设施的登记。中国科学院负责办理所属科研机构军工关键设备设施的登记。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门负责办理本行政区域内前款规定以外的企业、事业单位军工关键设备设施的登记。 第八条　企业、事业单位应当自军工关键设备设施投入使用之日起30日内向负责登记的部门、单位提交载明下列内容的文件材料，办理登记手续： (一)企业、事业单位的名称、住所等基本情况； (二)军工关键设备设施的名称、产地、价值、性能、状态、资金来源、权属等基本情况。 企业、事业单位应当对其提交的文件材料的真实性负责。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保持和提高国防科研生产能力，加强军工关键设备设施的管理，保障军工关键设备设施的安全、完整和有效使用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of maintaining and improving the national defense scientific research and production capacity, strengthening the management of key military equipment and facilities, and ensuring the safety, integrity, and effective use of key military equipment and facilities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称军工关键设备设施，是指直接用于武器装备科研生产的重要的实验设施、工艺设备、试验及测试设备等专用的军工设备设施。  
军工关键设备设施的目录，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门会同军队武器装备主管部门、国务院国有资产监督管理机构和国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "key military equipment and facilities" means important experimental facilities, process equipment, testing and test equipment, and other specialized military equipment and facilities directly used for scientific research and production of weapons and equipment.  
The catalog of key military equipment and facilities shall be formulated by the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council jointly with the military weapons and equipment competent department, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对军工关键设备设施实行登记管理，对使用国家财政资金购建的用于武器装备总体、关键分系统、核心配套产品科研生产的军工关键设备设施的处置实行审批管理。

**Article 3** The State shall implement registration management for key military equipment and facilities, and shall implement examination and approval management for the disposal of key military equipment and facilities that are purchased or constructed with state fiscal funds and used for scientific research and production of weapon system overall, key subsystems, and core supporting products.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门会同国务院有关部门依照本条例规定，对全国军工关键设备设施进行管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门会同同级有关部门依照本条例规定，对有关军工关键设备设施进行管理。

**Article 4** The competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, manage the key military equipment and facilities nationwide in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
The department responsible for the administration of national defense science, technology and industry of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with the relevant departments at the same level, manage the relevant key military equipment and facilities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军工关键设备设施管理，应当遵循严格责任、分工负责、方便有效的原则。

**Article 5** The management of key military equipment and facilities shall follow the principles of strict responsibility, division of responsibilities, and convenience and effectiveness.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　占有、使用军工关键设备设施的企业、事业单位(以下简称企业、事业单位)及其工作人员，负责军工关键设备设施管理的部门、单位及其工作人员，对知悉的国家秘密和商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 6** Enterprises and institutions (hereinafter referred to as enterprises and institutions) that possess or use key military equipment and facilities, and their staff, as well as departments and units responsible for the management of key military equipment and facilities, and their staff, shall have the obligation to keep confidential the state secrets and trade secrets that come to their knowledge.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中央管理的企业负责办理所属单位军工关键设备设施的登记。国务院教育主管部门负责办理所属高等学校军工关键设备设施的登记。中国科学院负责办理所属科研机构军工关键设备设施的登记。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门负责办理本行政区域内前款规定以外的企业、事业单位军工关键设备设施的登记。

**Article 7** Enterprises under central management shall be responsible for handling the registration of key military equipment and facilities of their subordinate units. The competent department of education of the State Council shall be responsible for handling the registration of key military equipment and facilities of its subordinate institutions of higher education. The Chinese Academy of Sciences shall be responsible for handling the registration of key military equipment and facilities of its subordinate scientific research institutions.  
The department responsible for the administration of national defense science, technology and industry of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for handling the registration of key military equipment and facilities of enterprises and institutions within its administrative region other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　企业、事业单位应当自军工关键设备设施投入使用之日起30日内向负责登记的部门、单位提交载明下列内容的文件材料，办理登记手续：  
(一)企业、事业单位的名称、住所等基本情况；  
(二)军工关键设备设施的名称、产地、价值、性能、状态、资金来源、权属等基本情况。  
企业、事业单位应当对其提交的文件材料的真实性负责。

**Article 8** An enterprise or institution shall, within 30 days from the date when the key military equipment and facilities are put into use, submit to the department or unit responsible for registration the documents and materials containing the following contents, and go through the registration formalities:  
(1) the name, domicile and other basic information of the enterprise or institution;  
(2) the name, place of origin, value, performance, status, source of funds, ownership and other basic information of the key military equipment and facilities.  
The enterprise or institution shall be responsible for the authenticity of the documents and materials submitted.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　负责登记的部门、单位应当自收到提交的文件材料之日起30日内办结登记，并对军工关键设备设施赋予专用代码。

**Article 9** The department or unit responsible for registration shall complete the registration within 30 days from the date of receipt of the submitted documents and materials, and shall assign a dedicated code to the key military equipment and facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军工关键设备设施登记的具体内容和专用代码，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门统一规定和分配。

**Article 10** The specific content of registration and the dedicated code for key military equipment and facilities shall be uniformly prescribed and allocated by the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　企业、事业单位占有、使用的军工关键设备设施损毁、报废、灭失或者权属发生变更的，应当自上述事实发生之日起30日内向负责登记的部门、单位报告。负责登记的部门、单位应当及时变更登记信息。

**Article 11** Where the key military equipment and facilities possessed or used by an enterprise or institution are damaged, scrapped, lost, or their ownership changes, the enterprise or institution shall, within 30 days from the date of occurrence of the aforesaid fact, report to the department or unit responsible for registration. The department or unit responsible for registration shall update the registration information in a timely manner.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　负责登记的部门、单位应当按照国务院国防科技工业主管部门的规定将登记信息报送国务院国防科技工业主管部门。  
国务院国防科技工业主管部门和负责登记的部门、单位可以根据需要，对登记信息进行核查。

**Article 12** The department or unit responsible for registration shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council, submit the registration information to that department.  
The competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council and the department or unit responsible for registration may, as needed, verify the registration information.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业、事业单位应当建立健全军工关键设备设施使用管理制度，保证军工关键设备设施的安全、完整和有效使用，并对其占有、使用的军工关键设备设施的名称、规格、性能、状态、数量、权属等基本情况作完整记录。

**Article 13** An enterprise or institution shall establish and improve a management system for the use of key military equipment and facilities, ensure the safety, integrity, and effective use of key military equipment and facilities, and keep complete records of the name, specification, performance, status, quantity, ownership and other basic information of the key military equipment and facilities it possesses or uses.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　企业、事业单位应当按照国务院国防科技工业主管部门的规定，在需要特殊管控的军工关键设施外围划定安全控制范围，并在其外沿设置安全警戒标志。

**Article 14** An enterprise or institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council, demarcate a safety control perimeter around key military facilities requiring special control, and set up safety warning signs on the outer edge thereof.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业、事业单位改变其占有、使用的军工关键设备设施的用途的，应当向负责登记的部门、单位提交有关文件材料，办理补充登记。负责登记的部门、单位应当按照国务院国防科技工业主管部门的规定向国务院国防科技工业主管部门报送补充登记信息。  
企业、事业单位改变使用国家财政资金购建的军工关键设备设施的用途，影响武器装备科研生产任务完成的，国务院国防科技工业主管部门应当及时予以纠正。

**Article 15** Where an enterprise or institution changes the use of the key military equipment and facilities it possesses or uses, it shall submit relevant documents and materials to the department or unit responsible for registration, and go through supplementary registration. The department or unit responsible for registration shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council, submit the supplementary registration information to the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council.  
Where an enterprise or institution changes the use of key military equipment and facilities purchased or constructed with state fiscal funds, thereby affecting the completion of scientific research and production tasks for weapons and equipment, the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council shall promptly rectify the situation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　企业、事业单位拟通过转让、租赁等方式处置使用国家财政资金购建的用于武器装备总体、关键分系统、核心配套产品科研生产的军工关键设备设施，应当经国务院国防科技工业主管部门批准。申请批准应当提交载明下列内容的文件材料：  
(一)军工关键设备设施的名称、数量、价值、性能、使用等情况；  
(二)不影响承担武器装备科研生产任务的情况说明；  
(三)处置的原因及方式；  
(四)受让人或者承租人的基本情况。

**Article 16** Where an enterprise or institution intends to dispose of key military equipment and facilities that are purchased or constructed with state fiscal funds and used for scientific research and production of weapon system overall, key subsystems, and core supporting products by means of transfer, lease, etc., it shall obtain approval from the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council. When applying for approval, the enterprise or institution shall submit documents and materials containing the following contents:  
(1) the name, quantity, value, performance, use, etc. of the key military equipment and facilities;  
(2) a statement that the scientific research and production tasks for weapons and equipment shall not be affected;  
(3) the reason and method of disposal;  
(4) the basic information of the transferee or lessee.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院国防科技工业主管部门应当自收到处置申请之日起30日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。作出批准决定的，国务院国防科技工业主管部门应当向申请人颁发批准文件；作出不予批准决定的，国务院国防科技工业主管部门应当书面通知申请人，并说明理由。  
国务院国防科技工业主管部门作出批准或者不予批准的决定，应当征求军队武器装备主管部门、国务院国有资产监督管理机构和国务院有关部门的意见。涉及国防科研生产能力、结构和布局调整的，应当按照国家有关规定会同军队武器装备主管部门、国务院国有资产监督管理机构和国务院有关部门，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。  
企业、事业单位取得批准文件后，应当依照[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定及时向负责登记的部门、单位报告。

**Article 17** The competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application for disposal, make a decision on approval or disapproval. If a decision of approval is made, the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council shall issue an approval document to the applicant; if a decision of disapproval is made, the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
When the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council makes a decision on approval or disapproval, it shall seek the opinions of the military weapons and equipment competent department, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and the relevant departments of the State Council. Where the adjustment of national defense scientific research and production capacity, structure, and layout is involved, the decision on approval or disapproval shall be made jointly with the military weapons and equipment competent department, the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council, and the relevant departments of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
After an enterprise or institution obtains the approval document, it shall promptly report to the department or unit responsible for registration in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国有资产监督管理机构等有关部门依照法定职责和程序决定企业、事业单位合并、分立、改制、解散、申请破产等重大事项，涉及使用国家财政资金购建的用于武器装备总体、关键分系统、核心配套产品科研生产的军工关键设备设施权属变更的，应当征求国防科技工业主管部门的意见。

**Article 18** Where the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission or other relevant departments decide, in accordance with their statutory duties and procedures, major matters such as merger, division, restructuring, dissolution, or filing for bankruptcy of an enterprise or institution, which involve a change in the ownership of key military equipment and facilities that are purchased or constructed with state fiscal funds and used for scientific research and production of weapon system overall, key subsystems, and core supporting products, they shall seek the opinions of the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　企业、事业单位未依照本条例规定办理军工关键设备设施登记，或者其占有、使用的军工关键设备设施损毁、报废、灭失或者权属发生变更未及时向负责登记的部门、单位报告的，责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处以1万元以上2万元以下罚款。

**Article 19** Where an enterprise or institution fails to go through the registration of key military equipment and facilities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or fails to promptly report to the department or unit responsible for registration when the key military equipment and facilities it possesses or uses are damaged, scrapped, lost, or their ownership changes, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　企业、事业单位提交虚假文件材料办理登记的，责令改正，处以1万元以上2万元以下罚款。

**Article 20** Where an enterprise or institution submits false documents and materials for registration, it shall be ordered to make corrections, and shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　企业、事业单位违反本条例规定，未经批准处置使用国家财政资金购建的用于武器装备总体、关键分系统、核心配套产品科研生产的军工关键设备设施的，责令限期改正，处以50万元以上100万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以5000元以上2万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 21** Where an enterprise or institution violates the provisions of these Regulations and disposes of key military equipment and facilities that are purchased or constructed with state fiscal funds and used for scientific research and production of weapon system overall, key subsystems, and core supporting products without approval, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, and shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业、事业单位以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得有关军工关键设备设施处置的批准文件的，处以5万元以上20万元以下罚款；对违法取得的批准文件依法予以撤销。

**Article 22** Where an enterprise or institution obtains an approval document for the disposal of key military equipment and facilities by means of deception, bribery, or other illegitimate means, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; the illegally obtained approval document shall be revoked according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例规定的行政处罚，由国务院国防科技工业主管部门决定。但是，对[本条例第七条](#art-7)第二款规定的企业、事业单位有[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定的违法行为的行政处罚，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责国防科技工业管理的部门决定。

**Article 23** Administrative penalties prescribed in these Regulations shall be decided by the competent department of national defense science, technology and industry of the State Council. However, administrative penalties for the illegal acts specified in Article 19 of these Regulations committed by enterprises and institutions specified in paragraph 2 of Article 7 of these Regulations shall be decided by the department responsible for the administration of national defense science, technology and industry of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　负责军工关键设备设施登记管理、处置审批管理的部门、单位的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where a staff member of a department or unit responsible for the registration management or the examination and approval management of the disposal of key military equipment and facilities abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例自2011年10月1日起施行。

**Article 25** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2011.
