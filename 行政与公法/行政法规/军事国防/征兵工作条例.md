# 征兵工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-04-01  
**生效日期**：2023-05-01

> （1985年10月24日国务院、中央军委发布　根据2001年9月5日《国务院、中央军事委员会关于修改〈征兵工作条例〉的决定》第一次修订　2023年4月1日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第759号第二次修订） 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范和加强征兵工作，根据《中华人民共和国兵役法》，制定本条例。 第二条　征兵工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，服从国防需要，聚焦备战打仗，依法、精准、高效征集高素质兵员。 第三条　征兵是保障军队兵员补充、建设巩固国防和强大军队的一项重要工作。根据国防需要征集公民服现役的工作，适用本条例。 各级人民政府和军事机关应当依法履行征兵工作职责，完成征兵任务。 公民应当依法服兵役，自觉按照本条例的规定接受征集。 第四条　全国的征兵工作，在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，由国防部负责，具体工作由国防部征兵办公室承办。国务院、中央军事委员会建立全国征兵工作部际联席会议制度，统筹协调全国征兵工作。 省、市、县各级征兵工作领导小组负责统筹协调本行政区域的征兵工作。县级以上地方人民政府组织兵役机关和宣传、教育、公安、人力资源社会保障、交通运输、卫生健康以及其他有关部门组成征兵办公室，负责组织实施本行政区域的征兵工作，承担本级征兵工作领导小组日常工作。有关部门在本级人民政府征兵办公室的统一组织下，按照职责分工做好征兵有关工作。 机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府以及街道办事处，应当根据县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府的安排和要求，办理本单位和本行政区域的征兵工作。设有人民武装部的单位，征兵工作由人民武装部办理；不设人民武装部的单位，确定一个部门办理。普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构，应当协助兵役机关办理征兵工作有关事项。 第五条　全国每年征兵的人数、次数、时间和要求，由国务院、中央军事委员会的征兵命令规定。 县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关根据上级的征兵命令，科学分配征兵任务，下达本级征兵命令，部署本行政区域的征兵工作。 县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关建立征兵任务统筹机制，优先保证普通高等学校毕业生和对政治、身体条件或者专业技能有特别要求的兵员征集；对本行政区域内普通高等学校，可以直接分配征兵任务；对遭受严重灾害或者有其他特殊情况的地区，可以酌情调整征兵任务。 第六条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关应当会同有关部门加强对本行政区域内征兵工作的监督检查。 县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关应当将征兵工作情况作为有关单位及其负责人考核评价的内容。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和加强征兵工作，根据《中华人民共和国兵役法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China to regulate and strengthen conscription work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　征兵工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻习近平强军思想，贯彻新时代军事战略方针，服从国防需要，聚焦备战打仗，依法、精准、高效征集高素质兵员。

**Article 2** Conscription work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement Xi Jinping's thought on strengthening the military, implement the military strategic guidelines for the new era, comply with the needs of national defense, focus on preparing for war, and legally, precisely, and efficiently recruit high-quality soldiers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　征兵是保障军队兵员补充、建设巩固国防和强大军队的一项重要工作。根据国防需要征集公民服现役的工作，适用本条例。  
各级人民政府和军事机关应当依法履行征兵工作职责，完成征兵任务。  
公民应当依法服兵役，自觉按照本条例的规定接受征集。

**Article 3** Conscription is an important task to ensure the replenishment of military personnel and to build a solid national defense and a strong military. These Regulations apply to the work of recruiting citizens for active service according to the needs of national defense.  
People's governments at all levels and military organs shall perform their conscription duties according to law and complete the conscription tasks.  
Citizens shall perform military service according to law and voluntarily accept conscription in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　全国的征兵工作，在国务院、中央军事委员会领导下，由国防部负责，具体工作由国防部征兵办公室承办。国务院、中央军事委员会建立全国征兵工作部际联席会议制度，统筹协调全国征兵工作。  
省、市、县各级征兵工作领导小组负责统筹协调本行政区域的征兵工作。县级以上地方人民政府组织兵役机关和宣传、教育、公安、人力资源社会保障、交通运输、卫生健康以及其他有关部门组成征兵办公室，负责组织实施本行政区域的征兵工作，承担本级征兵工作领导小组日常工作。有关部门在本级人民政府征兵办公室的统一组织下，按照职责分工做好征兵有关工作。  
机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府以及街道办事处，应当根据县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府的安排和要求，办理本单位和本行政区域的征兵工作。设有人民武装部的单位，征兵工作由人民武装部办理；不设人民武装部的单位，确定一个部门办理。普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构，应当协助兵役机关办理征兵工作有关事项。

**Article 4** National conscription work shall be led by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, be responsible for by the Ministry of National Defense, and the specific work shall be undertaken by the Conscription Office of the Ministry of National Defense. The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall establish an inter-ministerial joint conference system for national conscription work to coordinate national conscription work.  
Provincial, municipal, and county conscription leading groups at all levels shall be responsible for coordinating conscription work within their respective administrative regions. The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the military service organs and relevant departments such as propaganda, education, public security, human resources and social security, transportation, health, and other relevant departments to form a conscription office, which shall be responsible for organizing and implementing the conscription work within its administrative region and undertaking the daily work of the conscription leading group at the corresponding level. The relevant departments, under the unified organization of the conscription office of the people's government at the same level, shall perform conscription-related work according to their respective duties.  
Organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, towns, and sub-district offices shall, in accordance with the arrangements and requirements of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district, handle the conscription work of their own units and administrative regions. For units that have a People's Armed Forces Department, the conscription work shall be handled by the People's Armed Forces Department; for units that do not have such a department, a department shall be designated to handle it. The institution responsible for conscription work in an ordinary institution of higher education shall assist the military service organ in handling matters related to conscription work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全国每年征兵的人数、次数、时间和要求，由国务院、中央军事委员会的征兵命令规定。  
县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关根据上级的征兵命令，科学分配征兵任务，下达本级征兵命令，部署本行政区域的征兵工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关建立征兵任务统筹机制，优先保证普通高等学校毕业生和对政治、身体条件或者专业技能有特别要求的兵员征集；对本行政区域内普通高等学校，可以直接分配征兵任务；对遭受严重灾害或者有其他特殊情况的地区，可以酌情调整征兵任务。

**Article 5** The number, frequency, time, and requirements of national annual conscription shall be prescribed by the conscription orders of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The local people's government at or above the county level and the military organ at the same level shall, based on the conscription orders of the superior level, scientifically allocate conscription tasks, issue their own conscription orders, and deploy the conscription work within their administrative regions.  
The local people's government at or above the county level and the military organ at the same level shall establish a mechanism for coordinating conscription tasks, prioritize the recruitment of graduates from ordinary institutions of higher education and soldiers with special requirements for political, physical conditions, or professional skills; for ordinary institutions of higher education within their administrative regions, they may directly allocate conscription tasks; for areas suffering from severe disasters or other special circumstances, they may adjust conscription tasks as appropriate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关应当会同有关部门加强对本行政区域内征兵工作的监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府和同级军事机关应当将征兵工作情况作为有关单位及其负责人考核评价的内容。

**Article 6** The military service organ of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, strengthen supervision and inspection of the conscription work within its administrative region.  
The local people's government at or above the county level and the military organ at the same level shall include the status of conscription work as a part of the assessment and evaluation of the relevant units and their responsible persons.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　军地有关部门应当将征兵信息化建设纳入国家电子政务以及军队信息化建设，实现兵役机关与宣传、发展改革、教育、公安、人力资源社会保障、卫生健康、退役军人工作以及军地其他部门间的信息共享和业务协同。  
征兵工作有关部门及其工作人员应当对收集的个人信息依法予以保密，不得泄露或者向他人非法提供。

**Article 7** Military and local relevant departments shall incorporate the informationization of conscription into the national e-government and military informationization construction, achieving information sharing and business collaboration between the military service organ and departments such as propaganda, development and reform, education, public security, human resources and social security, health, veterans' affairs, and other military and local departments.  
Relevant departments of conscription work and their staff members shall keep the collected personal information confidential according to law and shall not disclose it or illegally provide it to others.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　机关、团体、企业事业组织应当深入开展爱国主义、革命英雄主义、军队光荣历史和服役光荣的教育，增强公民国防观念和依法服兵役意识。  
县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关应当会同宣传部门，协调组织网信、教育、文化等部门，开展征兵宣传工作，鼓励公民积极应征。

**Article 8** Organs, organizations, enterprises, and institutions shall conduct in-depth education on patriotism, revolutionary heroism, the glorious history of the military, and the honor of military service, enhancing citizens' awareness of national defense and their awareness of performing military service according to law.  
The military service organ of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the propaganda department, coordinate and organize the online information, education, culture and other departments to carry out conscription propaganda, encouraging citizens to actively respond to the call.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对在征兵工作中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家和军队有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to conscription work shall be given commendations and rewards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state and the military.

### 第二章　征兵准备

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室应当适时调整充实工作人员，开展征兵业务培训；根据需要，按照国家有关规定采取政府购买服务等方式开展征兵辅助工作。

**Article 10** The conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall timely adjust and supplement staff members and carry out conscription business training; where necessary, it may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, adopt methods such as government procurement of services to carry out auxiliary conscription work.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关应当适时发布兵役登记公告，组织机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府以及街道办事处，对本单位和本行政区域当年12月31日以前年满18周岁的男性公民进行初次兵役登记，对参加过初次兵役登记的适龄男性公民进行信息核验更新。  
公民初次兵役登记由其户籍所在地县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关负责，可以采取网络登记的方式进行，也可以到兵役登记站（点）现场登记。本人因身体等特殊原因不能自主完成登记的，可以委托其亲属代为登记，户籍所在地乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府以及街道办事处应当予以协助。

**Article 11** The military service organ of the people's government of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall timely issue a military service registration announcement, organize organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, towns, and sub-district offices to conduct initial military service registration for male citizens who have reached the age of 18 by December 31 of the current year within their units and administrative regions, and to verify and update the information of eligible male citizens who have participated in the initial military service registration.  
The initial military service registration of a citizen shall be responsible for by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where his household registration is located. It may be conducted online or by on-site registration at a military service registration station (point). If a citizen cannot complete the registration independently due to special physical or other reasons, he may entrust a relative to register on his behalf, and the people's government of the township, ethnic township, town, or sub-district office where his household registration is located shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关对经过初次兵役登记的男性公民，依法确定应服兵役、免服兵役或者不得服兵役，在公民兵役登记信息中注明，并出具兵役登记凭证。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府有关部门按照职责分工，为兵役机关核实公民兵役登记信息提供协助。  
根据军队需要，可以按照规定征集女性公民服现役。

**Article 12** The military service organ of the people's government of a county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall, for male citizens who have undergone initial military service registration, determine in accordance with law whether they shall perform military service, are exempted from military service, or are disqualified from military service, indicate this in the citizen's military service registration information, and issue a military service registration certificate. The relevant departments of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall, according to their duties, assist the military service organ in verifying the citizen's military service registration information.  
According to the needs of the military, female citizens may be recruited for active service in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　依照法律规定应服兵役的公民，经初步审查具备下列征集条件的，为应征公民：  
（一）拥护中华人民共和国宪法，拥护中国共产党领导和社会主义制度；  
（二）热爱国防和军队，遵纪守法，具有良好的政治素质和道德品行；  
（三）符合法律规定的征集年龄；  
（四）具有履行军队岗位职责的身体条件、心理素质和文化程度等；  
（五）法律规定的其他条件。

**Article 13** Citizens who are required to perform military service according to law and, upon preliminary review, meet the following recruitment conditions shall be considered as citizens to be conscripted (yingzheng gongmin):  
(1) They support the Constitution of the People's Republic of China, support the leadership of the Communist Party of China and the socialist system;  
(2) They are devoted to national defense and the military, abide by laws and regulations, and have good political quality and moral character;  
(3) They meet the legally prescribed recruitment age;  
(4) They have the physical conditions, psychological quality, and educational level required to perform military duties; and  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　应征公民缓征、不征集的，依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 14** The deferment or non-recruitment of citizens to be conscripted shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　应征公民应当在户籍所在地应征；经常居住地与户籍所在地不在同一省、自治区、直辖市，符合规定条件的，可以在经常居住地应征。应征公民为普通高等学校的全日制在校生、应届毕业生的，可以在入学前户籍所在地或者学校所在地应征。

**Article 15** Citizens to be conscripted shall respond to the call in the place of their household registration; if their habitual residence is not in the same province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government as their household registration, and they meet the prescribed conditions, they may respond to the call in their habitual residence. If the citizen to be conscripted is a full-time enrolled student or a fresh graduate of an ordinary institution of higher education, he may respond to the call in the place of his household registration before enrollment or in the place where the school is located.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府公安、卫生健康、教育等部门按照职责分工，对应征公民的思想政治、健康状况和文化程度等信息进行初步核查。  
应征公民根据乡、民族乡、镇和街道办事处人民武装部（以下统称基层人民武装部）或者普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构的通知，在规定时限内，自行到全国范围内任一指定的医疗机构参加初步体检，初步体检结果在全国范围内互认。

**Article 16** The public security, health, education and other departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their duties, conduct a preliminary verification of the ideological and political status, health condition, educational level and other information of citizens to be conscripted.  
Citizens to be conscripted shall, upon notification by the people's armed forces department of a township, ethnic township, town, or sub-district office (hereinafter collectively referred to as the grassroots people's armed forces department) or the institution responsible for conscription work in an ordinary institution of higher education, go to any designated medical institution within the national scope for a preliminary physical examination within the prescribed time limit. The results of the preliminary physical examination shall be mutually recognized nationwide.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　基层人民武装部和普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构选定初步核查、初步体检合格且思想政治好、身体素质强、文化程度高的应征公民为当年预定征集的对象，并通知本人。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关和基层人民武装部、普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构应当加强对预定征集的应征公民的管理、教育和考察，了解掌握基本情况。  
预定征集的应征公民应当保持与所在地基层人民武装部或者普通高等学校负责征兵工作的机构的联系，并根据县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关的通知按时应征。  
预定征集的应征公民所在的机关、团体、企业事业组织应当督促其按时应征，并提供便利。

**Article 17** The grassroots people's armed forces department and the institution responsible for conscription work in an ordinary institution of higher education shall select citizens to be conscripted who have passed the preliminary verification and preliminary physical examination, and have good political and ideological qualities, strong physical fitness, and high educational level, as the predetermined recruitment targets for the current year, and shall notify the individuals concerned.  
The military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district, the grassroots people's armed forces department, and the institution responsible for conscription work in an ordinary institution of higher education shall strengthen the management, education, and observation of predetermined recruitment targets, and keep track of their basic situation.  
Predetermined recruitment targets shall maintain contact with the local grassroots people's armed forces department or the institution responsible for conscription work in an ordinary institution of higher education, and shall report for conscription on time in accordance with the notification of the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district.  
The organs, organizations, enterprises, and institutions where the predetermined recruitment targets are located shall urge them to report for conscription on time and provide convenience.

### 第三章　体格检查

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　征兵体格检查由征集地的县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室统一组织，本级卫生健康行政部门具体负责实施，有关单位予以协助。

**Article 18** The physical examination for conscription shall be uniformly organized by the conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of recruitment, specifically implemented by the health administrative department at the same level, and assisted by relevant units.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室会同本级卫生健康行政部门指定符合标准条件和管理要求的医院或者体检机构设立征兵体检站。本行政区域内没有符合标准条件和管理要求的医院和体检机构的，经省级人民政府征兵办公室和卫生健康行政部门批准，可以选定适合场所设立临时征兵体检站。  
设立征兵体检站的具体办法，由中央军事委员会机关有关部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 19** The conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the health administrative department at the same level, designate hospitals or physical examination institutions that meet the standard conditions and management requirements to set up conscription physical examination stations. If there are no hospitals or physical examination institutions meeting the standard conditions and management requirements within the administrative region, with the approval of the provincial people's government conscription office and the health administrative department, a suitable venue may be selected to set up a temporary conscription physical examination station.  
The specific measures for establishing conscription physical examination stations shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission organs together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　基层人民武装部应当组织预定征集的应征公民按时到征兵体检站进行体格检查。送检人数由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室根据上级赋予的征兵任务和当地预定征集的应征公民体质情况确定。  
体格检查前，县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室应当组织对体检对象的身份、户籍、文化程度、专业技能、病史等相关信息进行现场核对。

**Article 20** The grassroots people's armed forces department shall organize predetermined recruitment targets to report to the conscription physical examination station on time for physical examination. The number of persons to be sent for examination shall be determined by the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district based on the conscription tasks assigned by the superior level and the physical condition of the local predetermined recruitment targets.  
Before the physical examination, the conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize on-site verification of the examinee's identity, household registration, educational level, professional skills, medical history and other relevant information.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　负责体格检查工作的医务人员，应当严格执行应征公民体格检查标准、检查办法和其他有关规定，保证体格检查工作的质量。  
对兵员身体条件有特别要求的，县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室应当安排部队接兵人员参与体格检查工作。

**Article 21** Medical personnel responsible for physical examination work shall strictly implement the standards, methods, and other relevant provisions for the physical examination of citizens to be conscripted to ensure the quality of the physical examination work.  
For soldiers with special physical condition requirements, the conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall arrange for military personnel receiving new recruits to participate in the physical examination work.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室根据需要组织对体格检查合格的应征公民进行抽查；抽查发现不合格人数比例较高的，应当全部进行复查。

**Article 22** The conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, as necessary, organize spot checks on citizens to be conscripted who have passed the physical examination; if the proportion of unqualified persons found in the spot checks is relatively high, a full re-examination shall be conducted.

### 第四章　政治考核

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　征兵政治考核由征集地的县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室统一组织，本级公安机关具体负责实施，有关单位予以协助。

**Article 23** The political assessment for conscription shall be uniformly organized by the conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of recruitment, specifically implemented by the public security organ at the same level, and assisted by relevant units.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　征兵政治考核主要考核预定征集的应征公民政治态度、现实表现及其家庭成员等情况。

**Article 24** The political assessment for conscription mainly assesses the political attitude, current performance, and family members of the predetermined recruitment targets.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对预定征集的应征公民进行政治考核，有关部门应当按照征兵政治考核的规定，核实核查情况，出具考核意见，形成考核结论。  
对政治条件有特别要求的，县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室还应当组织走访调查；走访调查应当安排部队接兵人员参加并签署意见，未经部队接兵人员签署意见的，不得批准入伍。

**Article 25** For the political assessment of predetermined recruitment targets, the relevant departments shall, in accordance with the provisions for political assessment of conscription, verify the investigation, issue assessment opinions, and form assessment conclusions.  
For those with special political condition requirements, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall also organize visits and investigations; the visits and investigations shall include the participation of military personnel receiving new recruits, who shall sign their opinions; if the military personnel receiving new recruits have not signed their opinions, the person shall not be approved for enlistment.

### 第五章　审定新兵

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室应当在审定新兵前，集中组织体格检查、政治考核合格的人员进行役前教育。役前教育的时间、内容、方式以及相关保障等由省级人民政府征兵办公室规定。

**Article 26** The conscription office of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, before finalizing the new recruits, centrally organize pre-enlistment education for those who have passed the physical examination and political assessment. The time, content, methods, and related support for pre-enlistment education shall be prescribed by the conscription office of the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室应当组织召开会议集体审定新兵，对体格检查、政治考核合格的人员军事职业适应能力、文化程度、身体和心理素质等进行分类考评、综合衡量，择优确定拟批准服现役的应征公民，并合理分配入伍去向。审定新兵的具体办法由国防部征兵办公室制定。

**Article 27** The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall convene a meeting to collectively finalize the new recruits. It shall conduct a classified assessment and comprehensive evaluation of the military vocational adaptability, educational level, physical and psychological quality of those who have passed the physical examination and political assessment, and select the best citizens to be approved for active service, and reasonably allocate their enlistment destinations. The specific measures for finalizing new recruits shall be formulated by the Conscription Office of the Ministry of National Defense.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　烈士、因公牺牲军人、病故军人的子女、兄弟姐妹和现役军人子女，本人自愿应征并且符合条件的，应当优先批准服现役。

**Article 28** The children, siblings of martyrs, servicemen who died in the line of duty, and servicemen who died of illness, as well as children of active-duty servicemen, who voluntarily enlist and meet the conditions, shall be given priority in being approved for active service.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　退出现役的士兵，本人自愿应征并且符合条件的，可以批准再次入伍，优先安排到原服现役单位或者同类型岗位服现役；具备任军士条件的，可以直接招收为军士。

**Article 29** Retired soldiers who voluntarily enlist and meet the conditions may be approved for re-enlistment and shall be given priority for assignment to their former service unit or a similar post; if they meet the conditions for serving as non-commissioned officers, they may be directly recruited as non-commissioned officers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室应当及时向社会公示拟批准服现役的应征公民名单，公示期不少于5个工作日。对被举报和反映有问题的拟批准服现役的应征公民，经调查核实不符合服现役条件或者有违反廉洁征兵有关规定情形的，取消入伍资格，出现的缺额从拟批准服现役的应征公民中依次递补。

**Article 30** The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall timely publish the list of citizens to be approved for active service to the public, with a public notice period of not less than 5 working days. For citizens to be approved for active service who have been reported or reflected as having problems, if investigation and verification confirm that they do not meet the conditions for active service or that there are circumstances violating the provisions on clean conscription, their enlistment qualifications shall be revoked, and the resulting vacancies shall be filled in order from among the citizens to be approved for active service.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公示期满，县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室应当为批准服现役的应征公民办理入伍手续，开具应征公民入伍批准书，发给入伍通知书，并通知其户籍所在地的户口登记机关。新兵自批准入伍之日起，按照规定享受现役军人有关待遇保障。新兵家属享受法律法规规定的义务兵家庭优待金和其他优待保障。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室应当为新兵建立入伍档案，将应征公民入伍批准书、应征公民政治考核表、应征公民体格检查表以及国防部征兵办公室规定的其他材料装入档案。

**Article 31** After the expiration of the public notice period, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall handle the enlistment procedures for citizens approved for active service, issue enlistment approval certificates, issue enlistment notices, and notify the household registration authority at their place of household registration. From the date of approval of enlistment, new recruits shall enjoy the relevant benefits and guarantees for active-duty servicemen in accordance with the provisions. The families of new recruits shall enjoy the preferential treatment for the families of conscripts and other preferential guarantees provided by laws and regulations.  
The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall establish enlistment files for new recruits, including the enlistment approval certificate, the political assessment form for citizens to be conscripted, the physical examination form for citizens to be conscripted, and other materials prescribed by the Conscription Office of the Ministry of National Defense.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府可以采取购买人身意外伤害保险等措施，为应征公民提供相应的权益保障。

**Article 32** The local people's government at or above the county level may adopt measures such as purchasing personal accident insurance to provide corresponding rights and interests protection for citizens to be conscripted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　已被普通高等学校录取或者正在普通高等学校就学的学生，被批准服现役的，服现役期间保留入学资格或者学籍，退出现役后两年内允许入学或者复学。

**Article 33** For a student who has been admitted to an ordinary institution of higher education or is currently studying at such an institution and is approved for active service, his admission qualification or student status shall be retained during the period of active service, and he shall be allowed to enroll or resume studies within two years after retirement from active service.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在征集期间，应征公民被征集服现役，同时被机关、团体、企业事业组织招录或者聘用的，应当优先履行服兵役义务；有关机关、团体、企业事业组织应当支持其应征入伍，有条件的应当允许其延后入职。  
被批准服现役的应征公民，是机关、团体、企业事业组织工作人员的，由原单位发给离职当月的全部工资、奖金及各种补贴。

**Article 34** During the conscription period, if a citizen to be conscripted is conscripted for active service and is simultaneously recruited or employed by an organ, organization, enterprise, or institution, he shall give priority to performing the obligation of military service; the relevant organ, organization, enterprise, or institution shall support his enlistment, and if conditions permit, shall allow him to defer taking up the post.  
If a citizen to be conscripted who is approved for active service is a staff member of an organ, organization, enterprise, or institution, his original unit shall pay him all wages, bonuses, and various subsidies for the month of his departure.

### 第六章　交接运输新兵

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　交接新兵采取兵役机关送兵、新兵自行报到以及部队派人领兵、接兵等方式进行。  
依托部队设立的新兵训练机构成规模集中组织新兵训练的，由兵役机关派人送兵或者新兵自行报到；对政治、身体条件或者专业技能有特别要求的兵员，通常由部队派人接兵；其他新兵通常由部队派人领兵。

**Article 35** The handover of new recruits shall be carried out by methods such as the military service organ sending the recruits, the recruits reporting on their own, or the military sending personnel to collect or receive the recruits.  
If a newly established recruit training institution affiliated with the military organizes centralized training on a large scale, the military service organ shall send personnel to deliver the recruits or the recruits shall report on their own; for recruits with special political, physical, or professional skill requirements, the military shall usually send personnel to receive them; for other recruits, the military shall usually send personnel to collect them.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在征兵开始日的15日前，军级以上单位应当派出联络组，与省级人民政府征兵办公室联系，商定补兵区域划分、新兵交接方式、被装保障、新兵运输等事宜。

**Article 36** Fifteen days before the start date of conscription, a unit at or above the military level shall send a liaison team to contact the conscription office of the provincial people's government to negotiate matters such as the division of recruitment areas, the method of handover of new recruits, equipment support, and transportation of new recruits.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　由兵役机关送兵的，应当做好下列工作：  
（一）省级人民政府征兵办公室与新兵训练机构商定送兵到达地点、途中转运和交接等有关事宜，制定送兵计划，明确送兵任务；  
（二）征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室于新兵起运前完成新兵档案审核并密封，出发前组织新兵与送兵人员集体见面；  
（三）新兵训练机构在驻地附近交通便利的车站、港口码头、机场设立接收点，负责接收新兵，并安全送达营区，于新兵到达营区24小时内与送兵人员办理完毕交接手续。

**Article 37** If the military service organ sends the recruits, the following work shall be done:  
(1) The conscription office of the provincial people's government shall negotiate with the recruit training institution on matters such as the arrival point, transit transfer, and handover, formulate a dispatch plan, and clarify dispatch tasks;  
(2) The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the place of recruitment shall complete the review and sealing of the recruits' files before the recruits depart, and organize a collective meeting between the recruits and the dispatch personnel before departure;  
(3) The recruit training institution shall set up a reception point at a conveniently located station, port, dock, or airport near its station, be responsible for receiving the recruits, and safely deliver them to the camp. The handover procedures with the dispatch personnel shall be completed within 24 hours of the recruits' arrival at the camp.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　由新兵自行报到的，应当做好下列工作：  
（一）县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室根据上级下达的计划，与新兵训练机构商定新兵报到地点、联系办法、档案交接和人员接收等有关事宜，及时向新兵训练机构通报新兵名单、人数、到达时间等事项；  
（二）县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室书面告知新兵报到地点、时限、联系办法、安全要求和其他注意事项；  
（三）新兵训练机构在新兵报到地点的车站、港口码头、机场设立报到处，组织接收新兵；  
（四）新兵训练机构将新兵实际到达时间、人员名单及时函告征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室；  
（五）新兵未能按时报到的，由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室查明情况，督促其尽快报到，并及时向新兵训练机构通报情况，无正当理由不按时报到或者不报到的，按照有关规定处理。

**Article 38** If the recruits report on their own, the following work shall be done:  
(1) The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall, based on the plan assigned by the superior level, negotiate with the recruit training institution on matters such as the reporting location, contact method, file handover, and personnel reception, and timely inform the recruit training institution of the list, number, and arrival time of the recruits;  
(2) The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall notify the recruits in writing of the reporting location, time limit, contact method, safety requirements, and other precautions;  
(3) The recruit training institution shall set up a registration point at the station, port, dock, or airport of the reporting location to organize the reception of new recruits;  
(4) The recruit training institution shall promptly inform the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in writing of the actual arrival time and list of recruits;  
(5) If a recruit fails to report on time, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall investigate the situation, urge him to report as soon as possible, and timely inform the recruit training institution of the situation; if there is no legitimate reason for failing to report on time or not reporting at all, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　由部队派人领兵的，应当做好下列工作：  
（一）领兵人员于新兵起运前7至10日内到达领兵地区，对新兵档案进行审核，与新兵集体见面，及时协商解决发现的问题。县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室于部队领兵人员到达后，及时将新兵档案提供给领兵人员；  
（二）交接双方于新兵起运前1日，在县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室所在地或者双方商定的交通便利的地点，一次性完成交接。

**Article 39** If the military sends personnel to collect recruits, the following work shall be done:  
(1) The collecting personnel shall arrive in the recruiting area 7 to 10 days before the recruits depart, review the recruits' files, meet with the recruits collectively, and timely negotiate to resolve any issues discovered. The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall provide the recruits' files to the collecting personnel promptly after their arrival;  
(2) The handover shall be completed at one time one day before the recruits depart, at the location of the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district, or at a mutually agreed location with convenient transportation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　由部队派人接兵的，应当做好下列工作：  
（一）接兵人员于征兵开始日前到达接兵地区，协助县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室开展工作，共同把好新兵质量关；  
（二）县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室向部队接兵人员介绍征兵工作情况，商定交接新兵等有关事宜；  
（三）交接双方在起运前完成新兵及其档案交接。

**Article 40** If the military sends personnel to receive recruits, the following work shall be done:  
(1) The receiving personnel shall arrive in the receiving area before the start date of conscription to assist the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in its work, and jointly ensure the quality of new recruits;  
(2) The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall introduce the situation of conscription work to the receiving personnel of the military and negotiate matters related to the handover of new recruits;  
(3) Both parties shall complete the handover of new recruits and their files before departure.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　兵役机关送兵和部队派人领兵、接兵的，在兵役机关与新兵训练机构、部队交接前发生的问题以兵役机关为主负责处理，交接后发生的问题以新兵训练机构或者部队为主负责处理。  
新兵自行报到的，新兵到达新兵训练机构前发生的问题以兵役机关为主负责处理，到达后发生的问题以新兵训练机构为主负责处理。

**Article 41** When the military service organ sends recruits or the military sends personnel to collect or receive recruits, any problems arising before the handover between the military service organ and the recruit training institution or military unit shall be mainly handled by the military service organ; any problems arising after the handover shall be mainly handled by the recruit training institution or military unit.  
When recruits report on their own, any problems arising before the recruits arrive at the recruit training institution shall be mainly handled by the military service organ; any problems arising after arrival shall be mainly handled by the recruit training institution.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　兵役机关送兵和部队派人领兵、接兵的，交接双方应当按照征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室统一编制的新兵花名册，清点人员，核对档案份数，当面点交清楚，并在新兵花名册上签名确认。交接双方在交接过程中，发现新兵人数、档案份数有问题的，应当协商解决后再办理交接手续；发现有其他问题的，先行办理交接手续，再按照有关规定处理。  
新兵自行报到的，档案由征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室自新兵起运后10日内通过机要邮寄或者派人送交新兵训练机构。

**Article 42** When the military service organ sends recruits or the military sends personnel to collect or receive recruits, both parties shall, in accordance with the new recruit roster uniformly compiled by the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the place of recruitment, count the persons, verify the number of files, hand them over clearly, and sign confirmation on the new recruit roster. If problems with the number of persons or files are discovered during the handover, both parties shall resolve them through negotiation before handling the handover procedures; if other problems are discovered, the handover procedures shall be handled first, and then the matters shall be processed according to the relevant provisions.  
When recruits report on their own, the files shall be sent by the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the place of recruitment to the recruit training institution via confidential mail or by personnel within 10 days after the recruits depart.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　新兵训练机构自收到新兵档案之日起5日内完成档案审查；部队领兵、接兵人员于新兵起运48小时前完成档案审查。档案审查发现问题的，函告或者当面告知征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室处理。  
对新兵档案中的问题，征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室自收到新兵训练机构公函之日起25日内处理完毕；部队领兵、接兵人员当面告知的，应当于新兵起运24小时前处理完毕。

**Article 43** The recruit training institution shall complete the file review within 5 days from the date of receiving the recruits' files; the military personnel collecting or receiving recruits shall complete the file review 48 hours before the recruits depart. If problems are found during the file review, the matter shall be communicated in writing or face-to-face to the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the place of recruitment for handling.  
For problems in the recruits' files, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the place of recruitment shall complete handling within 25 days from the date of receiving the official letter from the recruit training institution; if the military personnel collecting or receiving recruits inform the office face-to-face, the matter shall be handled within 24 hours before the recruits depart.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　新兵的被装，由军队被装调拨单位调拨到县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关指定地点，由县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关在新兵起运前发给新兵。

**Article 44** The equipment and clothing of new recruits shall be allocated by the military equipment and clothing allocation unit to the location designated by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district, and shall be issued to the new recruits by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district before the recruits depart.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中央军事委员会后勤保障部门应当会同国务院交通运输主管部门组织指导有关单位制定新兵运输计划。  
在征兵开始日后的5日内，省级人民政府征兵办公室应当根据新兵的人数和乘车、船、飞机起止地点，向联勤保障部队所属交通运输军事代表机构提出本行政区域新兵运输需求。

**Article 45** The Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall, together with the competent transport department of the State Council, organize and guide the relevant units to formulate the transport plan for new recruits.  
Within 5 days after the start date of conscription, the conscription office of the provincial people's government shall, based on the number of new recruits and the departure and arrival points by train, ship, or plane, propose the transport demand for new recruits within its administrative region to the transport military representative office attached to the Joint Logistics Support Force.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　联勤保障部队应当组织军地有关单位实施新兵运输计划。军地有关单位应当加强新兵运输工作协调配合，交通运输企业应当及时调配运力，保证新兵按照运输计划安全到达新兵训练机构或者部队。  
县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室和部队领兵、接兵人员，应当根据新兵运输计划按时组织新兵起运；在起运前，应当对新兵进行编组，并进行安全教育和检查，防止发生事故。  
交通运输军事代表机构以及沿途军用饮食供应站应当主动解决新兵运输中的有关问题。军用饮食供应站和送兵、领兵、接兵人员以及新兵应当接受交通运输军事代表机构的指导。

**Article 46** The Joint Logistics Support Force shall organize the relevant military and local units to implement the transport plan for new recruits. Military and local relevant units shall strengthen coordination and cooperation in the transport of new recruits, and transport enterprises shall timely allocate transport capacity to ensure that new recruits safely arrive at the recruit training institution or military unit in accordance with the transport plan.  
The conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district and the military personnel collecting or receiving recruits shall organize the departure of new recruits on time according to the transport plan; before departure, they shall organize new recruits into groups, conduct safety education and inspection to prevent accidents.  
The transport military representative office and the military food and supply stations along the route shall proactively resolve issues related to the transport of new recruits. The military food and supply stations, the dispatch personnel, and the military personnel collecting or receiving recruits, as well as the new recruits, shall accept the guidance of the transport military representative office.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　新兵起运时，有关地方人民政府应当组织欢送；新兵到达时，新兵训练机构或者部队应当组织欢迎。

**Article 47** When new recruits depart, the relevant local people's government shall organize farewell activities; when new recruits arrive, the recruit training institution or military unit shall organize welcome activities.

### 第七章　检疫、复查和退回

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　新兵到达新兵训练机构或者部队后，新兵训练机构或者部队应当按照规定组织新兵检疫和复查。经检疫发现新兵患传染病的，应当及时隔离治疗，并采取必要的防疫措施；经复查发现新兵入伍前有犯罪嫌疑的，应当采取必要的控制措施。

**Article 48** After new recruits arrive at the recruit training institution or military unit, the recruit training institution or military unit shall, in accordance with regulations, organize quarantine and re-examination of the new recruits. If infectious diseases are discovered during quarantine, the affected recruits shall be promptly isolated for treatment, and necessary epidemic prevention measures shall be taken; if a criminal suspicion before enlistment is discovered during re-examination, necessary control measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　经检疫和复查，发现新兵因身体原因不适宜服现役，或者政治情况不符合条件的，作退回处理。作退回处理的期限，自新兵到达新兵训练机构或者部队之日起，至有批准权的军队政治工作部门批准后向原征集地的设区的市级或者省级人民政府征兵办公室发函之日止，不超过45日。  
因身体原因退回的，须经军队医院检查证明，由旅级以上单位政治工作部门批准，并函告原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室。  
因政治原因退回的，新兵训练机构或者部队应当事先与原征集地的省级人民政府征兵办公室联系核查，确属不符合条件的，经旅级以上单位政治工作部门核实，由军级以上单位政治工作部门批准，并函告原征集地的省级人民政府征兵办公室。

**Article 49** If, after quarantine and re-examination, it is found that a new recruit is unfit for active service due to physical reasons, or does not meet the conditions due to political circumstances, he shall be returned. The period for such return shall not exceed 45 days, from the date the new recruit arrives at the recruit training institution or military unit to the date the military political work department with approval authority issues a letter to the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture or provincial level in the original place of recruitment after approval.  
For return due to physical reasons, it must be certified by a military hospital examination, approved by the political work department at the brigade level or above, and notified in writing to the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment.  
For return due to political reasons, the recruit training institution or military unit shall first contact and verify with the conscription office of the provincial people's government in the original place of recruitment; if it is indeed the case that the conditions are not met, after verification by the political work department at the brigade level or above and approval by the political work department at the military level or above, a letter shall be sent to the conscription office of the provincial people's government in the original place of recruitment.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　新兵自批准入伍之日起，至到达新兵训练机构或者部队后45日内，受伤或者患病的，军队医疗机构给予免费治疗，其中，可以治愈、不影响服现役的，不作退回处理；难以治愈或者治愈后影响服现役的，由旅级以上单位根据军队医院出具的认定结论，函告原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室，待病情稳定出院后作退回处理，退回时间不受限制。

**Article 50** If a new recruit is injured or becomes ill from the date of approval of enlistment to within 45 days after arrival at the recruit training institution or military unit, the military medical institution shall provide free treatment. If the injury or illness is curable and does not affect active service, no return shall be made; if it is difficult to cure or affects active service after cure, the brigade-level unit or above shall, based on the conclusion issued by the military hospital, notify the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment by letter, and after the condition stabilizes and the recruit is discharged from the hospital, the recruit shall be returned, without time limit for the return.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　退回人员返回原征集地后，由原征集地人民政府按照有关规定纳入社会保障体系，享受相应待遇。  
需回地方接续治疗的退回人员，旅级以上单位应当根据军队医院出具的证明，为其开具接续治疗函，并按照规定给予军人保险补偿；原征集地人民政府应当根据接续治疗函，安排有关医疗机构予以优先收治；已经参加当地基本医疗保险的，医疗费用按照规定由医保基金支付；符合医疗救助条件的，按照规定实施救助。

**Article 51** After the returned personnel return to the original place of recruitment, the original place of recruitment people's government shall incorporate them into the social security system in accordance with the relevant provisions and grant them corresponding benefits.  
For returned personnel who need follow-up treatment in the local area, the brigade-level unit or above shall, based on the certificate issued by the military hospital, issue a follow-up treatment letter for them and provide military insurance compensation in accordance with the provisions; the original place of recruitment people's government shall, based on the follow-up treatment letter, arrange for relevant medical institutions to give priority to admission; those who have already participated in the local basic medical insurance shall have their medical expenses paid by the medical insurance fund as stipulated; those who meet the conditions for medical assistance shall be given assistance in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　新兵作退回处理的，新兵训练机构或者部队应当做好退回人员的思想工作，派人将退回人员及其档案送回原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室；经与原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室协商达成一致，也可以由其接回退回人员及其档案。  
退回人员及其档案交接手续，应当自新兵训练机构、部队人员到达之日起7个工作日内，或者征兵办公室人员到达之日起7个工作日内办理完毕。

**Article 52** If a new recruit is returned, the recruit training institution or military unit shall provide ideological work for the returned person and send the returned person and his file back to the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment; if an agreement is reached through consultation with the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment, the conscription office may also take back the returned person and his file.  
The handover procedures for the returned person and his file shall be completed within 7 working days from the date of arrival of the personnel from the recruit training institution or military unit, or within 7 working days from the date of arrival of the conscription office personnel.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室应当及时核实退回原因以及有关情况，查验退回审批手续以及相关证明材料，核对新兵档案，按照国家和军队有关规定妥善保存和处置新兵档案。  
原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室对退回人员身体复查结果有异议的，按照规定向指定的医学鉴定机构提出鉴定申请；医学鉴定机构应当在5个工作日内完成鉴定工作，形成最终鉴定结论。经鉴定，符合退回条件的，由原征集地的设区的市级人民政府征兵办公室接收；不符合退回条件的，继续服现役。

**Article 53** The conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment shall timely verify the reasons for the return and the relevant circumstances, examine the return approval procedures and relevant certification materials, check the new recruit's file, and properly preserve and dispose of the new recruit's file in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state and the military.  
If the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment disagrees with the result of the physical re-examination of the returned person, it shall, in accordance with the provisions, submit an identification application to the designated medical identification institution; the medical identification institution shall complete the identification work within 5 working days and form a final identification conclusion. If the identification shows that the return conditions are met, the conscription office of the city divided into districts at the level of a prefecture in the original place of recruitment shall take over the person; if the return conditions are not met, the person shall continue to serve in active service.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对退回的人员，原征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室应当注销其应征公民入伍批准书，通知其户籍所在地的户口登记机关。

**Article 54** For returned personnel, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the original place of recruitment shall cancel their enlistment approval certificate and notify the household registration authority at their place of household registration.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　退回人员原是机关、团体、企业事业组织工作人员的，原单位应当按照有关规定准予复工、复职；原是已被普通高等学校录取或者正在普通高等学校就学的学生的，原学校应当按照有关规定准予入学或者复学。

**Article 55** If the returned person was originally a staff member of an organ, organization, enterprise, or institution, the original unit shall allow him to return to work or resume his post in accordance with the relevant provisions; if the returned person was originally a student who had been admitted to an ordinary institution of higher education or was studying at such an institution, the original school shall allow him to enroll or resume his studies in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　义务兵入伍前有下列行为之一的，作退回处理，作退回处理的期限不受[本条例第四十九条](#art-49)第一款的限制，因被征集服现役而取得的相关荣誉、待遇、抚恤优待以及其他利益，由有关部门予以取消、追缴：  
（一）入伍前有犯罪行为或者记录，故意隐瞒的；  
（二）入伍前患有精神类疾病、神经系统疾病、艾滋病（含病毒携带者）、恶性肿瘤等影响服现役的严重疾病，故意隐瞒的；  
（三）通过提供虚假入伍材料或者采取行贿等非法手段取得入伍资格的。  
按照前款规定作退回处理的，由军级以上单位政治工作部门函告原征集地的省级人民政府征兵办公室进行调查核实；情况属实的，报军级以上单位批准后，由原征集地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府征兵办公室负责接收。

**Article 56** If a conscript, before enlistment, commits any of the following acts, he shall be returned, and the time limit for the return shall not be subject to the restriction of the first paragraph of Article 49 of these Regulations. The relevant honorary titles, benefits, preferential treatment, and other interests obtained due to being conscripted for active service shall be canceled or recovered by the relevant departments:  
(1) Having a criminal act or record before enlistment, and intentionally concealing it;  
(2) Suffering from mental diseases, neurological diseases, AIDS (including virus carriers), malignant tumors, or other serious diseases affecting active service before enlistment, and intentionally concealing them;  
(3) Obtaining enlistment qualifications by providing false enlistment materials or by illegal means such as bribery.  
For a return made in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the political work department at the military level or above shall send a letter to the conscription office of the provincial people's government in the original place of recruitment for investigation and verification; if the situation is verified, after approval by the military level or above, the conscription office of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district in the original place of recruitment shall be responsible for receiving the person.

### 第八章　经费保障

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　开展征兵工作所需经费按照隶属关系分级保障。兵役征集费开支范围、管理使用办法，由中央军事委员会机关有关部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 57** The funds required for conducting conscription work shall be guaranteed at different levels according to the affiliation relationship. The scope of expenditure and management and use methods for conscription recruitment fees shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission organs together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　新兵被装调拨到县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关指定地点所需的费用，由军队被装调拨单位负责保障；县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府兵役机关下发新兵被装所需的运输费列入兵役征集费开支。

**Article 58** The expenses required for the allocation of new recruit equipment and clothing to the location designated by the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district shall be borne by the military equipment and clothing allocation unit; the transport costs for the military service organ of the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district to issue the new recruit equipment and clothing shall be included in the conscription recruitment fee expenditure.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　征集的新兵，实行兵役机关送兵或者新兵自行报到的，从县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区新兵集中点前往新兵训练机构途中所需的车船费、伙食费、住宿费，由新兵训练机构按照规定报销；部队派人领兵、接兵的，自部队接收之日起，所需费用由部队负责保障。军队有关部门按照统一组织实施的军事运输安排产生的运费，依照有关规定结算支付。

**Article 59** For newly recruited soldiers, if the military service organ sends the recruits or the recruits report on their own, the fares for vehicles and ships, meal expenses, and accommodation expenses incurred on the way from the assembly point of new recruits in the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district to the recruit training institution shall be reimbursed by the recruit training institution in accordance with the provisions; if the military sends personnel to collect or receive the recruits, from the date of acceptance by the military, the required expenses shall be borne by the military. The freight incurred in the transportation arrangements organized by the relevant military departments in accordance with a unified plan shall be settled and paid in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　送兵人员同新兵一起前往新兵训练机构途中所需的差旅费，由新兵训练机构按照规定报销；送兵人员在新兵训练机构办理新兵交接期间，住宿由新兵训练机构负责保障，伙食补助费和返回的差旅费列入兵役征集费开支。

**Article 60** The travel expenses incurred by the dispatch personnel traveling together with the new recruits to the recruit training institution shall be reimbursed by the recruit training institution in accordance with the provisions; during the period when the dispatch personnel handle the handover of new recruits at the recruit training institution, accommodation shall be provided by the recruit training institution, and the meal subsidies and return travel expenses shall be included in the conscription recruitment fee expenditure.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　新兵训练机构或者部队退回不合格新兵的费用，在与有关地方人民政府征兵办公室办理退回手续之前，由新兵训练机构或者部队负责；办理退回手续之后，新兵训练机构或者部队人员返回的差旅费由其所在单位按照规定报销，其他费用由有关地方人民政府征兵办公室负责。

**Article 61** The expenses for the return of unqualified new recruits by the recruit training institution or military unit shall be borne by the recruit training institution or military unit before the return procedures with the relevant local people's government conscription office are handled; after the return procedures are handled, the travel expenses for the return of the personnel from the recruit training institution or military unit shall be reimbursed by their units in accordance with the provisions, and other expenses shall be borne by the relevant local people's government conscription office.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　义务兵家庭优待金按照国家有关规定由中央财政和地方财政共同负担，实行城乡统一标准，由批准入伍地的县、自治县、不设区的市、市辖区人民政府按照规定发放。  
县级以上人民政府征兵办公室应当向本级财政、退役军人工作主管部门提供当年批准入伍人数，用于制定义务兵家庭优待金分配方案。

**Article 62** The preferential treatment funds for the families of conscripts shall be jointly borne by the central finance and local finance in accordance with the provisions of the state, and shall be implemented according to a unified urban and rural standard. They shall be issued in accordance with the provisions by the people's government of the county, autonomous county, city not divided into districts, or municipal district where the enlistment is approved.  
The conscription office of the people's government at or above the county level shall provide the number of persons approved for enlistment in the current year to the finance department and the competent veterans affairs department at the same level, for use in formulating the distribution plan for the preferential treatment funds for the families of conscripts.

### 第九章　战时征集

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家发布动员令或者国务院、中央军事委员会依法采取国防动员措施后，各级人民政府和军事机关必须按照要求组织战时征集。

**Article 63** After the state issues a mobilization order or the State Council and the Central Military Commission take national defense mobilization measures according to law, the people's governments at all levels and military organs must organize wartime conscription as required.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　战时根据需要，国务院和中央军事委员会可以在法律规定的范围内调整征集公民服现役的条件和办法。  
战时根据需要，可以重点征集退役军人，补充到原服现役单位或者同类型岗位。

**Article 64** During wartime, as needed, the State Council and the Central Military Commission may, within the scope prescribed by law, adjust the conditions and methods for conscripting citizens for active service.  
During wartime, as needed, retired soldiers may be given priority in recruitment and assigned to their former service unit or similar posts.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国防部征兵办公室根据战时兵员补充需求，指导县级以上地方人民政府征兵办公室按照战时征集的条件和办法组织实施征集工作。

**Article 65** The Conscription Office of the Ministry of National Defense shall, based on the needs of wartime personnel replenishment, guide the conscription offices of local people's governments at or above the county level to organize and implement the conscription work in accordance with the conditions and methods for wartime conscription.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　应征公民接到兵役机关的战时征集通知后，必须按期到指定地点参加应征。  
机关、团体、企业事业组织和乡、民族乡、镇的人民政府以及街道办事处必须组织本单位和本行政区域战时征集对象，按照规定的时间、地点报到。  
从事交通运输的单位和个人，应当优先运送战时征集对象；其他组织和个人应当为战时征集对象报到提供便利。

**Article 66** After receiving a wartime conscription notice from the military service organ, citizens to be conscripted must report to the designated location on time for conscription.  
Organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and the people's governments of townships, ethnic townships, towns, and sub-district offices must organize the wartime conscription targets within their units and administrative regions to report at the specified time and place.  
Units and individuals engaged in transportation shall give priority to transporting wartime conscription targets; other organizations and individuals shall provide convenience for wartime conscription targets to report.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　有服兵役义务的公民拒绝、逃避兵役登记的，应征公民拒绝、逃避征集服现役的，依法给予处罚。  
新兵以逃避服兵役为目的，拒绝履行职责或者逃离部队的，依法给予处分或者处罚。

**Article 67** If a citizen with the obligation to perform military service refuses or evades military service registration, or a citizen to be conscripted refuses or evades conscription for active service, he shall be punished according to law.  
If a new recruit, for the purpose of evading military service, refuses to perform duties or deserts the military, he shall be given a sanction or punishment according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　机关、团体、企业事业组织拒绝完成征兵任务的，阻挠公民履行兵役义务的，或者有其他妨害征兵工作行为的，对单位及负有责任的人员，依法给予处罚。

**Article 68** If an organ, organization, enterprise, or institution refuses to complete the conscription task, obstructs a citizen from performing the obligation of military service, or otherwise hinders the conscription work, the unit and the responsible persons shall be punished according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家工作人员、军队人员在征兵工作中，有贪污贿赂、徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守以及其他违反征兵工作规定行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 69** If a state functionary or military personnel, in the course of conscription work, commits acts of embezzlement, bribery, favoritism, fraud, abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or other acts violating the provisions on conscription work, they shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 70** If a violation of the provisions of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　[本条例第六十七条](#art-67)、第六十八条规定的处罚，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关会同有关部门查明事实，经同级地方人民政府作出处罚决定后，由县级以上地方人民政府兵役机关、发展改革、公安、卫生健康、教育、人力资源社会保障等部门按照职责分工具体执行。

**Article 71** The punishments prescribed in Articles 67 and 68 of these Regulations shall be carried out by the military service organ of the local people's government at or above the county level, together with the relevant departments, after ascertaining the facts. After the local people's government at the same level makes a punishment decision, the military service organ, development and reform, public security, health, education, human resources and social security and other departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall specifically execute the punishment according to their respective duties.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　征集公民到中国人民武装警察部队服现役的工作，适用本条例。

**Article 72** These Regulations apply to the work of conscripting citizens for active service in the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　从非军事部门招收现役军官（警官）、军士（警士）的体格检查、政治考核、办理入伍手续等工作，参照本条例有关规定执行。

**Article 73** The physical examination, political assessment, and enlistment procedures for recruiting active-duty officers (police officers) and non-commissioned officers (police sergeants) from non-military sectors shall be carried out with reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本条例自2023年5月1日起施行。

**Article 74** These Regulations shall come into force on May 1, 2023.
