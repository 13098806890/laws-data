# 全民阅读促进条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-12-09  
**生效日期**：2026-02-01

> 第十六条　报刊出版单位、通讯社、广播电台、电视台、新闻网站等新闻媒体应当通过新闻报道、专题栏目（节目）、公益广告等方式，宣传优秀出版物和全民阅读先进典型，推广全民阅读活动，营造全民阅读氛围。 第十七条　国家鼓励促进全民阅读的新技术、新载体、新设施等开发与应用，支持利用信息技术开展全民阅读推广活动，扩大全民阅读推广活动影响力。 第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，在规范和引导的基础上支持社会力量开展全民阅读推广活动。 社会力量开展全民阅读推广活动，应当接受县级以上新闻出版主管部门的指导、监督。 第三章　全民阅读服务 第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据基本公共文化服务有关要求，加强相关场所、设施统筹利用，科学合理规划全民阅读设施建设，有计划地设置覆盖城乡、实用便利、服务高效的全民阅读设施。 全民阅读设施主要包括政府或者社会力量举办的向公众提供阅读服务的公共图书馆、新时代文明实践中心（所、站）、工人文化宫、青少年宫、妇女儿童活动中心、老年人活动中心、乡镇（街道）和村（社区）基层综合性文化服务中心、社区图书室、农家（职工）书屋、公共阅报栏（屏）、公共数字阅读终端等场所和设备。 第二十条　全民阅读设施管理单位应当根据全民阅读设施的功能、特点，向公众提供阅读、借阅、阅读指导、阅读能力培训等服务，公示服务项目，改善服务条件，提高服务水平。公共图书馆应当免费向公众开放阅览室、自习室，配备书桌、座椅，提供相关阅读服务。 全民阅读设施管理单位应当按照国家规定的标准，配置和更新必需的服务内容和设备，加强全民阅读设施经常性维护管理工作，保障全民阅读设施的正常运行和使用。 符合条件的全民阅读设施应当按照国家有关规定免费或者优惠向公众开放，公示开放时间；有条件的全民阅读设施可以在国家法定节假日、休息日和学校寒暑假期间适当延长开放时间。 全民阅读设施管理单位应当建立健全安全管理制度，依法配备安全保护设备和人员，保障全民阅读设施和公众活动安全。 第二十一条　新建、改建和扩建居民住宅区配套建设的公共文化设施应当包括全民阅读设施，为居民提供阅读服务。 第二十二条　任何单位和个人不得擅自拆除全民阅读设施，不得擅自改变全民阅读设施功能、用途或者妨碍其正常运行。因城乡建设确需拆除或者改变功能、用途的，应当依法重建、改建全民阅读设施。 第二十三条　国家鼓励车站、机场、港口客运站、游客中心、宾馆、银行、商场、医院等公共服务机构和场所设立相应的阅读设施，支持在有条件的飞机、轮船、火车等公共交通工具上提供便利可及的阅读服务。 公园、旅游景区的管理单位可以根据自身条件，安排适宜的阅读设施。

### 正文

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励促进全民阅读的新技术、新载体、新设施等开发与应用，支持利用信息技术开展全民阅读推广活动，扩大全民阅读推广活动影响力。

**Article 17** The State shall encourage the development and application of new technologies, new carriers, and new facilities that promote nationwide reading, and shall support the use of information technology to carry out nationwide reading promotion activities, thereby expanding the influence of nationwide reading promotion activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，在规范和引导的基础上支持社会力量开展全民阅读推广活动。  
社会力量开展全民阅读推广活动，应当接受县级以上新闻出版主管部门的指导、监督。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with relevant provisions of the State, support social forces in carrying out nationwide reading promotion activities on the basis of regulation and guidance.  
Social forces carrying out nationwide reading promotion activities shall accept the guidance and supervision of the competent department of press and publication at or above the county level.

### 第三章　全民阅读服务

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据基本公共文化服务有关要求，加强相关场所、设施统筹利用，科学合理规划全民阅读设施建设，有计划地设置覆盖城乡、实用便利、服务高效的全民阅读设施。  
全民阅读设施主要包括政府或者社会力量举办的向公众提供阅读服务的公共图书馆、新时代文明实践中心（所、站）、工人文化宫、青少年宫、妇女儿童活动中心、老年人活动中心、乡镇（街道）和村（社区）基层综合性文化服务中心、社区图书室、农家（职工）书屋、公共阅报栏（屏）、公共数字阅读终端等场所和设备。

**Article 19** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant requirements regarding basic public cultural services, strengthen the integrated utilization of relevant venues and facilities, scientifically and rationally plan the construction of nationwide reading facilities, and set up nationwide reading facilities that cover both urban and rural areas, are practical and convenient, and provide efficient services in a planned manner.  
Nationwide reading facilities mainly include venues and equipment such as public libraries, New Era Civilization Practice Centers (stations, points), Workers' Cultural Palaces, Youth Palaces, Women's and Children's Activity Centers, Elderly Activity Centers, township (street) and village (community) grassroots comprehensive cultural service centers, community libraries, farm (staff) bookrooms, public newspaper reading boards (screens), and public digital reading terminals, which are operated by the government or social forces to provide reading services to the public.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　全民阅读设施管理单位应当根据全民阅读设施的功能、特点，向公众提供阅读、借阅、阅读指导、阅读能力培训等服务，公示服务项目，改善服务条件，提高服务水平。公共图书馆应当免费向公众开放阅览室、自习室，配备书桌、座椅，提供相关阅读服务。  
全民阅读设施管理单位应当按照国家规定的标准，配置和更新必需的服务内容和设备，加强全民阅读设施经常性维护管理工作，保障全民阅读设施的正常运行和使用。  
符合条件的全民阅读设施应当按照国家有关规定免费或者优惠向公众开放，公示开放时间；有条件的全民阅读设施可以在国家法定节假日、休息日和学校寒暑假期间适当延长开放时间。  
全民阅读设施管理单位应当建立健全安全管理制度，依法配备安全保护设备和人员，保障全民阅读设施和公众活动安全。

**Article 20** The management unit of a nationwide reading facility shall, based on the functions and characteristics of the facility, provide the public with services such as reading, borrowing, reading guidance, and reading ability training, publish the service items, improve service conditions, and enhance service levels. Public libraries shall open reading rooms and study rooms to the public free of charge, equip them with desks and chairs, and provide relevant reading services.  
The management unit of a nationwide reading facility shall, in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, configure and update the necessary service contents and equipment, strengthen the routine maintenance and management of the nationwide reading facility, and ensure its normal operation and use.  
Qualified nationwide reading facilities shall be opened to the public free of charge or at a discount in accordance with relevant provisions of the State, with their opening hours published; where conditions permit, nationwide reading facilities may appropriately extend their opening hours during statutory holidays, rest days, and winter and summer vacations of schools.  
The management unit of a nationwide reading facility shall establish and improve a safety management system, equip safety protection equipment and personnel according to law, and ensure the safety of the nationwide reading facility and public activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　新建、改建和扩建居民住宅区配套建设的公共文化设施应当包括全民阅读设施，为居民提供阅读服务。

**Article 21** The public cultural facilities constructed as supporting facilities for newly built, renovated, or expanded residential areas shall include nationwide reading facilities to provide reading services for residents.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位和个人不得擅自拆除全民阅读设施，不得擅自改变全民阅读设施功能、用途或者妨碍其正常运行。因城乡建设确需拆除或者改变功能、用途的，应当依法重建、改建全民阅读设施。

**Article 22** No unit or individual shall dismantle nationwide reading facilities without authorization, or change the functions or purposes of nationwide reading facilities or impede their normal operation. Where removal or alteration of functions or purposes is truly necessary due to urban and rural construction, the nationwide reading facilities shall be rebuilt or renovated according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家鼓励车站、机场、港口客运站、游客中心、宾馆、银行、商场、医院等公共服务机构和场所设立相应的阅读设施，支持在有条件的飞机、轮船、火车等公共交通工具上提供便利可及的阅读服务。  
公园、旅游景区的管理单位可以根据自身条件，安排适宜的阅读设施。

**Article 23** The State shall encourage public service institutions and venues such as railway stations, airports, port passenger terminals, tourist centers, hotels, banks, shopping malls, and hospitals to establish corresponding reading facilities, and shall support the provision of convenient and accessible reading services on public transport vehicles such as aircraft, ships, and trains where conditions permit.  
The management units of parks and tourist attractions may, based on their own conditions, arrange appropriate reading facilities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励机关、学校、企业事业单位等结合自身条件和实际需要，通过设置图书馆、阅览室、报刊架、阅报栏（屏）等，提供多种形式的阅读服务。

**Article 24** The State shall encourage state organs, schools, enterprises, and public institutions, among others, to provide various forms of reading services by setting up libraries, reading rooms, newspaper racks, newspaper reading boards (screens), etc., based on their own conditions and actual needs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府根据实际情况，采取政策措施支持实体书店发展；鼓励实体书店改善阅读条件、开展阅读活动，发挥全民阅读服务功能。

**Article 25** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on actual circumstances, adopt policy measures to support the development of physical bookstores; they shall encourage physical bookstores to improve reading conditions, carry out reading activities, and give play to their function of serving nationwide reading.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家支持数字阅读与传统阅读相结合，推动优质数字阅读内容供给，提升数字阅读便利性和满意度。  
数字阅读服务提供者应当加强数字阅读内容管理，推送优质数字阅读内容，营造健康向上的数字阅读环境。

**Article 26** The State shall support the combination of digital reading and traditional reading, promote the supply of high-quality digital reading content, and enhance the convenience and satisfaction of digital reading.  
Digital reading service providers shall strengthen the management of digital reading content, push high-quality digital reading content, and create a healthy and positive digital reading environment.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励和支持与全民阅读促进相关的文化产业高质量发展；鼓励和支持社会力量参与建设公共阅读空间，开展旧书交换流通，创新阅读服务模式，丰富全民阅读场景。  
国家鼓励和支持社会力量依法通过兴办实体、资助项目、赞助活动、提供设施、捐赠出版物等多种方式，参与提供阅读服务。

**Article 27** The State encourages and supports the high-quality development of the cultural industry related to the promotion of nationwide reading; encourages and supports social forces to participate in the construction of public reading spaces, to carry out the exchange and circulation of used books, to innovate reading service models, and to enrich the scenes of nationwide reading.  
The State encourages and supports social forces to participate in providing reading services according to law through various means such as establishing entities, funding projects, sponsoring activities, providing facilities, and donating publications.

### 第四章　全民阅读保障

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家加强全民阅读保障，扩大全民阅读覆盖面，重点扶助农村地区、革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、欠发达地区的全民阅读，促进全民阅读均衡协调发展。

**Article 28** The State strengthens the guarantee for nationwide reading, expands the coverage of nationwide reading, and gives priority to supporting nationwide reading in rural areas, old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and underdeveloped areas, and promotes balanced and coordinated development of nationwide reading.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家新闻出版主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定少年儿童阅读计划，针对少年儿童的心智发展水平、认知理解能力推广阶梯阅读，开展与其阅读特点和规律相适应的阅读指导活动。  
出版单位应当根据阶梯阅读的要求，有针对性地出版适宜不同年龄段少年儿童阅读的出版物。  
政府设立的公共图书馆应当设置少年儿童阅览区域，根据少年儿童的特点配备相应的专业人员，开展面向少年儿童的阅读指导。

**Article 29** The competent national press and publication department, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate a reading plan for children, promoting graded reading based on children's mental development level and cognitive comprehension ability, and conduct reading guidance activities that suit their reading characteristics and patterns.  
Publishing entities shall, in accordance with the requirements of graded reading, publish targeted publications suitable for children of different age groups.  
Public libraries established by the government shall set up reading areas for children, equip them with corresponding professionals based on the characteristics of children, and carry out reading guidance for children.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　未成年人的父母或者其他监护人应当发挥言传身教作用，开展力所能及的家庭阅读、亲子阅读等，帮助未成年人养成良好阅读习惯。

**Article 30** Parents or other guardians of minors shall play a role of teaching by words and deeds, conduct family reading and parent-child reading within their capabilities, and help minors develop good reading habits.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　幼儿园应当为幼儿提供适宜的读物，创设良好的阅读环境条件，培养幼儿阅读兴趣和阅读习惯。

**Article 31** Kindergartens shall provide suitable reading materials for young children, create good reading environment and conditions, and cultivate young children's reading interest and reading habits.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　中小学校应当按照素质教育的要求，加强书香校园建设，加大阅读在教学计划中的分量，开设阅读课程，开展阅读辅导，组织校园阅读活动，帮助学生养成阅读习惯，并加强对教师的阅读指导培训，提高教师的阅读指导能力。

**Article 32** Primary and secondary schools shall, in accordance with the requirements of quality-oriented education, strengthen the construction of bookish campuses, increase the weight of reading in teaching plans, offer reading courses, conduct reading tutoring, organize campus reading activities, help students develop reading habits, and strengthen reading guidance training for teachers to improve their reading guidance ability.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　高等学校应当将阅读作为重要的教育教学方式，组织学生开展多种形式的阅读活动，鼓励学生拓展阅读内容，丰富学生精神世界，提高学生综合素养。

**Article 33** Institutions of higher education shall take reading as an important educational teaching method, organize students to carry out reading activities in various forms, encourage students to expand their reading content, enrich their spiritual world, and improve their comprehensive competence.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家新闻出版主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定乡村阅读计划。县级以上地方新闻出版主管部门会同同级有关部门强化农村阅读促进工作职责，增加农村阅读内容供给，优化农村阅读环境条件，提高农村阅读服务效能，加强农村妇女儿童阅读服务保障，推动城乡基本公共文化服务均等化，丰富农村地区精神生活。  
面向农村提供的出版物应当符合农村特点和需求，提高针对性和时效性。

**Article 34** The competent national press and publication department, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate a rural reading plan. The local press and publication departments at or above the county level, together with the relevant departments at the same level, shall strengthen their responsibilities for promoting reading in rural areas, increase the supply of reading content in rural areas, optimize the reading environment and conditions in rural areas, improve the efficiency of reading services in rural areas, strengthen the guarantee of reading services for rural women and children, promote the equalization of basic public cultural services between urban and rural areas, and enrich the spiritual life in rural areas.  
Publications provided for rural areas shall conform to the characteristics and needs of rural areas, and improve their relevance and timeliness.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家提高各民族群众使用国家通用语言文字阅读的能力，支持民族地区开展使用国家通用语言文字的阅读活动，促进民族团结进步，铸牢中华民族共同体意识。

**Article 35** The State improves the ability of people of all ethnic groups to read using the standard spoken and written Chinese language, supports ethnic minority areas in carrying out reading activities using the standard spoken and written Chinese language, promotes ethnic unity and progress, and solidifies the sense of community for the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家将书香城市、书香村镇作为精神文明建设重要内容，持续涵育全社会阅读风尚，推进城市文明建设和文明乡风建设。

**Article 36** The State regards bookish cities, bookish villages and towns as important content of the construction of spiritual civilization, continuously cultivates the reading atmosphere of the whole society, and promotes the construction of urban civilization and the building of civilized rural customs.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励为阅读障碍者提供有声、大字、盲文、电子等无障碍格式版本的出版物，支持全民阅读无障碍设施建设。  
残疾人服务机构应当为残疾人提供必要阅读辅助设施和相应服务。

**Article 37** The State shall encourage the provision of publications in accessible formats such as audio, large print, braille, and electronic versions for persons with reading disabilities, and shall support the construction of accessible facilities for national reading.  
Disability service organizations shall provide necessary reading auxiliary facilities and corresponding services for persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　全民阅读设施管理单位应当考虑老年人阅读需求和特点，提供适老阅读内容，优化适老服务标准，为老年人提供阅读便利。  
有条件的老年大学（学校）、为老服务机构等应当开展老年人阅读活动，提高阅读服务水平。

**Article 38** The management units of national reading facilities shall consider the reading needs and characteristics of the elderly, provide age-appropriate reading content, optimize service standards for the elderly, and provide reading convenience for the elderly.  
Where conditions permit, universities for the elderly (schools), service institutions for the elderly, and others shall carry out reading activities for the elderly and improve the level of reading services.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家鼓励开展全民阅读研究，指导开展公民阅读状况调查，支持开展全民阅读国际交流合作。

**Article 39** The State shall encourage research on national reading, guide the conduct of surveys on citizens' reading status, and support international exchanges and cooperation on national reading.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　通过公益性社会组织或者县级以上人民政府及其部门捐赠财产用于全民阅读服务的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 40** Any entity that donates property for national reading services through a public welfare social organization or through the people's government at or above the county level and its departments shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上新闻出版主管部门会同同级有关部门依法开展阅读活动监管，优化阅读环境。  
任何单位和个人不得在阅读活动中损害国家利益、社会公共利益、他人合法权益或者公民身心健康。

**Article 41** The competent department of press and publication at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments at the same level, conduct supervision of reading activities according to law and optimize the reading environment.  
No entity or individual may harm the national interest, public interest, lawful rights and interests of others, or the physical and mental health of citizens in reading activities.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，地方各级人民政府和县级以上有关部门及其工作人员未履行全民阅读促进工作职责，侵占、挪用全民阅读经费，或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，由其上级机关或者监察机关责令限期改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments at or above the county level and their staff fail to perform their duties in promoting national reading, misappropriate or divert national reading funds, or commit other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, the superior organ or the supervisory organ shall order them to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be punished according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，全民阅读设施管理单位未按要求向公众开放，未公示服务项目、开放时间等事项，未建立安全管理制度或者未配备安全保护设备和人员，开展功能、用途不符的服务活动的，由其主管部门责令限期改正；造成严重后果的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the management unit of a national reading facility fails to open to the public as required, fails to publicly disclose service items, opening hours, and other matters, fails to establish a safety management system or fails to equip safety protection equipment and personnel, or conducts service activities that do not match its functions and purposes, the competent authority shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if serious consequences are caused, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be punished according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，损害他人民事权益的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Any person who, in violation of these Regulations, damages the civil rights and interests of others shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2026年2月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2026.
