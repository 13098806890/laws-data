# 营业性演出管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (2005年7月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第439号公布　根据2008年7月22日《国务院关于修改〈营业性演出管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订  根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对营业性演出的管理，促进文化产业的发展，繁荣社会主义文艺事业，满足人民群众文化生活的需要，促进社会主义精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of commercial performances, promote the development of the cultural industry, enrich the socialist literary and artistic cause, meet the cultural needs of the people, and promote the construction of socialist spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称营业性演出，是指以营利为目的为公众举办的现场文艺表演活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "commercial performance" means a live literary and artistic performance activity held for the public for profit-making purposes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　营业性演出必须坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向，把社会效益放在首位、实现社会效益和经济效益的统一，丰富人民群众的文化生活。

**Article 3** Commercial performances must adhere to the direction of serving the people and serving socialism, give priority to social benefits, achieve the unity of social and economic benefits, and enrich the cultural life of the people.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励文艺表演团体、演员创作和演出思想性艺术性统一、体现民族优秀文化传统、受人民群众欢迎的优秀节目，鼓励到农村、工矿企业演出和为少年儿童提供免费或者优惠的演出。

**Article 4** The State encourages troupes and performers to create and perform excellent programs that unify ideological and artistic quality, embody the fine cultural traditions of the nation, and are welcomed by the people; and encourages performances in rural areas, industrial and mining enterprises, and the provision of free or preferential performances for children and adolescents.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院文化主管部门主管全国营业性演出的监督管理工作。国务院公安部门、工商行政管理部门在各自职责范围内，主管营业性演出的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门负责本行政区域内营业性演出的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府公安部门、工商行政管理部门在各自职责范围内，负责本行政区域内营业性演出的监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The cultural administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of commercial performances nationwide. The public security department and the administrative department for industry and commerce of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be in charge of the supervision and administration of commercial performances.  
The cultural administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of commercial performances within their respective administrative regions. The public security departments and the administrative departments for industry and commerce of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of commercial performances within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　营业性演出经营主体的设立

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　文艺表演团体申请从事营业性演出活动，应当有与其业务相适应的专职演员和器材设备，并向县级人民政府文化主管部门提出申请；演出经纪机构申请从事营业性演出经营活动，应当有3名以上专职演出经纪人员和与其业务相适应的资金，并向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门提出申请。文化主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出决定。批准的，颁发营业性演出许可证；不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 6** A troupe applying to engage in commercial performance activities shall have full-time performers and equipment and facilities commensurate with its business, and shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the county people's government. A performance brokerage agency applying to engage in commercial performance business activities shall have no fewer than three full-time performance brokers and funds commensurate with its business, and shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The cultural administrative department shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of accepting the application. If approved, it shall issue a Commercial Performance Permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立演出场所经营单位，应当依法到工商行政管理部门办理注册登记，领取营业执照，并依照有关消防、卫生管理等法律、行政法规的规定办理审批手续。  
演出场所经营单位应当自领取营业执照之日起20日内向所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门备案。

**Article 7** To establish a performance venue operating unit, it shall, according to law, undergo registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, obtain a business license, and undergo approval procedures in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on fire control, health administration, etc.  
The performance venue operating unit shall, within 20 days from the date of obtaining the business license, file for record with the cultural administrative department of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　文艺表演团体变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人、营业性演出经营项目，应当向原发证机关申请换发营业性演出许可证，并依法到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记。  
演出场所经营单位变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人，应当依法到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记，并向原备案机关重新备案。

**Article 8** If a troupe changes its name, domicile, legal representative or principal responsible person, or commercial performance business items, it shall apply to the original issuing authority for a replacement of the Commercial Performance Permit, and undergo change registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.  
If a performance venue operating unit changes its name, domicile, legal representative or principal responsible person, it shall undergo change registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law, and re-file for record with the original record-filing authority.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　以从事营业性演出为职业的个体演员(以下简称个体演员)和以从事营业性演出的居间、代理活动为职业的个体演出经纪人(以下简称个体演出经纪人)，应当依法到工商行政管理部门办理注册登记，领取营业执照。  
个体演员、个体演出经纪人应当自领取营业执照之日起20日内向所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门备案。

**Article 9** Individual performers who engage in commercial performances as a profession (hereinafter referred to as "individual performers") and individual performance brokers who engage in intermediation and agency activities of commercial performances as a profession (hereinafter referred to as "individual performance brokers") shall, according to law, undergo registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license.  
Individual performers and individual performance brokers shall, within 20 days from the date of obtaining the business license, file for record with the cultural administrative department of the local county people's government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外国投资者可以依法在中国境内设立演出经纪机构、演出场所经营单位；不得设立文艺表演团体。  
外商投资的演出经纪机构申请从事营业性演出经营活动、外商投资的演出场所经营单位申请从事演出场所经营活动，应当向国务院文化主管部门提出申请。国务院文化主管部门应当自收到申请之日起20日内作出决定。批准的，颁发营业性演出许可证；不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 10** Foreign investors may, according to law, establish performance brokerage agencies and performance venue operating units within the territory of China; they shall not establish troupes.  
A foreign-invested performance brokerage agency applying to engage in commercial performance business activities, or a foreign-invested performance venue operating unit applying to engage in performance venue business activities, shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the State Council. The cultural administrative department of the State Council shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of receiving the application. If approved, it shall issue a Commercial Performance Permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的投资者可以在内地投资设立演出经纪机构、演出场所经营单位以及由内地方控股的文艺表演团体；香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的演出经纪机构可以在内地设立分支机构。  
台湾地区的投资者可以在大陆投资设立演出经纪机构、演出场所经营单位，不得设立文艺表演团体。  
依照本条规定设立的演出经纪机构、文艺表演团体申请从事营业性演出经营活动，依照本条规定设立的演出场所经营单位申请从事演出场所经营活动，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门应当自收到申请之日起20日内作出决定。批准的，颁发营业性演出许可证；不批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
依照本条规定设立演出经纪机构、演出场所经营单位的，还应当遵守我国其他法律、法规的规定。

**Article 11** Investors from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region may invest in establishing performance brokerage agencies, performance venue operating units, and troupes controlled by mainland parties within the mainland; performance brokerage agencies from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region may establish branches within the mainland.  
Investors from the Taiwan Region may invest in establishing performance brokerage agencies and performance venue operating units within the mainland, but shall not establish troupes.  
A performance brokerage agency or troupe established in accordance with this Article applying to engage in commercial performance business activities, or a performance venue operating unit established in accordance with this Article applying to engage in performance venue business activities, shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of receiving the application. If approved, it shall issue a Commercial Performance Permit; if not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The establishment of a performance brokerage agency or performance venue operating unit in accordance with this Article shall also comply with the provisions of other laws and regulations of China.

### 第三章　营业性演出规范

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　文艺表演团体、个体演员可以自行举办营业性演出，也可以参加营业性组台演出。  
营业性组台演出应当由演出经纪机构举办；但是，演出场所经营单位可以在本单位经营的场所内举办营业性组台演出。  
演出经纪机构可以从事营业性演出的居间、代理、行纪活动；个体演出经纪人只能从事营业性演出的居间、代理活动。

**Article 12** Troupes and individual performers may hold commercial performances on their own, and may also participate in commercial combined performances.  
Commercial combined performances shall be held by performance brokerage agencies; however, performance venue operating units may hold commercial combined performances within the venues operated by them.  
Performance brokerage agencies may engage in intermediation, agency, and commission activities for commercial performances; individual performance brokers may only engage in intermediation and agency activities for commercial performances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　举办营业性演出，应当向演出所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门提出申请。县级人民政府文化主管部门应当自受理申请之日起3日内作出决定。对符合[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的，发给批准文件；对不符合[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的，不予批准，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 13** To hold a commercial performance, an application shall be submitted to the cultural administrative department of the county people's government of the place where the performance is to be held. The cultural administrative department of the county people's government shall make a decision within 3 days from the date of accepting the application. If it complies with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall issue an approval document; if it does not comply with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall not grant approval, and shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　除演出经纪机构外，其他任何单位或者个人不得举办外国的或者香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出。但是，文艺表演团体自行举办营业性演出，可以邀请外国的或者香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的文艺表演团体、个人参加。  
举办外国的或者香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)有与其举办的营业性演出相适应的资金；  
(二)有2年以上举办营业性演出的经历；  
(三)举办营业性演出前2年内无违反本条例规定的记录。

**Article 14** Except for performance brokerage agencies, no other unit or individual may hold commercial performances in which foreign troupes or individuals, or troupes or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, or Taiwan Region participate. However, when a troupe holds a commercial performance on its own, it may invite foreign troupes or individuals, or troupes or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, or Taiwan Region to participate.  
To hold a commercial performance in which foreign troupes or individuals, or troupes or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, or Taiwan Region participate, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having funds commensurate with the commercial performance to be held;  
(2) having experience of holding commercial performances for at least two years;  
(3) having no record of violating the provisions of these Regulations within the two years before holding the commercial performance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　举办外国的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出，演出举办单位应当向演出所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门提出申请。  
举办香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出，演出举办单位应当向演出所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门提出申请；举办台湾地区的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出，演出举办单位应当向国务院文化主管部门会同国务院有关部门规定的审批机关提出申请。  
国务院文化主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出决定。对符合[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的，发给批准文件；对不符合[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定的，不予批准，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 15** To hold a commercial performance in which foreign troupes or individuals participate, the performance sponsor shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the performance is to be held.  
To hold a commercial performance in which troupes or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or Macao Special Administrative Region participate, the performance sponsor shall submit an application to the cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the performance is to be held; to hold a commercial performance in which troupes or individuals from the Taiwan Region participate, the performance sponsor shall submit an application to the approval authority designated by the cultural administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The cultural administrative department of the State Council or the cultural administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of accepting the application. If it complies with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall issue an approval document; if it does not comply with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall not grant approval, and shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请举办营业性演出，提交的申请材料应当包括下列内容：  
(一)演出名称、演出举办单位和参加演出的文艺表演团体、演员；  
(二)演出时间、地点、场次；  
(三)节目及其视听资料。  
申请举办营业性组台演出，还应当提交文艺表演团体、演员同意参加演出的书面函件。  
营业性演出需要变更申请材料所列事项的，应当分别依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)、第十五条规定重新报批。

**Article 16** The application materials submitted for applying to hold a commercial performance shall include the following:  
(1) the name of the performance, the performance sponsor, and the troupes and performers participating in the performance;  
(2) the time, place, and number of performances;  
(3) the programs and their audio-visual materials.  
When applying to hold a commercial combined performance, a written letter of consent from the troupes and performers agreeing to participate in the performance shall also be submitted.  
If matters listed in the application materials need to be changed for a commercial performance, a new application shall be submitted in accordance with the provisions of Articles 13 and 15 of these Regulations respectively.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　演出场所经营单位提供演出场地，应当核验演出举办单位取得的批准文件；不得为未经批准的营业性演出提供演出场地。

**Article 17** When providing a performance venue, the performance venue operating unit shall verify the approval document obtained by the performance sponsor; it shall not provide the venue for commercial performances that have not been approved.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　演出场所经营单位应当确保演出场所的建筑、设施符合国家安全标准和消防安全规范，定期检查消防安全设施状况，并及时维护、更新。  
演出场所经营单位应当制定安全保卫工作方案和灭火、应急疏散预案。  
演出举办单位在演出场所进行营业性演出，应当核验演出场所经营单位的消防安全设施检查记录、安全保卫工作方案和灭火、应急疏散预案，并与演出场所经营单位就演出活动中突发安全事件的防范、处理等事项签订安全责任协议。

**Article 18** A performance venue operating unit shall ensure that the buildings and facilities of the performance venue comply with national safety standards and fire safety regulations, regularly inspect the condition of fire safety facilities, and promptly maintain and update them.  
A performance venue operating unit shall formulate a security plan and fire-fighting and emergency evacuation plans.  
When a performance sponsor conducts a commercial performance at a performance venue, it shall verify the fire safety facility inspection records, security plan, and fire-fighting and emergency evacuation plans of the performance venue operating unit, and sign a safety responsibility agreement with the performance venue operating unit on matters such as the prevention and handling of emergency safety incidents during the performance.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在公共场所举办营业性演出，演出举办单位应当依照有关安全、消防的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定办理审批手续，并制定安全保卫工作方案和灭火、应急疏散预案。演出场所应当配备应急广播、照明设施，在安全出入口设置明显标识，保证安全出入口畅通；需要临时搭建舞台、看台的，演出举办单位应当按照国家有关安全标准搭建舞台、看台，确保安全。

**Article 19** When holding a commercial performance in a public place, the performance sponsor shall undergo approval procedures in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations, and state provisions on safety and fire control, and formulate a security plan and fire-fighting and emergency evacuation plans. The performance venue shall be equipped with emergency broadcasting and lighting facilities, set up clear signs at safety exits, and ensure that safety exits are unimpeded; if a stage or bleachers need to be temporarily erected, the performance sponsor shall erect them in accordance with the relevant national safety standards to ensure safety.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　审批临时搭建舞台、看台的营业性演出时，文化主管部门应当核验演出举办单位的下列文件：  
(一)依法验收后取得的演出场所合格证明；  
(二)安全保卫工作方案和灭火、应急疏散预案；  
(三)依法取得的安全、消防批准文件。

**Article 20** When approving a commercial performance with temporarily erected stages or bleachers, the cultural administrative department shall verify the following documents of the performance sponsor:  
(1) the certificate of conformity for the performance venue obtained after acceptance inspection according to law;  
(2) the security plan and fire-fighting and emergency evacuation plans;  
(3) the safety and fire control approval documents obtained according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　演出场所容纳的观众数量应当报公安部门核准；观众区域与缓冲区域应当由公安部门划定，缓冲区域应当有明显标识。  
演出举办单位应当按照公安部门核准的观众数量、划定的观众区域印制和出售门票。  
验票时，发现进入演出场所的观众达到核准数量仍有观众等待入场的，应当立即终止验票并同时向演出所在地县级人民政府公安部门报告；发现观众持有观众区域以外的门票或者假票的，应当拒绝其入场并同时向演出所在地县级人民政府公安部门报告。

**Article 21** The number of spectators that a performance venue can accommodate shall be reported to the public security department for approval; the spectator area and the buffer area shall be demarcated by the public security department, and the buffer area shall have clear markings.  
The performance organizer shall print and sell tickets in accordance with the number of spectators approved by the public security department and the demarcated spectator area.  
If, during ticket checking, it is found that the number of spectators entering the performance venue has reached the approved number and there are still spectators waiting for admission, the ticket checking shall be terminated immediately and the local public security department of the people's government at the county level where the performance is held shall be reported at the same time; if it is found that a spectator holds a ticket for an area outside the spectator area or a counterfeit ticket, such spectator shall be refused admission and the local public security department of the people's government at the county level where the performance is held shall be reported at the same time.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何人不得携带传染病病原体和爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、腐蚀性等危险物质或者非法携带枪支、弹药、管制器具进入营业性演出现场。  
演出场所经营单位应当根据公安部门的要求，配备安全检查设施，并对进入营业性演出现场的观众进行必要的安全检查；观众不接受安全检查或者有前款禁止行为的，演出场所经营单位有权拒绝其进入。

**Article 22** No person may carry infectious disease pathogens or explosive, flammable, radioactive, corrosive, or other dangerous substances, or illegally carry firearms, ammunition, or controlled instruments into a commercial performance site.  
The operator of the performance venue shall, in accordance with the requirements of the public security department, install security inspection facilities and carry out necessary security inspections on spectators entering the commercial performance site; if a spectator refuses to undergo security inspection or commits any act prohibited in the preceding paragraph, the operator of the performance venue shall have the right to refuse such spectator admission.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　演出举办单位应当组织人员落实营业性演出时的安全、消防措施，维护营业性演出现场秩序。  
演出举办单位和演出场所经营单位发现营业性演出现场秩序混乱，应当立即采取措施并同时向演出所在地县级人民政府公安部门报告。

**Article 23** The performance organizer shall arrange personnel to implement safety and fire prevention measures during the commercial performance and maintain order at the commercial performance site.  
If the performance organizer or the operator of the performance venue finds that order at the commercial performance site is chaotic, it shall immediately take measures and simultaneously report to the public security department of the people's government at the county level where the performance is held.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　演出举办单位不得以政府或者政府部门的名义举办营业性演出。  
营业性演出不得冠以“中国”、“中华”、“全国”、“国际”等字样。  
营业性演出广告内容必须真实、合法，不得误导、欺骗公众。

**Article 24** A performance organizer shall not hold a commercial performance in the name of the government or a government department.  
A commercial performance shall not be prefixed with such words as "China", "Zhonghua", "National", or "International".  
The content of advertisements for commercial performances must be truthful and lawful, and shall not mislead or deceive the public.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　营业性演出不得有下列情形：  
(一)反对宪法确定的基本原则的；  
(二)危害国家统一、主权和领土完整，危害国家安全，或者损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
(三)煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，侵害民族风俗习惯，伤害民族感情，破坏民族团结，违反宗教政策的；  
(四)扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
(五)危害社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
(六)宣扬淫秽、色情、邪教、迷信或者渲染暴力的；  
(七)侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
(八)表演方式恐怖、残忍，摧残演员身心健康的；  
(九)利用人体缺陷或者以展示人体变异等方式招徕观众的；  
(十)法律、行政法规禁止的其他情形。

**Article 25** No commercial performance shall have any of the following circumstances:  
(1) opposing the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) endangering national unity, sovereignty, and territorial integrity, jeopardizing national security, or harming national honor or interests;  
(3) inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, infringing upon ethnic customs and habits, hurting ethnic feelings, undermining ethnic unity, or violating religious policies;  
(4) disturbing social order or undermining social stability;  
(5) endangering social morality or the fine cultural traditions of the nation;  
(6) promoting obscenity, pornography, cults, superstition, or glorifying violence;  
(7) insulting or defaming others, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(8) using performance methods that are terrifying or cruel, or that harm the physical or mental health of performers;  
(9) attracting spectators by exploiting human defects or by displaying human variations; or  
(10) other circumstances prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　演出场所经营单位、演出举办单位发现营业性演出有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止情形的，应当立即采取措施予以制止并同时向演出所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门报告。

**Article 26** If the operator of a performance venue or the performance organizer discovers that a commercial performance involves any circumstance prohibited in Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall immediately take measures to stop it and simultaneously report to the competent cultural department and the public security department of the people's government at the county level where the performance is held.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　参加营业性演出的文艺表演团体、主要演员或者主要节目内容等发生变更的，演出举办单位应当及时告知观众并说明理由。观众有权退票。  
演出过程中，除因不可抗力不能演出的外，演出举办单位不得中止或者停止演出，演员不得退出演出。

**Article 27** If the performing arts troupe, principal performers, or main program content participating in a commercial performance changes, the performance organizer shall promptly inform the spectators and explain the reasons. Spectators shall have the right to refund tickets.  
During the performance, except where the performance cannot be held due to force majeure, the performance organizer shall not suspend or stop the performance, and performers shall not withdraw from the performance.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　演员不得以假唱欺骗观众，演出举办单位不得组织演员假唱。任何单位或者个人不得为假唱提供条件。  
演出举办单位应当派专人对演出进行监督，防止假唱行为的发生。

**Article 28** Performers shall not deceive spectators by lip-syncing, and performance organizers shall not organize performers to lip-sync. No unit or individual may provide conditions for lip-syncing.  
The performance organizer shall assign special personnel to supervise the performance to prevent the occurrence of lip-syncing.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　营业性演出经营主体应当对其营业性演出的经营收入依法纳税。  
演出举办单位在支付演员、职员的演出报酬时应当依法履行税款代扣代缴义务。

**Article 29** Commercial performance business entities shall pay taxes according to law on their operating income from commercial performances.  
When paying performance remuneration to performers and staff, the performance organizer shall fulfill the obligation to withhold and remit taxes according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　募捐义演的演出收入，除必要的成本开支外，必须全部交付受捐单位；演出举办单位、参加演出的文艺表演团体和演员、职员，不得获取经济利益。

**Article 30** The performance income from charitable fundraising performances must, except for necessary cost expenses, be fully delivered to the recipient unit; the performance organizer, the performing arts troupe participating in the performance, and the performers and staff shall not obtain economic benefits.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位或者个人不得伪造、变造、出租、出借或者买卖营业性演出许可证、批准文件或者营业执照，不得伪造、变造营业性演出门票或者倒卖伪造、变造的营业性演出门票。

**Article 31** No unit or individual may forge, alter, lease, lend, or sell a commercial performance permit, approval document, or business license, nor may they forge or alter commercial performance tickets or scalp forged or altered commercial performance tickets.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　除文化主管部门依照国家有关规定对体现民族特色和国家水准的演出给予补助外，各级人民政府和政府部门不得资助、赞助或者变相资助、赞助营业性演出，不得用公款购买营业性演出门票用于个人消费。

**Article 32** Except where the competent cultural department provides subsidies to performances that reflect national characteristics and national standards in accordance with relevant state regulations, the people's governments at all levels and government departments shall not subsidize, sponsor, or indirectly subsidize or sponsor commercial performances, nor shall they use public funds to purchase commercial performance tickets for personal consumption.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　文化主管部门应当加强对营业性演出的监督管理。  
演出所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门对外国的或者香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的文艺表演团体、个人参加的营业性演出和临时搭建舞台、看台的营业性演出，应当进行实地检查；对其他营业性演出，应当进行实地抽样检查。

**Article 33** The competent cultural department shall strengthen the supervision and administration of commercial performances.  
The competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level where the performance is held shall conduct on-site inspections of commercial performances involving the participation of foreign performing arts troupes or individuals or those from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, or Taiwan region, and of commercial performances with temporarily erected stages and viewing platforms; for other commercial performances, it shall conduct on-site sample inspections.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门应当充分发挥文化执法机构的作用，并可以聘请社会义务监督员对营业性演出进行监督。  
任何单位或者个人可以采取电话、手机短信等方式举报违反本条例规定的行为。县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门应当向社会公布举报电话，并保证随时有人接听。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门接到社会义务监督员的报告或者公众的举报，应当作出记录，立即赶赴现场进行调查、处理，并自处理完毕之日起7日内公布结果。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化主管部门对作出突出贡献的社会义务监督员应当给予表彰；公众举报经调查核实的，应当对举报人给予奖励。

**Article 34** The competent cultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give full play to the role of cultural law enforcement agencies, and may appoint voluntary social supervisors to supervise commercial performances.  
Any unit or individual may report acts violating the provisions of these Regulations by telephone, text message, or other means. The competent cultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall make the reporting telephone number available to the public and ensure that it is answered at all times.  
Upon receiving a report from a voluntary social supervisor or a complaint from the public, the competent cultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall make a record, immediately go to the scene to investigate and handle the matter, and publish the results within seven days from the date of completing the handling.  
The competent cultural department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall commend voluntary social supervisors who make outstanding contributions; if a complaint from the public is verified through investigation, the whistleblower shall be rewarded.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　文化主管部门应当建立营业性演出经营主体的经营活动信用监管制度，建立健全信用约束机制，并及时公布行政处罚信息。

**Article 35** The competent cultural department shall establish a credit supervision system for the business activities of commercial performance business entities, establish and improve credit constraint mechanisms, and promptly publish administrative penalty information.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公安部门对其依照有关法律、行政法规和国家有关规定批准的营业性演出，应当在演出举办前对营业性演出现场的安全状况进行实地检查；发现安全隐患的，在消除安全隐患后方可允许进行营业性演出。  
公安部门可以对进入营业性演出现场的观众进行必要的安全检查；发现观众有[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)第一款禁止行为的，在消除安全隐患后方可允许其进入。  
公安部门可以组织警力协助演出举办单位维持营业性演出现场秩序。

**Article 36** For commercial performances approved by the public security department in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and state regulations, the public security department shall conduct on-site inspections of the safety conditions of the commercial performance site before the performance; if any safety hazards are found, the commercial performance may be permitted only after the safety hazards are eliminated.  
The public security department may carry out necessary security inspections on spectators entering the commercial performance site; if a spectator is found to have committed any of the acts prohibited in the first paragraph of Article 22 of these Regulations, such spectator may be allowed entry only after the safety hazards are eliminated.  
The public security department may organize police forces to assist the performance organizer in maintaining order at the commercial performance site.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公安部门接到观众达到核准数量仍有观众等待入场或者演出秩序混乱的报告后，应当立即组织采取措施消除安全隐患。

**Article 37** After receiving a report that the number of spectators has reached the approved number and there are still spectators waiting for admission, or that the performance order is chaotic, the public security department shall immediately organize measures to eliminate safety hazards.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　承担现场管理检查任务的公安部门和文化主管部门的工作人员进入营业性演出现场，应当出示值勤证件。

**Article 38** Personnel of the public security department and the competent cultural department who undertake on-site management and inspection tasks shall present their duty identification when entering the commercial performance site.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　文化主管部门依法对营业性演出进行监督检查时，应当将监督检查的情况和处理结果予以记录，由监督检查人员签字后归档。公众有权查阅监督检查记录。

**Article 39** When carrying out supervision and inspection of commercial performances according to law, the competent cultural department shall record the circumstances and results of the supervision and inspection, and file them after being signed by the supervision and inspection personnel. The public shall have the right to consult the supervision and inspection records.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员不得向演出举办单位、演出场所经营单位索取演出门票。

**Article 40** The competent cultural department, public security department, other relevant departments, and their staff shall not demand performance tickets from the performance organizer or the operator of the performance venue.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院文化主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门，对在农村、工矿企业进行演出以及为少年儿童提供免费或者优惠演出表现突出的文艺表演团体、演员，应当给予表彰，并采取多种形式予以宣传。  
国务院文化主管部门对适合在农村、工矿企业演出的节目，可以在依法取得著作权人许可后，提供给文艺表演团体、演员在农村、工矿企业演出时使用。  
文化主管部门实施文艺评奖，应当适当考虑参评对象在农村、工矿企业的演出场次。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当对在农村、工矿企业演出的文艺表演团体、演员给予支持。

**Article 41** The competent cultural department of the State Council and the competent cultural departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall commend the art performance troupes and performers that have outstanding achievements in performing in rural areas and industrial and mining enterprises, and in providing free or discounted performances for children and adolescents, and shall publicize them through various means.  
The competent cultural department of the State Council may, after obtaining permission from the copyright owner according to law, provide programs suitable for performances in rural areas and industrial and mining enterprises to art performance troupes and performers for use in such performances.  
When implementing arts awards, the competent cultural departments shall give appropriate consideration to the number of performances given by the candidates in rural areas and industrial and mining enterprises.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall support art performance troupes and performers that perform in rural areas and industrial and mining enterprises.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　演出行业协会应当依照章程的规定，制定行业自律规范，指导、监督会员的经营活动，促进公平竞争。

**Article 42** The performing arts industry association shall, in accordance with its articles of association, formulate industry self-regulatory norms, guide and supervise the business activities of its members, and promote fair competition.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门予以取缔，没收演出器材和违法所得，并处违法所得8倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)、第十条、第十一条规定，擅自从事营业性演出经营活动的；  
(二)违反[本条例第十二条](#art-12)、第十四条规定，超范围从事营业性演出经营活动的；  
(三)违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一款规定，变更营业性演出经营项目未向原发证机关申请换发营业性演出许可证的。  
违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)、第九条规定，擅自设立演出场所经营单位或者擅自从事营业性演出经营活动的，由工商行政管理部门依法予以取缔、处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where any of the following acts is committed, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall ban it, confiscate the performance equipment and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than eight times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) engaging in commercial performance business activities without authorization in violation of the provisions of Articles 6, 10 and 11 of these Regulations;  
(2) engaging in commercial performance business activities beyond the approved scope in violation of the provisions of Articles 12 and 14 of these Regulations;  
(3) failing to apply to the original license-issuing authority for a new commercial performance license after changing the commercial performance business project in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 8 of these Regulations.  
Where a performance venue operation entity is established without authorization or commercial performance business activities are engaged in without authorization in violation of the provisions of Articles 7 and 9 of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall ban and punish it according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)、第十五条规定，未经批准举办营业性演出的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令停止演出，没收违法所得，并处违法所得8倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销营业性演出许可证。  
违反[本条例第十六条](#art-16)第三款规定，变更演出举办单位、参加演出的文艺表演团体、演员或者节目未重新报批的，依照前款规定处罚；变更演出的名称、时间、地点、场次未重新报批的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，给予警告，可以并处3万元以下的罚款。  
演出场所经营单位为未经批准的营业性演出提供场地的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处3万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 44** Where a commercial performance is held without approval in violation of the provisions of Articles 13 and 15 of these Regulations, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order the performance to stop, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than eight times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the commercial performance license.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of the third paragraph of Article 16 of these Regulations, the performance organizer, the art performance troupe, the performer or the program participating in the performance is changed without re-approval, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph; where the name, time, place or number of performances is changed without re-approval, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan.  
Where a performance venue operation entity provides a venue for an unapproved commercial performance, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定，伪造、变造、出租、出借、买卖营业性演出许可证、批准文件，或者以非法手段取得营业性演出许可证、批准文件的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门没收违法所得，并处违法所得8倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对原取得的营业性演出许可证、批准文件，予以吊销、撤销；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 31 of these Regulations, a commercial performance license or approval document is forged, altered, leased, lent, bought or sold, or such license or approval document is obtained by illegal means, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than eight times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; the originally obtained commercial performance license or approval document shall be revoked or canceled; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　营业性演出有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止情形的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令停止演出，没收违法所得，并处违法所得8倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销营业性演出许可证；违反治安管理规定的，由公安部门依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
演出场所经营单位、演出举办单位发现营业性演出有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止情形未采取措施予以制止的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门依据法定职权给予警告，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；未依照[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定报告的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门依据法定职权给予警告，并处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Where a commercial performance contains any of the prohibited circumstances specified in Article 25 of these Regulations, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order the performance to stop, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than eight times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the commercial performance license; if the provisions on public security administration are violated, the public security department shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a performance venue operation entity or a performance organizer discovers that a commercial performance has any of the prohibited circumstances specified in Article 25 of these Regulations but fails to take measures to stop it, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level or the public security department shall, within its statutory functions and powers, give a warning and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; where it fails to report in accordance with the provisions of Article 26 of these Regulations, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level or the public security department shall, within its statutory functions and powers, give a warning and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列行为之一的，对演出举办单位、文艺表演团体、演员，由国务院文化主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门向社会公布；演出举办单位、文艺表演团体在2年内再次被公布的，由原发证机关吊销营业性演出许可证；个体演员在2年内再次被公布的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照：  
(一)非因不可抗力中止、停止或者退出演出的；  
(二)文艺表演团体、主要演员或者主要节目内容等发生变更未及时告知观众的；  
(三)以假唱欺骗观众的；  
(四)为演员假唱提供条件的。  
有前款第(一)项、第(二)项和第(三)项所列行为之一的，观众有权在退场后依照有关消费者权益保护的法律规定要求演出举办单位赔偿损失；演出举办单位可以依法向负有责任的文艺表演团体、演员追偿。  
有本条第一款第(一)项、第(二)项和第(三)项所列行为之一的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有本条第一款第(四)项所列行为的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where any of the following acts is committed, the performance organizer, art performance troupe or performer shall be publicly announced by the competent cultural department of the State Council or the competent cultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; if the performance organizer or art performance troupe is again publicly announced within two years, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the commercial performance license; if an individual performer is again publicly announced within two years, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license:  
(1) suspending, stopping or withdrawing from a performance not due to force majeure;  
(2) failing to inform the audience in a timely manner of changes in the art performance troupe, principal performers or main program content;  
(3) deceiving the audience with lip-syncing;  
(4) providing conditions for performers to lip-sync.  
Where any of the acts listed in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph is committed, the audience shall have the right to demand compensation for losses from the performance organizer in accordance with the laws on the protection of consumer rights and interests after leaving the venue; the performance organizer may seek recourse from the art performance troupe or performer that bears responsibility according to law.  
Where any of the acts listed in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of the first paragraph of this Article is committed, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; where the act listed in subparagraph (4) of the first paragraph of this Article is committed, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　以政府或者政府部门的名义举办营业性演出，或者营业性演出冠以“中国”、“中华”、“全国”、“国际”等字样的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处3万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，由原发证机关吊销营业性演出许可证。  
营业性演出广告的内容误导、欺骗公众或者含有其他违法内容的，由工商行政管理部门责令停止发布，并依法予以处罚。

**Article 48** Where a commercial performance is held in the name of the government or a government department, or a commercial performance is prefixed with words such as "China", "Chinese", "National" or "International", the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the party refuses to correct or causes serious consequences, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the commercial performance license.  
Where the content of a commercial performance advertisement misleads or deceives the public or contains other illegal content, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order the cessation of publication and impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　演出举办单位或者其法定代表人、主要负责人及其他直接责任人员在募捐义演中获取经济利益的，由县级以上人民政府文化主管部门依据各自职权责令其退回并交付受捐单位；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上人民政府文化主管部门依据各自职权处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款，并由国务院文化主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门向社会公布违法行为人的名称或者姓名，直至由原发证机关吊销演出举办单位的营业性演出许可证。  
文艺表演团体或者演员、职员在募捐义演中获取经济利益的，由县级以上人民政府文化主管部门依据各自职权责令其退回并交付受捐单位。

**Article 49** Where a performance organizer or its legal representative, principal responsible person or other directly responsible personnel obtains economic benefits from a charity performance, the competent cultural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and powers, order it or him/her to return and deliver the benefits to the beneficiary unit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, the competent cultural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and powers, impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains, and the competent cultural department of the State Council or the competent cultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall publicly announce the name of the violator, until the original license-issuing authority revokes the commercial performance license of the performance organizer.  
Where an art performance troupe, performer or staff member obtains economic benefits from a charity performance, the competent cultural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and powers, order it or him/her to return and deliver the benefits to the beneficiary unit.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一款规定，变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人未向原发证机关申请换发营业性演出许可证的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，给予警告，并处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款。  
违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)第二款、第八条第二款、第九条第二款规定，未办理备案手续的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，给予警告，并处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 8 of these Regulations, the name, domicile, legal representative or principal responsible person is changed without applying to the original license-issuing authority for a new commercial performance license, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 7, the second paragraph of Article 8, or the second paragraph of Article 9 of these Regulations, the formalities for recordation are not completed, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　有下列行为之一的，由公安部门或者公安消防机构依据法定职权依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违反本条例安全、消防管理规定的；  
(二)伪造、变造营业性演出门票或者倒卖伪造、变造的营业性演出门票的。  
演出举办单位印制、出售超过核准观众数量的或者观众区域以外的营业性演出门票的，由县级以上人民政府公安部门依据各自职权责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处3万元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证机关吊销营业性演出许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where any of the following acts is committed, the public security department or the public security fire control institution shall impose a penalty according to law within its statutory functions and powers; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) violating the provisions on safety and fire control management of these Regulations;  
(2) forging or altering commercial performance tickets, or scalping forged or altered commercial performance tickets.  
Where a performance organizer prints or sells commercial performance tickets in excess of the approved number of spectators or beyond the spectator area, the public security department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and powers, order correction, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if serious consequences are caused, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the commercial performance license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　演出场所经营单位、个体演出经纪人、个体演员违反本条例规定，情节严重的，由县级以上人民政府文化主管部门依据各自职权责令其停止营业性演出经营活动，并通知工商行政管理部门，由工商行政管理部门依法吊销营业执照。其中，演出场所经营单位有其他经营业务的，由工商行政管理部门责令其办理变更登记，逾期不办理的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 52** Where a performance venue operation entity, an individual performance broker or an individual performer violates the provisions of these Regulations, and the circumstances are serious, the competent cultural department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and powers, order it or him/her to cease commercial performance business activities, and notify the administrative department for industry and commerce, which shall revoke the business license according to law. If the performance venue operation entity has other business operations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to undergo alteration registration, and if it fails to do so within the time limit, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　因违反本条例规定被文化主管部门吊销营业性演出许可证，或者被工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照或者责令变更登记的，自受到行政处罚之日起，当事人为单位的，其法定代表人、主要负责人5年内不得担任文艺表演团体、演出经纪机构或者演出场所经营单位的法定代表人、主要负责人；当事人为个人的，个体演员1年内不得从事营业性演出，个体演出经纪人5年内不得从事营业性演出的居间、代理活动。  
因营业性演出有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止情形被文化主管部门吊销营业性演出许可证，或者被工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照或者责令变更登记的，不得再次从事营业性演出或者营业性演出的居间、代理、行纪活动。  
因违反本条例规定2年内2次受到行政处罚又有应受本条例处罚的违法行为的，应当从重处罚。

**Article 53** Where a commercial performance license is revoked by the competent cultural department, or a business license is revoked or alteration registration is ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce for violation of these Regulations, from the date of receipt of the administrative penalty, if the party is an entity, its legal representative and principal responsible person shall not serve as the legal representative or principal responsible person of any art performance troupe, performance brokerage institution or performance venue operation entity within five years; if the party is an individual, an individual performer shall not engage in commercial performances within one year, and an individual performance broker shall not engage in intermediary or agency activities for commercial performances within five years.  
Where a commercial performance license is revoked by the competent cultural department, or a business license is revoked or alteration registration is ordered by the administrative department for industry and commerce because the commercial performance contains any of the prohibited circumstances specified in Article 25 of these Regulations, the party shall not again engage in commercial performances or intermediary, agency or brokerage activities for commercial performances.  
Where a party has been subject to administrative penalty twice within two years for violation of these Regulations and commits another illegal act subject to penalty under these Regulations, a severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各级人民政府或者政府部门非法资助、赞助，或者非法变相资助、赞助营业性演出，或者用公款购买营业性演出门票用于个人消费的，依照有关财政违法行为处罚处分的行政法规的规定责令改正。对单位给予警告或者通报批评。对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 54** Where a people's government at any level or a government department illegally provides financial assistance or sponsorship, or illegally provides disguised financial assistance or sponsorship to a commercial performance, or uses public funds to purchase commercial performance tickets for personal consumption, it shall be ordered to correct in accordance with the provisions of the administrative regulations on punishment and sanction for fiscal illegal acts. A warning or circular criticism shall be given to the entity. The directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be given a major demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, expulsion shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　文化主管部门、公安部门、工商行政管理部门的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者未依照本条例规定履行职责的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where a staff member of the competent cultural department, the public security department or the administrative department for industry and commerce abuses his or her powers, neglects his or her duties, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or fails to perform his or her duties in accordance with these Regulations, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　民间游散艺人的营业性演出，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以参照本条例的规定制定具体管理办法。

**Article 56** For commercial performances by itinerant folk artists, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate specific management measures by reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本条例自2005年9月1日起施行。1997年8月11日国务院发布的《营业性演出管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 57** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2005. The Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Performances promulgated by the State Council on August 11, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
