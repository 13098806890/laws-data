# 全民健身条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2009-10-01

> (2009年8月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第560号公布　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进全民健身活动的开展，保障公民在全民健身活动中的合法权益，提高公民身体素质，制定本条例。 第二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将全民健身事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，有计划地建设公共体育设施，加大对农村地区和城市社区等基层公共体育设施建设的投入，促进全民健身事业均衡协调发展。 国家支持、鼓励、推动与人民群众生活水平相适应的体育消费以及体育产业的发展。 第三条　国家推动基层文化体育组织建设，鼓励体育类社会团体、体育类民办非企业单位等群众性体育组织开展全民健身活动。 第四条　公民有依法参加全民健身活动的权利。 地方各级人民政府应当依法保障公民参加全民健身活动的权利。 第五条　国务院体育主管部门负责全国的全民健身工作，国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的全民健身工作。 县级以上地方人民政府主管体育工作的部门(以下简称体育主管部门)负责本行政区域内的全民健身工作，县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的全民健身工作。 第六条　国家鼓励对全民健身事业提供捐赠和赞助。 自然人、法人或者其他组织对全民健身事业提供捐赠的，依法享受税收优惠。 第七条　对在发展全民健身事业中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。 第二章　全民健身计划 第八条　国务院制定全民健身计划，明确全民健身工作的目标、任务、措施、保障等内容。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进全民健身活动的开展，保障公民在全民健身活动中的合法权益，提高公民身体素质，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of promoting the development of national fitness activities, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens in national fitness activities, and improving citizens' physical fitness.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将全民健身事业纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，有计划地建设公共体育设施，加大对农村地区和城市社区等基层公共体育设施建设的投入，促进全民健身事业均衡协调发展。  
国家支持、鼓励、推动与人民群众生活水平相适应的体育消费以及体育产业的发展。

**Article 2** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the national fitness cause into the national economic and social development plan of the corresponding level, plan the construction of public sports facilities in a systematic manner, increase investment in the construction of grassroots public sports facilities in rural areas and urban communities, and promote the balanced and coordinated development of the national fitness cause.  
The State supports, encourages, and promotes sports consumption and the development of the sports industry in line with the living standards of the people.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家推动基层文化体育组织建设，鼓励体育类社会团体、体育类民办非企业单位等群众性体育组织开展全民健身活动。

**Article 3** The State promotes the construction of grassroots cultural and sports organizations, and encourages mass sports organizations such as sports social groups and sports private non-enterprise units to carry out national fitness activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公民有依法参加全民健身活动的权利。  
地方各级人民政府应当依法保障公民参加全民健身活动的权利。

**Article 4** Citizens have the right to participate in national fitness activities according to law.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall, according to law, guarantee the right of citizens to participate in national fitness activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院体育主管部门负责全国的全民健身工作，国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的全民健身工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府主管体育工作的部门(以下简称体育主管部门)负责本行政区域内的全民健身工作，县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的全民健身工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of sports under the State Council shall be responsible for the national fitness work nationwide, and other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for related national fitness work within their respective functions and duties.  
The department in charge of sports work of the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the competent department of sports) shall be responsible for the national fitness work within its administrative region, and other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for related national fitness work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励对全民健身事业提供捐赠和赞助。  
自然人、法人或者其他组织对全民健身事业提供捐赠的，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 6** The State encourages donations and sponsorships to the national fitness cause.  
Natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations that make donations to the national fitness cause shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在发展全民健身事业中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of the national fitness cause shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　全民健身计划

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院制定全民健身计划，明确全民健身工作的目标、任务、措施、保障等内容。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据本地区的实际情况制定本行政区域的全民健身实施计划。  
制定全民健身计划和全民健身实施计划，应当充分考虑学生、老年人、残疾人和农村居民的特殊需求。

**Article 8** The State Council shall formulate a national fitness plan, specifying the objectives, tasks, measures, guarantees, and other contents of the national fitness work.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate national fitness implementation plans for their respective administrative regions based on the actual conditions of their regions.  
When formulating the national fitness plan and the national fitness implementation plans, special needs of students, the elderly, persons with disabilities, and rural residents shall be fully taken into account.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家定期开展公民体质监测和全民健身活动状况调查。  
公民体质监测由国务院体育主管部门会同有关部门组织实施；其中，对学生的体质监测由国务院教育主管部门组织实施。  
全民健身活动状况调查由国务院体育主管部门组织实施。

**Article 9** The State shall regularly carry out citizens' physical fitness monitoring and national fitness activity status surveys.  
Citizens' physical fitness monitoring shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of sports under the State Council jointly with relevant departments; among them, the physical fitness monitoring of students shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of education under the State Council.  
National fitness activity status surveys shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of sports under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院根据公民体质监测结果和全民健身活动状况调查结果，修订全民健身计划。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据公民体质监测结果和全民健身活动状况调查结果，修订全民健身实施计划。

**Article 10** The State Council shall revise the national fitness plan based on the results of citizens' physical fitness monitoring and national fitness activity status surveys.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall revise the national fitness implementation plans based on the results of citizens' physical fitness monitoring and national fitness activity status surveys.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　全民健身计划由县级以上人民政府体育主管部门会同有关部门组织实施。县级以上地方人民政府应当加强组织和协调，对本行政区域全民健身计划实施情况负责。  
县级以上人民政府体育主管部门应当在本级人民政府任期届满时会同有关部门对全民健身计划实施情况进行评估，并将评估结果向本级人民政府报告。

**Article 11** The national fitness program shall be organized and implemented by the competent sports departments of the people's governments at or above the county level together with the relevant departments. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen organization and coordination, and shall be responsible for the implementation of the national fitness program within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent sports departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, upon the expiration of the term of office of the people's government at the same level, evaluate the implementation of the national fitness program together with the relevant departments, and shall report the evaluation results to the people's government at the same level.

### 第三章　全民健身活动

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　每年8月8日为全民健身日。县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当在全民健身日加强全民健身宣传。  
国家机关、企业事业单位和其他组织应当在全民健身日结合自身条件组织本单位人员开展全民健身活动。  
县级以上人民政府体育主管部门应当在全民健身日组织开展免费健身指导服务。  
公共体育设施应当在全民健身日向公众免费开放；国家鼓励其他各类体育设施在全民健身日向公众免费开放。

**Article 12** August 8 of each year shall be the National Fitness Day. People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall strengthen national fitness publicity on the National Fitness Day.  
State organs, enterprises, institutions and other organizations shall, on the National Fitness Day, in light of their own conditions, organize personnel of their own units to carry out national fitness activities.  
The competent sports departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize and provide free fitness guidance services on the National Fitness Day.  
Public sports facilities shall be open to the public free of charge on the National Fitness Day; the State encourages other types of sports facilities to be open to the public free of charge on the National Fitness Day.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院体育主管部门应当定期举办全国性群众体育比赛活动；国务院其他有关部门、全国性社会团体等，可以根据需要举办相应的全国性群众体育比赛活动。  
地方人民政府应当定期举办本行政区域的群众体育比赛活动。

**Article 13** The competent sports department of the State Council shall periodically hold national mass sports competitions; the other relevant departments of the State Council, national social groups, etc., may, as needed, hold corresponding national mass sports competitions.  
Local people's governments shall periodically hold mass sports competitions within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级人民政府体育主管部门应当在传统节日和农闲季节组织开展与农村生产劳动和文化生活相适应的全民健身活动。

**Article 14** The competent sports departments of the people's governments at the county level shall, on traditional festivals and during slack farming seasons, organize and carry out national fitness activities that are compatible with rural production, labor and cultural life.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家机关、企业事业单位和其他组织应当组织本单位人员开展工间(前)操和业余健身活动；有条件的，可以举办运动会，开展体育锻炼测验、体质测定等活动。

**Article 15** State organs, enterprises, institutions and other organizations shall organize personnel of their own units to carry out exercises during work breaks (or before work) and amateur fitness activities; if conditions permit, they may hold sports meets, carry out physical exercise tests, physical fitness tests and other activities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　工会、共青团、妇联、残联等社会团体应当结合自身特点，组织成员开展全民健身活动。  
单项体育协会应当将普及推广体育项目和组织开展全民健身活动列入工作计划，并对全民健身活动给予指导和支持。

**Article 16** Social groups such as trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and disabled persons' federations shall, in light of their own characteristics, organize their members to carry out national fitness activities.  
Individual sport associations shall include popularizing and promoting sports programs and organizing and carrying out national fitness activities in their work plans, and shall provide guidance and support for national fitness activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　基层文化体育组织、居民委员会和村民委员会应当组织居民开展全民健身活动，协助政府做好相关工作。

**Article 17** Grass-roots cultural and sports organizations, residents' committees and villagers' committees shall organize residents to carry out national fitness activities, and shall assist the government in doing relevant work.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　鼓励全民健身活动站点、体育俱乐部等群众性体育组织开展全民健身活动，宣传科学健身知识；县级以上人民政府体育主管部门和其他有关部门应当给予支持。

**Article 18** The national fitness activity stations, sports clubs and other mass sports organizations are encouraged to carry out national fitness activities and publicize scientific fitness knowledge; the competent sports departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall provide support.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对于依法举办的群众体育比赛等全民健身活动，任何组织或者个人不得非法设置审批和收取审批费用。

**Article 19** For mass sports competitions and other national fitness activities legally held, no organization or individual may illegally set up approval procedures or charge approval fees.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　广播电台、电视台、报刊和互联网站等应当加强对全民健身活动的宣传报道，普及科学健身知识，增强公民健身意识。

**Article 20** Radio stations, television stations, newspapers, magazines and Internet websites shall strengthen the publicity and reporting of national fitness activities, popularize scientific fitness knowledge, and enhance citizens' fitness awareness.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　学校应当按照《中华人民共和国体育法》和《学校体育工作条例》的规定，根据学生的年龄、性别和体质状况，组织实施体育课教学，开展广播体操、眼保健操等体育活动，指导学生的体育锻炼，提高学生的身体素质。  
学校应当保证学生在校期间每天参加1小时的体育活动。

**Article 21** A school shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Sports Law of the People's Republic of China and the Regulations on School Physical Education Work, organize and implement physical education teaching, conduct sports activities such as broadcast gymnastics and eye exercises, guide students' physical exercise, and improve students' physical fitness based on students' age, gender, and physical condition.  
A school shall ensure that students participate in one hour of physical activity each day during school hours.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　学校每学年至少举办一次全校性的运动会；有条件的，还可以有计划地组织学生参加远足、野营、体育夏(冬)令营等活动。

**Article 22** A school shall hold at least one school-wide sports meeting per academic year; where conditions permit, it may also organize students to participate in activities such as hiking, camping, and summer (winter) sports camps in a planned manner.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　基层文化体育组织、学校、家庭应当加强合作，支持和引导学生参加校外体育活动。  
青少年活动中心、少年宫、妇女儿童中心等应当为学生开展体育活动提供便利。

**Article 23** Community-level cultural and sports organizations, schools, and families shall strengthen cooperation to support and guide students in participating in after-school sports activities.  
Youth activity centers, children's palaces, women and children's centers, etc. shall provide convenience for students to carry out sports activities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　组织大型全民健身活动，应当按照国家有关大型群众性活动安全管理的规定，做好安全工作。

**Article 24** Where organizing large-scale national fitness activities, safety work shall be carried out in accordance with the state regulations on safety management of large-scale mass activities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织或者个人不得利用健身活动从事宣扬封建迷信、违背社会公德、扰乱公共秩序、损害公民身心健康的行为。

**Article 25** No organization or individual may use fitness activities to engage in acts that promote feudal superstition, violate social morality, disrupt public order, or harm citizens' physical and mental health.

### 第四章　全民健身保障

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府应当将全民健身工作所需经费列入本级财政预算，并随着国民经济的发展逐步增加对全民健身的投入。  
按照国家有关彩票公益金的分配政策由体育主管部门分配使用的彩票公益金，应当根据国家有关规定用于全民健身事业。

**Article 26** People's governments at or above the county level shall include the funds required for national fitness work in their fiscal budgets, and gradually increase investment in national fitness as the national economy develops.  
The welfare proceeds from lottery tickets distributed and used by the competent sports department in accordance with the state's distribution policy for lottery welfare funds shall be used for the national fitness cause according to the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公共体育设施的规划、建设、使用、管理、保护和公共体育设施管理单位提供服务，应当遵守《公共文化体育设施条例》的规定。  
公共体育设施的规划、建设应当与当地经济发展水平相适应，方便群众就近参加健身活动；农村地区公共体育设施的规划、建设还应当考虑农村生产劳动和文化生活习惯。

**Article 27** The planning, construction, use, management, and protection of public sports facilities, as well as the provision of services by public sports facility management units, shall comply with the provisions of the Regulations on Public Cultural and Sports Facilities.  
The planning and construction of public sports facilities shall be compatible with the local economic development level and facilitate the masses to participate in fitness activities nearby; for rural areas, the planning and construction of public sports facilities shall also take into account rural production labor and cultural living habits.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　学校应当在课余时间和节假日向学生开放体育设施。公办学校应当积极创造条件向公众开放体育设施；国家鼓励民办学校向公众开放体育设施。  
县级人民政府对向公众开放体育设施的学校给予支持，为向公众开放体育设施的学校办理有关责任保险。  
学校可以根据维持设施运营的需要向使用体育设施的公众收取必要的费用。

**Article 28** A school shall open its sports facilities to students during after-school hours and holidays. Public schools shall actively create conditions to open sports facilities to the public; the State encourages private schools to open sports facilities to the public.  
The people's government at the county level shall provide support to schools that open sports facilities to the public and arrange for relevant liability insurance for such schools.  
A school may charge necessary fees to the public using sports facilities based on the need to maintain facility operation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公园、绿地等公共场所的管理单位，应当根据自身条件安排全民健身活动场地。县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门根据实际情况免费提供健身器材。  
居民住宅区的设计应当安排健身活动场地。

**Article 29** Management units of public places such as parks and green spaces shall arrange venues for national fitness activities based on their own conditions. The competent sports departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide fitness equipment free of charge based on actual circumstances.  
The design of residential areas shall include venues for fitness activities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公园、绿地、广场等公共场所和居民住宅区的管理单位，应当对该公共场所和居民住宅区配置的全民健身器材明确管理和维护责任人。

**Article 30** Management units of public places such as parks, green spaces, and squares, as well as residential areas, shall designate persons responsible for the management and maintenance of the national fitness equipment installed in such public places and residential areas.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家加强社会体育指导人员队伍建设，对全民健身活动进行科学指导。  
国家对不以收取报酬为目的向公众提供传授健身技能、组织健身活动、宣传科学健身知识等服务的社会体育指导人员实行技术等级制度。县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门应当免费为其提供相关知识和技能培训，并建立档案。  
国家对以健身指导为职业的社会体育指导人员实行职业资格证书制度。以对高危险性体育项目进行健身指导为职业的社会体育指导人员，应当依照国家有关规定取得职业资格证书。

**Article 31** The State shall strengthen the construction of the social sports instructor team and provide scientific guidance for the nationwide fitness activities.  
The State shall implement a technical grade system for social sports instructors who provide services such as imparting fitness skills, organizing fitness activities, and propagating scientific fitness knowledge to the public without receiving remuneration. The sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide free relevant knowledge and skills training for them and establish archives.  
The State shall implement a professional qualification certificate system for social sports instructors who take fitness guidance as their profession. Social sports instructors who take fitness guidance for high-risk sports as their profession shall obtain professional qualification certificates in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　企业、个体工商户经营高危险性体育项目的，应当符合下列条件，并向县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门提出申请：  
(一)相关体育设施符合国家标准；  
(二)具有达到规定数量的取得国家职业资格证书的社会体育指导人员和救助人员；  
(三)具有相应的安全保障制度和措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内进行实地核查，做出批准或者不予批准的决定。批准的，应当发给许可证；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
国务院体育主管部门应当会同有关部门制定、调整高危险性体育项目目录，经国务院批准后予以公布。

**Article 32** Enterprises and individual industrial and commercial households that operate high-risk sports shall meet the following conditions and submit an application to the sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) The relevant sports facilities comply with national standards;  
(2) They have the required number of social sports instructors and rescue personnel who have obtained national professional qualification certificates;  
(3) They have corresponding safety guarantee systems and measures.  
The sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, conduct an on-site inspection and make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approved, it shall issue a license; if disapproved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The sports administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate and adjust the catalogue of high-risk sports, which shall be published after approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家鼓励全民健身活动组织者和健身场所管理者依法投保有关责任保险。  
国家鼓励参加全民健身活动的公民依法投保意外伤害保险。

**Article 33** The State encourages the organizers of nationwide fitness activities and the managers of fitness venues to take out relevant liability insurance according to law.  
The State encourages citizens participating in nationwide fitness activities to take out accident injury insurance according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门对高危险性体育项目经营活动，应当依法履行监督检查职责。

**Article 34** The sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, perform the duty of supervision and inspection over the business activities of high-risk sports.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　学校违反本条例规定的，由县级以上人民政府教育主管部门按照管理权限责令改正；拒不改正的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** Where a school violates the provisions of these Regulations, the education administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its administrative authority, order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　未经批准，擅自经营高危险性体育项目的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门按照管理权限责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得不足3万元或者没有违法所得的，并处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；违法所得3万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 36** Where any person operates a high-risk sports project without approval, the sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its administrative authority, order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 30,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are more than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　高危险性体育项目经营者取得许可证后，不再符合本条例规定条件仍经营该体育项目的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育主管部门按照管理权限责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得不足3万元或者没有违法所得的，并处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款；违法所得3万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；拒不改正的，由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 37** Where an operator of a high-risk sports project, after obtaining the license, continues to operate the sports project without meeting the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, the sports administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its administrative authority, order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 30,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are more than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if it refuses to make corrections, the original issuing authority shall revoke the license.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　利用健身活动从事宣扬封建迷信、违背社会公德、扰乱公共秩序、损害公民身心健康的行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where any person uses fitness activities to engage in acts of propagating feudal superstition, violating social morality, disrupting public order, or harming citizens' physical and mental health, the public security organ shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门的工作人员在全民健身工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where any staff member of the people's government at or above the county level or its relevant departments neglects duties, abuses power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the nationwide fitness work, he or she shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自2009年10月1日起施行。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2009.
