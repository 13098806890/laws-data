# 退役军人安置条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-07-29  
**生效日期**：2024-09-01

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范退役军人安置工作，妥善安置退役军人，维护退役军人合法权益，让军人成为全社会尊崇的职业，根据《中华人民共和国退役军人保障法》、《中华人民共和国兵役法》、《中华人民共和国军人地位和权益保障法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Veterans, the Military Service Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of the Status and Rights and Interests of Military Personnel, for the purposes of regulating the resettlement of veterans, properly resettling veterans, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of veterans, and making the military a profession respected by the whole society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称退役军人，是指从中国人民解放军依法退出现役的军官、军士和义务兵等人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term "veterans" means officers, non-commissioned officers, and conscripts who have lawfully retired from active service in the People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　退役军人为国防和军队建设做出了重要贡献，是社会主义现代化建设的重要力量。  
国家关心、优待退役军人，保障退役军人依法享有相应的权益。  
全社会应当尊重、优待退役军人，支持退役军人安置工作。

**Article 3** Veterans have made important contributions to national defense and military construction and are an important force in socialist modernization.  
The State cares for and gives preferential treatment to veterans, and ensures that veterans enjoy corresponding rights and interests according to law.  
The whole society shall respect and give preferential treatment to veterans, and support the resettlement of veterans.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　退役军人安置工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持为经济社会发展服务、为国防和军队建设服务的方针，贯彻妥善安置、合理使用、人尽其才、各得其所的原则。  
退役军人安置工作应当公开、公平、公正，军地协同推进。

**Article 4** The resettlement of veterans shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to the policy of serving economic and social development, and serving national defense and military construction, and implement the principles of proper resettlement, rational use, placing people to the best use of their talents, and providing each with a suitable position.  
The resettlement of veterans shall be open, fair, and just, and shall be advanced through military-local coordination.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对退役的军官，国家采取退休、转业、逐月领取退役金、复员等方式妥善安置。  
对退役的军士，国家采取逐月领取退役金、自主就业、安排工作、退休、供养等方式妥善安置。  
对退役的义务兵，国家采取自主就业、安排工作、供养等方式妥善安置。  
对参战退役军人，担任作战部队师、旅、团、营级单位主官的转业军官，属于烈士子女、功臣模范的退役军人，以及长期在艰苦边远地区或者飞行、舰艇、涉核等特殊岗位服现役的退役军人，依法优先安置。

**Article 5** For retired officers, the State shall adopt such methods as retirement, job transfer, monthly retirement pay, or demobilization to properly resettle them.  
For retired non-commissioned officers, the State shall adopt such methods as monthly retirement pay, self-employment, job arrangement, retirement, or support to properly resettle them.  
For retired conscripts, the State shall adopt such methods as self-employment, job arrangement, or support to properly resettle them.  
Veterans who have participated in combat, transferred officers who served as principal leaders of division, brigade, regiment, or battalion units of combat forces, veterans who are children of martyrs or heroes and models, and veterans who have served for a long time in harsh and remote areas or in special posts such as aviation, naval vessels, or nuclear-related posts shall be resettled with priority according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中央退役军人事务工作领导机构负责退役军人安置工作顶层设计、统筹协调、整体推进、督促落实。地方各级退役军人事务工作领导机构负责本地区退役军人安置工作的组织领导和统筹实施。

**Article 6** The central leading body for veterans affairs shall be responsible for the top-level design, overall coordination, comprehensive advancement, and supervision of enforcement of the resettlement of veterans. The leading bodies for veterans affairs at various local levels shall be responsible for the organizational leadership and coordinated implementation of the resettlement of veterans in their respective regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院退役军人工作主管部门负责全国的退役军人安置工作。中央军事委员会政治工作部门负责组织指导全军军人退役工作。中央和国家有关机关、中央军事委员会机关有关部门应当在各自职责范围内做好退役军人安置工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门负责本行政区域的退役军人安置工作。军队团级以上单位政治工作部门（含履行政治工作职责的部门，下同）负责本单位军人退役工作。地方各级有关机关应当在各自职责范围内做好退役军人安置工作。  
省军区（卫戍区、警备区）负责全军到所在省、自治区、直辖市以转业、逐月领取退役金、复员方式安置的退役军官和逐月领取退役金的退役军士移交工作，配合安置地做好安置工作；配合做好退休军官、军士以及以安排工作、供养方式安置的退役军士和义务兵移交工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the resettlement of veterans nationwide. The political work department of the Central Military Commission shall be responsible for organizing and guiding the retirement of military personnel throughout the armed forces. Relevant organs of the Central and State organs and relevant departments under the Central Military Commission shall perform well the resettlement of veterans within their respective duties.  
The competent department of veterans affairs of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the resettlement of veterans within its administrative region. The political work departments (including departments performing political work functions, the same below) of military units at or above the regiment level shall be responsible for the retirement of military personnel in their own units. Relevant organs at various local levels shall perform well the resettlement of veterans within their respective duties.  
The provincial military commands (garrison commands, garrison commands) shall be responsible for the transfer of retired officers and retired non-commissioned officers who receive monthly retirement pay, who are resettled in the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government by means of job transfer, monthly retirement pay, or demobilization, and shall cooperate with the place of resettlement in carrying out the resettlement; and shall cooperate in the transfer of retired officers and non-commissioned officers who are retired, and retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are resettled by means of job arrangement or support.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　退役军人安置所需经费，按照中央与地方财政事权和支出责任划分原则，列入中央和地方预算，并根据经济社会发展水平适时调整。

**Article 8** The funds required for the resettlement of veterans shall be included in the central and local budgets in accordance with the principle of dividing fiscal powers and expenditure responsibilities between the central and local governments, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner according to the level of economic and social development.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　机关、群团组织、企业事业单位和社会组织应当依法接收安置退役军人，退役军人应当接受安置。  
退役军人应当模范遵守宪法和法律法规，保守军事秘密，保持发扬人民军队光荣传统和优良作风，积极投身全面建设社会主义现代化国家的事业。

**Article 9** Organs, mass organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations shall, according to law, accept and resettle veterans, and veterans shall accept resettlement.  
Veterans shall model compliance with the Constitution and laws and regulations, maintain military secrets, preserve and carry forward the glorious traditions and fine work style of the people's armed forces, and actively devote themselves to the cause of comprehensively building a modern socialist country.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当把退役军人安置工作纳入年度重点工作计划，纳入目标管理，建立健全安置工作责任制和考核评价制度，将安置工作完成情况纳入对本级人民政府负责退役军人有关工作的部门及其负责人、下级人民政府及其负责人的考核评价内容，作为双拥模范城（县）考评重要内容。

**Article 10** A local people's government at or above the county level shall include the resettlement of veterans in its annual key work plan and in target management, establish and improve a responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for resettlement work, and incorporate the completion status of resettlement work into the assessment and evaluation content of the department responsible for veterans affairs of the people's government at the same level and its leading persons, and of the lower-level people's governments and their leading persons, and as an important content for the evaluation of model cities (counties) for supporting the military and giving preferential treatment to families of military personnel and martyrs.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在退役军人安置工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Units and individuals that make outstanding contributions in the work of resettling veterans shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　退役军官安置方式

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军官退出现役，符合规定条件的，可以作退休、转业或者逐月领取退役金安置。  
军官退出现役，有规定情形的，作复员安置。

**Article 12** An officer who retires from active service may, if meeting the prescribed conditions, be resettled by retirement, job transfer, or monthly retirement pay.  
An officer who retires from active service shall, under the prescribed circumstances, be resettled by demobilization.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对退休军官，安置地人民政府应当按照国家保障与社会化服务相结合的方式，做好服务管理工作，保障其待遇。

**Article 13** For retired officers, the people's government of the place of resettlement shall, by combining State guarantee with socialized services, perform well service management and ensure their treatment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　安置地人民政府根据工作需要设置、调整退休军官服务管理机构，服务管理退休军官。

**Article 14** The people's government of the place of resettlement shall, as needed by the work, establish or adjust service management institutions for retired officers to serve and manage retired officers.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　转业军官由机关、群团组织、事业单位和国有企业接收安置。  
安置地人民政府应当根据转业军官德才条件以及服现役期间的职务、等级、所作贡献、专长等和工作需要，结合实际统筹采取考核选调、赋分选岗、考试考核、双向选择、直通安置、指令性分配等办法，妥善安排其工作岗位，确定相应的职务职级。

**Article 15** Transferred officers shall be accepted and resettled by organs, mass organizations, public institutions, and State-owned enterprises.  
The people's government of the place of resettlement shall, based on the overall consideration of the transferred officers' moral and professional qualifications, as well as their positions, ranks, contributions, specialties, etc. during active service, and work needs, and in light of actual conditions, adopt such methods as assessment and selection, scoring and position selection, examination and assessment, two-way selection, direct placement, or mandatory assignment to properly arrange their work positions and determine the corresponding positions and ranks.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　退役军官逐月领取退役金的具体办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 16** The specific measures for retired officers to receive monthly retirement pay shall be formulated by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　复员军官按照国务院退役军人工作主管部门、中央军事委员会政治工作部门制定的有关规定享受复员费以及其他待遇等。

**Article 17** Demobilized officers shall enjoy demobilization fees and other treatments in accordance with the relevant provisions formulated by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council and the political work department of the Central Military Commission.

### 第三章　退役军士和义务兵安置方式

#### 第一节　逐月领取退役金

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军士退出现役，符合规定条件的，可以作逐月领取退役金安置。

**Article 18** A non-commissioned officer who retires from active service may, if meeting the prescribed conditions, be resettled by monthly retirement pay.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　退役军士逐月领取退役金的具体办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 19** The specific measures for retired non-commissioned officers to receive monthly retirement pay shall be formulated by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

#### 第二节　自主就业

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　退役军士不符合逐月领取退役金、安排工作、退休、供养条件的，退役义务兵不符合安排工作、供养条件的，以自主就业方式安置。  
退役军士符合逐月领取退役金、安排工作条件的，退役义务兵符合安排工作条件的，可以选择以自主就业方式安置。

**Article 20** A retired non-commissioned officer who does not meet the conditions for monthly retirement pay, job arrangement, retirement, or support, and a retired conscript who does not meet the conditions for job arrangement or support, shall be resettled by self-employment.  
A retired non-commissioned officer who meets the conditions for monthly retirement pay or job arrangement, and a retired conscript who meets the conditions for job arrangement, may choose to be resettled by self-employment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对自主就业的退役军士和义务兵，根据其服现役年限发放一次性退役金。  
自主就业退役军士和义务兵的一次性退役金由中央财政专项安排，具体标准由国务院退役军人工作主管部门、中央军事委员会政治工作部门会同国务院财政部门，根据国民经济发展水平、国家财力情况、全国城镇单位就业人员平均工资和军人职业特殊性等因素确定，并适时调整。

**Article 21** For retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are self-employed, a one-time retirement pay shall be granted based on their length of active service.  
The one-time retirement pay for self-employed retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts shall be specially arranged by the central finance. The specific standard shall be determined by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council, the political work department of the Central Military Commission, together with the finance department of the State Council, based on factors such as the level of national economic development, the State's financial capacity, the average salary of employed persons in urban units nationwide, and the particularity of the military profession, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　自主就业的退役军士和义务兵服现役期间个人获得勋章、荣誉称号或者表彰奖励的，按照下列比例增发一次性退役金：  
（一）获得勋章、荣誉称号的，增发25%；  
（二）荣立一等战功或者获得一级表彰的，增发20%；  
（三）荣立二等战功、一等功或者获得二级表彰并经批准享受相关待遇的，增发15%；  
（四）荣立三等战功或者二等功的，增发10%；  
（五）荣立四等战功或者三等功的，增发5%。

**Article 22** If a self-employed retired non-commissioned officer or conscript, during his active service, individually received a medal, honorary title, or commendation or award, the one-time retirement pay shall be increased by the following percentages:  
(1) If he received a medal or honorary title, the increase shall be 25%;  
(2) If he achieved first-class combat merit or received a first-level commendation, the increase shall be 20%;  
(3) If he achieved second-class combat merit or first-class merit, or received a second-level commendation and was approved to enjoy the relevant treatment, the increase shall be 15%;  
(4) If he achieved third-class combat merit or second-class merit, the increase shall be 10%;  
(5) If he achieved fourth-class combat merit or third-class merit, the increase shall be 5%.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对自主就业的退役军士和义务兵，地方人民政府可以根据当地实际情况给予一次性经济补助，补助标准及发放办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 23** For self-employed retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts, the local people's government may, based on the actual local conditions, grant a one-time economic subsidy. The subsidy standard and issuance method shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因患精神障碍被评定为5级至6级残疾等级的初级军士和义务兵退出现役后，需要住院治疗或者无直系亲属照顾的，可以由安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门安排到有关医院接受治疗，依法给予保障。

**Article 24** After a junior non-commissioned officer or conscript who has been assessed as having a disability level of Class 5 or Class 6 due to mental disorder retires from active service, if he needs hospitalization or has no direct relative to care for him, the competent department of veterans affairs of the people's government of the place of resettlement may arrange for him to receive treatment in a relevant hospital, and shall provide guarantee according to law.

#### 第三节　安排工作

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军士和义务兵退出现役，符合下列条件之一的，由安置地人民政府安排工作：  
（一）军士服现役满12年的；  
（二）服现役期间个人获得勋章、荣誉称号的；  
（三）服现役期间个人荣获三等战功、二等功以上奖励的；  
（四）服现役期间个人获得一级表彰的；  
（五）因战致残被评定为5级至8级残疾等级的；  
（六）是烈士子女的。  
符合逐月领取退役金条件的军士，本人自愿放弃以逐月领取退役金方式安置的，可以选择以安排工作方式安置。  
因战致残被评定为5级至6级残疾等级的中级以上军士，本人自愿放弃以退休方式安置的，可以选择以安排工作方式安置。

**Article 25** A non-commissioned officer or conscript who retires from active service and meets any of the following conditions shall have a job arranged by the people's government of the place of resettlement:  
(1) The non-commissioned officer has served on active duty for at least 12 years;  
(2) He individually received a medal or honorary title during his active service;  
(3) He individually achieved third-class combat merit or second-class merit or above during his active service;  
(4) He individually received a first-level commendation during his active service;  
(5) He was disabled due to war and assessed as having a disability level of Class 5 to Class 8;  
(6) He is a child of a martyr.  
A non-commissioned officer who meets the conditions for monthly retirement pay and voluntarily gives up being resettled by monthly retirement pay may choose to be resettled by job arrangement.  
A non-commissioned officer at or above the intermediate level who was disabled due to war and assessed as having a disability level of Class 5 or Class 6 and voluntarily gives up being resettled by retirement may choose to be resettled by job arrangement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对安排工作的退役军士和义务兵，主要采取赋分选岗的办法安排到事业单位和国有企业；符合规定条件的，可以择优招录到基层党政机关公务员岗位。  
安排工作的退役军士和义务兵服现役表现量化评分的具体办法由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同中央军事委员会政治工作部门制定。

**Article 26** For retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged, they shall mainly be placed in public institutions and State-owned enterprises through the method of scoring and position selection; if they meet the prescribed conditions, they may be selectively recruited to civil servant posts in grassroots Party and government organs.  
The specific measures for quantitatively scoring the active service performance of retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged shall be formulated by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council together with the political work department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　根据工作需要和基层政权建设要求，省级公务员主管部门应当确定一定数量的基层公务员录用计划，综合考虑服现役表现等因素，按照[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)的规定择优招录具有本科以上学历的安排工作的退役军士和义务兵。招录岗位可以在省级行政区域内统筹安排。  
参加招录的退役军士和义务兵是烈士子女的，或者在艰苦边远地区服现役满5年的，同等条件下优先录用。  
艰苦边远地区和边疆民族地区在招录退役军士和义务兵时，可以根据本地实际适当放宽安置去向、年龄、学历等条件。

**Article 27** In light of work needs and the requirements of grassroots political power construction, the provincial-level competent department of civil service affairs shall determine a certain number of grassroots civil service recruitment plans, and, based on comprehensive consideration of factors such as active service performance, shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 26 of these Regulations, selectively recruit retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who have a bachelor's degree or above and are to have jobs arranged. The recruitment positions may be arranged in a coordinated manner within the provincial administrative region.  
If a retired non-commissioned officer or conscript participating in the recruitment is a child of a martyr, or has served on active duty in a harsh and remote area for at least 5 years, he shall be recruited with priority under equal conditions.  
When recruiting retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts in harsh and remote areas and border ethnic areas, conditions such as resettlement destination, age, and education may be appropriately relaxed based on local actual conditions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　根据安置工作需要，省级以上人民政府可以指定一批专项岗位，按照规定接收安置安排工作的退役军士和义务兵。

**Article 28** As needed for resettlement work, a people's government at or above the provincial level may designate a number of special posts to accept and resettle, in accordance with regulations, retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对安排到事业单位的退役军士和义务兵，应当根据其服现役期间所作贡献、专长特长等，合理安排工作岗位。符合相应岗位条件的，可以安排到管理岗位或者专业技术岗位。

**Article 29** For retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are placed in public institutions, work positions shall be reasonably arranged based on their contributions during active service, specialties, etc. If they meet the corresponding position conditions, they may be arranged to management posts or professional technical posts.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　机关、群团组织、事业单位接收安置安排工作的退役军士和义务兵的，应当按照国家有关规定给予编制保障。  
国有企业应当按照本企业全系统新招录职工数量的规定比例核定年度接收计划，用于接收安置安排工作的退役军士和义务兵。

**Article 30** If an organ, mass organization, or public institution accepts and resettles retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged, it shall provide staffing guarantees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
A State-owned enterprise shall, based on the prescribed proportion of the total number of newly recruited employees in its entire system, determine an annual reception plan for accepting and resettling retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对接收安置安排工作的退役军士和义务兵任务较重的地方，上级人民政府可以在本行政区域内统筹调剂安排。  
安置地人民政府应当在接收退役军士和义务兵的6个月内完成安排退役军士和义务兵工作的任务。

**Article 31** For a place with a heavy task of accepting and resettling retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged, the higher-level people's government may make coordinated adjustments within its administrative region.  
The people's government of the place of resettlement shall complete the task of arranging jobs for the retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts within six months of receiving them.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　安排工作的退役军士和义务兵的安置岗位需要签订聘用合同或者劳动合同的，用人单位应当按照规定与其签订不少于3年的中长期聘用合同或者劳动合同。其中，企业接收军龄10年以上的退役军士的，应当与其签订无固定期限劳动合同。

**Article 32** If a resettlement post for a retired non-commissioned officer or conscript who is to have a job arranged requires the signing of an employment contract or a labor contract, the employing unit shall, in accordance with regulations, sign with him a medium-to-long-term employment contract or labor contract with a term of not less than three years. Where an enterprise accepts a retired non-commissioned officer with military service of more than 10 years, it shall sign an open-ended labor contract with him.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对安排工作的残疾退役军士和义务兵，接收单位应当安排力所能及的工作。  
安排工作的因战、因公致残退役军士和义务兵，除依法享受工伤保险待遇外，还享受与所在单位工伤人员同等的生活福利、医疗等其他待遇。

**Article 33** For a disabled retired non-commissioned officer or conscript who is to have a job arranged, the receiving unit shall arrange work within his capability.  
A retired non-commissioned officer or conscript who has a job arranged and is disabled due to war or duty shall, in addition to enjoying work-related injury insurance benefits according to law, also enjoy the same living welfare, medical care, and other treatment as the unit's work-related injury personnel.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　符合安排工作条件的退役军士和义务兵无正当理由拒不服从安置地人民政府安排工作的，视为放弃安排工作待遇；在待安排工作期间被依法追究刑事责任的，取消其安排工作待遇。

**Article 34** If a retired non-commissioned officer or conscript who meets the conditions for job arrangement refuses, without justifiable reason, to obey the job arrangement made by the people's government of the place of resettlement, it shall be deemed that he has abandoned his entitlement to job arrangement; if he is investigated for criminal liability according to law during the period of waiting for job arrangement, his entitlement to job arrangement shall be revoked.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　军士和义务兵退出现役，有下列情形之一的，不以安排工作方式安置：  
（一）被开除中国共产党党籍的；  
（二）受过刑事处罚的；  
（三）法律法规规定的因被强制退役等原因不宜以安排工作方式安置的其他情形。

**Article 35** A non-commissioned officer or conscript who retires from active service shall not be resettled by job arrangement if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) He has been expelled from the Communist Party of China;  
(2) He has received criminal punishment;  
(3) Other circumstances prescribed by laws and regulations where resettlement by job arrangement is not suitable due to such reasons as forced retirement.

#### 第四节　退休与供养

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　中级以上军士退出现役，符合下列条件之一的，作退休安置：  
（一）退出现役时年满55周岁的；  
（二）服现役满30年的；  
（三）因战、因公致残被评定为1级至6级残疾等级的；  
（四）患有严重疾病且经医学鉴定基本丧失工作能力的。

**Article 36** A non-commissioned officer at or above the intermediate level who retires from active service shall be resettled by retirement if he meets any of the following conditions:  
(1) He has reached the age of 55 at the time of retirement from active service;  
(2) He has served on active duty for at least 30 years;  
(3) He was disabled due to war or duty and assessed as having a disability level of Class 1 to Class 6;  
(4) He suffers from a serious disease and has been determined through medical evaluation to have substantially lost his ability to work.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　退休军士移交政府安置服务管理工作，参照退休军官的有关规定执行。

**Article 37** The transfer of retired non-commissioned officers to government resettlement service management shall be carried out by reference to the relevant provisions on retired officers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　被评定为1级至4级残疾等级的初级军士和义务兵退出现役的，由国家供养终身。  
因战、因公致残被评定为1级至4级残疾等级的中级以上军士，本人自愿放弃退休安置的，可以选择由国家供养终身。  
国家供养分为集中供养和分散供养。

**Article 38** A junior non-commissioned officer or conscript who has been assessed as having a disability level of Class 1 to Class 4 and retires from active service shall be supported for life by the State.  
A non-commissioned officer at or above the intermediate level who was disabled due to war or duty and assessed as having a disability level of Class 1 to Class 4, and voluntarily gives up retirement resettlement, may choose to be supported for life by the State.  
State support is divided into centralized support and decentralized support.

### 第四章　移交接收

#### 第一节　安置计划

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　退役军人安置计划包括全国退役军人安置计划和地方退役军人安置计划，区分退役军官和退役军士、义务兵分类分批下达。  
全国退役军人安置计划，由国务院退役军人工作主管部门会同中央军事委员会政治工作部门、中央和国家有关机关编制下达。  
县级以上地方退役军人安置计划，由本级退役军人工作主管部门编制下达或者会同有关部门编制下达。

**Article 39** The resettlement plan for veterans includes the national resettlement plan for veterans and the local resettlement plan for veterans, and shall be issued in categories and batches, distinguishing between retired officers and retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts.  
The national resettlement plan for veterans shall be formulated and issued by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council together with the political work department of the Central Military Commission and relevant organs of the Central and State organs.  
The local resettlement plan for veterans at or above the county level shall be formulated and issued by the competent department of veterans affairs of the same level, or formulated and issued together with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　伤病残退役军人安置计划可以纳入[本条例第三十九条](#art-39)规定的计划一并编制下达，也可以专项编制下达。  
退役军人随调随迁配偶和子女安置计划与退役军人安置计划一并下达。

**Article 40** The resettlement plan for sick, wounded, or disabled veterans may be incorporated into the plan prescribed in Article 39 of these Regulations and formulated and issued together, or may be specially formulated and issued.  
The resettlement plan for the accompanying transferred or relocated spouses and children of veterans shall be issued together with the resettlement plan for veterans.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　中央和国家机关及其管理的企业事业单位接收退役军人的安置计划，按照国家有关规定编制下达。

**Article 41** The resettlement plan for central and State organs and the enterprises and public institutions under their management to accept veterans shall be formulated and issued in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　因军队体制编制调整，军人整建制成批次退出现役的安置，由国务院退役军人工作主管部门、中央军事委员会政治工作部门会同中央和国家有关机关协商办理。

**Article 42** The resettlement of military personnel who retire from active service in whole units and batches due to adjustments in the military's organizational structure shall be handled through consultation by the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council, the political work department of the Central Military Commission, together with the relevant organs of the Central and State organs.

#### 第二节　安置地

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　退役军人安置地按照服从工作需要、彰显服役贡献、有利于家庭生活的原则确定。

**Article 43** The place of resettlement for a veteran shall be determined based on the principles of complying with work needs, highlighting service contributions, and being conducive to family life.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　退役军官和以逐月领取退役金、退休方式安置的退役军士的安置地按照国家有关规定确定。

**Article 44** The place of resettlement for retired officers and for retired non-commissioned officers who are resettled by monthly retirement pay or retirement shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　退役义务兵和以自主就业、安排工作、供养方式安置的退役军士的安置地为其入伍时户口所在地。但是，入伍时是普通高等学校在校学生，退出现役后不复学的，其安置地为入学前的户口所在地。  
退役义务兵和以自主就业、安排工作、供养方式安置的退役军士有下列情形之一的，可以易地安置：  
（一）服现役期间父母任何一方户口所在地变更的，可以在父母任何一方现户口所在地安置；  
（二）退役军士已婚的，可以在配偶或者配偶父母任何一方户口所在地安置；  
（三）退役军士的配偶为现役军人且符合随军规定的，可以在配偶部队驻地安置；双方同时退役的，可以在配偶的安置地安置；  
（四）因其他特殊情况，由军队旅级以上单位政治工作部门出具证明，经省级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门批准，可以易地安置。  
退役军士按照前款第二项、第三项规定在国务院确定的中等以上城市安置的，应当结婚满2年。

**Article 45** The place of resettlement for a retired conscript and for a retired non-commissioned officer who is resettled by self-employment, job arrangement, or support shall be the place of household registration at the time of enlistment. However, if at the time of enlistment he was a student in a regular institution of higher education and does not resume studies after retiring from active service, his place of resettlement shall be the place of household registration before enrollment.  
A retired conscript and a retired non-commissioned officer who is resettled by self-employment, job arrangement, or support may be resettled in a different location if any of the following circumstances exists:  
(1) If the place of household registration of either parent changed during the period of active service, he may be resettled in the current place of household registration of either parent;  
(2) If the retired non-commissioned officer is married, he may be resettled in the place of household registration of his spouse or either parent of his spouse;  
(3) If the spouse of the retired non-commissioned officer is an active-duty servicemember and meets the conditions for accompanying the military, he may be resettled in the garrison area of the spouse's unit; if both retire at the same time, he may be resettled in the spouse's place of resettlement;  
(4) For other special circumstances, with a certificate issued by the political work department of a military unit at or above the brigade level and approved by the competent department of veterans affairs of a people's government at or above the provincial level, he may be resettled in a different location.  
If a retired non-commissioned officer is resettled in a medium or large city determined by the State Council in accordance with paragraph 2, item (2) or (3) of this Article, he shall have been married for at least 2 years.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　因国家重大改革、重点项目建设以及国防和军队改革需要等情况，退役军人经国务院退役军人工作主管部门批准，可以跨省、自治区、直辖市安置。  
符合安置地吸引人才特殊政策规定条件的退役军人，由接收安置单位所在省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门商同级人才工作主管部门同意，经国务院退役军人工作主管部门和中央军事委员会政治工作部门批准，可以跨省、自治区、直辖市安置。

**Article 46** Due to major national reforms, key project construction, or national defense and military reform needs, a veteran may, with the approval of the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council, be resettled across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
A veteran who meets the conditions prescribed in the special policies of the place of resettlement for attracting talent may, with the consent of the competent department of veterans affairs of the provincial-level people's government where the receiving unit is located after consultation with the competent department of talent work at the same level, and with the approval of the competent department of veterans affairs under the State Council and the political work department of the Central Military Commission, be resettled across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对因战致残、服现役期间个人荣获三等战功或者二等功以上奖励、是烈士子女的退役军人，以及父母双亡的退役军士和义务兵，可以根据本人申请，由省级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门按照有利于其生活的原则确定安置地。

**Article 47** For a veteran who is disabled due to war, individually achieved third-class combat merit or second-class merit or above during active service, or is a child of a martyr, and for a retired non-commissioned officer or conscript whose both parents are deceased, the place of resettlement may, upon his application, be determined by the competent department of veterans affairs of a people's government at or above the provincial level based on the principle of being conducive to his life.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　退役军人在国务院确定的超大城市安置的，除符合其安置方式对应的规定条件外，按照本人部队驻地安置的，还应当在驻该城市部队连续服役满规定年限；按照投靠方式安置的，还应当符合国家有关规定要求的其他资格条件。

**Article 48** If a veteran is resettled in a mega-city determined by the State Council, in addition to meeting the prescribed conditions corresponding to his method of resettlement, if resettled according to his own military garrison, he shall also have served continuously in the unit stationed in that city for the prescribed number of years; if resettled by way of affiliation, he shall also meet other qualification conditions required by the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　退役军人服现役期间个人获得勋章、荣誉称号的，荣立一等战功或者获得一级表彰的，可以在全国范围内选择安置地。其中，退役军人选择在国务院确定的超大城市安置的，不受[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)规定的限制。  
退役军人服现役期间个人荣立二等战功或者一等功的，获得二级表彰并经批准享受相关待遇的，在西藏、新疆、军队确定的四类以上艰苦边远地区、军队确定的二类以上岛屿或者飞行、舰艇、涉核等特殊岗位服现役累计满15年的，可以在符合安置条件的省级行政区域内选择安置地。  
退役军人在西藏、新疆、军队确定的四类以上艰苦边远地区、军队确定的二类以上岛屿或者飞行、舰艇、涉核等特殊岗位服现役累计满10年的，可以在符合安置条件的设区的市级行政区域内选择安置地。

**Article 49** If a veteran, during his active service, individually received a medal or honorary title, or achieved first-class combat merit or received a first-level commendation, he may choose his place of resettlement nationwide. Among them, if the veteran chooses to be resettled in a mega-city determined by the State Council, he shall not be subject to the restrictions of Article 48 of these Regulations.  
If a veteran, during his active service, individually achieved second-class combat merit or first-class merit, received a second-level commendation and was approved to enjoy the relevant treatment, or has served on active duty in Tibet, Xinjiang, areas classified by the armed forces as Category 4 or above harsh and remote areas, islands classified by the armed forces as Category 2 or above, or in special posts such as aviation, naval vessels, or nuclear-related posts for a cumulative period of at least 15 years, he may choose his place of resettlement within the provincial-level administrative regions that meet the resettlement conditions.  
If a veteran has served on active duty in Tibet, Xinjiang, areas classified by the armed forces as Category 4 or above harsh and remote areas, islands classified by the armed forces as Category 2 or above, or in special posts such as aviation, naval vessels, or nuclear-related posts for a cumulative period of at least 10 years, he may choose his place of resettlement within the city-level administrative regions divided into districts that meet the resettlement conditions.

#### 第三节　交接

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　以转业、逐月领取退役金、复员方式安置的退役军官和以逐月领取退役金方式安置的退役军士的人事档案，由中央军事委员会机关部委、中央军事委员会直属机构、中央军事委员会联合作战指挥中心、战区、军兵种、中央军事委员会直属单位等单位的政治工作部门向安置地省军区（卫戍区、警备区）移交后，由安置地省军区（卫戍区、警备区）向省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门进行移交。  
安排工作的退役军士和义务兵的人事档案，由中央军事委员会机关部委、中央军事委员会直属机构、中央军事委员会联合作战指挥中心、战区、军兵种、中央军事委员会直属单位等单位的政治工作部门向安置地省级人民政府退役军人工作主管部门进行移交。  
以自主就业、供养方式安置的退役军士和义务兵的人事档案，由军队师、旅、团级单位政治工作部门向安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门进行移交。

**Article 50** The personnel files of retired officers resettled by job transfer, monthly retirement pay, or demobilization, and of retired non-commissioned officers resettled by monthly retirement pay, shall be transferred by the political work departments of the departments and commissions of the Central Military Commission, the organs directly under the Central Military Commission, the Joint Operations Command Center of the Central Military Commission, the theater commands, the services and arms, and the units directly under the Central Military Commission to the provincial military command (garrison command, garrison command) of the place of resettlement, and thereafter the provincial military command (garrison command, garrison command) of the place of resettlement shall transfer them to the competent department of veterans affairs of the provincial-level people's government.  
The personnel files of retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts who are to have jobs arranged shall be transferred by the political work departments of the departments and commissions of the Central Military Commission, the organs directly under the Central Military Commission, the Joint Operations Command Center of the Central Military Commission, the theater commands, the services and arms, and the units directly under the Central Military Commission to the competent department of veterans affairs of the provincial-level people's government of the place of resettlement.  
The personnel files of retired non-commissioned officers and conscripts resettled by self-employment or support shall be transferred by the political work departments of military units at the division, brigade, or regiment level to the competent department of veterans affairs of the people's government of the place of resettlement.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　以转业、逐月领取退役金、复员方式安置的退役军官，由退役军人工作主管部门发出接收安置报到通知，所在部队应当及时为其办理相关手续，督促按时报到。  
以逐月领取退役金、安排工作、供养方式安置的退役军士和以安排工作、供养方式安置的退役义务兵，应当按照规定时间到安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门报到；自主就业的退役军士和义务兵，应当自被批准退出现役之日起30日内，到安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门报到。无正当理由不按照规定时间报到超过30日的，视为放弃安置待遇。

**Article 51** For retired military officers who are resettled through job transfer, monthly pension receipt, or demobilization, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen shall issue a notification for reception and resettlement registration, and the original unit shall promptly process relevant procedures and urge them to register on time.  
For retired non-commissioned officers who are resettled through monthly pension receipt, job placement, or support, and for retired compulsory servicemen who are resettled through job placement or support, they shall register with the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement within the prescribed time; retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are self-employed shall register with the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement within 30 days from the date of approval of their discharge from active service. Without justifiable reasons, failure to register within the prescribed time for more than 30 days shall be deemed as abandonment of resettlement benefits.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　退休军官和军士的移交接收，由退休军官和军士所在部队团级以上单位政治工作部门和安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门组织办理。

**Article 52** The transfer and reception of retired military officers and non-commissioned officers shall be organized and processed by the political work departments of units at or above the regimental level of the troops to which the retired military officers and non-commissioned officers belong, and the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　退役军人报到后，退役军人工作主管部门应当及时为需要办理户口登记的退役军人开具户口登记介绍信，公安机关据此办理户口登记。  
退役军人工作主管部门应当督促退役军人及时办理兵役登记信息变更。  
实行组织移交的复员军官，由军队旅级以上单位政治工作部门会同安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门和公安机关办理移交落户等相关手续。

**Article 53** After ex-servicemen register, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen shall promptly issue a letter of introduction for household registration for those who need to undergo household registration, and the public security organ shall process the household registration accordingly.  
The competent department for the work of ex-servicemen shall urge ex-servicemen to promptly handle changes in military service registration information.  
For demobilized military officers subject to organizational transfer, the political work departments of units at or above the brigade level of the military shall, jointly with the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement and the public security organ, handle relevant procedures such as transfer and household registration.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对符合移交条件的伤病残退役军人，军队有关单位和安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当及时移交接收，予以妥善安置。

**Article 54** For ex-servicemen with injuries, illnesses, or disabilities who meet the conditions for transfer, the relevant military units and the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall promptly transfer and receive them and provide proper resettlement.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对退役军人安置政策落实不到位、工作推进不力的地区和单位，由省级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门会同有关部门约谈该地区人民政府主要负责人或者该单位主要负责人；对拒绝接收安置退役军人或者未完成安置任务的部门和单位，组织、编制、人力资源社会保障等部门可以视情况暂缓办理其人员调动、录（聘）用和编制等审批事项。

**Article 55** For regions and units that fail to implement the ex-servicemen resettlement policy adequately or make insufficient progress in the work, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, jointly with relevant departments, interview the principal responsible person of the local people's government or the principal responsible person of the unit; for departments and units that refuse to receive and resettle ex-servicemen or fail to complete the resettlement tasks, the organizational, staffing, human resources and social security, and other departments may, depending on the circumstances, suspend the approval of matters such as personnel transfers, recruitment (employment), and staffing.

### 第五章　家属安置

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　以转业、逐月领取退役金、复员方式安置的退役军官和以逐月领取退役金、安排工作方式安置且符合家属随军规定的退役军士，其配偶可以随调随迁，未成年子女可以随迁。  
以转业、逐月领取退役金、复员方式安置的退役军官身边无子女的，可以随调一名已经工作的子女及其配偶。

**Article 56** For retired military officers resettled through job transfer, monthly pension receipt, or demobilization, and for retired non-commissioned officers resettled through monthly pension receipt or job placement who meet the provisions on family members accompanying the military, their spouses may be transferred and relocated, and their minor children may be relocated.  
For retired military officers resettled through job transfer, monthly pension receipt, or demobilization who have no children nearby, one working child and that child's spouse may be transferred.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　退役军人随调配偶在机关或者事业单位工作，符合有关法律法规规定的，安置地人民政府负责安排到相应的工作单位。对在其他单位工作或者无工作单位的随调随迁配偶，安置地人民政府应当提供就业指导，协助实现就业。  
对安排到企业事业单位的退役军人随调配偶，安置岗位需要签订聘用合同或者劳动合同的，用人单位应当与其签订不少于3年的中长期聘用合同或者劳动合同。  
鼓励和支持退役军人随调随迁家属自主就业创业。对有自主就业创业意愿的随调配偶，可以采取发放一次性就业补助费等措施进行安置，并提供就业指导服务。一次性就业补助费标准及发放办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。随调随迁家属按照规定享受就业创业扶持相关优惠政策。  
退役军人随调配偶应当与退役军人同时接收安置，同时发出报到通知。

**Article 57** If the spouse of an ex-serviceman who is transferred works in an organ or public institution and meets the relevant laws and regulations, the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall be responsible for arranging a corresponding work unit. For spouses who work in other units or are unemployed, the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall provide employment guidance and assist in achieving employment.  
For spouses transferred with ex-servicemen who are assigned to enterprises or public institutions, if the assigned position requires the signing of an employment contract or labor contract, the employer shall sign a medium- to long-term employment contract or labor contract of not less than three years.  
The state encourages and supports the family members of ex-servicemen who are transferred and relocated to seek self-employment or start their own businesses. For transferred spouses who have the will to seek self-employment or start businesses, measures such as issuing a one-time employment subsidy may be adopted for resettlement, and employment guidance services shall be provided. The standard and issuance method of the one-time employment subsidy shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The transferred and relocated family members shall enjoy relevant preferential policies for employment and entrepreneurship support according to regulations.  
The spouse of an ex-serviceman who is transferred shall be received and resettled simultaneously with the ex-serviceman, and the registration notification shall be issued at the same time.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　退役军人随调随迁家属户口的迁移、登记等手续，由安置地公安机关根据退役军人工作主管部门的通知及时办理。  
退役军人随迁子女需要转学、入学的，安置地人民政府教育行政部门应当及时办理。

**Article 58** The procedures for the transfer and registration of household registration of the family members of ex-servicemen who are transferred and relocated shall be promptly handled by the public security organ at the place of resettlement based on the notification from the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen.  
If the children of ex-servicemen who are relocated need to transfer schools or enroll, the education administrative department of the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall promptly handle the matter.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　转业军官和安排工作的退役军士自愿到艰苦边远地区工作的，其随调随迁配偶和子女可以在原符合安置条件的地区安置。

**Article 59** If job-transferred military officers and retired non-commissioned officers assigned to work voluntarily work in hard and remote areas, their transferred and relocated spouses and children may be resettled in the areas where they originally met the resettlement conditions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　退休军官、军士随迁配偶和子女的落户、各项社会保险关系转移接续以及随迁子女转学、入学，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 60** The settlement, transfer and continuation of various social insurance relationships of the spouses and children of retired military officers and non-commissioned officers who are relocated, as well as the transfer and enrollment of their relocated children, shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第六章　教育培训

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　退役军人离队前，所在部队在保证完成军事任务的前提下，应当根据需要开展教育培训，介绍国家改革发展形势，宣讲退役军人安置政策，组织法律法规和保密纪律等方面的教育。县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门应当给予支持配合。

**Article 61** Before ex-servicemen leave their units, the units they belong to shall, on the premise of ensuring the completion of military tasks, carry out education and training as needed, introduce the national reform and development situation, publicize the ex-servicemen resettlement policy, and organize education on laws, regulations, and confidentiality discipline. The competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide support and cooperation.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　军人退出现役后，退役军人工作主管部门和其他负责退役军人安置工作的部门应当区分不同安置方式的退役军人，组织适应性培训。  
对符合条件的退役军人，县级以上人民政府退役军人工作主管部门可以组织专业培训。

**Article 62** After military personnel retire from active service, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen and other departments responsible for ex-servicemen resettlement shall organize adaptive training based on the different resettlement methods of ex-servicemen.  
For ex-servicemen who meet the conditions, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the people's government at or above the county level may organize professional training.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　符合条件的退役军人定岗后，安置地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门、接收安置单位可以根据岗位需要和本人实际，选派到高等学校或者相关教育培训机构进行专项学习培训。退役军人参加专项学习培训期间同等享受所在单位相关待遇。

**Article 63** After eligible ex-servicemen are assigned to specific positions, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at the place of resettlement and the receiving and resettling unit may, based on the needs of the position and the individual's actual situation, select them to attend higher education institutions or relevant education and training institutions for specialized study and training. During the period of specialized study and training, ex-servicemen shall equally enjoy the relevant benefits of their unit.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　退役军人依法享受教育优待政策。  
退役军人在达到法定退休年龄前参加职业技能培训的，按照规定享受职业技能培训补贴等相应扶持政策。

**Article 64** Ex-servicemen shall enjoy preferential education policies according to law.  
If ex-servicemen participate in vocational skills training before reaching the statutory retirement age, they shall enjoy corresponding support policies such as vocational skills training subsidies according to regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　退役军人教育培训的规划、组织协调、督促检查、补助发放工作，以及师资、教学设施等方面保障，由退役军人工作主管部门和教育培训行政主管部门按照分工负责。

**Article 65** The planning, organization and coordination, supervision and inspection, and subsidy distribution of education and training for ex-servicemen, as well as the guarantee of teaching staff and teaching facilities, shall be the responsibility of the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen and the competent administrative department for education and training according to their respective division of work.

### 第七章　就业创业扶持

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家采取政府推动、市场引导、社会支持相结合的方式，鼓励和扶持退役军人就业创业。以逐月领取退役金、自主就业、复员方式安置的退役军人，按照规定享受相应就业创业扶持政策。

**Article 66** The state adopts a combination of government promotion, market guidance, and social support to encourage and support ex-servicemen in employment and entrepreneurship. Ex-servicemen resettled through monthly pension receipt, self-employment, or demobilization shall enjoy corresponding employment and entrepreneurship support policies according to regulations.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　各级人民政府应当加强对退役军人就业创业的指导和服务。县级以上地方人民政府每年应当组织开展退役军人专场招聘活动，帮助退役军人就业。  
对符合当地就业困难人员认定条件的退役军人，安置地人民政府应当将其纳入就业援助范围。对其中确实难以通过市场实现就业的，依法纳入公益性岗位保障范围。

**Article 67** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen guidance and services for ex-servicemen's employment and entrepreneurship. The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize special recruitment activities for ex-servicemen every year to help them find employment.  
For ex-servicemen who meet the conditions for being identified as persons with employment difficulties in the locality, the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall include them in the scope of employment assistance. For those who are truly unable to find employment through the market, they shall be included in the scope of public welfare job guarantees according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　机关、群团组织、事业单位和国有企业在招录或者招聘人员时，对退役军人的年龄和学历条件可以适当放宽，同等条件下优先招录、招聘退役军人。退役军官在军队团和相当于团以下单位工作的经历，退役军士和义务兵服现役的经历，视为基层工作经历。  
各地应当设置一定数量的基层公务员职位，面向服现役满5年的高校毕业生退役军人招考。  
用人单位招用退役军人符合国家规定的，依法享受税收优惠等政策。

**Article 68** When organs, mass organizations, public institutions, and state-owned enterprises recruit or hire personnel, they may appropriately relax the age and education conditions for ex-servicemen, and give priority to recruiting or hiring ex-servicemen under equal conditions. The experience of retired military officers working in military regiments and units below the regiment level, and the active service experience of retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen, shall be regarded as grassroots work experience.  
All localities shall set a certain number of grassroots civil servant positions for the recruitment of college graduate ex-servicemen who have served on active duty for at least five years.  
If an employer hires ex-servicemen in compliance with state regulations, it shall enjoy tax incentives and other policies according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　自主就业的退役军士和义务兵入伍前是机关、群团组织、事业单位或者国有企业人员的，退出现役后可以选择复职复工，其工资、福利待遇不得低于本单位同等条件人员的平均水平。

**Article 69** If retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are self-employed were personnel of organs, mass organizations, public institutions, or state-owned enterprises before enlistment, they may choose to return to their positions and resume work after retirement from active service, and their wages and welfare benefits shall not be lower than the average level of personnel with equivalent conditions in the same unit.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　自主就业的退役军士和义务兵入伍前通过家庭承包方式承包的农村土地，承包期内不得违法收回或者强迫、阻碍土地经营权流转；通过招标、拍卖、公开协商等非家庭承包方式承包的农村土地，承包期内其家庭成员可以继续承包；承包的农村土地被依法征收、征用或者占用的，与其他农村集体经济组织成员享有同等权利。  
符合条件的复员军官、自主就业的退役军士和义务兵回入伍时户口所在地落户，属于农村集体经济组织成员但没有承包农村土地的，可以申请承包农村土地，农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会、村民小组应当优先解决。

**Article 70** For rural land contracted by retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are self-employed through household contracting before enlistment, the land shall not be illegally taken back or the transfer of land management rights shall not be forced or hindered during the contract period; for rural land contracted through non-household contracting methods such as bidding, auction, or open negotiation, their family members may continue the contract during the contract period; if the contracted rural land is lawfully expropriated, requisitioned, or occupied, they shall enjoy equal rights with other members of the rural collective economic organization.  
If eligible demobilized military officers and self-employed retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen return to their original place of household registration before enlistment and are members of a rural collective economic organization but have no contracted rural land, they may apply to contract rural land, and the rural collective economic organization or the villagers' committee or villagers' group shall give priority to resolving the matter.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　服现役期间因战、因公、因病致残被评定残疾等级和退役后补评或者重新评定残疾等级的残疾退役军人，有劳动能力和就业意愿的，优先享受国家规定的残疾人就业优惠政策。退役军人所在单位不得因其残疾而辞退、解除聘用合同或者劳动合同。

**Article 71** For disabled ex-servicemen who were assessed with a disability grade due to war, duty, or illness during active service, or who have a supplementary or re-assessment of disability grade after retirement, if they have the ability to work and the will to seek employment, they shall enjoy priority in the preferential employment policies for persons with disabilities stipulated by the state. The unit where the ex-serviceman works shall not dismiss him or terminate the employment contract or labor contract due to his disability.

### 第八章　待遇保障

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　退休军官的政治待遇按照安置地国家机关相应职务层次退休公务员有关规定执行。退休军官和军士的生活待遇按照军队统一的项目和标准执行。

**Article 72** The political treatment of retired military officers shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions for retired civil servants of corresponding ranks in state organs at the place of resettlement. The living treatment of retired military officers and non-commissioned officers shall be implemented in accordance with the unified items and standards of the military.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　转业军官的待遇保障按照国家有关规定执行。  
安排工作的退役军士和义务兵的工资待遇按照国家有关规定确定，享受接收安置单位同等条件人员的其他相关待遇。

**Article 73** The treatment and guarantee of job-transferred military officers shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
The salary treatment of retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen assigned to work shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, and they shall enjoy other relevant benefits of personnel with equivalent conditions in the receiving and resettling unit.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　退役军人服现役年限计算为工龄，退役后与所在单位工作年限累计计算，享受国家和所在单位规定的与工龄有关的相应待遇。其中，安排工作的退役军士和义务兵的服现役年限以及符合本条例规定的待安排工作时间合并计算为工龄。

**Article 74** The years of active service of ex-servicemen shall be counted as length of service, and shall be cumulatively calculated with the years of work in their unit after retirement, and they shall enjoy the corresponding benefits related to length of service as stipulated by the state and their unit. For retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen assigned to work, their years of active service and the period of awaiting assignment that meets the provisions of these Regulations shall be combined and calculated as length of service.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　安排工作的退役军士和义务兵待安排工作期间，安置地人民政府应当按照当地月最低工资标准逐月发放生活补助。  
接收安置单位应当在安排工作介绍信开具30日内，安排退役军士和义务兵上岗。非因退役军士和义务兵本人原因，接收安置单位未按照规定安排上岗的，应当从介绍信开具当月起，按照不低于本单位同等条件人员平均工资80%的标准，逐月发放生活费直至上岗为止。

**Article 75** During the period when retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen assigned to work are awaiting assignment, the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall pay a monthly living allowance according to the local monthly minimum wage standard.  
The receiving and resettling unit shall arrange for the retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen to take up their posts within 30 days from the issuance of the job assignment letter. If, for reasons not attributable to the retired non-commissioned officers or compulsory servicemen themselves, the receiving and resettling unit fails to arrange for them to take up their posts as required, it shall, from the month when the letter is issued, pay a monthly living allowance at a rate not less than 80% of the average wage of personnel with equivalent conditions in the same unit until they take up their posts.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　军人服现役期间享受的残疾抚恤金、护理费等其他待遇，退出现役移交地方后按照地方有关规定执行。退休军官和军士享受的护理费等生活待遇按照军队有关规定执行。

**Article 76** Other benefits enjoyed by military personnel during active service, such as disability pension and nursing expenses, shall be implemented in accordance with local relevant provisions after retirement from active service and transfer to local authorities. The living benefits such as nursing expenses enjoyed by retired military officers and non-commissioned officers shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　符合条件的退役军人申请保障性住房和农村危房改造的，同等条件下予以优先安排。  
退役军人符合安置住房优待条件的，实行市场购买与军地集中统建相结合的方式解决安置住房，由安置地人民政府统筹规划、科学实施。

**Article 77** If eligible ex-servicemen apply for affordable housing or rural dilapidated house renovation, they shall be given priority arrangements under equal conditions.  
If ex-servicemen meet the preferential conditions for resettlement housing, the resettlement housing shall be provided through a combination of market purchase and centralized unified construction by the military and local authorities, which shall be coordinated and scientifically implemented by the local people's government at the place of resettlement.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　分散供养的退役军士和义务兵购（建）房所需经费的标准，按照安置地县（市、区、旗）经济适用住房平均价格和60平方米的建筑面积确定；没有经济适用住房的地区按照普通商品住房价格确定。所购（建）房屋产权归分散供养的退役军士和义务兵所有，依法办理不动产登记。  
分散供养的退役军士和义务兵自行解决住房的，按照前款规定的标准将购（建）房费用发给本人。

**Article 78** The standard of funds needed for the purchase (construction) of housing by retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are provided with decentralized support shall be determined based on the average price of affordable housing in the county (city, district, banner) of the place of resettlement and a construction area of 60 square meters; in areas without affordable housing, it shall be determined based on the price of ordinary commercial housing. The property rights of the purchased (constructed) house shall belong to the retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are provided with decentralized support, and real estate registration shall be handled according to law.  
If retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen who are provided with decentralized support resolve their housing by themselves, the costs for purchasing (constructing) housing shall be paid to them in accordance with the standard specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　军官和军士退出现役时，服现役期间的住房公积金按照规定一次性发给本人，也可以根据本人意愿转移接续到安置地，并按照当地规定缴存、使用住房公积金；服现役期间的住房补贴发放按照有关规定执行。

**Article 79** When military officers and non-commissioned officers retire from active service, the housing provident fund during their active service shall be paid to them in a lump sum according to regulations, or may be transferred and continued to the place of resettlement according to their wishes, and the housing provident fund shall be deposited and used in accordance with local regulations; the issuance of housing subsidies during active service shall be carried out in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　退役军人服现役期间获得功勋荣誉表彰的，退出现役后依法享受相应待遇。

**Article 80** If ex-servicemen receive meritorious honors and commendations during active service, they shall enjoy corresponding benefits according to law after retirement from active service.

### 第九章　社会保险

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　军人退出现役时，军队按照规定转移军人保险关系和相应资金，安置地社会保险经办机构应当及时办理相应的转移接续手续。  
退役军人依法参加养老、医疗、工伤、失业、生育等社会保险，缴纳社会保险费，享受社会保险待遇。  
退役军人服现役年限与入伍前、退役后参加社会保险的缴费年限依法合并计算。

**Article 81** When military personnel retire from active service, the military shall transfer the military insurance relationship and corresponding funds according to regulations, and the social insurance agency at the place of resettlement shall promptly handle the corresponding transfer and continuation procedures.  
Ex-servicemen shall participate in social insurance such as old-age, medical, work-related injury, unemployment, and maternity insurance according to law, pay social insurance premiums, and enjoy social insurance benefits.  
The years of active service of ex-servicemen and the years of contribution to social insurance before enlistment and after retirement shall be merged and calculated according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　安排工作的退役军士和义务兵在国家规定的待安排工作期间，按照规定参加安置地职工基本养老保险并享受相应待遇，所需费用由安置地人民政府同级财政资金安排。

**Article 82** During the period of awaiting assignment as stipulated by the state, retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen assigned to work shall participate in the basic old-age insurance for employees at the place of resettlement according to regulations and enjoy corresponding benefits. The required expenses shall be arranged by the fiscal funds of the same level of the local people's government at the place of resettlement.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　安置到机关、群团组织、企业事业单位的退役军人，依法参加职工基本医疗保险并享受相应待遇。  
安排工作的退役军士和义务兵在国家规定的待安排工作期间，依法参加安置地职工基本医疗保险并享受相应待遇，单位缴费部分由安置地人民政府缴纳，个人缴费部分由个人缴纳。  
逐月领取退役金的退役军官和军士、复员军官、自主就业的退役军士和义务兵依法参加职工基本医疗保险或者城乡居民基本医疗保险并享受相应待遇。

**Article 83** Ex-servicemen resettled in organs, mass organizations, enterprises, or public institutions shall participate in the basic medical insurance for employees according to law and enjoy corresponding benefits.  
Retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen assigned to work, during the period of awaiting assignment as stipulated by the state, shall participate in the basic medical insurance for employees at the place of resettlement according to law and enjoy corresponding benefits. The employer contribution portion shall be paid by the local people's government at the place of resettlement, and the individual contribution portion shall be paid by the individual.  
Retired military officers and non-commissioned officers receiving monthly pensions, demobilized military officers, and self-employed retired non-commissioned officers and compulsory servicemen shall participate in the basic medical insurance for employees or the basic medical insurance for urban and rural residents according to law and enjoy corresponding benefits.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　退休军官和军士移交人民政府安置后，由安置地人民政府按照有关规定纳入医疗保险和相关医疗补助。  
退休军官享受安置地国家机关相应职务层次退休公务员的医疗待遇，退休军士医疗待遇参照退休军官有关规定执行。

**Article 84** After retired military officers and non-commissioned officers are transferred to the people's government for resettlement, the local people's government at the place of resettlement shall include them in medical insurance and relevant medical subsidies according to relevant provisions.  
Retired military officers shall enjoy the medical treatment of retired civil servants of corresponding ranks in state organs at the place of resettlement, and the medical treatment of retired non-commissioned officers shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions for retired military officers.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　退役军人未及时就业的，可以依法向户口所在地人力资源社会保障部门申领失业保险待遇，服现役年限视同参保缴费年限，但是以退休、供养方式安置的退役军人除外。

**Article 85** If ex-servicemen fail to find employment promptly, they may apply for unemployment insurance benefits to the human resources and social security department at their place of household registration according to law, and the years of active service shall be deemed as years of insurance contribution, except for ex-servicemen resettled through retirement or support.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　退役军人随调随迁家属，已经参加社会保险的，其社会保险关系和相应资金转移接续由社会保险经办机构依法办理。

**Article 86** For family members of ex-servicemen who are transferred and relocated and have already participated in social insurance, the transfer and continuation of their social insurance relationship and corresponding funds shall be handled by the social insurance agency according to law.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　退役军人工作主管部门和其他负责退役军人安置工作的部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由其上级主管部门责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）违反国家政策另设接收条件、提高安置门槛的；  
（二）未按照规定确定退役军人安置待遇的；  
（三）在退役军人安置工作中出具虚假文件的；  
（四）挪用、截留、私分退役军人安置工作经费的；  
（五）在退役军人安置工作中利用职务之便为自己或者他人谋取私利的；  
（六）有其他违反退役军人安置法律法规行为的。

**Article 87** If the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen and other departments responsible for ex-servicemen resettlement and their staff members commit any of the following acts, the competent department at the next higher level shall order them to make corrections, and the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Setting additional reception conditions or raising the resettlement threshold in violation of national policies;  
(2) Failing to determine the resettlement benefits of ex-servicemen in accordance with regulations;  
(3) Issuing false documents in the work of ex-servicemen resettlement;  
(4) Misappropriating, intercepting, or privately dividing the funds for ex-servicemen resettlement;  
(5) Taking advantage of their positions to seek personal gain for themselves or others in the work of ex-servicemen resettlement;  
(6) Other acts violating the laws and regulations on ex-servicemen resettlement.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　接收安置退役军人的单位及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由当地人民政府退役军人工作主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，予以通报批评，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）拒绝或者无故拖延执行退役军人安置计划的；  
（二）在国家政策之外另设接收条件、提高安置门槛的；  
（三）将接收安置退役军人编制截留、挪用的；  
（四）未按照规定落实退役军人安置待遇的；  
（五）未依法与退役军人签订聘用合同或者劳动合同的；  
（六）违法与残疾退役军人解除聘用合同或者劳动合同的；  
（七）有其他违反退役军人安置法律法规行为的。  
对干扰退役军人安置工作、损害退役军人合法权益的其他单位和个人，依法追究责任。

**Article 88** If a unit that receives and resettles ex-servicemen or its staff members commit any of the following acts, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government shall order them to make corrections within a time limit; if they fail to make corrections within the time limit, they shall be given a notice of criticism, and the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Refusing or delaying without justifiable reasons the implementation of the ex-servicemen resettlement plan;  
(2) Setting additional reception conditions or raising the resettlement threshold beyond national policies;  
(3) Intercepting or misappropriating the staffing quotas for receiving and resettling ex-servicemen;  
(4) Failing to implement the resettlement benefits for ex-servicemen in accordance with regulations;  
(5) Failing to sign an employment contract or labor contract with ex-servicemen in accordance with law;  
(6) Unlawfully terminating an employment contract or labor contract with a disabled ex-serviceman;  
(7) Other acts violating the laws and regulations on ex-servicemen resettlement.  
Other units or individuals that interfere with the ex-servicemen resettlement work or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of ex-servicemen shall be held liable according to law.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　退役军人弄虚作假骗取安置待遇的，由县级以上地方人民政府退役军人工作主管部门取消相关待遇，追缴非法所得，依法追究责任。

**Article 89** If an ex-serviceman obtains resettlement benefits by fraud or falsehood, the competent department for the work of ex-servicemen of the local people's government at or above the county level shall cancel the relevant benefits, recover the illegal gains, and hold him liable according to law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 90** Violations of the provisions of these Regulations that constitute a violation of public security administration shall be given public security administration punishment according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　中国人民武装警察部队依法退出现役的警官、警士和义务兵等人员的安置，适用本条例。  
本条例有关军官的规定适用于军队文职干部。  
士兵制度改革后未进行军衔转换士官的退役安置，参照本条例有关规定执行。

**Article 91** These Regulations shall apply to the resettlement of officers, non-commissioned officers, and compulsory servicemen of the Chinese People's Armed Police Force who retire from active service according to law.  
The provisions of these Regulations concerning military officers shall apply to civilian cadres of the military.  
The resettlement of non-commissioned officers who have not undergone rank conversion after the reform of the soldier system shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　军官离职休养和少将以上军官退休后，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定安置管理。  
军队院校学员依法退出现役的，按照国家有关规定执行。  
已经按照自主择业方式安置的退役军人的待遇保障，按照国务院和中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 92** The resettlement and management of military officers who retire with leave or who are retired at the rank of major general or above shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
Cadets of military academies who retire from active service according to law shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
The treatment and guarantee of ex-servicemen who have been resettled through the self-selected employment method shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　本条例自2024年9月1日起施行。《退役士兵安置条例》同时废止。

**Article 93** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2024. The Regulations on the Resettlement of Retired Compulsory Servicemen shall be abolished simultaneously.
