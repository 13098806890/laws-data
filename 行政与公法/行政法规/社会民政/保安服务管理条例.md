# 保安服务管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-29  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

> （2009年10月13日中华人民共和国国务院令第564号公布　根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2022年3月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范保安服务活动，加强对从事保安服务的单位和保安员的管理，保护人身安全和财产安全，维护社会治安，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称保安服务是指： （一）保安服务公司根据保安服务合同，派出保安员为客户单位提供的门卫、巡逻、守护、押运、随身护卫、安全检查以及安全技术防范、安全风险评估等服务； （二）机关、团体、企业、事业单位招用人员从事的本单位门卫、巡逻、守护等安全防范工作； （三）物业服务企业招用人员在物业管理区域内开展的门卫、巡逻、秩序维护等服务。 前款第（二）项、第（三）项中的机关、团体、企业、事业单位和物业服务企业，统称自行招用保安员的单位。 第三条　国务院公安部门负责全国保安服务活动的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府公安机关负责本行政区域内保安服务活动的监督管理工作。 保安服务行业协会在公安机关的指导下，依法开展保安服务行业自律活动。 第四条　保安服务公司和自行招用保安员的单位（以下统称保安从业单位）应当建立健全保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度和保安员管理制度，加强对保安员的管理、教育和培训，提高保安员的职业道德水平、业务素质和责任意识。 第五条　保安从业单位应当依法保障保安员在社会保险、劳动用工、劳动保护、工资福利、教育培训等方面的合法权益。 第六条　保安服务活动应当文明、合法，不得损害社会公共利益或者侵犯他人合法权益。 保安员依法从事保安服务活动，受法律保护。 第七条　对在保护公共财产和人民群众生命财产安全、预防和制止违法犯罪活动中有突出贡献的保安从业单位和保安员，公安机关和其他有关部门应当给予表彰、奖励。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范保安服务活动，加强对从事保安服务的单位和保安员的管理，保护人身安全和财产安全，维护社会治安，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating security service activities, strengthening the administration of entities engaged in security services and security guards, protecting personal safety and property safety, and maintaining public order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称保安服务是指：  
（一）保安服务公司根据保安服务合同，派出保安员为客户单位提供的门卫、巡逻、守护、押运、随身护卫、安全检查以及安全技术防范、安全风险评估等服务；  
（二）机关、团体、企业、事业单位招用人员从事的本单位门卫、巡逻、守护等安全防范工作；  
（三）物业服务企业招用人员在物业管理区域内开展的门卫、巡逻、秩序维护等服务。  
前款第（二）项、第（三）项中的机关、团体、企业、事业单位和物业服务企业，统称自行招用保安员的单位。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "security service" means:  
(1) services such as entrance guarding, patrol, guarding, escort, personal protection, security inspection, and security technical prevention and security risk assessment provided by a security service company dispatching security guards to a client entity under a security service contract;  
(2) security prevention work such as entrance guarding, patrol and guarding conducted within an entity by personnel employed by a state organ, organization, enterprise, or institution; and  
(3) services such as entrance guarding, patrol, and order maintenance provided by a property service enterprise employing personnel within the property management area.  
The state organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions and property service enterprises referred to in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph are collectively referred to as entities that employ security guards on their own.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院公安部门负责全国保安服务活动的监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府公安机关负责本行政区域内保安服务活动的监督管理工作。  
保安服务行业协会在公安机关的指导下，依法开展保安服务行业自律活动。

**Article 3** The public security department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of security service activities nationwide. The public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of security service activities within their respective administrative areas.  
The trade association for the security service industry shall, under the guidance of the public security organs, carry out self-disciplinary activities in the security service industry according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保安服务公司和自行招用保安员的单位（以下统称保安从业单位）应当建立健全保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度和保安员管理制度，加强对保安员的管理、教育和培训，提高保安员的职业道德水平、业务素质和责任意识。

**Article 4** A security service company or an entity that employs security guards on its own (hereinafter collectively referred to as a "security service entity") shall establish and improve the security service management system, the post responsibility system and the security guard management system, strengthen the management, education and training of security guards, and improve the professional ethics, professional competence and sense of responsibility of security guards.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　保安从业单位应当依法保障保安员在社会保险、劳动用工、劳动保护、工资福利、教育培训等方面的合法权益。

**Article 5** A security service entity shall, according to law, protect the lawful rights and interests of security guards in respect of social insurance, labor employment, labor protection, wages and welfare, education and training, etc.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　保安服务活动应当文明、合法，不得损害社会公共利益或者侵犯他人合法权益。  
保安员依法从事保安服务活动，受法律保护。

**Article 6** Security service activities shall be conducted in a civilized and lawful manner, and shall not damage the public interest or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others.  
Security guards who engage in security service activities according to law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在保护公共财产和人民群众生命财产安全、预防和制止违法犯罪活动中有突出贡献的保安从业单位和保安员，公安机关和其他有关部门应当给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** The public security organs and other relevant departments shall give commendations and rewards to security service entities and security guards that have made outstanding contributions in protecting public property and the safety of people's lives and property, and in preventing and stopping illegal and criminal activities.

### 第二章　保安服务公司

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保安服务公司应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有不低于人民币100万元的注册资本；  
（二）拟任的保安服务公司法定代表人和主要管理人员应当具备任职所需的专业知识和有关业务工作经验，无被刑事处罚、劳动教养、收容教育、强制隔离戒毒或者被开除公职、开除军籍等不良记录；  
（三）有与所提供的保安服务相适应的专业技术人员，其中法律、行政法规有资格要求的专业技术人员，应当取得相应的资格；  
（四）有住所和提供保安服务所需的设施、装备；  
（五）有健全的组织机构和保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度、保安员管理制度。

**Article 8** A security service company shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a registered capital of not less than RMB 1 million yuan;  
(2) the proposed legal representative and key management personnel of the security service company shall have the professional knowledge and relevant work experience required for the position, and have no adverse records such as criminal punishment, reeducation through labor, shelter and reeducation, compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, or being discharged from public employment or expelled from military service;  
(3) having professional and technical personnel commensurate with the security services to be provided, among whom those for whom laws or administrative regulations prescribe qualification requirements shall have obtained the corresponding qualifications;  
(4) having a place of business and the facilities and equipment necessary for providing security services; and  
(5) having a sound organizational structure, security service management system, post responsibility system, and security guard management system.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立保安服务公司，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关提交申请书以及能够证明其符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定条件的材料。  
受理的公安机关应当自收到申请材料之日起15日内进行审核，并将审核意见报所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关应当自收到审核意见之日起15日内作出决定，对符合条件的，核发保安服务许可证；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 9** An applicant for establishing a security service company shall submit a written application and the materials capable of proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 8 of these Regulations to the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where it is located.  
The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the application materials, conduct a review and submit its review opinion to the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the place where it is located. The public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the review opinion, make a decision; for those that meet the conditions, it shall issue a security service permit; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　从事武装守护押运服务的保安服务公司，应当符合国务院公安部门对武装守护押运服务的规划、布局要求，具备[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的条件，并符合下列条件：  
（一）有不低于人民币1000万元的注册资本；  
（二）国有独资或者国有资本占注册资本总额的51%以上；  
（三）有符合《专职守护押运人员枪支使用管理条例》规定条件的守护押运人员；  
（四）有符合国家标准或者行业标准的专用运输车辆以及通信、报警设备。

**Article 10** A security service company engaging in armed guarding and escort services shall meet the planning and layout requirements of the public security department under the State Council for armed guarding and escort services, meet the conditions prescribed in Article 8 of these Regulations, and also meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a registered capital of not less than RMB 10 million yuan;  
(2) being wholly state-owned or having state-owned capital accounting for not less than 51% of the total registered capital;  
(3) having guard and escort personnel who meet the conditions prescribed in the Regulations on the Administration of the Use of Firearms by Full-time Guard and Escort Personnel; and  
(4) having special transport vehicles and communication and alarm equipment that comply with national standards or industry standards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请设立从事武装守护押运服务的保安服务公司，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关提交申请书以及能够证明其符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)、第十条规定条件的材料。保安服务公司申请增设武装守护押运业务的，无需再次提交证明其符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定条件的材料。  
受理的公安机关应当自收到申请材料之日起15日内进行审核，并将审核意见报所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关应当自收到审核意见之日起15日内作出决定，对符合条件的，核发从事武装守护押运业务的保安服务许可证或者在已有的保安服务许可证上增注武装守护押运服务；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 11** An applicant for establishing a security service company engaging in armed guarding and escort services shall submit a written application and the materials capable of proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Articles 8 and 10 of these Regulations to the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where it is located. If a security service company applies to add armed guarding and escort services, it is not required to submit again the materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 8 of these Regulations.  
The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the application materials, conduct a review and submit its review opinion to the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the place where it is located. The public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 15 days from the date of receiving the review opinion, make a decision; for those that meet the conditions, it shall issue a security service permit for armed guarding and escort services or add a notation of armed guarding and escort services on the existing security service permit; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　取得保安服务许可证的申请人，凭保安服务许可证到工商行政管理机关办理工商登记。取得保安服务许可证后超过6个月未办理工商登记的，取得的保安服务许可证失效。  
保安服务公司设立分公司的，应当向分公司所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关备案。备案应当提供总公司的保安服务许可证和工商营业执照，总公司法定代表人、分公司负责人和保安员的基本情况。  
保安服务公司的法定代表人变更的，应当经原审批公安机关审核，持审核文件到工商行政管理机关办理变更登记。

**Article 12** An applicant who has obtained a security service permit shall, on the strength of the security service permit, go through industrial and commercial registration with the administrative authority for industry and commerce. If the applicant fails to go through industrial and commercial registration within six months after obtaining the security service permit, the obtained security service permit shall cease to have effect.  
If a security service company establishes a branch, it shall file a record with the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where the branch is located. The record shall include the security service permit and the industrial and commercial business license of the head office, and the basic information of the legal representative of the head office, the person in charge of the branch, and the security guards.  
If the legal representative of a security service company changes, the company shall undergo examination by the original public security organ that approved the permit, and shall, on the strength of the examination document, go through the modification of registration with the administrative authority for industry and commerce.

### 第三章　自行招用保安员的单位

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　自行招用保安员的单位应当具有法人资格，有符合本条例规定条件的保安员，有健全的保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度和保安员管理制度。  
娱乐场所应当依照《娱乐场所管理条例》的规定，从保安服务公司聘用保安员，不得自行招用保安员。

**Article 13** An entity that employs security guards on its own shall have the status of a legal person, have security guards meeting the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, and have a sound security service management system, post responsibility system, and security guard management system.  
An entertainment venue shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Entertainment Venues, employ security guards from a security service company, and shall not employ security guards on its own.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　自行招用保安员的单位，应当自开始保安服务之日起30日内向所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关备案，备案应当提供下列材料：  
（一）法人资格证明；  
（二）法定代表人（主要负责人）、分管负责人和保安员的基本情况；  
（三）保安服务区域的基本情况；  
（四）建立保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度、保安员管理制度的情况。  
自行招用保安员的单位不再招用保安员进行保安服务的，应当自停止保安服务之日起30日内到备案的公安机关撤销备案。

**Article 14** An entity that employs security guards on its own shall, within 30 days from the date of commencing security services, file a record with the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where it is located, and the record shall provide the following materials:  
(1) a certificate of legal person status;  
(2) the basic information of the legal representative (principal responsible person), the responsible person in charge, and the security guards;  
(3) the basic information of the security service area; and  
(4) the information on the establishment of a security service management system, a post responsibility system, and a security guard management system.  
If an entity that employs security guards on its own no longer employs security guards to provide security services, it shall, within 30 days from the date of ceasing security services, withdraw its record with the public security organ where it filed the record.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自行招用保安员的单位不得在本单位以外或者物业管理区域以外提供保安服务。

**Article 15** An entity that employs security guards on its own shall not provide security services outside its own premises or outside the property management area.

### 第四章　保　安　员

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　年满18周岁，身体健康，品行良好，具有初中以上学历的中国公民可以申领保安员证，从事保安服务工作。申请人经设区的市级人民政府公安机关考试、审查合格并留存指纹等人体生物信息的，发给保安员证。  
提取、留存保安员指纹等人体生物信息的具体办法，由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 16** A Chinese citizen who has reached the age of 18, is in good health, has good conduct, and has at least a junior high school education may apply for a security guard certificate and engage in security service work. If an applicant passes the examination and review by the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts and has his or her fingerprints and other human biological information collected and retained, a security guard certificate shall be issued.  
The specific measures for the collection and retention of fingerprints and other human biological information of security guards shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任保安员：  
（一）曾被收容教育、强制隔离戒毒、劳动教养或者3次以上行政拘留的；  
（二）曾因故意犯罪被刑事处罚的；  
（三）被吊销保安员证未满3年的；  
（四）曾两次被吊销保安员证的。

**Article 17** A person shall not serve as a security guard under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having ever been subjected to shelter and reeducation, compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, reeducation through labor, or administrative detention three times or more;  
(2) having ever been subjected to criminal punishment for an intentional crime;  
(3) having had his or her security guard certificate revoked for less than three years; or  
(4) having had his or her security guard certificate revoked twice.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　保安从业单位应当招用符合保安员条件的人员担任保安员，并与被招用的保安员依法签订劳动合同。保安从业单位及其保安员应当依法参加社会保险。  
保安从业单位应当根据保安服务岗位需要定期对保安员进行法律、保安专业知识和技能培训。

**Article 18** A security service entity shall employ persons who meet the conditions for security guards to serve as security guards, and shall, according to law, conclude labor contracts with the employed security guards. The security service entity and its security guards shall participate in social insurance according to law.  
A security service entity shall, based on the needs of security service posts, provide security guards with regular training in law, professional knowledge and skills of security.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保安从业单位应当定期对保安员进行考核，发现保安员不合格或者严重违反管理制度，需要解除劳动合同的，应当依法办理。

**Article 19** A security service entity shall conduct regular assessments of security guards. If it finds that a security guard is unqualified or has seriously violated the management system and needs to terminate the labor contract, it shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　保安从业单位应当根据保安服务岗位的风险程度为保安员投保意外伤害保险。  
保安员因工伤亡的，依照国家有关工伤保险的规定享受工伤保险待遇；保安员牺牲被批准为烈士的，依照国家有关烈士褒扬的规定享受抚恤优待。

**Article 20** A security service entity shall, based on the risk level of the security service post, purchase accidental injury insurance for security guards.  
If a security guard suffers work-related injury or death, he or she shall enjoy work-related injury insurance benefits in accordance with the state provisions on work-related injury insurance; if a security guard is sacrificed and approved as a martyr, he or she shall enjoy pension and preferential treatment in accordance with the state provisions on martyr commendation.

### 第五章　保安服务

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　保安服务公司提供保安服务应当与客户单位签订保安服务合同，明确规定服务的项目、内容以及双方的权利义务。保安服务合同终止后，保安服务公司应当将保安服务合同至少留存2年备查。  
保安服务公司应当对客户单位要求提供的保安服务的合法性进行核查，对违法的保安服务要求应当拒绝，并向公安机关报告。

**Article 21** A security service company shall, when providing security services, conclude a security service contract with the client entity, clearly specifying the items and contents of the services and the rights and obligations of both parties. After the termination of the security service contract, the security service company shall retain the security service contract for at least two years for future reference.  
A security service company shall verify the lawfulness of the security services requested by the client entity, shall reject any unlawful request for security services, and shall report the matter to the public security organ.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　设区的市级以上地方人民政府确定的关系国家安全、涉及国家秘密等治安保卫重点单位不得聘请外商投资的保安服务公司提供保安服务。

**Article 22** Key entities for public security protection that involve national security or state secrets, as determined by the local people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts, shall not engage a security service company with foreign investment to provide security services.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　保安服务公司派出保安员跨省、自治区、直辖市为客户单位提供保安服务的，应当向服务所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关备案。备案应当提供保安服务公司的保安服务许可证和工商营业执照、保安服务合同、服务项目负责人和保安员的基本情况。

**Article 23** If a security service company dispatches security guards to provide security services to a client entity across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall file a record with the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where the service is provided. The record shall include the security service permit and the industrial and commercial business license of the security service company, the security service contract, and the basic information of the person in charge of the service project and the security guards.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　保安服务公司应当按照保安服务业服务标准提供规范的保安服务，保安服务公司派出的保安员应当遵守客户单位的有关规章制度。客户单位应当为保安员从事保安服务提供必要的条件和保障。

**Article 24** A security service company shall provide standardized security services in accordance with the service standards of the security service industry. The security guards dispatched by a security service company shall comply with the relevant rules and regulations of the client entity. The client entity shall provide the necessary conditions and guarantees for the security guards to engage in security services.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　保安服务中使用的技术防范产品，应当符合有关的产品质量要求。保安服务中安装监控设备应当遵守国家有关技术规范，使用监控设备不得侵犯他人合法权益或者个人隐私。  
保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录，应当至少留存30日备查，保安从业单位和客户单位不得删改或者扩散。

**Article 25** The technical prevention products used in security services shall meet the relevant product quality requirements. The installation of monitoring equipment in security services shall comply with the relevant state technical specifications, and the use of monitoring equipment shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests or personal privacy of others.  
The monitoring image data and alarm records generated in security services shall be retained for at least 30 days for future reference, and the security service entity and the client entity shall not delete, alter, or disseminate them.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　保安从业单位对保安服务中获知的国家秘密、商业秘密以及客户单位明确要求保密的信息，应当予以保密。  
保安从业单位不得指使、纵容保安员阻碍依法执行公务、参与追索债务、采用暴力或者以暴力相威胁的手段处置纠纷。

**Article 26** A security service entity shall keep confidential the state secrets, trade secrets, and information explicitly required to be kept confidential by the client entity that it obtains during security services.  
A security service entity shall not instruct or connive at security guards in obstructing the execution of official duties according to law, participating in debt collection, or using violence or threats of violence to settle disputes.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　保安员上岗应当着保安员服装，佩带全国统一的保安服务标志。保安员服装和保安服务标志应当与人民解放军、人民武装警察和人民警察、工商税务等行政执法机关以及人民法院、人民检察院工作人员的制式服装、标志服饰有明显区别。  
保安员服装由全国保安服务行业协会推荐式样，由保安服务从业单位在推荐式样范围内选用。保安服务标志式样由全国保安服务行业协会确定。

**Article 27** A security guard shall, when on duty, wear the security guard uniform and display the nationally uniform security service emblem. The security guard uniform and security service emblem shall be clearly distinguishable from the uniforms, insignia and apparel of the People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police, the people's police, the administrative law enforcement organs such as industrial and commercial administration and taxation, and the staff of the People's Courts and the People's Procuratorates.  
The styles of security guard uniforms shall be recommended by the national trade association for the security service industry, and security service entities shall select from among the recommended styles. The style of the security service emblem shall be determined by the national trade association for the security service industry.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　保安从业单位应当根据保安服务岗位的需要为保安员配备所需的装备。保安服务岗位装备配备标准由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 28** A security service entity shall, based on the needs of the security service post, equip security guards with necessary equipment. The standards for equipping security service posts shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在保安服务中，为履行保安服务职责，保安员可以采取下列措施：  
（一）查验出入服务区域的人员的证件，登记出入的车辆和物品；  
（二）在服务区域内进行巡逻、守护、安全检查、报警监控；  
（三）在机场、车站、码头等公共场所对人员及其所携带的物品进行安全检查，维护公共秩序；  
（四）执行武装守护押运任务，可以根据任务需要设立临时隔离区，但应当尽可能减少对公民正常活动的妨碍。  
保安员应当及时制止发生在服务区域内的违法犯罪行为，对制止无效的违法犯罪行为应当立即报警，同时采取措施保护现场。  
从事武装守护押运服务的保安员执行武装守护押运任务使用枪支，依照《专职守护押运人员枪支使用管理条例》的规定执行。

**Article 29** In security services, a security guard may take the following measures to perform his or her security service duties:  
(1) checking the credentials of persons entering or leaving the service area, and registering vehicles and articles entering or leaving the area;  
(2) conducting patrols, guarding, security inspections, and alarm monitoring within the service area;  
(3) conducting security inspections of persons and articles carried by them in public places such as airports, railway stations, and docks to maintain public order; and  
(4) when carrying out armed guarding and escort missions, setting up temporary exclusion zones as required by the mission, provided that the interference with citizens' normal activities is minimized to the extent possible.  
A security guard shall promptly stop illegal and criminal acts occurring within the service area; if such acts cannot be stopped, he or she shall immediately report to the police and take measures to protect the scene.  
When a security guard engaged in armed guarding and escort services uses firearms in carrying out armed guarding and escort missions, the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of the Use of Firearms by Full-time Guard and Escort Personnel shall apply.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　保安员不得有下列行为：  
（一）限制他人人身自由、搜查他人身体或者侮辱、殴打他人；  
（二）扣押、没收他人证件、财物；  
（三）阻碍依法执行公务；  
（四）参与追索债务、采用暴力或者以暴力相威胁的手段处置纠纷；  
（五）删改或者扩散保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录；  
（六）侵犯个人隐私或者泄露在保安服务中获知的国家秘密、商业秘密以及客户单位明确要求保密的信息；  
（七）违反法律、行政法规的其他行为。

**Article 30** A security guard shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) restricting the personal freedom of others, searching the body of others, or insulting, beating or striking others;  
(2) seizing or confiscating the certificates or property of others;  
(3) obstructing the execution of official duties according to law;  
(4) participating in debt collection, or using violence or threats of violence to settle disputes;  
(5) deleting, altering or disseminating monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services;  
(6) infringing upon personal privacy or disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or information explicitly required to be kept confidential by a client entity that is obtained during security services; or  
(7) other acts in violation of laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　保安员有权拒绝执行保安从业单位或者客户单位的违法指令。保安从业单位不得因保安员不执行违法指令而解除与保安员的劳动合同，降低其劳动报酬和其他待遇，或者停缴、少缴依法应当为其缴纳的社会保险费。

**Article 31** A security guard shall have the right to refuse to execute any illegal instruction from a security service entity or a client entity. A security service entity shall not, on the ground that a security guard refuses to execute an illegal instruction, terminate the labor contract with the security guard, reduce his or her labor remuneration and other benefits, or stop or reduce the social insurance premiums that it shall pay for the security guard according to law.

### 第六章　保安培训单位

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　保安培训单位应当具备下列条件：  
（一）是依法设立的具有法人资格的学校、职业培训机构；  
（二）有保安培训所需的专兼职师资力量；  
（三）有保安培训所需的场所、设施等教学条件。

**Article 32** A security training institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being a school or vocational training institution with the status of a legal person established according to law;  
(2) having full-time and part-time teaching staff necessary for security training; and  
(3) having venues, facilities and other teaching conditions necessary for security training.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事保安培训的单位，应当自开展保安培训之日起30日内向所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关备案，提交能够证明其符合[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定条件的材料。  
保安培训单位出资人、法定代表人（主要负责人）、住所、名称发生变化的，应当到原备案公安机关办理变更。  
保安培训单位终止培训的，应当自终止培训之日起30日内到原备案公安机关撤销备案。

**Article 33** An entity engaged in security training shall, within 30 days from the date of commencing security training, file a record with the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts at the place where it is located, and submit the materials capable of proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 32 of these Regulations.  
If the contributor, legal representative (principal responsible person), domicile, or name of a security training institution changes, it shall go through the modification procedures with the public security organ where it originally filed the record.  
If a security training institution terminates its training, it shall, within 30 days from the date of termination, withdraw its record with the public security organ where it originally filed the record.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事武装守护押运服务的保安员的枪支使用培训，应当由人民警察院校、人民警察培训机构负责。承担培训工作的人民警察院校、人民警察培训机构应当向所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 34** The training on the use of firearms for security guards engaged in armed guarding and escort services shall be conducted by people's police academies or people's police training institutions. The people's police academies or people's police training institutions undertaking such training shall file a record with the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the place where they are located.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　保安培训单位应当按照保安员培训教学大纲制订教学计划，对接受培训的人员进行法律、保安专业知识和技能培训以及职业道德教育。  
保安员培训教学大纲由国务院公安部门审定。

**Article 35** A security training institution shall formulate a teaching plan in accordance with the security guard training syllabus, and provide the persons receiving training with training in law, professional knowledge and skills of security, and education in professional ethics.  
The security guard training syllabus shall be examined and approved by the public security department under the State Council.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公安机关应当指导保安从业单位建立健全保安服务管理制度、岗位责任制度、保安员管理制度和紧急情况应急预案，督促保安从业单位落实相关管理制度。  
保安从业单位、保安培训单位和保安员应当接受公安机关的监督检查。

**Article 36** The public security organs shall guide security service entities in establishing and improving the security service management system, the post responsibility system, the security guard management system, and the emergency response plan, and urge security service entities to implement the relevant management systems.  
Security service entities, security training institutions, and security guards shall subject themselves to the supervision and inspection of the public security organs.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　公安机关建立保安服务监督管理信息系统，记录保安从业单位、保安培训单位和保安员的相关信息。  
公安机关应当对提取、留存的保安员指纹等人体生物信息予以保密。

**Article 37** The public security organs shall establish a security service supervision and management information system to record the relevant information on security service entities, security training institutions, and security guards.  
The public security organs shall keep confidential the fingerprints and other human biological information of security guards that are collected and retained.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　公安机关的人民警察对保安从业单位、保安培训单位实施监督检查应当出示证件，对监督检查中发现的问题，应当督促其整改。监督检查的情况和处理结果应当如实记录，并由公安机关的监督检查人员和保安从业单位、保安培训单位的有关负责人签字。

**Article 38** When carrying out supervision and inspection of security service entities or security training institutions, the people's police of the public security organs shall present their credentials, and shall urge the entities to rectify any problems discovered during the supervision and inspection. The information on the supervision and inspection and the handling results shall be truthfully recorded, and shall be signed by the supervision and inspection personnel of the public security organ and the relevant responsible persons of the security service entity or security training institution.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府公安机关应当公布投诉方式，受理社会公众对保安从业单位、保安培训单位和保安员的投诉。接到投诉的公安机关应当及时调查处理，并反馈查处结果。

**Article 39** The public security organs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall publish the methods for filing complaints, and accept complaints from the public against security service entities, security training institutions, and security guards. The public security organ that receives a complaint shall promptly investigate and handle it, and provide feedback on the investigation and handling results.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家机关及其工作人员不得设立保安服务公司，不得参与或者变相参与保安服务公司的经营活动。

**Article 40** State organs and their staff members shall not establish security service companies, and shall not participate or participate in a disguised form in the business operations of security service companies.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何组织或者个人未经许可，擅自从事保安服务的，依法给予治安管理处罚，并没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** If any organization or individual engages in security services without permission, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　保安从业单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正，给予警告；情节严重的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）保安服务公司法定代表人变更未经公安机关审核的；  
（二）未按照本条例的规定进行备案或者撤销备案的；  
（三）自行招用保安员的单位在本单位以外或者物业管理区域以外开展保安服务的；  
（四）招用不符合本条例规定条件的人员担任保安员的；  
（五）保安服务公司未对客户单位要求提供的保安服务的合法性进行核查的，或者未将违法的保安服务要求向公安机关报告的；  
（六）保安服务公司未按照本条例的规定签订、留存保安服务合同的；  
（七）未按照本条例的规定留存保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录的。  
客户单位未按照本条例的规定留存保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 42** If a security service entity falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) the change of the legal representative of a security service company has not been examined by the public security organ;  
(2) failing to file a record or withdraw a record in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) an entity that employs security guards on its own provides security services outside its own premises or outside the property management area;  
(4) employing a person who does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations to serve as a security guard;  
(5) a security service company fails to verify the lawfulness of the security services requested by the client entity, or fails to report an unlawful request for security services to the public security organ;  
(6) a security service company fails to conclude or retain a security service contract in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; or  
(7) failing to retain the monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
If a client entity fails to retain the monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　保安从业单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；违反治安管理的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的刑事责任：  
（一）泄露在保安服务中获知的国家秘密、商业秘密以及客户单位明确要求保密的信息的；  
（二）使用监控设备侵犯他人合法权益或者个人隐私的；  
（三）删改或者扩散保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录的；  
（四）指使、纵容保安员阻碍依法执行公务、参与追索债务、采用暴力或者以暴力相威胁的手段处置纠纷的；  
（五）对保安员疏于管理、教育和培训，发生保安员违法犯罪案件，造成严重后果的。  
客户单位删改或者扩散保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 43** If a security service entity falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and be fined not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if it violates public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated against the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons according to law:  
(1) disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or information explicitly required to be kept confidential by a client entity that is obtained during security services;  
(2) using monitoring equipment to infringe upon the lawful rights and interests or personal privacy of others;  
(3) deleting, altering or disseminating monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services;  
(4) instructing or conniving at security guards in obstructing the execution of official duties according to law, participating in debt collection, or using violence or threats of violence to settle disputes; or  
(5) failing to properly manage, educate and train security guards, resulting in illegal or criminal cases committed by security guards and causing serious consequences.  
If a client entity deletes, alters or disseminates monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services, it shall be punished in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　保安从业单位因保安员不执行违法指令而解除与保安员的劳动合同，降低其劳动报酬和其他待遇，或者停缴、少缴依法应当为其缴纳的社会保险费的，对保安从业单位的处罚和对保安员的赔偿依照有关劳动合同和社会保险的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 44** If a security service entity terminates the labor contract with a security guard, reduces his or her labor remuneration and other benefits, or stops or reduces the social insurance premiums that it shall pay for the security guard according to law on the ground that the security guard refuses to execute an illegal instruction, the punishment of the security service entity and the compensation to the security guard shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations on labor contracts and social insurance.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　保安员有下列行为之一的，由公安机关予以训诫；情节严重的，吊销其保安员证；违反治安管理的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）限制他人人身自由、搜查他人身体或者侮辱、殴打他人的；  
（二）扣押、没收他人证件、财物的；  
（三）阻碍依法执行公务的；  
（四）参与追索债务、采用暴力或者以暴力相威胁的手段处置纠纷的；  
（五）删改或者扩散保安服务中形成的监控影像资料、报警记录的；  
（六）侵犯个人隐私或者泄露在保安服务中获知的国家秘密、商业秘密以及客户单位明确要求保密的信息的；  
（七）有违反法律、行政法规的其他行为的。  
从事武装守护押运的保安员违反规定使用枪支的，依照《专职守护押运人员枪支使用管理条例》的规定处罚。

**Article 45** If a security guard commits any of the following acts, the public security organ shall give him or her a reprimand; if the circumstances are serious, his or her security guard certificate shall be revoked; if public security administration is violated, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) restricting the personal freedom of others, searching the body of others, or insulting, beating or striking others;  
(2) seizing or confiscating the certificates or property of others;  
(3) obstructing the execution of official duties according to law;  
(4) participating in debt collection, or using violence or threats of violence to settle disputes;  
(5) deleting, altering or disseminating monitoring image data or alarm records generated in security services;  
(6) infringing upon personal privacy or disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or information explicitly required to be kept confidential by a client entity that is obtained during security services; or  
(7) committing other acts in violation of laws or administrative regulations.  
If a security guard engaged in armed guarding and escort services uses a firearm in violation of the provisions, he or she shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of the Use of Firearms by Full-time Guard and Escort Personnel.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　保安员在保安服务中造成他人人身伤亡、财产损失的，由保安从业单位赔付；保安员有故意或者重大过失的，保安从业单位可以依法向保安员追偿。

**Article 46** If a security guard causes personal injury or death or property damage to another person in the course of providing a security service, the security service entity shall pay compensation; if the security guard has acted intentionally or with gross negligence, the security service entity may claim recourse from the security guard according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　从事保安培训的单位有下列情形之一的，责令限期改正，给予警告；情节严重的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照本条例的规定进行备案或者办理变更的；  
（二）不符合本条例规定条件的；  
（三）隐瞒有关情况、提供虚假材料或者拒绝提供反映其活动情况的真实材料的；  
（四）未按照本条例规定开展保安培训的。  
以保安培训为名进行诈骗活动的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** If an entity engaged in security training falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period and be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) failing to file a record or go through modification procedures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations;  
(3) concealing relevant information, providing false materials, or refusing to provide truthful materials reflecting its activities; or  
(4) failing to conduct security training in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
If a person engages in fraudulent activities under the pretext of security training, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家机关及其工作人员设立保安服务公司，参与或者变相参与保安服务公司经营活动的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 48** If a state organ or any of its staff members establishes a security service company, or participates or participates in a disguised form in the business operations of a security service company, a sanction shall be imposed upon the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　公安机关的人民警察在保安服务活动监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** If a people's policeman of a public security organ abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the supervision and administration of security service activities, a sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　保安服务许可证、保安员证的式样由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 50** The formats of the security service permit and the security guard certificate shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例施行前已经设立的保安服务公司，应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内重新申请保安服务许可证。本条例施行前自行招用保安员的单位，应当自本条例施行之日起3个月内向公安机关备案。  
本条例施行前已经从事保安服务的保安员，自本条例施行之日起1年内由保安员所在单位组织培训，经设区的市级人民政府公安机关考试、审查合格并留存指纹等人体生物信息的，发给保安员证。

**Article 51** Security service companies that have been established before the entry into force of these Regulations shall, within six months from the date of entry into force of these Regulations, re-apply for a security service permit. Entities that have employed security guards on their own before the entry into force of these Regulations shall, within three months from the date of entry into force of these Regulations, file a record with the public security organ.  
Security guards who have been engaged in security services before the entry into force of these Regulations shall, within one year from the date of entry into force of these Regulations, be organized by their employer units to receive training, and if they pass the examination and review by the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts and have their fingerprints and other human biological information collected and retained, a security guard certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2010.
