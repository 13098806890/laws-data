# 公共文化体育设施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2003-06-26  
**生效日期**：2003-08-01

> (2003年6月18日国务院第12次常务会议通过　2003年6月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第382号公布　自2003年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了促进公共文化体育设施的建设，加强对公共文化体育设施的管理和保护，充分发挥公共文化体育设施的功能，繁荣文化体育事业，满足人民群众开展文化体育活动的基本需求，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称公共文化体育设施，是指由各级人民政府举办或者社会力量举办的，向公众开放用于开展文化体育活动的公益性的图书馆、博物馆、纪念馆、美术馆、文化馆(站)、体育场(馆)、青少年宫、工人文化宫等的建筑物、场地和设备。 本条例所称公共文化体育设施管理单位，是指负责公共文化体育设施的维护，为公众开展文化体育活动提供服务的社会公共文化体育机构。 第三条　公共文化体育设施管理单位必须坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向，充分利用公共文化体育设施，传播有益于提高民族素质、有益于经济发展和社会进步的科学技术和文化知识，开展文明、健康的文化体育活动。 任何单位和个人不得利用公共文化体育设施从事危害公共利益的活动。 第四条　国家有计划地建设公共文化体育设施。对少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和农村地区的公共文化体育设施的建设予以扶持。 第五条　各级人民政府举办的公共文化体育设施的建设、维修、管理资金，应当列入本级人民政府基本建设投资计划和财政预算。 第六条　国家鼓励企业、事业单位、社会团体和个人等社会力量举办公共文化体育设施。 国家鼓励通过自愿捐赠等方式建立公共文化体育设施社会基金，并鼓励依法向人民政府、社会公益性机构或者公共文化体育设施管理单位捐赠财产。捐赠人可以按照税法的有关规定享受优惠。 国家鼓励机关、学校等单位内部的文化体育设施向公众开放。 第七条　国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据国务院规定的职责负责全国的公共文化体育设施的监督管理。 县级以上地方人民政府文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据本级人民政府规定的职责，负责本行政区域内的公共文化体育设施的监督管理。 第八条　对在公共文化体育设施的建设、管理和保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，由县级以上地方人民政府或者有关部门给予奖励。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进公共文化体育设施的建设，加强对公共文化体育设施的管理和保护，充分发挥公共文化体育设施的功能，繁荣文化体育事业，满足人民群众开展文化体育活动的基本需求，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of promoting the construction of public cultural and sports facilities, strengthening the management and protection of public cultural and sports facilities, giving full play to the functions of public cultural and sports facilities, prospering the cultural and sports undertakings, and meeting the basic needs of the people for carrying out cultural and sports activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称公共文化体育设施，是指由各级人民政府举办或者社会力量举办的，向公众开放用于开展文化体育活动的公益性的图书馆、博物馆、纪念馆、美术馆、文化馆(站)、体育场(馆)、青少年宫、工人文化宫等的建筑物、场地和设备。  
本条例所称公共文化体育设施管理单位，是指负责公共文化体育设施的维护，为公众开展文化体育活动提供服务的社会公共文化体育机构。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "public cultural and sports facilities" means buildings, premises and equipment such as libraries, museums, memorials, art galleries, cultural centers (stations), stadiums (gyms), youth palaces, workers' cultural palaces, etc., which are established by people's governments at all levels or by social forces and are open to the public for carrying out cultural and sports activities.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "management unit of public cultural and sports facilities" means a social public cultural and sports institution that is responsible for the maintenance of public cultural and sports facilities and provides services to the public for carrying out cultural and sports activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公共文化体育设施管理单位必须坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向，充分利用公共文化体育设施，传播有益于提高民族素质、有益于经济发展和社会进步的科学技术和文化知识，开展文明、健康的文化体育活动。  
任何单位和个人不得利用公共文化体育设施从事危害公共利益的活动。

**Article 3** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities must adhere to the orientation of serving the people and serving socialism, make full use of public cultural and sports facilities, disseminate science, technology and cultural knowledge that are conducive to improving the quality of the nation and to economic development and social progress, and carry out civilized and healthy cultural and sports activities.  
No unit or individual may use public cultural and sports facilities to engage in activities that harm the public interest.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家有计划地建设公共文化体育设施。对少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和农村地区的公共文化体育设施的建设予以扶持。

**Article 4** The State shall systematically construct public cultural and sports facilities. Support shall be given to the construction of public cultural and sports facilities in minority nationality areas, remote and poverty-stricken areas, and rural areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府举办的公共文化体育设施的建设、维修、管理资金，应当列入本级人民政府基本建设投资计划和财政预算。

**Article 5** Funds for the construction, maintenance and management of public cultural and sports facilities established by people's governments at all levels shall be included in the capital construction investment plan and the fiscal budget of the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励企业、事业单位、社会团体和个人等社会力量举办公共文化体育设施。  
国家鼓励通过自愿捐赠等方式建立公共文化体育设施社会基金，并鼓励依法向人民政府、社会公益性机构或者公共文化体育设施管理单位捐赠财产。捐赠人可以按照税法的有关规定享受优惠。  
国家鼓励机关、学校等单位内部的文化体育设施向公众开放。

**Article 6** The State encourages enterprises, institutions, social organizations and individuals and other social forces to establish public cultural and sports facilities.  
The State encourages the establishment of social funds for public cultural and sports facilities through voluntary donations, and encourages donations of property to the people's government, social public welfare institutions or management units of public cultural and sports facilities according to law. Donors may enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the tax law.  
The State encourages the opening of cultural and sports facilities within state organs, schools and other units to the public.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据国务院规定的职责负责全国的公共文化体育设施的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据本级人民政府规定的职责，负责本行政区域内的公共文化体育设施的监督管理。

**Article 7** The administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of public cultural and sports facilities nationwide.  
The administrative departments of culture and the administrative departments of sports under the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed by the people's governments at the corresponding level, be responsible for the supervision and administration of public cultural and sports facilities within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在公共文化体育设施的建设、管理和保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，由县级以上地方人民政府或者有关部门给予奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the construction, management and protection of public cultural and sports facilities shall be given awards by the local people's governments at or above the county level or the relevant departments.

### 第二章　规划和建设

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院发展和改革行政主管部门应当会同国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门，将全国公共文化体育设施的建设纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将本行政区域内的公共文化体育设施的建设纳入当地国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 9** The administrative department of development and reform under the State Council shall, together with the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the State Council, incorporate the construction of the national public cultural and sports facilities into the national economic and social development plan.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of public cultural and sports facilities within their respective administrative regions into the local national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公共文化体育设施的数量、种类、规模以及布局，应当根据国民经济和社会发展水平、人口结构、环境条件以及文化体育事业发展的需要，统筹兼顾，优化配置，并符合国家关于城乡公共文化体育设施用地定额指标的规定。  
公共文化体育设施用地定额指标，由国务院土地行政主管部门、建设行政主管部门分别会同国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门制定。

**Article 10** The quantity, type, scale and layout of public cultural and sports facilities shall be determined based on the level of national economic and social development, population structure, environmental conditions and the needs of the development of cultural and sports undertakings, with overall consideration and optimized allocation, and shall comply with the state's provisions on the quota indicators of land for urban and rural public cultural and sports facilities.  
The quota indicators of land for public cultural and sports facilities shall be formulated by the administrative department of land and the administrative department of construction under the State Council, respectively, together with the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公共文化体育设施的建设选址，应当符合人口集中、交通便利的原则。

**Article 11** The location for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities shall conform to the principles of concentrated population and convenient transportation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公共文化体育设施的设计，应当符合实用、安全、科学、美观等要求，并采取无障碍措施，方便残疾人使用。具体设计规范由国务院建设行政主管部门会同国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门制定。

**Article 12** The design of public cultural and sports facilities shall meet the requirements of practicality, safety, science, aesthetics, etc., and barrier-free measures shall be taken to facilitate the use by disabled persons. The specific design specifications shall be formulated by the administrative department of construction under the State Council together with the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设公共文化体育设施使用国有土地的，经依法批准可以以划拨方式取得。

**Article 13** Where State-owned land is used for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities, it may be obtained by means of allocation upon approval according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公共文化体育设施的建设预留地，由县级以上地方人民政府土地行政主管部门、城乡规划行政主管部门按照国家有关用地定额指标，纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，并依照法定程序审批。任何单位或者个人不得侵占公共文化体育设施建设预留地或者改变其用途。  
因特殊情况需要调整公共文化体育设施建设预留地的，应当依法调整城乡规划，并依照前款规定重新确定建设预留地。重新确定的公共文化体育设施建设预留地不得少于原有面积。

**Article 14** Reserved land for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities shall be included in the overall land use plan and urban and rural planning by the administrative department of land and the administrative department of urban and rural planning under the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the relevant land quota indicators of the State, and shall be examined and approved in accordance with legal procedures. No unit or individual may encroach upon the reserved land for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities or change its use.  
Where it is necessary to adjust the reserved land for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities due to special circumstances, the urban and rural planning shall be adjusted according to law, and the reserved land shall be re-determined in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph. The re-determined reserved land for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities shall not be less than the original area.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　新建、改建、扩建居民住宅区，应当按照国家有关规定规划和建设相应的文化体育设施。  
居民住宅区配套建设的文化体育设施，应当与居民住宅区的主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。任何单位或者个人不得擅自改变文化体育设施的建设项目和功能，不得缩小其建设规模和降低其用地指标。

**Article 15** When new residential areas are built, or existing ones are rebuilt or expanded, corresponding cultural and sports facilities shall be planned and constructed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The cultural and sports facilities constructed as supporting facilities for a residential area shall be designed, constructed and put into use simultaneously with the main project of the residential area. No unit or individual may change the construction items and functions of the cultural and sports facilities without authorization, nor may they reduce their construction scale or lower their land use indicators.

### 第三章　使用和服务

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公共文化体育设施管理单位应当完善服务条件，建立、健全服务规范，开展与公共文化体育设施功能、特点相适应的服务，保障公共文化体育设施用于开展文明、健康的文化体育活动。

**Article 16** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall improve service conditions, establish and improve service norms, carry out services commensurate with the functions and characteristics of public cultural and sports facilities, and ensure that public cultural and sports facilities are used for carrying out civilized and healthy cultural and sports activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公共文化体育设施应当根据其功能、特点向公众开放，开放时间应当与当地公众的工作时间、学习时间适当错开。  
公共文化体育设施的开放时间，不得少于省、自治区、直辖市规定的最低时限。国家法定节假日和学校寒暑假期间，应当适当延长开放时间。  
学校寒暑假期间，公共文化体育设施管理单位应当增设适合学生特点的文化体育活动。

**Article 17** Public cultural and sports facilities shall be open to the public in light of their functions and characteristics, and the opening hours shall be appropriately staggered from the working hours and study hours of the local public.  
The opening hours of public cultural and sports facilities shall not be less than the minimum time limit prescribed by the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. During the statutory holidays and school winter and summer vacations, the opening hours shall be appropriately extended.  
During the school winter and summer vacations, the management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall add additional cultural and sports activities suitable for the characteristics of students.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公共文化体育设施管理单位应当向公众公示其服务内容和开放时间。公共文化体育设施因维修等原因需要暂时停止开放的，应当提前7日向公众公示。

**Article 18** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall make public its service contents and opening hours to the public. Where a public cultural or sports facility needs to be temporarily closed due to repair or other reasons, it shall make public the closure to the public 7 days in advance.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公共文化体育设施管理单位应当在醒目位置标明设施的使用方法和注意事项。

**Article 19** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall indicate the usage methods and precautions of the facilities in a conspicuous position.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公共文化体育设施管理单位提供服务可以适当收取费用，收费项目和标准应当经县级以上人民政府有关部门批准。

**Article 20** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities may charge appropriate fees for providing services, and the items and standards of fees shall be subject to the approval of the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　需要收取费用的公共文化体育设施管理单位，应当根据设施的功能、特点对学生、老年人、残疾人等免费或者优惠开放，具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 21** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities that needs to charge fees shall, in light of the functions and characteristics of the facilities, provide free or preferential access to students, the elderly, disabled persons, etc. The specific measures shall be formulated by the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公共文化设施管理单位可以将设施出租用于举办文物展览、美术展览、艺术培训等文化活动。  
公共体育设施管理单位不得将设施的主体部分用于非体育活动。但是，因举办公益性活动或者大型文化活动等特殊情况临时出租的除外。临时出租时间一般不得超过10日；租用期满，租用者应当恢复原状，不得影响该设施的功能、用途。

**Article 22** A management unit of public cultural facilities may lease its facilities for cultural activities such as holding cultural relic exhibitions, art exhibitions, art training, etc.  
A management unit of public sports facilities may not use the main part of its facilities for non-sports activities, except for temporary leasing due to special circumstances such as holding public welfare activities or large cultural activities. The temporary leasing period generally shall not exceed 10 days; upon the expiration of the lease term, the lessee shall restore the premises to their original condition and shall not affect the function and purpose of the facilities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公众在使用公共文化体育设施时，应当遵守公共秩序，爱护公共文化体育设施。任何单位或者个人不得损坏公共文化体育设施。

**Article 23** The public, when using public cultural and sports facilities, shall observe public order and take good care of public cultural and sports facilities. No unit or individual may damage public cultural and sports facilities.

### 第四章　管理和保护

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公共文化体育设施管理单位应当将公共文化体育设施的名称、地址、服务项目等内容报所在地县级人民政府文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门备案。  
县级人民政府文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门应当向公众公布公共文化体育设施名录。

**Article 24** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall report the name, address, service items and other contents of the public cultural and sports facilities to the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the local people's government at the county level for recordation.  
The administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the people's government at the county level shall make public the list of public cultural and sports facilities to the public.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公共文化体育设施管理单位应当建立、健全安全管理制度，依法配备安全保护设施、人员，保证公共文化体育设施的完好，确保公众安全。  
公共体育设施内设置的专业性强、技术要求高的体育项目，应当符合国家规定的安全服务技术要求。

**Article 25** A management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall establish and improve the safety management system, equip safety protection facilities and personnel according to law, ensure the integrity of public cultural and sports facilities, and ensure public safety.  
Sports events with strong professionalism and high technical requirements set up in public sports facilities shall meet the safety service technical requirements prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　公共文化体育设施管理单位的各项收入，应当用于公共文化体育设施的维护、管理和事业发展，不得挪作他用。  
文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门、财政部门和其他有关部门，应当依法加强对公共文化体育设施管理单位收支的监督管理。

**Article 26** All income of a management unit of public cultural and sports facilities shall be used for the maintenance, management and development of public cultural and sports facilities, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.  
The administrative departments of culture, the administrative departments of sports, the financial departments and other relevant departments shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and administration of the income and expenditure of the management units of public cultural and sports facilities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　因城乡建设确需拆除公共文化体育设施或者改变其功能、用途的，有关地方人民政府在作出决定前，应当组织专家论证，并征得上一级人民政府文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门同意，报上一级人民政府批准。  
涉及大型公共文化体育设施的，上一级人民政府在批准前，应当举行听证会，听取公众意见。  
经批准拆除公共文化体育设施或者改变其功能、用途的，应当依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定择地重建。重新建设的公共文化体育设施，应当符合规划要求，一般不得小于原有规模。迁建工作应当坚持先建设后拆除或者建设拆除同时进行的原则。迁建所需费用由造成迁建的单位承担。

**Article 27** Where it is truly necessary to demolish a public cultural or sports facility or change its function or purpose due to urban and rural construction, the relevant local people's government shall, before making a decision, organize expert demonstration, obtain the consent of the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports at the next higher level, and report to the people's government at the next higher level for approval.  
Where a large public cultural or sports facility is involved, the people's government at the next higher level shall, before approval, hold a hearing to solicit public opinions.  
Where the demolition of a public cultural or sports facility or the change of its function or purpose is approved, it shall be rebuilt at a selected site in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State. The re-constructed public cultural and sports facilities shall meet the planning requirements and generally shall not be smaller than the original scale. The relocation work shall adhere to the principle of constructing after demolition or carrying out construction and demolition simultaneously. The expenses required for relocation shall be borne by the unit causing the relocation.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　文化、体育、城乡规划、建设、土地等有关行政主管部门及其工作人员，不依法履行职责或者发现违法行为不予依法查处的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where the relevant administrative departments of culture, sports, urban and rural planning, construction, land, etc., and their staff members fail to perform their duties according to law or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts found according to law, the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　侵占公共文化体育设施建设预留地或者改变其用途的，由土地行政主管部门、城乡规划行政主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，由作出决定的机关依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 29** Where a reserved land for the construction of public cultural and sports facilities is encroached upon or its use is changed, the administrative department of land and the administrative department of urban and rural planning shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order correction within a time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, the organ making the decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公共文化体育设施管理单位有下列行为之一的，由文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正；造成严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分：  
(一)未按照规定的最低时限对公众开放的；  
(二)未公示其服务项目、开放时间等事项的；  
(三)未在醒目位置标明设施的使用方法或者注意事项的；  
(四)未建立、健全公共文化体育设施的安全管理制度的；  
(五)未将公共文化体育设施的名称、地址、服务项目等内容报文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门备案的。

**Article 30** Where a management unit of public cultural and sports facilities commits any of the following acts, the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order correction within a time limit; if serious consequences are caused, the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to open to the public in accordance with the minimum time limit prescribed;  
(2) Failing to make public its service items, opening hours and other matters;  
(3) Failing to indicate the usage methods or precautions of the facilities in a conspicuous position;  
(4) Failing to establish and improve the safety management system of public cultural and sports facilities;  
(5) Failing to report the name, address, service items and other contents of the public cultural and sports facilities to the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports for recordation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公共文化体育设施管理单位，有下列行为之一的，由文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正，没收违法所得，违法所得5000元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得5000元以下的，可以处1万元以下的罚款；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分：  
(一)开展与公共文化体育设施功能、用途不相适应的服务活动的；  
(二)违反本条例规定出租公共文化体育设施的。

**Article 31** Where a management unit of public cultural and sports facilities commits any of the following acts, the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order correction within a time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and if the illegal gains are more than 5,000 yuan, concurrently impose a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan may be imposed; the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) Carrying out service activities that are not commensurate with the functions and purposes of the public cultural and sports facilities;  
(2) Leasing public cultural and sports facilities in violation of the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公共文化体育设施管理单位及其工作人员违反本条例规定，挪用公共文化体育设施管理单位的各项收入或者有条件维护而不履行维护义务的，由文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门依据各自职责责令限期改正；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where a management unit of public cultural and sports facilities or its staff members, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, misappropriate the various incomes of the management unit of public cultural and sports facilities or fail to perform the obligation of maintenance when conditions permit, the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order correction within a time limit; the persons in charge with responsibility and other directly responsible personnel shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家机关、学校等单位内部的文化体育设施向公众开放的，由国务院文化行政主管部门、体育行政主管部门会同有关部门依据本条例的原则另行制定管理办法。

**Article 33** Where cultural and sports facilities within state organs, schools and other units are open to the public, the administrative department of culture and the administrative department of sports under the State Council shall, together with other relevant departments, formulate separate administrative measures in accordance with the principles of these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自2003年8月1日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2003.
