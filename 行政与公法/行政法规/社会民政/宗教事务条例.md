# 宗教事务条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2017-08-26  
**生效日期**：2018-02-01

> (2004年11月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第426号公布　2017年6月14日国务院第176次常务会议修订通过　2017年8月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第686号公布　自2018年2月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障公民宗教信仰自由，维护宗教和睦与社会和谐，规范宗教事务管理，提高宗教工作法治化水平，根据宪法和有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　公民有宗教信仰自由。 任何组织或者个人不得强制公民信仰宗教或者不信仰宗教，不得歧视信仰宗教的公民(以下称信教公民)或者不信仰宗教的公民(以下称不信教公民)。 信教公民和不信教公民、信仰不同宗教的公民应当相互尊重、和睦相处。 第三条　宗教事务管理坚持保护合法、制止非法、遏制极端、抵御渗透、打击犯罪的原则。 第四条　国家依法保护正常的宗教活动，积极引导宗教与社会主义社会相适应，维护宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民的合法权益。 宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民应当遵守宪法、法律、法规和规章，践行社会主义核心价值观，维护国家统一、民族团结、宗教和睦与社会稳定。 任何组织或者个人不得利用宗教进行危害国家安全、破坏社会秩序、损害公民身体健康、妨碍国家教育制度，以及其他损害国家利益、社会公共利益和公民合法权益等违法活动。 任何组织或者个人不得在不同宗教之间、同一宗教内部以及信教公民与不信教公民之间制造矛盾与冲突，不得宣扬、支持、资助宗教极端主义，不得利用宗教破坏民族团结、分裂国家和进行恐怖活动。 第五条　各宗教坚持独立自主自办的原则，宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和宗教事务不受外国势力的支配。 宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所、宗教教职人员在相互尊重、平等、友好的基础上开展对外交往；其他组织或者个人在对外经济、文化等合作、交流活动中不得接受附加的宗教条件。 第六条　各级人民政府应当加强宗教工作，建立健全宗教工作机制，保障工作力量和必要的工作条件。 县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门依法对涉及国家利益和社会公共利益的宗教事务进行行政管理，县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内依法负责有关的行政管理工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公民宗教信仰自由，维护宗教和睦与社会和谐，规范宗教事务管理，提高宗教工作法治化水平，根据宪法和有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution and relevant laws to guarantee citizens' freedom of religious belief, maintain religious harmony and social harmony, regulate the administration of religious affairs, and improve the rule of law in religious work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公民有宗教信仰自由。  
任何组织或者个人不得强制公民信仰宗教或者不信仰宗教，不得歧视信仰宗教的公民(以下称信教公民)或者不信仰宗教的公民(以下称不信教公民)。  
信教公民和不信教公民、信仰不同宗教的公民应当相互尊重、和睦相处。

**Article 2** Citizens shall have freedom of religious belief.  
No organization or individual may compel citizens to believe in or not believe in a religion, nor discriminate against citizens who believe in a religion (hereinafter referred to as religious citizens) or citizens who do not believe in a religion (hereinafter referred to as non-religious citizens).  
Religious citizens and non-religious citizens, and citizens who believe in different religions shall respect each other and live in harmony.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　宗教事务管理坚持保护合法、制止非法、遏制极端、抵御渗透、打击犯罪的原则。

**Article 3** The administration of religious affairs shall adhere to the principles of protecting lawful activities, stopping illegal activities, curbing extremism, resisting infiltration, and combating crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家依法保护正常的宗教活动，积极引导宗教与社会主义社会相适应，维护宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民的合法权益。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民应当遵守宪法、法律、法规和规章，践行社会主义核心价值观，维护国家统一、民族团结、宗教和睦与社会稳定。  
任何组织或者个人不得利用宗教进行危害国家安全、破坏社会秩序、损害公民身体健康、妨碍国家教育制度，以及其他损害国家利益、社会公共利益和公民合法权益等违法活动。  
任何组织或者个人不得在不同宗教之间、同一宗教内部以及信教公民与不信教公民之间制造矛盾与冲突，不得宣扬、支持、资助宗教极端主义，不得利用宗教破坏民族团结、分裂国家和进行恐怖活动。

**Article 4** The State shall protect normal religious activities according to law, actively guide religions to adapt to the socialist society, and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of religious groups, religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious citizens.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious citizens shall abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations and rules, practice core socialist values, and safeguard national unity, ethnic unity, religious harmony, and social stability.  
No organization or individual may use religion to engage in illegal activities that endanger national security, disrupt social order, harm the physical health of citizens, impede the national education system, or otherwise damage the interests of the State, the public interest, or the legitimate rights and interests of citizens.  
No organization or individual may create conflicts and disputes among different religions, within the same religion, or between religious citizens and non-religious citizens, nor advocate, support, or finance religious extremism, nor use religion to undermine ethnic unity, split the country, or engage in terrorist activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各宗教坚持独立自主自办的原则，宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和宗教事务不受外国势力的支配。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所、宗教教职人员在相互尊重、平等、友好的基础上开展对外交往；其他组织或者个人在对外经济、文化等合作、交流活动中不得接受附加的宗教条件。

**Article 5** All religions shall adhere to the principle of independence and self-governance, and religious groups, religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious affairs shall not be dominated by foreign forces.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious clergy shall conduct foreign exchanges on the basis of mutual respect, equality, and friendship; no other organization or individual may accept additional religious conditions in foreign economic, cultural, and other cooperation and exchange activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当加强宗教工作，建立健全宗教工作机制，保障工作力量和必要的工作条件。  
县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门依法对涉及国家利益和社会公共利益的宗教事务进行行政管理，县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内依法负责有关的行政管理工作。  
乡级人民政府应当做好本行政区域的宗教事务管理工作。村民委员会、居民委员会应当依法协助人民政府管理宗教事务。  
各级人民政府应当听取宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民的意见，协调宗教事务管理工作，为宗教团体、宗教院校和宗教活动场所提供公共服务。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen religious work, establish and improve religious work mechanisms, and ensure work forces and necessary working conditions.  
The religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, conduct administrative management of religious affairs involving national interests and public interests; other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for relevant administrative management according to law.  
The people's government at the township level shall properly manage religious affairs within its administrative area. Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist the people's government in managing religious affairs according to law.  
People's governments at all levels shall hear opinions from religious groups, religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious citizens, coordinate the management of religious affairs, and provide public services to religious groups, religious colleges, and religious activity sites.

### 第二章　宗教团体

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　宗教团体的成立、变更和注销，应当依照国家社会团体管理的有关规定办理登记。  
宗教团体章程应当符合国家社会团体管理的有关规定。  
宗教团体按照章程开展活动，受法律保护。

**Article 7** The establishment, modification, and cancellation of a religious group shall be registered in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on the administration of social groups.  
The articles of association of a religious group shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on the administration of social groups.  
A religious group shall carry out activities in accordance with its articles of association, and shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　宗教团体具有下列职能：  
(一)协助人民政府贯彻落实法律、法规、规章和政策，维护信教公民的合法权益；  
(二)指导宗教教务，制定规章制度并督促落实；  
(三)从事宗教文化研究，阐释宗教教义教规，开展宗教思想建设；  
(四)开展宗教教育培训，培养宗教教职人员，认定、管理宗教教职人员；  
(五)法律、法规、规章和宗教团体章程规定的其他职能。

**Article 8** A religious group shall have the following functions:  
(1) assisting the people's government in implementing laws, regulations, rules, and policies, and safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of religious citizens;  
(2) guiding religious affairs, formulating rules and regulations and supervising their implementation;  
(3) engaging in research on religious culture, interpreting religious doctrines and canons, and carrying out the construction of religious ideology;  
(4) conducting religious education and training, cultivating religious clergy, and recognizing and managing religious clergy;  
(5) other functions as provided by laws, regulations, rules, and the articles of association of the religious group.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　全国性宗教团体和省、自治区、直辖市宗教团体可以根据本宗教的需要按照规定选派和接收宗教留学人员，其他任何组织或者个人不得选派和接收宗教留学人员。

**Article 9** National religious groups and religious groups of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the needs of their religions and pursuant to the provisions, select and admit religious students studying abroad; no other organization or individual may select or admit such students.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　宗教院校、宗教活动场所和宗教教职人员应当遵守宗教团体制定的规章制度。

**Article 10** Religious colleges, religious activity sites, and religious clergy shall abide by the rules and regulations formulated by religious groups.

### 第三章　宗教院校

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　宗教院校由全国性宗教团体或者省、自治区、直辖市宗教团体设立。其他任何组织或者个人不得设立宗教院校。

**Article 11** Religious colleges shall be established by national religious groups or religious groups of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government. No other organization or individual may establish a religious college.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设立宗教院校，应当由全国性宗教团体向国务院宗教事务部门提出申请，或者由省、自治区、直辖市宗教团体向拟设立的宗教院校所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内提出意见，报国务院宗教事务部门审批。  
国务院宗教事务部门应当自收到全国性宗教团体的申请或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门报送的材料之日起60日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。

**Article 12** To establish a religious college, a national religious group shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the State Council, or a religious group of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the proposed religious college is to be located. The religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application, provide an opinion and submit it to the religious affairs department of the State Council for approval.  
The religious affairs department of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application from the national religious group or the materials submitted by the religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, make a decision on approval or disapproval.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立宗教院校，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有明确的培养目标、办学章程和课程设置计划；  
(二)有符合培养条件的生源；  
(三)有必要的办学资金和稳定的经费来源；  
(四)有教学任务和办学规模所必需的教学场所、设施设备；  
(五)有专职的院校负责人、合格的专职教师和内部管理组织；  
(六)布局合理。

**Article 13** To establish a religious college, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having clear training objectives, articles of association for running the school, and curriculum plans;  
(2) having student sources that meet the training conditions;  
(3) having necessary funds for running the school and stable sources of funding;  
(4) having teaching venues, facilities, and equipment necessary for the teaching tasks and school scale;  
(5) having full-time college leaders, qualified full-time teachers, and internal management organizations;  
(6) having a reasonable layout.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经批准设立的宗教院校，可以按照有关规定申请法人登记。

**Article 14** A religious college established with approval may apply for legal person registration in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　宗教院校变更校址、校名、隶属关系、培养目标、学制、办学规模等以及合并、分设和终止，应当按照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)规定的程序办理。

**Article 15** The change of the location, name, affiliation relationship, training objectives, length of schooling, and school scale of a religious college, as well as its merger, division, and termination, shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed in Article 12 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　宗教院校实行特定的教师资格认定、职称评审聘任和学生学位授予制度，具体办法由国务院宗教事务部门另行制定。

**Article 16** Religious colleges shall implement specific systems for the qualification recognition of teachers, evaluation and appointment of professional titles, and conferment of academic degrees. The specific measures shall be formulated separately by the religious affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　宗教院校聘用外籍专业人员，应当经国务院宗教事务部门同意后，到所在地外国人工作管理部门办理相关手续。

**Article 17** For a religious college to employ foreign professionals, it shall, after obtaining consent from the religious affairs department of the State Council, go through the relevant formalities with the local foreigner work administration department.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　宗教团体和寺院、宫观、清真寺、教堂(以下称寺观教堂)开展培养宗教教职人员、学习时间在3个月以上的宗教教育培训，应当报设区的市级以上地方人民政府宗教事务部门审批。

**Article 18** Religious groups and monasteries, abbeys, mosques, and churches (hereinafter referred to as temples and churches) that conduct religious education and training for cultivating religious clergy with a study duration of more than three months shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the local people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts for approval.

### 第四章　宗教活动场所

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　宗教活动场所包括寺观教堂和其他固定宗教活动处所。  
寺观教堂和其他固定宗教活动处所的区分标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门制定，报国务院宗教事务部门备案。

**Article 19** Religious activity sites include temples and churches and other fixed religious activity premises.  
The criteria for distinguishing temples and churches from other fixed religious activity premises shall be formulated by the religious affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and submitted to the religious affairs department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　设立宗教活动场所，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)设立宗旨不违背[本条例第四条](#art-4)、第五条的规定；  
(二)当地信教公民有经常进行集体宗教活动的需要；  
(三)有拟主持宗教活动的宗教教职人员或者符合本宗教规定的其他人员；  
(四)有必要的资金，资金来源渠道合法；  
(五)布局合理，符合城乡规划要求，不妨碍周围单位和居民的正常生产、生活。

**Article 20** To establish a religious activity site, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) the purpose of establishment does not contravene the provisions of Articles 4 and 5 of these Regulations;  
(2) local religious citizens have a need to frequently conduct collective religious activities;  
(3) there are religious clergy who intend to preside over the religious activities or other personnel who meet the provisions of the relevant religion;  
(4) there are necessary funds, and the sources of funds are lawful;  
(5) the layout is reasonable, conforms to the requirements of urban and rural planning, and does not hinder the normal production and life of surrounding units and residents.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　筹备设立宗教活动场所，由宗教团体向拟设立的宗教活动场所所在地的县级人民政府宗教事务部门提出申请。县级人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内提出审核意见，报设区的市级人民政府宗教事务部门。  
设区的市级人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到县级人民政府宗教事务部门报送的材料之日起30日内，对申请设立其他固定宗教活动处所的，作出批准或者不予批准的决定；对申请设立寺观教堂的，提出审核意见，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门审批。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到设区的市级人民政府宗教事务部门报送的材料之日起30日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。  
宗教活动场所的设立申请获批准后，方可办理该宗教活动场所的筹建事项。

**Article 21** For the preparation to establish a site for religious activities, a religious group shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level where the proposed site for religious activities is located. The religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, provide its review opinion and report it to the religious affairs department of the people's government at the city with districts level.  
The religious affairs department of the people's government at the city with districts level shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the materials submitted by the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level, make a decision to approve or disapprove the application for the establishment of other fixed sites for religious activities; for an application for the establishment of a temple or church, it shall provide its review opinion and report it to the religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval.  
The religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the materials submitted by the religious affairs department of the people's government at the city with districts level, make a decision to approve or disapprove the application.  
Only after the application for the establishment of a site for religious activities is approved may the preparatory matters for such site for religious activities be processed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　宗教活动场所经批准筹备并建设完工后，应当向所在地的县级人民政府宗教事务部门申请登记。县级人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内对该宗教活动场所的管理组织、规章制度建设等情况进行审核，对符合条件的予以登记，发给《宗教活动场所登记证》。

**Article 22** After a site for religious activities has been approved for preparation and construction is completed, it shall apply for registration with the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level where it is located. The religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, review the management organization, construction of rules and regulations, and other matters of the site for religious activities, and register those that meet the conditions and issue the "Certificate of Registration for a Site for Religious Activities".

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　宗教活动场所符合法人条件的，经所在地宗教团体同意，并报县级人民政府宗教事务部门审查同意后，可以到民政部门办理法人登记。

**Article 23** If a site for religious activities meets the conditions for a legal person, with the consent of the local religious group and after the examination and consent of the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level, it may go through the legal person registration with the civil affairs department.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　宗教活动场所终止或者变更登记内容的，应当到原登记管理机关办理相应的注销或者变更登记手续。

**Article 24** If a site for religious activities terminates or changes the content of its registration, it shall go through the corresponding cancellation or change registration formalities with the original registration authority.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　宗教活动场所应当成立管理组织，实行民主管理。宗教活动场所管理组织的成员，经民主协商推选，并报该场所的登记管理机关备案。

**Article 25** A site for religious activities shall establish a management organization and implement democratic management. The members of the management organization of a site for religious activities shall be elected through democratic consultation and reported to the registration authority of the site for record.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　宗教活动场所应当加强内部管理，依照有关法律、法规、规章的规定，建立健全人员、财务、资产、会计、治安、消防、文物保护、卫生防疫等管理制度，接受当地人民政府有关部门的指导、监督、检查。

**Article 26** A site for religious activities shall strengthen its internal management, establish and improve management systems for personnel, finance, assets, accounting, public security, fire prevention, cultural relics protection, hygiene and epidemic prevention, etc., in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules, and accept the guidance, supervision, and inspection of the relevant departments of the local people's government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　宗教事务部门应当对宗教活动场所遵守法律、法规、规章情况，建立和执行场所管理制度情况，登记项目变更情况，以及宗教活动和涉外活动情况进行监督检查。宗教活动场所应当接受宗教事务部门的监督检查。

**Article 27** The religious affairs department shall supervise and inspect the compliance of sites for religious activities with laws, regulations, and rules, the establishment and implementation of site management systems, the changes in registration items, and the conduct of religious activities and activities involving foreign affairs. Sites for religious activities shall accept the supervision and inspection of the religious affairs department.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　宗教活动场所内可以经销宗教用品、宗教艺术品和宗教出版物。

**Article 28** Within a site for religious activities, religious articles, religious artworks, and religious publications may be sold.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　宗教活动场所应当防范本场所内发生重大事故或者发生违犯宗教禁忌等伤害信教公民宗教感情、破坏民族团结、影响社会稳定的事件。  
发生前款所列事故或者事件时，宗教活动场所应当立即报告所在地的县级人民政府宗教事务部门。

**Article 29** A site for religious activities shall prevent the occurrence of major accidents or incidents that violate religious taboos, hurt the religious feelings of religious citizens, undermine ethnic unity, or affect social stability within the site.  
When an accident or incident listed in the preceding paragraph occurs, the site for religious activities shall immediately report it to the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level where it is located.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　宗教团体、寺观教堂拟在寺观教堂内修建大型露天宗教造像，应当由省、自治区、直辖市宗教团体向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内提出意见，报国务院宗教事务部门审批。  
国务院宗教事务部门应当自收到修建大型露天宗教造像报告之日起60日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。  
宗教团体、寺观教堂以外的组织以及个人不得修建大型露天宗教造像。  
禁止在寺观教堂外修建大型露天宗教造像。

**Article 30** If a religious group or a temple or church intends to construct a large outdoor religious statue within the temple or church, the religious group of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The religious affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, provide its opinion and report it to the religious affairs department of the State Council for examination and approval.  
The religious affairs department of the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving the report on the construction of a large outdoor religious statue, make a decision to approve or disapprove.  
No organization or individual other than religious groups and temples or churches may construct large outdoor religious statues.  
It is prohibited to construct large outdoor religious statues outside temples or churches.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有关单位和个人在宗教活动场所内设立商业服务网点、举办陈列展览、拍摄电影电视片和开展其他活动，应当事先征得该宗教活动场所同意。

**Article 31** The relevant entities and individuals that establish commercial service outlets, hold exhibitions, shoot films or television programs, or carry out other activities within a site for religious activities shall obtain the consent of the site for religious activities in advance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　地方各级人民政府应当根据实际需要，将宗教活动场所建设纳入土地利用总体规划和城乡规划。  
宗教活动场所、大型露天宗教造像的建设应当符合土地利用总体规划、城乡规划和工程建设、文物保护等有关法律、法规。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at all levels shall, based on actual needs, incorporate the construction of sites for religious activities into the overall land use plan and urban and rural plans.  
The construction of sites for religious activities and large outdoor religious statues shall comply with the overall land use plan, urban and rural plans, and relevant laws and regulations on construction projects and cultural relics protection.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在宗教活动场所内改建或者新建建筑物，应当经所在地县级以上地方人民政府宗教事务部门批准后，依法办理规划、建设等手续。  
宗教活动场所扩建、异地重建的，应当按照[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)规定的程序办理。

**Article 33** The renovation or new construction of a building within a site for religious activities shall, after obtaining approval from the religious affairs department of the local people's government at or above the county level, go through the formalities for planning, construction, etc., in accordance with the law.  
The expansion or relocation of a site for religious activities shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed in Article 21 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　景区内有宗教活动场所的，其所在地的县级以上地方人民政府应当协调、处理宗教活动场所与景区管理组织及园林、林业、文物、旅游等方面的利益关系，维护宗教活动场所、宗教教职人员和信教公民的合法权益，保护正常的宗教活动。  
以宗教活动场所为主要游览内容的景区的规划建设，应当与宗教活动场所的风格、环境相协调。

**Article 34** If a scenic area contains a site for religious activities, the local people's government at or above the county level shall coordinate and handle the interest relationships between the site for religious activities and the scenic area management organization, as well as with aspects such as gardens, forestry, cultural relics, and tourism, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the site for religious activities, religious professionals, and religious citizens, and protect normal religious activities.  
The planning and construction of a scenic area that features a site for religious activities as its main attraction shall be coordinated with the style and environment of the site for religious activities.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　信教公民有进行经常性集体宗教活动需要，尚不具备条件申请设立宗教活动场所的，由信教公民代表向县级人民政府宗教事务部门提出申请，县级人民政府宗教事务部门征求所在地宗教团体和乡级人民政府意见后，可以为其指定临时活动地点。  
在县级人民政府宗教事务部门指导下，所在地乡级人民政府对临时活动地点的活动进行监管。具备设立宗教活动场所条件后，办理宗教活动场所设立审批和登记手续。  
临时活动地点的宗教活动应当符合本条例的相关规定。

**Article 35** If religious citizens have the need to conduct regular collective religious activities but do not yet meet the conditions to apply for the establishment of a site for religious activities, the representatives of the religious citizens shall submit an application to the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level, and the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level may, after soliciting the opinions of the local religious group and the people's government at the township level, designate a temporary activity site for them.  
Under the guidance of the religious affairs department of the people's government at the county level, the local people's government at the township level shall supervise the activities at the temporary activity site. When the conditions for establishing a site for religious activities are met, the procedures for examination and approval of the establishment of a site for religious activities and registration shall be completed.  
Religious activities at a temporary activity site shall comply with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

### 第五章　宗教教职人员

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　宗教教职人员经宗教团体认定，报县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门备案，可以从事宗教教务活动。  
藏传佛教活佛传承继位，在佛教团体的指导下，依照宗教仪轨和历史定制办理，报省级以上人民政府宗教事务部门或者省级以上人民政府批准。天主教的主教由天主教的全国性宗教团体报国务院宗教事务部门备案。  
未取得或者已丧失宗教教职人员资格的，不得以宗教教职人员的身份从事活动。

**Article 36** Religious professionals, after being recognized by a religious group and reported to the religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level for record, may engage in religious clerical activities.  
The transmission and succession of Tibetan Buddhist Living Buddhas shall be handled under the guidance of Buddhist groups, in accordance with religious rituals and historical conventions, and reported to the religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the provincial level or approved by the people's government at or above the provincial level. Catholic bishops shall be reported by the national Catholic religious group to the religious affairs department of the State Council for record.  
No person who has not obtained or has lost the qualification of a religious professional may engage in activities as a religious professional.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　宗教教职人员担任或者离任宗教活动场所主要教职，经本宗教的宗教团体同意后，报县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门备案。

**Article 37** When a religious professional assumes or leaves the main religious post of a site for religious activities, after obtaining the consent of the religious group of the religion, it shall be reported to the religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level for record.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　宗教教职人员主持宗教活动、举行宗教仪式、从事宗教典籍整理、进行宗教文化研究、开展公益慈善等活动，受法律保护。

**Article 38** Activities such as presiding over religious activities, conducting religious ceremonies, engaging in the compilation of religious scriptures, carrying out religious cultural research, and conducting public welfare and charitable activities by religious professionals are protected by law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　宗教教职人员依法参加社会保障并享有相关权利。宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所应当按照规定为宗教教职人员办理社会保险登记。

**Article 39** Religious professionals shall participate in social insurance in accordance with the law and enjoy relevant rights. Religious groups, religious schools, and sites for religious activities shall handle social insurance registration for religious professionals in accordance with the provisions.

### 第六章　宗教活动

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　信教公民的集体宗教活动，一般应当在宗教活动场所内举行，由宗教活动场所、宗教团体或者宗教院校组织，由宗教教职人员或者符合本宗教规定的其他人员主持，按照教义教规进行。

**Article 40** Collective religious activities of religious citizens shall generally be held within a site for religious activities, organized by the site for religious activities, a religious group, or a religious school, presided over by a religious professional or other persons who meet the provisions of the religion, and conducted in accordance with the doctrines and canons of the religion.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　非宗教团体、非宗教院校、非宗教活动场所、非指定的临时活动地点不得组织、举行宗教活动，不得接受宗教性的捐赠。  
非宗教团体、非宗教院校、非宗教活动场所不得开展宗教教育培训，不得组织公民出境参加宗教方面的培训、会议、活动等。

**Article 41** Non-religious groups, non-religious colleges, non-religious sites for religious activities, and non-designated temporary activity sites shall not organize or hold religious activities, nor accept religious donations.  
Non-religious groups, non-religious colleges, and non-religious sites for religious activities shall not conduct religious education or training, nor organize citizens to go abroad to participate in religious training, meetings, activities, etc.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　跨省、自治区、直辖市举行超过宗教活动场所容纳规模的大型宗教活动，或者在宗教活动场所外举行大型宗教活动，应当由主办的宗教团体、寺观教堂在拟举行日的30日前，向大型宗教活动举办地的设区的市级人民政府宗教事务部门提出申请。设区的市级人民政府宗教事务部门应当自受理之日起15日内，在征求本级人民政府公安机关意见后，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。作出批准决定的，由批准机关向省级人民政府宗教事务部门备案。  
大型宗教活动应当按照批准通知书载明的要求依宗教仪轨进行，不得违反[本条例第四条](#art-4)、第五条的有关规定。主办的宗教团体、寺观教堂应当采取有效措施防止意外事故的发生，保证大型宗教活动安全、有序进行。大型宗教活动举办地的乡级人民政府和县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当依据各自职责实施必要的管理和指导。

**Article 42** Where a large-scale religious activity is held across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities, exceeding the capacity of a religious activity site, or held outside a religious activity site, the sponsoring religious group or temple/church shall apply to the religious affairs department of the municipal people's government with districts where the large-scale religious activity is held 30 days before the proposed date. The religious affairs department of the municipal people's government with districts shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt, after soliciting opinions from the public security organ of the people's government at the same level, make a decision to approve or not approve. If a decision to approve is made, the approving organ shall file with the religious affairs department of the provincial people's government.  
Large-scale religious activities shall be conducted in accordance with the requirements stated in the approval notice and in accordance with religious rituals, and shall not violate the relevant provisions of Articles 4 and 5 of these Regulations. The sponsoring religious group or temple/church shall take effective measures to prevent accidents and ensure the safe and orderly conduct of large-scale religious activities. The township people's government and the relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level where the large-scale religious activity is held shall, according to their respective duties, implement necessary management and guidance.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　信仰伊斯兰教的中国公民前往国外朝觐，由伊斯兰教全国性宗教团体负责组织。

**Article 43** Chinese citizens of the Islamic faith going abroad for pilgrimage shall be organized by a national religious group of Islam.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　禁止在宗教院校以外的学校及其他教育机构传教、举行宗教活动、成立宗教组织、设立宗教活动场所。

**Article 44** It shall be prohibited to preach, hold religious activities, establish religious organizations, or set up religious activity sites in schools or other educational institutions other than religious colleges.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　宗教团体、宗教院校和寺观教堂按照国家有关规定可以编印、发送宗教内部资料性出版物。出版公开发行的宗教出版物，按照国家出版管理的规定办理。  
涉及宗教内容的出版物，应当符合国家出版管理的规定，并不得含有下列内容：  
(一)破坏信教公民与不信教公民和睦相处的；  
(二)破坏不同宗教之间和睦以及宗教内部和睦的；  
(三)歧视、侮辱信教公民或者不信教公民的；  
(四)宣扬宗教极端主义的；  
(五)违背宗教的独立自主自办原则的。

**Article 45** Religious groups, religious colleges, and temples/churches may compile and distribute internal religious publications in accordance with relevant state regulations. The publication of publicly distributed religious publications shall be handled in accordance with state publication management regulations.  
Publications containing religious content shall comply with state publication management regulations and shall not contain any of the following content:  
(1) content that undermines harmony between religious and non-religious citizens;  
(2) content that undermines harmony between different religions or within a religion;  
(3) content that discriminates against or insults religious or non-religious citizens;  
(4) content that promotes religious extremism;  
(5) content that violates the principle of independence and self-management of religions.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　超出个人自用、合理数量的宗教类出版物及印刷品进境，或者以其他方式进口宗教类出版物及印刷品，应当按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 46** The import of religious publications and printed materials exceeding personal use or reasonable quantities, or the import of religious publications and printed materials by other means, shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　从事互联网宗教信息服务，应当经省级以上人民政府宗教事务部门审核同意后，按照国家互联网信息服务管理有关规定办理。

**Article 47** Those engaging in internet religious information services shall, after obtaining examination and approval from the religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, handle the matter in accordance with relevant state regulations on internet information service management.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　互联网宗教信息服务的内容应当符合有关法律、法规、规章和宗教事务管理的相关规定。  
互联网宗教信息服务的内容，不得违反[本条例第四十五条](#art-45)第二款的规定。

**Article 48** The content of internet religious information services shall comply with relevant laws, regulations, rules, and relevant provisions on religious affairs management.  
The content of internet religious information services shall not violate the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 45 of these Regulations.

### 第七章　宗教财产

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所对依法占有的属于国家、集体所有的财产，依照法律和国家有关规定管理和使用；对其他合法财产，依法享有所有权或者其他财产权利。

**Article 49** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall manage and use property that is owned by the state or collectives and lawfully possessed by them in accordance with laws and relevant state regulations; with respect to other lawful property, they shall enjoy ownership or other property rights according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所合法使用的土地，合法所有或者使用的房屋、构筑物、设施，以及其他合法财产、收益，受法律保护。  
任何组织或者个人不得侵占、哄抢、私分、损毁或者非法查封、扣押、冻结、没收、处分宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所的合法财产，不得损毁宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所占有、使用的文物。

**Article 50** The land lawfully used by religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities, the houses, structures, facilities lawfully owned or used, and other lawful property and income shall be protected by law.  
No organization or individual may encroach upon, loot, divide privately, damage, or illegally seal up, seize, freeze, confiscate, or dispose of the lawful property of religious groups, religious colleges, or sites for religious activities, nor may they damage the cultural relics in the possession or use of religious groups, religious colleges, or sites for religious activities.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所所有的房屋和使用的土地等不动产，应当依法向县级以上地方人民政府不动产登记机构申请不动产登记，领取不动产权证书；产权变更、转移的，应当及时办理变更、转移登记。  
涉及宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所土地使用权变更或者转移时，不动产登记机构应当征求本级人民政府宗教事务部门的意见。

**Article 51** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall apply for real estate registration with the real estate registration authority of the local people's government at or above the county level for the houses and land they own or use, and obtain real estate ownership certificates; when property rights change or are transferred, they shall promptly handle change or transfer registration.  
When changes or transfers of land use rights of religious groups, religious colleges, or sites for religious activities are involved, the real estate registration authority shall solicit opinions from the religious affairs department of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所是非营利性组织，其财产和收入应当用于与其宗旨相符的活动以及公益慈善事业，不得用于分配。

**Article 52** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities are non-profit organizations, and their property and income shall be used for activities consistent with their purposes and for public charity, and shall not be distributed.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　任何组织或者个人捐资修建宗教活动场所，不享有该宗教活动场所的所有权、使用权，不得从该宗教活动场所获得经济收益。  
禁止投资、承包经营宗教活动场所或者大型露天宗教造像，禁止以宗教名义进行商业宣传。

**Article 53** Any organization or individual that donates funds to build a site for religious activities shall not have ownership or use rights over the site, nor shall they obtain economic benefits from the site.  
Investment in or contracted operation of sites for religious activities or large open-air religious statues shall be prohibited, and commercial promotion under the guise of religion shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　宗教活动场所用于宗教活动的房屋、构筑物及其附属的宗教教职人员生活用房不得转让、抵押或者作为实物投资。

**Article 54** The houses and structures used for religious activities in a site for religious activities and the attached living quarters for religious personnel shall not be transferred, mortgaged, or used as in-kind investment.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　为了公共利益需要，征收宗教团体、宗教院校或者宗教活动场所房屋的，应当按照国家房屋征收的有关规定执行。宗教团体、宗教院校或者宗教活动场所可以选择货币补偿，也可以选择房屋产权调换或者重建。

**Article 55** If, for the needs of public interest, the houses of a religious group, religious college, or site for religious activities are expropriated, the relevant state regulations on house expropriation shall be implemented. The religious group, religious college, or site for religious activities may choose monetary compensation, or may choose housing property rights exchange or reconstruction.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所、宗教教职人员可以依法兴办公益慈善事业。  
任何组织或者个人不得利用公益慈善活动传教。

**Article 56** Religious groups, religious colleges, sites for religious activities, and religious personnel may lawfully establish and operate public charity undertakings.  
No organization or individual may use public charity activities to preach.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所可以按照国家有关规定接受境内外组织和个人的捐赠，用于与其宗旨相符的活动。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所不得接受境外组织和个人附带条件的捐赠，接受捐赠金额超过10万元的，应当报县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门审批。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所可以按照宗教习惯接受公民的捐赠，但不得强迫或者摊派。

**Article 57** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities may accept donations from domestic and foreign organizations and individuals in accordance with relevant state regulations for activities consistent with their purposes.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall not accept conditional donations from foreign organizations or individuals. If the amount of donations accepted exceeds 100,000 yuan, it shall be reported to the religious affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level for approval.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities may accept donations from citizens in accordance with religious customs, but shall not compel or apportion.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所应当执行国家统一的财务、资产、会计制度，向所在地的县级以上人民政府宗教事务部门报告财务状况、收支情况和接受、使用捐赠情况，接受其监督管理，并以适当方式向信教公民公布。宗教事务部门应当与有关部门共享相关管理信息。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所应当按照国家有关财务、会计制度，建立健全会计核算、财务报告、财务公开等制度，建立健全财务管理机构，配备必要的财务会计人员，加强财务管理。  
政府有关部门可以组织对宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所进行财务、资产检查和审计。

**Article 58** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall implement the unified state financial, asset, and accounting systems, report financial status, revenue and expenditure, and the acceptance and use of donations to the religious affairs department of the local people's government at or above the county level, accept their supervision and management, and disclose to religious citizens in an appropriate manner. The religious affairs department shall share relevant management information with relevant departments.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall, in accordance with relevant state financial and accounting systems, establish and improve accounting, financial reporting, financial disclosure, and other systems, establish and improve financial management institutions, equip necessary financial accounting personnel, and strengthen financial management.  
Government relevant departments may organize financial, asset inspections and audits of religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所应当依法办理税务登记。  
宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和宗教教职人员应当依法办理纳税申报，按照国家有关规定享受税收优惠。  
税务部门应当依法对宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和宗教教职人员实施税收管理。

**Article 59** Religious groups, religious colleges, and sites for religious activities shall handle tax registration according to law.  
Religious groups, religious colleges, sites for religious activities, and religious personnel shall handle tax declaration according to law and enjoy tax preferences in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
Tax authorities shall implement tax management over religious groups, religious colleges, sites for religious activities, and religious personnel according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所注销或者终止的，应当进行财产清算，清算后的剩余财产应当用于与其宗旨相符的事业。

**Article 60** When a religious group, religious college, or site for religious activity is dissolved or terminated, property liquidation shall be carried out, and the remaining property after liquidation shall be used for undertakings consistent with its purposes.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家工作人员在宗教事务管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，应当给予处分的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** State functionaries who abuse their power, neglect their duty, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the administration of religious affairs shall be given sanctions according to law if sanctions are warranted; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　强制公民信仰宗教或者不信仰宗教，或者干扰宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所正常的宗教活动的，由宗教事务部门责令改正；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
侵犯宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所和信教公民合法权益的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Any person who compels a citizen to believe in or not to believe in a religion, or interferes with the normal religious activities of a religious group, a religious school, or a venue for religious activities, shall be ordered by the religious affairs department to make corrections; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law.  
Any person who infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a religious group, a religious school, a venue for religious activities, or a believing citizen shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　宣扬、支持、资助宗教极端主义，或者利用宗教进行危害国家安全、公共安全，破坏民族团结、分裂国家和恐怖活动，侵犯公民人身权利、民主权利，妨害社会管理秩序，侵犯公私财产等违法活动，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由有关部门依法给予行政处罚；对公民、法人或者其他组织造成损失的，依法承担民事责任。  
宗教团体、宗教院校或者宗教活动场所有前款行为，情节严重的，有关部门应当采取必要的措施对其进行整顿，拒不接受整顿的，由登记管理机关或者批准设立机关依法吊销其登记证书或者设立许可。

**Article 63** Any person who advocates, supports, or funds religious extremism, or uses religion to engage in illegal activities such as endangering national security or public security, undermining ethnic unity, splitting the country, engaging in terrorist activities, infringing upon citizens' personal rights or democratic rights, disrupting social management order, or encroaching upon public or private property, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the relevant department shall impose an administrative penalty according to law; if losses are caused to citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, civil liability shall be borne according to law.  
If a religious group, a religious school, or a venue for religious activities commits the act set forth in the preceding paragraph, and the circumstances are serious, the relevant department shall take necessary measures to rectify it. If it refuses to accept rectification, the registration authority or the approving authority shall revoke its registration certificate or establishment permit according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　大型宗教活动过程中发生危害国家安全、公共安全或者严重破坏社会秩序情况的，由有关部门依照法律、法规进行处置和处罚；主办的宗教团体、寺观教堂负有责任的，由登记管理机关责令其撤换主要负责人，情节严重的，由登记管理机关吊销其登记证书。  
擅自举行大型宗教活动的，由宗教事务部门会同有关部门责令停止活动，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；有违法所得、非法财物的，没收违法所得和非法财物。其中，大型宗教活动是宗教团体、宗教活动场所擅自举办的，登记管理机关还可以责令该宗教团体、宗教活动场所撤换直接负责的主管人员。

**Article 64** If, during the course of a large-scale religious activity, any situation endangering national security or public security or seriously disrupting social order occurs, the relevant department shall handle and impose penalties in accordance with laws and regulations; if the sponsoring religious group or temple or church is at fault, the registration authority shall order it to replace its principal responsible person; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority shall revoke its registration certificate.  
If a large-scale religious activity is held without authorization, the religious affairs department, together with the relevant departments, shall order the activity to cease, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 300,000; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, the illegal gains and unlawful property shall be confiscated. Where the large-scale religious activity is held without authorization by a religious group or a venue for religious activities, the registration authority may also order such religious group or venue for religious activities to replace the directly responsible person in charge.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所有下列行为之一的，由宗教事务部门责令改正；情节较重的，由登记管理机关或者批准设立机关责令该宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所撤换直接负责的主管人员；情节严重的，由登记管理机关或者批准设立机关责令停止日常活动，改组管理组织，限期整改，拒不整改的，依法吊销其登记证书或者设立许可；有违法所得、非法财物的，予以没收：  
(一)未按规定办理变更登记或者备案手续的；  
(二)宗教院校违反培养目标、办学章程和课程设置要求的；  
(三)宗教活动场所违反[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定，未建立有关管理制度或者管理制度不符合要求的；  
(四)宗教活动场所违反[本条例第五十四条](#art-54)规定，将用于宗教活动的房屋、构筑物及其附属的宗教教职人员生活用房转让、抵押或者作为实物投资的；  
(五)宗教活动场所内发生重大事故、重大事件未及时报告，造成严重后果的；  
(六)违反[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定，违背宗教的独立自主自办原则的；  
(七)违反国家有关规定接受境内外捐赠的；  
(八)拒不接受行政管理机关依法实施的监督管理的。

**Article 65** If a religious group, a religious school, or a venue for religious activities commits any of the following acts, the religious affairs department shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are relatively serious, the registration authority or the approving authority shall order such religious group, religious school, or venue for religious activities to replace the directly responsible person in charge; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority or the approving authority shall order it to suspend daily activities, restructure its management organization, and make corrections within a specified time limit; if it refuses to make corrections, its registration certificate or establishment permit shall be revoked according to law; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, they shall be confiscated:  
(1) failing to go through the formalities for change registration or filing as required;  
(2) a religious school violating the cultivation objectives, school charter, and curriculum requirements;  
(3) a venue for religious activities violating the provisions of Article 26 of these Regulations, failing to establish relevant management systems or having management systems that do not meet the requirements;  
(4) a venue for religious activities violating the provisions of Article 54 of these Regulations, transferring, mortgaging, or using as in-kind investment the buildings, structures, and their ancillary living quarters for religious personnel used for religious activities;  
(5) a major accident or major incident occurring within a venue for religious activities and failing to report in a timely manner, resulting in serious consequences;  
(6) violating the provisions of Article 5 of these Regulations, contravening the principle of independence and self-management of religions;  
(7) accepting donations from domestic or overseas sources in violation of the relevant state provisions;  
(8) refusing to accept the supervision and management lawfully exercised by the administrative authority.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　临时活动地点的活动违反本条例相关规定的，由宗教事务部门责令改正；情节严重的，责令停止活动，撤销该临时活动地点；有违法所得、非法财物的，予以没收。

**Article 66** If the activities at a temporary activity site violate the relevant provisions of these Regulations, the religious affairs department shall order corrections; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the cessation of activities and revoke the temporary activity site; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, they shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　宗教团体、宗教院校、宗教活动场所违反国家有关财务、会计、资产、税收管理规定的，由财政、税务等部门依据相关规定进行处罚；情节严重的，经财政、税务部门提出，由登记管理机关或者批准设立机关吊销其登记证书或者设立许可。

**Article 67** If a religious group, a religious school, or a venue for religious activities violates the national provisions on financial, accounting, asset, or tax management, the finance and taxation departments shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions; if the circumstances are serious, upon the proposal of the finance and taxation departments, the registration authority or the approving authority shall revoke its registration certificate or establishment permit.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　涉及宗教内容的出版物或者互联网宗教信息服务有[本条例第四十五条](#art-45)第二款禁止内容的，由有关部门对相关责任单位及人员依法给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
擅自从事互联网宗教信息服务或者超出批准或备案项目提供服务的，由有关部门根据相关法律、法规处理。

**Article 68** If a publication involving religious content or an Internet religious information service contains any content prohibited in paragraph 2 of Article 45 of these Regulations, the relevant department shall impose an administrative penalty on the responsible unit and personnel according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Any person who engages in Internet religious information service without authorization or provides services beyond the approved or filed items shall be dealt with by the relevant department in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　擅自设立宗教活动场所的，宗教活动场所已被撤销登记或者吊销登记证书仍然进行宗教活动的，或者擅自设立宗教院校的，由宗教事务部门会同有关部门予以取缔，有违法所得、非法财物的，没收违法所得和非法财物，违法所得无法确定的，处5万元以下的罚款；有违法房屋、构筑物的，由规划、建设等部门依法处理；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
非宗教团体、非宗教院校、非宗教活动场所、非指定的临时活动地点组织、举行宗教活动，接受宗教性捐赠的，由宗教事务部门会同公安、民政、建设、教育、文化、旅游、文物等有关部门责令停止活动；有违法所得、非法财物的，没收违法所得和非法财物，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；违法所得无法确定的，处5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** If anyone establishes a venue for religious activities without authorization, or continues to conduct religious activities at a venue for religious activities that has had its registration revoked or its registration certificate cancelled, or establishes a religious school without authorization, the religious affairs department together with the relevant departments shall ban it; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, the illegal gains and unlawful property shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains cannot be determined, a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed; if there are illegal buildings or structures, the planning and construction departments shall deal with them according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law.  
If a non-religious group, non-religious school, non-venue for religious activities, or non-designated temporary activity site organizes or holds religious activities or accepts religious donations, the religious affairs department together with the public security, civil affairs, construction, education, culture, tourism, cultural heritage and other relevant departments shall order the cessation of activities; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, the illegal gains and unlawful property shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount of the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if the illegal gains cannot be determined, a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　擅自组织公民出境参加宗教方面的培训、会议、朝觐等活动的，或者擅自开展宗教教育培训的，由宗教事务部门会同有关部门责令停止活动，可以并处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
在宗教院校以外的学校及其他教育机构传教、举行宗教活动、成立宗教组织、设立宗教活动场所的，由其审批机关或者其他有关部门责令限期改正并予以警告；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停止招生、吊销办学许可；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 70** If anyone organizes citizens to go abroad to participate in religious training, conferences, pilgrimages, or other activities without authorization, or conducts religious education and training without authorization, the religious affairs department together with the relevant departments shall order the cessation of activities, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If anyone preaches, holds religious activities, establishes a religious organization, or sets up a venue for religious activities in a school other than a religious school or in other educational institutions, the approving authority or other relevant department shall order corrections within a specified period and give a warning; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the authority shall order the cessation of enrollment and revoke the school-running permit; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　为违法宗教活动提供条件的，由宗教事务部门给予警告，有违法所得、非法财物的，没收违法所得和非法财物，情节严重的，并处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法房屋、构筑物的，由规划、建设等部门依法处理；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 71** If anyone provides conditions for illegal religious activities, the religious affairs department shall give a warning; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, the illegal gains and unlawful property shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000 shall also be imposed; if there are illegal buildings or structures, the planning and construction departments shall deal with them according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例规定修建大型露天宗教造像的，由宗教事务部门会同国土、规划、建设、旅游等部门责令停止施工，限期拆除，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，并处造像建设工程造价百分之五以上百分之十以下的罚款。  
投资、承包经营宗教活动场所或者大型露天宗教造像的，由宗教事务部门会同工商、规划、建设等部门责令改正，并没收违法所得；情节严重的，由登记管理机关吊销该宗教活动场所的登记证书，并依法追究相关人员的责任。

**Article 72** If anyone constructs a large-scale outdoor religious statue in violation of these Regulations, the religious affairs department together with the land, planning, construction, tourism and other departments shall order the cessation of construction and demolition within a specified time limit; if there are illegal gains, they shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than five percent but not more than ten percent of the construction cost of the statue shall also be imposed.  
If anyone invests in or contracts for the operation of a venue for religious activities or a large-scale outdoor religious statue, the religious affairs department together with the industry and commerce, planning, construction and other departments shall order corrections and confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, the registration authority shall revoke the registration certificate of the venue for religious activities, and the liability of the relevant personnel shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　宗教教职人员有下列行为之一的，由宗教事务部门给予警告，没收违法所得和非法财物；情节严重的，由宗教事务部门建议有关宗教团体、宗教院校或者宗教活动场所暂停其主持教务活动或者取消其宗教教职人员身份，并追究有关宗教团体、宗教院校或者宗教活动场所负责人的责任；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)宣扬、支持、资助宗教极端主义，破坏民族团结、分裂国家和进行恐怖活动或者参与相关活动的；  
(二)受境外势力支配，擅自接受境外宗教团体或者机构委任教职，以及其他违背宗教的独立自主自办原则的；  
(三)违反国家有关规定接受境内外捐赠的；  
(四)组织、主持未经批准的在宗教活动场所外举行的宗教活动的；  
(五)其他违反法律、法规、规章的行为。

**Article 73** If a religious professional commits any of the following acts, the religious affairs department shall give a warning and confiscate the illegal gains and unlawful property; if the circumstances are serious, the religious affairs department shall recommend to the relevant religious group, religious school, or venue for religious activities that it suspend his or her presiding over religious affairs or revoke his or her status as a religious professional, and investigate the liability of the person in charge of the relevant religious group, religious school, or venue for religious activities; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) advocating, supporting, or funding religious extremism, undermining ethnic unity, splitting the country, engaging in terrorist activities, or participating in related activities;  
(2) being controlled by overseas forces, accepting a religious post appointed by an overseas religious group or institution without authorization, or other acts contravening the principle of independence and self-management of religions;  
(3) accepting donations from domestic or overseas sources in violation of the relevant state provisions;  
(4) organizing or presiding over unauthorized religious activities held outside a venue for religious activities;  
(5) other acts violating laws, regulations, or rules.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　假冒宗教教职人员进行宗教活动或者骗取钱财等违法活动的，由宗教事务部门责令停止活动；有违法所得、非法财物的，没收违法所得和非法财物，并处1万元以下的罚款；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** If anyone impersonates a religious professional to conduct religious activities or to defraud money or property, the religious affairs department shall order the cessation of activities; if there are illegal gains or unlawful property, the illegal gains and unlawful property shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than RMB 10,000 shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　对宗教事务部门的行政行为不服的，可以依法申请行政复议；对行政复议决定不服的，可以依法提起行政诉讼。

**Article 75** If anyone is dissatisfied with an administrative act of the religious affairs department, he or she may apply for administrative reconsideration according to law; if dissatisfied with the decision of administrative reconsideration, he or she may initiate an administrative action according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区进行宗教交往，按照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定办理。

**Article 76** Religious exchanges between the mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region shall be conducted in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　本条例自2018年2月1日起施行。

**Article 77** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2018.
