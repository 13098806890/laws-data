### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对娱乐场所的管理，保障娱乐场所的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of entertainment venues and ensure their healthy development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称娱乐场所，是指以营利为目的，并向公众开放、消费者自娱自乐的歌舞、游艺等场所。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "entertainment venues" means places such as singing and dancing venues and amusement venues that are operated for profit, open to the public, and where consumers entertain themselves.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府文化主管部门负责对娱乐场所日常经营活动的监督管理；县级以上公安部门负责对娱乐场所消防、治安状况的监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent cultural departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the daily business activities of entertainment venues; the public security departments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of fire control and public security conditions of entertainment venues.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家机关及其工作人员不得开办娱乐场所，不得参与或者变相参与娱乐场所的经营活动。  
与文化主管部门、公安部门的工作人员有夫妻关系、直系血亲关系、三代以内旁系血亲关系以及近姻亲关系的亲属，不得开办娱乐场所，不得参与或者变相参与娱乐场所的经营活动。

**Article 4** State organs and their staff members shall not establish entertainment venues, and shall not participate or participate in a disguised form in the business activities of entertainment venues.  
Relatives who have a spousal relationship, direct blood relationship, collateral blood relationship within three generations, or close affinity relationship with the staff members of the competent cultural departments or public security departments shall not establish entertainment venues, and shall not participate or participate in a disguised form in the business activities of entertainment venues.

### 第二章　设立

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的人员，不得开办娱乐场所或者在娱乐场所内从业：  
(一)曾犯有组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫罪，制作、贩卖、传播淫秽物品罪，走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品罪，强奸罪，强制猥亵、侮辱妇女罪，赌博罪，洗钱罪，组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织罪的；  
(二)因犯罪曾被剥夺政治权利的；  
(三)因吸食、注射毒品曾被强制戒毒的；  
(四)因卖淫、嫖娼曾被处以行政拘留的。

**Article 5** Persons falling under any of the following circumstances shall not establish entertainment venues or work within entertainment venues:  
(1) Those who have been convicted of the crimes of organizing, forcing, luring, harboring or procuring prostitution; producing, selling or disseminating pornographic materials; smuggling, selling, transporting or manufacturing drugs; rape; forcible indecency or insulting women; gambling; money laundering; or organizing, leading or participating in a criminal organization of a mafia-like nature;  
(2) Those who have been deprived of political rights as a result of a crime;  
(3) Those who have been subjected to compulsory drug rehabilitation due to drug inhalation or injection;  
(4) Those who have been subjected to administrative detention for prostitution or patronizing prostitution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国投资者可以依法在中国境内设立娱乐场所。

**Article 6** Foreign investors may establish entertainment venues within the territory of China according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　娱乐场所不得设在下列地点：  
(一)居民楼、博物馆、图书馆和被核定为文物保护单位的建筑物内；  
(二)居民住宅区和学校、医院、机关周围；  
(三)车站、机场等人群密集的场所；  
(四)建筑物地下一层以下；  
(五)与危险化学品仓库毗连的区域。  
娱乐场所的边界噪声，应当符合国家规定的环境噪声标准。

**Article 7** Entertainment venues shall not be located in the following places:  
(1) Within residential buildings, museums, libraries, and buildings designated as cultural relics protection units;  
(2) In the vicinity of residential areas, schools, hospitals, and government offices;  
(3) Densely populated places such as railway stations and airports;  
(4) Below the first underground floor of a building;  
(5) Areas adjacent to warehouses of hazardous chemicals.  
The boundary noise of entertainment venues shall comply with the national environmental noise standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　娱乐场所的使用面积，不得低于国务院文化主管部门规定的最低标准；设立含有电子游戏机的游艺娱乐场所，应当符合国务院文化主管部门关于总量和布局的要求。

**Article 8** The usable floor area of an entertainment venue shall not be less than the minimum standard prescribed by the competent cultural department of the State Council; the establishment of amusement venues containing electronic game machines shall comply with the requirements of the competent cultural department of the State Council on total quantity and distribution.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　娱乐场所申请从事娱乐场所经营活动，应当向所在地县级人民政府文化主管部门提出申请；外商投资的娱乐场所申请从事娱乐场所经营活动，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化主管部门提出申请。  
娱乐场所申请从事娱乐场所经营活动，应当提交投资人员、拟任的法定代表人和其他负责人没有[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定情形的书面声明。申请人应当对书面声明内容的真实性负责。  
受理申请的文化主管部门应当就书面声明向公安部门或者其他有关单位核查，公安部门或者其他有关单位应当予以配合；经核查属实的，文化主管部门应当依据[本条例第七条](#art-7)、第八条的规定进行实地检查，作出决定。予以批准的，颁发娱乐经营许可证，并根据国务院文化主管部门的规定核定娱乐场所容纳的消费者数量；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
有关法律、行政法规规定需要办理消防、卫生、环境保护等审批手续的，从其规定。

**Article 9** An entertainment venue that applies to engage in entertainment business activities shall submit an application to the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level where it is located; an entertainment venue with foreign investment that applies to engage in entertainment business activities shall submit an application to the competent cultural department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
An entertainment venue applying to engage in entertainment business activities shall submit a written declaration that the investors, the proposed legal representative, and other responsible persons do not fall under any of the circumstances specified in Article 5 of these Regulations. The applicant shall be responsible for the authenticity of the content of the written declaration.  
The competent cultural department that accepts the application shall verify the written declaration with the public security department or other relevant units, and the public security department or other relevant units shall cooperate; after verification confirms the declaration, the competent cultural department shall conduct an on-site inspection in accordance with Articles 7 and 8 of these Regulations and make a decision. If the application is approved, it shall issue an entertainment business license and determine the number of consumers that the entertainment venue may accommodate according to the provisions of the competent cultural department of the State Council; if the application is not approved, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Where relevant laws or administrative regulations require the completion of approval procedures for fire control, sanitation, environmental protection, etc., those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　文化主管部门审批娱乐场所应当举行听证。有关听证的程序，依照《中华人民共和国行政许可法》的规定执行。

**Article 10** The competent cultural department shall hold a hearing when examining and approving entertainment venues. The procedures for such hearings shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　娱乐场所依法取得营业执照和相关批准文件、许可证后，应当在15日内向所在地县级公安部门备案。

**Article 11** After an entertainment venue has obtained a business license and relevant approval documents and permits according to law, it shall file with the public security department at the county level where it is located within 15 days.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　娱乐场所改建、扩建营业场所或者变更场地、主要设施设备、投资人员，或者变更娱乐经营许可证载明的事项的，应当向原发证机关申请重新核发娱乐经营许可证，并向公安部门备案；需要办理变更登记的，应当依法向工商行政管理部门办理变更登记。

**Article 12** Where an entertainment venue reconstructs or expands its business premises, or changes the site, major facilities and equipment, investors, or matters specified in the entertainment business license, it shall apply to the original licensing authority for reissuance of the entertainment business license and file with the public security department; if a change of registration is required, it shall undergo change registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

### 第三章　经营

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家倡导弘扬民族优秀文化，禁止娱乐场所内的娱乐活动含有下列内容：  
(一)违反宪法确定的基本原则的；  
(二)危害国家统一、主权或者领土完整的；  
(三)危害国家安全，或者损害国家荣誉、利益的；  
(四)煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，伤害民族感情或者侵害民族风俗、习惯，破坏民族团结的；  
(五)违反国家宗教政策，宣扬邪教、迷信的；  
(六)宣扬淫秽、赌博、暴力以及与毒品有关的违法犯罪活动，或者教唆犯罪的；  
(七)违背社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
(八)侮辱、诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
(九)法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容。

**Article 13** The State advocates the promotion of the excellent national culture and prohibits entertainment activities within entertainment venues from containing any of the following content:  
(1) Violating the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) Endangering the unity, sovereignty, or territorial integrity of the State;  
(3) Endangering national security, or harming national honor or interests;  
(4) Inciting ethnic hatred or discrimination, harming ethnic sentiments or infringing upon ethnic customs and habits, or undermining ethnic unity;  
(5) Violating the State's religious policies and propagating cults or superstitions;  
(6) Propagating obscenity, gambling, violence, and drug-related illegal or criminal activities, or instigating crimes;  
(7) Contravening social morality or the excellent national cultural traditions;  
(8) Insulting or slandering others, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) Other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　娱乐场所及其从业人员不得实施下列行为，不得为进入娱乐场所的人员实施下列行为提供条件：  
(一)贩卖、提供毒品，或者组织、强迫、教唆、引诱、欺骗、容留他人吸食、注射毒品；  
(二)组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫、嫖娼；  
(三)制作、贩卖、传播淫秽物品；  
(四)提供或者从事以营利为目的的陪侍；  
(五)赌博；  
(六)从事邪教、迷信活动；  
(七)其他违法犯罪行为。  
娱乐场所的从业人员不得吸食、注射毒品，不得卖淫、嫖娼；娱乐场所及其从业人员不得为进入娱乐场所的人员实施上述行为提供条件。

**Article 14** Entertainment venues and their employees shall not commit the following acts, and shall not provide conditions for persons entering the entertainment venues to commit the following acts:  
(1) Selling or providing drugs, or organizing, forcing, instigating, luring, deceiving, or harboring others to inhale or inject drugs;  
(2) Organizing, forcing, luring, harboring, or procuring others to engage in prostitution or patronizing prostitution;  
(3) Producing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials;  
(4) Providing or engaging in profit-oriented escort services;  
(5) Gambling;  
(6) Engaging in cult or superstitious activities;  
(7) Other illegal or criminal acts.  
Employees of entertainment venues shall not inhale or inject drugs, and shall not engage in prostitution or patronizing prostitution; entertainment venues and their employees shall not provide conditions for persons entering the entertainment venues to commit the acts mentioned above.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　歌舞娱乐场所应当按照国务院公安部门的规定在营业场所的出入口、主要通道安装闭路电视监控设备，并应当保证闭路电视监控设备在营业期间正常运行，不得中断。  
歌舞娱乐场所应当将闭路电视监控录像资料留存30日备查，不得删改或者挪作他用。

**Article 15** Singing and dancing entertainment venues shall install closed-circuit television monitoring equipment at the entrances, exits, and main passages of the business premises in accordance with the provisions of the public security department of the State Council, and shall ensure that the closed-circuit television monitoring equipment operates normally during business hours and is not interrupted.  
Singing and dancing entertainment venues shall retain the closed-circuit television monitoring video recordings for 30 days for inspection, and shall not delete, alter, or use them for other purposes.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　歌舞娱乐场所的包厢、包间内不得设置隔断，并应当安装展现室内整体环境的透明门窗。包厢、包间的门不得有内锁装置。

**Article 16** Inside the boxes or private rooms of singing and dancing entertainment venues, no partitions shall be installed, and transparent doors and windows that show the overall indoor environment shall be installed. The doors of boxes and private rooms shall not have internal locking devices.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　营业期间，歌舞娱乐场所内亮度不得低于国家规定的标准。

**Article 17** During business hours, the brightness inside a singing and dancing entertainment venue shall not be lower than the standard prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　娱乐场所使用的音像制品或者电子游戏应当是依法出版、生产或者进口的产品。  
歌舞娱乐场所播放的曲目和屏幕画面以及游艺娱乐场所的电子游戏机内的游戏项目，不得含有[本条例第十三条](#art-13)禁止的内容；歌舞娱乐场所使用的歌曲点播系统不得与境外的曲库联接。

**Article 18** The audio-visual products or electronic games used in entertainment venues shall be products that are published, produced, or imported according to law.  
The tracks and screen images played in singing and dancing entertainment venues, as well as the game items in the electronic game machines in amusement venues, shall not contain any content prohibited by Article 13 of these Regulations; the song-on-demand systems used in singing and dancing entertainment venues shall not be connected to overseas music libraries.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　游艺娱乐场所不得设置具有赌博功能的电子游戏机机型、机种、电路板等游戏设施设备，不得以现金或者有价证券作为奖品，不得回购奖品。

**Article 19** Amusement venues shall not install gaming facilities and equipment such as electronic game machine models, types, or circuit boards that have gambling functions, shall not use cash or securities as prizes, and shall not buy back prizes.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　娱乐场所的法定代表人或者主要负责人应当对娱乐场所的消防安全和其他安全负责。  
娱乐场所应当确保其建筑、设施符合国家安全标准和消防技术规范，定期检查消防设施状况，并及时维护、更新。  
娱乐场所应当制定安全工作方案和应急疏散预案。

**Article 20** The legal representative or principal responsible person of an entertainment venue shall be responsible for the fire safety and other safety of the entertainment venue.  
The entertainment venue shall ensure that its buildings and facilities comply with national safety standards and fire technical specifications, regularly inspect the condition of fire-fighting facilities, and promptly maintain and update them.  
The entertainment venue shall formulate a safety work plan and an emergency evacuation plan.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　营业期间，娱乐场所应当保证疏散通道和安全出口畅通，不得封堵、锁闭疏散通道和安全出口，不得在疏散通道和安全出口设置栅栏等影响疏散的障碍物。  
娱乐场所应当在疏散通道和安全出口设置明显指示标志，不得遮挡、覆盖指示标志。

**Article 21** During business hours, entertainment venues shall ensure that evacuation passages and safety exits are unobstructed, and shall not block or lock evacuation passages and safety exits, and shall not install obstacles such as railings that affect evacuation in evacuation passages and safety exits.  
Entertainment venues shall set up conspicuous directional signs at evacuation passages and safety exits, and shall not obscure or cover the directional signs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何人不得非法携带枪支、弹药、管制器具或者携带爆炸性、易燃性、毒害性、放射性、腐蚀性等危险物品和传染病病原体进入娱乐场所。  
迪斯科舞厅应当配备安全检查设备，对进入营业场所的人员进行安全检查。

**Article 22** No person shall illegally bring firearms, ammunition, controlled instruments, or dangerous goods such as explosive, flammable, toxic, radioactive, corrosive substances, or infectious disease pathogens into an entertainment venue.  
Disco halls shall be equipped with security inspection equipment to conduct security checks on persons entering the business premises.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　歌舞娱乐场所不得接纳未成年人。除国家法定节假日外，游艺娱乐场所设置的电子游戏机不得向未成年人提供。

**Article 23** Singing and dancing entertainment venues shall not admit minors. Except for statutory holidays of the State, electronic game machines installed in amusement entertainment venues shall not be made available to minors.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　娱乐场所不得招用未成年人；招用外国人的，应当按照国家有关规定为其办理外国人就业许可证。

**Article 24** Entertainment venues shall not employ minors; where foreigners are employed, the entertainment venue shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, handle the foreigner's employment permit.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　娱乐场所应当与从业人员签订文明服务责任书，并建立从业人员名簿；从业人员名簿应当包括从业人员的真实姓名、居民身份证复印件、外国人就业许可证复印件等内容。  
娱乐场所应当建立营业日志，记载营业期间从业人员的工作职责、工作时间、工作地点；营业日志不得删改，并应当留存60日备查。

**Article 25** Entertainment venues shall sign a civilized service responsibility letter with employees and establish an employee roster; the employee roster shall include the true names of employees, copies of their resident identity cards, copies of foreigner employment permits, and other such content.  
Entertainment venues shall establish business logs recording the work responsibilities, working hours, and work locations of employees during business hours; the business logs shall not be altered or deleted, and shall be retained for 60 days for inspection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　娱乐场所应当与保安服务企业签订保安服务合同，配备专业保安人员；不得聘用其他人员从事保安工作。

**Article 26** Entertainment venues shall sign a security service contract with a security service enterprise and employ professional security personnel; they shall not hire other persons to engage in security work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　营业期间，娱乐场所的从业人员应当统一着工作服，佩带工作标志并携带居民身份证或者外国人就业许可证。  
从业人员应当遵守职业道德和卫生规范，诚实守信，礼貌待人，不得侵害消费者的人身和财产权利。

**Article 27** During business hours, employees of entertainment venues shall wear uniforms uniformly, wear work badges, and carry their resident identity cards or foreigner employment permits.  
Employees shall abide by professional ethics and hygiene standards, be honest and trustworthy, treat others politely, and shall not infringe upon consumers' personal and property rights.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　每日凌晨2时至上午8时，娱乐场所不得营业。

**Article 28** Entertainment venues shall not operate between 2:00 a.m. and 8:00 a.m. daily.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　娱乐场所提供娱乐服务项目和出售商品，应当明码标价，并向消费者出示价目表；不得强迫、欺骗消费者接受服务、购买商品。

**Article 29** Entertainment venues shall clearly mark prices for entertainment service items and goods for sale, and shall present a price list to consumers; they shall not force or deceive consumers into accepting services or purchasing goods.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　娱乐场所应当在营业场所的大厅、包厢、包间内的显著位置悬挂含有禁毒、禁赌、禁止卖淫嫖娼等内容的警示标志、未成年人禁入或者限入标志。标志应当注明公安部门、文化主管部门的举报电话。

**Article 30** Entertainment venues shall display warning signs containing content such as prohibition of drugs, gambling, and prostitution, as well as signs indicating that minors are prohibited or restricted from entering, in conspicuous locations within the lobby, private rooms, and compartments of the business premises. The signs shall indicate the reporting telephone numbers of the public security department and the cultural administrative department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　娱乐场所应当建立巡查制度，发现娱乐场所内有违法犯罪活动的，应当立即向所在地县级公安部门、县级人民政府文化主管部门报告。

**Article 31** Entertainment venues shall establish a patrol system; if any illegal or criminal activity is found within the entertainment venue, it shall immediately report to the local county-level public security department and the county-level people's government cultural administrative department.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门的工作人员依法履行监督检查职责时，有权进入娱乐场所。娱乐场所应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。  
文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门的工作人员依法履行监督检查职责时，需要查阅闭路电视监控录像资料、从业人员名簿、营业日志等资料的，娱乐场所应当及时提供。

**Article 32** When staff members of the cultural administrative department, the public security department, and other relevant departments perform their supervisory inspection duties according to law, they have the right to enter entertainment venues. Entertainment venues shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.  
When staff members of the cultural administrative department, the public security department, and other relevant departments perform their supervisory inspection duties according to law and need to access materials such as closed-circuit television surveillance recordings, employee rosters, and business logs, the entertainment venue shall promptly provide them.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门应当记录监督检查的情况和处理结果。监督检查记录由监督检查人员签字归档。公众有权查阅监督检查记录。

**Article 33** The cultural administrative department, the public security department, and other relevant departments shall record the circumstances and results of their supervisory inspections. The supervisory inspection records shall be signed and filed by the supervisory inspectors. The public has the right to consult the supervisory inspection records.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门应当建立娱乐场所违法行为警示记录系统；对列入警示记录的娱乐场所，应当及时向社会公布，并加大监督检查力度。

**Article 34** The cultural administrative department, the public security department, and other relevant departments shall establish a warning record system for illegal acts of entertainment venues; for entertainment venues included in the warning records, they shall promptly make them public to the society and intensify supervisory inspections.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　文化主管部门应当建立娱乐场所的经营活动信用监管制度，建立健全信用约束机制，并及时公布行政处罚信息。

**Article 35** The cultural administrative department shall establish a credit supervision system for the business activities of entertainment venues, establish and improve a credit constraint mechanism, and promptly publish information on administrative penalties.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门应当建立相互间的信息通报制度，及时通报监督检查情况和处理结果。

**Article 36** The cultural administrative department, the public security department, and other relevant departments shall establish a mutual information notification system to promptly notify each other of supervisory inspection circumstances and handling results.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位或者个人发现娱乐场所内有违反本条例行为的，有权向文化主管部门、公安部门等有关部门举报。  
文化主管部门、公安部门等有关部门接到举报，应当记录，并及时依法调查、处理；对不属于本部门职责范围的，应当及时移送有关部门。

**Article 37** Any unit or individual that discovers any act violating these Regulations within an entertainment venue shall have the right to report it to the cultural administrative department, the public security department, or other relevant departments.  
Upon receiving a report, the cultural administrative department, the public security department, or other relevant departments shall record it and promptly investigate and handle it according to law; if the matter is not within the scope of their functions and duties, they shall promptly transfer it to the relevant department.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　上级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门在必要时，可以依照本条例的规定调查、处理由下级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门调查、处理的案件。  
下级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门认为案件重大、复杂的，可以请求移送上级人民政府文化主管部门、公安部门调查、处理。

**Article 38** When necessary, the cultural administrative department or the public security department of a higher-level people's government may, in accordance with these Regulations, investigate and handle cases that are investigated and handled by the cultural administrative department or the public security department of a lower-level people's government.  
Where a lower-level people's government's cultural administrative department or public security department considers a case to be major or complex, it may request to transfer the case to the higher-level people's government's cultural administrative department or public security department for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　文化主管部门、公安部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员违反本条例规定的，任何单位或者个人可以向依法有权处理的本级或者上一级机关举报。接到举报的机关应当依法及时调查、处理。

**Article 39** Where the cultural administrative department, the public security department, or other relevant departments or their staff members violate the provisions of these Regulations, any unit or individual may report to the organ at the same level or the organ at the next higher level that has the authority to handle the matter according to law. The organ that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle it according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　娱乐场所行业协会应当依照章程的规定，制定行业自律规范，加强对会员经营活动的指导、监督。

**Article 40** The entertainment venue industry association shall, in accordance with the provisions of its articles of association, formulate industry self-discipline norms, and strengthen guidance and supervision over the business activities of its members.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，擅自从事娱乐场所经营活动的，由文化主管部门依法予以取缔；公安部门在查处治安、刑事案件时，发现擅自从事娱乐场所经营活动的，应当依法予以取缔。

**Article 41** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, engages in business activities of an entertainment place without authorization, the competent cultural department shall ban such activities according to law; where the public security department, when investigating public security or criminal cases, discovers any unauthorized engagement in business activities of an entertainment place, it shall ban such activities according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，以欺骗等不正当手段取得娱乐经营许可证的，由原发证机关撤销娱乐经营许可证。

**Article 42** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, obtains an entertainment business license by fraudulent or other illegitimate means, the original issuing authority shall revoke the entertainment business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　娱乐场所实施[本条例第十四条](#art-14)禁止行为的，由县级公安部门没收违法所得和非法财物，责令停业整顿3个月至6个月；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销娱乐经营许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where an entertainment place commits any act prohibited under Article 14 of these Regulations, the public security department at the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and illegal property, and order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of three to six months; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the entertainment business license, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　娱乐场所违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级公安部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月：  
(一)照明设施、包厢、包间的设置以及门窗的使用不符合本条例规定的；  
(二)未按照本条例规定安装闭路电视监控设备或者中断使用的；  
(三)未按照本条例规定留存监控录像资料或者删改监控录像资料的；  
(四)未按照本条例规定配备安全检查设备或者未对进入营业场所的人员进行安全检查的；  
(五)未按照本条例规定配备保安人员的。

**Article 44** Where an entertainment place, in violation of these Regulations, falls under any of the following circumstances, the public security department at the county level shall order rectification and issue a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to three months:  
(1) where the lighting facilities, the setting of private rooms and compartments, and the use of doors and windows do not conform to the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) where closed-circuit television monitoring equipment is not installed in accordance with these Regulations or its use is interrupted;  
(3) where monitoring video recordings are not retained in accordance with these Regulations or are deleted or altered;  
(4) where security inspection equipment is not equipped in accordance with these Regulations or security inspections are not conducted on persons entering the business premises;  
(5) where security personnel are not equipped in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　娱乐场所违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级公安部门没收违法所得和非法财物，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月：  
(一)设置具有赌博功能的电子游戏机机型、机种、电路板等游戏设施设备的；  
(二)以现金、有价证券作为奖品，或者回购奖品的。

**Article 45** Where an entertainment place, in violation of these Regulations, falls under any of the following circumstances, the public security department at the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and illegal property, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 10,000 yuan, it shall concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to three months:  
(1) where electronic game machine models, types, circuit boards, or other game facilities and equipment with gambling functions are installed;  
(2) where cash or negotiable securities are used as prizes, or prizes are bought back.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　娱乐场所指使、纵容从业人员侵害消费者人身权利的，应当依法承担民事责任，并由县级公安部门责令停业整顿1个月至3个月；造成严重后果的，由原发证机关吊销娱乐经营许可证。

**Article 46** Where an entertainment place instructs or connives at its employees infringing upon the personal rights of consumers, it shall bear civil liability according to law, and the public security department at the county level shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to three months; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing authority shall revoke the entertainment business license.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　娱乐场所取得营业执照后，未按照本条例规定向公安部门备案的，由县级公安部门责令改正，给予警告。

**Article 47** Where an entertainment place, after obtaining a business license, fails to file with the public security department in accordance with these Regulations, the public security department at the county level shall order rectification and issue a warning.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门没收违法所得和非法财物，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，并处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至6个月：  
(一)歌舞娱乐场所的歌曲点播系统与境外的曲库联接的；  
(二)歌舞娱乐场所播放的曲目、屏幕画面或者游艺娱乐场所电子游戏机内的游戏项目含有[本条例第十三条](#art-13)禁止内容的；  
(三)歌舞娱乐场所接纳未成年人的；  
(四)游艺娱乐场所设置的电子游戏机在国家法定节假日外向未成年人提供的；  
(五)娱乐场所容纳的消费者超过核定人数的。

**Article 48** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and illegal property, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 10,000 yuan, it shall concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 30,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to six months:  
(1) where the song request system of a song and dance entertainment place is connected to a song library outside the territory;  
(2) where the songs played, screen images of a song and dance entertainment place, or the game items in the electronic game machines of a game entertainment place contain content prohibited under Article 13 of these Regulations;  
(3) where a song and dance entertainment place admits minors;  
(4) where the electronic game machines installed in a game entertainment place are provided to minors outside the statutory holidays;  
(5) where the number of consumers accommodated by the entertainment place exceeds the approved number.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　娱乐场所违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月：  
(一)变更有关事项，未按照本条例规定申请重新核发娱乐经营许可证的；  
(二)在本条例规定的禁止营业时间内营业的；  
(三)从业人员在营业期间未统一着装并佩带工作标志的。

**Article 49** Where an entertainment place, in violation of these Regulations, falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level shall order rectification and issue a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to three months:  
(1) where changes to relevant matters are made without applying for re-issuance of the entertainment business license in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) where business is conducted during the prohibited business hours as prescribed in these Regulations;  
(3) where employees fail to wear uniform attire and work badges during business hours.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　娱乐场所未按照本条例规定建立从业人员名簿、营业日志，或者发现违法犯罪行为未按照本条例规定报告的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门、县级公安部门依据法定职权责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月。

**Article 50** Where an entertainment place fails to establish an employee roster or business log in accordance with these Regulations, or fails to report any illegal or criminal acts discovered in accordance with these Regulations, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level or the public security department at the county level shall, within their statutory functions and powers, order rectification and issue a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of one to three months.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　娱乐场所未按照本条例规定悬挂警示标志、未成年人禁入或者限入标志的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门、县级公安部门依据法定职权责令改正，给予警告。

**Article 51** Where an entertainment place fails to display warning signs, signs prohibiting or restricting the entry of minors in accordance with these Regulations, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level or the public security department at the county level shall, within their statutory functions and powers, order rectification and issue a warning.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　娱乐场所招用未成年人的，由劳动保障行政部门责令改正，并按照每招用一名未成年人每月处5000元罚款的标准给予处罚。

**Article 52** Where an entertainment place employs minors, the labor and social security administrative department shall order rectification and impose a penalty at the standard of a fine of RMB 5,000 yuan per month for each minor employed.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　因擅自从事娱乐场所经营活动被依法取缔的，其投资人员和负责人终身不得投资开办娱乐场所或者担任娱乐场所的法定代表人、负责人。  
娱乐场所因违反本条例规定，被吊销或者撤销娱乐经营许可证的，自被吊销或者撤销之日起，其法定代表人、负责人5年内不得担任娱乐场所的法定代表人、负责人。  
娱乐场所因违反本条例规定，2年内被处以3次警告或者罚款又有违反本条例的行为应受行政处罚的，由县级人民政府文化主管部门、县级公安部门依据法定职权责令停业整顿3个月至6个月；2年内被2次责令停业整顿又有违反本条例的行为应受行政处罚的，由原发证机关吊销娱乐经营许可证。

**Article 53** Where anyone is banned according to law due to unauthorized engagement in business activities of an entertainment place, the investors and responsible persons shall be prohibited for life from investing in or establishing an entertainment place or serving as the legal representative or responsible person of an entertainment place.  
Where an entertainment place has its entertainment business license revoked or withdrawn due to violation of these Regulations, its legal representative and responsible person shall not serve as the legal representative or responsible person of an entertainment place within five years from the date of revocation or withdrawal.  
Where an entertainment place has been subjected to three warnings or fines within two years due to violation of these Regulations and commits another violation subject to administrative penalty, the competent cultural department of the people's government at the county level or the public security department at the county level shall, within their statutory functions and powers, order a suspension of business for rectification for a period of three to six months; where it has been ordered to suspend business for rectification twice within two years and commits another violation subject to administrative penalty, the original issuing authority shall revoke the entertainment business license.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　娱乐场所违反有关治安管理或者消防管理法律、行政法规规定的，由公安部门依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
娱乐场所违反有关卫生、环境保护、价格、劳动等法律、行政法规规定的，由有关部门依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
娱乐场所及其从业人员与消费者发生争议的，应当依照消费者权益保护的法律规定解决；造成消费者人身、财产损害的，由娱乐场所依法予以赔偿。

**Article 54** Where an entertainment place violates the provisions of any law or administrative regulation on public security administration or fire control administration, the public security department shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where an entertainment place violates the provisions of any law or administrative regulation on health, environmental protection, pricing, labor, etc., the relevant department shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a dispute arises between an entertainment place or its employees and a consumer, it shall be resolved in accordance with the legal provisions on the protection of consumer rights and interests; if damage is caused to the person or property of the consumer, the entertainment place shall make compensation according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家机关及其工作人员开办娱乐场所，参与或者变相参与娱乐场所经营活动的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予撤职或者开除的行政处分。  
文化主管部门、公安部门的工作人员明知其亲属开办娱乐场所或者发现其亲属参与、变相参与娱乐场所的经营活动，不予制止或者制止不力的，依法给予行政处分；情节严重的，依法给予撤职或者开除的行政处分。

**Article 55** Where any state organ or its staff member establishes an entertainment place, participates or participates in a disguised form in the business activities of an entertainment place, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given an administrative sanction of removal from office or expulsion according to law.  
Where a staff member of the competent cultural department or the public security department, knowing that a relative establishes an entertainment place or discovering that a relative participates or participates in a disguised form in the business activities of an entertainment place, fails to stop it or stops it ineffectively, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law; if the circumstances are serious, an administrative sanction of removal from office or expulsion shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　文化主管部门、公安部门、工商行政管理部门和其他有关部门的工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)向不符合法定设立条件的单位颁发许可证、批准文件、营业执照的；  
(二)不履行监督管理职责，或者发现擅自从事娱乐场所经营活动不依法取缔，或者发现违法行为不依法查处的；  
(三)接到对违法行为的举报、通报后不依法查处的；  
(四)利用职务之便，索取、收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
(五)利用职务之便，参与、包庇违法行为，或者向有关单位、个人通风报信的；  
(六)有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 56** Where a staff member of the competent cultural department, the public security department, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or any other relevant department commits any of the following acts, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing a license, approval document, or business license to a unit that does not meet the statutory establishment conditions;  
(2) failing to perform supervision and management duties, or failing to ban according to law any unauthorized engagement in business activities of an entertainment place when discovered, or failing to investigate and deal with according to law any illegal act when discovered;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with according to law any report or notification of an illegal act after receiving it;  
(4) using the position to extort or accept property from others or seek other benefits;  
(5) using the position to participate in or cover up an illegal act, or tip off relevant units or individuals;  
(6) committing any other act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本条例所称从业人员，包括娱乐场所的管理人员、服务人员、保安人员和在娱乐场所工作的其他人员。

**Article 57** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term 'employees' includes the managerial personnel, service personnel, security personnel, and other persons working in an entertainment place.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2006年3月1日起施行。1999年3月26日国务院发布的《娱乐场所管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2006. The Regulations on the Administration of Entertainment Places promulgated by the State Council on March 26, 1999 shall be abolished simultaneously.
