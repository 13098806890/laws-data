# 民族乡行政工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1993-09-15  
**生效日期**：1993-09-15

> (1993年8月29日国务院批准　1993年9月15日国家民族事务委员会令第1号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了促进民族乡经济、文化等项事业的发展，保障少数民族的合法权益，增强民族团结，根据宪法和法律的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　民族乡是在少数民族聚居的地方建立的乡级行政区域。 少数民族人口占全乡总人口30%以上的乡，可以按照规定申请设立民族乡；特殊情况的，可以略低于这个比例。 第三条　民族乡的建立，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定。 民族乡的名称，除特殊情况外，按照以地方名称加民族名称确定。 第四条　民族乡人民政府配备工作人员，应当尽量配备建乡的民族和其他少数民族人员。 第五条　民族乡人民政府在执行职务的时候，使用当地通用的语言文字。 第六条　民族乡人民政府依照法律、法规和国家有关规定，结合本乡的具体情况和民族特点，因地制宜地发展经济、教育、科技、文化、卫生等项事业。 第七条　民族乡人民政府在本行政区域各族人民中进行爱国主义、社会主义和民族政策、民族团结的教育，不断巩固和发展平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系。 第八条　民族乡财政由各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照优待民族乡的原则确定。 民族乡的上一级人民政府在编制财政预算时，应当给民族乡安排一定的机动财力，乡财政收入的超收部分和财政支出的节余部分，应当全部留给民族乡周转使用。 第九条　信贷部门应当根据法律、法规和国家其他有关规定，对经济发展水平较低的民族乡用于生产建设、资源开发和少数民族用品生产方面的贷款给予照顾。 第十条　县级以上地方各级人民政府依照税收法律、法规的规定及税收管理权限，可以采取减税、免税措施，扶持民族乡经济的发展。 第十一条　县级以上地方各级人民政府在分配支援经济不发达地区专项资金及其他固定或者临时专项资金时，对经济发展水平较低的民族乡给予照顾。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进民族乡经济、文化等项事业的发展，保障少数民族的合法权益，增强民族团结，根据宪法和法律的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Constitution and laws, for the purpose of promoting the development of the economy, culture and other undertakings of nationality townships, safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of minority nationalities, and strengthening national unity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　民族乡是在少数民族聚居的地方建立的乡级行政区域。  
少数民族人口占全乡总人口30%以上的乡，可以按照规定申请设立民族乡；特殊情况的，可以略低于这个比例。

**Article 2** A nationality township is a township-level administrative area established in a locality where minority nationalities live in concentrated communities.  
A township where the minority population accounts for more than 30% of the total population of the whole township may apply for the establishment of a nationality township in accordance with the provisions; under special circumstances, the proportion may be slightly lower.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民族乡的建立，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定。  
民族乡的名称，除特殊情况外，按照以地方名称加民族名称确定。

**Article 3** The establishment of a nationality township shall be decided by the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The name of a nationality township shall, except under special circumstances, be determined by adding the name of the locality to the name of the nationality.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民族乡人民政府配备工作人员，应当尽量配备建乡的民族和其他少数民族人员。

**Article 4** When staffing the people's government of a nationality township, personnel of the nationality that established the township and other minority nationalities shall, to the extent possible, be appointed.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民族乡人民政府在执行职务的时候，使用当地通用的语言文字。

**Article 5** When performing their duties, the people's government of a nationality township shall use the spoken and written languages commonly used in the locality.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民族乡人民政府依照法律、法规和国家有关规定，结合本乡的具体情况和民族特点，因地制宜地发展经济、教育、科技、文化、卫生等项事业。

**Article 6** The people's government of a nationality township shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and the relevant provisions of the State, and in light of the specific conditions of the township and the characteristics of the nationalities, develop the undertakings of economy, education, science and technology, culture, public health, etc., in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　民族乡人民政府在本行政区域各族人民中进行爱国主义、社会主义和民族政策、民族团结的教育，不断巩固和发展平等、团结、互助的社会主义民族关系。

**Article 7** The people's government of a nationality township shall conduct education among the people of all nationalities within its administrative area in patriotism, socialism, national policies and national unity, and constantly consolidate and develop socialist national relations of equality, unity and mutual assistance.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　民族乡财政由各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府按照优待民族乡的原则确定。  
民族乡的上一级人民政府在编制财政预算时，应当给民族乡安排一定的机动财力，乡财政收入的超收部分和财政支出的节余部分，应当全部留给民族乡周转使用。

**Article 8** The finance of a nationality township shall be determined by the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the principle of giving preferential treatment to nationality townships.  
When compiling the fiscal budget, the people's government at the next higher level of the nationality township shall allocate a certain amount of flexible financial resources to the nationality township; the over-collected portion of the township's fiscal revenue and the surplus portion of its fiscal expenditure shall be wholly retained by the nationality township for its revolving use.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　信贷部门应当根据法律、法规和国家其他有关规定，对经济发展水平较低的民族乡用于生产建设、资源开发和少数民族用品生产方面的贷款给予照顾。

**Article 9** Credit departments shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and other relevant provisions of the State, give preferential treatment to loans used by nationality townships with a relatively low level of economic development for production and construction, resource development, and the production of articles for minority nationalities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方各级人民政府依照税收法律、法规的规定及税收管理权限，可以采取减税、免税措施，扶持民族乡经济的发展。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at or above the county level may, in accordance with the provisions of tax laws and regulations and within the scope of their tax administration authority, adopt measures of tax reduction or tax exemption to support the economic development of nationality townships.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方各级人民政府在分配支援经济不发达地区专项资金及其他固定或者临时专项资金时，对经济发展水平较低的民族乡给予照顾。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府在分配扶贫专项物资时，应当照顾贫困民族乡的需要。

**Article 11** When allocating special funds for supporting economically underdeveloped areas and other fixed or temporary special funds, local people's governments at or above the county level shall give preferential treatment to nationality townships with a relatively low level of economic development.  
When allocating special materials for poverty alleviation, local people's governments at or above the county level shall give consideration to the needs of poverty-stricken nationality townships.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民族乡依照法律、法规和国家其他有关规定，管理和保护本乡的自然资源，并对可以由本乡开发的自然资源优先合理开发利用。  
在民族乡依法开发资源、兴办企业，应当照顾民族乡的利益和当地人民群众的生产、生活，在配套加工产品的生产和招收当地少数民族人员方面做出合理安排。

**Article 12** A nationality township shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and other relevant provisions of the State, manage and protect the natural resources within its territory, and give priority to the reasonable development and utilization of the natural resources that may be developed by the township.  
Where resources are developed and enterprises are established in a nationality township according to law, the interests of the nationality township and the production and living of the local people shall be taken into consideration, and reasonable arrangements shall be made in the production of related processed products and the recruitment of local minority personnel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当帮助民族乡加强农业、林业、牧业、副业、渔业和水利、电力等基础设施的建设，扶持民族乡发展交通事业。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall help nationality townships to strengthen the construction of infrastructure such as agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, sideline occupations, fishery, water conservancy and electric power, and support nationality townships in developing transportation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当在师资、经费、教学设施等方面采取优惠政策，帮助民族乡发展教育事业，提高教育质量。  
民族乡根据实际情况，可以兴办小学、中学和初级职业学校；牧区、山区以及经济困难的民族乡，在上级人民政府的帮助和指导下，可以设立以寄宿制和助学金为主的学校。  
民族乡的中小学可以使用当地少数民族通用的语言文字教学，同时推广全国通用的普通话。使用民族语言文字教学的中小学，其教育行政经费、教职工编制可以高于普通学校。  
民族乡在上级人民政府的帮助和指导下，积极开展扫盲工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府可以根据当地实际情况，在有关大中专院校和中学中设立民族班，尽可能使民族乡有一定数量的学生入学。

**Article 14** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall adopt preferential policies in respect of teachers, funds, teaching facilities, etc., to help nationality townships develop education and improve the quality of education.  
A nationality township may, in light of its actual conditions, set up primary schools, middle schools and elementary vocational schools; nationality townships in pastoral areas, mountainous areas and those with financial difficulties may, with the help and guidance of the people's government at a higher level, establish schools mainly based on boarding and grant-in-aid systems.  
Primary and middle schools in a nationality township may use the spoken and written languages commonly used by the local minority nationalities for instruction, while popularizing the common speech of the whole country (Putonghua). For primary and middle schools where minority languages are used for instruction, the educational administrative funds and the staffing of teaching and administrative personnel may be higher than those of ordinary schools.  
A nationality township shall, with the help and guidance of the people's government at a higher level, actively carry out the work of eliminating illiteracy.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may, in light of the local actual conditions, set up nationality classes in relevant colleges, secondary specialized schools and middle schools, so as to enable as far as possible a certain number of students from nationality townships to be enrolled.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当帮助民族乡开展科学技术知识的普及工作，组织和促进科学技术的交流和协作。

**Article 15** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall help nationality townships to carry out the popularization of scientific and technological knowledge, and organize and promote the exchange and cooperation of science and technology.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当积极帮助民族乡创办广播站、文化馆(站)等文化设施，丰富各族人民的文化生活，保护和继承具有民族特点的优秀文化遗产。

**Article 16** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall actively help nationality townships to establish broadcasting stations, cultural centers (stations) and other cultural facilities, enrich the cultural life of the people of all nationalities, and protect and inherit the fine cultural heritage with national characteristics.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当积极帮助民族乡发展医药卫生事业，扶持民族乡办好卫生院(所)，培养和使用少数民族医疗保健人员，加强对地方病、多发病、常见病的防治，积极开展妇幼保健工作。

**Article 17** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall actively help nationality townships to develop medical and health undertakings, support nationality townships in running health centers (stations) well, train and employ medical and health personnel of minority nationalities, strengthen the prevention and treatment of endemic diseases, frequent diseases and common diseases, and actively carry out maternal and child health care.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民族乡应当积极做好计划生育工作，搞好优生优育优教，提高人口素质。

**Article 18** A nationality township shall actively do a good job in family planning, promote sound prenatal care, sound child-rearing and sound education, and improve the quality of the population.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民族乡应当在上级人民政府的帮助和指导下，采取各种措施，加强对少数民族干部的培养和使用。

**Article 19** A nationality township shall, with the help and guidance of the people's government at a higher level, take various measures to strengthen the training and employment of cadres of minority nationalities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民族乡应当采取多种形式和提供优惠待遇，引进人才参加本乡的社会主义建设事业。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当采取调派、聘任、轮换等办法，组织教师、医生、科技人员等到民族乡工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府对长期在边远地区的民族乡工作的教师、医生和科技人员，应当给予优惠待遇。

**Article 20** A nationality township shall adopt various forms and offer preferential treatment to attract talented persons to participate in the socialist construction of the township.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, by means of transfer, appointment, rotation, etc., organize teachers, doctors, scientific and technical personnel, etc. to work in nationality townships.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall grant preferential treatment to teachers, doctors and scientific and technical personnel who have worked for a long time in nationality townships in outlying areas.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　少数民族聚居镇的行政工作，可以参照本条例执行。

**Article 21** The administrative work of towns where minority nationalities live in concentrated communities may be carried out with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　辖有民族乡的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 22** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government that have nationality townships under their jurisdiction may formulate measures for implementation in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例由国家民族事务委员会负责解释。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Nationalities Affairs Commission.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
