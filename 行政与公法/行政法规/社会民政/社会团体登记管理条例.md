# 社会团体登记管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2026-03-12  
**生效日期**：2026-03-12

> （1998年10月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第250号发布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2026年3月12日《国务院关于修改〈社会团体登记管理条例〉的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障公民的结社自由，维护社会团体的合法权益，加强对社会团体的登记管理，促进社会主义物质文明、精神文明建设，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称社会团体，是指中国公民自愿组成，为实现会员共同意愿，按照其章程开展活动的非营利性社会组织。 国家机关以外的组织可以作为单位会员加入社会团体。 第三条　成立社会团体，应当经其业务主管单位审查同意，并依照本条例的规定进行登记。 社会团体应当具备法人条件。 下列团体不属于本条例规定登记的范围： （一）参加中国人民政治协商会议的人民团体； （二）由国务院机构编制管理机关核定，并经国务院批准免于登记的团体； （三）机关、团体、企业事业单位内部经本单位批准成立、在本单位内部活动的团体。 第四条　社会团体必须遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策，不得反对宪法确定的基本原则，不得危害国家的统一、安全和民族的团结，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利益以及其他组织和公民的合法权益，不得违背社会道德风尚。 社会团体不得从事营利性经营活动。 第五条　国家保护社会团体依照法律、法规及其章程开展活动，任何组织和个人不得非法干涉。 第六条　国务院民政部门和县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门是本级人民政府的社会团体登记管理机关（以下简称登记管理机关）。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公民的结社自由，维护社会团体的合法权益，加强对社会团体的登记管理，促进社会主义物质文明、精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to safeguard citizens' freedom of association, protect the lawful rights and interests of social organizations, strengthen the registration and administration of social organizations, and promote the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称社会团体，是指中国公民自愿组成，为实现会员共同意愿，按照其章程开展活动的非营利性社会组织。  
国家机关以外的组织可以作为单位会员加入社会团体。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "social organizations" refer to non-profit social organizations voluntarily formed by Chinese citizens to achieve the common will of their members and conduct activities in accordance with their articles of association.  
Organizations other than state organs may join a social organization as institutional members.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　成立社会团体，应当经其业务主管单位审查同意，并依照本条例的规定进行登记。  
社会团体应当具备法人条件。  
下列团体不属于本条例规定登记的范围：  
（一）参加中国人民政治协商会议的人民团体；  
（二）由国务院机构编制管理机关核定，并经国务院批准免于登记的团体；  
（三）机关、团体、企业事业单位内部经本单位批准成立、在本单位内部活动的团体。

**Article 3** The establishment of a social organization shall be subject to the examination and consent of its business supervisory unit, and shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
A social organization shall meet the requirements for legal person status.  
The following groups are not within the scope of registration as prescribed by these Regulations:  
(1) people's organizations participating in the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference;  
(2) groups approved by the State Council's establishment management authority and exempted from registration with the approval of the State Council;  
(3) groups established with the approval of the unit itself and operating within that unit, such as government organs, groups, enterprises, and institutions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　社会团体必须遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策，不得反对宪法确定的基本原则，不得危害国家的统一、安全和民族的团结，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利益以及其他组织和公民的合法权益，不得违背社会道德风尚。  
社会团体不得从事营利性经营活动。

**Article 4** A social organization must abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, and state policies; shall not oppose the basic principles established by the Constitution; shall not endanger the unity, security, and ethnic solidarity of the state; shall not harm state interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of other organizations and citizens; and shall not violate social morals and customs.  
A social organization shall not engage in for-profit business activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家保护社会团体依照法律、法规及其章程开展活动，任何组织和个人不得非法干涉。

**Article 5** The state protects social organizations in carrying out activities in accordance with laws, regulations, and their articles of association, and no organization or individual may illegally interfere.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院民政部门和县级以上地方各级人民政府民政部门是本级人民政府的社会团体登记管理机关（以下简称登记管理机关）。  
国务院有关部门和县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门、国务院或者县级以上地方各级人民政府授权的组织，是有关行业、学科或者业务范围内社会团体的业务主管单位（以下简称业务主管单位）。  
法律、行政法规对社会团体的监督管理另有规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 6** The civil affairs department of the State Council and the civil affairs departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be the registration administration authorities for social organizations of the people's governments at the corresponding levels (hereinafter referred to as the registration administration authorities).  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, as well as organizations authorized by the State Council or by local people's governments at or above the county level, shall be the business supervisory units for social organizations within the relevant industries, disciplines, or business scopes (hereinafter referred to as the business supervisory units).  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the supervision and administration of social organizations, such laws or administrative regulations shall prevail.

### 第二章　管辖

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全国性的社会团体，由国务院的登记管理机关负责登记管理；地方性的社会团体，由所在地人民政府的登记管理机关负责登记管理；跨行政区域的社会团体，由所跨行政区域的共同上一级人民政府的登记管理机关负责登记管理。

**Article 7** National social organizations shall be registered and administered by the registration administration authority of the State Council; local social organizations shall be registered and administered by the registration administration authority of the people's government of the place where they are located; social organizations spanning multiple administrative regions shall be registered and administered by the registration administration authority of the common superior people's government of the regions spanned.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　登记管理机关、业务主管单位与其管辖的社会团体的住所不在一地的，可以委托社会团体住所地的登记管理机关、业务主管单位负责委托范围内的监督管理工作。

**Article 8** Where the registration administration authority or the business supervisory unit is not located in the same place as the domicile of the social organization under its jurisdiction, it may entrust the registration administration authority or business supervisory unit at the place of the social organization's domicile to be responsible for the supervisory and administrative work within the scope of the entrustment.

### 第三章　成立登记

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请成立社会团体，应当经其业务主管单位审查同意，由发起人向登记管理机关申请登记。  
筹备期间不得开展筹备以外的活动。

**Article 9** To apply for the establishment of a social organization, the matter shall be subject to the examination and consent of its business supervisory unit, and the sponsors shall apply to the registration administration authority for registration.  
During the preparatory period, no activities other than preparatory activities may be carried out.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　成立社会团体，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有50个以上的个人会员或者30个以上的单位会员；个人会员、单位会员混合组成的，会员总数不得少于50个；  
（二）有规范的名称和相应的组织机构；  
（三）有固定的住所；  
（四）有与其业务活动相适应的专职工作人员；  
（五）有合法的资产和经费来源，全国性的社会团体有10万元以上活动资金，地方性的社会团体和跨行政区域的社会团体有3万元以上活动资金；  
（六）有独立承担民事责任的能力。  
社会团体的名称应当符合法律、法规的规定，不得违背社会道德风尚。社会团体的名称应当与其业务范围、成员分布、活动地域相一致，准确反映其特征。全国性的社会团体的名称冠以“中国”、“全国”、“中华”等字样的，应当按照国家有关规定经过批准，地方性的社会团体的名称不得冠以“中国”、“全国”、“中华”等字样。

**Article 10** To establish a social organization, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having more than 50 individual members or more than 30 institutional members; where individual members and institutional members are mixed, the total number of members shall not be less than 50;  
(2) having a standardized name and corresponding organizational structure;  
(3) having a fixed domicile;  
(4) having full-time staff suitable for its business activities;  
(5) having lawful assets and sources of funding; national social organizations shall have operating funds of not less than 100,000 yuan; local social organizations and social organizations spanning administrative regions shall have operating funds of not less than 30,000 yuan;  
(6) having the capacity to independently bear civil liability.  
The name of a social organization shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations and shall not violate social morals and customs. The name of a social organization shall be consistent with its business scope, member distribution, and area of activity, and accurately reflect its characteristics. Where the name of a national social organization is prefixed with words such as "China", "National", or "Zhonghua", it shall be approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state; the name of a local social organization shall not be prefixed with such words.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请登记社会团体，发起人应当向登记管理机关提交下列文件：  
（一）登记申请书；  
（二）业务主管单位的批准文件；  
（三）验资报告、场所使用权证明；  
（四）发起人和拟任负责人的基本情况、身份证明；  
（五）章程草案。

**Article 11** To apply for registration of a social organization, the sponsors shall submit the following documents to the registration administration authority:  
(1) a written application for registration;  
(2) the document of approval from the business supervisory unit;  
(3) a capital verification report and proof of the right to use the premises;  
(4) basic information and identity certificates of the sponsors and proposed responsible persons;  
(5) a draft of the articles of association.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　登记管理机关应当自收到[本条例第十一条](#art-11)所列全部有效文件之日起60日内，作出准予或者不予登记的决定。准予登记的，发给《社会团体法人登记证书》；不予登记的，应当向发起人说明理由。  
社会团体登记事项包括：名称、住所、宗旨、业务范围、活动地域、法定代表人、活动资金和业务主管单位。  
社会团体的法定代表人，不得同时担任其他社会团体的法定代表人。

**Article 12** The registration administration authority shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving all valid documents listed in Article 11 of these Regulations, make a decision to approve or disapprove the registration. If registration is approved, it shall issue the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate"; if registration is disapproved, it shall state the reasons to the sponsors.  
The registration items of a social organization include: name, domicile, purpose, business scope, area of activity, legal representative, operating funds, and business supervisory unit.  
The legal representative of a social organization shall not concurrently serve as the legal representative of another social organization.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，登记管理机关不予登记：  
（一）有根据证明申请登记的社会团体的宗旨、业务范围不符合[本条例第四条](#art-4)的规定的；  
（二）在同一行政区域内已有业务范围相同或者相似的社会团体，没有必要成立的；  
（三）发起人、拟任负责人正在或者曾经受到剥夺政治权利的刑事处罚，或者不具有完全民事行为能力的；  
（四）在申请登记时弄虚作假的；  
（五）有法律、行政法规禁止的其他情形的。

**Article 13** The registration administration authority shall disapprove registration under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) there is evidence proving that the purpose or business scope of the social organization applying for registration does not comply with the provisions of Article 4 of these Regulations;  
(2) within the same administrative region, a social organization with the same or similar business scope already exists, making the establishment unnecessary;  
(3) the sponsors or proposed responsible persons are currently or have previously been subjected to criminal punishment of deprivation of political rights, or do not have full capacity for civil conduct;  
(4) fraud is committed in the registration application;  
(5) any other circumstances prohibited by laws or administrative regulations exist.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　社会团体的章程应当包括下列事项：  
（一）名称、住所；  
（二）宗旨、业务范围和活动地域；  
（三）会员资格及其权利、义务；  
（四）民主的组织管理制度，执行机构的产生程序；  
（五）负责人的条件和产生、罢免的程序；  
（六）资产管理和使用的原则；  
（七）章程的修改程序；  
（八）终止程序和终止后资产的处理；  
（九）应当由章程规定的其他事项。

**Article 14** The articles of association of a social organization shall include the following matters:  
(1) name and domicile;  
(2) purpose, business scope, and area of activity;  
(3) membership qualifications and the rights and obligations of members;  
(4) democratic organizational management system and the procedures for the formation of executive bodies;  
(5) conditions for responsible persons and procedures for their selection and removal;  
(6) principles for the management and use of assets;  
(7) procedures for amending the articles of association;  
(8) termination procedures and the disposition of assets after termination;  
(9) other matters that shall be stipulated in the articles of association.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依照法律规定，自批准成立之日起即具有法人资格的社会团体，应当自批准成立之日起60日内向登记管理机关提交批准文件，申领《社会团体法人登记证书》。登记管理机关自收到文件之日起30日内发给《社会团体法人登记证书》。

**Article 15** A social organization that, according to law, acquires legal person status from the date of approval of its establishment shall, within 60 days from the date of approval of its establishment, submit the approval document to the registration administration authority and apply for the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate". The registration administration authority shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the document, issue the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate".

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　社会团体凭《社会团体法人登记证书》申请刻制印章，开立银行账户。社会团体应当将印章式样和银行账号报登记管理机关备案。

**Article 16** A social organization shall, on the strength of its "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate", apply to have seals made and open bank accounts. The social organization shall report the seal impressions and bank account numbers to the registration administration authority for recordation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　社会团体的分支机构、代表机构是社会团体的组成部分，不具有法人资格，应当按照其所属于的社会团体的章程所规定的宗旨和业务范围，在该社会团体授权的范围内开展活动、发展会员。社会团体的分支机构不得再设立分支机构。  
社会团体不得设立地域性的分支机构。

**Article 17** The branch offices and representative offices of a social organization are constituent parts of the social organization and do not have legal person status. They shall carry out activities and develop members within the scope authorized by the social organization, in accordance with the purpose and business scope stipulated in the articles of association of the social organization to which they belong. A branch office of a social organization shall not establish any further branch offices.  
A social organization shall not establish regional branch offices.

### 第四章　变更登记、注销登记

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　社会团体的登记事项需要变更的，应当自业务主管单位审查同意之日起30日内，向登记管理机关申请变更登记。  
社会团体修改章程，应当自业务主管单位审查同意之日起30日内，报登记管理机关核准。

**Article 18** Where the registration items of a social organization need to be altered, it shall, within 30 days from the date of the business supervisory unit's examination and consent, apply to the registration administration authority for alteration registration.  
Where a social organization amends its articles of association, it shall, within 30 days from the date of the business supervisory unit's examination and consent, submit the amendment to the registration administration authority for approval.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　社会团体有下列情形之一的，应当在业务主管单位审查同意后，向登记管理机关申请注销登记：  
（一）完成社会团体章程规定的宗旨的；  
（二）自行解散的；  
（三）分立、合并的；  
（四）由于其他原因终止的。  
有关机关会同登记管理机关可以根据需要明确行业协会商会的行业管理部门。业务主管单位、行业管理部门及有关机关对存在会员或者机构规模过小、业务领域划分过细、功能作用较弱、业务交叉重叠等情形的行业协会商会，可以要求合并；对存在任务已完成、行业调整需要、运转严重失灵、严重扰乱秩序等情形的行业协会商会，可以要求终止。

**Article 19** A social organization shall, after obtaining the examination and consent of its business supervisory unit, apply to the registration administration authority for cancellation of registration under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the purpose stipulated in the articles of association of the social organization has been achieved;  
(2) it dissolves voluntarily;  
(3) it is divided or merged;  
(4) it terminates for any other reason.  
The relevant authorities, together with the registration administration authority, may, as necessary, designate the industry regulatory department for industry associations and chambers of commerce. The business supervisory unit, the industry regulatory department, and the relevant authorities may require the merger of an industry association or chamber of commerce that has too few members or is too small in scale, has an excessively subdivided business field, weak functions, or overlapping business; and may require the termination of an industry association or chamber of commerce whose tasks have been completed, is needed due to industry adjustment, is severely dysfunctional, or severely disrupts order.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　社会团体在办理注销登记前，应当在业务主管单位或者登记管理机关的指导下，成立清算组织，完成债务处理等清算工作，行业管理部门予以配合。  
社会团体未及时履行清算义务的，业务主管单位、行业管理部门、登记管理机关或者利害关系人可以依法申请人民法院指定有关人员组成清算组进行清算。  
清算期间，社会团体不得从事与清算无关的活动。

**Article 20** Before completing the cancellation of registration, a social organization shall, under the guidance of its business supervisory unit or the registration administration authority, establish a liquidation organization to complete the liquidation work such as the settlement of debts, and the industry regulatory department shall cooperate.  
Where a social organization fails to perform its liquidation obligations in a timely manner, the business supervisory unit, the industry regulatory department, the registration administration authority, or an interested party may, in accordance with law, apply to the People's Court to appoint relevant persons to form a liquidation group to conduct the liquidation.  
During the liquidation period, the social organization shall not engage in any activities unrelated to liquidation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　社会团体应当自清算结束之日起15日内向登记管理机关办理注销登记。办理注销登记，应当提交法定代表人签署的注销登记申请书、业务主管单位的审查文件和清算报告书。  
登记管理机关准予注销登记的，发给注销证明文件，收缴该社会团体的登记证书、印章和财务凭证。  
行业协会商会存在[本条例第十九条](#art-19)第二款规定情形的，由业务主管单位、行业管理部门或者有关机关制定合并或者终止方案，经登记管理机关会同有关机关审核后，有关行业协会商会应当依照本条例的规定办理变更登记、注销登记；有关行业协会商会难以自主办理的，由业务主管单位、行业管理部门及有关机关向登记管理机关提出变更、注销相关行业协会商会，登记管理机关予以办理。

**Article 21** A social organization shall, within 15 days from the date of conclusion of liquidation, go through the formalities for cancellation of registration with the registration administration authority. To go through the cancellation of registration, it shall submit the written application for cancellation of registration signed by the legal representative, the examination document from the business supervisory unit, and the liquidation report.  
Where the registration administration authority approves the cancellation of registration, it shall issue a certificate of cancellation and confiscate the registration certificate, seals, and financial vouchers of the social organization.  
Where an industry association or chamber of commerce falls under the circumstances specified in the second paragraph of Article 19 of these Regulations, the business supervisory unit, the industry regulatory department, or the relevant authority shall formulate a plan for merger or termination. After the registration administration authority, together with the relevant authority, examines and approves the plan, the relevant industry association or chamber of commerce shall go through the formalities for alteration or cancellation of registration in accordance with these Regulations; if the relevant industry association or chamber of commerce is unable to handle the procedures on its own, the business supervisory unit, the industry regulatory department, and the relevant authority shall propose to the registration administration authority to alter or cancel the relevant industry association or chamber of commerce, and the registration administration authority shall process it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　社会团体注销后剩余财产的处理，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 22** The disposition of the remaining property of a social organization after its cancellation shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　社会团体成立、注销或者变更名称、住所、法定代表人，由登记管理机关予以公告。

**Article 23** The establishment, cancellation, or change of name, domicile, or legal representative of a social organization shall be publicly announced by the registration administration authority.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　登记管理机关履行下列监督管理职责：  
（一）负责社会团体的成立、变更、注销的登记；  
（二）对社会团体实施年度检查；  
（三）对社会团体违反本条例的问题进行监督检查，对社会团体违反本条例的行为给予行政处罚。

**Article 24** The registration administration authority shall perform the following supervisory and administrative duties:  
(1) be responsible for the registration of the establishment, alteration, and cancellation of social organizations;  
(2) conduct annual inspections of social organizations;  
(3) supervise and inspect social organizations for violations of these Regulations, and impose administrative penalties for acts violating these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　业务主管单位履行下列监督管理职责：  
（一）负责社会团体成立登记、变更登记、注销登记前的审查；  
（二）监督、指导社会团体遵守宪法、法律、法规和国家政策，依据其章程开展活动；  
（三）负责社会团体年度检查的初审；  
（四）协助登记管理机关和其他有关部门查处社会团体的违法行为；  
（五）会同有关机关指导社会团体的清算事宜。  
业务主管单位履行前款规定的职责，不得向社会团体收取费用。

**Article 25** The business supervisory unit shall perform the following supervisory and administrative duties:  
(1) be responsible for the pre-registration examination of the establishment, alteration, and cancellation of social organizations;  
(2) supervise and guide social organizations to abide by the Constitution, laws, regulations, and state policies, and to carry out activities in accordance with their articles of association;  
(3) be responsible for the preliminary examination of the annual inspection of social organizations;  
(4) assist the registration administration authority and other relevant departments in investigating and dealing with illegal acts of social organizations;  
(5) together with the relevant authorities, guide the liquidation of social organizations.  
When performing the duties specified in the preceding paragraph, the business supervisory unit shall not charge any fees to social organizations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　社会团体的资产来源必须合法，任何单位和个人不得侵占、私分或者挪用社会团体的资产。  
社会团体的经费，以及开展章程规定的活动按照国家有关规定所取得的合法收入，必须用于章程规定的业务活动，不得在会员中分配。  
社会团体接受捐赠、资助，必须符合章程规定的宗旨和业务范围，必须根据与捐赠人、资助人约定的期限、方式和合法用途使用。社会团体应当向业务主管单位报告接受、使用捐赠、资助的有关情况，并应当将有关情况以适当方式向社会公布。  
社会团体专职工作人员的工资和保险福利待遇，参照国家对事业单位的有关规定执行。

**Article 26** The sources of assets of a social organization must be lawful. No unit or individual may embezzle, privately distribute, or misappropriate the assets of a social organization.  
The funds of a social organization, as well as the lawful income obtained from carrying out activities stipulated in its articles of association in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, must be used for the business activities stipulated in the articles of association and shall not be distributed among its members.  
Where a social organization accepts donations or grants, it must comply with the purpose and business scope stipulated in its articles of association, and must use them in accordance with the term, method, and lawful purpose agreed with the donor or grantor. The social organization shall report to its business supervisory unit on the acceptance and use of donations and grants, and shall publish the relevant information to the public in an appropriate manner.  
The salaries, insurance, and welfare benefits of full-time staff of a social organization shall be handled with reference to the relevant provisions of the state on public institutions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　社会团体必须执行国家规定的财务管理制度，接受财政部门的监督；资产来源属于国家拨款或者社会捐赠、资助的，还应当接受审计机关的监督。  
社会团体在换届或者更换法定代表人之前，登记管理机关、业务主管单位应当组织对其进行财务审计。

**Article 27** A social organization must implement the financial management system prescribed by the state and accept supervision by the financial department; where the source of assets is state appropriations or social donations or grants, it shall also accept supervision by the audit authority.  
Before a social organization undergoes a change of term or replaces its legal representative, the registration administration authority and the business supervisory unit shall organize a financial audit of it.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　社会团体应当于每年3月31日前向业务主管单位报送上一年度的工作报告，经业务主管单位初审同意后，于5月31日前报送登记管理机关，接受年度检查。工作报告的内容包括：本社会团体遵守法律法规和国家政策的情况、依照本条例履行登记手续的情况、按照章程开展活动的情况、人员和机构变动的情况以及财务管理的情况。  
对于依照[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定发给《社会团体法人登记证书》的社会团体，登记管理机关对其应当简化年度检查的内容。

**Article 28** A social organization shall, before March 31 of each year, submit a work report for the previous year to its business supervisory unit. After the preliminary examination and consent by the business supervisory unit, it shall submit the report to the registration administration authority before May 31 of the same year for annual inspection. The content of the work report shall include: the situation of the social organization's compliance with laws, regulations, and state policies; the situation of fulfilling registration procedures in accordance with these Regulations; the situation of carrying out activities in accordance with its articles of association; changes in personnel and organizational structure; and the situation of financial management.  
For social organizations to which the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate" is issued in accordance with Article 15 of these Regulations, the registration administration authority shall simplify the content of the annual inspection.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　社会团体在申请登记时弄虚作假，骗取登记的，或者自取得《社会团体法人登记证书》之日起1年未开展活动的，由登记管理机关予以撤销登记。

**Article 29** Where a social organization practices fraud during the application for registration and obtains registration by deception, or fails to carry out any activities within one year from the date of obtaining the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate", the registration administration authority shall revoke its registration.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　社会团体有下列情形之一的，由登记管理机关给予警告，责令改正，可以限期停止活动，并可以责令撤换直接负责的主管人员；情节严重的，吊销登记证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）涂改、出租、出借《社会团体法人登记证书》，或者出租、出借社会团体印章的；  
（二）超出章程规定的宗旨和业务范围进行活动的；  
（三）拒不接受或者不按照规定接受监督检查的；  
（四）内部管理不力导致运转失灵的；  
（五）不按照规定办理变更登记、注销登记的；  
（六）违反规定设立分支机构、代表机构，或者对分支机构、代表机构疏于管理，造成严重后果的；  
（七）从事营利性的经营活动的；  
（八）侵占、私分、挪用社会团体资产或者所接受的捐赠、资助的；  
（九）违反国家有关规定收取费用、筹集资金或者接受、使用捐赠、资助的。  
前款规定的行为有违法经营额或者违法所得的，予以没收，可以并处违法经营额1倍以上3倍以下或者违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Under any of the following circumstances, the registration administration authority shall give a warning to the social organization, order it to make corrections, may order it to suspend activities for a specified period, and may order it to replace the directly responsible persons in charge; if the circumstances are serious, the registration certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law:  
(1) altering, leasing, or lending the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate", or leasing or lending the seals of the social organization;  
(2) carrying out activities beyond the purpose and business scope stipulated in the articles of association;  
(3) refusing to accept supervision and inspection or failing to accept supervision and inspection in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) poor internal management leading to dysfunction;  
(5) failing to go through the formalities for alteration or cancellation of registration in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) establishing branch offices or representative offices in violation of the provisions, or neglecting the management of branch offices or representative offices, causing serious consequences;  
(7) engaging in for-profit business activities;  
(8) embezzling, privately distributing, or misappropriating the assets of the social organization or the donations or grants it accepts;  
(9) charging fees, raising funds, or accepting or using donations or grants in violation of the relevant provisions of the state.  
If there are illegal business turnover or illegal gains from the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, such turnover or gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal business turnover or not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains may be imposed concurrently.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　社会团体的活动违反其他法律、法规和国家有关规定的，由有关国家机关依法处理；有关国家机关认为依法应当吊销登记证书的，由登记管理机关吊销登记证书。

**Article 31** Where the activities of a social organization violate other laws, regulations, or relevant provisions of the state, the relevant state authority shall handle the matter in accordance with law; if the relevant state authority deems that the registration certificate should be revoked in accordance with law, the registration administration authority shall revoke the registration certificate.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　筹备期间开展筹备以外的活动，或者未经登记，擅自以社会团体名义进行活动，以及被撤销登记的社会团体继续以社会团体名义进行活动的，由登记管理机关予以取缔，没收非法财产；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 32** Where activities other than preparatory activities are carried out during the preparatory period, or activities are conducted without registration under the name of a social organization without authorization, or a social organization whose registration has been revoked continues to conduct activities under the name of a social organization, the registration administration authority shall ban such activities and confiscate the illegal property; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; if a crime is not constituted, a public security penalty shall be imposed in accordance with law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　社会团体被责令限期停止活动的，由登记管理机关封存《社会团体法人登记证书》、印章和财务凭证。  
社会团体被撤销登记、吊销登记证书的，由登记管理机关收缴《社会团体法人登记证书》和印章。

**Article 33** Where a social organization is ordered to suspend activities for a specified period, the registration administration authority shall seal up the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate", seals, and financial vouchers.  
Where a social organization's registration is revoked or its registration certificate is revoked, the registration administration authority shall confiscate the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate" and seals.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　登记管理机关、业务主管单位、行业管理部门及有关机关的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 34** Where staff members of the registration administration authority, the business supervisory unit, the industry regulatory department, or the relevant authorities abuse their powers, engage in malpractices for personal gain, or neglect their duties, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; if a crime is not constituted, sanctions shall be imposed in accordance with law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　《社会团体法人登记证书》的式样由国务院民政部门制定。  
对社会团体进行年度检查不得收取费用。

**Article 35** The format of the "Social Organization Legal Person Registration Certificate" shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council.  
No fees may be charged for conducting annual inspections of social organizations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例施行前已经成立的社会团体，应当自本条例施行之日起1年内依照本条例有关规定申请重新登记。

**Article 36** Social organizations that have already been established prior to the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within one year from the date of coming into force of these Regulations, apply for re-registration in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1989年10月25日国务院发布的《社会团体登记管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Regulations on the Registration and Administration of Social Organizations promulgated by the State Council on October 25, 1989 shall be abolished simultaneously.
