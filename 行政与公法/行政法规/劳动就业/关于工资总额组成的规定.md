### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了统一工资总额的计算范围，保证国家对工资进行统一的统计核算和会计核算，有利于编制、检查计划和进行工资管理以及正确地反映职工的工资收入，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of unifying the calculation scope of total wages, ensuring that the State conducts unified statistical accounting and financial accounting of wages, facilitating the formulation and inspection of plans and wage management, and correctly reflecting the wage income of employees.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　全民所有制和集体所有制企业、事业单位，各种合营单位，各级国家机关、政党机关和社会团体，在计划、统计、会计上有关工资总额范围的计算，均应遵守本规定。

**Article 2** Enterprises and institutions owned by the whole people or collectively owned, various joint venture units, state organs at all levels, party organs, and social organizations shall all comply with these Provisions in the calculation of the scope of total wages in planning, statistics, and accounting.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　工资总额是指各单位在一定时期内直接支付给本单位全部职工的劳动报酬总额。  
工资总额的计算应以直接支付给职工的全部劳动报酬为根据。

**Article 3** Total wages refers to the total amount of labor remuneration directly paid by each unit to all its employees within a certain period.  
The calculation of total wages shall be based on the total labor remuneration directly paid to employees.

### 第二章　工资总额的组成

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　工资总额由下列6个部分组成：  
(一)计时工资；  
(二)计件工资；  
(三)奖金；  
(四)津贴和补贴；  
(五)加班加点工资；  
(六)特殊情况下支付的工资。

**Article 4** Total wages shall consist of the following six components:  
(1) time wages;  
(2) piece wages;  
(3) bonuses;  
(4) allowances and subsidies;  
(5) overtime wages;  
(6) wages paid under special circumstances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　计时工资是指按计时工资标准(包括地区生活费补贴)和工作时间支付给个人的劳动报酬。包括：  
(一)对已做工作按计时工资标准支付的工资；  
(二)实行结构工资制的单位支付给职工的基础工资和职务(岗位)工资；  
(三)新参加工作职工的见习工资(学徒的生活费)；  
(四)运动员体育津贴。

**Article 5** Time wages refers to labor remuneration paid to individuals according to time wage standards (including regional living cost subsidies) and working hours. It includes:  
(1) wages paid for work done according to time wage standards;  
(2) basic wages and post (position) wages paid by units implementing a structural wage system to employees;  
(3) probationary wages for new employees (apprentices' living expenses);  
(4) sports allowances for athletes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　计件工资是指对已做工作按计件单价支付的劳动报酬。包括：  
(一)实行超额累进计件、直接无限计件、限额计件、超定额计件等工资制，按劳动部门或主管部门批准的定额和计件单价支付给个人的工资；  
(二)按工作任务包干方法支付给个人的工资；  
(三)按营业额提成或利润提成办法支付给个人的工资。

**Article 6** Piece wages refers to labor remuneration paid for work done according to piece rates. It includes:  
(1) wages paid to individuals according to quotas and piece rates approved by labor departments or competent authorities under wage systems such as progressive piece rates, direct unlimited piece rates, limited piece rates, and over-quota piece rates;  
(2) wages paid to individuals under the method of lump-sum payment for work tasks;  
(3) wages paid to individuals under the method of revenue sharing or profit sharing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　奖金是指支付给职工的超额劳动报酬和增收节支的劳动报酬。包括：  
(一)生产奖；  
(二)节约奖；  
(三)劳动竞赛奖；  
(四)机关、事业单位的奖励工资；  
(五)其他奖金。

**Article 7** Bonuses refer to labor remuneration paid to employees for exceeding labor quotas and for increasing revenue and saving expenses. They include:  
(1) production bonuses;  
(2) economy bonuses;  
(3) labor competition bonuses;  
(4) award wages for organs and institutions;  
(5) other bonuses.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　津贴和补贴是指为了补偿职工特殊或额外的劳动消耗和因其他特殊原因支付给职工的津贴，以及为了保证职工工资水平不受物价影响支付给职工的物价补贴。  
(一)津贴。包括：补偿职工特殊或额外劳动消耗的津贴，保健性津贴，技术性津贴，年功性津贴及其他津贴。  
(二)物价补贴。包括：为保证职工工资水平不受物价上涨或变动影响而支付的各种补贴。

**Article 8** Allowances and subsidies refer to allowances paid to employees to compensate for special or extra labor consumption and other special reasons, and price subsidies paid to employees to ensure that their wage levels are not affected by prices.  
(1) Allowances. They include allowances compensating for special or extra labor consumption, health allowances, technical allowances, seniority allowances, and other allowances.  
(2) Price subsidies. They include various subsidies paid to ensure that employees' wage levels are not affected by price increases or changes.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　加班加点工资是指按规定支付的加班工资和加点工资。

**Article 9** Overtime wages refers to overtime pay and overtime work pay paid in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　特殊情况下支付的工资。包括：  
(一)根据国家法律、法规和政策规定，因病、工伤、产假、计划生育假、婚丧假、事假、探亲假、定期休假、停工学习、执行国家或社会义务等原因按计时工资标准或计时工资标准的一定比例支付的工资；  
(二)附加工资、保留工资。

**Article 10** Wages paid under special circumstances. They include:  
(1) wages paid in accordance with the provisions of state laws, regulations, and policies due to illness, work-related injury, maternity leave, family planning leave, marriage or funeral leave, personal leave, family visit leave, regular leave, suspension from work for study, performance of state or social obligations, and other reasons, according to time wage standards or a certain percentage of time wage standards;  
(2) supplementary wages and retained wages.

### 第三章　工资总额不包括的项目

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　下列各项不列入工资总额的范围：  
(一)根据国务院发布的有关规定颁发的创造发明奖、自然科学奖、科学技术进步奖和支付的合理化建议和技术改进奖以及支付给运动员、教练员的奖金；  
(二)有关劳动保险和职工福利方面的各项费用；  
(三)有关离休、退休、退职人员待遇的各项支出；  
(四)劳动保护的各项支出；  
(五)稿费、讲课费及其他专门工作报酬；  
(六)出差伙食补助费、误餐补助、调动工作的旅费和安家费；  
(七)对自带工具、牲畜来企业工作职工所支付的工具、牲畜等的补偿费用；  
(八)实行租赁经营单位的承租人的风险性补偿收入；  
(九)对购买本企业股票和债券的职工所支付的股息(包括股金分红)和利息；  
(十)劳动合同制职工解除劳动合同时由企业支付的医疗补助费、生活补助费等；  
(十一)因录用临时工而在工资以外向提供劳动力单位支付的手续费或管理费；  
(十二)支付给家庭工人的加工费和按加工订货办法支付给承包单位的发包费用；  
(十三)支付给参加企业劳动的在校学生的补贴；  
(十四)计划生育独生子女补贴。

**Article 11** The following items shall not be included in the scope of total wages:  
(1) invention awards, natural science awards, scientific and technological progress awards, and payments for rationalization proposals and technical improvement awards awarded or paid in accordance with relevant provisions issued by the State Council, as well as bonuses paid to athletes and coaches;  
(2) various expenses related to labor insurance and employee welfare;  
(3) various expenditures related to the treatment of persons who have retired, resigned, or left their posts;  
(4) various expenditures for labor protection;  
(5) remuneration for manuscripts, lecture fees, and other remuneration for specialized work;  
(6) meal subsidies for business trips, meal allowance for missed meals, travel expenses for job transfers, and settling-in allowances;  
(7) compensation fees paid to employees who bring their own tools or livestock to the enterprise for work;  
(8) risk compensation income of lessees in units under lease management;  
(9) dividends (including share dividends) and interest paid to employees who purchase shares or bonds of the enterprise;  
(10) medical subsidies, living subsidies, etc. paid by the enterprise to employees with labor contracts upon termination of the labor contract;  
(11) handling fees or management fees paid to units providing labor for the recruitment of temporary workers, in addition to wages;  
(12) processing fees paid to homeworkers and contracting fees paid to contractor units under the processing and ordering method;  
(13) subsidies paid to students participating in enterprise labor;  
(14) subsidies for only children under family planning.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　前条所列各项按照国家规定另行统计。

**Article 12** The items listed in the preceding Article shall be separately counted in accordance with state regulations.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国境内的私营单位、华侨及港、澳、台工商业者经营单位和外商经营单位有关工资总额范围的计算，参照本规定执行。

**Article 13** Private units within the territory of the People's Republic of China, units operated by overseas Chinese and industrialists and businessmen from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, and units operated by foreign businessmen shall refer to these Provisions when calculating the scope of total wages.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定由国家统计局负责解释。

**Article 14** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the National Bureau of Statistics.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各地区、各部门可依据本规定制定有关工资总额组成的具体范围的规定。

**Article 15** All regions and departments may, pursuant to these Provisions, formulate provisions on the specific scope of the composition of total wages.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定自发布之日起施行。国务院1955年5月21日批准颁发的《关于工资总额组成的暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisional Regulations on the Composition of Total Wages approved and issued by the State Council on May 21, 1955 shall be abolished simultaneously.
