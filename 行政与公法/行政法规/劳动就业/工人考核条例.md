# 工人考核条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1990-07-11

> (1990年6月23日国务院批准　1990年7月12日劳动部令第1号发布) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了考察工人的思想政治表现和生产工作成绩，鉴定实际技术业务水平，调动工人生产劳动和学习政治、技术业务的积极性，全面提高工人队伍素质，适应我国社会主义现代化建设的需要，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于全民所有制企业、事业单位和国家机关。 第三条　国家实行工人考核制度。对工人的考核应当与使用相结合，并按照国家有关规定确定其工资待遇。 第四条　工人考核工作应从实际出发，统筹安排，严格标准，保证质量。 第二章　考核种类 第五条　工人考核分为录用考核、转正定级考核、上岗转岗考核、本等级考核、升级考核，以及技师、高级技师(以下统称技师)任职资格的考评。 第六条　企业、事业单位和国家机关从社会招收录用新工人，包括录用技工学校、职业学校、职业高中的毕业生，以及就业训练中心和其他各种就业训练班结业的学生，须经工人考核组织的录用考核，方能择优录用。 第七条　学徒(培训生)学习期满和工人见习、试用期满时，须经转正定级考核。经考核合格发给相应的《技术等级证书》或者《岗位合格证书》或者《特种作业人员操作证》之后，方能上生产工作岗位独立操作，并根据其思想政治表现、生产工作成绩和实际技能按照国家有关规定确定工资等级。考核不合格者准予延期补考。补考仍不合格者应当解除劳动合同或者调换其他工作。学徒、见习、试用期各方面表现优秀的，可以提前进行转正定级考核。 第八条　工人改变工种，调换新的岗位，或者操作新的先进设备时，应经过技术业务培训和上岗转岗考核合格后方能上岗。在精密稀有设备上工作和从事特种作业的工人，离开生产工作岗位一年以上，重新回到原岗位，应有一定的熟悉期，期满经技术业务考核合格后方能上岗，并按考核成绩，重新确定技术等级。 第九条　企业、事业单位和国家机关根据生产经营活动或者工作需要，对本单位的工人定期进行本等级的技术业务考核。考核不合格者允许补考。补考仍不合格者，应降低其技术等级或者调换工作岗位，重新确定技术等级和工资待遇。 第十条　工人经本等级考核合格的，可以申请参加升级考核。升级考核一般二至三年进行一次。有特殊贡献者经班组推荐和工人考核组织批准，可以提前参加升级考核或者越级考核。考核合格者发给相应的《技术等级证书》，作为使用和调整工资的依据。 第十一条　优秀的高级技术工人，可以按照国家有关规定申请参加技师任职资格的考评。有突出贡献的技师，可以按照国家有关规定参加高级技师任职资格的考评。考评合格，分别发给相应的《技师合格证书》，作为应聘职务的凭证。 第三章　考核内容

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了考察工人的思想政治表现和生产工作成绩，鉴定实际技术业务水平，调动工人生产劳动和学习政治、技术业务的积极性，全面提高工人队伍素质，适应我国社会主义现代化建设的需要，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of assessing workers' ideological and political performance and production work achievements, evaluating their actual technical and professional level, mobilizing workers' enthusiasm for production labor and learning politics, technology and professional skills, comprehensively improving the quality of the workforce, and meeting the needs of China's socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于全民所有制企业、事业单位和国家机关。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to enterprises owned by the whole people, institutions, and state organs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行工人考核制度。对工人的考核应当与使用相结合，并按照国家有关规定确定其工资待遇。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a worker assessment system. Assessment of workers shall be combined with their employment, and their wage treatment shall be determined in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　工人考核工作应从实际出发，统筹安排，严格标准，保证质量。

**Article 4** The work of worker assessment shall be based on actual conditions, planned as a whole, with strict standards, and ensuring quality.

### 第二章　考核种类

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　工人考核分为录用考核、转正定级考核、上岗转岗考核、本等级考核、升级考核，以及技师、高级技师(以下统称技师)任职资格的考评。

**Article 5** Worker assessment shall be divided into recruitment assessment, probation-to-regular and grade-fixing assessment, post-entry and job-transfer assessment, same-grade assessment, promotion assessment, and assessment for the qualification of technicians and senior technicians (hereinafter collectively referred to as technicians).

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业、事业单位和国家机关从社会招收录用新工人，包括录用技工学校、职业学校、职业高中的毕业生，以及就业训练中心和其他各种就业训练班结业的学生，须经工人考核组织的录用考核，方能择优录用。

**Article 6** When enterprises, institutions, and state organs recruit and employ new workers from society, including graduates of technical schools, vocational schools, and vocational high schools, as well as students who have completed courses at employment training centers and various other employment training classes, they shall undergo recruitment assessment by the worker assessment organization before they may be employed on a selective basis.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　学徒(培训生)学习期满和工人见习、试用期满时，须经转正定级考核。经考核合格发给相应的《技术等级证书》或者《岗位合格证书》或者《特种作业人员操作证》之后，方能上生产工作岗位独立操作，并根据其思想政治表现、生产工作成绩和实际技能按照国家有关规定确定工资等级。考核不合格者准予延期补考。补考仍不合格者应当解除劳动合同或者调换其他工作。学徒、见习、试用期各方面表现优秀的，可以提前进行转正定级考核。

**Article 7** When apprentices (trainees) complete their study period and when workers complete their probation or trial period, they shall undergo probation-to-regular and grade-fixing assessment. After passing the assessment and being issued the corresponding 'Technical Grade Certificate' or 'Post Qualification Certificate' or 'Special Operations Personnel Operation Permit', they may start independent operation on the production work post, and their wage grade shall be determined in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State based on their ideological and political performance, production work achievements, and actual skills. Those who fail the assessment shall be allowed to take a deferred make-up examination. If they still fail the make-up examination, their labor contract shall be dissolved or they shall be transferred to other jobs. Apprentices, probationers, and trial-period workers who have excellent performance in all aspects may undergo the probation-to-regular and grade-fixing assessment ahead of schedule.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工人改变工种，调换新的岗位，或者操作新的先进设备时，应经过技术业务培训和上岗转岗考核合格后方能上岗。在精密稀有设备上工作和从事特种作业的工人，离开生产工作岗位一年以上，重新回到原岗位，应有一定的熟悉期，期满经技术业务考核合格后方能上岗，并按考核成绩，重新确定技术等级。

**Article 8** When workers change their type of work, transfer to a new post, or operate new advanced equipment, they shall undergo technical and professional training and pass the post-entry and job-transfer assessment before they may take up the post. Workers working on precision and rare equipment and engaging in special operations who leave the production work post for more than one year and return to the original post shall have a certain familiarization period; after the period expires, they shall pass the technical and professional assessment before they may take up the post, and their technical grade shall be re-determined according to the assessment results.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业、事业单位和国家机关根据生产经营活动或者工作需要，对本单位的工人定期进行本等级的技术业务考核。考核不合格者允许补考。补考仍不合格者，应降低其技术等级或者调换工作岗位，重新确定技术等级和工资待遇。

**Article 9** Enterprises, institutions, and state organs shall, based on production and business activities or work needs, conduct regular same-grade technical and professional assessments for workers of their units. Those who fail the assessment shall be allowed to take a make-up examination. If they still fail the make-up examination, their technical grade shall be lowered or they shall be transferred to other posts, and their technical grade and wage treatment shall be re-determined.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　工人经本等级考核合格的，可以申请参加升级考核。升级考核一般二至三年进行一次。有特殊贡献者经班组推荐和工人考核组织批准，可以提前参加升级考核或者越级考核。考核合格者发给相应的《技术等级证书》，作为使用和调整工资的依据。

**Article 10** Workers who pass the same-grade assessment may apply to participate in the promotion assessment. The promotion assessment shall generally be conducted once every two to three years. Those with special contributions, upon recommendation by the work team and approval by the worker assessment organization, may participate in the promotion assessment ahead of schedule or skip grades. Those who pass the assessment shall be issued the corresponding 'Technical Grade Certificate', which shall serve as the basis for employment and wage adjustment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　优秀的高级技术工人，可以按照国家有关规定申请参加技师任职资格的考评。有突出贡献的技师，可以按照国家有关规定参加高级技师任职资格的考评。考评合格，分别发给相应的《技师合格证书》，作为应聘职务的凭证。

**Article 11** Outstanding senior technical workers may, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, apply to participate in the assessment for the qualification of technicians. Technicians with outstanding contributions may, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, participate in the assessment for the qualification of senior technicians. Those who pass the assessment shall be issued the corresponding 'Technician Qualification Certificate', which shall serve as the certificate for appointment to a position.

### 第三章　考核内容

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　工人考核的内容包括思想政治表现、生产工作成绩和技术业务水平。

**Article 12** The content of worker assessment shall include ideological and political performance, production work achievements, and technical and professional level.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　工人思想政治表现的考核，主要包括遵守宪法、法律和国家政策以及本单位的规章制度，树立良好的职业道德、劳动态度等方面。

**Article 13** The assessment of workers' ideological and political performance shall mainly include aspects such as abiding by the Constitution, laws, state policies, and the rules and regulations of the unit, and cultivating good professional ethics and work attitude.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　工人生产工作成绩的考核，主要包括完成生产任务的数量和质量，解决生产工作中技术业务问题的成果，传授技术、经验的成绩以及安全生产的情况等方面。

**Article 14** The assessment of workers' production work achievements shall mainly include aspects such as the quantity and quality of completed production tasks, achievements in solving technical and professional problems in production work, achievements in imparting technology and experience, and the situation of safe production.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　工人技术业务水平的考核，主要是按照现行《工人技术等级标准》或者《岗位规范》进行技术业务理论和实际操作技能的考核。  
工人技师任职资格的考评，应当按照国家有关规定进行。

**Article 15** The assessment of workers' technical and professional level shall mainly be conducted in accordance with the current 'Worker Technical Grade Standard' or 'Post Specification' to assess technical and professional theory and practical operation skills.  
The assessment for the qualification of worker technicians shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

### 第四章　考核方法

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　工人思想政治表现的考核，在加强班组日常管理的基础上，定期进行。  
工人生产工作成绩的考核，在加强班组日常管理的基础上，可以采用定量为主、定性为辅的方法，明确评分标准，定期进行。

**Article 16** The assessment of workers' ideological and political performance shall be conducted periodically on the basis of strengthening daily management of the work team.  
The assessment of workers' production work achievements, on the basis of strengthening daily management of the work team, may adopt a method mainly quantitative and supplemented by qualitative, with clear scoring standards, and shall be conducted periodically.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　工人技术业务理论考核以笔试为主，操作技能考核可以结合生产或者作业项目分期分批进行，也可以选择典型工件或作业项目专门组织进行。技术业务水平考核评定采用百分制，六十分为合格。

**Article 17** The technical and professional theory assessment of workers shall mainly be conducted through written examinations. The assessment of operation skills may be conducted in batches in conjunction with production or operation projects, or may be organized separately by selecting typical workpieces or operation projects. The assessment and evaluation of technical and professional level shall adopt a hundred-point system, with sixty points as passing.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　工人思想政治表现、生产工作成绩和技术业务水平三项考核成绩均合格的，即为考核合格。

**Article 18** If a worker passes all three assessment results of ideological and political performance, production work achievements, and technical and professional level, the assessment shall be deemed as passing.

### 第五章　考核组织和管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　全国工人考核工作由劳动部综合管理，并负责制定有关规定，指导协调工人考核工作。

**Article 19** The national worker assessment work shall be comprehensively managed by the Ministry of Labor, which shall be responsible for formulating relevant regulations and guiding and coordinating worker assessment work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各省、自治区、直辖市及计划单列市劳动行政部门和国务院有关部门的劳动工资机构，制定实施办法，分别负责综合管理本地区、本部门的工人考核工作。

**Article 20** The labor administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status, as well as the labor and wage institutions of the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate implementing measures and shall respectively be responsible for comprehensively managing the worker assessment work of their regions and departments.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各省、自治区、直辖市及计划单列市劳动行政部门会同本地区有关行业主管部门，国务院有关部门的劳动工资机构会同本部门的有关业务机构，分别成立工人考核委员会，负责组织本地区、本部门的工人考核工作。

**Article 21** The labor administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status shall, jointly with the relevant industry authorities of their regions, and the labor and wage institutions of the relevant departments of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant business institutions of their departments, establish worker assessment committees, which shall be responsible for organizing the worker assessment work of their regions and departments.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业、事业单位或者企业主管部门应当根据实际情况组成不同专业(工种)的考核组织，负责具体考核工作。各专业工种的考核组织成员中，应当有三分之二以上的专业技术人员、技师、高级技术工人。

**Article 22** Enterprises, institutions, or enterprise competent departments shall, based on actual conditions, establish assessment organizations for different specialties (types of work), which shall be responsible for specific assessment work. Among the members of assessment organizations for various specialties and types of work, there shall be at least two-thirds of professional technical personnel, technicians, and senior technical workers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　《技师合格证书》，地方所属单位由省、自治区、直辖市及计划单列市劳动行政部门核发；国务院各部门所属单位由其主管部门的劳动工资机构核发。  
《技术等级证书》的核发办法，地方所属单位由省、自治区、直辖市及计划单列市劳动行政部门规定；国务院各部门所属单位由其主管部门的劳动工资机构规定。  
企业内部的《岗位合格证书》的核发办法，由企业自行规定，但企业主管部门有统一规定的，应当按照统一规定办理。

**Article 23** The 'Technician Qualification Certificate' shall be issued by the labor administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status for units under local jurisdiction; for units under the departments of the State Council, it shall be issued by the labor and wage institutions of their competent departments.  
The measures for issuing the 'Technical Grade Certificate' shall be prescribed by the labor administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status for units under local jurisdiction; for units under the departments of the State Council, it shall be prescribed by the labor and wage institutions of their competent departments.  
The measures for issuing the internal 'Post Qualification Certificate' of enterprises shall be prescribed by the enterprise itself, but if the competent department of the enterprise has unified provisions, such unified provisions shall be followed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　《技师合格证书》由劳动部统一印制。  
《技术等级证书》由劳动部统一规定式样。  
《岗位合格证书》的式样，企业主管部门有规定的，按规定办；企业主管部门无规定的，由企业自行规定。  
《特种作业人员操作证》的式样、印制和核发办法，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 24** The 'Technician Qualification Certificate' shall be uniformly printed by the Ministry of Labor.  
The 'Technical Grade Certificate' shall have its format uniformly prescribed by the Ministry of Labor.  
The format of the 'Post Qualification Certificate' shall follow the provisions of the competent department of the enterprise if there are such provisions; if the competent department has no provisions, it shall be prescribed by the enterprise itself.  
The format, printing, and issuance measures of the 'Special Operations Personnel Operation Permit' shall be handled in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企业、事业单位和国家机关违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定招收录用新工人的，当地劳动部门不予办理招收录用手续。

**Article 25** If enterprises, institutions, and state organs violate the provisions of Article 6 of these Regulations in recruiting and employing new workers, the local labor department shall not process the recruitment and employment procedures.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　各级工人考核组织成员，企业、事业单位和国家机关的工作人员在工人考核、评审过程中弄虚作假、徇私舞弊的，应当视情节轻重，由其所在单位或者上级主管部门根据人事管理权限对直接责任人员给予行政处分。

**Article 26** If members of worker assessment organizations at various levels or staff members of enterprises, institutions, and state organs engage in fraud or practice favoritism in the process of worker assessment and evaluation, they shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions by their unit or the competent department at a higher level in accordance with the personnel management authority against the directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反《技师合格证书》、《技术等级证书》、《特种作业人员操作证》的核发办法和规定，滥发上述证书的，除应当宣布其所发证书无效外，还应视情节轻重，由其上级主管部门或者监察机关对有关责任人员给予行政处分；对其中通过滥发证书获取非法收入的，应当没收其非法所得，并可处以非法所得五倍以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，应当依法追究其刑事责任。

**Article 27** Anyone who violates the issuance measures and provisions of the 'Technician Qualification Certificate', 'Technical Grade Certificate', and 'Special Operations Personnel Operation Permit' and indiscriminately issues the above certificates shall, in addition to declaring the issued certificates invalid, be given administrative sanctions by their superior competent department or supervisory organ depending on the severity of the circumstances; for those who obtain illegal income through indiscriminate issuance, the illegal income shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than five times the illegal income may also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　集体所有制和私营企业、事业单位的工人考核工作参照本条例执行。

**Article 28** The worker assessment work of collectively-owned and private enterprises and institutions shall be carried out by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　工人考核工作所需经费由财政部会同劳动部另行规定。

**Article 29** The funds required for worker assessment work shall be separately prescribed by the Ministry of Finance in conjunction with the Ministry of Labor.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例由劳动部负责解释。

**Article 30** The interpretation of these Regulations shall be the responsibility of the Ministry of Labor.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　劳动部可以根据本条例制定实施细则。

**Article 31** The Ministry of Labor may, in accordance with these Regulations, formulate implementing rules.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。原劳动人事部一九八三年发布的《工人技术考核暂行条例》(试行)同时废止。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The 'Interim Regulations on Worker Technical Assessment' (for trial implementation) issued by the former Ministry of Labor and Personnel in 1983 shall be abolished simultaneously.
