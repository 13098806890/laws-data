# 保障中小企业款项支付条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-03-17  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （2020年7月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第728号公布　2025年3月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第802号修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了促进机关、事业单位和大型企业及时支付中小企业款项，维护中小企业合法权益，优化营商环境，根据《中华人民共和国中小企业促进法》等法律，制定本条例。 第二条　机关、事业单位和大型企业采购货物、工程、服务支付中小企业款项，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　本条例所称中小企业，是指在中华人民共和国境内依法设立，依据国务院批准的中小企业划分标准确定的中型企业、小型企业和微型企业；所称大型企业，是指中小企业以外的企业。 中小企业、大型企业依合同订立时的企业规模类型确定。中小企业与机关、事业单位、大型企业订立合同时，应当主动告知其属于中小企业。 第四条　保障中小企业款项支付工作，应当贯彻落实党和国家的路线方针政策、决策部署，坚持支付主体负责、行业规范自律、政府依法监管、社会协同监督的原则，依法防范和治理拖欠中小企业款项问题。 第五条　国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门对保障中小企业款项支付工作进行综合协调、监督检查。国务院发展改革、财政、住房城乡建设、交通运输、水利、金融管理、国有资产监管、市场监督管理等有关部门应当按照职责分工，负责保障中小企业款项支付相关工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对本行政区域内保障中小企业款项支付工作负总责，加强组织领导、统筹协调，健全制度机制。县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内保障中小企业款项支付的管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门和发展改革、财政、住房城乡建设、交通运输、水利、金融管理、国有资产监管、市场监督管理等有关部门应当按照职责分工，负责保障中小企业款项支付相关工作。 第六条　有关行业协会商会应当按照法律法规和组织章程，加强行业自律管理，规范引导本行业大型企业履行及时支付中小企业款项义务、不得利用优势地位拖欠中小企业款项，为中小企业提供信息咨询、权益保护、纠纷处理等方面的服务，保护中小企业合法权益。 鼓励大型企业公开承诺向中小企业采购货物、工程、服务的付款期限与方式。 第七条　机关、事业单位和大型企业不得要求中小企业接受不合理的付款期限、方式、条件和违约责任等交易条件，不得拖欠中小企业的货物、工程、服务款项。 中小企业应当依法经营，诚实守信，按照合同约定提供合格的货物、工程和服务。 第二章　款项支付规定

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进机关、事业单位和大型企业及时支付中小企业款项，维护中小企业合法权益，优化营商环境，根据《中华人民共和国中小企业促进法》等法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated pursuant to the Small and Medium-sized Enterprises Promotion Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws for the purposes of promoting timely payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises by state organs, public institutions and large enterprises, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of small and medium-sized enterprises, and improving the business environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　机关、事业单位和大型企业采购货物、工程、服务支付中小企业款项，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Where state organs, public institutions and large enterprises purchase goods, projects or services and pay sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises, they shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称中小企业，是指在中华人民共和国境内依法设立，依据国务院批准的中小企业划分标准确定的中型企业、小型企业和微型企业；所称大型企业，是指中小企业以外的企业。  
中小企业、大型企业依合同订立时的企业规模类型确定。中小企业与机关、事业单位、大型企业订立合同时，应当主动告知其属于中小企业。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "small and medium-sized enterprises" means medium-sized enterprises, small enterprises and micro enterprises that are legally established within the territory of the People's Republic of China and are determined according to the classification criteria for small and medium-sized enterprises approved by the State Council; "large enterprises" means enterprises other than small and medium-sized enterprises.  
Small and medium-sized enterprises and large enterprises shall be determined by the type of enterprise scale at the time of conclusion of the contract. When concluding a contract with a state organ, public institution or large enterprise, a small or medium-sized enterprise shall proactively inform the other party that it is a small or medium-sized enterprise.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　保障中小企业款项支付工作，应当贯彻落实党和国家的路线方针政策、决策部署，坚持支付主体负责、行业规范自律、政府依法监管、社会协同监督的原则，依法防范和治理拖欠中小企业款项问题。

**Article 4** The work of ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises shall implement the guidelines, principles, policies, decisions and arrangements of the Party and the State, adhere to the principles of the responsible payment entity, industry self-discipline, government supervision according to law, and social coordination and supervision, and prevent and manage the problem of arrears of sums owed to small and medium-sized enterprises according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门对保障中小企业款项支付工作进行综合协调、监督检查。国务院发展改革、财政、住房城乡建设、交通运输、水利、金融管理、国有资产监管、市场监督管理等有关部门应当按照职责分工，负责保障中小企业款项支付相关工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对本行政区域内保障中小企业款项支付工作负总责，加强组织领导、统筹协调，健全制度机制。县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域内保障中小企业款项支付的管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门和发展改革、财政、住房城乡建设、交通运输、水利、金融管理、国有资产监管、市场监督管理等有关部门应当按照职责分工，负责保障中小企业款项支付相关工作。

**Article 5** The department of the State Council responsible for comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises shall carry out comprehensive coordination, supervision and inspection of the work of ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises. The relevant departments of the State Council, including development and reform, finance, housing and urban-rural construction, transport, water resources, financial administration, state-owned assets supervision and administration, and market supervision and administration, shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the work related to ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises.  
  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall assume overall responsibility for the work of ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises within their respective administrative regions, strengthen organizational leadership and overall coordination, and improve systems and mechanisms. The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises within their respective administrative regions.  
  
The departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level responsible for comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises and the relevant departments including development and reform, finance, housing and urban-rural construction, transport, water resources, financial administration, state-owned assets supervision and administration, and market supervision and administration shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the work related to ensuring payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有关行业协会商会应当按照法律法规和组织章程，加强行业自律管理，规范引导本行业大型企业履行及时支付中小企业款项义务、不得利用优势地位拖欠中小企业款项，为中小企业提供信息咨询、权益保护、纠纷处理等方面的服务，保护中小企业合法权益。  
鼓励大型企业公开承诺向中小企业采购货物、工程、服务的付款期限与方式。

**Article 6** Relevant industry associations and chambers of commerce shall, in accordance with laws, regulations and their organizational articles, strengthen industry self-discipline management, regulate and guide large enterprises in the industry to perform the obligation of timely payment to small and medium-sized enterprises, and shall not use their advantageous position to delay payment to small and medium-sized enterprises, and provide services such as information consultation, rights protection and dispute resolution for small and medium-sized enterprises, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of small and medium-sized enterprises.  
Large enterprises are encouraged to publicly commit to the payment terms and methods for the purchase of goods, projects and services from small and medium-sized enterprises.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　机关、事业单位和大型企业不得要求中小企业接受不合理的付款期限、方式、条件和违约责任等交易条件，不得拖欠中小企业的货物、工程、服务款项。  
中小企业应当依法经营，诚实守信，按照合同约定提供合格的货物、工程和服务。

**Article 7** State organs, public institutions and large enterprises shall not require small and medium-sized enterprises to accept unreasonable transaction conditions such as payment terms, methods, conditions and liabilities for breach of contract, and shall not delay payment for goods, projects or services of small and medium-sized enterprises.  
Small and medium-sized enterprises shall operate according to law, be honest and trustworthy, and provide qualified goods, projects and services in accordance with the contract.

### 第二章　款项支付规定

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　机关、事业单位使用财政资金从中小企业采购货物、工程、服务，应当严格按照批准的预算执行，不得无预算、超预算开展采购。  
政府投资项目所需资金应当按照国家有关规定确保落实到位，不得由施工单位垫资建设。

**Article 8** Where state organs and public institutions use fiscal funds to purchase goods, projects or services from small and medium-sized enterprises, they shall strictly implement the approved budgets, and shall not conduct procurement without a budget or beyond the budget.  
The funds required for government-invested projects shall be ensured to be in place in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and construction units shall not be required to advance funds for construction.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　机关、事业单位从中小企业采购货物、工程、服务，应当自货物、工程、服务交付之日起30日内支付款项；合同另有约定的，从其约定，但付款期限最长不得超过60日。  
大型企业从中小企业采购货物、工程、服务，应当自货物、工程、服务交付之日起60日内支付款项；合同另有约定的，从其约定，但应当按照行业规范、交易习惯合理约定付款期限并及时支付款项，不得约定以收到第三方付款作为向中小企业支付款项的条件或者按照第三方付款进度比例支付中小企业款项。  
法律、行政法规或者国家有关规定对本条第一款、第二款付款期限另有规定的，从其规定。  
合同约定采取履行进度结算、定期结算等结算方式的，付款期限应当自双方确认结算金额之日起算。

**Article 9** Where a state organ or public institution purchases goods, projects or services from a small or medium-sized enterprise, it shall pay the sum within 30 days from the date of delivery of the goods, projects or services; if the contract provides otherwise, such provision shall prevail, but the maximum payment period shall not exceed 60 days.  
  
Where a large enterprise purchases goods, projects or services from a small or medium-sized enterprise, it shall pay the sum within 60 days from the date of delivery of the goods, projects or services; if the contract provides otherwise, such provision shall prevail, but the payment period shall be reasonably agreed upon in accordance with industry norms and trade practices and payment shall be made promptly. It shall not be agreed that receiving payment from a third party is a condition for paying the small or medium-sized enterprise, or that payment to the small or medium-sized enterprise shall be made in proportion to the payment progress of the third party.  
  
If laws, administrative regulations or relevant provisions of the State otherwise provide for the payment periods in the first and second paragraphs of this Article, such provisions shall prevail.  
  
Where the contract provides for settlement methods such as progress settlement or periodic settlement, the payment period shall be calculated from the date on which both parties confirm the settlement amount.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　机关、事业单位和大型企业与中小企业约定以货物、工程、服务交付后经检验或者验收合格作为支付中小企业款项条件的，付款期限应当自检验或者验收合格之日起算。  
合同双方应当在合同中约定明确、合理的检验或者验收期限，并在该期限内完成检验或者验收，法律、行政法规或者国家有关规定对检验或者验收期限另有规定的，从其规定。机关、事业单位和大型企业拖延检验或者验收的，付款期限自约定的检验或者验收期限届满之日起算。

**Article 10** Where a state organ, public institution or large enterprise agrees with a small or medium-sized enterprise that passing inspection or acceptance after delivery of goods, projects or services is a condition for payment, the payment period shall be calculated from the date of passing inspection or acceptance.  
Both parties to the contract shall agree in the contract on a clear and reasonable inspection or acceptance period, and shall complete the inspection or acceptance within such period. If laws, administrative regulations or relevant provisions of the State otherwise provide for the inspection or acceptance period, such provisions shall prevail. If the state organ, public institution or large enterprise delays inspection or acceptance, the payment period shall be calculated from the date on which the agreed inspection or acceptance period expires.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　机关、事业单位和大型企业使用商业汇票、应收账款电子凭证等非现金支付方式支付中小企业款项的，应当在合同中作出明确、合理约定，不得强制中小企业接受商业汇票、应收账款电子凭证等非现金支付方式，不得利用商业汇票、应收账款电子凭证等非现金支付方式变相延长付款期限。

**Article 11** Where state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises use non-cash payment methods such as commercial bills and electronic accounts receivable vouchers to pay sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises, they shall make clear and reasonable stipulations in the contract, shall not compel small and medium-sized enterprises to accept non-cash payment methods such as commercial bills and electronic accounts receivable vouchers, and shall not use non-cash payment methods such as commercial bills and electronic accounts receivable vouchers to covertly extend the payment term.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　机关、事业单位和国有大型企业不得强制要求以审计机关的审计结果作为结算依据，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 12** State organs, public institutions, and state-owned large enterprises shall not compulsorily require that the audit results of the audit authorities be taken as the basis for settlement, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　除依法设立的投标保证金、履约保证金、工程质量保证金、农民工工资保证金外，工程建设中不得以任何形式收取其他保证金。保证金的收取比例、方式应当符合法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。  
机关、事业单位和大型企业不得将保证金限定为现金。中小企业以金融机构出具的保函等提供保证的，机关、事业单位和大型企业应当接受。  
机关、事业单位和大型企业应当依法或者按照合同约定，在保证期限届满后及时与中小企业对收取的保证金进行核算并退还。

**Article 13** Except for bid bonds, performance bonds, project quality bonds, and migrant worker wage bonds established according to law, no other bonds shall be collected in any form during engineering construction. The proportion and method of collecting bonds shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions.  
State organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall not limit bonds to cash. Where small and medium-sized enterprises provide a guarantee by means of a letter of guarantee issued by a financial institution, state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall accept it.  
State organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall, according to law or as agreed in the contract, promptly settle and refund the bonds collected from small and medium-sized enterprises after the guarantee period expires.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　机关、事业单位和大型企业不得以法定代表人或者主要负责人变更，履行内部付款流程，或者在合同未作约定的情况下以等待竣工验收备案、决算审计等为由，拒绝或者迟延支付中小企业款项。

**Article 14** State organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall not refuse or delay payment of sums due to small and medium-sized enterprises on the grounds of a change in the legal representative or principal responsible person, performance of internal payment procedures, or, in the absence of a contractual stipulation, waiting for completion acceptance filing or final account audit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　机关、事业单位和大型企业与中小企业的交易，部分存在争议但不影响其他部分履行的，对于无争议部分应当履行及时付款义务。

**Article 15** Where a transaction between a state organ, public institution, or large enterprise and a small or medium-sized enterprise is partially in dispute but does not affect the performance of the other parts, the obligation to make timely payment for the undisputed part shall be performed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　鼓励、引导、支持商业银行等金融机构增加对中小企业的信贷投放，降低中小企业综合融资成本，为中小企业以应收账款、知识产权、政府采购合同、存货、机器设备等为担保品的融资提供便利。  
中小企业以应收账款融资的，机关、事业单位和大型企业应当自中小企业提出确权请求之日起30日内确认债权债务关系，支持中小企业融资。

**Article 16** Commercial banks and other financial institutions are encouraged, guided, and supported to increase credit supply to small and medium-sized enterprises, reduce the comprehensive financing costs of small and medium-sized enterprises, and facilitate the financing of small and medium-sized enterprises using accounts receivable, intellectual property, government procurement contracts, inventory, machinery and equipment, etc. as collateral.  
Where small and medium-sized enterprises finance with accounts receivable, state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises shall confirm the creditor-debtor relationship within 30 days from the date the small or medium-sized enterprise makes a request for confirmation of rights, to support the financing of the small or medium-sized enterprise.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　机关、事业单位和大型企业迟延支付中小企业款项的，应当支付逾期利息。双方对逾期利息的利率有约定的，约定利率不得低于合同订立时1年期贷款市场报价利率；未作约定的，按照每日利率万分之五支付逾期利息。

**Article 17** Where a state organ, public institution, or large enterprise delays payment of sums due to a small or medium-sized enterprise, it shall pay overdue interest. Where the parties have agreed on the interest rate for overdue interest, the agreed rate shall not be lower than the one-year loan prime rate at the time of contract conclusion; where there is no agreement, overdue interest shall be paid at a daily rate of 0.05 percent.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　机关、事业单位应当于每年3月31日前将上一年度逾期尚未支付中小企业款项的合同数量、金额等信息通过网站、报刊等便于公众知晓的方式公开。  
大型企业应当将逾期尚未支付中小企业款项的合同数量、金额等信息纳入企业年度报告，依法通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示。

**Article 18** State organs and public institutions shall, before March 31 each year, disclose the number and amount of contracts with overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises from the previous year through websites, newspapers, or other means easily accessible to the public.  
Large enterprises shall include the number and amount of contracts with overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises in their annual report, and shall publicly announce such information through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　大型企业应当将保障中小企业款项支付工作情况，纳入企业风险控制与合规管理体系，并督促其全资或者控股子公司及时支付中小企业款项。

**Article 19** Large enterprises shall incorporate the work of ensuring payment of sums to small and medium-sized enterprises into their risk control and compliance management system, and shall supervise their wholly-owned or controlled subsidiaries to make timely payment of sums to small and medium-sized enterprises.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机关、事业单位和大型企业及其工作人员不得以任何形式对提出付款请求或者投诉的中小企业及其工作人员进行恐吓、打击报复。

**Article 20** State organs, public institutions, and large enterprises, and their staff members, shall not, in any form, intimidate or retaliate against small and medium-sized enterprises or their staff members who have made payment requests or complaints.

### 第三章　监督管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门通过监督检查、函询约谈、督办通报、投诉处理等措施，加大对机关、事业单位和大型企业拖欠中小企业款项的清理力度。

**Article 21** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, through measures such as supervision and inspection, correspondence and interview, supervision and notification, and complaint handling, increase the efforts to clear up the overdue payments of small and medium-sized enterprises by state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上地方人民政府部门应当每年定期将上一年度逾期尚未支付中小企业款项情况按程序报告本级人民政府。事业单位、国有大型企业应当每年定期将上一年度逾期尚未支付中小企业款项情况按程序报其主管部门或者监管部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当每年定期听取本行政区域内保障中小企业款项支付工作汇报，加强督促指导，研究解决突出问题。

**Article 22** Departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, on a regular basis each year, report to the people's government at the same level the overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises in the preceding year in accordance with the procedures. Public institutions and state-owned large enterprises shall, on a regular basis each year, report to their competent departments or regulatory authorities the overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises in the preceding year in accordance with the procedures.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, on a regular basis each year, listen to reports on the work of ensuring payments to small and medium-sized enterprises within their administrative regions, strengthen supervision and guidance, and study and resolve prominent issues.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　省级以上人民政府建立督查制度，对保障中小企业款项支付工作进行监督检查，对政策落实不到位、工作推进不力的部门和地方人民政府主要负责人进行约谈。  
县级以上人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门对拖欠中小企业款项的机关、事业单位和大型企业，可以进行函询约谈，对情节严重的，予以督办通报，必要时可以会同拖欠单位上级机关、行业主管部门、监管部门联合进行。

**Article 23** People's governments at or above the provincial level shall establish a supervision system to supervise and inspect the work of ensuring payments to small and medium-sized enterprises, and to interview the principal heads of departments and local people's governments that fail to implement policies properly or make insufficient progress.  
The departments of the people's governments at or above the county level responsible for the comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises may conduct correspondence and interviews with state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises that have overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises; where the circumstances are serious, they shall supervise and notify; when necessary, they may jointly conduct such actions with the superior organs, competent industry departments, or regulatory authorities of the overdue entities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　省级以上人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门（以下统称受理投诉部门）应当建立便利畅通的渠道，受理对机关、事业单位和大型企业拖欠中小企业款项的投诉。  
国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门建立国家统一的拖欠中小企业款项投诉平台，加强投诉处理机制建设，与相关部门、地方人民政府信息共享、协同配合。

**Article 24** The departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level responsible for the comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises (hereinafter collectively referred to as the complaint acceptance departments) shall establish convenient and unimpeded channels to accept complaints about overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises by state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises.  
The department of the State Council responsible for the comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises shall establish a unified national complaint platform for overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises, strengthen the construction of complaint handling mechanisms, and share information and coordinate with relevant departments and local people's governments.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　受理投诉部门应当按照“属地管理、分级负责，谁主管谁负责、谁监管谁负责”的原则，自正式受理之日起10个工作日内，按程序将投诉转交有关部门或者地方人民政府指定的部门（以下统称处理投诉部门）处理。  
处理投诉部门应当自收到投诉材料之日起30日内形成处理结果，以书面形式反馈投诉人，并反馈受理投诉部门。情况复杂或者有其他特殊原因的，经部门负责人批准，可适当延长，但处理期限最长不得超过90日。  
被投诉人应当配合处理投诉部门工作。处理投诉部门应当督促被投诉人及时反馈情况。被投诉人未及时反馈或者未按规定反馈的，处理投诉部门应当向其发出督办书；收到督办书仍拒不配合的，处理投诉部门可以约谈、通报被投诉人，并责令整改。  
投诉人应当与被投诉人存在合同关系，不得虚假、恶意投诉。  
受理投诉部门和处理投诉部门的工作人员，对在履行职责中获悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人信息负有保密义务。

**Article 25** The complaint acceptance department shall, in accordance with the principle of "territorial management, graded responsibility, and whoever is in charge or supervises shall be responsible," within 10 working days from the date of formal acceptance, transfer the complaint to the relevant department or the department designated by the local people's government (hereinafter collectively referred to as the complaint handling department) for handling.  
The complaint handling department shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the complaint materials, form a handling result, provide written feedback to the complainant, and also provide feedback to the complaint acceptance department. If the circumstances are complex or there are other special reasons, the period may be appropriately extended with the approval of the head of the department, but the maximum processing period shall not exceed 90 days.  
The complained party shall cooperate with the work of the complaint handling department. The complaint handling department shall urge the complained party to provide feedback in a timely manner. If the complained party fails to provide feedback in a timely manner or in accordance with the regulations, the complaint handling department shall issue a supervision notice to it; if the complained party still refuses to cooperate after receiving the supervision notice, the complaint handling department may interview or notify the complained party and order it to make corrections.  
The complainant shall have a contractual relationship with the complained party and shall not file false or malicious complaints.  
Staff members of the complaint acceptance department and the complaint handling department shall have a duty of confidentiality regarding state secrets, trade secrets, and personal information learned in the course of performing their duties.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　机关、事业单位和大型企业拖欠中小企业款项依法依规被认定为失信的，受理投诉部门和有关部门按程序将有关失信情况记入相关主体信用记录。情节严重或者造成严重不良社会影响的，将相关信息纳入全国信用信息共享平台和国家企业信用信息公示系统，向社会公示；对机关、事业单位在公务消费、办公用房、经费安排等方面采取必要的限制措施，对大型企业在财政资金支持、投资项目审批、融资获取、市场准入、资质评定、评优评先等方面依法依规予以限制。

**Article 26** Where overdue payments to small and medium-sized enterprises by state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises are determined to be acts of dishonesty according to laws and regulations, the complaint acceptance department and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the procedures, record such dishonesty in the credit records of the relevant entities. If the circumstances are serious or cause severe adverse social impact, the relevant information shall be incorporated into the National Credit Information Sharing Platform and the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, and be disclosed to the public; necessary restrictive measures shall be taken against state organs and public institutions in terms of official consumption, office space, and fund arrangements, and restrictions shall be imposed on large enterprises in terms of financial fund support, investment project approval, financing access, market access, qualification evaluation, and evaluation of excellence, in accordance with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　审计机关依法对机关、事业单位和国有大型企业支付中小企业款项情况实施审计监督。

**Article 27** Audit institutions shall, according to law, carry out audit supervision on the payment of amounts to small and medium-sized enterprises by state organs, public institutions, and state-owned large enterprises.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家依法开展中小企业发展环境评估和营商环境评价时，应当将保障中小企业款项支付工作情况纳入评估和评价内容。

**Article 28** When the State conducts, according to law, assessments of the environment for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises and evaluations of the business environment, the work of ensuring payments to small and medium-sized enterprises shall be included in the content of such assessments and evaluations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门依据国务院批准的中小企业划分标准，建立企业规模类型测试平台，提供中小企业规模类型自测服务。  
对中小企业规模类型有争议的，可以向主张为中小企业一方所在地的县级以上地方人民政府负责中小企业促进工作综合管理的部门申请认定。人力资源社会保障、市场监督管理、统计等相关部门应当应认定部门的请求，提供必要的协助。

**Article 29** The department of the State Council responsible for the comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises shall, pursuant to the classification standards for small and medium-sized enterprises approved by the State Council, establish a platform for testing enterprise size types and provide self-testing services for small and medium-sized enterprise size types.  
If there is a dispute over the size type of a small or medium-sized enterprise, an application for determination may be submitted to the department responsible for the comprehensive management of the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises of the local people's government at or above the county level in the location of the party claiming to be a small or medium-sized enterprise. Relevant departments such as human resources and social security, market supervision and administration, and statistics shall provide necessary assistance upon the request of the determining department.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家鼓励法律服务机构为与机关、事业单位和大型企业存在支付纠纷的中小企业提供公益法律服务。  
新闻媒体应当开展对保障中小企业款项支付相关法律法规政策的公益宣传，依法加强对机关、事业单位和大型企业拖欠中小企业款项行为的舆论监督。

**Article 30** The State encourages legal service institutions to provide public interest legal services to small and medium-sized enterprises that have payment disputes with state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises.  
News media shall carry out public interest publicity on laws, regulations, and policies related to ensuring payments to small and medium-sized enterprises, and strengthen, according to law, public opinion supervision over the acts of state organs, public institutions, and large enterprises in delaying payments to small and medium-sized enterprises.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　机关、事业单位违反本条例，有下列情形之一的，由其上级机关、主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未在规定的期限内支付中小企业货物、工程、服务款项；  
（二）拖延检验、验收；  
（三）强制中小企业接受商业汇票、应收账款电子凭证等非现金支付方式，或者利用商业汇票、应收账款电子凭证等非现金支付方式变相延长付款期限；  
（四）没有法律、行政法规依据，要求以审计机关的审计结果作为结算依据；  
（五）违法收取保证金，拒绝接受中小企业以金融机构出具的保函等提供保证，或者不及时与中小企业对保证金进行核算并退还；  
（六）以法定代表人或者主要负责人变更，履行内部付款流程，或者在合同未作约定的情况下以等待竣工验收备案、决算审计等为由，拒绝或者迟延支付中小企业款项；  
（七）未按照规定公开逾期尚未支付中小企业款项信息。

**Article 31** Where an organ or public institution violates these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the superior organ or the competent department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to pay SMEs for goods, construction projects, or services within the prescribed time limit;  
(2) delaying inspection and acceptance;  
(3) compelling SMEs to accept non-cash payment methods such as commercial drafts and electronic vouchers for accounts receivable, or using such non-cash payment methods to indirectly extend the payment period;  
(4) requiring, without the basis of laws or administrative regulations, that the audit results of the audit authorities be used as the basis for settlement;  
(5) illegally collecting security deposits, refusing to accept guarantees provided by SMEs such as letters of guarantee issued by financial institutions, or failing to promptly calculate and return security deposits to SMEs;  
(6) refusing or delaying payment to SMEs on the grounds of a change of legal representative or principal responsible person, performing internal payment procedures, or, in the absence of contractual agreement, awaiting completion acceptance filing or final audit;  
(7) failing to disclose information on overdue payments to SMEs in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　机关、事业单位有下列情形之一的，依法追究责任：  
（一）使用财政资金从中小企业采购货物、工程、服务，未按照批准的预算执行；  
（二）要求施工单位对政府投资项目垫资建设。

**Article 32** Where an organ or public institution falls under any of the following circumstances, liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) using fiscal funds to purchase goods, construction projects, or services from SMEs without executing according to the approved budget;  
(2) requiring a construction unit to provide advance funds for the construction of a government-invested project.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国有大型企业拖欠中小企业款项，造成不良后果或者影响的，对负有责任的国有企业管理人员依法给予处分。  
国有大型企业没有法律、行政法规依据，要求以审计机关的审计结果作为结算依据的，由其监管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，对负有责任的国有企业管理人员依法给予处分。

**Article 33** Where a large State-owned enterprise delays payment to SMEs, causing adverse consequences or impacts, the responsible managerial personnel of the State-owned enterprise shall be given sanctions according to law.  
Where a large State-owned enterprise, without the basis of laws or administrative regulations, requires that the audit results of the audit authorities be used as the basis for settlement, the supervising department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the responsible managerial personnel of the State-owned enterprise shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　大型企业违反本条例，未按照规定在企业年度报告中公示逾期尚未支付中小企业款项信息或者隐瞒真实情况、弄虚作假的，由市场监督管理部门依法处理。

**Article 34** Where a large enterprise violates these Regulations by failing to disclose in its annual corporate report information on overdue payments to SMEs in accordance with regulations, or by concealing the true situation or practicing fraud, the market supervision and administration department shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　机关、事业单位和大型企业及其工作人员对提出付款请求或者投诉的中小企业及其工作人员进行恐吓、打击报复，或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分或者处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** Where an organ, public institution, or large enterprise, or any of their staff, intimidates or retaliates against an SME or its staff that has made a payment request or complaint, or commits any other act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions or punishments according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　部分或者全部使用财政资金的团体组织采购货物、工程、服务支付中小企业款项，参照本条例对机关、事业单位的有关规定执行。  
军队采购货物、工程、服务支付中小企业款项，按照军队的有关规定执行。

**Article 36** Where a group organization that uses fiscal funds in part or in full purchases goods, construction projects, or services and makes payments to SMEs, the relevant provisions of these Regulations on organs and public institutions shall be applied as a reference.  
Where the military purchases goods, construction projects, or services and makes payments to SMEs, the relevant provisions of the military shall be applied.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　本条例自2025年6月1日起施行。

**Article 37** These Regulations shall come into force on June 1, 2025.
