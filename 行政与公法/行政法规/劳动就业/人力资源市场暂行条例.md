# 人力资源市场暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-06-29  
**生效日期**：2018-10-01

> (2018年5月2日国务院第7次常务会议通过　2018年6月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第700号公布　自2018年10月1日起施行)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范人力资源市场活动，促进人力资源合理流动和优化配置，促进就业创业，根据《中华人民共和国就业促进法》和有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Employment Promotion Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, for the purposes of regulating human resources market activities, promoting the rational flow and optimal allocation of human resources, and promoting employment and business start-ups.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内通过人力资源市场求职、招聘和开展人力资源服务，适用本条例。  
法律、行政法规和国务院规定对求职、招聘和开展人力资源服务另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to job seeking, recruitment, and the provision of human resources services through the human resources market within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Where laws, administrative regulations, and provisions of the State Council provide otherwise on job seeking, recruitment, and the provision of human resources services, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　通过人力资源市场求职、招聘和开展人力资源服务，应当遵循合法、公平、诚实信用的原则。

**Article 3** Job seeking, recruitment, and the provision of human resources services through the human resources market shall follow the principles of lawfulness, fairness, and good faith.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院人力资源社会保障行政部门负责全国人力资源市场的统筹规划和综合管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府人力资源社会保障行政部门负责本行政区域人力资源市场的管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府发展改革、教育、公安、财政、商务、税务、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内做好人力资源市场的管理工作。

**Article 4** The human resources and social security administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the overall planning and comprehensive management of the national human resources market.  
The human resources and social security administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management of the human resources market within their respective administrative regions.  
The development and reform, education, public security, finance, commerce, taxation, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall perform their duties within the scope of their respective functions to manage the human resources market.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家加强人力资源服务标准化建设，发挥人力资源服务标准在行业引导、服务规范、市场监管等方面的作用。

**Article 5** The State shall strengthen the standardization of human resources services and shall leverage the role of human resources service standards in industry guidance, service regulation, and market supervision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人力资源服务行业协会应当依照法律、法规、规章及其章程的规定，制定行业自律规范，推进行业诚信建设，提高服务质量，对会员的人力资源服务活动进行指导、监督，依法维护会员合法权益，反映会员诉求，促进行业公平竞争。

**Article 6** Human resources service industry associations shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, rules, and their own articles of association, formulate industry self-discipline norms, promote industry integrity building, improve service quality, guide and supervise the human resources service activities of their members, protect the lawful rights and interests of members according to law, reflect members' demands, and promote fair competition in the industry.

### 第二章　人力资源市场培育

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立统一开放、竞争有序的人力资源市场体系，发挥市场在人力资源配置中的决定性作用，健全人力资源开发机制，激发人力资源创新创造创业活力，促进人力资源市场繁荣发展。

**Article 7** The State shall establish a unified, open, competitive, and orderly human resources market system, shall let the market play a decisive role in the allocation of human resources, shall improve the human resources development mechanism, shall stimulate the innovation, creation, and entrepreneurship vitality of human resources, and shall promote the prosperous development of the human resources market.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立政府宏观调控、市场公平竞争、单位自主用人、个人自主择业、人力资源服务机构诚信服务的人力资源流动配置机制，促进人力资源自由有序流动。

**Article 8** The State shall establish a human resources flow and allocation mechanism that features government macro-control, fair market competition, autonomous employment by entities, autonomous career choices by individuals, and honest services by human resources service institutions, so as to promote the free and orderly flow of human resources.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当将人力资源市场建设纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，运用区域、产业、土地等政策，推进人力资源市场建设，发展专业性、行业性人力资源市场，鼓励并规范高端人力资源服务等业态发展，提高人力资源服务业发展水平。  
国家鼓励社会力量参与人力资源市场建设。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the development of the human resources market into their national economic and social development plans, shall use regional, industrial, and land policies to promote the development of the human resources market, develop specialized and industry-specific human resources markets, encourage and regulate the development of high-end human resources services and other business forms, and improve the development level of the human resources service industry.  
The State shall encourage social forces to participate in the development of the human resources market.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府建立覆盖城乡和各行业的人力资源市场供求信息系统，完善市场信息发布制度，为求职、招聘提供服务。

**Article 10** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a human resources market supply and demand information system covering urban and rural areas and all industries, shall improve the market information release system, and shall provide services for job seeking and recruitment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家引导和促进人力资源在机关、企业、事业单位、社会组织之间以及不同地区之间合理流动。任何地方和单位不得违反国家规定在户籍、地域、身份等方面设置限制人力资源流动的条件。

**Article 11** The State shall guide and promote the rational flow of human resources among government organs, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and between different regions. No locality or entity shall, in violation of state regulations, set conditions restricting the flow of human resources in terms of household registration, region, identity, or other aspects.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当加强人力资源市场监管，维护市场秩序，保障公平竞争。

**Article 12** The human resources and social security administrative department shall strengthen the supervision of the human resources market, maintain market order, and ensure fair competition.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励开展平等、互利的人力资源国际合作与交流，充分开发利用国际国内人力资源。

**Article 13** The State shall encourage the development of equal and mutually beneficial international cooperation and exchanges in human resources, and shall fully develop and utilize international and domestic human resources.

### 第三章　人力资源服务机构

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本条例所称人力资源服务机构，包括公共人力资源服务机构和经营性人力资源服务机构。  
公共人力资源服务机构，是指县级以上人民政府设立的公共就业和人才服务机构。  
经营性人力资源服务机构，是指依法设立的从事人力资源服务经营活动的机构。

**Article 14** For the purposes of these Regulations, human resources service institutions include public human resources service institutions and for-profit human resources service institutions.  
Public human resources service institutions refer to public employment and talent service institutions established by people's governments at or above the county level.  
For-profit human resources service institutions refer to institutions lawfully established to engage in business activities of human resources services.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公共人力资源服务机构提供下列服务，不得收费：  
(一)人力资源供求、市场工资指导价位、职业培训等信息发布；  
(二)职业介绍、职业指导和创业开业指导；  
(三)就业创业和人才政策法规咨询；  
(四)对就业困难人员实施就业援助；  
(五)办理就业登记、失业登记等事务；  
(六)办理高等学校、中等职业学校、技工学校毕业生接收手续；  
(七)流动人员人事档案管理；  
(八)县级以上人民政府确定的其他服务。

**Article 15** Public human resources service institutions shall provide the following services free of charge:  
(1) Release of information on human resources supply and demand, market wage guidance prices, and vocational training;  
(2) Job placement, career guidance, and guidance on starting a business;  
(3) Consultation on employment, business start-up, and talent policies, laws, and regulations;  
(4) Implementation of employment assistance for persons with employment difficulties;  
(5) Handling of employment registration, unemployment registration, and other matters;  
(6) Handling of procedures for receiving graduates from institutions of higher learning, secondary vocational schools, and technical schools;  
(7) Management of personnel files for mobile personnel;  
(8) Other services determined by the people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公共人力资源服务机构应当加强信息化建设，不断提高服务质量和效率。  
公共人力资源服务经费纳入政府预算。人力资源社会保障行政部门应当依法加强公共人力资源服务经费管理。

**Article 16** Public human resources service institutions shall strengthen information technology development and continuously improve service quality and efficiency.  
Funds for public human resources services shall be included in the government budget. The human resources and social security administrative department shall, according to law, strengthen the management of funds for public human resources services.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家通过政府购买服务等方式支持经营性人力资源服务机构提供公益性人力资源服务。

**Article 17** The State shall support for-profit human resources service institutions in providing public-interest human resources services through means such as government procurement of services.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经营性人力资源服务机构从事职业中介活动的，应当依法向人力资源社会保障行政部门申请行政许可，取得人力资源服务许可证。  
经营性人力资源服务机构开展人力资源供求信息的收集和发布、就业和创业指导、人力资源管理咨询、人力资源测评、人力资源培训、承接人力资源服务外包等人力资源服务业务的，应当自开展业务之日起15日内向人力资源社会保障行政部门备案。  
经营性人力资源服务机构从事劳务派遣业务的，执行国家有关劳务派遣的规定。

**Article 18** Where a for-profit human resources service institution engages in job intermediation activities, it shall, according to law, apply to the human resources and social security administrative department for an administrative license and obtain a human resources service license.  
Where a for-profit human resources service institution carries out human resources service businesses such as the collection and release of human resources supply and demand information, employment and business start-up guidance, human resources management consulting, human resources assessment, human resources training, and undertaking human resources service outsourcing, it shall, within 15 days from the date of commencing such businesses, report to the human resources and social security administrative department for archiving.  
Where a for-profit human resources service institution engages in labor dispatch services, it shall implement the relevant state provisions on labor dispatch.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当自收到经营性人力资源服务机构从事职业中介活动的申请之日起20日内依法作出行政许可决定。符合条件的，颁发人力资源服务许可证；不符合条件的，作出不予批准的书面决定并说明理由。

**Article 19** The human resources and social security administrative department shall, within 20 days from the date of receiving an application from a for-profit human resources service institution to engage in job intermediation activities, make an administrative licensing decision according to law. Where the conditions are met, it shall issue a human resources service license; where the conditions are not met, it shall make a written decision of disapproval and state the reasons.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经营性人力资源服务机构设立分支机构的，应当自工商登记办理完毕之日起15日内，书面报告分支机构所在地人力资源社会保障行政部门。

**Article 20** Where a for-profit human resources service institution establishes a branch, it shall, within 15 days from the date of completing the industrial and commercial registration, submit a written report to the human resources and social security administrative department of the place where the branch is located.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经营性人力资源服务机构变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者终止经营活动的，应当自工商变更登记或者注销登记办理完毕之日起15日内，书面报告人力资源社会保障行政部门。

**Article 21** Where a for-profit human resources service organization changes its name, domicile, legal representative, or terminates its business activities, it shall, within 15 days from the date of completion of the industrial and commercial change registration or cancellation registration, make a written report to the human resources and social security administrative department.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当及时向社会公布取得行政许可或者经过备案的经营性人力资源服务机构名单及其变更、延续等情况。

**Article 22** The human resources and social security administrative department shall promptly publish to the public the list of for-profit human resources service organizations that have obtained an administrative license or completed filing, as well as information on their changes, renewals, and other relevant matters.

### 第四章　人力资源市场活动规范

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　个人求职，应当如实提供本人基本信息以及与应聘岗位相关的知识、技能、工作经历等情况。

**Article 23** An individual seeking employment shall truthfully provide their basic personal information, as well as knowledge, skills, work experience, and other relevant information related to the position for which they are applying.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用人单位发布或者向人力资源服务机构提供的单位基本情况、招聘人数、招聘条件、工作内容、工作地点、基本劳动报酬等招聘信息，应当真实、合法，不得含有民族、种族、性别、宗教信仰等方面的歧视性内容。  
用人单位自主招用人员，需要建立劳动关系的，应当依法与劳动者订立劳动合同，并按照国家有关规定办理社会保险等相关手续。

**Article 24** The recruitment information published by an employer or provided to a human resources service organization, including the basic information of the employer, the number of recruits, recruitment conditions, job content, work location, basic labor remuneration, and other such information, shall be truthful and lawful, and shall not contain discriminatory content regarding ethnicity, race, gender, religious belief, or any other aspect.  
Where an employer independently recruits personnel and needs to establish a labor relationship, it shall conclude a labor contract with the worker according to law and handle social insurance and other relevant procedures in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人力资源流动，应当遵守法律、法规对服务期、从业限制、保密等方面的规定。

**Article 25** The flow of human resources shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations concerning service periods, employment restrictions, confidentiality, and other aspects.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人力资源服务机构接受用人单位委托招聘人员，应当要求用人单位提供招聘简章、营业执照或者有关部门批准设立的文件、经办人的身份证件、用人单位的委托证明，并对所提供材料的真实性、合法性进行审查。

**Article 26** Where a human resources service organization accepts a commission from an employer to recruit personnel, it shall require the employer to provide the recruitment brochure, the business license or the document approving the establishment of the employer by the relevant department, the identity document of the handling person, and the employer's commission certificate, and shall examine the authenticity and legality of the materials provided.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人力资源服务机构接受用人单位委托招聘人员或者开展其他人力资源服务，不得采取欺诈、暴力、胁迫或者其他不正当手段，不得以招聘为名牟取不正当利益，不得介绍单位或者个人从事违法活动。

**Article 27** Where a human resources service organization accepts a commission from an employer to recruit personnel or carries out other human resources services, it shall not use fraud, violence, coercion, or other improper means, shall not seek improper benefits under the guise of recruitment, and shall not introduce any entity or individual to engage in illegal activities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人力资源服务机构举办现场招聘会，应当制定组织实施办法、应急预案和安全保卫工作方案，核实参加招聘会的招聘单位及其招聘简章的真实性、合法性，提前将招聘会信息向社会公布，并对招聘中的各项活动进行管理。  
举办大型现场招聘会，应当符合《大型群众性活动安全管理条例》等法律法规的规定。

**Article 28** Where a human resources service organization holds an on-site job fair, it shall formulate implementation and organization measures, an emergency response plan, and a security protection work plan, verify the authenticity and legality of the recruiting entities participating in the job fair and their recruitment brochures, publish the job fair information to the public in advance, and manage all activities during the recruitment.  
Holding a large-scale on-site job fair shall comply with the provisions of the Regulations on the Safety Management of Large-scale Mass Activities and other laws and regulations.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人力资源服务机构发布人力资源供求信息，应当建立健全信息发布审查和投诉处理机制，确保发布的信息真实、合法、有效。  
人力资源服务机构在业务活动中收集用人单位和个人信息的，不得泄露或者违法使用所知悉的商业秘密和个人信息。

**Article 29** Where a human resources service organization publishes human resources supply and demand information, it shall establish and improve a review mechanism for information publication and a complaint handling mechanism to ensure that the published information is truthful, lawful, and effective.  
Where a human resources service organization collects information on employers and individuals in its business activities, it shall not disclose or illegally use the trade secrets and personal information it has obtained.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　经营性人力资源服务机构接受用人单位委托提供人力资源服务外包的，不得改变用人单位与个人的劳动关系，不得与用人单位串通侵害个人的合法权益。

**Article 30** Where a for-profit human resources service organization accepts a commission from an employer to provide human resources service outsourcing, it shall not alter the labor relationship between the employer and the individual, and shall not collude with the employer to infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the individual.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人力资源服务机构通过互联网提供人力资源服务的，应当遵守本条例和国家有关网络安全、互联网信息服务管理的规定。

**Article 31** Where a human resources service organization provides human resources services through the Internet, it shall comply with these Regulations and the provisions of the state concerning network security and Internet information service management.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经营性人力资源服务机构应当在服务场所明示下列事项，并接受人力资源社会保障行政部门和市场监督管理、价格等主管部门的监督检查：  
(一)营业执照；  
(二)服务项目；  
(三)收费标准；  
(四)监督机关和监督电话。  
从事职业中介活动的，还应当在服务场所明示人力资源服务许可证。

**Article 32** A for-profit human resources service organization shall display the following items conspicuously at the place of service, and shall accept the supervision and inspection of the human resources and social security administrative department and the competent departments for market regulation, price, and other matters:  
(1) business license;  
(2) service items;  
(3) fee standards;  
(4) supervisory authority and supervision telephone number.  
Where it engages in occupational intermediation activities, it shall also display the human resources service license conspicuously at the place of service.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人力资源服务机构应当加强内部制度建设，健全财务管理制度，建立服务台账，如实记录服务对象、服务过程、服务结果等信息。服务台账应当保存2年以上。

**Article 33** A human resources service organization shall strengthen internal system construction, improve the financial management system, establish service records, and truthfully record information such as service targets, service processes, and service results. Service records shall be kept for at least two years.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人力资源社会保障行政部门对经营性人力资源服务机构实施监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)进入被检查单位进行检查；  
(二)询问有关人员，查阅服务台账等服务信息档案；  
(三)要求被检查单位提供与检查事项相关的文件资料，并作出解释和说明；  
(四)采取记录、录音、录像、照相或者复制等方式收集有关情况和资料；  
(五)法律、法规规定的其他措施。  
人力资源社会保障行政部门实施监督检查时，监督检查人员不得少于2人，应当出示执法证件，并对被检查单位的商业秘密予以保密。  
对人力资源社会保障行政部门依法进行的监督检查，被检查单位应当配合，如实提供相关资料和信息，不得隐瞒、拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 34** When the human resources and social security administrative department conducts supervision and inspection of a for-profit human resources service organization, it may take the following measures:  
(1) enter the unit under inspection for inspection;  
(2) inquire with relevant persons, and review service records and other service information files;  
(3) require the unit under inspection to provide documents and materials related to the matters under inspection, and to provide explanations and clarifications;  
(4) collect relevant information and materials by means of recording, audio recording, video recording, photographing, or reproduction;  
(5) other measures prescribed by laws and regulations.  
When the human resources and social security administrative department conducts supervision and inspection, there shall be at least two inspectors, who shall present their law enforcement credentials, and shall keep confidential the trade secrets of the unit under inspection.  
The unit under inspection shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection lawfully conducted by the human resources and social security administrative department, truthfully provide relevant materials and information, and shall not conceal, refuse, or obstruct.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人力资源社会保障行政部门采取随机抽取检查对象、随机选派执法人员的方式实施监督检查。  
监督检查的情况应当及时向社会公布。其中，行政处罚、监督检查结果可以通过国家企业信用信息公示系统或者其他系统向社会公示。

**Article 35** The human resources and social security administrative department shall implement supervision and inspection by means of randomly selecting inspection targets and randomly assigning law enforcement personnel.  
The situation of supervision and inspection shall be promptly published to the public. Among them, administrative penalties and supervision and inspection results may be publicly disclosed to the public through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System or other systems.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经营性人力资源服务机构应当在规定期限内，向人力资源社会保障行政部门提交经营情况年度报告。人力资源社会保障行政部门可以依法公示或者引导经营性人力资源服务机构依法公示年度报告的有关内容。  
人力资源社会保障行政部门应当加强与市场监督管理等部门的信息共享。通过信息共享可以获取的信息，不得要求经营性人力资源服务机构重复提供。

**Article 36** A for-profit human resources service organization shall, within the prescribed period, submit an annual report on its business operations to the human resources and social security administrative department. The human resources and social security administrative department may, according to law, publicly disclose or guide the for-profit human resources service organization to publicly disclose relevant contents of the annual report according to law.  
The human resources and social security administrative department shall strengthen information sharing with the market supervision and administration department and other departments. Information that can be obtained through information sharing shall not be required to be provided repeatedly by the for-profit human resources service organization.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当加强人力资源市场诚信建设，把用人单位、个人和经营性人力资源服务机构的信用数据和失信情况等纳入市场诚信建设体系，建立守信激励和失信惩戒机制，实施信用分类监管。

**Article 37** The human resources and social security administrative department shall strengthen the integrity construction of the human resources market, incorporate the credit data and dishonesty information of employers, individuals, and for-profit human resources service organizations into the market integrity construction system, establish mechanisms for rewarding trustworthiness and punishing dishonesty, and implement credit-based classified supervision.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当按照国家有关规定，对公共人力资源服务机构进行监督管理。

**Article 38** The human resources and social security administrative department shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, conduct supervision and management of public human resources service organizations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　在人力资源服务机构中，根据中国共产党章程及有关规定，建立党的组织并开展活动，加强对流动党员的教育监督和管理服务。人力资源服务机构应当为中国共产党组织的活动提供必要条件。

**Article 39** In human resources service organizations, Party organizations shall be established and activities conducted in accordance with the Constitution of the Communist Party of China and relevant regulations, to strengthen the education, supervision, management, and services for mobile Party members. Human resources service organizations shall provide the necessary conditions for the activities of the Communist Party of China organizations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人力资源社会保障行政部门应当畅通对用人单位和人力资源服务机构的举报投诉渠道，依法及时处理有关举报投诉。

**Article 40** The human resources and social security administrative department shall keep open channels for reporting and complaining against employers and human resources service organizations, and shall handle relevant reports and complaints promptly according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　公安机关应当依法查处人力资源市场的违法犯罪行为，人力资源社会保障行政部门予以配合。

**Article 41** . Public security organs shall, according to law, investigate and punish illegal and criminal acts in the human resources market, and the human resources and social security administrative department shall cooperate.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)第一款规定，未经许可擅自从事职业中介活动的，由人力资源社会保障行政部门予以关闭或者责令停止从事职业中介活动；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)第二款规定，开展人力资源服务业务未备案，违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)、第二十一条规定，设立分支机构、办理变更或者注销登记未书面报告的，由人力资源社会保障行政部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** . Where any entity engages in job intermediary activities without permission in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 18 of these Regulations, the human resources and social security administrative department shall order it to close down or cease engaging in job intermediary activities; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where any entity, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 18 of these Regulations, carries out human resources service business without filing, or in violation of the provisions of Article 20 and Article 21 of these Regulations, establishes a branch office, undergoes a change or deregistration without a written report, the human resources and social security administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)、第二十七条、第二十八条、第二十九条、第三十条、第三十一条规定，发布的招聘信息不真实、不合法，未依法开展人力资源服务业务的，由人力资源社会保障行政部门责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销人力资源服务许可证；给个人造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法给予处罚。

**Article 43** . Where any entity, in violation of the provisions of Articles 24, 27, 28, 29, 30, and 31 of these Regulations, publishes recruitment information that is untrue or illegal, or fails to carry out human resources service business according to law, the human resources and social security administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, its human resources service permit shall be revoked; if damage is caused to individuals, it shall bear civil liability according to law. If other laws and administrative regulations are violated, the relevant competent authorities shall give punishment according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　未按照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定明示有关事项，未按照[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)规定建立健全内部制度或者保存服务台账，未按照[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)规定提交经营情况年度报告的，由人力资源社会保障行政部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。违反其他法律、行政法规的，由有关主管部门依法给予处罚。

**Article 44** . Where any entity fails to clearly indicate relevant matters as required in Article 32 of these Regulations, or fails to establish and improve internal systems or keep service records as required in Article 33 of these Regulations, or fails to submit an annual report on its business operations as required in Article 36 of these Regulations, the human resources and social security administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed. If other laws and administrative regulations are violated, the relevant competent authorities shall give punishment according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　公共人力资源服务机构违反本条例规定的，由上级主管机关责令改正；拒不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 45** . Where a public human resources service institution violates the provisions of these Regulations, the competent authority at a higher level shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　人力资源社会保障行政部门和有关主管部门及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的领导人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)不依法作出行政许可决定；  
(二)在办理行政许可或者备案、实施监督检查中，索取或者收受他人财物，或者谋取其他利益；  
(三)不依法履行监督职责或者监督不力，造成严重后果；  
(四)其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的情形。

**Article 46** . Where the human resources and social security administrative department and relevant competent authorities and their staff members have any of the following circumstances, the leaders directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to make an administrative license decision according to law;  
(2) demanding or accepting property from others, or seeking other benefits, in handling administrative licenses or filings, or in conducting supervision and inspection;  
(3) failing to perform supervision duties according to law or performing supervision duties inadequately, causing serious consequences;  
(4) other circumstances of abuse of power, neglect of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** . Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes an act against public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自2018年10月1日起施行。

**Article 48** . These Regulations shall come into force on October 1, 2018.
