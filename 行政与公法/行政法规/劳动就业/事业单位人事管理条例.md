# 事业单位人事管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2014-04-25  
**生效日期**：2014-07-01

> (2014年2月26日国务院第40次常务会议通过　2014年4月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第652号公布　自2014年7月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范事业单位的人事管理，保障事业单位工作人员的合法权益，建设高素质的事业单位工作人员队伍，促进公共服务发展，制定本条例。 第二条　事业单位人事管理，坚持党管干部、党管人才原则，全面准确贯彻民主、公开、竞争、择优方针。 国家对事业单位工作人员实行分级分类管理。 第三条　中央事业单位人事综合管理部门负责全国事业单位人事综合管理工作。 县级以上地方各级事业单位人事综合管理部门负责本辖区事业单位人事综合管理工作。 事业单位主管部门具体负责所属事业单位人事管理工作。 第四条　事业单位应当建立健全人事管理制度。 事业单位制定或者修改人事管理制度，应当通过职工代表大会或者其他形式听取工作人员意见。 第二章　岗位设置 第五条　国家建立事业单位岗位管理制度，明确岗位类别和等级。 第六条　事业单位根据职责任务和工作需要，按照国家有关规定设置岗位。 岗位应当具有明确的名称、职责任务、工作标准和任职条件。 第七条　事业单位拟订岗位设置方案，应当报人事综合管理部门备案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范事业单位的人事管理，保障事业单位工作人员的合法权益，建设高素质的事业单位工作人员队伍，促进公共服务发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating the personnel management of public institutions, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of staff members of public institutions, building a high-quality team of staff members of public institutions, and promoting the development of public services.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　事业单位人事管理，坚持党管干部、党管人才原则，全面准确贯彻民主、公开、竞争、择优方针。  
国家对事业单位工作人员实行分级分类管理。

**Article 2** The personnel management of public institutions shall adhere to the principles of the Party supervising officials and the Party supervising personnel, and shall fully and accurately implement the policy of democracy, openness, competition, and merit selection.  
The State shall implement hierarchical and categorized management of staff members of public institutions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中央事业单位人事综合管理部门负责全国事业单位人事综合管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级事业单位人事综合管理部门负责本辖区事业单位人事综合管理工作。  
事业单位主管部门具体负责所属事业单位人事管理工作。

**Article 3** The comprehensive personnel management department for public institutions of the Central Government shall be responsible for the comprehensive personnel management of public institutions nationwide.  
The comprehensive personnel management departments for public institutions at or above the county level in local governments shall be responsible for the comprehensive personnel management of public institutions within their respective jurisdictions.  
The competent departments of public institutions shall be specifically responsible for the personnel management of the public institutions under their administration.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　事业单位应当建立健全人事管理制度。  
事业单位制定或者修改人事管理制度，应当通过职工代表大会或者其他形式听取工作人员意见。

**Article 4** Public institutions shall establish and improve personnel management systems.  
Where a public institution formulates or revises a personnel management system, it shall solicit opinions from staff members through the staff congress or other forms.

### 第二章　岗位设置

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立事业单位岗位管理制度，明确岗位类别和等级。

**Article 5** The State shall establish a post management system for public institutions, specifying the categories and grades of posts.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　事业单位根据职责任务和工作需要，按照国家有关规定设置岗位。  
岗位应当具有明确的名称、职责任务、工作标准和任职条件。

**Article 6** Public institutions shall set up posts based on their functions, tasks, and work needs, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Each post shall have a clear name, functions, tasks, work standards, and qualifications for holding the post.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　事业单位拟订岗位设置方案，应当报人事综合管理部门备案。

**Article 7** When a public institution drafts a post setup plan, it shall report it to the comprehensive personnel management department for recordation.

### 第三章　公开招聘和竞聘上岗

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　事业单位新聘用工作人员，应当面向社会公开招聘。但是，国家政策性安置、按照人事管理权限由上级任命、涉密岗位等人员除外。

**Article 8** When a public institution newly employs staff members, it shall conduct open recruitment to the public, except for personnel subject to national policy-based placement, personnel appointed by higher authorities according to the personnel management authority, personnel for confidential posts, and other similar personnel.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　事业单位公开招聘工作人员按照下列程序进行：  
(一)制定公开招聘方案；  
(二)公布招聘岗位、资格条件等招聘信息；  
(三)审查应聘人员资格条件；  
(四)考试、考察；  
(五)体检；  
(六)公示拟聘人员名单；  
(七)订立聘用合同，办理聘用手续。

**Article 9** The open recruitment of staff members by public institutions shall be carried out according to the following procedures:  
(1) formulating an open recruitment plan;  
(2) publishing recruitment information such as the posts and qualifications for application;  
(3) reviewing the qualifications of applicants;  
(4) conducting examinations and assessments;  
(5) conducting physical examinations;  
(6) publicizing the list of proposed appointees; and  
(7) concluding an employment contract and going through the employment formalities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　事业单位内部产生岗位人选，需要竞聘上岗的，按照下列程序进行：  
(一)制定竞聘上岗方案；  
(二)在本单位公布竞聘岗位、资格条件、聘期等信息；  
(三)审查竞聘人员资格条件；  
(四)考评；  
(五)在本单位公示拟聘人员名单；  
(六)办理聘任手续。

**Article 10** Where candidates for posts within a public institution are generated internally and competitive selection for appointment is required, the following procedures shall be followed:  
(1) formulating a competitive selection plan;  
(2) publishing information such as the posts, qualifications for application, and term of appointment within the institution;  
(3) reviewing the qualifications of candidates;  
(4) conducting evaluations;  
(5) publicizing the list of proposed appointees within the institution; and  
(6) going through the appointment formalities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　事业单位工作人员可以按照国家有关规定进行交流。

**Article 11** The staff members of public institutions may conduct exchanges in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第四章　聘用合同

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　事业单位与工作人员订立的聘用合同，期限一般不低于3年。

**Article 12** The term of an employment contract concluded between a public institution and its staff member shall generally not be less than three years.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　初次就业的工作人员与事业单位订立的聘用合同期限3年以上的，试用期为12个月。

**Article 13** Where a staff member employed for the first time concludes an employment contract with a public institution with a term of three years or more, the probation period shall be 12 months.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　事业单位工作人员在本单位连续工作满10年且距法定退休年龄不足10年，提出订立聘用至退休的合同的，事业单位应当与其订立聘用至退休的合同。

**Article 14** Where a staff member of a public institution who has continuously worked for the institution for a full period of ten years and is less than ten years away from the statutory retirement age proposes to conclude an employment contract until retirement, the public institution shall conclude an employment contract until retirement with him or her.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　事业单位工作人员连续旷工超过15个工作日，或者1年内累计旷工超过30个工作日的，事业单位可以解除聘用合同。

**Article 15** Where a staff member of a public institution is absent from work without leave for more than 15 consecutive working days, or for an accumulated total of more than 30 working days within one year, the public institution may dissolve the employment contract.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　事业单位工作人员年度考核不合格且不同意调整工作岗位，或者连续两年年度考核不合格的，事业单位提前30日书面通知，可以解除聘用合同。

**Article 16** Where a staff member of a public institution fails the annual assessment and does not agree to the adjustment of his or her work post, or fails the annual assessment for two consecutive years, the public institution may, by giving 30 days' prior written notice, dissolve the employment contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　事业单位工作人员提前30日书面通知事业单位，可以解除聘用合同。但是，双方对解除聘用合同另有约定的除外。

**Article 17** A staff member of a public institution may dissolve the employment contract by giving 30 days' prior written notice to the public institution, unless otherwise agreed by the parties on the dissolution of the employment contract.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　事业单位工作人员受到开除处分的，解除聘用合同。

**Article 18** Where a staff member of a public institution receives a sanction of dismissal, the employment contract shall be dissolved.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　自聘用合同依法解除、终止之日起，事业单位与被解除、终止聘用合同人员的人事关系终止。

**Article 19** From the date on which the employment contract is lawfully dissolved or terminated, the personnel relationship between the public institution and the person whose employment contract is dissolved or terminated shall be terminated.

### 第五章　考核和培训

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　事业单位应当根据聘用合同规定的岗位职责任务，全面考核工作人员的表现，重点考核工作绩效。考核应当听取服务对象的意见和评价。

**Article 20** A public institution shall, according to the post duties and tasks specified in the employment contract, comprehensively assess the performance of its staff members, with emphasis on work performance. In the assessment, the opinions and evaluations of the persons served shall be solicited.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　考核分为平时考核、年度考核和聘期考核。  
年度考核的结果可以分为优秀、合格、基本合格和不合格等档次，聘期考核的结果可以分为合格和不合格等档次。

**Article 21** Assessments shall include routine assessments, annual assessments, and employment period assessments.  
The results of annual assessments may be classified into grades such as excellent, qualified, basically qualified, and unqualified, and the results of employment period assessments may be classified into grades such as qualified and unqualified.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　考核结果作为调整事业单位工作人员岗位、工资以及续订聘用合同的依据。

**Article 22** The results of assessments shall serve as the basis for adjusting the posts and salaries of staff members of public institutions and for renewing their employment contracts.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　事业单位应当根据不同岗位的要求，编制工作人员培训计划，对工作人员进行分级分类培训。  
工作人员应当按照所在单位的要求，参加岗前培训、在岗培训、转岗培训和为完成特定任务的专项培训。

**Article 23** Public institutions shall, in accordance with the requirements of different posts, formulate training plans for staff members and provide them with training at different levels and by different categories.  
Staff members shall, in accordance with the requirements of their employing units, participate in pre-service training, on-the-job training, transfer training, and special training for completing specific tasks.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　培训经费按照国家有关规定列支。

**Article 24** Training funds shall be appropriated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　奖励和处分

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　事业单位工作人员或者集体有下列情形之一的，给予奖励：  
(一)长期服务基层，爱岗敬业，表现突出的；  
(二)在执行国家重要任务、应对重大突发事件中表现突出的；  
(三)在工作中有重大发明创造、技术革新的；  
(四)在培养人才、传播先进文化中作出突出贡献的；  
(五)有其他突出贡献的。

**Article 25** A staff member or a collective of a public institution shall be given rewards under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Having long served at the grassroots level, shown dedication to work, and achieved outstanding performance;  
(2) Having shown outstanding performance in carrying out important national tasks or responding to major emergencies;  
(3) Having made significant inventions, creations, or technical innovations in work;  
(4) Having made outstanding contributions in cultivating talents or disseminating advanced culture;  
(5) Having made other outstanding contributions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　奖励坚持精神奖励与物质奖励相结合、以精神奖励为主的原则。

**Article 26** Rewards shall adhere to the principle of combining spiritual rewards with material rewards, with spiritual rewards as the main focus.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　奖励分为嘉奖、记功、记大功、授予荣誉称号。

**Article 27** Rewards shall be classified into commendation, recording of merit, recording of major merit, and conferment of honorary titles.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　事业单位工作人员有下列行为之一的，给予处分：  
(一)损害国家声誉和利益的；  
(二)失职渎职的；  
(三)利用工作之便谋取不正当利益的；  
(四)挥霍、浪费国家资财的；  
(五)严重违反职业道德、社会公德的；  
(六)其他严重违反纪律的。

**Article 28** A staff member of a public institution shall be given a sanction under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Damaging the reputation and interests of the State;  
(2) Being derelict of duty or committing malfeasance;  
(3) Taking advantage of his or her position to seek improper benefits;  
(4) Squandering or wasting State assets;  
(5) Seriously violating professional ethics or social morality;  
(6) Otherwise seriously violating discipline.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　处分分为警告、记过、降低岗位等级或者撤职、开除。  
受处分的期间为：警告，6个月；记过，12个月；降低岗位等级或者撤职，24个月。

**Article 29** Sanctions shall be classified into warning, demerit recording, demotion in post grade or removal from post, and dismissal.  
The duration of a sanction shall be: warning, 6 months; demerit recording, 12 months; demotion in post grade or removal from post, 24 months.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　给予工作人员处分，应当事实清楚、证据确凿、定性准确、处理恰当、程序合法、手续完备。

**Article 30** When imposing a sanction on a staff member, the facts shall be clear, the evidence conclusive, the nature accurately determined, the disposition appropriate, the procedures lawful, and the formalities complete.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　工作人员受开除以外的处分，在受处分期间没有再发生违纪行为的，处分期满后，由处分决定单位解除处分并以书面形式通知本人。

**Article 31** Where a staff member receives a sanction other than dismissal and does not commit any further disciplinary violation during the sanction period, upon expiry of the sanction period, the entity that imposed the sanction shall lift the sanction and notify the staff member in writing.

### 第七章　工资福利和社会保险

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家建立激励与约束相结合的事业单位工资制度。  
事业单位工作人员工资包括基本工资、绩效工资和津贴补贴。  
事业单位工资分配应当结合不同行业事业单位特点，体现岗位职责、工作业绩、实际贡献等因素。

**Article 32** The State shall establish a wage system for public institutions that combines incentives and constraints.  
The wages of staff members of public institutions include basic wages, performance-based wages, and allowances and subsidies.  
Wage distribution in public institutions shall take into account the characteristics of public institutions in different industries, and reflect factors such as job responsibilities, work performance, and actual contributions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立事业单位工作人员工资的正常增长机制。  
事业单位工作人员的工资水平应当与国民经济发展相协调、与社会进步相适应。

**Article 33** The State shall establish a normal growth mechanism for the wages of staff members of public institutions.  
The wage level of staff members of public institutions shall be coordinated with national economic development and compatible with social progress.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　事业单位工作人员享受国家规定的福利待遇。  
事业单位执行国家规定的工时制度和休假制度。

**Article 34** Staff members of public institutions shall enjoy the welfare benefits prescribed by the State.  
Public institutions shall implement the working hours system and leave system prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　事业单位及其工作人员依法参加社会保险，工作人员依法享受社会保险待遇。

**Article 35** Public institutions and their staff members shall participate in social insurance according to law, and the staff members shall enjoy social insurance benefits according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　事业单位工作人员符合国家规定退休条件的，应当退休。

**Article 36** A staff member of a public institution who meets the retirement conditions prescribed by the State shall retire.

### 第八章　人事争议处理

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　事业单位工作人员与所在单位发生人事争议的，依照《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》等有关规定处理。

**Article 37** Where a personnel dispute arises between a staff member of a public institution and the institution, it shall be handled in accordance with the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　事业单位工作人员对涉及本人的考核结果、处分决定等不服的，可以按照国家有关规定申请复核、提出申诉。

**Article 38** Where a staff member of a public institution disagrees with an assessment result, a sanction decision, or the like that involves him or her, the staff member may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, apply for review or file a petition.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　负有事业单位聘用、考核、奖励、处分、人事争议处理等职责的人员履行职责，有下列情形之一的，应当回避：  
(一)与本人有利害关系的；  
(二)与本人近亲属有利害关系的；  
(三)其他可能影响公正履行职责的。

**Article 39** Where personnel who are responsible for duties such as employment, assessment, reward, sanction, and handling of personnel disputes in public institutions perform their duties under any of the following circumstances, they shall recuse themselves:  
(1) Where there is a conflict of interest with themselves;  
(2) Where there is a conflict of interest with their close relatives;  
(3) Other circumstances that may affect the impartial performance of their duties.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对事业单位人事管理工作中的违法违纪行为，任何单位或者个人可以向事业单位人事综合管理部门、主管部门或者监察机关投诉、举报，有关部门和机关应当及时调查处理。

**Article 40** With regard to any illegal or disciplinary act in the personnel management of public institutions, any entity or individual may file a complaint or report with the comprehensive personnel management department of public institutions, the competent department, or the supervisory authority, and the relevant department or authority shall promptly investigate and handle the matter.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　事业单位违反本条例规定的，由县级以上事业单位人事综合管理部门或者主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 41** Where a public institution violates these Regulations, the comprehensive personnel management department or the competent department of public institutions at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对事业单位工作人员的人事处理违反本条例规定给当事人造成名誉损害的，应当赔礼道歉、恢复名誉、消除影响；造成经济损失的，依法给予赔偿。

**Article 42** Where a personnel action against a staff member of a public institution violates these Regulations and causes damage to the reputation of the party, the institution shall make an apology, restore the reputation, and eliminate the adverse effects; where economic losses are caused, compensation shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　事业单位人事综合管理部门和主管部门的工作人员在事业单位人事管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where staff members of the comprehensive personnel management department and the competent departments of public institutions abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the personnel management work of public institutions, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2014年7月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2014.
