# 对外承包工程管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2008-09-01

> (2008年7月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第527号公布　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范对外承包工程，促进对外承包工程健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称对外承包工程，是指中国的企业或者其他单位(以下统称单位)承包境外建设工程项目(以下简称工程项目)的活动。 第三条　国家鼓励和支持开展对外承包工程，提高对外承包工程的质量和水平。 国务院有关部门制定和完善促进对外承包工程的政策措施，建立、健全对外承包工程服务体系和风险保障机制。 第四条　开展对外承包工程，应当维护国家利益和社会公共利益，保障外派人员的合法权益。 开展对外承包工程，应当遵守工程项目所在国家或者地区的法律，信守合同，尊重当地的风俗习惯，注重生态环境保护，促进当地经济社会发展。 第五条　国务院商务主管部门负责全国对外承包工程的监督管理，国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责与对外承包工程有关的管理工作。 国务院建设主管部门组织协调建设企业参与对外承包工程。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府商务主管部门负责本行政区域内对外承包工程的监督管理。 第六条　有关对外承包工程的协会、商会按照章程为其成员提供与对外承包工程有关的信息、培训等方面的服务，依法制定行业规范，发挥协调和自律作用，维护公平竞争和成员利益。 第二章　对外承包工程活动 第七条　国务院商务主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门建立对外承包工程安全风险评估机制，定期发布有关国家和地区安全状况的评估结果，及时提供预警信息，指导对外承包工程的单位做好安全风险防范。 第八条　对外承包工程的单位不得以不正当的低价承揽工程项目、串通投标，不得进行商业贿赂。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范对外承包工程，促进对外承包工程健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating foreign-contracted projects and promoting the healthy development of foreign-contracted projects.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称对外承包工程，是指中国的企业或者其他单位(以下统称单位)承包境外建设工程项目(以下简称工程项目)的活动。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "foreign-contracted projects" means the activities of Chinese enterprises or other entities (hereinafter collectively referred to as "entities") contracting for overseas construction projects (hereinafter referred to as "projects").

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励和支持开展对外承包工程，提高对外承包工程的质量和水平。  
国务院有关部门制定和完善促进对外承包工程的政策措施，建立、健全对外承包工程服务体系和风险保障机制。

**Article 3** The State encourages and supports the undertaking of foreign-contracted projects so as to improve the quality and level thereof.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall formulate and improve policies and measures for promoting foreign-contracted projects, and establish and improve the service system and risk guarantee mechanism for foreign-contracted projects.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　开展对外承包工程，应当维护国家利益和社会公共利益，保障外派人员的合法权益。  
开展对外承包工程，应当遵守工程项目所在国家或者地区的法律，信守合同，尊重当地的风俗习惯，注重生态环境保护，促进当地经济社会发展。

**Article 4** In undertaking foreign-contracted projects, entities shall safeguard national interests and public interests, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of dispatched personnel.  
In undertaking foreign-contracted projects, entities shall abide by the laws of the country or region where the project is located, keep contracts, respect local customs, pay attention to ecological and environmental protection, and promote local economic and social development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院商务主管部门负责全国对外承包工程的监督管理，国务院有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责与对外承包工程有关的管理工作。  
国务院建设主管部门组织协调建设企业参与对外承包工程。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府商务主管部门负责本行政区域内对外承包工程的监督管理。

**Article 5** The competent department of commerce of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of foreign-contracted projects nationwide, and the relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration related to foreign-contracted projects within their respective functions and duties.  
The competent department of construction of the State Council shall organize and coordinate construction enterprises to participate in foreign-contracted projects.  
The competent departments of commerce of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of foreign-contracted projects within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　有关对外承包工程的协会、商会按照章程为其成员提供与对外承包工程有关的信息、培训等方面的服务，依法制定行业规范，发挥协调和自律作用，维护公平竞争和成员利益。

**Article 6** Associations and chambers of commerce relating to foreign-contracted projects shall, in accordance with their articles of association, provide their members with services such as information and training relating to foreign-contracted projects, formulate industry norms according to law, play a role in coordination and self-discipline, and maintain fair competition and the interests of members.

### 第二章　对外承包工程活动

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院商务主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门建立对外承包工程安全风险评估机制，定期发布有关国家和地区安全状况的评估结果，及时提供预警信息，指导对外承包工程的单位做好安全风险防范。

**Article 7** The competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish a security risk assessment mechanism for foreign-contracted projects, regularly release the assessment results on the security situation of relevant countries and regions, provide timely early warning information, and guide the entities undertaking foreign-contracted projects to properly prevent security risks.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对外承包工程的单位不得以不正当的低价承揽工程项目、串通投标，不得进行商业贿赂。

**Article 8** Entities undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall not contract projects at improperly low prices, engage in bid rigging, or commit commercial bribery.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对外承包工程的单位应当与境外工程项目发包人订立书面合同，明确双方的权利和义务，并按照合同约定履行义务。

**Article 9** Entities undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall enter into a written contract with the project owner of the overseas project, specify the rights and obligations of both parties, and perform the obligations in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对外承包工程的单位应当加强对工程质量和安全生产的管理，建立、健全并严格执行工程质量和安全生产管理的规章制度。  
对外承包工程的单位将工程项目分包的，应当与分包单位订立专门的工程质量和安全生产管理协议，或者在分包合同中约定各自的工程质量和安全生产管理责任，并对分包单位的工程质量和安全生产工作统一协调、管理。  
对外承包工程的单位不得将工程项目分包给不具备国家规定的相应资质的单位；工程项目的建筑施工部分不得分包给未依法取得安全生产许可证的境内建筑施工企业。  
分包单位不得将工程项目转包或者再分包。对外承包工程的单位应当在分包合同中明确约定分包单位不得将工程项目转包或者再分包，并负责监督。

**Article 10** Entities undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall strengthen the management of project quality and work safety, establish, improve and strictly implement the rules and regulations for project quality and work safety management.  
Where an entity undertaking foreign-contracted projects subcontracts a project, it shall enter into a special agreement on project quality and work safety management with the subcontracting entity, or specify the respective responsibilities for project quality and work safety management in the subcontracting contract, and shall coordinate and manage the project quality and work safety of the subcontracting entity in a unified manner.  
An entity undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall not subcontract a project to an entity that does not have the corresponding qualifications as prescribed by the State; the construction part of a project shall not be subcontracted to a domestic construction enterprise that has not obtained a work safety permit according to law.  
A subcontracting entity shall not assign or re-subcontract a project. An entity undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall clearly stipulate in the subcontracting contract that the subcontracting entity shall not assign or re-subcontract the project, and shall be responsible for supervision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　从事对外承包工程外派人员中介服务的机构应当取得国务院商务主管部门的许可，并按照国务院商务主管部门的规定从事对外承包工程外派人员中介服务。  
对外承包工程的单位通过中介机构招用外派人员的，应当选择依法取得许可并合法经营的中介机构，不得通过未依法取得许可或者有重大违法行为的中介机构招用外派人员。

**Article 11** Institutions providing intermediary services for dispatching personnel to foreign-contracted projects shall obtain permission from the competent department of commerce of the State Council and engage in such intermediary services in accordance with the regulations of the competent department of commerce of the State Council.  
Where a unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects recruits dispatched personnel through an intermediary institution, it shall select an intermediary institution that has lawfully obtained permission and operates legally, and shall not recruit dispatched personnel through an intermediary institution that has not lawfully obtained permission or has committed major illegal acts.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对外承包工程的单位应当依法与其招用的外派人员订立劳动合同，按照合同约定向外派人员提供工作条件和支付报酬，履行用人单位义务。

**Article 12** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall, according to law, conclude labor contracts with the dispatched personnel it recruits, provide working conditions and pay remuneration to the dispatched personnel in accordance with the contract, and perform the obligations of the employer.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对外承包工程的单位应当有专门的安全管理机构和人员，负责保护外派人员的人身和财产安全，并根据所承包工程项目的具体情况，制定保护外派人员人身和财产安全的方案，落实所需经费。  
对外承包工程的单位应当根据工程项目所在国家或者地区的安全状况，有针对性地对外派人员进行安全防范教育和应急知识培训，增强外派人员的安全防范意识和自我保护能力。

**Article 13** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall have dedicated safety management institutions and personnel responsible for protecting the personal and property safety of dispatched personnel, and shall, based on the specific circumstances of the contracted project, formulate a plan for protecting the personal and property safety of dispatched personnel and allocate the necessary funds.  
A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall, in light of the security situation of the country or region where the project is located, provide targeted safety prevention education and emergency knowledge training to dispatched personnel to enhance their safety prevention awareness and self-protection capabilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对外承包工程的单位应当为外派人员购买境外人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 14** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall purchase overseas personal accident insurance for dispatched personnel.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对外承包工程的单位应当按照国务院商务主管部门和国务院财政部门的规定，及时存缴备用金。  
前款规定的备用金，用于支付对外承包工程的单位拒绝承担或者无力承担的下列费用：  
(一)外派人员的报酬；  
(二)因发生突发事件，外派人员回国或者接受其他紧急救助所需费用；  
(三)依法应当对外派人员的损失进行赔偿所需费用。

**Article 15** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the finance department of the State Council, timely deposit a reserve fund.  
The reserve fund as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be used to pay the following expenses that the unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects refuses or is unable to bear:  
(1) remuneration of dispatched personnel;  
(2) expenses necessary for dispatched personnel to return to China or receive other emergency relief in the event of an emergency;  
(3) expenses necessary for compensation for losses of dispatched personnel according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对外承包工程的单位与境外工程项目发包人订立合同后，应当及时向中国驻该工程项目所在国使馆(领馆)报告。  
对外承包工程的单位应当接受中国驻该工程项目所在国使馆(领馆)在突发事件防范、工程质量、安全生产及外派人员保护等方面的指导。

**Article 16** After concluding a contract with the overseas project owner, a unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall promptly report to the Chinese embassy (consulate) in the country where the project is located.  
A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall accept the guidance of the Chinese embassy (consulate) in the country where the project is located in such aspects as emergency prevention, project quality, production safety, and protection of dispatched personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对外承包工程的单位应当制定突发事件应急预案；在境外发生突发事件时，应当及时、妥善处理，并立即向中国驻该工程项目所在国使馆(领馆)和国内有关主管部门报告。  
国务院商务主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门，按照预防和处置并重的原则，建立、健全对外承包工程突发事件预警、防范和应急处置机制，制定对外承包工程突发事件应急预案。

**Article 17** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall formulate emergency response plans; when an emergency occurs abroad, it shall promptly and properly handle it and immediately report to the Chinese embassy (consulate) in the country where the project is located and the relevant competent authorities in China.  
The competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish and improve the early warning, prevention, and emergency response mechanism for emergencies in foreign-contracted projects, and formulate emergency response plans for emergencies in foreign-contracted projects, following the principle of equal emphasis on prevention and handling.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对外承包工程的单位应当定期向商务主管部门报告其开展对外承包工程的情况，并按照国务院商务主管部门和国务院统计部门的规定，向有关部门报送业务统计资料。

**Article 18** A unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects shall regularly report to the competent department of commerce on its foreign-contracted project operations, and submit business statistical data to the relevant departments in accordance with the regulations of the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the statistics department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院商务主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门建立对外承包工程信息收集、通报制度，向对外承包工程的单位无偿提供信息服务。  
有关部门应当在货物通关、人员出入境等方面，依法为对外承包工程的单位提供快捷、便利的服务。

**Article 19** The competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish a system for collecting and disseminating information on foreign-contracted projects, and provide information services to units undertaking foreign-contracted projects free of charge.  
The relevant departments shall, in accordance with law, provide fast and convenient services to units undertaking foreign-contracted projects in such aspects as customs clearance of goods and entry and exit of personnel.

### 第三章　法律责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对外承包工程的单位有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对其主要负责人处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，商务主管部门可以禁止其在1年以上3年以下的期限内对外承包新的工程项目；造成重大工程质量问题、发生较大事故以上生产安全事故或者造成其他严重后果的，建设主管部门或者其他有关主管部门可以降低其资质等级或者吊销其资质证书：  
(一)未建立并严格执行工程质量和安全生产管理的规章制度的；  
(二)没有专门的安全管理机构和人员负责保护外派人员的人身和财产安全，或者未根据所承包工程项目的具体情况制定保护外派人员人身和财产安全的方案并落实所需经费的；  
(三)未对外派人员进行安全防范教育和应急知识培训的；  
(四)未制定突发事件应急预案，或者在境外发生突发事件，未及时、妥善处理的。

**Article 20** Where a unit undertaking foreign-contracted projects commits any of the following circumstances, the competent department of commerce shall order it to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on its principal responsible person; if it refuses to make corrections, the competent department of commerce may prohibit it from undertaking new foreign-contracted projects within a period of not less than one year but not more than three years; if a major project quality problem, a relatively serious or above production safety accident, or other serious consequences are caused, the competent construction department or other relevant competent departments may lower its qualification grade or revoke its qualification certificate:  
(1) failing to establish and strictly implement rules and regulations on project quality and production safety management;  
(2) having no dedicated safety management institutions and personnel responsible for protecting the personal and property safety of dispatched personnel, or failing to formulate a plan for protecting the personal and property safety of dispatched personnel and allocate the necessary funds based on the specific circumstances of the contracted project;  
(3) failing to provide safety prevention education and emergency knowledge training to dispatched personnel;  
(4) failing to formulate emergency response plans, or failing to promptly and properly handle an emergency occurring abroad.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对外承包工程的单位有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正，处15万元以上30万元以下的罚款，对其主要负责人处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，商务主管部门可以禁止其在2年以上5年以下的期限内对外承包新的工程项目；造成重大工程质量问题、发生较大事故以上生产安全事故或者造成其他严重后果的，建设主管部门或者其他有关主管部门可以降低其资质等级或者吊销其资质证书：  
(一)以不正当的低价承揽工程项目、串通投标或者进行商业贿赂的；  
(二)未与分包单位订立专门的工程质量和安全生产管理协议，或者未在分包合同中约定各自的工程质量和安全生产管理责任，或者未对分包单位的工程质量和安全生产工作统一协调、管理的；  
(三)将工程项目分包给不具备国家规定的相应资质的单位，或者将工程项目的建筑施工部分分包给未依法取得安全生产许可证的境内建筑施工企业的；  
(四)未在分包合同中明确约定分包单位不得将工程项目转包或者再分包的。  
分包单位将其承包的工程项目转包或者再分包的，由建设主管部门责令改正，依照前款规定的数额对分包单位及其主要负责人处以罚款；造成重大工程质量问题，或者发生较大事故以上生产安全事故的，建设主管部门或者其他有关主管部门可以降低其资质等级或者吊销其资质证书。

**Article 21** Where a unit contracting for foreign projects commits any of the following acts, the competent commerce department shall order it to correct and impose a fine of not less than RMB 150,000 yuan but not more than RMB 300,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan on its principal responsible person; if it refuses to correct, the competent commerce department may prohibit it from contracting for new foreign projects within a period of not less than two years but not more than five years; if it causes a major project quality problem, a relatively serious or above production safety accident, or other serious consequences, the competent construction department or other relevant competent department may downgrade its qualification grade or revoke its qualification certificate:  
(1) winning a project by unfairly low price, colluding in bidding, or engaging in commercial bribery;  
(2) failing to conclude a special agreement on project quality and safety management with the subcontractor, or failing to specify the respective project quality and safety management responsibilities in the subcontract, or failing to coordinate and manage the project quality and safety work of subcontractors in a unified manner;  
(3) subcontracting a project to a unit that does not possess the corresponding qualifications prescribed by the state, or subcontracting the construction part of the project to a domestic construction enterprise that has not obtained a work safety permit according to law;  
(4) failing to expressly stipulate in the subcontract that the subcontractor shall not subcontract or further subcontract the project.  
Where a subcontractor subcontracts or further subcontracts the project it contracted, the competent construction department shall order it to correct and impose a fine on the subcontractor and its principal responsible person according to the amounts specified in the preceding paragraph; if it causes a major project quality problem, or a relatively serious or above production safety accident, the competent construction department or other relevant competent department may downgrade its qualification grade or revoke its qualification certificate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对外承包工程的单位有下列情形之一的，由商务主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，对其主要负责人处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款：  
(一)与境外工程项目发包人订立合同后，未及时向中国驻该工程项目所在国使馆(领馆)报告的；  
(二)在境外发生突发事件，未立即向中国驻该工程项目所在国使馆(领馆)和国内有关主管部门报告的；  
(三)未定期向商务主管部门报告其开展对外承包工程的情况，或者未按照规定向有关部门报送业务统计资料的。

**Article 22** Where a unit contracting for foreign projects commits any of the following acts, the competent commerce department shall order it to correct and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to correct, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on its principal responsible person:  
(1) failing to timely report to the Chinese embassy (consulate) in the country where the project is located after concluding a contract with the foreign project owner;  
(2) failing to immediately report to the Chinese embassy (consulate) in the country where the project is located and the relevant domestic competent department in the event of an emergency abroad;  
(3) failing to periodically report to the competent commerce department on its foreign project contracting activities, or failing to submit business statistics to the relevant department in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对外承包工程的单位通过未依法取得许可或者有重大违法行为的中介机构招用外派人员，或者不依照本条例规定为外派人员购买境外人身意外伤害保险，或者未按照规定存缴备用金的，由商务主管部门责令限期改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对其主要负责人处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，商务主管部门可以禁止其在1年以上3年以下的期限内对外承包新的工程项目。  
未取得国务院商务主管部门的许可，擅自从事对外承包工程外派人员中介服务的，由国务院商务主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对其主要负责人处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 23** Where a unit contracting for foreign projects recruits and sends personnel abroad through an intermediary that has not obtained a license according to law or has major illegal acts, or fails to purchase overseas personal accident insurance for the dispatched personnel in accordance with these Regulations, or fails to deposit and pay the reserve fund according to the provisions, the competent commerce department shall order it to correct within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan on its principal responsible person; if it fails to correct within the time limit, the competent commerce department may prohibit it from contracting for new foreign projects within a period of not less than one year but not more than three years.  
Where an entity, without obtaining a license from the competent commerce department of the State Council, engages in intermediary services for dispatching personnel for foreign project contracting, the competent commerce department of the State Council shall order it to correct and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on its principal responsible person.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　商务主管部门、建设主管部门和其他有关部门的工作人员在对外承包工程监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 24** Where a staff member of the competent commerce department, the competent construction department or other relevant department abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in irregularities for personal gain in the supervision and administration of foreign project contracting, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对外承包工程涉及的货物进出口、技术进出口、人员出入境、海关以及税收、外汇等事项，依照有关法律、行政法规和国家有关规定办理。

**Article 25** Matters concerning the import and export of goods, import and export of technology, entry and exit of personnel, customs, taxation, foreign exchange, and other matters involved in foreign project contracting shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对外承包工程的单位以投标、议标方式参与报价金额在国务院商务主管部门和国务院财政部门等有关部门规定标准以上的工程项目的，其银行保函的出具等事项，依照国务院商务主管部门和国务院财政部门等有关部门的规定办理。

**Article 26** Where a unit contracting for foreign projects participates, by tendering or negotiated bidding, in a project with a quotation amount above the standards prescribed by the competent commerce department of the State Council, the finance department of the State Council and other relevant departments, matters such as the issuance of its bank guarantee shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the competent commerce department of the State Council, the finance department of the State Council and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对外承包工程的单位承包特定工程项目，或者在国务院商务主管部门会同外交部等有关部门确定的特定国家或者地区承包工程项目的，应当经国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门批准。

**Article 27** Where a unit contracting for foreign projects contracts a specific project, or contracts a project in a specific country or region determined by the competent commerce department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and other relevant departments, it shall obtain approval from the competent commerce department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　中国内地的单位在香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区承包工程项目，参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 28** Where a unit from the mainland of China contracts a project in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region, these Regulations shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　中国政府对外援建的工程项目的实施及其管理，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 29** The implementation and management of projects constructed by the Chinese government as foreign aid shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2008年9月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2008.
