# 中国公民因私事往来香港地区或者澳门地区的暂行管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1986-12-25  
**生效日期**：1986-12-25

> (1986年12月3日国务院批准　1986年12月25日公安部公布　自公布之日起施行)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法》第十七条的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法适用于内地公民因私事往来香港地区(下称香港)或者澳门地区(下称澳门)以及港澳同胞来往内地。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to Mainland citizens traveling to and from Hong Kong (hereinafter referred to as Hong Kong) or Macao (hereinafter referred to as Macao) for private affairs, and to compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao traveling to and from the Mainland.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　内地公民因私事前往香港、澳门，凭我国公安机关签发的前往港澳通行证或者往来港澳通行证从指定的口岸通行；返回内地也可以从其他对外开放的口岸通行。  
指定的口岸：往香港是深圳，往澳门是拱北。

**Article 3** Mainland citizens traveling to Hong Kong or Macao for private affairs shall pass through designated ports by producing the Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao or the Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao issued by the public security organs of the State; on returning to the Mainland, they may also pass through other ports open to the public.  
Designated ports: for Hong Kong, Shenzhen; for Macao, Gongbei.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　港澳同胞来往于香港、澳门与内地之间，凭我国公安机关签发的港澳同胞回乡证或者入出境通行证，从中国对外开放的口岸通行。

**Article 4** Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao traveling between Hong Kong, Macao and the Mainland shall pass through ports open to the public of China by producing the Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots or the Entry-Exit Permit issued by the public security organs of the State.

### 第二章　内地公民前往香港、澳门

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　内地公民因私事前往香港、澳门定居，实行定额审批的办法，以利于维护和保持香港和澳门的经济繁荣和社会稳定。

**Article 5** A quota approval system shall be implemented for Mainland citizens going to Hong Kong or Macao for settlement due to private affairs, so as to maintain and sustain the economic prosperity and social stability of Hong Kong and Macao.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　内地公民因私事前往香港、澳门，须向户口所在地的市、县公安局出入境管理部门提出申请。

**Article 6** Mainland citizens going to Hong Kong or Macao for private affairs shall submit an application to the exit and entry administration department of the municipal or county public security organ at the place of their household registration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有下列情形之一的，可以申请前往香港、澳门定居：  
(一)夫妻一方定居香港、澳门，分居多年的；  
(二)定居香港、澳门的父母年老体弱，必须由内地子女前往照料的；  
(三)内地无依无靠的老人和儿童须投靠在香港、澳门的直系亲属和近亲属的；  
(四)定居香港、澳门直系亲属的产业无人继承，必须由内地子女去定居才能继承的；  
(五)有其他特殊情况必须去定居的。

**Article 7** An application may be made to go to Hong Kong or Macao for settlement in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where one spouse is settled in Hong Kong or Macao and the couple have been separated for many years;  
(2) Where parents settled in Hong Kong or Macao are old and infirm and must be taken care of by Mainland children;  
(3) Where elderly persons or children in the Mainland without support must seek refuge with their lineal relatives or close relatives in Hong Kong or Macao;  
(4) Where property of lineal relatives settled in Hong Kong or Macao is without an heir and must be inherited by Mainland children settling there;  
(5) Where there are other special circumstances requiring settlement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一的，可以申请短期前往香港、澳门：  
(一)在香港、澳门有定居的近亲属，须前往探望的；  
(二)直系亲属或者近亲属是台湾同胞，必须由内地亲人去香港、澳门会亲的；  
(三)归国华侨的直系亲属、兄弟姐妹和侨眷的直系亲属不能回内地探亲，必须去香港、澳门会面的；  
(四)必须去香港、澳门处理产业的；  
(五)有其他特殊情况，必须短期去香港、澳门的。

**Article 8** An application may be made for short-term travel to Hong Kong or Macao in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where close relatives are settled in Hong Kong or Macao and the applicant must visit them;  
(2) Where lineal relatives or close relatives are compatriots from Taiwan and must be met by Mainland relatives in Hong Kong or Macao;  
(3) Where lineal relatives, brothers and sisters of returned overseas Chinese, and lineal relatives of relatives of overseas Chinese cannot return to the Mainland for family visits and must meet in Hong Kong or Macao;  
(4) Where it is necessary to go to Hong Kong or Macao to deal with property;  
(5) Where there are other special circumstances requiring short-term travel to Hong Kong or Macao.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　内地公民因私事申请前往香港、澳门，须回答有关的询问并履行下列手续：  
(一)交验户口簿或者其他户籍证明；  
(二)填写申请表；  
(三)提交所在工作单位对申请人前往香港、澳门的意见；  
(四)提交与申请事由相应的证明。

**Article 9** A Mainland citizen applying to go to Hong Kong or Macao for private affairs shall answer relevant inquiries and go through the following formalities:  
(1) produce the household register or other household registration certificates for inspection;  
(2) fill out an application form;  
(3) submit the opinion of the applicant's work unit regarding the travel to Hong Kong or Macao;  
(4) submit certificates corresponding to the reason for the application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[本办法第九条](#art-9)第四项所称的证明是指：  
(一)夫妻团聚，须提交合法婚姻证明，以及配偶在香港、澳门有永久居住资格的证明；  
(二)去香港、澳门照顾年老体弱父母或者无依无靠的老人、儿童投靠香港、澳门亲属，须提交与香港、澳门亲属关系及其在香港、澳门有永久居住资格的证明；  
(三)继承或者处理产业，须提交产业状况和合法继承权的证明；  
(四)探望在香港、澳门亲属，须提交亲属函件；时间急迫的，应尽可能提交与申请事由相关的说明或者证明；  
(五)会见台湾亲属或者会见居住国外的亲属，须提交亲属到达香港、澳门日期的确切证明。

**Article 10** The certificates referred to in item (4) of Article 9 of these Measures mean:  
(1) For reunion of spouses, a lawful marriage certificate and a certificate proving that the spouse has permanent residence status in Hong Kong or Macao shall be submitted;  
(2) For going to Hong Kong or Macao to take care of elderly and infirm parents or for elderly persons and children without support to seek refuge with relatives in Hong Kong or Macao, a certificate proving the relationship with the relatives in Hong Kong or Macao and their permanent residence status in Hong Kong or Macao shall be submitted;  
(3) For inheriting or dealing with property, a certificate proving the status of the property and lawful inheritance rights shall be submitted;  
(4) For visiting relatives in Hong Kong or Macao, a letter from the relatives shall be submitted; in urgent cases, explanations or certificates relevant to the reason for the application shall be submitted as far as possible;  
(5) For meeting relatives from Taiwan or relatives residing abroad, a definite certificate proving the date of arrival of the relatives in Hong Kong or Macao shall be submitted.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公安机关出入境管理部门受理的前往香港、澳门的申请，应当在六十天内作出批准或者不批准的决定，通知申请人。

**Article 11** The exit and entry administration department of the public security organ shall, within sixty days from the date of accepting an application for travel to Hong Kong or Macao, make a decision of approval or disapproval and notify the applicant.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经批准前往香港、澳门定居的内地公民，由公安机关出入境管理部门发给前往港澳通行证。持证人应当在前往香港、澳门之前，到所在地公安派出所注销户口，并在规定的时间内前往香港、澳门。  
经批准短期前往香港、澳门的内地公民，发给往来港澳通行证。持证人应当在规定时间内前往并按期返回。

**Article 12** A Mainland citizen who is approved to go to Hong Kong or Macao for settlement shall be issued a Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao by the exit and entry administration department of the public security organ. The holder shall, before proceeding to Hong Kong or Macao, cancel his or her household registration at the local public security police station, and shall proceed to Hong Kong or Macao within the prescribed time limit.  
A Mainland citizen who is approved to go to Hong Kong or Macao for a short-term stay shall be issued a Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao. The holder shall proceed to Hong Kong or Macao within the prescribed time limit and return on schedule.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　内地公民申请去香港、澳门，有下列情形之一的，不予批准：  
(一)属于《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法》第八条规定情形的；  
(二)不属于[本办法第七条](#art-7)和第八条规定情形的；  
(三)编造情况、提供假证明，欺骗公安机关出入境管理部门的。

**Article 13** An application by a Mainland citizen for travel to Hong Kong or Macao shall not be approved in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) falling under the circumstances specified in Article 8 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) not falling under the circumstances specified in Articles 7 and 8 of these Measures;  
(3) fabricating facts, providing false certificates, or deceiving the exit and entry administration department of the public security organ.

### 第三章　港澳同胞来内地

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　港澳同胞来内地，须申请领取港澳同胞回乡证。港澳同胞回乡证由广东省公安厅签发。  
申领港澳同胞回乡证须交验居住身份证明、填写申请表。  
不经常来内地的港澳同胞，可申请领取入出境通行证。申领办法与申领港澳同胞回乡证相同。

**Article 14** Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao coming to the Mainland shall apply for and obtain a Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots. The Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots shall be issued by the Public Security Department of Guangdong Province.  
To apply for a Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, the applicant shall produce his or her residence identity certificate for inspection and fill out an application form.  
Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao who do not frequently come to the Mainland may apply for an Entry-Exit Permit. The application procedure shall be the same as that for the Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有下列情形之一的，不发给港澳同胞回乡证或者入出境通行证：  
(一)被认为有可能进行抢劫、盗窃、贩毒等犯罪活动的；  
(二)编造情况，提交假证明的；  
(三)精神病患者。

**Article 15** A Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots or an Entry-Exit Permit shall not be issued in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where the person is considered likely to engage in criminal activities such as robbery, theft, or drug trafficking;  
(2) where the person fabricates facts or submits false certificates;  
(3) where the person is a mental patient.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　港澳同胞驾驶机动车辆来内地，应当按照广东省人民政府有关规定申请行车执照，驾驶人员还须向广东省公安厅出入境管理处申请驾驶港澳机动车辆来往内地的许可。

**Article 16** Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao driving motor vehicles to the Mainland shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Government of Guangdong Province, apply for a driving license, and the driver shall also apply to the Exit and Entry Administration Office of the Public Security Department of Guangdong Province for a permit to drive Hong Kong and Macao motor vehicles to and from the Mainland.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　港澳同胞短期来内地，要按照户口管理规定，办理暂住登记。在宾馆、饭店、旅店、招待所、学校等企业、事业单位或者机关、团体及其他机构内住宿的，应当填写临时住宿登记表；住在亲友家的，由本人或者亲友在二十四小时内(农村可在七十二小时内)到住地公安派出所或者户籍办公室办理暂住登记。

**Article 17** Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao coming to the Mainland for a short-term stay shall, in accordance with the regulations on household registration, complete temporary residence registration. For accommodation in hotels, restaurants, inns, hostels, schools and other enterprises, institutions, or government organs, organizations and other entities, a temporary accommodation registration form shall be filled out; for accommodation in the homes of relatives or friends, the person concerned or the relative or friend shall, within 24 hours (or within 72 hours in rural areas), go to the local public security police station or household registration office to complete temporary residence registration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　港澳同胞要求回内地定居的，应当事先向拟定居地的市、县公安局提出申请，获准后，持注有回乡定居签注的港澳同胞回乡证，至定居地办理常住户口手续。

**Article 18** A compatriot from Hong Kong or Macao who wishes to return to the Mainland for settlement shall, in advance, submit an application to the public security bureau of the city or county at the intended place of settlement, and after approval, shall take the Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots bearing the endorsement for return for settlement to the place of settlement to complete the formalities for permanent residence.

### 第四章　出入境检查

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　内地公民往来香港、澳门以及港澳同胞来往内地，须向对外开放口岸或者指定口岸的边防检查站出示出入境证件，填交出境、入境登记卡，接受查验。

**Article 19** Mainland citizens traveling to and from Hong Kong and Macao and compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao traveling to and from the Mainland shall present their entry-exit documents to the frontier inspection station at a port open to the public or a designated port, fill out and submit entry and exit registration cards, and undergo inspection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，边防检查站有权阻止出境、入境：  
(一)未持有往来港澳通行证件、港澳同胞回乡证或其他有效证件的；  
(二)持用伪造、涂改等无效的往来港澳通行证件或者港澳同胞回乡证，冒用他人往来港澳通行证件或者港澳同胞回乡证的；  
(三)拒绝交验证件的。  
具有前款第二项规定的情形的，并可依照[本办法第二十六条](#art-26)的规定处理。

**Article 20** The frontier inspection station shall have the power to prevent exit or entry in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Not holding a valid travel document for travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao, a Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, or other valid documents;  
(2) Using a forged, altered or otherwise invalid travel document for travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao or Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, or fraudulently using another person's travel document or Home Return Permit;  
(3) Refusing to produce the documents for inspection.  
Where a person falls under the circumstance specified in item (2) of the preceding paragraph, the matter may also be dealt with in accordance with Article 26 of these Measures.

### 第五章　证件管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　港澳同胞回乡证由持证人保存，有效期十年，在有效期内可以多次使用。超过有效期或者查验页用完的，可以换领新证。申请新证按照[本办法第十四条](#art-14)规定办理。

**Article 21** The Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots shall be kept by the holder, and shall be valid for ten years, and may be used multiple times within the validity period. If it exceeds the validity period or the inspection pages are exhausted, a new permit may be exchanged. Application for a new permit shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of these Measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　前往港澳通行证在有效期内一次使用有效。往来港澳通行证有效期五年，可以延期二次，每次不超过五年，证件由持证人保存、使用，每次前往香港、澳门均须按照[本办法第六条](#art-6)、第八条、第十条的规定办理申请手续，经批准的作一次往返签注。经公安部特别授权的公安机关可以作多次往返签注。

**Article 22** A Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao shall be valid for single use within its validity period. A Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao shall be valid for five years, and may be extended twice, each time not exceeding five years. The permit shall be kept and used by the holder. Each time when traveling to Hong Kong or Macao, the holder shall go through the application procedures in accordance with the provisions of Articles 6, 8, and 10 of these Measures, and upon approval, a single round-trip endorsement shall be made. Public security organs specially authorized by the Ministry of Public Security may make multiple round-trip endorsements.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　港澳同胞来内地后遗失港澳同胞回乡证，应向遗失地的市、县或者交通运输部门的公安机关报失，经公安机关调查属实，出具证明，由公安机关出入境管理部门签发一次有效的入出境通行证，凭证返回香港、澳门。港澳同胞无论在香港、澳门或者内地遗失港澳同胞回乡证，均可以按照[本办法第十四条](#art-14)规定重新申请领取港澳同胞回乡证。

**Article 23** If a Hong Kong or Macao compatriot loses his or her Home Return Permit after coming to the Mainland, he or she shall report the loss to the public security organ of the city, county, or transportation department where the loss occurred. After investigation and verification by the public security organ, a certificate shall be issued, and the exit and entry administration department of the public security organ shall issue a one-time valid entry-exit permit, based on which the holder may return to Hong Kong or Macao. If a Hong Kong or Macao compatriot loses his or her Home Return Permit in Hong Kong, Macao, or the Mainland, he or she may reapply for a Home Return Permit in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of these Measures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　内地公民在前往香港、澳门之前遗失前往港澳通行证、往来港澳通行证的，应立即报告原发证机关，并由本人登报声明，经调查属实的，重新发给证件。

**Article 24** If a Mainland citizen loses a Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao or a Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao before traveling to Hong Kong or Macao, he or she shall immediately report to the original issuing authority, and shall make a newspaper announcement. After investigation and verification, a new permit shall be issued.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　港澳同胞回乡证持证人有[本办法第十五条](#art-15)规定情形之一的，证件应予以吊销。  
吊销证件由原发证机关或其上级机关决定并予以收缴。

**Article 25** If a holder of a Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots is under any of the circumstances specified in Article 15 of these Measures, the permit shall be revoked.  
The revocation of the permit shall be decided by the original issuing authority or its superior authority and shall be confiscated.

### 第六章　处罚

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　持用伪造、涂改等无效的或者冒用他人的前往港澳通行证、往来港澳通行证、港澳同胞回乡证、入出境通行证的，除可以没收证件外，并视情节轻重，处以警告或五日以下拘留。

**Article 26** Anyone who uses a forged, altered, or otherwise invalid Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao, Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao, Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, or entry-exit permit, or fraudulently uses another person's such permit, in addition to the possible confiscation of the permit, shall, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, be given a warning or be detained for not more than five days.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　伪造、涂改、转让前往港澳通行证、往来港澳通行证、港澳同胞回乡证、入出境通行证的，处十日以下拘留；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关条款的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Anyone who forges, alters, or transfers a Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao, Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao, Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, or entry-exit permit shall be detained for not more than ten days; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　编造情况，提供假证明，或者以行贿等手段，获取前往港澳通行证、往来港澳通行证、港澳同胞回乡证、入出境通行证，情节较轻的，处以警告或五日以下拘留；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关条款的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Anyone who fabricates circumstances, provides false certificates, or uses bribery or other means to obtain a Permit for Travel to Hong Kong and Macao, Permit for Travel to and from Hong Kong and Macao, Home Return Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Compatriots, or entry-exit permit, if the circumstances are minor, shall be given a warning or be detained for not more than five days; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公安机关的工作人员在执行本办法时，如有利用职权索取、收受贿赂或者有其他违法失职行为，情节轻微的，可以由主管部门酌情予以行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关条款的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** If a staff member of a public security organ, in executing these Measures, extorts or accepts bribes by taking advantage of his or her position, or commits other illegal or derelict acts, if the circumstances are minor, the competent authority may impose disciplinary sanctions as it deems appropriate; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本办法由公安部组织实施，负责解释。

**Article 30** These Measures shall be implemented and interpreted by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本办法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Measures shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
