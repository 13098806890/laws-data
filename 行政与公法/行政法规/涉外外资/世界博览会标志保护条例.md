# 世界博览会标志保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2004-10-20  
**生效日期**：2004-12-01

> (2004年10月13日国务院第66次常务会议通过　2004年10月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第422号公布　自2004年12月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对世界博览会标志的保护，维护世界博览会标志权利人的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称世界博览会标志，是指： (一)中国2010年上海世界博览会申办机构的名称(包括全称、简称、译名和缩写，下同)、徽记或者其他标志； (二)中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构的名称、徽记或者其他标志； (三)中国2010年上海世界博览会的名称、会徽、会旗、吉祥物、会歌、主题词、口号； (四)国际展览局的局旗。 第三条　本条例所称世界博览会标志权利人，是指中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构和国际展览局。 中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构为本条例第二条第(一)、(二)、(三)项规定的世界博览会标志的权利人。中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构和国际展览局之间关于本条例第二条第(四)项规定的世界博览会标志的权利划分，依照中国2010年上海世界博览会《申办报告》、《注册报告》和国际展览局《关于使用国际展览局局旗的规定》确定。 第四条　世界博览会标志权利人依照本条例享有世界博览会标志专有权。 未经世界博览会标志权利人许可，任何人不得为商业目的(含潜在商业目的，下同)使用世界博览会标志。 第五条　本条例所称为商业目的使用，是指以营利为目的，以下列方式使用世界博览会标志： (一)将世界博览会标志用于商品、商品包装或者容器以及商品交易文书上； (二)将世界博览会标志用于服务业中； (三)将世界博览会标志用于广告宣传、商业展览、营业性演出以及其他商业活动中；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对世界博览会标志的保护，维护世界博览会标志权利人的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the protection of World Expo logos and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of the holders of World Expo logos.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称世界博览会标志，是指：  
(一)中国2010年上海世界博览会申办机构的名称(包括全称、简称、译名和缩写，下同)、徽记或者其他标志；  
(二)中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构的名称、徽记或者其他标志；  
(三)中国2010年上海世界博览会的名称、会徽、会旗、吉祥物、会歌、主题词、口号；  
(四)国际展览局的局旗。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "World Expo logos" refer to:  
(1) the names (including full names, abbreviations, translations, and acronyms, the same below), emblems or other logos of the bid committee of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010;  
(2) the names, emblems or other logos of the organizing committee of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010;  
(3) the name, emblem, flag, mascot, anthem, theme and slogan of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010;  
(4) the flag of the Bureau of International Expositions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称世界博览会标志权利人，是指中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构和国际展览局。  
中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构为[本条例第二条](#art-2)第(一)、(二)、(三)项规定的世界博览会标志的权利人。中国2010年上海世界博览会组织机构和国际展览局之间关于[本条例第二条](#art-2)第(四)项规定的世界博览会标志的权利划分，依照中国2010年上海世界博览会《申办报告》、《注册报告》和国际展览局《关于使用国际展览局局旗的规定》确定。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, the holders of World Expo logos refer to the organizer of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010 and the Bureau of International Expositions.  
The organizer of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010 shall be the holder of the World Expo logos as specified in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 2 of these Regulations. The division of rights between the organizer of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010 and the Bureau of International Expositions regarding the World Expo logos as specified in subparagraph (4) of Article 2 of these Regulations shall be determined in accordance with the Bid Report, the Registration Report of the World Exposition Shanghai China 2010 and the Provisions of the Bureau of International Expositions on the Use of the BIE Flag.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　世界博览会标志权利人依照本条例享有世界博览会标志专有权。  
未经世界博览会标志权利人许可，任何人不得为商业目的(含潜在商业目的，下同)使用世界博览会标志。

**Article 4** The holder of a World Expo logo shall, in accordance with these Regulations, enjoy the exclusive right to use the World Expo logo.  
Without the permission of the holder, no person may use a World Expo logo for commercial purposes (including potential commercial purposes, the same below).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本条例所称为商业目的使用，是指以营利为目的，以下列方式使用世界博览会标志：  
(一)将世界博览会标志用于商品、商品包装或者容器以及商品交易文书上；  
(二)将世界博览会标志用于服务业中；  
(三)将世界博览会标志用于广告宣传、商业展览、营业性演出以及其他商业活动中；  
(四)销售、进口、出口含有世界博览会标志的商品；  
(五)制造或者销售世界博览会标志；  
(六)将世界博览会标志作为字号申请企业名称登记，可能造成市场误认、混淆的；  
(七)可能使他人认为行为人与世界博览会标志权利人之间存在许可使用关系而使用世界博览会标志的其他行为。

**Article 5** For the purposes of these Regulations, "use for commercial purposes" means the use of a World Expo logo for profit-making purposes in any of the following manners:  
(1) using the logo on goods, packaging or containers of goods, or on transaction documents;  
(2) using the logo in service industries;  
(3) using the logo in advertising, commercial exhibitions, commercial performances and other commercial activities;  
(4) selling, importing or exporting goods bearing the logo;  
(5) manufacturing or selling the logo;  
(6) applying for registration of an enterprise name using the logo as a trade name, which may cause market misidentification or confusion;  
(7) other acts that may lead others to believe that there is a licensing relationship between the actor and the holder of the logo.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院工商行政管理部门依照本条例的规定，负责全国的世界博览会标志保护工作。  
县级以上地方工商行政管理部门依照本条例的规定，负责本行政区域内的世界博览会标志保护工作。

**Article 6** The administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the protection of World Expo logos nationwide.  
The local administrative departments for industry and commerce at or above the county level shall, in accordance with these Regulations, be responsible for the protection of World Expo logos within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　世界博览会标志权利人应当将世界博览会标志报国务院工商行政管理部门备案，由国务院工商行政管理部门公告。

**Article 7** The holder of a World Expo logo shall report the World Expo logo to the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council for recordation, and the said department shall publish it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在本条例施行前已经依法使用世界博览会标志的，可以在原有范围内继续使用。

**Article 8** If a World Expo logo has been used lawfully before these Regulations come into force, it may continue to be used within the original scope.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　未经世界博览会标志权利人许可，为商业目的擅自使用世界博览会标志即侵犯世界博览会标志专有权，引起纠纷的，由当事人协商解决；不愿协商或者协商不成的，世界博览会标志权利人或者利害关系人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼，也可以请求工商行政管理部门处理。  
应当事人的请求，工商行政管理部门可以就侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的赔偿数额进行调解；调解不成的，当事人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 9** Any unauthorized use of a World Expo logo for commercial purposes without the permission of the holder shall constitute an infringement upon the exclusive right to use the World Expo logo. If a dispute arises therefrom, the parties may settle it through consultation; if they are unwilling to consult or consultation fails, the holder or an interested party may, according to law, file a lawsuit with the People's Court, or request the administrative department for industry and commerce to handle the matter.  
Upon request of the parties, the administrative department for industry and commerce may conduct mediation on the amount of damages for the infringement; if mediation fails, the parties may, according to law, file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　工商行政管理部门根据已经取得的违法嫌疑证据或者举报查处涉嫌侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的行为时，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)询问有关当事人，调查与侵犯世界博览会标志专有权有关的情况；  
(二)查阅、复制与侵权活动有关的合同、发票、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
(三)对当事人涉嫌侵犯世界博览会标志专有权活动的场所实施现场检查；  
(四)检查与侵权活动有关的物品；对有证据证明侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的物品，予以查封或者扣押。  
工商行政管理部门依法行使前款规定的职权时，当事人应当予以协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 10** When investigating and handling suspected infringement upon the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo based on obtained evidence of illegality or upon receiving a report, the administrative department for industry and commerce may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to inquire of the relevant parties and investigate the circumstances related to the infringement;  
(2) to consult and copy contracts, invoices, account books and other relevant materials related to the infringing activities;  
(3) to conduct on-site inspection of the places where the suspected infringement activities occur;  
(4) to inspect the articles related to the infringing activities; and to seal up or seize the articles with evidence proving infringement upon the exclusive right to use the World Expo logo.  
When exercising the powers specified in the preceding paragraph according to law, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall have the relevant parties assist and cooperate, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　工商行政管理部门处理侵犯世界博览会标志专有权行为时，认定侵权行为成立的，责令立即停止侵权行为，没收、销毁侵权商品和专门用于制造侵权商品或者为商业目的擅自制造世界博览会标志的工具，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以并处5万元以下的罚款。  
利用世界博览会标志进行诈骗等活动，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** When handling an infringement upon the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, if it confirms that the infringement is constituted, order immediate cessation of the infringement, and confiscate and destroy the infringing goods and the tools specially used for manufacturing infringing goods or for manufacturing the World Expo logo without authorization for commercial purposes; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains may be imposed concurrently; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed concurrently.  
Anyone who commits fraud or other activities by using a World Expo logo, which constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的货物禁止进出口。世界博览会标志专有权海关保护的程序适用《中华人民共和国知识产权海关保护条例》的规定。

**Article 12** Goods infringing upon the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo shall be prohibited from import and export. The procedures for customs protection of the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo shall be governed by the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Customs Protection of Intellectual Property Rights.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的赔偿数额，按照权利人因被侵权所受到的损失或者侵权人因侵权所获得的利益确定，包括为制止侵权行为所支付的合理开支；被侵权人的损失或者侵权人获得的利益难以确定的，参照该世界博览会标志许可使用费合理确定。  
销售不知道是侵犯世界博览会标志专有权的商品，能证明该商品是自己合法取得并说明提供者的，不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 13** The amount of damages for infringement upon the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo shall be determined based on the losses suffered by the holder due to the infringement or the profits gained by the infringer from the infringement, including the reasonable expenses incurred by the holder to stop the infringement; if the losses of the infringed party or the gains of the infringer are difficult to determine, the amount shall be reasonably determined by reference to the license fee for the use of the World Expo logo.  
Any person who sells goods that he or she does not know infringe the exclusive right to use a World Expo logo, and can prove that he or she has lawfully obtained the goods and identify the supplier, shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位或者个人可以向工商行政管理部门或有关行政管理部门举报违反本条例使用世界博览会标志的行为。

**Article 14** Any unit or individual may report to the administrative department for industry and commerce or the relevant administrative department any act of using a World Expo logo in violation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　世界博览会标志除依照本条例受到保护外，还可以依照《中华人民共和国著作权法》、《中华人民共和国商标法》、《中华人民共和国专利法》、《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》、《特殊标志管理条例》等法律、行政法规的规定获得保护。

**Article 15** In addition to the protection granted under these Regulations, World Expo logos may also be protected in accordance with laws and administrative regulations such as the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, the Trademark Law of the People's Republic of China, the Patent Law of the People's Republic of China, the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Regulations on the Administration of Special Signs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本条例自2004年12月1日起施行。

**Article 16** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2004.
