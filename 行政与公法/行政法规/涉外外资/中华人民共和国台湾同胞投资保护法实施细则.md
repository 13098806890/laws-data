# 中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (1999年12月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第274号发布　根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了保护和鼓励台湾同胞投资，促进海峡两岸的经济发展，实施《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》，制定本实施细则。 第二条　本实施细则所称台湾同胞投资是指台湾地区的公司、企业、其他经济组织或者个人作为投资者在其他省、自治区和直辖市(以下简称大陆)的投资。 第三条　国家依法保护台湾同胞投资。 台湾同胞投资者的投资、投资收益和其他合法权益受国家法律保护，任何机关、单位或者个人不得侵占、损害。 第四条　国家依法鼓励台湾同胞投资。 台湾同胞投资依照国家有关法律、行政法规和本实施细则的规定，享受优惠待遇。 第五条　台湾同胞投资适用《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》和本实施细则；《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》和本实施细则未规定的，比照适用国家有关涉外经济法律、行政法规。 第六条　台湾同胞投资，应当与国家国民经济和社会发展规划相适应，符合国家产业政策和投资导向的要求，比照适用国家关于指导外商投资方向的规定。 第七条　台湾同胞投资者可以用可自由兑换货币、机器设备或者其他实物、工业产权、非专利技术等作为投资。 台湾同胞投资者可以用投资获得的收益进行再投资。 第八条　台湾同胞投资，可以依法采用下列投资形式： (一)举办全部或者部分由台湾同胞投资者投资的企业（以下简称台湾同胞投资企业）； (二)合作勘探开发自然资源； (三)开展补偿贸易、加工装配、合作生产；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和鼓励台湾同胞投资，促进海峡两岸的经济发展，实施《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》，制定本实施细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Rules are formulated in order to protect and encourage investment by Taiwan compatriots, promote economic development across the Taiwan Strait, and implement the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Investment by Taiwan Compatriots.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本实施细则所称台湾同胞投资是指台湾地区的公司、企业、其他经济组织或者个人作为投资者在其他省、自治区和直辖市(以下简称大陆)的投资。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Detailed Rules, "investment by Taiwan compatriots" refers to investment made by companies, enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals from the Taiwan region as investors in other provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as the Mainland).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家依法保护台湾同胞投资。  
台湾同胞投资者的投资、投资收益和其他合法权益受国家法律保护，任何机关、单位或者个人不得侵占、损害。

**Article 3** The State shall protect investment by Taiwan compatriots according to law.  
The investment, investment returns and other lawful rights and interests of Taiwan compatriot investors shall be protected by the laws of the State, and no organ, unit or individual may infringe upon or damage them.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家依法鼓励台湾同胞投资。  
台湾同胞投资依照国家有关法律、行政法规和本实施细则的规定，享受优惠待遇。

**Article 4** The State shall encourage investment by Taiwan compatriots according to law.  
Investment by Taiwan compatriots shall enjoy preferential treatment in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State and the provisions of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　台湾同胞投资适用《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》和本实施细则；《中华人民共和国台湾同胞投资保护法》和本实施细则未规定的，比照适用国家有关涉外经济法律、行政法规。

**Article 5** Investment by Taiwan compatriots shall be governed by the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Investment by Taiwan Compatriots and these Detailed Rules; matters not provided for by the said Law and these Detailed Rules shall be governed by the relevant State laws and administrative regulations on foreign-related economic affairs, by applying them mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　台湾同胞投资，应当与国家国民经济和社会发展规划相适应，符合国家产业政策和投资导向的要求，比照适用国家关于指导外商投资方向的规定。

**Article 6** Investment by Taiwan compatriots shall be commensurate with the national economic and social development plan, and shall conform to the requirements of the national industrial policies and investment guidance, with the provisions of the State on guiding the direction of foreign investment applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　台湾同胞投资者可以用可自由兑换货币、机器设备或者其他实物、工业产权、非专利技术等作为投资。  
台湾同胞投资者可以用投资获得的收益进行再投资。

**Article 7** Taiwan compatriot investors may invest in convertible currencies, machinery and equipment or other tangible assets, industrial property rights, non-patented technology, etc.  
Taiwan compatriot investors may reinvest the returns obtained from their investment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　台湾同胞投资，可以依法采用下列投资形式：  
(一)举办全部或者部分由台湾同胞投资者投资的企业（以下简称台湾同胞投资企业）；  
(二)合作勘探开发自然资源；  
(三)开展补偿贸易、加工装配、合作生产；  
(四)购买企业的股票、债券；  
(五)购置房产；  
(六)取得土地使用权，开发经营；  
(七)购买国有小型企业或者集体企业、私营企业；  
(八)法律、行政法规允许的其他投资形式。

**Article 8** Investment by Taiwan compatriots may, according to law, take the following forms:  
(1) Establishing enterprises wholly or partially invested by Taiwan compatriot investors (hereinafter referred to as Taiwan compatriot-invested enterprises);  
(2) Cooperating in the exploration and development of natural resources;  
(3) Engaging in compensation trade, processing and assembly, and cooperative production;  
(4) Purchasing stocks and bonds of enterprises;  
(5) Purchasing real estate;  
(6) Obtaining the right to the use of land for development and operation;  
(7) Purchasing state-owned small enterprises, or collective enterprises, or private enterprises;  
(8) Other investment forms permitted by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　台湾同胞投资者进行投资，需要审批的，依照国家有关法律、行政法规规定的程序办理审批手续。

**Article 9** Where the investment by a Taiwan compatriot investor requires examination and approval, the formalities for examination and approval shall be handled in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　审批机关审批台湾同胞投资，应当提高办事效率，减少管理层次，简化审批程序，做到管理制度统一、公开、透明。

**Article 10** The examination and approval authorities shall, when examining and approving investment by Taiwan compatriots, improve work efficiency, reduce administrative levels, simplify examination and approval procedures, and ensure that the management system is unified, open and transparent.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　台湾同胞投资企业依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定，享受税收优惠待遇。

**Article 11** Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant national laws and administrative regulations, enjoy preferential tax treatment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　投资于大陆中西部地区的台湾同胞投资项目，可以按照国家有关规定给予鼓励或者适当放宽限制。

**Article 12** Taiwan compatriot investment projects in the central and western regions of the mainland may be granted encouragement or appropriately relaxed restrictions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　台湾同胞投资企业符合贷款原则的，可以按照国家有关规定给予必要的信贷支持。

**Article 13** Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises that meet the loan principles may be given necessary credit support in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　台湾同胞投资者个人及其随行家属和台湾同胞投资企业中的台湾同胞职工及其随行家属，可以依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定，向公安机关申请办理一定期限多次入出境手续和相应期限的暂住手续。台湾同胞投资企业中的外籍职工的入出境和暂住手续，依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 14** Taiwan compatriot investors individually and their accompanying family members, as well as Taiwan compatriot employees in Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises and their accompanying family members, may, in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the state, apply to the public security organ for handling multiple entry-exit procedures for a certain period and temporary residence procedures for a corresponding period. The entry-exit and temporary residence procedures for foreign employees of Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the state.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　台湾同胞投资者个人的子女和台湾同胞投资企业中的台湾同胞职工的子女，可以按照国家有关规定进入大陆的小学、中学和高等学校接受教育。  
台湾同胞投资者或者台湾同胞投资企业协会在台湾同胞投资集中的地区，可以按照国家有关规定申请设立台湾同胞子女学校。经批准设立的台湾同胞子女学校应当接受教育行政部门的监督。

**Article 15** Children of Taiwan compatriot investors individually and children of Taiwan compatriot employees in Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, enter primary schools, secondary schools, and institutions of higher education in the mainland to receive education.  
Taiwan compatriot investors or associations of Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises may, in areas where Taiwan compatriot investments are concentrated, apply to establish schools for children of Taiwan compatriots in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state. Schools for children of Taiwan compatriots established upon approval shall accept supervision by the education administrative departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　台湾同胞投资企业依法享有经营管理的自主权。  
台湾同胞投资企业经营管理的自主权受国家法律保护，不受任何机关、单位或者个人的非法干预和侵犯。

**Article 16** Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall, according to law, enjoy autonomy in operation and management.  
The autonomy in operation and management of Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall be protected by state laws and shall not be subject to illegal interference or infringement by any organ, unit, or individual.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　台湾同胞投资企业在购买机器设备、原材料及辅料等物资以及获得水、电、热、货物运输、劳务、广告、通信等服务方面，享有与大陆其他同类企业同等的待遇。  
台湾同胞投资者个人和台湾同胞投资企业中的台湾同胞职工在交通、通信、旅游、旅馆住宿等方面，享有与大陆同胞同等的待遇。

**Article 17** Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall enjoy equal treatment with other similar enterprises in the mainland in purchasing machinery, equipment, raw materials, auxiliary materials, and other materials, as well as in obtaining services such as water, electricity, heat, freight transport, labor, advertising, and communications.  
Taiwan compatriot investors individually and Taiwan compatriot employees in Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises shall enjoy equal treatment with mainland compatriots in transportation, communications, tourism, hotel accommodation, and other aspects.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　台湾同胞投资者投资的财产、工业产权、投资收益和其他合法权益，可以依法转让和继承。

**Article 18** The property, industrial property rights, investment returns, and other lawful rights and interests invested by Taiwan compatriot investors may be transferred and inherited according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　台湾同胞投资者依法获得的投资收益、其他合法收入和清算后的资金，可以依法汇回台湾或者汇往境外。  
台湾同胞投资企业中的台湾同胞职工的合法收入，可以依法汇回台湾或者汇往境外。

**Article 19** Investment returns, other lawful income, and funds after liquidation lawfully obtained by Taiwan compatriot investors may be remitted back to Taiwan or abroad according to law.  
Lawful income of Taiwan compatriot employees in Taiwan compatriot investment enterprises may be remitted back to Taiwan or abroad according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　台湾同胞投资者可以委托亲友或者他人作为其投资的代理人，代理人应当持有具有法律效力的授权委托书。

**Article 20** Taiwan compatriot investors may entrust relatives, friends, or other persons as their investment agents, and the agent shall hold a legally effective power of attorney.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家机关对台湾同胞投资企业收费的项目和标准，应当与大陆其他同类企业相同。任何机关或者单位不得对台湾同胞投资企业另立收费项目或者提高收费标准。  
任何机关或者单位不得向台湾同胞投资企业摊派人力、物力、财力，不得对台湾同胞投资企业进行法律、法规规定以外的检查、罚款，不得违反国家规定强制或者变相强制台湾同胞投资企业参加各类培训、评比、鉴定、考核等活动。  
台湾同胞投资企业对违反上述规定的行为，有权拒绝并向政府有关部门举报。接受举报的政府部门应当依法作出处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 21** The items and standards of fees charged by state organs to enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots shall be the same as those charged to other similar enterprises in the mainland. No organ or unit shall establish additional fee items or raise fee standards for enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots.  
No organ or unit shall apportion human, material or financial resources to enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots, nor conduct inspections or impose fines beyond those prescribed by laws and regulations on enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots, nor compel or covertly compel enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots to participate in various training, evaluation, appraisal, assessment or other activities in violation of state regulations.  
An enterprise with investment from Taiwan compatriots shall have the right to refuse and to report to the relevant government department any act violating the above provisions. The government department that receives the report shall handle the matter according to law and keep the informant confidential.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对台湾同胞投资者的投资不实行国有化和征收；在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，对台湾同胞投资者的投资可以依照法律程序实行征收，并给予相应的补偿。补偿相当于该投资在征收决定前一刻的价值，包括从征收之日起至支付之日止按合理利率计算的利息，并可以依法兑换外汇、汇回台湾或者汇往境外。

**Article 22** The state shall not nationalize or expropriate the investments of Taiwan compatriot investors; in special circumstances, where required by the public interest, the investments of Taiwan compatriot investors may be expropriated in accordance with legal procedures, with corresponding compensation given. The compensation shall be equivalent to the value of the investment immediately before the expropriation decision, including interest calculated at a reasonable rate from the date of expropriation to the date of payment, and may be converted into foreign exchange and remitted back to Taiwan or abroad according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家依法保护台湾同胞投资者个人及其随行家属和台湾同胞投资企业中的台湾同胞职工及其随行家属的人身自由和人身安全。除依照国家有关法律规定办理的外，不得对台湾同胞采取限制人身自由的强制措施。

**Article 23** The state shall protect according to law the personal freedom and personal safety of Taiwan compatriot investors, their accompanying family members, and Taiwan compatriot employees in enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots and their accompanying family members. Except where handled in accordance with relevant state laws and regulations, no compulsory measures restricting the personal freedom of Taiwan compatriots shall be taken.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在台湾同胞投资企业集中的地区，可以依法成立台湾同胞投资企业协会。  
台湾同胞投资企业协会的合法权益以及按照其章程所进行的合法活动，受法律保护。

**Article 24** In areas where enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots are concentrated, associations of enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots may be established according to law.  
The lawful rights and interests of associations of enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots and their lawful activities conducted in accordance with their articles of association shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各级人民政府应当对台湾同胞投资提供优质、规范、方便的服务。各级人民政府台湾事务办事机构应当做好台湾同胞投资的法律宣传与咨询、投诉受理和纠纷解决等工作。

**Article 25** People's governments at all levels shall provide high-quality, standardized and convenient services for investment by Taiwan compatriots. The offices of Taiwan affairs of people's governments at all levels shall properly carry out legal publicity and consultation, complaint acceptance and dispute resolution regarding investment by Taiwan compatriots.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　台湾同胞投资者、台湾同胞投资企业、台湾同胞投资企业协会认为行政机关或者行政机关工作人员的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的，可以依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定，申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 26** Where Taiwan compatriot investors, enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots, or associations of enterprises with investment from Taiwan compatriots consider that a specific administrative act of an administrative organ or its staff has infringed upon their lawful rights and interests, they may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action in accordance with the provisions of relevant state laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　台湾同胞投资者与大陆的公司、企业、其他经济组织或者个人之间发生的与投资有关的争议，当事人可以通过协商或者调解解决。  
当事人不愿协商、调解的，或者经协商、调解不成的，可以依照合同中的仲裁条款或者事后达成的书面仲裁协议，提交中国的仲裁机构仲裁。大陆的仲裁机构可以按照国家有关规定聘请台湾同胞担任仲裁员。  
当事人未在合同中订立仲裁条款，事后又未达成书面仲裁协议的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 27** Disputes arising between Taiwan compatriot investors and companies, enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals in the mainland in connection with investment may be settled by the parties through consultation or mediation.  
Where the parties are unwilling to engage in consultation or mediation, or where consultation or mediation fails, the dispute may be submitted to a Chinese arbitration institution for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration clause in the contract or a written arbitration agreement reached afterwards. A mainland arbitration institution may, in accordance with relevant state regulations, engage Taiwan compatriots as arbitrators.  
Where the parties have neither included an arbitration clause in the contract nor reached a written arbitration agreement afterwards, they may file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　台湾同胞以其设在其他国家或者地区的公司、企业或者其他经济组织作为投资者在大陆投资的，可以比照适用本实施细则。

**Article 28** Where Taiwan compatriots make investments in the mainland by using companies, enterprises or other economic organizations established in other countries or regions as investors, these Implementing Rules may be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本实施细则自发布之日起施行。

**Article 29** These Implementing Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
