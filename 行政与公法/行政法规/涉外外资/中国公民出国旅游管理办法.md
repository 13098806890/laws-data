# 中国公民出国旅游管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：2002-07-01

> (2002年5月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第354号公布　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了规范旅行社组织中国公民出国旅游活动，保障出国旅游者和出国旅游经营者的合法权益，制定本办法。 第二条　出国旅游的目的地国家，由国务院旅游行政部门会同国务院有关部门提出，报国务院批准后，由国务院旅游行政部门公布。 任何单位和个人不得组织中国公民到国务院旅游行政部门公布的出国旅游的目的地国家以外的国家旅游；组织中国公民到国务院旅游行政部门公布的出国旅游的目的地国家以外的国家进行涉及体育活动、文化活动等临时性专项旅游的，须经国务院旅游行政部门批准。 第三条　旅行社经营出国旅游业务，应当具备下列条件： (一)取得国际旅行社资格满1年； (二)经营入境旅游业务有突出业绩； (三)经营期间无重大违法行为和重大服务质量问题。 第四条　申请经营出国旅游业务的旅行社，应当向省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内，依据本办法第三条规定的条件对申请审查完毕，经审查同意的，报国务院旅游行政部门批准；经审查不同意的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。 国务院旅游行政部门批准旅行社经营出国旅游业务，应当符合旅游业发展规划及合理布局的要求。 未经国务院旅游行政部门批准取得出国旅游业务经营资格的，任何单位和个人不得擅自经营或者以商务、考察、培训等方式变相经营出国旅游业务。 第五条　国务院旅游行政部门应当将取得出国旅游业务经营资格的旅行社(以下简称组团社)名单予以公布，并通报国务院有关部门。 第六条　国务院旅游行政部门根据上年度全国入境旅游的业绩、出国旅游目的地的增加情况和出国旅游的发展趋势，在每年的2月底以前确定本年度组织出国旅游的人数安排总量，并下达省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门。 省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门根据本行政区域内各组团社上年度经营入境旅游的业绩、经营能力、服务质量，按照公平、公正、公开的原则，在每年的3月底以前核定各组团社本年度组织出国旅游的人数安排。 国务院旅游行政部门应当对省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门核定组团社年度出国旅游人数安排及组团社组织公民出国旅游的情况进行监督。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范旅行社组织中国公民出国旅游活动，保障出国旅游者和出国旅游经营者的合法权益，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of regulating travel agencies' organizing of Chinese citizens' outbound tourism activities and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of outbound tourists and outbound tourism operators.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　出国旅游的目的地国家，由国务院旅游行政部门会同国务院有关部门提出，报国务院批准后，由国务院旅游行政部门公布。  
任何单位和个人不得组织中国公民到国务院旅游行政部门公布的出国旅游的目的地国家以外的国家旅游；组织中国公民到国务院旅游行政部门公布的出国旅游的目的地国家以外的国家进行涉及体育活动、文化活动等临时性专项旅游的，须经国务院旅游行政部门批准。

**Article 2** The destination countries for outbound tourism shall be proposed by the tourism administration department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and upon approval by the State Council, shall be announced by the tourism administration department of the State Council.  
No unit or individual shall organize Chinese citizens to travel to countries other than the destination countries for outbound tourism announced by the tourism administration department of the State Council; where organizing Chinese citizens to travel to countries other than the destination countries for outbound tourism announced by the tourism administration department of the State Council for temporary special tourism involving sports activities, cultural activities, etc., approval from the tourism administration department of the State Council shall be obtained.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　旅行社经营出国旅游业务，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)取得国际旅行社资格满1年；  
(二)经营入境旅游业务有突出业绩；  
(三)经营期间无重大违法行为和重大服务质量问题。

**Article 3** A travel agency that operates outbound tourism business shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained the qualification as an international travel agency for at least one year;  
(2) having outstanding achievements in operating inbound tourism business;  
(3) having no major illegal acts or major service quality problems during its operation period.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请经营出国旅游业务的旅行社，应当向省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内，依据[本办法第三条](#art-3)规定的条件对申请审查完毕，经审查同意的，报国务院旅游行政部门批准；经审查不同意的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
国务院旅游行政部门批准旅行社经营出国旅游业务，应当符合旅游业发展规划及合理布局的要求。  
未经国务院旅游行政部门批准取得出国旅游业务经营资格的，任何单位和个人不得擅自经营或者以商务、考察、培训等方式变相经营出国旅游业务。

**Article 4** A travel agency applying for operation of outbound tourism business shall submit an application to the tourism administration department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The tourism administration department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, complete the review of the application in accordance with the conditions specified in Article 3 of these Measures; if the application is approved upon review, it shall be submitted to the tourism administration department of the State Council for approval; if the application is disapproved upon review, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
The approval by the tourism administration department of the State Council for a travel agency to operate outbound tourism business shall conform to the requirements of the tourism industry development plan and rational distribution.  
No unit or individual that has not obtained the qualification for outbound tourism business with the approval of the tourism administration department of the State Council shall, without authorization, operate outbound tourism business or covertly operate outbound tourism business in the form of business, inspection, training, etc.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院旅游行政部门应当将取得出国旅游业务经营资格的旅行社(以下简称组团社)名单予以公布，并通报国务院有关部门。

**Article 5** The tourism administration department of the State Council shall publish the list of travel agencies that have obtained the qualification for outbound tourism business (hereinafter referred to as the organizing travel agencies) and shall notify the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院旅游行政部门根据上年度全国入境旅游的业绩、出国旅游目的地的增加情况和出国旅游的发展趋势，在每年的2月底以前确定本年度组织出国旅游的人数安排总量，并下达省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门。  
省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门根据本行政区域内各组团社上年度经营入境旅游的业绩、经营能力、服务质量，按照公平、公正、公开的原则，在每年的3月底以前核定各组团社本年度组织出国旅游的人数安排。  
国务院旅游行政部门应当对省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门核定组团社年度出国旅游人数安排及组团社组织公民出国旅游的情况进行监督。

**Article 6** The tourism administration department of the State Council shall, based on the national inbound tourism performance of the previous year, the increase in outbound tourism destination countries, and the development trend of outbound tourism, determine the total number of arrangements for organizing outbound tourists for the current year before the end of February each year, and issue it to the tourism administration departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The tourism administration department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the inbound tourism performance, operational capability, and service quality of each organizing travel agency within its administrative region in the previous year, and in accordance with the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, verify the number of arrangements for organizing outbound tourists for each organizing travel agency for the current year before the end of March each year.  
The tourism administration department of the State Council shall supervise the verification by the tourism administration departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government of the annual outbound tourist number arrangements for organizing travel agencies and the circumstances of organizing citizens' outbound tourism by organizing travel agencies.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院旅游行政部门统一印制《中国公民出国旅游团队名单表》(以下简称《名单表》)，在下达本年度出国旅游人数安排时编号发放给省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门，由省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政部门核发给组团社。  
组团社应当按照核定的出国旅游人数安排组织出国旅游团队，填写《名单表》。旅游者及领队首次出境或者再次出境，均应当填写在《名单表》中，经审核后的《名单表》不得增添人员。

**Article 7** The tourism administration department of the State Council shall uniformly print the 'List of Teams for Chinese Citizens' Outbound Tourism' (hereinafter referred to as the 'List'), and when issuing the annual outbound tourist number arrangements, it shall number and distribute the List to the tourism administration departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, which shall then issue it to the organizing travel agencies.  
An organizing travel agency shall organize outbound tourism teams in accordance with the verified outbound tourist number arrangements and fill in the List. Tourists and the tour leader, whether traveling abroad for the first time or again, shall all be entered in the List; after verification, no person may be added to the List.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　《名单表》一式四联，分为：出境边防检查专用联、入境边防检查专用联、旅游行政部门审验专用联、旅行社自留专用联。  
组团社应当按照有关规定，在旅游团队出境、入境时及旅游团队入境后，将《名单表》分别交有关部门查验、留存。  
出国旅游兑换外汇，由旅游者个人按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 8** The List shall be in quadruplicate, divided into: the copy for exit border inspection, the copy for entry border inspection, the copy for verification by the tourism administration department, and the copy retained by the travel agency.  
An organizing travel agency shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, submit the List to the relevant departments for inspection and retention at the time of the tour team's exit and entry and after the tour team's entry.  
The exchange of foreign currency for outbound tourism shall be handled by individual tourists in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　旅游者持有有效普通护照的，可以直接到组团社办理出国旅游手续；没有有效普通护照的，应当依照《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法》的有关规定办理护照后再办理出国旅游手续。  
组团社应当为旅游者办理前往国签证等出境手续。

**Article 9** If a tourist holds a valid ordinary passport, he or she may directly go to the organizing travel agency to go through the outbound tourism formalities; if he or she does not hold a valid ordinary passport, he or she shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Exit and Entry of Citizens, apply for a passport before going through the outbound tourism formalities.  
The organizing travel agency shall handle the exit formalities such as the visa for the destination country for the tourist.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　组团社应当为旅游团队安排专职领队。  
领队在带团时，应当遵守本办法及国务院旅游行政部门的有关规定。

**Article 10** An organizing travel agency shall arrange a full-time tour leader for the tour team.  
When leading a team, the tour leader shall comply with these Measures and the relevant provisions of the tourism administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　旅游团队应当从国家开放口岸整团出入境。  
旅游团队出入境时，应当接受边防检查站对护照、签证、《名单表》的查验。经国务院有关部门批准，旅游团队可以到旅游目的地国家按照该国有关规定办理签证或者免签证。  
旅游团队出境前已确定分团入境的，组团社应当事先向出入境边防检查总站或者省级公安边防部门备案。  
旅游团队出境后因不可抗力或者其他特殊原因确需分团入境的，领队应当及时通知组团社，组团社应当立即向有关出入境边防检查总站或者省级公安边防部门备案。

**Article 11** A tour team shall exit and enter the country as a whole group from a state-open port.  
When a tour team exits or enters the country, it shall accept the inspection by the border inspection station of the passport, visa, and the List. Upon approval by the relevant department of the State Council, a tour team may apply for a visa or enjoy visa exemption in the destination country in accordance with the relevant provisions of that country.  
If it is determined before exit that a tour team will enter the country in subgroups, the organizing travel agency shall register with the exit-entry border inspection general station or the provincial public security border control department in advance.  
If a tour team needs to enter the country in subgroups after exit due to force majeure or other special reasons, the tour leader shall promptly notify the organizing travel agency, and the organizing travel agency shall immediately register with the relevant exit-entry border inspection general station or the provincial public security border control department.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　组团社应当维护旅游者的合法权益。  
组团社向旅游者提供的出国旅游服务信息必须真实可靠，不得作虚假宣传，报价不得低于成本。

**Article 12** An organizing travel agency shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of tourists.  
The outbound tourism service information provided by an organizing travel agency to tourists must be true and reliable; it shall not make false advertisements, and the quoted price shall not be lower than the cost.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　组团社经营出国旅游业务，应当与旅游者订立书面旅游合同。  
旅游合同应当包括旅游起止时间、行程路线、价格、食宿、交通以及违约责任等内容。旅游合同由组团社和旅游者各持一份。

**Article 13** An organizing travel agency that operates outbound tourism business shall conclude a written tourism contract with the tourists.  
The tourism contract shall include the start and end time of the tour, itinerary, price, accommodation, transportation, and liability for breach of contract, among other contents. The tourism contract shall be held by the organizing travel agency and the tourist respectively, each keeping one copy.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　组团社应当按照旅游合同约定的条件，为旅游者提供服务。  
组团社应当保证所提供的服务符合保障旅游者人身、财产安全的要求；对可能危及旅游者人身安全的情况，应当向旅游者作出真实说明和明确警示，并采取有效措施，防止危害的发生。

**Article 14** An organizing travel agency shall provide services to tourists in accordance with the conditions agreed in the tourism contract.  
An organizing travel agency shall ensure that the services provided meet the requirements for protecting the personal and property safety of tourists; in case of circumstances that may endanger the personal safety of tourists, it shall give true explanations and clear warnings to the tourists, and take effective measures to prevent the occurrence of harm.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　组团社组织旅游者出国旅游，应当选择在目的地国家依法设立并具有良好信誉的旅行社(以下简称境外接待社)，并与之订立书面合同后，方可委托其承担接待工作。

**Article 15** When an organizing travel agency organizes tourists for outbound tourism, it shall select a travel agency that is lawfully established in the destination country and has good reputation (hereinafter referred to as the overseas receiving travel agency), and only after concluding a written contract with it may it entrust it with the reception work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　组团社及其旅游团队领队应当要求境外接待社按照约定的团队活动计划安排旅游活动，并要求其不得组织旅游者参与涉及色情、赌博、毒品内容的活动或者危险性活动，不得擅自改变行程、减少旅游项目，不得强迫或者变相强迫旅游者参加额外付费项目。  
境外接待社违反组团社及其旅游团队领队根据前款规定提出的要求时，组团社及其旅游团队领队应当予以制止。

**Article 16** An organizing travel agency and its tour leader of the tour team shall require the overseas receiving travel agency to arrange tourism activities in accordance with the agreed team activity plan, and shall require it not to organize tourists to participate in activities involving pornography, gambling, or drugs or dangerous activities, not to change the itinerary without authorization or reduce tourism items, and not to force or covertly force tourists to participate in extra-paid items.  
When the overseas receiving travel agency violates the requirements raised by the organizing travel agency and its tour leader pursuant to the preceding paragraph, the organizing travel agency and its tour leader shall stop such violation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　旅游团队领队应当向旅游者介绍旅游目的地国家的相关法律、风俗习惯以及其他有关注意事项，并尊重旅游者的人格尊严、宗教信仰、民族风俗和生活习惯。

**Article 17** The tour leader of a tour team shall introduce to tourists the relevant laws, customs, and other related precautions of the destination country, and shall respect the tourists' personal dignity, religious beliefs, ethnic customs, and living habits.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　旅游团队领队在带领旅游者旅行、游览过程中，应当就可能危及旅游者人身安全的情况，向旅游者作出真实说明和明确警示，并按照组团社的要求采取有效措施，防止危害的发生。

**Article 18** During the process of leading tourists in travel and sightseeing, the tour leader shall, with respect to circumstances that may endanger the personal safety of tourists, give true explanations and clear warnings to the tourists, and take effective measures in accordance with the requirements of the organizing travel agency to prevent the occurrence of harm.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　旅游团队在境外遇到特殊困难和安全问题时，领队应当及时向组团社和中国驻所在国家使领馆报告；组团社应当及时向旅游行政部门和公安机关报告。

**Article 19** When a tour team encounters special difficulties or safety issues abroad, the tour leader shall promptly report to the organizing travel agency and the Chinese embassy or consulate in the host country; the organizing travel agency shall promptly report to the tourism administration department and the public security organ.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　旅游团队领队不得与境外接待社、导游及为旅游者提供商品或者服务的其他经营者串通欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费，不得向境外接待社、导游及其他为旅游者提供商品或者服务的经营者索要回扣、提成或者收受其财物。

**Article 20** The tour leader of a tour team shall not collude with the overseas receiving travel agency, tour guide, or other operators providing goods or services to tourists to deceive or coerce tourists into consumption, and shall not demand kickbacks, commissions, or accept property from the overseas receiving travel agency, tour guide, or other operators providing goods or services to tourists.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　旅游者应当遵守旅游目的地国家的法律，尊重当地的风俗习惯，并服从旅游团队领队的统一管理。

**Article 21** Tourists shall abide by the laws of the destination country, respect local customs and habits, and obey the unified management of the tour leader.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　严禁旅游者在境外滞留不归。  
旅游者在境外滞留不归的，旅游团队领队应当及时向组团社和中国驻所在国家使领馆报告，组团社应当及时向公安机关和旅游行政部门报告。有关部门处理有关事项时，组团社有义务予以协助。

**Article 22** Tourists shall be strictly prohibited from overstaying abroad.  
Where a tourist overstays abroad, the tour leader shall promptly report to the organizing travel agency and the Chinese embassy or consulate in the host country, and the organizing travel agency shall promptly report to the public security organ and the tourism administrative department. When the relevant department handles the relevant matters, the organizing travel agency shall have the obligation to provide assistance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　旅游者对组团社或者旅游团队领队违反本办法规定的行为，有权向旅游行政部门投诉。

**Article 23** Tourists shall have the right to file complaints with the tourism administrative department against the organizing travel agency or the tour leader for violating the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因组团社或者其委托的境外接待社违约，使旅游者合法权益受到损害的，组团社应当依法对旅游者承担赔偿责任。

**Article 24** Where the organizing travel agency or its entrusted overseas receiving agency breaches the contract, causing damage to the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, the organizing travel agency shall bear compensation liability to the tourists according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　组团社有下列情形之一的，旅游行政部门可以暂停其经营出国旅游业务；情节严重的，取消其出国旅游业务经营资格：  
(一)入境旅游业绩下降的；  
(二)因自身原因，在1年内未能正常开展出国旅游业务的；  
(三)因出国旅游服务质量问题被投诉并经查实的；  
(四)有逃汇、非法套汇行为的；  
(五)以旅游名义弄虚作假，骗取护照、签证等出入境证件或者送他人出境的；  
(六)国务院旅游行政部门认定的影响中国公民出国旅游秩序的其他行为。

**Article 25** Where the organizing travel agency is under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department may suspend its outbound tourism business; if the circumstances are serious, it shall cancel its qualification for outbound tourism business:  
(1) the inbound tourism performance declines;  
(2) due to its own reasons, it fails to normally carry out outbound tourism business within one year;  
(3) complaints are filed due to the quality of outbound tourism services and verified;  
(4) it engages in evasion of foreign exchange or illegal arbitrage of foreign exchange;  
(5) it practices fraud in the name of tourism to obtain passports, visas, or other exit-entry documents, or sends others abroad;  
(6) other acts determined by the tourism administrative department of the State Council that affect the order of Chinese citizens' outbound tourism.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　任何单位和个人违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)的规定，未经批准擅自经营或者以商务、考察、培训等方式变相经营出国旅游业务的，由旅游行政部门责令停止非法经营，没收违法所得，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Where any unit or individual violates Article 4 of these Measures by operating outbound tourism business without approval or in disguised form through business, investigation, training, or other means, the tourism administrative department shall order it to cease the illegal operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　组团社违反[本办法第十条](#art-10)的规定，不为旅游团队安排专职领队的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，并处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款，可以暂停其出国旅游业务经营资格；多次不安排专职领队的，并取消其出国旅游业务经营资格。

**Article 27** Where the organizing travel agency violates Article 10 of these Measures by failing to arrange a full-time tour leader for the tour group, the tourism administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan, and may suspend its qualification for outbound tourism business; if it repeatedly fails to arrange a full-time tour leader, its qualification for outbound tourism business shall be cancelled.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　组团社违反[本办法第十二条](#art-12)的规定，向旅游者提供虚假服务信息或者低于成本报价的，由工商行政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法》、《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》的有关规定给予处罚。

**Article 28** Where the organizing travel agency violates Article 12 of these Measures by providing false service information to tourists or offering prices below cost, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Consumer Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　组团社或者旅游团队领队违反[本办法第十四条](#art-14)第二款、第十八条的规定，对可能危及人身安全的情况未向旅游者作出真实说明和明确警示，或者未采取防止危害发生的措施的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对组团社暂停其出国旅游业务经营资格，并处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款，对旅游团队领队可以暂扣直至吊销其导游证；造成人身伤亡事故的，依法追究刑事责任，并承担赔偿责任。

**Article 29** Where the organizing travel agency or the tour leader violates Article 14, paragraph 2, or Article 18 of these Measures by failing to give truthful explanations and clear warnings to tourists regarding situations that may endanger personal safety, or failing to take measures to prevent the occurrence of hazards, the tourism administrative department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, for the organizing travel agency, its qualification for outbound tourism business shall be suspended and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; for the tour leader, his or her tour guide certificate may be temporarily seized until revoked; if personal injury or death accident is caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law, and compensation liability shall be borne.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　组团社或者旅游团队领队违反[本办法第十六条](#art-16)的规定，未要求境外接待社不得组织旅游者参与涉及色情、赌博、毒品内容的活动或者危险性活动，未要求其不得擅自改变行程、减少旅游项目、强迫或者变相强迫旅游者参加额外付费项目，或者在境外接待社违反前述要求时未制止的，由旅游行政部门对组团社处组织该旅游团队所收取费用2倍以上5倍以下的罚款，并暂停其出国旅游业务经营资格，对旅游团队领队暂扣其导游证；造成恶劣影响的，对组团社取消其出国旅游业务经营资格，对旅游团队领队吊销其导游证。

**Article 30** Where the organizing travel agency or the tour leader violates Article 16 of these Measures by failing to require the overseas receiving agency not to organize tourists to participate in activities involving pornography, gambling, or drugs, or dangerous activities, by failing to require it not to alter the itinerary without authorization, reduce tourism items, or force or covertly force tourists to participate in additional fee-charging items, or by failing to stop the overseas receiving agency when it violates the aforesaid requirements, the tourism administrative department shall impose on the organizing travel agency a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the fees charged for organizing the tour group, and suspend its qualification for outbound tourism business; and shall temporarily seize the tour leader's tour guide certificate; if it causes adverse effects, the organizing travel agency's qualification for outbound tourism business shall be cancelled, and the tour leader's tour guide certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　旅游团队领队违反[本办法第二十条](#art-20)的规定，与境外接待社、导游及为旅游者提供商品或者服务的其他经营者串通欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费或者向境外接待社、导游和其他为旅游者提供商品或者服务的经营者索要回扣、提成或者收受其财物的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，没收索要的回扣、提成或者收受的财物，并处索要的回扣、提成或者收受的财物价值2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，并吊销其导游证。

**Article 31** Where the tour leader violates Article 20 of these Measures by colluding with the overseas receiving agency, tour guides, or other operators providing goods or services to tourists to deceive or coerce tourists into consumption, or by demanding kickbacks, commissions, or accepting money or goods from the overseas receiving agency, tour guides, or other operators providing goods or services to tourists, the tourism administrative department shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the kickbacks, commissions, or money and goods demanded or accepted, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the kickbacks, commissions, or money and goods demanded or accepted; if the circumstances are serious, the tour guide certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反[本办法第二十二条](#art-22)的规定，旅游者在境外滞留不归，旅游团队领队不及时向组团社和中国驻所在国家使领馆报告，或者组团社不及时向有关部门报告的，由旅游行政部门给予警告，对旅游团队领队可以暂扣其导游证，对组团社可以暂停其出国旅游业务经营资格。  
旅游者因滞留不归被遣返回国的，由公安机关吊销其护照。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of Article 22 of these Measures, a tourist overstays abroad and the tour leader fails to promptly report to the organizing travel agency and the Chinese embassy or consulate in the host country, or the organizing travel agency fails to promptly report to the relevant department, the tourism administrative department shall give a warning, may temporarily seize the tour leader's tour guide certificate, and may suspend the organizing travel agency's qualification for outbound tourism business.  
Where a tourist is repatriated due to overstaying abroad, the public security organ shall revoke his or her passport.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本办法自2002年7月1日起施行。国务院1997年3月17日批准，国家旅游局、公安部1997年7月1日发布的《中国公民自费出国旅游管理暂行办法》同时废止。

**Article 33** These Measures shall come into force as of July 1, 2002. The Interim Measures for the Administration of Chinese Citizens' Outbound Tourism at Their Own Expense, approved by the State Council on March 17, 1997, and promulgated by the National Tourism Administration and the Ministry of Public Security on July 1, 1997, shall be abolished simultaneously.
