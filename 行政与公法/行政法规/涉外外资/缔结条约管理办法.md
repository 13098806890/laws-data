# 缔结条约管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-10-16  
**生效日期**：2023-01-01

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范缔结条约工作程序，加强对缔结条约事务的管理，根据《中华人民共和国缔结条约程序法》（以下简称缔约程序法）和有关法律规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Procedure of Concluding Treaties (hereinafter referred to as the Treaty Procedure Law) and relevant legal provisions for the purposes of standardizing the working procedures for concluding treaties and strengthening the management of treaty conclusion affairs.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国务院或者国务院有关部门缔结条约、协定和其他具有条约、协定性质的文件（以下统称条约），办理相关事务，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the conclusion of treaties, agreements and other documents of a treaty or agreement nature (hereinafter collectively referred to as treaties) by the State Council or the relevant departments of the State Council, and to the handling of relevant affairs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外交部在国务院领导下管理缔结条约的具体事务，指导、督促国务院有关部门依照法定程序办理缔结条约工作。  
国务院有关部门在各自职权范围内负责办理缔结条约的相关工作。

**Article 3** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, under the leadership of the State Council, manage the specific affairs concerning the conclusion of treaties, and guide and supervise the relevant departments of the State Council in processing the work of concluding treaties in accordance with statutory procedures.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and powers, be responsible for handling the relevant work of concluding treaties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　除中华人民共和国宪法、法律和国务院另有授权外，地方各级政府无权缔结条约。

**Article 4** No local government at any level shall have the authority to conclude treaties, unless otherwise authorized by the Constitution of the People's Republic of China, laws, or the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　下列条约，除法律另有规定外，应当以中华人民共和国名义缔结：  
（一）友好合作条约、和平条约等政治性条约；  
（二）有关领土和划定边界的条约，包括划定陆地边界和海域边界的条约；  
（三）有关司法合作的条约，包括司法协助、引渡、被判刑人移管、承认与执行外国法院判决或者仲裁裁决的条约；  
（四）其他涉及重大国家利益的条约。  
特殊情况下，经国务院审核决定，前款所列条约可以中华人民共和国政府名义缔结。

**Article 5** The following treaties shall be concluded in the name of the People's Republic of China, unless otherwise provided by law:  
(1) political treaties such as treaties of friendship and cooperation and peace treaties;  
(2) treaties concerning territory and delimitation of boundaries, including treaties on delimitation of land boundaries and maritime boundaries;  
(3) treaties concerning judicial cooperation, including treaties on judicial assistance, extradition, transfer of sentenced persons, recognition and enforcement of judgments of foreign courts or arbitral awards; and  
(4) other treaties involving major national interests.  
Under special circumstances, upon examination and decision by the State Council, the treaties enumerated in the preceding paragraph may be concluded in the name of the Government of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列条约，应当以中华人民共和国政府名义缔结：  
（一）涉及国务院职权范围的条约；  
（二）涉及两个以上国务院有关部门职权范围的条约；  
（三）其他需要以中华人民共和国政府名义缔结的条约。  
特殊情况下，经国务院审核决定，前款所列条约可以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结。

**Article 6** The following treaties shall be concluded in the name of the Government of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) treaties involving the functions and powers of the State Council;  
(2) treaties involving the functions and powers of two or more relevant departments of the State Council; and  
(3) other treaties that need to be concluded in the name of the Government of the People's Republic of China.  
Under special circumstances, upon examination and decision by the State Council, the treaties enumerated in the preceding paragraph may be concluded in the name of a department of the Government of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院各部、委员会、中国人民银行、审计署、具有行政管理职能的直属机构、根据法律规定或者国务院授权承担行政管理职能的国务院其他机构，可以就本部门职权范围内的事项，以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结条约。

**Article 7** The ministries and commissions of the State Council, the People's Bank of China, the National Audit Office, the institutions directly under the State Council performing administrative functions, and other bodies of the State Council that undertake administrative functions in accordance with legal provisions or authorization by the State Council may conclude treaties in the name of a department of the Government of the People's Republic of China on matters falling within their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　条约内容涉及政治、外交、经济、社会、安全等领域重大国家利益的，应当将条约草案及条约草案涉及的重大问题按照有关规定报告党中央。

**Article 8** Where the content of a treaty involves major national interests in the fields of politics, foreign affairs, economy, society, security, etc., the draft treaty and major issues involved in the draft treaty shall be reported to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　以中华人民共和国名义或者中华人民共和国政府名义谈判条约的，由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部在启动谈判前不少于20个工作日报请国务院决定。

**Article 9** Where a treaty is to be negotiated in the name of the People's Republic of China or the Government of the People's Republic of China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, shall submit a report to the State Council for decision no later than 20 working days before the commencement of negotiations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　以中华人民共和国政府部门名义谈判条约，有下列情形之一的，由国务院有关部门会同外交部在启动谈判前不少于20个工作日报请国务院决定：  
（一）条约内容涉及外交、经济、安全等领域的重大国家利益；  
（二）条约内容涉及国务院其他部门职权范围；  
（三）其他应当报请国务院决定的情形。

**Article 10** Where a treaty is to be negotiated in the name of a department of the Government of the People's Republic of China, in any of the following circumstances, the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall submit a report to the State Council for decision no later than 20 working days before the commencement of negotiations:  
(1) the content of the treaty involves major national interests in the fields of foreign affairs, economy, security, etc.;  
(2) the content of the treaty involves the functions and powers of other departments of the State Council; or  
(3) other circumstances that require reporting to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　条约谈判中，对经国务院决定的条约中方草案所作改动有下列情形之一的，应当重新报请国务院决定：  
（一）对中华人民共和国依据该条约享有的权利或者承担的义务有重大影响；  
（二）对中华人民共和国在有关重大问题上的立场有影响；  
（三）与中华人民共和国法律、行政法规或者中华人民共和国依据其他条约承担的国际义务不一致；  
（四）其他应当报请国务院决定的情形。

**Article 11** Where, during the negotiation of a treaty, changes are made to the Chinese draft treaty that has been decided by the State Council, and any of the following circumstances occurs, a new report shall be submitted to the State Council for decision:  
(1) the changes have a major impact on the rights enjoyed or obligations undertaken by the People's Republic of China pursuant to the treaty;  
(2) the changes affect the position of the People's Republic of China on major issues concerned;  
(3) the changes are inconsistent with the laws, administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China or the international obligations undertaken by the People's Republic of China under other treaties; or  
(4) other circumstances that require reporting to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　以中华人民共和国名义或者中华人民共和国政府名义签署条约，或者有[本办法第十条](#art-10)规定情形之一的，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部在签署前不少于10个工作日报请国务院决定。  
确因特殊情况未按前款规定时限报批的，应当向国务院说明理由。

**Article 12** Where a treaty is to be signed in the name of the People's Republic of China or the Government of the People's Republic of China, or any of the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Measures exists, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, shall submit a report to the State Council for decision no later than 10 working days before the signing.  
If, due to special circumstances, the report for approval is not submitted within the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph, reasons shall be provided to the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　条约签署前，国务院有关部门法制机构应当从法律角度对条约进行审查。  
以中华人民共和国名义或者中华人民共和国政府名义签署，且根据缔约程序法或者有关规定应当报请国务院审核并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入，或者报请国务院核准、决定加入或者接受的条约，内容与中华人民共和国法律、行政法规或者与中华人民共和国依据其他条约承担的国际义务不一致的，国务院有关部门应当在条约签署前不少于30个工作日征求司法部的意见。

**Article 13** Before a treaty is signed, the legal affairs department of the relevant department of the State Council shall review the treaty from a legal perspective.  
Where a treaty is to be signed in the name of the People's Republic of China or the Government of the People's Republic of China, and in accordance with the Treaty Procedure Law or relevant provisions shall be submitted to the State Council for examination and it is proposed that the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decide on ratification or accession, or submitted to the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance, and the content of the treaty is inconsistent with the laws, administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China, or the international obligations undertaken by the People's Republic of China under other treaties, the relevant department of the State Council shall seek the opinions of the Ministry of Justice no later than 30 working days before the signing of the treaty.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　下列情形，国务院有关部门应当在签署条约的请示中予以说明：  
（一）拟签署的条约内容与中华人民共和国法律、行政法规或者与中华人民共和国依据其他条约承担的国际义务是否一致；有不一致的，应当提出解决方案；根据[本办法第十三条](#art-13)规定应当征求司法部意见的，还应当附司法部的意见；  
（二）拟签署的条约是否需要征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府的意见以及征询意见的情况;  
（三）拟签署的条约属于多边条约的，是否需要作出声明或者保留以及声明或者保留的内容。

**Article 14** The relevant department of the State Council shall, in the request for instructions regarding the signing of a treaty, explain the following:  
(1) whether the content of the proposed treaty is consistent with the laws, administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China, or the international obligations undertaken by the People's Republic of China under other treaties; if there is any inconsistency, a solution shall be proposed; and if the opinions of the Ministry of Justice shall be sought in accordance with Article 13 of these Measures, the opinions of the Ministry of Justice shall also be attached;  
(2) whether the proposed treaty requires consultation with the governments of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the status of such consultation; and  
(3) if the proposed treaty is a multilateral treaty, whether a declaration or reservation is necessary and the content of such declaration or reservation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　条约的谈判代表或者签署代表需要出具全权证书的，国务院有关部门应当至少提前10个工作日书面通知外交部办理相关手续，并向外交部提供国务院同意委派该谈判代表或者签署代表的批件。  
条约的谈判代表或者签署代表需要出具授权证书的，由谈判代表或者签署代表所属的国务院有关部门办理。授权证书的格式由外交部规定。

**Article 15** Where full powers are required for the representative for negotiating or signing a treaty, the relevant department of the State Council shall notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing at least 10 working days in advance to handle the relevant formalities, and shall provide the Ministry of Foreign Affairs with the document approving the appointment of the representative for negotiating or signing as agreed by the State Council.  
Where letters of authorization are required for the representative for negotiating or signing a treaty, such letters shall be handled by the relevant department of the State Council to which the representative belongs. The format of the letter of authorization shall be specified by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下列条约，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部自签署之日起180日内报请国务院审核，并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准：  
（一）友好合作条约、和平条约等政治性条约；  
（二）有关领土和划定边界的条约，包括划定陆地边界和海域边界的条约；  
（三）有关司法合作的条约，包括司法协助、引渡、被判刑人移管、承认与执行外国法院判决或者仲裁裁决的条约；  
（四）与中华人民共和国法律有不同规定或者履行条约需要新制定法律的条约；  
（五）涉及中央预算调整的条约；  
（六）内容涉及[立法法第八条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国立法法.md#art-8)规定的只能制定法律的条约；  
（七）有关参加政治、经济、安全等领域重要国际组织的条约；  
（八）对外交、经济、安全等领域国家利益有重大影响的条约；  
（九）条约规定或者缔约各方议定须经批准的条约；  
（十）外交部或者国务院有关部门商外交部建议须经批准的条约。  
因特殊情况需要延长前款规定时限的，应当在期限届满前向国务院说明理由。

**Article 16** The following treaties shall be submitted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or by the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, to the State Council for examination within 180 days from the date of signing, and it shall be proposed that the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decide on ratification:  
(1) political treaties such as treaties of friendship and cooperation and peace treaties;  
(2) treaties concerning territory and delimitation of boundaries, including treaties on delimitation of land boundaries and maritime boundaries;  
(3) treaties concerning judicial cooperation, including treaties on judicial assistance, extradition, transfer of sentenced persons, recognition and enforcement of judgments of foreign courts or arbitral awards;  
(4) treaties that contain provisions different from those of the laws of the People's Republic of China, or that require new legislation for implementation;  
(5) treaties involving adjustment of the central budget;  
(6) treaties whose content falls within the scope of matters that may only be governed by law as specified in Article 8 of the Legislation Law;  
(7) treaties concerning participation in important international organizations in the fields of politics, economy, security, etc.;  
(8) treaties that have a significant impact on national interests in the fields of foreign affairs, economy, security, etc.;  
(9) treaties for which ratification is required as provided for in the treaty or as agreed by the parties; and  
(10) treaties that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or the relevant department of the State Council in consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, recommends shall be subject to ratification.  
If, due to special circumstances, the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph needs to be extended, reasons shall be provided to the State Council before the expiration of the time limit.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　下列条约，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部自签署之日起180日内报请国务院核准：  
（一）有关边界管理和边防事务的条约；  
（二）有关管制物资贸易或者技术合作的条约；  
（三）有关军工贸易和军控的条约；  
（四）与中华人民共和国行政法规有不同规定或者履行条约需要新制定行政法规的条约；  
（五）影响中央预算执行但不涉及预算调整的条约；  
（六）涉及中华人民共和国行政法规规定的税收制度的条约；  
（七）涉及扩大重要和关键行业外资准入的条约；  
（八）对外交、经济、安全等领域国家利益有较大影响的条约；  
（九）条约规定或者缔约各方议定须经核准的条约；  
（十）外交部或者国务院有关部门商外交部建议须经核准的条约。  
因特殊情况需要延长前款规定时限的，应当在期限届满前向国务院说明理由。

**Article 17** The following treaties shall be submitted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or by the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, to the State Council for ratification within 180 days from the date of signing:  
(1) treaties concerning boundary management and frontier defense affairs;  
(2) treaties concerning trade in controlled goods or technical cooperation;  
(3) treaties concerning military trade and arms control;  
(4) treaties that contain provisions different from those of the administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China, or that require new administrative regulations for implementation;  
(5) treaties affecting the implementation of the central budget but not involving budget adjustment;  
(6) treaties involving the taxation system as provided for in the administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China;  
(7) treaties involving the expansion of foreign investment access in important and key industries;  
(8) treaties that have a relatively significant impact on national interests in the fields of foreign affairs, economy, security, etc.;  
(9) treaties for which ratification is required as provided for in the treaty or as agreed by the parties; and  
(10) treaties that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or the relevant department of the State Council in consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, recommends shall be subject to ratification.  
If, due to special circumstances, the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph needs to be extended, reasons shall be provided to the State Council before the expiration of the time limit.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　加入[本办法第十六条](#art-16)所列范围的多边条约，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部报请国务院审核，并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定。

**Article 18** Accession to a multilateral treaty falling within the scope specified in Article 16 of these Measures shall be submitted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or by the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, to the State Council for examination, and it shall be proposed that the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decide.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　接受多边条约或者加入[本办法第十七条](#art-17)所列范围的多边条约，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部报请国务院决定。

**Article 19** Acceptance of a multilateral treaty or accession to a multilateral treaty falling within the scope specified in Article 17 of these Measures shall be submitted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or by the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, to the State Council for decision.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　报请国务院审核并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入条约的，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部向国务院报送请示，并附送国务院提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入该条约的议案说明、条约作准中文本及其电子文本或者中译本及其电子文本，以及国务院审核同意的条约谈判和签署请示。  
属于多边条约的，还应当附送多边条约缔约方的批准、核准、加入和接受情况或者缔约方清单。

**Article 20** Where a treaty is submitted to the State Council for examination and it is proposed that the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress decide on ratification or accession, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, or the relevant department of the State Council jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, shall submit a request for instructions to the State Council, together with the explanation of the proposal submitted by the State Council to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress for its decision on ratification or accession to the treaty, the authentic Chinese text of the treaty and its electronic version, or the Chinese translation thereof and its electronic version, as well as the request for instructions on the negotiation and signing of the treaty that has been examined and approved by the State Council.  
If the treaty is a multilateral treaty, the status of ratification, approval, accession and acceptance by the parties to the multilateral treaty, or a list of the parties, shall also be attached.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　报请国务院核准、决定加入或者接受条约的，应当由外交部或者国务院有关部门会同外交部向国务院报送审核并建议核准、决定加入或者接受该条约的请示,并附送条约作准中文本及其电子文本或者中译本及其电子文本，以及国务院审核同意的条约谈判和签署请示。  
属于多边条约的，还应当附送多边条约缔约方的批准、核准、加入和接受情况或者缔约方清单。

**Article 21** Where a treaty is submitted to the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the relevant department of the State Council shall, jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, submit to the State Council a request for examination and a recommendation for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance of the treaty, together with the authentic Chinese text of the treaty and its electronic version, or the Chinese translation and its electronic version, as well as the request for treaty negotiation and signing that has been examined and approved by the State Council.  
In the case of a multilateral treaty, the status of ratification, approval, accession and acceptance by the parties to the multilateral treaty, or a list of parties, shall also be attached.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　条约报送部门在将条约报请国务院审核前，应当对条约作准中文本或者中译本进行认真审核，确保内容及其文字表述准确、中外文本一致，格式符合规范要求。  
条约报送部门在将条约报请国务院审核前发现条约作准中文本有重大错误的，应当与相关国家、国际组织或者国际会议沟通，并在报送国务院的请示中就沟通情况和修改结果作出说明。

**Article 22** Before submitting a treaty to the State Council for examination, the department submitting the treaty shall carefully review the authentic Chinese text or the Chinese translation of the treaty to ensure that the content and wording are accurate, the Chinese and foreign texts are consistent, and the format complies with the prescribed requirements.  
If the department submitting the treaty discovers a major error in the authentic Chinese text of the treaty before submitting it to the State Council for examination, it shall communicate with the relevant country, international organization or international conference, and provide an explanation of the communication and the amendment results in the request submitted to the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　根据缔约程序法和有关规定，需要报国务院备案或者送外交部登记的条约，国务院有关部门应当自条约签署之日起90日内办理。

**Article 23** For a treaty that, in accordance with the Treaty-Making Procedure Law and relevant provisions, requires filing with the State Council or registration with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the relevant department of the State Council shall handle the matter within 90 days from the date of signature of the treaty.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　根据[本办法第十六条](#art-16)、第十七条、第十八条、第十九条规定报请国务院审核并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入，或者报请国务院核准、决定加入或者接受的条约由司法部审查，提出法律意见。

**Article 24** For a treaty that, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 16, 17, 18 and 19 of these Measures, is submitted to the State Council for examination with a recommendation to request the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress to decide on ratification or accession, or is submitted to the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance, the Ministry of Justice shall review the treaty and provide legal opinions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　缔结多边条约，除[本办法第二十七条](#art-27)规定的情形外，国务院有关部门应当在报请国务院审核并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入前，或者报请国务院核准、决定加入或者接受前，通过外交部分别征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府意见。多边条约规定缔约方不限于主权国家，且根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》和《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》，香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区有权单独签订的，可以不征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府意见。  
缔结双边条约，需要征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府意见的，由国务院有关部门会同外交部和国务院港澳事务机构参照前款规定办理。

**Article 25** In concluding a multilateral treaty, except as provided in Article 27 of these Measures, the relevant department of the State Council shall, before submitting the treaty to the State Council for examination with a recommendation to request the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress to decide on ratification or accession, or before submitting it to the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance, separately seek the views of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. If the multilateral treaty provides that the parties are not limited to sovereign states and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region are entitled to conclude it separately in accordance with the Basic Law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China and the Basic Law of the Macao Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China, the views of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region need not be sought.  
In concluding a bilateral treaty, if the views of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region need to be sought, the relevant department of the State Council shall, jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office of the State Council, handle the matter with reference to the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　根据[本办法第二十五条](#art-25)规定，外交部征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府意见的事项包括：  
（一）条约是否适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区；  
（二）拟对条约作出的有关声明或者保留是否适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区；  
（三）香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区是否需要作出其他声明或者保留；  
（四）条约已经适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区，且为香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区作出声明或者保留的，原声明或者保留是否继续有效。

**Article 26** In accordance with Article 25 of these Measures, the matters on which the Ministry of Foreign Affairs seeks the views of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region include:  
(1) whether the treaty shall apply to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(2) whether the relevant declarations or reservations to be made to the treaty are applicable to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region;  
(3) whether the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region need to make other declarations or reservations;  
(4) where the treaty has already applied to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region and declarations or reservations have been made for the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region, whether the original declarations or reservations shall remain in force.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　涉及外交、国防事务的条约，或者根据条约性质、规定应当适用于中华人民共和国全部领土的条约，国务院有关部门应当在报请国务院审核并建议提请全国人民代表大会常务委员会决定批准或者加入前，或者报请国务院核准、决定加入或者接受前，通过外交部通知香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府条约将适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区。  
拟对条约作出的声明或者保留，涉及外交、国防事务的，依照前款规定办理。

**Article 27** For a treaty that involves foreign affairs or national defense matters, or a treaty that, by its nature or provisions, shall apply to the entire territory of the People's Republic of China, the relevant department of the State Council shall, before submitting the treaty to the State Council for examination with a recommendation to request the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress to decide on ratification or accession, or before submitting it to the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance, notify the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs that the treaty will apply to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
Where a declaration or reservation to be made to the treaty involves foreign affairs or national defense matters, it shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院有关部门应当自收到全国人民代表大会常务委员会批准或者加入条约的决定之日起或者自收到国务院核准、决定加入或者接受条约的批复之日起20个工作日内，书面通知外交部办理制作、交存或者交换批准书、核准书、加入书或者接受书的具体手续。国务院有关部门对于交存或者交换时机有特别安排的，应当作出说明。  
需要向多边条约保存机关通知不接受多边条约的，国务院有关部门应当于条约规定的期限届满至少10个工作日前书面通知外交部办理具体手续。  
外交部在交存或者交换批准书、核准书、加入书或者接受书时应当提交政府声明。政府声明应当包括条约是否适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的内容。

**Article 28** The relevant department of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on ratification of or accession to the treaty, or from the date of receipt of the approval of the State Council for ratification, decision on accession or acceptance of the treaty, notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing to handle the specific procedures for the preparation, deposit or exchange of instruments of ratification, approval, accession or acceptance. If the relevant department of the State Council has special arrangements for the timing of deposit or exchange, it shall provide an explanation.  
Where it is necessary to notify the depositary of a multilateral treaty that the multilateral treaty is not accepted, the relevant department of the State Council shall notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing to handle the specific procedures at least 10 working days before the expiry of the period prescribed by the treaty.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall submit a government declaration when depositing or exchanging instruments of ratification, approval, accession or acceptance. The government declaration shall include information on whether the treaty applies to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　缔结双边条约，需要与缔约另一方相互通知已完成条约生效需要履行的国内法律程序的，国务院有关部门应当自完成批准、核准或者备案手续之日起20个工作日内或者在报送外交部登记时，书面通知外交部办理。

**Article 29** In concluding a bilateral treaty, if it is necessary to notify the other contracting party of the completion of the domestic legal procedures required for the entry into force of the treaty, the relevant department of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of completion of the approval, ratification or filing procedures, or at the time of registration with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing to handle the matter.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的条约，国务院有关部门应当自条约对中华人民共和国生效的手续办理完毕之日起20个工作日内，将条约适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的情况，通过外交部通知香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府。

**Article 30** For a treaty that applies to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region, the relevant department of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of completion of the formalities for the treaty's entry into force for the People's Republic of China, notify the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the application of the treaty to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　条约扩展适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的，国务院有关部门应当参照[本办法第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条的规定通过外交部分别征询香港特别行政区政府、澳门特别行政区政府意见，并会同外交部提出建议，报请国务院决定。  
外交部应当根据国务院批复，向多边条约保存机关提交多边条约扩展适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的政府声明，或者与双边条约缔约方达成条约适用于香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的具体安排。

**Article 31** Where a treaty is extended to apply to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region, the relevant department of the State Council shall, with reference to the provisions of Articles 25 and 26 of these Measures, separately seek the views of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and, jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, submit recommendations to the State Council for a decision.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall, in accordance with the approval of the State Council, submit to the depositary of the multilateral treaty a government declaration on the extension of the multilateral treaty to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region, or reach a specific arrangement with the other party to a bilateral treaty on the application of the treaty to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　根据缔约程序法第十四条的规定保存条约签字正本的，国务院有关部门应当自条约签署之日起90日内将条约签字正本送外交部保存,并附送条约作准中文本或者中译本、作准外文本和相关电子文本。  
多边条约拟由中华人民共和国作为正本保存国的，国务院有关部门应当事先商外交部同意，并由外交部履行保存机关职责。

**Article 32** Where the original signed text of a treaty is to be preserved in accordance with Article 14 of the Treaty-Making Procedure Law, the relevant department of the State Council shall, within 90 days from the date of signature of the treaty, deliver the original signed text to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for preservation, together with the authentic Chinese text or Chinese translation, the authentic foreign text and relevant electronic versions.  
If a multilateral treaty is intended to have the People's Republic of China as the depositary of the original text, the relevant department of the State Council shall obtain prior consent from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall perform the duties of the depositary.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院核准、决定加入或者接受的条约或者向国务院备案的条约，应当及时由国务院公报予以公布。  
以中华人民共和国政府部门名义缔结的条约，国务院有关部门应当及时予以公布。

**Article 33** Treaties ratified, decided on accession or acceptance by the State Council, or treaties filed with the State Council, shall be promptly published by the State Council Gazette.  
Treaties concluded in the name of a government department of the People's Republic of China shall be promptly published by the relevant department of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外交部应当编辑出版《中华人民共和国条约集》，并建设和维护数字化的条约数据库。

**Article 34** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall compile and publish the Collection of Treaties of the People's Republic of China, and build and maintain a digital treaty database.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　撤回或者修改对条约作出的声明或者保留，依照作出声明或者保留的程序办理，但[本办法第三十一条](#art-31)规定的情形除外。  
全国人民代表大会常务委员会或者国务院决定撤回或者修改对条约作出的声明或者保留的，国务院有关部门应当自撤回或者修改决定作出之日起20个工作日内，书面通知外交部办理通知多边条约保存机关的手续。

**Article 35** The withdrawal or modification of a declaration or reservation made to a treaty shall be handled in accordance with the procedures for making the declaration or reservation, except for the circumstances provided for in Article 31 of these Measures.  
Where the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress or the State Council decides to withdraw or modify a declaration or reservation made to a treaty, the relevant department of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of the decision on withdrawal or modification, notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in writing to handle the procedures for notifying the depositary of the multilateral treaty.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本办法自2023年1月1日起施行。

**Article 36** These Measures shall come into force as of January 1, 2023.
