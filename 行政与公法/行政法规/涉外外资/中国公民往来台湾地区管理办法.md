# 中国公民往来台湾地区管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2015-06-14  
**生效日期**：1992-05-01

> (1991年12月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第93号发布　根据2015年6月14日《国务院关于修改〈中国公民往来台湾地区管理办法〉的决定》修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障台湾海峡两岸人员往来，促进各方交流，维护社会秩序，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to ensure the travel of personnel across the Taiwan Strait, promote exchanges among all parties, and maintain social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　居住在大陆的中国公民(以下简称大陆居民)往来台湾地区(以下简称台湾)以及居住在台湾地区的中国公民(以下简称台湾居民)来往大陆，适用本办法。  
本办法未规定的事项，其他有关法律、法规有规定的，适用其他法律、法规。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the travel of Chinese citizens residing in the mainland (hereinafter referred to as mainland residents) to and from the Taiwan region (hereinafter referred to as Taiwan) and the travel of Chinese citizens residing in the Taiwan region (hereinafter referred to as Taiwan residents) to and from the mainland.  
Matters not provided for in these Measures shall be governed by other relevant laws and regulations if so provided.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　大陆居民前往台湾，凭公安机关出入境管理部门签发的旅行证件，从开放的或者指定的出入境口岸通行。

**Article 3** Mainland residents traveling to Taiwan shall pass through open or designated entry-exit ports with travel documents issued by the exit-entry administration department of the public security organ.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　台湾居民来大陆，凭国家主管机关签发的旅行证件，从开放的或者指定的入出境口岸通行。

**Article 4** Taiwan residents coming to the mainland shall pass through open or designated entry-exit ports with travel documents issued by the competent state authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中国公民往来台湾与大陆之间，不得有危害国家安全、荣誉和利益的行为。

**Article 5** Chinese citizens traveling between Taiwan and the mainland shall not engage in acts that harm national security, honor, or interests.

### 第二章　大陆居民前往台湾

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　大陆居民前往台湾定居、探亲、访友、旅游、接受和处理财产、处理婚丧事宜或者参加经济、科技、文化、教育、体育、学术等活动，须向户口所在地的市、县公安局提出申请。

**Article 6** A mainland resident who goes to Taiwan for the purpose of residence, visiting relatives, visiting friends, tourism, accepting or disposing of property, handling marriage or funeral matters, or participating in economic, scientific, technological, cultural, educational, sports, academic or other activities shall submit an application to the public security bureau of the city or county where his or her household registration is located.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　大陆居民申请前往台湾，须履行下列手续：  
(一)交验身份、户口证明；  
(二)填写前往台湾申请表；  
(三)在职、在学人员须提交所在单位对申请人前往台湾的意见；非在职、在学人员须提交户口所在地公安派出所对申请人前往台湾的意见；  
(四)提交与申请事由相应的证明。

**Article 7** A mainland resident applying to travel to Taiwan shall go through the following procedures:  
(1) produce and submit identity and household registration certificates;  
(2) fill in an application form for travel to Taiwan;  
(3) employed or enrolled persons shall submit the opinion of their employer or school on the applicant's travel to Taiwan; non-employed or non-enrolled persons shall submit the opinion of the public security police station at the place of household registration on the applicant's travel to Taiwan;  
(4) submit certificates corresponding to the purpose of the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[本办法第七条](#art-7)第四项所称的证明是指：  
(一)前往定居，须提交确能在台湾定居的证明；  
(二)探亲、访友，须提交台湾亲友关系的证明；  
(三)旅游，须提交旅行所需费用的证明；  
(四)接受、处理财产，须提交经过公证的对该项财产有合法权利的有关证明；  
(五)处理婚姻事务，须提交经过公证的有关婚姻状况的证明；  
(六)处理亲友丧事，须提交有关的函件或者通知；  
(七)参加经济、科技、文化、教育、体育、学术等活动，须提交台湾相应机构、团体、个人邀请或者同意参加该项活动的证明；  
(八)主管机关认为需要提交的其他证明。

**Article 8** The "certificates" referred to in item (4) of Article 7 of these Measures mean:  
(1) for residence, a certificate certifying the ability to reside in Taiwan shall be submitted;  
(2) for visiting relatives or friends, a certificate of kinship relations in Taiwan shall be submitted;  
(3) for tourism, a certificate of the funds required for travel shall be submitted;  
(4) for accepting or disposing of property, relevant notarized certificates of lawful rights to the property shall be submitted;  
(5) for handling marriage affairs, relevant notarized certificates of marital status shall be submitted;  
(6) for handling funeral matters of relatives or friends, relevant correspondence or notifications shall be submitted;  
(7) for participating in economic, scientific, technological, cultural, educational, sports, academic or other activities, a certificate of invitation or consent from the relevant institution, organization, or individual in Taiwan shall be submitted;  
(8) other certificates that the competent authorities deem necessary.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公安机关受理大陆居民前往台湾的申请，应当在30日内，地处偏僻、交通不便的应当在60日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定，通知申请人。紧急的申请，应当随时办理。

**Article 9** The public security organ, after accepting an application from a mainland resident to travel to Taiwan, shall make a decision to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant within 30 days, or within 60 days if the applicant's location is remote and transportation is inconvenient. Urgent applications shall be processed promptly.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　经批准前往台湾的大陆居民，由公安机关签发或者签注旅行证件。

**Article 10** A mainland resident who is approved to travel to Taiwan shall be issued a travel document or have a travel document endorsed by the public security organ.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经批准前往台湾的大陆居民，应当在所持旅行证件签注的有效期内前往，除定居的以外，应当按期返回。  
大陆居民前往台湾后，因病或者其他特殊情况，旅行证件到期不能按期返回的，可以向原发证的公安机关或者公安部出入境管理局派出的或者委托的有关机构申请办理延期手续；有特殊原因的也可以在入境口岸的公安机关申请办理入境手续。

**Article 11** A mainland resident who is approved to travel to Taiwan shall travel within the validity period of the endorsement on his or her travel document and, except for those going for residence, shall return on schedule.  
If a mainland resident, after traveling to Taiwan, is unable to return on schedule due to illness or other special circumstances and the travel document expires, he or she may apply to the public security organ that originally issued the document or to the agencies dispatched or authorized by the Exit-Entry Administration Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security for an extension; for special reasons, he or she may also apply for entry procedures at the public security organ at the port of entry.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请前往台湾的大陆居民有下列情形之一的，不予批准：  
(一)刑事案件的被告人或者犯罪嫌疑人；  
(二)人民法院通知有未了结诉讼事宜不能离境的；  
(三)被判处刑罚尚未执行完毕的；  
(四)正在被劳动教养的；  
(五)国务院有关主管部门认为出境后将对国家安全造成危害或者对国家利益造成重大损失的；  
(六)有编造情况、提供假证明等欺骗行为的。

**Article 12** The application of a mainland resident to travel to Taiwan shall not be approved under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the applicant is a defendant or criminal suspect in a criminal case;  
(2) the People's Court has notified that the applicant has unfinished litigation matters and may not leave the country;  
(3) the applicant has been sentenced to a penalty that has not yet been fully enforced;  
(4) the applicant is undergoing reeducation through labor;  
(5) the relevant competent department of the State Council finds that the applicant's departure would cause harm to national security or a major loss to national interests;  
(6) the applicant has engaged in fraudulent conduct such as fabricating information or providing false certificates.

### 第三章　台湾居民来大陆

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　台湾居民要求来大陆的，向下列有关机关申请办理旅行证件：  
(一)从台湾地区要求直接来大陆的，向公安部出入境管理局派出的或者委托的有关机构申请；有特殊事由的，也可以向指定口岸的公安机关申请；  
(二)到香港、澳门地区后要求来大陆的，向公安部出入境管理局派出的机构或者委托的在香港、澳门地区的有关机构申请；  
(三)经由外国来大陆的，依据《中华人民共和国护照法》，向中华人民共和国驻外国的外交代表机关、领事机关或者外交部授权的其他驻外机关申请。

**Article 13** A Taiwan resident who wishes to come to the mainland shall apply for a travel document from the following relevant authorities:  
(1) if coming directly from the Taiwan region, apply to the agencies dispatched or authorized by the Exit-Entry Administration Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security; for special reasons, may also apply to the public security organ at a designated port;  
(2) if coming to the mainland after arriving in the Hong Kong or Macao region, apply to the agencies dispatched by the Exit-Entry Administration Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security or the relevant agencies authorized in the Hong Kong or Macao region;  
(3) if coming via a foreign country, apply to the diplomatic missions, consular offices, or other foreign offices authorized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China in accordance with the Passport Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　台湾居民申请来大陆，须履行下列手续：  
(一)交验表明在台湾居住的有效身份证明和出境入境证件；  
(二)填写申请表；  
(三)提交符合规定的照片。  
国家主管机关可以根据具体情况要求台湾居民提交其他申请材料。

**Article 14** A Taiwan resident applying to come to the mainland shall go through the following procedures:  
(1) produce and submit valid identity documents indicating residence in Taiwan and exit-entry documents;  
(2) fill in an application form;  
(3) submit photographs meeting the requirements.  
The competent state authorities may, based on specific circumstances, require Taiwan residents to submit other application materials.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对批准来大陆的台湾居民，由国家主管机关签发旅行证件。

**Article 15** A travel document shall be issued by the competent state authorities to a Taiwan resident who is approved to come to the mainland.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　台湾居民来大陆，应当按照户口管理规定，办理暂住登记。在宾馆、饭店、招待所、旅店、学校等企业、事业单位或者机关、团体和其他机构内住宿的，应当填写临时住宿登记表；住在亲友家的，由本人或者亲友在24小时(农村72小时)内到当地公安派出所或者户籍办公室办理暂住登记手续。

**Article 16** A Taiwan resident coming to the mainland shall, in accordance with the provisions on household registration, undergo temporary residence registration. If staying in hotels, restaurants, guesthouses, inns, schools or other enterprises, institutions, organs, organizations, or other establishments, the resident shall fill in a temporary accommodation registration form; if staying at the home of relatives or friends, the resident or the relatives or friends shall, within 24 hours (72 hours in rural areas), go to the local public security police station or household registration office to complete the temporary residence registration procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　台湾居民要求来大陆定居的，应当在入境前向公安部出入境管理局派出的或者委托的有关机构提出申请，或者经由大陆亲属向拟定居地的市、县公安局提出申请。批准定居的，公安机关发给定居证明。

**Article 17** A Taiwan resident who wishes to settle in the mainland shall, before entry, submit an application to the agencies dispatched or authorized by the Exit-Entry Administration Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security, or submit an application through a mainland relative to the public security bureau of the city or county of intended residence. If approved for settlement, the public security organ shall issue a residence certificate.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　台湾居民来大陆后，应当在所持旅行证件有效期之内按期离境。所持证件有效期即将届满需要继续居留的，应当向市、县公安局申请换发。

**Article 18** After coming to the mainland, a Taiwan resident shall depart within the validity period of the travel document. If the validity period of the document is about to expire and the resident needs to continue staying, he or she shall apply to the public security bureau of the city or county for a renewal.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请来大陆的台湾居民有下列情形之一的，不予批准：  
(一)被认为有犯罪行为的；  
(二)被认为来大陆后可能进行危害国家安全、利益等活动的；  
(三)不符合申请条件或者有编造情况、提供假证明等欺骗行为的；  
(四)精神疾病或者严重传染病患者；  
(五)法律、行政法规规定不予批准的其他情形。  
治病或者其他特殊原因可以批准入境的除外。

**Article 19** An application by a Taiwan resident to come to the mainland shall not be approved under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the applicant is deemed to have committed a criminal act;  
(2) the applicant is deemed likely to engage in activities harmful to national security or interests after coming to the mainland;  
(3) the applicant does not meet the application conditions or has engaged in fraudulent conduct such as fabricating information or providing false certificates;  
(4) the applicant suffers from a mental illness or a serious infectious disease;  
(5) other circumstances where approval is not granted as provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Exception: Entry may be approved for the purpose of medical treatment or other special reasons.

### 第四章　出境入境检查

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　大陆居民往来台湾，台湾居民来往大陆，须向开放的或者指定的出入境口岸边防检查站出示证件，填交出境、入境登记卡，接受查验。

**Article 20** Mainland residents traveling to and from Taiwan and Taiwan residents traveling to and from the mainland shall present their documents to the border inspection station at open or designated entry-exit ports, fill in and submit exit-entry registration cards, and undergo inspection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列情形之一的，边防检查站有权阻止出境、入境：  
(一)未持有旅行证件的；  
(二)持用伪造、涂改等无效的旅行证件的；  
(三)拒绝交验旅行证件的；  
(四)[本办法第十二条](#art-12)、第十九条规定不予批准出境、入境的。

**Article 21** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the border inspection station shall have the right to prevent exit or entry:  
(a) not holding a travel document;  
(b) holding an invalid travel document such as a forged or altered one;  
(c) refusing to produce the travel document for inspection;  
(d) where exit or entry is not approved as provided in Articles 12 and 19 of these Measures.

### 第五章　证件管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　大陆居民往来台湾的旅行证件系指大陆居民往来台湾通行证和其他有效旅行证件。

**Article 22** The term “travel documents for mainland residents traveling to and from Taiwan” means the Mainland Resident Travel Permit to and from Taiwan and other valid travel documents.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　台湾居民来往大陆的旅行证件系指台湾居民来往大陆通行证和其他有效旅行证件。

**Article 23** The term “travel documents for Taiwan residents traveling to and from the mainland” means the Taiwan Resident Travel Permit to and from the Mainland and other valid travel documents.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　大陆居民往来台湾通行证有效期为10年；台湾居民来往大陆通行证分为5年有效和3个月一次有效两种。

**Article 24** The Mainland Resident Travel Permit to and from Taiwan shall be valid for 10 years; the Taiwan Resident Travel Permit to and from the Mainland shall have two types: one valid for five years and one valid for a single entry within three months.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　大陆居民往来台湾通行证实行逐次签注。签注分一次往返有效和多次往返有效。

**Article 25** The Mainland Resident Travel Permit to and from Taiwan shall be subject to endorsement on each occasion. Endorsements shall be divided into those valid for a single round trip and those valid for multiple round trips.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　大陆居民遗失旅行证件，应当向原发证的公安机关报失；经调查属实的，可补发给相应的旅行证件。

**Article 26** Where a mainland resident loses his or her travel document, he or she shall report the loss to the public security organ that issued the original document; if the loss is verified upon investigation, the corresponding travel document may be reissued.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　台湾居民在大陆遗失旅行证件，应当向当地的市、县公安机关报失；经调查属实的，可以允许重新申请领取相应的旅行证件，或者发给一次有效的出境通行证件。

**Article 27** Where a Taiwan resident loses his or her travel document in the mainland, he or she shall report the loss to the local public security organ at the city or county level; if the loss is verified upon investigation, he or she may be permitted to reapply for and obtain the corresponding travel document, or be issued a one-time valid exit travel document.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　大陆居民前往台湾和台湾居民来大陆旅行证件的持有人，有[本办法第十二条](#art-12)、第十九条规定情形之一的，其证件应当予以吊销或者宣布作废。

**Article 28** Where a holder of a travel document for a mainland resident going to Taiwan or for a Taiwan resident coming to the mainland falls under any of the circumstances specified in Articles 12 and 19 of these Measures, the document shall be revoked or declared null and void.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　审批签发旅行证件的机关，对已发出的旅行证件有权吊销或者宣布作废。公安部在必要时，可以变更签注、吊销旅行证件或者宣布作废。

**Article 29** The authority that examines, approves, and issues travel documents shall have the right to revoke or declare null and void any travel document already issued. The Ministry of Public Security may, when necessary, alter endorsements, revoke travel documents, or declare them null and void.

### 第六章　处罚

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　持用伪造、涂改等无效的旅行证件或者冒用他人的旅行证件出境、入境的，除依照《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法实施细则》第二十三条的规定处罚外，可以单处或者并处100元以上、500元以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Any person who exits or enters by holding an invalid travel document such as a forged or altered one, or by using another person's travel document under false pretences, shall, in addition to being punished in accordance with Article 23 of the Rules for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit of Citizens, be liable to a fine of not less than RMB 100 yuan but not more than RMB 500 yuan, which may be imposed separately or concurrently.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　伪造、涂改、转让、倒卖旅行证件的，除依照《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法实施细则》第二十四条的规定处罚外，可以单处或者并处500元以上、3000元以下的罚款。

**Article 31** Any person who forges, alters, transfers, or scalps travel documents shall, in addition to being punished in accordance with Article 24 of the Rules for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit of Citizens, be liable to a fine of not less than RMB 500 yuan but not more than RMB 3,000 yuan, which may be imposed separately or concurrently.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　编造情况，提供假证明，或者以行贿等手段获取旅行证件的，除依照《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法实施细则》第二十五条的规定处罚外，可以单处或者并处100元以上、500元以下的罚款。  
有前款情形的，在处罚执行完毕6个月以内不受理其出境、入境申请。

**Article 32** Any person who fabricates circumstances, provides false certificates, or uses bribery or other means to obtain a travel document shall, in addition to being punished in accordance with Article 25 of the Rules for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit of Citizens, be liable to a fine of not less than RMB 100 yuan but not more than RMB 500 yuan, which may be imposed separately or concurrently.  
Where the circumstances described in the preceding paragraph exist, no application for exit or entry from such person shall be accepted within six months after the punishment is executed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　机关、团体、企业、事业单位编造情况、出具假证明为申请人获取旅行证件的，暂停其出证权的行使；情节严重的，取消其出证资格；对直接责任人员，除依照《中华人民共和国公民出境入境管理法实施细则》第二十五条的规定处罚外，可以单处或者并处500元以上、1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 33** Where any organ, organization, enterprise, or institution fabricates circumstances or issues false certificates to enable an applicant to obtain a travel document, the exercise of its authority to issue certificates shall be suspended; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification to issue certificates shall be revoked. With respect to the persons directly responsible, in addition to being punished in accordance with Article 25 of the Rules for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit of Citizens, a fine of not less than RMB 500 yuan but not more than RMB 1,000 yuan may be imposed separately or concurrently.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反[本办法第十六条](#art-16)的规定，不办理暂住登记的，处以警告或者100元以上、500元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Any person who violates the provisions of Article 16 of these Measures by failing to register for temporary residence shall be given a warning or be fined not less than RMB 100 yuan but not more than RMB 500 yuan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反[本办法第十八条](#art-18)的规定，逾期非法居留的，处以警告，可以单处或者并处每逾期1日100元的罚款。

**Article 35** Any person who violates the provisions of Article 18 of these Measures by overstaying and illegally residing shall be given a warning and may be liable to a fine of RMB 100 yuan for each day of overstay, which may be imposed separately or concurrently.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　被处罚人对公安机关处罚不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起15日内，向上一级公安机关申请复议，由上一级公安机关作出最后的裁决；也可以直接向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 36** If a person subject to a penalty refuses to accept the penalty imposed by the public security organ, he or she may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply for reconsideration to the public security organ at the next higher level, which shall make a final ruling; he or she may also directly initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　来大陆的台湾居民违反本办法的规定或者有其他违法犯罪行为的，除依照本办法和其他有关法律、法规的规定处罚外，公安机关可以缩短其停留期限，限期离境，或者遣送出境。  
有[本办法第十九条](#art-19)规定不予批准情形之一的，应当立即遣送出境。

**Article 37** Where a Taiwan resident who comes to the mainland violates the provisions of these Measures or commits other illegal or criminal acts, in addition to being punished in accordance with these Measures and other relevant laws and regulations, the public security organ may shorten his or her period of stay, order him or her to leave within a time limit, or escort him or her out of the country.  
If the circumstances fall under any of the cases where approval is not granted as provided in Article 19 of these Measures, the person shall be immediately escorted out of the country.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　执行本办法的国家工作人员，利用职权索取、收受贿赂或者有其他违法失职行为，情节轻微的，由主管部门予以行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where any State functionary executing these Measures abuses his or her power to extort or accept bribes, or commits other illegal or derelict acts, if the circumstances are minor, the competent authority shall impose administrative sanctions; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对违反本办法所得的财物，应当予以追缴或者责令退赔；用于犯罪的本人财物应当没收。  
罚款及没收的财物上缴国库。

**Article 39** Any property obtained in violation of these Measures shall be recovered or ordered to be returned or compensated; the offender's own property used for criminal purposes shall be confiscated.  
Fines and confiscated property shall be turned over to the State Treasury.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本办法由公安部负责解释。

**Article 40** These Measures shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本办法自1992年5月1日起施行。

**Article 41** These Measures shall come into force on May 1, 1992.
