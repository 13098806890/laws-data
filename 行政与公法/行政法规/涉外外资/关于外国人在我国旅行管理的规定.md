### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>三、外国人旅行地区分为以下几类：  
1．甲类地区：外国人前往这类地区，不办旅行证，不需事先通知。现确定为二十九个市、县：  
北京市　天津市　上海市　秦皇岛市  
太原市　沈阳市　长春市　哈尔滨市  
南京市　苏州市　无锡市　杭州市  
济南市　青岛市　郑州市　开封市  
洛阳市　武汉市　长沙市　广州市  
佛山市　肇庆市　南宁市　桂林市  
西安市　成都市　重庆市　昆明市  
路南县(石林)  
2．乙类地区(见附表)：是指除甲类地区外，已经对外开放、控制开放和新增加开放的地区。外国人前往这类地区，仍需办旅行证，一般均可批准。  
3．丙类地区：是指一般性非开放地区，有外国人经常去考察、进行科技交流、现场施工等公务活动的地区。这些地区，可准许有关的外国人前往，但要办旅行证。这类地区的名单，由各大军区审核同意，公安部汇总通报有关部门和各地公安机关掌握。  
4．丁类地区：是指除甲、乙、丙三类地区以外的其它非开放地区。外国人因特殊情况需要去这类地区的，接待单位要事先征得前往地的省、自治区人民政府和大军区的同意，然后向公安机关申领旅行证，方可前往。

**Article 1** 3. The travel areas for foreigners are divided into the following categories:  
1. Category A areas: Foreigners traveling to such areas shall not require a travel permit and need not give prior notice. The following 29 cities and counties are currently designated:  
Beijing Municipality, Tianjin Municipality, Shanghai Municipality, Qinhuangdao City  
Taiyuan City, Shenyang City, Changchun City, Harbin City  
Nanjing City, Suzhou City, Wuxi City, Hangzhou City  
Jinan City, Qingdao City, Zhengzhou City, Kaifeng City  
Luoyang City, Wuhan City, Changsha City, Guangzhou City  
Foshan City, Zhaoqing City, Nanning City, Guilin City  
Xi'an City, Chengdu City, Chongqing City, Kunming City  
Lunan County (Stone Forest)  
2. Category B areas (see appendix): refer to areas that have been opened to the public, controlled areas, and newly opened areas other than Category A areas. Foreigners traveling to such areas shall still apply for a travel permit, and generally such applications may be approved.  
3. Category C areas: refer to generally non-open areas where foreigners frequently go for official activities such as inspections, scientific and technological exchanges, and on-site construction. These areas may permit relevant foreigners to travel there, but a travel permit shall be required. The list of such areas shall be reviewed and approved by the major military commands, and the Ministry of Public Security shall compile and notify the relevant departments and public security organs for their information.  
4. Category D areas: refer to other non-open areas except those of Categories A, B, and C. If a foreigner needs to go to such areas due to special circumstances, the receiving unit shall obtain the consent of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or the major military command in advance, and then apply to the public security organ for a travel permit before proceeding.

<a id="art-2"></a>四、甲、乙类地区内如有重要军事设施，则应划为非开放范围。

**Article 2** 4. If there are important military facilities in Category A or B areas, such areas shall be designated as non-open areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>五、各国驻华外交代表机关和国际组织驻华机构人员的旅行，一般按本规定办理。必要时，视有关国家对我驻外人员的旅行是否限制而采取对等措施。

**Article 3** 5. The travel of personnel of diplomatic missions of foreign states and offices of international organizations in China shall generally be handled in accordance with these Provisions. When necessary, reciprocal measures shall be taken depending on whether the relevant country restricts the travel of our personnel stationed abroad.

<a id="art-4"></a>六、外国人旅行，除北京到天津之间允许乘自备汽车外，其他地区不准乘自备交通工具。

**Article 4** 6. Foreigners traveling shall not be allowed to use their own means of transport in areas other than the route between Beijing and Tianjin, where self-driven cars are permitted.

<a id="art-5"></a>七、外国人在非开放地区的活动，接待单位或有关人员应按事先规定的路线和范围安排，不得随意改变。

**Article 5** 7. For the activities of foreigners in non-open areas, the receiving unit or relevant personnel shall arrange the routes and scope as previously specified and shall not change them arbitrarily.

<a id="art-6"></a>八、凡在非开放地区选定与外国合资、合办、考察项目，涉及几个省、自治区、直辖市的，事先要征求公安部的意见；其中在一个大军区范围内的，应征得所在大军区的同意；超出一个大军区范围的，还应征求总参谋部的意见，尔后按规定报批。

**Article 6** 8. Where projects for joint venture, cooperation, or inspection with foreign parties are selected in non-open areas and involve several provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the opinion of the Ministry of Public Security shall be sought in advance; if such projects are within the scope of one major military command, the consent of the relevant major military command shall be obtained; if they exceed the scope of one major military command, the opinion of the General Staff Department shall also be sought, and thereafter the matter shall be reported for approval according to the provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>九、外籍华人去非开放地区探亲、旅行，按一九八○年国务院、中央军委批准的公安部《关于取消对海外华侨、港澳同胞旅行限制的请示》的规定办理。

**Article 7** 9. Ethnic Chinese with foreign nationality visiting relatives or traveling in non-open areas shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the "Request for Cancellation of Travel Restrictions on Overseas Chinese and Compatriots from Hong Kong and Macao" submitted by the Ministry of Public Security and approved by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in 1980.

<a id="art-8"></a>十、凡涉及国家安全的地区、单位，都要做好保密工作。凡涉及国家安全的地段和部位以及国家列为重点保护的珍贵文物，不准摄影、录像的，应事先向陪同人员和外国人宣布。

**Article 8** 10. All areas and units involving national security shall maintain confidentiality. For sections and locations involving national security and precious cultural relics listed as key protected items by the State, where photography and video recording are prohibited, the accompanying personnel and foreigners shall be informed in advance.

<a id="art-9"></a>十一、本规定自批准之日起施行。过去有关规定与本规定有抵触的，以本规定为准。
