# 中华人民共和国进口计量器具监督管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06

> (1989年10月11日国务院批准　1989年11月4日国家技术监督局发布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强进口计量器具的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国计量法》和《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》的有关规定，制定本办法。 第二条　任何单位和个人进口计量器具，以及外商(含外国制造商、经销商，下同)或其代理人在中国销售计量器具，都必须遵守本办法。 第三条　进口计量器具的监督管理，由国务院计量行政部门主管，具体实施由国务院和地方有关部门分工负责。 第二章　进口计量器具的型式批准 第四条　凡进口或外商在中国境内销售列入本办法所附《中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式审查目录》内的计量器具的，应向国务院计量行政部门申请办理型式批准。 属进口的，由外商申请型式批准。 属外商在中国境内销售的，由外商或其代理人申请型式批准。 国务院计量行政部门可根据情况变化对《中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式审查目录》作个别调整。 第五条　外商或其代理人申请型式批准，须向国务院计量行政部门递交型式批准申请书、计量器具样机照片和必要的技术资料。 国务院计量行政部门应根据外商或其代理人递交的资料进行计量法制审查。 第六条　国务院计量行政部门接受申请后，负责安排授权的技术机构进行定型鉴定，并通知外商或其代理人向承担定型鉴定的技术机构提供样机和以下技术资料： (一)计量器具的技术说明书； (二)计量器具的总装图、结构图和电路图；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强进口计量器具的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国计量法》和《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》的有关规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the supervision and administration of imported measuring instruments and in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China and the Detailed Implementation Rules of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　任何单位和个人进口计量器具，以及外商(含外国制造商、经销商，下同)或其代理人在中国销售计量器具，都必须遵守本办法。

**Article 2** Any entity or individual that imports measuring instruments, and any foreign businessman (including foreign manufacturers and dealers, hereinafter the same) or its agent that sells measuring instruments in China must abide by these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　进口计量器具的监督管理，由国务院计量行政部门主管，具体实施由国务院和地方有关部门分工负责。

**Article 3** The supervision and administration of the import of measuring instruments shall be in charge of the metrological administrative department of the State Council, and the specific implementation shall be carried out with division of responsibilities by the relevant departments of the State Council and local authorities.

### 第二章　进口计量器具的型式批准

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　凡进口或外商在中国境内销售列入本办法所附《中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式审查目录》内的计量器具的，应向国务院计量行政部门申请办理型式批准。  
属进口的，由外商申请型式批准。  
属外商在中国境内销售的，由外商或其代理人申请型式批准。  
国务院计量行政部门可根据情况变化对《中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式审查目录》作个别调整。

**Article 4** Where any measuring instrument listed in the Catalogue of Type Approval for Imported Measuring Instruments of the People's Republic of China attached to these Measures is imported or sold by a foreign businessman within the territory of China, an application for type approval shall be filed with the metrological administrative department of the State Council.  
Where the instrument is imported, the foreign businessman shall apply for type approval.  
Where the instrument is sold by a foreign businessman within the territory of China, the foreign businessman or its agent shall apply for type approval.  
The metrological administrative department of the State Council may make individual adjustments to the Catalogue of Type Approval for Imported Measuring Instruments of the People's Republic of China according to changes in circumstances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外商或其代理人申请型式批准，须向国务院计量行政部门递交型式批准申请书、计量器具样机照片和必要的技术资料。  
国务院计量行政部门应根据外商或其代理人递交的资料进行计量法制审查。

**Article 5** A foreign businessman or its agent applying for type approval must submit to the metrological administrative department of the State Council an application for type approval, photographs of the prototype of the measuring instrument and necessary technical documents.  
The metrological administrative department of the State Council shall, based on the documents submitted by the foreign businessman or its agent, conduct a metrological legal review.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院计量行政部门接受申请后，负责安排授权的技术机构进行定型鉴定，并通知外商或其代理人向承担定型鉴定的技术机构提供样机和以下技术资料：  
(一)计量器具的技术说明书；  
(二)计量器具的总装图、结构图和电路图；  
(三)技术标准文件和检验方法；  
(四)样机测试报告；  
(五)使用说明书。  
定型鉴定所需的样机由外商或其代理人无偿提供。海关凭国务院计量行政部门的保函验放并免收关税。样机经鉴定后退还申请人。

**Article 6** After accepting the application, the metrological administrative department of the State Council shall arrange for an authorized technical institution to carry out the type evaluation, and shall notify the foreign businessman or its agent to provide the prototype and the following technical documents to the technical institution undertaking the type evaluation:  
(1) technical instruction manual of the measuring instrument;  
(2) general assembly drawing, structural drawing and circuit diagram of the measuring instrument;  
(3) technical standard documents and inspection methods;  
(4) prototype test report;  
(5) instruction manual.  
The prototype required for the type evaluation shall be provided gratuitously by the foreign businessman or its agent. The Customs shall release it upon the letter of guarantee issued by the metrological administrative department of the State Council and shall exempt it from customs duties. The prototype shall be returned to the applicant after evaluation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　定型鉴定按鉴定大纲进行。鉴定大纲由承担鉴定的技术机构，根据国务院计量行政部门发布的《计量器具定型鉴定技术规范》的要求制定。主要内容包括：外观检查、计量性能考核以及安全性、环境适应性、可靠性和寿命试验等。

**Article 7** The type evaluation shall be conducted in accordance with the evaluation outline. The evaluation outline shall be formulated by the technical institution undertaking the evaluation in accordance with the requirements of the Technical Specifications for Type Evaluation of Measuring Instruments issued by the metrological administrative department of the State Council. Its main contents include: appearance inspection, assessment of metrological performance, and tests of safety, environmental adaptability, reliability and service life, etc.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　定型鉴定的结果由承担鉴定的技术机构报国务院计量行政部门审核。经审核合格的，由国务院计量行政部门向申请人颁发《中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式批准证书》，并准予在相应的计量器具和包装上使用中华人民共和国进口计量器具型式批准的标志和编号。

**Article 8** The results of the type evaluation shall be submitted by the technical institution undertaking the evaluation to the metrological administrative department of the State Council for examination. Upon examination and approval, the metrological administrative department of the State Council shall issue a Certificate of Type Approval for Imported Measuring Instruments of the People's Republic of China to the applicant, and shall permit the use of the mark and number of the type approval for imported measuring instruments of the People's Republic of China on the corresponding measuring instruments and their packaging.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承担定型鉴定的技术机构及其工作人员，对申请人提供的技术资料必须保密。

**Article 9** The technical institution undertaking the type evaluation and its staff must keep confidential the technical documents provided by the applicant.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情况之一的，经国务院计量行政部门同意，可申请办理临时型式批准，具体办法由国务院计量行政部门规定：  
(一)展览会留购的；  
(二)确属急需的；  
(三)销售量极少的；  
(四)国内暂无定型鉴定能力的。

**Article 10** In any of the following circumstances, an application for temporary type approval may be filed with the consent of the metrological administrative department of the State Council, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the metrological administrative department of the State Council:  
(1) retained for purchase at an exhibition;  
(2) actually in urgent need;  
(3) extremely small sales volume;  
(4) no domestic capability for type evaluation at present.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国制造的计量器具经我国型式批准后，由国务院计量行政部门予以公布。

**Article 11** After a measuring instrument manufactured abroad is granted type approval in China, the metrological administrative department of the State Council shall make it public.

### 第三章　进口计量器具的审批

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请进口计量器具，按国家关于进口商品的规定程序进行审批。  
负责审批的有关主管部门和归口审查部门，应对申请进口《中华人民共和国依法管理的计量器具目录》内的计量器具进行法定计量单位的审查，对申请进口[本办法第四条](#art-4)规定的计量器具审查是否经过型式批准。经审查不合规定的，审批部门不得批准进口，外贸经营单位不得办理订货手续。  
海关对进口计量器具凭审批部门的批件验放。

**Article 12** The import of measuring instruments shall be subject to examination and approval in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State for the import of commodities.  
The competent department and the centralized review department responsible for examination and approval shall conduct a review of legal units of measurement for measuring instruments listed in the Catalogue of Measuring Instruments Subject to Compulsory Administration According to Law of the People's Republic of China, and for measuring instruments specified in Article 4 of these Measures, shall review whether they have undergone type approval. Where the review finds non-compliance with the provisions, the examination and approval department shall not approve the import, and the foreign trade operator shall not handle the order procedures.  
The Customs shall release the imported measuring instruments upon the approval document issued by the examination and approval department.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　因特殊需要，申请进口非法定计量单位的计量器具和国务院禁止使用的其他计量器具，须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府计量行政部门批准。

**Article 13** Where it is necessary to apply for the import of measuring instruments in non-legal units of measurement or other measuring instruments prohibited by the State Council due to special needs, the approval of the metrological administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government must be obtained.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请进口非法定计量单位的计量器具和国务院禁止使用的其他计量器具的单位，应向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府计量行政部门提供以下材料和文件：  
(一)申请报告；  
(二)计量器具的性能及技术指标；  
(三)计量器具的照片和使用说明；  
(四)本单位上级主管部门的批件。

**Article 14** An entity applying for the import of measuring instruments in non-legal units of measurement or other measuring instruments prohibited by the State Council shall provide the following materials and documents to the metrological administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) application report;  
(2) performance and technical specifications of the measuring instruments;  
(3) photographs and instructions for use of the measuring instruments;  
(4) approval document of the entity's superior competent department.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反本办法规定，进口非法定计量单位的计量器具或国务院禁止使用的其他计量器具的，按照[《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》第四十四条](../../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国计量法实施细则.md#art-44)规定追究法律责任。

**Article 15** Whoever, in violation of these Measures, imports measuring instruments in non-legal units of measurement or other measuring instruments prohibited by the State Council shall be subject to legal liability in accordance with Article 44 of the Detailed Implementation Rules of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)规定，进口或销售未经国务院计量行政部门型式批准的计量器具的，计量行政部门有权封存其计量器具，责令其补办型式批准手续，并可处以相当于进口或销售额百分之三十以下的罚款。

**Article 16** Whoever, in violation of Article 4 of these Measures, imports or sells measuring instruments without type approval from the metrological administrative department of the State Council, the metrological administrative department shall have the power to seal up such measuring instruments, order the party to go through the formalities for type approval retroactively, and may impose a fine of not more than thirty percent of the import or sales value.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　承担进口计量器具定型鉴定的技术机构违反[本办法第九条](#art-9)规定的，按照[《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》第五十八条](../../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国计量法实施细则.md#art-58)规定追究法律责任。

**Article 17** Where a technical institution undertaking the type evaluation of imported measuring instruments violates Article 9 of these Measures, it shall be subject to legal liability in accordance with Article 58 of the Detailed Implementation Rules of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　引进成套设备中配套的计量器具以及不以销售为目的的计量器具的监督管理，按国家有关规定办理。

**Article 18** The supervision and administration of measuring instruments that are ancillary to imported complete sets of equipment and of measuring instruments not intended for sale shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　与本办法有关的申请书、证书和标志式样，由国务院计量行政部门统一制定。

**Article 19** The forms of application forms, certificates and marks related to these Measures shall be uniformly formulated by the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请进口计量器具的型式批准和定型鉴定，应按国家有关规定缴纳费用。

**Article 20** Fees shall be paid in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State for the application for type approval and type evaluation of imported measuring instruments.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　进口用于统一量值的标准物质的监督管理，可参照本办法执行。

**Article 21** The supervision and administration of the import of standard materials used for unifying measurement values may be implemented by reference to these Measures.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本办法由国务院计量行政部门负责解释。

**Article 22** The power to interpret these Measures shall vest in the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本办法自发布之日起施行。
