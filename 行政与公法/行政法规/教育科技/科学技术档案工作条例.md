# 科学技术档案工作条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1980-12-27  
**生效日期**：1980-12-27

> (1980年12月9日国务院批准　1980年12月27日国家经济委员会、国家基本建设委员会、国家科学技术委员会、国家档案局发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了建立、健全科学技术档案工作，完整地保存和科学地管理科学技术档案(以下简称科技档案)，充分发挥科技档案在社会主义现代化建设中的作用，特制定本条例。 第二条　科技档案是指在自然科学研究、生产技术、基本建设(以下简称科研、生产、基建)等活动中形成的应当归档保存的图纸、图表、文字材料、计算材料、照片、影片、录像、录音带等科技文件材料。 第三条　科技档案工作是生产管理、技术管理、科研管理的重要组成部分，各工业、交通、基建、科研、农林、军事、地质、测绘、水文、气象、教育、卫生等单位(以下简称各单位)，都应当把科技档案工作纳入生产管理工作、技术管理工作、科研管理工作之中，加强领导。 第四条　各单位应当按照集中统一管理科技档案的基本原则，建立、健全科技档案工作，达到科技档案完整、准确、系统、安全和有效利用的要求。 第二章　科技文件材料的形成和归档 第五条　各单位应当建立、健全科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理、归档制度，做到每一项科研、生产、基建等活动，都有完整、准确、系统的科技文件材料归档保存。 第六条　各单位应当把科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理和归档纳入科技工作程序和科研、生产、基建等计划中，列入有关部门和有关人员的职责范围。 第七条　各单位在对每一项科研成果、产品试制、基建工程或其他技术项目进行鉴定、验收的时候，要有科技档案部门参加，对应当归档的科技文件材料加以验收。没有完整、准确、系统的科技文件材料的项目，不能验收。 第八条　一个科研课题、一个试制产品、一项工程或其他技术项目，在完成或告一段落以后，必须将所形成的科技文件材料加以系统整理，组成保管单位，填写保管期限，注明密级，由课题负责人、产品试制负责人、工程负责人等审查后，及时归档。 第九条　凡是需要归档的科技文件材料，都应当做到书写材料优良、字迹工整、图样清晰，有利于长久保存。 第十条　科技档案部门有责任检查和协助科技人员做好科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理和归档的工作。 第三章　科技档案的管理 第十一条　科技档案部门对接收来的科技档案，应当进行分类、编目、登记、统计和必要的加工整理。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了建立、健全科学技术档案工作，完整地保存和科学地管理科学技术档案(以下简称科技档案)，充分发挥科技档案在社会主义现代化建设中的作用，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of establishing and improving the work on scientific and technological archives, preserving them completely and managing them scientifically, and giving full play to the role of scientific and technological archives in the socialist modernization drive.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　科技档案是指在自然科学研究、生产技术、基本建设(以下简称科研、生产、基建)等活动中形成的应当归档保存的图纸、图表、文字材料、计算材料、照片、影片、录像、录音带等科技文件材料。

**Article 2** Scientific and technological archives refer to scientific and technological documentary materials such as drawings, charts, written materials, calculation materials, photographs, films, video recordings, and audio tapes that are formed in activities such as natural science research, production technology, and capital construction (hereinafter referred to as scientific research, production, and capital construction) and shall be filed and preserved.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　科技档案工作是生产管理、技术管理、科研管理的重要组成部分，各工业、交通、基建、科研、农林、军事、地质、测绘、水文、气象、教育、卫生等单位(以下简称各单位)，都应当把科技档案工作纳入生产管理工作、技术管理工作、科研管理工作之中，加强领导。

**Article 3** The work on scientific and technological archives is an important component of production management, technical management, and scientific research management. All units in industries, communications, capital construction, scientific research, agriculture and forestry, military affairs, geology, surveying and mapping, hydrology, meteorology, education, and public health (hereinafter referred to as each unit) shall incorporate the work on scientific and technological archives into their production management, technical management, and scientific research management, and strengthen leadership over it.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各单位应当按照集中统一管理科技档案的基本原则，建立、健全科技档案工作，达到科技档案完整、准确、系统、安全和有效利用的要求。

**Article 4** Each unit shall, in accordance with the basic principle of centralized and unified management of scientific and technological archives, establish and improve the work on scientific and technological archives so as to meet the requirements of completeness, accuracy, systematicness, safety, and effective utilization of scientific and technological archives.

### 第二章　科技文件材料的形成和归档

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各单位应当建立、健全科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理、归档制度，做到每一项科研、生产、基建等活动，都有完整、准确、系统的科技文件材料归档保存。

**Article 5** Each unit shall establish and improve a system for the formation, accumulation, arrangement, and archiving of scientific and technological documentary materials, ensuring that for every activity of scientific research, production, and capital construction, complete, accurate, and systematic scientific and technological documentary materials are archived and preserved.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各单位应当把科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理和归档纳入科技工作程序和科研、生产、基建等计划中，列入有关部门和有关人员的职责范围。

**Article 6** Each unit shall incorporate the formation, accumulation, arrangement, and archiving of scientific and technological documentary materials into the scientific and technological work procedures and the plans for scientific research, production, and capital construction, and shall include them in the scope of duties of relevant departments and personnel.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各单位在对每一项科研成果、产品试制、基建工程或其他技术项目进行鉴定、验收的时候，要有科技档案部门参加，对应当归档的科技文件材料加以验收。没有完整、准确、系统的科技文件材料的项目，不能验收。

**Article 7** When each unit conducts expert evaluation or acceptance checks on each scientific research achievement, trial production of a product, capital construction project, or other technical project, the department of scientific and technological archives shall participate and accept the scientific and technological documentary materials that shall be archived. No project that lacks complete, accurate, and systematic scientific and technological documentary materials may be accepted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　一个科研课题、一个试制产品、一项工程或其他技术项目，在完成或告一段落以后，必须将所形成的科技文件材料加以系统整理，组成保管单位，填写保管期限，注明密级，由课题负责人、产品试制负责人、工程负责人等审查后，及时归档。

**Article 8** After a scientific research project, a trial-manufactured product, an engineering project, or any other technical project is completed or has reached a certain stage, the scientific and technological documentary materials thus formed must be systematically arranged, formed into preservation units, filled in with the retention period, and marked with the security classification level. After being examined by the person in charge of the project, the person in charge of the trial production, the person in charge of the engineering project, etc., they shall be archived in a timely manner.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　凡是需要归档的科技文件材料，都应当做到书写材料优良、字迹工整、图样清晰，有利于长久保存。

**Article 9** All scientific and technological documentary materials that need to be archived shall have good writing materials, neat handwriting, and clear drawings, so as to facilitate long-term preservation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　科技档案部门有责任检查和协助科技人员做好科技文件材料的形成、积累、整理和归档的工作。

**Article 10** The department of scientific and technological archives has the responsibility to inspect and assist scientific and technical personnel in properly carrying out the formation, accumulation, arrangement, and archiving of scientific and technological documentary materials.

### 第三章　科技档案的管理

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　科技档案部门对接收来的科技档案，应当进行分类、编目、登记、统计和必要的加工整理。  
国务院所属各工业、交通、科研、基建等专业主管机关(以下简称专业主管机关)，应当拟定本专业系统的科技档案分类大纲。

**Article 11** The department of scientific and technological archives shall classify, catalog, register, compile statistics, and carry out necessary processing and arrangement of the scientific and technological archives it receives.  
The competent authorities for industries, communications, scientific research, capital construction, etc., under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as professional competent authorities) shall formulate the classification outlines for scientific and technological archives within their respective professional systems.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各单位应当建立和健全图纸更改、补充的制度。更改、补充图纸，必须履行审批手续。

**Article 12** Each unit shall establish and improve a system for modifying and supplementing drawings. Any modification or supplementation of drawings must undergo examination and approval procedures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　科技档案部门应当及时地提供科技档案为科研、生产、基建等各项工作服务，并编制必要的检索工具和参考资料。  
借阅和复制科技档案要有一定的批准手续。

**Article 13** The department of scientific and technological archives shall promptly provide scientific and technological archives to serve the various tasks of scientific research, production, capital construction, etc., and shall compile necessary retrieval tools and reference materials.  
The borrowing and reproduction of scientific and technological archives shall be subject to certain approval procedures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各单位应当定期对科技档案的密级进行审查，根据上级的规定，及时调整密级，扩大利用与交流的范围。

**Article 14** Each unit shall periodically review the security classification levels of scientific and technological archives, adjust such levels in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of higher authorities, and expand the scope of utilization and exchange.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　科技档案部门对科技档案的利用效果，应当进行必要的调查和建立借阅档案的统计制度。

**Article 15** The department of scientific and technological archives shall conduct necessary investigations into the utilization effects of scientific and technological archives and establish a statistical system for the borrowing of archives.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院所属各专业主管机关，应当编制本专业的科技档案保管期限表。科技档案的保管期限，分为永久、长期、定期三种。

**Article 16** The professional competent authorities under the State Council shall formulate the retention period schedules for scientific and technological archives in their respective professions. The retention periods for scientific and technological archives are divided into three categories: permanent, long-term, and fixed-term.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各单位应当定期做好科技档案保存价值的鉴定工作。鉴定的方法是直接鉴定档案的内容。鉴定工作要在总工程师或科研负责人的领导下，由科技领导干部、熟悉有关专业的科技人员和科技档案人员共同进行。

**Article 17** Each unit shall periodically carry out the appraisal of the preservation value of scientific and technological archives. The method of appraisal is to directly appraise the content of the archives. The appraisal work shall be conducted under the leadership of the chief engineer or the person in charge of scientific research, jointly by leading cadres in science and technology, scientific and technical personnel familiar with the relevant specialties, and scientific and technological archives personnel.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　要销毁的科技档案，必须造具清册，经单位领导审定，报送上级主管机关备案。销毁科技档案，要指定监销人，防止失密。

**Article 18** Scientific and technological archives to be destroyed must be listed in an inventory, examined and approved by the unit's leaders, and submitted to the superior competent authority for record. When destroying scientific and technological archives, a supervisor for the destruction shall be designated to prevent leakage of secrets.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　保管科技档案必须有专用库房，库房内应当保持适当的温度和湿度，并有防盗、防火、防晒、防虫、防尘等安全措施。科技档案部门应当定期检查科技档案的保管状况。对破损或变质的档案，要及时修补和复制。

**Article 19** A dedicated warehouse shall be used for the preservation of scientific and technological archives. The warehouse shall maintain appropriate temperature and humidity and shall be equipped with safety measures such as theft prevention, fire prevention, sun protection, insect prevention, and dust prevention. The department of scientific and technological archives shall periodically inspect the preservation status of scientific and technological archives. Archives that are damaged or deteriorated shall be promptly repaired and reproduced.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　科技档案部门对重要的科技档案应当复制副本，分别保存，以保证在非常情况下科技档案的安全和提供利用。

**Article 20** The department of scientific and technological archives shall reproduce copies of important scientific and technological archives and preserve them separately to ensure the safety and availability of scientific and technological archives under extraordinary circumstances.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　引进技术和设备的档案，由引进单位的科技档案部门统一管理。

**Article 21** The archives of imported technology and equipment shall be uniformly managed by the scientific and technological archives department of the importing unit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　凡是几个单位分工协作完成的科技项目或工程，由主办单位保存一整套档案，协作单位除保存与自己承担任务有关的档案正本以外，应将复制本送交主办单位保存。

**Article 22** For any scientific and technological project or engineering project completed through division of labor and cooperation among several units, the leading unit shall preserve a complete set of archives. The cooperating units, in addition to preserving the original archives related to their own tasks, shall deliver copies thereof to the leading unit for preservation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　凡单位撤销或变动，以及建筑物、构筑物、设备、仪器等转移使用关系时，其档案要妥善整理，并经领导人批准后向接受单位办理交接手续。

**Article 23** When a unit is abolished or undergoes changes, or when buildings, structures, equipment, instruments, etc., are transferred in terms of usage relationships, the archives thereof shall be properly arranged, and after approval by the leader, the procedures for handover to the receiving unit shall be completed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　科技档案部门增添设备和用品的费用，分别从企业、事业单位的生产费、科研费或事业费中开支。

**Article 24** The expenses for additional equipment and supplies of the scientific and technological archives department shall be disbursed from the production expenses, scientific research expenses, or operating expenses of the enterprise or institution, respectively.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　新建的企业、事业单位，应当同时建设符合要求的科技档案库房。

**Article 25** Newly established enterprises and institutions shall simultaneously construct warehouses for scientific and technological archives that meet the requirements.

### 第四章　科技档案工作管理体制

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家档案局和各级档案管理机关应当加强对科技档案工作的指导、监督和检查。

**Article 26** The National Archives Administration and archives management authorities at all levels shall strengthen guidance, supervision, and inspection of the work on scientific and technological archives.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　科技档案工作必须按专业实行统一管理。国务院所属的各专业主管机关和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所属的各专业主管机关，应当建立相应的档案机构，加强对所属企业、事业单位科技档案工作的领导。

**Article 27** The work on scientific and technological archives must be managed uniformly by profession. The professional competent authorities under the State Council and the professional competent authorities under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish corresponding archives institutions and strengthen leadership over the work on scientific and technological archives of the enterprises and institutions subordinate to them.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院所属的各专业主管机关，根据需要建立专业档案馆，收集和保管本专业需要长期和永久保存的科技档案。  
大中城市应当建立城市基本建设档案馆，收集和保管本城市应当长期和永久保存的基本建设档案。  
专业档案馆和城市基本建设档案馆是科学技术事业单位。

**Article 28** The professional competent authorities under the State Council shall, as needed, establish professional archives to collect and preserve scientific and technological archives that need to be retained for long-term or permanent preservation in their respective professions.  
Large and medium-sized cities shall establish urban capital construction archives to collect and preserve capital construction archives that need to be retained for long-term or permanent preservation in the city.  
Professional archives and urban capital construction archives are scientific and technological institutions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　大中型企业、事业单位要设立直属的科技档案机构；小型企业、事业单位可以设立单独的科技档案室，也可以设立文书档案和科技档案统一管理的档案室，或者配备专(兼)职人员管理。  
各单位的科技档案工作，由领导生产、科研的负责人或者总工程师分工领导。

**Article 29** Large and medium-sized enterprises and institutions shall establish directly subordinate scientific and technological archives institutions; small enterprises and institutions may establish separate scientific and technological archives rooms, or set up archives rooms that manage both administrative archives and scientific and technological archives in a unified manner, or assign full-time (or part-time) personnel to manage them.  
The work on scientific and technological archives of each unit shall be led by the person in charge of production and scientific research or the chief engineer under the division of responsibilities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　专业档案馆或各单位的科技档案机构，根据需要可以兼管科技资料工作。

**Article 30** Professional archives or the scientific and technological archives institutions of each unit may, as needed, concurrently manage the work on scientific and technological materials.

### 第五章　科技档案干部

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院所属的各专业主管机关和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所属的各专业主管机关都应当积极建设一支坚持社会主义道路，具有科技档案专业知识和懂得有关的科学技术，有一定工作能力的科技档案干部队伍。

**Article 31** The professional competent authorities under the State Council and the professional competent authorities under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall actively build a contingent of scientific and technological archives cadres who adhere to the socialist path, possess professional knowledge of scientific and technological archives, understand relevant science and technology, and have certain work capabilities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　科技档案干部要努力学习马列主义、毛泽东思想，认真执行国家的方针政策，刻苦钻研业务，不断总结经验，提高管理工作水平，积极为社会主义现代化建设服务。

**Article 32** Scientific and technological archives cadres shall diligently study Marxism-Leninism and Mao Zedong Thought, earnestly implement the state's principles and policies, assiduously study their professional work, constantly sum up experience, raise the level of management, and actively serve the socialist modernization drive.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　各单位要给科技档案部门配备足够数量和能胜任工作的干部，还应当配备一定数量的科技干部，以保证工作的需要。

**Article 33** Each unit shall assign a sufficient number of competent cadres to the scientific and technological archives department, and shall also assign a certain number of scientific and technical cadres to ensure the needs of the work.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　各单位要经常对科技档案干部进行保守国家机密的教育，检查遵守保密制度的情况。

**Article 34** Each unit shall regularly provide education to scientific and technological archives cadres on safeguarding state secrets and inspect their compliance with the confidentiality system.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国务院所属的各专业主管机关和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所属的各专业主管机关，可以根据本条例的精神，结合本系统、本地区科技档案工作情况，制定实施细则。

**Article 35** The professional competent authorities under the State Council and the professional competent authorities under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the spirit of these Regulations and in light of the actual conditions of scientific and technological archives work in their own systems and regions, formulate implementing rules.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自发布之日起施行。过去有关规定与本条例有抵触的，以本条例为准。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. In the event of any conflict between previous relevant provisions and these Regulations, these Regulations shall prevail.
