# 中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具检定管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1987-04-14  
**生效日期**：1987-07-01

> (1987年4月15日国务院发布) 第一条　为适应社会主义现代化建设需要，维护国家和消费者的利益，保护人民健康和生命、财产的安全，加强对强制检定的工作计量器具的管理，根据《中华人民共和国计量法》第九条的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　强制检定是指由县级以上人民政府计量行政部门所属或者授权的计量检定机构，对用于贸易结算、安全防护、医疗卫生、环境监测方面，并列入本办法所附《中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具目录》的计量器具实行定点定期检定。 进行强制检定工作及使用强制检定的工作计量器具，适用本办法。 第三条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门对本行政区域内的强制检定工作统一实施监督管理，并按照经济合理、就地就近的原则，指定所属或者授权的计量检定机构执行强制检定任务。 第四条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门所属计量检定机构，为实施国家强制检定所需要的计量标准和检定设施由当地人民政府负责配备。 第五条　使用强制检定的工作计量器具的单位或者个人，必须按照规定将其使用的强制检定的工作计量器具登记造册，报当地县(市)级人民政府计量行政部门备案，并向其指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。当地不能检定的，向上一级人民政府计量行政部门指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。 第六条　强制检定的周期，由执行强制检定的计量检定机构根据计量检定规程确定。 第七条　属于强制检定的工作计量器具，未按照本办法规定申请检定或者经检定不合格的，任何单位或者个人不得使用。 第八条　国务院计量行政部门和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府计量行政部门应当对各种强制检定的工作计量器具作出检定期限的规定。执行强制检定工作的机构应当在规定期限内按时完成检定。 第九条　执行强制检定的机构对检定合格的计量器具，发给国家统一规定的检定证书、检定合格证或者在计量器具上加盖检定合格印；对检定不合格的，发给检定结果通知书或者注销原检定合格印、证。 第十条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门按照有利于管理、方便生产和使用的原则，结合本地区的实际情况，可以授权有关单位的计量检定机构在规定的范围内执行强制检定工作。 第十一条　被授权执行强制检定任务的机构，其相应的计量标准，应当接受计量基准或者社会公用计量标准的检定；执行强制检定的人员，必须经授权单位考核合格；授权单位应当对其检定工作进行监督。 第十二条　被授权执行强制检定任务的机构成为计量纠纷中当事人一方时，按照《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》的有关规定处理。 第十三条　企业、事业单位应当对强制检定的工作计量器具的使用加强管理，制定相应的规章制度，保证按照周期进行检定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为适应社会主义现代化建设需要，维护国家和消费者的利益，保护人民健康和生命、财产的安全，加强对强制检定的工作计量器具的管理，根据[《中华人民共和国计量法》第九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国计量法.md#art-9)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China, for the purpose of meeting the needs of socialist modernization, safeguarding the interests of the State and consumers, protecting the health and safety of people's lives and property, and strengthening the management of working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　强制检定是指由县级以上人民政府计量行政部门所属或者授权的计量检定机构，对用于贸易结算、安全防护、医疗卫生、环境监测方面，并列入本办法所附《中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具目录》的计量器具实行定点定期检定。  
进行强制检定工作及使用强制检定的工作计量器具，适用本办法。

**Article 2** "Compulsory verification" means the fixed-point and periodic verification carried out by the metrological verification institutions affiliated to or authorized by the metrological administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, on the measuring instruments that are used for trade settlement, safety protection, medical and health care, environmental monitoring, and are listed in the Catalogue of Working Measuring Instruments Subject to Compulsory Verification of the People's Republic of China attached to these Measures.  
These Measures shall apply to the carrying out of compulsory verification work and the use of working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门对本行政区域内的强制检定工作统一实施监督管理，并按照经济合理、就地就近的原则，指定所属或者授权的计量检定机构执行强制检定任务。

**Article 3** The metrological administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the compulsory verification work within their respective administrative regions, and shall, in accordance with the principles of economic rationality and local proximity, designate their affiliated or authorized metrological verification institutions to carry out the tasks of compulsory verification.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门所属计量检定机构，为实施国家强制检定所需要的计量标准和检定设施由当地人民政府负责配备。

**Article 4** The metrological verification institutions affiliated to the metrological administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the metrological standards and verification facilities necessary for implementing the national compulsory verification equipped by the local people's government.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　使用强制检定的工作计量器具的单位或者个人，必须按照规定将其使用的强制检定的工作计量器具登记造册，报当地县(市)级人民政府计量行政部门备案，并向其指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。当地不能检定的，向上一级人民政府计量行政部门指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。

**Article 5** Any unit or individual that uses working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification must, in accordance with the provisions, register such instruments in a book, report to the local metrological administrative department of the people's government at the county (city) level for record, and apply to the metrological verification institution designated by such department for periodic verification. Where verification cannot be carried out locally, an application shall be made to the metrological verification institution designated by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　强制检定的周期，由执行强制检定的计量检定机构根据计量检定规程确定。

**Article 6** The period of compulsory verification shall be determined by the metrological verification institution that carries out the compulsory verification in accordance with the metrological verification regulation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　属于强制检定的工作计量器具，未按照本办法规定申请检定或者经检定不合格的，任何单位或者个人不得使用。

**Article 7** Any working measuring instrument subject to compulsory verification that has not been applied for verification in accordance with the provisions of these Measures or has been found to be unqualified after verification shall not be used by any unit or individual.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院计量行政部门和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府计量行政部门应当对各种强制检定的工作计量器具作出检定期限的规定。执行强制检定工作的机构应当在规定期限内按时完成检定。

**Article 8** The metrological administrative department of the State Council and the metrological administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall prescribe the verification time limits for various working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification. The institutions that carry out compulsory verification work shall complete the verification on time within the prescribed time limits.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　执行强制检定的机构对检定合格的计量器具，发给国家统一规定的检定证书、检定合格证或者在计量器具上加盖检定合格印；对检定不合格的，发给检定结果通知书或者注销原检定合格印、证。

**Article 9** The institution that carries out compulsory verification shall issue a verification certificate or a verification qualification certificate as uniformly prescribed by the State, or affix a verification qualification seal on the measuring instrument that has passed the verification; for those that fail the verification, it shall issue a notice of verification results or cancel the original verification qualification seal and certificate.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门按照有利于管理、方便生产和使用的原则，结合本地区的实际情况，可以授权有关单位的计量检定机构在规定的范围内执行强制检定工作。

**Article 10** The metrological administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level may, in accordance with the principles of facilitating management and production use, and in light of the actual conditions of their respective regions, authorize the metrological verification institutions of relevant entities to carry out compulsory verification work within the prescribed scope.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被授权执行强制检定任务的机构，其相应的计量标准，应当接受计量基准或者社会公用计量标准的检定；执行强制检定的人员，必须经授权单位考核合格；授权单位应当对其检定工作进行监督。

**Article 11** The corresponding metrological standards of the institutions authorized to carry out compulsory verification tasks shall be subject to verification by the metrological primary standards or public metrological standards; the personnel performing compulsory verification must pass the examination of the authorizing entity; the authorizing entity shall supervise their verification work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被授权执行强制检定任务的机构成为计量纠纷中当事人一方时，按照《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》的有关规定处理。

**Article 12** Where an institution authorized to carry out compulsory verification tasks becomes a party to a metrological dispute, the matter shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Implementing Regulations of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业、事业单位应当对强制检定的工作计量器具的使用加强管理，制定相应的规章制度，保证按照周期进行检定。

**Article 13** Enterprises and institutions shall strengthen the management of the use of working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification, formulate corresponding rules and regulations, and ensure that verification is carried out according to the period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　使用强制检定的工作计量器具的任何单位或者个人，计量监督、管理人员和执行强制检定工作的计量检定人员，违反本办法规定的，按照《中华人民共和国计量法实施细则》的有关规定，追究法律责任。

**Article 14** Any unit or individual that uses working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification, and metrological supervisors, administrators, and metrological verifiers that carry out compulsory verification work, who violate the provisions of these Measures, shall be investigated for legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Implementing Regulations of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　执行强制检定工作的机构，违反[本办法第八条](#art-8)规定拖延检定期限的，应当按照送检单位的要求，及时安排检定，并免收检定费。

**Article 15** Where an institution carrying out compulsory verification work delays the verification time limit in violation of the provisions of Article 8 of these Measures, it shall, at the request of the entity submitting the instrument for verification, arrange verification in a timely manner and exempt the verification fees.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院计量行政部门可以根据本办法和《中华人民共和国强制检定的工作计量器具目录》，制定强制检定的工作计量器具的明细目录。

**Article 16** The metrological administrative department of the State Council may, in accordance with these Measures and the Catalogue of Working Measuring Instruments Subject to Compulsory Verification of the People's Republic of China, formulate a detailed catalogue of working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法由国务院计量行政部门负责解释。

**Article 17** The power to interpret these Measures shall be vested in the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法自1987年7月1日起施行。
