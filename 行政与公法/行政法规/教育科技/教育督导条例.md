# 教育督导条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2012-09-09  
**生效日期**：2012-10-01

> (2012年8月29日国务院第215次常务会议通过　2012年9月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第624号公布　自2012年10月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保证教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的贯彻执行，实施素质教育，提高教育质量，促进教育公平，推动教育事业科学发展，制定本条例。 第二条　对法律、法规规定范围的各级各类教育实施教育督导，适用本条例。 教育督导包括以下内容： (一)县级以上人民政府对下级人民政府落实教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的督导； (二)县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域内的学校和其他教育机构(以下统称学校)教育教学工作的督导。 第三条　实施教育督导应当坚持以下原则： (一)以提高教育教学质量为中心； (二)遵循教育规律； (三)遵守教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的规定； (四)对政府履行教育工作相关职责的督导与对学校教育教学工作的督导并重，监督与指导并重； (五)实事求是、客观公正。 第四条　国务院教育督导机构承担全国的教育督导实施工作，制定教育督导的基本准则，指导地方教育督导工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责教育督导的机构承担本行政区域的教育督导实施工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保证教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的贯彻执行，实施素质教育，提高教育质量，促进教育公平，推动教育事业科学发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure the implementation of education laws, regulations, rules, and national education policies and guidelines, to implement quality education, to improve education quality, to promote education equity, and to advance the scientific development of education.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对法律、法规规定范围的各级各类教育实施教育督导，适用本条例。  
教育督导包括以下内容：  
(一)县级以上人民政府对下级人民政府落实教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的督导；  
(二)县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域内的学校和其他教育机构(以下统称学校)教育教学工作的督导。

**Article 2** The implementation of education supervision on education at all levels and of all types within the scope provided by laws and regulations shall be governed by these Regulations.  
Education supervision includes the following:  
(1) supervision by the people's governments at or above the county level on the implementation of education laws, regulations, rules, and national education policies and guidelines by the people's governments at lower levels; and  
(2) supervision by the local people's governments at or above the county level on the education and teaching work of schools and other educational institutions (hereinafter collectively referred to as "schools") within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　实施教育督导应当坚持以下原则：  
(一)以提高教育教学质量为中心；  
(二)遵循教育规律；  
(三)遵守教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策的规定；  
(四)对政府履行教育工作相关职责的督导与对学校教育教学工作的督导并重，监督与指导并重；  
(五)实事求是、客观公正。

**Article 3** The implementation of education supervision shall adhere to the following principles:  
(1) focusing on improving the quality of education and teaching;  
(2) following the laws of education;  
(3) complying with the provisions of education laws, regulations, rules, and national education policies and guidelines;  
(4) attaching equal importance to supervision of the government's performance of duties related to education work and supervision of school education and teaching work, and attaching equal importance to supervision and guidance; and  
(5) being practical, realistic, objective and fair.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院教育督导机构承担全国的教育督导实施工作，制定教育督导的基本准则，指导地方教育督导工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责教育督导的机构承担本行政区域的教育督导实施工作。  
国务院教育督导机构和县级以上地方人民政府负责教育督导的机构(以下统称教育督导机构)在本级人民政府领导下独立行使督导职能。

**Article 4** The education supervision institution of the State Council shall undertake the implementation of education supervision nationwide, formulate the basic norms for education supervision, and guide local education supervision work.  
The institution responsible for education supervision under the local people's government at or above the county level shall undertake the implementation of education supervision within its administrative region.  
The education supervision institution of the State Council and the institutions responsible for education supervision under the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as "education supervision institutions") shall independently exercise their supervision functions under the leadership of the people's government at the corresponding level.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将教育督导经费列入财政预算。

**Article 5** The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the expenses for education supervision in the fiscal budget.

### 第二章　督学

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家实行督学制度。  
县级以上人民政府根据教育督导工作需要，为教育督导机构配备专职督学。教育督导机构可以根据教育督导工作需要聘任兼职督学。  
兼职督学的任期为3年，可以连续任职，连续任职不得超过3个任期。

**Article 6** The state shall practice the system of education inspectors.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of education supervision work, assign full-time education inspectors to education supervision institutions. Education supervision institutions may, based on the needs of education supervision work, appoint part-time education inspectors.  
The term of office of part-time education inspectors shall be three years, and they may serve consecutive terms, but their consecutive service shall not exceed three terms.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　督学应当符合下列条件：  
(一)坚持党的基本路线，热爱社会主义教育事业；  
(二)熟悉教育法律、法规、规章和国家教育方针、政策，具有相应的专业知识和业务能力；  
(三)坚持原则，办事公道，品行端正，廉洁自律；  
(四)具有大学本科以上学历，从事教育管理、教学或者教育研究工作10年以上，工作实绩突出；  
(五)具有较强的组织协调能力和表达能力；  
(六)身体健康，能胜任教育督导工作。  
符合前款规定条件的人员经教育督导机构考核合格，可以由县级以上人民政府任命为督学，或者由教育督导机构聘任为督学。

**Article 7** An education inspector shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) adhering to the basic line of the Communist Party of China and being devoted to the socialist education cause;  
(2) being familiar with education laws, regulations, rules, and national education policies and guidelines, and possessing relevant professional knowledge and competence;  
(3) adhering to principles, being fair in handling affairs, having good moral character, and being honest and self-disciplined;  
(4) having a bachelor's degree or above, having been engaged in education administration, teaching, or education research for more than 10 years, and having outstanding work performance;  
(5) having strong organizational and coordination skills as well as expressive abilities; and  
(6) being in good health and capable of performing education supervision duties.  
Persons meeting the conditions set forth in the preceding paragraph, after passing the assessment by the education supervision institution, may be appointed as education inspectors by the people's government at or above the county level, or be engaged as education inspectors by the education supervision institution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　督学受教育督导机构的指派实施教育督导。  
教育督导机构应当加强对督学实施教育督导活动的管理，对其履行督学职责的情况进行考核。

**Article 8** An education inspector shall implement education supervision as assigned by the education supervision institution.  
The education supervision institution shall strengthen the management of the education inspector's implementation of education supervision activities, and assess the performance of his or her duties as an education inspector.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　督学实施教育督导，应当客观公正地反映实际情况，不得隐瞒或者虚构事实。

**Article 9** When implementing education supervision, an education inspector shall reflect the actual situation objectively and fairly, and shall not conceal or fabricate facts.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施督导的督学是被督导单位主要负责人的近亲属或者有其他可能影响客观公正实施教育督导情形的，应当回避。

**Article 10** If an education inspector conducting supervision is a close relative of the principal person in charge of the unit under supervision or has any other circumstances that may affect the objective and fair implementation of education supervision, he or she shall withdraw.

### 第三章　督导的实施

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　教育督导机构对下列事项实施教育督导：  
(一)学校实施素质教育的情况，教育教学水平、教育教学管理等教育教学工作情况；  
(二)校长队伍建设情况，教师资格、职务、聘任等管理制度建设和执行情况，招生、学籍等管理情况和教育质量，学校的安全、卫生制度建设和执行情况，校舍的安全情况，教学和生活设施、设备的配备和使用等教育条件的保障情况，教育投入的管理和使用情况；  
(三)义务教育普及水平和均衡发展情况，各级各类教育的规划布局、协调发展等情况；  
(四)法律、法规、规章和国家教育政策规定的其他事项。

**Article 11** An education supervision institution shall implement education supervision on the following matters:  
(1) the implementation of quality education by schools, and the condition of education and teaching work such as the level and management of education and teaching;  
(2) the construction of the principal team; the establishment and implementation of management systems for teacher qualifications, posts, and appointment; the management of enrollment, student status, and other matters and the quality of education; the establishment and implementation of school safety and hygiene systems; the safety condition of school buildings; the assurance of educational conditions such as the allocation and use of teaching and living facilities and equipment; and the management and use of education investment;  
(3) the popularization level and balanced development of compulsory education, and the planning, layout, and coordinated development of education at all levels and of all types; and  
(4) other matters prescribed by laws, regulations, rules, and national education policies.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　教育督导机构实施教育督导，可以行使下列职权：  
(一)查阅、复制财务账目和与督导事项有关的其他文件、资料；  
(二)要求被督导单位就督导事项有关问题作出说明；  
(三)就督导事项有关问题开展调查；  
(四)向有关人民政府或者主管部门提出对被督导单位或者其相关负责人给予奖惩的建议。  
被督导单位及其工作人员对教育督导机构依法实施的教育督导应当积极配合，不得拒绝和阻挠。

**Article 12** When implementing education supervision, an education supervision institution may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to review and copy financial accounts and other documents and materials related to the supervision matters;  
(2) to require the unit under supervision to provide explanations on issues related to the supervision matters;  
(3) to conduct investigations on issues related to the supervision matters; and  
(4) to submit proposals to the relevant people's government or competent department for rewarding or punishing the unit under supervision or its relevant responsible persons.  
The unit under supervision and its staff members shall actively cooperate with the education supervision institution in the education supervision implemented according to law, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级人民政府负责教育督导的机构应当根据本行政区域内的学校布局设立教育督导责任区，指派督学对责任区内学校的教育教学工作实施经常性督导。  
教育督导机构根据教育发展需要或者本级人民政府的要求，可以就[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的一项或者几项事项对被督导单位实施专项督导，也可以就[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的所有事项对被督导单位实施综合督导。

**Article 13** The institution responsible for education supervision under the county people's government shall, based on the distribution of schools within its administrative region, establish education supervision responsibility zones, and assign education inspectors to implement regular supervision on the education and teaching work of schools within the responsibility zones.  
Based on the needs of education development or the requirements of the people's government at the corresponding level, the education supervision institution may implement special supervision on one or several matters prescribed in Article 11 of these Regulations on the unit under supervision, or may implement comprehensive supervision on all matters prescribed in Article 11 of these Regulations on the unit under supervision.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　督学对责任区内学校实施经常性督导每学期不得少于2次。  
县级以上人民政府对下一级人民政府应当每5年至少实施一次专项督导或者综合督导；县级人民政府负责教育督导的机构对本行政区域内的学校，应当每3至5年实施一次综合督导。

**Article 14** An education inspector shall conduct regular supervision on schools within his or her responsibility zone not less than twice per semester.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall conduct special supervision or comprehensive supervision on the people's government at the next lower level at least once every five years; the institution responsible for education supervision under the county people's government shall conduct comprehensive supervision on schools within its administrative region once every three to five years.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　经常性督导结束，督学应当向教育督导机构提交报告；发现违法违规办学行为或者危及师生生命安全的隐患，应当及时督促学校和相关部门处理。

**Article 15** Upon the conclusion of regular supervision, an education inspector shall submit a report to the education supervision institution; if any illegal or irregular education activities or any hidden dangers endangering the life safety of teachers and students are discovered, the education inspector shall promptly urge the school and relevant departments to handle them.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　教育督导机构实施专项督导或者综合督导，应当事先确定督导事项，成立督导小组。督导小组由3名以上督学组成。  
教育督导机构可以根据需要联合有关部门实施专项督导或者综合督导，也可以聘请相关专业人员参加专项督导或者综合督导活动。

**Article 16** When implementing special supervision or comprehensive supervision, the education supervision institution shall determine the supervision matters in advance and establish a supervision panel. The supervision panel shall consist of not less than three education inspectors.  
The education supervision institution may, as needed, collaborate with relevant departments to implement special supervision or comprehensive supervision, or engage relevant professionals to participate in special supervision or comprehensive supervision activities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　教育督导机构实施专项督导或者综合督导，应当事先向被督导单位发出书面督导通知。

**Article 17** When implementing special supervision or comprehensive supervision, the education supervision institution shall issue a written supervision notice to the unit under supervision in advance.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　教育督导机构可以要求被督导单位组织自评。被督导单位应当按照要求进行自评，并将自评报告报送教育督导机构。督导小组应当审核被督导单位的自评报告。  
督导小组应当对被督导单位进行现场考察。

**Article 18** The education supervision institution may require the unit under supervision to conduct self-evaluation. The unit under supervision shall conduct self-evaluation as required and submit the self-evaluation report to the education supervision institution. The supervision panel shall review the self-evaluation report of the unit under supervision.  
The supervision panel shall conduct on-site inspection of the unit under supervision.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　教育督导机构实施专项督导或者综合督导，应当征求公众对被督导单位的意见，并采取召开座谈会或者其他形式专门听取学生及其家长和教师的意见。

**Article 19** When implementing special supervision or comprehensive supervision, the education supervision institution shall solicit public opinions on the unit under supervision, and shall hold forums or adopt other forms to specially hear opinions from students, their parents, and teachers.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　督导小组应当对被督导单位的自评报告、现场考察情况和公众的意见进行评议，形成初步督导意见。  
督导小组应当向被督导单位反馈初步督导意见；被督导单位可以进行申辩。

**Article 20** The supervision panel shall evaluate the self-evaluation report of the unit under supervision, the on-site inspection situation, and public opinions, and form a preliminary supervision opinion.  
The supervision panel shall provide feedback to the unit under supervision on the preliminary supervision opinion; the unit under supervision may make a statement or defense.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　教育督导机构应当根据督导小组的初步督导意见，综合分析被督导单位的申辩意见，向被督导单位发出督导意见书。  
督导意见书应当就督导事项对被督导单位作出客观公正的评价；对存在的问题，应当提出限期整改要求和建议。

**Article 21** The education supervision institution shall, based on the preliminary supervision opinion of the supervision panel and a comprehensive analysis of the statement or defense of the unit under supervision, issue a supervision opinion letter to the unit under supervision.  
The supervision opinion letter shall provide an objective and fair evaluation of the unit under supervision on the supervision matters; for existing problems, it shall propose requirements and suggestions for rectification within a specified period.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被督导单位应当根据督导意见书进行整改，并将整改情况报告教育督导机构。  
教育督导机构应当对被督导单位的整改情况进行核查。

**Article 22** The unit under supervision shall carry out rectification according to the supervision opinion letter, and report the rectification situation to the education supervision institution.  
The education supervision institution shall verify the rectification situation of the unit under supervision.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　专项督导或者综合督导结束，教育督导机构应当向本级人民政府提交督导报告；县级以上地方人民政府负责教育督导的机构还应当将督导报告报上一级人民政府教育督导机构备案。  
督导报告应当向社会公布。

**Article 23** Upon the conclusion of special supervision or comprehensive supervision, the education supervision institution shall submit a supervision report to the people's government at the corresponding level; the institution responsible for education supervision under the local people's government at or above the county level shall also file the supervision report with the education supervision institution of the people's government at the next higher level for record.  
The supervision report shall be published to the public.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府或者有关主管部门应当将督导报告作为对被督导单位及其主要负责人进行考核、奖惩的重要依据。

**Article 24** The people's government at or above the county level or the relevant competent department shall take the supervision report as an important basis for assessing, rewarding, and punishing the unit under supervision and its principal persons in charge.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　被督导单位及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，由教育督导机构通报批评并责令其改正；拒不改正或者情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员，由教育督导机构向有关人民政府或者主管部门提出给予处分的建议：  
(一)拒绝、阻挠教育督导机构或者督学依法实施教育督导的；  
(二)隐瞒实情、弄虚作假，欺骗教育督导机构或者督学的；  
(三)未根据督导意见书进行整改并将整改情况报告教育督导机构的；  
(四)打击报复督学的；  
(五)有其他严重妨碍教育督导机构或者督学依法履行职责情形的。

**Article 25** Where the unit under supervision or any of its staff members is in any of the following circumstances, the education supervision institution shall circulate a notice of criticism and order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections or the circumstances are serious, the education supervision institution shall propose to the relevant people's government or competent department to give sanctions to the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) refusing or obstructing the education supervision institution or an education inspector from implementing education supervision according to law;  
(2) concealing the truth, practicing fraud, or deceiving the education supervision institution or an education inspector;  
(3) failing to carry out rectification according to the supervision opinion letter and report the rectification situation to the education supervision institution;  
(4) retaliating against an education inspector; or  
(5) having any other circumstances that seriously impede the education supervision institution or an education inspector from performing duties according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　督学或者教育督导机构工作人员有下列情形之一的，由教育督导机构给予批评教育；情节严重的，依法给予处分，对督学还应当取消任命或者聘任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)玩忽职守，贻误督导工作的；  
(二)弄虚作假，徇私舞弊，影响督导结果公正的；  
(三)滥用职权，干扰被督导单位正常工作的。  
督学违反[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定，应当回避而未回避的，由教育督导机构给予批评教育。  
督学违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定，发现违法违规办学行为或者危及师生生命安全隐患而未及时督促学校和相关部门处理的，由教育督导机构给予批评教育；情节严重的，依法给予处分，取消任命或者聘任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** Where an education inspector or a staff member of the education supervision institution is in any of the following circumstances, the education supervision institution shall give criticism and education; if the circumstances are serious, shall give sanctions according to law, and for an education inspector, shall also cancel his or her appointment or engagement; if a crime is constituted, shall investigate criminal liability according to law:  
(1) neglecting duties and delaying the supervision work;  
(2) practicing fraud or engaging in irregularities for personal gain, thereby affecting the fairness of supervision results; or  
(3) abusing power and interfering with the normal work of the unit under supervision.  
If an education inspector violates the provisions of Article 10 of these Regulations by failing to withdraw when he or she should have withdrawn, the education supervision institution shall give him or her criticism and education.  
If an education inspector violates the provisions of Article 15 of these Regulations by failing to promptly urge the school and relevant departments to handle illegal or irregular education activities or hidden dangers endangering the life safety of teachers and students after discovering them, the education supervision institution shall give him or her criticism and education; if the circumstances are serious, shall give sanctions according to law, and cancel his or her appointment or engagement; if a crime is constituted, shall investigate criminal liability according to law.
