# 征收教育费附加的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1986-07-01

> (1986年4月28日国务院发布　根据1990年6月7日《国务院关于修改〈征收教育费附加的暂行规定〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2005年8月20日《国务院关于修改〈征收教育费附加的暂行规定〉的决定》第二次修订　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一条　为贯彻落实《中共中央关于教育体制改革的决定》，加快发展地方教育事业，扩大地方教育经费的资金来源，特制定本规定。 第二条　凡缴纳消费税、增值税、营业税的单位和个人，除按照《国务院关于筹措农村学校办学经费的通知》(国发〔1984〕174号文)的规定，缴纳农村教育事业费附加的单位外，都应当依照本规定缴纳教育费附加。 第三条　教育费附加，以各单位和个人实际缴纳的增值税、营业税、消费税的税额为计征依据，教育费附加率为3%，分别与增值税、营业税、消费税同时缴纳。 除国务院另有规定者外，任何地区、部门不得擅自提高或者降低教育费附加率。 第四条　依照现行有关规定，除铁道系统、中国人民银行总行、各专业银行总行、保险总公司的教育附加随同营业税上缴中央财政外，其余单位和个人的教育费附加，均就地上缴地方财政。 第五条　教育费附加由税务机关负责征收。 教育费附加纳入预算管理，作为教育专项资金，根据“先收后支、列收列支、收支平衡”的原则使用和管理。地方各级人民政府应当依照国家有关规定，使预算内教育事业费逐步增长，不得因教育费附加纳入预算专项资金管理而抵顶教育事业费拨款。 第六条　教育费附加的征收管理，按照消费税、增值税、营业税的有关规定办理。 第七条　企业缴纳的教育费附加，一律在销售收入(或营业收入)中支付。 第八条　地方征收的教育费附加，按专项资金管理，由教育部门统筹安排，提出分配方案，商同级财政部门同意后，用于改善中小学教学设施和办学条件，不得用于职工福利和发放奖金。 铁道系统、中国人民银行总行、各专业银行总行、保险总公司随同营业税上缴的教育费附加，由国家教育委员会按年度提出分配方案，商财政部同意后，用于基础教育的薄弱环节。 地方征收的教育费附加，主要留归当地安排使用。省、自治区、直辖市可根据各地征收教育费附加的实际情况，适当提取一部分数额，用于地区之间的调剂、平衡。 第九条　地方各级教育部门每年应定期向当地人民政府、上级主管部门和财政部门，报告教育费附加的收支情况。 第十条　凡办有职工子弟学校的单位，应当先按本规定缴纳教育费附加；教育部门可根据它们办学的情况酌情返还给办学单位，作为对所办学校经费的补贴。办学单位不得借口缴纳教育费附加而撤并学校，或者缩小办学规模。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为贯彻落实《中共中央关于教育体制改革的决定》，加快发展地方教育事业，扩大地方教育经费的资金来源，特制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of implementing the Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on the Reform of the Educational System, accelerating the development of local educational undertakings, and expanding the sources of funding for local educational expenses.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡缴纳消费税、增值税、营业税的单位和个人，除按照《国务院关于筹措农村学校办学经费的通知》(国发〔1984〕174号文)的规定，缴纳农村教育事业费附加的单位外，都应当依照本规定缴纳教育费附加。

**Article 2** All units and individuals that pay consumption tax, value-added tax, or business tax shall pay educational surcharges in accordance with these Provisions, except for units that pay rural educational undertaking surcharges in accordance with the provisions of the Notice of the State Council on Raising Funds for Running Rural Schools (Document No. 174 [1984] of the State Council).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　教育费附加，以各单位和个人实际缴纳的增值税、营业税、消费税的税额为计征依据，教育费附加率为3%，分别与增值税、营业税、消费税同时缴纳。  
除国务院另有规定者外，任何地区、部门不得擅自提高或者降低教育费附加率。

**Article 3** The educational surcharge shall be levied on the basis of the amount of value-added tax, business tax, and consumption tax actually paid by each unit and individual. The rate of the educational surcharge shall be 3%, and it shall be paid simultaneously with the value-added tax, business tax, and consumption tax respectively.  
Unless otherwise provided by the State Council, no region or department shall arbitrarily raise or lower the rate of the educational surcharge.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　依照现行有关规定，除铁道系统、中国人民银行总行、各专业银行总行、保险总公司的教育附加随同营业税上缴中央财政外，其余单位和个人的教育费附加，均就地上缴地方财政。

**Article 4** In accordance with the relevant existing provisions, except for the educational surcharge of the railway system, the head office of the People's Bank of China, the head offices of specialized banks, and the head office of the insurance company, which shall be turned over to the central finance together with the business tax, the educational surcharge of other units and individuals shall be turned over to the local finance on the spot.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　教育费附加由税务机关负责征收。  
教育费附加纳入预算管理，作为教育专项资金，根据“先收后支、列收列支、收支平衡”的原则使用和管理。地方各级人民政府应当依照国家有关规定，使预算内教育事业费逐步增长，不得因教育费附加纳入预算专项资金管理而抵顶教育事业费拨款。

**Article 5** The educational surcharge shall be collected by the tax authorities.  
The educational surcharge shall be incorporated into budgetary management and used as a special educational fund, and shall be used and managed in accordance with the principle of "collection before expenditure, listing both revenue and expenditure, and balancing revenue and expenditure." Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, gradually increase the budgetary educational undertaking funds, and shall not offset the appropriations for educational undertakings because the educational surcharge is incorporated into the management of special budgetary funds.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　教育费附加的征收管理，按照消费税、增值税、营业税的有关规定办理。

**Article 6** The collection and management of the educational surcharge shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on consumption tax, value-added tax, and business tax.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　企业缴纳的教育费附加，一律在销售收入(或营业收入)中支付。

**Article 7** The educational surcharge paid by enterprises shall be paid entirely from sales revenue (or operating revenue).

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　地方征收的教育费附加，按专项资金管理，由教育部门统筹安排，提出分配方案，商同级财政部门同意后，用于改善中小学教学设施和办学条件，不得用于职工福利和发放奖金。  
铁道系统、中国人民银行总行、各专业银行总行、保险总公司随同营业税上缴的教育费附加，由国家教育委员会按年度提出分配方案，商财政部同意后，用于基础教育的薄弱环节。  
地方征收的教育费附加，主要留归当地安排使用。省、自治区、直辖市可根据各地征收教育费附加的实际情况，适当提取一部分数额，用于地区之间的调剂、平衡。

**Article 8** The educational surcharge collected by local governments shall be managed as a special fund. It shall be planned and arranged in a coordinated manner by the education department, which shall propose a distribution plan, and after consultation with the finance department at the same level and obtaining its consent, shall be used to improve teaching facilities and school conditions in primary and secondary schools, and shall not be used for employee welfare or the distribution of bonuses.  
The educational surcharge turned over together with the business tax by the railway system, the head office of the People's Bank of China, the head offices of specialized banks, and the head office of the insurance company shall be used for the weak links of basic education, with the State Education Commission proposing a distribution plan on an annual basis, after consultation with the Ministry of Finance and obtaining its consent.  
The educational surcharge collected by local governments shall mainly be retained by the local authorities for their use. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the actual circumstances of the collection of educational surcharges in various localities, appropriately withdraw a portion of the amount for adjustment and balancing among regions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地方各级教育部门每年应定期向当地人民政府、上级主管部门和财政部门，报告教育费附加的收支情况。

**Article 9** Local education departments at all levels shall report regularly each year to the local people's government, the competent department at a higher level, and the finance department on the revenue and expenditure of the educational surcharge.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　凡办有职工子弟学校的单位，应当先按本规定缴纳教育费附加；教育部门可根据它们办学的情况酌情返还给办学单位，作为对所办学校经费的补贴。办学单位不得借口缴纳教育费附加而撤并学校，或者缩小办学规模。

**Article 10** Any unit that runs a school for the children of its employees shall first pay the educational surcharge in accordance with these Provisions; the education department may, in light of the circumstances of the school running, refund the amount to the unit running the school as a subsidy for the school's funds. The unit running the school shall not use the payment of the educational surcharge as a pretext to close or merge the school, or to reduce the scale of the school.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　征收教育费附加以后，地方各级教育部门和学校，不准以任何名目向学生家长和单位集资，或者变相集资，不准以任何借口不让学生入学。  
对违反前款规定者，其上级教育部门要予以制止，直接责任人员要给予行政处分。单位和个人有权拒缴。

**Article 11** After the collection of the educational surcharge, local education departments and schools at all levels shall not raise funds from students' parents or units under any pretext, nor engage in disguised fund-raising, and shall not refuse admission to students under any excuse.  
For those who violate the provisions of the preceding paragraph, their higher-level education departments shall stop them, and the directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions. Units and individuals shall have the right to refuse payment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本规定由财政部负责解释。各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可结合当地实际情况制定实施办法。

**Article 12** The Ministry of Finance shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Provisions. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in light of local actual conditions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定从1986年7月1日起施行。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 1986.
