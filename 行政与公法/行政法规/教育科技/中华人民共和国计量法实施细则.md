### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国计量法》的规定，制定本细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Rules are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家实行法定计量单位制度。法定计量单位的名称、符号按照国务院关于在我国统一实行法定计量单位的有关规定执行。

**Article 2** The State shall adopt the system of legal units of measurement. The names and symbols of the legal units of measurement shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council on the uniform adoption of legal units of measurement in China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家有计划地发展计量事业，用现代计量技术装备各级计量检定机构，为社会主义现代化建设服务，为工农业生产、国防建设、科学实验、国内外贸易以及人民的健康、安全提供计量保证，维护国家和人民的利益。

**Article 3** The State shall develop metrology in a planned manner, equip metrological verification institutions at all levels with modern metrological technology, serve the socialist modernization, provide metrological guarantees for industrial and agricultural production, national defense construction, scientific experiments, domestic and foreign trade, as well as the health and safety of the people, and safeguard the interests of the State and the people.

### 第二章　计量基准器具和计量标准器具

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　计量基准器具（简称计量基准，下同）的使用必须具备下列条件：  
（一）经国家鉴定合格；  
（二）具有正常工作所需要的环境条件；  
（三）具有称职的保存、维护、使用人员；  
（四）具有完善的管理制度。  
符合上述条件的，经国务院计量行政部门审批并颁发计量基准证书后，方可使用。

**Article 4** The use of primary reference standards of measurement (hereinafter referred to as primary standards) shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having passed national appraisal;  
(2) having the environmental conditions necessary for normal operation;  
(3) having competent personnel for preservation, maintenance and use; and  
(4) having a sound management system.  
Where the above conditions are met, the primary standards may be used only after being examined and approved by the metrological administrative department of the State Council and issued with a primary standard certificate.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　非经国务院计量行政部门批准，任何单位和个人不得拆卸、改装计量基准，或者自行中断其计量检定工作。

**Article 5** No unit or individual may dismantle or modify primary standards, or discontinue the metrological verification work thereof without the approval of the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　计量基准的量值应当与国际上的量值保持一致。国务院计量行政部门有权废除技术水平落后或者工作状况不适应需要的计量基准。

**Article 6** The value of primary standards shall be consistent with international values. The metrological administrative department of the State Council shall have the power to abolish primary standards that are technologically backward or whose working conditions are no longer suitable.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　计量标准器具（简称计量标准，下同）的使用，必须具备下列条件：  
（一）经计量检定合格；  
（二）具有正常工作所需要的环境条件；  
（三）具有称职的保存、维护、使用人员；  
（四）具有完善的管理制度。

**Article 7** The use of reference standards of measurement (hereinafter referred to as reference standards) shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having passed metrological verification;  
(2) having the environmental conditions necessary for normal operation;  
(3) having competent personnel for preservation, maintenance and use; and  
(4) having a sound management system.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　社会公用计量标准对社会上实施计量监督具有公证作用。县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门建立的本行政区域内最高等级的社会公用计量标准，须向上一级人民政府计量行政部门申请考核；其他等级的，由当地人民政府计量行政部门主持考核。  
经考核符合本细则第七条规定条件并取得考核合格证的，由当地县级以上人民政府计量行政部门审批颁发社会公用计量标准证书后，方可使用。

**Article 8** Public reference standards of measurement shall serve as notarial instruments for metrological supervision in society. The highest-level public reference standards of measurement established within their respective administrative areas by the metrological administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall apply for examination to the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level; the examination of other levels shall be presided over by the local metrological administrative department of the people's government.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions provided in Article 7 of these Detailed Rules are met and the examination certificate is obtained, the standards may be used only after being examined, approved and issued with a public reference standard certificate by the local metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院有关主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关主管部门建立的本部门各项最高计量标准，经同级人民政府计量行政部门考核，符合本细则第七条规定条件并取得考核合格证的，由有关主管部门批准使用。

**Article 9** The highest reference standards of measurement of each department established by the relevant competent departments of the State Council and the relevant competent departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, upon examination by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the same level, where the conditions provided in Article 7 of these Detailed Rules are met and the examination certificate is obtained, be approved for use by the relevant competent department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业、事业单位建立本单位各项最高计量标准，须向与其主管部门同级的人民政府计量行政部门申请考核。乡镇企业向当地县级人民政府计量行政部门申请考核。经考核符合本细则第七条规定条件并取得考核合格证的，企业、事业单位方可使用，并向其主管部门备案。

**Article 10** Where enterprises and institutions establish their own highest reference standards of measurement, they shall apply for examination to the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the same level as their competent department. Township enterprises shall apply for examination to the local metrological administrative department of the county people's government. Where, upon examination, the conditions provided in Article 7 of these Detailed Rules are met and the examination certificate is obtained, enterprises and institutions may use them and shall file them with their competent department.

### 第三章　计量检定

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　使用实行强制检定的计量标准的单位和个人，应当向主持考核该项计量标准的有关人民政府计量行政部门申请周期检定。  
使用实行强制检定的工作计量器具的单位和个人，应当向当地县（市）级人民政府计量行政部门指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。当地不能检定的，向上一级人民政府计量行政部门指定的计量检定机构申请周期检定。

**Article 11** Units and individuals using reference standards subject to compulsory verification shall apply for periodic verification to the relevant metrological administrative department of the people's government that presided over the examination of such reference standards.  
Units and individuals using working measuring instruments subject to compulsory verification shall apply for periodic verification to the metrological verification institution designated by the local metrological administrative department of the county (city) people's government. If the local institution cannot perform the verification, they shall apply for periodic verification to the metrological verification institution designated by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　企业、事业单位应当配备与生产、科研、经营管理相适应的计量检测设施，制定具体的检定管理办法和规章制度，规定本单位管理的计量器具明细目录及相应的检定周期，保证使用的非强制检定的计量器具定期检定。

**Article 12** Enterprises and institutions shall equip themselves with metrological testing facilities appropriate to their production, scientific research and business management, formulate specific verification management measures and rules and regulations, specify the detailed catalogue of measuring instruments under their management and the corresponding verification cycles, and ensure that the non-compulsory verification measuring instruments in use are verified periodically.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　计量检定工作应当符合经济合理、就地就近的原则，不受行政区划和部门管辖的限制。

**Article 13** Metrological verification work shall conform to the principles of economy and reasonableness, convenience and proximity, and shall not be restricted by administrative divisions or departmental jurisdiction.

### 第四章　计量器具的制造和修理

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　制造、修理计量器具的企业、事业单位和个体工商户须在固定的场所从事经营，具有符合国家规定的生产设施、检验条件、技术人员等，并满足安全要求。

**Article 14** Enterprises, institutions and individual industrial and commercial households that manufacture or repair measuring instruments shall engage in business in fixed premises, have production facilities, inspection conditions, technical personnel, etc. that conform to the provisions of the State, and meet safety requirements.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　凡制造在全国范围内从未生产过的计量器具新产品，必须经过定型鉴定。定型鉴定合格后，应当履行型式批准手续，颁发证书。在全国范围内已经定型，而本单位未生产过的计量器具新产品，应当进行样机试验。样机试验合格后，发给合格证书。凡未经型式批准或者未取得样机试验合格证书的计量器具，不准生产。

**Article 15** Any new type of measuring instrument that has never been produced within the territory of China must undergo type evaluation. After passing the type evaluation, the procedure for type approval shall be completed and a certificate shall be issued. For a new type of measuring instrument that has already been type-approved within the territory of China but has not been produced by the unit in question, prototype testing shall be carried out. After passing the prototype testing, a certificate of conformity shall be issued. No measuring instrument that has not been type-approved or has not obtained a prototype test certificate of conformity may be produced.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　计量器具新产品定型鉴定，由国务院计量行政部门授权的技术机构进行；样机试验由所在地方的省级人民政府计量行政部门授权的技术机构进行。  
计量器具新产品的型式，由当地省级人民政府计量行政部门批准。省级人民政府计量行政部门批准的型式，经国务院计量行政部门审核同意后，作为全国通用型式。

**Article 16** Type evaluation of new types of measuring instruments shall be carried out by the technical institution authorized by the metrological administrative department of the State Council; prototype testing shall be carried out by the technical institution authorized by the metrological administrative department of the provincial people's government in the locality.  
The type of a new type of measuring instrument shall be approved by the local metrological administrative department of the provincial people's government. The type approved by the metrological administrative department of the provincial people's government shall, after being examined and approved by the metrological administrative department of the State Council, become the type universally applicable throughout the country.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请计量器具新产品定型鉴定和样机试验的单位，应当提供新产品样机及有关技术文件、资料。  
负责计量器具新产品定型鉴定和样机试验的单位，对申请单位提供的样机和技术文件、资料必须保密。

**Article 17** The unit applying for type evaluation and prototype testing of a new type of measuring instrument shall provide the prototype of the new product and relevant technical documents and materials.  
The unit responsible for type evaluation and prototype testing of the new type of measuring instrument must keep confidential the prototype and technical documents and materials provided by the applicant unit.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对企业、事业单位制造、修理计量器具的质量，各有关主管部门应当加强管理，县级以上人民政府计量行政部门有权进行监督检查，包括抽检和监督试验。凡无产品合格印、证，或者经检定不合格的计量器具，不准出厂。

**Article 18** The quality of measuring instruments manufactured or repaired by enterprises and institutions shall be strengthened in management by the relevant competent departments. The metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall have the power to conduct supervision and inspection, including random inspection and supervisory testing. No measuring instrument without a product qualification mark or certificate, or failing verification, may leave the factory.

### 第五章　计量器具的销售和使用

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外商在中国销售计量器具，须比照本细则第十五条的规定向国务院计量行政部门申请型式批准。

**Article 19** Foreign businessmen selling measuring instruments in China shall, mutatis mutandis, apply to the metrological administrative department of the State Council for type approval in accordance with Article 15 of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门对当地销售的计量器具实施监督检查。凡没有产品合格印、证标志的计量器具不得销售。

**Article 20** The metrological administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervision and inspection on measuring instruments sold locally. No measuring instrument without a product qualification mark or certificate may be sold.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　任何单位和个人不得经营销售残次计量器具零配件，不得使用残次零配件组装和修理计量器具。

**Article 21** No unit or individual may deal in or sell defective spare parts of measuring instruments, or use defective spare parts to assemble or repair measuring instruments.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位和个人不准在工作岗位上使用无检定合格印、证或者超过检定周期以及经检定不合格的计量器具。在教学示范中使用计量器具不受此限。

**Article 22** No unit or individual may use measuring instruments on the job that lack a verification qualification mark or certificate, or that have exceeded the verification cycle, or that have failed verification. The use of measuring instruments for teaching demonstration shall not be subject to this restriction.

### 第六章　计量监督

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院计量行政部门和县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门监督和贯彻实施计量法律、法规的职责是：  
（一）贯彻执行国家计量工作的方针、政策和规章制度，推行国家法定计量单位；  
（二）制定和协调计量事业的发展规划，建立计量基准和社会公用计量标准，组织量值传递；  
（三）对制造、修理、销售、使用计量器具实施监督；  
（四）进行计量认证，组织仲裁检定，调解计量纠纷；  
（五）监督检查计量法律、法规的实施情况，对违反计量法律、法规的行为，按照本细则的有关规定进行处理。

**Article 23** The duties of the metrological administrative department of the State Council and the metrological administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level in supervising and implementing metrological laws and regulations are:  
(1) to implement the guidelines, policies and rules and regulations of the State on metrology work, and promote the national legal units of measurement;  
(2) to formulate and coordinate development plans for metrology, establish primary standards and public reference standards, and organize the dissemination of values;  
(3) to supervise the manufacture, repair, sale and use of measuring instruments;  
(4) to conduct metrological accreditation, organize arbitration verification, and mediate metrological disputes; and  
(5) to supervise and inspect the implementation of metrological laws and regulations, and deal with acts violating metrological laws and regulations in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门的计量管理人员，负责执行计量监督、管理任务；计量监督员负责在规定的区域、场所巡回检查，并可根据不同情况在规定的权限内对违反计量法律、法规的行为，进行现场处理，执行行政处罚。  
计量监督员必须经考核合格后，由县级以上人民政府计量行政部门任命并颁发监督员证件。

**Article 24** Metrological management personnel of the metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for carrying out metrological supervision and management tasks; metrological supervisors shall be responsible for making inspection tours in prescribed areas and places, and may, according to different circumstances and within the prescribed limits of authority, deal with acts violating metrological laws and regulations on the spot and impose administrative penalties.  
Metrological supervisors must pass examination and be appointed by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, which shall issue them with supervisor credentials.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门依法设置的计量检定机构，为国家法定计量检定机构。其职责是：负责研究建立计量基准、社会公用计量标准，进行量值传递，执行强制检定和法律规定的其他检定、测试任务，起草技术规范，为实施计量监督提供技术保证，并承办有关计量监督工作。

**Article 25** Metrological verification institutions established according to law by the metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be the State statutory metrological verification institutions. Their duties are: to be responsible for researching and establishing primary standards and public reference standards, carrying out dissemination of values, performing compulsory verification and other verification and testing tasks as provided by law, drafting technical specifications, providing technical guarantees for the implementation of metrological supervision, and undertaking relevant metrological supervision work.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家法定计量检定机构的计量检定人员，必须经考核合格。  
计量检定人员的技术职务系列，由国务院计量行政部门会同有关主管部门制定。

**Article 26** Metrological verification personnel of the State statutory metrological verification institutions must pass examination.  
The technical post series for metrological verification personnel shall be formulated by the metrological administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门可以根据需要，采取以下形式授权其他单位的计量检定机构和技术机构，在规定的范围内执行强制检定和其他检定、测试任务：  
（一）授权专业性或区域性计量检定机构，作为法定计量检定机构；  
（二）授权建立社会公用计量标准；  
（三）授权某一部门或某一单位的计量检定机构，对其内部使用的强制检定计量器具执行强制检定；  
（四）授权有关技术机构，承担法律规定的其他检定、测试任务。

**Article 27** The metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level may, as needed, authorize the metrological verification institutions and technical institutions of other units in the following forms to perform compulsory verification and other verification and testing tasks within the prescribed scope:  
(1) to authorize specialized or regional metrological verification institutions to serve as statutory metrological verification institutions;  
(2) to authorize the establishment of public reference standards;  
(3) to authorize the metrological verification institution of a certain department or unit to perform compulsory verification on the compulsory verification measuring instruments used internally; and  
(4) to authorize relevant technical institutions to undertake other verification and testing tasks as provided by law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　根据本细则第二十七条规定被授权的单位，应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）被授权单位执行检定、测试任务的人员，必须经考核合格；  
（二）被授权单位的相应计量标准，必须接受计量基准或者社会公用计量标准的检定；  
（三）被授权单位承担授权的检定、测试工作，须接受授权单位的监督；  
（四）被授权单位成为计量纠纷中当事人一方时，在双方协商不能自行解决的情况下，由县级以上有关人民政府计量行政部门进行调解和仲裁检定。

**Article 28** Units authorized in accordance with Article 27 of these Detailed Rules shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) the personnel of the authorized unit performing verification and testing tasks must pass examination;  
(2) the corresponding reference standards of the authorized unit must undergo verification against primary standards or public reference standards;  
(3) the authorized unit shall, when undertaking the authorized verification and testing work, accept supervision by the authorizing unit; and  
(4) when the authorized unit becomes a party to a metrological dispute and the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation between the parties, the relevant metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall conduct mediation and arbitration verification.

### 第七章　产品质量检验机构的计量认证

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　为社会提供公证数据的产品质量检验机构，必须经省级以上人民政府计量行政部门计量认证。

**Article 29** Product quality inspection institutions that provide notarial data to the public must undergo metrological accreditation by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　产品质量检验机构计量认证的内容：  
（一）计量检定、测试设备的性能；  
（二）计量检定、测试设备的工作环境和人员的操作技能；  
（三）保证量值统一、准确的措施及检测数据公正可靠的管理制度。

**Article 30** The contents of metrological accreditation of product quality inspection institutions are:  
(1) the performance of metrological verification and testing equipment;  
(2) the working environment of metrological verification and testing equipment and the operational skills of personnel; and  
(3) measures to ensure unity and accuracy of values and a management system to ensure the impartiality and reliability of test data.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　产品质量检验机构提出计量认证申请后，省级以上人民政府计量行政部门应指定所属的计量检定机构或者被授权的技术机构按照本细则第三十条规定的内容进行考核。考核合格后，由接受申请的省级以上人民政府计量行政部门发给计量认证合格证书。产品质量检验机构自愿签署告知承诺书并按要求提交材料的，按照告知承诺相关程序办理。未取得计量认证合格证书的，不得开展产品质量检验工作。

**Article 31** After a product quality inspection institution submits an application for metrological accreditation, the metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall designate a subordinate metrological verification institution or an authorized technical institution to conduct an examination in accordance with the contents provided in Article 30 of these Detailed Rules. Upon passing the examination, the metrological administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level that accepted the application shall issue a metrological accreditation certificate. Where a product quality inspection institution voluntarily signs a notification commitment letter and submits materials as required, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant procedures for notification commitment. Any institution that has not obtained a metrological accreditation certificate may not carry out product quality inspection work.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　省级以上人民政府计量行政部门有权对计量认证合格的产品质量检验机构，按照本细则第三十条规定的内容进行监督检查。

**Article 32** The metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the provincial level shall have the power to conduct supervision and inspection on product quality inspection institutions that have passed metrological accreditation, in accordance with the contents provided in Article 30 of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　已经取得计量认证合格证书的产品质量检验机构，需新增检验项目时，应按照本细则有关规定，申请单项计量认证。

**Article 33** Where a product quality inspection institution that has obtained a metrological accreditation certificate needs to add new inspection items, it shall apply for single-item metrological accreditation in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Detailed Rules.

### 第八章　计量调解和仲裁检定

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门负责计量纠纷的调解和仲裁检定，并可根据司法机关、合同管理机关、涉外仲裁机关或者其他单位的委托，指定有关计量检定机构进行仲裁检定。

**Article 34** The metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the mediation and arbitration verification of metrological disputes, and may, upon the entrustment of judicial organs, contract administration organs, foreign-related arbitration organs or other units, designate relevant metrological verification institutions to carry out arbitration verification.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在调解、仲裁及案件审理过程中，任何一方当事人均不得改变与计量纠纷有关的计量器具的技术状态。

**Article 35** During the process of mediation, arbitration and case trial, no party may alter the technical condition of the measuring instruments involved in the metrological dispute.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　计量纠纷当事人对仲裁检定不服的，可以在接到仲裁检定通知书之日起15日内向上一级人民政府计量行政部门申诉。上一级人民政府计量行政部门进行的仲裁检定为终局仲裁检定。

**Article 36** Where a party to a metrological dispute is dissatisfied with the arbitration verification, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the arbitration verification notice, appeal to the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level. The arbitration verification conducted by the metrological administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level shall be the final arbitration verification.

### 第九章　费用

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　建立计量标准申请考核，使用计量器具申请检定，制造计量器具新产品申请定型和样机试验，以及申请计量认证和仲裁检定，应当缴纳费用，具体收费办法或收费标准，由国务院计量行政部门会同国家财政、物价部门统一制定。

**Article 37** Fees shall be paid for applying for examination for establishing reference standards, applying for verification of measuring instruments in use, applying for type evaluation and prototype testing of new types of measuring instruments, and applying for metrological accreditation and arbitration verification. The specific fee collection methods or fee standards shall be uniformly formulated by the metrological administrative department of the State Council jointly with the State finance and pricing departments.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门实施监督检查所进行的检定和试验不收费。被检查的单位有提供样机和检定试验条件的义务。

**Article 38** The verification and testing carried out by the metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level for supervision and inspection purposes shall not be charged. The unit being inspected shall have the obligation to provide prototypes and conditions for verification and testing.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府计量行政部门所属的计量检定机构，为贯彻计量法律、法规，实施计量监督提供技术保证所需要的经费，按照国家财政管理体制的规定，分别列入各级财政预算。

**Article 39** The funds needed by metrological verification institutions subordinate to the metrological administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level to implement metrological laws and regulations and provide technical guarantees for the implementation of metrological supervision shall be included in the fiscal budgets at various levels in accordance with the provisions of the State financial management system.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本细则第二条规定，使用非法定计量单位的，责令其改正；属出版物的，责令其停止销售，可并处1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Whoever violates Article 2 of these Detailed Rules by using non-legal units of measurement shall be ordered to make corrections; if the violation concerns publications, the party shall be ordered to stop selling them, and may be concurrently fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反[《中华人民共和国计量法》第十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国计量法.md#art-14)规定，制造、销售和进口非法定计量单位的计量器具的，责令其停止制造、销售和进口，没收计量器具和全部违法所得，可并处相当其违法所得10%至50%的罚款。

**Article 41** Whoever violates Article 14 of the Metrology Law of the People's Republic of China by manufacturing, selling or importing measuring instruments in non-legal units of measurement shall be ordered to stop manufacturing, selling or importing, and his/her measuring instruments and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and he/she may be concurrently fined not less than 10% but not more than 50% of his/her illegal gains.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　部门和企业、事业单位的各项最高计量标准，未经有关人民政府计量行政部门考核合格而开展计量检定的，责令其停止使用，可并处1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where the highest reference standards of measurement of a department, enterprise or institution are used to carry out metrological verification without having passed examination by the relevant metrological administrative department of the people's government, the party shall be ordered to stop using them, and may be concurrently fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　属于强制检定范围的计量器具，未按照规定申请检定和属于非强制检定范围的计量器具未自行定期检定或者送其他计量检定机构定期检定的，以及经检定不合格继续使用的，责令其停止使用，可并处1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where measuring instruments within the scope of compulsory verification are not submitted for verification as required, or measuring instruments within the scope of non-compulsory verification are not periodically verified by themselves or sent to other metrological verification institutions for periodic verification, or where measuring instruments failing verification continue to be used, the party shall be ordered to stop using them, and may be concurrently fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　制造、销售未经型式批准或样机试验合格的计量器具新产品的，责令其停止制造、销售，封存该种新产品，没收全部违法所得，可并处3000元以下的罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国进口计量器具监督管理办法.md#art-15" title="被《中华人民共和国进口计量器具监督管理办法》第15条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 44** Whoever manufactures or sells new types of measuring instruments that have not been type-approved or have not passed prototype testing shall be ordered to stop manufacturing or selling, to seal up such new products, and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and he/she may be concurrently fined not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　制造、修理的计量器具未经出厂检定或者经检定不合格而出厂的，责令其停止出厂，没收全部违法所得；情节严重的，可并处3000元以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Where measuring instruments manufactured or repaired are shipped out of the factory without factory verification or having failed verification, the party shall be ordered to stop shipping, and all illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, he/she may be concurrently fined not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　使用不合格计量器具或者破坏计量器具准确度和伪造数据，给国家和消费者造成损失的，责令其赔偿损失，没收计量器具和全部违法所得，可并处2000元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Whoever uses unqualified measuring instruments or damages the accuracy of measuring instruments or forges data, causing losses to the State or consumers, shall be ordered to compensate for the losses, and his/her measuring instruments and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and he/she may be concurrently fined not more than 2,000 yuan.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　经营销售残次计量器具零配件的，责令其停止经营销售，没收残次计量器具零配件和全部违法所得，可并处2000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由工商行政管理部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 47** Whoever deals in or sells defective spare parts of measuring instruments shall be ordered to stop dealing or selling, and the defective spare parts and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and he/she may be concurrently fined not more than 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke his/her business license.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　制造、销售、使用以欺骗消费者为目的的计量器具的单位和个人，没收其计量器具和全部违法所得，可并处2000元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，对个人或者单位直接责任人员，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** For units and individuals that manufacture, sell or use measuring instruments for the purpose of deceiving consumers, their measuring instruments and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and they may be concurrently fined not more than 2,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, the individual or the person directly responsible of the unit shall be subject to criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　个体工商户制造、修理国家规定范围以外的计量器具或者不按照规定场所从事经营活动的，责令其停止制造、修理，没收全部违法所得，可并处以500元以下的罚款。

**Article 49** Where an individual industrial or commercial household manufactures or repairs measuring instruments beyond the scope specified by the State, or engages in business activities not at the prescribed premises, the party shall be ordered to stop manufacturing or repairing, and all illegal gains shall be confiscated, and he/she may be concurrently fined not more than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未取得计量认证合格证书的产品质量检验机构，为社会提供公证数据的，责令其停止检验，可并处1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Where a product quality inspection institution that has not obtained a metrological accreditation certificate provides notarial data to the public, it shall be ordered to stop inspection, and may be concurrently fined not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　伪造、盗用、倒卖强制检定印、证的，没收其非法检定印、证和全部违法所得，可并处2000元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Whoever forges, misappropriates or scalps compulsory verification stamps or certificates shall have his/her illegal verification stamps or certificates and all illegal gains confiscated, and may be concurrently fined not more than 2,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　计量监督管理人员违法失职，徇私舞弊，情节轻微的，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a metrological supervision and management person violates the law, neglects his/her duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, if the circumstances are minor, he/she shall be given an administrative sanction; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　负责计量器具新产品定型鉴定、样机试验的单位，违反本细则第十七条第二款规定的，应当按照国家有关规定，赔偿申请单位的损失，并给予直接责任人员行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Where a unit responsible for type evaluation or prototype testing of new types of measuring instruments violates the provisions of Article 17, paragraph 2 of these Detailed Rules, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, compensate the applicant unit for its losses, and the person directly responsible shall be given an administrative sanction; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　计量检定人员有下列行为之一的，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）伪造检定数据的；  
（二）出具错误数据，给送检一方造成损失的；  
（三）违反计量检定规程进行计量检定的；  
（四）使用未经考核合格的计量标准开展检定的；  
（五）未经考核合格执行计量检定的。

**Article 54** Where a metrological verification person commits any of the following acts, he/she shall be given an administrative sanction; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) forging verification data;  
(2) issuing erroneous data, causing losses to the party submitting for verification;  
(3) conducting metrological verification in violation of metrological verification regulations;  
(4) using reference standards that have not passed examination to carry out verification; or  
(5) performing metrological verification without having passed examination.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本细则规定的行政处罚，由县级以上地方人民政府计量行政部门决定。罚款1万元以上的，应当报省级人民政府计量行政部门决定。没收违法所得及罚款一律上缴国库。  
本细则第四十六条规定的行政处罚，也可以由工商行政管理部门决定。

**Article 55** The administrative penalties provided in these Detailed Rules shall be decided by the metrological administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level. A fine of more than 10,000 yuan shall be reported to the metrological administrative department of the provincial people's government for decision. All confiscated illegal gains and fines shall be turned over to the State treasury.  
The administrative penalty provided in Article 46 of these Detailed Rules may also be decided by the administrative department for industry and commerce.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本细则下列用语的含义是：  
（一）计量器具是指能用以直接或间接测出被测对象量值的装置、仪器仪表、量具和用于统一量值的标准物质，包括计量基准、计量标准、工作计量器具。  
（二）计量检定是指为评定计量器具的计量性能，确定其是否合格所进行的全部工作。  
（三）定型鉴定是指对计量器具新产品样机的计量性能进行全面审查、考核。  
（四）计量认证是指政府计量行政部门对有关技术机构计量检定、测试的能力和可靠性进行的考核和证明。  
（五）计量检定机构是指承担计量检定工作的有关技术机构。  
（六）仲裁检定是指用计量基准或者社会公用计量标准所进行的以裁决为目的的计量检定、测试活动。

**Article 56** For the purpose of these Detailed Rules, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "Measuring instruments" means devices, instruments, gauges, and reference materials used to unify values that can directly or indirectly measure the value of the measured object, including primary standards, reference standards, and working measuring instruments.  
(2) "Metrological verification" means all the work carried out to evaluate the metrological performance of measuring instruments and determine whether they are qualified.  
(3) "Type evaluation" means the comprehensive examination and assessment of the metrological performance of a prototype of a new type of measuring instrument.  
(4) "Metrological accreditation" means the examination and certification by the government metrological administrative department of the capability and reliability of metrological verification and testing of relevant technical institutions.  
(5) "Metrological verification institution" means the relevant technical institution that undertakes metrological verification work.  
(6) "Arbitration verification" means metrological verification and testing activities carried out using primary standards or public reference standards for the purpose of adjudication.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　中国人民解放军和国防科技工业系统涉及本系统以外的计量工作的监督管理，亦适用本细则。

**Article 57** These Detailed Rules shall also apply to the supervision and administration of metrology work involving systems outside their own systems by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the national defense science, technology and industry system.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本细则有关的管理办法、管理范围和各种印、证标志，由国务院计量行政部门制定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国进口计量器具监督管理办法.md#art-17" title="被《中华人民共和国进口计量器具监督管理办法》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 58** The relevant administrative measures, scope of administration and various stamps and certificate marks of these Detailed Rules shall be formulated by the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本细则由国务院计量行政部门负责解释。

**Article 59** These Detailed Rules shall be interpreted by the metrological administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本细则自发布之日起施行。

**Article 60** These Detailed Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
