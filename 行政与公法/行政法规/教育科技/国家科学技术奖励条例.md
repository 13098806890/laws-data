# 国家科学技术奖励条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-05-26  
**生效日期**：2024-05-26

> （1999年5月23日中华人民共和国国务院令第265号发布　根据2003年12月20日《国务院关于修改〈国家科学技术奖励条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　2020年10月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第731号第三次修订　根据2024年5月26日《国务院关于修改〈国家科学技术奖励条例〉的决定》第四次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了奖励在科学技术进步活动中做出突出贡献的个人、组织，调动科学技术工作者的积极性和创造性，建设创新型国家和世界科技强国，根据《中华人民共和国科学技术进步法》，制定本条例。 第二条　国家设立下列国家科学技术奖： （一）国家最高科学技术奖； （二）国家自然科学奖； （三）国家技术发明奖； （四）国家科学技术进步奖； （五）中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖。 第三条　国家科学技术奖应当坚持国家战略导向，与国家重大战略需要和中长期科技发展规划紧密结合。国家加大对自然科学基础研究和应用基础研究的奖励。国家自然科学奖应当注重前瞻性、理论性，国家技术发明奖应当注重原创性、实用性，国家科学技术进步奖应当注重创新性、效益性。 第四条　国家科学技术奖励工作坚持党中央集中统一领导，实施创新驱动发展战略，贯彻尊重劳动、尊重知识、尊重人才、尊重创造的方针，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观。 国家科学技术奖励工作重大事项，应当按照有关规定报党中央。 第五条　国家维护国家科学技术奖的公正性、严肃性、权威性和荣誉性，将国家科学技术奖授予追求真理、潜心研究、学有所长、研有所专、敢于超越、勇攀高峰的科技工作者。 国家科学技术奖的提名、评审和授予，不受任何组织或者个人干涉。 第六条　国务院科学技术行政部门负责国家科学技术奖的相关办法制定和评审活动的组织工作。对涉及国家安全的项目，应当采取严格的保密措施。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了奖励在科学技术进步活动中做出突出贡献的个人、组织，调动科学技术工作者的积极性和创造性，建设创新型国家和世界科技强国，根据《中华人民共和国科学技术进步法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Science and Technology Progress, for the purposes of rewarding individuals and organizations that have made outstanding contributions in science and technology progress activities, motivating the initiative and creativity of scientific and technical workers, and building an innovative country and a world leader in science and technology.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家设立下列国家科学技术奖：  
（一）国家最高科学技术奖；  
（二）国家自然科学奖；  
（三）国家技术发明奖；  
（四）国家科学技术进步奖；  
（五）中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖。

**Article 2** The State shall establish the following State Science and Technology Awards:  
(a) the State Preeminent Science and Technology Award;  
(b) the State Natural Science Award;  
(c) the State Technological Invention Award;  
(d) the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award; and  
(e) the People's Republic of China International Science and Technology Cooperation Award.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家科学技术奖应当坚持国家战略导向，与国家重大战略需要和中长期科技发展规划紧密结合。国家加大对自然科学基础研究和应用基础研究的奖励。国家自然科学奖应当注重前瞻性、理论性，国家技术发明奖应当注重原创性、实用性，国家科学技术进步奖应当注重创新性、效益性。

**Article 3** The State Science and Technology Awards shall adhere to the national strategic orientation and be closely integrated with the major strategic needs of the State and the medium- and long-term science and technology development plans. The State shall increase rewards for basic research in natural sciences and applied basic research. The State Natural Science Award shall focus on foresight and theoretical significance, the State Technological Invention Award shall focus on originality and practicality, and the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award shall focus on innovation and benefits.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家科学技术奖励工作坚持党中央集中统一领导，实施创新驱动发展战略，贯彻尊重劳动、尊重知识、尊重人才、尊重创造的方针，培育和践行社会主义核心价值观。  
国家科学技术奖励工作重大事项，应当按照有关规定报党中央。

**Article 4** The work of State science and technology awards shall adhere to the centralized and unified leadership of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, implement the innovation-driven development strategy, carry out the policy of respecting labor, knowledge, talent, and creation, and cultivate and practice the core socialist values.  
Major matters concerning the work of State science and technology awards shall be reported to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家维护国家科学技术奖的公正性、严肃性、权威性和荣誉性，将国家科学技术奖授予追求真理、潜心研究、学有所长、研有所专、敢于超越、勇攀高峰的科技工作者。  
国家科学技术奖的提名、评审和授予，不受任何组织或者个人干涉。

**Article 5** The State shall uphold the fairness, seriousness, authority, and honor of the State Science and Technology Awards, and confer these awards on scientific and technical workers who pursue truth, engage in dedicated research, have expertise in a specific field of study, specialize in a particular area of research, dare to surpass, and strive to reach new heights.  
The nomination, review, and conferment of the State Science and Technology Awards shall not be subject to interference by any organization or individual.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院科学技术行政部门负责国家科学技术奖的相关办法制定和评审活动的组织工作。对涉及国家安全的项目，应当采取严格的保密措施。  
国家科学技术奖励应当实施绩效管理。

**Article 6** The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating relevant measures for the State Science and Technology Awards and organizing review activities. For projects involving national security, strict confidentiality measures shall be taken.  
The State science and technology awards shall be subject to performance management.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家设立国家科学技术奖励委员会。国家科学技术奖励委员会聘请有关方面的专家、学者等组成评审委员会和监督委员会，负责国家科学技术奖的评审和监督工作。  
国家科学技术奖励委员会的组成人员人选由国务院科学技术行政部门提出，报党中央、国务院批准。

**Article 7** The State shall establish a National Science and Technology Awards Committee. The National Science and Technology Awards Committee shall engage experts and scholars from relevant fields to form a Review Committee and a Supervision Committee, which shall be responsible for the review and supervision of the State Science and Technology Awards.  
The candidates for members of the National Science and Technology Awards Committee shall be proposed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council and submitted to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council for approval.

### 第二章　国家科学技术奖的设置

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家最高科学技术奖授予下列中国公民：  
（一）在当代科学技术前沿取得重大突破或者在科学技术发展中有卓越建树的；  
（二）在科学技术创新、科学技术成果转化和高技术产业化中，创造巨大经济效益、社会效益、生态环境效益或者对维护国家安全做出巨大贡献的。  
国家最高科学技术奖不分等级，每次授予人数不超过2名。

**Article 8** The State Preeminent Science and Technology Award shall be conferred on Chinese citizens who meet one of the following criteria:  
(a) have made major breakthroughs at the frontiers of contemporary science and technology or have made outstanding achievements in science and technology development; or  
(b) have created enormous economic, social, or ecological and environmental benefits or have made tremendous contributions to the maintenance of national security in scientific and technological innovation, transformation of scientific and technological achievements, and high-tech industrialization.  
The State Preeminent Science and Technology Award shall have no grades, and the number of recipients shall not exceed two at each conferment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家自然科学奖授予在基础研究和应用基础研究中阐明自然现象、特征和规律，做出重大科学发现的个人。  
前款所称重大科学发现，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）前人尚未发现或者尚未阐明；  
（二）具有重大科学价值；  
（三）得到国内外自然科学界公认。

**Article 9** The State Natural Science Award shall be conferred on individuals who have elucidated natural phenomena, characteristics, and laws in basic research and applied basic research, and have made significant scientific discoveries.  
A significant scientific discovery as referred to in the preceding paragraph shall meet the following conditions:  
(a) it has not been previously discovered or elucidated by others;  
(b) it has significant scientific value; and  
(c) it is recognized by the natural science community both domestically and internationally.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家技术发明奖授予运用科学技术知识做出产品、工艺、材料、器件及其系统等重大技术发明的个人。  
前款所称重大技术发明，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）前人尚未发明或者尚未公开；  
（二）具有先进性、创造性、实用性；  
（三）经实施，创造显著经济效益、社会效益、生态环境效益或者对维护国家安全做出显著贡献，且具有良好的应用前景。

**Article 10** The State Technological Invention Award shall be conferred on individuals who have made major technological inventions, including products, processes, materials, devices, and their systems, by utilizing scientific and technological knowledge.  
A major technological invention as referred to in the preceding paragraph shall meet the following conditions:  
(a) it has not been previously invented or disclosed by others;  
(b) it is advanced, creative, and practical; and  
(c) after implementation, it has created significant economic, social, or ecological and environmental benefits, or has made significant contributions to the maintenance of national security, and has good application prospects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家科学技术进步奖授予完成和应用推广创新性科学技术成果，为推动科学技术进步和经济社会发展做出突出贡献的个人、组织。  
前款所称创新性科学技术成果，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）技术创新性突出，技术经济指标先进；  
（二）经应用推广，创造显著经济效益、社会效益、生态环境效益或者对维护国家安全做出显著贡献；  
（三）在推动行业科学技术进步等方面有重大贡献。

**Article 11** The State Scientific and Technological Progress Award shall be conferred on individuals and organizations that have completed, applied, and promoted innovative scientific and technological achievements, and have made outstanding contributions to promoting scientific and technological progress and economic and social development.  
An innovative scientific and technological achievement as referred to in the preceding paragraph shall meet the following conditions:  
(a) the technological innovation is outstanding, and the technical and economic indicators are advanced;  
(b) after application and promotion, it has created significant economic, social, or ecological and environmental benefits, or has made significant contributions to the maintenance of national security; and  
(c) it has made significant contributions to advancing scientific and technological progress in the relevant industry.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家自然科学奖、国家技术发明奖、国家科学技术进步奖分为一等奖、二等奖2个等级；对做出特别重大的科学发现、技术发明或者创新性科学技术成果的，可以授予特等奖。

**Article 12** The State Natural Science Award, the State Technological Invention Award, and the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award shall be divided into two grades: first prize and second prize. For achievements that constitute particularly significant scientific discoveries, technological inventions, or innovative scientific and technological achievements, a special prize may be conferred.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖授予对中国科学技术事业做出重要贡献的下列外国人或者外国组织：  
（一）同中国的公民或者组织合作研究、开发，取得重大科学技术成果的；  
（二）向中国的公民或者组织传授先进科学技术、培养人才，成效特别显著的；  
（三）为促进中国与外国的国际科学技术交流与合作，做出重要贡献的。  
中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖不分等级。

**Article 13** The People's Republic of China International Science and Technology Cooperation Award shall be conferred on foreigners or foreign organizations that have made important contributions to China's science and technology undertakings and meet one of the following criteria:  
(a) have cooperated with Chinese citizens or organizations in research and development and achieved significant scientific and technological results;  
(b) have imparted advanced science and technology to Chinese citizens or organizations and cultivated talents, with particularly notable results; or  
(c) have made important contributions to promoting international science and technology exchanges and cooperation between China and foreign countries.  
The People's Republic of China International Science and Technology Cooperation Award shall have no grades.

### 第三章　国家科学技术奖的提名、评审和授予

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家科学技术奖实行提名制度，不受理自荐。候选者由下列单位或者个人提名：  
（一）符合国务院科学技术行政部门规定的资格条件的专家、学者、组织机构；  
（二）中央和国家机关有关部门，中央军事委员会科学技术部门，省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市人民政府。  
香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区的有关个人、组织的提名资格条件，由国务院科学技术行政部门规定。  
中华人民共和国驻外使馆、领馆可以提名中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖的候选者。

**Article 14** The State Science and Technology Awards shall adopt a nomination system and shall not accept self-nominations. Candidates shall be nominated by the following entities or individuals:  
(a) experts, scholars, and organizations that meet the eligibility conditions prescribed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council;  
(b) relevant departments of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and state organs, the science and technology department of the Central Military Commission, and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status.  
The eligibility conditions for nomination by relevant individuals and organizations from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region shall be prescribed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council.  
The embassies and consulates of the People's Republic of China abroad may nominate candidates for the People's Republic of China International Science and Technology Cooperation Award.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　提名者应当严格按照提名办法提名，提供提名材料，对材料的真实性和准确性负责，并按照规定承担相应责任。  
提名办法由国务院科学技术行政部门制定。

**Article 15** A nominator shall make nominations strictly in accordance with the nomination measures, provide nomination materials, be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the materials, and bear corresponding liabilities as prescribed.  
The nomination measures shall be formulated by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在科学技术活动中有下列情形之一的，相关个人、组织不得被提名或者授予国家科学技术奖：  
（一）危害国家安全、损害社会公共利益、危害人体健康、违反伦理道德的；  
（二）有科研不端行为，按照国家有关规定被禁止参与国家科学技术奖励活动的；  
（三）有国务院科学技术行政部门规定的其他情形的。

**Article 16** Where any of the following circumstances exists in scientific and technological activities, the relevant individuals or organizations shall not be nominated for or conferred with a State Science and Technology Award:  
(a) endangering national security, harming public interests, endangering human health, or violating ethics and morality;  
(b) having committed scientific research misconduct and being prohibited from participating in State science and technology award activities according to the relevant State provisions; or  
(c) having other circumstances prescribed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院科学技术行政部门应当建立覆盖各学科、各领域的评审专家库，并及时更新。评审专家应当精通所从事学科、领域的专业知识，具有较高的学术水平和良好的科学道德。

**Article 17** The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall establish a database of review experts covering all disciplines and fields and update it in a timely manner. Review experts shall have a thorough command of the professional knowledge of their respective disciplines and fields, possess a high level of academic attainment, and have good scientific ethics.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　评审活动应当坚持公开、公平、公正的原则。评审专家与候选者有重大利害关系，可能影响评审公平、公正的，应当回避。  
评审委员会的评审委员和参与评审活动的评审专家应当遵守评审工作纪律，不得有利用评审委员、评审专家身份牟取利益或者与其他评审委员、评审专家串通表决等可能影响评审公平、公正的行为。  
评审办法由国务院科学技术行政部门制定。

**Article 18** The review activities shall adhere to the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality. Review experts who have a significant interest in the candidate that may affect the fairness and impartiality of the review shall withdraw.  
Members of the Review Committee and review experts participating in the review activities shall abide by the discipline of review work, and shall not engage in acts that may affect the fairness and impartiality of the review, such as using their status as review committee members or review experts to seek benefits or colluding with other review committee members or review experts in voting.  
The review measures shall be formulated by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　评审委员会设立评审组进行初评，评审组负责提出初评建议并提交评审委员会。  
参与初评的评审专家从评审专家库中抽取产生。

**Article 19** The Review Committee shall establish review groups to conduct preliminary reviews. The review groups shall be responsible for putting forward preliminary review recommendations and submitting them to the Review Committee.  
Review experts participating in the preliminary review shall be selected from the review expert database by drawing lots.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　评审委员会根据相关办法对初评建议进行评审，并向国家科学技术奖励委员会提出各奖种获奖者和奖励等级的建议。  
监督委员会根据相关办法对提名、评审和异议处理工作全程进行监督，并向国家科学技术奖励委员会报告监督情况。  
国家科学技术奖励委员会根据评审委员会的建议和监督委员会的报告，作出各奖种获奖者和奖励等级的决议。

**Article 20** The Review Committee shall review the preliminary review recommendations in accordance with relevant measures and shall submit to the National Science and Technology Awards Committee recommendations on the winners and prize grades for each award category.  
The Supervision Committee shall supervise the entire process of nomination, review, and objection handling in accordance with relevant measures and shall report the supervision situation to the National Science and Technology Awards Committee.  
The National Science and Technology Awards Committee shall, based on the recommendations of the Review Committee and the report of the Supervision Committee, make resolutions on the winners and prize grades for each award category.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院科学技术行政部门对国家科学技术奖励委员会作出的各奖种获奖者和奖励等级的决议进行审核，报党中央、国务院批准。

**Article 21** The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall review the resolutions made by the National Science and Technology Awards Committee on the winners and prize grades for each award category and submit them to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家最高科学技术奖报请国家主席签署并颁发奖章、证书和奖金。  
国家自然科学奖、国家技术发明奖、国家科学技术进步奖颁发证书和奖金。  
中华人民共和国国际科学技术合作奖颁发奖章和证书。

**Article 22** The State Preeminent Science and Technology Award shall be submitted to the President of the People's Republic of China for signature, and medals, certificates, and prize money shall be conferred.  
For the State Natural Science Award, the State Technological Invention Award, and the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award, certificates and prize money shall be conferred.  
For the People's Republic of China International Science and Technology Cooperation Award, medals and certificates shall be conferred.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家科学技术奖提名和评审的办法、奖励总数、奖励结果等信息应当向社会公布，接受社会监督。  
涉及国家安全的保密项目，应当严格遵守国家保密法律法规的有关规定，加强项目内容的保密管理，在适当范围内公布。

**Article 23** Information such as the nomination and review measures, total number of awards, and award results for the State Science and Technology Awards shall be made public to society and subject to public oversight.  
For confidential projects involving national security, the relevant provisions of the State secrecy laws and regulations shall be strictly observed, confidentiality management of project content shall be strengthened, and disclosure shall be made within an appropriate scope.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家科学技术奖励工作实行科研诚信审核制度。国务院科学技术行政部门负责建立提名专家、学者、组织机构和评审委员、评审专家、候选者的科研诚信严重失信行为数据库。  
禁止任何个人、组织进行可能影响国家科学技术奖提名和评审公平、公正的活动。

**Article 24** The work of State science and technology awards shall implement a scientific research integrity review system. The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for establishing a database of serious acts of dishonesty in scientific research integrity for nominating experts, scholars, and organizations, as well as review committee members, review experts, and candidates.  
No individual or organization shall engage in activities that may affect the fairness and impartiality of the nomination and review of the State Science and Technology Awards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家最高科学技术奖的奖金数额由国务院科学技术行政部门会同财政部门提出，报党中央、国务院批准。  
国家自然科学奖、国家技术发明奖、国家科学技术进步奖的奖金数额由国务院科学技术行政部门会同财政部门规定。  
国家科学技术奖的奖励经费列入中央预算。

**Article 25** The amount of prize money for the State Preeminent Science and Technology Award shall be proposed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council jointly with the finance department and submitted to the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council for approval.  
The amounts of prize money for the State Natural Science Award, the State Technological Invention Award, and the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award shall be prescribed by the science and technology administrative department of the State Council jointly with the finance department.  
The funds for the State Science and Technology Awards shall be included in the central budget.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　宣传国家科学技术奖获奖者的突出贡献和创新精神，应当遵守法律法规的规定，做到安全、保密、适度、严谨。

**Article 26** The publicity of the outstanding contributions and innovative spirit of the winners of the State Science and Technology Awards shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations, and shall be carried out in a safe, confidential, moderate, and rigorous manner.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　禁止使用国家科学技术奖名义牟取不正当利益。

**Article 27** The use of the name of a State Science and Technology Award to seek illegitimate benefits shall be prohibited.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　候选者进行可能影响国家科学技术奖提名和评审公平、公正的活动的，由国务院科学技术行政部门给予通报批评，取消其参评资格，并由所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。  
其他个人或者组织进行可能影响国家科学技术奖提名和评审公平、公正的活动的，由国务院科学技术行政部门给予通报批评；相关候选者有责任的，取消其参评资格。

**Article 28** If a candidate engages in activities that may affect the fairness and impartiality of the nomination and review of the State Science and Technology Awards, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall give a notice of criticism, disqualify the candidate from participation, and the candidate's employing unit or the relevant department shall impose sanctions in accordance with law.  
If other individuals or organizations engage in activities that may affect the fairness and impartiality of the nomination and review of the State Science and Technology Awards, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall give a notice of criticism; if the relevant candidate is at fault, the candidate shall be disqualified from participation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　评审委员、评审专家违反国家科学技术奖评审工作纪律的，由国务院科学技术行政部门取消其评审委员、评审专家资格，并由所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 29** If a review committee member or a review expert violates the discipline of review work for the State Science and Technology Awards, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall disqualify the person from being a review committee member or review expert, and the person's employing unit or the relevant department shall impose sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　获奖者剽窃、侵占他人的发现、发明或者其他科学技术成果的，或者以其他不正当手段骗取国家科学技术奖的，由国务院科学技术行政部门报党中央、国务院批准后撤销奖励，追回奖章、证书和奖金，并由所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 30** If a winner plagiarizes or misappropriates another person's discoveries, inventions, or other scientific and technological achievements, or obtains a State Science and Technology Award by other illegitimate means, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council, revoke the award, recover the medal, certificate, and prize money, and the winner's employing unit or the relevant department shall impose sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　提名专家、学者、组织机构提供虚假数据、材料，协助他人骗取国家科学技术奖的，由国务院科学技术行政部门给予通报批评；情节严重的，暂停或者取消其提名资格，并由所在单位或者有关部门依法给予处分。

**Article 31** If a nominating expert, scholar, or organization provides false data or materials to assist others in fraudulently obtaining a State Science and Technology Award, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall give a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the nominating qualification shall be suspended or revoked, and the employing unit or the relevant department shall impose sanctions in accordance with law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定的，由有关部门依照相关法律、行政法规的规定予以查处。

**Article 32** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 27 of these Regulations shall be investigated and dealt with by the relevant department in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对违反本条例规定，有科研诚信严重失信行为的个人、组织，记入科研诚信严重失信行为数据库，并共享至全国信用信息共享平台，按照国家有关规定实施联合惩戒。

**Article 33** Individuals or organizations that violate the provisions of these Regulations and have serious acts of dishonesty in scientific research integrity shall be recorded in the database of serious acts of dishonesty in scientific research integrity, and the information shall be shared to the national credit information sharing platform, and joint disciplinary actions shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家科学技术奖的候选者、获奖者、评审委员、评审专家和提名专家、学者涉嫌违反其他法律、行政法规的，国务院科学技术行政部门应当通报有关部门依法予以处理。

**Article 34** Where a candidate, winner, review committee member, review expert, or nominating expert or scholar of a State Science and Technology Award is suspected of violating other laws or administrative regulations, the science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall notify the relevant department to handle the matter in accordance with law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　参与国家科学技术奖评审组织工作的人员在评审活动中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 35** If a person participating in the organization of the review of the State Science and Technology Awards abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in favoritism or fraud in the review activities, the person shall be given sanctions in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有关部门根据国家安全领域的特殊情况，可以设立部级科学技术奖；省、自治区、直辖市、计划单列市人民政府可以设立一项省级科学技术奖。具体办法由设奖部门或者地方人民政府制定，并报国务院科学技术行政部门及有关单位备案。  
设立省部级科学技术奖，应当按照精简原则，严格控制奖励数量，提高奖励质量，优化奖励程序。其他国家机关、群众团体，以及参照公务员法管理的事业单位，不得设立科学技术奖。

**Article 36** The relevant departments may, based on the special circumstances of the field of national security, establish ministerial-level science and technology awards; the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and cities with independent planning status may establish one provincial-level science and technology award. The specific measures shall be formulated by the awarding department or the local people's government and shall be reported to the science and technology administrative department of the State Council and relevant entities for recordation.  
The establishment of provincial or ministerial-level science and technology awards shall follow the principle of streamlining, strictly control the number of awards, improve the quality of awards, and optimize the award procedures. No other state organs, mass organizations, or institutions managed with reference to the Civil Servant Law shall establish science and technology awards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励社会力量设立科学技术奖。社会力量设立科学技术奖的，在奖励活动中不得收取任何费用。  
国务院科学技术行政部门应当对社会力量设立科学技术奖的有关活动进行指导服务和监督管理，并制定具体办法。

**Article 37** The State encourages social forces to establish science and technology awards. Where social forces establish science and technology awards, no fees shall be charged in the awarding activities.  
The science and technology administrative department of the State Council shall provide guidance, service, supervision, and management for the relevant activities of science and technology awards established by social forces, and shall formulate specific measures.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自2020年12月1日起施行。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2020.
