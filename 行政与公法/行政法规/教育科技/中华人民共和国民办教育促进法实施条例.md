# 中华人民共和国民办教育促进法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-04-07  
**生效日期**：2021-09-01

> （2004年3月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第399号公布　2021年4月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第741号修订） 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法》（以下简称民办教育促进法），制定本条例。 第二条　国家机构以外的社会组织或者个人可以利用非国家财政性经费举办各级各类民办学校；但是，不得举办实施军事、警察、政治等特殊性质教育的民办学校。 民办教育促进法和本条例所称国家财政性经费，是指财政拨款、依法取得并应当上缴国库或者财政专户的财政性资金。 第三条　各级人民政府应当依法支持和规范社会力量举办民办教育，保障民办学校依法办学、自主管理，鼓励、引导民办学校提高质量、办出特色，满足多样化教育需求。 对于举办民办学校表现突出或者为发展民办教育事业做出突出贡献的社会组织或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励和表彰。 第四条　民办学校应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持社会主义办学方向，坚持教育公益性，对受教育者加强社会主义核心价值观教育，落实立德树人根本任务。 民办学校中的中国共产党基层组织贯彻党的方针政策，依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定参与学校重大决策并实施监督。 第二章　民办学校的设立 第五条　国家机构以外的社会组织或者个人可以单独或者联合举办民办学校。联合举办民办学校的，应当签订联合办学协议，明确合作方式、各方权利义务和争议解决方式等。 国家鼓励以捐资、设立基金会等方式依法举办民办学校。以捐资等方式举办民办学校，无举办者的，其办学过程中的举办者权责由发起人履行。 在中国境内设立的外商投资企业以及外方为实际控制人的社会组织不得举办、参与举办或者实际控制实施义务教育的民办学校；举办其他类型民办学校的，应当符合国家有关外商投资的规定。 第六条　举办民办学校的社会组织或者个人应当有良好的信用状况。举办民办学校可以用货币出资，也可以用实物、建设用地使用权、知识产权等可以用货币估价并可以依法转让的非货币财产作价出资；但是，法律、行政法规规定不得作为出资的财产除外。 第七条　实施义务教育的公办学校不得举办或者参与举办民办学校，也不得转为民办学校。其他公办学校不得举办或者参与举办营利性民办学校。但是，实施职业教育的公办学校可以吸引企业的资本、技术、管理等要素，举办或者参与举办实施职业教育的营利性民办学校。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国民办教育促进法》（以下简称民办教育促进法），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Promotion of Private Education (hereinafter referred to as the Private Education Promotion Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家机构以外的社会组织或者个人可以利用非国家财政性经费举办各级各类民办学校；但是，不得举办实施军事、警察、政治等特殊性质教育的民办学校。  
民办教育促进法和本条例所称国家财政性经费，是指财政拨款、依法取得并应当上缴国库或者财政专户的财政性资金。

**Article 2** Social organizations or individuals other than state organs may use non-state financial funds to establish private schools at various levels and of various types; however, they shall not establish private schools that provide education of a special nature such as military, police, or political education.  
The term 'state financial funds' as used in the Private Education Promotion Law and these Regulations means financial allocations, and financial funds legally obtained that shall be turned over to the state treasury or to special financial accounts.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级人民政府应当依法支持和规范社会力量举办民办教育，保障民办学校依法办学、自主管理，鼓励、引导民办学校提高质量、办出特色，满足多样化教育需求。  
对于举办民办学校表现突出或者为发展民办教育事业做出突出贡献的社会组织或者个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励和表彰。

**Article 3** People's governments at all levels shall, according to law, support and regulate the establishment of private education by non-governmental forces, ensure that private schools operate according to law and manage independently, and encourage and guide private schools to improve quality, develop distinctive features, and meet diversified educational needs.  
Social organizations or individuals that have demonstrated outstanding performance in establishing private schools or have made outstanding contributions to the development of private education shall be given rewards and commendations in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民办学校应当坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持社会主义办学方向，坚持教育公益性，对受教育者加强社会主义核心价值观教育，落实立德树人根本任务。  
民办学校中的中国共产党基层组织贯彻党的方针政策，依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定参与学校重大决策并实施监督。

**Article 4** Private schools shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, uphold the socialist orientation in running schools, uphold the public welfare nature of education, strengthen education on the core socialist values among educatees, and implement the fundamental task of fostering virtue through education.  
The primary-level organizations of the Communist Party of China in private schools shall implement the Party's principles and policies, participate in major decision-making of the school and exercise supervision in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and relevant state provisions.

### 第二章　民办学校的设立

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家机构以外的社会组织或者个人可以单独或者联合举办民办学校。联合举办民办学校的，应当签订联合办学协议，明确合作方式、各方权利义务和争议解决方式等。  
国家鼓励以捐资、设立基金会等方式依法举办民办学校。以捐资等方式举办民办学校，无举办者的，其办学过程中的举办者权责由发起人履行。  
在中国境内设立的外商投资企业以及外方为实际控制人的社会组织不得举办、参与举办或者实际控制实施义务教育的民办学校；举办其他类型民办学校的，应当符合国家有关外商投资的规定。

**Article 5** Social organizations or individuals other than state organs may establish private schools individually or jointly. Where private schools are jointly established, a joint school-running agreement shall be signed, specifying the cooperation mode, the rights and obligations of each party, and the dispute resolution method.  
The state encourages the establishment of private schools according to law by means of donations, establishment of foundations, etc. Where a private school is established by means of donations, etc. without a sponsor, the sponsors' rights and obligations during the school-running process shall be fulfilled by the initiator.  
Foreign-invested enterprises established within the territory of China and social organizations with foreign parties as actual controllers shall not establish, participate in establishing, or actually control private schools that provide compulsory education; where they establish other types of private schools, they shall comply with the state provisions on foreign investment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　举办民办学校的社会组织或者个人应当有良好的信用状况。举办民办学校可以用货币出资，也可以用实物、建设用地使用权、知识产权等可以用货币估价并可以依法转让的非货币财产作价出资；但是，法律、行政法规规定不得作为出资的财产除外。

**Article 6** Social organizations or individuals establishing private schools shall have a good credit standing. When establishing a private school, contributions may be made in currency, or in non-monetary property that can be valued in currency and transferred according to law, such as in-kind property, the right to use construction land, and intellectual property; however, property that is not allowed to be used as capital contribution under laws and administrative regulations is excluded.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实施义务教育的公办学校不得举办或者参与举办民办学校，也不得转为民办学校。其他公办学校不得举办或者参与举办营利性民办学校。但是，实施职业教育的公办学校可以吸引企业的资本、技术、管理等要素，举办或者参与举办实施职业教育的营利性民办学校。  
公办学校举办或者参与举办民办学校，不得利用国家财政性经费，不得影响公办学校教学活动，不得仅以品牌输出方式参与办学，并应当经其主管部门批准。公办学校举办或者参与举办非营利性民办学校，不得以管理费等方式取得或者变相取得办学收益。  
公办学校举办或者参与举办的民办学校应当具有独立的法人资格，具有与公办学校相分离的校园、基本教育教学设施和独立的专任教师队伍，按照国家统一的会计制度独立进行会计核算，独立招生，独立颁发学业证书。  
举办或者参与举办民办学校的公办学校依法享有举办者权益，依法履行国有资产管理义务。

**Article 7** Public schools providing compulsory education shall not establish or participate in establishing private schools, nor shall they be converted into private schools. Other public schools shall not establish or participate in establishing for-profit private schools. However, public schools providing vocational education may attract capital, technology, management and other factors from enterprises to establish or participate in establishing for-profit private schools providing vocational education.  
Where a public school establishes or participates in establishing a private school, it shall not use state financial funds, shall not affect the teaching activities of the public school, shall not participate in running the school solely by means of brand export, and shall obtain approval from its competent department. Where a public school establishes or participates in establishing a non-profit private school, it shall not obtain or indirectly obtain school-running proceeds by means of management fees or other means.  
A private school established or participated in by a public school shall have independent legal person status, have a campus, basic education and teaching facilities separate from those of the public school, and have an independent full-time teaching staff, conduct independent accounting in accordance with the unified accounting system of the state, recruit students independently, and issue academic certificates independently.  
A public school that establishes or participates in establishing a private school shall enjoy the rights and interests of the sponsor according to law, and fulfill the obligations of state-owned asset management according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　地方人民政府不得利用国有企业、公办教育资源举办或者参与举办实施义务教育的民办学校。  
以国有资产参与举办民办学校的，应当根据国家有关国有资产监督管理的规定，聘请具有评估资格的中介机构依法进行评估，根据评估结果合理确定出资额，并报对该国有资产负有监管职责的机构备案。

**Article 8** Local people's governments shall not use state-owned enterprises or public education resources to establish or participate in establishing private schools providing compulsory education.  
Where state-owned assets are used to participate in establishing a private school, an intermediary institution with appraisal qualifications shall be engaged according to law to conduct an appraisal in accordance with the state provisions on the supervision and management of state-owned assets, the amount of capital contribution shall be reasonably determined based on the appraisal results, and the matter shall be reported to the institution responsible for supervision of such state-owned assets for the record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励企业以独资、合资、合作等方式依法举办或者参与举办实施职业教育的民办学校。

**Article 9** The state encourages enterprises to establish or participate in establishing private schools providing vocational education according to law by means of sole proprietorship, joint venture, cooperation, etc.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　举办民办学校，应当按时、足额履行出资义务。民办学校存续期间，举办者不得抽逃出资，不得挪用办学经费。  
举办者可以依法募集资金举办营利性民办学校，所募集资金应当主要用于办学，不得擅自改变用途，并按规定履行信息披露义务。民办学校及其举办者不得以赞助费等名目向学生、学生家长收取或者变相收取与入学关联的费用。

**Article 10** When establishing a private school, the sponsor shall fulfill the obligation of capital contribution on time and in full. During the existence of a private school, the sponsor shall not withdraw capital contributions, and shall not misappropriate school-running funds.  
The sponsor may raise funds according to law to establish a for-profit private school; the funds raised shall be mainly used for school-running, and shall not be used for other purposes without authorization; and the sponsor shall perform the obligation of information disclosure as required. Private schools and their sponsors shall not collect or indirectly collect fees related to enrollment from students or their parents under the name of sponsorship fees or other similar names.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　举办者依法制定学校章程，负责推选民办学校首届理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构的组成人员。  
举办者可以依据法律、法规和学校章程规定的程序和要求参加或者委派代表参加理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构，并依据学校章程规定的权限行使相应的决策权、管理权。

**Article 11** The sponsor shall formulate the school charter according to law and be responsible for electing the members of the first council, board of directors, or other forms of decision-making body of the private school.  
The sponsor may participate in or appoint representatives to participate in the council, board of directors, or other forms of decision-making body in accordance with the procedures and requirements prescribed by laws, regulations and the school charter, and exercise corresponding decision-making and management powers within the scope of authority prescribed by the school charter.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民办学校举办者变更的，应当签订变更协议，但不得涉及学校的法人财产，也不得影响学校发展，不得损害师生权益；现有民办学校的举办者变更的，可以根据其依法享有的合法权益与继任举办者协议约定变更收益。  
民办学校的举办者不再具备法定条件的，应当在6个月内向审批机关提出变更；逾期不变更的，由审批机关责令变更。  
举办者为法人的，其控股股东和实际控制人应当符合法律、行政法规规定的举办民办学校的条件，控股股东和实际控制人变更的，应当报主管部门备案并公示。  
举办者变更，符合法定条件的，审批机关应当在规定的期限内予以办理。

**Article 12** Where the sponsor of a private school changes, a change agreement shall be signed, but it shall not involve the legal person property of the school, nor shall it affect the development of the school or harm the rights and interests of teachers and students; where the sponsor of an existing private school changes, the change consideration may be agreed upon by agreement between the sponsor and the successor sponsor based on the legitimate rights and interests that the sponsor enjoys according to law.  
Where the sponsor of a private school no longer meets the statutory conditions, it shall submit a change application to the approving authority within 6 months; if it fails to make the change within the time limit, the approving authority shall order the change.  
Where the sponsor is a legal person, its controlling shareholder and actual controller shall meet the conditions for establishing a private school prescribed by laws and administrative regulations; where the controlling shareholder or actual controller changes, the matter shall be reported to the competent department for the record and publicized.  
Where a change of sponsor meets the statutory conditions, the approving authority shall handle the matter within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　同时举办或者实际控制多所民办学校的，举办者或者实际控制人应当具备与其所开展办学活动相适应的资金、人员、组织机构等条件与能力，并对所举办民办学校承担管理和监督职责。  
同时举办或者实际控制多所民办学校的举办者或者实际控制人向所举办或者实际控制的民办学校提供教材、课程、技术支持等服务以及组织教育教学活动，应当符合国家有关规定并建立相应的质量标准和保障机制。  
同时举办或者实际控制多所民办学校的，应当保障所举办或者实际控制的民办学校依法独立开展办学活动，存续期间所有资产由学校依法管理和使用；不得改变所举办或者实际控制的非营利性民办学校的性质，直接或者间接取得办学收益；也不得滥用市场支配地位，排除、限制竞争。  
任何社会组织和个人不得通过兼并收购、协议控制等方式控制实施义务教育的民办学校、实施学前教育的非营利性民办学校。

**Article 13** Where a person simultaneously establishes or actually controls multiple private schools, the sponsor or actual controller shall have the conditions and capabilities, such as funds, personnel, and organizational structure, commensurate with the school-running activities it carries out, and shall assume the duties of management and supervision over the private schools it establishes.  
Where a sponsor or actual controller who simultaneously establishes or actually controls multiple private schools provides services such as teaching materials, courses, and technical support, as well as organizes educational and teaching activities, to the private schools it establishes or actually controls, such provision and organization shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state and establish corresponding quality standards and guarantee mechanisms.  
Where a person simultaneously establishes or actually controls multiple private schools, it shall ensure that the private schools it establishes or actually controls independently carry out school-running activities according to law, and that all assets during the existence period are managed and used by the schools according to law; it shall not change the nature of the non-profit private schools it establishes or actually controls, and shall not directly or indirectly obtain school-running proceeds; nor shall it abuse its dominant market position to exclude or restrict competition.  
No social organization or individual shall control private schools providing compulsory education or non-profit private schools providing preschool education through means such as mergers and acquisitions or agreement control.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　实施国家认可的教育考试、职业资格考试和职业技能等级考试等考试的机构，举办或者参与举办与其所实施的考试相关的民办学校应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 14** An institution that conducts national recognized examinations such as education examinations, professional qualification examinations, and vocational skill level examinations, and establishes or participates in establishing a private school related to the examinations it conducts, shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　设立民办学校的审批权限，依照有关法律、法规的规定执行。  
地方人民政府及其有关部门应当依法履行实施义务教育的职责。设立实施义务教育的民办学校，应当符合当地义务教育发展规划。

**Article 15** The authority to approve the establishment of private schools shall be exercised in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.  
Local people's governments and their relevant departments shall fulfill their duties of providing compulsory education according to law. The establishment of a private school providing compulsory education shall comply with the local compulsory education development plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励民办学校利用互联网技术在线实施教育活动。  
利用互联网技术在线实施教育活动应当符合国家互联网管理有关法律、行政法规的规定。利用互联网技术在线实施教育活动的民办学校应当取得相应的办学许可。  
民办学校利用互联网技术在线实施教育活动，应当依法建立并落实互联网安全管理制度和安全保护技术措施，发现法律、行政法规禁止发布或者传输的信息的，应当立即停止传输，采取消除等处置措施，防止信息扩散，保存有关记录，并向有关主管部门报告。  
外籍人员利用互联网技术在线实施教育活动，应当遵守教育和外国人在华工作管理等有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 16** The state encourages private schools to use internet technology to conduct educational activities online.  
The use of internet technology to conduct educational activities online shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on internet management of the state. A private school that uses internet technology to conduct educational activities online shall obtain the corresponding school-running license.  
Where a private school uses internet technology to conduct educational activities online, it shall establish and implement internet security management systems and security protection technical measures according to law; if it discovers information the release or transmission of which is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations, it shall immediately stop the transmission, take measures such as elimination to prevent the spread of the information, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant competent departments.  
Foreign personnel who use internet technology to conduct educational activities online shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on education and the management of foreigners working in China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民办学校的举办者在获得筹设批准书之日起3年内完成筹设的，可以提出正式设立申请。  
民办学校在筹设期内不得招生。

**Article 17** Where the sponsor of a private school completes the preparatory establishment within 3 years from the date of obtaining the preparatory establishment approval document, it may submit an application for formal establishment.  
A private school shall not recruit students during the preparatory establishment period.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请正式设立实施学历教育的民办学校的，审批机关受理申请后，应当组织专家委员会评议，由专家委员会提出咨询意见。

**Article 18** Where an application is made for the formal establishment of a private school providing academic education, after accepting the application, the approving authority shall organize an expert committee to conduct an evaluation, and the expert committee shall provide advisory opinions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民办学校的章程应当规定下列主要事项：  
（一）学校的名称、住所、办学地址、法人属性；  
（二）举办者的权利义务，举办者变更、权益转让的办法；  
（三）办学宗旨、发展定位、层次、类型、规模、形式等；  
（四）学校开办资金、注册资本，资产的来源、性质等；  
（五）理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构和监督机构的产生方法、人员构成、任期、议事规则等；  
（六）学校党组织负责人或者代表进入学校决策机构和监督机构的程序；  
（七）学校的法定代表人；  
（八）学校自行终止的事由，剩余资产处置的办法与程序；  
（九）章程修改程序。  
民办学校应当将章程向社会公示，修订章程应当事先公告，征求利益相关方意见。完成修订后，报主管部门备案或者核准。

**Article 19** The charter of a private school shall provide for the following main matters:  
(1) the name, domicile, school-running address, and legal person nature of the school;  
(2) the rights and obligations of the sponsor, and the methods for change of sponsor and transfer of rights and interests;  
(3) the school-running purpose, development orientation, level, type, scale, form, etc.;  
(4) the start-up funds, registered capital, and the source and nature of assets of the school;  
(5) the methods of formation, composition, term of office, and rules of procedure of the council, board of directors, or other forms of decision-making body and supervisory body;  
(6) the procedures for the leading person or representative of the school's Party organization to enter the decision-making body and supervisory body of the school;  
(7) the legal representative of the school;  
(8) the grounds for voluntary termination of the school, and the methods and procedures for disposal of remaining assets;  
(9) the procedures for amending the charter.  
A private school shall make its charter public to the society; any amendment to the charter shall be announced in advance to solicit opinions from interested parties. After the amendment is completed, it shall be reported to the competent department for the record or approval.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民办学校只能使用一个名称。  
民办学校的名称应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得损害社会公共利益，不得含有可能引发歧义的文字或者含有可能误导公众的其他法人名称。营利性民办学校可以在学校牌匾、成绩单、毕业证书、结业证书、学位证书及相关证明、招生广告和简章上使用经审批机关批准的法人简称。

**Article 20** A private school may only use one name.  
The name of a private school shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, shall not harm the public interest, and shall not contain words that may cause ambiguity or contain other legal person names that may mislead the public. A for-profit private school may use the abbreviated name of the legal person approved by the approving authority on its school plaque, transcripts, graduation certificates, completion certificates, degree certificates and related certificates, enrollment advertisements, and brochures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民办学校开办资金、注册资本应当与学校类型、层次、办学规模相适应。民办学校正式设立时，开办资金、注册资本应当缴足。

**Article 21** The start-up funds and registered capital of a private school shall be commensurate with the school type, level, and scale. When a private school is formally established, the start-up funds and registered capital shall be fully paid.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对批准正式设立的民办学校，审批机关应当颁发办学许可证，并向社会公告。  
办学许可的期限应当与民办学校的办学层次和类型相适应。民办学校在许可期限内无违法违规行为的，有效期届满可以自动延续、换领新证。  
民办学校办学许可证的管理办法由国务院教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门依据职责分工分别制定。

**Article 22** For a private school approved for formal establishment, the approving authority shall issue a school-running license and make a public announcement.  
The term of the school-running license shall be commensurate with the level and type of the private school. If a private school has no illegal or non-compliant conduct during the license term, the license may be automatically renewed and a new certificate obtained upon expiry.  
The measures for the administration of school-running licenses for private schools shall be separately formulated by the education administrative department and the human resources and social security administrative department of the State Council in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民办学校增设校区应当向审批机关申请地址变更；设立分校应当向分校所在地审批机关单独申请办学许可，并报原审批机关备案。

**Article 23** Where a private school adds a campus, it shall apply to the approving authority for a change of address; where it establishes a branch school, it shall separately apply for a school-running license with the approving authority at the location of the branch school, and report to the original approving authority for the record.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民办学校依照有关法律、行政法规的规定申请法人登记，登记机关应当依法予以办理。

**Article 24** Where a private school applies for legal person registration in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, the registration authority shall handle the matter according to law.

### 第三章　民办学校的组织与活动

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民办学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构的负责人应当具有中华人民共和国国籍，具有政治权利和完全民事行为能力，在中国境内定居，品行良好，无故意犯罪记录或者教育领域不良从业记录。  
民办学校法定代表人应当由民办学校决策机构负责人或者校长担任。

**Article 25** The responsible person of the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body of a private school shall be a national of the People's Republic of China, possess political rights and full civil capacity, reside within the territory of China, have good conduct, and have no record of intentional crime or any adverse practice record in the education field.  
The legal representative of a private school shall be the responsible person of the decision-making body of the private school or the principal.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民办学校的理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构应当由举办者或者其代表、校长、党组织负责人、教职工代表等共同组成。鼓励民办学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构吸收社会公众代表，根据需要设独立理事或者独立董事。实施义务教育的民办学校理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构组成人员应当具有中华人民共和国国籍，且应当有审批机关委派的代表。  
民办学校的理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构每年至少召开2次会议。经1／3以上组成人员提议，可以召开理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构临时会议。讨论下列重大事项，应当经2／3以上组成人员同意方可通过：  
（一）变更举办者；  
（二）聘任、解聘校长；  
（三）修改学校章程；  
（四）制定发展规划；  
（五）审核预算、决算；  
（六）决定学校的分立、合并、终止；  
（七）学校章程规定的其他重大事项。

**Article 26** The council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body of a private school shall be composed of the sponsor or its representatives, the principal, the responsible person of the Party organization, representatives of the teaching and administrative staff, etc. Private schools are encouraged to include representatives of the public in the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body, and to appoint independent directors or independent trustees as needed. The members of the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body of a private school providing compulsory education shall be nationals of the People's Republic of China, and there shall be representatives delegated by the approving authority.  
The council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body of a private school shall hold at least 2 meetings per year. Upon the proposal of more than 1/3 of its members, an interim meeting of the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body may be convened. The following major matters shall require the consent of more than 2/3 of the members for adoption:  
(1) change of sponsor;  
(2) appointment or removal of the principal;  
(3) amendment of the school charter;  
(4) formulation of development plans;  
(5) review of budgets and final accounts;  
(6) decision on the division, merger, or termination of the school;  
(7) other major matters specified in the school charter.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民办学校应当设立监督机构。监督机构应当有党的基层组织代表，且教职工代表不少于1／3。教职工人数少于20人的民办学校可以只设1至2名监事。  
监督机构依据国家有关规定和学校章程对学校办学行为进行监督。监督机构负责人或者监事应当列席学校决策机构会议。  
理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构组成人员及其近亲属不得兼任、担任监督机构组成人员或者监事。

**Article 27** A private school shall establish a supervisory body. The supervisory body shall include representatives of the primary-level Party organization, and the representatives of the teaching and administrative staff shall account for not less than 1/3. A private school with fewer than 20 teaching and administrative staff members may have only 1 or 2 supervisors.  
The supervisory body shall supervise the school-running conduct of the school in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state and the school charter. The responsible person of the supervisory body or the supervisor shall attend the meetings of the school's decision-making body as non-voting participants.  
Members of the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body and their close relatives shall not concurrently serve as or be appointed as members of the supervisory body or supervisors.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民办学校校长依法独立行使教育教学和行政管理职权。  
民办学校内部组织机构的设置方案由校长提出，报理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构批准。

**Article 28** The principal of a private school shall independently exercise the powers of education, teaching, and administrative management according to law.  
The plan for the establishment of the internal organizational structure of a private school shall be proposed by the principal and submitted to the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body for approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民办学校依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，自主开展教育教学活动；使用境外教材的，应当符合国家有关规定。  
实施高等教育和中等职业技术学历教育的民办学校，可以按照办学宗旨和培养目标自主设置专业、开设课程、选用教材。  
实施普通高中教育、义务教育的民办学校可以基于国家课程标准自主开设有特色的课程，实施教育教学创新，自主设置的课程应当报主管教育行政部门备案。实施义务教育的民办学校不得使用境外教材。  
实施学前教育的民办学校开展保育和教育活动，应当遵循儿童身心发展规律，设置、开发以游戏、活动为主要形式的课程。  
实施以职业技能为主的职业资格培训、职业技能培训的民办学校可以按照与培训专业（职业、工种）相对应的国家职业标准及相关职业培训要求开展培训活动，不得教唆、组织学员规避监管，以不正当手段获取职业资格证书、成绩证明等。

**Article 29** A private school shall independently carry out education and teaching activities in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and relevant state provisions; where it uses foreign textbooks, it shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state.  
Private schools providing higher education or secondary vocational and technical academic education may independently set up specialties, offer courses, and select teaching materials in accordance with their school-running purposes and training objectives.  
Private schools providing general senior high school education or compulsory education may independently set up distinctive courses based on the national curriculum standards and carry out education and teaching innovations; courses independently set up shall be reported to the competent education administrative department for the record. Private schools providing compulsory education shall not use foreign textbooks.  
Private schools providing preschool education shall, when carrying out childcare and educational activities, follow the laws of children's physical and mental development, and set up or develop courses with games and activities as the main forms.  
Private schools providing vocational qualification training or vocational skill training mainly focused on vocational skills may carry out training activities in accordance with the national occupational standards corresponding to the training specialties (occupations, types of work) and relevant vocational training requirements, and shall not instigate or organize trainees to circumvent supervision or obtain vocational qualification certificates, grade certificates, etc. by improper means.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民办学校应当按照招生简章或者招生广告的承诺，开设相应课程，开展教育教学活动，保证教育教学质量。  
民办学校应当提供符合标准的校舍和教育教学设施设备。

**Article 30** A private school shall, in accordance with the commitments in its enrollment brochure or enrollment advertisement, offer corresponding courses, carry out education and teaching activities, and ensure the quality of education and teaching.  
A private school shall provide school buildings and education and teaching facilities and equipment that meet the standards.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　实施学前教育、学历教育的民办学校享有与同级同类公办学校同等的招生权，可以在审批机关核定的办学规模内，自主确定招生的标准和方式，与公办学校同期招生。  
实施义务教育的民办学校应当在审批机关管辖的区域内招生，纳入审批机关所在地统一管理。实施普通高中教育的民办学校应当主要在学校所在设区的市范围内招生，符合省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门有关规定的可以跨区域招生。招收接受高等学历教育学生的应当遵守国家有关规定。  
县级以上地方人民政府教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门应当为外地的民办学校在本地招生提供平等待遇，不得设置跨区域招生障碍实行地区封锁。  
民办学校招收学生应当遵守招生规则，维护招生秩序，公开公平公正录取学生。实施义务教育的民办学校不得组织或者变相组织学科知识类入学考试，不得提前招生。  
民办学校招收境外学生，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 31** Private schools providing preschool education or academic education shall enjoy the same enrollment rights as public schools of the same level and type, and may independently determine the standards and methods of enrollment within the school-running scale approved by the approving authority, and enroll students at the same time as public schools.  
Private schools providing compulsory education shall enroll students within the area under the jurisdiction of the approving authority, and shall be included in the unified management of the locality where the approving authority is located. Private schools providing general senior high school education shall mainly enroll students within the scope of the city divided into districts where the school is located; those meeting the relevant provisions of the education administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may enroll students across regions. Those enrolling students receiving higher academic education shall comply with the relevant provisions of the state.  
The education administrative department and the human resources and social security administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide equal treatment to private schools from other regions in enrolling students locally, and shall not set up cross-regional enrollment barriers to implement regional blockades.  
When enrolling students, a private school shall comply with enrollment rules, maintain enrollment order, and admit students in an open, fair, and just manner. Private schools providing compulsory education shall not organize or indirectly organize subject-based knowledge entrance examinations, and shall not enroll students in advance.  
The enrollment of foreign students by private schools shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　实施高等学历教育的民办学校符合学位授予条件的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定经审批同意后，可以获得相应的学位授予资格。

**Article 32** A private school providing higher academic education that meets the conditions for conferring academic degrees may, upon examination and approval in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, obtain the corresponding qualification for conferring academic degrees.

### 第四章　教师与受教育者

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　民办学校聘任的教师或者教学人员应当具备相应的教师资格或者其他相应的专业资格、资质。  
民办学校应当有一定数量的专任教师；其中，实施学前教育、学历教育的民办学校应当按照国家有关规定配备专任教师。  
鼓励民办学校创新教师聘任方式，利用信息技术等手段提高教学效率和水平。

**Article 33** Teachers or teaching personnel employed by a private school shall possess the corresponding teacher qualifications or other corresponding professional qualifications or credentials.  
A private school shall have a certain number of full-time teachers; among them, private schools providing preschool education or academic education shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, be equipped with full-time teachers.  
Private schools are encouraged to innovate the methods of teacher employment and to use information technology and other means to improve teaching efficiency and quality.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　民办学校自主招聘教师和其他工作人员，并应当与所招聘人员依法签订劳动或者聘用合同，明确双方的权利义务等。  
民办学校聘任专任教师，在合同中除依法约定必备条款外，还应当对教师岗位及其职责要求、师德和业务考核办法、福利待遇、培训和继续教育等事项作出约定。  
公办学校教师未经所在学校同意不得在民办学校兼职。  
民办学校聘任外籍人员，按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 34** A private school shall independently recruit teachers and other staff, and shall sign labor or employment contracts with the recruited personnel according to law, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
Where a private school employs full-time teachers, in addition to the necessary terms agreed upon according to law, the contract shall also contain provisions on matters such as the position and job requirements of the teacher, the methods for assessing professional ethics and professional competence, welfare benefits, training and continuing education.  
Teachers from public schools shall not hold a concurrent post in a private school without the consent of their original school.  
The employment of foreign personnel by private schools shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　民办学校应当建立教师培训制度，为受聘教师接受相应的思想政治培训和业务培训提供条件。

**Article 35** A private school shall establish a teacher training system to provide conditions for employed teachers to receive corresponding ideological and political training and professional training.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　民办学校应当依法保障教职工待遇，按照学校登记的法人类型，按时足额支付工资，足额缴纳社会保险费和住房公积金。国家鼓励民办学校按照有关规定为教职工建立职业年金或者企业年金等补充养老保险。  
实施学前教育、学历教育的民办学校应当从学费收入中提取一定比例建立专项资金或者基金，由学校管理，用于教职工职业激励或者增加待遇保障。

**Article 36** A private school shall ensure the treatment of teaching and administrative staff according to law, pay wages on time and in full, and fully pay social insurance premiums and housing provident funds in accordance with the type of legal person under which the school is registered. The state encourages private schools to establish supplementary pension insurance such as occupational annuities or enterprise annuities for teaching and administrative staff in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Private schools providing preschool education or academic education shall set aside a certain proportion of tuition income to establish a special fund or foundation, which shall be managed by the school and used for professional incentives or enhanced treatment guarantees for teaching and administrative staff.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　教育行政部门应当会同有关部门建立民办幼儿园、中小学专任教师劳动、聘用合同备案制度，建立统一档案，记录教师的教龄、工龄，在培训、考核、专业技术职务评聘、表彰奖励、权利保护等方面，统筹规划、统一管理，与公办幼儿园、中小学聘任的教师平等对待。  
民办职业学校、高等学校按照国家有关规定自主开展教师专业技术职务评聘。  
教育行政部门应当会同有关部门完善管理制度，保证教师在公办学校和民办学校之间的合理流动；指导和监督民办学校建立健全教职工代表大会制度。

**Article 37** The education administrative department shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments, establish a filing system for labor and employment contracts of full-time teachers in private kindergartens, primary and secondary schools, establish a unified archive, record teachers' teaching years and length of service, and in terms of training, assessment, professional and technical title evaluation and employment, commendations and rewards, rights protection, etc., carry out overall planning and unified management, and treat them equally with teachers employed in public kindergartens, primary and secondary schools.  
Private vocational schools and higher education institutions shall independently carry out the evaluation and employment of teachers' professional and technical titles in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
The education administrative department shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments, improve the management system to ensure the reasonable flow of teachers between public and private schools; and guide and supervise private schools to establish and improve the system of the congress of teaching and administrative staff.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　实施学历教育的民办学校应当依法建立学籍和教学管理制度，并报主管部门备案。

**Article 38** A private school providing academic education shall establish a student status and teaching management system according to law, and report it to the competent department for the record.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　民办学校及其教师、职员、受教育者申请政府设立的有关科研项目、课题等，享有与同级同类公办学校及其教师、职员、受教育者同等的权利。相关项目管理部门应当按规定及时足额拨付科研项目、课题资金。  
各级人民政府应当保障民办学校的受教育者在升学、就业、社会优待、参加先进评选，以及获得助学贷款、奖助学金等国家资助等方面，享有与同级同类公办学校的受教育者同等的权利。  
实施学历教育的民办学校应当建立学生资助、奖励制度，并按照不低于当地同级同类公办学校的标准，从学费收入中提取相应资金用于资助、奖励学生。

**Article 39** Private schools and their teachers, staff, and educatees shall enjoy the same rights as public schools of the same level and type and their teachers, staff, and educatees when applying for relevant scientific research projects and topics established by the government. The relevant project management departments shall, in accordance with the provisions, allocate funds for scientific research projects and topics in a timely and full amount.  
People's governments at all levels shall ensure that educatees of private schools enjoy the same rights as educatees of public schools of the same level and type in terms of further studies, employment, social preferential treatment, participation in advanced selection, and obtaining national financial aid such as student loans, grants, and scholarships.  
Private schools providing academic education shall establish a system of student financial aid and rewards, and set aside corresponding funds from tuition income to be used for student financial aid and rewards at a standard not lower than that of local public schools of the same level and type.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门和其他有关部门，组织有关的评奖评优、文艺体育活动和课题、项目招标，应当为民办学校及其教师、职员、受教育者提供同等的机会。

**Article 40** The education administrative department, the human resources and social security administrative department, and other relevant departments shall, when organizing relevant award evaluations, cultural, artistic, and sports activities, and bidding for topics and projects, provide equal opportunities to private schools and their teachers, staff, and educatees.

### 第五章　民办学校的资产与财务管理

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　民办学校应当依照《中华人民共和国会计法》和国家统一的会计制度进行会计核算，编制财务会计报告。

**Article 41** A private school shall conduct accounting and prepare financial accounting reports in accordance with the Accounting Law of the People's Republic of China and the unified accounting system of the state.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　民办学校应当建立办学成本核算制度，基于办学成本和市场需求等因素，遵循公平、合法和诚实信用原则，考虑经济效益与社会效益，合理确定收费项目和标准。对公办学校参与举办、使用国有资产或者接受政府生均经费补助的非营利性民办学校，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以对其收费制定最高限价。

**Article 42** A private school shall establish a school-running cost accounting system, and based on factors such as school-running costs and market demand, follow the principles of fairness, legality, and good faith, consider economic benefits and social benefits, and reasonably determine the items and standards of fees. For non-profit private schools that are established with the participation of public schools, use state-owned assets, or receive government per-student funding subsidies, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may set a maximum price for their fees.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民办学校资产中的国有资产的监督、管理，按照国家有关规定执行。  
民办学校依法接受的捐赠财产的使用和管理，依照有关法律、行政法规执行。

**Article 43** The supervision and management of state-owned assets in the assets of a private school shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.  
The use and management of donated property accepted by a private school according to law shall be handled in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　非营利性民办学校收取费用、开展活动的资金往来，应当使用在有关主管部门备案的账户。有关主管部门应当对该账户实施监督。  
营利性民办学校收入应当全部纳入学校开设的银行结算账户，办学结余分配应当在年度财务结算后进行。

**Article 44** Non-profit private schools shall use accounts that have been filed with the relevant competent department for the collection of fees and the flow of funds for activities. The relevant competent department shall supervise such accounts.  
All income of a for-profit private school shall be included in the bank settlement account opened by the school, and the distribution of school-running surplus shall be carried out after the annual financial settlement.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　实施义务教育的民办学校不得与利益关联方进行交易。其他民办学校与利益关联方进行交易的，应当遵循公开、公平、公允的原则，合理定价、规范决策，不得损害国家利益、学校利益和师生权益。  
民办学校应当建立利益关联方交易的信息披露制度。教育、人力资源社会保障以及财政等有关部门应当加强对非营利性民办学校与利益关联方签订协议的监管，并按年度对关联交易进行审查。  
前款所称利益关联方是指民办学校的举办者、实际控制人、校长、理事、董事、监事、财务负责人等以及与上述组织或者个人之间存在互相控制和影响关系、可能导致民办学校利益被转移的组织或者个人。

**Article 45** A private school providing compulsory education shall not conduct transactions with interested parties. Where other private schools conduct transactions with interested parties, they shall follow the principles of openness, fairness, and fair dealing, reasonably price and standardize decision-making, and shall not harm the interests of the state, the school, or the rights and interests of teachers and students.  
A private school shall establish a system for the disclosure of information on transactions with interested parties. The education, human resources and social security, and finance departments and other relevant departments shall strengthen the supervision of agreements signed between non-profit private schools and interested parties, and conduct annual reviews of related-party transactions.  
The term 'interested parties' in the preceding paragraph refers to the sponsor, actual controller, principal, trustee, director, supervisor, person in charge of finance, etc. of a private school, as well as organizations or individuals that have a relationship of mutual control and influence with the aforementioned organizations or individuals and may cause the transfer of interests of the private school.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在每个会计年度结束时，民办学校应当委托会计师事务所对年度财务报告进行审计。非营利性民办学校应当从经审计的年度非限定性净资产增加额中，营利性民办学校应当从经审计的年度净收益中，按不低于年度非限定性净资产增加额或者净收益的10％的比例提取发展基金，用于学校的发展。

**Article 46** At the end of each fiscal year, a private school shall entrust an accounting firm to audit the annual financial report. A non-profit private school shall set aside a development fund from the audited annual increase in unrestricted net assets, and a for-profit private school shall set aside a development fund from the audited annual net income, at a rate not lower than 10% of the annual increase in unrestricted net assets or net income, to be used for the development of the school.

### 第六章　管理与监督

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立民办教育工作联席会议制度。教育、人力资源社会保障、民政、市场监督管理等部门应当根据职责会同有关部门建立民办学校年度检查和年度报告制度，健全日常监管机制。  
教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门及有关部门应当建立民办学校信用档案和举办者、校长执业信用制度，对民办学校进行执法监督的情况和处罚、处理结果应当予以记录，由执法、监督人员签字后归档，并依法依规公开执法监督结果。相关信用档案和信用记录依法纳入全国信用信息共享平台、国家企业信用信息公示系统。

**Article 47** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a joint meeting system for private education work. The education, human resources and social security, civil affairs, and market supervision and management departments shall, in accordance with their functions and duties and in conjunction with the relevant departments, establish an annual inspection and annual reporting system for private schools, and improve daily supervision mechanisms.  
The education administrative department, the human resources and social security administrative department, and the relevant departments shall establish credit files for private schools and a credit system for the practice of sponsors and principals; the circumstances of law enforcement supervision and the results of penalties and treatment in respect of private schools shall be recorded, signed by the enforcement and supervision personnel and filed, and the results of law enforcement supervision shall be disclosed in accordance with laws and regulations. The relevant credit files and credit records shall be incorporated into the National Credit Information Sharing Platform and the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　审批机关应当及时公开民办学校举办者情况、办学条件等审批信息。  
教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门应当依据职责分工，定期组织或者委托第三方机构对民办学校的办学水平和教育质量进行评估，评估结果应当向社会公开。

**Article 48** The approving authority shall promptly disclose approval information such as the circumstances of the sponsor and the school-running conditions of private schools.  
The education administrative department and the human resources and social security administrative department shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, regularly organize or entrust a third-party institution to conduct evaluations of the school-running level and education quality of private schools, and the evaluation results shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　教育行政部门及有关部门应当制定实施学前教育、学历教育民办学校的信息公示清单，监督民办学校定期向社会公开办学条件、教育质量等有关信息。  
营利性民办学校应当通过全国信用信息共享平台、国家企业信用信息公示系统公示相关信息。  
有关部门应当支持和鼓励民办学校依法建立行业组织，研究制定相应的质量标准，建立认证体系，制定推广反映行业规律和特色要求的合同示范文本。

**Article 49** The education administrative department and the relevant departments shall formulate a list of information to be disclosed by private schools providing preschool education or academic education, and supervise private schools to regularly disclose to the public information on school-running conditions, education quality, etc.  
For-profit private schools shall disclose relevant information through the National Credit Information Sharing Platform and the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.  
The relevant departments shall support and encourage private schools to establish industry organizations according to law, research and formulate corresponding quality standards, establish certification systems, and formulate and promote model contract texts that reflect industry laws and special requirements.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　民办学校终止的，应当交回办学许可证，向登记机关办理注销登记，并向社会公告。  
民办学校自己要求终止的，应当提前6个月发布拟终止公告，依法依章程制定终止方案。  
民办学校无实际招生、办学行为的，办学许可证到期后自然废止，由审批机关予以公告。民办学校自行组织清算后，向登记机关办理注销登记。  
对于因资不抵债无法继续办学而被终止的民办学校，应当向人民法院申请破产清算。

**Article 50** Where a private school terminates, it shall return the school-running license, handle the cancellation of registration with the registration authority, and make a public announcement.  
Where a private school requests to terminate on its own, it shall issue a notice of intended termination 6 months in advance, and formulate a termination plan in accordance with the law and the school charter.  
Where a private school has no actual enrollment or school-running activities, the school-running license shall be automatically abolished upon expiry, and the approving authority shall make a public announcement. After the private school organizes liquidation on its own, it shall handle the cancellation of registration with the registration authority.  
For a private school that is terminated because it is unable to continue operating due to insolvency, it shall apply to the People's Court for bankruptcy liquidation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院教育督导机构及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责教育督导的机构应当对县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门落实支持和规范民办教育发展法定职责的情况进行督导、检查。  
县级以上人民政府负责教育督导的机构依法对民办学校进行督导并公布督导结果，建立民办中小学、幼儿园责任督学制度。

**Article 51** The education supervision institution of the State Council and the institutions responsible for education supervision of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall supervise and inspect the implementation by local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments of the statutory duties of supporting and regulating the development of private education.  
The institutions responsible for education supervision of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise private schools according to law and publish the supervision results, and establish a system of responsible supervisors for private primary and secondary schools and kindergartens.

### 第七章　支持与奖励

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当依法健全对民办学校的支持政策，优先扶持办学质量高、特色明显、社会效益显著的民办学校。  
县级以上地方人民政府可以参照同级同类公办学校生均经费等相关经费标准和支持政策，对非营利性民办学校给予适当补助。  
地方人民政府出租、转让闲置的国有资产应当优先扶持非营利性民办学校。

**Article 52** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall, according to law, improve the support policies for private schools, and give priority support to private schools with high school-running quality, distinctive features, and significant social benefits.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may, by reference to the per-student funding standards and support policies of public schools of the same level and type, provide appropriate subsidies to non-profit private schools.  
Where local people's governments lease or transfer idle state-owned assets, they shall give priority support to non-profit private schools.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民办学校可以依法以捐赠者的姓名、名称命名学校的校舍或者其他教育教学设施、生活设施。捐赠者对民办学校发展做出特殊贡献的，实施高等学历教育的民办学校经国务院教育行政部门按照国家规定的条件批准，其他民办学校经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门或者人力资源社会保障行政部门按照国家规定的条件批准，可以以捐赠者的姓名或者名称作为学校校名。

**Article 53** A private school may, according to law, name school buildings or other educational and teaching facilities or living facilities after the name or title of a donor. Where a donor has made special contributions to the development of a private school, a private school providing higher academic education may, upon approval by the education administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the conditions prescribed by the state, and other private schools may, upon approval by the education administrative department or the human resources and social security administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the conditions prescribed by the state, use the name or title of the donor as the school name.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　民办学校享受国家规定的税收优惠政策；其中，非营利性民办学校享受与公办学校同等的税收优惠政策。

**Article 54** Private schools shall enjoy the tax preferential policies prescribed by the state; among them, non-profit private schools shall enjoy the same tax preferential policies as public schools.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　地方人民政府在制定闲置校园综合利用方案时，应当考虑当地民办教育发展需求。  
新建、扩建非营利性民办学校，地方人民政府应当按照与公办学校同等原则，以划拨等方式给予用地优惠。  
实施学前教育、学历教育的民办学校使用土地，地方人民政府可以依法以协议、招标、拍卖等方式供应土地，也可以采取长期租赁、先租后让、租让结合的方式供应土地，土地出让价款和租金可以在规定期限内按合同约定分期缴纳。

**Article 55** When formulating comprehensive utilization plans for idle campuses, local people's governments shall consider the development needs of local private education.  
Where non-profit private schools are newly built or expanded, local people's governments shall, in accordance with the same principle as for public schools, provide land use preferences by means of allocation, etc.  
Where private schools providing preschool education or academic education use land, local people's governments may supply land according to law by means of agreement, tender, auction, etc., or may supply land by means of long-term lease, lease first then transfer, or a combination of lease and transfer; the land transfer price and rent may be paid in installments within the prescribed period in accordance with the contract.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　在西部地区、边远地区和少数民族地区举办的民办学校申请贷款用于学校自身发展的，享受国家相关的信贷优惠政策。

**Article 56** Private schools established in the western regions, remote areas, and ethnic minority areas that apply for loans for the school's own development shall enjoy the relevant credit preferential policies of the state.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上地方人民政府可以根据本行政区域的具体情况，设立民办教育发展专项资金，用于支持民办学校提高教育质量和办学水平、奖励举办者等。  
国家鼓励社会力量依法设立民办教育发展方面的基金会或者专项基金，用于支持民办教育发展。

**Article 57** Local people's governments at or above the county level may, according to the specific circumstances of their respective administrative regions, set up special funds for the development of private education to support private schools in improving the quality of education and the level of school-running, and to reward sponsors, etc.  
The state encourages non-governmental forces to establish foundations or special funds for the development of private education according to law to support the development of private education.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级人民政府根据本行政区域实施学前教育、义务教育或者其他公共教育服务的需要，可以与民办学校签订协议，以购买服务等方式，委托其承担相应教育任务。  
委托民办学校承担普惠性学前教育、义务教育或者其他公共教育任务的，应当根据当地相关教育阶段的委托协议，拨付相应的教育经费。

**Article 58** County people's governments may, based on the needs of providing preschool education, compulsory education, or other public education services in their respective administrative regions, sign agreements with private schools and entrust them to undertake corresponding educational tasks by means of purchasing services, etc.  
Where a private school is entrusted to undertake public-interest preschool education, compulsory education, or other public education tasks, the corresponding education funds shall be allocated in accordance with the entrustment agreement for the relevant education stage in the locality.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上地方人民政府可以采取政府补贴、以奖代补等方式鼓励、支持非营利性民办学校保障教师待遇。

**Article 59** Local people's governments at or above the county level may adopt methods such as government subsidies and awards in lieu of subsidies to encourage and support non-profit private schools in ensuring the treatment of teachers.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家鼓励、支持保险机构设立适合民办学校的保险产品，探索建立行业互助保险等机制，为民办学校重大事故处理、终止善后、教职工权益保障等事项提供风险保障。  
金融机构可以在风险可控前提下开发适合民办学校特点的金融产品。民办学校可以以未来经营收入、知识产权等进行融资。

**Article 60** The state encourages and supports insurance institutions to develop insurance products suitable for private schools, and to explore the establishment of mechanisms such as industry mutual insurance, so as to provide risk protection for matters such as the handling of major accidents, termination and aftermath, and protection of the rights and interests of teaching and administrative staff in private schools.  
Financial institutions may, under the premise of controllable risks, develop financial products that suit the characteristics of private schools. Private schools may obtain financing by means of future operating income, intellectual property, etc.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　除民办教育促进法和本条例规定的支持与奖励措施外，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府还可以根据实际情况，制定本地区促进民办教育发展的支持与奖励措施。  
各级人民政府及有关部门在对现有民办学校实施分类管理改革时，应当充分考虑有关历史和现实情况，保障受教育者、教职工和举办者的合法权益，确保民办学校分类管理改革平稳有序推进。

**Article 61** In addition to the support and reward measures provided for in the Private Education Promotion Law and these Regulations, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may also formulate local support and reward measures to promote the development of private education based on the actual circumstances.  
When implementing the reform of classified management for existing private schools, people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall give full consideration to the relevant historical and actual circumstances, protect the legitimate rights and interests of educatees, teaching and administrative staff, and sponsors, and ensure that the reform of classified management of private schools is promoted smoothly and orderly.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　民办学校举办者及实际控制人、决策机构或者监督机构组成人员有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门或者其他有关部门依据职责分工责令限期改正，有违法所得的，退还所收费用后没收违法所得；情节严重的，1至5年内不得新成为民办学校举办者或实际控制人、决策机构或者监督机构组成人员；情节特别严重、社会影响恶劣的，永久不得新成为民办学校举办者或实际控制人、决策机构或者监督机构组成人员；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚;构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）利用办学非法集资，或者收取与入学关联的费用的；  
（二）未按时、足额履行出资义务，或者抽逃出资、挪用办学经费的；  
（三）侵占学校法人财产或者非法从学校获取利益的；  
（四）与实施义务教育的民办学校进行关联交易，或者与其他民办学校进行关联交易损害国家利益、学校利益和师生权益的；  
（五）伪造、变造、买卖、出租、出借办学许可证的；  
（六）干扰学校办学秩序或者非法干预学校决策、管理的;  
（七）擅自变更学校名称、层次、类型和举办者的；  
（八）有其他危害学校稳定和安全、侵犯学校法人权利或者损害教职工、受教育者权益的行为的。

**Article 62** Where a sponsor or actual controller of a private school, or a member of the decision-making body or supervisory body, is in any of the following circumstances, the education administrative department, the human resources and social security administrative department, or other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order a correction within a specified time limit; if there is illegal income, the fees collected shall be refunded and the illegal income shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, such person shall not become a sponsor or actual controller of a private school, or a member of the decision-making body or supervisory body, for a period of 1 to 5 years; if the circumstances are particularly serious and the social impact is adverse, such person shall be permanently banned from becoming a sponsor or actual controller of a private school, or a member of the decision-making body or supervisory body; if the conduct constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) illegally raising funds by using school-running, or collecting fees related to enrollment;  
(2) failing to perform the obligation of capital contribution on time and in full, or withdrawing capital contributions or misappropriating school-running funds;  
(3) encroaching on the legal person property of the school or illegally obtaining benefits from the school;  
(4) conducting related-party transactions with a private school providing compulsory education, or conducting related-party transactions with other private schools that harm the interests of the state, the school, or the rights and interests of teachers and students;  
(5) forging, altering, trading, leasing, or lending the school-running license;  
(6) interfering with the school-running order of the school or illegally interfering with the decision-making or management of the school;  
(7) arbitrarily changing the name, level, type, or sponsor of the school;  
(8) having other acts that endanger the stability and security of the school, infringe upon the legal person rights of the school, or harm the rights and interests of teaching and administrative staff or educatees.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　民办学校有下列情形之一的，依照[民办教育促进法第六十二条](../../../行政与公法/教育科技/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法.md#art-62)规定给予处罚：  
（一）违背国家教育方针，偏离社会主义办学方向，或者未保障学校党组织履行职责的；  
（二）违反法律、行政法规和国家有关规定开展教育教学活动的；  
（三）理事会、董事会或者其他形式决策机构未依法履行职责的；  
（四）教学条件明显不能满足教学要求、教育教学质量低下，未及时采取措施的；  
（五）校舍、其他教育教学设施设备存在重大安全隐患，未及时采取措施的；  
（六）侵犯受教育者的合法权益，产生恶劣社会影响的；  
（七）违反国家规定聘任、解聘教师，或者未依法保障教职工待遇的；  
（八）违反规定招生，或者在招生过程中弄虚作假的；  
（九）超出办学许可范围，擅自改变办学地址或者设立分校的；  
（十）未依法履行公示办学条件和教育质量有关材料、财务状况等信息披露义务，或者公示的材料不真实的；  
（十一）未按照国家统一的会计制度进行会计核算、编制财务会计报告，财务、资产管理混乱，或者违反法律、法规增加收费项目、提高收费标准的；  
（十二）有其他管理混乱严重影响教育教学的行为的。  
法律、行政法规对前款规定情形的处罚另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 63** Where a private school is in any of the following circumstances, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 62 of the Private Education Promotion Law:  
(1) deviating from the national education policy, departing from the socialist orientation in school-running, or failing to ensure that the school's Party organization performs its duties;  
(2) carrying out education and teaching activities in violation of laws, administrative regulations and relevant state provisions;  
(3) the council, board of directors, or other form of decision-making body fails to perform its duties according to law;  
(4) the teaching conditions are clearly unable to meet the teaching requirements, and the quality of education and teaching is low, and no timely measures have been taken;  
(5) there are major safety hazards in school buildings or other education and teaching facilities and equipment, and no timely measures have been taken;  
(6) infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of educatees, resulting in an adverse social impact;  
(7) employing or dismissing teachers in violation of state provisions, or failing to ensure the treatment of teaching and administrative staff according to law;  
(8) enrolling students in violation of regulations, or engaging in fraud in the enrollment process;  
(9) exceeding the scope of the school-running license, arbitrarily changing the school-running address, or establishing a branch school;  
(10) failing to perform the obligation of information disclosure, such as disclosing materials on school-running conditions and education quality, financial status, etc. according to law, or the disclosed materials are untrue;  
(11) failing to conduct accounting and prepare financial accounting reports in accordance with the unified accounting system of the state, resulting in chaotic financial and asset management, or increasing fee items or raising fee standards in violation of laws or regulations;  
(12) having other acts of chaotic management that seriously affect education and teaching.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the penalties for the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　民办学校有[民办教育促进法第六十二条](../../../行政与公法/教育科技/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法.md#art-62)或者[本条例第六十三条](#art-63)规定的违法情形的，由县级以上人民政府教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门或者其他有关部门依据职责分工对学校决策机构负责人、校长及直接责任人予以警告；情节严重的，1至5年内不得新成为民办学校决策机构负责人或者校长；情节特别严重、社会影响恶劣的，永久不得新成为民办学校决策机构负责人或者校长。  
同时举办或者实际控制多所民办学校的举办者或者实际控制人违反本条例规定，对所举办或者实际控制的民办学校疏于管理，造成恶劣影响的，由县级以上教育行政部门、人力资源社会保障行政部门或者其他有关部门依据职责分工责令限期整顿；拒不整改或者整改后仍发生同类问题的，1至5年内不得举办新的民办学校，情节严重的，10年内不得举办新的民办学校。

**Article 64** Where a private school is in any of the illegal circumstances specified in Article 62 of the Private Education Promotion Law or Article 63 of these Regulations, the education administrative department, the human resources and social security administrative department, or other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, give a warning to the responsible person of the decision-making body, the principal, and the person directly responsible; if the circumstances are serious, such persons shall not become the responsible person of a decision-making body or the principal of a private school for a period of 1 to 5 years; if the circumstances are particularly serious and the social impact is adverse, such persons shall be permanently banned from becoming the responsible person of a decision-making body or the principal of a private school.  
Where a sponsor or actual controller who simultaneously establishes or actually controls multiple private schools violates the provisions of these Regulations and neglects the management of the private schools it establishes or actually controls, resulting in an adverse impact, the education administrative department, the human resources and social security administrative department, or other relevant departments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order a rectification within a specified time limit; if the person refuses to rectify or after rectification the same problem still occurs, such person shall not establish a new private school for a period of 1 to 5 years; if the circumstances are serious, it shall not establish a new private school for a period of 10 years.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例规定举办、参与举办民办学校或者在民办学校筹设期内招生的，依照[民办教育促进法第六十四条](../../../行政与公法/教育科技/中华人民共和国民办教育促进法.md#art-64)规定给予处罚。

**Article 65** Where a person establishes or participates in establishing a private school in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, or enrolls students during the preparatory establishment period of a private school, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with Article 64 of the Private Education Promotion Law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本条例所称现有民办学校，是指2016年11月7日《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于修改〈中华人民共和国民办教育促进法〉的决定》公布前设立的民办学校。

**Article 66** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'existing private schools' refers to private schools established before the promulgation of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Amending the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Promotion of Private Education on November 7, 2016.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本条例规定的支持与奖励措施适用于中外合作办学机构。

**Article 67** The support and reward measures provided for in these Regulations shall apply to Sino-foreign cooperative education institutions.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例自2021年9月1日起施行。

**Article 68** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2021.
