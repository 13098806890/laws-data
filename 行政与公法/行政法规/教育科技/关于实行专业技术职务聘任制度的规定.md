### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、专业技术职务聘任制度的基本内容  
专业技术职务是根据实际工作需要设置的有明确职责、任职条件和任期，并需要具备专门的业务知识和技术水平才能担负的工作岗位，不同于一次获得后而终身拥有的学位、学衔等各种学术、技术称号。  
建立专业技术职务聘任制度，应当根据实际需要设置专业技术工作岗位，规定明确的职责和任职条件；在定编定员的基础上，确定高、中、初级专业技术职务的合理结构比例；由行政领导在经过评审委员会评定的、符合相应条件的专业技术人员中聘任；有一定的任期，在任职期间领取专业技术职务工资。

**Article 1** I. Basic Content of the System for Appointment of Professional and Technical Positions  
  
Professional and technical positions are work posts established based on actual work needs with clear duties, qualification requirements, and terms of office, and require specialized professional knowledge and technical proficiency to hold. They differ from academic and technical titles such as degrees and academic ranks, which are acquired once and held for life.  
  
To establish the system for appointment of professional and technical positions, professional and technical work posts shall be set up according to actual needs, with clear duties and qualification requirements prescribed; on the basis of determining the establishment and personnel quotas, a reasonable proportional structure for senior, intermediate, and junior professional and technical positions shall be determined; appointments shall be made by administrative leaders from among professional and technical personnel who have been assessed by the evaluation committee and meet the corresponding conditions; there shall be a fixed term of office, and the appointee shall receive professional and technical position salary during the term.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、专业技术职务的设置  
1．专业技术职务系列由国务院有关部门根据需要提出，经中央职称改革领导小组审核后报国务院批准。国务院委托专业技术职务系列的主管部门制订有关条例及实施意见，其内容应包括职务的名称、档次(或等级)、适用范围、高中初级专业技术职务的合理结构比例、岗位职责、任职条件、任期、评审和聘任办法、审批权限等，报送中央职称改革领导小组批准试行，经过一段实践，总结经验，进一步修改后，报国务院正式发布。  
2．各单位根据专业技术工作的实际需要，提出本单位选用专业技术职务系列和职务设置的意见，属于国务院各部门直属事业单位的，由各部门核准；属于地方的，由地方职称改革领导小组核准。  
3．直接从事专业技术管理工作的部门设置专业技术职务问题，由中央职称改革领导小组另行规定，报国务院批准。

**Article 2** II. Establishment of Professional and Technical Positions  
  
1. The series of professional and technical positions shall be proposed by the relevant departments of the State Council according to needs, examined by the Central Leading Group for Professional Title Reform, and submitted to the State Council for approval. The State Council shall entrust the competent departments in charge of the professional and technical position series to formulate relevant regulations and implementation opinions. The contents thereof shall include the name, grade (or rank), scope of application, reasonable proportional structure of senior, intermediate, and junior professional and technical positions, post duties, qualification requirements, term of office, evaluation and appointment methods, approval authority, etc., and shall be submitted to the Central Leading Group for Professional Title Reform for approval and pilot implementation. After a period of practice and experience summarization, further revisions shall be made, and then submitted to the State Council for formal promulgation.  
  
2. Each unit shall, based on the actual needs of professional and technical work, put forward opinions on the selection of professional and technical position series and the establishment of positions. For institutions directly under the departments of the State Council, opinions shall be verified by the respective departments; for local institutions, opinions shall be verified by the local leading group for professional title reform.  
  
3. The establishment of professional and technical positions in departments directly engaged in professional and technical management shall be separately prescribed by the Central Leading Group for Professional Title Reform and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、任职基本条件  
1．热爱祖国，遵守宪法和法律，积极为我国四化建设贡献自己的力量。  
2．具备履行相应职责的实际工作能力和业务知识。  
3．担任高级、中级、初级专业技术职务一般应相应具备大学本科、大专、中专毕业的学历。各专业技术职务系列可以根据各自的特点，提出各级职务的不同学历要求。  
对虽然不具备上述规定学历，但确有真才实学、成绩显著、贡献突出、符合任职条件的专业技术人员，也可根据需要聘任相应的专业技术职务。  
4．身体健康，能坚持正常工作。

**Article 3** III. Basic Conditions for Appointment  
  
1. Love the motherland, abide by the Constitution and laws, and actively contribute one's strength to the country's four modernizations.  
  
2. Possess the practical work ability and professional knowledge necessary to perform the corresponding duties.  
  
3. To hold a senior, intermediate, or junior professional and technical position, one shall generally have the corresponding educational background of a bachelor's degree, a college diploma, or a secondary specialized school diploma. Each series of professional and technical positions may, based on its own characteristics, propose different educational requirements for positions at various levels.  
  
For professional and technical personnel who, though lacking the above-specified educational background, have genuine talent, remarkable achievements, outstanding contributions, and meet the qualification conditions, they may also be appointed to corresponding professional and technical positions as needed.  
  
4. Be in good health and able to persist in normal work.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、各级专业技术职务结构比例及工资额的确定  
1．专业技术职务设高、中、初三级，也可以只设中、初两级或只设初级。  
专业技术职务的数量在国家规定的编制范围内有一定的限额，不同类别的单位和专业技术职务在不同档次之间应各有合理的结构比例。  
2．国务院各部门和各省、自治区、直辖市应在国家批准的编制和各专业技术职务系列主管部委规定的限额比例内，确定本部门、本地区所属事业单位各类专业技术职务的结构比例。  
国务院各部门和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府各工作部门，在劳动人事部会同国家科委提出并经国务院批准的国家机关专业技术职务的总结构比例内，提出本机关内各级专业技术职务的结构比例。属于国务院各部门的，报劳动人事部核定并报国家科委备案；属于省、自治区、直辖市人民政府工作部门的，报同级劳动人事部门核定并报同级科委备案。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府工作部门的高级职务的比例限额应低于国务院各部门高级职务的限额。  
3．专业技术职务各档次(或等级)对应的职务工资标准，报劳动人事部核准。  
国家机关和事业单位实行专业技术职务聘任制所需的增资额，均应在劳动人事部规定的增资指标内核定，未经批准不得突破。

**Article 4** IV. Determination of the Structural Proportion and Salary Amounts for Professional and Technical Positions at All Levels  
  
1. Professional and technical positions shall be divided into three levels: senior, intermediate, and junior. Alternatively, only intermediate and junior levels or only the junior level may be established.  
The number of professional and technical positions shall be subject to certain limits within the establishment prescribed by the State. Different types of units and professional and technical positions at different levels shall each have a reasonable structural proportion.  
  
2. The departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within the scope of the establishment approved by the State and the proportional limits prescribed by the competent ministries and commissions in charge of each professional and technical position series, determine the structural proportion of various types of professional and technical positions in the institutions under their respective departments or regions.  
The working departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within the overall structural proportion of professional and technical positions in state organs proposed by the Ministry of Labor and Personnel jointly with the State Science and Technology Commission and approved by the State Council, propose the structural proportion of professional and technical positions at each level within their own organs. For departments under the State Council, the proposal shall be reported to the Ministry of Labor and Personnel for verification and to the State Science and Technology Commission for recordation; for working departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, the proposal shall be reported to the labor and personnel department at the same level for verification and to the science and technology commission at the same level for recordation. The proportional limit for senior positions in the working departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be lower than the limit for senior positions in the departments of the State Council.  
  
3. The position salary standards corresponding to each grade (or rank) of professional and technical positions shall be reported to the Ministry of Labor and Personnel for approval.  
The additional salary amounts required for the implementation of the professional and technical position appointment system in state organs and institutions shall all be verified within the additional salary targets prescribed by the Ministry of Labor and Personnel. No breakthrough shall be made without approval.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、专业技术职务评审委员会  
评审委员会是负责评议、审定专业技术人员是否符合相应专业技术职务任职条件的组织。评审委员会应以民主程序进行工作。  
各部门和地方可根据实际需要分别建立高级、中级、初级职务评审委员会。评审委员会应由具有较高的专业技术水平或担任较高专业技术职务、作风正派、办事公道的专业技术人员组成，其中中、青年应占一定比例。评审委员会可以是常设的，也可以在需要时临时组成。评审委员会的人选由本单位专业技术人员酝酿推荐，单位专业技术负责人提名，经单位领导批准。中级职务评审委员会人选，还须报上一级主管部门批准。高级职务评审委员会一般应由国务院各部门和各省、自治区、直辖市组建，国务院各部门和各省、自治区、直辖市也可授权确实具备评审条件的下属单位直接组建，报部门或省、自治区、直辖市批准。本单位专业技术力量薄弱，不能成立评审委员会的，可以由上一级组织的评审委员会或聘请外单位专家与本单位专家共同组成的评审委员会承担评审任务。

**Article 5** V. Evaluation Committees for Professional and Technical Positions  
  
The evaluation committee is an organization responsible for assessing and determining whether professional and technical personnel meet the qualification conditions for corresponding professional and technical positions. The evaluation committee shall conduct its work through democratic procedures.  
  
Each department and locality may, based on actual needs, establish evaluation committees for senior, intermediate, and junior positions. The evaluation committee shall be composed of professional and technical personnel who possess a relatively high level of professional and technical expertise or hold a relatively high professional and technical position, are upright in conduct, and are fair in handling matters. Among them, middle-aged and young personnel shall account for a certain proportion. The evaluation committee may be a standing body or may be formed temporarily when needed. The candidates for the evaluation committee shall be discussed and recommended by the professional and technical personnel of the unit, nominated by the professional and technical leader of the unit, and approved by the head of the unit. The candidates for the intermediate position evaluation committee shall also be reported to the higher competent department for approval. The senior position evaluation committee shall generally be established by the departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may also authorize subordinate units that truly have the evaluation conditions to directly establish such committees, subject to approval by the department or the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. If the professional and technical strength of a unit is weak and it cannot establish an evaluation committee, the evaluation task may be undertaken by an evaluation committee organized by the higher-level authority or by an evaluation committee jointly formed by inviting experts from other units together with experts from the unit itself.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、聘任和任命  
1．事业单位的专业技术职务一般实行聘任制。事业单位的各级专业技术职务，由行政领导在经过评审委员会评定的符合相应任职条件的专业技术人员中聘任。行政领导应向被聘任的专业技术人员颁发聘书，双方签订聘约。  
2．三线、边远地区和不具备聘任条件的事业单位可以实行任命制，但应创造条件逐步实行聘任制。各级国家机关的专业技术职务实行任命制。实行任命制的部门和单位应按干部管理权限，由行政领导向被任命的专业技术人员颁发任命书。实行任命制的部门和单位和各级专业技术人员也须经过评审委员会评审，符合相应任职条件。  
3．专业技术职务的聘任或任命都不是终身的，应有一定的任期，每一任期一般不超过五年。如工作需要，可以连聘连任。  
4．聘任或任命单位对受聘或被任命的专业技术人员的业务水平、工作态度和成绩，应进行定期或不定期的考核。考核成绩记入考绩档案，作为提职、调薪、奖惩和能否续聘或任命的依据。

**Article 6** VI. Appointment and Designation  
  
1. For institutions, professional and technical positions shall generally adopt an appointment system. The professional and technical positions at all levels in institutions shall be filled by administrative leaders by appointing professional and technical personnel who have been evaluated by the evaluation committee as meeting the corresponding qualification conditions. The administrative leader shall issue a letter of appointment to the appointed professional and technical personnel, and both parties shall sign an engagement agreement.  
  
2. Institutions in third-front areas, remote border areas, and those not meeting the conditions for the appointment system may adopt a designation system, but they shall create conditions to gradually implement the appointment system. Professional and technical positions in state organs at all levels shall adopt a designation system. For departments and units implementing the designation system, the administrative leader shall, in accordance with the authority for cadre management, issue a letter of designation to the designated professional and technical personnel. Professional and technical personnel at all levels in departments and units implementing the designation system must also undergo evaluation by the evaluation committee and meet the corresponding qualification conditions.  
  
3. The appointment or designation of a professional or technical position is not for life; there shall be a fixed term of office. Each term shall generally not exceed five years. If work requires, the term may be renewed upon re-appointment or re-designation.  
  
4. The appointing or designating unit shall conduct regular or irregular assessments of the professional competence, work attitude, and performance of the appointed or designated professional and technical personnel. The assessment results shall be recorded in the performance evaluation file and shall serve as the basis for promotion, salary adjustment, reward and punishment, and whether reappointment or redesignation is possible.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、行政人员与专业技术人员相互兼任职务的问题  
1．行政领导一般不兼任专业技术职务。确需兼任的，必须经评审委员会确认符合相应职务任职条件，并按规定的手续聘任。兼职人员应履行相应的职责。  
2．专业技术人员兼任行政领导职务的，任职期间的工资待遇，在专业技术职务工资和行政职务工资中，按较高的职务工资标准执行。

**Article 7** VII. Issues Concerning Concurrent Holding of Positions by Administrative Personnel and Professional and Technical Personnel  
  
1. Administrative leaders shall generally not concurrently hold professional and technical positions. If concurrent holding is indeed necessary, the person must be confirmed by the evaluation committee to meet the corresponding qualification conditions for the position, and shall be appointed through the prescribed procedures. The concurrent holder shall perform the corresponding duties.  
  
2. Where a professional and technical person concurrently holds an administrative leadership position, the salary and benefits during the term of office shall be implemented in accordance with the higher of the professional and technical position salary standard and the administrative position salary standard.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、已获得职称人员的安排  
对于过去已获得职称的人员，原则上应承认他们具备担任相应专业技术职务的条件。在专业技术岗位上的合格人员，要给予妥善安排，根据需要聘任他们担任相应的专业技术职务，其中水平偏低的，应帮助其尽快提高水平；完全不合格的，不能承认其具备担任相应专业技术职务的条件；对个别弄虚作假骗取职称的，应严肃处理。  
一九八三年九月一日前，经过职称评定组织评定了相应职称，并已上报到有关部门“待批”或“待授”的人员，在这次专业技术职务聘任工作中，也按上述规定对待。

**Article 8** VIII. Arrangements for Personnel Who Have Already Acquired Professional Titles  
  
With respect to personnel who have already acquired professional titles in the past, it shall in principle be recognized that they meet the conditions for holding corresponding professional and technical positions. Qualified personnel who are in professional and technical posts shall be properly arranged and appointed to corresponding professional and technical positions as needed. For those whose level is relatively low, assistance shall be given to help them raise their level as soon as possible. For those who are entirely unqualified, their qualification for holding corresponding professional and technical positions shall not be recognized. For the few individuals who have obtained professional titles by fraudulent means, such as falsification, they shall be dealt with seriously.  
  
Personnel who, before September 1, 1983, had been evaluated by a professional title evaluation organization as having a corresponding professional title and reported to the relevant department as "pending approval" or "pending conferral" shall also be treated in accordance with the above provisions in this professional and technical position appointment work.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、待聘人员的安排和待遇  
1．实行聘任制后，对暂时未被聘任的专业技术人员，原单位要继续关心他们，并区别情况，妥善安排。要鼓励他们到更需要或更能发挥他们专长的单位去工作。待聘人员应积极应聘到其它单位工作，原单位科技干部管理部门要积极帮助联系，提供应聘方便。待聘人员在尚未应聘到其他单位工作以前，应做好原单位所安排的临时性工作。  
2．长期未受聘用的专业技术人员的工资待遇，原则上应低于受聘专业技术人员的工资待遇，以促进人才流动，具体办法由劳动人事部门另行规定。

**Article 9** IX. Arrangements and Treatment of Pending Appointment Personnel  
  
1. After the implementation of the appointment system, the original unit shall continue to care for professional and technical personnel who have not yet been appointed and shall make proper arrangements according to different circumstances. They shall be encouraged to work in units that have greater need or where their expertise can be better utilized. Personnel pending appointment shall actively apply for positions in other units. The science and technology cadre management department of the original unit shall actively assist in making contacts and provide convenience for their applications. Before being appointed to another unit, personnel pending appointment shall perform the temporary work assigned by the original unit.  
  
2. In principle, the salary and benefits of professional and technical personnel who have not been hired for a long period shall be lower than those of appointed professional and technical personnel, so as to promote talent mobility. Specific measures shall be separately prescribed by the labor and personnel departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、“待聘高级职务”的设置  
在少数人才密集的部门或单位，为解决历史遗留问题，鼓励人才合理流动，凡确实符合相应高级职务任职条件的中年专业技术骨干，由于限额已满，未受聘任的，可有控制地确定“待聘高级职务”，领取相应的职务工资，同时鼓励他们到其它单位任职。设置“待聘高级职务”一定要严格保证质量，绝不能降低标准，更不能滥竽充数。拟设“待聘高级职务”的单位和数额，经国务院有关部门或各省、自治区、直辖市核定后，报中央职称改革领导小组批准。宣布聘任结果时，应同时宣布确定的“待聘高级职务”人员名单。确定“待聘高级职务”的人员都应根据国家需要提出志愿去向，由单位帮助联系；到外地的，户口、家属可以不迁。

**Article 10** X. Establishment of "Senior Positions Pending Appointment"  
  
In a few departments or units where talent is highly concentrated, in order to resolve historical issues and encourage rational mobility of talent, for middle-aged professional and technical backbone personnel who truly meet the qualification conditions for corresponding senior positions but have not been appointed due to the limit being reached, a "senior position pending appointment" may be determined on a controlled basis. Such personnel shall receive the corresponding position salary, and at the same time, they shall be encouraged to take up positions in other units. The establishment of "senior positions pending appointment" shall strictly ensure quality; standards shall never be lowered, and there shall never be any fraudulent inclusion of unqualified personnel. The units that intend to establish "senior positions pending appointment" and the number of such positions shall, after verification by the relevant department of the State Council or the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, be reported to the Central Leading Group for Professional Title Reform for approval. When the appointment results are announced, the list of persons determined for "senior positions pending appointment" shall be announced at the same time. Persons determined for "senior positions pending appointment" shall, based on the needs of the State, indicate their preferred destinations, and the unit shall assist in making contacts. For those going to other localities, their household registration and family members may remain unchanged.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、离休、退休问题  
1．在实行专业技术职务聘任制的同时，应坚决执行国务院有关离休、退休的规定；  
2．在这次专业技术职务聘任工作中，达到规定离休、退休年龄的专业技术人员，凡符合专业技术职务聘任条件的，可在确定相应专业技术职务后，办理离休、退休手续。

**Article 11** XI. Issues Concerning Retirement and Resignation with Honors  
  
1. While implementing the professional and technical position appointment system, the relevant provisions of the State Council on retirement and resignation with honors shall be resolutely implemented.  
  
2. In this professional and technical position appointment work, professional and technical personnel who have reached the prescribed age for retirement or resignation with honors and who meet the conditions for appointment to a professional and technical position may, after being confirmed in the corresponding professional and technical position, go through the procedures for retirement or resignation with honors.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、聘任专业技术职务，必须实事求是，严格按照政策办事。对于借聘任之机打击迫害专业技术人员的，应按情节轻重，严肃处理；对于伪造学历、资历，谎报成果，骗取专业技术职务的，应予解聘，免除其担任的专业技术职务，并视情节轻重，严肃处理。

**Article 12** XII. In appointing professional and technical positions, facts must be strictly adhered to and affairs must be handled strictly in accordance with policies. For those who take advantage of the appointment process to retaliate against or persecute professional and technical personnel, they shall be dealt with seriously according to the severity of the circumstances. For those who forge academic qualifications, work experience, falsify achievements, or fraudulently obtain professional and technical positions, they shall be dismissed, removed from the professional and technical position they hold, and dealt with seriously depending on the severity of the circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、本规定适用于国家机关、事业单位。  
国务院各部门和各省、自治区、直辖市应根据有关专业技术职务条例和实施意见，结合本部门、本地区的工作需要，制订实施细则。  
企业单位也应参照上述规定，结合企业特点逐步实行专业技术职务聘任制度。

**Article 13** XIII. These Provisions shall apply to state organs and institutions.  
  
The departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the relevant regulations and implementation opinions on professional and technical positions, formulate implementation rules in light of the work needs of their respective departments and regions.  
  
Enterprises shall also refer to the above provisions and, taking into account the characteristics of enterprises, gradually implement the system for appointment of professional and technical positions.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、本规定自发布之日起实行。过去由国务院或国务院各部门颁发的有关职称评定的规定，即行废止。

**Article 14** XIV. These Provisions shall come into effect as of the date of promulgation. Any provisions concerning the evaluation of professional titles previously issued by the State Council or any department of the State Council shall be abolished forthwith.
