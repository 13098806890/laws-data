# 中华人民共和国档案法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-01-12  
**生效日期**：2024-03-01

> 第九条　国家鼓励和支持企业事业单位、社会组织和个人等社会力量通过依法兴办实体、资助项目、从事志愿服务以及开展科学研究、技术创新和科技成果推广等形式，参与和支持档案事业的发展。 档案行业组织依照法律、法规、规章及其章程的规定，加强行业自律，推动诚信建设，提供行业服务，开展学术交流和档案相关科普教育，参与政策咨询和标准制定等活动。 档案主管部门应当在职责范围内予以指导。 第十条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府、档案主管部门或者本单位按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励： （一）对档案收集、整理、保护、利用做出显著成绩的； （二）对档案科学研究、技术创新、宣传教育、交流合作做出显著成绩的； （三）在重大活动、突发事件应对活动相关档案工作中表现突出的； （四）将重要或者珍贵档案捐献给国家的； （五）同违反档案法律、法规的行为作斗争，表现突出的； （六）长期从事档案工作，表现突出的。 第二章　档案机构及其职责 第十一条　国家档案主管部门依照《档案法》第八条第一款的规定，履行下列职责： （一）根据有关法律、行政法规和国家有关方针政策，研究、制定部门规章、档案工作具体方针政策和标准； （二）组织协调全国档案事业的发展，制定国家档案事业发展综合规划和专项计划，并组织实施； （三）对有关法律、行政法规、部门规章和国家有关方针政策的实施情况进行监督检查，依法查处档案违法行为；

### 正文

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府、档案主管部门或者本单位按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励：  
（一）对档案收集、整理、保护、利用做出显著成绩的；  
（二）对档案科学研究、技术创新、宣传教育、交流合作做出显著成绩的；  
（三）在重大活动、突发事件应对活动相关档案工作中表现突出的；  
（四）将重要或者珍贵档案捐献给国家的；  
（五）同违反档案法律、法规的行为作斗争，表现突出的；  
（六）长期从事档案工作，表现突出的。

**Article 10** In any of the following circumstances, the people's government at or above the county level, the archives administrative department, or the entity concerned shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, commend and reward the relevant unit or individual:  
(1) having made outstanding achievements in the collection, arrangement, preservation, and utilization of archives;  
(2) having made outstanding achievements in scientific research, technological innovation, publicity, education, exchange, and cooperation in the field of archives;  
(3) having performed prominently in archive work related to major events or emergency response activities;  
(4) having donated important or precious archives to the State;  
(5) having performed prominently in fighting against acts violating archive laws and regulations;  
(6) having performed prominently in long-term archive work.

### 第二章　档案机构及其职责

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家档案主管部门依照[《档案法》第八条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-8)第一款的规定，履行下列职责：  
（一）根据有关法律、行政法规和国家有关方针政策，研究、制定部门规章、档案工作具体方针政策和标准；  
（二）组织协调全国档案事业的发展，制定国家档案事业发展综合规划和专项计划，并组织实施；  
（三）对有关法律、行政法规、部门规章和国家有关方针政策的实施情况进行监督检查，依法查处档案违法行为；  
（四）对中央国家机关各部门、中央管理的群团组织、中央企业以及中央和国务院直属事业单位的档案工作，中央级国家档案馆的工作，以及省、自治区、直辖市档案主管部门的工作，实施监督、指导；  
（五）组织、指导档案理论与科学技术研究、档案信息化建设、档案宣传教育、档案工作人员培训；  
（六）组织、开展档案领域的国际交流与合作。

**Article 11** The national archives administrative department shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 8 of the Archives Law, perform the following duties:  
(1) researching and formulating departmental rules, specific policies and standards for archive work in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and relevant policies of the State;  
(2) organizing and coordinating the development of the national archival undertaking, formulating comprehensive plans and special plans for the development of the national archival undertaking, and organizing their implementation;  
(3) supervising and inspecting the implementation of relevant laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and relevant policies of the State, and investigating and handling illegal archival acts according to law;  
(4) exercising supervision and guidance over the archive work of the departments of the central state organs, mass organizations under central management, central enterprises, and institutions directly under the Central Committee and the State Council, the work of national-level state archives, and the work of the archives administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) organizing and guiding research on archival theory and science and technology, archival informatization, archival publicity and education, and training of archival personnel;  
(6) organizing and carrying out international exchanges and cooperation in the field of archives.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方档案主管部门依照[《档案法》第八条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-8)第二款的规定，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻执行有关法律、法规、规章和国家有关方针政策；  
（二）制定本行政区域档案事业发展规划和档案工作制度规范，并组织实施；  
（三）监督、指导本行政区域档案工作，对有关法律、法规、规章和国家有关方针政策的实施情况进行监督检查，依法查处档案违法行为；  
（四）组织、指导本行政区域档案理论与科学技术研究、档案信息化建设、档案宣传教育、档案工作人员培训。

**Article 12** The local archives administrative departments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 8 of the Archives Law, perform the following duties:  
(1) implementing relevant laws, regulations, rules, and relevant policies of the State;  
(2) formulating development plans for the archival undertaking and rules and standards for archive work within their respective administrative regions, and organizing their implementation;  
(3) supervising and guiding archive work within their respective administrative regions, supervising and inspecting the implementation of relevant laws, regulations, rules, and relevant policies of the State, and investigating and handling illegal archival acts according to law;  
(4) organizing and guiding research on archival theory and science and technology, archival informatization, archival publicity and education, and training of archival personnel within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　乡镇人民政府依照[《档案法》第八条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-8)第三款的规定，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻执行有关法律、法规、规章和国家有关方针政策，建立健全档案工作制度规范；  
（二）指定人员管理本机关档案，并按照规定向有关档案馆移交档案；  
（三）监督、指导所属单位以及基层群众性自治组织等的档案工作。

**Article 13** The people's governments of townships and towns shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 8 of the Archives Law, perform the following duties:  
(1) implementing relevant laws, regulations, rules, and relevant policies of the State, and establishing and improving rules and standards for archive work;  
(2) designating personnel to manage the archives of their own organs, and transferring archives to relevant archives in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) supervising and guiding the archive work of their subordinate units and grassroots self-governing organizations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当确定档案机构或者档案工作人员，依照[《档案法》第九条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-9)第一款的规定，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻执行有关法律、法规、规章和国家有关方针政策，建立健全本单位档案工作制度规范；  
（二）指导本单位相关材料的形成、积累、整理和归档，统一管理本单位的档案，并按照规定向有关档案馆移交档案；  
（三）监督、指导所属单位的档案工作。

**Article 14** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall designate an archival institution or archival personnel and, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 9 of the Archives Law, perform the following duties:  
(1) implementing relevant laws, regulations, rules, and relevant policies of the State, and establishing and improving rules and standards for the archive work of their own units;  
(2) guiding the formation, accumulation, arrangement, and filing of relevant materials of their own units, managing the archives of their own units in a unified manner, and transferring archives to relevant archives in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) supervising and guiding the archive work of their subordinate units.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级各类档案馆的设置和管理应当符合国家有关规定。

**Article 15** The establishment and management of archives of all levels and types shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家档案馆应当配备与其职责和规模相适应的专业人员，依照[《档案法》第十条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-10)的规定，履行下列职责：  
（一）收集本馆分管范围内的档案；  
（二）按照规定整理、保管档案；  
（三）依法向社会开放档案，并采取各种形式研究、开发档案资源，为各方面利用档案资源提供服务；  
（四）开展宣传教育，发挥爱国主义教育和历史文化教育功能。  
按照国家有关规定设置的其他各类档案馆，参照前款规定依法履行相应职责。

**Article 16** The state archives shall be staffed with professional personnel commensurate with their functions and scale and, in accordance with the provisions of Article 10 of the Archives Law, perform the following duties:  
(1) collecting archives within their scope of responsibility;  
(2) arranging and preserving archives in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) opening archives to the society according to law, and researching and developing archive resources in various forms to provide services for the utilization of archive resources by all parties;  
(4) carrying out publicity and education, giving play to the functions of patriotic education and historical and cultural education.  
Other types of archives established in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State shall, by reference to the preceding paragraph, perform corresponding duties according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　档案主管部门、档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织应当为档案工作人员的教育培训、职称评审、岗位聘用等创造条件，不断提高档案工作人员的专业知识水平和业务能力。

**Article 17** The archives administrative department, archives, and state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall create conditions for the education and training, professional title evaluation, and post appointment of archival personnel, and continuously improve the professional knowledge and operational capabilities of archival personnel.

### 第三章　档案的管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　按照国家规定应当形成档案的机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织，应当建立档案工作责任制，确定档案工作组织结构、职责分工，落实档案工作领导责任、管理责任、执行责任，健全单位主要负责人承担档案完整与安全第一责任人职责相关制度，明确档案管理、档案基础设施建设、档案信息化等工作要求。

**Article 18** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations that are required to form archives in accordance with the provisions of the State shall establish an archive work responsibility system, determine the organizational structure and division of responsibilities for archive work, implement the leadership responsibility, management responsibility, and execution responsibility for archive work, improve the system concerning the primary responsibility of the principal person in charge of the unit for the completeness and security of archives, and clarify the work requirements for archive management, archive infrastructure construction, and archival informatization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　依照[《档案法》第十三条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-13)以及国家有关规定应当归档的材料，由机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的各内设机构收集齐全，规范整理，定期交本单位档案机构或者档案工作人员集中管理，任何内设机构和个人不得拒绝归档或者据为己有。  
机关、群团组织、国有企业事业单位应当明确本单位的归档范围和档案保管期限，经同级档案主管部门审核同意后施行。单位内设机构或者工作职能发生重大变化时，应当及时调整归档范围和档案保管期限，经重新审核同意后施行。  
机关、群团组织、国有企业事业单位负责所属单位的归档范围和档案保管期限的审核。

**Article 19** Materials that shall be filed in accordance with Article 13 of the Archives Law and the relevant provisions of the State shall be collected completely and arranged in a standardized manner by the internal departments of state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations, and shall be handed over to the archival institution or archival personnel of the unit for centralized management on a regular basis. No internal department or individual may refuse to file or keep them for themselves.  
State organs, mass organizations, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions shall specify the filing scope and retention periods for archives of their own units, and come into force upon examination and approval by the archives administrative department at the same level. When there is a major change in the internal departments or functional duties of the unit, the filing scope and retention periods shall be adjusted in a timely manner and come into force upon re-examination and approval.  
State organs, mass organizations, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions shall be responsible for the examination of the filing scope and retention periods for archives of their subordinate units.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织，应当按照国家档案主管部门关于档案移交的规定，定期向有关的国家档案馆移交档案。  
属于中央级和省级、设区的市级国家档案馆接收范围的档案，移交单位应当自档案形成之日起满二十年即向有关的国家档案馆移交。属于县级国家档案馆接收范围的档案，移交单位应当自档案形成之日起满十年即向有关的县级国家档案馆移交。  
经同级档案主管部门检查和同意，专业性较强或者需要保密的档案，可以延长向有关的国家档案馆移交的期限。已撤销单位的档案可以提前向有关的国家档案馆移交。  
由于单位保管条件不符合要求或者存在其他原因可能导致不安全或者严重损毁的档案，经协商可以提前交有关档案馆保管。

**Article 20** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall, in accordance with the provisions of the national archives administrative department on the transfer of archives, periodically transfer archives to the relevant state archives.  
Archives within the receiving scope of national-level, provincial-level, and districted-city-level state archives shall be transferred by the transferring unit to the relevant state archives twenty years after the date of formation of the archives. Archives within the receiving scope of county-level state archives shall be transferred by the transferring unit to the relevant county-level state archives ten years after the date of formation of the archives.  
Where the archives are highly professional or require confidentiality, the time limit for transfer to the relevant state archives may be extended upon inspection and consent of the archives administrative department at the same level. Archives of a dissolved unit may be transferred to the relevant state archives in advance.  
Where the preservation conditions of the unit do not meet the requirements or there are other reasons that may lead to unsafety or serious damage to the archives, the archives may be handed over to the relevant archives for preservation in advance upon consultation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　档案馆可以按照国家有关规定，通过接受捐献、购买、代存、交换等方式收集档案。  
档案馆通过前款规定方式收集档案时，应当考虑档案的珍稀程度、内容的重要性等，并以书面协议形式约定相关方的权利和义务，明确相关档案利用条件。  
国家鼓励单位和个人将属于其所有的对国家和社会具有重要保存价值的档案捐献给国家档案馆。国家档案馆应当维护捐献者的合法权益。

**Article 21** Archives may collect archives through means such as accepting donations, purchasing, custodianship, and exchange in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
When collecting archives through the means specified in the preceding paragraph, an archive shall consider the rarity and importance of the content of the archives, and shall stipulate the rights and obligations of the relevant parties in the form of a written agreement, specifying the conditions for the utilization of the relevant archives.  
The State encourages units and individuals to donate archives that are owned by them and have important preservation value to the State or society to the state archives. The state archives shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of the donors.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　档案馆应当对所保管的档案采取下列管理措施：  
（一）建立健全科学的管理制度和查阅利用规范，制定有针对性的安全风险管控措施和应急预案；  
（二）配置适宜安全保存档案、符合国家有关规定的专门库房，配备防火、防盗、防水、防光、防尘、防有害气体、防有害生物以及温湿度调控等必要的设施设备；  
（三）根据档案的不同等级，采取有效措施，加以保护和管理；  
（四）根据需要和可能，配备适应档案现代化管理需要的设施设备；  
（五）编制档案目录等便于档案查找和利用的检索工具。  
机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的档案保管，参照前款规定办理。

**Article 22** Archives shall adopt the following management measures for the archives under their custody:  
(1) establishing and improving scientific management systems and rules for access and utilization, formulating targeted safety risk control measures and emergency plans;  
(2) equipping specialized storerooms suitable for the safe preservation of archives and complying with the relevant provisions of the State, and providing necessary facilities and equipment such as those for fire prevention, theft prevention, waterproofing, light protection, dust prevention, harmful gas prevention, pest prevention, and temperature and humidity control;  
(3) adopting effective measures to protect and manage archives according to their different grades;  
(4) equipping facilities and equipment suitable for the modern management of archives as necessary and possible;  
(5) compiling finding aids such as archive catalogues to facilitate the search and utilization of archives.  
The preservation of archives in state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall be handled by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府应当采取措施，保障国家档案馆依法接收档案所需的库房及设施设备。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、挪用国家档案馆的馆舍，不得擅自改变国家档案馆馆舍的功能和用途。  
国家档案馆馆舍的建设，应当符合实用、安全、科学、美观、环保、节约的要求和国家有关工程建设标准，并配置无障碍设施设备。

**Article 23** The people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to ensure the storerooms and facilities and equipment required by state archives for receiving archives according to law.  
No unit or individual may occupy or misappropriate the premises of state archives, or alter the functions and purposes of the premises of state archives without authorization.  
The construction of the premises of state archives shall meet the requirements of practicality, safety, science, aesthetics, environmental protection, and economy, and comply with the relevant state standards for engineering construction, and shall be equipped with barrier-free facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当定期对本单位保管的保管期限届满的档案进行鉴定，形成鉴定工作报告。  
经鉴定仍需继续保存的档案，应当重新划定保管期限并作出标注。经鉴定需要销毁的档案，其销毁工作应当遵守国家有关规定。

**Article 24** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall periodically appraise the archives under their custody whose retention periods have expired, and form an appraisal work report.  
Archives that are still required to be preserved after appraisal shall have their retention periods re-determined and marked. For archives that need to be destroyed after appraisal, the destruction work shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上档案主管部门可以依托国家档案馆，对下列属于国家所有的档案中具有永久保存价值的档案分类别汇集有关目录数据：  
（一）机关、群团组织、国有企业事业单位形成的档案；  
（二）第一项所列单位之外的其他单位，经法律法规授权或者受国家机关依法委托管理公共事务形成的档案；  
（三）第一项所列单位之外的其他单位或者个人，由国家资金支持，从事或者参与建设工程、科学研究、技术创新等活动形成的且按照协议约定属于国家所有的档案；  
（四）国家档案馆保管的前三项以外的其他档案。  
涉及国防、外交、国家安全、公共安全等的档案的目录数据，其汇集范围由有关档案主管部门会同档案形成单位研究确定。

**Article 25** The archives administrative departments at or above the county level may, relying on the state archives, collect relevant catalog data by category for the archives that are owned by the State and have permanent preservation value in the following categories:  
(1) archives formed by state organs, mass organizations, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions;  
(2) archives formed by units other than those listed in item (1) that manage public affairs upon authorization by laws and regulations or upon lawful entrustment by state organs;  
(3) archives formed by units or individuals other than those listed in item (1) that are supported by state funds and engage in or participate in activities such as construction projects, scientific research, and technological innovation, and that are owned by the State as agreed in the agreement;  
(4) other archives preserved by state archives other than those specified in the preceding three items.  
For catalog data of archives involving national defense, foreign affairs, national security, and public security, the scope of collection shall be determined by the relevant archives administrative department jointly with the archive-forming unit.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织为了收集、交换散失在国外的档案、进行国际文化交流，以及适应经济建设、科学研究和科技成果推广等的需要，经国家档案主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市档案主管部门依据职权审查批准，可以向国内外的单位或者个人赠送、交换、出售属于国家所有的档案的复制件。

**Article 26** For the purposes of collecting or exchanging archives scattered abroad, conducting international cultural exchanges, and meeting the needs of economic construction, scientific research, and the promotion of scientific and technological achievements, archives and state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations may, upon examination and approval by the national archives administrative department or the archives administrative department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with their functions and powers, give, exchange, or sell reproductions of archives owned by the State to units or individuals at home or abroad.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　一级档案严禁出境。二级档案需要出境的，应当经国家档案主管部门审查批准。  
除前款规定之外，属于[《档案法》第二十五条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-25)规定的档案或者复制件确需出境的，有关档案馆、机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织以及个人应当按照管理权限，报国家档案主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市档案主管部门审查批准，海关凭批准文件查验放行。  
档案或者复制件出境涉及数据出境的，还应当符合国家关于数据出境的规定。  
相关单位和个人应当在档案或者复制件出境时主动向海关申报核验，并按照出境申请审查批准意见，妥善保管、处置出境的档案或者复制件。

**Article 27** Class One archives are strictly prohibited from being taken out of the country. If Class Two archives need to be taken out of the country, they shall be subject to examination and approval by the national archives administrative department.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, where archives or reproductions as specified in Article 25 of the Archives Law need to be taken out of the country, the relevant archives, state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations, as well as individuals, shall, in accordance with the limits of authority, submit to the national archives administrative department or the archives administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval. The Customs shall, upon the approval document, inspect and release them.  
Where the taking out of the country of archives or reproductions involves the taking out of the country of data, it shall also comply with the state provisions on data export.  
The relevant units and individuals shall proactively declare to the Customs for verification when the archives or reproductions are taken out of the country, and shall properly preserve and dispose of the archives or reproductions taken out of the country in accordance with the opinions of examination and approval of the exit application.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织依照[《档案法》第二十四条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-24)的规定委托档案服务时，应当确定受委托的档案服务企业符合下列条件：  
（一）具有企业法人资格和相应的经营范围；  
（二）具有与从事档案整理、寄存、开发利用、数字化等相关服务相适应的场所、设施设备、专业人员和专业能力；  
（三）具有保证档案安全的管理体系和保障措施。  
委托方应当对受托方的服务进行全程指导和监督，确保档案安全和服务质量。

**Article 28** When archives and state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations entrust archive services in accordance with Article 24 of the Archives Law, they shall ensure that the entrusted archive service enterprise meets the following conditions:  
(1) having the status of an enterprise legal person and corresponding business scope;  
(2) having premises, facilities and equipment, professional personnel, and professional capabilities suitable for engaging in related services such as archive arrangement, custody, development and utilization, and digitization;  
(3) having a management system and safeguard measures to ensure the security of archives.  
The entrusting party shall provide full-process guidance and supervision over the services of the entrusted party to ensure the security of archives and the quality of services.

### 第四章　档案的利用和公布

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家档案馆应当依照《档案法》的有关规定，分期分批向社会开放档案，并同时公布开放档案的目录。

**Article 29** State archives shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Archives Law, open archives to the society in batches and by stages, and at the same time publish the catalogues of the opened archives.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家档案馆应当建立馆藏档案开放审核协同机制，会同档案形成单位或者移交单位进行档案开放审核。档案形成单位或者移交单位撤销、合并、职权变更的，由有关的国家档案馆会同继续行使其职权的单位共同负责；无继续行使其职权的单位的，由有关的国家档案馆负责。  
尚未移交进馆档案的开放审核，由档案形成单位或者保管单位负责，并在移交进馆时附具到期开放意见、政府信息公开情况、密级变更情况等。  
县级以上档案主管部门应当加强对档案开放审核工作的统筹协调。

**Article 30** State archives shall establish a collaborative mechanism for the review of the opening of archives in their custody, and conduct the review of the opening of archives jointly with the archive-forming unit or the transferring unit. Where the archive-forming unit or the transferring unit is dissolved, merged, or its functions and powers are changed, the relevant state archives shall be jointly responsible with the unit that continues to exercise its functions and powers. If there is no unit that continues to exercise its functions and powers, the relevant state archives shall be responsible.  
The review of the opening of archives that have not yet been transferred to the archives shall be the responsibility of the archive-forming unit or the custodian unit, and when transferring to the archives, it shall attach opinions on opening upon expiry, information on the disclosure of government information, changes in classification level, etc.  
The archives administrative departments at or above the county level shall strengthen the overall coordination of the review of the opening of archives.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对于[《档案法》第二十七条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-27)规定的到期不宜开放的档案，经国家档案馆报同级档案主管部门同意，可以延期向社会开放。

**Article 31** For archives that are not suitable for opening upon expiry as specified in Article 27 of the Archives Law, the state archives may, with the consent of the archives administrative department at the same level, postpone their opening to the society.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　档案馆提供社会利用的档案，应当逐步实现以复制件代替原件。数字、缩微以及其他复制形式的档案复制件，载有档案保管单位签章标识的，具有与档案原件同等的效力。

**Article 32** Archives provided by archives for social utilization shall gradually realize the substitution of reproductions for originals. Reproductions of archives in digital, microform, or other reproduction forms, bearing the signature and seal of the custodian unit of the archives, shall have the same effect as the original archives.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　档案馆可以通过阅览、复制和摘录等形式，依法提供利用档案。  
国家档案馆应当明确档案利用的条件、范围、程序等，在档案利用接待场所和官方网站公布相关信息，创新档案利用服务形式，推进档案查询利用服务线上线下融合。

**Article 33** Archives may provide access to archives according to law through forms such as reading, reproduction, and extraction.  
State archives shall specify the conditions, scope, and procedures for the utilization of archives, publish relevant information at the reception site for archive utilization and on official websites, innovate the forms of archive utilization services, and promote the integration of online and offline archive inquiry and utilization services.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织以及公民利用国家档案馆保管的未开放的档案，应当经保管该档案的国家档案馆同意，必要时，国家档案馆应当征得档案形成单位或者移交单位同意。  
机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的档案机构保管的尚未向国家档案馆移交的档案，其他机关、团体、企业事业单位以及公民需要利用的，应当经档案形成单位或者保管单位同意。

**Article 34** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations, as well as citizens, who wish to utilize closed archives under the custody of a state archive shall obtain the consent of the state archive that preserves such archives. Where necessary, the state archive shall obtain the consent of the archive-forming unit or the transferring unit.  
For archives under the custody of the archival institution of a state organ, organization, enterprise, public institution, or other organization that have not yet been transferred to a state archive, if other state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, or citizens need to utilize them, they shall obtain the consent of the archive-forming unit or the custodian unit.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　[《档案法》第三十二条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-32)所称档案的公布，是指通过下列形式首次向社会公开档案的全部或者部分原文：  
（一）通过报纸、期刊、图书、音像制品、电子出版物等公开出版；  
（二）通过电台、电视台、计算机信息网络等公开传播；  
（三）在公开场合宣读、播放；  
（四）公开出售、散发或者张贴档案复制件；  
（五）在展览、展示中公开陈列。

**Article 35** The publication of archives as mentioned in Article 32 of the Archives Law means the first disclosure to the society of the whole or part of the original text of archives through the following forms:  
(1) publication through newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic publications, etc.;  
(2) dissemination through radio, television, computer information networks, etc.;  
(3) reading aloud or playing in public places;  
(4) selling, distributing, or posting reproductions of archives in public;  
(5) displaying in exhibitions or shows in public.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　公布属于国家所有的档案，按照下列规定办理：  
（一）保存在档案馆的，由档案馆公布；必要时，应当征得档案形成单位或者移交单位同意后公布，或者报经档案形成单位或者移交单位的上级主管部门同意后公布；  
（二）保存在各单位档案机构的，由各单位公布；必要时，应当报经其上级主管部门同意后公布；  
（三）利用属于国家所有的档案的单位和个人，未经档案馆或者有关单位同意，均无权公布档案。  
档案馆对寄存档案的公布，应当按照约定办理；没有约定的，应当征得档案所有者的同意。

**Article 36** The publication of archives owned by the State shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) archives preserved in an archive shall be published by the archive; where necessary, the archive shall publish after obtaining the consent of the archive-forming unit or the transferring unit, or after reporting to and obtaining the consent of the superior competent department of the archive-forming unit or the transferring unit;  
(2) archives preserved in the archival institution of a unit shall be published by that unit; where necessary, the unit shall publish after reporting to and obtaining the consent of its superior competent department;  
(3) units and individuals utilizing archives owned by the State shall have no right to publish the archives without the consent of the archive or the relevant unit.  
The publication of deposited archives by an archive shall be handled in accordance with the agreement; if there is no agreement, the archive shall obtain the consent of the archive owner.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家档案馆应当根据工作需要和社会需求，开展馆藏档案的开发利用和公布，促进档案文献出版物、档案文化创意产品等的提供和传播。  
国家鼓励和支持其他各类档案馆向社会开放和公布馆藏档案，促进档案资源的社会共享。

**Article 37** State archives shall, in accordance with work needs and social demands, carry out the development and utilization and publication of archives in their custody, and promote the provision and dissemination of archival literature publications, archival cultural creative products, etc.  
The State encourages and supports other types of archives to open and publish their archives to the society, and promote the social sharing of archival resources.

### 第五章　档案信息化建设

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当加强档案信息化建设，积极推进电子档案管理信息系统建设。  
机关、群团组织、国有企业事业单位应当将档案信息化建设纳入本单位信息化建设规划，加强办公自动化系统、业务系统归档功能建设，并与电子档案管理信息系统相互衔接，实现对电子档案的全过程管理。  
电子档案管理信息系统应当按照国家有关规定建设，并符合国家关于网络安全、数据安全以及保密等的规定。

**Article 38** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall strengthen the informatization construction of archives, and actively promote the construction of electronic archive management information systems.  
State organs, mass organizations, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions shall incorporate the informatization construction of archives into their own informatization construction plans, strengthen the construction of filing functions in office automation systems and business systems, and interconnect them with electronic archive management information systems to achieve full-process management of electronic archives.  
Electronic archive management information systems shall be constructed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall comply with the provisions of the State on network security, data security, and confidentiality.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当采取管理措施和技术手段保证电子档案来源可靠、程序规范、要素合规，符合以下条件：  
（一）形成者、形成活动、形成时间可确认，形成、办理、整理、归档、保管、移交等系统安全可靠；  
（二）全过程管理符合有关规定，并准确记录、可追溯；  
（三）内容、结构、背景信息和管理过程信息等构成要素符合规范要求。

**Article 39** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall adopt management measures and technical means to ensure that electronic archives are reliable in source, standardized in procedures, and compliant in elements, meeting the following conditions:  
(1) the creator, creation activity, and creation time can be confirmed, and the systems for creation, handling, arrangement, filing, preservation, and transfer are secure and reliable;  
(2) the full-process management complies with relevant provisions, and records are accurate and traceable;  
(3) the constituent elements such as content, structure, background information, and management process information comply with the requirements of the specifications.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当按照国家档案主管部门有关规定，定期向有关档案馆移交电子档案。电子档案移交接收网络以及系统环境应当符合国家关于网络安全、数据安全以及保密等的规定。不具备在线移交条件的，应当通过符合安全管理要求的存储介质向档案馆移交电子档案。  
档案馆应当在接收电子档案时进行真实性、完整性、可用性和安全性等方面的检测，并采取管理措施和技术手段保证电子档案在长期保存过程中的真实性、完整性、可用性和安全性。  
国家档案馆可以为未到[本条例第二十条](#art-20)第二款所规定的移交进馆期限的电子档案提供保管服务，涉及政府信息公开事项的，依照[《档案法》第十五条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国档案法.md#art-15)第二款的规定办理。

**Article 40** State organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the national archives administrative department, transfer electronic archives to the relevant archives on a regular basis. The network and system environment for the transfer and reception of electronic archives shall comply with the provisions of the State on network security, data security, and confidentiality. Where online transfer conditions are not available, electronic archives shall be transferred to the archive through storage media that meet the requirements of security management.  
An archive shall, when receiving electronic archives, conduct tests on authenticity, integrity, usability, and security, and adopt management measures and technical means to ensure the authenticity, integrity, usability, and security of electronic archives during long-term preservation.  
State archives may provide preservation services for electronic archives that have not yet reached the time limit for transfer to archives as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 20 of these Regulations. Matters involving the disclosure of government information shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 15 of the Archives Law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　档案馆对重要电子档案进行异地备份保管，应当采用磁介质、光介质、缩微胶片等符合安全管理要求的存储介质，定期检测载体的完好程度和数据的可读性。异地备份选址应当满足安全保密等要求。  
档案馆可以根据需要建设灾难备份系统，实现重要电子档案及其管理系统的备份与灾难恢复。

**Article 41** When conducting off-site backup preservation of important electronic archives, archives shall adopt storage media that meet the requirements of security management, such as magnetic media, optical media, and microfiche, and regularly test the integrity of the carriers and the readability of the data. The location for off-site backup shall meet the requirements of security and confidentiality.  
Archives may, as needed, build disaster backup systems to achieve backup and disaster recovery of important electronic archives and their management systems.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织开展传统载体档案数字化工作，应当符合国家档案主管部门有关规定，保证档案数字化成果的质量和安全。  
国家鼓励有条件的单位开展文字、语音、图像识别工作，加强档案资源深度挖掘和开发利用。

**Article 42** When carrying out digitization of traditional carrier archives, archives and state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall comply with the relevant provisions of the national archives administrative department, and ensure the quality and security of the digitized results of archives.  
The State encourages units with the capacity to carry out character, speech, and image recognition work, and to strengthen the in-depth mining and development and utilization of archival resources.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　档案馆应当积极创造条件，按照国家有关规定建设、运行维护数字档案馆，为不同网络环境中的档案数字资源的收集、长期安全保存和有效利用提供保障。  
国家鼓励有条件的机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织开展数字档案室建设，提升本单位的档案信息化水平。

**Article 43** Archives shall actively create conditions to build, operate, and maintain digital archives in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and provide guarantees for the collection, long-term safe preservation, and effective utilization of digital archival resources in different network environments.  
The State encourages state organs, organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations with the capacity to build digital archive rooms, and to enhance the level of archival informatization of their units.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家档案主管部门应当制定数据共享标准，提升档案信息共享服务水平，促进全国档案数字资源跨区域、跨层级、跨部门共享利用工作。  
县级以上地方档案主管部门应当推进本行政区域档案数字资源共享利用工作。

**Article 44** The national archives administrative department shall formulate data sharing standards, improve the level of archive information sharing services, and promote the cross-regional, cross-level, and cross-departmental sharing and utilization of national digital archive resources.  
The local archives administrative departments at or above the county level shall promote the sharing and utilization of digital archive resources within their respective administrative regions.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家档案馆和机关、群团组织、国有企业事业单位应当定期向同级档案主管部门报送本单位档案工作情况。

**Article 45** State archives and state organs, mass organizations, and state-owned enterprises and public institutions shall periodically report the archive work of their units to the archives administrative department at the same level.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　档案主管部门对处理投诉、举报和监督检查中发现的或者有关部门移送的涉嫌档案违法的线索和案件，应当及时依法组织调查。  
经调查，发现有档案违法行为的，档案主管部门应当依法予以处理。需要追究有关责任人责任的，档案主管部门可以依法向其所在单位或者任免机关、单位提出处理建议。有关机关、单位应当及时将处理结果书面告知提出处理建议的档案主管部门。

**Article 46** The archives administrative department shall, in a timely manner, organize investigations according to law into clues and cases involving suspected illegal archival acts discovered during the handling of complaints, reports, and supervision and inspection, or transferred by relevant departments.  
After investigation, if an illegal archival act is found, the archives administrative department shall handle it according to law. If it is necessary to pursue the liability of the responsible person, the archives administrative department may, according to law, make a suggestion for handling to the unit where the person works or the organ or unit with the power of appointment and removal. The relevant organ or unit shall promptly inform the archives administrative department that made the suggestion in writing of the handling result.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上档案主管部门应当加强档案行政执法队伍建设和对档案行政执法人员的教育培训。从事档案行政执法工作的人员，应当通过考试，取得行政执法证件。

**Article 47** The archives administrative departments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of archive administrative law enforcement teams and the education and training of archive administrative law enforcement personnel. Personnel engaged in archive administrative law enforcement work shall pass examinations and obtain administrative law enforcement certificates.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家档案馆违反国家规定擅自扩大或者缩小档案接收范围的，或者不按照国家规定开放、提供利用档案的，由县级以上档案主管部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，由有关机关对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 48** Where a state archive, in violation of state provisions, expands or narrows the scope of archive reception without authorization, or fails to open or provide access to archives in accordance with state provisions, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit. If the circumstances are serious, the relevant organ shall impose a sanction on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　单位或者个人将应当归档的材料据为己有，拒绝交档案机构、档案工作人员归档的，或者不按照国家规定向国家档案馆移交档案的，由县级以上档案主管部门责令限期改正；拒不改正的，由有关机关对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 49** Where a unit or individual keeps for themselves materials that shall be filed and refuses to hand them over to the archival institution or archival personnel for filing, or fails to transfer archives to a state archive in accordance with state provisions, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit. If it refuses to make corrections, the relevant organ shall impose a sanction on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　单位或者个人侵占、挪用国家档案馆的馆舍的，由县级以上档案主管部门责令限期改正；情节严重的，由有关机关对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 50** Where a unit or individual occupies or misappropriates the premises of a state archive, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit. If the circumstances are serious, the relevant organ shall impose a sanction on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law. If it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If property losses or other damages are caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　档案服务企业在提供服务过程中明知存在档案安全隐患而不采取措施的，档案主管部门可以采取约谈、责令限期改正等措施。  
档案服务企业因违反《档案法》和本条例规定受到行政处罚的，行政处罚信息依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以公示。

**Article 51** Where an archive service enterprise, in the course of providing services, knows that there is a potential archive safety risk but fails to take measures, the archives administrative department may take measures such as holding a talk and ordering it to make corrections within a time limit.  
Where an archive service enterprise has received an administrative penalty for violating the Archives Law and these Regulations, the information on the administrative penalty shall be publicized in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自2024年3月1日起施行。《中华人民共和国档案法实施办法》同时废止。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2024. The Measures for the Implementation of the Archives Law of the People's Republic of China shall be abolished simultaneously.
