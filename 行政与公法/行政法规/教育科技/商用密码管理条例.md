# 商用密码管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-04-27  
**生效日期**：2023-07-01

> （1999年10月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第273号发布　2023年4月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第760号修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范商用密码应用和管理，鼓励和促进商用密码产业发展，保障网络与信息安全，维护国家安全和社会公共利益，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国密码法》等法律，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的商用密码科研、生产、销售、服务、检测、认证、进出口、应用等活动及监督管理，适用本条例。 本条例所称商用密码，是指采用特定变换的方法对不属于国家秘密的信息等进行加密保护、安全认证的技术、产品和服务。 第三条　坚持中国共产党对商用密码工作的领导，贯彻落实总体国家安全观。国家密码管理部门负责管理全国的商用密码工作。县级以上地方各级密码管理部门负责管理本行政区域的商用密码工作。 网信、商务、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内负责商用密码有关管理工作。 第四条　国家加强商用密码人才培养，建立健全商用密码人才发展体制机制和人才评价制度，鼓励和支持密码相关学科和专业建设，规范商用密码社会化培训，促进商用密码人才交流。 第五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式加强商用密码宣传教育，增强公民、法人和其他组织的密码安全意识。 第六条　商用密码领域的学会、行业协会等社会组织依照法律、行政法规及其章程的规定，开展学术交流、政策研究、公共服务等活动，加强学术和行业自律，推动诚信建设，促进行业健康发展。 密码管理部门应当加强对商用密码领域社会组织的指导和支持。 第二章　科技创新与标准化 第七条　国家建立健全商用密码科学技术创新促进机制，支持商用密码科学技术自主创新，对作出突出贡献的组织和个人按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。 国家依法保护商用密码领域的知识产权。从事商用密码活动，应当增强知识产权意识，提高运用、保护和管理知识产权的能力。 国家鼓励在外商投资过程中基于自愿原则和商业规则开展商用密码技术合作。行政机关及其工作人员不得利用行政手段强制转让商用密码技术。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范商用密码应用和管理，鼓励和促进商用密码产业发展，保障网络与信息安全，维护国家安全和社会公共利益，保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国密码法》等法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Cryptography Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws, for the purpose of regulating the application and administration of commercial cryptography, encouraging and promoting the development of the commercial cryptography industry, ensuring network and information security, safeguarding national security and public interests, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内的商用密码科研、生产、销售、服务、检测、认证、进出口、应用等活动及监督管理，适用本条例。  
本条例所称商用密码，是指采用特定变换的方法对不属于国家秘密的信息等进行加密保护、安全认证的技术、产品和服务。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to activities such as research, production, sale, service, testing, certification, import and export, and application of commercial cryptography, as well as the supervision and administration thereof, within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
"Commercial cryptography" as used in these Regulations refers to technologies, products, and services that employ specific transformation methods to provide encryption protection and security authentication for information that does not constitute state secrets.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　坚持中国共产党对商用密码工作的领导，贯彻落实总体国家安全观。国家密码管理部门负责管理全国的商用密码工作。县级以上地方各级密码管理部门负责管理本行政区域的商用密码工作。  
网信、商务、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内负责商用密码有关管理工作。

**Article 3** The leadership of the Communist Party of China over commercial cryptography work shall be upheld, and the holistic approach to national security shall be implemented. The national cryptography administration department shall be responsible for administering commercial cryptography nationwide. The cryptography administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for administering commercial cryptography within their respective administrative regions.  
The cyberspace administration, commerce, customs, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments shall be responsible for the administration of commercial cryptography-related work within their respective functions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家加强商用密码人才培养，建立健全商用密码人才发展体制机制和人才评价制度，鼓励和支持密码相关学科和专业建设，规范商用密码社会化培训，促进商用密码人才交流。

**Article 4** The State shall strengthen the cultivation of commercial cryptography professionals, establish and improve the systems and mechanisms for the development of commercial cryptography professionals and the professional evaluation system, encourage and support the development of cryptography-related disciplines and specialties, regulate commercial cryptography social training, and promote the exchange of commercial cryptography professionals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当采取多种形式加强商用密码宣传教育，增强公民、法人和其他组织的密码安全意识。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall adopt various forms to strengthen publicity and education on commercial cryptography, and enhance the cryptographic security awareness of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　商用密码领域的学会、行业协会等社会组织依照法律、行政法规及其章程的规定，开展学术交流、政策研究、公共服务等活动，加强学术和行业自律，推动诚信建设，促进行业健康发展。  
密码管理部门应当加强对商用密码领域社会组织的指导和支持。

**Article 6** Social organizations such as societies and industry associations in the field of commercial cryptography shall carry out activities such as academic exchanges, policy research, and public services in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and their articles of association, strengthen academic and industry self-discipline, promote integrity building, and facilitate the healthy development of the industry.  
Cryptography administration departments shall strengthen guidance and support for social organizations in the field of commercial cryptography.

### 第二章　科技创新与标准化

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立健全商用密码科学技术创新促进机制，支持商用密码科学技术自主创新，对作出突出贡献的组织和个人按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。  
国家依法保护商用密码领域的知识产权。从事商用密码活动，应当增强知识产权意识，提高运用、保护和管理知识产权的能力。  
国家鼓励在外商投资过程中基于自愿原则和商业规则开展商用密码技术合作。行政机关及其工作人员不得利用行政手段强制转让商用密码技术。

**Article 7** The State shall establish and improve a mechanism for promoting scientific and technological innovation in commercial cryptography, support independent innovation in commercial cryptography science and technology, and commend and reward organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
The State shall protect intellectual property in the field of commercial cryptography according to law. Entities engaged in commercial cryptography activities shall enhance their awareness of intellectual property and improve their ability to use, protect, and manage intellectual property.  
The State shall encourage technical cooperation in commercial cryptography in the process of foreign investment based on the principle of voluntariness and commercial rules. Administrative organs and their staff shall not use administrative means to compel the transfer of commercial cryptography technology.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持商用密码科学技术成果转化和产业化应用，建立和完善商用密码科学技术成果信息汇交、发布和应用情况反馈机制。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support the transformation and industrial application of scientific and technological achievements in commercial cryptography, and establish and improve a mechanism for the collection, release, and feedback on the application of scientific and technological achievements in commercial cryptography.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家密码管理部门组织对法律、行政法规和国家有关规定要求使用商用密码进行保护的网络与信息系统所使用的密码算法、密码协议、密钥管理机制等商用密码技术进行审查鉴定。

**Article 9** The national cryptography administration department shall organize the review and evaluation of commercial cryptography technologies, including cryptographic algorithms, cryptographic protocols, and key management mechanisms, used in networks and information systems that are required by laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions to be protected with commercial cryptography.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院标准化行政主管部门和国家密码管理部门依据各自职责，组织制定商用密码国家标准、行业标准，对商用密码团体标准的制定进行规范、引导和监督。国家密码管理部门依据职责，建立商用密码标准实施信息反馈和评估机制，对商用密码标准实施进行监督检查。  
国家推动参与商用密码国际标准化活动，参与制定商用密码国际标准，推进商用密码中国标准与国外标准之间的转化运用，鼓励企业、社会团体和教育、科研机构等参与商用密码国际标准化活动。  
其他领域的标准涉及商用密码的，应当与商用密码国家标准、行业标准保持协调。

**Article 10** The standardization administrative department of the State Council and the national cryptography administration department shall, in accordance with their respective functions, organize the formulation of national standards and industry standards for commercial cryptography, and regulate, guide, and supervise the formulation of group standards for commercial cryptography. The national cryptography administration department shall, according to its functions, establish a mechanism for feedback and evaluation of the implementation of commercial cryptography standards, and supervise and inspect the implementation of commercial cryptography standards.  
The State shall promote participation in international standardization activities for commercial cryptography, participate in the formulation of international standards for commercial cryptography, advance the transformation and application of Chinese standards for commercial cryptography and foreign standards, and encourage enterprises, social groups, and educational and research institutions to participate in international standardization activities for commercial cryptography.  
Where standards in other fields involve commercial cryptography, they shall be coordinated with national standards and industry standards for commercial cryptography.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　从事商用密码活动，应当符合有关法律、行政法规、商用密码强制性国家标准，以及自我声明公开标准的技术要求。  
国家鼓励在商用密码活动中采用商用密码推荐性国家标准、行业标准，提升商用密码的防护能力，维护用户的合法权益。

**Article 11** Entities engaged in commercial cryptography activities shall comply with the technical requirements of relevant laws, administrative regulations, mandatory national standards for commercial cryptography, and self-declared public standards.  
The State shall encourage the adoption of recommended national standards and industry standards for commercial cryptography in commercial cryptography activities, to enhance the protection capability of commercial cryptography and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of users.

### 第三章　检测认证

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家推进商用密码检测认证体系建设，鼓励在商用密码活动中自愿接受商用密码检测认证。

**Article 12** The State shall advance the construction of a commercial cryptography testing and certification system, and encourage voluntary acceptance of commercial cryptography testing and certification in commercial cryptography activities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事商用密码产品检测、网络与信息系统商用密码应用安全性评估等商用密码检测活动，向社会出具具有证明作用的数据、结果的机构，应当经国家密码管理部门认定，依法取得商用密码检测机构资质。

**Article 13** Institutions that engage in commercial cryptography testing activities such as product testing and security assessment of commercial cryptography applications in networks and information systems, and issue data or results with evidentiary effect to the public, shall be recognized by the national cryptography administration department and obtain qualification as a commercial cryptography testing institution according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　取得商用密码检测机构资质，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）具有法人资格；  
（二）具有与从事商用密码检测活动相适应的资金、场所、设备设施、专业人员和专业能力；  
（三）具有保证商用密码检测活动有效运行的管理体系。

**Article 14** To obtain the qualification of a commercial cryptography testing institution, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having the funds, premises, equipment and facilities, professional personnel, and professional capabilities commensurate with the commercial cryptography testing activities;  
(3) having a management system that ensures the effective operation of commercial cryptography testing activities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请商用密码检测机构资质，应当向国家密码管理部门提出书面申请，并提交符合[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定条件的材料。  
国家密码管理部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，对申请进行审查，并依法作出是否准予认定的决定。  
需要对申请人进行技术评审的，技术评审所需时间不计算在本条规定的期限内。国家密码管理部门应当将所需时间书面告知申请人。

**Article 15** An applicant for the qualification of a commercial cryptography testing institution shall submit a written application to the national cryptography administration department, together with materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions set forth in Article 14 of these Regulations.  
The national cryptography administration department shall, within 20 working days from the date of acceptance of the application, review the application and make a decision on whether to grant recognition according to law.  
Where a technical review of the applicant is required, the time required for the technical review shall not be counted within the time limit specified in this Article. The national cryptography administration department shall notify the applicant in writing of the time required.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　商用密码检测机构应当按照法律、行政法规和商用密码检测技术规范、规则，在批准范围内独立、公正、科学、诚信地开展商用密码检测，对出具的检测数据、结果负责，并定期向国家密码管理部门报送检测实施情况。  
商用密码检测技术规范、规则由国家密码管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 16** Commercial cryptography testing institutions shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and commercial cryptography testing technical specifications and rules, carry out commercial cryptography testing independently, impartially, scientifically, and in good faith within the approved scope, be responsible for the test data and results they issue, and periodically report the implementation of testing to the national cryptography administration department.  
The commercial cryptography testing technical specifications and rules shall be formulated and promulgated by the national cryptography administration department.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院市场监督管理部门会同国家密码管理部门建立国家统一推行的商用密码认证制度，实行商用密码产品、服务、管理体系认证，制定并公布认证目录和技术规范、规则。

**Article 17** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council, together with the national cryptography administration department, shall establish a nationally unified commercial cryptography certification system, implement certification of commercial cryptography products, services, and management systems, and formulate and publish the certification catalog and technical specifications and rules.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事商用密码认证活动的机构，应当依法取得商用密码认证机构资质。  
申请商用密码认证机构资质，应当向国务院市场监督管理部门提出书面申请。申请人除应当符合法律、行政法规和国家有关规定要求的认证机构基本条件外，还应当具有与从事商用密码认证活动相适应的检测、检查等技术能力。  
国务院市场监督管理部门在审查商用密码认证机构资质申请时，应当征求国家密码管理部门的意见。

**Article 18** Institutions engaged in commercial cryptography certification activities shall obtain the qualification of a commercial cryptography certification institution according to law.  
An applicant for the qualification of a commercial cryptography certification institution shall submit a written application to the market supervision and administration department of the State Council. In addition to meeting the basic conditions for certification institutions required by law, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, the applicant shall also have the technical capabilities such as testing and inspection commensurate with the commercial cryptography certification activities.  
When reviewing the application for the qualification of a commercial cryptography certification institution, the market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall seek the opinion of the national cryptography administration department.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　商用密码认证机构应当按照法律、行政法规和商用密码认证技术规范、规则，在批准范围内独立、公正、科学、诚信地开展商用密码认证，对出具的认证结论负责。  
商用密码认证机构应当对其认证的商用密码产品、服务、管理体系实施有效的跟踪调查，以保证通过认证的商用密码产品、服务、管理体系持续符合认证要求。

**Article 19** Commercial cryptography certification institutions shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and commercial cryptography certification technical specifications and rules, carry out commercial cryptography certification independently, impartially, scientifically, and in good faith within the approved scope, and be responsible for the certification conclusions they issue.  
Commercial cryptography certification institutions shall implement effective follow-up investigations on the commercial cryptography products, services, and management systems they have certified, so as to ensure that the certified commercial cryptography products, services, and management systems continue to meet the certification requirements.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　涉及国家安全、国计民生、社会公共利益的商用密码产品，应当依法列入网络关键设备和网络安全专用产品目录，由具备资格的商用密码检测、认证机构检测认证合格后，方可销售或者提供。

**Article 20** Commercial cryptography products that involve national security, the national economy and people's livelihood, or public interests shall be included in the catalog of network key equipment and network security-specific products according to law, and shall be sold or provided only after having passed testing and certification by qualified commercial cryptography testing and certification institutions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　商用密码服务使用网络关键设备和网络安全专用产品的，应当经商用密码认证机构对该商用密码服务认证合格。

**Article 21** Commercial encryption services that use critical network equipment and cybersecurity-specific products shall pass certification by a commercial encryption certification body for such commercial encryption services.

### 第四章　电子认证

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　采用商用密码技术提供电子认证服务，应当具有与使用密码相适应的场所、设备设施、专业人员、专业能力和管理体系，依法取得国家密码管理部门同意使用密码的证明文件。

**Article 22** Entities that use commercial encryption technologies to provide electronic certification services shall have premises, equipment and facilities, professional personnel, professional capabilities, and management systems commensurate with the use of encryption, and shall obtain, according to law, certification documents from the national Cryptography Administration approving the use of encryption.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电子认证服务机构应当按照法律、行政法规和电子认证服务密码使用技术规范、规则，使用密码提供电子认证服务，保证其电子认证服务密码使用持续符合要求。  
电子认证服务密码使用技术规范、规则由国家密码管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 23** Electronic certification service institutions shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the technical specifications and rules for the use of encryption in electronic certification services, use encryption to provide electronic certification services, and ensure that their use of encryption for electronic certification services continuously meets requirements.  
The technical specifications and rules for the use of encryption in electronic certification services shall be formulated and published by the national Cryptography Administration.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　采用商用密码技术从事电子政务电子认证服务的机构，应当经国家密码管理部门认定，依法取得电子政务电子认证服务机构资质。

**Article 24** Institutions that use commercial encryption technologies to engage in e-government electronic certification services shall be recognized by the national Cryptography Administration and shall obtain, according to law, the qualifications of an e-government electronic certification service institution.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　取得电子政务电子认证服务机构资质，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）具有企业法人或者事业单位法人资格；  
（二）具有与从事电子政务电子认证服务活动及其使用密码相适应的资金、场所、设备设施和专业人员；  
（三）具有为政务活动提供长期电子政务电子认证服务的能力；  
（四）具有保证电子政务电子认证服务活动及其使用密码安全运行的管理体系。

**Article 25** To obtain the qualifications of an e-government electronic certification service institution, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) Having the legal person status of an enterprise legal person or a public institution legal person;  
(2) Having funds, premises, equipment and facilities, and professional personnel commensurate with engaging in e-government electronic certification service activities and the use of encryption;  
(3) Having the capability to provide long-term e-government electronic certification services for government activities;  
(4) Having a management system to ensure the safe operation of e-government electronic certification service activities and the use of encryption.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请电子政务电子认证服务机构资质，应当向国家密码管理部门提出书面申请，并提交符合[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)规定条件的材料。  
国家密码管理部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内，对申请进行审查，并依法作出是否准予认定的决定。  
需要对申请人进行技术评审的，技术评审所需时间不计算在本条规定的期限内。国家密码管理部门应当将所需时间书面告知申请人。

**Article 26** To apply for the qualifications of an e-government electronic certification service institution, a written application shall be submitted to the national Cryptography Administration, together with materials that meet the conditions specified in Article 25 of these Regulations.  
The national Cryptography Administration shall, within 20 working days from the date of acceptance of the application, review the application and make a decision on whether to grant recognition according to law.  
If a technical evaluation of the applicant is required, the time necessary for the technical evaluation shall not be counted within the time limit specified in this Article. The national Cryptography Administration shall inform the applicant in writing of the time required.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　外商投资电子政务电子认证服务，影响或者可能影响国家安全的，应当依法进行外商投资安全审查。

**Article 27** Where foreign investment in e-government electronic certification services affects or may affect national security, a national security review of the foreign investment shall be conducted according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　电子政务电子认证服务机构应当按照法律、行政法规和电子政务电子认证服务技术规范、规则，在批准范围内提供电子政务电子认证服务，并定期向主要办事机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市密码管理部门报送服务实施情况。  
电子政务电子认证服务技术规范、规则由国家密码管理部门制定并公布。

**Article 28** E-government electronic certification service institutions shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the technical specifications and rules for e-government electronic certification services, provide e-government electronic certification services within the approved scope, and shall regularly report the implementation of services to the cryptograph administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where their main office is located.  
The technical specifications and rules for e-government electronic certification services shall be formulated and published by the national Cryptography Administration.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家建立统一的电子认证信任机制。国家密码管理部门负责电子认证信任源的规划和管理，会同有关部门推动电子认证服务互信互认。

**Article 29** The state shall establish a unified electronic certification trust mechanism. The national Cryptography Administration shall be responsible for the planning and management of electronic certification trust sources, and shall, jointly with relevant departments, promote mutual trust and mutual recognition of electronic certification services.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　密码管理部门会同有关部门负责政务活动中使用电子签名、数据电文的管理。  
政务活动中电子签名、电子印章、电子证照等涉及的电子认证服务，应当由依法设立的电子政务电子认证服务机构提供。

**Article 30** The Cryptography Administration shall, jointly with relevant departments, be responsible for the management of the use of electronic signatures and data messages in government activities.  
Electronic certification services involved in electronic signatures, electronic seals, electronic certificates, etc., in government activities shall be provided by e-government electronic certification service institutions established according to law.

### 第五章　进出口

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　涉及国家安全、社会公共利益且具有加密保护功能的商用密码，列入商用密码进口许可清单，实施进口许可。涉及国家安全、社会公共利益或者中国承担国际义务的商用密码，列入商用密码出口管制清单，实施出口管制。  
商用密码进口许可清单和商用密码出口管制清单由国务院商务主管部门会同国家密码管理部门和海关总署制定并公布。  
大众消费类产品所采用的商用密码不实行进口许可和出口管制制度。

**Article 31** Commercial encryption that involves national security and public interests and has encryption protection functions shall be included in the commercial encryption import licensing list and subject to import licensing. Commercial encryption that involves national security, public interests, or China's international obligations shall be included in the commercial encryption export control list and subject to export control.  
The commercial encryption import licensing list and the commercial encryption export control list shall be formulated and published by the competent department of commerce of the State Council jointly with the national Cryptography Administration and the General Administration of Customs.  
The commercial encryption used in mass consumer products shall not be subject to the import licensing and export control systems.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　进口商用密码进口许可清单中的商用密码或者出口商用密码出口管制清单中的商用密码，应当向国务院商务主管部门申请领取进出口许可证。  
商用密码的过境、转运、通运、再出口，在境外与综合保税区等海关特殊监管区域之间进出，或者在境外与出口监管仓库、保税物流中心等保税监管场所之间进出的，适用前款规定。

**Article 32** To import commercial encryption included in the commercial encryption import licensing list or export commercial encryption included in the commercial encryption export control list, an import or export license shall be applied for from the competent department of commerce of the State Council.  
The preceding paragraph shall apply to the transit, transshipment, through shipment, and re-export of commercial encryption, as well as the movement between overseas areas and special customs supervision areas such as comprehensive bonded zones, or between overseas areas and bonded supervision sites such as export supervised warehouses and bonded logistics centers.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　进口商用密码进口许可清单中的商用密码或者出口商用密码出口管制清单中的商用密码时，应当向海关交验进出口许可证，并按照国家有关规定办理报关手续。  
进出口经营者未向海关交验进出口许可证，海关有证据表明进出口产品可能属于商用密码进口许可清单或者出口管制清单范围的，应当向进出口经营者提出质疑；海关可以向国务院商务主管部门提出组织鉴别，并根据国务院商务主管部门会同国家密码管理部门作出的鉴别结论依法处置。在鉴别或者质疑期间，海关对进出口产品不予放行。

**Article 33** When importing commercial encryption included in the commercial encryption import licensing list or exporting commercial encryption included in the commercial encryption export control list, the import or export license shall be presented to the customs, and customs declaration procedures shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
If an import or export operator fails to present the import or export license to the customs, and the customs has evidence indicating that the imported or exported product may fall within the scope of the commercial encryption import licensing list or export control list, the customs shall raise a question to the import or export operator; the customs may propose to the competent department of commerce of the State Council to organize an identification, and shall handle the matter according to law based on the identification conclusion made by the competent department of commerce of the State Council jointly with the national Cryptography Administration. During the identification or questioning period, the customs shall not release the imported or exported product.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　申请商用密码进出口许可，应当向国务院商务主管部门提出书面申请，并提交下列材料：  
（一）申请人的法定代表人、主要经营管理人以及经办人的身份证明；  
（二）合同或者协议的副本；  
（三）商用密码的技术说明；  
（四）最终用户和最终用途证明；  
（五）国务院商务主管部门规定提交的其他文件。  
国务院商务主管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内，会同国家密码管理部门对申请进行审查，并依法作出是否准予许可的决定。  
对国家安全、社会公共利益或者外交政策有重大影响的商用密码出口，由国务院商务主管部门会同国家密码管理部门等有关部门报国务院批准。报国务院批准的，不受前款规定时限的限制。

**Article 34** To apply for a commercial encryption import or export license, a written application shall be submitted to the competent department of commerce of the State Council, together with the following materials:  
(1) Identity documents of the applicant's legal representative, main management personnel, and handling personnel;  
(2) A copy of the contract or agreement;  
(3) A technical description of the commercial encryption;  
(4) Proof of end user and end use;  
(5) Other documents required to be submitted by the competent department of commerce of the State Council.  
The competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, within 45 working days from the date of acceptance of the application, jointly with the national Cryptography Administration review the application and make a decision on whether to grant the license according to law.  
For the export of commercial encryption that has a significant impact on national security, public interests, or foreign policy, the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, jointly with the national Cryptography Administration and other relevant departments, report to the State Council for approval. For matters reported to the State Council for approval, the time limit specified in the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

### 第六章　应用促进

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法使用商用密码保护网络与信息安全，鼓励使用经检测认证合格的商用密码。  
任何组织或者个人不得窃取他人加密保护的信息或者非法侵入他人的商用密码保障系统，不得利用商用密码从事危害国家安全、社会公共利益、他人合法权益等违法犯罪活动。

**Article 35** The state encourages citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to use commercial encryption according to law to protect network and information security, and encourages the use of commercial encryption that has passed testing and certification.  
No organization or individual may steal another person's encrypted information or illegally invade another person's commercial encryption security system, nor may they use commercial encryption to engage in illegal or criminal activities that endanger national security, public interests, or the legitimate rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家支持网络产品和服务使用商用密码提升安全性，支持并规范商用密码在信息领域新技术、新业态、新模式中的应用。

**Article 36** The state supports the use of commercial encryption in network products and services to enhance security, and supports and regulates the application of commercial encryption in new technologies, new business forms, and new models in the information field.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家建立商用密码应用促进协调机制，加强对商用密码应用的统筹指导。国家机关和涉及商用密码工作的单位在其职责范围内负责本机关、本单位或者本系统的商用密码应用和安全保障工作。  
密码管理部门会同有关部门加强商用密码应用信息收集、风险评估、信息通报和重大事项会商，并加强与网络安全监测预警和信息通报的衔接。

**Article 37** The state shall establish a coordination mechanism for promoting the application of commercial encryption to strengthen overall guidance on the application of commercial encryption. State organs and units involved in commercial encryption work shall, within their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the application and security assurance of commercial encryption in their own organs, units, or systems.  
The Cryptography Administration shall, jointly with relevant departments, strengthen the collection of information, risk assessment, information notification, and consultation on major matters regarding the application of commercial encryption, and shall strengthen the connection with network security monitoring, early warning, and information notification.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　法律、行政法规和国家有关规定要求使用商用密码进行保护的关键信息基础设施，其运营者应当使用商用密码进行保护，制定商用密码应用方案，配备必要的资金和专业人员，同步规划、同步建设、同步运行商用密码保障系统，自行或者委托商用密码检测机构开展商用密码应用安全性评估。  
前款所列关键信息基础设施通过商用密码应用安全性评估方可投入运行，运行后每年至少进行一次评估，评估情况按照国家有关规定报送国家密码管理部门或者关键信息基础设施所在地省、自治区、直辖市密码管理部门备案。

**Article 38** For critical information infrastructure that is required by laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions to be protected using commercial encryption, its operators shall use commercial encryption for protection, formulate commercial encryption application plans, allocate necessary funds and professional personnel, plan, construct, and operate the commercial encryption security system simultaneously, and conduct security assessments of commercial encryption application by themselves or entrust commercial encryption testing institutions to do so.  
The critical information infrastructure listed in the preceding paragraph may be put into operation only after passing the commercial encryption application security assessment, and shall undergo at least one assessment each year after operation. The assessment results shall be reported to the national Cryptography Administration or the cryptograph administration of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the critical information infrastructure is located for filing according to relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　法律、行政法规和国家有关规定要求使用商用密码进行保护的关键信息基础设施，使用的商用密码产品、服务应当经检测认证合格，使用的密码算法、密码协议、密钥管理机制等商用密码技术应当通过国家密码管理部门审查鉴定。

**Article 39** For critical information infrastructure that is required by laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions to be protected using commercial encryption, the commercial encryption products and services used shall pass testing and certification, and the commercial encryption technologies used, such as encryption algorithms, encryption protocols, and key management mechanisms, shall pass the review and evaluation of the national Cryptography Administration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　关键信息基础设施的运营者采购涉及商用密码的网络产品和服务，可能影响国家安全的，应当依法通过国家网信部门会同国家密码管理部门等有关部门组织的国家安全审查。

**Article 40** If an operator of critical information infrastructure procures network products and services involving commercial encryption that may affect national security, it shall pass the national security review organized by the national cyberspace administration jointly with the national Cryptography Administration and other relevant departments according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　网络运营者应当按照国家网络安全等级保护制度要求，使用商用密码保护网络安全。国家密码管理部门根据网络的安全保护等级，确定商用密码的使用、管理和应用安全性评估要求，制定网络安全等级保护密码标准规范。

**Article 41** Network operators shall, in accordance with the requirements of the national cybersecurity classified protection system, use commercial cryptography to protect network security. The national cryptographic administration shall, based on the security protection level of the network, determine the requirements for the use, management, and application security assessment of commercial cryptography, and formulate cryptographic standards and specifications for cybersecurity classified protection.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　商用密码应用安全性评估、关键信息基础设施安全检测评估、网络安全等级测评应当加强衔接，避免重复评估、测评。

**Article 42** The security assessment of commercial cryptography application, the security inspection and assessment of critical information infrastructure, and the cybersecurity classified evaluation shall strengthen coordination to avoid duplicate assessment and evaluation.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　密码管理部门依法组织对商用密码活动进行监督检查，对国家机关和涉及商用密码工作的单位的商用密码相关工作进行指导和监督。

**Article 43** The cryptographic administration shall, according to law, organize supervision and inspection of commercial cryptography activities, and guide and supervise the commercial cryptography-related work of state organs and units involved in commercial cryptography work.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　密码管理部门和有关部门建立商用密码监督管理协作机制，加强商用密码监督、检查、指导等工作的协调配合。

**Article 44** The cryptographic administration and relevant departments shall establish a collaborative mechanism for commercial cryptography supervision and management, and strengthen coordination and cooperation in the supervision, inspection, and guidance of commercial cryptography.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　密码管理部门和有关部门依法开展商用密码监督检查，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）进入商用密码活动场所实施现场检查；  
（二）向当事人的法定代表人、主要负责人和其他有关人员调查、了解有关情况；  
（三）查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿以及其他有关资料。

**Article 45** When carrying out commercial cryptography supervision and inspection according to law, the cryptographic administration and relevant departments may exercise the following powers:  
(1) Enter the premises of commercial cryptography activities to conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) Investigate and learn about relevant information from the legal representative, principal responsible persons, and other relevant persons of the parties;  
(3) Consult and copy relevant contracts, bills, account books, and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　密码管理部门和有关部门推进商用密码监督管理与社会信用体系相衔接，依法建立推行商用密码经营主体信用记录、信用分级分类监管、失信惩戒以及信用修复等机制。

**Article 46** The cryptographic administration and relevant departments shall promote the connection between commercial cryptography supervision and management and the social credit system, and establish and implement mechanisms such as credit records for commercial cryptography operators, credit-based graded and classified supervision, punishment for dishonesty, and credit restoration, according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　商用密码检测、认证机构和电子政务电子认证服务机构及其工作人员，应当对其在商用密码活动中所知悉的国家秘密和商业秘密承担保密义务。  
密码管理部门和有关部门及其工作人员不得要求商用密码科研、生产、销售、服务、进出口等单位和商用密码检测、认证机构向其披露源代码等密码相关专有信息，并对其在履行职责中知悉的商业秘密和个人隐私严格保密，不得泄露或者非法向他人提供。

**Article 47** Commercial cryptography testing and certification institutions, electronic government electronic certification service institutions, and their staff shall bear the obligation of confidentiality for state secrets and trade secrets learned in commercial cryptography activities.  
The cryptographic administration and relevant departments and their staff shall not require units engaged in commercial cryptography research and development, production, sales, service, import and export, and commercial cryptography testing and certification institutions to disclose source code and other cryptographic-related proprietary information to them, and shall strictly keep confidential the trade secrets and personal privacy learned in performing their duties, and shall not disclose or illegally provide them to others.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　密码管理部门和有关部门依法开展商用密码监督管理，相关单位和人员应当予以配合，任何单位和个人不得非法干预和阻挠。

**Article 48** When the cryptographic administration and relevant departments conduct commercial cryptography supervision and management according to law, the relevant units and persons shall cooperate, and no unit or individual may illegally intervene or obstruct.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　任何单位或者个人有权向密码管理部门和有关部门举报违反本条例的行为。密码管理部门和有关部门接到举报，应当及时核实、处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 49** Any unit or individual has the right to report to the cryptographic administration and relevant departments any violation of these Regulations. Upon receiving a report, the cryptographic administration and relevant departments shall promptly verify and handle it, and shall keep the reporter confidential.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，未经认定向社会开展商用密码检测活动，或者未经认定从事电子政务电子认证服务的，由密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法产品和违法所得；违法所得30万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足30万元的，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款。  
违反本条例规定，未经批准从事商用密码认证活动的，由市场监督管理部门会同密码管理部门依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit engages in commercial cryptography testing activities for the public without accreditation, or engages in electronic government electronic certification services without accreditation, the cryptographic administration shall order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal products and illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit engages in commercial cryptography certification activities without approval, the market regulatory department shall, jointly with the cryptographic administration, impose a penalty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　商用密码检测机构开展商用密码检测，有下列情形之一的，由密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法所得；违法所得30万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足30万元的，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，依法吊销商用密码检测机构资质：  
（一）超出批准范围；  
（二）存在影响检测独立、公正、诚信的行为；  
（三）出具的检测数据、结果虚假或者失实；  
（四）拒不报送或者不如实报送实施情况；  
（五）未履行保密义务；  
（六）其他违反法律、行政法规和商用密码检测技术规范、规则开展商用密码检测的情形。

**Article 51** Where a commercial cryptography testing institution carries out commercial cryptography testing and has one of the following circumstances, the cryptographic administration shall order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the qualification of the commercial cryptography testing institution shall be revoked according to law:  
(1) Exceeding the approved scope;  
(2) Engaging in conduct that affects the independence, impartiality, and integrity of testing;  
(3) Issuing false or inaccurate testing data or results;  
(4) Refusing to report or failing to truthfully report the implementation;  
(5) Failing to fulfill the confidentiality obligation;  
(6) Other circumstances of carrying out commercial cryptography testing in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and commercial cryptography testing technical specifications and rules.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　商用密码认证机构开展商用密码认证，有下列情形之一的，由市场监督管理部门会同密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法所得；违法所得30万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足30万元的，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，依法吊销商用密码认证机构资质：  
（一）超出批准范围；  
（二）存在影响认证独立、公正、诚信的行为；  
（三）出具的认证结论虚假或者失实；  
（四）未对其认证的商用密码产品、服务、管理体系实施有效的跟踪调查；  
（五）未履行保密义务；  
（六）其他违反法律、行政法规和商用密码认证技术规范、规则开展商用密码认证的情形。

**Article 52** Where a commercial cryptography certification institution carries out commercial cryptography certification and has one of the following circumstances, the market regulatory department shall, jointly with the cryptographic administration, order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the qualification of the commercial cryptography certification institution shall be revoked according to law:  
(1) Exceeding the approved scope;  
(2) Engaging in conduct that affects the independence, impartiality, and integrity of certification;  
(3) Issuing false or inaccurate certification conclusions;  
(4) Failing to conduct effective follow-up investigation on the commercial cryptography products, services, or management systems it has certified;  
(5) Failing to fulfill the confidentiality obligation;  
(6) Other circumstances of carrying out commercial cryptography certification in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and commercial cryptography certification technical specifications and rules.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)、第二十一条规定，销售或者提供未经检测认证或者检测认证不合格的商用密码产品，或者提供未经认证或者认证不合格的商用密码服务的，由市场监督管理部门会同密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法产品和违法所得；违法所得10万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，可以并处3万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 20 and Article 21 of these Regulations, a unit sells or provides commercial cryptography products that have not undergone testing and certification or have failed testing and certification, or provides commercial cryptography services that have not undergone certification or have failed certification, the market regulatory department shall, jointly with the cryptographic administration, order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal products and illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　电子认证服务机构违反法律、行政法规和电子认证服务密码使用技术规范、规则使用密码的，由密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法所得；违法所得30万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足30万元的，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，依法吊销电子认证服务使用密码的证明文件。

**Article 54** Where an electronic certification service institution uses cryptography in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and the technical specifications and rules for using cryptography in electronic certification services, the cryptographic administration shall order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the certification document for using cryptography in electronic certification services shall be revoked according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　电子政务电子认证服务机构开展电子政务电子认证服务，有下列情形之一的，由密码管理部门责令改正或者停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法所得；违法所得30万元以上的，可以并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足30万元的，可以并处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至吊销电子政务电子认证服务机构资质：  
（一）超出批准范围；  
（二）拒不报送或者不如实报送实施情况；  
（三）未履行保密义务；  
（四）其他违反法律、行政法规和电子政务电子认证服务技术规范、规则提供电子政务电子认证服务的情形。

**Article 55** Where an electronic government electronic certification service institution provides electronic government electronic certification services and has one of the following circumstances, the cryptographic administration shall order it to make corrections or cease the illegal act, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 300,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 300,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, until the qualification of the electronic government electronic certification service institution is revoked:  
(1) Exceeding the approved scope;  
(2) Refusing to report or failing to truthfully report the implementation;  
(3) Failing to fulfill the confidentiality obligation;  
(4) Other circumstances of providing electronic government electronic certification services in violation of laws, administrative regulations, and technical specifications and rules for electronic government electronic certification services.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　电子签名人或者电子签名依赖方因依据电子政务电子认证服务机构提供的电子签名认证服务在政务活动中遭受损失，电子政务电子认证服务机构不能证明自己无过错的，承担赔偿责任。

**Article 56** Where an electronic signatory or a relying party suffers losses in government activities due to reliance on the electronic signature certification service provided by an electronic government electronic certification service institution, and the electronic government electronic certification service institution cannot prove that it is not at fault, it shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　政务活动中电子签名、电子印章、电子证照等涉及的电子认证服务，违反[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定，未由依法设立的电子政务电子认证服务机构提供的，由密码管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者有其他严重情节的，由密码管理部门建议有关国家机关、单位对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分或者处理。有关国家机关、单位应当将处分或者处理情况书面告知密码管理部门。

**Article 57** Where the electronic certification services involved in electronic signatures, electronic seals, electronic certificates, and the like in government activities violate the provisions of Article 30 of these Regulations and are not provided by an electronic government electronic certification service institution established according to law, the cryptographic administration shall order corrections and give a warning; if the party refuses to make corrections or there are other serious circumstances, the cryptographic administration shall recommend the relevant state organs or units to impose sanctions or take actions according to law against the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons. The relevant state organs or units shall inform the cryptographic administration in writing of the sanctions or actions taken.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定进出口商用密码的，由国务院商务主管部门或者海关依法予以处罚。

**Article 58** Where commercial cryptography is imported or exported in violation of these Regulations, the competent department of commerce of the State Council or the customs shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　窃取他人加密保护的信息，非法侵入他人的商用密码保障系统，或者利用商用密码从事危害国家安全、社会公共利益、他人合法权益等违法活动的，由有关部门依照《中华人民共和国网络安全法》和其他有关法律、行政法规的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 59** Where a person steals information encrypted by others, illegally intrudes into another person's commercial cryptography protection system, or uses commercial cryptography to engage in illegal activities that endanger national security, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, the relevant departments shall investigate legal liability in accordance with the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　关键信息基础设施的运营者违反[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)、第三十九条规定，未按照要求使用商用密码，或者未按照要求开展商用密码应用安全性评估的，由密码管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者有其他严重情节的，处10万元以上100万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员处1万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 60** Where an operator of critical information infrastructure violates the provisions of Article 38 and Article 39 of these Regulations by failing to use commercial cryptography as required, or failing to conduct a security assessment of commercial cryptography application as required, the cryptographic administration shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections or there are other serious circumstances, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisor.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　关键信息基础设施的运营者违反[本条例第四十条](#art-40)规定，使用未经安全审查或者安全审查未通过的涉及商用密码的网络产品或者服务的，由有关主管部门责令停止使用，处采购金额1倍以上10倍以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 61** Where an operator of critical information infrastructure, in violation of the provisions of Article 40 of these Regulations, uses network products or services involving commercial cryptography that have not undergone security review or have not passed security review, the relevant competent authority shall order it to stop using them and shall impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the purchase amount; the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　网络运营者违反[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)规定，未按照国家网络安全等级保护制度要求使用商用密码保护网络安全的，由密码管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者导致危害网络安全等后果的，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员处5000元以上5万元以下罚款。

**Article 62** Where a network operator, in violation of the provisions of Article 41 of these Regulations, fails to use commercial cryptography to protect network security in accordance with the requirements of the national cybersecurity classified protection system, the cryptography administration department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections or causes consequences such as endangering network security, it shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and the persons directly in charge shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　无正当理由拒不接受、不配合或者干预、阻挠密码管理部门、有关部门的商用密码监督管理的，由密码管理部门、有关部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者有其他严重情节的，处5万元以上50万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节特别严重的，责令停业整顿，直至吊销商用密码许可证件。

**Article 63** Any person who, without justifiable reasons, refuses to accept or cooperate with, or interferes with or obstructs, the supervision and administration of commercial cryptography by the cryptography administration department or relevant departments shall be ordered by the cryptography administration department or relevant departments to make corrections and shall be given a warning; if it refuses to make corrections or there are other serious circumstances, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are especially serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, up to the revocation of the commercial cryptography license.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国家机关有[本条例第六十条](#art-60)、第六十一条、第六十二条、第六十三条所列违法情形的，由密码管理部门、有关部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者有其他严重情节的，由密码管理部门、有关部门建议有关国家机关对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分或者处理。有关国家机关应当将处分或者处理情况书面告知密码管理部门、有关部门。

**Article 64** Where a state organ commits any illegal act listed in Articles 60, 61, 62, or 63 of these Regulations, the cryptography administration department or relevant departments shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it refuses to make corrections or there are other serious circumstances, the cryptography administration department or relevant departments shall recommend that the relevant state organ impose sanctions or handle the matter on the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons according to law. The relevant state organ shall inform the cryptography administration department or relevant departments in writing of the sanctions or handling situation.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　密码管理部门和有关部门的工作人员在商用密码工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，或者泄露、非法向他人提供在履行职责中知悉的商业秘密、个人隐私、举报人信息的，依法给予处分。

**Article 65** Staff members of the cryptography administration department and relevant departments who abuse their powers, neglect their duties, engage in malpractice for personal gain, or disclose or illegally provide to others trade secrets, personal privacy, or whistleblower information learned in the performance of their duties shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；给他人造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 66** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; whoever causes damage to others shall bear civil liability according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本条例自2023年7月1日起施行。

**Article 67** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2023.
