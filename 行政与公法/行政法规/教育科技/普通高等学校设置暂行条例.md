# 普通高等学校设置暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1986-12-15

> (1986年12月15日国务院发布) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强高等教育的宏观管理，保证普通高等学校的教育质量，促进高等教育事业有计划、按比例地协调发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称的普通高等学校，是指以通过国家规定的专门入学考试的高级中学毕业学生为主要培养对象的全日制大学、独立设置的学院和高等专科学校、高等职业学校。 普通高等学校的设置，由国家教育委员会审批。 第三条　国家教育委员会应当根据经济建设和社会发展的需要、人才需求的科学预测和办学条件的实际可能，编制全国普通高等教育事业发展规划，调整普通高等教育的结构，妥善地处理发展普通高等教育同发展成人高等教育、中等专业教育和基础教育的关系，合理地确定科类和层次。 第四条　国家教育委员会应当根据学校的人才培养目标、招生及分配面向地区以及现有普通高等学校的分布状况等，统筹规划普通高等学校的布局，并注意在高等教育事业需要加强的省、自治区有计划地设置普通高等学校。 第五条　凡通过现有普通高等学校的扩大招生、增设专业、接受委托培养、联合办学及发展成人高等教育等途径，能够基本满足人才需求的，不另行增设普通高等学校。 第二章　设置标准 第六条　设置普通高等学校，应当配备具有较高政治素质和管理高等教育工作的能力、达到大学本科毕业文化水平的专职校(院)长和副校(院)长。同时，还应当配备专职思想政治工作和系科、专业的负责人。 第七条　设置普通高等学校，须按下列规定配备与学校的专业设置、学生人数相适应的合格教师。 (一) 大学及学院在建校招生时，各门公共必修课程和专业基础必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师二人；各门专业必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师一人。具有副教授职务以上的专任教师人数，应当不低于本校(院)专任教师总数的10%。 (二)高等专科学校及高等职业学校在建校招生时，各门公共必修课程和专业基础必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师二人；各门主要专业课程至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师一人。具有副教授职务以上的专任教师人数，应当不低于本校专任教师总数的5%。 (三) 大学及学院的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校(院)专任教师人数的四分之一；高等专科学校的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校专任教师的三分之一；高等职业学校的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校专任教师的二分之一。 少数地区或特殊科类的普通高等学校建校招生，具有副教授职务以上的专任教师达不到(一)、(二)项要求的，需经国家教育委员会批准。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强高等教育的宏观管理，保证普通高等学校的教育质量，促进高等教育事业有计划、按比例地协调发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the macro-management of higher education, ensuring the educational quality of regular institutions of higher learning, and promoting the planned, proportionate, and coordinated development of higher education.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称的普通高等学校，是指以通过国家规定的专门入学考试的高级中学毕业学生为主要培养对象的全日制大学、独立设置的学院和高等专科学校、高等职业学校。  
普通高等学校的设置，由国家教育委员会审批。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "regular institutions of higher learning" refers to full-time universities, independently established colleges, higher specialized schools, and higher vocational schools that primarily train senior high school graduates who have passed the state-prescribed special entrance examinations.  
The establishment of regular institutions of higher learning shall be subject to the examination and approval of the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家教育委员会应当根据经济建设和社会发展的需要、人才需求的科学预测和办学条件的实际可能，编制全国普通高等教育事业发展规划，调整普通高等教育的结构，妥善地处理发展普通高等教育同发展成人高等教育、中等专业教育和基础教育的关系，合理地确定科类和层次。

**Article 3** The State Education Commission shall, in light of the needs of economic construction and social development, the scientific forecast of talent demand, and the actual possibility of school-running conditions, formulate the national development plan for regular higher education, adjust the structure of regular higher education, properly handle the relationship between developing regular higher education and developing adult higher education, secondary specialized education, and basic education, and rationally determine the disciplines and levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家教育委员会应当根据学校的人才培养目标、招生及分配面向地区以及现有普通高等学校的分布状况等，统筹规划普通高等学校的布局，并注意在高等教育事业需要加强的省、自治区有计划地设置普通高等学校。

**Article 4** The State Education Commission shall, in light of the school's talent training objectives, the regions targeted for enrollment and job assignment, and the existing distribution of regular institutions of higher learning, make overall plans for the layout of regular institutions of higher learning, and shall pay attention to establishing regular institutions of higher learning in a planned manner in provinces and autonomous regions where higher education needs to be strengthened.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　凡通过现有普通高等学校的扩大招生、增设专业、接受委托培养、联合办学及发展成人高等教育等途径，能够基本满足人才需求的，不另行增设普通高等学校。

**Article 5** Where the demand for talents can be basically met through such means as expanding enrollment in existing regular institutions of higher learning, adding new specialties, accepting commissioned training, jointly running schools, and developing adult higher education, no additional regular institutions of higher learning shall be established.

### 第二章　设置标准

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设置普通高等学校，应当配备具有较高政治素质和管理高等教育工作的能力、达到大学本科毕业文化水平的专职校(院)长和副校(院)长。同时，还应当配备专职思想政治工作和系科、专业的负责人。

**Article 6** To establish a regular institution of higher learning, full-time president (or dean) and vice-president (or vice-dean) who have high political quality, the ability to manage higher education, and have attained the educational level of university undergraduate graduation shall be assigned. At the same time, full-time personnel responsible for ideological and political work and for departments and specialties shall also be assigned.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设置普通高等学校，须按下列规定配备与学校的专业设置、学生人数相适应的合格教师。  
(一) 大学及学院在建校招生时，各门公共必修课程和专业基础必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师二人；各门专业必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师一人。具有副教授职务以上的专任教师人数，应当不低于本校(院)专任教师总数的10%。  
(二)高等专科学校及高等职业学校在建校招生时，各门公共必修课程和专业基础必修课程，至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师二人；各门主要专业课程至少应当分别配备具有讲师职务以上的专任教师一人。具有副教授职务以上的专任教师人数，应当不低于本校专任教师总数的5%。  
(三) 大学及学院的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校(院)专任教师人数的四分之一；高等专科学校的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校专任教师的三分之一；高等职业学校的兼任教师人数，应当不超过本校专任教师的二分之一。  
少数地区或特殊科类的普通高等学校建校招生，具有副教授职务以上的专任教师达不到(一)、(二)项要求的，需经国家教育委员会批准。

**Article 7** To establish a regular institution of higher learning, qualified teachers appropriate for the school's specialty offerings and student numbers shall be assigned according to the following provisions:  
(1) For universities and colleges, when they start enrollment after establishment, for each compulsory public course and compulsory basic specialty course, at least two full-time teachers with the title of lecturer or above shall be assigned; for each compulsory specialized course, at least one full-time teacher with the title of lecturer or above shall be assigned. The number of full-time teachers with the title of associate professor or above shall not be less than 10% of the total number of full-time teachers of that institution.  
(2) For higher specialized schools and higher vocational schools, when they start enrollment after establishment, for each compulsory public course and compulsory basic specialty course, at least two full-time teachers with the title of lecturer or above shall be assigned; for each main specialized course, at least one full-time teacher with the title of lecturer or above shall be assigned. The number of full-time teachers with the title of associate professor or above shall not be less than 5% of the total number of full-time teachers of that school.  
(3) For universities and colleges, the number of part-time teachers shall not exceed one-quarter of the number of full-time teachers of that institution; for higher specialized schools, the number of part-time teachers shall not exceed one-third of the number of full-time teachers of that school; for higher vocational schools, the number of part-time teachers shall not exceed one-half of the number of full-time teachers of that school.  
Where, in establishing regular institutions of higher learning in a minority region or of a special type, the number of full-time teachers with the title of associate professor or above fails to meet the requirements of subparagraphs (1) and (2), approval from the State Education Commission shall be required.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设置普通高等学校，须有与学校的学科门类和规模相适应的土地和校舍，保证教学、生活、体育锻炼及学校长远发展的需要。普通高等学校的占地面积及校舍建筑面积，参照国家规定的一般高等学校校舍规划面积的定额核算。  
普通高等学校的校舍可分期建设，但其可供使用的校舍面积，应当保证各年度招生的需要。

**Article 8** To establish a regular institution of higher learning, there shall be land and school buildings appropriate for the disciplines and scale of the school, ensuring the needs of teaching, living, physical exercise, and the long-term development of the school. The land area and floor area of school buildings of a regular institution of higher learning shall be checked and calculated with reference to the state-prescribed norm for planned floor area of general institutions of higher learning.  
The school buildings of a regular institution of higher learning may be constructed in phases, but the area of school buildings available for use shall ensure the needs of enrollment in each year.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　普通高等学校在建校招生时，大学及学院的适用图书，文科、政法、财经院校应当不少于八万册；理、工、农、医院校应当不少于六万册。高等专科学校及高等职业学校的适用图书，文科、政法、财经学校应当不少于五万册；理、工、农、医学校应当不少于四万册。并应当按照专业性质、学生人数分别配置必需的仪器、设备、标本、模型。  
理、工、农院校应当有必需的教学实习工厂或农(林)场和固定的生产实习基地；师范院校应当有附属的实验学校或固定的实习学校；医学院校至少应当有一所附属医院和适应需要的教学医院。

**Article 9** When a regular institution of higher learning starts enrollment after establishment, for universities and colleges, the applicable books for institutions of liberal arts, political science and law, and finance and economics shall be not less than 80,000 volumes; for institutions of science, engineering, agriculture, and medicine, they shall be not less than 60,000 volumes. For higher specialized schools and higher vocational schools, the applicable books for schools of liberal arts, political science and law, and finance and economics shall be not less than 50,000 volumes; for schools of science, engineering, agriculture, and medicine, they shall be not less than 40,000 volumes. And the necessary instruments, equipment, specimens, and models shall be respectively allocated according to the nature of the specialties and the number of students.  
Institutions of science, engineering, and agriculture shall have necessary teaching practice factories or agricultural (forestry) farms and fixed production practice bases; normal institutions shall have attached experimental schools or fixed practice schools; medical institutions shall have at least one affiliated hospital and sufficient teaching hospitals to meet needs.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设置普通高等学校所需的基本建设投资和教育事业费，须有稳定的来源和切实的保证。

**Article 10** The capital construction investment and educational operating expenses needed for the establishment of a regular institution of higher learning shall have stable sources and reliable guarantees.

### 第三章　学校名称

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设置普通高等学校，应当根据学校的人才培养目标、学科门类、规模、领导体制、所在地等，确定名实相符的学校名称。

**Article 11** To establish a regular institution of higher learning, the name of the school shall be determined in accordance with the school's talent training objectives, disciplines, scale, leadership system, location, etc., and the name shall correspond to the actual conditions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　称为大学的，须符合下列规定：  
(一)主要培养本科及本科以上专门人才；  
(二)在文科(含文学、历史、哲学、艺术)、政法、财经、教育(含体育)、理科、工科、农林、医药等八个学科门类中，以三个以上不同学科为主要学科；  
(三)具有较强的教学、科学研究力量和较高的教学、科学研究水平；  
(四)全日制在校学生计划规模在五千人以上。但边远地区或有特殊需要，经国家教育委员会批准，可以不受此限。

**Article 12** An institution that is called a "university" shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) It mainly trains specialized talents at the undergraduate level and above;  
(2) Among the eight disciplines of liberal arts (including literature, history, philosophy, and art), political science and law, finance and economics, education (including physical education), science, engineering, agriculture and forestry, and medicine and pharmacy, it takes three or more different disciplines as its main disciplines;  
(3) It has relatively strong teaching and scientific research capabilities and a relatively high level of teaching and scientific research;  
(4) Its planned enrollment capacity for full-time students is 5,000 or more. However, this restriction may be exempted for remote areas or special needs subject to the approval of the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　称为学院的，须符合下列规定：  
(一)主要培养本科及本科以上专门人才；  
(二)以[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第(二)项所列学科门类中的一个学科为主要学科；  
(三)全日制在校学生计划规模在三千人以上。但艺术、体育及其他特殊科类或有特殊需要的学院，经国家教育委员会批准，可以不受此限。

**Article 13** An institution that is called a "college" shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) It mainly trains specialized talents at the undergraduate level and above;  
(2) It takes one of the disciplines listed in subparagraph (2) of Article 12 of these Regulations as its main discipline;  
(3) Its planned enrollment capacity for full-time students is 3,000 or more. However, this restriction may be exempted for colleges of art, physical education, or other special types or with special needs, subject to the approval of the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　称为高等专科学校的，须符合下列规定：  
(一)主要培养高等专科层次的专门人才；  
(二)以[本条例第十二条](#art-12)第(二)项所列学科门类中的一个学科为主要学科；  
(三)全日制在校学生计划规模在一千人以上。但边远地区或有特殊需要的学校，经国家教育委员会批准，可以不受此限。

**Article 14** An institution that is called a "higher specialized school" shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) It mainly trains specialized talents at the higher specialized level;  
(2) It takes one of the disciplines listed in subparagraph (2) of Article 12 of these Regulations as its main discipline;  
(3) Its planned enrollment capacity for full-time students is 1,000 or more. However, this restriction may be exempted for schools in remote areas or with special needs, subject to the approval of the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　称为高等职业学校的，须符合下列规定：  
(一)主要培养高等专科层次的专门人才；  
(二)以职业技术教育为主；  
(三)全日制在校学生计划规模在一千人以上。但边远地区或有特殊需要的学校，经国家教育委员会批准，可以不受此限。

**Article 15** An institution that is called a "higher vocational school" shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) It mainly trains specialized talents at the higher specialized level;  
(2) It mainly provides vocational and technical education;  
(3) Its planned enrollment capacity for full-time students is 1,000 or more. However, this restriction may be exempted for schools in remote areas or with special needs, subject to the approval of the State Education Commission.

### 第四章　审批验收

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家教育委员会每年第三季度办理设置普通高等学校的审批手续。设置普通高等学校的主管部门，应当在每年第三季度以前提出申请，逾期则延至下一年度审批时间办理。

**Article 16** The State Education Commission shall handle the formalities for examining and approving the establishment of regular institutions of higher learning in the third quarter of each year. The competent department responsible for establishing a regular institution of higher learning shall submit an application before the third quarter of each year; if the application is overdue, it shall be deferred to the approval period of the following year.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　设置普通高等学校的审批程序，一般分为审批筹建和审批正式建校招生两个阶段。完全具备建校招生条件的，也可以直接申请正式建校招生。

**Article 17** The examination and approval procedure for establishing a regular institution of higher learning shall generally be divided into two stages: examination and approval of preparatory establishment, and examination and approval of formal establishment for enrollment. Where all conditions for establishment and enrollment are met, an application may be made directly for formal establishment and enrollment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　设置普通高等学校，应当由学校的主管部门邀请教育、计划、人才需求预测、劳动人事、财政、基本建设等有关部门和专家共同进行论证，并提出论证报告。  
论证报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)拟建学校的名称、校址、学科门类、专业设置、人才培养目标、规模、领导体制、招生及分配面向地区；  
(二)人才需求预测、办学效益、高等教育的布局；  
(三)拟建学校的师资来源、经费来源、基建计划。

**Article 18** To establish a regular institution of higher learning, the competent department of the school shall invite relevant departments and experts from the fields of education, planning, talent demand forecasting, labor and personnel, finance, capital construction, etc., to jointly conduct a demonstration and submit a demonstration report.  
The demonstration report shall include the following:  
(1) The name, location, disciplines, specialty offerings, talent training objectives, scale, leadership system, and regions targeted for enrollment and job assignment of the proposed school;  
(2) The forecast of talent demand, school-running efficiency, and the layout of higher education;  
(3) The source of teachers, source of funds, and capital construction plan for the proposed school.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　凡经过论证，确需设置普通高等学校的，按学校隶属关系，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或国务院有关部门向国家教育委员会提出筹建普通高等学校申请书，并附交论证报告。  
国务院有关部门申请筹建普通高等学校，还应当附交学校所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的意见书。

**Article 19** Where after demonstration, it is confirmed that a regular institution of higher learning needs to be established, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the relevant department of the State Council shall, according to the affiliation of the school, submit an application for the preparatory establishment of a regular institution of higher learning to the State Education Commission, accompanied by the demonstration report.  
If the relevant department of the State Council applies for the preparatory establishment of a regular institution of higher learning, it shall also attach the opinion letter of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the school is to be located.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　普通高等学校的筹建期限，从批准之日起，应当不少于一年，但最长不得超过五年。

**Article 20** The period for preparatory establishment of a regular institution of higher learning shall be not less than one year from the date of approval, but shall not exceed five years at the maximum.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经批准筹建的普通高等学校，凡符合本条例第二章规定的，按学校隶属关系，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或国务院有关部门向国家教育委员会提出正式建校招生申请书，并附交筹建情况报告。

**Article 21** For a regular institution of higher learning that has been approved for preparatory establishment and that meets the provisions of Chapter II of these Regulations, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the relevant department of the State Council shall, according to the affiliation of the school, submit an application for formal establishment and enrollment to the State Education Commission, accompanied by a report on the status of the preparatory establishment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家教育委员会在接到筹建普通高等学校申请书，或正式建校招生申请书后，应当进行审查，并做出是否准予筹建或正式建校招生的决定。

**Article 22** Upon receiving an application for the preparatory establishment of a regular institution of higher learning, or an application for formal establishment and enrollment, the State Education Commission shall examine the application and make a decision on whether to grant approval for the preparatory establishment or formal establishment and enrollment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　为保证新建普通高等学校的办学质量，由国家教育委员会或它委托的机构，对新建普通高等学校第一届毕业生进行考核验收。

**Article 23** To ensure the school-running quality of newly established regular institutions of higher learning, the State Education Commission or an institution entrusted by it shall conduct an assessment and check of the first batch of graduates of newly established regular institutions of higher learning.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经批准建立的普通高等学校，从批准正式建校招生之日起十年内，应当达到审定的计划规模及正常的教师配备标准和办学条件。国家教育委员会或它委托的机构负责对此进行审核验收。

**Article 24** A regular institution of higher learning that has been approved for establishment shall, within ten years from the date of approval of formal establishment for enrollment, achieve the approved planned scale and the normal teacher allocation standards and school-running conditions. The State Education Commission or an institution entrusted by it shall be responsible for examining and checking this.

### 第五章　检查处理

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　凡违反本规定有下列情形之一的，由国家教育委员会区别情况，责令其调整、整顿、停止招生或停办：  
(一)虚报条件，筹建或建立普通高等学校的；  
(二)擅自筹建或建校招生的；  
(三)超过筹建期限，未具备招生条件的；  
(四)第一届毕业生经考核验收达不到规定要求的；  
(五)在规定期限内，达不到审定的计划规模及正常的教师配备标准和办学条件的。

**Article 25** Anyone who violates these Provisions under any of the following circumstances shall, as the case may be, be ordered by the State Education Commission to make adjustments, undergo rectification, suspend enrollment, or be closed down:  
(1) Making false reports on conditions to establish a regular institution of higher learning in preparation or formally;  
(2) Establishing a school in preparation or enrolling students without authorization;  
(3) Failing to meet the conditions for enrollment after the expiration of the preparatory establishment period;  
(4) Failing to meet the prescribed requirements for the first batch of graduates after assessment and check;  
(5) Failing to achieve the approved planned scale, normal teacher allocation standards, and school-running conditions within the prescribed period.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对本条例施行前设置或变更学校名称的普通高等学校，应当参照本条例，进行整顿。整顿办法，由国家教育委员会另行制定。

**Article 26** Regular institutions of higher learning that were established or changed their school names before the coming into force of these Regulations shall undergo rectification by reference to these Regulations. The measures for rectification shall be separately formulated by the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例由国家教育委员会负责解释。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Education Commission.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
