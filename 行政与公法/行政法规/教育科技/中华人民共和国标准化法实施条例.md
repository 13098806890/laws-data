# 中华人民共和国标准化法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （1990年4月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第53号发布　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国标准化法》（以下简称《标准化法》）的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　对下列需要统一的技术要求，应当制定标准： （一）工业产品的品种、规格、质量、等级或者安全、卫生要求； （二）工业产品的设计、生产、试验、检验、包装、储存、运输、使用的方法或者生产、储存、运输过程中的安全、卫生要求； （三）有关环境保护的各项技术要求和检验方法； （四）建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、验收的技术要求和方法； （五）有关工业生产、工程建设和环境保护的技术术语、符号、代号、制图方法、互换配合要求； （六）农业（含林业、牧业、渔业，下同）产品（含种子、种苗、种畜、种禽，下同）的品种、规格、质量、等级、检验、包装、储存、运输以及生产技术、管理技术的要求； （七）信息、能源、资源、交通运输的技术要求。 第三条　国家有计划地发展标准化事业。标准化工作应当纳入各级国民经济和社会发展计划。 第四条　国家鼓励采用国际标准和国外先进标准，积极参与制定国际标准。 第二章　标准化工作的管理 第五条　标准化工作的任务是制定标准、组织实施标准和对标准的实施进行监督。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国标准化法》（以下简称《标准化法》）的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Standardization Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Standardization Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对下列需要统一的技术要求，应当制定标准：  
（一）工业产品的品种、规格、质量、等级或者安全、卫生要求；  
（二）工业产品的设计、生产、试验、检验、包装、储存、运输、使用的方法或者生产、储存、运输过程中的安全、卫生要求；  
（三）有关环境保护的各项技术要求和检验方法；  
（四）建设工程的勘察、设计、施工、验收的技术要求和方法；  
（五）有关工业生产、工程建设和环境保护的技术术语、符号、代号、制图方法、互换配合要求；  
（六）农业（含林业、牧业、渔业，下同）产品（含种子、种苗、种畜、种禽，下同）的品种、规格、质量、等级、检验、包装、储存、运输以及生产技术、管理技术的要求；  
（七）信息、能源、资源、交通运输的技术要求。

**Article 2** Standards shall be formulated for the following technical requirements that need to be unified:  
(1) Varieties, specifications, quality, grades or safety and sanitation requirements of industrial products;  
(2) Methods for the design, production, testing, inspection, packaging, storage, transportation and use of industrial products, or safety and sanitation requirements during production, storage and transportation;  
(3) Various technical requirements and inspection methods related to environmental protection;  
(4) Technical requirements and methods for surveying, design, construction and acceptance of construction projects;  
(5) Technical terms, symbols, codes, drawing methods, and interchangeability and compatibility requirements related to industrial production, construction projects and environmental protection;  
(6) Requirements for varieties, specifications, quality, grades, inspection, packaging, storage, transportation, production techniques and management techniques of agricultural (including forestry, animal husbandry, fishery, the same below) products (including seeds, seedlings, breeding stock, breeding poultry, the same below);  
(7) Technical requirements for information, energy, resources and transportation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家有计划地发展标准化事业。标准化工作应当纳入各级国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 3** The State shall develop the standardization cause in a planned manner. The standardization work shall be incorporated into the national economic and social development plans at all levels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励采用国际标准和国外先进标准，积极参与制定国际标准。

**Article 4** The State encourages the adoption of international standards and advanced foreign standards, and actively participates in the formulation of international standards.

### 第二章　标准化工作的管理

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　标准化工作的任务是制定标准、组织实施标准和对标准的实施进行监督。

**Article 5** The tasks of standardization work are to formulate standards, organize the implementation of standards and supervise the implementation of standards.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院标准化行政主管部门统一管理全国标准化工作，履行下列职责：  
（一）组织贯彻国家有关标准化工作的法律、法规、方针、政策；  
（二）组织制定全国标准化工作规划、计划；  
（三）组织制定国家标准；  
（四）指导国务院有关行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门的标准化工作，协调和处理有关标准化工作问题；  
（五）组织实施标准；  
（六）对标准的实施情况进行监督检查；  
（七）统一管理全国的产品质量认证工作；  
（八）统一负责对有关国际标准化组织的业务联系。

**Article 6** The standardization administrative department of the State Council shall exercise unified management over the nationwide standardization work and perform the following duties:  
(1) Organize the implementation of the State's laws, regulations, guidelines and policies concerning standardization work;  
(2) Organize the formulation of nationwide standardization work plans and programs;  
(3) Organize the formulation of national standards;  
(4) Direct the standardization work of the relevant administrative departments of the State Council and the standardization administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and coordinate and handle relevant issues concerning standardization work;  
(5) Organize the implementation of standards;  
(6) Supervise and inspect the implementation of standards;  
(7) Exercise unified management over the nationwide product quality certification work;  
(8) Be solely responsible for the business liaison with relevant international standardization organizations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院有关行政主管部门分工管理本部门、本行业的标准化工作，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻国家标准化工作的法律、法规、方针、政策，并制定在本部门、本行业实施的具体办法；  
（二）制定本部门、本行业的标准化工作规划、计划；  
（三）承担国家下达的草拟国家标准的任务，组织制定行业标准；  
（四）指导省、自治区、直辖市有关行政主管部门的标准化工作；  
（五）组织本部门、本行业实施标准；  
（六）对标准实施情况进行监督检查；  
（七）经国务院标准化行政主管部门授权，分工管理本行业的产品质量认证工作。

**Article 7** The relevant administrative departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions, manage the standardization work of their own departments and trades, and perform the following duties:  
(1) Implement the State's laws, regulations, guidelines and policies concerning standardization work, and formulate specific measures for implementation within their own departments and trades;  
(2) Formulate plans and programs for the standardization work of their own departments and trades;  
(3) Undertake the tasks assigned by the State for drafting national standards, and organize the formulation of trade standards;  
(4) Direct the standardization work of the relevant administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) Organize the implementation of standards within their own departments and trades;  
(6) Supervise and inspect the implementation of standards;  
(7) Upon authorization by the standardization administrative department of the State Council, manage the product quality certification work of their own trades according to their respective functions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门统一管理本行政区域的标准化工作，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻国家标准化工作的法律、法规、方针、政策，并制定在本行政区域实施的具体办法；  
（二）制定地方标准化工作规划、计划；  
（三）组织制定地方标准；  
（四）指导本行政区域有关行政主管部门的标准化工作，协调和处理有关标准化工作问题；  
（五）在本行政区域组织实施标准；  
（六）对标准实施情况进行监督检查。

**Article 8** The standardization administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall exercise unified management over the standardization work within its administrative region, and perform the following duties:  
(1) Implement the State's laws, regulations, guidelines and policies concerning standardization work, and formulate specific measures for implementation within its administrative region;  
(2) Formulate plans and programs for local standardization work;  
(3) Organize the formulation of local standards;  
(4) Direct the standardization work of the relevant administrative departments within its administrative region, and coordinate and handle relevant issues concerning standardization work;  
(5) Organize the implementation of standards within its administrative region;  
(6) Supervise and inspect the implementation of standards.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　省、自治区、直辖市有关行政主管部门分工管理本行政区域内本部门、本行业的标准化工作，履行下列职责：  
（一）贯彻国家和本部门、本行业、本行政区域标准化工作的法律、法规、方针、政策，并制定实施的具体办法；  
（二）制定本行政区域内本部门、本行业的标准化工作规划、计划；  
（三）承担省、自治区、直辖市人民政府下达的草拟地方标准的任务；  
（四）在本行政区域内组织本部门、本行业实施标准；  
（五）对标准实施情况进行监督检查。

**Article 9** The relevant administrative department of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, according to its functions, manage the standardization work of its own department and trade within its administrative region, and perform the following duties:  
(1) Implement the laws, regulations, guidelines and policies of the State and of its own department, trade and administrative region concerning standardization work, and formulate specific measures for implementation;  
(2) Formulate plans and programs for the standardization work of its own department and trade within its administrative region;  
(3) Undertake the tasks assigned by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for drafting local standards;  
(4) Organize the implementation of standards within its own department and trade within its administrative region;  
(5) Supervise and inspect the implementation of standards.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　市、县标准化行政主管部门和有关行政主管部门的职责分工，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 10** The division of functions between the standardization administrative departments and the relevant administrative departments of cities and counties shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　标准的制定

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对需要在全国范围内统一的下列技术要求，应当制定国家标准（含标准样品的制作）：  
（一）互换配合、通用技术语言要求；  
（二）保障人体健康和人身、财产安全的技术要求；  
（三）基本原料、燃料、材料的技术要求；  
（四）通用基础件的技术要求；  
（五）通用的试验、检验方法；  
（六）通用的管理技术要求；  
（七）工程建设的重要技术要求；  
（八）国家需要控制的其他重要产品的技术要求。

**Article 11** National standards (including the preparation of standard samples) shall be formulated for the following technical requirements that need to be unified nationwide:  
(1) Requirements for interchangeability and compatibility, and general technical language;  
(2) Technical requirements for protecting human health and personal and property safety;  
(3) Technical requirements for basic raw materials, fuels and materials;  
(4) Technical requirements for general basic parts;  
(5) General testing and inspection methods;  
(6) General management technical requirements;  
(7) Important technical requirements for construction projects;  
(8) Technical requirements for other important products that the State needs to control.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家标准由国务院标准化行政主管部门编制计划，组织草拟，统一审批、编号、发布。  
工程建设、药品、食品卫生、兽药、环境保护的国家标准，分别由国务院工程建设主管部门、卫生主管部门、农业主管部门、环境保护主管部门组织草拟、审批；其编号、发布办法由国务院标准化行政主管部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门制定。  
法律对国家标准的制定另有规定的，依照法律的规定执行。

**Article 12** National standards shall be planned, drafted, examined, numbered and issued uniformly by the standardization administrative department of the State Council.  
National standards for construction projects, pharmaceuticals, food hygiene, veterinary drugs and environmental protection shall be respectively drafted and examined by the competent department of construction projects, the competent health department, the competent agricultural department and the competent environmental protection department of the State Council; their numbering and issuance methods shall be formulated by the standardization administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant administrative departments of the State Council.  
Where laws provide otherwise for the formulation of national standards, the provisions of such laws shall be complied with.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对没有国家标准而又需要在全国某个行业范围内统一的技术要求，可以制定行业标准（含标准样品的制作）。制定行业标准的项目由国务院有关行政主管部门确定。

**Article 13** For technical requirements for which there are no national standards but which need to be unified within a certain trade nationwide, trade standards (including the preparation of standard samples) may be formulated. The items for which trade standards are to be formulated shall be determined by the relevant administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　行业标准由国务院有关行政主管部门编制计划，组织草拟，统一审批、编号、发布，并报国务院标准化行政主管部门备案。  
行业标准在相应的国家标准实施后，自行废止。

**Article 14** Trade standards shall be planned, drafted, examined, numbered and issued uniformly by the relevant administrative department of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the standardization administrative department of the State Council for record.  
Trade standards shall automatically be abolished after the corresponding national standards come into force.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对没有国家标准和行业标准而又需要在省、自治区、直辖市范围内统一的工业产品的安全、卫生要求，可以制定地方标准。制定地方标准的项目，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门确定。

**Article 15** For safety and sanitation requirements of industrial products for which there are no national standards or trade standards but which need to be unified within a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, local standards may be formulated. The items for which local standards are to be formulated shall be determined by the standardization administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门编制计划，组织草拟，统一审批、编号、发布，并报国务院标准化行政主管部门和国务院有关行政主管部门备案。  
法律对地方标准的制定另有规定的，依照法律的规定执行。  
地方标准在相应的国家标准或行业标准实施后，自行废止。

**Article 16** Local standards shall be planned, drafted, examined, numbered and issued uniformly by the standardization administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the standardization administrative department of the State Council and the relevant administrative department of the State Council for record.  
Where laws provide otherwise for the formulation of local standards, the provisions of such laws shall be complied with.  
Local standards shall automatically be abolished after the corresponding national standards or trade standards come into force.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　企业生产的产品没有国家标准、行业标准和地方标准的，应当制定相应的企业标准，作为组织生产的依据。企业标准由企业组织制定（农业企业标准制定办法另定），并按省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定备案。  
对已有国家标准、行业标准或者地方标准的，鼓励企业制定严于国家标准、行业标准或者地方标准要求的企业标准，在企业内部适用。

**Article 17** Where the products produced by an enterprise have no national standards, trade standards or local standards, corresponding enterprise standards shall be formulated as the basis for organizing production. Enterprise standards shall be formulated by the enterprise (the methods for formulating agricultural enterprise standards shall be separately prescribed) and shall be submitted for record in accordance with the provisions of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where national standards, trade standards or local standards already exist, enterprises are encouraged to formulate enterprise standards that are stricter than the requirements of the national standards, trade standards or local standards, and such enterprise standards shall be applicable within the enterprise.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家标准、行业标准分为强制性标准和推荐性标准。  
下列标准属于强制性标准：  
（一）药品标准，食品卫生标准，兽药标准；  
（二）产品及产品生产、储运和使用中的安全、卫生标准，劳动安全、卫生标准，运输安全标准；  
（三）工程建设的质量、安全、卫生标准及国家需要控制的其他工程建设标准；  
（四）环境保护的污染物排放标准和环境质量标准；  
（五）重要的通用技术术语、符号、代号和制图方法；  
（六）通用的试验、检验方法标准；  
（七）互换配合标准；  
（八）国家需要控制的重要产品质量标准。  
国家需要控制的重要产品目录由国务院标准化行政主管部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门确定。  
强制性标准以外的标准是推荐性标准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门制定的工业产品的安全、卫生要求的地方标准，在本行政区域内是强制性标准。

**Article 18** National standards and trade standards are divided into mandatory standards and recommendary standards.  
The following standards are mandatory standards:  
(1) Pharmaceutical standards, food hygiene standards, veterinary drug standards;  
(2) Safety and sanitation standards for products and for the production, storage, transportation and use of products, labor safety and sanitation standards, transportation safety standards;  
(3) Quality, safety and sanitation standards for construction projects and other construction project standards that the State needs to control;  
(4) Environmental protection pollutant discharge standards and environmental quality standards;  
(5) Important general technical terms, symbols, codes and drawing methods;  
(6) General testing and inspection method standards;  
(7) Interchangeability and compatibility standards;  
(8) Quality standards for important products that the State needs to control.  
The list of important products that the State needs to control shall be determined by the standardization administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant administrative departments of the State Council.  
Standards other than mandatory standards are recommendary standards.  
Local standards for safety and sanitation requirements of industrial products formulated by the standardization administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government are mandatory standards within that administrative region.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　制定标准应当发挥行业协会、科学技术研究机构和学术团体的作用。  
制定国家标准、行业标准和地方标准的部门应当组织由用户、生产单位、行业协会、科学技术研究机构、学术团体及有关部门的专家组成标准化技术委员会，负责标准草拟和参加标准草案的技术审查工作。未组成标准化技术委员会的，可以由标准化技术归口单位负责标准草拟和参加标准草案的技术审查工作。  
制定企业标准应当充分听取使用单位、科学技术研究机构的意见。

**Article 19** The role of trade associations, scientific and technological research institutions and academic bodies shall be brought into play in formulating standards.  
The departments formulating national standards, trade standards and local standards shall organize the formation of standardization technical committees composed of experts from users, production units, trade associations, scientific and technological research institutions, academic bodies and relevant departments, which shall be responsible for drafting standards and participating in the technical review of draft standards. Where a standardization technical committee is not formed, the standardization technical centralized unit may be responsible for drafting standards and participating in the technical review of draft standards.  
In formulating enterprise standards, the opinions of user units and scientific and technological research institutions shall be fully solicited.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　标准实施后，制定标准的部门应当根据科学技术的发展和经济建设的需要适时进行复审。标准复审周期一般不超过五年。

**Article 20** After a standard is implemented, the department that formulated it shall conduct timely review in light of the development of science and technology and the needs of economic construction. The review period for a standard shall generally not exceed five years.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家标准、行业标准和地方标准的代号、编号办法，由国务院标准化行政主管部门统一规定。  
企业标准的代号、编号办法，由国务院标准化行政主管部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门规定。

**Article 21** The methods for codes and numbers of national standards, trade standards and local standards shall be uniformly prescribed by the standardization administrative department of the State Council.  
The methods for codes and numbers of enterprise standards shall be prescribed by the standardization administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant administrative departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　标准的出版、发行办法，由制定标准的部门规定。

**Article 22** The methods for publication and distribution of standards shall be prescribed by the department that formulates the standards.

### 第四章　标准的实施与监督

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　从事科研、生产、经营的单位和个人，必须严格执行强制性标准。不符合强制性标准的产品，禁止生产、销售和进口。

**Article 23** Units and individuals engaged in scientific research, production and business operations must strictly implement mandatory standards. Products that do not conform to mandatory standards are prohibited from being produced, sold or imported.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　企业生产执行国家标准、行业标准、地方标准或企业标准，应当在产品或其说明书、包装物上标注所执行标准的代号、编号、名称。

**Article 24** Where an enterprise implements national standards, trade standards, local standards or enterprise standards in production, it shall mark the codes, numbers and names of the implemented standards on the products or their instructions or packaging.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出口产品的技术要求由合同双方约定。  
出口产品在国内销售时，属于我国强制性标准管理范围的，必须符合强制性标准的要求。

**Article 25** The technical requirements for export products shall be agreed upon by the parties to the contract.  
When export products are sold domestically, they must conform to the requirements of mandatory standards if they fall within the scope of management of mandatory standards of the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　企业研制新产品、改进产品、进行技术改造，应当符合标准化要求。

**Article 26** An enterprise that develops new products, improves products or carries out technical renovation shall meet the requirements of standardization.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院标准化行政主管部门组织或授权国务院有关行政主管部门建立行业认证机构，进行产品质量认证工作。

**Article 27** The standardization administrative department of the State Council shall organize or authorize the relevant administrative departments of the State Council to establish trade certification bodies to carry out product quality certification.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院标准化行政主管部门统一负责全国标准实施的监督。国务院有关行政主管部门分工负责本部门、本行业的标准实施的监督。  
省、自治区、直辖市标准化行政主管部门统一负责本行政区域内的标准实施的监督。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关行政主管部门分工负责本行政区域内本部门、本行业的标准实施的监督。  
市、县标准化行政主管部门和有关行政主管部门，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的各自的职责，负责本行政区域内的标准实施的监督。

**Article 28** The standardization administrative department of the State Council shall be uniformly responsible for the supervision of the implementation of standards nationwide. The relevant administrative departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions, be responsible for the supervision of the implementation of standards within their own departments and trades.  
The standardization administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be uniformly responsible for the supervision of the implementation of standards within their respective administrative regions. The relevant administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their respective functions, be responsible for the supervision of the implementation of standards within their respective departments and trades within the administrative regions.  
The standardization administrative departments and relevant administrative departments of cities and counties shall, in accordance with their respective functions prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, be responsible for the supervision of the implementation of standards within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家检验机构由国务院标准化行政主管部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门规划、审查。地方检验机构由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府标准化行政主管部门会同省级有关行政主管部门规划、审查。  
处理有关产品是否符合标准的争议，以本条规定的检验机构的检验数据为准。

**Article 29** National inspection bodies shall be planned and examined by the standardization administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant administrative departments of the State Council. Local inspection bodies shall be planned and examined by the standardization administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government jointly with the relevant provincial administrative departments.  
In handling disputes concerning whether a product conforms to a standard, the inspection data from the inspection bodies specified in this Article shall prevail.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院有关行政主管部门可以根据需要和国家有关规定设立检验机构，负责本行业、本部门的检验工作。

**Article 30** The relevant administrative departments of the State Council may, according to needs and relevant State provisions, establish inspection bodies to be responsible for the inspection work within their own trades and departments.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家机关、社会团体、企业事业单位及全体公民均有权检举、揭发违反强制性标准的行为。

**Article 31** State organs, social organizations, enterprises, institutions and all citizens shall have the right to report and expose any act violating mandatory standards.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反《标准化法》和本条例有关规定，有下列情形之一的，由标准化行政主管部门或有关行政主管部门在各自的职权范围内责令限期改进，并可通报批评：  
（一）企业未按规定制定标准作为组织生产依据的；  
（二）企业未按规定要求将产品标准上报备案的；  
（三）企业的产品未按规定附有标识或与其标识不符的；  
（四）企业研制新产品、改进产品、进行技术改造，不符合标准化要求的；  
（五）科研、设计、生产中违反有关强制性标准规定的。

**Article 32** Any violation of the provisions of the Standardization Law and these Regulations in any of the following circumstances shall be ordered by the standardization administrative department or the relevant administrative department, within its respective functions and powers, to make corrections within a specified time limit, and a circular of criticism may also be issued:  
(1) An enterprise fails to formulate standards in accordance with regulations as the basis for organizing production;  
(2) An enterprise fails to submit product standards for record in accordance with the requirements of regulations;  
(3) An enterprise's products are not accompanied by markings in accordance with regulations, or do not conform to their markings;  
(4) An enterprise, in developing new products, improving products or carrying out technical renovation, does not meet the requirements of standardization;  
(5) In scientific research, design or production, the relevant mandatory standard provisions are violated.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　生产不符合强制性标准的产品的，应当责令其停止生产，并没收产品，监督销毁或作必要技术处理；处以该批产品货值金额百分之二十至百分之五十的罚款；对有关责任者处以五千元以下罚款。  
销售不符合强制性标准的商品的，应当责令其停止销售，并限期追回已售出的商品，监督销毁或作必要技术处理；没收违法所得；处以该批商品货值金额百分之十至百分之二十的罚款；对有关责任者处以五千元以下罚款。  
进口不符合强制性标准的产品的，应当封存并没收该产品，监督销毁或作必要技术处理；处以进口产品货值金额百分之二十至百分之五十的罚款；对有关责任者处以五千元以下罚款。  
本条规定的责令停止生产，由有关行政主管部门决定；其他行政处罚由市场监督管理部门决定。

**Article 33** If a product that does not conform to mandatory standards is produced, the production shall be ordered to stop, the product shall be confiscated, and it shall be supervised to be destroyed or subjected to necessary technical treatment; a fine of not less than 20 percent but not more than 50 percent of the value of the batch of products shall be imposed; and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the responsible persons.  
If a commodity that does not conform to mandatory standards is sold, the sale shall be ordered to stop, and the sold commodity shall be recovered within a specified time limit, and it shall be supervised to be destroyed or subjected to necessary technical treatment; the illegal gains shall be confiscated; a fine of not less than 10 percent but not more than 20 percent of the value of the batch of commodities shall be imposed; and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the responsible persons.  
If a product that does not conform to mandatory standards is imported, the product shall be sealed up and confiscated, and it shall be supervised to be destroyed or subjected to necessary technical treatment; a fine of not less than 20 percent but not more than 50 percent of the value of the imported product shall be imposed; and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the responsible persons.  
The order to stop production as prescribed in this Article shall be decided by the relevant administrative department; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the market supervision and administration department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　生产、销售、进口不符合强制性标准的产品，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 34** Where the production, sale or import of products that do not conform to mandatory standards causes serious consequences and constitutes a crime, the judicial organs shall investigate the criminal liability of the directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　当事人对没收产品、没收违法所得和罚款的处罚不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内，向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内，直接向人民法院起诉。当事人逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 35** If a party refuses to accept a penalty of confiscation of products, confiscation of illegal gains or a fine, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level of the organ that made the penalty decision; if it refuses to accept the reconsideration decision, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, file a prosecution with the People's Court. The party may also, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, directly file a prosecution with the People's Court. If the party, without applying for reconsideration or prosecuting to the People's Court within the time limit, fails to comply with the penalty decision, the organ that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)至第三十四条规定的处罚不免除由此产生的对他人的损害赔偿责任。受到损害的有权要求责任人赔偿损失。赔偿责任和赔偿金额纠纷可以由有关行政主管部门处理，当事人也可以直接向人民法院起诉。

**Article 36** The penalties prescribed in Articles 32 to 34 of these Regulations shall not exempt the liability for damages caused to others arising therefrom. The injured parties shall have the right to demand compensation for losses from the responsible persons. Disputes over compensation liability and the amount of compensation may be handled by the relevant administrative department, or the parties may directly file a prosecution with the People's Court.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　标准化工作的监督、检验、管理人员有下列行为之一的，由有关主管部门给予行政处分，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）违反本条例规定，工作失误，造成损失的；  
（二）伪造、篡改检验数据的；  
（三）徇私舞弊、滥用职权、索贿受贿的。

**Article 37** Where any supervisor, inspector or manager of standardization work commits any of the following acts, the relevant competent department shall impose an administrative sanction; if a crime is constituted, the judicial organs shall investigate criminal liability according to law:  
(1) Violating the provisions of these Regulations, making errors in work and causing losses;  
(2) Forging or tampering with inspection data;  
(3) Engaging in malpractices for personal gain, abusing power, or soliciting or accepting bribes.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　罚没收入全部上缴财政。对单位的罚款，一律从其自有资金中支付，不得列入成本。对责任人的罚款，不得从公款中核销。

**Article 38** All income from fines and confiscations shall be turned over to the State treasury in full. Fines imposed on units shall be paid from their own funds and shall not be included in costs. Fines imposed on responsible persons shall not be written off from public funds.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军用标准化管理条例，由国务院、中央军委另行制定。

**Article 39** The regulations for the administration of military standardization shall be separately formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　工程建设标准化管理规定，由国务院工程建设主管部门依据《标准化法》和本条例的有关规定另行制定，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 40** The administrative rules for standardization of construction projects shall be separately formulated by the competent department of construction projects of the State Council in accordance with the Standardization Law and the relevant provisions of these Regulations, and shall come into force after being approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 41** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
