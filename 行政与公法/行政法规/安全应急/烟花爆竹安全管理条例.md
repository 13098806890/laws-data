### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强烟花爆竹安全管理，预防爆炸事故发生，保障公共安全和人身、财产的安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the safety management of fireworks and firecrackers, preventing the occurrence of explosion accidents, and ensuring public safety and the safety of persons and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　烟花爆竹的生产、经营、运输和燃放，适用本条例。  
本条例所称烟花爆竹，是指烟花爆竹制品和用于生产烟花爆竹的民用黑火药、烟火药、引火线等物品。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the production, business operation, transport, and setting off of fireworks and firecrackers.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "fireworks and firecrackers" refer to fireworks and firecracker products and articles such as civilian black powder, pyrotechnic composition, and fuses used for producing fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对烟花爆竹的生产、经营、运输和举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动，实行许可证制度。  
未经许可，任何单位或者个人不得生产、经营、运输烟花爆竹，不得举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a licensing system for the production, business operation, transport of fireworks and firecrackers, and for holding fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities.  
Without a license, no unit or individual may produce, operate, or transport fireworks and firecrackers, or hold fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　安全生产监督管理部门负责烟花爆竹的安全生产监督管理；公安部门负责烟花爆竹的公共安全管理；质量监督检验部门负责烟花爆竹的质量监督和进出口检验。

**Article 4** The work safety supervision and administration department shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of work safety in respect of fireworks and firecrackers; the public security department shall be responsible for the public safety management of fireworks and firecrackers; and the quality supervision and inspection department shall be responsible for the quality supervision and import/export inspection of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公安部门、安全生产监督管理部门、质量监督检验部门、工商行政管理部门应当按照职责分工，组织查处非法生产、经营、储存、运输、邮寄烟花爆竹以及非法燃放烟花爆竹的行为。

**Article 5** The public security department, the work safety supervision and administration department, the quality supervision and inspection department, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, according to their respective functions and duties, organize the investigation and punishment of illegal production, business operation, storage, transport, mailing of fireworks and firecrackers, as well as illegal setting off of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　烟花爆竹生产、经营、运输企业和焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动主办单位的主要负责人，对本单位的烟花爆竹安全工作负责。  
烟花爆竹生产、经营、运输企业和焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动主办单位应当建立健全安全责任制，制定各项安全管理制度和操作规程，并对从业人员定期进行安全教育、法制教育和岗位技术培训。  
中华全国供销合作总社应当加强对本系统企业烟花爆竹经营活动的管理。

**Article 6** The principal persons-in-charge of enterprises that produce, operate, or transport fireworks and firecrackers, and of the entities sponsoring fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities, shall be responsible for the safety of fireworks and firecrackers in their respective units.  
Enterprises that produce, operate, or transport fireworks and firecrackers, and entities sponsoring fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities shall establish and improve the safety responsibility system, formulate various safety management systems and operating procedures, and regularly provide safety education, legal education, and job-specific technical training for their employees.  
The All China Federation of Supply and Marketing Cooperatives shall strengthen the management of fireworks and firecrackers business activities of enterprises under its system.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励烟花爆竹生产企业采用提高安全程度和提升行业整体水平的新工艺、新配方和新技术。

**Article 7** The State shall encourage enterprises that produce fireworks and firecrackers to adopt new processes, new formulas, and new technologies that enhance safety levels and improve the overall level of the industry.

### 第二章　生产安全

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合当地产业结构规划；  
(二)基本建设项目经过批准；  
(三)选址符合城乡规划，并与周边建筑、设施保持必要的安全距离；  
(四)厂房和仓库的设计、结构和材料以及防火、防爆、防雷、防静电等安全设备、设施符合国家有关标准和规范；  
(五)生产设备、工艺符合安全标准；  
(六)产品品种、规格、质量符合国家标准；  
(七)有健全的安全生产责任制；  
(八)有安全生产管理机构和专职安全生产管理人员；  
(九)依法进行了安全评价；  
(十)有事故应急救援预案、应急救援组织和人员，并配备必要的应急救援器材、设备；  
(十一)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 8** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being in line with the local industrial structure planning;  
(2) having its capital construction project approved;  
(3) having its site selection conform to urban and rural planning and maintaining the necessary safety distance from surrounding buildings and facilities;  
(4) having the design, structure, and materials of its workshops and warehouses, as well as safety equipment and facilities for fire prevention, explosion prevention, lightning protection, and static electricity protection, comply with relevant national standards and codes;  
(5) having its production equipment and processes comply with safety standards;  
(6) having its product varieties, specifications, and quality comply with national standards;  
(7) having a sound work safety responsibility system;  
(8) having a work safety management organization and full-time work safety management personnel;  
(9) having undergone a safety assessment according to law;  
(10) having an accident emergency rescue plan, emergency rescue organizations and personnel, and being equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices;  
(11) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当在投入生产前向所在地设区的市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出安全审查申请，并提交能够证明符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定条件的有关材料。设区的市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当自收到材料之日起20日内提出安全审查初步意见，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门审查。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当自受理申请之日起45日内进行安全审查，对符合条件的，核发《烟花爆竹安全生产许可证》；对不符合条件的，应当说明理由。

**Article 9** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall, before putting into production, submit an application for safety review to the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located, and submit relevant materials that can prove compliance with the conditions specified in Article 8 of these Regulations. The work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the city divided into districts shall, within 20 days from the date of receiving the materials, put forward a preliminary opinion on safety review and submit it to the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for review. The work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 45 days from the date of accepting the application, conduct a safety review. For those meeting the conditions, it shall issue a "Fireworks and Firecrackers Work Safety License"; for those not meeting the conditions, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　生产烟花爆竹的企业为扩大生产能力进行基本建设或者技术改造的，应当依照本条例的规定申请办理安全生产许可证。  
生产烟花爆竹的企业，持《烟花爆竹安全生产许可证》到工商行政管理部门办理登记手续后，方可从事烟花爆竹生产活动。

**Article 10** Where an enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers carries out capital construction or technical transformation to expand production capacity, it shall apply for a work safety license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers may engage in the production of fireworks and firecrackers only after it has gone through the registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce by presenting the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Work Safety License".

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当按照安全生产许可证核定的产品种类进行生产，生产工序和生产作业应当执行有关国家标准和行业标准。

**Article 11** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall carry out production in accordance with the product types approved on the work safety license, and its production processes and production operations shall comply with relevant national standards and industry standards.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当对生产作业人员进行安全生产知识教育，对从事药物混合、造粒、筛选、装药、筑药、压药、切引、搬运等危险工序的作业人员进行专业技术培训。从事危险工序的作业人员经设区的市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门考核合格，方可上岗作业。

**Article 12** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall provide safety production knowledge education to its production operators, and provide professional technical training to operators engaged in dangerous processes such as mixing of ingredients, granulation, sieving, charging, ramming, pressing, fuse cutting, and handling. Operators engaged in dangerous processes may take up their posts only after they have passed the examination conducted by the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the city divided into districts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产烟花爆竹使用的原料，应当符合国家标准的规定。生产烟花爆竹使用的原料，国家标准有用量限制的，不得超过规定的用量。不得使用国家标准规定禁止使用或者禁忌配伍的物质生产烟花爆竹。

**Article 13** The raw materials used for producing fireworks and firecrackers shall comply with the provisions of national standards. Where national standards impose quantity limits on the raw materials used for producing fireworks and firecrackers, the specified limits shall not be exceeded. Substances that are prohibited for use or are forbidden to be compounded under national standards shall not be used for producing fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当按照国家标准的规定，在烟花爆竹产品上标注燃放说明，并在烟花爆竹包装物上印制易燃易爆危险物品警示标志。

**Article 14** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall, in accordance with the provisions of national standards, mark setting off instructions on fireworks and firecracker products, and print warning signs for flammable and explosive dangerous articles on the packaging of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生产烟花爆竹的企业，应当对黑火药、烟火药、引火线的保管采取必要的安全技术措施，建立购买、领用、销售登记制度，防止黑火药、烟火药、引火线丢失。黑火药、烟火药、引火线丢失的，企业应当立即向当地安全生产监督管理部门和公安部门报告。

**Article 15** An enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers shall take necessary safety technical measures for the storage of black powder, pyrotechnic composition, and fuses, and establish a registration system for purchase, requisition, and sale to prevent the loss of black powder, pyrotechnic composition, and fuses. If black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses are lost, the enterprise shall immediately report to the local work safety supervision and administration department and the public security department.

### 第三章　经营安全

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　烟花爆竹的经营分为批发和零售。  
从事烟花爆竹批发的企业和零售经营者的经营布点，应当经安全生产监督管理部门审批。  
禁止在城市市区布设烟花爆竹批发场所；城市市区的烟花爆竹零售网点，应当按照严格控制的原则合理布设。

**Article 16** The business operation of fireworks and firecrackers is divided into wholesale and retail.  
The layout of business outlets of enterprises engaged in wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers and retail operators shall be subject to the examination and approval of the work safety supervision and administration department.  
It is prohibited to set up wholesale premises for fireworks and firecrackers in urban areas; retail outlets for fireworks and firecrackers in urban areas shall be rationally arranged in accordance with the principle of strict control.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事烟花爆竹批发的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有企业法人条件；  
(二)经营场所与周边建筑、设施保持必要的安全距离；  
(三)有符合国家标准的经营场所和储存仓库；  
(四)有保管员、仓库守护员；  
(五)依法进行了安全评价；  
(六)有事故应急救援预案、应急救援组织和人员，并配备必要的应急救援器材、设备；  
(七)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 17** An enterprise engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) meeting the conditions of a legal person enterprise;  
(2) maintaining the necessary safety distance between its business premises and surrounding buildings and facilities;  
(3) having business premises and storage warehouses that comply with national standards;  
(4) having custodians and warehouse guards;  
(5) having undergone a safety assessment according to law;  
(6) having an accident emergency rescue plan, emergency rescue organizations and personnel, and being equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices;  
(7) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　烟花爆竹零售经营者，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)主要负责人经过安全知识教育；  
(二)实行专店或者专柜销售，设专人负责安全管理；  
(三)经营场所配备必要的消防器材，张贴明显的安全警示标志；  
(四)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 18** A retail operator of fireworks and firecrackers shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the principal person-in-charge has received safety knowledge education;  
(2) selling through a specialized store or specialized counter, with a designated person responsible for safety management;  
(3) the business premises are equipped with necessary fire-fighting equipment and prominently display safety warning signs;  
(4) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请从事烟花爆竹批发的企业，应当向所在地设区的市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请，并提供能够证明符合[本条例第十七条](#art-17)规定条件的有关材料。受理申请的安全生产监督管理部门应当自受理申请之日起30日内对提交的有关材料和经营场所进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《烟花爆竹经营(批发)许可证》；对不符合条件的，应当说明理由。  
申请从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者，应当向所在地县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请，并提供能够证明符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定条件的有关材料。受理申请的安全生产监督管理部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内对提交的有关材料和经营场所进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《烟花爆竹经营(零售)许可证》；对不符合条件的，应当说明理由。  
《烟花爆竹经营(零售)许可证》，应当载明经营负责人、经营场所地址、经营期限、烟花爆竹种类和限制存放量。

**Article 19** An enterprise applying to engage in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located, and provide relevant materials that can prove compliance with the conditions specified in Article 17 of these Regulations. The work safety supervision and administration department that accepts the application shall, within 30 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials and the business premises, issue a "Fireworks and Firecrackers Business (Wholesale) License" to those that meet the conditions, and state the reasons for those that do not meet the conditions.  
An operator applying to engage in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the county where it is located, and provide relevant materials that can prove compliance with the conditions specified in Article 18 of these Regulations. The work safety supervision and administration department that accepts the application shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials and the business premises, issue a "Fireworks and Firecrackers Business (Retail) License" to those that meet the conditions, and state the reasons for those that do not meet the conditions.  
The "Fireworks and Firecrackers Business (Retail) License" shall state the responsible person for the business, the address of the business premises, the term of operation, the types of fireworks and firecrackers, and the restricted storage quantity.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　从事烟花爆竹批发的企业，应当向生产烟花爆竹的企业采购烟花爆竹，向从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者供应烟花爆竹。从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者，应当向从事烟花爆竹批发的企业采购烟花爆竹。  
从事烟花爆竹批发的企业、零售经营者不得采购和销售非法生产、经营的烟花爆竹。  
从事烟花爆竹批发的企业，不得向从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者供应按照国家标准规定应由专业燃放人员燃放的烟花爆竹。从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者，不得销售按照国家标准规定应由专业燃放人员燃放的烟花爆竹。

**Article 20** An enterprise engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers shall purchase fireworks and firecrackers from enterprises that produce fireworks and firecrackers, and supply fireworks and firecrackers to operators engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers. An operator engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers shall purchase fireworks and firecrackers from enterprises engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers.  
Enterprises engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers and retail operators shall not purchase or sell illegally produced or operated fireworks and firecrackers.  
An enterprise engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers shall not supply to operators engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers fireworks and firecrackers that, according to national standards, shall be set off by professional setting off personnel. An operator engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers shall not sell fireworks and firecrackers that, according to national standards, shall be set off by professional setting off personnel.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生产、经营黑火药、烟火药、引火线的企业，不得向未取得烟花爆竹安全生产许可的任何单位或者个人销售黑火药、烟火药和引火线。

**Article 21** An enterprise that produces or operates black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses shall not sell black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses to any unit or individual that has not obtained a fireworks and firecrackers work safety license.

### 第四章　运输安全

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经由道路运输烟花爆竹的，应当经公安部门许可。  
经由铁路、水路、航空运输烟花爆竹的，依照铁路、水路、航空运输安全管理的有关法律、法规、规章的规定执行。

**Article 22** Transport of fireworks and firecrackers by road shall be subject to the permission of the public security department.  
Transport of fireworks and firecrackers by railway, waterway, or air shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules on the safety management of railway, waterway, and air transport.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　经由道路运输烟花爆竹的，托运人应当向运达地县级人民政府公安部门提出申请，并提交下列有关材料：  
(一)承运人从事危险货物运输的资质证明；  
(二)驾驶员、押运员从事危险货物运输的资格证明；  
(三)危险货物运输车辆的道路运输证明；  
(四)托运人从事烟花爆竹生产、经营的资质证明；  
(五)烟花爆竹的购销合同及运输烟花爆竹的种类、规格、数量；  
(六)烟花爆竹的产品质量和包装合格证明；  
(七)运输车辆牌号、运输时间、起始地点、行驶路线、经停地点。

**Article 23** For transport of fireworks and firecrackers by road, the consignor shall submit an application to the public security department of the people's government of the county of destination, and provide the following relevant materials:  
(1) the qualification certificate of the carrier for engaging in the transport of dangerous goods;  
(2) the qualification certificate of the driver and escort for engaging in the transport of dangerous goods;  
(3) the road transport certificate of the vehicle for transporting dangerous goods;  
(4) the qualification certificate of the consignor for engaging in the production or business operation of fireworks and firecrackers;  
(5) the purchase and sale contract for fireworks and firecrackers and the types, specifications, and quantities of the fireworks and firecrackers to be transported;  
(6) the product quality and packaging conformity certificate of the fireworks and firecrackers;  
(7) the license plate number of the transport vehicle, the transport time, the starting point, the route, and the stopping points.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　受理申请的公安部门应当自受理申请之日起3日内对提交的有关材料进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》；对不符合条件的，应当说明理由。  
《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》应当载明托运人、承运人、一次性运输有效期限、起始地点、行驶路线、经停地点、烟花爆竹的种类、规格和数量。

**Article 24** The public security department that accepts the application shall, within 3 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials; for those meeting the conditions, it shall issue a "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit"; for those not meeting the conditions, it shall state the reasons.  
The "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" shall state the consignor, the carrier, the validity period of a single transport, the starting point, the route, the stopping points, and the types, specifications, and quantities of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经由道路运输烟花爆竹的，除应当遵守《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》外，还应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)随车携带《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》；  
(二)不得违反运输许可事项；  
(三)运输车辆悬挂或者安装符合国家标准的易燃易爆危险物品警示标志；  
(四)烟花爆竹的装载符合国家有关标准和规范；  
(五)装载烟花爆竹的车厢不得载人；  
(六)运输车辆限速行驶，途中经停必须有专人看守；  
(七)出现危险情况立即采取必要的措施，并报告当地公安部门。

**Article 25** For transport of fireworks and firecrackers by road, in addition to complying with the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the following provisions shall also be complied with:  
(1) carrying the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" on the vehicle;  
(2) not violating the matters permitted by the transport permit;  
(3) the transport vehicle shall display or install warning signs for flammable and explosive dangerous articles that comply with national standards;  
(4) the loading of fireworks and firecrackers shall comply with relevant national standards and codes;  
(5) no persons shall be carried in the compartment transporting fireworks and firecrackers;  
(6) the transport vehicle shall be driven at a restricted speed, and during stops on the way, a dedicated person shall be assigned to guard it;  
(7) in the event of a dangerous situation, immediately take necessary measures and report to the local public security department.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　烟花爆竹运达目的地后，收货人应当在3日内将《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》交回发证机关核销。

**Article 26** After the fireworks and firecrackers arrive at the destination, the consignee shall, within 3 days, return the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" to the issuing authority for cancellation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　禁止携带烟花爆竹搭乘公共交通工具。  
禁止邮寄烟花爆竹，禁止在托运的行李、包裹、邮件中夹带烟花爆竹。

**Article 27** It is prohibited to carry fireworks and firecrackers on public transport vehicles.  
It is prohibited to mail fireworks and firecrackers, and it is prohibited to conceal fireworks and firecrackers in checked baggage, parcels, or mail items.

### 第五章　燃放安全

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　燃放烟花爆竹，应当遵守有关法律、法规和规章的规定。县级以上地方人民政府可以根据本行政区域的实际情况，确定限制或者禁止燃放烟花爆竹的时间、地点和种类。

**Article 28** The setting off of fireworks and firecrackers shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules. The local people's government at or above the county level may, based on the actual circumstances of its administrative region, determine the time, place, and types for restricting or prohibiting the setting off of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当开展社会宣传活动，教育公民遵守有关法律、法规和规章，安全燃放烟花爆竹。  
广播、电视、报刊等新闻媒体，应当做好安全燃放烟花爆竹的宣传、教育工作。  
未成年人的监护人应当对未成年人进行安全燃放烟花爆竹的教育。

**Article 29** People's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall conduct social publicity activities to educate citizens to abide by relevant laws, regulations, and rules and to set off fireworks and firecrackers safely.  
News media such as radio, television, and newspapers shall carry out publicity and education on the safe setting off of fireworks and firecrackers.  
Guardians of minors shall educate minors on the safe setting off of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　禁止在下列地点燃放烟花爆竹：  
(一)文物保护单位；  
(二)车站、码头、飞机场等交通枢纽以及铁路线路安全保护区内；  
(三)易燃易爆物品生产、储存单位；  
(四)输变电设施安全保护区内；  
(五)医疗机构、幼儿园、中小学校、敬老院；  
(六)山林、草原等重点防火区；  
(七)县级以上地方人民政府规定的禁止燃放烟花爆竹的其他地点。

**Article 30** It is prohibited to set off fireworks and firecrackers at the following places:  
(1) cultural relic protection units;  
(2) transportation hubs such as railway stations, wharves, and airports, as well as the safety protection zones of railway lines;  
(3) units producing or storing flammable and explosive articles;  
(4) safety protection zones of power transmission and transformation facilities;  
(5) medical institutions, kindergartens, primary and secondary schools, and homes for the elderly;  
(6) key fire prevention areas such as mountain forests and grasslands;  
(7) other places where the setting off of fireworks and firecrackers is prohibited as stipulated by the local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　燃放烟花爆竹，应当按照燃放说明燃放，不得以危害公共安全和人身、财产安全的方式燃放烟花爆竹。

**Article 31** Fireworks and firecrackers shall be set off in accordance with the setting off instructions, and shall not be set off in a manner that endangers public safety or the safety of persons and property.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动，应当按照举办的时间、地点、环境、活动性质、规模以及燃放烟花爆竹的种类、规格和数量，确定危险等级，实行分级管理。分级管理的具体办法，由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 32** For holding fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities, the hazard level shall be determined based on the time, place, environment, nature, and scale of the activity, as well as the types, specifications, and quantities of fireworks and firecrackers to be set off, and graded management shall be implemented. The specific measures for graded management shall be stipulated by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　申请举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动，主办单位应当按照分级管理的规定，向有关人民政府公安部门提出申请，并提交下列有关材料：  
(一)举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动的时间、地点、环境、活动性质、规模；  
(二)燃放烟花爆竹的种类、规格、数量；  
(三)燃放作业方案；  
(四)燃放作业单位、作业人员符合行业标准规定条件的证明。  
受理申请的公安部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内对提交的有关材料进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《焰火燃放许可证》；对不符合条件的，应当说明理由。

**Article 33** When applying to hold fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities, the sponsoring entity shall, in accordance with the provisions on graded management, submit an application to the public security department of the relevant people's government, and provide the following relevant materials:  
(1) the time, place, environment, nature, and scale of the fireworks display or other large-scale fireworks setting off activity;  
(2) the types, specifications, and quantities of fireworks and firecrackers to be set off;  
(3) the setting off operation plan;  
(4) certificates proving that the setting off operation unit and its operators meet the conditions specified in industry standards.  
The public security department that accepts the application shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials; for those meeting the conditions, it shall issue a "Fireworks Setting Off Permit"; for those not meeting the conditions, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动燃放作业单位和作业人员，应当按照焰火燃放安全规程和经许可的燃放作业方案进行燃放作业。

**Article 34** The setting off operation unit and its operators for fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities shall carry out setting off operations in accordance with the fireworks setting off safety procedures and the approved setting off operation plan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公安部门应当加强对危险等级较高的焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动的监督检查。

**Article 35** The public security department shall strengthen supervision and inspection over fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities with relatively high hazard levels.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对未经许可生产、经营烟花爆竹制品，或者向未取得烟花爆竹安全生产许可的单位或者个人销售黑火药、烟火药、引火线的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止非法生产、经营活动，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并没收非法生产、经营的物品及违法所得。  
对未经许可经由道路运输烟花爆竹的，由公安部门责令停止非法运输活动，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并没收非法运输的物品及违法所得。  
非法生产、经营、运输烟花爆竹，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where anyone produces or operates fireworks and firecracker products without a license, or sells black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses to any unit or individual that has not obtained a fireworks and firecrackers work safety license, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order the cessation of the illegal production or business operation activities, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegally produced or operated articles and the illegal gains.  
Where anyone transports fireworks and firecrackers by road without a license, the public security department shall order the cessation of the illegal transport activities, impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegally transported articles and the illegal gains.  
Where the illegal production, business operation, or transport of fireworks and firecrackers constitutes an act against public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; where the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　生产烟花爆竹的企业有下列行为之一的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿，情节严重的，吊销安全生产许可证：  
(一)未按照安全生产许可证核定的产品种类进行生产的；  
(二)生产工序或者生产作业不符合有关国家标准、行业标准的；  
(三)雇佣未经设区的市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门考核合格的人员从事危险工序作业的；  
(四)生产烟花爆竹使用的原料不符合国家标准规定的，或者使用的原料超过国家标准规定的用量限制的；  
(五)使用按照国家标准规定禁止使用或者禁忌配伍的物质生产烟花爆竹的；  
(六)未按照国家标准的规定在烟花爆竹产品上标注燃放说明，或者未在烟花爆竹的包装物上印制易燃易爆危险物品警示标志的。

**Article 37** Where an enterprise that produces fireworks and firecrackers commits any of the following acts, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order it to make corrections within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification, and if the circumstances are serious, its work safety license shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to carry out production in accordance with the product types approved on the work safety license;  
(2) the production processes or production operations do not comply with relevant national standards or industry standards;  
(3) employing personnel who have not passed the examination conducted by the work safety supervision and administration department of the people's government of the city divided into districts to engage in dangerous process operations;  
(4) the raw materials used for producing fireworks and firecrackers do not comply with the provisions of national standards, or the raw materials used exceed the quantity limits specified in national standards;  
(5) using substances that are prohibited for use or are forbidden to be compounded under national standards to produce fireworks and firecrackers;  
(6) failing to mark setting off instructions on fireworks and firecracker products in accordance with the provisions of national standards, or failing to print warning signs for flammable and explosive dangerous articles on the packaging of fireworks and firecrackers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　从事烟花爆竹批发的企业向从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者供应非法生产、经营的烟花爆竹，或者供应按照国家标准规定应由专业燃放人员燃放的烟花爆竹的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止违法行为，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并没收非法经营的物品及违法所得；情节严重的，吊销烟花爆竹经营许可证。  
从事烟花爆竹零售的经营者销售非法生产、经营的烟花爆竹，或者销售按照国家标准规定应由专业燃放人员燃放的烟花爆竹的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止违法行为，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款，并没收非法经营的物品及违法所得；情节严重的，吊销烟花爆竹经营许可证。

**Article 38** Where an enterprise engaged in the wholesale of fireworks and firecrackers supplies to operators engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers illegally produced or operated fireworks and firecrackers, or supplies fireworks and firecrackers that, according to national standards, shall be set off by professional setting off personnel, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order it to cease the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegally operated articles and the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, its fireworks and firecrackers business license shall be revoked.  
Where an operator engaged in the retail of fireworks and firecrackers sells illegally produced or operated fireworks and firecrackers, or sells fireworks and firecrackers that, according to national standards, shall be set off by professional setting off personnel, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order it to cease the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegally operated articles and the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, its fireworks and firecrackers business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　生产、经营、使用黑火药、烟火药、引火线的企业，丢失黑火药、烟火药、引火线未及时向当地安全生产监督管理部门和公安部门报告的，由公安部门对企业主要负责人处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款，对丢失的物品予以追缴。

**Article 39** Where an enterprise that produces, operates, or uses black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses fails to promptly report the loss of black powder, pyrotechnic composition, or fuses to the local work safety supervision and administration department and public security department, the public security department shall impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the principal person-in-charge of the enterprise, and shall recover the lost articles.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　经由道路运输烟花爆竹，有下列行为之一的，由公安部门责令改正，处200元以上2000元以下的罚款：  
(一)违反运输许可事项的；  
(二)未随车携带《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》的；  
(三)运输车辆没有悬挂或者安装符合国家标准的易燃易爆危险物品警示标志的；  
(四)烟花爆竹的装载不符合国家有关标准和规范的；  
(五)装载烟花爆竹的车厢载人的；  
(六)超过危险物品运输车辆规定时速行驶的；  
(七)运输车辆途中经停没有专人看守的；  
(八)运达目的地后，未按规定时间将《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》交回发证机关核销的。

**Article 40** Where, during the transport of fireworks and firecrackers by road, any of the following acts is committed, the public security department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan:  
(1) violating the matters permitted by the transport permit;  
(2) failing to carry the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" on the vehicle;  
(3) the transport vehicle failing to display or install warning signs for flammable and explosive dangerous articles that comply with national standards;  
(4) the loading of fireworks and firecrackers failing to comply with relevant national standards and codes;  
(5) carrying persons in the compartment transporting fireworks and firecrackers;  
(6) driving at a speed exceeding the prescribed speed limit for vehicles transporting dangerous goods;  
(7) during stops on the way, failing to assign a dedicated person to guard the transport vehicle;  
(8) after the arrival at the destination, failing to return the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" to the issuing authority for cancellation within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对携带烟花爆竹搭乘公共交通工具，或者邮寄烟花爆竹以及在托运的行李、包裹、邮件中夹带烟花爆竹的，由公安部门没收非法携带、邮寄、夹带的烟花爆竹，可以并处200元以上1000元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where anyone carries fireworks and firecrackers on a public transport vehicle, or mails fireworks and firecrackers, or conceals fireworks and firecrackers in checked baggage, parcels, or mail items, the public security department shall confiscate the illegally carried, mailed, or concealed fireworks and firecrackers, and may also impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对未经许可举办焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动，或者焰火晚会以及其他大型焰火燃放活动燃放作业单位和作业人员违反焰火燃放安全规程、燃放作业方案进行燃放作业的，由公安部门责令停止燃放，对责任单位处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
在禁止燃放烟花爆竹的时间、地点燃放烟花爆竹，或者以危害公共安全和人身、财产安全的方式燃放烟花爆竹的，由公安部门责令停止燃放，处100元以上500元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 42** Where anyone holds fireworks displays or other large-scale fireworks setting off activities without a license, or the setting off operation unit and its operators for fireworks displays and other large-scale fireworks setting off activities carry out setting off operations in violation of the fireworks setting off safety procedures or the setting off operation plan, the public security department shall order the cessation of the setting off, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the responsible unit.  
Where anyone sets off fireworks and firecrackers at a time or place where setting off is prohibited, or sets off fireworks and firecrackers in a manner that endangers public safety or the safety of persons and property, the public security department shall order the cessation of the setting off, and impose a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan; if the act constitutes an act against public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对没收的非法烟花爆竹以及生产、经营企业弃置的废旧烟花爆竹，应当就地封存，并由公安部门组织销毁、处置。

**Article 43** Confiscated illegal fireworks and firecrackers and discarded old fireworks and firecrackers from production or business operation enterprises shall be sealed up on site, and the public security department shall organize their destruction and disposal.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　安全生产监督管理部门、公安部门、质量监督检验部门、工商行政管理部门的工作人员，在烟花爆竹安全监管工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 44** Where a staff member of the work safety supervision and administration department, the public security department, the quality supervision and inspection department, or the administrative department for industry and commerce abuses his/her powers, neglects his/her duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the work of fireworks and firecrackers safety supervision, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　《烟花爆竹安全生产许可证》、《烟花爆竹经营(批发)许可证》、《烟花爆竹经营(零售)许可证》，由国务院安全生产监督管理部门规定式样；《烟花爆竹道路运输许可证》、《焰火燃放许可证》，由国务院公安部门规定式样。

**Article 45** The format of the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Work Safety License", the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Business (Wholesale) License", and the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Business (Retail) License" shall be stipulated by the work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council; the format of the "Fireworks and Firecrackers Road Transport Permit" and the "Fireworks Setting Off Permit" shall be stipulated by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
