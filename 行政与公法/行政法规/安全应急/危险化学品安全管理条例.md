# 危险化学品安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2013-12-07  
**生效日期**：2011-12-01

> (2002年1月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第344号公布　2011年2月16日国务院第144次常务会议修订通过　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强危险化学品的安全管理，预防和减少危险化学品事故，保障人民群众生命财产安全，保护环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the safety management of hazardous chemicals, preventing and reducing hazardous chemical accidents, safeguarding the life and property safety of the people, and protecting the environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　危险化学品生产、储存、使用、经营和运输的安全管理，适用本条例。  
废弃危险化学品的处置，依照有关环境保护的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 2** The safety management of the production, storage, use, operation and transportation of hazardous chemicals shall be governed by these Regulations.  
The disposal of waste hazardous chemicals shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant laws, administrative regulations and state provisions on environmental protection.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称危险化学品，是指具有毒害、腐蚀、爆炸、燃烧、助燃等性质，对人体、设施、环境具有危害的剧毒化学品和其他化学品。  
危险化学品目录，由国务院安全生产监督管理部门会同国务院工业和信息化、公安、环境保护、卫生、质量监督检验检疫、交通运输、铁路、民用航空、农业主管部门，根据化学品危险特性的鉴别和分类标准确定、公布，并适时调整。

**Article 3** Hazardous chemicals as mentioned in these Regulations refer to highly toxic chemicals and other chemicals that are poisonous, corrosive, explosive, flammable, or combustion-supporting, and that are hazardous to human bodies, facilities and the environment.  
The catalogue of hazardous chemicals shall be determined and published by the safety production supervision and administration department of the State Council jointly with the competent departments of industry and information technology, public security, environmental protection, health, quality supervision, inspection and quarantine, transportation, railway, civil aviation, and agriculture under the State Council, in accordance with the identification and classification standards for chemical hazard characteristics, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　危险化学品安全管理，应当坚持安全第一、预防为主、综合治理的方针，强化和落实企业的主体责任。  
生产、储存、使用、经营、运输危险化学品的单位(以下统称危险化学品单位)的主要负责人对本单位的危险化学品安全管理工作全面负责。  
危险化学品单位应当具备法律、行政法规规定和国家标准、行业标准要求的安全条件，建立、健全安全管理规章制度和岗位安全责任制度，对从业人员进行安全教育、法制教育和岗位技术培训。从业人员应当接受教育和培训，考核合格后上岗作业；对有资格要求的岗位，应当配备依法取得相应资格的人员。

**Article 4** The safety management of hazardous chemicals shall adhere to the policy of safety first, prevention prioritized, and comprehensive management, and shall strengthen and implement the primary responsibility of enterprises.  
The principal responsible person of a unit that produces, stores, uses, operates or transports hazardous chemicals (hereinafter collectively referred to as a hazardous chemical unit) shall be fully responsible for the safety management of hazardous chemicals in that unit.  
A hazardous chemical unit shall possess the safety conditions required by laws, administrative regulations, and national standards or industry standards, establish and improve safety management rules and regulations and post safety responsibility system, and provide safety education, legal education and post technical training to its employees. Employees shall accept education and training and may only work at their posts after passing the assessment; for posts with qualification requirements, persons who have obtained corresponding qualifications according to law shall be assigned.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人不得生产、经营、使用国家禁止生产、经营、使用的危险化学品。  
国家对危险化学品的使用有限制性规定的，任何单位和个人不得违反限制性规定使用危险化学品。

**Article 5** No unit or individual may produce, operate or use any hazardous chemical the production, operation or use of which is prohibited by the State.  
Where the State has restrictive provisions on the use of hazardous chemicals, no unit or individual may use hazardous chemicals in violation of the restrictive provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对危险化学品的生产、储存、使用、经营、运输实施安全监督管理的有关部门(以下统称负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门)，依照下列规定履行职责：  
(一)安全生产监督管理部门负责危险化学品安全监督管理综合工作，组织确定、公布、调整危险化学品目录，对新建、改建、扩建生产、储存危险化学品(包括使用长输管道输送危险化学品，下同)的建设项目进行安全条件审查，核发危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品安全使用许可证和危险化学品经营许可证，并负责危险化学品登记工作。  
(二)公安机关负责危险化学品的公共安全管理，核发剧毒化学品购买许可证、剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，并负责危险化学品运输车辆的道路交通安全管理。  
(三)质量监督检验检疫部门负责核发危险化学品及其包装物、容器(不包括储存危险化学品的固定式大型储罐，下同)生产企业的工业产品生产许可证，并依法对其产品质量实施监督，负责对进出口危险化学品及其包装实施检验。  
(四)环境保护主管部门负责废弃危险化学品处置的监督管理，组织危险化学品的环境危害性鉴定和环境风险程度评估，确定实施重点环境管理的危险化学品，负责危险化学品环境管理登记和新化学物质环境管理登记；依照职责分工调查相关危险化学品环境污染事故和生态破坏事件，负责危险化学品事故现场的应急环境监测。  
(五)交通运输主管部门负责危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的许可以及运输工具的安全管理，对危险化学品水路运输安全实施监督，负责危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员的资格认定。铁路监管部门负责危险化学品铁路运输及其运输工具的安全管理。民用航空主管部门负责危险化学品航空运输以及航空运输企业及其运输工具的安全管理。  
(六)卫生主管部门负责危险化学品毒性鉴定的管理，负责组织、协调危险化学品事故受伤人员的医疗卫生救援工作。  
(七)工商行政管理部门依据有关部门的许可证件，核发危险化学品生产、储存、经营、运输企业营业执照，查处危险化学品经营企业违法采购危险化学品的行为。  
(八)邮政管理部门负责依法查处寄递危险化学品的行为。

**Article 6** The relevant departments that conduct safety supervision and administration over the production, storage, use, operation and transportation of hazardous chemicals (hereinafter collectively referred to as the departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals) shall perform their duties in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) The safety production supervision and administration department shall be responsible for the comprehensive work of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals, organize the determination, publication and adjustment of the catalogue of hazardous chemicals, conduct safety condition review for construction projects of newly built, rebuilt or expanded production and storage of hazardous chemicals (including the transportation of hazardous chemicals through long-distance pipelines, hereinafter the same), issue the hazardous chemical safety production license, the hazardous chemical safety use permit and the hazardous chemical operation permit, and be responsible for the registration of hazardous chemicals.  
(2) The public security organ shall be responsible for the public safety management of hazardous chemicals, issue the purchase permit for highly toxic chemicals and the road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals, and be responsible for the road traffic safety management of vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals.  
(3) The quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department shall be responsible for issuing the industrial product production license to enterprises producing hazardous chemicals and their packaging and containers (excluding fixed large storage tanks for storing hazardous chemicals, hereinafter the same), and supervise the quality of their products according to law, and be responsible for inspecting imported and exported hazardous chemicals and their packaging.  
(4) The competent environmental protection department shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the disposal of waste hazardous chemicals, organize the expert evaluation of environmental hazards and assessment of environmental risk degree of hazardous chemicals, determine the hazardous chemicals subject to key environmental management, be responsible for the registration of environmental management of hazardous chemicals and the registration of environmental management of new chemical substances; investigate relevant environmental pollution accidents and ecological damage incidents caused by hazardous chemicals in accordance with the division of duties, and be responsible for emergency environmental monitoring at the scene of hazardous chemical accidents.  
(5) The competent transportation department shall be responsible for the permission of road and waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals and the safety management of means of transport, supervise the safety of waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals, and be responsible for the qualification determination of drivers of road and waterway transportation enterprises for hazardous chemicals, crew members, loading and unloading managers, escorts, declarants, and container packing site inspectors. The railway supervision department shall be responsible for the safety management of railway transportation of hazardous chemicals and the means of transport thereof. The competent civil aviation department shall be responsible for the safety management of air transportation of hazardous chemicals and the means of transport of air transportation enterprises and their means of transport.  
(6) The competent health department shall be responsible for the management of the toxicity expert evaluation of hazardous chemicals, and be responsible for organizing and coordinating the medical and health rescue work for persons injured in hazardous chemical accidents.  
(7) The administrative department for industry and commerce shall, pursuant to the licenses of relevant departments, issue the business licenses for enterprises producing, storing, operating and transporting hazardous chemicals, and investigate and punish the acts of hazardous chemical operating enterprises illegally purchasing hazardous chemicals.  
(8) The postal administrative department shall be responsible for investigating and punishing the act of sending hazardous chemicals by post according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法进行监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
(一)进入危险化学品作业场所实施现场检查，向有关单位和人员了解情况，查阅、复制有关文件、资料；  
(二)发现危险化学品事故隐患，责令立即消除或者限期消除；  
(三)对不符合法律、行政法规、规章规定或者国家标准、行业标准要求的设施、设备、装置、器材、运输工具，责令立即停止使用；  
(四)经本部门主要负责人批准，查封违法生产、储存、使用、经营危险化学品的场所，扣押违法生产、储存、使用、经营、运输的危险化学品以及用于违法生产、使用、运输危险化学品的原材料、设备、运输工具；  
(五)发现影响危险化学品安全的违法行为，当场予以纠正或者责令限期改正。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法进行监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示执法证件；有关单位和个人对依法进行的监督检查应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻碍。

**Article 7** The departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals may take the following measures when conducting supervision and inspection according to law:  
(1) Enter the places where hazardous chemical operations are carried out to conduct on-site inspection, inquire about the situation from relevant units and individuals, and consult and copy relevant documents and materials;  
(2) If potential hazardous chemical accidents are discovered, order immediate elimination or elimination within a time limit;  
(3) If facilities, equipment, devices, apparatus, or means of transport that do not meet the requirements of laws, administrative regulations, rules or national standards or industry standards are found, order immediate cessation of use;  
(4) Seal up the places where hazardous chemicals are illegally produced, stored, used or operated and seize the hazardous chemicals illegally produced, stored, used, operated or transported and the raw materials, equipment and means of transport used for illegally producing, using or transporting hazardous chemicals, subject to the approval of the principal responsible person of the department;  
(5) If illegal acts affecting the safety of hazardous chemicals are discovered, correct them on the spot or order correction within a time limit.  
When the departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals conduct supervision and inspection according to law, there shall be no less than two inspectors, and they shall produce their law enforcement certificates; the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted according to law, and shall not refuse or obstruct it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府应当建立危险化学品安全监督管理工作协调机制，支持、督促负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门依法履行职责，协调、解决危险化学品安全监督管理工作中的重大问题。  
负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门应当相互配合、密切协作，依法加强对危险化学品的安全监督管理。

**Article 8** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism for the work of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals, support and supervise the departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals to perform their duties according to law, and coordinate and solve major problems in the work of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals.  
The departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals shall cooperate with each other and closely collaborate, and strengthen the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门举报。负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门接到举报，应当及时依法处理；对不属于本部门职责的，应当及时移送有关部门处理。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the departments with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals any act violating the provisions of these Regulations. Upon receiving a report, the department with the duty of safety supervision and administration over hazardous chemicals shall handle it in a timely manner according to law; if it is not within the functions and duties of such department, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the relevant department for handling.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励危险化学品生产企业和使用危险化学品从事生产的企业采用有利于提高安全保障水平的先进技术、工艺、设备以及自动控制系统，鼓励对危险化学品实行专门储存、统一配送、集中销售。

**Article 10** The State encourages enterprises that produce hazardous chemicals and enterprises that use hazardous chemicals for production to adopt advanced technologies, processes, equipment and automatic control systems that are conducive to raising the level of safety guarantee, and encourages the specialized storage, unified distribution and centralized sale of hazardous chemicals.

### 第二章　生产、储存安全

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家对危险化学品的生产、储存实行统筹规划、合理布局。  
国务院工业和信息化主管部门以及国务院其他有关部门依据各自职责，负责危险化学品生产、储存的行业规划和布局。  
地方人民政府组织编制城乡规划，应当根据本地区的实际情况，按照确保安全的原则，规划适当区域专门用于危险化学品的生产、储存。

**Article 11** The State shall implement overall planning and rational layout for the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.  
The competent department of industry and information technology under the State Council and other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective duties, be responsible for the industrial planning and layout of the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.  
When organizing the formulation of urban and rural planning, the local people's governments shall, based on the actual conditions of the region and in accordance with the principle of ensuring safety, plan appropriate areas specifically for the production and storage of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　新建、改建、扩建生产、储存危险化学品的建设项目(以下简称建设项目)，应当由安全生产监督管理部门进行安全条件审查。  
建设单位应当对建设项目进行安全条件论证，委托具备国家规定的资质条件的机构对建设项目进行安全评价，并将安全条件论证和安全评价的情况报告报建设项目所在地设区的市级以上人民政府安全生产监督管理部门；安全生产监督管理部门应当自收到报告之日起45日内作出审查决定，并书面通知建设单位。具体办法由国务院安全生产监督管理部门制定。  
新建、改建、扩建储存、装卸危险化学品的港口建设项目，由港口行政管理部门按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定进行安全条件审查。

**Article 12** For construction projects of newly built, rebuilt or expanded production and storage of hazardous chemicals (hereinafter referred to as construction projects), the safety production supervision and administration department shall conduct a safety condition review.  
The construction unit shall conduct a safety condition demonstration for the construction project, entrust an institution with the qualification conditions prescribed by the State to conduct a safety evaluation for the construction project, and submit a report on the safety condition demonstration and safety evaluation to the safety production supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts where the construction project is located; the safety production supervision and administration department shall, within 45 days from the date of receipt of the report, make a review decision and notify the construction unit in writing. The specific measures shall be formulated by the safety production supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
For port construction projects of newly built, rebuilt or expanded storage and loading/unloading of hazardous chemicals, the port administrative department shall conduct a safety condition review in accordance with the provisions of the competent transportation department of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当对其铺设的危险化学品管道设置明显标志，并对危险化学品管道定期检查、检测。  
进行可能危及危险化学品管道安全的施工作业，施工单位应当在开工的7日前书面通知管道所属单位，并与管道所属单位共同制定应急预案，采取相应的安全防护措施。管道所属单位应当指派专门人员到现场进行管道安全保护指导。

**Article 13** A unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals shall set up conspicuous signs for the hazardous chemical pipelines it has laid, and shall regularly inspect and test the hazardous chemical pipelines.  
Before carrying out construction operations that may endanger the safety of hazardous chemical pipelines, the construction unit shall notify the unit to which the pipelines belong in writing seven days prior to the commencement of the work, and shall jointly formulate an emergency response plan with the pipeline-owning unit and take corresponding safety protection measures. The pipeline-owning unit shall assign specialized personnel to provide guidance on pipeline safety protection at the site.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　危险化学品生产企业进行生产前，应当依照《安全生产许可证条例》的规定，取得危险化学品安全生产许可证。  
生产列入国家实行生产许可证制度的工业产品目录的危险化学品的企业，应当依照《中华人民共和国工业产品生产许可证管理条例》的规定，取得工业产品生产许可证。  
负责颁发危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证的部门，应当将其颁发许可证的情况及时向同级工业和信息化主管部门、环境保护主管部门和公安机关通报。

**Article 14** Before carrying out production, an enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals shall obtain a hazardous chemical safety production license in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Safety Production Licenses.  
An enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals listed in the catalogue of industrial products subject to the State's production license system shall obtain an industrial product production license in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Industrial Product Production Licenses.  
The departments responsible for issuing hazardous chemical safety production licenses and industrial product production licenses shall promptly notify the competent department of industry and information technology, the competent department of environmental protection and the public security organ at the same level of the licenses they have issued.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　危险化学品生产企业应当提供与其生产的危险化学品相符的化学品安全技术说明书，并在危险化学品包装(包括外包装件)上粘贴或者拴挂与包装内危险化学品相符的化学品安全标签。化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签所载明的内容应当符合国家标准的要求。  
危险化学品生产企业发现其生产的危险化学品有新的危险特性的，应当立即公告，并及时修订其化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签。

**Article 15** An enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals shall provide a chemical safety data sheet consistent with the hazardous chemicals it produces, and shall affix or attach a chemical safety label consistent with the hazardous chemicals inside the packaging onto the packaging of hazardous chemicals (including the outer packaging). The content contained in the chemical safety data sheet and the chemical safety label shall meet the requirements of national standards.  
If an enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals discovers that its hazardous chemicals have new hazardous characteristics, it shall immediately make an announcement and promptly revise the chemical safety data sheet and the chemical safety label.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生产实施重点环境管理的危险化学品的企业，应当按照国务院环境保护主管部门的规定，将该危险化学品向环境中释放等相关信息向环境保护主管部门报告。环境保护主管部门可以根据情况采取相应的环境风险控制措施。

**Article 16** An enterprise that produces hazardous chemicals subject to key environmental management shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent environmental protection department of the State Council, report to the competent environmental protection department information such as the release of such hazardous chemicals into the environment. The competent environmental protection department may, based on the circumstances, take corresponding environmental risk control measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　危险化学品的包装应当符合法律、行政法规、规章的规定以及国家标准、行业标准的要求。  
危险化学品包装物、容器的材质以及危险化学品包装的型式、规格、方法和单件质量(重量)，应当与所包装的危险化学品的性质和用途相适应。

**Article 17** The packaging of hazardous chemicals shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations and rules as well as the requirements of national standards and industry standards.  
The materials of hazardous chemical packaging and containers, and the form, specification, method and unit mass (weight) of hazardous chemical packaging shall be appropriate to the nature and use of the hazardous chemicals being packaged.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　生产列入国家实行生产许可证制度的工业产品目录的危险化学品包装物、容器的企业，应当依照《中华人民共和国工业产品生产许可证管理条例》的规定，取得工业产品生产许可证；其生产的危险化学品包装物、容器经国务院质量监督检验检疫部门认定的检验机构检验合格，方可出厂销售。  
运输危险化学品的船舶及其配载的容器，应当按照国家船舶检验规范进行生产，并经海事管理机构认定的船舶检验机构检验合格，方可投入使用。  
对重复使用的危险化学品包装物、容器，使用单位在重复使用前应当进行检查；发现存在安全隐患的，应当维修或者更换。使用单位应当对检查情况作出记录，记录的保存期限不得少于2年。

**Article 18** An enterprise that produces hazardous chemical packaging and containers listed in the catalogue of industrial products subject to the State's production license system shall obtain an industrial product production license in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Industrial Product Production Licenses; the hazardous chemical packaging and containers produced by it may leave the factory for sale only after passing the inspection conducted by an inspection institution recognized by the quality supervision, inspection and quarantine department of the State Council.  
Ships transporting hazardous chemicals and the containers loaded on them shall be produced in accordance with the national ship inspection specifications, and may be put into use only after passing the inspection conducted by a ship inspection institution recognized by the maritime administrative agency.  
For reusable hazardous chemical packaging and containers, the user unit shall inspect them before reusing them; if any potential safety hazard is found, it shall repair or replace them. The user unit shall keep records of the inspections, and the retention period of the records shall not be less than two years.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　危险化学品生产装置或者储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施(运输工具加油站、加气站除外)，与下列场所、设施、区域的距离应当符合国家有关规定：  
(一)居住区以及商业中心、公园等人员密集场所；  
(二)学校、医院、影剧院、体育场(馆)等公共设施；  
(三)饮用水源、水厂以及水源保护区；  
(四)车站、码头(依法经许可从事危险化学品装卸作业的除外)、机场以及通信干线、通信枢纽、铁路线路、道路交通干线、水路交通干线、地铁风亭以及地铁站出入口；  
(五)基本农田保护区、基本草原、畜禽遗传资源保护区、畜禽规模化养殖场(养殖小区)、渔业水域以及种子、种畜禽、水产苗种生产基地；  
(六)河流、湖泊、风景名胜区、自然保护区；  
(七)军事禁区、军事管理区；  
(八)法律、行政法规规定的其他场所、设施、区域。  
已建的危险化学品生产装置或者储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施不符合前款规定的，由所在地设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门会同有关部门监督其所属单位在规定期限内进行整改；需要转产、停产、搬迁、关闭的，由本级人民政府决定并组织实施。  
储存数量构成重大危险源的危险化学品储存设施的选址，应当避开地震活动断层和容易发生洪灾、地质灾害的区域。  
本条例所称重大危险源，是指生产、储存、使用或者搬运危险化学品，且危险化学品的数量等于或者超过临界量的单元(包括场所和设施)。

**Article 19** The distance between the hazardous chemical production facilities or the hazardous chemical storage facilities that constitute a major hazard source (excluding gas stations and fueling stations for means of transport) and the following places, facilities and areas shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) Residential areas as well as densely populated places such as commercial centers and parks;  
(2) Public facilities such as schools, hospitals, theaters, and stadiums (gymnasiums);  
(3) Drinking water sources, water plants and water source protection zones;  
(4) Stations, docks (excluding those legally licensed for hazardous chemical loading and unloading operations), airports, communication trunk lines, communication hubs, railway lines, road traffic arteries, waterway traffic arteries, subway ventilation shafts and subway station entrances and exits;  
(5) Basic farmland protection zones, basic grasslands, livestock and poultry genetic resources protection zones, large-scale livestock and poultry farms (breeding communities), fishery waters, and production bases for seeds, improved livestock and poultry breeds, and aquatic seedlings;  
(6) Rivers, lakes, scenic spots, and nature reserves;  
(7) Military restricted zones and military management zones;  
(8) Other places, facilities and areas stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.  
If existing hazardous chemical production facilities or hazardous chemical storage facilities that constitute a major hazard source do not conform to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the safety production supervision and administration department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts where they are located shall, together with other relevant departments, supervise the unit to which they belong to make rectifications within a specified time limit; if product conversion, production suspension, relocation or closure is required, the people's government at the same level shall make the decision and organize its implementation.  
The site selection for hazardous chemical storage facilities that constitute a major hazard source in terms of storage quantity shall avoid seismic active fault zones and areas prone to floods and geological disasters.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "major hazard source" means a unit (including places and facilities) that produces, stores, uses or transports hazardous chemicals, and the quantity of hazardous chemicals equals or exceeds the critical quantity.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当根据其生产、储存的危险化学品的种类和危险特性，在作业场所设置相应的监测、监控、通风、防晒、调温、防火、灭火、防爆、泄压、防毒、中和、防潮、防雷、防静电、防腐、防泄漏以及防护围堤或者隔离操作等安全设施、设备，并按照国家标准、行业标准或者国家有关规定对安全设施、设备进行经常性维护、保养，保证安全设施、设备的正常使用。  
生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当在其作业场所和安全设施、设备上设置明显的安全警示标志。

**Article 20** A unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals shall, according to the types and hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals it produces or stores, set up corresponding safety facilities and equipment such as monitoring, surveillance, ventilation, sun protection, temperature regulation, fire prevention, fire extinguishing, explosion proofing, pressure relief, poison prevention, neutralization, moisture proofing, lightning protection, antistatic, anti-corrosion, leakage prevention, protective dikes or isolation operation in the workplace, and shall regularly maintain and repair the safety facilities and equipment in accordance with national standards, industry standards or relevant provisions of the State to ensure the normal use of the safety facilities and equipment.  
A unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals shall set up conspicuous safety warning signs in its workplace and on safety facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位，应当在其作业场所设置通信、报警装置，并保证处于适用状态。

**Article 21** Units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall install communication and alarm devices in their workplaces and ensure they are in a usable condition.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产、储存危险化学品的企业，应当委托具备国家规定的资质条件的机构，对本企业的安全生产条件每3年进行一次安全评价，提出安全评价报告。安全评价报告的内容应当包括对安全生产条件存在的问题进行整改的方案。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业，应当将安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况报所在地县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门备案。在港区内储存危险化学品的企业，应当将安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况报港口行政管理部门备案。

**Article 22** Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall entrust an institution with the qualifications and conditions prescribed by the State to conduct a safety assessment on their own work safety conditions every three years and issue a safety assessment report. The contents of the safety assessment report shall include a plan for rectifying the problems existing in the work safety conditions.  
Enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall report the implementation of the safety assessment report and the rectification plan to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the county level for archival filing. For enterprises that store hazardous chemicals in a port area, they shall report the implementation of the safety assessment report and the rectification plan to the port administrative department for archival filing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生产、储存剧毒化学品或者国务院公安部门规定的可用于制造爆炸物品的危险化学品(以下简称易制爆危险化学品)的单位，应当如实记录其生产、储存的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的数量、流向，并采取必要的安全防范措施，防止剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗；发现剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗的，应当立即向当地公安机关报告。  
生产、储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位，应当设置治安保卫机构，配备专职治安保卫人员。

**Article 23** Units that produce or store highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals that may be used to produce explosive articles as prescribed by the public security department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as explosive-prone hazardous chemicals) shall faithfully record the quantities and flow directions of the highly toxic chemicals and explosive-prone hazardous chemicals they produce or store, and take necessary safety prevention measures to prevent the loss or theft of such chemicals; if they discover any loss or theft of such chemicals, they shall immediately report to the local public security organ.  
Units that produce or store highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals shall set up a public security protection institution and equip it with full-time public security protection personnel.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　危险化学品应当储存在专用仓库、专用场地或者专用储存室(以下统称专用仓库)内，并由专人负责管理；剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品，应当在专用仓库内单独存放，并实行双人收发、双人保管制度。  
危险化学品的储存方式、方法以及储存数量应当符合国家标准或者国家有关规定。

**Article 24** Hazardous chemicals shall be stored in dedicated warehouses, dedicated sites or dedicated storage rooms (hereinafter collectively referred to as dedicated warehouses) and shall be managed by specially assigned persons; highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose stored quantities constitute a major hazard source shall be stored separately in dedicated warehouses, and the system of dual-person receipt and dispatch, and dual-person custody shall be implemented.  
The storage mode, method and quantity of hazardous chemicals shall conform to the national standards or the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　储存危险化学品的单位应当建立危险化学品出入库核查、登记制度。  
对剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品，储存单位应当将其储存数量、储存地点以及管理人员的情况，报所在地县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门(在港区内储存的，报港口行政管理部门)和公安机关备案。

**Article 25** Units that store hazardous chemicals shall establish a system for checking and registering the entry and exit of hazardous chemicals.  
For highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose stored quantities constitute a major hazard source, the storage unit shall report the storage quantity, storage location, and information on the management personnel to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the county level (to the port administrative department if stored in a port area) and the public security organ for archival filing.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　危险化学品专用仓库应当符合国家标准、行业标准的要求，并设置明显的标志。储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的专用仓库，应当按照国家有关规定设置相应的技术防范设施。  
储存危险化学品的单位应当对其危险化学品专用仓库的安全设施、设备定期进行检测、检验。

**Article 26** Dedicated warehouses for hazardous chemicals shall meet the requirements of national standards and industry standards and shall be provided with conspicuous signs. Dedicated warehouses for storing highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals shall be equipped with corresponding technical prevention facilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Units that store hazardous chemicals shall regularly conduct testing and inspection of the safety facilities and equipment of their dedicated warehouses for hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　生产、储存危险化学品的单位转产、停产、停业或者解散的，应当采取有效措施，及时、妥善处置其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存的危险化学品，不得丢弃危险化学品；处置方案应当报所在地县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门、工业和信息化主管部门、环境保护主管部门和公安机关备案。安全生产监督管理部门应当会同环境保护主管部门和公安机关对处置情况进行监督检查，发现未依照规定处置的，应当责令其立即处置。

**Article 27** If a unit that produces or stores hazardous chemicals changes its line of production, suspends production, suspends business or dissolves, it shall take effective measures to promptly and properly dispose of its hazardous chemical production facilities, storage facilities and the hazardous chemicals in stock, and shall not discard hazardous chemicals; the disposal plan shall be reported to the work safety supervision and management department, the competent department of industry and information technology, the competent department of environmental protection, and the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level for archival filing. The work safety supervision and management department shall, together with the competent department of environmental protection and the public security organ, supervise and inspect the disposal, and if it discovers that the disposal has not been carried out in accordance with the provisions, it shall order the unit to dispose immediately.

### 第三章　使用安全

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　使用危险化学品的单位，其使用条件(包括工艺)应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和国家标准、行业标准的要求，并根据所使用的危险化学品的种类、危险特性以及使用量和使用方式，建立、健全使用危险化学品的安全管理规章制度和安全操作规程，保证危险化学品的安全使用。

**Article 28** Units that use hazardous chemicals shall ensure that their use conditions (including processes) comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the requirements of national standards and industry standards, and shall establish and improve safety management rules and regulations and safe operation procedures for the use of hazardous chemicals based on the types, hazardous characteristics, quantity used, and manner of use of the hazardous chemicals used, to ensure the safe use of hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　使用危险化学品从事生产并且使用量达到规定数量的化工企业(属于危险化学品生产企业的除外，下同)，应当依照本条例的规定取得危险化学品安全使用许可证。  
前款规定的危险化学品使用量的数量标准，由国务院安全生产监督管理部门会同国务院公安部门、农业主管部门确定并公布。

**Article 29** Chemical enterprises that use hazardous chemicals for production and whose usage volume reaches the prescribed quantity (excluding those that are hazardous chemical production enterprises, the same below) shall obtain a hazardous chemical safety use permit in accordance with these Regulations.  
The quantity standards for the usage volume of hazardous chemicals as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be determined and published by the work safety supervision and management department of the State Council jointly with the public security department of the State Council and the competent agricultural department.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请危险化学品安全使用许可证的化工企业，除应当符合[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)的规定外，还应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与所使用的危险化学品相适应的专业技术人员；  
(二)有安全管理机构和专职安全管理人员；  
(三)有符合国家规定的危险化学品事故应急预案和必要的应急救援器材、设备；  
(四)依法进行了安全评价。

**Article 30** In addition to complying with the provisions of Article 28 of these Regulations, a chemical enterprise applying for a hazardous chemical safety use permit shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having professional and technical personnel suitable for the hazardous chemicals used;  
(2) Having a safety management institution and full-time safety management personnel;  
(3) Having a hazardous chemical accident emergency response plan that conforms to the national provisions and necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices;  
(4) Having conducted a safety assessment according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　申请危险化学品安全使用许可证的化工企业，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请，并提交其符合[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定条件的证明材料。设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当依法进行审查，自收到证明材料之日起45日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发危险化学品安全使用许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
安全生产监督管理部门应当将其颁发危险化学品安全使用许可证的情况及时向同级环境保护主管部门和公安机关通报。

**Article 31** A chemical enterprise applying for a hazardous chemical safety use permit shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the level of a city divided into districts, and provide evidentiary materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 30 of these Regulations. The work safety supervision and management department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts shall conduct a review according to law, and shall make a decision to approve or disapprove within 45 days from the date of receiving the evidentiary materials. If approval is granted, a hazardous chemical safety use permit shall be issued; if disapproval is granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons given.  
The work safety supervision and management department shall promptly notify the competent department of environmental protection and the public security organ at the same level of the issuance of hazardous chemical safety use permits.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　[本条例第十六条](#art-16)关于生产实施重点环境管理的危险化学品的企业的规定，适用于使用实施重点环境管理的危险化学品从事生产的企业；第二十条、第二十一条、第二十三条第一款、第二十七条关于生产、储存危险化学品的单位的规定，适用于使用危险化学品的单位；第二十二条关于生产、储存危险化学品的企业的规定，适用于使用危险化学品从事生产的企业。

**Article 32** The provisions of Article 16 of these Regulations regarding enterprises that produce hazardous chemicals subject to key environmental management shall apply to enterprises that use such hazardous chemicals for production; the provisions of Articles 20, 21, the first paragraph of Article 23, and Article 27 regarding units that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall apply to units that use hazardous chemicals; the provisions of Article 22 regarding enterprises that produce or store hazardous chemicals shall apply to enterprises that use hazardous chemicals for production.

### 第四章　经营安全

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家对危险化学品经营(包括仓储经营，下同)实行许可制度。未经许可，任何单位和个人不得经营危险化学品。  
依法设立的危险化学品生产企业在其厂区范围内销售本企业生产的危险化学品，不需要取得危险化学品经营许可。  
依照《中华人民共和国港口法》的规定取得港口经营许可证的港口经营人，在港区内从事危险化学品仓储经营，不需要取得危险化学品经营许可。

**Article 33** The State implements a licensing system for the operation of hazardous chemicals (including storage operation, the same below). No unit or individual may operate hazardous chemicals without a license.  
A hazardous chemical production enterprise established according to law that sells the hazardous chemicals it produces within its own factory premises does not need to obtain a hazardous chemical operation license.  
A port operator that has obtained a port operation license in accordance with the provisions of the Port Law of the People's Republic of China and engages in storage operation of hazardous chemicals within a port area does not need to obtain a hazardous chemical operation license.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　从事危险化学品经营的企业应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有符合国家标准、行业标准的经营场所，储存危险化学品的，还应当有符合国家标准、行业标准的储存设施；  
(二)从业人员经过专业技术培训并经考核合格；  
(三)有健全的安全管理规章制度；  
(四)有专职安全管理人员；  
(五)有符合国家规定的危险化学品事故应急预案和必要的应急救援器材、设备；  
(六)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 34** An enterprise engaged in the operation of hazardous chemicals shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having an operating site that conforms to national standards and industry standards, and if it stores hazardous chemicals, it shall also have storage facilities that conform to national standards and industry standards;  
(2) Having personnel who have undergone professional technical training and passed assessment;  
(3) Having sound safety management rules and regulations;  
(4) Having full-time safety management personnel;  
(5) Having a hazardous chemical accident emergency response plan that conforms to the national provisions and necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices;  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从事剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品经营的企业，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请，从事其他危险化学品经营的企业，应当向所在地县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请(有储存设施的，应当向所在地设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门提出申请)。申请人应当提交其符合[本条例第三十四条](#art-34)规定条件的证明材料。设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门或者县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当依法进行审查，并对申请人的经营场所、储存设施进行现场核查，自收到证明材料之日起30日内作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发危险化学品经营许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门和县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当将其颁发危险化学品经营许可证的情况及时向同级环境保护主管部门和公安机关通报。  
申请人持危险化学品经营许可证向工商行政管理部门办理登记手续后，方可从事危险化学品经营活动。法律、行政法规或者国务院规定经营危险化学品还需要经其他有关部门许可的，申请人向工商行政管理部门办理登记手续时还应当持相应的许可证件。

**Article 35** An enterprise engaged in the operation of highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the level of a city divided into districts; an enterprise engaged in the operation of other hazardous chemicals shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the county level (if it has storage facilities, it shall submit an application to the work safety supervision and management department of the local people's government at the level of a city divided into districts). The applicant shall provide evidentiary materials proving that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 34 of these Regulations. The work safety supervision and management department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or at the county level shall conduct a review according to law, and conduct an on-site inspection of the applicant's operating site and storage facilities, and shall make a decision to approve or disapprove within 30 days from the date of receiving the evidentiary materials. If approval is granted, a hazardous chemical operation license shall be issued; if disapproval is granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons given.  
The work safety supervision and management departments of the people's governments at the level of a city divided into districts and at the county level shall promptly notify the competent department of environmental protection and the public security organ at the same level of the issuance of hazardous chemical operation licenses.  
Only after the applicant has gone through the registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce by presenting the hazardous chemical operation license may it engage in the operation of hazardous chemicals. If laws, administrative regulations or the State Council provide that the operation of hazardous chemicals also requires permission from other relevant departments, the applicant shall also present the corresponding license documents when going through the registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　危险化学品经营企业储存危险化学品的，应当遵守本条例第二章关于储存危险化学品的规定。危险化学品商店内只能存放民用小包装的危险化学品。

**Article 36** If a hazardous chemical operating enterprise stores hazardous chemicals, it shall comply with the provisions of Chapter II of these Regulations on the storage of hazardous chemicals. Only small-packaged hazardous chemicals for civilian use may be stored in hazardous chemical shops.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　危险化学品经营企业不得向未经许可从事危险化学品生产、经营活动的企业采购危险化学品，不得经营没有化学品安全技术说明书或者化学品安全标签的危险化学品。

**Article 37** A hazardous chemical operating enterprise shall not purchase hazardous chemicals from enterprises that engage in the production or operation of hazardous chemicals without a license, and shall not operate hazardous chemicals that are not accompanied by a chemical safety data sheet or a chemical safety label.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　依法取得危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品安全使用许可证、危险化学品经营许可证的企业，凭相应的许可证件购买剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品。民用爆炸物品生产企业凭民用爆炸物品生产许可证购买易制爆危险化学品。  
前款规定以外的单位购买剧毒化学品的，应当向所在地县级人民政府公安机关申请取得剧毒化学品购买许可证；购买易制爆危险化学品的，应当持本单位出具的合法用途说明。  
个人不得购买剧毒化学品(属于剧毒化学品的农药除外)和易制爆危险化学品。

**Article 38** Enterprises that have lawfully obtained a hazardous chemical safety production permit, a hazardous chemical safety use permit, or a hazardous chemical operation permit may purchase highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals by presenting the corresponding permit. Civil explosives production enterprises may purchase explosive-prone hazardous chemicals by presenting a civil explosives production permit.  
Units other than those prescribed in the preceding paragraph that purchase highly toxic chemicals shall apply to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level for a permit for the purchase of highly toxic chemicals; those that purchase explosive-prone hazardous chemicals shall present a statement of lawful use issued by the unit itself.  
Individuals shall not purchase highly toxic chemicals (excluding pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　申请取得剧毒化学品购买许可证，申请人应当向所在地县级人民政府公安机关提交下列材料：  
(一)营业执照或者法人证书(登记证书)的复印件；  
(二)拟购买的剧毒化学品品种、数量的说明；  
(三)购买剧毒化学品用途的说明；  
(四)经办人的身份证明。  
县级人民政府公安机关应当自收到前款规定的材料之日起3日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发剧毒化学品购买许可证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
剧毒化学品购买许可证管理办法由国务院公安部门制定。

**Article 39** To apply for a permit for the purchase of highly toxic chemicals, the applicant shall submit the following materials to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level:  
(1) A copy of the business license or the legal person certificate (registration certificate);  
(2) A statement on the types and quantities of the highly toxic chemicals to be purchased;  
(3) A statement on the purpose of the purchase of the highly toxic chemicals;  
(4) The identity certificate of the person handling the matter.  
The public security organ of the people's government at the county level shall, within 3 days from the date of receiving the materials specified in the preceding paragraph, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approval is granted, a permit for the purchase of highly toxic chemicals shall be issued; if disapproval is granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons given.  
The administrative measures for permits for the purchase of highly toxic chemicals shall be formulated by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，应当查验[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件，不得向不具有相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品。对持剧毒化学品购买许可证购买剧毒化学品的，应当按照许可证载明的品种、数量销售。  
禁止向个人销售剧毒化学品(属于剧毒化学品的农药除外)和易制爆危险化学品。

**Article 40** Hazardous chemical production enterprises and operating enterprises, when selling highly toxic chemicals or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals, shall examine the relevant license documents or supporting documents as prescribed in the first and second paragraphs of Article 38 of these Regulations, and shall not sell such chemicals to units that do not possess the relevant license documents or supporting documents. For those who purchase highly toxic chemicals by presenting a permit for the purchase of highly toxic chemicals, the sale shall be made in accordance with the types and quantities specified in the permit.  
It is prohibited to sell highly toxic chemicals (excluding pesticides that are highly toxic chemicals) or explosive-prone hazardous chemicals to individuals.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，应当如实记录购买单位的名称、地址、经办人的姓名、身份证号码以及所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、用途。销售记录以及经办人的身份证明复印件、相关许可证件复印件或者证明文件的保存期限不得少于1年。  
剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的销售企业、购买单位应当在销售、购买后5日内，将所销售、购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量以及流向信息报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案，并输入计算机系统。

**Article 41** A hazardous chemical production enterprise or trading enterprise that sells acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion shall truthfully record the name and address of the purchasing unit, the name and identity card number of the handling person, and the type, quantity, and purpose of the acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion purchased. The retention period for sales records, copies of the handling person's identity certificate, copies of relevant permits or certification documents shall not be less than one year.  
The selling enterprise or purchasing unit of acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion shall, within five days after the sale or purchase, report the type, quantity, and flow direction information of the acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion sold or purchased to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level for archival filing, and shall input such information into the computer system.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位不得出借、转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品；因转产、停产、搬迁、关闭等确需转让的，应当向具有[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位转让，并在转让后将有关情况及时向所在地县级人民政府公安机关报告。

**Article 42** A unit that uses acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion shall not lend or transfer the acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion it has purchased; where transfer is truly necessary due to conversion of production, cessation of production, relocation, closure, or other reasons, such transfer shall be made to a unit that holds the relevant permits or certification documents as specified in the first and second paragraphs of Article 38 of these Regulations, and the relevant information shall be reported to the public security organ of the local people's government at the county level in a timely manner after the transfer.

### 第五章　运输安全

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　从事危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的，应当分别依照有关道路运输、水路运输的法律、行政法规的规定，取得危险货物道路运输许可、危险货物水路运输许可，并向工商行政管理部门办理登记手续。  
危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业应当配备专职安全管理人员。

**Article 43** Any person engaged in the road transportation or waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals shall, in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations on road transportation or waterway transportation, obtain a permit for the road transportation of dangerous goods or a permit for the waterway transportation of dangerous goods, and shall undergo registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce.  
A hazardous chemical road transportation enterprise or waterway transportation enterprise shall be equipped with full-time safety management personnel.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业的驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员应当经交通运输主管部门考核合格，取得从业资格。具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门制定。  
危险化学品的装卸作业应当遵守安全作业标准、规程和制度，并在装卸管理人员的现场指挥或者监控下进行。水路运输危险化学品的集装箱装箱作业应当在集装箱装箱现场检查员的指挥或者监控下进行，并符合积载、隔离的规范和要求；装箱作业完毕后，集装箱装箱现场检查员应当签署装箱证明书。

**Article 44** The drivers, crew members, loading and unloading management personnel, escorts, declarants, and on-site container packing inspectors of hazardous chemical road transportation enterprises and waterway transportation enterprises shall pass the assessment conducted by the competent transport department and obtain the qualification for practice. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council.  
The loading and unloading operations of hazardous chemicals shall comply with the safety operation standards, procedures, and systems, and shall be conducted under the on-site direction or monitoring of the loading and unloading management personnel. The container packing operations for waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals shall be conducted under the direction or monitoring of the on-site container packing inspector, and shall comply with the specifications and requirements for stowage and segregation; after the packing operation is completed, the on-site container packing inspector shall sign the container packing certificate.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　运输危险化学品，应当根据危险化学品的危险特性采取相应的安全防护措施，并配备必要的防护用品和应急救援器材。  
用于运输危险化学品的槽罐以及其他容器应当封口严密，能够防止危险化学品在运输过程中因温度、湿度或者压力的变化发生渗漏、洒漏；槽罐以及其他容器的溢流和泄压装置应当设置准确、起闭灵活。  
运输危险化学品的驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员，应当了解所运输的危险化学品的危险特性及其包装物、容器的使用要求和出现危险情况时的应急处置方法。

**Article 45** When transporting hazardous chemicals, corresponding safety protection measures shall be taken according to the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, and necessary protective equipment and emergency rescue equipment shall be provided.  
Tank containers and other containers used for transporting hazardous chemicals shall be tightly sealed to prevent leakage or spillage of hazardous chemicals due to changes in temperature, humidity, or pressure during transportation; the overflow and pressure relief devices of tank containers and other containers shall be set accurately and operate flexibly.  
The drivers, crew members, loading and unloading management personnel, escorts, declarants, and on-site container packing inspectors of hazardous chemical transportation shall understand the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals being transported, the requirements for the use of their packaging and containers, and the emergency response methods in case of dangerous situations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　通过道路运输危险化学品的，托运人应当委托依法取得危险货物道路运输许可的企业承运。

**Article 46** Where hazardous chemicals are transported by road, the consignor shall entrust an enterprise that has obtained a permit for the road transportation of dangerous goods according to law to carry them.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　通过道路运输危险化学品的，应当按照运输车辆的核定载质量装载危险化学品，不得超载。  
危险化学品运输车辆应当符合国家标准要求的安全技术条件，并按照国家有关规定定期进行安全技术检验。  
危险化学品运输车辆应当悬挂或者喷涂符合国家标准要求的警示标志。

**Article 47** Where hazardous chemicals are transported by road, the hazardous chemicals shall be loaded in accordance with the rated load capacity of the transport vehicle, and overloading shall not be permitted.  
Hazardous chemical transport vehicles shall comply with the safety technical conditions required by national standards, and shall undergo periodic safety technical inspections in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Hazardous chemical transport vehicles shall display or be painted with warning signs that comply with the requirements of national standards.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　通过道路运输危险化学品的，应当配备押运人员，并保证所运输的危险化学品处于押运人员的监控之下。  
运输危险化学品途中因住宿或者发生影响正常运输的情况，需要较长时间停车的，驾驶人员、押运人员应当采取相应的安全防范措施；运输剧毒化学品或者易制爆危险化学品的，还应当向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 48** Where hazardous chemicals are transported by road, escorts shall be provided, and the hazardous chemicals being transported shall be kept under the monitoring of the escorts.  
If, during the transportation of hazardous chemicals, a long-term stop is necessary due to accommodation or the occurrence of circumstances affecting normal transportation, the driver and escort shall take corresponding safety precautions; if acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion are being transported, the matter shall also be reported to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　未经公安机关批准，运输危险化学品的车辆不得进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域。危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域由县级人民政府公安机关划定，并设置明显的标志。

**Article 49** Without the approval of the public security organ, vehicles transporting hazardous chemicals shall not enter areas where the passage of hazardous chemical transport vehicles is restricted. Areas where the passage of hazardous chemical transport vehicles is restricted shall be delineated by the public security organ of the people's government at the county level, and conspicuous signs shall be set up.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　通过道路运输剧毒化学品的，托运人应当向运输始发地或者目的地县级人民政府公安机关申请剧毒化学品道路运输通行证。  
申请剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，托运人应当向县级人民政府公安机关提交下列材料：  
(一)拟运输的剧毒化学品品种、数量的说明；  
(二)运输始发地、目的地、运输时间和运输路线的说明；  
(三)承运人取得危险货物道路运输许可、运输车辆取得营运证以及驾驶人员、押运人员取得上岗资格的证明文件；  
(四)[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的购买剧毒化学品的相关许可证件，或者海关出具的进出口证明文件。  
县级人民政府公安机关应当自收到前款规定的材料之日起7日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发剧毒化学品道路运输通行证；不予批准的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
剧毒化学品道路运输通行证管理办法由国务院公安部门制定。

**Article 50** Where acutely toxic chemicals are transported by road, the consignor shall apply to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level of the place of departure or destination for a road transport permit for acutely toxic chemicals.  
To apply for a road transport permit for acutely toxic chemicals, the consignor shall submit the following materials to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level:  
(1) a description of the type and quantity of the acutely toxic chemicals to be transported;  
(2) a description of the place of departure, destination, transport time, and transport route;  
(3) documentary evidence that the carrier has obtained a permit for the road transportation of dangerous goods, that the transport vehicle has obtained an operation certificate, and that the driver and escort have obtained the qualification for the post;  
(4) the relevant permits for purchasing acutely toxic chemicals as specified in the first and second paragraphs of Article 38 of these Regulations, or the import/export certification documents issued by the customs.  
The public security organ of the people's government at the county level shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the materials specified in the preceding paragraph, make a decision to approve or disapprove. If approval is granted, a road transport permit for acutely toxic chemicals shall be issued; if disapproval is granted, the applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons stated.  
The administrative measures for road transport permits for acutely toxic chemicals shall be formulated by the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品在道路运输途中丢失、被盗、被抢或者出现流散、泄漏等情况的，驾驶人员、押运人员应当立即采取相应的警示措施和安全措施，并向当地公安机关报告。公安机关接到报告后，应当根据实际情况立即向安全生产监督管理部门、环境保护主管部门、卫生主管部门通报。有关部门应当采取必要的应急处置措施。

**Article 51** If, during road transportation, acutely toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals prone to causing explosion are lost, stolen, or robbed, or are scattered or leaked, the driver and escort shall immediately take corresponding warning measures and safety measures, and shall report to the local public security organ. Upon receiving the report, the public security organ shall, according to the actual circumstances, immediately notify the competent department of work safety, the competent department of environmental protection, and the competent department of health. The relevant departments shall take necessary emergency response measures.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　通过水路运输危险化学品的，应当遵守法律、行政法规以及国务院交通运输主管部门关于危险货物水路运输安全的规定。

**Article 52** Where hazardous chemicals are transported by waterway, the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and the competent transport department of the State Council concerning the safety of waterway transportation of dangerous goods shall be complied with.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　海事管理机构应当根据危险化学品的种类和危险特性，确定船舶运输危险化学品的相关安全运输条件。  
拟交付船舶运输的化学品的相关安全运输条件不明确的，货物所有人或者代理人应当委托相关技术机构进行评估，明确相关安全运输条件并经海事管理机构确认后，方可交付船舶运输。

**Article 53** The maritime administration agency shall, based on the type and hazardous characteristics of hazardous chemicals, determine the relevant safe transport conditions for the transportation of hazardous chemicals by ship.  
If the relevant safe transport conditions of chemicals to be delivered for transport by ship are unclear, the cargo owner or agent shall entrust a relevant technical institution to conduct an assessment, and only after the relevant safe transport conditions are clarified and confirmed by the maritime administration agency may the chemicals be delivered for transport by ship.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　禁止通过内河封闭水域运输剧毒化学品以及国家规定禁止通过内河运输的其他危险化学品。  
前款规定以外的内河水域，禁止运输国家规定禁止通过内河运输的剧毒化学品以及其他危险化学品。  
禁止通过内河运输的剧毒化学品以及其他危险化学品的范围，由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院环境保护主管部门、工业和信息化主管部门、安全生产监督管理部门，根据危险化学品的危险特性、危险化学品对人体和水环境的危害程度以及消除危害后果的难易程度等因素规定并公布。

**Article 54** The transportation of acutely toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals that are prohibited from being transported through inland waterways by State provisions through closed inland water bodies is prohibited.  
In inland water areas other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, the transportation of acutely toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals that are prohibited from being transported through inland waterways by State provisions is prohibited.  
The scope of acutely toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals prohibited from being transported through inland waterways shall be prescribed and published by the competent transport department of the State Council jointly with the competent department of environmental protection of the State Council, the competent department of industry and information technology, and the competent department of work safety, based on factors such as the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, the degree of harm to the human body and the water environment, and the difficulty of eliminating the harm consequences.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院交通运输主管部门应当根据危险化学品的危险特性，对通过内河运输[本条例第五十四条](#art-54)规定以外的危险化学品(以下简称通过内河运输危险化学品)实行分类管理，对各类危险化学品的运输方式、包装规范和安全防护措施等分别作出规定并监督实施。

**Article 55** The competent transport department of the State Council shall, based on the hazardous characteristics of hazardous chemicals, implement classified management for the transportation through inland waterways of hazardous chemicals other than those specified in Article 54 of these Regulations (hereinafter referred to as the transportation of hazardous chemicals through inland waterways), and shall separately stipulate the transport modes, packaging specifications, safety protection measures, etc., for each type of hazardous chemical and supervise their implementation.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　通过内河运输危险化学品，应当由依法取得危险货物水路运输许可的水路运输企业承运，其他单位和个人不得承运。托运人应当委托依法取得危险货物水路运输许可的水路运输企业承运，不得委托其他单位和个人承运。

**Article 56** The transportation of hazardous chemicals through inland waterways shall be carried by a waterway transportation enterprise that has obtained a permit for the waterway transportation of dangerous goods according to law; no other unit or individual may carry them. The consignor shall entrust a waterway transportation enterprise that has obtained a permit for the waterway transportation of dangerous goods according to law to carry them, and shall not entrust any other unit or individual to carry them.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　通过内河运输危险化学品，应当使用依法取得危险货物适装证书的运输船舶。水路运输企业应当针对所运输的危险化学品的危险特性，制定运输船舶危险化学品事故应急救援预案，并为运输船舶配备充足、有效的应急救援器材和设备。  
通过内河运输危险化学品的船舶，其所有人或者经营人应当取得船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明。船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明的副本应当随船携带。

**Article 57** The transportation of hazardous chemicals through inland waterways shall use transport ships that have obtained a certificate of fitness for carrying dangerous goods according to law. The waterway transportation enterprise shall, based on the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals being transported, formulate an emergency rescue plan for hazardous chemical accidents on transport ships, and shall equip the transport ships with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment and devices.  
For ships transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waterways, the owner or operator shall obtain a certificate of pollution damage liability insurance or a financial guarantee certificate. A copy of the ship pollution damage liability insurance certificate or financial guarantee certificate shall be carried on board.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　通过内河运输危险化学品，危险化学品包装物的材质、型式、强度以及包装方法应当符合水路运输危险化学品包装规范的要求。国务院交通运输主管部门对单船运输的危险化学品数量有限制性规定的，承运人应当按照规定安排运输数量。

**Article 58** For the transportation of hazardous chemicals through inland waterways, the material, type, strength, and packaging method of the hazardous chemical packaging shall comply with the requirements of the packaging specifications for the waterway transportation of hazardous chemicals. If the competent transport department of the State Council has restrictive provisions on the quantity of hazardous chemicals transported by a single ship, the carrier shall arrange the transport quantity in accordance with such provisions.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位应当符合国家有关安全规范，与饮用水取水口保持国家规定的距离。有关管理单位应当制定码头、泊位危险化学品事故应急预案，并为码头、泊位配备充足、有效的应急救援器材和设备。  
用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位，经交通运输主管部门按照国家有关规定验收合格后方可投入使用。

**Article 59** Inland river wharves and berths used for hazardous chemical transport operations shall comply with the relevant safety specifications of the State and maintain a distance from drinking water intakes as specified by the State. The relevant management unit shall formulate emergency plans for hazardous chemical accidents at wharves and berths, and shall equip the wharves and berths with sufficient and effective emergency rescue equipment and devices.  
Inland river wharves and berths used for hazardous chemical transport operations may be put into use only after passing the acceptance inspection conducted by the competent transport department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　船舶载运危险化学品进出内河港口，应当将危险化学品的名称、危险特性、包装以及进出港时间等事项，事先报告海事管理机构。海事管理机构接到报告后，应当在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，同时通报港口行政管理部门。定船舶、定航线、定货种的船舶可以定期报告。  
在内河港口内进行危险化学品的装卸、过驳作业，应当将危险化学品的名称、危险特性、包装和作业的时间、地点等事项报告港口行政管理部门。港口行政管理部门接到报告后，应当在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的时间内作出是否同意的决定，通知报告人，同时通报海事管理机构。  
载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，通过过船建筑物的，应当提前向交通运输主管部门申报，并接受交通运输主管部门的管理。

**Article 60** Where a ship carrying hazardous chemicals enters or leaves an inland river port, matters such as the name, hazardous characteristics, packaging, and time of entry or departure of the hazardous chemicals shall be reported in advance to the maritime administration agency. Upon receiving the report, the maritime administration agency shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council, make a decision on whether to agree, notify the reporter, and at the same time inform the port administrative department. Ships with fixed ships, fixed routes, and fixed types of cargo may report periodically.  
Where loading, unloading, or lightering operations of hazardous chemicals are conducted within an inland river port, matters such as the name, hazardous characteristics, packaging, and time and place of the operation shall be reported to the port administrative department. Upon receiving the report, the port administrative department shall, within the time limit prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council, make a decision on whether to agree, notify the reporter, and at the same time inform the maritime administration agency.  
Where a ship carrying hazardous chemicals navigates in inland rivers and passes through a ship lock, it shall declare to the competent transport department in advance and be subject to the management of the competent transport department.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行、装卸或者停泊，应当悬挂专用的警示标志，按照规定显示专用信号。  
载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定需要引航的，应当申请引航。

**Article 61** A vessel carrying dangerous chemicals while navigating, loading, unloading, or berthing on inland waters shall display special warning signs and show special signals in accordance with regulations.  
A vessel carrying dangerous chemicals while navigating on inland waters shall apply for pilotage if pilotage is required in accordance with the provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家其他有关饮用水水源保护的规定。内河航道发展规划应当与依法经批准的饮用水水源保护区划定方案相协调。

**Article 62** A vessel carrying dangerous chemicals while navigating on inland waters shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and other state provisions concerning the protection of drinking water sources. The development plan for inland waterways shall be coordinated with the plan for the delimitation of drinking water source protection areas approved according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　托运危险化学品的，托运人应当向承运人说明所托运的危险化学品的种类、数量、危险特性以及发生危险情况的应急处置措施，并按照国家有关规定对所托运的危险化学品妥善包装，在外包装上设置相应的标志。  
运输危险化学品需要添加抑制剂或者稳定剂的，托运人应当添加，并将有关情况告知承运人。

**Article 63** Where dangerous chemicals are consigned for transport, the consignor shall explain to the carrier the types, quantities, hazardous properties, and emergency response measures for hazards of the consigned dangerous chemicals, and shall properly pack the consigned dangerous chemicals in accordance with the relevant state provisions, and affix corresponding marks on the outer packaging.  
Where inhibitors or stabilizers need to be added for the transport of dangerous chemicals, the consignor shall add them and inform the carrier of the relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　托运人不得在托运的普通货物中夹带危险化学品，不得将危险化学品匿报或者谎报为普通货物托运。  
任何单位和个人不得交寄危险化学品或者在邮件、快件内夹带危险化学品，不得将危险化学品匿报或者谎报为普通物品交寄。邮政企业、快递企业不得收寄危险化学品。  
对涉嫌违反本条第一款、第二款规定的，交通运输主管部门、邮政管理部门可以依法开拆查验。

**Article 64** A consignor shall not conceal dangerous chemicals among ordinary goods consigned for transport, nor shall it fail to report or falsely report dangerous chemicals as ordinary goods for consignment.  
No unit or individual may send dangerous chemicals or conceal them in mail or express items, nor fail to report or falsely report dangerous chemicals as ordinary items for sending. Postal enterprises and courier enterprises shall not accept dangerous chemicals for sending.  
Where any suspected violation of the provisions of the first or second paragraph of this Article occurs, the competent transport department or the postal administrative department may open and inspect the items according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　通过铁路、航空运输危险化学品的安全管理，依照有关铁路、航空运输的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行。

**Article 65** The safety management of dangerous chemicals transported by railway or air shall be implemented in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, and rules on railway and air transport.

### 第六章　危险化学品登记与事故应急救援

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家实行危险化学品登记制度，为危险化学品安全管理以及危险化学品事故预防和应急救援提供技术、信息支持。

**Article 66** The State implements a registration system for dangerous chemicals to provide technical and information support for the safety management of dangerous chemicals, the prevention of dangerous chemical accidents, and emergency rescue.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　危险化学品生产企业、进口企业，应当向国务院安全生产监督管理部门负责危险化学品登记的机构(以下简称危险化学品登记机构)办理危险化学品登记。  
危险化学品登记包括下列内容：  
(一)分类和标签信息；  
(二)物理、化学性质；  
(三)主要用途；  
(四)危险特性；  
(五)储存、使用、运输的安全要求；  
(六)出现危险情况的应急处置措施。  
对同一企业生产、进口的同一品种的危险化学品，不进行重复登记。危险化学品生产企业、进口企业发现其生产、进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性的，应当及时向危险化学品登记机构办理登记内容变更手续。  
危险化学品登记的具体办法由国务院安全生产监督管理部门制定。

**Article 67** A dangerous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise shall register dangerous chemicals with the institution responsible for dangerous chemical registration under the safety production supervision and management department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the dangerous chemical registration institution).  
Dangerous chemical registration includes the following:  
(1) classification and label information;  
(2) physical and chemical properties;  
(3) main uses;  
(4) hazardous properties;  
(5) safety requirements for storage, use, and transport;  
(6) emergency response measures in case of hazardous situations.  
Duplicate registration shall not be conducted for the same type of dangerous chemical produced or imported by the same enterprise. Where a dangerous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise discovers new hazardous properties of the dangerous chemicals it produces or imports, it shall promptly go through the formalities for changing the registration content with the dangerous chemical registration institution.  
The specific measures for dangerous chemical registration shall be formulated by the safety production supervision and management department of the State Council.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　危险化学品登记机构应当定期向工业和信息化、环境保护、公安、卫生、交通运输、铁路、质量监督检验检疫等部门提供危险化学品登记的有关信息和资料。

**Article 68** The dangerous chemical registration institution shall regularly provide relevant information and data on dangerous chemical registration to departments such as industry and information technology, environmental protection, public security, health, transport, railway, and quality supervision, inspection, and quarantine.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　县级以上地方人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当会同工业和信息化、环境保护、公安、卫生、交通运输、铁路、质量监督检验检疫等部门，根据本地区实际情况，制定危险化学品事故应急预案，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 69** The safety production supervision and management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with departments such as industry and information technology, environmental protection, public security, health, transport, railway, and quality supervision, inspection, and quarantine, formulate an emergency response plan for dangerous chemical accidents based on the actual conditions of the region, and submit it to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　危险化学品单位应当制定本单位危险化学品事故应急预案，配备应急救援人员和必要的应急救援器材、设备，并定期组织应急救援演练。  
危险化学品单位应当将其危险化学品事故应急预案报所在地设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门备案。

**Article 70** A dangerous chemical unit shall formulate an emergency response plan for dangerous chemical accidents of the unit, equip itself with emergency rescue personnel and necessary emergency rescue equipment and devices, and regularly organize emergency rescue drills.  
A dangerous chemical unit shall file its emergency response plan for dangerous chemical accidents with the safety production supervision and management department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　发生危险化学品事故，事故单位主要负责人应当立即按照本单位危险化学品应急预案组织救援，并向当地安全生产监督管理部门和环境保护、公安、卫生主管部门报告；道路运输、水路运输过程中发生危险化学品事故的，驾驶人员、船员或者押运人员还应当向事故发生地交通运输主管部门报告。

**Article 71** When a dangerous chemical accident occurs, the principal of the unit where the accident occurred shall immediately organize rescue in accordance with the unit's dangerous chemical emergency response plan, and report to the local safety production supervision and management department and the competent departments of environmental protection, public security, and health; where a dangerous chemical accident occurs during road or waterway transport, the driver, crew member, or escort shall also report to the competent transport department of the place where the accident occurred.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　发生危险化学品事故，有关地方人民政府应当立即组织安全生产监督管理、环境保护、公安、卫生、交通运输等有关部门，按照本地区危险化学品事故应急预案组织实施救援，不得拖延、推诿。  
有关地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照下列规定，采取必要的应急处置措施，减少事故损失，防止事故蔓延、扩大：  
(一)立即组织营救和救治受害人员，疏散、撤离或者采取其他措施保护危害区域内的其他人员；  
(二)迅速控制危害源，测定危险化学品的性质、事故的危害区域及危害程度；  
(三)针对事故对人体、动植物、土壤、水源、大气造成的现实危害和可能产生的危害，迅速采取封闭、隔离、洗消等措施；  
(四)对危险化学品事故造成的环境污染和生态破坏状况进行监测、评估，并采取相应的环境污染治理和生态修复措施。

**Article 72** When a dangerous chemical accident occurs, the relevant local people's government shall immediately organize the safety production supervision and management, environmental protection, public security, health, transport, and other relevant departments to organize and implement rescue in accordance with the regional dangerous chemical accident emergency response plan, and shall not delay or evade responsibility.  
The relevant local people's government and its relevant departments shall take necessary emergency response measures in accordance with the following provisions to reduce accident losses and prevent the accident from spreading or escalating:  
(1) immediately organize the rescue and treatment of affected persons, and evacuate, remove, or take other measures to protect other persons within the hazardous area;  
(2) promptly control the source of hazard, and determine the properties of the dangerous chemicals, the hazardous area of the accident, and the degree of hazard;  
(3) promptly take measures such as containment, isolation, and decontamination to address the actual and potential harm caused by the accident to humans, animals, plants, soil, water sources, and the atmosphere;  
(4) monitor and assess the environmental pollution and ecological damage caused by the dangerous chemical accident, and take corresponding measures for environmental pollution control and ecological restoration.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　有关危险化学品单位应当为危险化学品事故应急救援提供技术指导和必要的协助。

**Article 73** The relevant dangerous chemical unit shall provide technical guidance and necessary assistance for emergency rescue in dangerous chemical accidents.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　危险化学品事故造成环境污染的，由设区的市级以上人民政府环境保护主管部门统一发布有关信息。

**Article 74** Where a dangerous chemical accident causes environmental pollution, the competent environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the city divided into districts level shall uniformly release the relevant information.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　生产、经营、使用国家禁止生产、经营、使用的危险化学品的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止生产、经营、使用活动，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
有前款规定行为的，安全生产监督管理部门还应当责令其对所生产、经营、使用的危险化学品进行无害化处理。  
违反国家关于危险化学品使用的限制性规定使用危险化学品的，依照本条第一款的规定处理。

**Article 75** Where anyone produces, operates, or uses dangerous chemicals whose production, operation, or use is prohibited by the State, the safety production supervision and management department shall order the cessation of the production, operation, or use activities, impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any person commits an act specified in the preceding paragraph, the safety production supervision and management department shall also order it to carry out harmless treatment of the dangerous chemicals it has produced, operated, or used.  
Where anyone uses dangerous chemicals in violation of the restrictive provisions of the State on the use of dangerous chemicals, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　未经安全条件审查，新建、改建、扩建生产、储存危险化学品的建设项目的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止建设，限期改正；逾期不改正的，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
未经安全条件审查，新建、改建、扩建储存、装卸危险化学品的港口建设项目的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 76** Where any construction project for the production or storage of dangerous chemicals is newly built, rebuilt, or expanded without review of safety conditions, the safety production supervision and management department shall order the cessation of construction and rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any port construction project for the storage or loading and unloading of dangerous chemicals is newly built, rebuilt, or expanded without review of safety conditions, the port administrative department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　未依法取得危险化学品安全生产许可证从事危险化学品生产，或者未依法取得工业产品生产许可证从事危险化学品及其包装物、容器生产的，分别依照《安全生产许可证条例》、《中华人民共和国工业产品生产许可证管理条例》的规定处罚。  
违反本条例规定，化工企业未取得危险化学品安全使用许可证，使用危险化学品从事生产的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令限期改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产整顿。  
违反本条例规定，未取得危险化学品经营许可证从事危险化学品经营的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令停止经营活动，没收违法经营的危险化学品以及违法所得，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 77** Where anyone engages in the production of dangerous chemicals without having obtained a dangerous chemical safety production license according to law, or engages in the production of dangerous chemicals and their packaging, containers without having obtained an industrial product production license according to law, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Safety Production Licenses and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Industrial Product Production Licenses respectively.  
In violation of these Regulations, where a chemical enterprise uses dangerous chemicals for production without having obtained a dangerous chemical safety use license, the safety production supervision and management department shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if rectification is not made within the time limit, it shall order the suspension of production for rectification.  
In violation of these Regulations, where anyone engages in the operation of dangerous chemicals without having obtained a dangerous chemical operation license, the safety production supervision and management department shall order the cessation of operation activities, confiscate the illegally operated dangerous chemicals and illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　有下列情形之一的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业整顿：  
(一)生产、储存危险化学品的单位未对其铺设的危险化学品管道设置明显的标志，或者未对危险化学品管道定期检查、检测的；  
(二)进行可能危及危险化学品管道安全的施工作业，施工单位未按照规定书面通知管道所属单位，或者未与管道所属单位共同制定应急预案、采取相应的安全防护措施，或者管道所属单位未指派专门人员到现场进行管道安全保护指导的；  
(三)危险化学品生产企业未提供化学品安全技术说明书，或者未在包装(包括外包装件)上粘贴、拴挂化学品安全标签的；  
(四)危险化学品生产企业提供的化学品安全技术说明书与其生产的危险化学品不相符，或者在包装(包括外包装件)粘贴、拴挂的化学品安全标签与包装内危险化学品不相符，或者化学品安全技术说明书、化学品安全标签所载明的内容不符合国家标准要求的；  
(五)危险化学品生产企业发现其生产的危险化学品有新的危险特性不立即公告，或者不及时修订其化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签的；  
(六)危险化学品经营企业经营没有化学品安全技术说明书和化学品安全标签的危险化学品的；  
(七)危险化学品包装物、容器的材质以及包装的型式、规格、方法和单件质量(重量)与所包装的危险化学品的性质和用途不相适应的；  
(八)生产、储存危险化学品的单位未在作业场所和安全设施、设备上设置明显的安全警示标志，或者未在作业场所设置通信、报警装置的；  
(九)危险化学品专用仓库未设专人负责管理，或者对储存的剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品未实行双人收发、双人保管制度的；  
(十)储存危险化学品的单位未建立危险化学品出入库核查、登记制度的；  
(十一)危险化学品专用仓库未设置明显标志的；  
(十二)危险化学品生产企业、进口企业不办理危险化学品登记，或者发现其生产、进口的危险化学品有新的危险特性不办理危险化学品登记内容变更手续的。  
从事危险化学品仓储经营的港口经营人有前款规定情形的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处罚。储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的专用仓库未按照国家有关规定设置相应的技术防范设施的，由公安机关依照前款规定予以处罚。  
生产、储存剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位未设置治安保卫机构、配备专职治安保卫人员的，依照《企业事业单位内部治安保卫条例》的规定处罚。

**Article 78** In any of the following circumstances, the safety production supervision and management department shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the party refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the suspension of production or business for rectification:  
(1) a unit producing or storing dangerous chemicals fails to set up obvious signs for the dangerous chemical pipelines it has laid, or fails to regularly inspect or test the dangerous chemical pipelines;  
(2) where construction work that may endanger the safety of dangerous chemical pipelines is carried out, the construction unit fails to notify the pipeline owner in writing in accordance with regulations, or fails to jointly formulate an emergency response plan and take corresponding safety protection measures with the pipeline owner, or the pipeline owner fails to assign specialized personnel to the site for pipeline safety protection guidance;  
(3) a dangerous chemical production enterprise fails to provide a chemical safety data sheet, or fails to affix or attach a chemical safety label on the packaging (including outer packaging);  
(4) the chemical safety data sheet provided by a dangerous chemical production enterprise does not match the dangerous chemicals it produces, or the chemical safety label affixed or attached on the packaging (including outer packaging) does not match the dangerous chemicals inside the packaging, or the contents stated in the chemical safety data sheet or chemical safety label do not meet the requirements of national standards;  
(5) a dangerous chemical production enterprise, upon discovering new hazardous properties of the dangerous chemicals it produces, fails to immediately announce them or fails to promptly revise its chemical safety data sheet and chemical safety label;  
(6) a dangerous chemical operating enterprise operates dangerous chemicals without chemical safety data sheets and chemical safety labels;  
(7) the materials of dangerous chemical packaging or containers, as well as the form, specifications, methods, and unit mass (weight) of the packaging, are not suitable for the nature and use of the packaged dangerous chemicals;  
(8) a unit producing or storing dangerous chemicals fails to set up obvious safety warning signs in the workplace and on safety facilities and equipment, or fails to install communication and alarm devices in the workplace;  
(9) a dedicated warehouse for dangerous chemicals has no designated person responsible for management, or fails to implement the system of double-person receipt and double-person custody for stored highly toxic chemicals and other dangerous chemicals whose storage quantities constitute a major hazard source;  
(10) a unit storing dangerous chemicals fails to establish a system for checking and registering the storage and release of dangerous chemicals;  
(11) a dedicated warehouse for dangerous chemicals fails to set up obvious signs;  
(12) a dangerous chemical production enterprise or import enterprise fails to register dangerous chemicals, or fails to go through the formalities for changing the registration content upon discovering new hazardous properties of the dangerous chemicals it produces or imports.  
Where a port operator engaged in the warehousing operation of dangerous chemicals falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the port administrative department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph. Where a dedicated warehouse for storing highly toxic chemicals or chemicals that are prone to explode fails to install corresponding technical protection facilities in accordance with the relevant state provisions, the public security organ shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
Where a unit producing or storing highly toxic chemicals or chemicals that are prone to explode fails to establish a security guard institution or equip itself with full-time security guards, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Internal Security of Enterprises and Public Institutions.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　危险化学品包装物、容器生产企业销售未经检验或者经检验不合格的危险化学品包装物、容器的，由质量监督检验检疫部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
将未经检验合格的运输危险化学品的船舶及其配载的容器投入使用的，由海事管理机构依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 79** Where a manufacturer of dangerous chemical packaging or containers sells packaging or containers that have not been inspected or have been found to be non-conforming upon inspection, the quality supervision, inspection, and quarantine department shall order rectification, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if rectification is refused, it shall order the suspension of production or business for rectification; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a vessel carrying dangerous chemicals and its containers that have not passed inspection are put into use, the maritime administrative agency shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位有下列情形之一的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿直至由原发证机关吊销其相关许可证件，并由工商行政管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照；有关责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对重复使用的危险化学品包装物、容器，在重复使用前不进行检查的；  
(二)未根据其生产、储存的危险化学品的种类和危险特性，在作业场所设置相关安全设施、设备，或者未按照国家标准、行业标准或者国家有关规定对安全设施、设备进行经常性维护、保养的；  
(三)未依照本条例规定对其安全生产条件定期进行安全评价的；  
(四)未将危险化学品储存在专用仓库内，或者未将剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品在专用仓库内单独存放的；  
(五)危险化学品的储存方式、方法或者储存数量不符合国家标准或者国家有关规定的；  
(六)危险化学品专用仓库不符合国家标准、行业标准的要求的；  
(七)未对危险化学品专用仓库的安全设施、设备定期进行检测、检验的。  
从事危险化学品仓储经营的港口经营人有前款规定情形的，由港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 80** Where a unit producing, storing, or using dangerous chemicals falls under any of the following circumstances, the safety production supervision and management department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if rectification is refused, it shall order the suspension of production or business for rectification until the original issuing authority revokes the relevant license, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to go through the formalities for changing its business scope or revoke its business license; if the responsible person(s) constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to inspect reusable dangerous chemical packaging or containers before reuse;  
(2) failing to install relevant safety facilities and equipment in the workplace in accordance with the types and hazardous properties of the dangerous chemicals being produced or stored, or failing to carry out regular maintenance and upkeep of safety facilities and equipment in accordance with national standards, industry standards, or relevant state provisions;  
(3) failing to conduct regular safety assessments of its safety production conditions in accordance with these Regulations;  
(4) failing to store dangerous chemicals in a dedicated warehouse, or failing to store highly toxic chemicals and other dangerous chemicals whose storage quantities constitute a major hazard source separately in a dedicated warehouse;  
(5) the storage method, mode, or quantity of dangerous chemicals does not meet national standards or relevant state provisions;  
(6) the dedicated warehouse for dangerous chemicals fails to meet the requirements of national standards or industry standards;  
(7) failing to regularly inspect or test the safety facilities and equipment of the dedicated warehouse for dangerous chemicals.  
Where a port operator engaged in the warehousing operation of dangerous chemicals falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, the port administrative department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)生产、储存、使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位不如实记录生产、储存、使用的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的数量、流向的；  
(二)生产、储存、使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位发现剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品丢失或者被盗，不立即向公安机关报告的；  
(三)储存剧毒化学品的单位未将剧毒化学品的储存数量、储存地点以及管理人员的情况报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案的；  
(四)危险化学品生产企业、经营企业不如实记录剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品购买单位的名称、地址、经办人的姓名、身份证号码以及所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量、用途，或者保存销售记录和相关材料的时间少于1年的；  
(五)剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的销售企业、购买单位未在规定的时限内将所销售、购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的品种、数量以及流向信息报所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案的；  
(六)使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位依照本条例规定转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，未将有关情况向所在地县级人民政府公安机关报告的。  
生产、储存危险化学品的企业或者使用危险化学品从事生产的企业未按照本条例规定将安全评价报告以及整改方案的落实情况报安全生产监督管理部门或者港口行政管理部门备案，或者储存危险化学品的单位未将其剧毒化学品以及储存数量构成重大危险源的其他危险化学品的储存数量、储存地点以及管理人员的情况报安全生产监督管理部门或者港口行政管理部门备案的，分别由安全生产监督管理部门或者港口行政管理部门依照前款规定予以处罚。  
生产实施重点环境管理的危险化学品的企业或者使用实施重点环境管理的危险化学品从事生产的企业未按照规定将相关信息向环境保护主管部门报告的，由环境保护主管部门依照本条第一款的规定予以处罚。

**Article 81** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the public security organ shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) the entity producing, storing or using highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture fails to truthfully record the quantities and flows of the highly toxic chemicals and hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture that it produces, stores or uses;  
(2) the entity producing, storing or using highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture discovers that the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture are lost or stolen but fails to immediately report to the public security organ;  
(3) the entity storing highly toxic chemicals fails to report to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level for recordation the storage quantity, storage location and the information of the management personnel of the highly toxic chemicals;  
(4) the hazardous chemical production enterprise or operating enterprise fails to truthfully record the names, addresses, names of handling persons, identity card numbers of the entities purchasing highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture, and the types, quantities and uses of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture purchased, or fails to keep sales records and related materials for a period of less than one year;  
(5) the selling enterprise or purchasing entity of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture fails to report to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level for recordation the types, quantities and flow information of the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture sold or purchased within the prescribed time limit; or  
(6) the entity using highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture transfers the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture it purchased in accordance with these Regulations but fails to report the relevant information to the local public security organ of the people's government at the county level.  
If an enterprise producing or storing hazardous chemicals or an enterprise that uses hazardous chemicals for production fails to report to the work safety supervision and administration department or the port administrative department for recordation the safety assessment report and the implementation of the rectification plan in accordance with these Regulations, or if the entity storing hazardous chemicals fails to report to the work safety supervision and administration department or the port administrative department for recordation the storage quantity, storage location and the information of the management personnel of its highly toxic chemicals and other hazardous chemicals whose stored quantities constitute a major hazard source, the work safety supervision and administration department or the port administrative department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
If an enterprise producing hazardous chemicals subject to key environmental management or an enterprise using hazardous chemicals subject to key environmental management for production fails to report relevant information to the environmental protection department in accordance with the provisions, the environmental protection department shall impose a penalty in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位转产、停产、停业或者解散，未采取有效措施及时、妥善处置其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存的危险化学品，或者丢弃危险化学品的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
生产、储存、使用危险化学品的单位转产、停产、停业或者解散，未依照本条例规定将其危险化学品生产装置、储存设施以及库存危险化学品的处置方案报有关部门备案的，分别由有关部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 82** If a unit producing, storing or using hazardous chemicals changes production, suspends production, suspends business or dissolves, and fails to take effective measures to dispose of its hazardous chemical production facilities, storage facilities and stored hazardous chemicals in a timely and proper manner, or discards hazardous chemicals, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a unit producing, storing or using hazardous chemicals changes production, suspends production, suspends business or dissolves, and fails to report to the relevant departments for recordation the disposal plan for its hazardous chemical production facilities, storage facilities and stored hazardous chemicals in accordance with these Regulations, the relevant departments shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　危险化学品经营企业向未经许可违法从事危险化学品生产、经营活动的企业采购危险化学品的，由工商行政管理部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿直至由原发证机关吊销其危险化学品经营许可证，并由工商行政管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照。

**Article 83** If a hazardous chemical operating enterprise purchases hazardous chemicals from an enterprise illegally engaging in hazardous chemical production or operating activities without a license, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the enterprise shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification until the original issuing authority revokes its hazardous chemical operating license, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to undergo change registration of its business scope or revoke its business license.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　危险化学品生产企业、经营企业有下列情形之一的，由安全生产监督管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿直至吊销其危险化学品安全生产许可证、危险化学品经营许可证，并由工商行政管理部门责令其办理经营范围变更登记或者吊销其营业执照：  
(一)向不具有[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位销售剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的；  
(二)不按照剧毒化学品购买许可证载明的品种、数量销售剧毒化学品的；  
(三)向个人销售剧毒化学品(属于剧毒化学品的农药除外)、易制爆危险化学品的。  
不具有[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件或者证明文件的单位购买剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，或者个人购买剧毒化学品(属于剧毒化学品的农药除外)、易制爆危险化学品的，由公安机关没收所购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，可以并处5000元以下的罚款。  
使用剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品的单位出借或者向不具有[本条例第三十八条](#art-38)第一款、第二款规定的相关许可证件的单位转让其购买的剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品，或者向个人转让其购买的剧毒化学品(属于剧毒化学品的农药除外)、易制爆危险化学品的，由公安机关责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿。

**Article 84** If a hazardous chemical production enterprise or operating enterprise falls under any of the following circumstances, the work safety supervision and administration department shall order correction, confiscate illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the enterprise shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification until its hazardous chemical safety production license or hazardous chemical operating license is revoked, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order it to undergo change registration of its business scope or revoke its business license:  
(1) selling highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture to a unit that does not possess the relevant license or certification document as prescribed in paragraph 1 or 2 of Article 38 of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to sell highly toxic chemicals in accordance with the types and quantities specified in the highly toxic chemical purchase permit; or  
(3) selling highly toxic chemicals (except pesticides which are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture to an individual.  
If a unit that does not possess the relevant license or certification document as prescribed in paragraph 1 or 2 of Article 38 of these Regulations purchases highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture, or an individual purchases highly toxic chemicals (except pesticides which are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture, the public security organ shall confiscate the purchased highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan.  
If a unit using highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture lends or transfers to a unit that does not possess the relevant license as prescribed in paragraph 1 or 2 of Article 38 of these Regulations the highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture it purchased, or transfers to an individual the highly toxic chemicals (except pesticides which are highly toxic chemicals) or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture it purchased, the public security organ shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the unit shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　未依法取得危险货物道路运输许可、危险货物水路运输许可，从事危险化学品道路运输、水路运输的，分别依照有关道路运输、水路运输的法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 85** If a person engages in road transport or waterway transport of hazardous chemicals without having obtained the road transport permit for dangerous goods or the waterway transport permit for dangerous goods according to law, the penalty shall be imposed respectively in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on road transport and waterway transport.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业的驾驶人员、船员、装卸管理人员、押运人员、申报人员、集装箱装箱现场检查员未取得从业资格上岗作业的；  
(二)运输危险化学品，未根据危险化学品的危险特性采取相应的安全防护措施，或者未配备必要的防护用品和应急救援器材的；  
(三)使用未依法取得危险货物适装证书的船舶，通过内河运输危险化学品的；  
(四)通过内河运输危险化学品的承运人违反国务院交通运输主管部门对单船运输的危险化学品数量的限制性规定运输危险化学品的；  
(五)用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位不符合国家有关安全规范，或者未与饮用水取水口保持国家规定的安全距离，或者未经交通运输主管部门验收合格投入使用的；  
(六)托运人不向承运人说明所托运的危险化学品的种类、数量、危险特性以及发生危险情况的应急处置措施，或者未按照国家有关规定对所托运的危险化学品妥善包装并在外包装上设置相应标志的；  
(七)运输危险化学品需要添加抑制剂或者稳定剂，托运人未添加或者未将有关情况告知承运人的。

**Article 86** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent transport department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if correction is refused, production or business shall be ordered to suspend for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) the drivers, sailors, loading and unloading managers, escorts, declarants, and container stuffing site inspectors of a road transport enterprise or waterway transport enterprise for hazardous chemicals perform their duties without having obtained the qualification for employment;  
(2) when transporting hazardous chemicals, no corresponding safety protection measures are taken according to the hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals, or no necessary protective equipment and emergency rescue appliances are equipped;  
(3) using a vessel that has not obtained the certificate of fitness for the carriage of dangerous goods according to law to transport hazardous chemicals through inland waterways;  
(4) the carrier transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waterways violates the restrictive provisions of the competent transport department of the State Council on the quantity of hazardous chemicals transported by a single vessel;  
(5) the inland river wharves and berths used for hazardous chemical transport operations do not comply with the national safety standards, or fail to maintain the safety distance prescribed by the State from drinking water intakes, or are put into use without being checked and accepted by the competent transport department;  
(6) the consignor fails to inform the carrier of the types, quantities, hazardous characteristics of the hazardous chemicals consigned and the emergency response measures in case of a hazardous situation, or fails to properly pack the consigned hazardous chemicals in accordance with the relevant national provisions and affix corresponding marks on the outer packaging; or  
(7) where inhibitors or stabilizers need to be added for the transport of hazardous chemicals, the consignor fails to add them or fails to inform the carrier of the relevant information.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)委托未依法取得危险货物道路运输许可、危险货物水路运输许可的企业承运危险化学品的；  
(二)通过内河封闭水域运输剧毒化学品以及国家规定禁止通过内河运输的其他危险化学品的；  
(三)通过内河运输国家规定禁止通过内河运输的剧毒化学品以及其他危险化学品的；  
(四)在托运的普通货物中夹带危险化学品，或者将危险化学品谎报或者匿报为普通货物托运的。  
在邮件、快件内夹带危险化学品，或者将危险化学品谎报为普通物品交寄的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
邮政企业、快递企业收寄危险化学品的，依照《中华人民共和国邮政法》的规定处罚。

**Article 87** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent transport department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if correction is refused, production or business shall be ordered to suspend for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) entrusting an enterprise that has not obtained the road transport permit for dangerous goods or the waterway transport permit for dangerous goods according to law to carry hazardous chemicals;  
(2) transporting highly toxic chemicals or other hazardous chemicals prohibited by the State from being transported through inland waterways through enclosed inland waters;  
(3) transporting highly toxic chemicals or other hazardous chemicals prohibited by the State from being transported through inland waterways through inland waterways;  
(4) mixing hazardous chemicals into ordinary goods consigned for transport, or falsely reporting or concealing hazardous chemicals as ordinary goods for consignment.  
If hazardous chemicals are mixed into mail or express items, or falsely reported as ordinary articles for sending, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a postal enterprise or express enterprise accepts hazardous chemicals for delivery, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)超过运输车辆的核定载质量装载危险化学品的；  
(二)使用安全技术条件不符合国家标准要求的车辆运输危险化学品的；  
(三)运输危险化学品的车辆未经公安机关批准进入危险化学品运输车辆限制通行的区域的；  
(四)未取得剧毒化学品道路运输通行证，通过道路运输剧毒化学品的。

**Article 88** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the public security organ shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) loading hazardous chemicals in excess of the approved load capacity of the transport vehicle;  
(2) using a vehicle whose safety technical conditions do not meet the national standard requirements to transport hazardous chemicals;  
(3) the vehicle transporting hazardous chemicals enters an area where the passage of hazardous chemical transport vehicles is restricted without the approval of the public security organ; or  
(4) transporting highly toxic chemicals by road without having obtained a road transport permit for highly toxic chemicals.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚：  
(一)危险化学品运输车辆未悬挂或者喷涂警示标志，或者悬挂或者喷涂的警示标志不符合国家标准要求的；  
(二)通过道路运输危险化学品，不配备押运人员的；  
(三)运输剧毒化学品或者易制爆危险化学品途中需要较长时间停车，驾驶人员、押运人员不向当地公安机关报告的；  
(四)剧毒化学品、易制爆危险化学品在道路运输途中丢失、被盗、被抢或者发生流散、泄露等情况，驾驶人员、押运人员不采取必要的警示措施和安全措施，或者不向当地公安机关报告的。

**Article 89** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the public security organ shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) the hazardous chemical transport vehicle fails to display or spray warning signs, or the warning signs displayed or sprayed do not meet the national standard requirements;  
(2) when transporting hazardous chemicals by road, no escort personnel are provided;  
(3) when transporting highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture, if a longer stopover is needed during the journey, the driver or escort fails to report to the local public security organ;  
(4) in the event of loss, theft, robbery, scattering, leakage, etc. of highly toxic chemicals or hazardous chemicals susceptible to illegal manufacture during road transport, the driver or escort fails to take necessary warning measures and safety measures, or fails to report to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　对发生交通事故负有全部责任或者主要责任的危险化学品道路运输企业，由公安机关责令消除安全隐患，未消除安全隐患的危险化学品运输车辆，禁止上道路行驶。

**Article 90** If a hazardous chemical road transport enterprise bears full or primary responsibility for a traffic accident, the public security organ shall order it to eliminate the safety hazards; a hazardous chemical transport vehicle that has not eliminated safety hazards shall be prohibited from driving on the road.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　有下列情形之一的，由交通运输主管部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)危险化学品道路运输企业、水路运输企业未配备专职安全管理人员的；  
(二)用于危险化学品运输作业的内河码头、泊位的管理单位未制定码头、泊位危险化学品事故应急救援预案，或者未为码头、泊位配备充足、有效的应急救援器材和设备的。

**Article 91** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent transport department shall order correction and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) the road transport enterprise or waterway transport enterprise for hazardous chemicals fails to assign full-time safety management personnel; or  
(2) the management unit of the inland river wharves or berths used for hazardous chemical transport operations fails to formulate emergency rescue plans for hazardous chemical accidents at the wharves or berths, or fails to provide sufficient and effective emergency rescue appliances and equipment for the wharves or berths.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　有下列情形之一的，依照《中华人民共和国内河交通安全管理条例》的规定处罚：  
(一)通过内河运输危险化学品的水路运输企业未制定运输船舶危险化学品事故应急救援预案，或者未为运输船舶配备充足、有效的应急救援器材和设备的；  
(二)通过内河运输危险化学品的船舶的所有人或者经营人未取得船舶污染损害责任保险证书或者财务担保证明的；  
(三)船舶载运危险化学品进出内河港口，未将有关事项事先报告海事管理机构并经其同意的；  
(四)载运危险化学品的船舶在内河航行、装卸或者停泊，未悬挂专用的警示标志，或者未按照规定显示专用信号，或者未按照规定申请引航的。  
未向港口行政管理部门报告并经其同意，在港口内进行危险化学品的装卸、过驳作业的，依照《中华人民共和国港口法》的规定处罚。

**Article 92** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Traffic Safety on Inland Waters:  
(1) the waterway transport enterprise transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waterways fails to formulate emergency rescue plans for hazardous chemical accidents of transport vessels, or fails to provide sufficient and effective emergency rescue appliances and equipment for the transport vessels;  
(2) the owner or operator of a vessel transporting hazardous chemicals through inland waterways fails to obtain a certificate of insurance for vessel pollution damage liability or a financial guarantee;  
(3) a vessel carrying hazardous chemicals entering or leaving an inland river port fails to report the relevant matters to the maritime administrative agency in advance and obtain its consent; or  
(4) a vessel carrying hazardous chemicals navigating, loading, unloading or berthing on inland waters fails to display special warning signs, or fails to display special signals in accordance with the provisions, or fails to apply for pilotage in accordance with the provisions.  
If loading, unloading or lightering operations of hazardous chemicals are carried out in a port without reporting to and obtaining the consent of the port administrative department, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Port Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　伪造、变造或者出租、出借、转让危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证，或者使用伪造、变造的危险化学品安全生产许可证、工业产品生产许可证的，分别依照《安全生产许可证条例》、《中华人民共和国工业产品生产许可证管理条例》的规定处罚。  
伪造、变造或者出租、出借、转让本条例规定的其他许可证，或者使用伪造、变造的本条例规定的其他许可证的，分别由相关许可证的颁发管理机关处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 93** If a person forges, alters, rents, lends or transfers a hazardous chemical safety production license or an industrial product production license, or uses a forged or altered hazardous chemical safety production license or industrial product production license, the penalty shall be imposed respectively in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Safety Production License and the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Industrial Product Production Licenses.  
If a person forges, alters, rents, lends or transfers other licenses prescribed in these Regulations, or uses forged or altered other licenses prescribed in these Regulations, the issuing and management authority of the relevant license shall respectively impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and confiscate illegal gains if any; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　危险化学品单位发生危险化学品事故，其主要负责人不立即组织救援或者不立即向有关部门报告的，依照《生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例》的规定处罚。  
危险化学品单位发生危险化学品事故，造成他人人身伤害或者财产损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 94** If a hazardous chemical accident occurs in a hazardous chemical unit, and the person primarily in charge thereof fails to immediately organize rescue or fails to immediately report to the relevant department, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents.  
If a hazardous chemical accident occurs in a hazardous chemical unit, causing personal injury or property damage to others, the unit shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　发生危险化学品事故，有关地方人民政府及其有关部门不立即组织实施救援，或者不采取必要的应急处置措施减少事故损失，防止事故蔓延、扩大的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 95** If a hazardous chemical accident occurs, and the relevant local people's government and its relevant departments fail to immediately organize and implement rescue, or fail to take necessary emergency response measures to reduce accident losses and prevent the accident from spreading and expanding, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　负有危险化学品安全监督管理职责的部门的工作人员，在危险化学品安全监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 96** If a staff member of a department responsible for the safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of safety supervision and administration of hazardous chemicals, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the staff member shall be given sanctions according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　监控化学品、属于危险化学品的药品和农药的安全管理，依照本条例的规定执行；法律、行政法规另有规定的，依照其规定。  
民用爆炸物品、烟花爆竹、放射性物品、核能物质以及用于国防科研生产的危险化学品的安全管理，不适用本条例。  
法律、行政法规对燃气的安全管理另有规定的，依照其规定。  
危险化学品容器属于特种设备的，其安全管理依照有关特种设备安全的法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 97** The safety management of monitored chemicals, pharmaceuticals that are hazardous chemicals, and pesticides shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; if laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.  
These Regulations shall not apply to the safety management of civil explosives, fireworks and firecrackers, radioactive articles, nuclear materials, and hazardous chemicals used for national defense scientific research and production.  
If laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the safety management of fuel gas, such provisions shall prevail.  
If hazardous chemical containers are special equipment, their safety management shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations on the safety of special equipment.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　危险化学品的进出口管理，依照有关对外贸易的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行；进口的危险化学品的储存、使用、经营、运输的安全管理，依照本条例的规定执行。  
危险化学品环境管理登记和新化学物质环境管理登记，依照有关环境保护的法律、行政法规、规章的规定执行。危险化学品环境管理登记，按照国家有关规定收取费用。

**Article 98** The import and export management of hazardous chemicals shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules on foreign trade; the safety management of the storage, use, operation and transport of imported hazardous chemicals shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
The environmental management registration of hazardous chemicals and the environmental management registration of new chemical substances shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules on environmental protection. Fees shall be charged for the environmental management registration of hazardous chemicals in accordance with the relevant national provisions.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　公众发现、捡拾的无主危险化学品，由公安机关接收。公安机关接收或者有关部门依法没收的危险化学品，需要进行无害化处理的，交由环境保护主管部门组织其认定的专业单位进行处理，或者交由有关危险化学品生产企业进行处理。处理所需费用由国家财政负担。

**Article 99** Hazardous chemicals without an owner found or picked up by the public shall be taken over by the public security organ. If the hazardous chemicals taken over by the public security organ or confiscated by the relevant department according to law need to be rendered harmless, they shall be delivered to a professional unit recognized by the environmental protection department for treatment, or to the relevant hazardous chemical production enterprise for treatment. The expenses required for such treatment shall be borne by the state finance.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　化学品的危险特性尚未确定的，由国务院安全生产监督管理部门、国务院环境保护主管部门、国务院卫生主管部门分别负责组织对该化学品的物理危险性、环境危害性、毒理特性进行鉴定。根据鉴定结果，需要调整危险化学品目录的，依照[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款的规定办理。

**Article 100** If the hazardous characteristics of a chemical have not yet been determined, the work safety supervision and administration department of the State Council, the environmental protection department of the State Council, and the health department of the State Council shall respectively be responsible for organizing expert evaluations of the physical hazards, environmental hazards and toxicological characteristics of such chemical. If, based on the evaluation results, the catalogue of hazardous chemicals needs to be adjusted, such adjustment shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 3 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　本条例施行前已经使用危险化学品从事生产的化工企业，依照本条例规定需要取得危险化学品安全使用许可证的，应当在国务院安全生产监督管理部门规定的期限内，申请取得危险化学品安全使用许可证。

**Article 101** Chemical enterprises that have used hazardous chemicals for production before these Regulations come into force and need to obtain a hazardous chemical safety use permit in accordance with these Regulations shall, within the time limit prescribed by the safety production supervision and administration department of the State Council, apply for and obtain a hazardous chemical safety use permit.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　本条例自2011年12月1日起施行。

**Article 102** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2011.
