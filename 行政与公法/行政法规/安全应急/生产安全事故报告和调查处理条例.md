# 生产安全事故报告和调查处理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2007-04-09  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> (2007年3月28日国务院第172次常务会议通过　2007年4月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第493号公布　自2007年6月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范生产安全事故的报告和调查处理，落实生产安全事故责任追究制度，防止和减少生产安全事故，根据《中华人民共和国安全生产法》和有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　生产经营活动中发生的造成人身伤亡或者直接经济损失的生产安全事故的报告和调查处理，适用本条例；环境污染事故、核设施事故、国防科研生产事故的报告和调查处理不适用本条例。 第三条　根据生产安全事故(以下简称事故)造成的人员伤亡或者直接经济损失，事故一般分为以下等级： (一)特别重大事故，是指造成30人以上死亡，或者100人以上重伤(包括急性工业中毒，下同)，或者1亿元以上直接经济损失的事故； (二)重大事故，是指造成10人以上30人以下死亡，或者50人以上100人以下重伤，或者5000万元以上1亿元以下直接经济损失的事故； (三)较大事故，是指造成3人以上10人以下死亡，或者10人以上50人以下重伤，或者1000万元以上5000万元以下直接经济损失的事故； (四)一般事故，是指造成3人以下死亡，或者10人以下重伤，或者1000万元以下直接经济损失的事故。 国务院安全生产监督管理部门可以会同国务院有关部门，制定事故等级划分的补充性规定。 本条第一款所称的“以上”包括本数，所称的“以下”不包括本数。 第四条　事故报告应当及时、准确、完整，任何单位和个人对事故不得迟报、漏报、谎报或者瞒报。 事故调查处理应当坚持实事求是、尊重科学的原则，及时、准确地查清事故经过、事故原因和事故损失，查明事故性质，认定事故责任，总结事故教训，提出整改措施，并对事故责任者依法追究责任。 第五条　县级以上人民政府应当依照本条例的规定，严格履行职责，及时、准确地完成事故调查处理工作。 事故发生地有关地方人民政府应当支持、配合上级人民政府或者有关部门的事故调查处理工作，并提供必要的便利条件。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范生产安全事故的报告和调查处理，落实生产安全事故责任追究制度，防止和减少生产安全事故，根据《中华人民共和国安全生产法》和有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, for the purposes of regulating the reporting, investigation and handling of work safety accidents, implementing the accountability system for work safety accidents, and preventing and reducing work safety accidents.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　生产经营活动中发生的造成人身伤亡或者直接经济损失的生产安全事故的报告和调查处理，适用本条例；环境污染事故、核设施事故、国防科研生产事故的报告和调查处理不适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the reporting, investigation and handling of work safety accidents that cause personal injury or death or direct economic losses in production and business operation activities; the reporting, investigation and handling of environmental pollution accidents, nuclear facility accidents, and national defense scientific research and production accidents shall not be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　根据生产安全事故(以下简称事故)造成的人员伤亡或者直接经济损失，事故一般分为以下等级：  
(一)特别重大事故，是指造成30人以上死亡，或者100人以上重伤(包括急性工业中毒，下同)，或者1亿元以上直接经济损失的事故；  
(二)重大事故，是指造成10人以上30人以下死亡，或者50人以上100人以下重伤，或者5000万元以上1亿元以下直接经济损失的事故；  
(三)较大事故，是指造成3人以上10人以下死亡，或者10人以上50人以下重伤，或者1000万元以上5000万元以下直接经济损失的事故；  
(四)一般事故，是指造成3人以下死亡，或者10人以下重伤，或者1000万元以下直接经济损失的事故。  
国务院安全生产监督管理部门可以会同国务院有关部门，制定事故等级划分的补充性规定。  
本条第一款所称的“以上”包括本数，所称的“以下”不包括本数。

**Article 3** According to the personal injury or death or direct economic losses caused by a work safety accident (hereinafter referred to as an accident), accidents shall generally be classified into the following grades:  
(1) An especially serious accident refers to an accident causing death of 30 or more persons, or serious injury (including acute industrial poisoning, the same below) to 100 or more persons, or direct economic losses of 100 million yuan or more;  
(2) A serious accident refers to an accident causing death of 10 or more but less than 30 persons, or serious injury to 50 or more but less than 100 persons, or direct economic losses of 50 million yuan or more but less than 100 million yuan;  
(3) A relatively serious accident refers to an accident causing death of 3 or more but less than 10 persons, or serious injury to 10 or more but less than 50 persons, or direct economic losses of 10 million yuan or more but less than 50 million yuan;  
(4) A general accident refers to an accident causing death of less than 3 persons, or serious injury to less than 10 persons, or direct economic losses of less than 10 million yuan.  
The work safety regulatory authority under the State Council may, jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate supplementary provisions for the classification of accident grades.  
The term "or more" used in the first paragraph of this Article shall include the given number, and the term "less than" used therein shall not include the given number.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　事故报告应当及时、准确、完整，任何单位和个人对事故不得迟报、漏报、谎报或者瞒报。  
事故调查处理应当坚持实事求是、尊重科学的原则，及时、准确地查清事故经过、事故原因和事故损失，查明事故性质，认定事故责任，总结事故教训，提出整改措施，并对事故责任者依法追究责任。

**Article 4** Accident reporting shall be timely, accurate and complete, and no entity or individual shall delay reporting, omit reporting, make false reports or conceal the reporting of an accident.  
Investigation and handling of accidents shall adhere to the principles of seeking truth from facts and respecting science, timely and accurately ascertain the course of the accident, the causes of the accident and the losses caused by the accident, determine the nature of the accident, identify the responsibilities for the accident, summarize the lessons learned from the accident, propose corrective measures, and investigate the liability of those responsible for the accident according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当依照本条例的规定，严格履行职责，及时、准确地完成事故调查处理工作。  
事故发生地有关地方人民政府应当支持、配合上级人民政府或者有关部门的事故调查处理工作，并提供必要的便利条件。  
参加事故调查处理的部门和单位应当互相配合，提高事故调查处理工作的效率。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall strictly perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and complete the work of accident investigation and handling in a timely and accurate manner.  
The relevant local people's government of the place where the accident occurred shall support and cooperate with the superior people's government or the relevant departments in the work of accident investigation and handling, and provide necessary conveniences.  
The departments and units participating in accident investigation and handling shall cooperate with each other to enhance the efficiency of accident investigation and handling.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　工会依法参加事故调查处理，有权向有关部门提出处理意见。

**Article 6** Trade unions shall participate in accident investigation and handling according to law, and have the right to submit handling opinions to the relevant departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人不得阻挠和干涉对事故的报告和依法调查处理。

**Article 7** No entity or individual may obstruct or interfere with the reporting, investigation and handling of accidents according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对事故报告和调查处理中的违法行为，任何单位和个人有权向安全生产监督管理部门、监察机关或者其他有关部门举报，接到举报的部门应当依法及时处理。

**Article 8** With respect to illegal acts in accident reporting, investigation and handling, any entity or individual shall have the right to report to the work safety regulatory authority, supervisory authority or other relevant departments, and the department receiving the report shall handle it in a timely manner according to law.

### 第二章　事故报告

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　事故发生后，事故现场有关人员应当立即向本单位负责人报告；单位负责人接到报告后，应当于1小时内向事故发生地县级以上人民政府安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门报告。  
情况紧急时，事故现场有关人员可以直接向事故发生地县级以上人民政府安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门报告。

**Article 9** After an accident occurs, the relevant personnel at the accident scene shall immediately report to the person in charge of their own unit; after receiving the report, the person in charge of the unit shall, within one hour, report to the work safety regulatory authority of the people's government at or above the county level of the place where the accident occurred and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration.  
In an emergency, the relevant personnel at the accident scene may directly report to the work safety regulatory authority of the people's government at or above the county level of the place where the accident occurred and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门接到事故报告后，应当依照下列规定上报事故情况，并通知公安机关、劳动保障行政部门、工会和人民检察院：  
(一)特别重大事故、重大事故逐级上报至国务院安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门；  
(二)较大事故逐级上报至省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门；  
(三)一般事故上报至设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门。  
安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门依照前款规定上报事故情况，应当同时报告本级人民政府。国务院安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门以及省级人民政府接到发生特别重大事故、重大事故的报告后，应当立即报告国务院。  
必要时，安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门可以越级上报事故情况。

**Article 10** After receiving an accident report, the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration shall report the accident situation level by level in accordance with the following provisions, and shall notify the public security organ, the labor and social security administrative department, the trade union and the People's Procuratorate:  
(1) Especially serious accidents and serious accidents shall be reported level by level to the work safety regulatory authority under the State Council and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration;  
(2) Relatively serious accidents shall be reported level by level to the work safety regulatory authority of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration;  
(3) General accidents shall be reported to the work safety regulatory authority of the people's government at the city with districts level and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration.  
When reporting the accident situation in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration shall simultaneously report to the people's government at the same level. After receiving reports on especially serious accidents and serious accidents, the work safety regulatory authority under the State Council and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration, as well as the provincial people's government, shall immediately report to the State Council.  
When necessary, the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration may skip levels in reporting the accident situation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门逐级上报事故情况，每级上报的时间不得超过2小时。

**Article 11** Where the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration report the accident situation level by level, the time for reporting at each level shall not exceed two hours.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　报告事故应当包括下列内容：  
(一)事故发生单位概况；  
(二)事故发生的时间、地点以及事故现场情况；  
(三)事故的简要经过；  
(四)事故已经造成或者可能造成的伤亡人数(包括下落不明的人数)和初步估计的直接经济损失；  
(五)已经采取的措施；  
(六)其他应当报告的情况。

**Article 12** An accident report shall include the following contents:  
(1) An overview of the unit where the accident occurred;  
(2) The time and place of the accident and the condition of the accident scene;  
(3) A brief description of the course of the accident;  
(4) The number of casualties (including the number of persons whose whereabouts are unknown) already caused or likely to be caused by the accident, and the preliminary estimate of direct economic losses;  
(5) Measures already taken;  
(6) Other circumstances that shall be reported.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　事故报告后出现新情况的，应当及时补报。  
自事故发生之日起30日内，事故造成的伤亡人数发生变化的，应当及时补报。道路交通事故、火灾事故自发生之日起7日内，事故造成的伤亡人数发生变化的，应当及时补报。

**Article 13** If new circumstances arise after the accident report, a supplementary report shall be made in a timely manner.  
If the number of casualties caused by an accident changes within 30 days from the date of occurrence of the accident, a supplementary report shall be made in a timely manner. For road traffic accidents and fire accidents, if the number of casualties changes within 7 days from the date of occurrence, a supplementary report shall be made in a timely manner.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　事故发生单位负责人接到事故报告后，应当立即启动事故相应应急预案，或者采取有效措施，组织抢救，防止事故扩大，减少人员伤亡和财产损失。

**Article 14** After receiving the accident report, the person in charge of the unit where the accident occurred shall immediately activate the corresponding emergency plan for the accident, or take effective measures to organize rescue, prevent the accident from expanding, and reduce casualties and property losses.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　事故发生地有关地方人民政府、安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门接到事故报告后，其负责人应当立即赶赴事故现场，组织事故救援。

**Article 15** After receiving the accident report, the relevant local people's government of the place where the accident occurred, the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration, the persons in charge thereof shall immediately rush to the accident scene and organize accident rescue.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　事故发生后，有关单位和人员应当妥善保护事故现场以及相关证据，任何单位和个人不得破坏事故现场、毁灭相关证据。  
因抢救人员、防止事故扩大以及疏通交通等原因，需要移动事故现场物件的，应当做出标志，绘制现场简图并做出书面记录，妥善保存现场重要痕迹、物证。

**Article 16** After an accident occurs, the relevant units and personnel shall properly protect the accident scene and the relevant evidence; no entity or individual may damage the accident scene or destroy the relevant evidence.  
If objects at the accident scene need to be moved due to reasons such as rescuing persons, preventing the accident from expanding, or clearing traffic, marks shall be made, a sketch of the scene shall be drawn, written records shall be made, and important traces and material evidence at the scene shall be properly preserved.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　事故发生地公安机关根据事故的情况，对涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法立案侦查，采取强制措施和侦查措施。犯罪嫌疑人逃匿的，公安机关应当迅速追捕归案。

**Article 17** The public security organ of the place where the accident occurred shall, based on the circumstances of the accident, if any crime is suspected, file a case for investigation according to law and take compulsory measures and investigative measures. If a criminal suspect absconds, the public security organ shall promptly pursue and capture the suspect.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门应当建立值班制度，并向社会公布值班电话，受理事故报告和举报。

**Article 18** The work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration shall establish a duty system and make the duty telephone number known to the public for receiving accident reports and reports.

### 第三章　事故调查

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　特别重大事故由国务院或者国务院授权有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
重大事故、较大事故、一般事故分别由事故发生地省级人民政府、设区的市级人民政府、县级人民政府负责调查。省级人民政府、设区的市级人民政府、县级人民政府可以直接组织事故调查组进行调查，也可以授权或者委托有关部门组织事故调查组进行调查。  
未造成人员伤亡的一般事故，县级人民政府也可以委托事故发生单位组织事故调查组进行调查。

**Article 19** An especially serious accident shall be investigated by an accident investigation team organized by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council.  
Serious accidents, relatively serious accidents and general accidents shall be investigated by the provincial people's government, the people's government of a city with districts, and the county people's government of the place where the accident occurred, respectively. The provincial people's government, the people's government of a city with districts, and the county people's government may directly organize an accident investigation team to conduct the investigation, or may authorize or entrust the relevant departments to organize an accident investigation team to conduct the investigation.  
For a general accident that does not cause casualties, the county people's government may also entrust the unit where the accident occurred to organize an accident investigation team to conduct the investigation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　上级人民政府认为必要时，可以调查由下级人民政府负责调查的事故。  
自事故发生之日起30日内(道路交通事故、火灾事故自发生之日起7日内)，因事故伤亡人数变化导致事故等级发生变化，依照本条例规定应当由上级人民政府负责调查的，上级人民政府可以另行组织事故调查组进行调查。

**Article 20** Where the superior people's government deems it necessary, it may investigate an accident that shall be investigated by the subordinate people's government.  
Within 30 days from the date of occurrence of the accident (for road traffic accidents and fire accidents, within 7 days from the date of occurrence), if the grade of the accident changes due to changes in the number of casualties and, in accordance with these Regulations, the accident shall be investigated by the superior people's government, the superior people's government may organize a separate accident investigation team to conduct the investigation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　特别重大事故以下等级事故，事故发生地与事故发生单位不在同一个县级以上行政区域的，由事故发生地人民政府负责调查，事故发生单位所在地人民政府应当派人参加。

**Article 21** For an accident of a grade lower than an especially serious accident, if the place where the accident occurred and the location of the unit where the accident occurred are not in the same administrative area at or above the county level, the people's government of the place where the accident occurred shall be responsible for the investigation, and the people's government of the place where the unit where the accident occurred is located shall send personnel to participate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　事故调查组的组成应当遵循精简、效能的原则。  
根据事故的具体情况，事故调查组由有关人民政府、安全生产监督管理部门、负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门、监察机关、公安机关以及工会派人组成，并应当邀请人民检察院派人参加。  
事故调查组可以聘请有关专家参与调查。

**Article 22** The composition of an accident investigation team shall follow the principles of simplicity and efficiency.  
Based on the specific circumstances of the accident, the accident investigation team shall be composed of personnel sent by the relevant people's government, the work safety regulatory authority, the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration, the supervisory authority, the public security organ, and the trade union, and shall invite the People's Procuratorate to send personnel to participate.  
The accident investigation team may engage relevant experts to participate in the investigation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　事故调查组成员应当具有事故调查所需要的知识和专长，并与所调查的事故没有直接利害关系。

**Article 23** Members of an accident investigation team shall have the knowledge and expertise required for accident investigation, and shall have no direct interest in the accident under investigation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　事故调查组组长由负责事故调查的人民政府指定。事故调查组组长主持事故调查组的工作。

**Article 24** The head of an accident investigation team shall be designated by the people's government responsible for the accident investigation. The head of the accident investigation team shall preside over the work of the accident investigation team.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　事故调查组履行下列职责：  
(一)查明事故发生的经过、原因、人员伤亡情况及直接经济损失；  
(二)认定事故的性质和事故责任；  
(三)提出对事故责任者的处理建议；  
(四)总结事故教训，提出防范和整改措施；  
(五)提交事故调查报告。

**Article 25** An accident investigation team shall perform the following duties:  
(1) To ascertain the course of the accident, the causes, the casualties and the direct economic losses;  
(2) To determine the nature of the accident and the responsibilities for the accident;  
(3) To propose suggestions on handling of persons responsible for the accident;  
(4) To summarize the lessons learned from the accident and propose preventive and corrective measures;  
(5) To submit an accident investigation report.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　事故调查组有权向有关单位和个人了解与事故有关的情况，并要求其提供相关文件、资料，有关单位和个人不得拒绝。  
事故发生单位的负责人和有关人员在事故调查期间不得擅离职守，并应当随时接受事故调查组的询问，如实提供有关情况。  
事故调查中发现涉嫌犯罪的，事故调查组应当及时将有关材料或者其复印件移交司法机关处理。

**Article 26** An accident investigation team shall have the right to learn from relevant entities and individuals about circumstances related to the accident, and to require them to provide relevant documents and materials; the relevant entities and individuals may not refuse.  
The person in charge and relevant personnel of the unit where the accident occurred shall not leave their posts without authorization during the accident investigation, and shall at any time accept inquiries from the accident investigation team and truthfully provide relevant information.  
If a crime is suspected during the accident investigation, the accident investigation team shall promptly transfer the relevant materials or copies thereof to the judicial authorities for handling.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　事故调查中需要进行技术鉴定的，事故调查组应当委托具有国家规定资质的单位进行技术鉴定。必要时，事故调查组可以直接组织专家进行技术鉴定。技术鉴定所需时间不计入事故调查期限。

**Article 27** If a technical expert evaluation is needed during the accident investigation, the accident investigation team shall entrust a unit with the qualifications prescribed by the State to conduct the technical expert evaluation. When necessary, the accident investigation team may directly organize experts to conduct the technical expert evaluation. The time needed for technical expert evaluation shall not be counted in the accident investigation period.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　事故调查组成员在事故调查工作中应当诚信公正、恪尽职守，遵守事故调查组的纪律，保守事故调查的秘密。  
未经事故调查组组长允许，事故调查组成员不得擅自发布有关事故的信息。

**Article 28** Members of an accident investigation team shall, in the course of accident investigation work, act with integrity and impartiality, perform their duties diligently, abide by the discipline of the accident investigation team, and keep the secrets of the accident investigation.  
Without the permission of the head of the accident investigation team, no member of the accident investigation team may release information concerning the accident without authorization.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　事故调查组应当自事故发生之日起60日内提交事故调查报告；特殊情况下，经负责事故调查的人民政府批准，提交事故调查报告的期限可以适当延长，但延长的期限最长不超过60日。

**Article 29** An accident investigation team shall submit an accident investigation report within 60 days from the date of occurrence of the accident; under special circumstances, upon approval by the people's government responsible for the accident investigation, the time limit for submitting the accident investigation report may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed 60 days at most.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　事故调查报告应当包括下列内容：  
(一)事故发生单位概况；  
(二)事故发生经过和事故救援情况；  
(三)事故造成的人员伤亡和直接经济损失；  
(四)事故发生的原因和事故性质；  
(五)事故责任的认定以及对事故责任者的处理建议；  
(六)事故防范和整改措施。  
事故调查报告应当附具有关证据材料。事故调查组成员应当在事故调查报告上签名。

**Article 30** An accident investigation report shall include the following contents:  
(1) An overview of the unit where the accident occurred;  
(2) The course of the accident and the circumstances of accident rescue;  
(3) The casualties and direct economic losses caused by the accident;  
(4) The causes of the accident and the nature of the accident;  
(5) The determination of responsibilities for the accident and the suggestions on handling of persons responsible for the accident;  
(6) Preventive and corrective measures for accidents.  
The accident investigation report shall be accompanied by relevant evidence materials. Members of the accident investigation team shall sign the accident investigation report.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　事故调查报告报送负责事故调查的人民政府后，事故调查工作即告结束。事故调查的有关资料应当归档保存。

**Article 31** After the accident investigation report is submitted to the people's government responsible for the accident investigation, the accident investigation work shall be concluded. The relevant materials of the accident investigation shall be archived and preserved.

### 第四章　事故处理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　重大事故、较大事故、一般事故，负责事故调查的人民政府应当自收到事故调查报告之日起15日内做出批复；特别重大事故，30日内做出批复，特殊情况下，批复时间可以适当延长，但延长的时间最长不超过30日。  
有关机关应当按照人民政府的批复，依照法律、行政法规规定的权限和程序，对事故发生单位和有关人员进行行政处罚，对负有事故责任的国家工作人员进行处分。  
事故发生单位应当按照负责事故调查的人民政府的批复，对本单位负有事故责任的人员进行处理。  
负有事故责任的人员涉嫌犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** For a serious accident, a relatively serious accident, or a general accident, the people's government responsible for the accident investigation shall make a written reply within 15 days from the date of receipt of the accident investigation report; for an especially serious accident, it shall make a written reply within 30 days; under special circumstances, the time for making a reply may be appropriately extended, but the extension shall not exceed 30 days at most.  
The relevant authorities shall, in accordance with the written reply of the people's government and within the limits of their powers and in accordance with the procedures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, impose administrative penalties on the unit where the accident occurred and the relevant persons, and impose sanctions on state functionaries bearing responsibility for the accident.  
The unit where the accident occurred shall, in accordance with the written reply of the people's government responsible for the accident investigation, deal with the persons in the unit who are responsible for the accident.  
If a person responsible for the accident is suspected of committing a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　事故发生单位应当认真吸取事故教训，落实防范和整改措施，防止事故再次发生。防范和整改措施的落实情况应当接受工会和职工的监督。  
安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门应当对事故发生单位落实防范和整改措施的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 33** The unit where the accident occurred shall earnestly learn lessons from the accident, implement preventive and corrective measures, and prevent the recurrence of accidents. The implementation of preventive and corrective measures shall be subject to the supervision of the trade union and the employees.  
The work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration shall supervise and inspect the implementation of preventive and corrective measures by the unit where the accident occurred.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　事故处理的情况由负责事故调查的人民政府或者其授权的有关部门、机构向社会公布，依法应当保密的除外。

**Article 34** The circumstances of accident handling shall be made public by the people's government responsible for the accident investigation or the relevant departments or agencies authorized by it, except for those that shall be kept confidential according to law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　事故发生单位主要负责人有下列行为之一的，处上一年年收入40%至80%的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不立即组织事故抢救的；  
(二)迟报或者漏报事故的；  
(三)在事故调查处理期间擅离职守的。

**Article 35** If the principal person in charge of the unit where the accident occurred commits any of the following acts, a fine of 40% to 80% of his/her annual income of the previous year shall be imposed; if he/she is a state functionary, a sanction shall also be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to immediately organize accident rescue;  
(2) Delaying reporting or omitting reporting of the accident;  
(3) Leaving his/her post without authorization during the period of accident investigation and handling.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　事故发生单位及其有关人员有下列行为之一的，对事故发生单位处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处上一年年收入60%至100%的罚款；属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)谎报或者瞒报事故的；  
(二)伪造或者故意破坏事故现场的；  
(三)转移、隐匿资金、财产，或者销毁有关证据、资料的；  
(四)拒绝接受调查或者拒绝提供有关情况和资料的；  
(五)在事故调查中作伪证或者指使他人作伪证的；  
(六)事故发生后逃匿的。

**Article 36** If the unit where the accident occurred or the relevant persons thereof commit any of the following acts, a fine of not less than 1 million yuan but not more than 5 million yuan shall be imposed on the unit where the accident occurred; a fine of 60% to 100% of the annual income of the previous year shall be imposed on the principal person in charge, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons; if they are state functionaries, a sanction shall also be imposed according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Making false reports or concealing the accident;  
(2) Forging or deliberately damaging the accident scene;  
(3) Transferring or concealing funds or property, or destroying relevant evidence or materials;  
(4) Refusing to accept investigation or refusing to provide relevant information and materials;  
(5) Giving false testimony during the accident investigation or instigating others to give false testimony;  
(6) Absconding after the accident occurs.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　事故发生单位对事故发生负有责任的，依照下列规定处以罚款：  
(一)发生一般事故的，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；  
(二)发生较大事故的，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；  
(三)发生重大事故的，处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；  
(四)发生特别重大事故的，处200万元以上500万元以下的罚款。

**Article 37** Where the unit where the accident occurred bears responsibility for the accident, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) For a general accident, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan;  
(2) For a relatively serious accident, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan;  
(3) For a serious accident, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2 million yuan;  
(4) For an especially serious accident, a fine of not less than 2 million yuan but not more than 5 million yuan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　事故发生单位主要负责人未依法履行安全生产管理职责，导致事故发生的，依照下列规定处以罚款；属于国家工作人员的，并依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)发生一般事故的，处上一年年收入30%的罚款；  
(二)发生较大事故的，处上一年年收入40%的罚款；  
(三)发生重大事故的，处上一年年收入60%的罚款；  
(四)发生特别重大事故的，处上一年年收入80%的罚款。

**Article 38** If the principal person in charge of the unit where the accident occurred fails to perform the duties of work safety management according to law, resulting in the occurrence of the accident, a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the following provisions; if he/she is a state functionary, a sanction shall also be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) For a general accident, a fine of 30% of the annual income of the previous year;  
(2) For a relatively serious accident, a fine of 40% of the annual income of the previous year;  
(3) For a serious accident, a fine of 60% of the annual income of the previous year;  
(4) For an especially serious accident, a fine of 80% of the annual income of the previous year.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有关地方人民政府、安全生产监督管理部门和负有安全生产监督管理职责的有关部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不立即组织事故抢救的；  
(二)迟报、漏报、谎报或者瞒报事故的；  
(三)阻碍、干涉事故调查工作的；  
(四)在事故调查中作伪证或者指使他人作伪证的。

**Article 39** If the relevant local people's government, the work safety regulatory authority and the relevant departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration commit any of the following acts, sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to immediately organize accident rescue;  
(2) Delaying reporting, omitting reporting, making false reports or concealing the accident;  
(3) Obstructing or interfering with the accident investigation work;  
(4) Giving false testimony during the accident investigation or instigating others to give false testimony.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　事故发生单位对事故发生负有责任的，由有关部门依法暂扣或者吊销其有关证照；对事故发生单位负有事故责任的有关人员，依法暂停或者撤销其与安全生产有关的执业资格、岗位证书；事故发生单位主要负责人受到刑事处罚或者撤职处分的，自刑罚执行完毕或者受处分之日起，5年内不得担任任何生产经营单位的主要负责人。  
为发生事故的单位提供虚假证明的中介机构，由有关部门依法暂扣或者吊销其有关证照及其相关人员的执业资格；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where the unit where the accident occurred bears responsibility for the accident, the relevant department shall, according to law, suspend or revoke its relevant licenses and permits; for the relevant persons of the unit where the accident occurred who bear responsibility for the accident, their practice qualifications and position certificates related to work safety shall be suspended or revoked according to law; if the principal person in charge of the unit where the accident occurred is subject to criminal punishment or is removed from office, he/she shall not serve as the principal person in charge of any production or business operation unit within five years from the date of completion of execution of the punishment or from the date of the sanction.  
If an intermediary institution provides false certification documents for the unit where the accident occurred, the relevant department shall, according to law, suspend or revoke its relevant licenses and permits and the practice qualifications of its relevant personnel; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　参与事故调查的人员在事故调查中有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)对事故调查工作不负责任，致使事故调查工作有重大疏漏的；  
(二)包庇、袒护负有事故责任的人员或者借机打击报复的。

**Article 41** If a person participating in accident investigation commits any of the following acts during the accident investigation, a sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Being irresponsible in accident investigation work, causing major omissions in the accident investigation work;  
(2) Harboring or covering up persons responsible for the accident, or taking the opportunity to retaliate.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，有关地方人民政府或者有关部门故意拖延或者拒绝落实经批复的对事故责任人的处理意见的，由监察机关对有关责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the relevant local people's government or the relevant department deliberately delays or refuses to implement the approved handling opinions on persons responsible for the accident, the supervisory authority shall impose sanctions on the relevant liable persons according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本条例规定的罚款的行政处罚，由安全生产监督管理部门决定。  
法律、行政法规对行政处罚的种类、幅度和决定机关另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 43** The administrative penalty of a fine as provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the work safety regulatory authority.  
Where laws or administrative regulations otherwise provide for the types, ranges and deciding authorities of administrative penalties, those provisions shall prevail.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　没有造成人员伤亡，但是社会影响恶劣的事故，国务院或者有关地方人民政府认为需要调查处理的，依照本条例的有关规定执行。  
国家机关、事业单位、人民团体发生的事故的报告和调查处理，参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 44** Where an accident causes no casualties but has a serious adverse social impact, if the State Council or the relevant local people's government deems investigation and handling necessary, the relevant provisions of these Regulations shall be followed.  
For accidents occurring in state organs, public institutions, and people's organizations, the provisions of these Regulations shall be referred to for reporting, investigation and handling.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　特别重大事故以下等级事故的报告和调查处理，有关法律、行政法规或者国务院另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 45** Where relevant laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council otherwise provide for the reporting, investigation and handling of accidents of a grade lower than an especially serious accident, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自2007年6月1日起施行。国务院1989年3月29日公布的《特别重大事故调查程序暂行规定》和1991年2月22日公布的《企业职工伤亡事故报告和处理规定》同时废止。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2007. The Interim Provisions on Investigation Procedures for Especially Serious Accidents promulgated by the State Council on March 29, 1989, and the Provisions on Reporting and Handling of Casualty Accidents of Enterprise Employees promulgated on February 22, 1991, shall be abolished simultaneously.
