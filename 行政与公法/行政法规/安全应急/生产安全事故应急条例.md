### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范生产安全事故应急工作，保障人民群众生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国安全生产法》和《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Production Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China, for the purpose of regulating emergency response work for work safety accidents and safeguarding the life and property of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于生产安全事故应急工作；法律、行政法规另有规定的，适用其规定。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to emergency response work for work safety accidents; where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院统一领导全国的生产安全事故应急工作，县级以上地方人民政府统一领导本行政区域内的生产安全事故应急工作。生产安全事故应急工作涉及两个以上行政区域的，由有关行政区域共同的上一级人民政府负责，或者由各有关行政区域的上一级人民政府共同负责。  
县级以上人民政府应急管理部门和其他对有关行业、领域的安全生产工作实施监督管理的部门(以下统称负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门)在各自职责范围内，做好有关行业、领域的生产安全事故应急工作。  
县级以上人民政府应急管理部门指导、协调本级人民政府其他负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门和下级人民政府的生产安全事故应急工作。  
乡、镇人民政府以及街道办事处等地方人民政府派出机关应当协助上级人民政府有关部门依法履行生产安全事故应急工作职责。

**Article 3** The State Council shall exercise unified leadership over the national emergency response work for work safety accidents, and local people's governments at or above the county level shall exercise unified leadership over the emergency response work for work safety accidents within their respective administrative regions. Where emergency response work for work safety accidents involves two or more administrative regions, the common higher-level people's government of the relevant administrative regions shall be responsible, or the higher-level people's governments of the relevant administrative regions shall be jointly responsible.  
The emergency management departments of people's governments at or above the county level and other departments that supervise and administer work safety in relevant industries and fields (hereinafter collectively referred to as departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration) shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out emergency response work for work safety accidents in the relevant industries and fields.  
The emergency management departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall guide and coordinate the emergency response work for work safety accidents of other departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration under the same people's government and of lower-level people's governments.  
Township and town people's governments, sub-district offices, and other agencies dispatched by local people's governments shall assist the relevant departments of higher-level people's governments in performing their duties of emergency response work for work safety accidents according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产经营单位应当加强生产安全事故应急工作，建立、健全生产安全事故应急工作责任制，其主要负责人对本单位的生产安全事故应急工作全面负责。

**Article 4** Work safety-related entities shall strengthen emergency response work for work safety accidents, and establish and improve a responsibility system for such emergency response work. The principal person in charge of an entity shall assume overall responsibility for the entity's emergency response work for work safety accidents.

### 第二章　应急准备

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府及其负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门和乡、镇人民政府以及街道办事处等地方人民政府派出机关，应当针对可能发生的生产安全事故的特点和危害，进行风险辨识和评估，制定相应的生产安全事故应急救援预案，并依法向社会公布。  
生产经营单位应当针对本单位可能发生的生产安全事故的特点和危害，进行风险辨识和评估，制定相应的生产安全事故应急救援预案，并向本单位从业人员公布。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level and their departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration, township and town people's governments, sub-district offices, and other agencies dispatched by local people's governments shall, in light of the characteristics and hazards of potential work safety accidents, carry out risk identification and assessment, formulate corresponding emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents, and make them public to the society according to law.  
Work safety-related entities shall, in light of the characteristics and hazards of potential work safety accidents within their own units, carry out risk identification and assessment, formulate corresponding emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents, and make them public to their employees.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生产安全事故应急救援预案应当符合有关法律、法规、规章和标准的规定，具有科学性、针对性和可操作性，明确规定应急组织体系、职责分工以及应急救援程序和措施。  
有下列情形之一的，生产安全事故应急救援预案制定单位应当及时修订相关预案：  
(一)制定预案所依据的法律、法规、规章、标准发生重大变化；  
(二)应急指挥机构及其职责发生调整；  
(三)安全生产面临的风险发生重大变化；  
(四)重要应急资源发生重大变化；  
(五)在预案演练或者应急救援中发现需要修订预案的重大问题；  
(六)其他应当修订的情形。

**Article 6** Emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, rules and standards, be scientific, targeted and operable, and clearly specify the emergency organization system, division of responsibilities, and emergency rescue procedures and measures.  
Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the entity that formulated the emergency rescue plan for work safety accidents shall revise the relevant plan in a timely manner:  
(1) The laws, regulations, rules or standards based on which the plan was formulated undergo significant changes;  
(2) The emergency command organization or its responsibilities are adjusted;  
(3) The risks faced in work safety undergo significant changes;  
(4) Important emergency resources undergo significant changes;  
(5) Major problems requiring revision of the plan are discovered during plan drills or emergency rescue;  
(6) Other circumstances where revision is required.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当将其制定的生产安全事故应急救援预案报送本级人民政府备案；易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位，矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位，以及宾馆、商场、娱乐场所、旅游景区等人员密集场所经营单位，应当将其制定的生产安全事故应急救援预案按照国家有关规定报送县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门备案，并依法向社会公布。

**Article 7** Departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration of people's governments at or above the county level shall submit the emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents formulated by them to the people's government at the same level for recordation; entities that produce, operate, store or transport hazardous materials such as flammable and explosive substances and dangerous chemicals, mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, and construction entities, as well as entities operating densely populated places such as hotels, shopping malls, entertainment venues and tourist attractions, shall submit the emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents formulated by them to the departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration of people's governments at or above the county level for recordation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and make them public to the society according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府以及县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门，乡、镇人民政府以及街道办事处等地方人民政府派出机关，应当至少每2年组织1次生产安全事故应急救援预案演练。  
易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位，矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位，以及宾馆、商场、娱乐场所、旅游景区等人员密集场所经营单位，应当至少每半年组织1次生产安全事故应急救援预案演练，并将演练情况报送所在地县级以上地方人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当对本行政区域内前款规定的重点生产经营单位的生产安全事故应急救援预案演练进行抽查；发现演练不符合要求的，应当责令限期改正。

**Article 8** Local people's governments at or above the county level and their departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration, township and town people's governments, sub-district offices, and other agencies dispatched by local people's governments shall organize drills of emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents at least once every two years.  
Entities that produce, operate, store or transport hazardous materials such as flammable and explosive substances and dangerous chemicals, mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, and construction entities, as well as entities operating densely populated places such as hotels, shopping malls, entertainment venues and tourist attractions, shall organize drills of emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents at least once every six months, and report the drill situation to the department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place where the entity is located.  
The department with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration of a local people's government at or above the county level shall conduct spot checks on the drills of emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents of key work safety-related entities as specified in the preceding paragraph within its administrative region; if any drill fails to meet the requirements, the department shall order the entity to rectify within a time limit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对生产安全事故应急救援队伍建设的统一规划、组织和指导。  
县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门根据生产安全事故应急工作的实际需要，在重点行业、领域单独建立或者依托有条件的生产经营单位、社会组织共同建立应急救援队伍。  
国家鼓励和支持生产经营单位和其他社会力量建立提供社会化应急救援服务的应急救援队伍。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the unified planning, organization and guidance for the construction of emergency rescue teams for work safety accidents.  
Departments with responsibility for work safety supervision and administration of people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual needs of emergency response work for work safety accidents, independently establish or jointly establish emergency rescue teams with qualified work safety-related entities or social organizations in key industries and fields.  
The State encourages and supports work safety-related entities and other social forces to establish emergency rescue teams that provide socialized emergency rescue services.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位，矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位，以及宾馆、商场、娱乐场所、旅游景区等人员密集场所经营单位，应当建立应急救援队伍；其中，小型企业或者微型企业等规模较小的生产经营单位，可以不建立应急救援队伍，但应当指定兼职的应急救援人员，并且可以与邻近的应急救援队伍签订应急救援协议。  
工业园区、开发区等产业聚集区域内的生产经营单位，可以联合建立应急救援队伍。

**Article 10** Entities that produce, operate, store or transport hazardous materials such as flammable and explosive substances and dangerous chemicals, mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, and construction entities, as well as entities operating densely populated places such as hotels, shopping malls, entertainment venues and tourist attractions, shall establish emergency rescue teams; however, small or micro work safety-related entities of a smaller scale may not establish emergency rescue teams, but they shall designate part-time emergency rescue personnel and may sign emergency rescue agreements with nearby emergency rescue teams.  
Work safety-related entities within industrial parks, development zones and other industry cluster areas may jointly establish emergency rescue teams.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　应急救援队伍的应急救援人员应当具备必要的专业知识、技能、身体素质和心理素质。  
应急救援队伍建立单位或者兼职应急救援人员所在单位应当按照国家有关规定对应急救援人员进行培训；应急救援人员经培训合格后，方可参加应急救援工作。  
应急救援队伍应当配备必要的应急救援装备和物资，并定期组织训练。

**Article 11** Emergency rescue personnel of emergency rescue teams shall possess the necessary professional knowledge, skills, physical fitness, and psychological quality.  
The unit that establishes the emergency rescue team or the unit where part-time emergency rescue personnel work shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide training to emergency rescue personnel; emergency rescue personnel may participate in emergency rescue work only after passing the training.  
Emergency rescue teams shall be equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment and materials, and shall organize regular training.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　生产经营单位应当及时将本单位应急救援队伍建立情况按照国家有关规定报送县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门，并依法向社会公布。  
县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当定期将本行业、本领域的应急救援队伍建立情况报送本级人民政府，并依法向社会公布。

**Article 12** Workplace units shall promptly report the establishment of their emergency rescue teams to the departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall make the information public according to law.  
The departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration of the people's governments at or above the county level shall periodically report the establishment of emergency rescue teams in their respective industries and fields to the people's government at the same level, and shall make the information public according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域内可能发生的生产安全事故的特点和危害，储备必要的应急救援装备和物资，并及时更新和补充。  
易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位，矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位，以及宾馆、商场、娱乐场所、旅游景区等人员密集场所经营单位，应当根据本单位可能发生的生产安全事故的特点和危害，配备必要的灭火、排水、通风以及危险物品稀释、掩埋、收集等应急救援器材、设备和物资，并进行经常性维护、保养，保证正常运转。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the characteristics and hazards of production safety accidents that may occur within their administrative regions, stock necessary emergency rescue equipment and materials, and shall update and replenish them in a timely manner.  
Units that produce, operate, store, or transport dangerous goods such as flammable and explosive substances and hazardous chemicals, units engaged in mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, and construction, as well as units operating densely populated places such as hotels, shopping malls, entertainment venues, and tourist attractions, shall, based on the characteristics and hazards of production safety accidents that may occur in their units, be equipped with necessary emergency rescue apparatus, equipment, and materials for firefighting, drainage, ventilation, and dilution, burial, and collection of dangerous goods, and shall perform regular maintenance and servicing to ensure normal operation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　下列单位应当建立应急值班制度，配备应急值班人员：  
(一)县级以上人民政府及其负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门；  
(二)危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位以及矿山、金属冶炼、城市轨道交通运营、建筑施工单位；  
(三)应急救援队伍。  
规模较大、危险性较高的易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的生产、经营、储存、运输单位应当成立应急处置技术组，实行24小时应急值班。

**Article 14** The following units shall establish an emergency duty system and be equipped with emergency duty personnel:  
(1) People's governments at or above the county level and their departments responsible for work safety supervision and administration;  
(2) Units that produce, operate, store, or transport dangerous goods, and units engaged in mining, metal smelting, urban rail transit operation, and construction;  
(3) Emergency rescue teams.  
Units that produce, operate, store, or transport dangerous goods such as flammable and explosive substances and hazardous chemicals that are larger in scale and higher in risk shall set up emergency response technical groups and implement 24-hour emergency duty.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生产经营单位应当对从业人员进行应急教育和培训，保证从业人员具备必要的应急知识，掌握风险防范技能和事故应急措施。

**Article 15** Workplace units shall provide emergency education and training to their employees, ensuring that employees possess the necessary emergency knowledge and master risk prevention skills and accident emergency measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门应当按照国家有关规定建立生产安全事故应急救援信息系统，并采取有效措施，实现数据互联互通、信息共享。  
生产经营单位可以通过生产安全事故应急救援信息系统办理生产安全事故应急救援预案备案手续，报送应急救援预案演练情况和应急救援队伍建设情况；但依法需要保密的除外。

**Article 16** The department of the State Council responsible for work safety supervision and administration shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, establish a production safety accident emergency rescue information system, and shall take effective measures to achieve data interconnection and information sharing.  
Workplace units may, through the production safety accident emergency rescue information system, go through the procedures for filing the emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents, and submit the drill status of the emergency rescue plan and the establishment status of emergency rescue teams; except for those that need to be kept confidential according to law.

### 第三章　应急救援

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　发生生产安全事故后，生产经营单位应当立即启动生产安全事故应急救援预案，采取下列一项或者多项应急救援措施，并按照国家有关规定报告事故情况：  
(一)迅速控制危险源，组织抢救遇险人员；  
(二)根据事故危害程度，组织现场人员撤离或者采取可能的应急措施后撤离；  
(三)及时通知可能受到事故影响的单位和人员；  
(四)采取必要措施，防止事故危害扩大和次生、衍生灾害发生；  
(五)根据需要请求邻近的应急救援队伍参加救援，并向参加救援的应急救援队伍提供相关技术资料、信息和处置方法；  
(六)维护事故现场秩序，保护事故现场和相关证据；  
(七)法律、法规规定的其他应急救援措施。

**Article 17** After the occurrence of a production safety accident, the workplace unit shall immediately initiate the emergency rescue plan for the production safety accident, take one or more of the following emergency rescue measures, and report the accident situation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) Quickly control the source of danger and organize rescue of persons in distress;  
(2) Based on the degree of hazard of the accident, organize the evacuation of persons at the scene or take possible emergency measures before evacuation;  
(3) Timely notify units and persons that may be affected by the accident;  
(4) Take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of accident hazards and the occurrence of secondary and derivative disasters;  
(5) Request the assistance of neighboring emergency rescue teams as needed, and provide relevant technical data, information, and disposal methods to the emergency rescue teams participating in the rescue;  
(6) Maintain order at the accident scene, protect the accident scene and related evidence;  
(7) Other emergency rescue measures provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有关地方人民政府及其部门接到生产安全事故报告后，应当按照国家有关规定上报事故情况，启动相应的生产安全事故应急救援预案，并按照应急救援预案的规定采取下列一项或者多项应急救援措施：  
(一)组织抢救遇险人员，救治受伤人员，研判事故发展趋势以及可能造成的危害；  
(二)通知可能受到事故影响的单位和人员，隔离事故现场，划定警戒区域，疏散受到威胁的人员，实施交通管制；  
(三)采取必要措施，防止事故危害扩大和次生、衍生灾害发生，避免或者减少事故对环境造成的危害；  
(四)依法发布调用和征用应急资源的决定；  
(五)依法向应急救援队伍下达救援命令；  
(六)维护事故现场秩序，组织安抚遇险人员和遇险遇难人员亲属；  
(七)依法发布有关事故情况和应急救援工作的信息；  
(八)法律、法规规定的其他应急救援措施。  
有关地方人民政府不能有效控制生产安全事故的，应当及时向上级人民政府报告。上级人民政府应当及时采取措施，统一指挥应急救援。

**Article 18** After receiving a report of a production safety accident, the relevant local people's government and its departments shall report the accident situation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, initiate the corresponding emergency rescue plan for the production safety accident, and take one or more of the following emergency rescue measures in accordance with the provisions of the emergency rescue plan:  
(1) Organize rescue of persons in distress, treat injured persons, assess the development trend of the accident and the hazards that may be caused;  
(2) Notify units and persons that may be affected by the accident, isolate the accident scene, delineate warning areas, evacuate threatened persons, and implement traffic control;  
(3) Take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of accident hazards and the occurrence of secondary and derivative disasters, and to avoid or reduce environmental harm caused by the accident;  
(4) According to law, issue decisions on the dispatch and requisition of emergency resources;  
(5) According to law, issue rescue orders to emergency rescue teams;  
(6) Maintain order at the accident scene, organize to comfort the persons in distress and the relatives of the distressed and deceased persons;  
(7) According to law, release information on the accident situation and emergency rescue work;  
(8) Other emergency rescue measures provided by laws and regulations.  
If the relevant local people's government cannot effectively control the production safety accident, it shall promptly report to the people's government at a higher level. The people's government at a higher level shall promptly take measures and coordinate the emergency rescue.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　应急救援队伍接到有关人民政府及其部门的救援命令或者签有应急救援协议的生产经营单位的救援请求后，应当立即参加生产安全事故应急救援。  
应急救援队伍根据救援命令参加生产安全事故应急救援所耗费用，由事故责任单位承担；事故责任单位无力承担的，由有关人民政府协调解决。

**Article 19** After receiving a rescue order from the relevant people's government or its departments, or a rescue request from a workplace unit that has signed an emergency rescue agreement, the emergency rescue team shall immediately participate in the emergency rescue of the production safety accident.  
The costs incurred by the emergency rescue team for participating in the emergency rescue of the production safety accident in accordance with the rescue order shall be borne by the unit responsible for the accident; if the unit responsible for the accident is unable to bear such costs, the relevant people's government shall coordinate and resolve the matter.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　发生生产安全事故后，有关人民政府认为有必要的，可以设立由本级人民政府及其有关部门负责人、应急救援专家、应急救援队伍负责人、事故发生单位负责人等人员组成的应急救援现场指挥部，并指定现场指挥部总指挥。

**Article 20** After the occurrence of a production safety accident, if the relevant people's government deems it necessary, it may establish an on-site emergency rescue command composed of the head of the people's government at the same level and its relevant departments, emergency rescue experts, the head of the emergency rescue team, the head of the accident unit, and other persons, and shall designate a commander-in-chief for the on-site command.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　现场指挥部实行总指挥负责制，按照本级人民政府的授权组织制定并实施生产安全事故现场应急救援方案，协调、指挥有关单位和个人参加现场应急救援。  
参加生产安全事故现场应急救援的单位和个人应当服从现场指挥部的统一指挥。

**Article 21** The on-site command post shall adopt a system of commander responsibility, organize the formulation and implementation of on-site emergency rescue plans for work safety accidents in accordance with the authorization of the people's government at the same level, and coordinate and direct relevant entities and individuals to participate in the on-site emergency rescue.  
Entities and individuals participating in the on-site emergency rescue of a work safety accident shall comply with the unified command of the on-site command post.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在生产安全事故应急救援过程中，发现可能直接危及应急救援人员生命安全的紧急情况时，现场指挥部或者统一指挥应急救援的人民政府应当立即采取相应措施消除隐患，降低或者化解风险，必要时可以暂时撤离应急救援人员。

**Article 22** During the emergency rescue process of a work safety accident, if an emergency situation that may directly endanger the life safety of emergency rescue personnel is discovered, the on-site command post or the people's government that uniformly commands the emergency rescue shall immediately take corresponding measures to eliminate hidden dangers, reduce or resolve risks, and may temporarily evacuate the emergency rescue personnel when necessary.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　生产安全事故发生地人民政府应当为应急救援人员提供必需的后勤保障，并组织通信、交通运输、医疗卫生、气象、水文、地质、电力、供水等单位协助应急救援。

**Article 23** The people's government of the place where a work safety accident occurred shall provide necessary logistical support for emergency rescue personnel, and organize communications, transportation, medical and health, meteorology, hydrology, geology, electric power, water supply and other entities to assist in the emergency rescue.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　现场指挥部或者统一指挥生产安全事故应急救援的人民政府及其有关部门应当完整、准确地记录应急救援的重要事项，妥善保存相关原始资料和证据。

**Article 24** The on-site command post or the people's government that uniformly commands the emergency rescue of a work safety accident and its relevant departments shall completely and accurately record the important matters of the emergency rescue, and properly preserve the relevant original materials and evidence.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　生产安全事故的威胁和危害得到控制或者消除后，有关人民政府应当决定停止执行依照本条例和有关法律、法规采取的全部或者部分应急救援措施。

**Article 25** After the threat and harm of a work safety accident are controlled or eliminated, the relevant people's government shall decide to stop implementing all or part of the emergency rescue measures taken in accordance with these Regulations and relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　有关人民政府及其部门根据生产安全事故应急救援需要依法调用和征用的财产，在使用完毕或者应急救援结束后，应当及时归还。财产被调用、征用或者调用、征用后毁损、灭失的，有关人民政府及其部门应当按照国家有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 26** Property lawfully transferred and requisitioned by the relevant people's government and its departments based on the needs of emergency rescue of a work safety accident shall be promptly returned after use or after the emergency rescue ends. If the property is damaged or lost after being transferred or requisitioned, the relevant people's government and its departments shall provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　按照国家有关规定成立的生产安全事故调查组应当对应急救援工作进行评估，并在事故调查报告中作出评估结论。

**Article 27** The investigation team for a work safety accident established in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State shall evaluate the emergency rescue work and make an evaluation conclusion in the accident investigation report.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，对在生产安全事故应急救援中伤亡的人员及时给予救治和抚恤；符合烈士评定条件的，按照国家有关规定评定为烈士。

**Article 28** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, promptly provide medical treatment and pension to personnel who are injured or killed in the emergency rescue of a work safety accident; if they meet the conditions for being assessed as a martyr, they shall be assessed as a martyr in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　地方各级人民政府和街道办事处等地方人民政府派出机关以及县级以上人民政府有关部门违反本条例规定的，由其上级行政机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 29** If local people's governments at all levels, sub-district offices and other agencies dispatched by local people's governments, and relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level violate the provisions of these Regulations, their superior administrative organs shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　生产经营单位未制定生产安全事故应急救援预案、未定期组织应急救援预案演练、未对从业人员进行应急教育和培训，生产经营单位的主要负责人在本单位发生生产安全事故时不立即组织抢救的，由县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依照《中华人民共和国安全生产法》有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 30** If a production and business operation entity fails to formulate an emergency rescue plan for work safety accidents, fails to regularly organize emergency rescue plan drills, fails to provide emergency education and training to employees, or if the principal responsible person of the production and business operation entity fails to immediately organize rescue when a work safety accident occurs in the entity, the department responsible for the supervision and administration of work safety of the people's government at or above the county level shall investigate legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　生产经营单位未对应急救援器材、设备和物资进行经常性维护、保养，导致发生严重生产安全事故或者生产安全事故危害扩大，或者在本单位发生生产安全事故后未立即采取相应的应急救援措施，造成严重后果的，由县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依照《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 31** If a production and business operation entity fails to conduct regular maintenance and upkeep of emergency rescue equipment, devices, and materials, resulting in a serious production safety accident or expansion of the hazards of a production safety accident, or fails to immediately take corresponding emergency rescue measures after a production safety accident occurs in the entity, causing serious consequences, the department with responsibility for production safety supervision and administration under the people's government at or above the county level shall investigate legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Emergency Response Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　生产经营单位未将生产安全事故应急救援预案报送备案、未建立应急值班制度或者配备应急值班人员的，由县级以上人民政府负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处3万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 32** If a production and business operation entity fails to submit the emergency rescue plan for production safety accidents for recordation, fails to establish an emergency duty system, or fails to assign emergency duty personnel, the department with responsibility for production safety supervision and administration under the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a specified period; if rectification is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed, and a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 20,000 shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a public security violation, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　储存、使用易燃易爆物品、危险化学品等危险物品的科研机构、学校、医院等单位的安全事故应急工作，参照本条例有关规定执行。

**Article 34** The emergency work for safety accidents in scientific research institutions, schools, hospitals, and other entities that store or use flammable and explosive substances, dangerous chemicals, and other hazardous articles shall be carried out by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2019年4月1日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 2019.
