# 破坏性地震应急条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1995-04-01

> (1995年2月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第172号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对破坏性地震应急活动的管理，减轻地震灾害损失，保障国家财产和公民人身、财产安全，维护社会秩序，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事破坏性地震应急活动，必须遵守本条例。 第三条　地震应急工作实行政府领导、统一管理和分级、分部门负责的原则。 第四条　各级人民政府应当加强地震应急的宣传、教育工作，提高社会防震减灾意识。 第五条　任何组织和个人都有参加地震应急活动的义务。 中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队是地震应急工作的重要力量。 第二章　应急机构 第六条　国务院防震减灾工作主管部门指导和监督全国地震应急工作。国务院有关部门按照各自的职责，具体负责本部门的地震应急工作。 第七条　造成特大损失的严重破坏性地震发生后，国务院设立抗震救灾指挥部，国务院防震减灾工作主管部门为其办事机构；国务院有关部门设立本部门的地震应急机构。 第八条　县级以上地方人民政府防震减灾工作主管部门指导和监督本行政区域内的地震应急工作。 破坏性地震发生后，有关县级以上地方人民政府应当设立抗震救灾指挥部，对本行政区域内的地震应急工作实行集中领导，其办事机构设在本级人民政府防震减灾工作主管部门或者本级人民政府指定的其他部门；国务院另有规定的，从其规定。 第三章　应急预案 第九条　国家的破坏性地震应急预案，由国务院防震减灾工作主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对破坏性地震应急活动的管理，减轻地震灾害损失，保障国家财产和公民人身、财产安全，维护社会秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of emergency activities for destructive earthquakes, mitigating earthquake disaster losses, protecting State property and the safety of person and property of citizens, and maintaining social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事破坏性地震应急活动，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in emergency activities for destructive earthquakes within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地震应急工作实行政府领导、统一管理和分级、分部门负责的原则。

**Article 3** The principle of government leadership, unified management, and graded and departmental responsibility shall be implemented in earthquake emergency work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当加强地震应急的宣传、教育工作，提高社会防震减灾意识。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen publicity and education on earthquake emergency, and enhance the public awareness of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何组织和个人都有参加地震应急活动的义务。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队是地震应急工作的重要力量。

**Article 5** Any organization or individual has the obligation to participate in earthquake emergency activities.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force are important forces in earthquake emergency work.

### 第二章　应急机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院防震减灾工作主管部门指导和监督全国地震应急工作。国务院有关部门按照各自的职责，具体负责本部门的地震应急工作。

**Article 6** The competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the State Council shall guide and supervise the national earthquake emergency work. The relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be specifically responsible for the earthquake emergency work of their own departments.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　造成特大损失的严重破坏性地震发生后，国务院设立抗震救灾指挥部，国务院防震减灾工作主管部门为其办事机构；国务院有关部门设立本部门的地震应急机构。

**Article 7** After the occurrence of a severe destructive earthquake causing extraordinary losses, the State Council shall establish an earthquake resistance and disaster relief headquarters, and the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the State Council shall serve as its office; the relevant departments under the State Council shall establish their own earthquake emergency agencies.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府防震减灾工作主管部门指导和监督本行政区域内的地震应急工作。  
破坏性地震发生后，有关县级以上地方人民政府应当设立抗震救灾指挥部，对本行政区域内的地震应急工作实行集中领导，其办事机构设在本级人民政府防震减灾工作主管部门或者本级人民政府指定的其他部门；国务院另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 8** The competent departments of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide and supervise the earthquake emergency work within their respective administrative regions.  
After the occurrence of a destructive earthquake, the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish earthquake resistance and disaster relief headquarters to exercise centralized leadership over the earthquake emergency work within their respective administrative regions; the office thereof shall be established in the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the people's government at the same level or in another department designated by the people's government at the same level; if the State Council provides otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

### 第三章　应急预案

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家的破坏性地震应急预案，由国务院防震减灾工作主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定，报国务院批准。

**Article 9** The national contingency plan for destructive earthquakes shall be formulated by the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院有关部门应当根据国家的破坏性地震应急预案，制定本部门的破坏性地震应急预案，并报国务院防震减灾工作主管部门备案。

**Article 10** The relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the national contingency plan for destructive earthquakes, formulate their own departmental contingency plans for destructive earthquakes, and shall submit them to the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the State Council for archival filing.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　根据地震灾害预测，可能发生破坏性地震地区的县级以上地方人民政府防震减灾工作主管部门应当会同同级有关部门以及有关单位，参照国家的破坏性地震应急预案，制定本行政区域内的破坏性地震应急预案，报本级人民政府批准；省、自治区和人口在100万以上的城市的破坏性地震应急预案，还应当报国务院防震减灾工作主管部门备案。

**Article 11** The competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction of the local people's government at or above the county level in areas where destructive earthquakes may occur according to earthquake disaster prediction shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level and the relevant units, and with reference to the state's emergency plan for destructive earthquakes, formulate emergency plans for destructive earthquakes within their respective administrative regions, and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval; the emergency plans for destructive earthquakes of provinces, autonomous regions, and cities with a population of over one million shall also be reported to the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction under the State Council for record.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　部门和地方制定破坏性地震应急预案，应当从本部门或者本地区的实际情况出发，做到切实可行。

**Article 12** When formulating emergency plans for destructive earthquakes, departments and localities shall proceed from the actual conditions of their respective departments or localities and ensure that the plans are practical and feasible.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　破坏性地震应急预案应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)应急机构的组成和职责；  
(二)应急通信保障；  
(三)抢险救援的人员、资金、物资准备；  
(四)灾害评估准备；  
(五)应急行动方案。

**Article 13** Emergency plans for destructive earthquakes shall include the following main contents:  
(1) Composition and responsibilities of emergency response agencies;  
(2) Emergency communication support;  
(3) Preparation of personnel, funds, and materials for rescue and relief;  
(4) Preparation for disaster assessment;  
(5) Emergency action plans.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　制定破坏性地震应急预案的部门和地方，应当根据震情的变化以及实施中发现的问题，及时对其制定的破坏性地震应急预案进行修订、补充；涉及重大事项调整的，应当报经原批准机关同意。

**Article 14** Departments and localities that have formulated emergency plans for destructive earthquakes shall, in light of changes in seismic conditions and problems discovered during implementation, promptly revise and supplement their emergency plans for destructive earthquakes; where adjustments involving major issues are made, the consent of the original approving authority shall be obtained.

### 第四章　临震应急

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地震临震预报，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依照国务院有关发布地震预报的规定统一发布，其他任何组织或者个人不得发布地震预报。  
任何组织或者个人都不得传播有关地震的谣言。发生地震谣传时，防震减灾工作主管部门应当协助人民政府迅速予以平息和澄清。

**Article 15** The impending earthquake prediction shall be uniformly released by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the release of earthquake predictions. No other organization or individual shall release earthquake predictions.  
No organization or individual shall spread rumors about earthquakes. When earthquake rumors occur, the competent department of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction shall assist the people's government in rapidly quashing and clarifying them.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　破坏性地震临震预报发布后，有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以宣布预报区进入临震应急期，并指明临震应急期的起止时间。  
临震应急期一般为10日；必要时，可以延长10日。

**Article 16** After the release of an impending destructive earthquake prediction, the relevant people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may declare the forecast area to be in an impending earthquake emergency period and specify the start and end times of such period.  
The impending earthquake emergency period shall generally be 10 days; if necessary, it may be extended by 10 days.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在临震应急期，有关地方人民政府应当根据震情，统一部署破坏性地震应急预案的实施工作，并对临震应急活动中发生的争议采取紧急处理措施。

**Article 17** During the impending earthquake emergency period, the relevant local people's government shall, in light of the seismic conditions, uniformly deploy the implementation of the emergency plan for destructive earthquakes, and take emergency measures to deal with disputes arising from impending earthquake emergency activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在临震应急期，各级防震减灾工作主管部门应当协助本级人民政府对实施破坏性地震应急预案工作进行检查。

**Article 18** During the impending earthquake emergency period, the competent departments of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction at all levels shall assist the people's government at the same level in inspecting the implementation of the emergency plan for destructive earthquakes.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在临震应急期，有关地方人民政府应当根据实际情况，向预报区的居民以及其他人员提出避震撤离的劝告；情况紧急时，应当有组织地进行避震疏散。

**Article 19** During the impending earthquake emergency period, the relevant local people's government shall, according to the actual circumstances, advise residents and other persons in the forecast area to evacuate to avoid earthquakes; in case of emergency, it shall organize orderly evacuation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在临震应急期，有关地方人民政府有权在本行政区域内紧急调用物资、设备、人员和占用场地，任何组织或者个人都不得阻拦；调用物资、设备或者占用场地的，事后应当及时归还或者给予补偿。

**Article 20** During the impending earthquake emergency period, the relevant local people's government shall have the right to urgently requisition materials, equipment, and personnel, and to occupy sites within its administrative region; no organization or individual may obstruct such actions. Where materials, equipment, or sites are requisitioned or occupied, they shall be promptly returned or compensation provided thereafter.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在临震应急期，有关部门应当对生命线工程和次生灾害源采取紧急防护措施。

**Article 21** During the emergency period before an imminent earthquake, the relevant departments shall take emergency protective measures for lifeline projects and secondary disaster sources.

### 第五章　震后应急

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　破坏性地震发生后，有关的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当宣布灾区进入震后应急期，并指明震后应急期的起止时间。  
震后应急期一般为10日；必要时，可以延长20日。

**Article 22** After a destructive earthquake occurs, the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall declare that the disaster area has entered the post-earthquake emergency period, and shall specify the start and end times of the post-earthquake emergency period.  
The post-earthquake emergency period shall generally be 10 days; when necessary, it may be extended by 20 days.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　破坏性地震发生后，抗震救灾指挥部应当及时组织实施破坏性地震应急预案，及时将震情、灾情及其发展趋势等信息报告上一级人民政府。

**Article 23** After a destructive earthquake occurs, the earthquake relief headquarters shall promptly organize the implementation of the destructive earthquake emergency plan, and shall promptly report information such as the earthquake situation, disaster situation, and their development trends to the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　防震减灾工作主管部门应当加强现场地震监测预报工作，并及时会同有关部门评估地震灾害损失；灾情调查结果，应当及时报告本级人民政府抗震救灾指挥部和上一级防震减灾工作主管部门。

**Article 24** The competent department in charge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction shall strengthen on-site earthquake monitoring and prediction work, and shall promptly assess earthquake disaster losses jointly with relevant departments; the results of disaster investigation shall be promptly reported to the earthquake relief headquarters of the people's government at the same level and to the competent department in charge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction at the next higher level.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　交通、铁路、民航等部门应当尽快恢复被损毁的道路、铁路、水港、空港和有关设施，并优先保证抢险救援人员、物资的运输和灾民的疏散。其他部门有交通运输工具的，应当无条件服从抗震救灾指挥部的征用或者调用。

**Article 25** The departments of transportation, railway, civil aviation, etc. shall restore damaged roads, railways, water ports, airports, and related facilities as soon as possible, and shall give priority to ensuring the transportation of emergency rescue personnel and supplies and the evacuation of disaster victims. Other departments that have transportation vehicles shall unconditionally submit to the requisition or deployment by the earthquake relief headquarters.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　通信部门应当尽快恢复被破坏的通信设施，保证抗震救灾通信畅通。其他部门有通信设施的，应当优先为破坏性地震应急工作服务。

**Article 26** The communications department shall restore damaged communication facilities as soon as possible, and shall ensure smooth communication for earthquake relief. Other departments that have communication facilities shall give priority to serving the emergency work for destructive earthquakes.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　供水、供电部门应当尽快恢复被破坏的供水、供电设施，保证灾区用水、用电。

**Article 27** The water supply and power supply departments shall restore damaged water supply and power supply facilities as soon as possible, and shall ensure the use of water and electricity in the disaster area.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　卫生部门应当立即组织急救队伍，利用各种医疗设施或者建立临时治疗点，抢救伤员，及时检查、监测灾区的饮用水源、食品等，采取有效措施防止和控制传染病的暴发流行，并向受灾人员提供精神、心理卫生方面的帮助。医药部门应当及时提供救灾所需药品。其他部门应当配合卫生、医药部门，做好卫生防疫以及伤亡人员的抢救、处理工作。

**Article 28** The health department shall immediately organize emergency medical teams, use various medical facilities or establish temporary treatment points to rescue the injured, promptly inspect and monitor drinking water sources and food in the disaster area, take effective measures to prevent and control the outbreak and spread of infectious diseases, and provide mental and psychological health assistance to the affected persons. The pharmaceutical department shall promptly provide medicines needed for disaster relief. Other departments shall cooperate with the health and pharmaceutical departments to carry out health and epidemic prevention as well as the rescue and disposal of casualties.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民政部门应当迅速设置避难场所和救济物资供应点，提供救济物品等，保障灾民的基本生活，做好灾民的转移和安置工作。其他部门应当支持、配合民政部门妥善安置灾民。

**Article 29** The civil affairs department shall promptly set up shelters and relief supply points, provide relief items, etc., ensure the basic living of disaster victims, and carry out the transfer and resettlement of disaster victims. Other departments shall support and cooperate with the civil affairs department in properly resettling disaster victims.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公安部门应当加强灾区的治安管理和安全保卫工作，预防和制止各种破坏活动，维护社会治安，保证抢险救灾工作顺利进行，尽快恢复社会秩序。

**Article 30** The public security department shall strengthen public security management and security protection work in the disaster area, prevent and stop various destructive activities, maintain social order, ensure the smooth progress of emergency rescue and disaster relief work, and restore social order as soon as possible.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　石油、化工、水利、电力、建设等部门和单位以及危险品生产、储运等单位，应当按照各自的职责，对可能发生或者已经发生次生灾害的地点和设施采取紧急处置措施，并加强监视、控制，防止灾害扩展。  
公安消防机构应当严密监视灾区火灾的发生；出现火灾时，应当组织力量抢救人员和物资，并采取有效防范措施，防止火势扩大、蔓延。

**Article 31** Departments and units of petroleum, chemical industry, water conservancy, electric power, construction, and other sectors, as well as units engaged in the production, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods, shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, take emergency response measures at locations and facilities where secondary disasters may occur or have occurred, and shall strengthen monitoring and control to prevent the spread of disasters.  
Public security fire control agencies shall closely monitor the occurrence of fires in disaster areas; when a fire occurs, they shall organize forces to rescue persons and property, and shall take effective preventive measures to prevent the fire from expanding and spreading.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　广播电台、电视台等新闻单位应当根据抗震救灾指挥部提供的情况，按照规定及时向公众发布震情、灾情等有关信息，并做好宣传、报道工作。

**Article 32** News units such as radio stations and television stations shall, based on the information provided by the earthquake relief headquarters, promptly release to the public relevant information on earthquake conditions and disaster situations in accordance with regulations, and shall carry out publicity and reporting work well.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　抗震救灾指挥部可以请求非灾区的人民政府接受并妥善安置灾民和提供其他救援。

**Article 33** The earthquake relief headquarters may request the people's governments of non-disaster areas to accept and properly resettle disaster victims and to provide other relief.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　破坏性地震发生后，国内非灾区提供的紧急救援，由抗震救灾指挥部负责接受和安排；国际社会提供的紧急救援，由国务院民政部门负责接受和安排；国外红十字会和国际社会通过中国红十字会提供的紧急救援，由中国红十字会负责接受和安排。

**Article 34** After a destructive earthquake occurs, emergency relief provided by non-disaster areas within the country shall be accepted and arranged by the earthquake relief headquarters; emergency relief provided by the international community shall be accepted and arranged by the civil affairs department of the State Council; emergency relief provided by foreign Red Cross societies and the international community through the Red Cross Society of China shall be accepted and arranged by the Red Cross Society of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　因严重破坏性地震应急的需要，可以在灾区实行特别管制措施。省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的特别管制措施，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定；跨省、自治区、直辖市的特别管制措施，由有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府共同决定或者由国务院决定；中断干线交通或者封锁国境的特别管制措施，由国务院决定。  
特别管制措施的解除，由原决定机关宣布。

**Article 35** In cases where emergency response to a severely destructive earthquake necessitates, special control measures may be implemented in the disaster area. Special control measures within the administrative region of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be decided by the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality; special control measures involving multiple provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be jointly decided by the relevant people's governments or decided by the State Council; special control measures that interrupt mainline traffic or seal the national border shall be decided by the State Council.  
The lifting of special control measures shall be announced by the authority that originally decided them.

### 第六章　奖励和处罚

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在破坏性地震应急活动中有下列事迹之一的，由其所在单位、上级机关或者防震减灾工作主管部门给予表彰或者奖励：  
(一)出色完成破坏性地震应急任务的；  
(二)保护国家、集体和公民的财产或者抢救人员有功的；  
(三)及时排除险情，防止灾害扩大，成绩显著的；  
(四)对地震应急工作提出重大建议，实施效果显著的；  
(五)因震情、灾情测报准确和信息传递及时而减轻灾害损失的；  
(六)及时供应用于应急救灾的物资和工具或者节约经费开支，成绩显著的；  
(七)有其他特殊贡献的。

**Article 36** Any person who has performed one of the following deeds during destructive earthquake emergency response activities shall be commended or rewarded by the unit he belongs to, the superior organ, or the department in charge of earthquake prevention and disaster reduction:  
(1) Having completed the destructive earthquake emergency response tasks outstandingly;  
(2) Having performed meritorious services in protecting the property of the State, the collective, and citizens, or in rescuing persons;  
(3) Having eliminated risks in a timely manner, preventing the expansion of disasters, with remarkable results;  
(4) Having put forward major suggestions for earthquake emergency response work with significant implementation effects;  
(5) Having mitigated disaster losses due to accurate prediction and reporting of earthquake and disaster conditions and timely information transmission;  
(6) Having supplied materials and tools for emergency disaster relief in a timely manner or having saved financial expenditures, with remarkable results;  
(7) Having made other special contributions.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有下列行为之一的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；属于违反治安管理行为的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不按照本条例规定制定破坏性地震应急预案的；  
(二)不按照破坏性地震应急预案的规定和抗震救灾指挥部的要求实施破坏性地震应急预案的；  
(三)违抗抗震救灾指挥部命令，拒不承担地震应急任务的；  
(四)阻挠抗震救灾指挥部紧急调用物资、人员或者占用场地的；  
(五)贪污、挪用、盗窃地震应急工作经费或者物资的；  
(六)有特定责任的国家工作人员在临震应急期或者震后应急期不坚守岗位，不及时掌握震情、灾情，临阵脱逃或者玩忽职守的；  
(七)在临震应急期或者震后应急期哄抢国家、集体或者公民的财产的；  
(八)阻碍抗震救灾人员执行职务或者进行破坏活动的；  
(九)不按照规定和实际情况报告灾情的；  
(十)散布谣言，扰乱社会秩序，影响破坏性地震应急工作的；  
(十一)有对破坏性地震应急工作造成危害的其他行为的。

**Article 37** Where any of the following acts is committed, the persons in charge with direct liability and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Punishments; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to formulate a destructive earthquake emergency response plan in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Failing to implement the destructive earthquake emergency response plan in accordance with the provisions of the plan and the requirements of the earthquake relief headquarters;  
(3) Disobeying the orders of the earthquake relief headquarters and refusing to undertake earthquake emergency response tasks;  
(4) Obstructing the earthquake relief headquarters from urgently transferring materials, personnel, or occupying premises;  
(5) Embezzling, misappropriating, or stealing funds or materials for earthquake emergency response work;  
(6) State functionaries with specific responsibilities failing to remain at their posts, failing to keep abreast of earthquake and disaster conditions in a timely manner, deserting their posts, or neglecting their duties during the imminent earthquake emergency period or the post-earthquake emergency period;  
(7) Looting State, collective, or citizens' property during the imminent earthquake emergency period or the post-earthquake emergency period;  
(8) Obstructing earthquake relief personnel from performing their duties or engaging in sabotage activities;  
(9) Failing to report disaster situations in accordance with regulations and actual conditions;  
(10) Spreading rumors, disrupting social order, or affecting destructive earthquake emergency response work;  
(11) Engaging in other acts that cause harm to destructive earthquake emergency response work.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)“地震应急”，是指为了减轻地震灾害而采取的不同于正常工作程序的紧急防灾和抢险行动；  
(二)“破坏性地震”，是指造成一定数量的人员伤亡和经济损失的地震事件；  
(三)“严重破坏性地震”，是指造成严重的人员伤亡和经济损失，使灾区丧失或者部分丧失自我恢复能力，需要国家采取对抗行动的地震事件；  
(四)“生命线工程”，是指对社会生活、生产有重大影响的交通、通信、供水、排水、供电、供气、输油等工程系统；  
(五)“次生灾害源”，是指因地震而可能引发水灾、火灾、爆炸等灾害的易燃易爆物品、有毒物质贮存设施、水坝、堤岸等。

**Article 38** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Earthquake emergency response" refers to emergency disaster prevention and rescue actions taken to mitigate earthquake disasters, which differ from normal working procedures;  
(2) "Destructive earthquake" refers to an earthquake event that causes a certain number of casualties and economic losses;  
(3) "Severely destructive earthquake" refers to an earthquake event that causes severe casualties and economic losses, rendering the disaster area wholly or partially incapable of self-recovery, requiring the State to take countermeasures;  
(4) "Lifeline engineering" refers to engineering systems such as transportation, communications, water supply, drainage, power supply, gas supply, and oil pipelines that have a significant impact on social life and production;  
(5) "Sources of secondary disasters" refers to flammable and explosive materials, toxic substance storage facilities, dams, embankments, etc., that may trigger disasters such as floods, fires, and explosions due to earthquakes.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自1995年4月1日起施行。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 1995.
