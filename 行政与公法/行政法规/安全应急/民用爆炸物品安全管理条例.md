# 民用爆炸物品安全管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29  
**生效日期**：2006-09-01

> (2006年5月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第466号公布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对民用爆炸物品的安全管理，预防爆炸事故发生，保障公民生命、财产安全和公共安全，制定本条例。 第二条　民用爆炸物品的生产、销售、购买、进出口、运输、爆破作业和储存以及硝酸铵的销售、购买，适用本条例。 本条例所称民用爆炸物品，是指用于非军事目的、列入民用爆炸物品品名表的各类火药、炸药及其制品和雷管、导火索等点火、起爆器材。 民用爆炸物品品名表，由国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门会同国务院公安部门制订、公布。 第三条　国家对民用爆炸物品的生产、销售、购买、运输和爆破作业实行许可证制度。 未经许可，任何单位或者个人不得生产、销售、购买、运输民用爆炸物品，不得从事爆破作业。 严禁转让、出借、转借、抵押、赠送、私藏或者非法持有民用爆炸物品。 第四条　民用爆炸物品行业主管部门负责民用爆炸物品生产、销售的安全监督管理。 公安机关负责民用爆炸物品公共安全管理和民用爆炸物品购买、运输、爆破作业的安全监督管理，监控民用爆炸物品流向。 安全生产监督、铁路、交通、民用航空主管部门依照法律、行政法规的规定，负责做好民用爆炸物品的有关安全监督管理工作。 民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门按照职责分工，负责组织查处非法生产、销售、购买、储存、运输、邮寄、使用民用爆炸物品的行为。 第五条　民用爆炸物品生产、销售、购买、运输和爆破作业单位(以下称民用爆炸物品从业单位)的主要负责人是本单位民用爆炸物品安全管理责任人，对本单位的民用爆炸物品安全管理工作全面负责。 民用爆炸物品从业单位是治安保卫工作的重点单位，应当依法设置治安保卫机构或者配备治安保卫人员，设置技术防范设施，防止民用爆炸物品丢失、被盗、被抢。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对民用爆炸物品的安全管理，预防爆炸事故发生，保障公民生命、财产安全和公共安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the safety management of civil explosives, preventing explosion accidents, and safeguarding the life, property safety of citizens and public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　民用爆炸物品的生产、销售、购买、进出口、运输、爆破作业和储存以及硝酸铵的销售、购买，适用本条例。  
本条例所称民用爆炸物品，是指用于非军事目的、列入民用爆炸物品品名表的各类火药、炸药及其制品和雷管、导火索等点火、起爆器材。  
民用爆炸物品品名表，由国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门会同国务院公安部门制订、公布。

**Article 2** The production, sale, purchase, import, export, transport, blasting operations and storage of civil explosives, as well as the sale and purchase of ammonium nitrate, shall be governed by these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "civil explosives" means various types of gunpowder, explosives and their products, and detonators, fuses and other ignition and initiation devices that are listed in the Inventory of Civil Explosives and used for non-military purposes.  
The Inventory of Civil Explosives shall be formulated and published by the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council jointly with the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对民用爆炸物品的生产、销售、购买、运输和爆破作业实行许可证制度。  
未经许可，任何单位或者个人不得生产、销售、购买、运输民用爆炸物品，不得从事爆破作业。  
严禁转让、出借、转借、抵押、赠送、私藏或者非法持有民用爆炸物品。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a licensing system for the production, sale, purchase, transport and blasting operations of civil explosives.  
Without a license, no unit or individual may produce, sell, purchase or transport civil explosives, nor may it or he engage in blasting operations.  
It is strictly prohibited to transfer, lend, sub-lend, mortgage, give away, privately keep or illegally possess civil explosives.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民用爆炸物品行业主管部门负责民用爆炸物品生产、销售的安全监督管理。  
公安机关负责民用爆炸物品公共安全管理和民用爆炸物品购买、运输、爆破作业的安全监督管理，监控民用爆炸物品流向。  
安全生产监督、铁路、交通、民用航空主管部门依照法律、行政法规的规定，负责做好民用爆炸物品的有关安全监督管理工作。  
民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门按照职责分工，负责组织查处非法生产、销售、购买、储存、运输、邮寄、使用民用爆炸物品的行为。

**Article 4** The competent department of the civil explosives industry shall be responsible for the safety supervision and administration of the production and sale of civil explosives.  
The public security organs shall be responsible for the public safety management of civil explosives and the safety supervision and administration of the purchase, transport and blasting operations of civil explosives, and shall monitor the flow of civil explosives.  
The departments of work safety supervision, railways, transport and civil aviation shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the relevant work of safety supervision and administration of civil explosives.  
The competent department of the civil explosives industry, the public security organs and the administrative departments for industry and commerce shall, according to their respective functions and duties, be responsible for organizing the investigation and punishment of acts of illegal production, sale, purchase, storage, transport, mailing and use of civil explosives.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民用爆炸物品生产、销售、购买、运输和爆破作业单位(以下称民用爆炸物品从业单位)的主要负责人是本单位民用爆炸物品安全管理责任人，对本单位的民用爆炸物品安全管理工作全面负责。  
民用爆炸物品从业单位是治安保卫工作的重点单位，应当依法设置治安保卫机构或者配备治安保卫人员，设置技术防范设施，防止民用爆炸物品丢失、被盗、被抢。  
民用爆炸物品从业单位应当建立安全管理制度、岗位安全责任制度，制订安全防范措施和事故应急预案，设置安全管理机构或者配备专职安全管理人员。

**Article 5** The principal person in charge of a unit that produces, sells, purchases, transports civil explosives or engages in blasting operations (hereinafter referred to as a civil explosives practitioner) shall be the person responsible for the safety management of civil explosives in that unit, and shall assume full responsibility for the safety management of civil explosives in that unit.  
A civil explosives practitioner shall be a key unit for public security protection work, and shall, according to law, set up public security protection institutions or assign public security protection personnel, and install technical prevention facilities to prevent the loss, theft or robbery of civil explosives.  
A civil explosives practitioner shall establish a safety management system and a post safety responsibility system, formulate safety prevention measures and emergency response plans, and set up safety management institutions or assign full-time safety management personnel.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人或者曾因犯罪受过刑事处罚的人，不得从事民用爆炸物品的生产、销售、购买、运输和爆破作业。  
民用爆炸物品从业单位应当加强对本单位从业人员的安全教育、法制教育和岗位技术培训，从业人员经考核合格的，方可上岗作业；对有资格要求的岗位，应当配备具有相应资格的人员。

**Article 6** No person who has no capacity for civil conduct, has limited capacity for civil conduct, or has been subjected to criminal punishment for a crime may engage in the production, sale, purchase, transport of civil explosives or blasting operations.  
A civil explosives practitioner shall strengthen safety education, legal education, and post technical training for its employees. Only employees who have passed the assessment may take up their posts; for posts that require qualifications, persons with corresponding qualifications shall be assigned.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家建立民用爆炸物品信息管理系统，对民用爆炸物品实行标识管理，监控民用爆炸物品流向。  
民用爆炸物品生产企业、销售企业和爆破作业单位应当建立民用爆炸物品登记制度，如实将本单位生产、销售、购买、运输、储存、使用民用爆炸物品的品种、数量和流向信息输入计算机系统。

**Article 7** The State shall establish an information management system for civil explosives, implement identification management for civil explosives, and monitor the flow of civil explosives.  
Civil explosives production enterprises, sales enterprises and blasting operation units shall establish a civil explosives registration system, and shall truthfully input into the computer system the information on the varieties, quantities and flow of the civil explosives produced, sold, purchased, transported, stored and used by their own units.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位或者个人都有权举报违反民用爆炸物品安全管理规定的行为；接到举报的主管部门、公安机关应当立即查处，并为举报人员保密，对举报有功人员给予奖励。

**Article 8** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act that violates the provisions on safety management of civil explosives. The competent department or the public security organ that receives the report shall immediately investigate and handle the matter, keep the reporter confidential, and reward the person who has rendered meritorious service in reporting.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励民用爆炸物品从业单位采用提高民用爆炸物品安全性能的新技术，鼓励发展民用爆炸物品生产、配送、爆破作业一体化的经营模式。

**Article 9** The State shall encourage civil explosives practitioners to adopt new technologies that improve the safety performance of civil explosives, and encourage the development of an integrated business model of civil explosives production, distribution and blasting operations.

### 第二章　生产

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　设立民用爆炸物品生产企业，应当遵循统筹规划、合理布局的原则。

**Article 10** The establishment of a civil explosives production enterprise shall follow the principles of overall planning and rational layout.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请从事民用爆炸物品生产的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合国家产业结构规划和产业技术标准；  
(二)厂房和专用仓库的设计、结构、建筑材料、安全距离以及防火、防爆、防雷、防静电等安全设备、设施符合国家有关标准和规范；  
(三)生产设备、工艺符合有关安全生产的技术标准和规程；  
(四)有具备相应资格的专业技术人员、安全生产管理人员和生产岗位人员；  
(五)有健全的安全管理制度、岗位安全责任制度；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 11** An enterprise applying to engage in the production of civil explosives shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Complying with the State industrial structure plan and industrial technical standards;  
(2) The design, structure, building materials, safety distance of the workshop and dedicated warehouse, as well as safety equipment and facilities such as fire prevention, explosion prevention, lightning protection and static electricity prevention, conforming to the relevant State standards and specifications;  
(3) The production equipment and processes conforming to the relevant technical standards and procedures for work safety;  
(4) Having professional and technical personnel, work safety management personnel and production post personnel with corresponding qualifications;  
(5) Having a sound safety management system and post safety responsibility system;  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请从事民用爆炸物品生产的企业，应当向国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门提交申请书、可行性研究报告以及能够证明其符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定条件的有关材料。国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起45日内进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》；对不符合条件的，不予核发《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》，书面向申请人说明理由。  
民用爆炸物品生产企业为调整生产能力及品种进行改建、扩建的，应当依照前款规定申请办理《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》。  
民用爆炸物品生产企业持《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》到工商行政管理部门办理工商登记，并在办理工商登记后3日内，向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 12** An enterprise applying to engage in the production of civil explosives shall submit to the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council a written application, a feasibility study report, and relevant materials that can prove that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 11 of these Regulations. The competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council shall, within 45 days from the date of accepting the application, conduct an examination, and shall issue the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives to those that meet the conditions; and shall not issue the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives to those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
Where a civil explosives production enterprise undergoes reconstruction or expansion for the purpose of adjusting production capacity and varieties, it shall apply for the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
A civil explosives production enterprise shall, with the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives, go through the industrial and commercial registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, and shall, within 3 days after the industrial and commercial registration, file a record with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　取得《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》的企业应当在基本建设完成后，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门申请安全生产许可。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门应当依照《安全生产许可证条例》的规定对其进行查验，对符合条件的，核发《民用爆炸物品安全生产许可证》。民用爆炸物品生产企业取得《民用爆炸物品安全生产许可证》后，方可生产民用爆炸物品。

**Article 13** An enterprise that has obtained the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives shall, after the completion of the capital construction, apply to the competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for a safety production permit. The competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Safety Production Permits, verify the enterprise, and shall issue the Safety Production Permit for Civil Explosives to those that meet the conditions. A civil explosives production enterprise may produce civil explosives only after obtaining the Safety Production Permit for Civil Explosives.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民用爆炸物品生产企业应当严格按照《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》核定的品种和产量进行生产，生产作业应当严格执行安全技术规程的规定。

**Article 14** A civil explosives production enterprise shall strictly produce the varieties and quantities approved in the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives, and the production operations shall strictly comply with the provisions of the safety technical procedures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　民用爆炸物品生产企业应当对民用爆炸物品做出警示标识、登记标识，对雷管编码打号。民用爆炸物品警示标识、登记标识和雷管编码规则，由国务院公安部门会同国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门规定。

**Article 15** A civil explosives production enterprise shall affix warning marks and registration marks to civil explosives, and shall code and number detonators. The rules for warning marks, registration marks and detonator coding of civil explosives shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council jointly with the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民用爆炸物品生产企业应当建立健全产品检验制度，保证民用爆炸物品的质量符合相关标准。民用爆炸物品的包装，应当符合法律、行政法规的规定以及相关标准。

**Article 16** A civil explosives production enterprise shall establish and improve a product inspection system to ensure that the quality of civil explosives meets the relevant standards. The packaging of civil explosives shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations and relevant standards.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　试验或者试制民用爆炸物品，必须在专门场地或者专门的试验室进行。严禁在生产车间或者仓库内试验或者试制民用爆炸物品。

**Article 17** Testing or trial production of civil explosives shall be conducted in a dedicated site or a dedicated laboratory. It is strictly prohibited to test or trial produce civil explosives in production workshops or warehouses.

### 第三章　销售和购买

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请从事民用爆炸物品销售的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合对民用爆炸物品销售企业规划的要求；  
(二)销售场所和专用仓库符合国家有关标准和规范；  
(三)有具备相应资格的安全管理人员、仓库管理人员；  
(四)有健全的安全管理制度、岗位安全责任制度；  
(五)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 18** An enterprise applying to engage in the sale of civil explosives shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Complying with the planning requirements for civil explosives sales enterprises;  
(2) The sales premises and dedicated warehouse conforming to the relevant State standards and specifications;  
(3) Having safety management personnel and warehouse management personnel with corresponding qualifications;  
(4) Having a sound safety management system and post safety responsibility system;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请从事民用爆炸物品销售的企业，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门提交申请书、可行性研究报告以及能够证明其符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定条件的有关材料。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门应当自受理申请之日起30日内进行审查，并对申请单位的销售场所和专用仓库等经营设施进行查验，对符合条件的，核发《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》；对不符合条件的，不予核发《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》，书面向申请人说明理由。  
民用爆炸物品销售企业持《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》到工商行政管理部门办理工商登记后，方可销售民用爆炸物品。  
民用爆炸物品销售企业应当在办理工商登记后3日内，向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 19** An enterprise applying to engage in the sale of civil explosives shall submit to the competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in the place where it is located a written application, a feasibility study report, and relevant materials that can prove that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 18 of these Regulations. The competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 30 days from the date of accepting the application, conduct an examination, and shall inspect the business facilities such as the sales premises and dedicated warehouse of the applicant unit, and shall issue the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives to those that meet the conditions; and shall not issue the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives to those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
A civil explosives sales enterprise may sell civil explosives only after it has gone through the industrial and commercial registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce with the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives.  
A civil explosives sales enterprise shall, within 3 days after the industrial and commercial registration, file a record with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民用爆炸物品生产企业凭《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》，可以销售本企业生产的民用爆炸物品。  
民用爆炸物品生产企业销售本企业生产的民用爆炸物品，不得超出核定的品种、产量。

**Article 20** A civil explosives production enterprise may, on the strength of the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives, sell the civil explosives produced by its own enterprise.  
When a civil explosives production enterprise sells the civil explosives produced by its own enterprise, it shall not exceed the approved varieties and output.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民用爆炸物品使用单位申请购买民用爆炸物品的，应当向所在地县级人民政府公安机关提出购买申请，并提交下列有关材料：  
(一)工商营业执照或者事业单位法人证书；  
(二)《爆破作业单位许可证》或者其他合法使用的证明；  
(三)购买单位的名称、地址、银行账户；  
(四)购买的品种、数量和用途说明。  
受理申请的公安机关应当自受理申请之日起5日内对提交的有关材料进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》；对不符合条件的，不予核发《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》，书面向申请人说明理由。  
《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》应当载明许可购买的品种、数量、购买单位以及许可的有效期限。

**Article 21** Where a civil explosives using unit applies to purchase civil explosives, it shall submit a purchase application to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located, and shall submit the following relevant materials:  
(1) The business license for industry and commerce or the legal person certificate of a public institution;  
(2) The Permit for Blasting Operation Unit or other certificates for lawful use;  
(3) The name, address and bank account of the purchasing unit;  
(4) A description of the varieties, quantities and purposes of the purchase.  
The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 5 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials, and shall issue the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives to those that meet the conditions; and shall not issue the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives to those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
The Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives shall specify the varieties and quantities approved for purchase, the purchasing unit, and the validity period of the permit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民用爆炸物品生产企业凭《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》购买属于民用爆炸物品的原料，民用爆炸物品销售企业凭《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》向民用爆炸物品生产企业购买民用爆炸物品，民用爆炸物品使用单位凭《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》购买民用爆炸物品，还应当提供经办人的身份证明。  
销售民用爆炸物品的企业，应当查验前款规定的许可证和经办人的身份证明；对持《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》购买的，应当按照许可的品种、数量销售。

**Article 22** A civil explosives production enterprise shall purchase raw materials that are civil explosives on the strength of the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives; a civil explosives sales enterprise shall purchase civil explosives from a civil explosives production enterprise on the strength of the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives; a civil explosives using unit shall purchase civil explosives on the strength of the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives. It shall also provide the identity certificate of the handler.  
An enterprise that sells civil explosives shall check the permit prescribed in the preceding paragraph and the identity certificate of the handler; for purchases made with the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives, it shall sell according to the approved varieties and quantities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　销售、购买民用爆炸物品，应当通过银行账户进行交易，不得使用现金或者实物进行交易。  
销售民用爆炸物品的企业，应当将购买单位的许可证、银行账户转账凭证、经办人的身份证明复印件保存2年备查。

**Article 23** The sale and purchase of civil explosives shall be transacted through bank accounts, and shall not be transacted with cash or in kind.  
An enterprise that sells civil explosives shall keep the copies of the purchasing unit's permit, the bank account transfer voucher, and the handler's identity certificate for 2 years for future reference.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　销售民用爆炸物品的企业，应当自民用爆炸物品买卖成交之日起3日内，将销售的品种、数量和购买单位向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门和所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。  
购买民用爆炸物品的单位，应当自民用爆炸物品买卖成交之日起3日内，将购买的品种、数量向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 24** An enterprise that sells civil explosives shall, within 3 days from the date of the transaction of the sale of civil explosives, file a record of the varieties and quantities sold and the purchasing unit with the competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in the place where it is located and the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located.  
A unit that purchases civil explosives shall, within 3 days from the date of the transaction of the purchase of civil explosives, file a record of the varieties and quantities purchased with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　进出口民用爆炸物品，应当经国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门审批。进出口民用爆炸物品审批办法，由国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门会同国务院公安部门、海关总署规定。  
进出口单位应当将进出口的民用爆炸物品的品种、数量向收货地或者出境口岸所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 25** The import and export of civil explosives shall be subject to the examination and approval of the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council. The measures for the examination and approval of the import and export of civil explosives shall be prescribed by the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council jointly with the public security department under the State Council and the General Administration of Customs.  
An importing or exporting unit shall file a record of the varieties and quantities of the civil explosives imported or exported with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place of receipt or the place of exit port.

### 第四章　运输

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　运输民用爆炸物品，收货单位应当向运达地县级人民政府公安机关提出申请，并提交包括下列内容的材料：  
(一)民用爆炸物品生产企业、销售企业、使用单位以及进出口单位分别提供的《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》、《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》、《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》或者进出口批准证明；  
(二)运输民用爆炸物品的品种、数量、包装材料和包装方式；  
(三)运输民用爆炸物品的特性、出现险情的应急处置方法；  
(四)运输时间、起始地点、运输路线、经停地点。  
受理申请的公安机关应当自受理申请之日起3日内对提交的有关材料进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》；对不符合条件的，不予核发《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》，书面向申请人说明理由。  
《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》应当载明收货单位、销售企业、承运人，一次性运输有效期限、起始地点、运输路线、经停地点，民用爆炸物品的品种、数量。

**Article 26** To transport civil explosives, the consignee unit shall submit an application to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the destination, and shall submit materials including the following contents:  
(1) The Permit for Production of Civil Explosives, the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives, the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives, or the import/export approval certificate provided respectively by the civil explosives production enterprise, sales enterprise, using unit and importing/exporting unit;  
(2) The varieties, quantities, packaging materials and packaging methods of the civil explosives to be transported;  
(3) The characteristics of the civil explosives to be transported and the emergency response methods in case of danger;  
(4) The transport time, starting place, transport route, and stopover places.  
The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 3 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials, and shall issue the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives to those that meet the conditions; and shall not issue the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives to those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
The Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives shall specify the consignee unit, the sales enterprise, the carrier, the validity period for one-time transport, the starting place, the transport route, the stopover places, and the varieties and quantities of civil explosives.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　运输民用爆炸物品的，应当凭《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》，按照许可的品种、数量运输。

**Article 27** Whoever transports civil explosives shall, on the strength of the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives, transport according to the approved varieties and quantities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　经由道路运输民用爆炸物品的，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)携带《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》；  
(二)民用爆炸物品的装载符合国家有关标准和规范，车厢内不得载人；  
(三)运输车辆安全技术状况应当符合国家有关安全技术标准的要求，并按照规定悬挂或者安装符合国家标准的易燃易爆危险物品警示标志；  
(四)运输民用爆炸物品的车辆应当保持安全车速；  
(五)按照规定的路线行驶，途中经停应当有专人看守，并远离建筑设施和人口稠密的地方，不得在许可以外的地点经停；  
(六)按照安全操作规程装卸民用爆炸物品，并在装卸现场设置警戒，禁止无关人员进入；  
(七)出现危险情况立即采取必要的应急处置措施，并报告当地公安机关。

**Article 28** The transport of civil explosives by road shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Carrying the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives;  
(2) The loading of civil explosives conforming to the relevant State standards and specifications, and no persons shall be carried in the carriage;  
(3) The safety technical condition of the transport vehicle conforming to the requirements of the relevant State safety technical standards, and the vehicle shall be affixed or installed with warning signs for inflammable and explosive dangerous goods that conform to the national standards in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) The vehicle transporting civil explosives shall maintain a safe speed;  
(5) Driving according to the prescribed route, and stopovers en route shall be guarded by a specially assigned person, and shall be kept away from buildings and densely populated areas, and no stopover shall be made at places other than those permitted;  
(6) Loading and unloading civil explosives in accordance with the safe operation procedures, and setting up guards at the loading and unloading site to prohibit irrelevant persons from entering;  
(7) In the event of a dangerous situation, immediately taking necessary emergency response measures and reporting to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民用爆炸物品运达目的地，收货单位应当进行验收后在《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》上签注，并在3日内将《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》交回发证机关核销。

**Article 29** When civil explosives arrive at the destination, the consignee unit shall, after acceptance inspection, sign and note on the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives, and shall, within 3 days, return the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives to the issuing authority for cancellation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　禁止携带民用爆炸物品搭乘公共交通工具或者进入公共场所。  
禁止邮寄民用爆炸物品，禁止在托运的货物、行李、包裹、邮件中夹带民用爆炸物品。

**Article 30** It is prohibited to carry civil explosives when taking public transport or entering public places.  
It is prohibited to mail civil explosives, and it is prohibited to conceal civil explosives in consigned goods, luggage, parcels or mail.

### 第五章　爆破作业

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　申请从事爆破作业的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)爆破作业属于合法的生产活动；  
(二)有符合国家有关标准和规范的民用爆炸物品专用仓库；  
(三)有具备相应资格的安全管理人员、仓库管理人员和具备国家规定执业资格的爆破作业人员；  
(四)有健全的安全管理制度、岗位安全责任制度；  
(五)有符合国家标准、行业标准的爆破作业专用设备；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 31** A unit applying to engage in blasting operations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The blasting operations being lawful production activities;  
(2) Having dedicated warehouses for civil explosives that conform to the relevant State standards and specifications;  
(3) Having safety management personnel and warehouse management personnel with corresponding qualifications, and blasting operation personnel with the professional qualifications prescribed by the State;  
(4) Having a sound safety management system and post safety responsibility system;  
(5) Having dedicated equipment for blasting operations that conform to national standards and industry standards;  
(6) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　申请从事爆破作业的单位，应当按照国务院公安部门的规定，向有关人民政府公安机关提出申请，并提供能够证明其符合[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定条件的有关材料。受理申请的公安机关应当自受理申请之日起20日内进行审查，对符合条件的，核发《爆破作业单位许可证》；对不符合条件的，不予核发《爆破作业单位许可证》，书面向申请人说明理由。  
营业性爆破作业单位持《爆破作业单位许可证》到工商行政管理部门办理工商登记后，方可从事营业性爆破作业活动。  
爆破作业单位应当在办理工商登记后3日内，向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 32** A unit applying to engage in blasting operations shall, in accordance with the provisions of the public security department under the State Council, submit an application to the relevant public security organ of the people's government, and provide relevant materials that can prove that it meets the conditions prescribed in Article 31 of these Regulations. The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, conduct an examination, and shall issue the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit to those that meet the conditions; and shall not issue the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit to those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
A commercial blasting operation unit may engage in commercial blasting operation activities only after it has gone through the industrial and commercial registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce with the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit.  
A blasting operation unit shall, within 3 days after the industrial and commercial registration, file a record with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　爆破作业单位应当对本单位的爆破作业人员、安全管理人员、仓库管理人员进行专业技术培训。爆破作业人员应当经设区的市级人民政府公安机关考核合格，取得《爆破作业人员许可证》后，方可从事爆破作业。

**Article 33** A blasting operation unit shall provide professional technical training for its blasting operation personnel, safety management personnel and warehouse management personnel. Blasting operation personnel shall pass the examination conducted by the public security organ of the people's government at the level of a districted city and obtain the Permit for Blasting Operator before they may engage in blasting operations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　爆破作业单位应当按照其资质等级承接爆破作业项目，爆破作业人员应当按照其资格等级从事爆破作业。爆破作业的分级管理办法由国务院公安部门规定。

**Article 34** A blasting operation unit shall undertake blasting operation projects according to its qualification grade, and blasting operation personnel shall engage in blasting operations according to their qualification grades. The measures for the classified management of blasting operations shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在城市、风景名胜区和重要工程设施附近实施爆破作业的，应当向爆破作业所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关提出申请，提交《爆破作业单位许可证》和具有相应资质的安全评估企业出具的爆破设计、施工方案评估报告。受理申请的公安机关应当自受理申请之日起20日内对提交的有关材料进行审查，对符合条件的，作出批准的决定；对不符合条件的，作出不予批准的决定，并书面向申请人说明理由。  
实施前款规定的爆破作业，应当由具有相应资质的安全监理企业进行监理，由爆破作业所在地县级人民政府公安机关负责组织实施安全警戒。

**Article 35** Where blasting operations are to be carried out in or near cities, scenic spots and important engineering facilities, the blasting operation unit shall submit an application to the public security organ of the people's government at the level of a districted city in the place where the blasting operations are to be carried out, and submit the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit and the assessment report on the blasting design and construction plan issued by a safety assessment enterprise with corresponding qualifications. The public security organ that accepts the application shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, examine the submitted relevant materials, and shall make a decision to approve those that meet the conditions; and shall make a decision not to approve those that do not meet the conditions, and shall explain the reasons to the applicant in writing.  
The blasting operations prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be supervised by a safety supervision enterprise with corresponding qualifications, and the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where the blasting operations are carried out shall be responsible for organizing and implementing safety guard.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　爆破作业单位跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域从事爆破作业的，应当事先将爆破作业项目的有关情况向爆破作业所在地县级人民政府公安机关报告。

**Article 36** Where a blasting operation unit engages in blasting operations across the administrative regions of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, it shall, in advance, report the relevant information of the blasting operation project to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where the blasting operations are to be carried out.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　爆破作业单位应当如实记载领取、发放民用爆炸物品的品种、数量、编号以及领取、发放人员姓名。领取民用爆炸物品的数量不得超过当班用量，作业后剩余的民用爆炸物品必须当班清退回库。  
爆破作业单位应当将领取、发放民用爆炸物品的原始记录保存2年备查。

**Article 37** A blasting operation unit shall truthfully record the varieties, quantities and serial numbers of civil explosives collected and distributed, as well as the names of the persons collecting and distributing them. The quantity of civil explosives collected shall not exceed the amount to be used for the shift, and the remaining civil explosives after the operation shall be returned to the warehouse for clearance within the same shift.  
A blasting operation unit shall keep the original records of the collection and distribution of civil explosives for 2 years for future reference.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　实施爆破作业，应当遵守国家有关标准和规范，在安全距离以外设置警示标志并安排警戒人员，防止无关人员进入；爆破作业结束后应当及时检查、排除未引爆的民用爆炸物品。

**Article 38** When carrying out blasting operations, the relevant State standards and specifications shall be complied with, warning signs shall be set up outside the safe distance and guards shall be arranged to prevent irrelevant persons from entering; after the blasting operations are completed, the unexploded civil explosives shall be promptly inspected and removed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　爆破作业单位不再使用民用爆炸物品时，应当将剩余的民用爆炸物品登记造册，报所在地县级人民政府公安机关组织监督销毁。  
发现、拣拾无主民用爆炸物品的，应当立即报告当地公安机关。

**Article 39** When a blasting operation unit no longer uses civil explosives, it shall register the remaining civil explosives in a ledger, and report to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where it is located for organizing supervised destruction.  
Whoever discovers or picks up unclaimed civil explosives shall immediately report to the local public security organ.

### 第六章　储存

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民用爆炸物品应当储存在专用仓库内，并按照国家规定设置技术防范设施。

**Article 40** Civil explosives shall be stored in dedicated warehouses, and technical prevention facilities shall be installed in accordance with State regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　储存民用爆炸物品应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)建立出入库检查、登记制度，收存和发放民用爆炸物品必须进行登记，做到账目清楚，账物相符；  
(二)储存的民用爆炸物品数量不得超过储存设计容量，对性质相抵触的民用爆炸物品必须分库储存，严禁在库房内存放其他物品；  
(三)专用仓库应当指定专人管理、看护，严禁无关人员进入仓库区内，严禁在仓库区内吸烟和用火，严禁把其他容易引起燃烧、爆炸的物品带入仓库区内，严禁在库房内住宿和进行其他活动；  
(四)民用爆炸物品丢失、被盗、被抢，应当立即报告当地公安机关。

**Article 41** The storage of civil explosives shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Establishing a check and registration system for warehouse entry and exit; the receipt and issuance of civil explosives must be registered, so as to keep clear accounts and make the accounts match the articles;  
(2) The quantity of civil explosives stored shall not exceed the designed storage capacity; civil explosives with conflicting properties must be stored in separate warehouses, and it is strictly prohibited to store other articles in the warehouses;  
(3) The dedicated warehouse shall be managed and guarded by a specially assigned person; it is strictly prohibited for irrelevant persons to enter the warehouse area; it is strictly prohibited to smoke or use fire in the warehouse area; it is strictly prohibited to bring any other articles that may easily cause combustion or explosion into the warehouse area; it is strictly prohibited to stay overnight or engage in other activities in the warehouses;  
(4) In the event of loss, theft or robbery of civil explosives, it shall be immediately reported to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在爆破作业现场临时存放民用爆炸物品的，应当具备临时存放民用爆炸物品的条件，并设专人管理、看护，不得在不具备安全存放条件的场所存放民用爆炸物品。

**Article 42** Where civil explosives are temporarily stored at a blasting operation site, the conditions for temporary storage of civil explosives shall be met, and a specially assigned person shall be assigned to manage and guard them. Civil explosives shall not be stored at places that do not have the conditions for safe storage.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　民用爆炸物品变质和过期失效的，应当及时清理出库，并予以销毁。销毁前应当登记造册，提出销毁实施方案，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、所在地县级人民政府公安机关组织监督销毁。

**Article 43** Where civil explosives have deteriorated or become expired and ineffective, they shall be promptly removed from the warehouse and destroyed. Before destruction, they shall be registered in a ledger, a destruction implementation plan shall be proposed, and the matter shall be reported to the competent department of the civil explosives industry of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where they are located for organizing supervised destruction.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　非法制造、买卖、运输、储存民用爆炸物品，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪，有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
违反本条例规定，在生产、储存、运输、使用民用爆炸物品中发生重大事故，造成严重后果或者后果特别严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，未经许可生产、销售民用爆炸物品的，由民用爆炸物品行业主管部门责令停止非法生产、销售活动，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，并没收非法生产、销售的民用爆炸物品及其违法所得。  
违反本条例规定，未经许可购买、运输民用爆炸物品或者从事爆破作业的，由公安机关责令停止非法购买、运输、爆破作业活动，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，并没收非法购买、运输以及从事爆破作业使用的民用爆炸物品及其违法所得。  
民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、公安机关对没收的非法民用爆炸物品，应当组织销毁。

**Article 44** Whoever illegally manufactures, buys, sells, transports or stores civil explosives, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime but violates public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a major accident occurs in the production, storage, transport or use of civil explosives, causing serious consequences or particularly serious consequences, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, civil explosives are produced or sold without a permit, the competent department of the civil explosives industry shall order the cessation of the illegal production or sale activities, impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and confiscate the illegally produced or sold civil explosives and the illegal gains derived therefrom.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, civil explosives are purchased or transported without a permit, or blasting operations are engaged in without a permit, the public security organ shall order the cessation of the illegal purchase, transport or blasting operation activities, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and confiscate the civil explosives and the illegal gains derived from the illegal purchase, transport or blasting operations.  
The competent department of the civil explosives industry and the public security organ shall organize the destruction of the confiscated illegal civil explosives.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售民用爆炸物品的企业有下列行为之一的，由民用爆炸物品行业主管部门责令限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》或者《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》：  
(一)超出生产许可的品种、产量进行生产、销售的；  
(二)违反安全技术规程生产作业的；  
(三)民用爆炸物品的质量不符合相关标准的；  
(四)民用爆炸物品的包装不符合法律、行政法规的规定以及相关标准的；  
(五)超出购买许可的品种、数量销售民用爆炸物品的；  
(六)向没有《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》、《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》、《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》的单位销售民用爆炸物品的；  
(七)民用爆炸物品生产企业销售本企业生产的民用爆炸物品未按照规定向民用爆炸物品行业主管部门备案的；  
(八)未经审批进出口民用爆炸物品的。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, an enterprise that produces or sells civil explosives commits any of the following acts, the competent department of the civil explosives industry shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives or the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives shall be revoked:  
(1) Producing or selling beyond the varieties and output approved for production;  
(2) Engaging in production operations in violation of safety technical procedures;  
(3) The quality of civil explosives failing to meet the relevant standards;  
(4) The packaging of civil explosives failing to comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the relevant standards;  
(5) Selling civil explosives beyond the varieties and quantities approved for purchase;  
(6) Selling civil explosives to units that do not have the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives, the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives or the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives;  
(7) A civil explosives production enterprise selling the civil explosives produced by its own enterprise without filing a record with the competent department of the civil explosives industry in accordance with the provisions;  
(8) Importing or exporting civil explosives without examination and approval.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿：  
(一)未按照规定对民用爆炸物品做出警示标识、登记标识或者未对雷管编码打号的；  
(二)超出购买许可的品种、数量购买民用爆炸物品的；  
(三)使用现金或者实物进行民用爆炸物品交易的；  
(四)未按照规定保存购买单位的许可证、银行账户转账凭证、经办人的身份证明复印件的；  
(五)销售、购买、进出口民用爆炸物品，未按照规定向公安机关备案的；  
(六)未按照规定建立民用爆炸物品登记制度，如实将本单位生产、销售、购买、运输、储存、使用民用爆炸物品的品种、数量和流向信息输入计算机系统的；  
(七)未按照规定将《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》交回发证机关核销的。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any of the following circumstances occurs, the public security organ shall order corrections within a specified time limit and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if corrections are not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification:  
(1) Failing to affix warning marks or registration marks to civil explosives, or failing to code and number detonators in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) Purchasing civil explosives beyond the varieties and quantities approved for purchase;  
(3) Using cash or in kind to transact civil explosives;  
(4) Failing to keep copies of the purchasing unit's permit, bank account transfer voucher, and handler's identity certificate in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Failing to file a record with the public security organ in accordance with the provisions when selling, purchasing, importing or exporting civil explosives;  
(6) Failing to establish a civil explosives registration system in accordance with the provisions, and failing to truthfully input into the computer system the information on the varieties, quantities and flow of the civil explosives produced, sold, purchased, transported, stored or used by the unit;  
(7) Failing to return the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives to the issuing authority for cancellation in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，经由道路运输民用爆炸物品，有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款：  
(一)违反运输许可事项的；  
(二)未携带《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》的；  
(三)违反有关标准和规范混装民用爆炸物品的；  
(四)运输车辆未按照规定悬挂或者安装符合国家标准的易燃易爆危险物品警示标志的；  
(五)未按照规定的路线行驶，途中经停没有专人看守或者在许可以外的地点经停的；  
(六)装载民用爆炸物品的车厢载人的；  
(七)出现危险情况未立即采取必要的应急处置措施、报告当地公安机关的。

**Article 47** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, civil explosives are transported by road and any of the following circumstances occurs, the public security organ shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) Violating the matters approved for transport;  
(2) Failing to carry the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives;  
(3) Mixing the loading of civil explosives in violation of relevant standards and specifications;  
(4) The transport vehicle failing to affix or install warning signs for inflammable and explosive dangerous goods that conform to the national standards in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) Failing to drive according to the prescribed route, or failing to arrange a specially assigned person to guard during stopovers en route, or making stopovers at places other than those permitted;  
(6) Carrying persons in the carriage loaded with civil explosives;  
(7) Failing to immediately take necessary emergency response measures and report to the local public security organ when a dangerous situation occurs.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，从事爆破作业的单位有下列情形之一的，由公安机关责令停止违法行为或者限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销《爆破作业单位许可证》：  
(一)爆破作业单位未按照其资质等级从事爆破作业的；  
(二)营业性爆破作业单位跨省、自治区、直辖市行政区域实施爆破作业，未按照规定事先向爆破作业所在地的县级人民政府公安机关报告的；  
(三)爆破作业单位未按照规定建立民用爆炸物品领取登记制度、保存领取登记记录的；  
(四)违反国家有关标准和规范实施爆破作业的。  
爆破作业人员违反国家有关标准和规范的规定实施爆破作业的，由公安机关责令限期改正，情节严重的，吊销《爆破作业人员许可证》。

**Article 48** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a unit engaging in blasting operations commits any of the following acts, the public security organ shall order the cessation of the illegal act or order corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if corrections are not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit shall be revoked:  
(1) The blasting operation unit failing to engage in blasting operations according to its qualification grade;  
(2) A commercial blasting operation unit carrying out blasting operations across the administrative regions of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government without reporting in advance to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level in the place where the blasting operations are carried out;  
(3) The blasting operation unit failing to establish a collection registration system for civil explosives or failing to keep the collection registration records in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) Carrying out blasting operations in violation of relevant State standards and specifications.  
Where a blasting operator carries out blasting operations in violation of the provisions of relevant State standards and specifications, the public security organ shall order corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the Permit for Blasting Operator shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、公安机关按照职责责令限期改正，可以并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业整顿；情节严重的，吊销许可证：  
(一)未按照规定在专用仓库设置技术防范设施的；  
(二)未按照规定建立出入库检查、登记制度或者收存和发放民用爆炸物品，致使账物不符的；  
(三)超量储存、在非专用仓库储存或者违反储存标准和规范储存民用爆炸物品的；  
(四)有本条例规定的其他违反民用爆炸物品储存管理规定行为的。

**Article 49** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent department of the civil explosives industry or the public security organ shall, according to its functions and duties, order corrections within a specified time limit, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if corrections are not made within the time limit, it shall order suspension of production or business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the permit shall be revoked:  
(1) Failing to install technical prevention facilities in the dedicated warehouse in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) Failing to establish a check and registration system for warehouse entry and exit, or failing to properly receive and issue civil explosives, resulting in a discrepancy between the accounts and the articles;  
(3) Exceeding the storage capacity, storing in non-dedicated warehouses, or storing civil explosives in violation of storage standards and specifications;  
(4) Other acts that violate the provisions on storage management of civil explosives as prescribed in these Regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，民用爆炸物品从业单位有下列情形之一的，由公安机关处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销其许可证；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚：  
(一)违反安全管理制度，致使民用爆炸物品丢失、被盗、被抢的；  
(二)民用爆炸物品丢失、被盗、被抢，未按照规定向当地公安机关报告或者故意隐瞒不报的；  
(三)转让、出借、转借、抵押、赠送民用爆炸物品的。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a civil explosives practitioner commits any of the following acts, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its permit shall be revoked; if there is a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law:  
(1) Violating the safety management system, resulting in the loss, theft or robbery of civil explosives;  
(2) Failing to report to the local public security organ in accordance with the provisions, or deliberately concealing the loss, theft or robbery of civil explosives;  
(3) Transferring, lending, sub-lending, mortgaging or giving away civil explosives.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，携带民用爆炸物品搭乘公共交通工具或者进入公共场所，邮寄或者在托运的货物、行李、包裹、邮件中夹带民用爆炸物品，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚，没收非法的民用爆炸物品，处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 51** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person carries civil explosives when taking public transport or entering public places, or mails civil explosives or conceals civil explosives in consigned goods, luggage, parcels or mail, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall give a public security administration penalty according to law, confiscate the illegal civil explosives, and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民用爆炸物品从业单位的主要负责人未履行本条例规定的安全管理责任，导致发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对主要负责人给予撤职处分，对个人经营的投资人处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 52** Where the principal person in charge of a civil explosives practitioner fails to perform the safety management responsibilities prescribed in these Regulations, causing a major accident involving casualties or other serious consequences, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the principal person in charge shall be given the sanction of removal from office, and the investor of the individual business shall be imposed a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民用爆炸物品行业主管部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门的工作人员，在民用爆炸物品安全监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守或者徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 53** Where a staff member of the competent department of the civil explosives industry, the public security organ or the administrative department for industry and commerce abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the work of safety supervision and administration of civil explosives, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　《民用爆炸物品生产许可证》、《民用爆炸物品销售许可证》，由国务院民用爆炸物品行业主管部门规定式样；《民用爆炸物品购买许可证》、《民用爆炸物品运输许可证》、《爆破作业单位许可证》、《爆破作业人员许可证》，由国务院公安部门规定式样。

**Article 54** The forms of the Permit for Production of Civil Explosives and the Permit for Sale of Civil Explosives shall be prescribed by the competent department of the civil explosives industry under the State Council; the forms of the Permit for Purchase of Civil Explosives, the Permit for Transport of Civil Explosives, the Permit for Blasting Operation Unit and the Permit for Blasting Operator shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2006年9月1日起施行。1984年1月6日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国民用爆炸物品管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2006. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Civil Explosives promulgated by the State Council on January 6, 1984 shall be abolished simultaneously.
