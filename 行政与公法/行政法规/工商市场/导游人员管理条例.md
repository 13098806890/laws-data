# 导游人员管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：1999-10-01

> (1999年5月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第263号发布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了规范导游活动，保障旅游者和导游人员的合法权益，促进旅游业的健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称导游人员，是指依照本条例的规定取得导游证，接受旅行社委派，为旅游者提供向导、讲解及相关旅游服务的人员。 第三条　国家实行全国统一的导游人员资格考试制度。 具有高级中学、中等专业学校或者以上学历，身体健康，具有适应导游需要的基本知识和语言表达能力的中华人民共和国公民，可以参加导游人员资格考试；经考试合格的，由国务院旅游行政部门或者国务院旅游行政部门委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门颁发导游人员资格证书。 第四条　在中华人民共和国境内从事导游活动，必须取得导游证。 取得导游人员资格证书的，经与旅行社订立劳动合同或者在相关旅游行业组织注册，方可持所订立的劳动合同或者登记证明材料，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门申请领取导游证。 导游证的样式规格，由国务院旅游行政部门规定。 第五条　有下列情形之一的，不得颁发导游证： (一)无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的； (二)患有传染性疾病的； (三)受过刑事处罚的，过失犯罪的除外； (四)被吊销导游证的。 第六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门应当自收到申请领取导游证之日起15日内，颁发导游证；发现有本条例第五条规定情形，不予颁发导游证的，应当书面通知申请人。 第七条　导游人员应当不断提高自身业务素质和职业技能。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范导游活动，保障旅游者和导游人员的合法权益，促进旅游业的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating tour guide activities, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of tourists and tour guides, and promoting the healthy development of the tourism industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称导游人员，是指依照本条例的规定取得导游证，接受旅行社委派，为旅游者提供向导、讲解及相关旅游服务的人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "tour guide" means a person who, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, has obtained a tour guide certificate, accepts assignment by a travel agency, and provides tourists with guiding, interpretation and related tourism services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行全国统一的导游人员资格考试制度。  
具有高级中学、中等专业学校或者以上学历，身体健康，具有适应导游需要的基本知识和语言表达能力的中华人民共和国公民，可以参加导游人员资格考试；经考试合格的，由国务院旅游行政部门或者国务院旅游行政部门委托省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门颁发导游人员资格证书。

**Article 3** The State applies a unified national tour guide qualification examination system.  
A citizen of the People's Republic of China who has a senior high school, secondary specialized school or higher education level, is in good health, and possesses the basic knowledge and language expression ability suitable for tour guiding may take the tour guide qualification examination; those who pass the examination shall be issued a tour guide qualification certificate by the tourism administrative department of the State Council or by the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council as entrusted by the tourism administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在中华人民共和国境内从事导游活动，必须取得导游证。  
取得导游人员资格证书的，经与旅行社订立劳动合同或者在相关旅游行业组织注册，方可持所订立的劳动合同或者登记证明材料，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门申请领取导游证。  
导游证的样式规格，由国务院旅游行政部门规定。

**Article 4** Anyone who engages in tour guide activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must obtain a tour guide certificate.  
A person who has obtained a tour guide qualification certificate may, after concluding a labor contract with a travel agency or registering with a relevant tourism industry organization, apply to the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council for a tour guide certificate by presenting the labor contract or registration certification materials so concluded or obtained.  
The format and specifications of the tour guide certificate shall be prescribed by the tourism administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有下列情形之一的，不得颁发导游证：  
(一)无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的；  
(二)患有传染性疾病的；  
(三)受过刑事处罚的，过失犯罪的除外；  
(四)被吊销导游证的。

**Article 5** A tour guide certificate shall not be issued under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having no civil capacity or having limited civil capacity;  
(2) suffering from an infectious disease;  
(3) having been subjected to criminal punishment, except for crimes of negligence;  
(4) having had the tour guide certificate revoked.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门应当自收到申请领取导游证之日起15日内，颁发导游证；发现有[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定情形，不予颁发导游证的，应当书面通知申请人。

**Article 6** The tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall issue a tour guide certificate within 15 days from the date of receiving the application for a tour guide certificate; if it finds that any circumstance stipulated in Article 5 of these Regulations exists and decides not to issue the certificate, it shall notify the applicant in writing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　导游人员应当不断提高自身业务素质和职业技能。  
国家对导游人员实行等级考核制度。导游人员等级考核标准和考核办法，由国务院旅游行政部门制定。

**Article 7** Tour guides shall continuously improve their professional quality and vocational skills.  
The State applies a grading and assessment system for tour guides. The grading and assessment standards and methods for tour guides shall be formulated by the tourism administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　导游人员进行导游活动时，应当佩戴导游证。  
导游证的有效期限为3年。导游证持有人需要在有效期满后继续从事导游活动的，应当在有效期限届满3个月前，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门申请办理换发导游证手续。

**Article 8** When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall wear the tour guide certificate.  
The validity period of a tour guide certificate is three years. If the holder of a tour guide certificate needs to continue engaging in tour guide activities after the expiration of the validity period, the holder shall apply to the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council for renewal of the tour guide certificate three months before the expiry of the validity period.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　导游人员进行导游活动，必须经旅行社委派。  
导游人员不得私自承揽或者以其他任何方式直接承揽导游业务，进行导游活动。

**Article 9** A tour guide must be assigned by a travel agency when engaging in tour guide activities.  
A tour guide shall not privately solicit tour guide business or directly solicit tour guide business in any other manner to engage in tour guide activities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　导游人员进行导游活动时，其人格尊严应当受到尊重，其人身安全不受侵犯。  
导游人员有权拒绝旅游者提出的侮辱其人格尊严或者违反其职业道德的不合理要求。

**Article 10** When engaging in tour guide activities, the personal dignity of a tour guide shall be respected, and the personal safety of the tour guide shall not be violated.  
A tour guide has the right to refuse any unreasonable request made by tourists that insults the personal dignity of the tour guide or violates the professional ethics of the tour guide.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　导游人员进行导游活动时，应当自觉维护国家利益和民族尊严，不得有损害国家利益和民族尊严的言行。

**Article 11** When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall consciously safeguard national interests and national dignity, and shall not engage in any words or deeds that harm national interests and national dignity.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　导游人员进行导游活动时，应当遵守职业道德，着装整洁，礼貌待人，尊重旅游者的宗教信仰、民族风俗和生活习惯。  
导游人员进行导游活动时，应当向旅游者讲解旅游地点的人文和自然情况，介绍风土人情和习俗；但是，不得迎合个别旅游者的低级趣味，在讲解、介绍中掺杂庸俗下流的内容。

**Article 12** When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall abide by professional ethics, dress neatly, treat tourists politely, and respect the religious beliefs, ethnic customs and living habits of tourists.  
When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall explain the cultural and natural conditions of the tourist site to tourists, and introduce local customs and practices; however, the tour guide shall not cater to the vulgar tastes of individual tourists by mixing vulgar and obscene content into the explanations and introductions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　导游人员应当严格按照旅行社确定的接待计划，安排旅游者的旅行、游览活动，不得擅自增加、减少旅游项目或者中止导游活动。  
导游人员在引导旅游者旅行、游览过程中，遇有可能危及旅游者人身安全的紧急情形时，经征得多数旅游者的同意，可以调整或者变更接待计划，但是应当立即报告旅行社。

**Article 13** A tour guide shall strictly arrange the travel and sightseeing activities of tourists in accordance with the reception plan determined by the travel agency, and shall not increase or decrease tour items without authorization or suspend the tour guide activities.  
When guiding tourists during travel and sightseeing, if encountering an emergency situation that may endanger the personal safety of tourists, the tour guide may, after obtaining the consent of the majority of tourists, adjust or change the reception plan, but shall immediately report to the travel agency.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　导游人员在引导旅游者旅行、游览过程中，应当就可能发生危及旅游者人身、财物安全的情况，向旅游者作出真实说明和明确警示，并按照旅行社的要求采取防止危害发生的措施。

**Article 14** When guiding tourists during travel and sightseeing, a tour guide shall make truthful explanations and clear warnings to tourists regarding situations that may endanger the personal safety or property safety of tourists, and shall take measures to prevent the occurrence of harm as required by the travel agency.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　导游人员进行导游活动，不得向旅游者兜售物品或者购买旅游者的物品，不得以明示或者暗示的方式向旅游者索要小费。

**Article 15** When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall not peddle goods to tourists or purchase goods from tourists, and shall not solicit tips from tourists by express or implied means.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　导游人员进行导游活动，不得欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费或者与经营者串通欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费。

**Article 16** When engaging in tour guide activities, a tour guide shall not deceive or coerce tourists into consumption, or collude with business operators to deceive or coerce tourists into consumption.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　旅游者对导游人员违反本条例规定的行为，有权向旅游行政部门投诉。

**Article 17** Tourists have the right to lodge complaints with the tourism administrative department against any act of a tour guide that violates the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　无导游证进行导游活动的，由旅游行政部门责令改正并予以公告，处1000元以上3万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得。

**Article 18** Anyone who engages in tour guide activities without a tour guide certificate shall be ordered by the tourism administrative department to make corrections and shall be publicly announced; a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　导游人员未经旅行社委派，私自承揽或者以其他任何方式直接承揽导游业务，进行导游活动的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，处1000元以上3万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门吊销导游证并予以公告。

**Article 19** If a tour guide privately solicits tour guide business or directly solicits tour guide business in any other manner to engage in tour guide activities without assignment by a travel agency, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall revoke the tour guide certificate and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　导游人员进行导游活动时，有损害国家利益和民族尊严的言行的，由旅游行政部门责令改正；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门吊销导游证并予以公告；对该导游人员所在的旅行社给予警告直至责令停业整顿。

**Article 20** If a tour guide, when engaging in tour guide activities, engages in words or deeds that harm national interests and national dignity, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall revoke the tour guide certificate and make a public announcement; the travel agency that assigned the tour guide shall be given a warning or ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　导游人员进行导游活动时未佩戴导游证的，由旅游行政部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处500元以下的罚款。

**Article 21** If a tour guide fails to wear the tour guide certificate when engaging in tour guide activities, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections; if the tour guide refuses to make corrections, a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　导游人员有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，暂扣导游证3至6个月；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门吊销导游证并予以公告：  
(一)擅自增加或者减少旅游项目的；  
(二)擅自变更接待计划的；  
(三)擅自中止导游活动的。

**Article 22** If a tour guide falls under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections and temporarily detain the tour guide certificate for 3 to 6 months; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall revoke the tour guide certificate and make a public announcement:  
(1) increasing or decreasing tour items without authorization;  
(2) changing the reception plan without authorization;  
(3) suspending tour guide activities without authorization.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　导游人员进行导游活动，向旅游者兜售物品或者购买旅游者的物品的，或者以明示或者暗示的方式向旅游者索要小费的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，处1000元以上3万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门吊销导游证并予以公告；对委派该导游人员的旅行社给予警告直至责令停业整顿。

**Article 23** If a tour guide, when engaging in tour guide activities, peddles goods to tourists or purchases goods from tourists, or solicits tips from tourists by express or implied means, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall revoke the tour guide certificate and make a public announcement; the travel agency that assigned the tour guide shall be given a warning or ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　导游人员进行导游活动，欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费或者与经营者串通欺骗、胁迫旅游者消费的，由旅游行政部门责令改正，处1000元以上3万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，并处没收违法所得；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府旅游行政部门吊销导游证并予以公告；对委派该导游人员的旅行社给予警告直至责令停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** If a tour guide, when engaging in tour guide activities, deceives or coerces tourists into consumption, or colludes with business operators to deceive or coerce tourists into consumption, the tourism administrative department shall order the tour guide to make corrections, impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated concurrently; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council shall revoke the tour guide certificate and make a public announcement; the travel agency that assigned the tour guide shall be given a warning or ordered to suspend operations for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　旅游行政部门工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 25** If a staff member of a tourism administrative department neglects duties, abuses power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　景点景区的导游人员管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府参照本条例制定。

**Article 26** The measures for the administration of tour guides at scenic spots and tourist attractions shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the State Council with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自1999年10月1日起施行。1987年11月14日国务院批准、1987年12月1日国家旅游局发布的《导游人员管理暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1999. The Interim Provisions on the Administration of Tour Guides, approved by the State Council on November 14, 1987 and promulgated by the National Tourism Administration on December 1, 1987, shall be abolished simultaneously.
