# 促进个体工商户发展条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-10-01  
**生效日期**：2022-11-01

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了鼓励、支持和引导个体经济健康发展，维护个体工商户合法权益，稳定和扩大城乡就业，充分发挥个体工商户在国民经济和社会发展中的重要作用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of encouraging, supporting and guiding the healthy development of the individual economy, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of individual industrial and commercial households, stabilizing and expanding urban and rural employment, and giving full play to the important role of individual industrial and commercial households in the national economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　有经营能力的公民在中华人民共和国境内从事工商业经营，依法登记为个体工商户的，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to citizens with business capacity who engage in industrial and commercial operations within the territory of the People's Republic of China and are registered as individual industrial and commercial households according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　促进个体工商户发展工作坚持中国共产党的领导，发挥党组织在个体工商户发展中的引领作用和党员先锋模范作用。  
个体工商户中的党组织和党员按照中国共产党章程的规定开展党的活动。

**Article 3** The work of promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, give full play to the leading role of Party organizations in the development of individual industrial and commercial households and the vanguard and exemplary role of Party members.  
Party organizations and Party members among individual industrial and commercial households shall carry out Party activities in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution of the Communist Party of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　个体经济是社会主义市场经济的重要组成部分，个体工商户是重要的市场主体，在繁荣经济、增加就业、推动创业创新、方便群众生活等方面发挥着重要作用。  
国家持续深化简政放权、放管结合、优化服务改革，优化营商环境，积极扶持、加强引导、依法规范，为个体工商户健康发展创造有利条件。

**Article 4** The individual economy is an important part of the socialist market economy, and individual industrial and commercial households are important market entities, playing an important role in prospering the economy, increasing employment, promoting entrepreneurship and innovation, and facilitating people's life.  
The State shall continuously deepen the reform of streamlining administration, delegating powers, combining regulation with deregulation, and optimizing services, optimize the business environment, actively support, strengthen guidance, and regulate according to law, so as to create favorable conditions for the healthy development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对个体工商户实行市场平等准入、公平待遇的原则。

**Article 5** The State shall implement the principle of equal market access and fair treatment for individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　个体工商户可以个人经营，也可以家庭经营。个体工商户的财产权、经营自主权等合法权益受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵害或者非法干预。

**Article 6** Individual industrial and commercial households may operate as individuals or as families. The legitimate rights and interests of individual industrial and commercial households, such as property rights and operational autonomy, shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon or unlawfully interfere with them.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院建立促进个体工商户发展部际联席会议制度，研究并推进实施促进个体工商户发展的重大政策措施，统筹协调促进个体工商户发展工作中的重大事项。  
国务院市场监督管理部门会同有关部门加强对促进个体工商户发展工作的宏观指导、综合协调和监督检查。

**Article 7** The State Council shall establish an inter-ministerial joint meeting system for promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households, study and promote the implementation of major policies and measures for promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households, and coordinate major issues in the work of promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households.  
The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, together with relevant departments, strengthen macro guidance, comprehensive coordination, and supervision and inspection of the work of promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院发展改革、财政、人力资源社会保障、住房城乡建设、商务、金融、税务、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内研究制定税费支持、创业扶持、职业技能培训、社会保障、金融服务、登记注册、权益保护等方面的政策措施，做好促进个体工商户发展工作。

**Article 8** The relevant departments of the State Council, such as development and reform, finance, human resources and social security, housing and urban-rural construction, commerce, finance, taxation, and market supervision and administration, shall, within their respective functions and duties, study and formulate policies and measures in terms of tax and fee support, entrepreneurship support, vocational skills training, social insurance, financial services, registration, rights protection, etc., and do a good job in promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将促进个体工商户发展纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，结合本行政区域个体工商户发展情况制定具体措施并组织实施，为个体工商户发展提供支持。

**Article 9** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the promotion of the development of individual industrial and commercial households into their respective national economic and social development plans, formulate specific measures based on the development situation of individual industrial and commercial households within their administrative regions and organize their implementation, and provide support for the development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家加强个体工商户发展状况监测分析，定期开展抽样调查、监测统计和活跃度分析，强化个体工商户发展信息的归集、共享和运用。

**Article 10** The State shall strengthen the monitoring and analysis of the development status of individual industrial and commercial households, regularly conduct sample surveys, monitoring statistics and activity analysis, and enhance the collection, sharing and application of information on the development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　市场主体登记机关应当为个体工商户提供依法合规、规范统一、公开透明、便捷高效的登记服务。

**Article 11** The market entity registration authority shall provide registration services that are lawful, compliant, standardized, unified, open, transparent, convenient, and efficient for individual businesses.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院市场监督管理部门应当根据个体工商户发展特点，改革完善个体工商户年度报告制度，简化内容、优化流程，提供简易便捷的年度报告服务。

**Article 12** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, based on the development characteristics of individual businesses, reform and improve the annual reporting system for individual businesses, simplify content, optimize procedures, and provide simple and convenient annual reporting services.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　个体工商户可以自愿变更经营者或者转型为企业。变更经营者的，可以直接向市场主体登记机关申请办理变更登记。涉及有关行政许可的，行政许可部门应当简化手续，依法为个体工商户提供便利。  
个体工商户变更经营者或者转型为企业的，应当结清依法应缴纳的税款等，对原有债权债务作出妥善处理，不得损害他人的合法权益。

**Article 13** An individual business may voluntarily change its operator or transform itself into an enterprise. If the operator is changed, the business may directly apply to the market entity registration authority for the change registration. If an administrative license is involved, the administrative license department shall simplify the procedures and provide convenience to the individual business according to law.  
Where an individual business changes its operator or transforms into an enterprise, it shall settle the taxes and other amounts payable according to law, properly handle the original creditor's rights and debts, and shall not harm the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家加强个体工商户公共服务平台体系建设，为个体工商户提供法律政策、市场供求、招聘用工、创业培训、金融支持等信息服务。

**Article 14** The State shall strengthen the construction of a public service platform system for individual businesses, providing information services such as legal policies, market supply and demand, recruitment and employment, entrepreneurship training, and financial support.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依法成立的个体劳动者协会在市场监督管理部门指导下，充分发挥桥梁纽带作用，推动个体工商户党的建设，为个体工商户提供服务，维护个体工商户合法权益，引导个体工商户诚信自律。  
个体工商户自愿加入个体劳动者协会。

**Article 15** The individual laborers' association established according to law shall, under the guidance of the market supervision and administration department, fully play its role as a bridge and link, promote the Party building of individual businesses, provide services for individual businesses, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of individual businesses, and guide individual businesses to act in good faith and self-discipline.  
Individual businesses may voluntarily join the individual laborers' association.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　政府及其有关部门在制定相关政策措施时，应当充分听取个体工商户以及相关行业组织的意见，不得违反规定在资质许可、项目申报、政府采购、招标投标等方面对个体工商户制定或者实施歧视性政策措施。

**Article 16** When formulating relevant policies and measures, the government and its relevant departments shall fully solicit opinions from individual businesses and relevant industry organizations, and shall not formulate or implement discriminatory policy measures against individual businesses in terms of qualification licensing, project declaration, government procurement, bidding and tendering, etc., in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当结合本行政区域实际情况，根据个体工商户的行业类型、经营规模、经营特点等，对个体工商户实施分型分类培育和精准帮扶。

**Article 17** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the actual circumstances of its administrative region, carry out classified and categorized cultivation and targeted assistance for individual businesses according to their industry types, business scales, business characteristics, etc.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取有效措施，为个体工商户增加经营场所供给，降低经营场所使用成本。

**Article 18** The local people's government at or above the county level shall take effective measures to increase the supply of business premises for individual businesses and reduce the cost of using business premises.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励和引导创业投资机构和社会资金支持个体工商户发展。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当充分发挥各类资金作用，为个体工商户在创业创新、贷款融资、职业技能培训等方面提供资金支持。

**Article 19** The State shall encourage and guide venture capital institutions and social funds to support the development of individual businesses.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall fully utilize the role of various funds to provide financial support to individual businesses in terms of entrepreneurship and innovation, loan financing, vocational skills training, etc.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家实行有利于个体工商户发展的财税政策。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当严格落实相关财税支持政策，确保精准、及时惠及个体工商户。

**Article 20** The State shall implement fiscal and tax policies conducive to the development of individual businesses.  
The local people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments shall strictly implement the relevant fiscal and tax support policies to ensure that they are accurately and timely beneficial to individual businesses.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家推动建立和完善个体工商户信用评价体系，鼓励金融机构开发和提供适合个体工商户发展特点的金融产品和服务，扩大个体工商户贷款规模和覆盖面，提高贷款精准性和便利度。

**Article 21** The State promotes the establishment and improvement of a credit evaluation system for individually-owned businesses, encourages financial institutions to develop and provide financial products and services suitable for the development characteristics of individually-owned businesses, expand the scale and coverage of loans to individually-owned businesses, and increase the precision and convenience of loans.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当支持个体工商户参加社会保险，对符合条件的个体工商户给予相应的支持。

**Article 22** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall support individually-owned businesses in participating in social insurance, and provide corresponding support to individually-owned businesses that meet the conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当完善创业扶持政策，支持个体工商户参加职业技能培训，鼓励各类公共就业服务机构为个体工商户提供招聘用工服务。

**Article 23** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall improve entrepreneurship support policies, support individually-owned businesses in participating in vocational skills training, and encourage various types of public employment service agencies to provide recruitment and employment services for individually-owned businesses.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当结合城乡社区服务体系建设，支持个体工商户在社区从事与居民日常生活密切相关的经营活动，满足居民日常生活消费需求。

**Article 24** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the construction of urban and rural community service systems, support individually-owned businesses in engaging in business activities closely related to residents' daily lives within the community, so as to meet residents' daily consumption needs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家引导和支持个体工商户加快数字化发展、实现线上线下一体化经营。  
平台经营者应当在入驻条件、服务规则、收费标准等方面，为个体工商户线上经营提供支持，不得利用服务协议、平台规则、数据算法、技术等手段，对平台内个体工商户进行不合理限制、附加不合理条件或者收取不合理费用。

**Article 25** The State guides and supports individually-owned businesses in accelerating digital development and achieving integrated online and offline operations.  
Platform operators shall provide support to individually-owned businesses for online operations in terms of entry conditions, service rules, and charging standards, and shall not use service agreements, platform rules, data algorithms, technologies, or other means to impose unreasonable restrictions, attach unreasonable conditions, or charge unreasonable fees on individually-owned businesses within the platform.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家加大对个体工商户的字号、商标、专利、商业秘密等权利的保护力度。  
国家鼓励和支持个体工商户提升知识产权的创造运用水平、增强市场竞争力。

**Article 26** The State strengthens the protection of the rights of individually-owned businesses, including trade names, trademarks, patents, and trade secrets.  
The State encourages and supports individually-owned businesses in improving the level of creation and application of intellectual property rights and enhancing market competitiveness.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方人民政府制定实施城乡建设规划及城市和交通管理、市容环境治理、产业升级等相关政策措施，应当充分考虑个体工商户经营需要和实际困难，实施引导帮扶。

**Article 27** When formulating and implementing urban and rural construction plans and relevant policies and measures on urban and traffic management, city appearance and environmental governance, industrial upgrading, etc., local people's governments at or above the county level shall fully consider the operational needs and actual difficulties of individually-owned businesses and implement guidance and assistance.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级人民政府对因自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等原因造成经营困难的个体工商户，结合实际情况及时采取纾困帮扶措施。

**Article 28** People's governments at all levels shall, in light of the actual circumstances, promptly take relief and assistance measures for individually-owned businesses that have encountered operational difficulties due to natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, social security incidents, or other reasons.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　政府及其有关部门按照国家有关规定，对个体工商户先进典型进行表彰奖励，不断提升个体工商户经营者的荣誉感。

**Article 29** The government and its relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, commend and reward exemplary models of individually-owned businesses, and continuously enhance the sense of honor of operators of individually-owned businesses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　任何单位和个人不得违反法律法规和国家有关规定向个体工商户收费或者变相收费，不得擅自扩大收费范围或者提高收费标准，不得向个体工商户集资、摊派，不得强行要求个体工商户提供赞助或者接受有偿服务。  
任何单位和个人不得诱导、强迫劳动者登记注册为个体工商户。

**Article 30** No entity or individual may, in violation of laws, regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State, charge fees or collect fees in disguised form from individually-owned businesses, expand the scope of fees or raise the charging standards without authorization, raise funds or apportion expenses among individually-owned businesses, or compel individually-owned businesses to provide sponsorships or accept paid services.  
No entity or individual may induce or compel workers to register as individually-owned businesses.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　机关、企业事业单位不得要求个体工商户接受不合理的付款期限、方式、条件和违约责任等交易条件，不得违约拖欠个体工商户账款，不得通过强制个体工商户接受商业汇票等非现金支付方式变相拖欠账款。

**Article 31** Government organs, enterprises, and public institutions shall not require individual industrial and commercial households to accept unreasonable payment periods, methods, conditions, and liability for breach of contract, among other transaction terms; shall not default on payments owed to individual industrial and commercial households; and shall not delay payment in disguised form through forcing individual industrial and commercial households to accept commercial drafts or other non-cash payment methods.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当提升个体工商户发展质量，不得将个体工商户数量增长率、年度报告率等作为绩效考核评价指标。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall improve the development quality of individual industrial and commercial households, and shall not use the growth rate in number of individual industrial and commercial households, annual reporting rate, or the like, as performance assessment indicators.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　个体工商户对违反本条例规定、侵害自身合法权益的行为，有权向有关部门投诉、举报。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当畅通投诉、举报途径，并依法及时处理。

**Article 33** Individual industrial and commercial households shall have the right to lodge complaints and reports with the relevant departments against acts that violate the provisions of these Regulations and infringe upon their legitimate rights and interests.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall keep the channels for complaints and reports open, and handle such matters promptly according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　个体工商户应当依法经营、诚实守信，自觉履行劳动用工、安全生产、食品安全、职业卫生、环境保护、公平竞争等方面的法定义务。  
对涉及公共安全和人民群众生命健康等重点领域，有关行政部门应当加强监督管理，维护良好市场秩序。

**Article 34** Individual industrial and commercial households shall operate according to law, be honest and trustworthy, and voluntarily perform their statutory obligations in such aspects as labor employment, production safety, food safety, occupational health, environmental protection, and fair competition.  
For key areas involving public safety and people's life and health, the relevant administrative departments shall strengthen supervision and management to maintain good market order.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　个体工商户开展经营活动违反有关法律规定的，有关行政部门应当按照教育和惩戒相结合、过罚相当的原则，依法予以处理。

**Article 35** Where an individual industrial and commercial household, in engaging in business activities, violates relevant legal provisions, the relevant administrative department shall handle the matter according to law, following the principle of combining education with punishment and proportionality between the offense and the penalty.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　政府及其有关部门的工作人员在促进个体工商户发展工作中不履行或者不正确履行职责，损害个体工商户合法权益，造成严重后果的，依法依规给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where a staff member of the government or its relevant department fails to perform or incorrectly performs his or her duties in the work of promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households, thereby harming the legitimate rights and interests of individual industrial and commercial households and causing serious consequences, he or she shall be given sanctions according to laws and regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区永久性居民中的中国公民，台湾地区居民可以按照国家有关规定，申请登记为个体工商户。

**Article 37** Chinese citizens who are permanent residents of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and residents of the Taiwan region may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, apply for registration as individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　省、自治区、直辖市可以结合本行政区域实际情况，制定促进个体工商户发展的具体办法。

**Article 38** The provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, taking into account the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, formulate specific measures for promoting the development of individual industrial and commercial households.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自2022年11月1日起施行。《个体工商户条例》同时废止。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force on November 1, 2022. The Regulations on Individual Industrial and Commercial Households is simultaneously abolished.
