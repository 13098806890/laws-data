# 禁止向企业摊派暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1988-04-27  
**生效日期**：1988-05-20

> (1988年4月28日国务院发布　自1988年5月20日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了禁止向企业摊派，保护企业的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称摊派是指在法律、法规的规定之外，以任何方式要求企业提供财力、物力和人力的行为。 第三条　禁止任何国家机关、人民团体、部队、企业、事业单位和其他社会组织(以下统称单位)向企业摊派。 第二章　禁止各种形式的摊派 第四条　不得在法律、法规的规定之外向企业征收下列费用： (一)各地教育部门、学校自定的职工子女入学费； (二)建田费、垦复费； (三)进入城市落户的人头费； (四)煤气开发费； (五)集中供电费； (六)过路费； (七)过桥费(集资或用贷款建桥的除外)； (八)排水增容费；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了禁止向企业摊派，保护企业的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of prohibiting the imposition of apportionments on enterprises and protecting the lawful rights and interests of enterprises.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称摊派是指在法律、法规的规定之外，以任何方式要求企业提供财力、物力和人力的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "apportionment" means any act of requiring an enterprise to provide financial resources, materials, or manpower in any manner, beyond the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　禁止任何国家机关、人民团体、部队、企业、事业单位和其他社会组织(以下统称单位)向企业摊派。

**Article 3** No state organ, people's organization, military unit, enterprise, public institution, or any other social organization (hereinafter collectively referred to as "units") shall impose apportionments on enterprises.

### 第二章　禁止各种形式的摊派

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　不得在法律、法规的规定之外向企业征收下列费用：  
(一)各地教育部门、学校自定的职工子女入学费；  
(二)建田费、垦复费；  
(三)进入城市落户的人头费；  
(四)煤气开发费；  
(五)集中供电费；  
(六)过路费；  
(七)过桥费(集资或用贷款建桥的除外)；  
(八)排水增容费；  
(九)各种名目的治安管理费；  
(十)各种名目的卫生费；  
(十一)绿化费；  
(十二)支农费；  
(十三)各种名目的会议费；  
(十四)其他名目的费用。  
城市市区新建项目，需要征收城市建设配套费的，应当按照有关法律、法规的规定办理。

**Article 4** No unit shall levy the following fees on enterprises beyond the provisions of laws and regulations:  
(1) Fees for children of employees set by local education departments or schools;  
(2) Land creation fees and reclamation fees;  
(3) Per capita fees for urban settlement;  
(4) Gas development fees;  
(5) Centralized power supply fees;  
(6) Road tolls;  
(7) Bridge tolls (except for those built with pooled funds or loans);  
(8) Drainage capacity expansion fees;  
(9) Various types of public security management fees;  
(10) Various types of sanitation fees;  
(11) Greening fees;  
(12) Fees for supporting agriculture;  
(13) Various types of meeting fees;  
(14) Other miscellaneous fees.  
Where a new construction project in an urban area requires the levy of urban construction supporting fees, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　法律、法规规定的征收费用项目，任何单位不得超出征收的范围，提高征收的标准，变更征收的办法。

**Article 5** For fee collection items provided for by laws and regulations, no unit shall exceed the scope of collection, raise the standards of collection, or alter the methods of collection.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　不得强制企业赞助、资助、捐献财物。  
企业自愿赞助、资助、捐献的款项只能从企业的自有资金中支出，不得计入成本。

**Article 6** No unit shall compel an enterprise to sponsor, subsidize, or donate money or property.  
The funds voluntarily sponsored, subsidized, or donated by an enterprise shall be disbursed only from its own funds and shall not be included in costs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　除法律、法规规定外，不得强制企业购买有价证券或以其他形式向企业集资。

**Article 7** Except as provided for by laws and regulations, no unit shall compel an enterprise to purchase marketable securities or to raise funds from the enterprise in any other form.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　除法律、法规规定的强制保险项目外，不得强制企业参加保险。

**Article 8** Except for compulsory insurance items provided for by laws and regulations, no unit shall compel an enterprise to take out insurance.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　除法律、法规规定外，不得将公益性义务劳动改变为向企业摊派财物。

**Article 9** Except as provided for by laws and regulations, no unit shall convert voluntary public welfare labor into apportionment of money or property on enterprises.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　不得违反本条例规定向企业进行其他形式的摊派。

**Article 10** No unit shall impose other forms of apportionments on enterprises in violation of these Regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　禁止对抵制摊派的企业进行打击报复。  
不得利用职权或业务上的便利给予抵制摊派的企业以歧视性待遇。

**Article 11** It is prohibited to retaliate against enterprises that resist apportionments.  
No unit shall use its authority or business convenience to give discriminatory treatment to enterprises that resist apportionments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在发生自然灾害、意外事故等紧急情况时，各级地方人民政府作出决定要求企业提供财力、物力和人力的，不视为摊派，但事后须报上一级人民政府备案。

**Article 12** Where, in the event of an emergency such as a natural disaster or accident, local people's governments at various levels make a decision requiring an enterprise to provide financial resources, materials, or manpower, such action shall not be deemed as an apportionment, but the matter shall be reported to the people's government at the next higher level for recordation afterwards.

### 第三章　权利与责任

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　企业有权拒绝任何单位的摊派。  
企业对收取费用的项目性质不明确的，应当向收费单位上一级人民政府的财政部门报告，经审查符合法律、法规规定的，才能支付。

**Article 13** An enterprise shall have the right to refuse any unit's apportionment.  
Where an enterprise is unclear as to the nature of a fee collection item, it shall report to the finance department of the people's government at the level above the fee-collecting unit, and may only make payment after it is verified that the item is in conformity with laws and regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　财政部门对企业依照第十三条第二款提出的报告，应当在接到报告后三十日内作出应否缴纳的答复。期满不答复的，视为不同意缴纳。  
企业对财政部门的答复有异议的，可以向上一级财政部门反映。

**Article 14** The finance department shall, within thirty days of receiving the report submitted by the enterprise in accordance with the second paragraph of Article 13, make a reply as to whether the fee shall be paid. Failure to reply within the time limit shall be deemed as disagreement with the payment.  
Where an enterprise has any objection to the reply of the finance department, it may report to the finance department at the next higher level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　任何单位和个人都有权向审计机关或其他有关部门控告、检举、揭发摊派行为。

**Article 15** Any unit or individual shall have the right to accuse, report, or expose acts of apportionment to the audit authority or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审计机关对于收到的控告、检举、揭发以及其他部门根据第十七条规定移送的摊派案件，可以会同有关部门进行调查。被调查的单位必须如实提供情况，不得刁难和阻挠。

**Article 16** The audit authority may, together with the relevant departments, investigate cases of apportionment that it receives through accusations, reports, exposures, or that are transferred by other departments in accordance with Article 17. The unit under investigation must truthfully provide information and shall not create difficulties or obstruct the investigation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　计划、财政、物价、税务、银行等部门应当加强监督检查，发现摊派行为，应当及时制止；制止无效时应当移送审计机关立案处理。

**Article 17** The planning, finance, price, taxation, banking, and other departments shall strengthen supervision and inspection. Where they discover any act of apportionment, they shall promptly stop it; if the stop is ineffective, they shall transfer the case to the audit authority for case-filing and handling.

### 第四章　奖励与惩罚

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经审计机关确认为摊派行为的，审计机关应当通知摊派单位停止摊派行为，并限期退回摊派的财物；期满不退回的，审计机关可以书面通知摊派单位的开户银行从其有关存款中扣还。  
摊派财物已不存在而无法追回时，审计机关可以通知有关部门扣缴相当于所摊派财物价值的款额，或采取其他经济补偿措施。

**Article 18** Where an act is confirmed by the audit authority as an apportionment, the audit authority shall notify the unit that imposed the apportionment to cease the act and return the apportioned money or property within a specified time limit; if the unit fails to return it within the time limit, the audit authority may notify the bank with which the unit has an account to deduct and repay the amount from its relevant deposits.  
Where the apportioned money or property no longer exists and cannot be recovered, the audit authority may notify the relevant department to deduct and confiscate an amount equivalent to the value of the apportioned money or property, or take other economic compensation measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对摊派单位的负责人和直接责任人员，监察机关或有关主管部门可根据情节轻重给予相应的行政处分。

**Article 19** The supervisory authority or the relevant competent department may, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose corresponding administrative sanctions on the person in charge and the persons directly responsible of the unit that imposed the apportionment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，对控告、检举、揭发和抵制摊派的单位和人员进行打击报复的，应当从重处罚；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Anyone who, in violation of these Regulations, retaliates against a unit or individual that has accused, reported, exposed, or resisted an apportionment shall be given a severe punishment; if a crime is constituted, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　私分摊派财物的，由监察机关追究其单位负责人的行政责任；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where persons misappropriate or share the apportioned money or property, the supervisory authority shall investigate the administrative liability of the person in charge of the unit; if a crime is constituted, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对检举、揭发、抵制摊派有功的人员，审计机关可给予表彰。

**Article 22** The audit authority may commend persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting, exposing, or resisting apportionments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　追回的摊派财物，属于企业已报告或控告的，应当退还企业；未报告和控告的，一律上缴中央财政。

**Article 23** The recovered apportioned money or property shall, if the enterprise has reported or accused the matter, be returned to the enterprise; if no report or accusation has been made, it shall all be turned over to the central finance.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　摊派单位对审计机关作出的经济处罚决定不服的，可以在接到决定书之日起十五日内向上一级审计机关申请复审。在复审期间，不影响原处罚决定的执行。

**Article 24** Where the unit that imposed the apportionment disagrees with the decision on economic penalty made by the audit authority, it may, within fifteen days of receipt of the decision, apply to the audit authority at the next higher level for review. The execution of the original penalty decision shall not be suspended during the period of review.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例所称企业，是指依法在工商行政管理机关登记注册的企业。

**Article 25** For the purposes of these Regulations, "enterprise" means an enterprise registered with the administrative authority for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　向国家机关、人民团体、部队、事业单位和其他社会组织(含基本建设单位)以及个人的摊派，比照本条例处理。

**Article 26** The imposition of apportionments on state organs, people's organizations, military units, public institutions, and other social organizations (including capital construction units) and individuals shall be handled by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自1988年5月20日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 20, 1988.
