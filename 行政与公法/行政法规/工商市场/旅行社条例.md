# 旅行社条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对旅行社的管理，保障旅游者和旅行社的合法权益，维护旅游市场秩序，促进旅游业的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of travel agencies, protect the legitimate rights and interests of tourists and travel agencies, maintain the order of the tourism market, and promote the healthy development of the tourism industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内旅行社的设立及经营活动。  
本条例所称旅行社，是指从事招徕、组织、接待旅游者等活动，为旅游者提供相关旅游服务，开展国内旅游业务、入境旅游业务或者出境旅游业务的企业法人。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the establishment and business activities of travel agencies within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "travel agency" means an enterprise legal person that engages in such activities as soliciting, organizing, and receiving tourists, providing relevant tourism services to tourists, and conducting domestic tourism business, inbound tourism business or outbound tourism business.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院旅游行政主管部门负责全国旅行社的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府管理旅游工作的部门按照职责负责本行政区域内旅行社的监督管理工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府工商、价格、商务、外汇等有关部门，应当按照职责分工，依法对旅行社进行监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent department of tourism administration under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of travel agencies nationwide.  
The department in charge of tourism work of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of travel agencies within its administrative region according to its functions and duties.  
The relevant departments of industry and commerce, price, commerce, foreign exchange, etc. of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to the division of their functions and duties, supervise and administer travel agencies according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　旅行社在经营活动中应当遵循自愿、平等、公平、诚信的原则，提高服务质量，维护旅游者的合法权益。

**Article 4** A travel agency shall, in its business activities, follow the principles of voluntariness, equality, fairness, and good faith, improve service quality, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of tourists.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　旅行社行业组织应当按照章程为旅行社提供服务，发挥协调和自律作用，引导旅行社合法、公平竞争和诚信经营。

**Article 5** The trade organization of travel agencies shall, in accordance with its articles of association, provide services to travel agencies, play a coordinating and self-disciplinary role, and guide travel agencies to conduct lawful and fair competition and operate in good faith.

### 第二章　旅行社的设立

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请经营国内旅游业务和入境旅游业务的，应当取得企业法人资格，并且注册资本不少于30万元。

**Article 6** An applicant for operating domestic tourism business and inbound tourism business shall obtain the status of an enterprise legal person, and its registered capital shall not be less than 300,000 yuan.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请经营国内旅游业务和入境旅游业务的，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政管理部门或者其委托的设区的市级旅游行政管理部门提出申请，并提交符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定的相关证明文件。受理申请的旅游行政管理部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，向申请人颁发旅行社业务经营许可证；不予许可的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 7** An applicant for operating domestic tourism business and inbound tourism business shall submit an application to the tourism administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, or to the tourism administrative department at the level of a city divided into districts authorized by the former, and submit the relevant certifying documents that comply with the provisions of Article 6 of these Regulations. The tourism administrative department that accepts the application shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to permit or not to permit. If it grants permission, it shall issue a travel agency business permit to the applicant; if it does not grant permission, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　旅行社取得经营许可满两年，且未因侵害旅游者合法权益受到行政机关罚款以上处罚的，可以申请经营出境旅游业务。

**Article 8** A travel agency that has obtained the business permit for two years and has not been subjected to a penalty of a fine or above by an administrative organ for infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of tourists may apply for operating outbound tourism business.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请经营出境旅游业务的，应当向国务院旅游行政主管部门或者其委托的省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政管理部门提出申请，受理申请的旅游行政管理部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，向申请人换发旅行社业务经营许可证；不予许可的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 9** An applicant for operating outbound tourism business shall submit an application to the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council or to the tourism administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government authorized by the former. The tourism administrative department that accepts the application shall, within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to permit or not to permit. If it grants permission, it shall exchange a new travel agency business permit for the applicant; if it does not grant permission, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　旅行社设立分社的，应当向分社所在地的工商行政管理部门办理设立登记，并自设立登记之日起3个工作日内向分社所在地的旅游行政管理部门备案。  
旅行社分社的设立不受地域限制。分社的经营范围不得超出设立分社的旅行社的经营范围。

**Article 10** If a travel agency establishes a branch, it shall register the establishment with the administrative department for industry and commerce at the place where the branch is located, and file with the tourism administrative department at the place where the branch is located within 3 working days from the date of establishment registration.  
The establishment of a branch of a travel agency shall not be subject to geographical restrictions. The business scope of the branch shall not exceed the business scope of the travel agency that establishes the branch.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　旅行社设立专门招徕旅游者、提供旅游咨询的服务网点(以下简称旅行社服务网点)应当依法向工商行政管理部门办理设立登记手续，并向所在地的旅游行政管理部门备案。  
旅行社服务网点应当接受旅行社的统一管理，不得从事招徕、咨询以外的活动。

**Article 11** A travel agency that establishes a service outlet specifically for soliciting tourists and providing tourism consultation (hereinafter referred to as a travel agency service outlet) shall handle the establishment registration procedures with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law, and file with the local tourism administrative department.  
A travel agency service outlet shall accept the unified management of the travel agency, and shall not engage in activities other than solicitation and consultation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　旅行社变更名称、经营场所、法定代表人等登记事项或者终止经营的，应当到工商行政管理部门办理相应的变更登记或者注销登记，并在登记办理完毕之日起10个工作日内，向原许可的旅游行政管理部门备案，换领或者交回旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 12** If a travel agency changes its name, business premises, legal representative, or other registered items, or terminates its business, it shall go through the corresponding change registration or cancellation registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce, and within 10 working days from the date of completion of the registration, file with the original licensing tourism administrative department, and exchange for a new license or return the travel agency business permit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　旅行社应当自取得旅行社业务经营许可证之日起3个工作日内，在国务院旅游行政主管部门指定的银行开设专门的质量保证金账户，存入质量保证金，或者向作出许可的旅游行政管理部门提交依法取得的担保额度不低于相应质量保证金数额的银行担保。  
经营国内旅游业务和入境旅游业务的旅行社，应当存入质量保证金20万元；经营出境旅游业务的旅行社，应当增存质量保证金120万元。  
质量保证金的利息属于旅行社所有。

**Article 13** A travel agency shall, within 3 working days from the date of obtaining the travel agency business permit, open a special quality guarantee deposit account at a bank designated by the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council, and deposit the quality guarantee deposit, or submit to the tourism administrative department that granted the permit a bank guarantee legally obtained with a guarantee amount not less than the corresponding amount of the quality guarantee deposit.  
A travel agency operating domestic tourism business and inbound tourism business shall deposit a quality guarantee deposit of 200,000 yuan; a travel agency operating outbound tourism business shall additionally deposit a quality guarantee deposit of 1.2 million yuan.  
The interest on the quality guarantee deposit shall belong to the travel agency.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　旅行社每设立一个经营国内旅游业务和入境旅游业务的分社，应当向其质量保证金账户增存5万元；每设立一个经营出境旅游业务的分社，应当向其质量保证金账户增存30万元。

**Article 14** For each branch that a travel agency establishes for operating domestic tourism business and inbound tourism business, it shall additionally deposit 50,000 yuan into its quality guarantee deposit account; for each branch established for operating outbound tourism business, it shall additionally deposit 300,000 yuan into its quality guarantee deposit account.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　有下列情形之一的，旅游行政管理部门可以使用旅行社的质量保证金：  
(一)旅行社违反旅游合同约定，侵害旅游者合法权益，经旅游行政管理部门查证属实的；  
(二)旅行社因解散、破产或者其他原因造成旅游者预交旅游费用损失的。

**Article 15** Under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department may use the quality guarantee deposit of the travel agency:  
(1) The travel agency violates the provisions of the tourism contract and infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, which has been verified by the tourism administrative department;  
(2) The travel agency causes loss of prepaid tourism expenses of tourists due to dissolution, bankruptcy, or other reasons.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院判决、裁定及其他生效法律文书认定旅行社损害旅游者合法权益，旅行社拒绝或者无力赔偿的，人民法院可以从旅行社的质量保证金账户上划拨赔偿款。

**Article 16** If a judgment, ruling, or other effective legal document of a People's Court finds that a travel agency has damaged the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, and the travel agency refuses or is unable to pay compensation, the People's Court may transfer the compensation from the travel agency's quality guarantee deposit account.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　旅行社自交纳或者补足质量保证金之日起三年内未因侵害旅游者合法权益受到行政机关罚款以上处罚的，旅游行政管理部门应当将旅行社质量保证金的交存数额降低50%，并向社会公告。旅行社可凭省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政管理部门出具的凭证减少其质量保证金。

**Article 17** If a travel agency has not been subjected to a penalty of a fine or above by an administrative organ for infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of tourists within three years from the date of paying or replenishing the quality guarantee deposit, the tourism administrative department shall reduce the amount of the quality guarantee deposit to be deposited by the travel agency by 50% and make a public announcement. The travel agency may reduce its quality guarantee deposit on the strength of the certificate issued by the tourism administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　旅行社在旅游行政管理部门使用质量保证金赔偿旅游者的损失，或者依法减少质量保证金后，因侵害旅游者合法权益受到行政机关罚款以上处罚的，应当在收到旅游行政管理部门补交质量保证金的通知之日起5个工作日内补足质量保证金。

**Article 18** If a travel agency, after the tourism administrative department uses the quality guarantee deposit to compensate tourists for losses, or after it lawfully reduces the quality guarantee deposit, is subjected to a penalty of a fine or above by an administrative organ for infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, it shall replenish the quality guarantee deposit within 5 working days from the date of receiving the notice from the tourism administrative department to replenish the quality guarantee deposit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　旅行社不再从事旅游业务的，凭旅游行政管理部门出具的凭证，向银行取回质量保证金。

**Article 19** If a travel agency no longer engages in tourism business, it may retrieve the quality guarantee deposit from the bank on the strength of the certificate issued by the tourism administrative department.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　质量保证金存缴、使用的具体管理办法由国务院旅游行政主管部门和国务院财政部门会同有关部门另行制定。

**Article 20** The specific measures for the management of the deposit and use of the quality guarantee deposit shall be separately formulated by the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council and the finance department under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

### 第三章　外商投资旅行社

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外商投资旅行社适用本章规定；本章没有规定的，适用本条例其他有关规定。

**Article 21** Travel agencies with foreign investment shall be subject to the provisions of this Chapter; matters not covered in this Chapter shall be subject to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　外商投资企业申请经营旅行社业务，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政管理部门提出申请，并提交符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定条件的相关证明文件。省、自治区、直辖市旅游行政管理部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内审查完毕。予以许可的，颁发旅行社业务经营许可证；不予许可的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
设立外商投资旅行社，还应当遵守有关外商投资的法律、法规。

**Article 22** If a foreign-invested enterprise applies to operate travel agency business, it shall submit an application to the tourism administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and submit the relevant certifying documents that meet the conditions specified in Article 6 of these Regulations. The tourism administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the examination within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application. If it grants permission, it shall issue a travel agency business permit; if it does not grant permission, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
The establishment of a travel agency with foreign investment shall also comply with the laws and regulations on foreign investment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　外商投资旅行社不得经营中国内地居民出国旅游业务以及赴香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区旅游的业务，但是国务院决定或者我国签署的自由贸易协定和内地与香港、澳门关于建立更紧密经贸关系的安排另有规定的除外。

**Article 23** A travel agency with foreign investment shall not operate the business of outbound tourism for residents of China's mainland or the business of tourism to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region, unless otherwise provided by the decisions of the State Council or the free trade agreements signed by China and the Closer Economic Partnership Arrangements between the mainland and Hong Kong and Macao.

### 第四章　旅行社经营

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　旅行社向旅游者提供的旅游服务信息必须真实可靠，不得作虚假宣传。

**Article 24** The tourism service information provided by a travel agency to tourists must be true and reliable, and shall not make false publicity.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营出境旅游业务的旅行社不得组织旅游者到国务院旅游行政主管部门公布的中国公民出境旅游目的地之外的国家和地区旅游。

**Article 25** A travel agency operating outbound tourism business shall not organize tourists to travel to countries and regions other than the outbound tourism destinations for Chinese citizens published by the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　旅行社为旅游者安排或者介绍的旅游活动不得含有违反有关法律、法规规定的内容。

**Article 26** The tourism activities arranged or introduced by a travel agency for tourists shall not contain content that violates the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　旅行社不得以低于旅游成本的报价招徕旅游者。未经旅游者同意，旅行社不得在旅游合同约定之外提供其他有偿服务。

**Article 27** A travel agency shall not solicit tourists with a quotation lower than the cost of tourism. Without the consent of the tourist, a travel agency shall not provide other paid services beyond the terms of the tourism contract.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　旅行社为旅游者提供服务，应当与旅游者签订旅游合同并载明下列事项：  
(一)旅行社的名称及其经营范围、地址、联系电话和旅行社业务经营许可证编号；  
(二)旅行社经办人的姓名、联系电话；  
(三)签约地点和日期；  
(四)旅游行程的出发地、途经地和目的地；  
(五)旅游行程中交通、住宿、餐饮服务安排及其标准；  
(六)旅行社统一安排的游览项目的具体内容及时间；  
(七)旅游者自由活动的时间和次数；  
(八)旅游者应当交纳的旅游费用及交纳方式；  
(九)旅行社安排的购物次数、停留时间及购物场所的名称；  
(十)需要旅游者另行付费的游览项目及价格；  
(十一)解除或者变更合同的条件和提前通知的期限；  
(十二)违反合同的纠纷解决机制及应当承担的责任；  
(十三)旅游服务监督、投诉电话；  
(十四)双方协商一致的其他内容。

**Article 28** When providing services to tourists, a travel agency shall enter into a tourism contract with the tourist, which shall specify the following items:  
(1) the name, business scope, address, contact telephone number, and the number of the travel agency business permit;  
(2) the name and contact telephone number of the handling person of the travel agency;  
(3) the place and date of signing the contract;  
(4) the place of departure, places of transit, and the destination of the tourism itinerary;  
(5) the arrangements and standards for transportation, accommodation, and catering services during the tourism itinerary;  
(6) the specific content and time of the sightseeing items uniformly arranged by the travel agency;  
(7) the time and frequency of free activities for tourists;  
(8) the tourism expenses to be paid by the tourist and the method of payment;  
(9) the number of shopping trips arranged by the travel agency, the duration of stay, and the names of the shopping places;  
(10) the sightseeing items that require additional payment by the tourist and their prices;  
(11) the conditions for rescission or modification of the contract and the period for advance notice;  
(12) the dispute resolution mechanism for breach of contract and the liability to be borne;  
(13) the supervision and complaint telephone numbers for tourism services;  
(14) other contents agreed upon by both parties through consultation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　旅行社在与旅游者签订旅游合同时，应当对旅游合同的具体内容作出真实、准确、完整的说明。  
旅行社和旅游者签订的旅游合同约定不明确或者对格式条款的理解发生争议的，应当按照通常理解予以解释；对格式条款有两种以上解释的，应当作出有利于旅游者的解释；格式条款和非格式条款不一致的，应当采用非格式条款。

**Article 29** When a travel agency enters into a tourism contract with a tourist, it shall make a true, accurate, and complete explanation of the specific content of the tourism contract.  
If the terms of the tourism contract entered into between the travel agency and the tourist are unclear or there is a dispute over the understanding of standard terms, they shall be interpreted in accordance with the common understanding; if there are two or more interpretations of standard terms, the interpretation favorable to the tourist shall prevail; if the standard terms are inconsistent with the non-standard terms, the non-standard terms shall prevail.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　旅行社组织中国内地居民出境旅游的，应当为旅游团队安排领队全程陪同。

**Article 30** If a travel agency organizes outbound tourism for residents of China's mainland, it shall arrange a tour leader to accompany the tour group throughout the entire journey.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　旅行社为接待旅游者委派的导游人员，应当持有国家规定的导游证。  
取得出境旅游业务经营许可的旅行社为组织旅游者出境旅游委派的领队，应当取得导游证，具有相应的学历、语言能力和旅游从业经历，并与委派其从事领队业务的旅行社订立劳动合同。旅行社应当将本单位领队名单报所在地设区的市级旅游行政管理部门备案。

**Article 31** The tour guide assigned by a travel agency to receive tourists shall hold a tour guide certificate as prescribed by the state.  
The tour leader assigned by a travel agency that has obtained the permit for outbound tourism business to organize tourists for outbound tourism shall obtain a tour guide certificate, have the corresponding educational background, language ability, and tourism work experience, and enter into a labor contract with the travel agency that assigns it to engage in the tour leader business. The travel agency shall report the list of its tour leaders to the local tourism administrative department at the level of a city divided into districts for the record.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　旅行社聘用导游人员、领队人员应当依法签订劳动合同，并向其支付不低于当地最低工资标准的报酬。

**Article 32** When a travel agency employs tour guides and tour leaders, it shall enter into labor contracts with them according to law and pay them remuneration not lower than the local minimum wage standard.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　旅行社及其委派的导游人员和领队人员不得有下列行为：  
(一)拒绝履行旅游合同约定的义务；  
(二)非因不可抗力改变旅游合同安排的行程；  
(三)欺骗、胁迫旅游者购物或者参加需要另行付费的游览项目。

**Article 33** A travel agency and its assigned tour guides and tour leaders shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) refusing to perform the obligations stipulated in the tourism contract;  
(2) changing the itinerary arranged in the tourism contract without force majeure;  
(3) deceiving or coercing tourists into shopping or participating in sightseeing items that require additional payment.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　旅行社不得要求导游人员和领队人员接待不支付接待和服务费用或者支付的费用低于接待和服务成本的旅游团队，不得要求导游人员和领队人员承担接待旅游团队的相关费用。

**Article 34** A travel agency shall not require tour guides and tour leaders to receive tour groups that do not pay reception and service fees or pay fees lower than the reception and service costs, and shall not require tour guides and tour leaders to bear the relevant expenses for receiving tour groups.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　旅行社违反旅游合同约定，造成旅游者合法权益受到损害的，应当采取必要的补救措施，并及时报告旅游行政管理部门。

**Article 35** If a travel agency violates the provisions of the tourism contract and causes damage to the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, it shall take necessary remedial measures and promptly report to the tourism administrative department.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　旅行社需要对旅游业务作出委托的，应当委托给具有相应资质的旅行社，征得旅游者的同意，并与接受委托的旅行社就接待旅游者的事宜签订委托合同，确定接待旅游者的各项服务安排及其标准，约定双方的权利、义务。

**Article 36** If a travel agency needs to entrust tourism business to another, it shall entrust it to a travel agency with corresponding qualifications, obtain the consent of the tourists, and enter into an entrustment contract with the entrusted travel agency on matters concerning the reception of tourists, determine various service arrangements and their standards for receiving tourists, and agree on the rights and obligations of both parties.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　旅行社将旅游业务委托给其他旅行社的，应当向接受委托的旅行社支付不低于接待和服务成本的费用；接受委托的旅行社不得接待不支付或者不足额支付接待和服务费用的旅游团队。  
接受委托的旅行社违约，造成旅游者合法权益受到损害的，作出委托的旅行社应当承担相应的赔偿责任。作出委托的旅行社赔偿后，可以向接受委托的旅行社追偿。  
接受委托的旅行社故意或者重大过失造成旅游者合法权益损害的，应当承担连带责任。

**Article 37** If a travel agency entrusts tourism business to another travel agency, it shall pay the entrusted travel agency fees not lower than the reception and service costs; the entrusted travel agency shall not receive tour groups that do not pay or do not pay in full the reception and service fees.  
If the entrusted travel agency breaches the contract and causes damage to the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, the entrusting travel agency shall bear the corresponding compensation liability. After paying compensation, the entrusting travel agency may seek recourse against the entrusted travel agency.  
If the entrusted travel agency causes damage to the legitimate rights and interests of tourists due to willful misconduct or gross negligence, it shall bear joint and several liability.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　旅行社应当投保旅行社责任险。旅行社责任险的具体方案由国务院旅游行政主管部门会同国务院保险监督管理机构另行制定。

**Article 38** A travel agency shall take out travel agency liability insurance. The specific plan for travel agency liability insurance shall be separately formulated by the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council together with the insurance regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　旅行社对可能危及旅游者人身、财产安全的事项，应当向旅游者作出真实的说明和明确的警示，并采取防止危害发生的必要措施。  
发生危及旅游者人身安全的情形的，旅行社及其委派的导游人员、领队人员应当采取必要的处置措施并及时报告旅游行政管理部门；在境外发生的，还应当及时报告中华人民共和国驻该国使领馆、相关驻外机构、当地警方。

**Article 39** A travel agency shall make true explanations and clear warnings to tourists regarding matters that may endanger the personal or property safety of tourists, and take necessary measures to prevent the occurrence of harm.  
If a situation endangering the personal safety of tourists occurs, the travel agency and its assigned tour guides and tour leaders shall take necessary handling measures and promptly report to the tourism administrative department; if it occurs abroad, they shall also promptly report to the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country, relevant overseas institutions, and the local police.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　旅游者在境外滞留不归的，旅行社委派的领队人员应当及时向旅行社和中华人民共和国驻该国使领馆、相关驻外机构报告。旅行社接到报告后应当及时向旅游行政管理部门和公安机关报告，并协助提供非法滞留者的信息。  
旅行社接待入境旅游发生旅游者非法滞留我国境内的，应当及时向旅游行政管理部门、公安机关和外事部门报告，并协助提供非法滞留者的信息。

**Article 40** If a tourist overstays abroad without returning, the tour leader assigned by the travel agency shall promptly report to the travel agency and the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country and relevant overseas institutions. Upon receiving the report, the travel agency shall promptly report to the tourism administrative department and the public security organ, and assist in providing information on the illegal overstayer.  
If, during the reception of inbound tourism by a travel agency, a tourist illegally overstays within China's territory, the travel agency shall promptly report to the tourism administrative department, the public security organ, and the foreign affairs department, and assist in providing information on the illegal overstayer.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　旅游、工商、价格、商务、外汇等有关部门应当依法加强对旅行社的监督管理，发现违法行为，应当及时予以处理。

**Article 41** The relevant departments of tourism, industry and commerce, price, commerce, foreign exchange, etc. shall strengthen the supervision and administration of travel agencies according to law, and shall promptly handle any illegal acts discovered.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　旅游、工商、价格等行政管理部门应当及时向社会公告监督检查的情况。公告的内容包括旅行社业务经营许可证的颁发、变更、吊销、注销情况，旅行社的违法经营行为以及旅行社的诚信记录、旅游者投诉信息等。

**Article 42** The administrative departments of tourism, industry and commerce, price, etc. shall promptly make public announcements on the situation of supervision and inspection. The contents of the announcement shall include the issuance, modification, revocation, and cancellation of travel agency business permits, the illegal business activities of travel agencies, as well as the integrity records of travel agencies, tourist complaint information, etc.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　旅行社损害旅游者合法权益的，旅游者可以向旅游行政管理部门、工商行政管理部门、价格主管部门、商务主管部门或者外汇管理部门投诉，接到投诉的部门应当按照其职责权限及时调查处理，并将调查处理的有关情况告知旅游者。

**Article 43** If a travel agency damages the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, the tourist may lodge a complaint with the tourism administrative department, the administrative department for industry and commerce, the competent department of price, the competent department of commerce, or the foreign exchange administration department. The department that receives the complaint shall promptly investigate and handle it according to its functions and powers, and inform the tourist of the relevant circumstances of the investigation and handling.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　旅行社及其分社应当接受旅游行政管理部门对其旅游合同、服务质量、旅游安全、财务账簿等情况的监督检查，并按照国家有关规定向旅游行政管理部门报送经营和财务信息等统计资料。

**Article 44** A travel agency and its branches shall accept the supervision and inspection by the tourism administrative department on their tourism contracts, service quality, tourism safety, financial accounts, etc., and submit statistical data such as operational and financial information to the tourism administrative department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　旅游、工商、价格、商务、外汇等有关部门工作人员不得接受旅行社的任何馈赠，不得参加由旅行社支付费用的购物活动或者游览项目，不得通过旅行社为自己、亲友或者其他个人、组织牟取私利。

**Article 45** The staff members of the relevant departments of tourism, industry and commerce, price, commerce, foreign exchange, etc. shall not accept any gifts from travel agencies, shall not participate in shopping activities or sightseeing items paid for by travel agencies, and shall not seek personal gains for themselves, relatives, friends, or other individuals or organizations through travel agencies.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政管理部门或者工商行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法所得不足10万元或者没有违法所得的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未取得相应的旅行社业务经营许可，经营国内旅游业务、入境旅游业务、出境旅游业务的；  
(二)分社超出设立分社的旅行社的经营范围经营旅游业务的；  
(三)旅行社服务网点从事招徕、咨询以外的旅行社业务经营活动的。

**Article 46** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order correction, confiscate illegal gains, and if the illegal gains are more than 100,000 yuan, also impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan:  
(1) operating domestic tourism business, inbound tourism business, or outbound tourism business without obtaining the corresponding travel agency business permit;  
(2) a branch operating tourism business beyond the business scope of the travel agency that established the branch;  
(3) a travel agency service outlet engaging in travel agency business activities other than solicitation and consultation.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　旅行社转让、出租、出借旅行社业务经营许可证的，由旅游行政管理部门责令停业整顿1个月至3个月，并没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。受让或者租借旅行社业务经营许可证的，由旅游行政管理部门责令停止非法经营，没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** If a travel agency transfers, leases, or lends its travel agency business permit, the tourism administrative department shall order it to suspend business for rectification for one to three months and confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked. If a party acquires or rents a travel agency business permit, the tourism administrative department shall order it to cease illegal operations, confiscate the illegal gains, and also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社未在规定期限内向其质量保证金账户存入、增存、补足质量保证金或者提交相应的银行担保的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 48** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency fails to deposit, additionally deposit, or replenish the quality guarantee deposit into its quality guarantee deposit account within the prescribed time limit, or fails to submit the corresponding bank guarantee, the tourism administrative department shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社不投保旅行社责任险的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 49** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency fails to take out travel agency liability insurance, the tourism administrative department shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以下的罚款：  
(一)变更名称、经营场所、法定代表人等登记事项或者终止经营，未在规定期限内向原许可的旅游行政管理部门备案，换领或者交回旅行社业务经营许可证的；  
(二)设立分社未在规定期限内向分社所在地旅游行政管理部门备案的；  
(三)不按照国家有关规定向旅游行政管理部门报送经营和财务信息等统计资料的。

**Article 50** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency falls under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) changing the name, business premises, legal representative, or other registered items, or terminating business, without filing with the original licensing tourism administrative department, or exchanging for a new license or returning the travel agency business permit within the prescribed time limit;  
(2) establishing a branch without filing with the local tourism administrative department within the prescribed time limit;  
(3) failing to submit statistical data such as operational and financial information to the tourism administrative department in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例的规定，外商投资旅行社经营中国内地居民出国旅游业务以及赴香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区旅游业务，或者经营出境旅游业务的旅行社组织旅游者到国务院旅游行政主管部门公布的中国公民出境旅游目的地之外的国家和地区旅游的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法所得不足10万元或者没有违法所得的，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 51** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency with foreign investment operates the business of outbound tourism for residents of China's mainland or the business of tourism to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region, or if a travel agency operating outbound tourism business organizes tourists to travel to countries and regions other than the outbound tourism destinations for Chinese citizens published by the competent department of tourism administration under the State Council, the tourism administrative department shall order correction, confiscate illegal gains, and if the illegal gains are more than 100,000 yuan, also impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, also impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社为旅游者安排或者介绍的旅游活动含有违反有关法律、法规规定的内容的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 52** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if the tourism activities arranged or introduced by a travel agency for tourists contain content that violates the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, the tourism administrative department shall order correction, confiscate illegal gains, and also impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社向旅游者提供的旅游服务信息含有虚假内容或者作虚假宣传的，由工商行政管理部门依法给予处罚。  
违反本条例的规定，旅行社以低于旅游成本的报价招徕旅游者的，由价格主管部门依法给予处罚。

**Article 53** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if the tourism service information provided by a travel agency to tourists contains false content or makes false publicity, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a penalty according to law.  
In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency solicits tourists with a quotation lower than the cost of tourism, the competent department of price shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社未经旅游者同意在旅游合同约定之外提供其他有偿服务的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency provides other paid services beyond the terms of the tourism contract without the consent of the tourist, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月：  
(一)未与旅游者签订旅游合同；  
(二)与旅游者签订的旅游合同未载明[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)规定的事项；  
(三)未取得旅游者同意，将旅游业务委托给其他旅行社；  
(四)将旅游业务委托给不具有相应资质的旅行社；  
(五)未与接受委托的旅行社就接待旅游者的事宜签订委托合同。

**Article 55** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency falls under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of business for rectification for one to three months:  
(1) failing to enter into a tourism contract with the tourist;  
(2) the tourism contract entered into with the tourist does not specify the items required by Article 28 of these Regulations;  
(3) entrusting tourism business to another travel agency without obtaining the consent of the tourists;  
(4) entrusting tourism business to a travel agency without corresponding qualifications;  
(5) failing to enter into an entrustment contract with the entrusted travel agency on matters concerning the reception of tourists.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社组织中国内地居民出境旅游，不为旅游团队安排领队全程陪同的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿1个月至3个月。

**Article 56** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency organizes outbound tourism for residents of China's mainland but does not arrange a tour leader to accompany the tour group throughout the entire journey, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if it refuses to correct, it shall order suspension of business for rectification for one to three months.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社委派的导游人员未持有国家规定的导游证或者委派的领队人员不具备规定的领队条件的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，对旅行社处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 57** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if the tour guide assigned by a travel agency does not hold a tour guide certificate as prescribed by the state, or the tour leader assigned does not meet the required conditions for a tour leader, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the travel agency.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社不向其聘用的导游人员、领队人员支付报酬，或者所支付的报酬低于当地最低工资标准的，按照《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》的有关规定处理。

**Article 58** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency fails to pay remuneration to its employed tour guides and tour leaders, or the remuneration paid is lower than the local minimum wage standard, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，对旅行社，由旅游行政管理部门或者工商行政管理部门责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；对导游人员、领队人员，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证、导游证：  
(一)拒不履行旅游合同约定的义务的；  
(二)非因不可抗力改变旅游合同安排的行程的；  
(三)欺骗、胁迫旅游者购物或者参加需要另行付费的游览项目的。

**Article 59** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, with respect to the travel agency, the tourism administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; with respect to the tour guide or tour leader, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency business permit or the tour guide certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) refusing to perform the obligations stipulated in the tourism contract;  
(2) changing the itinerary arranged in the tourism contract without force majeure;  
(3) deceiving or coercing tourists into shopping or participating in sightseeing items that require additional payment.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社要求导游人员和领队人员接待不支付接待和服务费用、支付的费用低于接待和服务成本的旅游团队，或者要求导游人员和领队人员承担接待旅游团队的相关费用的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 60** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency requires tour guides and tour leaders to receive tour groups that do not pay reception and service fees or pay fees lower than the reception and service costs, or requires tour guides and tour leaders to bear the relevant expenses for receiving tour groups, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　旅行社违反旅游合同约定，造成旅游者合法权益受到损害，不采取必要的补救措施的，由旅游行政管理部门或者工商行政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由旅游行政管理部门吊销旅行社业务经营许可证。

**Article 61** If a travel agency violates the provisions of the tourism contract, causes damage to the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, and fails to take necessary remedial measures, the tourism administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the tourism administrative department shall revoke the travel agency business permit.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，停业整顿1个月至3个月；情节严重的，吊销旅行社业务经营许可证：  
(一)旅行社不向接受委托的旅行社支付接待和服务费用的；  
(二)旅行社向接受委托的旅行社支付的费用低于接待和服务成本的；  
(三)接受委托的旅行社接待不支付或者不足额支付接待和服务费用的旅游团队的。

**Article 62** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department shall order correction and suspend business for rectification for one to three months; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency business permit shall be revoked:  
(1) a travel agency fails to pay reception and service fees to the entrusted travel agency;  
(2) a travel agency pays fees lower than the reception and service costs to the entrusted travel agency;  
(3) the entrusted travel agency receives tour groups that do not pay or do not pay in full the reception and service fees.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，旅行社及其委派的导游人员、领队人员有下列情形之一的，由旅游行政管理部门责令改正，对旅行社处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对导游人员、领队人员处4000元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令旅行社停业整顿1个月至3个月，或者吊销旅行社业务经营许可证、导游证：  
(一)发生危及旅游者人身安全的情形，未采取必要的处置措施并及时报告的；  
(二)旅行社组织出境旅游的旅游者非法滞留境外，旅行社未及时报告并协助提供非法滞留者信息的；  
(三)旅行社接待入境旅游的旅游者非法滞留境内，旅行社未及时报告并协助提供非法滞留者信息的。

**Article 63** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if a travel agency and its assigned tour guides and tour leaders fall under any of the following circumstances, the tourism administrative department shall order correction, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the travel agency, and impose a fine of not less than 4,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the tour guide or tour leader; if the circumstances are serious, the travel agency shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification for one to three months, or the travel agency business permit or the tour guide certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) when a situation endangering the personal safety of tourists occurs, failing to take necessary handling measures and promptly report;  
(2) if a tourist organized for outbound tourism by the travel agency illegally overstays abroad, the travel agency fails to promptly report and assist in providing information on the illegal overstayer;  
(3) if a tourist received for inbound tourism by the travel agency illegally overstays within China, the travel agency fails to promptly report and assist in providing information on the illegal overstayer.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　因妨害国(边)境管理受到刑事处罚的，在刑罚执行完毕之日起五年内不得从事旅行社业务经营活动；旅行社被吊销旅行社业务经营许可的，其主要负责人在旅行社业务经营许可被吊销之日起五年内不得担任任何旅行社的主要负责人。

**Article 64** A person who has been subjected to criminal punishment for impairing the administration of national (border) frontier shall not engage in travel agency business activities within five years from the date of completion of the criminal penalty; if a travel agency has its business permit revoked, its principal responsible person shall not serve as the principal responsible person of any travel agency within five years from the date of revocation of the travel agency business permit.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　旅行社违反本条例的规定，损害旅游者合法权益的，应当承担相应的民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** If a travel agency violates the provisions of these Regulations and damages the legitimate rights and interests of tourists, it shall bear the corresponding civil liability; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，旅游行政管理部门或者其他有关部门及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)发现违法行为不及时予以处理的；  
(二)未及时公告对旅行社的监督检查情况的；  
(三)未及时处理旅游者投诉并将调查处理的有关情况告知旅游者的；  
(四)接受旅行社的馈赠的；  
(五)参加由旅行社支付费用的购物活动或者游览项目的；  
(六)通过旅行社为自己、亲友或者其他个人、组织牟取私利的。

**Article 66** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, if the tourism administrative department or other relevant departments and their staff members fall under any of the following circumstances, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) discovering illegal acts but failing to handle them promptly;  
(2) failing to promptly announce the situation of supervision and inspection of travel agencies;  
(3) failing to promptly handle tourist complaints and inform tourists of the relevant circumstances of investigation and handling;  
(4) accepting gifts from travel agencies;  
(5) participating in shopping activities or sightseeing items paid for by travel agencies;  
(6) seeking personal gains for themselves, relatives, friends, or other individuals or organizations through travel agencies.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的投资者在内地投资设立的旅行社，参照适用本条例。

**Article 67** Travel agencies established in the mainland by investors from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region shall apply these Regulations mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例自2009年5月1日起施行。1996年10月15日国务院发布的《旅行社管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 68** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2009. The Regulations on the Administration of Travel Agencies promulgated by the State Council on October 15, 1996 shall be abolished simultaneously.
