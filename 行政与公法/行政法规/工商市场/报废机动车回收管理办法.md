# 报废机动车回收管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-22  
**生效日期**：2019-06-01

> (2019年4月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第715号公布　自2019年6月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范报废机动车回收活动，保护环境，促进循环经济发展，保障道路交通安全，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法所称报废机动车，是指根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的规定应当报废的机动车。 不属于《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》规定的应当报废的机动车，机动车所有人自愿作报废处理的，依照本办法的规定执行。 第三条　国家鼓励特定领域的老旧机动车提前报废更新，具体办法由国务院有关部门另行制定。 第四条　国务院负责报废机动车回收管理的部门主管全国报废机动车回收(含拆解，下同)监督管理工作，国务院公安、生态环境、工业和信息化、交通运输、市场监督管理等部门在各自的职责范围内负责报废机动车回收有关的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门对本行政区域内报废机动车回收活动实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府公安、生态环境、工业和信息化、交通运输、市场监督管理等部门在各自的职责范围内对本行政区域内报废机动车回收活动实施有关的监督管理。 第五条　国家对报废机动车回收企业实行资质认定制度。未经资质认定，任何单位或者个人不得从事报废机动车回收活动。 国家鼓励机动车生产企业从事报废机动车回收活动。机动车生产企业按照国家有关规定承担生产者责任。 第六条　取得报废机动车回收资质认定，应当具备下列条件： (一)具有企业法人资格； (二)具有符合环境保护等有关法律、法规和强制性标准要求的存储、拆解场地，拆解设备、设施以及拆解操作规范； (三)具有与报废机动车拆解活动相适应的专业技术人员。 第七条　拟从事报废机动车回收活动的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当依法进行审查，对符合条件的，颁发资质认定书；对不符合条件的，不予资质认定并书面说明理由。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当充分利用计算机网络等先进技术手段，推行网上申请、网上受理等方式，为申请人提供便利条件。申请人可以在网上提出申请。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范报废机动车回收活动，保护环境，促进循环经济发展，保障道路交通安全，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of regulating the recycling activities of end-of-life motor vehicles, protecting the environment, promoting the development of circular economy, and ensuring road traffic safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称报废机动车，是指根据《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的规定应当报废的机动车。  
不属于《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》规定的应当报废的机动车，机动车所有人自愿作报废处理的，依照本办法的规定执行。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Measures, "end-of-life motor vehicles" refer to motor vehicles that shall be scrapped pursuant to the provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Motor vehicles that are not subject to mandatory scrapping under the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China but are voluntarily scrapped by the owner shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励特定领域的老旧机动车提前报废更新，具体办法由国务院有关部门另行制定。

**Article 3** The State encourages early scrapping and replacement of old motor vehicles in specific areas. Specific measures shall be separately formulated by relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院负责报废机动车回收管理的部门主管全国报废机动车回收(含拆解，下同)监督管理工作，国务院公安、生态环境、工业和信息化、交通运输、市场监督管理等部门在各自的职责范围内负责报废机动车回收有关的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门对本行政区域内报废机动车回收活动实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府公安、生态环境、工业和信息化、交通运输、市场监督管理等部门在各自的职责范围内对本行政区域内报废机动车回收活动实施有关的监督管理。

**Article 4** The department of the State Council responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall be in charge of the supervision and management of the recycling (including dismantling, the same below) of end-of-life motor vehicles nationwide. The public security, ecological environment, industry and information technology, transport, market supervision and administration departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the supervision and management related to the recycling of end-of-life motor vehicles.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the supervision and management of the recycling activities of end-of-life motor vehicles within their respective administrative regions. The public security, ecological environment, industry and information technology, transport, market supervision and administration departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, implement the supervision and management related to the recycling activities of end-of-life motor vehicles within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对报废机动车回收企业实行资质认定制度。未经资质认定，任何单位或者个人不得从事报废机动车回收活动。  
国家鼓励机动车生产企业从事报废机动车回收活动。机动车生产企业按照国家有关规定承担生产者责任。

**Article 5** The State implements a qualification accreditation system for enterprises engaged in the recycling of end-of-life motor vehicles. Without qualification accreditation, no unit or individual may engage in the recycling of end-of-life motor vehicles.  
The State encourages motor vehicle manufacturers to engage in the recycling of end-of-life motor vehicles. Motor vehicle manufacturers shall assume producer responsibilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　取得报废机动车回收资质认定，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有企业法人资格；  
(二)具有符合环境保护等有关法律、法规和强制性标准要求的存储、拆解场地，拆解设备、设施以及拆解操作规范；  
(三)具有与报废机动车拆解活动相适应的专业技术人员。

**Article 6** To obtain the qualification accreditation for recycling end-of-life motor vehicles, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) having the status of a legal person enterprise;  
(2) having storage and dismantling sites, dismantling equipment and facilities, and dismantling operation specifications that meet the requirements of environmental protection and other relevant laws, regulations and mandatory standards;  
(3) having professional technical personnel suitable for the dismantling activities of end-of-life motor vehicles.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拟从事报废机动车回收活动的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门提出申请。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当依法进行审查，对符合条件的，颁发资质认定书；对不符合条件的，不予资质认定并书面说明理由。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当充分利用计算机网络等先进技术手段，推行网上申请、网上受理等方式，为申请人提供便利条件。申请人可以在网上提出申请。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当将本行政区域内取得资质认定的报废机动车回收企业名单及时向社会公布。

**Article 7** Any person intending to engage in the recycling of end-of-life motor vehicles shall submit an application to the department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling. The said department shall conduct review according to law, and issue a qualification accreditation certificate to those that meet the conditions; for those that do not meet the conditions, the department shall refuse to grant the qualification accreditation and provide reasons in writing.  
The department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall make full use of advanced technical means such as computer networks, promote online applications and online acceptance, so as to provide convenience for applicants. Applicants may submit applications online.  
The department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall timely publish the list of enterprises that have obtained qualification accreditation for recycling end-of-life motor vehicles within its administrative region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位或者个人不得要求机动车所有人将报废机动车交售给指定的报废机动车回收企业。

**Article 8** No unit or individual may require the owner of an end-of-life motor vehicle to sell such vehicle to a designated end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　报废机动车回收企业对回收的报废机动车，应当向机动车所有人出具《报废机动车回收证明》，收回机动车登记证书、号牌、行驶证，并按照国家有关规定及时向公安机关交通管理部门办理注销登记，将注销证明转交机动车所有人。  
《报废机动车回收证明》样式由国务院负责报废机动车回收管理的部门规定。任何单位或者个人不得买卖或者伪造、变造《报废机动车回收证明》。

**Article 9** For an end-of-life motor vehicle recovered, the end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise shall issue a "Certificate of Recycling of End-of-Life Motor Vehicle" to the owner, retrieve the motor vehicle registration certificate, license plate, and driving license, and handle the cancellation of registration with the traffic management department of the public security organ in a timely manner in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and transfer the cancellation certificate to the owner.  
The format of the "Certificate of Recycling of End-of-Life Motor Vehicle" shall be prescribed by the department of the State Council responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling. No unit or individual may buy, sell, forge, or alter the "Certificate of Recycling of End-of-Life Motor Vehicle".

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　报废机动车回收企业对回收的报废机动车，应当逐车登记机动车的型号、号牌号码、发动机号码、车辆识别代号等信息；发现回收的报废机动车疑似赃物或者用于盗窃、抢劫等犯罪活动的犯罪工具的，应当及时向公安机关报告。  
报废机动车回收企业不得拆解、改装、拼装、倒卖疑似赃物或者犯罪工具的机动车或者其发动机、方向机、变速器、前后桥、车架(以下统称“五大总成”)和其他零部件。

**Article 10** For an end-of-life motor vehicle recovered, the end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise shall register the model, license plate number, engine number, vehicle identification number, and other information of each vehicle; if it discovers that the recovered end-of-life motor vehicle is suspected of being stolen goods or an instrument used for criminal activities such as theft or robbery, it shall promptly report to the public security organ.  
An end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise shall not dismantle, modify, assemble, or resell motor vehicles that are suspected of being stolen goods or criminal instruments, or their engines, steering gears, transmissions, front and rear axles, frames (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "five major assemblies") or other parts.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　回收的报废机动车必须按照有关规定予以拆解；其中，回收的报废大型客车、货车等营运车辆和校车，应当在公安机关的监督下解体。

**Article 11** The recovered end-of-life motor vehicles must be disassembled in accordance with the relevant provisions; among them, the recovered end-of-life large passenger cars, trucks and other operating vehicles and school buses shall be dismantled under the supervision of the public security organ.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　拆解的报废机动车“五大总成”具备再制造条件的，可以按照国家有关规定出售给具有再制造能力的企业经过再制造予以循环利用；不具备再制造条件的，应当作为废金属，交售给钢铁企业作为冶炼原料。  
拆解的报废机动车“五大总成”以外的零部件符合保障人身和财产安全等强制性国家标准，能够继续使用的，可以出售，但应当标明“报废机动车回用件”。

**Article 12** The "five major assemblies" of disassembled end-of-life motor vehicles that meet the conditions for remanufacturing may be sold to enterprises with remanufacturing capability for recycling through remanufacturing in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; where they do not meet the conditions for remanufacturing, they shall be sold as scrap metal to steel enterprises as smelting raw materials.  
Parts other than the "five major assemblies" of disassembled end-of-life motor vehicles that comply with the mandatory national standards for ensuring personal and property safety and can continue to be used may be sold, but shall be marked as "end-of-life motor vehicle reusable parts".

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当建立报废机动车回收信息系统。报废机动车回收企业应当如实记录本企业回收的报废机动车“五大总成”等主要部件的数量、型号、流向等信息，并上传至报废机动车回收信息系统。  
负责报废机动车回收管理的部门、公安机关应当通过政务信息系统实现信息共享。

**Article 13** The department of the State Council responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall establish an end-of-life motor vehicle recycling information system. End-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprises shall truthfully record the quantity, model, flow direction and other information of the "five major assemblies" and other main components of end-of-life motor vehicles recovered by the enterprise, and upload such information to the end-of-life motor vehicle recycling information system.  
The department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling and the public security organ shall achieve information sharing through the government affairs information system.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　拆解报废机动车，应当遵守环境保护法律、法规和强制性标准，采取有效措施保护环境，不得造成环境污染。

**Article 14** Disassembly of end-of-life motor vehicles shall comply with the environmental protection laws, regulations and mandatory standards, take effective measures to protect the environment, and shall not cause environmental pollution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止任何单位或者个人利用报废机动车“五大总成”和其他零部件拼装机动车，禁止拼装的机动车交易。  
除机动车所有人将报废机动车依法交售给报废机动车回收企业外，禁止报废机动车整车交易。

**Article 15** No unit or individual may assemble a motor vehicle using the "five major assemblies" and other parts of end-of-life motor vehicles, and the trading of assembled motor vehicles is prohibited.  
Except where the motor vehicle owner sells the end-of-life motor vehicle to an end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise according to law, the trading of complete end-of-life motor vehicles is prohibited.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当加强对报废机动车回收企业的监督检查，建立和完善以随机抽查为重点的日常监督检查制度，公布抽查事项目录，明确抽查的依据、频次、方式、内容和程序，随机抽取被检查企业，随机选派检查人员。抽查情况和查处结果应当及时向社会公布。  
在监督检查中发现报废机动车回收企业不具备本办法规定的资质认定条件的，应当责令限期改正；拒不改正或者逾期未改正的，由原发证部门吊销资质认定书。

**Article 16** The department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall strengthen supervision and inspection of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprises, establish and improve a daily supervision and inspection system focusing on random inspections, publish the catalogue of inspection items, specify the basis, frequency, method, content and procedure of inspection, randomly select enterprises to be inspected, and randomly assign inspectors. The inspection results and investigation outcomes shall be promptly announced to the public.  
Where during supervision and inspection it is found that an end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise does not meet the qualification certification conditions specified in these Measures, the enterprise shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it refuses to make corrections or fails to make corrections within the specified time limit, the original issuing department shall revoke the qualification certificate.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当向社会公布本部门的联系方式，方便公众举报违法行为。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责报废机动车回收管理的部门接到举报的，应当及时依法调查处理，并为举报人保密；对实名举报的，负责报废机动车回收管理的部门应当将处理结果告知举报人。

**Article 17** The department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall make public the contact information of the department to facilitate the public to report illegal acts.  
Where the department of the local people's government at or above the county level responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling receives a report, it shall promptly investigate and handle it according to law, and keep the reporter confidential; for a report with real name, the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall inform the reporter of the handling result.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　负责报废机动车回收管理的部门在监督管理工作中发现不属于本部门处理权限的违法行为的，应当及时移交有权处理的部门；有权处理的部门应当及时依法调查处理，并将处理结果告知负责报废机动车回收管理的部门。

**Article 18** Where the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling discovers an illegal act that is not within its handling authority during supervision and administration, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the department with authority to handle it; the department with authority shall promptly investigate and handle it according to law, and inform the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling of the handling result.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　未取得资质认定，擅自从事报废机动车回收活动的，由负责报废机动车回收管理的部门没收非法回收的报废机动车、报废机动车“五大总成”和其他零部件，没收违法所得；违法所得在5万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法所得不足5万元或者没有违法所得的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。对负责报废机动车回收管理的部门没收非法回收的报废机动车、报废机动车“五大总成”和其他零部件，必要时有关主管部门应当予以配合。

**Article 19** Where a person engages in end-of-life motor vehicle recycling activities without obtaining qualification certification, the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling shall confiscate the illegally recovered end-of-life motor vehicles, the "five major assemblies" and other parts of end-of-life motor vehicles, and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed. For the confiscation by the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling of illegally recovered end-of-life motor vehicles, "five major assemblies" and other parts of end-of-life motor vehicles, the relevant competent departments shall provide cooperation when necessary.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚：  
(一)买卖或者伪造、变造《报废机动车回收证明》；  
(二)报废机动车回收企业明知或者应当知道回收的机动车为赃物或者用于盗窃、抢劫等犯罪活动的犯罪工具，未向公安机关报告，擅自拆解、改装、拼装、倒卖该机动车。  
报废机动车回收企业有前款规定情形，情节严重的，由原发证部门吊销资质认定书。

**Article 20** In any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall give a public security penalty according to law:  
(1) buying or selling, or forging or altering the Certificate of Recycling of End-of-Life Motor Vehicles;  
(2) an end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise, knowing or should have known that the recovered motor vehicle is stolen property or a criminal tool used for theft, robbery or other criminal activities, fails to report to the public security organ, and arbitrarily disassembles, modifies, assembles or resells the motor vehicle.  
Where an end-of-life motor vehicle recycling enterprise falls under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, and the circumstances are serious, the original issuing department shall revoke the qualification certificate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　报废机动车回收企业有下列情形之一的，由负责报废机动车回收管理的部门责令改正，没收报废机动车“五大总成”和其他零部件，没收违法所得；违法所得在5万元以上的，并处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法所得不足5万元或者没有违法所得的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿直至由原发证部门吊销资质认定书：  
(一)出售不具备再制造条件的报废机动车“五大总成”；  
(二)出售不能继续使用的报废机动车“五大总成”以外的零部件；  
(三)出售的报废机动车“五大总成”以外的零部件未标明“报废机动车回用件”。

**Article 21** An enterprise recycling end-of-life motor vehicles that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be ordered by the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling to make corrections, the "five major assemblies" and other parts of end-of-life motor vehicles shall be confiscated, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan or there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the enterprise shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification until the original issuing department revokes the qualification certificate:  
(1) selling the "five major assemblies" of end-of-life motor vehicles that do not have the conditions for remanufacturing;  
(2) selling parts other than the "five major assemblies" of end-of-life motor vehicles that cannot be continued to be used;  
(3) failing to mark "reusable parts of end-of-life motor vehicles" on parts other than the "five major assemblies" of end-of-life motor vehicles that are sold.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　报废机动车回收企业对回收的报废机动车，未按照国家有关规定及时向公安机关交通管理部门办理注销登记并将注销证明转交机动车所有人的，由负责报废机动车回收管理的部门责令改正，可以处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
利用报废机动车“五大总成”和其他零部件拼装机动车或者出售报废机动车整车、拼装的机动车的，依照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的规定予以处罚。

**Article 22** If an enterprise recycling end-of-life motor vehicles fails to timely go through the cancellation of registration with the traffic management department of the public security organ and hand over the cancellation certificate to the motor vehicle owner in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, it shall be ordered by the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling to make corrections, and may be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
If a motor vehicle is assembled using the "five major assemblies" and other parts of end-of-life motor vehicles, or if a complete end-of-life motor vehicle or an assembled motor vehicle is sold, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　报废机动车回收企业未如实记录本企业回收的报废机动车“五大总成”等主要部件的数量、型号、流向等信息并上传至报废机动车回收信息系统的，由负责报废机动车回收管理的部门责令改正，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 23** If an enterprise recycling end-of-life motor vehicles fails to truthfully record the quantity, model, flow direction and other information of the "five major assemblies" and other major parts of the end-of-life motor vehicles it recycles and upload them to the end-of-life motor vehicle recycling information system, it shall be ordered by the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling to make corrections, and shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　报废机动车回收企业违反环境保护法律、法规和强制性标准，污染环境的，由生态环境主管部门责令限期改正，并依法予以处罚；拒不改正或者逾期未改正的，由原发证部门吊销资质认定书。

**Article 24** If an enterprise recycling end-of-life motor vehicles violates environmental protection laws, regulations and mandatory standards and pollutes the environment, it shall be ordered by the competent ecological environment department to make corrections within a time limit, and shall be punished according to law; if it refuses to make corrections or fails to make corrections within the time limit, the original issuing department shall revoke the qualification certificate.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　负责报废机动车回收管理的部门和其他有关部门的工作人员在监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 25** If a staff member of the department responsible for the administration of end-of-life motor vehicle recycling or other relevant departments abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in the supervision and administration work, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本办法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** If a violation of these Measures constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　报废新能源机动车回收的特殊事项，另行制定管理规定。  
军队报废机动车的回收管理，依照国家和军队有关规定执行。

**Article 27** Separate administrative provisions shall be formulated for the special matters concerning the recycling of new energy end-of-life motor vehicles.  
The recycling administration of military end-of-life motor vehicles shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the military.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本办法自2019年6月1日起施行。2001年6月16日国务院公布的《报废汽车回收管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 28** These Measures shall come into force as of June 1, 2019. The Measures for the Administration of Recycling of End-of-Life Vehicles promulgated by the State Council on June 16, 2001 shall be abolished simultaneously.
