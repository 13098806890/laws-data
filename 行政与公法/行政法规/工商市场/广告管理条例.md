# 广告管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1987-10-26  
**生效日期**：1987-12-01

> (1987年10月26日国务院发布) 第一条　为了加强广告管理，推动广告事业的发展，有效地利用广告媒介为社会主义建设服务，制定本条例。 第二条　凡通过报刊、广播、电视、电影、路牌、橱窗、印刷品、霓虹灯等媒介或者形式，在中华人民共和国境内刊播、设置、张贴广告，均属本条例管理范围。 第三条　广告内容必须真实、健康、清晰、明白，不得以任何形式欺骗用户和消费者。 第四条　在广告经营活动中，禁止垄断和不正当竞争行为。 第五条　广告的管理机关是国家工商行政管理机关和地方各级工商行政管理机关。 第六条　经营广告业务的单位和个体工商户(以下简称广告经营者)，应当按照本条例和有关法规的规定，向工商行政管理机关申请，分别情况办理审批登记手续： (一)专营广告业务的企业，发给《企业法人营业执照》； (二)兼营广告业务的事业单位，发给《广告经营许可证》； (三)具备经营广告业务能力的个体工商户，发给《营业执照》； (四)兼营广告业务的企业，应当办理经营范围变更登记。 第七条　广告客户申请刊播、设置、张贴的广告，其内容应当在广告客户的经营范围或者国家许可的范围内。 第八条　广告有下列内容之一的，不得刊播、设置、张贴： (一)违反我国法律、法规的； (二)损害我国民族尊严的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强广告管理，推动广告事业的发展，有效地利用广告媒介为社会主义建设服务，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen advertising management, promote the development of the advertising industry, and effectively use advertising media to serve socialist construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡通过报刊、广播、电视、电影、路牌、橱窗、印刷品、霓虹灯等媒介或者形式，在中华人民共和国境内刊播、设置、张贴广告，均属本条例管理范围。

**Article 2** All advertisements published, broadcast, placed, or posted through media or forms such as newspapers, periodicals, radio, television, films, billboards, display windows, printed matter, neon lights, etc., within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall fall within the scope of administration of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　广告内容必须真实、健康、清晰、明白，不得以任何形式欺骗用户和消费者。

**Article 3** The content of advertisements must be truthful, healthy, clear, and understandable, and shall not deceive users or consumers in any form.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在广告经营活动中，禁止垄断和不正当竞争行为。

**Article 4** Monopoly and unfair competition practices are prohibited in advertising business activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　广告的管理机关是国家工商行政管理机关和地方各级工商行政管理机关。

**Article 5** The administrative authority for advertisements is the State Administration for Industry and Commerce and the local administrations for industry and commerce at various levels.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营广告业务的单位和个体工商户(以下简称广告经营者)，应当按照本条例和有关法规的规定，向工商行政管理机关申请，分别情况办理审批登记手续：  
(一)专营广告业务的企业，发给《企业法人营业执照》；  
(二)兼营广告业务的事业单位，发给《广告经营许可证》；  
(三)具备经营广告业务能力的个体工商户，发给《营业执照》；  
(四)兼营广告业务的企业，应当办理经营范围变更登记。

**Article 6** Units and individual industrial and commercial households (hereinafter referred to as advertising operators) engaged in advertising business shall, in accordance with these Regulations and relevant laws and regulations, apply to the administrative authorities for industry and commerce and undergo the formalities of examination, approval, and registration according to the specific circumstances:  
(1) Enterprises specializing in advertising business shall be issued the "Business License for Enterprise Legal Person";  
(2) Public institutions concurrently engaged in advertising business shall be issued the "Advertising Business Permit";  
(3) Individual industrial and commercial households qualified to operate advertising business shall be issued the "Business License";  
(4) Enterprises concurrently engaged in advertising business shall undergo registration for alteration of their business scope.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　广告客户申请刊播、设置、张贴的广告，其内容应当在广告客户的经营范围或者国家许可的范围内。

**Article 7** The content of advertisements for which advertising clients apply to publish, broadcast, place, or post shall be within the business scope of the advertising client or within the scope permitted by the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　广告有下列内容之一的，不得刊播、设置、张贴：  
(一)违反我国法律、法规的；  
(二)损害我国民族尊严的；  
(三)有中国国旗、国徽、国歌标志、国歌音响的；  
(四)有反动、淫秽、迷信、荒诞内容的；  
(五)弄虚作假的；  
(六)贬低同类产品的。

**Article 8** Advertisements with any of the following contents shall not be published, broadcast, placed, or posted:  
(1) Those that violate the laws and regulations of China;  
(2) Those that harm the national dignity of China;  
(3) Those that bear the national flag, national emblem, national anthem signs, or national anthem sound of China;  
(4) Those with reactionary, obscene, superstitious, or absurd contents;  
(5) Those that are fraudulent;  
(6) Those that disparage similar products.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　新闻单位刊播广告，应当有明确的标志。新闻单位不得以新闻报道形式刊播广告，收取费用；新闻记者不得借采访名义招揽广告。

**Article 9** News units publishing or broadcasting advertisements shall have clear markings. News units shall not publish or broadcast advertisements in the form of news reports and collect fees; news reporters shall not solicit advertisements under the pretext of interviews.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　禁止利用广播、电视、报刊为卷烟做广告。  
获得国家级、部级、省级各类奖的优质名酒，经工商行政管理机关批准，可以做广告。

**Article 10** It is prohibited to use radio, television, newspapers, or periodicals to advertise cigarettes.  
High-quality famous liquors that have won various awards at the national, ministerial, or provincial levels may, with the approval of the administrative authorities for industry and commerce, be advertised.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请刊播、设置、张贴下列广告，应当提交有关证明：  
(一)标明质量标准的商品广告，应当提交省辖市以上标准化管理部门或者经计量认证合格的质量检验机构的证明；  
(二)标明获奖的商品广告，应当提交本届、本年度或者数届、数年度连续获奖的证书，并在广告中注明获奖级别和颁奖部门；  
(三)标明优质产品称号的商品广告，应当提交政府颁发的优质产品证书，并在广告中标明授予优质产品称号的时间和部门；  
(四)标明专利权的商品广告，应当提交专利证书；  
(五)标明注册商标的商品广告，应当提交商标注册证；  
(六)实施生产许可证的产品广告，应当提交生产许可证；  
(七)文化、教育、卫生广告，应当提交上级行政主管部门的证明；  
(八)其他各类广告，需要提交证明的，应当提交政府有关部门或者其授权单位的证明。

**Article 11** Applicants for publishing, broadcasting, placing, or posting the following advertisements shall submit relevant certificates:  
(1) For commodity advertisements indicating quality standards, a certificate from the standardization administration department at or above the level of a city under provincial jurisdiction or from a quality inspection institution qualified through metrological certification shall be submitted;  
(2) For commodity advertisements indicating awards, certificates of awards for the current session/current year or for consecutive sessions/years shall be submitted, and the level of the award and the awarding department shall be indicated in the advertisement;  
(3) For commodity advertisements indicating the title of high-quality product, a high-quality product certificate issued by the government shall be submitted, and the time and department that conferred the title of high-quality product shall be indicated in the advertisement;  
(4) For commodity advertisements indicating patent rights, a patent certificate shall be submitted;  
(5) For commodity advertisements indicating registered trademarks, a trademark registration certificate shall be submitted;  
(6) For product advertisements subject to a production license, a production license shall be submitted;  
(7) For cultural, educational, and health advertisements, a certificate from the competent administrative department at a higher level shall be submitted;  
(8) For other types of advertisements that require certificates, certificates from relevant government departments or their authorized entities shall be submitted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　广告经营者承办或者代理广告业务，应当查验证明，审查广告内容。对违反本条例规定的广告，不得刊播、设置、张贴。

**Article 12** Advertising operators undertaking or acting as agents for advertising business shall examine the certificates and review the content of advertisements. Advertisements that violate the provisions of these Regulations shall not be published, broadcast, placed, or posted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　户外广告的设置、张贴，由当地人民政府组织工商行政管理、城建、环保、公安等有关部门制订规划，工商行政管理机关负责监督实施。  
在政府机关和文物保护单位周围的建筑控制地带以及当地人民政府禁止设置、张贴广告的区域，不得设置、张贴广告。

**Article 13** The placement and posting of outdoor advertisements shall be planned by the local people's government in conjunction with relevant departments such as the administrative authorities for industry and commerce, urban construction, environmental protection, and public security, with the administrative authorities for industry and commerce responsible for supervising implementation.  
Advertisements shall not be placed or posted in the building control zones around government organs and cultural relics protection units, as well as in areas where the local people's government prohibits the placement or posting of advertisements.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　广告收费标准，由广告经营者制订，报当地工商行政管理机关和物价管理机关备案。

**Article 14** The fee standards for advertisements shall be formulated by advertising operators and reported to the local administrative authorities for industry and commerce and the price control authorities for record.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　广告业务代理费标准，由国家工商行政管理机关会同国家物价管理机关制定。  
户外广告场地费、建筑物占用费的收费标准，由当地工商行政管理机关会同物价、城建部门协商制订，报当地人民政府批准。

**Article 15** The fee standards for advertising business agency fees shall be formulated by the State Administration for Industry and Commerce jointly with the State Price Control Authority.  
The fee standards for outdoor advertising site fees and building occupancy fees shall be negotiated and formulated by the local administrative authorities for industry and commerce jointly with the price control and urban construction departments, and reported to the local people's government for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　广告经营者必须按照国家规定设置广告会计帐簿，依法纳税，并接受财政、审计、工商行政管理部门的监督检查。

**Article 16** Advertising operators must set up advertising accounting books in accordance with state provisions, pay taxes according to law, and accept supervision and inspection by the financial, auditing, and industry and commerce administrative departments.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　广告经营者承办或者代理广告业务，应当与客户或者被代理人签订书面合同，明确各方的责任。

**Article 17** Advertising operators undertaking or acting as agents for advertising business shall enter into written contracts with clients or principals, clearly defining the responsibilities of each party.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　广告客户或者广告经营者违反本条例规定，由工商行政管理机关根据其情节轻重，分别给予下列处罚：  
(一)停止发布广告；  
(二)责令公开更正；  
(三)通报批评；  
(四)没收非法所得；  
(五)罚款；  
(六)停业整顿；  
(七)吊销营业执照或者广告经营许可证。  
违反本条例规定，情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 18** Where an advertising client or advertising operator violates the provisions of these Regulations, the administrative authorities for industry and commerce shall impose the following penalties according to the seriousness of the circumstances:  
(1) Order cessation of advertisement publication;  
(2) Order public correction;  
(3) Circulate a notice of criticism;  
(4) Confiscate illegal gains;  
(5) Impose a fine;  
(6) Order suspension of business for rectification;  
(7) Revoke the business license or advertising business permit.  
Where a violation of these Regulations is serious enough to constitute a crime, the judicial authorities shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　广告客户和广告经营者对工商行政管理机关处罚决定不服的，可以在收到处罚通知之日起十五日内，向上一级工商行政管理机关申请复议。对复议决定仍不服的，可以在收到复议决定之日起三十日内，向人民法院起诉。

**Article 19** Where an advertising client or advertising operator disagrees with the penalty decision of the administrative authorities for industry and commerce, it may apply for review to the administrative authority for industry and commerce at the next higher level within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice. If it still disagrees with the review decision, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within thirty days from the date of receipt of the review decision.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　广告客户和广告经营者违反本条例规定，使用户和消费者蒙受损失，或者有其他侵权行为的，应当承担赔偿责任。  
损害赔偿，受害人可以请求县以上工商行政管理机关处理。当事人对工商行政管理机关处理不服的，可以向人民法院起诉。受害人也可以直接向人民法院起诉。

**Article 20** Where an advertising client or advertising operator, in violation of these Regulations, causes losses to users or consumers or commits other infringing acts, it shall bear compensation liability.  
For damages, the victim may request the administrative authority for industry and commerce at or above the county level to handle the matter. If a party disagrees with the handling by the administrative authority for industry and commerce, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court. The victim may also directly file a prosecution with the People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本条例由国家工商行政管理局负责解释；施行细则由国家工商行政管理局制定。

**Article 21** The power to interpret these Regulations rests with the State Administration for Industry and Commerce; the detailed rules for implementation shall be formulated by the State Administration for Industry and Commerce.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自1987年12月1日起施行。1982年2月6日国务院发布的《广告管理暂行条例》同时废止。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 1987. The Interim Regulations on Advertising Management promulgated by the State Council on February 6, 1982 shall be abolished simultaneously.
