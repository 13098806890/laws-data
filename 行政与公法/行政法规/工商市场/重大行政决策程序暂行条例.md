# 重大行政决策程序暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-20  
**生效日期**：2019-09-01

> (2019年4月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第713号公布　自2019年9月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了健全科学、民主、依法决策机制，规范重大行政决策程序，提高决策质量和效率，明确决策责任，根据宪法、地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法等规定，制定本条例。 第二条　县级以上地方人民政府(以下称决策机关)重大行政决策的作出和调整程序，适用本条例。 第三条　本条例所称重大行政决策事项(以下简称决策事项)包括： (一)制定有关公共服务、市场监管、社会管理、环境保护等方面的重大公共政策和措施； (二)制定经济和社会发展等方面的重要规划； (三)制定开发利用、保护重要自然资源和文化资源的重大公共政策和措施； (四)决定在本行政区域实施的重大公共建设项目； (五)决定对经济社会发展有重大影响、涉及重大公共利益或者社会公众切身利益的其他重大事项。 法律、行政法规对本条第一款规定事项的决策程序另有规定的，依照其规定。财政政策、货币政策等宏观调控决策，政府立法决策以及突发事件应急处置决策不适用本条例。 决策机关可以根据本条第一款的规定，结合职责权限和本地实际，确定决策事项目录、标准，经同级党委同意后向社会公布，并根据实际情况调整。 第四条　重大行政决策必须坚持和加强党的全面领导，全面贯彻党的路线方针政策和决策部署，发挥党的领导核心作用，把党的领导贯彻到重大行政决策全过程。 第五条　作出重大行政决策应当遵循科学决策原则，贯彻创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的发展理念，坚持从实际出发，运用科学技术和方法，尊重客观规律，适应经济社会发展和全面深化改革要求。 第六条　作出重大行政决策应当遵循民主决策原则，充分听取各方面意见，保障人民群众通过多种途径和形式参与决策。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了健全科学、民主、依法决策机制，规范重大行政决策程序，提高决策质量和效率，明确决策责任，根据宪法、地方各级人民代表大会和地方各级人民政府组织法等规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of improving the scientific, democratic, and law-based decision-making mechanism, regulating the procedures for major administrative decisions, improving the quality and efficiency of decisions, clarifying decision-making responsibilities, and in accordance with the Constitution, the Organic Law of the Local People's Congresses and Local People's Governments at Various Levels, and other provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上地方人民政府(以下称决策机关)重大行政决策的作出和调整程序，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the procedures for making and adjusting major administrative decisions of local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as decision-making organs).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称重大行政决策事项(以下简称决策事项)包括：  
(一)制定有关公共服务、市场监管、社会管理、环境保护等方面的重大公共政策和措施；  
(二)制定经济和社会发展等方面的重要规划；  
(三)制定开发利用、保护重要自然资源和文化资源的重大公共政策和措施；  
(四)决定在本行政区域实施的重大公共建设项目；  
(五)决定对经济社会发展有重大影响、涉及重大公共利益或者社会公众切身利益的其他重大事项。  
法律、行政法规对本条第一款规定事项的决策程序另有规定的，依照其规定。财政政策、货币政策等宏观调控决策，政府立法决策以及突发事件应急处置决策不适用本条例。  
决策机关可以根据本条第一款的规定，结合职责权限和本地实际，确定决策事项目录、标准，经同级党委同意后向社会公布，并根据实际情况调整。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, major administrative decision-making matters (hereinafter referred to as decision-making matters) include:  
(1) formulating major public policies and measures concerning public services, market supervision, social management, environmental protection, and the like;  
(2) formulating important plans for economic and social development and other aspects;  
(3) formulating major public policies and measures for developing, utilizing, and protecting important natural and cultural resources;  
(4) deciding on major public construction projects to be implemented within the administrative region;  
(5) deciding on other major matters that have a significant impact on economic and social development, involve significant public interests, or affect the vital interests of the public.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for the decision-making procedures for the matters specified in the first paragraph of this Article, those provisions shall prevail. Fiscal policy, monetary policy, and other macro-control decisions, government legislative decisions, and emergency response decisions shall not be subject to these Regulations.  
A decision-making organ may, in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article and in light of its functions, powers, and local实际情况, determine the catalogue and standards for decision-making matters, publish them to the public after obtaining the consent of the Party committee at the same level, and adjust them in light of actual conditions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　重大行政决策必须坚持和加强党的全面领导，全面贯彻党的路线方针政策和决策部署，发挥党的领导核心作用，把党的领导贯彻到重大行政决策全过程。

**Article 4** In major administrative decisions, the overall leadership of the Party must be upheld and strengthened, the Party's line, principles, policies, and decisions must be fully implemented, the core leadership role of the Party must be brought into play, and the Party's leadership must be carried through the entire process of major administrative decisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　作出重大行政决策应当遵循科学决策原则，贯彻创新、协调、绿色、开放、共享的发展理念，坚持从实际出发，运用科学技术和方法，尊重客观规律，适应经济社会发展和全面深化改革要求。

**Article 5** When making major administrative decisions, the principle of scientific decision-making shall be followed, the development concepts of innovation, coordination, green development, openness, and sharing shall be implemented, proceeding from reality shall be adhered to, scientific techniques and methods shall be applied, objective laws shall be respected, and the requirements of economic and social development and comprehensively deepening reform shall be met.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　作出重大行政决策应当遵循民主决策原则，充分听取各方面意见，保障人民群众通过多种途径和形式参与决策。

**Article 6** When making major administrative decisions, the principle of democratic decision-making shall be followed, opinions from all sides shall be fully solicited, and the people's participation in decision-making through various channels and forms shall be ensured.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　作出重大行政决策应当遵循依法决策原则，严格遵守法定权限，依法履行法定程序，保证决策内容符合法律、法规和规章等规定。

**Article 7** When making major administrative decisions, the principle of law-based decision-making shall be followed, statutory authority shall be strictly observed, statutory procedures shall be performed according to law, and the decision content shall be ensured to comply with laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　重大行政决策依法接受本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会的监督，根据法律、法规规定属于本级人民代表大会及其常务委员会讨论决定的重大事项范围或者应当在出台前向本级人民代表大会常务委员会报告的，按照有关规定办理。  
上级行政机关应当加强对下级行政机关重大行政决策的监督。审计机关按照规定对重大行政决策进行监督。

**Article 8** Major administrative decisions shall accept supervision by the people's congress and its standing committee at the same level according to law. Where, according to laws and regulations, a matter falls within the scope of significant matters to be discussed and decided by the people's congress and its standing committee at the same level, or shall be reported to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level before being issued, it shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The administrative organ at a higher level shall strengthen supervision over major administrative decisions of lower-level administrative organs. Audit organs shall supervise major administrative decisions in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　重大行政决策情况应当作为考核评价决策机关及其领导人员的重要内容。

**Article 9** The situation of major administrative decisions shall serve as an important element for assessing and evaluating decision-making organs and their leading personnel.

### 第二章　决策草案的形成

#### 第一节　决策启动

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对各方面提出的决策事项建议，按照下列规定进行研究论证后，报请决策机关决定是否启动决策程序：  
(一)决策机关领导人员提出决策事项建议的，交有关单位研究论证；  
(二)决策机关所属部门或者下一级人民政府提出决策事项建议的，应当论证拟解决的主要问题、建议理由和依据、解决问题的初步方案及其必要性、可行性等；  
(三)人大代表、政协委员等通过建议、提案等方式提出决策事项建议，以及公民、法人或者其他组织提出书面决策事项建议的，交有关单位研究论证。

**Article 10** Suggestions on decision-making matters raised by various parties shall be studied and demonstrated in accordance with the following provisions, and then submitted to the decision-making organ for a decision on whether to initiate the decision-making procedure:  
(1) Where a leading person of the decision-making organ proposes a suggestion on a decision-making matter, it shall be referred to the relevant unit for research and demonstration;  
(2) Where a department under the decision-making organ or a people's government at the next lower level proposes a suggestion on a decision-making matter, it shall demonstrate the main problems to be solved, the reasons and basis for the suggestion, the preliminary plan for solving the problems, and its necessity and feasibility, among others;  
(3) Where deputies to the people's congress, members of the political consultative conference, or others propose suggestions on decision-making matters through proposals, motions, or other means, or where citizens, legal persons, or other organizations submit written suggestions on decision-making matters, the suggestions shall be referred to the relevant unit for research and demonstration.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　决策机关决定启动决策程序的，应当明确决策事项的承办单位(以下简称决策承办单位)，由决策承办单位负责重大行政决策草案的拟订等工作。决策事项需要两个以上单位承办的，应当明确牵头的决策承办单位。

**Article 11** Where a decision-making organ decides to initiate the decision-making procedure, it shall designate the unit responsible for undertaking the decision-making matter (hereinafter referred to as the decision-undertaking unit), which shall be responsible for drafting the draft major administrative decision, among other tasks. Where two or more units are required to undertake a decision-making matter, the leading decision-undertaking unit shall be designated.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　决策承办单位应当在广泛深入开展调查研究、全面准确掌握有关信息、充分协商协调的基础上，拟订决策草案。  
决策承办单位应当全面梳理与决策事项有关的法律、法规、规章和政策，使决策草案合法合规、与有关政策相衔接。  
决策承办单位根据需要对决策事项涉及的人财物投入、资源消耗、环境影响等成本和经济、社会、环境效益进行分析预测。  
有关方面对决策事项存在较大分歧的，决策承办单位可以提出两个以上方案。

**Article 12** A decision-undertaking unit shall draft the decision draft on the basis of extensive and in-depth investigation and research, comprehensively and accurately grasping relevant information, and full consultation and coordination.  
A decision-undertaking unit shall comprehensively review laws, regulations, rules, and policies related to the decision-making matter, so as to ensure that the draft decision is lawful and compliant and is aligned with relevant policies.  
A decision-undertaking unit shall, as needed, analyze and forecast costs such as human, financial, and material inputs, resource consumption, and environmental impact, as well as the economic, social, and environmental benefits involved in the decision-making matter.  
Where there are significant differences among relevant parties on a decision-making matter, the decision-undertaking unit may propose two or more plans.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　决策事项涉及决策机关所属部门、下一级人民政府等单位的职责，或者与其关系紧密的，决策承办单位应当与其充分协商；不能取得一致意见的，应当向决策机关说明争议的主要问题，有关单位的意见，决策承办单位的意见、理由和依据。

**Article 13** Where a decision-making matter involves the functions of a department under the decision-making organ, a people's government at the next lower level, or other units, or is closely related to them, the decision-undertaking unit shall fully consult with them; where no consensus can be reached, it shall explain to the decision-making organ the main issues in dispute, the opinions of the relevant units, and the opinions, reasons, and basis of the decision-undertaking unit.

#### 第二节　公众参与

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　决策承办单位应当采取便于社会公众参与的方式充分听取意见，依法不予公开的决策事项除外。  
听取意见可以采取座谈会、听证会、实地走访、书面征求意见、向社会公开征求意见、问卷调查、民意调查等多种方式。  
决策事项涉及特定群体利益的，决策承办单位应当与相关人民团体、社会组织以及群众代表进行沟通协商，充分听取相关群体的意见建议。

**Article 14** A decision-undertaking unit shall fully solicit opinions in a manner convenient for public participation, except for decision-making matters that are not disclosed according to law.  
Opinions may be solicited through various methods such as symposiums, hearings, on-site visits, written requests for opinions, public solicitation of opinions, questionnaires, and public opinion surveys.  
Where a decision-making matter involves the interests of specific groups, the decision-undertaking unit shall communicate and consult with relevant people's organizations, social organizations, and representatives of the masses, and fully solicit opinions and suggestions from the relevant groups.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　决策事项向社会公开征求意见的，决策承办单位应当通过政府网站、政务新媒体以及报刊、广播、电视等便于社会公众知晓的途径，公布决策草案及其说明等材料，明确提出意见的方式和期限。公开征求意见的期限一般不少于30日；因情况紧急等原因需要缩短期限的，公开征求意见时应当予以说明。  
对社会公众普遍关心或者专业性、技术性较强的问题，决策承办单位可以通过专家访谈等方式进行解释说明。

**Article 15** Where a decision-making matter is subject to public solicitation of opinions, the decision-undertaking unit shall publish the draft decision and its explanatory materials through government websites, government new media, newspapers, radio, television, and other channels that are convenient for the public to be aware of, and shall clearly specify the method and time limit for submitting opinions. The time limit for public solicitation of opinions shall generally be not less than 30 days; where the time limit needs to be shortened due to urgent circumstances or other reasons, an explanation shall be provided when soliciting opinions publicly.  
For issues that are of general public concern or are highly professional or technical, the decision-undertaking unit may provide explanations through expert interviews or other means.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　决策事项直接涉及公民、法人、其他组织切身利益或者存在较大分歧的，可以召开听证会。法律、法规、规章对召开听证会另有规定的，依照其规定。  
决策承办单位或者组织听证会的其他单位应当提前公布决策草案及其说明等材料，明确听证时间、地点等信息。  
需要遴选听证参加人的，决策承办单位或者组织听证会的其他单位应当提前公布听证参加人遴选办法，公平公开组织遴选，保证相关各方都有代表参加听证会。听证参加人名单应当提前向社会公布。听证会材料应当于召开听证会7日前送达听证参加人。

**Article 16** Where a decision-making matter directly involves the vital interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, or where there are significant differences, a hearing may be held. Where laws, regulations, or rules provide otherwise for the holding of hearings, those provisions shall prevail.  
The decision-undertaking unit or other unit organizing the hearing shall publish the draft decision and its explanatory materials in advance, and shall specify the hearing time, location, and other information.  
Where the selection of hearing participants is required, the decision-undertaking unit or other unit organizing the hearing shall publish the selection method for hearing participants in advance, organize the selection fairly and openly, and ensure that all relevant parties have representatives to attend the hearing. The list of hearing participants shall be published to the public in advance. Hearing materials shall be delivered to the hearing participants 7 days before the hearing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　听证会应当按照下列程序公开举行：  
(一)决策承办单位介绍决策草案、依据和有关情况；  
(二)听证参加人陈述意见，进行询问、质证和辩论，必要时可以由决策承办单位或者有关专家进行解释说明；  
(三)听证参加人确认听证会记录并签字。

**Article 17** A hearing shall be held publicly in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The decision-undertaking unit introduces the draft decision, the basis, and relevant circumstances;  
(2) The hearing participants present their opinions, conduct inquiries, cross-examination, and debate, and where necessary, the decision-undertaking unit or relevant experts may provide explanations;  
(3) The hearing participants confirm the hearing record and sign it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　决策承办单位应当对社会各方面提出的意见进行归纳整理、研究论证，充分采纳合理意见，完善决策草案。

**Article 18** A decision-undertaking unit shall summarize, organize, research, and demonstrate opinions raised by all parties, fully adopt reasonable opinions, and improve the draft decision.

#### 第三节　专家论证

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对专业性、技术性较强的决策事项，决策承办单位应当组织专家、专业机构论证其必要性、可行性、科学性等，并提供必要保障。  
专家、专业机构应当独立开展论证工作，客观、公正、科学地提出论证意见，并对所知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私依法履行保密义务；提供书面论证意见的，应当署名、盖章。

**Article 19** For decision-making matters that are highly professional or technical, the decision-undertaking unit shall organize experts and professional institutions to demonstrate their necessity, feasibility, and scientific nature, among others, and shall provide necessary support.  
Experts and professional institutions shall independently carry out the demonstration work, provide objective, fair, and scientific demonstration opinions, and shall fulfill the duty of confidentiality according to law for any state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy they become aware of; where written demonstration opinions are provided, they shall be signed and sealed.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　决策承办单位组织专家论证，可以采取论证会、书面咨询、委托咨询论证等方式。选择专家、专业机构参与论证，应当坚持专业性、代表性和中立性，注重选择持不同意见的专家、专业机构，不得选择与决策事项有直接利害关系的专家、专业机构。

**Article 20** When organizing expert demonstration, a decision-undertaking unit may adopt methods such as demonstration meetings, written consultations, and commissioned consultation demonstrations. When selecting experts and professional institutions to participate in the demonstration, the principles of professionalism, representativeness, and neutrality shall be adhered to, and attention shall be paid to selecting experts and professional institutions with different opinions; experts or professional institutions that have a direct interest in the decision-making matter shall not be selected.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当建立决策咨询论证专家库，规范专家库运行管理制度，健全专家诚信考核和退出机制。  
市、县级人民政府可以根据需要建立决策咨询论证专家库。  
决策机关没有建立决策咨询论证专家库的，可以使用上级行政机关的专家库。

**Article 21** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish expert pools for decision-making consultation and demonstration, standardize the operation and management system of the expert pools, and improve the mechanisms for expert integrity assessment and withdrawal.  
People's governments at the city and county levels may establish expert pools for decision-making consultation and demonstration as needed.  
Where a decision-making organ has not established an expert pool for decision-making consultation and demonstration, it may use the expert pool of the administrative organ at a higher level.

#### 第四节　风险评估

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　重大行政决策的实施可能对社会稳定、公共安全等方面造成不利影响的，决策承办单位或者负责风险评估工作的其他单位应当组织评估决策草案的风险可控性。  
按照有关规定已对有关风险进行评价、评估的，不作重复评估。

**Article 22** Where the implementation of a major administrative decision may cause adverse impacts on social stability, public safety, or other aspects, the decision-undertaking unit or other unit responsible for risk assessment shall organize an assessment of the risk controllability of the draft decision.  
Where relevant risks have already been evaluated or assessed in accordance with relevant provisions, no repeated assessment shall be conducted.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　开展风险评估，可以通过舆情跟踪、重点走访、会商分析等方式，运用定性分析与定量分析等方法，对决策实施的风险进行科学预测、综合研判。  
开展风险评估，应当听取有关部门的意见，形成风险评估报告，明确风险点，提出风险防范措施和处置预案。  
开展风险评估，可以委托专业机构、社会组织等第三方进行。

**Article 23** When conducting risk assessment, methods such as public opinion tracking, targeted visits, and consultation analysis may be used, and methods such as qualitative analysis and quantitative analysis may be employed to scientifically predict and comprehensively judge the risks of decision implementation.  
When conducting risk assessment, opinions of relevant departments shall be solicited, a risk assessment report shall be prepared, risk points shall be identified, and risk prevention measures and contingency plans shall be proposed.  
Risk assessment may be entrusted to third parties such as professional institutions and social organizations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　风险评估结果应当作为重大行政决策的重要依据。决策机关认为风险可控的，可以作出决策；认为风险不可控的，在采取调整决策草案等措施确保风险可控后，可以作出决策。

**Article 24** The risk assessment results shall serve as an important basis for major administrative decisions. Where a decision-making organ considers the risks controllable, it may make the decision; where it considers the risks uncontrollable, it may make the decision after taking measures such as adjusting the draft decision to ensure that the risks are controllable.

### 第三章　合法性审查和集体讨论决定

#### 第一节　合法性审查

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　决策草案提交决策机关讨论前，应当由负责合法性审查的部门进行合法性审查。不得以征求意见等方式代替合法性审查。  
决策草案未经合法性审查或者经审查不合法的，不得提交决策机关讨论。对国家尚无明确规定的探索性改革决策事项，可以明示法律风险，提交决策机关讨论。

**Article 25** Before a draft decision is submitted to the decision-making organ for discussion, it shall be subject to legality review by the department responsible for legality review. Soliciting opinions or other methods shall not replace the legality review.  
Where a draft decision has not undergone legality review or is found to be illegal after review, it shall not be submitted to the decision-making organ for discussion. For exploratory reform decision-making matters for which the state has no clear provisions, the legal risks may be explicitly indicated, and the draft may be submitted to the decision-making organ for discussion.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　送请合法性审查，应当提供决策草案及相关材料，包括有关法律、法规、规章等依据和履行决策法定程序的说明等。提供的材料不符合要求的，负责合法性审查的部门可以退回，或者要求补充。  
送请合法性审查，应当保证必要的审查时间，一般不少于7个工作日。

**Article 26** When submitting for legality review, the draft decision and relevant materials shall be provided, including the basis of relevant laws, regulations, and rules, and a description of the performance of the statutory decision-making procedures. Where the materials provided do not meet the requirements, the department responsible for legality review may return them or request supplementation.  
When submitting for legality review, a sufficient review time shall be ensured, generally not less than 7 working days.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　合法性审查的内容包括：  
(一)决策事项是否符合法定权限；  
(二)决策草案的形成是否履行相关法定程序；  
(三)决策草案内容是否符合有关法律、法规、规章和国家政策的规定。

**Article 27** The content of legality review includes:  
(1) whether the decision-making matter is within statutory authority;  
(2) whether the formation of the draft decision has undergone the relevant statutory procedures;  
(3) whether the content of the draft decision complies with relevant laws, regulations, rules, and national policies.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　负责合法性审查的部门应当及时提出合法性审查意见，并对合法性审查意见负责。在合法性审查过程中，应当组织法律顾问、公职律师提出法律意见。决策承办单位根据合法性审查意见进行必要的调整或者补充。

**Article 28** The department responsible for legality review shall timely provide the legality review opinion and shall be responsible for it. During the legality review process, legal advisors and public lawyers shall be organized to provide legal opinions. The decision-undertaking unit shall make necessary adjustments or supplements based on the legality review opinion.

#### 第二节　集体讨论决定和决策公布

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　决策承办单位提交决策机关讨论决策草案，应当报送下列材料：  
(一)决策草案及相关材料，决策草案涉及市场主体经济活动的，应当包含公平竞争审查的有关情况；  
(二)履行公众参与程序的，同时报送社会公众提出的主要意见的研究采纳情况；  
(三)履行专家论证程序的，同时报送专家论证意见的研究采纳情况；  
(四)履行风险评估程序的，同时报送风险评估报告等有关材料；  
(五)合法性审查意见；  
(六)需要报送的其他材料。

**Article 29** When a decision-undertaking unit submits a draft decision to the decision-making organ for discussion, it shall submit the following materials:  
(1) the draft decision and related materials; where the draft decision involves economic activities of market entities, it shall include relevant information on fair competition review;  
(2) where the public participation procedure has been carried out, the status of study and adoption of the main opinions raised by the public shall also be submitted;  
(3) where the expert demonstration procedure has been carried out, the status of study and adoption of the expert demonstration opinions shall also be submitted;  
(4) where the risk assessment procedure has been carried out, the risk assessment report and other relevant materials shall also be submitted;  
(5) the legality review opinion;  
(6) other materials that need to be submitted.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　决策草案应当经决策机关常务会议或者全体会议讨论。决策机关行政首长在集体讨论的基础上作出决定。  
讨论决策草案，会议组成人员应当充分发表意见，行政首长最后发表意见。行政首长拟作出的决定与会议组成人员多数人的意见不一致的，应当在会上说明理由。  
集体讨论决定情况应当如实记录，不同意见应当如实载明。

**Article 30** The draft decision shall be discussed at the executive meeting or plenary meeting of the decision-making organ. The chief administrative officer of the decision-making organ shall make a decision based on collective discussion.  
When discussing the draft decision, the members of the meeting shall fully express their opinions, and the chief administrative officer shall express his or her opinion last. Where the decision to be made by the chief administrative officer is inconsistent with the majority opinion of the members of the meeting, the chief administrative officer shall explain the reasons at the meeting.  
The circumstances of the collective discussion and decision-making shall be truthfully recorded, and different opinions shall be truthfully documented.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　重大行政决策出台前应当按照规定向同级党委请示报告。

**Article 31** A major administrative decision shall, before being issued, be submitted to the Party committee at the same level for instructions and a report in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　决策机关应当通过本级人民政府公报和政府网站以及在本行政区域内发行的报纸等途径及时公布重大行政决策。对社会公众普遍关心或者专业性、技术性较强的重大行政决策，应当说明公众意见、专家论证意见的采纳情况，通过新闻发布会、接受访谈等方式进行宣传解读。依法不予公开的除外。

**Article 32** A decision-making organ shall promptly publish major administrative decisions through the gazette of the people's government at the same level, government websites, and newspapers distributed within its administrative region. For major administrative decisions that are of general public concern or are highly professional or technical, the organ shall explain the adoption status of public opinions and expert demonstration opinions, and shall conduct publicity and interpretation through press conferences, interviews, or other means. Except for those that are not publicly disclosed according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　决策机关应当建立重大行政决策过程记录和材料归档制度，由有关单位将履行决策程序形成的记录、材料及时完整归档。

**Article 33** A decision-making organ shall establish a system for recording the process of major administrative decisions and archiving materials, and the relevant units shall timely and completely archive the records and materials formed during the performance of decision-making procedures.

### 第四章　决策执行和调整

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　决策机关应当明确负责重大行政决策执行工作的单位(以下简称决策执行单位)，并对决策执行情况进行督促检查。决策执行单位应当依法全面、及时、正确执行重大行政决策，并向决策机关报告决策执行情况。

**Article 34** A decision-making organ shall designate the unit responsible for implementing the major administrative decision (hereinafter referred to as the decision-implementing unit), and shall supervise and inspect the implementation of the decision. The decision-implementing unit shall implement the major administrative decision comprehensively, timely, and correctly according to law, and shall report the implementation status to the decision-making organ.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　决策执行单位发现重大行政决策存在问题、客观情况发生重大变化，或者决策执行中发生不可抗力等严重影响决策目标实现的，应当及时向决策机关报告。  
公民、法人或者其他组织认为重大行政决策及其实施存在问题的，可以通过信件、电话、电子邮件等方式向决策机关或者决策执行单位提出意见建议。

**Article 35** Where a decision-implementing unit discovers problems in the major administrative decision, significant changes in objective circumstances, or force majeure or other events during implementation that seriously affect the achievement of the decision objectives, it shall promptly report to the decision-making organ.  
Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that believe there are problems with a major administrative decision or its implementation may submit opinions and suggestions to the decision-making organ or the decision-implementing unit by means of letters, telephone, email, or other means.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有下列情形之一的，决策机关可以组织决策后评估，并确定承担评估具体工作的单位：  
(一)重大行政决策实施后明显未达到预期效果；  
(二)公民、法人或者其他组织提出较多意见；  
(三)决策机关认为有必要。  
开展决策后评估，可以委托专业机构、社会组织等第三方进行，决策作出前承担主要论证评估工作的单位除外。  
开展决策后评估，应当注重听取社会公众的意见，吸收人大代表、政协委员、人民团体、基层组织、社会组织参与评估。  
决策后评估结果应当作为调整重大行政决策的重要依据。

**Article 36** Under any of the following circumstances, a decision-making organ may organize a post-decision evaluation and designate the unit responsible for the specific evaluation work:  
(1) after implementation of the major administrative decision, the expected results are clearly not achieved;  
(2) citizens, legal persons, or other organizations raise many opinions;  
(3) the decision-making organ considers it necessary.  
When conducting a post-decision evaluation, the organ may entrust it to a third party such as a professional institution or a social organization, except for the unit that undertook the main demonstration and evaluation work before the decision was made.  
When conducting a post-decision evaluation, the organ shall pay attention to soliciting opinions from the public, and shall involve deputies to the people's congress, members of the political consultative conference, people's organizations, grassroots organizations, and social organizations in the evaluation.  
The results of the post-decision evaluation shall serve as an important basis for adjusting the major administrative decision.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　依法作出的重大行政决策，未经法定程序不得随意变更或者停止执行；执行中出现[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)规定的情形、情况紧急的，决策机关行政首长可以先决定中止执行；需要作出重大调整的，应当依照本条例履行相关法定程序。

**Article 37** A major administrative decision made according to law shall not be changed arbitrarily or have its implementation stopped without statutory procedures. Where the circumstances specified in Article 35 of these Regulations occur during implementation and the situation is urgent, the chief administrative officer of the decision-making organ may first decide to suspend the implementation. Where major adjustments are needed, the relevant statutory procedures shall be performed in accordance with these Regulations.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　决策机关违反本条例规定的，由上一级行政机关责令改正，对决策机关行政首长、负有责任的其他领导人员和直接责任人员依法追究责任。  
决策机关违反本条例规定造成决策严重失误，或者依法应当及时作出决策而久拖不决，造成重大损失、恶劣影响的，应当倒查责任，实行终身责任追究，对决策机关行政首长、负有责任的其他领导人员和直接责任人员依法追究责任。  
决策机关集体讨论决策草案时，有关人员对严重失误的决策表示不同意见的，按照规定减免责任。

**Article 38** Where a decision-making organ violates these Regulations, the administrative organ at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections, and shall pursue responsibility according to law against the chief administrative officer of the decision-making organ and other leaders and directly responsible persons who bear responsibility.  
Where a decision-making organ violates these Regulations and causes a serious decision-making error, or fails to make a timely decision that should have been made according to law, resulting in major losses or adverse impacts, responsibility shall be traced retroactively, lifelong accountability shall be implemented, and responsibility shall be pursued according to law against the chief administrative officer of the decision-making organ and other leaders and directly responsible persons who bear responsibility.  
Where, during the collective discussion of a draft decision by the decision-making organ, relevant persons express different opinions on a seriously erroneous decision, their responsibility shall be reduced or exempted in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　决策承办单位或者承担决策有关工作的单位未按照本条例规定履行决策程序或者履行决策程序时失职渎职、弄虚作假的，由决策机关责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法追究责任。

**Article 39** Where the decision-undertaking unit or a unit responsible for decision-related work fails to perform the decision-making procedures in accordance with these Regulations, or is derelict of duty, neglectful, or fraudulent when performing the decision-making procedures, the decision-making organ shall order it to make corrections, and shall pursue responsibility according to law against the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　决策执行单位拒不执行、推诿执行、拖延执行重大行政决策，或者对执行中发现的重大问题瞒报、谎报或者漏报的，由决策机关责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法追究责任。

**Article 40** Where a decision-implementing unit refuses to implement, shirks implementation, or delays implementation of a major administrative decision, or conceals, falsely reports, or omits reporting major problems discovered during implementation, the decision-making organ shall order it to make corrections, and shall pursue responsibility according to law against the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　承担论证评估工作的专家、专业机构、社会组织等违反职业道德和本条例规定的，予以通报批评、责令限期整改；造成严重后果的，取消评估资格、承担相应责任。

**Article 41** Where experts, professional institutions, social organizations, or others undertaking demonstration and evaluation work violate professional ethics and the provisions of these Regulations, they shall be given a notice of criticism and be ordered to make corrections within a time limit. If serious consequences are caused, their evaluation qualification shall be revoked and they shall assume corresponding responsibility.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府部门和乡级人民政府重大行政决策的作出和调整程序，参照本条例规定执行。

**Article 42** The procedures for making and adjusting major administrative decisions by departments of people's governments at or above the county level and by people's governments at the township level shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本条例制定本行政区域重大行政决策程序的具体制度。  
国务院有关部门参照本条例规定，制定本部门重大行政决策程序的具体制度。

**Article 43** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate specific systems for the procedures of major administrative decisions within their respective administrative regions in accordance with these Regulations.  
Relevant departments of the State Council shall, with reference to these Regulations, formulate specific systems for the procedures of major administrative decisions within their respective departments.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2019年9月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2019.
