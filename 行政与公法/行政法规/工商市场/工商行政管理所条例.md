### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强工商行政管理所(以下简称工商所)的组织建设，使工商所工作规范化、制度化，更好地发挥工商行政管理的职能作用，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the organizational construction of sub-bureaus of industry and commerce (hereinafter referred to as the "sub-bureaus"), standardizing and institutionalizing the work of sub-bureaus, and better fulfilling the functional role of the administration for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　工商所是区、县(含县级市，下同)工商行政管理局(以下简称区、县工商局)的派出机构。工商所的人员编制、经费开支、干部管理和业务工作等由区、县工商局直接领导和管理。

**Article 2** A sub-bureau is a dispatched agency of the district or county (including county-level city, same hereinafter) administration for industry and commerce (hereinafter referred to as the "district/county administration"). The staffing, budget, cadre management, and business work of a sub-bureau shall be directly led and managed by the district/county administration.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　工商所的基本任务是：依据法律、法规的规定，对辖区内的企业、个体工商户和市场经济活动进行监督管理，保护合法经营，取缔非法经营，维护正常的经济秩序。

**Article 3** The basic tasks of a sub-bureau are: in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, to supervise and administer the enterprises, individual industrial and commercial households and market economic activities within its jurisdiction, protect lawful business operations, ban illegal business operations, and maintain normal economic order.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　工商所按经济区域设立。  
工商所的设立，由区、县工商局根据辖区大小、经济发展情况和管理任务需要，提出具体方案，报区、县人民政府批准。

**Article 4** Sub-bureaus shall be established according to economic regions.  
The establishment of a sub-bureau shall be proposed by the district/county administration based on the size of the jurisdiction, economic development, and the needs of administrative tasks, and then submitted to the district/county people's government for approval.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　工商所设所长一人；任务较多的，设副所长一至二人；工作人员若干人。  
工商所实行所长负责制。

**Article 5** A sub-bureau shall have one director; where tasks are heavy, one to two deputy directors may be appointed; and several staff members.  
The sub-bureau shall implement the director responsibility system.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　工商所的职责包括：  
(一)办理辖区内由区、县工商局登记管理的企业的登记初审和年检、换照的审查手续，并对区、县工商局核准登记的企业进行监督管理；  
(二)管理辖区内的集贸市场，监督集市贸易经济活动；  
(三)监督检查辖区内经济合同的订立及履行，调解经济合同纠纷；  
(四)受理、初审、呈报辖区内个体工商户的开业、变更、歇业的申请事项，对个体工商户的生产经营活动进行监督管理；  
(五)指导辖区内企业事业单位、个体工商户正确申请商标注册，并对其使用商标进行监督管理；  
(六)对辖区内设置、张贴的广告进行监督管理；  
(七)按规定收取、上缴各项工商收费及罚没款物；  
(八)宣传工商行政管理法律、法规和有关政策；  
(九)法律、法规规定的其他工商行政管理职责。

**Article 6** The duties of a sub-bureau include:  
(1) Handling the preliminary examination of enterprise registration, annual inspection, and renewal of licenses that are under the registration administration of the district/county administration, and supervising and administering the enterprises approved and registered by the district/county administration;  
(2) Managing the market fairs within the jurisdiction and supervising the economic activities in the fairs;  
(3) Supervising and examining the conclusion and performance of economic contracts within the jurisdiction, and mediating disputes over economic contracts;  
(4) Accepting, preliminarily examining, and reporting on the applications for starting business, changes, or closure of individual industrial and commercial households within the jurisdiction, and supervising and administering the production and business activities of individual industrial and commercial households;  
(5) Guiding enterprises, public institutions, and individual industrial and commercial households within the jurisdiction in correctly applying for trademark registration, and supervising and administering their use of trademarks;  
(6) Supervising and administering the placement and posting of advertisements within the jurisdiction;  
(7) Collecting and turning over various industry and commerce fees and confiscated money and goods according to regulations;  
(8) Publicizing laws, regulations, and relevant policies on the administration of industry and commerce;  
(9) Other duties concerning the administration of industry and commerce as stipulated by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　工商所的具体工作范围，由区、县工商局依照工商行政管理的有关法律、法规和规章的规定，根据辖区内管理工作的实际需要，在前条所列职责范围内予以确定，并报上一级工商行政管理机关备案。

**Article 7** The specific scope of work of a sub-bureau shall be determined by the district/county administration in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules on the administration of industry and commerce, based on the actual needs of administrative work within the jurisdiction, within the scope of duties listed in the preceding article, and shall be reported to the superior administrative organ for industry and commerce for record.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　工商所的具体行政行为是区、县工商局的具体行政行为，但有下列情况之一的，工商所可以以自己的名义作出具体行政行为：  
(一)对个体工商户违法行为的处罚；  
(二)对集市贸易中违法行为的处罚；  
(三)法律、法规和规章规定工商所以自己的名义作出的其他具体行政行为。  
前款第(一)、(二)项处罚不包括吊销营业执照。

**Article 8** The specific administrative acts of a sub-bureau shall be the specific administrative acts of the district/county administration, except that in any of the following circumstances, the sub-bureau may make specific administrative acts in its own name:  
(1) Imposing penalties on illegal acts of individual industrial and commercial households;  
(2) Imposing penalties on illegal acts in market fairs;  
(3) Other specific administrative acts that laws, regulations, and rules provide that the sub-bureau may make in its own name.  
The penalties under items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph shall not include the revocation of business licenses.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　工商所应当建立健全监督管理和行政执法的工作程序，公开办事制度和办事结果，接受群众监督，做到公正严明，管理有章，处罚有据，廉洁奉公。

**Article 9** A sub-bureau shall establish and improve the work procedures for supervision and administration and for administrative law enforcement, make public the work systems and work results, accept supervision by the masses, and achieve fairness and strictness, orderly management, penalty with evidence, and honesty and integrity.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　工商所应当建立健全岗位责任制，对工作人员的德、能、勤、绩进行定期考核，并将考核情况作为任职、奖励、晋升的主要依据。

**Article 10** A sub-bureau shall establish and improve the post responsibility system, conduct regular assessment of the staff's moral character, ability, diligence, and performance, and use the assessment results as the main basis for appointment, reward, and promotion.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　工商所应当建立健全财务管理和监督制度，配备专职财务人员。各项工商收费和罚没物资、现金、票证，应当分别登记造册，按规定处理。  
工商所的费用开支，按有关财务规定执行。

**Article 11** A sub-bureau shall establish and improve the financial management and supervision system, and assign full-time financial personnel. All industry and commerce fees and confiscated materials, cash, and vouchers shall be registered separately and handled according to regulations.  
The expenditure of a sub-bureau shall be implemented in accordance with relevant financial provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　工商所应当建立健全内勤工作制度。各类文件和资料应当及时登记归档；认真处理来信来访；严格执行用印制度。

**Article 12** A sub-bureau shall establish and improve the internal work system. All kinds of documents and materials shall be registered and filed in a timely manner; letters and visits shall be handled carefully; the seal use system shall be strictly implemented.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　工商所工作人员必须清正廉洁，秉公执法，不得以权谋私，索贿受贿。

**Article 13** The staff of a sub-bureau must be honest and upright, enforce the law impartially, and shall not abuse their power for personal gain, demand bribes, or accept bribes.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　工商所工作人员必须严格遵守国家及有关部门制定的政府机关工作人员的各项纪律、守则，积极工作，忠于职守。

**Article 14** The staff of a sub-bureau must strictly observe all disciplines and rules formulated by the State and relevant departments for personnel of government organs, work actively, and be devoted to their duties.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　工商所工作人员执行公务时必须按规定着装，仪表庄重，待人礼貌。

**Article 15** When performing official duties, the staff of a sub-bureau must dress according to regulations, maintain a dignified appearance, and treat others with courtesy.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　工商所工作人员在工作中取得突出成绩的，工商所可提请区、县工商局给予奖励。对违纪违法的，由区、县工商局依照国家有关规定给予处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 16** If a staff member of a sub-bureau achieves outstanding results in work, the sub-bureau may request the district/county administration to give a reward. Those who violate discipline or law shall be given sanctions by the district/county administration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, the judicial organ shall, according to law, investigate criminal liability.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　工商行政管理系统的其他专业所、队、站参照本条例规定执行。

**Article 17** Other specialized offices, teams, and stations within the administration for industry and commerce system shall implement their work with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例由国家工商行政管理局负责解释。

**Article 18** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Administration for Industry and Commerce.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 19** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
