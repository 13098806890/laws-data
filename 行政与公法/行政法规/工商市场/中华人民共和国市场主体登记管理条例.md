# 中华人民共和国市场主体登记管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-07-27  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范市场主体登记管理行为，推进法治化市场建设，维护良好市场秩序和市场主体合法权益，优化营商环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating the registration and administration of market entities, promoting the construction of a law-based market, maintaining good market order and the legitimate rights and interests of market entities, and optimizing the business environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称市场主体，是指在中华人民共和国境内以营利为目的从事经营活动的下列自然人、法人及非法人组织：  
（一）公司、非公司企业法人及其分支机构；  
（二）个人独资企业、合伙企业及其分支机构；  
（三）农民专业合作社（联合社）及其分支机构；  
（四）个体工商户；  
（五）外国公司分支机构；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他市场主体。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, market entities mean the following natural persons, legal persons and unincorporated organizations that engage in business activities for profit within the territory of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) companies, non-company enterprise legal persons and their branches;  
(2) sole proprietorships, partnerships and their branches;  
(3) farmers' specialized cooperatives (cooperative associations) and their branches;  
(4) individual industrial and commercial households;  
(5) branches of foreign companies;  
(6) other market entities as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　市场主体应当依照本条例办理登记。未经登记，不得以市场主体名义从事经营活动。法律、行政法规规定无需办理登记的除外。  
市场主体登记包括设立登记、变更登记和注销登记。

**Article 3** Market entities shall register in accordance with these Regulations. Without registration, no market entity may engage in business activities in the name of a market entity, except where otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations that registration is not required.  
Market entity registration includes registration of establishment, registration of alteration, and registration of cancellation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　市场主体登记管理应当遵循依法合规、规范统一、公开透明、便捷高效的原则。

**Article 4** The registration administration of market entities shall follow the principles of legality and compliance, standardization and uniformity, openness and transparency, and convenience and efficiency.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院市场监督管理部门主管全国市场主体登记管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理部门主管本辖区市场主体登记管理工作，加强统筹指导和监督管理。

**Article 5** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall be in charge of the registration administration of market entities nationwide.  
The market supervision and administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the registration administration of market entities within their respective jurisdictions, and shall strengthen overall coordination, guidance, and supervision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院市场监督管理部门应当加强信息化建设，制定统一的市场主体登记数据和系统建设规范。  
县级以上地方人民政府承担市场主体登记工作的部门（以下称登记机关）应当优化市场主体登记办理流程，提高市场主体登记效率，推行当场办结、一次办结、限时办结等制度，实现集中办理、就近办理、网上办理、异地可办，提升市场主体登记便利化程度。

**Article 6** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall strengthen information technology construction and formulate unified standards for market entity registration data and system construction.  
The departments of local people's governments at or above the county level that undertake the work of market entity registration (hereinafter referred to as the registration authorities) shall optimize the market entity registration procedures, improve the efficiency of market entity registration, implement systems such as on-site completion, one-time completion, and completion within a time limit, and realize centralized processing, nearby processing, online processing, and off-site processing, thereby enhancing the convenience of market entity registration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院市场监督管理部门和国务院有关部门应当推动市场主体登记信息与其他政府信息的共享和运用，提升政府服务效能。

**Article 7** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council and the relevant departments of the State Council shall promote the sharing and utilization of market entity registration information and other government information to enhance the efficiency of government services.

### 第二章　登记事项

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　市场主体的一般登记事项包括：  
（一）名称；  
（二）主体类型；  
（三）经营范围；  
（四）住所或者主要经营场所；  
（五）注册资本或者出资额；  
（六）法定代表人、执行事务合伙人或者负责人姓名。  
除前款规定外，还应当根据市场主体类型登记下列事项：  
（一）有限责任公司股东、股份有限公司发起人、非公司企业法人出资人的姓名或者名称；  
（二）个人独资企业的投资人姓名及居所；  
（三）合伙企业的合伙人名称或者姓名、住所、承担责任方式；  
（四）个体工商户的经营者姓名、住所、经营场所；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他事项。

**Article 8** The general registration items for market entities include:  
(1) name;  
(2) type of entity;  
(3) business scope;  
(4) domicile or principal place of business;  
(5) registered capital or amount of contribution;  
(6) name of the legal representative, managing partner, or responsible person.  
In addition to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the following items shall also be registered according to the type of market entity:  
(1) the names or titles of shareholders of a limited liability company, promoters of a joint stock limited company, and contributors of a non-company enterprise legal person;  
(2) the name and domicile of the investor of a sole proprietorship;  
(3) the names or titles, domiciles, and methods of assuming liability of the partners of a partnership;  
(4) the name, domicile, and place of business of the operator of an individual industrial and commercial household;  
(5) other items as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　市场主体的下列事项应当向登记机关办理备案：  
（一）章程或者合伙协议；  
（二）经营期限或者合伙期限；  
（三）有限责任公司股东或者股份有限公司发起人认缴的出资数额，合伙企业合伙人认缴或者实际缴付的出资数额、缴付期限和出资方式；  
（四）公司董事、监事、高级管理人员；  
（五）农民专业合作社（联合社）成员；  
（六）参加经营的个体工商户家庭成员姓名；  
（七）市场主体登记联络员、外商投资企业法律文件送达接受人；  
（八）公司、合伙企业等市场主体受益所有人相关信息；  
（九）法律、行政法规规定的其他事项。

**Article 9** The following items of a market entity shall be filed with the registration authority:  
(1) the articles of association or partnership agreement;  
(2) the term of operation or partnership term;  
(3) the amount of capital contribution subscribed by shareholders of a limited liability company or promoters of a joint stock limited company, and the amount of capital contribution subscribed or actually paid by partners of a partnership, the time limit for payment, and the method of contribution;  
(4) directors, supervisors, and senior officers of the company;  
(5) members of a farmers' specialized cooperative (cooperative association);  
(6) the names of family members of an individual industrial and commercial household participating in business operations;  
(7) the contact person for market entity registration and the recipient of legal documents for foreign-invested enterprises;  
(8) information on beneficial owners of market entities such as companies and partnerships;  
(9) other items as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　市场主体只能登记一个名称，经登记的市场主体名称受法律保护。  
市场主体名称由申请人依法自主申报。

**Article 10** A market entity may only register one name, and the registered name of a market entity shall be protected by law.  
The name of a market entity shall be independently declared by the applicant according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　市场主体只能登记一个住所或者主要经营场所。  
电子商务平台内的自然人经营者可以根据国家有关规定，将电子商务平台提供的网络经营场所作为经营场所。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据有关法律、行政法规的规定和本地区实际情况，自行或者授权下级人民政府对住所或者主要经营场所作出更加便利市场主体从事经营活动的具体规定。

**Article 11** A market entity may only register one domicile or principal place of business.  
Natural person operators within an e-commerce platform may, in accordance with relevant state regulations, use the online business venue provided by the e-commerce platform as their business venue.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations and the actual local conditions, formulate or authorize the people's governments at lower levels to formulate specific provisions on domiciles or principal places of business that are more convenient for market entities to engage in business activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任公司、非公司企业法人的法定代表人：  
（一）无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力；  
（二）因贪污、贿赂、侵占财产、挪用财产或者破坏社会主义市场经济秩序被判处刑罚，执行期满未逾5年，或者因犯罪被剥夺政治权利，执行期满未逾5年；  
（三）担任破产清算的公司、非公司企业法人的法定代表人、董事或者厂长、经理，对破产负有个人责任的，自破产清算完结之日起未逾3年；  
（四）担任因违法被吊销营业执照、责令关闭的公司、非公司企业法人的法定代表人，并负有个人责任的，自被吊销营业执照之日起未逾3年；  
（五）个人所负数额较大的债务到期未清偿；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 12** A person under any of the following circumstances shall not serve as the legal representative of a company or a non-company enterprise legal person:  
(1) having no civil capacity or having limited civil capacity;  
(2) having been sentenced to a criminal penalty for embezzlement, bribery, misappropriation of property, or undermining the socialist market economic order, and less than five years have elapsed since the completion of the sentence, or having been deprived of political rights due to a crime, and less than five years have elapsed since the completion of the sentence;  
(3) having served as the legal representative, director, factory director, or manager of a company or non-company enterprise legal person that underwent bankruptcy liquidation and bearing personal liability for the bankruptcy, and less than three years have elapsed since the date of completion of the bankruptcy liquidation;  
(4) having served as the legal representative of a company or non-company enterprise legal person whose business license was revoked or that was ordered to close due to illegal activities, and bearing personal liability, and less than three years have elapsed since the date of revocation of the business license;  
(5) having a relatively large amount of personal debt that is due and unpaid;  
(6) other circumstances as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　除法律、行政法规或者国务院决定另有规定外，市场主体的注册资本或者出资额实行认缴登记制，以人民币表示。  
出资方式应当符合法律、行政法规的规定。公司股东、非公司企业法人出资人、农民专业合作社（联合社）成员不得以劳务、信用、自然人姓名、商誉、特许经营权或者设定担保的财产等作价出资。

**Article 13** Except as otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council, the registered capital or amount of contribution of a market entity shall be subject to the system of subscription registration, and shall be expressed in Renminbi.  
The method of capital contribution shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations. Shareholders of a company, contributors of a non-company enterprise legal person, and members of a farmers' specialized cooperative (cooperative association) shall not make capital contributions by valuing labor services, credit, natural person names, goodwill, franchise rights, or property subject to security interests, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　市场主体的经营范围包括一般经营项目和许可经营项目。经营范围中属于在登记前依法须经批准的许可经营项目，市场主体应当在申请登记时提交有关批准文件。  
市场主体应当按照登记机关公布的经营项目分类标准办理经营范围登记。

**Article 14** The business scope of a market entity includes general business items and licensed business items. For licensed business items within the business scope that require approval according to law before registration, the market entity shall submit the relevant approval documents when applying for registration.  
A market entity shall register its business scope in accordance with the classification standards for business items published by the registration authority.

### 第三章　登记规范

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　市场主体实行实名登记。申请人应当配合登记机关核验身份信息。

**Article 15** Market entities shall be registered with real names. The applicant shall cooperate with the registration authority in verifying identity information.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　申请办理市场主体登记，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）申请人资格文件、自然人身份证明；  
（三）住所或者主要经营场所相关文件；  
（四）公司、非公司企业法人、农民专业合作社（联合社）章程或者合伙企业合伙协议；  
（五）法律、行政法规和国务院市场监督管理部门规定提交的其他材料。  
国务院市场监督管理部门应当根据市场主体类型分别制定登记材料清单和文书格式样本，通过政府网站、登记机关服务窗口等向社会公开。  
登记机关能够通过政务信息共享平台获取的市场主体登记相关信息，不得要求申请人重复提供。

**Article 16** To apply for market entity registration, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) an application;  
(2) documents on the qualification of the applicant and identity certificates for natural persons;  
(3) documents related to the domicile or principal place of business;  
(4) the articles of association of a company, non-company enterprise legal person, or farmers' specialized cooperative (cooperative association) or the partnership agreement of a partnership;  
(5) other materials required to be submitted by laws, administrative regulations, and the market supervision and administration department of the State Council.  
The market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, according to the type of market entity, respectively formulate a list of registration materials and sample document formats, and make them public through government websites, service windows of registration authorities, etc.  
If the registration authority can obtain the relevant information on market entity registration through the government affairs information sharing platform, it shall not require the applicant to submit it repeatedly.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请人应当对提交材料的真实性、合法性和有效性负责。

**Article 17** The applicant shall be responsible for the authenticity, legality, and validity of the submitted materials.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请人可以委托其他自然人或者中介机构代其办理市场主体登记。受委托的自然人或者中介机构代为办理登记事宜应当遵守有关规定，不得提供虚假信息和材料。

**Article 18** An applicant may entrust another natural person or an intermediary agency to handle market entity registration on its behalf. The entrusted natural person or intermediary agency handling registration matters shall comply with relevant provisions and shall not provide false information or materials.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　登记机关应当对申请材料进行形式审查。对申请材料齐全、符合法定形式的予以确认并当场登记。不能当场登记的，应当在3个工作日内予以登记；情形复杂的，经登记机关负责人批准，可以再延长3个工作日。  
申请材料不齐全或者不符合法定形式的，登记机关应当一次性告知申请人需要补正的材料。

**Article 19** The registration authority shall conduct a formal examination of the application materials. If the application materials are complete and conform to the statutory form, the registration authority shall confirm and register on the spot. If registration cannot be made on the spot, it shall be registered within three working days; if the situation is complex, with the approval of the head of the registration authority, the time may be extended by another three working days.  
If the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form, the registration authority shall inform the applicant at one time of the materials that need to be supplemented.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　登记申请不符合法律、行政法规规定，或者可能危害国家安全、社会公共利益的，登记机关不予登记并说明理由。

**Article 20** If a registration application does not comply with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, or may endanger national security or public interests, the registration authority shall not register and shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请人申请市场主体设立登记，登记机关依法予以登记的，签发营业执照。营业执照签发日期为市场主体的成立日期。  
法律、行政法规或者国务院决定规定设立市场主体须经批准的，应当在批准文件有效期内向登记机关申请登记。

**Article 21** If an applicant applies for the establishment registration of a market entity and the registration authority registers it according to law, it shall issue a business license. The date of issuance of the business license shall be the date of establishment of the market entity.  
If the establishment of a market entity requires approval as provided by laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council, the application for registration shall be submitted to the registration authority within the validity period of the approval document.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　营业执照分为正本和副本，具有同等法律效力。  
电子营业执照与纸质营业执照具有同等法律效力。  
营业执照样式、电子营业执照标准由国务院市场监督管理部门统一制定。

**Article 22** Business licenses are divided into originals and duplicates, which have equal legal effect.  
Electronic business licenses and paper business licenses have equal legal effect.  
The form of business licenses and the standards for electronic business licenses shall be uniformly formulated by the market supervision and administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　市场主体设立分支机构，应当向分支机构所在地的登记机关申请登记。

**Article 23** A market entity that establishes a branch shall apply for registration with the registration authority in the location of the branch.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　市场主体变更登记事项，应当自作出变更决议、决定或者法定变更事项发生之日起30日内向登记机关申请变更登记。  
市场主体变更登记事项属于依法须经批准的，申请人应当在批准文件有效期内向登记机关申请变更登记。

**Article 24** A market entity that alters registration items shall apply to the registration authority for alteration registration within 30 days from the date of making the alteration resolution, decision, or the occurrence of the statutory alteration event.  
If the alteration of registration items by a market entity requires approval according to law, the applicant shall apply to the registration authority for alteration registration within the validity period of the approval document.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公司、非公司企业法人的法定代表人在任职期间发生[本条例第十二条](#art-12)所列情形之一的，应当向登记机关申请变更登记。

**Article 25** If, during the term of office, the legal representative of a company or a non-company enterprise legal person falls under any of the circumstances listed in Article 12 of these Regulations, the market entity shall apply to the registration authority for alteration registration.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　市场主体变更经营范围，属于依法须经批准的项目的，应当自批准之日起30日内申请变更登记。许可证或者批准文件被吊销、撤销或者有效期届满的，应当自许可证或者批准文件被吊销、撤销或者有效期届满之日起30日内向登记机关申请变更登记或者办理注销登记。

**Article 26** If a market entity alters its business scope to include items that require approval according to law, it shall apply for alteration registration within 30 days from the date of approval. If the permit or approval document is revoked, cancelled, or expired, the market entity shall apply to the registration authority for alteration registration or cancellation registration within 30 days from the date of revocation, cancellation, or expiration.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　市场主体变更住所或者主要经营场所跨登记机关辖区的，应当在迁入新的住所或者主要经营场所前，向迁入地登记机关申请变更登记。迁出地登记机关无正当理由不得拒绝移交市场主体档案等相关材料。

**Article 27** If a market entity changes its domicile or principal place of business to a location within the jurisdiction of another registration authority, it shall apply to the registration authority of the place of relocation for alteration registration before moving into the new domicile or principal place of business. The registration authority of the place of original location shall not refuse to transfer the relevant materials such as the market entity's files without justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　市场主体变更登记涉及营业执照记载事项的，登记机关应当及时为市场主体换发营业执照。

**Article 28** If the alteration registration involves items recorded on the business license, the registration authority shall promptly replace the business license for the market entity.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　市场主体变更[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定的备案事项的，应当自作出变更决议、决定或者法定变更事项发生之日起30日内向登记机关办理备案。农民专业合作社（联合社）成员发生变更的，应当自本会计年度终了之日起90日内向登记机关办理备案。

**Article 29** If a market entity changes the filing items specified in Article 9 of these Regulations, it shall file with the registration authority within 30 days from the date of making the alteration resolution, decision, or the occurrence of the statutory alteration event. If there is a change in the members of a farmers' specialized cooperative (cooperative association), it shall file with the registration authority within 90 days from the end of the current fiscal year.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　因自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等原因造成经营困难的，市场主体可以自主决定在一定时期内歇业。法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
市场主体应当在歇业前与职工依法协商劳动关系处理等有关事项。  
市场主体应当在歇业前向登记机关办理备案。登记机关通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示歇业期限、法律文书送达地址等信息。  
市场主体歇业的期限最长不得超过3年。市场主体在歇业期间开展经营活动的，视为恢复营业，市场主体应当通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示。  
市场主体歇业期间，可以以法律文书送达地址代替住所或者主要经营场所。

**Article 30** If a market entity experiences operational difficulties due to natural disasters, accidents, public health incidents, social security incidents, or other reasons, it may independently decide to suspend business for a certain period of time, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Before suspending business, the market entity shall consult with employees according to law on matters such as the handling of labor relations.  
Before suspending business, the market entity shall file with the registration authority. The registration authority shall publicly announce information such as the suspension period and the address for service of legal documents through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.  
The maximum suspension period for a market entity shall not exceed three years. If a market entity engages in business activities during the suspension period, it shall be deemed to have resumed business, and the market entity shall publicly announce this through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.  
During the suspension period, a market entity may use the address for service of legal documents in lieu of its domicile or principal place of business.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　市场主体因解散、被宣告破产或者其他法定事由需要终止的，应当依法向登记机关申请注销登记。经登记机关注销登记，市场主体终止。  
市场主体注销依法须经批准的，应当经批准后向登记机关申请注销登记。

**Article 31** If a market entity needs to terminate due to dissolution, being declared bankrupt, or other statutory reasons, it shall apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration according to law. Upon cancellation registration by the registration authority, the market entity shall terminate.  
If the cancellation of a market entity requires approval according to law, it shall apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　市场主体注销登记前依法应当清算的，清算组应当自成立之日起10日内将清算组成员、清算组负责人名单通过国家企业信用信息公示系统公告。清算组可以通过国家企业信用信息公示系统发布债权人公告。  
清算组应当自清算结束之日起30日内向登记机关申请注销登记。市场主体申请注销登记前，应当依法办理分支机构注销登记。

**Article 32** If a market entity shall be liquidated according to law before cancellation registration, the liquidation group shall, within 10 days from the date of its establishment, announce the list of members of the liquidation group and the person in charge of the liquidation group through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. The liquidation group may publish a creditor announcement through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.  
The liquidation group shall apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration within 30 days from the date of conclusion of the liquidation. Before applying for cancellation registration, the market entity shall handle the cancellation registration of its branches according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　市场主体未发生债权债务或者已将债权债务清偿完结，未发生或者已结清清偿费用、职工工资、社会保险费用、法定补偿金、应缴纳税款（滞纳金、罚款），并由全体投资人书面承诺对上述情况的真实性承担法律责任的，可以按照简易程序办理注销登记。  
市场主体应当将承诺书及注销登记申请通过国家企业信用信息公示系统公示，公示期为20日。在公示期内无相关部门、债权人及其他利害关系人提出异议的，市场主体可以于公示期届满之日起20日内向登记机关申请注销登记。  
个体工商户按照简易程序办理注销登记的，无需公示，由登记机关将个体工商户的注销登记申请推送至税务等有关部门，有关部门在10日内没有提出异议的，可以直接办理注销登记。  
市场主体注销依法须经批准的，或者市场主体被吊销营业执照、责令关闭、撤销，或者被列入经营异常名录的，不适用简易注销程序。

**Article 33** If a market entity has no creditor's rights or debts, or has fully settled its creditor's rights and debts, has not incurred or has settled liquidation expenses, employee wages, social insurance premiums, statutory compensation, and taxes payable (including late fees and fines), and all investors have made a written commitment to bear legal liability for the authenticity of the above circumstances, it may handle cancellation registration through a simplified procedure.  
The market entity shall publicly announce the commitment letter and the cancellation registration application through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, with a publicity period of 20 days. If no relevant department, creditor, or other interested party raises an objection within the publicity period, the market entity may apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration within 20 days from the expiry of the publicity period.  
For individual industrial and commercial households that handle cancellation registration through the simplified procedure, no publicity is required; the registration authority shall push the cancellation registration application of the individual industrial and commercial household to the tax and other relevant departments, and if no objection is raised by the relevant departments within 10 days, the cancellation registration may be directly handled.  
The simplified cancellation procedure shall not apply if the cancellation of the market entity requires approval according to law, or if the market entity's business license has been revoked, it has been ordered to close, has been cancelled, or has been included in the list of enterprises with abnormal operations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　人民法院裁定强制清算或者裁定宣告破产的，有关清算组、破产管理人可以持人民法院终结强制清算程序的裁定或者终结破产程序的裁定，直接向登记机关申请办理注销登记。

**Article 34** If a People's Court issues a ruling on compulsory liquidation or declares bankruptcy, the relevant liquidation group or bankruptcy administrator may, by bearing the People's Court's ruling on the conclusion of the compulsory liquidation procedure or the conclusion of the bankruptcy procedure, directly apply to the registration authority for cancellation registration.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　市场主体应当按照国家有关规定公示年度报告和登记相关信息。

**Article 35** Market entities shall publicly announce annual reports and relevant registration information in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　市场主体应当将营业执照置于住所或者主要经营场所的醒目位置。从事电子商务经营的市场主体应当在其首页显著位置持续公示营业执照信息或者相关链接标识。

**Article 36** Market entities shall place the business license in a conspicuous position at their domicile or principal place of business. Market entities engaged in e-commerce operations shall continuously display the business license information or relevant link identifiers in a prominent position on their homepage.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得伪造、涂改、出租、出借、转让营业执照。  
营业执照遗失或者毁坏的，市场主体应当通过国家企业信用信息公示系统声明作废，申请补领。  
登记机关依法作出变更登记、注销登记和撤销登记决定的，市场主体应当缴回营业执照。拒不缴回或者无法缴回营业执照的，由登记机关通过国家企业信用信息公示系统公告营业执照作废。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may forge, alter, lease, lend, or transfer a business license.  
If a business license is lost or damaged, the market entity shall declare it void through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and apply for a replacement.  
If the registration authority makes a decision on alteration registration, cancellation registration, or revocation of registration according to law, the market entity shall return the business license. If the market entity refuses to return or cannot return the business license, the registration authority shall announce the voidance of the business license through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　登记机关应当根据市场主体的信用风险状况实施分级分类监管。  
登记机关应当采取随机抽取检查对象、随机选派执法检查人员的方式，对市场主体登记事项进行监督检查，并及时向社会公开监督检查结果。

**Article 38** Registration authorities shall implement tiered and categorized supervision based on the credit risk status of market entities.  
Registration authorities shall adopt a method of randomly selecting inspection targets and randomly assigning law enforcement inspectors to conduct supervision and inspection of market entity registration matters, and shall promptly disclose the results of supervision and inspection to the public.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　登记机关对市场主体涉嫌违反本条例规定的行为进行查处，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）进入市场主体的经营场所实施现场检查；  
（二）查阅、复制、收集与市场主体经营活动有关的合同、票据、账簿以及其他资料；  
（三）向与市场主体经营活动有关的单位和个人调查了解情况；  
（四）依法责令市场主体停止相关经营活动；  
（五）依法查询涉嫌违法的市场主体的银行账户；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他职权。  
登记机关行使前款第四项、第五项规定的职权的，应当经登记机关主要负责人批准。

**Article 39** When investigating and punishing acts suspected of violating the provisions of these Regulations, a registration authority may exercise the following powers:  
(1) enter the business premises of a market entity to conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) consult, copy, and collect contracts, bills, account books, and other materials related to the business activities of the market entity;  
(3) investigate and understand the situation from units and individuals related to the business activities of the market entity;  
(4) order the market entity to suspend relevant business activities according to law;  
(5) inquire into the bank accounts of a market entity suspected of illegal activities according to law;  
(6) other powers as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where a registration authority exercises the powers specified in items (4) and (5) of the preceding paragraph, it shall obtain the approval of the principal responsible person of the registration authority.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　提交虚假材料或者采取其他欺诈手段隐瞒重要事实取得市场主体登记的，受虚假市场主体登记影响的自然人、法人和其他组织可以向登记机关提出撤销市场主体登记的申请。  
登记机关受理申请后，应当及时开展调查。经调查认定存在虚假市场主体登记情形的，登记机关应当撤销市场主体登记。相关市场主体和人员无法联系或者拒不配合的，登记机关可以将相关市场主体的登记时间、登记事项等通过国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公示，公示期为45日。相关市场主体及其利害关系人在公示期内没有提出异议的，登记机关可以撤销市场主体登记。  
因虚假市场主体登记被撤销的市场主体，其直接责任人自市场主体登记被撤销之日起3年内不得再次申请市场主体登记。登记机关应当通过国家企业信用信息公示系统予以公示。

**Article 40** If a market entity obtains registration by submitting false materials or using other fraudulent means to conceal important facts, the natural persons, legal persons, and other organizations affected by the false market entity registration may file an application with the registration authority for revocation of the market entity registration.  
After accepting the application, the registration authority shall promptly conduct an investigation. If it is determined through investigation that false market entity registration exists, the registration authority shall revoke the market entity registration. If the relevant market entity or persons cannot be contacted or refuse to cooperate, the registration authority may publicly announce the registration time and registration matters of the relevant market entity through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System, with a publicity period of 45 days. If no objection is raised by the relevant market entity and its interested parties within the publicity period, the registration authority may revoke the market entity registration.  
For a market entity whose registration is revoked due to false registration, the directly responsible person shall not apply for market entity registration again within three years from the date of revocation of the market entity registration. The registration authority shall publicly announce this through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　有下列情形之一的，登记机关可以不予撤销市场主体登记：  
（一）撤销市场主体登记可能对社会公共利益造成重大损害；  
（二）撤销市场主体登记后无法恢复到登记前的状态；  
（三）法律、行政法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 41** Under any of the following circumstances, the registration authority may not revoke the market entity registration:  
(1) revocation of the market entity registration may cause significant harm to public interests;  
(2) after revocation of the market entity registration, it is impossible to restore the pre-registration status;  
(3) other circumstances as stipulated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　登记机关或者其上级机关认定撤销市场主体登记决定错误的，可以撤销该决定，恢复原登记状态，并通过国家企业信用信息公示系统公示。

**Article 42** If the registration authority or its superior authority determines that the decision to revoke a market entity registration was erroneous, it may revoke that decision, restore the original registration status, and publicly announce this through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　未经设立登记从事经营活动的，由登记机关责令改正，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，依法责令关闭停业，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Any person engaging in business activities without establishment registration shall be ordered by the registration authority to make corrections, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the person refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be ordered to close down and cease business according to law, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　提交虚假材料或者采取其他欺诈手段隐瞒重要事实取得市场主体登记的，由登记机关责令改正，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款，吊销营业执照。

**Article 44** Any person who obtains market entity registration by submitting false materials or using other fraudulent means to conceal important facts shall be ordered by the registration authority to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　实行注册资本实缴登记制的市场主体虚报注册资本取得市场主体登记的，由登记机关责令改正，处虚报注册资本金额5%以上15%以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。  
实行注册资本实缴登记制的市场主体的发起人、股东虚假出资，未交付或者未按期交付作为出资的货币或者非货币财产的，或者在市场主体成立后抽逃出资的，由登记机关责令改正，处虚假出资金额5%以上15%以下的罚款。

**Article 45** If a market entity subject to the system of paid-in registration of registered capital obtains market entity registration by falsely reporting the registered capital, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 15% of the falsely reported registered capital; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.  
If the promoters or shareholders of a market entity subject to the system of paid-in registration of registered capital make false capital contributions, fail to deliver or fail to deliver on time the monetary or non-monetary property as capital contributions, or withdraw capital contributions after the establishment of the market entity, the registration authority shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 15% of the falsely contributed amount.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　市场主体未依照本条例办理变更登记的，由登记机关责令改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 46** If a market entity fails to undergo alteration registration in accordance with these Regulations, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　市场主体未依照本条例办理备案的，由登记机关责令改正；拒不改正的，处5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** If a market entity fails to undergo filing in accordance with these Regulations, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　市场主体未依照本条例将营业执照置于住所或者主要经营场所醒目位置的，由登记机关责令改正；拒不改正的，处3万元以下的罚款。  
从事电子商务经营的市场主体未在其首页显著位置持续公示营业执照信息或者相关链接标识的，由登记机关依照《中华人民共和国电子商务法》处罚。  
市场主体伪造、涂改、出租、出借、转让营业执照的，由登记机关没收违法所得，处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，吊销营业执照。

**Article 48** If a market entity fails to place its business license in a conspicuous position at its domicile or principal place of business in accordance with these Regulations, the registration authority shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If a market entity engaged in e-commerce operations fails to continuously display business license information or relevant link identifiers in a prominent position on its homepage, the registration authority shall impose penalties in accordance with the Electronic Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If a market entity forges, alters, leases, lends, or transfers its business license, the registration authority shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定的，登记机关确定罚款金额时，应当综合考虑市场主体的类型、规模、违法情节等因素。

**Article 49** Where the provisions of these Regulations are violated, the registration authority, when determining the amount of the fine, shall comprehensively consider factors such as the type, scale, and circumstances of the violation of the market entity.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　登记机关及其工作人员违反本条例规定未履行职责或者履行职责不当的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 50** If a registration authority or its staff member fails to perform duties or performs duties improperly in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　法律、行政法规对市场主体登记管理违法行为处罚另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 52** Where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise for penalties for violations of market entity registration administration, such provisions shall prevail.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院市场监督管理部门可以依照本条例制定市场主体登记和监督管理的具体办法。

**Article 53** The market supervision and administration department of the State Council may formulate specific measures for market entity registration and supervision and administration in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　无固定经营场所摊贩的管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据当地实际情况另行规定。

**Article 54** The measures for the administration of vendors without a fixed place of business shall be separately formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on the actual local conditions.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2022年3月1日起施行。《中华人民共和国公司登记管理条例》、《中华人民共和国企业法人登记管理条例》、《中华人民共和国合伙企业登记管理办法》、《农民专业合作社登记管理条例》、《企业法人法定代表人登记管理规定》同时废止。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2022. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Registration Administration of Companies, the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Registration Administration of Enterprise Legal Persons, the Measures of the People's Republic of China for the Registration Administration of Partnerships, the Regulations on the Registration Administration of Farmers' Specialized Cooperatives, and the Provisions on the Registration Administration of Legal Representatives of Enterprise Legal Persons shall be simultaneously abolished.
