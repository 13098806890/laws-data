# 控制对企业进行经济检查的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1999-07-23  
**生效日期**：1999-07-23

> (1999年7月9日国务院批准　1999年7月23日国家经济贸易委员会发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了规范行政机关对企业进行经济检查的行为，减轻企业负担，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定所称经济检查，是指行政机关对企业生产、经营活动遵守法律、法规情况进行的检查。 前款行政机关包括经贸、财政、审计、价格、税务、工商行政管理、质量技术监督、外汇管理、统计等部门。 第三条　除法律、法规明确规定外，任何行政机关不得擅自对企业进行经济检查。 第四条　行政机关依照法律、法规对企业进行经济检查，应当统筹安排、注重效率、保证质量、避免重复。 第五条　行政机关对企业进行经济检查，应当事先拟订检查计划。检查计划应当包括检查依据、检查时间、检查对象、检查事项等内容。 行政机关应当将检查计划报同级人民政府指定的部门(以下统称指定部门)备案。指定部门应当对有关行政机关的检查计划进行必要的协调，能够合并的，应当合并；可以联合实施的，应当组织有关行政机关联合实施检查。 实行垂直管理体制的行政机关对企业进行经济检查，应当将检查计划报上一级机关备案。上一级机关应当对下一级行政机关的检查计划进行必要的协调，能够合并的，应当合并。 第六条　同一行政机关对同一企业的经济检查每年一般不得超过一次；法律、行政法规和本规定另有规定的除外。 第七条　税务机关对企业进行税务检查，应当按照归口管理、统一检查的原则，由国税机关、地税机关按照检查计划联合组织实施，省(自治区、直辖市)、市(州、盟)、县(旗)三级统筹协调。税务机关对同一企业的税务检查，每年不得超过两次。 质量技术监督机关对同一企业的产品质量进行监督抽查，除质量抽查不合格的外，每年不得超过两次。 对已经派入国务院稽察特派员的国有重点大型企业，除法律、行政法规另有规定外，有关行政机关不再对企业进行财务检查。 第八条　行政机关对企业进行经济检查时，应当出具检查通知书。检查通知书应当包括下列内容： (一)检查的法律、法规依据；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范行政机关对企业进行经济检查的行为，减轻企业负担，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purposes of regulating economic inspections conducted by administrative organs on enterprises, reducing the burden on enterprises, and maintaining the order of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称经济检查，是指行政机关对企业生产、经营活动遵守法律、法规情况进行的检查。  
前款行政机关包括经贸、财政、审计、价格、税务、工商行政管理、质量技术监督、外汇管理、统计等部门。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Provisions, "economic inspection" means the inspection conducted by an administrative organ on the compliance of an enterprise's production and business operations with laws and regulations.  
The administrative organs mentioned in the preceding paragraph include the departments of economy and trade, finance, audit, pricing, taxation, administrative regulation for industry and commerce, quality and technical supervision, foreign exchange administration, statistics, and other departments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　除法律、法规明确规定外，任何行政机关不得擅自对企业进行经济检查。

**Article 3** Unless explicitly provided for by laws or regulations, no administrative organ shall conduct economic inspections on enterprises without authorization.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　行政机关依照法律、法规对企业进行经济检查，应当统筹安排、注重效率、保证质量、避免重复。

**Article 4** Where an administrative organ conducts economic inspections on enterprises in accordance with laws or regulations, it shall make overall arrangements, focus on efficiency, ensure quality, and avoid duplication.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行政机关对企业进行经济检查，应当事先拟订检查计划。检查计划应当包括检查依据、检查时间、检查对象、检查事项等内容。  
行政机关应当将检查计划报同级人民政府指定的部门(以下统称指定部门)备案。指定部门应当对有关行政机关的检查计划进行必要的协调，能够合并的，应当合并；可以联合实施的，应当组织有关行政机关联合实施检查。  
实行垂直管理体制的行政机关对企业进行经济检查，应当将检查计划报上一级机关备案。上一级机关应当对下一级行政机关的检查计划进行必要的协调，能够合并的，应当合并。

**Article 5** Before conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise, an administrative organ shall formulate an inspection plan in advance. The inspection plan shall include the basis for the inspection, the time of inspection, the target of inspection, the matters to be inspected, and other contents.  
The administrative organ shall report the inspection plan to the department designated by the people's government at the same level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the designated department) for archival filing. The designated department shall make necessary coordination of the inspection plans of relevant administrative organs; where they can be merged, they shall be merged; where they can be implemented jointly, the designated department shall organize the relevant administrative organs to conduct the inspection jointly.  
Where an administrative organ under a vertical management system conducts economic inspections on enterprises, it shall report the inspection plan to the organ at the next higher level for archival filing. The organ at the next higher level shall make necessary coordination of the inspection plans of the administrative organ at the next lower level; where they can be merged, they shall be merged.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　同一行政机关对同一企业的经济检查每年一般不得超过一次；法律、行政法规和本规定另有规定的除外。

**Article 6** Generally, the same administrative organ shall conduct no more than one economic inspection on the same enterprise each year, except as otherwise provided for by laws, administrative regulations, or these Provisions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　税务机关对企业进行税务检查，应当按照归口管理、统一检查的原则，由国税机关、地税机关按照检查计划联合组织实施，省(自治区、直辖市)、市(州、盟)、县(旗)三级统筹协调。税务机关对同一企业的税务检查，每年不得超过两次。  
质量技术监督机关对同一企业的产品质量进行监督抽查，除质量抽查不合格的外，每年不得超过两次。  
对已经派入国务院稽察特派员的国有重点大型企业，除法律、行政法规另有规定外，有关行政机关不再对企业进行财务检查。

**Article 7** Where a tax authority conducts a tax inspection on an enterprise, it shall, in accordance with the principle of centralized management and unified inspection, be jointly organized and implemented by the State taxation authority and the local taxation authority according to the inspection plan, with overall coordination at the provincial (autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government), municipal (prefecture, league), and county (banner) levels. The tax authority shall conduct no more than two tax inspections on the same enterprise each year.  
The quality and technical supervision authority shall conduct no more than two supervisory spot checks on the product quality of the same enterprise each year, except where the product quality fails the spot check.  
For key large state-owned enterprises to which the State Council has sent special inspectors, no relevant administrative organ shall conduct financial inspections on the enterprises, except as otherwise provided for by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行政机关对企业进行经济检查时，应当出具检查通知书。检查通知书应当包括下列内容：  
(一)检查的法律、法规依据；  
(二)检查内容；  
(三)检查时限；  
(四)实施检查的人员及其负责人。

**Article 8** When conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise, the administrative organ shall produce a notice of inspection. The notice of inspection shall include the following contents:  
(1) the legal or regulatory basis for the inspection;  
(2) the contents of the inspection;  
(3) the time limit for the inspection;  
(4) the personnel conducting the inspection and the person in charge thereof.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政机关在对企业进行经济检查结束后，应当及时提出客观、真实、明确的检查报告，并报指定部门或者上级部门备案。指定部门或者上级部门应当根据情况，将检查报告抄送有关行政机关。  
行政机关对企业的检查报告能够满足其他行政机关履行其职责需要的，其他行政机关应当加以利用，避免重复检查。

**Article 9** After completing an economic inspection on an enterprise, the administrative organ shall promptly produce an objective, truthful, and clear inspection report, and report it to the designated department or the department at the next higher level for archival filing. The designated department or the department at the next higher level shall, as the case may be, send a copy of the inspection report to the relevant administrative organs.  
Where an administrative organ's inspection report on an enterprise can satisfy the needs of other administrative organs in performing their duties, such other administrative organs shall make use of it to avoid duplicate inspections.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　行政机关对企业进行经济检查，除法律、法规、国务院及财政部、国家计委以及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定的收费项目外，不得向企业收取任何费用，也不得以任何形式将检查费用转嫁给企业。

**Article 10** When conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise, an administrative organ shall not charge any fees to the enterprise, nor shift the inspection costs to the enterprise in any form, except for fees specified by laws, regulations, the State Council, the Ministry of Finance, the State Development Planning Commission, or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政机关的工作人员对企业进行经济检查时，不得接受被检查企业的任何馈赠、报酬、福利待遇，不得在被检查企业报销任何费用，不得参加被检查企业提供的宴请、娱乐、旅游等活动，不得通过检查工作为本人、亲友或者他人谋取利益。

**Article 11** When conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise, the staff members of the administrative organ shall not accept any gifts, remuneration, or welfare benefits from the enterprise under inspection, shall not claim reimbursement of any expenses from the enterprise under inspection, shall not participate in banquets, entertainment, tours, or other activities provided by the enterprise under inspection, and shall not seek benefits for themselves, their relatives, or others through the inspection work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行政机关有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
(一)没有检查通知书，擅自对企业进行经济检查的；  
(二)同一行政机关在一年内对同一企业的经济检查超过规定次数的；  
(三)违法向被检查企业收取费用或者将检查费用转嫁给企业的。

**Article 12** Where an administrative organ commits any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and the other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise without authorization, without a notice of inspection;  
(2) the same administrative organ conducts more than the prescribed number of economic inspections on the same enterprise within one year;  
(3) illegally charging fees to the enterprise under inspection or shifting the inspection costs to the enterprise.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　行政机关的工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予警告直至开除的行政处分：  
(一)违反规定，擅自到企业进行经济检查的；  
(二)接受被检查企业的馈赠、报酬、福利待遇的；  
(三)在被检查企业报销费用的；  
(四)参加被检查企业提供的宴请、娱乐、旅游等活动的；  
(五)通过检查工作为本人、亲友或者他人谋取利益的。  
前款行政机关的工作人员接受的被检查企业的物品、报酬、福利待遇，依法予以没收、追缴或者责令退赔；在被检查企业报销的费用或者参加被检查企业提供的宴请、娱乐、旅游活动，责令退赔或者自行支付相关费用。

**Article 13** Where a staff member of an administrative organ commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction ranging from a warning to dismissal according to law:  
(1) in violation of the provisions, conducting an economic inspection on an enterprise without authorization;  
(2) accepting gifts, remuneration, or welfare benefits from the enterprise under inspection;  
(3) claiming reimbursement of expenses from the enterprise under inspection;  
(4) participating in banquets, entertainment, tours, or other activities provided by the enterprise under inspection;  
(5) seeking benefits for himself, his relatives, or others through the inspection work.  
The items, remuneration, and welfare benefits accepted by the staff members of the administrative organ mentioned in the preceding paragraph from the enterprise under inspection shall be confiscated, recovered, or ordered to be refunded according to law; the expenses claimed for reimbursement from the enterprise under inspection, or the expenses incurred from participating in banquets, entertainment, tours, or other activities provided by the enterprise under inspection, shall be ordered to be refunded or paid by themselves.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对违反本规定对企业的经济检查，企业有权抵制，任何单位和个人有权向监察等有关部门检举。收到检举的部门应当负责保密并及时处理。

**Article 14** An enterprise shall have the right to resist an economic inspection conducted in violation of these Provisions, and any entity or individual shall have the right to report such inspection to the supervisory department or other relevant departments. The department that receives the report shall maintain confidentiality and handle the matter promptly.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　行政机关的工作人员对检举人进行报复陷害，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 15** Where a staff member of an administrative organ retaliates against a person who has made a report, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　行政机关有理由认为企业存在违法行为或者违法嫌疑，依法进行调查的，不受[本规定第四条](#art-4)、第五条、第六条、第七条、第八条、第九条的限制。

**Article 16** Where an administrative organ has reason to believe that an enterprise has committed an illegal act or is suspected of committing an illegal act, and conducts an investigation according to law, it shall not be restricted by Articles 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院决定、国务院有关部门经国务院批准决定或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定进行的专项检查，分别按照国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的专项检查的范围、内容、时限和程序进行。

**Article 17** Special inspections decided by the State Council, by the relevant department of the State Council with the approval of the State Council, or by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be conducted respectively in accordance with the scope, contents, time limit, and procedures determined by the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 18** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　其他行政机关对企业的检查，参照本规定执行。

**Article 19** Inspections conducted by other administrative organs on enterprises shall be carried out with reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
