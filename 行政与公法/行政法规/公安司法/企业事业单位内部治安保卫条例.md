# 企业事业单位内部治安保卫条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2004-09-27  
**生效日期**：2004-12-01

> (2004年9月13日国务院第64次常务会议通过　2004年9月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第421号公布　自2004年12月1日起施行) 第一条　为了规范企业、事业单位(以下简称单位)内部治安保卫工作，保护公民人身、财产安全和公共财产安全，维护单位的工作、生产、经营、教学和科研秩序，制定本条例。 第二条　单位内部治安保卫工作贯彻预防为主、单位负责、突出重点、保障安全的方针。 单位内部治安保卫工作应当突出保护单位内人员的人身安全，单位不得以经济效益、财产安全或者其他任何借口忽视人身安全。 第三条　国务院公安部门指导、监督全国的单位内部治安保卫工作，对行业、系统有监管职责的国务院有关部门指导、检查本行业、本系统的单位内部治安保卫工作；县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关指导、监督本行政区域内的单位内部治安保卫工作，对行业、系统有监管职责的县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门指导、检查本行政区域内的本行业、本系统的单位内部治安保卫工作，及时解决单位内部治安保卫工作中的突出问题。 第四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对本行政区域内的单位内部治安保卫工作的领导，督促公安机关和有关部门依法履行职责，并及时协调解决单位内部治安保卫工作中的重大问题。 第五条　单位的主要负责人对本单位的内部治安保卫工作负责。 第六条　单位应当根据内部治安保卫工作需要，设置治安保卫机构或者配备专职、兼职治安保卫人员。 治安保卫重点单位应当设置与治安保卫任务相适应的治安保卫机构，配备专职治安保卫人员，并将治安保卫机构的设置和人员的配备情况报主管公安机关备案。 第七条　单位内部治安保卫工作的要求是： (一)有适应单位具体情况的内部治安保卫制度、措施和必要的治安防范设施； (二)单位范围内的治安保卫情况有人检查，重要部位得到重点保护，治安隐患及时得到排查； (三)单位范围内的治安隐患和问题及时得到处理，发生治安案件、涉嫌刑事犯罪的案件及时得到处置。 第八条　单位制定的内部治安保卫制度应当包括下列内容： (一)门卫、值班、巡查制度；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范企业、事业单位(以下简称单位)内部治安保卫工作，保护公民人身、财产安全和公共财产安全，维护单位的工作、生产、经营、教学和科研秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to regulate the internal security work of enterprises and public institutions (hereinafter referred to as units), to protect the personal safety of citizens, property safety and public property safety, and to maintain the order of work, production, operation, teaching and scientific research of units.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　单位内部治安保卫工作贯彻预防为主、单位负责、突出重点、保障安全的方针。  
单位内部治安保卫工作应当突出保护单位内人员的人身安全，单位不得以经济效益、财产安全或者其他任何借口忽视人身安全。

**Article 2** The internal security work of a unit shall follow the principle of prevention first, unit responsibility, highlighting key points, and ensuring safety.  
The internal security work of a unit shall highlight the protection of personal safety of personnel within the unit, and the unit shall not neglect personal safety on the excuse of economic benefits, property safety or any other pretext.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院公安部门指导、监督全国的单位内部治安保卫工作，对行业、系统有监管职责的国务院有关部门指导、检查本行业、本系统的单位内部治安保卫工作；县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关指导、监督本行政区域内的单位内部治安保卫工作，对行业、系统有监管职责的县级以上地方各级人民政府有关部门指导、检查本行政区域内的本行业、本系统的单位内部治安保卫工作，及时解决单位内部治安保卫工作中的突出问题。

**Article 3** The public security department of the State Council shall guide and supervise the internal security work of units throughout the country, and the relevant departments of the State Council with regulatory responsibilities for industries and systems shall guide and inspect the internal security work of units in their respective industries and systems; the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide and supervise the internal security work of units within their respective administrative regions, and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level with regulatory responsibilities for industries and systems shall guide and inspect the internal security work of units in their respective industries and systems within their respective administrative regions, and shall promptly solve prominent problems in the internal security work of units.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对本行政区域内的单位内部治安保卫工作的领导，督促公安机关和有关部门依法履行职责，并及时协调解决单位内部治安保卫工作中的重大问题。

**Article 4** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over the internal security work of units within their respective administrative regions, urge the public security organs and relevant departments to perform their duties according to law, and shall promptly coordinate and solve major problems in the internal security work of units.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　单位的主要负责人对本单位的内部治安保卫工作负责。

**Article 5** The main person in charge of a unit shall be responsible for the internal security work of the unit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　单位应当根据内部治安保卫工作需要，设置治安保卫机构或者配备专职、兼职治安保卫人员。  
治安保卫重点单位应当设置与治安保卫任务相适应的治安保卫机构，配备专职治安保卫人员，并将治安保卫机构的设置和人员的配备情况报主管公安机关备案。

**Article 6** A unit shall, based on the needs of its internal security work, set up a security organ or assign full-time or part-time security personnel.  
A key security unit shall set up a security organ commensurate with its security tasks, assign full-time security personnel, and report the establishment of the security organ and the assignment of personnel to the competent public security organ for record.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　单位内部治安保卫工作的要求是：  
(一)有适应单位具体情况的内部治安保卫制度、措施和必要的治安防范设施；  
(二)单位范围内的治安保卫情况有人检查，重要部位得到重点保护，治安隐患及时得到排查；  
(三)单位范围内的治安隐患和问题及时得到处理，发生治安案件、涉嫌刑事犯罪的案件及时得到处置。

**Article 7** The requirements for the internal security work of a unit are as follows:  
(1) There shall be internal security systems, measures and necessary security prevention facilities suitable for the specific circumstances of the unit;  
(2) The security situation within the unit shall be inspected by designated personnel, key parts shall be given key protection, and potential security hazards shall be promptly investigated;  
(3) Potential security hazards and problems within the unit shall be promptly addressed, and when public security cases or cases suspected of criminal offenses occur, they shall be promptly handled.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　单位制定的内部治安保卫制度应当包括下列内容：  
(一)门卫、值班、巡查制度；  
(二)工作、生产、经营、教学、科研等场所的安全管理制度；  
(三)现金、票据、印鉴、有价证券等重要物品使用、保管、储存、运输的安全管理制度；  
(四)单位内部的消防、交通安全管理制度；  
(五)治安防范教育培训制度；  
(六)单位内部发生治安案件、涉嫌刑事犯罪案件的报告制度；  
(七)治安保卫工作检查、考核及奖惩制度；  
(八)存放有爆炸性、易燃性、放射性、毒害性、传染性、腐蚀性等危险物品和传染性菌种、毒种以及武器弹药的单位，还应当有相应的安全管理制度；  
(九)其他有关的治安保卫制度。  
单位制定的内部治安保卫制度不得与法律、法规、规章的规定相抵触。

**Article 8** The internal security system formulated by a unit shall include the following contents:  
(1) A system for gatekeeping, duty, and patrol;  
(2) A safety management system for places such as work, production, operation, teaching, and scientific research;  
(3) A safety management system for the use, custody, storage, and transportation of important items such as cash, bills, seals, and negotiable securities;  
(4) A fire control and traffic safety management system within the unit;  
(5) An education and training system for security prevention;  
(6) A reporting system for public security cases and cases suspected of criminal offenses occurring within the unit;  
(7) A system for inspection, assessment, rewards, and penalties for security work;  
(8) Units that store dangerous articles such as explosive, inflammable, radioactive, toxic, infectious, corrosive articles and infectious bacterial strains, virus strains, as well as weapons and ammunition, shall also have a corresponding safety management system;  
(9) Other relevant security systems.  
The internal security system formulated by a unit shall not conflict with the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　单位内部治安保卫人员应当接受有关法律知识和治安保卫业务、技能以及相关专业知识的培训、考核。

**Article 9** The security personnel of a unit shall undergo training and assessment in relevant legal knowledge, security work, skills, and related professional knowledge.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　单位内部治安保卫人员应当依法、文明履行职责，不得侵犯他人合法权益。治安保卫人员依法履行职责的行为受法律保护。

**Article 10** The security personnel of a unit shall perform their duties according to law and in a civilized manner, and shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of others. The act of security personnel in performing their duties according to law shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　单位内部治安保卫机构、治安保卫人员应当履行下列职责：  
(一)开展治安防范宣传教育，并落实本单位的内部治安保卫制度和治安防范措施；  
(二)根据需要，检查进入本单位人员的证件，登记出入的物品和车辆；  
(三)在单位范围内进行治安防范巡逻和检查，建立巡逻、检查和治安隐患整改记录；  
(四)维护单位内部的治安秩序，制止发生在本单位的违法行为，对难以制止的违法行为以及发生的治安案件、涉嫌刑事犯罪案件应当立即报警，并采取措施保护现场，配合公安机关的侦查、处置工作；  
(五)督促落实单位内部治安防范设施的建设和维护。

**Article 11** The security organ and security personnel of a unit shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Conduct publicity and education on security prevention, and implement the internal security system and security prevention measures of the unit;  
(2) Inspect the credentials of persons entering the unit as necessary, and register the articles and vehicles entering and leaving;  
(3) Conduct security prevention patrols and inspections within the unit, and maintain records of patrols, inspections, and rectification of potential security hazards;  
(4) Maintain the security order within the unit, stop illegal acts occurring in the unit, and for illegal acts that are difficult to stop, as well as public security cases and cases suspected of criminal offenses, shall immediately report to the police, take measures to protect the scene, and cooperate with the public security organs in investigation and handling;  
(5) Urge the implementation of the construction and maintenance of security prevention facilities within the unit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在单位管理范围内的人员，应当遵守单位的内部治安保卫制度。

**Article 12** Persons within the management scope of a unit shall abide by the internal security system of the unit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　关系全国或者所在地区国计民生、国家安全和公共安全的单位是治安保卫重点单位。治安保卫重点单位由县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关按照下列范围提出，报本级人民政府确定：  
(一)广播电台、电视台、通讯社等重要新闻单位；  
(二)机场、港口、大型车站等重要交通枢纽；  
(三)国防科技工业重要产品的研制、生产单位；  
(四)电信、邮政、金融单位；  
(五)大型能源动力设施、水利设施和城市水、电、燃气、热力供应设施；  
(六)大型物资储备单位和大型商贸中心；  
(七)教育、科研、医疗单位和大型文化、体育场所；  
(八)博物馆、档案馆和重点文物保护单位；  
(九)研制、生产、销售、储存危险物品或者实验、保藏传染性菌种、毒种的单位；  
(十)国家重点建设工程单位；  
(十一)其他需要列为治安保卫重点的单位。  
治安保卫重点单位应当遵守本条例对单位治安保卫工作的一般规定和对治安保卫重点单位的特别规定。

**Article 13** Units that are vital to the national economy, the people's livelihood, national security and public security of the whole country or the region where they are located are key security units. The key security units shall be proposed by the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level within the following scope and submitted to the people's government at the same level for determination:  
(1) Important news units such as radio stations, television stations, and news agencies;  
(2) Important transportation hubs such as airports, ports, and large railway stations;  
(3) Units for the research, development and production of important products of the national defense science, technology and industry;  
(4) Telecommunications, postal, and financial units;  
(5) Large energy and power facilities, water conservancy facilities, and urban water, electricity, gas, and heat supply facilities;  
(6) Large material reserve units and large commercial centers;  
(7) Educational, scientific research, medical units, and large cultural and sports venues;  
(8) Museums, archives, and key cultural relic protection units;  
(9) Units that research, develop, produce, sell, store dangerous articles or experiment with and preserve infectious bacterial strains and virus strains;  
(10) Units of key national construction projects;  
(11) Other units that need to be listed as key security units.  
Key security units shall comply with the general provisions of these Regulations on unit security work and the special provisions on key security units.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　治安保卫重点单位应当确定本单位的治安保卫重要部位，按照有关国家标准对重要部位设置必要的技术防范设施，并实施重点保护。

**Article 14** A key security unit shall determine the key security parts of the unit, set up necessary technical prevention facilities for the key parts in accordance with relevant national standards, and implement key protection.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　治安保卫重点单位应当在公安机关指导下制定单位内部治安突发事件处置预案，并定期演练。

**Article 15** A key security unit shall, under the guidance of the public security organ, formulate a plan for handling internal security emergencies and conduct regular drills.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公安机关对本行政区域内的单位内部治安保卫工作履行下列职责：  
(一)指导单位制定、完善内部治安保卫制度，落实治安防范措施，指导治安保卫人员队伍建设和治安保卫重点单位的治安保卫机构建设；  
(二)检查、指导单位的内部治安保卫工作，发现单位有违反本条例规定的行为或者治安隐患，及时下达整改通知书，责令限期整改；  
(三)接到单位内部发生治安案件、涉嫌刑事犯罪案件的报警，及时出警，依法处置。

**Article 16** The public security organs shall perform the following duties with respect to the internal security work of units within their respective administrative regions:  
(1) Guide units to formulate and improve internal security systems, implement security prevention measures, and guide the construction of the security personnel team and the security organs of key security units;  
(2) Inspect and guide the internal security work of units, and upon discovering that a unit has violated the provisions of these Regulations or has potential security hazards, shall promptly issue a notice of rectification and order rectification within a time limit;  
(3) Upon receiving an alarm of a public security case or a case suspected of a criminal offense occurring within a unit, shall promptly dispatch police and handle it according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对认真落实治安防范措施，严格执行治安保卫工作制度，在单位内部治安保卫工作中取得显著成绩的单位和个人，有关人民政府、公安机关和有关部门应当给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 17** Units and individuals that conscientiously implement security prevention measures, strictly enforce the security work system, and achieve outstanding results in the internal security work of the unit shall be given commendations and rewards by the relevant people's governments, public security organs, and relevant departments.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　单位治安保卫人员因履行治安保卫职责伤残或者死亡的，依照国家有关工伤保险、评定伤残、批准烈士的规定给予相应的待遇。

**Article 18** If a security personnel of a unit is disabled or dies as a result of performing security duties, the person shall be given corresponding treatment in accordance with the state provisions on work-related injury insurance, disability assessment, and approval of martyrs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　单位违反本条例的规定，存在治安隐患的，公安机关应当责令限期整改，并处警告；单位逾期不整改，造成公民人身伤害、公私财产损失，或者严重威胁公民人身安全、公私财产安全或者公共安全的，对单位处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对单位主要负责人和其他直接责任人员处500元以上5000元以下的罚款，并可以建议有关组织对单位主要负责人和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Where a unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and has potential security hazards, the public security organ shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a warning; if the unit fails to rectify within the time limit and causes personal injury to citizens, loss of public or private property, or seriously threatens the personal safety of citizens, public or private property safety or public security, the unit shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, the main person in charge of the unit and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan, and the public security organ may suggest that the relevant organization impose sanctions on the main person in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　单位治安保卫人员在履行职责时侵害他人合法权益的，应当赔礼道歉，给他人造成损害的，单位应当承担赔偿责任。单位赔偿后，有权责令因故意或者重大过失造成侵权的治安保卫人员承担部分或者全部赔偿的费用；对故意或者重大过失造成侵权的治安保卫人员，单位应当依法给予处分。治安保卫人员侵害他人合法权益的行为属于受单位负责人指使、胁迫的，对单位负责人依法给予处分，并由其承担赔偿责任；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where a security personnel of a unit infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of others when performing duties, the person shall make an apology; if damage is caused to others, the unit shall bear compensation liability. After the unit has compensated, it shall have the right to order the security personnel who caused the infringement intentionally or through gross negligence to bear part or all of the compensation costs; the unit shall impose sanctions on the security personnel who caused the infringement intentionally or through gross negligence according to law. If the act of the security personnel infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others is committed under the instruction or coercion of the person in charge of the unit, the person in charge shall be given sanctions according to law and shall bear compensation liability; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公安机关接到单位报警后不依法履行职责，致使公民人身、财产和公共财产遭受损失，或者有其他玩忽职守、滥用职权行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
对行业、系统有监管职责的人民政府有关部门在指导、检查本行业、本系统的单位内部治安保卫工作过程中有玩忽职守、滥用职权行为的，参照前款规定处罚。

**Article 21** Where a public security organ fails to perform its duties according to law after receiving an alarm from a unit, resulting in loss to the person and property of citizens or public property, or commits other acts of dereliction of duty or abuse of power, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the relevant departments of the people's government with regulatory responsibilities for industries and systems commit acts of dereliction of duty or abuse of power in the process of guiding and inspecting the internal security work of units in their respective industries and systems, punishment shall be imposed by reference to the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机关、团体的内部治安保卫工作参照本条例的有关规定执行。  
高等学校治安保卫工作的具体规定由国务院另行制定。

**Article 22** The internal security work of state organs and organizations shall be implemented by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.  
The specific provisions on the security work of institutions of higher education shall be formulated separately by the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自2004年12月1日起施行。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2004.
