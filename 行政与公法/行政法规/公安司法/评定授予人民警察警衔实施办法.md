# 评定授予人民警察警衔实施办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1992-08-06  
**生效日期**：1992-07-01

> (国务院批转　1992年8月6日公安部发布) 《中华人民共和国人民警察警衔条例》(以下简称《条例》)已经第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议通过，并于一九九二年七月一日公布施行。根据《条例》规定和国务院指示，拟定本实施办法。 一、评定授予警衔的范围 评定授予警衔的人员，必须是属于人民警察建制的在编在职的人民警察。凡不具有人民警察性质的单位和不担任人民警察职务的人员，不实行警衔制度。 (一)评定授予警衔的人员为：各级公安部门(包括公安部门设在铁道、交通、民航、林业部门的公安机构)、国家安全部门和劳动改造劳动教养管理部门中，从事指挥决策、监督保障和业务工作的人民警察；各级人民法院、人民检察院的司法警察；警察专业技术单位、院校、报社、医院中担任人民警察职务的人员。 评定授予警衔范围内的人员，由于目前有某种情况，如职务未定、出国留学进修、已连续病休二年以上未恢复工作、工作不称职、因违纪违法正在受审查等，暂缓评定授予警衔。 以工代警的人员，待办理干部录用手续后再评定授予警衔。 (二)不评定授予警衔的人员为：已批准离休、退休或在一九九二年六月三十日前已到离休、退休年龄的人民警察；不服从组织分配、拒不到职的人民警察；已决定调离人民警察工作服岗位的人员；企业单位的人员；事业单位中不担任人民警察职务的人员；群众性学术团体的人员；合同制民警；所有工勤人员；其他不担任人民警察职务的人员。 (三)企事业单位中政企(事)职能不分的公安机构及其人员，待清理整顿、理顺体制后，再确定是否实行警衔制度。各有关主管部门要做好思想政治工作，要求他们坚守岗位、忠于职守，继续做好维护治安秩序工作。 二、首次评定授予警衔的标准 评定授予警衔，应以人民警察的现任职务、德才表现、担任现职时间和工作年限为依据，通盘考虑，全面衡量。 (一)“德才表现”，是指人民警察政治品质、思想觉悟、遵纪守法、联系群众、实事求是、公道正派、谦洁奉公、英勇献身等表现和知识化、专业化程度，实际工作能力，执法水平。 “现任职务”，是指按干部管理权限由主管部门正式任命的职务，不是指临时委派或暂时指定代理的职务。 “担任现职时间”，是指现任职务从某主管部门正式下达任命通知之日起计算的任职的时间。连续担任同一等级职务的时间可以合并计算。 “工作年限”，是指按国家有关规定计算的正式参加工作时间的总和。工作满十二个月为工作年限一年。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、评定授予警衔的范围  
评定授予警衔的人员，必须是属于人民警察建制的在编在职的人民警察。凡不具有人民警察性质的单位和不担任人民警察职务的人员，不实行警衔制度。  
(一)评定授予警衔的人员为：各级公安部门(包括公安部门设在铁道、交通、民航、林业部门的公安机构)、国家安全部门和劳动改造劳动教养管理部门中，从事指挥决策、监督保障和业务工作的人民警察；各级人民法院、人民检察院的司法警察；警察专业技术单位、院校、报社、医院中担任人民警察职务的人员。  
评定授予警衔范围内的人员，由于目前有某种情况，如职务未定、出国留学进修、已连续病休二年以上未恢复工作、工作不称职、因违纪违法正在受审查等，暂缓评定授予警衔。  
以工代警的人员，待办理干部录用手续后再评定授予警衔。  
(二)不评定授予警衔的人员为：已批准离休、退休或在一九九二年六月三十日前已到离休、退休年龄的人民警察；不服从组织分配、拒不到职的人民警察；已决定调离人民警察工作服岗位的人员；企业单位的人员；事业单位中不担任人民警察职务的人员；群众性学术团体的人员；合同制民警；所有工勤人员；其他不担任人民警察职务的人员。  
(三)企事业单位中政企(事)职能不分的公安机构及其人员，待清理整顿、理顺体制后，再确定是否实行警衔制度。各有关主管部门要做好思想政治工作，要求他们坚守岗位、忠于职守，继续做好维护治安秩序工作。

**Article 1** . People's police holding administrative positions shall have their police ranks evaluated and awarded pursuant to the following standards:  
(1) Personnel at the principal ministerial level: may be awarded the rank of General Commissioner.  
(2) Personnel at the vice-ministerial level: may be awarded the rank of Deputy General Commissioner.  
(3) Personnel at the principal departmental level: Those currently serving as Assistant Minister, with good moral character and professional competence, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Supervisor.  
Other personnel at the principal departmental level, with good moral character and professional competence, who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full thirty-five years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Supervisor;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Supervisor.  
(4) Personnel at the vice-departmental level: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full six years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, or who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full thirty-five years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Supervisor;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Supervisor.  
(5) Personnel at the principal divisional level: Those currently serving as directors of public security bureaus of provincial capitals (or autonomous region capitals) and of prefecture-level cities with large populations and heavy public security tasks, with good moral character and professional competence, who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-six years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Supervisor; the remainder may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector.  
Other personnel at the principal divisional level, with good moral character and professional competence, who have served in their current position for a full five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-eight years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Supervisor;  
those who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full eighteen years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector.  
(6) Personnel at the vice-divisional level: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-six years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full sixteen years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Inspector.  
(7) Personnel at the principal section level: Those currently serving as directors and political commissars of county (city) public security bureaus and public security sub-bureaus under prefecture-level cities, with good moral character and professional competence, who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-six years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector; the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector.  
Other personnel at the principal section level, with good moral character and professional competence, who have served in their current position for a full five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-eight years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full twenty years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full twenty-four years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full ten years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full fourteen years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Inspector.  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant.  
(8) Personnel at the vice-section level: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full twenty-six years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full sixteen years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full twelve years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full eight years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full twelve years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant.  
(9) Personnel holding the position of clerk (police chief): Those with good moral character and professional competence who have participated in work for a full thirty years, or who currently serve as section, station, or team chiefs of a county (city) public security bureau and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have participated in work for a full eighteen years, or who currently serve as section, station, or team chiefs of a county (city) public security bureau and have participated in work for a full fourteen years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full eight years, or who currently serve as section, station, or team chiefs of a county (city) public security bureau and do not meet the conditions for award of First-Class Police Sergeant, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Sergeant.  
(10) Personnel holding the position of clerk (police officer): Those with good moral character and professional competence who have participated in work for a full twenty years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full twelve years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full eight years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full four years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Constable;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Constable.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、首次评定授予警衔的标准  
评定授予警衔，应以人民警察的现任职务、德才表现、担任现职时间和工作年限为依据，通盘考虑，全面衡量。  
(一)“德才表现”，是指人民警察政治品质、思想觉悟、遵纪守法、联系群众、实事求是、公道正派、谦洁奉公、英勇献身等表现和知识化、专业化程度，实际工作能力，执法水平。  
“现任职务”，是指按干部管理权限由主管部门正式任命的职务，不是指临时委派或暂时指定代理的职务。  
“担任现职时间”，是指现任职务从某主管部门正式下达任命通知之日起计算的任职的时间。连续担任同一等级职务的时间可以合并计算。  
“工作年限”，是指按国家有关规定计算的正式参加工作时间的总和。工作满十二个月为工作年限一年。  
(二)在警察专业技术单位、院校、报社、医院等事业单位从事专业技术工作的人民警察，已经评聘了专业技术职务的，可以按照专业技术职务等级评定授予专业技术警衔。其中担任行政职务兼任专业技术职务的人民警察，原则上按行政职务评定授予警衔，如低于按其专业技术职务评定授予的警衔，也可按专业技术职务评定授予警衔。  
鉴于目前国家机关未开展专业技术职务聘任工作，公安部门、国家安全部门、劳动改造劳动教养管理部门行政机关中从事专业技术工作的人民警察，不论是否评聘了专业技术职务，均先按照行政职务等级评定授予警衔。关于按照专业技术职务评定授予专业技术警衔的问题，另行研究确定。  
(三)各有关部门在执行评定授予警衔标准中遇有特殊问题，由主管部门提出处理意见，经公安部组织研究、协调处理，或报经国务院同意后办理。  
(四)对个别特殊情况，需要在警察职务等级编制警衔的幅度内高评一级警衔的，应当严格控制，并按批准权限单独报批。

**Article 2** . People's police holding professional and technical positions shall have their police ranks evaluated and awarded pursuant to the following standards:  
(1) Personnel holding senior professional and technical positions such as professor, researcher, etc.: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have made particularly outstanding contributions in professional and technical work and, at the same time, have served in their current position for a full eight years, or have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, or have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full thirty-five years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Supervisor;  
those who have served in their current position for a full eight years, or have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, or have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full thirty-five years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Supervisor;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Supervisor.  
(2) Personnel holding senior professional and technical positions such as associate professor, associate researcher, etc.: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have made outstanding contributions in professional and technical work and, at the same time, have served in their current position for a full five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-two years, or have served in their current position for less than five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-eight years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Supervisor;  
those who have served in their current position for a full five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-two years, or have served in their current position for less than five years and have participated in work for a full thirty-eight years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Supervisor;  
those who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full eighteen years, or have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector.  
(3) Personnel holding intermediate professional and technical positions such as lecturer, assistant researcher, engineer, etc.: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full five years and have participated in work for a full twenty-four years, or who have served in their current position for less than five years and have participated in work for a full thirty years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full eighteen years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full twelve years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full ten years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full six years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full ten years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant.  
(4) Personnel holding junior professional and technical positions such as teaching assistant, research assistant, assistant engineer, etc.: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-two years, or who have served in their current position for less than three years and have participated in work for a full twenty-six years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Inspector;  
those who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full ten years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full sixteen years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full six years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Sergeant.  
(5) Personnel holding junior professional and technical positions such as technician, laboratory assistant, etc.: Those with good moral character and professional competence who have served in their current position for a full two years and have participated in work for a full twelve years, or who have served in their current position for less than two years and have participated in work for a full eighteen years, may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full ten years, may be awarded the rank of Second-Class Police Sergeant;  
those who have participated in work for a full six years, may be awarded the rank of Third-Class Police Sergeant;  
the remainder may be awarded the rank of First-Class Police Constable.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、首次评定授予警衔工作的步骤安排  
为有利于加强领导，保证工作顺利开展，首次评定授予警衔工作分期分批进行。  
第一批：公安部机关(包括铁道、交通、民航、林业公安局机关)，国家安全部机关，司法部劳改局、劳教局机关，各省、自治区公安厅机关、国家安全厅机关和劳改局、劳教局(处)机关，北京、天津、上海市以及省、自治区政府所在市和重庆、深圳、珠海、厦门市上述部门中行政单位的人民警察。  
第二批：地市上述部门的人民警察和各警察专业技术单位、院校、报社、医院中的人民警察。  
第三批：公安部门、国家安全部门和劳动改造劳动教养管理部门中属于授予警衔范围，未安排在第一批和第二批的人民警察。  
司法警察评定授予警衔工作的步骤安排，分别由最高人民法院、最高人民检察院规定。  
首次评定授予警衔工作，要求在一九九三年三月底以前基本完成。

**Article 3** 3. Step-by-step arrangement of the first evaluation and award of police ranks  
In order to facilitate strengthening leadership and ensure the smooth progress of the work, the first evaluation and award of police ranks shall be carried out in batches.  
First batch: People's police officers in administrative units within the following organs: the Ministry of Public Security (including the public security bureaus for railways, transportation, civil aviation, and forestry), the Ministry of State Security, the Prison Administration Bureau and the Re-education through Labor Administration Bureau of the Ministry of Justice, the provincial and autonomous regional public security departments, state security departments, and prison administration and re-education through labor administration bureaus (divisions), and the People's Police in administrative units of the above-mentioned departments in Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, and the capital cities of provinces and autonomous regions, as well as in Chongqing, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, and Xiamen.  
Second batch: People's police officers in the above-mentioned departments at the prefecture and city levels, and People's Police in various police professional and technical units, academies and schools, newspapers, and hospitals.  
Third batch: People's police officers within the public security departments, state security departments, and prison administration and re-education through labor management departments who fall within the scope of police rank award but were not included in the first and second batches.  
The step-by-step arrangement of the evaluation and award of police ranks for judicial police shall be prescribed separately by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate.  
The first evaluation and award of police ranks is required to be substantially completed by the end of March 1993.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、首次评定授予警衔的组织实施  
(一)依照《条例》和国家的有关规定，在认真核定警察建制、编制员额和人民警察职务等级的基础上，明确应授、缓授和不授警衔的具体对象。  
(二)严肃整顿人民警察的纪律作风和警容风纪，坚决清理不合格人员。对工作不称职的人民警察组织集中培训；培训合格的可授予警衔，不合格的调出人民警察队伍。  
(三)搞好思想动员和宣传教育，使人民警察明确实行警衔制度的目的和意义，了解《条例》和有关规定的内容，统一思想认识，正确对待警衔的评定授予。尤其是各级领导干部要以身作则，严以律己，在评定授予警衔中起表率作用。  
(四)坚持实事求是，客观、准确、全面地进行组织鉴定。部级正副职人民警察由干部主客部门鉴定；担任省、地、县三级警察机构正副职的人民警察，按照下管一级的原则，分别由上一级人民警察主管部门鉴定；其余的人民警察，由所在单位政治工作部门鉴定。鉴定的内容包括德、才、绩三个方面，以近几年的表现为主。  
(五)负责组织鉴定的机关逐人填写评定授予警衔审批表，经干部主管部门审查后，按照批准权限逐级上报审批，并下达授予警衔命令。首次授予警衔命令的时间统一填写为一九九二年九月一日。  
凡拟评定授予三级警监以上警衔的人员名单及其审批表，由公安部和有关部门进行汇总研究、统一平衡后，确定拟授三级警监以上警衔的人数和控制指标，提出授予二级警监以上警衔的名单预案，提请国务院总理审批。

**Article 4** 4. Organization and implementation of the first evaluation and award of police ranks  
(1) In accordance with the Regulations and relevant state provisions, and on the basis of conscientiously verifying the police organizational structure, authorized strength, and post levels of people's police officers, the specific targets who shall be awarded, whose award shall be deferred, and who shall not be awarded police ranks shall be identified.  
(2) Strictly rectify the discipline, conduct, and uniform appearance of the People's Police, and resolutely remove unqualified personnel. Organize centralized training for People's Police officers who are incompetent in their work; those who pass the training may be awarded police ranks, and those who fail shall be transferred out of the People's Police force.  
(3) Carry out ideological mobilization and propaganda and education so that the People's Police understand the purpose and significance of implementing the police rank system, are aware of the contents of the Regulations and relevant provisions, unify ideological understanding, and correctly treat the evaluation and award of police ranks. In particular, leading cadres at all levels shall lead by example, strictly discipline themselves, and play an exemplary role in the evaluation and award of police ranks.  
(4) Adhere to seeking truth from facts and conduct organizational appraisals objectively, accurately, and comprehensively. The appraisal of People's Police officers at the principal and vice-ministerial levels shall be conducted by the competent personnel department; the appraisal of People's Police officers holding principal or vice-positions in police organs at the provincial, prefectural, and county levels shall, in accordance with the principle of managing one level below, be conducted by the superior People's Police competent department; the appraisal of other People's Police officers shall be conducted by the political work department of their units. The content of the appraisal shall cover three aspects: political integrity, professional competence, and performance, with emphasis on recent performance.  
(5) The organ responsible for organizational appraisal shall fill in the evaluation and award approval form for each individual, and after examination by the competent personnel department, shall submit it level by level for approval in accordance with the approval authority, and issue the order for awarding the police rank. The date of the first police rank award order shall be uniformly filled in as September 1, 1992.  
For all personnel proposed to be evaluated and awarded the rank of Grade III Commissioner or above, after the Ministry of Public Security and relevant departments compile and study the list and approval forms and conduct overall balancing, the number of persons to be awarded the rank of Grade III Commissioner or above and the control indicators shall be determined, and a draft list of persons to be awarded the rank of Grade II Commissioner or above shall be submitted to the Premier of the State Council for approval.  
(6) The ceremony for awarding the ranks of Commissioner General, Deputy Commissioner General, Grade I Commissioner, and Grade II Commissioner for the first time shall be organized and implemented by the State Council. The ceremony for awarding the ranks of Grade III Commissioner and Supervisor shall be organized and implemented by the Ministry of Public Security and relevant departments respectively. The ceremony for awarding the ranks of Superintendent and Constable shall be organized and implemented by the public security departments (bureaus) of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and relevant departments respectively.
