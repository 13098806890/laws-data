# 公安机关督察条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-08-31  
**生效日期**：2011-10-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了完善公安机关监督机制，保障公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权和遵守纪律，根据《中华人民共和国人民警察法》的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China with a view to perfecting the supervision mechanism of public security organs and ensuring that public security organs and their people's police perform their duties, exercise their powers and abide by discipline according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公安部督察委员会领导全国公安机关的督察工作，负责对公安部所属单位和下级公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权和遵守纪律的情况进行监督，对公安部部长负责。公安部督察机构承担公安部督察委员会办事机构职能。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关督察机构，负责对本级公安机关所属单位和下级公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权和遵守纪律的情况进行监督，对上一级公安机关督察机构和本级公安机关行政首长负责。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关的督察机构为执法勤务机构，由专职人员组成，实行队建制。

**Article 2** The Supervision Committee of the Ministry of Public Security shall lead the supervision work of public security organs nationwide, be responsible for supervising the public security organs directly under the Ministry of Public Security and lower-level public security organs and their people's police in terms of performing their duties, exercising their powers and abiding by discipline according to law, and shall be responsible to the Minister of Public Security. The Supervision Organ of the Ministry of Public Security shall perform the functions of the office of the Supervision Committee of the Ministry of Public Security.  
The supervision organs of local public security organs of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for supervising the public security organs directly under the current-level public security organ and lower-level public security organs and their people's police in terms of performing their duties, exercising their powers and abiding by discipline according to law, and shall be responsible to the supervision organ of the higher-level public security organ and the administrative head of the current-level public security organ.  
The supervision organs of local public security organs of people's governments at or above the county level are law enforcement service organs, composed of full-time personnel, and shall be organized in a detachment structure.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公安部设督察长，由公安部一名副职领导成员担任。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关设督察长，由公安机关行政首长兼任。

**Article 3** The Ministry of Public Security shall establish the post of Chief Supervisor, which shall be held by a deputy leader of the Ministry of Public Security.  
The local public security organs of people's governments at or above the county level shall establish the post of Chief Supervisor, which shall be concurrently held by the administrative head of the public security organ.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　督察机构对公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权和遵守纪律的下列事项，进行现场督察：  
(一)重要的警务部署、措施、活动的组织实施情况；  
(二)重大社会活动的秩序维护和重点地区、场所治安管理的组织实施情况；  
(三)治安突发事件的处置情况；  
(四)刑事案件、治安案件的受理、立案、侦查、调查、处罚和强制措施的实施情况；  
(五)治安、交通、户政、出入境、边防、消防、警卫等公安行政管理法律、法规的执行情况；  
(六)使用武器、警械以及警用车辆、警用标志的情况；  
(七)处置公民报警、请求救助和控告申诉的情况；  
(八)文明执勤、文明执法和遵守警容风纪规定的情况；  
(九)组织管理和警务保障的情况；  
(十)公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权和遵守纪律的其他情况。

**Article 4** The supervision organ shall conduct on-site supervision over the following matters concerning the performance of duties, exercise of powers and observance of discipline by public security organs and their people's police according to law:  
(1) the implementation of important police deployments, measures and activities;  
(2) the maintenance of order in major social activities and the organization and implementation of public security management in key areas and places;  
(3) the handling of public security emergencies;  
(4) the acceptance, registration, investigation, inquiry, punishment and implementation of compulsory measures in criminal cases and public security cases;  
(5) the implementation of laws and regulations on public security administration such as public security, traffic, household registration, exit and entry, border defense, fire protection and guard;  
(6) the use of weapons, police equipment, police vehicles and police insignia;  
(7) the handling of citizens' alarms, requests for assistance and complaints and petitions;  
(8) the observance of civil service conduct, law enforcement and police appearance and discipline;  
(9) organizational management and police support;  
(10) other circumstances concerning the performance of duties, exercise of powers and observance of discipline by public security organs and their people's police according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　督察机构可以向本级公安机关所属单位和下级公安机关派出督察人员进行督察，也可以指令下级公安机关督察机构对专门事项进行督察。

**Article 5** The supervision organ may dispatch supervisors to the units directly under the current-level public security organ or to lower-level public security organs to conduct supervision, and may also instruct the supervision organ of a lower-level public security organ to conduct supervision over a specific matter.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关督察机构查处违法违纪行为，应当向上一级公安机关督察机构报告查处情况；下级公安机关督察机构查处不力的，上级公安机关督察机构可以直接进行督察。

**Article 6** The supervision organ of a local public security organ of a people's government at or above the county level shall report to the supervision organ of the higher-level public security organ on the investigation and handling of illegal or disciplinary violations; if the supervision organ of a lower-level public security organ fails to conduct effective investigation and handling, the supervision organ of the higher-level public security organ may directly conduct supervision.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　督察机构可以派出督察人员参加本级公安机关或者下级公安机关的警务工作会议和重大警务活动的部署。

**Article 7** The supervision organ may dispatch supervisors to attend police work meetings and the deployment of major police activities of the current-level public security organ or lower-level public security organs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　督察机构应当开展警务评议活动，听取国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织和人民群众对公安机关及其人民警察的意见。

**Article 8** The supervision organ shall carry out police service evaluation activities and solicit opinions from state organs, social organizations, enterprises and institutions, and the public on public security organs and their people's police.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　督察机构对群众投诉的正在发生的公安机关及其人民警察违法违纪行为，应当及时出警，按照规定给予现场处置，并将处理结果及时反馈投诉人。  
投诉人的投诉事项已经进入信访、行政复议或者行政诉讼程序的，督察机构应当将投诉材料移交有关部门。

**Article 9** The supervision organ shall promptly dispatch police to handle ongoing illegal or disciplinary acts by public security organs and their people's police as complained by the public, give on-site disposal according to the regulations, and timely feedback the handling results to the complainant.  
If the matters complained of by the complainant have already entered the procedure of petition, administrative reconsideration or administrative action, the supervision organ shall transfer the complaint materials to the relevant department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　督察机构对本级公安机关所属单位和下级公安机关拒不执行法律、法规和上级决定、命令的，可以责令执行；对本级公安机关所属单位或者下级公安机关作出的错误决定、命令，可以决定撤销或者变更，报本级公安机关行政首长批准后执行。

**Article 10** If a unit directly under the current-level public security organ or a lower-level public security organ refuses to implement laws, regulations and decisions or orders of higher authorities, the supervision organ may order it to implement; if a unit directly under the current-level public security organ or a lower-level public security organ has made an erroneous decision or order, the supervision organ may decide to revoke or change it, subject to approval by the administrative head of the current-level public security organ before implementation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　督察人员在现场督察中发现公安机关人民警察违法违纪的，可以采取下列措施，当场处置：  
(一)对违反警容风纪规定的，可以当场予以纠正；  
(二)对违反规定使用武器、警械以及警用车辆、警用标志的，可以扣留其武器、警械、警用车辆、警用标志；  
(三)对违法违纪情节严重、影响恶劣的，以及拒绝、阻碍督察人员执行现场督察工作任务的，必要时，可以带离现场。

**Article 11** When, in the course of on-site supervision, supervisors find that a people's police officer of a public security organ has violated laws or discipline, they may take the following measures for on-the-spot disposition:  
(1) Where the officer violates regulations on police appearance and discipline, they may correct it on the spot;  
(2) Where the officer violates regulations on the use of weapons, police equipment, police vehicles, or police insignia, they may seize the weapons, police equipment, police vehicles, or police insignia;  
(3) Where the violation is serious in nature, has adverse effects, or the officer refuses or obstructs the supervisor from carrying out on-site supervision tasks, they may, when necessary, take the officer away from the scene.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　督察机构认为公安机关人民警察违反纪律需要采取停止执行职务、禁闭措施的，由督察机构作出决定，报本级公安机关督察长批准后执行。  
停止执行职务的期限为10日以上60日以下；禁闭的期限为1日以上7日以下。

**Article 12** Where a supervisory organ considers that a people's police officer of a public security organ has violated discipline and needs to be subjected to measures of suspension from duty or confinement, the supervisory organ shall make a decision and report it to the chief inspector of the same-level public security organ for approval before enforcement.  
The term of suspension from duty shall be not less than 10 days and not more than 60 days; the term of confinement shall be not less than 1 day and not more than 7 days.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　督察机构认为公安机关人民警察需要给予处分或者降低警衔、取消警衔的，督察机构应当提出建议，移送有关部门依法处理。  
督察机构在督察工作中发现公安机关人民警察涉嫌犯罪的，移送司法机关依法处理。

**Article 13** Where a supervisory organ considers that a people's police officer of a public security organ should be given a sanction or have his/her rank reduced or cancelled, the supervisory organ shall make a recommendation and transfer the matter to the relevant department for handling according to law.  
Where, in the course of supervision, the supervisory organ discovers that a people's police officer of a public security organ is suspected of committing a crime, the matter shall be transferred to a judicial organ for handling according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公安机关人民警察对停止执行职务和禁闭决定不服的，可以在被停止执行职务或者被禁闭期间向作出决定的公安机关的上一级公安机关提出申诉。由公安部督察机构作出的停止执行职务、禁闭的决定，受理申诉的机关是公安部督察委员会。  
受理申诉的公安机关对不服停止执行职务的申诉，应当自收到申诉之日起5日内作出是否撤销停止执行职务的决定；对不服禁闭的申诉，应当在收到申诉之时起24小时内作出是否撤销禁闭的决定。  
申诉期间，停止执行职务、禁闭决定不停止执行。  
受理申诉的公安机关认为停止执行职务、禁闭决定确有错误的，应当予以撤销，并在适当范围内为当事人消除影响，恢复名誉。

**Article 14** If a people's police officer of a public security organ is dissatisfied with a decision of suspension from duty or confinement, he/she may, during the period of suspension or confinement, file a petition with the public security organ one level above the organ that made the decision. For a decision of suspension from duty or confinement made by the supervisory organ of the Ministry of Public Security, the organ that accepts the petition shall be the Supervisory Committee of the Ministry of Public Security.  
The public security organ accepting the petition regarding dissatisfaction with suspension from duty shall, within 5 days from the date of receiving the petition, make a decision on whether to revoke the suspension from duty; for a petition regarding dissatisfaction with confinement, it shall, within 24 hours from the time of receiving the petition, make a decision on whether to revoke the confinement.  
During the period of petition, the decision of suspension from duty or confinement shall not be suspended.  
If the public security organ accepting the petition considers that the decision of suspension from duty or confinement is indeed erroneous, it shall revoke the decision and, within an appropriate scope, eliminate the adverse effects for the party and restore his/her reputation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　督察人员在督察工作中，必须实事求是，严格依法办事，接受监督。  
督察机构及其督察人员对于公安机关及其人民警察依法履行职责、行使职权的行为应当予以维护。

**Article 15** In their supervisory work, supervisors must seek truth from facts, strictly handle matters according to law, and accept supervision.  
Supervisory organs and their supervisors shall uphold the acts of public security organs and their people's police officers in lawfully performing their duties and exercising their powers.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　督察人员应当具备下列条件：  
(一)坚持原则，忠于职守，清正廉洁，不徇私情，严守纪律；  
(二)具有大学专科以上学历和法律专业知识、公安业务知识；  
(三)具有3年以上公安工作经历和一定的组织管理能力；  
(四)经过专门培训合格。

**Article 16** Supervisors shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Uphold principles, be devoted to their duties, be honest and incorruptible, refrain from favoritism, and strictly observe discipline;  
(2) Possess at least a college diploma and professional knowledge of law and public security operations;  
(3) Have at least three years of experience in public security work and a certain ability in organization and management;  
(4) Have passed specialized training.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　督察人员执行督察任务，应当佩带督察标志或者出示督察证件。  
督察标志和督察证件的式样由公安部制定。

**Article 17** When carrying out supervision tasks, supervisors shall wear supervision insignia or present supervision credentials.  
The patterns of supervision insignia and supervision credentials shall be formulated by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例自2011年10月1日起施行。

**Article 18** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 2011.
