# 国家赔偿费用管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-17  
**生效日期**：2011-01-17

> (2010年12月29日国务院第138次常务会议通过　2011年1月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第589号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强国家赔偿费用管理，保障公民、法人和其他组织享有依法取得国家赔偿的权利，促进国家机关依法行使职权，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》(以下简称国家赔偿法)，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称国家赔偿费用，是指依照国家赔偿法的规定，应当向赔偿请求人赔偿的费用。 第三条　国家赔偿费用由各级人民政府按照财政管理体制分级负担。 各级人民政府应当根据实际情况，安排一定数额的国家赔偿费用，列入本级年度财政预算。当年需要支付的国家赔偿费用超过本级年度财政预算安排的，应当按照规定及时安排资金。 第四条　国家赔偿费用由各级人民政府财政部门统一管理。 国家赔偿费用的管理应当依法接受监督。 第五条　赔偿请求人申请支付国家赔偿费用的，应当向赔偿义务机关提出书面申请，并提交与申请有关的生效判决书、复议决定书、赔偿决定书或者调解书以及赔偿请求人的身份证明。 赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以委托他人代书；也可以口头申请，由赔偿义务机关如实记录，交赔偿请求人核对或者向赔偿请求人宣读，并由赔偿请求人签字确认。 第六条　申请材料真实、有效、完整的，赔偿义务机关收到申请材料即为受理。赔偿义务机关受理申请的，应当书面通知赔偿请求人。 申请材料不完整的，赔偿义务机关应当当场或者在3个工作日内一次告知赔偿请求人需要补正的全部材料。赔偿请求人按照赔偿义务机关的要求提交补正材料的，赔偿义务机关收到补正材料即为受理。未告知需要补正材料的，赔偿义务机关收到申请材料即为受理。 申请材料虚假、无效，赔偿义务机关决定不予受理的，应当书面通知赔偿请求人并说明理由。 第七条　赔偿请求人对赔偿义务机关不予受理决定有异议的，可以自收到书面通知之日起10日内向赔偿义务机关的上一级机关申请复核。上一级机关应当自收到复核申请之日起5个工作日内依法作出决定。 上一级机关认为不予受理决定错误的，应当自作出复核决定之日起3个工作日内通知赔偿义务机关受理，并告知赔偿请求人。赔偿义务机关应当在收到通知后立即受理。 上一级机关维持不予受理决定的，应当自作出复核决定之日起3个工作日内书面通知赔偿请求人并说明理由。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国家赔偿费用管理，保障公民、法人和其他组织享有依法取得国家赔偿的权利，促进国家机关依法行使职权，根据《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》(以下简称国家赔偿法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the State Compensation Law) for the purposes of strengthening the management of state compensation expenses, ensuring the right of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to obtain state compensation according to law, and promoting state organs to exercise their functions and powers according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称国家赔偿费用，是指依照国家赔偿法的规定，应当向赔偿请求人赔偿的费用。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, state compensation expenses refer to the expenses that shall be compensated to the claimant for compensation in accordance with the provisions of the State Compensation Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家赔偿费用由各级人民政府按照财政管理体制分级负担。  
各级人民政府应当根据实际情况，安排一定数额的国家赔偿费用，列入本级年度财政预算。当年需要支付的国家赔偿费用超过本级年度财政预算安排的，应当按照规定及时安排资金。

**Article 3** State compensation expenses shall be borne by the people's governments at all levels according to the financial management system at different levels.  
The people's governments at all levels shall, based on the actual circumstances, allocate a certain amount of state compensation expenses and include them in the annual fiscal budget of the corresponding level. If the state compensation expenses to be paid in the current year exceed the arrangement of the annual fiscal budget at the corresponding level, the funds shall be arranged in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家赔偿费用由各级人民政府财政部门统一管理。  
国家赔偿费用的管理应当依法接受监督。

**Article 4** State compensation expenses shall be managed uniformly by the finance departments of the people's governments at all levels.  
The management of state compensation expenses shall accept supervision according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　赔偿请求人申请支付国家赔偿费用的，应当向赔偿义务机关提出书面申请，并提交与申请有关的生效判决书、复议决定书、赔偿决定书或者调解书以及赔偿请求人的身份证明。  
赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以委托他人代书；也可以口头申请，由赔偿义务机关如实记录，交赔偿请求人核对或者向赔偿请求人宣读，并由赔偿请求人签字确认。

**Article 5** Where a claimant for compensation applies for payment of state compensation expenses, it shall submit a written application to the organ liable for compensation and provide the effective judgment, reconsideration decision, compensation decision or mediation agreement related to the application, as well as the identity certificate of the claimant.  
Where the claimant for compensation has genuine difficulty in writing the application, it may entrust another person to write it on its behalf; or it may make an oral application, which shall be truthfully recorded by the organ liable for compensation, presented to the claimant for verification or read out to the claimant, and confirmed by the signature of the claimant.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请材料真实、有效、完整的，赔偿义务机关收到申请材料即为受理。赔偿义务机关受理申请的，应当书面通知赔偿请求人。  
申请材料不完整的，赔偿义务机关应当当场或者在3个工作日内一次告知赔偿请求人需要补正的全部材料。赔偿请求人按照赔偿义务机关的要求提交补正材料的，赔偿义务机关收到补正材料即为受理。未告知需要补正材料的，赔偿义务机关收到申请材料即为受理。  
申请材料虚假、无效，赔偿义务机关决定不予受理的，应当书面通知赔偿请求人并说明理由。

**Article 6** Where the application materials are authentic, valid, and complete, the application is deemed to be accepted upon the receipt of the materials by the organ liable for compensation. Where the organ liable for compensation accepts the application, it shall notify the claimant for compensation in writing.  
Where the application materials are incomplete, the organ liable for compensation shall, on the spot or within three working days, inform the claimant for compensation of all the materials that need to be supplemented or corrected at one time. Where the claimant for compensation submits the supplemented or corrected materials as required by the organ liable for compensation, the application is deemed to be accepted upon the receipt of the supplemented materials by the organ liable for compensation. If the organ fails to inform the claimant of the materials that need to be supplemented, the application is deemed to be accepted upon the receipt of the original application materials.  
Where the application materials are false or invalid, and the organ liable for compensation decides not to accept the application, it shall notify the claimant for compensation in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　赔偿请求人对赔偿义务机关不予受理决定有异议的，可以自收到书面通知之日起10日内向赔偿义务机关的上一级机关申请复核。上一级机关应当自收到复核申请之日起5个工作日内依法作出决定。  
上一级机关认为不予受理决定错误的，应当自作出复核决定之日起3个工作日内通知赔偿义务机关受理，并告知赔偿请求人。赔偿义务机关应当在收到通知后立即受理。  
上一级机关维持不予受理决定的，应当自作出复核决定之日起3个工作日内书面通知赔偿请求人并说明理由。

**Article 7** Where a claimant for compensation disagrees with the decision of the organ liable for compensation not to accept the application, it may apply for review to the organ at the next higher level within 10 days from the date of receipt of the written notice. The organ at the next higher level shall make a decision according to law within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the review application.  
Where the organ at the next higher level considers that the decision not to accept is erroneous, it shall, within 3 working days from the date of making the review decision, notify the organ liable for compensation to accept the application and inform the claimant for compensation. The organ liable for compensation shall accept the application immediately upon receipt of the notice.  
Where the organ at the next higher level upholds the decision not to accept, it shall, within 3 working days from the date of making the review decision, notify the claimant for compensation in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　赔偿义务机关应当自受理赔偿请求人支付申请之日起7日内，依照预算管理权限向有关财政部门提出书面支付申请，并提交下列材料：  
(一)赔偿请求人请求支付国家赔偿费用的申请；  
(二)生效的判决书、复议决定书、赔偿决定书或者调解书；  
(三)赔偿请求人的身份证明。

**Article 8** The organ liable for compensation shall, within 7 days from the date of accepting the application for payment by the claimant for compensation, submit a written application for payment to the relevant finance department in accordance with the budget management authority, and provide the following materials:  
(1) The application of the claimant for compensation requesting payment of state compensation expenses;  
(2) The effective judgment, reconsideration decision, compensation decision or mediation agreement;  
(3) The identity certificate of the claimant for compensation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　财政部门收到赔偿义务机关申请材料后，应当根据下列情况分别作出处理：  
(一)申请的国家赔偿费用依照预算管理权限不属于本财政部门支付的，应当在3个工作日内退回申请材料并书面通知赔偿义务机关向有管理权限的财政部门申请；  
(二)申请材料符合要求的，收到申请即为受理，并书面通知赔偿义务机关；  
(三)申请材料不符合要求的，应当在3个工作日内一次告知赔偿义务机关需要补正的全部材料。赔偿义务机关应当在5个工作日内按照要求提交全部补正材料，财政部门收到补正材料即为受理。

**Article 9** After receiving the application materials from the organ liable for compensation, the finance department shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) Where the applied state compensation expenses are not to be paid by this finance department in accordance with the budget management authority, it shall, within 3 working days, return the application materials and notify the organ liable for compensation in writing to apply to the finance department with competent authority;  
(2) Where the application materials meet the requirements, the application is deemed to be accepted upon receipt, and the organ liable for compensation shall be notified in writing;  
(3) Where the application materials do not meet the requirements, the finance department shall, within 3 working days, inform the organ liable for compensation of all the materials that need to be supplemented or corrected at one time. The organ liable for compensation shall submit all the supplemented or corrected materials as required within 5 working days, and the application is deemed to be accepted upon the receipt of the supplemented materials by the finance department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　财政部门应当自受理申请之日起15日内，按照预算和财政国库管理的有关规定支付国家赔偿费用。  
财政部门发现赔偿项目、计算标准违反国家赔偿法规定的，应当提交作出赔偿决定的机关或者其上级机关依法处理、追究有关人员的责任。

**Article 10** The finance department shall pay the state compensation expenses within 15 days from the date of accepting the application, in accordance with the relevant provisions on budget and treasury management.  
Where the finance department discovers that the compensation items or calculation standards violate the provisions of the State Compensation Law, it shall submit the matter to the organ that made the compensation decision or its superior organ for handling according to law and investigating the liability of the relevant personnel.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　财政部门自支付国家赔偿费用之日起3个工作日内告知赔偿义务机关、赔偿请求人。

**Article 11** The finance department shall, within 3 working days from the date of payment of state compensation expenses, notify the compensatory obligor and the claimant.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　赔偿义务机关应当依照[国家赔偿法第十六条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-16)、第三十一条的规定，责令有关工作人员、受委托的组织或者个人承担或者向有关工作人员追偿部分或者全部国家赔偿费用。  
赔偿义务机关依照前款规定作出决定后，应当书面通知有关财政部门。  
有关工作人员、受委托的组织或者个人应当依照财政收入收缴的规定上缴应当承担或者被追偿的国家赔偿费用。

**Article 12** The compensatory obligor shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 16 and 31 of the State Compensation Law, order the relevant staff members, entrusted organizations or individuals to bear, or recover from the relevant staff members, part or all of the state compensation expenses.  
After making a decision in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the compensatory obligor shall notify the relevant finance department in writing.  
The relevant staff members, entrusted organizations or individuals shall, in accordance with the provisions on revenue collection, turn over the state compensation expenses that they shall bear or are recovered.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　赔偿义务机关、财政部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一，根据《财政违法行为处罚处分条例》的规定处理、处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)以虚报、冒领等手段骗取国家赔偿费用的；  
(二)违反国家赔偿法规定的范围和计算标准实施国家赔偿造成财政资金损失的；  
(三)不依法支付国家赔偿费用的；  
(四)截留、滞留、挪用、侵占国家赔偿费用的；  
(五)未依照规定责令有关工作人员、受委托的组织或者个人承担国家赔偿费用或者向有关工作人员追偿国家赔偿费用的；  
(六)未依照规定将应当承担或者被追偿的国家赔偿费用及时上缴财政的。

**Article 13** Where the compensatory obligor, the finance department or their staff members commit any of the following acts, they shall be dealt with and sanctioned according to the provisions of the Regulations on Punishment and Disciplinary Sanctions for Fiscal Illegal Acts; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) defrauding state compensation expenses by means of false reports, false claims, etc.;  
(2) implementing state compensation in violation of the scope and calculation standards prescribed by the State Compensation Law, causing losses to fiscal funds;  
(3) failing to pay state compensation expenses according to law;  
(4) intercepting, retaining, misappropriating or encroaching upon state compensation expenses;  
(5) failing to order the relevant staff members, entrusted organizations or individuals to bear state compensation expenses or to recover state compensation expenses from the relevant staff members in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) failing to timely turn over to the finance the state compensation expenses that shall be borne or are recovered in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。1995年1月25日国务院发布的《国家赔偿费用管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 14** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Measures for Administration of State Compensation Expenses promulgated by the State Council on January 25, 1995 shall be abolished simultaneously.
