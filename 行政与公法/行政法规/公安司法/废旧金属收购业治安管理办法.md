# 废旧金属收购业治安管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

> （1994年1月5日国务院批准　1994年1月25日公安部令第16号发布　根据2023年7月20日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一条　为了加强对废旧金属收购业的治安管理，保护合法经营，预防和打击违法犯罪活动，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法所称废旧金属，是指生产性废旧金属和非生产性废旧金属。生产性废旧金属和非生产性废旧金属的具体分类由公安部会同有关部门规定。 第三条　生产性废旧金属，按照国务院有关规定由有权经营生产性废旧金属收购业的企业收购。收购废旧金属的其他企业和个体工商户只能收购非生产性废旧金属，不得收购生产性废旧金属。 第四条　收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户，应当在取得营业执照后15日内向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。 备案事项发生变更的，收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户应当自变更之日起15日内（属于工商登记事项的自工商登记变更之日起15日内）向县级人民政府公安机关办理变更手续。 公安机关可以通过网络等方式，便利企业和个体工商户备案。 第五条　收购废旧金属的企业应当有固定的经营场所。收购废旧金属的个体工商户应当有所在地常住户口或者暂住户口。 第六条　在铁路、矿区、油田、港口、机场、施工工地、军事禁区和金属冶炼加工企业附近，不得设点收购废旧金属。 第七条　收购废旧金属的企业在收购生产性废旧金属时，应当查验出售单位开具的证明，对出售单位的名称和经办人的姓名、住址、身份证号码以及物品的名称、数量、规格、新旧程度等如实进行登记。 第八条　收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户不得收购下列金属物品： （一）枪支、弹药和爆炸物品； （二）剧毒、放射性物品及其容器； （三）铁路、油田、供电、电信通讯、矿山、水利、测量和城市公用设施等专用器材； （四）公安机关通报寻查的赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对废旧金属收购业的治安管理，保护合法经营，预防和打击违法犯罪活动，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the public security administration of the scrap metal purchase industry, protecting lawful operations, and preventing and combating illegal and criminal activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称废旧金属，是指生产性废旧金属和非生产性废旧金属。生产性废旧金属和非生产性废旧金属的具体分类由公安部会同有关部门规定。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Measures, "scrap metal" means productive scrap metal and non-productive scrap metal. The specific classification of productive scrap metal and non-productive scrap metal shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　生产性废旧金属，按照国务院有关规定由有权经营生产性废旧金属收购业的企业收购。收购废旧金属的其他企业和个体工商户只能收购非生产性废旧金属，不得收购生产性废旧金属。

**Article 3** Productive scrap metal shall be purchased by enterprises that are authorized to engage in the productive scrap metal purchase industry in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council. Other enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal may only purchase non-productive scrap metal and shall not purchase productive scrap metal.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户，应当在取得营业执照后15日内向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。  
备案事项发生变更的，收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户应当自变更之日起15日内（属于工商登记事项的自工商登记变更之日起15日内）向县级人民政府公安机关办理变更手续。  
公安机关可以通过网络等方式，便利企业和个体工商户备案。

**Article 4** Enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal shall, within 15 days after obtaining a business license, file for record with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where they are located.  
Where the recorded items change, the enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal shall undergo the change formalities with the public security organ of the people's government at the county level within 15 days from the date of change (or, for items subject to industrial and commercial registration, within 15 days from the date of change of the industrial and commercial registration).  
The public security organ may facilitate the record-filing by enterprises and individually-owned businesses through the Internet or other means.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　收购废旧金属的企业应当有固定的经营场所。收购废旧金属的个体工商户应当有所在地常住户口或者暂住户口。

**Article 5** Enterprises that purchase scrap metal shall have fixed business premises. Individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal shall have a permanent residence registration or a temporary residence registration at the place where they are located.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在铁路、矿区、油田、港口、机场、施工工地、军事禁区和金属冶炼加工企业附近，不得设点收购废旧金属。

**Article 6** No purchase points for scrap metal shall be established near railways, mining areas, oil fields, ports, airports, construction sites, military restricted zones, or metal smelting and processing enterprises.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　收购废旧金属的企业在收购生产性废旧金属时，应当查验出售单位开具的证明，对出售单位的名称和经办人的姓名、住址、身份证号码以及物品的名称、数量、规格、新旧程度等如实进行登记。

**Article 7** When purchasing productive scrap metal, enterprises that purchase scrap metal shall examine the certificates issued by the selling entity, and truthfully register the name of the selling entity, the name, address, and identity card number of the person handling the transaction, as well as the name, quantity, specification, degree of wear and tear, etc., of the items.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户不得收购下列金属物品：  
（一）枪支、弹药和爆炸物品；  
（二）剧毒、放射性物品及其容器；  
（三）铁路、油田、供电、电信通讯、矿山、水利、测量和城市公用设施等专用器材；  
（四）公安机关通报寻查的赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品。

**Article 8** Enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal shall not purchase any of the following metal items:  
(1) firearms, ammunition, and explosive substances;  
(2) highly toxic and radioactive substances and their containers;  
(3) special equipment used for railways, oil fields, power supply, telecommunications, mines, water conservancy, surveying, and urban public utilities;  
(4) stolen goods that the public security organ has circulated a notice for search, or items suspected of being stolen goods.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户发现有出售公安机关通报寻查的赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品的，应当立即报告公安机关。  
公安机关对赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品应当予以扣留，并开付收据。有赃物嫌疑的物品经查明不是赃物的，应当及时退还；赃物或者有赃物嫌疑的物品经查明确属赃物的，依照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 9** Where enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal discover that stolen goods that the public security organ has circulated a notice for search, or items suspected of being stolen goods, are being sold, they shall immediately report to the public security organ.  
The public security organ shall detain the stolen goods or items suspected of being stolen goods and issue a receipt. If items suspected of being stolen goods are verified not to be stolen goods, they shall be returned promptly; if the stolen goods or items suspected of being stolen goods are verified to be stolen goods, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公安机关应当对收购废旧金属的企业和个体工商户进行治安业务指导和检查。收购企业和个体工商户应当协助公安人员查处违法犯罪分子，据实反映情况，不得知情不报或者隐瞒包庇。

**Article 10** The public security organ shall provide guidance and inspection on public security operations to enterprises and individually-owned businesses that purchase scrap metal. The purchasing enterprises and individually-owned businesses shall assist public security personnel in investigating and dealing with illegal and criminal offenders, truthfully report the situation, and shall not withhold information or conceal and shield them.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有下列情形之一的，由公安机关给予相应处罚：  
（一）违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)第一款规定，未履行备案手续收购生产性废旧金属的，予以警告，责令限期改正，逾期拒不改正的，视情节轻重，处以500元以上2000元以下的罚款；未履行备案手续收购非生产性废旧金属的，予以警告或者处以500元以下的罚款；  
（二）违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)第二款规定，未向公安机关办理变更手续的，予以警告或者处以200元以下的罚款；  
（三）违反[本办法第六条](#art-6)规定，非法设点收购废旧金属的，予以取缔，没收非法收购的物品及非法所得，可以并处5000元以上10000元以下的罚款；  
（四）违反[本办法第七条](#art-7)规定，收购生产性废旧金属时未如实登记的，视情节轻重，处以2000元以上5000元以下的罚款或者责令停业整顿；  
（五）违反[本办法第八条](#art-8)规定，收购禁止收购的金属物品的，视情节轻重，处以2000元以上10000元以下的罚款或者责令停业整顿。  
有前款所列第（一）、（三）、（四）、（五）项情形之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** Under any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall impose corresponding penalties:  
(1) Where a party violates the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 4 of these Measures by purchasing productive scrap metal without going through the record-filing formalities, a warning shall be given, and the party shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if the party refuses to make corrections upon expiration of the time limit, a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed depending on the seriousness of the circumstances; where a party purchases non-productive scrap metal without going through the record-filing formalities, a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) Where a party violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 4 of these Measures by failing to undergo the change formalities with the public security organ, a warning or a fine of not more than 200 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) Where a party violates the provisions of Article 6 of these Measures by illegally establishing a purchase point for scrap metal, the purchase point shall be banned, the illegally purchased items and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan may be imposed concurrently;  
(4) Where a party violates the provisions of Article 7 of these Measures by failing to truthfully register when purchasing productive scrap metal, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed, or the party shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances;  
(5) Where a party violates the provisions of Article 8 of these Measures by purchasing metal items that are prohibited from being purchased, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed, or the party shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances.  
If any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1), (3), (4), and (5) of the preceding paragraph constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人对公安机关作出的具体行政行为不服的，可以自得知该具体行政行为之日起15日内向上一级公安机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定通知之日起15日内向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 12** Where a party is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act taken by the public security organ, the party may, within 15 days from the date of learning of the specific administrative act, apply for reconsideration with the public security organ at the next higher level; if the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receiving the notice of the reconsideration decision, initiate an action in a People's Court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对严格执行本办法，协助公安机关查获违法犯罪分子，作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由公安机关给予表彰或者奖励。

**Article 13** Units and individuals that strictly implement these Measures and assist the public security organ in apprehending illegal and criminal offenders, achieving remarkable results, shall be given commendation or rewards by the public security organ.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 14** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
