# 中华人民共和国反间谍法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-11-22  
**生效日期**：2017-11-22

> (2017年11月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第692号　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国反间谍法》(以下简称《反间谍法》)，制定本实施细则。 第二条　国家安全机关负责本细则的实施。 公安、保密行政管理等其他有关部门和军队有关部门按照职责分工，密切配合，加强协调，依法做好有关工作。 第三条　《反间谍法》所称“境外机构、组织”包括境外机构、组织在中华人民共和国境内设立的分支(代表)机构和分支组织；所称“境外个人”包括居住在中华人民共和国境内不具有中华人民共和国国籍的人。 第四条　《反间谍法》所称“间谍组织代理人”，是指受间谍组织或者其成员的指使、委托、资助，进行或者授意、指使他人进行危害中华人民共和国国家安全活动的人。 间谍组织和间谍组织代理人由国务院国家安全主管部门确认。 第五条　《反间谍法》所称“敌对组织”，是指敌视中华人民共和国人民民主专政的政权和社会主义制度，危害国家安全的组织。 敌对组织由国务院国家安全主管部门或者国务院公安部门确认。 第六条　《反间谍法》所称“资助”实施危害中华人民共和国国家安全的间谍行为，是指境内外机构、组织、个人的下列行为： (一)向实施间谍行为的组织、个人提供经费、场所和物资的； (二)向组织、个人提供用于实施间谍行为的经费、场所和物资的。 第七条　《反间谍法》所称“勾结”实施危害中华人民共和国国家安全的间谍行为，是指境内外组织、个人的下列行为： (一)与境外机构、组织、个人共同策划或者进行危害国家安全的间谍活动的；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国反间谍法》(以下简称《反间谍法》)，制定本实施细则。

**Article 1** This Implementing Regulation is formulated in accordance with the Counter-Espionage Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Counter-Espionage Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家安全机关负责本细则的实施。  
公安、保密行政管理等其他有关部门和军队有关部门按照职责分工，密切配合，加强协调，依法做好有关工作。

**Article 2** The national security authorities shall be responsible for the implementation of this Regulation.  
The public security, secret administration and other relevant departments, as well as relevant departments of the military, shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, closely cooperate, strengthen coordination, and properly carry out relevant work according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　《反间谍法》所称“境外机构、组织”包括境外机构、组织在中华人民共和国境内设立的分支(代表)机构和分支组织；所称“境外个人”包括居住在中华人民共和国境内不具有中华人民共和国国籍的人。

**Article 3** The term "overseas institutions and organizations" as used in the Counter-Espionage Law shall include branch (representative) offices and branch organizations established by overseas institutions and organizations within the territory of the People's Republic of China; the term "overseas individual" shall include persons who reside within the territory of the People's Republic of China but do not hold the nationality of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　《反间谍法》所称“间谍组织代理人”，是指受间谍组织或者其成员的指使、委托、资助，进行或者授意、指使他人进行危害中华人民共和国国家安全活动的人。  
间谍组织和间谍组织代理人由国务院国家安全主管部门确认。

**Article 4** The term "agent of a spy organization" as used in the Counter-Espionage Law shall mean a person who, under the direction, commission, or funding of a spy organization or its members, carries out or instructs or directs others to carry out activities that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China.  
Spy organizations and agents of spy organizations shall be determined by the national security authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　《反间谍法》所称“敌对组织”，是指敌视中华人民共和国人民民主专政的政权和社会主义制度，危害国家安全的组织。  
敌对组织由国务院国家安全主管部门或者国务院公安部门确认。

**Article 5** The term "hostile organization" as used in the Counter-Espionage Law shall mean an organization that is hostile to the regime of the people's democratic dictatorship and the socialist system of the People's Republic of China, and endangers national security.  
Hostile organizations shall be determined by the national security authority of the State Council or the public security department of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　《反间谍法》所称“资助”实施危害中华人民共和国国家安全的间谍行为，是指境内外机构、组织、个人的下列行为：  
(一)向实施间谍行为的组织、个人提供经费、场所和物资的；  
(二)向组织、个人提供用于实施间谍行为的经费、场所和物资的。

**Article 6** The term "funding" of espionage activities that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China as used in the Counter-Espionage Law shall mean the following acts by domestic and overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals:  
(1) providing funds, premises, and materials to organizations or individuals that carry out espionage activities;  
(2) providing funds, premises, and materials to organizations or individuals for use in carrying out espionage activities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　《反间谍法》所称“勾结”实施危害中华人民共和国国家安全的间谍行为，是指境内外组织、个人的下列行为：  
(一)与境外机构、组织、个人共同策划或者进行危害国家安全的间谍活动的；  
(二)接受境外机构、组织、个人的资助或者指使，进行危害国家安全的间谍活动的；  
(三)与境外机构、组织、个人建立联系，取得支持、帮助，进行危害国家安全的间谍活动的。

**Article 7** The term "colluding" to carry out espionage activities that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China as used in the Counter-Espionage Law shall mean the following acts by domestic and overseas organizations or individuals:  
(1) jointly planning or conducting espionage activities that endanger national security with overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals;  
(2) accepting funding or direction from overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals to conduct espionage activities that endanger national security;  
(3) establishing contacts with overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals to obtain support or assistance, and conducting espionage activities that endanger national security.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下列行为属于[《反间谍法》第三十九条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-39)所称“间谍行为以外的其他危害国家安全行为”：  
(一)组织、策划、实施分裂国家、破坏国家统一，颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的；  
(二)组织、策划、实施危害国家安全的恐怖活动的；  
(三)捏造、歪曲事实，发表、散布危害国家安全的文字或者信息，或者制作、传播、出版危害国家安全的音像制品或者其他出版物的；  
(四)利用设立社会团体或者企业事业组织，进行危害国家安全活动的；  
(五)利用宗教进行危害国家安全活动的；  
(六)组织、利用邪教进行危害国家安全活动的；  
(七)制造民族纠纷，煽动民族分裂，危害国家安全的；  
(八)境外个人违反有关规定，不听劝阻，擅自会见境内有危害国家安全行为或者有危害国家安全行为重大嫌疑的人员的。

**Article 8** The following acts shall fall under the term "other acts endangering national security other than espionage" as used in Article 39 of the Counter-Espionage Law:  
(1) organizing, plotting, or carrying out acts to split the country, undermine national unity, subvert state power, or overthrow the socialist system;  
(2) organizing, plotting, or carrying out terrorist activities that endanger national security;  
(3) fabricating or distorting facts, publishing or disseminating text or information that endangers national security, or producing, disseminating, or publishing audio-visual products or other publications that endanger national security;  
(4) using the establishment of social organizations or enterprises and institutions to conduct activities that endanger national security;  
(5) using religion to conduct activities that endanger national security;  
(6) organizing or using cults to conduct activities that endanger national security;  
(7) creating ethnic disputes, inciting ethnic division, and endangering national security;  
(8) overseas individuals, in violation of relevant provisions, ignoring dissuasion, and meeting without authorization with persons within the territory who have engaged in acts endangering national security or who are under strong suspicion of such acts.

### 第二章　国家安全机关在反间谍工作中的职权

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　境外个人被认为入境后可能进行危害中华人民共和国国家安全活动的，国务院国家安全主管部门可以决定其在一定时期内不得入境。

**Article 9** Where an overseas individual is deemed likely to engage in activities that endanger the national security of the People's Republic of China after entering the country, the national security authority of the State Council may decide that the individual shall not be allowed to enter the country for a certain period.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对背叛祖国、危害国家安全的犯罪嫌疑人，依据[《反间谍法》第八条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-8)的规定，国家安全机关可以通缉、追捕。

**Article 10** For criminal suspects who betray the motherland and endanger national security, the national security authorities may, pursuant to Article 8 of the Counter-Espionage Law, issue wanted notices and pursue and arrest them.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家安全机关依法执行反间谍工作任务时，有权向有关组织和人员调查询问有关情况。

**Article 11** When performing tasks of counter-espionage according to law, the state security organ may investigate and inquire about relevant information from relevant organizations and personnel.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务时，对发现身份不明、有危害国家安全行为的嫌疑人员，可以检查其随带物品。

**Article 12** When staff members of a state security organ perform the task of counter-espionage according to law, they may inspect the belongings of suspects who are found to have unclear identities and have engaged in acts endangering national security.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家安全机关执行反间谍工作紧急任务的车辆，可以配置特别通行标志和警灯、警报器。

**Article 13** Vehicles of the state security organ performing urgent tasks of counter-espionage may be equipped with special passage signs and police lights and sirens.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务的行为，不受其他组织和个人的非法干涉。  
国家安全机关工作人员依法执行反间谍工作任务时，应当出示国家安全部侦察证或者其他相应证件。  
国家安全机关及其工作人员在工作中，应当严格依法办事，不得超越职权、滥用职权，不得侵犯组织和个人的合法权益。

**Article 14** The acts of staff members of a state security organ performing tasks of counter-espionage according to law shall not be subject to illegal interference by other organizations or individuals.  
When performing tasks of counter-espionage according to law, staff members of a state security organ shall present the reconnaissance certificate of the Ministry of State Security or other relevant credentials.  
In their work, the state security organ and its staff members shall strictly handle affairs according to law, shall not exceed their authority or abuse their power, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of organizations or individuals.

### 第三章　公民和组织维护国家安全的义务和权利

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　机关、团体和其他组织对本单位的人员进行维护国家安全的教育，动员、组织本单位的人员防范、制止间谍行为的工作，应当接受国家安全机关的协调和指导。  
机关、团体和其他组织不履行《反间谍法》和本细则规定的安全防范义务，未按照要求整改或者未达到整改要求的，国家安全机关可以约谈相关负责人，将约谈情况通报该单位上级主管部门，推动落实防范间谍行为和其他危害国家安全行为的责任。

**Article 15** Organs, organizations, and other entities that conduct education on safeguarding national security among their personnel, and mobilize and organize their personnel to prevent and stop espionage, shall accept the coordination and guidance of the state security organ.  
Where an organ, organization, or other entity fails to perform the security prevention obligations prescribed in the Counter-Espionage Law and these Rules, and fails to rectify as required or fails to meet the rectification requirements, the state security organ may interview the relevant responsible person, notify the superior competent department of the entity of the interview, and promote the implementation of the responsibility to prevent espionage and other acts endangering national security.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下列情形属于[《反间谍法》第七条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-7)所称“重大贡献”：  
(一)为国家安全机关提供重要线索，发现、破获严重危害国家安全的犯罪案件的；  
(二)为国家安全机关提供重要情况，防范、制止严重危害国家安全的行为发生的；  
(三)密切配合国家安全机关执行国家安全工作任务，表现突出的；  
(四)为维护国家安全，与危害国家安全的犯罪分子进行斗争，表现突出的；  
(五)在教育、动员、组织本单位的人员防范、制止危害国家安全行为的工作中，成绩显著的。

**Article 16** The following circumstances fall under the term “major contributions” as mentioned in Article 7 of the Counter-Espionage Law:  
(1) providing important clues to the state security organ, leading to the discovery and cracking of criminal cases that seriously endanger national security;  
(2) providing important information to the state security organ, preventing and stopping acts that seriously endanger national security;  
(3) closely cooperating with the state security organ in performing national security tasks, with outstanding performance;  
(4) struggling against criminals who endanger national security to safeguard national security, with outstanding performance;  
(5) achieving remarkable results in the work of educating, mobilizing, and organizing personnel of one's own unit to prevent and stop acts endangering national security.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　[《反间谍法》第二十四条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-24)所称“非法持有属于国家秘密的文件、资料和其他物品”是指：  
(一)不应知悉某项国家秘密的人员携带、存放属于该项国家秘密的文件、资料和其他物品的；  
(二)可以知悉某项国家秘密的人员，未经办理手续，私自携带、留存属于该项国家秘密的文件、资料和其他物品的。

**Article 17** The term “illegal possession of documents, data, and other articles belonging to state secrets” as mentioned in Article 24 of the Counter-Espionage Law means:  
(1) the carrying or storage of documents, data, and other articles belonging to a certain state secret by a person who should not have knowledge of that state secret;  
(2) the unauthorized carrying or retention of documents, data, and other articles belonging to a certain state secret by a person who may have knowledge of that state secret, without going through the formalities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[《反间谍法》第二十五条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-25)所称“专用间谍器材”，是指进行间谍活动特殊需要的下列器材：  
(一)暗藏式窃听、窃照器材；  
(二)突发式收发报机、一次性密码本、密写工具；  
(三)用于获取情报的电子监听、截收器材；  
(四)其他专用间谍器材。  
专用间谍器材的确认，由国务院国家安全主管部门负责。

**Article 18** The term “special espionage equipment” as mentioned in Article 25 of the Counter-Espionage Law means the following equipment specially needed for espionage activities:  
(1) concealed bugging and surveillance equipment;  
(2) burst-mode transceivers, one-time pads, and secret writing tools;  
(3) electronic monitoring and interception equipment used for obtaining intelligence;  
(4) other special espionage equipment.  
The confirmation of special espionage equipment shall be the responsibility of the national security competent department of the State Council.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　实施危害国家安全的行为，由有关部门依法予以处分，国家安全机关也可以予以警告；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** Anyone who commits an act endangering national security shall be subject to disciplinary action by the relevant department according to law, and the state security organ may also issue a warning; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　下列情形属于[《反间谍法》第二十七条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-27)所称“立功表现”：  
(一)揭发、检举危害国家安全的其他犯罪分子，情况属实的；  
(二)提供重要线索、证据，使危害国家安全的行为得以发现和制止的；  
(三)协助国家安全机关、司法机关捕获其他危害国家安全的犯罪分子的；  
(四)对协助国家安全机关维护国家安全有重要作用的其他行为。  
“重大立功表现”，是指在前款所列立功表现的范围内对国家安全工作有特别重要作用的。

**Article 20** The following circumstances fall under the term “meritorious service” as mentioned in Article 27 of the Counter-Espionage Law:  
(1) exposing or reporting other criminals who endanger national security, with the information confirmed true;  
(2) providing important clues or evidence, thereby enabling the discovery and prevention of acts endangering national security;  
(3) assisting the state security organ or judicial organ in capturing other criminals who endanger national security;  
(4) other acts that play an important role in assisting the state security organ to safeguard national security.  
The term “major meritorious service” refers to acts that have a particularly important effect on national security work within the scope of the meritorious service listed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有证据证明知道他人有间谍行为，或者经国家安全机关明确告知他人有危害国家安全的犯罪行为，在国家安全机关向其调查有关情况、收集有关证据时，拒绝提供的，依照[《反间谍法》第二十九条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-29)的规定处理。

**Article 21** Where there is evidence proving that a person knows of another person's espionage activities, or has been explicitly informed by a national security organ that another person has committed a criminal act endangering national security, but refuses to provide information when the national security organ investigates the relevant circumstances and collects relevant evidence from him, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 29 of the Counter-Espionage Law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家安全机关依法执行反间谍工作任务时，公民和组织依法有义务提供便利条件或者其他协助，拒不提供或者拒不协助，构成故意阻碍国家安全机关依法执行反间谍工作任务的，依照[《反间谍法》第三十条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-30)的规定处罚。

**Article 22** When a national security organ lawfully performs counter-espionage work tasks, citizens and organizations have the obligation to provide convenient conditions or other assistance according to law. If they refuse to provide or refuse to assist, constituting intentional obstruction of the national security organ's lawful performance of counter-espionage work tasks, they shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the Counter-Espionage Law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　故意阻碍国家安全机关依法执行反间谍工作任务，造成国家安全机关工作人员人身伤害或者财物损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任，并由司法机关或者国家安全机关依照[《反间谍法》第三十条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国反间谍法.md#art-30)的规定予以处罚。

**Article 23** Whoever intentionally obstructs a national security organ from lawfully performing counter-espionage work tasks, causing personal injury or property damage to a staff member of the national security organ, shall bear compensation liability according to law, and shall be punished by a judicial organ or the national security organ in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the Counter-Espionage Law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　对涉嫌间谍行为的人员，国家安全机关可以决定其在一定期限内不得出境。对违反《反间谍法》的境外个人，国务院国家安全主管部门可以决定限期离境或者驱逐出境，并决定其不得入境的期限。被驱逐出境的境外个人，自被驱逐出境之日起10年内不得入境。

**Article 24** For a person suspected of espionage activities, the national security organ may decide that he shall not leave the country within a certain period. For an overseas individual who violates the Counter-Espionage Law, the competent national security department of the State Council may decide to order him to leave the country within a specified time limit or to deport him, and decide on the period during which he shall not be allowed to enter the country. An overseas individual who is deported shall not be allowed to enter the country within ten years from the date of deportation.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家安全机关、公安机关依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，履行防范、制止和惩治间谍行为以外的其他危害国家安全行为的职责，适用本细则的有关规定。

**Article 25** Where national security organs and public security organs, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, perform the duty of preventing, stopping, and punishing acts endangering national security other than espionage activities, the relevant provisions of these Rules shall apply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本细则自公布之日起施行。1994年6月4日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国国家安全法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 26** These Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Implementing Rules of the Law of the People's Republic of China on National Security promulgated by the State Council on June 4, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
