# 中华人民共和国人民警察使用警械和武器条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1996-01-16  
**生效日期**：1996-01-16

> (1996年1月8日国务院第41次常务会议通过　1996年1月16日中华人民共和国国务院令第191号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保障人民警察依法履行职责，正确使用警械和武器，及时有效地制止违法犯罪行为，维护公共安全和社会秩序，保护公民的人身安全和合法财产，保护公共财产，根据《中华人民共和国人民警察法》和其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　人民警察制止违法犯罪行为，可以采取强制手段；根据需要，可以依照本条例的规定使用警械；使用警械不能制止，或者不使用武器制止，可能发生严重危害后果的，可以依照本条例的规定使用武器。 第三条　本条例所称警械，是指人民警察按照规定装备的警棍、催泪弹、高压水枪、特种防暴枪、手铐、脚镣、警绳等警用器械；所称武器，是指人民警察按照规定装备的枪支、弹药等致命性警用武器。 第四条　人民警察使用警械和武器，应当以制止违法犯罪行为，尽量减少人员伤亡、财产损失为原则。 第五条　人民警察依法使用警械和武器的行为，受法律保护。 人民警察不得违反本条例的规定使用警械和武器。 第六条　人民警察使用警械和武器前，应当命令在场无关人员躲避；在场无关人员应当服从人民警察的命令，避免受到伤害或者其他损失。 第二章　警械的使用 第七条　人民警察遇有下列情形之一，经警告无效的，可以使用警棍、催泪弹、高压水枪、特种防暴枪等驱逐性、制服性警械： (一)结伙斗殴、殴打他人、寻衅滋事、侮辱妇女或者进行其他流氓活动的； (二)聚众扰乱车站、码头、民用航空站、运动场等公共场所秩序的； (三)非法举行集会、游行、示威的； (四)强行冲越人民警察为履行职责设置的警戒线的；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障人民警察依法履行职责，正确使用警械和武器，及时有效地制止违法犯罪行为，维护公共安全和社会秩序，保护公民的人身安全和合法财产，保护公共财产，根据《中华人民共和国人民警察法》和其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the People's Police Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws for the purpose of ensuring that people's police officers perform their duties according to law, use police implements and weapons correctly, stop illegal and criminal acts in a timely and effective manner, maintain public security and social order, and protect the personal safety and lawful property of citizens and public property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民警察制止违法犯罪行为，可以采取强制手段；根据需要，可以依照本条例的规定使用警械；使用警械不能制止，或者不使用武器制止，可能发生严重危害后果的，可以依照本条例的规定使用武器。

**Article 2** When stopping illegal and criminal acts, people's police officers may take compulsory measures; where necessary, they may use police implements in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; where the use of police implements fails to stop such acts or the failure to use weapons to stop them may result in serious harmful consequences, they may use weapons in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称警械，是指人民警察按照规定装备的警棍、催泪弹、高压水枪、特种防暴枪、手铐、脚镣、警绳等警用器械；所称武器，是指人民警察按照规定装备的枪支、弹药等致命性警用武器。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "police implements" refer to police instruments and devices such as batons, tear gas, high-pressure water guns, special riot guns, handcuffs, leg irons, and police ropes that are equipped as prescribed by regulations; "weapons" refer to lethal police weapons such as firearms and ammunition that are equipped as prescribed by regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民警察使用警械和武器，应当以制止违法犯罪行为，尽量减少人员伤亡、财产损失为原则。

**Article 4** When using police implements and weapons, people's police officers shall adhere to the principle of stopping illegal and criminal acts while minimizing casualties and property damage.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民警察依法使用警械和武器的行为，受法律保护。  
人民警察不得违反本条例的规定使用警械和武器。

**Article 5** The acts of people's police officers in using police implements and weapons according to law shall be protected by law.  
People's police officers shall not use police implements and weapons in violation of the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民警察使用警械和武器前，应当命令在场无关人员躲避；在场无关人员应当服从人民警察的命令，避免受到伤害或者其他损失。

**Article 6** Before using police implements and weapons, people's police officers shall order irrelevant persons at the scene to take cover; such irrelevant persons shall obey the orders of the people's police officers to avoid injury or other losses.

### 第二章　警械的使用

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民警察遇有下列情形之一，经警告无效的，可以使用警棍、催泪弹、高压水枪、特种防暴枪等驱逐性、制服性警械：  
(一)结伙斗殴、殴打他人、寻衅滋事、侮辱妇女或者进行其他流氓活动的；  
(二)聚众扰乱车站、码头、民用航空站、运动场等公共场所秩序的；  
(三)非法举行集会、游行、示威的；  
(四)强行冲越人民警察为履行职责设置的警戒线的；  
(五)以暴力方法抗拒或者阻碍人民警察依法履行职责的；  
(六)袭击人民警察的；  
(七)危害公共安全、社会秩序和公民人身安全的其他行为，需要当场制止的；  
(八)法律、行政法规规定可以使用警械的其他情形。  
人民警察依照前款规定使用警械，应当以制止违法犯罪行为为限度；当违法犯罪行为得到制止时，应当立即停止使用。

**Article 7** People's police officers may use expulsive and subduing police implements such as batons, tear gas, high-pressure water guns, and special riot guns in any of the following circumstances where a warning is ineffective:  
(1) engaging in gang fighting, assaulting others, creating disturbances, insulting women, or engaging in other hooligan activities;  
(2) gathering a crowd to disrupt order at railway stations, docks, civil airports, sports venues, or other public places;  
(3) holding illegal assemblies, processions, or demonstrations;  
(4) forcibly crossing cordons set up by people's police officers for performing their duties;  
(5) violently resisting or obstructing people's police officers from performing their duties according to law;  
(6) attacking people's police officers;  
(7) other acts endangering public security, social order, or personal safety of citizens that need to be stopped on the spot; or  
(8) other circumstances where police implements may be used as provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
When using police implements in accordance with the preceding paragraph, people's police officers shall limit the use to stopping illegal and criminal acts; when such acts have been stopped, the use shall be discontinued immediately.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民警察依法执行下列任务，遇有违法犯罪分子可能脱逃、行凶、自杀、自伤或者有其他危险行为的，可以使用手铐、脚镣、警绳等约束性警械：  
(一)抓获违法犯罪分子或者犯罪重大嫌疑人的；  
(二)执行逮捕、拘留、看押、押解、审讯、拘传、强制传唤的；  
(三)法律、行政法规规定可以使用警械的其他情形。  
人民警察依照前款规定使用警械，不得故意造成人身伤害。

**Article 8** When lawfully performing any of the following tasks and encountering persons who have committed illegal or criminal acts who may escape, commit violence, commit suicide, inflict self-injury, or engage in other dangerous acts, people's police officers may use restraining police implements such as handcuffs, leg irons, and police ropes:  
(1) capturing persons who have committed illegal or criminal acts or major criminal suspects;  
(2) executing arrest, detention, custody, escort, interrogation, summons by force, or compulsory summons; or  
(3) other circumstances where police implements may be used as provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
When using police implements in accordance with the preceding paragraph, people's police officers shall not intentionally cause bodily injury.

### 第三章　武器的使用

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民警察判明有下列暴力犯罪行为的紧急情形之一，经警告无效的，可以使用武器：  
(一)放火、决水、爆炸等严重危害公共安全的；  
(二)劫持航空器、船舰、火车、机动车或者驾驶车、船等机动交通工具，故意危害公共安全的；  
(三)抢夺、抢劫枪支弹药、爆炸、剧毒等危险物品，严重危害公共安全的；  
(四)使用枪支、爆炸、剧毒等危险物品实施犯罪或者以使用枪支、爆炸、剧毒等危险物品相威胁实施犯罪的；  
(五)破坏军事、通讯、交通、能源、防险等重要设施，足以对公共安全造成严重、紧迫危险的；  
(六)实施凶杀、劫持人质等暴力行为，危及公民生命安全的；  
(七)国家规定的警卫、守卫、警戒的对象和目标受到暴力袭击、破坏或者有受到暴力袭击、破坏的紧迫危险的；  
(八)结伙抢劫或者持械抢劫公私财物的；  
(九)聚众械斗、暴乱等严重破坏社会治安秩序，用其他方法不能制止的；  
(十)以暴力方法抗拒或者阻碍人民警察依法履行职责或者暴力袭击人民警察，危及人民警察生命安全的；  
(十一)在押人犯、罪犯聚众骚乱、暴乱、行凶或者脱逃的；  
(十二)劫夺在押人犯、罪犯的；  
(十三)实施放火、决水、爆炸、凶杀、抢劫或者其他严重暴力犯罪行为后拒捕、逃跑的；  
(十四)犯罪分子携带枪支、爆炸、剧毒等危险物品拒捕、逃跑的；  
(十五)法律、行政法规规定可以使用武器的其他情形。  
人民警察依照前款规定使用武器，来不及警告或者警告后可能导致更为严重危害后果的，可以直接使用武器。

**Article 9** People's police officers may use weapons in any of the following emergency circumstances involving violent crimes where a warning is ineffective, provided that they have determined such circumstances:  
(1) arson, breaching dikes, explosion, or other acts seriously endangering public security;  
(2) hijacking aircraft, ships, trains, motor vehicles, or driving vehicles, ships, or other motorized means of transport, intentionally endangering public security;  
(3) seizing or robbing firearms, ammunition, explosives, highly toxic substances, or other dangerous articles, seriously endangering public security;  
(4) using firearms, explosives, highly toxic substances, or other dangerous articles to commit crimes, or threatening to use such articles to commit crimes;  
(5) destroying military, communication, transportation, energy, flood prevention, or other important facilities, which is sufficient to create a serious and imminent danger to public security;  
(6) committing violent acts such as murder or hostage-taking, endangering the lives of citizens;  
(7) the objects or targets of guard, security, or cordon as prescribed by the State are under violent attack or destruction, or face an imminent danger of such attack or destruction;  
(8) committing robbery in a gang or committing robbery with weapons against public or private property;  
(9) engaging in mass armed fights, riots, or other acts that seriously undermine social order and cannot be stopped by other means;  
(10) violently resisting or obstructing people's police officers from performing their duties according to law, or violently attacking people's police officers, endangering their lives;  
(11) detainees or criminals engaging in mass disturbances, riots, violence, or escape;  
(12) forcibly rescuing detainees or criminals;  
(13) resisting arrest or fleeing after committing arson, breaching dikes, explosion, murder, robbery, or other serious violent crimes;  
(14) criminals carrying firearms, explosives, highly toxic substances, or other dangerous articles while resisting arrest or fleeing; or  
(15) other circumstances where weapons may be used as provided by laws or administrative regulations.  
Where people's police officers use weapons in accordance with the preceding paragraph but have no time to give a warning or giving a warning may lead to more serious harmful consequences, they may use weapons directly.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民警察遇有下列情形之一的，不得使用武器：  
(一)发现实施犯罪的人为怀孕妇女、儿童的，但是使用枪支、爆炸、剧毒等危险物品实施暴力犯罪的除外；  
(二)犯罪分子处于群众聚集的场所或者存放大量易燃、易爆、剧毒、放射性等危险物品的场所的，但是不使用武器予以制止，将发生更为严重危害后果的除外。

**Article 10** People's police officers shall not use weapons in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where the person committing the crime is found to be a pregnant woman or a child, except where they use firearms, explosives, highly toxic substances, or other dangerous articles to commit violent crimes; or  
(2) where the criminal is in a place where people are gathered or a place where large quantities of flammable, explosive, highly toxic, radioactive, or other dangerous articles are stored, except where failure to use weapons to stop the act would result in more serious harmful consequences.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民警察遇有下列情形之一的，应当立即停止使用武器：  
(一)犯罪分子停止实施犯罪，服从人民警察命令的；  
(二)犯罪分子失去继续实施犯罪能力的。

**Article 11** People's police officers shall immediately discontinue the use of weapons in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the criminal ceases to commit the crime and obeys the orders of the people's police officers; or  
(2) the criminal loses the ability to continue committing the crime.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民警察使用武器造成犯罪分子或者无辜人员伤亡的，应当及时抢救受伤人员，保护现场，并立即向当地公安机关或者该人民警察所属机关报告。  
当地公安机关或者该人民警察所属机关接到报告后，应当及时进行勘验、调查，并及时通知当地人民检察院。  
当地公安机关或者该人民警察所属机关应当将犯罪分子或者无辜人员的伤亡情况，及时通知其家属或者其所在单位。

**Article 12** Where the use of weapons by people's police officers causes casualties to criminals or innocent persons, they shall promptly rescue the injured, protect the scene, and immediately report to the local public security organ or the organ to which the people's police officer belongs.  
After receiving the report, the local public security organ or the organ to which the people's police officer belongs shall promptly conduct an inspection and investigation, and promptly notify the local People's Procuratorate.  
The local public security organ or the organ to which the people's police officer belongs shall promptly notify the family members or the unit to which the criminal or innocent person belongs of the casualties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民警察使用武器的，应当将使用武器的情况如实向所属机关书面报告。

**Article 13** Where people's police officers use weapons, they shall submit a truthful written report on the use of weapons to the organ to which they belong.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民警察违法使用警械、武器，造成不应有的人员伤亡、财产损失，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分；对受到伤亡或者财产损失的人员，由该人民警察所属机关依照《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》的有关规定给予赔偿。

**Article 14** Where people's police officers illegally use police implements or weapons, causing unnecessary casualties or property damage, if such acts constitute a crime, they shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if they do not constitute a crime, they shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; persons who have suffered casualties or property damage shall be compensated by the organ to which the people's police officer belongs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民警察依法使用警械、武器，造成无辜人员伤亡或者财产损失的，由该人民警察所属机关参照《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》的有关规定给予补偿。

**Article 15** Where people's police officers lawfully use police implements or weapons, causing casualties or property damage to innocent persons, the organ to which the people's police officer belongs shall provide compensation by reference to the relevant provisions of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中国人民武装警察部队执行国家赋予的安全保卫任务时使用警械和武器，适用本条例的有关规定。

**Article 16** Where the Chinese People's Armed Police Force uses police implements and weapons while performing security protection tasks assigned by the State, the relevant provisions of these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。1980年7月5日公布施行的《人民警察使用武器和警械的规定》同时废止。

**Article 17** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Provisions on the Use of Weapons and Police Implements by People's Police promulgated and put into effect on July 5, 1980 shall be abolished simultaneously.
