# 公安部对部分刀具实行管制的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1983-03-12

> (1982年8月30日国务院批准) 第一条　为了保障公民人身安全，防止不法分子利用刀具作为凶器进行犯罪活动，特制订本规定。 第二条　本规定所管制的刀具是：匕首、三棱刀(包括机械加工用的三棱刮刀)、带有自锁装置的弹簧刀(跳刀)以及其它相类似的单刃、双刃、三棱尖刀。 第三条　匕首，除中国人民解放军和人民警察作为武器、警械配备的以外，专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员必须持有的，须由县以上主管单位出具证明，经县以上公安机关批准，发给《匕首佩带证》，方准持有佩带。 佩带匕首人员如果不再从事原来的职业，应将匕首交还配发单位，《匕首佩带证》交回原发证公安机关。 第四条　机械加工使用的三棱刮刀，只限工作人员在工作场所使用，不得随意带出工作场所。 第五条　制造上述管制范围内刀具的工厂、作坊，必须经县、市以上主管部门审查同意和所在地县、市公安局批准，发给《特种刀具生产许可证》，方准生产。刀具样品及其说明(名称、规格、型号、用途、产量)须送所在地县、市公安局备案。产品须铸刻商标和号码(顺序号或批号)。 第六条　经销上述管制范围内刀具的商店，必须经县、市以上主管部门审查同意和所在地县、市公安局批准。购销要建立登记制度，备公安机关检查。 第七条　购买上述管制范围内刀具的单位和个人，必须符合第三、四条关于持有和使用的规定。 军队和警察， 由县、团以上单位凭上一级主管部门批准的函件，向指定单位定购。专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员，由所属单位向所在地县、市公安局(公安分局)申请《特种刀具购买证》，凭证购买。三棱刮刀，凭单位介绍信向批准经销的商店购买。 第八条　使用上述管制范围内刀具的单位，必须建立健全使用保管制度，加强刀具的管理和检查，确保安全。持有上述刀具的个人，对刀具应妥善保管，不得随意赠送、转借他人使用。发现丢失、被盗，要及时报告公安保卫部门。凡因保管不当，造成丢失、被盗，而酿成严重后果的，要追究有关人员和单位领导的责任。 第九条　严禁任何单位和个人非法制造、销售和贩卖匕首、三棱刀、弹簧刀等属于管制范围内的各种刀具。 严禁非法携带上述刀具进入车站、 码头、机场、公园、商场、影剧院、展览馆或其它公共场所和乘坐火车、汽车、轮船、飞机。 第十条　本规定下达后，凡制造、销售上述管制范围内各种刀具的单位和持有匕首的专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员，须向公安机关补办登记许可手续。非因生产、工作需要持有上述刀具的，应一律自动送交当地公安机关。 第十一条　《特种刀具生产许可证》、《匕首佩带证》和《特种刀具购买证》，样式由公安部统一规定，各省、市、自治区公安厅、局统一印制． 第十二条　少数民族由于生活习惯需要佩带的刀具，由民族自治区制订办法管理。 少数民族使用的藏刀、腰刀、靴刀等，只准在民族自治地方(自治区、自治州、自治县)销售。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障公民人身安全，防止不法分子利用刀具作为凶器进行犯罪活动，特制订本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to safeguard the personal safety of citizens and prevent lawbreakers from using knives as lethal weapons to commit criminal activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所管制的刀具是：匕首、三棱刀(包括机械加工用的三棱刮刀)、带有自锁装置的弹簧刀(跳刀)以及其它相类似的单刃、双刃、三棱尖刀。

**Article 2** The knives controlled under these Provisions are: daggers, triangular-edged knives (including triangular-edged scrapers used in machining), spring-blade knives with self-locking devices (switchblades), and other similar single-edged, double-edged, or triangular-pointed knives.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　匕首，除中国人民解放军和人民警察作为武器、警械配备的以外，专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员必须持有的，须由县以上主管单位出具证明，经县以上公安机关批准，发给《匕首佩带证》，方准持有佩带。  
佩带匕首人员如果不再从事原来的职业，应将匕首交还配发单位，《匕首佩带证》交回原发证公安机关。

**Article 3** Daggers: Except for those equipped as weapons or police equipment by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the people's police, daggers that must be carried by professional hunters and personnel engaged in field operations such as geology and exploration shall be carried only after the issuing of a Dagger Carrying Permit by the public security organ at or above the county level upon application supported by a certificate issued by the responsible unit at or above the county level.  
If a person carrying a dagger no longer engages in the original occupation, such person shall return the dagger to the issuing unit and return the Dagger Carrying Permit to the original issuing public security organ.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　机械加工使用的三棱刮刀，只限工作人员在工作场所使用，不得随意带出工作场所。

**Article 4** Triangular-edged scrapers used in machining may only be used by staff members in the workplace and shall not be taken out of the workplace at will.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　制造上述管制范围内刀具的工厂、作坊，必须经县、市以上主管部门审查同意和所在地县、市公安局批准，发给《特种刀具生产许可证》，方准生产。刀具样品及其说明(名称、规格、型号、用途、产量)须送所在地县、市公安局备案。产品须铸刻商标和号码(顺序号或批号)。

**Article 5** Factories or workshops manufacturing knives within the controlled scope mentioned above must obtain examination and approval by the competent department at or above the county or city level and approval by the local county or city public security bureau, and shall be issued a Special Knife Production License before they may commence production. The knife samples and their descriptions (name, specifications, model, purpose, output) shall be submitted to the local county or city public security bureau for record. Products shall have trademarks and numbers (serial numbers or batch numbers) engraved or cast on them.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经销上述管制范围内刀具的商店，必须经县、市以上主管部门审查同意和所在地县、市公安局批准。购销要建立登记制度，备公安机关检查。

**Article 6** Shops selling knives within the controlled scope mentioned above must obtain examination and approval by the competent department at or above the county or city level and approval by the local county or city public security bureau. A registration system for purchases and sales shall be established for inspection by the public security organ.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　购买上述管制范围内刀具的单位和个人，必须符合第三、四条关于持有和使用的规定。 军队和警察， 由县、团以上单位凭上一级主管部门批准的函件，向指定单位定购。专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员，由所属单位向所在地县、市公安局(公安分局)申请《特种刀具购买证》，凭证购买。三棱刮刀，凭单位介绍信向批准经销的商店购买。

**Article 7** Units and individuals purchasing knives within the controlled scope mentioned above must comply with the provisions on possession and use as set forth in Articles 3 and 4. The military and the police shall order knives from designated units by means of letters approved by the competent department at the next higher level from units at or above the county or regiment level. Professional hunters and personnel engaged in field operations such as geology and exploration shall apply to the local county or city public security bureau (public security sub-bureau) through their units for a Special Knife Purchase Certificate, and shall purchase knives upon presentation of such certificate. Triangular-edged scrapers shall be purchased from approved selling shops upon presentation of a letter of introduction from the unit.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　使用上述管制范围内刀具的单位，必须建立健全使用保管制度，加强刀具的管理和检查，确保安全。持有上述刀具的个人，对刀具应妥善保管，不得随意赠送、转借他人使用。发现丢失、被盗，要及时报告公安保卫部门。凡因保管不当，造成丢失、被盗，而酿成严重后果的，要追究有关人员和单位领导的责任。

**Article 8** Units using knives within the controlled scope mentioned above must establish and improve a system for use and safekeeping, strengthen the management and inspection of knives, and ensure safety. Individuals possessing the knives mentioned above shall keep them in safe custody and shall not give them away or lend them to others at will. In case of loss or theft, the public security or security department shall be promptly reported. If any loss or theft due to improper custody leads to serious consequences, the relevant personnel and the leaders of the unit shall be held liable.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　严禁任何单位和个人非法制造、销售和贩卖匕首、三棱刀、弹簧刀等属于管制范围内的各种刀具。 严禁非法携带上述刀具进入车站、 码头、机场、公园、商场、影剧院、展览馆或其它公共场所和乘坐火车、汽车、轮船、飞机。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual is strictly prohibited from illegally manufacturing, selling, or trafficking daggers, triangular-edged knives, spring-blade knives, and other knives within the controlled scope. It is strictly prohibited to illegally carry the knives mentioned above to enter stations, docks, airports, parks, shopping malls, theaters, exhibition halls, or other public places, or to take trains, buses, ships, or aircraft.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本规定下达后，凡制造、销售上述管制范围内各种刀具的单位和持有匕首的专业狩猎人员和地质、勘探等野外作业人员，须向公安机关补办登记许可手续。非因生产、工作需要持有上述刀具的，应一律自动送交当地公安机关。

**Article 10** After these Provisions are issued, units manufacturing or selling all kinds of knives within the controlled scope mentioned above, as well as professional hunters and personnel engaged in field operations such as geology and exploration who carry daggers, shall go through the formalities of registration or permit with the public security organ retroactively. Those who possess the knives mentioned above not for production or work needs shall voluntarily deliver them to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　《特种刀具生产许可证》、《匕首佩带证》和《特种刀具购买证》，样式由公安部统一规定，各省、市、自治区公安厅、局统一印制．

**Article 11** The formats of the Special Knife Production License, the Dagger Carrying Permit, and the Special Knife Purchase Certificate shall be uniformly prescribed by the Ministry of Public Security and uniformly printed by the public security departments (bureaus) of provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　少数民族由于生活习惯需要佩带的刀具，由民族自治区制订办法管理。  
少数民族使用的藏刀、腰刀、靴刀等，只准在民族自治地方(自治区、自治州、自治县)销售。

**Article 12** Knives that are required to be carried by ethnic minorities due to their living customs shall be managed by measures formulated by the ethnic autonomous regions.  
Tibetan knives, waist knives, boot knives, and others used by ethnic minorities may only be sold in ethnic autonomous areas (autonomous regions, autonomous prefectures, autonomous counties).

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　违反本规定，非法制造、销售、携带和私自保存管制范围刀具的，公安机关应予取缔，没收其刀具，并按照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》有关条款予以治安处罚；有妨害公共安全行为，情节严重，触犯刑律的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where anyone violates these Provisions by illegally manufacturing, selling, carrying, or privately keeping knives within the controlled scope, the public security organ shall ban such activity, confiscate the knives, and impose a public security penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Penalties for Public Security; where the act endangers public security, the circumstances are serious, and the act violates the criminal law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各省、市、自治区人民政府可根据本规定，制订具体管理办法，报公安部备案。

**Article 14** The people's governments of provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions may formulate specific management measures in accordance with these Provisions and report them to the Ministry of Public Security for record.
