# 专职守护押运人员枪支使用管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2002-07-27  
**生效日期**：2002-07-27

> (2002年7月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第356号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对守护、押运公务用枪的管理，保障专职守护、押运人员正确使用枪支，根据《中华人民共和国枪支管理法》(以下简称枪支管理法)，并参照《中华人民共和国人民警察使用警械和武器条例》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称专职守护、押运人员，是指依法配备公务用枪的军工、金融、国家重要仓储、大型水利、电力、通讯工程、机要交通系统的专职守护、押运人员以及经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关批准从事武装守护、押运服务的保安服务公司的专职守护、押运人员。 第三条　配备公务用枪的专职守护、押运人员必须符合下列条件： (一)年满20周岁的中国公民，身心健康，品行良好； (二)没有精神病等不能控制自己行为能力的疾病病史； (三)没有行政拘留、收容教育、强制戒毒、收容教养、劳动教养和刑事处罚记录； (四)经过专业培训，熟悉有关枪支使用、管理法律法规和规章的规定； (五)熟练掌握枪支使用、保养技能。 配备公务用枪的专职守护、押运人员，必须严格依照前款规定的条件，由所在单位审查后，报所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关审查、考核；审查、考核合格的，依照枪支管理法的规定，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关审查批准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关发给持枪证件。 第四条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，方可依照本条例的规定携带、使用枪支。 专职守护、押运人员依法携带、使用枪支的行为，受法律保护；违法携带、使用枪支的，依法承担法律责任。 第五条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，能够以其他手段保护守护目标、押运物品安全的，不得使用枪支；确有必要使用枪支的，应当以保护守护目标、押运物品不被侵害为目的，并尽量避免或者减少人员伤亡、财产损失。 第六条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，遇有下列紧急情形之一，不使用枪支不足以制止暴力犯罪行为的，可以使用枪支： (一)守护目标、押运物品受到暴力袭击或者有受到暴力袭击的紧迫危险的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对守护、押运公务用枪的管理，保障专职守护、押运人员正确使用枪支，根据《中华人民共和国枪支管理法》(以下简称枪支管理法)，并参照《中华人民共和国人民警察使用警械和武器条例》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting, to ensure that full-time guarding and escorting personnel use firearms correctly, in accordance with the Firearms Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Firearms Administration Law) and with reference to the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Use of Police Implements and Weapons by the People's Police.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称专职守护、押运人员，是指依法配备公务用枪的军工、金融、国家重要仓储、大型水利、电力、通讯工程、机要交通系统的专职守护、押运人员以及经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关批准从事武装守护、押运服务的保安服务公司的专职守护、押运人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, full-time guarding and escorting personnel refer to full-time guarding and escorting personnel of military industrial, financial, national important storage, large-scale water conservancy, electric power, communication engineering, and confidential transportation systems who are equipped with firearms for official duties according to law, as well as full-time guarding and escorting personnel of security service companies that have been approved by the public security organs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government to engage in armed guarding and escorting services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　配备公务用枪的专职守护、押运人员必须符合下列条件：  
(一)年满20周岁的中国公民，身心健康，品行良好；  
(二)没有精神病等不能控制自己行为能力的疾病病史；  
(三)没有行政拘留、收容教育、强制戒毒、收容教养、劳动教养和刑事处罚记录；  
(四)经过专业培训，熟悉有关枪支使用、管理法律法规和规章的规定；  
(五)熟练掌握枪支使用、保养技能。  
配备公务用枪的专职守护、押运人员，必须严格依照前款规定的条件，由所在单位审查后，报所在地设区的市级人民政府公安机关审查、考核；审查、考核合格的，依照枪支管理法的规定，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关审查批准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关发给持枪证件。

**Article 3** Full-time guarding and escorting personnel equipped with firearms for official duties must meet the following conditions:  
(1) Chinese citizens who have reached the age of 20, are physically and mentally healthy, and have good conduct;  
(2) No medical history of mental illness or other diseases that render them unable to control their conduct;  
(3) No record of administrative detention, shelter and education, compulsory drug rehabilitation, shelter and rehabilitation, reeducation through labor, or criminal punishment;  
(4) Having undergone professional training and being familiar with relevant laws, regulations, and rules on the use and management of firearms;  
(5) Being proficient in the skills of using and maintaining firearms.  
Full-time guarding and escorting personnel equipped with firearms for official duties must strictly comply with the conditions prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and after being examined by their employing unit, shall be submitted to the public security organ of the city with districts at the place of domicile for examination and assessment; if qualified after examination and assessment, the matter shall be submitted to the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval in accordance with the provisions of the Firearms Administration Law, and the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue a firearm license.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，方可依照本条例的规定携带、使用枪支。  
专职守护、押运人员依法携带、使用枪支的行为，受法律保护；违法携带、使用枪支的，依法承担法律责任。

**Article 4** Full-time guarding and escorting personnel may carry and use firearms in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations only when performing guarding and escorting duties.  
The act of carrying and using firearms by full-time guarding and escorting personnel according to law shall be protected by law; those who carry or use firearms illegally shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，能够以其他手段保护守护目标、押运物品安全的，不得使用枪支；确有必要使用枪支的，应当以保护守护目标、押运物品不被侵害为目的，并尽量避免或者减少人员伤亡、财产损失。

**Article 5** When performing guarding and escorting duties, if full-time guarding and escorting personnel can protect the safety of the guarded targets or escorted articles by other means, they shall not use firearms; if it is truly necessary to use firearms, they shall do so for the purpose of protecting the guarded targets or escorted articles from infringement, and shall try to avoid or reduce casualties and property losses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　专职守护、押运人员执行守护、押运任务时，遇有下列紧急情形之一，不使用枪支不足以制止暴力犯罪行为的，可以使用枪支：  
(一)守护目标、押运物品受到暴力袭击或者有受到暴力袭击的紧迫危险的；  
(二)专职守护、押运人员受到暴力袭击危及生命安全或者所携带的枪支弹药受到抢夺、抢劫的。

**Article 6** When performing guarding and escorting duties, if any of the following emergency situations occurs and using firearms is the only way to stop violent criminal acts, full-time guarding and escorting personnel may use firearms:  
(1) The guarded targets or escorted articles are under violent attack or are in imminent danger of being violently attacked;  
(2) The full-time guarding or escorting personnel are under violent attack that endangers their life safety, or the firearms and ammunition they carry are being snatched or robbed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　专职守护、押运人员在存放大量易燃、易爆、剧毒、放射性等危险物品的场所，不得使用枪支；但是，不使用枪支制止犯罪行为将会直接导致严重危害后果发生的除外。

**Article 7** In places where a large amount of flammable, explosive, highly toxic, radioactive, or other dangerous articles are stored, full-time guarding and escorting personnel shall not use firearms; however, this shall not apply if failure to use firearms to stop criminal acts would directly result in serious harmful consequences.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　专职守护、押运人员遇有下列情形之一的，应当立即停止使用枪支：  
(一)有关行为人停止实施暴力犯罪行为的；  
(二)有关行为人失去继续实施暴力犯罪行为能力的。

**Article 8** Full-time guarding and escorting personnel shall immediately stop using firearms in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The relevant actor has stopped committing violent criminal acts;  
(2) The relevant actor has lost the ability to continue committing violent criminal acts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　专职守护、押运人员使用枪支后，应当立即向所在单位和案发地公安机关报告；所在单位和案发地公安机关接到报告后，应当立即派人抵达现场。  
专职守护、押运人员的所在单位接到专职守护、押运人员使用枪支的报告后，应当立即报告所在地公安机关，并在事后向所在地公安机关报送枪支使用情况的书面报告。

**Article 9** After using firearms, full-time guarding and escorting personnel shall immediately report to their employing unit and the public security organ at the place of incident; upon receiving the report, the employing unit and the public security organ at the place of incident shall immediately dispatch personnel to arrive at the scene.  
Upon receiving the report on the use of firearms by full-time guarding or escorting personnel, the employing unit shall immediately report to the public security organ at its place of location, and shall later submit a written report on the circumstances of firearms use to the public security organ at its place of location.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依法配备守护、押运公务用枪的单位，应当建立、健全持枪人员管理责任制度，枪支弹药保管、领用制度和枪支安全责任制度；对依照[本条例第三条](#art-3)的规定批准的持枪人员加强法制和安全教育，定期组织培训，经常检查枪支的保管和使用情况。

**Article 10** Units that are equipped with firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting according to law shall establish and improve the responsibility system for the management of firearm holders, the system for the storage and requisition of firearms and ammunition, and the safety responsibility system for firearms; shall strengthen legal and safety education for firearm holders approved in accordance with Article 3 of these Regulations, organize regular training, and frequently inspect the storage and use of firearms.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依法配备守护、押运公务用枪的单位应当设立专门的枪支保管库(室)或者使用专用保险柜，将配备的枪支、弹药集中统一保管。枪支与弹药必须分开存放，实行双人双锁，并且24小时有人值班。存放枪支、弹药的库(室)门窗必须坚固并安装防盗报警设施。

**Article 11** Units that are equipped with firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting according to law shall set up special firearm storage rooms (vaults) or use special safes to store all equipped firearms and ammunition in a centralized and unified manner. Firearms and ammunition must be stored separately, with a two-person two-lock system, and be guarded 24 hours a day. The doors and windows of the storage rooms (vaults) where firearms and ammunition are stored must be sturdy and equipped with anti-theft alarm facilities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　专职守护、押运人员执行任务携带枪支、弹药，必须妥善保管，严防丢失、被盗、被抢或者发生其他事故；任务执行完毕，必须立即将枪支、弹药交还。  
严禁非执行守护、押运任务时携带枪支、弹药，严禁携带枪支、弹药饮酒或者酒后携带枪支、弹药。

**Article 12** When carrying firearms and ammunition while performing duties, full-time guarding and escorting personnel must keep them properly to prevent loss, theft, robbery, or other accidents; after the completion of duties, they must immediately return the firearms and ammunition.  
It is strictly prohibited to carry firearms or ammunition when not performing guarding or escorting duties, and it is strictly prohibited to consume alcohol while carrying firearms or ammunition or to carry firearms or ammunition after consuming alcohol.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公安机关应当对其管辖范围内依法配备守护、押运公务用枪的单位建立、执行枪支管理制度的情况，定期进行检查、监督。

**Article 13** Public security organs shall regularly inspect and supervise the establishment and implementation of the firearm management system by units within their jurisdiction that are equipped with firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　专职守护、押运人员有下列情形之一的，所在单位应当停止其执行武装守护、押运任务，收回其持枪证件，并及时将持枪证件上缴公安机关：  
(一)拟调离专职守护、押运工作岗位的；  
(二)理论和实弹射击考核不合格的；  
(三)因刑事案件或者其他违法违纪案件被立案侦查、调查的；  
(四)擅自改动枪支、更换枪支零部件的；  
(五)违反规定携带、使用枪支或者将枪支交给他人，对枪支失去控制的；  
(六)丢失枪支或者在枪支被盗、被抢事故中负有责任的。  
专职守护、押运人员有前款第(四)项、第(五)项、第(六)项行为，造成严重后果的，依照刑法关于非法持有私藏枪支弹药罪、非法携带枪支弹药危及公共安全罪、非法出租出借枪支罪或者丢失枪支不报罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依照枪支管理法的规定，给予行政处罚。

**Article 14** If a full-time guarding or escorting personnel is in any of the following circumstances, the employing unit shall suspend him from performing armed guarding or escorting tasks, revoke his firearm license, and promptly surrender the firearm license to the public security organ:  
(1) He is to be transferred away from the full-time guarding or escorting post;  
(2) He fails the theoretical and live-fire shooting assessments;  
(3) He is placed on file for investigation or inquiry due to a criminal case or other illegal or disciplinary case;  
(4) He unauthorizedly alters the firearm or replaces firearm parts;  
(5) He carries or uses a firearm in violation of regulations or hands over the firearm to another person, thereby losing control of the firearm;  
(6) He loses a firearm or bears responsibility for a firearm theft or robbery incident.  
If a full-time guarding or escorting personnel commits the acts specified in subparagraphs (4), (5), or (6) of the preceding paragraph and causes serious consequences, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crimes of illegally possessing or concealing firearms and ammunition, illegally carrying firearms and ammunition endangering public safety, illegally renting or lending firearms, or failing to report a lost firearm; if the circumstances are not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Firearms Administration Law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依法配备守护、押运公务用枪的单位违反枪支管理规定，有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分或者相应的纪律处分；造成严重后果的，依照刑法关于玩忽职守罪、滥用职权罪、丢失枪支不报罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未建立或者未能有效执行持枪人员管理责任制度的；  
(二)将不符合法定条件的专职守护、押运人员报送公安机关审批或者允许没有持枪证件的人员携带、使用枪支的；  
(三)使用枪支后，不报告公安机关的；  
(四)未建立或者未能有效执行枪支、弹药管理制度，造成枪支、弹药被盗、被抢或者丢失的；  
(五)枪支、弹药被盗、被抢或者丢失，未及时报告公安机关的；  
(六)不按照规定审验枪支的；  
(七)不上缴报废枪支的；  
(八)发生其他涉枪违法违纪案件的。

**Article 15** If a unit that is equipped with firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting according to law violates firearm control regulations in any of the following circumstances, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions of a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office, or corresponding disciplinary sanctions according to law; if serious consequences are caused, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crimes of dereliction of duty, abuse of power, failure to report a lost firearm, or other crimes:  
(1) Failing to establish or effectively implement the responsibility system for the management of firearm holders;  
(2) Submitting full-time guarding or escorting personnel who do not meet the statutory conditions to the public security organ for approval or allowing persons without firearm licenses to carry or use firearms;  
(3) Failing to report to the public security organ after using firearms;  
(4) Failing to establish or effectively implement the management system for firearms and ammunition, resulting in theft, robbery, or loss of firearms or ammunition;  
(5) Failing to promptly report to the public security organ after firearms or ammunition are stolen, robbed, or lost;  
(6) Failing to inspect firearms in accordance with regulations;  
(7) Failing to surrender obsolete firearms;  
(8) Occurrence of other illegal or disciplinary cases involving firearms.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　专职守护、押运人员依照本条例的规定使用枪支，造成无辜人员伤亡或者财产损失的，由其所在单位依法补偿受害人的损失。  
专职守护、押运人员违反本条例的规定使用枪支，造成人员伤亡或者财产损失的，除依法受到刑事处罚或者行政处罚外，还应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 16** If a full-time guarding or escorting personnel uses a firearm in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and causes casualties or property losses to innocent persons, his employing unit shall compensate the victims' losses according to law.  
If a full-time guarding or escorting personnel uses a firearm in violation of the provisions of these Regulations and causes casualties or property losses, in addition to being subject to criminal or administrative penalties according to law, he shall also bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公安机关有下列行为之一，造成严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予记大过、降级或者撤职的行政处分：  
(一)超出法定范围批准有关单位配备守护、押运公务用枪的；  
(二)为不符合法定条件的人员发放守护、押运公务用枪持枪证件的；  
(三)不履行本条例规定的监督管理职责，造成后果的。

**Article 17** If a public security organ commits any of the following acts and causes serious consequences, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crimes of abuse of power and dereliction of duty; if the circumstances are not serious enough for criminal punishment, they shall be given administrative sanctions of a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office according to law:  
(1) Approving units to be equipped with firearms for official duties in guarding and escorting beyond the statutory scope;  
(2) Issuing firearm licenses for guarding and escorting official duties firearms to persons who do not meet the statutory conditions;  
(3) Failing to perform the supervisory and administrative duties stipulated in these Regulations, resulting in consequences.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 18** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
