**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2000-08-18  
**生效日期**：2000-10-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国人民警察警衔条例》的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Police Ranks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民警察佩带的警衔标志必须与所授予的警衔相符。

**Article 2** The police rank insignia worn by the people's police shall be consistent with the police rank granted.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民警察的警衔标志：总警监、副总警监警衔标志由银色橄榄枝环绕银色国徽组成，警监警衔标志由银色橄榄枝和银色四角星花组成，警督、警司警衔标志由银色横杠和银色四角星花组成，警员警衔标志由银色四角星花组成。

**Article 3** The police rank insignia of the people's police are: the rank insignia of Commissioner General and Deputy Commissioner General shall consist of a silver olive branch surrounding a silver national emblem; the rank insignia of Police Supervisor shall consist of a silver olive branch and silver four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Superintendent and Police Inspector shall consist of silver horizontal bars and silver four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Constable shall consist of silver four-pointed star patterns.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　警衔标志佩带在肩章上，肩章为剑形，分为硬肩章和软肩章。担任行政职务的人民警察的肩章版面为藏蓝色，担任专业技术职务的人民警察的肩章版面为蓝灰色。  
人民警察着常服、执勤服时，佩带硬肩章；着作训服、制式衬衣时，佩带扣式软肩章；着多功能服时，佩带套式软肩章。

**Article 4** The police rank insignia shall be worn on epaulettes, which are sword-shaped and divided into hard epaulettes and soft epaulettes. The background of the epaulettes for people's police holding administrative posts shall be navy blue, and the background of the epaulettes for people's police holding professional and technical posts shall be blue-gray.  
When the people's police wear service uniforms or duty uniforms, they shall wear hard epaulettes; when wearing training uniforms or standard shirts, they shall wear button-type soft epaulettes; when wearing multifunctional uniforms, they shall wear slip-on soft epaulettes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　总警监警衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝环绕一周的国徽，副总警监警衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝环绕半周的国徽。  
警监警衔标志缀钉一枚橄榄枝，一级警监警衔标志缀钉三枚四角星花；二级警监警衔标志缀钉二枚四角星花；三级警监警衔标志缀钉一枚四角星花。  
警督警衔标志缀钉二道横杠，一级警督警衔标志缀钉三枚四角星花；二级警督警衔标志缀钉二枚四角星花；三级警督警衔标志缀钉一枚四角星花。  
警司警衔标志缀钉一道横杠，一级警司警衔标志缀钉三枚四角星花；二级警司警衔标志缀钉二枚四角星花；三级警司警衔标志缀钉一枚四角星花。  
警员警衔标志缀钉四角星花，一级警员警衔标志缀钉二枚四角星花；二级警员警衔标志缀钉一枚四角星花。

**Article 5** The rank insignia of Commissioner General shall bear a national emblem surrounded by one circle of olive branch, and the rank insignia of Deputy Commissioner General shall bear a national emblem surrounded by a half circle of olive branch.  
The rank insignia of Police Supervisor shall bear one olive branch; the rank insignia of Police Supervisor, First Class shall bear three four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Supervisor, Second Class shall bear two four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Supervisor, Third Class shall bear one four-pointed star pattern.  
The rank insignia of Police Superintendent shall bear two horizontal bars; the rank insignia of Police Superintendent, First Class shall bear three four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Superintendent, Second Class shall bear two four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Superintendent, Third Class shall bear one four-pointed star pattern.  
The rank insignia of Police Inspector shall bear one horizontal bar; the rank insignia of Police Inspector, First Class shall bear three four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Inspector, Second Class shall bear two four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Inspector, Third Class shall bear one four-pointed star pattern.  
The rank insignia of Police Constable shall bear four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Constable, First Class shall bear two four-pointed star patterns; the rank insignia of Police Constable, Second Class shall bear one four-pointed star pattern.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　担任专业技术职务的人民警察警衔标志的缀钉、佩带办法与担任行政职务的相同衔级的人民警察相同。

**Article 6** The method of wearing and affixing the police rank insignia for the people's police holding professional and technical posts shall be the same as that for the people's police of the same rank holding administrative posts.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民警察晋升或者降低警衔时，由批准机关更换其警衔标志；取消警衔的，由批准机关收回其警衔标志。

**Article 7** When a people's policeman is promoted or demoted in police rank, the approving authority shall replace his or her police rank insignia; if the police rank is removed, the approving authority shall recover his or her police rank insignia.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民警察的警衔标志由公安部负责制作和管理。其他单位和个人不得制作、仿造、伪造和买卖、使用警衔标志，也不得使用与警衔标志相类似的标志。

**Article 8** The police rank insignia of the people's police shall be produced and managed by the Ministry of Public Security. No other unit or individual may produce, imitate, forge, buy, sell or use police rank insignia, nor may they use insignia similar to police rank insignia.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本办法由公安部负责解释。

**Article 9** These Measures shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本办法自2000年10月1日起施行。
