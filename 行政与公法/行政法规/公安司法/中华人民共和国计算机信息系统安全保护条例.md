# 中华人民共和国计算机信息系统安全保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1994年2月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第147号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了保护计算机信息系统的安全，促进计算机的应用和发展，保障社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称的计算机信息系统，是指由计算机及其相关的和配套的设备、设施(含网络)构成的，按照一定的应用目标和规则对信息进行采集、加工、存储、传输、检索等处理的人机系统。 第三条　计算机信息系统的安全保护，应当保障计算机及其相关的和配套的设备、设施(含网络)的安全，运行环境的安全，保障信息的安全，保障计算机功能的正常发挥，以维护计算机信息系统的安全运行。 第四条　计算机信息系统的安全保护工作，重点维护国家事务、经济建设、国防建设、尖端科学技术等重要领域的计算机信息系统的安全。 第五条　中华人民共和国境内的计算机信息系统的安全保护，适用本条例。 未联网的微型计算机的安全保护办法，另行制定。 第六条　公安部主管全国计算机信息系统安全保护工作。 国家安全部、国家保密局和国务院其他有关部门，在国务院规定的职责范围内做好计算机信息系统安全保护的有关工作。 第七条　任何组织或者个人，不得利用计算机信息系统从事危害国家利益、集体利益和公民合法利益的活动，不得危害计算机信息系统的安全。 第二章　安全保护制度 第八条　计算机信息系统的建设和应用，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家其他有关规定。 第九条　计算机信息系统实行安全等级保护。安全等级的划分标准和安全等级保护的具体办法，由公安部会同有关部门制定。 第十条　计算机机房应当符合国家标准和国家有关规定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护计算机信息系统的安全，促进计算机的应用和发展，保障社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to protect the security of computer information systems, promote the application and development of computers, and ensure the smooth progress of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称的计算机信息系统，是指由计算机及其相关的和配套的设备、设施(含网络)构成的，按照一定的应用目标和规则对信息进行采集、加工、存储、传输、检索等处理的人机系统。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "computer information system" means a man-machine system composed of computers and their related and supporting equipment and facilities (including networks), which processes information such as collection, processing, storage, transmission, and retrieval in accordance with certain application objectives and rules.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　计算机信息系统的安全保护，应当保障计算机及其相关的和配套的设备、设施(含网络)的安全，运行环境的安全，保障信息的安全，保障计算机功能的正常发挥，以维护计算机信息系统的安全运行。

**Article 3** The security protection of computer information systems shall ensure the security of computers and their related and supporting equipment and facilities (including networks), the security of the operating environment, the security of information, and the normal functioning of computers, so as to maintain the secure operation of computer information systems.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　计算机信息系统的安全保护工作，重点维护国家事务、经济建设、国防建设、尖端科学技术等重要领域的计算机信息系统的安全。

**Article 4** The security protection work of computer information systems shall focus on maintaining the security of computer information systems in important areas such as state affairs, economic construction, national defense construction, and advanced science and technology.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　中华人民共和国境内的计算机信息系统的安全保护，适用本条例。  
未联网的微型计算机的安全保护办法，另行制定。

**Article 5** The security protection of computer information systems within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be governed by these Regulations.  
The security protection measures for non-networked microcomputers shall be formulated separately.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公安部主管全国计算机信息系统安全保护工作。  
国家安全部、国家保密局和国务院其他有关部门，在国务院规定的职责范围内做好计算机信息系统安全保护的有关工作。

**Article 6** The Ministry of Public Security shall be in charge of the security protection work of computer information systems nationwide.  
The Ministry of State Security, the State Secrets Bureau, and other relevant departments of the State Council shall perform relevant work related to the security protection of computer information systems within the scope of their responsibilities as prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何组织或者个人，不得利用计算机信息系统从事危害国家利益、集体利益和公民合法利益的活动，不得危害计算机信息系统的安全。

**Article 7** No organization or individual may use a computer information system to engage in activities that harm the interests of the state, collective interests, or the legitimate interests of citizens, nor may they endanger the security of a computer information system.

### 第二章　安全保护制度

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　计算机信息系统的建设和应用，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家其他有关规定。

**Article 8** The construction and application of computer information systems shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　计算机信息系统实行安全等级保护。安全等级的划分标准和安全等级保护的具体办法，由公安部会同有关部门制定。

**Article 9** Computer information systems shall implement graded security protection. The classification standards for security grades and the specific measures for graded security protection shall be formulated by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with relevant departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　计算机机房应当符合国家标准和国家有关规定。  
在计算机机房附近施工，不得危害计算机信息系统的安全。

**Article 10** Computer rooms shall conform to national standards and relevant state provisions.  
Construction near computer rooms shall not endanger the security of computer information systems.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进行国际联网的计算机信息系统，由计算机信息系统的使用单位报省级以上人民政府公安机关备案。

**Article 11** A computer information system that is connected to international networks shall be reported by the user unit of the computer information system to the public security organ of the people's government at or above the provincial level for filing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　运输、携带、邮寄计算机信息媒体进出境的，应当如实向海关申报。

**Article 12** Anyone transporting, carrying, or mailing computer information media into or out of the country shall declare truthfully to the customs.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　计算机信息系统的使用单位应当建立健全安全管理制度，负责本单位计算机信息系统的安全保护工作。

**Article 13** The user unit of a computer information system shall establish and improve the security management system and be responsible for the security protection of its own computer information system.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对计算机信息系统中发生的案件，有关使用单位应当在24小时内向当地县级以上人民政府公安机关报告。

**Article 14** For cases occurring in a computer information system, the relevant user unit shall report to the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level within 24 hours.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对计算机病毒和危害社会公共安全的其他有害数据的防治研究工作，由公安部归口管理。

**Article 15** The prevention and research work on computer viruses and other harmful data that endangers public safety shall be managed by the Ministry of Public Security in a centralized manner.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对计算机信息系统安全专用产品的销售实行许可证制度。具体办法由公安部会同有关部门制定。

**Article 16** The State implements a license system for the sale of special security products for computer information systems. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Public Security in conjunction with relevant departments.

### 第三章　安全监督

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公安机关对计算机信息系统安全保护工作行使下列监督职权：  
(一)监督、检查、指导计算机信息系统安全保护工作；  
(二)查处危害计算机信息系统安全的违法犯罪案件；  
(三)履行计算机信息系统安全保护工作的其他监督职责。

**Article 17** The public security organ shall exercise the following supervisory powers over the security protection of computer information systems:  
(1) supervise, inspect, and guide the security protection work of computer information systems;  
(2) investigate and deal with illegal and criminal cases that endanger the security of computer information systems;  
(3) perform other supervisory duties for the security protection work of computer information systems.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公安机关发现影响计算机信息系统安全的隐患时，应当及时通知使用单位采取安全保护措施。

**Article 18** When a public security organ discovers a hidden danger affecting the security of a computer information system, it shall promptly notify the user unit to take security protection measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公安部在紧急情况下，可以就涉及计算机信息系统安全的特定事项发布专项通令。

**Article 19** The Ministry of Public Security may, in an emergency, issue a special order on specific matters concerning the security of computer information systems.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关处以警告或者停机整顿：  
(一)违反计算机信息系统安全等级保护制度，危害计算机信息系统安全的；  
(二)违反计算机信息系统国际联网备案制度的；  
(三)不按照规定时间报告计算机信息系统中发生的案件的；  
(四)接到公安机关要求改进安全状况的通知后，在限期内拒不改进的；  
(五)有危害计算机信息系统安全的其他行为的。

**Article 20** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning or ordered to suspend operations for rectification by the public security organ:  
(1) violating the security level protection system for computer information systems, thereby endangering the security of computer information systems;  
(2) violating the international networking filing system for computer information systems;  
(3) failing to report cases occurring in a computer information system within the prescribed time;  
(4) after receiving a notice from the public security organ requiring improvement of security conditions, refusing to make improvements within the time limit;  
(5) engaging in other acts that endanger the security of computer information systems.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　计算机机房不符合国家标准和国家其他有关规定的，或者在计算机机房附近施工危害计算机信息系统安全的，由公安机关会同有关单位进行处理。

**Article 21** Where computer rooms do not meet national standards or other relevant regulations of the State, or where construction activities near computer rooms endanger the security of computer information systems, the public security organ shall handle the matter jointly with the relevant units.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　运输、携带、邮寄计算机信息媒体进出境，不如实向海关申报的，由海关依照《中华人民共和国海关法》和本条例以及其他有关法律、法规的规定处理。

**Article 22** Where anyone transports, carries, or mails computer information media into or out of the country without declaring truthfully to the customs, the customs shall handle the matter in accordance with the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, these Regulations, and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　故意输入计算机病毒以及其他有害数据危害计算机信息系统安全的，或者未经许可出售计算机信息系统安全专用产品的，由公安机关处以警告或者对个人处以5000元以下的罚款、对单位处以1.5万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，除予以没收外，可以处以违法所得1至3倍的罚款。

**Article 23** Whoever intentionally inputs computer viruses or other harmful data that endangers the security of computer information systems, or sells specialized products for computer information system security without permission, shall be warned by the public security organ, or be fined not more than RMB 5,000 yuan for an individual or not more than RMB 15,000 yuan for a unit; if there are illegal gains, such gains shall be confiscated and, in addition, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains may be imposed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例的规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Regulations and constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织或者个人违反本条例的规定，给国家、集体或者他人财产造成损失的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 25** Any organization or individual that violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes losses to the property of the State, a collective, or another person shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　当事人对公安机关依照本条例所作出的具体行政行为不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 26** A party that is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act taken by the public security organ in accordance with these Regulations may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate administrative action.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　执行本条例的国家公务员利用职权，索取、收受贿赂或者有其他违法、失职行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。

**Article 27** Any national public servant who, in implementing these Regulations, abuses his or her authority to extort or accept bribes, or commits other illegal or derelict acts, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
计算机病毒，是指编制或者在计算机程序中插入的破坏计算机功能或者毁坏数据，影响计算机使用，并能自我复制的一组计算机指令或者程序代码。  
计算机信息系统安全专用产品，是指用于保护计算机信息系统安全的专用硬件和软件产品。

**Article 28** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings specified below:  
Computer virus means a set of computer instructions or program codes that are compiled or inserted into computer programs to damage computer functions or destroy data, affect computer use, and are capable of self-replication.  
Specialized products for computer information system security means dedicated hardware and software products used to protect the security of computer information systems.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　军队的计算机信息系统安全保护工作，按照军队的有关法规执行。

**Article 29** The security protection of computer information systems of the armed forces shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant regulations of the armed forces.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公安部可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 30** The Ministry of Public Security may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force from the date of promulgation.
