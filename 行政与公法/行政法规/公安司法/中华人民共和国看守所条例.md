**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1990-03-17

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障刑事诉讼活动的顺利进行，依据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》及其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure the smooth progress of criminal actions and pursuant to the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the provisions of other relevant laws.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　看守所是羁押依法被逮捕、刑事拘留的人犯的机关。  
被判处有期徒刑一年以下，或者余刑在一年以下，不便送往劳动改造场所执行的罪犯，也可以由看守所监管。

**Article 2** A detention house is an organ that detains criminal suspects who have been arrested or placed under criminal detention according to law.  
Criminal offenders sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than one year, or whose remaining sentence is not more than one year and who are not convenient to be sent to a reform-through-labor facility for enforcement, may also be supervised by the detention house.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　看守所的任务是依据国家法律对被羁押的人犯实行武装警戒看守，保障安全；对人犯进行教育；管理人犯的生活和卫生；保障侦查、起诉和审判工作的顺利进行。

**Article 3** The tasks of a detention house shall be to provide armed guard and watch over the detained criminal suspects according to the national laws to ensure safety; to educate the criminal suspects; to manage the living and hygiene of criminal suspects; and to ensure the smooth progress of investigation, prosecution, and adjudication.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　看守所监管人犯，必须坚持严密警戒看管与教育相结合的方针，坚持依法管理、严格管理、科学管理和文明管理，保障人犯的合法权益。严禁打骂、体罚、虐待人犯。

**Article 4** In supervising criminal suspects, a detention house must adhere to the policy of combining strict guard and watch with education, and must adhere to management according to law, strict management, scientific management, and civil management, and shall protect the lawful rights and interests of criminal suspects. Beating, corporal punishment, or maltreatment of criminal suspects shall be strictly prohibited.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　看守所以县级以上的行政区域为单位设置，由本级公安机关管辖。  
省、自治区、直辖市国家安全厅(局)根据需要，可以设置看守所。  
铁道、交通、林业、民航等系统相当于县级以上的公安机关，可以设置看守所。

**Article 5** Detention houses shall be established by administrative areas at or above the county level and shall be under the jurisdiction of the public security organ at the same level.  
The department (bureau) of state security of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, as needed, set up detention houses.  
Public security organs at or above the county level in the railway, transportation, forestry, civil aviation, and other systems may set up detention houses.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　看守所设所长一人，副所长一至二人；根据工作需要，配备看守、管教、医务、财会、炊事等工作人员若干人。  
看守所应当配备女工作人员管理女性人犯。

**Article 6** A detention house shall have one director and one to two deputy directors; it shall, according to work needs, be staffed with a number of personnel such as guards, correctional officers, medical staff, accountants, and cooks.  
A detention house shall be staffed with female personnel to manage female criminal suspects.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　看守所对人犯的武装警戒和押解由中国人民武装警察部队(以下简称武警)担任。看守所对执行任务的武警实行业务指导。

**Article 7** The armed guard and escort of criminal suspects of a detention house shall be undertaken by the Chinese People's Armed Police Force (hereinafter referred to as the Armed Police). The detention house shall provide professional guidance to the Armed Police carrying out the tasks.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　看守所的监管活动受人民检察院的法律监督。

**Article 8** The supervision activities of a detention house shall be subject to the legal supervision of the People's Procuratorate.

### 第二章　收押

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　看守所收押人犯，须凭送押机关持有的县级以上公安机关、国家安全机关签发的逮捕证、刑事拘留证或者县级以上公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱、劳动改造机关，人民法院、人民检察院追捕、押解人犯临时寄押的证明文书。没有上述凭证，或者凭证的记载与实际情况不符的，不予收押。

**Article 9** To admit a criminal suspect, a detention house must rely on the arrest warrant or criminal detention warrant issued by a public security organ or state security organ at or above the county level held by the sending organ, or a certificate document for temporary custody of a criminal suspect being pursued or escorted by a public security organ or state security organ at or above the county level, prison, reform-through-labor organ, People's Court, or People's Procuratorate. If there is no such certificate, or the contents of the certificate do not match the actual situation, the detention house shall not admit the criminal suspect.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　看守所收押人犯，应当进行健康检查，有下列情形之一的，不予收押：  
(一)患有精神病或者急性传染病的；  
(二)患有其他严重疾病，在羁押中可能发生生命危险或者生活不能自理的，但是罪大恶极不羁押对社会有危险性的除外；  
(三)怀孕或者哺乳自己不满一周岁的婴儿的妇女。

**Article 10** When admitting a criminal suspect, a detention house shall conduct a health examination. A criminal suspect shall not be admitted under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) suffering from mental illness or acute infectious disease;  
(2) suffering from other serious diseases that may cause life danger or inability to take care of oneself during custody, except where the criminal suspect is extremely wicked and would pose a danger to society if not detained;  
(3) being a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant under one year of age.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　看守所收押人犯，应当对其人身和携带的物品进行严格检查。非日常用品应当登记，代为保管，出所时核对发还或者转监狱、劳动改造机关。违禁物品予以没收。发现犯罪证据和可疑物品，要当场制作记录，由人犯签字捺指印后，送案件主管机关处理。  
对女性人犯的人身检查，由女工作人员进行。

**Article 11** When admitting a criminal suspect, a detention house shall conduct strict inspections of the person and the articles carried by the criminal suspect. Articles that are not daily necessities shall be registered and kept in custody, and shall be checked and returned or transferred to the prison or reform-through-labor organ upon release. Prohibited articles shall be confiscated. If evidence of a crime or suspicious articles are discovered, a record shall be made on the spot, signed and fingerprinted by the criminal suspect, and then sent to the competent authority handling the case for disposition.  
Body inspections of female criminal suspects shall be conducted by female personnel.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　收押人犯，应当建立人犯档案。

**Article 12** When admitting a criminal suspect, a detention house shall establish a file for the criminal suspect.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　收押人犯，应当告知人犯在羁押期间必须遵守的监规和享有的合法权益。

**Article 13** When admitting a criminal suspect, the detention house shall inform the criminal suspect of the detention center rules that must be observed and the lawful rights and interests enjoyed during the period of custody.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对男性人犯和女性人犯，成年人犯和未成年人犯，同案犯以及其他需要分别羁押的人犯，应当分别羁押。

**Article 14** Male and female criminal suspects, adult and juvenile criminal suspects, co-defendants, and other criminal suspects that need to be detained separately shall be detained in separate custody.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公安机关或者国家安全机关侦查终结、人民检察院决定受理的人犯，人民检察院审查或者侦查终结、人民法院决定受理的人犯，递次移送交接，均应办理换押手续，书面通知看守所。

**Article 15** When a criminal suspect whose investigation has been concluded by a public security organ or state security organ and whose case has been decided to be accepted by the People's Procuratorate, or a criminal suspect whose examination or investigation has been concluded by the People's Procuratorate and whose case has been decided to be accepted by the People's Court, is transferred in succession, the formalities for changing custody shall be handled, and the detention house shall be notified in writing.

### 第三章　警戒、看守

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　看守所实行二十四小时值班制度。值班人员应当坚守岗位，随时巡视监房。

**Article 16** A detention house shall implement a 24-hour duty system. The personnel on duty shall remain at their posts and patrol the cells at any time.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对已被判处死刑、尚未执行的犯人，必须加戴械具。  
对有事实表明可能行凶、暴动、脱逃、自杀的人犯，经看守所所长批准，可以使用械具。在紧急情况下，可以先行使用，然后报告看守所所长。上述情形消除后，应当予以解除。

**Article 17** For a criminal offender who has been sentenced to death penalty but has not yet been executed, restraint devices must be applied.  
For a criminal suspect who, as shown by facts, may commit assault, riot, escape, or suicide, restraint devices may be used with the approval of the director of the detention house. In an emergency, such devices may be used first and then reported to the director of the detention house. When the above circumstances are eliminated, the restraint devices shall be removed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　看守人员和武警遇有下列情形之一，采取其他措施不能制止时，可以按照有关规定开枪射击：  
(一) 人犯越狱或者暴动的；  
(二) 人犯脱逃不听制止，或者在追捕中抗拒逮捕的；  
(三) 劫持人犯的；  
(四) 人犯持有管制刀具或者其他危险物，正在行凶或者破坏的；  
(五) 人犯暴力威胁看守人员、武警的生命安全的。  
需要开枪射击时，除遇到特别紧迫的情况外，应当先鸣枪警告，人犯有畏服表示，应当立即停止射击。开枪射击后，应当保护现场，并立即报告主管公安机关和人民检察院。

**Article 18** When a custody officer or Armed Police encounters any of the following circumstances and other measures are insufficient to stop the act, he may, in accordance with relevant provisions, open fire with a gun:  
(1) The criminal suspect escapes from prison or riots;  
(2) The criminal suspect escapes and refuses to stop, or resists arrest during pursuit;  
(3) A criminal suspect is hijacked;  
(4) The criminal suspect holds a controlled knife or other dangerous objects and is committing assault or sabotage;  
(5) The criminal suspect violently threatens the life safety of custody officers or Armed Police.  
When firing a gun is necessary, except in cases of special urgency, a warning shot shall first be fired; if the criminal suspect shows submission, the firing shall immediately cease. After firing, the scene shall be protected, and the competent public security organ and People's Procuratorate shall be immediately reported.

### 第四章　提讯、押解

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公安机关、国家安全机关、人民检察院、人民法院提讯人犯时，必须持有提讯证或者提票。提讯人员不得少于二人。  
不符合前款规定的，看守所应当拒绝提讯。

**Article 19** When a public security organ, state security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court summons a criminal suspect for interrogation, it must hold a summons certificate or a summons ticket. The number of personnel conducting the interrogation shall not be less than two.  
If the preceding paragraph is not complied with, the detention house shall refuse the interrogation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　提讯人员讯问人犯完毕，应当立即将人犯交给值班看守人员收押，并收回提讯证或者提票。

**Article 20** After finishing the interrogation of a criminal suspect, the interrogation personnel shall immediately hand over the criminal suspect to the duty custody officer for detention and retrieve the summons certificate or summons ticket.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　押解人员在押解人犯途中，必须严密看管，防止发生意外。对被押解的人犯，可以使用械具。  
押解女性人犯，应当有女工作人员负责途中的生活管理。

**Article 21** Escort personnel must keep strict watch over the criminal suspect during the escort to prevent accidents. Restraint devices may be used on the escorted criminal suspect.  
When escorting a female criminal suspect, female personnel shall be responsible for the management of living affairs during the journey.

### 第五章　生活、卫生

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　监室应当通风、采光，能够防潮、防暑、防寒。看守所对监房应当经常检查，及时维修，防止火灾和其他自然灾害。  
被羁押人犯的居住面积，应当不影响其日常生活。

**Article 22** The cells shall be ventilated, lighted, and capable of being moisture-proof, heat-proof, and cold-proof. The detention house shall frequently inspect the cells and timely repair them to prevent fire and other natural disasters.  
The living space of detained criminal suspects shall not affect their daily life.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人犯在羁押期间的伙食按规定标准供应，禁止克扣、挪用。  
对少数民族人犯和外国籍人犯，应当考虑到他们的民族风俗习惯，在生活上予以适当照顾。

**Article 23** The meals for criminal suspects during the period of custody shall be supplied according to the prescribed standard; withholding or misappropriation is prohibited.  
For criminal suspects of ethnic minorities and foreign nationality, their ethnic customs and habits shall be taken into consideration, and appropriate care shall be given in daily life.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人犯应当自备衣服、被褥。确实不能自备的，由看守所提供。

**Article 24** Criminal suspects shall provide their own clothes and bedding. If they are truly unable to do so, the detention house shall provide them.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人犯每日应当有必要的睡眠时间和一至两小时的室外活动。  
看守所应当建立人犯的防疫和清洁卫生制度。

**Article 25** Criminal suspects shall have necessary sleeping time and one to two hours of outdoor activities each day.  
A detention house shall establish systems for epidemic prevention and sanitation for criminal suspects.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　看守所应当配备必要的医疗器械和常用药品。人犯患病，应当给予及时治疗；需要到医院治疗的，当地医院应当负责治疗；病情严重的可以依法取保候审。

**Article 26** A detention house shall be equipped with necessary medical instruments and common medicines. If a criminal suspect falls ill, timely treatment shall be provided; if hospital treatment is required, the local hospital shall be responsible for the treatment; if the condition is serious, the criminal suspect may be released on bail pending investigation or trial according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人犯在羁押期间死亡的，应当立即报告人民检察院和办案机关，由法医或者医生作出死亡原因的鉴定，并通知死者家属。

**Article 27** If a criminal suspect dies during custody, the detention house shall immediately report to the People's Procuratorate and the case-handling organ, and a forensic doctor or physician shall make an expert evaluation of the cause of death, and the family of the deceased shall be notified.

### 第六章　会见、通信

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人犯在羁押期间，经办案机关同意，并经公安机关批准，可以与近亲属通信、会见。

**Article 28** During the period of custody, a criminal suspect may, with the consent of the case-handling organ and the approval of the public security organ, correspond with or visit his close relatives.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　人犯的近亲属病重或者死亡时，应当及时通知人犯。  
人犯的配偶、父母或者子女病危时，除案情重大的以外，经办案机关同意，并经公安机关批准，在严格监护的条件下，允许人犯回家探视。

**Article 29** When a close relative of a criminal suspect is seriously ill or dies, the criminal suspect shall be promptly notified.  
When the spouse, parents, or children of a criminal suspect are critically ill, except in cases of major importance, with the consent of the case-handling organ and the approval of the public security organ, and under strict supervision, the criminal suspect shall be allowed to return home to visit.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人犯近亲属给人犯的物品，须经看守人员检查。

**Article 30** Articles given to a criminal suspect by his close relatives must be inspected by custody personnel.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　看守所接受办案机关的委托，对人犯发收的信件可以进行检查。如果发现有碍侦查、起诉、审判的，可以扣留，并移送办案机关处理。

**Article 31** A detention house, upon entrustment by a case-handling organ, may inspect the incoming and outgoing letters of criminal suspects. If any content is found to hinder investigation, prosecution, or adjudication, the letters may be detained and transferred to the case-handling organ for disposition.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民检察院已经决定提起公诉的案件，被羁押的人犯在接到起诉书副本后，可以与本人委托的辩护人或者由人民法院指定的辩护人会见、通信。

**Article 32** In a case for which the People's Procuratorate has decided to initiate a public prosecution, the detained criminal suspect may, after receiving a copy of the bill of prosecution, meet and correspond with a defender he has entrusted or a defender appointed by the People's Court.

### 第七章　教育、奖惩

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　看守所应当对人犯进行法制、道德以及必要的形势和劳动教育。

**Article 33** A detention house shall provide criminal suspects with education on the legal system, morality, and necessary education on the situation and labor.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在保证安全和不影响刑事诉讼活动的前提下，看守所可以组织人犯进行适当的劳动。  
人犯的劳动收入和支出，要建立帐目，严格手续。

**Article 34** On the premise of ensuring safety and without affecting criminal action, a detention house may organize criminal suspects to perform appropriate labor.  
Accounts shall be established for the income and expenditure from the labor of criminal suspects, and strict procedures shall be followed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人犯在被羁押期间，遵守监规，表现良好的，应当予以表扬和鼓励；有立功表现的，应当报请办案机关依法从宽处理。

**Article 35** If a criminal suspect, during the period of custody, observes the detention center rules and behaves well, he shall be given praise and encouragement; if he performs meritorious service, the detention house shall report to the case-handling organ for lenient treatment according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　看守所对于违反监规的人犯，可予以警告或者训诫；情节严重，经教育不改的，可以责令具结悔过或者经看守所所长批准予以禁闭。

**Article 36** A detention house may give a warning or admonition to a criminal suspect who violates the detention center rules; if the circumstances are serious and he does not correct after education, he may be ordered to write a statement of repentance or be subjected to confinement with the approval of the director of the detention house.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人犯在羁押期间有犯罪行为的，看守所应当及时将情况通知办案机关依法处理。

**Article 37** If a criminal suspect commits a crime during custody, the detention house shall promptly notify the case-handling organ of the situation for handling according to law.

### 第八章　出所

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对于被判处死刑缓期二年执行、无期徒刑、有期徒刑、拘役或者管制的罪犯，看守所根据人民法院的执行通知书、判决书办理出所手续。

**Article 38** For a criminal offender sentenced to death penalty with a two-year suspension of execution, life imprisonment, fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention, or public surveillance, the detention house shall handle the release procedures based on the enforcement notice and judgment document of the People's Court.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　对于被依法释放的人，看守所根据人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关或者国家安全机关的释放通知文书，办理释放手续。  
释放被羁押人，发给释放证明书。

**Article 39** For a person released according to law, the detention house shall handle the release procedures based on the release notice document of the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, public security organ, or state security organ.  
When releasing a detained person, a release certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　对于被决定劳动教养的人和转送外地羁押的人犯，看守所根据有关主管机关的证明文件，办理出所手续。

**Article 40** For a person who has been decided to be subjected to reeducation through labor or a criminal suspect transferred for custody elsewhere, the detention house shall handle the release procedures based on the certificate document of the relevant competent authority.

### 第九章　检察监督

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　看守所应当教育工作人员严格执法，严守纪律，向人民检察院报告监管活动情况。

**Article 41** A detention house shall educate its staff to strictly enforce the law, strictly observe discipline, and report the situation of supervision activities to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　看守所对人民检察院提出的违法情况的纠正意见，应当认真研究，及时处理，并将处理结果告知人民检察院。

**Article 42** A detention house shall seriously study and timely handle the corrective opinions on illegal situations raised by the People's Procuratorate, and shall inform the People's Procuratorate of the results of the handling.

### 第十章　其他规定

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　看守所对人犯的法定羁押期限即将到期而案件又尚未审理终结的，应当及时通知办案机关迅速审结；超过法定羁押期限的，应当将情况报告人民检察院。

**Article 43** If the statutory period of custody of a criminal suspect is about to expire but the case has not yet been concluded, the detention house shall promptly notify the case-handling organ to conclude the case quickly; if the statutory period of custody has been exceeded, the detention house shall report the situation to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　对于人民检察院或者人民法院没有决定停止行使选举权利的被羁押人犯，准予参加县级以下人民代表大会代表的选举。

**Article 44** A detained criminal suspect for whom the People's Procuratorate or People's Court has not decided to suspend the exercise of the right to vote shall be permitted to participate in the election of deputies to the people's congresses at or below the county level.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　看守所在人犯羁押期间发现人犯中有错拘、错捕或者错判的，应当及时通知办案机关查证核实，依法处理。

**Article 45** If, during the detention of criminal suspects, a detention house discovers that a criminal suspect has been wrongfully detained, arrested, or sentenced, it shall promptly notify the case-handling organ to verify and handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对人犯的上诉书、申诉书，看守所应当及时转送，不得阻挠和扣押。  
人犯揭发、控告司法工作人员违法行为的材料，应当及时报请人民检察院处理。

**Article 46** A detention house shall promptly forward a criminal suspect's petition for appeal or petition for complaint and shall not obstruct or withhold it.  
Materials in which a criminal suspect exposes or accuses a judicial officer of illegal acts shall be promptly submitted to the People's Procuratorate for handling.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　看守所监管已决犯，执行有关对已决犯管理的法律规定。

**Article 47** When supervising and managing convicted criminals, a detention house shall implement the legal provisions on the management of convicted criminals.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　看守所所需修缮费和人犯给养费应当编报预算，按隶属关系由各级财政专项拨付。  
看守所的经费开支，单立帐户，专款专用。  
新建和迁建的看守所应当纳入城市建设规划，列入基本建设项目。

**Article 48** The funds needed for repairs and maintenance and the subsistence expenses of criminal suspects of a detention house shall be budgeted and allocated as special funds by the financial authorities at various levels according to the subordination relationship.  
The expenditure of a detention house shall be kept in a separate account and used only for the designated purpose.  
Newly built or relocated detention houses shall be incorporated into urban construction planning and included in capital construction projects.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例所称“以上”、“以下”，均包括本数、本级在内。

**Article 49** The terms "not less than" and "not more than" as used in these Regulations include the specified number or level itself.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例由公安部负责解释，实施办法由公安部制定。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall be subject to interpretation by the Ministry of Public Security, and the implementation measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　中国人民解放军根据军队看守所的具体情况，可以另行制定实施办法。

**Article 51** The Chinese People's Liberation Army may separately formulate implementing measures based on the specific circumstances of military detention centers.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行，一九五四年九月七日政务院公布的《中华人民共和国劳动改造条例》中有关看守所的规定即行废止。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation, and the provisions concerning detention centers in the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Reform through Labor promulgated by the Government Administration Council on September 7, 1954 shall be abolished simultaneously.
