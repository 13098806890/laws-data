# 中华人民共和国行政复议法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2007-05-29  
**生效日期**：2007-08-01

> (2007年5月23日国务院第177次常务会议通过　2007年5月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第499号公布　自2007年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了进一步发挥行政复议制度在解决行政争议、建设法治政府、构建社会主义和谐社会中的作用，根据《中华人民共和国行政复议法》(以下简称行政复议法)，制定本条例。 第二条　各级行政复议机关应当认真履行行政复议职责，领导并支持本机关负责法制工作的机构(以下简称行政复议机构)依法办理行政复议事项，并依照有关规定配备、充实、调剂专职行政复议人员，保证行政复议机构的办案能力与工作任务相适应。 第三条　行政复议机构除应当依照行政复议法第三条的规定履行职责外，还应当履行下列职责： (一)依照行政复议法第十八条的规定转送有关行政复议申请； (二)办理行政复议法第二十九条规定的行政赔偿等事项； (三)按照职责权限，督促行政复议申请的受理和行政复议决定的履行； (四)办理行政复议、行政应诉案件统计和重大行政复议决定备案事项； (五)办理或者组织办理未经行政复议直接提起行政诉讼的行政应诉事项； (六)研究行政复议工作中发现的问题，及时向有关机关提出改进建议，重大问题及时向行政复议机关报告。 第四条　专职行政复议人员应当具备与履行行政复议职责相适应的品行、专业知识和业务能力，并取得相应资格。具体办法由国务院法制机构会同国务院有关部门规定。 第二章　行政复议申请 第一节　申　请　人 第五条　依照行政复议法和本条例的规定申请行政复议的公民、法人或者其他组织为申请人。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了进一步发挥行政复议制度在解决行政争议、建设法治政府、构建社会主义和谐社会中的作用，根据《中华人民共和国行政复议法》(以下简称行政复议法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Administrative Reconsideration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Administrative Reconsideration Law) for the purpose of further giving play to the role of the administrative reconsideration system in resolving administrative disputes, building a law-based government, and constructing a socialist harmonious society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级行政复议机关应当认真履行行政复议职责，领导并支持本机关负责法制工作的机构(以下简称行政复议机构)依法办理行政复议事项，并依照有关规定配备、充实、调剂专职行政复议人员，保证行政复议机构的办案能力与工作任务相适应。

**Article 2** Administrative reconsideration authorities at all levels shall earnestly perform their administrative reconsideration duties, lead and support the institutions responsible for legal affairs of their own authorities (hereinafter referred to as administrative reconsideration institutions) to handle administrative reconsideration matters according to law, and shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, equip, supplement, and redeploy full-time administrative reconsideration personnel to ensure that the case-handling capacity of the administrative reconsideration institution corresponds to its work tasks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　行政复议机构除应当依照[行政复议法第三条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-3)的规定履行职责外，还应当履行下列职责：  
(一)依照[行政复议法第十八条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-18)的规定转送有关行政复议申请；  
(二)办理[行政复议法第二十九条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-29)规定的行政赔偿等事项；  
(三)按照职责权限，督促行政复议申请的受理和行政复议决定的履行；  
(四)办理行政复议、行政应诉案件统计和重大行政复议决定备案事项；  
(五)办理或者组织办理未经行政复议直接提起行政诉讼的行政应诉事项；  
(六)研究行政复议工作中发现的问题，及时向有关机关提出改进建议，重大问题及时向行政复议机关报告。

**Article 3** In addition to performing the duties in accordance with the provisions of Article 3 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, an administrative reconsideration institution shall also perform the following duties:  
(1) To forward relevant administrative reconsideration applications in accordance with the provisions of Article 18 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law;  
(2) To handle matters such as administrative compensation provided for in Article 29 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law;  
(3) To supervise, within the scope of its functions and powers, the acceptance of administrative reconsideration applications and the performance of administrative reconsideration decisions;  
(4) To compile statistics on administrative reconsideration and administrative response to lawsuits cases, and handle the filing of major administrative reconsideration decisions;  
(5) To handle or organize the handling of administrative response to lawsuits matters where an administrative action is directly initiated without administrative reconsideration;  
(6) To study problems discovered in the course of administrative reconsideration work, promptly propose improvement suggestions to relevant authorities, and report major issues to the administrative reconsideration authority in a timely manner.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　专职行政复议人员应当具备与履行行政复议职责相适应的品行、专业知识和业务能力，并取得相应资格。具体办法由国务院法制机构会同国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 4** Full-time administrative reconsideration personnel shall possess the moral character, professional knowledge, and operational capability commensurate with performing administrative reconsideration duties, and shall obtain corresponding qualifications. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the legal affairs institution of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第二章　行政复议申请

#### 第一节　申　请　人

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　依照行政复议法和本条例的规定申请行政复议的公民、法人或者其他组织为申请人。

**Article 5** Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that apply for administrative reconsideration in accordance with the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations shall be the applicants.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　合伙企业申请行政复议的，应当以核准登记的企业为申请人，由执行合伙事务的合伙人代表该企业参加行政复议；其他合伙组织申请行政复议的，由合伙人共同申请行政复议。  
前款规定以外的不具备法人资格的其他组织申请行政复议的，由该组织的主要负责人代表该组织参加行政复议；没有主要负责人的，由共同推选的其他成员代表该组织参加行政复议。

**Article 6** Where a partnership enterprise applies for administrative reconsideration, the enterprise approved and registered shall be the applicant, and the partner executing the partnership affairs shall represent the enterprise in participating in the administrative reconsideration; where other partnership organizations apply for administrative reconsideration, the partners shall jointly apply for administrative reconsideration.  
Where other organizations that do not have legal personality other than those specified in the preceding paragraph apply for administrative reconsideration, the principal person in charge of such organization shall represent the organization in participating in the administrative reconsideration; where there is no principal person in charge, other members jointly elected shall represent the organization in participating in the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　股份制企业的股东大会、股东代表大会、董事会认为行政机关作出的具体行政行为侵犯企业合法权益的，可以以企业的名义申请行政复议。

**Article 7** Where the shareholders' general meeting, the shareholders' representative meeting, or the board of directors of a joint-stock enterprise deems that a specific administrative act of an administrative organ infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the enterprise, it may apply for administrative reconsideration in the name of the enterprise.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　同一行政复议案件申请人超过5人的，推选1至5名代表参加行政复议。

**Article 8** Where there are more than five applicants in the same administrative reconsideration case, one to five representatives shall be elected to participate in the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政复议期间，行政复议机构认为申请人以外的公民、法人或者其他组织与被审查的具体行政行为有利害关系的，可以通知其作为第三人参加行政复议。  
行政复议期间，申请人以外的公民、法人或者其他组织与被审查的具体行政行为有利害关系的，可以向行政复议机构申请作为第三人参加行政复议。  
第三人不参加行政复议，不影响行政复议案件的审理。

**Article 9** During the period of administrative reconsideration, if the administrative reconsideration institution considers that any citizen, legal person, or other organization other than the applicant has an interest in the specific administrative act under review, it may notify such person to participate in the administrative reconsideration as a third party.  
During the period of administrative reconsideration, any citizen, legal person, or other organization other than the applicant that has an interest in the specific administrative act under review may apply to the administrative reconsideration institution to participate in the administrative reconsideration as a third party.  
The non-participation of a third party in the administrative reconsideration shall not affect the hearing of the administrative reconsideration case.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请人、第三人可以委托1至2名代理人参加行政复议。申请人、第三人委托代理人的，应当向行政复议机构提交授权委托书。授权委托书应当载明委托事项、权限和期限。公民在特殊情况下无法书面委托的，可以口头委托。口头委托的，行政复议机构应当核实并记录在卷。申请人、第三人解除或者变更委托的，应当书面报告行政复议机构。

**Article 10** Applicants and third parties may appoint one to two agents to participate in the administrative reconsideration. Where an applicant or a third party appoints an agent, a power of attorney shall be submitted to the administrative reconsideration institution. The power of attorney shall clearly state the matters authorized, the scope of authority, and the term of authority. If a citizen, under special circumstances, is unable to make the appointment in writing, oral appointment may be made. In the case of oral appointment, the administrative reconsideration institution shall verify and record it in the file. Where an applicant or a third party terminates or changes the appointment, it shall report to the administrative reconsideration institution in writing.

#### 第二节　被 申 请 人

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关的具体行政行为不服，依照行政复议法和本条例的规定申请行政复议的，作出该具体行政行为的行政机关为被申请人。

**Article 11** Where a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept a specific administrative act of an administrative organ and applies for administrative reconsideration in accordance with the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations, the administrative organ that made the specific administrative act shall be the respondent.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行政机关与法律、法规授权的组织以共同的名义作出具体行政行为的，行政机关和法律、法规授权的组织为共同被申请人。  
行政机关与其他组织以共同名义作出具体行政行为的，行政机关为被申请人。

**Article 12** Where an administrative organ and an organization authorized by a law or regulation make a specific administrative act in a joint name, the administrative organ and the organization authorized by the law or regulation shall be the joint respondents.  
Where an administrative organ makes a specific administrative act in a joint name with another organization, the administrative organ shall be the respondent.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下级行政机关依照法律、法规、规章规定，经上级行政机关批准作出具体行政行为的，批准机关为被申请人。

**Article 13** Where a lower-level administrative organ makes a specific administrative act upon approval by a higher-level administrative organ pursuant to laws, regulations, or rules, the approving organ shall be the respondent.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　行政机关设立的派出机构、内设机构或者其他组织，未经法律、法规授权，对外以自己名义作出具体行政行为的，该行政机关为被申请人。

**Article 14** Where a dispatched agency, an internal institution, or any other organization established by an administrative organ, without authorization by any law or regulation, makes a specific administrative act in its own name externally, the administrative organ that established it shall be the respondent.

#### 第三节　行政复议申请期限

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　[行政复议法第九条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-9)第一款规定的行政复议申请期限的计算，依照下列规定办理：  
(一)当场作出具体行政行为的，自具体行政行为作出之日起计算；  
(二)载明具体行政行为的法律文书直接送达的，自受送达人签收之日起计算；  
(三)载明具体行政行为的法律文书邮寄送达的，自受送达人在邮件签收单上签收之日起计算；没有邮件签收单的，自受送达人在送达回执上签名之日起计算；  
(四)具体行政行为依法通过公告形式告知受送达人的，自公告规定的期限届满之日起计算；  
(五)行政机关作出具体行政行为时未告知公民、法人或者其他组织，事后补充告知的，自该公民、法人或者其他组织收到行政机关补充告知的通知之日起计算；  
(六)被申请人能够证明公民、法人或者其他组织知道具体行政行为的，自证据材料证明其知道具体行政行为之日起计算。  
行政机关作出具体行政行为，依法应当向有关公民、法人或者其他组织送达法律文书而未送达的，视为该公民、法人或者其他组织不知道该具体行政行为。

**Article 15** The calculation of the time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration as provided in the first paragraph of Article 9 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If the specific administrative act is made on the spot, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the specific administrative act is made;  
(2) If the legal document containing the specific administrative act is served directly, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the recipient signs for it;  
(3) If the legal document containing the specific administrative act is served by mail, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the recipient signs for it on the mail receipt; if there is no mail receipt, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the recipient signs the acknowledgment of service;  
(4) If the specific administrative act is notified to the recipient through public announcement according to law, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the period specified in the announcement expires;  
(5) If the administrative organ does not notify the citizen, legal person, or other organization at the time of making the specific administrative act, but supplements the notification afterwards, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the citizen, legal person, or other organization receives the supplementary notification from the administrative organ;  
(6) If the respondent can prove that the citizen, legal person, or other organization has knowledge of the specific administrative act, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the evidence establishes such knowledge.  
If the administrative organ, when making a specific administrative act, fails to serve the legal document to the relevant citizen, legal person, or other organization as required by law, the citizen, legal person, or other organization shall be deemed not to have knowledge of the specific administrative act.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公民、法人或者其他组织依照[行政复议法第六条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-6)第(八)项、第(九)项、第(十)项的规定申请行政机关履行法定职责，行政机关未履行的，行政复议申请期限依照下列规定计算：  
(一)有履行期限规定的，自履行期限届满之日起计算；  
(二)没有履行期限规定的，自行政机关收到申请满60日起计算。  
公民、法人或者其他组织在紧急情况下请求行政机关履行保护人身权、财产权的法定职责，行政机关不履行的，行政复议申请期限不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 16** Where a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization applies to an administrative organ to perform its statutory duties in accordance with subparagraphs (8), (9), and (10) of Article 6 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, and the administrative organ fails to perform them, the time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration shall be calculated in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If there is a specified time limit for performance, the time limit shall be calculated from the date on which the time limit for performance expires;  
(2) If there is no specified time limit for performance, the time limit shall be calculated from the date 60 days after the administrative organ receives the application.  
If a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization, under emergency circumstances, requests an administrative organ to perform its statutory duties of protecting personal rights or property rights, and the administrative organ fails to perform them, the time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration shall not be subject to the restrictions set out in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　行政机关作出的具体行政行为对公民、法人或者其他组织的权利、义务可能产生不利影响的，应当告知其申请行政复议的权利、行政复议机关和行政复议申请期限。

**Article 17** Where a specific administrative act made by an administrative organ may have an adverse effect on the rights and obligations of a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization, the administrative organ shall inform the party of its right to apply for administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration authority, and the time limit for applying for administrative reconsideration.

#### 第四节　行政复议申请的提出

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请人书面申请行政复议的，可以采取当面递交、邮寄或者传真等方式提出行政复议申请。  
有条件的行政复议机构可以接受以电子邮件形式提出的行政复议申请。

**Article 18** Where an applicant applies for administrative reconsideration in writing, the application may be submitted by means of hand-delivery, mail, or fax.  
An administrative reconsideration institution that has the necessary conditions may accept an application for administrative reconsideration submitted by electronic mail.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人书面申请行政复议的，应当在行政复议申请书中载明下列事项：  
(一)申请人的基本情况，包括：公民的姓名、性别、年龄、身份证号码、工作单位、住所、邮政编码；法人或者其他组织的名称、住所、邮政编码和法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务；  
(二)被申请人的名称；  
(三)行政复议请求、申请行政复议的主要事实和理由；  
(四)申请人的签名或者盖章；  
(五)申请行政复议的日期。

**Article 19** Where an applicant applies for administrative reconsideration in writing, the written application for administrative reconsideration shall clearly state the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the applicant, including: for a citizen, his/her name, sex, age, identity card number, work unit, domicile, and postal code; for a legal person or other organization, its name, domicile, postal code, and the name and position of its legal representative or principal person in charge;  
(2) The name of the respondent;  
(3) The request for administrative reconsideration, and the main facts and grounds for applying for administrative reconsideration;  
(4) The signature or seal of the applicant; and  
(5) The date on which the application for administrative reconsideration is made.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请人口头申请行政复议的，行政复议机构应当依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定的事项，当场制作行政复议申请笔录交申请人核对或者向申请人宣读，并由申请人签字确认。

**Article 20** Where an applicant applies for administrative reconsideration orally, the administrative reconsideration institution shall, in accordance with the matters specified in Article 19 of these Regulations, prepare a written record of the administrative reconsideration application on the spot and hand it over to the applicant for verification or read it out to the applicant, and the applicant shall sign to confirm it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列情形之一的，申请人应当提供证明材料：  
(一)认为被申请人不履行法定职责的，提供曾经要求被申请人履行法定职责而被申请人未履行的证明材料；  
(二)申请行政复议时一并提出行政赔偿请求的，提供受具体行政行为侵害而造成损害的证明材料；  
(三)法律、法规规定需要申请人提供证据材料的其他情形。

**Article 21** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the applicant shall provide evidentiary materials:  
(1) Where the applicant claims that the respondent fails to perform its statutory duties, the applicant shall provide evidentiary materials that it once requested the respondent to perform its statutory duties and the respondent failed to perform them;  
(2) Where the applicant submits a claim for administrative compensation concurrently with the application for administrative reconsideration, the applicant shall provide evidentiary materials on the damage caused by the infringement of the specific administrative act; and  
(3) Other circumstances where laws or regulations require the applicant to provide evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请人提出行政复议申请时错列被申请人的，行政复议机构应当告知申请人变更被申请人。

**Article 22** Where the applicant incorrectly names the respondent when submitting an application for administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration institution shall inform the applicant to change the respondent.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　申请人对两个以上国务院部门共同作出的具体行政行为不服的，依照[行政复议法第十四条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-14)的规定，可以向其中任何一个国务院部门提出行政复议申请，由作出具体行政行为的国务院部门共同作出行政复议决定。

**Article 23** Where an applicant refuses to accept a specific administrative act jointly made by two or more departments of the State Council, the applicant may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, submit an application for administrative reconsideration to any one of such departments of the State Council, and the departments of the State Council that made the specific administrative act shall jointly make an administrative reconsideration decision.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请人对经国务院批准实行省以下垂直领导的部门作出的具体行政行为不服的，可以选择向该部门的本级人民政府或者上一级主管部门申请行政复议；省、自治区、直辖市另有规定的，依照省、自治区、直辖市的规定办理。

**Article 24** Where an applicant refuses to accept a specific administrative act made by a department that, with the approval of the State Council, is under the vertical leadership below the provincial level, the applicant may choose to apply for administrative reconsideration to the people's government at the same level as the department or to the competent department at the next higher level; if there are other provisions in the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　申请人依照[行政复议法第三十条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-30)第二款的规定申请行政复议的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出行政复议申请。

**Article 25** Where an applicant applies for administrative reconsideration in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 30 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the application for administrative reconsideration shall be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　依照[行政复议法第七条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-7)的规定，申请人认为具体行政行为所依据的规定不合法的，可以在对具体行政行为申请行政复议的同时一并提出对该规定的审查申请；申请人在对具体行政行为提出行政复议申请时尚不知道该具体行政行为所依据的规定的，可以在行政复议机关作出行政复议决定前向行政复议机关提出对该规定的审查申请。

**Article 26** Where, in accordance with the provisions of Article 7 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, an applicant considers that the provisions on which a specific administrative act is based are illegal, the applicant may, at the same time as applying for administrative reconsideration of the specific administrative act, submit an application for review of such provisions; if the applicant does not know the provisions on which the specific administrative act is based at the time of submitting the application for administrative reconsideration of the specific administrative act, the applicant may submit an application for review of such provisions to the administrative reconsideration authority before it makes an administrative reconsideration decision.

### 第三章　行政复议受理

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益提出行政复议申请，除不符合行政复议法和本条例规定的申请条件的，行政复议机关必须受理。

**Article 27** Where a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization files an application for administrative reconsideration on the grounds that a specific administrative act of an administrative organ infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, the administrative reconsideration authority must accept the application, unless it does not meet the conditions for application as provided by the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　行政复议申请符合下列规定的，应当予以受理：  
(一)有明确的申请人和符合规定的被申请人；  
(二)申请人与具体行政行为有利害关系；  
(三)有具体的行政复议请求和理由；  
(四)在法定申请期限内提出；  
(五)属于行政复议法规定的行政复议范围；  
(六)属于收到行政复议申请的行政复议机构的职责范围；  
(七)其他行政复议机关尚未受理同一行政复议申请，人民法院尚未受理同一主体就同一事实提起的行政诉讼。

**Article 28** An application for administrative reconsideration shall be accepted if it meets the following requirements:  
(1) There is a clear applicant and a respondent that meets the requirements;  
(2) The applicant has an interest in the specific administrative act;  
(3) There are specific requests for administrative reconsideration and grounds therefor;  
(4) The application is made within the statutory time limit;  
(5) The matter falls within the scope of administrative reconsideration as provided by the Administrative Reconsideration Law;  
(6) The matter falls within the responsibilities of the administrative reconsideration institution that received the application; and  
(7) No other administrative reconsideration authority has accepted the same application for administrative reconsideration, and no people's court has accepted an administrative action initiated by the same subject on the same facts.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行政复议申请材料不齐全或者表述不清楚的，行政复议机构可以自收到该行政复议申请之日起5日内书面通知申请人补正。补正通知应当载明需要补正的事项和合理的补正期限。无正当理由逾期不补正的，视为申请人放弃行政复议申请。补正申请材料所用时间不计入行政复议审理期限。

**Article 29** Where the materials submitted with an application for administrative reconsideration are incomplete or the statements are unclear, the administrative reconsideration institution may, within five days from the date of receiving the application, notify the applicant in writing to make corrections. The notice of correction shall clearly state the matters to be corrected and a reasonable time limit for making corrections. If the applicant fails to make corrections within the time limit without justifiable reasons, the applicant shall be deemed to have abandoned the application for administrative reconsideration. The time spent in correcting the application materials shall not be counted in the time limit for hearing the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　申请人就同一事项向两个或者两个以上有权受理的行政机关申请行政复议的，由最先收到行政复议申请的行政机关受理；同时收到行政复议申请的，由收到行政复议申请的行政机关在10日内协商确定；协商不成的，由其共同上一级行政机关在10日内指定受理机关。协商确定或者指定受理机关所用时间不计入行政复议审理期限。

**Article 30** Where an applicant applies for administrative reconsideration on the same matter to two or more administrative organs that have the authority to accept the application, the administrative organ that first receives the application shall accept it; if the applications are received at the same time, the administrative organs that received the applications shall, within 10 days, consult and determine which one shall accept the case; if they fail to reach an agreement through consultation, the common higher-level administrative organ shall, within 10 days, designate the accepting organ. The time spent in consultation or designation shall not be counted in the time limit for hearing the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　依照[行政复议法第二十条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-20)的规定，上级行政机关认为行政复议机关不予受理行政复议申请的理由不成立的，可以先行督促其受理；经督促仍不受理的，应当责令其限期受理，必要时也可以直接受理；认为行政复议申请不符合法定受理条件的，应当告知申请人。

**Article 31** Where, in accordance with the provisions of Article 20 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, a higher-level administrative organ considers that the reasons given by an administrative reconsideration authority for not accepting an application for administrative reconsideration are not valid, the higher-level administrative organ may first urge the administrative reconsideration authority to accept the application; if the authority still does not accept it after being urged, the higher-level administrative organ shall order it to accept the application within a time limit, and may also directly accept the application if necessary; if the higher-level administrative organ considers that the application does not meet the statutory conditions for acceptance, it shall notify the applicant accordingly.

### 第四章　行政复议决定

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　行政复议机构审理行政复议案件，应当由2名以上行政复议人员参加。

**Article 32** The hearing of an administrative reconsideration case by an administrative reconsideration institution shall be conducted by two or more administrative reconsideration personnel.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　行政复议机构认为必要时，可以实地调查核实证据；对重大、复杂的案件，申请人提出要求或者行政复议机构认为必要时，可以采取听证的方式审理。

**Article 33** Where an administrative reconsideration institution considers it necessary, it may conduct on-site investigation to verify evidence; for significant or complex cases, if the applicant requests or the administrative reconsideration institution considers it necessary, the case may be heard by means of a hearing.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　行政复议人员向有关组织和人员调查取证时，可以查阅、复制、调取有关文件和资料，向有关人员进行询问。  
调查取证时，行政复议人员不得少于2人，并应当向当事人或者有关人员出示证件。被调查单位和人员应当配合行政复议人员的工作，不得拒绝或者阻挠。  
需要现场勘验的，现场勘验所用时间不计入行政复议审理期限。

**Article 34** When investigating and collecting evidence from relevant organizations and individuals, administrative reconsideration personnel may consult, copy, and requisition relevant documents and materials, and may inquire of relevant individuals.  
When investigating and collecting evidence, there shall be no fewer than two administrative reconsideration personnel, and they shall present their credentials to the parties or relevant individuals. The units and individuals under investigation shall cooperate with the work of the administrative reconsideration personnel and shall not refuse or obstruct it.  
If an on-site inspection is necessary, the time spent on the on-site inspection shall not be counted in the time limit for hearing the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　行政复议机关应当为申请人、第三人查阅有关材料提供必要条件。

**Article 35** The administrative reconsideration authority shall provide necessary conditions for applicants and third parties to consult relevant materials.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　依照[行政复议法第十四条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-14)的规定申请原级行政复议的案件，由原承办具体行政行为有关事项的部门或者机构提出书面答复，并提交作出具体行政行为的证据、依据和其他有关材料。

**Article 36** For a case where an application for administrative reconsideration is made to the original authority in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the department or institution that originally handled the matters related to the specific administrative act shall submit a written reply and present the evidence, basis, and other relevant materials for making the specific administrative act.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　行政复议期间涉及专门事项需要鉴定的，当事人可以自行委托鉴定机构进行鉴定，也可以申请行政复议机构委托鉴定机构进行鉴定。鉴定费用由当事人承担。鉴定所用时间不计入行政复议审理期限。

**Article 37** Where, during the period of administrative reconsideration, expert evaluation is needed for a specialized matter, the parties may entrust an appraisal institution to conduct the evaluation on their own, or may apply to the administrative reconsideration institution to entrust an appraisal institution to conduct the evaluation. The appraisal costs shall be borne by the parties. The time spent on the appraisal shall not be counted in the time limit for hearing the administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　申请人在行政复议决定作出前自愿撤回行政复议申请的，经行政复议机构同意，可以撤回。  
申请人撤回行政复议申请的，不得再以同一事实和理由提出行政复议申请。但是，申请人能够证明撤回行政复议申请违背其真实意思表示的除外。

**Article 38** Where an applicant voluntarily withdraws the application for administrative reconsideration before an administrative reconsideration decision is made, the withdrawal may be allowed upon the consent of the administrative reconsideration institution.  
An applicant who withdraws the application for administrative reconsideration shall not file another application for administrative reconsideration on the same facts and grounds, unless the applicant can prove that the withdrawal of the application for administrative reconsideration was contrary to its true intention.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　行政复议期间被申请人改变原具体行政行为的，不影响行政复议案件的审理。但是，申请人依法撤回行政复议申请的除外。

**Article 39** Where, during the period of administrative reconsideration, the respondent changes the original specific administrative act, this shall not affect the hearing of the administrative reconsideration case, except where the applicant withdraws the application for administrative reconsideration according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关行使法律、法规规定的自由裁量权作出的具体行政行为不服申请行政复议，申请人与被申请人在行政复议决定作出前自愿达成和解的，应当向行政复议机构提交书面和解协议；和解内容不损害社会公共利益和他人合法权益的，行政复议机构应当准许。

**Article 40** Where a citizen, a legal person, or any other organization, refusing to accept a specific administrative act made by an administrative organ in exercising its discretionary power provided by laws or regulations, applies for administrative reconsideration, and the applicant and the respondent voluntarily reach a settlement before the administrative reconsideration decision is made, they shall submit a written settlement agreement to the administrative reconsideration institution; if the content of the settlement does not harm the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others, the administrative reconsideration institution shall permit it.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　行政复议期间有下列情形之一，影响行政复议案件审理的，行政复议中止：  
(一)作为申请人的自然人死亡，其近亲属尚未确定是否参加行政复议的；  
(二)作为申请人的自然人丧失参加行政复议的能力，尚未确定法定代理人参加行政复议的；  
(三)作为申请人的法人或者其他组织终止，尚未确定权利义务承受人的；  
(四)作为申请人的自然人下落不明或者被宣告失踪的；  
(五)申请人、被申请人因不可抗力，不能参加行政复议的；  
(六)案件涉及法律适用问题，需要有权机关作出解释或者确认的；  
(七)案件审理需要以其他案件的审理结果为依据，而其他案件尚未审结的；  
(八)其他需要中止行政复议的情形。  
行政复议中止的原因消除后，应当及时恢复行政复议案件的审理。  
行政复议机构中止、恢复行政复议案件的审理，应当告知有关当事人。

**Article 41** During the period of administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration shall be suspended if any of the following circumstances occurs and affects the hearing of the administrative reconsideration case:  
(1) The applicant is a natural person who dies, and his/her near relatives have not yet decided whether to participate in the administrative reconsideration;  
(2) The applicant is a natural person who loses the capacity to participate in the administrative reconsideration, and a statutory agent has not yet been determined to participate in the administrative reconsideration;  
(3) The applicant is a legal person or other organization that is terminated, and the successor to its rights and obligations has not yet been determined;  
(4) The applicant is a natural person who is missing or has been declared missing;  
(5) The applicant or the respondent is unable to participate in the administrative reconsideration due to force majeure;  
(6) The case involves issues of law application that require interpretation or confirmation by an authority;  
(7) The hearing of the case needs to be based on the result of the hearing of another case, which has not yet been concluded; or  
(8) Other circumstances requiring suspension of the administrative reconsideration.  
After the cause of suspension of the administrative reconsideration is eliminated, the hearing of the administrative reconsideration case shall be resumed promptly.  
The administrative reconsideration institution shall notify the relevant parties when suspending or resuming the hearing of an administrative reconsideration case.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　行政复议期间有下列情形之一的，行政复议终止：  
(一)申请人要求撤回行政复议申请，行政复议机构准予撤回的；  
(二)作为申请人的自然人死亡，没有近亲属或者其近亲属放弃行政复议权利的；  
(三)作为申请人的法人或者其他组织终止，其权利义务的承受人放弃行政复议权利的；  
(四)申请人与被申请人依照[本条例第四十条](#art-40)的规定，经行政复议机构准许达成和解的；  
(五)申请人对行政拘留或者限制人身自由的行政强制措施不服申请行政复议后，因申请人同一违法行为涉嫌犯罪，该行政拘留或者限制人身自由的行政强制措施变更为刑事拘留的。  
依照[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)第一款第(一)项、第(二)项、第(三)项规定中止行政复议，满60日行政复议中止的原因仍未消除的，行政复议终止。

**Article 42** During the period of administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration shall be terminated if any of the following circumstances occurs:  
(1) The applicant requests withdrawal of the application for administrative reconsideration, and the administrative reconsideration institution approves the withdrawal;  
(2) The applicant is a natural person who dies, and there is no near relative or the near relative waives the right to administrative reconsideration;  
(3) The applicant is a legal person or other organization that is terminated, and the successor to its rights and obligations waives the right to administrative reconsideration;  
(4) The applicant and the respondent reach a settlement in accordance with Article 40 of these Regulations, with the approval of the administrative reconsideration institution;  
(5) After the applicant applies for administrative reconsideration because he/she refuses to accept an administrative detention or an administrative compulsory measure restricting personal freedom, the administrative detention or administrative compulsory measure restricting personal freedom is changed to criminal detention due to the same illegal act of the applicant being suspected of a crime.  
If the administrative reconsideration is suspended in accordance with subparagraphs (1), (2), and (3) of the first paragraph of Article 41 of these Regulations, and the cause of suspension has not been eliminated after 60 days, the administrative reconsideration shall be terminated.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　依照[行政复议法第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-28)第一款第(一)项规定，具体行政行为认定事实清楚，证据确凿，适用依据正确，程序合法，内容适当的，行政复议机关应当决定维持。

**Article 43** Where, in accordance with subparagraph (1) of the first paragraph of Article 28 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the specific administrative act has clear fact determination, conclusive evidence, correct application of basis, lawful procedures, and appropriate content, the administrative reconsideration authority shall decide to uphold it.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　依照[行政复议法第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-28)第一款第(二)项规定，被申请人不履行法定职责的，行政复议机关应当决定其在一定期限内履行法定职责。

**Article 44** Where, in accordance with subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph of Article 28 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the respondent fails to perform its statutory duties, the administrative reconsideration authority shall decide that the respondent shall perform its statutory duties within a certain time limit.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　具体行政行为有[行政复议法第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-28)第一款第(三)项规定情形之一的，行政复议机关应当决定撤销、变更该具体行政行为或者确认该具体行政行为违法；决定撤销该具体行政行为或者确认该具体行政行为违法的，可以责令被申请人在一定期限内重新作出具体行政行为。

**Article 45** Where a specific administrative act falls under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraph (3) of the first paragraph of Article 28 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the administrative reconsideration authority shall decide to revoke or change the specific administrative act, or confirm that the specific administrative act is illegal; if it decides to revoke the specific administrative act or confirm that it is illegal, it may order the respondent to make a new specific administrative act within a certain time limit.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　被申请人未依照[行政复议法第二十三条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-23)的规定提出书面答复、提交当初作出具体行政行为的证据、依据和其他有关材料的，视为该具体行政行为没有证据、依据，行政复议机关应当决定撤销该具体行政行为。

**Article 46** Where the respondent fails to submit a written reply and present the evidence, basis, and other relevant materials for making the original specific administrative act in accordance with the provisions of Article 23 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the specific administrative act shall be deemed to have no evidence or basis, and the administrative reconsideration authority shall decide to revoke the specific administrative act.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　具体行政行为有下列情形之一，行政复议机关可以决定变更：  
(一)认定事实清楚，证据确凿，程序合法，但是明显不当或者适用依据错误的；  
(二)认定事实不清，证据不足，但是经行政复议机关审理查明事实清楚，证据确凿的。

**Article 47** An administrative reconsideration authority may decide to change a specific administrative act under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The facts are clearly determined, the evidence is conclusive, and the procedures are lawful, but the act is obviously inappropriate or the basis applied is wrong; or  
(2) The facts are unclear and the evidence is insufficient, but upon hearing by the administrative reconsideration authority, the facts become clear and the evidence is conclusive.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有下列情形之一的，行政复议机关应当决定驳回行政复议申请：  
(一)申请人认为行政机关不履行法定职责申请行政复议，行政复议机关受理后发现该行政机关没有相应法定职责或者在受理前已经履行法定职责的；  
(二)受理行政复议申请后，发现该行政复议申请不符合行政复议法和本条例规定的受理条件的。  
上级行政机关认为行政复议机关驳回行政复议申请的理由不成立的，应当责令其恢复审理。

**Article 48** An administrative reconsideration authority shall decide to reject an application for administrative reconsideration under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The applicant applies for administrative reconsideration on the ground that an administrative organ fails to perform its statutory duties, and after acceptance, the administrative reconsideration authority discovers that the administrative organ has no corresponding statutory duties or has performed its statutory duties before acceptance; or  
(2) After accepting the application for administrative reconsideration, it is discovered that the application does not meet the conditions for acceptance as provided by the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations.  
If a higher-level administrative organ considers that the reasons given by the administrative reconsideration authority for rejecting the application for administrative reconsideration are not valid, it shall order the authority to resume the hearing.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　行政复议机关依照[行政复议法第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-28)的规定责令被申请人重新作出具体行政行为的，被申请人应当在法律、法规、规章规定的期限内重新作出具体行政行为；法律、法规、规章未规定期限的，重新作出具体行政行为的期限为60日。  
公民、法人或者其他组织对被申请人重新作出的具体行政行为不服，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 49** Where an administrative reconsideration authority orders the respondent to make a new specific administrative act in accordance with the provisions of Article 28 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, the respondent shall make the new specific administrative act within the time limit prescribed by laws, regulations, or rules; if no time limit is prescribed by laws, regulations, or rules, the time limit for making the new specific administrative act shall be 60 days.  
A citizen, a legal person, or any other organization that refuses to accept the new specific administrative act made by the respondent may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　有下列情形之一的，行政复议机关可以按照自愿、合法的原则进行调解：  
(一)公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关行使法律、法规规定的自由裁量权作出的具体行政行为不服申请行政复议的；  
(二)当事人之间的行政赔偿或者行政补偿纠纷。  
当事人经调解达成协议的，行政复议机关应当制作行政复议调解书。调解书应当载明行政复议请求、事实、理由和调解结果，并加盖行政复议机关印章。行政复议调解书经双方当事人签字，即具有法律效力。  
调解未达成协议或者调解书生效前一方反悔的，行政复议机关应当及时作出行政复议决定。

**Article 50** An administrative reconsideration authority may conduct mediation in accordance with the principles of voluntariness and legality under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) A citizen, a legal person, or any other organization, refusing to accept a specific administrative act made by an administrative organ in exercising its discretionary power provided by laws or regulations, applies for administrative reconsideration; or  
(2) There is a dispute over administrative compensation or administrative indemnity between the parties.  
Where the parties reach an agreement through mediation, the administrative reconsideration authority shall prepare a mediation agreement for administrative reconsideration. The mediation agreement shall state the requests for administrative reconsideration, the facts, the grounds, and the mediation result, and shall bear the seal of the administrative reconsideration authority. The mediation agreement for administrative reconsideration shall have legal effect upon being signed by both parties.  
Where no agreement is reached through mediation or one party retracts before the mediation agreement takes effect, the administrative reconsideration authority shall make an administrative reconsideration decision promptly.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　行政复议机关在申请人的行政复议请求范围内，不得作出对申请人更为不利的行政复议决定。

**Article 51** The administrative reconsideration authority shall not make an administrative reconsideration decision that is more unfavorable to the applicant within the scope of the applicant's request for administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　第三人逾期不起诉又不履行行政复议决定的，依照[行政复议法第三十三条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-33)的规定处理。

**Article 52** Where a third party neither institutes a prosecution within the time limit nor performs the administrative reconsideration decision, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law.

### 第五章　行政复议指导和监督

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　行政复议机关应当加强对行政复议工作的领导。  
行政复议机构在本级行政复议机关的领导下，按照职责权限对行政复议工作进行督促、指导。

**Article 53** The administrative reconsideration authority shall strengthen its leadership over the administrative reconsideration work.  
The administrative reconsideration body shall, under the leadership of the administrative reconsideration authority at the same level, supervise and guide the administrative reconsideration work in accordance with its functions and powers.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上各级人民政府应当加强对所属工作部门和下级人民政府履行行政复议职责的监督。  
行政复议机关应当加强对其行政复议机构履行行政复议职责的监督。

**Article 54** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the performance of administrative reconsideration functions by their subordinate departments and by the people's governments at lower levels.  
The administrative reconsideration authority shall strengthen supervision over the performance of administrative reconsideration functions by its administrative reconsideration body.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当建立健全行政复议工作责任制，将行政复议工作纳入本级政府目标责任制。

**Article 55** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the responsibility system for administrative reconsideration work, and incorporate the administrative reconsideration work into the target responsibility system of the government at the same level.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当按照职责权限，通过定期组织检查、抽查等方式，对所属工作部门和下级人民政府行政复议工作进行检查，并及时向有关方面反馈检查结果。

**Article 56** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and powers, inspect the administrative reconsideration work of their subordinate departments and of the people's governments at lower levels by means of organizing regular inspections and spot checks, and shall promptly provide feedback on the inspection results to the relevant parties.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　行政复议期间行政复议机关发现被申请人或者其他下级行政机关的相关行政行为违法或者需要做好善后工作的，可以制作行政复议意见书。有关机关应当自收到行政复议意见书之日起60日内将纠正相关行政违法行为或者做好善后工作的情况通报行政复议机构。  
行政复议期间行政复议机构发现法律、法规、规章实施中带有普遍性的问题，可以制作行政复议建议书，向有关机关提出完善制度和改进行政执法的建议。

**Article 57** If, during the administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration authority finds that the relevant administrative act of the respondent or other subordinate administrative organ is illegal or requires follow-up work, it may issue an administrative reconsideration opinion letter. The relevant organ shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the administrative reconsideration opinion letter, notify the administrative reconsideration body of the situation of correcting the relevant illegal administrative act or completing the follow-up work.  
If, during the administrative reconsideration, the administrative reconsideration body finds any common problems in the implementation of laws, regulations, or rules, it may issue an administrative reconsideration proposal letter to the relevant organ, proposing suggestions for improving the system and enhancing administrative law enforcement.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上各级人民政府行政复议机构应当定期向本级人民政府提交行政复议工作状况分析报告。

**Article 58** The administrative reconsideration body of a people's government at or above the county level shall periodically submit to the people's government at the same level an analysis report on the status of administrative reconsideration work.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　下级行政复议机关应当及时将重大行政复议决定报上级行政复议机关备案。

**Article 59** The administrative reconsideration authority at a lower level shall promptly report major administrative reconsideration decisions to the administrative reconsideration authority at a higher level for record.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　各级行政复议机构应当定期组织对行政复议人员进行业务培训，提高行政复议人员的专业素质。

**Article 60** Administrative reconsideration bodies at all levels shall periodically organize professional training for administrative reconsideration personnel to improve their professional competence.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　各级行政复议机关应当定期总结行政复议工作，对在行政复议工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，依照有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 61** Administrative reconsideration authorities at all levels shall periodically summarize the administrative reconsideration work, and give commendation and rewards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in administrative reconsideration work in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　被申请人在规定期限内未按照行政复议决定的要求重新作出具体行政行为，或者违反规定重新作出具体行政行为的，依照[行政复议法第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-37)的规定追究法律责任。

**Article 62** If the respondent fails to re-make a specific administrative act within the prescribed time limit as required by the administrative reconsideration decision, or re-makes a specific administrative act in violation of the provisions, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 37 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　拒绝或者阻挠行政复议人员调查取证、查阅、复制、调取有关文件和资料的，对有关责任人员依法给予处分或者治安处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 63** Anyone who refuses or obstructs an administrative reconsideration personnel in investigating and collecting evidence, consulting, copying, or retrieving relevant documents and materials shall, if the act constitutes a crime, be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the responsible person shall be given a sanction or a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　行政复议机关或者行政复议机构不履行行政复议法和本条例规定的行政复议职责，经有权监督的行政机关督促仍不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予警告、记过、记大过的处分；造成严重后果的，依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分。

**Article 64** If an administrative reconsideration authority or an administrative reconsideration body fails to perform its administrative reconsideration duties as provided for in the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations, and fails to make corrections after being urged by the administrative organ with supervisory authority, the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given warnings, demerits, or major demerits according to law; if serious consequences result, they shall be given demotions, removals from office, or expulsion according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　行政机关及其工作人员违反行政复议法和本条例规定的，行政复议机构可以向人事、监察部门提出对有关责任人员的处分建议，也可以将有关人员违法的事实材料直接转送人事、监察部门处理；接受转送的人事、监察部门应当依法处理，并将处理结果通报转送的行政复议机构。

**Article 65** If an administrative organ or its staff member violates the provisions of the Administrative Reconsideration Law and these Regulations, the administrative reconsideration body may submit a proposal for sanction against the responsible person to the personnel or supervisory department, or may directly transmit the factual materials concerning the illegal act of the relevant person to the personnel or supervisory department for handling; the personnel or supervisory department receiving such transmission shall handle the matter according to law and notify the transmitting administrative reconsideration body of the handling result.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本条例自2007年8月1日起施行。

**Article 66** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2007.
