# 治安保卫委员会暂行组织条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1952-08-11

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为发动群众，协助人民政府防奸、防谍、防盗、防火，肃清反革命活动，以保卫国家和公众治安，特规定全国各城市于镇压反革命运动开展后、农村于土地改革完成后，普遍建立治安保卫委员会。

**Article 1** For the purpose of mobilizing the masses to assist the people's government in preventing espionage, counter-espionage, theft, and fire, and in eliminating counter-revolutionary activities, so as to safeguard the state and public security, it is hereby prescribed that public security defense committees shall be universally established in all cities after the launch of the campaign to suppress counter-revolutionaries and in rural areas after the completion of land reform.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　治安保卫委员会是群众性的治安保卫组织，在基层政府和公安保卫机关领导下负责进行工作。

**Article 2** The public security defense committee is a mass-type public security defense organization, which shall be responsible for carrying out its work under the leadership of the grassroots government and the public security defense organs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　治安保卫委员会的建立，城市一般以机关、工厂、企业、学校、街道为单位，农村以行政村为单位，其委员名额，应视各单位人数多寡、情况繁简，由三人至十一人组成之，设主任一人并得设副主任一人至二人。  
各地于治安保卫委员会建立后，视情况需要，经市、县公安局批准，得建立治安保卫小组，由群众推选积极分子三人至五人组成之；内设组长一人，在治安保卫委员会领导下进行工作。

**Article 3** The establishment of public security defense committees shall, in cities, generally take organs, factories, enterprises, schools, or neighborhoods as units, and in rural areas, take administrative villages as units. The number of committee members shall be composed of three to eleven persons, depending on the size and complexity of the unit, and a director shall be appointed, with the possibility of appointing one or two deputy directors.  
After the establishment of the public security defense committee, where the circumstances require, with the approval of the municipal or county public security bureau, a public security defense small group may be established, composed of three to five activists elected by the masses, with a group leader appointed, who shall work under the leadership of the public security defense committee.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　治安保卫委员会委员的选举：  
(一)凡人民中历史清楚、作风正派、善于联系群众、热心治安保卫工作者，均得当选为委员。  
(二)治安保卫委员会委员之选举，事前应作充分准备，由群众提出候选人名单，经过介绍、审查、评议、酝酿成熟后再行选举，每半年改选一次，连选得连任。但在任期内如大多数群众认为必要改选时，得改选之。

**Article 4** Election of members of the public security defense committee:  
(1) Any person among the people who has a clear history, honest conduct, is good at maintaining contact with the masses, and is enthusiastic about public security defense work, shall be eligible to be elected as a member.  
(2) The election of members of the public security defense committee shall be fully prepared in advance, with a list of candidates proposed by the masses. After introduction, review, evaluation, and full deliberation, the election shall be conducted. A re-election shall be held every six months, and a person may be re-elected for consecutive terms. However, if during the term of office the majority of the masses deem a re-election necessary, a re-election may be held.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　治安保卫委员会的具体任务：  
(一)密切联系群众，对群众经常进行防奸、防谍、防火、防盗与镇压反革命活动的宣传教育，以提高群众的政治警惕性。  
(二)组织与领导群众协助政府、公安机关检举、监督和管制反革命分子，以严防反革命破坏活动。  
(三)组织与领导群众协助政府、公安机关对反革命家属进行教育和思想改造工作，争取他们拥护政府的政策措施。  
(四)发动群众共同制定防奸的爱国公约，并组织群众认真执行，以维护社会治安。

**Article 5** Specific tasks of the public security defense committee:  
(1) To maintain close ties with the masses, and regularly conduct publicity and education among the masses on preventing espionage, counter-espionage, fire prevention, theft prevention, and suppressing counter-revolutionary activities, so as to enhance the political vigilance of the masses.  
(2) To organize and lead the masses in assisting the government and the public security organs in exposing, supervising, and controlling counter-revolutionaries, so as to strictly prevent counter-revolutionary sabotage activities.  
(3) To organize and lead the masses in assisting the government and the public security organs in carrying out education and ideological reformation work for the family members of counter-revolutionaries, and to win them over to support the government's policies and measures.  
(4) To mobilize the masses to jointly formulate patriotic conventions against espionage, and organize the masses to earnestly implement them, so as to maintain public order.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　治安保卫委员会的职权：  
(一)对现行的反革命分子与通缉在逃的罪犯，有捕送政府、公安机关之责；但无审讯、关押、处理之权。  
(二)对非现行的反革命分子，有调查、监视、检举、报告之责；但无逮捕、扣押、搜查、取缔之权。  
(三)对社会治安与管制工作，有教育群众维护革命秩序，监督被管制者劳动生产，不准其乱说乱动，并向公安机关及时反映其表现情况之责；但无拘留、外罚、驱逐之权。  
(四)对反革命破坏之场所，应协助公安人员维持秩序，保护现场，以便公安机关进行勘查，但不得变更与处理现场。

**Article 6** Powers and functions of the public security defense committee:  
(1) With respect to flagrant counter-revolutionaries and fugitive offenders wanted for arrest, the committee shall have the duty to arrest and deliver them to the government or the public security organs; but shall have no power to interrogate, detain, or dispose of them.  
(2) With respect to non-flagrant counter-revolutionaries, the committee shall have the duty to investigate, monitor, expose, and report; but shall have no power to arrest, detain, search, or ban them.  
(3) With respect to public security and control work, the committee shall have the duty to educate the masses to maintain revolutionary order, supervise controlled persons in their labor and production, prohibit them from making reckless statements or actions, and promptly report their conduct to the public security organs; but shall have no power to detain, impose external punishment, or expel.  
(4) With respect to places of counter-revolutionary sabotage, the committee shall assist public security personnel in maintaining order and protecting the scene, so that the public security organs may conduct investigation; but shall not alter or dispose of the scene.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　治安保卫委员会委员必须严格遵守下列各项纪律：  
(一)遵守政府法令。  
(二)保守工作秘密，不得泄漏。  
(三)站稳人民革命立场，不得包庇反革命分子，不得挟嫌诬告，不得贪污受贿。  
(四)团结群众，帮助群众，不得强迫命令，借势欺人。

**Article 7** Members of the public security defense committee must strictly observe the following discipline:  
(1) Observe government decrees.  
(2) Keep work secrets and shall not divulge them.  
(3) Firmly stand on the people's revolutionary standpoint, and shall not harbor counter-revolutionaries, shall not make false accusations out of personal spite, and shall not engage in embezzlement or bribery.  
(4) Unite with the masses, assist the masses, and shall not resort to coercion and commandism, or bully others by virtue of one's position.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　治安保卫委员会的领导关系：  
(一)各机关、工厂、企业、学校之治安保卫委员会，受该单位行政机关及公安保卫部门领导。  
(二)城市街道之治安保卫委员会，受公安派出所领导，有居民委员会者受派出所及居民委员会双重领导。郊区无派出所者受公安分局、区公安助理员领导。  
(三)农村行政村之治安保卫委员会，受村政府、村公安员领导。  
(四)沿海村庄之治安保卫委员会，由海防派出所、海防公安员领导。

**Article 8** Leadership relationships of the public security defense committee:  
(1) The public security defense committee of each organ, factory, enterprise, or school shall be under the leadership of the administrative organ of that unit and the public security defense department.  
(2) The public security defense committee of a city neighborhood shall be under the leadership of the public security police station; where there is a residents' committee, it shall be under the dual leadership of the police station and the residents' committee. In suburbs without a police station, it shall be under the leadership of the public security sub-bureau or the district public security assistant.  
(3) The public security defense committee of a rural administrative village shall be under the leadership of the village government and the village public security officer.  
(4) The public security defense committee of a coastal village shall be under the leadership of the coastal defense police station and the coastal defense public security officer.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各地基层政府、公安机关，应加强对治安保卫委员会工作的领导，并建立必要的制度：  
(一)应使每一治安保卫委员会，定期向当地群众报告工作，征求群众的意见，接受群众的批评。  
(二)对工作积极有显著成绩者，及时给以表扬、奖励，对脱离群众违犯纪律者，及时给以批评、惩处。奖励与惩处均须经当地群众讨论议定及领导机关批准后执行。

**Article 9** The local grassroots governments and public security organs shall strengthen the leadership of the work of the public security defense committees and establish necessary systems:  
(1) They shall ensure that each public security defense committee regularly reports its work to the local masses, solicits opinions from the masses, and accepts criticism from the masses.  
(2) Those who are active in work and achieve remarkable results shall be promptly given commendation and rewards; those who become divorced from the masses or violate discipline shall be promptly given criticism and punishment. Both rewards and punishments shall be executed only after discussion and decision by the local masses and approval by the leading organ.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　省、市公安厅、局可根据本条例精神，拟定具体执行办法，并报大行政区、中央公安部备案。

**Article 10** The provincial and municipal public security departments and bureaus may, in accordance with the spirit of these Regulations, formulate specific enforcement measures and report them to the greater administrative region and the Central Ministry of Public Security for the record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本条例经中央人民政府政务院批准由中央公安部发布施行。

**Article 11** These Regulations are approved by the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government and promulgated by the Central Ministry of Public Security for implementation.
