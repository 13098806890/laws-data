# 中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-07-10  
**生效日期**：2024-09-01

> （2014年1月17日中华人民共和国国务院令第646号公布　2024年7月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第786号修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》（以下简称保密法）的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　坚持和加强中国共产党对保守国家秘密（以下简称保密）工作的领导。 中央保密工作领导机构领导全国保密工作，负责全国保密工作的顶层设计、统筹协调、整体推进、督促落实。 地方各级保密工作领导机构领导本地区保密工作，按照中央保密工作领导机构统一部署，贯彻落实党和国家保密工作战略及重大政策措施，统筹协调保密重大事项和重要工作，督促保密法律法规严格执行。 第三条　国家保密行政管理部门主管全国的保密工作。县级以上地方各级保密行政管理部门在上级保密行政管理部门指导下，主管本行政区域的保密工作。 第四条　中央国家机关在其职权范围内管理或者指导本系统的保密工作，监督执行保密法律法规，可以根据实际情况制定或者会同有关部门制定主管业务方面的保密规定。 第五条　国家机关和涉及国家秘密的单位（以下简称机关、单位）不得将依法应当公开的事项确定为国家秘密，不得将涉及国家秘密的信息公开。 第六条　机关、单位实行保密工作责任制，承担本机关、本单位保密工作主体责任。机关、单位主要负责人对本机关、本单位的保密工作负总责，分管保密工作的负责人和分管业务工作的负责人在职责范围内对保密工作负领导责任，工作人员对本岗位的保密工作负直接责任。 机关、单位应当加强保密工作力量建设，中央国家机关应当设立保密工作机构，配备专职保密干部，其他机关、单位应当根据保密工作需要设立保密工作机构或者指定人员专门负责保密工作。 机关、单位及其工作人员履行保密工作责任制情况应当纳入年度考评和考核内容。 第七条　县级以上人民政府应当加强保密基础设施建设和关键保密科学技术产品的配备。 省级以上保密行政管理部门应当推动保密科学技术自主创新，促进关键保密科学技术产品的研发工作，鼓励和支持保密科学技术研究和应用。 第八条　保密行政管理部门履行职责所需的经费，应当列入本级预算。机关、单位开展保密工作所需经费应当列入本机关、本单位的年度预算或者年度收支计划。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》（以下简称保密法）的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets (hereinafter referred to as the Secrecy Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　坚持和加强中国共产党对保守国家秘密（以下简称保密）工作的领导。  
中央保密工作领导机构领导全国保密工作，负责全国保密工作的顶层设计、统筹协调、整体推进、督促落实。  
地方各级保密工作领导机构领导本地区保密工作，按照中央保密工作领导机构统一部署，贯彻落实党和国家保密工作战略及重大政策措施，统筹协调保密重大事项和重要工作，督促保密法律法规严格执行。

**Article 2** The leadership of the Chinese Communist Party over the work of guarding state secrets (hereinafter referred to as secrecy work) shall be upheld and strengthened.  
The central secrecy work leading body shall lead the national secrecy work and be responsible for the top-level design, overall coordination, comprehensive advancement, and supervision and implementation of national secrecy work.  
Local secrecy work leading bodies at all levels shall lead secrecy work in their respective regions, implement the Party and state secrecy strategies and major policy measures in accordance with the unified deployment of the central secrecy work leading body, coordinate major secrecy matters and important tasks, and supervise the strict implementation of secrecy laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家保密行政管理部门主管全国的保密工作。县级以上地方各级保密行政管理部门在上级保密行政管理部门指导下，主管本行政区域的保密工作。

**Article 3** The state administrative department for secrecy shall be in charge of national secrecy work. Local administrative departments for secrecy at or above the county level shall, under the guidance of the administrative department for secrecy at the next higher level, be in charge of secrecy work in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中央国家机关在其职权范围内管理或者指导本系统的保密工作，监督执行保密法律法规，可以根据实际情况制定或者会同有关部门制定主管业务方面的保密规定。

**Article 4** Central state organs shall, within the scope of their functions and powers, manage or guide the secrecy work of their respective systems, supervise the implementation of secrecy laws and regulations, and may, in light of actual circumstances, formulate or jointly formulate with relevant departments secrecy provisions concerning the business under their charge.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家机关和涉及国家秘密的单位（以下简称机关、单位）不得将依法应当公开的事项确定为国家秘密，不得将涉及国家秘密的信息公开。

**Article 5** State organs and entities involving state secrets (hereinafter referred to as organs and entities) shall not determine matters that shall be made public according to law as state secrets, and shall not disclose information involving state secrets.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　机关、单位实行保密工作责任制，承担本机关、本单位保密工作主体责任。机关、单位主要负责人对本机关、本单位的保密工作负总责，分管保密工作的负责人和分管业务工作的负责人在职责范围内对保密工作负领导责任，工作人员对本岗位的保密工作负直接责任。  
机关、单位应当加强保密工作力量建设，中央国家机关应当设立保密工作机构，配备专职保密干部，其他机关、单位应当根据保密工作需要设立保密工作机构或者指定人员专门负责保密工作。  
机关、单位及其工作人员履行保密工作责任制情况应当纳入年度考评和考核内容。

**Article 6** Organs and entities shall implement a responsibility system for secrecy work and assume the main responsibility for their own secrecy work. The principal leading person of an organ or entity shall assume overall responsibility for the secrecy work of that organ or entity; the leading person in charge of secrecy work and the leading person in charge of business work shall, within the scope of their duties, assume leadership responsibility for secrecy work; and staff members shall assume direct responsibility for secrecy work in their respective posts.  
Organs and entities shall strengthen the capacity building of their secrecy work. Central state organs shall establish secrecy work institutions and be staffed with full-time secrecy cadres. Other organs and entities shall, according to the needs of secrecy work, establish secrecy work institutions or designate personnel to be specifically responsible for secrecy work.  
The performance of the secrecy work responsibility system by organs, entities and their staff members shall be included in the annual evaluation and assessment content.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府应当加强保密基础设施建设和关键保密科学技术产品的配备。  
省级以上保密行政管理部门应当推动保密科学技术自主创新，促进关键保密科学技术产品的研发工作，鼓励和支持保密科学技术研究和应用。

**Article 7** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of secrecy infrastructure and the deployment of key secrecy science and technology products.  
Administrative departments for secrecy at or above the provincial level shall promote independent innovation in secrecy science and technology, facilitate the research and development of key secrecy science and technology products, and encourage and support research and application of secrecy science and technology.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　保密行政管理部门履行职责所需的经费，应当列入本级预算。机关、单位开展保密工作所需经费应当列入本机关、本单位的年度预算或者年度收支计划。

**Article 8** The funds necessary for administrative departments for secrecy to perform their duties shall be included in the budgets at the corresponding levels. The funds required by organs and entities to carry out secrecy work shall be included in their annual budgets or annual revenue and expenditure plans.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　保密行政管理部门应当组织开展经常性的保密宣传教育。干部教育培训主管部门应当会同保密行政管理部门履行干部保密教育培训工作职责。干部教育培训机构应当将保密教育纳入教学体系。教育行政部门应当推动保密教育纳入国民教育体系。宣传部门应当指导鼓励大众传播媒介充分发挥作用，普及保密知识，宣传保密法治，推动全社会增强保密意识。  
机关、单位应当定期对本机关、本单位工作人员进行保密工作优良传统、保密形势任务、保密法律法规、保密技术防范、保密违法案例警示等方面的教育培训。

**Article 9** Administrative departments for secrecy shall organize and carry out regular publicity and education on secrecy. The competent departments for cadre education and training shall, together with the administrative departments for secrecy, perform the duties of cadre secrecy education and training. Cadre education and training institutions shall incorporate secrecy education into their teaching systems. Education administrative departments shall promote the inclusion of secrecy education in the national education system. Propaganda departments shall guide and encourage mass media to give full play to their role in popularizing secrecy knowledge, publicizing the rule of law on secrecy, and promoting the enhancement of secrecy awareness of the whole society.  
Organs and entities shall regularly conduct education and training for their staff members on aspects such as the fine traditions of secrecy work, the current situation and tasks of secrecy, secrecy laws and regulations, secrecy technology prevention, and warnings of illegal secrecy cases.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　保密行政管理部门应当按照国家有关规定完善激励保障机制，加强专门人才队伍建设、专业培训和装备配备，提升保密工作专业化能力和水平。教育行政部门应当加强保密相关学科专业建设指导和支持。

**Article 10** Administrative departments for secrecy shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, improve the incentive and guarantee mechanism, strengthen the building of a specialized talent team, professional training, and equipment deployment, so as to enhance the professional capacity and level of secrecy work. Education administrative departments shall strengthen guidance and support for the construction of secrecy-related disciplines and specialties.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对有下列表现之一的组织和个人，应当按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励：  
（一）在危急情况下保护国家秘密安全的；  
（二）在重大涉密活动中，为维护国家秘密安全做出突出贡献的；  
（三）在保密科学技术研发中取得重大成果或者显著成绩的；  
（四）及时检举泄露或者非法获取、持有国家秘密行为的；  
（五）发现他人泄露或者可能泄露国家秘密，立即采取补救措施，避免或者减轻危害后果的；  
（六）在保密管理等涉密岗位工作，忠于职守，严守国家秘密，表现突出的；  
（七）其他在保守、保护国家秘密工作中做出突出贡献的。

**Article 11** Organizations and individuals that have one of the following performances shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state regulations:  
(1) protecting the security of state secrets in critical situations;  
(2) making outstanding contributions to safeguarding the security of state secrets in major secret-related activities;  
(3) achieving major results or notable achievements in the research and development of secrecy science and technology;  
(4) promptly reporting acts of disclosing or illegally obtaining or holding state secrets;  
(5) immediately taking remedial measures upon discovering that others have disclosed or may disclose state secrets, thereby avoiding or mitigating harmful consequences;  
(6) working in secrecy management or other secret-related posts, being faithful to their duties, strictly guarding state secrets, and demonstrating outstanding performance;  
(7) making other outstanding contributions in the work of guarding and protecting state secrets.

### 第二章　国家秘密的范围和密级

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家秘密及其密级的具体范围（以下称保密事项范围）应当明确规定国家秘密具体事项的名称、密级、保密期限、知悉范围和产生层级。  
保密事项范围应当根据情况变化及时调整。制定、修订保密事项范围应当充分论证，听取有关机关、单位和相关行业、领域专家的意见。

**Article 12** The specific scope of state secrets and their classification levels (hereinafter referred to as the scope of secrecy matters) shall clearly specify the names, classification levels, duration of secrecy, scope of knowledge, and level of creation of specific state secret matters.  
The scope of secrecy matters shall be adjusted in a timely manner in light of changes in circumstances. The formulation and revision of the scope of secrecy matters shall be fully demonstrated and opinions of relevant organs, entities, and experts in related industries and fields shall be heard.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有定密权限的机关、单位应当依据本行业、本领域以及相关行业、领域保密事项范围，制定国家秘密事项一览表，并报同级保密行政管理部门备案。国家秘密事项一览表应当根据保密事项范围及时修订。

**Article 13** Organs and entities with the authority to determine secrets shall, in accordance with the scope of secrecy matters of their own industry, field, and related industries and fields, formulate a list of state secret matters and report it to the administrative department for secrecy at the same level for record. The list of state secret matters shall be revised in a timely manner according to the scope of secrecy matters.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　机关、单位主要负责人为本机关、本单位法定定密责任人，根据工作需要，可以明确本机关、本单位其他负责人、内设机构负责人或者其他人员为指定定密责任人。  
定密责任人、承办人应当接受定密培训，熟悉定密职责和保密事项范围，掌握定密程序和方法。

**Article 14** The principal leading person of an organ or entity shall be the statutory person responsible for determining secrets of that organ or entity. According to work needs, other leading persons, heads of internal organs, or other personnel of the organ or entity may be designated as designated persons responsible for determining secrets.  
Persons responsible for determining secrets and handlers shall receive training on secret determination, be familiar with the duties of secret determination and the scope of secrecy matters, and master the procedures and methods of secret determination.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　定密责任人在职责范围内承担国家秘密确定、变更和解除工作，指导、监督职责范围内的定密工作。具体职责是：  
（一）审核批准承办人拟定的国家秘密的密级、保密期限和知悉范围；  
（二）对本机关、本单位确定的尚在保密期限内的国家秘密进行审核，作出是否变更或者解除的决定；  
（三）参与制定修订本机关、本单位国家秘密事项一览表；  
（四）对是否属于国家秘密和属于何种密级不明确的事项先行拟定密级、保密期限和知悉范围，并按照规定的程序报保密行政管理部门确定。

**Article 15** A person responsible for determining secrets shall, within the scope of his/her duties, undertake the work of determining, changing, and declassifying state secrets, and guide and supervise the work of determining secrets within the scope of his/her duties. The specific duties are:  
(1) reviewing and approving the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge of state secrets drafted by handlers;  
(2) reviewing state secrets determined by the organ or entity that are still within the duration of secrecy, and making a decision on whether to change or declassify them;  
(3) participating in the formulation and revision of the list of state secret matters of the organ or entity;  
(4) for matters where it is unclear whether they are state secrets and what classification level they belong to, first drafting the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge, and reporting to the administrative department for secrecy for determination in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中央国家机关、省级机关以及设区的市级机关可以根据保密工作需要或者有关机关、单位申请，在国家保密行政管理部门规定的定密权限、授权范围内作出定密授权。  
无法按照前款规定授权的，省级以上保密行政管理部门可以根据保密工作需要或者有关机关、单位申请，作出定密授权。  
定密授权应当以书面形式作出。授权机关应当对被授权机关、单位履行定密授权的情况进行监督。被授权机关、单位不得再授权。  
中央国家机关、省级机关和省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门作出的定密授权，报国家保密行政管理部门备案；设区的市级机关作出的定密授权，报省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门备案。

**Article 16** Central state organs, provincial organs, and organs of cities divided into districts may, in light of the needs of secrecy work or upon the application of relevant organs or entities, make authorizations for determining secrets within the scope of authority and authorization prescribed by the state administrative department for secrecy.  
Where authorization cannot be made in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the administrative department for secrecy at or above the provincial level may, in light of the needs of secrecy work or upon the application of relevant organs or entities, make authorizations for determining secrets.  
Authorization for determining secrets shall be made in writing. The authorizing organ shall supervise the performance of the authorization for determining secrets by the authorized organ or entity. The authorized organ or entity shall not sub-authorize.  
Authorizations for determining secrets made by central state organs, provincial organs, and administrative departments for secrecy of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be reported to the state administrative department for secrecy for record; authorizations made by organs of cities divided into districts shall be reported to the administrative department for secrecy of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for record.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　机关、单位应当在国家秘密产生的同时，由承办人依据有关保密事项范围拟定密级、保密期限和知悉范围，报定密责任人审核批准，并采取相应保密措施。  
机关、单位对应当定密但本机关、本单位没有定密权限的事项，先行采取保密措施，并依照法定程序，报上级机关、单位确定；没有上级机关、单位的，报有定密权限的业务主管部门或者保密行政管理部门确定。  
机关、单位确定国家秘密，能够明确密点的，按照国家保密规定确定并标注。

**Article 17** Organs and entities shall, at the same time as the creation of a state secret, have the handler draft the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge in accordance with the relevant scope of secrecy matters, submit them to the person responsible for determining secrets for review and approval, and take corresponding secrecy measures.  
For matters that shall be classified but for which the organ or entity does not have the authority to determine secrets, the organ or entity shall first take secrecy measures and report to the higher-level organ or entity for determination in accordance with legal procedures; if there is no higher-level organ or entity, it shall report to the competent business department or the administrative department for secrecy with the authority to determine secrets for determination.  
Where an organ or entity determines a state secret and can identify the specific secret point, it shall determine and mark it in accordance with state secrecy regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　机关、单位执行上级确定的国家秘密事项或者办理其他机关、单位确定的国家秘密事项，有下列情形之一的，应当根据所执行、办理的国家秘密事项的密级、保密期限和知悉范围派生定密：  
（一）与已确定的国家秘密事项完全一致的；  
（二）涉及已确定的国家秘密事项密点的；  
（三）对已确定的国家秘密事项进行概括总结、编辑整合、具体细化的；  
（四）原定密机关、单位对使用已确定的国家秘密事项有明确定密要求的。

**Article 18** Where an organ or entity implements a state secret matter determined by a higher authority or handles a state secret matter determined by another organ or entity, and one of the following circumstances exists, it shall carry out derivative classification based on the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge of the state secret matter being implemented or handled:  
(1) it is completely identical to a determined state secret matter;  
(2) it involves a secret point of a determined state secret matter;  
(3) it summarizes, compiles, integrates, or specifies a determined state secret matter;  
(4) the original determining organ or entity has a clear classification requirement for the use of the determined state secret matter.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　机关、单位对所产生的国家秘密，应当按照保密事项范围的规定确定具体的保密期限或者解密时间；不能确定的，应当确定解密条件。  
国家秘密的保密期限，自标明的制发日起计算；不能标明制发日的，确定该国家秘密的机关、单位应当书面通知知悉范围内的机关、单位和人员，保密期限自通知之日起计算。

**Article 19** Organs and entities shall determine the specific duration of secrecy or declassification time for the state secrets they generate in accordance with the provisions of the scope of secrecy matters; if it cannot be determined, they shall determine the conditions for declassification.  
The duration of secrecy of a state secret shall be calculated from the date of issuance indicated thereon; if the date of issuance cannot be indicated, the organ or entity that determines the state secret shall notify in writing the organs, entities, and personnel within the scope of knowledge, and the duration of secrecy shall be calculated from the date of notification.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机关、单位应当依法限定国家秘密的知悉范围，对知悉机密级以上国家秘密的人员，应当作出记录。

**Article 20** Organs and entities shall, according to law, limit the scope of knowledge of state secrets, and shall keep records of personnel who have knowledge of state secrets at the confidential level or above.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家秘密载体以及属于国家秘密的设备、产品（以下简称密品）的明显部位应当作出国家秘密标志。国家秘密标志应当标注密级、保密期限。国家秘密的密级或者保密期限发生变更的，应当及时对原国家秘密标志作出变更。  
无法作出国家秘密标志的，确定该国家秘密的机关、单位应当书面通知知悉范围内的机关、单位和人员。

**Article 21** Conspicuous parts of state secret carriers and equipment or products that are state secrets (hereinafter referred to as secret articles) shall be marked with a state secret insignia. The state secret insignia shall indicate the classification level and the duration of secrecy. Where the classification level or duration of secrecy of a state secret changes, the original state secret insignia shall be changed in a timely manner.  
Where a state secret insignia cannot be made, the organ or entity that determines the state secret shall notify in writing the organs, entities, and personnel within the scope of knowledge.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　机关、单位对所确定的国家秘密，认为符合保密法有关解除或者变更规定的，应当及时解除或者变更。  
机关、单位对不属于本机关、本单位确定的国家秘密，认为符合保密法有关解除或者变更规定的，可以向原定密机关、单位或者其上级机关、单位提出建议。  
已经依法移交各级国家档案馆的属于国家秘密的档案，由原定密机关、单位按照国家有关规定进行解密审核。

**Article 22** Where an organ or entity believes that a state secret it has determined meets the provisions of the Secrecy Law concerning declassification or change, it shall promptly declassify or change it.  
Where an organ or entity believes that a state secret not determined by it meets the provisions of the Secrecy Law concerning declassification or change, it may make a proposal to the original determining organ or entity or its higher-level organ or entity.  
For archives that are state secrets and have been transferred to state archives at various levels according to law, the original determining organ or entity shall conduct declassification review in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　机关、单位被撤销或者合并、分立的，该机关、单位所确定国家秘密的变更和解除，由承担其职能的机关、单位负责；没有相应机关、单位的，由其上级机关、单位或者同级保密行政管理部门指定的机关、单位负责。

**Article 23** Where an organ or entity is abolished, merged, or divided, the organ or entity that assumes its functions shall be responsible for the change and declassification of state secrets determined by the original organ or entity; if there is no corresponding organ or entity, the higher-level organ or entity or the organ or entity designated by the administrative department for secrecy at the same level shall be responsible.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　机关、单位发现本机关、本单位国家秘密的确定、变更和解除不当的，应当及时纠正；上级机关、单位发现下级机关、单位国家秘密的确定、变更和解除不当的，应当及时通知其纠正，也可以直接纠正。

**Article 24** Where an organ or entity discovers that the determination, change, or declassification of state secrets by itself is improper, it shall promptly correct it; where a higher-level organ or entity discovers that the determination, change, or declassification of state secrets by a lower-level organ or entity is improper, it shall promptly notify it to make corrections, and may also directly make corrections.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　机关、单位对符合保密法的规定，但保密事项范围没有规定的不明确事项，应当先行拟定密级、保密期限和知悉范围，采取相应的保密措施，并自拟定之日起10个工作日内报有关部门确定。拟定为绝密级的事项和中央国家机关拟定的机密级、秘密级的事项，报国家保密行政管理部门确定；其他机关、单位拟定的机密级、秘密级的事项，报省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门确定。  
保密行政管理部门接到报告后，应当在10个工作日内作出决定。省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门还应当将所作决定及时报国家保密行政管理部门备案。

**Article 25** For matters that meet the provisions of the Secrecy Law but are not covered by the scope of secrecy matters (unclear matters), organs and entities shall first draft the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge, take corresponding secrecy measures, and within 10 working days from the date of drafting, report to the relevant department for determination. Matters drafted as top secret and matters drafted as confidential or secret by central state organs shall be reported to the state administrative department for secrecy for determination; matters drafted as confidential or secret by other organs or entities shall be reported to the administrative department for secrecy of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for determination.  
Upon receiving the report, the administrative department for secrecy shall make a decision within 10 working days. The administrative department for secrecy of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall also promptly report the decision to the state administrative department for secrecy for record.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　机关、单位对已确定的国家秘密事项是否属于国家秘密或者属于何种密级有不同意见的，可以向原定密机关、单位提出异议，由原定密机关、单位作出决定。  
机关、单位对原定密机关、单位未予处理或者对作出的决定仍有异议的，按照下列规定办理：  
（一）确定为绝密级的事项和中央国家机关确定的机密级、秘密级的事项，报国家保密行政管理部门确定；  
（二）其他机关、单位确定的机密级、秘密级的事项，报省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门确定；对省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门作出的决定有异议的，可以报国家保密行政管理部门确定。  
在原定密机关、单位或者保密行政管理部门作出决定前，对有关事项应当按照主张密级中的最高密级采取相应的保密措施。

**Article 26** Where an organ or entity has a different opinion on whether a determined state secret matter is a state secret or what classification level it belongs to, it may raise an objection with the original determining organ or entity, and the original determining organ or entity shall make a decision.  
Where the original determining organ or entity fails to handle the matter or the organ or entity still has an objection to the decision made, it shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) matters determined as top secret and matters determined as confidential or secret by central state organs shall be reported to the state administrative department for secrecy for determination;  
(2) matters determined as confidential or secret by other organs or entities shall be reported to the administrative department for secrecy of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for determination; if there is an objection to the decision made by the administrative department for secrecy of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, it may be reported to the state administrative department for secrecy for determination.  
Before the original determining organ or entity or the administrative department for secrecy makes a decision, corresponding secrecy measures shall be taken for the relevant matter in accordance with the highest classification level among the claimed classification levels.

### 第三章　保密制度

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家秘密载体管理应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）制作国家秘密载体，应当由本机关、本单位或者取得国家秘密载体制作、复制资质的单位承担，制作场所、设备应当符合国家保密规定；  
（二）收发国家秘密载体，应当履行清点、编号、登记、签收手续；  
（三）传递国家秘密载体，应当通过机要交通、机要通信或者其他符合国家保密规定的方式进行；  
（四）阅读、使用国家秘密载体，应当在符合国家保密规定的场所进行；  
（五）复制国家秘密载体或者摘录、引用、汇编属于国家秘密的内容，应当按照规定报批，不得擅自改变原件的密级、保密期限和知悉范围，复制件应当加盖复制机关、单位戳记，并视同原件进行管理；  
（六）保存国家秘密载体的场所、设施、设备，应当符合国家保密规定；  
（七）维修国家秘密载体，应当由本机关、本单位专门技术人员负责。确需外单位人员维修的，应当由本机关、本单位的人员现场监督。确需在本机关、本单位以外维修的，应当符合国家保密规定；  
（八）携带国家秘密载体外出，应当符合国家保密规定，并采取可靠的保密措施。携带国家秘密载体出境，应当按照国家保密规定办理审批手续；  
（九）清退国家秘密载体，应当按照制发机关、单位要求办理。

**Article 27** The management of state secret carriers shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) The production of state secret carriers shall be undertaken by the organ or entity itself or by an entity that has obtained the qualification for production or reproduction of state secret carriers, and the production premises and equipment shall comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(2) The receipt and dispatch of state secret carriers shall go through the procedures of counting, numbering, registering, and signing;  
(3) The transmission of state secret carriers shall be conducted through confidential transportation, confidential communications, or other methods that comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(4) The reading and use of state secret carriers shall be conducted in premises that comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(5) The reproduction of state secret carriers or the extraction, citation, or compilation of content that is a state secret shall be submitted for approval in accordance with regulations, and the classification level, duration of secrecy, and scope of knowledge of the original shall not be altered without authorization; the copy shall be stamped with the seal of the reproducing organ or entity and managed as the original;  
(6) The premises, facilities, and equipment for storing state secret carriers shall comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(7) The maintenance of state secret carriers shall be the responsibility of specialized technical personnel of the organ or entity. If maintenance by personnel from an external entity is truly necessary, personnel from the organ or entity shall supervise on-site. If maintenance outside the organ or entity is truly necessary, state secrecy regulations shall be complied with;  
(8) Carrying state secret carriers outside the premises shall comply with state secrecy regulations and reliable secrecy measures shall be taken. Carrying state secret carriers out of the country shall go through approval procedures in accordance with state secrecy regulations;  
(9) The return of state secret carriers shall be handled in accordance with the requirements of the issuing organ or entity.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　销毁国家秘密载体，应当符合国家保密规定和标准，确保销毁的国家秘密信息无法还原。  
销毁国家秘密载体，应当履行清点、登记、审批手续，并送交保密行政管理部门设立的工作机构或者指定的单位销毁。机关、单位因工作需要，自行销毁少量国家秘密载体的，应当使用符合国家保密标准的销毁设备和方法。

**Article 28** The destruction of state secret carriers shall comply with state secrecy regulations and standards, ensuring that the destroyed state secret information cannot be recovered.  
The destruction of state secret carriers shall go through the procedures of counting, registering, and approving, and shall be delivered to the working institution established by the administrative department for secrecy or the designated unit for destruction. Where organs or entities, due to work needs, destroy a small number of state secret carriers on their own, they shall use destruction equipment and methods that meet state secrecy standards.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　绝密级国家秘密载体管理还应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）收发绝密级国家秘密载体，应当指定专人负责；  
（二）传递、携带绝密级国家秘密载体，应当两人以上同行，所用包装应当符合国家保密规定；  
（三）阅读、使用绝密级国家秘密载体，应当在符合国家保密规定的指定场所进行；  
（四）禁止复制、下载、汇编、摘抄绝密级文件信息资料，确有工作需要的，应当征得原定密机关、单位或者其上级机关同意；  
（五）禁止将绝密级国家秘密载体携带出境，国家另有规定的从其规定。

**Article 29** The management of top secret state secret carriers shall also comply with the following provisions:  
(1) The receipt and dispatch of top secret state secret carriers shall be handled by designated persons;  
(2) The transmission and carrying of top secret state secret carriers shall be conducted by at least two persons traveling together, and the packaging used shall comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(3) The reading and use of top secret state secret carriers shall be conducted in designated premises that comply with state secrecy regulations;  
(4) The reproduction, downloading, compilation, or extraction of top secret documents and information materials is prohibited; if truly necessary for work, consent shall be obtained from the original determining organ or entity or its higher-level organ;  
(5) Carrying top secret state secret carriers out of the country is prohibited, unless otherwise provided by the state.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　机关、单位应当依法对密品的研制、生产、试验、运输、使用、保存、维修、销毁等进行管理。  
机关、单位应当及时确定密品的密级和保密期限，严格控制密品的接触范围，对放置密品的场所、部位采取安全保密防范措施。  
绝密级密品的研制、生产、维修应当在符合国家保密规定的封闭场所进行，并设置专门放置、保存场所。  
密品的零件、部件、组件等物品，涉及国家秘密的，按照国家保密规定管理。

**Article 30** Organs and entities shall, according to law, manage the research and development, production, testing, transportation, use, storage, maintenance, and destruction of secret articles.  
Organs and entities shall promptly determine the classification level and duration of secrecy of secret articles, strictly control the scope of access to secret articles, and take safety and secrecy protection measures for the premises and locations where secret articles are placed.  
The research and development, production, and maintenance of top secret articles shall be conducted in enclosed premises that comply with state secrecy regulations, and dedicated premises for placement and storage shall be set up.  
Parts, components, and assemblies of secret articles that involve state secrets shall be managed in accordance with state secrecy regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　机关、单位应当依法确定保密要害部门、部位，报同级保密行政管理部门确认，严格保密管理。

**Article 31** Organs and entities shall, according to law, determine the vital secrecy departments and parts, report them to the administrative department for secrecy at the same level for confirmation, and strictly enforce secrecy management.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　涉密信息系统按照涉密程度分为绝密级、机密级、秘密级。机关、单位应当根据涉密信息系统存储、处理信息的最高密级确定保护等级，按照分级保护要求采取相应的安全保密防护措施。

**Article 32** Secret-related information systems are classified into top secret, confidential, and secret levels according to the degree of secret-relatedness. Organs and entities shall determine the protection level based on the highest classification level of information stored or processed in the secret-related information system, and take corresponding security and secrecy protection measures in accordance with graded protection requirements.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　涉密信息系统应当由国家保密行政管理部门设立或者授权的机构进行检测评估，并经设区的市级以上保密行政管理部门审查合格，方可投入使用。  
公安机关、国家安全机关的涉密信息系统测评审查工作按照国家保密行政管理部门会同国务院公安、国家安全部门制定的有关规定执行。

**Article 33** A secret-related information system shall be tested and evaluated by an institution established or authorized by the state administrative department for secrecy, and may only be put into use after being reviewed and approved by the administrative department for secrecy at or above the city divided into districts level.  
The evaluation and review work for secret-related information systems of public security organs and state security organs shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant regulations formulated by the state administrative department for secrecy together with the public security and state security departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　机关、单位应当加强信息系统、信息设备的运行维护、使用管理，指定专门机构或者人员负责运行维护、安全保密管理和安全审计，按照国家保密规定建设保密自监管设施，定期开展安全保密检查和风险评估，配合保密行政管理部门排查预警事件，及时发现并处置安全保密风险隐患。

**Article 34** Organs and entities shall strengthen the operation, maintenance, and usage management of information systems and information equipment, designate specialized institutions or personnel to be responsible for operation and maintenance, security and secrecy management, and security auditing, build self-supervision facilities for secrecy in accordance with state secrecy regulations, regularly carry out security and secrecy inspections and risk assessments, cooperate with administrative departments for secrecy in investigating and pre-warning incidents, and promptly discover and handle security and secrecy risks and hidden dangers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　机关、单位应当按照国家保密规定，对绝密级信息系统每年至少开展一次安全保密风险评估，对机密级及以下信息系统每两年至少开展一次安全保密风险评估。机关、单位涉密信息系统的密级、使用范围和使用环境等发生变化可能产生新的安全保密风险隐患的，应当按照国家保密规定和标准采取相应防护措施，并开展安全保密风险评估。  
涉密信息系统中使用的信息设备应当安全可靠，以无线方式接入涉密信息系统的，应当符合国家保密和密码管理规定、标准。  
涉密信息系统不再使用的，应当按照国家保密规定和标准对相关保密设施、设备进行处理，并及时向相关保密行政管理部门备案。

**Article 35** Organs and entities shall, in accordance with state secrecy regulations, conduct a security and secrecy risk assessment for top secret information systems at least once a year, and for confidential and secret information systems at least once every two years. Where the classification level, scope of use, or usage environment of an organ's or entity's secret-related information system changes and may create new security and secrecy risks and hidden dangers, corresponding protective measures shall be taken in accordance with state secrecy regulations and standards, and a security and secrecy risk assessment shall be conducted.  
Information equipment used in secret-related information systems shall be safe and reliable. Where wireless access to a secret-related information system is used, it shall comply with state secrecy and cryptography management regulations and standards.  
Where a secret-related information system is no longer in use, the relevant secrecy facilities and equipment shall be disposed of in accordance with state secrecy regulations and standards, and the matter shall be promptly reported to the relevant administrative department for secrecy for record.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　研制、生产、采购、配备用于保护国家秘密的安全保密产品和保密技术装备应当符合国家保密规定和标准。  
国家鼓励研制生产单位根据保密工作需要，采用新技术、新方法、新工艺等创新安全保密产品和保密技术装备。

**Article 36** The research and development, production, procurement, and deployment of security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment used to protect state secrets shall comply with state secrecy regulations and standards.  
The state encourages research and development and production entities to adopt new technologies, methods, and processes to innovate security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment according to the needs of secrecy work.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　研制生产单位应当为用于保护国家秘密的安全保密产品和保密技术装备持续提供维修维护服务，建立漏洞、缺陷发现和处理机制，不得在安全保密产品和保密技术装备中设置恶意程序。  
研制生产单位可以向国家保密行政管理部门设立或者授权的机构申请对安全保密产品和保密技术装备进行检测，检测合格的，上述机构颁发合格证书。研制生产单位生产的安全保密产品和保密技术装备应当与送检样品一致。

**Article 37** Research and development and production entities shall continuously provide maintenance and repair services for security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment used to protect state secrets, establish mechanisms for discovering and handling vulnerabilities and defects, and shall not install malicious programs in security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment.  
Research and development and production entities may apply to an institution established or authorized by the state administrative department for secrecy for testing of security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment; if the test passes, the aforementioned institution shall issue a certificate of conformity. The security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment produced by research and development and production entities shall be consistent with the samples submitted for testing.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家保密行政管理部门组织其设立或者授权的机构开展用于保护国家秘密的安全保密产品和保密技术装备抽检、复检，发现不符合国家保密规定和标准的，应当责令整改；存在重大缺陷或者重大泄密隐患的，应当责令采取停止销售、召回产品等补救措施，相关单位应当配合。

**Article 38** The state administrative department for secrecy shall organize the institutions it establishes or authorizes to carry out spot checks and re-inspections of security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment used to protect state secrets. If non-compliance with state secrecy regulations and standards is found, the entities shall be ordered to rectify; if there are major defects or major hidden dangers of leakage, remedial measures such as ceasing sales and recalling products shall be ordered, and relevant entities shall cooperate.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　网络运营者应当遵守保密法律法规和国家有关规定，建立保密违法行为投诉、举报、发现、处置制度，完善受理和处理工作机制，制定泄密应急预案。发生泄密事件时，网络运营者应当立即启动应急预案，采取补救措施，并向保密行政管理部门或者公安机关、国家安全机关报告。

**Article 39** Network operators shall comply with secrecy laws and regulations and relevant state provisions, establish systems for complaints, reports, discovery, and handling of illegal secrecy acts, improve the working mechanism for acceptance and handling, and formulate emergency plans for leakage. When a leakage incident occurs, the network operator shall immediately activate the emergency plan, take remedial measures, and report to the administrative department for secrecy or the public security organ or state security organ.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　网络运营者对保密行政管理部门依法实施的保密违法案件调查和预警事件排查，应当予以配合。  
省级以上保密行政管理部门在履行保密监督管理职责中，发现网络存在较大泄密隐患或者发生泄密事件的，可以按照规定权限和程序对该网络运营者的法定代表人或者主要负责人进行约谈，督促其及时整改，消除隐患。

**Article 40** Network operators shall cooperate with the investigation of illegal secrecy cases and the screening of pre-warning events carried out by administrative departments for secrecy according to law.  
Where administrative departments for secrecy at or above the provincial level, in the performance of their secrecy supervision and management duties, discover that a network has a major leakage hidden danger or a leakage incident has occurred, they may, in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures, conduct an interview with the legal representative or principal leading person of the network operator, urging them to promptly rectify and eliminate the hidden danger.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　机关、单位应当加强对互联网使用的保密管理。机关、单位工作人员使用智能终端产品等应当符合国家保密规定，不得违反有关规定使用非涉密信息系统、信息设备存储、处理、传输国家秘密。

**Article 41** Organs and entities shall strengthen the secrecy management of Internet use. Staff members of organs and entities using smart terminal products and the like shall comply with state secrecy regulations and shall not, in violation of relevant provisions, use non-secret-related information systems or information equipment to store, process, or transmit state secrets.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　机关、单位应当健全信息公开保密审查工作机制，明确审查机构，规范审查程序，按照先审查、后公开的原则，对拟公开的信息逐项进行保密审查。

**Article 42** Organs and entities shall improve the working mechanism for secrecy review of information disclosure, specify the review institution, standardize the review procedures, and, following the principle of review before disclosure, conduct secrecy review on each item of information to be disclosed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　机关、单位应当承担涉密数据安全保护责任，涉密数据收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供等处理活动应当符合国家保密规定。  
省级以上保密行政管理部门应当会同有关部门建立动态监测、综合评估等安全保密防控机制，指导机关、单位落实安全保密防控措施，防范数据汇聚、关联引发的泄密风险。  
机关、单位应当对汇聚、关联后属于国家秘密事项的数据依法加强安全管理，落实安全保密防控措施。

**Article 43** Organs and entities shall assume the responsibility for the security protection of secret-related data, and the processing activities such as collection, storage, use, processing, transmission, and provision of secret-related data shall comply with state secrecy regulations.  
Administrative departments for secrecy at or above the provincial level shall, together with relevant departments, establish dynamic monitoring, comprehensive assessment, and other security and secrecy prevention and control mechanisms, guide organs and entities in implementing security and secrecy prevention and control measures, and prevent leakage risks arising from data aggregation and correlation.  
Organs and entities shall strengthen the security management of data that, after aggregation or correlation, falls within the scope of state secret matters, and implement security and secrecy prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　机关、单位向境外或者向境外在中国境内设立的组织、机构提供国家秘密，任用、聘用的境外人员因工作需要知悉国家秘密的，应当按照国家保密规定办理，进行审查评估，签订保密协议，督促落实保密管理要求。

**Article 44** Where organs or entities provide state secrets to foreign entities or to organizations or institutions established in China by foreign entities, or where foreign personnel employed or hired need to have knowledge of state secrets due to work needs, the matter shall be handled in accordance with state secrecy regulations, including conducting review and assessment, signing secrecy agreements, and supervising the implementation of secrecy management requirements.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　举办会议或者其他活动涉及国家秘密的，主办单位应当采取下列保密措施，承办、参加单位和人员应当配合：  
（一）根据会议、活动的内容确定密级，制定保密方案，限定参加人员和工作人员范围；  
（二）使用符合国家保密规定和标准的场所、设施、设备，采取必要保密技术防护等措施；  
（三）按照国家保密规定管理国家秘密载体；  
（四）对参加人员和工作人员进行身份核实和保密教育，提出具体保密要求；  
（五）保密法律法规和国家保密规定要求的其他措施。  
通过电视、电话、网络等方式举办会议或者其他活动涉及国家秘密的，还应当符合国家有关保密标准。

**Article 45** Where a meeting or other activity involves state secrets, the sponsor shall take the following secrecy measures, and the organizer, participants, and units and personnel shall cooperate:  
(1) determining the classification level based on the content of the meeting or activity, formulating a secrecy plan, and limiting the scope of participants and working personnel;  
(2) using premises, facilities, and equipment that comply with state secrecy regulations and standards, and taking necessary technical protection measures for secrecy;  
(3) managing state secret carriers in accordance with state secrecy regulations;  
(4) verifying the identities of participants and working personnel and conducting secrecy education, and putting forward specific secrecy requirements;  
(5) other measures required by secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions.  
Where a meeting or other activity is held via television, telephone, network, or other means and involves state secrets, it shall also comply with relevant state secrecy standards.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　保密行政管理部门及其他主管部门应当加强对涉密军事设施及其他重要涉密单位周边区域保密管理工作的指导和监督，建立协调机制，加强军地协作，组织督促整改，有关机关、单位应当配合，及时发现并消除安全保密风险隐患。

**Article 46** Administrative departments for secrecy and other competent departments shall strengthen guidance and supervision over secrecy management in the surrounding areas of secret-related military facilities and other important secret-related entities, establish coordination mechanisms, enhance military-local cooperation, organize and supervise rectification, and relevant organs and entities shall cooperate to promptly discover and eliminate security and secrecy risks and hidden dangers.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　从事涉及国家秘密业务（以下简称涉密业务）的企业事业单位应当符合下列条件：  
（一）在中华人民共和国境内依法成立1年以上的法人，国家另有规定的从其规定；  
（二）无犯罪记录，近1年内未发生泄密案件；  
（三）从事涉密业务的人员具有中华人民共和国国籍，国家另有规定的从其规定；  
（四）保密制度完善，有专门的机构或者人员负责保密工作；  
（五）用于涉密业务的场所、设施、设备符合国家保密规定和标准；  
（六）具有从事涉密业务的专业能力；  
（七）保密法律法规和国家保密规定要求的其他条件。

**Article 47** Enterprises and public institutions engaged in businesses involving state secrets (hereinafter referred to as secret-related businesses) shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being a legal person lawfully established within the territory of the People's Republic of China for more than one year, unless otherwise provided by the state;  
(2) having no criminal record and no leakage case within the past one year;  
(3) personnel engaged in secret-related businesses having the nationality of the People's Republic of China, unless otherwise provided by the state;  
(4) having a complete secrecy system and a specialized institution or personnel responsible for secrecy work;  
(5) premises, facilities, and equipment used for secret-related businesses complying with state secrecy regulations and standards;  
(6) having the professional capacity to engage in secret-related businesses;  
(7) other conditions required by secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　从事国家秘密载体制作、复制、维修、销毁，涉密信息系统集成，武器装备科研生产，或者涉密军事设施建设等涉密业务的企业事业单位，应当由保密行政管理部门单独或者会同有关部门进行保密审查，取得保密资质。  
取得保密资质的企业事业单位，不得有下列行为：  
（一）超出保密资质业务种类范围承担其他需要取得保密资质的业务；  
（二）变造、出卖、出租、出借保密资质证书；  
（三）将涉密业务转包给其他单位或者分包给无相应保密资质的单位；  
（四）其他违反保密法律法规和国家保密规定的行为。  
取得保密资质的企业事业单位实行年度自检制度，应当每年向作出准予行政许可决定的保密行政管理部门报送上一年度自检报告。

**Article 48** Enterprises and public institutions engaged in secret-related businesses such as production, reproduction, maintenance, and destruction of state secret carriers, secret-related information system integration, scientific research and production of weapons and equipment, or construction of secret-related military facilities shall undergo secrecy review by the administrative department for secrecy alone or jointly with relevant departments, and obtain secrecy qualifications.  
Enterprises and public institutions that have obtained secrecy qualifications shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) undertaking other businesses requiring secrecy qualifications beyond the scope of the business type of the secrecy qualification;  
(2) altering, selling, renting, or lending the secrecy qualification certificate;  
(3) subcontracting secret-related businesses to other units or subcontracting to units without corresponding secrecy qualifications;  
(4) other acts violating secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions.  
Enterprises and public institutions that have obtained secrecy qualifications shall implement an annual self-inspection system and shall submit the self-inspection report of the previous year each year to the administrative department for secrecy that made the decision to grant the administrative license.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　机关、单位采购涉及国家秘密的工程、货物、服务，或者委托企业事业单位从事涉密业务，应当根据国家保密规定确定密级，并符合国家保密规定和标准。机关、单位应当与有关单位、个人签订保密协议，提出保密要求，采取保密措施，实施全过程管理。  
机关、单位采购或者委托企业事业单位从事[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)第一款规定的涉密业务的，应当核验承担单位的保密资质。采购或者委托企业事业单位从事其他涉密业务的，应当核查参与单位的业务能力和保密管理能力。  
政府采购监督管理部门、保密行政管理部门应当依法加强对涉及国家秘密的工程、货物、服务采购或者其他委托开展涉密业务的监督管理。

**Article 49** Where organs or entities procure projects, goods, or services involving state secrets, or entrust enterprises or public institutions to engage in secret-related businesses, they shall determine the classification level in accordance with state secrecy regulations and comply with state secrecy regulations and standards. Organs and entities shall sign secrecy agreements with relevant units and individuals, put forward secrecy requirements, take secrecy measures, and implement whole-process management.  
Where organs or entities procure or entrust enterprises or public institutions to engage in the secret-related businesses specified in the first paragraph of Article 48 of these Regulations, they shall verify the secrecy qualifications of the undertaking units. Where they procure or entrust enterprises or public institutions to engage in other secret-related businesses, they shall examine the business capacity and secrecy management capacity of the participating units.  
The supervision and administration department for government procurement and the administrative department for secrecy shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and management of procurement of projects, goods, and services involving state secrets or other entrustments for secret-related businesses.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　机关、单位应当依法确定涉密岗位，对拟任用、聘用到涉密岗位工作的人员进行上岗前保密审查，确认其是否具备在涉密岗位工作的条件和能力。未通过保密审查的，不得任用、聘用到涉密岗位工作。  
机关、单位组织人事部门负责组织实施保密审查时，拟任用、聘用到涉密岗位工作的人员应当如实提供有关情况；需要其原工作、学习单位以及居住地有关部门和人员配合的，相关单位、部门和人员应当配合。必要时，公安机关、国家安全机关依申请协助审查。  
机关、单位组织人事部门应当定期组织复审，确保涉密人员符合涉密岗位工作要求。

**Article 50** Organs and entities shall, according to law, determine secret-related positions, and conduct pre-employment secrecy review of personnel to be appointed or employed to work in secret-related positions to confirm whether they have the conditions and capabilities to work in secret-related positions. Those who fail the secrecy review shall not be appointed or employed to work in secret-related positions.  
When the organizational and personnel department of an organ or entity organizes and implements the secrecy review, the personnel to be appointed or employed to work in secret-related positions shall truthfully provide relevant information; if the cooperation of their former work or study units and the relevant departments and personnel of their place of residence is required, the relevant units, departments, and personnel shall cooperate. When necessary, public security organs and state security organs shall assist in the review upon application.  
The organizational and personnel department of an organ or entity shall organize regular re-reviews to ensure that secret-related personnel meet the work requirements of secret-related positions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　涉密人员应当遵守保密法律法规和本机关、本单位保密制度，严格遵守保密纪律、履行保密承诺，接受保密管理，不得以任何方式泄露国家秘密。

**Article 51** Secret-related personnel shall comply with secrecy laws and regulations and the secrecy system of their own organs or entities, strictly observe secrecy discipline, fulfill secrecy commitments, accept secrecy management, and shall not disclose state secrets by any means.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　机关、单位组织人事部门会同保密工作机构负责涉密人员保密管理工作。机关、单位保密工作机构应当对涉密人员履行保密责任情况开展经常性的监督检查，会同组织人事部门加强保密教育培训。  
涉密人员出境，由机关、单位组织人事部门和保密工作机构提出意见，按照人事、外事审批权限审批。涉密人员出境应当经过保密教育培训，及时报告在境外相关情况。

**Article 52** The organizational and personnel department of an organ or entity, together with the secrecy work institution, shall be responsible for the secrecy management of secret-related personnel. The secrecy work institution of an organ or entity shall regularly carry out supervision and inspection of the performance of secrecy responsibilities by secret-related personnel, and together with the organizational and personnel department, strengthen secrecy education and training.  
Where a secret-related person leaves the country, the organizational and personnel department and the secrecy work institution of the organ or entity shall provide opinions, and approval shall be granted in accordance with the personnel and foreign affairs approval authority. A secret-related person leaving the country shall undergo secrecy education and training and promptly report relevant information while abroad.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　涉密人员离岗离职应当遵守有关法律法规规定；离岗离职前，应当接受保密提醒谈话，签订离岗离职保密承诺书。机关、单位应当开展保密教育提醒，清退国家秘密载体、涉密设备，取消涉密信息系统访问权限，确定脱密期期限。涉密人员在脱密期内就业、出境应当遵守国家保密规定。涉密人员不得利用知悉的国家秘密为有关组织、个人提供服务或者谋取利益。

**Article 53** Secret-related personnel who leave their posts or resign shall comply with relevant laws and regulations; before leaving their posts or resigning, they shall receive a secrecy reminder talk and sign a secrecy commitment letter for leaving the post or resignation. Organs and entities shall conduct secrecy education and reminders, clear and return state secret carriers and secret-related equipment, revoke access rights to secret-related information systems, and determine the duration of the declassification period. Secret-related personnel shall, during the declassification period, comply with state secrecy regulations regarding employment and leaving the country. Secret-related personnel shall not use the state secrets they know to provide services to or seek benefits for relevant organizations or individuals.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　涉密人员擅自离职或者脱密期内严重违反国家保密规定的，机关、单位应当及时报告同级保密行政管理部门，由保密行政管理部门会同有关部门依法采取处置措施。

**Article 54** Where a secret-related person leaves the post without authorization or seriously violates state secrecy regulations during the declassification period, the organ or entity shall promptly report to the administrative department for secrecy at the same level, and the administrative department for secrecy shall, together with relevant departments, take disposal measures according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　机关、单位应当建立健全涉密人员权益保障制度，按照国家有关规定给予因履行保密义务导致合法权益受到影响和限制的人员相应待遇或者补偿。

**Article 55** Organs and entities shall establish and improve the rights and interests protection system for secret-related personnel, and provide corresponding treatment or compensation to personnel whose lawful rights and interests are affected or restricted due to the fulfillment of secrecy obligations, in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　机关、单位应当向同级保密行政管理部门报送本机关、本单位年度保密工作情况。下级保密行政管理部门应当向上级保密行政管理部门报送本行政区域年度保密工作情况。

**Article 56** Organs and entities shall report their annual secrecy work situation to the administrative department for secrecy at the same level. Lower-level administrative departments for secrecy shall report the annual secrecy work situation of their administrative regions to the higher-level administrative department for secrecy.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家建立和完善保密标准体系。国家保密行政管理部门依照法律、行政法规的规定制定国家保密标准；相关学会、协会等社会团体可以制定团体标准；相关企业可以制定企业标准。

**Article 57** The state shall establish and improve the secrecy standards system. The state administrative department for secrecy shall formulate national secrecy standards in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations; relevant learned societies, associations, and other social organizations may formulate group standards; relevant enterprises may formulate enterprise standards.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　机关、单位应当对遵守保密法律法规和相关制度情况开展自查自评。保密行政管理部门依法对下列情况进行检查：  
（一）保密工作责任制落实情况；  
（二）保密制度建设情况；  
（三）保密宣传教育培训情况；  
（四）涉密人员保密管理情况；  
（五）国家秘密确定、变更、解除情况；  
（六）国家秘密载体管理情况；  
（七）信息系统和信息设备保密管理情况；  
（八）互联网使用保密管理情况；  
（九）涉密场所及保密要害部门、部位管理情况；  
（十）采购涉及国家秘密的工程、货物、服务，或者委托企业事业单位从事涉密业务管理情况；  
（十一）涉及国家秘密会议、活动管理情况；  
（十二）信息公开保密审查情况；  
（十三）其他遵守保密法律法规和相关制度的情况。

**Article 58** Organs and entities shall carry out self-inspection and self-assessment of their compliance with secrecy laws, regulations, and relevant systems. Administrative departments for secrecy shall, according to law, inspect the following circumstances:  
(1) implementation of the secrecy work responsibility system;  
(2) establishment of secrecy systems;  
(3) secrecy publicity, education, and training;  
(4) secrecy management of secret-related personnel;  
(5) determination, change, and declassification of state secrets;  
(6) management of state secret carriers;  
(7) secrecy management of information systems and information equipment;  
(8) secrecy management of Internet use;  
(9) management of secret-related premises and vital secrecy departments and parts;  
(10) management of procurement of projects, goods, and services involving state secrets, or entrustment of enterprises or public institutions to engage in secret-related businesses;  
(11) management of meetings and activities involving state secrets;  
(12) secrecy review of information disclosure;  
(13) other circumstances of compliance with secrecy laws, regulations, and relevant systems.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　保密行政管理部门依法开展保密检查和案件调查处理，查阅有关材料、询问人员、记录情况，对有关设施、设备、文件资料等登记保存，进行保密技术检测，应当遵守国家有关规定和程序。  
有关机关、单位和个人应当配合保密行政管理部门依法履行职责，如实反映情况，提供必要资料，不得弄虚作假，隐匿、销毁证据，或者以其他方式逃避、妨碍保密监督管理。  
保密行政管理部门实施保密检查后，应当出具检查意见，对需要整改的，应当明确整改内容和期限，并在一定范围内通报检查结果。

**Article 59** When administrative departments for secrecy carry out secrecy inspections and case investigations and handling according to law, review relevant materials, interview personnel, record information, register and preserve relevant facilities, equipment, documents and materials, and conduct secrecy technical testing, they shall comply with relevant state regulations and procedures.  
Relevant organs, entities, and individuals shall cooperate with administrative departments for secrecy in lawfully performing their duties, truthfully reflect the situation, provide necessary materials, and shall not engage in fraud, conceal or destroy evidence, or escape or obstruct secrecy supervision and management by other means.  
After conducting a secrecy inspection, the administrative department for secrecy shall issue an inspection opinion; if rectification is required, it shall specify the rectification content and deadline, and notify the inspection results within a certain scope.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　保密行政管理部门对涉嫌保密违法的线索和案件，应当依法及时调查处理或者组织、督促有关机关、单位调查处理；发现需要采取补救措施的，应当立即责令有关机关、单位和人员停止违法行为，采取有效补救措施。调查工作结束后，有违反保密法律法规的事实，需要追究责任的，保密行政管理部门应当依法作出行政处罚决定或者提出处理建议；涉嫌犯罪的，应当依法移送监察机关、司法机关处理。有关机关、单位应当及时将处理结果书面告知同级保密行政管理部门。

**Article 60** Administrative departments for secrecy shall, according to law, promptly investigate and handle clues and cases of suspected illegal acts concerning secrecy, or organize and urge relevant organs and entities to investigate and handle them; if remedial measures are found to be necessary, they shall immediately order the relevant organs, entities, and personnel to cease the illegal acts and take effective remedial measures. After the investigation, if there are facts of violation of secrecy laws and regulations that need to be held accountable, the administrative department for secrecy shall, according to law, make a decision on administrative penalty or propose a handling suggestion; if a crime is suspected, the matter shall be transferred to the supervisory organ or judicial organ for handling according to law. Relevant organs and entities shall promptly inform the administrative department for secrecy at the same level in writing of the handling results.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　机关、单位发现国家秘密已经泄露或者可能泄露的，应当立即采取补救措施，并在24小时内向同级保密行政管理部门和上级主管部门报告。  
地方各级保密行政管理部门接到泄密报告的，应当在24小时内逐级报至国家保密行政管理部门。  
保密行政管理部门依法受理公民对涉嫌保密违法线索的举报，并保护举报人的合法权益。

**Article 61** Where an organ or entity discovers that state secrets have been or may be leaked, it shall immediately take remedial measures and report to the administrative department for secrecy at the same level and the higher-level competent department within 24 hours.  
Upon receiving a report of leakage, local administrative departments for secrecy at all levels shall, within 24 hours, report level by level to the state administrative department for secrecy.  
Administrative departments for secrecy shall, according to law, accept citizens' reports of clues of suspected illegal acts concerning secrecy, and protect the lawful rights and interests of the reporting persons.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　保密行政管理部门收缴非法获取、持有的国家秘密载体，应当进行登记并出具清单，查清密级、数量、来源、扩散范围等，并采取相应的保密措施。  
保密行政管理部门可以提请公安、市场监督管理等有关部门协助收缴非法获取、持有的国家秘密载体，有关部门应当予以配合。

**Article 62** Where administrative departments for secrecy confiscate state secret carriers that have been illegally obtained or held, they shall register them and issue a list, ascertain the classification level, quantity, source, and scope of dissemination, and take corresponding secrecy measures.  
Administrative departments for secrecy may request the public security organ, market supervision and administration department, and other relevant departments to assist in confiscating state secret carriers that have been illegally obtained or held, and such departments shall cooperate.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　办理涉嫌泄密案件的地方各级监察机关、司法机关申请国家秘密和情报鉴定的，向所在省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门提出；办理涉嫌泄密案件的中央一级监察机关、司法机关申请国家秘密和情报鉴定的，向国家保密行政管理部门提出。  
国家秘密和情报鉴定应当根据保密法律法规和保密事项范围等进行。保密行政管理部门受理鉴定申请后，应当自受理之日起30日内出具鉴定结论；不能按期出具的，经保密行政管理部门负责人批准，可以延长30日。专家咨询等时间不计入鉴定办理期限。

**Article 63** Where local supervisory organs or judicial organs at all levels handling suspected leakage cases apply for identification of state secrets and intelligence, they shall submit the application to the administrative department for secrecy of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located; where central-level supervisory organs or judicial organs handling suspected leakage cases apply for identification of state secrets and intelligence, they shall submit the application to the state administrative department for secrecy.  
Identification of state secrets and intelligence shall be conducted in accordance with secrecy laws and regulations and the scope of secrecy matters, among others. After accepting an application for identification, the administrative department for secrecy shall issue an identification conclusion within 30 days from the date of acceptance; if it cannot be issued on time, with the approval of the head of the administrative department for secrecy, it may be extended by 30 days. The time for expert consultation, among others, shall not be counted in the time limit for handling the identification.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　设区的市级以上保密行政管理部门应当建立监测预警制度，分析研判保密工作有关情况，配备监测预警设施和相应工作力量，发现、识别、处置安全保密风险隐患，及时发出预警通报。

**Article 64** Administrative departments for secrecy at or above the level of cities divided into districts shall establish a monitoring and early warning system, analyze and assess relevant situations of secrecy work, equip themselves with monitoring and early warning facilities and corresponding working forces, discover, identify, and handle security and secrecy risks and hidden dangers, and issue early warning notices in a timely manner.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　保密行政管理部门和其他相关部门应当在保密工作中加强协调配合，及时通报情况。

**Article 65** Administrative departments for secrecy and other relevant departments shall strengthen coordination and cooperation in secrecy work and promptly exchange information.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　保密行政管理部门及其工作人员应当按照法定的职权和程序开展工作，做到严格规范公正文明执法，依法接受监督。

**Article 66** Administrative departments for secrecy and their staff members shall carry out work in accordance with statutory functions, powers, and procedures, achieve strict, standardized, fair, and civilized law enforcement, and accept supervision according to law.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　机关、单位违反保密法律法规发生泄密案件，有下列情形之一的，根据情节轻重，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未落实保密工作责任制的；  
（二）未依法确定、变更或者解除国家秘密的；  
（三）未按照要求对涉密场所以及保密要害部门、部位进行防护或者管理的；  
（四）涉密信息系统未按照规定进行测评审查而投入使用，经责令整改仍不改正的；  
（五）未经保密审查或者保密审查不严，公开国家秘密的；  
（六）委托不具备从事涉密业务条件的单位从事涉密业务的；  
（七）违反涉密人员保密管理规定的；  
（八）发生泄密案件未按照规定报告或者未及时采取补救措施的；  
（九）未依法履行涉密数据安全管理责任的；  
（十）其他违反保密法律法规的情形。  
有前款情形尚不构成犯罪，且不适用处分的人员，由保密行政管理部门督促其主管部门予以处理。

**Article 67** Where an organ or entity violates secrecy laws and regulations and a leakage case occurs, under any of the following circumstances, the directly responsible leading persons and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to implement the secrecy work responsibility system;  
(2) failing to determine, change, or declassify state secrets according to law;  
(3) failing to protect or manage secret-related premises and vital secrecy departments and parts as required;  
(4) putting a secret-related information system into use without evaluation and review as required, and failing to rectify after being ordered to do so;  
(5) disclosing state secrets without secrecy review or due to lax secrecy review;  
(6) entrusting a unit that does not meet the conditions for engaging in secret-related businesses to engage in secret-related businesses;  
(7) violating the secrecy management regulations for secret-related personnel;  
(8) failing to report a leakage case as required or failing to take timely remedial measures;  
(9) failing to fulfill the responsibility for secret-related data security management according to law;  
(10) other circumstances of violation of secrecy laws and regulations.  
Where the circumstances in the preceding paragraph do not constitute a crime and the persons are not subject to sanctions, the administrative department for secrecy shall urge their competent departments to handle the matter.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　在保密检查或者保密违法案件调查处理中，有关机关、单位及其工作人员拒不配合，弄虚作假，隐匿、销毁证据，或者以其他方式逃避、妨碍保密检查或者保密违法案件调查处理的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；不适用处分的人员，由保密行政管理部门督促其主管部门予以处理。  
企业事业单位及其工作人员协助机关、单位逃避、妨碍保密检查或者保密违法案件调查处理的，由有关主管部门依法予以处罚。

**Article 68** Where, in the course of a secrecy inspection or investigation and handling of an illegal secrecy case, the relevant organ, entity, or its staff members refuse to cooperate, engage in fraud, conceal or destroy evidence, or escape or obstruct the secrecy inspection or investigation and handling of the illegal secrecy case by other means, the directly responsible leading persons and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the persons are not subject to sanctions, the administrative department for secrecy shall urge their competent departments to handle the matter.  
Where an enterprise or public institution or its staff members assist an organ or entity in escaping or obstructing a secrecy inspection or investigation and handling of an illegal secrecy case, the relevant competent department shall impose a penalty according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　网络运营者违反保密法律法规，有下列情形之一的，由保密行政管理等部门按照各自职责分工责令限期整改，给予警告或者通报批评；情节严重的，处5万元以上50万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上10万元以下罚款：  
（一）发生泄密事件，未依法采取补救措施的；  
（二）未依法配合保密行政管理部门实施保密违法案件调查、预警事件排查的。

**Article 69** Where a network operator violates secrecy laws and regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the administrative department for secrecy and other departments shall, in accordance with their respective division of responsibilities, order rectification within a time limit, give a warning, or circulate a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the directly responsible leading persons and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to take remedial measures according to law after a leakage incident occurs;  
(2) failing to cooperate according to law with the administrative department for secrecy in carrying out investigations of illegal secrecy cases and screening of early warning incidents.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　用于保护国家秘密的安全保密产品和保密技术装备不符合国家保密规定和标准，有下列情形之一的，由保密行政管理等部门对研制生产单位给予警告或者通报批评，责令有关检测机构取消合格证书；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）研制生产单位拒不整改或者整改后仍不符合国家保密规定和标准的；  
（二）安全保密产品和保密技术装备存在重大缺陷或者重大泄密隐患的；  
（三）造成国家秘密泄露的；  
（四）其他严重危害国家秘密安全的。

**Article 70** Where security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment used to protect state secrets do not comply with state secrecy regulations and standards, and there is any of the following circumstances, the administrative department for secrecy and other departments shall give a warning or circulate a notice of criticism to the research and development and production entity, order the relevant testing institution to cancel the certificate of conformity; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) the research and development and production entity refuses to rectify or still does not comply with state secrecy regulations and standards after rectification;  
(2) the security and secrecy products and secrecy technology equipment have major defects or major hidden dangers of leakage;  
(3) causing the disclosure of state secrets;  
(4) other circumstances seriously endangering the security of state secrets.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　从事涉密业务的企业事业单位违反保密法律法规及国家保密规定的，由保密行政管理部门责令限期整改，给予警告或者通报批评；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
取得保密资质的企业事业单位，有下列情形之一的，并处暂停涉密业务、降低资质等级：  
（一）超出保密资质业务种类范围承担其他需要取得保密资质业务的；  
（二）未按照保密行政管理部门要求时限完成整改或者整改后仍不符合保密法律法规及国家保密规定的；  
（三）其他违反保密法律法规及国家保密规定，存在重大泄密隐患的。  
取得保密资质的企业事业单位，有下列情形之一的，并处吊销保密资质：  
（一）变造、出卖、出租、出借保密资质证书的；  
（二）将涉密业务转包给其他单位或者分包给无相应保密资质单位的；  
（三）发现国家秘密已经泄露或者可能泄露，未立即采取补救措施或者未按照规定时限报告的；  
（四）拒绝、逃避、妨碍保密检查的；  
（五）暂停涉密业务期间承接新的涉密业务的；  
（六）暂停涉密业务期满仍不符合保密法律法规及国家保密规定的；  
（七）发生重大泄密案件的；  
（八）其他严重违反保密法律法规及国家保密规定行为的。

**Article 71** Where an enterprise or public institution engaged in secret-related businesses violates secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions, the administrative department for secrecy shall order rectification within a time limit, give a warning, or circulate a notice of criticism; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where an enterprise or public institution that has obtained secrecy qualifications has any of the following circumstances, it shall also be subject to the suspension of secret-related businesses and reduction of qualification level:  
(1) undertaking other businesses requiring secrecy qualifications beyond the scope of the business type of the secrecy qualification;  
(2) failing to complete rectification within the time limit required by the administrative department for secrecy or still not complying with secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions after rectification;  
(3) other violations of secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions that involve major hidden dangers of leakage.  
Where an enterprise or public institution that has obtained secrecy qualifications has any of the following circumstances, its secrecy qualification shall also be revoked:  
(1) altering, selling, renting, or lending the secrecy qualification certificate;  
(2) subcontracting secret-related businesses to other units or subcontracting to units without corresponding secrecy qualifications;  
(3) discovering that state secrets have been or may be leaked, failing to take immediate remedial measures or failing to report within the prescribed time limit;  
(4) refusing, escaping, or obstructing secrecy inspections;  
(5) undertaking new secret-related businesses during the period of suspension of secret-related businesses;  
(6) still not complying with secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions after the expiration of the suspension period for secret-related businesses;  
(7) having a major leakage case occur;  
(8) other serious violations of secrecy laws, regulations, and state secrecy provisions.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　保密行政管理部门未依法履行职责，或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Where an administrative department for secrecy fails to perform its duties according to law, or abuses its powers, neglects its duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, the directly responsible leading persons and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　中央国家机关应当结合工作实际制定本行业、本领域工作秘密事项具体范围，报国家保密行政管理部门备案。  
机关、单位应当加强本机关、本单位工作秘密管理，采取技术防护、自监管等保护措施。违反有关规定造成工作秘密泄露，情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 73** Central state organs shall, in light of actual work, formulate the specific scope of work secret matters in their respective industries and fields, and report to the state administrative department for secrecy for record.  
Organs and entities shall strengthen the management of their work secrets, and adopt protective measures such as technical protection and self-supervision. Where violations of relevant provisions result in the disclosure of work secrets, if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible leading persons and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　本条例自2024年9月1日起施行。

**Article 74** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2024.
