### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国集会游行示威法》(以下简称《集会游行示威法》)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations (hereinafter referred to as the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　各级人民政府应当依法保障公民行使集会、游行、示威的权利，维护社会安定和公共秩序，保障依法举行的集会、游行、示威不受任何人以暴力、胁迫或者其他非法手段进行扰乱、冲击和破坏。

**Article 2** People's governments at all levels shall, according to law, guarantee citizens' exercise of their rights to assemble, parade and demonstrate, maintain social stability and public order, and ensure that assemblies, processions and demonstrations held according to law are not disturbed, attacked or destroyed by any person by means of violence, coercion or other illegal means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[《集会游行示威法》第二条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-2)所称露天公共场所是指公众可以自由出入的或者凭票可以进入的室外公共场所，不包括机关、团体、企业事业组织管理的内部露天场所；公共道路是指除机关、团体、企业事业组织内部的专用道路以外的道路和水路。

**Article 3** The term 'open-air public places' as mentioned in Article 2 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations means outdoor public places to which the public may have free access or may enter with tickets, excluding internal open-air places under the management of state organs, organizations, enterprises and institutions; the term 'public roads' means roads and waterways other than the special-purpose roads within state organs, organizations, enterprises and institutions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　文娱、体育活动，正常的宗教活动，传统的民间习俗活动，由各级人民政府或者有关主管部门依照有关的法律、法规和国家其他有关规定进行管理。

**Article 4** Cultural and recreational activities, sports activities, normal religious activities, and traditional folk custom activities shall be managed by the people's governments at various levels or the relevant competent departments in accordance with relevant laws, regulations and other relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[《集会游行示威法》第五条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-5)所称武器是指各种枪支、弹药以及其他可用于伤害人身的器械；管制刀具是指匕首、三棱刀、弹簧刀以及其他依法管制的刀具；爆炸物是指具有爆发力和破坏性能，瞬间可以造成人员伤亡、物品毁损的一切爆炸物品。  
前款所列武器、管制刀具、爆炸物，在集会、游行、示威中不得携带，也不得运往集会、游行、示威的举行地。

**Article 5** The term 'weapons' as mentioned in Article 5 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations means various kinds of firearms, ammunition and other instruments that may be used to injure the human body; the term 'controlled knives' means daggers, three-edged knives, switchblades and other knives controlled according to law; the term 'explosives' means all explosive articles with explosive force and destructive capability that can instantly cause casualties and damage to property.  
The weapons, controlled knives and explosives listed in the preceding paragraph shall not be carried or transported to the place where an assembly, procession or demonstration is held.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第七条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-7)第二款的规定，举行不需要申请的活动，应当维护交通秩序和社会秩序。

**Article 6** In accordance with the provisions of Article 7, paragraph 2 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, the person holding an activity that does not require an application shall maintain traffic order and social order.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　集会、游行、示威由举行地的市、县公安局、城市公安分局主管。  
游行、示威路线在同一直辖市、省辖市、自治区辖市或者省、自治区人民政府派出机关所在地区经过两个以上区、县的，由该市公安局或者省、自治区人民政府派出机关的公安处主管；在同一省、自治区行政区域内经过两个以上省辖市、自治区辖市或者省、自治区人民政府派出机关所在地区的，由所在省、自治区公安厅主管；经过两个以上省、自治区、直辖市的，由公安部主管，或者由公安部授权的省、自治区、直辖市公安机关主管。

**Article 7** Assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall be under the administration of the public security bureau of the city or county or the urban public security sub-bureau of the place where they are held.  
Where the route of a procession or demonstration passes through two or more districts or counties within the same municipality directly under the Central Government, city under the jurisdiction of a province, city under the jurisdiction of an autonomous region, or the area where the agency of the people's government of a province or autonomous region is located, the public security bureau of that city or the public security department of the agency of the people's government of the province or autonomous region shall be in charge; where the route passes through two or more cities under the jurisdiction of a province, cities under the jurisdiction of an autonomous region, or areas where the agencies of the people's government of a province or autonomous region are located within the same province or autonomous region, the public security department of that province or autonomous region shall be in charge; where the route passes through two or more provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the Ministry of Public Security shall be in charge, or the public security organ of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government authorized by the Ministry of Public Security shall be in charge.

### 第二章　集会游行示威的申请和许可

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　举行集会、游行、示威，必须有负责人。  
下列人员不得担任集会、游行、示威的负责人：  
(一)无行为能力人或者限制行为能力人；  
(二)被判处刑罚尚未执行完毕的；  
(三)正在被劳动教养的；  
(四)正在被依法采取刑事强制措施或者法律规定的其他限制人身自由措施的。

**Article 8** An assembly, procession or demonstration must have a person in charge.  
The following persons shall not serve as the person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration:  
(1) persons without capacity or with limited capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) persons who have been sentenced to criminal punishment and have not yet completed the execution thereof;  
(3) persons who are undergoing re-education through labor;  
(4) persons who are under criminal compulsory measures taken according to law or other measures restricting personal freedom as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　举行集会、游行、示威，必须由其负责人向[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的主管公安机关亲自递交书面申请；不是由负责人亲自递交书面申请的，主管公安机关不予受理。  
集会、游行、示威的负责人在递交书面申请时，应当出示本人的居民身份证或者其他有效证件，并如实填写申请登记表。

**Article 9** For holding an assembly, procession or demonstration, the person in charge must personally submit a written application to the competent public security organ as prescribed in Article 7 of these Regulations; if the written application is not submitted personally by the person in charge, the competent public security organ shall not accept it.  
When submitting the written application, the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration shall present his/her resident identity card or other valid identification, and shall truthfully fill in the application registration form.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　主管公安机关接到集会、游行、示威的申请书后，应当及时审查，在法定期限内作出许可或者不许可的书面决定；决定书应当载明许可的内容，或者不许可的理由。  
决定书应当在申请举行集会、游行、示威日的2日前送达其负责人，由负责人在送达通知书上签字。负责人拒绝签收的，送达人应当邀请其所在地基层组织的代表或者其他人作为见证人到场说明情况，在送达通知书上写明拒收的事由和日期，由见证人、送达人签名，将决定书留在负责人的住处，即视为已经送达。  
事前约定送达的具体时间、地点，集会、游行、示威的负责人不在约定的时间、地点等候而无法送达的，视为自行撤销申请；主管公安机关未按约定的时间、地点送达的，视为许可。

**Article 10** After receiving the application for an assembly, procession or demonstration, the competent public security organ shall promptly examine it and make a written decision of approval or disapproval within the statutory time limit; the decision shall specify the content of the approval, or the reasons for disapproval.  
The decision shall be served on the person in charge two days before the date of the proposed assembly, procession or demonstration, and the person in charge shall sign the service notice. If the person in charge refuses to sign, the server shall invite a representative of the grassroots organization at the person's place of residence or other persons as witnesses to be present to explain the situation, and shall state the reason for refusal and the date on the service notice, and have the witnesses and the server sign, and leave the decision at the residence of the person in charge, which shall be deemed as having been served.  
If the specific time and place of service have been agreed upon in advance, and the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration does not wait at the agreed time and place, resulting in failure to serve, it shall be deemed that the application has been withdrawn voluntarily; if the competent public security organ fails to serve at the agreed time and place, it shall be deemed that the application has been approved.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请举行集会、游行、示威要求解决具体问题的，主管公安机关应当自接到申请书之日起2日内将《协商解决具体问题通知书》分别送交集会、游行、示威的负责人和有关机关或者单位，必要时可以同时送交有关机关或者单位的上级主管部门。有关机关或者单位和申请集会、游行、示威的负责人，应当自接到公安机关的《协商解决具体问题通知书》的次日起2日内进行协商。达成协议的，协议书经双方负责人签字后，由有关机关或者单位及时送交主管公安机关；未达成协议或者自接到《协商解决具体问题通知书》的次日起2日内未进行协商，申请人坚持举行集会、游行、示威的，有关机关或者单位应当及时通知主管公安机关，主管公安机关应当依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的程序及时作出许可或者不许可的决定。  
主管公安机关通知协商解决具体问题的一方或者双方在外地的，《协商解决具体问题通知书》、双方协商达成的协议书或者未达成协议的通知，送交的开始日和在路途上的时间不计算在法定期间内。

**Article 11** Where an application for an assembly, procession or demonstration is made to solve specific problems, the competent public security organ shall, within two days from the date of receipt of the application, deliver the Notice on Negotiating the Solution of Specific Problems to the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration and the relevant organ or unit, and if necessary, may also deliver it to the superior competent department of the relevant organ or unit. The relevant organ or unit and the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration shall negotiate within two days from the day after receiving the Notice on Negotiating the Solution of Specific Problems from the public security organ. If an agreement is reached, the agreement shall be signed by the heads of both parties and then delivered promptly by the relevant organ or unit to the competent public security organ; if no agreement is reached or negotiation is not held within two days from the day after receiving the Notice on Negotiating the Solution of Specific Problems, and the applicant insists on holding the assembly, procession or demonstration, the relevant organ or unit shall promptly notify the competent public security organ, and the competent public security organ shall promptly make a decision on approval or disapproval in accordance with the procedures prescribed in Article 10 of these Regulations.  
If the competent public security organ notifies one or both parties who are in other places to negotiate the solution of specific problems, the start date of delivery and the time spent on the journey for the Notice on Negotiating the Solution of Specific Problems, the agreement or the notice of no agreement reached by the parties shall not be counted in the statutory time limit.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第十五条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-15)的规定，公民不得在其居住地以外的城市发动、组织、参加当地公民的集会、游行、示威。本条所称居住地，是指公民常住户口所在地或者向暂住地户口登记机关办理了暂住登记并持续居住半年以上的地方。

**Article 12** In accordance with the provisions of Article 15 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, citizens shall not launch, organize or participate in assemblies, processions or demonstrations of local citizens in cities other than their place of residence. The term 'place of residence' as used in this Article means the place of permanent residence of a citizen or the place where the citizen has registered with the household registration authority for temporary residence and has continuously resided for more than half a year.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　主管公安机关接到举行集会、游行、示威的申请书后，在决定许可时，有下列情形之一的，可以变更举行集会、游行、示威的时间、地点、路线，并及时通知其负责人：  
(一)举行时间在交通高峰期，可能造成交通较长时间严重堵塞的；  
(二)举行地或者行经路线正在施工，不能通行的；  
(三)举行地为渡口、铁路道口或者是毗邻国(边)境的；  
(四)所使用的机动车辆不符合道路养护规定的；  
(五)在申请举行集会、游行、示威的同一时间、地点有重大国事活动的；  
(六)在申请举行集会、游行、示威的同一时间、地点、路线已经许可他人举行集会、游行、示威的。  
主管公安机关在决定许可时，认为需要变更举行集会、游行、示威的时间、地点、路线的，应当在许可决定书中写明。  
在决定许可后，申请举行集会、游行、示威的地点、经过的路段发生自然灾害事故、治安灾害事故，尚在进行抢险救灾，举行日前不能恢复正常秩序的，主管公安机关可以变更举行集会、游行、示威的时间、地点、路线，但是应当将《集会游行示威事项变更决定书》于申请举行之日前送达集会、游行、示威的负责人。

**Article 13** After receiving the application for an assembly, procession or demonstration, when making a decision of approval, the competent public security organ may change the time, place and route of the assembly, procession or demonstration under any of the following circumstances, and shall promptly notify the person in charge:  
(1) the time of holding falls within the peak traffic hours, which may cause serious traffic congestion for a relatively long period;  
(2) the place or the route is under construction and impassable;  
(3) the place is a ferry, railway crossing or adjacent to the national border (frontier);  
(4) the motor vehicles used do not comply with road maintenance regulations;  
(5) there are major state affairs activities at the same time and place as the proposed assembly, procession or demonstration;  
(6) at the same time, place and route of the proposed assembly, procession or demonstration, permission has already been granted to another person to hold an assembly, procession or demonstration.  
When making the decision of approval, if the competent public security organ deems it necessary to change the time, place or route of the assembly, procession or demonstration, it shall state the change in the decision of approval.  
After the decision of approval is made, if a natural disaster accident or a public security accident occurs at the place or along the route of the proposed assembly, procession or demonstration, and rescue and relief operations are still underway, so that normal order cannot be restored before the date of the proposed activity, the competent public security organ may change the time, place or route of the assembly, procession or demonstration, but shall serve the Decision on Changing Matters of an Assembly, Procession or Demonstration on the person in charge before the date of the proposed activity.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　集会、游行、示威的负责人对主管公安机关不许可的决定不服的，可以自接到不许可决定书之日起3日内向同级人民政府申请复议。人民政府应当自接到复议申请书之日起3日内作出维持或者撤销主管公安机关原决定的复议决定，并将《集会游行示威复议决定书》送达集会、游行、示威的负责人，同时将副本送作出原决定的主管公安机关。人民政府作出的复议决定，主管公安机关和集会、游行、示威的负责人必须执行。

**Article 14** If the person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration does not accept the decision of disapproval by the competent public security organ, he/she may, within three days from the date of receipt of the decision of disapproval, apply for reconsideration to the people's government at the same level. The people's government shall, within three days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration, make a reconsideration decision to uphold or revoke the original decision of the competent public security organ, and shall serve the Reconsideration Decision on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations on the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration, and simultaneously send a copy to the competent public security organ that made the original decision. The reconsideration decision made by the people's government must be executed by the competent public security organ and the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　集会、游行、示威的负责人在提出申请后接到主管公安机关的通知前，撤回申请的，应当及时到受理申请的主管公安机关办理撤回手续。  
集会、游行、示威的负责人接到主管公安机关许可的通知或者人民政府许可的复议决定后，决定不举行集会、游行、示威的，应当在原定举行集会、游行、示威的时间前到原受理的主管公安机关或者人民政府交回许可决定书或者复议决定书。

**Article 15** Where the person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration withdraws his/her application after submission but before receiving the notice from the competent public security organ, he/she shall promptly go through the withdrawal formalities at the competent public security organ that accepted the application.  
After receiving the notice of approval from the competent public security organ or the reconsideration decision of approval from the people's government, if the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration decides not to hold the assembly, procession or demonstration, he/she shall return the decision of approval or the reconsideration decision to the competent public security organ that originally accepted the application or to the people's government before the originally scheduled time of the assembly, procession or demonstration.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　以国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织的名义组织或者参加集会、游行、示威的，其负责人在递交申请书时，必须同时递交该国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织负责人签署并加盖公章的证明文件。

**Article 16** Where an assembly, procession or demonstration is organized or participated in in the name of a state organ, social organization, enterprise or institution, the person in charge shall, when submitting the application, also submit a certifying document signed and affixed with the official seal by the responsible person of that state organ, social organization, enterprise or institution.

### 第三章　集会游行示威的举行

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对依法举行的集会，公安机关应当根据实际需要，派出人民警察维持秩序，保障集会的顺利举行。  
对依法举行的游行、示威，负责维持秩序的人民警察应当在主管公安机关许可举行游行、示威的路线或者地点疏导交通，防止他人扰乱、破坏游行、示威秩序，必要时还可以临时变通执行交通规则的有关规定，保障游行、示威的顺利进行。

**Article 17** For an assembly held according to law, the public security organ shall, in light of actual needs, dispatch people's police to maintain order and ensure the smooth holding of the assembly.  
For processions and demonstrations held according to law, the people's police responsible for maintaining order shall direct traffic along the route or at the place approved by the competent public security organ, prevent others from disturbing or disrupting the order of the procession or demonstration, and if necessary, may also temporarily adapt the relevant provisions on traffic rules to ensure the smooth holding of the procession or demonstration.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　负责维持交通秩序和社会秩序的人民警察，由主管公安机关指派的现场负责人统一指挥。人民警察现场负责人应当同集会、游行、示威的负责人保持联系。

**Article 18** The people's police responsible for maintaining traffic order and social order shall be under the unified command of the on-site person in charge designated by the competent public security organ. The on-site person in charge of the people's police shall maintain contact with the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　游行队伍在行进中遇有前方路段临时发生自然灾害事故、交通事故及其他治安灾害事故，或者游行队伍之间、游行队伍与围观群众之间发生严重冲突和混乱，以及突然发生其他不可预料的情况，致使游行队伍不能按照许可的路线行进时，人民警察现场负责人有权临时决定改变游行队伍的行进路线。

**Article 19** When a procession in progress encounters a natural disaster, traffic accident or other public security accident that occurs temporarily on the road ahead, or serious conflict and confusion between processions or between a procession and onlookers, or other unforeseen circumstances that make it impossible for the procession to proceed along the approved route, the on-site person in charge of the people's police shall have the authority to temporarily decide to change the route of the procession.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　主管公安机关临时设置的警戒线，应当有明显的标志，必要时还可以设置障碍物。

**Article 20** The cordon temporarily set up by the competent public security organ shall have conspicuous signs, and may also be supplemented with obstacles if necessary.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[《集会游行示威法》第二十三条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-23)所列不得举行集会、游行、示威的场所的周边距离，是指自上述场所的建筑物周边向外扩展的距离；有围墙或者栅栏的，从围墙或者栅栏的周边开始计算。不得举行集会、游行、示威的场所具体周边距离，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定并予以公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定不得举行集会、游行、示威的场所具体周边距离，应当有利于保护上述场所的安全和秩序，同时便于合法的集会、游行、示威的举行。

**Article 21** The surrounding distance of the places where assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall not be held as listed in Article 23 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations means the distance extending outward from the perimeter of the buildings of the said places; where there are walls or fences, the distance shall be calculated from the perimeter of the walls or fences. The specific surrounding distance of places where assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall not be held shall be prescribed and announced by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
When prescribing the specific surrounding distance of places where assemblies, processions and demonstrations shall not be held, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall ensure it is conducive to the protection of the safety and order of the said places and at the same time facilitate the holding of lawful assemblies, processions and demonstrations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　集会、游行、示威的负责人必须负责维持集会、游行、示威的秩序，遇有其他人加入集会、游行、示威队伍的，应当进行劝阻；对不听劝阻的，应当立即报告现场维持秩序的人民警察。人民警察接到报告后，应当予以制止。  
集会、游行、示威的负责人指定协助人民警察维持秩序的人员所佩戴的标志，应当在举行日前将式样报主管公安机关备案。

**Article 22** The person in charge of an assembly, procession or demonstration must be responsible for maintaining the order of the assembly, procession or demonstration, and when other persons join the assembly, procession or demonstration, he/she shall dissuade them; if the dissuasion is not heeded, he/she shall immediately report to the people's police maintaining order on the scene. Upon receipt of the report, the people's police shall stop them.  
The badge worn by the person designated by the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration to assist the people's police in maintaining order shall be reported to the competent public security organ for record in terms of its design before the date of the activity.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第二十七条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-27)的规定，对非法举行集会、游行、示威或者在集会、游行、示威进行中出现危害公共安全或者严重破坏社会秩序情况的，人民警察有权立即予以制止。对不听制止，需要命令解散的，应当通过广播、喊话等明确方式告知在场人员在限定时间内按照指定通道离开现场。对在限定时间内拒不离去的，人民警察现场负责人有权依照国家有关规定，命令使用警械或者采用其他警用手段强行驱散；对继续滞留现场的人员，可以强行带离现场或者立即予以拘留。

**Article 23** In accordance with the provisions of Article 27 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, where an assembly, procession or demonstration is held unlawfully, or during the course of an assembly, procession or demonstration, circumstances arise that endanger public safety or seriously disrupt social order, the people's police shall have the authority to stop it immediately. If the order to stop is not heeded and it is necessary to order the dissolution, the people's police shall inform the persons present, by means of broadcasting, loudspeaker, etc., to leave the scene within a specified time through designated passages. If they refuse to leave within the specified time, the on-site person in charge of the people's police shall have the authority, in accordance with relevant state regulations, to order the use of police equipment or other police means to forcibly disperse them; those who continue to stay on the scene may be forcibly taken away from the scene or immediately detained.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　拒绝、阻碍人民警察依法执行维持交通秩序和社会秩序职务，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本条例第五条](#art-5)的规定，尚不构成犯罪的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定予以处罚。

**Article 24** Where a person refuses or obstructs the people's police from lawfully performing their duties of maintaining traffic order and social order, and a public security penalty should be imposed, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Penalties; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a person violates the provisions of Article 5 of these Regulations, and a crime is not constituted, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Security Administration Penalties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第二十九条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-29)、第三十条的规定，需要依法追究刑事责任的，由举行地主管公安机关依照刑事诉讼法规定的程序办理。

**Article 25** Where criminal liability needs to be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of Articles 29 and 30 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, the competent public security organ at the place of the activity shall handle the matter in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第三十三条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-33)的规定予以拘留的，公安机关应当在24小时内进行讯问；需要强行遣回原地的，由行为地的主管公安机关制作《强行遣送决定书》，并派人民警察执行。负责执行的人民警察应当将被遣送人送回其居住地，连同《强行遣送决定书》交给被遣送人居住地公安机关，由居住地公安机关依法处理。

**Article 26** Where detention is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, the public security organ shall conduct interrogation within 24 hours; if the person needs to be forcibly sent back to his/her original place, the competent public security organ at the place where the act occurred shall make a Decision on Forcible Repatriation and dispatch people's police to execute it. The people's police responsible for execution shall escort the repatriated person back to his/her place of residence and deliver the Decision on Forcible Repatriation together with the person to the public security organ at the person's place of residence, which shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　依照[《集会游行示威法》第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-28)、第三十条以及[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)的规定，对当事人给予治安管理处罚的，依照治安管理处罚法规定的程序，由行为地公安机关决定和执行。被处罚人对处罚决定不服的，可以申请复议；对上一级公安机关的复议决定不服的，可以依照法律规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 27** Where a public security penalty is imposed on a party in accordance with the provisions of Articles 28 and 30 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations and Article 24 of these Regulations, the decision and execution shall be made by the public security organ at the place of the act in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Law on Public Security Administration Penalties. If the person subjected to the penalty does not accept the penalty decision, he/she may apply for reconsideration; if he/she does not accept the reconsideration decision of the public security organ at the next higher level, he/she may bring a lawsuit to the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对于依照[《集会游行示威法》第二十七条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国集会游行示威法.md#art-27)的规定被强行带离现场或者立即予以拘留的，公安机关应当在24小时以内进行讯问。不需要追究法律责任的，可以令其具结悔过后释放；需要追究法律责任的，依照有关法律规定办理。

**Article 28** For a person who is forcibly taken away from the scene or immediately detained in accordance with the provisions of Article 27 of the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations, the public security organ shall conduct interrogation within 24 hours. If no legal liability needs to be investigated, the person may be released after making a statement of repentance; if legal liability needs to be investigated, the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在举行集会、游行、示威的过程中，破坏公私财物或者侵害他人身体造成伤亡的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 29** Where, during the course of an assembly, procession or demonstration, public or private property is damaged or another person's body is injured causing casualties, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　外国人在中国境内举行集会、游行、示威，适用本条例的规定。  
外国人在中国境内要求参加中国公民举行的集会、游行、示威的，集会、游行、示威的负责人在申请书中应当载明；未经主管公安机关批准，不得参加。

**Article 30** Where an alien holds an assembly, procession or demonstration within the territory of China, the provisions of these Regulations shall apply.  
Where an alien requests to participate in an assembly, procession or demonstration held by Chinese citizens within the territory of China, the person in charge of the assembly, procession or demonstration shall state it in the application; without the approval of the competent public security organ, the alien shall not participate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市的人民代表大会常务委员会根据《集会游行示威法》制定的实施办法适用于本行政区域；与本条例相抵触的，以本条例为准。

**Article 31** The implementation rules formulated by the standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the Law on Assemblies, Processions and Demonstrations shall apply within their respective administrative regions; in case of conflict with these Regulations, these Regulations shall prevail.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例具体应用中的问题由公安部负责解释。

**Article 32** Questions arising from the application of these Regulations shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 33** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
