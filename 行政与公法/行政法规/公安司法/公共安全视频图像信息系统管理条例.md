# 公共安全视频图像信息系统管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-01-13  
**生效日期**：2025-04-01

> 第二条　本条例所称公共安全视频图像信息系统（以下简称公共安全视频系统），是指通过在公共场所安装图像采集设备及相关设施，对涉及公共安全的区域进行视频图像信息收集、传输、显示、存储的系统。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公共安全视频系统管理工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻党和国家路线方针政策和决策部署。  
建设、使用公共安全视频系统，应当遵守法律法规，坚持统筹规划、合理适度、标准引领、安全可控，不得危害国家安全、公共利益，不得损害个人、组织的合法权益。

**Article 3** The management of public security video systems shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China and implement the lines, principles, policies, and decisions of the Party and the State.  
The construction and use of public security video systems shall comply with laws and regulations, adhere to overall planning, rational moderation, standard guidance, safety and controllability, shall not endanger national security or public interests, and shall not harm the lawful rights and interests of individuals or organizations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励和支持视频图像领域的技术创新与发展，建立和完善相关标准体系，支持有关行业组织依法加强行业自律，提高公共安全保障能力和个人信息保护水平。

**Article 4** The State encourages and supports technological innovation and development in the field of video images, establishes and improves relevant standard systems, and supports relevant industry organizations to strengthen industry self-discipline according to law, so as to improve public security protection capabilities and the level of personal information protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院公安部门负责全国公共安全视频系统建设、使用的指导和监督管理工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责公共安全视频系统建设、使用的相关管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关负责本行政区域内公共安全视频系统建设、使用的指导和监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责公共安全视频系统建设、使用的相关管理工作。

**Article 5** The public security department of the State Council shall be responsible for the guidance and supervision of the construction and use of public security video systems nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant management of the construction and use of public security video systems.  
The public security organs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the guidance and supervision of the construction and use of public security video systems within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant management of the construction and use of public security video systems.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对公共安全视频系统建设的统筹规划，充分利用现有资源，避免重复建设。

**Article 6** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the overall planning for the construction of public security video systems, make full use of existing resources, and avoid duplication of construction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　城乡主要路段、行政区域道路边界、桥梁、隧道、地下通道、广场、治安保卫重点单位周边区域等公共场所的公共安全视频系统，由县级以上地方人民政府按照建设规划组织有关部门建设，纳入公共基础设施管理，建设、维护经费列入本级政府预算。  
下列公共场所涉及公共安全区域的公共安全视频系统，由对相应场所负有经营管理责任的单位按照相关标准建设，安装图像采集设备的重点部位由县级以上地方人民政府各有关部门按照职责分工指导确定：  
（一）商贸中心、会展中心、旅游景区、文化体育娱乐场所、教育机构、医疗机构、政务服务大厅、公园、公共停车场等人员聚集场所；  
（二）出境入境口岸（通道）、机场、港口客运站、通航建筑物、铁路客运站、汽车客运站、城市轨道交通站等交通枢纽；  
（三）客运列车、营运载客汽车、城市轨道交通车辆、客运船舶等大中型公共交通工具；  
（四）高速公路、普通国省干线的服务区。  
在前两款规定的场所、区域内安装图像采集设备及相关设施，应当为维护公共安全所必需，除前两款规定的政府有关部门、负有经营管理责任的单位（以下统称公共安全视频系统管理单位）外，其他任何单位或者个人不得安装。

**Article 7** The public security video systems in public places such as main urban and rural sections, administrative regional road boundaries, bridges, tunnels, underpasses, squares, and surrounding areas of key public security units shall be constructed by the relevant departments organized by local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with construction plans, incorporated into public infrastructure management, and the construction and maintenance costs shall be included in the budget of the same level government.  
The public security video systems in the following public places involving public security areas shall be constructed by the units responsible for the operation and management of the corresponding premises in accordance with relevant standards, and the key parts for installing image capture equipment shall be guided and determined by the relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level according to their respective functions and duties:  
(1) commercial centers, exhibition centers, tourist attractions, cultural, sports and entertainment venues, educational institutions, medical institutions, government service halls, parks, public parking lots, and other places where people gather;  
(2) ports (channels) for entry and exit, airports, port passenger stations, navigable structures, railway passenger stations, bus passenger stations, urban rail transit stations, and other transportation hubs;  
(3) large and medium-sized public transport vehicles such as passenger trains, operating passenger buses, urban rail transit vehicles, and passenger ships;  
(4) service areas of expressways and ordinary national and provincial trunk highways.  
The installation of image capture equipment and related facilities in the places and areas specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall be necessary for maintaining public security. Except for the government departments and units responsible for operation and management specified in the preceding two paragraphs (hereinafter collectively referred to as the management units of public security video systems), no other unit or individual may install them.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　禁止在公共场所的下列区域、部位安装图像采集设备及相关设施：  
（一）旅馆、饭店、宾馆、招待所、民宿等经营接待食宿场所的客房或者包间内部；  
（二）学生宿舍的房间内部，或者单位为内部人员提供住宿、休息服务的房间内部；  
（三）公共的浴室、卫生间、更衣室、哺乳室、试衣间的内部；  
（四）安装图像采集设备后能够拍摄、窥视、窃听他人隐私的其他区域、部位。  
对上述区域、部位负有经营管理责任的单位或者个人，应当加强日常管理和检查，发现在前款所列区域、部位安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，应当立即报告所在地公安机关处理。

**Article 8** It is prohibited to install image capture equipment and related facilities in the following areas or parts of public places:  
(1) inside guest rooms or private rooms of hotels, restaurants, inns, hostels, homestays, and other places that provide accommodation and meals;  
(2) inside dormitory rooms of students, or inside rooms provided by units for internal personnel accommodation or rest services;  
(3) inside public bathrooms, toilets, changing rooms, nursing rooms, and fitting rooms;  
(4) other areas or parts where installation of image capture equipment may take pictures, pry into, or eavesdrop on the privacy of others.  
Units or individuals responsible for the operation and management of the above areas or parts shall strengthen daily management and inspection, and if they discover the installation of image capture equipment and related facilities in the areas or parts listed in the preceding paragraph, they shall immediately report to the local public security organ for handling.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定之外的其他公共场所安装图像采集设备及相关设施，应当为维护公共安全所必需，仅限于对该场所负有安全防范义务的单位或者个人安装，其他任何单位或者个人不得安装。  
依照前款规定安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，应当遵守[本条例除第十一条](#art-11)、第十四条、第十五条、第十六条第二款、第十七条规定的强制性要求之外的其他各项规定。

**Article 9** The installation of image capture equipment and related facilities in other public places other than those specified in Article 7 of these Regulations shall be necessary for maintaining public security, and shall be limited to units or individuals that have the obligation of safety prevention for the said places. No other unit or individual may install them.  
Those that install image capture equipment and related facilities in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall comply with all other provisions of these Regulations except for the mandatory requirements stipulated in Articles 11, 14, 15, paragraph 2 of Article 16, and Article 17.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　依照本条例安装图像采集设备及相关设施，位于军事禁区、军事管理区以及国家机关等涉密单位周边的，应当事先征得相关涉密单位的同意。

**Article 10** Where image capture equipment and related facilities installed in accordance with these Regulations are located in the vicinity of military restricted zones, military administrative zones, and state organs and other classified units, consent shall be obtained from the relevant classified units in advance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　公共安全视频系统管理单位应当按照相关标准建设公共安全视频系统，开展设计、施工、检验、验收等工作，并依法保存、管理相关档案资料。

**Article 11** The management units of public security video systems shall construct public security video systems in accordance with relevant standards, carry out design, construction, inspection, acceptance and other tasks, and preserve and manage relevant archives and materials according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公共安全视频系统采用的产品、服务应当符合国家标准的强制性要求。产品、服务的提供者不得设置恶意程序；发现其产品、服务存在安全缺陷、漏洞等风险时，应当立即采取补救措施，按照规定及时告知用户并向有关主管部门报告。

**Article 12** The products and services adopted in public security video systems shall comply with the mandatory requirements of national standards. Providers of products and services shall not set up malicious programs; upon discovering that their products or services have risks such as security defects or vulnerabilities, they shall immediately take remedial measures, promptly inform users in accordance with regulations, and report to the relevant competent authorities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公共安全视频系统管理单位应当按照维护公共安全所必需、注重保护个人隐私和个人信息权益的要求，合理确定图像采集设备的安装位置、角度和采集范围，并设置显著的提示标识。未设置显著提示标识的，由公安机关责令改正。

**Article 13** The management units of public security video systems shall, in accordance with the requirements of being necessary for maintaining public security and giving due regard to protecting personal privacy and personal information rights and interests, reasonably determine the installation positions, angles, and collection ranges of image capture equipment, and set up conspicuous warning signs. If no conspicuous warning signs are set up, the public security organ shall order rectification.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公共安全视频系统管理单位应当在系统投入使用之日起30日内，将单位基本情况、公共安全视频系统建设位置、图像采集设备数量及类型、视频图像信息存储期限等基本信息，向所在地县级人民政府公安机关备案。本条例施行前已经启用的，应当在本条例施行之日起90日内备案。公共安全视频系统备案事项发生变化的，应当及时办理备案变更。  
公共安全视频系统管理单位应当对备案信息的真实性负责。  
公安机关应当加强信息化建设，为公共安全视频系统管理单位办理备案提供便利，能够通过部门间信息共享获得的备案信息，不要求当事人提供。

**Article 14** The management units of public security video systems shall, within 30 days from the date of putting the system into use, submit basic information including the basic circumstances of the unit, the construction location of the public security video system, the quantity and type of image capture equipment, and the storage period of video image information, to the public security organ of the people's government at the county level where the unit is located for record. If the system has already been put into use before the coming into force of these Regulations, it shall be filed within 90 days from the date of coming into force of these Regulations. If the recorded items of the public security video system change, the record shall be changed in a timely manner.  
The management units of public security video systems shall be responsible for the authenticity of the recorded information.  
The public security organ shall strengthen informatization construction to facilitate the filing by the management units of public security video systems, and shall not require the party to provide the record information that can be obtained through inter-departmental information sharing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公共安全视频系统管理单位应当履行系统运行安全管理职责，履行网络安全、数据安全和个人信息保护义务，建立健全管理制度，完善防攻击、防入侵、防病毒、防篡改、防泄露等安全技术措施，定期维护设备设施，保障系统连续、稳定、安全运行，确保视频图像信息的原始完整。  
公共安全视频系统管理单位委托他人运营的，应当通过签订安全保密协议等方式，约定前款规定的网络安全、数据安全和个人信息保护义务并监督受托方履行。

**Article 15** The management units of public security video systems shall perform the duties of system operation security management, fulfill the obligations of network security, data security and personal information protection, establish and improve management systems, perfect security technical measures such as anti-attack, anti-intrusion, anti-virus, anti-tampering and anti-leakage, regularly maintain equipment and facilities, ensure the continuous, stable and safe operation of the system, and ensure the original integrity of video image information.  
If a management unit of a public security video system entrusts others with operation, it shall, by means of signing a security and confidentiality agreement or other means, stipulate the obligations of network security, data security and personal information protection as specified in the preceding paragraph and supervise the performance of the entrusted party.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公共安全视频系统管理单位使用视频图像信息，应当遵守法律法规，依法保护国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，不得滥用、泄露。  
公共安全视频系统管理单位应当采取下列措施，防止滥用、泄露视频图像信息：  
（一）建立系统监看、管理等重要岗位人员的入职审查、保密教育、岗位培训等管理制度；  
（二）采取授权管理、访问控制等技术措施，严格规范内部人员对视频图像信息的查阅、处理；  
（三）建立信息调用登记制度，如实记录查阅、调取视频图像信息的事由、内容及调用人员的单位、姓名等信息；  
（四）其他防止滥用、泄露视频图像信息的措施。

**Article 16** The management units of public security video systems, when using video image information, shall comply with laws and regulations, protect state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy and personal information according to law, and shall not abuse or disclose them.  
The management units of public security video systems shall take the following measures to prevent abuse or disclosure of video image information:  
(1) establish management systems such as pre-employment review, confidentiality education, and job training for personnel in important positions such as system monitoring and management;  
(2) adopt technical measures such as authorization management and access control to strictly regulate internal personnel's access to and processing of video image information;  
(3) establish an information call registration system to truthfully record the reasons and content of accessing or retrieving video image information, as well as the unit and name of the person requesting access;  
(4) other measures to prevent abuse or disclosure of video image information.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公共安全视频系统收集的视频图像信息应当保存不少于30日；30日后，对已经实现处理目的的视频图像信息，应当予以删除。法律、行政法规对视频图像信息保存期限另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 17** Video image information collected by public security video systems shall be kept for not less than 30 days; after 30 days, video image information for which the processing purpose has been achieved shall be deleted. If laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the retention period of video image information, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　为公共安全视频系统提供网络传输服务的电信业务经营者，应当加强对视频图像信息传输的安全管理，依照法律、行政法规的规定和国家标准的强制性要求，采取技术措施和其他必要措施，保障网络安全、稳定运行，维护数据的完整性、保密性和可用性。

**Article 18** Telecommunications service operators that provide network transmission services for public security video systems shall strengthen the security management of video image information transmission, and in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the mandatory requirements of national standards, take technical measures and other necessary measures to ensure the safe and stable operation of the network, and maintain the integrity, confidentiality and availability of data.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　接受委托承担公共安全视频系统设计、施工、检验、验收、维护等工作的单位及其工作人员，应当对接触到的视频图像信息和相关档案资料予以保密，不得用于与受托工作无关的活动，不得擅自留存、加工、泄露或者向他人提供。

**Article 19** Units and their staff that are entrusted to undertake the design, construction, inspection, acceptance, maintenance or other work of public security video systems shall keep confidential the video image information and relevant archives and materials that they come into contact with, shall not use them for activities unrelated to the entrusted work, and shall not retain, process, disclose, or provide them to others without authorization.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家机关为履行执法办案、处置突发事件等法定职责，查阅、调取公共安全视频系统收集的视频图像信息，应当依照法律、行政法规规定的权限、程序进行，并严格遵守保密规定，不得超出履行法定职责所必需的范围和限度。

**Article 20** State organs, in order to perform statutory duties such as law enforcement, handling of cases, or responding to emergencies, may access or retrieve video image information collected by public security video systems, and shall do so in accordance with the authority and procedures prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, strictly abide by confidentiality provisions, and shall not exceed the scope and limits necessary for performing statutory duties.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　为了保护自然人的生命健康、财产安全，经公共安全视频系统管理单位同意，本人、近亲属或者其他负有监护、看护、代管责任的人可以查阅关联的视频图像信息；对获悉的涉及公共安全、个人隐私和个人信息的视频图像信息，不得非法对外提供或者公开传播。

**Article 21** In order to protect the life, health, and property safety of natural persons, with the consent of the management unit of the public security video system, the person concerned, close relatives, or other persons with guardianship, care, or custodial responsibilities may access the relevant video image information; they shall not illegally provide or publicly disseminate the video image information that they have learned, which involves public security, personal privacy, or personal information.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公共安全视频系统收集的视频图像信息被依法用于公开传播，可能损害个人、组织合法权益的，应当对涉及的人脸、机动车号牌等敏感个人信息，以及法人、非法人组织的名称、营业执照等信息采取严格保护措施。

**Article 22** Where video image information collected by public security video systems is used for public dissemination according to law and may harm the lawful rights and interests of individuals or organizations, strict protection measures shall be taken for the sensitive personal information involved, such as faces and motor vehicle license plates, as well as information such as the names and business licenses of legal persons and unincorporated organizations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　任何单位或者个人不得实施下列行为：  
（一）违反法律法规规定，对外提供或者公开传播公共安全视频系统收集的视频图像信息；  
（二）擅自改动、迁移、拆除依据[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定安装的图像采集设备及相关设施，或者以喷涂、遮挡等方式妨碍其正常运行；  
（三）非法侵入、控制公共安全视频系统；  
（四）非法获取公共安全视频系统中的数据；  
（五）非法删除、隐匿、修改、增加公共安全视频系统中的数据或者应用程序；  
（六）其他妨碍公共安全视频系统正常运行，危害网络安全、数据安全、个人信息安全的行为。

**Article 23** No entity or individual shall commit any of the following acts:  
(1) Providing or publicly disseminating video image information collected by the public security video system to external parties in violation of laws and regulations;  
(2) Without authorization, altering, relocating, or dismantling the image capture equipment and related facilities installed in accordance with Article 7 of these Regulations, or obstructing their normal operation by means such as spray painting or covering;  
(3) Illegally intruding into or controlling the public security video system;  
(4) Illegally obtaining data from the public security video system;  
(5) Illegally deleting, concealing, modifying, or adding data or applications in the public security video system;  
(6) Other acts that hinder the normal operation of the public security video system and endanger cybersecurity, data security, or personal information security.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公安机关对公共安全视频系统的建设、使用情况实施监督检查，有关单位或者个人应当予以协助、配合。  
有关单位或者个人发现有违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)第三款、第八条第一款、第九条第一款规定安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，可以向公安机关举报。公安机关应当依法及时处理。

**Article 24** The public security organ shall supervise and inspect the construction and use of the public security video system, and relevant entities or individuals shall provide assistance and cooperation.  
Where a relevant entity or individual discovers the installation of image capture equipment and related facilities in violation of the provisions of Article 7, paragraph 3, Article 8, paragraph 1, or Article 9, paragraph 1 of these Regulations, they may report the matter to the public security organ. The public security organ shall handle the matter in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　公安机关应当严格执行内部监督制度，对其工作人员履行公共安全视频系统建设、使用职责情况进行监督。  
公安机关及其工作人员在履行公共安全视频系统建设、使用、监督管理职责过程中，有违反本条例规定，或者其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，任何单位或者个人有权检举、控告。

**Article 25** The public security organ shall strictly implement the internal supervision system and supervise the performance of its staff members in fulfilling their duties regarding the construction and use of the public security video system.  
Where a public security organ or any of its staff members, in the course of performing their duties of construction, use, supervision, and management of the public security video system, violates the provisions of these Regulations or commits any other act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, any entity or individual shall have the right to report and accuse such act.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)第三款、第九条第一款规定安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，由公安机关责令限期改正，并删除所收集的视频图像信息；拒不改正的，没收相关设备设施，对违法个人并处5000元以下罚款，对违法单位并处2万元以下罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以下罚款。

**Article 26** Where an entity or individual installs image capture equipment and related facilities in violation of the provisions of Article 7, paragraph 3, or Article 9, paragraph 1 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall order rectification within a specified period and shall delete the collected video image information; if the violation is refused to be rectified, the relevant equipment and facilities shall be confiscated, and a concurrent fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal individual, a concurrent fine of not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal entity, and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons thereof.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)第一款规定安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，由公安机关没收相关设备设施，删除所收集的视频图像信息，对违法个人并处5000元以上1万元以下罚款，对违法单位并处1万元以上2万元以下罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上1万元以下罚款；偷窥、偷拍、窃听他人隐私，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
对相应区域、部位负有经营管理责任的单位或者个人未履行[本条例第八条](#art-8)第二款规定的日常管理和检查义务的，由公安机关责令改正；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，对违法个人处5000元以上1万元以下罚款，对违法单位处1万元以上2万元以下罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上1万元以下罚款，并通报有关主管部门根据情节轻重责令暂停相关业务或者停业整顿、吊销相关业务许可或者吊销营业执照。

**Article 27** Where an entity or individual installs image capture equipment and related facilities in violation of the provisions of Article 8, paragraph 1 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall confiscate the relevant equipment and facilities, delete the collected video image information, impose a concurrent fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the illegal individual, a concurrent fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the illegal entity, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons thereof; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration by peeping, secretly photographing, or eavesdropping on the privacy of others, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where an entity or individual that bears the responsibility of operation and management of the corresponding area or location fails to fulfill the daily management and inspection obligations as provided in Article 8, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall order rectification; if rectification is refused or if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal individual, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal entity, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons thereof; and the matter shall be reported to the relevant competent authority, which shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, order the suspension of relevant business or business consolidation, or revoke the relevant business license or the business license.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　未依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定征得相关涉密单位同意安装图像采集设备及相关设施的，由公安机关没收相关设备设施，删除所收集的视频图像信息，对违法个人并处5000元以上1万元以下罚款，对违法单位并处1万元以上2万元以下罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以上1万元以下罚款；非法获取国家秘密、军事秘密的，依照有关法律的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where an entity or individual installs image capture equipment and related facilities without obtaining the consent of the relevant classified entity as provided in Article 10 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall confiscate the relevant equipment and facilities, delete the collected video image information, impose a concurrent fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the illegal individual, a concurrent fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan on the illegal entity, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan on the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons thereof; if state secrets or military secrets are illegally obtained, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　未依照[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定备案或者提供虚假备案信息的，由公安机关责令限期改正；拒不改正的，处1万元以下罚款。

**Article 29** Where an entity or individual fails to file for record or provides false record information as provided in Article 14 of these Regulations, the public security organ shall order rectification within a specified period; if rectification is refused, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第二项规定擅自改动、迁移、拆除图像采集设备及相关设施的，由公安机关责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正或者造成严重后果的，对违法个人处5000元以下罚款，对违法单位处5000元以上1万元以下罚款，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处5000元以下罚款。

**Article 30** Where an entity or individual, in violation of Article 23, item (2) of these Regulations, without authorization alters, relocates, or dismantles image capture equipment and related facilities, the public security organ shall order rectification and give a warning; if rectification is refused or if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal individual, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the illegal entity, and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the person directly in charge and other directly liable persons thereof.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，未履行网络安全、数据安全和个人信息保护义务，或者非法对外提供、公开传播视频图像信息的，依照《中华人民共和国网络安全法》、《中华人民共和国数据安全法》、《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》的规定给予处罚；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 31** Where an entity or individual, in violation of these Regulations, fails to fulfill the obligations of cybersecurity, data security, and personal information protection, or illegally provides or publicly disseminates video image information to external parties, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公安机关及其工作人员在履行公共安全视频系统建设、使用、监督管理职责过程中，违反本条例规定，或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，由上级公安机关或者有关主管部门责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
其他国家机关及其工作人员在履行公共安全视频系统建设、使用、相关管理职责过程中，违反本条例规定，或者在依照[本条例第二十条](#art-20)规定查阅、调取视频图像信息过程中，有滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，由其上级机关或者有关主管部门责令改正，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 32** Where a public security organ or any of its staff members, in the course of performing their duties of construction, use, supervision, and management of the public security video system, violates the provisions of these Regulations or commits any other act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, the superior public security organ or the relevant competent authority shall order rectification, and the responsible leaders and directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any other state organ or any of its staff members, in the course of performing their duties of construction, use, and related management of the public security video system, violates the provisions of these Regulations, or commits any act of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in the process of consulting or retrieving video image information in accordance with Article 20 of these Regulations, the superior organ or the relevant competent authority shall order rectification, and the responsible leaders and directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在非公共场所安装图像采集设备及相关设施，不得危害公共安全或者侵犯他人的合法权益，对收集到的涉及公共安全、个人隐私和个人信息的视频图像信息，不得非法对外提供或者公开传播。  
违反前款规定的，依照[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定给予处罚。

**Article 33** Where image capture equipment and related facilities are installed in non-public places, they shall not endanger public security or infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others, and the collected video image information involving public security, personal privacy, and personal information shall not be illegally provided to external parties or publicly disseminated.  
Where the preceding paragraph is violated, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with Article 31 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例自2025年4月1日起施行。

**Article 34** These Regulations shall come into force as of April 1, 2025.
