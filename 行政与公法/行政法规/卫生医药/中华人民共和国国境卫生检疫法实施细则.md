# 中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法

### 第一章　一般规定

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法》(以下称《国境卫生检疫法》)的规定，制定本细则。

**Article 1** These Rules are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　《国境卫生检疫法》和本细则所称：  
“查验”指国境卫生检疫机关(以下称卫生检疫机关)实施的医学检查和卫生检查。  
“染疫人”指正在患检疫传染病的人，或者经卫生检疫机关初步诊断，认为已经感染检疫传染病或者已经处于检疫传染病潜伏期的人。  
“染疫嫌疑人”指接触过检疫传染病的感染环境，并且可能传播检疫传染病的人。  
“隔离”指将染疫人收留在指定的处所，限制其活动并进行治疗，直到消除传染病传播的危险。  
“留验”指将染疫嫌疑人收留在指定的处所进行诊察和检验。  
“就地诊验”指一个人在卫生检疫机关指定的期间，到就近的卫生检疫机关或者其他医疗卫生单位去接受诊察和检验；或者卫生检疫机关、其他医疗卫生单位到该人员的居留地，对其进行诊察和检验。  
“运输设备”指货物集装箱。  
“卫生处理”指隔离、留验和就地诊验等医学措施，以及消毒、除鼠、除虫等卫生措施。  
“传染病监测”指对特定环境、人群进行流行病学、血清学、病原学、临床症状以及其他有关影响因素的调查研究，预测有关传染病的发生、发展和流行。  
“卫生监督”指执行卫生法规和卫生标准所进行的卫生检查、卫生鉴定、卫生评价和采样检验。  
“交通工具”指船舶、航空器、列车和其他车辆。  
“国境口岸”指国际通航的港口、机场、车站、陆地边境和国界江河的关口。

**Article 2** For the purposes of the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law and these Rules:  
"Inspection" means the medical inspection and sanitary inspection carried out by the frontier health and quarantine authorities (hereinafter referred to as the health and quarantine authorities).  
"Person with plague" means a person who is suffering from a quarantinable infectious disease, or who, after preliminary diagnosis by the health and quarantine authorities, is considered to have been infected with a quarantinable infectious disease or to be in the incubation period of a quarantinable infectious disease.  
"Suspect of plague" means a person who has been exposed to an infected environment of a quarantinable infectious disease and who may spread the quarantinable infectious disease.  
"Isolation" means detaining a person with plague in a designated place, restricting his or her activities and providing treatment until the danger of the spread of the infectious disease is eliminated.  
"Detention for observation" means detaining a suspect of plague in a designated place for diagnosis and examination.  
"Medical surveillance at designated place" means that a person shall, within the period designated by the health and quarantine authorities, go to the nearest health and quarantine authority or other medical and health unit to receive diagnosis and examination; or the health and quarantine authorities or other medical and health units shall go to the place of residence of such person to conduct diagnosis and examination.  
"Transport equipment" means cargo containers.  
"Sanitary treatment" means medical measures such as isolation, detention for observation and medical surveillance at designated place, as well as sanitary measures such as disinfection, deratting, and disinsection.  
"Surveillance of infectious diseases" means the investigation and study of specific environments and populations in terms of epidemiology, serology, etiology, clinical symptoms and other relevant influencing factors, to predict the occurrence, development and prevalence of relevant infectious diseases.  
"Sanitary supervision" means the sanitary inspection, sanitary expert evaluation, sanitary assessment and sampling inspection carried out in implementing sanitary laws and regulations and sanitary standards.  
"Conveyance" means ships, aircraft, trains and other vehicles.  
"Frontier port" means the international ports open to navigation, airports, railway stations, land borders and the border crossings of boundary rivers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　卫生检疫机关在国境口岸工作的范围，是指为国境口岸服务的涉外宾馆、饭店、俱乐部，为入境、出境交通工具提供饮食、服务的单位和对入境、出境人员、交通工具、集装箱和货物实施检疫、监测、卫生监督的场所。

**Article 3** The scope of work of the health and quarantine authorities at frontier ports shall include foreign-related hotels, restaurants and clubs serving the frontier ports, units that provide food and services for inbound and outbound conveyances, and places where quarantine, surveillance and sanitary supervision are carried out on inbound and outbound persons, conveyances, containers and goods.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　入境、出境的人员、交通工具和集装箱，以及可能传播检疫传染病的行李、货物、邮包等，均应当按照本细则的规定接受检疫，经卫生检疫机关许可，方准入境或者出境。

**Article 4** Inbound and outbound persons, conveyances and containers, as well as luggage, goods and postal parcels that may spread quarantinable infectious diseases, shall all undergo quarantine inspection in accordance with the provisions of these Rules, and may enter or leave the country only after obtaining permission from the health and quarantine authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　卫生检疫机关发现染疫人时，应当立即将其隔离，防止任何人遭受感染，并按照本细则第八章的规定处理。  
卫生检疫机关发现染疫嫌疑人时，应当按照本细则第八章的规定处理。但对第八章规定以外的其他病种染疫嫌疑人，可以从该人员离开感染环境的时候算起，实施不超过该传染病最长潜伏期的就地诊验或者留验以及其他的卫生处理。

**Article 5** When the health and quarantine authorities discover a person with plague, they shall immediately isolate such person to prevent anyone from being infected, and shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VIII of these Rules.  
When the health and quarantine authorities discover a suspect of plague, they shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of Chapter VIII of these Rules. However, for suspects of plagues of diseases other than those provided for in Chapter VIII, the health and quarantine authorities may, from the time such person leaves the infected environment, carry out medical surveillance at designated place or detention for observation not exceeding the longest incubation period of the infectious disease, as well as other sanitary treatment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　卫生检疫机关应当阻止染疫人、染疫嫌疑人出境，但是对来自国外并且在到达时受就地诊验的人，本人要求出境的，可以准许出境；如果乘交通工具出境，检疫医师应当将这种情况在出境检疫证上签注，同时通知交通工具负责人采取必要的预防措施。

**Article 6** The health and quarantine authorities shall prevent persons with plague and suspects of plague from leaving the country; however, for persons who come from abroad and are subject to medical surveillance at designated place upon arrival, if they themselves request to leave the country, they may be permitted to leave; if they leave the country by conveyance, the quarantine physician shall make an endorsement to that effect on the exit quarantine certificate, and shall simultaneously notify the person in charge of the conveyance to take necessary preventive measures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在国境口岸以及停留在该场所的入境、出境交通工具上，所有非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明的尸体，必须经卫生检疫机关查验，并签发尸体移运许可证后，方准移运。

**Article 7** All corpses that die of unknown causes and not from accidental injury at frontier ports or on inbound and outbound conveyances staying at such places shall be inspected by the health and quarantine authorities, and may be transported only after a permit for transportation of the corpse is issued.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　来自国内疫区的交通工具，或者在国内航行中发现检疫传染病、疑似检疫传染病，或者有人非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明的，交通工具负责人应当向到达的国境口岸卫生检疫机关报告，接受临时检疫。

**Article 8** If a conveyance comes from an epidemic area within the country, or if a quarantinable infectious disease or suspected quarantinable infectious disease is discovered during domestic navigation, or if a person dies of unknown cause not from accidental injury, the person in charge of the conveyance shall report to the health and quarantine authority at the frontier port of arrival and shall undergo provisional quarantine.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在国内或者国外检疫传染病大流行的时候，国务院卫生行政部门应当立即报请国务院决定采取下列检疫措施的一部或者全部：  
(一)下令封锁陆地边境、国界江河的有关区域；  
(二)指定某些物品必须经过消毒、除虫，方准由国外运进或者由国内运出；  
(三)禁止某些物品由国外运进或者由国内运出；  
(四)指定第一入境港口、降落机场。对来自国外疫区的船舶、航空器，除因遇险或者其他特殊原因外，没有经第一入境港口、机场检疫的，不准进入其他港口和机场。

**Article 9** When a quarantinable infectious disease is prevalent widely at home or abroad, the health administration department under the State Council shall immediately report to the State Council for decision on adopting some or all of the following quarantine measures:  
(1) Order the blockade of relevant areas of land borders and boundary rivers;  
(2) Designate certain articles that must undergo disinfection and disinsection before they may be shipped into or out of the country;  
(3) Prohibit certain articles from being shipped into or out of the country;  
(4) Designate the first port of entry and the first airport of landing. For ships and aircraft coming from foreign epidemic areas, unless due to distress or other special reasons, those that have not undergone quarantine at the first port of entry or first airport of landing shall not be allowed to enter other ports or airports.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　入境、出境的集装箱、货物、废旧物等物品在到达口岸的时候，承运人、代理人或者货主，必须向卫生检疫机关申报并接受卫生检疫。对来自疫区的、被传染病污染的以及可能传播检疫传染病或者发现与人类健康有关的啮齿动物和病媒昆虫的集装箱、货物、废旧物等物品，应当实施消毒、除鼠、除虫或者其他必要的卫生处理。  
集装箱、货物、废旧物等物品的货主要求在其他地方实施卫生检疫、卫生处理的，卫生检疫机关可以给予方便，并按规定办理。

**Article 10** When inbound and outbound containers, goods, waste articles and other articles arrive at the port, the carrier, agent or owner shall declare to the health and quarantine authorities and undergo health quarantine. For containers, goods, waste articles and other articles that come from epidemic areas, are contaminated by infectious diseases, may spread quarantinable infectious diseases, or are found to contain rodents and vector insects related to human health, disinfection, deratting, disinsection or other necessary sanitary treatment shall be carried out.  
If the owner of containers, goods, waste articles and other articles requests that health quarantine and sanitary treatment be carried out elsewhere, the health and quarantine authorities may provide convenience and handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　入境、出境的微生物、人体组织、生物制品、血液及其制品等特殊物品的携带人、托运人或者邮递人，必须向卫生检疫机关申报并接受卫生检疫，凭卫生检疫机关签发的特殊物品审批单办理通关手续。未经卫生检疫机关许可，不准入境、出境。

**Article 11** : Persons carrying, consigning, or mailing special items such as microorganisms, human tissues, biological products, blood and its products that enter or exit the country must declare to the health and quarantine authority and undergo health quarantine, and shall go through customs clearance procedures based on the special item approval certificate issued by the health and quarantine authority. Without permission from the health and quarantine authority, such items shall not be allowed to enter or exit the country.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　入境、出境的旅客、员工个人携带或者托运可能传播传染病的行李和物品，应当接受卫生检查。卫生检疫机关对来自疫区或者被传染病污染的各种食品、饮料、水产品等应当实施卫生处理或者销毁，并签发卫生处理证明。

**Article 12** : Passengers and employees entering or exiting the country who personally carry or consign luggage and articles that may spread infectious diseases shall undergo health inspection. The health and quarantine authority shall carry out health treatment or destruction of various foods, beverages, aquatic products, etc., that come from epidemic areas or are contaminated by infectious diseases, and shall issue a health treatment certificate.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　卫生检疫机关对应当实施卫生检疫的邮包进行卫生检查和必要的卫生处理时，邮政部门应予配合。未经卫生检疫机关许可，邮政部门不得运递。

**Article 13** : When the health and quarantine authority conducts health inspection and necessary health treatment on postal parcels that shall be subject to health quarantine, the postal department shall cooperate. Without permission from the health and quarantine authority, the postal department shall not transport or deliver such parcels.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　卫生检疫单、证的种类、式样和签发办法，由海关总署规定。

**Article 14** : The types, forms, and issuance procedures of health quarantine documents and certificates shall be prescribed by the General Administration of Customs.

### 第二章　疫情通报

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在国境口岸以及停留在国境口岸的交通工具上，发现检疫传染病、疑似检疫传染病，或者有人非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明时，国境口岸有关单位以及交通工具的负责人，应当立即向卫生检疫机关报告。

**Article 15** : When a quarantinable infectious disease, suspected quarantinable infectious disease is discovered, or a person dies from a cause other than accidental injury and the cause of death is unknown, at a frontier port or on a means of transportation staying at a frontier port, the relevant entity at the frontier port and the person in charge of the means of transportation shall immediately report to the health and quarantine authority.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　卫生检疫机关发现检疫传染病、监测传染病、疑似检疫传染病时，应当向当地卫生行政部门和卫生防疫机构通报；发现检疫传染病时，还应当用最快的办法向国务院卫生行政部门报告。  
当地卫生防疫机构发现检疫传染病、监测传染病时，应当向卫生检疫机关通报。

**Article 16** : When the health and quarantine authority discovers a quarantinable infectious disease, a monitored infectious disease, or a suspected quarantinable infectious disease, it shall notify the local health administrative department and the health and epidemic prevention institution; when a quarantinable infectious disease is discovered, it shall also report to the health administration department of the State Council by the fastest means.  
When a local health and epidemic prevention institution discovers a quarantinable infectious disease or a monitored infectious disease, it shall notify the health and quarantine authority.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在国内或者国外某一地区发生检疫传染病流行时，国务院卫生行政部门可以宣布该地区为疫区。

**Article 17** : When a quarantinable infectious disease is prevalent in a region within China or abroad, the health administration department of the State Council may declare that region as an epidemic area.

### 第三章　卫生检疫机关

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　卫生检疫机关根据工作需要，可以设立派出机构。卫生检疫机关的设立、合并或者撤销，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 18** : The health and quarantine authority may establish field agencies based on work needs. The establishment, merger, or dissolution of health and quarantine authorities shall be carried out in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　卫生检疫机关的职责：  
(一)执行《国境卫生检疫法》及其实施细则和国家有关卫生法规；  
(二)收集、整理、报告国际和国境口岸传染病的发生、流行和终息情况；  
(三)对国境口岸的卫生状况实施卫生监督；对入境、出境的交通工具、人员、集装箱、尸体、骸骨以及可能传播检疫传染病的行李、货物、邮包等实施检疫查验、传染病监测、卫生监督和卫生处理；  
(四)对入境、出境的微生物、生物制品、人体组织、血液及其制品等特殊物品以及能传播人类传染病的动物，实施卫生检疫；  
(五)对入境、出境人员进行预防接种、健康检查、医疗服务、国际旅行健康咨询和卫生宣传；  
(六)签发卫生检疫证件；  
(七)进行流行病学调查研究，开展科学实验；  
(八)执行海关总署、国务院卫生行政部门指定的其他工作。

**Article 19** : The responsibilities of the health and quarantine authority:  
(1) To enforce the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law and its implementing rules, as well as relevant national health regulations;  
(2) To collect, compile, and report on the occurrence, prevalence, and termination of infectious diseases internationally and at frontier ports;  
(3) To carry out health supervision over the sanitary conditions of frontier ports; to conduct quarantine inspection, infectious disease monitoring, health supervision, and health treatment on means of transportation, persons, containers, corpses, human remains, and luggage, goods, and postal parcels that may spread quarantinable infectious diseases entering or exiting the country;  
(4) To implement health quarantine on special items such as microorganisms, biological products, human tissues, blood and its products entering or exiting the country, as well as animals capable of transmitting human infectious diseases;  
(5) To provide preventive vaccination, health examination, medical services, international travel health consultation, and health education for persons entering or exiting the country;  
(6) To issue health quarantine certificates;  
(7) To conduct epidemiological investigation and research, and to carry out scientific experiments;  
(8) To perform other tasks designated by the General Administration of Customs or the health administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国境口岸卫生监督员的职责：  
(一)对国境口岸和停留在国境口岸的入境、出境交通工具进行卫生监督和卫生宣传；  
(二)在消毒、除鼠、除虫等卫生处理方面进行技术指导；  
(三)对造成传染病传播、啮齿动物和病媒昆虫扩散、食物中毒、食物污染等事故进行调查，并提出控制措施。

**Article 20** : The responsibilities of frontier port health supervisors:  
(1) To carry out health supervision and health education on frontier ports and on means of transportation entering or exiting the country that stay at frontier ports;  
(2) To provide technical guidance on health treatment such as disinfection, deratting, and disinsection;  
(3) To investigate incidents that cause the spread of infectious diseases, the proliferation of rodents and disease vector insects, food poisoning, or food contamination, and to propose control measures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　卫生检疫机关工作人员、国境口岸卫生监督员在执行任务时，应当穿着检疫制服，佩戴检疫标志；卫生检疫机关的交通工具在执行任务期间，应当悬挂检疫旗帜。  
检疫制服、标志、旗帜的式样和使用办法由海关总署会同有关部门制定，报国务院审批。

**Article 21** Staff of the health and quarantine authorities and health supervisors at frontier ports shall wear quarantine uniforms and display quarantine badges when performing their duties; the means of transport of the health and quarantine authorities shall display quarantine flags during the performance of duties.  
The styles and usage methods of quarantine uniforms, badges, and flags shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs in conjunction with relevant departments and submitted to the State Council for approval.

### 第四章　海港检疫

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　船舶的入境检疫，必须在港口的检疫锚地或者经卫生检疫机关同意的指定地点实施。  
检疫锚地由港务监督机关和卫生检疫机关会商确定，报国务院交通运输主管部门和海关总署备案。

**Article 22** Entry quarantine inspection of vessels must be conducted at the quarantine anchorage of the port or at a designated place approved by the health and quarantine authorities.  
The quarantine anchorage shall be determined through consultation between the harbor superintendency authorities and the health and quarantine authorities, and reported to the competent transport department of the State Council and the General Administration of Customs for record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　船舶代理应当在受入境检疫的船舶到达以前，尽早向卫生检疫机关通知下列事项：  
(一)船名、国籍、预定到达检疫锚地的日期和时间；  
(二)发航港、最后寄港；  
(三)船员和旅客人数；  
(四)货物种类。  
港务监督机关应当将船舶确定到达检疫锚地的日期和时间尽早通知卫生检疫机关。

**Article 23** The vessel agent shall, prior to the arrival of the vessel subject to entry quarantine, notify the health and quarantine authorities as early as possible of the following:  
(1) name and nationality of the vessel, scheduled date and time of arrival at the quarantine anchorage;  
(2) port of departure and last port of call;  
(3) number of crew members and passengers;  
(4) types of cargo.  
The harbor superintendency authorities shall notify the health and quarantine authorities as early as possible of the confirmed date and time of the vessel's arrival at the quarantine anchorage.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　受入境检疫的船舶，在航行中，发现检疫传染病、疑似检疫传染病，或者有人非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明的，船长必须立即向实施检疫港口的卫生检疫机关报告下列事项：  
(一)船名、国籍、预定到达检疫锚地的日期和时间；  
(二)发航港、最后寄港；  
(三)船员和旅客人数；  
(四)货物种类；  
(五)病名或者主要症状、患病人数、死亡人数；  
(六)船上有无船医。

**Article 24** If, during navigation, a vessel subject to entry quarantine discovers a quarantinable disease, a suspected quarantinable disease, or any person dies from causes other than accidental injury and the cause of death is unknown, the master must immediately report to the health and quarantine authorities at the port of quarantine the following:  
(1) name and nationality of the vessel, scheduled date and time of arrival at the quarantine anchorage;  
(2) port of departure and last port of call;  
(3) number of crew members and passengers;  
(4) types of cargo;  
(5) name or main symptoms of the disease, number of cases, number of deaths;  
(6) whether there is a ship surgeon on board.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　受入境检疫的船舶，必须按照下列规定悬挂检疫信号等候查验，在卫生检疫机关发给入境检疫证前，不得降下检疫信号。  
昼间在明显处所悬挂国际通语信号旗：  
(一)“Q”字旗表示：本船没有染疫，请发给入境检疫证；  
(二)“QQ”字旗表示：本船有染疫或者染疫嫌疑，请即刻实施检疫。  
夜间在明显处所垂直悬挂灯号：  
(一)红灯三盏表示：本船没有染疫，请发给入境检疫证；  
(二)红、红、白、红灯四盏表示：本船有染疫或者染疫嫌疑，请即刻实施检疫。

**Article 25** A vessel subject to entry quarantine must display quarantine signals in accordance with the following provisions for inspection, and shall not lower the quarantine signals before the health and quarantine authorities issue the entry quarantine certificate.  
During the daytime, the international signal flags shall be displayed in a conspicuous place:  
(1) A "Q" flag indicates: This vessel is not infected, please issue the entry quarantine certificate;  
(2) A "QQ" flag indicates: This vessel is infected or suspected of infection, please implement quarantine immediately.  
At nighttime, the following lights shall be displayed vertically in a conspicuous place:  
(1) Three red lights indicate: This vessel is not infected, please issue the entry quarantine certificate;  
(2) Four lights of red, red, white, and red indicate: This vessel is infected or suspected of infection, please implement quarantine immediately.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　悬挂检疫信号的船舶，除引航员和经卫生检疫机关许可的人员外，其他人员不准上船，不准装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品，其他船舶不准靠近；船上的人员，除因船舶遇险外，未经卫生检疫机关许可，不准离船；引航员不得将船引离检疫锚地。

**Article 26** No persons other than the pilot and those authorized by the health and quarantine authorities shall board a vessel displaying quarantine signals; no loading or unloading of luggage, cargo, postal packages, or other articles shall be allowed; no other vessels shall approach; no persons on board, except in the case of a vessel in distress, shall leave the vessel without permission from the health and quarantine authorities; the pilot shall not pilot the vessel away from the quarantine anchorage.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请电讯检疫的船舶，首先向卫生检疫机关申请卫生检查，合格者发给卫生证书。该证书自签发之日起12个月内可以申请电讯检疫。

**Article 27** A vessel applying for telecommunication quarantine shall first apply to the health and quarantine authorities for a sanitary inspection; if found qualified, it shall be issued a sanitary certificate. Within 12 months from the date of issuance of the certificate, the vessel may apply for telecommunication quarantine.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　持有效卫生证书的船舶在入境前24小时，应当向卫生检疫机关报告下列事项：  
(一)船名、国籍、预定到达检疫锚地的日期和时间；  
(二)发航港、最后寄港；  
(三)船员和旅客人数及健康状况；  
(四)货物种类；  
(五)船舶卫生证书的签发日期和编号、除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书的签发日期和签发港，以及其他卫生证件。  
经卫生检疫机关对上述报告答复同意后，即可进港。

**Article 28** A vessel holding a valid sanitary certificate shall, 24 hours before entry, report to the health and quarantine authorities the following:  
(1) name and nationality of the vessel, scheduled date and time of arrival at the quarantine anchorage;  
(2) port of departure and last port of call;  
(3) number of crew members and passengers and their health status;  
(4) types of cargo;  
(5) date of issuance and number of the ship sanitary certificate, date of issuance and port of issuance of the deratting certificate or deratting exemption certificate, and other sanitary documents.  
After the health and quarantine authorities give consent in reply to the above report, the vessel may proceed into the port.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对船舶的入境检疫，在日出后到日落前的时间内实施；凡具备船舶夜航条件，夜间可靠离码头和装卸作业的港口口岸，应实行24小时检疫。对来自疫区的船舶，不实行夜间检疫。

**Article 29** Entry quarantine inspection of vessels shall be carried out between sunrise and sunset; at port gateways that have conditions for vessel nighttime navigation and can safely berth and unberth at docks and conduct loading and unloading operations at night, 24-hour quarantine shall be implemented. For vessels arriving from an epidemic area, nighttime quarantine shall not be implemented.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　受入境检疫船舶的船长，在检疫医师到达船上时，必须提交由船长签字或者有船医附签的航海健康申报书、船员名单、旅客名单、载货申报单，并出示除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书。  
在查验中，检疫医师有权查阅航海日志和其他有关证件；需要进一步了解船舶航行中卫生情况时，检疫医师可以向船长、船医提出询问，船长、船医必须如实回答。用书面回答时，须经船长签字和船医附签。

**Article 30** The master of a vessel subject to entry quarantine must, when the quarantine medical officer arrives on board, submit a maritime declaration of health signed by the master or countersigned by the ship surgeon, a crew list, a passenger list, and a cargo declaration, and present the deratting certificate or deratting exemption certificate.  
During inspection, the quarantine medical officer has the authority to examine the ship's logbook and other relevant documents; where it is necessary to further understand the sanitary conditions during the vessel's voyage, the quarantine medical officer may put questions to the master and the ship surgeon, and the master and the ship surgeon must answer truthfully. If the answer is given in writing, it shall be signed by the master and countersigned by the ship surgeon.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　船舶实施入境查验完毕以后，对没有染疫的船舶，检疫医师应当立即签发入境检疫证；如果该船有受卫生处理或者限制的事项，应当在入境检疫证上签注，并按照签注事项办理。对染疫船舶、染疫嫌疑船舶，除通知港务监督机关外，对该船舶还应当发给卫生处理通知书，该船舶上的引航员和经卫生检疫机关许可上船的人员应当视同员工接受有关卫生处理，在卫生处理完毕以后，再发给入境检疫证。  
船舶领到卫生检疫机关签发的入境检疫证后，可以降下检疫信号。

**Article 31** After completion of entry quarantine inspection of a vessel, for a vessel without any quarantinable disease, the quarantine physician shall immediately issue an Entry Quarantine Certificate; if the vessel is subject to sanitary treatment or restrictions, the quarantine physician shall endorse such matters on the Entry Quarantine Certificate, and handle them in accordance with the endorsements. For a vessel with quarantinable disease or a suspected quarantinable disease, in addition to notifying the harbour superintendency administration, the quarantine physician shall also issue a Notice of Sanitary Treatment to the vessel; the pilot on board and persons permitted by the sanitary quarantine organ to board the vessel shall be regarded as crew members and shall receive relevant sanitary treatment; after completion of the sanitary treatment, an Entry Quarantine Certificate shall be issued.  
After the vessel obtains the Entry Quarantine Certificate issued by the sanitary quarantine organ, it may lower the quarantine signal.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　船舶代理应当在受出境检疫的船舶启航以前，尽早向卫生检疫机关通知下列事项：  
(一)船名、国籍、预定开航的日期和时间；  
(二)目的港、最初寄港；  
(三)船员名单和旅客名单；  
(四)货物种类。  
港务监督机关应当将船舶确定开航的日期和时间尽早通知卫生检疫机关。  
船舶的入境、出境检疫在同一港口实施时，如果船员、旅客没有变动，可以免报船员名单和旅客名单；有变动的，报变动船员、旅客名单。

**Article 32** The ship agent shall, before departure of a vessel subject to exit quarantine, notify the sanitary quarantine organ of the following matters as early as possible:  
(1) Name and nationality of the vessel, scheduled date and time of departure;  
(2) Port of destination, first port of call;  
(3) List of crew members and list of passengers;  
(4) Types of cargo.  
The harbour superintendency administration shall notify the sanitary quarantine organ of the confirmed date and time of departure of the vessel as early as possible.  
When entry and exit quarantine inspections of a vessel are carried out at the same port, if there is no change in crew members or passengers, the lists of crew members and passengers may be exempted from submission; if there are changes, the changed lists of crew members and passengers shall be submitted.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　受出境检疫的船舶，船长应当向卫生检疫机关出示除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书和其他有关检疫证件。检疫医师可以向船长、船医提出有关船员、旅客健康情况和船上卫生情况的询问，船长、船医对上述询问应当如实回答。

**Article 33** For a vessel subject to exit quarantine, the master shall present to the sanitary quarantine organ the Deratting Certificate or Deratting Exemption Certificate and other relevant quarantine documents. The quarantine physician may ask the master or the ship's doctor questions concerning the health condition of the crew and passengers and the sanitary condition on board; the master or the ship's doctor shall answer the above questions truthfully.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对船舶实施出境检疫完毕以后，检疫医师应当按照检疫结果立即签发出境检疫证，如果因卫生处理不能按原定时间启航，应当及时通知港务监督机关。

**Article 34** After completion of exit quarantine inspection of a vessel, the quarantine physician shall immediately issue an Exit Quarantine Certificate based on the quarantine results; if the vessel cannot depart at the scheduled time due to sanitary treatment, the harbour superintendency administration shall be promptly notified.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对船舶实施出境检疫完毕以后，除引航员和经卫生检疫机关许可的人员外，其他人员不准上船，不准装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品。如果违反上述规定，该船舶必须重新实施出境检疫。

**Article 35** After completion of exit quarantine inspection of a vessel, no other persons other than the pilot and persons permitted by the sanitary quarantine organ shall board the vessel, and no loading or unloading of luggage, cargo, postal parcels or other articles shall be allowed. If the above provisions are violated, the vessel must undergo exit quarantine inspection again.

### 第五章　航空检疫

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　航空器在飞行中，不得向下投掷或者任其坠下能传播传染病的任何物品。

**Article 36** An aircraft in flight shall not throw down or allow to fall any articles that may spread infectious diseases.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实施卫生检疫机场的航空站，应当在受入境检疫的航空器到达以前，尽早向卫生检疫机关通知下列事项：  
(一)航空器的国籍、机型、号码、识别标志、预定到达时间；  
(二)出发站、经停站；  
(三)机组和旅客人数。

**Article 37** The airport authority of an airport where sanitary quarantine is carried out shall, before the arrival of an aircraft subject to entry quarantine, notify the sanitary quarantine organ of the following matters as early as possible:  
(1) Nationality, type, number, identification marks and scheduled time of arrival of the aircraft;  
(2) Departure station, stopover station;  
(3) Number of crew members and passengers.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　受入境检疫的航空器，如果在飞行中发现检疫传染病、疑似检疫传染病，或者有人非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明时，机长应当立即通知到达机场的航空站，向卫生检疫机关报告下列事项：  
(一)航空器的国籍、机型、号码、识别标志、预定到达时间；  
(二)出发站、经停站；  
(三)机组和旅客人数；  
(四)病名或者主要症状、患病人数、死亡人数。

**Article 38** If, during flight, an aircraft subject to entry quarantine discovers a quarantinable disease, a suspected quarantinable disease, or the death of a person from causes other than accidental injury with unknown cause of death, the pilot-in-command shall immediately notify the airport authority of the arrival airport and report the following matters to the sanitary quarantine organ:  
(1) Nationality, type, number, identification marks and scheduled time of arrival of the aircraft;  
(2) Departure station, stopover station;  
(3) Number of crew members and passengers;  
(4) Name or main symptoms of the disease, number of persons affected, number of deaths.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　受入境检疫的航空器到达机场以后，检疫医师首先登机。机长或者其授权的代理人，必须向卫生检疫机关提交总申报单、旅客名单、货物仓单和有效的灭蚊证书，以及其他有关检疫证件；对检疫医师提出的有关航空器上卫生状况的询问，机长或者其授权的代理人应当如实回答。在检疫没有结束之前，除经卫生检疫机关许可外，任何人不得上下航空器，不准装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品。

**Article 39** After the arrival of an aircraft subject to entry quarantine at the airport, the quarantine physician shall board the aircraft first. The pilot-in-command or his authorized agent must submit to the sanitary quarantine organ the general declaration, passenger list, cargo manifest, valid mosquito destruction certificate, and other relevant quarantine documents; the pilot-in-command or his authorized agent shall truthfully answer questions raised by the quarantine physician concerning the sanitary condition on board the aircraft. Before the quarantine is completed, no person shall board or leave the aircraft, and no loading or unloading of luggage, cargo, postal parcels or other articles shall be allowed, except with the permission of the sanitary quarantine organ.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　入境旅客必须在指定的地点，接受入境查验，同时用书面或者口头回答检疫医师提出的有关询问。在此期间，入境旅客不得离开查验场所。

**Article 40** Entry passengers must undergo entry inspection at designated places and shall answer in writing or orally relevant questions raised by the quarantine physician. During this period, entry passengers may not leave the inspection site.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对入境航空器查验完毕以后，根据查验结果，对没有染疫的航空器，检疫医师应当签发入境检疫证；如果该航空器有受卫生处理或者限制的事项，应当在入境检疫证上签注，由机长或者其授权的代理人负责执行；对染疫或者有染疫嫌疑的航空器，除通知航空站外，对该航空器应当发给卫生处理通知单，在规定的卫生处理完毕以后，再发给入境检疫证。

**Article 41** After the inspection of an incoming aircraft is completed, based on the inspection results, the quarantine physician shall issue an Entry Quarantine Certificate for aircraft that are not infected; if there are matters requiring sanitary treatment or restrictions on the aircraft, a notation shall be made on the Entry Quarantine Certificate, and the pilot-in-command or his authorized agent shall be responsible for implementation; for aircraft that are infected or suspected of being infected, in addition to notifying the airport, a sanitary treatment notice shall be issued to such aircraft, and after the prescribed sanitary treatment is completed, an Entry Quarantine Certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　实施卫生检疫机场的航空站，应当在受出境检疫的航空器起飞以前，尽早向卫生检疫机关提交总申报单、货物仓单和其他有关检疫证件，并通知下列事项：  
(一)航空器的国籍、机型、号码、识别标志、预定起飞时间；  
(二)经停站、目的站；  
(三)机组和旅客人数。

**Article 42** The airport implementing sanitary quarantine shall, before the departure of an aircraft subject to exit quarantine, submit to the sanitary quarantine authority as early as possible the general declaration, cargo manifest and other relevant quarantine documents, and shall notify the following:  
(1) nationality, type, number, identification marks and scheduled departure time of the aircraft;  
(2) stopover stations and destination stations;  
(3) number of crew members and passengers.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对出境航空器查验完毕以后，如果没有染疫，检疫医师应当签发出境检疫证或者在必要的卫生处理完毕以后，再发给出境检疫证；如果该航空器因卫生处理不能按原定时间起飞，应当及时通知航空站。

**Article 43** After the inspection of an outgoing aircraft is completed, if it is not infected, the quarantine physician shall issue an Exit Quarantine Certificate, or shall issue an Exit Quarantine Certificate after the necessary sanitary treatment is completed; if the aircraft cannot depart as scheduled due to sanitary treatment, the airport shall be notified in a timely manner.

### 第六章　陆地边境检疫

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　实施卫生检疫的车站，应当在受入境检疫的列车到达之前，尽早向卫生检疫机关通知下列事项：  
(一)列车的车次，预定到达的时间；  
(二)始发站；  
(三)列车编组情况。

**Article 44** The station implementing sanitary quarantine shall, before the arrival of a train subject to entry quarantine, notify the sanitary quarantine authority as early as possible of the following:  
(1) the train number and scheduled arrival time;  
(2) the originating station;  
(3) the marshalling situation of the train.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　受入境检疫的列车和其他车辆到达车站、关口后，检疫医师首先登车，列车长或者其他车辆负责人，应当口头或者书面向卫生检疫机关申报该列车或者其他车辆上人员的健康情况，对检疫医师提出有关卫生状况和人员健康的询问，应当如实回答。

**Article 45** After the train or other vehicle subject to entry quarantine arrives at the station or checkpoint, the quarantine physician shall board first; the train conductor or other person in charge of the vehicle shall, orally or in writing, declare to the sanitary quarantine authority the health status of persons on the train or other vehicle, and shall truthfully answer inquiries raised by the quarantine physician regarding sanitary conditions and human health.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　受入境检疫的列车和其他车辆到达车站、关口，在实施入境检疫而未取得入境检疫证以前，未经卫生检疫机关许可，任何人不准上下列车或者其他车辆，不准装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品。

**Article 46** After the train or other vehicle subject to entry quarantine arrives at the station or checkpoint, before entry quarantine is carried out and the Entry Quarantine Certificate is obtained, without the permission of the sanitary quarantine authority, no person shall board or disembark from the train or other vehicle, and no luggage, cargo, postal parcels or other articles shall be loaded or unloaded.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　实施卫生检疫的车站，应当在受出境检疫列车发车以前，尽早向卫生检疫机关通知下列事项：  
(一)列车的车次，预定发车的时间；  
(二)终到站；  
(三)列车编组情况。

**Article 47** The station implementing sanitary quarantine shall, before the departure of a train subject to exit quarantine, notify the sanitary quarantine authority as early as possible of the following:  
(1) the train number and scheduled departure time;  
(2) the final destination station;  
(3) the marshalling situation of the train.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　应当受入境、出境检疫的列车和其他车辆，如果在行程中发现检疫传染病、疑似检疫传染病，或者有人非因意外伤害而死亡并死因不明的，列车或者其他车辆到达车站、关口时，列车长或者其他车辆负责人应当向卫生检疫机关报告。

**Article 48** If a train or other vehicle that shall be subject to entry or exit quarantine has, during its journey, discovered a quarantinable epidemic disease or a suspected quarantinable epidemic disease, or if a person has died not due to accidental injury and the cause of death is unknown, when the train or other vehicle arrives at the station or checkpoint, the train conductor or other person in charge of the vehicle shall report to the sanitary quarantine authority.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　受入境、出境检疫的列车，在查验中发现检疫传染病或者疑似检疫传染病，或者因受卫生处理不能按原定时间发车，卫生检疫机关应当及时通知车站的站长。如果列车在原停车地点不宜实施卫生处理，站长可以选择站内其他地点实施卫生处理。在处理完毕之前，未经卫生检疫机关许可，任何人不准上下列车，不准装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品。  
为了保证入境直通列车的正常运输，卫生检疫机关可以派员随车实施检疫，列车长应当提供方便。

**Article 49** If a train subject to entry or exit quarantine is found, during inspection, to have a quarantinable epidemic disease or a suspected quarantinable epidemic disease, or if it cannot depart as scheduled due to sanitary treatment, the sanitary quarantine authority shall notify the station master in a timely manner. If the sanitary treatment cannot be appropriately carried out at the original parking place of the train, the station master may select another location within the station for sanitary treatment. Before the treatment is completed, without the permission of the sanitary quarantine authority, no person shall board or disembark from the train, and no luggage, cargo, postal parcels or other articles shall be loaded or unloaded.  
In order to ensure the normal transportation of inbound through trains, the sanitary quarantine authority may assign personnel to accompany the train for quarantine implementation, and the train conductor shall provide facilitation.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对列车或者其他车辆实施入境、出境检疫完毕后，检疫医师应当根据检疫结果分别签发入境、出境检疫证，或者在必要的卫生处理完毕后，再分别签发入境、出境检疫证。

**Article 50** After the entry or exit quarantine of a train or other vehicle is completed, the quarantine physician shall, based on the quarantine results, issue an Entry or Exit Quarantine Certificate respectively, or shall issue an Entry or Exit Quarantine Certificate respectively after the necessary sanitary treatment is completed.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　徒步入境、出境的人员，必须首先在指定的场所接受入境、出境查验，未经卫生检疫机关许可，不准离开指定的场所。

**Article 51** Persons crossing the border on foot must first undergo entry and exit inspection at designated places. Without permission from the health quarantine authority, they shall not leave the designated places.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　受入境、出境检疫的列车以及其他车辆，载有来自疫区、有染疫或者染疫嫌疑或者夹带能传播传染病的病媒昆虫和啮齿动物的货物，应当接受卫生检查和必要的卫生处理。

**Article 52** Trains and other vehicles subject to entry and exit quarantine, carrying goods from epidemic areas, goods infected or suspected of being infected, or goods with insect vectors and rodents that can spread infectious diseases, shall undergo health inspection and necessary sanitary treatment.

### 第七章　卫生处理

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　卫生检疫机关的工作人员在实施卫生处理时，必须注意下列事项：  
(一)防止对任何人的健康造成危害；  
(二)防止对交通工具的结构和设备造成损害；  
(三)防止发生火灾；  
(四)防止对行李、货物造成损害。

**Article 53** Staff members of the health quarantine authority, when implementing sanitary treatment, must pay attention to the following matters:  
(1) preventing harm to the health of any person;  
(2) preventing damage to the structure and equipment of means of transport;  
(3) preventing fires;  
(4) preventing damage to luggage and goods.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　入境、出境的集装箱、行李、货物、邮包等物品需要卫生处理的，由卫生检疫机关实施。  
入境、出境的交通工具有下列情形之一的，应当由卫生检疫机关实施消毒、除鼠、除虫或者其他卫生处理：  
(一)来自检疫传染病疫区的；  
(二)被检疫传染病污染的；  
(三)发现有与人类健康有关的啮齿动物或者病媒昆虫，超过国家卫生标准的。

**Article 54** If containers, luggage, goods, postal parcels, or other articles for entry or exit require sanitary treatment, the health quarantine authority shall implement such treatment.  
Means of transport for entry or exit in any of the following circumstances shall undergo disinfection, deratization, disinsection, or other sanitary treatment implemented by the health quarantine authority:  
(1) coming from an area where a quarantinable infectious disease is epidemic;  
(2) contaminated by a quarantinable infectious disease;  
(3) found to have rodents or insect vectors relevant to human health exceeding the national health standards.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　由国外起运经过中华人民共和国境内的货物，如果不在境内换装，除发生在流行病学上有重要意义的事件，需要实施卫生处理外，在一般情况下不实施卫生处理。

**Article 55** Goods shipped from abroad and passing through the territory of the People's Republic of China, if not transshipped within the territory, shall not undergo sanitary treatment under normal circumstances, except where events of epidemiological significance occur and sanitary treatment is required.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　卫生检疫机关对入境、出境的废旧物品和曾行驶于境外港口的废旧交通工具，根据污染程度，分别实施消毒、除鼠、除虫，对污染严重的实施销毁。

**Article 56** The health quarantine authority shall, according to the degree of contamination, implement disinfection, deratization, and disinsection respectively for waste articles and used means of transport that have been operated in foreign ports for entry or exit, and implement destruction for those seriously contaminated.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　入境、出境的尸体、骸骨托运人或者代理人应当申请卫生检疫，并出示死亡证明或者其他有关证件，对不符合卫生要求的，必须接受卫生检疫机关实施的卫生处理。经卫生检疫合格后，方准运进或者运出。  
对因患检疫传染病而死亡的病人尸体，必须就近火化，不准移运。

**Article 57** The consignor or agent of human remains or ashes for entry or exit shall apply for health quarantine and present a death certificate or other relevant documents. If they do not meet the sanitary requirements, they must accept the sanitary treatment implemented by the health quarantine authority. Only after passing the health quarantine may they be transported in or out.  
The body of a patient who died of a quarantinable infectious disease must be cremated nearby and shall not be moved.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　卫生检疫机关对已在到达本口岸前的其他口岸实施卫生处理的交通工具不再重复实施卫生处理。但有下列情形之一的，仍需实施卫生处理：  
(一)在原实施卫生处理的口岸或者该交通工具上，发生流行病学上有重要意义的事件，需要进一步实施卫生处理的；  
(二)在到达本口岸前的其他口岸实施的卫生处理没有实际效果的。

**Article 58** The health quarantine authority shall not repeat sanitary treatment for means of transport that have already undergone sanitary treatment at other ports before arriving at the present port. However, in any of the following circumstances, sanitary treatment shall still be implemented:  
(1) events of epidemiological significance occur at the port where the original sanitary treatment was implemented or on the means of transport, requiring further sanitary treatment;  
(2) the sanitary treatment implemented at other ports before arriving at the present port has no actual effect.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　在国境口岸或者交通工具上发现啮齿动物有反常死亡或者死因不明的，国境口岸有关单位或者交通工具的负责人，必须立即向卫生检疫机关报告，迅速查明原因，实施卫生处理。

**Article 59** If rodents with abnormal death or unknown cause of death are found at a frontier port or on a means of transport, the relevant unit at the frontier port or the person in charge of the means of transport must immediately report to the health quarantine authority, promptly ascertain the cause, and implement sanitary treatment.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国际航行船舶的船长，必须每隔6个月向卫生检疫机关申请一次鼠患检查，卫生检疫机关根据检查结果实施除鼠或者免予除鼠，并且分别发给除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书。该证书自签发之日起6个月内有效。

**Article 60** The master of an internationally navigating vessel must apply to the health quarantine authority for a rodent infestation inspection every six months. The health quarantine authority shall, based on the inspection results, implement deratization or exempt from deratization, and issue a deratization certificate or a deratization exemption certificate respectively. Such certificate shall be valid for six months from the date of issuance.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　卫生检疫机关只有在下列之一情况下，经检查确认船舶无鼠害的，方可签发免予除鼠证书：  
(一)空舱；  
(二)舱内虽然装有压舱物品或者其他物品，但是这些物品不引诱鼠类，放置情况又不妨碍实施鼠患检查。  
对油轮在实舱时进行检查，可以签发免予除鼠证书。

**Article 61** The health quarantine authority may issue a Deratting Exemption Certificate only after inspection confirms that the ship is free of rats under one of the following circumstances:  
(1) empty holds;  
(2) although holds contain ballast or other items, such items do not attract rats and are placed in a manner that does not impede the conduct of a rat inspection.  
For oil tankers, inspection may be conducted when the tanks are laden, and a Deratting Exemption Certificate may be issued.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　对船舶的鼠患检查或者除鼠，应当尽量在船舶空舱的时候进行。如果船舶因故不宜按期进行鼠患检查或者蒸熏除鼠，并且该船又开往便于实施鼠患检查或者蒸熏除鼠的港口，可以准许该船原有的除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书的有效期延长1个月，并签发延长证明。

**Article 62** The rat inspection or deratting of a ship shall, as far as possible, be conducted when the ship's holds are empty. If a ship cannot undergo rat inspection or fumigation deratting on schedule for some reason, and the ship is sailing to a port where such rat inspection or fumigation deratting can be conveniently conducted, the original Deratting Certificate or Deratting Exemption Certificate of the ship may be permitted to be extended by one month, and an extension certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　对国际航行的船舶，按照国家规定的标准，应当用蒸熏的方法除鼠时，如果该船的除鼠证书或者免予除鼠证书尚未失效，除该船染有鼠疫或者鼠疫嫌疑外，卫生检疫机关应当将除鼠理由通知船长。船长应当按照要求执行。

**Article 63** For ships engaged in international navigation, when deratting by fumigation shall be carried out in accordance with the standards prescribed by the State, if the Deratting Certificate or Deratting Exemption Certificate of the ship has not yet ceased to have effect, the health quarantine authority shall notify the master of the reasons for deratting, unless the ship is infected with plague or suspected of plague. The master shall execute as required.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　船舶在港口停靠期间，船长应当负责采取下列的措施：  
(一)缆绳上必须使用有效的防鼠板，或者其他防鼠装置；  
(二)夜间放置扶梯、桥板时，应当用强光照射；  
(三)在船上发现死鼠或者捕获到鼠类时，应当向卫生检疫机关报告。

**Article 64** During the period when a ship is docked at a port, the master shall be responsible for taking the following measures:  
(1) effective rat guards or other anti-rat devices must be used on the mooring lines;  
(2) when placing gangways and gangplanks at night, they shall be illuminated with strong light;  
(3) when dead rats are found or rats are captured on board, they shall be reported to the health quarantine authority.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在国境口岸停留的国内航行的船舶如果存在鼠患，船方应当进行除鼠。根据船方申请，也可由卫生检疫机关实施除鼠。

**Article 65** If a ship engaged in domestic navigation staying at a frontier port has a rat infestation, the ship owner shall carry out deratting. Upon application by the ship owner, the health quarantine authority may also carry out deratting.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国务院卫生行政部门认为必要时，可以要求来自国外或者国外某些地区的人员在入境时，向卫生检疫机关出示有效的某种预防接种证书或者健康证明。

**Article 66** The health administration department under the State Council may, when it deems necessary, require persons coming from foreign countries or certain foreign regions to present a valid certificate of vaccination against a certain disease or a health certificate to the health quarantine authority upon entry.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　预防接种的有效期如下：  
(一)黄热病疫苗自接种后第10日起，10年内有效。如果前次接种不满10年又经复种，自复种的当日起，10年内有效；  
(二)其他预防接种的有效期，按照有关规定执行。

**Article 67** The validity period of vaccination shall be as follows:  
(1) yellow fever vaccine shall be valid for 10 years, starting from the 10th day after inoculation. If a person has been revaccinated within 10 years after the previous vaccination, the validity shall be 10 years from the date of revaccination;  
(2) the validity period of other vaccinations shall be implemented in accordance with relevant regulations.

### 第八章　检疫传染病管理

#### 第一节　鼠疫

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　鼠疫的潜伏期为6日。

**Article 68** The incubation period of plague shall be 6 days.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　船舶、航空器在到达时，有下列情形之一的，为染有鼠疫：  
(一)船舶、航空器上有鼠疫病例的；  
(二)船舶、航空器上发现有感染鼠疫的啮齿动物的；  
(三)船舶上曾经有人在上船6日以后患鼠疫的。

**Article 69** A ship or aircraft, upon arrival, shall be considered infected with plague if any of the following circumstances exist:  
(1) there are cases of plague on board the ship or aircraft;  
(2) rodents infected with plague are found on board the ship or aircraft;  
(3) a person on board the ship has contracted plague 6 days after boarding.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　船舶在到达时，有下列情形之一的，为染有鼠疫嫌疑：  
(一)船舶上没有鼠疫病例，但曾经有人在上船后6日以内患鼠疫的；  
(二)船上啮齿动物有反常死亡，并且死因不明的。

**Article 70** A ship, upon arrival, shall be considered suspected of plague if any of the following circumstances exist:  
(1) there are no cases of plague on board, but a person has contracted plague within 6 days after boarding;  
(2) there are abnormal deaths of rodents on board with unknown cause of death.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　对染有鼠疫的船舶、航空器应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)对染疫人实施隔离；  
(二)对染疫嫌疑人实施除虫，并且从到达时算起，实施不超过6日的就地诊验或者留验。在此期间，船上的船员除因工作需要并且经卫生检疫机关许可外，不准上岸；  
(三)对染疫人、染疫嫌疑人的行李、使用过的其他物品和卫生检疫机关认为有污染嫌疑的物品，实施除虫，必要时实施消毒；  
(四)对染疫人占用过的部位和卫生检疫机关认为有污染嫌疑的部位，实施除虫，必要时实施消毒；  
(五)船舶、航空器上有感染鼠疫的啮齿动物，卫生检疫机关必须实施除鼠。如果船舶上发现只有未感染鼠疫的啮齿动物，卫生检疫机关也可以实施除鼠。实施除鼠可以在隔离的情况下进行。对船舶的除鼠应当在卸货以前进行；  
(六)卸货应当在卫生检疫机关的监督下进行，并且防止卸货的工作人员遭受感染，必要时，对卸货的工作人员从卸货完毕时算起，实施不超过6日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 71** The following sanitary treatment shall be implemented for ships and aircraft infected with plague:  
(1) Isolation shall be implemented for infected persons;  
(2) Disinsection shall be implemented for suspected infected persons, and medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for a period not exceeding 6 days from the time of arrival. During this period, crew members on board shall not go ashore unless required by work and permitted by the health and quarantine authority;  
(3) Disinsection shall be implemented for the luggage of infected persons and suspected infected persons, other articles used by them, and articles deemed by the health and quarantine authority as suspected of contamination; disinfection shall be implemented when necessary;  
(4) Disinsection shall be implemented for the areas occupied by infected persons and areas deemed by the health and quarantine authority as suspected of contamination; disinfection shall be implemented when necessary;  
(5) If there are rodents infected with plague on the ship or aircraft, the health and quarantine authority must implement deratting. If only rodents not infected with plague are found on the ship, the health and quarantine authority may also implement deratting. Deratting may be carried out under isolation conditions. Deratting of the ship shall be carried out before unloading;  
(6) Unloading shall be carried out under the supervision of the health and quarantine authority, and workers engaged in unloading shall be prevented from becoming infected; where necessary, medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for the workers engaged in unloading for a period not exceeding 6 days from the completion of unloading.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　对染有鼠疫嫌疑的船舶，应当实施本细则第七十一条第(二)至第(六)项规定的卫生处理。

**Article 72** For ships suspected of being infected with plague, the sanitary treatment provided in subparagraphs (2) to (6) of Article 71 of these Detailed Rules shall be implemented.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　对没有染疫的船舶、航空器，如果来自鼠疫疫区，卫生检疫机关认为必要时，可以实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)对离船、离航空器的染疫嫌疑人，从船舶、航空器离开疫区的时候算起，实施不超过6日的就地诊验或者留验；  
(二)在特殊情况下，对船舶、航空器实施除鼠。

**Article 73** For ships and aircraft not infected with plague, if they come from a plague epidemic area, the health and quarantine authority may, when it deems necessary, implement the following sanitary treatment:  
(1) Medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for suspected infected persons leaving the ship or aircraft for a period not exceeding 6 days from the time the ship or aircraft leaves the epidemic area;  
(2) Under special circumstances, deratting shall be implemented for the ship or aircraft.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对到达的时候载有鼠疫病例的列车和其他车辆，应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)本细则第七十一条第(一)、第(三)、第(四)、第(六)项规定的卫生处理；  
(二)对染疫嫌疑人实施除虫，并且从到达时算起，实施不超过6日的就地诊验或者留验；  
(三)必要时，对列车和其他车辆实施除鼠。

**Article 74** The following sanitary treatment shall be implemented for trains and other vehicles carrying plague cases at the time of arrival:  
(1) The sanitary treatment provided in subparagraphs (1), (3), (4) and (6) of Article 71 of these Detailed Rules;  
(2) Disinsection shall be implemented for suspected infected persons, and medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for a period not exceeding 6 days from the time of arrival;  
(3) When necessary, deratting shall be implemented for trains and other vehicles.

#### 第二节　霍乱

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　霍乱潜伏期为5日。

**Article 75** The incubation period of cholera shall be 5 days.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　船舶在到达的时候载有霍乱病例，或者在到达前5日以内，船上曾经有霍乱病例发生，为染有霍乱。  
船舶在航行中曾经有霍乱病例发生，但是在到达前5日以内，没有发生新病例，为染有霍乱嫌疑。

**Article 76** A ship carrying cholera cases at the time of arrival, or on which cholera cases have occurred within 5 days prior to arrival, shall be deemed to be infected with cholera.  
A ship on which cholera cases have occurred during the voyage but no new cases have occurred within 5 days prior to arrival shall be deemed to be suspected of being infected with cholera.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　航空器在到达的时候载有霍乱病例，为染有霍乱。  
航空器在航行中曾经有霍乱病例发生，但在到达以前该病员已经离去，为染有霍乱嫌疑。

**Article 77** An aircraft carrying cholera cases at the time of arrival shall be deemed to be infected with cholera.  
An aircraft on which cholera cases have occurred during the flight but the patients have left before arrival shall be deemed to be suspected of being infected with cholera.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　对染有霍乱的船舶、航空器，应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)对染疫人实施隔离；  
(二)对离船、离航空器的员工、旅客，从卫生处理完毕时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验；从船舶到达时算起5日内，船上的船员除因工作需要，并且经卫生检疫机关许可外，不准上岸；  
(三)对染疫人、染疫嫌疑人的行李，使用过的其他物品和有污染嫌疑的物品、食品实施消毒；  
(四)对染疫人占用的部位，污染嫌疑部位，实施消毒；  
(五)对污染或者有污染嫌疑的饮用水，应当实施消毒后排放，并在储水容器消毒后再换清洁饮用水；  
(六)人的排泄物、垃圾、废水、废物和装自霍乱疫区的压舱水，未经消毒，不准排放和移下；  
(七)卸货必须在卫生检疫机关监督下进行，并且防止工作人员遭受感染，必要时，对卸货工作人员从卸货完毕时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 78** The following sanitary treatment shall be implemented for ships and aircraft infected with cholera:  
(1) Isolation shall be implemented for infected persons;  
(2) Medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for employees and passengers leaving the ship or aircraft for a period not exceeding 5 days from the completion of sanitary treatment; within 5 days from the time of arrival, crew members on board shall not go ashore unless required by work and permitted by the health and quarantine authority;  
(3) Disinfection shall be implemented for the luggage of infected persons and suspected infected persons, other articles used by them, and articles and food suspected of contamination;  
(4) Disinfection shall be implemented for the areas occupied by infected persons and areas suspected of contamination;  
(5) Contaminated drinking water or drinking water suspected of contamination shall be disinfected before discharge, and the storage containers shall be disinfected before being refilled with clean drinking water;  
(6) Human excreta, garbage, wastewater, waste, and ballast water taken from cholera epidemic areas shall not be discharged or removed without disinfection;  
(7) Unloading must be carried out under the supervision of the health and quarantine authority, and workers shall be prevented from becoming infected; where necessary, medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for unloading workers for a period not exceeding 5 days from the completion of unloading.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　对染有霍乱嫌疑的船舶、航空器应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)本细则第七十八条第(二)至第(七)项规定的卫生处理；  
(二)对离船、离航空器的员工、旅客从到达时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验。在此期间，船上的船员除因工作需要，并经卫生检疫机关许可外，不准离开口岸区域；或者对离船、离航空器的员工、旅客，从离开疫区时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 79** The following sanitary treatment shall be implemented for ships and aircraft suspected of being infected with cholera:  
(1) The sanitary treatment provided in subparagraphs (2) to (7) of Article 78 of these Detailed Rules;  
(2) Medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for employees and passengers leaving the ship or aircraft for a period not exceeding 5 days from the time of arrival. During this period, crew members on board shall not leave the port area unless required by work and permitted by the health and quarantine authority; or medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for employees and passengers leaving the ship or aircraft for a period not exceeding 5 days from the time they leave the epidemic area.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　对没有染疫的船舶、航空器，如果来自霍乱疫区，卫生检疫机关认为必要时，可以实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)本细则第七十八条第(五)、第(六)项规定的卫生处理；  
(二)对离船、离航空器的员工、旅客，从离开疫区时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 80** For ships and aircraft not infected with cholera, if they come from a cholera epidemic area, the health and quarantine authority may, when it deems necessary, implement the following sanitary treatment:  
(1) The sanitary treatment provided in subparagraphs (5) and (6) of Article 78 of these Detailed Rules;  
(2) Medical examination on the spot or observation shall be implemented for employees and passengers leaving the ship or aircraft for a period not exceeding 5 days from the time they leave the epidemic area.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　对到达时载有霍乱病例的列车和其他车辆应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)按本细则第七十八条第(一)、第(三)、第(四)、第(五)、第(七)项规定的卫生处理；  
(二)对染疫嫌疑人从到达时算起，实施不超过5日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 81** For trains and other vehicles carrying cholera cases upon arrival, the following sanitary treatment shall be implemented:  
(1) Sanitary treatment as prescribed in subparagraphs (1), (3), (4), (5), and (7) of Article 78 of these Rules;  
(2) For suspected cholera cases, from the time of arrival, local examination or retention for observation shall be carried out for no more than five days.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　对来自霍乱疫区的或者染有霍乱嫌疑的交通工具，卫生检疫机关认为必要时，可以实施除虫、消毒；如果交通工具载有水产品、水果、蔬菜、饮料及其他食品，除装在密封容器内没有被污染外，未经卫生检疫机关许可，不准卸下，必要时可以实施卫生处理。

**Article 82** For means of transport coming from a cholera epidemic area or suspected of being contaminated with cholera, the health and quarantine organ may, when deemed necessary, carry out deinsectization and disinfection; if the means of transport carries aquatic products, fruits, vegetables, beverages, and other food, except those packed in sealed containers and not contaminated, no unloading shall be allowed without the permission of the health and quarantine organ, and sanitary treatment may be carried out when necessary.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　对来自霍乱疫区的水产品、水果、蔬菜、饮料以及装有这些制品的邮包，卫生检疫机关在查验时，为了判明是否被污染，可以抽样检验，必要时可以实施卫生处理。

**Article 83** For aquatic products, fruits, vegetables, beverages, and postal packages containing such products coming from a cholera epidemic area, the health and quarantine organ may, during inspection, take samples for testing to determine whether they are contaminated, and may carry out sanitary treatment when necessary.

#### 第三节　黄热病

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　黄热病的潜伏期为6日。

**Article 84** The incubation period of yellow fever shall be six days.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　来自黄热病疫区的人员，在入境时，必须向卫生检疫机关出示有效的黄热病预防接种证书。  
对无有效的黄热病预防接种证书的人员，卫生检疫机关可以从该人员离开感染环境的时候算起，实施6日的留验，或者实施预防接种并留验到黄热病预防接种证书生效时为止。

**Article 85** Persons coming from a yellow fever epidemic area must, upon entry, present a valid yellow fever vaccination certificate to the health and quarantine organ.  
For persons without a valid yellow fever vaccination certificate, the health and quarantine organ may, from the time such person leaves the infected environment, carry out retention for observation for six days, or carry out vaccination and retention for observation until the yellow fever vaccination certificate takes effect.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　航空器到达时载有黄热病病例，为染有黄热病。

**Article 86** If an aircraft carries yellow fever cases upon arrival, it shall be considered as contaminated with yellow fever.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　来自黄热病疫区的航空器，应当出示在疫区起飞前的灭蚊证书；如果在到达时不出示灭蚊证书，或者卫生检疫机关认为出示的灭蚊证书不符合要求，并且在航空器上发现活蚊，为染有黄热病嫌疑。

**Article 87** An aircraft coming from a yellow fever epidemic area shall present a mosquito eradication certificate issued before departure from the epidemic area; if no such certificate is presented upon arrival, or the health and quarantine organ deems the certificate presented does not meet the requirements, and live mosquitoes are found on the aircraft, it shall be considered as suspected of being contaminated with yellow fever.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　船舶在到达时载有黄热病病例，或者在航行中曾经有黄热病病例发生，为染有黄热病。  
船舶在到达时，如果离开黄热病疫区没有满6日，或者没有满30日并且在船上发现埃及伊蚊或者其他黄热病媒介，为染有黄热病嫌疑。

**Article 88** If a vessel carries yellow fever cases upon arrival, or has had yellow fever cases occur during the voyage, it shall be considered as contaminated with yellow fever.  
If, upon arrival, the vessel has not yet completed six days since leaving the yellow fever epidemic area, or has not completed thirty days and Aedes aegypti or other yellow fever vectors are found on board, it shall be considered as suspected of being contaminated with yellow fever.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　对染有黄热病的船舶、航空器，应当实施下列卫生处理：  
(一)对染疫人实施隔离；  
(二)对离船、离航空器又无有效的黄热病预防接种证书的员工、旅客，实施本细则第八十五条规定的卫生处理；  
(三)彻底杀灭船舶、航空器上的埃及伊蚊及其虫卵、幼虫和其他黄热病媒介，并且在没有完成灭蚊以前限制该船与陆地和其他船舶的距离不少于400米；  
(四)卸货应当在灭蚊以后进行，如果在灭蚊以前卸货，应当在卫生检疫机关监督下进行，并且采取预防措施，使卸货的工作人员免受感染，必要时，对卸货的工作人员，从卸货完毕时算起，实施6日的就地诊验或者留验。

**Article 89** For vessels and aircraft contaminated with yellow fever, the following sanitary treatment shall be implemented:  
(1) Isolation of infected persons;  
(2) For crew members and passengers leaving the vessel or aircraft without valid yellow fever vaccination certificates, sanitary treatment as prescribed in Article 85 of these Rules shall be carried out;  
(3) Thorough killing of Aedes aegypti and its eggs, larvae, and other yellow fever vectors on the vessel or aircraft, and before mosquito eradication is completed, the distance between the vessel and land or other vessels shall be limited to no less than 400 meters;  
(4) Unloading shall be carried out after mosquito eradication; if unloading is carried out before mosquito eradication, it shall be done under the supervision of the health and quarantine organ, and preventive measures shall be taken to protect the unloading workers from infection; when necessary, for the unloading workers, from the time unloading is completed, local examination or retention for observation for six days shall be carried out.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　对染有黄热病嫌疑的船舶、航空器，应当实施本细则第八十九条第(二)至第(四)项规定的卫生处理。

**Article 90** For vessels and aircraft suspected of being contaminated with yellow fever, the sanitary treatment prescribed in subparagraphs (2) to (4) of Article 89 of these Rules shall be implemented.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　对没有染疫的船舶、航空器，如果来自黄热病疫区，卫生检疫机关认为必要时，可以实施本细则第八十九条第(三)项规定的卫生处理。

**Article 91** For ships and aircraft free from plague, if coming from a yellow fever epidemic area, the health quarantine organ may, when it deems necessary, implement the health treatment specified in Article 89, item (3) of these Detailed Rules.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　对到达的时候载有黄热病病例的列车和其他车辆，或者来自黄热病疫区的列车和其他车辆，应当实施本细则第八十九条第(一)、第(四)项规定的卫生处理；对列车、车辆彻底杀灭成蚊及其虫卵、幼虫；对无有效黄热病预防接种证书的员工、旅客，应当实施本细则第八十五条规定的卫生处理。

**Article 92** Trains and other vehicles that at the time of arrival carry yellow fever cases, or trains and other vehicles coming from a yellow fever epidemic area, shall implement the health treatment specified in Article 89, items (1) and (4) of these Detailed Rules; thoroughly kill adult mosquitoes and their eggs and larvae in the trains and vehicles; and implement the health treatment specified in Article 85 of these Detailed Rules for staff members and passengers who do not possess a valid yellow fever vaccination certificate.

#### 第四节　就地诊验、留验和隔离

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　卫生检疫机关对受就地诊验的人员，应当发给就地诊验记录簿，必要的时候，可以在该人员出具履行就地诊验的保证书以后，再发给其就地诊验记录簿。  
受就地诊验的人员应当携带就地诊验记录簿，按照卫生检疫机关指定的期间、地点，接受医学检查；如果就地诊验的结果没有染疫，就地诊验期满的时候，受就地诊验的人员应当将就地诊验记录簿退还卫生检疫机关。

**Article 93** The health quarantine organ shall issue a record book for on-site medical examination to persons undergoing on-site medical examination, and when necessary, may issue the record book for on-site medical examination after the person provides a written guarantee to undergo on-site medical examination.  
Persons undergoing on-site medical examination shall carry the record book for on-site medical examination and undergo medical examination at the time and place designated by the health quarantine organ; if the result of the on-site medical examination shows no infection, when the period of on-site medical examination expires, the person undergoing on-site medical examination shall return the record book for on-site medical examination to the health quarantine organ.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　卫生检疫机关应当将受就地诊验人员的情况，用最快的方法通知受就地诊验人员的旅行停留地的卫生检疫机关或者其他医疗卫生单位。  
卫生检疫机关、医疗卫生单位遇有受就地诊验的人员请求医学检查时，应当视同急诊给予医学检查，并将检查结果在就地诊验记录簿上签注；如果发现其患检疫传染病或者监测传染病、疑似检疫传染病或者疑似监测传染病时，应当立即采取必要的卫生措施，将其就地诊验记录簿收回存查，并且报告当地卫生防疫机构和签发就地诊验记录簿的卫生检疫机关。

**Article 94** The health quarantine organ shall, by the fastest means, notify the health quarantine organ or other medical and health unit at the travel stopover place of the person undergoing on-site medical examination of the circumstances of such person.  
When a health quarantine organ or medical and health unit receives a request from a person undergoing on-site medical examination for medical examination, it shall provide medical examination as if it were an emergency case and record the examination result in the record book for on-site medical examination; if it discovers that the person suffers from a quarantinable infectious disease or a monitored infectious disease, or is suspected of suffering from a quarantinable infectious disease or a monitored infectious disease, it shall immediately take necessary health measures, retrieve the record book for on-site medical examination for retention and inspection, and report to the local health and epidemic prevention institution and the health quarantine organ that issued the record book.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　受留验的人员必须在卫生检疫机关指定的场所接受留验；但是有下列情形之一的，经卫生检疫机关同意，可以在船上留验：  
(一)船长请求船员在船上留验的；  
(二)旅客请求在船上留验，经船长同意，并且船上有船医和医疗、消毒设备的。

**Article 95** Persons undergoing quarantine inspection must undergo quarantine inspection at the place designated by the health quarantine organ; however, under any of the following circumstances, with the consent of the health quarantine organ, they may undergo quarantine inspection on board the ship:  
(1) The captain requests that crew members undergo quarantine inspection on board the ship;  
(2) A passenger requests to undergo quarantine inspection on board the ship, with the consent of the captain, and there is a ship's doctor and medical and disinfection equipment on board.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　受留验的人员在留验期间如果出现检疫传染病的症状，卫生检疫机关应当立即对该人员实施隔离，对与其接触的其他受留验的人员，应当实施必要的卫生处理，并且从卫生处理完毕时算起，重新计算留验时间。

**Article 96** If a person undergoing quarantine inspection develops symptoms of a quarantinable infectious disease during the quarantine inspection period, the health quarantine organ shall immediately isolate such person, carry out necessary health treatment on other persons undergoing quarantine inspection who have been in contact with the said person, and recalculate the quarantine inspection period from the time the health treatment is completed.

### 第九章　传染病监测

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　入境、出境的交通工具、人员、食品、饮用水和其他物品以及病媒昆虫、动物，均为传染病监测的对象。

**Article 97** Entry and exit means of transport, persons, food, drinking water and other articles, as well as vector insects and animals, are all subjects of infectious disease surveillance.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　传染病监测内容是：  
(一)首发病例的个案调查；  
(二)暴发流行的流行病学调查；  
(三)传染源调查；  
(四)国境口岸内监测传染病的回顾性调查；  
(五)病原体的分离、鉴定，人群、有关动物血清学调查以及流行病学调查；  
(六)有关动物、病媒昆虫、食品、饮用水和环境因素的调查；  
(七)消毒、除鼠、除虫的效果观察与评价；  
(八)国境口岸以及国内外监测传染病疫情的收集、整理、分析和传递；  
(九)对监测对象开展健康检查和对监测传染病病人、疑似病人、密切接触人员的管理。

**Article 98** The contents of infectious disease surveillance are:  
(1) Case investigation of the first case;  
(2) Epidemiological investigation of outbreaks;  
(3) Investigation of infection sources;  
(4) Retrospective investigation of monitored infectious diseases within the frontier port;  
(5) Isolation and identification of pathogens, serological investigation of populations and relevant animals, and epidemiological investigation;  
(6) Investigation of relevant animals, vector insects, food, drinking water and environmental factors;  
(7) Observation and evaluation of the effects of disinfection, deratting, and disinsection;  
(8) Collection, sorting, analysis and transmission of epidemic information of monitored infectious diseases at frontier ports and domestically and internationally;  
(9) Conducting health checks on surveillance subjects and management of patients with monitored infectious diseases, suspected patients, and persons in close contact.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　卫生检疫机关应当阻止患有严重精神病、传染性肺结核病或者有可能对公共卫生造成重大危害的其他传染病的外国人入境。

**Article 99** The health quarantine organ shall prevent the entry of foreigners who suffer from severe mental illness, infectious pulmonary tuberculosis, or other infectious diseases that may cause major harm to public health.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　受入境、出境检疫的人员，必须根据检疫医师的要求，如实填报健康申明卡，出示某种有效的传染病预防接种证书、健康证明或者其他有关证件。

**Article 100** Persons subject to entry and exit quarantine must, as required by the quarantine physician, truthfully fill in a health declaration card and present a valid certificate of vaccination against certain infectious diseases, a health certificate, or other relevant documents.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　卫生检疫机关对国境口岸的涉外宾馆、饭店内居住的入境、出境人员及工作人员实施传染病监测，并区别情况采取必要的预防、控制措施。  
对来自检疫传染病和监测传染病疫区的人员，检疫医师可以根据流行病学和医学检查结果，发给就诊方便卡。  
卫生检疫机关、医疗卫生单位遇到持有就诊方便卡的人员请求医学检查时，应当视同急诊给予医学检查；如果发现其患检疫传染病或者监测传染病，疑似检疫传染病或者疑似监测传染病，应当立即实施必要的卫生措施，并且将情况报告当地卫生防疫机构和签发就诊方便卡的卫生检疫机关。

**Article 101** The health and quarantine organ shall conduct infectious disease surveillance over inbound and outbound persons and staff members residing in foreign-related hotels and restaurants within the frontier port, and shall take necessary prevention and control measures in light of specific circumstances.  
For persons from areas where quarantinable infectious diseases or surveillance infectious diseases are prevalent, quarantine physicians may, based on epidemiological and medical examination results, issue a convenience card for medical consultation.  
When a health and quarantine organ or a medical and health unit receives a request for medical examination from a person holding a convenience card for medical consultation, it shall treat the case as an emergency and provide medical examination; if it finds that the person suffers from a quarantinable infectious disease or a surveillance infectious disease, or is suspected of suffering from such a disease, it shall immediately take necessary health measures and report the situation to the local health and epidemic prevention agency and the health and quarantine organ that issued the convenience card for medical consultation.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　凡申请出境居住1年以上的中国籍人员，必须持有卫生检疫机关签发的健康证明。中国公民出境、入境管理机关凭卫生检疫机关签发的健康证明办理出境手续。  
凡在境外居住1年以上的中国籍人员，入境时必须向卫生检疫机关申报健康情况，并在入境后1个月内到就近的卫生检疫机关或者县级以上的医院进行健康检查。公安机关凭健康证明办理有关手续。健康证明的副本应当寄送到原入境口岸的卫生检疫机关备案。  
国际通行交通工具上的中国籍员工，应当持有卫生检疫机关或者县级以上医院出具的健康证明。健康证明的项目、格式由海关总署统一规定，有效期为12个月。

**Article 102** Any Chinese national who applies to reside abroad for more than one year must hold a health certificate issued by the health and quarantine organ. The administration organ for exit and entry of Chinese citizens shall, on the basis of the health certificate issued by the health and quarantine organ, handle the exit procedures.  
Any Chinese national who has resided abroad for more than one year must, upon entry, declare his or her health status to the health and quarantine organ and undergo a health check-up at the nearest health and quarantine organ or a hospital at or above the county level within one month after entry. The public security organ shall handle relevant procedures on the basis of the health certificate. A copy of the health certificate shall be sent to the health and quarantine organ at the original port of entry for record.  
Chinese national staff members on international transport vehicles shall hold a health certificate issued by the health and quarantine organ or a hospital at or above the county level. The items and format of the health certificate shall be uniformly prescribed by the General Administration of Customs, and the validity period shall be 12 months.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　卫生检疫机关在国境口岸内设立传染病监测点时，有关单位应当给予协助并提供方便。

**Article 103** When the health and quarantine organ sets up infectious disease surveillance points within the frontier port, the relevant units shall provide assistance and facilitate such work.

### 第十章　卫生监督

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　卫生检疫机关依照[《国境卫生检疫法》第十八条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法.md#art-18)、第十九条规定的内容，对国境口岸和交通工具实施卫生监督。

**Article 104** The health and quarantine organ shall, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 18 and 19 of the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law, exercise health supervision over frontier ports and means of transport.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　对国境口岸的卫生要求是：  
(一)国境口岸和国境口岸内涉外的宾馆、生活服务单位以及候船、候车、候机厅(室)应当有健全的卫生制度和必要的卫生设施，并保持室内外环境整洁、通风良好；  
(二)国境口岸有关部门应当采取切实可行的措施，控制啮齿动物、病媒昆虫，使其数量降低到不足为害的程度。仓库、货场必须具有防鼠设施；  
(三)国境口岸的垃圾、废物、污水、粪便必须进行无害化处理，保持国境口岸环境整洁卫生。

**Article 105** The hygiene requirements for frontier ports are as follows:  
(1) Frontier ports and the foreign-related hotels, living service units, and waiting rooms (halls) for ships, trains, and airplanes within the frontier port shall have sound hygiene systems and necessary hygiene facilities, and shall maintain a clean and tidy indoor and outdoor environment with good ventilation;  
(2) Relevant departments of the frontier port shall take practical and feasible measures to control rodents and disease-carrying insects so as to reduce their numbers to a level that does not cause harm. Warehouses and storage yards must be equipped with rodent-proof facilities;  
(3) Garbage, waste, sewage, and excrement at the frontier port must be rendered harmless, and the environment of the frontier port shall be kept clean and hygienic.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　对交通工具的卫生要求是：  
(一)交通工具上的宿舱、车厢必须保持清洁卫生，通风良好；  
(二)交通工具上必须备有足够的消毒、除鼠、除虫药物及器械，并备有防鼠装置；  
(三)交通工具上的货舱、行李舱、货车车厢在装货前或者卸货后应当进行彻底清扫，有毒物品和食品不得混装，防止污染；  
(四)对不符合卫生要求的入境、出境交通工具，必须接受卫生检疫机关的督导立即进行改进。

**Article 106** The hygiene requirements for means of transport are as follows:  
(1) The sleeping compartments and carriages on means of transport must be kept clean and hygienic with good ventilation;  
(2) Means of transport must be equipped with sufficient disinfectants, rodenticides, and insecticides and their applicators, as well as rodent-proof devices;  
(3) The cargo holds, baggage compartments, and freight cars on means of transport shall be thoroughly cleaned before loading or after unloading, and toxic substances and food shall not be mixed loaded to prevent contamination;  
(4) For inbound and outbound means of transport that do not meet hygiene requirements, they must accept the guidance of the health and quarantine organ and immediately make improvements.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　对饮用水、食品及从业人员的卫生要求是：  
(一)国境口岸和交通工具上的食品、饮用水必须符合有关的卫生标准；  
(二)国境口岸内的涉外宾馆，以及向入境、出境的交通工具提供饮食服务的部门，必须取得卫生检疫机关发放的卫生许可证；  
(三)国境口岸内涉外的宾馆和入境、出境交通工具上的食品、饮用水从业人员应当持有有效健康证明。

**Article 107** The hygiene requirements for drinking water, food, and practitioners are as follows:  
(1) Food and drinking water at frontier ports and on means of transport must comply with relevant hygiene standards;  
(2) Foreign-related hotels within the frontier port and departments providing catering services to inbound and outbound means of transport must obtain a hygiene permit issued by the health and quarantine organ;  
(3) Practitioners engaged in food and drinking water services at foreign-related hotels within the frontier port and on inbound and outbound means of transport shall hold valid health certificates.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　国境口岸有关单位和交通工具负责人应当遵守下列事项：  
(一)遵守《国境卫生检疫法》和本细则及有关卫生法规的规定；  
(二)接受卫生监督员的监督和检查，并为其工作提供方便；  
(三)按照卫生监督员的建议，对国境口岸和交通工具的卫生状况及时采取改进措施。

**Article 108** The relevant units at the frontier port and the persons in charge of means of transport shall comply with the following matters:  
(1) Abide by the provisions of the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law, these Rules, and relevant hygiene regulations;  
(2) Accept the supervision and inspection of health supervisors and provide convenience for their work;  
(3) Take timely improvement measures on the hygiene conditions of the frontier port and means of transport in accordance with the suggestions of the health supervisors.

### 第十一章　罚则

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　《国境卫生检疫法》和本细则所规定的应当受行政处罚的行为是指：  
(一)应当受入境检疫的船舶，不悬挂检疫信号的；  
(二)入境、出境的交通工具，在入境检疫之前或者在出境检疫之后，擅自上下人员，装卸行李、货物、邮包等物品的；  
(三)拒绝接受检疫或者抵制卫生监督，拒不接受卫生处理的；  
(四)伪造或者涂改检疫单、证、不如实申报疫情的；  
(五)瞒报携带禁止进口的微生物、人体组织、生物制品、血液及其制品或者其他可能引起传染病传播的动物和物品的；  
(六)未经检疫的入境、出境交通工具，擅自离开检疫地点，逃避查验的；  
(七)隐瞒疫情或者伪造情节的；  
(八)未经卫生检疫机关实施卫生处理，擅自排放压舱水，移下垃圾、污物等控制的物品的；  
(九)未经卫生检疫机关实施卫生处理，擅自移运尸体、骸骨的；  
(十)废旧物品、废旧交通工具，未向卫生检疫机关申报，未经卫生检疫机关实施卫生处理和签发卫生检疫证书而擅自入境、出境或者使用、拆卸的；  
(十一)未经卫生检疫机关检查，从交通工具上移下传染病病人造成传染病传播危险的。

**Article 109** Acts subject to administrative penalties as provided for in the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law and these Rules refer to:  
(1) Failing to hang the quarantine signal on a ship subject to inbound quarantine;  
(2) Embarking or disembarking persons, loading or unloading luggage, goods, postal parcels, or other articles without authorization before inbound quarantine or after outbound quarantine on inbound or outbound means of transport;  
(3) Refusing to undergo quarantine or resisting health supervision, or refusing to accept health treatment;  
(4) Forging or altering quarantine documents or certificates, or failing to truthfully report the epidemic situation;  
(5) Concealing the carrying of microorganisms, human tissues, biological products, blood and blood products, or other animals and articles that may cause the spread of infectious diseases which are prohibited from import;  
(6) Inbound or outbound means of transport that have not been quarantined leaving the quarantine site without authorization to evade inspection;  
(7) Concealing the epidemic situation or fabricating circumstances;  
(8) Discharging ballast water, unloading garbage, waste, or other controlled articles without authorization without having undergone health treatment by the health and quarantine organ;  
(9) Moving corpses or remains without authorization without having undergone health treatment by the health and quarantine organ;  
(10) Waste articles or waste means of transport entering or leaving the country, or being used or dismantled without authorization without having been declared to the health and quarantine organ, without having undergone health treatment by the health and quarantine organ, and without having obtained a health and quarantine certificate;  
(11) Removing a person suffering from an infectious disease from a means of transport without inspection by the health and quarantine organ, thereby causing a risk of spread of an infectious disease.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　具有本细则第一百零九条所列第(一)至第(五)项行为的，处以警告或者100元以上5000元以下的罚款；  
具有本细则第一百零九条所列第(六)至第(九)项行为的，处以1000元以上1万元以下的罚款；  
具有本细则第一百零九条所列第(十)、第(十一)项行为的，处以5000元以上3万元以下的罚款。

**Article 110** A person who commits any of the acts listed in items (1) through (5) of Article 109 of these Rules shall be given a warning or a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan;  
A person who commits any of the acts listed in items (6) through (9) of Article 109 of these Rules shall be given a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan;  
A person who commits any of the acts listed in items (10) and (11) of Article 109 of these Rules shall be given a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　卫生检疫机关在收取罚款时，应当出具正式的罚款收据。罚款全部上交国库。

**Article 111** When imposing a fine, a health quarantine organ shall issue a formal fine receipt. The fine shall be turned over to the state treasury in full.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　国境卫生检疫机关实施卫生检疫的收费标准，由海关总署会同国务院财政、物价部门共同制定。

**Article 112** The fee standards for health quarantine implemented by frontier health quarantine organs shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs together with the finance department and the price control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　本细则自发布之日起施行。

**Article 113** These Detailed Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
