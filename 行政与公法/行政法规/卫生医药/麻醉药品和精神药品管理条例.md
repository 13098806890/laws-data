### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强麻醉药品和精神药品的管理，保证麻醉药品和精神药品的合法、安全、合理使用，防止流入非法渠道，根据药品管理法和其他有关法律的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and other relevant laws to strengthen the management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, ensure their lawful, safe and rational use, and prevent their diversion into illicit channels.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　麻醉药品药用原植物的种植，麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输等活动以及监督管理，适用本条例。  
麻醉药品和精神药品的进出口依照有关法律的规定办理。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants and to activities such as experimental research, production, operation, use, storage, transportation, and supervision and management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
Import and export of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称麻醉药品和精神药品，是指列入本条第二款规定的目录（以下称目录）的药品和其他物质。  
麻醉药品和精神药品按照药用类和非药用类分类列管。药用类麻醉药品和精神药品目录由国务院药品监督管理部门会同国务院公安部门、国务院卫生主管部门制定、调整并公布。其中，药用类精神药品分为第一类精神药品和第二类精神药品。非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品目录由国务院公安部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院卫生主管部门制定、调整并公布。非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品发现药用用途的，调整列入药用类麻醉药品和精神药品目录，不再列入非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品目录。  
国家组织开展药品和其他物质滥用监测，对药品和其他物质滥用情况进行评估，建立健全目录动态调整机制。上市销售但尚未列入目录的药品和其他物质或者第二类精神药品发生滥用，已经造成或者可能造成严重社会危害的，国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院公安部门、国务院卫生主管部门应当依照前款的规定及时将该药品和该物质列入目录或者将该第二类精神药品调整为第一类精神药品。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../刑事/毒品与走私/最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定毒品依据的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定毒品依据的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances" mean the drugs and other substances listed in the catalogue prescribed in paragraph 2 of this Article (hereinafter referred to as the Catalogue).  
Narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be classified and controlled according to medicinal and non-medicinal categories. The catalogue of medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council jointly with the public security department of the State Council and the health department of the State Council. Among them, medicinal psychotropic substances are divided into Category I psychotropic substances and Category II psychotropic substances. The catalogue of non-medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the public security department of the State Council jointly with the drug regulatory department of the State Council and the health department of the State Council. If a non-medicinal narcotic drug or psychotropic substance is found to have medicinal uses, it shall be adjusted and included in the catalogue of medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and shall no longer be included in the catalogue of non-medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The state shall organize monitoring of abuse of drugs and other substances, evaluate the abuse of drugs and other substances, and establish a sound dynamic adjustment mechanism for the Catalogue. If any drug or other substance sold on the market but not yet included in the Catalogue, or any Category II psychotropic substance, is abused and has caused or may cause serious social harm, the drug regulatory department of the State Council, the public security department of the State Council and the health department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, promptly include such drug or substance in the Catalogue, or adjust such Category II psychotropic substance to Category I psychotropic substance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对麻醉药品药用原植物以及麻醉药品和精神药品实行管制。除本条例另有规定的外，任何单位、个人不得进行麻醉药品药用原植物的种植以及麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输等活动。  
对药用类麻醉药品和精神药品，可以依照本条例的规定进行实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输；对非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品，可以依照本条例的规定进行实验研究，不得生产、经营、使用、储存、运输。  
国家建立麻醉药品和精神药品追溯管理体系。国务院药品监督管理部门应当制定统一的麻醉药品和精神药品追溯标准和规范，推进麻醉药品和精神药品追溯信息互通互享，实现麻醉药品和精神药品可追溯。

**Article 4** The state shall exercise control over narcotic drug raw plants as well as narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. Unless otherwise provided for in these Regulations, no unit or individual may engage in the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants or the experimental research, production, operation, use, storage, transportation, etc. of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
With respect to medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, experimental research, production, operation, use, storage and transportation may be carried out in accordance with these Regulations; with respect to non-medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, experimental research may be carried out in accordance with these Regulations, but production, operation, use, storage and transportation shall not be allowed.  
The state shall establish a traceability management system for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall formulate unified traceability standards and specifications for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, promote the exchange and sharing of traceability information for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, and achieve traceability of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院药品监督管理部门负责全国麻醉药品和精神药品的监督管理工作，并会同国务院农业主管部门对麻醉药品药用原植物实施监督管理。国务院公安部门负责对造成麻醉药品药用原植物、麻醉药品和精神药品流入非法渠道的行为进行查处。国务院其他有关主管部门在各自的职责范围内负责与麻醉药品和精神药品有关的管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门和设区的市级、县级人民政府承担药品监督管理职责的部门（以下称药品监督管理部门）负责本行政区域内麻醉药品和精神药品的监督管理工作。县级以上地方公安机关负责对本行政区域内造成麻醉药品和精神药品流入非法渠道的行为进行查处。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关主管部门在各自的职责范围内负责与麻醉药品和精神药品有关的管理工作。

**Article 5** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances nationwide, and shall, jointly with the agricultural department of the State Council, exercise supervision and management of narcotic drug raw plants. The public security department of the State Council shall be responsible for investigating and punishing acts that cause narcotic drug raw plants, narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to flow into illicit channels. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions, be responsible for the management work related to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The drug regulatory departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the departments responsible for drug supervision and management at the city with districts level and the county level (hereinafter referred to as drug regulatory departments) shall be responsible for the supervision and management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances within their respective administrative regions. The public security organs at or above the county level shall be responsible for investigating and punishing acts that cause narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to flow into illicit channels within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions, be responsible for the management work related to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　麻醉药品和精神药品生产、经营企业和使用单位可以依法参加行业协会。行业协会应当加强行业自律管理。

**Article 6** Enterprises producing and operating narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and their using units may participate in trade associations according to law. Trade associations shall strengthen industry self-discipline management.

### 第二章　种植、实验研究和生产

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家根据麻醉药品和精神药品的医疗、国家储备和企业生产所需原料的需要确定需求总量，对麻醉药品药用原植物的种植、麻醉药品和精神药品的生产实行总量控制。  
国务院药品监督管理部门根据麻醉药品和精神药品的需求总量制定年度生产计划。  
国务院药品监督管理部门和国务院农业主管部门根据麻醉药品年度生产计划，制定麻醉药品药用原植物年度种植计划。

**Article 7** The state shall determine the total demand for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances based on the needs for medical treatment, national reserves and raw materials for enterprise production, and exercise overall control over the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants and the production of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall formulate an annual production plan based on the total demand for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council and the agricultural department of the State Council shall, based on the annual production plan for narcotic drugs, formulate an annual planting plan for narcotic drug raw plants.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业应当根据年度种植计划，种植麻醉药品药用原植物。  
麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业应当向国务院药品监督管理部门和国务院农业主管部门定期报告种植情况。

**Article 8** The enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants shall cultivate narcotic drug raw plants according to the annual planting plan.  
The enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants shall regularly report the cultivation situation to the drug regulatory department of the State Council and the agricultural department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业由国务院药品监督管理部门和国务院农业主管部门共同确定，其他单位和个人不得种植麻醉药品药用原植物。

**Article 9** The enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants shall be jointly designated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council and the agricultural department of the State Council. No other unit or individual may cultivate narcotic drug raw plants.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　开展麻醉药品和精神药品实验研究活动应当具备下列条件，并经国务院药品监督管理部门批准：  
（一）以医疗、科学研究或者教学为目的；  
（二）有保证实验所需麻醉药品和精神药品安全的措施和管理制度；  
（三）单位及其工作人员2年内没有违反有关禁毒的法律、行政法规规定的行为。

**Article 10** To carry out experimental research activities on narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the following conditions shall be met and approval from the drug regulatory department of the State Council obtained:  
(1) For medical, scientific research or teaching purposes;  
(2) Having measures and management systems to ensure the safety of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances required for the experiment;  
(3) The unit and its staff members have not violated any laws or administrative regulations on drug control within the past 2 years.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究单位申请相关药品批准证明文件，应当依照药品管理法的规定办理；需要转让研究成果的，应当经国务院药品监督管理部门批准。

**Article 11** Where the unit conducting experimental research on narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances applies for the relevant drug approval certificate, it shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law; if it needs to transfer the research results, it shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　药品研究单位在普通药品的实验研究过程中，产生本条例规定的管制品种的，应当立即停止实验研究活动，并向国务院药品监督管理部门报告。国务院药品监督管理部门应当根据情况，及时作出是否同意其继续实验研究的决定。

**Article 12** If, during the experimental research of ordinary drugs, a drug research unit produces a controlled variety as specified in these Regulations, it shall immediately stop the experimental research activities and report to the drug regulatory department of the State Council. The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, based on the circumstances, promptly make a decision on whether to permit the continuation of the experimental research.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的临床试验，不得以健康人为受试对象。

**Article 13** In clinical trials of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances, healthy persons shall not be used as subjects.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对麻醉药品和精神药品实行定点生产制度。  
国务院药品监督管理部门应当根据麻醉药品和精神药品的需求总量，确定麻醉药品和精神药品定点生产企业的数量和布局，并根据年度需求总量对数量和布局进行调整、公布。

**Article 14** The state shall implement a system of designated production for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, based on the total demand for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, determine the number and distribution of designated manufacturers for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, and adjust and publish the number and distribution according to the annual total demand.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　麻醉药品和精神药品的定点生产企业应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有药品生产许可证；  
（二）有麻醉药品和精神药品实验研究批准文件；  
（三）有符合规定的麻醉药品和精神药品生产设施、储存条件和相应的安全管理设施；  
（四）有通过网络实施企业安全生产管理和向药品监督管理部门报告生产信息的能力；  
（五）有保证麻醉药品和精神药品安全生产的管理制度；  
（六）有与麻醉药品和精神药品安全生产要求相适应的管理水平和经营规模；  
（七）麻醉药品和精神药品生产管理、质量管理部门的人员应当熟悉麻醉药品和精神药品管理以及有关禁毒的法律、行政法规；  
（八）没有生产、销售假药、劣药或者违反有关禁毒的法律、行政法规规定的行为；  
（九）符合国务院药品监督管理部门公布的麻醉药品和精神药品定点生产企业数量和布局的要求。

**Article 15** A designated manufacturer of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having a Drug Manufacturing License;  
(2) Having the approval document for experimental research on narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances;  
(3) Having production facilities, storage conditions and corresponding safety management facilities for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that conform to the regulations;  
(4) Having the ability to implement enterprise safety production management through the network and to report production information to the drug regulatory department;  
(5) Having a management system to ensure the safe production of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances;  
(6) Having a management level and business scale commensurate with the safety production requirements for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances;  
(7) Personnel in the production management and quality management departments for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be familiar with the management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and the relevant laws and administrative regulations on drug control;  
(8) Having no act of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard drugs, or violating laws or administrative regulations on drug control;  
(9) Complying with the requirements on the number and distribution of designated manufacturers for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事麻醉药品、精神药品生产的企业，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。

**Article 16** An enterprise engaged in the production of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　定点生产企业生产麻醉药品和精神药品，应当依照药品管理法的规定取得药品批准文号。  
国务院药品监督管理部门应当组织医学、药学、社会学、伦理学和禁毒等方面的专家成立专家组，由专家组对申请首次上市的麻醉药品和精神药品的社会危害性和被滥用的可能性进行评价，并提出是否批准的建议。  
未取得药品批准文号的，不得生产麻醉药品和精神药品。

**Article 17** A designated manufacturer producing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances shall obtain a drug approval number in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall organize experts in fields such as medicine, pharmacy, sociology, ethics and drug control to form an expert group, which shall evaluate the social harmfulness and potential for abuse of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances for which initial marketing is applied, and make recommendations on whether to approve.  
No narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances may be produced without obtaining a drug approval number.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　发生重大突发事件，定点生产企业无法正常生产或者不能保证供应麻醉药品和精神药品时，国务院药品监督管理部门可以决定其他药品生产企业生产麻醉药品和精神药品。  
重大突发事件结束后，国务院药品监督管理部门应当及时决定前款规定的企业停止麻醉药品和精神药品的生产。

**Article 18** If a major emergency occurs and the designated manufacturer is unable to produce normally or cannot guarantee the supply of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the drug regulatory department of the State Council may decide that other pharmaceutical manufacturers produce narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
After the end of the major emergency, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall promptly decide that the enterprises specified in the preceding paragraph cease the production of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　定点生产企业应当严格按照麻醉药品和精神药品年度生产计划安排生产，并依照规定向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报告生产情况。

**Article 19** A designated manufacturer shall strictly arrange production according to the annual production plan for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, and shall report the production situation to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located in accordance with the regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　定点生产企业应当依照本条例的规定，将麻醉药品和精神药品销售给具有麻醉药品和精神药品经营资格的企业或者依照本条例规定批准的其他单位。

**Article 20** A designated manufacturer shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, sell narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to enterprises qualified to operate narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances or to other units approved in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　麻醉药品和精神药品的标签应当印有国务院药品监督管理部门规定的标志。

**Article 21** The labels of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall bear the symbols prescribed by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

### 第三章　经营

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对麻醉药品和精神药品实行定点经营制度。  
国务院药品监督管理部门应当根据麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的需求总量，确定麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的定点批发企业布局，并应当根据年度需求总量对布局进行调整、公布。  
药品经营企业不得经营麻醉药品原料药和第一类精神药品原料药。但是，供医疗、科学研究、教学使用的小包装的上述药品可以由国务院药品监督管理部门规定的药品批发企业经营。

**Article 22** The state shall implement a system of designated operation for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, based on the total demand for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances, determine the distribution of designated wholesale enterprises for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances, and shall adjust and publish the distribution according to the annual total demand.  
A pharmaceutical trading enterprise shall not deal in bulk narcotic drugs (active pharmaceutical ingredients) and bulk Category I psychotropic substances (active pharmaceutical ingredients). However, the above-mentioned drugs in small packages for medical, scientific research or teaching purposes may be dealt in by pharmaceutical wholesale enterprises designated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　麻醉药品和精神药品定点批发企业除应当具备药品管理法规定的药品经营企业的开办条件外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合本条例规定的麻醉药品和精神药品储存条件；  
（二）有通过网络实施企业安全管理和向药品监督管理部门报告经营信息的能力；  
（三）单位及其工作人员2年内没有违反有关禁毒的法律、行政法规规定的行为；  
（四）符合国务院药品监督管理部门公布的定点批发企业布局。  
麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的定点批发企业，还应当具有保证供应责任区域内医疗机构所需麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的能力，并具有保证麻醉药品和第一类精神药品安全经营的管理制度。

**Article 23** In addition to the conditions for establishing a pharmaceutical trading enterprise as prescribed in the Drug Administration Law, a designated wholesale enterprise for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having storage conditions for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that conform to the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Having the ability to implement enterprise security management through the network and to report business information to the drug regulatory department;  
(3) The unit and its staff members have not violated any laws or administrative regulations on drug control within the past 2 years;  
(4) Complying with the distribution of designated wholesale enterprises published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.  
A designated wholesale enterprise for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall also have the ability to guarantee the supply of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances required by medical institutions within its responsible area, and have a management system to ensure the safe operation of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　跨省、自治区、直辖市从事麻醉药品和第一类精神药品批发业务的企业（以下称全国性批发企业），应当经国务院药品监督管理部门批准；在本省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内从事麻醉药品和第一类精神药品批发业务的企业（以下称区域性批发企业），应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。  
专门从事第二类精神药品批发业务的企业，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。  
全国性批发企业和区域性批发企业可以从事第二类精神药品批发业务。

**Article 24** An enterprise engaged in the wholesale of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as a national wholesale enterprise) shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the State Council; an enterprise engaged in the wholesale of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances within the administrative region of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as a regional wholesale enterprise) shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
An enterprise exclusively engaged in the wholesale of Category II psychotropic substances shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
National wholesale enterprises and regional wholesale enterprises may engage in the wholesale of Category II psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　全国性批发企业可以向区域性批发企业，或者经批准可以向取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品使用资格的医疗机构以及依照本条例规定批准的其他单位销售麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。  
全国性批发企业向取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品使用资格的医疗机构销售麻醉药品和第一类精神药品，应当经医疗机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。  
国务院药品监督管理部门在批准全国性批发企业时，应当明确其所承担供药责任的区域。

**Article 25** A national wholesale enterprise may sell narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances to regional wholesale enterprises, or, with approval, to medical institutions that have obtained the qualification to use narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances and to other units approved in accordance with these Regulations.  
A national wholesale enterprise selling narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances to medical institutions that have obtained the qualification to use narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the medical institution is located.  
When approving a national wholesale enterprise, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall specify the area for which it assumes responsibility for drug supply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　区域性批发企业可以向本省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品使用资格的医疗机构销售麻醉药品和第一类精神药品；由于特殊地理位置的原因，需要就近向其他省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品使用资格的医疗机构销售的，应当经企业所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。审批情况由负责审批的药品监督管理部门在批准后5日内通报医疗机构所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门在批准区域性批发企业时，应当明确其所承担供药责任的区域。  
区域性批发企业之间因医疗急需、运输困难等特殊情况需要调剂麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的，应当在调剂后2日内将调剂情况分别报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。

**Article 26** A regional wholesale enterprise may sell narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances to medical institutions that have obtained the qualification to use narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances within the administrative region of its own province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; if, due to special geographical location, it is necessary to sell such drugs to medical institutions that have obtained such qualification in the administrative region of another province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government nearby, approval shall be obtained from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the enterprise is located. The drug regulatory department responsible for approval shall, within 5 days after the approval, notify the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the medical institution is located of the approval situation.  
When approving a regional wholesale enterprise, the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall specify the area for which it assumes responsibility for drug supply.  
If regional wholesale enterprises, due to special circumstances such as medical urgency or transportation difficulties, need to transfer narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances among themselves, they shall, within 2 days after the transfer, report the transfer situation to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located for the record.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　全国性批发企业应当从定点生产企业购进麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。  
区域性批发企业可以从全国性批发企业购进麻醉药品和第一类精神药品；经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，也可以从定点生产企业购进麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。

**Article 27** A national wholesale enterprise shall purchase narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances from designated manufacturers.  
A regional wholesale enterprise may purchase narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances from national wholesale enterprises; with the approval of the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, it may also purchase narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances from designated manufacturers.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　全国性批发企业和区域性批发企业向医疗机构销售麻醉药品和第一类精神药品，应当将药品送至医疗机构。医疗机构不得自行提货。

**Article 28** When selling narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances to medical institutions, a national wholesale enterprise or a regional wholesale enterprise shall deliver the drugs to the medical institutions. The medical institutions shall not pick up the goods by themselves.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　第二类精神药品定点批发企业可以向医疗机构、定点批发企业和符合[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)规定的药品零售企业以及依照本条例规定批准的其他单位销售第二类精神药品。

**Article 29** A designated wholesale enterprise for Category II psychotropic substances may sell Category II psychotropic substances to medical institutions, designated wholesale enterprises, pharmaceutical retail enterprises that meet the requirements of Article 31 of these Regulations, and other units approved in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　麻醉药品和第一类精神药品不得零售。  
禁止使用现金进行麻醉药品和精神药品交易，但是个人合法购买麻醉药品和精神药品的除外。

**Article 30** Narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall not be retailed.  
It is prohibited to use cash in transactions of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, except where an individual lawfully purchases narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　经所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门批准，实行统一进货、统一配送、统一管理的药品零售连锁企业可以从事第二类精神药品零售业务。

**Article 31** A pharmaceutical retail chain enterprise that implements unified procurement, unified distribution and unified management may, upon approval from the drug regulatory department at the city with districts level where it is located, engage in the retail business of Category II psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　第二类精神药品零售企业应当凭执业医师出具的处方，按规定剂量销售第二类精神药品，并将处方保存2年备查；禁止超剂量或者无处方销售第二类精神药品；不得向未成年人销售第二类精神药品。

**Article 32** A retail enterprise of Category II psychotropic substances shall sell Category II psychotropic substances on the basis of a prescription issued by a licensed physician and within the prescribed dosage, and shall keep the prescription for 2 years for future reference; it is prohibited to sell Category II psychotropic substances in excess of the dosage or without a prescription; Category II psychotropic substances shall not be sold to minors.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　麻醉药品和第一类精神药品实行政府指导价。具体办法由国务院医疗保障主管部门制定。

**Article 33** The prices of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall be subject to government guidance. The specific measures shall be formulated by the medical security department of the State Council.

### 第四章　使用

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　药品生产企业需要以麻醉药品和第一类精神药品为原料生产普通药品的，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报送年度需求计划，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门汇总报国务院药品监督管理部门批准后，向定点生产企业购买。  
药品生产企业需要以第二类精神药品为原料生产普通药品的，应当将年度需求计划报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门，并向定点批发企业或者定点生产企业购买。

**Article 34** If a pharmaceutical manufacturer needs to use narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances as raw materials to produce ordinary drugs, it shall submit an annual demand plan to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The said drug regulatory department shall summarize and submit it to the drug regulatory department of the State Council for approval, and then the manufacturer shall purchase from designated manufacturers.  
If a pharmaceutical manufacturer needs to use Category II psychotropic substances as raw materials to produce ordinary drugs, it shall submit the annual demand plan to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and purchase from designated wholesale enterprises or designated manufacturers.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　食品、食品添加剂、化妆品、油漆等非药品生产企业需要使用咖啡因作为原料的，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，向定点批发企业或者定点生产企业购买。  
科学研究、教学单位需要使用麻醉药品和精神药品开展实验、教学活动的，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，向定点批发企业或者定点生产企业购买。  
需要使用麻醉药品和精神药品的标准品、对照品的，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准，向国务院药品监督管理部门批准的单位购买。

**Article 35** If a non-pharmaceutical manufacturer of foods, food additives, cosmetics, paints, etc. needs to use caffeine as a raw material, it shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and purchase from designated wholesale enterprises or designated manufacturers.  
If a scientific research or teaching unit needs to use narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to carry out experiments or teaching activities, it shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and purchase from designated wholesale enterprises or designated manufacturers.  
If a unit needs to use reference standards or control samples of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, it shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and purchase from units approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　医疗机构需要使用麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的，应当经所在地设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门批准，取得麻醉药品、第一类精神药品购用印鉴卡（以下称印鉴卡）。医疗机构应当凭印鉴卡向本省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内的定点批发企业购买麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。  
设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门发给医疗机构印鉴卡时，应当将取得印鉴卡的医疗机构情况抄送所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门，并报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门备案。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门应当将取得印鉴卡的医疗机构名单向本行政区域内的定点批发企业通报。

**Article 36** If a medical institution needs to use narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances, it shall obtain approval from the health department of the people's government at the city with districts level where it is located, and obtain a seal card for purchasing narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances (hereinafter referred to as the Seal Card). The medical institution shall, on the basis of the Seal Card, purchase narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances from designated wholesale enterprises within the administrative region of its own province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
When the health department of the people's government at the city with districts level issues a Seal Card to a medical institution, it shall send a copy of the information on the medical institution that has obtained the Seal Card to the drug regulatory department at the same city with districts level, and report it to the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for the record. The health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall notify the designated wholesale enterprises within its administrative region of the list of medical institutions that have obtained the Seal Card.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　医疗机构取得印鉴卡应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有专职的麻醉药品和第一类精神药品管理人员；  
（二）有获得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师；  
（三）有保证麻醉药品和第一类精神药品安全储存的设施和管理制度。

**Article 37** A medical institution shall meet the following conditions to obtain the Seal Card:  
(1) Having full-time personnel for the management of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances;  
(2) Having licensed physicians who have obtained the prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances;  
(3) Having facilities and a management system to ensure the safe storage of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　医疗机构应当按照国务院卫生主管部门的规定，对本单位执业医师进行有关麻醉药品和精神药品使用知识的培训、考核，经考核合格的，授予麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格。执业医师取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的处方资格后，方可在本医疗机构开具麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方，但不得为自己开具该种处方。  
医疗机构应当将具有麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师名单及其变更情况，定期报送所在地设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门，并抄送同级药品监督管理部门。  
医务人员应当根据国务院卫生主管部门制定的临床应用指导原则，使用麻醉药品和精神药品。

**Article 38** A medical institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health department of the State Council, provide training and assessment on the knowledge of the use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to its licensed physicians. Those who pass the assessment shall be granted the prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances. Only after obtaining the prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances may a licensed physician issue prescriptions for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances in the medical institution, but shall not issue such prescriptions for himself or herself.  
The medical institution shall regularly submit the list of licensed physicians who have the prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances and any changes thereto to the health department of the people's government at the city with districts level where it is located, and send a copy to the drug regulatory department at the same level.  
Medical personnel shall use narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the clinical application guidelines formulated by the health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　具有麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师，根据临床应用指导原则，对确需使用麻醉药品或者第一类精神药品的患者，应当满足其合理用药需求。在医疗机构就诊的癌症疼痛患者和其他危重患者得不到麻醉药品或者第一类精神药品时，患者或者其亲属可以向执业医师提出申请。具有麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师认为要求合理的，应当及时为患者提供所需麻醉药品或者第一类精神药品。

**Article 39** A licensed physician with prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall, in accordance with the clinical application guidelines, satisfy the rational medication needs of patients who truly need to use narcotic drugs or Category I psychotropic substances. If a patient with cancer pain or other critically ill patient treated in a medical institution cannot obtain narcotic drugs or Category I psychotropic substances, the patient or his/her relatives may make a request to a licensed physician. If the licensed physician with prescription qualification for narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances considers the request reasonable, he/she shall promptly provide the patient with the required narcotic drugs or Category I psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　执业医师应当使用专用处方开具麻醉药品和精神药品，单张处方的最大用量应当符合国务院卫生主管部门的规定。执业医师开具麻醉药品和精神药品处方，应当对患者的信息进行核对；因抢救患者等紧急情况，无法核对患者信息的，执业医师可以先行开具麻醉药品和精神药品处方。  
对麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方，处方的调配人、核对人应当仔细核对，签署姓名，并予以登记；对不符合本条例规定的，处方的调配人、核对人应当拒绝发药。  
麻醉药品和精神药品专用处方的格式由国务院卫生主管部门规定。

**Article 40** A licensed physician shall use a special prescription to prescribe narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The maximum dosage of a single prescription shall comply with the provisions of the health department of the State Council. When prescribing narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the licensed physician shall verify the patient's information; in emergency situations such as rescuing a patient where the patient's information cannot be verified, the licensed physician may first issue the prescription for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
For prescriptions of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances, the person dispensing and the person checking the prescription shall carefully check, sign their names, and register; if the prescription does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, the person dispensing and the person checking the prescription shall refuse to dispense the drugs.  
The format of the special prescription for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be prescribed by the health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　医疗机构应当对麻醉药品和精神药品处方进行专册登记，加强管理。麻醉药品处方至少保存3年，精神药品处方至少保存2年。医疗机构应当按照国务院卫生主管部门的规定及时报送麻醉药品和精神药品处方信息。

**Article 41** A medical institution shall maintain a special register for prescriptions of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and strengthen management. Prescriptions for narcotic drugs shall be kept for at least 3 years, and prescriptions for psychotropic substances shall be kept for at least 2 years. The medical institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health department of the State Council, promptly submit the prescription information of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　医疗机构抢救病人急需麻醉药品和第一类精神药品而本医疗机构无法提供时，可以从其他医疗机构或者定点批发企业紧急借用；抢救工作结束后，应当及时将借用情况报所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门和卫生主管部门备案。

**Article 42** If a medical institution urgently needs narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances for rescuing a patient but cannot provide them, it may urgently borrow them from other medical institutions or designated wholesale enterprises; after the rescue work is completed, it shall promptly report the borrowing situation to the drug regulatory department and the health department at the city with districts level where it is located for the record.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对临床需要而市场无供应的麻醉药品和精神药品，持有医疗机构制剂许可证和印鉴卡的医疗机构需要配制制剂的，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门批准。医疗机构配制的麻醉药品和精神药品制剂只能在本医疗机构使用，不得对外销售。

**Article 43** If a medical institution holding a Medical Institution Preparation License and the Seal Card needs to prepare preparations of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that are clinically needed but not available on the market, it shall obtain approval from the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The preparations of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances prepared by the medical institution may only be used within that medical institution and shall not be sold externally.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　因治疗疾病需要，个人凭医疗机构出具的医疗诊断书、本人身份证明，可以携带单张处方最大用量以内的麻醉药品和第一类精神药品；携带麻醉药品和第一类精神药品出入境的，由海关根据自用、合理的原则放行。  
医务人员为了医疗需要携带少量麻醉药品和精神药品出入境的，应当持有省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门发放的携带麻醉药品和精神药品证明。海关凭携带麻醉药品和精神药品证明放行。

**Article 44** If needed for the treatment of diseases, an individual may carry narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances within the maximum dosage of a single prescription on the basis of a medical diagnosis document issued by a medical institution and his/her identity certificate; if carrying narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances into or out of the country, the Customs shall release them based on the principle of self-use and reasonableness.  
If medical personnel need to carry a small quantity of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances into or out of the country for medical purposes, they shall hold a certificate for carrying narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances issued by the drug regulatory department of a people's government at or above the provincial level. The Customs shall release them on the strength of the certificate for carrying narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　医疗机构、戒毒机构以开展戒毒治疗为目的，可以使用美沙酮或者国家确定的其他用于戒毒治疗的麻醉药品和精神药品。具体管理办法由国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院公安部门和国务院卫生主管部门制定。

**Article 45** For the purpose of carrying out drug addiction treatment, medical institutions and drug rehabilitation institutions may use methadone or other narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances designated by the state for drug addiction treatment. The specific management measures shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, the public security department of the State Council and the health department of the State Council.

### 第五章　储存

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业、定点生产企业、全国性批发企业和区域性批发企业以及国家设立的麻醉药品储存单位，应当设置储存麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的专库。该专库应当符合下列要求：  
（一）安装专用防盗门，实行双人双锁管理；  
（二）具有相应的防火设施；  
（三）具有监控设施和报警装置，报警装置应当与公安机关报警系统联网。  
全国性批发企业经国务院药品监督管理部门批准设立的药品储存点应当符合前款的规定。  
麻醉药品定点生产企业应当将麻醉药品原料药和制剂分别存放。

**Article 46** The enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants, designated manufacturers, national wholesale enterprises, regional wholesale enterprises, and drug storage units established by the state shall set up dedicated warehouses for storing narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances. Such dedicated warehouses shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) Installing special anti-theft doors and implementing dual-person and dual-lock management;  
(2) Having corresponding fire prevention facilities;  
(3) Having monitoring facilities and alarm devices, and the alarm devices shall be networked with the alarm system of the public security organs.  
The drug storage points established by a national wholesale enterprise with the approval of the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall comply with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
A designated manufacturer of narcotic drugs shall separately store bulk narcotic drugs (active pharmaceutical ingredients) and preparations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的使用单位应当设立专库或者专柜储存麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。专库应当设有防盗设施并安装报警装置；专柜应当使用保险柜。专库和专柜应当实行双人双锁管理。

**Article 47** The using units of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall set up dedicated warehouses or dedicated cabinets for storing narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances. Dedicated warehouses shall be equipped with anti-theft facilities and alarm devices; dedicated cabinets shall be safes. Dedicated warehouses and dedicated cabinets shall be subject to dual-person and dual-lock management.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业、定点生产企业、全国性批发企业和区域性批发企业、国家设立的麻醉药品储存单位以及麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的使用单位，应当配备专人负责管理工作，并建立储存麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的专用账册。药品入库双人验收，出库双人复核，做到账物相符。专用账册的保存期限应当自药品有效期期满之日起不少于5年。

**Article 48** The enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants, designated manufacturers, national wholesale enterprises, regional wholesale enterprises, drug storage units established by the state, and the using units of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances shall assign dedicated personnel to be responsible for the management work, and establish special ledgers for the storage of narcotic drugs and Category I psychotropic substances. Drugs shall be received by two persons upon warehousing and checked by two persons upon out-warehousing, so as to ensure that the accounts and the goods are consistent. The retention period of the special ledgers shall be no less than 5 years from the date of expiry of the drug validity period.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　第二类精神药品经营企业应当在药品库房中设立独立的专库或者专柜储存第二类精神药品，并建立专用账册，实行专人管理。专用账册的保存期限应当自药品有效期期满之日起不少于5年。

**Article 49** An enterprise dealing in Category II psychotropic substances shall set up an independent dedicated warehouse or dedicated cabinet in its drug warehouse for storing Category II psychotropic substances, establish a special ledger, and implement dedicated personnel management. The retention period of the special ledger shall be no less than 5 years from the date of expiry of the drug validity period.

### 第六章　运输

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　托运、承运和自行运输麻醉药品和精神药品的，应当采取安全保障措施，防止麻醉药品和精神药品在运输过程中被盗、被抢、丢失。

**Article 50** Where consigning, carrying or self-transporting narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, safety protection measures shall be taken to prevent the narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances from being stolen, robbed or lost during transportation.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　通过铁路运输麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的，应当使用集装箱或者铁路行李车运输，具体办法由国务院药品监督管理部门会同国务院铁路主管部门制定。  
没有铁路需要通过公路或者水路运输麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的，应当由专人负责押运。

**Article 51** Where narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I are transported by railway, containers or railway luggage cars shall be used. The specific measures shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council jointly with the competent railway department under the State Council.  
Where there is no railway and narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I need to be transported by road or waterway, such transportation shall be escorted by designated personnel.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　托运或者自行运输麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的单位，应当向所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门申请领取运输证明。运输证明有效期为1年。  
运输证明应当由专人保管，不得涂改、转让、转借。

**Article 52** A unit that consigns or transports by itself narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I shall apply to the drug regulatory department of the city divided into districts at its locality for a transportation certificate. The transportation certificate shall be valid for one year.  
The transportation certificate shall be kept by designated personnel and shall not be altered, transferred, or lent.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　托运人办理麻醉药品和第一类精神药品运输手续，应当将运输证明副本交付承运人。承运人应当查验、收存运输证明副本，并检查货物包装。没有运输证明或者货物包装不符合规定的，承运人不得承运。  
承运人在运输过程中应当携带运输证明副本，以备查验。

**Article 53** When a consignor handles the transport procedures for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, it shall deliver a copy of the transportation certificate to the carrier. The carrier shall check and keep the copy of the transportation certificate and inspect the packaging of the goods. Where there is no transportation certificate or the packaging of the goods does not comply with the provisions, the carrier shall not carry the goods.  
The carrier shall carry the copy of the transportation certificate during transportation for inspection purposes.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　邮寄麻醉药品和精神药品，寄件人应当提交所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门出具的准予邮寄证明。邮政营业机构应当查验、收存准予邮寄证明；没有准予邮寄证明的，邮政营业机构不得收寄。  
省、自治区、直辖市邮政主管部门指定符合安全保障条件的邮政营业机构负责收寄麻醉药品和精神药品。邮政营业机构收寄麻醉药品和精神药品，应当依法对收寄的麻醉药品和精神药品予以查验。  
邮寄麻醉药品和精神药品的具体管理办法，由国务院药品监督管理部门会同国务院邮政主管部门制定。

**Article 54** To mail narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the sender shall submit a certificate of permission for mailing issued by the drug regulatory department of the city divided into districts at its locality. The postal operating entity shall check and keep the certificate of permission for mailing; where there is no such certificate, the postal operating entity shall not accept the mail.  
The competent postal departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall designate postal operating entities that meet the conditions for safety and security to be responsible for accepting narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by mail. When accepting narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by mail, the postal operating entity shall inspect them according to law.  
The specific measures for the administration of mailing narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council jointly with the competent postal department under the State Council.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　定点生产企业、全国性批发企业和区域性批发企业之间运输麻醉药品、第一类精神药品，发货人在发货前应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报送本次运输的相关信息。属于跨省、自治区、直辖市运输的，收到信息的药品监督管理部门应当向收货人所在地的同级药品监督管理部门通报；属于在本省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内运输的，收到信息的药品监督管理部门应当向收货人所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门通报。

**Article 55** When designated manufacturers, national wholesale enterprises, and regional wholesale enterprises transport narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I among themselves, the consignor shall, before dispatching the goods, report the relevant information of such transport to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at its locality. If the transport crosses provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the drug regulatory department that receives the information shall notify the drug regulatory department at the same level at the consignee's locality; if the transport is within the administrative region of the same province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the drug regulatory department that receives the information shall notify the drug regulatory department of the city divided into districts at the consignee's locality.

### 第七章　审批程序和监督管理

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　申请人提出本条例规定的审批事项申请，应当提交能够证明其符合本条例规定条件的相关资料。审批部门应当自收到申请之日起40日内作出是否批准的决定；作出批准决定的，发给许可证明文件或者在相关许可证明文件上加注许可事项；作出不予批准决定的，应当书面说明理由。  
确定定点生产企业和定点批发企业，审批部门应当在经审查符合条件的企业中，根据布局的要求，通过公平竞争的方式初步确定定点生产企业和定点批发企业，并予公布。其他符合条件的企业可以自公布之日起10日内向审批部门提出异议。审批部门应当自收到异议之日起20日内对异议进行审查，并作出是否调整的决定。

**Article 56** An applicant applying for approval matters provided for in these Regulations shall submit relevant materials that can prove that it meets the conditions specified in these Regulations. The examination and approval authority shall, within 40 days from the date of receiving the application, make a decision on whether to grant approval; if a decision of approval is made, it shall issue a license document or annotate the licensed item on the relevant license document; if a decision of disapproval is made, it shall state the reasons in writing.  
To designate the manufacturers and the wholesale enterprises, the examination and approval authority shall, among the enterprises that meet the conditions upon examination, preliminarily determine the designated manufacturers and designated wholesale enterprises in accordance with the requirements of layout through fair competition, and shall publish them. Other enterprises that meet the conditions may raise an objection to the examination and approval authority within 10 days from the date of publication. The examination and approval authority shall, within 20 days from the date of receiving the objection, examine the objection and make a decision on whether to make adjustments.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　药品监督管理部门应当根据规定的职责权限，对麻醉药品药用原植物的种植以及麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输活动进行监督检查。

**Article 57** The drug regulatory department shall, in accordance with the prescribed functions and powers, conduct supervision and inspection over the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants and the experimental research, production, operation, use, storage, and transport of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门根据实际情况建立监控信息网络，对定点生产企业、定点批发企业和使用单位的麻醉药品和精神药品生产、进货、销售、库存、使用的数量以及流向实行实时监控，并与同级公安机关做到信息共享。

**Article 58** The drug regulatory departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, based on the actual situation, establish a monitoring information network to carry out real-time monitoring of the quantities and flow directions of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances produced, purchased, sold, stored, and used by designated manufacturers, designated wholesale enterprises, and user units, and shall share information with the public security organs at the same level.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　尚未连接监控信息网络的麻醉药品和精神药品定点生产企业、定点批发企业和使用单位，应当每月通过电子信息、传真、书面等方式，将本单位麻醉药品和精神药品生产、进货、销售、库存、使用的数量以及流向，报所在地设区的市级药品监督管理部门和公安机关；医疗机构还应当报所在地设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门。  
设区的市级药品监督管理部门应当每3个月向上一级药品监督管理部门报告本地区麻醉药品和精神药品的相关情况。

**Article 59** Designated manufacturers of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, designated wholesale enterprises, and user units that have not yet been connected to the monitoring information network shall, on a monthly basis, report to the drug regulatory department of the city divided into districts at their locality and the public security organ the quantities and flow directions of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances produced, purchased, sold, stored, and used by their units by means of electronic information, facsimile, writing, or other methods; medical institutions shall also report to the competent health department of the people's government of the city divided into districts at their locality.  
The drug regulatory department of a city divided into districts shall, every three months, report to the drug regulatory department at the next higher level the relevant information on narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in its region.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对已经发生滥用，造成严重社会危害的麻醉药品和精神药品品种，国务院药品监督管理部门应当采取在一定期限内中止生产、经营、使用或者限定其使用范围和用途等措施。对不再作为药品使用的麻醉药品和精神药品，国务院药品监督管理部门应当撤销其药品批准文号和药品标准，并予以公布。  
药品监督管理部门、卫生主管部门发现生产、经营企业和使用单位的麻醉药品和精神药品管理存在安全隐患时，应当责令其立即排除或者限期排除；对有证据证明可能流入非法渠道的，应当及时采取查封、扣押的行政强制措施，在7日内作出行政处理决定，并通报同级公安机关。  
药品监督管理部门发现取得印鉴卡的医疗机构未依照规定购买麻醉药品和第一类精神药品时，应当及时通报同级卫生主管部门。接到通报的卫生主管部门应当立即调查处理。必要时，药品监督管理部门可以责令定点批发企业中止向该医疗机构销售麻醉药品和第一类精神药品。

**Article 60** With respect to varieties of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that have been abused and have caused serious social harm, the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall take measures such as suspending their production, operation, and use within a certain period, or limiting their scope and purpose of use. For narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that are no longer used as medicines, the drug regulatory department under the State Council shall revoke their drug approval numbers and drug standards and shall publish such revocation.  
When a drug regulatory department or the competent health department discovers that there are potential safety hazards in the management of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by a production enterprise, operation enterprise, or user unit, it shall order the unit to immediately eliminate the hazards or eliminate them within a time limit; where there is evidence proving that the drugs may have flowed into illegal channels, it shall promptly take administrative compulsory measures of sealing up or seizing, make an administrative handling decision within 7 days, and notify the public security organ at the same level.  
When a drug regulatory department discovers that a medical institution that has obtained a seal-verification card fails to purchase narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I in accordance with the provisions, it shall promptly notify the competent health department at the same level. The competent health department that receives the notification shall immediately investigate and handle the matter. When necessary, the drug regulatory department may order the designated wholesale enterprise to suspend the sale of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I to that medical institution.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　麻醉药品和精神药品的生产、经营企业和使用单位对过期、损坏的麻醉药品和精神药品应当登记造册，并向所在地县级药品监督管理部门申请销毁。药品监督管理部门应当自接到申请之日起5日内到场监督销毁。医疗机构对存放在本单位的过期、损坏麻醉药品和精神药品，应当按照本条规定的程序向卫生主管部门提出申请，由卫生主管部门负责监督销毁。  
对依法收缴的麻醉药品和精神药品，除经国务院药品监督管理部门或者国务院公安部门批准用于科学研究外，应当依照国家有关规定予以销毁。

**Article 61** Production enterprises, operation enterprises, and user units of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall register expired or damaged narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in a roster and apply to the drug regulatory department at the county level at their locality for destruction. The drug regulatory department shall, within 5 days from the date of receiving the application, be present to supervise the destruction. For expired or damaged narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances stored in a medical institution, the medical institution shall apply to the competent health department in accordance with the procedures specified in this Article, and the competent health department shall be responsible for supervising the destruction.  
The narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that have been confiscated according to law shall, unless approved by the drug regulatory department under the State Council or the public security department under the State Council for scientific research, be destroyed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门应当对执业医师开具麻醉药品和精神药品处方的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 62** The competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervision and inspection over the prescription of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by practicing physicians.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　药品监督管理部门、卫生主管部门和公安机关应当互相通报麻醉药品和精神药品生产、经营企业和使用单位的名单以及其他管理信息。  
各级药品监督管理部门应当将在麻醉药品药用原植物的种植以及麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用、储存、运输等各环节的管理中的审批、撤销等事项通报同级公安机关。  
麻醉药品和精神药品的经营企业、使用单位报送各级药品监督管理部门的备案事项，应当同时报送同级公安机关。

**Article 63** The drug regulatory departments, the competent health departments, and the public security organs shall notify each other of the lists of production enterprises, operation enterprises, and user units of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and other management information.  
The drug regulatory departments at all levels shall notify the public security organs at the same level of matters such as approval and revocation in the management of the cultivation of narcotic drug raw plants and all aspects of experimental research, production, operation, use, storage, and transport of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.  
Matters filed by operation enterprises and user units of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances with the drug regulatory departments at all levels shall, at the same time, be filed with the public security organs at the same level.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　发生麻醉药品和精神药品被盗、被抢、丢失或者其他流入非法渠道的情形的，案发单位应当立即采取必要的控制措施，同时报告所在地县级公安机关和药品监督管理部门。医疗机构发生上述情形的，还应当报告其主管部门。  
公安机关接到报告、举报，或者有证据证明麻醉药品和精神药品可能流入非法渠道时，应当及时开展调查，并可以对相关单位采取必要的控制措施。  
药品监督管理部门、卫生主管部门以及其他有关部门应当配合公安机关开展工作。

**Article 64** Where narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances are stolen, robbed, lost, or otherwise flow into illegal channels, the unit where the incident occurs shall immediately take necessary control measures and, at the same time, report to the public security organ at the county level at its locality and the drug regulatory department. Where the above situation occurs in a medical institution, it shall also report to its competent department.  
When a public security organ receives a report or tip-off, or has evidence proving that narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances may have flowed into illegal channels, it shall promptly conduct an investigation and may take necessary control measures against the relevant units.  
The drug regulatory department, the competent health department, and other relevant departments shall cooperate with the public security organ in carrying out its work.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　药品监督管理部门、卫生主管部门违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由其上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）对不符合条件的申请人准予行政许可或者超越法定职权作出准予行政许可决定的；  
（二）未到场监督销毁过期、损坏的麻醉药品和精神药品的；  
（三）未依法履行监督检查职责，应当发现而未发现违法行为、发现违法行为不及时查处，或者未依照本条例规定的程序实施监督检查的；  
（四）违反本条例规定的其他失职、渎职行为。

**Article 65** Where a drug regulatory department or a competent health department, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the administrative organ at the next higher level or the supervisory organ shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law on the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) granting an administrative license to an applicant that does not meet the conditions, or making a decision to grant an administrative license beyond the statutory functions and powers;  
(2) failing to be present to supervise the destruction of expired or damaged narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances;  
(3) failing to perform the duty of supervision and inspection according to law, failing to discover illegal acts that should have been discovered, failing to investigate and deal with discovered illegal acts in a timely manner, or failing to conduct supervision and inspection in accordance with the procedures prescribed in these Regulations;  
(4) other acts of dereliction of duty or malfeasance in violation of the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　麻醉药品药用原植物种植企业违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由药品监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，取消其种植资格：  
（一）未依照麻醉药品药用原植物年度种植计划进行种植的；  
（二）未依照规定报告种植情况的；  
（三）未依照规定储存麻醉药品的。

**Article 66** Where an enterprise cultivating narcotic drug raw plants, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its cultivation qualification shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to cultivate in accordance with the annual cultivation plan for narcotic drug raw plants;  
(2) failing to report the cultivation situation in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to store narcotic drugs in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　定点生产企业违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由药品监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并没收违法所得和违法销售的药品；逾期不改正的，责令停产，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，取消其定点生产资格：  
（一）未按照麻醉药品和精神药品年度生产计划安排生产的；  
（二）未依照规定向药品监督管理部门报告生产情况的；  
（三）未依照规定储存麻醉药品和精神药品，或者未依照规定建立、保存专用账册的；  
（四）未依照规定销售麻醉药品和精神药品的；  
（五）未依照规定销毁麻醉药品和精神药品的。

**Article 67** Where a designated manufacturer, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally sold drugs; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production and shall also be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its designation as a manufacturer shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to arrange production in accordance with the annual production plan for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances;  
(2) failing to report the production situation to the drug regulatory department in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to store narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions, or failing to establish or keep special ledgers in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) failing to sell narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) failing to destroy narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　定点批发企业违反本条例的规定销售麻醉药品和精神药品，或者违反本条例的规定经营麻醉药品原料药和第一类精神药品原料药的，由药品监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并没收违法所得和违法销售的药品；逾期不改正的，责令停业，并处违法销售药品货值金额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，取消其定点批发资格。

**Article 68** Where a designated wholesale enterprise sells narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, or deals in narcotic drug raw materials and raw materials for psychotropic substances of Category I in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally sold drugs; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend business and shall also be fined not less than two times but not more than five times the value of the illegally sold drugs; if the circumstances are serious, its designation as a wholesale enterprise shall be revoked.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　定点批发企业违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由药品监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，责令停业，并处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，取消其定点批发资格：  
（一）未依照规定购进麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的；  
（二）未保证供药责任区域内的麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的供应的；  
（三）未对医疗机构履行送货义务的；  
（四）未依照规定报告麻醉药品和精神药品的进货、销售、库存数量以及流向的；  
（五）未依照规定储存麻醉药品和精神药品，或者未依照规定建立、保存专用账册的；  
（六）未依照规定销毁麻醉药品和精神药品的；  
（七）区域性批发企业之间违反本条例的规定调剂麻醉药品和第一类精神药品，或者因特殊情况调剂麻醉药品和第一类精神药品后未依照规定备案的。

**Article 69** Where a designated wholesale enterprise, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend business and shall also be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its designation as a wholesale enterprise shall be revoked:  
(1) failing to purchase narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to ensure the supply of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I within its responsible supply area;  
(3) failing to perform the obligation of delivering drugs to medical institutions;  
(4) failing to report the purchase, sale, inventory quantities and flow directions of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) failing to store narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions, or failing to establish or keep special ledgers in accordance with the provisions;  
(6) failing to destroy narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions;  
(7) where regional wholesale enterprises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, adjust narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I among themselves, or fail to file for the record in accordance with the provisions after adjusting narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I due to special circumstances.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　第二类精神药品零售企业违反本条例的规定储存、销售或者销毁第二类精神药品的，由药品监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告，并没收违法所得和违法销售的药品；逾期不改正的，责令停业，并处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，取消其第二类精神药品零售资格。

**Article 70** Where a retail enterprise for psychotropic substances of Category II stores, sells, or destroys psychotropic substances of Category II in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally sold drugs; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend business and shall also be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its retail qualification for psychotropic substances of Category II shall be revoked.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　[本条例第三十四条](#art-34)、第三十五条规定的单位违反本条例的规定，购买麻醉药品和精神药品的，由药品监督管理部门没收违法购买的麻醉药品和精神药品，责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，责令停产或者停止相关活动，并处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Where a unit specified in Articles 34 and 35 of these Regulations, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, purchases narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the drug regulatory department shall confiscate the illegally purchased narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, order it to make corrections within a time limit, and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production or cease the relevant activities and shall also be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　取得印鉴卡的医疗机构违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销其印鉴卡；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分：  
（一）未依照规定购买、储存麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的；  
（二）未依照规定保存麻醉药品和精神药品专用处方，或者未依照规定进行处方专册登记的；  
（三）未依照规定报告麻醉药品和精神药品的进货、库存、使用数量的；  
（四）紧急借用麻醉药品和第一类精神药品后未备案的；  
（五）未依照规定销毁麻醉药品和精神药品的。

**Article 72** Where a medical institution that has obtained a seal-verification card, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the competent health department of the people's government of the city divided into districts shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its seal-verification card shall be revoked; and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or expulsion according to law:  
(1) failing to purchase and store narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to keep the special prescriptions for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions, or failing to make special prescription registration in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to report the purchase, inventory, and usage quantities of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) failing to file for the record after emergency borrowing of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I;  
(5) failing to destroy narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　具有麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师，违反本条例的规定开具麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方，或者未按照临床应用指导原则的要求使用麻醉药品和第一类精神药品的，由其所在医疗机构取消其麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销其执业证书。执业医师未按照临床应用指导原则的要求使用第二类精神药品或者未使用专用处方开具第二类精神药品，造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销其执业证书。  
未取得麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方资格的执业医师擅自开具麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门给予警告，暂停其执业活动；造成严重后果的，吊销其执业证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
处方的调配人、核对人违反本条例的规定未对麻醉药品和第一类精神药品处方进行核对，造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销其执业证书。

**Article 73** Where a practicing physician who has the qualification to prescribe narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, prescribes narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, or uses narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I not in accordance with the requirements of the clinical application guidelines, the medical institution where he or she works shall revoke his or her qualification to prescribe narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing authority shall revoke his or her practice certificate. Where a practicing physician uses psychotropic substances of Category II not in accordance with the requirements of the clinical application guidelines or does not use a special prescription to prescribe psychotropic substances of Category II, causing serious consequences, the original issuing authority shall revoke his or her practice certificate.  
Where a practicing physician who does not have the qualification to prescribe narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I arbitrarily prescribes narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, the competent health department of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and suspend his or her practice activities; if serious consequences are caused, his or her practice certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a dispenser or checker of prescriptions, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to check the prescriptions for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, causing serious consequences, the original issuing authority shall revoke his or her practice certificate.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本条例的规定运输麻醉药品和精神药品的，由药品监督管理部门和运输管理部门依照各自职责，责令改正，给予警告，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款。  
收寄麻醉药品、精神药品的邮政营业机构未依照本条例的规定办理邮寄手续的，由邮政主管部门责令改正，给予警告；造成麻醉药品、精神药品邮件丢失的，依照邮政法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 74** Where narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances are transported in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department and the transport administration department shall, within their respective functions and powers, order corrections, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
Where a postal operating entity that accepts narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by mail fails to handle the mailing procedures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the competent postal department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the mail containing narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances is lost, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of postal laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　提供虚假材料、隐瞒有关情况，或者采取其他欺骗手段取得麻醉药品和精神药品的实验研究、生产、经营、使用资格的，由原审批部门撤销其已取得的资格，5年内不得提出有关麻醉药品和精神药品的申请；情节严重的，处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款，有药品生产许可证、药品经营许可证、医疗机构执业许可证的，依法吊销其许可证明文件。

**Article 75** Where a person obtains the qualification for experimental research, production, operation, or use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by providing false materials, concealing relevant information, or by other fraudulent means, the original examination and approval authority shall revoke the qualification already obtained, and the person shall not be allowed to submit any application relating to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances within five years; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the person holds a drug manufacturing license, a drug distribution license, or a medical institution practice license, such license shall be revoked according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　药品研究单位在普通药品的实验研究和研制过程中，产生本条例规定管制的麻醉药品和精神药品，未依照本条例的规定报告的，由药品监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告，没收违法药品；拒不改正的，责令停止实验研究和研制活动。

**Article 76** Where a drug research unit, during the experimental research and development of ordinary drugs, produces narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances controlled under these Regulations but fails to report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections, give a warning, and confiscate the illegal drugs; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to stop the experimental research and development activities.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　药物临床试验机构以健康人为麻醉药品和第一类精神药品临床试验的受试对象的，由药品监督管理部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告；情节严重的，取消其药物临床试验机构的资格；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。对受试对象造成损害的，药物临床试验机构依法承担治疗和赔偿责任。

**Article 77** Where a drug clinical trial institution uses healthy persons as subjects for clinical trials of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances of Category I, the drug regulatory department shall order it to cease the illegal act and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification as a drug clinical trial institution shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If damage is caused to the subjects, the drug clinical trial institution shall assume liability for treatment and compensation according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　定点生产企业、定点批发企业和第二类精神药品零售企业生产、销售假劣麻醉药品和精神药品的，由药品监督管理部门取消其定点生产资格、定点批发资格或者第二类精神药品零售资格，并依照药品管理法的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 78** Where a designated manufacturer, a designated wholesale enterprise, or a retail enterprise for psychotropic substances of Category II produces or sells counterfeit or substandard narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the drug regulatory department shall revoke its designation as a manufacturer, designation as a wholesale enterprise, or retail qualification for psychotropic substances of Category II, and shall impose penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　定点生产企业、定点批发企业和其他单位使用现金进行麻醉药品和精神药品交易的，由药品监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告，没收违法交易的药品，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 79** Where a designated manufacturer, a designated wholesale enterprise, or any other unit uses cash to conduct transactions in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the drug regulatory department shall order it to make corrections, give a warning, confiscate the drugs involved in the illegal transaction, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　发生麻醉药品和精神药品被盗、被抢、丢失案件的单位，违反本条例的规定未采取必要的控制措施或者未依照本条例的规定报告的，由药品监督管理部门和卫生主管部门依照各自职责，责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款；有上级主管部门的，由其上级主管部门对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级、撤职的处分。

**Article 80** Where a unit that has experienced theft, robbery, or loss of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances fails to take necessary control measures or fails to report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the drug regulatory department and the competent health department shall, within their respective functions and powers, order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the unit has a competent department at a higher level, such competent department at a higher level shall impose sanctions of demotion or removal from office on the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　依法取得麻醉药品药用原植物种植或者麻醉药品和精神药品实验研究、生产、经营、使用、运输等资格的单位，倒卖、转让、出租、出借、涂改其麻醉药品和精神药品许可证明文件的，由原审批部门吊销相应许可证明文件，没收违法所得；情节严重的，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** Where a unit that has lawfully obtained the qualification for cultivating narcotic drug raw plants or for experimental research, production, operation, use, or transport of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances scalps, transfers, leases, lends, or alters its license documents for narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the original examination and approval authority shall revoke the relevant license documents and confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本条例的规定，致使麻醉药品和精神药品流入非法渠道造成危害，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上公安机关处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；由原发证部门吊销其药品生产、经营和使用许可证明文件。  
药品监督管理部门、卫生主管部门在监督管理工作中发现前款规定情形的，应当立即通报所在地同级公安机关，并依照国家有关规定，将案件以及相关材料移送公安机关。

**Article 82** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances flow into illegal channels, causing harm, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if a crime is not constituted, the public security organ at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; and the original issuing authority shall revoke its drug production, operation, and use license documents.  
Where the drug regulatory department or the competent health department discovers the circumstances prescribed in the preceding paragraph during supervision and administration work, it shall immediately notify the public security organ at the same level at its locality, and shall transfer the case and relevant materials to the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本章规定由药品监督管理部门作出的行政处罚，由县级以上药品监督管理部门按照国务院药品监督管理部门规定的职责分工决定。

**Article 83** The administrative penalties prescribed in this Chapter to be imposed by the drug regulatory department shall be decided by the drug regulatory department at or above the county level in accordance with the division of functions and powers specified by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　本条例所称实验研究是指以医疗、科学研究或者教学为目的的临床前药物研究。

**Article 84** For the purposes of these Regulations, “experimental research” means preclinical drug research conducted for the purposes of medical treatment, scientific research, or teaching.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　药用类麻醉药品中的罂粟壳只能用于中药饮片和中成药的生产以及医疗配方使用。具体管理办法由国务院药品监督管理部门另行制定。

**Article 85** Poppy shells among medicinal narcotic drugs may only be used for the production of pieces of Chinese herbal medicines and Chinese patent medicines and for medical prescription use. The specific measures for administration shall be separately formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　生产含麻醉药品的复方制剂，需要购进、储存、使用麻醉药品原料药的，应当遵守本条例有关麻醉药品管理的规定。

**Article 86** Where the production of compound preparations containing narcotic drugs requires the purchase, storage, and use of narcotic drug raw materials, the provisions of these Regulations on the administration of narcotic drugs shall be observed.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管理的具体办法，由国务院公安部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院卫生主管部门依据本条例制定。

**Article 87** The specific measures for the administration of non-medicinal narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council jointly with the drug regulatory department under the State Council and the competent health department under the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　军队医疗机构麻醉药品和精神药品的供应、使用，由国务院药品监督管理部门会同中央军事委员会后勤保障部依据本条例制定具体管理办法。

**Article 88** The specific measures for the administration of the supply and use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by military medical institutions shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department under the State Council jointly with the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　对动物用麻醉药品和精神药品的管理，由国务院兽医主管部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门依据本条例制定具体管理办法。

**Article 89** The specific measures for the administration of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances used in animals shall be formulated by the competent veterinary department under the State Council jointly with the drug regulatory department under the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　本条例自2005年11月1日起施行。1987年11月28日国务院发布的《麻醉药品管理办法》和1988年12月27日国务院发布的《精神药品管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 90** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2005. The Measures for the Administration of Narcotic Drugs promulgated by the State Council on November 28, 1987, and the Measures for the Administration of Psychotropic Substances promulgated by the State Council on December 27, 1988, shall be abolished simultaneously.
