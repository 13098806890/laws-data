# 实验动物管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01

> (1988年10月31日国务院批准　1988年11月14日国家科学技术委员会令第2号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年7月18日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2017年3月1日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强实验动物的管理工作，保证实验动物质量，适应科学研究、经济建设和社会发展的需要，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称实验动物，是指经人工饲育，对其携带的微生物实行控制，遗传背景明确或者来源清楚的，用于科学研究、教学、生产、检定以及其他科学实验的动物。 第三条　本条例适用于从事实验动物的研究、保种、饲育、供应、应用、管理和监督的单位和个人。 第四条　实验动物的管理，应当遵循统一规划、合理分工，有利于促进实验动物科学研究和应用的原则。 第五条　国家科学技术委员会主管全国实验动物工作。 省、自治区、直辖市科学技术委员会主管本地区的实验动物工作。 国务院各有关部门负责管理本部门的实验动物工作。 第六条　国家实行实验动物的质量监督和质量合格认证制度。具体办法由国家科学技术委员会另行制定。 第七条　实验动物遗传学、微生物学、营养学和饲育环境等方面的国家标准由国家技术监督局制定。 第二章　实验动物的饲育管理 第八条　从事实验动物饲育工作的单位，必须根据遗传学、微生物学、营养学和饲育环境方面的标准，定期对实验动物进行质量监测。各项作业过程和监测数据应有完整、准确的记录，并建立统计报告制度。 第九条　实验动物的饲育室、实验室应设在不同区域，并进行严格隔离。 实验动物饲育室、实验室要有科学的管理制度和操作规程。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强实验动物的管理工作，保证实验动物质量，适应科学研究、经济建设和社会发展的需要，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of laboratory animals, ensure the quality of laboratory animals, and meet the needs of scientific research, economic construction, and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称实验动物，是指经人工饲育，对其携带的微生物实行控制，遗传背景明确或者来源清楚的，用于科学研究、教学、生产、检定以及其他科学实验的动物。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "laboratory animals" means animals that are artificially bred, with micro-organisms carried by them controlled, having a clear genetic background or known origin, and used for scientific research, teaching, production, testing, and other scientific experiments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例适用于从事实验动物的研究、保种、饲育、供应、应用、管理和监督的单位和个人。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to entities and individuals engaged in the research, conservation, breeding, supply, application, administration, and supervision of laboratory animals.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实验动物的管理，应当遵循统一规划、合理分工，有利于促进实验动物科学研究和应用的原则。

**Article 4** The administration of laboratory animals shall follow the principles of unified planning, rational division of work, and being conducive to promoting scientific research and application of laboratory animals.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家科学技术委员会主管全国实验动物工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市科学技术委员会主管本地区的实验动物工作。  
国务院各有关部门负责管理本部门的实验动物工作。

**Article 5** The State Science and Technology Commission shall be in charge of the laboratory animal work nationwide.  
The science and technology commissions of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be in charge of the laboratory animal work in their respective regions.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of laboratory animal work within their respective departments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家实行实验动物的质量监督和质量合格认证制度。具体办法由国家科学技术委员会另行制定。

**Article 6** The State shall implement a system of quality supervision and quality certification of laboratory animals. The specific measures shall be separately formulated by the State Science and Technology Commission.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实验动物遗传学、微生物学、营养学和饲育环境等方面的国家标准由国家技术监督局制定。

**Article 7** The national standards regarding genetics, microbiology, nutrition, and breeding environment of laboratory animals shall be formulated by the State Bureau of Technical Supervision.

### 第二章　实验动物的饲育管理

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　从事实验动物饲育工作的单位，必须根据遗传学、微生物学、营养学和饲育环境方面的标准，定期对实验动物进行质量监测。各项作业过程和监测数据应有完整、准确的记录，并建立统计报告制度。

**Article 8** Entities engaged in the breeding of laboratory animals must conduct periodic quality monitoring of laboratory animals in accordance with the standards of genetics, microbiology, nutrition, and breeding environment. Complete and accurate records shall be kept for all operation processes and monitoring data, and a statistical reporting system shall be established.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实验动物的饲育室、实验室应设在不同区域，并进行严格隔离。  
实验动物饲育室、实验室要有科学的管理制度和操作规程。

**Article 9** The breeding rooms and laboratories for laboratory animals shall be located in different areas and shall be strictly isolated.  
The breeding rooms and laboratories for laboratory animals shall have scientific management systems and operating procedures.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实验动物的保种、饲育应采用国内或国外认可的品种、品系，并持有效的合格证书。

**Article 10** The conservation and breeding of laboratory animals shall adopt domestic or foreign approved varieties and strains, and valid certificates of conformity shall be held.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　实验动物必须按照不同来源，不同品种、品系和不同的实验目的，分开饲养。

**Article 11** Laboratory animals must be reared separately according to different sources, different breeds, strains and different experimental purposes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　实验动物分为四级：一级，普通动物；二级，清洁动物；三级，无特定病原体动物；四级，无菌动物。  
对不同等级的实验动物，应当按照相应的微生物控制标准进行管理。

**Article 12** Laboratory animals are divided into four grades: Grade I, conventional animals; Grade II, clean animals; Grade III, specific pathogen-free animals; Grade IV, germ-free animals.  
Laboratory animals of different grades shall be managed according to the corresponding microbiological control standards.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　实验动物必须饲喂质量合格的全价饲料。霉烂、变质、虫蛀、污染的饲料，不得用于饲喂实验动物。直接用作饲料的蔬菜、水果等，要经过清洗消毒，并保持新鲜。

**Article 13** Laboratory animals must be fed with qualified full-price feed of acceptable quality. Mouldy, deteriorated, insect-infested or contaminated feed shall not be used to feed laboratory animals. Vegetables, fruits, etc., directly used as feed shall be cleaned and disinfected and kept fresh.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　一级实验动物的饮水，应当符合城市生活饮水的卫生标准。二、三、四级实验动物的饮水，应当符合城市生活饮水的卫生标准并经灭菌处理。

**Article 14** The drinking water for Grade I laboratory animals shall comply with the sanitary standards for urban domestic drinking water. The drinking water for Grade II, III and IV laboratory animals shall comply with the sanitary standards for urban domestic drinking water and be sterilized.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　实验动物的垫料应当按照不同等级实验动物的需要，进行相应处理，达到清洁、干燥、吸水、无毒、无虫、无感染源、无污染。

**Article 15** The bedding for laboratory animals shall be treated accordingly as required by laboratory animals of different grades, and shall be clean, dry, absorbent, non-toxic, free from insects, free from sources of infection and free from contamination.

### 第三章　实验动物的检疫和传染病控制

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对引入的实验动物，必须进行隔离检疫。  
为补充种源或开发新品种而捕捉的野生动物，必须在当地进行隔离检疫，并取得动物检疫部门出具的证明。野生动物运抵实验动物处所，需经再次检疫，方可进入实验动物饲育室。

**Article 16** Introduced laboratory animals must undergo quarantine in isolation.  
Wild animals captured for replenishing breeding stock or developing new breeds must undergo quarantine in isolation at the local place, and obtain a certificate issued by the animal quarantine authority. Wild animals transported to the laboratory animal facility shall undergo quarantine again before they may enter the laboratory animal rearing room.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对必须进行预防接种的实验动物，应当根据实验要求或者按照《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》的有关规定，进行预防接种，但用作生物制品原料的实验动物除外。

**Article 17** Laboratory animals that must undergo preventive vaccination shall be vaccinated according to the experimental requirements or in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China, except for laboratory animals used as raw materials for biological products.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　实验动物患病死亡的，应当及时查明原因，妥善处理，并记录在案。  
实验动物患有传染性疾病的，必须立即视情况分别予以销毁或者隔离治疗。对可能被传染的实验动物，进行紧急预防接种，对饲育室内外可能被污染的区域采取严格消毒措施，并报告上级实验动物管理部门和当地动物检疫、卫生防疫单位，采取紧急预防措施，防止疫病蔓延。

**Article 18** Where laboratory animals die of diseases, the cause shall be promptly identified, properly handled, and recorded.  
Where laboratory animals suffer from infectious diseases, they must be immediately destroyed or isolated for treatment depending on the circumstances. Emergency preventive vaccination shall be given to laboratory animals that may be infected, strict disinfection measures shall be taken in areas that may be contaminated inside and outside the rearing room, and the higher-level laboratory animal administrative department and the local animal quarantine and health and epidemic prevention units shall be reported to take emergency preventive measures to prevent the spread of the epidemic.

### 第四章　实验动物的应用

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　应用实验动物应当根据不同的实验目的，选用相应的合格实验动物。申报科研课题和鉴定科研成果，应当把应用合格实验动物作为基本条件。应用不合格实验动物取得的检定或者安全评价结果无效，所生产的制品不得使用。

**Article 19** In the application of laboratory animals, corresponding qualified laboratory animals shall be selected according to different experimental purposes. When applying for scientific research projects and appraising scientific research achievements, the use of qualified laboratory animals shall be taken as a basic condition. The results of verification or safety evaluation obtained by using unqualified laboratory animals shall be invalid, and the products so produced shall not be used.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　供应用的实验动物应当具备下列完整的资料：  
(一)品种、品系及亚系的确切名称；  
(二)遗传背景或其来源；  
(三)微生物检测状况；  
(四)合格证书；  
(五)饲育单位负责人签名。  
无上述资料的实验动物不得应用。

**Article 20** Laboratory animals supplied for use shall have the following complete data:  
(1) exact names of breed, strain and substrain;  
(2) genetic background or source thereof;  
(3) microbiological detection status;  
(4) certificate of conformity;  
(5) signature of the person in charge of the rearing unit.  
Laboratory animals without the above data shall not be used.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　实验动物的运输工作应当有专人负责。实验动物的装运工具应当安全、可靠。不得将不同品种、品系或者不同等级的实验动物混合装运。

**Article 21** Transport of laboratory animals shall be handled by specially assigned personnel. The containers for transporting laboratory animals shall be safe and reliable. Different species, strains, or grades of laboratory animals shall not be mixed in transport.

### 第五章　实验动物的进口与出口管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从国外进口作为原种的实验动物，应附有饲育单位负责人签发的品系和亚系名称以及遗传和微生物状况等资料。  
无上述资料的实验动物不得进口和应用。

**Article 22** Laboratory animals imported from abroad as original breeding stock shall be accompanied by documents, signed by the person in charge of the breeding unit, indicating the name of the strain and substrain, as well as the genetic and microbiological status.  
Laboratory animals without the above documents shall not be imported or used.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出口应用国家重点保护的野生动物物种开发的实验动物，必须按照国家的有关规定，取得出口许可证后，方可办理出口手续。

**Article 23** For the export of laboratory animals developed from wild animal species under key state protection, the exporter must obtain an export license in accordance with the relevant state provisions before going through the export formalities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　进口、出口实验动物的检疫工作，按照《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》的规定办理。

**Article 24** Quarantine for the import and export of laboratory animals shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Exiting the Country.

### 第六章　从事实验动物工作的人员

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　实验动物工作单位应当根据需要，配备科技人员和经过专业培训的饲育人员。各类人员都要遵守实验动物饲育管理的各项制度，熟悉、掌握操作规程。

**Article 25** Units engaged in laboratory animal work shall, as needed, staff with scientific and technical personnel and breeding personnel who have received specialized training. All personnel shall comply with the various rules for the breeding and management of laboratory animals, and be familiar with and master the operating procedures.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　实验动物工作单位对直接接触实验动物的工作人员，必须定期组织体格检查。对患有传染性疾病，不宜承担所做工作的人员，应当及时调换工作。

**Article 26** Units engaged in laboratory animal work shall regularly arrange physical examinations for personnel who directly come into contact with laboratory animals. Personnel who suffer from infectious diseases and are unsuitable for their assigned work shall be promptly transferred to other duties.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事实验动物工作的人员对实验动物必须爱护，不得戏弄或虐待。

**Article 27** Personnel engaged in laboratory animal work must treat laboratory animals with care and shall not tease or abuse them.

### 第七章　奖励与处罚

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对长期从事实验动物饲育管理，取得显著成绩的单位或者个人，由管理实验动物工作的部门给予表彰或奖励。

**Article 28** Units or individuals that have long been engaged in the breeding and management of laboratory animals and have achieved notable results shall be commended or rewarded by the department in charge of laboratory animal work.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对违反本条例规定的单位，由管理实验动物工作的部门视情节轻重，分别给予警告、限期改进、责令关闭的行政处罚。

**Article 29** Units that violate these Regulations shall, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, be given administrative penalties by the department in charge of laboratory animal work, including warnings, ordering rectification within a time limit, or ordering closure.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对违反本条例规定的有关工作人员，由其所在单位视情节轻重，根据国家有关规定，给予行政处分。

**Article 30** Staff members concerned who violate these Regulations shall, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, be given administrative sanctions by their units in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院有关部门，可以根据本条例，结合具体情况，制定实施办法。  
军队系统的实验动物管理工作参照本条例执行。

**Article 31** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as the relevant departments of the State Council, may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations and in light of specific circumstances.  
The management of experimental animals within the military system shall be carried out with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例由国家科学技术委员会负责解释。

**Article 32** The interpretation of these Regulations shall be the responsibility of the State Science and Technology Commission.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 33** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
