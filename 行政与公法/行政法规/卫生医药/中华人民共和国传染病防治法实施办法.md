**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1991-12-06  
**生效日期**：1991-12-06

> (1991年10月4日国务院批准　1991年12月6日卫生部令第17号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》(以下简称《传染病防治法》)的规定，制定本办法。 第二条　国家对传染病实行预防为主的方针，各级政府在制定社会经济发展规划时，必须包括传染病防治目标，并组织有关部门共同实施。 第三条　各级政府卫生行政部门对传染病防治工作实施统一监督管理。 受国务院卫生行政部门委托的其他有关部门卫生主管机构，在本系统内行使《传染病防治法》第三十二条第一款所列职权。 军队的传染病防治工作，依照《传染病防治法》和本办法中的有关规定以及国家其他有关规定，由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门实施监督管理。 第四条　各级各类卫生防疫机构按照专业分工承担传染病监测管理的责任和范围，由省级政府卫生行政部门确定。 铁路、交通、民航、厂(场)矿的卫生防疫机构，承担本系统传染病监测管理工作，并接受本系统上级卫生主管机构和省级政府卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构的业务指导。 第五条　各级各类医疗保健机构承担传染病防治管理的责任和范围，由当地政府卫生行政部门确定。 第六条　各级政府对预防、控制传染病做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，应当给予奖励。 第二章　预防 第七条　各级政府应当组织有关部门，开展传染病预防知识和防治措施的卫生健康教育。 第八条　各级政府组织开展爱国卫生活动。 铁路、交通、民航部门负责组织消除交通工具的鼠害和各种病媒昆虫的危害。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》(以下简称《传染病防治法》)的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases (hereinafter referred to as the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对传染病实行预防为主的方针，各级政府在制定社会经济发展规划时，必须包括传染病防治目标，并组织有关部门共同实施。

**Article 2** The State shall adopt a policy of prevention first for infectious diseases. When formulating socio-economic development plans, governments at all levels must include goals for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, and shall organize the relevant departments to implement them jointly.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　各级政府卫生行政部门对传染病防治工作实施统一监督管理。  
受国务院卫生行政部门委托的其他有关部门卫生主管机构，在本系统内行使[《传染病防治法》第三十二条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-32)第一款所列职权。  
军队的传染病防治工作，依照《传染病防治法》和本办法中的有关规定以及国家其他有关规定，由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门实施监督管理。

**Article 3** The health administrative departments of governments at all levels shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.  
The health competent authorities of other relevant departments entrusted by the health administrative department of the State Council shall exercise the powers listed in Article 32, Paragraph 1 of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases within their respective systems.  
The prevention and treatment of infectious diseases in the armed forces shall be subject to supervision and administration by the health competent authority of the Chinese People's Liberation Army in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases, the relevant provisions of these Measures, and other relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级各类卫生防疫机构按照专业分工承担传染病监测管理的责任和范围，由省级政府卫生行政部门确定。  
铁路、交通、民航、厂(场)矿的卫生防疫机构，承担本系统传染病监测管理工作，并接受本系统上级卫生主管机构和省级政府卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构的业务指导。

**Article 4** The responsibilities and scope of infectious disease surveillance and management undertaken by health and anti-epidemic institutions of various levels and types according to their professional division of labor shall be determined by the health administrative departments of provincial governments.  
Health and anti-epidemic institutions of railways, communications, civil aviation, and factories (mines) shall undertake the infectious disease surveillance and management of their respective systems, and shall accept professional guidance from the higher health competent authorities of their systems and from the health and anti-epidemic institutions designated by the health administrative departments of provincial governments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级各类医疗保健机构承担传染病防治管理的责任和范围，由当地政府卫生行政部门确定。

**Article 5** The responsibilities and scope of infectious disease prevention and treatment management undertaken by medical and health care institutions of various levels and types shall be determined by the health administrative departments of local governments.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级政府对预防、控制传染病做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，应当给予奖励。

**Article 6** Governments at all levels shall reward units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements and contributions in the prevention and control of infectious diseases.

### 第二章　预防

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级政府应当组织有关部门，开展传染病预防知识和防治措施的卫生健康教育。

**Article 7** Governments at all levels shall organize relevant departments to carry out health education on knowledge of infectious disease prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级政府组织开展爱国卫生活动。  
铁路、交通、民航部门负责组织消除交通工具的鼠害和各种病媒昆虫的危害。  
农业、林业部门负责组织消除农田、牧场及林区的鼠害。  
国务院各有关部委消除钉螺危害的分工，按照国务院的有关规定办理。

**Article 8** Governments at all levels shall organize and carry out patriotic health campaigns.  
Departments of railways, communications, and civil aviation shall be responsible for organizing the elimination of rodent pests and hazards of various vector insects on means of transportation.  
Departments of agriculture and forestry shall be responsible for organizing the elimination of rodent pests in farmlands, pastures, and forest areas.  
The division of responsibilities among the relevant ministries and commissions under the State Council for the elimination of snail hazards shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　集中式供水必须符合国家《生活饮用水卫生标准》。  
各单位自备水源，未经城市建设部门和卫生行政部门批准，不得与城镇集中式供水系统连接。

**Article 9** Centralized water supply must conform to the national Hygienic Standard for Drinking Water.  
Water sources self-provided by various units shall not be connected to the urban centralized water supply system without approval from the urban construction department and the health administrative department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方各级政府应当有计划地建设和改造公共卫生设施。  
城市应当按照城市环境卫生设施标准修建公共厕所、垃圾粪便的无害化处理场和污水、雨水排放处理系统等公共卫生设施。  
农村应当逐步改造厕所，对粪便进行无害化处理，加强对公共生活用水的卫生管理，建立必要的卫生管理制度。饮用水水源附近禁止有污水池、粪堆(坑)等污染源。禁止在饮用水水源附近洗刷便器和运输粪便的工具。

**Article 10** Local governments at all levels shall plan to construct and renovate public health facilities.  
Cities shall construct public toilets, harmless treatment sites for garbage and feces, and sewage and rainwater discharge treatment systems and other public health facilities in accordance with urban environmental sanitation facility standards.  
Rural areas shall gradually renovate toilets, carry out harmless treatment of feces, strengthen the sanitation management of public domestic water, and establish necessary sanitation management systems. Sewage pits, manure heaps (pits), and other pollution sources are prohibited near drinking water sources. It is prohibited to wash toilet bowls and tools for transporting feces near drinking water sources.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家实行有计划的预防接种制度。  
中华人民共和国境内的任何人均应按照有关规定接受预防接种。  
各省、自治区、直辖市政府卫生行政部门可以根据当地传染病的流行情况，增加预防接种项目。

**Article 11** The State shall implement a planned vaccination system.  
Any person within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall receive vaccination in accordance with relevant regulations.  
The health administrative departments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may increase vaccination items based on the local epidemic situation of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对儿童实行预防接种证制度。  
适龄儿童应当按照国家有关规定，接受预防接种。适龄儿童的家长或者监护人应当及时向医疗保健机构申请办理预防接种证。  
托幼机构、学校在办理入托、入学手续时，应当查验预防接种证，未按规定接种的儿童应当及时补种。

**Article 12** The State shall implement a vaccination certificate system for children.  
Children of appropriate age shall receive vaccination in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. Parents or guardians of children of appropriate age shall promptly apply for vaccination certificates from medical and health care institutions.  
Nurseries, kindergartens, and schools shall check vaccination certificates when handling admission procedures, and children who have not been vaccinated as required shall be vaccinated in a timely manner.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级各类医疗保健机构的预防保健组织或者人员，在本单位及责任地段内承担下列工作：  
(一)传染病疫情报告和管理；  
(二)传染病预防和控制工作；  
(三)卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构交付的传染病防治和监测任务。

**Article 13** Preventive health care organizations or personnel of medical and health care institutions at various levels and types shall undertake the following work within their own units and responsible areas:  
(1) reporting and management of infectious disease epidemics;  
(2) prevention and control of infectious diseases;  
(3) tasks of infectious disease prevention, treatment, and surveillance assigned by health and anti-epidemic institutions designated by health administrative departments.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　医疗保健机构必须按照国务院卫生行政部门的有关规定，严格执行消毒隔离制度，防止医院内感染和医源性感染。

**Article 14** Medical and health care institutions must strictly implement the disinfection and isolation system in accordance with the relevant provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council to prevent nosocomial infections and iatrogenic infections.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　卫生防疫机构和从事致病性微生物实验的科研、教学、生产等单位必须做到：  
(一)建立健全防止致病性微生物扩散的制度和人体防护措施；  
(二)严格执行实验操作规程，对实验后的样品、器材、污染物品等，按照有关规定严格消毒后处理；  
(三)实验动物必须按照国家有关规定进行管理。

**Article 15** Health and anti-epidemic institutions and scientific research, teaching, and production units engaged in experiments with pathogenic microorganisms must:  
(1) establish and improve systems for preventing the spread of pathogenic microorganisms and human protection measures;  
(2) strictly implement experimental operating procedures, and strictly disinfect and then dispose of samples, equipment, and contaminated items after experiments in accordance with relevant regulations;  
(3) manage experimental animals in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　传染病的菌(毒)种分为下列三类：  
一类：鼠疫耶尔森氏菌、霍乱弧菌；天花病毒、艾滋病病毒；  
二类：布氏菌、炭疽菌、麻风杆菌；肝炎病毒、狂犬病毒、出血热病毒、登革热病毒；斑疹伤寒立克次体；  
三类：脑膜炎双球菌、链球菌、淋病双球菌、结核杆菌、百日咳嗜血杆菌、白喉棒状杆菌、沙门氏菌、志贺氏菌、破伤风梭状杆菌；钩端螺旋体、梅毒螺旋体；乙型脑炎病毒、脊髓灰质炎病毒、流感病毒、流行性腮腺炎病毒、麻疹病毒、风疹病毒。  
国务院卫生行政部门可以根据情况增加或者减少菌(毒)种的种类。

**Article 16** Bacterial (viral) strains of infectious diseases are classified into the following three categories:  
Category 1: Yersinia pestis, Vibrio cholerae; smallpox virus, HIV;  
Category 2: Brucella, Bacillus anthracis, Mycobacterium leprae; hepatitis virus, rabies virus, hemorrhagic fever virus, dengue virus; Rickettsia prowazekii;  
Category 3: Neisseria meningitidis, Streptococcus, Neisseria gonorrhoeae, Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Bordetella pertussis, Corynebacterium diphtheriae, Salmonella, Shigella, Clostridium tetani; Leptospira, Treponema pallidum; Japanese encephalitis virus, poliovirus, influenza virus, mumps virus, measles virus, rubella virus.  
The health administrative department of the State Council may increase or decrease the types of bacterial (viral) strains based on circumstances.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对传染病菌(毒)种的保藏、携带、运输实行严格管理：  
(一)菌(毒)种的保藏由国务院卫生行政部门指定的单位负责。  
(二)一、二类菌(毒)种的供应由国务院卫生行政部门指定的保藏管理单位供应。三类菌(毒)种由设有专业实验室的单位或者国务院卫生行政部门指定的保藏管理单位供应。  
(三)使用一类菌(毒)种的单位，必须经国务院卫生行政部门批准；使用二类菌(毒)种的单位必须经省级政府卫生行政部门批准；使用三类菌(毒)种的单位，应当经县级政府卫生行政部门批准。  
(四)一、二类菌(毒)种，应派专人向供应单位领取，不得邮寄；三类菌(毒)种的邮寄必须持有邮寄单位的证明，并按照菌(毒)种邮寄与包装的有关规定办理。

**Article 17** The State shall implement strict management of the preservation, carrying, and transportation of bacterial (viral) strains of infectious diseases:  
(1) The preservation of bacterial (viral) strains shall be the responsibility of units designated by the health administrative department of the State Council.  
(2) The supply of Category 1 and 2 bacterial (viral) strains shall be provided by the preservation management units designated by the health administrative department of the State Council. Category 3 bacterial (viral) strains shall be supplied by units with professional laboratories or by preservation management units designated by the health administrative department of the State Council.  
(3) Units using Category 1 bacterial (viral) strains must obtain approval from the health administrative department of the State Council; units using Category 2 bacterial (viral) strains must obtain approval from the health administrative department of provincial governments; units using Category 3 bacterial (viral) strains shall obtain approval from the health administrative department of county governments.  
(4) Category 1 and 2 bacterial (viral) strains shall be collected by designated personnel from the supplying units and shall not be sent by mail; the mailing of Category 3 bacterial (viral) strains must hold a certificate from the mailing unit and shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions on the mailing and packaging of bacterial (viral) strains.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对患有下列传染病的病人或者病原携带者予以必要的隔离治疗，直至医疗保健机构证明其不具有传染性时，方可恢复工作：  
(一)鼠疫、霍乱；  
(二)艾滋病、病毒性肝炎、细菌性和阿米巴痢疾、伤寒和副伤寒、炭疽、斑疹伤寒、麻疹、百日咳、白喉、脊髓灰质炎、流行性脑脊髓膜炎、猩红热、流行性出血热、登革热、淋病、梅毒；  
(三)肺结核、麻风病、流行性腮腺炎、风疹、急性出血性结膜炎。

**Article 18** Patients or pathogen carriers suffering from the following infectious diseases shall be given necessary isolation treatment and may resume work only after medical and health care institutions have proven that they are no longer infectious:  
(1) plague, cholera;  
(2) AIDS, viral hepatitis, bacillary and amoebic dysentery, typhoid and paratyphoid fever, anthrax, typhus, measles, pertussis, diphtheria, poliomyelitis, epidemic cerebrospinal meningitis, scarlet fever, epidemic hemorrhagic fever, dengue fever, gonorrhea, syphilis;  
(3) pulmonary tuberculosis, leprosy, mumps, rubella, acute hemorrhagic conjunctivitis.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事饮水、饮食、整容、保育等易使传染病扩散工作的从业人员，必须按照国家有关规定取得健康合格证后方可上岗。

**Article 19** Employees engaged in work that easily spreads infectious diseases, such as drinking water, food, cosmetology, and childcare, must obtain a health certificate in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State before they may take up their posts.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　招用流动人员二百人以上的用工单位，应当向当地政府卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构报告，并按照要求采取预防控制传染病的卫生措施。

**Article 20** Employers recruiting more than 200 migrant workers shall report to the health and anti-epidemic institution designated by the local government health administrative department and take sanitary measures to prevent and control infectious diseases as required.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被甲类传染病病原体污染的污水、污物、粪便，有关单位和个人必须在卫生防疫人员的指导监督下，按照下列要求进行处理：  
(一)被鼠疫病原体污染  
1．被污染的室内空气、地面、四壁必须进行严格消毒，被污染的物品必须严格消毒或者焚烧处理；  
2．彻底消除鼠疫疫区内的鼠类、蚤类；发现病鼠、死鼠应当送检；解剖检验后的鼠尸必须焚化；  
3．疫区内啮齿类动物的皮毛不能就地进行有效的消毒处理时，必须在卫生防疫机构的监督下焚烧。  
(二)被霍乱病原体污染  
1．被污染的饮用水，必须进行严格消毒处理；  
2．污水经消毒处理后排放；  
3．被污染的食物要就地封存，消毒处理；  
4．粪便消毒处理达到无害化；  
5．被污染的物品，必须进行严格消毒或者焚烧处理。

**Article 21** For sewage, waste, and feces contaminated by pathogens of Category A infectious diseases, the relevant units and individuals must, under the guidance and supervision of health and anti-epidemic personnel, handle them in accordance with the following requirements:  
(1) Contamination by plague pathogens  
1. Contaminated indoor air, floors, and walls must be strictly disinfected; contaminated items must be strictly disinfected or incinerated;  
2. Rodents and fleas in the plague epidemic area must be completely eliminated; sick or dead rats found shall be sent for examination; rat carcasses after dissection and examination must be cremated;  
3. When the fur of rodents in the epidemic area cannot be effectively disinfected on site, they must be incinerated under the supervision of a health and anti-epidemic institution.  
(2) Contamination by cholera pathogens  
1. Contaminated drinking water must be strictly disinfected;  
2. Sewage shall be discharged after disinfection treatment;  
3. Contaminated food shall be sealed on site and disinfected;  
4. Feces shall be disinfected to achieve harmlessness;  
5. Contaminated items must be strictly disinfected or incinerated.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被伤寒和副伤寒、细菌性痢疾、脊髓灰质炎、病毒性肝炎病原体污染的水、物品、粪便，有关单位和个人应当按照下列要求进行处理：  
(一)被污染的饮用水，应当进行严格消毒处理；  
(二)污水经消毒处理后排放；  
(三)被污染的物品，应当进行严格消毒处理或者焚烧处理；  
(四)粪便消毒处理达到无害化。  
死于炭疽的动物尸体必须就地焚化，被污染的用具必须消毒处理，被污染的土地、草皮消毒后，必须将10厘米厚的表层土铲除，并在远离水源及河流的地方深埋。

**Article 22** For water, items, and feces contaminated by pathogens of typhoid and paratyphoid fever, bacillary dysentery, poliomyelitis, and viral hepatitis, the relevant units and individuals shall handle them in accordance with the following requirements:  
(1) Contaminated drinking water shall be strictly disinfected;  
(2) Sewage shall be discharged after disinfection treatment;  
(3) Contaminated items shall be strictly disinfected or incinerated;  
(4) Feces shall be disinfected to achieve harmlessness.  
Animal carcasses that died of anthrax must be cremated on site, contaminated tools must be disinfected, and after disinfection of contaminated land and turf, the top 10 cm thick layer of soil must be removed and buried deep away from water sources and rivers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出售、运输被传染病病原体污染或者来自疫区可能被传染病病原体污染的皮毛、旧衣物及生活用品等，必须按照卫生防疫机构的要求进行必要的卫生处理。

**Article 23** The sale or transportation of fur, old clothing, and daily necessities that are contaminated by infectious disease pathogens or from epidemic areas and may be contaminated by infectious disease pathogens must undergo necessary sanitary treatment in accordance with the requirements of the health and anti-epidemic institution.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用于预防传染病的菌苗、疫苗等生物制品，由各省、自治区、直辖市卫生防疫机构统一向生物制品生产单位订购，其他任何单位和个人不得经营。  
用于预防传染病的菌苗、疫苗等生物制品必须在卫生防疫机构监督指导下使用。

**Article 24** Biological products such as bacterial vaccines and viral vaccines for preventing infectious diseases shall be uniformly ordered from biological product production units by the health and anti-epidemic institutions of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; no other units or individuals may deal in them.  
Biological products such as bacterial vaccines and viral vaccines for preventing infectious diseases must be used under the supervision and guidance of health and anti-epidemic institutions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　凡从事可能导致经血液传播传染病的美容、整容等单位和个人，必须执行国务院卫生行政部门的有关规定。

**Article 25** Any units and individuals engaged in cosmetology or plastic surgery that may lead to blood-borne infectious diseases must comply with the relevant provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　血站(库)、生物制品生产单位，必须严格执行国务院卫生行政部门的有关规定，保证血液、血液制品的质量，防止因输入血液、血液制品引起病毒性肝炎、艾滋病、疟疾等疾病的发生。任何单位和个人不准使用国务院卫生行政部门禁止进口的血液和血液制品。

**Article 26** Blood stations (banks) and biological product production units must strictly implement the relevant provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council, ensure the quality of blood and blood products, and prevent the occurrence of diseases such as viral hepatitis, AIDS, and malaria caused by transfusion of blood or blood products. No unit or individual may use blood or blood products whose import is prohibited by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　生产、经营、使用消毒药剂和消毒器械、卫生用品、卫生材料、一次性医疗器材、隐形眼镜、人造器官等必须符合国家有关标准，不符合国家有关标准的不得生产、经营和使用。

**Article 27** The production, operation, and use of disinfectants and disinfection devices, sanitary products, sanitary materials, disposable medical equipment, contact lenses, artificial organs, etc. must comply with relevant national standards; those that do not meet the relevant national standards shall not be produced, operated, or used.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　发现人畜共患传染病已在人、畜间流行时，卫生行政部门与畜牧兽医部门应当深入疫区，按照职责分别对人、畜开展防治工作。  
传染病流行区的家畜家禽，未经畜牧兽医部门检疫不得外运。  
进入鼠疫自然疫源地捕猎旱獭应按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 28** When a zoonotic infectious disease is found to be prevalent among humans and animals, the health administrative department and the animal husbandry and veterinary department shall go deep into the epidemic area and carry out prevention and treatment work for humans and animals respectively according to their duties.  
Livestock and poultry in infectious disease epidemic areas shall not be transported out without quarantine by the animal husbandry and veterinary department.  
Entering a natural plague focus to hunt marmots shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　狂犬病的防治管理工作按照下列规定分工负责：  
(一)公安部门负责县以上城市养犬的审批与违章养犬的处理，捕杀狂犬、野犬。  
(二)畜牧兽医部门负责兽用狂犬病疫苗的研制、生产和供应；对城乡经批准的养犬进行预防接种、登记和发放“家犬免疫证”；对犬类狂犬病的疫情进行监测和负责进出口犬类的检疫、免疫及管理。  
(三)乡(镇)政府负责辖区内养犬的管理，捕杀狂犬、野犬。  
(四)卫生部门负责人用狂犬病疫苗的供应、接种和病人的诊治。

**Article 29** The prevention, treatment, and management of rabies shall be divided among the following departments according to their responsibilities:  
(1) Public security departments shall be responsible for the approval of dog keeping in cities at and above the county level and the handling of illegal dog keeping, and for the killing of rabid and stray dogs.  
(2) Animal husbandry and veterinary departments shall be responsible for the research, production, and supply of veterinary rabies vaccines; for conducting preventive vaccination, registration, and issuing of 'Domestic Dog Immunity Certificates' for approved dog keeping in urban and rural areas; for monitoring rabies epidemics in dogs and for the quarantine, immunization, and management of imported and exported dogs.  
(3) Township (town) governments shall be responsible for the management of dog keeping within their jurisdictions and for the killing of rabid and stray dogs.  
(4) Health departments shall be responsible for the supply and vaccination of human rabies vaccines and for the diagnosis and treatment of patients.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　自然疫源地或者可能是自然疫源地的地区计划兴建大型建设项目时，建设单位在设计任务书批准后，应当向当地卫生防疫机构申请对施工环境进行卫生调查，并根据卫生防疫机构的意见采取必要的卫生防疫措施后，方可办理开工手续。  
兴建城市规划内的建设项目，属于在自然疫源地和可能是自然疫源地范围内的，城市规划主管部门在核发建设工程规划许可证明中，必须有卫生防疫部门提出的有关意见及结论。建设单位在施工过程中，必须采取预防传染病传播和扩散的措施。

**Article 30** When a large-scale construction project is planned in a natural focus or area that may be a natural focus, after the design task document is approved, the construction unit shall apply to the local health and anti-epidemic institution for a sanitary investigation of the construction environment, and may only go through the commencement procedures after taking necessary health and anti-epidemic measures based on the opinions of the health and anti-epidemic institution.  
For construction projects within urban planning that fall within the scope of natural foci or areas that may be natural foci, the urban planning competent authority must include the relevant opinions and conclusions of the health and anti-epidemic department when issuing the construction project planning permit. The construction unit must take measures to prevent the spread and dissemination of infectious diseases during the construction process.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　卫生防疫机构接到在自然疫源地和可能是自然疫源地范围内兴办大型建设项目的建设单位的卫生调查申请后，应当及时组成调查组到现场进行调查，并提出该地区自然环境中可能存在的传染病病种、流行范围、流行强度及预防措施等意见和结论。

**Article 31** After receiving an application for a sanitary investigation from a construction unit undertaking a large-scale construction project within a natural focus or area that may be a natural focus, the health and anti-epidemic institution shall promptly form an investigation team to conduct an on-site investigation and provide opinions and conclusions on the types of infectious diseases that may exist in the natural environment of the area, the scope and intensity of the epidemic, and preventive measures.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　在自然疫源地或者可能是自然疫源地内施工的建设单位，应当设立预防保健组织负责施工期间的卫生防疫工作。

**Article 32** Construction units operating in a natural focus or area that may be a natural focus shall establish a preventive health care organization to be responsible for health and anti-epidemic work during the construction period.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　凡在生产、工作中接触传染病病原体的工作人员，可以按照国家有关规定申领卫生防疫津贴。

**Article 33** Workers who come into contact with infectious disease pathogens in production or work may apply for a health and anti-epidemic allowance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第三章　疫情报告

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　执行职务的医疗保健人员、卫生防疫人员为责任疫情报告人。  
责任疫情报告人应当按照[本办法第三十五条](#art-35)规定的时限向卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构报告疫情，并做疫情登记。

**Article 34** Medical and health care personnel and health and anti-epidemic personnel on duty shall be responsible epidemic reporters.  
Responsible epidemic reporters shall report the epidemic to the health and anti-epidemic institution designated by the health administrative department within the time limit specified in Article 35 of these Measures, and shall register the epidemic.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　责任疫情报告人发现甲类传染病和乙类传染病中的艾滋病、肺炭疽的病人、病原携带者和疑似传染病病人时，城镇于六小时内，农村于十二小时内，以最快的通讯方式向发病地的卫生防疫机构报告，并同时报出传染病报告卡。  
责任疫情报告人发现乙类传染病病人、病原携带者和疑似传染病病人时，城镇于十二小时内，农村于二十四小时内向发病地的卫生防疫机构报出传染病报告卡。  
责任疫情报告人在丙类传染病监测区内发现丙类传染病病人时，应当在二十四小时内向发病地的卫生防疫机构报出传染病报告卡。

**Article 35** When a responsible epidemic reporter discovers a patient, pathogen carrier, or suspected infectious disease patient of Category A infectious diseases and AIDS and pulmonary anthrax among Category B infectious diseases, they shall report to the health and anti-epidemic institution at the place of onset within 6 hours in urban areas and within 12 hours in rural areas by the fastest means of communication, and shall simultaneously submit an infectious disease report card.  
When a responsible epidemic reporter discovers a patient, pathogen carrier, or suspected infectious disease patient of Category B infectious diseases, they shall submit an infectious disease report card to the health and anti-epidemic institution at the place of onset within 12 hours in urban areas and within 24 hours in rural areas.  
When a responsible epidemic reporter discovers a patient with a Category C infectious disease within a Category C infectious disease surveillance area, they shall submit an infectious disease report card to the health and anti-epidemic institution at the place of onset within 24 hours.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　传染病暴发、流行时，责任疫情报告人应当以最快的通讯方式向当地卫生防疫机构报告疫情。接到疫情报告的卫生防疫机构应当以最快的通讯方式报告上级卫生防疫机构和当地政府卫生行政部门，卫生行政部门接到报告后，应当立即报告当地政府。  
省级政府卫生行政部门接到发现甲类传染病和发生传染病暴发、流行的报告后，应当于六小时内报告国务院卫生行政部门。

**Article 36** When an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic occurs, the responsible epidemic reporter shall report the epidemic to the local health and anti-epidemic institution by the fastest means of communication. The health and anti-epidemic institution that receives the epidemic report shall report to the higher health and anti-epidemic institution and the local government health administrative department by the fastest means of communication. After receiving the report, the health administrative department shall immediately report to the local government.  
After receiving a report of the discovery of a Category A infectious disease or the occurrence of an infectious disease outbreak or epidemic, the health administrative department of a provincial government shall report to the health administrative department of the State Council within 6 hours.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　流动人员中的传染病病人、病原携带者和疑似传染病病人的传染病报告、处理由诊治地负责，其疫情登记、统计由户口所在地负责。

**Article 37** The reporting and handling of infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, and suspected infectious disease patients among migrant populations shall be the responsibility of the place where diagnosis and treatment are provided, and the registration and statistics of the epidemic shall be the responsibility of the place of household registration.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　铁路、交通、民航、厂(场)矿的卫生防疫机构，应当定期向所在地卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构报告疫情。

**Article 38** Health and anti-epidemic institutions of railways, communications, civil aviation, and factories (mines) shall regularly report epidemics to the health and anti-epidemic institutions designated by the local health administrative department.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　军队的传染病疫情，由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门根据军队有关规定向国务院卫生行政部门报告。  
军队的医疗保健和卫生防疫机构，发现地方就诊的传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似传染病病人时，应当按照[本办法第三十五条](#art-35)的规定报告疫情，并接受当地卫生防疫机构的业务指导。

**Article 39** The epidemic of infectious diseases in the armed forces shall be reported by the health competent authority of the Chinese People's Liberation Army to the health administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of the military.  
When medical and health care and health and anti-epidemic institutions of the armed forces discover infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, or suspected infectious disease patients from the local population seeking treatment, they shall report the epidemic in accordance with Article 35 of these Measures and accept professional guidance from the local health and anti-epidemic institutions.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国境口岸所在地卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构和港口、机场、铁路卫生防疫机构和国境卫生检疫机关在发现国境卫生检疫法规定的检疫传染病时，应当互相通报疫情。  
发现人畜共患传染病时，卫生防疫机构和畜牧兽医部门应当互相通报疫情。

**Article 40** When health and anti-epidemic institutions designated by the health administrative department of the place where a frontier port is located, as well as health and anti-epidemic institutions at ports, airports, and railways, and frontier health and quarantine authorities, discover a quarantinable infectious disease as defined in the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law, they shall notify each other of the epidemic.  
When a zoonotic infectious disease is discovered, health and anti-epidemic institutions and animal husbandry and veterinary departments shall notify each other of the epidemic.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各级政府卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构应当对辖区内各类医疗保健机构的疫情登记报告和管理情况定期进行核实、检查、指导。

**Article 41** Health and anti-epidemic institutions designated by the health administrative departments of governments at all levels shall regularly verify, inspect, and guide the epidemic registration, reporting, and management of various types of medical and health care institutions within their jurisdictions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　传染病报告卡片邮寄信封应当印有明显的“红十字”标志及写明××卫生防疫机构收的字样。  
邮电部门应当及时传递疫情报告的电话或者信卡，并实行邮资总付。

**Article 42** Envelopes for mailing infectious disease report cards shall bear a clear 'Red Cross' symbol and the words 'To be received by XX Health and Anti-epidemic Institution'.  
Post and telecommunications departments shall promptly transmit telephone calls or mail cards for epidemic reports and shall implement total postage payment.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　医务人员未经县级以上政府卫生行政部门批准，不得将就诊的淋病、梅毒、麻风病、艾滋病病人和艾滋病病原携带者及其家属的姓名、住址和个人病史公开。

**Article 43** Without approval from the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level, medical personnel shall not disclose the names, addresses, and personal medical histories of patients with gonorrhea, syphilis, leprosy, or AIDS, as well as AIDS pathogen carriers and their family members.

### 第四章　控制

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　卫生防疫机构和医疗保健机构传染病的疫情处理实行分级分工管理。

**Article 44** The epidemic handling of infectious diseases by health and anti-epidemic institutions and medical and health care institutions shall be subject to graded and divided management.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　艾滋病的监测管理按照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 45** The surveillance and management of AIDS shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　淋病、梅毒病人应当在医疗保健机构、卫生防疫机构接受治疗。尚未治愈前，不得进入公共浴池、游泳池。

**Article 46** Patients with gonorrhea and syphilis shall receive treatment in medical and health care institutions or health and anti-epidemic institutions. Before they are cured, they shall not enter public baths or swimming pools.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　医疗保健机构或者卫生防疫机构在诊治中发现甲类传染病的疑似病人，应当在二日内作出明确诊断。

**Article 47** When a medical and health care institution or a health and anti-epidemic institution discovers a suspected patient of a Category A infectious disease during diagnosis and treatment, it shall make a definite diagnosis within two days.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　甲类传染病病人和病原携带者以及乙类传染病中的艾滋病、淋病、梅毒病人的密切接触者必须按照有关规定接受检疫、医学检查和防治措施。  
前款以外的乙类传染病病人及病原携带者的密切接触者，应当接受医学检查和防治措施。

**Article 48** Close contacts of patients and pathogen carriers of Category A infectious diseases, as well as patients of AIDS, gonorrhea, and syphilis among Category B infectious diseases, must undergo quarantine, medical examination, and prevention and treatment measures in accordance with relevant regulations.  
Close contacts of patients of Category B infectious diseases other than those in the preceding paragraph and pathogen carriers shall undergo medical examination and prevention and treatment measures.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　甲类传染病疑似病人或者病原携带者的密切接触者，经留验排除是病人或者病原携带者后，留验期间的工资福利待遇由所属单位按出勤照发。

**Article 49** For close contacts of suspected patients or pathogen carriers of Category A infectious diseases, after being excluded as patients or pathogen carriers through observation, their wages and welfare benefits during the observation period shall be paid by their affiliated units as if they were on duty.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　发现甲类传染病病人、病原携带者或者疑似病人的污染场所，卫生防疫机构接到疫情报告后，应立即进行严格的卫生处理。

**Article 50** After receiving an epidemic report of a contaminated site of a patient, pathogen carrier, or suspected patient of a Category A infectious disease, the health and anti-epidemic institution shall immediately carry out strict sanitary treatment.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　地方各级政府卫生行政部门发现本地区发生从未有过的传染病或者国家已宣布消除的传染病时，应当立即采取措施，必要时，向当地政府报告。

**Article 51** When the health administrative department of a local government at any level discovers that an infectious disease which has never occurred before in the region or an infectious disease which the State has declared to be eliminated occurs, it shall immediately take measures and, if necessary, report to the local government.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在传染病暴发、流行区域，当地政府应当根据传染病疫情控制的需要，组织卫生、医药、公安、工商、交通、水利、城建、农业、商业、民政、邮电、广播电视等部门采取下列预防、控制措施：  
(一)对病人进行抢救、隔离治疗；  
(二)加强粪便管理，清除垃圾、污物；  
(三)加强自来水和其他饮用水的管理，保护饮用水源；  
(四)消除病媒昆虫、钉螺、鼠类及其他染疫动物；  
(五)加强易使传染病传播扩散活动的卫生管理；  
(六)开展防病知识的宣传；  
(七)组织对传染病病人、病原携带者、染疫动物密切接触人群的检疫、预防服药、应急接种等；  
(八)供应用于预防和控制疫情所必需的药品、生物制品、消毒药品、器械等；  
(九)保证居民生活必需品的供应。

**Article 52** In an area where an infectious disease breaks out or becomes prevalent, the local government shall, according to the needs for controlling the epidemic of the infectious disease, organize the departments of health, medicine, public security, industry and commerce, transportation, water conservancy, urban construction, agriculture, commerce, civil affairs, post and telecommunications, radio and television, etc. to take the following prevention and control measures:  
(1) carry out rescue and isolation treatment of patients;  
(2) strengthen the management of excreta and remove garbage and dirt;  
(3) strengthen the management of tap water and other drinking water, and protect drinking water sources;  
(4) eliminate vector insects, oncomelania snails, rodents and other animals infected with epidemic;  
(5) strengthen the hygiene management of activities liable to cause the spread and dissemination of infectious diseases;  
(6) conduct publicity of knowledge on disease prevention;  
(7) organize quarantine, preventive medication, emergency vaccination, etc. for persons in close contact with infectious disease patients, pathogen carriers, and animals infected with epidemic;  
(8) supply such articles as medicines, biological products, disinfection medicines, instruments, etc. necessary for prevention and control of the epidemic;  
(9) ensure the supply of daily necessities for residents.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上政府接到下一级政府关于采取[《传染病防治法》第二十五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-25)规定的紧急措施报告时，应当在二十四小时内做出决定。下一级政府在上一级政府作出决定前，必要时，可以临时采取[《传染病防治法》第二十五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-25)第一款第(一)、(四)项紧急措施，但不得超过二十四小时。

**Article 53** When a government at or above the county level receives a report from the government at the next lower level on the adoption of the emergency measures stipulated in Article 25 of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases, it shall make a decision within twenty-four hours. The government at the next lower level may, before the government at the next higher level makes a decision, temporarily adopt the emergency measures under Items (1) and (4) of the first paragraph of Article 25 of the said Law, but no such measures shall last for more than twenty-four hours.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　撤销采取[《传染病防治法》第二十五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-25)紧急措施的条件是：  
(一)甲类传染病病人、病原携带者全部治愈，乙类传染病病人、病原携带者得到有效的隔离治疗；病人尸体得到严格消毒处理；  
(二)污染的物品及环境已经过消毒等卫生处理；有关病媒昆虫、染疫动物基本消除；  
(三)暴发、流行的传染病病种，经过最长潜伏期后，未发现新的传染病病人，疫情得到有效的控制。

**Article 54** The conditions for revocation of the emergency measures adopted under Article 25 of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases are as follows:  
(1) all patients and pathogen carriers of Class A infectious diseases are cured, and patients and pathogen carriers of Class B infectious diseases are effectively isolated and treated; the bodies of deceased patients have been strictly disinfected and treated;  
(2) the contaminated articles and environment have undergone such hygiene treatments as disinfection; relevant vector insects and animals infected with epidemic are basically eliminated;  
(3) after the longest incubation period for the infectious disease that broke out or became prevalent, no new infectious disease patients are found, and the epidemic is effectively controlled.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　因患鼠疫、霍乱和炭疽病死亡的病人尸体，由治疗病人的医疗单位负责消毒处理，处理后应当立即火化。  
患病毒性肝炎、伤寒和副伤寒、艾滋病、白喉、炭疽、脊髓灰质炎死亡的病人尸体，由治疗病人的医疗单位或者当地卫生防疫机构消毒处理后火化。  
不具备火化条件的农村、边远地区，由治疗病人的医疗单位或者当地卫生防疫机构负责消毒后，可选远离居民点五百米以外、远离饮用水源五十米以外的地方，将尸体在距地面两米以下深埋。  
民族自治地方执行前款的规定，依照[《传染病防治法》第二十八条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-28)第三款的规定办理。

**Article 55** The body of a patient who dies of plague, cholera or anthrax shall be disinfected and treated by the medical institution that treated the patient, and shall be cremated immediately after treatment.  
The body of a patient who dies of viral hepatitis, typhoid fever, paratyphoid fever, AIDS, diphtheria, anthrax or poliomyelitis shall be disinfected and treated by the medical institution that treated the patient or by the local health and anti-epidemic agency, and then cremated.  
In rural or remote areas where cremation conditions are not available, after disinfection by the medical institution that treated the patient or the local health and anti-epidemic agency, the body may be buried deeply at a place not less than 500 meters away from residential areas and not less than 50 meters away from drinking water sources, with the body at least two meters below the ground.  
For the implementation of the preceding paragraph in national autonomous areas, the provisions of the third paragraph of Article 28 of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases shall be complied with.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　医疗保健机构、卫生防疫机构经县级以上政府卫生行政部门的批准可以对传染病病人尸体或者疑似传染病病人的尸体进行解剖查验。

**Article 56** A medical and health care institution or health and anti-epidemic agency may, with the approval of the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level, perform an autopsy on the body of an infectious disease patient or a suspected infectious disease patient.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　卫生防疫机构处理传染病疫情的人员，可以凭当地政府卫生行政部门出具的处理疫情证明及有效的身份证明，优先在铁路、交通、民航部门购票，铁路、交通、民航部门应当保证售给最近一次通往目的地的车、船、机票。  
交付运输的处理疫情的物品应当有明显标志，铁路、交通、民航部门应当保证用最快通往目的地的交通工具运出。

**Article 57** Personnel of a health and anti-epidemic agency handling an epidemic of an infectious disease may, by virtue of the certificate for handling the epidemic issued by the health administrative department of the local government and a valid identity certificate, purchase tickets on a priority basis from the railway, transportation or civil aviation departments, and the railway, transportation or civil aviation departments shall guarantee to sell them the tickets for the nearest train, ship or airplane bound for the destination.  
Articles for handling the epidemic that are delivered for transportation shall have conspicuous marks, and the railway, transportation or civil aviation departments shall guarantee to transport them by the fastest means of transportation to the destination.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　用于传染病监督控制的车辆，其标志由国务院卫生行政部门会同有关部门统一制定。任何单位和个人不得阻拦依法执行处理疫情任务的车辆和人员。

**Article 58** The signs for vehicles used for supervision and control of infectious diseases shall be uniformly formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council together with relevant departments. No unit or individual shall obstruct the vehicles and personnel that are lawfully carrying out the task of handling an epidemic.

### 第五章　监督

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　地方各级政府卫生行政部门、卫生防疫机构和受国务院卫生行政部门委托的其他有关部门卫生主管机构推荐的传染病管理监督员，由省级以上政府卫生行政部门聘任并发给证件。  
省级政府卫生行政部门聘任的传染病管理监督员，报国务院卫生行政部门备案。

**Article 59** The infectious disease management supervisors recommended by the health administrative departments of local governments at all levels, the health and anti-epidemic agencies, and the health competent organs of other relevant departments authorized by the health administrative department of the State Council shall be appointed and issued with certificates by the health administrative departments of governments at or above the provincial level.  
The infectious disease management supervisors appointed by the health administrative department of a provincial government shall be reported to the health administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　传染病管理监督员执行下列任务：  
(一)监督检查《传染病防治法》及本办法的执行情况；  
(二)进行现场调查，包括采集必需的标本及查阅、索取、翻印复制必要的文字、图片、声像资料等，并根据调查情况写出书面报告；  
(三)对违法单位或者个人提出处罚建议；  
(四)执行卫生行政部门或者其他有关部门卫生主管机构交付的任务；  
(五)及时提出预防和控制传染病措施的建议。

**Article 60** The infectious disease management supervisors shall perform the following tasks:  
(1) supervise and inspect the implementation of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and these Measures;  
(2) conduct on-the-spot investigations, including collecting necessary specimens and consulting, obtaining, reprinting and reproducing necessary written materials, pictures, audio-video materials, etc., and write a written report based on the investigation findings;  
(3) propose penalties against illegal units or individuals;  
(4) carry out tasks assigned by the health administrative department or the health competent organ of other relevant department;  
(5) promptly propose measures for prevention and control of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　各级各类医疗保健机构内设立的传染病管理检查员，由本单位推荐，经县级以上政府卫生行政部门或受国务院卫生行政部门委托的其他部门卫生主管机构批准并发给证件。

**Article 61** The infectious disease management inspectors set up within medical and health care institutions of various types and at various levels shall be recommended by the respective unit, and shall be approved and issued with certificates by the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level or the health competent organ of another department authorized by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　传染病管理检查员执行下列任务：  
(一)宣传《传染病防治法》及本办法，检查本单位和责任地段的传染病防治措施的实施和疫情报告执行情况；  
(二)对本单位和责任地段的传染病防治工作进行技术指导；  
(三)执行卫生行政部门和卫生防疫机构对本单位及责任地段提出的改进传染病防治管理工作的意见；  
(四)定期向卫生行政部门指定的卫生防疫机构汇报工作情况，遇到紧急情况及时报告。

**Article 62** The infectious disease management inspectors shall perform the following tasks:  
(1) publicize the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and these Measures, and inspect the implementation of infectious disease prevention and treatment measures and the execution of epidemic reporting in their own units and within their responsible areas;  
(2) provide technical guidance on infectious disease prevention and treatment work in their own units and within their responsible areas;  
(3) carry out the opinions put forward by the health administrative department and the health and anti-epidemic agency on improving the infectious disease prevention and treatment management work in their own units and within their responsible areas;  
(4) report work progress regularly to the health and anti-epidemic agency designated by the health administrative department, and report promptly in case of an emergency.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　传染病管理监督员、传染病管理检查员执行任务时，有关单位和个人必须给予协助。

**Article 63** When infectious disease management supervisors and infectious disease management inspectors are performing their tasks, the relevant units and individuals must provide assistance.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　传染病管理监督员的解聘和传染病管理检查员资格的取消，由原发证机关决定，并通知其所在单位和个人。

**Article 64** The dismissal of an infectious disease management supervisor and the revocation of the qualification of an infectious disease management inspector shall be decided by the original certificate-issuing organ, and the unit and individual concerned shall be notified.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　县级以上政府卫生行政部门和受国务院卫生行政部门委托的部门，可以成立传染病技术鉴定组织。

**Article 65** The health administrative department of a government at or above the county level and the department authorized by the health administrative department of the State Council may establish an infectious disease technical expert appraisal organization.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上政府卫生行政部门责令限期改正，可以处五千元以下的罚款；情节较严重的，可以处五千元以上二万元以下的罚款，对主管人员和直接责任人员由其所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分：  
(一)集中式供水单位供应的饮用水不符合国家规定的《生活饮用水卫生标准》的；  
(二)单位自备水源未经批准与城镇供水系统连接的；  
(三)未按城市环境卫生设施标准修建公共卫生设施致使垃圾、粪便、污水不能进行无害化处理的；  
(四)对被传染病病原体污染的污水、污物、粪便不按规定进行消毒处理的；  
(五)对被甲类和乙类传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似传染病病人污染的场所、物品未按照卫生防疫机构的要求实施必要的卫生处理的；  
(六)造成传染病的医源性感染、医院内感染、实验室感染和致病性微生物扩散的；  
(七)生产、经营、使用消毒药剂和消毒器械、卫生用品、卫生材料、一次性医疗器材、隐形眼镜、人造器官等不符合国家卫生标准，可能造成传染病的传播、扩散或者造成传染病的传播、扩散的；  
(八)准许或者纵容传染病病人、病原携带者和疑似传染病病人，从事国务院卫生行政部门规定禁止从事的易使该传染病扩散的工作的；  
(九)传染病病人、病原携带者故意传播传染病，造成他人感染的；  
(十)甲类传染病病人、病原携带者或者疑似传染病病人，乙类传染病中艾滋病、肺炭疽病人拒绝进行隔离治疗的；  
(十一)招用流动人员的用工单位，未向卫生防疫机构报告并未采取卫生措施，造成传染病传播、流行的；  
(十二)违章养犬或者拒绝、阻挠捕杀违章犬，造成咬伤他人或者导致人群中发生狂犬病的。  
前款所称情节较严重的，是指下列情形之一：  
(一)造成甲类传染病、艾滋病、肺炭疽传播危险的；  
(二)造成除艾滋病、肺炭疽之外的乙、丙类传染病暴发、流行的；  
(三)造成传染病菌(毒)种扩散的；  
(四)造成病人残疾、死亡的；  
(五)拒绝执行《传染病防治法》及本办法的规定，屡经教育仍继续违法的。

**Article 66** Whoever commits one of the following acts shall be ordered by the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level to make corrections within a specified time limit, and may be fined not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan may be imposed, and the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions by their own unit or by the organ at a higher level:  
(1) the drinking water supplied by a centralized water supply unit does not conform to the national "Sanitary Standard for Drinking Water";  
(2) a unit connects its self-provided water source with the urban water supply system without approval;  
(3) fails to construct public sanitation facilities in accordance with the urban environmental sanitation facility standards, resulting in the failure of harmless treatment of garbage, excrement, and sewage;  
(4) fails to disinfect sewage, dirt, and excrement contaminated by the causative agents of infectious diseases in accordance with the regulations;  
(5) fails to implement necessary sanitary treatment as required by the health and anti-epidemic agency on the places and articles contaminated by patients of Class A and B infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, or suspected patients;  
(6) causes iatrogenic infection, nosocomial infection, laboratory infection, or spread of pathogenic microorganisms;  
(7) produces, operates, or uses disinfectants and disinfection apparatus, sanitary products, sanitary materials, disposable medical devices, contact lenses, artificial organs, etc. that do not meet the national health standards, which may cause the spread or dissemination of infectious diseases or actually cause such spread or dissemination;  
(8) permits or connives at patients of infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, or suspected patients to engage in work prohibited by the health administrative department of the State Council that is liable to spread the infectious disease;  
(9) an infectious disease patient or pathogen carrier intentionally spreads the infectious disease, causing infection of another person;  
(10) a Class A infectious disease patient, pathogen carrier, or suspected patient, or an AIDS or pulmonary anthrax patient in Class B infectious disease, refuses to undergo isolation treatment;  
(11) the employer that recruits floating population fails to report to the health and anti-epidemic agency and fails to take sanitary measures, causing the spread or prevalence of an infectious disease;  
(12) raises dogs in violation of regulations or refuses or obstructs the killing of dogs raised in violation of regulations, causing bite injuries to others or leading to the occurrence of rabies among the population.  
The "relatively serious circumstances" mentioned in the preceding paragraph refer to any of the following situations:  
(1) creates a risk of spread of Class A infectious diseases, AIDS, or pulmonary anthrax;  
(2) causes an outbreak or prevalence of Class B or C infectious diseases other than AIDS and pulmonary anthrax;  
(3) causes the spread of strains (or species) of infectious disease bacteria (viruses);  
(4) causes disability or death of a patient;  
(5) refuses to implement the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and these Measures, and continues to violate the law after repeated education.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在自然疫源地和可能是自然疫源地的地区兴建大型建设项目未经卫生调查即进行施工的，由县级以上政府卫生行政部门责令限期改正，可以处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 67** Where a large construction project is constructed in a natural focus or an area that may be a natural focus without a health investigation, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit and may impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　单位和个人出售、运输被传染病病原体污染和来自疫区可能被传染病病原体污染的皮毛、旧衣物及生活用品的，由县级以上政府卫生行政部门责令限期进行卫生处理，可以处出售金额一倍以下的罚款；造成传染病流行的，根据情节，可以处相当出售金额三倍以下的罚款，危害严重，出售金额不满二千元的，以二千元计算；对主管人员和直接责任人员由所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分。

**Article 68** Where a unit or individual sells or transports fur, old clothing, or daily necessities that are contaminated by the causative agents of infectious diseases or come from an epidemic area and may be contaminated by such agents, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall order sanitary treatment within a specified time limit and may impose a fine of not more than one time the sale amount; if the sale causes the prevalence of an infectious disease, a fine of not more than three times the sale amount may be imposed according to the circumstances, and if the harm is serious and the sale amount is less than 2,000 yuan, the fine shall be calculated at 2,000 yuan; the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions by their own unit or by the organ at a higher level.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　单位和个人非法经营、出售用于预防传染病菌苗、疫苗等生物制品的，县级以上政府卫生行政部门可以处相当出售金额三倍以下的罚款，危害严重，出售金额不满五千元的，以五千元计算；对主管人员和直接责任人员由所在单位或者上级机关根据情节，可以给予行政处分。

**Article 69** Where a unit or individual illegally operates or sells biological products such as bacterial vaccines and vaccines for prevention of infectious diseases, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level may impose a fine of not more than three times the sale amount; if the harm is serious and the sale amount is less than 5,000 yuan, the fine shall be calculated at 5,000 yuan; the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible may be given administrative sanctions by their own unit or by the organ at a higher level according to the circumstances.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有下列行为之一的单位和个人，县级以上政府卫生行政部门报请同级政府批准，对单位予以通报批评；对主管人员和直接责任人员由所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分：  
(一)传染病暴发、流行时，妨碍或者拒绝执行政府采取紧急措施的；  
(二)传染病暴发、流行时，医疗保健人员、卫生防疫人员拒绝执行各级政府卫生行政部门调集其参加控制疫情的决定的；  
(三)对控制传染病暴发、流行负有责任的部门拒绝执行政府有关控制疫情决定的；  
(四)无故阻止和拦截依法执行处理疫情任务的车辆和人员的。

**Article 70** For a unit or individual that commits one of the following acts, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall submit to the government at the same level for approval, and shall circulate a notice of criticism to the unit; the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions by their own unit or by the organ at a higher level:  
(1) obstructs or refuses to implement the emergency measures taken by the government during the outbreak or prevalence of an infectious disease;  
(2) during the outbreak or prevalence of an infectious disease, medical and health care personnel or health and anti-epidemic personnel refuse to implement the decision of the health administrative department of a government at any level to mobilize them to participate in controlling the epidemic;  
(3) a department responsible for controlling the outbreak or prevalence of an infectious disease refuses to implement the government's decision concerning control of the epidemic;  
(4) without justifiable reasons, stops or intercepts vehicles and personnel lawfully carrying out the task of handling an epidemic.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　执行职务的医疗保健人员、卫生防疫人员和责任单位，不报、漏报、迟报传染病疫情的，由县级以上政府卫生行政部门责令限期改正，对主管人员和直接责任人员由其所在单位或者上级机关根据情节，可以给予行政处分。  
个体行医人员在执行职务时，不报、漏报、迟报传染病疫情的，由县级以上政府卫生行政部门责令限期改正，限期内不改的，可以处一百元以上五百元以下罚款；对造成传染病传播流行的，可以处二百元以上二千元以下罚款。

**Article 71** Where medical and health care personnel, health and anti-epidemic personnel, and responsible units on duty fail to report, omit to report, or delay reporting an epidemic of an infectious disease, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit, and the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible may be given administrative sanctions by their own unit or by the organ at a higher level according to the circumstances.  
Where an individual medical practitioner on duty fails to report, omits to report, or delays reporting an epidemic of an infectious disease, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan may be imposed; if the spread or prevalence of an infectious disease is caused, a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　县级政府卫生行政部门可以作出处一万元以下罚款的决定；决定处一万元以上罚款的，须报上一级政府卫生行政部门批准。  
受国务院卫生行政部门委托的有关部门卫生主管机构可以作出处二千元以下罚款的决定；决定处二千元以上罚款的，须报当地县级以上政府卫生行政部门批准。  
县级以上政府卫生行政部门在收取罚款时，应当出具正式的罚款收据。罚款全部上缴国库。

**Article 72** The health administrative department of a county government may make a decision to impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if a fine of more than 10,000 yuan is to be imposed, the decision shall be submitted to the health administrative department of the government at the next higher level for approval.  
The health competent organ of a relevant department authorized by the health administrative department of the State Council may make a decision to impose a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan; if a fine of more than 2,000 yuan is to be imposed, the decision shall be submitted to the local health administrative department of a government at or above the county level for approval.  
When collecting a fine, the health administrative department of a government at or above the county level shall issue a formal receipt for the fine. All fines shall be turned over to the State treasury.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　《传染病防治法》及本办法的用语含义如下：  
传染病病人、疑似传染病病人：指根据国务院卫生行政部门发布的《中华人民共和国传染病防治法规定管理的传染病诊断标准》，符合传染病病人和疑似传染病病人诊断标准的人。  
病原携带者：指感染病原体无临床症状但能排出病原体的人。  
暴发：指在一个局部地区，短期内，突然发生多例同一种传染病病人。  
流行：指一个地区某种传染病发病率显著超过该病历年的一般发病率水平。  
重大传染病疫情：指[《传染病防治法》第二十五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国传染病防治法.md#art-25)所称的传染病的暴发、流行。  
传染病监测：指对人群传染病的发生、流行及影响因素进行有计划地、系统地长期观察。  
疫区：指传染病在人群中暴发或者流行，其病原体向周围传播时可能波及的地区。  
人畜共患传染病：指鼠疫、流行性出血热、狂犬病、钩端螺旋体病、布鲁氏菌病、炭疽、流行性乙型脑炎、黑热病、包虫病、血吸虫病。  
自然疫源地：指某些传染病的病原体在自然界的野生动物中长期保存并造成动物间流行的地区。  
可能是自然疫源地：指在自然界中具有自然疫源性疾病存在的传染源和传播媒介，但尚未查明的地区。  
医源性感染：指在医学服务中，因病原体传播引起的感染。  
医院内感染：指就诊患者在医疗保健机构内受到的感染。  
实验室感染：指从事实验室工作时，因接触病原体所致的感染。  
消毒：指用化学、物理、生物的方法杀灭或者消除环境中的致病性微生物。  
卫生处理：指消毒、杀虫、灭鼠等卫生措施以及隔离、留验、就地检验等医学措施。  
卫生防疫机构：指卫生防疫站、结核病防治研究所(院)、寄生虫病防治研究所(站)、血吸虫病防治研究所(站)、皮肤病性病防治研究所(站)、地方病防治研究所(站)、鼠疫防治站(所)、乡镇预防保健站(所)及与上述机构专业相同的单位。  
医疗保健机构：指医院、卫生院(所)、门诊部(所)、疗养院(所)、妇幼保健院(站)及与上述机构业务活动相同的单位。

**Article 73** The meanings of the terms used in the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and these Measures are as follows:  
Infectious disease patients and suspected infectious disease patients: refer to persons who meet the diagnostic criteria for infectious disease patients and suspected infectious disease patients according to the "Diagnostic Criteria for Infectious Diseases Managed under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases" promulgated by the health administrative department of the State Council.  
Pathogen carriers: refer to persons who are infected with a pathogen but have no clinical symptoms, yet can excrete the pathogen.  
Outbreak: refers to the sudden occurrence of multiple cases of the same infectious disease within a short period in a local area.  
Prevalence: refers to the incidence rate of a certain infectious disease in an area significantly exceeding the general incidence level of that disease in previous years.  
Major infectious disease epidemic: refers to the outbreak or prevalence of an infectious disease as mentioned in Article 25 of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases.  
Infectious disease surveillance: refers to the planned, systematic, and long-term observation of the occurrence, prevalence, and influencing factors of infectious diseases in the population.  
Epidemic area: refers to an area where an infectious disease breaks out or becomes prevalent among the population, and which may be affected when the pathogen spreads to the surrounding areas.  
Zoonotic infectious diseases: refer to plague, epidemic hemorrhagic fever, rabies, leptospirosis, brucellosis, anthrax, Japanese encephalitis, kala-azar, echinococcosis, and schistosomiasis.  
Natural focus: refers to an area where the causative agents of certain infectious diseases are preserved for a long time in wild animals in nature and cause inter-animal prevalence.  
Possible natural focus: refers to an area where there are sources of infection and vectors of natural focal diseases in nature, but which has not yet been identified.  
Iatrogenic infection: refers to an infection caused by the transmission of a pathogen in the course of medical services.  
Nosocomial infection: refers to an infection acquired by a patient while receiving medical treatment in a medical and health care institution.  
Laboratory infection: refers to an infection caused by contact with a pathogen during laboratory work.  
Disinfection: refers to the use of chemical, physical, or biological methods to kill or eliminate pathogenic microorganisms in the environment.  
Sanitary treatment: refers to such sanitary measures as disinfection, insecticide, and deratization, as well as such medical measures as isolation, retention for observation, and on-site inspection.  
Health and anti-epidemic agency: refers to health and anti-epidemic stations, tuberculosis prevention and treatment institutes (hospitals), parasitic disease prevention and treatment institutes (stations), schistosomiasis prevention and treatment institutes (stations), skin disease and sexually transmitted disease prevention and treatment institutes (stations), endemic disease prevention and treatment institutes (stations), plague prevention and treatment stations (institutes), township preventive health care stations (institutes), and units with the same professional focus as the above institutions.  
Medical and health care institution: refers to hospitals, health centers (clinics), outpatient departments (clinics), sanatoriums (rest homes), maternal and child health care centers (stations), and units with the same business activities as the above institutions.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　省、自治区、直辖市政府可以根据《传染病防治法》和本办法制定实施细则。

**Article 74** The governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate detailed implementation rules in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and these Measures.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本办法由国务院卫生行政部门负责解释。

**Article 75** These Measures shall be interpreted by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 76** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
