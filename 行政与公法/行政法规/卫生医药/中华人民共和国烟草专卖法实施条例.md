### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国烟草专卖法》（以下简称《烟草专卖法》），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Tobacco Monopoly Law).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　烟草专卖是指国家对烟草专卖品的生产、销售和进出口业务实行垄断经营、统一管理的制度。

**Article 2** Tobacco monopoly refers to the system under which the State exercises monopolistic operation and unified management over the production, sale, and import and export of tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　烟草专卖品中的烟丝是指用烟叶、复烤烟叶、烟草薄片为原料加工制成的丝、末、粒状商品。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, "cut tobacco" among tobacco monopoly products refers to commodities in the form of shreds, powder, or granules processed from tobacco leaves, redried tobacco leaves, and tobacco sheets as raw materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院和省、自治区、直辖市的烟草专卖行政主管部门职责及领导体制，依照[《烟草专卖法》第四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法.md#art-4)的规定执行。设有烟草专卖行政主管部门的市、县，由市、县烟草专卖行政主管部门主管本行政区内的烟草专卖工作，受上一级烟草专卖行政主管部门和本级人民政府的双重领导，以上一级烟草专卖行政主管部门的领导为主。

**Article 4** The functions and leadership system of the tobacco monopoly administrative departments of the State Council and of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 4 of the Tobacco Monopoly Law. For cities and counties that have tobacco monopoly administrative departments, the city or county tobacco monopoly administrative department shall be responsible for the tobacco monopoly work within its administrative region, subject to the dual leadership of the higher-level tobacco monopoly administrative department and the people's government at the same level, with the leadership of the higher-level tobacco monopoly administrative department as the main.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对卷烟、雪茄烟焦油含量和用于卷烟、雪茄烟的主要添加剂实行控制。烟草制品生产企业不得违反国家有关规定使用有害的添加剂和色素。

**Article 5** The State exercises control over the tar content of cigarettes and cigars and the main additives used for cigarettes and cigars. Tobacco product manufacturers shall not use harmful additives and pigments in violation of the relevant State regulations.

### 第二章　烟草专卖许可证

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事烟草专卖品的生产、批发、零售业务，以及经营烟草专卖品进出口业务和经营外国烟草制品购销业务的，必须依照《烟草专卖法》和本条例的规定，申请领取烟草专卖许可证。  
烟草专卖许可证分为：  
（一）烟草专卖生产企业许可证；  
（二）烟草专卖批发企业许可证；  
（三）烟草专卖零售许可证。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual engaging in the production, wholesale, or retail of tobacco monopoly products, or engaging in the import and export of tobacco monopoly products or the purchase and sale of foreign tobacco products, must apply for and obtain a tobacco monopoly license in accordance with the Tobacco Monopoly Law and these Regulations.  
Tobacco monopoly licenses are divided into:  
(1) Tobacco Monopoly Production Enterprise License;  
(2) Tobacco Monopoly Wholesale Enterprise License;  
(3) Tobacco Monopoly Retail License.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与生产烟草专卖品相适应的资金；  
（二）有生产烟草专卖品所需要的技术、设备条件；  
（三）符合国家烟草行业的产业政策要求；  
（四）国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 7** To obtain a Tobacco Monopoly Production Enterprise License, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) Having funds commensurate with the production of tobacco monopoly products;  
(2) Having the technical and equipment conditions necessary for the production of tobacco monopoly products;  
(3) Meeting the requirements of the national tobacco industry's industrial policies;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　取得烟草专卖批发企业许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与经营烟草制品批发业务相适应的资金；  
（二）有固定的经营场所和必要的专业人员；  
（三）符合烟草专卖批发企业合理布局的要求；  
（四）国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 8** To obtain a Tobacco Monopoly Wholesale Enterprise License, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) Having funds commensurate with the wholesale business of tobacco products;  
(2) Having a fixed business premises and necessary professional personnel;  
(3) Meeting the requirements for reasonable distribution of tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprises;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　取得烟草专卖零售许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与经营烟草制品零售业务相适应的资金；  
（二）有固定的经营场所；  
（三）符合烟草制品零售点合理布局的要求；  
（四）国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定的其他条件。

**Article 9** To obtain a Tobacco Monopoly Retail License, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) Having funds commensurate with the retail business of tobacco products;  
(2) Having a fixed business premises;  
(3) Meeting the requirements for reasonable distribution of tobacco product retail outlets;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　烟草专卖行政主管部门依照《烟草专卖法》及本条例的规定，发放烟草专卖许可证和烟草专卖品准运证并实施管理。

**Article 10** The tobacco monopoly administrative department shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Tobacco Monopoly Law and these Regulations, issue tobacco monopoly licenses and tobacco monopoly product transportation permits, and exercise administration thereof.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请领取烟草专卖生产企业许可证的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市（以下简称省级）烟草专卖行政主管部门提出申请，由省级烟草专卖行政主管部门审查签署意见，报国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门审批发证。

**Article 11** An applicant for a Tobacco Monopoly Production Enterprise License shall submit an application to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government (hereinafter referred to as the provincial-level tobacco monopoly administrative department), which shall review the application and sign comments, and then submit it to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for approval and issuance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请领取烟草专卖批发企业许可证，进行跨省、自治区、直辖市经营的，应当向省级烟草专卖行政主管部门提出申请，由省级烟草专卖行政主管部门审查签署意见，报国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门审批发证。  
申请领取烟草专卖批发企业许可证，在省、自治区、直辖市范围内经营的，应当向企业所在地烟草专卖行政主管部门提出申请，由企业所在地烟草专卖行政主管部门审查签署意见，报省级烟草专卖行政主管部门审批发证。

**Article 12** An applicant for a Tobacco Monopoly Wholesale Enterprise License to engage in trans-provincial, autonomous region, or municipality operations shall submit an application to the provincial-level tobacco monopoly administrative department, which shall review and sign comments, and then submit it to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for approval and issuance.  
An applicant for a Tobacco Monopoly Wholesale Enterprise License to engage in operations within the province, autonomous region, or municipality shall submit an application to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the locality where the enterprise is located, which shall review and sign comments, and then submit it to the provincial-level tobacco monopoly administrative department for approval and issuance.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请领取烟草专卖零售许可证，依照《烟草专卖法》的规定办理。

**Article 13** An application for a Tobacco Monopoly Retail License shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Tobacco Monopoly Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　烟草专卖许可证的发证机关可以定期或者不定期地对取得烟草专卖许可证的企业、个人进行检查。经检查不符合《烟草专卖法》和本条例规定条件的，烟草专卖许可证的发证机关可以责令暂停烟草专卖业务、进行整顿，直至取消其从事烟草专卖业务的资格。  
烟草专卖许可证的具体管理办法，由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门根据本条例的规定制定。

**Article 14** The authority issuing the tobacco monopoly license may conduct inspections on enterprises or individuals that have obtained the tobacco monopoly license on a regular or irregular basis. If upon inspection it is found that the conditions prescribed by the Tobacco Monopoly Law and these Regulations are not met, the issuing authority may order the suspension of tobacco monopoly business for rectification, or even revoke the qualification to engage in tobacco monopoly business.  
The specific administrative measures for tobacco monopoly licenses shall be formulated by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.

### 第三章　烟叶的种植、收购和调拨

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门按照合理布局的要求，会同有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府依据国家计划，根据良种化、区域化、规范化的原则制定烟叶种植规划。

**Article 15** The tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the requirements of reasonable distribution, jointly with the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate tobacco leaf planting plans based on the State plan and in accordance with the principles of improved seed selection, regionalization, and standardization.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　烟叶由烟草公司或其委托单位依法统一收购。烟草公司或其委托单位根据需要，可以在国家下达烟叶收购计划的地区设立烟叶收购站（点）收购烟叶。设立烟叶收购站（点），应当经设区的市级烟草专卖行政主管部门批准。未经批准，任何单位和个人不得收购烟叶。

**Article 16** Tobacco leaves shall be uniformly purchased by tobacco companies or their entrusted entities according to law. If necessary, tobacco companies or their entrusted entities may establish tobacco leaf purchasing stations (points) in areas where the State has assigned tobacco leaf purchasing plans. The establishment of a tobacco leaf purchasing station (point) shall be approved by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of a city divided into districts. Without approval, no entity or individual may purchase tobacco leaves.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方烟草专卖行政主管部门组织同级有关部门和烟叶生产者的代表组成烟叶评级小组，协调烟叶收购等级评定工作。

**Article 17** The local tobacco monopoly administrative department shall organize the relevant departments at the same level and representatives of tobacco leaf producers to form a tobacco leaf grading group to coordinate the grading appraisal for tobacco leaf purchases.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家储备、出口烟叶的计划和烟叶调拨计划，由国务院计划部门下达。

**Article 18** The plans for State reserve and export of tobacco leaves, as well as the plans for tobacco leaf allocation, shall be issued by the planning department of the State Council.

### 第四章　烟草制品的生产

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　设立烟草制品生产企业，应当由省级烟草专卖行政主管部门报经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准，取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证，并经工商行政管理部门核准登记。

**Article 19** The establishment of a tobacco product manufacturer shall be reported by the provincial-level tobacco monopoly administrative department to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for approval, obtaining a Tobacco Monopoly Production Enterprise License, and shall be registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce after verification.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　烟草制品生产企业必须严格执行国家下达的生产计划。

**Article 20** Tobacco product manufacturers must strictly implement the production plans issued by the State.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止使用霉烂烟叶生产卷烟、雪茄烟和烟丝。

**Article 21** The use of moldy or rotten tobacco leaves to produce cigarettes, cigars, and cut tobacco is prohibited.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　卷烟、雪茄烟和有包装的烟丝，应当使用注册商标。

**Article 22** Cigarettes, cigars, and packaged cut tobacco shall use registered trademarks.

### 第五章　烟草制品的销售

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　取得烟草专卖批发企业许可证的企业，应当在许可证规定的经营范围和地域范围内，从事烟草制品的批发业务。  
取得烟草专卖零售许可证的企业或者个人，应当在当地的烟草专卖批发企业进货，并接受烟草专卖许可证发证机关的监督管理。

**Article 23** Enterprises that have obtained a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license shall engage in the wholesale business of tobacco products within the scope of business and territorial scope prescribed by the license.  
Enterprises or individuals that have obtained a tobacco monopoly retail license shall purchase goods from local tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprises and shall accept supervision and management by the issuing authority of the tobacco monopoly license.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　无烟草专卖批发企业许可证的单位或者个人，一次销售卷烟、雪茄烟50条以上的，视为无烟草专卖批发企业许可证从事烟草制品批发业务。

**Article 24** Any unit or individual without a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license that sells more than 50 cartons of cigarettes or cigars at one time shall be deemed to have engaged in tobacco products wholesale business without a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何单位或者个人不得销售非法生产的烟草制品。

**Article 25** No unit or individual may sell illegally produced tobacco products.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　烟草专卖生产企业和烟草专卖批发企业，不得向无烟草专卖零售许可证的单位或者个人提供烟草制品。

**Article 26** Tobacco monopoly production enterprises and tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprises may not provide tobacco products to units or individuals without a tobacco monopoly retail license.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在中国境内销售的卷烟、雪茄烟，应当在小包、条包上标注焦油含量级和“吸烟有害健康”的中文字样。

**Article 27** Cigarettes and cigars sold within the territory of China shall have the tar content level and the Chinese characters "Smoking is harmful to health" printed on the small packs and cartons.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门在必要时，可以根据市场供需情况下达省、自治区、直辖市之间的卷烟、雪茄烟调拨任务。

**Article 28** The tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council may, when necessary, assign allocation tasks for cigarettes and cigars among provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on market supply and demand.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　严禁销售霉坏、变质的烟草制品。霉坏、变质的烟草制品，由烟草专卖行政主管部门或者有关行政管理部门监督销毁。

**Article 29** The sale of moldy, rotten, or deteriorated tobacco products is strictly prohibited. Moldy, rotten, or deteriorated tobacco products shall be destroyed under the supervision of the tobacco monopoly administrative department or the relevant administrative department.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　有关部门依法查获的假冒商标烟草制品，应当交由烟草专卖行政主管部门按照国家有关规定公开销毁，禁止以任何方式销售。

**Article 30** Counterfeit trademark tobacco products seized by relevant authorities according to law shall be handed over to the tobacco monopoly administrative department for public destruction in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. No such products may be sold by any means.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　假冒商标烟草制品的鉴别检测工作，由国务院产品质量监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府产品质量监督管理部门指定的烟草质量检测站进行。

**Article 31** The identification and testing of counterfeit trademark tobacco products shall be conducted by tobacco quality testing stations designated by the product quality supervision and administration department of the State Council and the product quality supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第六章　烟草专卖品的运输

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　烟草专卖品准运证由省级以上烟草专卖行政主管部门或其授权的机构审批、发放。烟草专卖品准运证的管理办法由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门制定。

**Article 32** The tobacco monopoly product transport permit shall be examined, approved, and issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department at or above the provincial level or its authorized institution. The measures for the administration of the tobacco monopoly product transport permit shall be formulated by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　跨省、自治区、直辖市运输进口的烟草专卖品、国产烟草专用机械和烟用丝束、滤嘴棒以及分切的进口卷烟纸，应当凭国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门或其授权的机构签发的烟草专卖品准运证办理托运或者自运。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市运输除国产烟草专用机械、烟用丝束、滤嘴棒以及分切的进口卷烟纸以外的其他国产烟草专卖品，应当凭国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门或者省级烟草专卖行政主管部门签发的烟草专卖品准运证办理托运或者自运。  
在省、自治区、直辖市内跨市、县运输烟草专卖品，应当凭省级烟草专卖行政主管部门或其授权的机构签发的烟草专卖品准运证办理托运或者自运。  
运输依法没收的走私烟草专卖品，应当凭国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门签发的烟草专卖品准运证办理托运或者自运。

**Article 33** For the transportation of imported tobacco monopoly products, domestically produced tobacco-specific machinery, tobacco tow, filter rods, and cut imported cigarette paper across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, consignment or self-transportation shall be handled with a tobacco monopoly product transport permit issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council or its authorized institution.  
For the transportation of domestically produced tobacco monopoly products other than domestically produced tobacco-specific machinery, tobacco tow, filter rods, and cut imported cigarette paper across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, consignment or self-transportation shall be handled with a tobacco monopoly product transport permit issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council or the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department.  
For the transportation of tobacco monopoly products across cities or counties within a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, consignment or self-transportation shall be handled with a tobacco monopoly product transport permit issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department or its authorized institution.  
For the transportation of smuggled tobacco monopoly products confiscated according to law, consignment or self-transportation shall be handled with a tobacco monopoly product transport permit issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　有下列情形之一的，为无烟草专卖品准运证运输烟草专卖品：  
（一）超过烟草专卖品准运证规定的数量和范围运输烟草专卖品的；  
（二）使用过期、涂改、复印的烟草专卖品准运证的；  
（三）无烟草专卖品准运证又无法提供在当地购买烟草专卖品的有效证明的；  
（四）无烟草专卖品准运证运输烟草专卖品的其他行为。

**Article 34** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute transportation of tobacco monopoly products without a tobacco monopoly product transport permit:  
(1) transporting tobacco monopoly products exceeding the quantity and scope specified in the tobacco monopoly product transport permit;  
(2) using an expired, altered, or photocopied tobacco monopoly product transport permit;  
(3) failing to have a tobacco monopoly product transport permit and being unable to provide valid proof of local purchase of the tobacco monopoly products;  
(4) other acts of transporting tobacco monopoly products without a tobacco monopoly product transport permit.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　海关监管的烟草制品的转关运输，按照国家有关海关转关运输的规定办理运输手续。

**Article 35** The customs transit transportation of tobacco products under customs supervision shall go through the transportation formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on customs transit transportation.

### 第七章　卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　烟草专卖批发企业和烟草制品生产企业只能从取得烟草专卖生产企业许可证的企业购买卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束和烟草专用机械。  
卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的生产企业不得将其产品销售给无烟草专卖生产企业许可证的单位或者个人。

**Article 36** Tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprises and tobacco product production enterprises may only purchase cigarette paper, filter rods, tobacco tow, and tobacco-specific machinery from enterprises that have obtained a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license.  
Enterprises that produce cigarette paper, filter rods, tobacco tow, and tobacco-specific machinery may not sell their products to units or individuals without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　烟草专用机械的购进、出售、转让，必须经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准。  
烟草专用机械的名录由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定。

**Article 37** The purchase, sale, and transfer of tobacco-specific machinery must be approved by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.  
The catalog of tobacco-specific machinery shall be prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　任何单位或者个人不得销售非法生产的烟草专用机械、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒及烟用丝束。  
淘汰报废、非法拼装的烟草专用机械，残次的卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束及下脚料，由当地烟草专卖行政主管部门监督处理，不得以任何方式销售。

**Article 38** No unit or individual may sell illegally produced tobacco-specific machinery, cigarette paper, filter rods, or tobacco tow.  
Tobacco-specific machinery that has been eliminated, scrapped, or illegally assembled, and defective or waste cigarette paper, filter rods, tobacco tow, and scraps shall be disposed of under the supervision of the local tobacco monopoly administrative department and may not be sold by any means.

### 第八章　进出口贸易和对外经济技术合作

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　设立外商投资的烟草专卖生产企业，应当报经国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门审查同意后，方可按照国家有关规定批准立项。

**Article 39** The establishment of a foreign-invested tobacco monopoly production enterprise shall be reported to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for examination and approval, and only thereafter may the project be approved for initiation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　进口烟草专卖品的计划应当报国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门审查批准。

**Article 40** The plan for importing tobacco monopoly products shall be submitted to the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council for examination and approval.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　免税进口的烟草制品应当存放在海关指定的保税仓库内，并由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门指定的地方烟草专卖行政主管部门与海关共同加锁管理。海关凭国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门批准的免税进口计划分批核销免税进口外国烟草制品的数量。

**Article 41** Duty-free imported tobacco products shall be stored in bonded warehouses designated by the Customs and shall be jointly lock-managed by the local tobacco monopoly administrative department designated by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council and the Customs. The Customs shall, based on the duty-free import plan approved by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council, write off in batches the quantity of duty-free imported foreign tobacco products.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在海关监管区内经营免税的卷烟、雪茄烟的，只能零售，并应当在卷烟、雪茄烟的小包、条包上标注国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定的专门标志。

**Article 42** Entities operating duty-free cigarettes and cigars within the Customs supervision zone may only engage in retail and shall mark on the small packs and cartons of cigarettes and cigars the special marks specified by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　专供出口的卷烟、雪茄烟，应当在小包、条包上标注“专供出口”中文字样。

**Article 43** Cigarettes and cigars exclusively for export shall bear the Chinese characters "专供出口" (for export only) on the small packs and cartons.

### 第九章　监督检查

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　烟草专卖行政主管部门依法对执行《烟草专卖法》及本条例的情况进行监督、检查，查处违反《烟草专卖法》及本条例的案件，并会同国家有关部门查处烟草专卖品的走私贩私、假冒伪劣行为。

**Article 44** The tobacco monopoly administrative department shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the implementation of the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, investigate and handle cases violating the said Law and these Regulations, and shall, jointly with the relevant state departments, investigate and handle activities of smuggling and trafficking in tobacco monopoly products and the production and sale of counterfeit and inferior products.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门在必要时，可以根据烟草专卖工作的实际情况，在重点地区设立派出机构；省级烟草专卖行政主管部门在必要时，可以向烟草专卖品生产、经营企业派驻人员。派出机构和派驻人员在派出部门的授权范围内监督、检查烟草专卖品的生产、经营活动。

**Article 45** The tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council may, when necessary and based on the actual circumstances of tobacco monopoly work, establish representative offices in key regions; the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative departments may, when necessary, station personnel in tobacco monopoly product production and operation enterprises. The representative offices and stationed personnel shall, within the scope of authorization of the dispatching department, supervise and inspect the production and operation activities of tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　烟草专卖行政主管部门查处违反《烟草专卖法》和本条例的案件时，可以行使下列职权：  
（一）询问违法案件的当事人、嫌疑人和证人；  
（二）检查违法案件当事人的经营场所，依法对违法生产或者经营的烟草专卖品进行处理；  
（三）查阅、复制与违法活动有关的合同、发票、账册、单据、记录、文件、业务函电和其他资料。

**Article 46** When investigating and handling cases violating the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China and these Regulations, the tobacco monopoly administrative department may exercise the following powers:  
(1) to interrogate the parties, suspects, and witnesses of the illegal case;  
(2) to inspect the business premises of the parties involved in the illegal case and, according to law, dispose of the tobacco monopoly products illegally produced or operated;  
(3) to consult and copy contracts, invoices, account books, receipts, records, documents, business correspondence, and other materials related to the illegal activities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　烟草专卖行政主管部门或者烟草专卖行政主管部门会同有关部门，可以依法对非法运输烟草专卖品的活动进行检查、处理。

**Article 47** The tobacco monopoly administrative department, or the tobacco monopoly administrative department jointly with the relevant departments, may, according to law, inspect and handle activities of illegally transporting tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　人民法院和行政机关依法没收的烟草专卖品以及充抵罚金、罚款和税款的烟草专卖品，按照国家有关规定进行拍卖的，竞买人应当持有烟草专卖批发企业许可证。  
依法设立的拍卖企业拍卖烟草专卖品，应当对竞买人进行资格验证。拍卖企业拍卖烟草专卖品，应当接受烟草专卖行政主管部门的监督。

**Article 48** Where tobacco monopoly products confiscated according to law by the People's Courts or administrative organs and tobacco monopoly products used to offset fines, penalties, and taxes are auctioned in accordance with the relevant state provisions, bidders shall hold a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license.  
A lawfully established auction enterprise auctioning tobacco monopoly products shall verify the qualifications of bidders. Auction enterprises auctioning tobacco monopoly products shall accept supervision by the tobacco monopoly administrative department.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　烟草专卖行政主管部门的专卖管理检查人员执行公务时，应当佩戴国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门制发的徽章，出示省级以上烟草专卖行政主管部门签发的检查证件。

**Article 49** When performing official duties, monopoly management inspection personnel of the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall wear the badges issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council and present inspection certificates issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对检举烟草专卖违法案件的有功单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 50** Units and individuals that have rendered meritorious service in reporting cases of tobacco monopoly violations shall be rewarded.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　依照[《烟草专卖法》第二十八条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法.md#art-28)规定处罚的，按照下列规定执行：  
（一）擅自收购烟叶的，可以处非法收购烟叶价值20%以上50%以下的罚款，并按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烟叶平均收购价格的70%收购违法收购的烟叶；  
（二）擅自收购烟叶1000公斤以上的，依法没收其违法收购的烟叶和违法所得。

**Article 51** Where penalties are imposed in accordance with Article 28 of the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China, they shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) Where tobacco leaves are illegally purchased without authorization, a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the value of the illegally purchased tobacco leaves may be imposed, and the illegally purchased tobacco leaves shall be purchased at 70% of the average purchase price of tobacco leaves of the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where the seizure is made;  
(2) Where 1,000 kilograms or more of tobacco leaves are illegally purchased without authorization, the illegally purchased tobacco leaves and illegal gains shall be confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　依照[《烟草专卖法》第二十九条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法.md#art-29)规定处罚的，按照下列规定执行：  
（一）无准运证或者超过准运证规定的数量托运或者自运烟草专卖品的，处以违法运输的烟草专卖品价值20%以上50%以下的罚款，可以按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烟叶平均收购价格的70%收购违法运输的烟叶，以及按照市场批发价格的70%收购违法运输的除烟叶外的其他烟草专卖品。  
（二）有下列情形之一的，没收违法运输的烟草专卖品和违法所得：  
1.非法运输的烟草专卖品价值超过5万元或者运输卷烟数量超过100件（每1万支为1件）的；  
2.被烟草专卖行政主管部门处罚两次以上的；  
3.抗拒烟草专卖行政主管部门的监督检查人员依法实施检查的；  
4.非法运输走私烟草专卖品的；  
5.运输无烟草专卖生产企业许可证的企业生产的烟草专卖品的；  
6.利用伪装非法运输烟草专卖品的；  
7.利用特种车辆运输烟草专卖品逃避检查的；  
8.其他非法运输行为，情节严重的。  
（三）承运人明知是烟草专卖品而为无准运证的单位、个人运输的，没收违法所得，可以并处违法运输的烟草专卖品价值10%以上20%以下的罚款。  
（四）邮寄、异地携带烟叶、烟草制品超过国务院有关部门规定的限量1倍以上的，依照本条第（一）项的规定处罚。

**Article 52** Where penalties are imposed in accordance with Article 29 of the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China, they shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) Where tobacco monopoly products are consigned for transport or personally transported without a transport permit or in excess of the quantity specified in the transport permit, a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the value of the illegally transported tobacco monopoly products shall be imposed, and the illegally transported tobacco leaves may be purchased at 70% of the average purchase price of tobacco leaves of the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where the seizure is made, and other tobacco monopoly products other than tobacco leaves that are illegally transported may be purchased at 70% of the market wholesale price.  
(2) In any of the following circumstances, the illegally transported tobacco monopoly products and illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
1. The value of the illegally transported tobacco monopoly products exceeds 50,000 yuan or the quantity of transported cigarettes exceeds 100 cartons (with every 10,000 sticks being one carton);  
2. Having been punished twice or more by the tobacco monopoly administrative department;  
3. Resisting inspection according to law by the supervision and inspection personnel of the tobacco monopoly administrative department;  
4. Illegally transporting smuggled tobacco monopoly products;  
5. Transporting tobacco monopoly products produced by enterprises without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license;  
6. Illegally transporting tobacco monopoly products under disguise;  
7. Using special vehicles to transport tobacco monopoly products to evade inspection;  
8. Other illegal transport activities with serious circumstances.  
(3) Where a carrier, knowing that the goods are tobacco monopoly products, transports them for a unit or individual without a transport permit, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 20% of the value of the illegally transported tobacco monopoly products may be concurrently imposed.  
(4) Where tobacco leaves or tobacco products are mailed or carried from one place to another in excess of twice the limit prescribed by the relevant departments of the State Council, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with subparagraph (1) of this Article.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　依照[《烟草专卖法》第三十条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法.md#art-30)规定处罚的，按照下列规定执行：  
（一）无烟草专卖生产企业许可证生产烟草制品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令关闭，没收违法所得，处以所生产烟草制品价值1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，并将其违法生产的烟草制品公开销毁；  
（二）无烟草专卖生产企业许可证生产卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束或者烟草专用机械的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令停止生产，没收违法所得，处以违法生产的烟草专卖品价值1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，并将其违法生产的烟草专卖品公开销毁。

**Article 53** Where penalties are imposed in accordance with Article 30 of the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China, they shall be executed according to the following provisions:  
(1) Where tobacco products are produced without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall order closure, confiscate illegal gains, impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the value of the tobacco products produced, and publicly destroy the illegally produced tobacco products;  
(2) Where cigarette paper, filter rods, tobacco tow, or tobacco-specific machinery are produced without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall order cessation of production, confiscate illegal gains, impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the value of the illegally produced tobacco monopoly products, and publicly destroy the illegally produced tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依照[《烟草专卖法》第三十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国烟草专卖法.md#art-31)规定，无烟草专卖批发企业许可证经营烟草制品批发业务的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令关闭或者停止经营烟草制品批发业务，没收违法所得，处以违法批发的烟草制品价值50%以上1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 54** In accordance with Article 31 of the Tobacco Monopoly Law of the People's Republic of China, where a person without a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license engages in the wholesale of tobacco products, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall order closure or cessation of the wholesale business of tobacco products, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50% but not more than one time the value of the illegally wholesaled tobacco products.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　取得烟草专卖批发企业许可证的单位违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第一款的规定，超越经营范围和地域范围，从事烟草制品批发业务的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令暂停经营批发业务，没收违法所得，处以违法经营的烟草制品价值10%以上20%以下的罚款。

**Article 55** Where a unit that has obtained a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license violates the provisions of Article 23, paragraph 1 of these Regulations by engaging in the wholesale of tobacco products beyond the business scope and geographical scope, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall order suspension of the wholesale business, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 20% of the value of the illegally operated tobacco products.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　取得烟草专卖零售许可证的企业或者个人违反[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第二款的规定，未在当地烟草专卖批发企业进货的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门没收违法所得，可处以进货总额5%以上10%以下的罚款。

**Article 56** Where an enterprise or individual that has obtained a tobacco monopoly retail license violates the provisions of Article 23, paragraph 2 of these Regulations by failing to purchase goods from the local tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall confiscate illegal gains and may impose a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 10% of the total purchase amount.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　无烟草专卖零售许可证经营烟草制品零售业务的，由工商行政管理部门或者由工商行政管理部门根据烟草专卖行政主管部门的意见，责令停止经营烟草制品零售业务，没收违法所得，处以违法经营总额20%以上50%以下的罚款。

**Article 57** Where a person without a tobacco monopoly retail license engages in the retail business of tobacco products, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or the administrative department for industry and commerce based on the opinion of the tobacco monopoly administrative department, shall order cessation of the retail business of tobacco products, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the total illegal business amount.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第三十八条第一款规定销售非法生产的烟草专卖品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门责令停止销售，没收违法所得，处以违法销售总额20%以上50%以下的罚款，并将非法销售的烟草专卖品公开销毁。

**Article 58** Where a person violates the provisions of Article 25 and Article 38, paragraph 1 of these Regulations by selling illegally produced tobacco monopoly products, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall order cessation of sales, confiscate illegal gains, impose a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the total illegal sales amount, and publicly destroy the illegally sold tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，未取得国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门颁发的烟草专卖批发企业许可证，擅自跨省、自治区、直辖市从事烟草制品批发业务的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门处以批发总额10%以上20%以下的罚款。

**Article 59** Where a person violates the provisions of these Regulations by engaging without authorization in the wholesale of tobacco products across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government without obtaining a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 10% but not more than 20% of the total wholesale amount.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)、第三十六条第二款规定，为无烟草专卖许可证的单位或者个人提供烟草专卖品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门没收违法所得，并处以销售总额20%以上50%以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where a person violates the provisions of Article 26 and Article 36, paragraph 2 of these Regulations by providing tobacco monopoly products to a unit or individual without a tobacco monopoly license, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall confiscate illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the total sales amount.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)第一款规定，烟草专卖批发企业和烟草制品生产企业从无烟草专卖生产企业许可证的企业购买卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门处以所购烟草专卖品价值50%以上1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 61** Where a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise or tobacco product manufacturing enterprise, in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 36 of these Regulations, purchases cigarette paper, filter tips, tobacco tow, or tobacco-specific machinery from an enterprise without a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 50% but not more than 100% of the value of the purchased tobacco monopoly products.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反[本条例第四十一条](#art-41)规定，免税进口的烟草制品不按规定存放在烟草制品保税仓库内的，可以处烟草制品价值50%以下的罚款。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 41 of these Regulations, duty-free imported tobacco products are not stored in a bonded warehouse for tobacco products as required, a fine of not more than 50% of the value of the tobacco products may be imposed.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反[本条例第四十二条](#art-42)规定，在海关监管区内经营免税的卷烟、雪茄烟没有在小包、条包上标注国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门规定的专门标志的，可以处非法经营总额50%以下的罚款。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 42 of these Regulations, duty-free cigarettes or cigars are operated within a customs supervision zone without bearing the special markings prescribed by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council on the packets or cartons, a fine of not more than 50% of the total amount of illegal business may be imposed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)的规定，拍卖企业未对竞买人进行资格验证，或者不接受烟草专卖行政主管部门的监督，擅自拍卖烟草专卖品的，由烟草专卖行政主管部门处以拍卖的烟草专卖品价值20%以上50%以下的罚款，并依法取消其拍卖烟草专卖品的资格。

**Article 64** Where an auction enterprise, in violation of the provisions of Article 48 of these Regulations, fails to verify the qualifications of a bidder, or refuses to accept the supervision of the tobacco monopoly administrative department, and auctions a tobacco monopoly product without authorization, the tobacco monopoly administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 20% but not more than 50% of the value of the auctioned tobacco monopoly product, and shall, according to law, cancel its qualification to auction tobacco monopoly products.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　电子烟等新型烟草制品参照本条例卷烟的有关规定执行。

**Article 65** Electronic cigarettes and other novel tobacco products shall be governed by reference to the relevant provisions on cigarettes under these Regulations.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 66** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of issuance.
