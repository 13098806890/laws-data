# 国务院关于加强食品等产品安全监督管理的特别规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2007-07-26  
**生效日期**：2007-07-26

> (2007年7月25日国务院第186次常务会议通过　2007年7月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第503号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强食品等产品安全监督管理，进一步明确生产经营者、监督管理部门和地方人民政府的责任，加强各监督管理部门的协调、配合，保障人体健康和生命安全，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定所称产品除食品外，还包括食用农产品、药品等与人体健康和生命安全有关的产品。 对产品安全监督管理，法律有规定的，适用法律规定；法律没有规定或者规定不明确的，适用本规定。 第三条　生产经营者应当对其生产、销售的产品安全负责，不得生产、销售不符合法定要求的产品。 依照法律、行政法规规定生产、销售产品需要取得许可证照或者需要经过认证的，应当按照法定条件、要求从事生产经营活动。不按照法定条件、要求从事生产经营活动或者生产、销售不符合法定要求产品的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得、产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等物品，货值金额不足5000元的，并处5万元罚款；货值金额5000元以上不足1万元的，并处10万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照；构成非法经营罪或者生产、销售伪劣商品罪等犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 生产经营者不再符合法定条件、要求，继续从事生产经营活动的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照，并在当地主要媒体上公告被吊销许可证照的生产经营者名单；构成非法经营罪或者生产、销售伪劣商品罪等犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 依法应当取得许可证照而未取得许可证照从事生产经营活动的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得、产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等物品，货值金额不足1万元的，并处10万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；构成非法经营罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 有关行业协会应当加强行业自律，监督生产经营者的生产经营活动；加强公众健康知识的普及、宣传，引导消费者选择合法生产经营者生产、销售的产品以及有合法标识的产品。 第四条　生产者生产产品所使用的原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品，应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和国家强制性标准。 违反前款规定，违法使用原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责没收违法所得，货值金额不足5000元的，并处2万元罚款；货值金额5000元以上不足1万元的，并处5万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照；构成生产、销售伪劣商品罪的，依法追究刑事责任。 第五条　销售者必须建立并执行进货检查验收制度，审验供货商的经营资格，验明产品合格证明和产品标识，并建立产品进货台账，如实记录产品名称、规格、数量、供货商及其联系方式、进货时间等内容。从事产品批发业务的销售企业应当建立产品销售台账，如实记录批发的产品品种、规格、数量、流向等内容。在产品集中交易场所销售自制产品的生产企业应当比照从事产品批发业务的销售企业的规定，履行建立产品销售台账的义务。进货台账和销售台账保存期限不得少于2年。销售者应当向供货商按照产品生产批次索要符合法定条件的检验机构出具的检验报告或者由供货商签字或者盖章的检验报告复印件；不能提供检验报告或者检验报告复印件的产品，不得销售。 违反前款规定的，由工商、药品监督管理部门依据各自职责责令停止销售；不能提供检验报告或者检验报告复印件销售产品的，没收违法所得和违法销售的产品，并处货值金额3倍的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照。 第六条　产品集中交易市场的开办企业、产品经营柜台出租企业、产品展销会的举办企业，应当审查入场销售者的经营资格，明确入场销售者的产品安全管理责任，定期对入场销售者的经营环境、条件、内部安全管理制度和经营产品是否符合法定要求进行检查，发现销售不符合法定要求产品或者其他违法行为的，应当及时制止并立即报告所在地工商行政管理部门。 违反前款规定的，由工商行政管理部门处以1000元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿；造成严重后果的，吊销营业执照。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强食品等产品安全监督管理，进一步明确生产经营者、监督管理部门和地方人民政府的责任，加强各监督管理部门的协调、配合，保障人体健康和生命安全，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of strengthening safety supervision and administration of food and other products, further clarifying the responsibilities of producers and business operators, supervision and administration departments, and local people's governments, enhancing coordination and cooperation among various supervision and administration departments, and ensuring human health and life safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称产品除食品外，还包括食用农产品、药品等与人体健康和生命安全有关的产品。  
对产品安全监督管理，法律有规定的，适用法律规定；法律没有规定或者规定不明确的，适用本规定。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Provisions, "products" include, in addition to food, edible agricultural products, pharmaceuticals, and other products related to human health and life safety.  
Where there are legal provisions governing the safety supervision and administration of products, such legal provisions shall apply; where there are no legal provisions or the provisions are unclear, these Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　生产经营者应当对其生产、销售的产品安全负责，不得生产、销售不符合法定要求的产品。  
依照法律、行政法规规定生产、销售产品需要取得许可证照或者需要经过认证的，应当按照法定条件、要求从事生产经营活动。不按照法定条件、要求从事生产经营活动或者生产、销售不符合法定要求产品的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得、产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等物品，货值金额不足5000元的，并处5万元罚款；货值金额5000元以上不足1万元的，并处10万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照；构成非法经营罪或者生产、销售伪劣商品罪等犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
生产经营者不再符合法定条件、要求，继续从事生产经营活动的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照，并在当地主要媒体上公告被吊销许可证照的生产经营者名单；构成非法经营罪或者生产、销售伪劣商品罪等犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
依法应当取得许可证照而未取得许可证照从事生产经营活动的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得、产品和用于违法生产的工具、设备、原材料等物品，货值金额不足1万元的，并处10万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；构成非法经营罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
有关行业协会应当加强行业自律，监督生产经营者的生产经营活动；加强公众健康知识的普及、宣传，引导消费者选择合法生产经营者生产、销售的产品以及有合法标识的产品。

**Article 3** A producer or business operator shall be responsible for the safety of the products it produces or sells, and shall not produce or sell products that do not meet the statutory requirements.  
Where a permit or certification is required for the production or sale of products in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, the production or business operation activities shall be carried out in accordance with the statutory conditions and requirements. If any producer or business operator fails to carry out production or business operation activities in accordance with the statutory conditions and requirements, or produces or sells products that do not meet the statutory requirements, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, confiscate illegal gains, products, and items such as tools, equipment, and raw materials used for illegal production. If the value of the goods is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the goods is 5,000 yuan or more but less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing department shall revoke the permit; if the act constitutes the crime of illegal business operation or the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard goods, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a producer or business operator no longer meets the statutory conditions or requirements and continues to engage in production or business operation activities, the original issuing department shall revoke the permit and announce the list of producers or business operators whose permits have been revoked in the local major media; if the act constitutes the crime of illegal business operation or the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard goods, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If any person engages in production or business operation activities without obtaining the required permit according to law, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, confiscate illegal gains, products, and items such as tools, equipment, and raw materials used for illegal production. If the value of the goods is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes the crime of illegal business operation, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
The relevant trade associations shall strengthen industry self-discipline, supervise the production and business operation activities of producers and business operators; strengthen the popularization and publicity of public health knowledge, and guide consumers to choose products produced or sold by lawful producers and business operators, as well as products with lawful labels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产者生产产品所使用的原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品，应当符合法律、行政法规的规定和国家强制性标准。  
违反前款规定，违法使用原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责没收违法所得，货值金额不足5000元的，并处2万元罚款；货值金额5000元以上不足1万元的，并处5万元罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照；构成生产、销售伪劣商品罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 4** The raw materials, auxiliary materials, additives, and agricultural inputs used by a producer in producing products shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the national mandatory standards.  
If any person violates the preceding paragraph and illegally uses raw materials, auxiliary materials, additives, or agricultural inputs, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, confiscate the illegal gains. If the value of the goods is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the goods is 5,000 yuan or more but less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value of the goods is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing department shall revoke the permit; if the act constitutes the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard goods, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　销售者必须建立并执行进货检查验收制度，审验供货商的经营资格，验明产品合格证明和产品标识，并建立产品进货台账，如实记录产品名称、规格、数量、供货商及其联系方式、进货时间等内容。从事产品批发业务的销售企业应当建立产品销售台账，如实记录批发的产品品种、规格、数量、流向等内容。在产品集中交易场所销售自制产品的生产企业应当比照从事产品批发业务的销售企业的规定，履行建立产品销售台账的义务。进货台账和销售台账保存期限不得少于2年。销售者应当向供货商按照产品生产批次索要符合法定条件的检验机构出具的检验报告或者由供货商签字或者盖章的检验报告复印件；不能提供检验报告或者检验报告复印件的产品，不得销售。  
违反前款规定的，由工商、药品监督管理部门依据各自职责责令停止销售；不能提供检验报告或者检验报告复印件销售产品的，没收违法所得和违法销售的产品，并处货值金额3倍的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照。

**Article 5** A seller must establish and implement a system for inspection and acceptance of purchased goods, examine the business qualifications of the supplier, verify the product conformity certificate and product labeling, and establish a product purchase ledger, truthfully recording the product name, specifications, quantity, supplier and its contact information, purchase time, and other relevant content. A sales enterprise engaged in wholesale business of products shall establish a product sales ledger, truthfully recording the variety, specifications, quantity, flow direction, and other content of the products sold wholesale. A production enterprise selling self-made products in a centralized product trading venue shall, by reference to the provisions applicable to a sales enterprise engaged in wholesale business of products, fulfill the obligation of establishing a product sales ledger. The purchase ledger and sales ledger shall be kept for a period of not less than two years. The seller shall, according to the product production batch, demand from the supplier an inspection report issued by an inspection institution meeting the statutory conditions, or a copy of the inspection report signed or sealed by the supplier; products for which no inspection report or copy of inspection report can be provided shall not be sold.  
If any person violates the preceding paragraph, the department of industry and commerce or pharmaceuticals shall, within its respective functions, order the cessation of sales; if any person sells products without being able to provide an inspection report or a copy of the inspection report, the illegal gains and the illegally sold products shall be confiscated, and a fine of three times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing department shall revoke the permit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　产品集中交易市场的开办企业、产品经营柜台出租企业、产品展销会的举办企业，应当审查入场销售者的经营资格，明确入场销售者的产品安全管理责任，定期对入场销售者的经营环境、条件、内部安全管理制度和经营产品是否符合法定要求进行检查，发现销售不符合法定要求产品或者其他违法行为的，应当及时制止并立即报告所在地工商行政管理部门。  
违反前款规定的，由工商行政管理部门处以1000元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿；造成严重后果的，吊销营业执照。

**Article 6** An enterprise that establishes a centralized product trading market, an enterprise that leases product sales counters, or an enterprise that organizes product trade fairs shall examine the business qualifications of the on-site sellers, clarify the product safety management responsibilities of the on-site sellers, regularly inspect whether the business environment, conditions, internal safety management systems, and the products sold by the on-site sellers comply with the statutory requirements, and if it finds any sale of products not meeting statutory requirements or other illegal acts, it shall promptly stop such acts and immediately report to the local administrative department for industry and commerce.  
If any enterprise violates the preceding paragraph, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order a suspension of business for rectification; if serious consequences are caused, it shall revoke the business license.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　出口产品的生产经营者应当保证其出口产品符合进口国(地区)的标准或者合同要求。法律规定产品必须经过检验方可出口的，应当经符合法律规定的机构检验合格。  
出口产品检验人员应当依照法律、行政法规规定和有关标准、程序、方法进行检验，对其出具的检验证单等负责。  
出入境检验检疫机构和商务、药品等监督管理部门应当建立出口产品的生产经营者良好记录和不良记录，并予以公布。对有良好记录的出口产品的生产经营者，简化检验检疫手续。  
出口产品的生产经营者逃避产品检验或者弄虚作假的，由出入境检验检疫机构和药品监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得和产品，并处货值金额3倍的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** A producer or business operator of export products shall ensure that its export products comply with the standards of the importing country (region) or the contractual requirements. Where laws stipulate that a product must undergo inspection before it may be exported, it shall pass the inspection by an institution that meets the statutory requirements.  
The inspection personnel for export products shall carry out inspections in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and the relevant standards, procedures, and methods, and shall be responsible for the inspection certificates they issue.  
The entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities and the supervision and administration departments of commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall establish records of good conduct and bad conduct of producers and business operators of export products and make them public. For producers and business operators of export products with good conduct records, the inspection and quarantine procedures shall be simplified.  
If a producer or business operator of export products evades product inspection or engages in fraud, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities and the pharmaceutical supervision and administration department shall, within their respective functions, confiscate the illegal gains and the products, and impose a fine of three times the value of the goods; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　进口产品应当符合我国国家技术规范的强制性要求以及我国与出口国(地区)签订的协议规定的检验要求。  
质检、药品监督管理部门依据生产经营者的诚信度和质量管理水平以及进口产品风险评估的结果，对进口产品实施分类管理，并对进口产品的收货人实施备案管理。进口产品的收货人应当如实记录进口产品流向。记录保存期限不得少于2年。  
质检、药品监督管理部门发现不符合法定要求产品时，可以将不符合法定要求产品的进货人、报检人、代理人列入不良记录名单。进口产品的进货人、销售者弄虚作假的，由质检、药品监督管理部门依据各自职责，没收违法所得和产品，并处货值金额3倍的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。进口产品的报检人、代理人弄虚作假的，取消报检资格，并处货值金额等值的罚款。

**Article 8** Imported products shall comply with the mandatory requirements of the national technical specifications of China and the inspection requirements stipulated in the agreements signed between China and the exporting country (region).  
The quality supervision and pharmaceutical supervision and administration departments shall, based on the integrity and quality management level of the producers or business operators and the results of risk assessment of imported products, implement classified management of imported products and implement record-filing management for the consignees of imported products. The consignee of imported products shall truthfully record the flow direction of imported products. The record shall be kept for a period of not less than two years.  
If the quality supervision or pharmaceutical supervision and administration department discovers a product that does not meet the statutory requirements, it may include the purchaser, the inspection applicant, or the agent of such non-conforming product in the bad conduct record list. If the purchaser or seller of imported products engages in fraud, the quality supervision or pharmaceutical supervision and administration department shall, within its respective functions, confiscate the illegal gains and the products, and impose a fine of three times the value of the goods; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If the inspection applicant or agent of imported products engages in fraud, the qualification for applying for inspection shall be revoked, and a fine equal to the value of the goods shall be imposed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　生产企业发现其生产的产品存在安全隐患，可能对人体健康和生命安全造成损害的，应当向社会公布有关信息，通知销售者停止销售，告知消费者停止使用，主动召回产品，并向有关监督管理部门报告；销售者应当立即停止销售该产品。销售者发现其销售的产品存在安全隐患，可能对人体健康和生命安全造成损害的，应当立即停止销售该产品，通知生产企业或者供货商，并向有关监督管理部门报告。  
生产企业和销售者不履行前款规定义务的，由农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门依据各自职责，责令生产企业召回产品、销售者停止销售，对生产企业并处货值金额3倍的罚款，对销售者并处1000元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成严重后果的，由原发证部门吊销许可证照。

**Article 9** If a production enterprise discovers that the products it produces have potential safety hazards that may cause harm to human health and life safety, it shall make the relevant information public, notify the seller to stop selling, inform the consumer to stop using, voluntarily recall the products, and report to the relevant supervision and administration department; the seller shall immediately stop selling such products. If a seller discovers that the products it sells have potential safety hazards that may cause harm to human health and life safety, it shall immediately stop selling such products, notify the production enterprise or the supplier, and report to the relevant supervision and administration department.  
If a production enterprise or a seller fails to perform the obligations stipulated in the preceding paragraph, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, order the production enterprise to recall the products and the seller to stop selling, and in addition, impose a fine of three times the value of the goods on the production enterprise, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the seller; if serious consequences are caused, the original issuing department shall revoke the permit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将产品安全监督管理纳入政府工作考核目标，对本行政区域内的产品安全监督管理负总责，统一领导、协调本行政区域内的监督管理工作，建立健全监督管理协调机制，加强对行政执法的协调、监督；统一领导、指挥产品安全突发事件应对工作，依法组织查处产品安全事故；建立监督管理责任制，对各监督管理部门进行评议、考核。质检、工商和药品等监督管理部门应当在所在地同级人民政府的统一协调下，依法做好产品安全监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府不履行产品安全监督管理的领导、协调职责，本行政区域内一年多次出现产品安全事故、造成严重社会影响的，由监察机关或者任免机关对政府的主要负责人和直接负责的主管人员给予记大过、降级或者撤职的处分。

**Article 10** A local people's government at or above the county level shall include product safety supervision and administration in the assessment targets for government work, assume overall responsibility for product safety supervision and administration within its administrative region, exercise unified leadership and coordination over the supervision and administration work within its administrative region, establish and improve the coordination mechanism for supervision and administration, and strengthen the coordination and supervision of administrative law enforcement; exercise unified leadership and command over the response to product safety emergencies, and organize the investigation and handling of product safety accidents according to law; establish a responsibility system for supervision and administration, and conduct evaluation and assessment of each supervision and administration department. The quality supervision, industry and commerce, and pharmaceutical supervision and administration departments shall, under the unified coordination of the people's government at the same level in the locality, properly carry out product safety supervision and administration according to law.  
If a local people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its leadership and coordination duties for product safety supervision and administration, resulting in multiple product safety accidents within its administrative region in one year and causing serious social impact, the supervisory organ or the appointment and removal organ shall impose a record of a major demerit, demotion, or removal from office on the principal leading person of the government and the person directly in charge.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院质检、卫生、农业等主管部门在各自职责范围内尽快制定、修改或者起草相关国家标准，加快建立统一管理、协调配套、符合实际、科学合理的产品标准体系。

**Article 11** The competent departments of quality supervision, health, agriculture, and others under the State Council shall, within their respective functions, formulate, revise, or draft relevant national standards as soon as possible, and accelerate the establishment of a product standard system that is uniformly managed, coordinated and compatible, practical, and scientifically reasonable.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府及其部门对产品安全实施监督管理，应当按照法定权限和程序履行职责，做到公开、公平、公正。对生产经营者同一违法行为，不得给予2次以上罚款的行政处罚；对涉嫌构成犯罪、依法需要追究刑事责任的，应当依照《行政执法机关移送涉嫌犯罪案件的规定》，向公安机关移送。  
农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门应当依据各自职责对生产经营者进行监督检查，并对其遵守强制性标准、法定要求的情况予以记录，由监督检查人员签字后归档。监督检查记录应当作为其直接负责主管人员定期考核的内容。公众有权查阅监督检查记录。

**Article 12** When exercising product safety supervision and administration, the people's governments at or above the county level and their departments shall perform their duties in accordance with the statutory powers and procedures, and act in an open, fair, and just manner. For the same illegal act by a producer or business operator, no administrative penalty of a fine shall be imposed more than twice; if the act is suspected of constituting a crime and criminal liability needs to be investigated according to law, the case shall be transferred to the public security organ in accordance with the Provisions on the Transfer of Suspected Criminal Cases by Administrative Law Enforcement Organs.  
The supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, conduct supervision and inspection of producers and business operators, and record their compliance with mandatory standards and statutory requirements. The records shall be signed by the inspection personnel and filed. The supervision and inspection records shall serve as part of the content for regular assessment of the directly responsible supervisors. The public shall have the right to consult the supervision and inspection records.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产经营者有下列情形之一的，农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门应当依据各自职责采取措施，纠正违法行为，防止或者减少危害发生，并依照本规定予以处罚：  
(一)依法应当取得许可证照而未取得许可证照从事生产经营活动的；  
(二)取得许可证照或者经过认证后，不按照法定条件、要求从事生产经营活动或者生产、销售不符合法定要求产品的；  
(三)生产经营者不再符合法定条件、要求继续从事生产经营活动的；  
(四)生产者生产产品不按照法律、行政法规的规定和国家强制性标准使用原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品的；  
(五)销售者没有建立并执行进货检查验收制度，并建立产品进货台账的；  
(六)生产企业和销售者发现其生产、销售的产品存在安全隐患，可能对人体健康和生命安全造成损害，不履行本规定的义务的；  
(七)生产经营者违反法律、行政法规和本规定的其他有关规定的。  
农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门不履行前款规定职责、造成后果的，由监察机关或者任免机关对其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过或者降级的处分；造成严重后果的，给予其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员撤职或者开除的处分；其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成渎职罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本规定，滥用职权或者有其他渎职行为的，由监察机关或者任免机关对其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记过或者记大过的处分；造成严重后果的，给予其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员降级或者撤职的处分；其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成渎职罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where a producer or business operator falls under any of the following circumstances, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall, within their respective functions, take measures to correct the illegal act, prevent or reduce the occurrence of harm, and impose penalties in accordance with these Provisions:  
(1) Engaging in production or business operation activities without obtaining the required permit according to law;  
(2) After obtaining the permit or certification, failing to engage in production or business operation activities in accordance with the statutory conditions and requirements, or producing or selling products that do not meet the statutory requirements;  
(3) The producer or business operator no longer meets the statutory conditions or requirements but continues to engage in production or business operation activities;  
(4) The producer fails to use raw materials, auxiliary materials, additives, or agricultural inputs in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations and national mandatory standards;  
(5) The seller fails to establish and implement a system for inspection and acceptance of purchased goods, and fails to establish a product purchase ledger;  
(6) The production enterprise or the seller discovers that the products it produces or sell have potential safety hazards that may cause harm to human health and life safety, and fails to perform the obligations under these Provisions;  
(7) The producer or business operator violates other relevant provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and these Provisions.  
If any supervision and administration department of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., fails to perform the duties specified in the preceding paragraph and causes consequences, the supervisory organ or the appointment and removal organ shall impose a record of a major demerit or demotion on its principal leading person, the person directly in charge, and other directly responsible persons; if serious consequences are caused, it shall impose removal from office or expulsion on its principal leading person, the person directly in charge, and other directly responsible persons; if the principal leading person, the person directly in charge, or other directly responsible persons constitute the crime of malfeasance, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If any person violates these Provisions by abusing powers or committing other acts of malfeasance, the supervisory organ or the appointment and removal organ shall impose a record of a demerit or a major demerit on its principal leading person, the person directly in charge, and other directly responsible persons; if serious consequences are caused, it shall impose demotion or removal from office on its principal leading person, the person directly in charge, and other directly responsible persons; if the principal leading person, the person directly in charge, or other directly responsible persons constitute the crime of malfeasance, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门发现违反本规定的行为，属于其他监督管理部门职责的，应当立即书面通知并移交有权处理的监督管理部门处理。有权处理的部门应当立即处理，不得推诿；因不立即处理或者推诿造成后果的，由监察机关或者任免机关对其主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过或者降级的处分。

**Article 14** If the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., discover any act violating these Provisions that falls within the functions of another supervision and administration department, they shall immediately notify in writing and transfer the case to the competent supervision and administration department for handling. The competent department shall handle the case immediately and shall not shift responsibility; if consequences arise from failure to handle immediately or from shifting responsibility, the supervisory organ or the appointment and removal organ shall impose a record of a major demerit or demotion on its principal leading person, the person directly in charge, and other directly responsible persons.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门履行各自产品安全监督管理职责，有下列职权：  
(一)进入生产经营场所实施现场检查；  
(二)查阅、复制、查封、扣押有关合同、票据、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
(三)查封、扣押不符合法定要求的产品，违法使用的原料、辅料、添加剂、农业投入品以及用于违法生产的工具、设备；  
(四)查封存在危害人体健康和生命安全重大隐患的生产经营场所。

**Article 15** The supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall have the following functions when performing their respective duties of product safety supervision and administration:  
(1) Entering the premises of production or business operation to conduct on-site inspections;  
(2) Consulting, making copies of, sealing up, or seizing relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials;  
(3) Sealing up or seizing products that do not meet the statutory requirements, illegally used raw materials, auxiliary materials, additives, agricultural inputs, and tools and equipment used for illegal production;  
(4) Sealing up premises of production or business operation that pose major potential hazards to human health and life safety.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门应当建立生产经营者违法行为记录制度，对违法行为的情况予以记录并公布；对有多次违法行为记录的生产经营者，吊销许可证照。

**Article 16** The supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall establish a record system for illegal acts of producers and business operators, record and publicize the details of illegal acts; for producers or business operators with multiple records of illegal acts, their permits shall be revoked.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　检验检测机构出具虚假检验报告，造成严重后果的，由授予其资质的部门吊销其检验检测资质；构成犯罪的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 17** If an inspection and testing institution issues a false inspection report, causing serious consequences, the department that granted its qualification shall revoke its inspection and testing qualification; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated against the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　发生产品安全事故或者其他对社会造成严重影响的产品安全事件时，农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门必须在各自职责范围内及时作出反应，采取措施，控制事态发展，减少损失，依照国务院规定发布信息，做好有关善后工作。

**Article 18** When a product safety accident or other product safety incident that has a serious social impact occurs, the supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., must, within their respective functions, promptly respond, take measures to control the development of the situation, reduce losses, release information in accordance with the provisions of the State Council, and properly carry out the relevant aftermath work.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　任何组织或者个人对违反本规定的行为有权举报。接到举报的部门应当为举报人保密。举报经调查属实的，受理举报的部门应当给予举报人奖励。  
农业、卫生、质检、商务、工商、药品等监督管理部门应当公布本单位的电子邮件地址或者举报电话；对接到的举报，应当及时、完整地进行记录并妥善保存。举报的事项属于本部门职责的，应当受理，并依法进行核实、处理、答复；不属于本部门职责的，应当转交有权处理的部门，并告知举报人。

**Article 19** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report any act violating these Provisions. The department that receives the report shall keep the reporter confidential. If the report is verified to be true upon investigation, the department that accepts the report shall reward the reporter.  
The supervision and administration departments of agriculture, health, quality supervision, commerce, industry and commerce, pharmaceuticals, etc., shall publish their email addresses or telephone numbers for reporting; they shall promptly, completely record and properly keep the reports received. If the reported matter falls within their functions, they shall accept it, and carry out verification, handling, and response according to law; if it does not fall within their functions, they shall transfer it to the competent department and inform the reporter.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定自公布之日起施行。

**Article 20** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
