# 艾滋病防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2006-03-01

> (2006年1月29日中华人民共和国国务院令第457号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了预防、控制艾滋病的发生与流行，保障人体健康和公共卫生，根据传染病防治法，制定本条例。 第二条　艾滋病防治工作坚持预防为主、防治结合的方针，建立政府组织领导、部门各负其责、全社会共同参与的机制，加强宣传教育，采取行为干预和关怀救助等措施，实行综合防治。 第三条　任何单位和个人不得歧视艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属。艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属享有的婚姻、就业、就医、入学等合法权益受法律保护。 第四条　县级以上人民政府统一领导艾滋病防治工作，建立健全艾滋病防治工作协调机制和工作责任制，对有关部门承担的艾滋病防治工作进行考核、监督。 县级以上人民政府有关部门按照职责分工负责艾滋病防治及其监督管理工作。 第五条　国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定国家艾滋病防治规划；县级以上地方人民政府依照本条例规定和国家艾滋病防治规划，制定并组织实施本行政区域的艾滋病防治行动计划。 第六条　国家鼓励和支持工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、红十字会等团体协助各级人民政府开展艾滋病防治工作。 居民委员会和村民委员会应当协助地方各级人民政府和政府有关部门开展有关艾滋病防治的法律、法规、政策和知识的宣传教育，发展有关艾滋病防治的公益事业，做好艾滋病防治工作。 第七条　各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当采取措施，鼓励和支持有关组织和个人依照本条例规定以及国家艾滋病防治规划和艾滋病防治行动计划的要求，参与艾滋病防治工作，对艾滋病防治工作提供捐赠，对有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群进行行为干预，对艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属提供关怀和救助。 第八条　国家鼓励和支持开展与艾滋病预防、诊断、治疗等有关的科学研究，提高艾滋病防治的科学技术水平；鼓励和支持开展传统医药以及传统医药与现代医药相结合防治艾滋病的临床治疗与研究。 国家鼓励和支持开展艾滋病防治工作的国际合作与交流。 第九条　县级以上人民政府和政府有关部门对在艾滋病防治工作中做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。 对因参与艾滋病防治工作或者因执行公务感染艾滋病病毒，以及因此致病、丧失劳动能力或者死亡的人员，按照有关规定给予补助、抚恤。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防、控制艾滋病的发生与流行，保障人体健康和公共卫生，根据传染病防治法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases for the purpose of preventing and controlling the occurrence and prevalence of AIDS, ensuring human health and public health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　艾滋病防治工作坚持预防为主、防治结合的方针，建立政府组织领导、部门各负其责、全社会共同参与的机制，加强宣传教育，采取行为干预和关怀救助等措施，实行综合防治。

**Article 2** AIDS prevention and treatment work shall adhere to the policy of prevention first, combining prevention and treatment, establish a mechanism of government organization and leadership, departments assuming their respective responsibilities, and the whole society participating together, strengthen publicity and education, adopt measures such as behavioral intervention and care and assistance, and implement comprehensive prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　任何单位和个人不得歧视艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属。艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属享有的婚姻、就业、就医、入学等合法权益受法律保护。

**Article 3** No unit or individual shall discriminate against HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members. The lawful rights and interests enjoyed by HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members in marriage, employment, medical treatment, schooling, etc., shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府统一领导艾滋病防治工作，建立健全艾滋病防治工作协调机制和工作责任制，对有关部门承担的艾滋病防治工作进行考核、监督。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门按照职责分工负责艾滋病防治及其监督管理工作。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall exercise unified leadership over AIDS prevention and treatment work, establish and improve coordination mechanisms and work responsibility systems for AIDS prevention and treatment, and assess and supervise the AIDS prevention and treatment work undertaken by relevant departments.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for AIDS prevention and treatment and their supervision and administration according to their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定国家艾滋病防治规划；县级以上地方人民政府依照本条例规定和国家艾滋病防治规划，制定并组织实施本行政区域的艾滋病防治行动计划。

**Article 5** The health administrative department of the State Council, together with other relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate the national AIDS prevention and treatment plan; local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the national AIDS prevention and treatment plan, formulate and organize the implementation of AIDS prevention and treatment action plans for their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、红十字会等团体协助各级人民政府开展艾滋病防治工作。  
居民委员会和村民委员会应当协助地方各级人民政府和政府有关部门开展有关艾滋病防治的法律、法规、政策和知识的宣传教育，发展有关艾滋病防治的公益事业，做好艾滋病防治工作。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, the Red Cross Society, and other organizations to assist people's governments at all levels in carrying out AIDS prevention and treatment work.  
Residents committees and villagers committees shall assist local people's governments at all levels and relevant government departments in carrying out publicity and education on laws, regulations, policies, and knowledge relating to AIDS prevention and treatment, develop public welfare undertakings relating to AIDS prevention and treatment, and perform well the AIDS prevention and treatment work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当采取措施，鼓励和支持有关组织和个人依照本条例规定以及国家艾滋病防治规划和艾滋病防治行动计划的要求，参与艾滋病防治工作，对艾滋病防治工作提供捐赠，对有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群进行行为干预，对艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属提供关怀和救助。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall take measures to encourage and support relevant organizations and individuals to participate in AIDS prevention and treatment work in accordance with these Regulations and the requirements of the national AIDS prevention and treatment plan and AIDS prevention and treatment action plans, provide donations for AIDS prevention and treatment work, conduct behavioral intervention among populations at risk of HIV infection, and provide care and assistance to HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持开展与艾滋病预防、诊断、治疗等有关的科学研究，提高艾滋病防治的科学技术水平；鼓励和支持开展传统医药以及传统医药与现代医药相结合防治艾滋病的临床治疗与研究。  
国家鼓励和支持开展艾滋病防治工作的国际合作与交流。

**Article 8** The State encourages and supports the conduct of scientific research relating to AIDS prevention, diagnosis, treatment, etc., to improve the scientific and technological level of AIDS prevention and treatment; encourages and supports the conduct of clinical treatment and research using traditional medicine and the combination of traditional medicine and modern medicine for AIDS prevention and treatment.  
The State encourages and supports the conduct of international cooperation and exchange in AIDS prevention and treatment work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上人民政府和政府有关部门对在艾滋病防治工作中做出显著成绩和贡献的单位和个人，给予表彰和奖励。  
对因参与艾滋病防治工作或者因执行公务感染艾滋病病毒，以及因此致病、丧失劳动能力或者死亡的人员，按照有关规定给予补助、抚恤。

**Article 9** People's governments at or above the county level and relevant government departments shall give commendations and rewards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements and contributions in AIDS prevention and treatment work.  
For persons who become infected with HIV due to participation in AIDS prevention and treatment work or performance of official duties, and thereby become ill, lose their ability to work, or die, subsidies and pensions shall be given in accordance with relevant regulations.

### 第二章　宣传教育

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地方各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当组织开展艾滋病防治以及关怀和不歧视艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属的宣传教育，提倡健康文明的生活方式，营造良好的艾滋病防治的社会环境。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall organize and carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment, as well as care and non-discrimination against HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members, advocate a healthy and civilized lifestyle, and create a good social environment for AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地方各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当在车站、码头、机场、公园等公共场所以及旅客列车和从事旅客运输的船舶等公共交通工具显著位置，设置固定的艾滋病防治广告牌或者张贴艾滋病防治公益广告，组织发放艾滋病防治宣传材料。

**Article 11** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall set up fixed AIDS prevention and treatment billboards or post AIDS prevention and treatment public service advertisements in prominent positions at public places such as stations, docks, airports, parks, etc., and on public transport such as passenger trains and ships engaged in passenger transport, and organize the distribution of AIDS prevention and treatment publicity materials.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门应当加强艾滋病防治的宣传教育工作，对有关部门、组织和个人开展艾滋病防治的宣传教育工作提供技术支持。  
医疗卫生机构应当组织工作人员学习有关艾滋病防治的法律、法规、政策和知识；医务人员在开展艾滋病、性病等相关疾病咨询、诊断和治疗过程中，应当对就诊者进行艾滋病防治的宣传教育。

**Article 12** The health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment, and provide technical support to relevant departments, organizations, and individuals in carrying out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment.  
Medical and health institutions shall organize their staff to study laws, regulations, policies, and knowledge relating to AIDS prevention and treatment; medical personnel shall, in the process of providing consultation, diagnosis, and treatment for AIDS, sexually transmitted diseases, and other related diseases, carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment for patients.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府教育主管部门应当指导、督促高等院校、中等职业学校和普通中学将艾滋病防治知识纳入有关课程，开展有关课外教育活动。  
高等院校、中等职业学校和普通中学应当组织学生学习艾滋病防治知识。

**Article 13** The education administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall guide and urge institutions of higher learning, secondary vocational schools, and ordinary middle schools to incorporate AIDS prevention and treatment knowledge into relevant courses and carry out relevant extracurricular educational activities.  
Institutions of higher learning, secondary vocational schools, and ordinary middle schools shall organize students to study AIDS prevention and treatment knowledge.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门应当利用计划生育宣传和技术服务网络，组织开展艾滋病防治的宣传教育。  
计划生育技术服务机构向育龄人群提供计划生育技术服务和生殖健康服务时，应当开展艾滋病防治的宣传教育。

**Article 14** The health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall utilize the family planning publicity and technical service network to organize and carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment.  
When family planning technical service institutions provide family planning technical services and reproductive health services to people of childbearing age, they shall carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府有关部门和从事劳务中介服务的机构，应当对进城务工人员加强艾滋病防治的宣传教育。

**Article 15** Relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and institutions engaged in labor intermediary services shall strengthen publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment for migrant workers in urban areas.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出入境检验检疫机构应当在出入境口岸加强艾滋病防治的宣传教育工作，对出入境人员有针对性地提供艾滋病防治咨询和指导。

**Article 16** Entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall strengthen publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment at entry-exit ports, and provide targeted consultation and guidance on AIDS prevention and treatment to persons entering or exiting the country.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励和支持妇女联合会、红十字会开展艾滋病防治的宣传教育，将艾滋病防治的宣传教育纳入妇女儿童工作内容，提高妇女预防艾滋病的意识和能力，组织红十字会会员和红十字会志愿者开展艾滋病防治的宣传教育。

**Article 17** The State encourages and supports women's federations and the Red Cross Society to carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment, incorporate publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment into the work content for women and children, enhance women's awareness and ability to prevent AIDS, and organize Red Cross members and Red Cross volunteers to carry out publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　地方各级人民政府和政府有关部门应当采取措施，鼓励和支持有关组织和个人对有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群开展艾滋病防治的咨询、指导和宣传教育。

**Article 18** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant government departments shall take measures to encourage and support relevant organizations and individuals to carry out consultation, guidance, and publicity and education on AIDS prevention and treatment for populations at risk of HIV infection.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　广播、电视、报刊、互联网等新闻媒体应当开展艾滋病防治的公益宣传。

**Article 19** News media such as radio, television, newspapers, and the Internet shall carry out public interest publicity on AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　机关、团体、企业事业单位、个体经济组织应当组织本单位从业人员学习有关艾滋病防治的法律、法规、政策和知识，支持本单位从业人员参与艾滋病防治的宣传教育活动。

**Article 20** State organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and individual economic organizations shall organize their employees to study laws, regulations, policies, and knowledge relating to AIDS prevention and treatment, and support their employees to participate in publicity and education activities on AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当在医疗卫生机构开通艾滋病防治咨询服务电话，向公众提供艾滋病防治咨询服务和指导。

**Article 21** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall set up AIDS prevention and treatment consultation service hotlines in medical and health institutions to provide consultation services and guidance on AIDS prevention and treatment to the public.

### 第三章　预防与控制

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家建立健全艾滋病监测网络。  
国务院卫生主管部门制定国家艾滋病监测规划和方案。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门根据国家艾滋病监测规划和方案，制定本行政区域的艾滋病监测计划和工作方案，组织开展艾滋病监测和专题调查，掌握艾滋病疫情变化情况和流行趋势。  
疾病预防控制机构负责对艾滋病发生、流行以及影响其发生、流行的因素开展监测活动。  
出入境检验检疫机构负责对出入境人员进行艾滋病监测，并将监测结果及时向卫生主管部门报告。

**Article 22** The State shall establish and improve the AIDS surveillance network.  
The health administrative department of the State Council shall formulate national AIDS surveillance plans and programs. The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national AIDS surveillance plans and programs, formulate AIDS surveillance plans and work programs for their respective administrative regions, organize and carry out AIDS surveillance and special investigations, and grasp the changing situation and epidemic trends of AIDS.  
Disease prevention and control institutions shall be responsible for conducting surveillance activities on the occurrence and prevalence of AIDS and the factors affecting its occurrence and prevalence.  
Entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall be responsible for conducting AIDS surveillance on persons entering or exiting the country, and shall promptly report the surveillance results to the health administrative departments.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家实行艾滋病自愿咨询和自愿检测制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门指定的医疗卫生机构，应当按照国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门制定的艾滋病自愿咨询和检测办法，为自愿接受艾滋病咨询、检测的人员免费提供咨询和初筛检测。

**Article 23** The State implements a system of voluntary counseling and voluntary testing for AIDS.  
Medical and health institutions designated by the health administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the measures for voluntary counseling and testing for AIDS formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council together with other relevant departments of the State Council, provide free counseling and initial screening testing to persons who voluntarily accept AIDS counseling and testing.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门根据预防、控制艾滋病的需要，可以规定应当进行艾滋病检测的情形。

**Article 24** The health administrative department of the State Council, together with other relevant departments of the State Council, may, based on the need for prevention and control of AIDS, prescribe the circumstances under which AIDS testing shall be conducted.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门根据医疗卫生机构布局和艾滋病流行情况，按照国家有关规定确定承担艾滋病检测工作的实验室。  
国家出入境检验检疫机构按照国务院卫生主管部门规定的标准和规范，确定承担出入境人员艾滋病检测工作的实验室。

**Article 25** The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, based on the layout of medical and health institutions and the epidemic situation of AIDS, and in accordance with relevant state regulations, determine the laboratories that carry out AIDS testing work.  
The state entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, in accordance with the standards and norms prescribed by the health administrative department of the State Council, determine the laboratories that carry out AIDS testing work for persons entering or exiting the country.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方人民政府和政府有关部门应当依照本条例规定，根据本行政区域艾滋病的流行情况，制定措施，鼓励和支持居民委员会、村民委员会以及其他有关组织和个人推广预防艾滋病的行为干预措施，帮助有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群改变行为。  
有关组织和个人对有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群实施行为干预措施，应当符合本条例的规定以及国家艾滋病防治规划和艾滋病防治行动计划的要求。

**Article 26** Local people's governments at or above the county level and relevant government departments shall, in accordance with these Regulations and based on the epidemic situation of AIDS in their respective administrative regions, formulate measures to encourage and support residents committees, villagers committees, and other relevant organizations and individuals to promote behavioral intervention measures for preventing AIDS, and help populations at risk of HIV infection to change their behavior.  
When relevant organizations and individuals implement behavioral intervention measures for populations at risk of HIV infection, they shall comply with the provisions of these Regulations and the requirements of the national AIDS prevention and treatment plan and AIDS prevention and treatment action plans.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府应当建立艾滋病防治工作与禁毒工作的协调机制，组织有关部门落实针对吸毒人群的艾滋病防治措施。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生、公安和药品监督管理部门应当互相配合，根据本行政区域艾滋病流行和吸毒者的情况，积极稳妥地开展对吸毒成瘾者的药物维持治疗工作，并有计划地实施其他干预措施。

**Article 27** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism between AIDS prevention and treatment work and drug control work, and organize relevant departments to implement AIDS prevention and treatment measures targeting drug users.  
The health, public security, and drug regulatory departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall cooperate with each other, and based on the AIDS epidemic situation and the situation of drug users in their respective administrative regions, actively and steadily carry out drug maintenance treatment work for drug addicts, and implement other intervention measures in a planned manner.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府卫生、市场监督管理、药品监督管理、广播电视等部门应当组织推广使用安全套，建立和完善安全套供应网络。

**Article 28** The health, market supervision and administration, drug regulatory, and radio and television departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the promotion of condom use, and establish and improve the condom supply network.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的公共场所的经营者应当在公共场所内放置安全套或者设置安全套发售设施。

**Article 29** Operators of public places designated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall place condoms or set up condom vending facilities in public places.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公共场所的服务人员应当依照《公共场所卫生管理条例》的规定，定期进行相关健康检查，取得健康合格证明；经营者应当查验其健康合格证明，不得允许未取得健康合格证明的人员从事服务工作。

**Article 30** Service personnel in public places shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Public Place Sanitation, undergo relevant health examinations periodically and obtain health certificates; operators shall check their health certificates, and shall not allow personnel who have not obtained health certificates to engage in service work.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安、司法行政机关对被依法逮捕、拘留和在监狱中执行刑罚以及被依法收容教育、强制戒毒和劳动教养的艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人，应当采取相应的防治措施，防止艾滋病传播。  
对公安、司法行政机关依照前款规定采取的防治措施，县级以上地方人民政府应当给予经费保障，疾病预防控制机构应当予以技术指导和配合。

**Article 31** Public security and judicial administrative organs shall take corresponding prevention and treatment measures for HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients who are arrested, detained, serving sentences in prison, or subjected to reeducation through detention, compulsory drug rehabilitation, and reeducation through labor according to law, to prevent the spread of AIDS.  
For the prevention and treatment measures taken by public security and judicial administrative organs in accordance with the preceding paragraph, local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide funding guarantees, and disease prevention and control institutions shall provide technical guidance and cooperation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对卫生技术人员和在执行公务中可能感染艾滋病病毒的人员，县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门和其他有关部门应当组织开展艾滋病防治知识和专业技能的培训，有关单位应当采取有效的卫生防护措施和医疗保健措施。

**Article 32** For health technical personnel and persons who may become infected with HIV in the performance of official duties, the health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall organize and carry out training on AIDS prevention and treatment knowledge and professional skills, and relevant units shall take effective health protection measures and medical care measures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　医疗卫生机构和出入境检验检疫机构应当按照国务院卫生主管部门的规定，遵守标准防护原则，严格执行操作规程和消毒管理制度，防止发生艾滋病医院感染和医源性感染。

**Article 33** Medical and health institutions and entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council, comply with the standard protection principles, strictly implement operating procedures and disinfection management systems, and prevent the occurrence of hospital-acquired and iatrogenic infections of AIDS.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　疾病预防控制机构应当按照属地管理的原则，对艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人进行医学随访。

**Article 34** Disease prevention and control institutions shall, in accordance with the principle of territorial management, conduct medical follow-up of HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　血站、单采血浆站应当对采集的人体血液、血浆进行艾滋病检测；不得向医疗机构和血液制品生产单位供应未经艾滋病检测或者艾滋病检测阳性的人体血液、血浆。  
血液制品生产单位应当在原料血浆投料生产前对每一份血浆进行艾滋病检测；未经艾滋病检测或者艾滋病检测阳性的血浆，不得作为原料血浆投料生产。  
医疗机构应当对因应急用血而临时采集的血液进行艾滋病检测，对临床用血艾滋病检测结果进行核查；对未经艾滋病检测、核查或者艾滋病检测阳性的血液，不得采集或者使用。

**Article 35** Blood stations and single plasma collection stations shall conduct AIDS testing on the human blood and plasma collected; they shall not supply human blood or plasma that has not been tested for AIDS or that tests positive for AIDS to medical institutions or blood product production units.  
Blood product production units shall conduct AIDS testing on each unit of plasma before putting the source plasma into production; plasma that has not been tested for AIDS or that tests positive for AIDS shall not be used as source plasma for production.  
Medical institutions shall conduct AIDS testing on blood temporarily collected for emergency use, and verify the AIDS test results of clinically used blood; blood that has not been tested or verified for AIDS or that tests positive for AIDS shall not be collected or used.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　采集或者使用人体组织、器官、细胞、骨髓等的，应当进行艾滋病检测；未经艾滋病检测或者艾滋病检测阳性的，不得采集或者使用。但是，用于艾滋病防治科研、教学的除外。

**Article 36** When collecting or using human tissues, organs, cells, bone marrow, etc., AIDS testing shall be conducted; those that have not been tested for AIDS or that test positive for AIDS shall not be collected or used, except for those used for scientific research and teaching in AIDS prevention and treatment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　进口人体血液制品，应当依照药品管理法的规定，经国务院药品监督管理部门批准，取得进口药品注册证书。  
禁止进出口用于临床医疗的人体血液、血浆、组织、器官、细胞、骨髓等。但是，出于人道主义、救死扶伤目的，可以进出口临床急需、捐献配型的特殊血型血液、骨髓造血干细胞、外周血造血干细胞、脐带血造血干细胞，由中国红十字会总会办理出入境手续；具体办法由国务院卫生主管部门会同国家出入境检验检疫机构制定。  
依照前款规定进出口的特殊血型血液、骨髓造血干细胞、外周血造血干细胞、脐带血造血干细胞，应当依照国境卫生检疫法律、行政法规的有关规定，接受出入境检验检疫机构的检疫。未经检疫或者检疫不合格的，不得进出口。

**Article 37** The import of human blood products shall be approved by the drug regulatory department of the State Council and an import drug registration certificate shall be obtained in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law.  
The import and export of human blood, plasma, tissues, organs, cells, bone marrow, etc., for clinical medical use is prohibited. However, for humanitarian and life-saving purposes, special blood type blood, bone marrow hematopoietic stem cells, peripheral blood hematopoietic stem cells, and umbilical cord blood hematopoietic stem cells that are clinically urgently needed and donated with matching types may be imported or exported, and the entry and exit procedures shall be handled by the China Red Cross National Headquarters; the specific measures shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council together with the state entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions.  
The special blood type blood, bone marrow hematopoietic stem cells, peripheral blood hematopoietic stem cells, and umbilical cord blood hematopoietic stem cells imported or exported in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall undergo quarantine by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions in accordance with the relevant provisions of border health quarantine laws and administrative regulations. Those that have not undergone quarantine or fail quarantine shall not be imported or exported.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人应当履行下列义务：  
(一)接受疾病预防控制机构或者出入境检验检疫机构的流行病学调查和指导；  
(二)将感染或者发病的事实及时告知与其有性关系者；  
(三)就医时，将感染或者发病的事实如实告知接诊医生；  
(四)采取必要的防护措施，防止感染他人。  
艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人不得以任何方式故意传播艾滋病。

**Article 38** HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) Accept epidemiological investigation and guidance from disease prevention and control institutions or entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions;  
(2) Timely inform persons with whom they have sexual relations of the fact of infection or onset of the disease;  
(3) When seeking medical treatment, truthfully inform the attending doctor of the fact of infection or onset of the disease;  
(4) Take necessary protective measures to prevent infecting others.  
HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients shall not intentionally transmit AIDS in any way.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　疾病预防控制机构和出入境检验检疫机构进行艾滋病流行病学调查时，被调查单位和个人应当如实提供有关情况。  
未经本人或者其监护人同意，任何单位或者个人不得公开艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属的姓名、住址、工作单位、肖像、病史资料以及其他可能推断出其具体身份的信息。

**Article 39** When disease prevention and control institutions and entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions conduct epidemiological investigations on AIDS, the investigated units and individuals shall truthfully provide relevant information.  
Without the consent of the person concerned or his/her guardian, no unit or individual shall disclose the name, address, work unit, portrait, medical history data, and other information that may deduce the specific identity of HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门和出入境检验检疫机构可以封存有证据证明可能被艾滋病病毒污染的物品，并予以检验或者进行消毒。经检验，属于被艾滋病病毒污染的物品，应当进行卫生处理或者予以销毁；对未被艾滋病病毒污染的物品或者经消毒后可以使用的物品，应当及时解除封存。

**Article 40** The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions may seal up articles that have evidence proving that they may be contaminated with HIV, and conduct testing or disinfection. After testing, articles that are contaminated with HIV shall be subjected to sanitary treatment or destroyed; articles that are not contaminated with HIV or that can be used after disinfection shall be released from sealing in a timely manner.

### 第四章　治疗与救助

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　医疗机构应当为艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人提供艾滋病防治咨询、诊断和治疗服务。  
医疗机构不得因就诊的病人是艾滋病病毒感染者或者艾滋病病人，推诿或者拒绝对其其他疾病进行治疗。

**Article 41** Medical institutions shall provide consultation, diagnosis, and treatment services for AIDS prevention and treatment to HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients.  
Medical institutions shall not, on the grounds that a patient is an HIV-infected person or an AIDS patient, refuse or delay treatment of other diseases for them.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　对确诊的艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人，医疗卫生机构的工作人员应当将其感染或者发病的事实告知本人；本人为无行为能力人或者限制行为能力人的，应当告知其监护人。

**Article 42** For confirmed HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients, the staff of medical and health institutions shall inform the person concerned of the fact of infection or onset of the disease; if the person concerned is a person without capacity or with limited capacity, his/her guardian shall be informed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　医疗卫生机构应当按照国务院卫生主管部门制定的预防艾滋病母婴传播技术指导方案的规定，对孕产妇提供艾滋病防治咨询和检测，对感染艾滋病病毒的孕产妇及其婴儿，提供预防艾滋病母婴传播的咨询、产前指导、阻断、治疗、产后访视、婴儿随访和检测等服务。

**Article 43** Medical and health institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the technical guidance plan for preventing mother-to-child transmission of AIDS formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council, provide consultation and testing for AIDS prevention and treatment to pregnant and lying-in women, and provide services such as consultation, prenatal guidance, blocking, treatment, postpartum follow-up, infant follow-up, and testing for prevention of mother-to-child transmission of AIDS to pregnant and lying-in women infected with HIV and their infants.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府应当采取下列艾滋病防治关怀、救助措施：  
(一)向农村艾滋病病人和城镇经济困难的艾滋病病人免费提供抗艾滋病病毒治疗药品；  
(二)对农村和城镇经济困难的艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人适当减免抗机会性感染治疗药品的费用；  
(三)向接受艾滋病咨询、检测的人员免费提供咨询和初筛检测；  
(四)向感染艾滋病病毒的孕产妇免费提供预防艾滋病母婴传播的治疗和咨询。

**Article 44** People's governments at or above the county level shall take the following care and assistance measures for AIDS prevention and treatment:  
(1) Provide free antiretroviral treatment drugs to rural AIDS patients and urban AIDS patients with financial difficulties;  
(2) Appropriately reduce or waive the fees for drugs for treating opportunistic infections for rural and urban HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients with financial difficulties;  
(3) Provide free consultation and initial screening testing to persons who accept AIDS consultation and testing;  
(4) Provide free treatment and consultation for prevention of mother-to-child transmission of AIDS to pregnant and lying-in women infected with HIV.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　生活困难的艾滋病病人遗留的孤儿和感染艾滋病病毒的未成年人接受义务教育的，应当免收杂费、书本费；接受学前教育和高中阶段教育的，应当减免学费等相关费用。

**Article 45** For orphans left by AIDS patients with financial difficulties and minors infected with HIV who receive compulsory education, sundry fees and textbook fees shall be exempted; for those receiving preschool education and high school education, tuition and other related fees shall be reduced or exempted.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当对生活困难并符合社会救助条件的艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人及其家属给予生活救助。

**Article 46** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide living assistance to HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients, and their family members who have financial difficulties and meet the conditions for social assistance.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当创造条件，扶持有劳动能力的艾滋病病毒感染者和艾滋病病人，从事力所能及的生产和工作。

**Article 47** Relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall create conditions to support HIV-infected persons and AIDS patients who have the ability to work in engaging in production and work within their capabilities.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府应当将艾滋病防治工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，加强和完善艾滋病预防、检测、控制、治疗和救助服务网络的建设，建立健全艾滋病防治专业队伍。  
各级人民政府应当根据艾滋病防治工作需要，将艾滋病防治经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 48** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate AIDS prevention and treatment work into national economic and social development plans, strengthen and improve the construction of service networks for AIDS prevention, testing, control, treatment, and assistance, and establish and improve professional teams for AIDS prevention and treatment.  
People's governments at all levels shall, based on the needs of AIDS prevention and treatment work, include AIDS prevention and treatment funds in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府按照本级政府的职责，负责艾滋病预防、控制、监督工作所需经费。  
国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门，根据艾滋病流行趋势，确定全国与艾滋病防治相关的宣传、培训、监测、检测、流行病学调查、医疗救治、应急处置以及监督检查等项目。中央财政对在艾滋病流行严重地区和贫困地区实施的艾滋病防治重大项目给予补助。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本行政区域的艾滋病防治工作需要和艾滋病流行趋势，确定与艾滋病防治相关的项目，并保障项目的实施经费。

**Article 49** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the funds needed for AIDS prevention, control, and supervision work.  
The health administrative department of the State Council, together with other relevant departments of the State Council, shall, based on the epidemic trend of AIDS, determine nationwide projects related to AIDS prevention and treatment, including publicity, training, surveillance, testing, epidemiological investigation, medical treatment, emergency response, and supervision and inspection. The central finance shall provide subsidies for major AIDS prevention and treatment projects implemented in areas with severe AIDS epidemics and impoverished areas.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the needs of AIDS prevention and treatment work in their respective administrative regions and the epidemic trend of AIDS, determine projects related to AIDS prevention and treatment, and ensure the implementation funds for the projects.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府应当根据艾滋病防治工作需要和艾滋病流行趋势，储备抗艾滋病病毒治疗药品、检测试剂和其他物资。

**Article 50** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the needs of AIDS prevention and treatment work and the epidemic trend of AIDS, stockpile antiretroviral drugs, testing reagents, and other materials.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　地方各级人民政府应当制定扶持措施，对有关组织和个人开展艾滋病防治活动提供必要的资金支持和便利条件。有关组织和个人参与艾滋病防治公益事业，依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 51** Local people's governments at all levels shall formulate support measures to provide necessary financial support and favorable conditions for relevant organizations and individuals to carry out AIDS prevention and treatment activities. Relevant organizations and individuals that participate in the public welfare undertakings of AIDS prevention and treatment shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地方各级人民政府未依照本条例规定履行组织、领导、保障艾滋病防治工作职责，或者未采取艾滋病防治和救助措施的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；造成艾滋病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a local people's government at any level fails to perform its duties of organizing, leading, and guaranteeing AIDS prevention and treatment work, or fails to take AIDS prevention, treatment and relief measures in accordance with these Regulations, the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; where the spread or epidemic of AIDS or any other serious consequence is caused, the competent person in charge shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府卫生主管部门责令改正，通报批评；造成艾滋病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未履行艾滋病防治宣传教育职责的；  
(二)对有证据证明可能被艾滋病病毒污染的物品，未采取控制措施的；  
(三)其他有关失职、渎职行为。  
出入境检验检疫机构有前款规定情形的，由其上级主管部门依照本条规定予以处罚。

**Article 53** Where the health administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the people's government at the same level or the health administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; where the spread or epidemic of AIDS or any other serious consequence is caused, the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to perform the duty of AIDS prevention and treatment publicity and education;  
(2) failing to take control measures against articles that have evidence proving may be contaminated by HIV;  
(3) other acts of dereliction of duty or malfeasance.  
Where an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution falls under any of the circumstances set forth in the preceding paragraph, its competent department at the next higher level shall impose a penalty in accordance with this Article.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上人民政府有关部门未依照本条例规定履行宣传教育、预防控制职责的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评；造成艾滋病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where a relevant department of a people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its duties of publicity, education, prevention and control in accordance with these Regulations, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; where the spread or epidemic of AIDS or any other serious consequence is caused, the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　医疗卫生机构未依照本条例规定履行职责，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门责令限期改正，通报批评，给予警告；造成艾滋病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分，并可以依法吊销有关机构或者责任人员的执业许可证件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未履行艾滋病监测职责的；  
(二)未按照规定免费提供咨询和初筛检测的；  
(三)对临时应急采集的血液未进行艾滋病检测，对临床用血艾滋病检测结果未进行核查，或者将艾滋病检测阳性的血液用于临床的；  
(四)未遵守标准防护原则，或者未执行操作规程和消毒管理制度，发生艾滋病医院感染或者医源性感染的；  
(五)未采取有效的卫生防护措施和医疗保健措施的；  
(六)推诿、拒绝治疗艾滋病病毒感染者或者艾滋病病人的其他疾病，或者对艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人未提供咨询、诊断和治疗服务的；  
(七)未对艾滋病病毒感染者或者艾滋病病人进行医学随访的；  
(八)未按照规定对感染艾滋病病毒的孕产妇及其婴儿提供预防艾滋病母婴传播技术指导的。  
出入境检验检疫机构有前款第(一)项、第(四)项、第(五)项规定情形的，由其上级主管部门依照前款规定予以处罚。

**Article 55** Where a medical and health institution fails to perform its duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; where the spread or epidemic of AIDS or any other serious consequence is caused, the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or expulsion according to law, and the practicing license of the relevant institution or the liable person may be revoked according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to perform the duty of AIDS surveillance;  
(2) failing to provide free consultation and preliminary screening tests as required;  
(3) failing to conduct AIDS tests on blood collected for temporary emergency use, failing to verify the AIDS test results of blood for clinical use, or using blood with positive AIDS test results for clinical purposes;  
(4) failing to observe the principles of standard precautions, or failing to implement operating procedures and disinfection management systems, resulting in nosocomial AIDS infection or iatrogenic infection;  
(5) failing to take effective health protection measures and medical care measures;  
(6) shirking or refusing to treat other diseases of HIV-infected persons or AIDS patients, or failing to provide consultation, diagnosis and treatment services to HIV-infected persons or AIDS patients;  
(7) failing to conduct medical follow-up on HIV-infected persons or AIDS patients;  
(8) failing to provide technical guidance on prevention of mother-to-child transmission of AIDS to pregnant and lying-in women infected with HIV and their infants as required.  
Where an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution falls under the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1), (4), or (5) of the preceding paragraph, its competent department at the next higher level shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　医疗卫生机构违反[本条例第三十九条](#art-39)第二款规定，公开艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人或者其家属的信息的，依照传染病防治法的规定予以处罚。  
出入境检验检疫机构、计划生育技术服务机构或者其他单位、个人违反[本条例第三十九条](#art-39)第二款规定，公开艾滋病病毒感染者、艾滋病病人或者其家属的信息的，由其上级主管部门责令改正，通报批评，给予警告，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，由原发证部门吊销有关机构或者责任人员的执业许可证件。

**Article 56** Where a medical and health institution violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 39 of these Regulations and discloses the information of HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients or their family members, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases.  
Where an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, family planning technical service institution or any other unit or individual violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 39 of these Regulations and discloses the information of HIV-infected persons, AIDS patients or their family members, the competent department at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, give a warning, and impose a sanction on the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department shall revoke the practicing license of the relevant institution or the liable person.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　血站、单采血浆站违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门依照献血法和《血液制品管理条例》的规定予以处罚；造成艾滋病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分，并可以依法吊销血站、单采血浆站的执业许可证：  
(一)对采集的人体血液、血浆未进行艾滋病检测，或者发现艾滋病检测阳性的人体血液、血浆仍然采集的；  
(二)将未经艾滋病检测的人体血液、血浆，或者艾滋病检测阳性的人体血液、血浆供应给医疗机构和血液制品生产单位的。

**Article 57** Where a blood station or a plasma collection station violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if no crime is constituted, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Blood Donation Law and the Regulations on the Administration of Blood Products; where the spread or epidemic of AIDS or any other serious consequence is caused, the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or expulsion according to law, and the practice license of the blood station or plasma collection station may be revoked according to law:  
(1) failing to conduct AIDS tests on the collected human blood or plasma, or continuing to collect human blood or plasma with positive AIDS test results after detection;  
(2) supplying human blood or plasma that has not been tested for AIDS, or human blood or plasma with positive AIDS test results, to medical institutions and blood product production units.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)规定采集或者使用人体组织、器官、细胞、骨髓等的，由县级人民政府卫生主管部门责令改正，通报批评，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，有执业许可证件的，由原发证部门暂扣或者吊销其执业许可证件。

**Article 58** Where any person collects or uses human tissues, organs, cells, bone marrow, etc. in violation of the provisions of Article 36 of these Regulations, the health administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order it to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the suspension of business for rectification, and if it holds a practice license, the original licensing department shall temporarily suspend or revoke its practice license.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对不符合[本条例第三十七条](#art-37)第二款规定进出口的人体血液、血浆、组织、器官、细胞、骨髓等，进出口口岸出入境检验检疫机构应当禁止出入境或者监督销毁。提供、使用未经出入境检验检疫机构检疫的进口人体血液、血浆、组织、器官、细胞、骨髓等的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门没收违法物品以及违法所得，并处违法物品货值金额3倍以上5倍以下的罚款；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员由其所在单位或者上级主管部门依法给予处分。  
未经国务院药品监督管理部门批准，进口血液制品的，依照药品管理法的规定予以处罚。

**Article 59** With respect to the import or export of human blood, plasma, tissues, organs, cells, bone marrow, etc. that do not conform to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 37 of these Regulations, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution at the port of import or export shall prohibit the entry or exit or supervise the destruction. Where any person provides or uses imported human blood, plasma, tissues, organs, cells, bone marrow, etc. that have not been quarantined by an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal articles and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the value of the illegal articles; the competent person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a sanction by their own unit or the competent department at the next higher level according to law.  
Where any person imports blood products without the approval of the drug regulatory department of the State Council, he shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　血站、单采血浆站、医疗卫生机构和血液制品生产单位违反法律、行政法规的规定，造成他人感染艾滋病病毒的，应当依法承担民事赔偿责任。

**Article 60** Where a blood station, plasma collection station, medical and health institution or blood product production unit violates the provisions of laws or administrative regulations and causes another person to be infected with HIV, it shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　公共场所的经营者未查验服务人员的健康合格证明或者允许未取得健康合格证明的人员从事服务工作，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的公共场所的经营者未在公共场所内放置安全套或者设置安全套发售设施的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处500元以上5000元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，责令停业整顿；情节严重的，由原发证部门依法吊销其执业许可证件。

**Article 61** Where an operator of a public place fails to check the health certificates of service personnel or allows personnel who have not obtained health certificates to engage in service work, or where an operator of a public place designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government fails to place condoms or set up condom vending facilities in the public place, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit, give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing department shall revoke its practice license according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　艾滋病病毒感染者或者艾滋病病人故意传播艾滋病的，依法承担民事赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where an HIV-infected person or an AIDS patient intentionally spreads AIDS, he shall bear civil liability for compensation according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
艾滋病，是指人类免疫缺陷病毒(艾滋病病毒)引起的获得性免疫缺陷综合征。  
对吸毒成瘾者的药物维持治疗，是指在批准开办戒毒治疗业务的医疗卫生机构中，选用合适的药物，对吸毒成瘾者进行维持治疗，以减轻对毒品的依赖，减少注射吸毒引起艾滋病病毒的感染和扩散，减少毒品成瘾引起的疾病、死亡和引发的犯罪。  
标准防护原则，是指医务人员将所有病人的血液、其他体液以及被血液、其他体液污染的物品均视为具有传染性的病原物质，医务人员在接触这些物质时，必须采取防护措施。  
有易感染艾滋病病毒危险行为的人群，是指有卖淫、嫖娼、多性伴、男性同性性行为、注射吸毒等危险行为的人群。  
艾滋病监测，是指连续、系统地收集各类人群中艾滋病(或者艾滋病病毒感染)及其相关因素的分布资料，对这些资料综合分析，为有关部门制定预防控制策略和措施提供及时可靠的信息和依据，并对预防控制措施进行效果评价。  
艾滋病检测，是指采用实验室方法对人体血液、其他体液、组织器官、血液衍生物等进行艾滋病病毒、艾滋病病毒抗体及相关免疫指标检测，包括监测、检验检疫、自愿咨询检测、临床诊断、血液及血液制品筛查工作中的艾滋病检测。  
行为干预措施，是指能够有效减少艾滋病传播的各种措施，包括：针对经注射吸毒传播艾滋病的美沙酮维持治疗等措施；针对经性传播艾滋病的安全套推广使用措施，以及规范、方便的性病诊疗措施；针对母婴传播艾滋病的抗病毒药物预防和人工代乳品喂养等措施；早期发现感染者和有助于危险行为改变的自愿咨询检测措施；健康教育措施；提高个人规范意识以及减少危险行为的针对性同伴教育措施。

**Article 63** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:  
AIDS means the acquired immunodeficiency syndrome caused by the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).  
Drug maintenance treatment for drug addicts means the use of appropriate drugs in medical and health institutions approved to carry out drug addiction treatment to provide maintenance treatment for drug addicts, so as to reduce their dependence on drugs, reduce the infection and spread of HIV caused by injection drug use, and reduce the diseases, deaths and crimes caused by drug addiction.  
Standard precaution principle means that medical personnel shall regard all patients' blood, other body fluids, and articles contaminated by blood or other body fluids as infectious pathogenic substances, and medical personnel must take protective measures when contacting these substances.  
Populations at risk of HIV infection means populations with risk behaviors such as prostitution, patronizing prostitutes, multiple sexual partners, male homosexual behavior, injection drug use, etc.  
AIDS surveillance means the continuous and systematic collection of distribution data on AIDS (or HIV infection) and related factors among various populations, comprehensive analysis of such data to provide timely and reliable information and basis for relevant departments to formulate prevention and control strategies and measures, and evaluation of the effectiveness of prevention and control measures.  
AIDS testing means the laboratory testing of human blood, other body fluids, tissues and organs, blood derivatives, etc. for HIV, HIV antibodies and related immune indicators, including AIDS testing in surveillance, inspection and quarantine, voluntary counseling and testing, clinical diagnosis, and blood and blood product screening.  
Behavioral intervention measures means various measures that can effectively reduce the spread of AIDS, including: methadone maintenance treatment and other measures for preventing HIV transmission through injection drug use; condom promotion and use measures and standardized and convenient STD diagnosis and treatment measures for preventing sexual transmission of HIV; antiretroviral drug prevention and artificial milk feeding measures for preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV; voluntary counseling and testing measures for early detection of infected persons and facilitation of behavior change; health education measures; targeted peer education measures to enhance individual awareness of norms and reduce risk behaviors.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本条例自2006年3月1日起施行。1987年12月26日经国务院批准，1988年1月14日由卫生部、外交部、公安部、原国家教育委员会、国家旅游局、原中国民用航空局、国家外国专家局发布的《艾滋病监测管理的若干规定》同时废止。

**Article 64** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2006. The Several Provisions on the Surveillance and Management of AIDS, approved by the State Council on December 26, 1987, and promulgated by the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Public Security, the former State Education Commission, the National Tourism Administration, the former Civil Aviation Administration of China, and the State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs on January 14, 1988, shall be abolished simultaneously.
