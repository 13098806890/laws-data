# 血液制品管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06

> (1996年12月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第208号发布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强血液制品管理，预防和控制经血液途径传播的疾病，保证血液制品的质量，根据药品管理法和传染病防治法，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内从事原料血浆的采集、供应以及血液制品的生产、经营活动。 第三条　国务院卫生行政部门对全国的原料血浆的采集、供应和血液制品的生产、经营活动实施监督管理。 县级以上地方各级人民政府卫生行政部门对本行政区域内的原料血浆的采集、供应和血液制品的生产、经营活动，依照本条例第三十条规定的职责实施监督管理。 第二章　原料血浆的管理 第四条　国家实行单采血浆站统一规划、设置的制度。 国务院卫生行政部门根据核准的全国生产用原料血浆的需求，对单采血浆站的布局、数量和规模制定总体规划。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门根据总体规划制定本行政区域内单采血浆站设置规划和采集血浆的区域规划，并报国务院卫生行政部门备案。 第五条　单采血浆站由血液制品生产单位设置或者由县级人民政府卫生行政部门设置，专门从事单采血浆活动，具有独立法人资格。其他任何单位和个人不得从事单采血浆活动。 第六条　设置单采血浆站，必须具备下列条件： (一)符合单采血浆站布局、数量、规模的规划； (二)具有与所采集原料血浆相适应的卫生专业技术人员； (三)具有与所采集原料血浆相适应的场所及卫生环境； (四)具有识别供血浆者的身份识别系统；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强血液制品管理，预防和控制经血液途径传播的疾病，保证血液制品的质量，根据药品管理法和传染病防治法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases for the purposes of strengthening the administration of blood products, preventing and controlling diseases transmitted through blood, and ensuring the quality of blood products.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内从事原料血浆的采集、供应以及血液制品的生产、经营活动。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the collection and supply of source plasma and the production and business operation of blood products within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院卫生行政部门对全国的原料血浆的采集、供应和血液制品的生产、经营活动实施监督管理。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府卫生行政部门对本行政区域内的原料血浆的采集、供应和血液制品的生产、经营活动，依照[本条例第三十条](#art-30)规定的职责实施监督管理。

**Article 3** The health administrative department under the State Council shall exercise supervision and administration over the collection and supply of source plasma and the production and business operation of blood products nationwide.  
The health administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed in Article 30 of these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the collection and supply of source plasma and the production and business operation of blood products within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　原料血浆的管理

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行单采血浆站统一规划、设置的制度。  
国务院卫生行政部门根据核准的全国生产用原料血浆的需求，对单采血浆站的布局、数量和规模制定总体规划。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门根据总体规划制定本行政区域内单采血浆站设置规划和采集血浆的区域规划，并报国务院卫生行政部门备案。

**Article 4** The State shall practice a system of unified planning and establishment of plasma collection stations.  
The health administrative department under the State Council shall, based on the verified national demand for source plasma for production, formulate an overall plan for the layout, number, and scale of plasma collection stations. The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the overall plan, formulate the plan for the establishment of plasma collection stations and the regional plan for plasma collection within their respective administrative regions, and shall report them to the health administrative department under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　单采血浆站由血液制品生产单位设置或者由县级人民政府卫生行政部门设置，专门从事单采血浆活动，具有独立法人资格。其他任何单位和个人不得从事单采血浆活动。

**Article 5** Plasma collection stations shall be established either by blood product production entities or by the health administrative departments of the county people's governments. Such stations shall exclusively engage in plasma collection activities and shall have the status of an independent legal person. No other unit or individual may engage in plasma collection activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　设置单采血浆站，必须具备下列条件：  
(一)符合单采血浆站布局、数量、规模的规划；  
(二)具有与所采集原料血浆相适应的卫生专业技术人员；  
(三)具有与所采集原料血浆相适应的场所及卫生环境；  
(四)具有识别供血浆者的身份识别系统；  
(五)具有与所采集原料血浆相适应的单采血浆机械及其他设施；  
(六)具有对所采集原料血浆进行质量检验的技术人员以及必要的仪器设备。

**Article 6** The following conditions must be met for the establishment of a plasma collection station:  
(1) compliance with the plan on the layout, number, and scale of plasma collection stations;  
(2) having health professional and technical personnel commensurate with the source plasma to be collected;  
(3) having premises and a sanitary environment commensurate with the source plasma to be collected;  
(4) having an identification system for identifying plasma donors;  
(5) having plasma collection machines and other facilities commensurate with the source plasma to be collected; and  
(6) having technical personnel capable of conducting quality inspection of the collected source plasma as well as necessary instruments and equipment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请设置单采血浆站的，由县级人民政府卫生行政部门初审，经设区的市、自治州人民政府卫生行政部门或者省、自治区人民政府设立的派出机关的卫生行政机构审查同意，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门审批；经审查符合条件的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门核发《单采血浆许可证》，并报国务院卫生行政部门备案。  
单采血浆站只能对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门划定区域内的供血浆者进行筛查和采集血浆。

**Article 7** An applicant for the establishment of a plasma collection station shall undergo a preliminary examination by the health administrative department of the county people's government, be examined and approved by the health administrative department of the people's government of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture, or by the health administrative organ of the agency established by the people's government of a province or an autonomous region, and then be submitted to the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval. Upon examination, if the conditions are met, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue the Plasma Collection Station Permit and report it to the health administrative department under the State Council for recordation.  
A plasma collection station may only screen and collect plasma from plasma donors within the area designated by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　《单采血浆许可证》应当规定有效期。

**Article 8** The Plasma Collection Station Permit shall specify its term of validity.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在一个采血浆区域内，只能设置一个单采血浆站。  
严禁单采血浆站采集非划定区域内的供血浆者和其他人员的血浆。

**Article 9** Only one plasma collection station may be established within a plasma collection area.  
It is strictly prohibited for a plasma collection station to collect plasma from plasma donors outside its designated area or from other persons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　单采血浆站必须对供血浆者进行健康检查；检查合格的，由县级人民政府卫生行政部门核发《供血浆证》。  
供血浆者健康检查标准，由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 10** A plasma collection station must conduct health examinations on plasma donors. For those who pass the examination, the health administrative department of the county people's government shall issue a Plasma Donor Certificate.  
The health examination standards for plasma donors shall be formulated by the health administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　《供血浆证》由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门负责设计和印制。《供血浆证》不得涂改、伪造、转让。

**Article 11** The Plasma Donor Certificate shall be designed and printed under the responsibility of the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The Plasma Donor Certificate shall not be altered, forged, or transferred.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　单采血浆站在采集血浆前，必须对供血浆者进行身份识别并核实其《供血浆证》，确认无误的，方可按照规定程序进行健康检查和血液化验；对检查、化验合格的，按照有关技术操作标准及程序采集血浆，并建立供血浆者健康检查及供血浆记录档案；对检查、化验不合格的，由单采血浆站收缴《供血浆证》，并由所在地县级人民政府卫生行政部门监督销毁。  
严禁采集无《供血浆证》者的血浆。  
血浆采集技术操作标准及程序，由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 12** Before collecting plasma, a plasma collection station must identify the plasma donor and verify his or her Plasma Donor Certificate. Only after confirmation may the station conduct health examinations and blood tests in accordance with the prescribed procedures. For those who pass the examinations and tests, the station shall collect plasma in accordance with the relevant technical operation standards and procedures, and shall establish a record file for the plasma donor's health examinations and plasma donation. For those who fail the examinations or tests, the plasma collection station shall confiscate the Plasma Donor Certificate and have it destroyed under the supervision of the health administrative department of the local county people's government.  
It is strictly prohibited to collect plasma from persons without a Plasma Donor Certificate.  
The technical operation standards and procedures for plasma collection shall be formulated by the health administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　单采血浆站只能向一个与其签订质量责任书的血液制品生产单位供应原料血浆，严禁向其他任何单位供应原料血浆。

**Article 13** A plasma collection station may only supply source plasma to one blood product production entity that has signed a quality responsibility letter with it. It is strictly prohibited to supply source plasma to any other unit.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　单采血浆站必须使用单采血浆机械采集血浆，严禁手工操作采集血浆。采集的血浆必须按单人份冰冻保存，不得混浆。  
严禁单采血浆站采集血液或者将所采集的原料血浆用于临床。

**Article 14** A plasma collection station must use plasma collection machines to collect plasma. Manual collection of plasma is strictly prohibited. The collected plasma must be frozen and stored in individual portions and shall not be mixed.  
It is strictly prohibited for a plasma collection station to collect blood or to use the collected source plasma for clinical purposes.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　单采血浆站必须使用有产品批准文号并经国家药品生物制品检定机构逐批检定合格的体外诊断试剂以及合格的一次性采血浆器材。  
采血浆器材等一次性消耗品使用后，必须按照国家有关规定予以销毁，并作记录。

**Article 15** A plasma collection station must use in vitro diagnostic reagents that have a product approval number and have passed batch-by-batch inspection by the national drug and biological product testing institution, as well as qualified disposable plasma collection equipment.  
After use, disposable consumables such as plasma collection equipment must be destroyed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and records shall be made thereof.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　单采血浆站采集的原料血浆的包装、储存、运输，必须符合国家规定的卫生标准和要求。

**Article 16** The packaging, storage, and transportation of source plasma collected by a plasma collection station must comply with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　单采血浆站必须依照传染病防治法及其实施办法等有关规定，严格执行消毒管理及疫情上报制度。

**Article 17** A plasma collection station must, in accordance with the Law on the Prevention and Control of Infectious Diseases and its implementing measures and other relevant provisions, strictly implement the disinfection management system and the epidemic reporting system.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　单采血浆站应当每半年向所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政部门报告有关原料血浆采集情况，同时抄报设区的市、自治州人民政府卫生行政部门或者省、自治区人民政府设立的派出机关的卫生行政机构及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门应当每年向国务院卫生行政部门汇总报告本行政区域内原料血浆的采集情况。

**Article 18** A plasma collection station shall report the relevant information on source plasma collection to the health administrative department of the local county people's government every six months, with copies to the health administrative department of the people's government of the city divided into districts or autonomous prefecture, or the health administrative organ of the agency established by the people's government of the province or autonomous region, and the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall submit a summary report on the collection of source plasma within its administrative region to the health administrative department under the State Council each year.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家禁止出口原料血浆。

**Article 19** The State prohibits the export of source plasma.

### 第三章　血液制品生产经营单位管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　新建、改建或者扩建血液制品生产单位，经国务院卫生行政部门根据总体规划进行立项审查同意后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门依照药品管理法的规定审核批准。

**Article 20** For the construction, reconstruction, or expansion of a blood product production entity, after the project is examined and approved by the health administrative department under the State Council in accordance with the overall plan, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall examine and approve it in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　血液制品生产单位必须达到国务院卫生行政部门制定的《药品生产质量管理规范》规定的标准，经国务院卫生行政部门审查合格，并依法向工商行政管理部门申领营业执照后，方可从事血液制品的生产活动。

**Article 21** A blood product production entity must meet the standards prescribed in the Good Manufacturing Practice for Pharmaceutical Products formulated by the health administrative department under the State Council, pass the examination by the health administrative department under the State Council, and apply for and obtain a business license from the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law before it may engage in the production of blood products.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　血液制品生产单位应当积极开发新品种，提高血浆综合利用率。  
血液制品生产单位生产国内已经生产的品种，必须依法向国务院卫生行政部门申请产品批准文号；国内尚未生产的品种，必须按照国家有关新药审批的程序和要求申报。

**Article 22** A blood product production entity shall actively develop new varieties and improve the comprehensive utilization rate of plasma.  
If a blood product production entity produces a variety that has already been produced domestically, it must apply to the health administrative department under the State Council for a product approval number according to law. For a variety not yet produced domestically, it must file an application in accordance with the procedures and requirements of the State concerning the approval of new drugs.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　严禁血液制品生产单位出让、出租、出借以及与他人共用《药品生产企业许可证》和产品批准文号。

**Article 23** It is strictly prohibited for a blood product production entity to assign, lease, lend, or share with others the Pharmaceutical Production Enterprise License and the product approval number.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　血液制品生产单位不得向无《单采血浆许可证》的单采血浆站或者未与其签订质量责任书的单采血浆站及其他任何单位收集原料血浆。  
血液制品生产单位不得向其他任何单位供应原料血浆。

**Article 24** A blood product production entity may not collect source plasma from a plasma collection station that does not have a Plasma Collection Station Permit, or from a plasma collection station that has not signed a quality responsibility letter with it, or from any other unit.  
A blood product production entity may not supply source plasma to any other unit.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　血液制品生产单位在原料血浆投料生产前，必须使用有产品批准文号并经国家药品生物制品检定机构逐批检定合格的体外诊断试剂，对每一人份血浆进行全面复检，并作检测记录。  
原料血浆经复检不合格的，不得投料生产，并必须在省级药品监督员监督下按照规定程序和方法予以销毁，并作记录。  
原料血浆经复检发现有经血液途径传播的疾病的，必须通知供应血浆的单采血浆站，并及时上报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门。

**Article 25** Before putting source plasma into production, a blood product production entity must use in vitro diagnostic reagents that have a product approval number and have passed batch-by-batch inspection by the national drug and biological product testing institution to conduct a comprehensive retest on each individual portion of plasma, and shall make test records.  
If the source plasma fails the retest, it shall not be put into production and must be destroyed in accordance with the prescribed procedures and methods under the supervision of a provincial drug inspector, and records shall be made thereof.  
If a disease transmitted through blood is detected in the source plasma upon retest, the plasma collection station that supplied the plasma must be notified, and a report shall be promptly submitted to the health administrative department of the local people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　血液制品出厂前，必须经过质量检验；经检验不符合国家标准的，严禁出厂。

**Article 26** Before leaving the factory, blood products must undergo quality inspection. Those that fail to meet the national standards upon inspection are strictly prohibited from leaving the factory.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　开办血液制品经营单位，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门审核批准。

**Article 27** The establishment of a blood product business operation entity shall be examined and approved by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　血液制品经营单位应当具备与所经营的产品相适应的冷藏条件和熟悉所经营品种的业务人员。

**Article 28** A blood product business operation entity shall have refrigeration conditions commensurate with the products it deals in and have business personnel familiar with the varieties it deals in.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　血液制品生产经营单位生产、包装、储存、运输、经营血液制品，应当符合国家规定的卫生标准和要求。

**Article 29** Blood product production and business operation entities shall, in producing, packaging, storing, transporting, and dealing in blood products, comply with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the State.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方各级人民政府卫生行政部门依照本条例的规定负责本行政区域内的单采血浆站、供血浆者、原料血浆的采集及血液制品经营单位的监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门依照本条例的规定负责本行政区域内的血液制品生产单位的监督管理。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府卫生行政部门的监督人员执行职务时，可以按照国家有关规定抽取样品和索取有关资料，有关单位不得拒绝和隐瞒。

**Article 30** The health administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the supervision and administration of plasma collection stations, plasma donors, the collection of source plasma, and blood product business operation entities within their respective administrative regions.  
The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the supervision and administration of blood product production entities within their respective administrative regions.  
When supervisory personnel of the health administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level perform their duties, they may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, take samples and request relevant materials, and the units concerned may not refuse or conceal them.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门每年组织1次对本行政区域内单采血浆站的监督检查并进行年度注册。  
设区的市、自治州人民政府卫生行政部门或者省、自治区人民政府设立的派出机关的卫生行政机构每半年对本行政区域内的单采血浆站进行1次检查。

**Article 31** The health administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize one supervision and inspection of the plasma collection stations within their respective administrative regions each year and conduct annual registration.  
The health administrative departments of the people's governments of cities divided into districts or autonomous prefectures, or the health administrative organs of the agencies established by the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions, shall conduct one inspection of the plasma collection stations within their respective administrative regions every six months.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家药品生物制品检定机构及国务院卫生行政部门指定的省级药品检验机构，应当依照本条例和国家规定的标准和要求，对血液制品生产单位生产的产品定期进行检定。

**Article 32** The national drug and biological product testing institution and the provincial drug testing institutions designated by the health administrative department under the State Council shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the standards and requirements prescribed by the State, conduct periodic testing of the products produced by blood product production entities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院卫生行政部门负责全国进出口血液制品的审批及监督管理。

**Article 33** The health administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the examination, approval, and supervision and administration of the import and export of blood products nationwide.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，未取得省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门核发的《单采血浆许可证》，非法从事组织、采集、供应、倒卖原料血浆活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门予以取缔，没收违法所得和从事违法活动的器材、设备，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成经血液途径传播的疾病传播、人身伤害等危害，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 34** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, engages in the illegal organization, collection, supply, or resale of source plasma without obtaining the Plasma Collection Station Permit issued by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be banned by the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level. The illegal gains and the equipment and devices used for the illegal activities shall be confiscated. In addition, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the act causes the spread of diseases transmitted through blood, personal injury, or other harm, and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　单采血浆站有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门责令限期改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有第八项所列行为的，或者有下列其他行为并且情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门吊销《单采血浆许可证》；构成犯罪的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)采集血浆前，未按照国务院卫生行政部门颁布的健康检查标准对供血浆者进行健康检查和血液化验的；  
(二)采集非划定区域内的供血浆者或者其他人员的血浆的，或者不对供血浆者进行身份识别，采集冒名顶替者、健康检查不合格者或者无《供血浆证》者的血浆的；  
(三)违反国务院卫生行政部门制定的血浆采集技术操作标准和程序，过频过量采集血浆的；  
(四)向医疗机构直接供应原料血浆或者擅自采集血液的；  
(五)未使用单采血浆机械进行血浆采集的；  
(六)未使用有产品批准文号并经国家药品生物制品检定机构逐批检定合格的体外诊断试剂以及合格的一次性采血浆器材的；  
(七)未按照国家规定的卫生标准和要求包装、储存、运输原料血浆的；  
(八) 对国家规定检测项目检测结果呈阳性的血浆不清除、不及时上报的；  
(九) 对污染的注射器、采血浆器材及不合格血浆等不经消毒处理，擅自倾倒，污染环境，造成社会危害的；  
(十) 重复使用一次性采血浆器材的；  
(十一) 向与其签订质量责任书的血液制品生产单位以外的其他单位供应原料血浆的。

**Article 35** If a plasma collection station commits any of the following acts, the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan. If the act falls under item (8), or if it commits any other act listed below under aggravating circumstances, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke the Plasma Collection Station Permit. If a crime is constituted, the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) failing to conduct health examinations and blood tests on plasma donors in accordance with the health examination standards promulgated by the health administrative department under the State Council before collecting plasma;  
(2) collecting plasma from plasma donors outside the designated area or from other persons, or failing to identify plasma donors and collecting plasma from impostors, persons who fail health examinations, or persons without a Plasma Donor Certificate;  
(3) conducting plasma collection excessively in frequency or volume in violation of the plasma collection technical operation standards and procedures formulated by the health administrative department under the State Council;  
(4) directly supplying source plasma to medical institutions or collecting blood without authorization;  
(5) failing to use plasma collection machines for plasma collection;  
(6) failing to use in vitro diagnostic reagents that have a product approval number and have passed batch-by-batch inspection by the national drug and biological product testing institution, or failing to use qualified disposable plasma collection equipment;  
(7) failing to package, store, or transport source plasma in accordance with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the State;  
(8) failing to remove or promptly report plasma that tests positive for the items required to be tested under the provisions of the State;  
(9) dumping, without disinfection treatment, contaminated syringes, plasma collection equipment, or unqualified plasma without authorization, thereby polluting the environment and causing social harm;  
(10) reusing disposable plasma collection equipment; or  
(11) supplying source plasma to a unit other than the blood product production entity with which it has signed a quality responsibility letter.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　单采血浆站已知其采集的血浆检测结果呈阳性，仍向血液制品生产单位供应的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门吊销《单采血浆许可证》，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门没收违法所得，并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；造成经血液途径传播的疾病传播、人身伤害等危害，构成犯罪的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** If a plasma collection station, knowing that the test result of the plasma it has collected is positive, still supplies such plasma to a blood product production entity, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke the Plasma Collection Station Permit; the health administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan. If the act causes the spread of diseases transmitted through blood, personal injury, or other harm, and constitutes a crime, the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　涂改、伪造、转让《供血浆证》的，由县级人民政府卫生行政部门收缴《供血浆证》，没收违法所得，并处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，并处1万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Whoever alters, forges, or transfers a Plasma Donor Certificate shall have the certificate confiscated by the health administrative department of the county people's government. The illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　血液制品生产单位有下列行为之一的，由省级以上人民政府卫生行政部门依照药品管理法及其实施办法等有关规定，按照生产假药、劣药予以处罚；构成犯罪的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)使用无《单采血浆许可证》的单采血浆站或者未与其签订质量责任书的单采血浆站及其他任何单位供应的原料血浆的，或者非法采集原料血浆的；  
(二)投料生产前未对原料血浆进行复检的，或者使用没有产品批准文号或者未经国家药品生物制品检定机构逐批检定合格的体外诊断试剂进行复检的，或者将检测不合格的原料血浆投入生产的；  
(三)擅自更改生产工艺和质量标准的，或者将检验不合格的产品出厂的；  
(四)与他人共用产品批准文号的。

**Article 38** If a blood product production entity commits any of the following acts, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and its implementing measures and other relevant provisions, punish it as producing counterfeit or substandard drugs. If a crime is constituted, the person in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) using source plasma supplied by a plasma collection station without a Plasma Collection Station Permit, or by a plasma collection station that has not signed a quality responsibility letter with it, or by any other unit, or illegally collecting source plasma;  
(2) failing to retest the source plasma before putting it into production, or using in vitro diagnostic reagents without a product approval number or without having passed batch-by-batch inspection by the national drug and biological product testing institution for the retest, or putting source plasma that fails the test into production;  
(3) altering the production process or quality standards without authorization, or releasing products that fail the inspection from the factory; or  
(4) sharing product approval numbers with others.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　血液制品生产单位违反本条例规定，擅自向其他单位出让、出租、出借以及与他人共用《药品生产企业许可证》、产品批准文号或者供应原料血浆的，由省级以上人民政府卫生行政部门没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** If a blood product production entity, in violation of these Regulations, assigns, leases, lends, or shares the Pharmaceutical Production Enterprise License or product approval number with other units, or supplies source plasma to other units without authorization, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains. If there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，血液制品生产经营单位生产、包装、储存、运输、经营血液制品不符合国家规定的卫生标准和要求的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** If, in violation of these Regulations, a blood product production or business operation entity fails to comply with the hygiene standards and requirements prescribed by the State in producing, packaging, storing, transporting, or dealing in blood products, the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在血液制品生产单位成品库待出厂的产品中，经抽检有一批次达不到国家规定的指标，经复检仍不合格的，由国务院卫生行政部门撤销该血液制品批准文号。

**Article 41** If, upon spot inspection of the products awaiting delivery from the finished product warehouse of a blood product production entity, one batch fails to meet the targets prescribed by the State and still fails after retest, the health administrative department under the State Council shall revoke the approval number for that blood product.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，擅自进出口血液制品或者出口原料血浆的，由省级以上人民政府卫生行政部门没收所进出口的血液制品或者所出口的原料血浆和违法所得，并处所进出口的血液制品或者所出口的原料血浆总值3倍以上5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Whoever, in violation of these Regulations, imports or exports blood products or exports source plasma without authorization shall have the imported or exported blood products or the exported source plasma and the illegal gains confiscated by the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the total value of the imported or exported blood products or the exported source plasma.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　血液制品检验人员虚报、瞒报、涂改、伪造检验报告及有关资料的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** If a blood product inspection personnel makes false reports, conceals, alters, or forges inspection reports or relevant materials, he or she shall be given a disciplinary sanction according to law. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　卫生行政部门工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、索贿受贿，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 44** If a staff member of the health administrative department abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or solicits or accepts bribes, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. If the act does not yet constitute a crime, a disciplinary sanction shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
血液制品，是特指各种人血浆蛋白制品。  
原料血浆，是指由单采血浆站采集的专用于血液制品生产原料的血浆。  
供血浆者，是指提供血液制品生产用原料血浆的人员。  
单采血浆站，是指根据地区血源资源，按照有关标准和要求并经严格审批设立，采集供应血液制品生产用原料血浆的单位。

**Article 45** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
"Blood products" means specifically various human plasma protein products.  
"Source plasma" means the plasma collected by a plasma collection station exclusively for use as raw material in the production of blood products.  
"Plasma donor" means a person who provides source plasma for the production of blood products.  
"Plasma collection station" means a unit established after strict examination and approval in accordance with the relevant standards and requirements based on regional blood resources, which collects and supplies source plasma for the production of blood products.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例施行前已经设立的单采血浆站和血液制品生产经营单位应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内，依照本条例的规定重新办理审批手续；凡不符合本条例规定的，一律予以关闭。  
本条例施行前已经设立的单采血浆站适用[本条例第六条](#art-6)第五项的时间，由国务院卫生行政部门另行规定。

**Article 46** Plasma collection stations and blood product production and business operation entities that were established before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within six months from the date on which these Regulations come into force, undergo the re-examination and approval procedures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations. Any that do not comply with the provisions of these Regulations shall be closed down.  
The time for applying item (5) of Article 6 of these Regulations to plasma collection stations established before the coming into force of these Regulations shall be separately prescribed by the health administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
