# 人体器官捐献和移植条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-04  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> 第十三条　国家鼓励遗体器官捐献。公民可以通过中国红十字会总会建立的登记服务系统表示捐献其遗体器官的意愿。 第十四条　红十字会向遗体器官捐献人亲属颁发捐献证书，动员社会各方力量设置遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念设施。设置遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念设施应当因地制宜、注重实效。 中国红十字会总会、国务院卫生健康部门应当定期组织开展遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念活动。 ​ 第三章　人体器官的获取和移植 第十五条　医疗机构从事遗体器官获取，应当具备下列条件： （一）有专门负责遗体器官获取的部门以及与从事遗体器官获取相适应的管理人员、执业医师和其他医务人员； （二）有满足遗体器官获取所需要的设备、设施和技术能力； （三）有符合本条例第十八条第一款规定的人体器官移植伦理委员会； （四）有完善的遗体器官获取质量管理和控制等制度。 从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构同时从事人体器官移植的，负责遗体器官获取的部门应当独立于负责人体器官移植的科室。 第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门根据本行政区域遗体器官捐献情况，制定遗体器官获取服务规划，并结合医疗机构的条件和服务能力，确定本行政区域从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构，划定其提供遗体器官获取服务的区域。 从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构应当在所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门划定的区域内提供遗体器官获取服务。 医疗机构发现符合捐献条件且有捐献意愿的潜在遗体器官捐献人的，应当向负责提供其所在区域遗体器官获取服务的医疗机构报告，接到报告的医疗机构应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市红十字会通报。 任何组织或者个人不得以获取遗体器官为目的跨区域转运潜在遗体器官捐献人，不得向本条第三款规定之外的组织或者个人转介潜在遗体器官捐献人的相关信息。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　红十字会向遗体器官捐献人亲属颁发捐献证书，动员社会各方力量设置遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念设施。设置遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念设施应当因地制宜、注重实效。  
中国红十字会总会、国务院卫生健康部门应当定期组织开展遗体器官捐献人缅怀纪念活动。  
​

**Article 14** The Red Cross Society shall issue donation certificates to the relatives of cadaveric organ donors, and mobilize social forces to establish remembrance facilities for cadaveric organ donors. The establishment of remembrance facilities for cadaveric organ donors shall be adapted to local conditions and focus on practical results.  
The Red Cross Society of China and the health department of the State Council shall regularly organize remembrance activities for cadaveric organ donors.

### 第三章　人体器官的获取和移植

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　医疗机构从事遗体器官获取，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有专门负责遗体器官获取的部门以及与从事遗体器官获取相适应的管理人员、执业医师和其他医务人员；  
（二）有满足遗体器官获取所需要的设备、设施和技术能力；  
（三）有符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)第一款规定的人体器官移植伦理委员会；  
（四）有完善的遗体器官获取质量管理和控制等制度。  
从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构同时从事人体器官移植的，负责遗体器官获取的部门应当独立于负责人体器官移植的科室。

**Article 15** A medical institution that engages in the procurement of cadaveric organs shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having a department specifically responsible for cadaveric organ procurement, along with management personnel, licensed physicians, and other medical personnel appropriate for the procurement of cadaveric organs;  
(2) Having the equipment, facilities, and technical capacity necessary for the procurement of cadaveric organs;  
(3) Having a human organ transplantation ethics committee that complies with the provisions of Article 18, paragraph 1 of these Regulations;  
(4) Having comprehensive systems for quality management and control of cadaveric organ procurement.  
If a medical institution engaged in the procurement of cadaveric organs also engages in human organ transplantation, the department responsible for cadaveric organ procurement shall be independent from the department responsible for human organ transplantation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门根据本行政区域遗体器官捐献情况，制定遗体器官获取服务规划，并结合医疗机构的条件和服务能力，确定本行政区域从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构，划定其提供遗体器官获取服务的区域。  
从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构应当在所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门划定的区域内提供遗体器官获取服务。  
医疗机构发现符合捐献条件且有捐献意愿的潜在遗体器官捐献人的，应当向负责提供其所在区域遗体器官获取服务的医疗机构报告，接到报告的医疗机构应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市红十字会通报。  
任何组织或者个人不得以获取遗体器官为目的跨区域转运潜在遗体器官捐献人，不得向本条第三款规定之外的组织或者个人转介潜在遗体器官捐献人的相关信息。

**Article 16** The health department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on the cadaveric organ donation situation in its administrative region, formulate a service plan for cadaveric organ procurement, and in consideration of the conditions and service capabilities of medical institutions, designate medical institutions within its administrative region that shall engage in cadaveric organ procurement, and delineate the areas in which they shall provide cadaveric organ procurement services.  
A medical institution engaged in the procurement of cadaveric organs shall provide cadaveric organ procurement services within the area delineated by the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
If a medical institution identifies a potential cadaveric organ donor who meets the donation criteria and is willing to donate, it shall report to the medical institution responsible for providing cadaveric organ procurement services in its region, and the medical institution that receives the report shall notify the Red Cross Society of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
No organization or individual may transfer a potential cadaveric organ donor across regions for the purpose of procuring cadaveric organs, or refer information about a potential cadaveric organ donor to any organization or individual other than those specified in paragraph 3 of this Article.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　获取遗体器官前，负责遗体器官获取的部门应当向其所在医疗机构的人体器官移植伦理委员会提出获取遗体器官审查申请。

**Article 17** Before procuring cadaveric organs, the department responsible for cadaveric organ procurement shall submit an application for review of cadaveric organ procurement to the human organ transplantation ethics committee of its affiliated medical institution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人体器官移植伦理委员会由医学、法学、伦理学等方面专家组成，委员会中从事人体器官移植的医学专家不超过委员人数的四分之一。人体器官移植伦理委员会的组成和工作规则，由国务院卫生健康部门制定。  
人体器官移植伦理委员会收到获取遗体器官审查申请后，应当及时对下列事项进行审查：  
（一）遗体器官捐献意愿是否真实；  
（二）有无买卖或者变相买卖遗体器官的情形。  
经三分之二以上委员同意，人体器官移植伦理委员会方可出具同意获取遗体器官的书面意见。人体器官移植伦理委员会同意获取的，医疗机构方可获取遗体器官。

**Article 18** The human organ transplantation ethics committee shall be composed of experts in medicine, law, ethics, and other fields. Medical experts engaged in human organ transplantation in the committee shall not exceed one-fourth of the committee members. The composition and working rules of the human organ transplantation ethics committee shall be formulated by the health department of the State Council.  
After receiving an application for review of cadaveric organ procurement, the human organ transplantation ethics committee shall promptly review the following matters:  
(1) Whether the wish to donate cadaveric organs is genuine;  
(2) Whether there are any circumstances of trading or trading in disguised form of cadaveric organs.  
Only after obtaining consent from at least two-thirds of the committee members may the human organ transplantation ethics committee issue a written opinion agreeing to the procurement of cadaveric organs. If the human organ transplantation ethics committee agrees to the procurement, the medical institution may then procure the cadaveric organs.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　获取遗体器官，应当在依法判定遗体器官捐献人死亡后进行。从事人体器官获取、移植的医务人员不得参与遗体器官捐献人的死亡判定。  
获取遗体器官，应当经人体器官捐献协调员见证。获取遗体器官前，从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构应当通知所在地省、自治区、直辖市红十字会。接到通知的红十字会应当及时指派2名以上人体器官捐献协调员对遗体器官获取进行见证。  
从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构及其医务人员应当维护遗体器官捐献人的尊严；获取器官后，应当对遗体进行符合伦理原则的医学处理，除用于移植的器官以外，应当恢复遗体外观。

**Article 19** Procurement of cadaveric organs shall be carried out after death has been determined in accordance with law. Medical personnel engaged in organ procurement and transplantation shall not participate in the determination of death of the cadaveric organ donor.  
Procurement of cadaveric organs shall be witnessed by a human organ donation coordinator. Prior to the procurement of cadaveric organs, the medical institution engaged in the procurement of cadaveric organs shall notify the Red Cross Society of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The Red Cross Society that receives the notification shall promptly assign at least two human organ donation coordinators to witness the procurement of cadaveric organs.  
A medical institution engaged in the procurement of cadaveric organs and its medical personnel shall maintain the dignity of the cadaveric organ donor; after procuring the organs, they shall carry out ethically compliant medical treatment of the cadaver, and except for the organs used for transplantation, they shall restore the appearance of the cadaver.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　遗体器官的分配，应当符合医疗需要，遵循公平、公正和公开的原则。具体办法由国务院卫生健康部门制定。  
患者申请人体器官移植手术，其配偶、直系血亲或者三代以内旁系血亲曾经捐献遗体器官的，在同等条件下优先排序。

**Article 20** The allocation of cadaveric organs shall meet medical needs and follow the principles of fairness, justice, and openness. The specific measures shall be formulated by the health department of the State Council.  
When a patient applies for human organ transplant surgery, if his or her spouse, direct blood relatives, or collateral blood relatives within three generations have previously donated cadaveric organs, the patient shall be given priority in the ranking under equal conditions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　遗体器官应当通过国务院卫生健康部门建立的分配系统统一分配。从事遗体器官获取、移植的医疗机构应当在分配系统中如实录入遗体器官捐献人、申请人体器官移植手术患者的相关医学数据并及时更新，不得伪造、篡改数据。  
医疗机构及其医务人员应当执行分配系统分配结果。禁止医疗机构及其医务人员使用未经分配系统分配的遗体器官或者来源不明的人体器官实施人体器官移植。  
国务院卫生健康部门应当定期公布遗体器官捐献和分配情况。

**Article 21** Deceased human organs shall be uniformly allocated through the allocation system established by the health department of the State Council. Medical institutions engaged in the procurement and transplantation of deceased human organs shall accurately enter the relevant medical data of the deceased organ donor and the patient applying for organ transplantation surgery into the allocation system and update it in a timely manner, and shall not forge or tamper with the data.  
Medical institutions and their medical personnel shall implement the allocation results of the allocation system. Medical institutions and their medical personnel are prohibited from using deceased human organs that have not been allocated through the allocation system or human organs of unknown origin to perform human organ transplantation.  
The health department of the State Council shall regularly publish the donation and allocation status of deceased human organs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院卫生健康部门会同国务院公安、交通运输、铁路、民用航空等部门和中国红十字会总会建立遗体器官运送绿色通道工作机制，确保高效、畅通运送遗体器官。

**Article 22** The health department of the State Council shall, together with the public security, transport, railway, civil aviation and other departments of the State Council and the Red Cross Society of China, establish a green channel working mechanism for the transport of deceased human organs to ensure the efficient and smooth transport of deceased human organs.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　医疗机构从事人体器官移植，应当向国务院卫生健康部门提出申请。国务院卫生健康部门应当自受理申请之日起5个工作日内组织专家评审，于专家评审完成后15个工作日内作出决定并书面告知申请人。国务院卫生健康部门审查同意的，通知申请人所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门办理人体器官移植诊疗科目登记，在申请人的执业许可证上注明获准从事的人体器官移植诊疗科目。具体办法由国务院卫生健康部门制定。  
医疗机构从事人体器官移植，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与从事人体器官移植相适应的管理人员、执业医师和其他医务人员；  
（二）有满足人体器官移植所需要的设备、设施和技术能力；  
（三）有符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)第一款规定的人体器官移植伦理委员会；  
（四）有完善的人体器官移植质量管理和控制等制度。

**Article 23** A medical institution that engages in human organ transplantation shall submit an application to the health department of the State Council. The health department of the State Council shall, within 5 working days from the date of accepting the application, organize expert review, and within 15 working days after the completion of the expert review, make a decision and notify the applicant in writing. If the health department of the State Council approves after review, it shall notify the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located to handle the registration of the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation, and indicate the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation that is approved to be engaged in on the applicant's practice license. The specific measures shall be formulated by the health department of the State Council.  
A medical institution that engages in human organ transplantation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having managerial personnel, practicing physicians, and other medical personnel suitable for engaging in human organ transplantation;  
(2) having the equipment, facilities, and technical capabilities required for human organ transplantation;  
(3) having an ethics committee for human organ transplantation that complies with the provisions of Article 18, paragraph 1 of these Regulations;  
(4) having a comprehensive system for quality management and control of human organ transplantation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院卫生健康部门审查医疗机构的申请，除依据[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第二款规定的条件外，还应当考虑申请人所在省、自治区、直辖市人体器官移植的医疗需求、现有服务能力和人体器官捐献情况。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门应当及时公布已经办理人体器官移植诊疗科目登记的医疗机构名单。

**Article 24** When reviewing an application from a medical institution, the health department of the State Council shall, in addition to the conditions specified in Article 23, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, also consider the medical demand for human organ transplantation, the existing service capacity, and the status of human organ donation in the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located.  
The health department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall promptly publish the list of medical institutions that have completed registration for the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　已经办理人体器官移植诊疗科目登记的医疗机构不再具备[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第二款规定条件的，应当停止从事人体器官移植，并向原登记部门报告。原登记部门应当自收到报告之日起2个工作日内注销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目登记，向国务院卫生健康部门报告，并予以公布。

**Article 25** If a medical institution that has completed registration for the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation no longer meets the conditions specified in Article 23, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, it shall cease engaging in human organ transplantation and report to the original registration department. The original registration department shall, within 2 working days from the date of receiving the report, cancel the registration of the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation of that medical institution, report to the health department of the State Council, and make it public.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　省级以上人民政府卫生健康部门应当建立人体器官移植质量管理和控制制度，定期对医疗机构的人体器官移植技术临床应用能力进行评估，并及时公布评估结果；对评估不合格的，国务院卫生健康部门通知原登记部门注销其人体器官移植诊疗科目登记。具体办法由国务院卫生健康部门制定。

**Article 26** The health department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall establish a quality management and control system for human organ transplantation, regularly evaluate the clinical application capability of human organ transplantation technology of medical institutions, and promptly publish the evaluation results; for those that fail the evaluation, the health department of the State Council shall notify the original registration department to cancel their registration of the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation. The specific measures shall be formulated by the health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　实施人体器官移植手术的执业医师应当具备下列条件，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门认定，并在执业证书上注明：  
（一）有与实施人体器官移植手术相适应的专业技术职务任职资格；  
（二）有与实施人体器官移植手术相适应的临床工作经验；  
（三）经培训并考核合格。

**Article 27** A practicing physician who performs human organ transplantation surgery shall meet the following conditions, and shall be recognized by the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, with the notation on the practice certificate:  
(1) having the professional technical post qualification suitable for performing human organ transplantation surgery;  
(2) having clinical work experience suitable for performing human organ transplantation surgery;  
(3) having undergone training and passed the assessment.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　移植活体器官的，由从事人体器官移植的医疗机构获取活体器官。获取活体器官前，负责人体器官移植的科室应当向其所在医疗机构的人体器官移植伦理委员会提出获取活体器官审查申请。  
人体器官移植伦理委员会收到获取活体器官审查申请后，应当及时对下列事项进行审查：  
（一）活体器官捐献意愿是否真实；  
（二）有无买卖或者变相买卖活体器官的情形；  
（三）活体器官捐献人与接受人是否存在本条例第十一条规定的关系；  
（四）活体器官的配型和接受人的适应证是否符合伦理原则和人体器官移植技术临床应用管理规范。  
经三分之二以上委员同意，人体器官移植伦理委员会方可出具同意获取活体器官的书面意见。人体器官移植伦理委员会同意获取的，医疗机构方可获取活体器官。

**Article 28** For transplantation of living human organs, the medical institution engaged in human organ transplantation shall procure the living organ. Before procuring a living organ, the department responsible for human organ transplantation shall submit an application for review of the procurement of the living organ to the ethics committee for human organ transplantation of its medical institution.  
After receiving the application for review of procurement of a living organ, the ethics committee for human organ transplantation shall promptly review the following matters:  
(1) whether the intention to donate the living organ is genuine;  
(2) whether there is any circumstance of sale or disguised sale of a living organ;  
(3) whether the living organ donor and the recipient have the relationship specified in Article 11 of these Regulations;  
(4) whether the matching of the living organ and the indications of the recipient comply with ethical principles and the clinical application management norms for human organ transplantation technology.  
Only with the consent of more than two-thirds of the members may the ethics committee for human organ transplantation issue a written opinion agreeing to the procurement of the living organ. Only if the ethics committee for human organ transplantation agrees to the procurement may the medical institution procure the living organ.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　从事人体器官移植的医疗机构及其医务人员获取活体器官前，应当履行下列义务：  
（一）向活体器官捐献人说明器官获取手术的风险、术后注意事项、可能发生的并发症及其预防措施等，并与活体器官捐献人签署知情同意书；  
（二）查验活体器官捐献人同意捐献其器官的书面意愿、活体器官捐献人与接受人存在本条例第十一条规定关系的证明材料；  
（三）确认除获取器官产生的直接后果外不会损害活体器官捐献人其他正常的生理功能。  
从事人体器官移植的医疗机构应当保存活体器官捐献人的医学资料，并进行随访。

**Article 29** Before procuring a living organ, a medical institution engaged in human organ transplantation and its medical personnel shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) explaining to the living organ donor the risks of the organ procurement surgery, postoperative precautions, possible complications and their preventive measures, etc., and signing an informed consent form with the living organ donor;  
(2) verifying the written intention of the living organ donor to consent to donate his/her organ, and the certification materials proving that the living organ donor and the recipient have the relationship specified in Article 11 of these Regulations;  
(3) confirming that, except for the direct consequences of organ procurement, no other normal physiological functions of the living organ donor will be impaired.  
A medical institution engaged in human organ transplantation shall preserve the medical records of the living organ donor and conduct follow-up.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　医疗机构及其医务人员从事人体器官获取、移植，应当遵守伦理原则和相关技术临床应用管理规范。

**Article 30** Medical institutions and their medical personnel engaged in the procurement and transplantation of human organs shall comply with ethical principles and relevant technical clinical application management norms.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　医疗机构及其医务人员获取、移植人体器官，应当对人体器官捐献人和获取的人体器官进行医学检查，对接受人接受人体器官移植的风险进行评估，并采取措施降低风险。

**Article 31** When procuring and transplanting human organs, medical institutions and their medical personnel shall conduct medical examinations on the human organ donor and the procured human organs, assess the risks for the recipient to undergo human organ transplantation, and take measures to reduce the risks.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　从事人体器官移植的医疗机构实施人体器官移植手术，除向接受人收取下列费用外，不得收取或者变相收取所移植人体器官的费用：  
（一）获取活体器官、切除病损器官、植入人体器官所发生的手术费、检查费、检验费等医疗服务费以及药费、医用耗材费；  
（二）向从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构支付的遗体器官获取成本费用。  
遗体器官获取成本费用，包括为获取遗体器官而发生的评估、维护、获取、保存、修复和运送等成本。遗体器官获取成本费用的收费原则由国务院卫生健康部门会同国务院发展改革、财政、医疗保障等部门制定，具体收费标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门会同同级发展改革、财政、医疗保障等部门制定。  
从事遗体器官获取的医疗机构应当对遗体器官获取成本费用进行单独核算。

**Article 32** When performing human organ transplantation surgery, a medical institution engaged in human organ transplantation shall not charge or covertly charge fees for the transplanted human organs, except for charging the following fees from the recipient:  
(1) medical service fees such as surgery fees, examination fees, test fees, etc., incurred in procuring living organs, resecting diseased organs, and implanting human organs, as well as medicine fees and medical consumables fees;  
(2) cost fees for procurement of deceased human organs paid to medical institutions engaged in procurement of deceased human organs.  
Cost fees for procurement of deceased human organs include costs incurred for assessment, maintenance, procurement, preservation, repair, and transport, etc., for the purpose of procuring deceased human organs. The charging principles for cost fees for procurement of deceased human organs shall be formulated by the health department of the State Council together with the development and reform, finance, medical security and other departments of the State Council; specific charging standards shall be formulated by the health department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government together with the development and reform, finance, medical security and other departments at the same level.  
A medical institution engaged in procurement of deceased human organs shall conduct separate accounting for the cost fees for procurement of deceased human organs.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人体器官捐献协调员、医疗机构及其工作人员应当对人体器官捐献人、接受人和申请人体器官移植手术患者的个人信息依法予以保护。

**Article 33** Human organ donation coordinators, medical institutions and their staff shall protect the personal information of human organ donors, recipients, and patients applying for human organ transplantation surgery according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家建立人体器官获取、移植病例登记报告制度。从事人体器官获取、移植的医疗机构应当将实施人体器官获取、移植的情况向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门报告。

**Article 34** The State shall establish a registration and reporting system for cases of human organ procurement and transplantation. Medical institutions engaged in the procurement and transplantation of human organs shall report the circumstances of the human organ procurement and transplantation to the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家健全行政执法与刑事司法衔接机制，依法查处人体器官捐献和移植中的违法犯罪行为。

**Article 35** The State shall improve the connection mechanism between administrative law enforcement and criminal justice, and investigate and punish illegal and criminal acts in human organ donation and transplantation according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）组织他人出卖人体器官；  
（二）未经本人同意获取其活体器官，或者获取未满18周岁公民的活体器官，或者强迫、欺骗他人捐献活体器官；  
（三）违背本人生前意愿获取其遗体器官，或者本人生前未表示同意捐献其遗体器官，违反国家规定，违背其配偶、成年子女、父母意愿获取其遗体器官。  
医务人员有前款所列情形被依法追究刑事责任的，由原执业注册部门吊销其执业证书，终身禁止其从事医疗卫生服务。

**Article 36** Anyone who, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) organizing others to sell human organs;  
(2) procuring a living organ of another person without the person's consent, or procuring a living organ of a citizen under the age of 18, or coercing or deceiving another person to donate a living organ;  
(3) procuring a deceased organ of another person contrary to that person's living will, or where the person did not consent to the donation of the deceased organ during his/her lifetime, in violation of state regulations and contrary to the wishes of his/her spouse, adult children, or parents.  
If a medical personnel member is investigated for criminal liability according to law for any of the circumstances in the preceding paragraph, the original practice registration department shall revoke his/her practice certificate and permanently prohibit him/her from engaging in medical and health services.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，买卖人体器官或者从事与买卖人体器官有关活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门没收违法所得，并处交易额10倍以上20倍以下的罚款；医疗机构参与上述活动的，还应当由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其10年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分，情节严重的，由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证或者由原备案部门责令其停止执业活动；医务人员参与上述活动的，还应当由原执业注册部门吊销其执业证书，终身禁止其从事医疗卫生服务；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
公职人员参与买卖人体器官或者从事与买卖人体器官有关活动的，依法给予撤职、开除处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Anyone who, in violation of these Regulations, trades human organs or engages in activities related to the trading of human organs, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the transaction amount; if a medical institution participates in the above activities, the original registration department shall also revoke the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation of that medical institution, prohibit it from engaging in the procurement of human organs or applying for human organ transplantation for 10 years, and impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law, and if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall revoke the practice license of that medical institution or the original filing department shall order it to cease practice activities; if a medical personnel member participates in the above activities, the original practice registration department shall also revoke his/her practice certificate and permanently prohibit him/her from engaging in medical and health services; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a public official participates in the trading of human organs or engages in activities related to the trading of human organs, he/she shall be given sanctions of removal from office or expulsion according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　医疗机构未办理人体器官移植诊疗科目登记，擅自从事人体器官移植的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门没收违法所得，并处违法所得10倍以上20倍以下的罚款，禁止其5年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分，对有关医务人员责令暂停1年执业活动；情节严重的，还应当由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证或者由原备案部门责令其停止执业活动，并由原执业注册部门吊销有关医务人员的执业证书。  
医疗机构不再具备[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)第二款规定的条件，仍从事人体器官移植的，由原登记部门没收违法所得，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其3年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，还应当由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证，并对有关医务人员责令暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动。

**Article 38** If a medical institution, without having completed registration for the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation, engages in human organ transplantation without authorization, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the illegal gains, prohibit it from engaging in the procurement of human organs or applying for human organ transplantation for 5 years, impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law, and order the relevant medical personnel to suspend their practice activities for 1 year; if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall also revoke the practice license of that medical institution or the original filing department shall order it to cease practice activities, and the original practice registration department shall revoke the practice certificates of the relevant medical personnel.  
If a medical institution, no longer meeting the conditions specified in Article 23, paragraph 2 of these Regulations, still engages in human organ transplantation, the original registration department shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal gains, revoke the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation of that medical institution, prohibit it from engaging in the procurement of human organs or applying for human organ transplantation for 3 years, and impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall also revoke the practice license of that medical institution, and order the relevant medical personnel to suspend their practice activities for not less than 6 months but not more than 1 year.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　医疗机构安排不符合[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定的人员实施人体器官移植手术的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其3年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，还应当由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证；对有关人员，依照有关医师管理的法律的规定予以处罚。

**Article 39** If a medical institution arranges for a person who does not meet the requirements of Article 27 of these Regulations to perform human organ transplantation surgery, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, the original registration department shall revoke the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation of that medical institution, prohibit it from engaging in the procurement of human organs or applying for human organ transplantation for 3 years, and impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall also revoke the practice license of that medical institution; the relevant persons shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws on the management of physicians.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　医疗机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分，对有关医务人员责令暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动，并可以由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其3年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植；情节严重的，还应当由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证或者由原备案部门责令其停止执业活动，并可以由原执业注册部门吊销有关医务人员的执业证书：  
（一）不具备[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第一款规定的条件从事遗体器官获取；  
（二）未按照所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生健康部门划定的区域提供遗体器官获取服务；  
（三）从事人体器官获取、移植的医务人员参与遗体器官捐献人的死亡判定；  
（四）未通过分配系统分配遗体器官，或者不执行分配系统分配结果；  
（五）使用未经分配系统分配的遗体器官或者来源不明的人体器官实施人体器官移植；  
（六）获取活体器官前未依照[本条例第二十九条](#art-29)第一款的规定履行说明、查验、确认义务；  
（七）以伪造、篡改数据等方式干扰遗体器官分配。

**Article 40** If a medical institution, in violation of these Regulations, has any of the following circumstances, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, impose sanctions on the responsible leaders and directly responsible persons according to law, order the relevant medical personnel to suspend their practice activities for not less than 6 months but not more than 1 year, and may revoke the diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation of that medical institution by the original registration department, prohibit it from engaging in the procurement of human organs or applying for human organ transplantation for 3 years; if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall also revoke the practice license of that medical institution or the original filing department shall order it to cease practice activities, and the original practice registration department may also revoke the practice certificates of the relevant medical personnel:  
(1) engaging in the procurement of deceased human organs without meeting the conditions specified in Article 15, paragraph 1 of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to provide procurement services for deceased human organs in the area designated by the health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located;  
(3) medical personnel engaged in the procurement and transplantation of human organs participating in the determination of death of a deceased organ donor;  
(4) failing to allocate deceased human organs through the allocation system, or failing to implement the allocation results of the allocation system;  
(5) using deceased human organs that have not been allocated through the allocation system or human organs of unknown origin to perform human organ transplantation;  
(6) failing to perform the obligations of explanation, verification, and confirmation in accordance with Article 29, paragraph 1 of these Regulations before procuring a living organ;  
(7) interfering with the allocation of deceased human organs by means such as forging or tampering with data.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门没收违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；医疗机构有下列情形之一的，还应当由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其3年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，情节严重的，由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证或者由原备案部门责令其停止执业活动；医务人员有下列情形之一的，还应当责令其暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动，情节严重的，由原执业注册部门吊销其执业证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）以获取遗体器官为目的跨区域转运潜在遗体器官捐献人；  
（二）违反[本条例第十六条](#art-16)第四款规定，转介潜在遗体器官捐献人的相关信息；  
（三）在人体器官捐献和移植中提供虚假材料。

**Article 41** In violation of these Regulations, any of the following circumstances shall be subject to confiscation of illegal gains by the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the leading personnel with responsibility and the directly responsible personnel shall be given sanctions according to law; where a medical institution has any of the following circumstances, the original registration department shall also revoke the medical institution's diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation, prohibit it from engaging in human organ procurement or applying for human organ transplantation for 3 years, and if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall revoke the medical institution's practice license or the original filing department shall order it to cease practice activities; where a medical practitioner has any of the following circumstances, the medical practitioner shall also be ordered to suspend practice activities for not less than 6 months but not more than 1 year, and if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall revoke his or her practice certificate; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) transferring potential deceased organ donors across regions for the purpose of procuring deceased organs;  
(2) in violation of the provisions of Article 16, paragraph 4 of these Regulations, referring relevant information of potential deceased organ donors;  
(3) providing false materials in human organ donation and transplantation.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　医疗机构未经人体器官移植伦理委员会审查同意获取人体器官的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其3年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，并对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，还应当由原执业登记部门吊销该医疗机构的执业许可证，并由原执业注册部门吊销有关医务人员的执业证书。  
人体器官移植伦理委员会审查获取人体器官申请时违反伦理原则或者出具虚假审查意见的，对有关责任人员依法给予处分，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门终身禁止其从事医学伦理审查活动。

**Article 42** Where a medical institution procures human organs without the review and consent of the Human Organ Transplantation Ethics Committee, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, the original registration department shall revoke the medical institution's diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation, prohibit it from engaging in human organ procurement or applying for human organ transplantation for 3 years, and shall impose sanctions on the leading personnel with responsibility and the directly responsible personnel according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original practice registration department shall also revoke the medical institution's practice license, and the original practice registration department shall revoke the practice certificates of the relevant medical practitioners.  
Where the Human Organ Transplantation Ethics Committee violates ethical principles or issues false review opinions when reviewing applications for human organ procurement, sanctions shall be imposed on the responsible personnel according to law, and the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall permanently prohibit them from engaging in medical ethics review activities.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　医疗机构违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康部门处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重的，还应当由原登记部门吊销该医疗机构的人体器官移植诊疗科目，禁止其1年内从事人体器官获取或者申请从事人体器官移植，对有关医务人员责令暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动：  
（一）负责遗体器官获取的部门未独立于负责人体器官移植的科室；  
（二）未经人体器官捐献协调员见证实施遗体器官获取；  
（三）获取器官后，未依照[本条例第十九条](#art-19)第三款的规定对遗体进行符合伦理原则的医学处理，恢复遗体外观；  
（四）未依照[本条例第三十四条](#art-34)的规定报告人体器官获取、移植实施情况。

**Article 43** Where a medical institution, in violation of these Regulations, has any of the following circumstances, the health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and shall impose sanctions on the leading personnel with responsibility and the directly responsible personnel according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original registration department shall also revoke the medical institution's diagnosis and treatment subject of human organ transplantation, prohibit it from engaging in human organ procurement or applying for human organ transplantation for 1 year, and order the relevant medical practitioners to suspend practice activities for not less than 6 months but not more than 1 year:  
(1) the department responsible for deceased organ procurement is not independent from the department responsible for human organ transplantation;  
(2) procuring deceased organs without the witnessing of a human organ donation coordinator;  
(3) after procuring organs, failing to perform ethically compliant medical treatment on the body and restore its appearance in accordance with Article 19, paragraph 3 of these Regulations;  
(4) failing to report the implementation of human organ procurement and transplantation in accordance with Article 34 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　医疗机构及其医务人员违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，依照有关医疗纠纷预防和处理、医疗事故处理的行政法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未对人体器官捐献人或者获取的人体器官进行医学检查；  
（二）未对接受人接受人体器官移植的风险进行评估并采取相应措施；  
（三）未遵守相关技术临床应用管理规范。

**Article 44** Where a medical institution and its medical practitioners, in violation of these Regulations, have any of the following circumstances, they shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the administrative regulations on the prevention and handling of medical disputes and the handling of medical accidents; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to conduct medical examinations on the human organ donor or the procured human organ;  
(2) failing to assess the risks of the recipient undergoing human organ transplantation and take corresponding measures;  
(3) failing to comply with relevant technical clinical application management norms.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　人体器官捐献协调员、医疗机构及其工作人员违反本条例规定，泄露人体器官捐献人、接受人或者申请人体器官移植手术患者个人信息的，依照法律、行政法规关于个人信息保护的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where a human organ donation coordinator, a medical institution, or its staff, in violation of these Regulations, discloses the personal information of a human organ donor, recipient, or patient applying for human organ transplantation surgery, they shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the protection of personal information; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)第一款规定收取费用的，依照有关价格、医疗保障基金管理的法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 46** Where fees are charged in violation of Article 32, paragraph 1 of these Regulations, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on price and medical insurance fund management.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　人体器官捐献协调员违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，依法给予处分，由省、自治区、直辖市红十字会注销其人体器官捐献协调员工作证件，终身不得担任人体器官捐献协调员：  
（一）接到指派后未对遗体器官获取进行见证；  
（二）出具虚假见证意见。

**Article 47** Where a human organ donation coordinator, in violation of these Regulations, has any of the following circumstances, sanctions shall be imposed according to law, and the Red Cross Society of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall cancel his or her human organ donation coordinator work certificate, and he or she shall be permanently prohibited from serving as a human organ donation coordinator:  
(1) failing to witness deceased organ procurement after being assigned;  
(2) issuing false witnessing opinions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　公职人员在人体器官捐献和移植工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where a public official abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of human organ donation and transplantation, sanctions shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，给他人造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。  
附　　则

**Article 49** In violation of these Regulations, causing damage to others, civil liability shall be borne according to law.  
Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例自2024年5月1日起施行。《人体器官移植条例》同时废止。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2024. The Regulations on Human Organ Transplantation shall be abolished simultaneously.
