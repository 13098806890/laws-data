# 化妆品监督管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-06-16  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范化妆品生产经营活动，加强化妆品监督管理，保证化妆品质量安全，保障消费者健康，促进化妆品产业健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating cosmetics production and operation activities, strengthening cosmetics supervision and administration, ensuring cosmetics quality and safety, protecting consumers' health, and promoting the healthy development of the cosmetics industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事化妆品生产经营活动及其监督管理，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Those engaged in cosmetics production and operation activities and the supervision and administration thereof within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称化妆品，是指以涂擦、喷洒或者其他类似方法，施用于皮肤、毛发、指甲、口唇等人体表面，以清洁、保护、美化、修饰为目的的日用化学工业产品。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'cosmetics' means daily-use chemical industrial products applied to the human body surface such as skin, hair, nails, and lips by means of smearing, spraying, or other similar methods, for the purposes of cleansing, protecting, beautifying, or modifying.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家按照风险程度对化妆品、化妆品原料实行分类管理。  
化妆品分为特殊化妆品和普通化妆品。国家对特殊化妆品实行注册管理，对普通化妆品实行备案管理。  
化妆品原料分为新原料和已使用的原料。国家对风险程度较高的化妆品新原料实行注册管理，对其他化妆品新原料实行备案管理。

**Article 4** The State implements categorized administration of cosmetics and cosmetic ingredients according to the degree of risk.  
Cosmetics are classified into special cosmetics and ordinary cosmetics. The State implements registration administration for special cosmetics and filing administration for ordinary cosmetics.  
Cosmetic ingredients are classified into new ingredients and used ingredients. The State implements registration administration for new cosmetic ingredients with a relatively high degree of risk, and filing administration for other new cosmetic ingredients.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院药品监督管理部门负责全国化妆品监督管理工作。国务院有关部门在各自职责范围内负责与化妆品有关的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门负责本行政区域的化妆品监督管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内负责与化妆品有关的监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of cosmetics nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration related to cosmetics within their respective functions.  
The drug regulatory departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of cosmetics within their respective administrative regions. The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration related to cosmetics within their respective functions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　化妆品注册人、备案人对化妆品的质量安全和功效宣称负责。  
化妆品生产经营者应当依照法律、法规、强制性国家标准、技术规范从事生产经营活动，加强管理，诚信自律，保证化妆品质量安全。

**Article 6** Cosmetic registrants and filers shall be responsible for the quality, safety, and efficacy claims of cosmetics.  
Cosmetic producers and operators shall engage in production and operation activities in accordance with laws, regulations, mandatory national standards, and technical specifications, strengthen management, act with integrity and self-discipline, and ensure the quality and safety of cosmetics.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　化妆品行业协会应当加强行业自律，督促引导化妆品生产经营者依法从事生产经营活动，推动行业诚信建设。

**Article 7** Cosmetic industry associations shall strengthen industry self-discipline, supervise and guide cosmetic producers and operators to engage in production and operation activities according to law, and promote the construction of industry integrity.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　消费者协会和其他消费者组织对违反本条例规定损害消费者合法权益的行为，依法进行社会监督。

**Article 8** Consumer associations and other consumer organizations shall exercise social supervision according to law over acts that violate the provisions of these Regulations and harm the legitimate rights and interests of consumers.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持开展化妆品研究、创新，满足消费者需求，推进化妆品品牌建设，发挥品牌引领作用。国家保护单位和个人开展化妆品研究、创新的合法权益。  
国家鼓励和支持化妆品生产经营者采用先进技术和先进管理规范，提高化妆品质量安全水平；鼓励和支持运用现代科学技术，结合我国传统优势项目和特色植物资源研究开发化妆品。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports the conduct of cosmetics research and innovation to meet consumer demands, promote cosmetics brand building, and give play to the leading role of brands. The State protects the legitimate rights and interests of units and individuals in conducting cosmetics research and innovation.  
The State encourages and supports cosmetic producers and operators to adopt advanced technologies and advanced management norms to improve the quality and safety level of cosmetics; and encourages and supports the use of modern science and technology, combined with China's traditional advantageous projects and characteristic plant resources, to research and develop cosmetics.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家加强化妆品监督管理信息化建设，提高在线政务服务水平，为办理化妆品行政许可、备案提供便利，推进监督管理信息共享。

**Article 10** The State shall strengthen the informationization construction of cosmetics supervision and administration, improve the level of online government services, facilitate the handling of cosmetic administrative licensing and filing, and promote the sharing of supervision and administration information.

### 第二章　原料与产品

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在我国境内首次使用于化妆品的天然或者人工原料为化妆品新原料。具有防腐、防晒、着色、染发、祛斑美白功能的化妆品新原料，经国务院药品监督管理部门注册后方可使用；其他化妆品新原料应当在使用前向国务院药品监督管理部门备案。国务院药品监督管理部门可以根据科学研究的发展，调整实行注册管理的化妆品新原料的范围，经国务院批准后实施。

**Article 11** Natural or artificial ingredients first used in cosmetics within China are new cosmetic ingredients. New cosmetic ingredients with functions of antisepsis, sun protection, coloring, hair dyeing, and freckle removal and whitening may only be used after registration with the drug regulatory department of the State Council; other new cosmetic ingredients shall be filed with the drug regulatory department of the State Council before use. The drug regulatory department of the State Council may, based on the development of scientific research, adjust the scope of new cosmetic ingredients subject to registration administration, and shall implement such adjustments after approval by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请化妆品新原料注册或者进行化妆品新原料备案，应当提交下列资料：  
（一）注册申请人、备案人的名称、地址、联系方式；  
（二）新原料研制报告；  
（三）新原料的制备工艺、稳定性及其质量控制标准等研究资料；  
（四）新原料安全评估资料。  
注册申请人、备案人应当对所提交资料的真实性、科学性负责。

**Article 12** To apply for registration of a new cosmetic ingredient or to file a new cosmetic ingredient, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) the name, address, and contact information of the registration applicant or filer;  
(2) a research report on the new ingredient;  
(3) research materials on the preparation process, stability, and quality control standards of the new ingredient;  
(4) safety assessment materials for the new ingredient.  
The registration applicant or filer shall be responsible for the authenticity and scientific nature of the submitted materials.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院药品监督管理部门应当自受理化妆品新原料注册申请之日起3个工作日内将申请资料转交技术审评机构。技术审评机构应当自收到申请资料之日起90个工作日内完成技术审评，向国务院药品监督管理部门提交审评意见。国务院药品监督管理部门应当自收到审评意见之日起20个工作日内作出决定。对符合要求的，准予注册并发给化妆品新原料注册证；对不符合要求的，不予注册并书面说明理由。  
化妆品新原料备案人通过国务院药品监督管理部门在线政务服务平台提交本条例规定的备案资料后即完成备案。  
国务院药品监督管理部门应当自化妆品新原料准予注册之日起、备案人提交备案资料之日起5个工作日内向社会公布注册、备案有关信息。

**Article 13** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, within 3 working days from the date of accepting an application for registration of a new cosmetic ingredient, transfer the application materials to a technical review institution. The technical review institution shall, within 90 working days from the date of receiving the application materials, complete the technical review and submit a review opinion to the drug regulatory department of the State Council. The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of receiving the review opinion, make a decision. If the requirements are met, it shall approve the registration and issue a registration certificate for the new cosmetic ingredient; if the requirements are not met, it shall not approve the registration and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.  
A filer of a new cosmetic ingredient shall complete the filing upon submitting the filing materials prescribed in these Regulations through the online government service platform of the drug regulatory department of the State Council.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall disclose to the public the relevant information on registration or filing within 5 working days from the date of approving the registration of the new cosmetic ingredient or from the date the filer submits the filing materials.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经注册、备案的化妆品新原料投入使用后3年内，新原料注册人、备案人应当每年向国务院药品监督管理部门报告新原料的使用和安全情况。对存在安全问题的化妆品新原料，由国务院药品监督管理部门撤销注册或者取消备案。3年期满未发生安全问题的化妆品新原料，纳入国务院药品监督管理部门制定的已使用的化妆品原料目录。  
经注册、备案的化妆品新原料纳入已使用的化妆品原料目录前，仍然按照化妆品新原料进行管理。

**Article 14** Within three years after a registered or filed new cosmetic ingredient is put into use, the registrant or filer of the new ingredient shall annually report to the drug regulatory department of the State Council on the use and safety of the new ingredient. For a new cosmetic ingredient that has safety issues, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall revoke the registration or cancel the filing. A new cosmetic ingredient that has no safety issues after the three-year period shall be included in the catalogue of used cosmetic ingredients formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.  
Before a registered or filed new cosmetic ingredient is included in the catalogue of used cosmetic ingredients, it shall still be managed as a new cosmetic ingredient.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止用于化妆品生产的原料目录由国务院药品监督管理部门制定、公布。

**Article 15** The catalogue of ingredients prohibited for use in cosmetics production shall be formulated and published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　用于染发、烫发、祛斑美白、防晒、防脱发的化妆品以及宣称新功效的化妆品为特殊化妆品。特殊化妆品以外的化妆品为普通化妆品。  
国务院药品监督管理部门根据化妆品的功效宣称、作用部位、产品剂型、使用人群等因素，制定、公布化妆品分类规则和分类目录。

**Article 16** Cosmetics used for hair dyeing, perming, freckle removal and whitening, sun protection, and hair loss prevention, as well as cosmetics claiming new efficacy, are special cosmetics. Cosmetics other than special cosmetics are ordinary cosmetics.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall, based on factors such as the efficacy claims, site of action, product dosage form, and target population of cosmetics, formulate and publish cosmetic classification rules and a classification catalogue.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　特殊化妆品经国务院药品监督管理部门注册后方可生产、进口。国产普通化妆品应当在上市销售前向备案人所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门备案。进口普通化妆品应当在进口前向国务院药品监督管理部门备案。

**Article 17** Special cosmetics may only be produced or imported after registration with the drug regulatory department of the State Council. Domestic ordinary cosmetics shall be filed with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the central government where the filer is located before being sold on the market. Imported ordinary cosmetics shall be filed with the drug regulatory department of the State Council before import.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　化妆品注册申请人、备案人应当具备下列条件：  
（一）是依法设立的企业或者其他组织；  
（二）有与申请注册、进行备案的产品相适应的质量管理体系；  
（三）有化妆品不良反应监测与评价能力。

**Article 18** An applicant for cosmetic registration or a filer shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being an enterprise or other organization established according to law;  
(2) having a quality management system appropriate for the product for which registration or filing is sought;  
(3) having the capability to monitor and evaluate adverse reactions to cosmetics.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请特殊化妆品注册或者进行普通化妆品备案，应当提交下列资料：  
（一）注册申请人、备案人的名称、地址、联系方式；  
（二）生产企业的名称、地址、联系方式；  
（三）产品名称；  
（四）产品配方或者产品全成分；  
（五）产品执行的标准；  
（六）产品标签样稿；  
（七）产品检验报告；  
（八）产品安全评估资料。  
注册申请人首次申请特殊化妆品注册或者备案人首次进行普通化妆品备案的，应当提交其符合[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定条件的证明资料。申请进口特殊化妆品注册或者进行进口普通化妆品备案的，应当同时提交产品在生产国（地区）已经上市销售的证明文件以及境外生产企业符合化妆品生产质量管理规范的证明资料；专为向我国出口生产、无法提交产品在生产国（地区）已经上市销售的证明文件的，应当提交面向我国消费者开展的相关研究和试验的资料。  
注册申请人、备案人应当对所提交资料的真实性、科学性负责。

**Article 19** To apply for registration of a special cosmetic or to file an ordinary cosmetic, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) the name, address, and contact information of the registration applicant or filer;  
(2) the name, address, and contact information of the manufacturer;  
(3) the product name;  
(4) the product formula or list of all ingredients;  
(5) the standards implemented for the product;  
(6) a draft of the product label;  
(7) a product test report;  
(8) product safety assessment materials.  
Where a registration applicant applies for registration of a special cosmetic for the first time, or a filer files an ordinary cosmetic for the first time, it shall submit proof materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions set forth in Article 18 of these Regulations. Where applying for registration of an imported special cosmetic or filing an imported ordinary cosmetic, it shall also submit documents certifying that the product has been marketed in the country (region) of production and proof materials that the overseas manufacturer complies with the cosmetic production quality management standards; if the product is produced exclusively for export to China and the documents certifying marketing in the country (region) of production cannot be submitted, it shall submit materials on relevant research and tests conducted for Chinese consumers.  
The registration applicant or filer shall be responsible for the authenticity and scientific nature of the submitted materials.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院药品监督管理部门依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)第一款规定的化妆品新原料注册审查程序对特殊化妆品注册申请进行审查。对符合要求的，准予注册并发给特殊化妆品注册证；对不符合要求的，不予注册并书面说明理由。已经注册的特殊化妆品在生产工艺、功效宣称等方面发生实质性变化的，注册人应当向原注册部门申请变更注册。  
普通化妆品备案人通过国务院药品监督管理部门在线政务服务平台提交本条例规定的备案资料后即完成备案。  
省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门应当自特殊化妆品准予注册之日起、普通化妆品备案人提交备案资料之日起5个工作日内向社会公布注册、备案有关信息。

**Article 20** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall examine applications for registration of special cosmetics in accordance with the review procedure for registration of new cosmetic ingredients as specified in paragraph 1 of Article 13 of these Regulations. If the requirements are met, it shall approve the registration and issue a registration certificate for the special cosmetic; if the requirements are not met, it shall not approve the registration and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons. Where a registered special cosmetic undergoes material changes in the production process, efficacy claims, or other aspects, the registrant shall apply to the original registration department for a change of registration.  
A filer of an ordinary cosmetic shall complete the filing upon submitting the filing materials prescribed in these Regulations through the online government service platform of the drug regulatory department of the State Council.  
The drug regulatory department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall disclose to the public the relevant information on registration or filing within 5 working days from the date of approving the registration of the special cosmetic or from the date the filer of the ordinary cosmetic submits the filing materials.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　化妆品新原料和化妆品注册、备案前，注册申请人、备案人应当自行或者委托专业机构开展安全评估。  
从事安全评估的人员应当具备化妆品质量安全相关专业知识，并具有5年以上相关专业从业经历。

**Article 21** Before registration or recordation of a new cosmetic ingredient or a cosmetic product, the registration applicant or the recordation party shall conduct a safety assessment on its own or through entrusting a professional institution.  
The personnel engaged in safety assessment shall possess professional knowledge of cosmetic quality and safety, and shall have more than 5 years of relevant professional experience.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　化妆品的功效宣称应当有充分的科学依据。化妆品注册人、备案人应当在国务院药品监督管理部门规定的专门网站公布功效宣称所依据的文献资料、研究数据或者产品功效评价资料的摘要，接受社会监督。

**Article 22** The efficacy claims of cosmetics shall have sufficient scientific basis. The cosmetics registrant or recordation party shall publish on the dedicated website designated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council the summary of literature, research data, or product efficacy evaluation data on which the efficacy claims are based, and shall accept public supervision.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　境外化妆品注册人、备案人应当指定我国境内的企业法人办理化妆品注册、备案，协助开展化妆品不良反应监测、实施产品召回。

**Article 23** An overseas cosmetics registrant or recordation party shall designate an enterprise legal person within the territory of China to handle cosmetic registration and recordation, assist in conducting cosmetic adverse reaction monitoring, and implement product recall.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　特殊化妆品注册证有效期为5年。有效期届满需要延续注册的，应当在有效期届满30个工作日前提出延续注册的申请。除有本条第二款规定情形外，国务院药品监督管理部门应当在特殊化妆品注册证有效期届满前作出准予延续的决定；逾期未作决定的，视为准予延续。  
有下列情形之一的，不予延续注册：  
（一）注册人未在规定期限内提出延续注册申请；  
（二）强制性国家标准、技术规范已经修订，申请延续注册的化妆品不能达到修订后标准、技术规范的要求。

**Article 24** The validity period of a special cosmetics registration certificate shall be five years. Where renewal of registration is required upon expiration, an application for renewal shall be filed within 30 working days prior to the expiry date. Unless there is a circumstance specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall make a decision to approve the renewal before the expiration of the special cosmetics registration certificate; if no decision is made within the prescribed time limit, it shall be deemed as approval of renewal.  
In any of the following circumstances, the renewal shall not be granted:  
(1) The registrant fails to file an application for renewal within the prescribed time limit;  
(2) The mandatory national standards or technical specifications have been revised, and the cosmetic product for which renewal is applied cannot meet the requirements of the revised standards or technical specifications.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院药品监督管理部门负责化妆品强制性国家标准的项目提出、组织起草、征求意见和技术审查。国务院标准化行政部门负责化妆品强制性国家标准的立项、编号和对外通报。  
化妆品国家标准文本应当免费向社会公开。  
化妆品应当符合强制性国家标准。鼓励企业制定严于强制性国家标准的企业标准。

**Article 25** The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall be responsible for the proposal, organization of drafting, solicitation of comments, and technical review of mandatory national standards for cosmetics. The standardization administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the project initiation, numbering, and external notification of mandatory national standards for cosmetics.  
The text of national standards for cosmetics shall be made available to the public free of charge.  
Cosmetics shall comply with mandatory national standards. Enterprises are encouraged to formulate enterprise standards stricter than the mandatory national standards.

### 第三章　生产经营

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　从事化妆品生产活动，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）是依法设立的企业；  
（二）有与生产的化妆品相适应的生产场地、环境条件、生产设施设备；  
（三）有与生产的化妆品相适应的技术人员；  
（四）有能对生产的化妆品进行检验的检验人员和检验设备；  
（五）有保证化妆品质量安全的管理制度。

**Article 26** Any entity engaged in the production of cosmetics shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) It is an enterprise established according to law;  
(2) It has production premises, environmental conditions, and production facilities and equipment appropriate for the cosmetics being produced;  
(3) It has technical personnel appropriate for the cosmetics being produced;  
(4) It has inspection personnel and inspection equipment capable of testing the cosmetics being produced;  
(5) It has a management system to ensure the quality and safety of cosmetics.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事化妆品生产活动，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门提出申请，提交其符合[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定条件的证明资料，并对资料的真实性负责。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门应当对申请资料进行审核，对申请人的生产场所进行现场核查，并自受理化妆品生产许可申请之日起30个工作日内作出决定。对符合规定条件的，准予许可并发给化妆品生产许可证；对不符合规定条件的，不予许可并书面说明理由。  
化妆品生产许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满需要延续的，依照《中华人民共和国行政许可法》的规定办理。

**Article 27** Any entity engaged in the production of cosmetics shall file an application with the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, submit the certification materials demonstrating that it meets the conditions provided for in Article 26 of these Regulations, and assume responsibility for the authenticity of the materials.  
The drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall review the application materials, conduct on-site inspection of the applicant's production premises, and make a decision within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application for cosmetics production license. For those meeting the prescribed conditions, the license shall be granted and a cosmetics production license issued; for those not meeting the prescribed conditions, the license shall not be granted and reasons shall be provided in writing.  
The validity period of a cosmetics production license shall be five years. Where renewal is required upon expiration, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　化妆品注册人、备案人可以自行生产化妆品，也可以委托其他企业生产化妆品。  
委托生产化妆品的，化妆品注册人、备案人应当委托取得相应化妆品生产许可的企业，并对受委托企业（以下称受托生产企业）的生产活动进行监督，保证其按照法定要求进行生产。受托生产企业应当依照法律、法规、强制性国家标准、技术规范以及合同约定进行生产，对生产活动负责，并接受化妆品注册人、备案人的监督。

**Article 28** A cosmetics registrant or recordation party may produce cosmetics on its own or may entrust another enterprise to produce cosmetics.  
Where a cosmetics product is produced through entrustment, the cosmetics registrant or recordation party shall entrust an enterprise that has obtained the corresponding cosmetics production license, and shall supervise the production activities of the entrusted enterprise (hereinafter referred to as the contract manufacturing enterprise) to ensure that it produces in accordance with legal requirements. The contract manufacturing enterprise shall produce in accordance with laws, regulations, mandatory national standards, technical specifications, and the contract, be responsible for the production activities, and accept supervision from the cosmetics registrant or recordation party.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当按照国务院药品监督管理部门制定的化妆品生产质量管理规范的要求组织生产化妆品，建立化妆品生产质量管理体系，建立并执行供应商遴选、原料验收、生产过程及质量控制、设备管理、产品检验及留样等管理制度。  
化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当按照化妆品注册或者备案资料载明的技术要求生产化妆品。

**Article 29** A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall organize the production of cosmetics in accordance with the requirements of the cosmetics production quality management standards formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, establish a cosmetics production quality management system, and establish and implement management systems for supplier selection, raw material acceptance, production process and quality control, equipment management, product inspection, and sample retention.  
A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall produce cosmetics in accordance with the technical requirements specified in the cosmetics registration or recordation documents.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　化妆品原料、直接接触化妆品的包装材料应当符合强制性国家标准、技术规范。  
不得使用超过使用期限、废弃、回收的化妆品或者化妆品原料生产化妆品。

**Article 30** Cosmetic ingredients and packaging materials that come into direct contact with cosmetics shall comply with mandatory national standards and technical specifications.  
Cosmetics or cosmetic ingredients that have exceeded their shelf life, been discarded, or recycled shall not be used in the production of cosmetics.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当建立并执行原料以及直接接触化妆品的包装材料进货查验记录制度、产品销售记录制度。进货查验记录和产品销售记录应当真实、完整，保证可追溯，保存期限不得少于产品使用期限届满后1年；产品使用期限不足1年的，记录保存期限不得少于2年。  
化妆品经出厂检验合格后方可上市销售。

**Article 31** A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall establish and implement a system for keeping records of the inspection of purchased ingredients and packaging materials that come into direct contact with cosmetics, and a system for keeping records of product sales. The records of inspection upon purchase and product sales shall be truthful and complete, ensure traceability, and the retention period shall not be less than one year after the expiration of the product's shelf life; if the product's shelf life is less than one year, the retention period of the records shall not be less than two years.  
Cosmetics may only be marketed and sold after passing factory inspection.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当设质量安全负责人，承担相应的产品质量安全管理和产品放行职责。  
质量安全负责人应当具备化妆品质量安全相关专业知识，并具有5年以上化妆品生产或者质量安全管理经验。

**Article 32** A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall designate a quality safety officer to assume the corresponding responsibilities for product quality safety management and product release.  
The quality safety officer shall possess professional knowledge related to cosmetic quality and safety, and shall have more than five years of experience in cosmetic production or quality safety management.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当建立并执行从业人员健康管理制度。患有国务院卫生主管部门规定的有碍化妆品质量安全疾病的人员不得直接从事化妆品生产活动。

**Article 33** A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall establish and implement a health management system for employees. Persons suffering from diseases that are stipulated by the health department of the State Council as affecting cosmetics quality and safety shall not directly engage in the production of cosmetics.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业应当定期对化妆品生产质量管理规范的执行情况进行自查；生产条件发生变化，不再符合化妆品生产质量管理规范要求的，应当立即采取整改措施；可能影响化妆品质量安全的，应当立即停止生产并向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报告。

**Article 34** A cosmetics registrant, recordation party, or contract manufacturing enterprise shall periodically conduct self-inspection on the implementation of the cosmetics production quality management standards; if the production conditions change and no longer meet the requirements of the cosmetics production quality management standards, corrective measures shall be taken immediately; if the quality and safety of cosmetics may be affected, production shall be immediately suspended and the matter shall be reported to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　化妆品的最小销售单元应当有标签。标签应当符合相关法律、行政法规、强制性国家标准，内容真实、完整、准确。  
进口化妆品可以直接使用中文标签，也可以加贴中文标签；加贴中文标签的，中文标签内容应当与原标签内容一致。

**Article 35** The smallest sales unit of cosmetics shall have a label. The label shall comply with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and mandatory national standards, and its content shall be truthful, complete, and accurate.  
Imported cosmetics may directly use Chinese labels or may have Chinese labels affixed; where Chinese labels are affixed, the content of the Chinese labels shall be consistent with the original labels.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　化妆品标签应当标注下列内容：  
（一）产品名称、特殊化妆品注册证编号；  
（二）注册人、备案人、受托生产企业的名称、地址；  
（三）化妆品生产许可证编号；  
（四）产品执行的标准编号；  
（五）全成分；  
（六）净含量；  
（七）使用期限、使用方法以及必要的安全警示；  
（八）法律、行政法规和强制性国家标准规定应当标注的其他内容。

**Article 36** A cosmetics label shall indicate the following:  
(1) The product name and the special cosmetics registration certificate number;  
(2) The name and address of the registrant, recordation party, and contract manufacturing enterprise;  
(3) The cosmetics production license number;  
(4) The standard number implemented for the product;  
(5) Full ingredient list;  
(6) Net content;  
(7) Shelf life, directions for use, and necessary safety warnings;  
(8) Other contents that shall be indicated as required by laws, administrative regulations, and mandatory national standards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　化妆品标签禁止标注下列内容：  
（一）明示或者暗示具有医疗作用的内容；  
（二）虚假或者引人误解的内容；  
（三）违反社会公序良俗的内容；  
（四）法律、行政法规禁止标注的其他内容。

**Article 37** The following contents are prohibited from being indicated on cosmetics labels:  
(1) Contents that explicitly or implicitly claim therapeutic effects;  
(2) False or misleading contents;  
(3) Contents that violate public order and good customs;  
(4) Other contents prohibited from being indicated by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　化妆品经营者应当建立并执行进货查验记录制度，查验供货者的市场主体登记证明、化妆品注册或者备案情况、产品出厂检验合格证明，如实记录并保存相关凭证。记录和凭证保存期限应当符合[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)第一款的规定。  
化妆品经营者不得自行配制化妆品。

**Article 38** A cosmetics business operator shall establish and implement a system for keeping records of inspection upon purchase, and shall inspect the supplier's market entity registration certificate, the registration or recordation status of the cosmetics, and the certificate of factory inspection conformity for the product, and shall truthfully record and retain the relevant documents. The retention period of records and documents shall comply with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 31 of these Regulations.  
A cosmetics business operator shall not prepare cosmetics by itself.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　化妆品生产经营者应当依照有关法律、法规的规定和化妆品标签标示的要求贮存、运输化妆品，定期检查并及时处理变质或者超过使用期限的化妆品。

**Article 39** A cosmetics producer or business operator shall store and transport cosmetics in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations and the requirements indicated on the cosmetics label, and shall regularly inspect and promptly dispose of cosmetics that have deteriorated or exceeded their shelf life.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　化妆品集中交易市场开办者、展销会举办者应当审查入场化妆品经营者的市场主体登记证明，承担入场化妆品经营者管理责任，定期对入场化妆品经营者进行检查；发现入场化妆品经营者有违反本条例规定行为的，应当及时制止并报告所在地县级人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门。

**Article 40** The organizer of a centralized cosmetics trading market or the organizer of a promotion and exhibition shall review the market entity registration certificates of the cosmetics business operators entering the venue, assume the management responsibility for such operators, and regularly inspect them; if any violation of these Regulations is discovered, the organizer shall promptly stop the violation and report to the drug regulatory department of the people's government at the county level where it is located.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　电子商务平台经营者应当对平台内化妆品经营者进行实名登记，承担平台内化妆品经营者管理责任，发现平台内化妆品经营者有违反本条例规定行为的，应当及时制止并报告电子商务平台经营者所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门；发现严重违法行为的，应当立即停止向违法的化妆品经营者提供电子商务平台服务。  
平台内化妆品经营者应当全面、真实、准确、及时披露所经营化妆品的信息。

**Article 41** An e-commerce platform operator shall conduct real-name registration of cosmetics operators on the platform, assume the responsibility for managing cosmetics operators on the platform, and, upon discovering that a cosmetics operator on the platform has violated these Regulations, shall promptly stop such violation and report it to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the e-commerce platform operator is located; if a serious violation is discovered, the e-commerce platform operator shall immediately cease providing e-commerce platform services to the violating cosmetics operator.  
Cosmetics operators on the platform shall disclose information about the cosmetics they operate in a comprehensive, truthful, accurate, and timely manner.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　美容美发机构、宾馆等在经营中使用化妆品或者为消费者提供化妆品的，应当履行本条例规定的化妆品经营者义务。

**Article 42** Beauty salons, barbershops, hotels, and similar entities that use cosmetics in their business operations or provide cosmetics to consumers shall fulfill the obligations of cosmetics operators as specified in these Regulations.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　化妆品广告的内容应当真实、合法。  
化妆品广告不得明示或者暗示产品具有医疗作用，不得含有虚假或者引人误解的内容，不得欺骗、误导消费者。

**Article 43** The content of cosmetics advertisements shall be truthful and lawful.  
Cosmetics advertisements shall not expressly or implicitly indicate that the product has medical effects, shall not contain false or misleading content, and shall not deceive or mislead consumers.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　化妆品注册人、备案人发现化妆品存在质量缺陷或者其他问题，可能危害人体健康的，应当立即停止生产，召回已经上市销售的化妆品，通知相关化妆品经营者和消费者停止经营、使用，并记录召回和通知情况。化妆品注册人、备案人应当对召回的化妆品采取补救、无害化处理、销毁等措施，并将化妆品召回和处理情况向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门报告。  
受托生产企业、化妆品经营者发现其生产、经营的化妆品有前款规定情形的，应当立即停止生产、经营，通知相关化妆品注册人、备案人。化妆品注册人、备案人应当立即实施召回。  
负责药品监督管理的部门在监督检查中发现化妆品有本条第一款规定情形的，应当通知化妆品注册人、备案人实施召回，通知受托生产企业、化妆品经营者停止生产、经营。  
化妆品注册人、备案人实施召回的，受托生产企业、化妆品经营者应当予以配合。  
化妆品注册人、备案人、受托生产企业、经营者未依照本条规定实施召回或者停止生产、经营的，负责药品监督管理的部门责令其实施召回或者停止生产、经营。

**Article 44** Where a cosmetics registrant or record-filer discovers that a cosmetic product has a quality defect or other problem that may harm human health, it shall immediately stop production, recall the cosmetic that has already been placed on the market, notify the relevant cosmetics operators and consumers to cease business operations and use, and record the recall and notification. The cosmetics registrant or record-filer shall take remedial measures, harmless treatment, destruction, or other measures for the recalled cosmetics, and report the recall and handling to the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
Where an entrusted production enterprise or a cosmetics operator discovers that the cosmetics it produces or operates fall under the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall immediately stop production or business operations and notify the relevant cosmetics registrant or record-filer. The cosmetics registrant or record-filer shall immediately implement a recall.  
Where the drug regulatory department discovers, during supervision and inspection, that cosmetics fall under the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of this Article, it shall notify the cosmetics registrant or record-filer to implement a recall and notify the entrusted production enterprise and cosmetics operator to stop production or business operations.  
Where the cosmetics registrant or record-filer implements a recall, the entrusted production enterprise and cosmetics operator shall cooperate.  
Where a cosmetics registrant, record-filer, entrusted production enterprise, or operator fails to implement a recall or cease production or business operations in accordance with this Article, the drug regulatory department shall order it to implement a recall or cease production or business operations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　出入境检验检疫机构依照《中华人民共和国进出口商品检验法》的规定对进口的化妆品实施检验；检验不合格的，不得进口。  
进口商应当对拟进口的化妆品是否已经注册或者备案以及是否符合本条例和强制性国家标准、技术规范进行审核；审核不合格的，不得进口。进口商应当如实记录进口化妆品的信息，记录保存期限应当符合[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)第一款的规定。  
出口的化妆品应当符合进口国（地区）的标准或者合同要求。

**Article 45** Entry-exit inspection and quarantine agencies shall conduct inspection of imported cosmetics in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection of Import and Export Commodities; cosmetics that fail the inspection shall not be imported.  
An importer shall verify whether the cosmetics to be imported have been registered or recorded and comply with these Regulations and the mandatory national standards and technical specifications; if the verification fails, the cosmetics shall not be imported. The importer shall truthfully record the information on the imported cosmetics, and the retention period of the records shall comply with the provisions of Article 31, paragraph 1 of these Regulations.  
Exported cosmetics shall meet the standards of the importing country (region) or the requirements of the contract.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　负责药品监督管理的部门对化妆品生产经营进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生产经营场所实施现场检查；  
（二）对生产经营的化妆品进行抽样检验；  
（三）查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿以及其他有关资料；  
（四）查封、扣押不符合强制性国家标准、技术规范或者有证据证明可能危害人体健康的化妆品及其原料、直接接触化妆品的包装材料，以及有证据证明用于违法生产经营的工具、设备；  
（五）查封违法从事生产经营活动的场所。

**Article 46** When conducting supervision and inspection of the production and business operations of cosmetics, the drug regulatory department shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Enter the production and business premises to conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) Conduct sampling inspection of the cosmetics being produced or operated;  
(3) Examine and copy relevant contracts, invoices, account books, and other relevant materials;  
(4) Seal up or seize cosmetics and their raw materials, packaging materials in direct contact with cosmetics that do not meet mandatory national standards or technical specifications or for which there is evidence that they may harm human health, as well as tools and equipment for which there is evidence that they are used for illegal production or business operations;  
(5) Seal up premises used for illegal production or business operations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　负责药品监督管理的部门对化妆品生产经营进行监督检查时，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示执法证件。监督检查人员对监督检查中知悉的被检查单位的商业秘密，应当依法予以保密。被检查单位对监督检查应当予以配合，不得隐瞒有关情况。  
负责药品监督管理的部门应当对监督检查情况和处理结果予以记录，由监督检查人员和被检查单位负责人签字；被检查单位负责人拒绝签字的，应当予以注明。

**Article 47** When the drug regulatory department conducts supervision and inspection of the production and business operations of cosmetics, the number of inspectors shall not be less than two, and they shall present their law enforcement credentials. The inspectors shall keep confidential, in accordance with law, any trade secrets of the inspected entity that come to their knowledge during the supervision and inspection. The inspected entity shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection and shall not conceal any relevant information.  
The drug regulatory department shall record the supervision and inspection and the results of the handling, and such records shall be signed by the inspectors and the person in charge of the inspected entity; if the person in charge of the inspected entity refuses to sign, such refusal shall be noted.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门应当组织对化妆品进行抽样检验；对举报反映或者日常监督检查中发现问题较多的化妆品，负责药品监督管理的部门可以进行专项抽样检验。  
进行抽样检验，应当支付抽取样品的费用，所需费用纳入本级政府预算。  
负责药品监督管理的部门应当按照规定及时公布化妆品抽样检验结果。

**Article 48** The drug regulatory departments of people's governments at or above the provincial level shall organize sampling inspection of cosmetics; for cosmetics that have been reported or that have been found to have frequent problems in routine supervision and inspection, the drug regulatory department may conduct special sampling inspection.  
When conducting sampling inspection, the costs of the samples drawn shall be paid, and such costs shall be included in the budget of the government at the same level.  
The drug regulatory department shall promptly publish the results of the sampling inspection of cosmetics in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　化妆品检验机构按照国家有关认证认可的规定取得资质认定后，方可从事化妆品检验活动。化妆品检验机构的资质认定条件由国务院药品监督管理部门、国务院市场监督管理部门制定。  
化妆品检验规范以及化妆品检验相关标准品管理规定，由国务院药品监督管理部门制定。

**Article 49** A cosmetics inspection institution may engage in cosmetics inspection activities only after obtaining qualification accreditation in accordance with the relevant state provisions on certification and accreditation. The qualification accreditation conditions for cosmetics inspection institutions shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council and the market regulatory department of the State Council.  
The specifications for cosmetics inspection and the regulations on the management of reference substances related to cosmetics inspection shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对可能掺杂掺假或者使用禁止用于化妆品生产的原料生产的化妆品，按照化妆品国家标准规定的检验项目和检验方法无法检验的，国务院药品监督管理部门可以制定补充检验项目和检验方法，用于对化妆品的抽样检验、化妆品质量安全案件调查处理和不良反应调查处置。

**Article 50** Where cosmetics that may be adulterated or produced using raw materials prohibited for use in cosmetic production cannot be tested in accordance with the inspection items and methods specified in national cosmetics standards, the drug regulatory department of the State Council may formulate supplementary inspection items and methods for use in sampling inspection of cosmetics, investigation and handling of cosmetics quality and safety cases, and investigation and handling of adverse reactions.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　对依照本条例规定实施的检验结论有异议的，化妆品生产经营者可以自收到检验结论之日起7个工作日内向实施抽样检验的部门或者其上一级负责药品监督管理的部门提出复检申请，由受理复检申请的部门在复检机构名录中随机确定复检机构进行复检。复检机构出具的复检结论为最终检验结论。复检机构与初检机构不得为同一机构。复检机构名录由国务院药品监督管理部门公布。

**Article 51** Where a cosmetics producer or operator has an objection to the inspection conclusion made in accordance with these Regulations, it may, within 7 working days from the date of receipt of the inspection conclusion, submit an application for re-inspection to the department that conducted the sampling inspection or to the drug regulatory department at the next higher level. The department that accepts the application for re-inspection shall randomly select a re-inspection institution from the list of re-inspection institutions to conduct the re-inspection. The re-inspection conclusion issued by the re-inspection institution shall be the final inspection conclusion. The re-inspection institution and the initial inspection institution shall not be the same institution. The list of re-inspection institutions shall be published by the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家建立化妆品不良反应监测制度。化妆品注册人、备案人应当监测其上市销售化妆品的不良反应，及时开展评价，按照国务院药品监督管理部门的规定向化妆品不良反应监测机构报告。受托生产企业、化妆品经营者和医疗机构发现可能与使用化妆品有关的不良反应的，应当报告化妆品不良反应监测机构。鼓励其他单位和个人向化妆品不良反应监测机构或者负责药品监督管理的部门报告可能与使用化妆品有关的不良反应。  
化妆品不良反应监测机构负责化妆品不良反应信息的收集、分析和评价，并向负责药品监督管理的部门提出处理建议。  
化妆品生产经营者应当配合化妆品不良反应监测机构、负责药品监督管理的部门开展化妆品不良反应调查。  
化妆品不良反应是指正常使用化妆品所引起的皮肤及其附属器官的病变，以及人体局部或者全身性的损害。

**Article 52** The state shall establish a system for monitoring adverse reactions to cosmetics. A cosmetics registrant or record-filer shall monitor adverse reactions to the cosmetics it has placed on the market, conduct timely evaluations, and report to the cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution in accordance with the provisions of the drug regulatory department of the State Council. Where an entrusted production enterprise, cosmetics operator, or medical institution discovers an adverse reaction that may be related to the use of cosmetics, it shall report to the cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution. Other entities and individuals are encouraged to report to the cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution or the drug regulatory department any adverse reactions that may be related to the use of cosmetics.  
A cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution shall be responsible for the collection, analysis, and evaluation of information on adverse reactions to cosmetics, and shall propose handling suggestions to the drug regulatory department.  
A cosmetics producer or operator shall cooperate with the cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution and the drug regulatory department in conducting investigations into cosmetics adverse reactions.  
"Adverse reactions to cosmetics" refer to pathological changes in the skin and its appendages, as well as local or systemic damage to the human body, caused by the normal use of cosmetics.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家建立化妆品安全风险监测和评价制度，对影响化妆品质量安全的风险因素进行监测和评价，为制定化妆品质量安全风险控制措施和标准、开展化妆品抽样检验提供科学依据。  
国家化妆品安全风险监测计划由国务院药品监督管理部门制定、发布并组织实施。国家化妆品安全风险监测计划应当明确重点监测的品种、项目和地域等。  
国务院药品监督管理部门建立化妆品质量安全风险信息交流机制，组织化妆品生产经营者、检验机构、行业协会、消费者协会以及新闻媒体等就化妆品质量安全风险信息进行交流沟通。

**Article 53** The state shall establish a system for monitoring and evaluating cosmetics safety risks, to monitor and evaluate risk factors affecting the quality and safety of cosmetics, and to provide a scientific basis for formulating cosmetics quality and safety risk control measures and standards and for conducting sampling inspection of cosmetics.  
The national cosmetics safety risk monitoring plan shall be formulated, published, and organized for implementation by the drug regulatory department of the State Council. The national cosmetics safety risk monitoring plan shall specify the varieties, items, and regions to be monitored as a priority.  
The drug regulatory department of the State Council shall establish a mechanism for exchanging information on cosmetics quality and safety risks, and organize cosmetics producers and operators, inspection institutions, industry associations, consumer associations, news media, and other entities to exchange and communicate on information concerning cosmetics quality and safety risks.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对造成人体伤害或者有证据证明可能危害人体健康的化妆品，负责药品监督管理的部门可以采取责令暂停生产、经营的紧急控制措施，并发布安全警示信息；属于进口化妆品的，国家出入境检验检疫部门可以暂停进口。

**Article 54** With respect to cosmetics that have caused human injury or for which there is evidence that they may harm human health, the drug regulatory department may take emergency control measures such as ordering the suspension of production and business operations, and issue safety warning information; if they are imported cosmetics, the national entry-exit inspection and quarantine department may suspend importation.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　根据科学研究的发展，对化妆品、化妆品原料的安全性有认识上的改变的，或者有证据表明化妆品、化妆品原料可能存在缺陷的，省级以上人民政府药品监督管理部门可以责令化妆品、化妆品新原料的注册人、备案人开展安全再评估或者直接组织开展安全再评估。再评估结果表明化妆品、化妆品原料不能保证安全的，由原注册部门撤销注册、备案部门取消备案，由国务院药品监督管理部门将该化妆品原料纳入禁止用于化妆品生产的原料目录，并向社会公布。

**Article 55** Where, due to developments in scientific research, there is a change in understanding regarding the safety of cosmetics or cosmetic raw materials, or where there is evidence indicating that cosmetics or cosmetic raw materials may have defects, the drug regulatory department of a people's government at or above the provincial level may order the registrant or record-filer of the cosmetics or new cosmetic raw materials to conduct a safety re-evaluation, or may directly organize and conduct a safety re-evaluation. If the re-evaluation result shows that the cosmetics or cosmetic raw materials cannot be guaranteed to be safe, the original registration department shall revoke the registration, the record-filing department shall cancel the record, and the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall include such cosmetic raw materials in the catalogue of raw materials prohibited for use in cosmetic production, and shall make a public announcement.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　负责药品监督管理的部门应当依法及时公布化妆品行政许可、备案、日常监督检查结果、违法行为查处等监督管理信息。公布监督管理信息时，应当保守当事人的商业秘密。  
负责药品监督管理的部门应当建立化妆品生产经营者信用档案。对有不良信用记录的化妆品生产经营者，增加监督检查频次；对有严重不良信用记录的生产经营者，按照规定实施联合惩戒。

**Article 56** The drug regulatory department shall, in accordance with law, promptly publish the administrative licensing, record-filing, routine supervision and inspection results, investigation and punishment of illegal acts, and other supervision and management information concerning cosmetics. When publishing supervision and management information, it shall keep confidential the trade secrets of the parties concerned.  
The drug regulatory department shall establish credit files for cosmetics producers and operators. For cosmetics producers and operators with adverse credit records, the frequency of supervision and inspection shall be increased; for producers and operators with serious adverse credit records, joint punishment shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　化妆品生产经营过程中存在安全隐患，未及时采取措施消除的，负责药品监督管理的部门可以对化妆品生产经营者的法定代表人或者主要负责人进行责任约谈。化妆品生产经营者应当立即采取措施，进行整改，消除隐患。责任约谈情况和整改情况应当纳入化妆品生产经营者信用档案。

**Article 57** Where there are safety hazards in the production or business operations of cosmetics and timely measures have not been taken to eliminate them, the drug regulatory department may conduct a responsibility interview with the legal representative or principal responsible person of the cosmetics producer or operator. The cosmetics producer or operator shall immediately take measures to carry out rectification and eliminate the hazards. The circumstances of the responsibility interview and the rectification shall be included in the credit file of the cosmetics producer or operator.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　负责药品监督管理的部门应当公布本部门的网站地址、电子邮件地址或者电话，接受咨询、投诉、举报，并及时答复或者处理。对查证属实的举报，按照国家有关规定给予举报人奖励。

**Article 58** The drug regulatory department shall publish its website address, email address, or telephone number, to receive inquiries, complaints, and reports, and shall promptly respond to or handle them. For reports that are verified to be true, the whistleblower shall be given a reward in accordance with the relevant state provisions.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有下列情形之一的，由负责药品监督管理的部门没收违法所得、违法生产经营的化妆品和专门用于违法生产经营的原料、包装材料、工具、设备等物品；违法生产经营的化妆品货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上15万元以下罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额15倍以上30倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业、由备案部门取消备案或者由原发证部门吊销化妆品许可证件，10年内不予办理其提出的化妆品备案或者受理其提出的化妆品行政许可申请，对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的3倍以上5倍以下罚款，终身禁止其从事化妆品生产经营活动；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未经许可从事化妆品生产活动，或者化妆品注册人、备案人委托未取得相应化妆品生产许可的企业生产化妆品；  
（二）生产经营或者进口未经注册的特殊化妆品；  
（三）使用禁止用于化妆品生产的原料、应当注册但未经注册的新原料生产化妆品，在化妆品中非法添加可能危害人体健康的物质，或者使用超过使用期限、废弃、回收的化妆品或者原料生产化妆品。

**Article 59** In any of the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall confiscate the illegal gains, the cosmetics illegally produced or operated, and the raw materials, packaging materials, tools, equipment, and other articles specially used for the illegal production or operation; if the value of the cosmetics illegally produced or operated is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the department shall order the suspension of production or business operations, the record-filing department shall cancel the record, or the original licensing department shall revoke the cosmetics license, and the department shall not process any cosmetics record-filing application or accept any cosmetics administrative licensing application submitted by the violator for 10 years, and shall impose on the legal representative or principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons of the violator a fine of not less than 3 times but not more than 5 times the income they received from the entity in the preceding year, and permanently prohibit them from engaging in cosmetics production or business activities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law:  
(1) Engaging in cosmetics production activities without a license, or a cosmetics registrant or record-filer entrusting an enterprise that has not obtained the corresponding cosmetics production license to produce cosmetics;  
(2) Producing, operating, or importing special cosmetics that have not been registered;  
(3) Using raw materials prohibited for use in cosmetic production, or new raw materials that should be registered but have not been registered, to produce cosmetics, illegally adding substances that may harm human health to cosmetics, or using cosmetics or raw materials that have exceeded their shelf life, are discarded, or are recycled to produce cosmetics.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列情形之一的，由负责药品监督管理的部门没收违法所得、违法生产经营的化妆品和专门用于违法生产经营的原料、包装材料、工具、设备等物品；违法生产经营的化妆品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额5倍以上20倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业、由备案部门取消备案或者由原发证部门吊销化妆品许可证件，对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的1倍以上3倍以下罚款，10年内禁止其从事化妆品生产经营活动；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）使用不符合强制性国家标准、技术规范的原料、直接接触化妆品的包装材料，应当备案但未备案的新原料生产化妆品，或者不按照强制性国家标准或者技术规范使用原料；  
（二）生产经营不符合强制性国家标准、技术规范或者不符合化妆品注册、备案资料载明的技术要求的化妆品；  
（三）未按照化妆品生产质量管理规范的要求组织生产；  
（四）更改化妆品使用期限；  
（五）化妆品经营者擅自配制化妆品，或者经营变质、超过使用期限的化妆品；  
（六）在负责药品监督管理的部门责令其实施召回后拒不召回，或者在负责药品监督管理的部门责令停止或者暂停生产、经营后拒不停止或者暂停生产、经营。

**Article 60** In any of the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall confiscate the illegal gains, the cosmetics illegally produced or operated, and the raw materials, packaging materials, tools, equipment, and other articles specially used for the illegal production or operation; if the value of the cosmetics illegally produced or operated is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 20 times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the department shall order the suspension of production or business operations, the record-filing department shall cancel the record, or the original licensing department shall revoke the cosmetics license, and shall impose on the legal representative or principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons of the violator a fine of not less than 1 time but not more than 3 times the income they received from the entity in the preceding year, and prohibit them from engaging in cosmetics production or business activities for 10 years; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law:  
(1) Using raw materials or packaging materials in direct contact with cosmetics that do not meet mandatory national standards or technical specifications, or new raw materials that should have been recorded but have not been recorded, to produce cosmetics, or failing to use raw materials in accordance with mandatory national standards or technical specifications;  
(2) Producing or operating cosmetics that do not meet mandatory national standards or technical specifications, or that do not meet the technical requirements specified in the cosmetics registration or record-filing materials;  
(3) Failing to organize production in accordance with the requirements of the cosmetics production quality management standards;  
(4) Altering the shelf life of cosmetics;  
(5) A cosmetics operator preparing cosmetics without authorization, or operating cosmetics that have deteriorated or exceeded their shelf life;  
(6) Refusing to implement a recall after being ordered to do so by the drug regulatory department, or refusing to stop or suspend production or business operations after being ordered to do so by the drug regulatory department.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有下列情形之一的，由负责药品监督管理的部门没收违法所得、违法生产经营的化妆品，并可以没收专门用于违法生产经营的原料、包装材料、工具、设备等物品；违法生产经营的化妆品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上3万元以下罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额3倍以上10倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业、由备案部门取消备案或者由原发证部门吊销化妆品许可证件，对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的1倍以上2倍以下罚款，5年内禁止其从事化妆品生产经营活动：  
（一）上市销售、经营或者进口未备案的普通化妆品；  
（二）未依照本条例规定设质量安全负责人；  
（三）化妆品注册人、备案人未对受托生产企业的生产活动进行监督；  
（四）未依照本条例规定建立并执行从业人员健康管理制度；  
（五）生产经营标签不符合本条例规定的化妆品。  
生产经营的化妆品的标签存在瑕疵但不影响质量安全且不会对消费者造成误导的，由负责药品监督管理的部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处2000元以下罚款。

**Article 61** In any of the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall confiscate the illegal gains and the illegally produced or traded cosmetics, and may confiscate raw materials, packaging materials, tools, equipment and other articles specially used for the illegal production or trading; if the value of the illegally produced or traded cosmetics is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than three times but not more than ten times the value of the cosmetics shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend production and business operation, the filing department shall cancel the filing, or the original licensing department shall revoke the cosmetics license, and the legal representative or principal responsible person of the illegal entity, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than one time but not more than two times their income obtained from the entity in the previous year, and they shall be prohibited from engaging in cosmetics production or trading for five years:  
(1) listing, trading or importing ordinary cosmetics that have not been filed;  
(2) failing to appoint a quality safety responsible person in accordance with these Regulations;  
(3) the cosmetics registrant or filer fails to supervise the production activities of the entrusted manufacturer;  
(4) failing to establish and implement a health management system for employees in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) producing or trading cosmetics whose labels do not conform to the provisions of these Regulations.  
If the labels of the cosmetics produced or traded have defects but do not affect quality and safety and will not mislead consumers, the drug regulatory department shall order correction; if the violator refuses to correct, a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列情形之一的，由负责药品监督管理的部门责令改正，给予警告，并处1万元以上3万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业，并处3万元以上5万元以下罚款，对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处1万元以上3万元以下罚款：  
（一）未依照本条例规定公布化妆品功效宣称依据的摘要；  
（二）未依照本条例规定建立并执行进货查验记录制度、产品销售记录制度；  
（三）未依照本条例规定对化妆品生产质量管理规范的执行情况进行自查；  
（四）未依照本条例规定贮存、运输化妆品；  
（五）未依照本条例规定监测、报告化妆品不良反应，或者对化妆品不良反应监测机构、负责药品监督管理的部门开展的化妆品不良反应调查不予配合。  
进口商未依照本条例规定记录、保存进口化妆品信息的，由出入境检验检疫机构依照前款规定给予处罚。

**Article 62** In any of the following circumstances, the drug regulatory department shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend production and business operation, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the legal representative or principal responsible person of the illegal entity, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to publish the summary of the basis for cosmetic efficacy claims in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) failing to establish and implement the incoming goods inspection and record system and the product sales record system in accordance with these Regulations;  
(3) failing to conduct self-inspection on the implementation of the cosmetics production quality management standards in accordance with these Regulations;  
(4) failing to store and transport cosmetics in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) failing to monitor and report adverse reactions to cosmetics in accordance with these Regulations, or failing to cooperate with the investigation of adverse reactions to cosmetics conducted by the adverse reaction monitoring institutions or the drug regulatory department.  
If an importer fails to record and preserve information on imported cosmetics in accordance with these Regulations, the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority shall impose a penalty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　化妆品新原料注册人、备案人未依照本条例规定报告化妆品新原料使用和安全情况的，由国务院药品监督管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销化妆品新原料注册证或者取消化妆品新原料备案，并处20万元以上50万元以下罚款。

**Article 63** If a registrant or filer of a new cosmetic ingredient fails to report the use and safety of the new cosmetic ingredient in accordance with these Regulations, the drug regulatory department of the State Council shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the certificate of registration for the new cosmetic ingredient shall be revoked or the filing of the new cosmetic ingredient shall be cancelled, and a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　在申请化妆品行政许可时提供虚假资料或者采取其他欺骗手段的，不予行政许可，已经取得行政许可的，由作出行政许可决定的部门撤销行政许可，5年内不受理其提出的化妆品相关许可申请，没收违法所得和已经生产、进口的化妆品；已经生产、进口的化妆品货值金额不足1万元的，并处5万元以上15万元以下罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额15倍以上30倍以下罚款；对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的3倍以上5倍以下罚款，终身禁止其从事化妆品生产经营活动。  
伪造、变造、出租、出借或者转让化妆品许可证件的，由负责药品监督管理的部门或者原发证部门予以收缴或者吊销，没收违法所得；违法所得不足1万元的，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；违法所得1万元以上的，并处违法所得10倍以上20倍以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** If a person provides false materials or resorts to other deceptive means when applying for an administrative license for cosmetics, the administrative license shall not be granted; if the administrative license has already been granted, the department that made the license decision shall revoke the administrative license, and no application for cosmetics-related licenses submitted by such person shall be accepted within five years; the illegal gains and the cosmetics that have been produced or imported shall be confiscated; if the value of the cosmetics that have been produced or imported is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the cosmetics shall also be imposed; the legal representative or principal responsible person of the illegal entity, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than three times but not more than five times their income obtained from the entity in the previous year, and they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in cosmetics production or trading.  
If a person forges, alters, leases, lends or transfers a cosmetics license, the drug regulatory department or the original licensing department shall confiscate or revoke the license and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　备案时提供虚假资料的，由备案部门取消备案，3年内不予办理其提出的该项备案，没收违法所得和已经生产、进口的化妆品；已经生产、进口的化妆品货值金额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上3万元以下罚款；货值金额1万元以上的，并处货值金额3倍以上10倍以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产停业直至由原发证部门吊销化妆品生产许可证，对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的1倍以上2倍以下罚款，5年内禁止其从事化妆品生产经营活动。  
已经备案的资料不符合要求的，由备案部门责令限期改正，其中，与化妆品、化妆品新原料安全性有关的备案资料不符合要求的，备案部门可以同时责令暂停销售、使用；逾期不改正的，由备案部门取消备案。  
备案部门取消备案后，仍然使用该化妆品新原料生产化妆品或者仍然上市销售、进口该普通化妆品的，分别依照[本条例第六十条](#art-60)、第六十一条的规定给予处罚。

**Article 65** If a person provides false materials when filing, the filing department shall cancel the filing, and shall not process the same filing submitted by such person within three years; the illegal gains and the cosmetics that have been produced or imported shall be confiscated; if the value of the cosmetics that have been produced or imported is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the value is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than three times but not more than ten times the value of the cosmetics shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend production and business operation until the original licensing department revokes the cosmetics production license, and the legal representative or principal responsible person of the illegal entity, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than one time but not more than two times their income obtained from the entity in the previous year, and they shall be prohibited from engaging in cosmetics production or trading for five years.  
If the filed materials do not meet the requirements, the filing department shall order correction within a specified period; if the filed materials related to the safety of cosmetics or new cosmetic ingredients do not meet the requirements, the filing department may also order the suspension of sale and use; if correction is not made within the specified period, the filing department shall cancel the filing.  
If, after the filing department cancels the filing, the new cosmetic ingredient is still used to produce cosmetics, or the ordinary cosmetics are still listed, sold or imported, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with Articles 60 and 61 of these Regulations respectively.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　化妆品集中交易市场开办者、展销会举办者未依照本条例规定履行审查、检查、制止、报告等管理义务的，由负责药品监督管理的部门处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业，并处10万元以上50万元以下罚款。

**Article 66** If the organizer of a centralized cosmetics trading market or the sponsor of a cosmetics exhibition fails to perform management obligations such as review, inspection, stopping, and reporting in accordance with these Regulations, the drug regulatory department shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend business, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　电子商务平台经营者未依照本条例规定履行实名登记、制止、报告、停止提供电子商务平台服务等管理义务的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门依照《中华人民共和国电子商务法》的规定给予处罚。

**Article 67** If an e-commerce platform operator fails to perform management obligations such as real-name registration, stopping, reporting, and ceasing to provide e-commerce platform services in accordance with these Regulations, the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Electronic Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　化妆品经营者履行了本条例规定的进货查验记录等义务，有证据证明其不知道所采购的化妆品是不符合强制性国家标准、技术规范或者不符合化妆品注册、备案资料载明的技术要求的，收缴其经营的不符合强制性国家标准、技术规范或者不符合化妆品注册、备案资料载明的技术要求的化妆品，可以免除行政处罚。

**Article 68** If a cosmetics trader has fulfilled obligations such as maintaining incoming goods inspection records as required by these Regulations, and there is evidence proving that it did not know that the cosmetics it purchased did not comply with mandatory national standards, technical specifications, or the technical requirements stated in the cosmetics registration or filing materials, the cosmetics that it trades which do not comply with mandatory national standards, technical specifications, or the technical requirements stated in the cosmetics registration or filing materials shall be confiscated, and administrative penalties may be exempted.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　化妆品广告违反本条例规定的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的规定给予处罚；采用其他方式对化妆品作虚假或者引人误解的宣传的，依照有关法律的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** If a cosmetics advertisement violates the provisions of these Regulations, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China; if other means are used to make false or misleading publicity about cosmetics, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　境外化妆品注册人、备案人指定的在我国境内的企业法人未协助开展化妆品不良反应监测、实施产品召回的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门责令改正，给予警告，并处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款，5年内禁止其法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员从事化妆品生产经营活动。  
境外化妆品注册人、备案人拒不履行依据本条例作出的行政处罚决定的，10年内禁止其化妆品进口。

**Article 70** If the enterprise legal person within the territory of China designated by an overseas cosmetics registrant or filer fails to assist in carrying out adverse reaction monitoring of cosmetics or implementing product recall, the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and its legal representative or principal responsible person, directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be prohibited from engaging in cosmetics production or trading for five years.  
If an overseas cosmetics registrant or filer refuses to comply with an administrative penalty decision made in accordance with these Regulations, the import of its cosmetics shall be prohibited for ten years.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　化妆品检验机构出具虚假检验报告的，由认证认可监督管理部门吊销检验机构资质证书，10年内不受理其资质认定申请，没收所收取的检验费用，并处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；对其法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处以其上一年度从本单位取得收入的1倍以上3倍以下罚款，依法给予或者责令给予降低岗位等级、撤职或者开除的处分，受到开除处分的，10年内禁止其从事化妆品检验工作；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** If a cosmetics testing institution issues a false testing report, the certification and accreditation regulatory department shall revoke the qualification certificate of the testing institution, shall not accept its application for qualification accreditation within ten years, confiscate the testing fees collected, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; its legal representative or principal responsible person, directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than one time but not more than three times their income obtained from the entity in the previous year, and shall be given or ordered to be given a sanction of demotion, removal from office or dismissal according to law; if a person is dismissed, he or she shall be prohibited from engaging in cosmetics testing work for ten years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　化妆品技术审评机构、化妆品不良反应监测机构和负责化妆品安全风险监测的机构未依照本条例规定履行职责，致使技术审评、不良反应监测、安全风险监测工作出现重大失误的，由负责药品监督管理的部门责令改正，给予警告，通报批评；造成严重后果的，对其法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予或者责令给予降低岗位等级、撤职或者开除的处分。

**Article 72** If a cosmetics technical review institution, a cosmetics adverse reaction monitoring institution, or an institution responsible for cosmetics safety risk monitoring fails to perform its duties in accordance with these Regulations, resulting in major errors in the technical review, adverse reaction monitoring, or safety risk monitoring work, the drug regulatory department shall order correction, give a warning, and circulate a notice of criticism; if serious consequences are caused, its legal representative or principal responsible person, directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be given or ordered to be given a sanction of demotion, removal from office or dismissal according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　化妆品生产经营者、检验机构招用、聘用不得从事化妆品生产经营活动的人员或者不得从事化妆品检验工作的人员从事化妆品生产经营或者检验的，由负责药品监督管理的部门或者其他有关部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，责令停产停业直至吊销化妆品许可证件、检验机构资质证书。

**Article 73** If a cosmetics producer or trader, or a testing institution, recruits or employs a person who is prohibited from engaging in cosmetics production or trading or from engaging in cosmetics testing work to engage in cosmetics production, trading or testing, the drug regulatory department or other relevant department shall order correction and give a warning; if the violator refuses to correct, it shall be ordered to suspend production and business operation until the cosmetics license or the qualification certificate of the testing institution is revoked.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　有下列情形之一，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）阻碍负责药品监督管理的部门工作人员依法执行职务；  
（二）伪造、销毁、隐匿证据或者隐藏、转移、变卖、损毁依法查封、扣押的物品。

**Article 74** Under any of the following circumstances, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) obstructing the personnel of the drug regulatory department from performing their duties according to law;  
(2) forging, destroying, or concealing evidence, or hiding, transferring, selling, or damaging articles that have been sealed up or seized according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　负责药品监督管理的部门工作人员违反本条例规定，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予警告、记过或者记大过的处分；造成严重后果的，依法给予降级、撤职或者开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 75** If a staff member of the drug regulatory department violates the provisions of these Regulations by abusing power, neglecting duties, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, he or she shall be given a sanction of warning, demerit recording or major demerit recording according to law; if serious consequences are caused, he or she shall be given a sanction of demotion, removal from office or dismissal according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本条例规定，造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 76** Any person who violates the provisions of these Regulations and causes personal injury, property damage or other damage shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　牙膏参照本条例有关普通化妆品的规定进行管理。牙膏备案人按照国家标准、行业标准进行功效评价后，可以宣称牙膏具有防龋、抑牙菌斑、抗牙本质敏感、减轻牙龈问题等功效。牙膏的具体管理办法由国务院药品监督管理部门拟订，报国务院市场监督管理部门审核、发布。  
香皂不适用本条例，但是宣称具有特殊化妆品功效的适用本条例。

**Article 77** Toothpaste shall be managed by reference to the provisions of these Regulations on ordinary cosmetics. After conducting efficacy evaluation in accordance with national standards or industry standards, the toothpaste filer may claim that the toothpaste has the effects of preventing caries, inhibiting dental plaque, anti-dentin hypersensitivity, alleviating gum problems, etc. The specific management measures for toothpaste shall be formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council and submitted to the market regulatory department of the State Council for review and issuance.  
Soap is not subject to these Regulations, but if it claims to have the effects of special cosmetics, these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　对本条例施行前已经注册的用于育发、脱毛、美乳、健美、除臭的化妆品自本条例施行之日起设置5年的过渡期，过渡期内可以继续生产、进口、销售，过渡期满后不得生产、进口、销售该化妆品。

**Article 78** For cosmetics that have been registered before the coming into force of these Regulations and are used for hair growth promotion, hair removal, breast enhancement, body shaping, or deodorization, a five-year transitional period shall be set from the date when these Regulations come into force; during the transitional period, such cosmetics may continue to be produced, imported and sold; after the expiry of the transitional period, such cosmetics shall not be produced, imported or sold.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　本条例所称技术规范，是指尚未制定强制性国家标准、国务院药品监督管理部门结合监督管理工作需要制定的化妆品质量安全补充技术要求。

**Article 79** For the purpose of these Regulations, "technical specifications" mean supplementary technical requirements for cosmetics quality and safety formulated by the drug regulatory department of the State Council based on the needs of supervision and administration, where mandatory national standards have not yet been established.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　本条例自2021年1月1日起施行。《化妆品卫生监督条例》同时废止。

**Article 80** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2021. The Regulations on the Supervision of Cosmetics Hygiene shall be abolished simultaneously.
