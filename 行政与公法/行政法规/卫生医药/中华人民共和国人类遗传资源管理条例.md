# 中华人民共和国人类遗传资源管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

> （2019年5月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第717号公布　根据2024年3月10日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了有效保护和合理利用我国人类遗传资源，维护公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称人类遗传资源包括人类遗传资源材料和人类遗传资源信息。 人类遗传资源材料是指含有人体基因组、基因等遗传物质的器官、组织、细胞等遗传材料。 人类遗传资源信息是指利用人类遗传资源材料产生的数据等信息资料。 第三条　采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，应当遵守本条例。 为临床诊疗、采供血服务、查处违法犯罪、兴奋剂检测和殡葬等活动需要，采集、保藏器官、组织、细胞等人体物质及开展相关活动，依照相关法律、行政法规规定执行。 第四条　国务院卫生健康主管部门负责全国人类遗传资源管理工作；国务院其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关人类遗传资源管理工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门负责本行政区域人类遗传资源管理工作；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责本行政区域有关人类遗传资源管理工作。 第五条　国家加强对我国人类遗传资源的保护，开展人类遗传资源调查，对重要遗传家系和特定地区人类遗传资源实行申报登记制度。 国务院卫生健康主管部门负责组织我国人类遗传资源调查，制定重要遗传家系和特定地区人类遗传资源申报登记具体办法。 第六条　国家支持合理利用人类遗传资源开展科学研究、发展生物医药产业、提高诊疗技术，提高我国生物安全保障能力，提升人民健康保障水平。 第七条　外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构不得在我国境内采集、保藏我国人类遗传资源，不得向境外提供我国人类遗传资源。 第八条　采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，不得危害我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了有效保护和合理利用我国人类遗传资源，维护公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of effectively protecting and rationally utilizing human genetic resources of China, and safeguarding public health, national security and the public interest.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称人类遗传资源包括人类遗传资源材料和人类遗传资源信息。  
人类遗传资源材料是指含有人体基因组、基因等遗传物质的器官、组织、细胞等遗传材料。  
人类遗传资源信息是指利用人类遗传资源材料产生的数据等信息资料。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, human genetic resources include human genetic resource materials and human genetic resource information.  
Human genetic resource materials refer to genetic materials such as organs, tissues, and cells that contain genetic materials including the human genome and genes.  
Human genetic resource information refers to data and other information generated from the use of human genetic resource materials.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，应当遵守本条例。  
为临床诊疗、采供血服务、查处违法犯罪、兴奋剂检测和殡葬等活动需要，采集、保藏器官、组织、细胞等人体物质及开展相关活动，依照相关法律、行政法规规定执行。

**Article 3** The collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
The collection and preservation of human substances such as organs, tissues, and cells, and the conduct of related activities, for the purposes of clinical diagnosis and treatment, blood collection and supply services, investigation and punishment of illegal and criminal acts, doping testing, and funeral and interment, shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院卫生健康主管部门负责全国人类遗传资源管理工作；国务院其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关人类遗传资源管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门负责本行政区域人类遗传资源管理工作；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责本行政区域有关人类遗传资源管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent health department of the State Council shall be responsible for the management of human genetic resources nationwide; other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the management of human genetic resources within their respective functions and duties.  
The competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the management of human genetic resources within their respective administrative regions; other relevant departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the management of human genetic resources within their respective administrative regions and within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家加强对我国人类遗传资源的保护，开展人类遗传资源调查，对重要遗传家系和特定地区人类遗传资源实行申报登记制度。  
国务院卫生健康主管部门负责组织我国人类遗传资源调查，制定重要遗传家系和特定地区人类遗传资源申报登记具体办法。

**Article 5** The State shall strengthen the protection of human genetic resources of China, conduct surveys of human genetic resources, and implement a declaration and registration system for important genetic pedigrees and human genetic resources in specific regions.  
The competent health department of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the survey of human genetic resources of China and formulating specific measures for the declaration and registration of important genetic pedigrees and human genetic resources in specific regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家支持合理利用人类遗传资源开展科学研究、发展生物医药产业、提高诊疗技术，提高我国生物安全保障能力，提升人民健康保障水平。

**Article 6** The State shall support the rational use of human genetic resources to conduct scientific research, develop the biomedical industry, improve diagnosis and treatment technologies, enhance China's biosecurity capabilities, and raise the level of health protection for the people.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构不得在我国境内采集、保藏我国人类遗传资源，不得向境外提供我国人类遗传资源。

**Article 7** Foreign organizations, individuals, and institutions established or actually controlled by them shall not collect or preserve human genetic resources of China within the territory of China, and shall not provide human genetic resources of China to foreign countries.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，不得危害我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益。

**Article 8** The collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China shall not endanger public health, national security, or the public interest of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，应当符合伦理原则，并按照国家有关规定进行伦理审查。  
采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，应当尊重人类遗传资源提供者的隐私权，取得其事先知情同意，并保护其合法权益。  
采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源，应当遵守国务院卫生健康主管部门制定的技术规范。

**Article 9** The collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China shall conform to ethical principles and be subject to ethical review in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China shall respect the privacy rights of the providers of human genetic resources, obtain their prior informed consent, and protect their lawful rights and interests.  
The collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China shall comply with the technical specifications formulated by the competent health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　禁止买卖人类遗传资源。  
为科学研究依法提供或者使用人类遗传资源并支付或者收取合理成本费用，不视为买卖。

**Article 10** The purchase or sale of human genetic resources is prohibited.  
The provision or use of human genetic resources for scientific research in accordance with law and the payment or receipt of reasonable costs and expenses shall not be deemed as purchase or sale.

### 第二章　采集和保藏

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　采集我国重要遗传家系、特定地区人类遗传资源或者采集国务院卫生健康主管部门规定种类、数量的人类遗传资源的，应当符合下列条件，并经国务院卫生健康主管部门批准：  
（一）具有法人资格；  
（二）采集目的明确、合法；  
（三）采集方案合理；  
（四）通过伦理审查；  
（五）具有负责人类遗传资源管理的部门和管理制度；  
（六）具有与采集活动相适应的场所、设施、设备和人员。

**Article 11** Anyone that collects important genetic pedigrees, human genetic resources in specific regions, or human genetic resources of such types and quantities as prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council shall meet the following conditions and obtain the approval of the competent health department of the State Council:  
(1) possessing the status of a legal person;  
(2) having a clear and lawful collection purpose;  
(3) having a reasonable collection plan;  
(4) having passed ethical review;  
(5) having a department responsible for human genetic resources management and relevant management systems;  
(6) having premises, facilities, equipment, and personnel commensurate with the collection activity.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　采集我国人类遗传资源，应当事先告知人类遗传资源提供者采集目的、采集用途、对健康可能产生的影响、个人隐私保护措施及其享有的自愿参与和随时无条件退出的权利，征得人类遗传资源提供者书面同意。  
在告知人类遗传资源提供者前款规定的信息时，必须全面、完整、真实、准确，不得隐瞒、误导、欺骗。

**Article 12** Before collecting human genetic resources of China, the collector shall inform the provider of human genetic resources of the purpose and use of the collection, the possible impact on health, the measures for personal privacy protection, and the provider's right to voluntarily participate and withdraw at any time unconditionally, and shall obtain the written consent of the provider.  
When informing the provider of human genetic resources of the information specified in the preceding paragraph, the collector must provide it comprehensively, completely, truthfully, and accurately, and shall not conceal, mislead, or deceive.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家加强人类遗传资源保藏工作，加快标准化、规范化的人类遗传资源保藏基础平台和人类遗传资源大数据建设，为开展相关研究开发活动提供支撑。  
国家鼓励科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构、企业根据自身条件和相关研究开发活动需要开展人类遗传资源保藏工作，并为其他单位开展相关研究开发活动提供便利。

**Article 13** The State shall strengthen the preservation of human genetic resources, accelerate the construction of standardized and normalized basic platforms for human genetic resource preservation and big data on human genetic resources, and provide support for relevant research and development activities.  
The State shall encourage scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, medical institutions, and enterprises to carry out the preservation of human genetic resources according to their own conditions and the needs of relevant research and development activities, and to provide convenience for other entities to carry out relevant research and development activities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　保藏我国人类遗传资源、为科学研究提供基础平台的，应当符合下列条件，并经国务院卫生健康主管部门批准：  
（一）具有法人资格；  
（二）保藏目的明确、合法；  
（三）保藏方案合理；  
（四）拟保藏的人类遗传资源来源合法；  
（五）通过伦理审查；  
（六）具有负责人类遗传资源管理的部门和保藏管理制度；  
（七）具有符合国家人类遗传资源保藏技术规范和要求的场所、设施、设备和人员。

**Article 14** Anyone that preserves human genetic resources of China or provides a basic platform for scientific research shall meet the following conditions and obtain the approval of the competent health department of the State Council:  
(1) possessing the status of a legal person;  
(2) having a clear and lawful preservation purpose;  
(3) having a reasonable preservation plan;  
(4) having a lawful source of the human genetic resources to be preserved;  
(5) having passed ethical review;  
(6) having a department responsible for human genetic resources management and a preservation management system;  
(7) having premises, facilities, equipment, and personnel that comply with the national technical specifications and requirements for the preservation of human genetic resources.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　保藏单位应当对所保藏的人类遗传资源加强管理和监测，采取安全措施，制定应急预案，确保保藏、使用安全。  
保藏单位应当完整记录人类遗传资源保藏情况，妥善保存人类遗传资源的来源信息和使用信息，确保人类遗传资源的合法使用。  
保藏单位应当就本单位保藏人类遗传资源情况向国务院卫生健康主管部门提交年度报告。

**Article 15** The preservation entity shall strengthen the management and monitoring of the human genetic resources it preserves, take safety measures, formulate emergency plans, and ensure the safety of preservation and use.  
The preservation entity shall fully record the preservation of human genetic resources, properly preserve the source information and use information of human genetic resources, and ensure the lawful use of human genetic resources.  
The preservation entity shall submit an annual report to the competent health department of the State Council on the preservation of human genetic resources by the entity.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家人类遗传资源保藏基础平台和数据库应当依照国家有关规定向有关科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构、企业开放。  
为公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益需要，国家可以依法使用保藏单位保藏的人类遗传资源。

**Article 16** The national basic platforms for human genetic resource preservation and databases shall be opened to relevant scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, medical institutions, and enterprises in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For the needs of public health, national security, and the public interest, the State may use human genetic resources preserved by preservation entities in accordance with law.

### 第三章　利用和对外提供

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门对利用人类遗传资源开展科学研究、发展生物医药产业统筹规划，合理布局，加强创新体系建设，促进生物科技和产业创新、协调发展。

**Article 17** The competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, make comprehensive planning and rational layout for the use of human genetic resources to conduct scientific research and develop the biomedical industry, strengthen the construction of the innovation system, and promote innovation and coordinated development of biotechnology and industry.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构、企业利用人类遗传资源开展研究开发活动，对其研究开发活动以及成果的产业化依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定予以支持。

**Article 18** Scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, medical institutions, and enterprises that use human genetic resources to carry out research and development activities shall be supported in their research and development activities and the industrialization of their achievements in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构、企业根据自身条件和相关研究开发活动需要，利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，提升相关研究开发能力和水平。

**Article 19** The State shall encourage scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, medical institutions, and enterprises, according to their own conditions and the needs of relevant research and development activities, to use human genetic resources of China to carry out international cooperative scientific research and enhance the relevant research and development capabilities and levels.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展生物技术研究开发活动或者开展临床试验的，应当遵守有关生物技术研究、临床应用管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。

**Article 20** Anyone that uses human genetic resources of China to carry out biotechnology research and development activities or to conduct clinical trials shall comply with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State on the management of biotechnology research and clinical application.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　外国组织及外国组织、个人设立或者实际控制的机构（以下称外方单位）需要利用我国人类遗传资源开展科学研究活动的，应当遵守我国法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，并采取与我国科研机构、高等学校、医疗机构、企业（以下称中方单位）合作的方式进行。

**Article 21** Where foreign organizations and institutions established or actually controlled by foreign organizations or individuals (hereinafter referred to as foreign entities) need to use human genetic resources of China to conduct scientific research activities, they shall comply with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of China, and shall carry out such activities through cooperation with scientific research institutions, institutions of higher education, medical institutions, and enterprises of China (hereinafter referred to as Chinese entities).

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究的，应当符合下列条件，并由合作双方共同提出申请，经国务院卫生健康主管部门批准：  
（一）对我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益没有危害；  
（二）合作双方为具有法人资格的中方单位、外方单位，并具有开展相关工作的基础和能力；  
（三）合作研究目的和内容明确、合法，期限合理；  
（四）合作研究方案合理；  
（五）拟使用的人类遗传资源来源合法，种类、数量与研究内容相符；  
（六）通过合作双方各自所在国（地区）的伦理审查；  
（七）研究成果归属明确，有合理明确的利益分配方案。  
为获得相关药品和医疗器械在我国上市许可，在临床机构利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作临床试验、不涉及人类遗传资源材料出境的，不需要审批。但是，合作双方在开展临床试验前应当将拟使用的人类遗传资源种类、数量及其用途向国务院卫生健康主管部门备案。国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门加强对备案事项的监管。

**Article 22** Anyone that uses human genetic resources of China to carry out international cooperative scientific research shall meet the following conditions, and the cooperating parties shall jointly file an application for approval by the competent health department of the State Council:  
(1) no harm to public health, national security, or the public interest of China;  
(2) both cooperating parties are Chinese entities and foreign entities with the status of a legal person and have the foundation and capability to carry out the relevant work;  
(3) the purpose and content of the cooperative research are clear and lawful, and the period is reasonable;  
(4) the cooperative research plan is reasonable;  
(5) the source of the human genetic resources to be used is lawful, and the types and quantities are consistent with the research content;  
(6) ethical review has been passed in the respective countries (regions) of both cooperating parties;  
(7) the ownership of research results is clear, and there is a reasonable and clear plan for benefit sharing.  
Where international cooperative clinical trials are conducted in clinical institutions using human genetic resources of China to obtain marketing authorization for relevant drugs and medical devices in China, and no human genetic resource materials are to be shipped out of the country, approval is not required. However, before conducting the clinical trials, the cooperating parties shall file with the competent health department of the State Council the types, quantities, and uses of the human genetic resources to be used. The competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen the supervision of matters filed.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究过程中，合作方、研究目的、研究内容、合作期限等重大事项发生变更的，应当办理变更审批手续。

**Article 23** Where major matters such as the cooperating parties, research purpose, research content, or cooperation period are changed during the process of using human genetic resources of China to carry out international cooperative scientific research, the formalities for approval of the modification shall be completed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，应当保证中方单位及其研究人员在合作期间全过程、实质性地参与研究，研究过程中的所有记录以及数据信息等完全向中方单位开放并向中方单位提供备份。  
利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，产生的成果申请专利的，应当由合作双方共同提出申请，专利权归合作双方共有。研究产生的其他科技成果，其使用权、转让权和利益分享办法由合作双方通过合作协议约定；协议没有约定的，合作双方都有使用的权利，但向第三方转让须经合作双方同意，所获利益按合作双方贡献大小分享。

**Article 24** Where human genetic resources of China are used to carry out international cooperative scientific research, the Chinese entities and their researchers shall be guaranteed to participate fully and substantively in the research throughout the cooperation period, and all records and data information during the research process shall be fully open to the Chinese entities and backup copies provided to the Chinese entities.  
Where a patent application is filed for achievements arising from international cooperative scientific research using human genetic resources of China, the application shall be jointly filed by both cooperating parties, and the patent right shall be jointly owned by both cooperating parties. For other scientific and technological achievements arising from the research, the right to use, the right to transfer, and the method of benefit sharing shall be agreed upon by both cooperating parties through a cooperation agreement; if no agreement is made, both cooperating parties have the right to use the achievements, but transfer to a third party shall require the consent of both cooperating parties, and the benefits obtained shall be shared according to the contributions of both cooperating parties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，合作双方应当按照平等互利、诚实信用、共同参与、共享成果的原则，依法签订合作协议，并依照[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)的规定对相关事项作出明确、具体的约定。

**Article 25** Where human genetic resources of China are used to carry out international cooperative scientific research, both cooperating parties shall sign a cooperation agreement in accordance with law on the principles of equality and mutual benefit, good faith, joint participation, and sharing of results, and shall make clear and specific agreements on relevant matters in accordance with Article 24 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，合作双方应当在国际合作活动结束后6个月内共同向国务院卫生健康主管部门提交合作研究情况报告。

**Article 26** Where human genetic resources of China are used to carry out international cooperative scientific research, both cooperating parties shall jointly submit a report on the cooperative research to the competent health department of the State Council within six months after the conclusion of the international cooperation activity.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，或者因其他特殊情况确需将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境的，应当符合下列条件，并取得国务院卫生健康主管部门出具的人类遗传资源材料出境证明：  
（一）对我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益没有危害；  
（二）具有法人资格；  
（三）有明确的境外合作方和合理的出境用途；  
（四）人类遗传资源材料采集合法或者来自合法的保藏单位；  
（五）通过伦理审查。  
利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究，需要将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境的，可以单独提出申请，也可以在开展国际合作科学研究申请中列明出境计划一并提出申请，由国务院卫生健康主管部门合并审批。  
将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境的，凭人类遗传资源材料出境证明办理海关手续。

**Article 27** Anyone that uses human genetic resources of China to carry out international cooperative scientific research, or that truly needs to transport, mail, or carry human genetic resource materials of China out of the country due to other special circumstances, shall meet the following conditions and obtain a certificate for the exit of human genetic resource materials issued by the competent health department of the State Council:  
(1) no harm to public health, national security, or the public interest of China;  
(2) possessing the status of a legal person;  
(3) having a clear foreign cooperating party and a reasonable purpose for exit;  
(4) the human genetic resource materials are collected lawfully or come from a lawful preservation entity;  
(5) having passed ethical review.  
Where human genetic resources of China are used to carry out international cooperative scientific research and it is necessary to transport, mail, or carry human genetic resource materials of China out of the country, an application may be filed separately, or the exit plan may be listed in the application for international cooperative scientific research and filed jointly, and the competent health department of the State Council shall examine and approve them in a consolidated manner.  
Anyone that transports, mails, or carries human genetic resource materials of China out of the country shall complete customs formalities on the strength of the certificate for the exit of human genetic resource materials.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　将人类遗传资源信息向外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构提供或者开放使用，不得危害我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益；可能影响我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益的，应当通过国务院卫生健康主管部门组织的安全审查。  
将人类遗传资源信息向外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构提供或者开放使用的，应当向国务院卫生健康主管部门备案并提交信息备份。  
利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究产生的人类遗传资源信息，合作双方可以使用。

**Article 28** The provision or open use of human genetic resource information to foreign organizations, individuals, and institutions established or actually controlled by them shall not endanger public health, national security, or the public interest of China; where it may affect public health, national security, or the public interest of China, a security review organized by the competent health department of the State Council shall be passed.  
Anyone that provides or opens the use of human genetic resource information to foreign organizations, individuals, and institutions established or actually controlled by them shall file with the competent health department of the State Council and submit a backup of the information.  
Both cooperating parties may use human genetic resource information generated from international cooperative scientific research using human genetic resources of China.

### 第四章　服务和监督

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国务院卫生健康主管部门应当加强电子政务建设，方便申请人利用互联网办理审批、备案等事项。

**Article 29** The competent health department of the State Council shall strengthen the construction of e-government to facilitate applicants in handling matters such as approval and filing via the Internet.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院卫生健康主管部门应当制定并及时发布有关采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源的审批指南和示范文本，加强对申请人办理有关审批、备案等事项的指导。

**Article 30** The competent health department of the State Council shall formulate and publish in a timely manner approval guidelines and model documents concerning the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China, and shall strengthen guidance for applicants in handling relevant approval, filing, and other matters.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院卫生健康主管部门应当聘请生物技术、医药、卫生、伦理、法律等方面的专家组成专家评审委员会，对依照本条例规定提出的采集、保藏我国人类遗传资源，开展国际合作科学研究以及将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境的申请进行技术评审。评审意见作为作出审批决定的参考依据。

**Article 31** The competent health department of the State Council shall engage experts in fields such as biotechnology, medicine, health, ethics, and law to form an expert review committee to conduct technical reviews of applications for the collection and preservation of human genetic resources of China, the conduct of international cooperative scientific research, and the transport, mailing, or carrying of human genetic resource materials of China out of the country as provided for in these Regulations. The review opinions shall serve as a reference basis for making approval decisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院卫生健康主管部门应当自受理依照本条例规定提出的采集、保藏我国人类遗传资源，开展国际合作科学研究以及将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境申请之日起20个工作日内，作出批准或者不予批准的决定；不予批准的，应当说明理由。因特殊原因无法在规定期限内作出审批决定的，经国务院卫生健康主管部门负责人批准，可以延长10个工作日。

**Article 32** The competent health department of the State Council shall make a decision to approve or not to approve an application for the collection and preservation of human genetic resources of China, the conduct of international cooperative scientific research, and the transport, mailing, or carrying of human genetic resource materials of China out of the country within 20 working days from the date of acceptance of the application; if the application is not approved, the reasons shall be stated. Where an approval decision cannot be made within the prescribed time limit due to special reasons, the time limit may be extended by 10 working days with the approval of the person in charge of the competent health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门应当加强对采集、保藏、利用、对外提供人类遗传资源活动各环节的监督检查，发现违反本条例规定的，及时依法予以处理并向社会公布检查、处理结果。

**Article 33** The competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen supervision and inspection over all aspects of activities involving the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources; where any violation of these Regulations is discovered, they shall handle it in a timely manner in accordance with law and announce the results of the inspection and handling to the public.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门进行监督检查，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入现场检查；  
（二）询问相关人员；  
（三）查阅、复制有关资料；  
（四）查封、扣押有关人类遗传资源。

**Article 34** The competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may take the following measures when conducting supervision and inspection:  
(1) entering the site for inspection;  
(2) inquiring of relevant persons;  
(3) consulting and copying relevant materials;  
(4) sealing up or seizing relevant human genetic resources.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门投诉、举报。  
国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门应当公布投诉、举报电话和电子邮件地址，接受相关投诉、举报。对查证属实的，给予举报人奖励。

**Article 35** Any entity or individual shall have the right to complain or report any act violating the provisions of these Regulations to the competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The competent health department of the State Council and the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall publish complaint and reporting telephone numbers and email addresses to receive relevant complaints and reports. Where a report is verified to be true, a reward shall be given to the reporter.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法采集、保藏的人类遗传资源和违法所得，处50万元以上500万元以下罚款，违法所得在100万元以上的，处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下罚款：  
（一）未经批准，采集我国重要遗传家系、特定地区人类遗传资源，或者采集国务院卫生健康主管部门规定种类、数量的人类遗传资源；  
（二）未经批准，保藏我国人类遗传资源；  
（三）未经批准，利用我国人类遗传资源开展国际合作科学研究；  
（四）未通过安全审查，将可能影响我国公众健康、国家安全和社会公共利益的人类遗传资源信息向外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构提供或者开放使用；  
（五）开展国际合作临床试验前未将拟使用的人类遗传资源种类、数量及其用途向国务院卫生健康主管部门备案。

**Article 36** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent health department of the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally collected or preserved human genetic resources and any illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5 million yuan; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 1 million yuan, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed:  
(1) collecting important genetic pedigrees of China, human genetic resources in specific regions, or human genetic resources of such types and quantities as prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council without approval;  
(2) preserving human genetic resources of China without approval;  
(3) using human genetic resources of China to carry out international cooperative scientific research without approval;  
(4) providing or opening the use of human genetic resource information that may affect public health, national security, or the public interest of China to foreign organizations, individuals, and institutions established or actually controlled by them without passing a security review;  
(5) failing to file with the competent health department of the State Council the types, quantities, and uses of human genetic resources to be used before conducting international cooperative clinical trials.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　提供虚假材料或者采取其他欺骗手段取得行政许可的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门撤销已经取得的行政许可，处50万元以上500万元以下罚款，5年内不受理相关责任人及单位提出的许可申请。

**Article 37** Where an administrative license is obtained by providing false materials or by other deceptive means, the competent health department of the State Council shall revoke the obtained administrative license, impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 5 million yuan, and shall not accept any application for a license filed by the responsible person or the entity within five years.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，未经批准将我国人类遗传资源材料运送、邮寄、携带出境的，由海关依照法律、行政法规的规定处罚。人类遗传资源主管部门应当配合海关开展鉴定等执法协助工作。海关应当将依法没收的人类遗传资源材料移送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门进行处理。

**Article 38** Where human genetic resource materials of China are transported, mailed, or carried out of the country without approval in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the customs shall impose a penalty in accordance with laws and administrative regulations. The competent departments for human genetic resources shall cooperate with the customs in carrying out law enforcement assistance work such as expert evaluation. The customs shall transfer the human genetic resource materials confiscated in accordance with law to the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for disposal.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门责令停止开展相关活动，没收违法采集、保藏的人类遗传资源和违法所得，处50万元以上100万元以下罚款，违法所得在100万元以上的，处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下罚款：  
（一）采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源未通过伦理审查；  
（二）采集我国人类遗传资源未经人类遗传资源提供者事先知情同意，或者采取隐瞒、误导、欺骗等手段取得人类遗传资源提供者同意；  
（三）采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源违反相关技术规范；  
（四）将人类遗传资源信息向外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构提供或者开放使用，未向国务院卫生健康主管部门备案或者提交信息备份。

**Article 39** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall order the cessation of the relevant activities, confiscate the illegally collected or preserved human genetic resources and any illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 yuan but not more than RMB 1 million yuan; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 1 million yuan, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed:  
(1) collecting, preserving, utilizing, or externally providing human genetic resources of China without passing ethical review;  
(2) collecting human genetic resources of China without obtaining prior informed consent from the provider, or obtaining the consent of the provider by means of concealment, misrepresentation, or deception;  
(3) collecting, preserving, utilizing, or externally providing human genetic resources of China in violation of the relevant technical specifications;  
(4) providing or opening the use of human genetic resource information to foreign organizations, individuals, and institutions established or actually controlled by them without filing with the competent health department of the State Council or submitting a backup of the information.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门责令改正，给予警告，可以处50万元以下罚款：  
（一）保藏我国人类遗传资源过程中未完整记录并妥善保存人类遗传资源的来源信息和使用信息；  
（二）保藏我国人类遗传资源未提交年度报告；  
（三）开展国际合作科学研究未及时提交合作研究情况报告。

**Article 40** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent health department of the State Council shall order rectification, give a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than RMB 500,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to fully record and properly preserve the source information and use information of human genetic resources during the preservation of human genetic resources of China;  
(2) failing to submit an annual report on the preservation of human genetic resources of China;  
(3) failing to submit a report on the cooperative research in a timely manner when carrying out international cooperative scientific research.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　外国组织、个人及其设立或者实际控制的机构违反本条例规定，在我国境内采集、保藏我国人类遗传资源，利用我国人类遗传资源开展科学研究，或者向境外提供我国人类遗传资源的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法采集、保藏的人类遗传资源和违法所得，处100万元以上1000万元以下罚款，违法所得在100万元以上的，处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下罚款。

**Article 41** Where foreign organizations, individuals, or institutions established or actually controlled by them, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, collect or preserve human genetic resources of China within the territory of China, use human genetic resources of China to conduct scientific research, or provide human genetic resources of China to foreign countries, the competent health department of the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally collected or preserved human genetic resources and any illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1 million yuan but not more than RMB 10 million yuan; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 1 million yuan, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，买卖人类遗传资源的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法采集、保藏的人类遗传资源和违法所得，处100万元以上1000万元以下罚款，违法所得在100万元以上的，处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下罚款。

**Article 42** Where anyone purchases or sells human genetic resources in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the competent health department of the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally collected or preserved human genetic resources and any illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1 million yuan but not more than RMB 10 million yuan; if the illegal gains exceed RMB 1 million yuan, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　对有[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)、第三十九条、第四十一条、第四十二条规定违法行为的单位，情节严重的，由国务院卫生健康主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门依据职责禁止其1至5年内从事采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源的活动；情节特别严重的，永久禁止其从事采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源的活动。  
对有[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)至第三十九条、第四十一条、第四十二条规定违法行为的单位的法定代表人、主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员以及其他责任人员，依法给予处分，并由国务院卫生健康主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门依据职责没收其违法所得，处50万元以下罚款；情节严重的，禁止其1至5年内从事采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源的活动；情节特别严重的，永久禁止其从事采集、保藏、利用、对外提供我国人类遗传资源的活动。  
单位和个人有本条例规定违法行为的，记入信用记录，并依照有关法律、行政法规的规定向社会公示。

**Article 43** Where an entity commits an illegal act as specified in Article 36, 39, 41, or 42 of these Regulations, if the circumstances are serious, the competent health department of the State Council or the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their functions and duties, prohibit it from engaging in the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China for a period of one to five years; if the circumstances are especially serious, it shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China.  
With respect to the legal representative, principal person in charge, directly responsible supervisor, and other responsible persons of an entity that commits an illegal act as specified in Articles 36 to 39, 41, or 42 of these Regulations, a sanction shall be imposed in accordance with law, and the competent health department of the State Council or the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to their functions and duties, confiscate their illegal gains and impose a fine of not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be prohibited from engaging in the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China for a period of one to five years; if the circumstances are especially serious, they shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in the collection, preservation, utilization, and external provision of human genetic resources of China.  
Where an entity or individual commits an illegal act as provided for in these Regulations, it shall be recorded in the credit record and publicly disclosed to the public in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，侵害他人合法权益的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of others, it shall bear civil liability in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院卫生健康主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府人类遗传资源主管部门的工作人员违反本条例规定，不履行职责或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where any staff member of the competent health department of the State Council or the competent departments for human genetic resources of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to perform their duties, or abuses their power, neglects their duties, or engages in favoritism and malpractice, a sanction shall be imposed in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　人类遗传资源相关信息属于国家秘密的，应当依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》和国家其他有关保密规定实施保密管理。

**Article 46** Where information related to human genetic resources falls within State secrets, confidentiality management shall be implemented in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and other relevant State secrecy provisions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例自2019年7月1日起施行。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2019.
