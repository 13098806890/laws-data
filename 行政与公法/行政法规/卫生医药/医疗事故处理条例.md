# 医疗事故处理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2002-04-04  
**生效日期**：2002-09-01

> (2002年2月20日国务院第55次常务会议通过　2002年4月4日中华人民共和国国务院令第351号公布　自2002年9月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了正确处理医疗事故，保护患者和医疗机构及其医务人员的合法权益，维护医疗秩序，保障医疗安全，促进医学科学的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称医疗事故，是指医疗机构及其医务人员在医疗活动中，违反医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规，过失造成患者人身损害的事故。 第三条　处理医疗事故，应当遵循公开、公平、公正、及时、便民的原则，坚持实事求是的科学态度，做到事实清楚、定性准确、责任明确、处理恰当。 第四条　根据对患者人身造成的损害程度，医疗事故分为四级： 一级医疗事故：造成患者死亡、重度残疾的； 二级医疗事故：造成患者中度残疾、器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的； 三级医疗事故：造成患者轻度残疾、器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的； 四级医疗事故：造成患者明显人身损害的其他后果的。 具体分级标准由国务院卫生行政部门制定。 第二章　医疗事故的预防与处置 第五条　医疗机构及其医务人员在医疗活动中，必须严格遵守医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规，恪守医疗服务职业道德。 第六条　医疗机构应当对其医务人员进行医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规的培训和医疗服务职业道德教育。 第七条　医疗机构应当设置医疗服务质量监控部门或者配备专(兼)职人员，具体负责监督本医疗机构的医务人员的医疗服务工作，检查医务人员执业情况，接受患者对医疗服务的投诉，向其提供咨询服务。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了正确处理医疗事故，保护患者和医疗机构及其医务人员的合法权益，维护医疗秩序，保障医疗安全，促进医学科学的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of correctly handling medical accidents, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of patients, medical institutions and their medical personnel, maintaining medical order, ensuring medical safety, and promoting the development of medical science.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称医疗事故，是指医疗机构及其医务人员在医疗活动中，违反医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规，过失造成患者人身损害的事故。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, a medical accident means an accident in which a medical institution or its medical personnel, in medical activities, negligently causes personal injury to a patient by violating laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and norms and routines of diagnosis, treatment and nursing care on health management.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　处理医疗事故，应当遵循公开、公平、公正、及时、便民的原则，坚持实事求是的科学态度，做到事实清楚、定性准确、责任明确、处理恰当。

**Article 3** In handling medical accidents, the principles of openness, fairness, justice, timeliness and convenience for the people shall be followed, a scientific attitude of seeking truth from facts shall be adhered to, and the facts shall be clear, the nature shall be accurate, the liability shall be definite, and the handling shall be appropriate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　根据对患者人身造成的损害程度，医疗事故分为四级：  
一级医疗事故：造成患者死亡、重度残疾的；  
二级医疗事故：造成患者中度残疾、器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的；  
三级医疗事故：造成患者轻度残疾、器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
四级医疗事故：造成患者明显人身损害的其他后果的。  
具体分级标准由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 4** Medical accidents are classified into four levels according to the degree of personal injury caused to the patient:  
Level I medical accident: causing death or severe disability to the patient;  
Level II medical accident: causing moderate disability, or organ or tissue injury resulting in severe functional impairment to the patient;  
Level III medical accident: causing mild disability, or organ or tissue injury resulting in general functional impairment to the patient;  
Level IV medical accident: causing other consequences of obvious personal injury to the patient.  
The specific classification standards shall be formulated by the health administration department of the State Council.

### 第二章　医疗事故的预防与处置

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　医疗机构及其医务人员在医疗活动中，必须严格遵守医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规，恪守医疗服务职业道德。

**Article 5** Medical institutions and their medical personnel must strictly abide by the laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and the norms and routines of diagnosis, treatment and nursing care on health management, and scrupulously observe the professional ethics of medical services.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　医疗机构应当对其医务人员进行医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规的培训和医疗服务职业道德教育。

**Article 6** Medical institutions shall provide training on the laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and the norms and routines of diagnosis, treatment and nursing care on health management, and education on the professional ethics of medical services, to their medical personnel.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　医疗机构应当设置医疗服务质量监控部门或者配备专(兼)职人员，具体负责监督本医疗机构的医务人员的医疗服务工作，检查医务人员执业情况，接受患者对医疗服务的投诉，向其提供咨询服务。

**Article 7** Medical institutions shall set up a department for monitoring the quality of medical services or assign full-time (or part-time) personnel to be specifically responsible for supervising the medical service work of the medical personnel of the institution, inspecting the practice of medical personnel, accepting complaints from patients about medical services, and providing advisory services to them.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　医疗机构应当按照国务院卫生行政部门规定的要求，书写并妥善保管病历资料。  
因抢救急危患者，未能及时书写病历的，有关医务人员应当在抢救结束后6小时内据实补记，并加以注明。

**Article 8** Medical institutions shall write and properly keep medical records in accordance with the requirements prescribed by the health administration department of the State Council.  
Where medical records are not written in a timely manner due to emergency rescue of a critical patient, the relevant medical personnel shall make supplementary records truthfully within 6 hours after the rescue is completed, and shall indicate the reason.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　严禁涂改、伪造、隐匿、销毁或者抢夺病历资料。

**Article 9** It is strictly prohibited to alter, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical records.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　患者有权复印或者复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单(检验报告)、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录单、病理资料、护理记录以及国务院卫生行政部门规定的其他病历资料。  
患者依照前款规定要求复印或者复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复印或者复制服务并在复印或者复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记。复印或者复制病历资料时，应当有患者在场。  
医疗机构应患者的要求，为其复印或者复制病历资料，可以按照规定收取工本费。具体收费标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门会同同级卫生行政部门规定。

**Article 10** Patients have the right to photocopy or duplicate their outpatient medical records, inpatient admission records, temperature charts, medical orders sheets, laboratory test sheets (test reports), medical imaging examination materials, special examination consent forms, surgical consent forms, surgical and anesthesia records, pathological materials, nursing records, and other medical records as prescribed by the health administration department of the State Council.  
Where a patient requests photocopying or duplication of medical records in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the medical institution shall provide photocopying or duplication services and affix a certification stamp on the photocopied or duplicated medical records. The patient shall be present when the medical records are photocopied or duplicated.  
When photocopying or duplicating medical records at the request of a patient, a medical institution may charge a cost price according to the relevant provisions. The specific charging standards shall be formulated by the competent department of price of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the health administration department at the same level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在医疗活动中，医疗机构及其医务人员应当将患者的病情、医疗措施、医疗风险等如实告知患者，及时解答其咨询；但是，应当避免对患者产生不利后果。

**Article 11** In medical activities, medical institutions and their medical personnel shall truthfully inform patients of their conditions, medical measures, medical risks, etc., and answer their inquiries in a timely manner; however, they shall avoid causing adverse consequences to the patients.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　医疗机构应当制定防范、处理医疗事故的预案，预防医疗事故的发生，减轻医疗事故的损害。

**Article 12** Medical institutions shall formulate plans for preventing and handling medical accidents, prevent the occurrence of medical accidents, and mitigate the harm of medical accidents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　医务人员在医疗活动中发生或者发现医疗事故、可能引起医疗事故的医疗过失行为或者发生医疗事故争议的，应当立即向所在科室负责人报告，科室负责人应当及时向本医疗机构负责医疗服务质量监控的部门或者专(兼)职人员报告；负责医疗服务质量监控的部门或者专(兼)职人员接到报告后，应当立即进行调查、核实，将有关情况如实向本医疗机构的负责人报告，并向患者通报、解释。

**Article 13** Where a medical accident, a medical negligent act that may cause a medical accident, or a medical accident dispute occurs or is discovered in medical activities by medical personnel, they shall immediately report to the person in charge of the department they belong to; the person in charge of the department shall promptly report to the department responsible for monitoring the quality of medical services of the medical institution or the full-time (or part-time) personnel; after receiving the report, the department or the full-time (or part-time) personnel responsible for monitoring the quality of medical services shall immediately investigate and verify the situation, truthfully report the relevant circumstances to the person in charge of the medical institution, and notify and explain to the patient.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　发生医疗事故的，医疗机构应当按照规定向所在地卫生行政部门报告。  
发生下列重大医疗过失行为的，医疗机构应当在12小时内向所在地卫生行政部门报告：  
(一)导致患者死亡或者可能为二级以上的医疗事故；  
(二)导致3人以上人身损害后果；  
(三)国务院卫生行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 14** Where a medical accident occurs, the medical institution shall report to the local health administration department in accordance with the provisions.  
Where any of the following major medical negligent acts occurs, the medical institution shall report to the local health administration department within 12 hours:  
(1) causing death of a patient or likely to be a Level II or above medical accident;  
(2) causing personal injury to three or more persons;  
(3) other circumstances prescribed by the health administration department of the State Council and the health administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发生或者发现医疗过失行为，医疗机构及其医务人员应当立即采取有效措施，避免或者减轻对患者身体健康的损害，防止损害扩大。

**Article 15** Where a medical negligent act occurs or is discovered, the medical institution and its medical personnel shall immediately take effective measures to avoid or mitigate the harm to the patient's health and prevent the harm from escalating.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　发生医疗事故争议时，死亡病例讨论记录、疑难病例讨论记录、上级医师查房记录、会诊意见、病程记录应当在医患双方在场的情况下封存和启封。封存的病历资料可以是复印件，由医疗机构保管。

**Article 16** Where a medical accident dispute arises, the records of discussion on death cases, records of discussion on difficult cases, records of rounds by senior physicians, consultation opinions, and progress notes shall be sealed and unsealed in the presence of both the medical institution and the patient. The sealed medical records may be copies and shall be kept by the medical institution.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　疑似输液、输血、注射、药物等引起不良后果的，医患双方应当共同对现场实物进行封存和启封，封存的现场实物由医疗机构保管；需要检验的，应当由双方共同指定的、依法具有检验资格的检验机构进行检验；双方无法共同指定时，由卫生行政部门指定。  
疑似输血引起不良后果，需要对血液进行封存保留的，医疗机构应当通知提供该血液的采供血机构派员到场。

**Article 17** Where adverse consequences are suspected to be caused by infusion, blood transfusion, injection, medication, etc., both the medical institution and the patient shall jointly seal and unseal the physical evidence at the scene, and the sealed physical evidence shall be kept by the medical institution; where examination is necessary, it shall be conducted by an examination institution designated by both parties jointly and possessing examination qualifications according to law; where the two parties cannot jointly designate, the health administration department shall make the designation.  
Where adverse consequences are suspected to be caused by blood transfusion and the blood needs to be sealed and kept, the medical institution shall notify the blood collection and supply institution that provided the blood to send personnel to be present.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　患者死亡，医患双方当事人不能确定死因或者对死因有异议的，应当在患者死亡后48小时内进行尸检；具备尸体冻存条件的，可以延长至7日。尸检应当经死者近亲属同意并签字。  
尸检应当由按照国家有关规定取得相应资格的机构和病理解剖专业技术人员进行。承担尸检任务的机构和病理解剖专业技术人员有进行尸检的义务。  
医疗事故争议双方当事人可以请法医病理学人员参加尸检，也可以委派代表观察尸检过程。拒绝或者拖延尸检，超过规定时间，影响对死因判定的，由拒绝或者拖延的一方承担责任。

**Article 18** Where a patient dies and the two parties to the medical accident dispute cannot determine the cause of death or have objections to the cause of death, an autopsy shall be performed within 48 hours after the patient's death; where conditions for body preservation exist, the time limit may be extended to 7 days. The autopsy shall be subject to the consent and signature of the patient's close relatives.  
The autopsy shall be performed by an institution and pathologists with appropriate qualifications obtained in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The institution undertaking the autopsy and the pathologists have the obligation to perform the autopsy.  
The two parties to the medical accident dispute may invite forensic pathologists to participate in the autopsy, or may appoint representatives to observe the autopsy process. Where a party refuses or delays the autopsy beyond the prescribed time limit, affecting the determination of the cause of death, that party shall bear the liability.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　患者在医疗机构内死亡的，尸体应当立即移放太平间。死者尸体存放时间一般不得超过2周。逾期不处理的尸体，经医疗机构所在地卫生行政部门批准，并报经同级公安部门备案后，由医疗机构按照规定进行处理。

**Article 19** Where a patient dies within a medical institution, the body shall be immediately moved to the mortuary. The storage period for the body shall generally not exceed 2 weeks. Where the body is not disposed of upon expiry of the time limit, the medical institution may dispose of the body in accordance with the provisions after obtaining approval from the local health administration department and filing a record with the public security department at the same level.

### 第三章　医疗事故的技术鉴定

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　卫生行政部门接到医疗机构关于重大医疗过失行为的报告或者医疗事故争议当事人要求处理医疗事故争议的申请后，对需要进行医疗事故技术鉴定的，应当交由负责医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织鉴定；医患双方协商解决医疗事故争议，需要进行医疗事故技术鉴定的，由双方当事人共同委托负责医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织鉴定。

**Article 20** After receiving a report from a medical institution on a major medical negligent act or an application from a party to a medical accident dispute requesting the handling of the dispute, the health administration department shall, if a technical appraisal of the medical accident is necessary, refer the matter to the medical association responsible for the technical appraisal of medical accidents for organization of the appraisal; where the medical institution and the patient resolve the medical accident dispute through consultation and a technical appraisal of the medical accident is necessary, the two parties shall jointly entrust the medical association responsible for the technical appraisal of medical accidents to organize the appraisal.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设区的市级地方医学会和省、自治区、直辖市直接管辖的县(市)地方医学会负责组织首次医疗事故技术鉴定工作。省、自治区、直辖市地方医学会负责组织再次鉴定工作。  
必要时，中华医学会可以组织疑难、复杂并在全国有重大影响的医疗事故争议的技术鉴定工作。

**Article 21** The local medical associations at the level of a city divided into districts and at the level of a county (city) directly under the jurisdiction of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for organizing the first instance technical appraisal of medical accidents. The local medical associations at the level of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for organizing the second instance appraisal.  
Where necessary, the Chinese Medical Association may organize the technical appraisal of medical accident disputes that are difficult, complex, and have a significant impact nationwide.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　当事人对首次医疗事故技术鉴定结论不服的，可以自收到首次鉴定结论之日起15日内向医疗机构所在地卫生行政部门提出再次鉴定的申请。

**Article 22** Where a party disagrees with the conclusion of the first instance technical appraisal of a medical accident, it may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the first instance appraisal conclusion, submit an application for a second instance appraisal to the health administration department of the place where the medical institution is located.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会应当建立专家库。  
专家库由具备下列条件的医疗卫生专业技术人员组成：  
(一)有良好的业务素质和执业品德；  
(二)受聘于医疗卫生机构或者医学教学、科研机构并担任相应专业高级技术职务3年以上。  
符合前款第(一)项规定条件并具备高级技术任职资格的法医可以受聘进入专家库。  
负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会依照本条例规定聘请医疗卫生专业技术人员和法医进入专家库，可以不受行政区域的限制。

**Article 23** The medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents shall establish a database of experts.  
The database of experts shall be composed of medical and health professionals meeting the following conditions:  
(1) having good professional competence and practice ethics;  
(2) being employed by a medical and health institution or a medical teaching or research institution and holding a senior technical position in the corresponding specialty for at least 3 years.  
Forensic doctors who meet the conditions specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph and hold senior technical qualifications may be employed to enter the database of experts.  
The medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents may, in appointing medical and health professionals and forensic doctors to the database of experts in accordance with these Regulations, not be restricted by administrative regions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗事故技术鉴定，由负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织专家鉴定组进行。  
参加医疗事故技术鉴定的相关专业的专家，由医患双方在医学会主持下从专家库中随机抽取。在特殊情况下，医学会根据医疗事故技术鉴定工作的需要，可以组织医患双方在其他医学会建立的专家库中随机抽取相关专业的专家参加鉴定或者函件咨询。  
符合[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)规定条件的医疗卫生专业技术人员和法医有义务受聘进入专家库，并承担医疗事故技术鉴定工作。

**Article 24** The technical appraisal of medical accidents shall be conducted by an expert appraisal panel organized by the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents.  
The experts of relevant specialties participating in the technical appraisal of medical accidents shall be randomly selected from the database of experts under the auspices of the medical association by both the medical institution and the patient. Under special circumstances, the medical association may, based on the needs of the technical appraisal of medical accidents, organize the two parties to randomly select experts of relevant specialties from the expert database established by another medical association to participate in the appraisal or provide consultation by correspondence.  
Medical and health professionals and forensic doctors who meet the conditions specified in Article 23 of these Regulations have the obligation to be employed to enter the database of experts and to undertake the technical appraisal of medical accidents.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　专家鉴定组进行医疗事故技术鉴定，实行合议制。专家鉴定组人数为单数，涉及的主要学科的专家一般不得少于鉴定组成员的二分之一；涉及死因、伤残等级鉴定的，并应当从专家库中随机抽取法医参加专家鉴定组。

**Article 25** The expert appraisal panel shall conduct the technical appraisal of medical accidents under a collegial system. The number of members of the expert appraisal panel shall be an odd number, and the experts in the principal disciplines involved shall generally account for no less than one half of the appraisal panel members; where the appraisal involves the cause of death or the level of disability, a forensic doctor shall be randomly selected from the database of experts to participate in the expert appraisal panel.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　专家鉴定组成员有下列情形之一的，应当回避，当事人也可以以口头或者书面的方式申请其回避：  
(一)是医疗事故争议当事人或者当事人的近亲属的；  
(二)与医疗事故争议有利害关系的；  
(三)与医疗事故争议当事人有其他关系，可能影响公正鉴定的。

**Article 26** A member of the expert appraisal panel shall withdraw in any of the following circumstances, and a party may also apply for his or her withdrawal orally or in writing:  
(1) being a party to the medical accident dispute or a close relative of a party;  
(2) having an interest in the medical accident dispute;  
(3) having other relationships with a party to the medical accident dispute that may affect the impartiality of the appraisal.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　专家鉴定组依照医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规，运用医学科学原理和专业知识，独立进行医疗事故技术鉴定，对医疗事故进行鉴别和判定，为处理医疗事故争议提供医学依据。  
任何单位或者个人不得干扰医疗事故技术鉴定工作，不得威胁、利诱、辱骂、殴打专家鉴定组成员。  
专家鉴定组成员不得接受双方当事人的财物或者其他利益。

**Article 27** The expert appraisal panel shall independently conduct the technical appraisal of medical accidents, applying medical scientific principles and professional knowledge, in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and norms and routines of diagnosis, treatment and nursing care on health management, to identify and determine medical accidents, and to provide medical evidence for handling medical accident disputes.  
No unit or individual may interfere with the technical appraisal of medical accidents, or threaten, bribe, insult, or assault members of the expert appraisal panel.  
Members of the expert appraisal panel shall not accept money, property, or other benefits from either party.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会应当自受理医疗事故技术鉴定之日起5日内通知医疗事故争议双方当事人提交进行医疗事故技术鉴定所需的材料。  
当事人应当自收到医学会的通知之日起10日内提交有关医疗事故技术鉴定的材料、书面陈述及答辩。医疗机构提交的有关医疗事故技术鉴定的材料应当包括下列内容：  
(一)住院患者的病程记录、死亡病例讨论记录、疑难病例讨论记录、会诊意见、上级医师查房记录等病历资料原件；  
(二)住院患者的住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单(检验报告)、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录单、病理资料、护理记录等病历资料原件；  
(三)抢救急危患者，在规定时间内补记的病历资料原件；  
(四)封存保留的输液、注射用物品和血液、药物等实物，或者依法具有检验资格的检验机构对这些物品、实物作出的检验报告；  
(五)与医疗事故技术鉴定有关的其他材料。  
在医疗机构建有病历档案的门诊、急诊患者，其病历资料由医疗机构提供；没有在医疗机构建立病历档案的，由患者提供。  
医患双方应当依照本条例的规定提交相关材料。医疗机构无正当理由未依照本条例的规定如实提供相关材料，导致医疗事故技术鉴定不能进行的，应当承担责任。

**Article 28** The medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents shall, within 5 days from the date of acceptance of the application for technical appraisal of medical accident, notify the two parties to the medical accident dispute to submit the materials required for the technical appraisal of the medical accident.  
The parties shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of the notice from the medical association, submit the materials related to the technical appraisal of the medical accident, written statements, and replies. The materials related to the technical appraisal of the medical accident submitted by the medical institution shall include the following:  
(1) original medical records such as the progress notes of inpatients, records of discussion on death cases, records of discussion on difficult cases, consultation opinions, records of rounds by senior physicians;  
(2) original medical records such as inpatient admission records, temperature charts, medical orders sheets, laboratory test sheets (test reports), medical imaging examination materials, special examination consent forms, surgical consent forms, surgical and anesthesia records, pathological materials, and nursing records of inpatients;  
(3) original medical records supplemented within the prescribed time limit for emergency rescue of critical patients;  
(4) sealed and retained physical evidence such as items used for infusion and injection, blood, and medications, or examination reports issued by examination institutions possessing examination qualifications according to law on such items and physical evidence;  
(5) other materials related to the technical appraisal of the medical accident.  
For outpatients and emergency patients who have medical records files established in the medical institution, the medical records shall be provided by the medical institution; for those without medical records files established in the medical institution, they shall be provided by the patient.  
Both the medical institution and the patient shall submit relevant materials in accordance with these Regulations. Where a medical institution fails to truthfully provide relevant materials in accordance with these Regulations without justifiable reasons, resulting in the inability to conduct the technical appraisal of the medical accident, it shall bear the liability.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会应当自接到当事人提交的有关医疗事故技术鉴定的材料、书面陈述及答辩之日起45日内组织鉴定并出具医疗事故技术鉴定书。  
负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会可以向双方当事人调查取证。

**Article 29** The medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents shall, within 45 days from the date of receipt of the materials related to the technical appraisal of the medical accident, written statements, and replies submitted by the parties, organize the appraisal and issue a written technical appraisal of the medical accident.  
The medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents may investigate and collect evidence from the two parties.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　专家鉴定组应当认真审查双方当事人提交的材料，听取双方当事人的陈述及答辩并进行核实。  
双方当事人应当按照本条例的规定如实提交进行医疗事故技术鉴定所需要的材料，并积极配合调查。当事人任何一方不予配合，影响医疗事故技术鉴定的，由不予配合的一方承担责任。

**Article 30** The expert appraisal panel shall carefully review the materials submitted by the two parties, hear their statements and replies, and verify them.  
Both parties shall truthfully submit the materials required for the technical appraisal of the medical accident in accordance with these Regulations and actively cooperate with the investigation. Where either party fails to cooperate, affecting the technical appraisal of the medical accident, that party shall bear the liability.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　专家鉴定组应当在事实清楚、证据确凿的基础上，综合分析患者的病情和个体差异，作出鉴定结论，并制作医疗事故技术鉴定书。鉴定结论以专家鉴定组成员的过半数通过。鉴定过程应当如实记载。  
医疗事故技术鉴定书应当包括下列主要内容：  
(一)双方当事人的基本情况及要求；  
(二)当事人提交的材料和负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会的调查材料；  
(三)对鉴定过程的说明；  
(四)医疗行为是否违反医疗卫生管理法律、行政法规、部门规章和诊疗护理规范、常规；  
(五)医疗过失行为与人身损害后果之间是否存在因果关系；  
(六)医疗过失行为在医疗事故损害后果中的责任程度；  
(七)医疗事故等级；  
(八)对医疗事故患者的医疗护理医学建议。

**Article 31** The expert appraisal panel shall, on the basis of clear facts and conclusive evidence, make a comprehensive analysis of the patient's condition and individual differences, reach an appraisal conclusion, and prepare a written technical appraisal of the medical accident. The appraisal conclusion shall be adopted by a majority of the members of the expert appraisal panel. The appraisal process shall be truthfully recorded.  
The written technical appraisal of a medical accident shall include the following main contents:  
(1) the basic information and requests of the two parties;  
(2) the materials submitted by the parties and the investigation materials of the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of the medical accident;  
(3) a description of the appraisal process;  
(4) whether the medical act violates laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and norms and routines of diagnosis, treatment and nursing care on health management;  
(5) whether there is a causal relationship between the medical negligent act and the personal injury consequence;  
(6) the degree of liability of the medical negligent act in the harmful consequences of the medical accident;  
(7) the level of the medical accident;  
(8) medical and nursing recommendations for the patient of the medical accident.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　医疗事故技术鉴定办法由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 32** The measures for technical appraisal of medical accidents shall be formulated by the health administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　有下列情形之一的，不属于医疗事故：  
(一)在紧急情况下为抢救垂危患者生命而采取紧急医学措施造成不良后果的；  
(二)在医疗活动中由于患者病情异常或者患者体质特殊而发生医疗意外的；  
(三)在现有医学科学技术条件下，发生无法预料或者不能防范的不良后果的；  
(四)无过错输血感染造成不良后果的；  
(五)因患方原因延误诊疗导致不良后果的；  
(六)因不可抗力造成不良后果的。

**Article 33** The following circumstances shall not be deemed as medical accidents:  
(1) adverse consequences caused by emergency medical measures taken to save the life of a critically ill patient in an emergency situation;  
(2) medical incidents occurring in medical activities due to the patient's abnormal condition or special constitution;  
(3) adverse consequences that are unpredictable or unpreventable under the current level of medical science and technology;  
(4) adverse consequences caused by blood transfusion without fault;  
(5) adverse consequences caused by delayed diagnosis and treatment due to the patient's reasons;  
(6) adverse consequences caused by force majeure.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　医疗事故技术鉴定，可以收取鉴定费用。经鉴定，属于医疗事故的，鉴定费用由医疗机构支付；不属于医疗事故的，鉴定费用由提出医疗事故处理申请的一方支付。鉴定费用标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门会同同级财政部门、卫生行政部门规定。

**Article 34** Fees may be charged for the technical appraisal of medical accidents. Where the appraisal determines that it is a medical accident, the appraisal fee shall be paid by the medical institution; where it is determined not to be a medical accident, the appraisal fee shall be paid by the party who filed the application for handling the medical accident. The fee standards for appraisal shall be formulated by the competent department of price of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the financial department and the health administration department at the same level.

### 第四章　医疗事故的行政处理与监督

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　卫生行政部门应当依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规、部门规章的规定，对发生医疗事故的医疗机构和医务人员作出行政处理。

**Article 35** The health administration department shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, and departmental rules, impose administrative sanctions on the medical institution and medical personnel involved in the medical accident.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　卫生行政部门接到医疗机构关于重大医疗过失行为的报告后，除责令医疗机构及时采取必要的医疗救治措施，防止损害后果扩大外，应当组织调查，判定是否属于医疗事故；对不能判定是否属于医疗事故的，应当依照本条例的有关规定交由负责医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织鉴定。

**Article 36** After receiving a report from a medical institution on a major medical negligent act, the health administration department shall, in addition to ordering the medical institution to promptly take necessary medical treatment measures to prevent the escalation of harmful consequences, organize an investigation to determine whether it constitutes a medical accident; where it cannot be determined whether it is a medical accident, it shall refer the matter to the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　发生医疗事故争议，当事人申请卫生行政部门处理的，应当提出书面申请。申请书应当载明申请人的基本情况、有关事实、具体请求及理由等。  
当事人自知道或者应当知道其身体健康受到损害之日起1年内，可以向卫生行政部门提出医疗事故争议处理申请。

**Article 37** Where a medical accident dispute arises and a party applies to the health administration department for handling, a written application shall be submitted. The application shall state the basic information of the applicant, the relevant facts, specific requests, and reasons, etc.  
A party may submit an application for handling a medical accident dispute to the health administration department within one year from the date on which it knows or should know that its health has been harmed.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发生医疗事故争议，当事人申请卫生行政部门处理的，由医疗机构所在地的县级人民政府卫生行政部门受理。医疗机构所在地是直辖市的，由医疗机构所在地的区、县人民政府卫生行政部门受理。  
有下列情形之一的，县级人民政府卫生行政部门应当自接到医疗机构的报告或者当事人提出医疗事故争议处理申请之日起7日内移送上一级人民政府卫生行政部门处理：  
(一)患者死亡；  
(二)可能为二级以上的医疗事故；  
(三)国务院卫生行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 38** Where a medical accident dispute arises and a party applies to the health administration department for handling, the case shall be accepted by the health administration department of the people's government at the county level of the place where the medical institution is located. Where the place where the medical institution is located is a municipality directly under the Central Government, the case shall be accepted by the health administration department of the district or county people's government of the place where the medical institution is located.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, the health administration department of the people's government at the county level shall, within 7 days from the date of receiving the report from the medical institution or the application from the party for handling the medical accident dispute, transfer the case to the health administration department of the people's government at the next higher level for handling:  
(1) death of a patient;  
(2) likely to be a Level II or above medical accident;  
(3) other circumstances prescribed by the health administration department of the State Council and the health administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　卫生行政部门应当自收到医疗事故争议处理申请之日起10日内进行审查，作出是否受理的决定。对符合本条例规定，予以受理，需要进行医疗事故技术鉴定的，应当自作出受理决定之日起5日内将有关材料交由负责医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织鉴定并书面通知申请人；对不符合本条例规定，不予受理的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
当事人对首次医疗事故技术鉴定结论有异议，申请再次鉴定的，卫生行政部门应当自收到申请之日起7日内交由省、自治区、直辖市地方医学会组织再次鉴定。

**Article 39** The health administration department shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of the application for handling a medical accident dispute, review the application and decide whether to accept it. Where the application meets the requirements of these Regulations and is accepted, and a technical appraisal of the medical accident is necessary, the relevant materials shall be referred to the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents within 5 days from the date of the acceptance decision, and the applicant shall be notified in writing; where the application does not meet the requirements of these Regulations and is not accepted, the applicant shall be notified in writing with reasons explained.  
Where a party disagrees with the conclusion of the first instance technical appraisal of a medical accident and applies for a second instance appraisal, the health administration department shall, within 7 days from the date of receipt of the application, refer the matter to the local medical association of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to organize the second instance appraisal.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人既向卫生行政部门提出医疗事故争议处理申请，又向人民法院提起诉讼的，卫生行政部门不予受理；卫生行政部门已经受理的，应当终止处理。

**Article 40** Where a party both submits an application to the health administration department for handling a medical accident dispute and initiates a lawsuit in the People's Court, the health administration department shall not accept the application; if it has already accepted it, it shall terminate the handling.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　卫生行政部门收到负责组织医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会出具的医疗事故技术鉴定书后，应当对参加鉴定的人员资格和专业类别、鉴定程序进行审核；必要时，可以组织调查，听取医疗事故争议双方当事人的意见。

**Article 41** After receiving the written technical appraisal of a medical accident issued by the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents, the health administration department shall review the qualifications and professional categories of the personnel participating in the appraisal and the appraisal procedures; where necessary, it may organize an investigation and hear the opinions of the two parties to the medical accident dispute.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　卫生行政部门经审核，对符合本条例规定作出的医疗事故技术鉴定结论，应当作为对发生医疗事故的医疗机构和医务人员作出行政处理以及进行医疗事故赔偿调解的依据；经审核，发现医疗事故技术鉴定不符合本条例规定的，应当要求重新鉴定。

**Article 42** Where, after review, the health administration department finds that the conclusion of the technical appraisal of a medical accident is made in accordance with these Regulations, it shall use it as the basis for imposing administrative sanctions on the medical institution and medical personnel involved in the medical accident and for mediating compensation for the medical accident; where, after review, it finds that the technical appraisal of a medical accident does not comply with these Regulations, it shall require a new appraisal.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　医疗事故争议由双方当事人自行协商解决的，医疗机构应当自协商解决之日起7日内向所在地卫生行政部门作出书面报告，并附具协议书。

**Article 43** Where a medical accident dispute is resolved through consultation between the two parties, the medical institution shall, within 7 days from the date of resolution through consultation, submit a written report to the local health administration department, together with the agreement.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　医疗事故争议经人民法院调解或者判决解决的，医疗机构应当自收到生效的人民法院的调解书或者判决书之日起7日内向所在地卫生行政部门作出书面报告，并附具调解书或者判决书。

**Article 44** Where a medical accident dispute is resolved through mediation or judgment by the People's Court, the medical institution shall, within 7 days from the date of receipt of the effective mediation document or judgment of the People's Court, submit a written report to the local health administration department, together with the mediation document or judgment.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当按照规定逐级将当地发生的医疗事故以及依法对发生医疗事故的医疗机构和医务人员作出行政处理的情况，上报国务院卫生行政部门。

**Article 45** The health administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, level by level, report to the health administration department of the State Council the medical accidents occurring in their localities and the administrative sanctions imposed according to law on the medical institutions and medical personnel involved in the medical accidents.

### 第五章　医疗事故的赔偿

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　发生医疗事故的赔偿等民事责任争议，医患双方可以协商解决；不愿意协商或者协商不成的，当事人可以向卫生行政部门提出调解申请，也可以直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼。

**Article 46** Where disputes over civil liability such as compensation for medical accidents arise, the medical institution and the patient may resolve them through consultation; if they are unwilling to consult or consultation fails, the parties may apply to the health administration department for mediation, or may directly initiate a civil action in the People's Court.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　双方当事人协商解决医疗事故的赔偿等民事责任争议的，应当制作协议书。协议书应当载明双方当事人的基本情况和医疗事故的原因、双方当事人共同认定的医疗事故等级以及协商确定的赔偿数额等，并由双方当事人在协议书上签名。

**Article 47** Where the two parties resolve through consultation disputes over civil liability such as compensation for a medical accident, they shall prepare an agreement. The agreement shall state the basic information of the two parties, the cause of the medical accident, the level of the medical accident as jointly determined by the two parties, and the amount of compensation determined through consultation, etc., and shall be signed by both parties.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　已确定为医疗事故的，卫生行政部门应医疗事故争议双方当事人请求，可以进行医疗事故赔偿调解。调解时，应当遵循当事人双方自愿原则，并应当依据本条例的规定计算赔偿数额。  
经调解，双方当事人就赔偿数额达成协议的，制作调解书，双方当事人应当履行；调解不成或者经调解达成协议后一方反悔的，卫生行政部门不再调解。

**Article 48** Where a medical accident has been confirmed, the health administration department may, at the request of both parties to the medical accident dispute, conduct mediation on compensation for the medical accident. During mediation, the principle of voluntariness of both parties shall be followed, and the amount of compensation shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
If, through mediation, the two parties reach an agreement on the amount of compensation, a mediation document shall be prepared, and both parties shall perform it; if mediation fails or one party reneges after reaching an agreement through mediation, the health administration department shall no longer mediate.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　医疗事故赔偿，应当考虑下列因素，确定具体赔偿数额：  
(一)医疗事故等级；  
(二)医疗过失行为在医疗事故损害后果中的责任程度；  
(三)医疗事故损害后果与患者原有疾病状况之间的关系。  
不属于医疗事故的，医疗机构不承担赔偿责任。

**Article 49** For compensation for a medical accident, the specific amount of compensation shall be determined by taking into account the following factors:  
(1) the level of the medical accident;  
(2) the degree of liability of the medical negligent act in the harmful consequences of the medical accident;  
(3) the relationship between the harmful consequences of the medical accident and the patient's pre-existing disease condition.  
If it is not a medical accident, the medical institution shall not bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　医疗事故赔偿，按照下列项目和标准计算：  
(一)医疗费：按照医疗事故对患者造成的人身损害进行治疗所发生的医疗费用计算，凭据支付，但不包括原发病医疗费用。结案后确实需要继续治疗的，按照基本医疗费用支付。  
(二)误工费：患者有固定收入的，按照本人因误工减少的固定收入计算，对收入高于医疗事故发生地上一年度职工年平均工资3倍以上的，按照3倍计算；无固定收入的，按照医疗事故发生地上一年度职工年平均工资计算。  
(三)住院伙食补助费：按照医疗事故发生地国家机关一般工作人员的出差伙食补助标准计算。  
(四)陪护费：患者住院期间需要专人陪护的，按照医疗事故发生地上一年度职工年平均工资计算。  
(五)残疾生活补助费：根据伤残等级，按照医疗事故发生地居民年平均生活费计算，自定残之月起最长赔偿30年；但是，60周岁以上的，不超过15年；70周岁以上的，不超过5年。  
(六)残疾用具费：因残疾需要配置补偿功能器具的，凭医疗机构证明，按照普及型器具的费用计算。  
(七)丧葬费：按照医疗事故发生地规定的丧葬费补助标准计算。  
(八)被扶养人生活费：以死者生前或者残疾者丧失劳动能力前实际扶养且没有劳动能力的人为限，按照其户籍所在地或者居所地居民最低生活保障标准计算。对不满16周岁的，扶养到16周岁。对年满16周岁但无劳动能力的，扶养20年；但是，60周岁以上的，不超过15年；70周岁以上的，不超过5年。  
(九)交通费：按照患者实际必需的交通费用计算，凭据支付。  
(十)住宿费：按照医疗事故发生地国家机关一般工作人员的出差住宿补助标准计算，凭据支付。  
(十一)精神损害抚慰金：按照医疗事故发生地居民年平均生活费计算。造成患者死亡的，赔偿年限最长不超过6年；造成患者残疾的，赔偿年限最长不超过3年。

**Article 50** Compensation for a medical accident shall be calculated according to the following items and standards:  
(1) Medical expenses: calculated based on the medical expenses incurred for treating the personal injury caused to the patient by the medical accident, paid against receipts, but excluding expenses for the original disease. If continued treatment is genuinely necessary after the case is closed, payment shall be made based on basic medical expenses.  
(2) Lost wages: If the patient has a fixed income, compensation shall be calculated based on the fixed income reduced due to lost working time; if the income is more than three times the average annual wage of employees in the year preceding the medical accident in the place where the medical accident occurred, it shall be calculated based on three times; if there is no fixed income, it shall be calculated based on the average annual wage of employees in the year preceding the medical accident in the place where the medical accident occurred.  
(3) Hospital meal subsidies: calculated according to the standard of meal subsidies for business trips for ordinary staff of state organs in the place where the medical accident occurred.  
(4) Nursing expenses: If the patient requires dedicated nursing during hospitalization, compensation shall be calculated based on the average annual wage of employees in the year preceding the medical accident in the place where the medical accident occurred.  
(5) Disability living allowance: calculated according to the level of disability, based on the average annual living expenses of residents in the place where the medical accident occurred, for a maximum of 30 years from the month of disability determination; however, for persons aged 60 or above, it shall not exceed 15 years; for persons aged 70 or above, it shall not exceed 5 years.  
(6) Expenses for disability assistive devices: If the disability requires configuring compensatory functional devices, the cost shall be calculated based on the cost of universal-type devices, with certification from a medical institution.  
(7) Funeral expenses: calculated according to the standard of funeral expense subsidies prescribed in the place where the medical accident occurred.  
(8) Living expenses for dependents: limited to persons who were actually supported by the deceased before death or by the disabled person before losing working capacity and who lack working capacity, calculated according to the minimum living security standard for residents in their registered domicile or place of residence. For persons under 16 years of age, support shall be provided until they reach 16 years of age. For persons who have reached 16 years of age but lack working capacity, support shall be provided for 20 years; however, for persons aged 60 or above, it shall not exceed 15 years; for persons aged 70 or above, it shall not exceed 5 years.  
(9) Transportation expenses: calculated based on the actual necessary transportation expenses for the patient, paid against receipts.  
(10) Accommodation expenses: calculated according to the standard of accommodation subsidies for business trips for ordinary staff of state organs in the place where the medical accident occurred, paid against receipts.  
(11) Solatium for mental damages: calculated according to the average annual living expenses of residents in the place where the medical accident occurred. If the medical accident causes death, the maximum compensation period shall not exceed 6 years; if it causes disability, the maximum compensation period shall not exceed 3 years.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　参加医疗事故处理的患者近亲属所需交通费、误工费、住宿费，参照[本条例第五十条](#art-50)的有关规定计算，计算费用的人数不超过2人。  
医疗事故造成患者死亡的，参加丧葬活动的患者的配偶和直系亲属所需交通费、误工费、住宿费，参照[本条例第五十条](#art-50)的有关规定计算，计算费用的人数不超过2人。

**Article 51** The transportation expenses, lost wages, and accommodation expenses needed for close relatives of a patient who participate in the handling of a medical accident shall be calculated with reference to the relevant provisions of Article 50 of these Regulations, and the number of persons for whom expenses are calculated shall not exceed two.  
In the event that a medical accident causes the death of a patient, the transportation expenses, lost wages, and accommodation expenses needed for the patient's spouse and lineal relatives who participate in the funeral activities shall be calculated with reference to the relevant provisions of Article 50 of these Regulations, and the number of persons for whom expenses are calculated shall not exceed two.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　医疗事故赔偿费用，实行一次性结算，由承担医疗事故责任的医疗机构支付。

**Article 52** The compensation expenses for a medical accident shall be settled in a lump sum and shall be paid by the medical institution that bears liability for the medical accident.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　卫生行政部门的工作人员在处理医疗事故过程中违反本条例的规定，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他利益，滥用职权，玩忽职守，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果的，依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他有关罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 53** Where a staff member of the health administrative department, in the course of handling a medical accident, violates the provisions of these Regulations, takes advantage of his or her position to accept property or other benefits from others, abuses power, neglects duty, or fails to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of acceptance of bribes, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of neglect of duty, or other relevant crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　卫生行政部门违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由上级卫生行政部门给予警告并责令限期改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
(一)接到医疗机构关于重大医疗过失行为的报告后，未及时组织调查的；  
(二)接到医疗事故争议处理申请后，未在规定时间内审查或者移送上一级人民政府卫生行政部门处理的；  
(三)未将应当进行医疗事故技术鉴定的重大医疗过失行为或者医疗事故争议移交医学会组织鉴定的；  
(四)未按照规定逐级将当地发生的医疗事故以及依法对发生医疗事故的医疗机构和医务人员的行政处理情况上报的；  
(五)未依照本条例规定审核医疗事故技术鉴定书的。

**Article 54** Where a health administrative department violates the provisions of these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the superior health administrative department shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if the circumstances are serious, the person in charge who bears responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to organize an investigation in a timely manner after receiving a report from a medical institution concerning a major medical negligence act;  
(2) failing to examine or transfer an application for handling a medical accident dispute to the health administrative department of the higher level people's government within the prescribed time limit after receiving such application;  
(3) failing to refer a major medical negligence act or a medical accident dispute that should undergo technical appraisal of a medical accident to a medical association for organization of appraisal;  
(4) failing to report to the higher authorities level by level the occurrence of local medical accidents and the administrative handling of the medical institutions and medical personnel that caused the medical accidents according to law;  
(5) failing to examine the technical appraisal certificate of a medical accident in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　医疗机构发生医疗事故的，由卫生行政部门根据医疗事故等级和情节，给予警告；情节严重的，责令限期停业整顿直至由原发证部门吊销执业许可证，对负有责任的医务人员依照刑法关于医疗事故罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分。  
对发生医疗事故的有关医务人员，除依照前款处罚外，卫生行政部门并可以责令暂停6个月以上1年以下执业活动；情节严重的，吊销其执业证书。

**Article 55** Where a medical institution causes a medical accident, the health administrative department shall give it a warning based on the level and circumstances of the medical accident; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the institution to suspend operations for rectification within a specified time limit, or even the original issuing department shall revoke its practice license; the medical personnel responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of medical accident; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to law.  
In addition to the punishment under the preceding paragraph, the health administrative department may also order the relevant medical personnel who caused the medical accident to suspend their practice activities for a period of not less than six months and not more than one year; if the circumstances are serious, their practice certificates shall be revoked.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　医疗机构违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由卫生行政部门责令改正；情节严重的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分：  
(一)未如实告知患者病情、医疗措施和医疗风险的；  
(二)没有正当理由，拒绝为患者提供复印或者复制病历资料服务的；  
(三)未按照国务院卫生行政部门规定的要求书写和妥善保管病历资料的；  
(四)未在规定时间内补记抢救工作病历内容的；  
(五)未按照本条例的规定封存、保管和启封病历资料和实物的；  
(六)未设置医疗服务质量监控部门或者配备专(兼)职人员的；  
(七)未制定有关医疗事故防范和处理预案的；  
(八)未在规定时间内向卫生行政部门报告重大医疗过失行为的；  
(九)未按照本条例的规定向卫生行政部门报告医疗事故的；  
(十)未按照规定进行尸检和保存、处理尸体的。

**Article 56** Where a medical institution violates the provisions of these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the health administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the person in charge who bears responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to truthfully inform the patient of the patient's condition, medical measures, and medical risks;  
(2) refusing, without justifiable reasons, to provide the patient with services for copying or reproducing medical records;  
(3) failing to write and properly keep medical records in accordance with the requirements prescribed by the health administrative department of the State Council;  
(4) failing to supplement the contents of medical records for rescue work within the prescribed time limit;  
(5) failing to seal, keep, and unseal medical records and physical evidence in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(6) failing to establish a department for monitoring medical service quality or to assign full-time or part-time personnel;  
(7) failing to formulate contingency plans for preventing and handling medical accidents;  
(8) failing to report a major medical negligence act to the health administrative department within the prescribed time limit;  
(9) failing to report a medical accident to the health administrative department in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(10) failing to conduct an autopsy and to preserve and dispose of the corpse in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　参加医疗事故技术鉴定工作的人员违反本条例的规定，接受申请鉴定双方或者一方当事人的财物或者其他利益，出具虚假医疗事故技术鉴定书，造成严重后果的，依照刑法关于受贿罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由原发证部门吊销其执业证书或者资格证书。

**Article 57** Where any person participating in the technical appraisal work of a medical accident violates the provisions of these Regulations, accepts property or other benefits from either or both parties applying for appraisal, and issues a false technical appraisal certificate of a medical accident, thereby causing serious consequences, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of acceptance of bribes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the original issuing department shall revoke his or her practice certificate or qualification certificate.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　医疗机构或者其他有关机构违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由卫生行政部门责令改正，给予警告；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；情节严重的，由原发证部门吊销其执业证书或者资格证书：  
(一)承担尸检任务的机构没有正当理由，拒绝进行尸检的；  
(二)涂改、伪造、隐匿、销毁病历资料的。

**Article 58** Where a medical institution or any other relevant institution violates the provisions of these Regulations under any of the following circumstances, the health administrative department shall order it to make corrections and give it a warning; the person in charge who bears responsibility and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing department shall revoke its practice certificate or qualification certificate:  
(1) an institution undertaking the task of autopsy refuses, without justifiable reasons, to perform an autopsy;  
(2) altering, forging, concealing, or destroying medical records.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　以医疗事故为由，寻衅滋事、抢夺病历资料，扰乱医疗机构正常医疗秩序和医疗事故技术鉴定工作，依照刑法关于扰乱社会秩序罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 59** Where anyone, on the pretext of a medical accident, engages in quarrels and fights, seizes medical records, disturbs the normal medical order of a medical institution and the technical appraisal work of a medical accident, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of disturbing social order; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　本条例所称医疗机构，是指依照《医疗机构管理条例》的规定取得《医疗机构执业许可证》的机构。  
县级以上城市从事计划生育技术服务的机构依照《计划生育技术服务管理条例》的规定开展与计划生育有关的临床医疗服务，发生的计划生育技术服务事故，依照本条例的有关规定处理；但是，其中不属于医疗机构的县级以上城市从事计划生育技术服务的机构发生的计划生育技术服务事故，由计划生育行政部门行使依照本条例有关规定由卫生行政部门承担的受理、交由负责医疗事故技术鉴定工作的医学会组织鉴定和赔偿调解的职能；对发生计划生育技术服务事故的该机构及其有关责任人员，依法进行处理。

**Article 60** For the purposes of these Regulations, a medical institution means an institution that has obtained the "Practice License for Medical Institutions" in accordance with the provisions of the "Regulations on the Administration of Medical Institutions".  
Where an institution engaged in family planning technical services in a city at or above the county level carries out clinical medical services related to family planning in accordance with the provisions of the "Regulations on the Administration of Family Planning Technical Services", any accident arising from such family planning technical services shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations; however, for an accident arising from family planning technical services occurring in an institution engaged in family planning technical services in a city at or above the county level that is not a medical institution, the family planning administrative department shall exercise the functions that, according to the relevant provisions of these Regulations, shall be exercised by the health administrative department, namely, accepting the case, referring it to the medical association responsible for organizing the technical appraisal of medical accidents and mediating compensation; the institution and the relevant responsible persons for the accident arising from family planning technical services shall be dealt with according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　非法行医，造成患者人身损害，不属于医疗事故，触犯刑律的，依法追究刑事责任；有关赔偿，由受害人直接向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 61** Where practice of medicine without a license causes personal injury to a patient, it shall not be a medical accident; if it violates the criminal law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; for compensation therefor, the victim shall directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　军队医疗机构的医疗事故处理办法，由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门会同国务院卫生行政部门依据本条例制定。

**Article 62** The measures for handling medical accidents in military medical institutions shall be formulated by the health authority of the Chinese People's Liberation Army jointly with the health administrative department of the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例自2002年9月1日起施行。1987年6月29日国务院发布的《医疗事故处理办法》同时废止。本条例施行前已经处理结案的医疗事故争议，不再重新处理。

**Article 63** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2002. The "Measures for Handling Medical Accidents" promulgated by the State Council on June 29, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously. For medical accident disputes that have already been handled and concluded before the coming into force of these Regulations, no new handling shall be conducted.
