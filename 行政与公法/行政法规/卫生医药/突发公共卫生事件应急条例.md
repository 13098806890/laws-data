# 突发公共卫生事件应急条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：2011-01-08

> (2003年5月9日中华人民共和国国务院令第376号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了有效预防、及时控制和消除突发公共卫生事件的危害，保障公众身体健康与生命安全，维护正常的社会秩序，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称突发公共卫生事件(以下简称突发事件)，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成社会公众健康严重损害的重大传染病疫情、群体性不明原因疾病、重大食物和职业中毒以及其他严重影响公众健康的事件。 第三条　突发事件发生后，国务院设立全国突发事件应急处理指挥部，由国务院有关部门和军队有关部门组成，国务院主管领导人担任总指挥，负责对全国突发事件应急处理的统一领导、统一指挥。 国务院卫生行政主管部门和其他有关部门，在各自的职责范围内做好突发事件应急处理的有关工作。 第四条　突发事件发生后，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府成立地方突发事件应急处理指挥部，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府主要领导人担任总指挥，负责领导、指挥本行政区域内突发事件应急处理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门，具体负责组织突发事件的调查、控制和医疗救治工作。 县级以上地方人民政府有关部门，在各自的职责范围内做好突发事件应急处理的有关工作。 第五条　突发事件应急工作，应当遵循预防为主、常备不懈的方针，贯彻统一领导、分级负责、反应及时、措施果断、依靠科学、加强合作的原则。 第六条　县级以上各级人民政府应当组织开展防治突发事件相关科学研究，建立突发事件应急流行病学调查、传染源隔离、医疗救护、现场处置、监督检查、监测检验、卫生防护等有关物资、设备、设施、技术与人才资源储备，所需经费列入本级政府财政预算。 国家对边远贫困地区突发事件应急工作给予财政支持。 第七条　国家鼓励、支持开展突发事件监测、预警、反应处理有关技术的国际交流与合作。 第八条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，应当建立严格的突发事件防范和应急处理责任制，切实履行各自的职责，保证突发事件应急处理工作的正常进行。 第九条　县级以上各级人民政府及其卫生行政主管部门，应当对参加突发事件应急处理的医疗卫生人员，给予适当补助和保健津贴；对参加突发事件应急处理作出贡献的人员，给予表彰和奖励；对因参与应急处理工作致病、致残、死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定，给予相应的补助和抚恤。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了有效预防、及时控制和消除突发公共卫生事件的危害，保障公众身体健康与生命安全，维护正常的社会秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of effectively preventing, promptly controlling and eliminating the hazards of public health emergencies, safeguarding the health and life safety of the public, and maintaining normal social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称突发公共卫生事件(以下简称突发事件)，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成社会公众健康严重损害的重大传染病疫情、群体性不明原因疾病、重大食物和职业中毒以及其他严重影响公众健康的事件。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "public health emergencies" (hereinafter referred to as "emergencies") means the outbreak of a major infectious disease or a mass disease of unknown cause, a major incident of food or occupational poisoning, or any other incident that occurs suddenly and causes or may cause serious damage to the public health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　突发事件发生后，国务院设立全国突发事件应急处理指挥部，由国务院有关部门和军队有关部门组成，国务院主管领导人担任总指挥，负责对全国突发事件应急处理的统一领导、统一指挥。  
国务院卫生行政主管部门和其他有关部门，在各自的职责范围内做好突发事件应急处理的有关工作。

**Article 3** After the occurrence of an emergency, the State Council shall establish the National Emergency Response Headquarters for the emergency, which shall be composed of the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the armed forces. The leading person in charge of the State Council shall serve as the Commander-in-Chief, responsible for the unified leadership and command of the national emergency response.  
The competent health department of the State Council and other relevant departments shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out the work related to the emergency response.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　突发事件发生后，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府成立地方突发事件应急处理指挥部，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府主要领导人担任总指挥，负责领导、指挥本行政区域内突发事件应急处理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门，具体负责组织突发事件的调查、控制和医疗救治工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门，在各自的职责范围内做好突发事件应急处理的有关工作。

**Article 4** After the occurrence of an emergency, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall establish local emergency response headquarters, whose chief leaders shall serve as the commanders, responsible for leading and commanding the emergency response work within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent health departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the investigation, control, and medical treatment of the emergency.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, properly carry out the work related to the emergency response.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　突发事件应急工作，应当遵循预防为主、常备不懈的方针，贯彻统一领导、分级负责、反应及时、措施果断、依靠科学、加强合作的原则。

**Article 5** Emergency response work shall follow the principle of prevention first and constant preparedness, and shall implement the principles of unified leadership, graded responsibility, timely response, decisive measures, reliance on science, and enhanced cooperation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上各级人民政府应当组织开展防治突发事件相关科学研究，建立突发事件应急流行病学调查、传染源隔离、医疗救护、现场处置、监督检查、监测检验、卫生防护等有关物资、设备、设施、技术与人才资源储备，所需经费列入本级政府财政预算。  
国家对边远贫困地区突发事件应急工作给予财政支持。

**Article 6** The people's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out scientific research related to the prevention and treatment of emergencies, and establish reserves of materials, equipment, facilities, technologies, and human resources for emergency epidemiological investigation, isolation of sources of infection, medical aid, on-site disposal, supervision and inspection, monitoring and testing, and health protection, and the necessary funds shall be included in the fiscal budgets of the governments at the corresponding levels.  
The State shall provide financial support for emergency response work in remote and impoverished areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励、支持开展突发事件监测、预警、反应处理有关技术的国际交流与合作。

**Article 7** The State encourages and supports international exchanges and cooperation in technologies related to emergency monitoring, early warning, and response.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，应当建立严格的突发事件防范和应急处理责任制，切实履行各自的职责，保证突发事件应急处理工作的正常进行。

**Article 8** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall establish a strict responsibility system for the prevention of and emergency response to emergencies, earnestly perform their respective duties, and ensure the normal conduct of emergency response work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上各级人民政府及其卫生行政主管部门，应当对参加突发事件应急处理的医疗卫生人员，给予适当补助和保健津贴；对参加突发事件应急处理作出贡献的人员，给予表彰和奖励；对因参与应急处理工作致病、致残、死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定，给予相应的补助和抚恤。

**Article 9** The people's governments at or above the county level and their competent health departments shall provide appropriate subsidies and health allowances to the medical and health personnel participating in emergency response; shall commend and reward persons who have made contributions to emergency response; and shall grant corresponding subsidies and pensions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State to persons who have become ill, disabled, or died as a result of participation in emergency response work.

### 第二章　预防与应急准备

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院卫生行政主管部门按照分类指导、快速反应的要求，制定全国突发事件应急预案，报请国务院批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据全国突发事件应急预案，结合本地实际情况，制定本行政区域的突发事件应急预案。

**Article 10** The competent health department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the requirements of classified guidance and rapid response, formulate a national contingency plan for emergencies, and shall submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national contingency plan for emergencies and taking into account the actual local conditions, formulate contingency plans for emergencies within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　全国突发事件应急预案应当包括以下主要内容：  
(一)突发事件应急处理指挥部的组成和相关部门的职责；  
(二)突发事件的监测与预警；  
(三)突发事件信息的收集、分析、报告、通报制度；  
(四)突发事件应急处理技术和监测机构及其任务；  
(五)突发事件的分级和应急处理工作方案；  
(六)突发事件预防、现场控制，应急设施、设备、救治药品和医疗器械以及其他物资和技术的储备与调度；  
(七)突发事件应急处理专业队伍的建设和培训。

**Article 11** The national contingency plan for emergencies shall include the following main contents:  
(1) The composition of the emergency response headquarters and the duties of the relevant departments;  
(2) Monitoring and early warning of emergencies;  
(3) The system for collecting, analyzing, reporting, and disseminating information on emergencies;  
(4) The technical and monitoring institutions for emergency response and their tasks;  
(5) The classification of emergencies and work plans for emergency response;  
(6) The stockpiling and dispatch of emergency facilities, equipment, medicines for treatment, medical devices, and other materials and technologies for the prevention and on-site control of emergencies;  
(7) The building and training of professional teams for emergency response.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　突发事件应急预案应当根据突发事件的变化和实施中发现的问题及时进行修订、补充。

**Article 12** The contingency plans for emergencies shall be revised and supplemented in a timely manner in light of changes in emergencies and problems discovered during implementation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民政府应当依照法律、行政法规的规定，做好传染病预防和其他公共卫生工作，防范突发事件的发生。  
县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政主管部门和其他有关部门，应当对公众开展突发事件应急知识的专门教育，增强全社会对突发事件的防范意识和应对能力。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, properly carry out the prevention of infectious diseases and other public health work to prevent the occurrence of emergencies.  
The competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall carry out special education on emergency response knowledge among the public, so as to enhance the awareness of the whole society of emergency prevention and the capacity to respond.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立统一的突发事件预防控制体系。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当建立和完善突发事件监测与预警系统。  
县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政主管部门，应当指定机构负责开展突发事件的日常监测，并确保监测与预警系统的正常运行。

**Article 14** The State shall establish a unified system for the prevention and control of emergencies.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve systems for monitoring and early warning of emergencies.  
The competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall designate institutions to be responsible for carrying out routine monitoring of emergencies, and shall ensure the normal operation of the monitoring and early warning systems.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　监测与预警工作应当根据突发事件的类别，制定监测计划，科学分析、综合评价监测数据。对早期发现的潜在隐患以及可能发生的突发事件，应当依照本条例规定的报告程序和时限及时报告。

**Article 15** Monitoring and early warning work shall, in light of the categories of emergencies, formulate monitoring plans, and scientifically analyze and comprehensively evaluate monitoring data. Potential hazards discovered at an early stage and emergencies that may occur shall be promptly reported in accordance with the reporting procedures and time limits prescribed in these Regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，应当根据突发事件应急预案的要求，保证应急设施、设备、救治药品和医疗器械等物资储备。

**Article 16** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of the contingency plans for emergencies, ensure the stockpiling of emergency facilities, equipment, medicines for treatment, medical devices, and other materials.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上各级人民政府应当加强急救医疗服务网络的建设，配备相应的医疗救治药物、技术、设备和人员，提高医疗卫生机构应对各类突发事件的救治能力。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府应当设置与传染病防治工作需要相适应的传染病专科医院，或者指定具备传染病防治条件和能力的医疗机构承担传染病防治任务。

**Article 17** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of emergency medical service networks, and equip them with appropriate medical treatment drugs, technologies, equipment, and personnel, so as to enhance the capacity of medical and health institutions to respond to various types of emergencies.  
Local people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall set up specialized hospitals for infectious diseases appropriate to the needs of infectious disease prevention and control, or designate medical institutions with the conditions and capabilities for infectious disease prevention and control to undertake the tasks of infectious disease prevention and control.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门，应当定期对医疗卫生机构和人员开展突发事件应急处理相关知识、技能的培训，定期组织医疗卫生机构进行突发事件应急演练，推广最新知识和先进技术。

**Article 18** The competent health departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly conduct training on knowledge and skills related to emergency response for medical and health institutions and personnel, regularly organize medical and health institutions to conduct emergency drills, and disseminate the latest knowledge and advanced technologies.

### 第三章　报告与信息发布

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家建立突发事件应急报告制度。  
国务院卫生行政主管部门制定突发事件应急报告规范，建立重大、紧急疫情信息报告系统。  
有下列情形之一的，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当在接到报告1小时内，向国务院卫生行政主管部门报告：  
(一)发生或者可能发生传染病暴发、流行的；  
(二)发生或者发现不明原因的群体性疾病的；  
(三)发生传染病菌种、毒种丢失的；  
(四)发生或者可能发生重大食物和职业中毒事件的。  
国务院卫生行政主管部门对可能造成重大社会影响的突发事件，应当立即向国务院报告。

**Article 19** The State shall establish an emergency reporting system for emergencies.  
The competent health department of the State Council shall formulate specifications for emergency reporting and establish a reporting system for major and urgent epidemic information.  
In any of the following circumstances, the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, within one hour of receiving the report, report to the competent health department of the State Council:  
(1) The occurrence or possible occurrence of an outbreak or epidemic of an infectious disease;  
(2) The occurrence or discovery of a mass disease of unknown cause;  
(3) The loss of strains or species of infectious disease agents;  
(4) The occurrence or possible occurrence of a major incident of food or occupational poisoning.  
The competent health department of the State Council shall immediately report to the State Council any emergency that may cause a major social impact.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　突发事件监测机构、医疗卫生机构和有关单位发现有[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定情形之一的，应当在2小时内向所在地县级人民政府卫生行政主管部门报告；接到报告的卫生行政主管部门应当在2小时内向本级人民政府报告，并同时向上级人民政府卫生行政主管部门和国务院卫生行政主管部门报告。  
县级人民政府应当在接到报告后2小时内向设区的市级人民政府或者上一级人民政府报告；设区的市级人民政府应当在接到报告后2小时内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报告。

**Article 20** Where an emergency monitoring institution, a medical and health institution, or a relevant unit discovers any of the circumstances specified in Article 19 of these Regulations, it shall report to the competent health department of the people's government at the county level where it is located within two hours; the health department that receives the report shall, within two hours, report to the people's government at the same level and shall also report to the competent health department at the next higher level and to the competent health department of the State Council.  
The people's government at the county level shall, within two hours of receiving the report, report to the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or to the people's government at the next higher level; the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts shall, within two hours of receiving the report, report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　任何单位和个人对突发事件，不得隐瞒、缓报、谎报或者授意他人隐瞒、缓报、谎报。

**Article 21** No unit or individual may conceal, delay reporting, or make false reports of an emergency, or instigate others to do so.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　接到报告的地方人民政府、卫生行政主管部门依照本条例规定报告的同时，应当立即组织力量对报告事项调查核实、确证，采取必要的控制措施，并及时报告调查情况。

**Article 22** While reporting in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the local people's government or the competent health department that receives the report shall immediately organize forces to investigate and verify the reported matter, confirm it, take necessary control measures, and promptly report the investigation findings.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院卫生行政主管部门应当根据发生突发事件的情况，及时向国务院有关部门和各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门以及军队有关部门通报。  
突发事件发生地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门，应当及时向毗邻省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门通报。  
接到通报的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门，必要时应当及时通知本行政区域内的医疗卫生机构。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门，已经发生或者发现可能引起突发事件的情形时，应当及时向同级人民政府卫生行政主管部门通报。

**Article 23** The competent health department of the State Council shall, in light of the situation of the occurrence of an emergency, promptly notify the relevant departments of the State Council, the competent health departments of the people's governments of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the relevant departments of the armed forces.  
The competent health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the emergency occurs shall promptly notify the competent health departments of the people's governments of the adjacent provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The competent health department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that receives such notification shall, when necessary, promptly notify the medical and health institutions within its administrative region.  
Where a relevant department of a local people's government at or above the county level finds that an emergency has occurred or may occur, it shall promptly notify the competent health department of the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家建立突发事件举报制度，公布统一的突发事件报告、举报电话。  
任何单位和个人有权向人民政府及其有关部门报告突发事件隐患，有权向上级人民政府及其有关部门举报地方人民政府及其有关部门不履行突发事件应急处理职责，或者不按照规定履行职责的情况。接到报告、举报的有关人民政府及其有关部门，应当立即组织对突发事件隐患、不履行或者不按照规定履行突发事件应急处理职责的情况进行调查处理。  
对举报突发事件有功的单位和个人，县级以上各级人民政府及其有关部门应当予以奖励。

**Article 24** The State shall establish a reporting system for emergencies, and shall publish a unified telephone number for reporting and tip-offs on emergencies.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the people's government and its relevant departments on potential hazards of emergencies, and shall have the right to report to the people's government at a higher level and its relevant departments on the failure of a local people's government or its relevant department to perform its duties in emergency response, or on its failure to perform its duties in accordance with the provisions. The relevant people's government or its relevant department that receives such report or tip-off shall immediately organize an investigation and handle the matters concerning the potential hazards or the failure to perform or the failure to properly perform emergency response duties.  
Units and individuals that have rendered meritorious service in reporting emergencies shall be rewarded by the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家建立突发事件的信息发布制度。  
国务院卫生行政主管部门负责向社会发布突发事件的信息。必要时，可以授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政主管部门向社会发布本行政区域内突发事件的信息。  
信息发布应当及时、准确、全面。

**Article 25** The State shall establish an information release system for emergencies.  
The competent health department of the State Council shall be responsible for releasing information on emergencies to the public. When necessary, it may authorize the competent health departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government to release information on emergencies within their respective administrative regions to the public.  
Information shall be released in a timely, accurate, and comprehensive manner.

### 第四章　应急处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　突发事件发生后，卫生行政主管部门应当组织专家对突发事件进行综合评估，初步判断突发事件的类型，提出是否启动突发事件应急预案的建议。

**Article 26** After the occurrence of an emergency, the competent health department shall organize experts to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the emergency, make a preliminary determination of the type of emergency, and put forward a proposal on whether to activate the contingency plan for the emergency.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　在全国范围内或者跨省、自治区、直辖市范围内启动全国突发事件应急预案，由国务院卫生行政主管部门报国务院批准后实施。省、自治区、直辖市启动突发事件应急预案，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定，并向国务院报告。

**Article 27** Activation of the national contingency plan for emergencies within the whole country or across provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be subject to approval by the State Council upon the submission by the competent health department of the State Council and shall then be implemented. Activation of a contingency plan for emergencies by a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be decided by the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　全国突发事件应急处理指挥部对突发事件应急处理工作进行督察和指导，地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当予以配合。  
省、自治区、直辖市突发事件应急处理指挥部对本行政区域内突发事件应急处理工作进行督察和指导。

**Article 28** The National Emergency Response Headquarters for emergencies shall supervise and guide the emergency response work, and local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall cooperate.  
The emergency response headquarters of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall supervise and guide the emergency response work within its administrative region.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　省级以上人民政府卫生行政主管部门或者其他有关部门指定的突发事件应急处理专业技术机构，负责突发事件的技术调查、确证、处置、控制和评价工作。

**Article 29** The professional technical institutions for emergency response designated by the competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level or by other relevant departments shall be responsible for the technical investigation, confirmation, disposal, control, and evaluation of emergencies.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院卫生行政主管部门对新发现的突发传染病，根据危害程度、流行强度，依照《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》的规定及时宣布为法定传染病；宣布为甲类传染病的，由国务院决定。

**Article 30** The competent health department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the harmfulness and epidemic intensity of a newly discovered emergent infectious disease, promptly declare it as a statutory infectious disease in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases; if it is declared as a Class A infectious disease, the decision shall be made by the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　应急预案启动前，县级以上各级人民政府有关部门应当根据突发事件的实际情况，做好应急处理准备，采取必要的应急措施。  
应急预案启动后，突发事件发生地的人民政府有关部门，应当根据预案规定的职责要求，服从突发事件应急处理指挥部的统一指挥，立即到达规定岗位，采取有关的控制措施。  
医疗卫生机构、监测机构和科学研究机构，应当服从突发事件应急处理指挥部的统一指挥，相互配合、协作，集中力量开展相关的科学研究工作。

**Article 31** Before the activation of a contingency plan, the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the actual situation of the emergency, make preparations for emergency response and take necessary emergency measures.  
After the activation of a contingency plan, the relevant departments of the people's government of the place where the emergency occurs shall, in accordance with the duties specified in the plan, obey the unified command of the emergency response headquarters, immediately proceed to the designated posts, and take relevant control measures.  
Medical and health institutions, monitoring institutions, and scientific research institutions shall obey the unified command of the emergency response headquarters, cooperate and collaborate with each other, and concentrate their efforts on conducting relevant scientific research.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　突发事件发生后，国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，应当保证突发事件应急处理所需的医疗救护设备、救治药品、医疗器械等物资的生产、供应；铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门应当保证及时运送。

**Article 32** After the occurrence of an emergency, the relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall ensure the production and supply of medical rescue equipment, medicines for treatment, medical devices, and other materials required for emergency response; the competent administrative departments of railways, transportation, and civil aviation shall ensure timely transportation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　根据突发事件应急处理的需要，突发事件应急处理指挥部有权紧急调集人员、储备的物资、交通工具以及相关设施、设备；必要时，对人员进行疏散或者隔离，并可以依法对传染病疫区实行封锁。

**Article 33** In light of the needs of emergency response, the emergency response headquarters shall have the power to urgently mobilize personnel, stockpiled materials, means of transportation, and relevant facilities and equipment; when necessary, it may evacuate or isolate personnel, and may, in accordance with law, impose a lockdown on an epidemic area of an infectious disease.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　突发事件应急处理指挥部根据突发事件应急处理的需要，可以对食物和水源采取控制措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门应当对突发事件现场等采取控制措施，宣传突发事件防治知识，及时对易受感染的人群和其他易受损害的人群采取应急接种、预防性投药、群体防护等措施。

**Article 34** The emergency response headquarters may, in light of the needs of emergency response, take control measures over food and water sources.  
The competent health departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall take control measures over the emergency scene, etc., publicize knowledge on the prevention and treatment of emergencies, and promptly take measures such as emergency vaccination, preventive medication, and group protection for susceptible populations and other populations vulnerable to harm.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　参加突发事件应急处理的工作人员，应当按照预案的规定，采取卫生防护措施，并在专业人员的指导下进行工作。

**Article 35** Personnel participating in emergency response shall take health protection measures in accordance with the provisions of the contingency plan, and shall work under the guidance of professionals.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院卫生行政主管部门或者其他有关部门指定的专业技术机构，有权进入突发事件现场进行调查、采样、技术分析和检验，对地方突发事件的应急处理工作进行技术指导，有关单位和个人应当予以配合；任何单位和个人不得以任何理由予以拒绝。

**Article 36** Professional technical institutions designated by the competent health department of the State Council or other relevant departments shall have the right to enter the emergency scene to carry out investigations, collect samples, conduct technical analysis and testing, and provide technical guidance for local emergency response work; the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate; no unit or individual may refuse on any ground.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对新发现的突发传染病、不明原因的群体性疾病、重大食物和职业中毒事件，国务院卫生行政主管部门应当尽快组织力量制定相关的技术标准、规范和控制措施。

**Article 37** With respect to newly discovered emergent infectious diseases, mass diseases of unknown cause, and major incidents of food or occupational poisoning, the competent health department of the State Council shall, as soon as possible, organize forces to formulate relevant technical standards, specifications, and control measures.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　交通工具上发现根据国务院卫生行政主管部门的规定需要采取应急控制措施的传染病病人、疑似传染病病人，其负责人应当以最快的方式通知前方停靠点，并向交通工具的营运单位报告。交通工具的前方停靠点和营运单位应当立即向交通工具营运单位行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政主管部门报告。卫生行政主管部门接到报告后，应当立即组织有关人员采取相应的医学处置措施。  
交通工具上的传染病病人密切接触者，由交通工具停靠点的县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政主管部门或者铁路、交通、民用航空行政主管部门，根据各自的职责，依照传染病防治法律、行政法规的规定，采取控制措施。  
涉及国境口岸和入出境的人员、交通工具、货物、集装箱、行李、邮包等需要采取传染病应急控制措施的，依照国境卫生检疫法律、行政法规的规定办理。

**Article 38** If any infectious disease patient or suspected infectious disease patient who requires emergency control measures according to the provisions of the competent health department of the State Council is found on a means of transport, the person in charge of such means of transport shall, by the fastest means, notify the next stopping place and report to the operating unit of the means of transport. The next stopping place and the operating unit of the means of transport shall immediately report to the competent administrative department of the operating unit of the means of transport and to the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level. Upon receiving the report, the competent health department shall immediately organize relevant personnel to take appropriate medical disposal measures.  
Close contacts of infectious disease patients on the means of transport shall be subject to control measures taken by the competent health department of the people's governments at or above the county level of the stopping place of the means of transport, or by the competent administrative departments of railways, transportation, or civil aviation, in accordance with their respective functions and duties and in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.  
If emergency control measures for infectious diseases are required in respect of border ports, persons entering or leaving the country, means of transport, goods, containers, luggage, postal parcels, etc., the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on border health and quarantine.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　医疗卫生机构应当对因突发事件致病的人员提供医疗救护和现场救援，对就诊病人必须接诊治疗，并书写详细、完整的病历记录；对需要转送的病人，应当按照规定将病人及其病历记录的复印件转送至接诊的或者指定的医疗机构。  
医疗卫生机构内应当采取卫生防护措施，防止交叉感染和污染。  
医疗卫生机构应当对传染病病人密切接触者采取医学观察措施，传染病病人密切接触者应当予以配合。  
医疗机构收治传染病病人、疑似传染病病人，应当依法报告所在地的疾病预防控制机构。接到报告的疾病预防控制机构应当立即对可能受到危害的人员进行调查，根据需要采取必要的控制措施。

**Article 39** Medical and health institutions shall provide medical aid and on-site rescue to persons who have become ill due to an emergency; must accept and treat patients who come for medical consultation, and shall write detailed and complete medical records; for patients requiring transfer, they shall transfer the patients and copies of their medical records to the receiving or designated medical institution in accordance with the provisions.  
Health protection measures shall be taken within medical and health institutions to prevent cross-infection and contamination.  
Medical and health institutions shall take medical observation measures on close contacts of infectious disease patients, and the close contacts of infectious disease patients shall cooperate.  
When a medical institution admits an infectious disease patient or a suspected infectious disease patient, it shall report to the local disease prevention and control institution in accordance with law. The disease prevention and control institution that receives the report shall immediately investigate the persons who may be endangered, and take necessary control measures as needed.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　传染病暴发、流行时，街道、乡镇以及居民委员会、村民委员会应当组织力量，团结协作，群防群治，协助卫生行政主管部门和其他有关部门、医疗卫生机构做好疫情信息的收集和报告、人员的分散隔离、公共卫生措施的落实工作，向居民、村民宣传传染病防治的相关知识。

**Article 40** When an infectious disease breaks out or becomes epidemic, sub-districts, townships, and residents' committees and villagers' committees shall organize forces, work in solidarity and cooperation, engage in mass prevention and mass treatment, assist the competent health departments and other relevant departments and medical and health institutions in properly carrying out the collection and reporting of epidemic information, the decentralized isolation of personnel, and the implementation of public health measures, and publicize to residents and villagers the relevant knowledge on the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对传染病暴发、流行区域内流动人口，突发事件发生地的县级以上地方人民政府应当做好预防工作，落实有关卫生控制措施；对传染病病人和疑似传染病病人，应当采取就地隔离、就地观察、就地治疗的措施。对需要治疗和转诊的，应当依照[本条例第三十九条](#art-39)第一款的规定执行。

**Article 41** For the floating population within the area where an infectious disease has broken out or become epidemic, the local people's governments at or above the county level of the place where the emergency occurs shall properly carry out preventive work, and implement relevant health control measures; for infectious disease patients and suspected infectious disease patients, measures of on-site isolation, on-site observation, and on-site treatment shall be taken. For those who need treatment or referral, the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 39 of these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有关部门、医疗卫生机构应当对传染病做到早发现、早报告、早隔离、早治疗，切断传播途径，防止扩散。

**Article 42** The relevant departments and medical and health institutions shall achieve early detection, early reporting, early isolation, and early treatment of infectious diseases, cut off transmission routes, and prevent spread.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上各级人民政府应当提供必要资金，保障因突发事件致病、致残的人员得到及时、有效的救治。具体办法由国务院财政部门、卫生行政主管部门和劳动保障行政主管部门制定。

**Article 43** The people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary funds to ensure that persons who have become ill or disabled due to an emergency receive timely and effective treatment. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department, the competent health department, and the competent department of labor and social security of the State Council.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在突发事件中需要接受隔离治疗、医学观察措施的病人、疑似病人和传染病病人密切接触者在卫生行政主管部门或者有关机构采取医学措施时应当予以配合；拒绝配合的，由公安机关依法协助强制执行。

**Article 44** Patients, suspected patients, and close contacts of infectious disease patients who need to undergo isolation treatment or medical observation measures during an emergency shall cooperate when the competent health department or relevant institution takes medical measures; if they refuse to cooperate, the public security organ shall, in accordance with law, assist in compulsory execution.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府及其卫生行政主管部门未依照本条例的规定履行报告职责，对突发事件隐瞒、缓报、谎报或者授意他人隐瞒、缓报、谎报的，对政府主要领导人及其卫生行政主管部门主要负责人，依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成传染病传播、流行或者对社会公众健康造成其他严重危害后果的，依法给予开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where a local people's government at or above the county level or its competent health department fails to perform its reporting duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, and conceals, delays reporting of, or makes false reports of an emergency, or instigates others to do so, the principal leading person of the government and the principal responsible person of its competent health department shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if the act causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or causes other serious harm to the public health, an administrative sanction of dismissal shall be given in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门未依照本条例的规定，完成突发事件应急处理所需要的设施、设备、药品和医疗器械等物资的生产、供应、运输和储备的，对政府主要领导人和政府部门主要负责人依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；造成传染病传播、流行或者对社会公众健康造成其他严重危害后果的，依法给予开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where the relevant departments of the State Council, a local people's government at or above the county level, or their relevant departments fail to complete the production, supply, transportation, and stockpiling of the facilities, equipment, medicines, medical devices, and other materials required for emergency response in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the principal leading persons of the government and the principal responsible persons of the government departments shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if the act causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or causes other serious harm to the public health, an administrative sanction of dismissal shall be given in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　突发事件发生后，县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门对上级人民政府有关部门的调查不予配合，或者采取其他方式阻碍、干涉调查的，对政府主要领导人和政府部门主要负责人依法给予降级或者撤职的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where, after the occurrence of an emergency, a local people's government at or above the county level or its relevant department fails to cooperate with the investigation conducted by the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level, or obstructs or interferes with the investigation by other means, the principal leading person of the government and the principal responsible person of the government department shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上各级人民政府卫生行政主管部门和其他有关部门在突发事件调查、控制、医疗救治工作中玩忽职守、失职、渎职的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正、通报批评、给予警告；对主要负责人、负有责任的主管人员和其他责任人员依法给予降级、撤职的行政处分；造成传染病传播、流行或者对社会公众健康造成其他严重危害后果的，依法给予开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where the competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant departments are derelict of duty, neglect their duties, or commit malfeasance in the work of emergency investigation, control, or medical treatment, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order them to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, and give them a warning; the principal responsible persons, the persons in charge with responsibility, and other persons directly responsible shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if the act causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or causes other serious harm to the public health, an administrative sanction of dismissal shall be given in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上各级人民政府有关部门拒不履行应急处理职责的，由同级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正、通报批评、给予警告；对主要负责人、负有责任的主管人员和其他责任人员依法给予降级、撤职的行政处分；造成传染病传播、流行或者对社会公众健康造成其他严重危害后果的，依法给予开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level refuse to perform their duties in emergency response, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order them to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, and give them a warning; the principal responsible persons, the persons in charge with responsibility, and other persons directly responsible shall be given an administrative sanction of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if the act causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or causes other serious harm to the public health, an administrative sanction of dismissal shall be given in accordance with law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　医疗卫生机构有下列行为之一的，由卫生行政主管部门责令改正、通报批评、给予警告；情节严重的，吊销《医疗机构执业许可证》；对主要负责人、负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级或者撤职的纪律处分；造成传染病传播、流行或者对社会公众健康造成其他严重危害后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未依照本条例的规定履行报告职责，隐瞒、缓报或者谎报的；  
(二)未依照本条例的规定及时采取控制措施的；  
(三)未依照本条例的规定履行突发事件监测职责的；  
(四)拒绝接诊病人的；  
(五)拒不服从突发事件应急处理指挥部调度的。

**Article 50** Where a medical and health institution commits any of the following acts, the competent health department shall order it to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, and give it a warning; if the circumstances are serious, its Medical Institution Practice Permit shall be revoked; the principal responsible persons, the persons in charge with responsibility, and other persons directly responsible shall be given disciplinary sanctions of demotion or removal from office in accordance with law; if the act causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or causes other serious harm to the public health, and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law:  
(1) Failing to perform reporting duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, and concealing, delaying reporting of, or making false reports;  
(2) Failing to take control measures in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Failing to perform the duty of monitoring emergencies in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(4) Refusing to accept patients for medical consultation;  
(5) Refusing to obey the dispatch of the emergency response headquarters.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　在突发事件应急处理工作中，有关单位和个人未依照本条例的规定履行报告职责，隐瞒、缓报或者谎报，阻碍突发事件应急处理工作人员执行职务，拒绝国务院卫生行政主管部门或者其他有关部门指定的专业技术机构进入突发事件现场，或者不配合调查、采样、技术分析和检验的，对有关责任人员依法给予行政处分或者纪律处分；触犯《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** In the emergency handling of an unexpected incident, if the relevant entities or individuals fail to perform their reporting duties in accordance with these Regulations, conceal, delay, or make false reports, obstruct the emergency response staff from performing their duties, refuse the entry of professional and technical institutions designated by the health administration department of the State Council or other relevant departments into the site of the unexpected incident, or fail to cooperate with investigations, sampling, technical analysis, or inspections, the responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions or disciplinary sanctions according to law; if they violate the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments and constitute an act against public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在突发事件发生期间，散布谣言、哄抬物价、欺骗消费者，扰乱社会秩序、市场秩序的，由公安机关或者工商行政管理部门依法给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** During the period of an unexpected incident, anyone who spreads rumors, profiteering by raising prices, deceives consumers, or disrupts social order or market order shall be given an administrative penalty by the public security organ or the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　中国人民解放军、武装警察部队医疗卫生机构参与突发事件应急处理的，依照本条例的规定和军队的相关规定执行。

**Article 53** Where the medical and health institutions of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Armed Police Force participate in the emergency handling of an unexpected incident, they shall act in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and the relevant provisions of the armed forces.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 54** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
