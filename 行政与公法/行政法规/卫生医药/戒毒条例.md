# 戒毒条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-18  
**生效日期**：2018-09-18

> (2011年6月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第597号公布　根据2018年9月18日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范戒毒工作，帮助吸毒成瘾人员戒除毒瘾，维护社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国禁毒法》，制定本条例。 第二条　县级以上人民政府应当建立政府统一领导，禁毒委员会组织、协调、指导，有关部门各负其责，社会力量广泛参与的戒毒工作体制。 戒毒工作坚持以人为本、科学戒毒、综合矫治、关怀救助的原则，采取自愿戒毒、社区戒毒、强制隔离戒毒、社区康复等多种措施，建立戒毒治疗、康复指导、救助服务兼备的工作体系。 第三条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家有关规定将戒毒工作所需经费列入本级财政预算。 第四条　县级以上地方人民政府设立的禁毒委员会可以组织公安机关、卫生行政和负责药品监督管理的部门开展吸毒监测、调查，并向社会公开监测、调查结果。 县级以上地方人民政府公安机关负责对涉嫌吸毒人员进行检测，对吸毒人员进行登记并依法实行动态管控，依法责令社区戒毒、决定强制隔离戒毒、责令社区康复，管理公安机关的强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供指导和支持。 设区的市级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门负责管理司法行政部门的强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供指导和支持。 县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责戒毒医疗机构的监督管理，会同公安机关、司法行政等部门制定戒毒医疗机构设置规划，对戒毒医疗服务提供指导和支持。 县级以上地方人民政府民政、人力资源社会保障、教育等部门依据各自的职责，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供康复和职业技能培训等指导和支持。 第五条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处负责社区戒毒、社区康复工作。 第六条　县级、设区的市级人民政府需要设置强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所的，应当合理布局，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并纳入当地国民经济和社会发展规划。 强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所的建设标准，由国务院建设部门、发展改革部门会同国务院公安部门、司法行政部门制定。 第七条　戒毒人员在入学、就业、享受社会保障等方面不受歧视。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范戒毒工作，帮助吸毒成瘾人员戒除毒瘾，维护社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国禁毒法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Drug Control Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of regulating drug rehabilitation work, helping drug addicts overcome their addiction, and maintaining social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　县级以上人民政府应当建立政府统一领导，禁毒委员会组织、协调、指导，有关部门各负其责，社会力量广泛参与的戒毒工作体制。  
戒毒工作坚持以人为本、科学戒毒、综合矫治、关怀救助的原则，采取自愿戒毒、社区戒毒、强制隔离戒毒、社区康复等多种措施，建立戒毒治疗、康复指导、救助服务兼备的工作体系。

**Article 2** People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a drug rehabilitation work system featuring unified leadership by the government, organization, coordination, and guidance by the drug control committee, responsibility of relevant departments for their respective work, and extensive participation by social forces.  
Drug rehabilitation work shall adhere to the principles of people-orientedness, scientific drug rehabilitation, comprehensive correction, and care and rescue, adopt multiple measures such as voluntary drug rehabilitation, community drug rehabilitation, compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, and community rehabilitation, and establish a work system integrating drug rehabilitation treatment, rehabilitation guidance, and assistance services.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当按照国家有关规定将戒毒工作所需经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 3** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, include the funds necessary for drug rehabilitation work in the fiscal budgets at the same level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上地方人民政府设立的禁毒委员会可以组织公安机关、卫生行政和负责药品监督管理的部门开展吸毒监测、调查，并向社会公开监测、调查结果。  
县级以上地方人民政府公安机关负责对涉嫌吸毒人员进行检测，对吸毒人员进行登记并依法实行动态管控，依法责令社区戒毒、决定强制隔离戒毒、责令社区康复，管理公安机关的强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供指导和支持。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门负责管理司法行政部门的强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供指导和支持。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门负责戒毒医疗机构的监督管理，会同公安机关、司法行政等部门制定戒毒医疗机构设置规划，对戒毒医疗服务提供指导和支持。  
县级以上地方人民政府民政、人力资源社会保障、教育等部门依据各自的职责，对社区戒毒、社区康复工作提供康复和职业技能培训等指导和支持。

**Article 4** The drug control committee established by a local people's government at or above the county level may organize public security organs, health administrative departments, and departments responsible for drug supervision and administration to carry out drug abuse monitoring and investigation, and shall make the monitoring and investigation results public to society.  
The public security organ of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for testing persons suspected of drug abuse, registering drug abusers and exercising dynamic control according to law, ordering community drug rehabilitation, deciding compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, ordering community rehabilitation according to law, managing the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facilities and drug rehabilitation facilities of public security organs, and providing guidance and support for community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation work.  
The judicial administrative department of a local people's government at or above the city with districts shall be responsible for managing the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facilities and drug rehabilitation facilities of the judicial administrative department, and providing guidance and support for community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation work.  
The health administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of drug rehabilitation medical institutions, jointly with public security organs, judicial administrative departments and other departments, formulate plans for the establishment of drug rehabilitation medical institutions, and provide guidance and support for drug rehabilitation medical services.  
The civil affairs, human resources and social security, education and other departments of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, pursuant to their respective functions, provide guidance and support for community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation work in terms of rehabilitation and vocational skills training, etc.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处负责社区戒毒、社区康复工作。

**Article 5** The people's government of a township (town) or the sub-district office of a city shall be responsible for community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级、设区的市级人民政府需要设置强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所的，应当合理布局，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，并纳入当地国民经济和社会发展规划。  
强制隔离戒毒场所、戒毒康复场所的建设标准，由国务院建设部门、发展改革部门会同国务院公安部门、司法行政部门制定。

**Article 6** Where a people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts needs to establish compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facilities or drug rehabilitation facilities, it shall make a reasonable layout, report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval, and incorporate the facilities into the local national economic and social development plan.  
The construction standards for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facilities and drug rehabilitation facilities shall be formulated by the construction department and the development and reform department of the State Council jointly with the public security department and the judicial administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　戒毒人员在入学、就业、享受社会保障等方面不受歧视。  
对戒毒人员戒毒的个人信息应当依法予以保密。对戒断3年未复吸的人员，不再实行动态管控。

**Article 7** Drug rehabilitation persons shall not be discriminated against in school enrollment, employment, enjoyment of social security, etc.  
The personal information of drug rehabilitation persons regarding their drug rehabilitation shall be kept confidential according to law. For persons who have abstained from drugs for three years without relapse, dynamic control shall no longer be exercised.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励、扶持社会组织、企业、事业单位和个人参与戒毒科研、戒毒社会服务和戒毒社会公益事业。  
对在戒毒工作中有显著成绩和突出贡献的，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 8** The State encourages and supports social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and individuals to participate in drug rehabilitation scientific research, drug rehabilitation social services, and drug rehabilitation social welfare undertakings.  
Those who have achieved remarkable results and made outstanding contributions in drug rehabilitation work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　自愿戒毒

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励吸毒成瘾人员自行戒除毒瘾。吸毒人员可以自行到戒毒医疗机构接受戒毒治疗。对自愿接受戒毒治疗的吸毒人员，公安机关对其原吸毒行为不予处罚。

**Article 9** The State encourages drug addicts to voluntarily overcome their addiction. Drug abusers may voluntarily go to drug rehabilitation medical institutions to receive drug rehabilitation treatment. For drug abusers who voluntarily receive drug rehabilitation treatment, the public security organ shall not impose punishment on their original drug abuse behavior.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　戒毒医疗机构应当与自愿戒毒人员或者其监护人签订自愿戒毒协议，就戒毒方法、戒毒期限、戒毒的个人信息保密、戒毒人员应当遵守的规章制度、终止戒毒治疗的情形等作出约定，并应当载明戒毒疗效、戒毒治疗风险。

**Article 10** A drug rehabilitation medical institution shall sign a voluntary drug rehabilitation agreement with a voluntary drug rehabilitation person or his or her guardian, stipulating matters such as drug rehabilitation methods, drug rehabilitation period, confidentiality of the drug rehabilitation person's personal information, rules and regulations to be complied with by the drug rehabilitation person, and circumstances for termination of drug rehabilitation treatment, and shall specify the drug rehabilitation efficacy and risks of drug rehabilitation treatment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　戒毒医疗机构应当履行下列义务：  
(一)对自愿戒毒人员开展艾滋病等传染病的预防、咨询教育；  
(二)对自愿戒毒人员采取脱毒治疗、心理康复、行为矫治等多种治疗措施，并应当符合国务院卫生行政部门制定的戒毒治疗规范；  
(三)采用科学、规范的诊疗技术和方法，使用的药物、医院制剂、医疗器械应当符合国家有关规定；  
(四)依法加强药品管理，防止麻醉药品、精神药品流失滥用。

**Article 11** A drug rehabilitation medical institution shall perform the following obligations:  
(1) carry out prevention and counseling education on HIV/AIDS and other infectious diseases for voluntary drug rehabilitation persons;  
(2) adopt multiple treatment measures such as detoxification treatment, psychological rehabilitation, and behavioral correction for voluntary drug rehabilitation persons, and the measures shall comply with the drug rehabilitation treatment standards formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council;  
(3) adopt scientific and standardized diagnosis and treatment technologies and methods, and the drugs, hospital preparations, and medical devices used shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) strengthen drug management according to law to prevent the loss and abuse of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　符合参加戒毒药物维持治疗条件的戒毒人员，由本人申请，并经登记，可以参加戒毒药物维持治疗。登记参加戒毒药物维持治疗的戒毒人员的信息应当及时报公安机关备案。  
戒毒药物维持治疗的管理办法，由国务院卫生行政部门会同国务院公安部门、药品监督管理部门制定。

**Article 12** A drug rehabilitation person who meets the conditions for participating in drug rehabilitation medication maintenance treatment may, upon his or her own application and after registration, participate in drug rehabilitation medication maintenance treatment. The information of drug rehabilitation persons registered to participate in drug rehabilitation medication maintenance treatment shall be promptly reported to the public security organ for record.  
The administrative measures for drug rehabilitation medication maintenance treatment shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council jointly with the public security department and the drug supervision and administration department of the State Council.

### 第三章　社区戒毒

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对吸毒成瘾人员，县级、设区的市级人民政府公安机关可以责令其接受社区戒毒，并出具责令社区戒毒决定书，送达本人及其家属，通知本人户籍所在地或者现居住地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处。

**Article 13** For a drug addict, the public security organ of a people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts may order him or her to undergo community drug rehabilitation, issue a decision order on community drug rehabilitation, serve it on the person and his or her family, and notify the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the place of his or her registered residence or current residence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　社区戒毒人员应当自收到责令社区戒毒决定书之日起15日内到社区戒毒执行地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处报到，无正当理由逾期不报到的，视为拒绝接受社区戒毒。  
社区戒毒的期限为3年，自报到之日起计算。

**Article 14** A community drug rehabilitation person shall report to the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the place of community drug rehabilitation enforcement within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision order on community drug rehabilitation; failure to report within the time limit without justifiable grounds shall be deemed refusal to accept community drug rehabilitation.  
The period of community drug rehabilitation shall be three years, calculated from the date of reporting.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处应当根据工作需要成立社区戒毒工作领导小组，配备社区戒毒专职工作人员，制定社区戒毒工作计划，落实社区戒毒措施。

**Article 15** The people's government of a township (town) or the sub-district office of a city shall set up a community drug rehabilitation work leadership group according to work requirements, assign full-time community drug rehabilitation staff, formulate community drug rehabilitation work plans, and implement community drug rehabilitation measures.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处，应当在社区戒毒人员报到后及时与其签订社区戒毒协议，明确社区戒毒的具体措施、社区戒毒人员应当遵守的规定以及违反社区戒毒协议应承担的责任。

**Article 16** The people's government of a township (town) or the sub-district office of a city shall, promptly after the community drug rehabilitation person reports, sign a community drug rehabilitation agreement with him or her, specifying the specific measures of community drug rehabilitation, the rules to be complied with by the community drug rehabilitation person, and the responsibilities to be assumed for violating the community drug rehabilitation agreement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　社区戒毒专职工作人员、社区民警、社区医务人员、社区戒毒人员的家庭成员以及禁毒志愿者共同组成社区戒毒工作小组具体实施社区戒毒。

**Article 17** The full-time community drug rehabilitation staff, community police officers, community medical personnel, family members of the community drug rehabilitation person, and anti-drug volunteers shall jointly form a community drug rehabilitation working group to specifically implement community drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处和社区戒毒工作小组应当采取下列措施管理、帮助社区戒毒人员：  
(一)戒毒知识辅导；  
(二)教育、劝诫；  
(三)职业技能培训，职业指导，就学、就业、就医援助；  
(四)帮助戒毒人员戒除毒瘾的其他措施。

**Article 18** The people's government of a township (town) or the sub-district office of a city and the community drug rehabilitation working group shall adopt the following measures to manage and assist community drug rehabilitation persons:  
(1) guidance on drug rehabilitation knowledge;  
(2) education and admonishment;  
(3) vocational skills training, vocational guidance, and assistance in school enrollment, employment, and medical treatment;  
(4) other measures to help drug rehabilitation persons overcome drug addiction.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　社区戒毒人员应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)履行社区戒毒协议；  
(二)根据公安机关的要求，定期接受检测；  
(三)离开社区戒毒执行地所在县(市、区)3日以上的，须书面报告。

**Article 19** A community drug rehabilitation person shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) perform the community drug rehabilitation agreement;  
(2) undergo regular testing as required by the public security organ;  
(3) if leaving the county (city, district) where the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place is located for more than three days, he or she shall report in writing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　社区戒毒人员在社区戒毒期间，逃避或者拒绝接受检测3次以上，擅自离开社区戒毒执行地所在县(市、区)3次以上或者累计超过30日的，属于《中华人民共和国禁毒法》规定的“严重违反社区戒毒协议”。

**Article 20** If a community drug rehabilitation person, during community drug rehabilitation, evades or refuses to undergo testing three times or more, leaves the county (city, district) where the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place is located without authorization three times or more or for a cumulative period exceeding 30 days, it shall constitute a "serious violation of the community drug rehabilitation agreement" as provided in the Drug Control Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　社区戒毒人员拒绝接受社区戒毒，在社区戒毒期间又吸食、注射毒品，以及严重违反社区戒毒协议的，社区戒毒专职工作人员应当及时向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 21** If a community drug rehabilitation person refuses to accept community drug rehabilitation, once again ingests or injects drugs during the community drug rehabilitation period, or seriously violates the community drug rehabilitation agreement, the full-time community drug rehabilitation staff shall promptly report to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　社区戒毒人员的户籍所在地或者现居住地发生变化，需要变更社区戒毒执行地的，社区戒毒执行地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处应当将有关材料转送至变更后的乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处。  
社区戒毒人员应当自社区戒毒执行地变更之日起15日内前往变更后的乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处报到，社区戒毒时间自报到之日起连续计算。  
变更后的乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处，应当按照[本条例第十六条](#art-16)的规定，与社区戒毒人员签订新的社区戒毒协议，继续执行社区戒毒。

**Article 22** If the place of registered residence or current residence of a community drug rehabilitation person changes, necessitating a change in the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place, the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the original community drug rehabilitation enforcement place shall transfer the relevant materials to the changed people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city.  
The community drug rehabilitation person shall report to the changed people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city within 15 days from the date of change of the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place, and the community drug rehabilitation period shall be calculated consecutively from the date of reporting.  
The changed people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city shall, in accordance with Article 16 of these Regulations, sign a new community drug rehabilitation agreement with the community drug rehabilitation person and continue the enforcement of community drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　社区戒毒自期满之日起解除。社区戒毒执行地公安机关应当出具解除社区戒毒通知书送达社区戒毒人员本人及其家属，并在7日内通知社区戒毒执行地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处。

**Article 23** Community drug rehabilitation shall be dissolved from the date of expiration of the period. The public security organ at the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place shall issue a notice of dissolution of community drug rehabilitation, serve it on the community drug rehabilitation person and his or her family, and notify the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the community drug rehabilitation enforcement place within seven days.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　社区戒毒人员被依法收监执行刑罚、采取强制性教育措施的，社区戒毒终止。  
社区戒毒人员被依法拘留、逮捕的，社区戒毒中止，由羁押场所给予必要的戒毒治疗，释放后继续接受社区戒毒。

**Article 24** If a community drug rehabilitation person is taken into custody to execute a criminal penalty or is subjected to compulsory educational measures according to law, the community drug rehabilitation shall terminate.  
If a community drug rehabilitation person is detained or arrested according to law, the community drug rehabilitation shall be suspended, and the custody place shall provide necessary drug rehabilitation treatment; after release, he or she shall continue to undergo community drug rehabilitation.

### 第四章　强制隔离戒毒

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　吸毒成瘾人员有[《中华人民共和国禁毒法》第三十八条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国禁毒法.md#art-38)第一款所列情形之一的，由县级、设区的市级人民政府公安机关作出强制隔离戒毒的决定。  
对于吸毒成瘾严重，通过社区戒毒难以戒除毒瘾的人员，县级、设区的市级人民政府公安机关可以直接作出强制隔离戒毒的决定。  
吸毒成瘾人员自愿接受强制隔离戒毒的，经强制隔离戒毒场所所在地县级、设区的市级人民政府公安机关同意，可以进入强制隔离戒毒场所戒毒。强制隔离戒毒场所应当与其就戒毒治疗期限、戒毒治疗措施等作出约定。

**Article 25** If a drug addict falls under any of the circumstances listed in Article 38, paragraph 1 of the Drug Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the public security organ of the people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts shall make a decision on compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation.  
For a drug addict with severe addiction who is difficult to overcome through community drug rehabilitation, the public security organ of the people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts may directly make a decision on compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation.  
If a drug addict voluntarily accepts compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, with the consent of the public security organ of the people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts at the location of the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility, he or she may enter the facility for drug rehabilitation. The compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall make an agreement with him or her on the drug rehabilitation treatment period, drug rehabilitation treatment measures, etc.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对依照[《中华人民共和国禁毒法》第三十九条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国禁毒法.md#art-39)第一款规定不适用强制隔离戒毒的吸毒成瘾人员，县级、设区的市级人民政府公安机关应当作出社区戒毒的决定，依照本条例第三章的规定进行社区戒毒。

**Article 26** For a drug addict to whom compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation is not applicable in accordance with Article 39, paragraph 1 of the Drug Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the public security organ of the people's government at the county level or at the level of a city with districts shall make a decision on community drug rehabilitation, and community drug rehabilitation shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Chapter III of these Regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　强制隔离戒毒的期限为2年，自作出强制隔离戒毒决定之日起计算。  
被强制隔离戒毒的人员在公安机关的强制隔离戒毒场所执行强制隔离戒毒3个月至6个月后，转至司法行政部门的强制隔离戒毒场所继续执行强制隔离戒毒。  
执行前款规定不具备条件的省、自治区、直辖市，由公安机关和司法行政部门共同提出意见报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府决定具体执行方案，但在公安机关的强制隔离戒毒场所执行强制隔离戒毒的时间不得超过12个月。

**Article 27** The period of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall be two years, calculated from the date on which the decision on compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation is made.  
A person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall, after undergoing compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation in a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility of a public security organ for three to six months, be transferred to a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility of a judicial administrative department to continue the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation.  
For a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that does not meet the conditions for implementing the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the public security organ and the judicial administrative department shall jointly submit opinions to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for a decision on the specific implementation plan; however, the period of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation executed in the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility of the public security organ shall not exceed 12 months.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　强制隔离戒毒场所对强制隔离戒毒人员的身体和携带物品进行检查时发现的毒品等违禁品，应当依法处理；对生活必需品以外的其他物品，由强制隔离戒毒场所代为保管。  
女性强制隔离戒毒人员的身体检查，应当由女性工作人员进行。

**Article 28** When a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility inspects the body and carried articles of a person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, any narcotic drugs or other prohibited articles discovered shall be dealt with according to law; articles other than daily necessities shall be kept in custody by the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility on behalf of the person.  
Physical examination of a female person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall be conducted by female staff members.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　强制隔离戒毒场所设立戒毒医疗机构应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生行政部门批准。强制隔离戒毒场所应当配备设施设备及必要的管理人员，依法为强制隔离戒毒人员提供科学规范的戒毒治疗、心理治疗、身体康复训练和卫生、道德、法制教育，开展职业技能培训。

**Article 29** The establishment of a drug rehabilitation medical institution in a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall be subject to approval by the health administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government at the location. A compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall be equipped with facilities and equipment and necessary management personnel, and shall provide scientific and standardized drug rehabilitation treatment, psychological treatment, physical rehabilitation training, and health, moral, and legal education to persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation according to law, and carry out vocational skills training.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　强制隔离戒毒场所应当根据强制隔离戒毒人员的性别、年龄、患病等情况对强制隔离戒毒人员实行分别管理；对吸食不同种类毒品的，应当有针对性地采取必要的治疗措施；根据戒毒治疗的不同阶段和强制隔离戒毒人员的表现，实行逐步适应社会的分级管理。

**Article 30** A compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall implement separate management of persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation based on their gender, age, illness conditions, etc.; for those who have ingested different types of drugs, targeted necessary treatment measures shall be adopted; based on the different stages of drug rehabilitation treatment and the performance of persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, a graded management that gradually adapts to society shall be implemented.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　强制隔离戒毒人员患严重疾病，不出所治疗可能危及生命的，经强制隔离戒毒场所主管机关批准，并报强制隔离戒毒决定机关备案，强制隔离戒毒场所可以允许其所外就医。所外就医的费用由强制隔离戒毒人员本人承担。  
所外就医期间，强制隔离戒毒期限连续计算。对于健康状况不再适宜回所执行强制隔离戒毒的，强制隔离戒毒场所应当向强制隔离戒毒决定机关提出变更为社区戒毒的建议，强制隔离戒毒决定机关应当自收到建议之日起7日内，作出是否批准的决定。经批准变更为社区戒毒的，已执行的强制隔离戒毒期限折抵社区戒毒期限。

**Article 31** If a person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation suffers from a serious illness and staying in the facility for treatment may be life-threatening, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility may, with the approval of the competent authority of the facility and after reporting to the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation for record, allow the person to receive medical treatment outside the facility. The expenses for medical treatment outside the facility shall be borne by the person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation.  
During the period of medical treatment outside the facility, the period of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall be calculated consecutively. If the health condition no longer makes it suitable for the person to return to the facility to undergo compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall propose to the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation to change to community drug rehabilitation, and the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the proposal, make a decision on whether to approve. If the change to community drug rehabilitation is approved, the period of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation already executed shall be offset against the period of community drug rehabilitation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　强制隔离戒毒人员脱逃的，强制隔离戒毒场所应当立即通知所在地县级人民政府公安机关，并配合公安机关追回脱逃人员。被追回的强制隔离戒毒人员应当继续执行强制隔离戒毒，脱逃期间不计入强制隔离戒毒期限。被追回的强制隔离戒毒人员不得提前解除强制隔离戒毒。

**Article 32** If a person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation escapes, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall immediately notify the public security organ of the people's government at the county level at the location, and shall cooperate with the public security organ to recover the escaped person. The recovered person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall continue to undergo compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, and the period of escape shall not be counted as part of the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation period. The recovered person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall not have his or her compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation dissolved early.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对强制隔离戒毒场所依照[《中华人民共和国禁毒法》第四十七条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国禁毒法.md#art-47)第二款、第三款规定提出的提前解除强制隔离戒毒、延长戒毒期限的意见，强制隔离戒毒决定机关应当自收到意见之日起7日内，作出是否批准的决定。对提前解除强制隔离戒毒或者延长强制隔离戒毒期限的，批准机关应当出具提前解除强制隔离戒毒决定书或者延长强制隔离戒毒期限决定书，送达被决定人，并在送达后24小时以内通知被决定人的家属、所在单位以及其户籍所在地或者现居住地公安派出所。

**Article 33** With respect to the opinions on early dissolution of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation or extension of the drug rehabilitation period submitted by a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility in accordance with Article 47, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Drug Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the opinions, make a decision on whether to approve. If early dissolution of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation or extension of the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation period is approved, the approving organ shall issue a decision on early dissolution of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation or a decision on extension of the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation period, serve it on the person subject to the decision, and notify the person's family members, work unit, and the public security police station at the place of his or her registered residence or current residence within 24 hours after service.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　解除强制隔离戒毒的，强制隔离戒毒场所应当在解除强制隔离戒毒3日前通知强制隔离戒毒决定机关，出具解除强制隔离戒毒证明书送达戒毒人员本人，并通知其家属、所在单位、其户籍所在地或者现居住地公安派出所将其领回。

**Article 34** If compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation is dissolved, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility shall, three days before the dissolution, notify the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, issue a certificate of dissolution of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation and serve it on the drug rehabilitation person, and notify his or her family members, work unit, and the public security police station at the place of his or her registered residence or current residence to take him or her back.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　强制隔离戒毒诊断评估办法由国务院公安部门、司法行政部门会同国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 35** The diagnostic evaluation measures for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall be formulated by the public security department and the judicial administrative department of the State Council jointly with the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　强制隔离戒毒人员被依法收监执行刑罚、采取强制性教育措施或者被依法拘留、逮捕的，由监管场所、羁押场所给予必要的戒毒治疗，强制隔离戒毒的时间连续计算；刑罚执行完毕时、解除强制性教育措施时或者释放时强制隔离戒毒尚未期满的，继续执行强制隔离戒毒。

**Article 36** If a person subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation is taken into custody to execute a criminal penalty, is subjected to compulsory educational measures, or is detained or arrested according to law, the detention or custody place shall provide necessary drug rehabilitation treatment, and the period of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall be calculated consecutively; if the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation period has not expired at the time of completion of the criminal penalty execution, dissolution of the compulsory educational measure, or release, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall continue to be executed.

### 第五章　社区康复

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对解除强制隔离戒毒的人员，强制隔离戒毒的决定机关可以责令其接受不超过3年的社区康复。  
社区康复在当事人户籍所在地或者现居住地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处执行，经当事人同意，也可以在戒毒康复场所中执行。

**Article 37** For a person whose compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation has been dissolved, the decision-making organ for compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation may order him or her to undergo community rehabilitation for a period not exceeding three years.  
Community rehabilitation shall be executed at the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the party's registered residence or current residence, and with the party's consent, may also be executed in a drug rehabilitation facility.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　被责令接受社区康复的人员，应当自收到责令社区康复决定书之日起15日内到户籍所在地或者现居住地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处报到，签订社区康复协议。  
被责令接受社区康复的人员拒绝接受社区康复或者严重违反社区康复协议，并再次吸食、注射毒品被决定强制隔离戒毒的，强制隔离戒毒不得提前解除。

**Article 38** A person ordered to undergo community rehabilitation shall report to the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the place of his or her registered residence or current residence within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision order on community rehabilitation, and sign a community rehabilitation agreement.  
If a person ordered to undergo community rehabilitation refuses to accept community rehabilitation or seriously violates the community rehabilitation agreement, and is once again subjected to a decision on compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation due to ingesting or injecting drugs, the compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation shall not be dissolved early.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　负责社区康复工作的人员应当为社区康复人员提供必要的心理治疗和辅导、职业技能培训、职业指导以及就学、就业、就医援助。

**Article 39** Personnel responsible for community rehabilitation work shall provide community rehabilitation persons with necessary psychological treatment and counseling, vocational skills training, vocational guidance, and assistance in school enrollment, employment, and medical treatment.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　社区康复自期满之日起解除。社区康复执行地公安机关出具解除社区康复通知书送达社区康复人员本人及其家属，并在7日内通知社区康复执行地乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处。

**Article 40** Community rehabilitation shall be dissolved from the date of expiration of the period. The public security organ at the community rehabilitation enforcement place shall issue a notice of dissolution of community rehabilitation, serve it on the community rehabilitation person and his or her family, and notify the people's government of the township (town) or the sub-district office of the city at the community rehabilitation enforcement place within seven days.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　自愿戒毒人员、社区戒毒、社区康复的人员可以自愿与戒毒康复场所签订协议，到戒毒康复场所戒毒康复、生活和劳动。  
戒毒康复场所应当配备必要的管理人员和医务人员，为戒毒人员提供戒毒康复、职业技能培训和生产劳动条件。

**Article 41** Voluntary drug rehabilitation persons, community drug rehabilitation persons, and community rehabilitation persons may voluntarily sign an agreement with a drug rehabilitation facility to undergo drug rehabilitation, live, and work at the facility.  
A drug rehabilitation facility shall be equipped with necessary management personnel and medical personnel, and shall provide drug rehabilitation persons with drug rehabilitation, vocational skills training, and production labor conditions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　戒毒康复场所应当加强管理，严禁毒品流入，并建立戒毒康复人员自我管理、自我教育、自我服务的机制。  
戒毒康复场所组织戒毒人员参加生产劳动，应当参照国家劳动用工制度的规定支付劳动报酬。

**Article 42** A drug rehabilitation facility shall strengthen management, strictly prohibit the entry of drugs, and establish a mechanism for drug rehabilitation persons to self-manage, self-educate, and self-serve.  
If a drug rehabilitation facility organizes drug rehabilitation persons to participate in production labor, it shall pay labor remuneration with reference to the provisions of the national labor employment system.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　公安、司法行政、卫生行政等有关部门工作人员泄露戒毒人员个人信息的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** If staff members of relevant departments such as public security, judicial administration, and health administration disclose the personal information of drug rehabilitation persons, they shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　乡(镇)人民政府、城市街道办事处负责社区戒毒、社区康复工作的人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分：  
(一)未与社区戒毒、社区康复人员签订社区戒毒、社区康复协议，不落实社区戒毒、社区康复措施的；  
(二)不履行[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)规定的报告义务的；  
(三)其他不履行社区戒毒、社区康复监督职责的行为。

**Article 44** If a person in charge of community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation work of the people's government of a township (town) or the sub-district office of a city commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to sign a community drug rehabilitation or community rehabilitation agreement with a community drug rehabilitation or community rehabilitation person, and failing to implement community drug rehabilitation or community rehabilitation measures;  
(2) failing to perform the reporting obligation provided in Article 21 of these Regulations;  
(3) other acts of failing to perform the duty of supervision over community drug rehabilitation and community rehabilitation.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　强制隔离戒毒场所的工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)侮辱、虐待、体罚强制隔离戒毒人员的；  
(二)收受、索要财物的；  
(三)擅自使用、损毁、处理没收或者代为保管的财物的；  
(四)为强制隔离戒毒人员提供麻醉药品、精神药品或者违反规定传递其他物品的；  
(五)在强制隔离戒毒诊断评估工作中弄虚作假的；  
(六)私放强制隔离戒毒人员的；  
(七)其他徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、不履行法定职责的行为。

**Article 45** If a staff member of a compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation facility commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) insulting, mistreating, or physically punishing persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation;  
(2) accepting or soliciting property;  
(3) unauthorized use, damage, or disposal of property confiscated or kept in custody on behalf of others;  
(4) providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances to persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation, or delivering other articles in violation of regulations;  
(5) committing fraud in the diagnostic evaluation of compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation;  
(6) illegally releasing persons subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation;  
(7) other acts of engaging in malpractice for personal gain, dereliction of duty, or failing to perform statutory duties.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。1995年1月12日国务院发布的《强制戒毒办法》同时废止。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Measures for Compulsory Drug Rehabilitation promulgated by the State Council on January 12, 1995 shall be abolished simultaneously.
