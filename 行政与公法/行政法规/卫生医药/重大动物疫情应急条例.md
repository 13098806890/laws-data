# 重大动物疫情应急条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2017-10-07

> (2005年11月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第450号发布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了迅速控制、扑灭重大动物疫情，保障养殖业生产安全，保护公众身体健康与生命安全，维护正常的社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称重大动物疫情，是指高致病性禽流感等发病率或者死亡率高的动物疫病突然发生，迅速传播，给养殖业生产安全造成严重威胁、危害，以及可能对公众身体健康与生命安全造成危害的情形，包括特别重大动物疫情。 第三条　重大动物疫情应急工作应当坚持加强领导、密切配合，依靠科学、依法防治，群防群控、果断处置的方针，及时发现，快速反应，严格处理，减少损失。 第四条　重大动物疫情应急工作按照属地管理的原则，实行政府统一领导、部门分工负责，逐级建立责任制。 县级以上人民政府兽医主管部门具体负责组织重大动物疫情的监测、调查、控制、扑灭等应急工作。 县级以上人民政府林业主管部门、兽医主管部门按照职责分工，加强对陆生野生动物疫源疫病的监测。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，做好重大动物疫情的应急工作。 第五条　出入境检验检疫机关应当及时收集境外重大动物疫情信息，加强进出境动物及其产品的检验检疫工作，防止动物疫病传入和传出。兽医主管部门要及时向出入境检验检疫机关通报国内重大动物疫情。 第六条　国家鼓励、支持开展重大动物疫情监测、预防、应急处理等有关技术的科学研究和国际交流与合作。 第七条　县级以上人民政府应当对参加重大动物疫情应急处理的人员给予适当补助，对作出贡献的人员给予表彰和奖励。 第八条　对不履行或者不按照规定履行重大动物疫情应急处理职责的行为，任何单位和个人有权检举控告。 第二章　应急准备 第九条　国务院兽医主管部门应当制定全国重大动物疫情应急预案，报国务院批准，并按照不同动物疫病病种及其流行特点和危害程度，分别制定实施方案，报国务院备案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了迅速控制、扑灭重大动物疫情，保障养殖业生产安全，保护公众身体健康与生命安全，维护正常的社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China with a view to rapidly controlling and eradicating major animal epidemics, ensuring the safety of animal husbandry production, protecting public health and safety, and maintaining normal social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称重大动物疫情，是指高致病性禽流感等发病率或者死亡率高的动物疫病突然发生，迅速传播，给养殖业生产安全造成严重威胁、危害，以及可能对公众身体健康与生命安全造成危害的情形，包括特别重大动物疫情。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the term 'major animal epidemics' means the sudden outbreak and rapid spread of animal diseases with high morbidity or mortality, such as highly pathogenic avian influenza, which seriously threaten or endanger the safety of animal husbandry production and may endanger public health and safety, including particularly major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　重大动物疫情应急工作应当坚持加强领导、密切配合，依靠科学、依法防治，群防群控、果断处置的方针，及时发现，快速反应，严格处理，减少损失。

**Article 3** The emergency response to major animal epidemics shall adhere to the principles of strengthening leadership and close cooperation, relying on science and handling according to law, mass prevention and control, and decisive handling, so as to achieve timely detection, rapid response, strict treatment, and loss reduction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　重大动物疫情应急工作按照属地管理的原则，实行政府统一领导、部门分工负责，逐级建立责任制。  
县级以上人民政府兽医主管部门具体负责组织重大动物疫情的监测、调查、控制、扑灭等应急工作。  
县级以上人民政府林业主管部门、兽医主管部门按照职责分工，加强对陆生野生动物疫源疫病的监测。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，做好重大动物疫情的应急工作。

**Article 4** The emergency response to major animal epidemics shall follow the principle of territorial management, implement unified leadership by the government and division of responsibilities among departments, and establish a responsibility system at each level.  
The veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall be specifically responsible for organizing emergency work such as monitoring, investigation, control, and eradication of major animal epidemics.  
The forestry administrative department and the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective responsibilities, strengthen the monitoring of epidemic sources and diseases of terrestrial wild animals.  
Other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective responsibilities, properly carry out the emergency response work for major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　出入境检验检疫机关应当及时收集境外重大动物疫情信息，加强进出境动物及其产品的检验检疫工作，防止动物疫病传入和传出。兽医主管部门要及时向出入境检验检疫机关通报国内重大动物疫情。

**Article 5** The entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities shall promptly collect information on major animal epidemics abroad, strengthen the inspection and quarantine of inbound and outbound animals and their products, and prevent the introduction and spread of animal diseases. The veterinary administrative department shall promptly notify the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authorities of major animal epidemics in China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励、支持开展重大动物疫情监测、预防、应急处理等有关技术的科学研究和国际交流与合作。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports scientific research and international exchanges and cooperation on technologies related to the monitoring, prevention, and emergency response to major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府应当对参加重大动物疫情应急处理的人员给予适当补助，对作出贡献的人员给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** The people's government at or above the county level shall provide appropriate subsidies to personnel participating in the emergency response to major animal epidemics, and shall commend and reward those who have made contributions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对不履行或者不按照规定履行重大动物疫情应急处理职责的行为，任何单位和个人有权检举控告。

**Article 8** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report and accuse acts of failing to perform or failing to perform according to the provisions the duties of emergency response to major animal epidemics.

### 第二章　应急准备

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院兽医主管部门应当制定全国重大动物疫情应急预案，报国务院批准，并按照不同动物疫病病种及其流行特点和危害程度，分别制定实施方案，报国务院备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据本地区的实际情况，制定本行政区域的重大动物疫情应急预案，报上一级人民政府兽医主管部门备案。县级以上地方人民政府兽医主管部门，应当按照不同动物疫病病种及其流行特点和危害程度，分别制定实施方案。  
重大动物疫情应急预案及其实施方案应当根据疫情的发展变化和实施情况，及时修改、完善。

**Article 9** The veterinary administrative department of the State Council shall formulate a national contingency plan for major animal epidemics, submit it to the State Council for approval, and formulate implementation plans respectively according to different types of animal diseases and their epidemic characteristics and degree of harm, and submit them to the State Council for record.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the actual circumstances of its region, formulate a contingency plan for major animal epidemics for its administrative region, and submit it to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level for record. The veterinary administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to different types of animal diseases and their epidemic characteristics and degree of harm, formulate implementation plans respectively.  
The contingency plan for major animal epidemics and its implementation plans shall be revised and improved in a timely manner in light of the development and changes of the epidemic and the implementation situation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　重大动物疫情应急预案主要包括下列内容：  
(一)应急指挥部的职责、组成以及成员单位的分工；  
(二)重大动物疫情的监测、信息收集、报告和通报；  
(三)动物疫病的确认、重大动物疫情的分级和相应的应急处理工作方案；  
(四)重大动物疫情疫源的追踪和流行病学调查分析；  
(五)预防、控制、扑灭重大动物疫情所需资金的来源、物资和技术的储备与调度；  
(六)重大动物疫情应急处理设施和专业队伍建设。

**Article 10** The contingency plan for major animal epidemics shall mainly include the following contents:  
(1) the responsibilities, composition, and division of work of member units of the emergency command;  
(2) the monitoring, information collection, reporting, and notification of major animal epidemics;  
(3) the confirmation of animal diseases, the grading of major animal epidemics, and the corresponding emergency response work plans;  
(4) the tracing of the source of major animal epidemics and epidemiological investigation and analysis;  
(5) the sources of funds, the reserve and dispatch of materials and technologies required for the prevention, control, and eradication of major animal epidemics;  
(6) the construction of facilities and professional teams for emergency response to major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门，应当根据重大动物疫情应急预案的要求，确保应急处理所需的疫苗、药品、设施设备和防护用品等物资的储备。

**Article 11** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the requirements of the contingency plan for major animal epidemics, ensure the reserve of materials such as vaccines, drugs, facilities and equipment, and protective equipment needed for emergency response.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府应当建立和完善重大动物疫情监测网络和预防控制体系，加强动物防疫基础设施和乡镇动物防疫组织建设，并保证其正常运行，提高对重大动物疫情的应急处理能力。

**Article 12** The people's government at or above the county level shall establish and improve the monitoring network and prevention and control system for major animal epidemics, strengthen the construction of animal epidemic prevention infrastructure and township animal epidemic prevention organizations, and ensure their normal operation, so as to improve the capability of emergency response to major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府根据重大动物疫情应急需要，可以成立应急预备队，在重大动物疫情应急指挥部的指挥下，具体承担疫情的控制和扑灭任务。  
应急预备队由当地兽医行政管理人员、动物防疫工作人员、有关专家、执业兽医等组成；必要时，可以组织动员社会上有一定专业知识的人员参加。公安机关、中国人民武装警察部队应当依法协助其执行任务。  
应急预备队应当定期进行技术培训和应急演练。

**Article 13** The local people's government at or above the county level may, as required for emergency response to major animal epidemics, establish emergency preparedness teams, which shall, under the command of the emergency command for major animal epidemics, specifically undertake the tasks of controlling and eradicating the epidemic.  
The emergency preparedness teams shall be composed of local veterinary administrative personnel, animal epidemic prevention workers, relevant experts, licensed veterinarians, etc.; when necessary, personnel with certain professional knowledge from the society may be organized and mobilized to participate. The public security organ and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall assist them in performing their tasks according to law.  
The emergency preparedness teams shall conduct regular technical training and emergency drills.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府及其兽医主管部门应当加强对重大动物疫情应急知识和重大动物疫病科普知识的宣传，增强全社会的重大动物疫情防范意识。

**Article 14** The people's government at or above the county level and its veterinary administrative department shall strengthen the publicity of knowledge on emergency response to major animal epidemics and popular science knowledge on major animal diseases, so as to enhance the awareness of the whole society in preventing major animal epidemics.

### 第三章　监测、报告和公布

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　动物防疫监督机构负责重大动物疫情的监测，饲养、经营动物和生产、经营动物产品的单位和个人应当配合，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 15** The animal epidemic prevention supervision institution shall be responsible for the monitoring of major animal epidemics. Units and individuals that raise or deal in animals and produce or deal in animal products shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　从事动物隔离、疫情监测、疫病研究与诊疗、检验检疫以及动物饲养、屠宰加工、运输、经营等活动的有关单位和个人，发现动物出现群体发病或者死亡的，应当立即向所在地的县(市)动物防疫监督机构报告。

**Article 16** Relevant units and individuals engaged in activities such as animal isolation, epidemic monitoring, disease research and diagnosis, inspection and quarantine, as well as animal raising, slaughtering and processing, transportation, and dealing, shall, upon discovering that animals manifest group morbidity or death, immediately report to the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution of the county (city) where they are located.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县(市)动物防疫监督机构接到报告后，应当立即赶赴现场调查核实。初步认为属于重大动物疫情的，应当在2小时内将情况逐级报省、自治区、直辖市动物防疫监督机构，并同时报所在地人民政府兽医主管部门；兽医主管部门应当及时通报同级卫生主管部门。  
省、自治区、直辖市动物防疫监督机构应当在接到报告后1小时内，向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医主管部门和国务院兽医主管部门所属的动物防疫监督机构报告。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医主管部门应当在接到报告后1小时内报本级人民政府和国务院兽医主管部门。  
重大动物疫情发生后，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院兽医主管部门应当在4小时内向国务院报告。

**Article 17** Upon receiving a report, the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution of the county (city) shall immediately rush to the scene to investigate and verify. If it initially considers that it constitutes a major animal epidemic, it shall report the situation level by level to the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 2 hours, and at the same time report to the veterinary administrative department of the local people's government; the veterinary administrative department shall promptly notify the health administrative department at the same level.  
The animal epidemic prevention supervision institution of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 1 hour after receiving the report, report to the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution under the veterinary administrative department of the State Council.  
The veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 1 hour after receiving the report, report to the people's government at the same level and the veterinary administrative department of the State Council.  
After a major animal epidemic occurs, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and the veterinary administrative department of the State Council shall report to the State Council within 4 hours.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　重大动物疫情报告包括下列内容：  
(一)疫情发生的时间、地点；  
(二)染疫、疑似染疫动物种类和数量、同群动物数量、免疫情况、死亡数量、临床症状、病理变化、诊断情况；  
(三)流行病学和疫源追踪情况；  
(四)已采取的控制措施；  
(五)疫情报告的单位、负责人、报告人及联系方式。

**Article 18** The report on a major animal epidemic shall include the following contents:  
(1) the time and place of the occurrence of the epidemic;  
(2) the species and numbers of infected and suspected infected animals, the numbers of animals in the same group, the immune status, the number of deaths, clinical symptoms, pathological changes, and diagnosis situation;  
(3) the status of epidemiological investigation and source tracing;  
(4) the control measures already taken;  
(5) the unit reporting the epidemic, the person in charge, the reporter, and contact information.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　重大动物疫情由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医主管部门认定；必要时，由国务院兽医主管部门认定。

**Article 19** A major animal epidemic shall be confirmed by the veterinary administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; when necessary, it shall be confirmed by the veterinary administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　重大动物疫情由国务院兽医主管部门按照国家规定的程序，及时准确公布；其他任何单位和个人不得公布重大动物疫情。

**Article 20** A major animal epidemic shall be published promptly and accurately by the veterinary administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State; no other unit or individual may publish a major animal epidemic.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　重大动物疫病应当由动物防疫监督机构采集病料。其他单位和个人采集病料的，应当具备以下条件：  
(一)重大动物疫病病料采集目的、病原微生物的用途应当符合国务院兽医主管部门的规定；  
(二)具有与采集病料相适应的动物病原微生物实验室条件；  
(三)具有与采集病料所需要的生物安全防护水平相适应的设备，以及防止病原感染和扩散的有效措施。  
从事重大动物疫病病原分离的，应当遵守国家有关生物安全管理规定，防止病原扩散。

**Article 21** The disease materials for major animal diseases shall be collected by the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution. If other units or individuals collect disease materials, they shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the purpose of collecting disease materials for major animal diseases and the use of pathogenic microorganisms shall comply with the provisions of the veterinary administrative department of the State Council;  
(2) having laboratory conditions for animal pathogenic microorganisms suitable for the collection of disease materials;  
(3) having equipment suitable for the level of biosafety protection required for the collection of disease materials, as well as effective measures to prevent infection and spread of pathogens.  
Those engaged in the isolation of pathogens of major animal diseases shall comply with the relevant state regulations on biosafety management and prevent the spread of pathogens.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院兽医主管部门应当及时向国务院有关部门和军队有关部门以及各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府兽医主管部门通报重大动物疫情的发生和处理情况。

**Article 22** The veterinary administrative department of the State Council shall promptly notify the relevant departments of the State Council, the relevant departments of the military, and the veterinary administrative departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government of the occurrence and handling of major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　发生重大动物疫情可能感染人群时，卫生主管部门应当对疫区内易受感染的人群进行监测，并采取相应的预防、控制措施。卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门应当及时相互通报情况。

**Article 23** When a major animal epidemic occurs that may infect the human population, the health administrative department shall monitor the population susceptible to infection within the epidemic area and take corresponding prevention and control measures. The health administrative department and the veterinary administrative department shall promptly notify each other of the situation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有关单位和个人对重大动物疫情不得瞒报、谎报、迟报，不得授意他人瞒报、谎报、迟报，不得阻碍他人报告。

**Article 24** Relevant units and individuals shall not conceal, misrepresent, or delay the reporting of a major animal epidemic, shall not instigate others to conceal, misrepresent, or delay the reporting, and shall not obstruct others from reporting.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在重大动物疫情报告期间，有关动物防疫监督机构应当立即采取临时隔离控制措施；必要时，当地县级以上地方人民政府可以作出封锁决定并采取扑杀、销毁等措施。有关单位和个人应当执行。

**Article 25** During the period of reporting a major animal epidemic, the relevant animal epidemic prevention supervision institution shall immediately take temporary isolation and control measures; when necessary, the local people's government at or above the county level may make a decision to blockade and take measures such as culling and destruction. The relevant units and individuals shall implement them.

### 第四章　应急处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　重大动物疫情发生后，国务院和有关地方人民政府设立的重大动物疫情应急指挥部统一领导、指挥重大动物疫情应急工作。

**Article 26** After a major animal epidemic occurs, the emergency command for major animal epidemics established by the State Council and the relevant local people's governments shall exercise unified leadership and command over the emergency response work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　重大动物疫情发生后，县级以上地方人民政府兽医主管部门应当立即划定疫点、疫区和受威胁区，调查疫源，向本级人民政府提出启动重大动物疫情应急指挥系统、应急预案和对疫区实行封锁的建议，有关人民政府应当立即作出决定。  
疫点、疫区和受威胁区的范围应当按照不同动物疫病病种及其流行特点和危害程度划定，具体划定标准由国务院兽医主管部门制定。

**Article 27** After a major animal epidemic occurs, the veterinary administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall immediately delimit the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area, investigate the source of the epidemic, and submit proposals to the people's government at the same level on initiating the emergency command system for major animal epidemics, activating the contingency plan, and imposing a blockade on the epidemic area. The relevant people's government shall make a decision immediately.  
The scope of the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area shall be delimited according to different types of animal diseases and their epidemic characteristics and degree of harm. The specific delimitation standards shall be formulated by the veterinary administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家对重大动物疫情应急处理实行分级管理，按照应急预案确定的疫情等级，由有关人民政府采取相应的应急控制措施。

**Article 28** The State implements graded management for the emergency response to major animal epidemics. The relevant people's government shall take corresponding emergency control measures in accordance with the epidemic grade determined in the contingency plan.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对疫点应当采取下列措施：  
(一)扑杀并销毁染疫动物和易感染的动物及其产品；  
(二)对病死的动物、动物排泄物、被污染饲料、垫料、污水进行无害化处理；  
(三)对被污染的物品、用具、动物圈舍、场地进行严格消毒。

**Article 29** The following measures shall be taken at the epidemic point:  
(1) cull and destroy infected animals and susceptible animals and their products;  
(2) conduct harmless treatment of dead animals, animal excrement, contaminated feed, bedding, and sewage;  
(3) strictly disinfect contaminated articles, tools, animal enclosures, and sites.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　对疫区应当采取下列措施：  
(一)在疫区周围设置警示标志，在出入疫区的交通路口设置临时动物检疫消毒站，对出入的人员和车辆进行消毒；  
(二)扑杀并销毁染疫和疑似染疫动物及其同群动物，销毁染疫和疑似染疫的动物产品，对其他易感染的动物实行圈养或者在指定地点放养，役用动物限制在疫区内使役；  
(三)对易感染的动物进行监测，并按照国务院兽医主管部门的规定实施紧急免疫接种，必要时对易感染的动物进行扑杀；  
(四)关闭动物及动物产品交易市场，禁止动物进出疫区和动物产品运出疫区；  
(五)对动物圈舍、动物排泄物、垫料、污水和其他可能受污染的物品、场地，进行消毒或者无害化处理。

**Article 30** The following measures shall be taken in the epidemic area:  
(1) set up warning signs around the epidemic area, set up temporary animal quarantine and disinfection stations at traffic entrances and exits of the epidemic area, and disinfect persons and vehicles entering and leaving;  
(2) cull and destroy infected and suspected infected animals and their group animals, destroy infected and suspected infected animal products, confine other susceptible animals to enclosures or graze them at designated sites, and restrict draught animals to use within the epidemic area;  
(3) monitor susceptible animals and implement emergency vaccination according to the provisions of the veterinary administrative department of the State Council, and cull susceptible animals when necessary;  
(4) close the trading markets for animals and animal products, prohibit animals from entering or leaving the epidemic area and animal products from being transported out of the epidemic area;  
(5) disinfect or conduct harmless treatment of animal enclosures, animal excrement, bedding, sewage, and other potentially contaminated articles and sites.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对受威胁区应当采取下列措施：  
(一)对易感染的动物进行监测；  
(二)对易感染的动物根据需要实施紧急免疫接种。

**Article 31** The following measures shall be taken in the threatened area:  
(1) monitor susceptible animals;  
(2) implement emergency vaccination for susceptible animals as needed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　重大动物疫情应急处理中设置临时动物检疫消毒站以及采取隔离、扑杀、销毁、消毒、紧急免疫接种等控制、扑灭措施的，由有关重大动物疫情应急指挥部决定，有关单位和个人必须服从；拒不服从的，由公安机关协助执行。

**Article 32** In the emergency response to major animal epidemics, the establishment of temporary animal quarantine and disinfection stations and the adoption of control and eradication measures such as isolation, culling, destruction, disinfection, and emergency vaccination shall be decided by the relevant emergency command for major animal epidemics, and the relevant units and individuals must comply; if they refuse to comply, the public security organ shall assist in enforcement.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家对疫区、受威胁区内易感染的动物免费实施紧急免疫接种；对因采取扑杀、销毁等措施给当事人造成的已经证实的损失，给予合理补偿。紧急免疫接种和补偿所需费用，由中央财政和地方财政分担。

**Article 33** The State shall implement emergency vaccination free of charge for susceptible animals in the epidemic area and threatened area; and shall provide reasonable compensation for the confirmed losses caused to the parties by measures such as culling and destruction. The expenses required for emergency vaccination and compensation shall be shared by the central finance and local finance.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　重大动物疫情应急指挥部根据应急处理需要，有权紧急调集人员、物资、运输工具以及相关设施、设备。  
单位和个人的物资、运输工具以及相关设施、设备被征集使用的，有关人民政府应当及时归还并给予合理补偿。

**Article 34** The emergency command for major animal epidemics shall have the right, as needed for emergency response, to urgently mobilize personnel, materials, means of transport, and related facilities and equipment.  
If the materials, means of transport, and related facilities and equipment of units and individuals are requisitioned for use, the relevant people's government shall promptly return them and provide reasonable compensation.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　重大动物疫情发生后，县级以上人民政府兽医主管部门应当及时提出疫点、疫区、受威胁区的处理方案，加强疫情监测、流行病学调查、疫源追踪工作，对染疫和疑似染疫动物及其同群动物和其他易感染动物的扑杀、销毁进行技术指导，并组织实施检验检疫、消毒、无害化处理和紧急免疫接种。

**Article 35** After a major animal epidemic occurs, the veterinary administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall promptly propose a treatment plan for the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area, strengthen epidemic monitoring, epidemiological investigation, and source tracing, provide technical guidance on the culling and destruction of infected and suspected infected animals and their group animals and other susceptible animals, and organize the implementation of inspection and quarantine, disinfection, harmless treatment, and emergency vaccination.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　重大动物疫情应急处理中，县级以上人民政府有关部门应当在各自的职责范围内，做好重大动物疫情应急所需的物资紧急调度和运输、应急经费安排、疫区群众救济、人的疫病防治、肉食品供应、动物及其产品市场监管、出入境检验检疫和社会治安维护等工作。  
中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队应当支持配合驻地人民政府做好重大动物疫情的应急工作。

**Article 36** In the emergency response to major animal epidemics, the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective responsibilities, properly carry out the work of emergency mobilization and transportation of materials needed for the emergency response to major animal epidemics, arrangement of emergency funds, relief of the masses in the epidemic area, prevention and treatment of human diseases, supply of meat food, market supervision of animals and their products, entry-exit inspection and quarantine, and maintenance of social order.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall support and cooperate with the local people's government in carrying out the emergency response to major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　重大动物疫情应急处理中，乡镇人民政府、村民委员会、居民委员会应当组织力量，向村民、居民宣传动物疫病防治的相关知识，协助做好疫情信息的收集、报告和各项应急处理措施的落实工作。

**Article 37** In the emergency response to major animal epidemics, the township people's government, villagers' committees, and residents' committees shall organize efforts to publicize to villagers and residents the knowledge on animal disease prevention and control, and assist in the collection and reporting of epidemic information and the implementation of various emergency response measures.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　重大动物疫情发生地的人民政府和毗邻地区的人民政府应当通力合作，相互配合，做好重大动物疫情的控制、扑灭工作。

**Article 38** The people's government of the place where a major animal epidemic occurs and the people's government of the adjacent area shall cooperate fully and coordinate with each other to control and eradicate the major animal epidemic.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有关人民政府及其有关部门对参加重大动物疫情应急处理的人员，应当采取必要的卫生防护和技术指导等措施。

**Article 39** The relevant people's governments and their relevant departments shall take measures such as necessary health protection and technical guidance for personnel participating in the emergency response to major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　自疫区内最后一头(只)发病动物及其同群动物处理完毕起，经过一个潜伏期以上的监测，未出现新的病例的，彻底消毒后，经上一级动物防疫监督机构验收合格，由原发布封锁令的人民政府宣布解除封锁，撤销疫区；由原批准机关撤销在该疫区设立的临时动物检疫消毒站。

**Article 40** Starting from the completion of the handling of the last sick animal and its group animals in the epidemic area, after monitoring for more than one incubation period without any new cases, after thorough disinfection and upon acceptance inspection by the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution at the next higher level, the people's government that originally issued the blockade order shall declare the lifting of the blockade and the cancellation of the epidemic area; the original approving authority shall cancel the temporary animal quarantine and disinfection station established in the epidemic area.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府应当将重大动物疫情确认、疫区封锁、扑杀及其补偿、消毒、无害化处理、疫源追踪、疫情监测以及应急物资储备等应急经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 41** The people's government at or above the county level shall include in its fiscal budget the emergency funds for confirmation of major animal epidemics, blockade of epidemic areas, culling and compensation, disinfection, harmless treatment, source tracing, epidemic monitoring, and reserve of emergency materials.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，兽医主管部门及其所属的动物防疫监督机构有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令立即改正、通报批评、给予警告；对主要负责人、负有责任的主管人员和其他责任人员，依法给予记大过、降级、撤职直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不履行疫情报告职责，瞒报、谎报、迟报或者授意他人瞒报、谎报、迟报，阻碍他人报告重大动物疫情的；  
(二)在重大动物疫情报告期间，不采取临时隔离控制措施，导致动物疫情扩散的；  
(三)不及时划定疫点、疫区和受威胁区，不及时向本级人民政府提出应急处理建议，或者不按照规定对疫点、疫区和受威胁区采取预防、控制、扑灭措施的；  
(四)不向本级人民政府提出启动应急指挥系统、应急预案和对疫区的封锁建议的；  
(五)对动物扑杀、销毁不进行技术指导或者指导不力，或者不组织实施检验检疫、消毒、无害化处理和紧急免疫接种的；  
(六)其他不履行本条例规定的职责，导致动物疫病传播、流行，或者对养殖业生产安全和公众身体健康与生命安全造成严重危害的。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the veterinary administrative department or the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution under it commits one of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order immediate correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; the principal responsible person, the person in charge with responsibility, and other responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions of recording a major demerit, demotion, removal from office, or even expulsion according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to perform the duty of epidemic reporting, concealing, misrepresenting, or delaying the report, or instigating others to conceal, misrepresent, or delay the report, or obstructing others from reporting a major animal epidemic;  
(2) failing to take temporary isolation and control measures during the reporting period of a major animal epidemic, resulting in the spread of the animal epidemic;  
(3) failing to promptly delimit the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area, failing to promptly submit emergency response proposals to the people's government at the same level, or failing to take prevention, control, and eradication measures at the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area in accordance with regulations;  
(4) failing to submit proposals to the people's government at the same level on initiating the emergency command system, activating the contingency plan, and imposing a blockade on the epidemic area;  
(5) failing to provide technical guidance or providing inadequate technical guidance for the culling and destruction of animals, or failing to organize the implementation of inspection and quarantine, disinfection, harmless treatment, and emergency vaccination;  
(6) other failures to perform the duties prescribed in these Regulations, resulting in the transmission and prevalence of animal diseases, or causing serious harm to the safety of animal husbandry production and to public health and safety.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府有关部门不履行应急处理职责，不执行对疫点、疫区和受威胁区采取的措施，或者对上级人民政府有关部门的疫情调查不予配合或者阻碍、拒绝的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令立即改正、通报批评、给予警告；对主要负责人、负有责任的主管人员和其他责任人员，依法给予记大过、降级、撤职直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level fails to perform its emergency response duties, fails to implement the measures taken at the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area, or fails to cooperate with, or obstructs or refuses, the epidemic investigation by the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level shall order immediate correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; the principal responsible person, the person in charge with responsibility, and other responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions of recording a major demerit, demotion, removal from office, or even expulsion according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，有关地方人民政府阻碍报告重大动物疫情，不履行应急处理职责，不按照规定对疫点、疫区和受威胁区采取预防、控制、扑灭措施，或者对上级人民政府有关部门的疫情调查不予配合或者阻碍、拒绝的，由上级人民政府责令立即改正、通报批评、给予警告；对政府主要领导人依法给予记大过、降级、撤职直至开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a relevant local people's government obstructs the reporting of a major animal epidemic, fails to perform its emergency response duties, fails to take prevention, control, and eradication measures at the epidemic point, epidemic area, and threatened area in accordance with regulations, or fails to cooperate with, or obstructs or refuses, the epidemic investigation by the relevant department of the people's government at a higher level, the people's government at a higher level shall order immediate correction, circulate a notice of criticism, and give a warning; the principal leading person of the government shall be given administrative sanctions of recording a major demerit, demotion, removal from office, or even expulsion according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　截留、挪用重大动物疫情应急经费，或者侵占、挪用应急储备物资的，按照《财政违法行为处罚处分条例》的规定处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Whoever intercepts or misappropriates the emergency funds for major animal epidemics, or embezzles or misappropriates the emergency reserve materials, shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Punishment and Sanctions for Fiscal Illegal Acts; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，拒绝、阻碍动物防疫监督机构进行重大动物疫情监测，或者发现动物出现群体发病或者死亡，不向当地动物防疫监督机构报告的，由动物防疫监督机构给予警告，并处2000元以上5000元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person refuses or obstructs the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution from conducting monitoring of major animal epidemics, or fails to report to the local animal epidemic prevention supervision institution upon discovering that animals manifest group morbidity or death, the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution shall give a warning and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，不符合相应条件采集重大动物疫病病料，或者在重大动物疫病病原分离时不遵守国家有关生物安全管理规定的，由动物防疫监督机构给予警告，并处5000元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a person collects disease materials for major animal diseases without meeting the corresponding conditions, or fails to comply with the relevant state regulations on biosafety management when isolating the pathogens of major animal diseases, the animal epidemic prevention supervision institution shall give a warning and impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　在重大动物疫情发生期间，哄抬物价、欺骗消费者，散布谣言、扰乱社会秩序和市场秩序的，由价格主管部门、工商行政管理部门或者公安机关依法给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** During the occurrence of a major animal epidemic, whoever hikes prices to deceive consumers, spreads rumors, or disrupts social order and market order shall be given administrative penalties according to law by the price authority, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or the public security organ; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
