# 护士条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-03-27  
**生效日期**：2020-03-27

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护护士的合法权益，规范护理行为，促进护理事业发展，保障医疗安全和人体健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of protecting the lawful rights and interests of nurses, regulating nursing practice, promoting the development of nursing, and ensuring medical safety and human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称护士，是指经执业注册取得护士执业证书，依照本条例规定从事护理活动，履行保护生命、减轻痛苦、增进健康职责的卫生技术人员。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "nurses" means health technical personnel who have obtained a nurse practice certificate through practice registration and who engage in nursing activities in accordance with these Regulations, performing the duties of protecting life, alleviating suffering, and promoting health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　护士人格尊严、人身安全不受侵犯。护士依法履行职责，受法律保护。  
全社会应当尊重护士。

**Article 3** The personal dignity and personal safety of nurses shall not be violated. Nurses shall perform their duties according to law and shall be protected by law.  
The entire society shall respect nurses.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门以及乡(镇)人民政府应当采取措施，改善护士的工作条件，保障护士待遇，加强护士队伍建设，促进护理事业健康发展。  
国务院有关部门和县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，鼓励护士到农村、基层医疗卫生机构工作。

**Article 4** The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, and the people's governments of townships (towns) shall take measures to improve the working conditions of nurses, ensure the treatment of nurses, strengthen the construction of the nursing team, and promote the healthy development of the nursing cause.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to encourage nurses to work in rural areas and primary medical and health institutions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院卫生主管部门负责全国的护士监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门负责本行政区域的护士监督管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent health department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of nurses nationwide.  
The competent health departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of nurses within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院有关部门对在护理工作中做出杰出贡献的护士，应当授予全国卫生系统先进工作者荣誉称号或者颁发白求恩奖章，受到表彰、奖励的护士享受省部级劳动模范、先进工作者待遇；对长期从事护理工作的护士应当颁发荣誉证书。具体办法由国务院有关部门制定。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门对本行政区域内做出突出贡献的护士，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 6** The relevant departments of the State Council shall confer the honorary title of Advanced Worker of the National Health System or issue the Norman Bethune Medal to nurses who have made outstanding contributions in nursing work; nurses who receive commendations and rewards shall enjoy the treatment of provincial/ministerial-level model workers and advanced workers; nurses who have been engaged in nursing work for a long time shall be issued an honorary certificate. The specific measures shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall give commendations and rewards to nurses who have made outstanding contributions within their respective administrative regions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第二章　执业注册

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　护士执业，应当经执业注册取得护士执业证书。  
申请护士执业注册，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有完全民事行为能力；  
(二)在中等职业学校、高等学校完成国务院教育主管部门和国务院卫生主管部门规定的普通全日制3年以上的护理、助产专业课程学习，包括在教学、综合医院完成8个月以上护理临床实习，并取得相应学历证书；  
(三)通过国务院卫生主管部门组织的护士执业资格考试；  
(四)符合国务院卫生主管部门规定的健康标准。  
护士执业注册申请，应当自通过护士执业资格考试之日起3年内提出；逾期提出申请的，除应当具备前款第(一)项、第(二)项和第(四)项规定条件外，还应当在符合国务院卫生主管部门规定条件的医疗卫生机构接受3个月临床护理培训并考核合格。  
护士执业资格考试办法由国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院人事部门制定。

**Article 7** Nurses in practice shall obtain a nurse practice certificate through practice registration.  
An applicant for nurse practice registration shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having full civil capacity;  
(2) having completed, in a secondary vocational school or institution of higher education, the regular full-time nursing or midwifery professional courses of more than three years as prescribed by the competent education department of the State Council and the competent health department of the State Council, including completing more than eight months of clinical nursing internship in teaching or general hospitals, and having obtained the corresponding academic certificate;  
(3) having passed the nurse practice qualification examination organized by the competent health department of the State Council;  
(4) meeting the health standards prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council.  
An application for nurse practice registration shall be filed within three years from the date of passing the nurse practice qualification examination; if the application is filed after the expiration of the time limit, in addition to meeting the conditions specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), and (4) of the preceding paragraph, the applicant shall also have received three months of clinical nursing training at a medical and health institution that meets the conditions prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council and have passed the assessment.  
The measures for the nurse practice qualification examination shall be formulated by the competent health department of the State Council jointly with the personnel department of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请护士执业注册的，应当向批准设立拟执业医疗机构或者为该医疗机构备案的卫生主管部门提出申请。收到申请的卫生主管部门应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内做出决定，对具备本条例规定条件的，准予注册，并发给护士执业证书；对不具备本条例规定条件的，不予注册，并书面说明理由。  
护士执业注册有效期为5年。

**Article 8** An applicant for nurse practice registration shall submit an application to the competent health department that approved the establishment of the intended medical and health institution or that has recorded the institution. The competent health department receiving the application shall make a decision within 20 working days from the date of receiving the application; for those who meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, it shall grant registration and issue a nurse practice certificate; for those who do not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, it shall not grant registration and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.  
The validity period of nurse practice registration shall be five years.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　护士在其执业注册有效期内变更执业地点的，应当向批准设立拟执业医疗机构或者为该医疗机构备案的卫生主管部门报告。收到报告的卫生主管部门应当自收到报告之日起7个工作日内为其办理变更手续。护士跨省、自治区、直辖市变更执业地点的，收到报告的卫生主管部门还应当向其原注册部门通报。

**Article 9** If a nurse changes his or her practice location within the validity period of practice registration, he or she shall report to the competent health department that approved the establishment of the intended medical and health institution or that has recorded the institution. The competent health department receiving the report shall handle the change procedures for him or her within seven working days from the date of receiving the report. If a nurse changes his or her practice location across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the competent health department receiving the report shall also notify the original registration department.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　护士执业注册有效期届满需要继续执业的，应当在护士执业注册有效期届满前30日向批准设立执业医疗机构或者为该医疗机构备案的卫生主管部门申请延续注册。收到申请的卫生主管部门对具备本条例规定条件的，准予延续，延续执业注册有效期为5年；对不具备本条例规定条件的，不予延续，并书面说明理由。  
护士有行政许可法规定的应当予以注销执业注册情形的，原注册部门应当依照行政许可法的规定注销其执业注册。

**Article 10** If a nurse needs to continue practicing upon the expiration of the validity period of practice registration, he or she shall apply to the competent health department that approved the establishment of the medical and health institution where he or she practices or that has recorded the institution for renewal of registration 30 days before the expiration of the validity period of practice registration. For those who meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, the competent health department receiving the application shall grant renewal, and the validity period of the renewed practice registration shall be five years; for those who do not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, it shall not grant renewal and shall provide a written explanation of the reasons.  
If a nurse falls under any of the circumstances under which the practice registration shall be cancelled as provided for in the Administrative License Law, the original registration department shall cancel his or her practice registration in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative License Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门应当建立本行政区域的护士执业良好记录和不良记录，并将该记录记入护士执业信息系统。  
护士执业良好记录包括护士受到的表彰、奖励以及完成政府指令性任务的情况等内容。护士执业不良记录包括护士因违反本条例以及其他卫生管理法律、法规、规章或者诊疗技术规范的规定受到行政处罚、处分的情况等内容。

**Article 11** The competent health departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish records of good practice and records of adverse practice for nurses within their respective administrative regions, and shall enter such records into the nurse practice information system.  
The records of good practice for nurses include the commendations and rewards received by nurses and the completion of government-assigned tasks. The records of adverse practice for nurses include the administrative penalties and disciplinary sanctions imposed on nurses for violating the provisions of these Regulations or other health management laws, regulations, rules, or diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications.

### 第三章　权利和义务

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　护士执业，有按照国家有关规定获取工资报酬、享受福利待遇、参加社会保险的权利。任何单位或者个人不得克扣护士工资，降低或者取消护士福利等待遇。

**Article 12** In practicing nursing, nurses shall have the right to receive wages and remuneration, enjoy welfare benefits, and participate in social insurance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. No unit or individual may deduct nurses' wages or reduce or cancel nurses' welfare benefits.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　护士执业，有获得与其所从事的护理工作相适应的卫生防护、医疗保健服务的权利。从事直接接触有毒有害物质、有感染传染病危险工作的护士，有依照有关法律、行政法规的规定接受职业健康监护的权利；患职业病的，有依照有关法律、行政法规的规定获得赔偿的权利。

**Article 13** In practicing nursing, nurses shall have the right to obtain health protection and medical care services commensurate with the nursing work they perform. Nurses engaged in work involving direct contact with toxic and harmful substances or at risk of contracting infectious diseases shall have the right to receive occupational health monitoring in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; those who suffer from occupational diseases shall have the right to obtain compensation in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　护士有按照国家有关规定获得与本人业务能力和学术水平相应的专业技术职务、职称的权利；有参加专业培训、从事学术研究和交流、参加行业协会和专业学术团体的权利。

**Article 14** Nurses shall have the right to obtain professional technical positions and titles commensurate with their professional competence and academic level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; they shall have the right to participate in professional training, engage in academic research and exchanges, and join industry associations and professional academic organizations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　护士有获得疾病诊疗、护理相关信息的权利和其他与履行护理职责相关的权利，可以对医疗卫生机构和卫生主管部门的工作提出意见和建议。

**Article 15** Nurses shall have the right to obtain information related to disease diagnosis, treatment, and nursing care, as well as other rights related to the performance of nursing duties, and may provide opinions and suggestions on the work of medical and health institutions and the competent health departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　护士执业，应当遵守法律、法规、规章和诊疗技术规范的规定。

**Article 16** In practicing nursing, nurses shall comply with the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, and diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　护士在执业活动中，发现患者病情危急，应当立即通知医师；在紧急情况下为抢救垂危患者生命，应当先行实施必要的紧急救护。  
护士发现医嘱违反法律、法规、规章或者诊疗技术规范规定的，应当及时向开具医嘱的医师提出；必要时，应当向该医师所在科室的负责人或者医疗卫生机构负责医疗服务管理的人员报告。

**Article 17** In the course of practice, if a nurse finds that a patient's condition is critical, he or she shall immediately notify a physician; in an emergency situation to save the life of a critically ill patient, the nurse shall first carry out necessary emergency rescue.  
If a nurse finds that a medical order violates the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, or diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications, he or she shall promptly raise the issue with the physician who issued the medical order; if necessary, the nurse shall report to the head of the department where the physician works or to the person responsible for medical service management in the medical and health institution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　护士应当尊重、关心、爱护患者，保护患者的隐私。

**Article 18** Nurses shall respect, care for, and protect patients, and shall protect patients' privacy.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　护士有义务参与公共卫生和疾病预防控制工作。发生自然灾害、公共卫生事件等严重威胁公众生命健康的突发事件，护士应当服从县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者所在医疗卫生机构的安排，参加医疗救护。

**Article 19** Nurses have the obligation to participate in public health and disease prevention and control work. When natural disasters, public health incidents, or other emergencies that seriously threaten public life and health occur, nurses shall obey the arrangements of the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the medical and health institution where they work, and shall participate in medical rescue.

### 第四章　医疗卫生机构的职责

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　医疗卫生机构配备护士的数量不得低于国务院卫生主管部门规定的护士配备标准。

**Article 20** The number of nurses allocated by medical and health institutions shall not be lower than the nurse allocation standards prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　医疗卫生机构不得允许下列人员在本机构从事诊疗技术规范规定的护理活动：  
(一)未取得护士执业证书的人员；  
(二)未依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定办理执业地点变更手续的护士；  
(三)护士执业注册有效期届满未延续执业注册的护士。  
在教学、综合医院进行护理临床实习的人员应当在护士指导下开展有关工作。

**Article 21** Medical and health institutions shall not permit the following persons to engage in nursing activities prescribed in the diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications within their institutions:  
(1) persons who have not obtained a nurse practice certificate;  
(2) nurses who have not gone through the procedures for changing the practice location in accordance with Article 9 of these Regulations;  
(3) nurses whose practice registration validity period has expired and who have not renewed their practice registration.  
Persons conducting clinical nursing internships in teaching or general hospitals shall carry out relevant work under the guidance of nurses.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　医疗卫生机构应当为护士提供卫生防护用品，并采取有效的卫生防护措施和医疗保健措施。

**Article 22** Medical and health institutions shall provide nurses with health protection supplies and shall take effective health protection measures and medical care measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　医疗卫生机构应当执行国家有关工资、福利待遇等规定，按照国家有关规定为在本机构从事护理工作的护士足额缴纳社会保险费用，保障护士的合法权益。  
对在艰苦边远地区工作，或者从事直接接触有毒有害物质、有感染传染病危险工作的护士，所在医疗卫生机构应当按照国家有关规定给予津贴。

**Article 23** Medical and health institutions shall implement the provisions of the State concerning wages, welfare benefits, etc., and shall pay in full the social insurance premiums for nurses engaged in nursing work in their institutions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall protect the lawful rights and interests of nurses.  
For nurses working in harsh and remote areas, or engaged in work involving direct contact with toxic and harmful substances or at risk of contracting infectious diseases, the medical and health institution where they work shall provide subsidies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　医疗卫生机构应当制定、实施本机构护士在职培训计划，并保证护士接受培训。  
护士培训应当注重新知识、新技术的应用；根据临床专科护理发展和专科护理岗位的需要，开展对护士的专科护理培训。

**Article 24** Medical and health institutions shall formulate and implement on-the-job training plans for nurses in their institutions and shall ensure that nurses receive training.  
Nurse training shall focus on the application of new knowledge and new technologies; specialized nursing training for nurses shall be carried out based on the development of clinical specialist nursing and the needs of specialist nursing positions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　医疗卫生机构应当按照国务院卫生主管部门的规定，设置专门机构或者配备专(兼)职人员负责护理管理工作。

**Article 25** Medical and health institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department of the State Council, establish specialized agencies or assign full-time or part-time personnel to be responsible for nursing management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　医疗卫生机构应当建立护士岗位责任制并进行监督检查。  
护士因不履行职责或者违反职业道德受到投诉的，其所在医疗卫生机构应当进行调查。经查证属实的，医疗卫生机构应当对护士做出处理，并将调查处理情况告知投诉人。

**Article 26** Medical and health institutions shall establish a nurse post responsibility system and shall supervise and inspect it.  
If a nurse is complained against for failing to perform duties or violating professional ethics, the medical and health institution where he or she works shall conduct an investigation. If the complaint is verified as true, the medical and health institution shall take disciplinary action against the nurse and shall inform the complainant of the investigation and handling situation.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　卫生主管部门的工作人员未依照本条例规定履行职责，在护士监督管理工作中滥用职权、徇私舞弊，或者有其他失职、渎职行为的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** If a staff member of a competent health department fails to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, abuses power, engages in favoritism or fraud, or commits other acts of dereliction or malfeasance in the supervision and administration of nurses, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　医疗卫生机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门依据职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，根据国务院卫生主管部门规定的护士配备标准和在医疗卫生机构合法执业的护士数量核减其诊疗科目，或者暂停其6个月以上1年以下执业活动；国家举办的医疗卫生机构有下列情形之一、情节严重的，还应当对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)违反本条例规定，护士的配备数量低于国务院卫生主管部门规定的护士配备标准的；  
(二)允许未取得护士执业证书的人员或者允许未依照本条例规定办理执业地点变更手续、延续执业注册有效期的护士在本机构从事诊疗技术规范规定的护理活动的。

**Article 28** If a medical and health institution is in any of the following circumstances, the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its division of responsibilities, order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and give it a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the specified time limit, it shall reduce its diagnosis and treatment specialties in accordance with the nurse allocation standards prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council and the number of nurses legally practicing in the medical and health institution, or suspend its practice activities for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year; if a medical and health institution run by the State is in any of the following circumstances and the circumstances are serious, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall also be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the number of nurses allocated is lower than the nurse allocation standards prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council;  
(2) permitting persons who have not obtained a nurse practice certificate or permitting nurses who have not gone through the procedures for changing the practice location or renewing the validity period of practice registration in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations to engage in nursing activities prescribed in the diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications within its institution.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　医疗卫生机构有下列情形之一的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚；国家举办的医疗卫生机构有下列情形之一、情节严重的，还应当对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
(一)未执行国家有关工资、福利待遇等规定的；  
(二)对在本机构从事护理工作的护士，未按照国家有关规定足额缴纳社会保险费用的；  
(三)未为护士提供卫生防护用品，或者未采取有效的卫生防护措施、医疗保健措施的；  
(四)对在艰苦边远地区工作，或者从事直接接触有毒有害物质、有感染传染病危险工作的护士，未按照国家有关规定给予津贴的。

**Article 29** If a medical and health institution is in any of the following circumstances, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; if a medical and health institution run by the State is in any of the following circumstances and the circumstances are serious, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall also be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to implement the provisions of the State concerning wages, welfare benefits, etc.;  
(2) failing to pay in full the social insurance premiums for nurses engaged in nursing work in its institution in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) failing to provide health protection supplies for nurses or failing to take effective health protection measures or medical care measures;  
(4) failing to provide subsidies to nurses working in harsh and remote areas or engaged in work involving direct contact with toxic and harmful substances or at risk of contracting infectious diseases in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　医疗卫生机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门依据职责分工责令限期改正，给予警告：  
(一)未制定、实施本机构护士在职培训计划或者未保证护士接受培训的；  
(二)未依照本条例规定履行护士管理职责的。

**Article 30** If a medical and health institution is in any of the following circumstances, the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its division of responsibilities, order it to make corrections within a specified time limit and give it a warning:  
(1) failing to formulate or implement on-the-job training plans for nurses in its institution or failing to ensure that nurses receive training;  
(2) failing to perform the duties of nurse management in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　护士在执业活动中有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门依据职责分工责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，暂停其6个月以上1年以下执业活动，直至由原发证部门吊销其护士执业证书：  
(一)发现患者病情危急未立即通知医师的；  
(二)发现医嘱违反法律、法规、规章或者诊疗技术规范的规定，未依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定提出或者报告的；  
(三)泄露患者隐私的；  
(四)发生自然灾害、公共卫生事件等严重威胁公众生命健康的突发事件，不服从安排参加医疗救护的。  
护士在执业活动中造成医疗事故的，依照医疗事故处理的有关规定承担法律责任。

**Article 31** If a nurse is in any of the following circumstances in the course of practice, the competent health department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to its division of responsibilities, order him or her to make corrections and give him or her a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall suspend his or her practice activities for a period of not less than six months but not more than one year, up to revocation of his or her nurse practice certificate by the original issuing department:  
(1) failing to immediately notify a physician upon finding that a patient's condition is critical;  
(2) failing to raise or report a medical order that violates the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, or diagnostic and therapeutic technical specifications in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations;  
(3) disclosing a patient's privacy;  
(4) in the event of natural disasters, public health incidents, or other emergencies that seriously threaten public life and health, refusing to obey arrangements to participate in medical rescue.  
If a nurse causes a medical accident in the course of practice, he or she shall bear legal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions on medical accident handling.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　护士被吊销执业证书的，自执业证书被吊销之日起2年内不得申请执业注册。

**Article 32** If a nurse's practice certificate is revoked, he or she shall not apply for practice registration within two years from the date of revocation.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　扰乱医疗秩序，阻碍护士依法开展执业活动，侮辱、威胁、殴打护士，或者有其他侵犯护士合法权益行为的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的规定给予处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 33** Anyone who disrupts medical order, obstructs nurses from carrying out practice activities according to law, insults, threatens, or beats nurses, or commits other acts infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of nurses, shall be given a penalty by the public security organ in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例施行前按照国家有关规定已经取得护士执业证书或者护理专业技术职称、从事护理活动的人员，经执业地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门审核合格，换领护士执业证书。  
本条例施行前，尚未达到护士配备标准的医疗卫生机构，应当按照国务院卫生主管部门规定的实施步骤，自本条例施行之日起3年内达到护士配备标准。

**Article 34** Persons who, before these Regulations come into force, have already obtained a nurse practice certificate or a professional technical title in nursing and have been engaged in nursing activities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, shall, after passing the examination by the competent health department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government of the place of practice, exchange for a nurse practice certificate.  
For medical and health institutions that have not yet met the nurse allocation standards before these Regulations come into force, they shall, in accordance with the implementation steps prescribed by the competent health department of the State Council, meet the nurse allocation standards within three years from the date these Regulations come into force.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2008年5月12日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 12, 2008.
