# 中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1996-12-02  
**生效日期**：1997-01-01

> (1996年12月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第206号发布　自1997年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》(以下简称进出境动植物检疫法)的规定，制定本条例。 第二条　下列各物，依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例的规定实施检疫： (一)进境、出境、过境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物； (二)装载动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的装载容器、包装物、铺垫材料； (三)来自动植物疫区的运输工具； (四)进境拆解的废旧船舶； (五)有关法律、行政法规、国际条约规定或者贸易合同约定应当实施进出境动植物检疫的其他货物、物品。 第三条　国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国进出境动植物检疫工作。 中华人民共和国动植物检疫局(以下简称国家动植物检疫局)统一管理全国进出境动植物检疫工作，收集国内外重大动植物疫情，负责国际间进出境动植物检疫的合作与交流。 国家动植物检疫局在对外开放的口岸和进出境动植物检疫业务集中的地点设立的口岸动植物检疫机关，依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例的规定，实施进出境动植物检疫。 第四条　国(境)外发生重大动植物疫情并可能传入中国时，根据情况采取下列紧急预防措施： (一)国务院可以对相关边境区域采取控制措施，必要时下令禁止来自动植物疫区的运输工具进境或者封锁有关口岸； (二)国务院农业行政主管部门可以公布禁止从动植物疫情流行的国家和地区进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的名录；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》(以下简称进出境动植物检疫法)的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine (hereinafter referred to as the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列各物，依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例的规定实施检疫：  
(一)进境、出境、过境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物；  
(二)装载动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的装载容器、包装物、铺垫材料；  
(三)来自动植物疫区的运输工具；  
(四)进境拆解的废旧船舶；  
(五)有关法律、行政法规、国际条约规定或者贸易合同约定应当实施进出境动植物检疫的其他货物、物品。

**Article 2** The following articles shall be subject to quarantine in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and these Regulations:  
(1) animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects entering, exiting or transiting the country;  
(2) containers, packaging materials and padding materials used for carrying animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects;  
(3) means of transport from animal or plant epidemic areas;  
(4) disassembled waste ships entering the country;  
(5) other goods and articles which, as stipulated by relevant laws, administrative regulations, international treaties or agreed in trade contracts, shall be subject to entry and exit animal and plant quarantine.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国进出境动植物检疫工作。  
中华人民共和国动植物检疫局(以下简称国家动植物检疫局)统一管理全国进出境动植物检疫工作，收集国内外重大动植物疫情，负责国际间进出境动植物检疫的合作与交流。  
国家动植物检疫局在对外开放的口岸和进出境动植物检疫业务集中的地点设立的口岸动植物检疫机关，依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例的规定，实施进出境动植物检疫。

**Article 3** The competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council shall be in charge of the national entry and exit animal and plant quarantine.  
The Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau) shall uniformly administer the national entry and exit animal and plant quarantine, collect information on major animal and plant epidemics at home and abroad, and be responsible for international cooperation and exchange in entry and exit animal and plant quarantine.  
The port animal and plant quarantine offices established by the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau at ports open to foreign countries and at locations where entry and exit animal and plant quarantine services are concentrated shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and these Regulations, carry out entry and exit animal and plant quarantine.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国(境)外发生重大动植物疫情并可能传入中国时，根据情况采取下列紧急预防措施：  
(一)国务院可以对相关边境区域采取控制措施，必要时下令禁止来自动植物疫区的运输工具进境或者封锁有关口岸；  
(二)国务院农业行政主管部门可以公布禁止从动植物疫情流行的国家和地区进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的名录；  
(三)有关口岸动植物检疫机关可以对可能受病虫害污染的[本条例第二条](#art-2)所列进境各物采取紧急检疫处理措施；  
(四)受动植物疫情威胁地区的地方人民政府可以立即组织有关部门制定并实施应急方案，同时向上级人民政府和国家动植物检疫局报告。  
邮电、运输部门对重大动植物疫情报告和送检材料应当优先传送。

**Article 4** When a major animal or plant epidemic occurs outside China (or outside the territory) and may be introduced into China, the following emergency preventive measures shall be taken according to the circumstances:  
(1) The State Council may take control measures over relevant border areas, and when necessary, order the prohibition of entry of means of transport from animal or plant epidemic areas or seal off relevant ports;  
(2) The competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council may publish a list of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects the entry of which is prohibited from countries or regions where animal or plant epidemics are prevalent;  
(3) The relevant port animal and plant quarantine offices may take emergency quarantine treatment measures on the articles listed in Article 2 of these Regulations that may be contaminated with pests or diseases;  
(4) The local people's government in the area threatened by the animal or plant epidemic may immediately organize relevant departments to formulate and implement emergency plans, and at the same time report to the higher people's government and the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau.  
The postal and transport departments shall give priority to transmitting reports of major animal or plant epidemics and materials submitted for inspection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　享有外交、领事特权与豁免的外国机构和人员公用或者自用的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境，应当依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例的规定实施检疫；口岸动植物检疫机关查验时，应当遵守有关法律的规定。

**Article 5** Where animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects for official or personal use by foreign institutions and personnel enjoying diplomatic and consular privileges and immunities enter the country, quarantine shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and these Regulations; when conducting inspection, the port animal and plant quarantine offices shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海关依法配合口岸动植物检疫机关，对进出境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物实行监管。具体办法由国务院农业行政主管部门会同海关总署制定。

**Article 6** The Customs shall, according to law, assist the port animal and plant quarantine offices in supervising and controlling the entry and exit of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council jointly with the General Administration of Customs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　进出境动植物检疫法所称动植物疫区和动植物疫情流行的国家与地区的名录，由国务院农业行政主管部门确定并公布。

**Article 7** The list of animal or plant epidemic areas and countries or regions where animal or plant epidemics are prevalent as referred to in the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine shall be determined and published by the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对贯彻执行进出境动植物检疫法和本条例做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in implementing the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine and these Regulations shall be given awards.

### 第二章　检疫审批

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　输入动物、动物产品和[进出境动植物检疫法第五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法.md#art-5)第一款所列禁止进境物的检疫审批，由国家动植物检疫局或者其授权的口岸动植物检疫机关负责。  
输入植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料的检疫审批，由植物检疫条例规定的机关负责。

**Article 9** The quarantine approval for the import of animals, animal products, and the articles the entry of which is prohibited as listed in Article 5, paragraph 1 of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine shall be the responsibility of the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau or the port animal and plant quarantine offices authorized by it.  
The quarantine approval for the import of plant seeds, seedlings and other propagation materials shall be the responsibility of the organ prescribed by the Regulations on Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　符合下列条件的，方可办理进境检疫审批手续：  
(一)输出国家或者地区无重大动植物疫情；  
(二)符合中国有关动植物检疫法律、法规、规章的规定；  
(三)符合中国与输出国家或者地区签订的有关双边检疫协定(含检疫协议、备忘录等，下同)。

**Article 10** The formalities for quarantine approval for entry may be handled only when the following conditions are met:  
(1) There is no major animal or plant epidemic in the exporting country or region;  
(2) The requirements of relevant Chinese laws, regulations and rules on animal and plant quarantine are met;  
(3) The relevant bilateral quarantine agreements (including quarantine agreements, memorandums, etc., hereinafter the same) concluded between China and the exporting country or region are complied with.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　检疫审批手续应当在贸易合同或者协议签订前办妥。

**Article 11** The formalities for quarantine approval shall be completed before the conclusion of the trade contract or agreement.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　携带、邮寄植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料进境的，必须事先提出申请，办理检疫审批手续；因特殊情况无法事先办理的，携带人或者邮寄人应当在口岸补办检疫审批手续，经审批机关同意并经检疫合格后方准进境。

**Article 12** Any person who carries or mails plant seeds, seedlings and other propagation materials into the country must file an application in advance and go through the formalities for quarantine approval. If, due to special circumstances, it is impossible to go through such formalities in advance, the carrier or the mailer shall go through supplementary quarantine approval formalities at the port, and may be permitted to bring the materials into the country only after obtaining the consent of the approving authority and passing quarantine.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　要求运输动物过境的，货主或者其代理人必须事先向国家动植物检疫局提出书面申请，提交输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的疫情证明、输入国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的准许该动物进境的证件，并说明拟过境的路线，国家动植物检疫局审查同意后，签发《动物过境许可证》。

**Article 13** Where a request is made for transporting animals in transit, the owner or his agent must submit a written application in advance to the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau, provide an epidemic certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region, an entry permit for such animals issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the importing country or region, and state the intended transit route. Upon examination and approval by the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau, an "Animal Transit Permit" shall be issued.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　因科学研究等特殊需要，引进[进出境动植物检疫法第五条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法.md#art-5)第一款所列禁止进境物的，办理禁止进境物特许检疫审批手续时，货主、物主或者其代理人必须提交书面申请，说明其数量、用途、引进方式、进境后的防疫措施，并附具有关口岸动植物检疫机关签署的意见。

**Article 14** Where articles the entry of which is prohibited as listed in Article 5, paragraph 1 of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine are introduced due to special needs such as scientific research, when going through the formalities for special quarantine approval for such prohibited articles, the owner, the owner of the goods, or his agent must submit a written application, stating the quantity, purpose, method of introduction, and epidemic prevention measures after entry, and attach the comments signed by the relevant port animal and plant quarantine office.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　办理进境检疫审批手续后，有下列情况之一的，货主、物主或者其代理人应当重新申请办理检疫审批手续：  
(一)变更进境物的品种或者数量的；  
(二)变更输出国家或者地区的；  
(三)变更进境口岸的；  
(四)超过检疫审批有效期的。

**Article 15** After completing the formalities for quarantine approval for entry, if any of the following circumstances occurs, the owner, the owner of the goods, or his agent shall reapply for quarantine approval:  
(1) The variety or quantity of the entry articles is changed;  
(2) The exporting country or region is changed;  
(3) The port of entry is changed;  
(4) The validity period of the quarantine approval has expired.

### 第三章　进境检疫

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[进出境动植物检疫法第十一条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法.md#art-11)所称中国法定的检疫要求，是指中国的法律、行政法规和国务院农业行政主管部门规定的动植物检疫要求。

**Article 16** The term "statutory quarantine requirements of China" as used in Article 11 of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine means the animal and plant quarantine requirements prescribed by Chinese laws, administrative regulations, and the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对向中国输出动植物产品的国外生产、加工、存放单位，实行注册登记制度。具体办法由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 17** The State shall implement a registration system for foreign units that produce, process or store animal or plant products for export to China. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的，货主或者其代理人应当在进境前或者进境时向进境口岸动植物检疫机关报检。属于调离海关监管区检疫的，运达指定地点时，货主或者其代理人应当通知有关口岸动植物检疫机关。属于转关货物的，货主或者其代理人应当在进境时向进境口岸动植物检疫机关申报；到达指运地时，应当向指运地口岸动植物检疫机关报检。  
输入种畜禽及其精液、胚胎的，应当在进境前30日报检；输入其他动物的，应当在进境前15日报检；输入植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料的，应当在进境前7日报检。  
动植物性包装物、铺垫材料进境时，货主或者其代理人应当及时向口岸动植物检疫机关申报；动植物检疫机关可以根据具体情况对申报物实施检疫。  
前款所称动植物性包装物、铺垫材料，是指直接用作包装物、铺垫材料的动物产品和植物、植物产品。

**Article 18** For the import of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, the owner or his agent shall declare for quarantine to the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry before or at the time of entry. If quarantine is to be carried out after removal from the customs supervision area, when arriving at the designated place, the owner or his agent shall notify the relevant port animal and plant quarantine office. If the goods are in transit under customs seal, the owner or his agent shall declare to the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry at the time of entry; upon arrival at the place of destination, they shall declare for quarantine to the port animal and plant quarantine office at the place of destination.  
For the import of breeding livestock and poultry and their semen and embryos, declaration shall be made 30 days before entry; for the import of other animals, declaration shall be made 15 days before entry; for the import of plant seeds, seedlings and other propagation materials, declaration shall be made 7 days before entry.  
When animal or plant packaging materials and padding materials enter the country, the owner or his agent shall promptly declare to the port animal and plant quarantine office; the animal and plant quarantine office may conduct quarantine on the declared articles according to the specific circumstances.  
The term "animal or plant packaging materials and padding materials" referred to in the preceding paragraph means animal products, plants and plant products directly used as packaging materials or padding materials.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　向口岸动植物检疫机关报检时，应当填写报检单，并提交输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的检疫证书、产地证书和贸易合同、信用证、发票等单证；依法应当办理检疫审批手续的，还应当提交检疫审批单。无输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的有效检疫证书，或者未依法办理检疫审批手续的，口岸动植物检疫机关可以根据具体情况，作退回或者销毁处理。

**Article 19** When declaring for quarantine to the port animal and plant quarantine office, a declaration form shall be filled out, and the quarantine certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region, the certificate of origin, and documents such as the trade contract, letter of credit, and invoice shall be submitted; if quarantine approval formalities are required by law, the quarantine approval form shall also be submitted. If there is no valid quarantine certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region, or if the quarantine approval formalities have not been completed according to law, the port animal and plant quarantine office may, depending on the specific circumstances, order the return or destruction of the articles.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　输入的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物运达口岸时，检疫人员可以到运输工具上和货物现场实施检疫，核对货、证是否相符，并可以按照规定采取样品。承运人、货主或者其代理人应当向检疫人员提供装载清单和有关资料。

**Article 20** When the imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects arrive at the port, the quarantine officers may conduct quarantine on board the means of transport and at the cargo site, check whether the goods and the certificates match, and may take samples in accordance with the provisions. The carrier, the owner or his agent shall provide the quarantine officers with the loading list and relevant information.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　装载动物的运输工具抵达口岸时，上下运输工具或者接近动物的人员，应当接受口岸动植物检疫机关实施的防疫消毒，并执行其采取的其他现场预防措施。

**Article 21** When a means of transport carrying animals arrives at the port, persons boarding or leaving the means of transport or approaching the animals shall undergo epidemic prevention disinfection carried out by the port animal and plant quarantine office and comply with other on-site preventive measures taken by it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　检疫人员应当按照下列规定实施现场检疫：  
(一)动物：检查有无疫病的临床症状。发现疑似感染传染病或者已死亡的动物时，在货主或者押运人的配合下查明情况，立即处理。动物的铺垫材料、剩余饲料和排泄物等，由货主或者其代理人在检疫人员的监督下，作除害处理。  
(二)动物产品：检查有无腐败变质现象，容器、包装是否完好。符合要求的，允许卸离运输工具。发现散包、容器破裂的，由货主或者其代理人负责整理完好，方可卸离运输工具。根据情况，对运输工具的有关部位及装载动物产品的容器、外表包装、铺垫材料、被污染场地等进行消毒处理。需要实施实验室检疫的，按照规定采取样品。对易滋生植物害虫或者混藏杂草种子的动物产品，同时实施植物检疫。  
(三)植物、植物产品：检查货物和包装物有无病虫害，并按照规定采取样品。发现病虫害并有扩散可能时，及时对该批货物、运输工具和装卸现场采取必要的防疫措施。对来自动物传染病疫区或者易带动物传染病和寄生虫病病原体并用作动物饲料的植物产品，同时实施动物检疫。  
(四)动植物性包装物、铺垫材料：检查是否携带病虫害、混藏杂草种子、沾带土壤，并按照规定采取样品。  
(五)其他检疫物：检查包装是否完好及是否被病虫害污染。发现破损或者被病虫害污染时，作除害处理。

**Article 22** The quarantine officers shall conduct on-site quarantine in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Animals: Check for clinical symptoms of disease. If animals suspected of being infected with an infectious disease or dead animals are found, the situation shall be investigated with the cooperation of the owner or the attendant and be dealt with immediately. The padding materials, leftover feed, and excreta of the animals shall be treated for elimination of harmful effects by the owner or his agent under the supervision of the quarantine officers.  
(2) Animal products: Check for any signs of spoilage or deterioration, and whether the containers and packaging are intact. If they meet the requirements, they may be allowed to be unloaded from the means of transport. If loose packages or broken containers are found, the owner or his agent shall be responsible for making them tidy and intact before unloading. Depending on the circumstances, disinfection treatment shall be carried out on relevant parts of the means of transport and on the containers, outer packaging, padding materials, contaminated sites, etc. that hold the animal products. If laboratory quarantine is required, samples shall be taken in accordance with the provisions. For animal products that are prone to breed plant pests or contain hidden weed seeds, plant quarantine shall also be carried out at the same time.  
(3) Plants and plant products: Check the goods and packaging materials for the presence of pests or diseases, and take samples in accordance with the provisions. If pests or diseases are found and there is a possibility of spread, necessary epidemic prevention measures shall be taken promptly for the batch of goods, the means of transport, and the loading and unloading site. For plant products originating from animal infectious disease epidemic areas or that are likely to carry pathogens of animal infectious diseases or parasitic diseases and are used as animal feed, animal quarantine shall also be carried out at the same time.  
(4) Animal or plant packaging materials and padding materials: Check whether they carry pests or diseases, contain hidden weed seeds, or are contaminated with soil, and take samples in accordance with the provisions.  
(5) Other quarantine objects: Check whether the packaging is intact and whether they are contaminated by pests or diseases. If damage or contamination by pests or diseases is found, treatment for elimination of harmful effects shall be carried out.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对船舶、火车装运的大宗动植物产品，应当就地分层检查；限于港口、车站的存放条件，不能就地检查的，经口岸动植物检疫机关同意，也可以边卸载边疏运，将动植物产品运往指定的地点存放。在卸货过程中经检疫发现疫情时，应当立即停止卸货，由货主或者其代理人按照口岸动植物检疫机关的要求，对已卸和未卸货物作除害处理，并采取防止疫情扩散的措施；对被病虫害污染的装卸工具和场地，也应当作除害处理。

**Article 23** For large quantities of animal or plant products shipped by vessel or train, layer-by-layer inspection shall be carried out on the spot. If on-the-spot inspection cannot be performed due to storage conditions at the port or station, with the consent of the port animal and plant quarantine office, the products may be unloaded and transported away simultaneously, and be taken to designated locations for storage. If an epidemic is discovered through quarantine during the unloading process, unloading shall be stopped immediately, and the owner or his agent shall, in accordance with the requirements of the port animal and plant quarantine office, carry out treatment for elimination of harmful effects on both the unloaded and ununloaded goods, and take measures to prevent the spread of the epidemic. The loading and unloading tools and sites contaminated by pests or diseases shall also be treated for elimination of harmful effects.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　输入种用大中家畜的，应当在国家动植物检疫局设立的动物隔离检疫场所隔离检疫45日；输入其他动物的，应当在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的动物隔离检疫场所隔离检疫30日。动物隔离检疫场所管理办法，由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 24** For the import of large and medium-sized breeding livestock, isolation quarantine shall be carried out for 45 days at an animal isolation quarantine facility established by the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau; for the import of other animals, isolation quarantine shall be carried out for 30 days at an animal isolation quarantine facility designated by the port animal and plant quarantine office. The measures for the administration of animal isolation quarantine facilities shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for agriculture under the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　进境的同一批动植物产品分港卸货时，口岸动植物检疫机关只对本港卸下的货物进行检疫，先期卸货港的口岸动植物检疫机关应当将检疫及处理情况及时通知其他分卸港的口岸动植物检疫机关；需要对外出证的，由卸毕港的口岸动植物检疫机关汇总后统一出具检疫证书。  
在分卸港实施检疫中发现疫情并必须进行船上熏蒸、消毒时，由该分卸港的口岸动植物检疫机关统一出具检疫证书，并及时通知其他分卸港的口岸动植物检疫机关。

**Article 25** When the same batch of imported animal or plant products is discharged at multiple ports, the port animal and plant quarantine office at each port shall only quarantine the goods discharged at that port. The port animal and plant quarantine office at the port where discharge first occurred shall promptly notify the port animal and plant quarantine offices at the other discharging ports of the quarantine and treatment situation. If it is necessary to issue a certificate for external purposes, the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port where discharge is completed shall issue a unified quarantine certificate after summarizing the information.  
If an epidemic is discovered during quarantine at a discharging port and shipboard fumigation or disinfection is necessary, the port animal and plant quarantine office at that discharging port shall issue a unified quarantine certificate and promptly notify the port animal and plant quarantine offices at the other discharging ports.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对输入的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，按照中国的国家标准、行业标准以及国家动植物检疫局的有关规定实施检疫。

**Article 26** Quarantine of imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects shall be carried out in accordance with China's national standards, industry standards, and the relevant provisions of the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫合格的，由口岸动植物检疫机关在报关单上加盖印章或者签发《检疫放行通知单》；需要调离进境口岸海关监管区检疫的，由进境口岸动植物检疫机关签发《检疫调离通知单》。货主或者其代理人凭口岸动植物检疫机关在报关单上加盖的印章或者签发的《检疫放行通知单》、《检疫调离通知单》办理报关、运递手续。海关对输入的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，凭口岸动植物检疫机关在报关单上加盖的印章或者签发的《检疫放行通知单》、《检疫调离通知单》验放。运输、邮电部门凭单运递，运递期间国内其他检疫机关不再检疫。

**Article 27** If imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects pass quarantine, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall affix its seal on the customs declaration form or issue a "Quarantine Release Notice". If quarantine needs to be carried out after removal from the customs supervision area at the port of entry, the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry shall issue a "Quarantine Removal Notice". The owner or his agent shall, based on the seal affixed on the customs declaration form by the port animal and plant quarantine office or the "Quarantine Release Notice" or "Quarantine Removal Notice" issued by it, go through the formalities for customs declaration and transport. The Customs shall release the imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects upon presentation of the seal affixed on the customs declaration form by the port animal and plant quarantine office or the "Quarantine Release Notice" or "Quarantine Removal Notice" issued by it. The transport and postal departments shall transport the articles based on such documents, and no other domestic quarantine authorities shall conduct quarantine during the period of transport.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫不合格的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发《检疫处理通知单》，通知货主或者其代理人在口岸动植物检疫机关的监督和技术指导下，作除害处理；需要对外索赔的，由口岸动植物检疫机关出具检疫证书。

**Article 28** If imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects fail quarantine, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall issue a "Quarantine Treatment Notice" to notify the owner or his agent to carry out treatment for elimination of harmful effects under the supervision and technical guidance of the port animal and plant quarantine office. If a claim needs to be lodged with the foreign party, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall issue a quarantine certificate.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家动植物检疫局根据检疫需要，并商输出动植物、动植物产品国家或者地区政府有关机关同意，可以派检疫人员进行预检、监装或者产地疫情调查。

**Article 29** The State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau may, based on quarantine needs and with the consent of the relevant government authorities of the country or region exporting animals, plants, animal products or plant products, send quarantine personnel to conduct pre-inspection, supervise loading, or investigate epidemic situations at the place of origin.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　海关、边防等部门截获的非法进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，应当就近交由口岸动植物检疫机关检疫。

**Article 30** Any animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects illegally brought into the country that are intercepted by the Customs, border defense authorities or other departments shall be handed over to the nearest port animal and plant quarantine office for quarantine.

### 第四章　出境检疫

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　货主或者其代理人依法办理动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的出境报检手续时，应当提供贸易合同或者协议。

**Article 31** When the owner or his agent goes through the formalities for declaring export quarantine of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects according to law, the trade contract or agreement shall be provided.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对输入国要求中国对向其输出的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的生产、加工、存放单位注册登记的，口岸动植物检疫机关可以实行注册登记，并报国家动植物检疫局备案。

**Article 32** If an importing country requires China to register the units that produce, process or store animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects for export to that country, the port animal and plant quarantine offices may implement such registration and report it to the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau for record.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　输出动物，出境前需经隔离检疫的，在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的隔离场所检疫。输出植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的，在仓库或者货场实施检疫；根据需要，也可以在生产、加工过程中实施检疫。  
待检出境植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，应当数量齐全、包装完好、堆放整齐、唛头标记明显。

**Article 33** For the export of animals that require isolation quarantine before leaving the country, quarantine shall be carried out at an isolation facility designated by the port animal and plant quarantine office. For the export of plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, quarantine shall be carried out at the warehouse or storage yard; if necessary, quarantine may also be carried out during the process of production or processing.  
The plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects to be exported awaiting quarantine shall be complete in quantity, with intact packaging, neatly stacked, and with clear marks and labels.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　输出动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的检疫依据：  
(一)输入国家或者地区和中国有关动植物检疫规定；  
(二)双边检疫协定；  
(三)贸易合同中订明的检疫要求。

**Article 34** The basis for quarantine of exported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects shall be:  
(1) The relevant animal and plant quarantine provisions of the importing country or region and of China;  
(2) Bilateral quarantine agreements;  
(3) The quarantine requirements specified in the trade contract.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经启运地口岸动植物检疫机关检疫合格的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，运达出境口岸时，按照下列规定办理：  
(一)动物应当经出境口岸动植物检疫机关临床检疫或者复检；  
(二)植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物从启运地随原运输工具出境的，由出境口岸动植物检疫机关验证放行；改换运输工具出境的，换证放行；  
(三)植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物到达出境口岸后拼装的，因变更输入国家或者地区而有不同检疫要求的，或者超过规定的检疫有效期的，应当重新报检。

**Article 35** Where animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects that have passed quarantine at the port animal and plant quarantine office at the place of departure arrive at the port of exit, the following provisions shall apply:  
(1) Animals shall undergo clinical quarantine or re-inspection by the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of exit;  
(2) For plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects that are exported from the place of departure on the same means of transport, the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of exit shall verify the documents and release them; if they are exported after being transferred to another means of transport, a new certificate shall be issued for release;  
(3) If plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects are consolidated after arriving at the port of exit, or if different quarantine requirements arise due to a change in the importing country or region, or if the prescribed validity period of quarantine has expired, a new declaration for quarantine shall be made.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　输出动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经启运地口岸动植物检疫机关检疫合格的，运往出境口岸时，运输、邮电部门凭启运地口岸动植物检疫机关签发的检疫单证运递，国内其他检疫机关不再检疫。

**Article 36** Where animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects for export have passed quarantine at the port animal and plant quarantine office at the place of departure, when transported to the port of exit, the transport and postal departments shall transport them based on the quarantine documents issued by the port animal and plant quarantine office at the place of departure, and no other domestic quarantine authorities shall conduct quarantine.

### 第五章　过境检疫

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　运输动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物过境(含转运，下同)的，承运人或者押运人应当持货运单和输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的证书，向进境口岸动植物检疫机关报检；运输动物过境的，还应当同时提交国家动植物检疫局签发的《动物过境许可证》。

**Article 37** For the transport of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects in transit (including transshipment, hereinafter the same), the carrier or the attendant shall declare for quarantine to the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry by presenting the waybill and the certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region; for the transport of animals in transit, an "Animal Transit Permit" issued by the State Animal and Plant Quarantine Bureau shall also be submitted at the same time.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　过境动物运达进境口岸时，由进境口岸动植物检疫机关对运输工具、容器的外表进行消毒并对动物进行临床检疫，经检疫合格的，准予过境。进境口岸动植物检疫机关可以派检疫人员监运至出境口岸，出境口岸动植物检疫机关不再检疫。

**Article 38** When transit animals arrive at the port of entry, the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry shall disinfect the exterior of the means of transport and containers and conduct clinical quarantine on the animals. If the animals pass quarantine, they shall be permitted to transit. The port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of entry may dispatch quarantine personnel to escort the animals to the port of exit, and the port animal and plant quarantine office at the port of exit shall not conduct quarantine again.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　装载过境植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的运输工具和包装物、装载容器必须完好。经口岸动植物检疫机关检查，发现运输工具或者包装物、装载容器有可能造成途中散漏的，承运人或者押运人应当按照口岸动植物检疫机关的要求，采取密封措施；无法采取密封措施的，不准过境。

**Article 39** The means of transport, packaging materials and load containers for carrying plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects in transit must be intact. If, upon inspection by the port animal and plant quarantine office, the means of transport or packaging materials or load containers are found to be likely to cause leakage or spillage during the journey, the carrier or the attendant shall take sealing measures in accordance with the requirements of the port animal and plant quarantine office; if sealing measures cannot be taken, transit shall not be permitted.

### 第六章　携带、邮寄物检疫

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　携带、邮寄植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料进境，未依法办理检疫审批手续的，由口岸动植物检疫机关作退回或者销毁处理。邮件作退回处理的，由口岸动植物检疫机关在邮件及发递单上批注退回原因；邮件作销毁处理的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发通知单，通知寄件人。

**Article 40** If plant seeds, seedlings and other propagation materials are carried or mailed into the country without going through the formalities for quarantine approval according to law, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall order the return or destruction of such materials. If the postal item is to be returned, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall note the reason for return on the item and the dispatch slip; if the postal item is to be destroyed, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall issue a notice and notify the sender.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　携带动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境的，进境时必须向海关申报并接受口岸动植物检疫机关检疫。海关应当将申报或者查获的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物及时交由口岸动植物检疫机关检疫。未经检疫的，不得携带进境。

**Article 41** Any person who carries animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects into the country shall declare to the Customs at the time of entry and accept quarantine by the port animal and plant quarantine office. The Customs shall promptly hand over any declared or seized animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects to the port animal and plant quarantine office for quarantine. No such articles may be brought into the country without undergoing quarantine.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　口岸动植物检疫机关可以在港口、机场、车站的旅客通道、行李提取处等现场进行检查，对可能携带动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物而未申报的，可以进行查询并抽检其物品，必要时可以开包(箱)检查。  
旅客进出境检查现场应当设立动植物检疫台位和标志。

**Article 42** The port animal and plant quarantine offices may conduct inspections at passenger passages, baggage claim areas, and other sites at ports, airports, and railway stations. For persons who may be carrying animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects without declaration, the offices may make inquiries and conduct spot checks of their belongings, and when necessary, may open bags (or boxes) for inspection.  
Animal and plant quarantine counters and signs shall be set up at inspection sites for passengers entering and leaving the country.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　携带动物进境的，必须持有输出动物的国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的检疫证书，经检疫合格后放行；携带犬、猫等宠物进境的，还必须持有疫苗接种证书。没有检疫证书、疫苗接种证书的，由口岸动植物检疫机关作限期退回或者没收销毁处理。作限期退回处理的，携带人必须在规定的时间内持口岸动植物检疫机关签发的截留凭证，领取并携带出境；逾期不领取的，作自动放弃处理。  
携带植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境，经现场检疫合格的，当场放行；需要作实验室检疫或者隔离检疫的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发截留凭证。截留检疫合格的，携带人持截留凭证向口岸动植物检疫机关领回；逾期不领回的，作自动放弃处理。  
禁止携带、邮寄[进出境动植物检疫法第二十九条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法.md#art-29)规定的名录所列动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境。

**Article 43** Any person who carries animals into the country must hold a quarantine certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region, and the animals shall be released only after passing quarantine; if dogs, cats or other pets are carried into the country, a vaccination certificate must also be held. In the absence of a quarantine certificate or vaccination certificate, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall order their return within a specified time limit or confiscate and destroy them. If return within a specified time limit is ordered, the carrier must, within the prescribed time limit, claim the animals by presenting the seizure receipt issued by the port animal and plant quarantine office and take them out of the country; if they are not claimed within the time limit, they shall be deemed as abandoned.  
If plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects are carried into the country and pass on-site quarantine, they shall be released on the spot; if laboratory quarantine or isolation quarantine is required, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall issue a seizure receipt. If they pass quarantine after seizure, the carrier shall claim them back from the port animal and plant quarantine office by presenting the seizure receipt; if they are not claimed within the time limit, they shall be deemed as abandoned.  
It is prohibited to carry or mail into the country any animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects listed in the catalogue specified in Article 29 of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　邮寄进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，由口岸动植物检疫机关在国际邮件互换局(含国际邮件快递公司及其他经营国际邮件的单位，以下简称邮局)实施检疫。邮局应当提供必要的工作条件。  
经现场检疫合格的，由口岸动植物检疫机关加盖检疫放行章，交邮局运递。需要作实验室检疫或者隔离检疫的，口岸动植物检疫机关应当向邮局办理交接手续；检疫合格的，加盖检疫放行章，交邮局运递。

**Article 44** Animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects mailed into the country shall be quarantined by the port animal and plant quarantine office at the international mail exchange office (including international express mail companies and other units operating international mail, hereinafter referred to as the post office). The post office shall provide necessary working conditions.  
If they pass on-site quarantine, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall affix a quarantine release stamp and hand them over to the post office for delivery. If laboratory quarantine or isolation quarantine is required, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall go through the handover formalities with the post office; if they pass quarantine, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall affix a quarantine release stamp and hand them over to the post office for delivery.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　携带、邮寄进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫不合格又无有效方法作除害处理的，作退回或者销毁处理，并签发《检疫处理通知单》交携带人、寄件人。

**Article 45** If animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects carried or mailed into the country fail quarantine and there is no effective method for treatment to eliminate harmful effects, they shall be returned or destroyed, and a "Quarantine Treatment Notice" shall be issued to the carrier or sender.

### 第七章　运输工具检疫

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　口岸动植物检疫机关对来自动植物疫区的船舶、飞机、火车，可以登船、登机、登车实施现场检疫。有关运输工具负责人应当接受检疫人员的询问并在询问记录上签字，提供运行日志和装载货物的情况，开启舱室接受检疫。  
口岸动植物检疫机关应当对前款运输工具可能隐藏病虫害的餐车、配餐间、厨房、储藏室、食品舱等动植物产品存放、使用场所和泔水、动植物性废弃物的存放场所以及集装箱箱体等区域或者部位，实施检疫；必要时，作防疫消毒处理。

**Article 46** For vessels, aircraft, and trains from animal or plant epidemic areas, the port animal and plant quarantine offices may board them to conduct on-site quarantine. The person in charge of the relevant means of transport shall answer the inquiries of the quarantine officers and sign the questioning record, provide the operation log and information on the loaded cargo, and open the cabins for quarantine.  
The port animal and plant quarantine offices shall conduct quarantine on the means of transport referred to in the preceding paragraph in areas or parts where pests or diseases may be hidden, such as dining cars, pantry rooms, kitchens, storage rooms, food holds and other places where animal or plant products are stored or used, as well as places where swill and animal or plant waste are stored, and container bodies; when necessary, epidemic prevention disinfection shall be carried out.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　来自动植物疫区的船舶、飞机、火车，经检疫发现有[进出境动植物检疫法第十八条](../../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法.md#art-18)规定的名录所列病虫害的，必须作熏蒸、消毒或者其他除害处理。发现有禁止进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的，必须作封存或者销毁处理；作封存处理的，在中国境内停留或者运行期间，未经口岸动植物检疫机关许可，不得启封动用。对运输工具上的泔水、动植物性废弃物及其存放场所、容器，应当在口岸动植物检疫机关的监督下作除害处理。

**Article 47** If vessels, aircraft, or trains from animal or plant epidemic areas are found through quarantine to have pests or diseases listed in the catalogue specified in Article 18 of the Law on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine, they must undergo fumigation, disinfection or other treatment for elimination of harmful effects. If articles whose entry is prohibited (animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects) are found, they must be sealed or destroyed; if sealed, they shall not be unsealed or used during their stay or operation within China without the permission of the port animal and plant quarantine office. The swill, animal or plant waste on the means of transport, and the places and containers where they are stored, shall be treated for elimination of harmful effects under the supervision of the port animal and plant quarantine office.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　来自动植物疫区的进境车辆，由口岸动植物检疫机关作防疫消毒处理。装载进境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的车辆，经检疫发现病虫害的，连同货物一并作除害处理。装运供应香港、澳门地区的动物的回空车辆，实施整车防疫消毒。

**Article 48** For vehicles entering the country from animal or plant epidemic areas, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall carry out epidemic prevention disinfection. For vehicles carrying imported animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, if pests or diseases are found through quarantine, treatment for elimination of harmful effects shall be carried out together with the goods. For empty vehicles returning from transporting animals destined for Hong Kong and Macao, full-vehicle epidemic prevention disinfection shall be carried out.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　进境拆解的废旧船舶，由口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫。发现病虫害的，在口岸动植物检疫机关监督下作除害处理。发现有禁止进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的，在口岸动植物检疫机关的监督下作销毁处理。

**Article 49** For disassembled waste ships entering the country, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall conduct quarantine. If pests or diseases are found, treatment for elimination of harmful effects shall be carried out under the supervision of the port animal and plant quarantine office. If articles whose entry is prohibited (animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects) are found, destruction shall be carried out under the supervision of the port animal and plant quarantine office.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　来自动植物疫区的进境运输工具经检疫或者经消毒处理合格后，运输工具负责人或者其代理人要求出证的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发《运输工具检疫证书》或者《运输工具消毒证书》。

**Article 50** After a means of transport entering the country from an animal or plant epidemic area has undergone quarantine or disinfection treatment and passed, if the person in charge of the means of transport or his agent requests a certificate, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall issue a "Means of Transport Quarantine Certificate" or a "Means of Transport Disinfection Certificate".

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　进境、过境运输工具在中国境内停留期间，交通员工和其他人员不得将所装载的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物带离运输工具；需要带离时，应当向口岸动植物检疫机关报检。

**Article 51** During the stay of inbound and transit means of transport within the territory of China, transportation employees and other persons shall not take the loaded animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects off the means of transport; if it is necessary to take them off, they shall apply to the port animal and plant quarantine office for inspection.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　装载动物出境的运输工具，装载前应当在口岸动植物检疫机关监督下进行消毒处理。  
装载植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物出境的运输工具，应当符合国家有关动植物防疫和检疫的规定。发现危险性病虫害或者超过规定标准的一般性病虫害的，作除害处理后方可装运。

**Article 52** Means of transport carrying animals for export shall be disinfected under the supervision of the port animal and plant quarantine office before loading.  
Means of transport carrying plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects for export shall conform to the provisions of the State on animal and plant epidemic prevention and quarantine. Where dangerous pests or general pests exceeding the prescribed standards are found, loading and shipment may only be carried out after disinfestation treatment.

### 第八章　检疫监督

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家动植物检疫局和口岸动植物检疫机关对进出境动植物、动植物产品的生产、加工、存放过程，实行检疫监督制度。具体办法由国务院农业行政主管部门制定。

**Article 53** The State Administration of Animal and Plant Quarantine and the port animal and plant quarantine offices shall implement a quarantine supervision system over the production, processing and storage processes of inbound and outbound animals, plants, animal products and plant products. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent agricultural administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　进出境动物和植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料，需要隔离饲养、隔离种植的，在隔离期间，应当接受口岸动植物检疫机关的检疫监督。

**Article 54** Where inbound and outbound animals, plant seeds, seedlings and other propagating materials need to be raised or planted in isolation, they shall accept quarantine supervision of the port animal and plant quarantine office during the isolation period.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　从事进出境动植物检疫熏蒸、消毒处理业务的单位和人员，必须经口岸动植物检疫机关考核合格。  
口岸动植物检疫机关对熏蒸、消毒工作进行监督、指导，并负责出具熏蒸、消毒证书。

**Article 55** Units and persons engaged in fumigation and disinfection treatment for inbound and outbound animal and plant quarantine must pass the assessment of the port animal and plant quarantine office.  
The port animal and plant quarantine office shall supervise and guide the fumigation and disinfection work, and shall be responsible for issuing fumigation or disinfection certificates.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　口岸动植物检疫机关可以根据需要，在机场、港口、车站、仓库、加工厂、农场等生产、加工、存放进出境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的场所实施动植物疫情监测，有关单位应当配合。  
未经口岸动植物检疫机关许可，不得移动或者损坏动植物疫情监测器具。

**Article 56** The port animal and plant quarantine office may, as necessary, carry out animal and plant epidemic monitoring at places such as airports, ports, railway stations, warehouses, processing plants, farms and other places where inbound and outbound animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects are produced, processed or stored. The relevant units shall cooperate.  
Without the permission of the port animal and plant quarantine office, no person shall move or damage the animal and plant epidemic monitoring instruments.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　口岸动植物检疫机关根据需要，可以对运载进出境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的运输工具、装载容器加施动植物检疫封识或者标志；未经口岸动植物检疫机关许可，不得开拆或者损毁检疫封识、标志。  
动植物检疫封识和标志由国家动植物检疫局统一制发。

**Article 57** The port animal and plant quarantine office may, as necessary, affix animal and plant quarantine seals or marks on means of transport and containers carrying inbound and outbound animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects; without the permission of the port animal and plant quarantine office, no person shall open or damage the quarantine seals or marks.  
Animal and plant quarantine seals and marks shall be uniformly manufactured and issued by the State Administration of Animal and Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　进境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，装载动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的装载容器、包装物，运往保税区(含保税工厂、保税仓库等)的，在进境口岸依法实施检疫；口岸动植物检疫机关可以根据具体情况实施检疫监督；经加工复运出境的，依照进出境动植物检疫法和本条例有关出境检疫的规定办理。

**Article 58** Inbound animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, and the containers and packaging materials carrying animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, which are transported to a bonded area (including bonded factories, bonded warehouses, etc.), shall be quarantined according to law at the port of entry; the port animal and plant quarantine office may, based on specific circumstances, implement quarantine supervision; where such goods are processed and then re-exported, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and the provisions of these Regulations on exit quarantine.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有下列违法行为之一的，由口岸动植物检疫机关处5000元以下的罚款：  
(一)未报检或者未依法办理检疫审批手续或者未按检疫审批的规定执行的；  
(二)报检的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物与实际不符的。  
有前款第(二)项所列行为，已取得检疫单证的，予以吊销。

**Article 59** Whoever commits any of the following illegal acts shall be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan by the port animal and plant quarantine office:  
(1) failing to apply for inspection, failing to go through the formalities of quarantine approval according to law, or failing to execute the provisions of the quarantine approval;  
(2) the animals, plants, animal products, plant products or other quarantine objects declared for inspection do not conform to the actual situation.  
Where a person has committed the act listed in item (2) of the preceding paragraph and has obtained quarantine certificates, the certificates shall be revoked.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　有下列违法行为之一的，由口岸动植物检疫机关处3000元以上3万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未经口岸动植物检疫机关许可擅自将进境、过境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物卸离运输工具或者运递的；  
(二)擅自调离或者处理在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的隔离场所中隔离检疫的动植物的；  
(三)擅自开拆过境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的包装，或者擅自开拆、损毁动植物检疫封识或者标志的；  
(四)擅自抛弃过境动物的尸体、排泄物、铺垫材料或者其他废弃物，或者未按规定处理运输工具上的泔水、动植物性废弃物的。

**Article 60** Whoever commits any of the following illegal acts shall be imposed a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan by the port animal and plant quarantine office:  
(1) without the permission of the port animal and plant quarantine office, unloading inbound or transit animals, plants, animal products, plant products or other quarantine objects from the means of transport, or transporting them;  
(2) without authorization, transferring or disposing of animals or plants under quarantine isolation in the isolation places designated by the port animal and plant quarantine office;  
(3) without authorization, opening the packaging of transit animals, plants, animal products, plant products or other quarantine objects, or opening or damaging animal and plant quarantine seals or marks;  
(4) without authorization, discarding the carcasses, excrement, bedding materials or other wastes of transit animals, or failing to dispose of swill, animal or plant wastes on the means of transport in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)、第三十二条的规定注册登记的生产、加工、存放动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的单位，进出境的上述物品经检疫不合格的，除依照本条例有关规定作退回、销毁或者除害处理外，情节严重的，由口岸动植物检疫机关注销注册登记。

**Article 61** Where a unit registered in accordance with the provisions of Articles 17 and 32 of these Regulations for the production, processing or storage of animals, plants, animal products, plant products or other quarantine objects, and the inbound or outbound items under this Article are found to be unqualified upon quarantine, apart from being returned, destroyed or subjected to disinfestation treatment in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, if the circumstances are serious, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall cancel its registration.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列违法行为之一的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪或者犯罪情节显著轻微依法不需要判处刑罚的，由口岸动植物检疫机关处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)引起重大动植物疫情的；  
(二)伪造、变造动植物检疫单证、印章、标志、封识的。

**Article 62** Whoever commits any of the following illegal acts shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime or the crime is so minor that no penalty is required by law, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) causing a major animal or plant epidemic;  
(2) forging or altering animal or plant quarantine certificates, seals, marks or seals.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　从事进出境动植物检疫熏蒸、消毒处理业务的单位和人员，不按照规定进行熏蒸和消毒处理的，口岸动植物检疫机关可以视情节取消其熏蒸、消毒资格。

**Article 63** Where a unit or person engaged in fumigation and disinfection treatment for inbound and outbound animal and plant quarantine fails to carry out fumigation and disinfection treatment in accordance with the provisions, the port animal and plant quarantine office may, depending on the circumstances, revoke its or his fumigation or disinfection qualification.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　进出境动植物检疫法和本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)“植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料”，是指栽培、野生的可供繁殖的植物全株或者部分，如植株、苗木(含试管苗)、果实、种子、砧木、接穗、插条、叶片、芽体、块根、块茎、鳞茎、球茎、花粉、细胞培养材料等；  
(二)“装载容器”，是指可以多次使用、易受病虫害污染并用于装载进出境货物的容器，如笼、箱、桶、筐等；  
(三)“其他有害生物”，是指动物传染病、寄生虫病和植物危险性病、虫、杂草以外的各种为害动植物的生物有机体、病原微生物，以及软体类、啮齿类、螨类、多足虫类动物和危险性病虫的中间寄主、媒介生物等；  
(四)“检疫证书”，是指动植物检疫机关出具的关于动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物健康或者卫生状况的具有法律效力的文件，如《动物检疫证书》、《植物检疫证书》、《动物健康证书》、《兽医卫生证书》、《熏蒸/消毒证书》等。

**Article 64** The meanings of the following terms used in the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine Law and these Regulations:  
(1) "Plant seeds, seedlings and other propagating materials" means the whole or part of cultivated or wild plants that can be used for propagation, such as plants, seedlings (including test-tube plantlets), fruits, seeds, rootstocks, scions, cuttings, leaves, buds, tubers, rhizomes, bulbs, corms, pollen, cell culture materials, etc.;  
(2) "Containers" means containers that can be used repeatedly, are easily contaminated by pests and diseases, and are used for loading inbound or outbound goods, such as cages, boxes, barrels, baskets, etc.;  
(3) "Other harmful organisms" means various living organisms and pathogenic microorganisms harmful to animals and plants other than animal infectious diseases, parasitic diseases, and dangerous plant diseases, pests and weeds, as well as molluscs, rodents, mites, myriapods, intermediate hosts and vectors of dangerous pests and diseases, etc.;  
(4) "Quarantine certificates" means legally effective documents issued by the animal and plant quarantine office concerning the health or sanitary status of animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects, such as the Animal Quarantine Certificate, Plant Quarantine Certificate, Animal Health Certificate, Veterinary Health Certificate, Fumigation/Disinfection Certificate, etc.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对进出境动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物因实施检疫或者按照规定作熏蒸、消毒、退回、销毁等处理所需费用或者招致的损失，由货主、物主或者其代理人承担。

**Article 65** The expenses incurred or losses caused by the implementation of quarantine or the treatment of fumigation, disinfection, return, destruction, etc. in accordance with the provisions on inbound and outbound animals, plants, animal products, plant products and other quarantine objects shall be borne by the cargo owner, the owner of the goods or their agent.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　口岸动植物检疫机关依法实施检疫，需要采取样品时，应当出具采样凭单；验余的样品，货主、物主或者其代理人应当在规定的期限内领回；逾期不领回的，由口岸动植物检疫机关按照规定处理。

**Article 66** Where the port animal and plant quarantine office implements quarantine according to law and needs to take samples, it shall issue a sampling receipt; the remaining samples after inspection shall be retrieved by the cargo owner, the owner of the goods or their agent within the prescribed time limit; if they are not retrieved within the time limit, the port animal and plant quarantine office shall dispose of them in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　贸易性动物产品出境的检疫机关，由国务院根据情况规定。

**Article 67** The quarantine authority for the export of commercial animal products shall be specified by the State Council according to the circumstances.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例自1997年1月1日起施行。

**Article 68** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1997.
