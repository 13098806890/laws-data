# 中华人民共和国畜禽遗传资源进出境和对外合作研究利用审批办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2008-08-28  
**生效日期**：2008-10-01

> (2008年8月20日国务院第23次常务会议通过　2008年8月28日中华人民共和国国务院令第533号公布　自2008年10月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对畜禽遗传资源进出境和对外合作研究利用的管理，保护和合理利用畜禽遗传资源，防止畜禽遗传资源流失，促进畜牧业持续健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国畜牧法》，制定本办法。 第二条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，应当遵守《中华人民共和国畜牧法》，并依照本办法的规定办理审批手续。 第三条　本办法所称畜禽，是指列入依照《中华人民共和国畜牧法》第十一条规定公布的畜禽遗传资源目录的畜禽。 本办法所称畜禽遗传资源，是指畜禽及其卵子(蛋)、胚胎、精液、基因物质等遗传材料。 第四条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，应当具备下列条件： (一)引进的目的明确、用途合理； (二)符合畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划； (三)引进的畜禽遗传资源来自非疫区； (四)符合进出境动植物检疫和农业转基因生物安全的有关规定，不对境内畜禽遗传资源和生态环境安全构成威胁。 第五条　拟从境外引进畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当向其所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门提出申请，并提交畜禽遗传资源买卖合同或者赠与协议。 引进种用畜禽遗传资源的，还应当提交下列资料： (一)种畜禽生产经营许可证； (二)出口国家或者地区法定机构出具的种畜系谱或者种禽代次证明； (三)首次引进的，同时提交种用畜禽遗传资源的产地、分布、培育过程、生态特征、生产性能、群体存在的主要遗传缺陷和特有疾病等资料。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对畜禽遗传资源进出境和对外合作研究利用的管理，保护和合理利用畜禽遗传资源，防止畜禽遗传资源流失，促进畜牧业持续健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国畜牧法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the administration of the entry into and exit from China of livestock and poultry genetic resources and their use in foreign cooperation research, protecting and rationally utilizing livestock and poultry genetic resources, preventing the loss of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and promoting the sustainable and healthy development of animal husbandry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，应当遵守《中华人民共和国畜牧法》，并依照本办法的规定办理审批手续。

**Article 2** The introduction of livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China, the export of such resources to outside China, or the cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall comply with the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China and undergo the examination and approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法所称畜禽，是指列入依照[《中华人民共和国畜牧法》第十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国畜牧法.md#art-11)规定公布的畜禽遗传资源目录的畜禽。  
本办法所称畜禽遗传资源，是指畜禽及其卵子(蛋)、胚胎、精液、基因物质等遗传材料。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Measures, "livestock and poultry" means the livestock and poultry included in the catalog of livestock and poultry genetic resources published in accordance with the provisions of Article 11 of the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of these Measures, "livestock and poultry genetic resources" means livestock and poultry and their genetic materials such as ova (eggs), embryos, semen, and genetic substances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)引进的目的明确、用途合理；  
(二)符合畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划；  
(三)引进的畜禽遗传资源来自非疫区；  
(四)符合进出境动植物检疫和农业转基因生物安全的有关规定，不对境内畜禽遗传资源和生态环境安全构成威胁。

**Article 4** The introduction of livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the purpose of introduction is clear and the use is reasonable;  
(2) it conforms to the plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources;  
(3) the livestock and poultry genetic resources to be introduced are from areas free of epidemic diseases;  
(4) it complies with the relevant provisions on the quarantine of animals and plants entering or exiting China and on the safety of agricultural genetically modified organisms, and shall not pose a threat to the safety of domestic livestock and poultry genetic resources and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　拟从境外引进畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当向其所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门提出申请，并提交畜禽遗传资源买卖合同或者赠与协议。  
引进种用畜禽遗传资源的，还应当提交下列资料：  
(一)种畜禽生产经营许可证；  
(二)出口国家或者地区法定机构出具的种畜系谱或者种禽代次证明；  
(三)首次引进的，同时提交种用畜禽遗传资源的产地、分布、培育过程、生态特征、生产性能、群体存在的主要遗传缺陷和特有疾病等资料。

**Article 5** A unit that intends to introduce livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and submit a contract for the purchase or a gift agreement for the livestock and poultry genetic resources.  
Where such genetic resources for breeding purposes are to be introduced, the following documents shall also be submitted:  
(1) the production and operation license for breeding livestock and poultry;  
(2) the certificate of pedigree for breeding livestock or the certificate of generation for breeding poultry issued by the statutory authority of the exporting country or region;  
(3) in the case of first introduction, the information on the origin, distribution, cultivation process, ecological characteristics, production performance, main genetic defects and specific diseases existing in the population of the genetic resources for breeding purposes shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　向境外输出列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)用途明确；  
(二)符合畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划；  
(三)不对境内畜牧业生产和畜禽产品出口构成威胁；  
(四)国家共享惠益方案合理。

**Article 6** The export to outside China of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the purpose is clear;  
(2) it conforms to the plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources;  
(3) it shall not pose a threat to domestic animal husbandry production and the export of livestock and poultry products;  
(4) the national benefit-sharing plan is reasonable.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拟向境外输出列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当向其所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门提出申请，并提交下列资料：  
(一)畜禽遗传资源买卖合同或者赠与协议；  
(二)与境外进口方签订的国家共享惠益方案。

**Article 7** A unit that intends to export to outside China livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and submit the following documents:  
(1) a contract for the purchase or a gift agreement for the livestock and poultry genetic resources;  
(2) the national benefit-sharing plan signed with the foreign importer.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)研究目的、范围和合作期限明确；  
(二)符合畜禽遗传资源保护和利用规划；  
(三)知识产权归属明确、研究成果共享方案合理；  
(四)不对境内畜禽遗传资源和生态环境安全构成威胁；  
(五)国家共享惠益方案合理。  
在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当是依法取得法人资格的中方教育科研机构、中方独资企业。

**Article 8** The cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the research purpose, scope, and cooperation period are clear;  
(2) it conforms to the plan for the protection and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources;  
(3) the ownership of intellectual property rights is clear and the plan for sharing research results is reasonable;  
(4) it shall not pose a threat to the safety of domestic livestock and poultry genetic resources and the ecological environment;  
(5) the national benefit-sharing plan is reasonable.  
A unit conducting cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources shall be a Chinese educational or scientific research institution or a wholly Chinese-owned enterprise that has obtained the status of a legal person according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　拟在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当向其所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门提出申请，并提交下列资料：  
(一)项目可行性研究报告；  
(二)合作研究合同；  
(三)与境外合作者签订的国家共享惠益方案。

**Article 9** A unit that intends to conduct cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall submit an application to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and submit the following documents:  
(1) a project feasibility study report;  
(2) a cooperative research contract;  
(3) the national benefit-sharing plan signed with the foreign partner.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　禁止向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用我国特有的、新发现未经鉴定的畜禽遗传资源以及国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门禁止出口的其他畜禽遗传资源。

**Article 10** It is prohibited to export to outside China or conduct cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources that are unique to China, newly discovered and not yet subject to expert evaluation, as well as other livestock and poultry genetic resources whose export is prohibited by the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门，应当自收到畜禽遗传资源引进、输出或者对外合作研究利用申请之日起20个工作日内完成审核工作，并将审核意见和申请资料报国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门审批。

**Article 11** The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the review within 20 working days from the date of receipt of an application for the introduction, export, or foreign cooperative research and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, and shall submit the review opinions and application materials to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门，应当自收到畜禽遗传资源引进、输出或者对外合作研究利用审核意见和申请资料之日起20个工作日内，对具备[本办法第四条](#art-4)、第六条、第八条规定条件的，签发审批表；对不具备条件的，书面通知申请人，并说明理由。其中，对输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，或者首次引进畜禽遗传资源的，国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门应当自收到审核意见和申请资料之日起3个工作日内，将审核意见和申请资料送国家畜禽遗传资源委员会评估或者评审。评估或者评审时间不计入审批期限。

**Article 12** The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall, within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the review opinions and application materials for the introduction, export, or foreign cooperative research and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, issue an approval form to those that meet the conditions specified in Articles 4, 6, and 8 of these Measures; and shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons for those that do not meet the conditions. For the export or the cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, or for the first-time introduction of livestock and poultry genetic resources, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall, within 3 working days from the date of receipt of the review opinions and application materials, forward the review opinions and application materials to the National Commission on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources for assessment or review. The time for assessment or review shall not be counted in the approval period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门在20个工作日内不能做出审批决定的，经本部门负责人批准，可以延长10个工作日。延长期限的理由应当告知申请人。

**Article 13** Where the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council is unable to make an approval decision within 20 working days, it may, upon approval by the head of the department, extend the period by 10 working days. The reasons for the extension shall be notified to the applicant.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　畜禽遗传资源引进、输出审批表的有效期为6个月；需要延续的，申请人应当在有效期届满10个工作日前向原审批机关申请延续。延续期不得超过3个月。

**Article 14** The approval form for the introduction or export of livestock and poultry genetic resources shall be valid for six months. If an extension is required, the applicant shall apply to the original approving authority for extension 10 working days before the expiry of the validity period. The extension period shall not exceed three months.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　从境外引进畜禽遗传资源、向境外输出列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，凭审批表办理检疫手续。海关凭出入境检验检疫部门出具的进出境货物通关单办理验放手续。从境外引进畜禽遗传资源、向境外输出列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当自海关放行之日起10个工作日内，将实际引进、输出畜禽遗传资源的数量报国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门备案。国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门应当定期将有关资料抄送国务院环境保护行政主管部门。

**Article 15** A unit that introduces livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China or exports livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources to outside China shall go through quarantine formalities with the approval form. The Customs shall handle inspection and release procedures based on the customs clearance certificate for entry/exit goods issued by the entry-exit inspection and quarantine authority. The unit that introduces such resources from outside China or exports such resources to outside China shall, within 10 working days from the date of Customs release, report the actual quantity of livestock and poultry genetic resources introduced or exported to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council for the record. The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall regularly send copies of the relevant information to the competent administrative department for environmental protection of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在对外合作研究利用过程中需要更改研究目的和范围、合作期限、知识产权归属、研究成果共享方案或者国家共享惠益方案的，在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当按照原申请程序重新办理审批手续。

**Article 16** Where it is necessary to change the research purpose and scope, the cooperation period, the ownership of intellectual property rights, the plan for sharing research results, or the national benefit-sharing plan in the course of foreign cooperative research and utilization, the unit conducting cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall undergo the examination and approval formalities anew in accordance with the original application procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门应当对引进的畜禽遗传资源进行跟踪评价，组织专家对引进的畜禽遗传资源的生产性能、健康状况、适应性以及对生态环境和本地畜禽遗传资源的影响等进行测定、评估，并及时将测定、评估结果报国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门。  
发现引进的畜禽遗传资源对境内畜禽遗传资源、生态环境有危害或者可能产生危害的，国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门应当商有关主管部门，采取相应的安全控制措施。

**Article 17** The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall conduct follow-up evaluation on the introduced livestock and poultry genetic resources, organize experts to test and assess the production performance, health status, adaptability, and the impact on the ecological environment and local livestock and poultry genetic resources of the introduced resources, and shall promptly report the test and assessment results to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council.  
Where the introduced livestock and poultry genetic resources are found to cause or likely to cause harm to domestic livestock and poultry genetic resources and the ecological environment, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall consult with the relevant competent departments to take appropriate safety control measures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，应当于每年12月31日前，将合作研究利用畜禽遗传资源的情况报所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门应当对合作研究利用情况提出审核意见，一并报国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门备案。

**Article 18** A unit conducting cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources shall, before December 31 each year, report the status of the cooperative research and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall provide review opinions on the cooperative research and utilization status and shall submit them together to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　与畜禽遗传资源引进、输出和对外合作研究利用的单位以及与境外机构或者个人有利害关系的人员，不得参与对有关申请的评估、评审以及对进境畜禽遗传资源的测定、评估工作。

**Article 19** Persons who have an interest in a unit involved in the introduction, export, or foreign cooperative research and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, or who have an interest in foreign institutions or individuals, shall not participate in the assessment or review of the relevant applications or in the testing or assessment of imported livestock and poultry genetic resources.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　我国的畜禽遗传资源信息，包括重要的畜禽遗传家系和特定地区遗传资源及其数据、资料、样本等，未经国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门许可，任何单位或者个人不得向境外机构和个人转让。

**Article 20** Without the permission of the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council, no unit or individual may transfer to foreign institutions or individuals any information on China's livestock and poultry genetic resources, including important livestock and poultry genetic lineages and genetic resources of specific areas, as well as their data, materials, and samples.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　畜牧兽医行政主管部门工作人员在畜禽遗传资源引进、输出和对外合作研究利用审批过程中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where any staff member of the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine neglects his/her duty, abuses his/her power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the process of approving the introduction, export, or foreign cooperative research and utilization of livestock and poultry genetic resources, disciplinary action shall be taken according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　依照本办法的规定参与评估、评审、测定的专家，利用职务上的便利收取他人财物或者谋取其他利益，或者出具虚假意见的，没收违法所得，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 22** Where any expert participating in the assessment, review, or testing in accordance with the provisions of these Measures accepts property from others or seeks other benefits by taking advantage of his/her position, or issues false opinions, his/her illegal gains shall be confiscated, and disciplinary action shall be taken according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　申请从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的单位，隐瞒有关情况或者提供虚假资料的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门给予警告，3年内不再受理该单位的同类申请。

**Article 23** Where a unit applying for the introduction of livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China, the export to outside China, or the cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources conceals relevant information or provides false materials, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall give it a warning and shall not accept any similar application from the same unit within three years.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得批准从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的，由国务院畜牧兽医行政主管部门撤销批准决定，没收有关畜禽遗传资源和违法所得，并处以1万元以上5万元以下罚款，10年内不再受理该单位的同类申请；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where a unit obtains approval for the introduction of livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China, the export to outside China, or the cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources by fraudulent, bribery or other illegitimate means, the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the State Council shall revoke the approval decision, confiscate the relevant livestock and poultry genetic resources and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000, and shall not accept any similar application from the same unit within ten years; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　未经审核批准，从境外引进畜禽遗传资源，或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用未经国家畜禽遗传资源委员会鉴定的新发现的畜禽遗传资源的，依照《中华人民共和国畜牧法》的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 25** Where any person introduces livestock and poultry genetic resources from outside China without review and approval, or conducts cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, or conducts cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of newly discovered livestock and poultry genetic resources that have not been subject to expert evaluation by the National Commission on Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　未经审核批准，向境外输出列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源的，依照《中华人民共和国海关法》的有关规定追究法律责任。海关应当将扣留的畜禽遗传资源移送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府畜牧兽医行政主管部门处理。

**Article 26** Where any person exports to outside China livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources without review and approval, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China. The Customs shall transfer the detained livestock and poultry genetic resources to the competent administrative department for animal husbandry and veterinary medicine of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for handling.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　向境外输出或者在境内与境外机构、个人合作研究利用列入畜禽遗传资源保护名录的畜禽遗传资源，违反国家保密规定的，依照《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》的有关规定追究法律责任。

**Article 27** Where the export to outside China or the cooperative research and utilization in China with foreign institutions or individuals of livestock and poultry genetic resources included in the List of Protected Livestock and Poultry Genetic Resources violates the state provisions on confidentiality, legal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本办法自2008年10月1日起施行。

**Article 28** These Measures shall come into force as of October 1, 2008.
