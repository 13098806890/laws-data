**法律部门**：行政法

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范签证的签发和外国人在中国境内停留居留的服务和管理，根据《中华人民共和国出境入境管理法》（以下简称出境入境管理法）制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Exit and Entry Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Exit and Entry Administration Law) for the purpose of regulating the issuance of visas and the services and management of the stay and residence of foreigners in China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家建立外国人入境出境服务和管理工作协调机制，加强外国人入境出境服务和管理工作的统筹、协调与配合。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据需要建立外国人入境出境服务和管理工作协调机制，加强信息交流与协调配合，做好本行政区域的外国人入境出境服务和管理工作。

**Article 2** The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for the services and management of the entry and exit of foreigners to strengthen the overall planning, coordination, and cooperation in the services and management of the entry and exit of foreigners.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, as needed, establish coordination mechanisms for the services and management of the entry and exit of foreigners, strengthen information exchange and coordination, and properly carry out the services and management of the entry and exit of foreigners within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公安部应当会同国务院有关部门建立外国人入境出境服务和管理信息平台，实现有关信息的共享。

**Article 3** The Ministry of Public Security shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, establish an information platform for the services and management of the entry and exit of foreigners to achieve the sharing of relevant information.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在签证签发管理和外国人在中国境内停留居留管理工作中，外交部、公安部等国务院部门应当在部门门户网站、受理出境入境证件申请的地点等场所，提供外国人入境出境管理法律法规和其他需要外国人知悉的信息。

**Article 4** In the management of visa issuance and the management of the stay and residence of foreigners in China, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Public Security, and other departments of the State Council shall provide laws and regulations on the management of the entry and exit of foreigners and other information that foreigners need to know at their department portals, places where applications for entry and exit documents are accepted, and other locations.

### 第二章　签证的类别和签发

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外交签证、礼遇签证、公务签证的签发范围和签发办法由外交部规定。

**Article 5** The scope and methods for issuing diplomatic visas, courtesy visas, and service visas shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　普通签证分为以下类别，并在签证上标明相应的汉语拼音字母：  
（一）C字签证，发给执行乘务、航空、航运任务的国际列车乘务员、国际航空器机组人员、国际航行船舶的船员及船员随行家属和从事国际道路运输的汽车驾驶员。  
（二）D字签证，发给入境永久居留的人员。  
（三）F字签证，发给入境从事交流、访问、考察等活动的人员。  
（四）G字签证，发给经中国过境的人员。  
（五）J1字签证，发给外国常驻中国新闻机构的外国常驻记者；J2字签证，发给入境进行短期采访报道的外国记者。  
（六）K字签证，发给入境的外国青年科技人才。  
（七）L字签证，发给入境旅游的人员；以团体形式入境旅游的，可以签发团体L字签证。  
（八）M字签证，发给入境进行商业贸易活动的人员。  
（九）Q1字签证，发给因家庭团聚申请入境居留的中国公民的家庭成员和具有中国永久居留资格的外国人的家庭成员，以及因寄养等原因申请入境居留的人员；Q2字签证，发给申请入境短期探亲的居住在中国境内的中国公民的亲属和具有中国永久居留资格的外国人的亲属。  
（十）R字签证，发给国家需要的外国高层次人才和急需紧缺专门人才。  
（十一）S1字签证，发给申请入境长期探亲的因工作、学习等事由在中国境内居留的外国人的配偶、父母、未满18周岁的子女、配偶的父母，以及因其他私人事务需要在中国境内居留的人员；S2字签证，发给申请入境短期探亲的因工作、学习等事由在中国境内停留居留的外国人的家庭成员，以及因其他私人事务需要在中国境内停留的人员。  
（十二）X1字签证，发给申请在中国境内长期学习的人员；X2字签证，发给申请在中国境内短期学习的人员。  
（十三）Z字签证，发给申请在中国境内工作的人员。

**Article 6** Ordinary visas are divided into the following categories and shall be marked with the corresponding Chinese phonetic letters on the visa:  
(1) Visa C, issued to international train crew, international aircraft crew, crew members of international voyage ships and their accompanying family members, and motor vehicle drivers engaged in international road transport who perform crew, aviation, or shipping duties.  
(2) Visa D, issued to persons entering China for permanent residence.  
(3) Visa F, issued to persons entering China for activities such as exchanges, visits, or inspections.  
(4) Visa G, issued to persons transiting through China.  
(5) Visa J1, issued to foreign resident journalists of permanent foreign news agencies in China; Visa J2, issued to foreign journalists entering China for short-term interviews and reporting.  
(6) Visa K, issued to foreign young science and technology talents entering China.  
(7) Visa L, issued to persons entering China for tourism; for group tourism, a group L visa may be issued.  
(8) Visa M, issued to persons entering China for commercial and trade activities.  
(9) Visa Q1, issued to family members of Chinese citizens and family members of foreigners with permanent residence status in China who apply for entry and residence for family reunion, and persons applying for entry and residence due to fosterage or other reasons; Visa Q2, issued to relatives of Chinese citizens residing in China and relatives of foreigners with permanent residence status in China who apply for short-term family visit entry.  
(10) Visa R, issued to high-level foreign talents and urgently needed specialized talents required by the State.  
(11) Visa S1, issued to spouses, parents, children under 18, and parents of spouses of foreigners who reside in China for work, study, or other reasons and apply for long-term family visit entry, and persons who need to reside in China for other private affairs; Visa S2, issued to family members of foreigners who stay or reside in China for work, study, or other reasons and apply for short-term family visit entry, and persons who need to stay in China for other private affairs.  
(12) Visa X1, issued to persons applying for long-term study in China; Visa X2, issued to persons applying for short-term study in China.  
(13) Visa Z, issued to persons applying for work in China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国人申请办理签证，应当填写申请表，提交本人的护照或者其他国际旅行证件以及符合规定的照片和申请事由的相关材料。  
（一）申请C字签证，应当提交外国运输公司出具的担保函件或者中国境内有关单位出具的邀请函件。  
（二）申请D字签证，应当提交公安部签发的外国人永久居留身份确认表。  
（三）申请F字签证，应当提交中国境内的邀请方出具的邀请函件。  
（四）申请G字签证，应当提交前往国家（地区）的已确定日期、座位的联程机（车、船）票。  
（五）申请J1字及J2字签证，应当按照中国有关外国常驻新闻机构和外国记者采访的规定履行审批手续并提交相应的申请材料。  
（六）申请K字签证，应当符合中国政府有关主管部门规定的外国青年科技人才的条件和要求，并提交相应的证明材料。  
（七）申请L字签证，应当按照要求提交旅行计划行程安排等材料；以团体形式入境旅游的，还应当提交旅行社出具的邀请函件。  
（八）申请M字签证，应当按照要求提交中国境内商业贸易合作方出具的邀请函件。  
（九）申请Q1字签证，因家庭团聚申请入境居留的，应当提交居住在中国境内的中国公民、具有永久居留资格的外国人出具的邀请函件和家庭成员关系证明，因寄养等原因申请入境的，应当提交委托书等证明材料；申请Q2字签证，应当提交居住在中国境内的中国公民、具有永久居留资格的外国人出具的邀请函件等证明材料。  
（十）申请R字签证，应当符合中国政府有关主管部门确定的外国高层次人才和急需紧缺专门人才的引进条件和要求，并按照规定提交相应的证明材料。  
（十一）申请S1字及S2字签证，应当按照要求提交因工作、学习等事由在中国境内停留居留的外国人出具的邀请函件、家庭成员关系证明，或者入境处理私人事务所需的证明材料。  
（十二）申请X1字签证应当按照规定提交招收单位出具的录取通知书和主管部门出具的证明材料；申请X2字签证，应当按照规定提交招收单位出具的录取通知书等证明材料。  
（十三）申请Z字签证，应当按照规定提交工作许可等证明材料。  
签证机关可以根据具体情况要求外国人提交其他申请材料。

**Article 7** A foreigner applying for a visa shall fill in an application form, submit his or her passport or other international travel documents, together with photos that meet the requirements and relevant materials concerning the purpose of application.  
(1) For a C visa, a letter of guarantee issued by a foreign transport company or a letter of invitation issued by a relevant entity in China shall be submitted.  
(2) For a D visa, the Confirmation Form for Foreigner's Permanent Residence Status issued by the Ministry of Public Security shall be submitted.  
(3) For an F visa, a letter of invitation issued by the inviting party in China shall be submitted.  
(4) For a G visa, a connecting ticket (air, train, or ship) with a confirmed date and seat to the destination country (region) shall be submitted.  
(5) For J1 and J2 visas, the examination and approval procedures shall be completed in accordance with China's regulations on foreign permanent news agencies and foreign journalists' interviews, and corresponding application materials shall be submitted.  
(6) For a K visa, the conditions and requirements for foreign young science and technology talents prescribed by the competent authorities of the Chinese government shall be met, and corresponding supporting materials shall be submitted.  
(7) For an L visa, materials such as travel itinerary and arrangements shall be submitted as required; for group tourism entry, a letter of invitation issued by a travel agency shall also be submitted.  
(8) For an M visa, a letter of invitation issued by the commercial and trade partner in China shall be submitted as required.  
(9) For a Q1 visa, if applying for entry and residence for family reunion, a letter of invitation issued by a Chinese citizen residing in China or a foreigner with permanent residence status and proof of family relationship shall be submitted; if applying for entry due to fosterage or other reasons, supporting materials such as a power of attorney shall be submitted; for a Q2 visa, supporting materials such as a letter of invitation issued by a Chinese citizen residing in China or a foreigner with permanent residence status shall be submitted.  
(10) For an R visa, the conditions and requirements for introducing high-level foreign talents and urgently needed specialized talents determined by the competent authorities of the Chinese government shall be met, and corresponding supporting materials shall be submitted in accordance with regulations.  
(11) For S1 and S2 visas, a letter of invitation issued by a foreigner who stays or resides in China for work, study, or other reasons, proof of family relationship, or supporting materials needed for handling private affairs upon entry shall be submitted as required.  
(12) For an X1 visa, the admission notice issued by the recruiting entity and supporting materials issued by the competent authorities shall be submitted in accordance with regulations; for an X2 visa, supporting materials such as the admission notice issued by the recruiting entity shall be submitted in accordance with regulations.  
(13) For a Z visa, supporting materials such as a work permit shall be submitted in accordance with regulations.  
The visa authority may, based on specific circumstances, require the foreigner to submit other application materials.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国人有下列情形之一的，应当按照驻外签证机关要求接受面谈：  
（一）申请入境居留的；  
（二）个人身份信息、入境事由需要进一步核实的；  
（三）曾有不准入境、被限期出境记录的；  
（四）有必要进行面谈的其他情形。  
驻外签证机关签发签证需要向中国境内有关部门、单位核实有关信息的，中国境内有关部门、单位应当予以配合。

**Article 8** A foreigner who falls under any of the following circumstances shall be interviewed as required by the overseas visa authority:  
(1) applying for entry and residence;  
(2) individual identity information or the purpose of entry needs further verification;  
(3) having records of being denied entry or being ordered to leave within a time limit;  
(4) other circumstances where an interview is necessary.  
If the overseas visa authority, when issuing a visa, needs to verify relevant information with relevant departments or entities in China, the relevant departments or entities in China shall cooperate.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　签证机关经审查认为符合签发条件的，签发相应类别签证。对入境后需要办理居留证件的，签证机关应当在签证上注明入境后办理居留证件的时限。

**Article 9** If the visa authority, upon examination, deems that the conditions for issuance are met, it shall issue the corresponding category of visa. For those who need to apply for a residence permit after entry, the visa authority shall indicate on the visa the time limit for applying for a residence permit after entry.

### 第三章　停留居留管理

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外国人持签证入境后，按照国家规定可以变更停留事由、给予入境便利的，或者因使用新护照、持团体签证入境后由于客观原因需要分团停留的，可以向停留地县级以上地方人民政府公安机关出入境管理机构申请换发签证。

**Article 10** After entering China with a visa, a foreigner who, in accordance with national regulations, may change the purpose of stay or be granted entry facilitation, or who needs to stay in separate groups due to objective reasons after entering with a group visa or using a new passport, may apply to the exit and entry administration of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of stay for a new visa.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在中国境内的外国人所持签证遗失、损毁、被盗抢的，应当及时向停留地县级以上地方人民政府公安机关出入境管理机构申请补发签证。

**Article 11** Where the visa held by a foreigner within the territory of China is lost, damaged, stolen, or robbed, he/she shall promptly apply to the exit and entry administration of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of stay for a replacement visa.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国人申请签证的延期、换发、补发和申请办理停留证件，应当填写申请表，提交本人的护照或者其他国际旅行证件以及符合规定的照片和申请事由的相关材料。

**Article 12** A foreigner applying for visa extension, renewal, replacement, or applying for a stay permit shall fill in an application form and submit his/her passport or other international travel documents, as well as photographs conforming to the requirements and relevant materials concerning the purpose of application.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国人申请签证延期、换发、补发和申请办理停留证件符合受理规定的，公安机关出入境管理机构应当出具有效期不超过7日的受理回执，并在受理回执有效期内作出是否签发的决定。  
外国人申请签证延期、换发、补发和申请办理停留证件的手续或者材料不符合规定的，公安机关出入境管理机构应当一次性告知申请人需要履行的手续和补正的申请材料。  
申请人所持护照或者其他国际旅行证件因办理证件被收存期间，可以凭受理回执在中国境内合法停留。

**Article 13** Where the application of a foreigner for visa extension, renewal, replacement, or for a stay permit meets the requirements for acceptance, the exit and entry administration of the public security organ shall issue an acceptance receipt valid for not more than 7 days, and shall decide whether to issue the visa or permit within the validity period of the acceptance receipt.  
Where the procedures or materials for a foreigner's application for visa extension, renewal, replacement, or for a stay permit do not meet the requirements, the exit and entry administration of the public security organ shall inform the applicant at one time of the procedures to be completed and the application materials to be supplemented or corrected.  
During the period when the applicant's passport or other international travel documents are held by the authority for processing the relevant certificate, the applicant may legally stay within China by presenting the acceptance receipt.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公安机关出入境管理机构作出的延长签证停留期限决定，仅对本次入境有效，不影响签证的入境次数和入境有效期，并且累计延长的停留期限不得超过原签证注明的停留期限。  
签证停留期限延长后，外国人应当按照原签证规定的事由和延长的期限停留。

**Article 14** A decision made by the exit and entry administration of the public security organ to extend the duration of stay of a visa shall be valid only for the current entry, shall not affect the number of entries and the validity period for entry of the visa, and the cumulatively extended duration of stay shall not exceed the duration of stay specified in the original visa.  
After the extension of the duration of stay of the visa, the foreigner shall stay for the extended period in accordance with the purpose specified in the original visa.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　居留证件分为以下种类：  
（一）工作类居留证件，发给在中国境内工作的人员；  
（二）学习类居留证件，发给在中国境内长期学习的人员；  
（三）记者类居留证件，发给外国常驻中国新闻机构的外国常驻记者；  
（四）团聚类居留证件，发给因家庭团聚需要在中国境内居留的中国公民的家庭成员和具有中国永久居留资格的外国人的家庭成员，以及因寄养等原因需要在中国境内居留的人员；  
（五）私人事务类居留证件，发给入境长期探亲的因工作、学习等事由在中国境内居留的外国人的配偶、父母、未满18周岁的子女、配偶的父母，以及因其他私人事务需要在中国境内居留的人员。

**Article 15** Residence permits are divided into the following categories:  
(1) Work residence permits, which shall be issued to persons working within the territory of China;  
(2) Study residence permits, which shall be issued to persons studying for a long term within the territory of China;  
(3) Journalist residence permits, which shall be issued to foreign resident correspondents of foreign permanent news agencies in China;  
(4) Family reunion residence permits, which shall be issued to family members of Chinese citizens who need to reside in China for family reunion, family members of foreigners with permanent residence status in China, and persons who need to reside in China due to foster care or other reasons;  
(5) Private affairs residence permits, which shall be issued to spouses, parents, children under the age of 18, and parents of spouses of foreigners residing in China for work, study, or other purposes who enter China for long-term family visits, as well as persons who need to reside in China for other private affairs.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国人申请办理外国人居留证件，应当提交本人护照或者其他国际旅行证件以及符合规定的照片和申请事由的相关材料，本人到居留地县级以上地方人民政府公安机关出入境管理机构办理相关手续，并留存指纹等人体生物识别信息。  
（一）工作类居留证件，应当提交工作许可等证明材料；属于国家需要的外国高层次人才和急需紧缺专门人才的，应当按照规定提交有关证明材料。  
（二）学习类居留证件，应当按照规定提交招收单位出具的注明学习期限的函件等证明材料。  
（三）记者类居留证件，应当提交有关主管部门出具的函件和核发的记者证。  
（四）团聚类居留证件，因家庭团聚需要在中国境内居留的，应当提交家庭成员关系证明和与申请事由相关的证明材料；因寄养等原因需要在中国境内居留的，应当提交委托书等证明材料。  
（五）私人事务类居留证件，长期探亲的，应当按照要求提交亲属关系证明、被探望人的居留证件等证明材料；入境处理私人事务的，应当提交因处理私人事务需要在中国境内居留的相关证明材料。  
外国人申请有效期1年以上的居留证件的，应当按照规定提交健康证明。健康证明自开具之日起6个月内有效。

**Article 16** A foreigner applying for a residence permit shall submit his/her passport or other international travel documents, as well as photographs conforming to the requirements and relevant materials concerning the purpose of application, appear in person at the exit and entry administration of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of residence to undergo relevant procedures, and provide fingerprints and other human biometric identification information.  
(1) For a work residence permit, supporting materials such as a work permit shall be submitted; if the foreigner is a high-level foreign talent or urgently needed specialized talent required by the State, relevant supporting materials shall be submitted in accordance with regulations.  
(2) For a study residence permit, supporting materials such as a letter issued by the admitting institution specifying the duration of study shall be submitted in accordance with regulations.  
(3) For a journalist residence permit, a letter issued by the relevant competent authority and a valid press card shall be submitted.  
(4) For a family reunion residence permit, if the need to reside in China is for family reunion, a certificate of family relationship and supporting materials related to the purpose of application shall be submitted; if the need to reside in China is due to foster care or other reasons, supporting materials such as a power of attorney shall be submitted.  
(5) For a private affairs residence permit, if the applicant is entering for long-term family visits, a certificate of kinship, the residence permit of the person being visited, and other supporting materials shall be submitted as required; if the applicant is entering to handle private affairs, relevant supporting materials showing the need to reside in China for handling private affairs shall be submitted.  
A foreigner applying for a residence permit valid for one year or more shall submit a health certificate in accordance with regulations. A health certificate shall be valid for six months from the date of issuance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　外国人申请办理居留证件的延期、换发、补发，应当填写申请表，提交本人的护照或者其他国际旅行证件以及符合规定的照片和申请事由的相关材料。

**Article 17** A foreigner applying for extension, renewal, or replacement of a residence permit shall fill in an application form and submit his/her passport or other international travel documents, as well as photographs conforming to the requirements and relevant materials concerning the purpose of application.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　外国人申请居留证件或者申请居留证件的延期、换发、补发符合受理规定的，公安机关出入境管理机构应当出具有效期不超过15日的受理回执，并在受理回执有效期内作出是否签发的决定。  
外国人申请居留证件或者申请居留证件的延期、换发、补发的手续或者材料不符合规定的，公安机关出入境管理机构应当一次性告知申请人需要履行的手续和补正的申请材料。  
申请人所持护照或者其他国际旅行证件因办理证件被收存期间，可以凭受理回执在中国境内合法居留。

**Article 18** Where an application by a foreigner for a residence permit or for extension, renewal, or replacement of a residence permit meets the requirements for acceptance, the exit and entry administration of the public security organ shall issue an acceptance receipt valid for not more than 15 days, and shall decide whether to issue the permit within the validity period of the acceptance receipt.  
Where the procedures or materials for a foreigner's application for a residence permit or for extension, renewal, or replacement of a residence permit do not meet the requirements, the exit and entry administration of the public security organ shall inform the applicant at one time of the procedures to be completed and the application materials to be supplemented or corrected.  
During the period when the applicant's passport or other international travel documents are held by the authority for processing the relevant certificate, the applicant may legally reside within China by presenting the acceptance receipt.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国人申请签证和居留证件的延期、换发、补发，申请办理停留证件，有下列情形之一的，可以由邀请单位或者个人、申请人的亲属、有关专门服务机构代为申请：  
（一）未满16周岁或者已满60周岁以及因疾病等原因行动不便的；  
（二）非首次入境且在中国境内停留居留记录良好的；  
（三）邀请单位或者个人对外国人在中国境内期间所需费用提供保证措施的。  
外国人申请居留证件，属于国家需要的外国高层次人才和急需紧缺专门人才以及前款第一项规定情形的，可以由邀请单位或者个人、申请人的亲属、有关专门服务机构代为申请。

**Article 19** Where a foreigner applies for extension, renewal, or replacement of a visa or residence permit, or applies for a stay permit, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the application may be submitted on his/her behalf by the inviting entity or individual, the applicant's relatives, or relevant specialized service agencies:  
(1) Being under the age of 16 or over the age of 60, or having difficulty in moving due to illness or other reasons;  
(2) Not entering China for the first time and having a good record of stay and residence in China;  
(3) The inviting entity or individual provides guarantee measures for the expenses required by the foreigner during his/her stay in China.  
Where a foreigner applies for a residence permit and falls under the category of high-level foreign talent or urgently needed specialized talent required by the State, or under the circumstances specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph, the application may be submitted on his/her behalf by the inviting entity or individual, the applicant's relatives, or relevant specialized service agencies.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公安机关出入境管理机构可以通过面谈、电话询问、实地调查等方式核实申请事由的真实性，申请人以及出具邀请函件、证明材料的单位或者个人应当予以配合。

**Article 20** The exit and entry administration of the public security organ may verify the authenticity of the purpose of application through interviews, telephone inquiries, on-site investigations, or other means, and the applicant, as well as the unit or individual that issues the invitation letter or supporting materials, shall cooperate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　公安机关出入境管理机构对有下列情形之一的外国人，不予批准签证和居留证件的延期、换发、补发，不予签发停留证件：  
（一）不能按照规定提供申请材料的；  
（二）在申请过程中弄虚作假的；  
（三）违反中国有关法律、行政法规规定，不适合在中国境内停留居留的；  
（四）不宜批准签证和居留证件的延期、换发、补发或者签发停留证件的其他情形。

**Article 21** The exit-entry administration of the public security organ shall not approve the extension, reissuance, or replacement of visas and residence permits, and shall not issue stay permits, to any foreigner under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) failing to provide application materials as required;  
(2) resorting to fraud in the application process;  
(3) violating the relevant laws and administrative regulations of China and being unsuitable for staying or residing in China; or  
(4) other circumstances under which it is inappropriate to approve the extension, reissuance, or replacement of visas and residence permits or to issue stay permits.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　持学习类居留证件的外国人需要在校外勤工助学或者实习的，应当经所在学校同意后，向公安机关出入境管理机构申请居留证件加注勤工助学或者实习地点、期限等信息。  
持学习类居留证件的外国人所持居留证件未加注前款规定信息的，不得在校外勤工助学或者实习。

**Article 22** A foreigner holding a study-type residence permit who needs to engage in work-study or internship off-campus shall, after obtaining the consent of the school, apply to the exit-entry administration of the public security organ for an endorsement of the location and duration of the work-study or internship on the residence permit.  
If the residence permit held by a foreigner holding a study-type residence permit does not bear the endorsement of the information specified in the preceding paragraph, such foreigner shall not engage in work-study or internship off-campus.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在中国境内的外国人因证件遗失、损毁、被盗抢等原因未持有效护照或者国际旅行证件，无法在本国驻中国有关机构补办的，可以向停留居留地县级以上地方人民政府公安机关出入境管理机构申请办理出境手续。

**Article 23** If a foreigner in China does not hold a valid passport or other international travel document due to loss, damage, or theft of the document and is unable to obtain a replacement from the relevant institution of his or her country in China, the foreigner may apply to the exit-entry administration of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place of stay or residence for exit formalities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　所持出境入境证件注明停留区域的外国人、出入境边防检查机关批准临时入境且限定停留区域的外国人，应当在限定的区域内停留。

**Article 24** Foreigners whose exit-entry documents specify the area of stay, and foreigners who are approved by the border inspection authorities for temporary entry with a restricted stay area, shall stay within the restricted area.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　外国人在中国境内有下列情形之一的，属于非法居留：  
（一）超过签证、停留居留证件规定的停留居留期限停留居留的；  
（二）免办签证入境的外国人超过免签期限停留且未办理停留居留证件的；  
（三）外国人超出限定的停留居留区域活动的；  
（四）其他非法居留的情形。

**Article 25** A foreigner in China in any of the following circumstances shall be considered to be in unlawful residence:  
(1) staying or residing beyond the period specified in the visa, stay or residence permit;  
(2) entering China without a visa and staying beyond the visa-free period without obtaining a stay or residence permit;  
(3) moving beyond the restricted area of stay or residence; or  
(4) other circumstances of unlawful residence.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　聘用外国人工作或者招收外国留学生的单位，发现有下列情形之一的，应当及时向所在地县级以上地方人民政府公安机关出入境管理机构报告：  
（一）聘用的外国人离职或者变更工作地域的；  
（二）招收的外国留学生毕业、结业、肄业、退学，离开原招收单位的；  
（三）聘用的外国人、招收的外国留学生违反出境入境管理规定的；  
（四）聘用的外国人、招收的外国留学生出现死亡、失踪等情形的。

**Article 26** A unit that employs a foreigner to work or recruits a foreign student shall promptly report to the exit-entry administration of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in the place where it is located if any of the following circumstances is discovered:  
(1) the employed foreigner leaves employment or changes the work area;  
(2) the recruited foreign student graduates, completes their studies, discontinues their studies, or withdraws from school and leaves the original recruiting unit;  
(3) the employed foreigner or recruited foreign student violates the provisions on exit and entry administration; or  
(4) the employed foreigner or recruited foreign student dies, goes missing, or encounters other such circumstances.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　金融、教育、医疗、电信等单位在办理业务时需要核实外国人身份信息的，可以向公安机关出入境管理机构申请核实。

**Article 27** A unit in finance, education, medical care, telecommunications or any other sector that needs to verify the identity information of a foreigner when handling business may apply to the exit-entry administration of the public security organ for verification.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　外国人因外交、公务事由在中国境内停留居留证件的签发管理，按照外交部的规定执行。

**Article 28** The issuance and administration of stay and residence permits for foreigners in China for diplomatic or official purposes shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

### 第四章　调查和遣返

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公安机关根据实际需要可以设置遣返场所。  
依照[出境入境管理法第六十条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国出境入境管理法.md#art-60)的规定对外国人实施拘留审查的，应当在24小时内将被拘留审查的外国人送到拘留所或者遣返场所。  
由于天气、当事人健康状况等原因无法立即执行遣送出境、驱逐出境的，应当凭相关法律文书将外国人羁押在拘留所或者遣返场所。

**Article 29** Public security organs may establish repatriation centers as needed.  
Where a foreigner is placed under detention for investigation in accordance with Article 60 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law, such foreigner shall be sent to a detention center or a repatriation center within 24 hours.  
If due to weather, the health condition of the party, or other reasons, the repatriation or deportation cannot be executed immediately, the foreigner shall be held in custody at a detention center or a repatriation center on the strength of the relevant legal documents.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　依照[出境入境管理法第六十一条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国出境入境管理法.md#art-61)的规定，对外国人限制活动范围的，应当出具限制活动范围决定书。被限制活动范围的外国人，应当在指定的时间到公安机关报到；未经决定机关批准，不得变更生活居所或者离开限定的区域。

**Article 30** Where a foreigner's scope of activities is restricted in accordance with Article 61 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law, a decision on restricting the scope of activities shall be issued. A foreigner whose scope of activities is restricted shall report to the public security organ at the designated time, and shall not change his or her place of residence or leave the restricted area without the approval of the decision-making organ.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　依照[出境入境管理法第六十二条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国出境入境管理法.md#art-62)的规定，对外国人实施遣送出境的，作出遣送出境决定的机关应当依法确定被遣送出境的外国人不准入境的具体期限。

**Article 31** In accordance with the provisions of Article 62 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law, where a foreigner is ordered to be repatriated, the organ that makes the repatriation decision shall, according to law, determine the specific period during which the repatriated foreigner shall be prohibited from entering China.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　外国人被遣送出境所需的费用由本人承担。本人无力承担的，属于非法就业的，由非法聘用的单位、个人承担；属于其他情形的，由对外国人在中国境内停留居留提供保证措施的单位或者个人承担。  
遣送外国人出境，由县级以上地方人民政府公安机关或者出入境边防检查机关实施。

**Article 32** The expenses required for the repatriation of a foreigner shall be borne by the foreigner himself or herself. If the foreigner is unable to bear such expenses, where the case involves illegal employment, they shall be borne by the unit or individual that illegally employed the foreigner; under other circumstances, they shall be borne by the unit or individual that provided guarantee measures for the foreigner's stay or residence in China.  
The repatriation of a foreigner from China shall be carried out by the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level or by the exit-entry border inspection authority.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　外国人被决定限期出境的，作出决定的机关应当在注销或者收缴其原出境入境证件后，为其补办停留手续并限定出境的期限。限定出境期限最长不得超过15日。

**Article 33** Where a foreigner is ordered to leave China within a specified period, the organ that makes the decision shall, after canceling or confiscating the foreigner's original exit-entry documents, handle the procedures for his or her stay and set a time limit for departure. The time limit for departure shall not exceed 15 days at most.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外国人有下列情形之一的，其所持签证、停留居留证件由签发机关宣布作废：  
（一）签证、停留居留证件损毁、遗失、被盗抢的；  
（二）被决定限期出境、遣送出境、驱逐出境，其所持签证、停留居留证件未被收缴或者注销的；  
（三）原居留事由变更，未在规定期限内向公安机关出入境管理机构申报，经公安机关公告后仍未申报的；  
（四）有[出境入境管理法第二十一条](../../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国出境入境管理法.md#art-21)、第三十一条规定的不予签发签证、居留证件情形的。  
签发机关对签证、停留居留证件依法宣布作废的，可以当场宣布作废或者公告宣布作废。

**Article 34** Where a foreigner is in any of the following circumstances, the visa or stay or residence certificate held by him or her shall be declared invalid by the issuing authority:  
(1) The visa or stay or residence certificate is damaged, lost, or stolen;  
(2) The foreigner is ordered to leave China within a specified period, repatriated, or deported, and the visa or stay or residence certificate held by him or her has not been confiscated or canceled;  
(3) The original reason for residence has changed, and the foreigner fails to report to the exit-entry administration agency of the public security organ within the prescribed time limit, and still fails to report after a public notice issued by the public security organ;  
(4) There are circumstances provided for in Articles 21 and 31 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law under which visas or residence certificates shall not be issued.  
Where the issuing authority declares a visa or stay or residence certificate invalid according to law, it may make the invalidity declaration on the spot or by public notice.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　外国人所持签证、停留居留证件有下列情形之一的，由公安机关注销或者收缴：  
（一）被签发机关宣布作废或者被他人冒用的；  
（二）通过伪造、变造、骗取或者其他方式非法获取的；  
（三）持有人被决定限期出境、遣送出境、驱逐出境的。  
作出注销或者收缴决定的机关应当及时通知签发机关。

**Article 35** Where a visa or stay or residence certificate held by a foreigner is in any of the following circumstances, the public security organ shall cancel or confiscate it:  
(1) It is declared invalid by the issuing authority or is used fraudulently by another person;  
(2) It is obtained illegally through forgery, alteration, deception, or other means;  
(3) The holder is ordered to leave China within a specified period, repatriated, or deported.  
The organ that makes the cancellation or confiscation decision shall promptly notify the issuing authority.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
（一）签证的入境次数，是指持证人在签证入境有效期内可以入境的次数。  
（二）签证的入境有效期，是指持证人所持签证入境的有效时间范围。非经签发机关注明，签证自签发之日起生效，于有效期满当日北京时间24时失效。  
（三）签证的停留期限，是指持证人每次入境后被准许停留的时限，自入境次日开始计算。  
（四）短期，是指在中国境内停留不超过180日（含180日）。  
（五）长期、常驻，是指在中国境内居留超过180日。  
本条例规定的公安机关出入境管理机构审批期限和受理回执有效期以工作日计算，不含法定节假日。

**Article 36** For the purposes of these Regulations:  
(1) The number of entries of a visa means the number of times the holder may enter China within the validity period for entry of the visa.  
(2) The validity period for entry of a visa means the time range during which the holder's visa is valid for entry. Unless otherwise indicated by the issuing authority, the visa shall take effect on the date of issuance and shall cease to have effect at 24:00 Beijing time on the day when the validity period expires.  
(3) The duration of stay of a visa means the time limit during which the holder is permitted to stay after each entry, calculated from the day following the day of entry.  
(4) Short-term means staying in China for not more than 180 days (including 180 days).  
(5) Long-term or permanent residence means residing in China for more than 180 days.  
The time limits for examination and approval by the exit-entry administration agencies of the public security organs and the validity period of acceptance receipts as provided for in these Regulations shall be calculated in working days, excluding statutory holidays.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　经外交部批准，驻外签证机关可以委托当地有关机构承办外国人签证申请的接件、录入、咨询等服务性事务。

**Article 37** With the approval of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, visa authorities stationed abroad may entrust relevant local agencies to handle service matters such as receiving applications, data entry, and consultation regarding foreigners' visa applications.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　签证的式样由外交部会同公安部规定。停留居留证件的式样由公安部规定。

**Article 38** The format of visas shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs jointly with the Ministry of Public Security. The format of stay or residence certificates shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自2013年9月1日起施行。1986年12月3日国务院批准，1986年12月27日公安部、外交部公布，1994年7月13日、2010年4月24日国务院修订的《中华人民共和国外国人入境出境管理法实施细则》同时废止。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2013. The Rules for the Implementation of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit of Aliens, which were approved by the State Council on December 3, 1986, promulgated by the Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on December 27, 1986, and revised by the State Council on July 13, 1994 and April 24, 2010, shall be abolished simultaneously.
