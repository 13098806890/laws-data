# 中华人民共和国军品出口管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2002-10-15  
**生效日期**：1998-01-01

> (1997年10月22日中华人民共和国国务院、中华人民共和国中央军事委员会令第234号发布　根据2002年10月15日《国务院、中央军事委员会关于修改〈中华人民共和国军品出口管理条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对军品出口的统一管理，维护正常的军品出口秩序，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称军品出口，是指用于军事目的的装备、专用生产设备及其他物资、技术和有关服务的贸易性出口。 前款所称军品出口，纳入军品出口管理清单。军品出口管理清单由国家军品出口主管部门制定、调整并公布。 第三条　国家军品出口主管部门在国务院、中央军事委员会的领导下，主管全国的军品出口工作，对全国的军品出口实施监督管理。 第四条　国家实行统一的军品出口管理制度，禁止任何损害国家的利益和安全的军品出口行为，依法保障正常的军品出口秩序。 第五条　军品出口应当遵循下列原则： (一)有助于接受国的正当自卫能力； (二)不损害有关地区的和世界的和平、安全与稳定； (三)不干涉接受国的内政。 第六条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本条例有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。 第二章　军品贸易公司 第七条　本条例所称军品贸易公司，是指依法取得军品出口经营权，并在核定的经营范围内从事军品出口经营活动的企业法人。 第八条　军品出口经营权由国家军品出口主管部门审查批准。具体办法由国家军品出口主管部门规定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对军品出口的统一管理，维护正常的军品出口秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the unified administration of military product exports and maintaining the normal order of military product exports.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称军品出口，是指用于军事目的的装备、专用生产设备及其他物资、技术和有关服务的贸易性出口。  
前款所称军品出口，纳入军品出口管理清单。军品出口管理清单由国家军品出口主管部门制定、调整并公布。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, military product export refers to the trade export of equipment, specialized production equipment and other materials, technologies and related services for military purposes.  
The military product export in the preceding paragraph shall be included in the military product export management list. The military product export management list shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the national competent department for military product exports.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家军品出口主管部门在国务院、中央军事委员会的领导下，主管全国的军品出口工作，对全国的军品出口实施监督管理。

**Article 3** The national competent department for military product exports shall, under the leadership of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, take charge of the nationwide military product export work and exercise supervision and administration over military product exports nationwide.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行统一的军品出口管理制度，禁止任何损害国家的利益和安全的军品出口行为，依法保障正常的军品出口秩序。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a unified military product export management system, prohibit any military product export act that harms the interests and security of the State, and safeguard the normal order of military product exports according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　军品出口应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)有助于接受国的正当自卫能力；  
(二)不损害有关地区的和世界的和平、安全与稳定；  
(三)不干涉接受国的内政。

**Article 5** Military product exports shall follow the following principles:  
(1) contributing to the legitimate self-defense capability of the recipient country;  
(2) not harming the peace, security and stability of the relevant regions and the world;  
(3) not interfering in the internal affairs of the recipient country.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约同本条例有不同规定的，适用国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 6** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions different from those of these Regulations, the provisions of the international treaty shall prevail, except for the clauses on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

### 第二章　军品贸易公司

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本条例所称军品贸易公司，是指依法取得军品出口经营权，并在核定的经营范围内从事军品出口经营活动的企业法人。

**Article 7** For the purposes of these Regulations, military product trading company refers to an enterprise legal person that has obtained the right to operate military product exports according to law and engages in military product export business within the approved scope of business.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　军品出口经营权由国家军品出口主管部门审查批准。具体办法由国家军品出口主管部门规定。

**Article 8** The right to operate military product exports shall be examined and approved by the national competent department for military product exports. Specific measures shall be formulated by the national competent department for military product exports.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军品贸易公司依法自主经营、自负盈亏。

**Article 9** A military product trading company shall operate independently and assume sole responsibility for its profits and losses according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军品贸易公司应当信守合同，保证商品质量，完善售后服务。

**Article 10** A military product trading company shall abide by contracts, ensure product quality, and improve after-sales service.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　军品贸易公司应当按照国家军品出口主管部门的规定，如实提交与其军品出口经营活动有关的文件及资料。国家军品出口主管部门应当为军品贸易公司保守商业秘密，维护军品贸易公司的合法权益。

**Article 11** A military product trading company shall, in accordance with the provisions of the national competent department for military product exports, truthfully submit documents and materials related to its military product export business. The national competent department for military product exports shall maintain the confidentiality of trade secrets for military product trading companies and protect the legitimate rights and interests of military product trading companies.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　军品贸易公司可以委托经批准的军品出口运输企业，办理军品出口运输及相关业务。具体办法由国家军品出口主管部门规定。

**Article 12** A military product trading company may entrust an approved military product export transport enterprise to handle military product export transport and related business. Specific measures shall be formulated by the national competent department for military product exports.

### 第三章　军品出口管理

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对军品出口实行许可制度。  
军品出口项目、合同，应当依照本条例的规定申请审查批准。军品出口，应当凭军品出口许可证。

**Article 13** The State shall implement a licensing system for military product exports.  
Military product export projects and contracts shall be subject to application for examination and approval in accordance with these Regulations. Military product exports shall be made with a military product export license.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军品出口项目，由国家军品出口主管部门或者由国家军品出口主管部门会同国务院、中央军事委员会的有关部门审查批准。

**Article 14** Military product export projects shall be examined and approved by the national competent department for military product exports, or by the national competent department for military product exports jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　军品出口项目经批准后，军品贸易公司可以对外签订军品出口合同。军品出口合同签订后，应当向国家军品出口主管部门申请审查批准；国家军品出口主管部门应当自收到申请之日起20日内作出决定。军品出口合同获得批准，方可生效。  
军品贸易公司向国家军品出口主管部门申请批准军品出口合同时，应当附送接受国的有效证明文件。

**Article 15** After a military product export project is approved, the military product trading company may sign a military product export contract with foreign parties. After a military product export contract is signed, it shall apply to the national competent department for military product exports for examination and approval; the national competent department for military product exports shall make a decision within 20 days from the date of receipt of the application. A military product export contract shall take effect only after being approved.  
When applying to the national competent department for military product exports for approval of a military product export contract, the military product trading company shall attach valid certification documents of the recipient country.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　重大的军品出口项目、合同，应当经国家军品出口主管部门会同国务院、中央军事委员会的有关部门审查，报国务院和中央军事委员会批准。

**Article 16** Major military product export projects and contracts shall be examined by the national competent department for military product exports jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and submitted to the State Council and the Central Military Commission for approval.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军品贸易公司在军品出口前，应当凭军品出口合同批准文件，向国家军品出口主管部门申请领取军品出口许可证；符合军品出口合同规定的，国家军品出口主管部门应当自收到申请之日起10日内签发军品出口许可证。  
海关凭军品出口许可证接受申报，并按照国家有关规定验放。

**Article 17** Before exporting military products, the military product trading company shall, on the basis of the approval documents for the military product export contract, apply to the national competent department for military product exports for a military product export license; if it complies with the provisions of the military product export contract, the national competent department for military product exports shall issue a military product export license within 10 days from the date of receipt of the application.  
The customs shall accept the declaration on the basis of the military product export license and examine and release the goods in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　军品出口项目、合同的审查批准办法和军品出口许可证的签发办法，由国家军品出口主管部门制定。

**Article 18** The measures for the examination and approval of military product export projects and contracts and the measures for the issuance of military product export licenses shall be formulated by the national competent department for military product exports.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　军品出口，由国家军品出口主管部门会同有关部门下达军品出口通知。有关部门和地方人民政府收到军品出口通知后，应当按照国家有关规定认真履行职责，保证军品出口的安全、迅速、准确。

**Article 19** For military product exports, the national competent department for military product exports shall, jointly with the relevant departments, issue a military product export notice. The relevant departments and local people's governments shall, after receiving a military product export notice, earnestly perform their duties in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and ensure the safety, speed and accuracy of military product exports.

### 第四章　军品出口秩序

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　未取得军品出口经营权的任何单位或者组织，不得从事军品出口经营活动。  
国家禁止个人从事军品出口经营活动。

**Article 20** No unit or organization that has not obtained the right to operate military product exports shall engage in military product export business.  
The State prohibits individuals from engaging in military product export business.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　军品贸易公司在军品出口经营活动中，应当遵守法律和行政法规的规定，维护正常的军品出口秩序。

**Article 21** A military product trading company shall, in its military product export business activities, abide by the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, and maintain the normal order of military product exports.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军品贸易公司在军品出口经营活动中，不得有下列行为：  
(一)危害国家安全或者社会公共利益；  
(二)以不正当竞争手段排挤竞争对手；  
(三)侵害中华人民共和国法律保护的知识产权；  
(四)伪造、变造、骗取或者转让军品出口项目批准文件、合同批准文件、许可证和接受国的有效证明文件等单证；  
(五)超越核定的经营范围经营；  
(六)违反法律和行政法规规定的其他行为。

**Article 22** A military product trading company shall not, in its military product export business activities, commit any of the following acts:  
(1) endangering national security or public interests;  
(2) squeezing out competitors by means of unfair competition;  
(3) infringing intellectual property rights protected by the laws of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) forging, altering, fraudulently obtaining or transferring documents such as approval documents for military product export projects, approval documents for contracts, licenses and valid certification documents of the recipient country;  
(5) engaging in business beyond the approved scope of business;  
(6) other acts violating the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家军品出口主管部门认为必要时或者根据军品贸易公司的请求，可以对妨碍正常的军品出口秩序的行为进行处理。

**Article 23** When it deems it necessary or upon the request of a military product trading company, the national competent department for military product exports may deal with acts that hinder the normal order of military product exports.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　军品贸易公司违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的，由国家军品出口主管部门责令限期改正，予以警告；逾期不改正的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，暂停直至撤销其军品出口经营权。

**Article 24** Where a military product trading company violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Regulations, the national competent department for military product exports shall order it to make corrections within a time limit and give it a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and its right to operate military product exports shall be suspended or even revoked.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　军品贸易公司违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)第(四)项、第(五)项规定，触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪，伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由国家军品出口主管部门予以警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上3倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款，暂停直至撤销其军品出口经营权。  
军品贸易公司违反[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)第(一)项、第(二)项、第(三)项规定或者其他法律、行政法规规定的，由国家有关主管部门依照有关法律和行政法规的规定予以处罚，国家军品出口主管部门并可以暂停直至撤销其军品出口经营权；触犯刑律的，依照刑法有关规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where a military product trading company violates the provisions of subparagraphs (4) and (5) of Article 22 of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation, the crime of forging, altering or buying or selling official documents, certificates or seals of State organs, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, the national competent department for military product exports shall give it a warning, confiscate its illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed, and its right to operate military product exports shall be suspended or even revoked.  
Where a military product trading company violates the provisions of subparagraphs (1), (2) or (3) of Article 22 of these Regulations or other provisions of laws or administrative regulations, the relevant competent department of the State shall impose punishment in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, and the national competent department for military product exports may suspend or even revoke its right to operate military product exports; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)规定的，由国家军品出口主管部门取缔非法活动；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由国家军品出口主管部门予以警告，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 26** Where any person violates the provisions of Article 20 of these Regulations, the national competent department for military product exports shall ban the illegal activities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, the national competent department for military product exports shall give a warning, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　军品贸易公司对国家军品出口主管部门作出的具体行政行为不服的，应当先依法申请行政复议；对行政复议决定仍不服的，可以依法向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 27** Where a military product trading company is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act of the national competent department for military product exports, it shall first apply for administrative reconsideration according to law; if it is still dissatisfied with the administrative reconsideration decision, it may file an administrative action with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家军品出口管理工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守或者利用职务上的便利收受、索取他人财物，触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪、受贿罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 28** Where a staff member of the national competent department for military product exports abuses his or her power, neglects his or her duty, or takes or extorts property from others by taking advantage of his or her position, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, the crime of taking bribes or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　警用装备的出口适用本条例。

**Article 29** The export of police equipment shall be governed by these Regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自1998年1月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1998.
