# 城市房地产开发经营管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (1998年7月20日中华人民共和国国务院令第248号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2019年3月24日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　根据2020年3月27日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订  根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第五次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范房地产开发经营行为，加强对城市房地产开发经营活动的监督管理，促进和保障房地产业的健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》的有关规定，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称房地产开发经营，是指房地产开发企业在城市规划区内国有土地上进行基础设施建设、房屋建设，并转让房地产开发项目或者销售、出租商品房的行为。 第三条　房地产开发经营应当按照经济效益、社会效益、环境效益相统一的原则，实行全面规划、合理布局、综合开发、配套建设。 第四条　国务院建设行政主管部门负责全国房地产开发经营活动的监督管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府房地产开发主管部门负责本行政区域内房地产开发经营活动的监督管理工作。 县级以上人民政府负责土地管理工作的部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，负责与房地产开发经营有关的土地管理工作。 第二章　房地产开发企业 第五条　设立房地产开发企业，除应当符合有关法律、行政法规规定的企业设立条件外，还应当具备下列条件： (一)有100万元以上的注册资本； (二)有4名以上持有资格证书的房地产专业、建筑工程专业的专职技术人员，2名以上持有资格证书的专职会计人员。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地方的实际情况，对设立房地产开发企业的注册资本和专业技术人员的条件作出高于前款的规定。 第六条　外商投资设立房地产开发企业的，除应当符合本条例第五条的规定外，还应当符合外商投资法律、行政法规的规定。 第七条　设立房地产开发企业，应当向县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门申请登记。工商行政管理部门对符合本条例第五条规定条件的，应当自收到申请之日起30日内予以登记；对不符合条件不予登记的，应当说明理由。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范房地产开发经营行为，加强对城市房地产开发经营活动的监督管理，促进和保障房地产业的健康发展，根据《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》的有关规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Urban Real Estate Administration Law of the People's Republic of China for the purpose of regulating the acts of real estate development and management, strengthening the supervision and administration of urban real estate development and management activities, and promoting and ensuring the healthy development of the real estate industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称房地产开发经营，是指房地产开发企业在城市规划区内国有土地上进行基础设施建设、房屋建设，并转让房地产开发项目或者销售、出租商品房的行为。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "real estate development and management" means the acts of a real estate development enterprise in carrying out infrastructure construction and housing construction on state-owned land within urban planning areas, and assigning real estate development projects or selling or leasing commercial housing.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　房地产开发经营应当按照经济效益、社会效益、环境效益相统一的原则，实行全面规划、合理布局、综合开发、配套建设。

**Article 3** Real estate development and management shall follow the principle of integrating economic benefits, social benefits, and environmental benefits, and shall implement comprehensive planning, rational layout, integrated development, and supporting construction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院建设行政主管部门负责全国房地产开发经营活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府房地产开发主管部门负责本行政区域内房地产开发经营活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府负责土地管理工作的部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，负责与房地产开发经营有关的土地管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent construction administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of real estate development and management activities nationwide.  
The competent real estate development administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of real estate development and management activities within its administrative region.  
The department in charge of land administration of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, be responsible for the land administration related to real estate development and management.

### 第二章　房地产开发企业

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　设立房地产开发企业，除应当符合有关法律、行政法规规定的企业设立条件外，还应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有100万元以上的注册资本；  
(二)有4名以上持有资格证书的房地产专业、建筑工程专业的专职技术人员，2名以上持有资格证书的专职会计人员。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本地方的实际情况，对设立房地产开发企业的注册资本和专业技术人员的条件作出高于前款的规定。

**Article 5** To establish a real estate development enterprise, in addition to meeting the conditions for enterprise establishment prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations, the following conditions shall also be satisfied:  
(1) Having a registered capital of not less than RMB 1,000,000;  
(2) Having at least 4 full-time technical personnel with qualification certificates in real estate or construction engineering, and at least 2 full-time accounting personnel with qualification certificates.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the actual local conditions, stipulate conditions for the registered capital and professional technical personnel for the establishment of real estate development enterprises that are higher than those prescribed in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外商投资设立房地产开发企业的，除应当符合[本条例第五条](#art-5)的规定外，还应当符合外商投资法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 6** Where a real estate development enterprise is established with foreign investment, in addition to meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on foreign investment shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立房地产开发企业，应当向县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门申请登记。工商行政管理部门对符合[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定条件的，应当自收到申请之日起30日内予以登记；对不符合条件不予登记的，应当说明理由。  
工商行政管理部门在对设立房地产开发企业申请登记进行审查时，应当听取同级房地产开发主管部门的意见。

**Article 7** To establish a real estate development enterprise, an application for registration shall be made to the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level. The administrative department for industry and commerce shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application, register an enterprise that meets the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations; if it refuses to register due to non-compliance with the conditions, it shall state the reasons.  
When examining an application for registration of a real estate development enterprise, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall solicit the opinions of the competent real estate development administrative department at the same level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　房地产开发企业应当自领取营业执照之日起30日内，提交下列纸质或者电子材料，向登记机关所在地的房地产开发主管部门备案：  
(一)营业执照复印件；  
(二)企业章程；  
(三)专业技术人员的资格证书和聘用合同。

**Article 8** A real estate development enterprise shall, within 30 days from the date of obtaining its business license, submit the following paper or electronic materials to the competent real estate development administrative department at the location of the registration authority for record-filing:  
(1) A copy of the business license;  
(2) The enterprise's articles of association;  
(3) The qualification certificates and employment contracts of the professional technical personnel.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　房地产开发主管部门应当根据房地产开发企业的资产、专业技术人员和开发经营业绩等，对备案的房地产开发企业核定资质等级。房地产开发企业应当按照核定的资质等级，承担相应的房地产开发项目。具体办法由国务院建设行政主管部门制定。

**Article 9** The competent real estate development administrative department shall, based on the assets, professional technical personnel, and development and management performance of the real estate development enterprise, determine the qualification grade of the real estate development enterprise that has filed for record. A real estate development enterprise shall undertake the corresponding real estate development projects in accordance with the determined qualification grade. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent construction administrative department of the State Council.

### 第三章　房地产开发建设

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　确定房地产开发项目，应当符合土地利用总体规划、年度建设用地计划和城市规划、房地产开发年度计划的要求；按照国家有关规定需要经计划主管部门批准的，还应当报计划主管部门批准，并纳入年度固定资产投资计划。

**Article 10** The determination of a real estate development project shall comply with the requirements of the overall land use plan, the annual construction land plan, the urban plan, and the annual plan for real estate development; if approval by the competent planning department is required in accordance with relevant state provisions, approval shall also be obtained from the competent planning department and the project shall be included in the annual fixed asset investment plan.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　确定房地产开发项目，应当坚持旧区改建和新区建设相结合的原则，注重开发基础设施薄弱、交通拥挤、环境污染严重以及危旧房屋集中的区域，保护和改善城市生态环境，保护历史文化遗产。

**Article 11** The determination of a real estate development project shall adhere to the principle of combining the renovation of old districts with the construction of new districts, focus on developing areas with weak infrastructure, heavy traffic congestion, severe environmental pollution, and concentrated dilapidated and old houses, protect and improve the urban ecological environment, and protect historical and cultural heritage.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　房地产开发用地应当以出让方式取得；但是，法律和国务院规定可以采用划拨方式的除外。  
土地使用权出让或者划拨前，县级以上地方人民政府城市规划行政主管部门和房地产开发主管部门应当对下列事项提出书面意见，作为土地使用权出让或者划拨的依据之一：  
(一)房地产开发项目的性质、规模和开发期限；  
(二)城市规划设计条件；  
(三)基础设施和公共设施的建设要求；  
(四)基础设施建成后的产权界定；  
(五)项目拆迁补偿、安置要求。

**Article 12** Land for real estate development shall be obtained by means of grant; except where allocation may be used as provided by laws and the State Council.  
Before the grant or allocation of land use rights, the competent urban planning administrative department and the competent real estate development administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall provide written opinions on the following matters, as one of the bases for the grant or allocation of land use rights:  
(1) The nature, scale, and development period of the real estate development project;  
(2) The urban planning and design conditions;  
(3) The construction requirements for infrastructure and public facilities;  
(4) The definition of property rights after the completion of infrastructure;  
(5) The compensation for demolition and relocation and resettlement requirements for the project.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　房地产开发项目应当建立资本金制度，资本金占项目总投资的比例不得低于20%。

**Article 13** A real estate development project shall establish a capital system, and the proportion of capital funds to the total investment of the project shall not be less than 20%.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　房地产开发项目的开发建设应当统筹安排配套基础设施，并根据先地下、后地上的原则实施。

**Article 14** The development and construction of a real estate development project shall coordinate and arrange supporting infrastructure, and shall be implemented in accordance with the principle of "underground first, aboveground afterward".

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　房地产开发企业应当按照土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途、动工开发期限进行项目开发建设。出让合同约定的动工开发期限满1年未动工开发的，可以征收相当于土地使用权出让金20%以下的土地闲置费；满2年未动工开发的，可以无偿收回土地使用权。但是，因不可抗力或者政府、政府有关部门的行为或者动工开发必需的前期工作造成动工迟延的除外。

**Article 15** A real estate development enterprise shall carry out project development and construction in accordance with the land use purpose and commencement and development period agreed upon in the land use right grant contract. Where development has not commenced within one year after the expiration of the commencement and development period agreed upon in the grant contract, a land idle fee of not more than 20% of the land use right grant price may be levied; where development has not commenced within two years, the land use right may be taken back without compensation. However, cases where the delay in commencement is caused by force majeure, or acts of the government or relevant government departments, or the preliminary work necessary for commencement, are excepted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　房地产开发企业开发建设的房地产项目，应当符合有关法律、法规的规定和建筑工程质量、安全标准、建筑工程勘察、设计、施工的技术规范以及合同的约定。  
房地产开发企业应当对其开发建设的房地产开发项目的质量承担责任。  
勘察、设计、施工、监理等单位应当依照有关法律、法规的规定或者合同的约定，承担相应的责任。

**Article 16** The real estate projects developed and constructed by a real estate development enterprise shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, the quality and safety standards for construction projects, the technical specifications for surveying, design, and construction of construction projects, and the agreements of the contract.  
A real estate development enterprise shall bear responsibility for the quality of the real estate development projects it develops and constructs.  
Units engaged in surveying, design, construction, and supervision shall bear corresponding responsibilities in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations or the agreements of the contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　房地产开发项目竣工，依照《建设工程质量管理条例》的规定验收合格后，方可交付使用。

**Article 17** After the completion of a real estate development project, it may be delivered for use only after it has passed the acceptance inspection in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Quality Management of Construction Projects.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　房地产开发企业应当将房地产开发项目建设过程中的主要事项记录在房地产开发项目手册中，并定期送房地产开发主管部门备案。

**Article 18** A real estate development enterprise shall record the major matters in the process of real estate development project construction in the real estate development project manual, and regularly submit it to the competent real estate development administrative department for record-filing.

### 第四章　房地产经营

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　转让房地产开发项目，应当符合[《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》第三十九条](../../../行政与公法/行政法律/中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法.md#art-39)、第四十条规定的条件。

**Article 19** The assignment of a real estate development project shall meet the conditions prescribed in Articles 39 and 40 of the Urban Real Estate Administration Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　转让房地产开发项目，转让人和受让人应当自土地使用权变更登记手续办理完毕之日起30日内，持房地产开发项目转让合同到房地产开发主管部门备案。

**Article 20** Where a real estate development project is assigned, the assignor and the assignee shall, within 30 days from the date of completion of the modification registration procedures for the land use right, take the contract for assignment of the real estate development project to the competent real estate development administrative department for record-filing.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　房地产开发企业转让房地产开发项目时，尚未完成拆迁补偿安置的，原拆迁补偿安置合同中有关的权利、义务随之转移给受让人。项目转让人应当书面通知被拆迁人。

**Article 21** Where a real estate development enterprise assigns a real estate development project while the demolition compensation and resettlement have not yet been completed, the relevant rights and obligations in the original demolition compensation and resettlement contract shall be transferred to the assignee. The project assignor shall notify the persons whose properties are demolished in writing.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　房地产开发企业预售商品房，应当符合下列条件：  
(一)已交付全部土地使用权出让金，取得土地使用权证书；  
(二)持有建设工程规划许可证和施工许可证；  
(三)按提供的预售商品房计算，投入开发建设的资金达到工程建设总投资的25%以上，并已确定施工进度和竣工交付日期；  
(四)已办理预售登记，取得商品房预售许可证明。

**Article 22** A real estate development enterprise that pre-sells commercial housing shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) Having paid in full the grant price for the land use right and obtained the certificate of land use right;  
(2) Holding the construction project planning permit and the construction permit;  
(3) Having invested in the development and construction an amount that reaches more than 25% of the total investment in the project, calculated on the basis of the commercial housing offered for pre-sale, and having determined the construction schedule and the date of completion and delivery;  
(4) Having completed the pre-sale registration and obtained the pre-sale permit certificate for commercial housing.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　房地产开发企业申请办理商品房预售登记，应当提交下列文件：  
(一)[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)第(一)项至第(三)项规定的证明材料；  
(二)营业执照和资质等级证书；  
(三)工程施工合同；  
(四)预售商品房分层平面图；  
(五)商品房预售方案。

**Article 23** A real estate development enterprise applying for pre-sale registration of commercial housing shall submit the following documents:  
(1) The certification documents prescribed in items (1) to (3) of Article 22 of these Regulations;  
(2) The business license and qualification grade certificate;  
(3) The construction project contract;  
(4) The floor plan of the pre-sale commercial housing;  
(5) The pre-sale plan of the commercial housing.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　房地产开发主管部门应当自收到商品房预售申请之日起10日内，作出同意预售或者不同意预售的答复。同意预售的，应当核发商品房预售许可证明；不同意预售的，应当说明理由。

**Article 24** The competent real estate development administrative department shall, within 10 days from the date of receiving the application for pre-sale of commercial housing, make a reply agreeing to the pre-sale or not agreeing to the pre-sale. If it agrees to the pre-sale, it shall issue a pre-sale permit certificate for commercial housing; if it does not agree, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　房地产开发企业不得进行虚假广告宣传，商品房预售广告中应当载明商品房预售许可证明的文号。

**Article 25** A real estate development enterprise shall not engage in false advertising; the advertisement for pre-sale of commercial housing shall indicate the document number of the pre-sale permit certificate for commercial housing.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　房地产开发企业预售商品房时，应当向预购人出示商品房预售许可证明。  
房地产开发企业应当自商品房预售合同签订之日起30日内，到商品房所在地的县级以上人民政府房地产开发主管部门和负责土地管理工作的部门备案。

**Article 26** When pre-selling commercial housing, a real estate development enterprise shall show the pre-sale permit certificate for commercial housing to the prospective purchaser.  
A real estate development enterprise shall, within 30 days from the date of signing the pre-sale contract for commercial housing, go to the competent real estate development administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level at the location of the commercial housing and the department in charge of land administration for record-filing.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　商品房销售，当事人双方应当签订书面合同。合同应当载明商品房的建筑面积和使用面积、价格、交付日期、质量要求、物业管理方式以及双方的违约责任。

**Article 27** For the sale of commercial housing, both parties shall sign a written contract. The contract shall specify the floor area and usable area of the commercial housing, price, delivery date, quality requirements, property management method, and the liability for breach of contract of both parties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　房地产开发企业委托中介机构代理销售商品房的，应当向中介机构出具委托书。中介机构销售商品房时，应当向商品房购买人出示商品房的有关证明文件和商品房销售委托书。

**Article 28** Where a real estate development enterprise entrusts an intermediary agency to sell commercial housing on its behalf, it shall issue a power of attorney to the intermediary agency. When selling commercial housing, the intermediary agency shall show the relevant certification documents of the commercial housing and the power of attorney for sale of commercial housing to the purchaser.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　房地产开发项目转让和商品房销售价格，由当事人协商议定；但是，享受国家优惠政策的居民住宅价格，应当实行政府指导价或者政府定价。

**Article 29** The prices for the assignment of real estate development projects and the sale of commercial housing shall be negotiated between the parties; however, the prices of residential housing that enjoy state preferential policies shall be subject to government guidance pricing or government pricing.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　房地产开发企业应当在商品房交付使用时，向购买人提供住宅质量保证书和住宅使用说明书。  
住宅质量保证书应当列明工程质量监督单位核验的质量等级、保修范围、保修期和保修单位等内容。房地产开发企业应当按照住宅质量保证书的约定，承担商品房保修责任。  
保修期内，因房地产开发企业对商品房进行维修，致使房屋原使用功能受到影响，给购买人造成损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 30** A real estate development enterprise shall, at the time of delivery of commercial housing, provide the purchaser with a residential quality warranty and a residential use manual.  
The residential quality warranty shall specify the quality grade verified by the engineering quality supervision unit, the scope of warranty, the warranty period, and the warranty unit, among other contents. A real estate development enterprise shall bear the warranty responsibility for commercial housing in accordance with the agreements in the residential quality warranty.  
Within the warranty period, if the repair of commercial housing by the real estate development enterprise affects the original functions of the housing and causes losses to the purchaser, the enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　商品房交付使用后，购买人认为主体结构质量不合格的，可以向工程质量监督单位申请重新核验。经核验，确属主体结构质量不合格的，购买人有权退房；给购买人造成损失的，房地产开发企业应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 31** After the delivery of commercial housing, if the purchaser believes that the quality of the main structure is not up to standard, the purchaser may apply to the engineering quality supervision unit for re-inspection. If after verification it is confirmed that the quality of the main structure is not up to standard, the purchaser shall have the right to return the housing; if losses are caused to the purchaser, the real estate development enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　预售商品房的购买人应当自商品房交付使用之日起90日内，办理土地使用权变更和房屋所有权登记手续；现售商品房的购买人应当自销售合同签订之日起90日内，办理土地使用权变更和房屋所有权登记手续。房地产开发企业应当协助商品房购买人办理土地使用权变更和房屋所有权登记手续，并提供必要的证明文件。

**Article 32** The purchaser of pre-sold commercial housing shall, within 90 days from the date of delivery of the commercial housing, complete the procedures for modification registration of land use right and registration of housing ownership; the purchaser of commercial housing sold on the spot shall, within 90 days from the date of signing the sales contract, complete the procedures for modification registration of land use right and registration of housing ownership. The real estate development enterprise shall assist the purchaser of commercial housing in completing the procedures for modification registration of land use right and registration of housing ownership, and provide necessary certification documents.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，未取得营业执照，擅自从事房地产开发经营的，由县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令停止房地产开发经营活动，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../民事与商事/合同与债权/最高人民法院关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engaging in real estate development and management without obtaining a business license, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the real estate development and management activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，未取得资质等级证书或者超越资质等级从事房地产开发经营的，由县级以上人民政府房地产开发主管部门责令限期改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 34** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engaging in real estate development and management without obtaining a qualification grade certificate or exceeding the qualification grade, the competent real estate development administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a specified period and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if correction is not made within the specified period, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，擅自转让房地产开发项目的，由县级以上人民政府负责土地管理工作的部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得5倍以下的罚款。

**Article 35** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, assigning a real estate development project without authorization, the department in charge of land administration of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，擅自预售商品房的，由县级以上人民政府房地产开发主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以并处已收取的预付款1%以下的罚款。

**Article 36** In violation of the provisions of these Regulations, pre-selling commercial housing without authorization, the competent real estate development administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than 1% of the prepayments already collected.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家机关工作人员在房地产开发经营监督管理工作中玩忽职守、徇私舞弊、滥用职权，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 37** Where a functionary of a state organ neglects duty, engages in malpractices for personal gain, or abuses power in the supervision and administration of real estate development and management, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在城市规划区外国有土地上从事房地产开发经营，实施房地产开发经营监督管理，参照本条例执行。

**Article 38** The engagement in real estate development and management on state-owned land outside urban planning areas and the implementation of supervision and administration of real estate development and management shall be carried out by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　城市规划区内集体所有的土地，经依法征收转为国有土地后，方可用于房地产开发经营。

**Article 39** Collectively-owned land within urban planning areas, after being lawfully expropriated and converted into state-owned land, may be used for real estate development and management.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
