# 土地调查条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-03-19  
**生效日期**：2018-03-19

> （2008年2月7日中华人民共和国国务院令第518号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施土地调查，保障土地调查数据的真实性、准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。 第二条　土地调查的目的，是全面查清土地资源和利用状况，掌握真实准确的土地基础数据，为科学规划、合理利用、有效保护土地资源，实施最严格的耕地保护制度，加强和改善宏观调控提供依据，促进经济社会全面协调可持续发展。 第三条　土地调查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。 第四条　土地调查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。 土地调查经费应当统一管理、专款专用、从严控制支出。 第五条　报刊、广播、电视和互联网等新闻媒体，应当及时开展土地调查工作的宣传报道。 第二章　土地调查的内容和方法 第六条　国家根据国民经济和社会发展需要，每10年进行一次全国土地调查；根据土地管理工作的需要，每年进行土地变更调查。 第七条　土地调查包括下列内容： （一）土地利用现状及变化情况，包括地类、位置、面积、分布等状况； （二）土地权属及变化情况，包括土地的所有权和使用权状况； （三）土地条件，包括土地的自然条件、社会经济条件等状况。 进行土地利用现状及变化情况调查时，应当重点调查基本农田现状及变化情况，包括基本农田的数量、分布和保护状况。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了科学、有效地组织实施土地调查，保障土地调查数据的真实性、准确性和及时性，根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国统计法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purpose of organizing and implementing land surveys scientifically and effectively, ensuring the authenticity, accuracy and timeliness of land survey data, in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and the Statistics Law of the People's Republic of China, these Regulations are formulated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　土地调查的目的，是全面查清土地资源和利用状况，掌握真实准确的土地基础数据，为科学规划、合理利用、有效保护土地资源，实施最严格的耕地保护制度，加强和改善宏观调控提供依据，促进经济社会全面协调可持续发展。

**Article 2** The purpose of land surveys is to comprehensively ascertain the status of land resources and their use, to obtain true and accurate basic land data, to provide a basis for scientific planning, rational utilization, effective protection of land resources, implementation of the strictest farmland protection system, strengthening and improving macro-control, and to promote comprehensive, coordinated and sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　土地调查工作按照全国统一领导、部门分工协作、地方分级负责、各方共同参与的原则组织实施。

**Article 3** Land survey work shall be organized and implemented in accordance with the principles of unified national leadership, division of labor and cooperation among departments, hierarchical responsibility at local levels, and joint participation by all parties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　土地调查所需经费，由中央和地方各级人民政府共同负担，列入相应年度的财政预算，按时拨付，确保足额到位。  
土地调查经费应当统一管理、专款专用、从严控制支出。

**Article 4** The funds required for land surveys shall be borne jointly by the Central Government and the local people's governments at all levels, shall be included in the fiscal budgets for the corresponding years, and shall be allocated in a timely manner to ensure full sufficiency and availability.  
Land survey funds shall be managed uniformly, used exclusively for designated purposes, and expenditures shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　报刊、广播、电视和互联网等新闻媒体，应当及时开展土地调查工作的宣传报道。

**Article 5** News media such as newspapers, radio, television and the Internet shall timely carry out publicity and reporting on land survey work.

### 第二章　土地调查的内容和方法

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家根据国民经济和社会发展需要，每10年进行一次全国土地调查；根据土地管理工作的需要，每年进行土地变更调查。

**Article 6** The State shall conduct a national land survey every 10 years based on the needs of national economic and social development; a land change survey shall be conducted every year based on the needs of land administration work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　土地调查包括下列内容：  
（一）土地利用现状及变化情况，包括地类、位置、面积、分布等状况；  
（二）土地权属及变化情况，包括土地的所有权和使用权状况；  
（三）土地条件，包括土地的自然条件、社会经济条件等状况。  
进行土地利用现状及变化情况调查时，应当重点调查基本农田现状及变化情况，包括基本农田的数量、分布和保护状况。

**Article 7** Land surveys shall include the following content:  
(1) Current status and changes of land use, including conditions such as land type, location, area, distribution, etc.;  
(2) Current status and changes of land ownership and rights, including the status of ownership and use rights of land;  
(3) Land conditions, including conditions such as natural conditions and socio-economic conditions of land.  
When conducting surveys on current status and changes of land use, the current status and changes of basic farmland shall be surveyed with emphasis, including the quantity, distribution and protection status of basic farmland.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　土地调查采用全面调查的方法，综合运用实地调查统计、遥感监测等手段。

**Article 8** Land surveys shall adopt the method of comprehensive survey, comprehensively utilizing means such as field survey statistics and remote sensing monitoring.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　土地调查采用《土地利用现状分类》国家标准、统一的技术规程和按照国家统一标准制作的调查基础图件。  
土地调查技术规程，由国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 9** Land surveys shall adopt the national standard "Classification of Current Land Use", uniform technical specifications, and basic survey maps produced in accordance with the unified national standards.  
The technical specifications for land surveys shall be formulated by the competent department of land and resources under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

### 第三章　土地调查的组织实施

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级有关部门进行土地调查。  
乡（镇）人民政府、街道办事处和村（居）民委员会应当广泛动员和组织社会力量积极参与土地调查工作。

**Article 10** The competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall conduct land surveys jointly with the relevant departments at the same level.  
The people's governments of townships (towns), sub-district offices and villagers (residents) committees shall extensively mobilize and organize social forces to actively participate in land survey work.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当积极参与和密切配合土地调查工作，依法提供土地调查需要的相关资料。  
社会团体以及与土地调查有关的单位和个人应当依照本条例的规定，配合土地调查工作。

**Article 11** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall actively participate in and closely cooperate with the land survey work, and provide relevant information required for the land survey according to law.  
Social organizations, and units and individuals involved in the land survey shall, in accordance with these Regulations, cooperate with the land survey work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全国土地调查总体方案由国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门拟订，报国务院批准。县级以上地方人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级有关部门按照国家统一要求，根据本行政区域的土地利用特点，编制地方土地调查实施方案，报上一级人民政府国土资源主管部门备案。

**Article 12** The overall plan for the national land survey shall be formulated by the competent department of land and resources of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval. The competent department of land and resources of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level, prepare a local land survey implementation plan in accordance with the national unified requirements and based on the characteristics of land use in its administrative region, and shall submit it to the competent department of land and resources of the people's government at the next higher level for filing.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在土地调查中，需要面向社会选择专业调查队伍承担的土地调查任务，应当通过招标投标方式组织实施。  
承担土地调查任务的单位应当具备以下条件：  
（一）具有法人资格；  
（二）有与土地调查相关的工作业绩；  
（三）有完备的技术和质量管理制度；  
（四）有经过培训且考核合格的专业技术人员。  
国务院国土资源主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门加强对承担土地调查任务单位的监管和服务。

**Article 13** In the land survey, where it is necessary to select professional survey teams from the public to undertake land survey tasks, such tasks shall be organized and implemented by means of bidding.  
The units undertaking land survey tasks shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having the status of a legal person;  
(2) Having work performance related to land survey;  
(3) Having complete technical and quality management systems;  
(4) Having professional technical personnel who have been trained and passed the assessment.  
The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, strengthen supervision and service over the units undertaking land survey tasks.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　土地调查人员应当坚持实事求是，恪守职业道德，具有执行调查任务所需要的专业知识。  
土地调查人员应当接受业务培训，经考核合格领取全国统一的土地调查员工作证。

**Article 14** Land survey personnel shall adhere to the principle of seeking truth from facts, abide by professional ethics, and possess the professional knowledge necessary for performing survey tasks.  
Land survey personnel shall receive professional training, and shall obtain the nationally unified land surveyor work certificate after passing the assessment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　土地调查人员应当严格执行全国土地调查总体方案和地方土地调查实施方案、《土地利用现状分类》国家标准和统一的技术规程，不得伪造、篡改调查资料，不得强令、授意调查对象提供虚假的调查资料。  
土地调查人员应当对其登记、审核、录入的调查资料与现场调查资料的一致性负责。

**Article 15** Land survey personnel shall strictly implement the overall plan for the national land survey and local land survey implementation plans, the national standard of 'Classification of Land Use Status' and the unified technical specifications, and shall not forge or tamper with survey data, and shall not compel or induce survey subjects to provide false survey data.  
Land survey personnel shall be responsible for the consistency of the survey data they register, review, and enter with the field survey data.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　土地调查人员依法独立行使调查、报告、监督和检查职权，有权根据工作需要进行现场调查，并按照技术规程进行现场作业。  
土地调查人员有权就与调查有关的问题询问有关单位和个人，要求有关单位和个人如实提供相关资料。  
土地调查人员进行现场调查、现场作业以及询问有关单位和个人时，应当出示土地调查员工作证。

**Article 16** Land survey personnel shall independently exercise the functions and powers of survey, reporting, supervision and inspection according to law, and have the right to conduct field surveys as needed for the work, and to perform field operations in accordance with the technical specifications.  
Land survey personnel have the right to inquire of relevant units and individuals about issues related to the survey, and request that such units and individuals provide relevant information truthfully.  
When conducting field surveys, field operations, and inquiries of relevant units and individuals, land survey personnel shall present their land surveyor work certificates.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　接受调查的有关单位和个人应当如实回答询问，履行现场指界义务，按照要求提供相关资料，不得转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录和土地登记簿等相关资料。

**Article 17** The relevant units and individuals under survey shall truthfully answer inquiries, perform the obligation of on-site boundary designation, provide relevant information as required, and shall not transfer, conceal, tamper with, or destroy original records, land registers, or other relevant materials.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各地方、各部门、各单位的负责人不得擅自修改土地调查资料、数据，不得强令或者授意土地调查人员篡改调查资料、数据或者编造虚假数据，不得对拒绝、抵制篡改调查资料、数据或者编造虚假数据的土地调查人员打击报复。

**Article 18** The responsible persons of all localities, departments and units shall not modify survey data and information without authorization, shall not compel or induce land survey personnel to tamper with survey data and information or fabricate false data, and shall not retaliate against land survey personnel who refuse or resist the tampering of survey data and information or the fabrication of false data.

### 第四章　调查成果处理和质量控制

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　土地调查形成下列调查成果：  
（一）数据成果；  
（二）图件成果；  
（三）文字成果；  
（四）数据库成果。

**Article 19** The land survey shall produce the following survey results:  
(1) Data results;  
(2) Map results;  
(3) Text results;  
(4) Database results.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　土地调查成果实行逐级汇交、汇总统计制度。  
土地调查数据的处理和上报应当按照全国土地调查总体方案和有关标准进行。

**Article 20** The survey results shall be subject to a system of hierarchical submission and summary statistics.  
The processing and reporting of land survey data shall be carried out in accordance with the overall plan for the national land survey and relevant standards.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域的土地调查成果质量负总责，主要负责人是第一责任人。  
县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级有关部门对调查的各个环节实行质量控制，建立土地调查成果质量控制岗位责任制，切实保证调查的数据、图件和被调查土地实际状况三者一致，并对其加工、整理、汇总的调查成果的准确性负责。

**Article 21** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall bear overall responsibility for the quality of land survey results within their respective administrative regions, and the principal responsible person shall be the primary responsible person.  
The competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level, together with the relevant departments at the same level, shall implement quality control over each link of the survey, establish a post responsibility system for quality control of land survey results, and effectively ensure that the survey data, maps, and the actual conditions of the surveyed land are consistent, and shall be responsible for the accuracy of the processed, sorted and summarized survey results.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门统一组织土地调查成果质量的抽查工作。抽查结果作为评价土地调查成果质量的重要依据。

**Article 22** The competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, uniformly organize random inspection of the quality of land survey results. The results of the random inspection shall serve as an important basis for evaluating the quality of land survey results.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　土地调查成果实行分阶段、分级检查验收制度。前一阶段土地调查成果经检查验收合格后，方可开展下一阶段的调查工作。  
土地调查成果检查验收办法，由国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 23** Land survey results shall be subject to a system of phased and graded inspection and acceptance. Only after the land survey results of the previous phase have passed inspection and acceptance may the survey work of the next phase be carried out.  
The measures for inspection and acceptance of land survey results shall be formulated by the competent department of land and resources of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

### 第五章　调查成果公布和应用

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家建立土地调查成果公布制度。  
土地调查成果应当向社会公布，并接受公开查询，但依法应当保密的除外。

**Article 24** The State shall establish a system for publication of land survey results.  
Land survey results shall be published to the public and subject to public inquiry, except those that shall be kept confidential according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　全国土地调查成果，报国务院批准后公布。  
地方土地调查成果，经本级人民政府审核，报上一级人民政府批准后公布。  
全国土地调查成果公布后，县级以上地方人民政府方可逐级依次公布本行政区域的土地调查成果。

**Article 25** National land survey results shall be published after being submitted to the State Council for approval.  
Local land survey results shall be published after being reviewed by the people's government at the same level and submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval.  
After the national land survey results are published, local people's governments at or above the county level may publish the land survey results of their respective administrative regions level by level in order.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门会同同级有关部门做好土地调查成果的保存、管理、开发、应用和为社会公众提供服务等工作。  
国家通过土地调查，建立互联共享的土地调查数据库，并做好维护、更新工作。

**Article 26** The competent department of land and resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, properly carry out the preservation, management, development, application and provision of services to the public of land survey results.  
The State shall, through land survey, establish an interconnected and shared land survey database, and properly carry out maintenance and update work.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　土地调查成果是编制国民经济和社会发展规划以及从事国土资源规划、管理、保护和利用的重要依据。

**Article 27** Land survey results shall be an important basis for formulating national economic and social development plans and for engaging in the planning, management, protection and utilization of land and resources.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　土地调查成果应当严格管理和规范使用，不作为依照其他法律、行政法规对调查对象实施行政处罚的依据，不作为划分部门职责分工和管理范围的依据。

**Article 28** Land survey results shall be strictly managed and used in a standardized manner, and shall not serve as a basis for imposing administrative penalties on survey subjects in accordance with other laws and administrative regulations, nor as a basis for dividing departmental responsibilities and management scope.

### 第六章　表彰和处罚

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对在土地调查工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，应当按照国家有关规定给予表彰或者奖励。

**Article 29** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in land survey work shall be commended or rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　地方、部门、单位的负责人有下列行为之一的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）擅自修改调查资料、数据的；  
（二）强令、授意土地调查人员篡改调查资料、数据或者编造虚假数据的；  
（三）对拒绝、抵制篡改调查资料、数据或者编造虚假数据的土地调查人员打击报复的。

**Article 30** Where the person in charge of a local government, department or unit commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given a sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Unauthorized alteration of survey materials or data;  
(2) Ordering or instructing land survey personnel to tamper with survey materials or data or fabricate false data;  
(3) Retaliating against land survey personnel who refuse or resist tampering with survey materials or data or fabricating false data.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　土地调查人员不执行全国土地调查总体方案和地方土地调查实施方案、《土地利用现状分类》国家标准和统一的技术规程，或者伪造、篡改调查资料，或者强令、授意接受调查的有关单位和个人提供虚假调查资料的，依法给予处分，并由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门、统计机构予以通报批评。

**Article 31** Where land surveyors fail to implement the national overall plan for land survey and local land survey implementation plans, the national standard of "Classification of Land Use Status" and the unified technical procedures, or forge or tamper with survey data, or force or instigate the relevant units and individuals subject to survey to provide false survey data, they shall be given sanctions according to law, and shall be given a notice of criticism by the competent departments of land and resources and the statistical institutions of the people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　接受调查的单位和个人有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门责令限期改正，可以处5万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）拒绝或者阻挠土地调查人员依法进行调查的；  
（二）提供虚假调查资料的；  
（三）拒绝提供调查资料的；  
（四）转移、隐匿、篡改、毁弃原始记录、土地登记簿等相关资料的。

**Article 32** Where the units and individuals subject to survey commit any of the following acts, the competent departments of land and resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall order them to make corrections within a time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing or obstructing land surveyors from conducting surveys according to law;  
(2) Providing false survey data;  
(3) Refusing to provide survey data;  
(4) Transferring, concealing, tampering with, or destroying original records, land registers, or other relevant data.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府有下列行为之一的，由上级人民政府予以通报批评；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未按期完成土地调查工作，被责令限期完成，逾期仍未完成的；  
（二）提供的土地调查数据失真，被责令限期改正，逾期仍未改正的。

**Article 33** Where a local people's government at or above the county level commits any of the following acts, the people's government at a higher level shall give it a notice of criticism; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to complete the land survey work within the prescribed time limit, being ordered to complete it within a time limit, but still failing to do so upon expiration of the time limit;  
(2) Providing distorted land survey data, being ordered to make corrections within a time limit, but still failing to do so upon expiration of the time limit.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　军用土地调查，由国务院国土资源主管部门会同军队有关部门按照国家统一规定和要求制定具体办法。  
中央单位使用土地的调查数据汇总内容的确定和成果的应用管理，由国务院国土资源主管部门会同国务院管理机关事务工作的机构负责。

**Article 34** For military land survey, the competent department of land and resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the military, formulate specific measures in accordance with the unified provisions and requirements of the state.  
The determination of the content of summary survey data for land used by central units and the application and management of the results shall be the responsibility of the competent department of land and resources of the State Council, together with the administrative office of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府可以按照全国土地调查总体方案和地方土地调查实施方案成立土地调查领导小组，组织和领导土地调查工作。必要时，可以设立土地调查领导小组办公室负责土地调查日常工作。

**Article 35** The people's governments at or above the county level may, in accordance with the national overall plan for land survey and the local land survey implementation plans, establish a land survey leading group to organize and lead the land survey work. When necessary, they may set up an office of the land survey leading group to be responsible for the daily work of land survey.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 36** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
