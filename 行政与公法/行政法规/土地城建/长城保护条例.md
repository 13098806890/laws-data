# 长城保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2006-10-01  
**生效日期**：2006-12-01

> (2006年9月20日国务院第150次常务会议通过　2006年10月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第476号公布　自2006年12月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对长城的保护，规范长城的利用行为，根据《中华人民共和国文物保护法》(以下简称文物保护法)，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称长城，包括长城的墙体、城堡、关隘、烽火台、敌楼等。 受本条例保护的长城段落，由国务院文物主管部门认定并公布。 第三条　长城保护应当贯彻文物工作方针，坚持科学规划、原状保护的原则。 第四条　国家对长城实行整体保护、分段管理。 国务院文物主管部门负责长城整体保护工作，协调、解决长城保护中的重大问题，监督、检查长城所在地各地方的长城保护工作。 长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其文物主管部门依照文物保护法、本条例和其他有关行政法规的规定，负责本行政区域内的长城保护工作。 第五条　长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当将长城保护经费纳入本级财政预算。 国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织通过捐赠等方式设立长城保护基金，专门用于长城保护。长城保护基金的募集、使用和管理，依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。 第六条　国家对长城保护实行专家咨询制度。制定长城保护总体规划、审批与长城有关的建设工程、决定与长城保护有关的其他重大事项，应当听取专家意见。 第七条　公民、法人和其他组织都有依法保护长城的义务。 国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织参与长城保护。 第八条　国务院文物主管部门、长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其文物主管部门应当对在长城保护中作出突出贡献的组织或者个人给予奖励。 第九条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当对本行政区域内的长城进行调查；对认为属于长城的段落，应当报国务院文物主管部门认定，并自认定之日起1年内依法核定公布为省级文物保护单位。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对长城的保护，规范长城的利用行为，根据《中华人民共和国文物保护法》(以下简称文物保护法)，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protection of Cultural Relics (hereinafter referred to as the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics) for the purposes of strengthening the protection of the Great Wall and regulating the utilization of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称长城，包括长城的墙体、城堡、关隘、烽火台、敌楼等。  
受本条例保护的长城段落，由国务院文物主管部门认定并公布。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, the Great Wall includes the wall, castles, passes, beacon towers, and watchtowers of the Great Wall, among others.  
The sections of the Great Wall protected under these Regulations shall be ascertained and announced by the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　长城保护应当贯彻文物工作方针，坚持科学规划、原状保护的原则。

**Article 3** The protection of the Great Wall shall implement the policy for cultural relics work and adhere to the principles of scientific planning and protection of the original state.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对长城实行整体保护、分段管理。  
国务院文物主管部门负责长城整体保护工作，协调、解决长城保护中的重大问题，监督、检查长城所在地各地方的长城保护工作。  
长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其文物主管部门依照文物保护法、本条例和其他有关行政法规的规定，负责本行政区域内的长城保护工作。

**Article 4** The State shall implement overall protection and segmented management of the Great Wall.  
The competent department of cultural relics under the State Council shall be responsible for the overall protection of the Great Wall, coordinate and solve major issues in the protection of the Great Wall, and supervise and inspect the protection of the Great Wall by various localities where the Great Wall is located.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level where the Great Wall is located and their competent departments of cultural relics shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics, these Regulations and other relevant administrative regulations, be responsible for the protection of the Great Wall within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当将长城保护经费纳入本级财政预算。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织通过捐赠等方式设立长城保护基金，专门用于长城保护。长城保护基金的募集、使用和管理，依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 5** The local people's governments at or above the county level where the Great Wall is located shall incorporate the funds for the protection of the Great Wall into their respective fiscal budgets.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to establish a fund for the protection of the Great Wall through donations and other means, which shall be used exclusively for the protection of the Great Wall. The raising, use and management of the fund for the protection of the Great Wall shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家对长城保护实行专家咨询制度。制定长城保护总体规划、审批与长城有关的建设工程、决定与长城保护有关的其他重大事项，应当听取专家意见。

**Article 6** The State shall implement an expert consultation system for the protection of the Great Wall. When formulating the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall, examining and approving construction projects related to the Great Wall, and deciding other major matters related to the protection of the Great Wall, the opinions of experts shall be heard.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公民、法人和其他组织都有依法保护长城的义务。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织参与长城保护。

**Article 7** All citizens, legal persons and other organizations shall have the obligation to protect the Great Wall according to law.  
The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to participate in the protection of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院文物主管部门、长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府及其文物主管部门应当对在长城保护中作出突出贡献的组织或者个人给予奖励。

**Article 8** The competent department of cultural relics under the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level where the Great Wall is located and their competent departments of cultural relics shall give rewards to organizations or individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当对本行政区域内的长城进行调查；对认为属于长城的段落，应当报国务院文物主管部门认定，并自认定之日起1年内依法核定公布为省级文物保护单位。  
本条例施行前已经认定为长城但尚未核定公布为全国重点文物保护单位或者省级文物保护单位的段落，应当自本条例施行之日起1年内依法核定公布为全国重点文物保护单位或者省级文物保护单位。

**Article 9** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall conduct surveys of the Great Wall within their respective administrative regions; for sections deemed to belong to the Great Wall, they shall report them to the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council for ascertainment, and shall, within one year from the date of ascertainment, verify and announce them according to law as cultural relics protection units at the provincial level.  
Sections that have been ascertained as the Great Wall but have not yet been verified and announced as major historical and cultural sites protected at the national level or cultural relics protection units at the provincial level before these Regulations come into force shall, within one year from the date these Regulations come into force, be verified and announced according to law as major historical and cultural sites protected at the national level or cultural relics protection units at the provincial level.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家实行长城保护总体规划制度。  
国务院文物主管部门会同国务院有关部门，根据文物保护法的规定和长城保护的实际需要，制定长城保护总体规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。长城保护总体规划应当明确长城的保护标准和保护重点，分类确定保护措施，并确定禁止在保护范围内进行工程建设的长城段落。  
长城所在地县级以上地方人民政府制定本行政区域的国民经济和社会发展计划、土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，应当落实长城保护总体规划规定的保护措施。

**Article 10** The State shall implement a system of overall planning for the protection of the Great Wall.  
The competent department of cultural relics under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate an overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics and the actual needs of the protection of the Great Wall, and shall organize its implementation after submitting it to the State Council for approval. The overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall shall specify the protection standards and protection priorities of the Great Wall, classify and determine protection measures, and determine the sections of the Great Wall where construction projects are prohibited within the protection zones.  
When formulating the national economic and social development plans, overall plans for land use, and urban and rural plans for their respective administrative regions, the local people's governments at or above the county level where the Great Wall is located shall implement the protection measures specified in the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当按照长城保护总体规划的要求，划定本行政区域内长城的保护范围和建设控制地带，并予以公布。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物主管部门应当将公布的保护范围和建设控制地带报国务院文物主管部门备案。

**Article 11** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall, in accordance with the requirements of the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall, delimit the protection zones and construction control zones for the Great Wall within their respective administrative regions, and shall make them public.  
The competent departments of cultural relics of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall submit the published protection zones and construction control zones to the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　任何单位或者个人不得在长城保护总体规划禁止工程建设的保护范围内进行工程建设。在建设控制地带或者长城保护总体规划未禁止工程建设的保护范围内进行工程建设，应当遵守[文物保护法第十七条](../../../行政与公法/社会民政/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-17)、第十八条的规定。  
进行工程建设应当绕过长城。无法绕过的，应当采取挖掘地下通道的方式通过长城；无法挖掘地下通道的，应当采取架设桥梁的方式通过长城。任何单位或者个人进行工程建设，不得拆除、穿越、迁移长城。

**Article 12** No unit or individual may carry out construction projects within the protection zones where construction is prohibited under the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall. Where construction projects are carried out within construction control zones or within protection zones where construction is not prohibited under the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall, the provisions of Articles 17 and 18 of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics shall be complied with.  
Construction projects shall bypass the Great Wall. Where bypassing is impossible, the method of excavating an underground passage shall be adopted to pass through the Great Wall; where excavation of an underground passage is impossible, the method of erecting a bridge shall be adopted to pass over the Great Wall. No unit or individual may, in carrying out construction projects, dismantle, cross, or relocate the Great Wall.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当在长城沿线的交通路口和其他需要提示公众的地段设立长城保护标志。设立长城保护标志不得对长城造成损坏。  
长城保护标志应当载明长城段落的名称、修筑年代、保护范围、建设控制地带和保护机构。

**Article 13** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall set up protection signs for the Great Wall at traffic intersections along the Great Wall and at other locations where the public needs to be informed. The setting up of protection signs shall not cause damage to the Great Wall.  
The protection signs for the Great Wall shall indicate the name of the section of the Great Wall, the date of construction, the protection zone, the construction control zone, and the protection institution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当建立本行政区域内的长城档案，其文物主管部门应当将长城档案报国务院文物主管部门备案。  
国务院文物主管部门应当建立全国的长城档案。

**Article 14** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall establish archives of the Great Wall within their respective administrative regions, and their competent departments of cultural relics shall submit the archives of the Great Wall to the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council for recordation.  
The competent department of cultural relics under the State Council shall establish national archives of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当为本行政区域内的长城段落确定保护机构；长城段落有利用单位的，该利用单位可以确定为保护机构。  
保护机构应当对其所负责保护的长城段落进行日常维护和监测，并建立日志；发现安全隐患，应当立即采取控制措施，并及时向县级人民政府文物主管部门报告。

**Article 15** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall designate protection institutions for the sections of the Great Wall within their respective administrative regions; where a section of the Great Wall has a utilization unit, such utilization unit may be designated as the protection institution.  
The protection institution shall carry out daily maintenance and monitoring of the section of the Great Wall under its responsibility, and shall keep logs; where it discovers a potential safety hazard, it shall immediately take control measures and promptly report to the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地处偏远、没有利用单位的长城段落，所在地县级人民政府或者其文物主管部门可以聘请长城保护员对长城进行巡查、看护，并对长城保护员给予适当补助。

**Article 16** For sections of the Great Wall located in remote areas with no utilization unit, the local people's government at the county level or its competent department of cultural relics may engage Great Wall protection workers to patrol and guard the Great Wall, and shall provide appropriate subsidies to such Great Wall protection workers.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　长城段落为行政区域边界的，其毗邻的县级以上地方人民政府应当定期召开由相关部门参加的联席会议，研究解决长城保护中的重大问题。

**Article 17** Where a section of the Great Wall serves as an administrative boundary, the adjoining local people's governments at or above the county level shall periodically convene joint meetings attended by relevant departments to study and resolve major issues in the protection of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　禁止在长城上从事下列活动：  
(一)取土、取砖(石)或者种植作物；  
(二)刻划、涂污；  
(三)架设、安装与长城保护无关的设施、设备；  
(四)驾驶交通工具，或者利用交通工具等跨越长城；  
(五)展示可能损坏长城的器具；  
(六)有组织地在未辟为参观游览区的长城段落举行活动；  
(七)文物保护法禁止的其他活动。

**Article 18** The following activities are prohibited on the Great Wall:  
(1) extracting earth, bricks (stones) or planting crops;  
(2) carving or defacing;  
(3) erecting or installing facilities or equipment unrelated to the protection of the Great Wall;  
(4) driving vehicles, or using vehicles to cross the Great Wall;  
(5) displaying implements that may damage the Great Wall;  
(6) holding organized activities on sections of the Great Wall that have not been opened as tourist and sightseeing areas; and  
(7) other activities prohibited by the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　将长城段落辟为参观游览区，应当坚持科学规划、原状保护的原则，并应当具备下列条件：  
(一)该长城段落的安全状况适宜公众参观游览；  
(二)该长城段落有明确的保护机构，已依法划定保护范围、建设控制地带，并已建立保护标志、档案；  
(三)符合长城保护总体规划的要求。

**Article 19** To open a section of the Great Wall as a tourist and sightseeing area, the principles of scientific planning and protection of the original state shall be adhered to, and the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) the safety condition of such section of the Great Wall is suitable for public visit and sightseeing;  
(2) such section of the Great Wall has a clear protection institution, and its protection zone and construction control zone have been delimited according to law, and protection signs and archives have been established; and  
(3) the requirements of the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall are met.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　将长城段落辟为参观游览区，应当自辟为参观游览区之日起5日内向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物主管部门备案；长城段落属于全国重点文物保护单位的，应当自辟为参观游览区之日起5日内向国务院文物主管部门备案。备案材料应当包括参观游览区的旅游容量指标。  
所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物主管部门和国务院文物主管部门，应当自收到备案材料之日起20日内按照职权划分核定参观游览区的旅游容量指标。

**Article 20** Where a section of the Great Wall is opened as a tourist and sightseeing area, the matter shall be reported to the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation within five days from the date of opening; where the section of the Great Wall is a major historical and cultural site protected at the national level, the matter shall be reported to the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council for recordation within five days from the date of opening. The materials submitted for recordation shall include the tourist capacity indicator of the tourist and sightseeing area.  
The competent department of cultural relics of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government and the competent department of cultural relics under the State Council shall, within 20 days from the date of receiving the materials submitted for recordation, verify the tourist capacity indicator of the tourist and sightseeing area according to their respective functions and powers.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在参观游览区内举行活动，其人数不得超过核定的旅游容量指标。  
在参观游览区内设置服务项目，应当符合长城保护总体规划的要求。

**Article 21** Where activities are held within a tourist and sightseeing area, the number of participants shall not exceed the verified tourist capacity indicator.  
Service projects set up within a tourist and sightseeing area shall meet the requirements of the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位或者个人发现长城遭受损坏向保护机构或者所在地县级人民政府文物主管部门报告的，接到报告的保护机构或者县级人民政府文物主管部门应当立即采取控制措施，并向县级人民政府和上一级人民政府文物主管部门报告。

**Article 22** Where any unit or individual discovers that the Great Wall has suffered damage and reports it to the protection institution or the competent department of cultural relics of the local people's government at the county level, the protection institution or the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level that receives the report shall immediately take control measures and report the matter to the people's government at the county level and the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对长城进行修缮，应当依照文物保护法的规定办理审批手续，由依法取得文物保护工程资质证书的单位承担。长城的修缮，应当遵守不改变原状的原则。  
长城段落已经损毁的，应当实施遗址保护，不得在原址重建。  
长城段落因人为原因造成损坏的，其修缮费用由造成损坏的单位或者个人承担。

**Article 23** Where the Great Wall is to be repaired, the approval formalities shall be gone through in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics, and the repair work shall be undertaken by a unit that has obtained a qualification certificate for cultural relics protection projects according to law. The repair of the Great Wall shall comply with the principle of not altering the original state.  
Where a section of the Great Wall has been damaged, site protection shall be implemented, and reconstruction on the original site is prohibited.  
Where a section of the Great Wall has been damaged due to human causes, the repair costs shall be borne by the unit or individual that caused the damage.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，造成长城损毁，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪，违反有关治安管理的法律规定的，由公安机关依法给予治安处罚。

**Article 24** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations causes damage to the Great Wall and constitutes a criminal offense, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; where the conduct does not constitute a criminal offense but violates the provisions of the law on public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，依照[文物保护法第六十六条](../../../行政与公法/社会民政/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-66)的规定责令改正，造成严重后果的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销资质证书：  
(一)在禁止工程建设的长城段落的保护范围内进行工程建设的；  
(二)在长城的保护范围或者建设控制地带内进行工程建设，未依法报批的；  
(三)未采取本条例规定的方式进行工程建设，或者因工程建设拆除、穿越、迁移长城的。

**Article 25** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations falls under any of the following circumstances, the matter shall be ordered to make corrections in accordance with Article 66 of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are severe, the original issuing authority shall revoke the qualification certificate:  
(1) carrying out construction projects within the protection zones of sections of the Great Wall where construction is prohibited;  
(2) carrying out construction projects within the protection zone or construction control zone of the Great Wall without submitting for approval according to law; or  
(3) failing to carry out construction projects in the manner prescribed in these Regulations, or dismantling, crossing, or relocating the Great Wall due to construction projects.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　将不符合本条例规定条件的长城段落辟为参观游览区的，由省级以上人民政府文物主管部门按照职权划分依法取缔，没收违法所得；造成长城损坏的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款。  
将长城段落辟为参观游览区未按照本条例规定备案的，由省级以上人民政府文物主管部门按照职权划分责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，依照前款规定处罚。  
在参观游览区内设置的服务项目不符合长城保护总体规划要求的，由县级人民政府文物主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得。

**Article 26** Where a section of the Great Wall that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations is opened as a tourist and sightseeing area, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, according to the division of functions and powers, ban it according to law and confiscate the illegal gains; if damage is caused to the Great Wall, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a section of the Great Wall is opened as a tourist and sightseeing area without being filed for recordation in accordance with these Regulations, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, according to the division of functions and powers, order corrections within a time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.  
Where service projects set up within a tourist and sightseeing area do not meet the requirements of the overall plan for the protection of the Great Wall, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level shall order corrections and confiscate the illegal gains.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级人民政府文物主管部门责令改正，造成严重后果的，对个人处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对单位处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在长城上架设、安装与长城保护无关的设施、设备的；  
(二)在长城上驾驶交通工具，或者利用交通工具等跨越长城的；  
(三)在长城上展示可能损坏长城的器具的；  
(四)在参观游览区接待游客超过旅游容量指标的。

**Article 27** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level shall order corrections; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit:  
(1) erecting or installing facilities or equipment unrelated to the protection of the Great Wall on the Great Wall;  
(2) driving vehicles on the Great Wall, or using vehicles to cross the Great Wall;  
(3) displaying implements that may damage the Great Wall on the Great Wall; or  
(4) receiving tourists in a tourist and sightseeing area exceeding the tourist capacity indicator.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级人民政府文物主管部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对个人并处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款，对单位并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在长城上取土、取砖(石)或者种植作物的；  
(二)有组织地在未辟为参观游览区的长城段落举行活动的。

**Article 28** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government at the county level shall order corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are severe, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed on the individual, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed on the unit:  
(1) extracting earth, bricks (stones) or planting crops on the Great Wall; or  
(2) holding organized activities on sections of the Great Wall that have not been opened as tourist and sightseeing areas.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　行政机关有下列情形之一的，由上级行政机关责令改正，通报批评；对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照[文物保护法第七十六条](../../../行政与公法/社会民政/中华人民共和国文物保护法.md#art-76)的规定给予行政处分；情节严重的，依法开除公职：  
(一)未依照本条例的规定，确定保护机构、划定保护范围或者建设控制地带、设立保护标志或者建立档案的；  
(二)发现不符合条件的长城段落辟为参观游览区未依法查处的；  
(三)有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守行为，造成长城损坏的。

**Article 29** Where an administrative organ falls under any of the following circumstances, the superior administrative organ shall order corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given an administrative sanction in accordance with Article 76 of the Law on Protection of Cultural Relics; if the circumstances are severe, they shall be dismissed from public employment according to law:  
(1) failing to designate a protection institution, delimit a protection zone or construction control zone, set up protection signs, or establish archives in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with according to law the opening of a section of the Great Wall that does not meet the conditions as a tourist and sightseeing area; or  
(3) having other acts of abuse of power or dereliction of duty that cause damage to the Great Wall.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　保护机构有下列情形之一的，由长城所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文物主管部门责令改正，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；情节严重的，依法开除公职：  
(一)未对长城进行日常维护、监测或者未建立日志的；  
(二)发现长城存在安全隐患，未采取控制措施或者未及时报告的。

**Article 30** Where a protection institution falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of cultural relics of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the Great Wall is located shall order corrections, and the person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the circumstances are severe, they shall be dismissed from public employment according to law:  
(1) failing to carry out daily maintenance or monitoring of the Great Wall, or failing to keep logs; or  
(2) discovering a potential safety hazard on the Great Wall but failing to take control measures or failing to report it promptly.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例自2006年12月1日起施行。

**Article 31** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2006.
