# 地图管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2015-11-26  
**生效日期**：2016-01-01

> (2015年11月11日国务院第111次常务会议通过　2015年11月26日中华人民共和国国务院令第664号公布　自2016年1月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强地图管理，维护国家主权、安全和利益，促进地理信息产业健康发展，为经济建设、社会发展和人民生活服务，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事向社会公开的地图的编制、审核、出版和互联网地图服务以及监督检查活动，应当遵守本条例。 第三条　地图工作应当遵循维护国家主权、保障地理信息安全、方便群众生活的原则。 地图的编制、审核、出版和互联网地图服务应当遵守有关保密法律、法规的规定。 第四条　国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门负责全国地图工作的统一监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责有关的地图工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘地理信息工作的行政部门(以下称测绘地理信息行政主管部门)负责本行政区域地图工作的统一监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责有关的地图工作。 第五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门、新闻媒体应当加强国家版图宣传教育，增强公民的国家版图意识。 国家版图意识教育应当纳入中小学教学内容。 公民、法人和其他组织应当使用正确表示国家版图的地图。 第六条　国家鼓励编制和出版符合标准和规定的各类地图产品，支持地理信息科学技术创新和产业发展，加快地理信息产业结构调整和优化升级，促进地理信息深层次应用。 县级以上人民政府应当建立健全政府部门间地理信息资源共建共享机制。 县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当采取有效措施，及时获取、处理、更新基础地理信息数据，通过地理信息公共服务平台向社会提供地理信息公共服务，实现地理信息数据开放共享。 第二章　地图编制

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强地图管理，维护国家主权、安全和利益，促进地理信息产业健康发展，为经济建设、社会发展和人民生活服务，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purposes of strengthening map management, safeguarding national sovereignty, security and interests, promoting the healthy development of the geographic information industry, and serving economic construction, social development and people's livelihood, these Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事向社会公开的地图的编制、审核、出版和互联网地图服务以及监督检查活动，应当遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in the compilation, review, publication and Internet map services of maps open to the public, as well as supervision and inspection activities, within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　地图工作应当遵循维护国家主权、保障地理信息安全、方便群众生活的原则。  
地图的编制、审核、出版和互联网地图服务应当遵守有关保密法律、法规的规定。

**Article 3** Map work shall follow the principles of safeguarding national sovereignty, ensuring geographic information security, and facilitating the daily lives of the people.  
The compilation, review, publication and Internet map services of maps shall comply with the provisions of relevant secrecy laws and regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门负责全国地图工作的统一监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责有关的地图工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘地理信息工作的行政部门(以下称测绘地理信息行政主管部门)负责本行政区域地图工作的统一监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责有关的地图工作。

**Article 4** The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of map work nationwide. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall be responsible for relevant map work in accordance with the division of responsibilities stipulated by the State Council.  
The administrative department in charge of surveying, mapping and geographic information work of the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information) shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of map work within its administrative region. Other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant map work in accordance with the division of responsibilities stipulated by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门、新闻媒体应当加强国家版图宣传教育，增强公民的国家版图意识。  
国家版图意识教育应当纳入中小学教学内容。  
公民、法人和其他组织应当使用正确表示国家版图的地图。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels, relevant departments thereof, and news media shall strengthen publicity and education on the national territory map and enhance citizens' awareness of the national territory map.  
Education on the awareness of the national territory map shall be incorporated into the teaching content of primary and secondary schools.  
Citizens, legal persons and other organizations shall use maps that correctly represent the national territory map.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励编制和出版符合标准和规定的各类地图产品，支持地理信息科学技术创新和产业发展，加快地理信息产业结构调整和优化升级，促进地理信息深层次应用。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立健全政府部门间地理信息资源共建共享机制。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当采取有效措施，及时获取、处理、更新基础地理信息数据，通过地理信息公共服务平台向社会提供地理信息公共服务，实现地理信息数据开放共享。

**Article 6** The State encourages the compilation and publication of various map products that conform to standards and regulations, supports scientific and technological innovation and industrial development of geographic information, accelerates the structural adjustment and optimization and upgrading of the geographic information industry, and promotes the deep-level application of geographic information.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a mechanism for co-construction and sharing of geographic information resources among government departments.  
The administrative departments of surveying, mapping and geographic information of people's governments at or above the county level shall take effective measures to timely acquire, process and update basic geographic information data, and provide public services of geographic information to the public through the geographic information public service platform, so as to realize the open sharing of geographic information data.

### 第二章　地图编制

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事地图编制活动的单位应当依法取得相应的测绘资质证书，并在资质等级许可的范围内开展地图编制工作。

**Article 7** A unit engaged in map compilation activities shall, according to law, obtain the corresponding surveying and mapping qualification certificate and carry out map compilation work within the scope permitted by the qualification level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　编制地图，应当执行国家有关地图编制标准，遵守国家有关地图内容表示的规定。  
地图上不得表示下列内容：  
(一)危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
(二)危害国家安全、损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
(三)属于国家秘密的；  
(四)影响民族团结、侵害民族风俗习惯的；  
(五)法律、法规规定不得表示的其他内容。

**Article 8** When compiling maps, the national standards for map compilation shall be implemented, and the national provisions on the representation of map content shall be complied with.  
The following contents shall not be represented on maps:  
(1) those that jeopardize national unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity;  
(2) those that endanger national security, damage national honor and interests;  
(3) those that constitute State secrets;  
(4) those that affect ethnic unity or infringe upon ethnic customs and habits;  
(5) other contents that shall not be represented as prescribed by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　编制地图，应当选用最新的地图资料并及时补充或者更新，正确反映各要素的地理位置、形态、名称及相互关系，且内容符合地图使用目的。  
编制涉及中华人民共和国国界的世界地图、全国地图，应当完整表示中华人民共和国疆域。

**Article 9** When compiling maps, the latest map data shall be selected and supplemented or updated in a timely manner, correctly reflecting the geographical location, form, name and interrelationship of each element, and the content shall conform to the purpose of map use.  
When compiling world maps or national maps involving the national boundary of the People's Republic of China, the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be completely represented.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在地图上绘制中华人民共和国国界、中国历史疆界、世界各国间边界、世界各国间历史疆界，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)中华人民共和国国界，按照中国国界线画法标准样图绘制；  
(二)中国历史疆界，依据有关历史资料，按照实际历史疆界绘制；  
(三)世界各国间边界，按照世界各国国界线画法参考样图绘制；  
(四)世界各国间历史疆界，依据有关历史资料，按照实际历史疆界绘制。  
中国国界线画法标准样图、世界各国国界线画法参考样图，由外交部和国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门拟订，报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 10** When drawing the national boundary of the People's Republic of China, China's historical boundaries, boundaries among countries worldwide, and historical boundaries among countries worldwide on maps, the following provisions shall be complied with:  
(1) the national boundary of the People's Republic of China shall be drawn according to the standard sample map for drawing the national boundary of China;  
(2) China's historical boundaries shall be drawn based on relevant historical materials according to the actual historical boundaries;  
(3) boundaries among countries worldwide shall be drawn according to the reference sample map for drawing the national boundaries of countries worldwide;  
(4) historical boundaries among countries worldwide shall be drawn based on relevant historical materials according to the actual historical boundaries.  
The standard sample map for drawing the national boundary of China and the reference sample map for drawing the national boundaries of countries worldwide shall be formulated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information under the State Council, and shall be published after being approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在地图上绘制我国县级以上行政区域界线或者范围，应当符合行政区域界线标准画法图、国务院批准公布的特别行政区行政区域图和国家其他有关规定。  
行政区域界线标准画法图由国务院民政部门和国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门拟订，报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 11** The drawing of boundaries or extents of administrative divisions at or above the county level of China on maps shall conform to the standard drawings of administrative division boundaries, the administrative division maps of special administrative regions approved and published by the State Council, and other relevant provisions of the State.  
The standard drawings of administrative division boundaries shall be formulated by the civil affairs department of the State Council and the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council, and shall be published after being submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在地图上表示重要地理信息数据，应当使用依法公布的重要地理信息数据。

**Article 12** The representation of important geographic information data on maps shall use such data as have been published according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　利用涉及国家秘密的测绘成果编制地图的，应当依法使用经国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门进行保密技术处理的测绘成果。

**Article 13** Where maps are compiled using surveying and mapping results involving State secrets, such surveying and mapping results as have undergone confidentiality technical treatment by the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council or the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be used according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当向社会公布公益性地图，供无偿使用。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当及时组织收集与地图内容相关的行政区划、地名、交通、水系、植被、公共设施、居民点等的变更情况，用于定期更新公益性地图。有关部门和单位应当及时提供相关更新资料。

**Article 14** The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government at or above the county level shall publish public welfare maps to the public for free use.  
The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government at or above the county level shall timely organize the collection of changes in administrative divisions, place names, transportation, water systems, vegetation, public facilities, residential areas, etc., relevant to the contents of maps, for the periodical update of public welfare maps. Relevant departments and entities shall timely provide relevant updated materials.

### 第三章　地图审核

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行地图审核制度。  
向社会公开的地图，应当报送有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门审核。但是，景区图、街区图、地铁线路图等内容简单的地图除外。  
地图审核不得收取费用。

**Article 15** The State applies a map review system.  
Maps to be made public shall be submitted to the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with review authority for review, except for maps with simple contents such as scenic area maps, neighborhood maps, and subway route maps.  
No fees shall be charged for map review.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　出版地图的，由出版单位送审；展示或者登载不属于出版物的地图的，由展示者或者登载者送审；进口不属于出版物的地图或者附着地图图形的产品的，由进口者送审；进口属于出版物的地图，依照《出版管理条例》的有关规定执行；出口不属于出版物的地图或者附着地图图形的产品的，由出口者送审；生产附着地图图形的产品的，由生产者送审。  
送审应当提交以下材料：  
(一)地图审核申请表；  
(二)需要审核的地图样图或者样品；  
(三)地图编制单位的测绘资质证书。  
进口不属于出版物的地图和附着地图图形的产品的，仅需提交前款第一项、第二项规定的材料。利用涉及国家秘密的测绘成果编制地图的，还应当提交保密技术处理证明。

**Article 16** Where maps are published, the publishing entity shall submit them for review; where maps not belonging to publications are displayed or carried, the displayer or carrier shall submit them for review; where maps not belonging to publications or products bearing map graphics are imported, the importer shall submit them for review; where maps belonging to publications are imported, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Publication; where maps not belonging to publications or products bearing map graphics are exported, the exporter shall submit them for review; where products bearing map graphics are produced, the producer shall submit them for review.  
The following materials shall be submitted for review:  
(1) an application form for map review;  
(2) a sample map or sample product to be reviewed;  
(3) the surveying and mapping qualification certificate of the entity that compiled the map.  
Where maps not belonging to publications and products bearing map graphics are imported, only the materials specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph need to be submitted. Where maps are compiled using surveying and mapping results involving State secrets, a certificate of confidentiality technical treatment shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门负责下列地图的审核：  
(一)全国地图以及主要表现地为两个以上省、自治区、直辖市行政区域的地图；  
(二)香港特别行政区地图、澳门特别行政区地图以及台湾地区地图；  
(三)世界地图以及主要表现地为国外的地图；  
(四)历史地图。

**Article 17** The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council shall be responsible for the review of the following maps:  
(1) national maps and maps mainly representing the administrative divisions of two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) maps of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, maps of the Macao Special Administrative Region, and maps of the Taiwan region;  
(3) world maps and maps mainly representing foreign areas;  
(4) historical maps.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门负责审核主要表现地在本行政区域范围内的地图。其中，主要表现地在设区的市行政区域范围内不涉及国界线的地图，由设区的市级人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门负责审核。

**Article 18** The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for reviewing maps mainly representing areas within its administrative division. Among them, maps mainly representing areas within an administrative division of a city divided into districts and not involving national boundaries shall be reviewed by the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of that city divided into districts.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当自受理地图审核申请之日起20个工作日内，作出审核决定。  
时事宣传地图、时效性要求较高的图书和报刊等插附地图的，应当自受理地图审核申请之日起7个工作日内，作出审核决定。  
应急保障等特殊情况需要使用地图的，应当即送即审。

**Article 19** The administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with review authority shall make a review decision within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application for map review.  
For current affairs promotional maps, and maps inserted in books and periodicals with high timeliness requirements, a review decision shall be made within 7 working days from the date of accepting the application for map review.  
Where maps need to be used in special circumstances such as emergency support, they shall be reviewed immediately upon submission.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　涉及专业内容的地图，应当依照国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门会同有关部门制定的审核依据进行审核。没有明确审核依据的，由有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门征求有关部门的意见，有关部门应当自收到征求意见材料之日起20个工作日内提出意见。征求意见时间不计算在地图审核的期限内。  
世界地图、历史地图、时事宣传地图没有明确审核依据的，由国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门商外交部进行审核。

**Article 20** Maps involving specialized contents shall be reviewed in accordance with the review criteria formulated by the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council jointly with relevant departments. Where there are no clear review criteria, the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with review authority shall seek opinions from relevant departments, and the relevant departments shall provide opinions within 20 working days from the date of receiving the materials for seeking opinions. The time for seeking opinions shall not be counted in the period for map review.  
Where there are no clear review criteria for world maps, historical maps, and current affairs promotional maps, the administrative department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council shall review them in consultation with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　送审地图符合下列规定的，由有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门核发地图审核批准文件，并注明审图号：  
(一)符合国家有关地图编制标准，完整表示中华人民共和国疆域；  
(二)国界、边界、历史疆界、行政区域界线或者范围、重要地理信息数据、地名等符合国家有关地图内容表示的规定；  
(三)不含有地图上不得表示的内容。  
地图审核批准文件和审图号应当在有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门网站或者其他新闻媒体上及时公告。

**Article 21** Where a map submitted for review meets the following requirements, the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with examination and approval authority shall issue a map review approval document and indicate the review number:  
(1) it complies with the relevant national standards for map compilation and completely represents the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) the national boundaries, borders, historical boundaries, administrative division boundaries or extents, important geographic information data, place names, etc., comply with the relevant national provisions on map content representation;  
(3) it does not contain content that shall not be shown on a map.  
The map review approval document and the review number shall be promptly publicized on the website of the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with examination and approval authority or other news media.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经审核批准的地图，应当在地图或者附着地图图形的产品的适当位置显著标注审图号。其中，属于出版物的，应当在版权页标注审图号。

**Article 22** A map that has been reviewed and approved shall have the review number prominently marked in an appropriate position on the map or on the product bearing the map image. If it is a publication, the review number shall be marked on the copyright page.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　全国性中小学教学地图，由国务院教育行政部门会同国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门、外交部组织审定；地方性中小学教学地图，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府教育行政部门会同省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门组织审定。

**Article 23** Nationwide primary and secondary school teaching maps shall be organized for review and approval by the education administrative department of the State Council jointly with the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the State Council and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; local primary and secondary school teaching maps shall be organized for review and approval by the education administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　任何单位和个人不得出版、展示、登载、销售、进口、出口不符合国家有关标准和规定的地图，不得携带、寄递不符合国家有关标准和规定的地图进出境。  
进口、出口地图的，应当向海关提交地图审核批准文件和审图号。

**Article 24** No unit or individual may publish, display, upload, sell, import, or export maps that do not meet the relevant national standards and regulations, nor may they carry or send such maps across the border.  
For importing or exporting maps, the map review approval document and the review number shall be submitted to the customs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经审核批准的地图，送审者应当按照有关规定向有审核权的测绘地理信息行政主管部门免费送交样本。

**Article 25** For a map that has been reviewed and approved, the person who submitted it for review shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, deliver samples free of charge to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation with examination and approval authority.

### 第四章　地图出版

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门应当加强对地图出版活动的监督管理，依法对地图出版违法行为进行查处。

**Article 26** The publishing administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and administration over map publishing activities, and shall investigate and deal with illegal map publishing acts according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　出版单位从事地图出版活动的，应当具有国务院出版行政主管部门审核批准的地图出版业务范围，并依照《出版管理条例》的有关规定办理审批手续。

**Article 27** A publishing entity that engages in map publishing activities shall have a map publishing business scope reviewed and approved by the publishing administrative department of the State Council, and shall go through the examination and approval procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Publishing.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　出版单位根据需要，可以在出版物中插附经审核批准的地图。

**Article 28** A publishing entity may, as needed, insert a map that has been reviewed and approved into a publication.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　任何出版单位不得出版未经审定的中小学教学地图。

**Article 29** No publishing entity may publish primary or secondary school teaching maps that have not been reviewed and approved.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　出版单位出版地图，应当按照国家有关规定向国家图书馆、中国版本图书馆和国务院出版行政主管部门免费送交样本。

**Article 30** A publishing entity that publishes a map shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, deliver samples free of charge to the National Library of China, the China National Publications Library, and the publishing administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　地图著作权的保护，依照有关著作权法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 31** The protection of copyright in maps shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant copyright laws and regulations.

### 第五章　互联网地图服务

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励和支持互联网地图服务单位开展地理信息开发利用和增值服务。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强对互联网地图服务行业的政策扶持和监督管理。

**Article 32** The State encourages and supports Internet map service units to carry out the development, utilization and value-added services of geographic information.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen policy support and supervision over the Internet map service industry.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　互联网地图服务单位向公众提供地理位置定位、地理信息上传标注和地图数据库开发等服务的，应当依法取得相应的测绘资质证书。  
互联网地图服务单位从事互联网地图出版活动的，应当经国务院出版行政主管部门依法审核批准。

**Article 33** An Internet map service unit that provides services such as geographic location positioning, geographic information upload and labeling, and map database development to the public shall obtain the corresponding surveying and mapping qualification certificate according to law.  
An Internet map service unit that engages in Internet map publishing activities shall be subject to examination and approval by the competent publishing administrative department of the State Council in accordance with law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　互联网地图服务单位应当将存放地图数据的服务器设在中华人民共和国境内，并制定互联网地图数据安全管理制度和保障措施。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当会同有关部门加强对互联网地图数据安全的监督管理。

**Article 34** An Internet map service unit shall set up the server storing map data within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and formulate a security management system and safeguard measures for Internet map data.  
The competent administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information of the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments, strengthen supervision over the security of Internet map data.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　互联网地图服务单位收集、使用用户个人信息的，应当明示收集、使用信息的目的、方式和范围，并经用户同意。  
互联网地图服务单位需要收集、使用用户个人信息的，应当公开收集、使用规则，不得泄露、篡改、出售或者非法向他人提供用户的个人信息。  
互联网地图服务单位应当采取技术措施和其他必要措施，防止用户的个人信息泄露、丢失。

**Article 35** Where an Internet map service unit collects or uses users' personal information, it shall clearly indicate the purpose, method and scope of collection and use, and obtain the users' consent.  
Where an Internet map service unit needs to collect or use users' personal information, it shall make public the rules for collection and use, and shall not disclose, tamper with, sell or illegally provide users' personal information to others.  
An Internet map service unit shall take technical measures and other necessary measures to prevent the disclosure or loss of users' personal information.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　互联网地图服务单位用于提供服务的地图数据库及其他数据库不得存储、记录含有按照国家有关规定在地图上不得表示的内容。互联网地图服务单位发现其网站传输的地图信息含有不得表示的内容的，应当立即停止传输，保存有关记录，并向县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门、出版行政主管部门、网络安全和信息化主管部门等有关部门报告。

**Article 36** The map database and other databases used by an Internet map service unit for providing services shall not store or record any content that, according to the relevant provisions of the State, may not be indicated on a map. Where an Internet map service unit discovers that map information transmitted on its website contains content that may not be indicated, it shall immediately stop the transmission, preserve the relevant records, and report to the competent administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information, the competent administrative department of publishing, the competent department of network security and informatization, and other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得通过互联网上传标注含有按照国家有关规定在地图上不得表示的内容。

**Article 37** No unit or individual may upload or mark any content that, according to the relevant provisions of the State, may not be indicated on a map through the Internet.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　互联网地图服务单位应当使用经依法审核批准的地图，加强对互联网地图新增内容的核查校对，并按照国家有关规定向国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市测绘地理信息行政主管部门备案。

**Article 38** An Internet map service unit shall use maps that have been examined and approved in accordance with law, strengthen the verification and proofreading of newly added content on Internet maps, and, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, file a record with the competent administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information of the State Council or the competent administrative department of surveying, mapping and geographic information of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　互联网地图服务单位对在工作中获取的涉及国家秘密、商业秘密的信息，应当保密。

**Article 39** An Internet map service unit shall keep confidential any information involving State secrets or trade secrets obtained in the course of its work.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　互联网地图服务单位应当加强行业自律，推进行业信用体系建设，提高服务水平。

**Article 40** Internet map service units shall strengthen industry self-discipline, promote the construction of an industry credit system, and improve the service level.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从事互联网地图服务活动，适用本章的规定；本章没有规定的，适用本条例其他有关规定。

**Article 41** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to the activities of providing Internet map services; where there are no provisions in this Chapter, the other relevant provisions of these Regulations shall apply.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当依法加强对地图编制、出版、展示、登载、生产、销售、进口、出口等活动的监督检查。

**Article 42** The people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant departments thereof shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and inspection over the activities of compilation, publication, display, publishing, production, sale, import and export of maps.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门、出版行政主管部门和其他有关部门依法进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)进入涉嫌地图违法行为的场所实施现场检查；  
(二)查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿等资料；  
(三)查封、扣押涉嫌违法的地图、附着地图图形的产品以及用于实施地图违法行为的设备、工具、原材料等。

**Article 43** When carrying out supervision and inspection according to law, the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation, the competent departments of publication and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) to enter the places suspected of map-related illegal acts and conduct on-site inspection;  
(2) to consult and copy relevant contracts, bills, account books and other materials;  
(3) to seal up or seize the maps suspected of being illegal, products bearing map graphics, and the equipment, tools, raw materials, etc. used to commit map-related illegal acts.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院测绘地理信息行政主管部门、国务院出版行政主管部门应当建立健全地图监督管理信息系统，实现信息资源共享，方便公众查询。

**Article 44** The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council and the competent department of publication under the State Council shall establish and improve the information system for map supervision and administration, to realize the sharing of information resources and facilitate public inquiry.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门应当根据国家有关标准和技术规范，加强地图质量监督管理。  
地图编制、出版、展示、登载、生产、销售、进口、出口单位应当建立健全地图质量责任制度，采取有效措施，保证地图质量。

**Article 45** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant national standards and technical specifications, strengthen the supervision and administration of map quality.  
The entities that compile, publish, display, publish, produce, sell, import or export maps shall establish and improve the map quality responsibility system and take effective measures to ensure map quality.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　任何单位和个人对地图违法行为有权进行举报。  
接到举报的人民政府或者有关部门应当及时依法调查处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 46** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any map-related illegal act.  
The people's government or the relevant department that receives the report shall conduct investigation and handle the matter according to law in a timely manner, and shall keep the reporter confidential.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由主管机关或者监察机关责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的行为构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不依法作出行政许可决定或者办理批准文件的；  
(二)发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处的；  
(三)其他未依照本条例规定履行职责的行为。

**Article 47** Where the people's governments at or above the county level and the relevant departments thereof violate the provisions of these Regulations and commit any of the following acts, the competent authority or the supervisory authority shall order them to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the acts of the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to make an administrative license decision or handle the approval documents according to law;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with an illegal act discovered or a report received concerning an illegal act;  
(3) other acts of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，未取得测绘资质证书或者超越测绘资质等级许可的范围从事地图编制活动或者互联网地图服务活动的，依照《中华人民共和国测绘法》的有关规定进行处罚。

**Article 48** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engages in map compilation activities or Internet map service activities without obtaining a surveying and mapping qualification certificate or beyond the scope permitted by the surveying and mapping qualification grade, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，应当送审而未送审的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法地图或者附着地图图形的产品，可以处10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, fails to submit a map for examination as required, the said person shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and the illegal maps or products bearing map graphics shall be confiscated; a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may also be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，不需要送审的地图不符合国家有关标准和规定的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法地图或者附着地图图形的产品，可以处10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，可以向社会通报；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, produces a map that is not required to be submitted for examination but does not conform to the relevant national standards and provisions, the said person shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and the illegal maps or products bearing map graphics shall be confiscated; a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may also be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, a public notice may be issued; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，经审核不符合国家有关标准和规定的地图未按照审核要求修改即向社会公开的，责令改正，给予警告，没收违法地图或者附着地图图形的产品，可以处10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书，可以向社会通报；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where a map that, upon examination, fails to meet the relevant national standards and provisions is made available to the public without being modified in accordance with the examination requirements in violation of these Regulations, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, its illegal maps or products with map graphics attached shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked, and a public notice may be given; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，弄虚作假、伪造申请材料骗取地图审核批准文件，或者伪造、冒用地图审核批准文件和审图号的，责令停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法地图和附着地图图形的产品，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, practices fraud, forges application materials to obtain a map examination approval document by deception, or forges or fraudulently uses a map examination approval document and examination number, the violator shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, given a warning, its illegal maps and products with map graphics attached shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，未在地图的适当位置显著标注审图号，或者未按照有关规定送交样本的，责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书。

**Article 53** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, fails to conspicuously mark the examination number at an appropriate position on the map, or fails to submit samples in accordance with the relevant provisions, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections and given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，互联网地图服务单位使用未经依法审核批准的地图提供服务，或者未对互联网地图新增内容进行核查校对的，责令改正，给予警告，可以处20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where an Internet map service unit, in violation of these Regulations, uses a map that has not been examined and approved according to law to provide services, or fails to verify and check the new content of an Internet map, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan may be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the violator shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，通过互联网上传标注了含有按照国家有关规定在地图上不得表示的内容的，责令改正，给予警告，可以处10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, uploads and marks through the Internet content that, according to the relevant provisions of the State, shall not be shown on maps, the violator shall be ordered to make corrections, given a warning, and a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本条例规定的降低资质等级、吊销测绘资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书的部门决定；其他行政处罚由县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息行政主管部门决定。

**Article 56** The administrative penalty of lowering the qualification grade or revoking the surveying and mapping qualification certificate as provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the department that issues the qualification certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government at or above the county level.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　军队单位编制的地图的管理以及海图的管理，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的规定执行。

**Article 57** The management of maps compiled by military units and the management of nautical charts shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本条例自2016年1月1日起施行。国务院1995年7月10日发布的《中华人民共和国地图编制出版管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 58** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2016. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Map Compilation and Publication promulgated by the State Council on July 10, 1995 shall be abolished simultaneously.
