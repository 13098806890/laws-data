### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城市供水管理，发展城市供水事业，保障城市生活、生产用水和其他各项建设用水，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the management of urban water supply, developing the urban water supply undertaking, and ensuring water for urban domestic use, production and other construction purposes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称城市供水，是指城市公共供水和自建设施供水。  
本条例所称城市公共供水，是指城市自来水供水企业以公共供水管道及其附属设施向单位和居民的生活、生产和其他各项建设提供用水。  
本条例所称自建设施供水，是指城市的用水单位以其自行建设的供水管道及其附属设施主要向本单位的生活、生产和其他各项建设提供用水。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "urban water supply" means urban public water supply and self-built facility water supply.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "urban public water supply" means the supply of water by urban tap water supply enterprises through public water supply pipelines and their ancillary facilities to units and residents for domestic use, production and other construction purposes.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "self-built facility water supply" means the supply of water by urban water-using units through self-built water supply pipelines and their ancillary facilities mainly to their own units for domestic use, production and other construction purposes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事城市供水工作和使用城市供水，必须遵守本条例。

**Article 3** Any person engaged in urban water supply work or using urban water supply must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　城市供水工作实行开发水源和计划用水、节约用水相结合的原则。

**Article 4** The principle of combining water source development with planned water use and water conservation shall be implemented in urban water supply work.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将发展城市供水事业纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the development of the urban water supply undertaking into the national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家实行有利于城市供水事业发展的政策，鼓励城市供水科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市供水的现代化水平。

**Article 6** The State shall implement policies conducive to the development of the urban water supply undertaking, encourage scientific and technological research on urban water supply, promote advanced technology, and enhance the modernization level of urban water supply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院城市建设行政主管部门主管全国城市供水工作。  
省、自治区人民政府城市建设行政主管部门主管本行政区域内的城市供水工作。  
县级以上城市人民政府确定的城市供水行政主管部门(以下简称城市供水行政主管部门)主管本行政区域内的城市供水工作。

**Article 7** The administrative department of urban construction under the State Council shall be responsible for the national urban water supply work.  
The administrative department of urban construction under the people's government of a province or autonomous region shall be responsible for the urban water supply work within its administrative region.  
The administrative department of urban water supply (hereinafter referred to as the urban water supply administrative department) designated by the people's government of a city at or above the county level shall be responsible for the urban water supply work within its administrative region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在城市供水工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 8** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in urban water supply work shall be given awards.

### 第二章　城市供水水源

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上城市人民政府应当组织城市规划行政主管部门、水行政主管部门、城市供水行政主管部门和地质矿产行政主管部门等共同编制城市供水水源开发利用规划，作为城市供水发展规划的组成部分，纳入城市总体规划。

**Article 9** The people's government of a city at or above the county level shall organize the administrative department of urban planning, the administrative department of water resources, the urban water supply administrative department, the administrative department of geology and mineral resources, and other relevant departments to jointly formulate a plan for the development and utilization of urban water supply water sources, which shall be incorporated as a component of the urban water supply development plan into the overall urban plan.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　编制城市供水水源开发利用规划，应当从城市发展的需要出发，并与水资源统筹规划和水长期供求计划相协调。

**Article 10** The formulation of a plan for the development and utilization of urban water supply water sources shall proceed from the needs of urban development and shall be coordinated with the overall plan for water resources and the long-term water supply and demand plan.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　编制城市供水水源开发利用规划，应当根据当地情况，合理安排利用地表水和地下水。

**Article 11** The formulation of a plan for the development and utilization of urban water supply water sources shall, based on local conditions, make reasonable arrangements for the use of surface water and groundwater.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　编制城市供水水源开发利用规划，应当优先保证城市生活用水，统筹兼顾工业用水和其他各项建设用水。

**Article 12** The formulation of a plan for the development and utilization of urban water supply water sources shall give priority to ensuring urban domestic water use and shall make overall plans for industrial water use and other construction water uses.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府环境保护部门应当会同城市供水行政主管部门、水行政主管部门和卫生行政主管部门等共同划定饮用水水源保护区，经本级人民政府批准后公布；划定跨省、市、县的饮用水水源保护区，应当由有关人民政府共同商定并经其共同的上级人民政府批准后公布。

**Article 13** The environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the urban water supply administrative department, the water administrative department, the health administrative department, and other relevant departments, delimit drinking water source protection zones, which shall be published after approval by the people's government at the same level. The delimitation of drinking water source protection zones that cross provinces, cities, or counties shall be jointly decided by the relevant people's governments and published after approval by their common higher-level people's government.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在饮用水水源保护区内，禁止一切污染水质的活动。

**Article 14** All activities that pollute water quality shall be prohibited within drinking water source protection zones.

### 第三章　城市供水工程建设

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市供水工程的建设，应当按照城市供水发展规划及其年度建设计划进行。

**Article 15** The construction of urban water supply projects shall be carried out in accordance with the urban water supply development plan and its annual construction plan.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城市供水工程的设计、施工，应当委托持有相应资质证书的设计、施工单位承担，并遵守国家有关技术标准和规范。禁止无证或者超越资质证书规定的经营范围承担城市供水工程的设计、施工任务。

**Article 16** The design and construction of urban water supply projects shall be entrusted to design and construction units holding corresponding qualification certificates and shall comply with the relevant national technical standards and specifications. No unit without a certificate or exceeding the business scope specified in the qualification certificate shall undertake the design or construction of urban water supply projects.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市供水工程竣工后，应当按照国家规定组织验收；未经验收或者验收不合格的，不得投入使用。

**Article 17** Upon completion of an urban water supply project, acceptance inspection shall be organized in accordance with state regulations. No project that has not undergone acceptance inspection or has failed the acceptance inspection shall be put into use.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　城市新建、扩建、改建工程项目需要增加用水的，其工程项目总概算应当包括供水工程建设投资；需要增加城市公共供水量的，应当将其供水工程建设投资交付城市供水行政主管部门，由其统一组织城市公共供水工程建设。

**Article 18** Where a new, expanded, or renovated urban construction project requires additional water, the total budget estimate of the project shall include the investment in water supply project construction. Where additional urban public water supply capacity is needed, the investment in water supply project construction shall be delivered to the urban water supply administrative department, which shall organize the construction of the urban public water supply project in a unified manner.

### 第四章　城市供水经营

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施对外供水的企业，经工商行政管理机关登记注册后，方可从事经营活动。

**Article 19** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water externally through self-built facilities may engage in business operations only after being registered with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施对外供水的企业，应当建立、健全水质检测制度，确保城市供水的水质符合国家规定的饮用水卫生标准。

**Article 20** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water externally through self-built facilities shall establish and improve water quality testing systems to ensure that the quality of urban water supply complies with the national hygienic standards for drinking water.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施对外供水的企业，应当按照国家有关规定设置管网测压点，做好水压监测工作，确保供水管网的压力符合国家规定的标准。  
禁止在城市公共供水管道上直接装泵抽水。

**Article 21** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water externally through self-built facilities shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, set up pipeline network pressure measurement points, carry out water pressure monitoring work, and ensure that the pressure of the water supply pipeline network complies with the standards prescribed by the State.  
It is prohibited to directly install pumps to draw water from urban public water supply pipelines.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施对外供水的企业应当保持不间断供水。由于工程施工、设备维修等原因确需停止供水的，应当经城市供水行政主管部门批准并提前24小时通知用水单位和个人；因发生灾害或者紧急事故，不能提前通知的，应当在抢修的同时通知用水单位和个人，尽快恢复正常供水，并报告城市供水行政主管部门。

**Article 22** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water externally through self-built facilities shall maintain uninterrupted water supply. Where it is indeed necessary to stop water supply due to engineering construction, equipment maintenance, or other reasons, approval from the urban water supply administrative department shall be obtained, and water-using units and individuals shall be notified 24 hours in advance. Where notification cannot be given in advance due to a disaster or emergency, the water-using units and individuals shall be notified at the same time as emergency repairs are carried out, normal water supply shall be restored as soon as possible, and the urban water supply administrative department shall be reported.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施对外供水的企业应当实行职工持证上岗制度。具体办法由国务院城市建设行政主管部门会同人事部门等制定。

**Article 23** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water externally through self-built facilities shall implement a system requiring employees to hold certificates for their posts. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of urban construction under the State Council jointly with the personnel department and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　用水单位和个人应当按照规定的计量标准和水价标准按时缴纳水费。

**Article 24** Water-using units and individuals shall pay water charges on time in accordance with the prescribed metering standards and water price standards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　禁止盗用或者转供城市公共供水。

**Article 25** It is prohibited to steal or resell urban public water supply.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　城市供水价格应当按照生活用水保本微利、生产和经营用水合理计价的原则制定。  
城市供水价格制定办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 26** Urban water supply prices shall be formulated in accordance with the principle of covering costs plus a small profit for domestic water use and reasonable pricing for production and operation water use.  
The measures for formulating urban water supply prices shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第五章　城市供水设施维护

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城市自来水供水企业和自建设施供水的企业对其管理的城市供水的专用水库、引水渠道、取水口、泵站、井群、输(配)水管网、进户总水表、净(配)水厂、公用水站等设施，应当定期检查维修，确保安全运行。

**Article 27** Urban tap water supply enterprises and enterprises supplying water through self-built facilities shall regularly inspect and maintain the facilities under their management, including special-purpose reservoirs, diversion channels, water intakes, pumping stations, well groups, water transmission (distribution) pipeline networks, main water meters at household entrances, water purification (distribution) plants, and public water stations, to ensure safe operation.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　用水单位自行建设的与城市公共供水管道连接的户外管道及其附属设施，必须经城市自来水供水企业验收合格并交其统一管理后，方可使用。

**Article 28** Outdoor pipelines and their ancillary facilities constructed by water-using units themselves and connected to urban public water supply pipelines must be accepted as qualified by the urban tap water supply enterprise and handed over to it for unified management before they may be used.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在规定的城市公共供水管道及其附属设施的地面和地下的安全保护范围内，禁止挖坑取土或者修建建筑物、构筑物等危害供水设施安全的活动。

**Article 29** Within the prescribed safety protection zones on the ground and underground of urban public water supply pipelines and their ancillary facilities, activities that jeopardize the safety of water supply facilities, such as digging pits, taking soil, or constructing buildings or structures, are prohibited.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　因工程建设确需改装、拆除或者迁移城市公共供水设施的，建设单位应当报经县级以上人民政府城市规划行政主管部门和城市供水行政主管部门批准，并采取相应的补救措施。

**Article 30** Where it is indeed necessary to alter, remove, or relocate urban public water supply facilities due to engineering construction, the construction unit shall report to and obtain approval from the administrative department of urban planning and the urban water supply administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, and shall take corresponding remedial measures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　涉及城市公共供水设施的建设工程开工前，建设单位或者施工单位应当向城市自来水供水企业查明地下供水管网情况。施工影响城市公共供水设施安全的，建设单位或者施工单位应当与城市自来水供水企业商定相应的保护措施，由施工单位负责实施。

**Article 31** Before the commencement of a construction project involving urban public water supply facilities, the construction unit or the construction contractor shall ascertain the conditions of the underground water supply pipeline network from the urban tap water supply enterprise. Where the construction affects the safety of urban public water supply facilities, the construction unit or the construction contractor shall consult with the urban tap water supply enterprise on corresponding protective measures, which shall be implemented by the construction contractor.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　禁止擅自将自建设施供水管网系统与城市公共供水管网系统连接；因特殊情况确需连接的，必须经城市自来水供水企业同意，并在管道连接处采取必要的防护措施。  
禁止产生或者使用有毒有害物质的单位将其生产用水管网系统与城市公共供水管网系统直接连接。

**Article 32** It is prohibited to connect the self-built facility water supply pipeline network system with the urban public water supply pipeline network system without authorization. Where connection is indeed necessary due to special circumstances, the consent of the urban tap water supply enterprise must be obtained, and necessary protective measures shall be taken at the connection point of the pipelines.  
It is prohibited for units that generate or use toxic or hazardous substances to directly connect their production water pipeline network system with the urban public water supply pipeline network system.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　城市自来水供水企业或者自建设施对外供水的企业有下列行为之一的，由城市供水行政主管部门责令改正，可以处以罚款；情节严重的，报经县级以上人民政府批准，可以责令停业整顿；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，其所在单位或者上级机关可以给予行政处分：  
(一)供水水质、水压不符合国家规定标准的；  
(二)擅自停止供水或者未履行停水通知义务的；  
(三)未按照规定检修供水设施或者在供水设施发生故障后未及时抢修的。

**Article 33** Where an urban tap water supply enterprise or an enterprise supplying water externally through self-built facilities commits any of the following acts, the urban water supply administrative department shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine; if the circumstances are serious, upon approval by the people's government at or above the county level, it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification; the unit to which the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons belong or the superior organ may impose administrative sanctions on them:  
(1) The water quality or water pressure of the water supply does not meet the standards prescribed by the State;  
(2) Unauthorized cessation of water supply or failure to fulfill the obligation of notifying water users of the cessation;  
(3) Failure to inspect and repair water supply facilities in accordance with regulations or failure to carry out emergency repairs in a timely manner after a breakdown of water supply facilities.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由城市供水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，可以处以罚款；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，其所在单位或者上级机关可以给予行政处分：  
(一)无证或者超越资质证书规定的经营范围进行城市供水工程的设计或者施工的；  
(二)未按国家规定的技术标准和规范进行城市供水工程的设计或者施工的；  
(三)违反城市供水发展规划及其年度建设计划兴建城市供水工程的。

**Article 34** Where any person violates these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the urban water supply administrative department shall order the cessation of the illegal act and may impose a fine; the unit to which the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons belong or the superior organ may impose administrative sanctions on them:  
(1) Designing or constructing urban water supply projects without a certificate or exceeding the business scope specified in the qualification certificate;  
(2) Designing or constructing urban water supply projects not in accordance with the technical standards and specifications prescribed by the State;  
(3) Constructing urban water supply projects in violation of the urban water supply development plan and its annual construction plan.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由城市供水行政主管部门或者其授权的单位责令限期改正，可以处以罚款：  
(一)盗用或者转供城市公共供水的；  
(二)在规定的城市公共供水管道及其附属设施的安全保护范围内进行危害供水设施安全活动的；  
(三)擅自将自建设施供水管网系统与城市公共供水管网系统连接的；  
(四)产生或者使用有毒有害物质的单位将其生产用水管网系统与城市公共供水管网系统直接连接的；  
(五)在城市公共供水管道上直接装泵抽水的；  
(六)擅自拆除、改装或者迁移城市公共供水设施的。  
有前款第(一)项、第(三)项、第(四)项、第(五)项、第(六)项所列行为之一，情节严重的，经县级以上人民政府批准，还可以在一定时间内停止供水。

**Article 35** Where any person violates these Regulations by committing any of the following acts, the urban water supply administrative department or its authorized unit shall order rectification within a specified period and may impose a fine:  
(1) Stealing or reselling urban public water supply;  
(2) Carrying out activities that jeopardize the safety of water supply facilities within the prescribed safety protection zones of urban public water supply pipelines and their ancillary facilities;  
(3) Connecting the self-built facility water supply pipeline network system with the urban public water supply pipeline network system without authorization;  
(4) Units that generate or use toxic or hazardous substances directly connecting their production water pipeline network system with the urban public water supply pipeline network system;  
(5) Directly installing pumps to draw water from urban public water supply pipelines;  
(6) Removing, altering, or relocating urban public water supply facilities without authorization.  
Where the circumstances of any act listed in items (1), (3), (4), (5) or (6) of the preceding paragraph are serious, upon approval by the people's government at or above the county level, the water supply may also be suspended for a certain period.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　建设工程施工危害城市公共供水设施的，由城市供水行政主管部门责令停止危害活动；造成损失的，由责任方依法赔偿损失；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，其所在单位或者上级机关可以给予行政处分。

**Article 36** Where the construction of a construction project endangers urban public water supply facilities, the urban water supply administrative department shall order the cessation of the endangering activity. If losses are caused, the responsible party shall compensate for the losses according to law. The unit to which the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons belong or the superior organ may impose administrative sanctions on them.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　城市供水行政主管部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where a staff member of the urban water supply administrative department neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, the unit to which he belongs or the superior organ shall impose administrative sanctions on him. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　[本条例第三十三条](#art-33)、第三十四条、第三十五条规定的罚款数额由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 38** The amounts of fines specified in Articles 33, 34 and 35 of these Regulations shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例自1994年10月1日起施行。

**Article 39** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1994.
