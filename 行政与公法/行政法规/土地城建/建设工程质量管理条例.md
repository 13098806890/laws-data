# 建设工程质量管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-23

> (2000年1月30日中华人民共和国国务院令第279号发布　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年4月23日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对建设工程质量的管理，保证建设工程质量，保护人民生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国建筑法》，制定本条例。 第二条　凡在中华人民共和国境内从事建设工程的新建、扩建、改建等有关活动及实施对建设工程质量监督管理的，必须遵守本条例。 本条例所称建设工程，是指土木工程、建筑工程、线路管道和设备安装工程及装修工程。 第三条　建设单位、勘察单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位依法对建设工程质量负责。 第四条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当加强对建设工程质量的监督管理。 第五条　从事建设工程活动，必须严格执行基本建设程序，坚持先勘察、后设计、再施工的原则。 县级以上人民政府及其有关部门不得超越权限审批建设项目或者擅自简化基本建设程序。 第六条　国家鼓励采用先进的科学技术和管理方法，提高建设工程质量。 第二章　建设单位的质量责任和义务 第七条　建设单位应当将工程发包给具有相应资质等级的单位。 建设单位不得将建设工程肢解发包。 第八条　建设单位应当依法对工程建设项目的勘察、设计、施工、监理以及与工程建设有关的重要设备、材料等的采购进行招标。 第九条　建设单位必须向有关的勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位提供与建设工程有关的原始资料。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对建设工程质量的管理，保证建设工程质量，保护人民生命和财产安全，根据《中华人民共和国建筑法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Construction Law of the People's Republic of China to strengthen the management of construction project quality, ensure the quality of construction projects, and protect the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　凡在中华人民共和国境内从事建设工程的新建、扩建、改建等有关活动及实施对建设工程质量监督管理的，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所称建设工程，是指土木工程、建筑工程、线路管道和设备安装工程及装修工程。

**Article 2** Any entity that engages in activities such as new construction, expansion, or renovation of construction projects within the territory of the People's Republic of China, and that implements supervision and administration of construction project quality, must comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'construction project' means civil engineering works, building works, line, pipeline and equipment installation works, and decoration works.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　建设单位、勘察单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位依法对建设工程质量负责。

**Article 3** The construction entity, survey entity, design entity, construction entity, and project supervision entity shall be responsible for the quality of construction projects according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当加强对建设工程质量的监督管理。

**Article 4** The competent administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall strengthen the supervision and administration of construction project quality.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事建设工程活动，必须严格执行基本建设程序，坚持先勘察、后设计、再施工的原则。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门不得超越权限审批建设项目或者擅自简化基本建设程序。

**Article 5** Any entity that engages in construction project activities must strictly implement the capital construction procedures and adhere to the principle of survey first, then design, and then construction.  
The people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments shall not approve construction projects beyond their authority or arbitrarily simplify the capital construction procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励采用先进的科学技术和管理方法，提高建设工程质量。

**Article 6** The State encourages the adoption of advanced science and technology and management methods to improve the quality of construction projects.

### 第二章　建设单位的质量责任和义务

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　建设单位应当将工程发包给具有相应资质等级的单位。  
建设单位不得将建设工程肢解发包。

**Article 7** The construction entity shall contract out the project to an entity with the corresponding qualification grade.  
The construction entity shall not break up the construction project into parts for contracting.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　建设单位应当依法对工程建设项目的勘察、设计、施工、监理以及与工程建设有关的重要设备、材料等的采购进行招标。

**Article 8** The construction entity shall, according to law, conduct tenders for the survey, design, construction, supervision of the construction project, as well as for the procurement of important equipment, materials, etc., related to the construction project.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　建设单位必须向有关的勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位提供与建设工程有关的原始资料。  
原始资料必须真实、准确、齐全。

**Article 9** The construction entity must provide the original materials related to the construction project to the relevant survey, design, construction, and project supervision entities.  
The original materials must be authentic, accurate, and complete.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建设工程发包单位，不得迫使承包方以低于成本的价格竞标，不得任意压缩合理工期。  
建设单位不得明示或者暗示设计单位或者施工单位违反工程建设强制性标准，降低建设工程质量。

**Article 10** The entity that contracts out the construction project shall not compel the contractor to bid at a price lower than cost, and shall not arbitrarily shorten the reasonable construction period.  
The construction entity shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct the design entity or the construction entity to violate the mandatory standards for construction projects and thereby reduce the quality of the construction project.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　施工图设计文件审查的具体办法，由国务院建设行政主管部门、国务院其他有关部门制定。  
施工图设计文件未经审查批准的，不得使用。

**Article 11** The specific measures for the examination of construction drawing design documents shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council and other relevant departments of the State Council.  
Construction drawing design documents that have not been examined and approved shall not be used.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　实行监理的建设工程，建设单位应当委托具有相应资质等级的工程监理单位进行监理，也可以委托具有工程监理相应资质等级并与被监理工程的施工承包单位没有隶属关系或者其他利害关系的该工程的设计单位进行监理。  
下列建设工程必须实行监理：  
(一)国家重点建设工程；  
(二)大中型公用事业工程；  
(三)成片开发建设的住宅小区工程；  
(四)利用外国政府或者国际组织贷款、援助资金的工程；  
(五)国家规定必须实行监理的其他工程。

**Article 12** For a construction project subject to supervision, the construction entity shall appoint a project supervision entity with the corresponding qualification grade to carry out supervision, or may appoint the design entity of the project that has the corresponding qualification grade for project supervision and has no subordinate relationship or other conflict of interest with the construction contractor of the supervised project.  
The following construction projects must be subject to supervision:  
(1) key state construction projects;  
(2) large and medium-sized public utility projects;  
(3) residential district projects developed on a large scale;  
(4) projects using loans or aid funds from foreign governments or international organizations;  
(5) other projects that the State stipulates must be subject to supervision.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设单位在开工前，应当按照国家有关规定办理工程质量监督手续，工程质量监督手续可以与施工许可证或者开工报告合并办理。

**Article 13** Before commencement of construction, the construction entity shall, according to the relevant provisions of the State, go through the procedures for quality supervision of the construction project. The procedures for quality supervision may be combined with the construction permit or the commencement report.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　按照合同约定，由建设单位采购建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的，建设单位应当保证建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备符合设计文件和合同要求。  
建设单位不得明示或者暗示施工单位使用不合格的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备。

**Article 14** Where, in accordance with the contract, the construction entity procures building materials, building components and parts, and equipment, the construction entity shall ensure that such materials, components, parts, and equipment meet the requirements of the design documents and the contract.  
The construction entity shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct the construction entity to use substandard building materials, building components and parts, and equipment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　涉及建筑主体和承重结构变动的装修工程，建设单位应当在施工前委托原设计单位或者具有相应资质等级的设计单位提出设计方案；没有设计方案的，不得施工。  
房屋建筑使用者在装修过程中，不得擅自变动房屋建筑主体和承重结构。

**Article 15** For a decoration project involving changes to the main body or load-bearing structure of a building, the construction entity shall, before construction, appoint the original design entity or a design entity with the corresponding qualification grade to produce a design proposal; where there is no design proposal, construction shall not proceed.  
The user of a building shall not, during the decoration process, arbitrarily change the main body or load-bearing structure of the building.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建设单位收到建设工程竣工报告后，应当组织设计、施工、工程监理等有关单位进行竣工验收。  
建设工程竣工验收应当具备下列条件：  
(一)完成建设工程设计和合同约定的各项内容；  
(二)有完整的技术档案和施工管理资料；  
(三)有工程使用的主要建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的进场试验报告；  
(四)有勘察、设计、施工、工程监理等单位分别签署的质量合格文件；  
(五)有施工单位签署的工程保修书。  
建设工程经验收合格的，方可交付使用。

**Article 16** After receiving the completion report of the construction project, the construction entity shall organize the design entity, construction entity, project supervision entity, and other relevant entities to carry out the final acceptance inspection.  
The final acceptance inspection of a construction project shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) completion of all contents specified in the design documents and the contract;  
(2) availability of complete technical archives and construction management materials;  
(3) availability of inspection reports for the main building materials, building components and parts, and equipment used in the project upon arrival at the site;  
(4) availability of quality acceptance documents signed respectively by the survey, design, construction, and project supervision entities;  
(5) availability of the project warranty certificate signed by the construction entity.  
Only after a construction project passes the final acceptance inspection may it be delivered for use.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设单位应当严格按照国家有关档案管理的规定，及时收集、整理建设项目各环节的文件资料，建立、健全建设项目档案，并在建设工程竣工验收后，及时向建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门移交建设项目档案。

**Article 17** The construction entity shall strictly follow the relevant provisions of the State on archives management, timely collect and organize the documents and materials of all stages of the construction project, establish and improve the construction project archives, and after the final acceptance inspection of the construction project, timely transfer the construction project archives to the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments.

### 第三章　勘察、设计单位的质量责任和义务

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　从事建设工程勘察、设计的单位应当依法取得相应等级的资质证书，并在其资质等级许可的范围内承揽工程。  
禁止勘察、设计单位超越其资质等级许可的范围或者以其他勘察、设计单位的名义承揽工程。禁止勘察、设计单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位的名义承揽工程。  
勘察、设计单位不得转包或者违法分包所承揽的工程。

**Article 18** The entity engaged in survey or design of construction projects shall obtain a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade according to law, and shall undertake projects within the scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
It is prohibited for a survey or design entity to undertake projects beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade or under the name of another survey or design entity. It is prohibited for a survey or design entity to permit another entity or individual to undertake projects under its name.  
A survey or design entity shall not subcontract or illegally subcontract the projects it undertakes.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　勘察、设计单位必须按照工程建设强制性标准进行勘察、设计，并对其勘察、设计的质量负责。  
注册建筑师、注册结构工程师等注册执业人员应当在设计文件上签字，对设计文件负责。

**Article 19** A survey or design entity must carry out survey or design in accordance with the mandatory standards for construction projects, and shall be responsible for the quality of its survey or design.  
Registered practicing personnel such as registered architects and registered structural engineers shall sign the design documents and shall be responsible for the design documents.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　勘察单位提供的地质、测量、水文等勘察成果必须真实、准确。

**Article 20** The survey results such as geological, measurement, and hydrological data provided by the survey entity must be authentic and accurate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设计单位应当根据勘察成果文件进行建设工程设计。  
设计文件应当符合国家规定的设计深度要求，注明工程合理使用年限。

**Article 21** A design entity shall carry out the design of a construction project based on the survey result documents.  
The design documents shall meet the design depth requirements specified by the State, and shall indicate the reasonable service life of the project.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　设计单位在设计文件中选用的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备，应当注明规格、型号、性能等技术指标，其质量要求必须符合国家规定的标准。  
除有特殊要求的建筑材料、专用设备、工艺生产线等外，设计单位不得指定生产厂、供应商。

**Article 22** The building materials, building components and parts, and equipment selected by the design entity in the design documents shall be annotated with technical indicators such as specifications, models, and performance, and their quality requirements must comply with the standards specified by the State.  
Except for building materials, special equipment, and production lines with special requirements, the design entity shall not designate manufacturers or suppliers.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　设计单位应当就审查合格的施工图设计文件向施工单位作出详细说明。

**Article 23** The design entity shall provide detailed explanations to the construction entity regarding the construction drawing design documents that have passed examination.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　设计单位应当参与建设工程质量事故分析，并对因设计造成的质量事故，提出相应的技术处理方案。

**Article 24** The design entity shall participate in the analysis of quality accidents of construction projects, and for quality accidents caused by design, shall propose corresponding technical treatment plans.

### 第四章　施工单位的质量责任和义务

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　施工单位应当依法取得相应等级的资质证书，并在其资质等级许可的范围内承揽工程。  
禁止施工单位超越本单位资质等级许可的业务范围或者以其他施工单位的名义承揽工程。禁止施工单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位的名义承揽工程。  
施工单位不得转包或者违法分包工程。

**Article 25** The construction entity shall obtain a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade according to law, and shall undertake projects within the scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
It is prohibited for a construction entity to undertake projects beyond the business scope permitted by its qualification grade or under the name of another construction entity. It is prohibited for a construction entity to permit another entity or individual to undertake projects under its name.  
A construction entity shall not subcontract or illegally subcontract projects.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　施工单位对建设工程的施工质量负责。  
施工单位应当建立质量责任制，确定工程项目的项目经理、技术负责人和施工管理负责人。  
建设工程实行总承包的，总承包单位应当对全部建设工程质量负责；建设工程勘察、设计、施工、设备采购的一项或者多项实行总承包的，总承包单位应当对其承包的建设工程或者采购的设备的质量负责。

**Article 26** The construction entity shall be responsible for the construction quality of the construction project.  
The construction entity shall establish a quality responsibility system and determine the project manager, technical director, and construction management director of the project.  
Where a construction project is subject to general contracting, the general contractor shall be responsible for the quality of the entire construction project; where one or more of the survey, design, construction, or equipment procurement of the construction project are subject to general contracting, the general contractor shall be responsible for the quality of the construction project it contracts or the equipment it procures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　总承包单位依法将建设工程分包给其他单位的，分包单位应当按照分包合同的约定对其分包工程的质量向总承包单位负责，总承包单位与分包单位对分包工程的质量承担连带责任。

**Article 27** Where a general contractor subcontracts a construction project to another entity according to law, the subcontractor shall be responsible to the general contractor for the quality of the subcontracted project in accordance with the subcontract agreement, and the general contractor and the subcontractor shall bear joint and several liability for the quality of the subcontracted project.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　施工单位必须按照工程设计图纸和施工技术标准施工，不得擅自修改工程设计，不得偷工减料。  
施工单位在施工过程中发现设计文件和图纸有差错的，应当及时提出意见和建议。

**Article 28** The construction entity must construct in accordance with the engineering design drawings and construction technical standards, shall not arbitrarily modify the engineering design, and shall not cheat on materials or skimp on work.  
Where, during the construction process, the construction entity discovers errors in the design documents or drawings, it shall promptly raise opinions and suggestions.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　施工单位必须按照工程设计要求、施工技术标准和合同约定，对建筑材料、建筑构配件、设备和商品混凝土进行检验，检验应当有书面记录和专人签字；未经检验或者检验不合格的，不得使用。

**Article 29** The construction entity must, in accordance with the engineering design requirements, construction technical standards, and the contract, inspect the building materials, building components and parts, equipment, and commercial concrete. The inspection shall have written records and be signed by a designated person; those that have not been inspected or have failed inspection shall not be used.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　施工单位必须建立、健全施工质量的检验制度，严格工序管理，作好隐蔽工程的质量检查和记录。隐蔽工程在隐蔽前，施工单位应当通知建设单位和建设工程质量监督机构。

**Article 30** The construction entity must establish and improve the inspection system for construction quality, strictly manage construction procedures, and properly carry out quality inspection and records of concealed works. Before concealing a concealed work, the construction entity shall notify the construction entity and the construction project quality supervision agency.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　施工人员对涉及结构安全的试块、试件以及有关材料，应当在建设单位或者工程监理单位监督下现场取样，并送具有相应资质等级的质量检测单位进行检测。

**Article 31** Construction personnel shall, under the supervision of the construction entity or the project supervision entity, take samples on site of test blocks, test pieces, and related materials involving structural safety, and send them to a quality testing entity with the corresponding qualification grade for testing.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　施工单位对施工中出现质量问题的建设工程或者竣工验收不合格的建设工程，应当负责返修。

**Article 32** The construction entity shall be responsible for repair of a construction project that has quality problems during construction or that fails the final acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　施工单位应当建立、健全教育培训制度，加强对职工的教育培训；未经教育培训或者考核不合格的人员，不得上岗作业。

**Article 33** The construction entity shall establish and improve the education and training system, and strengthen education and training for its employees; personnel who have not undergone education and training or who have failed the assessment shall not commence work.

### 第五章　工程监理单位的质量责任和义务

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　工程监理单位应当依法取得相应等级的资质证书，并在其资质等级许可的范围内承担工程监理业务。  
禁止工程监理单位超越本单位资质等级许可的范围或者以其他工程监理单位的名义承担工程监理业务。禁止工程监理单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位的名义承担工程监理业务。  
工程监理单位不得转让工程监理业务。

**Article 34** The project supervision entity shall obtain a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade according to law, and shall undertake project supervision business within the scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
It is prohibited for a project supervision entity to undertake project supervision business beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade or under the name of another project supervision entity. It is prohibited for a project supervision entity to permit another entity or individual to undertake project supervision business under its name.  
A project supervision entity shall not transfer the project supervision business.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　工程监理单位与被监理工程的施工承包单位以及建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备供应单位有隶属关系或者其他利害关系的，不得承担该项建设工程的监理业务。

**Article 35** A project supervision entity that has a subordinate relationship or other conflict of interest with the construction contractor or the supplier of building materials, building components and parts, and equipment of the supervised project shall not undertake the supervision business of that construction project.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　工程监理单位应当依照法律、法规以及有关技术标准、设计文件和建设工程承包合同，代表建设单位对施工质量实施监理，并对施工质量承担监理责任。

**Article 36** The project supervision entity shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant technical standards, design documents, and the construction project contract, carry out supervision on behalf of the construction entity with respect to construction quality, and shall bear supervision responsibility for construction quality.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　工程监理单位应当选派具备相应资格的总监理工程师和监理工程师进驻施工现场。  
未经监理工程师签字，建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备不得在工程上使用或者安装，施工单位不得进行下一道工序的施工。未经总监理工程师签字，建设单位不拨付工程款，不进行竣工验收。

**Article 37** The project supervision entity shall select and dispatch a chief supervising engineer and supervising engineers with appropriate qualifications to be stationed at the construction site.  
Without the signature of the supervising engineer, building materials, building components and parts, and equipment shall not be used or installed in the project, and the construction entity shall not proceed with the next construction process. Without the signature of the chief supervising engineer, the construction entity shall not release project funds and shall not carry out the final acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　监理工程师应当按照工程监理规范的要求，采取旁站、巡视和平行检验等形式，对建设工程实施监理。

**Article 38** Supervising engineers shall, in accordance with the requirements of the project supervision specifications, carry out supervision of the construction project in such forms as side station monitoring, patrol inspection, and parallel inspection.

### 第六章　建设工程质量保修

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　建设工程实行质量保修制度。  
建设工程承包单位在向建设单位提交工程竣工验收报告时，应当向建设单位出具质量保修书。质量保修书中应当明确建设工程的保修范围、保修期限和保修责任等。

**Article 39** A quality warranty system shall be implemented for construction projects.  
When submitting the project completion acceptance report to the construction entity, the contractor of the construction project shall issue a quality warranty certificate to the construction entity. The quality warranty certificate shall specify the scope, period, and responsibility of the warranty for the construction project.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　在正常使用条件下，建设工程的最低保修期限为：  
(一)基础设施工程、房屋建筑的地基基础工程和主体结构工程，为设计文件规定的该工程的合理使用年限；  
(二)屋面防水工程、有防水要求的卫生间、房间和外墙面的防渗漏，为5年；  
(三)供热与供冷系统，为2个采暖期、供冷期；  
(四)电气管线、给排水管道、设备安装和装修工程，为2年。  
其他项目的保修期限由发包方与承包方约定。  
建设工程的保修期，自竣工验收合格之日起计算。

**Article 40** Under normal usage conditions, the minimum warranty period for a construction project is:  
(1) for infrastructure works, foundation works and main structure works of buildings, the reasonable service life of the project as specified in the design documents;  
(2) for roof waterproofing works, waterproofing of bathrooms, rooms, and external walls with waterproofing requirements, five years;  
(3) for heating and cooling systems, two heating periods and cooling periods;  
(4) for electrical wiring, water supply and drainage pipelines, equipment installation, and decoration works, two years.  
The warranty period for other items shall be agreed upon by the contract-issuing party and the contractor.  
The warranty period of a construction project shall be calculated from the date of passing the final acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　建设工程在保修范围和保修期限内发生质量问题的，施工单位应当履行保修义务，并对造成的损失承担赔偿责任。

**Article 41** Where a quality problem occurs within the scope and period of warranty of a construction project, the construction entity shall perform the warranty obligations and bear compensation liability for the losses caused.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　建设工程在超过合理使用年限后需要继续使用的，产权所有人应当委托具有相应资质等级的勘察、设计单位鉴定，并根据鉴定结果采取加固、维修等措施，重新界定使用期。

**Article 42** Where a construction project needs to continue to be used after its reasonable service life has expired, the owner of the property rights shall appoint a survey or design entity with the corresponding qualification grade to carry out an expert evaluation, and based on the evaluation results, take measures such as reinforcement and repair, and redefine the service period.

### 第七章　监督管理

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家实行建设工程质量监督管理制度。  
国务院建设行政主管部门对全国的建设工程质量实施统一监督管理。国务院铁路、交通、水利等有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责对全国的有关专业建设工程质量的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门对本行政区域内的建设工程质量实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府交通、水利等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责对本行政区域内的专业建设工程质量的监督管理。

**Article 43** The State implements a supervision and administration system for construction project quality.  
The competent administrative department of construction of the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the quality of construction projects nationwide. The relevant departments of the State Council such as railway, transport, and water resources shall, in accordance with the division of responsibilities stipulated by the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality of relevant specialized construction projects nationwide.  
The competent administrative department of construction of the local people's government at or above the county level shall exercise supervision and administration over the quality of construction projects within its administrative region. The relevant departments such as transport and water resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and responsibilities, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality of specialized construction projects within their administrative regions.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院建设行政主管部门和国务院铁路、交通、水利等有关部门应当加强对有关建设工程质量的法律、法规和强制性标准执行情况的监督检查。

**Article 44** The competent administrative department of construction of the State Council and the relevant departments of the State Council such as railway, transport, and water resources shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of laws, regulations, and mandatory standards related to construction project quality.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院发展计划部门按照国务院规定的职责，组织稽察特派员，对国家出资的重大建设项目实施监督检查。  
国务院经济贸易主管部门按照国务院规定的职责，对国家重大技术改造项目实施监督检查。

**Article 45** The development planning department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and responsibilities stipulated by the State Council, organize inspection commissioners to carry out supervision and inspection over major construction projects funded by the State.  
The competent economic and trade department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and responsibilities stipulated by the State Council, carry out supervision and inspection over major technological transformation projects of the State.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　建设工程质量监督管理，可以由建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门委托的建设工程质量监督机构具体实施。  
从事房屋建筑工程和市政基础设施工程质量监督的机构，必须按照国家有关规定经国务院建设行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府建设行政主管部门考核；从事专业建设工程质量监督的机构，必须按照国家有关规定经国务院有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门考核。经考核合格后，方可实施质量监督。

**Article 46** The supervision and administration of construction project quality may be specifically implemented by a construction project quality supervision agency entrusted by the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments.  
An agency engaged in quality supervision of building construction and municipal infrastructure projects must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, pass an assessment by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council or the competent administrative department of construction of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; an agency engaged in quality supervision of specialized construction projects must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, pass an assessment by the relevant department of the State Council or the relevant department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. Only after passing the assessment may it implement quality supervision.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当加强对有关建设工程质量的法律、法规和强制性标准执行情况的监督检查。

**Article 47** The competent administrative department of construction of the local people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the implementation of laws, regulations, and mandatory standards related to construction project quality.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)要求被检查的单位提供有关工程质量的文件和资料；  
(二)进入被检查单位的施工现场进行检查；  
(三)发现有影响工程质量的问题时，责令改正。

**Article 48** When performing supervision and inspection duties, the competent administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) require the entity under inspection to provide documents and materials related to the project quality;  
(2) enter the construction site of the entity under inspection to carry out inspection;  
(3) order rectification when problems affecting project quality are discovered.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　建设单位应当自建设工程竣工验收合格之日起15日内，将建设工程竣工验收报告和规划、公安消防、环保等部门出具的认可文件或者准许使用文件报建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门备案。  
建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门发现建设单位在竣工验收过程中有违反国家有关建设工程质量管理规定行为的，责令停止使用，重新组织竣工验收。

**Article 49** Within 15 days from the date of passing the final acceptance inspection of a construction project, the construction entity shall submit the completion acceptance inspection report and the approval documents or permission for use documents issued by departments such as planning, public security fire protection, and environmental protection to the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments for filing.  
Where the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments discover that the construction entity has violated the provisions of the State on construction project quality management during the final acceptance inspection, it shall order the suspension of use and the re-convening of the final acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　有关单位和个人对县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门进行的监督检查应当支持与配合，不得拒绝或者阻碍建设工程质量监督检查人员依法执行职务。

**Article 50** Relevant entities and individuals shall support and cooperate with the supervision and inspection carried out by the competent administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments, and shall not refuse or obstruct the construction project quality supervision and inspection personnel from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　供水、供电、供气、公安消防等部门或者单位不得明示或者暗示建设单位、施工单位购买其指定的生产供应单位的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备。

**Article 51** Departments or entities such as water supply, electricity supply, gas supply, and public security fire protection shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct the construction entity or construction entity to purchase building materials, building components and parts, and equipment from the production and supply entities designated by them.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　建设工程发生质量事故，有关单位应当在24小时内向当地建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门报告。对重大质量事故，事故发生地的建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当按照事故类别和等级向当地人民政府和上级建设行政主管部门和其他有关部门报告。  
特别重大质量事故的调查程序按照国务院有关规定办理。

**Article 52** Where a quality accident occurs in a construction project, the relevant entity shall report to the local competent administrative department of construction and other relevant departments within 24 hours. For a major quality accident, the competent administrative department of construction and other relevant departments at the location of the accident shall, in accordance with the category and grade of the accident, report to the local people's government and the superior competent administrative department of construction and other relevant departments.  
The investigation procedures for particularly major quality accidents shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　任何单位和个人对建设工程的质量事故、质量缺陷都有权检举、控告、投诉。

**Article 53** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report, accuse, or complain about quality accidents and quality defects of construction projects.

### 第八章　罚则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，建设单位将建设工程发包给不具有相应资质等级的勘察、设计、施工单位或者委托给不具有相应资质等级的工程监理单位的，责令改正，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity contracts out a construction project to a survey, design, or construction entity without the corresponding qualification grade, or entrusts it to a project supervision entity without the corresponding qualification grade, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，建设单位将建设工程肢解发包的，责令改正，处工程合同价款0.5%以上1%以下的罚款；对全部或者部分使用国有资金的项目，并可以暂停项目执行或者暂停资金拨付。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity breaks up a construction project into parts for contracting, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 0.5% but not more than 1% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed; for projects that use wholly or partially state-owned funds, the implementation of the project may be suspended or the allocation of funds may be suspended.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，建设单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款：  
(一)迫使承包方以低于成本的价格竞标的；  
(二)任意压缩合理工期的；  
(三)明示或者暗示设计单位或者施工单位违反工程建设强制性标准，降低工程质量的；  
(四)施工图设计文件未经审查或者审查不合格，擅自施工的；  
(五)建设项目必须实行工程监理而未实行工程监理的；  
(六)未按照国家规定办理工程质量监督手续的；  
(七)明示或者暗示施工单位使用不合格的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的；  
(八)未按照国家规定将竣工验收报告、有关认可文件或者准许使用文件报送备案的。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) compelling the contractor to bid at a price lower than cost;  
(2) arbitrarily shortening the reasonable construction period;  
(3) explicitly or implicitly instructing the design entity or the construction entity to violate the mandatory standards for construction projects and thereby reduce the quality of the project;  
(4) commencing construction without examination or after failing examination of the construction drawing design documents;  
(5) failing to implement project supervision where the construction project must be subject to supervision;  
(6) failing to go through the procedures for quality supervision in accordance with the provisions of the State;  
(7) explicitly or implicitly instructing the construction entity to use substandard building materials, building components and parts, and equipment;  
(8) failing to submit the completion acceptance inspection report and relevant approval documents or permission for use documents for filing in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，建设单位未取得施工许可证或者开工报告未经批准，擅自施工的，责令停止施工，限期改正，处工程合同价款1%以上2%以下的罚款。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity commences construction without obtaining a construction permit or without approval of the commencement report, it shall be ordered to suspend construction, correct within a time limit, and a fine of not less than 1% but not more than 2% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，建设单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处工程合同价款2%以上4%以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
(一)未组织竣工验收，擅自交付使用的；  
(二)验收不合格，擅自交付使用的；  
(三)对不合格的建设工程按照合格工程验收的。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) delivering the project for use without organizing the final acceptance inspection;  
(2) delivering the project for use despite failing the final acceptance inspection;  
(3) accepting a substandard construction project as a standard project.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，建设工程竣工验收后，建设单位未向建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门移交建设项目档案的，责令改正，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of these Regulations, after the final acceptance inspection of a construction project, the construction entity fails to transfer the construction project archives to the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位超越本单位资质等级承揽工程的，责令停止违法行为，对勘察、设计单位或者工程监理单位处合同约定的勘察费、设计费或者监理酬金1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；对施工单位处工程合同价款2%以上4%以下的罚款，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书；有违法所得的，予以没收。  
未取得资质证书承揽工程的，予以取缔，依照前款规定处以罚款；有违法所得的，予以没收。  
以欺骗手段取得资质证书承揽工程的，吊销资质证书，依照本条第一款规定处以罚款；有违法所得的，予以没收。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a survey, design, construction, or project supervision entity undertakes a project beyond its qualification grade, it shall be ordered to cease the illegal act; for a survey, design, or project supervision entity, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the contractually agreed survey fee, design fee, or supervision remuneration shall be imposed; for a construction entity, a fine of not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed, and it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where an entity undertakes a project without obtaining a qualification certificate, it shall be banned, and a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where an entity obtains a qualification certificate by fraudulent means and undertakes a project, its qualification certificate shall be revoked, and a fine shall be imposed in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位名义承揽工程的，责令改正，没收违法所得，对勘察、设计单位和工程监理单位处合同约定的勘察费、设计费和监理酬金1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；对施工单位处工程合同价款2%以上4%以下的罚款；可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a survey, design, construction, or project supervision entity permits another entity or individual to undertake a project under its name, it shall be ordered to correct, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; for a survey, design, or project supervision entity, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the contractually agreed survey fee, design fee, and supervision remuneration shall be imposed; for a construction entity, a fine of not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed; it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本条例规定，承包单位将承包的工程转包或者违法分包的，责令改正，没收违法所得，对勘察、设计单位处合同约定的勘察费、设计费25%以上50%以下的罚款；对施工单位处工程合同价款0.5%以上1%以下的罚款；可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。  
工程监理单位转让工程监理业务的，责令改正，没收违法所得，处合同约定的监理酬金25%以上50%以下的罚款；可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a contractor subcontracts or illegally subcontracts the contracted project, it shall be ordered to correct, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated; for a survey or design entity, a fine of not less than 25% but not more than 50% of the contractually agreed survey fee or design fee shall be imposed; for a construction entity, a fine of not less than 0.5% but not more than 1% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed; it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.  
Where a project supervision entity transfers the project supervision business, it shall be ordered to correct, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 25% but not more than 50% of the contractually agreed supervision remuneration shall be imposed; it may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款：  
(一)勘察单位未按照工程建设强制性标准进行勘察的；  
(二)设计单位未根据勘察成果文件进行工程设计的；  
(三)设计单位指定建筑材料、建筑构配件的生产厂、供应商的；  
(四)设计单位未按照工程建设强制性标准进行设计的。  
有前款所列行为，造成工程质量事故的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/建设工程勘察设计管理条例.md#art-41" title="被《建设工程勘察设计管理条例》第41条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 63** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the entity shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) a survey entity fails to carry out survey in accordance with the mandatory standards for construction projects;  
(2) a design entity fails to carry out engineering design based on the survey result documents;  
(3) a design entity designates the manufacturers or suppliers of building materials and building components and parts;  
(4) a design entity fails to carry out design in accordance with the mandatory standards for construction projects.  
Where an act listed in the preceding paragraph causes a project quality accident, the entity shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade shall be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本条例规定，施工单位在施工中偷工减料的，使用不合格的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的，或者有不按照工程设计图纸或者施工技术标准施工的其他行为的，责令改正，处工程合同价款2%以上4%以下的罚款；造成建设工程质量不符合规定的质量标准的，负责返工、修理，并赔偿因此造成的损失；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书。

**Article 64** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity cheats on materials or skimps on work, uses substandard building materials, building components and parts, and equipment, or commits other acts of not constructing in accordance with the engineering design drawings or construction technical standards, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the contract price of the project shall be imposed; if the quality of the construction project fails to meet the specified quality standards, it shall be responsible for rework and repair, and compensate for the losses caused thereby; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade shall be lowered or its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例规定，施工单位未对建筑材料、建筑构配件、设备和商品混凝土进行检验，或者未对涉及结构安全的试块、试件以及有关材料取样检测的，责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity fails to inspect building materials, building components and parts, equipment, and commercial concrete, or fails to take and test samples of test blocks, test pieces, and related materials involving structural safety, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification and its qualification grade shall be lowered or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例规定，施工单位不履行保修义务或者拖延履行保修义务的，责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，并对在保修期内因质量缺陷造成的损失承担赔偿责任。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity fails to perform the warranty obligations or delays in performing the warranty obligations, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it shall bear compensation liability for losses caused by quality defects within the warranty period.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　工程监理单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；有违法所得的，予以没收；造成损失的，承担连带赔偿责任：  
(一)与建设单位或者施工单位串通，弄虚作假、降低工程质量的；  
(二)将不合格的建设工程、建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备按照合格签字的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/土地城建/民用建筑节能条例.md#art-42" title="被《民用建筑节能条例》第42条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 67** Where a project supervision entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed, and its qualification grade shall be lowered or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if losses are caused, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability:  
(1) colluding with the construction entity or the construction entity to practice fraud and reduce project quality;  
(2) signing off on substandard construction projects, building materials, building components and parts, and equipment as standard.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例规定，工程监理单位与被监理工程的施工承包单位以及建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备供应单位有隶属关系或者其他利害关系承担该项建设工程的监理业务的，责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；有违法所得的，予以没收。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a project supervision entity undertakes the supervision business of a construction project while having a subordinate relationship or other conflict of interest with the construction contractor or the supplier of building materials, building components and parts, and equipment of the supervised project, it shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and its qualification grade shall be lowered or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本条例规定，涉及建筑主体或者承重结构变动的装修工程，没有设计方案擅自施工的，责令改正，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；房屋建筑使用者在装修过程中擅自变动房屋建筑主体和承重结构的，责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
有前款所列行为，造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 69** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a decoration project involving changes to the main body or load-bearing structure of a building is constructed without a design proposal, the entity shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the user of a building arbitrarily changes the main body or load-bearing structure of the building during the decoration process, the user shall be ordered to correct, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where an act listed in the preceding paragraph causes losses, the entity shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　发生重大工程质量事故隐瞒不报、谎报或者拖延报告期限的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他责任人员依法给予行政处分。

**Article 70** Where a major project quality accident occurs and there is concealment, false reporting, or delay in reporting, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本条例规定，供水、供电、供气、公安消防等部门或者单位明示或者暗示建设单位或者施工单位购买其指定的生产供应单位的建筑材料、建筑构配件和设备的，责令改正。

**Article 71** Where, in violation of these Regulations, departments or entities such as water supply, electricity supply, gas supply, and public security fire protection explicitly or implicitly instruct the construction entity or construction entity to purchase building materials, building components and parts, and equipment from the production and supply entities designated by them, they shall be ordered to correct.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例规定，注册建筑师、注册结构工程师、监理工程师等注册执业人员因过错造成质量事故的，责令停止执业1年；造成重大质量事故的，吊销执业资格证书，5年以内不予注册；情节特别恶劣的，终身不予注册。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a registered practicing personnel such as a registered architect, registered structural engineer, or supervising engineer causes a quality accident due to fault, the person shall be ordered to suspend practice for one year; if a major quality accident is caused, the practice qualification certificate shall be revoked, and the person shall not be registered within five years; if the circumstances are particularly egregious, the person shall not be registered for life.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　依照本条例规定，给予单位罚款处罚的，对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处单位罚款数额5%以上10%以下的罚款。

**Article 73** Where a fine is imposed on an entity in accordance with these Regulations, a fine of not less than 5% but not more than 10% of the amount of the fine imposed on the entity shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons of the entity.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　建设单位、设计单位、施工单位、工程监理单位违反国家规定，降低工程质量标准，造成重大安全事故，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** Where a construction entity, design entity, construction entity, or project supervision entity violates the provisions of the State, lowers the project quality standards, and causes a major safety accident, which constitutes a crime, the directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本条例规定的责令停业整顿，降低资质等级和吊销资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书的机关决定；其他行政处罚，由建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依照法定职权决定。  
依照本条例规定被吊销资质证书的，由工商行政管理部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 75** The administrative penalties of ordering suspension of operations for rectification, lowering of qualification grade, and revocation of qualification certificate as provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the authority that issued the qualification certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments in accordance with their statutory functions and powers.  
Where a qualification certificate is revoked in accordance with these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国家机关工作人员在建设工程质量监督管理工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 76** Where state organ functionaries, in the work of supervision and administration of construction project quality, neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or engage in malpractices for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, they shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, they shall be given an administrative sanction according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　建设、勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位的工作人员因调动工作、退休等原因离开该单位后，被发现在该单位工作期间违反国家有关建设工程质量管理规定，造成重大工程质量事故的，仍应当依法追究法律责任。

**Article 77** Where a staff member of a construction, survey, design, construction, or project supervision entity, after leaving the entity due to job transfer, retirement, or other reasons, is found to have violated the provisions of the State on construction project quality management during his or her period of work in the entity and caused a major project quality accident, legal liability shall still be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　本条例所称肢解发包，是指建设单位将应当由一个承包单位完成的建设工程分解成若干部分发包给不同的承包单位的行为。  
本条例所称违法分包，是指下列行为：  
(一)总承包单位将建设工程分包给不具备相应资质条件的单位的；  
(二)建设工程总承包合同中未有约定，又未经建设单位认可，承包单位将其承包的部分建设工程交由其他单位完成的；  
(三)施工总承包单位将建设工程主体结构的施工分包给其他单位的；  
(四)分包单位将其承包的建设工程再分包的。  
本条例所称转包，是指承包单位承包建设工程后，不履行合同约定的责任和义务，将其承包的全部建设工程转给他人或者将其承包的全部建设工程肢解以后以分包的名义分别转给其他单位承包的行为。

**Article 78** For the purposes of these Regulations, 'breaking up a construction project into parts for contracting' means an act by which a construction entity divides a construction project that should be completed by one contractor into several parts and contracts them out to different contractors.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'illegal subcontracting' means any of the following acts:  
(1) a general contractor subcontracts a construction project to an entity that does not have the corresponding qualification conditions;  
(2) where there is no agreement in the general construction contract and without the consent of the construction entity, the contractor assigns part of the contracted construction project to another entity for completion;  
(3) the general construction contractor subcontracts the construction of the main structure of the construction project to another entity;  
(4) a subcontractor further subcontracts the construction project it has contracted.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, 'subcontracting' means an act by which, after contracting a construction project, the contractor, without performing the responsibilities and obligations stipulated in the contract, transfers all of the contracted construction project to another person or divides all of the contracted construction project into parts and respectively transfers them to other entities for contracting under the guise of subcontracting.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　本条例规定的罚款和没收的违法所得，必须全部上缴国库。

**Article 79** All fines and confiscated illegal gains prescribed in these Regulations must be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　抢险救灾及其他临时性房屋建筑和农民自建低层住宅的建设活动，不适用本条例。

**Article 80** These Regulations shall not apply to construction activities for emergency rescue and disaster relief, other temporary building construction, and low-rise residential buildings built by farmers themselves.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　军事建设工程的管理，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 81** The management of military construction projects shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 82** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
