# 城镇燃气管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2016-02-06  
**生效日期**：2011-03-01

> (2010年11月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第583号公布　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强城镇燃气管理，保障燃气供应，防止和减少燃气安全事故，保障公民生命、财产安全和公共安全，维护燃气经营者和燃气用户的合法权益，促进燃气事业健康发展，制定本条例。 第二条　城镇燃气发展规划与应急保障、燃气经营与服务、燃气使用、燃气设施保护、燃气安全事故预防与处理及相关管理活动，适用本条例。 天然气、液化石油气的生产和进口，城市门站以外的天然气管道输送，燃气作为工业生产原料的使用，沼气、秸秆气的生产和使用，不适用本条例。 本条例所称燃气，是指作为燃料使用并符合一定要求的气体燃料，包括天然气(含煤层气)、液化石油气和人工煤气等。 第三条　燃气工作应当坚持统筹规划、保障安全、确保供应、规范服务、节能高效的原则。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对燃气工作的领导，并将燃气工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。 第五条　国务院建设主管部门负责全国的燃气管理工作。 县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门负责本行政区域内的燃气管理工作。 县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例和其他有关法律、法规的规定，在各自职责范围内负责有关燃气管理工作。 第六条　国家鼓励、支持燃气科学技术研究，推广使用安全、节能、高效、环保的燃气新技术、新工艺和新产品。 第七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当建立健全燃气安全监督管理制度，宣传普及燃气法律、法规和安全知识，提高全民的燃气安全意识。 第二章　燃气发展规划与应急保障 第八条　国务院建设主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门，依据国民经济和社会发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划以及能源规划，结合全国燃气资源总量平衡情况，组织编制全国燃气发展规划并组织实施。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城镇燃气管理，保障燃气供应，防止和减少燃气安全事故，保障公民生命、财产安全和公共安全，维护燃气经营者和燃气用户的合法权益，促进燃气事业健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of urban gas, ensure gas supply, prevent and reduce gas safety accidents, safeguard citizens' life, property safety and public safety, protect the legitimate rights and interests of gas operators and gas users, and promote the healthy development of the gas industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　城镇燃气发展规划与应急保障、燃气经营与服务、燃气使用、燃气设施保护、燃气安全事故预防与处理及相关管理活动，适用本条例。  
天然气、液化石油气的生产和进口，城市门站以外的天然气管道输送，燃气作为工业生产原料的使用，沼气、秸秆气的生产和使用，不适用本条例。  
本条例所称燃气，是指作为燃料使用并符合一定要求的气体燃料，包括天然气(含煤层气)、液化石油气和人工煤气等。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the planning and emergency support for urban gas development, gas operation and service, gas use, gas facility protection, prevention and handling of gas safety accidents, and related management activities.  
The production and import of natural gas and liquefied petroleum gas, the pipeline transportation of natural gas beyond the city gate station, the use of gas as an industrial production raw material, and the production and use of biogas and straw gas shall not be governed by these Regulations.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "gas" refers to gaseous fuels used as fuel and meeting certain requirements, including natural gas (including coalbed methane), liquefied petroleum gas, and manufactured coal gas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　燃气工作应当坚持统筹规划、保障安全、确保供应、规范服务、节能高效的原则。

**Article 3** The gas work shall adhere to the principles of overall planning, safety assurance, supply guarantee, standardized service, energy conservation and efficiency.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对燃气工作的领导，并将燃气工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 4** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the leadership over the gas work and incorporate the gas work into the national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院建设主管部门负责全国的燃气管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门负责本行政区域内的燃气管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例和其他有关法律、法规的规定，在各自职责范围内负责有关燃气管理工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of construction of the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide gas management.  
The gas management departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the gas management within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with these Regulations and other relevant laws and regulations, be responsible for the related gas management within their respective duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励、支持燃气科学技术研究，推广使用安全、节能、高效、环保的燃气新技术、新工艺和新产品。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on gas, and promotes the use of safe, energy-saving, efficient and environmentally friendly new gas technologies, new processes and new products.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当建立健全燃气安全监督管理制度，宣传普及燃气法律、法规和安全知识，提高全民的燃气安全意识。

**Article 7** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the gas safety supervision and management system, publicize and popularize gas laws, regulations and safety knowledge, and enhance the gas safety awareness of the whole population.

### 第二章　燃气发展规划与应急保障

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院建设主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门，依据国民经济和社会发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划以及能源规划，结合全国燃气资源总量平衡情况，组织编制全国燃气发展规划并组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门应当会同有关部门，依据国民经济和社会发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划、能源规划以及上一级燃气发展规划，组织编制本行政区域的燃气发展规划，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施，并报上一级人民政府燃气管理部门备案。

**Article 8** The competent department of construction of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, and in accordance with the national economic and social development plan, the overall land use plan, the urban and rural plan and the energy plan, taking into account the overall balance of national gas resources, organize the formulation of the national gas development plan and organize its implementation.  
The gas management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the relevant departments, and in accordance with the national economic and social development plan, the overall land use plan, the urban and rural plan, the energy plan and the gas development plan at the next higher level, organize the formulation of the gas development plan for its administrative region, and submit it to the people's government at the same level for approval before organizing its implementation, and shall report it to the gas management department of the people's government at the next higher level for record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　燃气发展规划的内容应当包括：燃气气源、燃气种类、燃气供应方式和规模、燃气设施布局和建设时序、燃气设施建设用地、燃气设施保护范围、燃气供应保障措施和安全保障措施等。

**Article 9** The contents of the gas development plan shall include: gas sources, gas types, gas supply methods and scale, layout and construction sequence of gas facilities, land for gas facility construction, protection scope of gas facilities, gas supply guarantee measures and safety guarantee measures, etc.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据燃气发展规划的要求，加大对燃气设施建设的投入，并鼓励社会资金投资建设燃气设施。

**Article 10** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the gas development plan, increase investment in the construction of gas facilities and encourage social funds to invest in the construction of gas facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　进行新区建设、旧区改造，应当按照城乡规划和燃气发展规划配套建设燃气设施或者预留燃气设施建设用地。  
对燃气发展规划范围内的燃气设施建设工程，城乡规划主管部门在依法核发选址意见书时，应当就燃气设施建设是否符合燃气发展规划征求燃气管理部门的意见；不需要核发选址意见书的，城乡规划主管部门在依法核发建设用地规划许可证或者乡村建设规划许可证时，应当就燃气设施建设是否符合燃气发展规划征求燃气管理部门的意见。  
燃气设施建设工程竣工后，建设单位应当依法组织竣工验收，并自竣工验收合格之日起15日内，将竣工验收情况报燃气管理部门备案。

**Article 11** For the construction of new areas and renovation of old areas, gas facilities shall be constructed or land for gas facilities shall be reserved in accordance with the urban and rural planning and the gas development plan.  
With respect to the construction of gas facilities within the scope of the gas development plan, when lawfully issuing the site selection opinion, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall seek the opinion of the gas regulatory department on whether the construction of gas facilities conforms to the gas development plan; if no site selection opinion is required to be issued, when lawfully issuing the construction land planning permit or the rural construction planning permit, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall seek the opinion of the gas regulatory department on whether the construction of gas facilities conforms to the gas development plan.  
After the completion of the gas facility construction project, the construction unit shall lawfully organize the completion acceptance, and within 15 days from the date of passing the completion acceptance, report the completion acceptance to the gas regulatory department for recordation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立健全燃气应急储备制度，组织编制燃气应急预案，采取综合措施提高燃气应急保障能力。  
燃气应急预案应当明确燃气应急气源和种类、应急供应方式、应急处置程序和应急救援措施等内容。  
县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门应当会同有关部门对燃气供求状况实施监测、预测和预警。

**Article 12** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the gas emergency reserve system, organize the formulation of gas emergency plans, and take comprehensive measures to enhance the gas emergency support capability.  
The gas emergency plan shall specify the emergency gas sources and types, emergency supply methods, emergency response procedures, and emergency rescue measures, among other contents.  
The gas regulatory department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments, monitor, forecast, and issue early warnings on the gas supply and demand situation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　燃气供应严重短缺、供应中断等突发事件发生后，县级以上地方人民政府应当及时采取动用储备、紧急调度等应急措施，燃气经营者以及其他有关单位和个人应当予以配合，承担相关应急任务。

**Article 13** After the occurrence of emergencies such as severe shortage of gas supply or supply interruption, the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly take emergency measures such as using reserves and emergency dispatch, and gas operators and other relevant units and individuals shall cooperate and undertake relevant emergency tasks.

### 第三章　燃气经营与服务

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　政府投资建设的燃气设施，应当通过招标投标方式选择燃气经营者。  
社会资金投资建设的燃气设施，投资方可以自行经营，也可以另行选择燃气经营者。

**Article 14** For gas facilities built with government investment, gas operators shall be selected through tendering and bidding.  
For gas facilities built with social funds, the investor may operate the facilities by itself, or may select another gas operator.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家对燃气经营实行许可证制度。从事燃气经营活动的企业，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)符合燃气发展规划要求；  
(二)有符合国家标准的燃气气源和燃气设施；  
(三)有固定的经营场所、完善的安全管理制度和健全的经营方案；  
(四)企业的主要负责人、安全生产管理人员以及运行、维护和抢修人员经专业培训并考核合格；  
(五)法律、法规规定的其他条件。  
符合前款规定条件的，由县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门核发燃气经营许可证。

**Article 15** The State implements a licensing system for gas operation. An enterprise engaged in gas operation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) conform to the requirements of the gas development plan;  
(2) have gas sources and gas facilities that meet national standards;  
(3) have a fixed business site, a comprehensive safety management system, and a sound business plan;  
(4) the principal responsible person, safety production management personnel, and operation, maintenance, and emergency repair personnel of the enterprise have received professional training and passed the assessment;  
(5) other conditions as provided by laws and regulations.  
Where the conditions set forth in the preceding paragraph are met, the gas regulatory department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall issue a gas operation license.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　禁止个人从事管道燃气经营活动。  
个人从事瓶装燃气经营活动的，应当遵守省、自治区、直辖市的有关规定。

**Article 16** Individuals are prohibited from engaging in pipeline gas operation.  
Where an individual engages in bottled gas operation, the individual shall comply with the relevant provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　燃气经营者应当向燃气用户持续、稳定、安全供应符合国家质量标准的燃气，指导燃气用户安全用气、节约用气，并对燃气设施定期进行安全检查。  
燃气经营者应当公示业务流程、服务承诺、收费标准和服务热线等信息，并按照国家燃气服务标准提供服务。

**Article 17** A gas operator shall supply gas that meets national quality standards to gas users continuously, stably, and safely, guide gas users to use gas safely and economically, and conduct regular safety inspections on gas facilities.  
A gas operator shall publicize information such as business processes, service commitments, charging standards, and service hotlines, and provide services in accordance with the national gas service standards.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　燃气经营者不得有下列行为：  
(一)拒绝向市政燃气管网覆盖范围内符合用气条件的单位或者个人供气；  
(二)倒卖、抵押、出租、出借、转让、涂改燃气经营许可证；  
(三)未履行必要告知义务擅自停止供气、调整供气量，或者未经审批擅自停业或者歇业；  
(四)向未取得燃气经营许可证的单位或者个人提供用于经营的燃气；  
(五)在不具备安全条件的场所储存燃气；  
(六)要求燃气用户购买其指定的产品或者接受其提供的服务；  
(七)擅自为非自有气瓶充装燃气；  
(八)销售未经许可的充装单位充装的瓶装燃气或者销售充装单位擅自为非自有气瓶充装的瓶装燃气；  
(九)冒用其他企业名称或者标识从事燃气经营、服务活动。

**Article 18** A gas operator shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) refusing to supply gas to units or individuals within the coverage of the municipal gas pipeline network that meet the conditions for gas use;  
(2) scalping, mortgaging, leasing, lending, transferring, or altering the gas operation license;  
(3) stopping gas supply or adjusting the gas supply volume without authorization without fulfilling the necessary notification obligations, or suspending business or ceasing operations without approval;  
(4) providing gas for operation to units or individuals that have not obtained a gas operation license;  
(5) storing gas in places that do not meet safety conditions;  
(6) requiring gas users to purchase its designated products or accept services provided by it;  
(7) filling gas into non-owned cylinders without authorization;  
(8) selling bottled gas filled by unlicensed filling units or selling bottled gas filled by filling units into non-owned cylinders without authorization;  
(9) using another enterprise's name or logo to engage in gas operation or service activities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　管道燃气经营者对其供气范围内的市政燃气设施、建筑区划内业主专有部分以外的燃气设施，承担运行、维护、抢修和更新改造的责任。  
管道燃气经营者应当按照供气、用气合同的约定，对单位燃气用户的燃气设施承担相应的管理责任。

**Article 19** A pipeline gas operator shall bear the responsibility for operation, maintenance, emergency repair, and renovation of municipal gas facilities within its gas supply scope and gas facilities other than the exclusive parts of owners within the building zone.  
A pipeline gas operator shall, in accordance with the gas supply and use contract, bear the corresponding management responsibility for the gas facilities of unit gas users.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　管道燃气经营者因施工、检修等原因需要临时调整供气量或者暂停供气的，应当将作业时间和影响区域提前48小时予以公告或者书面通知燃气用户，并按照有关规定及时恢复正常供气；因突发事件影响供气的，应当采取紧急措施并及时通知燃气用户。  
燃气经营者停业、歇业的，应当事先对其供气范围内的燃气用户的正常用气作出妥善安排，并在90个工作日前向所在地燃气管理部门报告，经批准方可停业、歇业。

**Article 20** Where a pipeline gas operator needs to temporarily adjust the gas supply volume or suspend gas supply due to construction, maintenance, or other reasons, it shall announce in advance or notify gas users in writing of the operation time and affected area 48 hours in advance, and promptly resume normal gas supply in accordance with relevant regulations; where gas supply is affected by an emergency, it shall take emergency measures and promptly notify gas users.  
Where a gas operator suspends business or ceases operations, it shall make proper arrangements for the normal gas use of gas users within its gas supply scope in advance, and report to the local gas regulatory department 90 working days in advance, and may suspend business or cease operations only after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列情况之一的，燃气管理部门应当采取措施，保障燃气用户的正常用气：  
(一)管道燃气经营者临时调整供气量或者暂停供气未及时恢复正常供气的；  
(二)管道燃气经营者因突发事件影响供气未采取紧急措施的；  
(三)燃气经营者擅自停业、歇业的；  
(四)燃气管理部门依法撤回、撤销、注销、吊销燃气经营许可的。

**Article 21** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the gas管理部门 shall take measures to ensure the normal gas use of gas users:  
(1) The pipeline gas operator temporarily adjusts the gas supply volume or suspends gas supply without timely restoring normal gas supply;  
(2) The pipeline gas operator fails to take emergency measures when gas supply is affected due to an emergency;  
(3) The gas operator suspends business or ceases business without authorization;  
(4) The gas管理部门 revokes, cancels, or withdraws the gas operation license according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　燃气经营者应当建立健全燃气质量检测制度，确保所供应的燃气质量符合国家标准。  
县级以上地方人民政府质量监督、工商行政管理、燃气管理等部门应当按照职责分工，依法加强对燃气质量的监督检查。

**Article 22** A gas operator shall establish and improve a gas quality inspection system to ensure that the gas supplied complies with national standards.  
The quality supervision, industrial and commercial administration, and gas管理部门 of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, strengthen the supervision and inspection of gas quality according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　燃气销售价格，应当根据购气成本、经营成本和当地经济社会发展水平合理确定并适时调整。县级以上地方人民政府价格主管部门确定和调整管道燃气销售价格，应当征求管道燃气用户、管道燃气经营者和有关方面的意见。

**Article 23** The gas sales price shall be reasonably determined and adjusted in a timely manner based on gas purchase costs, operating costs, and the local economic and social development level. When the price department of the local people's government at or above the county level determines and adjusts the pipeline gas sales price, it shall solicit opinions from pipeline gas users, pipeline gas operators, and relevant parties.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　通过道路、水路、铁路运输燃气的，应当遵守法律、行政法规有关危险货物运输安全的规定以及国务院交通运输部门、国务院铁路部门的有关规定；通过道路或者水路运输燃气的，还应当分别依照有关道路运输、水路运输的法律、行政法规的规定，取得危险货物道路运输许可或者危险货物水路运输许可。

**Article 24** Where gas is transported by road, waterway, or railway, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations concerning the safety of dangerous goods transport, as well as the relevant provisions of the transport department of the State Council and the railway department of the State Council, shall be complied with; where gas is transported by road or waterway, the dangerous goods road transport permit or dangerous goods waterway transport permit shall also be obtained respectively in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on road transport or waterway transport.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　燃气经营者应当对其从事瓶装燃气送气服务的人员和车辆加强管理，并承担相应的责任。  
从事瓶装燃气充装活动，应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家标准有关气瓶充装的规定。

**Article 25** A gas operator shall strengthen the management of its personnel and vehicles engaged in bottled gas delivery services and shall bear corresponding liabilities.  
Engaging in bottled gas filling activities shall comply with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and national standards concerning cylinder filling.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　燃气经营者应当依法经营，诚实守信，接受社会公众的监督。  
燃气行业协会应当加强行业自律管理，促进燃气经营者提高服务质量和技术水平。

**Article 26** A gas operator shall operate according to law, act in good faith, and accept supervision by the public.  
The gas industry association shall strengthen industry self-discipline management and promote gas operators to improve service quality and technical levels.

### 第四章　燃气使用

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　燃气用户应当遵守安全用气规则，使用合格的燃气燃烧器具和气瓶，及时更换国家明令淘汰或者使用年限已届满的燃气燃烧器具、连接管等，并按照约定期限支付燃气费用。  
单位燃气用户还应当建立健全安全管理制度，加强对操作维护人员燃气安全知识和操作技能的培训。

**Article 27** A gas user shall observe the rules for safe gas use, use qualified gas combustion appliances and cylinders, timely replace gas combustion appliances, connecting pipes, etc. that have been explicitly eliminated by the state or have exceeded their service life, and pay gas fees within the agreed period.  
An institutional gas user shall also establish and improve safety management systems and strengthen the training of operation and maintenance personnel in gas safety knowledge and operational skills.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　燃气用户及相关单位和个人不得有下列行为：  
(一)擅自操作公用燃气阀门；  
(二)将燃气管道作为负重支架或者接地引线；  
(三)安装、使用不符合气源要求的燃气燃烧器具；  
(四)擅自安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施和燃气计量装置；  
(五)在不具备安全条件的场所使用、储存燃气；  
(六)盗用燃气；  
(七)改变燃气用途或者转供燃气。

**Article 28** A gas user and any relevant entity or individual shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) Operating public gas valves without authorization;  
(2) Using gas pipelines as load-bearing supports or grounding conductors;  
(3) Installing or using gas combustion appliances that do not meet the gas source requirements;  
(4) Installing, modifying, or removing indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices without authorization;  
(5) Using or storing gas in places that do not meet safety conditions;  
(6) Stealing gas;  
(7) Changing the purpose of gas use or resupplying gas.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　燃气用户有权就燃气收费、服务等事项向燃气经营者进行查询，燃气经营者应当自收到查询申请之日起5个工作日内予以答复。  
燃气用户有权就燃气收费、服务等事项向县级以上地方人民政府价格主管部门、燃气管理部门以及其他有关部门进行投诉，有关部门应当自收到投诉之日起15个工作日内予以处理。

**Article 29** A gas user shall have the right to inquire with the gas operator about gas fees, services, and other matters, and the gas operator shall reply within 5 working days from the date of receipt of the inquiry.  
A gas user shall have the right to lodge complaints with the price department, the gas管理部门, and other relevant departments of the local people's government at or above the county level regarding gas fees, services, and other matters, and the relevant departments shall handle the complaint within 15 working days from the date of receipt.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施的，应当按照国家有关工程建设标准实施作业。

**Article 30** Where indoor gas facilities are installed, modified, or removed, the operation shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant national engineering construction standards.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　燃气管理部门应当向社会公布本行政区域内的燃气种类和气质成分等信息。  
燃气燃烧器具生产单位应当在燃气燃烧器具上明确标识所适应的燃气种类。

**Article 31** The gas management department shall publish to the public information on the types and gas quality components of gas within its administrative region.  
Gas-burning appliance manufacturers shall clearly mark on the gas-burning appliances the types of gas they are suitable for.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　燃气燃烧器具生产单位、销售单位应当设立或者委托设立售后服务站点，配备经考核合格的燃气燃烧器具安装、维修人员，负责售后的安装、维修服务。  
燃气燃烧器具的安装、维修，应当符合国家有关标准。

**Article 32** Gas-burning appliance manufacturers and sellers shall establish or authorize the establishment of after-sales service stations, and deploy qualified gas-burning appliance installation and maintenance personnel who have passed assessment, responsible for after-sale installation and maintenance services.  
The installation and maintenance of gas-burning appliances shall comply with relevant national standards.

### 第五章　燃气设施保护

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门应当会同城乡规划等有关部门按照国家有关标准和规定划定燃气设施保护范围，并向社会公布。  
在燃气设施保护范围内，禁止从事下列危及燃气设施安全的活动：  
(一)建设占压地下燃气管线的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施；  
(二)进行爆破、取土等作业或者动用明火；  
(三)倾倒、排放腐蚀性物质；  
(四)放置易燃易爆危险物品或者种植深根植物；  
(五)其他危及燃气设施安全的活动。

**Article 33** The gas management department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with urban and rural planning and other relevant departments, delimit the protection scope of gas facilities in accordance with national standards and regulations, and publish it to the public.  
Within the protection scope of gas facilities, the following activities endangering the safety of gas facilities are prohibited:  
(1) Constructing buildings, structures or other facilities that occupy and pressurize underground gas pipelines;  
(2) Carrying out operations such as blasting, earth taking, or using open flames;  
(3) Dumping or discharging corrosive substances;  
(4) Placing flammable and explosive dangerous goods or planting deep-rooted plants;  
(5) Other activities endangering the safety of gas facilities.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　在燃气设施保护范围内，有关单位从事敷设管道、打桩、顶进、挖掘、钻探等可能影响燃气设施安全活动的，应当与燃气经营者共同制定燃气设施保护方案，并采取相应的安全保护措施。

**Article 34** Where, within the protection scope of gas facilities, relevant units engage in activities such as laying pipelines, pile driving, jacking, excavation, drilling, etc., that may affect the safety of gas facilities, they shall jointly formulate a gas facility protection plan with the gas operator and adopt corresponding safety protection measures.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　燃气经营者应当按照国家有关工程建设标准和安全生产管理的规定，设置燃气设施防腐、绝缘、防雷、降压、隔离等保护装置和安全警示标志，定期进行巡查、检测、维修和维护，确保燃气设施的安全运行。

**Article 35** The gas operator shall, in accordance with national standards for engineering construction and regulations on work safety management, install protective devices and safety warning signs such as anti-corrosion, insulation, lightning protection, pressure reduction, and isolation for gas facilities, and regularly conduct inspections, tests, repairs, and maintenance to ensure the safe operation of gas facilities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　任何单位和个人不得侵占、毁损、擅自拆除或者移动燃气设施，不得毁损、覆盖、涂改、擅自拆除或者移动燃气设施安全警示标志。  
任何单位和个人发现有可能危及燃气设施和安全警示标志的行为，有权予以劝阻、制止；经劝阻、制止无效的，应当立即告知燃气经营者或者向燃气管理部门、安全生产监督管理部门和公安机关报告。

**Article 36** No unit or individual shall encroach upon, damage, dismantle without authorization, or move gas facilities, nor damage, cover, alter, dismantle without authorization, or move safety warning signs for gas facilities.  
Any unit or individual that discovers acts that may endanger gas facilities and safety warning signs may dissuade or stop such acts; if dissuasion or stopping is ineffective, it shall immediately inform the gas operator or report to the gas management department, the work safety supervision and management department, and the public security organ.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　新建、扩建、改建建设工程，不得影响燃气设施安全。  
建设单位在开工前，应当查明建设工程施工范围内地下燃气管线的相关情况；燃气管理部门以及其他有关部门和单位应当及时提供相关资料。  
建设工程施工范围内有地下燃气管线等重要燃气设施的，建设单位应当会同施工单位与管道燃气经营者共同制定燃气设施保护方案。建设单位、施工单位应当采取相应的安全保护措施，确保燃气设施运行安全；管道燃气经营者应当派专业人员进行现场指导。法律、法规另有规定的，依照有关法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 37** Construction of new projects, expansion, or renovation of existing projects shall not affect the safety of gas facilities.  
Before construction commences, the construction unit shall ascertain the relevant information of underground gas pipelines within the construction scope; the gas management department and other relevant departments and units shall promptly provide relevant materials.  
Where there are underground gas pipelines and other important gas facilities within the construction scope, the construction unit shall, together with the construction contractor, jointly formulate a gas facility protection plan with the pipeline gas operator. The construction unit and the construction contractor shall adopt corresponding safety protection measures to ensure the safe operation of gas facilities; the pipeline gas operator shall dispatch professional personnel for on-site guidance. Where laws and regulations provide otherwise, the provisions of such laws and regulations shall prevail.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　燃气经营者改动市政燃气设施，应当制定改动方案，报县级以上地方人民政府燃气管理部门批准。  
改动方案应当符合燃气发展规划，明确安全施工要求，有安全防护和保障正常用气的措施。

**Article 38** Where a gas operator modifies municipal gas facilities, it shall formulate a modification plan and submit it to the gas management department of the local people's government at or above the county level for approval.  
The modification plan shall conform to the gas development plan, specify safety construction requirements, and include measures for safety protection and ensuring normal gas use.

### 第六章　燃气安全事故预防与处理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　燃气管理部门应当会同有关部门制定燃气安全事故应急预案，建立燃气事故统计分析制度，定期通报事故处理结果。  
燃气经营者应当制定本单位燃气安全事故应急预案，配备应急人员和必要的应急装备、器材，并定期组织演练。

**Article 39** The gas management department shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, formulate emergency response plans for gas safety accidents, establish a statistical analysis system for gas accidents, and regularly report on the results of accident handling.  
The gas operator shall formulate its own emergency response plan for gas safety accidents, equip emergency personnel and necessary emergency equipment and supplies, and regularly organize drills.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　任何单位和个人发现燃气安全事故或者燃气安全事故隐患等情况，应当立即告知燃气经营者，或者向燃气管理部门、公安机关消防机构等有关部门和单位报告。

**Article 40** Any unit or individual that discovers a gas safety accident or potential gas safety accident hazard, etc., shall immediately inform the gas operator, or report to the gas management department, the fire department of the public security organ, and other relevant departments and units.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　燃气经营者应当建立健全燃气安全评估和风险管理体系，发现燃气安全事故隐患的，应当及时采取措施消除隐患。  
燃气管理部门以及其他有关部门和单位应当根据各自职责，对燃气经营、燃气使用的安全状况等进行监督检查，发现燃气安全事故隐患的，应当通知燃气经营者、燃气用户及时采取措施消除隐患；不及时消除隐患可能严重威胁公共安全的，燃气管理部门以及其他有关部门和单位应当依法采取措施，及时组织消除隐患，有关单位和个人应当予以配合。

**Article 41** Gas business operators shall establish and improve a gas safety assessment and risk management system. Where a hidden danger of gas safety accident is discovered, they shall promptly take measures to eliminate the hidden danger.  
The gas administrative department and other relevant departments and units shall, according to their respective functions and duties, conduct supervision and inspection over the safety conditions of gas operation and gas use, etc. Where a hidden danger of gas safety accident is discovered, they shall notify the gas business operator and gas user to promptly take measures to eliminate the hidden danger; if failing to eliminate the hidden danger in time may seriously threaten public safety, the gas administrative department and other relevant departments and units shall take measures according to law and promptly organize the elimination of the hidden danger, and the relevant units and individuals shall provide cooperation.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　燃气安全事故发生后，燃气经营者应当立即启动本单位燃气安全事故应急预案，组织抢险、抢修。  
燃气安全事故发生后，燃气管理部门、安全生产监督管理部门和公安机关消防机构等有关部门和单位，应当根据各自职责，立即采取措施防止事故扩大，根据有关情况启动燃气安全事故应急预案。

**Article 42** After a gas safety accident occurs, the gas business operator shall immediately activate its own emergency plan for gas safety accidents and organize rescue and emergency repair.  
After a gas safety accident occurs, the gas administrative department, the work safety supervision and administration department, the fire control institution of the public security organ, and other relevant departments and units shall, according to their respective functions and duties, immediately take measures to prevent the accident from escalating, and activate the emergency plan for gas safety accidents based on the relevant circumstances.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　燃气安全事故经调查确定为责任事故的，应当查明原因、明确责任，并依法予以追究。  
对燃气生产安全事故，依照有关生产安全事故报告和调查处理的法律、行政法规的规定报告和调查处理。

**Article 43** Where a gas safety accident is determined to be an accident involving liability after investigation, the cause shall be ascertained, the responsibility shall be clearly defined, and the liability shall be investigated according to law.  
For gas production safety accidents, reporting and investigation and handling shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations concerning the reporting and investigation and handling of production safety accidents.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，县级以上地方人民政府及其燃气管理部门和其他有关部门，不依法作出行政许可决定或者办理批准文件的，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处的，或者有其他未依照本条例规定履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的行为构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a local people's government at or above the county level and its gas administrative department and other relevant departments fail to make an administrative license decision or handle approval documents according to law, fail to investigate illegal acts upon discovery or upon receiving reports of illegal acts, or have other acts of failing to perform their duties in accordance with these Regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if the acts of the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，未取得燃气经营许可证从事燃气经营活动的，由燃气管理部门责令停止违法行为，处5万元以上50万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，燃气经营者不按照燃气经营许可证的规定从事燃气经营活动的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正，处3万元以上20万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销燃气经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person engages in gas business activities without obtaining a gas business operation permit, the gas administrative department shall order the cessation of the illegal act, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a gas business operator engages in gas business activities not in accordance with the provisions of the gas business operation permit, the gas administrative department shall order rectification within a time limit, impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the gas business operation permit shall be revoked; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，燃气经营者有下列行为之一的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，吊销燃气经营许可证；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)拒绝向市政燃气管网覆盖范围内符合用气条件的单位或者个人供气的；  
(二)倒卖、抵押、出租、出借、转让、涂改燃气经营许可证的；  
(三)未履行必要告知义务擅自停止供气、调整供气量，或者未经审批擅自停业或者歇业的；  
(四)向未取得燃气经营许可证的单位或者个人提供用于经营的燃气的；  
(五)在不具备安全条件的场所储存燃气的；  
(六)要求燃气用户购买其指定的产品或者接受其提供的服务；  
(七)燃气经营者未向燃气用户持续、稳定、安全供应符合国家质量标准的燃气，或者未对燃气用户的燃气设施定期进行安全检查。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a gas business operator commits any of the following acts, the gas administrative department shall order rectification within a time limit, impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the gas business operation permit shall be revoked; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Refusing to supply gas to units or individuals that meet the gas use conditions within the coverage of the municipal gas pipeline network;  
(2) Scalping, mortgaging, leasing, lending, transferring, or altering the gas business operation permit;  
(3) Without fulfilling the necessary notification obligation, arbitrarily suspending gas supply or adjusting gas supply volume, or arbitrarily suspending business or closing down without approval;  
(4) Supplying gas for business operation to units or individuals that have not obtained a gas business operation permit;  
(5) Storing gas in places that do not meet safety conditions;  
(6) Requiring gas users to purchase products designated by it or accept services provided by it;  
(7) The gas business operator fails to continuously, stably, and safely supply gas that meets national quality standards to gas users, or fails to conduct regular safety inspections on gas users' gas facilities.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，擅自为非自有气瓶充装燃气或者销售未经许可的充装单位充装的瓶装燃气的，依照国家有关气瓶安全监察的规定进行处罚。  
违反本条例规定，销售充装单位擅自为非自有气瓶充装的瓶装燃气的，由燃气管理部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下罚款。  
违反本条例规定，冒用其他企业名称或者标识从事燃气经营、服务活动，依照有关反不正当竞争的法律规定进行处罚。

**Article 47** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person arbitrarily fills gas into non-self-owned gas cylinders or sells bottled gas filled by a filling unit that has not obtained a permit, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the state concerning safety supervision of gas cylinders.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person sells bottled gas that is arbitrarily filled by the filling unit into non-self-owned gas cylinders, the gas administrative department shall order corrective action and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person fraudulently uses another enterprise's name or logo to engage in gas business or service activities, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the legal provisions concerning anti-unfair competition.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，燃气经营者未按照国家有关工程建设标准和安全生产管理的规定，设置燃气设施防腐、绝缘、防雷、降压、隔离等保护装置和安全警示标志的，或者未定期进行巡查、检测、维修和维护的，或者未采取措施及时消除燃气安全事故隐患的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款。

**Article 48** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a gas business operator fails to install protective devices and safety warning signs such as anti-corrosion, insulation, lightning protection, pressure reduction, and isolation for gas facilities in accordance with the state's relevant engineering construction standards and work safety management provisions, or fails to conduct regular inspections, tests, repairs, and maintenance, or fails to take measures to promptly eliminate hidden dangers of gas safety accidents, the gas administrative department shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，燃气用户及相关单位和个人有下列行为之一的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对单位可以处10万元以下罚款，对个人可以处1000元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)擅自操作公用燃气阀门的；  
(二)将燃气管道作为负重支架或者接地引线的；  
(三)安装、使用不符合气源要求的燃气燃烧器具的；  
(四)擅自安装、改装、拆除户内燃气设施和燃气计量装置的；  
(五)在不具备安全条件的场所使用、储存燃气的；  
(六)改变燃气用途或者转供燃气的；  
(七)未设立售后服务站点或者未配备经考核合格的燃气燃烧器具安装、维修人员的；  
(八)燃气燃烧器具的安装、维修不符合国家有关标准的。  
盗用燃气的，依照有关治安管理处罚的法律规定进行处罚。

**Article 49** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a gas user and relevant units or individuals commit any of the following acts, the gas administrative department shall order rectification within a time limit; if no rectification is made within the time limit, a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan may be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Arbitrarily operating public gas valves;  
(2) Using gas pipelines as load-bearing supports or grounding leads;  
(3) Installing or using gas burning appliances that do not meet the gas source requirements;  
(4) Arbitrarily installing, modifying, or removing indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices;  
(5) Using or storing gas in places that do not meet safety conditions;  
(6) Changing the use of gas or reselling gas;  
(7) Failing to set up after-sales service stations or failing to equip with gas burning appliance installation and maintenance personnel who have passed assessment;  
(8) The installation or maintenance of gas burning appliances does not comply with the relevant state standards.  
For theft of gas, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the legal provisions concerning public security administration punishments.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，在燃气设施保护范围内从事下列活动之一的，由燃气管理部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，对单位处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，对个人处5000元以上5万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)进行爆破、取土等作业或者动用明火的；  
(二)倾倒、排放腐蚀性物质的；  
(三)放置易燃易爆物品或者种植深根植物的；  
(四)未与燃气经营者共同制定燃气设施保护方案，采取相应的安全保护措施，从事敷设管道、打桩、顶进、挖掘、钻探等可能影响燃气设施安全活动的。  
违反本条例规定，在燃气设施保护范围内建设占压地下燃气管线的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的，依照有关城乡规划的法律、行政法规的规定进行处罚。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person engages in any of the following activities within the protected area of gas facilities, the gas administrative department shall order the cessation of the illegal act, require restoration to the original condition or take other remedial measures within a time limit, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the unit, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Carrying out operations such as blasting, earth taking, or using open flames;  
(2) Dumping or discharging corrosive substances;  
(3) Placing flammable or explosive articles or planting deep-rooted plants;  
(4) Without jointly formulating a gas facility protection plan with the gas business operator and taking corresponding safety protection measures, engaging in activities such as laying pipelines, piling, jacking, excavation, drilling, etc., that may affect the safety of gas facilities.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, any person constructs buildings, structures, or other facilities that press on underground gas pipelines within the protected area of gas facilities, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the laws and administrative regulations concerning urban and rural planning.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，侵占、毁损、擅自拆除、移动燃气设施或者擅自改动市政燃气设施的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，对单位处5万元以上10万元以下罚款，对个人处5000元以上5万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，毁损、覆盖、涂改、擅自拆除或者移动燃气设施安全警示标志的，由燃气管理部门责令限期改正，恢复原状，可以处5000元以下罚款。

**Article 51** Any person who, in violation of these Regulations, encroaches upon, damages, dismantles without authorization, removes gas facilities or alters municipal gas facilities without authorization shall be ordered by the gas regulatory department to make corrections within a specified time limit, restore the facilities to their original condition or take other remedial measures, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; where losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Any person who, in violation of these Regulations, damages, covers, alters, removes or dismantles without authorization safety warning signs for gas facilities shall be ordered by the gas regulatory department to make corrections within a specified time limit and restore the signs to their original condition, and may be fined not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，建设工程施工范围内有地下燃气管线等重要燃气设施，建设单位未会同施工单位与管道燃气经营者共同制定燃气设施保护方案，或者建设单位、施工单位未采取相应的安全保护措施的，由燃气管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where, in violation of these Regulations, there are important gas facilities such as underground gas pipelines within the construction scope of a construction project, and the construction entity fails to work together with the construction contractor and the pipeline gas operator to formulate a gas facility protection plan, or the construction entity or the construction contractor fails to take corresponding safety protection measures, the gas regulatory department shall order corrections and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; where losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)燃气设施，是指人工煤气生产厂、燃气储配站、门站、气化站、混气站、加气站、灌装站、供应站、调压站、市政燃气管网等的总称，包括市政燃气设施、建筑区划内业主专有部分以外的燃气设施以及户内燃气设施等。  
(二)燃气燃烧器具，是指以燃气为燃料的燃烧器具，包括居民家庭和商业用户所使用的燃气灶、热水器、沸水器、采暖器、空调器等器具。

**Article 53** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "Gas facilities" means the general term for manufactured gas production plants, gas storage and distribution stations, gate stations, gasification stations, mixing stations, filling stations, bottling stations, supply stations, pressure regulating stations, municipal gas pipeline networks, etc., including municipal gas facilities, gas facilities within the building zone other than the exclusive parts of the owners, and indoor gas facilities, etc.  
(2) "Gas burning appliances" means the combustion appliances using gas as fuel, including gas stoves, water heaters, boilers, heaters, air conditioners and other appliances used by households and commercial users.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　农村的燃气管理参照本条例的规定执行。

**Article 54** The administration of gas in rural areas shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2011年3月1日起施行。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of March 1, 2011.
