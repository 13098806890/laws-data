# 中华人民共和国测绘成果管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2006-05-27  
**生效日期**：2006-09-01

> (2006年5月17日国务院第136次常务会议通过　2006年5月27日中华人民共和国国务院令第469号公布　自2006年9月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对测绘成果的管理，维护国家安全，促进测绘成果的利用，满足经济建设、国防建设和社会发展的需要，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。 第二条　测绘成果的汇交、保管、利用和重要地理信息数据的审核与公布，适用本条例。 本条例所称测绘成果，是指通过测绘形成的数据、信息、图件以及相关的技术资料。测绘成果分为基础测绘成果和非基础测绘成果。 第三条　国务院测绘行政主管部门负责全国测绘成果工作的统一监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘成果工作。 县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘工作的部门(以下称测绘行政主管部门)负责本行政区域测绘成果工作的统一监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘成果工作。 第四条　汇交、保管、公布、利用、销毁测绘成果应当遵守有关保密法律、法规的规定，采取必要的保密措施，保障测绘成果的安全。 第五条　对在测绘成果管理工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，由有关人民政府或者部门给予表彰和奖励。 第二章　汇交与保管 第六条　中央财政投资完成的测绘项目，由承担测绘项目的单位向国务院测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料；地方财政投资完成的测绘项目，由承担测绘项目的单位向测绘项目所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料；使用其他资金完成的测绘项目，由测绘项目出资人向测绘项目所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料。 第七条　测绘成果属于基础测绘成果的，应当汇交副本；属于非基础测绘成果的，应当汇交目录。测绘成果的副本和目录实行无偿汇交。 下列测绘成果为基础测绘成果： (一)为建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统进行的天文测量、三角测量、水准测量、卫星大地测量、重力测量所获取的数据、图件； (二)基础航空摄影所获取的数据、影像资料；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对测绘成果的管理，维护国家安全，促进测绘成果的利用，满足经济建设、国防建设和社会发展的需要，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the management of surveying and mapping achievements, maintaining national security, promoting the utilization of surveying and mapping achievements, and meeting the needs of economic construction, national defense construction and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　测绘成果的汇交、保管、利用和重要地理信息数据的审核与公布，适用本条例。  
本条例所称测绘成果，是指通过测绘形成的数据、信息、图件以及相关的技术资料。测绘成果分为基础测绘成果和非基础测绘成果。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the submission, custody, utilization and publication of surveying and mapping achievements, as well as the examination and publication of important geographic information data.  
Surveying and mapping achievements as mentioned in these Regulations refer to data, information, maps and related technical materials formed through surveying and mapping. Surveying and mapping achievements are classified into basic surveying and mapping achievements and non-basic surveying and mapping achievements.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院测绘行政主管部门负责全国测绘成果工作的统一监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘成果工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘工作的部门(以下称测绘行政主管部门)负责本行政区域测绘成果工作的统一监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘成果工作。

**Article 3** The administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of surveying and mapping achievements nationwide. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the surveying and mapping achievements related to their respective departments.  
The department responsible for the administration of surveying and mapping work under the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the administrative department of surveying and mapping) shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of surveying and mapping achievements within its administrative region. Other relevant departments under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the surveying and mapping achievements related to their respective departments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　汇交、保管、公布、利用、销毁测绘成果应当遵守有关保密法律、法规的规定，采取必要的保密措施，保障测绘成果的安全。

**Article 4** The submission, custody, publication, utilization and destruction of surveying and mapping achievements shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on confidentiality, take necessary confidentiality measures, and ensure the security of surveying and mapping achievements.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对在测绘成果管理工作中作出突出贡献的单位和个人，由有关人民政府或者部门给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 5** Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the management of surveying and mapping achievements shall be commended and rewarded by the relevant people's government or department.

### 第二章　汇交与保管

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中央财政投资完成的测绘项目，由承担测绘项目的单位向国务院测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料；地方财政投资完成的测绘项目，由承担测绘项目的单位向测绘项目所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料；使用其他资金完成的测绘项目，由测绘项目出资人向测绘项目所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果资料。

**Article 6** For surveying and mapping projects completed with investment from the central government finance, the unit undertaking the surveying and mapping project shall submit the surveying and mapping achievement materials to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council; for surveying and mapping projects completed with investment from local government finance, the unit undertaking the surveying and mapping project shall submit the surveying and mapping achievement materials to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the surveying and mapping project is located; for surveying and mapping projects completed with other funds, the investor of the surveying and mapping project shall submit the surveying and mapping achievement materials to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the surveying and mapping project is located.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　测绘成果属于基础测绘成果的，应当汇交副本；属于非基础测绘成果的，应当汇交目录。测绘成果的副本和目录实行无偿汇交。  
下列测绘成果为基础测绘成果：  
(一)为建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统进行的天文测量、三角测量、水准测量、卫星大地测量、重力测量所获取的数据、图件；  
(二)基础航空摄影所获取的数据、影像资料；  
(三)遥感卫星和其他航天飞行器对地观测所获取的基础地理信息遥感资料；  
(四)国家基本比例尺地图、影像图及其数字化产品；  
(五)基础地理信息系统的数据、信息等。

**Article 7** If a surveying and mapping achievement is a basic surveying and mapping achievement, a duplicate thereof shall be submitted; if it is a non-basic surveying and mapping achievement, a catalogue thereof shall be submitted. Duplicates and catalogues of surveying and mapping achievements shall be submitted free of charge.  
The following surveying and mapping achievements are basic surveying and mapping achievements:  
(1) Data and maps obtained through astronomical surveying, triangulation, leveling, satellite geodetic surveying and gravity surveying for the establishment of a unified national surveying and mapping datum and surveying and mapping system;  
(2) Data and image materials obtained through basic aerial photography;  
(3) Remote sensing materials of basic geographic information obtained through earth observation by remote sensing satellites and other space vehicles;  
(4) National basic scale maps, image maps and their digital products;  
(5) Data and information of basic geographic information systems.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国的组织或者个人依法与中华人民共和国有关部门或者单位合资、合作，经批准在中华人民共和国领域内从事测绘活动的，测绘成果归中方部门或者单位所有，并由中方部门或者单位向国务院测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果副本。  
外国的组织或者个人依法在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事测绘活动的，由其按照国务院测绘行政主管部门的规定汇交测绘成果副本或者目录。

**Article 8** Where a foreign organization or individual, in accordance with law, engages in surveying and mapping activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China through joint venture or cooperation with a relevant department or unit of the People's Republic of China upon approval, the surveying and mapping achievements shall be owned by the Chinese department or unit, and the Chinese department or unit shall submit duplicates of the surveying and mapping achievements to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council.  
Where a foreign organization or individual, in accordance with law, engages in surveying and mapping activities in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, it or he shall, in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council, submit duplicates or catalogues of the surveying and mapping achievements.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　测绘项目出资人或者承担国家投资的测绘项目的单位应当自测绘项目验收完成之日起3个月内，向测绘行政主管部门汇交测绘成果副本或者目录。测绘行政主管部门应当在收到汇交的测绘成果副本或者目录后，出具汇交凭证。  
汇交测绘成果资料的范围由国务院测绘行政主管部门商国务院有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 9** The investor of a surveying and mapping project or the unit undertaking a surveying and mapping project with state investment shall, within three months from the date of completion of the project acceptance, submit duplicates or catalogues of the surveying and mapping achievements to the administrative department of surveying and mapping. The administrative department of surveying and mapping shall, after receiving the submitted duplicates or catalogues of the surveying and mapping achievements, issue a submission receipt.  
The scope of surveying and mapping achievement materials to be submitted shall be formulated and published by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council after consultation with other relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　测绘行政主管部门自收到汇交的测绘成果副本或者目录之日起10个工作日内，应当将其移交给测绘成果保管单位。  
国务院测绘行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当定期编制测绘成果资料目录，向社会公布。

**Article 10** The administrative department of surveying and mapping shall, within ten working days from the date of receiving the submitted duplicates or catalogues of the surveying and mapping achievements, transfer them to the custodian unit of surveying and mapping achievements.  
The administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council and the administrative departments of surveying and mapping under the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall regularly compile catalogues of surveying and mapping achievement materials and publish them to the public.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　测绘成果保管单位应当建立健全测绘成果资料的保管制度，配备必要的设施，确保测绘成果资料的安全，并对基础测绘成果资料实行异地备份存放制度。  
测绘成果资料的存放设施与条件，应当符合国家保密、消防及档案管理的有关规定和要求。

**Article 11** The custodian unit of surveying and mapping achievements shall establish a sound custody system for surveying and mapping achievement materials, equip necessary facilities to ensure the safety of surveying and mapping achievement materials, and implement a system of off-site backup storage for basic surveying and mapping achievement materials.  
The storage facilities and conditions for surveying and mapping achievement materials shall comply with the relevant provisions and requirements of the state on confidentiality, fire protection and archive management.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　测绘成果保管单位应当按照规定保管测绘成果资料，不得损毁、散失、转让。

**Article 12** The custodian unit of surveying and mapping achievements shall keep the surveying and mapping achievement materials in accordance with the provisions, and shall not damage, lose or transfer them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　测绘项目的出资人或者承担测绘项目的单位，应当采取必要的措施，确保其获取的测绘成果的安全。

**Article 13** The investor of a surveying and mapping project or the unit undertaking the surveying and mapping project shall take necessary measures to ensure the safety of the surveying and mapping achievements it obtains.

### 第三章　利用

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当积极推进公众版测绘成果的加工和编制工作，并鼓励公众版测绘成果的开发利用，促进测绘成果的社会化应用。

**Article 14** The administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government at or above the county level shall actively promote the processing and compilation of public version surveying and mapping achievements, encourage the development and utilization of public version surveying and mapping achievements, and promote the social application of surveying and mapping achievements.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　使用财政资金的测绘项目和使用财政资金的建设工程测绘项目，有关部门在批准立项前应当书面征求本级人民政府测绘行政主管部门的意见。测绘行政主管部门应当自收到征求意见材料之日起10日内，向征求意见的部门反馈意见。有适宜测绘成果的，应当充分利用已有的测绘成果，避免重复测绘。

**Article 15** For surveying and mapping projects using fiscal funds and construction engineering surveying and mapping projects using fiscal funds, the relevant department shall, before approving the project, consult in writing the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government at the same level. The administrative department of surveying and mapping shall, within ten days from the date of receiving the consultation materials, give feedback to the consulting department. Where there are suitable surveying and mapping achievements, the existing surveying and mapping achievements shall be fully utilized to avoid repeated surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家保密工作部门、国务院测绘行政主管部门应当商军队测绘主管部门，依照有关保密法律、行政法规的规定，确定测绘成果的秘密范围和秘密等级。  
利用涉及国家秘密的测绘成果开发生产的产品，未经国务院测绘行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门进行保密技术处理的，其秘密等级不得低于所用测绘成果的秘密等级。

**Article 16** The state secret-keeping department and the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council shall, after consulting the military surveying and mapping department, determine the secret scope and secret grade of surveying and mapping achievements in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations on secrecy.  
For products developed and produced by utilizing surveying and mapping achievements involving state secrets, if no confidentiality technical treatment has been conducted by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council or the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, the secret grade thereof shall not be lower than that of the surveying and mapping achievements used.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　法人或者其他组织需要利用属于国家秘密的基础测绘成果的，应当提出明确的利用目的和范围，报测绘成果所在地的测绘行政主管部门审批。  
测绘行政主管部门审查同意的，应当以书面形式告知测绘成果的秘密等级、保密要求以及相关著作权保护要求。

**Article 17** Where a legal person or other organization needs to utilize basic surveying and mapping achievements that are state secrets, it shall specify the purpose and scope of utilization, and report to the administrative department of surveying and mapping where the surveying and mapping achievements are located for examination and approval.  
Where the administrative department of surveying and mapping approves the utilization upon examination, it shall inform the user in writing of the secret grade, confidentiality requirements and relevant copyright protection requirements of the surveying and mapping achievements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对外提供属于国家秘密的测绘成果，应当按照国务院和中央军事委员会规定的审批程序，报国务院测绘行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门审批；测绘行政主管部门在审批前，应当征求军队有关部门的意见。

**Article 18** For providing surveying and mapping achievements that are state secrets to foreign parties, the matter shall be reported to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council or the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval in accordance with the examination and approval procedures prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission; before approving the matter, the administrative department of surveying and mapping shall consult the relevant military department.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　基础测绘成果和财政投资完成的其他测绘成果，用于国家机关决策和社会公益性事业的，应当无偿提供。  
除前款规定外，测绘成果依法实行有偿使用制度。但是，各级人民政府及其有关部门和军队因防灾、减灾、国防建设等公共利益的需要，可以无偿使用测绘成果。  
依法有偿使用测绘成果的，使用人与测绘项目出资人应当签订书面协议，明确双方的权利和义务。

**Article 19** Basic surveying and mapping achievements and other surveying and mapping achievements completed with fiscal investment shall be provided free of charge when used for decision-making by state organs and for public welfare undertakings.  
Except as provided in the preceding paragraph, surveying and mapping achievements shall be subject to a paid-use system in accordance with law. However, people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments and the military may use surveying and mapping achievements free of charge when needed for the public interests such as disaster prevention and reduction, national defense construction, etc.  
Where surveying and mapping achievements are used on a paid basis in accordance with law, the user and the investor of the surveying and mapping project shall enter into a written agreement specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　测绘成果涉及著作权保护和管理的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 20** Where surveying and mapping achievements involve copyright protection and management, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建立以地理信息数据为基础的信息系统，应当利用符合国家标准的基础地理信息数据。

**Article 21** For establishing an information system based on geographic information data, basic geographic information data that conform to national standards shall be used.

### 第四章　重要地理信息数据的审核与公布

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家对重要地理信息数据实行统一审核与公布制度。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自公布重要地理信息数据。

**Article 22** The state implements a system of unified examination and publication of important geographic information data.  
No unit or individual may publish important geographic information data without authorization.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　重要地理信息数据包括：  
(一)国界、国家海岸线长度；  
(二)领土、领海、毗连区、专属经济区面积；  
(三)国家海岸滩涂面积、岛礁数量和面积；  
(四)国家版图的重要特征点，地势、地貌分区位置；  
(五)国务院测绘行政主管部门商国务院其他有关部门确定的其他重要自然和人文地理实体的位置、高程、深度、面积、长度等地理信息数据。

**Article 23** Important geographic information data include:  
(1) The length of national boundaries and national coastlines;  
(2) The areas of territory, territorial sea, contiguous zone and exclusive economic zone;  
(3) The area of national coastal tidal flats, and the number and area of islands and reefs;  
(4) Important feature points of the national territory, the location of topographic and geomorphic divisions;  
(5) Geographic information data such as the location, elevation, depth, area and length of other important natural and human geographic entities determined by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council after consultation with other relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　提出公布重要地理信息数据建议的单位或者个人，应当向国务院测绘行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门报送建议材料。  
对需要公布的重要地理信息数据，国务院测绘行政主管部门应当提出审核意见，并与国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘主管部门会商后，报国务院批准。具体办法由国务院测绘行政主管部门制定。

**Article 24** The unit or individual that proposes to publish important geographic information data shall submit the proposal materials to the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council or the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For important geographic information data that need to be published, the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council shall put forward examination opinions, and after consultation with other relevant departments under the State Council and the military surveying and mapping department, report to the State Council for approval. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院批准公布的重要地理信息数据，由国务院或者国务院授权的部门以公告形式公布。  
在行政管理、新闻传播、对外交流、教学等对社会公众有影响的活动中，需要使用重要地理信息数据的，应当使用依法公布的重要地理信息数据。

**Article 25** Important geographic information data approved for publication by the State Council shall be published in the form of an announcement by the State Council or a department authorized by the State Council.  
Where important geographic information data need to be used in activities that have an impact on the public, such as administrative management, news dissemination, foreign exchanges, and teaching, the important geographic information data published in accordance with law shall be used.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府测绘行政主管部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)接收汇交的测绘成果副本或者目录，未依法出具汇交凭证的；  
(二)未及时向测绘成果保管单位移交测绘成果资料的；  
(三)未依法编制和公布测绘成果资料目录的；  
(四)发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报后，不及时进行处理的；  
(五)不依法履行监督管理职责的其他行为。

**Article 26** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government at or above the county level commits any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the people's government at a higher level shall order it to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law:  
(1) Failing to issue a submission receipt in accordance with law upon receiving the submitted duplicates or catalogues of surveying and mapping achievements;  
(2) Failing to transfer surveying and mapping achievement materials to the custodian unit of surveying and mapping achievements in a timely manner;  
(3) Failing to compile and publish catalogues of surveying and mapping achievement materials in accordance with law;  
(4) Failing to deal with illegal acts in a timely manner upon discovery or upon receipt of a report on illegal acts;  
(5) Other acts of failing to perform supervision and administration duties in accordance with law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，未汇交测绘成果资料的，依照[《中华人民共和国测绘法》第四十七条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国测绘法.md#art-47)的规定进行处罚。

**Article 27** Where, in violation of these Regulations, surveying and mapping achievement materials are not submitted, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 47 of the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，测绘成果保管单位有下列行为之一的，由测绘行政主管部门给予警告，责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)未按照测绘成果资料的保管制度管理测绘成果资料，造成测绘成果资料损毁、散失的；  
(二)擅自转让汇交的测绘成果资料的；  
(三)未依法向测绘成果的使用人提供测绘成果资料的。

**Article 28** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the custodian unit of surveying and mapping achievements commits any of the following acts, the administrative department of surveying and mapping shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability in accordance with law; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law:  
(1) Failing to manage surveying and mapping achievement materials in accordance with the custody system for surveying and mapping achievement materials, resulting in damage or loss of surveying and mapping achievement materials;  
(2) Transferring the submitted surveying and mapping achievement materials without authorization;  
(3) Failing to provide surveying and mapping achievement materials to the user of surveying and mapping achievements in accordance with law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由测绘行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告，可以处10万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
(一)建立以地理信息数据为基础的信息系统，利用不符合国家标准的基础地理信息数据的；  
(二)擅自公布重要地理信息数据的；  
(三)在对社会公众有影响的活动中使用未经依法公布的重要地理信息数据的。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department of surveying and mapping or other relevant department shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order it to make corrections, give a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions in accordance with law:  
(1) Establishing an information system based on geographic information data and using basic geographic information data that do not conform to national standards;  
(2) Publishing important geographic information data without authorization;  
(3) Using important geographic information data that have not been published in accordance with law in activities that have an impact on the public.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　法律、行政法规对编制出版地图的管理另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 30** Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the management of compilation and publication of maps, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　军事测绘成果的管理，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 31** The management of military surveying and mapping achievements shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自2006年9月1日起施行。1989年3月21日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国测绘成果管理规定》同时废止。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2006. The Provisions on the Administration of Surveying and Mapping Achievements of the People's Republic of China promulgated by the State Council on March 21, 1989 shall be abolished simultaneously.
