# 城市节约用水管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1988-12-20  
**生效日期**：1989-01-01

> (1988年11月30日国务院批准　1988年12月20日中华人民共和国建设部令第1号发布　自1989年1月1日起施行) 第一条　为加强城市节约用水管理，保护和合理利用水资源，促进国民经济和社会发展，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于城市规划区内节约用水的管理工作。 在城市规划区内使用公共供水和自建设施供水的单位和个人，必须遵守本规定。 第三条　城市实行计划用水和节约用水。 第四条　国家鼓励城市节约用水科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市节约用水科学技术水平。 在城市节约用水工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。 第五条　国务院城市建设行政主管部门主管全国的城市节约用水工作，业务上受国务院水行政主管部门指导。 国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责本行业的节约用水管理工作。 省、自治区人民政府和县级以上城市人民政府城市建设行政主管部门和其他有关行业行政主管部门，按照同级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责城市节约用水管理工作。 第六条　城市人民政府应当在制定城市供水发展规划的同时，制定节约用水发展规划，并根据节约用水发展规划制定节约用水年度计划。 各有关行业行政主管部门应当制定本行业的节约用水发展规划和节约用水年度计划。 第七条　工业用水重复利用率低于40%(不包括热电厂用水)的城市，新建供水工程时，未经上一级城市建设行政主管部门的同意，不得新增工业用水量。 第八条　单位自建供水设施取用地下水，必须经城市建设行政主管部门核准后，依照国家规定申请取水许可。 第九条　城市的新建、扩建和改建工程项目，应当配套建设节约用水设施。城市建设行政主管部门应当参加节约用水设施的竣工验收。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强城市节约用水管理，保护和合理利用水资源，促进国民经济和社会发展，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the management of urban water conservation, protecting and rationally utilizing water resources, and promoting national economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于城市规划区内节约用水的管理工作。  
在城市规划区内使用公共供水和自建设施供水的单位和个人，必须遵守本规定。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to the management of water conservation within urban planning areas.  
Units and individuals that use public water supply or self-built facilities for water supply within urban planning areas must comply with these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市实行计划用水和节约用水。

**Article 3** Cities shall implement planned water use and water conservation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励城市节约用水科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市节约用水科学技术水平。  
在城市节约用水工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 4** The State encourages scientific and technological research on urban water conservation, promotes advanced technologies, and improves the level of science and technology in urban water conservation.  
Units and individuals that have made notable achievements in urban water conservation shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院城市建设行政主管部门主管全国的城市节约用水工作，业务上受国务院水行政主管部门指导。  
国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责本行业的节约用水管理工作。  
省、自治区人民政府和县级以上城市人民政府城市建设行政主管部门和其他有关行业行政主管部门，按照同级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责城市节约用水管理工作。

**Article 5** The administrative department of urban construction under the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide urban water conservation work and shall receive guidance from the administrative department of water resources under the State Council in terms of professional operations.  
Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the water conservation management in their respective industries.  
The administrative departments of urban construction and other relevant administrative departments of industries of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and cities at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the people's governments at the same level, be responsible for the management of urban water conservation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　城市人民政府应当在制定城市供水发展规划的同时，制定节约用水发展规划，并根据节约用水发展规划制定节约用水年度计划。  
各有关行业行政主管部门应当制定本行业的节约用水发展规划和节约用水年度计划。

**Article 6** The people's government of a city shall, while formulating the development plan for urban water supply, formulate a development plan for water conservation, and shall formulate an annual plan for water conservation based on the water conservation development plan.  
Each relevant administrative department of industry shall formulate a water conservation development plan and an annual water conservation plan for its own industry.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　工业用水重复利用率低于40%(不包括热电厂用水)的城市，新建供水工程时，未经上一级城市建设行政主管部门的同意，不得新增工业用水量。

**Article 7** In cities where the industrial water reuse rate is below 40% (excluding water used by thermal power plants), when constructing new water supply projects, no additional industrial water consumption shall be added without the consent of the administrative department of urban construction at the next higher level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　单位自建供水设施取用地下水，必须经城市建设行政主管部门核准后，依照国家规定申请取水许可。

**Article 8** Units that extract groundwater through self-built water supply facilities must obtain approval from the administrative department of urban construction and then apply for a water extraction permit in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市的新建、扩建和改建工程项目，应当配套建设节约用水设施。城市建设行政主管部门应当参加节约用水设施的竣工验收。

**Article 9** For new, expanded, or renovated construction projects in cities, water conservation facilities shall be built as supporting facilities. The administrative department of urban construction shall participate in the completion acceptance of water conservation facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　城市建设行政主管部门应当会同有关行业行政主管部门制定行业综合用水定额和单项用水定额。

**Article 10** The administrative department of urban construction shall, together with the relevant administrative departments of industries, formulate comprehensive water consumption quotas and individual water consumption quotas for industries.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城市用水计划由城市建设行政主管部门根据水资源统筹规划和水长期供求计划制定，并下达执行。  
超计划用水必须缴纳超计划用水加价水费。超计划用水加价水费，应当从税后留利或者预算包干经费中支出，不得纳入成本或者从当年预算中支出。  
超计划用水加价水费的具体征收办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 11** Urban water use plans shall be formulated by the administrative department of urban construction based on the overall planning of water resources and long-term water supply and demand plans, and shall be issued for implementation.  
Water consumption exceeding the plan must be subject to the payment of additional water charges for excess water use. Such additional water charges shall be paid from post-tax retained profits or from budgetary lump-sum funds, and shall not be included in costs or paid from the current year's budget.  
The specific measures for collecting additional water charges for excess water use shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　生活用水按户计量收费。新建住宅应当安装分户计量水表；现有住户未装分户计量水表的，应当限期安装。

**Article 12** Domestic water use shall be metered and charged by household. Newly built residential buildings shall be installed with household water meters; existing households without household water meters shall be required to install them within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各用水单位应当在用水设备上安装计量水表，进行用水单耗考核，降低单位产品用水量；应当采取循环用水、一水多用等措施，在保证用水质量标准的前提下，提高水的重复利用率。

**Article 13** Each water-using unit shall install water meters on its water-using equipment, conduct assessment of water consumption per unit of product, and reduce water consumption per unit of product; it shall adopt measures such as recycling water and multiple uses of water, and improve the water reuse rate while ensuring the water quality standards.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　水资源紧缺城市，应当在保证用水质量标准的前提下，采取措施提高城市污水利用率。  
沿海城市应当积极开发利用海水资源。  
有咸水资源的城市，应当合理开发利用咸水资源。

**Article 14** Cities with scarce water resources shall, while ensuring water quality standards, take measures to improve the utilization rate of urban sewage.  
Coastal cities shall actively develop and utilize seawater resources.  
Cities with brackish water resources shall rationally develop and utilize brackish water resources.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城市供水企业、自建供水设施的单位应当加强供水设施的维修管理，减少水的漏损量。

**Article 15** Urban water supply enterprises and units with self-built water supply facilities shall strengthen the maintenance and management of water supply facilities to reduce water leakage and loss.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级统计部门、城市建设行政主管部门应当做好城市节约用水统计工作。

**Article 16** The statistical departments at all levels and the administrative departments of urban construction shall properly conduct statistical work on urban water conservation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市的新建、扩建和改建工程项目未按规定配套建设节约用水设施或者节约用水设施经验收不合格的，由城市建设行政主管部门限制其用水量，并责令其限期完善节约用水设施，可以并处罚款。

**Article 17** Where a new, expanded, or renovated construction project in a city fails to construct water conservation facilities as supporting facilities in accordance with regulations, or where such facilities fail to pass acceptance inspection, the administrative department of urban construction shall restrict its water consumption and order it to complete the water conservation facilities within a time limit, and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　超计划用水加价水费必须按规定的期限缴纳。逾期不缴纳的，城市建设行政主管部门除限期缴纳外，并按日加收超计划用水加价水费50‰的滞纳金。

**Article 18** The additional water charges for excess water use must be paid within the prescribed time limit. If payment is overdue, the administrative department of urban construction shall, in addition to ordering payment within a time limit, impose a daily surcharge of 50‰ of the overdue additional water charges for excess water use.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　拒不安装生活用水分户计量水表的，城市建设行政主管部门应当责令其限期安装；逾期仍不安装的，由城市建设行政主管部门限制其用水量，可以并处罚款。

**Article 19** Where any person refuses to install household water meters for domestic water use, the administrative department of urban construction shall order him to install them within a time limit; if he still fails to install them upon expiration of the time limit, the administrative department of urban construction shall restrict his water consumption and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知次日起十五日内，向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议通知次日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。逾期不申请复议或者不向人民法院起诉又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 20** Where a party is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty decision, he may, within fifteen days from the day following receipt of the notice of penalty, apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level over the organ that made the decision; if dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, he may, within fifteen days from the day following receipt of the reconsideration notice, institute a prosecution in a People's Court. If no application for reconsideration is filed and no prosecution is instituted within the time limit, and the penalty decision is not complied with, the organ that made the penalty decision shall apply to a People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　城市建设行政主管部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管部门给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where any staff member of the administrative department of urban construction neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, an administrative sanction shall be imposed by the unit to which he belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level; if the act constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate the criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本规定制定实施办法。

**Article 22** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Provisions, formulate implementation measures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定由国务院城市建设行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 23** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the administrative department of urban construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本规定自1989年1月1日起施行。

**Article 24** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 1989.
