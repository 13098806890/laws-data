# 中华人民共和国城镇国有土地使用权出让和转让暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

> (1990年5月19日中华人民共和国国务院令第55号发布　根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了改革城镇国有土地使用制度，合理开发、利用、经营土地，加强土地管理，促进城市建设和经济发展，制定本条例。 第二条　国家按照所有权与使用权分离的原则，实行城镇国有土地使用权出让、转让制度，但地下资源、埋藏物和市政公用设施除外。 前款所称城镇国有土地是指市、县城、建制镇、工矿区范围内属于全民所有的土地(以下简称土地)。 第三条　中华人民共和国境内外的公司、企业、其他组织和个人，除法律另有规定者外，均可依照本条例的规定取得土地使用权，进行土地开发、利用、经营。 第四条　依照本条例的规定取得土地使用权的土地使用者，其使用权在使用年限内可以转让、出租、抵押或者用于其他经济活动，合法权益受国家法律保护。 第五条　土地使用者开发、利用、经营土地的活动，应当遵守国家法律、法规的规定，并不得损害社会公共利益。 第六条　县级以上人民政府土地管理部门依法对土地使用权的出让、转让、出租、抵押、终止进行监督检查。 第七条　土地使用权出让、转让、出租、抵押、终止及有关的地上建筑物、其他附着物的登记，由政府土地管理部门、房产管理部门依照法律和国务院的有关规定办理。 登记文件可以公开查阅。 第二章　土地使用权出让 第八条　土地使用权出让是指国家以土地所有者的身份将土地使用权在一定年限内让与土地使用者，并由土地使用者向国家支付土地使用权出让金的行为。 土地使用权出让应当签订出让合同。 第九条　土地使用权的出让，由市、县人民政府负责，有计划、有步骤地进行。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了改革城镇国有土地使用制度，合理开发、利用、经营土地，加强土地管理，促进城市建设和经济发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** For the purpose of reforming the urban state-owned land use system, rationally developing, utilizing, and operating land, strengthening land administration, and promoting urban construction and economic development, these Regulations are formulated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家按照所有权与使用权分离的原则，实行城镇国有土地使用权出让、转让制度，但地下资源、埋藏物和市政公用设施除外。  
前款所称城镇国有土地是指市、县城、建制镇、工矿区范围内属于全民所有的土地(以下简称土地)。

**Article 2** The State shall implement a system of granting and transferring the right to use urban state-owned land on the principle of separation of ownership from the right to use, except for underground resources, buried objects, and municipal public facilities.  
Urban state-owned land mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to land owned by the whole people within the scope of cities, county towns, established towns, and industrial and mining areas (hereinafter referred to as land).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国境内外的公司、企业、其他组织和个人，除法律另有规定者外，均可依照本条例的规定取得土地使用权，进行土地开发、利用、经营。

**Article 3** Companies, enterprises, other organizations, and individuals within or outside the People's Republic of China may, unless otherwise provided by law, obtain the right to use land and carry out land development, utilization, and operation in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　依照本条例的规定取得土地使用权的土地使用者，其使用权在使用年限内可以转让、出租、抵押或者用于其他经济活动，合法权益受国家法律保护。

**Article 4** A land user who has obtained the right to use land in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations may transfer, lease, mortgage, or use such right for other economic activities within the term of use, and the lawful rights and interests shall be protected by the laws of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　土地使用者开发、利用、经营土地的活动，应当遵守国家法律、法规的规定，并不得损害社会公共利益。

**Article 5** A land user shall, in activities of developing, utilizing, and operating land, abide by the provisions of the laws and regulations of the State, and shall not harm the public interest.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府土地管理部门依法对土地使用权的出让、转让、出租、抵押、终止进行监督检查。

**Article 6** The land administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the granting, transfer, lease, mortgage, and termination of the right to use land.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　土地使用权出让、转让、出租、抵押、终止及有关的地上建筑物、其他附着物的登记，由政府土地管理部门、房产管理部门依照法律和国务院的有关规定办理。  
登记文件可以公开查阅。

**Article 7** Registration of the granting, transfer, lease, mortgage, termination of the right to use land and related buildings and other attachments on the land shall be handled by the government land administration department and the real estate administration department in accordance with the law and the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Registration documents may be publicly inspected.

### 第二章　土地使用权出让

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　土地使用权出让是指国家以土地所有者的身份将土地使用权在一定年限内让与土地使用者，并由土地使用者向国家支付土地使用权出让金的行为。  
土地使用权出让应当签订出让合同。

**Article 8** The granting of the right to use land means the act by which the State, as the land owner, transfers the right to use land to a land user for a certain period, and the land user pays the State a granting fee for the right to use land.  
A grant contract shall be signed for the granting of the right to use land.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　土地使用权的出让，由市、县人民政府负责，有计划、有步骤地进行。

**Article 9** The granting of the right to use land shall be the responsibility of the people's government of the city or county and shall be carried out in a planned and step-by-step manner.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　土地使用权出让的地块、用途、年限和其他条件，由市、县人民政府土地管理部门会同城市规划和建设管理部门、房产管理部门共同拟定方案，按照国务院规定的批准权限报经批准后，由土地管理部门实施。

**Article 10** The plot, purpose, term, and other conditions for the granting of the right to use land shall be jointly proposed by the land administration department of the people's government of the city or county together with the urban planning and construction administration department and the real estate administration department, and after being submitted for approval according to the approval authority prescribed by the State Council, shall be implemented by the land administration department.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　土地使用权出让合同应当按照平等、自愿、有偿的原则，由市、县人民政府土地管理部门(以下简称出让方)与土地使用者签订。

**Article 11** A contract for the granting of the right to use land shall be signed between the land administration department of the people's government of the city or county (hereinafter referred to as the grantor) and the land user in accordance with the principles of equality, voluntariness, and compensation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地使用权出让最高年限按下列用途确定：  
(一)居住用地七十年；  
(二)工业用地五十年；  
(三)教育、科技、文化、卫生、体育用地五十年；  
(四)商业、旅游、娱乐用地四十年；  
(五)综合或者其他用地五十年。

**Article 12** The maximum term for the granting of the right to use land shall be determined according to the following purposes:  
(1) 70 years for residential land;  
(2) 50 years for industrial land;  
(3) 50 years for land used for education, science, technology, culture, health, and sports;  
(4) 40 years for land used for commerce, tourism, and entertainment;  
(5) 50 years for comprehensive or other land.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　土地使用权出让可以采取下列方式：  
(一)协议；  
(二)招标；  
(三)拍卖。  
依照前款规定方式出让土地使用权的具体程序和步骤，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 13** The granting of the right to use land may adopt the following methods:  
(1) agreement;  
(2) invitation to tender;  
(3) auction.  
The specific procedures and steps for granting the right to use land by the methods specified in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　土地使用者应当在签订土地使用权出让合同后六十日内，支付全部土地使用权出让金。逾期未全部支付的，出让方有权解除合同，并可请求违约赔偿。

**Article 14** A land user shall pay the full amount of the granting fee for the right to use land within 60 days after signing the grant contract. If the full amount is not paid within the time limit, the grantor shall have the right to dissolve the contract and may claim compensation for breach of contract.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　出让方应当按照合同规定，提供出让的土地使用权。未按合同规定提供土地使用权的，土地使用者有权解除合同，并可请求违约赔偿。

**Article 15** The grantor shall provide the granted right to use land in accordance with the contract provisions. If the right to use land is not provided in accordance with the contract provisions, the land user shall have the right to dissolve the contract and may claim compensation for breach of contract.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　土地使用者在支付全部土地使用权出让金后，应当依照规定办理登记，领取土地使用证，取得土地使用权。

**Article 16** After paying the full amount of the granting fee for the right to use land, the land user shall, in accordance with the provisions, register, obtain the certificate for the right to use state-owned land, and obtain the right to use land.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　土地使用者应当按照土地使用权出让合同的规定和城市规划的要求，开发、利用、经营土地。  
未按合同规定的期限和条件开发、利用土地的，市、县人民政府土地管理部门应当予以纠正，并根据情节可以给予警告、罚款直至无偿收回土地使用权的处罚。

**Article 17** A land user shall develop, utilize, and operate land in accordance with the provisions of the grant contract for the right to use land and the requirements of urban planning.  
If land is not developed and utilized within the time limit and under the conditions specified in the contract, the land administration department of the people's government of the city or county shall order rectification and may, depending on the circumstances, impose a warning, a fine, or even a penalty of recovering the right to use land without compensation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　土地使用者需要改变土地使用权出让合同规定的土地用途的，应当征得出让方同意并经土地管理部门和城市规划部门批准，依照本章的有关规定重新签订土地使用权出让合同，调整土地使用权出让金，并办理登记。

**Article 18** If a land user needs to change the land purpose specified in the grant contract for the right to use land, the land user shall obtain the consent of the grantor and the approval of the land administration department and the urban planning department, and shall re-sign a grant contract for the right to use land in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Chapter, adjust the granting fee, and register.

### 第三章　土地使用权转让

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　土地使用权转让是指土地使用者将土地使用权再转移的行为，包括出售、交换和赠与。  
未按土地使用权出让合同规定的期限和条件投资开发、利用土地的，土地使用权不得转让。

**Article 19** The transfer of the right to use land means the act by which a land user retransfers the right to use land, including sale, exchange, and donation.  
If land has not been invested, developed, and utilized within the time limit and under the conditions specified in the grant contract, the right to use land shall not be transferred.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　土地使用权转让应当签订转让合同。

**Article 20** A transfer contract shall be signed for the transfer of the right to use land.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　土地使用权转让时，土地使用权出让合同和登记文件中所载明的权利、义务随之转移。

**Article 21** When the right to use land is transferred, the rights and obligations specified in the grant contract and registration documents shall be transferred accordingly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　土地使用者通过转让方式取得的土地使用权，其使用年限为土地使用权出让合同规定的使用年限减去原土地使用者已使用年限后的剩余年限。

**Article 22** The term of use for the right to use land obtained by a land user through transfer shall be the remaining term after deducting the term already used by the original land user from the term specified in the grant contract.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　土地使用权转让时，其地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权随之转让。

**Article 23** When the right to use land is transferred, the ownership of the buildings and other attachments on such land shall be transferred accordingly.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　地上建筑物、其他附着物的所有人或者共有人，享有该建筑物、附着物使用范围内的土地使用权。  
土地使用者转让地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权时，其使用范围内的土地使用权随之转让，但地上建筑物、其他附着物作为动产转让的除外。

**Article 24** The owner or co-owner of a building and other attachments on the land shall enjoy the right to use land within the area covered by the building or attachment.  
When a land user transfers the ownership of a building or other attachment on the land, the right to use land within the area covered shall be transferred accordingly, except where the building or attachment is transferred as movable property.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权转让，应当依照规定办理过户登记。  
土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权分割转让的，应当经市、县人民政府土地管理部门和房产管理部门批准，并依照规定办理过户登记。

**Article 25** The transfer of the right to use land and the ownership of the buildings and other attachments on the land shall be registered for change of ownership in accordance with the provisions.  
If the right to use land and the ownership of the buildings and other attachments on the land are transferred in separate parts, approval shall be obtained from the land administration department and the real estate administration department of the people's government of the city or county, and registration for change of ownership shall be handled in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　土地使用权转让价格明显低于市场价格的，市、县人民政府有优先购买权。  
土地使用权转让的市场价格不合理上涨时，市、县人民政府可以采取必要的措施。

**Article 26** If the price for the transfer of the right to use land is obviously lower than the market price, the people's government of the city or county shall have the pre-emptive right to purchase.  
If the market price for the transfer of the right to use land rises unreasonably, the people's government of the city or county may take necessary measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　土地使用权转让后，需要改变土地使用权出让合同规定的土地用途的，依照[本条例第十八条](#art-18)的规定办理。

**Article 27** After the transfer of the right to use land, if it is necessary to change the land purpose specified in the grant contract, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations.

### 第四章　土地使用权出租

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　土地使用权出租是指土地使用者作为出租人将土地使用权随同地上建筑物、其他附着物租赁给承租人使用，由承租人向出租人支付租金的行为。  
未按土地使用权出让合同规定的期限和条件投资开发、利用土地的，土地使用权不得出租。

**Article 28** The lease of the right to use land means the act by which a land user, as the lessor, leases the right to use land together with the buildings and other attachments on the land to a lessee for use, and the lessee pays rent to the lessor.  
If land has not been invested, developed, and utilized within the time limit and under the conditions specified in the grant contract, the right to use land shall not be leased.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　土地使用权出租，出租人与承租人应当签订租赁合同。  
租赁合同不得违背国家法律、法规和土地使用权出让合同的规定。

**Article 29** For the lease of the right to use land, the lessor and the lessee shall sign a lease contract.  
The lease contract shall not violate the provisions of the laws, regulations, and the grant contract for the right to use land.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　土地使用权出租后，出租人必须继续履行土地使用权出让合同。

**Article 30** After the right to use land is leased, the lessor must continue to perform the grant contract for the right to use land.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物出租，出租人应当依照规定办理登记。

**Article 31** If the right to use land and the buildings and other attachments on the land are leased, the lessor shall register in accordance with the provisions.

### 第五章　土地使用权抵押

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　土地使用权可以抵押。

**Article 32** The right to use land may be mortgaged.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　土地使用权抵押时，其地上建筑物、其他附着物随之抵押。  
地上建筑物、其他附着物抵押时，其使用范围内的土地使用权随之抵押。

**Article 33** When the right to use land is mortgaged, the buildings and other attachments on such land shall be mortgaged accordingly.  
When a building or other attachment on the land is mortgaged, the right to use land within the area covered shall be mortgaged accordingly.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　土地使用权抵押，抵押人与抵押权人应当签订抵押合同。  
抵押合同不得违背国家法律、法规和土地使用权出让合同的规定。

**Article 34** For the mortgage of the right to use land, the mortgagor and the mortgagee shall sign a mortgage contract.  
The mortgage contract shall not violate the provisions of the laws, regulations, and the grant contract for the right to use land.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物抵押，应当依照规定办理抵押登记。

**Article 35** The mortgage of the right to use land and the buildings and other attachments on the land shall be registered for mortgage in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　抵押人到期未能履行债务或者在抵押合同期间宣告解散、破产的，抵押权人有权依照国家法律、法规和抵押合同的规定处分抵押财产。  
因处分抵押财产而取得土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权的，应当依照规定办理过户登记。

**Article 36** If the mortgagor fails to perform the obligation at maturity or is declared dissolved or bankrupt during the term of the mortgage contract, the mortgagee shall have the right to dispose of the mortgaged property in accordance with the provisions of the laws, regulations, and the mortgage contract.  
A person who obtains the right to use land and the ownership of buildings and other attachments on the land through the disposal of mortgaged property shall register for change of ownership in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　处分抵押财产所得，抵押权人有优先受偿权。

**Article 37** The mortgagee shall have the priority right to be compensated from the proceeds of the disposal of the mortgaged property.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　抵押权因债务清偿或者其他原因而消灭的，应当依照规定办理注销抵押登记。

**Article 38** If the mortgage right is extinguished due to the discharge of the debt or other reasons, the registration of the mortgage shall be cancelled in accordance with the provisions.

### 第六章　土地使用权终止

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　土地使用权因土地使用权出让合同规定的使用年限届满、提前收回及土地灭失等原因而终止。

**Article 39** The right to use land shall terminate due to the expiry of the term specified in the grant contract, early recovery, loss of the land, or other reasons.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　土地使用权期满，土地使用权及其地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权由国家无偿取得。土地使用者应当交还土地使用证，并依照规定办理注销登记。

**Article 40** Upon the expiry of the term of the right to use land, the right to use land and the ownership of the buildings and other attachments on such land shall be acquired by the State without compensation. The land user shall return the certificate for the right to use state-owned land and cancel the registration in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　土地使用权期满，土地使用者可以申请续期。需要续期的，应当依照本条例第二章的规定重新签订合同，支付土地使用权出让金，并办理登记。

**Article 41** Upon the expiry of the term of the right to use land, the land user may apply for renewal. If renewal is needed, a new contract shall be signed in accordance with the provisions of Chapter II of these Regulations, the granting fee for the right to use land shall be paid, and registration shall be handled.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家对土地使用者依法取得的土地使用权不提前收回。在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，国家可以依照法律程序提前收回，并根据土地使用者已使用的年限和开发、利用土地的实际情况给予相应的补偿。

**Article 42** The State shall not recover the right to use land lawfully obtained by a land user before the expiry of the term. Under special circumstances, if required by the public interest, the State may recover the right before the expiry of the term in accordance with legal procedures and shall provide corresponding compensation based on the term already used by the land user and the actual situation of development and utilization of the land.

### 第七章　划拨土地使用权

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　划拨土地使用权是指土地使用者通过各种方式依法无偿取得的土地使用权。  
前款土地使用者应当依照《中华人民共和国城镇土地使用税暂行条例》的规定缴纳土地使用税。

**Article 43** Allocated right to use land means the right to use land lawfully obtained by a land user without compensation through various means.  
The land user mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall pay land use tax in accordance with the provisions of the Interim Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Urban Land Use Tax.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　划拨土地使用权，除[本条例第四十五条](#art-45)规定的情况外，不得转让、出租、抵押。

**Article 44** The allocated right to use land shall not be transferred, leased, or mortgaged except as provided in Article 45 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　符合下列条件的，经市、县人民政府土地管理部门和房产管理部门批准，其划拨土地使用权和地上建筑物、其他附着物所有权可以转让、出租、抵押：  
(一)土地使用者为公司、企业、其他经济组织和个人；  
(二)领有国有土地使用证；  
(三)具有地上建筑物、其他附着物合法的产权证明；  
(四)依照本条例第二章的规定签订土地使用权出让合同，向当地市、县人民政府补交土地使用权出让金或者以转让、出租、抵押所获收益抵交土地使用权出让金。  
转让、出租、抵押前款划拨土地使用权的，分别依照本条例第三章、第四章和第五章的规定办理。

**Article 45** If the following conditions are met, with the approval of the land administration department and the real estate administration department of the people's government of the city or county, the allocated right to use land and the ownership of the buildings and other attachments on the land may be transferred, leased, or mortgaged:  
(1) The land user is a company, enterprise, other economic organization, or individual;  
(2) In possession of the certificate for the right to use state-owned land;  
(3) In possession of lawful ownership certificates for the buildings and other attachments on the land;  
(4) A grant contract for the right to use land has been signed in accordance with the provisions of Chapter II of these Regulations, and the granting fee for the right to use land has been paid to the people's government of the local city or county as a supplement, or the proceeds from transfer, lease, or mortgage have been used to set off the granting fee.  
The transfer, lease, or mortgage of the allocated right to use land mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Chapter III, Chapter IV, and Chapter V of these Regulations respectively.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对未经批准擅自转让、出租、抵押划拨土地使用权的单位和个人，市、县人民政府土地管理部门应当没收其非法收入，并根据情节处以罚款。

**Article 46** With respect to units and individuals that transfer, lease, or mortgage the allocated right to use land without authorization, the land administration department of the people's government of the city or county shall confiscate the illegal income and impose a fine depending on the circumstances.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　无偿取得划拨土地使用权的土地使用者，因迁移、解散、撤销、破产或者其他原因而停止使用土地的，市、县人民政府应当无偿收回其划拨土地使用权，并可依照本条例的规定予以出让。  
对划拨土地使用权，市、县人民政府根据城市建设发展需要和城市规划的要求，可以无偿收回，并可依照本条例的规定予以出让。  
无偿收回划拨土地使用权时，对其地上建筑物、其他附着物，市、县人民政府应当根据实际情况给予适当补偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** If a land user who has obtained the allocated right to use land without compensation ceases to use the land due to relocation, dissolution, cancellation, bankruptcy, or other reasons, the people's government of the city or county shall recover the allocated right to use land without compensation and may grant it in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
For the allocated right to use land, the people's government of the city or county may recover it without compensation in accordance with the needs of urban construction development and urban planning requirements, and may grant it in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
When recovering the allocated right to use land without compensation, the people's government of the city or county shall provide appropriate compensation for the buildings and other attachments on such land based on the actual situation.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　依照本条例的规定取得土地使用权的个人，其土地使用权可以继承。

**Article 48** The right to use land obtained by an individual in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations may be succeeded.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　土地使用者应当依照国家税收法规的规定纳税。

**Article 49** A land user shall pay taxes in accordance with the provisions of the State tax laws and regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　依照本条例收取的土地使用权出让金列入财政预算，作为专项基金管理，主要用于城市建设和土地开发。具体使用管理办法，由财政部另行制定。

**Article 50** The granting fee for the right to use land collected in accordance with these Regulations shall be included in the fiscal budget and managed as a special fund, mainly used for urban construction and land development. The specific use and management measures shall be separately formulated by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当根据本条例的规定和当地的实际情况选择部分条件比较成熟的城镇先行试点。

**Article 51** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the provisions of these Regulations and local conditions, select some towns with relatively mature conditions for pilot experiments first.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　本条例由国家土地管理局负责解释；实施办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 52** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the State Land Administration; the implementation measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 53** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
