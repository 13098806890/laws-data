# 住房租赁条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-07-16  
**生效日期**：2025-09-15

> 出租人应当按照规定，通过住房租赁管理服务平台等方式将住房租赁合同向租赁住房所在地房产管理部门备案。房产管理部门应当提高住房租赁合同备案服务水平，不得就住房租赁合同备案收取任何费用。 出租人未办理住房租赁合同备案的，承租人可以办理备案。 第九条　出租人应当遵守下列规定： （一）向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息； （二）核验承租人的身份证明材料，不得将住房出租给拒绝出示身份证明材料的单位或者个人； （三）不得擅自进入租赁住房，但是经承租人同意或者依法可以进入的除外。 第十条　出租人收取押金的，应当在住房租赁合同中约定押金的数额、返还时间以及扣减押金的情形等事项。除住房租赁合同约定的情形以外，出租人无正当理由不得扣减押金。 第十一条　承租人应当遵守下列规定： （一）向出租人出示身份证明材料； （二）安全、合理使用租赁住房，不得损坏、擅自拆除、停用消防设施或者改动租赁住房承重结构，不得私拉乱接水、电、燃气管线； （三）未经出租人同意，不得擅自改变租赁住房用途、拆改室内设施或者改动租赁住房其他结构； （四）遵守物业管理规约，不得任意弃置垃圾、超标准排放污染物或者产生噪声、违反规定饲养动物、违章搭建、侵占公共通道、高空抛物或者实施其他损害他人合法权益的行为； （五）对出租人依法确需进入租赁住房的，予以配合。 第十二条　出租人依法解除住房租赁合同的，应当通知承租人，并为承租人腾退租赁住房留出合理时间。 出租人不得采取暴力、威胁或者其他非法方式迫使承租人解除住房租赁合同或者腾退租赁住房。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出租人应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息；  
（二）核验承租人的身份证明材料，不得将住房出租给拒绝出示身份证明材料的单位或者个人；  
（三）不得擅自进入租赁住房，但是经承租人同意或者依法可以进入的除外。

**Article 9** A lessor shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Present to the lessee identity certification documents, the certificate of immovable property title for the housing to be leased, or other materials proving the lessor's lawful right to lease, and cooperate with the lessee in inquiring about and verifying relevant information on the housing to be leased according to law;  
(2) Verify the lessee's identity certification documents, and shall not lease the housing to any entity or individual that refuses to present identity certification documents;  
(3) Shall not enter the leased housing without authorization, except with the consent of the lessee or where entry is permitted according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　出租人收取押金的，应当在住房租赁合同中约定押金的数额、返还时间以及扣减押金的情形等事项。除住房租赁合同约定的情形以外，出租人无正当理由不得扣减押金。

**Article 10** Where a lessor collects a deposit, the lessor shall specify in the housing lease contract the amount of the deposit, the time for its return, and the circumstances under which the deposit may be deducted. Except for the circumstances specified in the housing lease contract, the lessor shall not deduct the deposit without justifiable reasons.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　承租人应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）向出租人出示身份证明材料；  
（二）安全、合理使用租赁住房，不得损坏、擅自拆除、停用消防设施或者改动租赁住房承重结构，不得私拉乱接水、电、燃气管线；  
（三）未经出租人同意，不得擅自改变租赁住房用途、拆改室内设施或者改动租赁住房其他结构；  
（四）遵守物业管理规约，不得任意弃置垃圾、超标准排放污染物或者产生噪声、违反规定饲养动物、违章搭建、侵占公共通道、高空抛物或者实施其他损害他人合法权益的行为；  
（五）对出租人依法确需进入租赁住房的，予以配合。

**Article 11** A lessee shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Present identity certification documents to the lessor;  
(2) Use the leased housing safely and reasonably, and shall not damage, dismantle without authorization, or discontinue the use of fire-fighting facilities, or alter the load-bearing structure of the leased housing, and shall not privately connect water, electricity, or gas pipelines in a disorderly manner;  
(3) Without the consent of the lessor, shall not change the use of the leased housing, dismantle or alter indoor facilities, or alter other structures of the leased housing without authorization;  
(4) Comply with the property management rules, and shall not arbitrarily dispose of garbage, discharge pollutants exceeding standards, or produce noise, raise animals in violation of regulations, build unauthorized structures, encroach upon common passages, throw objects from heights, or commit other acts that harm the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(5) Cooperate with the lessor when the lessor lawfully needs to enter the leased housing.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　出租人依法解除住房租赁合同的，应当通知承租人，并为承租人腾退租赁住房留出合理时间。  
出租人不得采取暴力、威胁或者其他非法方式迫使承租人解除住房租赁合同或者腾退租赁住房。

**Article 12** Where a lessor rescinds a housing lease contract according to law, the lessor shall notify the lessee and allow a reasonable period for the lessee to vacate the leased housing.  
A lessor shall not use violence, threats, or other illegal means to compel the lessee to rescind the housing lease contract or vacate the leased housing.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　住房租赁合同连续履行达到规定期限的，出租人按照有关规定享受相应的政策支持，承租人按照有关规定享受相应的基本公共服务。

**Article 13** Where a housing lease contract has been performed continuously for the prescribed period, the lessor shall be entitled to corresponding policy support in accordance with relevant provisions, and the lessee shall be entitled to corresponding basic public services in accordance with relevant provisions.

### 第三章　住房租赁企业

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家完善政策措施，培育市场化、专业化的住房租赁企业。  
住房租赁企业，是指以自有住房或者依法取得经营管理权的他人住房开展住房租赁经营业务的企业。住房租赁企业应当具备与其经营规模相适应的自有资金、从业人员和管理能力。  
住房租赁企业出租住房，适用本条例有关出租人的规定。

**Article 14** The State shall improve policies and measures to cultivate market-oriented and professional housing rental enterprises.  
A housing rental enterprise refers to an enterprise that carries out housing rental business using its own housing or housing of others over which it has lawfully obtained the right of operation and management. A housing rental enterprise shall have its own funds, employees, and management capabilities commensurate with its business scale.  
Where a housing rental enterprise leases housing, the provisions of these Regulations concerning lessors shall apply.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　住房租赁企业依法登记的经营范围应当使用“住房租赁”的表述。  
住房租赁企业应当自领取营业执照之日起30日内，向所在地县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门报送开业信息。房产管理部门应当将住房租赁企业开业信息向社会公开。  
住房租赁企业应当在其经营场所醒目位置公示本企业开业信息、服务规范和标准等。

**Article 15** The business scope registered by a housing rental enterprise according to law shall use the term "housing rental".  
A housing rental enterprise shall, within 30 days from the date of obtaining its business license, submit its business commencement information to the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level. The real estate administrative department shall make the business commencement information of the housing rental enterprise public.  
A housing rental enterprise shall display its business commencement information, service specifications and standards, etc., at a conspicuous position in its business premises.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　住房租赁企业发布的住房地址、面积、租金等房源信息应当真实、准确、完整，在其经营场所、互联网等不同渠道发布的房源信息应当一致，发布的房源图片应当与实物房源一致，不得发布虚假或者误导性房源信息，不得隐瞒或者拒绝提供拟出租住房的有关重要信息。

**Article 16** Housing listing information, including the address, area, rent, etc., published by a housing rental enterprise shall be true, accurate, and complete. The listing information published through different channels, such as its business premises and the Internet, shall be consistent, and the listing images published shall be consistent with the actual housing. A housing rental enterprise shall not publish false or misleading listing information, and shall not conceal or refuse to provide relevant important information about the housing to be leased.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　住房租赁企业应当建立住房租赁档案，如实记载相关信息，并健全住房租赁信息查验等内部管理制度。  
住房租赁企业不得非法收集、使用、加工、传输他人个人信息，不得非法买卖、提供或者公开他人个人信息。

**Article 17** A housing rental enterprise shall establish housing rental files, truthfully record relevant information, and improve internal management systems such as verification of housing rental information.  
A housing rental enterprise shall not illegally collect, use, process, or transmit personal information of others, and shall not illegally buy, sell, provide, or disclose personal information of others.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　住房租赁企业应当按照规定向所在地县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门报送其经营的租赁住房信息及其变化情况。

**Article 18** A housing rental enterprise shall, in accordance with regulations, report to the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level the information on the leased housing under its operation and any changes thereto.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事转租经营的住房租赁企业应当按照规定设立住房租赁资金监管账户并向社会公示，并通过该账户办理住房租赁资金收付业务，具体办法由国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 19** A housing rental enterprise engaged in subleasing operations shall, in accordance with regulations, establish a housing rental fund supervision account and make it public, and shall conduct housing rental fund receipt and payment transactions through such account. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　自然人转租他人住房开展住房租赁经营业务，经营规模达到国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院市场监督管理部门等有关部门规定标准的，适用本条例有关住房租赁企业的规定。

**Article 20** Where a natural person subleases housing of others to carry out housing rental business, and the business scale reaches the standards prescribed by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council jointly with the market supervision and administration department of the State Council and other relevant departments, the provisions of these Regulations concerning housing rental enterprises shall apply.

### 第四章　经纪机构

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事住房租赁业务的房地产经纪机构（以下简称住房租赁经纪机构）应当具备与其经营规模相适应的自有资金、从业人员和管理能力。

**Article 21** A real estate brokerage agency engaged in housing rental business (hereinafter referred to as a housing rental brokerage agency) shall have its own funds, employees, and management capabilities commensurate with its business scale.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　住房租赁经纪机构应当将其从业人员名单向所在地县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门备案。住房租赁经纪机构的从业人员不得同时在两个或者两个以上住房租赁经纪机构从事业务。  
住房租赁经纪机构的从业人员不得以个人名义承接住房租赁经纪业务。

**Article 22** A housing rental brokerage agency shall file the list of its employees with the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level. An employee of a housing rental brokerage agency shall not concurrently engage in business at two or more housing rental brokerage agencies.  
An employee of a housing rental brokerage agency shall not undertake housing rental brokerage business in his or her own name.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　住房租赁经纪机构发布房源信息前，应当核对并记录委托人的身份信息、住房权属信息，实地查看房源，与委托人签订住房租赁经纪服务合同，编制住房状况说明书。住房租赁经纪服务合同、住房状况说明书应当加盖住房租赁经纪机构印章，并按照有关规定保存。

**Article 23** Before publishing housing listing information, a housing rental brokerage agency shall verify and record the identity information of the client and the housing ownership information, inspect the housing on site, sign a housing rental brokerage service contract with the client, and prepare a housing condition statement. The housing rental brokerage service contract and the housing condition statement shall be affixed with the seal of the housing rental brokerage agency and shall be kept in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　[本条例第十五条](#art-15)第二款和第三款、第十六条、第十七条的规定，适用于住房租赁经纪机构。

**Article 24** The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 15, Article 16, and Article 17 of these Regulations shall apply to housing rental brokerage agencies.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　住房租赁经纪机构不得有下列行为：  
（一）为出租不符合建筑、消防、燃气、室内装饰装修等方面的法律、法规、规章或者强制性标准的住房提供经纪服务；  
（二）为单独出租厨房、卫生间、阳台、过道、地下储藏室、车库等非居住空间用于居住提供经纪服务；  
（三）为单间租住人数上限或者人均最低租住面积不符合规定标准的住房出租提供经纪服务；  
（四）代收、代付住房租金、押金；  
（五）未经当事人同意，以当事人名义签订住房租赁合同；  
（六）法律、法规、规章禁止从事的其他行为。

**Article 25** A housing rental brokerage agency shall not engage in the following acts:  
(1) Providing brokerage services for the lease of housing that does not comply with the laws, regulations, rules, or mandatory standards concerning construction, fire safety, gas, interior decoration, etc.;  
(2) Providing brokerage services for the separate lease of non-residential spaces such as kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, hallways, underground storage rooms, or garages for residential use;  
(3) Providing brokerage services for the lease of housing where the maximum number of occupants per room or the minimum average living area per person does not meet the prescribed standards;  
(4) Collecting or paying housing rent or deposits on behalf of either party;  
(5) Signing a housing lease contract in the name of a party without the party's consent;  
(6) Other acts prohibited by laws, regulations, or rules.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　住房租赁经纪机构应当对收费服务项目明码标价，不得在标价之外加价或者收取未予标明的费用。

**Article 26** A housing rental brokerage agency shall clearly mark the prices for its fee-based service items, and shall not increase prices beyond the marked prices or charge any fees not clearly marked.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　出租人和承租人通过住房租赁经纪机构签订住房租赁合同的，由住房租赁经纪机构办理住房租赁合同备案。

**Article 27** Where a lessor and a lessee sign a housing lease contract through a housing rental brokerage agency, the housing rental brokerage agency shall handle the record-filing of the housing lease contract.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院市场监督管理部门制定并公布住房租赁合同、住房租赁经纪服务合同示范文本。

**Article 28** The competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council shall, jointly with the market supervision and administration department of the State Council, formulate and publish model texts for housing lease contracts and housing rental brokerage service contracts.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　设区的市级以上地方人民政府应当建立住房租金监测机制，定期公布本行政区域内不同区域、不同类型住房的租金水平信息。

**Article 29** Local people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall establish a housing rent monitoring mechanism and regularly publish information on rent levels for different areas and types of housing within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门应当通过住房租赁管理服务平台开展合同备案、租赁住房信息管理、统计监测等管理与服务，并与民政、自然资源、教育、市场监督管理、金融管理、公安、税务、统计等部门建立信息共享机制。

**Article 30** The real estate administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, through a housing rental management service platform, carry out management and services such as contract record-filing, leased housing information management, and statistical monitoring, and shall establish an information sharing mechanism with departments of civil affairs, natural resources, education, market supervision and administration, financial management, public security, taxation, and statistics.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门应当会同有关部门加强对住房租赁活动的监督检查，依法及时处理违法行为。

**Article 31** The real estate administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments, strengthen supervision and inspection of housing rental activities and handle illegal acts in a timely manner according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门可以委托实施单位承担住房租赁管理的支持辅助等相关具体工作。房产管理部门委托的实施单位不得以营利为目的。  
县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门应当对其委托的实施单位加强监督，并对实施单位在委托范围内的行为后果承担法律责任。

**Article 32** The real estate administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level may entrust an implementation unit to undertake specific tasks related to support and assistance in housing rental management. The implementation unit entrusted by the real estate administrative department shall not operate for profit.  
The real estate administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision over the implementation unit it entrusts, and shall bear legal liability for the consequences of the acts of the implementation unit within the scope of the entrustment.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门应当会同有关部门、住房租赁相关行业组织加强住房租赁行业诚信建设，建立住房租赁企业、住房租赁经纪机构及其从业人员信用评价制度，将相关违法违规行为记入信用记录，纳入全国信用信息共享平台，并根据信用状况实施分级分类监管。

**Article 33** The real estate administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments and industry organizations related to housing rental, strengthen the integrity building of the housing rental industry, establish a credit evaluation system for housing rental enterprises, housing rental brokerage agencies, and their employees, record relevant illegal or non-compliant acts in credit records, incorporate them into the national credit information sharing platform, and implement graded and classified supervision based on credit status.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　住房租赁相关行业组织应当建立健全行业规范，加强行业自律管理，促进行业健康发展。

**Article 34** Industry organizations related to housing rental shall establish and improve industry norms, strengthen industry self-discipline management, and promote the healthy development of the industry.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在住房租赁活动中，因押金返还、住房维修、住房腾退等产生纠纷的，由出租人和承租人协商解决；协商不成的，可以依法申请调解、仲裁或者提起诉讼。

**Article 35** Where disputes arise in housing rental activities over the return of deposits, housing repairs, vacating of housing, etc., the lessor and the lessee shall resolve them through negotiation; if negotiation fails, they may apply for mediation, arbitration, or initiate litigation according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　提供住房租赁信息发布服务的网络平台经营者（以下简称网络平台经营者）应当核验住房租赁信息发布者的真实身份信息。  
网络平台经营者知道或者应当知道信息发布者有提供虚假信息等违法情形的，应当依法采取删除相关信息等必要措施，保存相关记录，并向有关部门报告。  
网络平台经营者不得代收、代付住房租金、押金。

**Article 36** A network platform operator that provides housing rental information publication services (hereinafter referred to as a network platform operator) shall verify the true identity information of the housing rental information publisher.  
Where a network platform operator knows or should know that the information publisher has illegal circumstances such as providing false information, the operator shall take necessary measures such as deleting the relevant information according to law, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant departments.  
A network platform operator shall not collect or pay housing rent or deposits on behalf of either party.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　出租人应当按规定如实登记并报送承租人及实际居住人信息，发现违法犯罪活动应当及时向公安机关报告。具体办法由国务院公安部门制定。  
承租人不得利用租赁住房实施违法犯罪活动。

**Article 37** A lessor shall truthfully register and submit information on the lessee and actual occupants in accordance with regulations, and shall promptly report to the public security organ upon discovering illegal or criminal activities. The specific measures shall be formulated by the public security department of the State Council.  
A lessee shall not use the leased housing to carry out illegal or criminal activities.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　有下列情形之一的，依照有关法律、法规、规章的规定处罚：  
（一）出租不符合建筑、消防、燃气、室内装饰装修等方面的法律、法规、规章或者强制性标准的住房；  
（二）损坏、擅自拆除、停用消防设施；  
（三）改动租赁住房承重结构；  
（四）私拉乱接水、电、燃气管线。

**Article 38** Under any of the following circumstances, penalties shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules:  
(1) Leasing housing that does not comply with the laws, regulations, rules, or mandatory standards concerning construction, fire safety, gas, interior decoration, etc.;  
(2) Damaging, dismantling without authorization, or discontinuing the use of fire-fighting facilities;  
(3) Altering the load-bearing structure of the leased housing;  
(4) Privately connecting water, electricity, or gas pipelines in a disorderly manner.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　将厨房、卫生间、阳台、过道、地下储藏室、车库等非居住空间单独出租用于居住，或者租赁住房不符合规定的单间租住人数上限或者人均最低租住面积标准的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对个人处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 39** Where non-residential spaces such as kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, hallways, underground storage rooms, or garages are separately leased for residential use, or where the leased housing does not meet the prescribed maximum number of occupants per room or the minimum average living area per person standard, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and give a warning; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　住房租赁企业、住房租赁经纪机构不具备与其经营规模相适应的自有资金、从业人员、管理能力的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正、造成严重后果的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 40** Where a housing rental enterprise or a housing rental brokerage agency does not have its own funds, employees, or management capabilities commensurate with its business scale, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and give a warning; if correction is refused and serious consequences are caused, the department shall order a suspension of business for rectification.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　住房租赁企业、住房租赁经纪机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告；拒不改正的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定报送开业信息；  
（二）未在其经营场所醒目位置公示本企业开业信息、服务规范和标准等；  
（三）未建立住房租赁档案并如实记载相关信息，或者未健全住房租赁信息查验等内部管理制度；  
（四）未按照规定办理住房租赁合同备案。  
住房租赁企业未按照规定报送其经营的租赁住房信息及其变化情况，或者住房租赁经纪机构未按照规定与委托人签订住房租赁经纪服务合同或者未编制住房状况说明书的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 41** Where a housing rental enterprise or a housing rental brokerage agency falls under any of the following circumstances, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and give a warning; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) Failing to report business commencement information in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Failing to display its business commencement information, service specifications and standards, etc., at a conspicuous position in its business premises;  
(3) Failing to establish housing rental files and truthfully record relevant information, or failing to improve internal management systems such as verification of housing rental information;  
(4) Failing to handle the record-filing of the housing lease contract in accordance with regulations.  
Where a housing rental enterprise fails to report the information on the leased housing under its operation and any changes thereto in accordance with regulations, or a housing rental brokerage agency fails to sign a housing rental brokerage service contract with the client or fails to prepare a housing condition statement in accordance with regulations, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　住房租赁企业、住房租赁经纪机构发布虚假或者误导性房源信息，隐瞒或者拒绝提供拟出租住房有关重要信息的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿；情节严重的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门提请同级市场监督管理部门依法吊销其营业执照。  
住房租赁企业、住房租赁经纪机构非法收集、使用、加工、传输他人个人信息，或者非法买卖、提供或者公开他人个人信息的，依照有关个人信息保护的法律、法规的规定处罚。

**Article 42** Where a housing rental enterprise or a housing rental brokerage agency publishes false or misleading housing listing information, or conceals or refuses to provide relevant important information about the housing to be leased, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if correction is refused, the department shall order a suspension of business for rectification; if the circumstances are serious, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall request the market supervision and administration department at the same level to revoke its business license according to law.  
Where a housing rental enterprise or a housing rental brokerage agency illegally collects, uses, processes, or transmits personal information of others, or illegally buys, sells, provides, or discloses personal information of others, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the laws and regulations on personal information protection.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　从事转租经营的住房租赁企业未按照规定设立住房租赁资金监管账户并向社会公示，或者未按照规定通过该账户办理住房租赁资金收付业务的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where a housing rental enterprise engaged in subleasing operations fails to establish a housing rental fund supervision account and make it public in accordance with regulations, or fails to conduct housing rental fund receipt and payment transactions through such account in accordance with regulations, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　住房租赁经纪机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿：  
（一）发布房源信息前未核对并记录委托人的身份信息、住房权属信息，或者未实地查看房源；  
（二）为出租不符合建筑、消防、燃气、室内装饰装修等方面的法律、法规、规章或者强制性标准的住房提供经纪服务；  
（三）为单独出租厨房、卫生间、阳台、过道、地下储藏室、车库等非居住空间用于居住提供经纪服务；  
（四）为单间租住人数上限或者人均最低租住面积不符合规定标准的住房出租提供经纪服务；  
（五）代收、代付住房租金、押金；  
（六）未经当事人同意，以当事人名义签订住房租赁合同；  
（七）未按规定将其从业人员名单备案。  
网络平台经营者代收、代付住房租金、押金的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 44** Where a housing rental brokerage agency falls under any of the following circumstances, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if correction is refused, the department shall order a suspension of business for rectification:  
(1) Failing to verify and record the identity information of the client or the housing ownership information before publishing housing listing information, or failing to inspect the housing on site;  
(2) Providing brokerage services for the lease of housing that does not comply with the laws, regulations, rules, or mandatory standards concerning construction, fire safety, gas, interior decoration, etc.;  
(3) Providing brokerage services for the separate lease of non-residential spaces such as kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, hallways, underground storage rooms, or garages for residential use;  
(4) Providing brokerage services for the lease of housing where the maximum number of occupants per room or the minimum average living area per person does not meet the prescribed standards;  
(5) Collecting or paying housing rent or deposits on behalf of either party;  
(6) Signing a housing lease contract in the name of a party without the party's consent;  
(7) Failing to file the list of its employees in accordance with regulations.  
Where a network platform operator collects or pays housing rent or deposits on behalf of either party, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　住房租赁经纪机构的从业人员同时在两个或者两个以上住房租赁经纪机构从事业务，或者以个人名义承接住房租赁经纪业务的，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，给予警告，可以处1万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；拒不改正的，1年内不得从事住房租赁经纪业务；情节严重的，5年内不得从事住房租赁经纪业务。

**Article 45** Where an employee of a housing rental brokerage agency concurrently engages in business at two or more housing rental brokerage agencies, or undertakes housing rental brokerage business in his or her own name, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, give a warning, and may impose a fine of not more than RMB 10,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if correction is refused, the employee shall be prohibited from engaging in housing rental brokerage business for one year; if the circumstances are serious, the employee shall be prohibited from engaging in housing rental brokerage business for five years.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　网络平台经营者未履行核验责任的，县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门应当通报网信部门，由网信部门依据职责予以处置，可以处10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以责令暂停相关业务或者停业整顿。

**Article 46** Where a network platform operator fails to perform its verification responsibilities, the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall notify the cyberspace administration department, which shall handle the matter in accordance with its duties and may impose a fine of not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the cyberspace administration department may order a suspension of relevant business or a suspension of business for rectification.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　房产管理部门和其他有关部门工作人员在住房租赁活动监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 47** Where any staff member of the real estate administrative department or other relevant departments abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in favoritism or irregularities in the supervision and management of housing rental activities, the staff member shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where any violation of these Regulations causes loss to others, the violator shall bear compensation liability according to law; where the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administrative penalty according to law; where the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　保障性住房的租赁活动及其监督管理，国家另有规定的，从其规定。  
集体土地上住房租赁活动，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据实际情况，制定管理办法。

**Article 49** Where there are separate provisions of the State on the rental activities of indemnificatory housing and the supervision and management thereof, such provisions shall prevail.  
For housing rental activities on collectively-owned land, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, based on actual conditions, formulate administrative measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例自2025年9月15日起施行。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 15, 2025.
