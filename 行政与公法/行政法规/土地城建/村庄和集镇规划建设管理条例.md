### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强村庄、集镇的规划建设管理，改善村庄、集镇的生产、生活环境，促进农村经济和社会发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the planning and construction management of villages and market towns, improving the production and living environment of villages and market towns, and promoting the development of rural economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　制定和实施村庄、集镇规划，在村庄、集镇规划区内进行居民住宅、乡(镇)村企业、乡(镇)村公共设施和公益事业等的建设，必须遵守本条例。但是，国家征用集体所有的土地进行的建设除外。  
在城市规划区内的村庄、集镇规划的制定和实施，依照城市规划法及其实施条例执行。

**Article 2** The formulation and implementation of plans for villages and market towns, and the construction of residential houses, township (town) and village enterprises, township (town) and village public facilities and public welfare undertakings within the planned areas of villages and market towns must comply with these Regulations. However, construction conducted by the State on expropriated collectively-owned land shall be excluded.  
The formulation and implementation of plans for villages and market towns located within urban planning areas shall be carried out in accordance with the Urban Planning Law and its implementing regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本条例所称村庄，是指农村村民居住和从事各种生产的聚居点。  
本条例所称集镇，是指乡、民族乡人民政府所在地和经县级人民政府确认由集市发展而成的作为农村一定区域经济、文化和生活服务中心的非建制镇。  
本条例所称村庄、集镇规划区，是指村庄、集镇建成区和因村庄、集镇建设及发展需要实行规划控制的区域。村庄、集镇规划区的具体范围，在村庄、集镇总体规划中划定。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Regulations, "villages" refer to settlements where rural residents live and engage in various production activities.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "market towns" refer to the seats of the people's governments of townships and ethnic townships, and unincorporated towns confirmed by the people's government at the county level as having developed from fairs and serving as economic, cultural and life service centers in certain rural areas.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "planned areas of villages and market towns" refer to the built-up areas of villages and market towns and areas subject to planning control due to the needs of construction and development of villages and market towns. The specific scope of planned areas of villages and market towns shall be delineated in the overall plans of the villages and market towns.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　村庄、集镇规划建设管理，应当坚持合理布局、节约用地的原则，全面规划，正确引导，依靠群众，自力更生，因地制宜，量力而行，逐步建设，实现经济效益、社会效益和环境效益的统一。

**Article 4** The planning, construction and management of villages and market towns shall adhere to the principles of rational layout and economical land use, comprehensive planning, correct guidance, reliance on the masses, self-reliance, adaptation to local conditions, action within capabilities, and gradual construction, so as to achieve the unity of economic, social and environmental benefits.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地处洪涝、地震、台风、滑坡等自然灾害易发地区的村庄和集镇，应当按照国家和地方的有关规定，在村庄、集镇总体规划中制定防灾措施。

**Article 5** Villages and market towns located in areas prone to natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, typhoons, and landslides shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and localities, formulate disaster prevention measures in their overall plans.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院建设行政主管部门主管全国的村庄、集镇规划建设管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门主管本行政区域的村庄、集镇规划建设管理工作。  
乡级人民政府负责本行政区域的村庄、集镇规划建设管理工作。

**Article 6** The administrative department of construction under the State Council shall be in charge of the planning, construction and management of villages and market towns nationwide.  
The administrative departments of construction of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of the planning, construction and management of villages and market towns within their respective administrative areas.  
The people's government at the township level shall be responsible for the planning, construction and management of villages and market towns within its administrative area.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励村庄、集镇规划建设管理的科学研究，推广先进技术，提倡在村庄和集镇建设中，结合当地特点，采用新工艺、新材料、新结构。

**Article 7** The State encourages scientific research on the planning, construction and management of villages and market towns, promotes advanced technologies, and advocates the adoption of new techniques, new materials, and new structures in the construction of villages and market towns in light of local characteristics.

### 第二章　村庄和集镇规划的制定

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　村庄、集镇规划由乡级人民政府负责组织编制，并监督实施。

**Article 8** The plans for villages and market towns shall be organized and formulated by the people's government at the township level, which shall also supervise their implementation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　村庄、集镇规划的编制，应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)根据国民经济和社会发展计划，结合当地经济发展的现状和要求，以及自然环境、资源条件和历史情况等，统筹兼顾，综合部署村庄和集镇的各项建设；  
(二)处理好近期建设与远景发展、改造与新建的关系，使村庄、集镇的性质和建设的规模、速度和标准，同经济发展和农民生活水平相适应；  
(三)合理用地，节约用地，各项建设应当相对集中，充分利用原有建设用地，新建、扩建工程及住宅应当尽量不占用耕地和林地；  
(四)有利生产，方便生活，合理安排住宅、乡(镇)村企业、乡(镇)村公共设施和公益事业等的建设布局，促进农村各项事业协调发展，并适当留有发展余地；  
(五)保护和改善生态环境，防治污染和其他公害，加强绿化和村容镇貌、环境卫生建设。

**Article 9** The formulation of plans for villages and market towns shall follow the following principles:  
(1) In light of the national economic and social development plan, and taking into consideration the current situation and needs of local economic development, as well as the natural environment, resource conditions, and historical circumstances, overall planning shall be made and various construction projects in villages and market towns shall be comprehensively arranged;  
(2) The relationship between short-term construction and long-term development, and between renovation and new construction shall be properly handled, so as to make the nature and the scale, speed, and standards of construction of villages and market towns commensurate with economic development and the living standards of farmers;  
(3) Land shall be used rationally and economically, all types of construction shall be relatively concentrated, existing construction land shall be fully utilized, and new construction, extension projects and residential houses shall, as far as possible, not occupy arable land or forestland;  
(4) Production shall be facilitated and living conditions improved, the construction layout of residential houses, township (town) and village enterprises, township (town) and village public facilities and public welfare undertakings shall be rationally arranged to promote the coordinated development of various rural undertakings, with due allowance for future development;  
(5) The ecological environment shall be protected and improved, pollution and other public hazards shall be prevented and controlled, and afforestation, village appearance and environmental sanitation shall be strengthened.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　村庄、集镇规划的编制，应当以县域规划、农业区划、土地利用总体规划为依据，并同有关部门的专业规划相协调。  
县级人民政府组织编制的县域规划，应当包括村庄、集镇建设体系规划。

**Article 10** The formulation of plans for villages and market towns shall be based on the county plan, agricultural regionalization, and overall land use planning, and shall be coordinated with the specialized plans of relevant departments.  
The county plan organized and formulated by the people's government at the county level shall include the system plan for the construction of villages and market towns.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　编制村庄、集镇规划，一般分为村庄、集镇总体规划和村庄、集镇建设规划两个阶段进行。

**Article 11** The formulation of plans for villages and market towns shall generally be carried out in two stages: the overall plan for villages and market towns and the construction plan for villages and market towns.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　村庄、集镇总体规划，是乡级行政区域内村庄和集镇布点规划及相应的各项建设的整体部署。  
村庄、集镇总体规划的主要内容包括：乡级行政区域的村庄、集镇布点，村庄和集镇的位置、性质、规模和发展方向，村庄和集镇的交通、供水、供电、邮电、商业、绿化等生产和生活服务设施的配置。

**Article 12** The overall plan for villages and market towns is the overall deployment of the layout of villages and market towns and the corresponding various constructions within the administrative area at the township level.  
The main contents of the overall plan for villages and market towns include: the layout of villages and market towns within the administrative area at the township level, the locations, nature, scale, and development direction of villages and market towns, and the configuration of production and living service facilities such as transportation, water supply, power supply, postal and telecommunications, commerce, and greening in villages and market towns.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　村庄、集镇建设规划，应当在村庄、集镇总体规划指导下，具体安排村庄、集镇的各项建设。  
集镇建设规划的主要内容包括：住宅、乡(镇)村企业、乡(镇)村公共设施、公益事业等各项建设的用地布局、用地规模，有关的技术经济指标，近期建设工程以及重点地段建设具体安排。  
村庄建设规划的主要内容，可以根据本地区经济发展水平，参照集镇建设规划的编制内容，主要对住宅和供水、供电、道路、绿化、环境卫生以及生产配套设施作出具体安排。

**Article 13** The construction plan for villages and market towns shall, under the guidance of the overall plan for villages and market towns, concretely arrange various constructions in villages and market towns.  
The main contents of the construction plan for market towns include: the land use layout and land use scale for various constructions such as residential houses, township (town) and village enterprises, township (town) and village public facilities, and public welfare undertakings, relevant technical and economic indicators, short-term construction projects, and specific arrangements for construction in key areas.  
The main contents of the construction plan for villages may, based on the level of economic development of the region and with reference to the contents of the construction plan for market towns, mainly make specific arrangements for residential houses, water supply, power supply, roads, greening, environmental sanitation, and production supporting facilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　村庄、集镇总体规划和集镇建设规划，须经乡级人民代表大会审查同意，由乡级人民政府报县级人民政府批准。  
村庄建设规划，须经村民会议讨论同意，由乡级人民政府报县级人民政府批准。

**Article 14** The overall plan for villages and market towns and the construction plan for market towns shall be subject to the examination and approval of the people's congress at the township level, and shall be submitted by the people's government at the township level to the people's government at the county level for approval.  
The construction plan for villages shall be discussed and approved by the villagers' meeting, and shall be submitted by the people's government at the township level to the people's government at the county level for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　根据社会经济发展需要，依照[本条例第十四条](#art-14)的规定，经乡级人民代表大会或者村民会议同意，乡级人民政府可以对村庄、集镇规划进行局部调整，并报县级人民政府备案。涉及村庄、集镇的性质、规模、发展方向和总体布局重大变更的，依照[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定的程序办理。

**Article 15** In light of the needs of social and economic development, and in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of these Regulations, upon the consent of the people's congress at the township level or the villagers' meeting, the people's government at the township level may make partial adjustments to the plans for villages and market towns, and shall report such adjustments to the people's government at the county level for the record. Where major changes involving the nature, scale, development direction, or overall layout of villages and market towns are involved, the procedures prescribed in Article 14 of these Regulations shall be followed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　村庄、集镇规划期限，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本地区实际情况规定。

**Article 16** The planning period for villages and market towns shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government according to the actual conditions of the region.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　村庄、集镇规划经批准后，由乡级人民政府公布。

**Article 17** After the plans for villages and market towns are approved, they shall be published by the people's government at the township level.

### 第三章　村庄和集镇规划的实施

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农村村民在村庄、集镇规划区内建住宅的，应当先向村集体经济组织或者村民委员会提出建房申请，经村民会议讨论通过后，按照下列审批程序办理：  
(一)需要使用耕地的，经乡级人民政府审核、县级人民政府建设行政主管部门审查同意并出具选址意见书后，方可依照《土地管理法》向县级人民政府土地管理部门申请用地，经县级人民政府批准后，由县级人民政府土地管理部门划拨土地；  
(二)使用原有宅基地、村内空闲地和其他土地的，由乡级人民政府根据村庄、集镇规划和土地利用规划批准。  
城镇非农业户口居民在村庄、集镇规划区内需要使用集体所有的土地建住宅的，应当经其所在单位或者居民委员会同意后，依照前款第(一)项规定的审批程序办理。  
回原籍村庄、集镇落户的职工、退伍军人和离休、退休干部以及回乡定居的华侨、港澳台同胞，在村庄、集镇规划区内需要使用集体所有的土地建住宅的，依照本条第一款第(一)项规定的审批程序办理。

**Article 18** Where a rural resident intends to build a residential house within the planned area of a village or a market town, he or she shall first submit an application for building a house to the collective economic organization of the village or the villagers' committee, and after the application is discussed and adopted by the villagers' meeting, the following approval procedures shall be followed:  
(1) If arable land is needed, upon examination by the people's government at the township level and review and consent by the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level, which shall issue a site selection opinion letter, the applicant may apply to the land administration department of the people's government at the county level for land use in accordance with the Land Administration Law, and after approval by the people's government at the county level, the land administration department of the people's government at the county level shall allocate the land;  
(2) If the original homestead, idle land within the village, or other land is used, the application shall be approved by the people's government at the township level in accordance with the plan for the village or market town and the land use plan.  
Where a resident with non-agricultural household registration in a town or city needs to use collectively-owned land within the planned area of a village or a market town to build a residential house, the resident shall, upon consent of his or her work unit or the residents' committee, go through the approval procedures as prescribed in subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph.  
Where workers, demobilized soldiers, retired cadres, and returned overseas Chinese, compatriots from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan who settle down in the villages or market towns of their original domicile need to use collectively-owned land within the planned area of a village or a market town to build a residential house, they shall go through the approval procedures as prescribed in subparagraph (1) of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　兴建乡(镇)村企业，必须持县级以上地方人民政府批准的设计任务书或者其他批准文件，向县级人民政府建设行政主管部门申请选址定点，县级人民政府建设行政主管部门审查同意并出具选址意见书后，建设单位方可依法向县级人民政府土地管理部门申请用地，经县级以上人民政府批准后，由土地管理部门划拨土地。

**Article 19** To construct a township (town) or village enterprise, the construction entity must hold the design task document approved by the local people's government at or above the county level or other approval documents, apply to the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level for site selection and location, and after the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level reviews and consents and issues a site selection opinion letter, the construction entity may apply to the land administration department of the people's government at the county level for land use according to law, and after approval by the people's government at or above the county level, the land administration department shall allocate the land.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　乡(镇)村公共设施、公益事业建设，须经乡级人民政府审核、县级人民政府建设行政主管部门审查同意并出具选址意见书后，建设单位方可依法向县级人民政府土地管理部门申请用地，经县级以上人民政府批准后，由土地管理部门划拨土地。

**Article 20** For the construction of township (town) and village public facilities and public welfare undertakings, the construction entity may apply to the land administration department of the people's government at the county level for land use according to law only after the examination by the people's government at the township level and the review and consent of the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level, which shall issue a site selection opinion letter. After approval by the people's government at or above the county level, the land administration department shall allocate the land.

### 第四章　村庄和集镇建设的设计、施工管理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在村庄、集镇规划区内，凡建筑跨度、跨径或者高度超出规定范围的乡(镇)村企业、乡(镇)村公共设施和公益事业的建筑工程，以及二层(含二层)以上的住宅，必须由取得相应的设计资质证书的单位进行设计，或者选用通用设计、标准设计。  
跨度、跨径和高度的限定，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者其授权的部门规定。

**Article 21** Within the planned areas of villages and market towns, all construction projects of township (town) and village enterprises, township (town) and village public facilities, and public welfare undertakings with a building span, clear span, or height exceeding the prescribed limits, as well as residential buildings of two or more storeys (including two storeys), must be designed by a unit that has obtained the corresponding design qualification certificate, or shall adopt standard designs or universal designs.  
The limits of span, clear span, and height shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government or by the departments authorized thereby.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　建筑设计应当贯彻适用、经济、安全和美观的原则，符合国家和地方有关节约资源、抗御灾害的规定，保持地方特色和民族风格，并注意与周围环境相协调。  
农村居民住宅设计应当符合紧凑、合理、卫生和安全的要求。

**Article 22** Architectural design shall implement the principles of suitability, economy, safety, and aesthetics, comply with the provisions of the State and localities on resource conservation and disaster resistance, preserve local characteristics and ethnic style, and maintain harmony with the surrounding environment.  
The design of rural residential houses shall meet the requirements of compactness, rationality, sanitation, and safety.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　承担村庄、集镇规划区内建筑工程施工任务的单位，必须具有相应的施工资质等级证书或者资质审查证书，并按照规定的经营范围承担施工任务。  
在村庄、集镇规划区内从事建筑施工的个体工匠，除承担房屋修缮外，须按有关规定办理施工资质审批手续。

**Article 23** Any unit undertaking construction tasks within the planned areas of villages and market towns must possess the corresponding construction qualification grade certificate or qualification review certificate, and shall undertake construction tasks within the prescribed scope of business.  
Individual craftsmen engaged in construction within the planned areas of villages and market towns, except for undertaking house repair, shall go through the formalities for examination and approval of construction qualifications in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　施工单位应当按照设计图纸施工。任何单位和个人不得擅自修改设计图纸；确需修改的，须经原设计单位同意，并出具变更设计通知单或者图纸。

**Article 24** The construction unit shall carry out construction in accordance with the design drawings. No unit or individual may alter the design drawings without authorization; if any modification is truly necessary, it shall be subject to the consent of the original design unit, and a design change notice or drawings shall be issued.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　施工单位应当确保施工质量，按照有关的技术规定施工，不得使用不符合工程质量要求的建筑材料和建筑构件。

**Article 25** The construction unit shall ensure construction quality, carry out construction in accordance with the relevant technical specifications, and shall not use building materials or building components that do not meet the quality requirements of the project.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　乡(镇)村企业、乡(镇)村公共设施、公益事业等建设，在开工前，建设单位和个人应当向县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门提出开工申请，经县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门对设计、施工条件予以审查批准后，方可开工。  
农村居民住宅建设开工的审批程序，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 26** Before the commencement of construction of township (town) and village enterprises, township (town) and village public facilities, and public welfare undertakings, the construction entity or individual shall submit an application for commencement to the administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level, and may commence construction only after the design and construction conditions have been examined and approved by the said department.  
The approval procedures for the commencement of rural residential house construction shall be prescribed by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级人民政府建设行政主管部门，应当对村庄、集镇建设的施工质量进行监督检查。村庄、集镇的建设工程竣工后，应当按照国家的有关规定，经有关部门竣工验收合格后，方可交付使用。

**Article 27** The administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level shall supervise and inspect the construction quality of villages and market towns. After the completion of construction projects in villages and market towns, the projects shall be subject to completion inspection and acceptance by the relevant departments in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and may be delivered for use only after passing such inspection and acceptance.

### 第五章　房屋、公共设施、村容镇貌和环境卫生管理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门，应当加强对村庄、集镇房屋的产权、产籍的管理，依法保护房屋所有人对房屋的所有权。具体办法由国务院建设行政主管部门制定。

**Article 28** The administrative departments of construction of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the management of property rights and property registration of houses in villages and market towns, and protect the ownership of house owners over their houses according to law. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　任何单位和个人都应当遵守国家和地方有关村庄、集镇的房屋、公共设施的管理规定，保证房屋的使用安全和公共设施的正常使用，不得破坏或者损毁村庄、集镇的道路、桥梁、供水、排水、供电、邮电、绿化等设施。

**Article 29** All units and individuals shall abide by the provisions of the State and localities on the management of houses and public facilities in villages and market towns, ensure the safe use of houses and the normal use of public facilities, and shall not damage or destroy the roads, bridges, water supply, drainage, power supply, postal and telecommunications, greening, and other facilities in villages and market towns.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从集镇收取的城市维护建设税，应当用于集镇公共设施的维护和建设，不得挪作他用。

**Article 30** The urban maintenance and construction tax collected from market towns shall be used for the maintenance and construction of public facilities in market towns, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　乡级人民政府应当采取措施，保护村庄、集镇饮用水源；有条件的地方，可以集中供水，使水质逐步达到国家规定的生活饮用水卫生标准。

**Article 31** The people's government at the township level shall take measures to protect the drinking water sources of villages and market towns; where conditions permit, centralized water supply may be provided, so as to gradually make the water quality meet the national hygiene standards for drinking water for daily life.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　未经乡级人民政府批准，任何单位和个人不得擅自在村庄、集镇规划区内的街道、广场、市场和车站等场所修建临时建筑物、构筑物和其他设施。

**Article 32** Without the approval of the people's government at the township level, no unit or individual may, without authorization, construct temporary buildings, structures, or other facilities on streets, squares, markets, stations, or other places within the planned areas of villages and market towns.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　任何单位和个人都应当维护村容镇貌和环境卫生，妥善处理粪堆、垃圾堆、柴草堆，养护树木花草，美化环境。

**Article 33** All units and individuals shall maintain the village and town appearance and environmental sanitation, properly dispose of dung heaps, garbage heaps, and firewood piles, tend trees, flowers, and grass, and beautify the environment.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　任何单位和个人都有义务保护村庄、集镇内的文物古迹、古树名木和风景名胜、军事设施、防汛设施，以及国家邮电、通信、输变电、输油管道等设施，不得损坏。

**Article 34** All units and individuals have the obligation to protect cultural relics and historic sites, ancient and famous trees, scenic spots, military installations, flood control facilities, and State postal, telecommunications, power transmission and transformation, oil pipeline, and other facilities within villages and market towns, and shall not damage them.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　乡级人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，对村庄、集镇建设中形成的具有保存价值的文件、图纸、资料等及时整理归档。

**Article 35** The people's government at the township level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, sort out and file in a timely manner the documents, drawings, data, and other materials of preservation value generated during the construction of villages and market towns.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在村庄、集镇规划区内，未按规划审批程序批准而取得建设用地批准文件，占用土地的，批准文件无效，占用的土地由乡级以上人民政府责令退回。

**Article 36** Where, within the planned areas of villages and market towns, a land use approval document is obtained and land is occupied without approval in accordance with the planning approval procedures, the approval document shall be null and void, and the occupied land shall be ordered to be returned by the people's government at or above the township level.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在村庄、集镇规划区内，未按规划审批程序批准或者违反规划的规定进行建设，严重影响村庄、集镇规划的，由县级人民政府建设行政主管部门责令停止建设，限期拆除或者没收违法建筑物、构筑物和其他设施；影响村庄、集镇规划，尚可采取改正措施的，由县级人民政府建设行政主管部门责令限期改正，处以罚款。  
农村居民未经批准或者违反规划的规定建住宅的，乡级人民政府可以依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 37** Where, within the planned areas of villages and market towns, construction is carried out without approval in accordance with the planning approval procedures or in violation of the provisions of the plan, and such construction seriously affects the plan for the village or market town, the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level shall order the construction to be stopped and the illegal buildings, structures, or other facilities to be demolished within a specified period or confiscated; where the construction affects the plan for the village or market town but corrective measures can still be taken, the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level shall order corrections within a specified period and impose a fine.  
Where a rural resident builds a residential house without approval or in violation of the provisions of the plan, the people's government at the township level may impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　有下列行为之一的，由县级人民政府建设行政主管部门责令停止设计或者施工、限期改正，并可处以罚款：  
(一)未取得设计资质证书，承担建筑跨度、跨径和高度超出规定范围的工程以及二层以上住宅的设计任务或者未按设计资质证书规定的经营范围，承担设计任务的；  
(二)未取得施工资质等级证书或者资质审查证书或者未按规定的经营范围，承担施工任务的；  
(三)不按有关技术规定施工或者使用不符合工程质量要求的建筑材料和建筑构件的；  
(四)未按设计图纸施工或者擅自修改设计图纸的。  
取得设计或者施工资质证书的勘察设计、施工单位，为无证单位提供资质证书，超过规定的经营范围，承担设计、施工任务或者设计、施工的质量不符合要求，情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销设计或者施工的资质证书。

**Article 38** Any unit or individual that commits one of the following acts shall be ordered by the administrative department of construction of the people's government at the county level to cease the design or construction, make corrections within a specified period, and may also be fined:  
(1) Undertaking design tasks for projects with a building span, clear span, or height exceeding the prescribed limits, or for residential buildings of two or more storeys, without obtaining a design qualification certificate, or undertaking design tasks beyond the scope of business prescribed in the design qualification certificate;  
(2) Undertaking construction tasks without obtaining a construction qualification grade certificate or qualification review certificate, or undertaking construction tasks beyond the prescribed scope of business;  
(3) Carrying out construction not in accordance with the relevant technical specifications, or using building materials or building components that do not meet the quality requirements of the project;  
(4) Carrying out construction not in accordance with the design drawings, or altering the design drawings without authorization.  
Where a survey, design, or construction unit that has obtained a design or construction qualification certificate provides its qualification certificate to an unlicensed unit, undertakes design or construction tasks beyond the prescribed scope of business, or the quality of design or construction fails to meet the requirements, and the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the design or construction qualification certificate.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　有下列行为之一的，由乡级人民政府责令停止侵害，可以处以罚款；造成损失的，并应当赔偿：  
(一)损坏村庄和集镇的房屋、公共设施的；  
(二)乱堆粪便、垃圾、柴草，破坏村容镇貌和环境卫生的。

**Article 39** Any person that commits one of the following acts shall be ordered by the people's government at the township level to cease the infringement, and may be fined; if losses are caused, compensation shall also be paid:  
(1) Damaging houses or public facilities in villages or market towns;  
(2) Piling dung, garbage, or firewood in a disorderly manner, damaging the village or town appearance or environmental sanitation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　擅自在村庄、集镇规划区内的街道、广场、市场和车站等场所修建临时建筑物、构筑物和其他设施的，由乡级人民政府责令限期拆除，并可处以罚款。

**Article 40** Anyone that, without authorization, constructs temporary buildings, structures, or other facilities on streets, squares, markets, stations, or other places within the planned areas of villages and market towns shall be ordered by the people's government at the township level to demolish them within a specified period, and may also be fined.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　损坏村庄、集镇内的文物古迹、古树名木和风景名胜、军事设施、防汛设施，以及国家邮电、通信、输变电、输油管道等设施的，依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚。

**Article 41** Anyone that damages cultural relics and historic sites, ancient and famous trees, scenic spots, military installations, flood control facilities, or State postal, telecommunications, power transmission and transformation, oil pipeline, or other facilities within villages or market towns shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例，构成违反治安管理行为的，依照治安管理处罚条例的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes an act against public security administration, the offender shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　村庄、集镇建设管理人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由所在单位或者上级主管部门给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where a person in charge of the construction management of villages and market towns neglects his or her duty, abuses his or her power, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, he or she shall be given an administrative sanction by the unit to which he or she belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定之日起15日内，向人民法院提起诉讼。当事人也可以自接到处罚决定通知之日起15日内，直接向人民法院起诉。当事人逾期不申请复议，也不向人民法院提起诉讼，又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的机关可以申请人民法院强制执行或者依法强制执行。

**Article 44** Where a party is dissatisfied with a decision on administrative penalty, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the penalty decision; if the party is dissatisfied with the reconsideration decision, the party may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision, file a lawsuit with the People's Court. The party may also, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the notice of the penalty decision, directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court. If the party neither applies for reconsideration nor institutes legal proceedings within the prescribed period, nor complies with the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution or carry out compulsory execution according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　未设镇建制的国营农场场部、国营林场场部及其基层居民点的规划建设管理，分别由国营农场、国营林场主管部门负责，参照本条例执行。

**Article 45** The planning, construction and management of the headquarters of State farms and State forestry centers that have not been established as towns, and their grassroots residential areas, shall be respectively responsible by the competent departments of the State farms and State forestry centers, and shall be implemented with reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 46** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementing measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本条例由国务院建设行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 47** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the administrative department of construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例自一九九三年十一月一日起施行。

**Article 48** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 1993.
