# 基础测绘条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-05-12  
**生效日期**：2009-08-01

> (2009年5月6日国务院第62次常务会议通过　2009年5月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第556号公布　自2009年8月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强基础测绘管理，规范基础测绘活动，保障基础测绘事业为国家经济建设、国防建设和社会发展服务，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事基础测绘活动，适用本条例。 本条例所称基础测绘，是指建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统，进行基础航空摄影，获取基础地理信息的遥感资料，测制和更新国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品，建立、更新基础地理信息系统。 在中华人民共和国领海、中华人民共和国领海基线向陆地一侧至海岸线的海域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋基础测绘活动，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。 第三条　基础测绘是公益性事业。 县级以上人民政府应当加强对基础测绘工作的领导，将基础测绘纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划及年度计划，所需经费列入本级财政预算。 国家对边远地区和少数民族地区的基础测绘给予财政支持。具体办法由财政部门会同同级测绘行政主管部门制定。 第四条　基础测绘工作应当遵循统筹规划、分级管理、定期更新、保障安全的原则。 第五条　国务院测绘行政主管部门负责全国基础测绘工作的统一监督管理。 县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘工作的行政部门(以下简称测绘行政主管部门)负责本行政区域基础测绘工作的统一监督管理。 第六条　国家鼓励在基础测绘活动中采用先进科学技术和先进设备，加强基础研究和信息化测绘体系建设，建立统一的基础地理信息公共服务平台，实现基础地理信息资源共享，提高基础测绘保障服务能力。 第二章　基础测绘规划 第七条　国务院测绘行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘主管部门，组织编制全国基础测绘规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强基础测绘管理，规范基础测绘活动，保障基础测绘事业为国家经济建设、国防建设和社会发展服务，根据《中华人民共和国测绘法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Surveying and Mapping Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the management of basic surveying and mapping, regulating basic surveying and mapping activities, and ensuring that the basic surveying and mapping undertakings serve the national economic construction, national defense construction, and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事基础测绘活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称基础测绘，是指建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统，进行基础航空摄影，获取基础地理信息的遥感资料，测制和更新国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品，建立、更新基础地理信息系统。  
在中华人民共和国领海、中华人民共和国领海基线向陆地一侧至海岸线的海域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事海洋基础测绘活动，按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 2** These Regulations apply to basic surveying and mapping activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, basic surveying and mapping means establishing a unified national surveying and mapping datum and surveying and mapping system, conducting basic aerial photography, obtaining remote sensing data for basic geographic information, producing and updating national basic scale maps, image maps, and digital products, and establishing and updating basic geographic information systems.  
Marine basic surveying and mapping activities conducted in the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, the sea areas from the territorial sea baseline of the People's Republic of China landward to the coastline, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　基础测绘是公益性事业。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强对基础测绘工作的领导，将基础测绘纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划及年度计划，所需经费列入本级财政预算。  
国家对边远地区和少数民族地区的基础测绘给予财政支持。具体办法由财政部门会同同级测绘行政主管部门制定。

**Article 3** Basic surveying and mapping is a public welfare undertaking.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen their leadership over basic surveying and mapping, incorporate basic surveying and mapping into their respective national economic and social development plans and annual plans, and include the required expenditures in their respective fiscal budgets.  
The State shall provide financial support for basic surveying and mapping in remote areas and ethnic minority areas. Specific measures shall be formulated by the financial department together with the administrative department of surveying and mapping at the same level.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　基础测绘工作应当遵循统筹规划、分级管理、定期更新、保障安全的原则。

**Article 4** Basic surveying and mapping work shall follow the principles of overall planning, tiered management, regular updating, and ensuring security.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院测绘行政主管部门负责全国基础测绘工作的统一监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责管理测绘工作的行政部门(以下简称测绘行政主管部门)负责本行政区域基础测绘工作的统一监督管理。

**Article 5** The administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of basic surveying and mapping nationwide.  
The administrative department in charge of surveying and mapping of a local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the administrative department of surveying and mapping) shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of basic surveying and mapping within its administrative region.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励在基础测绘活动中采用先进科学技术和先进设备，加强基础研究和信息化测绘体系建设，建立统一的基础地理信息公共服务平台，实现基础地理信息资源共享，提高基础测绘保障服务能力。

**Article 6** The State encourages the adoption of advanced science, technology, and equipment in basic surveying and mapping activities, strengthens basic research and the construction of an information-based surveying and mapping system, establishes a unified public service platform for basic geographic information, realizes the sharing of basic geographic information resources, and improves the capability of basic surveying and mapping to provide support services.

### 第二章　基础测绘规划

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院测绘行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘主管部门，组织编制全国基础测绘规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府测绘行政主管部门会同本级人民政府其他有关部门，根据国家和上一级人民政府的基础测绘规划和本行政区域的实际情况，组织编制本行政区域的基础测绘规划，报本级人民政府批准，并报上一级测绘行政主管部门备案后组织实施。

**Article 7** The administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council, together with other relevant departments under the State Council and the competent department of surveying and mapping of the military, shall organize the formulation of a national basic surveying and mapping plan, which shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The administrative department of surveying and mapping of a local people's government at or above the county level, together with other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, shall, based on the basic surveying and mapping plans of the State and the people's government at the next higher level and the actual conditions of its administrative region, organize the formulation of a basic surveying and mapping plan for its administrative region, which shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval and to the administrative department of surveying and mapping at the next higher level for filing before implementation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　基础测绘规划报送审批前，组织编制机关应当组织专家进行论证，并征求有关部门和单位的意见。其中，地方的基础测绘规划，涉及军事禁区、军事管理区或者作战工程的，还应当征求军事机关的意见。  
基础测绘规划报送审批文件中应当附具意见采纳情况及理由。

**Article 8** Before a basic surveying and mapping plan is submitted for approval, the organizing formulation authority shall organize expert demonstration and solicit opinions from relevant departments and entities. Where a local basic surveying and mapping plan involves military restricted zones, military management zones, or combat engineering, opinions from military authorities shall also be solicited.  
The submission document for the approval of a basic surveying and mapping plan shall include an explanation of the opinions adopted and the reasons therefor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　组织编制机关应当依法公布经批准的基础测绘规划。  
经批准的基础测绘规划是开展基础测绘工作的依据，未经法定程序不得修改；确需修改的，应当按照本条例规定的原审批程序报送审批。

**Article 9** The organizing formulation authority shall publish the approved basic surveying and mapping plan according to law.  
The approved basic surveying and mapping plan shall be the basis for carrying out basic surveying and mapping work and shall not be modified without legal procedures; if modification is truly necessary, it shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the original approval procedures specified in these Regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院发展改革部门会同国务院测绘行政主管部门，编制全国基础测绘年度计划。  
县级以上地方人民政府发展改革部门会同同级测绘行政主管部门，编制本行政区域的基础测绘年度计划，并分别报上一级主管部门备案。

**Article 10** The development and reform department under the State Council, together with the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council, shall formulate a national annual plan for basic surveying and mapping.  
The development and reform department of a local people's government at or above the county level, together with the administrative department of surveying and mapping at the same level, shall formulate an annual plan for basic surveying and mapping for its administrative region and submit it to the competent department at the next higher level for filing.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当根据应对自然灾害等突发事件的需要，制定相应的基础测绘应急保障预案。  
基础测绘应急保障预案的内容应当包括：应急保障组织体系，应急装备和器材配备，应急响应，基础地理信息数据的应急测制和更新等应急保障措施。

**Article 11** The administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the need to respond to natural disasters and other emergencies, formulate corresponding emergency support plans for basic surveying and mapping.  
The content of an emergency support plan for basic surveying and mapping shall include: the emergency support organization system, emergency equipment and apparatus, emergency response, emergency production and updating of basic geographic information data, and other emergency support measures.

### 第三章　基础测绘项目的组织实施

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　下列基础测绘项目，由国务院测绘行政主管部门组织实施：  
(一)建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统；  
(二)建立和更新国家基础地理信息系统；  
(三)组织实施国家基础航空摄影；  
(四)获取国家基础地理信息遥感资料；  
(五)测制和更新全国1∶100万至1∶2.5万国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品；  
(六)国家急需的其他基础测绘项目。

**Article 12** The following basic surveying and mapping projects shall be organized and implemented by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council:  
(1) establishing a unified national surveying and mapping datum and surveying and mapping system;  
(2) establishing and updating the national basic geographic information system;  
(3) organizing and implementing national basic aerial photography;  
(4) obtaining remote sensing data for national basic geographic information;  
(5) producing and updating national basic scale maps, image maps, and digital products from 1:1,000,000 to 1:25,000; and  
(6) other basic surveying and mapping projects urgently needed by the State.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　下列基础测绘项目，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘行政主管部门组织实施：  
(一)建立本行政区域内与国家测绘系统相统一的大地控制网和高程控制网；  
(二)建立和更新地方基础地理信息系统；  
(三)组织实施地方基础航空摄影；  
(四)获取地方基础地理信息遥感资料；  
(五)测制和更新本行政区域1∶1万至1∶5000国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品。

**Article 13** The following basic surveying and mapping projects shall be organized and implemented by the administrative department of surveying and mapping of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) establishing a geodetic control network and an elevation control network within its administrative region that are unified with the national surveying and mapping system;  
(2) establishing and updating local basic geographic information systems;  
(3) organizing and implementing local basic aerial photography;  
(4) obtaining remote sensing data for local basic geographic information; and  
(5) producing and updating national basic scale maps, image maps, and digital products at scales of 1:10,000 to 1:5,000 for its administrative region.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　设区的市、县级人民政府依法组织实施1∶2000至1∶500比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品的测制和更新以及地方性法规、地方政府规章确定由其组织实施的基础测绘项目。

**Article 14** The people's government of a city divided into districts or a county shall, according to law, organize the production and updating of maps, image maps, and digital products at scales of 1:2,000 to 1:500, as well as basic surveying and mapping projects that are determined by local regulations or local government rules to be organized and implemented by them.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　组织实施基础测绘项目，应当依据基础测绘规划和基础测绘年度计划，依法确定基础测绘项目承担单位。

**Article 15** The organization and implementation of a basic surveying and mapping project shall, based on the basic surveying and mapping plan and the annual plan for basic surveying and mapping, determine the unit undertaking the basic surveying and mapping project according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　基础测绘项目承担单位应当具有与所承担的基础测绘项目相应等级的测绘资质，并不得超越其资质等级许可的范围从事基础测绘活动。  
基础测绘项目承担单位应当具备健全的保密制度和完善的保密设施，严格执行有关保守国家秘密法律、法规的规定。

**Article 16** The unit undertaking a basic surveying and mapping project shall have surveying and mapping qualifications corresponding to the grade of the basic surveying and mapping project it undertakes, and shall not engage in basic surveying and mapping activities beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
The unit undertaking a basic surveying and mapping project shall have a sound confidentiality system and complete confidentiality facilities, and shall strictly implement the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on the protection of state secrets.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事基础测绘活动，应当使用全国统一的大地基准、高程基准、深度基准、重力基准，以及全国统一的大地坐标系统、平面坐标系统、高程系统、地心坐标系统、重力测量系统，执行国家规定的测绘技术规范和标准。  
因建设、城市规划和科学研究的需要，确需建立相对独立的平面坐标系统的，应当与国家坐标系统相联系。

**Article 17** In engaging in basic surveying and mapping activities, it shall use the unified national geodetic datum, elevation datum, depth datum, gravity datum, as well as the unified national geodetic coordinate system, plane coordinate system, elevation system, geocentric coordinate system, and gravity measurement system, and shall implement the surveying and mapping technical specifications and standards prescribed by the State.  
If it is truly necessary to establish a relatively independent plane coordinate system due to the needs of construction, urban planning, and scientific research, it shall be connected to the national coordinate system.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当遵循科学规划、合理布局、有效利用、兼顾当前与长远需要的原则，加强基础测绘设施建设，避免重复投资。  
国家安排基础测绘设施建设资金，应当优先考虑航空摄影测量、卫星遥感、数据传输以及基础测绘应急保障的需要。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall follow the principles of scientific planning, rational layout, effective utilization, and balancing current and long-term needs, strengthen the construction of basic surveying and mapping facilities, and avoid duplicate investment.  
When the State allocates funds for the construction of basic surveying and mapping facilities, it shall give priority to the needs of aerial photogrammetry, satellite remote sensing, data transmission, and emergency support for basic surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家依法保护基础测绘设施。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、损毁、拆除或者擅自移动基础测绘设施。基础测绘设施遭受破坏的，县级以上地方人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当及时采取措施，组织力量修复，确保基础测绘活动正常进行。

**Article 19** The State shall protect basic surveying and mapping facilities according to law.  
No entity or individual may occupy, damage, demolish, or without authorization move basic surveying and mapping facilities. Where basic surveying and mapping facilities are damaged, the administrative department of surveying and mapping of the local people's government at or above the county level shall promptly take measures, organize forces to repair, and ensure the normal conduct of basic surveying and mapping activities.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当加强基础航空摄影和用于测绘的高分辨率卫星影像获取与分发的统筹协调，做好基础测绘应急保障工作，配备相应的装备和器材，组织开展培训和演练，不断提高基础测绘应急保障服务能力。  
自然灾害等突发事件发生后，县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当立即启动基础测绘应急保障预案，采取有效措施，开展基础地理信息数据的应急测制和更新工作。

**Article 20** The administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the overall coordination of basic aerial photography and the acquisition and distribution of high-resolution satellite images for surveying and mapping, do a good job in emergency support for basic surveying and mapping, equip corresponding equipment and apparatus, organize training and drills, and continuously improve the emergency support service capability of basic surveying and mapping.  
After the occurrence of natural disasters or other emergencies, the administrative department of surveying and mapping of the people's government at or above the county level shall immediately activate the emergency support plan for basic surveying and mapping, take effective measures, and carry out emergency production and updating of basic geographic information data.

### 第四章　基础测绘成果的更新与利用

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家实行基础测绘成果定期更新制度。  
基础测绘成果更新周期应当根据不同地区国民经济和社会发展的需要、测绘科学技术水平和测绘生产能力、基础地理信息变化情况等因素确定。其中，1∶100万至1∶5000国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品至少5年更新一次；自然灾害多发地区以及国民经济、国防建设和社会发展急需的基础测绘成果应当及时更新。  
基础测绘成果更新周期确定的具体办法，由国务院测绘行政主管部门会同军队测绘主管部门和国务院其他有关部门制定。

**Article 21** The State implements a system of regular updating of basic surveying and mapping results.  
The updating cycle of basic surveying and mapping results shall be determined based on factors such as the needs of national economic and social development in different regions, the level of surveying and mapping science and technology and surveying and mapping production capacity, and changes in basic geographic information. Among them, national basic scale maps, image maps, and digital products at scales of 1:1,000,000 to 1:5,000 shall be updated at least once every five years; basic surveying and mapping results in areas prone to natural disasters and those urgently needed for national economic, national defense construction, and social development shall be updated promptly.  
Specific measures for determining the updating cycle of basic surveying and mapping results shall be formulated by the administrative department of surveying and mapping under the State Council together with the competent department of surveying and mapping of the military and other relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当及时收集有关行政区域界线、地名、水系、交通、居民点、植被等地理信息的变化情况，定期更新基础测绘成果。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门和单位应当对测绘行政主管部门的信息收集工作予以支持和配合。

**Article 22** The administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level shall promptly collect changes in geographic information such as administrative boundaries, place names, water systems, transportation, residential areas, and vegetation, and regularly update basic surveying and mapping results.  
Other relevant departments and units of the people's government at or above the county level shall support and cooperate with the information collection work of the administrative department of surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　按照国家规定需要有关部门批准或者核准的测绘项目，有关部门在批准或者核准前应当书面征求同级测绘行政主管部门的意见，有适宜基础测绘成果的，应当充分利用已有的基础测绘成果，避免重复测绘。

**Article 23** For surveying and mapping projects that require approval or verification from relevant departments according to State regulations, the relevant department shall, before approval or verification, seek written opinions from the administrative department of surveying and mapping at the same level. Where suitable basic surveying and mapping results exist, the existing basic surveying and mapping results shall be fully utilized to avoid duplicate surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门应当采取措施，加强对基础地理信息测制、加工、处理、提供的监督管理，确保基础测绘成果质量。

**Article 24** The administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen the supervision and administration of the production, processing, processing, and provision of basic geographic information to ensure the quality of basic surveying and mapping results.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　基础测绘项目承担单位应当建立健全基础测绘成果质量管理制度，严格执行国家规定的测绘技术规范和标准，对其完成的基础测绘成果质量负责。

**Article 25** The unit undertaking a basic surveying and mapping project shall establish and improve the quality management system for basic surveying and mapping results, strictly implement the surveying and mapping technical specifications and standards prescribed by the State, and be responsible for the quality of the basic surveying and mapping results it completes.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　基础测绘成果的利用，按照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 26** The utilization of basic surveying and mapping results shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门和其他有关主管部门将基础测绘项目确定由不具有测绘资质或者不具有相应等级测绘资质的单位承担的，责令限期改正，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 27** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level or other relevant competent department assigns a basic surveying and mapping project to a unit that does not have surveying and mapping qualifications or does not have the corresponding grade of surveying and mapping qualifications, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, and the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门和其他有关主管部门的工作人员利用职务上的便利收受他人财物、其他好处，或者玩忽职守，不依法履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 28** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a staff member of the administrative department of surveying and mapping of a people's government at or above the county level or other relevant competent department, taking advantage of his position, accepts money, goods, or other benefits from others, or neglects his duty, fails to perform supervision and administration duties according to law, or fails to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, causing serious consequences, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例规定，未取得测绘资质证书从事基础测绘活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和测绘成果，并处测绘约定报酬1倍以上2倍以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit engages in basic surveying and mapping activities without obtaining a surveying and mapping qualification certificate, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed surveying and mapping remuneration shall be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　违反本条例规定，基础测绘项目承担单位超越资质等级许可的范围从事基础测绘活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和测绘成果，处测绘约定报酬1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿或者降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销测绘资质证书。

**Article 30** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit undertaking a basic surveying and mapping project engages in basic surveying and mapping activities beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed surveying and mapping remuneration shall be imposed, and the unit may be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违反本条例规定，实施基础测绘项目，不使用全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统或者不执行国家规定的测绘技术规范和标准的，责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处10万元以下罚款；对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 31** Where, in violation of these Regulations, in implementing a basic surveying and mapping project, a unit does not use the unified national surveying and mapping datum and surveying and mapping system or does not implement the surveying and mapping technical specifications and standards prescribed by the State, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, given a warning, and may be concurrently fined not more than 100,000 yuan; the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反本条例规定，侵占、损毁、拆除或者擅自移动基础测绘设施的，责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处5万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit or individual occupies, damages, demolishes, or without authorization moves basic surveying and mapping facilities, it shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, given a warning, and may be concurrently fined not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本条例规定，基础测绘成果质量不合格的，责令基础测绘项目承担单位补测或者重测；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级直至吊销测绘资质证书；给用户造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 33** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the quality of basic surveying and mapping results is unqualified, the unit undertaking the basic surveying and mapping project shall be ordered to make supplementary survey or re-survey; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused to users, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本条例规定的降低资质等级、吊销测绘资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书的部门决定；其他行政处罚由县级以上人民政府测绘行政主管部门决定。

**Article 34** The administrative penalty of lowering the qualification grade or revoking the surveying and mapping qualification certificate as provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the department that issued the qualification certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the administrative department of surveying and mapping of the people's government at or above the county level.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本条例自2009年8月1日起施行。

**Article 35** These Regulations shall come into force as of August 1, 2009.
