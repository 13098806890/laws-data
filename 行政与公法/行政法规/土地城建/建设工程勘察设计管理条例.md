# 建设工程勘察设计管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-10-07  
**生效日期**：2017-10-07

> (2000年9月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第293号公布　根据2015年6月12日《国务院关于修改〈建设工程勘察设计管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2017年10月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对建设工程勘察、设计活动的管理，保证建设工程勘察、设计质量，保护人民生命和财产安全，制定本条例。 第二条　从事建设工程勘察、设计活动，必须遵守本条例。 本条例所称建设工程勘察，是指根据建设工程的要求，查明、分析、评价建设场地的地质地理环境特征和岩土工程条件，编制建设工程勘察文件的活动。 本条例所称建设工程设计，是指根据建设工程的要求，对建设工程所需的技术、经济、资源、环境等条件进行综合分析、论证，编制建设工程设计文件的活动。 第三条　建设工程勘察、设计应当与社会、经济发展水平相适应，做到经济效益、社会效益和环境效益相统一。 第四条　从事建设工程勘察、设计活动，应当坚持先勘察、后设计、再施工的原则。 第五条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和交通、水利等有关部门应当依照本条例的规定，加强对建设工程勘察、设计活动的监督管理。 建设工程勘察、设计单位必须依法进行建设工程勘察、设计，严格执行工程建设强制性标准，并对建设工程勘察、设计的质量负责。 第六条　国家鼓励在建设工程勘察、设计活动中采用先进技术、先进工艺、先进设备、新型材料和现代管理方法。 第二章　资质资格管理 第七条　国家对从事建设工程勘察、设计活动的单位，实行资质管理制度。具体办法由国务院建设行政主管部门商国务院有关部门制定。 第八条　建设工程勘察、设计单位应当在其资质等级许可的范围内承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。 禁止建设工程勘察、设计单位超越其资质等级许可的范围或者以其他建设工程勘察、设计单位的名义承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。禁止建设工程勘察、设计单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位的名义承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对建设工程勘察、设计活动的管理，保证建设工程勘察、设计质量，保护人民生命和财产安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of survey and design activities of construction projects, ensuring the quality of survey and design of construction projects, and protecting the safety of life and property of the people.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事建设工程勘察、设计活动，必须遵守本条例。  
本条例所称建设工程勘察，是指根据建设工程的要求，查明、分析、评价建设场地的地质地理环境特征和岩土工程条件，编制建设工程勘察文件的活动。  
本条例所称建设工程设计，是指根据建设工程的要求，对建设工程所需的技术、经济、资源、环境等条件进行综合分析、论证，编制建设工程设计文件的活动。

**Article 2** Any entity or individual that engages in survey and design activities of construction projects must comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "survey of construction projects" means the activities of ascertaining, analyzing, and evaluating the geological and geographic environmental characteristics and geotechnical engineering conditions of the construction site, and compiling survey documents of construction projects in accordance with the requirements of the construction project.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, "design of construction projects" means the activities of conducting comprehensive analysis and demonstration of the technical, economic, resource, environmental and other conditions required for the construction project, and compiling design documents of construction projects in accordance with the requirements of the construction project.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　建设工程勘察、设计应当与社会、经济发展水平相适应，做到经济效益、社会效益和环境效益相统一。

**Article 3** The survey and design of construction projects shall be commensurate with the level of social and economic development, and achieve the unity of economic benefits, social benefits and environmental benefits.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事建设工程勘察、设计活动，应当坚持先勘察、后设计、再施工的原则。

**Article 4** Any entity or individual that engages in survey and design activities of construction projects shall adhere to the principle of survey first, then design, and then construction.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府建设行政主管部门和交通、水利等有关部门应当依照本条例的规定，加强对建设工程勘察、设计活动的监督管理。  
建设工程勘察、设计单位必须依法进行建设工程勘察、设计，严格执行工程建设强制性标准，并对建设工程勘察、设计的质量负责。

**Article 5** The competent administrative department of construction of the people's government at or above the county level and the relevant departments such as transportation and water resources shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, strengthen the supervision and administration of survey and design activities of construction projects.  
A survey or design unit of construction projects must conduct survey or design of construction projects according to law, strictly implement the mandatory standards for construction projects, and be responsible for the quality of survey or design of construction projects.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励在建设工程勘察、设计活动中采用先进技术、先进工艺、先进设备、新型材料和现代管理方法。

**Article 6** The State encourages the adoption of advanced technologies, advanced techniques, advanced equipment, new materials and modern management methods in survey and design activities of construction projects.

### 第二章　资质资格管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对从事建设工程勘察、设计活动的单位，实行资质管理制度。具体办法由国务院建设行政主管部门商国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 7** The State implements a qualification administration system for entities that engage in survey and design activities of construction projects. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　建设工程勘察、设计单位应当在其资质等级许可的范围内承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。  
禁止建设工程勘察、设计单位超越其资质等级许可的范围或者以其他建设工程勘察、设计单位的名义承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。禁止建设工程勘察、设计单位允许其他单位或者个人以本单位的名义承揽建设工程勘察、设计业务。

**Article 8** A survey or design unit of construction projects shall undertake survey or design business of construction projects within the scope permitted by its qualification grade.  
It is prohibited for a survey or design unit of construction projects to undertake survey or design business of construction projects beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade or in the name of another survey or design unit of construction projects. It is prohibited for a survey or design unit of construction projects to permit another entity or individual to undertake survey or design business of construction projects in its name.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家对从事建设工程勘察、设计活动的专业技术人员，实行执业资格注册管理制度。  
未经注册的建设工程勘察、设计人员，不得以注册执业人员的名义从事建设工程勘察、设计活动。

**Article 9** The State implements a practice qualification registration administration system for professional technicians who engage in survey and design activities of construction projects.  
A survey or design professional of construction projects who has not been registered shall not engage in survey or design activities of construction projects in the name of a registered practitioner.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建设工程勘察、设计注册执业人员和其他专业技术人员只能受聘于一个建设工程勘察、设计单位；未受聘于建设工程勘察、设计单位的，不得从事建设工程的勘察、设计活动。

**Article 10** A registered practitioner or other professional technician of survey and design of construction projects may only be employed by one survey or design unit of construction projects; those who are not employed by a survey or design unit of construction projects shall not engage in survey or design activities of construction projects.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设工程勘察、设计单位资质证书和执业人员注册证书，由国务院建设行政主管部门统一制作。

**Article 11** The qualification certificates of survey and design units of construction projects and the registration certificates of practitioners shall be uniformly produced by the competent administrative department of construction of the State Council.

### 第三章　建设工程勘察设计发包与承包

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　建设工程勘察、设计发包依法实行招标发包或者直接发包。

**Article 12** The contracting of survey and design of construction projects shall, according to law, be carried out by means of bidding and contracting or direct contracting.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设工程勘察、设计应当依照《中华人民共和国招标投标法》的规定，实行招标发包。

**Article 13** The survey and design of construction projects shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Bidding and Tendering, be carried out by means of bidding and contracting.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设工程勘察、设计方案评标，应当以投标人的业绩、信誉和勘察、设计人员的能力以及勘察、设计方案的优劣为依据，进行综合评定。

**Article 14** The evaluation of bids for survey and design schemes of construction projects shall be based on the performance and reputation of the bidder, the capability of the survey and design personnel, and the quality of the survey and design schemes, and a comprehensive assessment shall be conducted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　建设工程勘察、设计的招标人应当在评标委员会推荐的候选方案中确定中标方案。但是，建设工程勘察、设计的招标人认为评标委员会推荐的候选方案不能最大限度满足招标文件规定的要求的，应当依法重新招标。

**Article 15** The tenderer of survey and design of construction projects shall determine the winning scheme among the candidate schemes recommended by the bid evaluation committee. However, if the tenderer of survey and design of construction projects considers that the candidate schemes recommended by the bid evaluation committee cannot satisfy the requirements specified in the tender documents to the maximum extent, the tenderer shall conduct a new tender according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下列建设工程的勘察、设计，经有关主管部门批准，可以直接发包：  
(一)采用特定的专利或者专有技术的；  
(二)建筑艺术造型有特殊要求的；  
(三)国务院规定的其他建设工程的勘察、设计。

**Article 16** The survey and design of the following construction projects may, upon approval by the relevant competent department, be directly contracted:  
(1) those adopting specific patents or proprietary technologies;  
(2) those with special requirements for architectural art form;  
(3) the survey and design of other construction projects as prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　发包方不得将建设工程勘察、设计业务发包给不具有相应勘察、设计资质等级的建设工程勘察、设计单位。

**Article 17** The contract letting party shall not contract the survey or design business of a construction project to a survey or design unit of construction projects that does not have the corresponding qualification grade for survey or design.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　发包方可以将整个建设工程的勘察、设计发包给一个勘察、设计单位；也可以将建设工程的勘察、设计分别发包给几个勘察、设计单位。

**Article 18** The contract letting party may contract the survey and design of the entire construction project to one survey or design unit; it may also contract the survey and design of the construction project separately to several survey or design units.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　除建设工程主体部分的勘察、设计外，经发包方书面同意，承包方可以将建设工程其他部分的勘察、设计再分包给其他具有相应资质等级的建设工程勘察、设计单位。

**Article 19** Except for the survey and design of the main part of a construction project, with the written consent of the contract letting party, the contractor may subcontract the survey and design of other parts of the construction project to other survey or design units of construction projects that have the corresponding qualification grade.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建设工程勘察、设计单位不得将所承揽的建设工程勘察、设计转包。

**Article 20** A survey or design unit of construction projects shall not subcontract the survey or design of a construction project that it has undertaken.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　承包方必须在建设工程勘察、设计资质证书规定的资质等级和业务范围内承揽建设工程的勘察、设计业务。

**Article 21** The contractor must undertake survey or design business of a construction project within the qualification grade and business scope specified in the survey or design qualification certificate of construction projects.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　建设工程勘察、设计的发包方与承包方，应当执行国家规定的建设工程勘察、设计程序。

**Article 22** The contract letting party and the contractor of survey and design of a construction project shall implement the procedures for survey and design of construction projects as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　建设工程勘察、设计的发包方与承包方应当签订建设工程勘察、设计合同。

**Article 23** The contract letting party and the contractor of survey and design of a construction project shall enter into a contract for survey and design of a construction project.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建设工程勘察、设计发包方与承包方应当执行国家有关建设工程勘察费、设计费的管理规定。

**Article 24** The contract letting party and the contractor of survey and design of a construction project shall implement the State's administrative provisions on survey fees and design fees for construction projects.

### 第四章　建设工程勘察设计文件的编制与实施

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　编制建设工程勘察、设计文件，应当以下列规定为依据：  
(一)项目批准文件；  
(二)城乡规划；  
(三)工程建设强制性标准；  
(四)国家规定的建设工程勘察、设计深度要求。  
铁路、交通、水利等专业建设工程，还应当以专业规划的要求为依据。

**Article 25** The compilation of survey and design documents of a construction project shall be based on the following provisions:  
(1) project approval documents;  
(2) urban and rural planning;  
(3) mandatory standards for construction projects;  
(4) the depth requirements for survey and design of construction projects as prescribed by the State.  
For specialized construction projects such as railways, transportation, and water resources, they shall also be based on the requirements of specialized planning.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　编制建设工程勘察文件，应当真实、准确，满足建设工程规划、选址、设计、岩土治理和施工的需要。  
编制方案设计文件，应当满足编制初步设计文件和控制概算的需要。  
编制初步设计文件，应当满足编制施工招标文件、主要设备材料订货和编制施工图设计文件的需要。  
编制施工图设计文件，应当满足设备材料采购、非标准设备制作和施工的需要，并注明建设工程合理使用年限。

**Article 26** The compilation of survey documents of a construction project shall be truthful and accurate to meet the needs of planning, site selection, design, geotechnical treatment and construction of the construction project.  
The compilation of scheme design documents shall meet the needs of compiling preliminary design documents and controlling budgetary estimates.  
The compilation of preliminary design documents shall meet the needs of compiling construction tender documents, ordering major equipment and materials, and compiling construction drawing design documents.  
The compilation of construction drawing design documents shall meet the needs of procurement of equipment and materials, manufacture of non-standard equipment, and construction, and shall indicate the reasonable service life of the construction project.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　设计文件中选用的材料、构配件、设备，应当注明其规格、型号、性能等技术指标，其质量要求必须符合国家规定的标准。  
除有特殊要求的建筑材料、专用设备和工艺生产线等外，设计单位不得指定生产厂、供应商。

**Article 27** The materials, components, and equipment selected in design documents shall indicate their technical specifications such as specifications, models, and performance, and their quality requirements must comply with the standards prescribed by the State.  
Except for building materials, special equipment, and production lines with special requirements, the design unit shall not designate manufacturers or suppliers.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　建设单位、施工单位、监理单位不得修改建设工程勘察、设计文件；确需修改建设工程勘察、设计文件的，应当由原建设工程勘察、设计单位修改。经原建设工程勘察、设计单位书面同意，建设单位也可以委托其他具有相应资质的建设工程勘察、设计单位修改。修改单位对修改的勘察、设计文件承担相应责任。  
施工单位、监理单位发现建设工程勘察、设计文件不符合工程建设强制性标准、合同约定的质量要求的，应当报告建设单位，建设单位有权要求建设工程勘察、设计单位对建设工程勘察、设计文件进行补充、修改。  
建设工程勘察、设计文件内容需要作重大修改的，建设单位应当报经原审批机关批准后，方可修改。

**Article 28** The construction entity, construction unit, and supervision unit shall not modify the survey or design documents of a construction project; if modification is truly necessary, it shall be made by the original survey or design unit of the construction project. With the written consent of the original survey or design unit, the construction entity may also entrust another survey or design unit with the corresponding qualifications to make the modification. The modifying unit shall bear corresponding liability for the modified survey or design documents.  
If the construction unit or supervision unit discovers that the survey or design documents of a construction project do not comply with the mandatory standards for construction projects or the quality requirements agreed in the contract, it shall report to the construction entity, and the construction entity has the right to require the survey or design unit of the construction project to supplement or modify the survey or design documents.  
If the content of the survey or design documents of a construction project requires major modification, the construction entity shall report to the original approving authority for approval before making the modification.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　建设工程勘察、设计文件中规定采用的新技术、新材料，可能影响建设工程质量和安全，又没有国家技术标准的，应当由国家认可的检测机构进行试验、论证，出具检测报告，并经国务院有关部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关部门组织的建设工程技术专家委员会审定后，方可使用。

**Article 29** If the new technologies and new materials specified in the survey or design documents of a construction project may affect the quality and safety of the construction project and there is no national technical standard, they shall be tested and demonstrated by a testing institution recognized by the State, which shall issue a testing report, and may only be used after being examined and approved by the construction technical expert committee organized by the relevant department of the State Council or the relevant department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　建设工程勘察、设计单位应当在建设工程施工前，向施工单位和监理单位说明建设工程勘察、设计意图，解释建设工程勘察、设计文件。  
建设工程勘察、设计单位应当及时解决施工中出现的勘察、设计问题。

**Article 30** A survey or design unit of a construction project shall, before the construction of the construction project, explain the survey and design intent of the construction project to the construction unit and the supervision unit, and interpret the survey and design documents of the construction project.  
The survey or design unit of a construction project shall promptly solve the survey and design problems arising during construction.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院建设行政主管部门对全国的建设工程勘察、设计活动实施统一监督管理。国务院铁路、交通、水利等有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责对全国的有关专业建设工程勘察、设计活动的监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府建设行政主管部门对本行政区域内的建设工程勘察、设计活动实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府交通、水利等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责对本行政区域内的有关专业建设工程勘察、设计活动的监督管理。

**Article 31** The competent administrative department of construction of the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the survey and design activities of construction projects throughout the country. The relevant departments of the State Council, such as railways, transportation, and water resources, shall, in accordance with the division of responsibilities prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the survey and design activities of relevant specialized construction projects throughout the country.  
The competent administrative department of construction of the local people's government at or above the county level shall exercise supervision and administration over the survey and design activities of construction projects within its administrative region. The relevant departments such as transportation and water resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the survey and design activities of relevant specialized construction projects within their administrative regions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　建设工程勘察、设计单位在建设工程勘察、设计资质证书规定的业务范围内跨部门、跨地区承揽勘察、设计业务的，有关地方人民政府及其所属部门不得设置障碍，不得违反国家规定收取任何费用。

**Article 32** If a survey or design unit of a construction project undertakes survey or design business across departments or regions within the business scope specified in the survey or design qualification certificate of construction projects, the relevant local people's government and its subordinate departments shall not set up obstacles and shall not charge any fees in violation of State regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　施工图设计文件审查机构应当对房屋建筑工程、市政基础设施工程施工图设计文件中涉及公共利益、公众安全、工程建设强制性标准的内容进行审查。县级以上人民政府交通运输等有关部门应当按照职责对施工图设计文件中涉及公共利益、公众安全、工程建设强制性标准的内容进行审查。  
施工图设计文件未经审查批准的，不得使用。

**Article 33** The review institution for construction drawing design documents shall review the contents of construction drawing design documents of housing construction projects and municipal infrastructure projects that involve public interests, public safety, and mandatory standards for construction projects. The relevant departments such as transportation of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their duties, review the contents of construction drawing design documents that involve public interests, public safety, and mandatory standards for construction projects.  
Construction drawing design documents that have not been reviewed and approved shall not be used.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　任何单位和个人对建设工程勘察、设计活动中的违法行为都有权检举、控告、投诉。

**Article 34** Any entity or individual has the right to report, accuse, and complain about illegal acts in survey and design activities of construction projects.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的，责令停止违法行为，处合同约定的勘察费、设计费1倍以上2倍以下的罚款，有违法所得的，予以没收；可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。  
未取得资质证书承揽工程的，予以取缔，依照前款规定处以罚款；有违法所得的，予以没收。  
以欺骗手段取得资质证书承揽工程的，吊销资质证书，依照本条第一款规定处以罚款；有违法所得的，予以没收。

**Article 35** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 8 of these Regulations shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the survey fee or design fee agreed in the contract shall be imposed; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.  
If a project is undertaken without obtaining a qualification certificate, it shall be banned, and a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
If a project is undertaken by obtaining a qualification certificate by fraudulent means, the qualification certificate shall be revoked, and a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，未经注册，擅自以注册建设工程勘察、设计人员的名义从事建设工程勘察、设计活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下罚款；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 36** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, without registration, engages in survey or design activities of construction projects in the name of a registered survey or design professional of construction projects shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be imposed a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if losses are caused to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例规定，建设工程勘察、设计注册执业人员和其他专业技术人员未受聘于一个建设工程勘察、设计单位或者同时受聘于两个以上建设工程勘察、设计单位，从事建设工程勘察、设计活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以责令停止执行业务或者吊销资格证书；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 37** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a registered practitioner or other professional technician of survey and design of construction projects, not being employed by one survey or design unit of construction projects or being employed by two or more survey or design units of construction projects at the same time, engages in survey or design activities of construction projects shall be ordered to cease the illegal act, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be imposed a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, it may be ordered to cease practice or have its qualification certificate revoked; if losses are caused to others, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，发包方将建设工程勘察、设计业务发包给不具有相应资质等级的建设工程勘察、设计单位的，责令改正，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 38** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the contract letting party contracts the survey or design business of a construction project to a survey or design unit of construction projects that does not have the corresponding qualification grade shall be ordered to make corrections and be imposed a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，建设工程勘察、设计单位将所承揽的建设工程勘察、设计转包的，责令改正，没收违法所得，处合同约定的勘察费、设计费25%以上50%以下的罚款，可以责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书。

**Article 39** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a survey or design unit of construction projects subcontracts the survey or design of a construction project that it has undertaken shall be ordered to make corrections, have its illegal gains confiscated, and be imposed a fine of not less than 25% but not more than 50% of the survey fee or design fee agreed in the contract; it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，勘察、设计单位未依据项目批准文件，城乡规划及专业规划，国家规定的建设工程勘察、设计深度要求编制建设工程勘察、设计文件的，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；造成工程质量事故或者环境污染和生态破坏的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级；情节严重的，吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 40** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a survey or design unit fails to compile survey or design documents of a construction project on the basis of the project approval documents, urban and rural planning and specialized planning, and the depth requirements for survey and design of construction projects as prescribed by the State shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the act causes a quality accident of the project or environmental pollution and ecological damage, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification and have its qualification grade lowered; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，依照[《建设工程质量管理条例》第六十三条](../../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/建设工程质量管理条例.md#art-63)的规定给予处罚：  
(一)勘察单位未按照工程建设强制性标准进行勘察的；  
(二)设计单位未根据勘察成果文件进行工程设计的；  
(三)设计单位指定建筑材料、建筑构配件的生产厂、供应商的；  
(四)设计单位未按照工程建设强制性标准进行设计的。

**Article 41** Anyone who, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits one of the following acts shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 63 of the Regulations on the Administration of Quality of Construction Projects:  
(1) the survey unit fails to conduct survey in accordance with the mandatory standards for construction projects;  
(2) the design unit fails to carry out engineering design based on the survey results documents;  
(3) the design unit designates the manufacturers or suppliers of building materials and building components;  
(4) the design unit fails to carry out design in accordance with the mandatory standards for construction projects.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例规定的责令停业整顿、降低资质等级和吊销资质证书、资格证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书、资格证书的机关决定；其他行政处罚，由建设行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依据法定职权范围决定。  
依照本条例规定被吊销资质证书的，由工商行政管理部门吊销其营业执照。

**Article 42** The administrative penalties of ordering suspension of business for rectification, lowering of qualification grade, and revocation of qualification certificates and practice certificates provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the authority that issued the qualification certificates and practice certificates; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the competent administrative department of construction or other relevant departments in accordance with their statutory scope of authority.  
If a qualification certificate is revoked in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家机关工作人员在建设工程勘察、设计活动的监督管理工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 43** If a functionary of a State organ, in the supervision and administration of survey and design activities of construction projects, neglects duties, abuses powers, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be given according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　抢险救灾及其他临时性建筑和农民自建两层以下住宅的勘察、设计活动，不适用本条例。

**Article 44** These Regulations do not apply to the survey and design activities of emergency rescue and disaster relief and other temporary buildings, and residential houses with less than two floors built by farmers themselves.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　军事建设工程勘察、设计的管理，按照中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 45** The administration of survey and design of military construction projects shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
