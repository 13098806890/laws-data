### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国土地管理法》（以下简称《土地管理法》），制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Land Administration Law").

### 第二章　国土空间规划

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家建立国土空间规划体系。  
土地开发、保护、建设活动应当坚持规划先行。经依法批准的国土空间规划是各类开发、保护、建设活动的基本依据。  
已经编制国土空间规划的，不再编制土地利用总体规划和城乡规划。在编制国土空间规划前，经依法批准的土地利用总体规划和城乡规划继续执行。

**Article 2** The State shall establish a territorial spatial planning system.  
Land development, protection, and construction activities shall adhere to the principle of planning first. The territorial spatial planning approved according to law shall be the basic basis for various development, protection and construction activities.  
Where a territorial spatial plan has been formulated, no general land use plan or urban and rural plan shall be formulated. Before the formulation of a territorial spatial plan, the general land use plan and urban and rural plan approved according to law shall continue to be implemented.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国土空间规划应当细化落实国家发展规划提出的国土空间开发保护要求，统筹布局农业、生态、城镇等功能空间，划定落实永久基本农田、生态保护红线和城镇开发边界。  
国土空间规划应当包括国土空间开发保护格局和规划用地布局、结构、用途管制要求等内容，明确耕地保有量、建设用地规模、禁止开垦的范围等要求，统筹基础设施和公共设施用地布局，综合利用地上地下空间，合理确定并严格控制新增建设用地规模，提高土地节约集约利用水平，保障土地的可持续利用。

**Article 3** The territorial spatial plan shall refine and implement the requirements for territorial spatial development and protection put forward by the national development plan, make overall arrangements for functional spaces such as agriculture, ecology, and urban areas, and delimit and implement permanent basic farmland, ecological protection redlines, and urban development boundaries.  
The territorial spatial plan shall include contents such as the pattern of territorial spatial development and protection, the layout, structure, and use control requirements of planned land, specify the requirements for the amount of cultivated land retained, the scale of construction land, and the scope of prohibited reclamation, make overall arrangements for the land use for infrastructure and public facilities, comprehensively utilize above-ground and underground space, reasonably determine and strictly control the scale of newly added construction land, improve the level of economical and intensive land use, and ensure the sustainable use of land.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　土地调查应当包括下列内容：  
（一）土地权属以及变化情况；  
（二）土地利用现状以及变化情况；  
（三）土地条件。  
全国土地调查成果，报国务院批准后向社会公布。地方土地调查成果，经本级人民政府审核，报上一级人民政府批准后向社会公布。全国土地调查成果公布后，县级以上地方人民政府方可自上而下逐级依次公布本行政区域的土地调查成果。  
土地调查成果是编制国土空间规划以及自然资源管理、保护和利用的重要依据。  
土地调查技术规程由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 4** Land surveys shall include the following:  
(1) Land ownership and changes thereto;  
(2) Land use status and changes thereto;  
(3) Land conditions.  
The results of a national land survey shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before being published to the public. The results of a local land survey shall be examined by the people's government at the same level and submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval before being published to the public. After the publication of the results of the national land survey, the local people's governments at or above the county level may publish the results of the land survey of their respective administrative regions in a top-down manner level by level.  
The results of land surveys shall be an important basis for formulating territorial spatial plans, as well as for the management, protection and utilization of natural resources.  
The technical regulations for land surveys shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门制定土地等级评定标准。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当会同有关部门根据土地等级评定标准，对土地等级进行评定。地方土地等级评定结果经本级人民政府审核，报上一级人民政府自然资源主管部门批准后向社会公布。  
根据国民经济和社会发展状况，土地等级每五年重新评定一次。

**Article 5** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, jointly with relevant departments, formulate the standards for land grade assessment.  
The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with relevant departments, assess the grades of land in accordance with the standards for land grade assessment. The results of local land grade assessment shall be examined by the people's government at the same level and submitted to the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at the next higher level for approval before being published to the public.  
Based on the state of national economic and social development, land grades shall be reassessed every five years.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强信息化建设，建立统一的国土空间基础信息平台，实行土地管理全流程信息化管理，对土地利用状况进行动态监测，与发展改革、住房和城乡建设等有关部门建立土地管理信息共享机制，依法公开土地管理信息。

**Article 6** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen informatization construction, establish a unified basic information platform for territorial space, implement whole-process informatization management of land administration, conduct dynamic monitoring of land use conditions, establish a mechanism for sharing land administration information with relevant departments such as development and reform, housing and urban-rural construction, and disclose land administration information according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当加强地籍管理，建立健全地籍数据库。

**Article 7** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen cadastral management and establish and improve a cadastral database.

### 第三章　耕地保护

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实行占用耕地补偿制度。在国土空间规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地范围内经依法批准占用耕地，以及在国土空间规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地范围外的能源、交通、水利、矿山、军事设施等建设项目经依法批准占用耕地的，分别由县级人民政府、农村集体经济组织和建设单位负责开垦与所占用耕地的数量和质量相当的耕地；没有条件开垦或者开垦的耕地不符合要求的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定缴纳耕地开垦费，专款用于开垦新的耕地。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当组织自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门对开垦的耕地进行验收，确保开垦的耕地落实到地块。划入永久基本农田的还应当纳入国家永久基本农田数据库严格管理。占用耕地补充情况应当按照国家有关规定向社会公布。  
个别省、直辖市需要易地开垦耕地的，依照[《土地管理法》第三十二条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-32)的规定执行。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a compensation system for occupation of cultivated land. Where cultivated land is occupied upon approval according to law within the scope of construction land for cities, villages, and market towns determined by the territorial spatial plan, or where cultivated land is occupied upon approval according to law for construction projects such as energy, transportation, water conservancy, mining, and military facilities outside the scope of such construction land, the people's government at the county level, the rural collective economic organization, and the construction unit shall be responsible for reclaiming cultivated land of a quantity and quality equivalent to the occupied cultivated land; if there is no condition for reclamation or the reclaimed cultivated land does not meet the requirements, a cultivated land reclamation fee shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and such fee shall be used exclusively for reclaiming new cultivated land.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall organize the competent departments of natural resources and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs to accept the reclaimed cultivated land to ensure that the reclaimed cultivated land is allocated to specific plots. Where the reclaimed cultivated land is designated as permanent basic farmland, it shall be included in the national database of permanent basic farmland for strict management. The status of replenishment of occupied cultivated land shall be published to the public in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
Where a few provinces or municipalities directly under the Central Government need to reclaim cultivated land elsewhere, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of the Land Administration Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　禁止任何单位和个人在国土空间规划确定的禁止开垦的范围内从事土地开发活动。  
按照国土空间规划，开发未确定土地使用权的国有荒山、荒地、荒滩从事种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业生产的，应当向土地所在地的县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请，按照省、自治区、直辖市规定的权限，由县级以上地方人民政府批准。

**Article 9** No unit or individual may engage in land development activities within the scope prohibited for reclamation as determined by the territorial spatial plan.  
Where, in accordance with the territorial spatial plan, unclaimed state-owned barren mountains, barren lands, and barren beaches are developed for crop farming, forestry, animal husbandry, or fishery production, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level where the land is located, and approval shall be granted by the local people's government at or above the county level within the authority prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级人民政府应当按照国土空间规划关于统筹布局农业、生态、城镇等功能空间的要求，制定土地整理方案，促进耕地保护和土地节约集约利用。  
县、乡（镇）人民政府应当组织农村集体经济组织，实施土地整理方案，对闲散地和废弃地有计划地整治、改造。土地整理新增耕地，可以用作建设所占用耕地的补充。  
鼓励社会主体依法参与土地整理。

**Article 10** The people's government at the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the territorial spatial plan for making overall arrangements for functional spaces such as agriculture, ecology, and urban areas, formulate a land consolidation plan to promote the protection of cultivated land and the economical and intensive use of land.  
The people's governments at the county and township (town) levels shall organize rural collective economic organizations to implement the land consolidation plan and systematically regulate and improve scattered and abandoned land. The newly added cultivated land through land consolidation may be used as a supplement for cultivated land occupied for construction.  
Social entities are encouraged to participate in land consolidation according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，预防和治理耕地土壤流失、污染，有计划地改造中低产田，建设高标准农田，提高耕地质量，保护黑土地等优质耕地，并依法对建设所占用耕地耕作层的土壤利用作出合理安排。  
非农业建设依法占用永久基本农田的，建设单位应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定，将所占用耕地耕作层的土壤用于新开垦耕地、劣质地或者其他耕地的土壤改良。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对农业结构调整的引导和管理，防止破坏耕地耕作层；设施农业用地不再使用的，应当及时组织恢复种植条件。

**Article 11** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to prevent and control soil erosion and pollution of cultivated land, improve medium- and low-yield fields in a planned manner, build high-standard farmland, improve the quality of cultivated land, protect high-quality cultivated land such as black soil, and make reasonable arrangements according to law for the utilization of the soil of the plough layer of the cultivated land occupied for construction.  
Where non-agricultural construction occupies permanent basic farmland according to law, the construction unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, use the soil of the plough layer of the occupied cultivated land for soil improvement of newly reclaimed cultivated land, poor-quality land, or other cultivated land.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen guidance and management of agricultural structural adjustment to prevent damage to the plough layer of cultivated land; where facility agricultural land is no longer used, they shall promptly organize the restoration of planting conditions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对耕地实行特殊保护，严守耕地保护红线，严格控制耕地转为林地、草地、园地等其他农用地，并建立耕地保护补偿制度，具体办法和耕地保护补偿实施步骤由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门规定。  
非农业建设必须节约使用土地，可以利用荒地的，不得占用耕地；可以利用劣地的，不得占用好地。禁止占用耕地建窑、建坟或者擅自在耕地上建房、挖砂、采石、采矿、取土等。禁止占用永久基本农田发展林果业和挖塘养鱼。  
耕地应当优先用于粮食和棉、油、糖、蔬菜等农产品生产。按照国家有关规定需要将耕地转为林地、草地、园地等其他农用地的，应当优先使用难以长期稳定利用的耕地。

**Article 12** The State implements special protection for cultivated land, strictly adheres to the red line for cultivated land protection, strictly controls the conversion of cultivated land into other agricultural land such as forest land, grassland, and garden land, and establishes a compensation system for cultivated land protection. The specific measures and implementation steps for compensation for cultivated land protection shall be prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council jointly with relevant departments.  
Non-agricultural construction must economize on land use; where wasteland may be used, cultivated land shall not be occupied; where poor-quality land may be used, good land shall not be occupied. It is prohibited to occupy cultivated land for building kilns or tombs, or without authorization building houses, digging sand, quarrying stone, mining, or extracting earth on cultivated land. It is prohibited to occupy permanent basic farmland for developing forestry, fruit industry, or digging ponds for fish farming.  
Cultivated land shall be used preferentially for the production of agricultural products such as grain, cotton, oil crops, sugar crops, and vegetables. Where it is necessary to convert cultivated land into other agricultural land such as forest land, grassland, or garden land in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, cultivated land that is difficult to use stably over a long term shall be used on a priority basis.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对本行政区域耕地保护负总责，其主要负责人是本行政区域耕地保护的第一责任人。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将国务院确定的耕地保有量和永久基本农田保护任务分解下达，落实到具体地块。  
国务院对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府耕地保护责任目标落实情况进行考核。

**Article 13** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall assume overall responsibility for the protection of cultivated land within their respective administrative regions, and their principal leaders shall be the first persons responsible for the protection of cultivated land within their respective administrative regions.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall break down and assign the tasks for the amount of cultivated land and the protection of permanent basic farmland determined by the State Council, and implement them down to specific plots.  
The State Council shall assess the performance of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in fulfilling their responsibility targets for cultivated land protection.

### 第四章　建设用地

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设项目需要使用土地的，应当符合国土空间规划、土地利用年度计划和用途管制以及节约资源、保护生态环境的要求，并严格执行建设用地标准，优先使用存量建设用地，提高建设用地使用效率。  
从事土地开发利用活动，应当采取有效措施，防止、减少土壤污染，并确保建设用地符合土壤环境质量要求。

**Article 14** Where a construction project needs to use land, it shall comply with the requirements of territorial space planning, annual land use plans, use control, resource conservation, and ecological environment protection, strictly implement construction land standards, give priority to the use of existing construction land, and improve the efficiency of construction land use.  
Those engaged in land development and utilization activities shall take effective measures to prevent and reduce soil pollution and ensure that construction land meets the requirements for soil environmental quality.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级人民政府应当依据国民经济和社会发展规划及年度计划、国土空间规划、国家产业政策以及城乡建设、土地利用的实际状况等，加强土地利用计划管理，实行建设用地总量控制，推动城乡存量建设用地开发利用，引导城镇低效用地再开发，落实建设用地标准控制制度，开展节约集约用地评价，推广应用节地技术和节地模式。

**Article 15** The people's governments at all levels shall, based on the national economic and social development plans and annual plans, territorial space plans, national industrial policies, and the actual conditions of urban and rural construction and land use, strengthen the management of land use plans, implement total amount control of construction land, promote the development and utilization of existing urban and rural construction land, guide the redevelopment of low-efficiency urban land, implement the standard control system for construction land, conduct evaluation of economical and intensive land use, and promote and apply land-saving technologies and models.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门应当将本级人民政府确定的年度建设用地供应总量、结构、时序、地块、用途等在政府网站上向社会公布，供社会公众查阅。

**Article 16** The competent departments of natural resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall publish on government websites the total amount, structure, timing, parcels, and purposes of annual construction land supply as determined by the people's governments at the same level, for public inspection.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　建设单位使用国有土地，应当以有偿使用方式取得；但是，法律、行政法规规定可以以划拨方式取得的除外。  
国有土地有偿使用的方式包括：  
（一）国有土地使用权出让；  
（二）国有土地租赁；  
（三）国有土地使用权作价出资或者入股。

**Article 17** Where a construction unit uses State-owned land, it shall obtain it by means of compensated use; except where laws and administrative regulations provide that it may be obtained by means of allocation.  
The means of compensated use of State-owned land include:  
(1) granting the right to use State-owned land;  
(2) leasing State-owned land;  
(3) contributing or converting the right to use State-owned land as capital contribution or shares.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国有土地使用权出让、国有土地租赁等应当依照国家有关规定通过公开的交易平台进行交易，并纳入统一的公共资源交易平台体系。除依法可以采取协议方式外，应当采取招标、拍卖、挂牌等竞争性方式确定土地使用者。

**Article 18** The granting of the right to use State-owned land, leasing of State-owned land, etc., shall be transacted through open trading platforms in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and be incorporated into the unified public resource trading platform system. Except where the negotiated method may be adopted according to law, competitive methods such as invitation for bids, auction, or listing shall be adopted to determine the land user.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　[《土地管理法》第五十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-55)规定的新增建设用地的土地有偿使用费，是指国家在新增建设用地中应取得的平均土地纯收益。

**Article 19** The land compensation fees for the use of new construction land as provided for in Article 55 of the "Land Administration Law" refers to the average net land proceeds that the State shall obtain from new construction land.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建设项目施工、地质勘查需要临时使用土地的，应当尽量不占或者少占耕地。  
临时用地由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门批准，期限一般不超过二年；建设周期较长的能源、交通、水利等基础设施建设使用的临时用地，期限不超过四年；法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。  
土地使用者应当自临时用地期满之日起一年内完成土地复垦，使其达到可供利用状态，其中占用耕地的应当恢复种植条件。

**Article 20** Where temporary use of land is needed for construction project construction or geological exploration, efforts shall be made to occupy no or little cultivated land.  
Temporary land use shall be approved by the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level, and the term shall generally not exceed two years; for temporary land used for the construction of infrastructure such as energy, transportation, and water conservancy projects with a longer construction period, the term shall not exceed four years; except where otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
The land user shall complete land reclamation within one year from the date of expiry of the temporary land use period, so that it reaches a state available for use; where cultivated land is occupied, the planting conditions shall be restored.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　抢险救灾、疫情防控等急需使用土地的，可以先行使用土地。其中，属于临时用地的，用后应当恢复原状并交还原土地使用者使用，不再办理用地审批手续；属于永久性建设用地的，建设单位应当在不晚于应急处置工作结束六个月内申请补办建设用地审批手续。

**Article 21** Where land is urgently needed for emergency rescue and disaster relief, epidemic prevention and control, or other such purposes, the land may be used in advance. Where the land is temporarily used, the original state shall be restored after use and the land shall be returned to the original land user for use, without going through the formalities for land use approval; where the land is permanently used for construction, the construction entity shall apply for retroactive completion of the formalities for construction land use approval within six months after the end of the emergency response work.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　具有重要生态功能的未利用地应当依法划入生态保护红线，实施严格保护。  
建设项目占用国土空间规划确定的未利用地的，按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定办理。

**Article 22** Unused land with important ecological functions shall be delimited into the ecological protection red line according to law and be strictly protected.  
Where construction projects occupy unused land determined in the territorial space planning, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

#### 第二节　农用地转用

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在国土空间规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地范围内，为实施该规划而将农用地转为建设用地的，由市、县人民政府组织自然资源等部门拟订农用地转用方案，分批次报有批准权的人民政府批准。  
农用地转用方案应当重点对建设项目安排、是否符合国土空间规划和土地利用年度计划以及补充耕地情况作出说明。  
农用地转用方案经批准后，由市、县人民政府组织实施。

**Article 23** Where, within the scope of urban, village, or town construction land determined in the territorial space planning, agricultural land is converted into construction land for the implementation of such planning, the city or county people's government shall organize the departments of natural resources, among others, to formulate an agricultural land conversion plan, and submit it in batches to the people's government with approval authority for approval.  
The agricultural land conversion plan shall focus on providing explanations concerning the arrangement of construction projects, compliance with the territorial space planning and the annual plan for land use, and the situation of supplementary farmland.  
After the agricultural land conversion plan is approved, the city or county people's government shall organize its implementation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建设项目确需占用国土空间规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地范围外的农用地，涉及占用永久基本农田的，由国务院批准；不涉及占用永久基本农田的，由国务院或者国务院授权的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。具体按照下列规定办理：  
（一）建设项目批准、核准前或者备案前后，由自然资源主管部门对建设项目用地事项进行审查，提出建设项目用地预审意见。建设项目需要申请核发选址意见书的，应当合并办理建设项目用地预审与选址意见书，核发建设项目用地预审与选址意见书。  
（二）建设单位持建设项目的批准、核准或者备案文件，向市、县人民政府提出建设用地申请。市、县人民政府组织自然资源等部门拟订农用地转用方案，报有批准权的人民政府批准；依法应当由国务院批准的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审核后上报。农用地转用方案应当重点对是否符合国土空间规划和土地利用年度计划以及补充耕地情况作出说明，涉及占用永久基本农田的，还应当对占用永久基本农田的必要性、合理性和补划可行性作出说明。  
（三）农用地转用方案经批准后，由市、县人民政府组织实施。

**Article 24** Where a construction project indeed needs to occupy agricultural land outside the scope of urban, village, or town construction land determined in the territorial space planning, and involves the occupation of permanent basic farmland, the matter shall be approved by the State Council; where it does not involve the occupation of permanent basic farmland, the matter shall be approved by the State Council or the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government authorized by the State Council. The matter shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Before or after the approval or record-filing of the construction project, the department of natural resources shall examine the matters concerning the land use of the construction project and issue preliminary examination opinions on the land use of the construction project. Where the construction project needs to apply for the issuance of a site selection opinion letter, the preliminary examination of land use for the construction project and the site selection opinion letter shall be combined and processed, and the preliminary examination of land use for the construction project and the site selection opinion letter shall be issued.  
(2) The construction entity, holding the approval, record-filing, or verification documents of the construction project, shall submit a land use application to the city or county people's government. The city or county people's government shall organize the departments of natural resources, among others, to formulate an agricultural land conversion plan and submit it to the people's government with approval authority for approval; where approval by the State Council is required according to law, the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall review and then submit it to the higher authority. The agricultural land conversion plan shall focus on providing explanations concerning compliance with the territorial space planning and the annual plan for land use and the situation of supplementary farmland; where the occupation of permanent basic farmland is involved, explanations shall also be provided on the necessity, rationality, and feasibility of replacement of the permanent basic farmland.  
(3) After the agricultural land conversion plan is approved, the city or county people's government shall organize its implementation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　建设项目需要使用土地的，建设单位原则上应当一次申请，办理建设用地审批手续，确需分期建设的项目，可以根据可行性研究报告确定的方案，分期申请建设用地，分期办理建设用地审批手续。建设过程中用地范围确需调整的，应当依法办理建设用地审批手续。  
农用地转用涉及征收土地的，还应当依法办理征收土地手续。

**Article 25** Where a construction project needs to use land, the construction entity shall, in principle, apply once for the completion of the formalities for construction land use approval. If the project indeed needs to be constructed in phases, the construction entity may, based on the plan determined in the feasibility study report, apply for construction land in phases and complete the formalities for construction land use approval in phases. Where the scope of land use indeed needs to be adjusted during the construction process, the formalities for construction land use approval shall be completed according to law.  
Where the conversion of agricultural land involves the expropriation of land, the formalities for land expropriation shall also be completed according to law.

#### 第三节　土地征收

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　需要征收土地，县级以上地方人民政府认为符合[《土地管理法》第四十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-45)规定的，应当发布征收土地预公告，并开展拟征收土地现状调查和社会稳定风险评估。  
征收土地预公告应当包括征收范围、征收目的、开展土地现状调查的安排等内容。征收土地预公告应当采用有利于社会公众知晓的方式，在拟征收土地所在的乡（镇）和村、村民小组范围内发布，预公告时间不少于十个工作日。自征收土地预公告发布之日起，任何单位和个人不得在拟征收范围内抢栽抢建；违反规定抢栽抢建的，对抢栽抢建部分不予补偿。  
土地现状调查应当查明土地的位置、权属、地类、面积，以及农村村民住宅、其他地上附着物和青苗等的权属、种类、数量等情况。  
社会稳定风险评估应当对征收土地的社会稳定风险状况进行综合研判，确定风险点，提出风险防范措施和处置预案。社会稳定风险评估应当有被征地的农村集体经济组织及其成员、村民委员会和其他利害关系人参加，评估结果是申请征收土地的重要依据。

**Article 26** Where land needs to be expropriated and the local people's government at or above the county level considers that it meets the provisions of Article 45 of the Land Administration Law, it shall issue a pre-announcement of land expropriation and conduct a survey of the current status of the land to be expropriated and a social stability risk assessment.  
The pre-announcement of land expropriation shall include the scope of expropriation, the purpose of expropriation, and the arrangements for conducting the survey of the current status of the land. The pre-announcement of land expropriation shall be made in a manner conducive to public knowledge and be published within the township (town) and village and villagers' group where the land to be expropriated is located, and the pre-announcement period shall be no less than ten working days. From the date of issuance of the pre-announcement of land expropriation, no unit or individual may hastily plant crops or construct buildings within the scope of the proposed expropriation; if such actions are taken in violation of the provisions, no compensation shall be given for the hastily planted crops or constructed buildings.  
The survey of the current status of the land shall ascertain the location, ownership, type, and area of the land, as well as the ownership, type, and quantity of rural villagers' houses, other above-ground attachments, and young crops.  
The social stability risk assessment shall comprehensively analyze the social stability risk status of the land expropriation, identify risk points, and propose risk prevention measures and disposal plans. The social stability risk assessment shall involve the rural collective economic organization whose land is to be expropriated and its members, the villagers' committee, and other interested parties, and the assessment results shall be an important basis for applying for land expropriation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当依据社会稳定风险评估结果，结合土地现状调查情况，组织自然资源、财政、农业农村、人力资源和社会保障等有关部门拟定征地补偿安置方案。  
征地补偿安置方案应当包括征收范围、土地现状、征收目的、补偿方式和标准、安置对象、安置方式、社会保障等内容。

**Article 27** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the results of the social stability risk assessment and in light of the survey of the current status of the land, organize the relevant departments such as natural resources, finance, agriculture and rural affairs, human resources and social security to draft a plan for land expropriation compensation and resettlement.  
The plan for land expropriation compensation and resettlement shall include the scope of expropriation, the current status of the land, the purpose of expropriation, the methods and standards of compensation, the targets of resettlement, the methods of resettlement, and social security, among other contents.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　征地补偿安置方案拟定后，县级以上地方人民政府应当在拟征收土地所在的乡（镇）和村、村民小组范围内公告，公告时间不少于三十日。  
征地补偿安置公告应当同时载明办理补偿登记的方式和期限、异议反馈渠道等内容。  
多数被征地的农村集体经济组织成员认为拟定的征地补偿安置方案不符合法律、法规规定的，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织听证。

**Article 28** After the plan for land expropriation compensation and resettlement is drafted, the local people's government at or above the county level shall publish it within the township (town) and village and villagers' group where the land to be expropriated is located, and the publication period shall be no less than thirty days.  
The announcement of land expropriation compensation and resettlement shall also specify the methods and time limits for handling compensation registration, the channels for raising objections, and other contents.  
If a majority of the members of the rural collective economic organization whose land is to be expropriated consider that the drafted plan for land expropriation compensation and resettlement does not comply with the provisions of laws or regulations, the local people's government at or above the county level shall organize a hearing.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府根据法律、法规规定和听证会等情况确定征地补偿安置方案后，应当组织有关部门与拟征收土地的所有权人、使用权人签订征地补偿安置协议。征地补偿安置协议示范文本由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。  
对个别确实难以达成征地补偿安置协议的，县级以上地方人民政府应当在申请征收土地时如实说明。

**Article 29** After the local people's government at or above the county level determines the plan for land expropriation compensation and resettlement in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and in light of the hearing, etc., it shall organize the relevant departments to sign land expropriation compensation and resettlement agreements with the owners and users of the land to be expropriated. The model text of the land expropriation compensation and resettlement agreement shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Where it is indeed difficult to reach a land expropriation compensation and resettlement agreement for a few individual cases, the local people's government at or above the county level shall truthfully explain the situation when applying for land expropriation.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上地方人民政府完成本条例规定的征地前期工作后，方可提出征收土地申请，依照[《土地管理法》第四十六条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-46)的规定报有批准权的人民政府批准。  
有批准权的人民政府应当对征收土地的必要性、合理性、是否符合[《土地管理法》第四十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-45)规定的为了公共利益确需征收土地的情形以及是否符合法定程序进行审查。

**Article 30** The local people's government at or above the county level may submit an application for land expropriation only after completing the preliminary work for land expropriation as provided in these Regulations, and shall submit it to the people's government with approval authority for approval in accordance with the provisions of Article 46 of the Land Administration Law.  
The people's government with approval authority shall review the necessity and rationality of the land expropriation, whether it meets the circumstances of land expropriation genuinely needed for the public interest as provided in Article 45 of the Land Administration Law, and whether it complies with the statutory procedures.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　征收土地申请经依法批准后，县级以上地方人民政府应当自收到批准文件之日起十五个工作日内在拟征收土地所在的乡（镇）和村、村民小组范围内发布征收土地公告，公布征收范围、征收时间等具体工作安排，对个别未达成征地补偿安置协议的应当作出征地补偿安置决定，并依法组织实施。

**Article 31** After the land acquisition application is approved according to law, the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within fifteen working days from the date of receipt of the approval document, issue a land acquisition announcement within the township (town) and village or villagers' group where the land to be acquired is located, publish the specific work arrangements such as the scope and time of acquisition, and shall, for any individual who has not reached a land acquisition compensation and resettlement agreement, make a decision on land acquisition compensation and resettlement, and organize the implementation according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　省、自治区、直辖市应当制定公布区片综合地价，确定征收农用地的土地补偿费、安置补助费标准，并制定土地补偿费、安置补助费分配办法。  
地上附着物和青苗等的补偿费用，归其所有权人所有。  
社会保障费用主要用于符合条件的被征地农民的养老保险等社会保险缴费补贴，按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定单独列支。  
申请征收土地的县级以上地方人民政府应当及时落实土地补偿费、安置补助费、农村村民住宅以及其他地上附着物和青苗等的补偿费用、社会保障费用等，并保证足额到位，专款专用。有关费用未足额到位的，不得批准征收土地。

**Article 32** Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate and publish regional comprehensive land prices, determine the standards for land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies for the acquisition of agricultural land, and formulate distribution methods for land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies.  
Compensation fees for above-ground attachments and young crops shall belong to their owners.  
Social security fees shall be mainly used for subsidies for social insurance contributions such as old-age insurance for eligible farmers whose land has been acquired, and shall be separately accounted for in accordance with the provisions of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
The local people's government at or above the county level applying for land acquisition shall promptly implement the land compensation fees, resettlement subsidies, compensation fees for rural villagers' houses and other above-ground attachments and young crops, social security fees, etc., and ensure that they are fully paid in place and used exclusively for designated purposes. If the relevant fees are not fully paid in place, the acquisition of land shall not be approved.

#### 第四节　宅基地管理

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　农村居民点布局和建设用地规模应当遵循节约集约、因地制宜的原则合理规划。县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家规定安排建设用地指标，合理保障本行政区域农村村民宅基地需求。  
乡（镇）、县、市国土空间规划和村庄规划应当统筹考虑农村村民生产、生活需求，突出节约集约用地导向，科学划定宅基地范围。

**Article 33** The layout of rural residential areas and the scale of construction land shall be reasonably planned in accordance with the principles of economical and intensive use and adaptation to local conditions. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall arrange construction land quotas in accordance with state regulations, and reasonably ensure the demand for homestead land of rural villagers within their administrative regions.  
The territorial space planning of townships (towns), counties, and cities, as well as village planning, shall comprehensively consider the production and living needs of rural villagers, highlight the orientation of economical and intensive land use, and scientifically delineate the scope of homestead land.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　农村村民申请宅基地的，应当以户为单位向农村集体经济组织提出申请；没有设立农村集体经济组织的，应当向所在的村民小组或者村民委员会提出申请。宅基地申请依法经农村村民集体讨论通过并在本集体范围内公示后，报乡（镇）人民政府审核批准。  
涉及占用农用地的，应当依法办理农用地转用审批手续。

**Article 34** Where a rural villager applies for homestead land, the application shall be made to the rural collective economic organization on a household basis; where no rural collective economic organization has been established, the application shall be made to the villagers' group or villagers' committee to which the applicant belongs. After the homestead application is approved by the collective discussion of rural villagers according to law and publicly announced within the collective, it shall be submitted to the township (town) people's government for review and approval.  
If the occupation of agricultural land is involved, the approval procedures for the conversion of agricultural land shall be handled according to law.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家允许进城落户的农村村民依法自愿有偿退出宅基地。乡（镇）人民政府和农村集体经济组织、村民委员会等应当将退出的宅基地优先用于保障该农村集体经济组织成员的宅基地需求。

**Article 35** The State allows rural villagers who have settled in cities to voluntarily and with compensation withdraw from their homestead land according to law. Township (town) people's governments, rural collective economic organizations, villagers' committees, and other entities shall give priority to using the withdrawn homestead land to meet the homestead needs of members of that rural collective economic organization.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　依法取得的宅基地和宅基地上的农村村民住宅及其附属设施受法律保护。  
禁止违背农村村民意愿强制流转宅基地，禁止违法收回农村村民依法取得的宅基地，禁止以退出宅基地作为农村村民进城落户的条件，禁止强迫农村村民搬迁退出宅基地。

**Article 36** Homestead land acquired according to law, as well as rural villagers' houses and ancillary facilities on such homestead land, shall be protected by law.  
It is prohibited to forcibly transfer homestead land against the will of rural villagers, to illegally reclaim homestead land acquired by rural villagers according to law, to take the withdrawal of homestead land as a condition for rural villagers to settle in cities, or to force rural villagers to relocate and withdraw from homestead land.

#### 第五节 集体经营性建设用地管理

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国土空间规划应当统筹并合理安排集体经营性建设用地布局和用途，依法控制集体经营性建设用地规模，促进集体经营性建设用地的节约集约利用。  
鼓励乡村重点产业和项目使用集体经营性建设用地。

**Article 37** Territorial space planning shall coordinate and reasonably arrange the layout and use of collective-operated construction land, control the scale of collective-operated construction land according to law, and promote the economical and intensive use of collective-operated construction land.  
The use of collective-operated construction land for key rural industries and projects is encouraged.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国土空间规划确定为工业、商业等经营性用途，且已依法办理土地所有权登记的集体经营性建设用地，土地所有权人可以通过出让、出租等方式交由单位或者个人在一定年限内有偿使用。

**Article 38** For collective-operated construction land that is designated for industrial, commercial, or other operational purposes in the territorial space planning and for which land ownership registration has been completed according to law, the land owner may, through transfer, lease, or other methods, provide it to units or individuals for paid use within a certain period of time.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　土地所有权人拟出让、出租集体经营性建设用地的，市、县人民政府自然资源主管部门应当依据国土空间规划提出拟出让、出租的集体经营性建设用地的规划条件，明确土地界址、面积、用途和开发建设强度等。  
市、县人民政府自然资源主管部门应当会同有关部门提出产业准入和生态环境保护要求。

**Article 39** Where a land owner intends to transfer or lease collective-operated construction land, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the city or county shall, based on the territorial space planning, propose the planning conditions for the collective-operated construction land to be transferred or leased, specifying the boundaries, area, use, and development intensity of the land.  
The competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the city or county shall, together with relevant departments, propose industrial access and ecological environment protection requirements.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　土地所有权人应当依据规划条件、产业准入和生态环境保护要求等，编制集体经营性建设用地出让、出租等方案，并依照[《土地管理法》第六十三条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-63)的规定，由本集体经济组织形成书面意见，在出让、出租前不少于十个工作日报市、县人民政府。市、县人民政府认为该方案不符合规划条件或者产业准入和生态环境保护要求等的，应当在收到方案后五个工作日内提出修改意见。土地所有权人应当按照市、县人民政府的意见进行修改。  
集体经营性建设用地出让、出租等方案应当载明宗地的土地界址、面积、用途、规划条件、产业准入和生态环境保护要求、使用期限、交易方式、入市价格、集体收益分配安排等内容。

**Article 40** The land owner shall, in accordance with the planning conditions, industrial access requirements, ecological environment protection requirements, etc., prepare a plan for the transfer or lease of collective-operated construction land, and shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 63 of the Land Administration Law, have the collective economic organization form a written opinion, and submit it to the people's government of the city or county no less than ten working days before the transfer or lease. If the people's government of the city or county considers that the plan does not conform to the planning conditions or the industrial access and ecological environment protection requirements, it shall propose modification opinions within five working days after receiving the plan. The land owner shall make modifications in accordance with the opinions of the people's government of the city or county.  
The plan for the transfer or lease of collective-operated construction land shall specify the boundaries, area, use, planning conditions, industrial access requirements, ecological environment protection requirements, term of use, transaction method, market entry price, and arrangements for the distribution of collective income, among other matters.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　土地所有权人应当依据集体经营性建设用地出让、出租等方案，以招标、拍卖、挂牌或者协议等方式确定土地使用者，双方应当签订书面合同，载明土地界址、面积、用途、规划条件、使用期限、交易价款支付、交地时间和开工竣工期限、产业准入和生态环境保护要求，约定提前收回的条件、补偿方式、土地使用权届满续期和地上建筑物、构筑物等附着物处理方式，以及违约责任和解决争议的方法等，并报市、县人民政府自然资源主管部门备案。未依法将规划条件、产业准入和生态环境保护要求纳入合同的，合同无效；造成损失的，依法承担民事责任。合同示范文本由国务院自然资源主管部门制定。

**Article 41** The land ownership right holder shall determine the land user through methods such as tender, auction, listing, or agreement in accordance with the plan for the transfer or lease of collective operational construction land. Both parties shall conclude a written contract specifying the boundaries, area, use, planning conditions, term of use, payment of transaction price, land delivery time and commencement and completion time, industrial access and ecological and environmental protection requirements, and agreeing on the conditions for early recovery, compensation method, renewal upon expiration of the land use right, and treatment of above-ground buildings, structures, and other attachments, as well as liability for breach of contract and methods for dispute resolution, and shall report to the natural resources department of the people's government at the city or county level for recordation. If planning conditions, industrial access, and ecological and environmental protection requirements are not incorporated into the contract according to law, the contract shall be void; if losses are caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law. The model contract text shall be formulated by the natural resources department of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　集体经营性建设用地使用者应当按照约定及时支付集体经营性建设用地价款，并依法缴纳相关税费，对集体经营性建设用地使用权以及依法利用集体经营性建设用地建造的建筑物、构筑物及其附属设施的所有权，依法申请办理不动产登记。

**Article 42** The user of collective operational construction land shall pay the price for collective operational construction land in a timely manner in accordance with the agreement, and shall pay relevant taxes and fees according to law, and shall apply for real estate registration according to law for the right to use collective operational construction land and the ownership of buildings, structures, and their ancillary facilities constructed using collective operational construction land according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　通过出让等方式取得的集体经营性建设用地使用权依法转让、互换、出资、赠与或者抵押的，双方应当签订书面合同，并书面通知土地所有权人。  
集体经营性建设用地的出租，集体建设用地使用权的出让及其最高年限、转让、互换、出资、赠与、抵押等，参照同类用途的国有建设用地执行，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 43** Where the right to use collective operational construction land obtained through transfer or other methods is transferred, exchanged, contributed as capital, donated, or mortgaged according to law, both parties shall conclude a written contract and shall notify the land ownership right holder in writing.  
The lease of collective operational construction land, the transfer of the right to use collective construction land and its maximum term, transfer, exchange, contribution as capital, donation, mortgage, etc., shall be handled by reference to state-owned construction land of similar use, unless otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家自然资源督察机构根据授权对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府以及国务院确定的城市人民政府下列土地利用和土地管理情况进行督察：  
（一）耕地保护情况；  
（二）土地节约集约利用情况；  
（三）国土空间规划编制和实施情况；  
（四）国家有关土地管理重大决策落实情况；  
（五）土地管理法律、行政法规执行情况；  
（六）其他土地利用和土地管理情况。

**Article 44** The national natural resources inspection agency shall, according to its authorization, inspect the following land use and land management situations of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as the people's governments of cities determined by the State Council:  
(1) the protection of cultivated land;  
(2) the economical and intensive use of land;  
(3) the formulation and implementation of territorial space planning;  
(4) the implementation of major national decisions on land management;  
(5) the implementation of laws and administrative regulations on land management;  
(6) other land use and land management situations.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家自然资源督察机构进行督察时，有权向有关单位和个人了解督察事项有关情况，有关单位和个人应当支持、协助督察机构工作，如实反映情况，并提供有关材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../../行政与公法/行政法律/最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村集体土地行政案件若干问题的规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村集体土地行政案件若干问题的规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 45** When conducting an inspection, the national natural resources inspection agency shall have the right to inquire about the relevant circumstances of the inspection matters from the relevant units and individuals. The relevant units and individuals shall support and assist the inspection agency in its work, truthfully report the circumstances, and provide relevant materials.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　被督察的地方人民政府违反土地管理法律、行政法规，或者落实国家有关土地管理重大决策不力的，国家自然资源督察机构可以向被督察的地方人民政府下达督察意见书，地方人民政府应当认真组织整改，并及时报告整改情况；国家自然资源督察机构可以约谈被督察的地方人民政府有关负责人，并可以依法向监察机关、任免机关等有关机关提出追究相关责任人责任的建议。

**Article 46** Where the inspected local people's government violates the laws or administrative regulations on land management, or fails to effectively implement major national decisions on land management, the national natural resources inspection agency may issue an inspection opinion letter to the inspected local people's government. The local people's government shall earnestly organize rectification and promptly report the rectification situation. The national natural resources inspection agency may interview the relevant responsible persons of the inspected local people's government, and may, according to law, propose to the supervisory organs, appointment and removal organs, and other relevant organs to investigate the liability of the relevant responsible persons.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　土地管理监督检查人员应当经过培训，经考核合格，取得行政执法证件后，方可从事土地管理监督检查工作。

**Article 47** Land management supervision and inspection personnel shall undergo training, pass the assessment, and obtain an administrative law enforcement certificate before engaging in land management supervision and inspection work.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门按照职责分工进行监督检查时，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）询问违法案件涉及的单位或者个人；  
（二）进入被检查单位或者个人涉嫌土地违法的现场进行拍照、摄像；  
（三）责令当事人停止正在进行的土地违法行为；  
（四）对涉嫌土地违法的单位或者个人，在调查期间暂停办理与该违法案件相关的土地审批、登记等手续；  
（五）对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存，责令涉嫌土地违法的单位或者个人在调查期间不得变卖、转移与案件有关的财物；  
（六）[《土地管理法》第六十八条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-68)规定的其他监督检查措施。

**Article 48** When conducting supervision and inspection according to their respective functions and duties, the natural resources department and the agriculture and rural affairs department may take the following measures:  
(1) interrogate the units or individuals involved in the illegal case;  
(2) enter the scene where the inspected unit or individual is suspected of land violations to take photos and videos;  
(3) order the party to stop the ongoing land violations;  
(4) suspend the handling of land approval, registration, and other procedures related to the illegal case during the investigation period for the unit or individual suspected of land violations;  
(5) seal up documents and materials that may be transferred, destroyed, concealed, or altered, and order the unit or individual suspected of land violations not to sell or transfer the property related to the case during the investigation period;  
(6) other supervision and inspection measures as provided in Article 68 of the Land Administration Law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十三条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-73)的规定给予处分的，应当按照管理权限由责令作出行政处罚决定或者直接给予行政处罚的上级人民政府自然资源主管部门或者其他任免机关、单位作出。

**Article 49** Where a sanction is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 73 of the Land Administration Law, it shall be made, according to the administrative authority, by the natural resources department of the superior people's government that ordered the administrative penalty decision or directly imposed the administrative penalty, or by other appointment and removal organs or units.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门应当会同有关部门建立信用监管、动态巡查等机制，加强对建设用地供应交易和供后开发利用的监管，对建设用地市场重大失信行为依法实施惩戒，并依法公开相关信息。

**Article 50** The natural resources department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, establish mechanisms such as credit supervision and dynamic inspection, strengthen the supervision of the supply, transaction, and post-supply development and utilization of construction land, impose punishment according to law for major dishonest acts in the construction land market, and publicly disclose relevant information according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反[《土地管理法》第三十七条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-37)的规定，非法占用永久基本农田发展林果业或者挖塘养鱼的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，按占用面积处耕地开垦费2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；破坏种植条件的，依照[《土地管理法》第七十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-75)的规定处罚。

**Article 51** A party that, in violation of the provisions of Article 37 of the Land Administration Law, illegally occupies permanent basic farmland to develop forestry and fruit industry or to dig ponds for fish farming shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a specified time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the farmland reclamation fee shall be imposed based on the area occupied; if planting conditions are damaged, the party shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of Article 75 of the Land Administration Law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反[《土地管理法》第五十七条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-57)的规定，在临时使用的土地上修建永久性建筑物的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期拆除，按占用面积处土地复垦费5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；逾期不拆除的，由作出行政决定的机关依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 52** A party that, in violation of the provisions of Article 57 of the Land Administration Law, constructs permanent buildings on temporarily used land shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to demolish within a specified time limit, and a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the land reclamation fee shall be imposed based on the area occupied; if no demolition is made within the time limit, the authority that made the administrative decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反[《土地管理法》第六十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-65)的规定，对建筑物、构筑物进行重建、扩建的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期拆除；逾期不拆除的，由作出行政决定的机关依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 53** A party that, in violation of the provisions of Article 65 of the Land Administration Law, rebuilds or expands buildings or structures shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to demolish within a specified time limit; if no demolition is made within the time limit, the authority that made the administrative decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十四条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-74)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为违法所得的10%以上50%以下。

**Article 54** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 74 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than 10 percent but not more than 50 percent of the illegal gains.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十五条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-75)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为耕地开垦费的5倍以上10倍以下；破坏黑土地等优质耕地的，从重处罚。

**Article 55** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 75 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than five times but not more than ten times the farmland reclamation fee; if black soil or other high-quality farmland is damaged, a severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十六条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-76)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为土地复垦费的2倍以上5倍以下。  
违反本条例规定，临时用地期满之日起一年内未完成复垦或者未恢复种植条件的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正，依照[《土地管理法》第七十六条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-76)的规定处罚，并由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同农业农村主管部门代为完成复垦或者恢复种植条件。

**Article 56** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 76 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than two times but not more than five times the land reclamation fee.  
If, in violation of these Regulations, a party fails to complete reclamation or restore planting conditions within one year from the date of expiration of the term of temporary use of land, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit, impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of Article 76 of the Land Administration Law, and the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall, jointly with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs, complete the reclamation or restore the planting conditions on behalf of the party.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十七条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-77)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为非法占用土地每平方米100元以上1000元以下。  
违反本条例规定，在国土空间规划确定的禁止开垦的范围内从事土地开发活动的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正，并依照[《土地管理法》第七十七条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-77)的规定处罚。

**Article 57** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 77 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than 100 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan per square meter of illegally occupied land.  
If, in violation of these Regulations, a party engages in land development activities within the scope of prohibited reclamation as determined by the territorial spatial planning, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit and impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of Article 77 of the Land Administration Law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　依照[《土地管理法》第七十四条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-74)、第七十七条的规定，县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门没收在非法转让或者非法占用的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施的，应当于九十日内交由本级人民政府或者其指定的部门依法管理和处置。

**Article 58** Where, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 74 and 77 of the Land Administration Law, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level confiscates newly built buildings and other facilities on illegally transferred or illegally occupied land, it shall, within ninety days, turn them over to the people's government at the same level or the department designated by it for management and disposal according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　依照[《土地管理法》第八十一条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-81)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为非法占用土地每平方米100元以上500元以下。

**Article 59** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 81 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan per square meter of illegally occupied land.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　依照[《土地管理法》第八十二条](../../../行政与公法/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法.md#art-82)的规定处以罚款的，罚款额为违法所得的10%以上30%以下。

**Article 60** Where a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 82 of the Land Administration Law, the amount of the fine shall be not less than 10 percent but not more than 30 percent of the illegal gains.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　阻碍自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门的工作人员依法执行职务，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 61** Where a person obstructs the staff members of the competent department of natural resources and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs from performing their duties according to law, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, the person shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反土地管理法律、法规规定，阻挠国家建设征收土地的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令交出土地；拒不交出土地的，依法申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 62** Where a person, in violation of the laws and regulations on land administration, obstructs the expropriation of land for national construction, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the person to hand over the land; if the person refuses to hand over the land, the said government shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，侵犯农村村民依法取得的宅基地权益的，责令限期改正，对有关责任单位通报批评、给予警告；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 63** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of rural villagers in the use of homestead land obtained according to law, the relevant authority shall order the person to make corrections within a time limit, circulate a notice of criticism and give a warning to the relevant responsible entity; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　贪污、侵占、挪用、私分、截留、拖欠征地补偿安置费用和其他有关费用的，责令改正，追回有关款项，限期退还违法所得，对有关责任单位通报批评、给予警告；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 64** Where a person embezzles, appropriates, misappropriates, privately divides, intercepts, or defaults on the payment of land expropriation compensation and resettlement fees and other relevant expenses, the relevant authority shall order the person to make corrections, recover the relevant sums, order the return of illegal gains within a time limit, circulate a notice of criticism and give a warning to the relevant responsible entity; if losses are caused, the person shall bear compensation liability according to law; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　各级人民政府及自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 65** Where any staff member of a people's government at any level or the competent department of natural resources or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs neglects his or her duty, abuses his or her power, or engages in irregularities for personal gain, the staff member shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　本条例自2021年9月1日起施行。

**Article 67** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2021.
