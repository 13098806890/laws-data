**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-03-30  
**生效日期**：2022-05-01

### 正文

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地名的命名、更名、使用、文化保护应当遵守法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，反映当地地理、历史和文化特征，尊重当地群众意愿，方便生产生活。

**Article 5** The naming, renaming, use, and cultural protection of geographical names shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State, reflect the local geographical, historical, and cultural characteristics, respect the will of the local masses, and facilitate production and daily life.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当建立健全地名管理工作协调机制，指导、督促、监督地名管理工作。

**Article 6** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the coordination mechanism for geographical name administration, and guide, supervise, and monitor the geographical name administration.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院民政部门（以下称国务院地名行政主管部门）负责全国地名工作的统一监督管理。  
国务院外交、公安、自然资源、住房和城乡建设、交通运输、水利、文化和旅游、市场监管、林业草原、语言文字工作、新闻出版等其他有关部门，在各自职责范围内负责相关的地名管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门负责本行政区域的地名管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责本行政区域的相关地名管理工作。

**Article 7** The civil affairs department of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council) shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of geographical name work nationwide.  
The other relevant departments of the State Council, including those for foreign affairs, public security, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, transport, water resources, culture and tourism, market regulation, forestry and grassland, language work, and press and publication, shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant geographical name administration.  
The competent departments of geographical name administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for geographical name administration within their respective administrative regions. The other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the division of functions and duties prescribed by the people's government at the same level, be responsible for the relevant geographical name administration within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门会同有关部门编制本行政区域的地名方案，经本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 8** The competent department of geographical name administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate a geographical name plan for its administrative region, and organize its implementation after approval by the people's government at the same level.

### 第二章　地名的命名、更名

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　地名由专名和通名两部分组成。地名的命名应当遵循下列规定：  
（一）含义明确、健康，不违背公序良俗；  
（二）符合地理实体的实际地域、规模、性质等特征；  
（三）使用国家通用语言文字，避免使用生僻字；  
（四）一般不以人名作地名，不以国家领导人的名字作地名；  
（五）不以外国人名、地名作地名；  
（六）不以企业名称或者商标名称作地名；  
（七）国内著名的自然地理实体名称，全国范围内的县级以上行政区划名称，不应重名，并避免同音；  
（八）同一个省级行政区域内的乡、镇名称，同一个县级行政区域内的村民委员会、居民委员会所在地名称，同一个建成区内的街路巷名称，同一个建成区内的具有重要地理方位意义的住宅区、楼宇名称，不应重名，并避免同音；  
（九）不以国内著名的自然地理实体、历史文化遗产遗址、超出本行政区域范围的地理实体名称作行政区划专名；  
（十）具有重要地理方位意义的交通运输、水利、电力、通信、气象等设施名称，一般应当与所在地地名统一。  
法律、行政法规对地名命名规则另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 9** A geographical name consists of two parts: a specific term and a generic term. The naming of geographical names shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) the meaning shall be clear and wholesome, and shall not violate public order and good customs;  
(2) it shall conform to the characteristics of the geographical entity in terms of actual area, scale, nature, etc.;  
(3) the standard spoken and written Chinese language shall be used, and the use of rare characters shall be avoided;  
(4) generally, geographical names shall not be named after personal names, nor after the names of state leaders;  
(5) geographical names shall not be named after foreign personal names or place names;  
(6) geographical names shall not be named after enterprise names or trademark names;  
(7) well-known natural geographical entity names within China, and administrative division names at or above the county level nationwide, shall not duplicate and shall avoid homophony;  
(8) names of townships and towns within the same provincial administrative region, names of locations of villagers' committees and residents' committees within the same county-level administrative region, names of streets, roads, and lanes within the same built-up area, and names of residential areas and buildings with important geographical orientation significance within the same built-up area shall not duplicate and shall avoid homophony;  
(9) the specific term of an administrative division shall not be the name of a well-known natural geographical entity, historical and cultural heritage site, or a geographical entity beyond the scope of its administrative region;  
(10) names of facilities for transport, water resources, electricity, communications, meteorology, etc., with important geographical orientation significance, shall generally be unified with the geographical names of their locations.  
Where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise for the rules on naming of geographical names, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　地名依法命名后，因行政区划变更、城乡建设、自然变化等原因导致地名名实不符的，应当及时更名。地名更名应当符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定。  
具有重要历史文化价值、体现中华历史文脉的地名，一般不得更名。

**Article 10** After a geographical name is lawfully named, if the geographical name no longer matches its actual entity due to changes in administrative divisions, urban and rural construction, natural changes, or other reasons, it shall be promptly renamed. The renaming of a geographical name shall comply with the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations.  
Geographical names that have important historical and cultural value and reflect the historical lineage of the Chinese nation shall generally not be renamed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　机关、企业事业单位、基层群众性自治组织等申请地名命名、更名应当提交申请书。申请书应当包括下列材料：  
（一）命名、更名的方案及理由；  
（二）地理实体的位置、规模、性质等基本情况；  
（三）国务院地名行政主管部门规定应当提交的其他材料。  
行政区划的命名、更名，应当按照《行政区划管理条例》的规定，提交风险评估报告、专家论证报告、征求社会公众等意见报告。其他地名的命名、更名，应当综合考虑社会影响、专业性、技术性以及与群众生活的密切程度等因素，组织开展综合评估、专家论证、征求意见并提交相关报告。

**Article 11** When applying for the naming or renaming of a geographical name, state organs, enterprises, public institutions, grassroots self-governing mass organizations, and others, shall submit a written application. The application shall include the following materials:  
(1) the plan and reasons for naming or renaming;  
(2) the basic information on the location, scale, nature, etc., of the geographical entity;  
(3) other materials that the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council prescribes shall be submitted.  
The naming and renaming of administrative divisions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Administrative Divisions, submit risk assessment reports, expert demonstration reports, and reports on the solicitation of opinions from the public and others. The naming and renaming of other geographical names shall, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as social impact, professionalism, technicality, and the degree of closeness to the lives of the masses, organize comprehensive assessment, expert demonstration, and solicitation of opinions, and submit relevant reports.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　批准地名命名、更名应当遵循下列规定：  
（一）具有重要历史文化价值、体现中华历史文脉以及有重大社会影响的国内著名自然地理实体或者涉及两个省、自治区、直辖市以上的自然地理实体的命名、更名，边境地区涉及国界线走向和海上涉及岛屿、岛礁归属界线以及载入边界条约和议定书中的自然地理实体和村民委员会、居民委员会所在地等居民点的命名、更名，由相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府提出申请，报国务院批准；无居民海岛、海域、海底地理实体的命名、更名，由国务院自然资源主管部门会同有关部门批准；其他自然地理实体的命名、更名，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定批准；  
（二）行政区划的命名、更名，按照《行政区划管理条例》的规定批准；  
（三）本条第一项规定以外的村民委员会、居民委员会所在地的命名、更名，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定批准；  
（四）城市公园、自然保护地的命名、更名，按照国家有关规定批准；  
（五）街路巷的命名、更名，由直辖市、市、县人民政府批准；  
（六）具有重要地理方位意义的住宅区、楼宇的命名、更名，由直辖市、市、县人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门征求同级人民政府地名行政主管部门的意见后批准；  
（七）具有重要地理方位意义的交通运输、水利、电力、通信、气象等设施的命名、更名，应当根据情况征求所在地相关县级以上地方人民政府的意见，由有关主管部门批准。

**Article 12** The approval of naming and renaming of geographical names shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) For the naming and renaming of well-known natural geographical entities within China that have important historical and cultural value, reflect the historical lineage of the Chinese nation, and have significant social impact, or natural geographical entities involving two or more provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as the naming and renaming of natural geographical entities and settlements such as locations of villagers' committees and residents' committees which are involved in the direction of boundary lines in border areas, the attribution lines of islands and islets at sea, and are recorded in boundary treaties and protocols, the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government shall submit applications for approval by the State Council; the naming and renaming of uninhabited islands, sea areas, and submarine geographical entities shall be approved by the competent department of natural resources under the State Council together with the relevant departments; the naming and renaming of other natural geographical entities shall be approved in accordance with the provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) The naming and renaming of administrative divisions shall be approved in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Administration of Administrative Divisions;  
(3) The naming and renaming of locations of villagers' committees and residents' committees other than those specified in item (1) of this Article shall be approved in accordance with the provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(4) The naming and renaming of urban parks and nature reserves shall be approved in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(5) The naming and renaming of streets, roads, and lanes shall be approved by the people's governments of municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities, or counties;  
(6) The naming and renaming of residential areas and buildings with important geographical orientation significance shall be approved by the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of the people's governments of municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities, or counties after soliciting the opinions of the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at the same level;  
(7) The naming and renaming of facilities for transport, water resources, electricity, communications, meteorology, etc., with important geographical orientation significance, shall, based on the circumstances, solicit the opinions of the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level, and shall be approved by the relevant competent departments.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地名命名、更名后，由批准机关自批准之日起15日内按照下列规定报送备案：  
（一）国务院有关部门批准的地名报送国务院备案，备案材料径送国务院地名行政主管部门；  
（二）县级以上地方人民政府批准的地名报送上一级人民政府备案，备案材料径送上一级人民政府地名行政主管部门；  
（三）县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门批准的地名报送上一级人民政府地名行政主管部门备案；  
（四）其他有关部门批准的地名报送同级人民政府地名行政主管部门备案。

**Article 13** After a geographical name is named or renamed, the approving authority shall, within 15 days from the date of approval, submit it for record in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) Geographical names approved by the relevant departments of the State Council shall be submitted to the State Council for record, with the record materials sent directly to the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council;  
(2) Geographical names approved by local people's governments at or above the county level shall be submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for record, with the record materials sent directly to the competent department of geographical name administration of the people's government at the next higher level;  
(3) Geographical names approved by the competent departments of geographical name administration of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be submitted to the competent department of geographical name administration of the people's government at the next higher level for record;  
(4) Geographical names approved by other relevant departments shall be submitted to the competent department of geographical name administration of the people's government at the same level for record.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　按照本条例规定，县级以上人民政府或者由县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门批准的地名，自批准之日起15日内，由同级人民政府地名行政主管部门向社会公告；县级以上人民政府其他有关部门批准的地名，自按规定报送备案之日起15日内，由同级人民政府地名行政主管部门向社会公告。

**Article 14** For geographical names approved by the people's governments at or above the county level or by the competent departments of geographical name administration of local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at the same level shall, within 15 days from the date of approval, announce them to the public; for geographical names approved by other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at the same level shall, within 15 days from the date of submission for record in accordance with the provisions, announce them to the public.

### 第三章　地名使用

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　地名的使用应当标准、规范。  
地名的罗马字母拼写以《汉语拼音方案》作为统一规范，按照国务院地名行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定的规则拼写。  
按照本条例规定批准的地名为标准地名。  
标准地名应当符合地名的用字读音审定规范和少数民族语地名、外国语地名汉字译写等规范。

**Article 15** The use of geographical names shall be standardized and normative.  
The Roman alphabet spelling of geographical names shall be unified and standardized in accordance with the Scheme for the Chinese Phonetic Alphabet, and spelled according to the rules formulated by the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
Geographical names approved in accordance with these Regulations are standard geographical names.  
Standard geographical names shall comply with the norms for the verification of the pronunciation and characters of geographical names and the norms for the transcription of geographical names in minority languages and foreign languages into Chinese characters.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院地名行政主管部门统一建立国家地名信息库，公布标准地名等信息，充分发挥国家地名信息库在服务群众生活、社会治理、科学研究、国防建设等方面的积极作用，提高服务信息化、智能化、便捷化水平，方便公众使用。

**Article 16** The competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council shall uniformly establish a national geographical name information database, publish standard geographical names and other information, and fully leverage the positive role of the national geographical name information database in serving the daily life of the masses, social governance, scientific research, national defense construction, etc., so as to enhance the level of informatization, intelligence, and convenience of services and facilitate public use.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门之间应当建立健全地名信息资源共建共享机制。

**Article 17** The competent departments of geographical name administration of local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall establish and improve a mechanism for the joint construction and sharing of geographical name information resources.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　下列范围内必须使用标准地名：  
（一）地名标志、交通标志、广告牌匾等标识；  
（二）通过报刊、广播、电视等新闻媒体和政府网站等公共平台发布的信息；  
（三）法律文书、身份证明、商品房预售许可证明、不动产权属证书等各类公文、证件；  
（四）辞书等工具类以及教材教辅等学习类公开出版物；  
（五）向社会公开的地图；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定应当使用标准地名的其他情形。

**Article 18** Standard geographical names must be used in the following contexts:  
(1) Signs such as geographical name signs, traffic signs, and advertising billboards;  
(2) Information published through news media such as newspapers, radio, television, and public platforms such as government websites;  
(3) Various official documents and certificates, such as legal documents, identity certificates, commercial housing presale permits, and real estate ownership certificates;  
(4) Public publications such as reference books like dictionaries and learning materials like textbooks and supplementary teaching materials;  
(5) Maps made available to the public;  
(6) Other circumstances where laws and administrative regulations require the use of standard geographical names.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　标准地名及相关信息应当在地名标志上予以标示。任何单位和个人不得擅自设置、拆除、移动、涂改、遮挡、损毁地名标志。

**Article 19** Standard geographical names and related information shall be indicated on geographical name signs. No unit or individual may, without authorization, install, remove, move, alter, obscure, or damage geographical name signs.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强地名标志的设置和管理。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the installation and management of geographical name signs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　直辖市、市、县人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当在各自职责范围内，依据标准地名编制标准地址并设置标志。

**Article 21** The competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments of municipalities directly under the Central Government, cities, and counties, and other relevant departments, shall, within their respective functions and duties, compile standard addresses based on standard geographical names and install signs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　标准地名出版物由地名机构负责汇集出版。其中行政区划名称，由负责行政区划具体管理工作的部门汇集出版。

**Article 22** Publications of standard geographical names shall be compiled and published by geographical name institutions. Among them, the names of administrative divisions shall be compiled and published by the departments responsible for the specific administration of administrative divisions.

### 第四章　地名文化保护

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　县级以上人民政府应当从我国地名的历史和实际出发，加强地名文化公益宣传，组织研究、传承地名文化。

**Article 23** People's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the history and reality of geographical names in China, strengthen public welfare publicity of geographical name culture, and organize research and inheritance of geographical name culture.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府应当加强地名文化遗产保护，并将符合条件的地名文化遗产依法列入非物质文化遗产保护范围。

**Article 24** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the protection of geographical name cultural heritage, and include eligible geographical name cultural heritage in the scope of intangible cultural heritage protection according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门应当对本行政区域内具有重要历史文化价值、体现中华历史文脉的地名进行普查，做好收集、记录、统计等工作，制定保护名录。列入保护名录的地名确需更名的，所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当预先制定相应的保护措施。

**Article 25** The competent departments of geographical name administration of local people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct a general survey of geographical names within their respective administrative regions that have important historical and cultural value and reflect the historical lineage of the Chinese nation, carry out collection, recording, and statistics, and formulate a protection list. If a geographical name on the protection list truly needs to be renamed, the local people's government at or above the county level shall formulate corresponding protection measures in advance.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当做好地名档案管理工作。地名档案管理的具体办法，由国务院地名行政主管部门会同国家档案行政管理部门制定。

**Article 26** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall properly manage geographical name archives. The specific measures for the management of geographical name archives shall be formulated by the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council together with the national archives administration department.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励公民、企业和社会组织参与地名文化保护活动。

**Article 27** The State encourages citizens, enterprises, and social organizations to participate in activities for the protection of geographical name culture.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　上级人民政府地名行政主管部门应当加强对下级人民政府地名行政主管部门地名管理工作的指导、监督。上级人民政府其他有关部门应当加强对下级人民政府相应部门地名管理工作的指导、监督。

**Article 28** The competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at a higher level shall strengthen guidance and supervision over the geographical name administration of the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at a lower level. The other relevant departments of the people's governments at a higher level shall strengthen guidance and supervision over the geographical name administration of the corresponding departments of the people's governments at a lower level.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门应当依法加强对地名的命名、更名、使用、文化保护的监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强地名管理能力建设。

**Article 29** The competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall, according to law, strengthen supervision and inspection over the naming, renaming, use, and cultural protection of geographical names.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the capacity building of geographical name administration.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门对地名管理工作进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）询问有关当事人，调查与地名管理有关的情况；  
（二）查阅、复制有关资料；  
（三）对涉嫌存在地名违法行为的场所实施现场检查；  
（四）检查与涉嫌地名违法行为有关的物品；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他措施。  
县级以上人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门依法行使前款规定的职权时，当事人应当予以协助、配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 30** When conducting supervision and inspection over geographical name administration, the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) questioning the relevant parties and investigating the circumstances related to geographical name administration;  
(2) consulting and copying relevant materials;  
(3) conducting on-site inspections of places suspected of illegal acts concerning geographical names;  
(4) inspecting articles suspected of being related to illegal acts concerning geographical names;  
(5) other measures provided for by laws and administrative regulations.  
When the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments exercise the powers prescribed in the preceding paragraph according to law, the parties concerned shall provide assistance and cooperation, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门在监督检查中发现地名的命名、更名、使用、文化保护存在问题的，应当及时提出整改建议，下达整改通知书，依法向有关部门提出处理建议；对涉嫌违反本条例规定的有关责任人员，必要时可以采取约谈措施，并向社会通报。

**Article 31** If, during supervision and inspection, the competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments find problems in the naming, renaming, use, or cultural protection of geographical names, they shall promptly put forward rectification suggestions, issue rectification notices, and submit handling suggestions to the relevant departments according to law; where the relevant responsible persons are suspected of violating the provisions of these Regulations, they may, when necessary, adopt interview measures and notify the public.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府地名行政主管部门和其他有关部门可以委托第三方机构对地名的命名、更名、使用、文化保护等情况进行评估。

**Article 32** The competent departments of geographical name administration of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments may entrust third-party institutions to assess the naming, renaming, use, cultural protection, and other aspects of geographical names.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　任何单位和个人发现违反本条例规定行为的，可以向县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门或者其他有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当依法处理。有关部门应当对举报人的相关信息予以保密。

**Article 33** Any unit or individual that discovers any act in violation of the provisions of these Regulations may report it to the competent department of geographical name administration of the local people's government at or above the county level or other relevant departments. The department that receives the report shall handle it according to law. The relevant departments shall keep confidential the relevant information of the whistleblower.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府地名批准机关违反本条例规定进行地名命名、更名的，由其上一级行政机关责令改正，对该批准机关负有责任的领导人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 34** If the approving authority for geographical names of a local people's government at or above the county level carries out the naming or renaming of geographical names in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, the administrative organ at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections, and shall, according to law, impose sanctions on the leading persons liable and other directly responsible persons of the said approving authority.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府地名批准机关不报送备案或者未按时报送备案的，由国务院地名行政主管部门或者上一级人民政府地名行政主管部门通知该批准机关，限期报送；逾期仍未报送的，对直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 35** If the approving authority for geographical names of a local people's government at or above the county level fails to submit for record or fails to submit for record on time, the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council or the competent department of geographical name administration of the people's government at the next higher level shall notify the said approving authority to submit within a time limit; if it still fails to submit within the time limit, sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible persons according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反[本条例第四条](#art-4)、第九条、第十条、第十二条规定，擅自进行地名命名、更名的，由有审批权的行政机关责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，予以取缔，并对违法单位通报批评。

**Article 36** Whoever, in violation of Article 4, Article 9, Article 10, or Article 12 of these Regulations, carries out the naming or renaming of geographical names without authorization, the administrative organ with examination and approval authority shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be banned and the illegal unit shall be criticized in a circular.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定，未使用或者未规范使用标准地名的，由县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门或者其他有关部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，对违法单位通报批评，并通知有关主管部门依法处理；对违法单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2000元以上1万元以下罚款。

**Article 37** Whoever, in violation of Article 18 of these Regulations, fails to use or fails to use standard geographical names in a standardized manner, the competent department of geographical name administration of the local people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the illegal unit shall be criticized in a circular, and the relevant competent department shall be notified to handle it according to law; the legal representative or principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons of the illegal unit shall be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　擅自设置、拆除、移动、涂改、遮挡、损毁地名标志的，由地名标志设置、维护和管理部门责令改正并对责任人员处1000元以上5000元以下罚款。

**Article 38** Whoever, without authorization, installs, removes, moves, alters, obscures, or damages geographical name signs, the department for installation, maintenance, and management of geographical name signs shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan on the responsible person.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　第三方机构对地名的命名、更名、使用、文化保护等情况出具虚假评估报告的，由县级以上地方人民政府地名行政主管部门给予警告，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，5年内禁止从事地名相关评估工作。

**Article 39** If a third-party institution issues a false assessment report on the naming, renaming, use, cultural protection, or other aspects of geographical names, the competent department of geographical name administration of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give it a warning, and if there are illegal gains, confiscate the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be prohibited from engaging in assessment work related to geographical names for five years.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公职人员在地名管理工作中有滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 40** If a public official engages in abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in geographical name administration, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　各国管辖范围外区域的地理实体和天体地理实体命名、更名的规则和程序，由国务院地名行政主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 41** The rules and procedures for the naming and renaming of geographical entities in areas outside the jurisdiction of any country and celestial geographical entities shall be formulated by the competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　纪念设施、遗址的命名、更名，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 42** The naming and renaming of memorial facilities and sites shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院地名行政主管部门可以依据本条例的规定，制定具体实施办法。

**Article 43** The competent department of geographical name administration under the State Council may, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, formulate specific implementation measures.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例自2022年5月1日起施行。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2022.
