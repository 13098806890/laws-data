# 建设工程抗震管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-07-19  
**生效日期**：2021-09-01

> 国务院有关部门和国务院标准化行政主管部门依据职责依法制定和发布抗震设防强制性标准。 第十条　建设单位应当对建设工程勘察、设计和施工全过程负责，在勘察、设计和施工合同中明确拟采用的抗震设防强制性标准，按照合同要求对勘察设计成果文件进行核验，组织工程验收，确保建设工程符合抗震设防强制性标准。 建设单位不得明示或者暗示勘察、设计、施工等单位和从业人员违反抗震设防强制性标准，降低工程抗震性能。 第十一条　建设工程勘察文件中应当说明抗震场地类别，对场地地震效应进行分析，并提出工程选址、不良地质处置等建议。 建设工程设计文件中应当说明抗震设防烈度、抗震设防类别以及拟采用的抗震设防措施。采用隔震减震技术的建设工程，设计文件中应当对隔震减震装置技术性能、检验检测、施工安装和使用维护等提出明确要求。 第十二条　对位于高烈度设防地区、地震重点监视防御区的下列建设工程，设计单位应当在初步设计阶段按照国家有关规定编制建设工程抗震设防专篇，并作为设计文件组成部分： （一）重大建设工程； （二）地震时可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程； （三）地震时使用功能不能中断或者需要尽快恢复的建设工程。 第十三条　对超限高层建筑工程，设计单位应当在设计文件中予以说明，建设单位应当在初步设计阶段将设计文件等材料报送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门进行抗震设防审批。住房和城乡建设主管部门应当组织专家审查，对采取的抗震设防措施合理可行的，予以批准。超限高层建筑工程抗震设防审批意见应当作为施工图设计和审查的依据。 前款所称超限高层建筑工程，是指超出国家现行标准所规定的适用高度和适用结构类型的高层建筑工程以及体型特别不规则的高层建筑工程。 第十四条　工程总承包单位、施工单位及工程监理单位应当建立建设工程质量责任制度，加强对建设工程抗震设防措施施工质量的管理。 国家鼓励工程总承包单位、施工单位采用信息化手段采集、留存隐蔽工程施工质量信息。 施工单位应当按照抗震设防强制性标准进行施工。 第十五条　建设单位应当将建筑的设计使用年限、结构体系、抗震设防烈度、抗震设防类别等具体情况和使用维护要求记入使用说明书，并将使用说明书交付使用人或者买受人。

### 正文

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　建设单位应当对建设工程勘察、设计和施工全过程负责，在勘察、设计和施工合同中明确拟采用的抗震设防强制性标准，按照合同要求对勘察设计成果文件进行核验，组织工程验收，确保建设工程符合抗震设防强制性标准。  
建设单位不得明示或者暗示勘察、设计、施工等单位和从业人员违反抗震设防强制性标准，降低工程抗震性能。

**Article 10** The construction entity shall be responsible for the entire process of survey, design, and construction of construction projects, specify in the survey, design, and construction contracts the mandatory seismic fortification standards to be adopted, verify the survey and design deliverables in accordance with the contract requirements, organize project acceptance, and ensure that the construction project complies with the mandatory seismic fortification standards.  
The construction entity shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct the survey, design, construction, and other units and practitioners to violate the mandatory seismic fortification standards, thereby reducing the seismic performance of the project.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设工程勘察文件中应当说明抗震场地类别，对场地地震效应进行分析，并提出工程选址、不良地质处置等建议。  
建设工程设计文件中应当说明抗震设防烈度、抗震设防类别以及拟采用的抗震设防措施。采用隔震减震技术的建设工程，设计文件中应当对隔震减震装置技术性能、检验检测、施工安装和使用维护等提出明确要求。

**Article 11** The survey documents of a construction project shall specify the seismic site category, analyze the seismic effects on the site, and provide recommendations on project site selection, disposal of adverse geological conditions, etc.  
The design documents of a construction project shall specify the seismic fortification intensity, seismic fortification category, and the seismic fortification measures to be adopted. For a construction project adopting seismic isolation and energy dissipation technologies, the design documents shall set forth clear requirements on the technical performance, inspection and testing, construction and installation, and use and maintenance of seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对位于高烈度设防地区、地震重点监视防御区的下列建设工程，设计单位应当在初步设计阶段按照国家有关规定编制建设工程抗震设防专篇，并作为设计文件组成部分：  
（一）重大建设工程；  
（二）地震时可能发生严重次生灾害的建设工程；  
（三）地震时使用功能不能中断或者需要尽快恢复的建设工程。

**Article 12** For the following construction projects located in high-intensity fortification areas or key earthquake monitoring and defense areas, the design entity shall, during the preliminary design stage, prepare a special chapter on seismic fortification of the construction project in accordance with relevant state regulations and include it as part of the design documents:  
(1) Major construction projects;  
(2) Construction projects that may cause serious secondary disasters during an earthquake;  
(3) Construction projects whose functions may not be interrupted or need to be restored as soon as possible during an earthquake.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对超限高层建筑工程，设计单位应当在设计文件中予以说明，建设单位应当在初步设计阶段将设计文件等材料报送省、自治区、直辖市人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门进行抗震设防审批。住房和城乡建设主管部门应当组织专家审查，对采取的抗震设防措施合理可行的，予以批准。超限高层建筑工程抗震设防审批意见应当作为施工图设计和审查的依据。  
前款所称超限高层建筑工程，是指超出国家现行标准所规定的适用高度和适用结构类型的高层建筑工程以及体型特别不规则的高层建筑工程。

**Article 13** For an out-of-code high-rise building project, the design entity shall include an explanation in the design documents, and the construction entity shall, during the preliminary design stage, submit the design documents and other materials to the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for seismic fortification approval. The competent department of housing and urban-rural development shall organize expert review, and if the seismic fortification measures to be taken are reasonable and feasible, shall grant approval. The seismic fortification approval opinion for the out-of-code high-rise building project shall serve as the basis for construction drawing design and review.  
The term "out-of-code high-rise building project" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph refers to a high-rise building project that exceeds the applicable height and applicable structural types specified in the current national standards, or a high-rise building project with particularly irregular shape.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　工程总承包单位、施工单位及工程监理单位应当建立建设工程质量责任制度，加强对建设工程抗震设防措施施工质量的管理。  
国家鼓励工程总承包单位、施工单位采用信息化手段采集、留存隐蔽工程施工质量信息。  
施工单位应当按照抗震设防强制性标准进行施工。

**Article 14** The general engineering contracting entity, the construction entity, and the project supervision entity shall establish a quality responsibility system for construction projects and strengthen the management of construction quality of seismic fortification measures for construction projects.  
The State encourages general engineering contracting entities and construction entities to adopt information technology means to collect and retain quality information of concealed works.  
The construction entity shall carry out construction in accordance with the mandatory seismic fortification standards.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　建设单位应当将建筑的设计使用年限、结构体系、抗震设防烈度、抗震设防类别等具体情况和使用维护要求记入使用说明书，并将使用说明书交付使用人或者买受人。

**Article 15** The construction entity shall record the specific information such as the design service life, structural system, seismic fortification intensity, and seismic fortification category of the building, as well as the use and maintenance requirements, into the user manual, and shall deliver the user manual to the user or buyer.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建筑工程根据使用功能以及在抗震救灾中的作用等因素，分为特殊设防类、重点设防类、标准设防类和适度设防类。学校、幼儿园、医院、养老机构、儿童福利机构、应急指挥中心、应急避难场所、广播电视等建筑，应当按照不低于重点设防类的要求采取抗震设防措施。  
位于高烈度设防地区、地震重点监视防御区的新建学校、幼儿园、医院、养老机构、儿童福利机构、应急指挥中心、应急避难场所、广播电视等建筑应当按照国家有关规定采用隔震减震等技术，保证发生本区域设防地震时能够满足正常使用要求。  
国家鼓励在除前款规定以外的建设工程中采用隔震减震等技术，提高抗震性能。

**Article 16** Construction projects are divided into special fortification category, key fortification category, standard fortification category, and moderate fortification category based on factors such as their use functions and roles in earthquake resistance and disaster relief. Buildings such as schools, kindergartens, hospitals, elderly care institutions, child welfare institutions, emergency command centers, emergency shelters, radio and television broadcasting stations, etc., shall adopt seismic fortification measures that are not lower than those required for the key fortification category.  
Newly built schools, kindergartens, hospitals, elderly care institutions, child welfare institutions, emergency command centers, emergency shelters, radio and television broadcasting stations, etc., located in high-intensity fortification areas or key earthquake monitoring and defense areas, shall adopt technologies such as seismic isolation and energy dissipation in accordance with relevant state regulations to ensure that they can meet normal use requirements when an earthquake of the fortification level in the region occurs.  
The State encourages the adoption of technologies such as seismic isolation and energy dissipation in construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph to improve seismic performance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院有关部门和国务院标准化行政主管部门应当依据各自职责推动隔震减震装置相关技术标准的制定，明确通用技术要求。鼓励隔震减震装置生产企业制定严于国家标准、行业标准的企业标准。  
隔震减震装置生产经营企业应当建立唯一编码制度和产品检验合格印鉴制度，采集、存储隔震减震装置生产、经营、检测等信息，确保隔震减震装置质量信息可追溯。隔震减震装置质量应当符合有关产品质量法律、法规和国家相关技术标准的规定。  
建设单位应当组织勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位建立隔震减震工程质量可追溯制度，利用信息化手段对隔震减震装置采购、勘察、设计、进场检测、安装施工、竣工验收等全过程的信息资料进行采集和存储，并纳入建设项目档案。

**Article 17** The relevant departments of the State Council and the standardization administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions, promote the formulation of technical standards related to seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, and specify general technical requirements. Enterprises producing seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices are encouraged to formulate enterprise standards stricter than national standards and industry standards.  
Enterprises that produce and operate seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices shall establish a unique coding system and a product inspection qualification stamp system, collect and store information on the production, operation, and testing of seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, and ensure the traceability of quality information of seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices. The quality of seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on product quality and relevant national technical standards.  
The construction entity shall organize the survey, design, construction, and project supervision entities to establish a traceability system for the quality of seismic isolation and energy dissipation works, use information technology to collect and store information and data on the entire process of procurement, survey, design, incoming inspection, installation and construction, and completion acceptance of seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, and incorporate them into the construction project archives.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　隔震减震装置用于建设工程前，施工单位应当在建设单位或者工程监理单位监督下进行取样，送建设单位委托的具有相应建设工程质量检测资质的机构进行检测。禁止使用不合格的隔震减震装置。  
实行施工总承包的，隔震减震装置属于建设工程主体结构的施工，应当由总承包单位自行完成。  
工程质量检测机构应当建立建设工程过程数据和结果数据、检测影像资料及检测报告记录与留存制度，对检测数据和检测报告的真实性、准确性负责，不得出具虚假的检测数据和检测报告。

**Article 18** Before seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices are used in a construction project, the construction entity shall conduct sampling under the supervision of the construction entity or the project supervision entity, and send the samples to an institution commissioned by the construction entity with corresponding qualifications for construction project quality testing for testing. The use of unqualified seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices is prohibited.  
Where a general construction contract is adopted, the seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices belong to the construction of the main structure of the construction project, and shall be completed by the general contracting entity itself.  
A construction project quality testing institution shall establish a system for recording and retaining process data and result data, testing video materials, and testing reports, be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the testing data and testing reports, and shall not issue false testing data and testing reports.

### 第三章　鉴定、加固和维护

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家实行建设工程抗震性能鉴定制度。  
按照[《中华人民共和国防震减灾法》第三十九条](../../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国防震减灾法.md#art-39)规定应当进行抗震性能鉴定的建设工程，由所有权人委托具有相应技术条件和技术能力的机构进行鉴定。  
国家鼓励对除前款规定以外的未采取抗震设防措施或者未达到抗震设防强制性标准的已经建成的建设工程进行抗震性能鉴定。

**Article 19** The State implements a seismic performance appraisal system for construction projects.  
For construction projects that shall undergo seismic performance appraisal in accordance with Article 39 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Protecting Against and Mitigating Earthquake Disasters, the owner shall entrust an institution with appropriate technical conditions and technical capabilities to conduct the appraisal.  
The State encourages the seismic performance appraisal of completed construction projects, other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, that have not adopted seismic fortification measures or have not met the mandatory seismic fortification standards.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　抗震性能鉴定结果应当对建设工程是否存在严重抗震安全隐患以及是否需要进行抗震加固作出判定。  
抗震性能鉴定结果应当真实、客观、准确。

**Article 20** The results of the seismic performance expert evaluation shall make a determination on whether a construction project has serious seismic safety hazards and whether seismic reinforcement is necessary.  
The results of the seismic performance expert evaluation shall be true, objective, and accurate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建设工程所有权人应当对存在严重抗震安全隐患的建设工程进行安全监测，并在加固前采取停止或者限制使用等措施。  
对抗震性能鉴定结果判定需要进行抗震加固且具备加固价值的已经建成的建设工程，所有权人应当进行抗震加固。  
位于高烈度设防地区、地震重点监视防御区的学校、幼儿园、医院、养老机构、儿童福利机构、应急指挥中心、应急避难场所、广播电视等已经建成的建筑进行抗震加固时，应当经充分论证后采用隔震减震等技术，保证其抗震性能符合抗震设防强制性标准。

**Article 21** The owner of a construction project shall conduct safety monitoring of a construction project with serious seismic safety hazards, and shall take measures such as suspension or restriction of use before reinforcement.  
For an existing construction project that is determined by the seismic performance expert evaluation to require seismic reinforcement and has reinforcement value, the owner shall carry out seismic reinforcement.  
When carrying out seismic reinforcement of existing buildings such as schools, kindergartens, hospitals, elderly care institutions, child welfare institutions, emergency command centers, emergency shelters, and radio and television stations located in high-intensity fortification areas or key earthquake monitoring and defense areas, technologies such as seismic isolation and energy dissipation shall be adopted after full demonstration to ensure that their seismic performance complies with the mandatory standards for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　抗震加固应当依照《建设工程质量管理条例》等规定执行，并符合抗震设防强制性标准。  
竣工验收合格后，应当通过信息化手段或者在建设工程显著部位设置永久性标牌等方式，公示抗震加固时间、后续使用年限等信息。

**Article 22** Seismic reinforcement shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on Quality Management of Construction Projects and other regulations, and shall comply with the mandatory standards for seismic fortification.  
After passing the completion acceptance inspection, information such as the time of seismic reinforcement and the remaining service life shall be disclosed through information technology or by setting up a permanent signboard in a conspicuous part of the construction project.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　建设工程所有权人应当按照规定对建设工程抗震构件、隔震沟、隔震缝、隔震减震装置及隔震标识进行检查、修缮和维护，及时排除安全隐患。  
任何单位和个人不得擅自变动、损坏或者拆除建设工程抗震构件、隔震沟、隔震缝、隔震减震装置及隔震标识。  
任何单位和个人发现擅自变动、损坏或者拆除建设工程抗震构件、隔震沟、隔震缝、隔震减震装置及隔震标识的行为，有权予以制止，并向住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门报告。

**Article 23** The owner of a construction project shall, in accordance with regulations, inspect, repair, and maintain the seismic components, seismic isolation trenches, seismic isolation joints, seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, and seismic identification signs of the construction project, and shall promptly eliminate safety hazards.  
No unit or individual shall arbitrarily change, damage, or remove the seismic components, seismic isolation trenches, seismic isolation joints, seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, or seismic identification signs of a construction project.  
Any unit or individual that discovers acts of arbitrarily changing, damaging, or removing the seismic components, seismic isolation trenches, seismic isolation joints, seismic isolation and energy dissipation devices, or seismic identification signs of a construction project shall have the right to stop such acts and report to the competent department of housing and urban-rural construction or other relevant supervisory departments.

### 第四章　农村建设工程抗震设防

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当加强对农村建设工程抗震设防的管理，提高农村建设工程抗震性能。

**Article 24** The people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall strengthen the management of seismic fortification of rural construction projects and improve the seismic performance of rural construction projects.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府对经抗震性能鉴定未达到抗震设防强制性标准的农村村民住宅和乡村公共设施建设工程抗震加固给予必要的政策支持。  
实施农村危房改造、移民搬迁、灾后恢复重建等，应当保证建设工程达到抗震设防强制性标准。

**Article 25** The people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary policy support for the seismic reinforcement of rural residential houses of rural villagers and rural public facility construction projects that have been determined by seismic performance expert evaluation not to meet the mandatory standards for seismic fortification.  
When implementing renovation of dilapidated rural houses, relocation of immigrants, post-disaster recovery and reconstruction, etc., it shall be ensured that the construction projects meet the mandatory standards for seismic fortification.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上地方人民政府应当编制、发放适合农村的实用抗震技术图集。  
农村村民住宅建设可以选用抗震技术图集，也可以委托设计单位进行设计，并根据图集或者设计的要求进行施工。

**Article 26** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall compile and distribute practical seismic technical atlas suitable for rural areas.  
For rural residential house construction of rural villagers, the seismic technical atlas may be selected, or a design unit may be commissioned to carry out the design, and construction shall be carried out in accordance with the atlas or the design requirements.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对农村村民住宅和乡村公共设施建设工程抗震的指导和服务，加强技术培训，组织建设抗震示范住房，推广应用抗震性能好的结构形式及建造方法。

**Article 27** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen guidance and services for the seismic aspects of rural residential houses of rural villagers and rural public facility construction projects, strengthen technical training, organize the construction of demonstration seismic housing, and promote and apply structural forms and construction methods with good seismic performance.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对建设工程抗震管理工作的组织领导，建立建设工程抗震管理工作机制，将相关工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划。  
县级以上人民政府应当将建设工程抗震工作所需经费列入本级预算。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门，结合本地区实际开展地震风险分析，并按照风险程度实行分类管理。

**Article 28** The people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the organizational leadership of seismic management work of construction projects, establish a working mechanism for seismic management of construction projects, and incorporate relevant work into the national economic and social development plans at the corresponding level.  
The people's governments at or above the county level shall include the necessary funds for seismic work of construction projects in the budgets at the corresponding level.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments to carry out seismic risk analysis based on the actual conditions of the region, and implement classified management according to the degree of risk.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上地方人民政府对未采取抗震设防措施或者未达到抗震设防强制性标准的老旧房屋抗震加固给予必要的政策支持。  
国家鼓励建设工程所有权人结合电梯加装、节能改造等开展抗震加固，提升老旧房屋抗震性能。

**Article 29** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary policy support for the seismic reinforcement of old houses that have not taken seismic fortification measures or have not met the mandatory standards for seismic fortification.  
The State encourages owners of construction projects to carry out seismic reinforcement in conjunction with elevator installation, energy-saving renovation, etc., to enhance the seismic performance of old houses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家鼓励金融机构开发、提供金融产品和服务，促进建设工程抗震防灾能力提高，支持建设工程抗震相关产业发展和新技术应用。  
县级以上地方人民政府鼓励和引导社会力量参与抗震性能鉴定、抗震加固。

**Article 30** The State encourages financial institutions to develop and provide financial products and services to promote the improvement of the seismic fortification capacity of construction projects, and to support the development of industries related to seismic fortification of construction projects and the application of new technologies.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall encourage and guide social forces to participate in seismic performance expert evaluation and seismic retrofitting.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励科研教育机构设立建设工程抗震技术实验室和人才实训基地。  
县级以上人民政府应当依法对建设工程抗震新技术产业化项目用地、融资等给予政策支持。

**Article 31** The State encourages scientific research and educational institutions to establish laboratories for seismic fortification technology of construction projects and talent training bases.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, provide policy support for land use, financing, etc., of industrialization projects of new seismic fortification technologies for construction projects.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门应当制定建设工程抗震新技术推广目录，加强对建设工程抗震管理和技术人员的培训。

**Article 32** The competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant supervision and management departments shall formulate a catalogue for the promotion of new seismic fortification technologies for construction projects, and strengthen the training of construction project seismic fortification management and technical personnel.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　地震灾害发生后，县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门应当开展建设工程安全应急评估和建设工程震害调查，收集、保存相关资料。

**Article 33** After an earthquake disaster occurs, the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant supervision and management departments shall carry out emergency safety assessment of construction projects and earthquake damage investigation of construction projects, and collect and preserve relevant materials.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门和其他有关监督管理部门应当按照职责分工，加强对建设工程抗震设防强制性标准执行情况的监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门应当会同有关部门建立完善建设工程抗震设防数据信息库，并与应急管理、地震等部门实时共享数据。

**Article 34** The competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant supervision and management departments shall, in accordance with the division of their functions and duties, strengthen supervision and inspection of the implementation of mandatory standards for seismic fortification of construction projects.  
The competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, establish and improve a database of seismic fortification data for construction projects, and share data in real time with departments such as emergency management and earthquake administration.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门履行建设工程抗震监督管理职责时，有权采取以下措施：  
（一）对建设工程或者施工现场进行监督检查；  
（二）向有关单位和人员调查了解相关情况；  
（三）查阅、复制被检查单位有关建设工程抗震的文件和资料；  
（四）对抗震结构材料、构件和隔震减震装置实施抽样检测；  
（五）查封涉嫌违反抗震设防强制性标准的施工现场；  
（六）发现可能影响抗震质量的问题时，责令相关单位进行必要的检测、鉴定。

**Article 35** When performing the duties of seismic fortification supervision and management of construction projects, the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant supervision and management departments shall have the right to take the following measures:  
(1) carry out supervision and inspection of construction projects or construction sites;  
(2) investigate and ascertain relevant circumstances from the relevant entities and individuals;  
(3) consult and copy documents and materials related to seismic fortification of construction projects of the entity under inspection;  
(4) conduct sampling tests on seismic structural materials, components, and isolation and damping devices;  
(5) seal up construction sites suspected of violating mandatory standards for seismic fortification;  
(6) when discovering issues that may affect seismic quality, order the relevant entity to carry out necessary testing and expert evaluation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门开展监督检查时，可以委托专业机构进行抽样检测、抗震性能鉴定等技术支持工作。

**Article 36** When conducting supervision and inspection, the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant supervision and management departments may entrust professional institutions to carry out technical support work such as sampling testing and seismic performance expert evaluation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门应当建立建设工程抗震责任企业及从业人员信用记录制度，将相关信用记录纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 37** The competent departments of housing and urban-rural development of people's governments at or above the county level or other relevant supervision and management departments shall establish a credit record system for enterprises responsible for seismic fortification of construction projects and their practitioners, and incorporate the relevant credit records into the national credit information sharing platform.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的违法行为，有权进行举报。  
接到举报的住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门应当进行调查，依法处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 38** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report any illegal act that violates the provisions of these Regulations.  
The competent department of housing and urban-rural development or other relevant supervision and management department that receives the report shall investigate it, handle it according to law, and keep the confidentiality of the whistleblower.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门工作人员在监督管理工作中玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, staff members of the competent departments of housing and urban-rural development or other relevant supervision and management departments neglect their duties, abuse their authority, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，建设单位明示或者暗示勘察、设计、施工等单位和从业人员违反抗震设防强制性标准，降低工程抗震性能的，责令改正，处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处50万元以上500万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，建设单位未经超限高层建筑工程抗震设防审批进行施工的，责令停止施工，限期改正，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，建设单位未组织勘察、设计、施工、工程监理单位建立隔震减震工程质量可追溯制度的，或者未对隔震减震装置采购、勘察、设计、进场检测、安装施工、竣工验收等全过程的信息资料进行采集和存储，并纳入建设项目档案的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the construction entity explicitly or implicitly instructs the survey, design, construction and other entities and practitioners to violate the mandatory seismic fortification standards and reduce the seismic performance of the project, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, the construction entity carries out construction without the seismic fortification approval for super-limited high-rise buildings, it shall be ordered to stop construction, make corrections within a time limit, and be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, the construction entity fails to organize the survey, design, construction and engineering supervision entities to establish a quality traceability system for isolation and damping engineering, or fails to collect and store information data on the entire process of procurement, survey, design, incoming inspection, installation construction, completion acceptance and others of isolation and damping devices, and incorporate them into the construction project archives, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本条例规定，设计单位有下列行为之一的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）未按照超限高层建筑工程抗震设防审批意见进行施工图设计；  
（二）未在初步设计阶段将建设工程抗震设防专篇作为设计文件组成部分；  
（三）未按照抗震设防强制性标准进行设计。

**Article 41** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a design entity commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) failing to carry out the construction drawing design in accordance with the seismic fortification approval opinions for super-limited high-rise buildings;  
(2) failing to include the special chapter on seismic fortification of construction projects as a part of the design documents at the preliminary design stage;  
(3) failing to carry out the design in accordance with the mandatory seismic fortification standards.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本条例规定，施工单位在施工中未按照抗震设防强制性标准进行施工的，责令改正，处工程合同价款2%以上4%以下的罚款；造成建设工程不符合抗震设防强制性标准的，负责返工、加固，并赔偿因此造成的损失；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书。

**Article 42** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity fails to carry out construction in accordance with the mandatory seismic fortification standards, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 2% but not more than 4% of the contract price of the project; if the construction project is rendered non-compliant with the mandatory seismic fortification standards, it shall be responsible for rework and reinforcement, and shall compensate for the losses caused thereby; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反本条例规定，施工单位未对隔震减震装置取样送检或者使用不合格隔震减震装置的，责令改正，处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 43** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a construction entity fails to take samples of isolation and damping devices for inspection or uses unqualified isolation and damping devices, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, its qualification grade shall be lowered, or its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，工程质量检测机构未建立建设工程过程数据和结果数据、检测影像资料及检测报告记录与留存制度的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，工程质量检测机构出具虚假的检测数据或者检测报告的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销资质证书和负有直接责任的注册执业人员的执业资格证书，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员终身禁止从事工程质量检测业务；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 44** Where, in violation of these Regulations, an engineering quality testing institution fails to establish a system for recording and retaining the process data and result data, testing image data and testing reports of construction projects, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, an engineering quality testing institution issues false testing data or testing reports, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, its qualification certificate and the practice qualification certificates of the registered practitioners directly responsible shall be revoked, and its directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in engineering quality testing business; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，抗震性能鉴定机构未按照抗震设防强制性标准进行抗震性能鉴定的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，抗震性能鉴定机构出具虚假鉴定结果的，责令改正，处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款，吊销负有直接责任的注册执业人员的执业资格证书，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员终身禁止从事抗震性能鉴定业务；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 45** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a seismic performance appraisal institution fails to conduct seismic performance appraisal in accordance with the mandatory seismic fortification standards, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and be fined not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a seismic performance appraisal institution issues false appraisal results, it shall be ordered to make corrections and be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and be fined not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and the practice qualification certificates of the registered practitioners directly responsible shall be revoked, and its directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in seismic performance appraisal business; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，擅自变动、损坏或者拆除建设工程抗震构件、隔震沟、隔震缝、隔震减震装置及隔震标识的，责令停止违法行为，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，对个人处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，对单位处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, seismic components, isolation ditches, isolation joints, isolation and damping devices, and isolation markings of a construction project are altered, damaged or removed without authorization, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, restoration to the original condition or other remedial measures shall be taken, and an individual shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and a entity shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　依照本条例规定，给予单位罚款处罚的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处单位罚款数额5%以上10%以下的罚款。  
本条例规定的降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发资质证书的机关决定；其他行政处罚，由住房和城乡建设主管部门或者其他有关监督管理部门依照法定职权决定。

**Article 47** Where, in accordance with these Regulations, a fine is imposed on an entity, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons thereof shall be fined not less than 5% but not more than 10% of the amount of the fine imposed on the entity.  
The administrative penalty of lowering the qualification grade or revoking the qualification certificate as provided for in these Regulations shall be decided by the authority that issued the qualification certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the competent department of housing and urban-rural construction or other relevant supervisory and administrative departments in accordance with their statutory functions and powers.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 48** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
（一）建设工程：主要包括土木工程、建筑工程、线路管道和设备安装工程等。  
（二）抗震设防强制性标准：是指包括抗震设防类别、抗震性能要求和抗震设防措施等内容的工程建设强制性标准。  
（三）地震时使用功能不能中断或者需要尽快恢复的建设工程：是指发生地震后提供应急医疗、供水、供电、交通、通信等保障或者应急指挥、避难疏散功能的建设工程。  
（四）高烈度设防地区：是指抗震设防烈度为8度及以上的地区。  
（五）地震重点监视防御区：是指未来5至10年内存在发生破坏性地震危险或者受破坏性地震影响，可能造成严重的地震灾害损失的地区和城市。

**Article 49** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings as defined below:  
(1) Construction project: mainly includes civil engineering, building engineering, line, pipeline and equipment installation engineering, etc.  
(2) Mandatory seismic fortification standards: refer to the mandatory engineering construction standards that include the seismic fortification category, seismic performance requirements and seismic fortification measures.  
(3) Construction projects whose functions must not be interrupted or need to be restored as soon as possible during earthquakes: refer to construction projects that provide emergency medical care, water supply, power supply, transportation, communication and other guarantees or functions of emergency command, refuge and evacuation after an earthquake.  
(4) High-intensity fortification area: refers to an area where the seismic fortification intensity is 8 degrees or above.  
(5) Key earthquake monitoring and defense area: refers to an area or city where there is a risk of a destructive earthquake or is affected by a destructive earthquake within the next 5 to 10 years, which may cause severe earthquake disaster losses.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　抢险救灾及其他临时性建设工程不适用本条例。  
军事建设工程的抗震管理，中央军事委员会另有规定的，适用有关规定。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall not apply to emergency rescue, disaster relief, and other temporary construction projects.  
For the seismic management of military construction projects, if the Central Military Commission provides otherwise, the relevant provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　本条例自2021年9月1日起施行。

**Article 51** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2021.
