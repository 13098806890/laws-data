# 军用土地管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-09-17  
**生效日期**：2025-11-01

> （三）1987年1月1日《中华人民共和国土地管理法》施行前由军队单位占有并一直使用管理至今的土地。 第九条　军队单位按照国家自然资源调查监测、地籍调查要求和军队建设发展需要，组织军用土地调查、统计。 第十条　军队建设使用土地，应当优先使用存量军用土地；确需新增建设用地的，应当符合国土空间规划、土地利用年度计划和用途管制等要求，尽可能使用未利用地，并依法办理建设项目用地相关手续。 军队建设使用土地，应当不占或者少占耕地；确需占用耕地的，应当依法落实耕地占补平衡要求。 县级以上人民政府组织编制国土空间规划和土地利用年度计划，应当充分考虑保障军队建设用地需要，并征求军队有关单位的意见。 第十一条　国家健全军队建设项目用地需求通报和军地对接机制。 中央军事委员会后勤保障部根据中央军事委员会批准的军队建设年度计划等决策部署，编制年度新增用地需求清单，会同国务院自然资源主管部门将年度新增用地需求清单通报相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府。相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将年度新增用地需求清单转发相关市、县人民政府。相关人民政府应当依照土地管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，对军队建设项目用地应保尽保。 军队建设项目用地按照国家有关规定享受税费优惠。 第十二条　前条规定的军队建设项目新增建设用地的，由军队团级以上单位依照土地管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，向土地所在地市、县人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请，经有批准权的人民政府批准后，由市、县人民政府依法及时、足量供应土地；依法属于划拨供地范围的，以划拨方式供应。涉及征收农民集体所有土地的，军队单位应当按照国家有关规定支付相关费用。 第十三条　军队重点建设项目用地确需调整国土空间规划的，应当依法报经批准。 军队重点建设项目确需提前开工建设的，按照国家有关规定经国务院自然资源主管部门审核批准，可以先行用地；经批准先行用地的，军队单位应当依法及时办理建设用地相关手续。 军队重点建设项目清单由中央军事委员会后勤保障部根据中央军事委员会有关规定编制，按程序会同国务院有关部门联合下达。 第十四条　军队建设项目用地实行标准控制制度。军队单位应当落实军队建设项目用地标准，科学测算建设项目用地需求，严格控制用地规模。 军队建设项目用地标准由中央军事委员会确定。 第十五条　在军用土地的上方建设架空设施或者在军用土地的下方建设地下管线、轨道交通设施等公共基础设施的，应当经军队团级以上单位按照中央军事委员会的相关规定报经批准；涉及军事设施的，依照军事设施保护法律、行政法规的规定执行。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　军队单位按照国家自然资源调查监测、地籍调查要求和军队建设发展需要，组织军用土地调查、统计。

**Article 9** Military units shall organize military land surveys and statistics in accordance with the national requirements for natural resource survey and monitoring, cadastral survey, and the needs of military construction and development.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　军队建设使用土地，应当优先使用存量军用土地；确需新增建设用地的，应当符合国土空间规划、土地利用年度计划和用途管制等要求，尽可能使用未利用地，并依法办理建设项目用地相关手续。  
军队建设使用土地，应当不占或者少占耕地；确需占用耕地的，应当依法落实耕地占补平衡要求。  
县级以上人民政府组织编制国土空间规划和土地利用年度计划，应当充分考虑保障军队建设用地需要，并征求军队有关单位的意见。

**Article 10** For land used for military construction, priority shall be given to using existing military land; where new construction land is truly necessary, it shall comply with the requirements of territorial space planning, annual land use plans, and use control, etc., use unused land as much as possible, and go through the relevant procedures for construction project land use according to law.  
Land used for military construction shall not, or shall minimally, occupy arable land; where occupation of arable land is truly necessary, the requirement for balancing occupation and compensation of arable land shall be implemented according to law.  
When organizing the preparation of territorial space plans and annual land use plans, the people's governments at or above the county level shall fully consider the need to guarantee land for military construction and solicit opinions from relevant military units.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家健全军队建设项目用地需求通报和军地对接机制。  
中央军事委员会后勤保障部根据中央军事委员会批准的军队建设年度计划等决策部署，编制年度新增用地需求清单，会同国务院自然资源主管部门将年度新增用地需求清单通报相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府。相关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将年度新增用地需求清单转发相关市、县人民政府。相关人民政府应当依照土地管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，对军队建设项目用地应保尽保。  
军队建设项目用地按照国家有关规定享受税费优惠。

**Article 11** The State shall improve the notification mechanism for land use demands of military construction projects and the coordination mechanism between military and local authorities.  
The Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission shall, based on the decisions and deployments such as the annual military construction plan approved by the Central Military Commission, compile an annual list of new land use demands, and, jointly with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, notify the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government of the said list. The relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall forward the annual list of new land use demands to the relevant people's governments of cities and counties. The relevant people's governments shall ensure full guarantee of land for military construction projects in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions on land management.  
Land for military construction projects shall enjoy tax and fee preferences in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　前条规定的军队建设项目新增建设用地的，由军队团级以上单位依照土地管理法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，向土地所在地市、县人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请，经有批准权的人民政府批准后，由市、县人民政府依法及时、足量供应土地；依法属于划拨供地范围的，以划拨方式供应。涉及征收农民集体所有土地的，军队单位应当按照国家有关规定支付相关费用。

**Article 12** For new construction land for military construction projects as specified in the preceding article, a military unit at or above the regimental level shall, in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions on land management, file an application with the competent department of natural resources of the people's government of the city or county where the land is located, and after approval by the people's government with the approval authority, the people's government of the city or county shall supply the land in a timely manner and in sufficient quantity according to law; if the land falls within the scope of allocation-based land supply according to law, it shall be supplied by way of allocation. Where the expropriation of collectively-owned land of farmers is involved, the military unit shall pay the relevant fees in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　军队重点建设项目用地确需调整国土空间规划的，应当依法报经批准。  
军队重点建设项目确需提前开工建设的，按照国家有关规定经国务院自然资源主管部门审核批准，可以先行用地；经批准先行用地的，军队单位应当依法及时办理建设用地相关手续。  
军队重点建设项目清单由中央军事委员会后勤保障部根据中央军事委员会有关规定编制，按程序会同国务院有关部门联合下达。

**Article 13** Where land for key military construction projects truly requires adjustment of the territorial space plan, approval shall be sought according to law.  
Where key military construction projects truly need to start construction ahead of schedule, with the review and approval of the competent department of natural resources of the State Council in accordance with relevant state provisions, land may be used in advance; where land is approved for advance use, the military unit shall promptly go through the relevant procedures for construction land according to law.  
The list of key military construction projects shall be compiled by the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission, and issued jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council according to procedures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军队建设项目用地实行标准控制制度。军队单位应当落实军队建设项目用地标准，科学测算建设项目用地需求，严格控制用地规模。  
军队建设项目用地标准由中央军事委员会确定。

**Article 14** A standard control system shall be implemented for land for military construction projects. Military units shall implement the land use standards for military construction projects, scientifically estimate the land use demands of construction projects, and strictly control the scale of land use.  
The land use standards for military construction projects shall be determined by the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在军用土地的上方建设架空设施或者在军用土地的下方建设地下管线、轨道交通设施等公共基础设施的，应当经军队团级以上单位按照中央军事委员会的相关规定报经批准；涉及军事设施的，依照军事设施保护法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 15** Where overhead facilities are built above military land, or public infrastructure such as underground pipelines and rail transit facilities are built below military land, approval shall be sought from a military unit at or above the regimental level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission; where military facilities are involved, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on the protection of military facilities.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级以上人民政府因基础设施建设、开展公益事业等原因需要借用军用土地的，应当与军队团级以上单位签订协议，按照协议约定的用途和期限使用土地，并不得在借用的军用土地上修建永久性建筑物。协议应当按照中央军事委员会的有关规定报经批准。协议约定的期限一般不超过2年，期满后确需继续借用的，应当重新签订协议并报经批准。  
县级以上地方人民政府因组织抢险救灾、疫情防控等紧急需要，可以在商请军队师级以上单位同意后临时借用军用土地，军队单位同步办理审批手续。

**Article 16** Where a people's government at or above the county level needs to borrow military land for infrastructure construction, public welfare undertakings, or other reasons, it shall sign an agreement with a military unit at or above the regimental level, use the land in accordance with the purposes and term agreed in the agreement, and shall not construct permanent buildings on the borrowed military land. The agreement shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission. The term agreed in the agreement shall generally not exceed two years. If continued borrowing is truly necessary after expiration, a new agreement shall be signed and submitted for approval.  
Where a local people's government at or above the county level, due to emergency needs such as organizing emergency rescue and disaster relief or epidemic prevention and control, may, after consulting with and obtaining consent from a military unit at or above the division level, temporarily borrow military land, and the military unit shall simultaneously go through the approval procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　根据国防和军队建设以及经济社会发展需要，军队单位可以采取军队与地方土地使用权置换整合或者向地方转移军用土地的使用权等方式处置军用土地的使用权。  
处置军用土地的使用权，应当按照中央军事委员会的有关规定报经批准；未经批准的，处置行为无效。

**Article 17** According to the needs of national defense and military construction as well as economic and social development, a military unit may dispose of the right to use military land by means of swapping and integrating the right to use land between the military and local authorities, or by transferring the right to use military land to local authorities.  
The disposal of the right to use military land shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission; without approval, the disposal shall be invalid.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　处置军用土地的使用权应当优先采取军队与地方土地使用权置换整合方式，将布局分散、利用率低、使用受限的军用土地置换整合为适应国防和军队建设发展需要的土地资源。  
军队与地方土地使用权置换整合可以跨县级以上行政区域组织实施，有关地方人民政府应当予以支持，具体办法由国务院自然资源主管部门、中央军事委员会后勤保障部制定。

**Article 18** When disposing of the right to use military land, priority shall be given to the method of swapping and integrating the right to use land between the military and local authorities, so as to swap and integrate military land that is scattered in layout, low in utilization rate, or restricted in use into land resources suitable for the development needs of national defense and military construction.  
The swapping and integration of the right to use land between the military and local authorities may be organized and implemented across administrative divisions at or above the county level, and the relevant local people's governments shall provide support. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council and the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列情形之一的，军用土地的使用权可以按照有关规定向地方转移：  
（一）地方为实施国土空间规划进行旧城区改建以及其他公共利益需要，确需使用军用土地；  
（二）军用机场、港口、码头等军事设施报废、迁建或者改作民用，原设施占用的土地军队不再使用、保留；  
（三）军用土地失去军事利用价值，不再需要使用、保留。  
军用土地的使用权向地方转移按照有关规定报经批准后，军队团级以上单位和县级以上地方人民政府应当自批准之日起2年内完成土地使用权交接，依法办理不动产登记手续。逾期未完成的，军队团级以上单位应当按照程序上报原审批机关。  
军用土地的使用权转移登记或者依法注销后，县级以上地方人民政府自然资源等主管部门应当做好相应的土地管理工作。

**Article 19** The right to use military land may be transferred to local authorities in accordance with relevant provisions under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) where local authorities need to use military land for the implementation of territorial space planning, including reconstruction of old urban areas and other public interest needs;  
(2) where military facilities such as military airports, ports, docks, etc. are decommissioned, relocated, or converted to civilian use, and the land occupied by the original facilities is no longer used or retained by the military;  
(3) where military land has lost its military use value and is no longer needed for use or retention.  
After the transfer of the right to use military land to local authorities is approved according to relevant provisions, the military unit at or above the regiment level and the local people's government at or above the county level shall complete the handover of the land use right within two years from the date of approval, and handle real estate registration procedures according to law. If the handover is not completed within the time limit, the military unit at or above the regiment level shall report to the original approving authority in accordance with the procedures.  
After the transfer registration of the right to use military land or its cancellation according to law, the natural resources and other competent departments of the local people's government at or above the county level shall carry out the corresponding land management work.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　军队单位处置军用土地的使用权，应当依法组织地价评估，评估价格作为置换土地或者获得补偿的依据。军用土地地价评估办法由国务院自然资源主管部门、中央军事委员会后勤保障部制定。

**Article 20** Where a military unit disposes of the right to use military land, it shall organize a land price evaluation according to law, and the evaluated price shall serve as the basis for land replacement or obtaining compensation. The measures for the evaluation of military land prices shall be formulated by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council and the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止侵占、污染、破坏军用土地。  
任何组织或者个人对侵占、污染、破坏军用土地的行为，有权检举、控告。

**Article 21** Encroachment, pollution, or damage to military land is prohibited.  
Any organization or individual has the right to report or accuse any act of encroachment, pollution, or damage to military land.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　军队后勤保障部门和县级以上人民政府自然资源等主管部门建立军用土地管理信息通报机制，及时互相通报相关违法行为线索等信息。

**Article 22** The military logistics support departments and the competent departments of natural resources and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a mechanism for the exchange of information on military land management, and shall promptly notify each other of information on clues related to illegal acts.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对土地使用权有争议的，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由县级以上人民政府处理。当事人对有关人民政府的处理决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 23** Where there is a dispute over the right to use land, the parties shall settle the dispute through negotiation; if negotiation fails, the dispute shall be handled by the people's government at or above the county level. If a party is dissatisfied with the handling decision of the relevant people's government, it may apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　因军队战备训练、科研试验等专项军事活动确需借用国有土地或者农民集体所有土地，不改变土地用途的，经军队军级以上单位同意，可以由军队单位与有关地方人民政府自然资源主管部门或者农村集体经济组织、土地使用权人签订用地协议，按照约定的方式和期限使用土地并给予补偿；涉及改变土地用途的，应当依法办理相关手续。

**Article 24** Where, due to special military activities such as combat readiness training and scientific research experiments, it is necessary to borrow State-owned land or land collectively owned by farmers without changing the land use purpose, with the consent of the military unit at or above the corps level, the military unit may sign a land use agreement with the competent department of natural resources of the relevant local people's government, the rural collective economic organization, or the land use right holder, and use the land in accordance with the agreed methods and term and provide compensation; if the change of land use purpose is involved, the relevant procedures shall be handled according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本条例规定，侵占、污染、破坏军用土地的，由县级以上地方人民政府组织自然资源、生态环境、农业农村、林业草原等主管部门依法处罚。

**Article 25** Whoever violates these Regulations by encroaching upon, polluting, or damaging military land shall be punished according to law by the competent departments of natural resources, ecological environment, agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, etc., organized by the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　军队人员、国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府及其有关部门的工作人员在军用土地管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法依规给予处分。

**Article 26** Any military personnel, staff member of the relevant departments of the State Council, or staff member of the local people's governments at various levels and their relevant departments who abuses power, neglects duties, or engages in malpractice for personal gain in the work of military land management shall be given sanctions according to laws and regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例规定，造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 27** Whoever violates these Regulations and causes losses shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例适用于中国人民武装警察部队。  
军队划入有关单位运营的土地的管理，依照有关法律、行政法规、军事法规的规定以及国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定执行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall apply to the Chinese People's Armed Police Force.  
The management of land allocated by the military to relevant units for operation shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws, administrative regulations, military regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　开展重大基础设施建设使用军用土地的条件、办理程序、补偿标准等事项，由国务院自然资源主管部门、中央军事委员会后勤保障部规定。

**Article 29** The conditions, procedures, and compensation standards for using military land in the development of major infrastructure projects shall be prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council and the Logistics Support Department of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例自2025年11月1日起施行。

**Article 30** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 1, 2025.
