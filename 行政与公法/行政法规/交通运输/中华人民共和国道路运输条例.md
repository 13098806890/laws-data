### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护道路运输市场秩序，保障道路运输安全，保护道路运输有关各方当事人的合法权益，促进道路运输业的健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to maintain the order of the road transport market, ensure road transport safety, protect the lawful rights and interests of the parties concerned in road transport, and promote the healthy development of the road transport industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事道路运输经营以及道路运输相关业务的，应当遵守本条例。  
前款所称道路运输经营包括道路旅客运输经营（以下简称客运经营）和道路货物运输经营（以下简称货运经营）；道路运输相关业务包括站（场）经营、机动车维修经营、机动车驾驶员培训。

**Article 2** Any person engaged in road transport operations and road transport related businesses shall abide by these Regulations.  
The road transport operations referred to in the preceding paragraph include road passenger transport operations (hereinafter referred to as passenger transport operations) and road freight transport operations (hereinafter referred to as freight transport operations); road transport related businesses include station (yard) operations, motor vehicle maintenance operations, and motor vehicle driver training.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事道路运输经营以及道路运输相关业务，应当依法经营，诚实信用，公平竞争。

**Article 3** Any person engaged in road transport operations and road transport related businesses shall operate according to law, in good faith and with fair competition.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　道路运输管理，应当公平、公正、公开和便民。

**Article 4** Road transport administration shall be fair, just, open, and convenient for the people.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家鼓励发展乡村道路运输，并采取必要的措施提高乡镇和行政村的通班车率，满足广大农民的生活和生产需要。

**Article 5** The State encourages the development of rural road transport and shall take necessary measures to increase the rate of scheduled bus service to townships and administrative villages to meet the living and production needs of the vast number of farmers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励道路运输企业实行规模化、集约化经营。任何单位和个人不得封锁或者垄断道路运输市场。

**Article 6** The State encourages road transport enterprises to adopt large-scale and intensive operations. No unit or individual may block or monopolize the road transport market.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国道路运输管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门负责本行政区域的道路运输管理工作。

**Article 7** The competent transport department of the State Council shall be in charge of the road transport administration work nationwide.  
The competent transport departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the road transport administration work within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　道路运输经营

#### 第一节　客运

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请从事客运经营的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与其经营业务相适应并经检测合格的车辆；  
（二）有符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定条件的驾驶人员；  
（三）有健全的安全生产管理制度。  
申请从事班线客运经营的，还应当有明确的线路和站点方案。

**Article 8** An applicant for passenger transport operation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having vehicles that are suitable for and have passed inspection in respect of the business;  
(2) having drivers who meet the conditions specified in Article 9 of these Regulations;  
(3) having a sound safe production management system.  
An applicant for scheduled passenger transport operation shall also have clear route and stop plans.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事客运经营的驾驶人员，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）取得相应的机动车驾驶证；  
（二）年龄不超过60周岁；  
（三）3年内无重大以上交通责任事故记录；  
（四）经设区的市级人民政府交通运输主管部门对有关客运法律法规、机动车维修和旅客急救基本知识考试合格。

**Article 9** Drivers engaged in passenger transport operation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained a corresponding motor vehicle driver's license;  
(2) being under 60 years of age;  
(3) having no record of a major or more serious traffic accident for which they bear responsibility within the preceding three years;  
(4) having passed an examination administered by the transport administrative department of the people's government of a city divided into districts on relevant passenger transport laws and regulations, motor vehicle maintenance, and basic knowledge of passenger first aid.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请从事客运经营的，应当依法向市场监督管理部门办理有关登记手续后，按照下列规定提出申请并提交符合[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定条件的相关材料：  
（一）从事县级行政区域内和毗邻县行政区域间客运经营的，向所在地县级人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请；  
（二）从事省际、市际、县际（除毗邻县行政区域间外）客运经营的，向所在地设区的市级人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请；  
（三）在直辖市申请从事客运经营的，向所在地直辖市人民政府确定的交通运输主管部门提出申请。  
依照前款规定收到申请的交通运输主管部门，应当自受理申请之日起20日内审查完毕，作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，向申请人颁发道路运输经营许可证，并向申请人投入运输的车辆配发车辆营运证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
对从事省际和市际客运经营的申请，收到申请的交通运输主管部门依照本条第二款规定颁发道路运输经营许可证前，应当与运输线路目的地的相应交通运输主管部门协商，协商不成的，应当按程序报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门协商决定。对从事设区的市内毗邻县客运经营的申请，有关交通运输主管部门应当进行协商，协商不成的，报所在地市级人民政府交通运输主管部门决定。

**Article 10** An applicant for passenger transport operation shall, after completing relevant registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department according to law, submit an application and the relevant materials meeting the conditions specified in Article 8 of these Regulations in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for passenger transport operation within an administrative area at the county level or between neighboring county-level administrative areas, an application shall be submitted to the transport administrative department of the local people's government at the county level;  
(2) for passenger transport operation between provinces, between cities, or between counties (except between neighboring county-level administrative areas), an application shall be submitted to the transport administrative department of the local people's government of a city divided into districts;  
(3) for passenger transport operation within a municipality directly under the Central Government, an application shall be submitted to the transport administrative department designated by the local people's government of the municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The transport administrative department that receives an application under the preceding paragraph shall complete the examination within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, and make a decision to approve or not to approve. If approval is granted, the department shall issue a road transport operation permit to the applicant, and issue a vehicle operation certificate for the vehicles put into transport by the applicant; if approval is not granted, the department shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
For an application for passenger transport operation between provinces or between cities, the transport administrative department that receives the application shall, before issuing the road transport operation permit in accordance with paragraph 2 of this Article, consult with the corresponding transport administrative department of the destination of the transport route; if consultation fails, the matter shall be reported to the transport administrative department of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for consultation and decision. For an application for passenger transport operation between neighboring counties within a city divided into districts, the relevant transport administrative departments shall conduct consultation; if consultation fails, the matter shall be reported to the transport administrative department of the local people's government at the city level for a decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　取得道路运输经营许可证的客运经营者，需要增加客运班线的，应当依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)的规定办理有关手续。

**Article 11** A passenger transport operator that has obtained a road transport operation permit and needs to add a passenger transport route shall go through the relevant procedures in accordance with Article 10 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门在审查客运申请时，应当考虑客运市场的供求状况、普遍服务和方便群众等因素。  
同一线路有3个以上申请人时，可以通过招标的形式作出许可决定。

**Article 12** When examining a passenger transport application, the transport administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall consider factors such as the supply and demand situation of the passenger transport market, universal service, and convenience for the public.  
Where there are three or more applicants for the same route, the approval decision may be made by means of bidding.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门应当定期公布客运市场供求状况。

**Article 13** The transport administrative department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall regularly publish the supply and demand situation of the passenger transport market.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　客运班线的经营期限为4年到8年。经营期限届满需要延续客运班线经营许可的，应当重新提出申请。

**Article 14** The business term for a passenger transport route shall be 4 to 8 years. If a passenger transport route operation permit needs to be renewed upon expiration of the business term, a new application shall be submitted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　客运经营者需要终止客运经营的，应当在终止前30日内告知原许可机关。

**Article 15** A passenger transport operator that needs to terminate passenger transport operations shall notify the original licensing authority within 30 days before termination.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　客运经营者应当为旅客提供良好的乘车环境，保持车辆清洁、卫生，并采取必要的措施防止在运输过程中发生侵害旅客人身、财产安全的违法行为。

**Article 16** A passenger transport operator shall provide passengers with a good riding environment, keep vehicles clean and sanitary, and take necessary measures to prevent illegal acts that infringe upon the personal safety or property safety of passengers during transport.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　旅客应当持有效客票乘车，遵守乘车秩序，讲究文明卫生，不得携带国家规定的危险物品及其他禁止携带的物品乘车。

**Article 17** Passengers shall board with valid tickets, observe boarding order, maintain civility and hygiene, and shall not carry dangerous articles specified by the State or other articles prohibited from being carried.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　班线客运经营者取得道路运输经营许可证后，应当向公众连续提供运输服务，不得擅自暂停、终止或者转让班线运输。

**Article 18** After obtaining a road transport operation permit, a scheduled passenger transport operator shall continuously provide transport services to the public, and shall not suspend, terminate, or transfer the scheduled transport without authorization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事包车客运的，应当按照约定的起始地、目的地和线路运输。  
从事旅游客运的，应当在旅游区域按照旅游线路运输。

**Article 19** A charter passenger transport operator shall transport in accordance with the agreed starting place, destination, and route.  
A tourist passenger transport operator shall transport within the tourist area according to the tourist route.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　客运经营者不得强迫旅客乘车，不得甩客、敲诈旅客；不得擅自更换运输车辆。

**Article 20** A passenger transport operator shall not force passengers to board, leave passengers behind, extort passengers, or change transport vehicles without authorization.

#### 第二节　货运

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请从事货运经营的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与其经营业务相适应并经检测合格的车辆；  
（二）有符合[本条例第二十二条](#art-22)规定条件的驾驶人员；  
（三）有健全的安全生产管理制度。

**Article 21** An applicant for freight transport operation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having vehicles that are suitable for and have passed inspection in respect of the business;  
(2) having drivers who meet the conditions specified in Article 22 of these Regulations;  
(3) having a sound safe production management system.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　从事货运经营的驾驶人员，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）取得相应的机动车驾驶证；  
（二）年龄不超过60周岁；  
（三）经设区的市级人民政府交通运输主管部门对有关货运法律法规、机动车维修和货物装载保管基本知识考试合格（使用总质量4500千克及以下普通货运车辆的驾驶人员除外）。

**Article 22** Drivers engaged in freight transport operation shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained a corresponding motor vehicle driver's license;  
(2) being under 60 years of age;  
(3) having passed an examination administered by the transport administrative department of the people's government of a city divided into districts on relevant freight transport laws and regulations, motor vehicle maintenance, and basic knowledge of cargo loading and storage (except for drivers of ordinary freight vehicles with a gross mass of 4,500 kg or less).

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　申请从事危险货物运输经营的，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有5辆以上经检测合格的危险货物运输专用车辆、设备；  
（二）有经所在地设区的市级人民政府交通运输主管部门考试合格，取得上岗资格证的驾驶人员、装卸管理人员、押运人员；  
（三）危险货物运输专用车辆配有必要的通讯工具；  
（四）有健全的安全生产管理制度。

**Article 23** An applicant for dangerous goods transport operation shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) having five or more dedicated dangerous goods transport vehicles and equipment that have passed inspection;  
(2) having drivers, loading and unloading managers, and escorts who have passed the examination administered by the transport administrative department of the local people's government of a city divided into districts and obtained qualification certificates;  
(3) the dedicated dangerous goods transport vehicles being equipped with necessary communication tools;  
(4) having a sound safe production management system.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请从事货运经营的，应当依法向市场监督管理部门办理有关登记手续后，按照下列规定提出申请并分别提交符合[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)、第二十三条规定条件的相关材料：  
（一）从事危险货物运输经营以外的货运经营的，向县级人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请；  
（二）从事危险货物运输经营的，向设区的市级人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请。  
依照前款规定收到申请的交通运输主管部门，应当自受理申请之日起20日内审查完毕，作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，向申请人颁发道路运输经营许可证，并向申请人投入运输的车辆配发车辆营运证；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
使用总质量4500千克及以下普通货运车辆从事普通货运经营的，无需按照本条规定申请取得道路运输经营许可证及车辆营运证。

**Article 24** An applicant for freight transport operation shall, after completing relevant registration procedures with the market supervision and administration department according to law, submit an application and the relevant materials meeting the conditions specified in Articles 21 and 23 of these Regulations in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) for freight transport operation other than dangerous goods transport operation, an application shall be submitted to the transport administrative department of the people's government at the county level;  
(2) for dangerous goods transport operation, an application shall be submitted to the transport administrative department of the people's government of a city divided into districts.  
The transport administrative department that receives an application under the preceding paragraph shall complete the examination within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, and make a decision to approve or not to approve. If approval is granted, the department shall issue a road transport operation permit to the applicant, and issue a vehicle operation certificate for the vehicles put into transport by the applicant; if approval is not granted, the department shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
Those who use ordinary freight vehicles with a gross mass of 4,500 kg or less for ordinary freight transport do not need to apply for a road transport operation permit and a vehicle operation certificate in accordance with this Article.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　货运经营者不得运输法律、行政法规禁止运输的货物。  
法律、行政法规规定必须办理有关手续后方可运输的货物，货运经营者应当查验有关手续。

**Article 25** A freight transport operator shall not transport goods the transport of which is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.  
For goods the transport of which is permitted only after completing relevant procedures as prescribed by laws or administrative regulations, the freight transport operator shall verify the relevant procedures.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家鼓励货运经营者实行封闭式运输，保证环境卫生和货物运输安全。  
货运经营者应当采取必要措施，防止货物脱落、扬撒等。  
运输危险货物应当采取必要措施，防止危险货物燃烧、爆炸、辐射、泄漏等。

**Article 26** The State encourages freight transport operators to adopt enclosed transport to ensure environmental hygiene and cargo transport safety.  
Freight transport operators shall take necessary measures to prevent cargo from falling off, scattering, and the like.  
Dangerous goods shall be transported with necessary measures taken to prevent combustion, explosion, radiation, leakage, and the like of the dangerous goods.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　运输危险货物应当配备必要的押运人员，保证危险货物处于押运人员的监管之下，并悬挂明显的危险货物运输标志。  
托运危险货物的，应当向货运经营者说明危险货物的品名、性质、应急处置方法等情况，并严格按照国家有关规定包装，设置明显标志。

**Article 27** For the transport of dangerous goods, necessary escorts shall be assigned to ensure that the dangerous goods are under the supervision of the escorts, and conspicuous dangerous goods transport signs shall be displayed.  
Where dangerous goods are consigned, the consignor shall inform the freight transport operator of the name, nature, emergency response methods, and other information of the dangerous goods, and shall strictly package and attach conspicuous marks in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

#### 第三节　客运和货运的共同规定

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　客运经营者、货运经营者应当加强对从业人员的安全教育、职业道德教育，确保道路运输安全。  
道路运输从业人员应当遵守道路运输操作规程，不得违章作业。驾驶人员连续驾驶时间不得超过4个小时。

**Article 28** Passenger transport operators and freight transport operators shall strengthen safety education and professional ethics education for employees to ensure road transport safety.  
Road transport employees shall comply with road transport operation procedures and shall not operate in violation of rules. The continuous driving time of drivers shall not exceed 4 hours.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生产（改装）客运车辆、货运车辆的企业应当按照国家规定标定车辆的核定人数或者载重量，严禁多标或者少标车辆的核定人数或者载重量。  
客运经营者、货运经营者应当使用符合国家规定标准的车辆从事道路运输经营。

**Article 29** Enterprises that produce (refit) passenger transport vehicles or freight transport vehicles shall, in accordance with state regulations, mark the verified number of passengers or load capacity of the vehicles, and it is strictly prohibited to mark more or less than the verified number of passengers or load capacity.  
Passenger transport operators and freight transport operators shall use vehicles that meet the standards prescribed by the state to engage in road transport operations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　客运经营者、货运经营者应当加强对车辆的维护和检测，确保车辆符合国家规定的技术标准；不得使用报废的、擅自改装的和其他不符合国家规定的车辆从事道路运输经营。

**Article 30** Passenger transport operators and freight transport operators shall strengthen the maintenance and inspection of vehicles to ensure that vehicles meet the technical standards prescribed by the state; they shall not use vehicles that are scrapped, unauthorizedly refitted, or otherwise not in conformity with state regulations to engage in road transport operations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　客运经营者、货运经营者应当制定有关交通事故、自然灾害以及其他突发事件的道路运输应急预案。应急预案应当包括报告程序、应急指挥、应急车辆和设备的储备以及处置措施等内容。

**Article 31** Passenger transport operators and freight transport operators shall develop road transport emergency plans for traffic accidents, natural disasters, and other emergencies. The emergency plans shall include reporting procedures, emergency command, reserve of emergency vehicles and equipment, and response measures.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　发生交通事故、自然灾害以及其他突发事件，客运经营者和货运经营者应当服从县级以上人民政府或者有关部门的统一调度、指挥。

**Article 32** In the event of traffic accidents, natural disasters, and other emergencies, passenger transport operators and freight transport operators shall follow the unified dispatch and command of the people's government at or above the county level or the relevant departments.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　道路运输车辆应当随车携带车辆营运证，不得转让、出租。

**Article 33** Road transport vehicles shall carry the vehicle operation certificate with the vehicle, and shall not transfer or lease it.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　道路运输车辆运输旅客的，不得超过核定的人数，不得违反规定载货；运输货物的，不得运输旅客，运输的货物应当符合核定的载重量，严禁超载；载物的长、宽、高不得违反装载要求。  
违反前款规定的，由公安机关交通管理部门依照《中华人民共和国道路交通安全法》的有关规定进行处罚。

**Article 34** Where a road transport vehicle transports passengers, it shall not exceed the verified number of passengers and shall not carry goods in violation of regulations; where it transports goods, it shall not transport passengers, the goods transported shall conform to the verified load capacity, and overloading is strictly prohibited; the length, width, and height of the loaded objects shall not violate the loading requirements.  
Whoever violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be penalized by the traffic management department of the public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　客运经营者、危险货物运输经营者应当分别为旅客或者危险货物投保承运人责任险。

**Article 35** Passenger transport operators and dangerous goods transport operators shall respectively purchase carrier liability insurance for passengers or dangerous goods.

### 第三章　道路运输相关业务

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　从事道路运输站（场）经营的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有经验收合格的运输站（场）；  
（二）有相应的专业人员和管理人员；  
（三）有相应的设备、设施；  
（四）有健全的业务操作规程和安全管理制度。

**Article 36** Any person engaged in road transport station (yard) operations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a transport station (yard) that has been inspected and accepted as qualified;  
(2) having corresponding professional and managerial personnel;  
(3) having corresponding equipment and facilities;  
(4) having sound business operation procedures and safety management systems.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事机动车维修经营的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有相应的机动车维修场地；  
（二）有必要的设备、设施和技术人员；  
（三）有健全的机动车维修管理制度；  
（四）有必要的环境保护措施。  
国务院交通运输主管部门根据前款规定的条件，制定机动车维修经营业务标准。

**Article 37** Any person engaged in motor vehicle maintenance operations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having corresponding motor vehicle maintenance premises;  
(2) having necessary equipment, facilities, and technical personnel;  
(3) having a sound motor vehicle maintenance management system;  
(4) having necessary environmental protection measures.  
The competent transport department of the State Council shall, based on the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, formulate business standards for motor vehicle maintenance operations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　从事机动车驾驶员培训的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）取得企业法人资格；  
（二）有健全的培训机构和管理制度；  
（三）有与培训业务相适应的教学人员、管理人员；  
（四）有必要的教学车辆和其他教学设施、设备、场地。

**Article 38** Any person engaged in motor vehicle driver training shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained the status of enterprise legal person;  
(2) having a sound training institution and management system;  
(3) having teaching and managerial personnel suitable for the training business;  
(4) having necessary teaching vehicles and other teaching facilities, equipment, and premises.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　申请从事道路旅客运输站（场）经营业务的，应当在依法向市场监督管理部门办理有关登记手续后，向所在地县级人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请，并附送符合[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)规定条件的相关材料。县级人民政府交通运输主管部门应当自受理申请之日起15日内审查完毕，作出许可或者不予许可的决定，并书面通知申请人。  
从事道路货物运输站（场）经营、机动车维修经营和机动车驾驶员培训业务的，应当在依法向市场监督管理部门办理有关登记手续后，向所在地县级人民政府交通运输主管部门进行备案，并分别附送符合[本条例第三十六条](#art-36)、第三十七条、第三十八条规定条件的相关材料。

**Article 39** Any applicant applying for engaging in road passenger transport station (yard) operations shall, after handling relevant registration formalities with the market supervision and administration department according to law, submit an application to the competent transport department of the local people's government at the county level, and attach relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions prescribed in Article 36 of these Regulations. The competent transport department of the county people's government shall complete the examination within 15 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision to grant or deny the license, and notify the applicant in writing.  
Any person engaged in road freight transport station (yard) operations, motor vehicle maintenance operations, and motor vehicle driver training shall, after handling relevant registration formalities with the market supervision and administration department according to law, file a record with the competent transport department of the local people's government at the county level, and respectively attach relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions prescribed in Articles 36, 37, and 38 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　道路运输站（场）经营者应当对出站的车辆进行安全检查，禁止无证经营的车辆进站从事经营活动，防止超载车辆或者未经安全检查的车辆出站。  
道路运输站（场）经营者应当公平对待使用站（场）的客运经营者和货运经营者，无正当理由不得拒绝道路运输车辆进站从事经营活动。  
道路运输站（场）经营者应当向旅客和货主提供安全、便捷、优质的服务；保持站（场）卫生、清洁；不得随意改变站（场）用途和服务功能。

**Article 40** A road transport station (yard) operator shall conduct safety inspections on vehicles departing from the station, prohibit vehicles operating without a license from entering the station for business activities, and prevent overloaded vehicles or vehicles that have not undergone safety inspection from departing the station.  
A road transport station (yard) operator shall treat passenger transport operators and freight transport operators using the station (yard) fairly, and shall not refuse road transport vehicles to enter the station for business activities without justifiable reasons.  
A road transport station (yard) operator shall provide safe, convenient, and quality services to passengers and cargo owners; maintain the station (yard) in a hygienic and clean condition; and shall not arbitrarily change the use or service functions of the station (yard).

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　道路旅客运输站（场）经营者应当为客运经营者合理安排班次，公布其运输线路、起止经停站点、运输班次、始发时间、票价，调度车辆进站、发车，疏导旅客，维持上下车秩序。  
道路旅客运输站（场）经营者应当设置旅客购票、候车、行李寄存和托运等服务设施，按照车辆核定载客限额售票，并采取措施防止携带危险品的人员进站乘车。

**Article 41** A road passenger transport station (yard) operator shall reasonably arrange schedules for passenger transport operators, publish their transport routes, starting, ending and stopping stations, service schedules, departure times, and ticket prices, dispatch vehicles to enter and depart from the station, manage passenger flow, and maintain order for boarding and alighting.  
A road passenger transport station (yard) operator shall set up service facilities for ticket purchasing, waiting, luggage storage and consignment, sell tickets in accordance with the approved passenger capacity of vehicles, and take measures to prevent persons carrying dangerous goods from entering the station and boarding vehicles.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　道路货物运输站（场）经营者应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门规定的业务操作规程装卸、储存、保管货物。

**Article 42** A road freight transport station (yard) operator shall load, store, and preserve goods in accordance with the business operation procedures prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　机动车维修经营者应当按照国家有关技术规范对机动车进行维修，保证维修质量，不得使用假冒伪劣配件维修机动车。  
机动车维修经营者应当公布机动车维修工时定额和收费标准，合理收取费用，维修服务完成后应当提供维修费用明细单。

**Article 43** A motor vehicle maintenance operator shall repair motor vehicles in accordance with the relevant national technical specifications, ensure repair quality, and shall not use counterfeit or substandard parts to repair motor vehicles.  
A motor vehicle maintenance operator shall publish the labor hour norms and fee standards for motor vehicle maintenance, charge fees reasonably, and shall provide an itemized bill of repair fees after completion of the maintenance service.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　机动车维修经营者对机动车进行二级维护、总成修理或者整车修理的，应当进行维修质量检验。检验合格的，维修质量检验人员应当签发机动车维修合格证。  
机动车维修实行质量保证期制度。质量保证期内因维修质量原因造成机动车无法正常使用的，机动车维修经营者应当无偿返修。  
机动车维修质量保证期制度的具体办法，由国务院交通运输主管部门制定。

**Article 44** Where a motor vehicle maintenance operator performs secondary maintenance, assembly repair, or complete vehicle overhaul on a motor vehicle, it shall conduct a maintenance quality inspection. If the inspection is passed, the maintenance quality inspector shall issue a motor vehicle maintenance certificate.  
A quality guarantee period system shall be implemented for motor vehicle maintenance. If, during the quality guarantee period, a motor vehicle cannot be used normally due to repair quality, the motor vehicle maintenance operator shall make free re-repairs.  
The specific measures for the motor vehicle maintenance quality guarantee period system shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　机动车维修经营者不得承修已报废的机动车，不得擅自改装机动车。

**Article 45** A motor vehicle maintenance operator shall not undertake the repair of a motor vehicle that has been scrapped, and shall not modify a motor vehicle without authorization.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　机动车驾驶员培训机构应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门规定的教学大纲进行培训，确保培训质量。培训结业的，应当向参加培训的人员颁发培训结业证书。

**Article 46** A motor vehicle driver training institution shall conduct training in accordance with the teaching syllabus prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council and ensure training quality. Upon completion of training, it shall issue a training completion certificate to the persons who participated in the training.

### 第四章　国际道路运输

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国务院交通运输主管部门应当及时向社会公布中国政府与有关国家政府签署的双边或者多边道路运输协定确定的国际道路运输线路。

**Article 47** The competent transport department of the State Council shall promptly publish to the public the international road transport routes determined by the bilateral or multilateral road transport agreements signed by the Chinese government and the governments of relevant countries.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　从事国际道路运输经营的，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）依照[本条例第十条](#art-10)、第二十四条规定取得道路运输经营许可证的企业法人；  
（二）在国内从事道路运输经营满3年，且未发生重大以上道路交通责任事故。

**Article 48** Any person engaged in international road transport operations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being an enterprise legal person that has obtained a road transport operation permit in accordance with Articles 10 and 24 of these Regulations;  
(2) having been engaged in road transport operations domestically for at least three years and without having had a major or above road traffic liability accident.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　申请从事国际道路旅客运输经营的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门提出申请并提交符合[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)规定条件的相关材料。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，应当向国务院交通运输主管部门备案；不予批准的，应当向当事人说明理由。  
从事国际道路货物运输经营的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门进行备案，并附送符合[本条例第四十八条](#art-48)规定条件的相关材料。  
国际道路运输经营者应当持有关文件依法向有关部门办理相关手续。

**Article 49** An applicant for engaging in international road passenger transport business shall submit an application to the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and provide relevant materials meeting the conditions specified in Article 48 of these Regulations. The competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the review within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, and make a decision to approve or disapprove. If the application is approved, the department shall report it to the competent transport department of the State Council for record; if not approved, the department shall explain the reasons to the party.  
A party engaging in international road cargo transport business shall file a record with the competent transport department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and attach relevant materials meeting the conditions specified in Article 48 of these Regulations.  
An international road transport operator shall, in accordance with the law, go through the relevant formalities with the relevant department by holding the relevant documents.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　中国国际道路运输经营者应当在其投入运输车辆的显著位置，标明中国国籍识别标志。  
外国国际道路运输经营者的车辆在中国境内运输，应当标明本国国籍识别标志，并按照规定的运输线路行驶；不得擅自改变运输线路，不得从事起止地都在中国境内的道路运输经营。

**Article 50** A Chinese international road transport operator shall mark the nationality identification mark of China on the prominent position of the vehicles put into transport.  
Vehicles of a foreign international road transport operator operating within the territory of China shall mark their own nationality identification mark, and shall travel in accordance with the prescribed transport routes; they shall not change transport routes without authorization, and shall not engage in road transport operations whose origin and destination are both within the territory of China.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　在口岸设立的国际道路运输管理机构应当加强对出入口岸的国际道路运输的监督管理。

**Article 51** The international road transport management institution established at the port shall strengthen the supervision and management of international road transport entering and leaving the port.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　外国国际道路运输经营者依法在中国境内设立的常驻代表机构不得从事经营活动。

**Article 52** A permanent representative office established within the territory of China by a foreign international road transport operator according to law shall not engage in business activities.

### 第五章　执法监督

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上地方人民政府交通运输、公安、市场监督管理等部门应当建立信息共享和协同监管机制，按照职责分工加强对道路运输及相关业务的监督管理。

**Article 53** The competent transport, public security, market supervision and management departments of a local people's government at or above the county level shall establish an information sharing and coordinated supervision mechanism, and, in accordance with the division of responsibilities, strengthen the supervision and management of road transport and related businesses.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当加强执法队伍建设，提高其工作人员的法制、业务素质。  
县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员应当接受法制和道路运输管理业务培训、考核，考核不合格的，不得上岗执行职务。

**Article 54** The competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of law enforcement teams, and improve the legal and professional quality of its staff.  
The staff members of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall receive training and assessment on legal system and road transport management business. Those who fail the assessment shall not assume their posts to perform their duties.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　上级交通运输主管部门应当对下级交通运输主管部门的执法活动进行监督。  
县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当建立健全内部监督制度，对其工作人员执法情况进行监督检查。

**Article 55** The competent transport department at a higher level shall supervise the law enforcement activities of the competent transport department at a lower level.  
The competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall establish and improve an internal supervision system, and conduct supervision and inspection on the law enforcement of its staff.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门及其工作人员执行职务时，应当自觉接受社会和公民的监督。

**Article 56** When performing their duties, the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level and its staff shall consciously accept supervision from the society and citizens.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当建立道路运输举报制度，公开举报电话号码、通信地址或者电子邮件信箱。  
任何单位和个人都有权对县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊的行为进行举报。县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门及其他有关部门收到举报后，应当依法及时查处。

**Article 57** The competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall establish a reporting system for road transport, and make public the telephone number, correspondence address or email box for reporting.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act of abuse of power or favoritism committed by the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level. Upon receiving such reports, the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall promptly investigate and handle them according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员应当严格按照职责权限和程序进行监督检查，不得乱设卡、乱收费、乱罚款。  
县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员应当重点在道路运输及相关业务经营场所、客货集散地进行监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员在公路路口进行监督检查时，不得随意拦截正常行驶的道路运输车辆。

**Article 58** The staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall strictly carry out supervision and inspection according to their functions, powers, and procedures, and shall not set up arbitrary checkpoints, impose arbitrary fees, or impose arbitrary fines.  
The staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall focus on conducting supervision and inspection at road transport and related business premises, and passenger and cargo distribution centers.  
When conducting supervision and inspection at highway intersections, the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall not randomly intercept road transport vehicles traveling normally.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员实施监督检查时，应当有2名以上人员参加，并向当事人出示执法证件。

**Article 59** When carrying out supervision and inspection, the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level shall have at least two persons participating, and shall present law enforcement certificates to the party concerned.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员实施监督检查时，可以向有关单位和个人了解情况，查阅、复制有关资料。但是，应当保守被调查单位和个人的商业秘密。  
被监督检查的单位和个人应当接受依法实施的监督检查，如实提供有关资料或者情况。

**Article 60** When carrying out supervision and inspection, the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level may inquire about the situation of relevant units and individuals, and consult and duplicate relevant materials. However, they shall keep the trade secrets of the investigated units and individuals confidential.  
The units and individuals under supervision and inspection shall accept the supervision and inspection carried out according to law, and truthfully provide relevant materials or information.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员在实施道路运输监督检查过程中，发现车辆超载行为的，应当立即予以制止，并采取相应措施安排旅客改乘或者强制卸货。

**Article 61** If, in the course of carrying out road transport supervision and inspection, the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level find any act of overloading, they shall immediately stop it, and take corresponding measures to arrange for passengers to transfer or to force unloading of goods.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员在实施道路运输监督检查过程中，对没有车辆营运证又无法当场提供其他有效证明的车辆予以暂扣的，应当妥善保管，不得使用，不得收取或者变相收取保管费用。

**Article 62** If, in the course of carrying out road transport supervision and inspection, the staff of the competent transport department of a people's government at or above the county level temporarily detain a vehicle that does not have an operation permit for vehicles and cannot provide other valid certificates on the spot, they shall keep it properly, shall not use it, and shall not charge or charge in disguised form any storage fees.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止经营，并处罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未取得道路运输经营许可，擅自从事道路普通货物运输经营，违法所得超过1万元的，没收违法所得，处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处3000元以上1万元以下的罚款，情节严重的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；  
（二）未取得道路运输经营许可，擅自从事道路客运经营，违法所得超过2万元的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足2万元的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；  
（三）未取得道路运输经营许可，擅自从事道路危险货物运输经营，违法所得超过2万元的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足2万元的，处3万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 63** In violation of these Regulations, under any of the following circumstances, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of business and impose a fine; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Engaging in ordinary road cargo transport business without obtaining a road transport operation permit, if the illegal gains exceed RMB 10,000 yuan, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 3,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(2) Engaging in road passenger transport business without obtaining a road transport operation permit, if the illegal gains exceed RMB 20,000 yuan, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed;  
(3) Engaging in road dangerous cargo transport business without obtaining a road transport operation permit, if the illegal gains exceed RMB 20,000 yuan, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 30,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　不符合[本条例第九条](#art-9)、第二十二条规定条件的人员驾驶道路运输经营车辆的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处200元以上2000元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** If a person who does not meet the conditions specified in Articles 9 and 22 of these Regulations drives a road transport business vehicle, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本条例的规定，未经许可擅自从事道路旅客运输站（场）经营的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止经营；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
从事机动车维修经营业务不符合国务院交通运输主管部门制定的机动车维修经营业务标准的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；情节严重的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停业整顿。  
从事道路货物运输站（场）经营、机动车驾驶员培训业务，未按规定进行备案的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款。  
从事机动车维修经营业务，未按规定进行备案的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处3000元以上1万元以下的罚款。  
备案时提供虚假材料情节严重的，其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员5年内不得从事原备案的业务。

**Article 65** In violation of these Regulations, engaging in road passenger transport station (yard) business without a permit, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of business; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than RMB 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If engaging in motor vehicle maintenance business does not meet the standards for motor vehicle maintenance business formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order suspension of business for rectification.  
If engaging in road cargo transport station (yard) business or motor vehicle driver training business without filing records as required, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If engaging in motor vehicle maintenance business without filing records as required, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if it refuses to correct, a fine of not less than RMB 3,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If false materials are provided in the filing record and the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall not engage in the originally filed business within 5 years.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本条例的规定，客运经营者、货运经营者、道路运输相关业务经营者非法转让、出租道路运输许可证件的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止违法行为，收缴有关证件，处2000元以上1万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 66** In violation of these Regulations, if a passenger transport operator, cargo transport operator, or road transport related business operator illegally transfers or leases a road transport permit, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the relevant permit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本条例的规定，客运经营者、危险货物运输经营者未按规定投保承运人责任险的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令限期投保；拒不投保的，由原许可机关吊销道路运输经营许可证。

**Article 67** In violation of these Regulations, if a passenger transport operator or dangerous cargo transport operator fails to insure the carrier's liability insurance as required, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order insurance to be taken out within a time limit; if it refuses to take out insurance, the original licensing authority shall revoke the road transport operation permit.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本条例的规定，客运经营者有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处1000元以上2000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原许可机关吊销道路运输经营许可证：  
（一）不按批准的客运站点停靠或者不按规定的线路、公布的班次行驶的；  
（二）在旅客运输途中擅自变更运输车辆或者将旅客移交他人运输的；  
（三）未报告原许可机关，擅自终止客运经营的。  
客运经营者强行招揽旅客，货运经营者强行招揽货物或者没有采取必要措施防止货物脱落、扬撒等的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处1000元以上3000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原许可机关吊销道路运输经营许可证。

**Article 68** In violation of these Regulations, if a passenger transport operator falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 2,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke the road transport operation permit:  
(1) Failing to stop at the approved passenger station or failing to travel in accordance with the prescribed routes and published schedules;  
(2) Changing the transport vehicle without authorization during the passenger transport journey or transferring passengers to others for transport;  
(3) Terminating passenger transport operations without authorization without reporting to the original licensing authority.  
If a passenger transport operator forcibly solicits passengers, or a cargo transport operator forcibly solicits cargo or fails to take necessary measures to prevent cargo from falling off or scattering, the competent transport department of a local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 yuan but not more than RMB 3,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke the road transport operation permit.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本条例的规定，客运经营者、货运经营者不按规定维护和检测运输车辆的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例的规定，客运经营者、货运经营者擅自改装已取得车辆营运证的车辆的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 69** Where a passenger or freight transport operator fails to maintain and inspect transport vehicles in accordance with these Regulations, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.  
Where a passenger or freight transport operator, in violation of these Regulations, illegally modifies a vehicle that has obtained a vehicle operation certificate, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本条例的规定，道路旅客运输站（场）经营者允许无证经营的车辆进站从事经营活动以及超载车辆、未经安全检查的车辆出站或者无正当理由拒绝道路运输车辆进站从事经营活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款。  
道路货物运输站（场）经营者有前款违法情形的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处3000元以上3万元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例的规定，道路运输站（场）经营者擅自改变道路运输站（场）的用途和服务功能，或者不公布运输线路、起止经停站点、运输班次、始发时间、票价的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处3000元的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 70** Where a road passenger transport station (yard) operator, in violation of these Regulations, allows vehicles without permits to enter the station for business operations, or allows overloaded vehicles or vehicles that have not undergone safety inspection to leave the station, or refuses without justifiable cause to allow road transport vehicles to enter the station for business operations, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan.  
Where a road freight transport station (yard) operator commits the illegal act specified in the preceding paragraph, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan.  
Where a road transport station (yard) operator, in violation of these Regulations, changes the use or service functions of a road transport station (yard) without authorization, or fails to publish the transport routes, stops, departure times, and ticket prices, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification; if the operator refuses to rectify, a fine of 3,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本条例的规定，机动车维修经营者使用假冒伪劣配件维修机动车，承修已报废的机动车或者擅自改装机动车的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款，没收假冒伪劣配件及报废车辆；情节严重的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** Where a motor vehicle maintenance operator, in violation of these Regulations, uses counterfeit or inferior parts to repair motor vehicles, undertakes repair of motor vehicles that have been scrapped, or illegally modifies motor vehicles, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed and the counterfeit or inferior parts and the scrapped vehicles shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order suspension of business for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本条例的规定，机动车维修经营者签发虚假的机动车维修合格证，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足3000元的，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停业整顿；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 72** Where a motor vehicle maintenance operator, in violation of these Regulations, issues a false motor vehicle maintenance certificate, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 3,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order suspension of business for rectification; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本条例的规定，机动车驾驶员培训机构不严格按照规定进行培训或者在培训结业证书发放时弄虚作假的，由县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 73** Where a motor vehicle driver training institution, in violation of these Regulations, fails to strictly carry out training in accordance with the provisions or engages in fraudulent practices in issuing training completion certificates, the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification; if it refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本条例的规定，外国国际道路运输经营者未按照规定的线路运输，擅自从事中国境内道路运输的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止运输；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，处违法所得2倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足1万元的，处3万元以上6万元以下的罚款。  
外国国际道路运输经营者未按照规定标明国籍识别标志的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止运输，处200元以上2000元以下的罚款。  
从事国际道路货物运输经营，未按规定进行备案的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 74** Where a foreign international road transport operator, in violation of these Regulations, carries out road transport within China without authorization by not following the prescribed routes, the transport authority of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order cessation of transport; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated and a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 60,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a foreign international road transport operator fails to display the nationality identification mark as required, the transport authority of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order cessation of transport and impose a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan.  
Where a person engages in international road freight transport without completing the required filing, the transport authority of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order rectification; if it refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门应当将道路运输及其相关业务经营者和从业人员的违法行为记入信用记录，并依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以公示。

**Article 75** The transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall record the illegal acts of road transport and related business operators and practitioners into the credit record, and shall publish them in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本条例的规定，县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员有下列情形之一的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不依照本条例规定的条件、程序和期限实施行政许可的；  
（二）参与或者变相参与道路运输经营以及道路运输相关业务的；  
（三）发现违法行为不及时查处的；  
（四）违反规定拦截、检查正常行驶的道路运输车辆的；  
（五）违法扣留运输车辆、车辆营运证的；  
（六）索取、收受他人财物，或者谋取其他利益的；  
（七）其他违法行为。

**Article 76** Where any staff member of the transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to implement administrative licenses in accordance with the conditions, procedures, and time limits prescribed in these Regulations;  
(2) participating or participating in a disguised form in road transport operations and road transport related businesses;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts in a timely manner upon discovery;  
(4) intercepting and inspecting road transport vehicles that are operating normally in violation of regulations;  
(5) illegally detaining transport vehicles or vehicle operation certificates;  
(6) demanding or accepting property from others, or seeking other benefits;  
(7) other illegal acts.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区之间的道路运输，参照本条例的有关规定执行。

**Article 77** Road transport between the mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be carried out by reference to the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　外商可以依照有关法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，在中华人民共和国境内采用中外合资、中外合作、独资形式投资有关的道路运输经营以及道路运输相关业务。

**Article 78** Foreign investors may, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, invest in road transport operations and road transport related businesses within the territory of the People's Republic of China through the forms of Chinese-foreign equity joint ventures, Chinese-foreign contractual joint ventures, or wholly foreign-owned enterprises.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　从事非经营性危险货物运输的，应当遵守本条例有关规定。

**Article 79** Persons engaging in non-operational transport of dangerous goods shall comply with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门依照本条例发放经营许可证件和车辆营运证，可以收取工本费。工本费的具体收费标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门、价格主管部门会同同级交通运输主管部门核定。

**Article 80** The transport authority of the local people's government at or above the county level may charge a cost fee for issuing operation permits and vehicle operation certificates in accordance with these Regulations. The specific fee standard shall be determined by the finance department and the price department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the transport authority at the same level.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　出租车客运和城市公共汽车客运的管理办法由国务院另行规定。

**Article 81** The administrative measures for taxi passenger transport and urban bus passenger transport shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本条例自2004年7月1日起施行。

**Article 82** These Regulations shall come into force as of July 1, 2004.
