### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强对外商参与打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物活动的管理，保障有关各方的合法权益，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to strengthen the administration of foreign merchants' participation in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in the coastal waters of China and to protect the lawful rights and interests of the relevant parties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法适用于外商参与打捞中国沿海水域具有商业价值的沉船沉物活动。  
沉船沉物的所有人自行打捞或者聘请打捞机构打捞其在中国沿海水域的沉船沉物，不适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures apply to activities of foreign merchants participating in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles of commercial value in the coastal waters of China.  
These Measures shall not apply where the owner of the shipwrecks or sunken articles conducts salvage by itself or engages a salvage institution to salvage its shipwrecks or sunken articles in the coastal waters of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法下列用语的定义：  
(一)外商，是指外国的企业或者其他经济组织或者个人。  
(二)沿海水域，是指中华人民共和国内海、领海和属于中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域。  
(三)沉船沉物，是指沉没于中国沿海水域水面以下或者淤埋海底泥面以下的各类船舶和器物，包括沉船沉物的主体及其设备、所载的全部货物或者其他物品。  
具有重要军事价值的沉没舰船和武器装备及被确认为文物的沉船沉物不在外商参与打捞的对象之列。  
(四)打捞作业，是指根据共同打捞合同或者打捞企业合同，对沉船沉物进行的各种施工活动，包括扫测探摸、实施打捞及相关的活动。  
(五)打捞作业者，是指具体实施打捞作业的单位或者个人。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Measures, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Foreign merchant" means a foreign enterprise, other economic organization, or individual.  
(2) "Coastal waters" means the internal waters, territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
(3) "Shipwrecks and sunken articles" means all types of vessels and objects that have sunk below the water surface of China's coastal waters or are buried beneath the mud surface of the seabed, including the main body of the shipwrecks and sunken articles and their equipment, all cargo or other items carried on board.  
Sunken warships and weaponry of significant military value, as well as shipwrecks and sunken articles identified as cultural relics, shall not be subject to foreign merchant participation in salvage.  
(4) "Salvage operations" means various construction activities carried out for shipwrecks and sunken articles under a joint salvage contract or a salvage enterprise contract, including sweeping, detection, exploration, implementation of salvage, and related activities.  
(5) "Salvage operator" means the unit or individual that actually carries out the salvage operations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国政府依法保护参与打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物的中外双方(以下简称参与打捞的中外双方)的应得收益和其他合法权益。  
参与打捞的中外双方的一切活动必须遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规和规章，接受中国政府有关主管部门的管理与监督。

**Article 4** The Chinese government shall, according to law, protect the due proceeds and other lawful rights and interests of the Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in the coastal waters of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage).  
All activities of the Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage must comply with the relevant laws, regulations, and rules of the People's Republic of China and shall be subject to the administration and supervision of the relevant competent authorities of the Chinese government.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管有关外商参与打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物的事宜。

**Article 5** The competent department of transportation under the State Council shall be responsible for matters relating to the participation of foreign merchants in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in the coastal waters of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外商参与打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物，可以采取下列方式：  
(一)与中方打捞人签订共同打捞合同，依照合同规定的双方权利和义务，实施打捞活动；  
(二)与中方打捞人成立打捞企业，实施打捞活动。

**Article 6** Foreign merchants participating in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in the coastal waters of China may adopt either of the following methods:  
(1) sign a joint salvage contract with a Chinese salvor and carry out salvage activities in accordance with the rights and obligations of both parties as stipulated in the contract;  
(2) establish a salvage enterprise with a Chinese salvor and carry out salvage activities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　共同打捞合同和打捞企业合同必须有明确的打捞标的。参与打捞的中外双方在打捞过程中发现的不属于合同标的的其他沉船沉物，不得擅自实施打捞。

**Article 7** A joint salvage contract or a salvage enterprise contract shall have a definite subject matter of salvage. The Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage shall not salvage without authorization any other shipwrecks or sunken articles discovered during the salvage process that are not the subject matter of the contract.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院交通运输主管部门负责统一组织与外商洽谈打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物的事宜，确立打捞项目，并组织中方打捞人与外商依法签订共同打捞合同或者打捞企业合同；涉及军事禁区、军事管理区的，应当符合《中华人民共和国军事设施保护法》的有关规定。

**Article 8** The competent department of transportation under the State Council shall be responsible for uniformly organizing negotiations with foreign merchants on matters of salvaging shipwrecks and sunken articles in the coastal waters of China, establishing salvage projects, and organizing Chinese salvors to sign, according to law, joint salvage contracts or salvage enterprise contracts with foreign merchants; where military restricted zones or military administrative zones are involved, the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Military Facilities shall be complied with.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外商与中方打捞人签订共同打捞合同，应当符合《中华人民共和国合同法》的有关规定。共同打捞合同签订后，外商应当按照规定向市场监督管理部门申请营业登记，并向当地税务机关办理税务登记手续。

**Article 9** When a foreign merchant signs a joint salvage contract with a Chinese salvor, the provisions of the Contract Law of the People's Republic of China shall be complied with. After the joint salvage contract is signed, the foreign merchant shall, according to the provisions, apply to the market supervision and administration department for business registration and go through the formalities of tax registration with the local tax authority.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外商参与在中华人民共和国内海或者领海内打捞沉船沉物，应当承担打捞作业期间的全部费用和经济风险。中方打捞人负责与有关部门的协调，办理必要的手续及打捞作业期间的监护。  
外商参与在中华人民共和国内海、领海外属中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内打捞沉船沉物，应当承担扫测探摸阶段的全部费用和经济风险。需要打捞的，由中外双方按照合同规定实施打捞。

**Article 10** Where a foreign merchant participates in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in the internal waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, it shall bear all costs and economic risks during the salvage operations. The Chinese salvor shall be responsible for coordination with relevant departments, handling necessary formalities, and providing surveillance during the salvage operations.  
Where a foreign merchant participates in the salvage of shipwrecks and sunken articles in sea areas other than the internal waters and territorial sea of the People's Republic of China but under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, it shall bear all costs and economic risks during the stage of sweeping, detection, and exploration. If salvage is necessary, the Chinese and foreign parties shall carry out the salvage in accordance with the provisions of the contract.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外商为履行共同打捞合同所需船舶、设备及劳务，在同等条件下应当优先向中方打捞人租用和雇佣。

**Article 11** Foreign investors shall, for the purpose of performing the joint salvage contract, give priority to leasing and hiring from Chinese salvors regarding the vessels, equipment, and labor services required, under the same conditions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外商参与打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物的捞获物(以下简称捞获物)应当按照下列方式处理：  
(一)在中华人民共和国内海或者领海内捞获的沉船沉物，属中华人民共和国所有，外商根据共同打捞合同或者打捞企业合同的规定，从捞获物或者其折价中取得收益；  
中方打捞人根据国家有关规定或者打捞企业合同的规定从捞获物或者其折价中取得收益。  
(二)在中华人民共和国内海、领海外属中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内捞获的沉船沉物，由参与打捞的中外双方按照合同规定的比例对捞获物或者其折价进行分成。  
(三)捞获物中夹带有文物或者在打捞作业活动中发现文物的，应当立即报告当地文物行政管理部门，由文物行政管理部门按照中华人民共和国有关文物保护的法律、法规处理，并给有关人员适当的奖励。

**Article 12** The salvaged objects from foreign investors' participation in the salvage of sunken ships and objects in the coastal waters of China (hereinafter referred to as salvaged objects) shall be disposed of in the following manner:  
(1) Sunken ships and objects salvaged in the internal seas or territorial seas of the People's Republic of China shall be owned by the People's Republic of China. Foreign investors shall obtain proceeds from the salvaged objects or the monetary value thereof in accordance with the provisions of the joint salvage contract or the salvage enterprise contract;  
Chinese salvors shall obtain proceeds from the salvaged objects or the monetary value thereof in accordance with the relevant state provisions or the provisions of the salvage enterprise contract.  
(2) Sunken ships and objects salvaged in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China beyond the internal seas and territorial seas shall be shared between the Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage in accordance with the proportions stipulated in the contract, either in kind or in monetary value.  
(3) Where cultural relics are mixed in the salvaged objects or are discovered during salvage operations, the local administrative department of cultural relics shall be immediately reported, and such department shall handle the matter in accordance with the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China on the protection of cultural relics and shall give appropriate rewards to the persons concerned.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外商依法取得的捞获物可以按照国际市场价格由中国政府有关部门收购或者由外商依法纳税并办理海关手续后运往国外。  
外商所得外汇收入或者其他收益，可以在纳税后依法汇往国外。

**Article 13** The salvaged objects lawfully obtained by foreign investors may be purchased by the relevant departments of the Chinese government at international market prices, or may be shipped abroad by the foreign investors after paying taxes and completing customs formalities according to law.  
Any foreign exchange income or other proceeds obtained by foreign investors may be remitted abroad according to law after taxation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　打捞作业者在实施打捞作业前，应当按照《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》的有关规定申请发布航行警告。  
国务院交通运输主管部门应当将打捞作业的起止时间、地理位置等情况通报国家海洋局等有关部门。  
打捞作业者实施打捞作业时，必须在港务监督核准的作业区域内进行，并按照港务监督的要求报告有关活动情况。实施打捞作业不得使用危害海洋资源、海洋环境、海底设施、海上军事设施和其他损害中华人民共和国利益的方法。

**Article 14** Before carrying out salvage operations, a salvor shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, apply for the issuance of a navigation warning.  
The competent transport department of the State Council shall notify the State Oceanic Administration and other relevant departments of the start and end times, geographical location, and other information concerning the salvage operations.  
When carrying out salvage operations, the salvor must conduct operations within the area approved by the harbor superintendency administration, and shall report relevant activities as required by the harbor superintendency administration. No salvor shall use methods that harm marine resources, the marine environment, seabed facilities, military installations at sea, or otherwise damage the interests of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　实施打捞作业应当自始至终有参与打捞的中外双方的有关人员参加，双方共同负责捞获物的登记和保管工作。

**Article 15** In carrying out salvage operations, relevant personnel from both the Chinese and foreign parties participating in the salvage shall be present throughout the process, and the two parties shall jointly be responsible for the registration and custody of the salvaged objects.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　所有捞获物应当在中国政府主管部门指定的地点接受有关部门的检查。

**Article 16** All salvaged objects shall be subject to inspection by the relevant departments at the site designated by the competent department of the Chinese government.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反本办法打捞中国沿海水域沉船沉物的，港务监督有权责令其停止打捞作业，并可给予警告、罚款的处罚，其中已给国家和他人造成损失的，应当承担赔偿责任。  
罚款数额按照国家有关海上交通管理处罚的规定执行。

**Article 17** Where anyone violates these Measures by salvaging sunken ships and objects in the coastal waters of China, the harbor superintendency administration shall have the power to order the cessation of salvage operations, and may impose a warning or a fine. If losses have been caused to the State or to others, the violator shall bear compensation liability.  
The amount of the fine shall be determined in accordance with the relevant state provisions on penalties for maritime traffic management.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　香港、澳门、台湾的企业、个人及其他经济组织参与打捞中国沿海水域的沉船沉物，参照本办法执行。

**Article 18** Enterprises, individuals, and other economic organizations from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan participating in the salvage of sunken ships and objects in the coastal waters of China shall refer to these Measures for implementation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 19** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
