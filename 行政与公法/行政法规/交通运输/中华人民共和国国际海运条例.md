### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范国际海上运输活动，保护公平竞争，维护国际海上运输市场秩序，保障国际海上运输各方当事人的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of regulating international maritime transport activities, protecting fair competition, maintaining the order of the international maritime transport market, and safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of all parties involved in international maritime transport.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于进出中华人民共和国港口的国际海上运输经营活动以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动。  
前款所称与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动，包括国际船舶代理、国际船舶管理、国际海运货物装卸、国际海运货物仓储、国际海运集装箱站和堆场、国际航运交易平台服务等业务。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the operational activities of international maritime transport entering or leaving the ports of the People's Republic of China and the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport.  
The auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport referred to in the preceding paragraph include international ship agency, international ship management, international maritime cargo handling, international maritime cargo warehousing, international maritime container depots and yards, international shipping trading platform services, and other such businesses.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事国际海上运输经营活动以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动，应当遵循诚实信用的原则，依法经营，公平竞争。

**Article 3** Any party engaging in the operational activities of international maritime transport and the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport shall adhere to the principle of good faith, operate according to law, and compete fairly.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院交通运输主管部门和有关的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门依照本条例规定，对国际海上运输经营活动实施监督管理，并对与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动实施有关的监督管理。  
国务院交通运输主管部门和有关的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门应当对国际海上运输及其辅助性业务的经营者和从业人员实施信用管理，并将相关信用记录纳入全国信用信息共享平台。

**Article 4** The competent transport department of the State Council and the competent transport departments of the relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the operational activities of international maritime transport and exercise relevant supervision and administration over the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport.  
The competent transport department of the State Council and the competent transport departments of the relevant local people's governments shall implement credit management over the operators and practitioners of international maritime transport and its auxiliary businesses, and shall incorporate the relevant credit records into the national credit information sharing platform.

### 第二章　国际海上运输及其辅助性业务的经营者

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输业务，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）取得企业法人资格；  
（二）有与经营业务相适应的船舶，其中必须有中国籍船舶；  
（三）投入运营的船舶符合国家规定的海上交通安全技术标准；  
（四）有提单、客票或者多式联运单证；  
（五）有具备国务院交通运输主管部门规定的从业资格的高级业务管理人员。  
经营国际集装箱船、国际普通货船运输业务，应当取得企业法人资格，并有与经营业务相适应的船舶。

**Article 5** To operate international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport business, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having obtained the status of an enterprise legal person;  
(2) having vessels suitable for the business, among which there shall be Chinese-flagged vessels;  
(3) the vessels put into operation comply with the maritime traffic safety technical standards prescribed by the State;  
(4) having bills of lading, passenger tickets or multimodal transport documents;  
(5) having senior business management personnel who possess the professional qualifications prescribed by the competent transport department of the State Council.  
To operate international container ship or international general cargo ship transport business, the operator shall have obtained the status of an enterprise legal person and have vessels suitable for the business.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输业务，应当向国务院交通运输主管部门提出申请，并附送符合[本条例第五条](#art-5)规定条件的相关材料。国务院交通运输主管部门应当自受理申请之日起30日内审核完毕，作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，向申请人颁发《国际船舶运输经营许可证》；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并告知理由。  
国务院交通运输主管部门审核国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输业务申请时，应当考虑国家关于国际海上运输业发展的政策和国际海上运输市场竞争状况。  
申请经营国际客船运输业务，并同时申请经营国际班轮运输业务的，还应当附送[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的相关材料，由国务院交通运输主管部门一并审核、登记。  
经营国际集装箱船、国际普通货船运输业务，应当自开业之日起15日内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门备案，备案信息包括企业名称、注册地、联系方式、船舶情况。

**Article 6** To operate international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport business, an application shall be submitted to the competent transport department of the State Council, together with the relevant materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions prescribed in Article 5 of these Regulations. The competent transport department of the State Council shall complete the review within 30 days from the date of acceptance of the application and make a decision to grant or deny the license. If the license is granted, it shall issue the "Permit for International Ship Transport Operation" to the applicant; if the license is denied, it shall give written notice to the applicant and state the reasons.  
When reviewing an application for international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport business, the competent transport department of the State Council shall take into account the national policies on the development of the international maritime transport industry and the competitive conditions of the international maritime transport market.  
If an applicant applies to operate international passenger ship transport business and also applies to operate international liner transport business, it shall additionally submit the relevant materials prescribed in Article 11 of these Regulations, and the competent transport department of the State Council shall conduct a consolidated review and registration.  
To operate international container ship or international general cargo ship transport business, the operator shall file for record with the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 15 days from the date of commencing business, and the record information shall include the enterprise name, place of registration, contact information, and vessel particulars.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经营无船承运业务，应当自开业之日起15日内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门备案，备案信息包括企业名称、注册地、联系方式。  
前款所称无船承运业务，是指无船承运业务经营者以承运人身份接受托运人的货载，签发自己的提单或者其他运输单证，向托运人收取运费，通过国际船舶运输经营者完成国际海上货物运输，承担承运人责任的国际海上运输经营活动。  
在中国境内经营无船承运业务，应当在中国境内依法设立企业法人。

**Article 7** To operate a non-vessel operating common carrier (NVOCC) business, the operator shall file for record with the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 15 days from the date of commencing business, and the record information shall include the enterprise name, place of registration, and contact information.  
The term "non-vessel operating common carrier business" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means an international maritime transport operational activity in which the NVOCC operator accepts cargo from a shipper in the capacity of a carrier, issues its own bill of lading or other transport documents, collects freight from the shipper, completes the international maritime cargo transport through an international ship transport operator, and assumes the liability of a carrier.  
To operate an NVOCC business within the territory of China, an enterprise legal person shall be established in China according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输经营者，不得将依法取得的经营资格提供给他人使用。

**Article 8** An operator of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport shall not provide the operational qualification obtained according to law to any other person for use.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输经营者依照本条例的规定取得相应的经营资格后，不再具备本条例规定的条件的，国务院交通运输主管部门应当立即取消其经营资格。

**Article 9** If an operator of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport, after obtaining the corresponding operational qualification in accordance with these Regulations, no longer meets the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, the competent transport department of the State Council shall immediately revoke its operational qualification.

### 第三章　国际海上运输及其辅助性业务经营活动

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国际船舶运输经营者经营进出中国港口的国际班轮运输业务，应当依照本条例的规定取得国际班轮运输经营资格。  
未取得国际班轮运输经营资格的，不得从事国际班轮运输经营活动，不得对外公布班期、接受订舱。  
以共同派船、舱位互换、联合经营等方式经营国际班轮运输的，适用本条第一款的规定。

**Article 10** An international ship transport operator that operates international liner transport business to or from Chinese ports shall obtain the operational qualification for international liner transport in accordance with these Regulations.  
Any operator that has not obtained the operational qualification for international liner transport shall not engage in the operational activities of international liner transport, and shall not publish sailing schedules or accept bookings.  
Where international liner transport is operated by means of joint vessel deployment, slot exchange, joint operation, or other similar methods, the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article shall apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经营国际班轮运输业务，应当向国务院交通运输主管部门提出申请，并附送下列材料：  
（一）国际船舶运输经营者的名称、注册地、营业执照副本、主要出资人；  
（二）经营者的主要管理人员的姓名及其身份证明；  
（三）运营船舶资料；  
（四）拟开航的航线、班期及沿途停泊港口；  
（五）运价本；  
（六）提单、客票或者多式联运单证。  
国务院交通运输主管部门应当自收到经营国际班轮运输业务申请之日起30日内审核完毕。申请材料真实、齐备的，予以登记，并通知申请人；申请材料不真实或者不齐备的，不予登记，书面通知申请人并告知理由。

**Article 11** To operate international liner transport business, an application shall be submitted to the competent transport department of the State Council, together with the following materials:  
(1) the name, place of registration, copy of business license, and principal contributors of the international ship transport operator;  
(2) the names and identity documents of the principal management personnel of the operator;  
(3) particulars of the vessels to be operated;  
(4) the intended routes, sailing schedules, and ports of call along the way;  
(5) the tariff;  
(6) bills of lading, passenger tickets or multimodal transport documents.  
The competent transport department of the State Council shall complete the review within 30 days from the date of receiving the application for operating international liner transport business. If the application materials are authentic and complete, it shall register the application and notify the applicant; if the application materials are not authentic or not complete, it shall not register the application, give written notice to the applicant, and state the reasons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　取得国际班轮运输经营资格的国际船舶运输经营者，应当自取得资格之日起180日内开航；因不可抗力并经国务院交通运输主管部门同意，可以延期90日。逾期未开航的，国际班轮运输经营资格自期满之日起丧失。

**Article 12** An international ship transport operator that has obtained the operational qualification for international liner transport shall commence its sailings within 180 days from the date of obtaining the qualification; with the consent of the competent transport department of the State Council due to force majeure, the period may be extended by 90 days. If it fails to commence sailings within the prescribed period, the operational qualification for international liner transport shall be forfeited from the date of expiry of such period.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　新开、停开国际班轮运输航线，或者变更国际班轮运输船舶、班期的，应当提前15日予以公告，并应当自行为发生之日起15日内向国务院交通运输主管部门备案。

**Article 13** If a new international liner transport route is opened, an existing route is suspended, or the vessel or sailing schedule for international liner transport is changed, such change shall be publicly announced 15 days in advance, and a record shall be filed with the competent transport department of the State Council within 15 days from the date on which such act occurs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经营国际班轮运输业务的国际船舶运输经营者的运价和无船承运业务经营者的运价，应当按照规定格式向国务院交通运输主管部门备案。国务院交通运输主管部门应当指定专门机构受理运价备案。  
备案的运价包括公布运价和协议运价。公布运价，是指国际船舶运输经营者和无船承运业务经营者运价本上载明的运价；协议运价，是指国际船舶运输经营者与货主、无船承运业务经营者约定的运价。  
公布运价自国务院交通运输主管部门受理备案之日起满30日生效；协议运价自国务院交通运输主管部门受理备案之时起满24小时生效。  
国际船舶运输经营者和无船承运业务经营者应当执行生效的备案运价。

**Article 14** The tariffs of international ship transport operators operating international liner transport business and the tariffs of NVOCC operators shall be filed for record with the competent transport department of the State Council in the prescribed format. The competent transport department of the State Council shall designate a specialized agency to accept tariff filings.  
The tariffs filed for record include published tariffs and negotiated tariffs. "Published tariffs" means the tariffs set out in the tariff books of international ship transport operators and NVOCC operators; "negotiated tariffs" means the tariffs agreed between international ship transport operators and cargo owners or NVOCC operators.  
Published tariffs shall take effect 30 days after the date on which the competent transport department of the State Council accepts the filing; negotiated tariffs shall take effect 24 hours after the time when the competent transport department of the State Council accepts the filing.  
International ship transport operators and NVOCC operators shall implement the filed tariffs that have taken effect.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　从事国际班轮运输的国际船舶运输经营者之间订立涉及中国港口的班轮公会协议、运营协议、运价协议等，应当自协议订立之日起15日内将协议副本向国务院交通运输主管部门备案。

**Article 15** Where international ship transport operators engaged in international liner transport conclude among themselves liner conference agreements, operational agreements, tariff agreements, or the like that involve Chinese ports, a copy of the agreement shall be filed for record with the competent transport department of the State Council within 15 days from the date of conclusion of the agreement.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输经营者有下列情形之一的，应当在情形发生之日起15日内，向国务院交通运输主管部门备案：  
（一）终止经营；  
（二）减少运营船舶；  
（三）变更提单、客票或者多式联运单证；  
（四）在境外设立分支机构或者子公司经营相应业务；  
（五）拥有的船舶在境外注册，悬挂外国旗。  
国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输经营者增加运营船舶的，增加的运营船舶必须符合国家规定的安全技术标准，并应当于投入运营前15日内向国务院交通运输主管部门备案。国务院交通运输主管部门应当自收到备案材料之日起3日内出具备案证明文件。  
其他中国企业有本条第一款第（四）项、第（五）项所列情形之一的，应当依照本条第一款规定办理备案手续。  
国际集装箱船运输经营者、国际普通货船运输经营者和无船承运业务经营者终止经营的，应当自终止经营之日起15日内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门备案。

**Article 16** Where an operator of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall file for record with the competent transport department of the State Council within 15 days from the date on which the circumstance occurs:  
(1) termination of business;  
(2) reduction of operating vessels;  
(3) change of bills of lading, passenger tickets or multimodal transport documents;  
(4) establishment of a branch or subsidiary abroad to operate corresponding business;  
(5) the vessels it owns are registered abroad and fly foreign flags.  
If an operator of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport increases its operating vessels, the additional vessels must comply with the safety technical standards prescribed by the State, and a record shall be filed with the competent transport department of the State Council within 15 days before they are put into operation. The competent transport department of the State Council shall issue a certificate of record within 3 days from the date of receiving the filing materials.  
If any other Chinese enterprise falls under any of the circumstances listed in subparagraphs (4) and (5) of paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall go through the filing formalities in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article.  
If an operator of international container ship transport, international general cargo ship transport, or an NVOCC operator terminates its business, it shall file for record with the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government within 15 days from the date of termination.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营国际船舶运输业务、无船承运业务和国际船舶代理业务，在中国境内收取、代为收取运费以及其他相关费用，应当向付款人出具中国税务机关统一印制的发票。

**Article 17** Any operator of international ship transport business, NVOCC business, or international ship agency business that collects or collects on behalf of others freight and other relevant fees within the territory of China shall issue to the payer an invoice printed uniformly by the tax authorities of China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经营国际船舶运输业务和无船承运业务，不得有下列行为：  
（一）以低于正常、合理水平的运价提供服务，妨碍公平竞争；  
（二）在会计账簿之外暗中给予托运人回扣，承揽货物；  
（三）滥用优势地位，以歧视性价格或者其他限制性条件给交易对方造成损害；  
（四）其他损害交易对方或者国际海上运输市场秩序的行为。

**Article 18** Any operator of international ship transport business or NVOCC business shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) providing services at tariffs lower than a normal and reasonable level, thereby impeding fair competition;  
(2) secretly offering rebates to shippers outside the accounting books to solicit cargo;  
(3) abusing a dominant position to cause harm to the other party to a transaction through discriminatory prices or other restrictive conditions;  
(4) other acts that harm the other party to a transaction or the order of the international maritime transport market.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外国国际船舶运输经营者从事本章规定的有关国际船舶运输活动，应当遵守本条例有关规定。  
外国国际船舶运输经营者不得经营中国港口之间的船舶运输业务，也不得利用租用的中国籍船舶或者舱位，或者以互换舱位等方式变相经营中国港口之间的船舶运输业务。

**Article 19** Foreign international ship transport operators that engage in the relevant international ship transport activities stipulated in this Chapter shall comply with the relevant provisions of these Regulations.  
Foreign international ship transport operators shall not operate vessel transport services between Chinese ports, nor shall they use chartered Chinese-flagged vessels or slots, or swap slots or adopt other means to indirectly operate vessel transport services between Chinese ports.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国际船舶代理经营者接受船舶所有人或者船舶承租人、船舶经营人的委托，可以经营下列业务：  
（一）办理船舶进出港口手续，联系安排引航、靠泊和装卸；  
（二）代签提单、运输合同，代办接受订舱业务；  
（三）办理船舶、集装箱以及货物的报关手续；  
（四）承揽货物、组织货载，办理货物、集装箱的托运和中转；  
（五）代收运费，代办结算；  
（六）组织客源，办理有关海上旅客运输业务；  
（七）其他相关业务。  
国际船舶代理经营者应当按照国家有关规定代扣代缴其所代理的外国国际船舶运输经营者的税款。

**Article 20** An international ship agency operator, upon the entrustment of a shipowner, a ship charterer, or a ship operator, may operate the following businesses:  
(1) handling procedures for vessel entry and exit of ports, arranging pilotage, berthing, and loading and unloading;  
(2) signing bills of lading and transport contracts on behalf of others, and handling booking acceptance on behalf of others;  
(3) handling customs declaration procedures for vessels, containers, and cargo;  
(4) soliciting cargo, organizing cargo loads, handling consignment and transshipment of cargo and containers;  
(5) collecting freight on behalf of others, and handling settlement on behalf of others;  
(6) organizing passenger sources, handling relevant maritime passenger transport businesses;  
(7) other related businesses.  
An international ship agency operator shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, withhold and remit the taxes of the foreign international ship transport operators it represents.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国际船舶管理经营者接受船舶所有人或者船舶承租人、船舶经营人的委托，可以经营下列业务：  
（一）船舶买卖、租赁以及其他船舶资产管理；  
（二）机务、海务和安排维修；  
（三）船员招聘、训练和配备；  
（四）保证船舶技术状况和正常航行的其他服务。

**Article 21** An international ship management operator, upon the entrustment of a shipowner, a ship charterer, or a ship operator, may operate the following businesses:  
(1) ship purchase and sale, chartering, and other ship asset management;  
(2) engine and marine affairs, and arrangement of repairs;  
(3) recruitment, training, and manning of crew;  
(4) other services to ensure the technical condition and normal navigation of vessels.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国际航运交易平台经营者应当向国务院交通运输主管部门报送经营者名称、注册地、联系方式、平台服务协议、航运交易规则等信息，具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门制定。

**Article 22** An operator of an international shipping trading platform shall submit to the competent transport department of the State Council information including the operator's name, place of registration, contact information, platform service agreement, and shipping trading rules. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council.

### 第四章　外商投资经营国际海上运输及其辅助性业务的特别规定

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　外商在中国境内投资经营国际海上运输业务以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性业务，适用本章规定；本章没有规定的，适用本条例其他有关规定。

**Article 23** Where a foreign investor invests in and operates international maritime transport businesses and auxiliary businesses related to international maritime transport within the territory of China, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply; where this Chapter has no provisions, the other relevant provisions of these Regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　外商可以依照有关法律、行政法规以及国家其他有关规定，投资经营国际船舶运输、国际船舶代理、国际船舶管理、国际海运货物装卸、国际海运货物仓储、国际海运集装箱站和堆场业务。

**Article 24** A foreign investor may, in accordance with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and other relevant provisions of the State, invest in and operate the businesses of international ship transport, international ship agency, international ship management, international maritime cargo handling, international maritime cargo warehousing, and international maritime container depots and yards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　外国国际船舶运输经营者以及外国国际海运辅助企业在中国境内设立的常驻代表机构，不得从事经营活动。

**Article 25** The permanent representative offices established within the territory of China by foreign international ship transport operators and foreign international maritime auxiliary enterprises shall not engage in business operations.

### 第五章　调查与处理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院交通运输主管部门应利害关系人的请求或者自行决定，可以对下列情形实施调查：  
（一）经营国际班轮运输业务的国际船舶运输经营者之间订立的涉及中国港口的班轮公会协议、运营协议、运价协议等，可能对公平竞争造成损害的；  
（二）经营国际班轮运输业务的国际船舶运输经营者通过协议产生的各类联营体，其服务涉及中国港口某一航线的承运份额，持续1年超过该航线总运量的30%，并可能对公平竞争造成损害的；  
（三）有[本条例第十八条](#art-18)规定的行为之一的；  
（四）可能损害国际海运市场公平竞争的其他行为。

**Article 26** The competent transport department of the State Council may, at the request of an interested party or on its own initiative, conduct investigations into the following circumstances:  
(1) liner conference agreements, operational agreements, tariff agreements, or the like concluded among international ship transport operators operating international liner transport business that involve Chinese ports, which may cause damage to fair competition;  
(2) various consortia formed through agreements by international ship transport operators operating international liner transport business, whose services involve a certain route to or from Chinese ports, and whose carrying share has continuously exceeded 30% of the total cargo volume on that route for one year and may cause damage to fair competition;  
(3) any act prescribed in Article 18 of these Regulations;  
(4) other acts that may damage fair competition in the international maritime transport market.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院交通运输主管部门实施调查，应当会同国务院市场监督管理部门（以下统称调查机关）共同进行。

**Article 27** Where the competent transport department of the State Council conducts an investigation, it shall do so jointly with the market supervision and administration department of the State Council (hereinafter collectively referred to as the investigation authorities).

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　调查机关实施调查，应当成立调查组。调查组成员不少于3人。调查组可以根据需要，聘请有关专家参加工作。  
调查组进行调查前，应当将调查目的、调查原因、调查期限等事项通知被调查人。调查期限不得超过1年；必要时，经调查机关批准，可以延长半年。

**Article 28** The investigation authorities shall form an investigation team to conduct an investigation. The investigation team shall consist of no fewer than three members. The investigation team may, if necessary, engage relevant experts to participate in the work.  
Before conducting the investigation, the investigation team shall inform the person under investigation of matters such as the purpose, reasons, and time limit of the investigation. The investigation period shall not exceed one year; if necessary, with the approval of the investigation authorities, it may be extended by six months.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　调查人员进行调查，可以向被调查人以及与其有业务往来的单位和个人了解有关情况，并可查阅、复制有关单证、协议、合同文本、会计账簿、业务函电、电子数据等有关资料。  
调查人员进行调查，应当保守被调查人以及与其有业务往来的单位和个人的商业秘密。

**Article 29** When conducting an investigation, the investigators may inquire about relevant information from the person under investigation and from any units or individuals having business dealings with such person, and may examine and copy relevant documents, agreements, contracts, accounting books, business correspondence, electronic data, and other relevant materials.  
When conducting an investigation, the investigators shall maintain the confidentiality of the trade secrets of the person under investigation and of any units or individuals having business dealings with such person.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　被调查人应当接受调查，如实提供有关情况和资料，不得拒绝调查或者隐匿真实情况、谎报情况。

**Article 30** The person under investigation shall submit to the investigation, truthfully provide relevant information and materials, and shall not refuse the investigation or conceal the true facts or make false reports.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　调查结束，调查机关应当作出调查结论，书面通知被调查人、利害关系人。  
对公平竞争造成损害的，调查机关可以采取责令修改有关协议、限制班轮航班数量、中止运价本或者暂停受理运价备案、责令定期报送有关资料等禁止性、限制性措施。

**Article 31** Upon conclusion of the investigation, the investigation authorities shall make an investigation conclusion and give written notice to the person under investigation and the interested parties.  
If damage to fair competition has been caused, the investigation authorities may take prohibitive or restrictive measures such as ordering the modification of relevant agreements, restricting the number of liner sailings, suspending the tariff or suspending the acceptance of tariff filings, ordering the regular submission of relevant materials, and the like.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　调查机关在作出采取禁止性、限制性措施的决定前，应当告知当事人有要求举行听证的权利；当事人要求听证的，应当举行听证。

**Article 32** Before deciding to take prohibitive or restrictive measures, the investigation authorities shall inform the party of its right to request a hearing; if the party requests a hearing, a hearing shall be held.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　未取得《国际船舶运输经营许可证》，擅自经营国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输业务的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止经营；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得50万元以上的，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。  
从事国际集装箱船、国际普通货船运输业务没有与经营国际海上运输业务相适应的船舶的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 33** Where a person, without obtaining the "Permit for International Ship Transport Operation", engages without authorization in the operation of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport business, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order cessation of the business; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a person engaged in international container ship or international general cargo ship transport business does not have vessels suitable for the operation of international maritime transport business, the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外国国际船舶运输经营者经营中国港口之间的船舶运输业务，或者利用租用的中国籍船舶和舱位以及用互换舱位等方式经营中国港口之间的船舶运输业务的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止经营；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得50万元以上的，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。拒不停止经营的，拒绝进港；情节严重的，撤销其国际班轮运输经营资格。

**Article 34** Where a foreign international ship transport operator operates vessel transport services between Chinese ports, or uses chartered Chinese-flagged vessels and slots, or adopts slot exchange or other means to operate vessel transport services between Chinese ports, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order cessation of the business; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the operator refuses to cease the business, it shall be denied entry into port; if the circumstances are serious, its operational qualification for international liner transport shall be revoked.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　未取得国际班轮运输经营资格，擅自经营国际班轮运输的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令停止经营；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得50万元以上的，处违法所得2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。拒不停止经营的，拒绝进港。

**Article 35** Where a person, without obtaining the operational qualification for international liner transport, engages without authorization in international liner transport, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order cessation of the business; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 500,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than two times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 500,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed. If the person refuses to cease the business, it shall be denied entry into port.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国际客船、国际散装液体危险品船运输经营者将其依法取得的经营资格提供给他人使用的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，撤销其经营资格。

**Article 36** Where an operator of international passenger ship or international bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport provides the operational qualification obtained according to law to any other person for use, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order correction within a specified period; if the operator fails to correct within the specified period, its operational qualification shall be revoked.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　未履行本条例规定的备案手续的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门责令限期补办备案手续；逾期不补办的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并可以撤销其相应资格。

**Article 37** Where a person fails to perform the filing formalities prescribed in these Regulations, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order the person to complete the filing formalities within a specified period; if the person fails to do so within the specified period, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the corresponding qualification may be revoked.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　未履行本条例规定的运价备案手续或者未执行备案运价的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令限期改正，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 38** Where a person fails to perform the tariff filing formalities prescribed in these Regulations or fails to implement the filed tariffs, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order correction within a specified period and shall concurrently impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国际航运交易平台经营者未按照规定报送相关信息的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令限期补报；逾期不补报的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停止开展相关业务。

**Article 39** Where an operator of an international shipping trading platform fails to submit relevant information as required, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order the operator to submit the information within a specified period; if the operator fails to do so within the specified period, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operator shall be ordered to cease carrying out the relevant business.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依据调查结论应当给予行政处罚或者有[本条例第十八条](#art-18)所列违法情形的，由交通运输主管部门、市场监督管理部门依照有关法律、行政法规的规定给予处罚。

**Article 40** Where an administrative penalty shall be imposed based on the investigation conclusion or where there is any illegal circumstance listed in Article 18 of these Regulations, the competent transport department and the market supervision and administration department shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　外国国际船舶运输经营者以及外国国际海运辅助企业常驻代表机构从事经营活动的，由市场监督管理部门责令停止经营活动，并依法给予处罚。

**Article 41** Where a permanent representative office of a foreign international ship transport operator or a foreign international maritime auxiliary enterprise engages in business operations, the market supervision and administration department shall order it to cease business operations and impose penalties according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　拒绝调查机关及其工作人员依法实施调查，或者隐匿、谎报有关情况和资料的，由国务院交通运输主管部门或者其授权的地方人民政府交通运输主管部门责令改正，处2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where a person refuses an investigation lawfully conducted by the investigation authorities or their staff, or conceals or falsely reports relevant information and materials, the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of the local people's government authorized by it shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　非法从事进出中国港口的国际海上运输经营活动以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动，扰乱国际海上运输市场秩序的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where a person illegally engages in the operational activities of international maritime transport entering or leaving Chinese ports and the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport, thereby disrupting the order of the international maritime transport market, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院交通运输主管部门和有关地方人民政府交通运输主管部门的工作人员有下列情形之一，造成严重后果，触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分：  
（一）对符合本条例规定条件的申请者不予审批、许可、登记、备案，或者对不符合本条例规定条件的申请者予以审批、许可、登记、备案的；  
（二）对经过审批、许可、登记、备案的国际船舶运输经营者、无船承运业务经营者不依照本条例的规定实施监督管理，或者发现其不再具备本条例规定的条件而不撤销其相应的经营资格，或者发现其违法行为后不予以查处的；  
（三）对监督检查中发现的未依法履行审批、许可、登记、备案的单位和个人擅自从事国际海上运输经营活动以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动，不立即予以取缔，或者接到举报后不依法予以处理的。

**Article 44** Where a staff member of the competent transport department of the State Council or the competent transport department of a relevant local people's government commits any of the following acts, causing serious consequences, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law:  
(1) failing to examine, approve, register, or file an applicant that meets the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, or examining, approving, registering, or filing an applicant that does not meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations;  
(2) failing to exercise supervision and administration over international ship transport operators or NVOCC operators that have undergone examination, approval, registration, or filing in accordance with these Regulations, or failing to revoke the corresponding operational qualification upon discovering that such operators no longer meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, or failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts upon discovery;  
(3) failing to immediately ban any unit or individual that, without having undergone examination, approval, registration, or filing according to law, engages without authorization in the operational activities of international maritime transport or the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport discovered during supervision and inspection, or failing to deal with the matter according to law upon receiving a report.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的投资者在内地投资经营国际海上运输业务以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性业务，比照适用本条例。

**Article 45** Where investors from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan region invest in and operate international maritime transport businesses and auxiliary businesses related to international maritime transport in the mainland, these Regulations shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　外国国际船舶运输经营者未经国务院交通运输主管部门批准，不得经营中国内地与台湾地区之间的双向直航和经第三地的船舶运输业务。  
外国国际船舶运输经营者未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门批准，不得经营中国内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区之间的客船、散装液体危险品船运输业务。外国国际船舶运输经营者经营中国内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区之间的集装箱船、普通货船运输业务应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府交通运输主管部门备案。

**Article 46** A foreign international ship transport operator shall not, without the approval of the competent transport department of the State Council, operate two-way direct shipping services or shipping services via a third place between the mainland of China and the Taiwan region.  
A foreign international ship transport operator shall not, without the approval of the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, operate passenger ship or bulk liquid dangerous goods ship transport services between the mainland of China and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region. A foreign international ship transport operator that operates container ship or general cargo ship transport services between the mainland of China and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region shall file for record with the competent transport department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　内地与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区之间的海上运输，由国务院交通运输主管部门依照本条例制定管理办法。  
内地与台湾地区之间的海上运输，依照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 47** The maritime transport between the mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region shall be governed by administrative measures formulated by the competent transport department of the State Council in accordance with these Regulations.  
The maritime transport between the mainland and the Taiwan region shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　与中华人民共和国缔结或者共同参加国际海运相关条约、协定的国家或者地区，违反条约、协定的规定，使中华人民共和国根据该条约、协定享有的利益丧失或者受损，或者阻碍条约、协定目标实现的，中华人民共和国政府有权要求有关国家或者地区政府终止上述行为，采取适当的补救措施，并可以根据有关条约、协定中止或者终止履行相关义务。  
任何国家或者地区对中华人民共和国国际海上运输及其辅助性业务的经营者、船舶或者船员采取或者协助、支持采取歧视性的禁止、限制或者其他类似措施的，除有关条约、协定能够提供充分有效的救济外，中华人民共和国政府根据实际情况采取必要的反制措施，包括但不限于向靠泊中国港口的该国家或者地区的船舶收取特别费用，禁止或者限制该国家或者地区的船舶进出中国港口，禁止或者限制该国家或者地区的组织和个人获取中国国际海上运输相关数据、信息以及经营进出中国港口的国际海上运输及其辅助性业务。

**Article 48** Where a country or region that has concluded or jointly acceded to a treaty or agreement relating to international maritime transport with the People's Republic of China violates the provisions of such treaty or agreement, thereby causing the loss or impairment of the interests enjoyed by the People's Republic of China under such treaty or agreement, or obstructing the realization of the objectives of such treaty or agreement, the Government of the People's Republic of China shall have the right to demand that the government of the relevant country or region terminate the aforesaid acts, take appropriate remedial measures, and may, in accordance with the relevant treaty or agreement, suspend or terminate the performance of relevant obligations.  
Where any country or region takes, or assists or supports the taking of, discriminatory prohibitive, restrictive, or other similar measures against the operators, vessels, or crew of the international maritime transport and its auxiliary businesses of the People's Republic of China, unless the relevant treaty or agreement provides sufficient and effective remedies, the Government of the People's Republic of China shall, based on the actual circumstances, take necessary countermeasures, including but not limited to imposing special fees on vessels of that country or region that call at Chinese ports, prohibiting or restricting the entry or exit of vessels of that country or region from Chinese ports, and prohibiting or restricting organizations and individuals of that country or region from obtaining data and information related to China's international maritime transport and from operating international maritime transport and its auxiliary businesses entering or leaving Chinese ports.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例施行前已从事国际海上运输经营活动以及与国际海上运输相关的辅助性经营活动的，应当在本条例施行之日起60日内按照本条例的规定补办有关手续。

**Article 49** Any person that has already engaged in the operational activities of international maritime transport or the auxiliary operational activities related to international maritime transport before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within 60 days from the date on which these Regulations come into force, complete the relevant formalities in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例自2002年1月1日起施行。1990年12月5日国务院发布、1998年4月18日国务院修订发布的《中华人民共和国海上国际集装箱运输管理规定》同时废止。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2002. The "Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Maritime International Container Transport" promulgated by the State Council on December 5, 1990 and revised and promulgated by the State Council on April 18, 1998 shall be abolished simultaneously.
