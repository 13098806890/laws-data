# 快递暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-04-13  
**生效日期**：2025-06-01

> （2018年3月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第697号公布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2025年4月13日《国务院关于修改〈快递暂行条例〉的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为促进快递业健康发展，保障快递安全，保护快递用户合法权益，加强对快递业的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国邮政法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事快递业务经营、接受快递服务以及对快递业实施监督管理，适用本条例。 第三条　快递业发展应当遵循市场主导、保障安全、创新驱动、协同发展的原则，构建普惠城乡、技术先进、服务优质、安全高效、绿色节能的快递服务体系。 第四条　地方各级人民政府应当创造良好的快递业营商环境，支持经营快递业务的企业创新商业模式和服务方式，引导经营快递业务的企业加强服务质量管理、健全规章制度、完善安全保障措施，为用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便的快递服务。 地方各级人民政府应当确保政府相关行为符合公平竞争要求和相关法律法规，维护快递业竞争秩序，不得出台违反公平竞争、可能造成地区封锁和行业垄断的政策措施。 第五条　任何单位或者个人不得利用信件、包裹、印刷品以及其他寄递物品（以下统称快件）从事危害国家安全、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的活动。 除有关部门依照法律对快件进行检查外，任何单位或者个人不得非法检查他人快件。任何单位或者个人不得私自开拆、隐匿、毁弃、倒卖他人快件。 第六条　国务院邮政管理部门负责对全国快递业实施监督管理。国务院公安、国家安全、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关的快递监督管理工作。 省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构和按照国务院规定设立的省级以下邮政管理机构负责对本辖区的快递业实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关的快递监督管理工作。 第七条　国务院邮政管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构以及省级以下邮政管理机构（以下统称邮政管理部门）应当与公安、国家安全、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门相互配合，建立健全快递安全监管机制，加强对快递业安全运行的监测预警，收集、共享与快递业安全运行有关的信息，依法处理影响快递业安全运行的事件。 第八条　依法成立的快递行业组织应当保护企业合法权益，加强行业自律，促进企业守法、诚信、安全经营，督促企业落实安全生产主体责任，引导企业不断提高快递服务质量和水平。 第九条　国家加强快递业诚信体系建设，建立健全快递业信用记录、信息公开、信用评价制度，依法实施联合惩戒措施，提高快递业信用水平。 第十条　国家完善综合性支持政策，推进快递包装绿色化、减量化、可循环。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为促进快递业健康发展，保障快递安全，保护快递用户合法权益，加强对快递业的监督管理，根据《中华人民共和国邮政法》和其他有关法律，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws, for the purposes of promoting the healthy development of the express delivery industry, ensuring the safety of express delivery, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of express delivery users, and strengthening the supervision and administration of the express delivery industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事快递业务经营、接受快递服务以及对快递业实施监督管理，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the operation of express delivery business, the receipt of express delivery services, and the supervision and administration of the express delivery industry within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　快递业发展应当遵循市场主导、保障安全、创新驱动、协同发展的原则，构建普惠城乡、技术先进、服务优质、安全高效、绿色节能的快递服务体系。

**Article 3** The development of the express delivery industry shall follow the principles of market orientation, safety assurance, innovation-driven growth, and coordinated development, and shall establish an express delivery service system that benefits urban and rural areas, features advanced technology, high-quality service, safety, efficiency, greenness and energy conservation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民政府应当创造良好的快递业营商环境，支持经营快递业务的企业创新商业模式和服务方式，引导经营快递业务的企业加强服务质量管理、健全规章制度、完善安全保障措施，为用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便的快递服务。  
地方各级人民政府应当确保政府相关行为符合公平竞争要求和相关法律法规，维护快递业竞争秩序，不得出台违反公平竞争、可能造成地区封锁和行业垄断的政策措施。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at all levels shall create a favorable business environment for the express delivery industry, support enterprises engaged in express delivery business to innovate business models and service methods, and guide such enterprises to strengthen service quality management, improve rules and regulations, and enhance safety assurance measures, so as to provide users with prompt, accurate, safe, and convenient express delivery services.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall ensure that relevant government actions comply with the requirements of fair competition and relevant laws and regulations, maintain the competition order of the express delivery industry, and shall not introduce policies or measures that violate fair competition or may cause regional blockades and industry monopolies.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位或者个人不得利用信件、包裹、印刷品以及其他寄递物品（以下统称快件）从事危害国家安全、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的活动。  
除有关部门依照法律对快件进行检查外，任何单位或者个人不得非法检查他人快件。任何单位或者个人不得私自开拆、隐匿、毁弃、倒卖他人快件。

**Article 5** No unit or individual may use letters, parcels, printed matter, or other postal items (hereinafter collectively referred to as express items) to engage in activities that endanger national security, public interests, or the legitimate rights and interests of others.  
Except where relevant authorities inspect express items in accordance with law, no unit or individual may illegally inspect the express items of others. No unit or individual may privately open, conceal, destroy, discard, or resell the express items of others.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院邮政管理部门负责对全国快递业实施监督管理。国务院公安、国家安全、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关的快递监督管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构和按照国务院规定设立的省级以下邮政管理机构负责对本辖区的快递业实施监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府有关部门在各自职责范围内负责相关的快递监督管理工作。

**Article 6** The postal administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the express delivery industry nationwide. The relevant departments under the State Council, such as the public security, national security, customs, and market supervision and administration departments, shall be responsible for the relevant express delivery supervision and administration work within their respective functions and duties.  
The postal administration bodies of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the postal administration bodies below the provincial level established according to the provisions of the State Council, shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the express delivery industry within their respective jurisdictions. The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the relevant express delivery supervision and administration work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院邮政管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市邮政管理机构以及省级以下邮政管理机构（以下统称邮政管理部门）应当与公安、国家安全、海关、市场监督管理等有关部门相互配合，建立健全快递安全监管机制，加强对快递业安全运行的监测预警，收集、共享与快递业安全运行有关的信息，依法处理影响快递业安全运行的事件。

**Article 7** The postal administration department under the State Council, the postal administration bodies of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the postal administration bodies below the provincial level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the postal administration departments) shall cooperate with relevant departments such as public security, national security, customs, and market supervision and administration, establish and improve the express delivery safety supervision mechanism, strengthen the monitoring and early warning for the safe operation of the express delivery industry, collect and share information related to the safe operation of the express delivery industry, and handle incidents affecting the safe operation of the express delivery industry in accordance with law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　依法成立的快递行业组织应当保护企业合法权益，加强行业自律，促进企业守法、诚信、安全经营，督促企业落实安全生产主体责任，引导企业不断提高快递服务质量和水平。

**Article 8** The express delivery industry organizations established according to law shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of enterprises, strengthen industry self-discipline, promote enterprises to operate in compliance with laws, with integrity and safety, urge enterprises to fulfill their primary responsibility for safety production, and guide enterprises to continuously improve the quality and level of express delivery services.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家加强快递业诚信体系建设，建立健全快递业信用记录、信息公开、信用评价制度，依法实施联合惩戒措施，提高快递业信用水平。

**Article 9** The State shall strengthen the construction of the credit system for the express delivery industry, establish and improve the credit recording, information disclosure, and credit evaluation systems for the express delivery industry, implement joint punishment measures in accordance with law, and improve the credit level of the express delivery industry.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家完善综合性支持政策，推进快递包装绿色化、减量化、可循环。  
国家鼓励经营快递业务的企业和寄件人使用可降解、可重复利用的环保包装材料。

**Article 10** The State shall improve comprehensive support policies to promote the greening, reduction, and recyclability of express delivery packaging.  
The State encourages enterprises engaged in express delivery business and senders to use biodegradable and reusable environmentally friendly packaging materials.

### 第二章　发展保障

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院邮政管理部门应当制定快递业发展规划，促进快递业健康发展。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将快递业发展纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，在国土空间规划中统筹考虑快件大型集散、分拣等基础设施用地的需要。  
县级以上地方人民政府建立健全促进快递业健康发展的政策措施，完善相关配套规定，依法保障经营快递业务的企业及其从业人员的合法权益。

**Article 11** The postal administrative department of the State Council shall formulate the development plan for the express delivery industry to promote the healthy development of the express delivery industry.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the development of the express delivery industry into their respective national economic and social development plans, and comprehensively consider the need for land for large-scale distribution, sorting and other infrastructure of express items in territorial space plans.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve policy measures to promote the healthy development of the express delivery industry, improve relevant supporting regulations, and protect the lawful rights and interests of enterprises engaged in express delivery business and their employees according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持和鼓励经营快递业务的企业在农村、偏远地区发展快递服务网络，完善快递末端网点布局。

**Article 12** The State supports and encourages enterprises engaged in express delivery business to develop express service networks in rural and remote areas, and to improve the layout of express terminal service outlets.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励和引导经营快递业务的企业采用先进技术，促进自动化分拣设备、机械化装卸设备、智能末端服务设施、快递电子运单以及快件信息化管理系统等的推广应用。

**Article 13** The State encourages and guides enterprises engaged in express delivery business to adopt advanced technology, and to promote the application and popularization of automated sorting equipment, mechanized loading and unloading equipment, intelligent terminal service facilities, express electronic waybills, and express information management systems.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上地方人民政府公安、交通运输等部门和邮政管理部门应当加强协调配合，建立健全快递运输保障机制，依法保障快递服务车辆通行和临时停靠的权利，不得禁止快递服务车辆依法通行。  
邮政管理部门会同县级以上地方人民政府公安等部门，依法规范快递服务车辆的管理和使用，对快递专用电动三轮车的行驶时速、装载质量等作出规定，并对快递服务车辆加强统一编号和标识管理。经营快递业务的企业应当对其从业人员加强道路交通安全培训。  
快递从业人员应当遵守道路交通安全法律法规的规定，按照操作规范安全、文明驾驶车辆。快递从业人员因执行工作任务造成他人损害的，由快递从业人员所属的经营快递业务的企业依照民事侵权责任相关法律的规定承担侵权责任。

**Article 14** The public security, transportation and other departments of local people's governments at or above the county level and the postal administrative department shall strengthen coordination and cooperation, establish and improve an express transportation guarantee mechanism, protect the right of express service vehicles to pass and temporarily park according to law, and shall not prohibit express service vehicles from passing according to law.  
The postal administrative department, in conjunction with the public security and other departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, shall regulate the management and use of express service vehicles according to law, make provisions on the driving speed, loading quality, etc. of special electric tricycles for express delivery, and strengthen unified numbering and marking management of express service vehicles. Enterprises engaged in express delivery business shall strengthen road traffic safety training for their employees.  
Express delivery practitioners shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on road traffic safety, and drive safely and civilly in accordance with operating norms. If express delivery practitioners cause damage to others due to their performance of work tasks, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business to which the practitioner belongs shall bear tort liability in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws on civil tort liability.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　企业事业单位、住宅小区管理单位应当根据实际情况，采取与经营快递业务的企业签订合同、设置快件收寄投递专门场所等方式，为开展快递服务提供必要的便利。鼓励多个经营快递业务的企业共享末端服务设施，为用户提供便捷的快递末端服务。

**Article 15** Enterprises, institutions, and residential community management entities shall, based on actual circumstances, adopt methods such as signing contracts with enterprises engaged in express delivery business and setting up special places for collection and delivery of express items, to provide necessary convenience for the development of express services. Multiple enterprises engaged in express delivery business are encouraged to share terminal service facilities to provide users with convenient express terminal services.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励快递业与制造业、农业、商贸业等行业建立协同发展机制，推动快递业与电子商务融合发展，加强信息沟通，共享设施和网络资源。  
国家引导和推动快递业与铁路、公路、水路、民航等行业的标准对接，支持在大型车站、码头、机场等交通枢纽配套建设快件运输通道和接驳场所。

**Article 16** The State encourages the express delivery industry to establish a coordinated development mechanism with manufacturing, agriculture, commerce and trade and other industries, promote the integrated development of the express delivery industry and e-commerce, strengthen information communication, and share facilities and network resources.  
The State guides and promotes the alignment of standards between the express delivery industry and railways, highways, waterways, civil aviation and other industries, and supports the construction of express transportation channels and transfer facilities in large stations, docks, airports and other transport hubs.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励经营快递业务的企业依法开展进出境快递业务，支持在重点口岸建设进出境快件处理中心、在境外依法开办快递服务机构并设置快件处理场所。  
海关、邮政管理等部门应当建立协作机制，完善进出境快件管理，推动实现快件便捷通关。

**Article 17** The State encourages enterprises engaged in express delivery business to carry out inbound and outbound express delivery business according to law, and supports the construction of inbound and outbound express processing centers at key ports, the establishment of express service institutions abroad and the setting up of express processing sites abroad according to law.  
The customs, postal administrative and other departments shall establish a cooperation mechanism to improve the management of inbound and outbound express items, and promote the realization of convenient customs clearance for express items.

### 第三章　经营主体

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经营快递业务，应当依法取得快递业务经营许可。邮政管理部门应当根据[《中华人民共和国邮政法》第五十二条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-52)、第五十三条规定的条件和程序核定经营许可的业务范围和地域范围，向社会公布取得快递业务经营许可的企业名单，并及时更新。

**Article 18** To engage in express delivery business, one shall obtain an express delivery business operation permit according to law. The postal administrative department shall verify the business scope and geographical scope of the operation permit in accordance with the conditions and procedures stipulated in Articles 52 and 53 of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China, publish the list of enterprises that have obtained express delivery business operation permits to the public, and update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　经营快递业务的企业及其分支机构可以根据业务需要开办快递末端网点，并应当自开办之日起20日内向所在地邮政管理部门备案。快递末端网点无需办理营业执照。

**Article 19** Enterprises engaged in express delivery business and their branches may, based on business needs, establish express terminal service outlets, and shall file with the local postal administrative department within 20 days from the date of establishment. Express terminal service outlets are not required to obtain a business license.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　两个以上经营快递业务的企业可以使用统一的商标、字号或者快递运单经营快递业务。  
前款规定的经营快递业务的企业应当签订书面协议明确各自的权利义务，遵守共同的服务约定，在服务质量、安全保障、业务流程等方面实行统一管理，为用户提供统一的快件跟踪查询和投诉处理服务。  
用户的合法权益因快件延误、丢失、损毁或者内件短少而受到损害的，用户可以要求该商标、字号或者快递运单所属企业赔偿，也可以要求实际提供快递服务的企业赔偿。

**Article 20** Two or more enterprises engaged in express delivery business may use uniform trademarks, trade names or express waybills to operate express delivery business.  
The enterprises engaged in express delivery business as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall enter into a written agreement specifying their respective rights and obligations, abide by common service agreements, exercise unified management in aspects such as service quality, safety guarantee, and business processes, and provide users with unified express tracking, inquiry and complaint handling services.  
If the lawful rights and interests of a user are damaged due to delay, loss, damage or shortage of contents of an express item, the user may claim compensation from the enterprise to which the trademark, trade name or express waybill belongs, and may also claim compensation from the enterprise that actually provides the express service.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经营快递业务的企业应当依法保护其从业人员的合法权益。  
经营快递业务的企业应当对其从业人员加强职业操守、服务规范、作业规范、安全生产、车辆安全驾驶等方面的教育和培训。

**Article 21** An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of its employees according to law.  
An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall strengthen education and training for its employees in aspects such as professional ethics, service standards, operational norms, work safety, and safe driving of vehicles.

### 第四章　快递服务

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经营快递业务的企业在寄件人填写快递运单前，应当提醒其阅读快递服务合同条款、遵守禁止寄递和限制寄递物品的有关规定，告知相关保价规则和保险服务项目。  
寄件人交寄贵重物品的，应当事先声明；经营快递业务的企业可以要求寄件人对贵重物品予以保价。

**Article 22** An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall, before the sender fills in the express waybill, remind the sender to read the terms of the express service contract, comply with the relevant provisions on prohibited and restricted items for delivery, and inform the sender of the relevant valuation rules and insurance service items.  
Where a sender delivers valuable items, the sender shall declare in advance; an enterprise engaged in express delivery business may require the sender to insure the valuable items.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　寄件人交寄快件，应当如实提供以下事项：  
（一）寄件人姓名、地址、联系电话；  
（二）收件人姓名（名称）、地址、联系电话；  
（三）寄递物品的名称、性质、数量。  
除信件和已签订安全协议用户交寄的快件外，经营快递业务的企业收寄快件，应当对寄件人身份进行查验，并登记身份信息，但不得在快递运单上记录除姓名（名称）、地址、联系电话以外的用户身份信息。寄件人拒绝提供身份信息或者提供身份信息不实的，经营快递业务的企业不得收寄。

**Article 23** A sender delivering an express item shall truthfully provide the following information:  
(1) the name, address, and contact telephone number of the sender;  
(2) the name (title), address, and contact telephone number of the consignee;  
(3) the name, nature, and quantity of the delivered item.  
Except for letters and express items delivered by users who have signed a security agreement, an enterprise engaged in express delivery business, when accepting an express item, shall verify the identity of the sender and register the identity information, but shall not record on the express waybill any user identity information other than the name (title), address, and contact telephone number. If the sender refuses to provide identity information or provides false identity information, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall not accept the express item.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励经营快递业务的企业在节假日期间根据业务量变化实际情况，为用户提供正常的快递服务。

**Article 24** The State encourages enterprises engaged in express delivery business to provide normal express services for users during holidays based on actual changes in business volume.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经营快递业务的企业应当规范操作，防止造成快件损毁。  
法律法规对食品、药品等特定物品的运输有特殊规定的，寄件人、经营快递业务的企业应当遵守相关规定。

**Article 25** An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall standardize operations to prevent damage to express items.  
Where laws and regulations have special provisions on the transportation of specific items such as food and pharmaceuticals, the sender and the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall comply with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经营快递业务的企业应当将快件投递到约定的收件地址、收件人或者收件人指定的代收人，并告知收件人或者代收人当面验收。收件人或者代收人有权当面验收。

**Article 26** An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall deliver the express item to the agreed consignee address, the consignee, or a person designated by the consignee to receive it, and shall notify the consignee or the designated person to inspect it in person. The consignee or the designated person shall have the right to inspect the item in person.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　快件无法投递的，经营快递业务的企业应当退回寄件人或者根据寄件人的要求进行处理；属于进出境快件的，经营快递业务的企业应当依法办理海关和检验检疫手续。  
快件无法投递又无法退回的，依照下列规定处理：  
（一）属于信件，自确认无法退回之日起超过6个月无人认领的，由经营快递业务的企业在所在地邮政管理部门的监督下销毁；  
（二）属于信件以外其他快件的，经营快递业务的企业应当登记，并按照国务院邮政管理部门的规定处理；  
（三）属于进境快件的，交由海关依法处理。

**Article 27** If an express item cannot be delivered, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall return it to the sender or handle it according to the sender's request; if the express item is an inbound or outbound express item, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall go through the customs and inspection and quarantine procedures according to law.  
If an express item cannot be delivered nor returned, it shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) If it is a letter and remains unclaimed for more than six months from the date it is confirmed that it cannot be returned, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall destroy it under the supervision of the local postal administrative department;  
(2) If it is an express item other than a letter, the enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall register it and handle it in accordance with the provisions of the postal administrative department of the State Council;  
(3) If it is an inbound express item, it shall be handed over to the customs for handling according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　快件延误、丢失、损毁或者内件短少的，对保价的快件，应当按照经营快递业务的企业与寄件人约定的保价规则确定赔偿责任；对未保价的快件，依照民事法律的有关规定确定赔偿责任。  
国家鼓励保险公司开发快件损失赔偿责任险种，鼓励经营快递业务的企业投保。

**Article 28** Where an express item is delayed, lost, damaged, or has missing contents, for a valued express item, the compensation liability shall be determined in accordance with the valuation rules agreed upon between the enterprise engaged in express delivery business and the sender; for an unvalued express item, the compensation liability shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of civil law.  
The State encourages insurance companies to develop insurance types for loss and compensation liability of express items, and encourages enterprises engaged in express delivery business to take out insurance.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　经营快递业务的企业应当实行快件寄递全程信息化管理，公布联系方式，保证与用户的联络畅通，向用户提供业务咨询、快件查询等服务。用户对快递服务质量不满意的，可以向经营快递业务的企业投诉，经营快递业务的企业应当自接到投诉之日起7日内予以处理并告知用户。

**Article 29** An enterprise engaged in express delivery business shall implement information-based management throughout the entire process of express delivery, publish contact information, ensure smooth communication with users, and provide users with services such as business consultation and express item tracking. If a user is dissatisfied with the quality of express services, the user may file a complaint with the enterprise engaged in express delivery business, and the enterprise shall handle the complaint within seven days from the date of receiving it and notify the user.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　经营快递业务的企业停止经营的，应当提前10日向社会公告，书面告知邮政管理部门，交回快递业务经营许可证，并依法妥善处理尚未投递的快件。  
经营快递业务的企业或者其分支机构因不可抗力或者其他特殊原因暂停快递服务的，应当及时向邮政管理部门报告，向社会公告暂停服务的原因和期限，并依法妥善处理尚未投递的快件。

**Article 30** If an enterprise engaged in express delivery business ceases operation, it shall make a public announcement 10 days in advance, notify the postal administrative department in writing, return the express delivery business permit, and properly handle the undelivered express items according to law.  
If an enterprise engaged in express delivery business or its branch suspends express services due to force majeure or other special reasons, it shall promptly report to the postal administrative department, publicly announce the reason and duration of the suspension, and properly handle the undelivered express items according to law.

### 第五章　快递安全

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　寄件人交寄快件和经营快递业务的企业收寄快件应当遵守[《中华人民共和国邮政法》第二十四条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-24)关于禁止寄递或者限制寄递物品的规定。  
禁止寄递物品的目录及管理办法，由国务院邮政管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 31** The sender consigning express items and the enterprise operating express business receiving express items shall comply with the provisions of Article 24 of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China concerning articles prohibited or restricted for posting.  
The catalogue and management measures for prohibited articles shall be formulated and published by the postal administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经营快递业务的企业收寄快件，应当依照《中华人民共和国邮政法》的规定验视内件，并作出验视标识。寄件人拒绝验视的，经营快递业务的企业不得收寄。  
经营快递业务的企业受寄件人委托，长期、批量提供快递服务的，应当与寄件人签订安全协议，明确双方的安全保障义务。

**Article 32** An enterprise operating express business, when receiving express items, shall inspect the contents in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China and affix an inspection mark. If the sender refuses inspection, the enterprise operating express business shall not accept the item.  
Where an enterprise operating express business, entrusted by the sender, provides express services on a long-term and bulk basis, it shall enter into a safety agreement with the sender, specifying the safety assurance obligations of both parties.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　经营快递业务的企业可以自行或者委托第三方企业对快件进行安全检查，并对经过安全检查的快件作出安全检查标识。经营快递业务的企业委托第三方企业对快件进行安全检查的，不免除委托方对快件安全承担的责任。  
经营快递业务的企业或者接受委托的第三方企业应当使用符合强制性国家标准的安全检查设备，并加强对安全检查人员的背景审查和技术培训；经营快递业务的企业或者接受委托的第三方企业对安全检查人员进行背景审查，公安机关等相关部门应当予以配合。

**Article 33** An enterprise operating express business may conduct security checks on express items by itself or entrust a third-party enterprise to do so, and shall affix a security check mark on express items that have undergone security checks. Where an enterprise operating express business entrusts a third-party enterprise to conduct security checks on express items, the entrusting party shall not be exempted from the liability for the security of the express items.  
The enterprise operating express business or the entrusted third-party enterprise shall use security check equipment that meets the mandatory national standards, and shall strengthen the background examination and technical training of security check personnel; where the enterprise operating express business or the entrusted third-party enterprise conducts background examination of security check personnel, the public security organ and other relevant departments shall provide cooperation.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　经营快递业务的企业发现寄件人交寄禁止寄递物品的，应当拒绝收寄；发现已经收寄的快件中有疑似禁止寄递物品的，应当立即停止分拣、运输、投递。对快件中依法应当没收、销毁或者可能涉及违法犯罪的物品，经营快递业务的企业应当立即向有关部门报告并配合调查处理；对其他禁止寄递物品以及限制寄递物品，经营快递业务的企业应当按照法律、行政法规或者国务院和国务院有关主管部门的规定处理。

**Article 34** Where an enterprise operating express business discovers that the sender has consigned articles prohibited for posting, it shall refuse to accept them; where it discovers that there are suspected prohibited articles in express items already accepted, it shall immediately stop sorting, transporting, and delivering them. For articles in express items that shall be confiscated or destroyed according to law, or articles that may involve illegality or crime, the enterprise operating express business shall immediately report to the relevant departments and cooperate with the investigation and handling; for other prohibited articles and restricted articles, the enterprise operating express business shall handle them in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, or the State Council and the competent departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　经营快递业务的企业应当建立快递运单及电子数据管理制度，妥善保管用户信息等电子数据，定期销毁快递运单，采取有效技术手段保证用户信息安全。具体办法由国务院邮政管理部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
经营快递业务的企业及其从业人员不得出售、泄露或者非法提供快递服务过程中知悉的用户信息。发生或者可能发生用户信息泄露的，经营快递业务的企业应当立即采取补救措施，并向所在地邮政管理部门报告。

**Article 35** An enterprise operating express business shall establish a management system for express waybills and electronic data, properly keep electronic data such as user information, periodically destroy express waybills, and adopt effective technical measures to ensure the security of user information. Specific measures shall be formulated by the postal administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
An enterprise operating express business and its employees shall not sell, disclose, or illegally provide user information learned during the express service process. Where a leakage of user information occurs or may occur, the enterprise operating express business shall immediately take remedial measures and report to the local postal administrative department.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经营快递业务的企业应当依法建立健全安全生产责任制，确保快递服务安全。  
经营快递业务的企业应当依法制定突发事件应急预案，定期开展突发事件应急演练；发生突发事件的，应当按照应急预案及时、妥善处理，并立即向所在地邮政管理部门报告。

**Article 36** An enterprise operating express business shall, according to law, establish and improve a production safety responsibility system to ensure the safety of express services.  
An enterprise operating express business shall, according to law, formulate emergency response plans for emergencies and regularly conduct emergency drills; where an emergency occurs, it shall handle it promptly and properly in accordance with the emergency plan, and immediately report to the local postal administrative department.

### 第六章　快递包装

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　快递包装应当符合寄递生产作业的要求，节约使用资源，避免过度包装，防止污染环境。  
国务院标准化行政主管部门和国务院邮政管理等部门按照职责分工组织制定快递包装的国家标准、行业标准。快递包装应当符合强制性国家标准。

**Article 37** Express packaging shall meet the requirements of production operations for posting, save resources, avoid excessive packaging, and prevent environmental pollution.  
The standardization administrative department of the State Council and the postal administration department of the State Council, among others, shall, according to their respective functions, organize the formulation of national standards and industry standards for express packaging. Express packaging shall comply with mandatory national standards.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家鼓励科技创新，支持采用新技术、新材料、新工艺研发、生产符合绿色环保要求的快递包装。

**Article 38** The State encourages scientific and technological innovation, and supports the adoption of new technologies, new materials, and new processes to research, develop, and produce express packaging that meets green environmental protection requirements.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　经营快递业务的企业应当在保障快递安全的前提下，优化快递包装方式和包装结构设计，节约使用包装物。  
鼓励经营快递业务的企业使用通过绿色产品认证的包装物。

**Article 39** An enterprise operating express business shall, on the premise of ensuring express safety, optimize express packaging methods and packaging structure design, and save the use of packaging materials.  
Enterprises operating express business are encouraged to use packaging materials that have passed green product certification.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家推动经营快递业务的企业与商品生产企业、电子商务企业协同发展，推广商品原装直发，减少寄递环节的二次包装。

**Article 40** The State promotes the coordinated development of enterprises operating express business with commodity production enterprises and e-commerce enterprises, promotes the direct shipment of goods in original packaging, and reduces secondary packaging during the posting process.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　经营快递业务的企业应当制定并实施快递包装操作规范，加强对其从业人员快递包装操作技能的培训。

**Article 41** An enterprise operating express delivery business shall formulate and implement operational specifications for express packaging, and strengthen the training of its employees in express packaging operation skills.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　经营快递业务的企业应当制定并实施包装物回收利用管理制度，优化业务流程，提高包装物的回收利用率。  
鼓励在快递经营场所和企业事业单位、住宅小区等其他适当场所设置包装物回收设施设备。

**Article 42** An enterprise operating express delivery business shall formulate and implement a management system for the recycling and reuse of packaging materials, optimize business processes, and increase the recycling rate of packaging materials.  
Encouragement is given to set up packaging material recycling facilities and equipment at express delivery business premises and other appropriate places such as enterprises, public institutions, and residential communities.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　经营快递业务的企业应当按照国家有关规定向邮政管理部门报告包装物中一次性塑料制品的使用、回收情况。

**Article 43** An enterprise operating express delivery business shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, report to the postal administrative department the use and recycling of disposable plastic products in packaging materials.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当组织开展多种形式的宣传教育活动，新闻媒体应当开展公益宣传，提高公众的环保包装意识。  
鼓励经营快递业务的企业通过积分奖励、寄件优惠等方式引导用户重复使用包装物。

**Article 44** The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall organize and carry out various forms of publicity and education activities, and the news media shall carry out public welfare publicity to raise the public's awareness of environmentally friendly packaging.  
Encouragement is given to enterprises operating express delivery business to guide users to reuse packaging materials through measures such as point rewards and shipping discounts.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　依法成立的快递行业组织应当将经营快递业务的企业使用、回收包装物等情况纳入行业自律范围，并及时向社会公布有关情况。

**Article 45** A lawfully established express delivery industry organization shall include the use and recycling of packaging materials by enterprises operating express delivery business within the scope of industry self-discipline, and shall promptly make relevant information public.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　邮政管理部门应当加强对快递业的监督检查。监督检查应当以下列事项为重点：  
（一）从事快递活动的企业是否依法取得快递业务经营许可；  
（二）经营快递业务的企业的安全管理制度是否健全并有效实施；  
（三）经营快递业务的企业是否妥善处理用户的投诉、保护用户合法权益；  
（四）经营快递业务的企业是否落实快递包装有关管理制度和强制性国家标准。

**Article 46** The postal administrative department shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of the express delivery industry. Such supervision and inspection shall focus on the following matters:  
(1) whether an enterprise engaged in express delivery activities has lawfully obtained a permit for express delivery business operation;  
(2) whether the safety management system of an enterprise operating express delivery business is sound and effectively implemented;  
(3) whether an enterprise operating express delivery business properly handles user complaints and protects users' lawful rights and interests;  
(4) whether an enterprise operating express delivery business implements relevant management systems and mandatory national standards for express packaging.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　邮政管理部门应当建立和完善以随机抽查为重点的日常监督检查制度，公布抽查事项目录，明确抽查的依据、频次、方式、内容和程序，随机抽取被检查企业，随机选派检查人员。抽查情况和查处结果应当及时向社会公布。  
邮政管理部门应当充分利用计算机网络等先进技术手段，加强对快递业务活动的日常监督检查，提高快递业管理水平。

**Article 47** The postal administrative department shall establish and improve a daily supervision and inspection system focusing on random inspections, publish a catalogue of inspection items, specify the basis, frequency, method, content and procedure of inspections, randomly select enterprises to be inspected, and randomly assign inspectors. The results of inspections and the handling of violations shall be promptly made public.  
The postal administrative department shall make full use of advanced technical means such as computer networks to strengthen the daily supervision and inspection of express delivery business activities and improve the management level of the express delivery industry.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　邮政管理部门依法履行职责，有权采取[《中华人民共和国邮政法》第六十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-61)规定的监督检查措施。邮政管理部门实施现场检查，有权查阅经营快递业务的企业管理快递业务的电子数据。  
国家安全机关、公安机关为维护国家安全和侦查犯罪活动的需要依法开展执法活动，经营快递业务的企业应当提供技术支持和协助。  
[《中华人民共和国邮政法》第十一条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-11)规定的处理场所，包括快件处理场地、设施、设备。

**Article 48** When performing its duties according to law, the postal administrative department has the power to adopt the supervision and inspection measures provided for in Article 61 of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China. When carrying out on-site inspections, the postal administrative department has the power to inspect the electronic data of enterprises operating express delivery business in managing express delivery business.  
State security organs and public security organs shall carry out law enforcement activities according to law for the needs of maintaining national security and investigating criminal activities, and enterprises operating express delivery business shall provide technical support and assistance.  
The processing premises specified in Article 11 of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China include the express handling sites, facilities and equipment.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　邮政管理部门应当向社会公布本部门的联系方式，方便公众举报违法行为。  
邮政管理部门接到举报的，应当及时依法调查处理，并为举报人保密。对实名举报的，邮政管理部门应当将处理结果告知举报人。

**Article 49** The postal administrative department shall make public its contact information to facilitate the public to report illegal acts.  
Upon receiving a report, the postal administrative department shall promptly investigate and handle it according to law and keep the reporter's identity confidential. For real-name reports, the postal administrative department shall inform the reporter of the handling results.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　未取得快递业务经营许可从事快递活动的，由邮政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国邮政法》的规定予以处罚。  
经营快递业务的企业或者其分支机构有下列行为之一的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿：  
（一）开办快递末端网点未向所在地邮政管理部门备案；  
（二）停止经营快递业务，未提前10日向社会公告，未书面告知邮政管理部门并交回快递业务经营许可证，或者未依法妥善处理尚未投递的快件；  
（三）因不可抗力或者其他特殊原因暂停快递服务，未及时向邮政管理部门报告并向社会公告暂停服务的原因和期限，或者未依法妥善处理尚未投递的快件。

**Article 50** Where an enterprise engages in express delivery activities without obtaining a permit for express delivery business operation, the postal administrative department shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where an enterprise operating express delivery business or its branch commits any of the following acts, the postal administrative department shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification:  
(1) establishing an express delivery terminal outlet without filing with the local postal administrative department;  
(2) ceasing the operation of express delivery business without making a public announcement 10 days in advance, without notifying the postal administrative department in writing and returning the express delivery business operation permit, or without properly handling undelivered express items according to law;  
(3) suspending express service due to force majeure or other special reasons without promptly reporting to the postal administrative department and making a public announcement of the reason and duration of the suspension, or without properly handling undelivered express items according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　两个以上经营快递业务的企业使用统一的商标、字号或者快递运单经营快递业务，未遵守共同的服务约定，在服务质量、安全保障、业务流程等方面未实行统一管理，或者未向用户提供统一的快件跟踪查询和投诉处理服务的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿。

**Article 51** Where two or more enterprises operating express delivery business use a unified trademark, trade name or express waybill to operate express delivery business, fail to comply with the common service agreement, fail to implement unified management in terms of service quality, safety guarantee, business process, etc., or fail to provide users with unified tracking and inquiry of express items and complaint handling services, the postal管理部门 shall order them to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and they may be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　冒领、私自开拆、隐匿、毁弃、倒卖或者非法检查他人快件，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
经营快递业务的企业有前款规定行为，或者非法扣留快件的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处10万元以上20万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证。

**Article 52** Where a person fraudulently claims, opens without authorization, conceals, damages, sells or illegally inspects another person's express items, which does not constitute a crime, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.  
If an enterprise operating express delivery business commits any of the acts prescribed in the preceding paragraph, or illegally detains express items, the postal管理部门 shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed, and it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its express delivery business permit revoked.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　经营快递业务的企业有下列情形之一的，由邮政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国邮政法》、《中华人民共和国反恐怖主义法》的规定予以处罚：  
（一）不建立或者不执行收寄验视制度；  
（二）违反法律、行政法规以及国务院和国务院有关部门关于禁止寄递或者限制寄递物品的规定；  
（三）收寄快件未查验寄件人身份并登记身份信息，或者发现寄件人提供身份信息不实仍予收寄；  
（四）未按照规定对快件进行安全检查。  
寄件人在快件中夹带禁止寄递的物品，尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 53** Where an enterprise operating express delivery business falls under any of the following circumstances, the postal管理部门 shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China and the Counter-Terrorism Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Failing to establish or implement the system of inspection before acceptance;  
(2) Violating laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council and relevant departments of the State Council on prohibited or restricted articles for delivery;  
(3) When accepting express items, failing to verify the identity of the sender and register the identity information, or accepting the items despite discovering that the sender has provided false identity information;  
(4) Failing to conduct security checks on express items in accordance with regulations.  
If a sender inserts prohibited articles in an express item, which does not constitute a crime, a public security penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　经营快递业务的企业有下列行为之一的，由邮政管理部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿直至吊销其快递业务经营许可证：  
（一）未按照规定建立快递运单及电子数据管理制度；  
（二）未定期销毁快递运单；  
（三）出售、泄露或者非法提供快递服务过程中知悉的用户信息；  
（四）发生或者可能发生用户信息泄露的情况，未立即采取补救措施，或者未向所在地邮政管理部门报告。

**Article 54** Where an enterprise operating express delivery business commits any of the following acts, the postal管理部门 shall order it to make corrections, confiscate its illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed, and it may be ordered to suspend business for rectification or have its express delivery business permit revoked:  
(1) Failing to establish a management system for express waybills and electronic data in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Failing to regularly destroy express waybills;  
(3) Selling, disclosing, or illegally providing user information learned during the provision of express delivery services;  
(4) In the event of a leak or potential leak of user information, failing to immediately take remedial measures, or failing to report to the local postal管理部门.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　经营快递业务的企业及其从业人员在经营活动中有危害国家安全行为的，依法追究法律责任；对经营快递业务的企业，由邮政管理部门吊销其快递业务经营许可证。

**Article 55** Where an enterprise operating express delivery business or its从业人员 engages in any act endangering national security during its business activities, legal liability shall be investigated according to law; for the enterprise operating express delivery business, the postal管理部门 shall revoke its express delivery business permit.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　经营快递业务的企业采用的快递包装不符合强制性国家标准，或者未按照国家有关规定向邮政管理部门报告包装物中一次性塑料制品的使用情况的，由邮政管理部门依照《中华人民共和国标准化法》、《中华人民共和国固体废物污染环境防治法》等法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。  
经营快递业务的企业未按照规定制定、实施快递包装操作规范或者包装物回收利用管理制度的，由邮政管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 56** Where the express packaging used by an enterprise operating express delivery business does not comply with mandatory national standards, or where it fails to report to the postal管理部门 the use of disposable plastic products in packaging materials in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the postal管理部门 shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Standardization Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution by Solid Waste, and other laws and administrative regulations.  
If an enterprise operating express delivery business fails to formulate and implement operational specifications for express packaging or a management system for the recovery and reuse of packaging materials in accordance with regulations, the postal管理部门 shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　邮政管理部门和其他有关部门的工作人员在监督管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 57** Where any staff member of the postal管理部门 or other relevant departments abuses his power, neglects his duty, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in supervision and administration, he shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 58** Any violation of these Regulations that constitutes a crime shall be subject to criminal liability according to law; if any personal injury, property damage or other damage is caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例自2018年5月1日起施行。

**Article 59** These Regulations shall come into force as of May 1, 2018.
