# 中华人民共和国航道管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2008-12-27  
**生效日期**：1987-10-01

> (1987年8月22日国务院发布　根据2008年12月27日《国务院关于修改〈中华人民共和国航道管理条例〉的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为加强航道管理，改善通航条件，保证航道畅通和航行安全，充分发挥水上交通在国民经济和国防建设中的作用，特制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国沿海和内河的航道、航道设施以及与通航有关的设施。 第三条　国家鼓励和保护在统筹兼顾、综合利用水资源的原则下，开发利用航道，发展水运事业。 第四条　中华人民共和国交通部主管全国航道事业。 第五条　航道分为国家航道、地方航道和专用航道。 第六条　国家航道及其航道设施按海区和内河水系，由交通部或者交通部授权的省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门管理。 地方航道及其航道设施由省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门管理。 专用航道及其航道设施由专用部门管理。 国家航道和地方航道上的过船建筑物，按照国务院规定管理。 第二章　航道的规划和建设 第七条　航道发展规划应当依据统筹兼顾、综合利用的原则，结合水利水电、城市建设以及铁路、公路、水运发展规划和国家批准的水资源综合规划制定。 第八条　国家航道发展规划由交通部编制，报国务院审查批准后实施。 地方航道发展规划由省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门编制，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查批准后实施，并抄报交通部备案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为加强航道管理，改善通航条件，保证航道畅通和航行安全，充分发挥水上交通在国民经济和国防建设中的作用，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening waterway management, improving navigation conditions, ensuring unobstructed waterways and navigation safety, and giving full play to the role of water transportation in national economic development and national defense construction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国沿海和内河的航道、航道设施以及与通航有关的设施。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the waterways, waterway facilities and facilities related to navigation in the coastal areas and inland rivers of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励和保护在统筹兼顾、综合利用水资源的原则下，开发利用航道，发展水运事业。

**Article 3** The State encourages and protects the development of waterways and the development of water transport undertakings under the principle of overall planning and comprehensive utilization of water resources.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中华人民共和国交通部主管全国航道事业。

**Article 4** The Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China shall be in charge of the national waterway undertakings.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　航道分为国家航道、地方航道和专用航道。

**Article 5** Waterways shall be classified into national waterways, local waterways and special-purpose waterways.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家航道及其航道设施按海区和内河水系，由交通部或者交通部授权的省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门管理。  
地方航道及其航道设施由省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门管理。  
专用航道及其航道设施由专用部门管理。  
国家航道和地方航道上的过船建筑物，按照国务院规定管理。

**Article 6** National waterways and their facilities shall be managed by the Ministry of Communications or the competent departments of communications of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government authorized by the Ministry of Communications, according to the sea areas and inland river systems.  
Local waterways and their facilities shall be managed by the competent departments of communications of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Special-purpose waterways and their facilities shall be managed by the special-purpose departments.  
The navigation structures on national waterways and local waterways shall be managed in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

### 第二章　航道的规划和建设

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　航道发展规划应当依据统筹兼顾、综合利用的原则，结合水利水电、城市建设以及铁路、公路、水运发展规划和国家批准的水资源综合规划制定。

**Article 7** Waterway development plans shall be formulated pursuant to the principles of overall planning and comprehensive utilization, taking into account water conservancy and hydropower, urban construction, and the development plans for railways, highways, water transport, and the comprehensive water resources plans approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家航道发展规划由交通部编制，报国务院审查批准后实施。  
地方航道发展规划由省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门编制，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查批准后实施，并抄报交通部备案。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市的地方航道的发展规划，由有关省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门共同编制，报有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府联合审查批准后实施，并抄报交通部备案；必要时报交通部审查批准后实施。  
专用航道发展规划由专用航道管理部门会同同级交通主管部门编制，报同级人民政府批准后实施。

**Article 8** The development plan for national waterways shall be formulated by the Ministry of Communications, submitted to the State Council for examination and approval before implementation.  
The development plan for local waterways shall be formulated by the competent departments of communications of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, submitted to the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for examination and approval before implementation, and a copy shall be submitted to the Ministry of Communications for record.  
The development plan for local waterways crossing provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be jointly formulated by the competent departments of communications of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, submitted to the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government for joint examination and approval before implementation, and a copy shall be submitted to the Ministry of Communications for record; when necessary, it shall be submitted to the Ministry of Communications for examination and approval before implementation.  
The development plan for special-purpose waterways shall be formulated by the competent department of the special-purpose waterway jointly with the competent department of communications at the same level, submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级水利电力主管部门编制河流流域规划和与航运有关的水利、水电工程规划以及进行上述工程设计时，必须有同级交通主管部门参加。  
各级交通主管部门编制渠化河流和人工运河航道发展规划和进行与水利水电有关的工程设计时，必须有同级水利电力主管部门参加。  
各级水利电力主管部门、交通主管部门编制上述规划，涉及运送木材的河流和重要的渔业水域时，必须有同级林业、渔业主管部门参加。

**Article 9** When the competent departments of water conservancy and electric power at various levels formulate river basin plans, water conservancy and hydropower project plans related to navigation, and carry out the design of the aforementioned projects, the competent departments of communications at the same level shall participate.  
When the competent departments of communications at various levels formulate the development plans for canalized rivers and artificial canal waterways and carry out the design of projects related to water conservancy and hydropower, the competent departments of water conservancy and electric power at the same level shall participate.  
When the competent departments of water conservancy and electric power and the competent departments of communications at various levels formulate the above-mentioned plans, if they involve rivers for timber transportation and important fishery waters, the competent departments of forestry and fisheries at the same level shall participate.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　航道应当划分技术等级。航道技术等级的划分，由省、自治区、直辖市交通主管部门或交通部派驻水系的管理机构根据通航标准提出方案。一至四级航道由交通部会同水利电力部及其他有关部门研究批准，报国务院备案；四级以下的航道，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，报交通部备案。

**Article 10** Waterways shall be classified into technical grades. The classification of technical grades of waterways shall be proposed by the competent departments of communications of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government or the management agencies dispatched by the Ministry of Communications to the river systems based on navigation standards. Grades one to four waterways shall be studied and approved by the Ministry of Communications jointly with the Ministry of Water Conservancy and Electric Power and other relevant departments, and submitted to the State Council for record; waterways below grade four shall be approved by the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and submitted to the Ministry of Communications for record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　建设航道及其设施，必须遵守国家基本建设程序的规定。工程竣工经验收合格后，方能交付使用。

**Article 11** The construction of waterways and their facilities must comply with the provisions of the basic construction procedures of the State. The project may be delivered for use only after completion and acceptance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　建设航道及其设施，不得危及水利水电工程、跨河建筑物和其他设施的安全。  
因建设航道及其设施损坏水利水电工程、跨河建筑物和其他设施的，建设单位应当给予赔偿或者修复。  
在行洪河道上建设航道，必须符合行洪安全的要求。

**Article 12** The construction of waterways and their facilities shall not endanger the safety of water conservancy and hydropower projects, river-crossing structures, and other facilities.  
If the construction of waterways and their facilities causes damage to water conservancy and hydropower projects, river-crossing structures, and other facilities, the construction unit shall provide compensation or repair.  
The construction of waterways on flood channels must meet the requirements for flood safety.

### 第三章　航道的保护

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　航道和航道设施受国家保护，任何单位和个人均不得侵占或者破坏。交通部门应当加强对航道的养护，保证航道畅通。

**Article 13** Waterways and waterway facilities shall be protected by the State, and no unit or individual may occupy or damage them. The communications departments shall strengthen the maintenance of waterways to ensure their unobstructed condition.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　修建与通航有关的设施或者治理河道、引水灌溉，必须符合国家规定的通航标准和技术要求，并应当事先征求交通主管部门的意见。  
违反前款规定，中断或者恶化通航条件的，由建设单位或者个人赔偿损失，并在规定期限内负责恢复通航。

**Article 14** The construction of facilities related to navigation or the regulation of river courses, diversion of water for irrigation must comply with the navigation standards and technical requirements prescribed by the State, and the opinions of the competent departments of communications shall be sought in advance.  
If the preceding paragraph is violated, causing interruption or deterioration of navigation conditions, the construction unit or individual shall compensate for losses and shall be responsible for restoring navigation within a prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在通航河流上建设永久性拦河闸坝，建设单位必须按照设计和施工方案，同时建设适当规模的过船、过木、过鱼建筑物，并解决施工期间的船舶、排筏通航问题。过船、过木、过鱼建筑物的建设费用，由建设单位承担。  
在不通航河流或者人工渠道上建设闸坝后可以通航的，建设单位应当同时建设适当规模的过船建筑物；不能同时建设的，应当预留建设过船建筑物的位置。过船建筑物的建设费用，除国家另有规定外，应当由交通部门承担。  
过船、过木、过鱼建筑物的设计任务书、设计文件和施工方案，必须取得交通、林业、渔业主管部门的同意。

**Article 15** When constructing permanent river-closing locks and dams on navigable rivers, the construction unit must, in accordance with the design and construction plan, simultaneously construct navigation structures, log-passing structures, and fish-passing structures of appropriate scale, and solve the problem of navigation for ships and rafts during the construction period. The construction costs of the navigation structures, log-passing structures, and fish-passing structures shall be borne by the construction unit.  
When a dam is constructed on a non-navigable river or artificial channel and navigation becomes possible thereafter, the construction unit shall simultaneously construct navigation structures of appropriate scale; if simultaneous construction is not possible, the location for future construction of navigation structures shall be reserved. The construction costs of the navigation structures shall be borne by the communications department unless otherwise provided by the State.  
The design task documents, design documents, and construction plans for navigation structures, log-passing structures, and fish-passing structures must obtain the consent of the competent departments of communications, forestry, and fisheries.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　因紧急抗旱需要，在通航河流上建临时闸坝，必须经县级以上人民政府批准。旱情解除后，建闸坝单位必须及时拆除闸坝，恢复通航条件。

**Article 16** If it is necessary to construct temporary locks and dams on navigable rivers due to emergency drought relief needs, approval from the people's government at or above the county level shall be obtained. After the drought situation is relieved, the unit that constructed the locks and dams must promptly dismantle them and restore navigation conditions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对通航河流上碍航的闸坝、桥梁和其他建筑物以及由建筑物所造成的航道淤积，由地方人民政府按照“谁造成碍航谁恢复通航”的原则，责成有关部门改建碍航建筑物或者限期补建过船、过木、过鱼建筑物，清除淤积，恢复通航。

**Article 17** For locks and dams, bridges, and other structures that obstruct navigation on navigable rivers, and for waterway siltation caused by such structures, the local people's government shall, in accordance with the principle of 'whoever causes obstruction shall restore navigation', charge the relevant departments with rebuilding the obstructing structures or supplementing the construction of navigation structures, log-passing structures, and fish-passing structures within a specified time limit, clearing the silt, and restoring navigation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在通航河段或其上游兴建水利工程控制或引走水源，建设单位应当保证航道和船闸所需要的通航流量。在特殊情况下，由于控制水源或大量引水影响通航时，建设单位应当采取相应的工程措施，地方人民政府应当组织有关部门协商，合理分配水量。

**Article 18** If a water conservancy project is constructed to control or divert water sources on a navigable river section or upstream thereof, the construction unit shall guarantee the navigation flow required for the waterway and ship locks. Under special circumstances, if navigation is affected by the control of water sources or large-scale water diversion, the construction unit shall take corresponding engineering measures, and the local people's government shall organize consultations among relevant departments to reasonably allocate water quantities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　水利水电工程设施管理部门制定调度运行方案，涉及通航流量、水位和航行安全时，应当事先与交通主管部门协商。协商不一致时，由县级以上人民政府决定。

**Article 19** When the management departments of water conservancy and hydropower project facilities formulate operation and dispatch plans involving navigation flow, water level, and navigation safety, they shall consult with the competent departments of communications in advance. If no agreement is reached, the matter shall be decided by the people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在防洪、排涝、抗旱时，综合利用水利枢纽过船建筑物应当服从防汛抗旱指挥机构统一安排。

**Article 20** During flood control, drainage for waterlogging, and drought relief, the navigation structures of comprehensive water conservancy projects shall be subject to the unified arrangements of the flood control and drought relief command.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　沿海和通航河流上设置的助航标志必须符合国家规定的标准。  
在沿海和通航河流上设置专用标志必须经交通主管部门同意；设置渔标和军用标，必须报交通主管部门备案。

**Article 21** Navigation aids installed on coastal areas and navigable rivers must comply with the standards prescribed by the State.  
The installation of special aids on coastal areas and navigable rivers must obtain the consent of the competent departments of communications; the installation of fishing marks and military marks must be reported to the competent departments of communications for record.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止向河道倾倒沙石泥土和废弃物。  
在通航河道内挖取沙石泥土、堆存材料，不得恶化通航条件。

**Article 22** It is prohibited to dump sand, stone, earth, and waste into river courses.  
The extraction of sand, stone, and earth or the stacking of materials in navigable river courses shall not deteriorate navigation conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　在航道内施工工程完成后，施工单位应当及时清除遗留物。

**Article 23** After the completion of construction work within a waterway, the construction unit shall promptly remove the remnants.

### 第四章　航道养护经费

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　经国家批准计征港务费的沿海和内河港口，进出港航道的维护费用由港务费开支。

**Article 24** For coastal and inland river ports approved by the State to levy port dues, the maintenance costs of the approach channels shall be covered by port dues.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　专用航道的维护费用，由专用部门自行解决。

**Article 25** The maintenance costs of special-purpose waterways shall be borne by the special-purpose department itself.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对中央、地方财政拨给的航道维护费用，必须坚持专款专用的原则。

**Article 26** The maintenance funds for waterways allocated by the central and local financial authorities must adhere to the principle of exclusive use for designated purposes.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对违反本条例规定的单位和个人，县以上交通主管部门可以视情节轻重给予警告、罚款的处罚。

**Article 27** For units and individuals that violate the provisions of these Regulations, the competent departments of communications at or above the county level may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose penalties such as warnings or fines.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　当事人对交通主管部门的处罚不服的，可以向上级交通主管部门提出申诉；对上级交通主管部门的处理不服的，可以在接到处理决定书之日起15日内向人民法院起诉。逾期不起诉又不履行的，交通主管部门可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 28** If a party refuses to accept the penalty imposed by the competent department of communications, it may file a petition with the competent department of communications at a higher level; if it refuses to accept the decision of the higher competent department of communications, it may file a prosecution in the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the decision notice. If no prosecution is filed within the time limit and the decision is not complied with, the competent department of communications may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本条例的规定，应当受治安管理处罚的，由公安机关处理；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 29** If a violation of these Regulations is subject to public security penalty, it shall be handled by the public security organ; if a crime is constituted, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
“航道”是指中华人民共和国沿海、江河、湖泊、运河内船舶、排筏可以通航的水域。  
“国家航道”是指：(一)构成国家航道网、可以通航五百吨级以上船舶的内河干线航道；(二)跨省、自治区、直辖市，可以常年通航三百吨级以上船舶的内河干线航道；(三)沿海干线航道和主要海港航道；(四)国家指定的重要航道。  
“专用航道”是指由军事、水利电力、林业、水产等部门以及其他企业事业单位自行建设、使用的航道。  
“地方航道”是指国家航道和专用航道以外的航道。  
“航道设施”是指航道的助航导航设施、整治建筑物、航运梯级、过船建筑物(包括过船闸坝)和其他航道工程设施。  
“与通航有关的设施”是指对航道的通航条件有影响的闸坝、桥梁、码头、架空电线、水下电缆、管道等拦河、跨河、临河建筑物和其他工程设施。

**Article 30** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
"Waterway" means the waters within the coastal areas, rivers, lakes, and canals of the People's Republic of China where ships and rafts can navigate.  
"National waterway" means: (1) main inland river waterways that form the national waterway network and can accommodate ships of 500 tons or more; (2) main inland river waterways that cross provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and can accommodate ships of 300 tons or more year-round; (3) main coastal waterways and major harbor approaches; (4) important waterways designated by the State.  
"Special-purpose waterway" means waterways built and used by military, water conservancy and electric power, forestry, fisheries, and other departments, as well as other enterprises and institutions.  
"Local waterway" means a waterway other than national waterways and special-purpose waterways.  
"Waterway facilities" means navigation aids and marking facilities, regulation structures, navigation steps, navigation structures (including navigation locks and dams), and other waterway engineering facilities.  
"Facilities related to navigation" means such river-closing, river-crossing, and riverine structures and other engineering facilities as locks and dams, bridges, wharves, overhead electric wires, submarine cables, pipelines, etc., that have an impact on the navigation conditions of waterways.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本条例由交通部负责解释。交通部可以根据本条例制定实施细则。

**Article 31** The Ministry of Communications shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Regulations. The Ministry of Communications may formulate implementing rules pursuant to these Regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本条例自1987年10月1日起施行。

**Article 32** These Regulations shall come into force as of October 1, 1987.
