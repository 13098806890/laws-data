# 国境河流外国籍船舶管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1966-04-19

> (1966年3月15日国务院批准　1966年4月19日交通部发布) 第一条　为了维护中华人民共和国主权，便利外国籍船舶进出我国同邻国互为边界的国境河流港口(以下简称国境河流港口)和我国同邻国相通的河流(以下简称同邻国相通河流)，保障外国籍船舶航行安全，特制订本办法。 第二条　进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流的所有外国籍船舶，都应当遵守本办法。 第三条　在国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流的港口，设立中华人民共和国港务监督。 港务监督负责监督本办法和有关航务法令、规章的贯彻执行；审批外国籍船舶进出口的申请；对准予进出口的外国籍船舶实施强制引水，组织联合检查；监督进出口的外国籍船舶的技术状况并维持航行安全秩序；调查处理进出口的外国籍船舶的海损事故。 第四条　外国籍船舶，只有根据其所属国家政府同我国政府签订的有关商船通航协定，或者获得我国政府的许可，才可以进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流；每次进出口，还必须依照本办法第五条和第九条第二款的规定，报请港务监督批准。 第五条　外国籍船舶要求进入国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流，船长应当在船舶从出发港开航前，将船名、船队艘数、最大吃水、载货名称和数量、载客人数、预定到达国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流联合检查(引水)锚地的时间，通过其在港代理人，报请预定到达的国境河流港口或同邻国相通河流的第一港口的港务监督审批。外国籍船舶的出发港在国境河流预定到达港对岸的，可以简化手续，用港务监督规定的信号报请审批。 第六条　外国籍船舶进出国境河流港口和在同邻国相通河流航行，应当悬挂各项规定号帜，白昼还应当悬挂其所属国籍的国旗；在同邻国相通河流航行，还应当在前桅顶部悬挂中华人民共和国国旗。 第七条　外国籍船舶进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流，必须向港务监督申请引水员引航。外国籍船舶在港口停泊期间，非经引水员引航不得擅自移泊。如果国境河流港口因特殊情况未划定引水锚地，港务监督可以免除进出口外国籍船舶的引水申请。 第八条　外国籍船舶进入引水锚地，船长应将船上所有武器、弹药、无线电发报机、无线电话发射机、雷达、火箭信号、信号炮等的名称、数量向港务监督申报，并遵守下列规定： (一)武器、弹药由港务监督予以封存； (二)无线电发报机、无线电话发射机、雷达、火箭信号、信号炮等禁止使用。 船舶在遇险或发生意外事故时，可启用本条前款(二)项所列的物品，但启用后应立即向港务监督报告。 第九条　外国籍船舶进入引水锚地，船长应当填报港务监督规定的表报，呈验有关船舶文书，并接受港务监督和有关部门的联合检查。 外国籍船舶出口，船长应当通过其在港代理人报请港务监督审批，然后填报规定表报，接受联合检查，经港务监督发给出口许可证，始得出口。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护中华人民共和国主权，便利外国籍船舶进出我国同邻国互为边界的国境河流港口(以下简称国境河流港口)和我国同邻国相通的河流(以下简称同邻国相通河流)，保障外国籍船舶航行安全，特制订本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purposes of safeguarding the sovereignty of the People's Republic of China, facilitating the entry and exit of foreign vessels into and out of the ports on the boundary rivers between China and neighboring countries (hereinafter referred to as "ports on boundary rivers") and the rivers connecting China and neighboring countries (hereinafter referred to as "connecting rivers with neighboring countries"), and ensuring navigation safety of foreign vessels.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流的所有外国籍船舶，都应当遵守本办法。

**Article 2** All foreign vessels entering or leaving the ports on boundary rivers and the connecting rivers with neighboring countries shall comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流的港口，设立中华人民共和国港务监督。  
港务监督负责监督本办法和有关航务法令、规章的贯彻执行；审批外国籍船舶进出口的申请；对准予进出口的外国籍船舶实施强制引水，组织联合检查；监督进出口的外国籍船舶的技术状况并维持航行安全秩序；调查处理进出口的外国籍船舶的海损事故。

**Article 3** In the ports on boundary rivers and the ports of the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, the Harbour Superintendency of the People's Republic of China shall be established.  
The Harbour Superintendency shall be responsible for supervising the implementation and enforcement of these Measures and relevant navigation laws and regulations; examining and approving applications for the entry and exit of foreign vessels; implementing compulsory pilotage for foreign vessels permitted to enter or leave and organizing joint inspections; supervising the technical condition of foreign vessels entering or leaving and maintaining navigation safety and order; investigating and handling maritime accidents involving foreign vessels entering or leaving.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国籍船舶，只有根据其所属国家政府同我国政府签订的有关商船通航协定，或者获得我国政府的许可，才可以进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流；每次进出口，还必须依照[本办法第五条](#art-5)和第九条第二款的规定，报请港务监督批准。

**Article 4** Foreign vessels may enter or leave the ports on boundary rivers and the connecting rivers with neighboring countries only in accordance with the agreement on merchant ship navigation signed between the government of their flag state and the Chinese government, or upon obtaining permission from the Chinese government; for each entry or exit, they must also report to the Harbour Superintendency for approval in accordance with Article 5 and paragraph 2 of Article 9 of these Measures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外国籍船舶要求进入国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流，船长应当在船舶从出发港开航前，将船名、船队艘数、最大吃水、载货名称和数量、载客人数、预定到达国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流联合检查(引水)锚地的时间，通过其在港代理人，报请预定到达的国境河流港口或同邻国相通河流的第一港口的港务监督审批。外国籍船舶的出发港在国境河流预定到达港对岸的，可以简化手续，用港务监督规定的信号报请审批。

**Article 5** When a foreign vessel intends to enter a port on a boundary river or a connecting river with a neighboring country, the master, before the vessel's departure from the port of departure, shall report to the Harbour Superintendency of the intended port of arrival on the boundary river or the first port of the connecting river with a neighboring country for approval, through the vessel's agent in the port, the name of the vessel, the number of vessels in the fleet, the maximum draft, the names and quantities of cargo, the number of passengers carried, and the scheduled time of arrival at the joint inspection (pilotage) anchorage of the port on the boundary river or the connecting river with a neighboring country. If the port of departure of the foreign vessel is on the opposite bank of the intended port of arrival on the boundary river, the formalities may be simplified by reporting for approval with signals prescribed by the Harbour Superintendency.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国籍船舶进出国境河流港口和在同邻国相通河流航行，应当悬挂各项规定号帜，白昼还应当悬挂其所属国籍的国旗；在同邻国相通河流航行，还应当在前桅顶部悬挂中华人民共和国国旗。

**Article 6** Foreign vessels, when entering or leaving the ports on boundary rivers and navigating in the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, shall fly various prescribed signal flags, and during the daytime shall also fly the national flag of their flag state; when navigating in the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, they shall also fly the national flag of the People's Republic of China at the top of the foremast.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国籍船舶进出国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流，必须向港务监督申请引水员引航。外国籍船舶在港口停泊期间，非经引水员引航不得擅自移泊。如果国境河流港口因特殊情况未划定引水锚地，港务监督可以免除进出口外国籍船舶的引水申请。

**Article 7** Foreign vessels, when entering or leaving the ports on boundary rivers and the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, must apply to the Harbour Superintendency for pilotage by a pilot. During the period of berthing in the port, a foreign vessel shall not move berth without being piloted by a pilot. If, due to special circumstances, the pilotage anchorage has not been designated in a port on a boundary river, the Harbour Superintendency may waive the pilotage application for foreign vessels entering or leaving.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国籍船舶进入引水锚地，船长应将船上所有武器、弹药、无线电发报机、无线电话发射机、雷达、火箭信号、信号炮等的名称、数量向港务监督申报，并遵守下列规定：  
(一)武器、弹药由港务监督予以封存；  
(二)无线电发报机、无线电话发射机、雷达、火箭信号、信号炮等禁止使用。  
船舶在遇险或发生意外事故时，可启用本条前款(二)项所列的物品，但启用后应立即向港务监督报告。

**Article 8** When a foreign vessel enters the pilotage anchorage, the master shall declare to the Harbour Superintendency the names and quantities of all weapons, ammunition, radio transmitters, radiotelephone transmitters, radar, rocket signals, signal guns, etc. on board, and shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Weapons and ammunition shall be sealed up by the Harbour Superintendency;  
(2) Radio transmitters, radiotelephone transmitters, radar, rocket signals, signal guns, etc. shall be prohibited from use.  
In the event of distress or accident, the vessel may use the items listed in subparagraph (2) of the preceding paragraph, but shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency after such use.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国籍船舶进入引水锚地，船长应当填报港务监督规定的表报，呈验有关船舶文书，并接受港务监督和有关部门的联合检查。  
外国籍船舶出口，船长应当通过其在港代理人报请港务监督审批，然后填报规定表报，接受联合检查，经港务监督发给出口许可证，始得出口。

**Article 9** When a foreign vessel enters the pilotage anchorage, the master shall fill in the forms prescribed by the Harbour Superintendency, submit relevant ship documents for inspection, and accept the joint inspection by the Harbour Superintendency and relevant authorities.  
For the departure of a foreign vessel, the master shall report to the Harbour Superintendency for approval through the vessel's agent in the port, then fill in the prescribed forms, undergo joint inspection, and may only depart after the Harbour Superintendency issues an exit permit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外国籍船舶在同邻国相通河流内航行时，港务监督或其他有关检查机关可派员随船工作，船长应给予方便。

**Article 10** When foreign vessels are navigating in the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, the Harbour Superintendency or other relevant inspection authorities may dispatch personnel to work on board, and the master shall provide convenience.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国籍船舶及其船员、旅客进入国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流，禁止下列行为：  
(一)摄影、绘图；  
(二)游泳、渔猎；  
(三)测深；  
(四)在港口抛掷或排出压仓物、煤渣、垃圾、污油、含油污水等。

**Article 11** Foreign vessels, their crew members and passengers, when entering the ports on boundary rivers and the connecting rivers with neighboring countries, are prohibited from the following acts:  
(1) Photography and drawing;  
(2) Swimming, fishing and hunting;  
(3) Sounding;  
(4) Dumping or discharging ballast, cinders, garbage, waste oil, oily water, etc. in the port.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国籍船舶，禁止在国境河流我方沿岸未经指定的地点和同邻国相通河流未经指定的地点停靠、锚泊、上下人员和装卸货物。在发生海损事故或遇有不可抗力情况时，可以例外，但船长应立即向港务监督或当地人民委员会报告。

**Article 12** Foreign vessels are prohibited from berthing, anchoring, embarking or disembarking persons, and loading or discharging cargo at places on the Chinese bank of the boundary river or in the connecting river with a neighboring country that have not been designated. In the event of a maritime accident or force majeure, exceptions may be made, but the master shall immediately report to the Harbour Superintendency or the local People's Committee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　外国籍船舶在国境河流港口和同邻国相通河流发生海损事故时，船长应当迅速向港务监督提出海损事故报告书，听候港务监督进行调查处理。

**Article 13** When a maritime accident occurs to a foreign vessel in a port on a boundary river or a connecting river with a neighboring country, the master shall promptly submit a report on the maritime accident to the Harbour Superintendency and await investigation and handling by the Harbour Superintendency.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国籍船舶有下列情况之一时，港务监督可以禁止其出航：  
(一)失去适航状态；  
(二)违反安全航行规定；  
(三)未缴付港口费用；  
(四)未缴付应缴的赔偿款项又未提出适当担保。

**Article 14** Where a foreign vessel is in any of the following circumstances, the Harbour Superintendency may prohibit it from departing:  
(1) It is not in a seaworthy condition;  
(2) It violates the provisions on safe navigation;  
(3) It has not paid port charges;  
(4) It has not paid the compensation payable or provided appropriate security.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　外国籍船舶及其船员、旅客违反本办法和有关航务法令、规章的，港务监督应当根据情节，予以处理。

**Article 15** Where a foreign vessel, its crew members or passengers violate these Measures or relevant navigation laws and regulations, the Harbour Superintendency shall, based on the circumstances, handle the matter.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　外国籍船舶所属国家政府已经同我国政府签订有商船通航协定的，可以仍按协定执行；协定中未规定的，按照本办法执行。

**Article 16** Where the government of the flag state of a foreign vessel has signed an agreement on merchant ship navigation with the Chinese government, the provisions of the agreement shall still be implemented; matters not provided for in the agreement shall be implemented in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　各有关省、自治区、直辖市人民委员会，可以根据本办法，结合当地具体情况，制订实施细则。

**Article 17** The People's Committees of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with these Measures and in light of local specific conditions, formulate implementing rules.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本办法经国务院批准，由交通部发布施行。

**Article 18** These Measures are approved by the State Council and shall be promulgated and come into force by the Ministry of Communications.
