# 中华人民共和国打捞沉船管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1957-10-11

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保证航行安全，加强沉船的打捞和管理工作，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to ensure navigation safety and strengthen the salvage and management of sunken vessels.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　除军事舰艇和木帆船外，在中华人民共和国领海和内河的沉船，包括沉船本体、船上器物以及货物都适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to sunken vessels in the territorial sea and inland rivers of the People's Republic of China, including the sunken vessel itself, its onboard equipment, and cargo, with the exception of military vessels and wooden sailing vessels.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　下列沉船应当进行打捞：  
(一)妨碍船舶航行、航道整治或者工程建筑的沉船；  
(二)有修复使用价值的沉船；  
(三)虽无修复使用价值而有拆卸利用价值的沉船。

**Article 3** The following sunken vessels shall be salvaged:  
(1) Sunken vessels that impede vessel navigation, waterway regulation, or engineering construction;  
(2) Sunken vessels with repair and use value;  
(3) Sunken vessels that, although having no repair and use value, have dismantling and utilization value.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　严重危害船舶安全航行的沉船，有关港(航)务主管机关有权立即进行打捞或者解体清除，但是所采取措施应即通知沉船所有人，如果沉船所有人不明或者无法通知时，应当在当地和中央报纸上公告。

**Article 4** For sunken vessels that seriously endanger the safe navigation of vessels, the competent port (navigation) authority may immediately carry out salvage or dismantling and removal; however, the measures taken shall be notified to the owner of the sunken vessel immediately. If the owner of the sunken vessel is unknown or cannot be notified, a public announcement shall be made in local and central newspapers.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　妨碍船舶航行、航道整治或者工程建筑的沉船，有关港(航)务主管机关应当根据具体情况规定申请期限和打捞期限，通知或公告沉船所有人。  
沉船所有人必须在规定期限以内提出申请和进行打捞；否则，有关港(航)务主管机关可以进行打捞或者予以解体清除。

**Article 5** For sunken vessels that impede vessel navigation, waterway regulation, or engineering construction, the competent port (navigation) authority shall, in light of the specific circumstances, set a time limit for application and a time limit for salvage, and notify or announce to the owner of the sunken vessel.  
The owner of the sunken vessel must submit an application and carry out salvage within the prescribed time limit; otherwise, the competent port (navigation) authority may carry out salvage or dismantle and remove the vessel.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　其他不属于第五条规定范围的沉船，沉船所有人应当自船舶沉没之日起一年以内提出打捞计划和完工期限，经有关港(航)务主管机关批准后进行打捞。

**Article 6** For other sunken vessels not falling within the scope of Article 5, the owner of the sunken vessel shall, within one year from the date of the vessel's sinking, submit a salvage plan and completion deadline, and after obtaining approval from the competent port (navigation) authority, carry out salvage.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　沉船所有人除遇有特殊情况向有关港(航)务主管机关申请延期并经核准外，在下列情况下即丧失各该沉船的所有权：  
(一)妨碍船舶航行、航道整治或者工程建筑的沉船，在申请期限以内没有申请或者声明放弃；或者打捞期限届满，而没有完成打捞；  
(二)其他不属于第五条规定范围的沉船自沉没之日起一年以内没有申请打捞；或者完工期限已经届满，而没有打捞。

**Article 7** The owner of a sunken vessel shall lose its ownership of the respective sunken vessel, unless it has applied for an extension to the competent port (navigation) authority due to special circumstances and obtained approval, in the following circumstances:  
(1) For sunken vessels that impede vessel navigation, waterway regulation, or engineering construction, no application has been made or a declaration of abandonment has been made within the application period; or the salvage period has expired without completion of the salvage;  
(2) For other sunken vessels not falling within the scope of Article 5, no salvage application has been made within one year from the date of sinking; or the completion deadline has expired without salvage being carried out.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有关港(航)务主管机关根据第四条规定所捞起的船体、船用器物、货物或者解体所得的钢材、机件等，在无法或者不易保管的情况下，可以作价处理。  
沉船所有人自船舶沉没之日起一年以内，可以申请发还捞起的原物或者处理原物所得的价款，过期如不申请即丧失其所有权。  
沉船所有人在领回原物或者价款时，应当偿还有关打捞、保管和处理等费用。

**Article 8** The competent port (navigation) authority may dispose of the hull, vessel equipment, cargo, or steel materials and components obtained from dismantling, which are salvaged pursuant to Article 4, at a valuation if they cannot be stored or are difficult to store.  
The owner of the sunken vessel may, within one year from the date of sinking, apply for the return of the salvaged original items or the proceeds from the disposal of the original items; if no application is made within the time limit, its ownership shall be lost.  
When the owner of the sunken vessel retrieves the original items or the proceeds, it shall repay the expenses related to salvage, storage, and disposal.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　打捞和解体清除沉船必须经过批准，批准权限如下：  
(一)500总吨以上或者300匹指示马力以上的沉船由交通部批准；  
(二)不满前项规定的沉船，由有关港(航)务主管机关批准，并由各该港(航)务主管机关依其行政系统分别报请交通部或者有关省人民委员会备案。

**Article 9** Salvage and dismantling removal of sunken vessels must be approved. The authority for approval is as follows:  
(1) Sunken vessels of 500 gross tonnage or above, or 300 indicated horsepower or above, shall be approved by the Ministry of Communications;  
(2) For sunken vessels not meeting the provisions of the preceding item, the approval shall be granted by the competent port (navigation) authority, and the said authority shall, according to its administrative system, report to the Ministry of Communications or the relevant provincial people's committee for the record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　未经过批准，任何人都不得擅自打捞或拆除沉船。偶然捞获的沉没物资应当送当地港(航)务主管机关处理，由各该机关酌情给予奖励。

**Article 10** Without approval, no person may salvage or dismantle a sunken vessel without authorization. Any sunken materials accidentally salvaged shall be delivered to the local port (navigation) authority for disposal, and the said authority may, as it deems appropriate, give a reward.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有修复价值而不严重妨碍航行安全的沉船，不得解体。申请解体沉船必须提出该沉船确无修复价值的可靠勘测资料。解体打捞或者清除机动船舶，不得在水下破坏主副机、锅炉等物，应当尽量完整捞起，并经有关港(航)务主管机关鉴定后，才可以处理。

**Article 11** Sunken vessels that have repair value and do not seriously impede navigation safety shall not be dismantled. To apply for the dismantling of a sunken vessel, reliable survey data proving that the vessel has no repair value must be submitted. When dismantling, salvaging, or removing a motor vessel, the main and auxiliary engines, boilers, and other items shall not be damaged underwater; they shall be salvaged as intact as possible, and may be disposed of only after expert evaluation by the competent port (navigation) authority.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　全国各地沉船勘测工作，由交通部打捞专业机构负责进行，但是有关港(航)务主管机关因业务上的需要，也可以自行勘测。其他机关、企业、个人必须经有关港(航)务主管机关批准才可以进行沉船勘测工作，并应将勘测结果抄送原批准机关。

**Article 12** The survey of sunken vessels throughout the country shall be carried out by the professional salvage institution of the Ministry of Communications; however, the competent port (navigation) authority may also carry out surveys on its own for operational needs. Other organs, enterprises, or individuals must obtain approval from the competent port (navigation) authority before carrying out survey work on sunken vessels, and shall send a copy of the survey results to the approving authority.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本办法经国务院批准由交通部发布施行。

**Article 13** These Measures have been approved by the State Council and are issued by the Ministry of Communications for coming into force.
