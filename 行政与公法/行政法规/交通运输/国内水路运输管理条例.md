### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范国内水路运输经营行为，维护国内水路运输市场秩序，保障国内水路运输安全，促进国内水路运输业健康发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of regulating domestic waterway transport business operations, maintaining the order of the domestic waterway transport market, ensuring the safety of domestic waterway transport, and promoting the healthy development of the domestic waterway transport industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　经营国内水路运输以及水路运输辅助业务，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称国内水路运输（以下简称水路运输），是指始发港、挂靠港和目的港均在中华人民共和国管辖的通航水域内的经营性旅客运输和货物运输。  
本条例所称水路运输辅助业务，是指直接为水路运输提供服务的船舶管理、船舶代理、水路旅客运输代理和水路货物运输代理等经营活动。

**Article 2** The operation of domestic waterway transport and waterway transport auxiliary services shall comply with these Regulations.  
The term "domestic waterway transport" (hereinafter referred to as waterway transport) as used in these Regulations refers to the commercial passenger transport and cargo transport where the port of departure, port of call, and port of destination are all within the navigable waters under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
The term "waterway transport auxiliary services" as used in these Regulations refers to business activities such as ship management, ship agency, waterway passenger transport agency, and waterway cargo transport agency that directly provide services for waterway transport.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家鼓励和保护水路运输市场的公平竞争，禁止垄断和不正当竞争行为。  
国家运用经济、技术政策等措施，支持和鼓励水路运输经营者实行规模化、集约化经营，促进水路运输行业结构调整；支持和鼓励水路运输经营者采用先进适用的水路运输设备和技术，保障运输安全，促进节约能源，减少污染物排放。  
国家保护水路运输经营者、旅客和货主的合法权益。

**Article 3** The State encourages and protects fair competition in the waterway transport market, and prohibits monopolistic and unfair competition practices.  
The State adopts economic, technical policies and other measures to support and encourage waterway transport operators to implement large-scale and intensive operation, and promote the structural adjustment of the waterway transport industry; supports and encourages waterway transport operators to adopt advanced and applicable waterway transport equipment and technologies to ensure transport safety, promote energy conservation, and reduce pollutant emissions.  
The State protects the legitimate rights and interests of waterway transport operators, passengers, and cargo owners.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院交通运输主管部门主管全国水路运输管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门主管本行政区域的水路运输管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府负责水路运输管理的部门或者机构（以下统称负责水路运输管理的部门）承担本条例规定的水路运输管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of transport under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of waterway transport nationwide.  
The competent departments of transport of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of waterway transport within their respective administrative regions. The departments or agencies responsible for waterway transport administration of local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the departments responsible for waterway transport administration) shall undertake the waterway transport administration work as specified in these Regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经营水路运输及其辅助业务，应当遵守法律、法规，诚实守信。  
国务院交通运输主管部门和负责水路运输管理的部门应当依法对水路运输市场实施监督管理，对水路运输及其辅助业务的违法经营活动实施处罚，并建立经营者诚信管理制度，及时向社会公告监督检查情况。

**Article 5** The operation of waterway transport and its auxiliary services shall comply with laws and regulations, and shall be conducted in good faith.  
The competent department of transport under the State Council and the departments responsible for waterway transport administration shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over the waterway transport market, impose penalties on illegal business activities in waterway transport and its auxiliary services, establish a credit management system for operators, and timely announce the supervision and inspection information to the public.

### 第二章　水路运输经营者

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请经营水路运输业务，除[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定的情形外，申请人应当符合下列条件：  
（一）取得企业法人资格；  
（二）有符合[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定的船舶，并且自有船舶运力符合国务院交通运输主管部门的规定；  
（三）有明确的经营范围，其中申请经营水路旅客班轮运输业务的，还应当有可行的航线营运计划；  
（四）有与其申请的经营范围和船舶运力相适应的海务、机务管理人员；  
（五）与其直接订立劳动合同的高级船员占全部船员的比例符合国务院交通运输主管部门的规定；  
（六）有健全的安全管理制度；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 6** An applicant for the operation of waterway transport business, except as otherwise provided in Article 7 of these Regulations, shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained the status of a legal person enterprise;  
(2) having vessels that meet the requirements of Article 13 of these Regulations, and the self-owned vessel capacity complies with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council;  
(3) having a clear scope of business, and if applying for the operation of waterway passenger liner transport business, also having a feasible route operation plan;  
(4) having nautical and engineering management personnel commensurate with the scope of business and vessel capacity applied for;  
(5) the proportion of senior crew members with whom a labor contract has been directly concluded to the total crew members complies with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council;  
(6) having a sound safety management system;  
(7) other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　个人可以申请经营内河普通货物运输业务。  
申请经营内河普通货物运输业务的个人，应当有符合[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定且船舶吨位不超过国务院交通运输主管部门规定的自有船舶，并应当符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)第六项、第七项规定的条件。

**Article 7** An individual may apply to operate inland river ordinary cargo transport business.  
An individual applying to operate inland river ordinary cargo transport business shall have self-owned vessels that meet the requirements of Article 13 of these Regulations and whose tonnage does not exceed the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council, and shall meet the conditions specified in items (6) and (7) of Article 6 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　经营水路运输业务，应当按照国务院交通运输主管部门的规定，经国务院交通运输主管部门或者设区的市级以上地方人民政府负责水路运输管理的部门批准。  
申请经营水路运输业务，应当向前款规定的负责审批的部门提交申请书和证明申请人符合[本条例第六条](#art-6)或者第七条规定条件的相关材料。  
负责审批的部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内审查完毕，作出准予许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，发给水路运输业务经营许可证件，并为申请人投入运营的船舶配发船舶营运证件；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 8** The operation of waterway transport business shall be approved by the competent department of transport under the State Council or the department responsible for waterway transport administration of the local people's government at or above the city with districts level, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council.  
An application to operate waterway transport business shall submit a written application and relevant materials proving that the applicant meets the conditions specified in Article 6 or Article 7 of these Regulations to the department responsible for examination and approval as prescribed in the preceding paragraph.  
The department responsible for examination and approval shall complete the examination within 30 working days from the date of accepting the application, and make a decision to grant or not to grant the license. If the license is granted, it shall issue a waterway transport business operation license, and issue vessel operation certificates for the vessels put into operation by the applicant; if the license is not granted, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级交通运输主管部门应当做好水路运输市场统计和调查分析工作，定期向社会公布水路运输市场运力供需状况。

**Article 9** The competent departments of transport at all levels shall properly conduct statistical work and investigation and analysis of the waterway transport market, and regularly publish to the public the supply and demand situation of transport capacity in the waterway transport market.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　为保障水路运输安全，维护水路运输市场的公平竞争秩序，国务院交通运输主管部门可以根据水路运输市场监测情况，决定在特定的旅客班轮运输和散装液体危险货物运输航线、水域暂停新增运力许可。  
采取前款规定的运力调控措施，应当符合公开、公平、公正的原则，在开始实施的60日前向社会公告，说明采取措施的理由以及采取措施的范围、期限等事项。

**Article 10** To ensure the safety of waterway transport and maintain the order of fair competition in the waterway transport market, the competent department of transport under the State Council may, based on the monitoring of the waterway transport market, decide to suspend new capacity licenses on specific passenger liner transport and bulk liquid dangerous cargo transport routes and waters.  
The adoption of capacity control measures as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall comply with the principles of openness, fairness, and impartiality, and shall be announced to the public 60 days before the implementation, explaining the reasons for taking the measures and the scope, duration, etc. of the measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国的企业、其他经济组织和个人不得经营水路运输业务，也不得以租用中国籍船舶或者舱位等方式变相经营水路运输业务。  
香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区的企业、其他经济组织以及个人参照适用前款规定，国务院另有规定的除外。

**Article 11** Foreign enterprises, other economic organizations, and individuals shall not operate waterway transport business, nor shall they operate waterway transport business in disguised form by chartering Chinese-flagged vessels or space, etc.  
Enterprises, other economic organizations, and individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, and the Taiwan Region shall apply the provisions of the preceding paragraph mutatis mutandis, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　依照本条例取得许可的水路运输经营者终止经营的，应当自终止经营之日起15个工作日内向原许可机关办理注销许可手续，交回水路运输业务经营许可证件。

**Article 12** Where a waterway transport operator that has obtained a license in accordance with these Regulations terminates its business, it shall, within 15 working days from the date of termination, go through the procedures for cancellation of the license with the original licensing authority, and return the waterway transport business operation license.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　水路运输经营者投入运营的船舶应当符合下列条件：  
（一）与经营者的经营范围相适应；  
（二）取得有效的船舶登记证书和检验证书；  
（三）符合国务院交通运输主管部门关于船型技术标准和船龄的要求；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 13** Vessels put into operation by a waterway transport operator shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) being compatible with the operator's scope of business;  
(2) having obtained valid ship registration certificates and inspection certificates;  
(3) complying with the requirements of the competent department of transport under the State Council regarding ship type technical standards and ship age;  
(4) other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　水路运输经营者新增船舶投入运营的，应当凭水路运输业务经营许可证件、船舶登记证书和检验证书向国务院交通运输主管部门或者设区的市级以上地方人民政府负责水路运输管理的部门领取船舶营运证件。  
从事水路运输经营的船舶应当随船携带船舶营运证件。  
海事管理机构在现场监督检查时，发现从事水路运输的船舶不能提供有效的船舶营运证件的，应当通知有关主管部门依法处理。

**Article 14** Where a waterway transport operator adds new vessels for operation, it shall, by presenting the waterway transport business operation license, ship registration certificate, and inspection certificate, obtain vessel operation certificates from the competent department of transport under the State Council or the department responsible for waterway transport administration of the local people's government at or above the city with districts level.  
Vessels engaged in waterway transport operations shall carry the vessel operation certificates on board.  
When conducting on-site supervision and inspection, if the maritime administration agency finds that a vessel engaged in waterway transport cannot provide a valid vessel operation certificate, it shall notify the relevant competent department to handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家根据保障运输安全、保护水环境、节约能源、提高航道和通航设施利用效率的需求，制定并实施新的船型技术标准时，对正在使用的不符合新标准但符合原有标准且未达到规定报废船龄的船舶，可以采取资金补贴等措施，引导、鼓励水路运输经营者进行更新、改造；需要强制提前报废的，应当对船舶所有人给予补偿。具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院财政部门制定。

**Article 15** When the State formulates and implements new ship type technical standards to meet the needs of ensuring transport safety, protecting the water environment, conserving energy, and improving the utilization efficiency of waterways and navigation facilities, it may adopt measures such as financial subsidies to guide and encourage waterway transport operators to renew or retrofit vessels that are in use and do not meet the new standards but meet the original standards and have not reached the prescribed scrapping age; if early forced scrapping is necessary, compensation shall be given to the shipowner. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of transport under the State Council jointly with the finance department under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　水路运输经营者不得使用外国籍船舶经营水路运输业务。但是，在国内没有能够满足所申请运输要求的中国籍船舶，并且船舶停靠的港口或者水域为对外开放的港口或者水域的情况下，经国务院交通运输主管部门许可，水路运输经营者可以在国务院交通运输主管部门规定的期限或者航次内，临时使用外国籍船舶运输。  
在香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区进行船籍登记的船舶，参照适用本条例关于外国籍船舶的规定，国务院另有规定的除外。

**Article 16** A waterway transport operator shall not use foreign-flagged vessels to operate waterway transport business. However, where there is no Chinese-flagged vessel that can meet the transport requirements applied for within the country, and the port or waters where the vessel calls are open to foreign countries, with the permission of the competent department of transport under the State Council, a waterway transport operator may temporarily use a foreign-flagged vessel for transport within the period or voyage prescribed by the competent department of transport under the State Council.  
Vessels registered in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan Region shall apply the provisions of these Regulations concerning foreign-flagged vessels mutatis mutandis, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.

### 第三章　水路运输经营活动

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　水路运输经营者应当在依法取得许可的经营范围内从事水路运输经营。

**Article 17** A waterway transport operator shall engage in waterway transport operations within the scope of business for which it has obtained a license according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　水路运输经营者应当使用符合本条例规定条件、配备合格船员的船舶，并保证船舶处于适航状态。  
水路运输经营者应当按照船舶核定载客定额或者载重量载运旅客、货物，不得超载或者使用货船载运旅客。

**Article 18** A waterway transport operator shall use vessels that meet the conditions prescribed in these Regulations and are manned with qualified crew, and shall ensure that the vessels are in a seaworthy condition.  
A waterway transport operator shall carry passengers and cargo in accordance with the approved passenger capacity or deadweight tonnage of the vessel, and shall not overload or use cargo vessels to carry passengers.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　水路运输经营者应当依照法律、行政法规和国务院交通运输主管部门关于水路旅客、货物运输的规定、质量标准以及合同的约定，为旅客、货主提供安全、便捷、优质的服务，保证旅客、货物运输安全。  
水路旅客运输业务经营者应当为其客运船舶投保承运人责任保险或者取得相应的财务担保。

**Article 19** A waterway transport operator shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council on waterway passenger and cargo transport, quality standards, and the contract, provide safe, convenient, and high-quality services to passengers and cargo owners, and ensure the safety of passenger and cargo transport.  
An operator of waterway passenger transport business shall take out carrier liability insurance for its passenger vessels or obtain corresponding financial guarantees.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　水路运输经营者运输危险货物，应当遵守法律、行政法规以及国务院交通运输主管部门关于危险货物运输的规定，使用依法取得危险货物适装证书的船舶，按照规定的安全技术规范进行配载和运输，保证运输安全。

**Article 20** Where a waterway transport operator transports dangerous goods, it shall comply with laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the competent department of transport under the State Council on the transport of dangerous goods, use vessels that have obtained a certificate of fitness for dangerous goods according to law, and stow and transport in accordance with the prescribed safety technical specifications to ensure transport safety.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　旅客班轮运输业务经营者应当自取得班轮航线经营许可之日起60日内开航，并在开航15日前公布所使用的船舶、班期、班次、运价等信息。  
旅客班轮运输应当按照公布的班期、班次运行；变更班期、班次、运价的，应当在15日前向社会公布；停止经营部分或者全部班轮航线的，应当在30日前向社会公布并报原许可机关备案。

**Article 21** Operators of passenger liner transport services shall commence navigation within 60 days from the date of obtaining the administrative license for the liner route, and shall publish information on the vessels used, schedules, frequencies, and freight rates 15 days prior to commencement of navigation.  
Passenger liner transport shall operate according to the published schedules and frequencies; if the schedules, frequencies, or freight rates are changed, such changes shall be published to the public 15 days in advance; if a part or all of the liner routes are to be ceased, such cessation shall be published to the public 30 days in advance and reported to the original licensing authority for record.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　货物班轮运输业务经营者应当在班轮航线开航的7日前，公布所使用的船舶以及班期、班次和运价。  
货物班轮运输应当按照公布的班期、班次运行；变更班期、班次、运价或者停止经营部分或者全部班轮航线的，应当在7日前向社会公布。

**Article 22** Operators of cargo liner transport services shall, 7 days prior to the commencement of navigation on the liner route, publish the vessels used, schedules, frequencies, and freight rates.  
Cargo liner transport shall operate according to the published schedules and frequencies; if the schedules, frequencies, freight rates are changed, or if a part or all of the liner routes are to be ceased, such changes or cessation shall be published to the public 7 days in advance.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　水路运输经营者应当依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，优先运送处置突发事件所需的物资、设备、工具、应急救援人员和受到突发事件危害的人员，重点保障紧急、重要的军事运输。  
出现关系国计民生的紧急运输需求时，国务院交通运输主管部门按照国务院的部署，可以要求水路运输经营者优先运输需要紧急运输的物资。水路运输经营者应当按照要求及时运输。

**Article 23** Waterway transport operators shall, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant provisions of the State, give priority to the transport of materials, equipment, tools, emergency rescue personnel, and persons affected by emergencies needed for the handling of emergencies, and shall give key protection to urgent and important military transport.  
Where there is an emergency transport need relating to the national economy and the people's livelihood, the competent transport department of the State Council may, in accordance with the deployment of the State Council, require waterway transport operators to give priority to the transport of materials needing emergency transport. Waterway transport operators shall transport such materials in a timely manner in accordance with the requirements.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　水路运输经营者应当按照统计法律、行政法规的规定报送统计信息。

**Article 24** Waterway transport operators shall submit statistical information in accordance with the provisions of statistical laws and administrative regulations.

### 第四章　水路运输辅助业务

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　运输船舶的所有人、经营人可以委托船舶管理业务经营者为其提供船舶海务、机务管理等服务。

**Article 25** The owners or operators of transport vessels may entrust ship management business operators to provide them with services such as ship nautical affairs and engineering management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请经营船舶管理业务，申请人应当符合下列条件：  
（一）取得企业法人资格；  
（二）有健全的安全管理制度；  
（三）有与其申请管理的船舶运力相适应的海务、机务管理人员；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 26** To apply for operation of ship management business, an applicant shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having obtained the status of an enterprise legal person;  
(2) Having a sound safety management system;  
(3) Having nautical and engineering management personnel commensurate with the capacity of the vessels applied for management; and  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　经营船舶管理业务，应当经设区的市级以上地方人民政府负责水路运输管理的部门批准。  
申请经营船舶管理业务，应当向前款规定的部门提交申请书和证明申请人符合[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定条件的相关材料。  
受理申请的部门应当自受理申请之日起30个工作日内审查完毕，作出准予许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，发给船舶管理业务经营许可证件，并向国务院交通运输主管部门备案；不予许可的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 27** The operation of ship management business shall be subject to the approval of the department responsible for waterway transport management under the local people's government at or above the city with districts level.  
To apply for operation of ship management business, an applicant shall submit a written application and relevant materials proving that the applicant meets the conditions prescribed in Article 26 of these Regulations to the department mentioned in the preceding paragraph.  
The department receiving the application shall complete the examination within 30 working days from the date of receiving the application, and make a decision to grant or not to grant the permission. If the permission is granted, it shall issue a ship management business operation license and report to the competent transport department of the State Council for record; if the permission is not granted, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　船舶管理业务经营者接受委托提供船舶管理服务，应当与委托人订立书面合同，并将合同报所在地海事管理机构备案。  
船舶管理业务经营者应当按照国家有关规定和合同约定履行有关船舶安全和防止污染的管理义务。

**Article 28** Where a ship management business operator accepts a commission to provide ship management services, it shall conclude a written contract with the principal and submit the contract to the local maritime administration agency for record.  
A ship management business operator shall perform its management obligations concerning ship safety and pollution prevention in accordance with relevant national provisions and the contract.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　水路运输经营者可以委托船舶代理、水路旅客运输代理、水路货物运输代理业务的经营者，代办船舶进出港手续等港口业务，代为签订运输合同，代办旅客、货物承揽业务以及其他水路运输代理业务。

**Article 29** Waterway transport operators may entrust operators of ship agency, waterway passenger transport agency, or waterway cargo transport agency businesses to handle port business such as ship entry and exit formalities, to conclude transport contracts on their behalf, and to handle passenger and cargo solicitation business and other waterway transport agency business.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　船舶代理、水路旅客运输代理业务的经营者应当自企业设立登记之日起15个工作日内，向所在地设区的市级人民政府负责水路运输管理的部门备案。

**Article 30** Operators of ship agency and waterway passenger transport agency businesses shall, within 15 working days from the date of enterprise establishment registration, report to the department responsible for waterway transport management under the local people's government at the city with districts level for record.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　船舶代理、水路旅客运输代理、水路货物运输代理业务的经营者接受委托提供代理服务，应当与委托人订立书面合同，按照国家有关规定和合同约定办理代理业务，不得强行代理，不得为未依法取得水路运输业务经营许可或者超越许可范围的经营者办理代理业务。

**Article 31** Where operators of ship agency, waterway passenger transport agency, or waterway cargo transport agency businesses accept a commission to provide agency services, they shall conclude a written contract with the principal, handle the agency business in accordance with relevant national provisions and the contract, shall not force agency, and shall not handle agency business for operators that have not obtained an administrative license for waterway transport business according to law or that exceed the scope of the license.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　[本条例第十二条](#art-12)、第十七条的规定适用于船舶管理业务经营者。[本条例第十一条](#art-11)、第二十四条的规定适用于船舶管理、船舶代理、水路旅客运输代理和水路货物运输代理业务经营活动。  
国务院交通运输主管部门应当依照本条例的规定制定水路运输辅助业务的具体管理办法。

**Article 32** The provisions of Articles 12 and 17 of these Regulations shall apply to ship management business operators. The provisions of Articles 11 and 24 of these Regulations shall apply to the business operation activities of ship management, ship agency, waterway passenger transport agency, and waterway cargo transport agency.  
The competent transport department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, formulate specific management measures for waterway transport auxiliary services.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　未经许可擅自经营或者超越许可范围经营水路运输业务或者国内船舶管理业务的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令停止经营，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足3万元的，处3万元以上15万元以下的罚款。

**Article 33** Where a person engages in waterway transport business or domestic ship management business without permission or beyond the scope of permission, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order the cessation of the operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　水路运输经营者使用未取得船舶营运证件的船舶从事水路运输的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令该船停止经营，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足2万元的，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 34** Where a waterway transport operator uses a vessel that has not obtained a ship operation certificate to engage in waterway transport, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order the vessel to cease operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　水路运输经营者未经国务院交通运输主管部门许可或者超越许可范围使用外国籍船舶经营水路运输业务，或者外国的企业、其他经济组织和个人经营或者以租用中国籍船舶或者舱位等方式变相经营水路运输业务的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令停止经营，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足20万元的，处20万元以上100万元以下的罚款。

**Article 35** Where a waterway transport operator, without permission from the competent transport department of the State Council or beyond the scope of permission, uses vessels of foreign nationality to operate waterway transport business, or where a foreign enterprise, other economic organization, or individual operates or operates waterway transport business in a disguised form by chartering Chinese vessels or space, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order the cessation of the operation, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 200,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　以欺骗或者贿赂等不正当手段取得本条例规定的行政许可的，由原许可机关撤销许可，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；国务院交通运输主管部门或者负责水路运输管理的部门自撤销许可之日起3年内不受理其对该项许可的申请。

**Article 36** Where a person obtains an administrative license prescribed in these Regulations by fraudulent or bribery means or other illegitimate means, the original licensing authority shall revoke the license and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the competent transport department of the State Council or the department responsible for waterway transport management shall not accept any application for such license from that person within three years from the date of revocation.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　出租、出借、倒卖本条例规定的行政许可证件或者以其他方式非法转让本条例规定的行政许可的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足3万元的，处3万元以上15万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原许可机关吊销相应的许可证件。  
伪造、变造、涂改本条例规定的行政许可证件的，由负责水路运输管理的部门没收伪造、变造、涂改的许可证件，处3万元以上15万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 37** Where a person rents, lends, or scalps the administrative license documents prescribed in these Regulations, or illegally transfers the administrative license prescribed in these Regulations by other means, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order rectification, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke the corresponding license documents.  
Where a person forges, alters, or defaces the administrative license documents prescribed in these Regulations, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall confiscate the forged, altered, or defaced license documents, impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　水路运输经营者有下列情形之一的，由海事管理机构依法予以处罚：  
（一）未按照规定配备船员或者未使船舶处于适航状态；  
（二）超越船舶核定载客定额或者核定载重量载运旅客或者货物；  
（三）使用货船载运旅客；  
（四）使用未取得危险货物适装证书的船舶运输危险货物。

**Article 38** Where a waterway transport operator is under any of the following circumstances, the maritime administration agency shall impose a penalty according to law:  
(1) Failing to man the vessel according to the provisions or failing to keep the vessel in a seaworthy condition;  
(2) Carrying passengers or cargo exceeding the vessel's certified passenger capacity or deadweight tonnage;  
(3) Using a cargo vessel to carry passengers;  
(4) Using a vessel that has not obtained a dangerous goods suitability certificate to transport dangerous goods.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　水路旅客运输业务经营者未为其经营的客运船舶投保承运人责任保险或者取得相应的财务担保的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令限期改正，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，由原许可机关吊销该客运船舶的船舶营运许可证件。

**Article 39** Where a waterway passenger transport business operator fails to take out carrier liability insurance or obtain corresponding financial guarantee for the passenger vessels it operates, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the operator fails to rectify within the time limit, the original licensing authority shall revoke the ship operation certificate of that passenger vessel.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　班轮运输业务经营者未提前向社会公布所使用的船舶、班期、班次和运价或者其变更信息的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令改正，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 40** Where a liner transport business operator fails to publish in advance to the public the vessels used, schedules, frequencies, and freight rates or changes thereof, the department responsible for waterway transport management shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　旅客班轮运输业务经营者自取得班轮航线经营许可之日起60日内未开航的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令改正；拒不改正的，由原许可机关撤销该项经营许可。

**Article 41** Where a passenger liner transport business operator fails to commence operation within 60 days from the date of obtaining the administrative license for the liner route, the department in charge of waterway transport management shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the original licensing authority shall revoke the administrative license.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　水路运输、船舶管理业务经营者取得许可后，不再具备本条例规定的许可条件的，由负责水路运输管理的部门责令限期整改；在规定期限内整改仍不合格的，由原许可机关撤销其经营许可。

**Article 42** After obtaining the license, if a waterway transport or vessel management business operator no longer meets the licensing conditions prescribed in these Regulations, the department in charge of waterway transport management shall order it to make rectification within a specified time limit; if the rectification is still not up to standard within the specified time limit, the original licensing authority shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　负责水路运输管理的国家工作人员在水路运输管理活动中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，不依法履行职责的，依法给予处分。

**Article 43** Where any state functionary in charge of waterway transport management abuses his power, neglects his duty, engages in malpractice for personal gain, or fails to perform his duties according to law in waterway transport management activities, he shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 44** Any violation of the provisions of these Regulations that constitutes a violation of public security administration shall be given public security administration penalties according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　载客12人以下的客运船舶以及乡、镇客运渡船运输的管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府另行制定。

**Article 45** The administrative measures for the transport of passenger ships carrying 12 or fewer passengers and township passenger ferries shall be separately formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本条例自2013年1月1日起施行。1987年5月12日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国水路运输管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 46** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2013. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Waterway Transport promulgated by the State Council on May 12, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously.
