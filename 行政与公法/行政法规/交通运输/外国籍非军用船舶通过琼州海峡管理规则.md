# 外国籍非军用船舶通过琼州海峡管理规则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1964-06-08  
**生效日期**：1964-06-08

> 外国籍非军用船舶通过琼州海峡管理规则 (1964年6月8日国务院公布　自公布之日起施行)

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国政府关于领海的声明》，琼州海峡是中国的内海，一切外国籍军用船舶不得通过，一切外国籍非军用船舶如需通过，必须按照本规则的规定申请批准。

**Article 1** In accordance with the Declaration of the Government of the People's Republic of China on the Territorial Sea, the Qiongzhou Strait is the inland sea of China. All foreign military vessels shall not pass through, and all foreign non-military vessels, if they need to pass through, must apply for approval in accordance with the provisions of these Rules.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　为便利对琼州海峡进行管理，设“中华人民共和国琼州海峡管理处”(以下简称琼州海峡管理处)。  
本规则由琼州海峡管理处监督执行。

**Article 2** For the convenience of managing the Qiongzhou Strait, the "Qiongzhou Strait Administration of the People's Republic of China" (hereinafter referred to as the Qiongzhou Strait Administration) is established.  
These Rules shall be supervised and enforced by the Qiongzhou Strait Administration.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　琼州海峡管理处对琼州海峡管理的区域(以下简称管理区)暂定如下：木栏头灯桩(约北纬二十度零九分三十七秒、东经一百一十度四十一分)与生狗吼沙灯桩(约北纬二十度二十六分、东经一百一十度三十分二十二秒)联线(简称东线)以西，尾角灯桩(约北纬二十度十三分三十秒、东经一百零九度五十五分三十秒)与临高角灯桩(约北纬二十度零零分二十二秒、东经一百零九度四十二分零六秒)联线(简称西线)以东的水域。

**Article 3** The area under the management of the Qiongzhou Strait Administration (hereinafter referred to as the management area) is provisionally defined as follows: the waters west of the line connecting the Mulan Tou Light Beacon (approximately 20°09'37"N, 110°41'E) and the Shenggouhou Sha Light Beacon (approximately 20°26'N, 110°30'22"E) (hereinafter referred to as the Eastern Line), and east of the line connecting the Wei Jiao Light Beacon (approximately 20°13'30"N, 109°55'30"E) and the Lingao Jiao Light Beacon (approximately 20°00'22"N, 109°42'06"E) (hereinafter referred to as the Western Line).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国籍非军用船舶如需通过琼州海峡，必须办理以下手续：  
(一)在进入管理区四十八小时以前或在开出港启航以前，将船名、国籍、总吨、航速、船身颜色、烟囱标志以及何日、何时由何地开往何处等情况，详细电报琼州海峡管理处，请求过峡。  
(二)接到批准过峡的通知后，应在进入管理区二十四小时以前或在开出港启航后二小时内，向琼州海峡管理处确报进入管理区的时间。  
以上请求过峡和批准过峡等的来往电报，均由中国外轮代理公司海口分公司代转。

**Article 4** If a foreign non-military vessel needs to pass through the Qiongzhou Strait, it must complete the following formalities:  
(1) At least 48 hours before entering the management area or before departure from the port of departure, the vessel shall report in detail by telegraph to the Qiongzhou Strait Administration its name, nationality, gross tonnage, speed, hull color, funnel mark, and the date, time, place of departure and destination, requesting permission to pass through the strait.  
(2) After receiving the notice of approval to pass through the strait, the vessel shall confirm by telegraph to the Qiongzhou Strait Administration the time of entering the management area at least 24 hours before entering such area or within two hours after departure from the port of departure.  
All the above telecommunications for requesting and approving passage through the strait shall be transmitted through the Haikou Branch of China Ocean Shipping Agency.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　琼州海峡管理处对于已经批准通过海峡的外国籍非军用船舶，认为必要时，可以临时通知禁止通过。

**Article 5** The Qiongzhou Strait Administration may, when it deems necessary, temporarily notify a foreign non-military vessel that has been approved to pass through the strait that it is prohibited from passing through.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　外国籍非军用船舶通过管理区的时间，一律以白天为限，均应在日出后进入管理区，日落前全部通过管理区。琼州海峡管理处根据申请过峡外国籍非军用船舶的航速，核准其进出海峡的具体时间。

**Article 6** The time for foreign non-military vessels to pass through the management area shall be limited to daytime. All such vessels shall enter the management area after sunrise and completely pass through the management area before sunset. The Qiongzhou Strait Administration shall, based on the speed of the foreign non-military vessel applying to pass through the strait, approve the specific time for its entry into and exit from the strait.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　外国籍非军用船舶进出琼州海峡一律走中水道，但经琼州海峡管理处特别许可的不在此限。

**Article 7** All foreign non-military vessels entering and leaving the Qiongzhou Strait shall use the middle channel, unless otherwise specially permitted by the Qiongzhou Strait Administration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国籍非军用船舶通过管理区时，应在下列规定的航区范围内航行：自东线距木栏头灯桩四浬处至西线距临高角灯桩四浬处联线以北，自东线距木栏头灯桩六浬处至排尾角灯桩正南四浬处继至西线距临高角灯桩十四浬处联线以南的水域。

**Article 8** When passing through the management area, a foreign non-military vessel shall navigate within the navigational zone specified as follows: the waters north of the line from a point 4 nautical miles from the Mulan Tou Light Beacon on the Eastern Line to a point 4 nautical miles from the Lingao Jiao Light Beacon on the Western Line, and south of the line from a point 6 nautical miles from the Mulan Tou Light Beacon on the Eastern Line to a point 4 nautical miles due south of the Paiwei Jiao Light Beacon and then to a point 14 nautical miles from the Lingao Jiao Light Beacon on the Western Line.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国籍非军用船舶通过琼州海峡，必须严格按照所报时间及规定的航区通行。在进入管理区和在管理区通行时，如见有从岸上或舰艇上发出的信号，应立即回答并无条件地执行信号要求。由于不遵守上述规定而发生的后果概由船方自行负责。

**Article 9** A foreign non-military vessel passing through the Qiongzhou Strait must strictly follow the reported time and the prescribed navigational zone. When entering and navigating within the management area, if it sees signals issued from the shore or from naval vessels, it shall immediately respond and unconditionally comply with the requirements of the signals. Any consequences arising from failure to comply with the above provisions shall be solely the responsibility of the vessel.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　外国籍非军用船舶通过琼州海峡时，不得使用雷达。如在航行中遇浓雾、暴雨等视线恶劣的情况，需要使用雷达时，应向琼州海峡管理处提出报告，说明理由以及当时的船位、航速等，经批准后方准使用。如当时情况紧急，危及船舶航行安全时，可以一面申报一面使用，并在事后，将使用起迄时间和经过情况详细报告琼州海峡管理处备案。

**Article 10** When passing through the Qiongzhou Strait, a foreign non-military vessel shall not use radar. If it encounters heavy fog, rainstorm, or other conditions of poor visibility during navigation and needs to use radar, it shall submit a report to the Qiongzhou Strait Administration, stating the reasons and the vessel's position, speed, etc., and may use radar only after approval. If the situation is urgent and endangers the safety of the vessel's navigation, it may apply while using radar, and shall thereafter report in detail to the Qiongzhou Strait Administration the starting and ending times of radar use and the circumstances for the record.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外国籍非军用船舶通过琼州海峡时，不准进行照像、测量以及其他违反中华人民共和国法令的行为。

**Article 11** When passing through the Qiongzhou Strait, a foreign non-military vessel shall not engage in photography, surveying, or any other acts that violate the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　外国籍非军用船舶违反本规则，按下列规定处理：  
(一)在未进入管理区之前，可以命令其停止进入管理区，由原路返航绕海南岛航行，或者俟其办妥过峡手续并经批准后，再行过峡。  
(二)如果已经进入管理区，可以命令其停航并监送至海口港进行检查，根据检查情况给予处分。在处理后，视情况可以准其通过管理区，或令其退出管理区，直到押送其退出海峡。

**Article 12** A foreign non-military vessel that violates these Rules shall be dealt with according to the following provisions:  
(1) Before it enters the management area, it may be ordered to stop entering the management area, return by the original route and navigate around Hainan Island, or wait until it has completed the passage formalities and obtained approval before passing through the strait.  
(2) If it has already entered the management area, it may be ordered to stop navigation and be escorted to Haikou Port for inspection, and a sanction shall be imposed based on the inspection results. After the sanction, the vessel may be permitted to pass through the management area or ordered to withdraw from the management area, up to being escorted out of the strait, depending on the circumstances.
