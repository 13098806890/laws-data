# 中华人民共和国民用航空器适航管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1987-05-03  
**生效日期**：1987-06-01

> （1987年5月4日国务院发布） 第一条　为保障民用航空安全，维护公众利益，促进民用航空事业的发展，特制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事民用航空器(含航空发动机和螺旋桨，下同)的设计、生产、使用和维修的单位或者个人，向中华人民共和国出口民用航空器的单位或者个人，以及在中华人民共和国境外维修在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器的单位或者个人，均须遵守本条例。 第三条　民用航空器的适航管理，是根据国家的有关规定，对民用航空器的设计、生产、使用和维修，实施以确保飞行安全为目的的技术鉴定和监督。 第四条　民用航空器的适航管理由中国民用航空局(以下简称民航局)负责。 第五条　民用航空器的适航管理，必须执行规定的适航标准和程序。 第六条　任何单位或者个人设计民用航空器，应当持航空工业部对该设计项目的审核批准文件，向民航局申请型号合格证。民航局接受型号合格证申请后，应当按照规定进行型号合格审定；审定合格的，颁发型号合格证。 第七条　任何单位或者个人生产民用航空器，应当具有必要的生产能力，并应当持本条例第六条规定的型号合格证，经航空工业部同意后，向民航局申请生产许可证。民航局接受生产许可证申请后，应当按照规定进行生产许可审定；审定合格的，颁发生产许可证，并按照规定颁发适航证。 任何单位或者个人未按照前款规定取得生产许可证的，均不得生产民用航空器。但本条例第八条规定的除外。 第八条　任何单位或者个人未取得生产许可证，但因特殊需要，申请生产民用航空器的，须经民航局批准。 按照前款规定生产的民用航空器，须经民航局逐一审查合格后，颁发适航证。 第九条　民用航空器必须具有民航局颁发的适航证，方可飞行。 民航局颁发的适航证应当规定该民用航空器所适用的活动类别、证书的有效期限及安全所需的其他条件和限制。 第十条　持有民用航空器生产许可证的单位生产的民用航空器，经国务院有关主管部门批准需要出口时，由民航局签发出口适航证。 第十一条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的民用航空器必须具有国籍登记证。在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器，具有中华人民共和国国籍，国籍登记证由民航局颁发。民用航空器取得国籍登记证后，必须按照规定在该民用航空器的外表标明国籍登记识别标志。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保障民用航空安全，维护公众利益，促进民用航空事业的发展，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of ensuring civil aviation safety, safeguarding public interests, and promoting the development of civil aviation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事民用航空器(含航空发动机和螺旋桨，下同)的设计、生产、使用和维修的单位或者个人，向中华人民共和国出口民用航空器的单位或者个人，以及在中华人民共和国境外维修在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器的单位或者个人，均须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Units or individuals that engage in the design, production, use, and maintenance of civil aircraft (including aircraft engines and propellers, hereinafter the same) within the territory of the People's Republic of China, units or individuals that export civil aircraft to the People's Republic of China, and units or individuals that maintain civil aircraft registered in the People's Republic of China outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, shall all comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用航空器的适航管理，是根据国家的有关规定，对民用航空器的设计、生产、使用和维修，实施以确保飞行安全为目的的技术鉴定和监督。

**Article 3** The airworthiness management of civil aircraft is the technical appraisal and supervision implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on the design, production, use, and maintenance of civil aircraft, with the purpose of ensuring flight safety.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民用航空器的适航管理由中国民用航空局(以下简称民航局)负责。

**Article 4** The airworthiness management of civil aircraft shall be the responsibility of the Civil Aviation Administration of China (hereinafter referred to as the CAAC).

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民用航空器的适航管理，必须执行规定的适航标准和程序。

**Article 5** The airworthiness management of civil aircraft shall comply with the prescribed airworthiness standards and procedures.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位或者个人设计民用航空器，应当持航空工业部对该设计项目的审核批准文件，向民航局申请型号合格证。民航局接受型号合格证申请后，应当按照规定进行型号合格审定；审定合格的，颁发型号合格证。

**Article 6** Any unit or individual that designs a civil aircraft shall hold the document of examination and approval of the Ministry of Aviation Industry for the design project and apply to the CAAC for a type certificate. After accepting the application for a type certificate, the CAAC shall conduct type certification in accordance with the provisions; if the certification is passed, it shall issue a type certificate.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位或者个人生产民用航空器，应当具有必要的生产能力，并应当持[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定的型号合格证，经航空工业部同意后，向民航局申请生产许可证。民航局接受生产许可证申请后，应当按照规定进行生产许可审定；审定合格的，颁发生产许可证，并按照规定颁发适航证。  
任何单位或者个人未按照前款规定取得生产许可证的，均不得生产民用航空器。但[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的除外。

**Article 7** Any unit or individual that produces civil aircraft shall have the necessary production capacity, and shall hold the type certificate provided for in Article 6 of these Regulations and, with the consent of the Ministry of Aviation Industry, apply to the CAAC for a production license. After accepting the application for a production license, the CAAC shall conduct production license certification in accordance with the provisions; if the certification is passed, it shall issue a production license, and shall issue an airworthiness certificate in accordance with the provisions.  
Any unit or individual that has not obtained a production license in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall not produce civil aircraft, except as provided for in Article 8 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位或者个人未取得生产许可证，但因特殊需要，申请生产民用航空器的，须经民航局批准。  
按照前款规定生产的民用航空器，须经民航局逐一审查合格后，颁发适航证。

**Article 8** Any unit or individual that has not obtained a production license but, due to special needs, applies to produce civil aircraft shall be subject to approval by the CAAC.  
The civil aircraft produced in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be subject to individual examination and certification by the CAAC before an airworthiness certificate is issued.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民用航空器必须具有民航局颁发的适航证，方可飞行。  
民航局颁发的适航证应当规定该民用航空器所适用的活动类别、证书的有效期限及安全所需的其他条件和限制。

**Article 9** A civil aircraft shall have an airworthiness certificate issued by the CAAC before it can fly.  
The airworthiness certificate issued by the CAAC shall specify the category of activities for which the civil aircraft is suitable, the validity period of the certificate, and other conditions and restrictions required for safety.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　持有民用航空器生产许可证的单位生产的民用航空器，经国务院有关主管部门批准需要出口时，由民航局签发出口适航证。

**Article 10** When a civil aircraft produced by a unit holding a production license for civil aircraft needs to be exported with the approval of the relevant competent department of the State Council, the CAAC shall issue an export airworthiness certificate.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在中华人民共和国境内飞行的民用航空器必须具有国籍登记证。在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器，具有中华人民共和国国籍，国籍登记证由民航局颁发。民用航空器取得国籍登记证后，必须按照规定在该民用航空器的外表标明国籍登记识别标志。

**Article 11** A civil aircraft flying within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall have a nationality registration certificate. A civil aircraft registered in the People's Republic of China shall have the nationality of the People's Republic of China, and the nationality registration certificate shall be issued by the CAAC. After obtaining the nationality registration certificate, the civil aircraft shall, in accordance with the provisions, display the nationality registration identification marks on its exterior.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中华人民共和国的任何单位或者个人进口外国生产的任何型号的民用航空器，如系首次进口并用于民用航空活动时，出口民用航空器的单位或者个人必须向民航局申请型号审查。民航局接受申请后，应当按照规定对该型号民用航空器进行型号审查；审查合格的，颁发准予进口的型号认可证书。

**Article 12** When any unit or individual of the People's Republic of China imports a civil aircraft of any type produced abroad, if it is the first import and is used for civil aviation activities, the unit or individual exporting the civil aircraft shall apply to the CAAC for type review. After accepting the application, the CAAC shall conduct type review of that type of civil aircraft in accordance with the provisions; if the review is passed, it shall issue a type acceptance certificate permitting import.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　中华人民共和国的任何单位或者个人租用的外国民用航空器，必须经民航局对其原登记国颁发的适航证审查认可或者另行颁发适航证后，方可飞行。

**Article 13** A foreign civil aircraft leased by any unit or individual of the People's Republic of China shall be subject to the CAAC's review and recognition of the airworthiness certificate issued by its country of original registration, or the CAAC shall issue a separate airworthiness certificate, before it can fly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位或者个人的民用航空器取得适航证以后，必须按照民航局的有关规定和适航指令，使用和维修民用航空器，保证其始终处于持续适航状态。

**Article 14** After obtaining an airworthiness certificate, any unit or individual's civil aircraft shall be used and maintained in accordance with the relevant provisions and airworthiness directives of the CAAC to ensure that it remains in a state of continuous airworthiness at all times.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　加装或者改装已取得适航证的民用航空器，必须经民航局批准，涉及的重要部件、附件必须经民航局审定。

**Article 15** The addition or modification of a civil aircraft that has obtained an airworthiness certificate shall be subject to approval by the CAAC, and the important parts and accessories involved shall be subject to certification by the CAAC.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中华人民共和国境内和境外任何维修单位或者个人，承担在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器的维修业务的，必须向民航局申请维修许可证，经民航局对其维修设施、技术人员、质量管理系统审查合格，并颁发维修许可证后，方可从事批准范围内的维修业务活动。

**Article 16** Any maintenance unit or individual within or outside the territory of the People's Republic of China that undertakes the maintenance of civil aircraft registered in the People's Republic of China shall apply to the CAAC for a maintenance license. After the CAAC has examined and approved the maintenance facilities, technical personnel, and quality management system, and issued the maintenance license, such unit or individual may engage in maintenance business activities within the approved scope.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　负责维修并放行在中华人民共和国注册登记的民用航空器的维修技术人员，必须向民航局提出申请，经民航局或者其授权单位考核合格并取得维修人员执照或者相应的证明文件后，方可从事民用航空器的维修并放行工作。

**Article 17** Maintenance technical personnel responsible for the maintenance and release of civil aircraft registered in the People's Republic of China shall submit an application to the CAAC. After passing the examination conducted by the CAAC or its authorized unit and obtaining a maintenance personnel license or corresponding certification documents, such personnel may engage in the maintenance and release of civil aircraft.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民用航空器的适航审查应当收取费用。收费办法由民航局会同财政部制定。

**Article 18** Fees shall be charged for airworthiness certification of civil aircraft. The fee collection measures shall be formulated by the CAAC jointly with the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民航局有权对生产、使用、维修民用航空器的单位或者个人以及取得适航证的民用航空器进行定期检查或者抽查；经检查与抽查不合格的，民航局除按照本条例的有关规定对其处罚外，还可吊销其有关证件。

**Article 19** The CAAC shall have the power to conduct periodic inspections or spot checks on units or individuals that produce, use, or maintain civil aircraft, and on civil aircraft that have obtained airworthiness certificates; if any inspection or check fails, the CAAC may, in addition to imposing penalties in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Regulations, revoke the relevant certificates.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　使用民用航空器进行飞行活动的任何单位或者个人有下列情形之一的，民航局有权责令其停止飞行，并视情节轻重，处以罚款：  
一、民用航空器未取得适航证的；  
二、民用航空器适航证已经失效的；  
三、使用民用航空器超越适航证规定范围的。

**Article 20** Where any unit or individual using a civil aircraft for flight activities has any of the following circumstances, the CAAC shall have the power to order the suspension of the flight and, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine:  
(1) The civil aircraft has not obtained an airworthiness certificate;  
(2) The airworthiness certificate of the civil aircraft has become invalid;  
(3) The civil aircraft is used beyond the scope specified in the airworthiness certificate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　维修民用航空器的单位或者个人，有下列情形之一的，民航局有权责令其停止维修业务或者吊销其维修许可证，并视情节轻重，处以罚款：  
一、未取得维修许可证，擅自承接维修业务的；  
二、超过维修许可证规定的业务范围，承接维修业务的；  
三、由未取得维修人员执照的人员负责民用航空器的维修并放行的。

**Article 21** Where a unit or individual that maintains civil aircraft has any of the following circumstances, the CAAC shall have the power to order the suspension of the maintenance business or revoke the maintenance license, and depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine:  
(1) Undertaking maintenance business without obtaining a maintenance license;  
(2) Undertaking maintenance business beyond the scope specified in the maintenance license;  
(3) Having the maintenance and release of civil aircraft carried out by personnel who have not obtained a maintenance personnel license.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位或者个人违反[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定，擅自生产民用航空器的，民航局有权责令其停止生产，并视情节轻重，处以罚款。

**Article 22** Where any unit or individual violates the provisions of Article 7 of these Regulations and produces civil aircraft without authorization, the CAAC shall have the power to order the suspension of production and, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a fine.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　按照本条例受到处罚的单位的上级主管机关，应当根据民航局的建议对受罚单位的主要负责人或者直接责任人员给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** The competent authority at a higher level of a unit that has been penalized in accordance with these Regulations shall, based on the recommendation of the CAAC, impose administrative sanctions on the principal person in charge or the directly responsible personnel of the penalized unit; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民航局因适航管理工作的过失造成人身伤亡或者重大财产损失的，应当承担赔偿责任，并对直接责任人员给予行政处分；直接责任人员的行为构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** Where the CAAC causes personal injury or death or significant property loss due to negligence in airworthiness management, it shall bear compensation liability and shall impose administrative sanctions on the directly responsible personnel; if the act of the directly responsible personnel constitutes a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民航局从事适航管理的工作人员，利用职务之便营私舞弊的，应当给予行政处分；情节严重，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 25** Where a staff member of the CAAC engaged in airworthiness management engages in malpractice for personal gain by taking advantage of their position, an administrative sanction shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, the judicial authority shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　任何单位或者个人对民航局作出的罚款决定不服的，可以在接到罚款通知书之日起十五日内向民航局提请复议，也可以直接向人民法院起诉；期满不提请复议也不起诉又不执行的，民航局可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 26** Where any unit or individual disagrees with a fine decision made by the CAAC, it may, within fifteen days of receiving the notice of fine, apply for reconsideration to the CAAC, or directly file a prosecution with the People's Court; if it neither applies for reconsideration nor initiates prosecution within the time limit and fails to execute the decision, the CAAC may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民航局应当在广泛征求航空工业部及各有关部门意见的基础上，制定本条例的实施细则及有关技术标准。

**Article 27** The CAAC shall, on the basis of extensively soliciting opinions from the Ministry of Aviation Industry and relevant departments, formulate the implementing rules and relevant technical standards for these Regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例由民航局负责解释。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the CAAC.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本条例自一九八七年六月一日起施行。

**Article 29** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 1987.
