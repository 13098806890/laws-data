# 中华人民共和国搜寻援救民用航空器规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1992-12-28  
**生效日期**：1992-12-28

> (1992年12月8日国务院批准　1992年12月28日中国民用航空局令第29号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了及时有效地搜寻援救遇到紧急情况的民用航空器，避免或者减少人员伤亡和财产损失，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于中华人民共和国领域内以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定由中国承担搜寻援救工作的公海区域内搜寻援救民用航空器的活动。 第三条　海上搜寻援救民用航空器，除适用本规定外，并应当遵守国务院有关海上搜寻援救的规定。 第四条　搜寻援救民用航空器按照下列规定分工负责： (一)中国民用航空局(以下简称民航局)负责统一指导全国范围的搜寻援救民用航空器的工作； (二)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责本行政区域内陆地搜寻援救民用航空器的工作，民用航空地区管理局(以下简称地区管理局)予以协助； (三)国家海上搜寻援救组织负责海上搜寻援救民用航空器工作，有关部门予以配合。 第五条　民航局搜寻援救协调中心和地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心承担陆上搜寻援救民用航空器的协调工作。 第六条　中华人民共和国领域内以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定由中国承担搜寻援救工作的公海区域内为中华人民共和国民用航空搜寻援救区，该区域内划分若干地区民用航空搜寻援救区，具体地区划分范围由民航局公布。 第七条　使用航空器执行搜寻援救任务，以民用航空力量为主，民用航空搜寻援救力量不足的，由军队派出航空器给予支援。 第八条　为执行搜寻援救民用航空器的紧急任务，有关地方、部门、单位和人员必须积极行动，互相配合，努力完成任务；对执行搜寻援救任务成绩突出的单位和个人，由其上级机关给予奖励。 第二章　搜寻援救的准备 第九条　各地区管理局应当拟定在陆上使用航空器搜寻援救民用航空器的方案，经民航局批准后，报有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了及时有效地搜寻援救遇到紧急情况的民用航空器，避免或者减少人员伤亡和财产损失，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft in emergency situations in a timely and effective manner, and avoiding or reducing casualties and property losses.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于中华人民共和国领域内以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定由中国承担搜寻援救工作的公海区域内搜寻援救民用航空器的活动。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to activities of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft within the territory of the People's Republic of China and within the high seas areas where the People's Republic of China, under international treaties concluded or acceded to by it, undertakes the search and rescue work.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海上搜寻援救民用航空器，除适用本规定外，并应当遵守国务院有关海上搜寻援救的规定。

**Article 3** In addition to the application of these Provisions, the search and rescue of civil aircraft at sea shall also comply with the relevant provisions of the State Council on maritime search and rescue.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　搜寻援救民用航空器按照下列规定分工负责：  
(一)中国民用航空局(以下简称民航局)负责统一指导全国范围的搜寻援救民用航空器的工作；  
(二)省、自治区、直辖市人民政府负责本行政区域内陆地搜寻援救民用航空器的工作，民用航空地区管理局(以下简称地区管理局)予以协助；  
(三)国家海上搜寻援救组织负责海上搜寻援救民用航空器工作，有关部门予以配合。

**Article 4** The search and rescue of civil aircraft shall be carried out with division of responsibilities according to the following provisions:  
(1) The Civil Aviation Administration of China (hereinafter referred to as CAAC) shall be responsible for uniformly guiding the work of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft nationwide;  
(2) The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the work of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft on land within their respective administrative regions, and the Regional Administration of Civil Aviation (hereinafter referred to as the Regional Administration) shall provide assistance;  
(3) The national maritime search and rescue organization shall be responsible for the work of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft at sea, and relevant departments shall provide cooperation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民航局搜寻援救协调中心和地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心承担陆上搜寻援救民用航空器的协调工作。

**Article 5** The CAAC Search and Rescue Coordination Center and the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Centers shall undertake the coordination work for the search and rescue of civil aircraft on land.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中华人民共和国领域内以及中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定由中国承担搜寻援救工作的公海区域内为中华人民共和国民用航空搜寻援救区，该区域内划分若干地区民用航空搜寻援救区，具体地区划分范围由民航局公布。

**Article 6** The area within the territory of the People's Republic of China and the high seas areas where the People's Republic of China undertakes the search and rescue work under international treaties concluded or acceded to by it shall be the civil aviation search and rescue area of the People's Republic of China. Within this area, several regional civil aviation search and rescue areas shall be divided, and the specific scope of the regional divisions shall be published by CAAC.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　使用航空器执行搜寻援救任务，以民用航空力量为主，民用航空搜寻援救力量不足的，由军队派出航空器给予支援。

**Article 7** The execution of search and rescue missions using aircraft shall be primarily carried out by civil aviation forces; where civil aviation search and rescue forces are insufficient, the military shall dispatch aircraft to provide support.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　为执行搜寻援救民用航空器的紧急任务，有关地方、部门、单位和人员必须积极行动，互相配合，努力完成任务；对执行搜寻援救任务成绩突出的单位和个人，由其上级机关给予奖励。

**Article 8** For the purpose of executing urgent missions of searching for and rescuing civil aircraft, the relevant localities, departments, units, and personnel must take active actions, cooperate with each other, and make every effort to accomplish the tasks; units and individuals with outstanding achievements in executing search and rescue missions shall be rewarded by their superior organs.

### 第二章　搜寻援救的准备

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各地区管理局应当拟定在陆上使用航空器搜寻援救民用航空器的方案，经民航局批准后，报有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府备案。

**Article 9** Each Regional Administration shall formulate plans for using aircraft to search for and rescue civil aircraft on land, and after approval by CAAC, shall submit them to the relevant people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government for record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　沿海省、自治区、直辖市海上搜寻援救组织，应当拟定在海上使用船舶、航空器搜寻援救民用航空器的方案，经国家海上搜寻援救组织批准后，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和民航局备案，同时抄送有关地区管理局。

**Article 10** The maritime search and rescue organizations of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate plans for using vessels and aircraft to search for and rescue civil aircraft at sea, and after approval by the national maritime search and rescue organization, shall submit them to the provincial, autonomous regional, or municipal people's government and CAAC for record, and at the same time send a copy to the relevant Regional Administration.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　搜寻援救民用航空器方案应当包括下列内容：  
(一)使用航空器、船舶执行搜寻援救任务的单位，航空器、船舶的类型，以及日常准备工作的规定；  
(二)航空器使用的机场和船舶使用的港口，担任搜寻援救的区域和有关保障工作方面的规定；  
(三)执行海上搜寻援救任务的船舶、航空器协同配合方面的规定；  
(四)民用航空搜寻援救力量不足的，商请当地驻军派出航空器、舰艇支援的规定。

**Article 11** The plan for searching for and rescuing civil aircraft shall include the following contents:  
(1) The units to use aircraft and vessels to execute search and rescue missions, the types of aircraft and vessels, and provisions on daily preparation work;  
(2) The airports to be used by aircraft and the ports to be used by vessels, the areas assigned for search and rescue, and provisions on relevant support work;  
(3) Provisions on the coordination and cooperation between vessels and aircraft executing maritime search and rescue missions;  
(4) Where civil aviation search and rescue forces are insufficient, provisions on consulting with local military units to dispatch aircraft and warships for support.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地区管理局和沿海省、自治区、直辖市海上搜寻援救组织应当按照批准的方案定期组织演习。

**Article 12** The Regional Administrations and the maritime search and rescue organizations of coastal provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall conduct regular exercises in accordance with the approved plans.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　搜寻援救民用航空器的通信联络，应当符合下列规定：  
(一)民用航空空中交通管制单位和担任搜寻援救任务的航空器，应当配备121.5兆赫航空紧急频率的通信设备，并逐步配备243兆赫航空紧急频率的通信设备；  
(二)担任海上搜寻援救任务的航空器，应当配备2182千赫海上遇险频率的通信设备；  
(三)担任搜寻援救任务的部分航空器，应当配备能够向遇险民用航空器所发出的航空器紧急示位信标归航设备，以及在156.8兆赫(调频)频率上同搜寻援救船舶联络的通信设备。

**Article 13** The communication and liaison for searching for and rescuing civil aircraft shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Civil aviation air traffic control units and aircraft undertaking search and rescue missions shall be equipped with communication equipment operating on the aeronautical emergency frequency of 121.5 MHz, and shall gradually be equipped with communication equipment operating on the aeronautical emergency frequency of 243 MHz;  
(2) Aircraft undertaking maritime search and rescue missions shall be equipped with communication equipment operating on the maritime distress frequency of 2182 kHz;  
(3) Some of the aircraft undertaking search and rescue missions shall be equipped with homing equipment for the emergency locator transmitter emitted by the distressed civil aircraft, as well as communication equipment for liaison with search and rescue vessels on the frequency of 156.8 MHz (FM).

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当同有关省、自治区、直辖市海上搜寻援救组织建立直接的通信联络。

**Article 14** The Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall establish direct communication and liaison with the relevant maritime search and rescue organizations of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　向遇险待救人员空投救生物品，由执行搜寻援救任务的单位按照下列规定负责准备：  
(一)药物和急救物品为红色；  
(二)食品和水为蓝色；  
(三)防护服装和毯子为黄色；  
(四)其他物品为黑色；  
(五)一个容器或者包装内，装有上述多种物品时为混合色。  
每一容器或者包装内，应当装有用汉语、英语和另选一种语言的救生物品使用说明。

**Article 15** The airdropping of survival supplies to persons awaiting rescue in distress shall be prepared by the units executing search and rescue missions according to the following provisions:  
(1) Medicines and first-aid items shall be red;  
(2) Food and water shall be blue;  
(3) Protective clothing and blankets shall be yellow;  
(4) Other items shall be black;  
(5) When a container or package contains multiple items of the above types, it shall be a mixed color.  
Each container or package shall contain instructions for using the survival supplies in Chinese, English, and one other language.

### 第三章　搜寻援救的实施

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　发现或者收听到民用航空器遇到紧急情况的单位或者个人，应当立即通知有关地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心；发现失事的民用航空器，其位置在陆地的，并应当同时通知当地政府；其位置在海上的，并应当同时通知当地海上搜寻援救组织。

**Article 16** Any unit or individual that discovers or receives information about a civil aircraft in an emergency shall immediately notify the relevant Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center; if a crashed civil aircraft is discovered, and its location is on land, the local government shall also be notified at the same time; if its location is at sea, the local maritime search and rescue organization shall also be notified at the same time.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心收到民用航空器紧急情况的信息后，必须立即做出判断，分别按照[本规定第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条、第二十一条的规定，采取搜寻援救措施，并及时向民航局搜寻援救协调中心以及有关单位报告或者通报。

**Article 17** Upon receiving information about a civil aircraft in an emergency, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center must immediately make a judgment, and take search and rescue measures respectively in accordance with the provisions of Article 19, Article 20, and Article 21 of these Provisions, and shall promptly report or notify the CAAC Search and Rescue Coordination Center and relevant units.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定所指民用航空器的紧急情况分为以下三个阶段：  
(一)情况不明阶段是指民用航空器的安全出现下列令人疑虑的情况：  
1．空中交通管制部门在规定的时间内同民用航空器没有取得联络；  
2．民用航空器在规定的时间内没有降落，并且没有其他信息。  
(二)告警阶段是指民用航空器的安全出现下列令人担忧的情况：  
1．对情况不明阶段的民用航空器，仍然不能同其沟通联络；  
2．民用航空器的飞行能力受到损害，但是尚未达到迫降的程度；  
3．与已经允许降落的民用航空器失去通信联络，并且该民用航空器在预计降落时间后5分钟内没有降落。  
(三)遇险阶段是指确信民用航空器遇到下列紧急和严重危险，需要立即进行援救的情况：  
1．根据油量计算，告警阶段的民用航空器难以继续飞行；  
2．民用航空器的飞行能力受到严重损害，达到迫降程度；  
3．民用航空器已经迫降或者坠毁。

**Article 18** The emergency situations of civil aircraft referred to in these Provisions are divided into the following three phases:  
(1) The uncertainty phase refers to a situation where the safety of a civil aircraft gives rise to the following causes for concern:  
a. The air traffic control unit has not established contact with the civil aircraft within the prescribed time;  
b. The civil aircraft has not landed within the prescribed time and no other information is available.  
(2) The alert phase refers to a situation where the safety of a civil aircraft gives rise to the following causes for worry:  
a. For a civil aircraft in the uncertainty phase, contact still cannot be established with it;  
b. The flight capability of the civil aircraft has been impaired, but it has not yet reached the point of forced landing;  
c. Communication has been lost with a civil aircraft that has been cleared to land, and the civil aircraft has not landed within five minutes after the expected landing time.  
(3) The distress phase refers to a situation where there is a reasonable certainty that a civil aircraft is in the following serious and imminent danger and requires immediate rescue:  
a. According to fuel calculation, the civil aircraft in the alert phase cannot continue flight;  
b. The flight capability of the civil aircraft has been seriously impaired, reaching the point of forced landing;  
c. The civil aircraft has already made a forced landing or has crashed.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对情况不明阶段的民用航空器，地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当：  
(一)根据具体情况，确定搜寻的区域；  
(二)通知开放有关的航空电台、导航台、定向台和雷达等设施，搜寻掌握该民用航空器的空中位置；  
(三)尽速同该民用航空器沟通联络，进行有针对性的处置。

**Article 19** For a civil aircraft in the uncertainty phase, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall:  
(1) Determine the search area based on the specific circumstances;  
(2) Notify the activation of relevant aeronautical radio stations, navigation stations, direction-finding stations, radar, and other facilities to search for and ascertain the airborne location of the civil aircraft;  
(3) Establish contact with the civil aircraft as soon as possible and carry out targeted handling.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对告警阶段的民用航空器，地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当：  
(一)立即向有关单位发出告警通知；  
(二)要求担任搜寻援救任务的航空器、船舶立即进入待命执行任务状态；  
(三)督促检查各种电子设施，对情况不明的民用航空器继续进行联络和搜寻；  
(四)根据该民用航空器飞行能力受损情况和机长的意见，组织引导其在就近机场降落；  
(五)会同接受降落的机场，迅速查明预计降落时间后五分钟内还没有降落的民用航空器的情况并进行处理。

**Article 20** For a civil aircraft in the alert phase, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall:  
(1) Immediately issue an alert notice to relevant units;  
(2) Require aircraft and vessels assigned for search and rescue missions to immediately enter a standby state for executing tasks;  
(3) Supervise and inspect various electronic facilities, and continue to attempt contact and searching for the civil aircraft in the uncertainty phase;  
(4) Based on the extent of impairment of the civil aircraft's flight capability and the opinion of the pilot-in-command, organize and guide it to land at the nearest airport;  
(5) Together with the airport accepting the landing, promptly ascertain the situation of the civil aircraft that has not landed within five minutes after the expected landing time and take action.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对遇险阶段的民用航空器，地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当：  
(一)立即向有关单位发出民用航空器遇险的通知；  
(二)对燃油已尽，位置仍然不明的民用航空器，分析其可能遇险的区域，并通知搜寻援救单位派人或者派航空器、船舶，立即进行搜寻援救；  
(三)对飞行能力受到严重损害、达到迫降程度的民用航空器，通知搜寻援救单位派航空器进行护航，或者根据预定迫降地点，派人或者派航空器、船舶前往援救；  
(四)对已经迫降或者失事的民用航空器，其位置在陆地的，立即报告省、自治区、直辖市人民政府；其位置在海上的，立即通报沿海有关省、自治区、直辖市的海上搜寻援救组织。

**Article 21** For a civil aircraft in the distress phase, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall:  
(1) Immediately issue a notice of distress of the civil aircraft to relevant units;  
(2) For a civil aircraft that has exhausted its fuel and whose location remains unknown, analyze the area where it may be in distress, and notify search and rescue units to dispatch personnel, aircraft, or vessels for immediate search and rescue;  
(3) For a civil aircraft whose flight capability has been seriously impaired, reaching the point of forced landing, notify search and rescue units to dispatch aircraft for escort, or, based on the predetermined forced landing location, dispatch personnel, aircraft, or vessels to proceed for rescue;  
(4) For a civil aircraft that has already made a forced landing or crashed, if its location is on land, immediately report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; if its location is at sea, immediately notify the relevant coastal maritime search and rescue organizations of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府或者沿海省、自治区、直辖市海上搜寻援救组织收到关于民用航空器迫降或者失事的报告或者通报后，应当立即组织有关方面和当地驻军进行搜寻援救，并指派现场负责人。

**Article 22** Upon receiving a report or notification concerning a forced landing or crash of a civil aircraft, the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the maritime search and rescue organization of a coastal province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, shall immediately organize relevant parties and local military units to conduct search and rescue, and designate an on-site commander.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　现场负责人的主要职责是：  
(一)组织抢救幸存人员；  
(二)对民用航空器采取措施防火、灭火；  
(三)保护好民用航空器失事现场；为抢救人员或者灭火必须变动现场时，应当进行拍照或者录相；  
(四)保护好失事的民用航空器及机上人员的财物。

**Article 23** The main duties of the on-site commander are:  
(1) Organize the rescue of survivors;  
(2) Take measures to prevent and extinguish fires on the civil aircraft;  
(3) Protect the scene of the civil aircraft accident; if it is necessary to alter the scene for the purpose of rescuing persons or extinguishing fires, photographs or video recordings shall be taken;  
(4) Protect the crashed civil aircraft and the property of persons on board.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　指派的现场负责人未到达现场的，由第一个到达现场的援救单位的有关人员担任现场临时负责人，行使[本规定第二十三条](#art-23)规定的职责，并负责向到达后的现场负责人移交工作。

**Article 24** If the designated on-site commander has not yet arrived at the scene, the relevant personnel of the first rescue unit to arrive at the scene shall serve as the temporary on-site commander, exercise the duties specified in Article 23 of these Provisions, and be responsible for handing over work to the on-site commander upon their arrival.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对处于紧急情况下的民用航空器，地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当设法将已经采取的援救措施通报该民用航空器机组。

**Article 25** For a civil aircraft in an emergency, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall take steps to notify the crew of the civil aircraft of the rescue measures that have been taken.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　执行搜寻援救任务的航空器与船舶、遇险待救人员、搜寻援救工作组之间，应当使用无线电进行联络。条件不具备或者无线电联络失效的，应当依照本规定附录规定的国际通用的《搜寻援救的信号》进行联络。

**Article 26** Communication between aircraft and vessels executing search and rescue missions, persons awaiting rescue in distress, and search and rescue working groups shall be conducted by radio. Where conditions do not permit or radio communication fails, communication shall be conducted in accordance with the internationally used "Search and Rescue Signals" specified in the appendix to these Provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民用航空器的紧急情况已经不存在或者可以结束搜寻援救工作的，地区管理局搜寻援救协调中心应当按照规定程序及时向有关单位发出解除紧急情况的通知。

**Article 27** If the emergency situation of a civil aircraft no longer exists, or the search and rescue work may be terminated, the Regional Administration Search and Rescue Coordination Center shall, in accordance with the prescribed procedures, promptly issue a notice of cancellation of the emergency to the relevant units.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对违反本规定，有下列行为之一的人员，由其所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不积极行动配合完成搜寻援救任务，造成重大损失的；  
(二)不积极履行职责或者不服从指挥，致使损失加重的；  
(三)玩忽职守，对民用航空器紧急情况判断、处置不当，贻误时机，造成损失的。

**Article 28** Any person who violates these Provisions and commits any of the following acts shall be given administrative sanctions by the unit to which they belong or by the superior organ; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to take active actions to cooperate in completing search and rescue missions, causing heavy losses;  
(2) Failing to actively perform duties or failing to obey commands, thereby increasing the losses;  
(3) Being negligent in their duties, improperly judging or handling the emergency situation of a civil aircraft, missing opportunities, and causing losses.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　航空器执行搜寻援救任务所需经费，国家可以给予一定补贴。具体补贴办法由有关部门会同财政部门协商解决。

**Article 29** The State may provide certain subsidies for the funds required for aircraft to execute search and rescue missions. The specific subsidy measures shall be resolved by the relevant departments through consultation with the financial departments.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本规定由民航局负责解释。

**Article 30** These Provisions shall be interpreted by CAAC.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 31** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
