# 无人驾驶航空器飞行管理暂行条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-05-31  
**生效日期**：2024-01-01

> 第六条　无人驾驶航空器有关行业协会应当通过制定、实施团体标准等方式加强行业自律，宣传无人驾驶航空器管理法律法规及有关知识，增强有关单位和人员依法开展无人驾驶航空器飞行以及有关活动的意识。 第二章　民用无人驾驶航空器及操控员管理 第七条　国务院标准化行政主管部门和国务院其他有关部门按照职责分工组织制定民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产和使用的国家标准、行业标准。 第八条　从事中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、进口、飞行和维修活动，应当依法向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得适航许可。 从事微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、进口、飞行、维修以及组装、拼装活动，无需取得适航许可，但相关产品应当符合产品质量法律法规的有关规定以及有关强制性国家标准。 从事民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、使用活动，应当符合国家有关实名登记激活、飞行区域限制、应急处置、网络信息安全等规定，并采取有效措施减少大气污染物和噪声排放。 第九条　民用无人驾驶航空器系统生产者应当按照国务院工业和信息化主管部门的规定为其生产的无人驾驶航空器设置唯一产品识别码。 微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的生产者应当在无人驾驶航空器机体标注产品类型以及唯一产品识别码等信息，在产品外包装显著位置标明守法运行要求和风险警示。 第十条　民用无人驾驶航空器所有者应当依法进行实名登记，具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门会同有关部门制定。 涉及境外飞行的民用无人驾驶航空器，应当依法进行国籍登记。 第十一条　使用除微型以外的民用无人驾驶航空器从事飞行活动的单位应当具备下列条件，并向国务院民用航空主管部门或者地区民用航空管理机构（以下统称民用航空管理部门）申请取得民用无人驾驶航空器运营合格证（以下简称运营合格证）： （一）有实施安全运营所需的管理机构、管理人员和符合本条例规定的操控人员； （二）有符合安全运营要求的无人驾驶航空器及有关设施、设备； （三）有实施安全运营所需的管理制度和操作规程，保证持续具备按照制度和规程实施安全运营的能力； （四）从事经营性活动的单位，还应当为营利法人。

### 第二章　民用无人驾驶航空器及操控员管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院标准化行政主管部门和国务院其他有关部门按照职责分工组织制定民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产和使用的国家标准、行业标准。

**Article 7** The standardization administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, according to their respective functions and duties, organize the formulation of national and industry standards for the design, production and use of civil unmanned aircraft systems.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　从事中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、进口、飞行和维修活动，应当依法向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得适航许可。  
从事微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、进口、飞行、维修以及组装、拼装活动，无需取得适航许可，但相关产品应当符合产品质量法律法规的有关规定以及有关强制性国家标准。  
从事民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、使用活动，应当符合国家有关实名登记激活、飞行区域限制、应急处置、网络信息安全等规定，并采取有效措施减少大气污染物和噪声排放。

**Article 8** A person engaged in the design, production, import, flight and maintenance of medium-sized or large civil unmanned aircraft systems shall apply to the civil aviation主管部门 of the State Council for airworthiness certification according to law.  
A person engaged in the design, production, import, flight, maintenance, assembly and integration of micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft systems need not obtain airworthiness certification, provided that the relevant products conform to the relevant provisions of laws and regulations on product quality and the relevant compulsory national standards.  
A person engaged in the design, production or use of civil unmanned aircraft systems shall comply with the state provisions on real-name registration activation, flight area restrictions, emergency response, network information security, etc., and take effective measures to reduce the discharge of atmospheric pollutants and noise.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民用无人驾驶航空器系统生产者应当按照国务院工业和信息化主管部门的规定为其生产的无人驾驶航空器设置唯一产品识别码。  
微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的生产者应当在无人驾驶航空器机体标注产品类型以及唯一产品识别码等信息，在产品外包装显著位置标明守法运行要求和风险警示。

**Article 9** A producer of civil unmanned aircraft systems shall set a unique product identification code for the unmanned aircraft it produces in accordance with the provisions of the competent industry and information technology department of the State Council.  
A producer of micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft systems shall mark on the airframe the product type and the unique product identification code and other information, and indicate on the prominent position of the outer package the requirements for lawful operation and risk warnings.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民用无人驾驶航空器所有者应当依法进行实名登记，具体办法由国务院民用航空主管部门会同有关部门制定。  
涉及境外飞行的民用无人驾驶航空器，应当依法进行国籍登记。

**Article 10** The owner of a civil unmanned aircraft shall carry out real-name registration according to law. The specific measures shall be formulated by the civil aviation authority of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments.  
A civil unmanned aircraft involved in overseas flight shall undergo nationality registration according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　使用除微型以外的民用无人驾驶航空器从事飞行活动的单位应当具备下列条件，并向国务院民用航空主管部门或者地区民用航空管理机构（以下统称民用航空管理部门）申请取得民用无人驾驶航空器运营合格证（以下简称运营合格证）：  
（一）有实施安全运营所需的管理机构、管理人员和符合本条例规定的操控人员；  
（二）有符合安全运营要求的无人驾驶航空器及有关设施、设备；  
（三）有实施安全运营所需的管理制度和操作规程，保证持续具备按照制度和规程实施安全运营的能力；  
（四）从事经营性活动的单位，还应当为营利法人。  
民用航空管理部门收到申请后，应当进行运营安全评估，根据评估结果依法作出许可或者不予许可的决定。予以许可的，颁发运营合格证；不予许可的，书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
使用最大起飞重量不超过150千克的农用无人驾驶航空器在农林牧渔区域上方的适飞空域内从事农林牧渔作业飞行活动（以下称常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动），无需取得运营合格证。  
取得运营合格证后从事经营性通用航空飞行活动，以及从事常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动，无需取得通用航空经营许可证和运行合格证。

**Article 11** A unit that uses a civil unmanned aircraft other than a micro one to conduct flight activities shall meet the following conditions and apply to the civil aviation authority of the State Council or the regional civil aviation administration (hereinafter collectively referred to as the civil aviation administrative department) for obtaining a civil unmanned aircraft operation certificate (hereinafter referred to as the operation certificate):  
(1) having the management agencies, management personnel and operators that meet the requirements of these Regulations for safe operation;  
(2) having unmanned aircraft and related facilities and equipment that meet the requirements for safe operation;  
(3) having the management systems and operating procedures necessary for safe operation, and ensuring the continuous capability to implement safe operation in accordance with such systems and procedures;  
(4) a unit engaged for-profit activities shall also be a for-profit legal person.  
Upon receipt of an application, the civil aviation administrative department shall conduct an operational safety assessment and, based on the assessment result, make a decision on whether to grant or deny the license according to law. If granted, the operation certificate shall be issued; if denied, the applicant shall be notified in writing and the reasons shall be stated.  
A unit that uses an agricultural unmanned aircraft with a maximum take-off weight not exceeding 150 kilograms to conduct agricultural, forestry, animal husbandry or fishery operation flight activities in the flyable airspace above the agricultural, forestry, animal husbandry or fishery areas (hereinafter referred to as conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities) need not obtain an operation certificate.  
After obtaining an operation certificate, a unit that engages in for-profit general aviation flight activities or conducts conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities need not obtain a general aviation business license or a flight operations certificate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　使用民用无人驾驶航空器从事经营性飞行活动，以及使用小型、中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器从事非经营性飞行活动，应当依法投保责任保险。

**Article 12** A person who uses a civil unmanned aircraft to engage in for-profit flight activities, or uses a small, medium-sized or large civil unmanned aircraft to engage in non-profit flight activities, shall take out liability insurance according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统投放市场后，发现存在缺陷的，其生产者、进口商应当停止生产、销售，召回缺陷产品，并通知有关经营者、使用者停止销售、使用。生产者、进口商未依法实施召回的，由国务院市场监督管理部门依法责令召回。  
中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器系统不能持续处于适航状态的，由国务院民用航空主管部门依照有关适航管理的规定处理。

**Article 13** If a defect is found in a micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft system after being placed on the market, its producer or importer shall stop production and sale, recall the defective product, and notify the relevant operators and users to stop sale and use. If the producer or importer fails to implement the recall according to law, the market supervision and administration department of the State Council shall order the recall according to law.  
If a medium-sized or large civil unmanned aircraft system fails to continuously maintain airworthiness status, the civil aviation authority of the State Council shall handle it in accordance with the relevant provisions on airworthiness management.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对已经取得适航许可的民用无人驾驶航空器系统进行重大设计更改并拟将其用于飞行活动的，应当重新申请取得适航许可。  
对微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统进行改装的，应当符合有关强制性国家标准。民用无人驾驶航空器系统的空域保持能力、可靠被监视能力、速度或者高度等出厂性能以及参数发生改变的，其所有者应当及时在无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台更新性能、参数信息。  
改装民用无人驾驶航空器的，应当遵守改装后所属类别的管理规定。

**Article 14** Where a major design change is made to a civil unmanned aircraft system that has obtained an airworthiness certification and it is intended to be used for flight activities, a new application for airworthiness certification shall be submitted.  
Where a micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft system is modified, the relevant compulsory national standards shall be complied with. If the airspace holding capability, reliable monitored capability, speed or altitude and other出厂性能 and parameters of the civil unmanned aircraft system change, the owner shall promptly update the performance and parameter information on the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform.  
Where a civil unmanned aircraft is modified, the management provisions applicable to the category after modification shall be complied with.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生产、维修、使用民用无人驾驶航空器系统，应当遵守无线电管理法律法规以及国家有关规定。但是，民用无人驾驶航空器系统使用国家无线电管理机构确定的特定无线电频率，且有关无线电发射设备取得无线电发射设备型号核准的，无需取得无线电频率使用许可和无线电台执照。

**Article 15** The production, maintenance and use of civil unmanned aircraft systems shall comply with the laws, regulations and relevant state provisions on radio management. However, if a civil unmanned aircraft system uses a specific radio frequency determined by the state radio regulatory authority and the relevant radio transmitting equipment has obtained type approval for radio transmitting equipment, no radio frequency use license or radio station license need be obtained.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　操控小型、中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行的人员应当具备下列条件，并向国务院民用航空主管部门申请取得相应民用无人驾驶航空器操控员（以下简称操控员）执照：  
（一）具备完全民事行为能力；  
（二）接受安全操控培训，并经民用航空管理部门考核合格；  
（三）无可能影响民用无人驾驶航空器操控行为的疾病病史，无吸毒行为记录；  
（四）近5年内无因危害国家安全、公共安全或者侵犯公民人身权利、扰乱公共秩序的故意犯罪受到刑事处罚的记录。  
从事常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动的人员无需取得操控员执照，但应当由农用无人驾驶航空器系统生产者按照国务院民用航空、农业农村主管部门规定的内容进行培训和考核，合格后取得操作证书。

**Article 16** A person who operates a small, medium-sized or large civil unmanned aircraft for flight shall meet the following conditions and apply to the civil aviation authority of the State Council for obtaining the corresponding civil unmanned aircraft operator's license (hereinafter referred to as the operator's license):  
(1) having full civil capacity;  
(2) having received safety operation training and passed the assessment conducted by the civil aviation administrative department;  
(3) having no medical history that may affect the operation of a civil unmanned aircraft, and no record of drug abuse;  
(4) having no record of criminal punishment within the past five years for intentional crimes endangering national security, public security, infringing upon citizens' personal rights, or disturbing public order.  
A person engaged in conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities need not obtain an operator's license, but shall be trained and assessed by the producer of the agricultural unmanned aircraft system in accordance with the contents prescribed by the civil aviation and agriculture and rural affairs authorities of the State Council, and obtain an operation certificate upon passing the assessment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　操控微型、轻型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行的人员，无需取得操控员执照，但应当熟练掌握有关机型操作方法，了解风险警示信息和有关管理制度。  
无民事行为能力人只能操控微型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行，限制民事行为能力人只能操控微型、轻型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行。无民事行为能力人操控微型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行或者限制民事行为能力人操控轻型民用无人驾驶航空器飞行，应当由符合前款规定条件的完全民事行为能力人现场指导。  
操控轻型民用无人驾驶航空器在无人驾驶航空器管制空域内飞行的人员，应当具有完全民事行为能力，并按照国务院民用航空主管部门的规定经培训合格。

**Article 17** A person who operates a micro or light civil unmanned aircraft for flight need not obtain an operator's license, but shall have a thorough command of the operation method for the relevant model, and understand the risk warning information and relevant management systems.  
A person without civil capacity may only operate a micro civil unmanned aircraft for flight; a person with limited civil capacity may only operate a micro or light civil unmanned aircraft for flight. When a person without civil capacity operates a micro civil unmanned aircraft or a person with limited civil capacity operates a light civil unmanned aircraft, the operation shall be guided on site by a person with full civil capacity who meets the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph.  
A person who operates a light civil unmanned aircraft for flight within the controlled airspace for unmanned aircraft shall have full civil capacity and shall have passed the training in accordance with the provisions of the civil aviation authority of the State Council.

### 第三章　空域和飞行活动管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　划设无人驾驶航空器飞行空域应当遵循统筹配置、安全高效原则，以隔离飞行为主，兼顾融合飞行需求，充分考虑飞行安全和公众利益。  
划设无人驾驶航空器飞行空域应当明确水平、垂直范围和使用时间。  
空中交通管理机构应当为无人驾驶航空器执行军事、警察、海关、应急管理飞行任务优先划设空域。

**Article 18** The delineation of flight airspace for unmanned aircraft shall follow the principles of overall planning and allocation, safety and efficiency, prioritize isolated flights, take into account the needs of integrated flights, and fully consider flight safety and public interests.  
The delineation of flight airspace for unmanned aircraft shall specify the horizontal and vertical scope and the time of use.  
The air traffic management authority shall give priority to delineating airspace for unmanned aircraft performing military, police, customs or emergency management flight missions.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家根据需要划设无人驾驶航空器管制空域（以下简称管制空域）。  
真高120米以上空域，空中禁区、空中限制区以及周边空域，军用航空超低空飞行空域，以及下列区域上方的空域应当划设为管制空域：  
（一）机场以及周边一定范围的区域；  
（二）国界线、实际控制线、边境线向我方一侧一定范围的区域；  
（三）军事禁区、军事管理区、监管场所等涉密单位以及周边一定范围的区域；  
（四）重要军工设施保护区域、核设施控制区域、易燃易爆等危险品的生产和仓储区域，以及可燃重要物资的大型仓储区域；  
（五）发电厂、变电站、加油（气）站、供水厂、公共交通枢纽、航电枢纽、重大水利设施、港口、高速公路、铁路电气化线路等公共基础设施以及周边一定范围的区域和饮用水水源保护区；  
（六）射电天文台、卫星测控（导航）站、航空无线电导航台、雷达站等需要电磁环境特殊保护的设施以及周边一定范围的区域；  
（七）重要革命纪念地、重要不可移动文物以及周边一定范围的区域；  
（八）国家空中交通管理领导机构规定的其他区域。  
管制空域的具体范围由各级空中交通管理机构按照国家空中交通管理领导机构的规定确定，由设区的市级以上人民政府公布，民用航空管理部门和承担相应职责的单位发布航行情报。  
未经空中交通管理机构批准，不得在管制空域内实施无人驾驶航空器飞行活动。  
管制空域范围以外的空域为微型、轻型、小型无人驾驶航空器的适飞空域（以下简称适飞空域）。

**Article 19** The state shall, as necessary, designate controlled airspace for unmanned aircraft (hereinafter referred to as controlled airspace).  
The airspace above 120 meters above true altitude, airspace of prohibited areas, restricted areas and surrounding airspace, military aviation ultra-low altitude flight airspace, and the airspace above the following areas shall be designated as controlled airspace:  
(1) airports and surrounding areas within a certain range;  
(2) areas within a certain range on the side of the national boundary, the actual line of control or the border line facing our side;  
(3) military restricted zones, military management zones, supervision places and other units involving secrets and surrounding areas within a certain range;  
(4) protection areas of important military industrial facilities, control areas of nuclear facilities, production and storage areas of flammable, explosive and other dangerous goods, and large storage areas of important combustible materials;  
(5) public infrastructure such as power plants, substations, gas stations, water supply plants, public transport hubs, navigation and power hubs, major water conservancy facilities, ports, highways, railway electrification lines, and surrounding areas within a certain range, as well as drinking water source protection areas;  
(6) facilities requiring special electromagnetic environment protection such as radio astronomy observatories, satellite measurement and control (navigation) stations, aeronautical radio navigation stations, radar stations, and surrounding areas within a certain range;  
(7) important revolutionary memorial sites, important immovable cultural relics and surrounding areas within a certain range;  
(8) other areas prescribed by the national air traffic management leading authority.  
The specific scope of controlled airspace shall be determined by air traffic management authorities at all levels in accordance with the provisions of the national air traffic management leading authority, published by the people's governments at the level of a city divided into districts or above, and the civil aviation administrative department and the units undertaking corresponding responsibilities shall issue aeronautical information.  
Without the approval of the air traffic management authority, no person shall conduct unmanned aircraft flight activities in controlled airspace.  
Airspace outside the scope of controlled airspace shall be the flyable airspace for micro, light and small unmanned aircraft (hereinafter referred to as flyable airspace).

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　遇有特殊情况，可以临时增加管制空域，由空中交通管理机构按照国家有关规定确定有关空域的水平、垂直范围和使用时间。  
保障国家重大活动以及其他大型活动的，在临时增加的管制空域生效24小时前，由设区的市级以上地方人民政府发布公告，民用航空管理部门和承担相应职责的单位发布航行情报。  
保障执行军事任务或者反恐维稳、抢险救灾、医疗救护等其他紧急任务的，在临时增加的管制空域生效30分钟前，由设区的市级以上地方人民政府发布紧急公告，民用航空管理部门和承担相应职责的单位发布航行情报。

**Article 20** Under special circumstances, controlled airspace may be temporarily added, and the horizontal and vertical scope and the time of use of the relevant airspace shall be determined by the air traffic management authority in accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
Where a major state activity or other large-scale activity is to be secured, a notice shall be published by the local people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or above, and the civil aviation administrative department and the units undertaking corresponding responsibilities shall issue aeronautical information, 24 hours before the temporarily added controlled airspace takes effect.  
Where a military mission or other emergency tasks such as counter-terrorism and stability maintenance, rescue and disaster relief, or medical rescue are to be secured, an emergency notice shall be issued by the local people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or above, and the civil aviation administrative department and the units undertaking corresponding responsibilities shall issue aeronautical information, 30 minutes before the temporarily added controlled airspace takes effect.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　按照国家空中交通管理领导机构的规定需要设置管制空域的地面警示标志的，设区的市级人民政府应当组织设置并加强日常巡查。

**Article 21** Where ground warning signs for controlled airspace are required to be installed in accordance with the provisions of the national air traffic management leading authority, the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts shall organize the installation and strengthen daily inspections.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　无人驾驶航空器通常应当与有人驾驶航空器隔离飞行。  
属于下列情形之一的，经空中交通管理机构批准，可以进行融合飞行：  
（一）根据任务或者飞行课目需要，警察、海关、应急管理部门辖有的无人驾驶航空器与本部门、本单位使用的有人驾驶航空器在同一空域或者同一机场区域的飞行；  
（二）取得适航许可的大型无人驾驶航空器的飞行；  
（三）取得适航许可的中型无人驾驶航空器不超过真高300米的飞行；  
（四）小型无人驾驶航空器不超过真高300米的飞行；  
（五）轻型无人驾驶航空器在适飞空域上方不超过真高300米的飞行。  
属于下列情形之一的，进行融合飞行无需经空中交通管理机构批准：  
（一）微型、轻型无人驾驶航空器在适飞空域内的飞行；  
（二）常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动。

**Article 22** Unmanned aircraft shall generally fly in isolation from manned aircraft.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, integrated flight may be conducted upon approval by the air traffic management authority:  
(1) flights by unmanned aircraft under the administration of the police, customs or emergency management departments with manned aircraft used by the same department or unit in the same airspace or the same airport area as required by tasks or flight subjects;  
(2) flights of large unmanned aircraft that have obtained airworthiness certification;  
(3) flights of medium-sized unmanned aircraft that have obtained airworthiness certification not exceeding 300 meters above true altitude;  
(4) flights of small unmanned aircraft not exceeding 300 meters above true altitude;  
(5) flights of light unmanned aircraft not exceeding 300 meters above true altitude above the flyable airspace.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, integrated flight may be conducted without approval from the air traffic management authority:  
(1) flights of micro or light unmanned aircraft within flyable airspace;  
(2) conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家空中交通管理领导机构统筹建设无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台，对全国无人驾驶航空器实施动态监管与服务。  
空中交通管理机构和民用航空、公安、工业和信息化等部门、单位按照职责分工采集无人驾驶航空器生产、登记、使用的有关信息，依托无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台共享，并采取相应措施保障信息安全。

**Article 23** The national air traffic management leading authority shall coordinate the construction of an integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform to conduct dynamic supervision and provide services for unmanned aircraft nationwide.  
The air traffic management authority and the departments and units of civil aviation, public security, industry and information technology shall, according to their respective functions and duties, collect relevant information on the production, registration and use of unmanned aircraft, share it through the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform, and take corresponding measures to ensure information security.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　除微型以外的无人驾驶航空器实施飞行活动，操控人员应当确保无人驾驶航空器能够按照国家有关规定向无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台报送识别信息。  
微型、轻型、小型无人驾驶航空器在飞行过程中应当广播式自动发送识别信息。

**Article 24** Where a flight activity is conducted with an unmanned aircraft other than a micro one, the operator shall ensure that the unmanned aircraft can report identification information to the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform in accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
Micro, light and small unmanned aircraft shall automatically broadcast and transmit identification information during flight.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　组织无人驾驶航空器飞行活动的单位或者个人应当遵守有关法律法规和规章制度，主动采取事故预防措施，对飞行安全承担主体责任。

**Article 25** A unit or individual that organizes unmanned aircraft flight activities shall comply with the relevant laws, regulations and rules, proactively take accident prevention measures, and bear the primary responsibility for flight safety.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　除[本条例第三十一条](#art-31)另有规定外，组织无人驾驶航空器飞行活动的单位或者个人应当在拟飞行前1日12时前向空中交通管理机构提出飞行活动申请。空中交通管理机构应当在飞行前1日21时前作出批准或者不予批准的决定。  
按照国家空中交通管理领导机构的规定在固定空域内实施常态飞行活动的，可以提出长期飞行活动申请，经批准后实施，并应当在拟飞行前1日12时前将飞行计划报空中交通管理机构备案。

**Article 26** Unless otherwise provided in Article 31 of these Regulations, a unit or individual that organizes unmanned aircraft flight activities shall submit a flight activity application to the air traffic management authority before 12:00 on the day prior to the intended flight. The air traffic management authority shall make a decision on approval or disapproval before 21:00 on the day prior to the flight.  
Where routine flight activities are conducted in fixed airspace in accordance with the provisions of the national air traffic management leading authority, a long-term flight activity application may be submitted, and the activities shall be implemented after approval; the flight plan shall be reported to the air traffic management authority for record before 12:00 on the day prior to the intended flight.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　无人驾驶航空器飞行活动申请应当包括下列内容：  
（一）组织飞行活动的单位或者个人、操控人员信息以及有关资质证书；  
（二）无人驾驶航空器的类型、数量、主要性能指标和登记管理信息；  
（三）飞行任务性质和飞行方式，执行国家规定的特殊通用航空飞行任务的还应当提供有效的任务批准文件；  
（四）起飞、降落和备降机场（场地）；  
（五）通信联络方法；  
（六）预计飞行开始、结束时刻；  
（七）飞行航线、高度、速度和空域范围，进出空域方法；  
（八）指挥控制链路无线电频率以及占用带宽；  
（九）通信、导航和被监视能力；  
（十）安装二次雷达应答机或者有关自动监视设备的，应当注明代码申请；  
（十一）应急处置程序；  
（十二）特殊飞行保障需求；  
（十三）国家空中交通管理领导机构规定的与空域使用和飞行安全有关的其他必要信息。

**Article 27** An application for unmanned aircraft flight activities shall include the following:  
(1) information on the unit or individual organizing the flight activity, the operator, and relevant qualification certificates;  
(2) the type, quantity, main performance indicators and registration management information of the unmanned aircraft;  
(3) the nature of the flight mission and the flight method; if a special general aviation flight mission as prescribed by the state is to be executed, a valid mission approval document shall also be provided;  
(4) the take-off, landing and alternate airports (sites);  
(5) communication contact methods;  
(6) the expected start and end times of the flight;  
(7) the flight route, altitude, speed and airspace scope, and the method of entering and leaving the airspace;  
(8) the radio frequency of the command and control link and the occupied bandwidth;  
(9) communication, navigation and monitored capabilities;  
(10) if a secondary surveillance radar transponder or relevant automatic surveillance equipment is installed, the code application shall be indicated;  
(11) emergency response procedures;  
(12) special flight support needs;  
(13) other necessary information related to airspace use and flight safety as prescribed by the national air traffic management leading authority.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　无人驾驶航空器飞行活动申请按照下列权限批准：  
（一）在飞行管制分区内飞行的，由负责该飞行管制分区的空中交通管理机构批准；  
（二）超出飞行管制分区在飞行管制区内飞行的，由负责该飞行管制区的空中交通管理机构批准；  
（三）超出飞行管制区飞行的，由国家空中交通管理领导机构授权的空中交通管理机构批准。

**Article 28** Applications for unmanned aircraft flight activities shall be approved in accordance with the following authority:  
(1) if the flight is within a flight control sub-area, it shall be approved by the air traffic management authority responsible for that flight control sub-area;  
(2) if the flight exceeds a flight control sub-area but is within a flight control area, it shall be approved by the air traffic management authority responsible for that flight control area;  
(3) if the flight exceeds a flight control area, it shall be approved by the air traffic management authority authorized by the national air traffic management leading authority.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　使用无人驾驶航空器执行反恐维稳、抢险救灾、医疗救护等紧急任务的，应当在计划起飞30分钟前向空中交通管理机构提出飞行活动申请。空中交通管理机构应当在起飞10分钟前作出批准或者不予批准的决定。执行特别紧急任务的，使用单位可以随时提出飞行活动申请。

**Article 29** Where an unmanned aircraft is used to execute emergency missions such as counter-terrorism and stability maintenance, rescue and disaster relief, or medical rescue, a flight activity application shall be submitted to the air traffic management authority no later than 30 minutes before the planned take-off. The air traffic management authority shall make a decision on approval or disapproval no later than 10 minutes before take-off. Where an exceptionally urgent mission is executed, the using unit may submit a flight activity application at any time.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　飞行活动已获得批准的单位或者个人组织无人驾驶航空器飞行活动的，应当在计划起飞1小时前向空中交通管理机构报告预计起飞时刻和准备情况，经空中交通管理机构确认后方可起飞。

**Article 30** Where a unit or individual whose flight activity has been approved organizes an unmanned aircraft flight activity, it shall report the estimated take-off time and readiness to the air traffic management authority one hour before the planned take-off, and may take off only after confirmation by the air traffic management authority.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　组织无人驾驶航空器实施下列飞行活动，无需向空中交通管理机构提出飞行活动申请：  
（一）微型、轻型、小型无人驾驶航空器在适飞空域内的飞行活动；  
（二）常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动；  
（三）警察、海关、应急管理部门辖有的无人驾驶航空器，在其驻地、地面（水面）训练场、靶场等上方不超过真高120米的空域内的飞行活动；但是，需在计划起飞1小时前经空中交通管理机构确认后方可起飞；  
（四）民用无人驾驶航空器在民用运输机场管制地带内执行巡检、勘察、校验等飞行任务；但是，需定期报空中交通管理机构备案，并在计划起飞1小时前经空中交通管理机构确认后方可起飞。  
前款规定的飞行活动存在下列情形之一的，应当依照[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)的规定提出飞行活动申请：  
（一）通过通信基站或者互联网进行无人驾驶航空器中继飞行；  
（二）运载危险品或者投放物品（常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动除外）；  
（三）飞越集会人群上空；  
（四）在移动的交通工具上操控无人驾驶航空器；  
（五）实施分布式操作或者集群飞行。  
微型、轻型无人驾驶航空器在适飞空域内飞行的，无需取得特殊通用航空飞行任务批准文件。

**Article 31** Where the following flight activities are organized with unmanned aircraft, no flight activity application need be submitted to the air traffic management authority:  
(1) flight activities of micro, light and small unmanned aircraft within flyable airspace;  
(2) conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities;  
(3) flight activities of unmanned aircraft under the administration of the police, customs or emergency management departments within the airspace not exceeding 120 meters above true altitude above their stations, ground (water surface) training grounds, shooting ranges, etc.; however, take-off may only occur after confirmation by the air traffic management authority one hour before the planned take-off;  
(4) flight missions such as inspection, survey and calibration conducted by civil unmanned aircraft within the control zone of a civil transport airport; however, they shall be reported to the air traffic management authority for record on a regular basis, and take-off may only occur after confirmation by the air traffic management authority one hour before the planned take-off.  
If any of the following circumstances exists in the flight activities specified in the preceding paragraph, a flight activity application shall be submitted in accordance with Article 26 of these Regulations:  
(1) conducting relay flights of unmanned aircraft through communication base stations or the Internet;  
(2) carrying dangerous goods or dropping articles (except for conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities);  
(3) flying over the area where a gathering crowd is located;  
(4) operating an unmanned aircraft on a moving vehicle;  
(5) implementing distributed operation or cluster flight.  
Micro and light unmanned aircraft flying within flyable airspace need not obtain approval documents for special general aviation flight missions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　操控无人驾驶航空器实施飞行活动，应当遵守下列行为规范：  
（一）依法取得有关许可证书、证件，并在实施飞行活动时随身携带备查；  
（二）实施飞行活动前做好安全飞行准备，检查无人驾驶航空器状态，并及时更新电子围栏等信息；  
（三）实时掌握无人驾驶航空器飞行动态，实施需经批准的飞行活动应当与空中交通管理机构保持通信联络畅通，服从空中交通管理，飞行结束后及时报告；  
（四）按照国家空中交通管理领导机构的规定保持必要的安全间隔；  
（五）操控微型无人驾驶航空器的，应当保持视距内飞行；  
（六）操控小型无人驾驶航空器在适飞空域内飞行的，应当遵守国家空中交通管理领导机构关于限速、通信、导航等方面的规定；  
（七）在夜间或者低能见度气象条件下飞行的，应当开启灯光系统并确保其处于良好工作状态；  
（八）实施超视距飞行的，应当掌握飞行空域内其他航空器的飞行动态，采取避免相撞的措施；  
（九）受到酒精类饮料、麻醉剂或者其他药物影响时，不得操控无人驾驶航空器；  
（十）国家空中交通管理领导机构规定的其他飞行活动行为规范。

**Article 32** When operating an unmanned aircraft to conduct flight activities, the following rules of conduct shall be observed:  
(1) obtaining the relevant licenses and certificates according to law, and carrying them for inspection during flight activities;  
(2) making preparations for safe flight before conducting flight activities, checking the condition of the unmanned aircraft, and promptly updating the electronic fence and other information;  
(3) monitoring the flight dynamics of the unmanned aircraft in real time; where a flight activity requiring approval is conducted, maintaining smooth communication with the air traffic management authority, complying with air traffic control, and reporting promptly after the flight ends;  
(4) maintaining the necessary safe separation in accordance with the provisions of the national air traffic management leading authority;  
(5) when operating a micro unmanned aircraft, maintaining flight within visual line of sight;  
(6) when operating a small unmanned aircraft in flyable airspace, complying with the provisions of the national air traffic management leading authority on speed limits, communication, navigation, etc.;  
(7) when flying at night or under low-visibility meteorological conditions, turning on the lighting system and ensuring it is in good working condition;  
(8) when conducting beyond visual line of sight flight, monitoring the flight dynamics of other aircraft in the flight airspace and taking measures to avoid collisions;  
(9) not operating an unmanned aircraft when under the influence of alcoholic beverages, narcotics or other drugs;  
(10) other rules of conduct for flight activities prescribed by the national air traffic management leading authority.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　操控无人驾驶航空器实施飞行活动，应当遵守下列避让规则：  
（一）避让有人驾驶航空器、无动力装置的航空器以及地面、水上交通工具；  
（二）单架飞行避让集群飞行；  
（三）微型无人驾驶航空器避让其他无人驾驶航空器；  
（四）国家空中交通管理领导机构规定的其他避让规则。

**Article 33** When operating an unmanned aircraft to conduct flight activities, the following avoidance rules shall be observed:  
(1) giving way to manned aircraft, unpowered aircraft and ground or water vehicles;  
(2) single aircraft flight giving way to cluster flight;  
(3) micro unmanned aircraft giving way to other unmanned aircraft;  
(4) other avoidance rules prescribed by the national air traffic management leading authority.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　禁止利用无人驾驶航空器实施下列行为：  
（一）违法拍摄军事设施、军工设施或者其他涉密场所；  
（二）扰乱机关、团体、企业、事业单位工作秩序或者公共场所秩序；  
（三）妨碍国家机关工作人员依法执行职务；  
（四）投放含有违反法律法规规定内容的宣传品或者其他物品；  
（五）危及公共设施、单位或者个人财产安全；  
（六）危及他人生命健康，非法采集信息，或者侵犯他人其他人身权益；  
（七）非法获取、泄露国家秘密，或者违法向境外提供数据信息；  
（八）法律法规禁止的其他行为。

**Article 34** It is prohibited to use an unmanned aircraft to commit any of the following acts:  
(1) illegally photographing military installations, military industrial facilities or other places involving secrets;  
(2) disrupting the work order of state organs, organizations, enterprises or institutions, or the order of public places;  
(3) obstructing state functionaries from performing their duties according to law;  
(4) dropping propaganda materials or other objects containing content that violates laws or regulations;  
(5) endangering the property safety of public facilities, units or individuals;  
(6) endangering the life or health of others, illegally collecting information, or infringing upon other personal rights and interests of others;  
(7) illegally obtaining or disclosing state secrets, or illegally providing data or information to overseas parties;  
(8) other acts prohibited by laws or regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　使用民用无人驾驶航空器从事测绘活动的单位依法取得测绘资质证书后，方可从事测绘活动。  
外国无人驾驶航空器或者由外国人员操控的无人驾驶航空器不得在我国境内实施测绘、电波参数测试等飞行活动。

**Article 35** A unit that uses civil unmanned aircraft to engage in surveying and mapping activities may only conduct such activities after obtaining a surveying and mapping qualification certificate according to law.  
Foreign unmanned aircraft or unmanned aircraft operated by foreign personnel shall not conduct flight activities such as surveying and mapping or radio wave parameter testing within the territory of China.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　模型航空器应当在空中交通管理机构为航空飞行营地划定的空域内飞行，但国家空中交通管理领导机构另有规定的除外。

**Article 36** Model aircraft shall fly within the airspace delineated by the air traffic management authority for aviation flight camps, unless otherwise provided by the national air traffic management leading authority.

### 第四章　监督管理和应急处置

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家空中交通管理领导机构应当组织有关部门、单位在无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台上向社会公布审批事项、申请办理流程、受理单位、联系方式、举报受理方式等信息并及时更新。

**Article 37** The national air traffic management leading authority shall organize the relevant departments and units to publish on the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform information such as approval items, application procedures, accepting units, contact methods, and methods for accepting reports, and update such information in a timely manner.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　任何单位或者个人发现违反本条例规定行为的，可以向空中交通管理机构、民用航空管理部门或者当地公安机关举报。收到举报的部门、单位应当及时依法作出处理；不属于本部门、本单位职责的，应当及时移送有权处理的部门、单位。

**Article 38** Any unit or individual that discovers any act violating the provisions of these Regulations may report it to the air traffic management authority, the civil aviation administrative department or the local public security organ. The department or unit that receives the report shall promptly handle it according to law; if it is not within the functions and duties of that department or unit, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the department or unit with competent authority.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　空中交通管理机构、民用航空管理部门以及县级以上公安机关应当制定有关无人驾驶航空器飞行安全管理的应急预案，定期演练，提高应急处置能力。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将无人驾驶航空器安全应急管理纳入突发事件应急管理体系，健全信息互通、协同配合的应急处置工作机制。  
无人驾驶航空器系统的设计者、生产者，应当确保无人驾驶航空器具备紧急避让、降落等应急处置功能，避免或者减轻无人驾驶航空器发生事故时对生命财产的损害。  
使用无人驾驶航空器的单位或者个人应当按照有关规定，制定飞行紧急情况处置预案，落实风险防范措施，及时消除安全隐患。

**Article 39** The air traffic management authority, the civil aviation administrative department and the public security organs at or above the county level shall formulate emergency plans for the safety management of unmanned aircraft flights, conduct regular drills, and enhance emergency response capabilities.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the safety emergency management of unmanned aircraft into the emergency management system for emergencies, and improve the emergency response work mechanism for information exchange and coordinated cooperation.  
Designers and producers of unmanned aircraft systems shall ensure that unmanned aircraft are equipped with emergency response functions such as emergency avoidance and landing, so as to avoid or mitigate damage to life and property in the event of an unmanned aircraft accident.  
Units or individuals that use unmanned aircraft shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, formulate emergency response plans for flight emergencies, implement risk prevention measures, and promptly eliminate safety hazards.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　无人驾驶航空器飞行发生异常情况时，组织飞行活动的单位或者个人应当及时处置，服从空中交通管理机构的指令；导致发生飞行安全问题的，组织飞行活动的单位或者个人还应当在无人驾驶航空器降落后24小时内向空中交通管理机构报告有关情况。

**Article 40** When an abnormal situation occurs during an unmanned aircraft flight, the unit or individual organizing the flight activity shall promptly handle it and comply with the instructions of the air traffic management authority; if a flight safety problem results, the unit or individual organizing the flight activity shall also report the relevant circumstances to the air traffic management authority within 24 hours after the unmanned aircraft lands.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对空中不明情况和无人驾驶航空器违规飞行，公安机关在条件有利时可以对低空目标实施先期处置，并负责违规飞行无人驾驶航空器落地后的现场处置。有关军事机关、公安机关、国家安全机关等单位按职责分工组织查证处置，民用航空管理等其他有关部门应当予以配合。

**Article 41** With respect to unidentified situations in the air and illegal flights of unmanned aircraft, the public security organ may, when conditions are favorable, conduct preliminary disposal of low-altitude targets and shall be responsible for on-site disposal after the illegally flying unmanned aircraft lands. The relevant military organs, public security organs, state security organs and other units shall organize investigation and disposal according to their respective functions and duties, and the civil aviation administration and other relevant departments shall provide cooperation.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　无人驾驶航空器违反飞行管理规定、扰乱公共秩序或者危及公共安全的，空中交通管理机构、民用航空管理部门和公安机关可以依法采取必要技术防控、扣押有关物品、责令停止飞行、查封违法活动场所等紧急处置措施。

**Article 42** Where an unmanned aircraft violates flight management provisions, disturbs public order or endangers public safety, the air traffic management authority, the civil aviation administrative department and the public security organ may take emergency disposal measures according to law, such as necessary technical prevention and control, seizure of relevant articles, ordering the suspension of flight, and sealing the premises of illegal activities.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　军队、警察以及按照国家反恐怖主义工作领导机构有关规定由公安机关授权的高风险反恐怖重点目标管理单位，可以依法配备无人驾驶航空器反制设备，在公安机关或者有关军事机关的指导监督下从严控制设置和使用。  
无人驾驶航空器反制设备配备、设置、使用以及授权管理办法，由国务院工业和信息化、公安、国家安全、市场监督管理部门会同国务院有关部门、有关军事机关制定。  
任何单位或者个人不得非法拥有、使用无人驾驶航空器反制设备。

**Article 43** The military, the police, and high-risk counter-terrorism key target management units authorized by the public security organs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the national counter-terrorism work leadership authority may be equipped with unmanned aircraft countermeasure equipment according to law, and shall strictly control the installation and use under the guidance and supervision of the public security organ or the relevant military organ.  
Measures for the management of the equipping, installation, use and authorization of unmanned aircraft countermeasure equipment shall be formulated by the competent departments of industry and information technology, public security, state security and market supervision and administration of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant military organs.  
No unit or individual shall illegally possess or use unmanned aircraft countermeasure equipment.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本条例规定，从事中型、大型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的设计、生产、进口、飞行和维修活动，未依法取得适航许可的，由民用航空管理部门责令停止有关活动，没收违法所得，并处无人驾驶航空器系统货值金额1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 44** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, engages in the design, production, import, flight or maintenance of medium-sized or large civil unmanned aircraft systems without obtaining an airworthiness certification according to law, the civil aviation administrative department shall order the cessation of the relevant activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the unmanned aircraft systems; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order the suspension of business for rectification.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本条例规定，民用无人驾驶航空器系统生产者未按照国务院工业和信息化主管部门的规定为其生产的无人驾驶航空器设置唯一产品识别码的，由县级以上人民政府工业和信息化主管部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处3万元以上30万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 45** Where a producer of civil unmanned aircraft systems, in violation of these Regulations, fails to set a unique product identification code for the unmanned aircraft it produces in accordance with the provisions of the competent industry and information technology department of the State Council, the competent industry and information technology department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，对已经取得适航许可的民用无人驾驶航空器系统进行重大设计更改，未重新申请取得适航许可并将其用于飞行活动的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，处无人驾驶航空器系统货值金额1倍以上5倍以下的罚款。  
违反本条例规定，改变微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统的空域保持能力、可靠被监视能力、速度或者高度等出厂性能以及参数，未及时在无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台更新性能、参数信息的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款。

**Article 46** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, makes a major design change to a civil unmanned aircraft system that has obtained an airworthiness certification, and fails to re-apply for airworthiness certification and uses it for flight activities, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the unmanned aircraft system.  
Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, changes the factory performance and parameters of a micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft system, such as the airspace holding capability, reliable monitored capability, speed or altitude, and fails to promptly update the performance and parameter information on the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，民用无人驾驶航空器未经实名登记实施飞行活动的，由公安机关责令改正，可以处200元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例规定，涉及境外飞行的民用无人驾驶航空器未依法进行国籍登记的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Where a civil unmanned aircraft, in violation of these Regulations, conducts a flight activity without real-name registration, the public security organ shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 200 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a civil unmanned aircraft involved in overseas flight, in violation of these Regulations, fails to undergo nationality registration according to law, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，民用无人驾驶航空器未依法投保责任保险的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令从事飞行活动的单位停业整顿直至吊销其运营合格证。

**Article 48** Where a civil unmanned aircraft, in violation of these Regulations, fails to take out liability insurance according to law, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the unit engaged in flight activities shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification until its operation certificate is revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，未取得运营合格证或者违反运营合格证的要求实施飞行活动的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿直至吊销其运营合格证。

**Article 49** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, conducts a flight activity without obtaining an operation certificate or in violation of the requirements of the operation certificate, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification until its operation certificate is revoked.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人违反本条例规定操控民用无人驾驶航空器飞行的，由公安机关对其监护人处500元以上5000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，没收实施违规飞行的无人驾驶航空器。  
违反本条例规定，未取得操控员执照操控民用无人驾驶航空器飞行的，由民用航空管理部门处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款。  
违反本条例规定，超出操控员执照载明范围操控民用无人驾驶航空器飞行的，由民用航空管理部门处2000元以上2万元以下的罚款，并处暂扣操控员执照6个月至12个月；情节严重的，吊销其操控员执照，2年内不受理其操控员执照申请。  
违反本条例规定，未取得操作证书从事常规农用无人驾驶航空器作业飞行活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止作业，并处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Where a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, in violation of these Regulations, operates a civil unmanned aircraft for flight, the public security organ shall impose a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan on the guardian thereof; if the circumstances are serious, the unmanned aircraft used for the illegal flight shall be confiscated.  
Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, operates a civil unmanned aircraft for flight without obtaining an operator's license, the civil aviation administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, operates a civil unmanned aircraft for flight beyond the scope indicated in the operator's license, the civil aviation administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan and also suspend the operator's license for a period of not less than six months but not more than twelve months; if the circumstances are serious, the operator's license shall be revoked, and no application for an operator's license shall be accepted within two years.  
Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, engages in conventional agricultural unmanned aircraft operation flight activities without obtaining an operation certificate, the competent agriculture and rural affairs department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the operation and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　组织飞行活动的单位或者个人违反[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)、第三十三条规定的，由民用航空管理部门责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，并处暂扣运营合格证、操控员执照1个月至3个月；情节严重的，由空中交通管理机构责令停止飞行6个月至12个月，由民用航空管理部门处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并可以吊销相应许可证件，2年内不受理其相应许可申请。  
违反本条例规定，未经批准操控微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器在管制空域内飞行，或者操控模型航空器在空中交通管理机构划定的空域外飞行的，由公安机关责令停止飞行，可以处500元以下的罚款；情节严重的，没收实施违规飞行的无人驾驶航空器，并处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 51** Where a unit or individual organizing flight activities violates the provisions of Article 32 or Article 33 of these Regulations, the civil aviation administrative department shall order it to make corrections and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if it refuses to make corrections, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the operation certificate or operator's license shall be suspended for a period of not less than one month but not more than three months; if the circumstances are serious, the air traffic management authority shall order the suspension of flight for a period of not less than six months but not more than twelve months, and the civil aviation administrative department shall impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and may revoke the relevant license, and no corresponding license application shall be accepted within two years.  
Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, operates a micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft for flight in controlled airspace without approval, or operates a model aircraft to fly outside the airspace delineated by the air traffic management authority, the public security organ shall order the cessation of flight and may impose a fine of not more than 500 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the unmanned aircraft used for the illegal flight shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，非法拥有、使用无人驾驶航空器反制设备的，由无线电管理机构、公安机关按照职责分工予以没收，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 52** Where a person, in violation of these Regulations, illegally possesses or uses unmanned aircraft countermeasure equipment, the radio regulatory authority or the public security organ shall confiscate the equipment according to their respective duties and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，外国无人驾驶航空器或者由外国人员操控的无人驾驶航空器在我国境内实施测绘飞行活动的，由县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得、测绘成果和实施违规飞行的无人驾驶航空器，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，由公安机关、国家安全机关按照职责分工决定限期出境或者驱逐出境。

**Article 53** Where a foreign unmanned aircraft or an unmanned aircraft operated by foreign personnel, in violation of these Regulations, conducts surveying and mapping flight activities within the territory of China, the competent surveying, mapping and geographic information department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, the surveying and mapping results and the unmanned aircraft used for the illegal flight, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall also be imposed, and the public security organ or the state security organ shall, according to their respective duties, decide on a time limit for departure or expulsion.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　生产、改装、组装、拼装、销售和召回微型、轻型、小型民用无人驾驶航空器系统，违反产品质量或者标准化管理等有关法律法规的，由县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门依法处罚。  
除根据[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定无需取得无线电频率使用许可和无线电台执照的情形以外，生产、维修、使用民用无人驾驶航空器系统，违反无线电管理法律法规以及国家有关规定的，由无线电管理机构依法处罚。  
无人驾驶航空器飞行活动违反军事设施保护法律法规的，依照有关法律法规的规定执行。

**Article 54** Where the production, modification, assembly, integration, sale or recall of micro, light or small civil unmanned aircraft systems violates the relevant laws and regulations on product quality or standardization management, the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose punishment according to law.  
Except for the circumstances where no radio frequency use license or radio station license is required under Article 15 of these Regulations, if the production, maintenance or use of civil unmanned aircraft systems violates the laws, regulations and relevant state provisions on radio management, the radio regulatory authority shall impose punishment according to law.  
Where an unmanned aircraft flight activity violates the laws and regulations on the protection of military installations, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，有关部门、单位及其工作人员在无人驾驶航空器飞行以及有关活动的管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊或者有其他违法行为的，依法给予处分。

**Article 55** Where a relevant department, unit or its staff member, in violation of these Regulations, abuses its power, neglects its duties, engages in malpractices for personal gain or commits any other illegal act in the management of unmanned aircraft flights and related activities, it shall be given a sanction according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 56** Where a violation of these Regulations constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration punishment according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if personal injury, property damage or other damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　在我国管辖的其他空域内实施无人驾驶航空器飞行活动，应当遵守本条例的有关规定。  
无人驾驶航空器在室内飞行不适用本条例。  
自备动力系统的飞行玩具适用本条例的有关规定，具体办法由国务院工业和信息化主管部门、有关空中交通管理机构会同国务院公安、民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 57** The relevant provisions of these Regulations shall be complied with when conducting unmanned aircraft flight activities in other airspace under the jurisdiction of China.  
These Regulations shall not apply to indoor flights of unmanned aircraft.  
Flying toys with their own power systems shall be subject to the relevant provisions of these Regulations, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the competent industry and information technology department of the State Council and the relevant air traffic management authority in conjunction with the public security and civil aviation authorities of the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　无人驾驶航空器飞行以及有关活动，本条例没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国民用航空法》、《中华人民共和国飞行基本规则》、《通用航空飞行管制条例》以及有关法律、行政法规。

**Article 58** For matters concerning unmanned aircraft flights and related activities not covered by these Regulations, the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China, the Basic Rules of Flight of the People's Republic of China, the Regulations on the Control of General Aviation Flights, and the relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　军用无人驾驶航空器的管理，国务院、中央军事委员会另有规定的，适用其规定。  
警察、海关、应急管理部门辖有的无人驾驶航空器的适航、登记、操控员等事项的管理办法，由国务院有关部门另行制定。

**Article 59** Where the State Council and the Central Military Commission have other provisions on the management of military unmanned aircraft, such provisions shall apply.  
Measures for the management of airworthiness, registration, operators and other matters concerning unmanned aircraft under the administration of the police, customs or emergency management departments shall be separately formulated by the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　模型航空器的分类、生产、登记、操控人员、航空飞行营地等事项的管理办法，由国务院体育主管部门会同有关空中交通管理机构，国务院工业和信息化、公安、民用航空主管部门另行制定。

**Article 60** Measures for the management of the classification, production, registration, operators, aviation flight camps and other matters concerning model aircraft shall be separately formulated by the competent sports department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant air traffic management authority and the competent departments of industry and information technology, public security and civil aviation of the State Council.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本条例施行前生产的民用无人驾驶航空器不能按照国家有关规定自动向无人驾驶航空器一体化综合监管服务平台报送识别信息的，实施飞行活动应当依照本条例的规定向空中交通管理机构提出飞行活动申请，经批准后方可飞行。

**Article 61** Where a civil unmanned aircraft produced before the coming into force of these Regulations cannot automatically report identification information to the integrated unmanned aircraft comprehensive supervision and service platform in accordance with the relevant state provisions, the flight activity shall be conducted only after a flight activity application is submitted to the air traffic management authority in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and approved.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
（一）空中交通管理机构，是指军队和民用航空管理部门内负责有关责任区空中交通管理的机构。  
（二）微型无人驾驶航空器，是指空机重量小于0.25千克，最大飞行真高不超过50米，最大平飞速度不超过40千米/小时，无线电发射设备符合微功率短距离技术要求，全程可以随时人工介入操控的无人驾驶航空器。  
（三）轻型无人驾驶航空器，是指空机重量不超过4千克且最大起飞重量不超过7千克，最大平飞速度不超过100千米/小时，具备符合空域管理要求的空域保持能力和可靠被监视能力，全程可以随时人工介入操控的无人驾驶航空器，但不包括微型无人驾驶航空器。  
（四）小型无人驾驶航空器，是指空机重量不超过15千克且最大起飞重量不超过25千克，具备符合空域管理要求的空域保持能力和可靠被监视能力，全程可以随时人工介入操控的无人驾驶航空器，但不包括微型、轻型无人驾驶航空器。  
（五）中型无人驾驶航空器，是指最大起飞重量不超过150千克的无人驾驶航空器，但不包括微型、轻型、小型无人驾驶航空器。  
（六）大型无人驾驶航空器，是指最大起飞重量超过150千克的无人驾驶航空器。  
（七）无人驾驶航空器系统，是指无人驾驶航空器以及与其有关的遥控台（站）、任务载荷和控制链路等组成的系统。其中，遥控台（站）是指遥控无人驾驶航空器的各种操控设备（手段）以及有关系统组成的整体。  
（八）农用无人驾驶航空器，是指最大飞行真高不超过30米，最大平飞速度不超过50千米/小时，最大飞行半径不超过2000米，具备空域保持能力和可靠被监视能力，专门用于植保、播种、投饵等农林牧渔作业，全程可以随时人工介入操控的无人驾驶航空器。  
（九）隔离飞行，是指无人驾驶航空器与有人驾驶航空器不同时在同一空域内的飞行。  
（十）融合飞行，是指无人驾驶航空器与有人驾驶航空器同时在同一空域内的飞行。  
（十一）分布式操作，是指把无人驾驶航空器系统操作分解为多个子业务，部署在多个站点或者终端进行协同操作的模式。  
（十二）集群，是指采用具备多台无人驾驶航空器操控能力的同一系统或者平台，为了处理同一任务，以各无人驾驶航空器操控数据互联协同处理为特征，在同一时间内并行操控多台无人驾驶航空器以相对物理集中的方式进行飞行的无人驾驶航空器运行模式。  
（十三）模型航空器，也称航空模型，是指有尺寸和重量限制，不能载人，不具有高度保持和位置保持飞行功能的无人驾驶航空器，包括自由飞、线控、直接目视视距内人工不间断遥控、借助第一视角人工不间断遥控的模型航空器等。  
（十四）无人驾驶航空器反制设备，是指专门用于防控无人驾驶航空器违规飞行，具有干扰、截控、捕获、摧毁等功能的设备。  
（十五）空域保持能力，是指通过电子围栏等技术措施控制无人驾驶航空器的高度与水平范围的能力。

**Article 62** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Air traffic management authority" means the organizations within the military and the civil aviation administrative department responsible for the air traffic management in the relevant responsible airspace.  
(2) "Micro unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with an empty weight of less than 0.25 kg, a maximum flight true altitude not exceeding 50 m, a maximum level flight speed not exceeding 40 km/h, radio transmitting equipment that meets the technical requirements for micro-power short-distance communication, and which can be manually intervened at any time throughout the flight.  
(3) "Light unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with an empty weight not exceeding 4 kg and a maximum take-off weight not exceeding 7 kg, a maximum level flight speed not exceeding 100 km/h, having airspace holding capability and reliable monitored capability that meet airspace management requirements, and which can be manually intervened at any time throughout the flight, but excluding micro unmanned aircraft.  
(4) "Small unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with an empty weight not exceeding 15 kg and a maximum take-off weight not exceeding 25 kg, having airspace holding capability and reliable monitored capability that meet airspace management requirements, and which can be manually intervened at any time throughout the flight, but excluding micro and light unmanned aircraft.  
(5) "Medium-sized unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with a maximum take-off weight not exceeding 150 kg, but excluding micro, light and small unmanned aircraft.  
(6) "Large unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with a maximum take-off weight exceeding 150 kg.  
(7) "Unmanned aircraft system" means a system composed of an unmanned aircraft and its associated remote control station(s), mission payloads and control links. Among them, "remote control station(s)" means the assembly of various control devices (means) for remotely controlling the unmanned aircraft and the related systems.  
(8) "Agricultural unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft with a maximum flight true altitude not exceeding 30 m, a maximum level flight speed not exceeding 50 km/h, a maximum flight radius not exceeding 2,000 m, having airspace holding capability and reliable monitored capability, specially used for plant protection, seeding, baiting and other agricultural, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery operations, and which can be manually intervened at any time throughout the flight.  
(9) "Isolated flight" means a flight in which an unmanned aircraft and a manned aircraft are not simultaneously in the same airspace.  
(10) "Integrated flight" means a flight in which an unmanned aircraft and a manned aircraft are simultaneously in the same airspace.  
(11) "Distributed operation" means a mode in which the operation of an unmanned aircraft system is decomposed into multiple sub-tasks and deployed at multiple stations or terminals for coordinated operation.  
(12) "Cluster" means an unmanned aircraft operation mode in which a single system or platform capable of controlling multiple unmanned aircraft is used, characterized by the interconnected and coordinated processing of control data for each unmanned aircraft to handle the same task, and multiple unmanned aircraft are operated in parallel in a relatively physically concentrated manner within the same time period.  
(13) "Model aircraft", also known as aviation model, means an unmanned aircraft with size and weight restrictions, incapable of carrying persons, and without altitude hold or position hold flight functions, including free-flight, line-controlled, direct visual line-of-sight continuous manual remote control, and first-person-view continuous manual remote control model aircraft, etc.  
(14) "Unmanned aircraft countermeasure equipment" means equipment specially used for preventing and controlling illegal flights of unmanned aircraft, having functions such as jamming, interception and control, capture, and destruction.  
(15) "Airspace holding capability" means the capability to control the altitude and horizontal range of an unmanned aircraft through technical measures such as electronic fences.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本条例自2024年1月1日起施行。

**Article 63** These Regulations shall come into force on January 1, 2024.
