# 中华人民共和国民用航空安全保卫条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1996年7月6日中华人民共和国国务院令第201号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防止对民用航空活动的非法干扰，维护民用航空秩序，保障民用航空安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to prevent unlawful interference with civil aviation activities, maintain civil aviation order, and ensure civil aviation safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国领域内的一切民用航空活动以及与民用航空活动有关的单位和个人。  
在中华人民共和国领域外从事民用航空活动的具有中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器适用本条例；但是，中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的除外。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to all civil aviation activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and to units and individuals involved in such activities.  
Civil aircraft registered in the People's Republic of China engaging in civil aviation activities outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be subject to these Regulations; however, unless otherwise provided for in international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用航空安全保卫工作实行统一管理、分工负责的原则。  
民用航空公安机关(以下简称民航公安机关)负责对民用航空安全保卫工作实施统一管理、检查和监督。

**Article 3** Civil aviation security work shall implement the principle of unified management and division of responsibilities.  
Civil aviation public security organs (hereinafter referred to as civil aviation public security organs) shall be responsible for implementing unified management, inspection, and supervision of civil aviation security work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　有关地方人民政府与民用航空单位应当密切配合，共同维护民用航空安全。

**Article 4** Relevant local people's governments and civil aviation units shall closely cooperate to jointly maintain civil aviation safety.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　旅客、货物托运人和收货人以及其他进入机场的人员，应当遵守民用航空安全管理的法律、法规和规章。

**Article 5** Passengers, cargo consignors and consignees, and other persons entering the airport shall comply with laws, regulations, and rules governing civil aviation safety management.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民用机场经营人和民用航空器经营人应当履行下列职责：  
(一)制定本单位民用航空安全保卫方案，并报国务院民用航空主管部门备案；  
(二)严格实行有关民用航空安全保卫的措施；  
(三)定期进行民用航空安全保卫训练，及时消除危及民用航空安全的隐患。  
与中华人民共和国通航的外国民用航空企业，应当向国务院民用航空主管部门报送民用航空安全保卫方案。

**Article 6** Airport operators and civil aircraft operators shall perform the following duties:  
(1) formulate their own civil aviation security programs and file them with the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council for record;  
(2) strictly implement relevant civil aviation security measures;  
(3) conduct regular civil aviation security training and promptly eliminate hidden dangers endangering civil aviation safety.  
Foreign civil aviation enterprises operating flights to and from the People's Republic of China shall submit their civil aviation security programs to the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公民有权向民航公安机关举报预谋劫持、破坏民用航空器或者其他危害民用航空安全的行为。

**Article 7** Citizens shall have the right to report to the civil aviation public security organs any acts of attempted hijacking, destruction of civil aircraft, or other acts endangering civil aviation safety.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对维护民用航空安全做出突出贡献的单位或者个人，由有关人民政府或者国务院民用航空主管部门给予奖励。

**Article 8** Units or individuals that have made outstanding contributions to maintaining civil aviation safety shall be rewarded by the relevant people's government or the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

### 第二章　民用机场的安全保卫

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　民用机场(包括军民合用机场中的民用部分，下同)的新建、改建或者扩建，应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门关于民用机场安全保卫设施建设的规定。

**Article 9** The construction, renovation, or expansion of civil airports (including the civil sections of military-civilian airports, the same below) shall comply with the regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council on the construction of civil airport security facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民用机场开放使用，应当具备下列安全保卫条件：  
(一)设有机场控制区并配备专职警卫人员；  
(二)设有符合标准的防护围栏和巡逻通道；  
(三)设有安全保卫机构并配备相应的人员和装备；  
(四)设有安全检查机构并配备与机场运输量相适应的人员和检查设备；  
(五)设有专职消防组织并按照机场消防等级配备人员和设备；  
(六)订有应急处置方案并配备必要的应急援救设备。

**Article 10** A civil airport that is opened for use shall meet the following security conditions:  
(1) have an airport control area and be equipped with full-time security guards;  
(2) have protective fences and patrol passages that meet standards;  
(3) have a security organization and be equipped with corresponding personnel and equipment;  
(4) have a security inspection organization and be equipped with personnel and inspection equipment commensurate with the airport's traffic volume;  
(5) have a full-time firefighting organization and be equipped with personnel and equipment according to the airport's fire rating;  
(6) have an emergency response plan and be equipped with necessary emergency rescue equipment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　机场控制区应当根据安全保卫的需要，划定为候机隔离区、行李分检装卸区、航空器活动区和维修区、货物存放区等，并分别设置安全防护设施和明显标志。

**Article 11** The airport control area shall, based on security needs, be divided into waiting isolation areas, baggage sorting and loading areas, aircraft activity and maintenance areas, cargo storage areas, etc., and each shall be provided with security facilities and conspicuous signs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　机场控制区应当有严密的安全保卫措施，实行封闭式分区管理。具体管理办法由国务院民用航空主管部门制定。

**Article 12** The airport control area shall have strict security measures and implement closed zoning management. Specific management measures shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人员与车辆进入机场控制区，必须佩带机场控制区通行证并接受警卫人员的检查。  
机场控制区通行证，由民航公安机关按照国务院民用航空主管部门的有关规定制发和管理。

**Article 13** Persons and vehicles entering the airport control area must wear airport control area passes and accept inspection by security guards.  
Airport control area passes shall be issued and managed by the civil aviation public security organs in accordance with the relevant regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在航空器活动区和维修区内的人员、车辆必须按照规定路线行进，车辆、设备必须在指定位置停放，一切人员、车辆必须避让航空器。

**Article 14** Persons and vehicles in aircraft activity and maintenance areas must travel along prescribed routes; vehicles and equipment must be parked in designated locations; all persons and vehicles must yield to aircraft.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　停放在机场的民用航空器必须有专人警卫；各有关部门及其工作人员必须严格执行航空器警卫交接制度。

**Article 15** Civil aircraft parked at the airport must have dedicated guards; all relevant departments and their staff must strictly implement the aircraft guard handover system.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　机场内禁止下列行为：  
(一)攀(钻)越、损毁机场防护围栏及其他安全防护设施；  
(二)在机场控制区内狩猎、放牧、晾晒谷物、教练驾驶车辆；  
(三)无机场控制区通行证进入机场控制区；  
(四)随意穿越航空器跑道、滑行道；  
(五)强行登、占航空器；  
(六)谎报险情，制造混乱；  
(七)扰乱机场秩序的其他行为。

**Article 16** The following acts are prohibited within the airport:  
(1) climbing over (or drilling through) or damaging airport protective fences or other safety protection facilities;  
(2) hunting, grazing, drying grain, or teaching driving within the airport control area;  
(3) entering the airport control area without a pass;  
(4) arbitrarily crossing aircraft runways or taxiways;  
(5) forcibly boarding or occupying an aircraft;  
(6) falsely reporting dangers or creating confusion;  
(7) other acts disrupting airport order.

### 第三章　民用航空营运的安全保卫

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　承运人及其代理人出售客票，必须符合国务院民用航空主管部门的有关规定；对不符合规定的，不得售予客票。

**Article 17** When selling tickets, carriers and their agents must comply with the relevant regulations of the competent civil aviation authority of the State Council; tickets shall not be sold to persons who do not meet the requirements.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　承运人办理承运手续时，必须核对乘机人和行李。

**Article 18** When handling carriage procedures, the carrier must verify the passengers and their baggage.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　旅客登机时，承运人必须核对旅客人数。  
对已经办理登机手续而未登机的旅客的行李，不得装入或者留在航空器内。  
旅客在航空器飞行中途中止旅行时，必须将其行李卸下。

**Article 19** When passengers board the aircraft, the carrier must verify the number of passengers.  
Baggage of passengers who have checked in but have not boarded the aircraft shall not be loaded or left on the aircraft.  
If a passenger terminates their journey during a flight, their baggage must be unloaded.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　承运人对承运的行李、货物，在地面存储和运输期间，必须有专人监管。

**Article 20** The carrier must have dedicated personnel supervise the baggage and cargo being carried during ground storage and transportation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　配制、装载供应品的单位对装入航空器的供应品，必须保证其安全性。

**Article 21** The unit that prepares and loads supplies must ensure the safety of the supplies loaded into the aircraft.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　航空器在飞行中的安全保卫工作由机长统一负责。  
航空安全员在机长领导下，承担安全保卫的具体工作。  
机长、航空安全员和机组其他成员，应当严格履行职责，保护民用航空器及其所载人员和财产的安全。

**Article 22** The pilot-in-command shall be uniformly responsible for the security guard work of the aircraft during flight.  
The aviation security officer, under the leadership of the pilot-in-command, shall undertake the specific work of security guard.  
The pilot-in-command, the aviation security officer and other crew members shall strictly perform their duties and protect the safety of the civil aircraft and the persons and property on board.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　机长在执行职务时，可以行使下列权力：  
(一)在航空器起飞前，发现有关方面对航空器未采取本条例规定的安全措施的，拒绝起飞；  
(二)在航空器飞行中，对扰乱航空器内秩序，干扰机组人员正常工作而不听劝阻的人，采取必要的管束措施；  
(三)在航空器飞行中，对劫持、破坏航空器或者其他危及安全的行为，采取必要的措施；  
(四)在航空器飞行中遇到特殊情况时，对航空器的处置作最后决定。

**Article 23** When performing his duties, the pilot-in-command may exercise the following powers:  
(1) Before the aircraft takes off, if he discovers that the relevant party has not taken the safety measures provided for in these Regulations concerning the aircraft, he may refuse to take off;  
(2) During the flight of the aircraft, he may take necessary restraint measures against any person who disturbs order on board the aircraft, interferes with the normal work of the crew members and refuses to heed advice;  
(3) During the flight of the aircraft, he may take necessary measures against any act of hijacking or damaging the aircraft or any other act endangering safety; and  
(4) When encountering special circumstances during the flight of the aircraft, he shall make the final decision on the disposition of the aircraft.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　禁止下列扰乱民用航空营运秩序的行为：  
(一)倒卖购票证件、客票和航空运输企业的有效订座凭证；  
(二)冒用他人身份证件购票、登机；  
(三)利用客票交运或者捎带非旅客本人的行李物品；  
(四)将未经安全检查或者采取其他安全措施的物品装入航空器。

**Article 24** The following acts disturbing the order of civil aviation operations are prohibited:  
(1) Scalping of ticket purchasing certificates, passenger tickets and valid reservation vouchers of air transport enterprises;  
(2) Purchasing tickets or boarding by fraudulently using another person's identity certificate;  
(3) Using a passenger ticket to check in or carry luggage or articles not belonging to the passenger himself; and  
(4) Loading into an aircraft articles that have not undergone security check or have not been subject to other security measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　航空器内禁止下列行为：  
(一)在禁烟区吸烟；  
(二)抢占座位、行李舱(架)；  
(三)打架、酗酒、寻衅滋事；  
(四)盗窃、故意损坏或者擅自移动救生物品和设备；  
(五)危及飞行安全和扰乱航空器内秩序的其他行为。

**Article 25** The following acts are prohibited on board an aircraft:  
(1) Smoking in no-smoking areas;  
(2) Forcibly occupying seats or luggage compartments (racks);  
(3) Fighting, getting drunk, or picking quarrels and making trouble;  
(4) Stealing, intentionally damaging, or without authorization moving survival articles and equipment; and  
(5) Other acts endangering flight safety or disturbing order on board an aircraft.

### 第四章　安全检查

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　乘坐民用航空器的旅客和其他人员及其携带的行李物品，必须接受安全检查；但是，国务院规定免检的除外。  
拒绝接受安全检查的，不准登机，损失自行承担。

**Article 26** Passengers and other persons on board a civil aircraft and their luggage and articles carried must undergo security check, except those exempted by the State Council.  
Anyone who refuses to undergo security check shall not be allowed to board and shall bear the losses himself.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　安全检查人员应当查验旅客客票、身份证件和登机牌，使用仪器或者手工对旅客及其行李物品进行安全检查，必要时可以从严检查。  
已经安全检查的旅客应当在候机隔离区等待登机。

**Article 27** Security check personnel shall examine the passenger's ticket, identity certificate and boarding pass, and conduct security check on passengers and their luggage and articles by using instruments or by hand, and may conduct a stricter check when necessary.  
Passengers who have undergone security check shall wait for boarding in the departure security isolation area.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　进入候机隔离区的工作人员(包括机组人员)及其携带的物品，应当接受安全检查。  
接送旅客的人员和其他人员不得进入候机隔离区。

**Article 28** Staff members (including crew members) entering the departure security isolation area and the articles they carry shall undergo security check.  
Persons meeting or seeing off passengers and other persons shall not enter the departure security isolation area.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　外交邮袋免予安全检查。外交信使及其随身携带的其他物品应当接受安全检查；但是，中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的除外。

**Article 29** Diplomatic bags are exempt from security check. Diplomatic couriers and other articles carried by them on their persons shall undergo security check, unless otherwise provided for in international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　空运的货物必须经过安全检查或者对其采取的其他安全措施。  
货物托运人不得伪报品名托运或者在货物中夹带危险物品。

**Article 30** Goods to be carried by air must undergo security check or be subject to other security measures.  
A consignor of goods shall not consign goods by falsely reporting the description or conceal dangerous articles in the goods.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　航空邮件必须经过安全检查。发现可疑邮件时，安全检查部门应当会同邮政部门开包查验处理。

**Article 31** Air mail must undergo security check. When suspicious mail is discovered, the security check department shall, jointly with the postal department, open and inspect and handle it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　除国务院另有规定的外，乘坐民用航空器的，禁止随身携带或者交运下列物品：  
(一)枪支、弹药、军械、警械；  
(二)管制刀具；  
(三)易燃、易爆、有毒、腐蚀性、放射性物品；  
(四)国家规定的其他禁运物品。

**Article 32** Unless otherwise provided by the State Council, persons on board a civil aircraft are prohibited from carrying on their persons or checking in the following articles:  
(1) Firearms, ammunition, military weapons, and police weapons;  
(2) Controlled knives;  
(3) Inflammable, explosive, toxic, corrosive, or radioactive articles; and  
(4) Other articles prohibited for carriage as prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　除[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定的物品外，其他可以用于危害航空安全的物品，旅客不得随身携带，但是可以作为行李交运或者按照国务院民用航空主管部门的有关规定由机组人员带到目的地后交还。  
对含有易燃物质的生活用品实行限量携带。限量携带的物品及其数量，由国务院民用航空主管部门规定。

**Article 33** Apart from the articles specified in Article 32 of these Regulations, passengers shall not carry on their persons other articles that may be used to endanger aviation safety, but may check them in as luggage or, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, have them taken by crew members to the destination and returned thereafter.  
Articles for daily use containing inflammable substances shall be carried in limited quantities. The articles to be carried in limited quantities and the amounts thereof shall be prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　违反[本条例第十四条](#art-14)的规定或者有[本条例第十六条](#art-16)、第二十四条第一项、第二十五条所列行为，构成违反治安管理行为的，由民航公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》有关规定予以处罚；有[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)第二项所列行为的，由民航公安机关依照《中华人民共和国居民身份证法》有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 34** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 14 of these Regulations or commits any of the acts listed in Article 16, Article 24(1), or Article 25 of these Regulations, which constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be punished by the civil aviation public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Penalties for Administration of Public Security; whoever commits the act listed in Article 24(2) of these Regulations shall be punished by the civil aviation public security organ in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Resident Identity Cards.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本条例的有关规定，由民航公安机关按照下列规定予以处罚：  
(一)有[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)第四项所列行为的，可以处以警告或者3000元以下的罚款；  
(二)有[本条例第二十四条](#art-24)第三项所列行为的，可以处以警告、没收非法所得或者5000元以下罚款；  
(三)违反[本条例第三十条](#art-30)第二款、第三十二条的规定，尚未构成犯罪的，可以处以5000元以下罚款、没收或者扣留非法携带的物品。

**Article 35** Whoever violates the relevant provisions of these Regulations shall be punished by the civil aviation public security organ according to the following provisions:  
(1) Whoever commits the act listed in Article 24(4) of these Regulations may be given a warning or a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan;  
(2) Whoever commits the act listed in Article 24(3) of these Regulations may be given a warning, confiscation of illegal income, or a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan;  
(3) Whoever violates the provisions of Article 30(2) or Article 32 of these Regulations, if the act does not yet constitute a crime, may be given a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan, or confiscation or detention of the illegally carried articles.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例的规定，有下列情形之一的，民用航空主管部门可以对有关单位处以警告、停业整顿或者5万元以下的罚款；民航公安机关可以对直接责任人员处以警告或者500元以下的罚款：  
(一)违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)的规定，造成航空器失控的；  
(二)违反[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定，出售客票的；  
(三)违反[本条例第十八条](#art-18)的规定，承运人办理承运手续时，不核对乘机人和行李的；  
(四)违反[本条例第十九条](#art-19)的规定的；  
(五)违反[本条例第二十条](#art-20)、第二十一条、第三十条第一款、第三十一条的规定，对收运、装入航空器的物品不采取安全措施的。

**Article 36** In any of the following circumstances where the provisions of these Regulations are violated, the competent civil aviation authority may impose on the relevant entity a warning, an order to suspend operations for rectification, or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; the civil aviation public security organ may impose on the person directly responsible a warning or a fine of not more than 500 yuan:  
(1) Violating the provisions of Article 15 of these Regulations, causing the aircraft to become out of control;  
(2) Violating the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations by selling passenger tickets;  
(3) Violating the provisions of Article 18 of these Regulations, where the carrier fails to check the passenger and luggage when handling the carriage procedures;  
(4) Violating the provisions of Article 19 of these Regulations;  
(5) Violating the provisions of Articles 20, 21, 30(1), or 31 of these Regulations by failing to take safety measures for articles received, carried or loaded into the aircraft.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　违反本条例的有关规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Whoever violates the relevant provisions of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定的，除依照本章的规定予以处罚外，给单位或者个人造成财产损失的，应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 38** Where a violation of the provisions of these Regulations causes property losses to a unit or individual, the violator shall, in addition to being punished in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter, bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
“机场控制区”，是指根据安全需要在机场内划定的进出受到限制的区域。  
“候机隔离区”，是指根据安全需要在候机楼(室)内划定的供已经安全检查的出港旅客等待登机的区域及登机通道、摆渡车。  
“航空器活动区”，是指机场内用于航空器起飞、着陆以及与此有关的地面活动区域，包括跑道、滑行道、联络道、客机坪。

**Article 39** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings assigned to them:  
“Airport control area” means an area within an airport designated for security reasons where entry and exit are restricted.  
“Departure security isolation area” means the area within the terminal building (room) designated for security reasons for departing passengers who have undergone security check to wait for boarding, as well as the boarding passages and shuttle buses.  
“Aircraft movement area” means the area within an airport used for aircraft takeoff and landing and related ground activities, including runways, taxiways, connecting taxiways, and aircraft parking aprons.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 40** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
