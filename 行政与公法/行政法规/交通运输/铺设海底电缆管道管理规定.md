# 铺设海底电缆管道管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1989-02-11  
**生效日期**：1989-03-01

> (1989年1月20日国务院第32次常务会议通过　1989年2月11日中华人民共和国国务院令第27号发布　自1989年3月1日起施行) 第一条　为维护中华人民共和国国家主权和权益，合理开发利用海洋，有秩序地铺设和保护海底电缆、管道，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于在中华人民共和国内海、领海及大陆架上铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测及其他有关活动。 第三条　在中华人民共和国内海、领海及大陆架上铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测及其他有关活动的主管机关是中华人民共和国国家海洋局(以下简称主管机关)。 第四条　中国的企业、事业单位铺设海底电缆、管道，经其上级业务主管部门审批同意后，为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测等活动，依照本规定执行。 外国的公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人需要在中华人民共和国内海、领海铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测等活动，应当依照本规定报经主管机关批准；需要在中华人民共和国大陆架上进行上述活动的，应当事先通知主管机关，但其确定的海底电缆、管道路由，需经主管机关同意。 第五条　海底电缆、管道所有者(以下简称所有者)，须在为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测实施60天前，向主管机关提出书面申请。申请书应当包括以下内容： (一)所有者的名称、国籍、住所； (二)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测单位的名称、国籍、住所及主要负责人； (三)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测的精确地理区域； (四)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测的时间、内容、方法和设备，包括所用船舶的船名、国籍、吨位及其主要装备和性能。 主管机关应当自收到申请之日起30天内作出答复。 第六条　海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测完成后，所有者应当在计划铺设施工60天前，将最后确定的海底电缆、管道路由报主管机关审批，并附具以下资料： (一)海底电缆、管道的用途、使用材料及其特性； (二)精确的海底电缆、管道路线图和位置表以及起止点、中断点(站)和总长度；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为维护中华人民共和国国家主权和权益，合理开发利用海洋，有秩序地铺设和保护海底电缆、管道，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of safeguarding the sovereignty and rights and interests of the People's Republic of China, rationally developing and utilizing the oceans, and laying and protecting submarine cables and pipelines in an orderly manner.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于在中华人民共和国内海、领海及大陆架上铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测及其他有关活动。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to the laying of submarine cables and pipelines in the internal waters, territorial sea and continental shelf of the People's Republic of China, and to the route survey, exploration and other related activities carried out for such laying.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国内海、领海及大陆架上铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测及其他有关活动的主管机关是中华人民共和国国家海洋局(以下简称主管机关)。

**Article 3** The competent authority for the laying of submarine cables and pipelines in the internal waters, territorial sea and continental shelf of the People's Republic of China, and for the route survey, exploration and other related activities carried out for such laying, shall be the State Oceanic Administration of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the competent authority).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国的企业、事业单位铺设海底电缆、管道，经其上级业务主管部门审批同意后，为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测等活动，依照本规定执行。  
外国的公司、企业和其他经济组织或者个人需要在中华人民共和国内海、领海铺设海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测等活动，应当依照本规定报经主管机关批准；需要在中华人民共和国大陆架上进行上述活动的，应当事先通知主管机关，但其确定的海底电缆、管道路由，需经主管机关同意。

**Article 4** Where Chinese enterprises and institutions lay submarine cables and pipelines, the route survey, exploration and other activities carried out for such laying shall be conducted in accordance with these Provisions after approval by the competent business department at a higher level.  
Foreign companies, enterprises, other economic organizations or individuals that need to lay submarine cables and pipelines in the internal waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, or carry out route survey, exploration and other activities for such laying, shall report to the competent authority for approval according to these Provisions; where it is necessary to carry out the aforesaid activities on the continental shelf of the People's Republic of China, they shall notify the competent authority in advance, but the determined route of the submarine cable or pipeline shall be subject to the consent of the competent authority.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　海底电缆、管道所有者(以下简称所有者)，须在为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测实施60天前，向主管机关提出书面申请。申请书应当包括以下内容：  
(一)所有者的名称、国籍、住所；  
(二)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测单位的名称、国籍、住所及主要负责人；  
(三)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测的精确地理区域；  
(四)海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测的时间、内容、方法和设备，包括所用船舶的船名、国籍、吨位及其主要装备和性能。  
主管机关应当自收到申请之日起30天内作出答复。

**Article 5** The owner of a submarine cable or pipeline (hereinafter referred to as the owner) shall submit a written application to the competent authority 60 days before the implementation of the route survey and exploration carried out for laying. The application shall include the following contents:  
(1) the name, nationality and domicile of the owner;  
(2) the name, nationality and domicile of the unit conducting the route survey and exploration of the submarine cable or pipeline, and its principal responsible person;  
(3) the precise geographical area of the route survey and exploration of the submarine cable or pipeline;  
(4) the time, content, method and equipment of the route survey and exploration of the submarine cable or pipeline, including the name, nationality, tonnage and main equipment and performance of the vessel used.  
The competent authority shall make a reply within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海底电缆、管道路由调查、勘测完成后，所有者应当在计划铺设施工60天前，将最后确定的海底电缆、管道路由报主管机关审批，并附具以下资料：  
(一)海底电缆、管道的用途、使用材料及其特性；  
(二)精确的海底电缆、管道路线图和位置表以及起止点、中断点(站)和总长度；  
(三)铺设工程的施工单位、施工时间、施工计划、技术设备等；  
(四)铺设海底管道工程对海洋资源和环境影响报告书；  
(五)其他有关说明资料。  
主管机关应当自收到申请之日起30天内作出答复。

**Article 6** After the completion of the route survey and exploration of a submarine cable or pipeline, the owner shall, 60 days before the planned laying construction, submit the finally determined route of the submarine cable or pipeline to the competent authority for approval, and attach the following materials:  
(1) the purpose, materials used and characteristics of the submarine cable or pipeline;  
(2) the precise route chart and location table of the submarine cable or pipeline, as well as the starting and ending points, intermediate points (stations) and total length;  
(3) the construction unit, construction time, construction plan, technical equipment, etc. of the laying project;  
(4) the report on the impact of the submarine pipeline laying project on marine resources and environment;  
(5) other relevant explanatory materials.  
The competent authority shall make a reply within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　铺设施工完毕后，所有者应当将海底电缆、管道的路线图、位置表等说明资料报送主管机关备案，并抄送港监机关。  
在国家进行海洋开发利用、管理需要时，所有者有义务向主管机关进一步提供海底电缆、管道的准确资料。

**Article 7** After the completion of the laying construction, the owner shall submit the route chart, location table and other explanatory materials of the submarine cable or pipeline to the competent authority for record, and send a copy to the harbor superintendency authority.  
When the State needs to carry out marine development, utilization or management, the owner shall be obligated to further provide accurate information on the submarine cable or pipeline to the competent authority.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　海底电缆、管道的铺设和为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测活动，不得在获准作业区域以外的海域作业，也不得在获准区域内进行未经批准的作业。

**Article 8** The laying of submarine cables and pipelines and the route survey, exploration and other activities carried out for such laying shall not be conducted in sea areas outside the approved operation area, nor shall any unauthorized operation be conducted within the approved area.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　获准施工的海底电缆、管道在施工前或施工中如需变动，所有者应当及时向主管机关报告。如该项变动重大，主管机关可采取相应措施，直至责令其停止施工。

**Article 9** Where changes are required before or during construction of an approved submarine cable or pipeline, the owner shall report to the competent authority in a timely manner. If such changes are significant, the competent authority may take corresponding measures, up to ordering the suspension of construction.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海底电缆、管道的维修、改造、拆除和废弃，所有者应当提前向主管机关报告。路由变动较大的改造，依照本规定重新办理有关手续。  
外国船舶需要进入中国内海、领海进行海底电缆、管道的维修、改造、拆除活动时，除履行本条第一款规定的程序外，还应当依照中国法律的规定，报经中国有关机关批准。  
铺设在中国大陆架上的海底电缆、管道遭受损害，需要紧急修理时，外国维修船可在向主管机关报告的同时进入现场作业，但不得妨害中国的主权权利和管辖权。

**Article 10** The owner shall report to the competent authority in advance regarding the maintenance, alteration, removal or abandonment of a submarine cable or pipeline. For an alteration involving significant changes in the route, the relevant formalities shall be handled anew according to these Provisions.  
Where a foreign vessel needs to enter the internal waters or territorial sea of China to carry out maintenance, alteration or removal of submarine cables or pipelines, in addition to complying with the procedures set forth in the first paragraph of this Article, it shall also report to the relevant Chinese authorities for approval in accordance with the provisions of Chinese laws.  
Where a submarine cable or pipeline laid on the continental shelf of China is damaged and requires emergency repair, a foreign repair vessel may enter the site for operation while reporting to the competent authority, but shall not prejudice the sovereign rights and jurisdiction of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海底电缆、管道的路由调查、勘测和铺设、维修、拆除等施工作业，不得妨害海上正常秩序。  
海底电缆、管道的铺设或者拆除工程的遗留物，应当妥善处理，不得妨害海上正常秩序。

**Article 11** The construction operations such as route survey, exploration, laying, maintenance and removal of submarine cables and pipelines shall not interfere with the normal order on the sea.  
The remnants of laying or removal projects of submarine cables and pipelines shall be properly disposed of and shall not interfere with the normal order on the sea.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　铺设海底电缆、管道及其他海上作业，需要移动已铺设的海底电缆、管道时，应当先与所有者协商，并经主管机关批准后方可施工。

**Article 12** Where the laying of submarine cables or pipelines or other maritime operations require the movement of an already laid submarine cable or pipeline, consultation shall first be held with the owner, and construction may proceed only after approval by the competent authority.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事海上各种活动的作业者，必须保护已铺设的海底电缆、管道。造成损害的应当依法赔偿。  
其他海洋开发利用和已铺设的海底电缆、管道的正常使用发生纠纷时，由主管机关调解解决。

**Article 13** Operators engaged in various activities on the sea must protect the already laid submarine cables and pipelines. Where damage is caused, compensation shall be made according to law.  
Where a dispute arises between other marine development and utilization and the normal use of already laid submarine cables and pipelines, it shall be settled by the competent authority through mediation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　主管机关有权对海底电缆、管道的铺设、维修、改造、拆除、废弃以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测活动进行监督和检查。对违反本规定的，主管机关可处以警告、罚款直至责令其停止海上作业。  
前款所列处罚的具体办法，由主管机关商国务院有关主管部门制定。

**Article 14** The competent authority shall have the power to supervise and inspect the laying, maintenance, alteration, removal, abandonment of submarine cables and pipelines, as well as the route survey, exploration and other activities carried out for laying. For violations of these Provisions, the competent authority may impose a warning, a fine, or even order the suspension of maritime operations.  
The specific measures for the penalties listed in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the competent authority in consultation with the relevant department under the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　为海洋石油开发所铺设的超出石油开发区的海底电缆、管道的路由，应当在油(气)田总体开发方案审批前报主管机关，由主管机关商国家能源主管部门批准。  
在海洋石油开发区内铺设平台间或者平台与单点系泊间的海底电缆、管道，所有者应当在为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测和施工前，分别将[本规定第五条](#art-5)、第六条规定提供的内容，报主管机关备案。

**Article 15** The route of a submarine cable or pipeline laid for offshore oil development that extends beyond the oil development zone shall be submitted to the competent authority before the approval of the overall development plan for the oil (gas) field, and shall be approved by the competent authority in consultation with the national energy administration.  
For the laying of submarine cables or pipelines between platforms or between a platform and a single point mooring within an offshore oil development zone, the owner shall, before the route survey, exploration and construction carried out for such laying, submit the contents provided for in Article 5 and Article 6 of these Provisions to the competent authority for record.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　铺设、维修、改造、拆除、废弃海底电缆、管道以及为铺设所进行的路由调查、勘测活动，本规定未作规定的，适用国家其他有关法律、法规的规定。

**Article 16** For matters not provided for in these Provisions concerning the laying, maintenance, alteration, removal, abandonment of submarine cables and pipelines, and the route survey, exploration and other activities carried out for laying, the provisions of other relevant national laws and regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中华人民共和国军用海底电缆、管道的铺设依照本规定执行。军队可以制定具体实施办法。

**Article 17** The laying of military submarine cables and pipelines of the People's Republic of China shall be carried out in accordance with these Provisions. The armed forces may formulate specific implementation measures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　主管机关应当收集海底地形、海上构筑物分布等方面的资料，为海底电缆、管道的铺设及其调查、勘测活动提供咨询服务。

**Article 18** The competent authority shall collect data on seabed topography, distribution of maritime structures and other aspects, and shall provide consulting services for the laying of submarine cables and pipelines and their survey and exploration activities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定中的“电缆”系指通信电缆及电力电缆；“管道”系指输水、输气、输油及输送其他物质的管状输送设施。

**Article 19** The term "cable" as used in these Provisions means communication cables and power cables; the term "pipeline" means tubular conveying facilities for transporting water, gas, oil or other substances.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定由中华人民共和国国家海洋局负责解释。

**Article 20** The interpretation of these Provisions shall be the responsibility of the State Oceanic Administration of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定自1989年3月1日起施行。

**Article 21** These Provisions shall come into force as of March 1, 1989.
