# 中华人民共和国邮政法实施细则

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1990-11-12  
**生效日期**：1990-11-12

> (1990年11月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第65号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　根据《中华人民共和国邮政法》(以下简称《邮政法》)的规定，制定本实施细则。 第二条　中华人民共和国邮电部(以下简称邮电部)是国务院邮政主管部门，管理全国邮政工作。 各省、自治区、直辖市邮电管理局(以下简称邮电管理局)是地区邮政管理机构，管理该地区的邮政工作。 第三条　市、县邮电局(含邮政局，下同)是全民所有制的经营邮政业务的公用企业(以下简称邮政企业)，经邮电管理局授权，管理该地区的邮政工作。 邮电支局、邮电所、邮政支局、邮政所是办理邮政业务的分支机构(以下简称分支机构)；邮亭、邮政报刊亭等是邮政企业的服务点。 邮电代办所视同邮政企业所属的分支机构。 第四条　未经邮政企业委托，任何单位或者个人不得经营信函、明信片或者其他具有信件性质的物品的寄递业务，但国务院另有规定的除外。 信函是指以套封形式传递的缄封的信息的载体。其他具有信件性质的物品是指以符号、图像、音响等方式传递的信息的载体。具体内容由邮电部规定。 第五条　邮政企业委托其他单位或者个人代办邮政业务时，应当协商一致，并签订代办合同。 第六条　凡使用我国邮政业务的一切单位或者个人统称邮政用户(以下简称用户)。 第七条　邮政企业应当为用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便的邮政服务，保障用户使用邮政的合法权益。 任何单位或者个人均负有保护通信自由、通信秘密和邮件安全的责任；任何单位或者个人不得利用邮政业务进行法律、法规和政策所禁止的活动。 除因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪需要，由公安机关、国家安全机关或者检察机关依法对通信进行检查外，邮件在运输、传递过程中，任何单位或者个人不得以任何理由检查、扣留。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据《中华人民共和国邮政法》(以下简称《邮政法》)的规定，制定本实施细则。

**Article 1** These Detailed Implementation Rules are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Postal Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Postal Law").

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国邮电部(以下简称邮电部)是国务院邮政主管部门，管理全国邮政工作。  
各省、自治区、直辖市邮电管理局(以下简称邮电管理局)是地区邮政管理机构，管理该地区的邮政工作。

**Article 2** The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications") is the competent postal department under the State Council, and shall administer the postal work of the whole country.  
The provincial, autonomous regional, and municipal postal administrations (hereinafter referred to as "Postal Administrations") are regional postal management organs, and shall administer postal work in their respective regions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　市、县邮电局(含邮政局，下同)是全民所有制的经营邮政业务的公用企业(以下简称邮政企业)，经邮电管理局授权，管理该地区的邮政工作。  
邮电支局、邮电所、邮政支局、邮政所是办理邮政业务的分支机构(以下简称分支机构)；邮亭、邮政报刊亭等是邮政企业的服务点。  
邮电代办所视同邮政企业所属的分支机构。

**Article 3** Municipal and county posts and telecommunications offices (including postal offices, the same below) are public enterprises under ownership by the whole people that operate postal services (hereinafter referred to as "postal enterprises"), and shall, upon authorization by the Postal Administration, administer postal work in their respective regions.  
Suboffices of posts and telecommunications, postal stations, sub-post offices, and postal stations are branches handling postal business (hereinafter referred to as "branches"); postal kiosks, postal news stands, and the like are service points of postal enterprises.  
Postal agencies authorized to handle postal services shall be deemed as branches of postal enterprises.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　未经邮政企业委托，任何单位或者个人不得经营信函、明信片或者其他具有信件性质的物品的寄递业务，但国务院另有规定的除外。  
信函是指以套封形式传递的缄封的信息的载体。其他具有信件性质的物品是指以符号、图像、音响等方式传递的信息的载体。具体内容由邮电部规定。

**Article 4** Without entrustment by a postal enterprise, no unit or individual may engage in the business of delivering letters, postcards, or other articles of a letter nature, unless otherwise provided by the State Council.  
A letter refers to a sealed carrier of information transmitted in an enveloped form. Other articles of a letter nature refer to carriers of information transmitted by means of symbols, images, sounds, etc. The specific content thereof shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　邮政企业委托其他单位或者个人代办邮政业务时，应当协商一致，并签订代办合同。

**Article 5** Where a postal enterprise entrusts another unit or individual to handle postal business on its behalf, the parties shall reach consensus through consultation and conclude an agency contract.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　凡使用我国邮政业务的一切单位或者个人统称邮政用户(以下简称用户)。

**Article 6** All units or individuals that use the postal services of our country are collectively referred to as postal users (hereinafter referred to as "users").

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　邮政企业应当为用户提供迅速、准确、安全、方便的邮政服务，保障用户使用邮政的合法权益。  
任何单位或者个人均负有保护通信自由、通信秘密和邮件安全的责任；任何单位或者个人不得利用邮政业务进行法律、法规和政策所禁止的活动。  
除因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪需要，由公安机关、国家安全机关或者检察机关依法对通信进行检查外，邮件在运输、传递过程中，任何单位或者个人不得以任何理由检查、扣留。

**Article 7** A postal enterprise shall provide users with prompt, accurate, secure, and convenient postal services, and protect the lawful rights and interests of users in using postal services.  
Every unit or individual shall bear the responsibility of protecting the freedom and secrecy of correspondence and the security of mail matter; no unit or individual may use postal business to engage in activities prohibited by laws, regulations, and policies.  
Except where, for the needs of national security or the investigation of criminal offenses, the public security organs, state security organs, or procuratorial organs examine correspondence in accordance with the law, no unit or individual may examine or detain mail matter during transportation or transmission for any reason.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　因国家安全或者追查刑事犯罪需要，公安机关、国家安全机关、检察机关检查、扣留邮件，冻结汇款、储蓄存款时，必须依法向相关县或者县级以上的邮政企业、邮电管理局出具相应的检查、扣留、冻结通知书，并开列邮件、汇款、储蓄存款的具体节目，办理检查、扣留、冻结手续后，由邮政企业指派专人负责拣出，逐件登记后办理交接手续；对于不需要继续检查、扣留、冻结或者查明与案件无关的邮件、汇款、储蓄存款，应当及时退还邮政企业。邮件、汇款、储蓄存款在检查、扣留、冻结期间造成丢失、损毁的，由相关的公安机关、国家安全机关、检察机关负责赔偿。

**Article 8** Where, for the needs of national security or the investigation of criminal offenses, the public security organs, state security organs, or procuratorial organs examine or detain mail matter, or freeze remittances or savings deposits, they must, in accordance with the law, issue to the relevant county-level or higher postal enterprise or Postal Administration a corresponding notice of examination, detention, or freezing, and list the specific items of mail matter, remittances, or savings deposits; after going through the formalities of examination, detention, or freezing, the postal enterprise shall assign a specially designated person to pick them out, register them item by item, and then go through the handover formalities; for mail matter, remittances, or savings deposits that no longer need to be examined, detained, or frozen, or that are found to be irrelevant to the case, they shall be promptly returned to the postal enterprise. If loss or damage is caused to mail matter, remittances, or savings deposits during the period of examination, detention, or freezing, the relevant public security organ, state security organ, or procuratorial organ shall be liable for compensation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院、检察机关依法没收国内邮件、汇款、储蓄存款时，必须出具法律文书，向相关县或者县级以上邮政企业、邮电管理局办理手续。没收进出口国际邮递物品应当由海关作出决定，并办理手续。

**Article 9** Where a People's Court or a procuratorial organ confiscates domestic mail matter, remittances, or savings deposits in accordance with the law, it must produce legal documents and go through formalities with the relevant county-level or higher postal enterprise or Postal Administration. The confiscation of import or export international postal items shall be decided by the Customs, and the relevant formalities shall be gone through.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有关单位依照法律规定需要收集、调取证据、查阅邮政业务档案时，必须凭相关邮政企业所在地的公安机关、国家安全机关、检察机关、人民法院出具的书面证明，并开列邮件具体节目，向相关县或者县级以上的邮政企业、邮电管理局办理手续。

**Article 10** Where a relevant unit needs to collect or obtain evidence or consult postal business archives in accordance with legal provisions, it must present a written certificate issued by the public security organ, state security organ, procuratorial organ, or People's Court located in the place of the relevant postal enterprise, list the specific items of mail matter, and go through formalities with the relevant county-level or higher postal enterprise or Postal Administration.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位或者个人不得从事下列行为妨害邮政工作的正常进行：  
(一)损坏邮政设施；  
(二)在邮政企业及分支机构门前或者出入通道设摊、堆物，妨害用户用邮或者影响运邮车辆通行；  
(三)在办理邮政业务的场所无理取闹或者扰乱正常秩序；  
(四)阻碍邮政工作人员依法执行公务或者寻衅滋事；  
(五)拦截邮政运输工具、非法阻碍邮件运递或者强行登乘邮政运输工具；  
(六)非法检查或者截留邮件；  
(七)其他妨害邮政企业及分支机构或者邮政工作人员正常工作的行为。

**Article 11** No unit or individual may engage in any of the following acts that hinder the normal conduct of postal work:  
(1) damaging postal facilities;  
(2) setting up stalls or piling up objects in front of postal enterprises and branches or on access roads, thereby hindering users' use of postal services or affecting the passage of postal vehicles;  
(3) making unreasonable troubles or disrupting normal order in premises where postal business is conducted;  
(4) obstructing postal staff from performing official duties in accordance with the law or picking quarrels and provoking trouble;  
(5) intercepting postal transport vehicles, illegally obstructing the transportation or delivery of mail matter, or forcibly boarding postal transport vehicles;  
(6) illegally examining or intercepting mail matter;  
(7) other acts that hinder the normal work of postal enterprises and branches or postal staff.

### 第二章　邮政企业的设置和邮政设施

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　邮政企业及分支机构的设置标准，由邮电部规定；邮政企业的设置或者撤销，由邮电部批准；分支机构的设置或者撤销，由邮电管理局批准，报邮电部备案。

**Article 12** The standards for the establishment of postal enterprises and branches shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications; the establishment or dissolution of postal enterprises shall be subject to approval by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications; the establishment or dissolution of branches shall be subject to approval by the Postal Administration and shall be reported to the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications for recordation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　地方各级人民政府应当将邮政企业及分支机构的设置和邮政设施的建设纳入城乡建设规划。

**Article 13** Local people's governments at all levels shall incorporate the establishment of postal enterprises and branches and the construction of postal facilities into urban and rural construction plans.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设城市新区、独立工矿区、住宅区或者旧城区成片改造，应当同时规划和设置与之配套的邮政企业及分支机构和邮政设施。

**Article 14** When constructing new urban areas, independent industrial and mining areas, residential areas, or carrying out block renovation of old urban districts, the planning and establishment of supporting postal enterprises, branches, and postal facilities shall be carried out simultaneously.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　邮政企业依法设置邮亭、邮政报刊亭、邮筒、信箱或者流动服务时，有关单位或者个人应当提供方便。

**Article 15** Where a postal enterprise sets up postal kiosks, postal news stands, postboxes, mailboxes, or provides mobile services in accordance with the law, the relevant units or individuals shall provide convenience.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　接收邮件的信报箱是居民楼房的配套设施，设计单位应当将其纳入民用住宅建筑设计标准。  
居民楼房每一单元的地面层应当安装与住户房号相适应的信报箱或者在楼房集中处设置信报箱间(群)，供住户接收邮件使用。  
信报箱由居民楼房的产权所有者或者管理单位负责维修、更换，也可以委托当地邮政企业或者其分支机构维修、更换，所需工料费由委托单位支付。

**Article 16** Mailboxes for receiving mail are ancillary facilities of residential buildings, and the designing unit shall incorporate them into the design standards for civil residential buildings.  
At the ground floor of each unit of a residential building, mailboxes corresponding to the household numbers shall be installed, or a mailbox room (group) shall be set up at a centralized location of the building, for residents to receive mail.  
Mailboxes shall be maintained or replaced by the owner of the property right of the residential building or the managing unit; they may also be entrusted to the local postal enterprise or its branch for maintenance or replacement, with the required labor and material costs paid by the entrusting unit.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　较大的车站、机场、港口、饭店，应当在方便旅客的地方提供办理邮政业务的场所；邮政企业应当提供邮政业务服务。

**Article 17** Larger railway stations, airports, ports, and hotels shall provide premises for handling postal business in places convenient for passengers; postal enterprises shall provide postal business services.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　任何单位因建设需要，征用、拆迁邮政企业及分支机构或者邮政设施时，应当与当地邮政企业协商，在保证邮政通信正常进行的情况下，应当将邮政企业及分支机构、邮政设施迁至适宜的地方或者另建，所需费用由征用、拆迁单位承担。

**Article 18** Where any unit, due to construction needs, requisitions or demolishes postal enterprises and branches or postal facilities, it shall consult with the local postal enterprise; under the premise of ensuring the normal operation of postal communications, it shall relocate the postal enterprises, branches, and postal facilities to suitable places or build them anew, with the required expenses borne by the requisitioning or demolishing unit.

### 第三章　邮政业务的种类

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　邮政企业经营国内、国际邮件的寄递业务和邮件的特快专递业务。  
国内邮件是指在中华人民共和国领域内互寄的邮件，其中寄自或者寄往香港、澳门、台湾地区的邮件称港澳台地区邮件；国际邮件是指中华人民共和国与其他国家、地区之间互寄的邮件和其他国家、地区通过中国境内经转的邮件。

**Article 19** Postal enterprises shall operate the business of domestic and international mail delivery and the express mail service for mail matter.  
Domestic mail refers to mail exchanged within the territory of the People's Republic of China, among which mail sent from or to Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan is called mail from/to Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan; international mail refers to mail exchanged between the People's Republic of China and other countries or regions, and mail from other countries or regions that transits through China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国内报刊发行业务是指报刊社委托邮政企业发行报纸、杂志的业务。

**Article 20** The domestic newspaper and periodical distribution business refers to the business where newspaper and periodical agencies entrust postal enterprises to distribute newspapers and magazines.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　报刊社委托邮政企业发行报刊时，应当根据报刊发行的范围向指定的邮政企业或者邮政报刊发行局，出具有关主管部门批准出版和领有报纸、期刊登记证的证明。邮政企业有接办发行能力的，应当与报刊社遵循平等互利、协商一致、等价有偿的原则，按照国家有关规定签订报刊发行合同。

**Article 21** When a newspaper or periodical agency entrusts a postal enterprise to distribute its newspapers or periodicals, it shall, based on the scope of distribution, present to the designated postal enterprise or postal newspaper and periodical distribution bureau a certificate proving that the publication has been approved for publication by the relevant competent authority and that a registration certificate for a newspaper or periodical has been obtained. If the postal enterprise has the capability to undertake the distribution, it shall sign a newspaper and periodical distribution contract with the agency in accordance with the principles of equality and mutual benefit, consensus through consultation, and compensation for equivalent value, and pursuant to the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　邮政储蓄、邮政汇兑业务是邮政企业为国家积聚资金、沟通经济往来所经办的金融业务，由邮电部统一管理，并按照国家有关规定，在金融业务上接受中国人民银行的指导。各相关银行应当为邮政企业办理的储蓄、汇兑业务提供便利。

**Article 22** Postal savings and postal remittance businesses are financial businesses operated by postal enterprises to accumulate funds for the State and facilitate economic exchanges; they shall be uniformly managed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications and, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, shall accept guidance from the People's Bank of China in financial business matters. Relevant banks shall provide convenience for the savings and remittance businesses handled by postal enterprises.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各项邮政业务的具体种类和邮件分类，由邮电部规定。

**Article 23** The specific types of various postal services and the classification of mail matter shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

### 第四章　邮政业务资费和邮资凭证

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　邮政业务的基本资费是指邮政专营的国内平常信函、明信片的资费；非基本资费是指基本资费以外的邮政业务资费。  
邮政业务的基本资费由国务院物价主管部门制定，报国务院批准；非基本资费由邮电部规定。

**Article 24** The basic tariff for postal services refers to the tariff for ordinary domestic letters and postcards, which are exclusively operated by the postal service; non-basic tariff refers to the tariff for postal services other than the basic tariff.  
The basic tariff for postal services shall be formulated by the competent pricing authority under the State Council and reported to the State Council for approval; the non-basic tariff shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　制定和调整邮政业务资费的依据是：  
(一)以保证邮政通信企业的成本费用和自我发展能力为原则，适应社会需要；  
(二)国内邮政业务资费，依据支出费用的变动相应调整；  
(三)国际邮政业务资费，依据“万国邮政联盟”的规定和国际、国内成本费用以及人民币汇率比价的变化作相应调整。

**Article 25** The bases for establishing and adjusting postal service tariffs are:  
(1) taking the principle of ensuring the cost expenses and self-development capability of postal communications enterprises and meeting social needs;  
(2) domestic postal service tariffs shall be adjusted correspondingly based on changes in expenditure costs;  
(3) international postal service tariffs shall be adjusted correspondingly based on the provisions of the Universal Postal Union and changes in international and domestic costs and the exchange rate of Renminbi.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　邮资凭证是邮电部发行的，作为邮件纳费标志的有价证券，包括邮票，印在邮资信封、邮资明信片、邮资邮简上的邮票图案，邮资机打印的邮资符志。

**Article 26** Postage certificates are valuable securities issued by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications as evidence of payment of postage for mail matter, including stamps, stamp patterns printed on stamped envelopes, stamped postcards, and stamped letter sheets, and postage indicia printed by postage meters.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　“万国邮政联盟”发行的国际回信券，可以根据国际统一规定，兑换成等于特定重量级别的特定类别资费的邮票，但是不得兑换现金。

**Article 27** International reply coupons issued by the Universal Postal Union may, in accordance with uniform international regulations, be exchanged for stamps equal to the tariff of a specific category for a specific weight class, but may not be exchanged for cash.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　因工作需要，仿印邮票图案的，必须按照仿印邮票图案的有关规定，报经邮电部邮票主管部门或者有关邮电管理局审核、批准。  
印刷单位不得承印未经批准的仿印邮票图案和与邮票相似的印件。

**Article 28** Where, for work needs, a stamp pattern is imitated and printed, the matter must be submitted to the competent stamp department of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications or the relevant Postal Administration for examination and approval in accordance with the relevant provisions on imitating and printing stamp patterns.  
No printing unit may accept the printing of unauthorized imitated stamp patterns or printing matter similar to stamps.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　印制通信使用的信封必须符合国家标准，并由当地邮电管理局监制。

**Article 29** Envelopes used for correspondence shall conform to national standards and shall be manufactured under the supervision of the local Postal Administration.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　印制明信片必须符合邮电部规定的规格标准。  
县以上邮政企业，经邮电管理局批准，可以印制、发行带有“中国人民邮政”字样的明信片；其他单位印制明信片，由当地邮电管理局监制，但不得带有“中国人民邮政”字样。

**Article 30** The printing of postcards shall conform to the specifications and standards prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.  
Postal enterprises at or above the county level may, with the approval of the Postal Administration, print and issue postcards bearing the characters "People's Republic of China Postal Service"; other units printing postcards shall have them manufactured under the supervision of the local Postal Administration, but such postcards shall not bear the characters "People's Republic of China Postal Service".

### 第五章　邮件的寄递和损失赔偿

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　义务兵从部队发出的平常信函，免费寄递，其他军人不得免费寄递信函。义务兵寄递平常信函的监督管理办法由邮电部会同中国人民解放军有关部门制定。

**Article 31** Ordinary letters sent by compulsory servicemen from their military units shall be delivered free of charge; other military personnel may not send letters free of charge. The supervisory and administrative measures for the sending of ordinary letters by compulsory servicemen shall be formulated by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications jointly with the relevant departments of the Chinese People's Liberation Army.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　用户交寄邮件应当符合邮电部规定的准寄内容、封装规格、书写格式，并正确书写邮政编码，其中，用户交寄信函使用的信封，必须符合国家标准；邮件封面和邮政业务单式上不得印(写)有或者粘贴与邮件无关的文字或者其他物品；邮资凭证正面不得涂抹、覆盖其他物品；不得使用伪造、仿印、剪割拼补、加工去污的邮资凭证。

**Article 32** When sending mail, users shall comply with the permissible contents, packaging specifications, and writing formats prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, and correctly write the postal code; among these, the envelopes used by users for sending letters must conform to national standards; no text or other articles irrelevant to the mail may be printed (written) on or pasted onto the cover of the mail or postal business forms; the front of postage certificates may not be smeared or covered by other articles; no forged, imitated, cut, patched, processed, or defaced postage certificates may be used.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　禁止寄递或者在邮件内夹带下列物品：  
(一)法律规定禁止流通或者寄递的物品；  
(二)反动报刊书籍、宣传品或者淫秽物品；  
(三)爆炸性、易燃性、腐蚀性、放射性、毒性等危险物品；  
(四)妨害公共卫生的物品；  
(五)容易腐烂的物品；  
(六)各种活的动物；  
(七)各种货币；  
(八)不适合邮寄条件的物品；  
(九)包装不妥，可能危害人身安全、污染或者损毁其他邮件、设备的物品。  
前款物品，符合邮电部特准交寄规定并确保安全的，可以收寄。

**Article 33** The following articles are prohibited from being sent or enclosed in mail matter:  
(1) articles whose circulation or delivery is prohibited by law;  
(2) reactionary newspapers, books, periodicals, propaganda materials, or obscene articles;  
(3) explosive, flammable, corrosive, radioactive, toxic, and other dangerous articles;  
(4) articles harmful to public health;  
(5) perishable articles;  
(6) various live animals;  
(7) various currencies;  
(8) articles unsuitable for mailing conditions;  
(9) articles that are improperly packed and may endanger personal safety, or contaminate or damage other mail or equipment.  
Articles listed in the preceding paragraph may be accepted for delivery if they comply with the special provisions of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications and safety is ensured.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国内限量寄递物品由邮电部会同国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 34** Domestic articles subject to limited quantity for delivery shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对于违反禁寄、限寄规定寄递的物品，邮政企业或者分支机构应当根据其种类、性质、数量等分别作出下列处理：  
(一)不予寄递；  
(二)通知寄件人限期领回，逾期不领的就地处理；  
(三)移送相关部门依法处理；  
(四)造成危害人身安全或者污染、损毁其他邮件、设备的，由寄件人承担赔偿责任。  
按照前款(二)项、(三)项处理所需的费用，由寄件人承担。

**Article 35** For articles sent in violation of the prohibitions or restrictions on delivery, the postal enterprise or branch shall, based on their category, nature, quantity, etc., handle them respectively as follows:  
(1) refusing to deliver them;  
(2) notifying the sender to take them back within a specified time limit, and handling them locally if they are not taken back within the time limit;  
(3) transferring them to the relevant department for handling in accordance with the law;  
(4) if they cause harm to personal safety or contaminate or damage other mail or equipment, the sender shall bear compensation liability.  
The expenses required for handling pursuant to items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph shall be borne by the sender.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　新建的企业、事业、居民住宅，应当由单位或者居民住宅的主管部门到当地邮政企业或者分支机构办理邮件投递登记手续；单位更改名称、收件人变更地址，应当事先通知当地邮政企业或者分支机构，也可以办理邮件改寄新址手续。邮政企业应当公布登记地点和电话号码。  
具备下列条件者，有关邮政企业或者分支机构应当予以登记，并自登记之日起九十日内安排投递：  
(一)具备邮政车辆和邮政工作人员执行公务的通行条件；  
(二)有公安机关统一编制的门牌号数；  
(三)已安装接收邮件的信报箱或者已设立收发室；  
(四)按规定需要办理中外文名称登记的，应当办妥手续。

**Article 36** For newly built enterprises, institutions, or residential buildings, the unit or the competent department of the residential building shall go through the formalities of mail delivery registration with the local postal enterprise or branch; if a unit changes its name or a recipient changes his/her address, the unit or the recipient shall notify the local postal enterprise or branch in advance, and may also go through the formalities of forwarding the mail to the new address. The postal enterprise shall publicize the registration location and telephone number.  
If the following conditions are met, the relevant postal enterprise or branch shall register the matter and arrange delivery within ninety days from the date of registration:  
(1) conditions exist for postal vehicles and postal staff to pass through for performing official duties;  
(2) a house number uniformly compiled by the public security organ is available;  
(3) a mailbox for receiving mail has been installed or a mail room has been set up;  
(4) if name registration in both Chinese and foreign languages is required according to regulations, such formalities shall have been completed.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　邮件的投递方式，除邮电部另有规定外，按下列方式投递：  
(一)按址投递  
城镇居民的邮件，按收件人地址投递到平房院落门口或者楼房地面层的信报箱或者收发室。单位、单位内附设的机构和个人以及单位院内宿舍用户的邮件，投递到单位收发室。收发室应当设在楼房的地面层，两个以上单位同在一处的，应当商定统一接收邮件的地点。需要上楼投递邮件、报刊的，用户应当与相关邮政企业或者分支机构协商，并按照规定由用户支付特殊服务费。  
农村、牧区的邮件，根据交通条件和邮件量的具体情况，一般投递到乡或者行政村的固定地点；乡或者行政村以下的邮件，由乡人民政府或者村民委员会与邮政企业或者分支机构协商妥交收件人的方式。  
寄交船舶的邮件，投递到船舶隶属单位的收发室。  
(二)用户领取  
必须凭通知单到邮政企业或者分支机构办理手续才能领取的邮件，以邮政信箱(用户专用信箱)号码为收件人地址的邮件，存局候领的邮件，超出按址投递规定重量的邮件以及大宗邮件，采用用户领取的方式。

**Article 37** The method of mail delivery, unless otherwise provided by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, shall be as follows:  
(1) Delivery by address  
Mail for urban residents shall be delivered to the doorstep of a single-story courtyard or to the mailbox or mail room on the ground floor of a multi-story building according to the recipient's address. Mail for units, agencies attached to units, individuals, and users living in dormitories within a unit's compound shall be delivered to the unit's mail room. The mail room shall be located on the ground floor of the building; if two or more units are located in the same place, they shall agree on a unified location for receiving mail. If delivery of mail or newspapers and periodicals requires going upstairs, the user shall consult with the relevant postal enterprise or branch and pay special service fees in accordance with the regulations.  
Mail for rural and pastoral areas, depending on the traffic conditions and the specific circumstances of the volume of mail, shall generally be delivered to a fixed location in the township or administrative village; for mail below the township or administrative village level, the township people's government or the villagers' committee shall consult with the postal enterprise or branch to determine the method of delivery to the recipient.  
Mail addressed to ships shall be delivered to the mail room of the unit to which the ship belongs.  
(2) User collection  
Mail that must be collected by presenting a notice and going through formalities at the postal enterprise or branch, mail addressed to a post office box (user-specific box) number, mail to be held at the post office until called for, mail exceeding the weight limit for delivery by address, and bulk mail shall be collected by the user.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　收件人领取给据邮件，收款人兑领汇款，应当向相关邮政企业或者分支机构交验本人有效证件，并在相关单式上盖章或者签名。  
代收人受收件(款)人委托，代收给据邮件(汇款)时，应当交验收件(款)人和代收人的有效证件，经邮政企业或者分支机构确认后，由代收人盖章或者签名接收。  
有效证件包括居民身份证、户口簿、工作证。

**Article 38** When a recipient collects registered mail or a payee cashes a remittance, the said person shall present his/her valid identification to the relevant postal enterprise or branch and affix his/her seal or signature on the relevant form.  
When a proxy, authorized by the recipient (payee), collects registered mail (remittance) on his/her behalf, the proxy shall present the valid identification of both the recipient (payee) and the proxy; after confirmation by the postal enterprise or branch, the proxy shall affix his/her seal or signature to receive the item.  
Valid identification includes the resident identity card, household registration booklet, and work card.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　收件人接收给据邮件时发现封皮破损，应当场声明，并核对内件。确属邮政企业或者分支机构的责任而造成内件短少、损毁的，或者由于邮政企业、分支机构的责任造成给据邮件丢失、损毁的，邮政企业或者分支机构应当按照规定予以赔偿。由于收件人所在单位收发人员的过失造成给据邮件丢失、损毁、内件短少者，相关收发人员应当承担规定的赔偿责任。  
邮件运递的具体要求由邮电部规定，并予以公告。邮件运递违反邮电部规定的，邮政企业或者分支机构应当向用户补偿，具体补偿办法由邮电部规定。

**Article 39** When a recipient discovers that the cover of a registered mail item is damaged upon receipt, the recipient shall declare it on the spot and check the contents. If it is indeed due to the fault of the postal enterprise or branch that the contents are missing or damaged, or if the registered mail item is lost or damaged due to the fault of the postal enterprise or branch, the postal enterprise or branch shall provide compensation in accordance with the regulations. If the loss, damage, or shortage of contents of a registered mail item is caused by the negligence of the mail room staff of the unit where the recipient works, the relevant mail room staff shall bear the prescribed compensation liability.  
The specific requirements for mail transportation and delivery shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications and shall be made public. If mail transportation and delivery violate the provisions of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, the postal enterprise or branch shall provide compensation to the user; the specific compensation measures shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　用户误收的邮件，应当及时退还邮政企业或者分支机构；用户误拆的邮件应当重封签章后退还邮政企业或者分支机构，并对误拆邮件的内容保守秘密。

**Article 40** If a user receives mail by mistake, the user shall promptly return it to the postal enterprise or branch; if a user opens mail by mistake, the user shall reseal it, affix a seal or signature, and then return it to the postal enterprise or branch, and shall keep confidential the contents of the mistakenly opened mail.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　单位收发人员接收给据邮件时，应当认真点核无误后，在相关清单上盖章签收。  
收发人员对于各种邮件负有保护和及时传送的责任，不得私拆、隐匿、毁弃邮件或者撕揭邮票。

**Article 41** When receiving registered mail, the mail room staff of a unit shall carefully check and confirm it is correct, then affix their seal and sign for receipt on the relevant list.  
Mail room staff shall bear the responsibility of protecting and promptly transmitting all kinds of mail and shall not open, conceal, destroy, or discard mail, nor tear off stamps.

### 第六章　邮件的运输、验关和检疫

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　邮电部、邮电管理局应当把邮件运输流向流量变化情况及时通告相关运输部门。各运输部门应当根据邮政通信需要，优先提供有效的车次、航班、舱容。  
相关运输部门应当在车站、机场、港口、码头妥善安排装卸、储存邮件和作业所需的场地、出入通道、房屋，以及通报行车或者航行情况的信息设施。  
新建、改建、扩建车站、机场、港口、码头，应当统一规划邮件存放、转运所需的场地和通道，其有关基建费用由邮政企业承担。

**Article 42** The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications and the Postal Administrations shall promptly notify the relevant transport departments of changes in the flow and volume of mail transportation. Each transport department shall, based on the needs of postal communications, give priority to providing effective train schedules, flights, and cargo space.  
The relevant transport departments shall properly arrange at railway stations, airports, ports, and wharves the premises, access roads, rooms, and information facilities for loading, unloading, storing mail and for operations, as well as for reporting traffic or navigation conditions.  
When building, rebuilding, or expanding railway stations, airports, ports, or wharves, unified planning shall be made for the premises and access roads needed for storing and transferring mail, with the relevant capital construction expenses borne by the postal enterprises.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　邮政企业委托运输单位运送邮件，应当签订运邮协议。

**Article 43** Where a postal enterprise entrusts a transport unit to transport mail, it shall enter into a mail transport agreement.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　运输单位承运的邮件应当先于货物发运。因故临时停运或者改变运行时间、停靠位置时，运输单位应当及时通知邮政企业或者分支机构。

**Article 44** Mail carried by a transport unit shall be dispatched before cargo. If transportation is temporarily suspended or the running time or stopping position is changed for any reason, the transport unit shall promptly notify the postal enterprise or branch.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　载有邮件的船舶应当悬挂邮旗，各有关港口对于悬挂邮旗的船舶应当优先放行。

**Article 45** Vessels carrying mail shall fly a postal flag; relevant ports shall give priority to passage for vessels flying a postal flag.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　运输单位承运邮件，除邮政企业或者分支机构派员押运者外，应当与邮政企业或者分支机构办理交接签收手续。  
除不可抗力原因外，邮件在运输单位保管期间或者在运输途中发生丢失、短少、损毁等，相关运输单位应当按照运邮协议的规定予以赔偿。  
运邮船舶发生海难必须抛弃所载货物时，非至最后，不得抛弃所运邮件。

**Article 46** When a transport unit carries mail, unless personnel are dispatched by the postal enterprise or branch to escort the mail, the transport unit shall go through the handover and receipt formalities with the postal enterprise or branch.  
Except for the cause of force majeure, if mail suffers loss, shortage, damage, etc., during the period when it is under the custody of the transport unit or during transportation, the relevant transport unit shall provide compensation in accordance with the provisions of the mail transport agreement.  
If a vessel transporting mail encounters a maritime peril and the carried cargo must be jettisoned, the mail being carried shall not be jettisoned unless it is the last resort.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　执行邮件运输和投递任务的车、船、邮政工作人员通过桥梁、渡口、隧道、检查站时，有关方面应当优先放行。带有邮政专用标志的邮政车辆在运递邮件时，凭公安机关核发的通行证，可以不受禁行路线、禁停地段的限制。运邮车辆或者邮政工作人员在运递邮件途中违章，有关主管部门应当记录后放行，待其完成运递任务后，再行处理。

**Article 47** When vehicles, vessels, or postal staff performing tasks of mail transportation and delivery pass through bridges, ferries, tunnels, or checkpoints, the relevant parties shall give them priority passage. Postal vehicles bearing special postal marks, when transporting mail, may, upon presentation of a pass issued by the public security organ, be exempted from restrictions on prohibited routes and no-parking zones. If a mail transport vehicle or postal staff violates traffic regulations while transporting or delivering mail, the relevant competent authority shall record the violation and then let them pass, and shall handle the matter after they have completed the transportation or delivery task.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　邮政企业依据运输工具到站(港)、离站(港)时间和运递时限制订的作业时间表应当在变更前三日通知海关。海关应当按照邮政企业通知的作业时间表派员到场监管国际邮袋、查验进出口国际邮递物品；逾时不到场，延误运递时限造成的相关责任，由海关承担。  
海关依法查验国际邮包时，在设关地应当与用户当面查验。收、寄件人不能到场的，由海关开拆查验，邮政工作人员在场配合。被开拆查验的邮包，由海关和邮政企业共同封装，双方加具封签或者戳记。海关依法开拆查验的印刷品，应当重封并加具海关封签或者戳记。

**Article 48** The operation schedule formulated by a postal enterprise based on the arrival (port) and departure (port) times of transport means and the prescribed delivery time limits shall be notified to the Customs three days before any change. The Customs shall, in accordance with the operation schedule notified by the postal enterprise, dispatch personnel to the site to supervise international mailbags and inspect import and export international postal items; if the Customs fails to arrive on time, causing delays in delivery time limits, the relevant liability shall be borne by the Customs.  
When the Customs inspects an international postal parcel in accordance with the law, it shall conduct the inspection in the presence of the user at the place where the Customs office is located. If the recipient or sender cannot be present, the Customs shall open and inspect the parcel, with postal staff present to assist. The parcel that has been opened and inspected shall be resealed jointly by the Customs and the postal enterprise, with both parties affixing seals or marks. Printed matter that has been opened and inspected by the Customs in accordance with the law shall be resealed and affixed with a Customs seal or mark.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　用户交寄应当施行卫生检疫或者动植物检疫的邮件，必须附有检疫证书。检疫部门应当及时对邮件进行验放，以保证邮件的运递时限。

**Article 49** When a user sends mail that is subject to sanitary quarantine or animal and plant quarantine, the user must attach the relevant quarantine certificate. The quarantine department shall promptly inspect and release the mail so as to ensure the delivery time limit of the mail.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　海关、检疫部门依法查验国际邮递物品或者检疫邮件，应当注意爱护；需要封存时，除向寄件人或者收件人发出通知外，应当同邮政企业或者分支机构履行交接手续，并负责保管，封存期不得超过四十五日。特殊情况需要延长封存期的，应当征得邮政企业或者分支机构及寄件人或者收件人的同意，并以不致造成被封存国际邮递物品或者邮件的损失为前提。被封存国际邮递物品或者邮件退还邮政企业或者分支机构时，邮政工作人员应当核对无误后予以签收。  
依法没收国际邮递物品或者经卫生、动植物检疫必须依法销毁的邮件，海关或者检疫部门应当出具没收或者检疫处理通知单，并及时通知寄件人或者收件人和邮政企业或者分支机构。  
国际邮递物品在依法查验、封存期间，发生丢失、短少、损毁等，由海关或者检疫部门负责赔偿或处理。

**Article 50** When the Customs and quarantine departments inspect international postal items or quarantine mail in accordance with the law, they shall take care to protect them; if sealing is required, they shall, in addition to issuing a notice to the sender or recipient, go through handover formalities with the postal enterprise or branch and be responsible for custody; the period of sealing shall not exceed forty-five days. If an extension of the sealing period is necessary due to special circumstances, the consent of the postal enterprise or branch and the sender or recipient shall be obtained, provided that such extension does not cause damage to the sealed international postal items or mail. When sealed international postal items or mail are returned to the postal enterprise or branch, postal staff shall check them and confirm they are correct, then sign for receipt.  
If international postal items are confiscated in accordance with the law, or mail must be destroyed in accordance with the law due to sanitary or animal and plant quarantine, the Customs or quarantine department shall issue a notice of confiscation or quarantine disposal and promptly notify the sender or recipient and the postal enterprise or branch.  
If loss, shortage, damage, etc., occur to international postal items during the period of lawful inspection or sealing, the Customs or quarantine department shall be responsible for compensation or disposition.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　依法查验邮递物品或者对邮件实施检疫需要使用邮政企业或者分支机构的场地和房屋时，由邮政企业与有关部门根据工作需要和实际可能协商解决。

**Article 51** Where it is necessary to use the premises or buildings of a postal enterprise or its branch for inspecting postal items according to law or for implementing quarantine on mail matters, the postal enterprise and the relevant department shall resolve the matter through consultation based on work needs and actual feasibility.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　由海关依法处理的无着进口国际邮包，海关应当支付相关邮政费用。

**Article 52** The Customs shall pay the relevant postal expenses for undeliverable imported international postal parcels that are disposed of by the Customs according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　出口国际邮件的海关关单的传递方式由海关总署与邮电部商定。

**Article 53** The method of transmission of customs declarations for outgoing international mail matters shall be determined by the General Administration of Customs and the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications through consultation.

### 第七章　罚则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本细则第十一条规定的，由有关部门按照国家有关规定根据情节轻重，予以处罚；违反治安管理有关规定的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》处罚。

**Article 54** Anyone who violates the provisions of Article 11 of these Rules shall be punished by the relevant department according to the relevant provisions of the State and based on the severity of the circumstances; if the violation involves relevant provisions on public security administration, the public security organ shall impose punishment in accordance with the 'Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Penalties for Public Security'.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本细则第三十三条第三项规定，尚未造成严重后果的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚条例》处理。

**Article 55** Anyone who violates the provisions of Item 3 of Article 33 of these Rules but has not caused serious consequences shall be dealt with by the public security organ in accordance with the 'Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Administrative Penalties for Public Security'.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　伪造或者冒用邮政专用标志、邮政标志服或者邮政日戳、邮政夹钳、邮袋等邮政专用品的，由邮电管理局或其授权单位处以一千五百元以下罚款，并没收有关物品。

**Article 56** Anyone who forges or fraudulently uses postal special signs, postal uniform, or postal date stamp, postal gripper, mail bag or other postal special articles shall be imposed a fine of not more than 1,500 yuan by the postal administration or its authorized unit, and the relevant articles shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　以营利为目的，伪造邮资凭证，未经许可仿印邮票图案或者印制带有“中国人民邮政”字样明信片的，由邮电管理局或其授权单位处以五千元以下罚款，并没收非法所得和非法物品。  
由于用户的故意，交寄的邮件使用不符合规定的邮资凭证的，邮政企业或者分支机构不予发寄，通知寄件人限期撤回，并处以应付邮资十倍的罚款；无法通知寄件人或者逾期不办理撤回手续的，作为无着邮件处理。

**Article 57** Anyone who, for profit, forges postage certificates, or without permission reproduces stamp designs or prints postcards bearing the words 'China People's Post' shall be imposed a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan by the postal administration or its authorized unit, and the illegal gains and illegal articles shall be confiscated.  
If, due to the user's intent, the mail matter sent uses postage certificates that do not conform to the provisions, the postal enterprise or its branch shall not dispatch it, shall notify the sender to withdraw it within a time limit, and shall impose a fine of ten times the payable postage; if the sender cannot be notified or fails to go through the withdrawal procedures within the time limit, the mail matter shall be treated as undeliverable mail.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　邮政工作人员隐匿、毁弃、私拆、盗窃邮件，贪污、冒领用户款项的，邮政企业应当追回赃款赃物，可以并处罚款，还可以根据情节轻重，给予行政处分。具体办法由邮电部规定。

**Article 58** If postal staff conceal, destroy, privately open, or steal mail matters, or embezzle or fraudulently claim users' money, the postal enterprise shall recover the stolen money and goods, may also impose a fine, and may also impose administrative sanctions based on the severity of the circumstances. The specific measures shall be formulated by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本细则第四十一条第二款规定的，依照[《邮政法》第三十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-36)规定追究责任。

**Article 59** Anyone who violates the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 41 of these Rules shall be held liable in accordance with the provisions of Article 36 of the 'Postal Law'.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本细则规定，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Anyone who violates the provisions of these Rules and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability by the judicial organ according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　误收、误拆他人信件不予退还或者已退还但泄露信件内容，侵犯他人通信自由权利的，依照[《邮政法》第三十六条](../../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国邮政法.md#art-36)规定追究责任。

**Article 61** Anyone who mistakenly receives or opens another person's letter and fails to return it, or returns it but discloses its content, thereby infringing upon another person's right to freedom of correspondence, shall be held liable in accordance with the provisions of Article 36 of the 'Postal Law'.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本细则所称公安机关、国家安全机关、检察机关是指县以上(含县级)公安机关、国家安全机关、检察机关。

**Article 62** The terms 'public security organ', 'state security organ', and 'procuratorial organ' as used in these Rules refer to public security organs, state security organs, and procuratorial organs at or above the county level (including the county level).

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　邮电部可以根据本细则制定有关规章。

**Article 63** The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications may formulate relevant rules in accordance with these Rules.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本细则由邮电部负责解释。

**Article 64** These Rules shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本细则自发布之日起施行。

**Article 65** These Rules shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
