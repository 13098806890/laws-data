# 中华人民共和国外国籍船舶航行长江水域管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02

> (1983年4月9日国务院批准　1983年4月20日交通部发布　1986年2月6日国务院批准修订　1986年3月1日交通部发布　1992年6月6日国务院批准第二次修订　1992年7月25日交通部发布　1997年5月26日国务院批准第三次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订) 第一条　长江是中华人民共和国内河。为维护中华人民共和国主权，保障船舶安全，维持长江水域及其港口秩序，依据《中华人民共和国对外国籍船舶管理规则》，制定本规定。 第二条　在中华人民共和国长江水域及其港口航行、停泊的外国籍机动船、非机动船和其他水上运输工具(以下简称船舶)，必须遵守本规定和中华人民共和国的一切有关法律、行政法规和规章。 第三条　本规定所称长江水域，是指浏河口下游的浏黑屋(北纬31°30′、东经121°18′)与崇明岛施翘河口下游的施信杆(北纬31°37′、东经121°22′)联线沿长江向上至长江中游的北岸窑咀(北纬29°29′、东经113°6′)与南岸七洲(北纬29°28′、东经113°6′)联线及洞庭湖口湘江的南岸七里山(北纬29°24′、东经113°7′)与北岸芦席洲(北纬29°25′、东经113°6′)联线之间的干线水域。 本规定所称港口，是指前款长江水域中对外国籍船舶开放的港口。 第四条　本规定由中华人民共和国港务监督负责实施，任何船舶都必须接受港务监督的监督管理。 第五条　船舶进入长江水域及其港口，必须经中华人民共和国港务监督批准。 经批准进入长江水域及其港口的船舶，应当接受港务监督、边防检查、海关等部门的检查，并办理有关手续。必要时，上述有关部门有权进行随船监督。 第六条　进入长江水域的船舶，不得经营沿长江各港口之间以及长江各港口与上海港之间的运输或者其他未经批准的业务。 第七条　进入长江港口的船舶，必须遵守下列规定： (一)在预定经过上海港区1星期之前，委托有关港口外轮代理公司向所要到达的长江港口港务监督办理进口申请批准手续； (二)在经过上海港区24小时之前(航程不足24小时的，在驶离前一港口时)，将预计经过上海港区和到达长江港口的时间、船舶尺度、前后吃水及实际吃水线以上最大高度等情况，通过外轮代理公司向上海港和所要到达港港务监督报告； (三)前款所报时间如有变化，应当随时报告。 第八条　船舶在长江水域及其港口航行或移泊，必须向中华人民共和国长江港口港务监督申请指派引航员引航；途经上海港水域必须向中华人民共和国上海港务监督申请指派引航员引航。 第九条　船舶抵港后，应即呈报进口报告书及其他有关表报，同时交验船舶证书及有关文件，并接受检查。船舶出港前，应当通过外轮代理公司向港务监督预报开航时间、驶往港等情况，并办理出口手续，取得出口许可证后，方可出港。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　长江是中华人民共和国内河。为维护中华人民共和国主权，保障船舶安全，维持长江水域及其港口秩序，依据《中华人民共和国对外国籍船舶管理规则》，制定本规定。

**Article 1** The Yangtze River is an inland river of the People's Republic of China. For the purpose of safeguarding the sovereignty of the People's Republic of China, ensuring the safety of vessels, and maintaining order in the Yangtze River waters and its ports, these Provisions are formulated pursuant to the Rules for the Control of Foreign Vessels of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国长江水域及其港口航行、停泊的外国籍机动船、非机动船和其他水上运输工具(以下简称船舶)，必须遵守本规定和中华人民共和国的一切有关法律、行政法规和规章。

**Article 2** Foreign motor vessels, non-motor vessels and other waterborne transport vehicles (hereinafter referred to as vessels) navigating and berthing in the Yangtze River waters and ports of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Provisions and all relevant laws, administrative regulations and rules of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本规定所称长江水域，是指浏河口下游的浏黑屋(北纬31°30′、东经121°18′)与崇明岛施翘河口下游的施信杆(北纬31°37′、东经121°22′)联线沿长江向上至长江中游的北岸窑咀(北纬29°29′、东经113°6′)与南岸七洲(北纬29°28′、东经113°6′)联线及洞庭湖口湘江的南岸七里山(北纬29°24′、东经113°7′)与北岸芦席洲(北纬29°25′、东经113°6′)联线之间的干线水域。  
本规定所称港口，是指前款长江水域中对外国籍船舶开放的港口。

**Article 3** The Yangtze River waters as referred to in these Provisions means the main stem waters between the line connecting Liuheiwu (31°30′N, 121°18′E) downstream of Liuhekou and Shixin Gan (31°37′N, 121°22′E) downstream of Shiqiaokou on Chongming Island, extending upstream along the Yangtze River to the line connecting Yao Zui (29°29′N, 113°6′E) on the north bank and Qizhou (29°28′N, 113°6′E) on the south bank in the middle reaches of the Yangtze River, and the line connecting Qilishan (29°24′N, 113°7′E) on the south bank of the Xiangjiang River at the mouth of Dongting Lake and Luxizhou (29°25′N, 113°6′E) on the north bank.  
Ports as referred to in these Provisions means the ports in the Yangtze River waters mentioned in the preceding paragraph that are open to foreign vessels.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本规定由中华人民共和国港务监督负责实施，任何船舶都必须接受港务监督的监督管理。

**Article 4** These Provisions shall be implemented by the Harbor Superintendency Administration of the People's Republic of China, and any vessel must accept the supervision and management of the Harbor Superintendency Administration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　船舶进入长江水域及其港口，必须经中华人民共和国港务监督批准。  
经批准进入长江水域及其港口的船舶，应当接受港务监督、边防检查、海关等部门的检查，并办理有关手续。必要时，上述有关部门有权进行随船监督。

**Article 5** Vessels entering the Yangtze River waters and ports must be approved by the Harbor Superintendency Administration of the People's Republic of China.  
Vessels approved to enter the Yangtze River waters and ports shall undergo inspection by the Harbor Superintendency Administration, border inspection, Customs and other departments, and handle relevant formalities. When necessary, the aforesaid relevant departments have the right to carry out on-vessel supervision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　进入长江水域的船舶，不得经营沿长江各港口之间以及长江各港口与上海港之间的运输或者其他未经批准的业务。

**Article 6** Vessels entering the Yangtze River waters shall not engage in transportation between ports along the Yangtze River or between ports on the Yangtze River and the Port of Shanghai, or other unapproved business.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　进入长江港口的船舶，必须遵守下列规定：  
(一)在预定经过上海港区1星期之前，委托有关港口外轮代理公司向所要到达的长江港口港务监督办理进口申请批准手续；  
(二)在经过上海港区24小时之前(航程不足24小时的，在驶离前一港口时)，将预计经过上海港区和到达长江港口的时间、船舶尺度、前后吃水及实际吃水线以上最大高度等情况，通过外轮代理公司向上海港和所要到达港港务监督报告；  
(三)前款所报时间如有变化，应当随时报告。

**Article 7** Vessels entering Yangtze River ports must comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Entrust the relevant port foreign vessel agency to handle the import application and approval procedures with the Harbor Superintendency Administration of the Yangtze River port to be reached, one week before the scheduled passage through the Shanghai port area;  
(2) Report to the Harbor Superintendency Administration of the Port of Shanghai and the harbor superintendency administration of the port to be reached, through the foreign vessel agency, the estimated time of passage through the Shanghai port area and arrival at the Yangtze River port, vessel dimensions, forward and aft drafts, and maximum height above the actual waterline, 24 hours before passing through the Shanghai port area (or when departing from the previous port if the voyage is less than 24 hours);  
(3) If there is any change in the time reported in the preceding paragraph, it shall be reported promptly.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶在长江水域及其港口航行或移泊，必须向中华人民共和国长江港口港务监督申请指派引航员引航；途经上海港水域必须向中华人民共和国上海港务监督申请指派引航员引航。

**Article 8** Vessels navigating or shifting berths within the Yangtze River waters and ports must apply to the Harbor Superintendency Administration of the Yangtze River ports of the People's Republic of China for the assignment of a pilot; when passing through the Shanghai port waters, they must apply to the Shanghai Harbor Superintendency Administration of the People's Republic of China for the assignment of a pilot.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船舶抵港后，应即呈报进口报告书及其他有关表报，同时交验船舶证书及有关文件，并接受检查。船舶出港前，应当通过外轮代理公司向港务监督预报开航时间、驶往港等情况，并办理出口手续，取得出口许可证后，方可出港。

**Article 9** After a vessel arrives at the port, it shall immediately submit the import report and other relevant forms, and at the same time present for inspection the vessel's certificates and relevant documents, and undergo inspection. Before a vessel departs from the port, it shall, through the foreign vessel agency, report to the Harbor Superintendency Administration the departure time, destination port and other information, and go through the departure formalities, and may depart only after obtaining the departure permit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船舶在长江水域及其港口航行、停泊，白天应当在前桅顶部悬挂中华人民共和国国旗，船尾悬挂船籍国国旗，有引航员在船时应当加挂“H”旗。进出港口或在港内移泊，应当加挂船名呼号旗和其他规定的信号。

**Article 10** When a vessel navigates or berths in the Yangtze River waters and ports, during the daytime it shall hoist the national flag of the People's Republic of China at the top of the foremast, hoist the flag of the vessel's country of registry at the stern, and when a pilot is on board, it shall additionally hoist the 'H' flag. When entering or leaving a port or shifting berths within the port, it shall additionally hoist the vessel's call sign flag and other prescribed signals.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶在长江水域及其港口使用甚高频无线电话，按《中华人民共和国交通部关于外轮使用甚高频无线电话暂行办法》办理。

**Article 11** . Vessels using VHF radiotelephone in the Yangtze River waters and its ports shall act in accordance with the "Interim Measures of the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China Concerning the Use of VHF Radiotelephone by Foreign Vessels."

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　船舶的无线电报、无线电话发射机，在长江水域期间只准与中华人民共和国江岸、海岸电台通讯；在港口期间只有在危急情况下才可以使用，并且使用后必须向港务监督报告。  
船舶的火箭信号、火焰信号、信号枪，在长江水域及其港口期间，只有在危急情况下才可以使用，并且使用后必须向港务监督报告。

**Article 12** . During the period of navigation in the Yangtze River waters, the radio telegraph and radio telephone transmitters of a vessel shall only be permitted to communicate with the coastal and shore radio stations of the People's Republic of China; during the period of berthing in port, they may only be used in emergency situations, and after such use, a report shall be made to the harbor superintendency.  
During the period of navigation in the Yangtze River waters and its ports, the rocket signals, flare signals, and signal pistols of a vessel may only be used in emergency situations, and after such use, a report shall be made to the harbor superintendency.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　船舶在长江水域及其港口航行、停泊时，禁止进行下列活动：  
(一)对军事设施和军用船舶摄影、写生、录像和测绘；  
(二)射击、游泳、钓鱼、鸣放鞭炮或焰火；  
(三)其他危害中华人民共和国安全、权益或安全秩序的活动。

**Article 13** . When navigating or berthing in the Yangtze River waters and its ports, vessels are prohibited from engaging in the following activities:  
(1) photographing, sketching, videotaping, and surveying military installations and military vessels;  
(2) shooting, swimming, fishing, setting off firecrackers or fireworks;  
(3) other activities that endanger the security, rights and interests, or public order of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶在长江水域航行，如遇恶劣气象、洪峰等特殊情况需要临时停泊，应当尽量靠航道一侧的边缘停泊，不得占用主航道，并应及时向就近港务监督报告抛锚时间、位置和驶离时间，未经当地公安部门批准，船上的人员不得登陆。

**Article 14** . When navigating in the Yangtze River waters, if a vessel needs to temporarily anchor due to special circumstances such as severe weather, flood peaks, etc., it shall anchor as close as possible to the edge of the channel and shall not occupy the main channel, and shall promptly report to the nearest harbor superintendency the time and location of anchoring and the time of departure; without the approval of the local public security department, personnel on board shall not go ashore.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船舶不得以危及其他船舶和两岸设施安全的速度航行。

**Article 15** . A vessel shall not navigate at a speed that endangers the safety of other vessels and facilities on both banks.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　船舶在长江水域及其港口期间，有关航行、停泊和避让事宜，按照中华人民共和国交通部颁布的《内河避碰规则》执行。

**Article 16** . During the period of navigation in the Yangtze River waters and its ports, matters concerning navigation, berthing, and collision avoidance shall be executed in accordance with the "Rules for Avoiding Collisions on Inland Waters" promulgated by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　船舶使用信号应当遵守中华人民共和国交通部颁布的《内河避碰规则》和其他有关信号的规定；未作规定的依照有关国际信号规则办理。

**Article 17** . Vessels using signals shall comply with the "Rules for Avoiding Collisions on Inland Waters" promulgated by the Ministry of Communications of the People's Republic of China and other relevant signal regulations; for matters not provided for, the relevant international signal rules shall be followed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　船舶在长江水域及其港口期间，不得任意排放和抛弃油类、油性混合物，以及其他污染物质和废弃物。

**Article 18** . During the period of navigation in the Yangtze River waters and its ports, vessels shall not arbitrarily discharge or discard oil, oily mixtures, or other pollutants and waste.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定已列事项按照本规定执行；未列事项按照《中华人民共和国对外国籍船舶管理规则》及其他有关规定执行。

**Article 19** . Matters provided for in these Provisions shall be implemented in accordance with these Provisions; matters not provided for shall be implemented in accordance with the "Rules for the Administration of Foreign Vessels of the People's Republic of China" and other relevant regulations.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 20** . These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
