# 中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-11-29  
**生效日期**：2020-11-29

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对民用航空器国籍的管理，保障民用航空活动安全，维护民用航空活动秩序，根据《中华人民共和国民用航空法》的规定，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Aviation Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strengthening the administration of the nationality of civil aircraft, ensuring the safety of civil aviation activities, and maintaining the order of civil aviation activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列民用航空器应当依照本条例进行国籍登记：  
(一)中华人民共和国国家机构的民用航空器；  
(二)依照中华人民共和国法律设立的企业法人的民用航空器；  
(三)国务院民用航空主管部门准予登记的其他民用航空器。  
自境外租赁的民用航空器，承租人符合前款规定，该民用航空器的机组人员由承租人配备的，可以申请登记中华人民共和国国籍；但是，必须先予注销该民用航空器原国籍登记。

**Article 2** The following civil aircraft shall undergo nationality registration in accordance with these Regulations:  
(1) civil aircraft of the state organs of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) civil aircraft of enterprise legal persons established in accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) other civil aircraft approved for registration by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
Where a civil aircraft is leased from abroad, if the lessee meets the provisions of the preceding paragraph and the crew of such civil aircraft is provided by the lessee, the lessee may apply for the registration of the nationality of the People's Republic of China; however, the original nationality registration of such civil aircraft shall be cancelled first.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用航空器经依法登记，取得中华人民共和国国籍。

**Article 3** A civil aircraft registered according to law shall acquire the nationality of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　民用航空器不得具有双重国籍。未注销外国国籍的民用航空器，不得在中华人民共和国办理国籍登记；未注销中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，不得在外国办理国籍登记。

**Article 4** No civil aircraft may have dual nationality. A civil aircraft that has not cancelled its foreign nationality may not undergo nationality registration in the People's Republic of China; a civil aircraft that has not cancelled its nationality of the People's Republic of China may not undergo nationality registration in a foreign country.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院民用航空主管部门主管中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记工作，设立中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记簿，统一记载民用航空器的国籍登记事项。

**Article 5** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall be in charge of the nationality registration of civil aircraft of the People's Republic of China, establish the Register of Nationality of Civil Aircraft of the People's Republic of China, and uniformly record the matters of nationality registration of civil aircraft.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民用航空器国籍登记，不得作为民用航空器所有权的证据。

**Article 6** The nationality registration of a civil aircraft shall not be used as evidence of ownership of such civil aircraft.

### 第二章　国籍登记

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记的，申请人应当按照国务院民用航空主管部门规定的格式如实填写民用航空器国籍登记申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交下列文件：  
(一)证明申请人合法身份的文件；  
(二)作为取得民用航空器所有权证明的购买合同和交接文书，或者作为占有民用航空器证明的租赁合同和交接文书；  
(三)未在外国登记国籍或者已注销外国国籍的证明；  
(四)国务院民用航空主管部门要求提交的其他有关文件。

**Article 7** Where an applicant applies for the nationality registration of a civil aircraft of the People's Republic of China, it/he shall truthfully fill in the application form for nationality registration of a civil aircraft in the form prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, and submit the following documents to the said authority:  
(1) a document certifying the lawful identity of the applicant;  
(2) a purchase contract and a handover document as proof of acquisition of ownership of the civil aircraft, or a lease contract and a handover document as proof of possession of the civil aircraft;  
(3) a certificate evidencing that the civil aircraft has not been registered for nationality in a foreign country or that its foreign nationality has been cancelled;  
(4) other relevant documents required to be submitted by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当自收到民用航空器国籍登记申请之日起7个工作日内，对申请书及有关证明文件进行审查；经审查，符合本条例规定的，应当向申请人颁发中华人民共和国民用航空器国籍登记证书。

**Article 8** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall, within 7 working days from the date of receipt of an application for nationality registration of a civil aircraft, examine the application form and the relevant certifying documents; if upon examination it finds that the application conforms to the provisions of these Regulations, it shall issue to the applicant a Certificate of Nationality Registration of Civil Aircraft of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院民用航空主管部门应当在民用航空器国籍登记簿中载明下列事项：  
(一)民用航空器国籍标志和登记标志；  
(二)民用航空器制造人名称；  
(三)民用航空器型号；  
(四)民用航空器出厂序号；  
(五)民用航空器所有人名称及其地址；  
(六)民用航空器占有人名称及其地址；  
(七)民用航空器登记日期；  
(八)民用航空器国籍登记证书签发人姓名。

**Article 9** The competent civil aviation authority under the State Council shall record the following items in the Register of Nationality of Civil Aircraft:  
(1) the nationality mark and registration mark of the civil aircraft;  
(2) the name of the manufacturer of the civil aircraft;  
(3) the type of the civil aircraft;  
(4) the serial number of the civil aircraft;  
(5) the name and address of the owner of the civil aircraft;  
(6) the name and address of the holder of the civil aircraft;  
(7) the date of registration of the civil aircraft;  
(8) the name of the person who issued the Certificate of Nationality Registration of the civil aircraft.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　民用航空器国籍登记证书应当放置于民用航空器内显著位置，以备查验。

**Article 10** The Certificate of Nationality Registration of a civil aircraft shall be placed in a conspicuous position inside the civil aircraft for inspection.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，遇有下列情形之一时，应当申请办理变更登记：  
(一)民用航空器所有人或其地址变更；  
(二)民用航空器占有人或其地址变更；  
(三)国务院民用航空主管部门规定需要办理变更登记的其他情形。

**Article 11** Where a civil aircraft that has acquired the nationality of the People's Republic of China falls under any of the following circumstances, an application shall be made for change of registration:  
(1) the owner of the civil aircraft or its/his address is changed;  
(2) the holder of the civil aircraft or its/his address is changed;  
(3) other circumstances under which change of registration is required as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，遇有下列情形之一时，应当申请办理注销登记：  
(一)民用航空器所有权依法转移境外并已办理出口适航证的；  
(二)民用航空器退出使用或者报废的；  
(三)民用航空器失事或者失踪并停止搜寻的；  
(四)符合[本条例第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的民用航空器租赁合同终止的；  
(五)国务院民用航空主管部门规定需要办理注销登记的其他情形。

**Article 12** Where a civil aircraft that has acquired the nationality of the People's Republic of China falls under any of the following circumstances, an application shall be made for cancellation of registration:  
(1) the ownership of the civil aircraft has been transferred abroad according to law and an export airworthiness certificate has been obtained;  
(2) the civil aircraft is withdrawn from use or is scrapped;  
(3) the civil aircraft has been involved in an accident or has been missing and the search has ceased;  
(4) the lease contract for the civil aircraft as specified in the second paragraph of Article 2 of these Regulations terminates;  
(5) other circumstances under which cancellation of registration is required as prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请人办理民用航空器国籍登记，应当缴纳登记费。登记费的收费标准由国务院民用航空主管部门会同国务院价格主管部门制定。

**Article 13** An applicant shall pay a registration fee for undergoing nationality registration of a civil aircraft. The fee standard for registration shall be formulated by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council jointly with the competent pricing authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　民用航空器没有或者未携带民用航空器国籍登记证书的，国务院民用航空主管部门或者其授权的地区民用航空管理机构可以禁止该民用航空器起飞。

**Article 14** Where a civil aircraft does not have or does not carry its Certificate of Nationality Registration, the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council or its authorized regional civil aviation administrative organ may prohibit the takeoff of such civil aircraft.

### 第三章　国籍标志和登记标志

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中华人民共和国民用航空器的国籍标志为罗马体大写字母B。  
中华人民共和国民用航空器的登记标志为阿拉伯数字、罗马体大写字母或者二者的组合。

**Article 15** The nationality mark of a civil aircraft of the People's Republic of China shall be the capital letter B in Roman type.  
The registration mark of a civil aircraft of the People's Republic of China shall be Arabic numerals, capital letters in Roman type, or a combination thereof.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　中华人民共和国民用航空器的国籍标志置于登记标志之前，国籍标志和登记标志之间加一短横线。

**Article 16** The nationality mark of a civil aircraft of the People's Republic of China shall be placed before the registration mark, with a hyphen between the nationality mark and the registration mark.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，应当将国籍标志和登记标志喷涂在民用航空器上或者用其他能够保持同等耐久性的方法附着在民用航空器上，并保持清晰可见。  
国籍标志和登记标志在民用航空器上的位置、尺寸和字体，由国务院民用航空主管部门规定。

**Article 17** A civil aircraft that has acquired the nationality of the People's Republic of China shall have the nationality mark and registration mark painted on the civil aircraft or attached to the civil aircraft by other methods capable of maintaining equal durability, and shall keep them clearly visible.  
The position, size and font of the nationality mark and registration mark on the civil aircraft shall be prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　任何单位或者个人不得在民用航空器上喷涂、粘贴易与国籍标志和登记标志相混淆的图案、标记或者符号。

**Article 18** No unit or individual may paint or affix on a civil aircraft any pattern, mark or symbol that may be easily confused with the nationality mark and registration mark.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　取得中华人民共和国国籍的民用航空器，应当载有一块刻有国籍标志和登记标志并用耐火金属或者其他耐火材料制成的识别牌。

**Article 19** A civil aircraft that has acquired the nationality of the People's Republic of China shall carry an identification plate engraved with the nationality mark and registration mark and made of fire-resistant metal or other fire-resistant materials.

### 第四章　临时登记

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对未取得民用航空器国籍登记证书的民用航空器，申请人应当在进行下列飞行前30日内，按照国务院民用航空主管部门规定的格式如实填写申请书，并向国务院民用航空主管部门提交有关证明文件，办理临时登记：  
(一)验证试验飞行、生产试验飞行；  
(二)表演飞行；  
(三)为交付或者出口的调机飞行；  
(四)其他必要的飞行。  
前款申请人是指民用航空器制造人、销售人或者国务院民用航空主管部门认可的其他申请人。  
国务院民用航空主管部门准予临时登记的，应当确定临时登记标志，颁发临时登记证书。

**Article 20** For a civil aircraft that has not obtained a Certificate of Nationality Registration, the applicant shall, within 30 days prior to conducting any of the following flights, truthfully fill in an application form in the form prescribed by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council, submit the relevant certifying documents to the said authority, and undergo temporary registration:  
(1) verification test flights and production test flights;  
(2) demonstration flights;  
(3) ferry flights for delivery or export;  
(4) other necessary flights.  
The applicant in the preceding paragraph means the manufacturer, seller or other applicant recognized by the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council.  
Where the competent civil aviation authority under the State Council approves temporary registration, it shall determine a temporary registration mark and issue a temporary registration certificate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　取得临时登记标志的民用航空器出口时，可以使用易于去除的材料将临时登记标志附着在民用航空器上，并应当完全覆盖外方要求预先喷涂的外国国籍标志和登记标志。

**Article 21** Where a civil aircraft that has obtained a temporary registration mark is exported, the temporary registration mark may be attached to the civil aircraft using materials that are easy to remove, and shall completely cover the foreign nationality mark and registration mark required to be pre-painted by the foreign party.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 22** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
