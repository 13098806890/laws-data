# 中华人民共和国船舶登记条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-29  
**生效日期**：1995-01-01

> (1994年6月2日中华人民共和国国务院令第155号发布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强国家对船舶的监督管理，保障船舶登记有关各方的合法权益，制定本条例。 第二条　下列船舶应当依照本条例规定进行登记： (一)在中华人民共和国境内有住所或者主要营业所的中国公民的船舶。 (二)依据中华人民共和国法律设立的主要营业所在中华人民共和国境内的企业法人的船舶。但是，在该法人的注册资本中有外商出资的，中方投资人的出资额不得低于50%。 (三)中华人民共和国政府公务船舶和事业法人的船舶。 (四)中华人民共和国港务监督机构认为应当登记的其他船舶。 军事船舶、渔业船舶和体育运动船艇的登记依照有关法规的规定办理。 第三条　船舶经依法登记，取得中华人民共和国国籍，方可悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行；未经登记的，不得悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行。 第四条　船舶不得具有双重国籍。凡在外国登记的船舶，未中止或者注销原登记国国籍的，不得取得中华人民共和国国籍。 第五条　船舶所有权的取得、转让和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。 船舶由二个以上的法人或者个人共有的，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。 第六条　船舶抵押权、光船租赁权的设定、转移和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。 第七条　中国籍船舶上应持适任证书的船员，必须持有相应的中华人民共和国船员适任证书。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强国家对船舶的监督管理，保障船舶登记有关各方的合法权益，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the state's supervision and management of ships and to protect the lawful rights and interests of the parties concerned in ship registration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列船舶应当依照本条例规定进行登记：  
(一)在中华人民共和国境内有住所或者主要营业所的中国公民的船舶。  
(二)依据中华人民共和国法律设立的主要营业所在中华人民共和国境内的企业法人的船舶。但是，在该法人的注册资本中有外商出资的，中方投资人的出资额不得低于50%。  
(三)中华人民共和国政府公务船舶和事业法人的船舶。  
(四)中华人民共和国港务监督机构认为应当登记的其他船舶。  
军事船舶、渔业船舶和体育运动船艇的登记依照有关法规的规定办理。

**Article 2** The following ships shall be registered in accordance with these Regulations:  
(1) Ships owned by Chinese citizens who have domiciles or principal business establishments within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
(2) Ships owned by enterprise legal persons established according to the laws of the People's Republic of China with their principal business establishments within the territory of the People's Republic of China. However, where there is foreign investment in the registered capital of such legal person, the investment contribution of the Chinese investors shall not be less than 50%.  
(3) Ships owned by the government of the People's Republic of China for public service and ships owned by institutional legal persons.  
(4) Other ships which the port superintendence agency of the People's Republic of China considers shall be registered.  
Registration of military ships, fishing ships, and sports boats shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　船舶经依法登记，取得中华人民共和国国籍，方可悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行；未经登记的，不得悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行。

**Article 3** A ship that has been registered according to law and has acquired the nationality of the People's Republic of China may fly the national flag of the People's Republic of China while navigating; a ship that has not been registered shall not fly the national flag of the People's Republic of China while navigating.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　船舶不得具有双重国籍。凡在外国登记的船舶，未中止或者注销原登记国国籍的，不得取得中华人民共和国国籍。

**Article 4** A ship shall not have dual nationality. Any ship registered in a foreign country that has not suspended or cancelled the nationality of the original country of registration shall not acquire the nationality of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　船舶所有权的取得、转让和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。  
船舶由二个以上的法人或者个人共有的，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。

**Article 5** The acquisition, transfer, and extinction of ship ownership shall be registered with the ship registration authority; if not registered, it shall not be asserted against a third party.  
If a ship is owned in common by two or more legal persons or individuals, it shall be registered with the ship registration authority; if not registered, it shall not be asserted against a third party.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船舶抵押权、光船租赁权的设定、转移和消灭，应当向船舶登记机关登记；未经登记的，不得对抗第三人。

**Article 6** The creation, transfer, and extinction of a ship mortgage or a bareboat charter right shall be registered with the ship registration authority; if not registered, it shall not be asserted against a third party.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国籍船舶上应持适任证书的船员，必须持有相应的中华人民共和国船员适任证书。

**Article 7** Crew members on ships of Chinese nationality who are required to hold certificates of competency must hold corresponding certificates of competency for seafarers of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构是船舶登记主管机关。  
各港的港务监督机构是具体实施船舶登记的机关(以下简称船舶登记机关)，其管辖范围由中华人民共和国港务监督机构确定。

**Article 8** The port superintendence agency of the People's Republic of China is the competent authority for ship registration.  
The port superintendence agency at each port is the authority that specifically implements ship registration (hereinafter referred to as the ship registration authority), and its scope of jurisdiction shall be determined by the port superintendence agency of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船舶登记港为船籍港。  
船舶登记港由船舶所有人依据其住所或者主要营业所所在地就近选择，但是不得选择2个或者2个以上的船舶登记港。

**Article 9** The port of ship registration shall be the port of registry.  
The port of ship registration shall be chosen by the ship owner based on the proximity of his domicile or the location of his principal business establishment, but no ship owner may choose two or more ports of ship registration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一艘船舶只准使用一个名称。  
船名由船籍港船舶登记机关核定。船名不得与登记在先的船舶重名或者同音。

**Article 10** A ship shall be permitted to use only one name.  
The name of a ship shall be verified by the ship registration authority at the port of registry. The name of a ship shall not be identical or homonymous with that of a ship registered earlier.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　船舶登记机关应当建立船舶登记簿。  
船舶登记机关应当允许利害关系人查阅船舶登记簿。

**Article 11** The ship registration authority shall establish a ship register.  
The ship registration authority shall allow interested parties to inspect the ship register.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家所有的船舶由国家授予具有法人资格的全民所有制企业经营管理的，本条例有关船舶所有人的规定适用于该法人。

**Article 12** Where a ship owned by the state is granted by the state to an enterprise owned by the whole people with legal person status for operation and management, the provisions of these Regulations concerning the ship owner shall apply to such legal person.

### 第二章　船舶所有权登记

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　船舶所有人申请船舶所有权登记，应当向船籍港船舶登记机关交验足以证明其合法身份的文件，并提供有关船舶技术资料和船舶所有权取得的证明文件的正本、副本。  
就购买取得的船舶申请船舶所有权登记的，应当提供下列文件：  
(一)购船发票或者船舶的买卖合同和交接文件；  
(二)原船籍港船舶登记机关出具的船舶所有权登记注销证明书；  
(三)未进行抵押的证明文件或者抵押权人同意被抵押船舶转让他人的文件。  
就新造船舶申请船舶所有权登记的，应当提供船舶建造合同和交接文件。但是，就建造中的船舶申请船舶所有权登记的，仅需提供船舶建造合同；就自造自用船舶申请船舶所有权登记的，应当提供足以证明其所有权取得的文件。  
就因继承、赠与、依法拍卖以及法院判决取得的船舶申请船舶所有权登记的，应当提供具有相应法律效力的船舶所有权取得的证明文件。

**Article 13** When a ship owner applies for registration of ship ownership, he shall submit to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for examination documents sufficient to prove his lawful identity, and provide original and duplicate copies of relevant ship technical data and documents evidencing the acquisition of ship ownership.  
If applying for registration of ship ownership in respect of a ship acquired by purchase, the following documents shall be provided:  
(1) the invoice for the purchase of the ship or the contract for the sale and purchase of the ship and the delivery document;  
(2) a certificate of cancellation of ship ownership registration issued by the ship registration authority of the original port of registry;  
(3) a document evidencing that no mortgage exists or a document showing that the mortgagee consents to the transfer of the mortgaged ship to another person.  
If applying for registration of ship ownership in respect of a newly-built ship, the ship building contract and the delivery document shall be provided. However, if applying for registration of ship ownership in respect of a ship under construction, only the ship building contract needs to be provided; if applying for registration of ship ownership in respect of a self-built and self-used ship, documents sufficient to prove the acquisition of ownership shall be provided.  
If applying for registration of ship ownership in respect of a ship acquired by succession, gift, public auction according to law, or court judgment, documents evidencing the acquisition of ship ownership with corresponding legal effect shall be provided.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船籍港船舶登记机关应当对船舶所有权登记申请进行审查核实；对符合本条例规定的，应当自收到申请之日起7日内向船舶所有人颁发船舶所有权登记证书，授予船舶登记号码，并在船舶登记簿中载明下列事项：  
(一)船舶名称、船舶呼号；  
(二)船籍港和登记号码、登记标志；  
(三)船舶所有人的名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(四)船舶所有权的取得方式和取得日期；  
(五)船舶所有权登记日期；  
(六)船舶建造商名称、建造日期和建造地点；  
(七)船舶价值、船体材料和船舶主要技术数据；  
(八)船舶的曾用名、原船籍港以及原船舶登记的注销或者中止的日期；  
(九)船舶为数人共有的，还应当载明船舶共有人的共有情况；  
(十)船舶所有人不实际使用和控制船舶的，还应当载明光船承租人或者船舶经营人的名称、地址及其法定代表人的姓名；  
(十一)船舶已设定抵押权的，还应当载明船舶抵押权的设定情况。  
船舶登记机关对不符合本条例规定的，应当自收到申请之日起7日内书面通知船舶所有人。

**Article 14** The ship registration authority at the port of registry shall examine and verify the application for registration of ship ownership; if it complies with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall, within 7 days from the date of receipt of the application, issue a certificate of ship ownership registration to the ship owner, assign a ship registration number, and record the following particulars in the ship register:  
(1) the name of the ship and its call sign;  
(2) the port of registry, registration number, and registration mark;  
(3) the name and address of the ship owner and the name of his legal representative;  
(4) the method and date of acquisition of ship ownership;  
(5) the date of registration of ship ownership;  
(6) the name of the shipbuilder, date of build, and place of build;  
(7) the value of the ship, hull material, and main technical data of the ship;  
(8) former names of the ship, the original port of registry, and the date of cancellation or suspension of the original ship registration;  
(9) where the ship is owned by several persons in common, the particulars of the common ownership of the co-owners shall also be recorded;  
(10) where the ship owner does not actually use and control the ship, the name and address of the bareboat charterer or ship operator and the name of his legal representative shall also be recorded;  
(11) where a mortgage has been created on the ship, the particulars of the creation of the ship mortgage shall also be recorded.  
If the ship registration authority finds that the application does not comply with the provisions of these Regulations, it shall, within 7 days from the date of receipt of the application, notify the ship owner in writing.

### 第三章　船舶国籍

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船舶所有人申请船舶国籍，除应当交验依照本条例取得的船舶所有权登记证书外，还应当按照船舶航区相应交验下列文件：  
(一)航行国际航线的船舶，船舶所有人应当根据船舶的种类交验法定的船舶检验机构签发的下列有效船舶技术证书：  
1．国际吨位丈量证书；  
2．国际船舶载重线证书；  
3．货船构造安全证书；  
4．货船设备安全证书；  
5．乘客定额证书；  
6．客船安全证书；  
7．货船无线电报安全证书；  
8．国际防止油污证书；  
9．船舶航行安全证书；  
10．其他有关技术证书。  
(二)国内航行的船舶，船舶所有人应当根据船舶的种类交验法定的船舶检验机构签发的船舶检验证书簿和其他有效船舶技术证书。  
从境外购买具有外国国籍的船舶，船舶所有人在申请船舶国籍时，还应当提供原船籍港船舶登记机关出具的注销原国籍的证明书或者将于重新登记时立即注销原国籍的证明书。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的船舶，船籍港船舶登记机关予以核准并发给船舶国籍证书。

**Article 15** When applying for ship nationality, the ship owner shall, in addition to submitting for examination the certificate of ship ownership registration obtained in accordance with these Regulations, also submit for examination the following documents corresponding to the ship's navigation zone:  
(1) For ships navigating on international routes, the ship owner shall, depending on the type of ship, submit for examination the following valid ship technical certificates issued by the statutory ship inspection authority:  
1. International Tonnage Certificate;  
2. International Load Line Certificate;  
3. Cargo Ship Safety Construction Certificate;  
4. Cargo Ship Safety Equipment Certificate;  
5. Passenger Ship Safety Certificate (Passenger Capacity Certificate);  
6. Passenger Ship Safety Certificate;  
7. Cargo Ship Safety Radiotelegraphy Certificate;  
8. International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate;  
9. Ship Navigation Safety Certificate;  
10. Other relevant technical certificates.  
(2) For ships navigating domestically, the ship owner shall, depending on the type of ship, submit for examination the ship inspection certificate book and other valid ship technical certificates issued by the statutory ship inspection authority.  
If a ship with foreign nationality is purchased from abroad, the ship owner shall, when applying for ship nationality, also provide a certificate of cancellation of the original nationality issued by the ship registration authority of the original port of registry, or a certificate that the original nationality will be cancelled immediately upon re-registration.  
For a ship that, upon examination, complies with the provisions of these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall approve and issue a certificate of ship nationality.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定申请登记的船舶，经核准后，船舶登记机关发给船舶国籍证书。船舶国籍证书的有效期为5年。

**Article 16** For a ship registered upon application in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of these Regulations, after approval, the ship registration authority shall issue a certificate of ship nationality. The certificate of ship nationality shall be valid for five years.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　向境外出售新造的船舶，船舶所有人应当持船舶所有权取得的证明文件和有效船舶技术证书，到建造地船舶登记机关申请办理临时船舶国籍证书。  
从境外购买新造的船舶，船舶所有人应当持船舶所有权取得的证明文件和有效船舶技术证书，到中华人民共和国驻外大使馆、领事馆申请办理临时船舶国籍证书。  
境内异地建造船舶，需要办理临时船舶国籍证书的，船舶所有人应当持船舶建造合同和交接文件以及有效船舶技术证书，到建造地船舶登记机关申请办理临时船舶国籍证书。  
在境外建造船舶，船舶所有人应当持船舶建造合同和交接文件以及有效船舶技术证书，到中华人民共和国驻外大使馆、领事馆申请办理临时船舶国籍证书。  
以光船条件从境外租进船舶，光船承租人应当持光船租赁合同和原船籍港船舶登记机关出具的中止或者注销原国籍的证明书，或者将于重新登记时立即中止或者注销原国籍的证明书到船舶登记机关申请办理临时船舶国籍证书。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的船舶，船舶登记机关或者中华人民共和国驻外大使馆、领事馆予以核准并发给临时船舶国籍证书。

**Article 17** If a newly-built ship is sold abroad, the ship owner shall, holding the documents evidencing the acquisition of ship ownership and valid ship technical certificates, apply to the ship registration authority at the place of build for a provisional certificate of ship nationality.  
If a newly-built ship is purchased from abroad, the ship owner shall, holding the documents evidencing the acquisition of ship ownership and valid ship technical certificates, apply to the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that foreign country for a provisional certificate of ship nationality.  
If a ship is built in a different place within the country and a provisional certificate of ship nationality is required, the ship owner shall, holding the ship building contract and delivery document as well as valid ship technical certificates, apply to the ship registration authority at the place of build for a provisional certificate of ship nationality.  
If a ship is built abroad, the ship owner shall, holding the ship building contract and delivery document as well as valid ship technical certificates, apply to the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that foreign country for a provisional certificate of ship nationality.  
If a ship is bareboat chartered from abroad, the bareboat charterer shall, holding the bareboat charter contract and a certificate of suspension or cancellation of the original nationality issued by the ship registration authority of the original port of registry, or a certificate that the original nationality will be immediately suspended or cancelled upon re-registration, apply to the ship registration authority for a provisional certificate of ship nationality.  
For a ship that, upon examination, complies with the provisions of these Regulations, the ship registration authority or the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China shall approve and issue a provisional certificate of ship nationality.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　临时船舶国籍证书的有效期一般不超过1年。  
以光船租赁条件从境外租进的船舶，临时船舶国籍证书的期限可以根据租期确定，但是最长不得超过2年。光船租赁合同期限超过2年的，承租人应当在证书有效期内，到船籍港船舶登记机关申请换发临时船舶国籍证书。

**Article 18** The validity period of a provisional certificate of ship nationality shall generally not exceed one year.  
For a ship bareboat chartered from abroad, the term of the provisional certificate of ship nationality may be determined according to the charter period, but shall not exceed two years at most. Where the term of the bareboat charter contract exceeds two years, the charterer shall, within the validity period of the certificate, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for a renewal of the provisional certificate of ship nationality.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　临时船舶国籍证书和船舶国籍证书具有同等法律效力。

**Article 19** A provisional certificate of ship nationality and a certificate of ship nationality shall have the same legal effect.

### 第四章　船舶抵押权登记

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对20总吨以上的船舶设定抵押权时，抵押权人和抵押人应当持下列文件到船籍港船舶登记机关申请办理船舶抵押权登记：  
(一)双方签字的书面申请书；  
(二)船舶所有权登记证书或者船舶建造合同；  
(三)船舶抵押合同。  
该船舶设定有其他抵押权的，还应当提供有关证明文件。  
船舶共有人就共有船舶设定抵押权时，还应当提供2/3以上份额或者约定份额的共有人的同意证明文件。

**Article 20** When creating a mortgage on a ship of 20 gross tons or more, the mortgagee and the mortgagor shall, holding the following documents, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the ship mortgage:  
(1) a written application signed by both parties;  
(2) a certificate of ship ownership registration or a ship building contract;  
(3) the ship mortgage contract.  
If other mortgages have been created on the same ship, relevant certification documents shall also be provided.  
Where a co-owner of a ship creates a mortgage on the co-owned ship, a document of consent from co-owners holding two-thirds or more of the shares or the agreed share shall also be provided.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当自收到申请之日起7日内将有关抵押人、抵押权人和船舶抵押情况以及抵押登记日期载入船舶登记簿和船舶所有权登记证书，并向抵押权人核发船舶抵押权登记证书。

**Article 21** Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall, within seven days from the date of receipt of the application, record the relevant mortgagor, mortgagee, ship mortgage particulars and the date of mortgage registration in the ship register and the Certificate of Ship Ownership, and shall issue a Certificate of Ship Mortgage Registration to the mortgagee.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　船舶抵押权登记，包括下列主要事项：  
(一)抵押权人和抵押人的姓名或者名称、地址；  
(二)被抵押船舶的名称、国籍，船舶所有权登记证书的颁发机关和号码；  
(三)所担保的债权数额、利息率、受偿期限。  
船舶登记机关应当允许公众查询船舶抵押权的登记状况。

**Article 22** Registration of a ship mortgage shall include the following main items:  
(1) the name or title and address of the mortgagee and mortgagor;  
(2) the name and nationality of the mortgaged ship, and the issuing authority and number of the Certificate of Ship Ownership;  
(3) the amount of the secured claim, the interest rate, and the term for satisfaction.  
The ship registration authority shall permit the public to inquire into the registered status of the ship mortgage.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　船舶抵押权转移时，抵押权人和承转人应当持船舶抵押权转移合同到船籍港船舶登记机关申请办理抵押权转移登记。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当将承转人作为抵押权人载入船舶登记簿和船舶所有权登记证书，并向承转人核发船舶抵押权登记证书，封存原船舶抵押权登记证书。  
办理船舶抵押权转移前，抵押权人应当通知抵押人。

**Article 23** When a ship mortgage is transferred, the mortgagee and the transferee shall, holding the ship mortgage transfer contract, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the transfer of the mortgage.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall record the transferee as the mortgagee in the ship register and the Certificate of Ship Ownership, and shall issue a Certificate of Ship Mortgage Registration to the transferee, and seal up the original Certificate of Ship Mortgage Registration.  
Prior to the transfer of the ship mortgage, the mortgagee shall notify the mortgagor.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　同一船舶设定2个以上抵押权的，船舶登记机关应当按照抵押权登记申请日期的先后顺序进行登记，并在船舶登记簿上载明登记日期。  
登记申请日期为登记日期；同日申请的，登记日期应当相同。

**Article 24** Where two or more mortgages are created on the same ship, the ship registration authority shall register them in the chronological order of the dates of application for mortgage registration, and shall indicate the registration date in the ship register.  
The date of application for registration shall be the registration date; if applications are made on the same day, the registration dates shall be the same.

### 第五章　光船租赁登记

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一的，出租人、承租人应当办理光船租赁登记：  
(一)中国籍船舶以光船条件出租给本国企业的；  
(二)中国企业以光船条件租进外国籍船舶的；  
(三)中国籍船舶以光船条件出租境外的。

**Article 25** Under any of the following circumstances, the lessor and the lessee shall register the bareboat charter:  
(1) a ship of Chinese nationality is chartered out on bareboat terms to a domestic enterprise;  
(2) a Chinese enterprise charters in on bareboat terms a ship of foreign nationality; or  
(3) a ship of Chinese nationality is chartered out on bareboat terms overseas.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　船舶在境内出租时，出租人和承租人应当在船舶起租前，持船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书和光船租赁合同正本、副本，到船籍港船舶登记机关申请办理光船租赁登记。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当将船舶租赁情况分别载入船舶所有权登记证书和船舶登记簿，并向出租人、承租人核发光船租赁登记证明书各1份。

**Article 26** When a ship is chartered out domestically, the lessor and the lessee shall, prior to the commencement of the charter, hold the Certificate of Ship Ownership, the Certificate of Ship Nationality, and the original and duplicate of the bareboat charter contract, and apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the bareboat charter.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall record the ship charter particulars in the Certificate of Ship Ownership and the ship register respectively, and shall issue a Certificate of Bareboat Charter Registration to each of the lessor and the lessee.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　船舶以光船条件出租境外时，出租人应当持[本条例第二十六条](#art-26)规定的文件到船籍港船舶登记机关申请办理光船租赁登记。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当依照[本条例第四十二条](#art-42)规定中止或者注销其船舶国籍，并发给光船租赁登记证明书一式2份。

**Article 27** When a ship is chartered out on bareboat terms overseas, the lessor shall, holding the documents specified in Article 26 of these Regulations, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the bareboat charter.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall suspend or cancel the ship's nationality in accordance with Article 42 of these Regulations, and shall issue a Certificate of Bareboat Charter Registration in duplicate.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　以光船条件从境外租进船舶，承租人应当比照[本条例第九条](#art-9)规定确定船籍港，并在船舶起租前持下列文件，到船舶登记机关申请办理光船租赁登记：  
(一)光船租赁合同正本、副本；  
(二)法定的船舶检验机构签发的有效船舶技术证书；  
(三)原船籍港船舶登记机关出具的中止或者注销船舶国籍证明书，或者将于重新登记时立即中止或者注销船舶国籍的证明书。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船舶登记机关应当发给光船租赁登记证明书，并应当依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定发给临时船舶国籍证书，在船舶登记簿上载明原登记国。

**Article 28** Where a ship is chartered in on bareboat terms from overseas, the lessee shall determine the port of registry by analogy with the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations, and prior to the commencement of the charter, shall hold the following documents and apply to the ship registration authority for registration of the bareboat charter:  
(1) the original and duplicate of the bareboat charter contract;  
(2) valid ship technical certificates issued by a statutory ship inspection body;  
(3) a certificate of suspension or cancellation of ship nationality issued by the ship registration authority at the original port of registry, or a certificate that the ship nationality will be suspended or cancelled immediately upon re-registration.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority shall issue a Certificate of Bareboat Charter Registration, and shall issue a provisional Certificate of Ship Nationality in accordance with Article 17 of these Regulations, and shall indicate the original country of registration in the ship register.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　需要延长光船租赁期限的，出租人、承租人应当在光船租赁合同期满前15日，持光船租赁登记证明书和续租合同正本、副本，到船舶登记机关申请办理续租登记。

**Article 29** Where an extension of the bareboat charter period is required, the lessor and the lessee shall, 15 days prior to the expiry of the bareboat charter contract, hold the Certificate of Bareboat Charter Registration and the original and duplicate of the renewal contract, and apply to the ship registration authority for registration of the renewal.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　在光船租赁期间，未经出租人书面同意，承租人不得申请光船转租登记。

**Article 30** During the period of a bareboat charter, the lessee shall not apply for registration of a sub-charter on bareboat terms without the written consent of the lessor.

### 第六章　船舶标志和公司旗

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　船舶应当具有下列标志：  
(一)船首两舷和船尾标明船名；  
(二)船尾船名下方标明船籍港；  
(三)船名、船籍港下方标明汉语拼音；  
(四)船首和船尾两舷标明吃水标尺；  
(五)船舶中部两舷标明载重线。  
受船型或者尺寸限制不能在前款规定的位置标明标志的船舶，应当在船上显著位置标明船名和船籍港。

**Article 31** A ship shall bear the following marks:  
(1) the ship's name shall be marked on both sides of the bow and on the stern;  
(2) the port of registry shall be marked below the ship's name on the stern;  
(3) the Chinese phonetic alphabet shall be marked below the ship's name and port of registry;  
(4) draft marks shall be marked on both sides of the bow and stern; and  
(5) the load line shall be marked on both sides amidships.  
A ship that cannot bear marks in the positions specified in the preceding paragraph due to restrictions of ship type or size shall bear the ship's name and port of registry in a conspicuous position on the ship.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　船舶所有人设置船舶烟囱标志、公司旗，可以向船籍港船舶登记机关申请登记，并按照规定提供标准设计图纸。

**Article 32** A shipowner who sets up a ship funnel mark or a company flag may apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration, and shall provide standard design drawings in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　同一公司的船舶只准使用一个船舶烟囱标志、公司旗。  
船舶烟囱标志、公司旗由船籍港船舶登记机关审核。  
船舶烟囱标志、公司旗不得与登记在先的船舶烟囱标志、公司旗相同或者相似。

**Article 33** Ships of the same company shall use only one funnel mark and one company flag.  
The ship funnel mark and company flag shall be examined by the ship registration authority at the port of registry.  
The ship funnel mark and company flag shall not be identical or similar to a ship funnel mark or company flag that has been registered earlier.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　船籍港船舶登记机关对经核准予以登记的船舶烟囱标志、公司旗应当予以公告。  
业经登记的船舶烟囱标志、公司旗属登记申请人专用，其他船舶或者公司不得使用。

**Article 34** The ship registration authority at the port of registry shall announce the ship funnel mark or company flag that has been approved for registration.  
A registered ship funnel mark or company flag shall be exclusively used by the applicant for registration, and no other ship or company may use it.

### 第七章　变更登记和注销登记

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　船舶登记项目发生变更时，船舶所有人应当持船舶登记的有关证明文件和变更证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理变更登记。

**Article 35** When a registered item of a ship changes, the shipowner shall, holding the relevant supporting documents for ship registration and the documents evidencing the change, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the change.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　船舶变更船籍港时，船舶所有人应当持船舶国籍证书和变更证明文件，到原船籍港船舶登记机关申请办理船籍港变更登记。对经审查符合本条例规定的，原船籍港船舶登记机关应当在船舶国籍证书签证栏内注明，并将船舶有关登记档案转交新船籍港船舶登记机关，船舶所有人再到新船籍港船舶登记机关办理登记。

**Article 36** When a ship changes its port of registry, the shipowner shall, holding the Certificate of Ship Nationality and the documents evidencing the change, apply to the ship registration authority at the original port of registry for registration of the change of port of registry. Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the original port of registry shall make a notation in the endorsement column of the Certificate of Ship Nationality, and shall transfer the relevant ship registration files to the ship registration authority at the new port of registry, and the shipowner shall then register with the ship registration authority at the new port of registry.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　船舶共有情况发生变更时，船舶所有人应当持船舶所有权登记证书和有关船舶共有情况变更的证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理有关变更登记。

**Article 37** When the co-ownership status of a ship changes, the shipowner shall, holding the Certificate of Ship Ownership and the relevant documents evidencing the change in the co-ownership status, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for the relevant registration of the change.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　船舶抵押合同变更时，抵押权人和抵押人应当持船舶所有权登记证书、船舶抵押权登记证书和船舶抵押合同变更的证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理变更登记。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当在船舶所有权登记证书和船舶抵押权登记证书以及船舶登记簿上注明船舶抵押合同的变更事项。

**Article 38** When a ship mortgage contract is amended, the mortgagee and the mortgagor shall, holding the Certificate of Ship Ownership, the Certificate of Ship Mortgage Registration, and the documents evidencing the amendment of the ship mortgage contract, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for registration of the change.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall indicate the items of amendment of the ship mortgage contract on the Certificate of Ship Ownership, the Certificate of Ship Mortgage Registration, and the ship register.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　船舶所有权发生转移时，原船舶所有人应当持船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书和其他有关证明文件到船籍港船舶登记机关办理注销登记。  
对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当注销该船舶在船舶登记簿上的所有权登记以及与之相关的登记，收回有关登记证书，并向船舶所有人出具相应的船舶登记注销证明书。向境外出售的船舶，船舶登记机关可以根据具体情况出具注销国籍的证明书或者将于重新登记时立即注销国籍的证明书。

**Article 39** When ownership of a ship is transferred, the original shipowner shall, holding the Certificate of Ship Ownership, the Certificate of Ship Nationality, and other relevant supporting documents, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for deregistration.  
Where, upon examination, the conditions are in conformity with these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall cancel the ownership registration of the ship in the ship register and the related registrations, collect the relevant registration certificates, and issue a corresponding Certificate of Deregistration of Ship Registration to the shipowner. For a ship sold overseas, the ship registration authority may, according to the specific circumstances, issue a certificate of cancellation of nationality or a certificate that the nationality will be cancelled immediately upon re-registration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　船舶灭失(含船舶拆解、船舶沉没)和船舶失踪，船舶所有人应当自船舶灭失(含船舶拆解、船舶沉没)或者船舶失踪之日起3个月内持船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书和有关船舶灭失(含船舶拆解、船舶沉没)、船舶失踪的证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理注销登记。经审查核实，船籍港船舶登记机关应当注销该船舶在船舶登记簿上的登记，收回有关登记证书，并向船舶所有人出具船舶登记注销证明书。

**Article 40** In the event of loss of a ship (including scrapping, sinking) or disappearance of a ship, the shipowner shall, within three months from the date of the loss (including scrapping, sinking) or disappearance, hold the Certificate of Ship Ownership, the Certificate of Ship Nationality, and the relevant documents evidencing the loss (including scrapping, sinking) or disappearance of the ship, and apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for deregistration. Upon examination and verification, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall cancel the registration of the ship in the ship register, collect the relevant registration certificates, and issue a Certificate of Deregistration of Ship Registration to the shipowner.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　船舶抵押合同解除，抵押权人和抵押人应当持船舶所有权登记证书、船舶抵押权登记证书和经抵押权人签字的解除抵押合同的文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理注销登记。对经审查符合本条例规定的，船籍港船舶登记机关应当注销其在船舶所有权登记证书和船舶登记簿上的抵押登记的记录。

**Article 41** Where a ship mortgage contract is dissolved, the mortgagee and the mortgagor shall, by presenting the ship ownership registration certificate, the ship mortgage registration certificate and the document for dissolution of the mortgage contract signed by the mortgagee, apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for the cancellation registration. For those which, upon examination, comply with the provisions of these Regulations, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall cancel the record of the mortgage registration on the ship ownership registration certificate and the ship register.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　以光船条件出租到境外的船舶，出租人除依照[本条例第二十七条](#art-27)规定办理光船租赁登记外，还应当办理船舶国籍的中止或者注销登记。船籍港船舶登记机关应当封存原船舶国籍证书，发给中止或者注销船舶国籍证明书。特殊情况下，船籍港船舶登记机关可以发给将于重新登记时立即中止或者注销船舶国籍的证明书。

**Article 42** For a ship bareboat chartered out to a place outside the territory, the lessor shall, in addition to going through the bareboat charter registration in accordance with Article 27 of these Regulations, also go through the suspension or cancellation registration of the ship's nationality. The ship registration authority at the port of registry shall seal up the original ship nationality certificate and issue a certificate of suspension or cancellation of ship nationality. Under special circumstances, the ship registration authority at the port of registry may issue a certificate of immediate suspension or cancellation of ship nationality upon re-registration.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　光船租赁合同期满或者光船租赁关系终止，出租人应当自光船租赁合同期满或者光船租赁关系终止之日起15日内，持船舶所有权登记证书、光船租赁合同或者终止光船租赁关系的证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理光船租赁注销登记。  
以光船条件出租到境外的船舶，出租人还应当提供承租人所在地船舶登记机关出具的注销船舶国籍证明书或者将于重新登记时立即注销船舶国籍的证明书。  
经核准后，船籍港船舶登记机关应当注销其在船舶所有权登记证书和船舶登记簿上的光船租赁登记的记录，并发还原船舶国籍证书。

**Article 43** Where the bareboat charter contract expires or the bareboat charter relationship terminates, the lessor shall, within 15 days from the date of expiry of the bareboat charter contract or termination of the bareboat charter relationship, present the ship ownership registration certificate, the bareboat charter contract or the certification document for termination of the bareboat charter relationship, and apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for the cancellation of the bareboat charter registration.  
For a ship bareboat chartered out to a place outside the territory, the lessor shall also provide the certificate of cancellation of ship nationality issued by the ship registration authority of the place where the charterer is located, or the certificate of immediate cancellation of ship nationality upon re-registration.  
Upon approval, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall cancel the record of the bareboat charter registration on the ship ownership registration certificate and the ship register, and return the original ship nationality certificate.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　以光船条件租进的船舶，承租人应当自光船租赁合同期满或者光船租赁关系终止之日起15日内，持光船租赁合同、终止光船租赁关系的证明文件，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理注销登记。  
以光船条件从境外租进的船舶，还应当提供临时船舶国籍证书。  
经核准后，船籍港船舶登记机关应当注销其在船舶登记簿上的光船租赁登记，收回临时船舶国籍证书，并出具光船租赁登记注销证明书和临时船舶国籍注销证明书。

**Article 44** For a ship chartered in under a bareboat charter, the charterer shall, within 15 days from the date of expiry of the bareboat charter contract or termination of the bareboat charter relationship, present the bareboat charter contract and the certification document for termination of the bareboat charter relationship, and apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for the cancellation registration.  
For a ship chartered in from a place outside the territory under a bareboat charter, the charterer shall also provide the provisional ship nationality certificate.  
Upon approval, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall cancel the bareboat charter registration on the ship register, withdraw the provisional ship nationality certificate, and issue a certificate of cancellation of bareboat charter registration and a certificate of cancellation of provisional ship nationality.

### 第八章　船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书的换发和补发

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　船舶国籍证书有效期届满前1年内，船舶所有人应当持船舶国籍证书和有效船舶技术证书，到船籍港船舶登记机关办理证书换发手续。

**Article 45** Within one year before the expiration of the validity period of the ship nationality certificate, the shipowner shall present the ship nationality certificate and the valid ship technical certificate to the ship registration authority at the port of registry to go through the formalities for renewal of the certificate.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书污损不能使用的，持证人应当向船籍港船舶登记机关申请换发。

**Article 46** Where the ship ownership registration certificate or the ship nationality certificate is soiled or damaged and cannot be used, the certificate holder shall apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for renewal.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　船舶所有权登记证书、船舶国籍证书遗失的，持证人应当书面叙明理由，附具有关证明文件，向船籍港船舶登记机关申请补发。  
船籍港船舶登记机关应当在当地报纸上公告声明原证书作废。

**Article 47** Where the ship ownership registration certificate or the ship nationality certificate is lost, the certificate holder shall state the reasons in writing, attach relevant supporting documents, and apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for reissuance.  
The ship registration authority at the port of registry shall publish an announcement in a local newspaper declaring the original certificate null and void.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　船舶所有人在境外发现船舶国籍证书遗失或者污损时，应当向中华人民共和国驻外大使馆、领事馆申请办理临时船舶国籍证书，但是必须在抵达本国第一个港口后及时向船籍港船舶登记机关申请换发船舶国籍证书。

**Article 48** Where the shipowner, while outside the territory, discovers that the ship nationality certificate is lost or soiled or damaged, the shipowner shall apply to the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country for a provisional ship nationality certificate, but shall, after arriving at the first port of the home country, promptly apply to the ship registration authority at the port of registry for renewal of the ship nationality certificate.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　假冒中华人民共和国国籍，悬挂中华人民共和国国旗航行的，由船舶登记机关依法没收该船舶。  
中国籍船舶假冒外国国籍，悬挂外国国旗航行的，适用前款规定。

**Article 49** Where a ship falsely assumes the nationality of the People's Republic of China and sails flying the national flag of the People's Republic of China, the ship registration authority shall confiscate the ship according to law.  
Where a ship of Chinese nationality falsely assumes a foreign nationality and sails flying a foreign national flag, the preceding paragraph shall apply.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　隐瞒在境内或者境外的登记事实，造成双重国籍的，由船籍港船舶登记机关吊销其船舶国籍证书，并视情节处以下列罚款：  
(一)500总吨以下的船舶，处2000元以上、1万元以下的罚款；  
(二)501总吨以上、10000总吨以下的船舶，处以1万元以上、5万元以下的罚款；  
(三)10001总吨以上的船舶，处以5万元以上、20万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Where a person conceals the fact of registration within or outside the territory, resulting in dual nationality, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall revoke the ship nationality certificate of the ship and, depending on the circumstances, impose the following fines:  
(1) For a ship of less than 500 gross tonnage, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 10,000 yuan;  
(2) For a ship of between 501 and 10,000 gross tonnage, a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 yuan but not more than RMB 50,000 yuan;  
(3) For a ship of more than 10,001 gross tonnage, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列情形之一的，船籍港船舶登记机关可以视情节给予警告、根据船舶吨位处以[本条例第五十条](#art-50)规定的罚款数额的50%直至没收船舶登记证书：  
(一)在办理登记手续时隐瞒真实情况、弄虚作假的；  
(二)隐瞒登记事实，造成重复登记的；  
(三)伪造、涂改船舶登记证书的。

**Article 51** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances, the ship registration authority at the port of registry may, depending on the circumstances, give a warning, impose a fine of 50% of the amount specified in Article 50 of these Regulations based on the ship's tonnage, up to confiscation of the ship registration certificate:  
(1) Concealing the true situation or practicing fraud when going through the registration formalities;  
(2) Concealing the fact of registration, resulting in duplicate registration;  
(3) Forging or altering the ship registration certificate.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　不按照规定办理变更或者注销登记的，或者使用过期的船舶国籍证书或者临时船舶国籍证书的，由船籍港船舶登记机关责令其补办有关登记手续；情节严重的，可以根据船舶吨位处以[本条例第五十条](#art-50)规定的罚款数额的10%。

**Article 52** Where any person fails to go through the formalities for modification or cancellation registration in accordance with the provisions, or uses an expired ship nationality certificate or provisional ship nationality certificate, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall order the person to complete the relevant registration formalities; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of 10% of the amount specified in Article 50 of these Regulations may be imposed based on the ship's tonnage.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，使用他人业经登记的船舶烟囱标志、公司旗的，由船籍港船舶登记机关责令其改正；拒不改正的，可以根据船舶吨位处以[本条例第五十条](#art-50)规定的罚款数额的10%；情节严重的，并可以吊销其船舶国籍证书或者临时船舶国籍证书。

**Article 53** Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations by using another person's registered ship funnel mark or company flag, the ship registration authority at the port of registry shall order the person to make corrections; if the person refuses to make corrections, a fine of 10% of the amount specified in Article 50 of these Regulations may be imposed based on the ship's tonnage; if the circumstances are serious, the ship nationality certificate or provisional ship nationality certificate may also be revoked.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　船舶登记机关的工作人员滥用职权、徇私舞弊、玩忽职守、严重失职的，由所在单位或者上级机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where any staff member of the ship registration authority abuses power, engages in malpractices for personal gain, neglects duties, or is seriously derelict, the person shall be given administrative sanctions by the unit to which the person belongs or by the competent authority at a higher level; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　当事人对船舶登记机关的具体行政行为不服的，可以依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定申请复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 55** Where a party is dissatisfied with a specific administrative act of the ship registration authority, the party may apply for reconsideration or initiate an administrative action in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　本条例下列用语的含义是：  
(一)“船舶”系指各类机动、非机动船舶以及其他水上移动装置，但是船舶上装备的救生艇筏和长度小于5米的艇筏除外。  
(二)“渔业船舶”系指从事渔业生产的船舶以及属于水产系统为渔业生产服务的船舶。  
(三)“公务船舶”系指用于政府行政管理目的的船舶。

**Article 56** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(1) "Ship" means all kinds of motorized and non-motorized ships and other mobile installations on water, but excluding lifeboats and rafts equipped on ships and boats and rafts with a length of less than 5 meters.  
(2) "Fishing vessel" means a ship engaged in fishing production and a ship belonging to the fisheries system that serves fishing production.  
(3) "Government vessel" means a ship used for the purposes of government administration and management.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　除公务船舶外，船舶登记机关按照规定收取船舶登记费。船舶登记费的收费标准和管理办法，由国务院财政部门、物价行政主管部门会同国务院交通行政主管部门制定。

**Article 57** With the exception of government vessels, the ship registration authority shall charge ship registration fees in accordance with the provisions. The fee standards and administrative measures for ship registration fees shall be formulated by the finance department and the price administrative department of the State Council jointly with the transport administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　船舶登记簿、船舶国籍证书、临时船舶国籍证书、船舶所有权登记证书、船舶抵押权登记证书、光船租赁登记证明书、申请书以及其他证明书的格式，由中华人民共和国港务监督机构统一制定。

**Article 58** The formats of the ship register, ship nationality certificate, provisional ship nationality certificate, ship ownership registration certificate, ship mortgage registration certificate, bareboat charter registration certificate, application form and other certificates shall be uniformly prescribed by the harbor superintendency administration of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例自1995年1月1日起施行。

**Article 59** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 1995.
