# 中华人民共和国航标条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08

> (1995年12月3日中华人民共和国国务院令第187号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订)

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对航标的管理和保护，保证航标处于良好的使用状态，保障船舶航行安全，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management and protection of navigation aids, ensure that navigation aids are in good working condition, and guarantee the safety of ship navigation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国的领域及管辖的其他海域设置的航标。  
本条例所称航标，是指供船舶定位、导航或者用于其他专用目的的助航设施，包括视觉航标、无线电导航设施和音响航标。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to navigation aids established in the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, navigation aids refer to aids to navigation used for ship positioning, navigation, or other specific purposes, including visual aids, radio navigation aids, and sound signals.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院交通行政主管部门负责管理和保护除军用航标和渔业航标以外的航标。国务院交通行政主管部门设立的流域航道管理机构、海区港务监督机构和县级以上地方人民政府交通行政主管部门，负责管理和保护本辖区内军用航标和渔业航标以外的航标。交通行政主管部门和国务院交通行政主管部门设立的流域航道管理机构、海区港务监督机构统称航标管理机关。  
军队的航标管理机构、渔政渔港监督管理机构，在军用航标、渔业航标的管理和保护方面分别行使航标管理机关的职权。

**Article 3** The competent administrative department of transportation under the State Council shall be responsible for the management and protection of navigation aids except for military navigation aids and fishery navigation aids. The river basin waterway management institutions, maritime port superintendency agencies established by the competent administrative department of transportation under the State Council, and the competent administrative departments of transportation of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the management and protection of navigation aids within their respective jurisdictions, except for military navigation aids and fishery navigation aids. The competent administrative departments of transportation and the river basin waterway management institutions and maritime port superintendency agencies established by the competent administrative department of transportation under the State Council are collectively referred to as the navigation aids management authorities.  
The navigation aids management institutions of the military and the fishery administration and fishing port superintendency agencies shall exercise the functions and powers of the navigation aids management authorities in respect of the management and protection of military navigation aids and fishery navigation aids respectively.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　航标的管理和保护，实行统一管理、分级负责和专业保护与群众保护相结合的原则。

**Article 4** The management and protection of navigation aids shall follow the principles of unified management, graded responsibility, and the combination of professional protection with public protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人都有保护航标的义务。  
禁止一切危害航标安全和损害航标工作效能的行为。  
对于危害航标安全或者损害航标工作效能的行为，任何单位和个人都有权制止、检举和控告。

**Article 5** All units and individuals shall have the duty to protect navigation aids.  
All acts that endanger the safety of navigation aids or impair the effectiveness of navigation aids shall be prohibited.  
For any act that endangers the safety of navigation aids or impairs the effectiveness of navigation aids, all units and individuals shall have the right to stop it, report it, and lodge accusations against it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　航标由航标管理机关统一设置；但是，本条第二款规定的航标除外。  
专业单位可以自行设置自用的专用航标。专用航标的设置、撤除、位置移动和其他状况改变，应当经航标管理机关同意。

**Article 6** Navigation aids shall be uniformly established by the navigation aids management authorities; however, the navigation aids specified in paragraph 2 of this Article are excepted.  
Professional units may establish their own special navigation aids for their own use. The establishment, removal, relocation, or any other change of the status of special navigation aids shall be subject to the approval of the navigation aids management authorities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　航标管理机关和专业单位设置航标，应当符合国家有关规定和技术标准。

**Article 7** The navigation aids management authorities and professional units shall, when establishing navigation aids, comply with the relevant state regulations and technical standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　航标管理机关设置、撤除航标或者移动航标位置以及改变航标的其他状况时，应当及时通报有关部门。

**Article 8** When the navigation aids management authorities establish or remove navigation aids, or relocate navigation aids, or change any other status of navigation aids, they shall promptly notify the relevant departments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　航标管理机关和专业单位分别负责各自设置的航标的维护保养，保证航标处于良好的使用状态。

**Article 9** The navigation aids management authorities and professional units shall respectively be responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of the navigation aids they establish, to ensure that the navigation aids are in good working condition.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　任何单位或者个人发现航标损坏、失常、移位或者漂失时，应当立即向航标管理机关报告。

**Article 10** Any unit or individual that discovers that a navigation aid is damaged, malfunctioning, displaced, or adrift shall immediately report to the navigation aids management authorities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　任何单位和个人不得在航标附近设置可能被误认为航标或者影响航标工作效能的灯光或者音响装置。

**Article 11** No unit or individual may install lights or sound devices near navigation aids that may be mistaken for navigation aids or affect the effectiveness of navigation aids.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　因施工作业需要搬迁、拆除航标的，应当征得航标管理机关同意，在采取替补措施后方可搬迁、拆除。搬迁、拆除航标所需的费用，由施工作业单位或者个人承担。

**Article 12** Where it is necessary to relocate or remove navigation aids due to construction operations, the consent of the navigation aids management authorities shall be obtained, and relocation or removal may only be carried out after alternative measures have been taken. The expenses necessary for relocating or removing navigation aids shall be borne by the construction unit or individual.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在视觉航标的通视方向或者无线电导航设施的发射方向，不得构筑影响航标正常工作效能的建筑物、构筑物，不得种植影响航标正常工作效能的植物。

**Article 13** In the line of sight direction of visual aids or the emission direction of radio navigation aids, no buildings or structures that affect the normal working effectiveness of navigation aids shall be constructed, and no plants that affect the normal working effectiveness of navigation aids shall be planted.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　船舶航行时，应当与航标保持适当距离，不得触碰航标。  
船舶触碰航标，应当立即向航标管理机关报告。

**Article 14** When navigating, ships shall keep an appropriate distance from navigation aids and shall not touch them.  
If a ship touches a navigation aid, it shall immediately report to the navigation aids management authorities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止下列危害航标的行为：  
(一)盗窃、哄抢或者以其他方式非法侵占航标、航标器材；  
(二)非法移动、攀登或者涂抹航标；  
(三)向航标射击或者投掷物品；  
(四)在航标上攀架物品，拴系牲畜、船只、渔业捕捞器具、爆炸物品等；  
(五)损坏航标的其他行为。

**Article 15** The following acts that endanger navigation aids are prohibited:  
(1) Stealing, seizing, or illegally occupying navigation aids or navigation aid equipment by any other means;  
(2) Illegally moving, climbing over, or daubing navigation aids;  
(3) Shooting at or throwing objects at navigation aids;  
(4) Hanging objects on navigation aids, or tying livestock, vessels, fishing gear, explosive materials, etc. to them;  
(5) Other acts that damage navigation aids.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　禁止破坏航标辅助设施的行为。  
前款所称航标辅助设施，是指为航标及其管理人员提供能源、水和其他所需物资而设置的各类设施，包括航标场地、直升机平台、登陆点、码头、趸船、水塔、储水池、水井、油(水)泵房、电力设施、业务用房以及专用道路、仓库等。

**Article 16** Acts that damage navigation aid auxiliary facilities are prohibited.  
For the purposes of the preceding paragraph, navigation aid auxiliary facilities refer to various facilities established to provide energy, water, and other necessary materials to navigation aids and their management personnel, including navigation aid sites, helicopter platforms, landing points, piers, pontoons, water towers, reservoirs, wells, oil (water) pump houses, power facilities, service buildings, dedicated roads, warehouses, etc.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　禁止下列影响航标工作效能的行为：  
(一)在航标周围20米内或者在埋有航标地下管道、线路的地面钻孔、挖坑、采掘土石、堆放物品或者进行明火作业；  
(二)在航标周围150米内进行爆破作业；  
(三)在航标周围500米内烧荒；  
(四)在无线电导航设施附近设置、使用影响导航设施工作效能的高频电磁辐射装置、设备；  
(五)在航标架空线路上附挂其他电力、通信线路；  
(六)在航标周围抛锚、拖锚、捕鱼或者养殖水生物；  
(七)影响航标工作效能的其他行为。

**Article 17** The following acts that affect the effectiveness of navigation aids are prohibited:  
(1) Drilling, digging pits, excavating earth or stone, piling objects, or conducting open flame operations within 20 meters around navigation aids or on the ground where underground pipelines or cables of navigation aids are buried;  
(2) Conducting blasting operations within 150 meters around navigation aids;  
(3) Burning grass within 500 meters around navigation aids;  
(4) Installing or using high-frequency electromagnetic radiation devices or equipment near radio navigation aids that affect the effectiveness of such aids;  
(5) Attaching other power or communication lines to the overhead lines of navigation aids;  
(6) Anchoring, dragging anchors, fishing, or cultivating aquatic organisms around navigation aids;  
(7) Other acts that affect the effectiveness of navigation aids.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对有下列行为之一的单位和个人，由航标管理机关给予奖励：  
(一)检举、控告危害航标的行为，对破案有功的；  
(二)及时制止危害航标的行为，防止事故发生或者减少损失的；  
(三)捞获水上漂流航标，主动送交航标管理机关的。

**Article 18** Units or individuals that have performed any of the following acts shall be rewarded by the navigation aids management authorities:  
(1) Reporting or accusing acts that endanger navigation aids, and having rendered meritorious service in solving a case;  
(2) Timely stopping acts that endanger navigation aids, thereby preventing accidents or reducing losses;  
(3) Fishing out drifting navigation aids on the water and voluntarily delivering them to the navigation aids management authorities.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反[本条例第六条](#art-6)第二款的规定，擅自设置、撤除、移动专用航标或者改变专用航标的其他状况的，由航标管理机关责令限期拆除、重新设置、调整专用航标。

**Article 19** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 6 of these Regulations, establishes, removes, relocates special navigation aids without authorization, or changes any other status of special navigation aids, shall be ordered by the navigation aids management authorities to remove, re-establish, or adjust the special navigation aids within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列行为之一的，由航标管理机关责令限期改正或者采取相应的补救措施：  
(一)违反[本条例第十一条](#art-11)的规定，在航标附近设置灯光或者音响装置的；  
(二)违反[本条例第十三条](#art-13)的规定，构筑建筑物、构筑物或者种植植物的。

**Article 20** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the navigation aids management authorities to make corrections within a specified time limit or to take corresponding remedial measures:  
(1) Installing lights or sound devices near navigation aids in violation of Article 11 of these Regulations;  
(2) Constructing buildings or structures or planting plants in violation of Article 13 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　船舶违反[本条例第十四条](#art-14)第二款的规定，触碰航标不报告的，航标管理机关可以根据情节处以2万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，应当依法赔偿。

**Article 21** . Where a vessel violates the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 14 of these Regulations by failing to report after striking a navigation mark, the navigation mark administration authority may, according to the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反[本条例第十五条](#art-15)、第十六条、第十七条的规定，危害航标及其辅助设施或者影响航标工作效能的，由航标管理机关责令其限期改正，给予警告，可以并处2000元以下的罚款；造成损失的，应当依法赔偿。

**Article 22** . Where any person violates the provisions of Articles 15, 16, and 17 of these Regulations by endangering navigation marks or their auxiliary facilities or affecting the working efficiency of navigation marks, the navigation mark administration authority shall order that person to make corrections within a specified period, give a warning, and may also impose a fine of not more than 2,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例，危害军用航标及其辅助设施或者影响军用航标工作效能，应当处以罚款的，由军队的航标管理机构移交航标管理机关处罚。

**Article 23** . Where any person violates these Regulations by endangering military navigation marks or their auxiliary facilities or affecting the working efficiency of military navigation marks, and a fine shall be imposed, the military navigation mark administration authority shall transfer the case to the navigation mark administration authority for punishment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 24** . Where any person violates the provisions of these Regulations, constituting a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose punishment in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law of the People's Republic of China; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 25** . These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
