# 国际航行船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸检查办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02

> (1995年3月21日中华人民共和国国务院令第175号发布　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强对国际航行船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸的管理，便利船舶进出口岸，提高口岸效能，制定本办法。 第二条　进出中华人民共和国口岸的国际航行船舶(以下简称船舶)及其所载船员、旅客、货物和其他物品，由本办法第三条规定的机关依照本办法实施检查；但是，法律另有特别规定的，或者国务院另有特别规定的，从其规定。 第三条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构(以下简称港务监督机构)、中华人民共和国海关(以下简称海关)、中华人民共和国出入境边防检查机关是负责对船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸实施检查的机关(以下统称检查机关)。 第四条　检查机关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定实施检查并对违法行为进行处理。 港务监督机构负责召集有其他检查机关参加的船舶进出口岸检查联席会议，研究、解决船舶进出口岸检查的有关问题。 第五条　船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸，由船方或其代理人依照本办法有关规定办理进出口岸手续。除本办法第十条第二款、第十一条规定的情形或者其他特殊情形外，检查机关不登船检查。 船方或其代理人办理船舶进出口岸手续时，应当按照检查机关的有关规定准确填写报表，并如实提供有关证件、资料。 第六条　船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计抵达口岸7日前(航程不足7日的，在驶离上一口岸时)，填写《国际航行船舶进口岸申请书》，报请抵达口岸的港务监督机构审批。 拟进入长江水域的船舶，船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计经上海港区7日前(航程不足7日的，在驶离上一口岸时)，填写《国际航行船舶进口岸申请书》，报请抵达口岸的港务监督机构审批。 第七条　船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计抵达口岸24小时前(航程不足24小时的，在驶离上一口岸时)，将抵达时间、停泊地点、靠泊移泊计划及船员、旅客的有关情况报告检查机关。 第八条　船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前未办妥进口岸手续的，须在船舶抵达口岸24小时内到检查机关办理进口岸手续。 船舶在口岸停泊时间不足24小时的，经检查机关同意，船方或其代理人在办理进口岸手续时，可以同时办理出口岸手续。 第九条　船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前已经办妥进口岸手续的，船舶抵达后即可上下人员、装卸货物和其他物品。 船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前未办妥进口岸手续的，船舶抵达后，除检查机关办理进口岸检查手续的工作人员和引航员外，其他人员不得上下船舶、不得装卸货物和其他物品；船舶进出的上一口岸是中华人民共和国口岸的，船舶抵达后即可上下人员、装卸货物和其他物品，但是应当立即办理进口岸手续。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对国际航行船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸的管理，便利船舶进出口岸，提高口岸效能，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to strengthen the management of international voyage vessels entering and leaving the ports of the People's Republic of China, facilitate the entry and exit of vessels, and enhance port efficiency.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进出中华人民共和国口岸的国际航行船舶(以下简称船舶)及其所载船员、旅客、货物和其他物品，由[本办法第三条](#art-3)规定的机关依照本办法实施检查；但是，法律另有特别规定的，或者国务院另有特别规定的，从其规定。

**Article 2** The international voyage vessels (hereinafter referred to as vessels) entering and leaving the ports of the People's Republic of China, and their crew, passengers, cargo and other articles carried thereby, shall be inspected by the authorities specified in Article 3 of these Measures in accordance with these Measures; provided that if laws or the State Council provide otherwise, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国港务监督机构(以下简称港务监督机构)、中华人民共和国海关(以下简称海关)、中华人民共和国出入境边防检查机关是负责对船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸实施检查的机关(以下统称检查机关)。

**Article 3** The Harbor Superintendency Administration of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Harbor Superintendency Administration), the Customs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Customs), and the Exit-Entry Frontier Inspection Authority of the People's Republic of China are the authorities responsible for conducting inspections on vessels entering and leaving the ports of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter collectively referred to as the inspection authorities).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　检查机关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定实施检查并对违法行为进行处理。  
港务监督机构负责召集有其他检查机关参加的船舶进出口岸检查联席会议，研究、解决船舶进出口岸检查的有关问题。

**Article 4** The inspection authorities shall conduct inspections and handle illegal acts in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The Harbor Superintendency Administration shall be responsible for convening joint meetings on vessel entry and exit inspection attended by other inspection authorities to study and resolve issues concerning vessel entry and exit inspection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　船舶进出中华人民共和国口岸，由船方或其代理人依照本办法有关规定办理进出口岸手续。除[本办法第十条](#art-10)第二款、第十一条规定的情形或者其他特殊情形外，检查机关不登船检查。  
船方或其代理人办理船舶进出口岸手续时，应当按照检查机关的有关规定准确填写报表，并如实提供有关证件、资料。

**Article 5** For vessels entering and leaving the ports of the People's Republic of China, the vessel owner or its agent shall go through the entry and exit formalities in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Measures. Except for the circumstances specified in the second paragraph of Article 10 and Article 11 of these Measures or other special circumstances, the inspection authorities shall not board the vessel for inspection.  
When the vessel owner or its agent goes through the formalities for vessel entry and exit, they shall accurately fill in the forms in accordance with the relevant provisions of the inspection authorities and truthfully provide the relevant certificates and documents.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计抵达口岸7日前(航程不足7日的，在驶离上一口岸时)，填写《国际航行船舶进口岸申请书》，报请抵达口岸的港务监督机构审批。  
拟进入长江水域的船舶，船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计经上海港区7日前(航程不足7日的，在驶离上一口岸时)，填写《国际航行船舶进口岸申请书》，报请抵达口岸的港务监督机构审批。

**Article 6** The vessel owner or its agent shall, seven days before the vessel's scheduled arrival at the port (or, if the voyage is less than seven days, at the time of departure from the previous port), fill in the Application for Entry of International Voyage Vessel and submit it to the Harbor Superintendency Administration at the port of arrival for approval.  
For a vessel intending to enter the Yangtze River waters, the vessel owner or its agent shall, seven days before the vessel's scheduled passage through the Shanghai port area (or, if the voyage is less than seven days, at the time of departure from the previous port), fill in the Application for Entry of International Voyage Vessel and submit it to the Harbor Superintendency Administration at the port of arrival for approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船方或其代理人应当在船舶预计抵达口岸24小时前(航程不足24小时的，在驶离上一口岸时)，将抵达时间、停泊地点、靠泊移泊计划及船员、旅客的有关情况报告检查机关。

**Article 7** The vessel owner or its agent shall, 24 hours before the vessel's scheduled arrival at the port (or, if the voyage is less than 24 hours, at the time of departure from the previous port), report to the inspection authorities the time of arrival, berthing location, berthing and shifting plans, and relevant information on crew and passengers.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前未办妥进口岸手续的，须在船舶抵达口岸24小时内到检查机关办理进口岸手续。  
船舶在口岸停泊时间不足24小时的，经检查机关同意，船方或其代理人在办理进口岸手续时，可以同时办理出口岸手续。

**Article 8** If the vessel owner or its agent fails to complete the entry formalities before the vessel's arrival at the port, they must complete the entry formalities with the inspection authorities within 24 hours after the vessel's arrival at the port.  
If the vessel's berthing time at the port is less than 24 hours, with the consent of the inspection authorities, the vessel owner or its agent may simultaneously go through the exit formalities when going through the entry formalities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前已经办妥进口岸手续的，船舶抵达后即可上下人员、装卸货物和其他物品。  
船方或其代理人在船舶抵达口岸前未办妥进口岸手续的，船舶抵达后，除检查机关办理进口岸检查手续的工作人员和引航员外，其他人员不得上下船舶、不得装卸货物和其他物品；船舶进出的上一口岸是中华人民共和国口岸的，船舶抵达后即可上下人员、装卸货物和其他物品，但是应当立即办理进口岸手续。

**Article 9** If the vessel owner or its agent has completed the entry formalities before the vessel's arrival at the port, personnel may embark or disembark, and cargo and other articles may be loaded or unloaded immediately after the vessel's arrival.  
If the vessel owner or its agent fails to complete the entry formalities before the vessel's arrival at the port, after the vessel's arrival, no personnel other than the staff of the inspection authorities handling the entry inspection formalities and the pilot shall embark or disembark, and no cargo or other articles shall be loaded or unloaded; however, if the previous port of entry or exit of the vessel is a port of the People's Republic of China, personnel may embark or disembark, and cargo and other articles may be loaded or unloaded immediately after the vessel's arrival, but the entry formalities shall be completed without delay.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　海关对船舶实施电讯检疫。持有卫生证书的船舶，其船方或其代理人可以向海关申请电讯检疫。  
对来自疫区的船舶，载有检疫传染病染疫人、疑似检疫传染病染疫人、非意外伤害而死亡且死因不明尸体的船舶，未持有卫生证书或者证书过期或者卫生状况不符合要求的船舶，海关应当在锚地实施检疫。

**Article 10** The Customs shall carry out telecommunication quarantine on vessels. For vessels holding a sanitary certificate, the vessel owner or its agent may apply to the Customs for telecommunication quarantine.  
For vessels from epidemic areas, vessels carrying persons infected with a quarantinable infectious disease, persons suspected of being infected with a quarantinable infectious disease, or dead bodies from non-accidental injuries with unknown causes, vessels not holding a sanitary certificate, or vessels with an expired certificate or whose sanitary conditions do not meet the requirements, the Customs shall carry out quarantine at the anchorage.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海关对来自动植物疫区的船舶和船舶装载的动植物、动植物产品及其他检疫物，可以在锚地实施检疫。

**Article 11** The Customs may carry out quarantine at the anchorage on vessels from animal or plant epidemic areas and on animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine articles loaded on vessels.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　船方或其代理人应当在船舶驶离口岸前4小时内(船舶在口岸停泊时间不足4小时的，在抵达口岸时)，到检查机关办理必要的出口岸手续。有关检查机关应当在《船舶出口岸手续联系单》上签注；船方或其代理人持《船舶出口岸手续联系单》和港务监督机构要求的其他证件、资料，到港务监督机构申请领取出口岸许可证。

**Article 12** The vessel owner or its agent shall, within four hours before the vessel's departure from the port (or, if the vessel's berthing time at the port is less than four hours, at the time of arrival at the port), go to the inspection authorities to go through the necessary exit formalities. The relevant inspection authorities shall endorse the Ship Exit Formalities Contact Sheet; the vessel owner or its agent shall, holding the Ship Exit Formalities Contact Sheet and other certificates and documents required by the Harbor Superintendency Administration, apply to the Harbor Superintendency Administration for the exit permit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　船舶领取出口岸许可证后，情况发生变化或者24小时内未能驶离口岸的，船方或其代理人应当报告港务监督机构，由港务监督机构商其他检查机关决定是否重新办理出口岸手续。

**Article 13** After a vessel obtains the exit permit, if circumstances change or the vessel fails to depart from the port within 24 hours, the vessel owner or its agent shall report to the Harbor Superintendency Administration, which shall consult with the other inspection authorities to decide whether to re-go through the exit formalities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　定航线、定船员并在24小时内往返一个或者一个以上航次的船舶，船方或其代理人可以向港务监督机构书面申请办理定期进出口岸手续。受理申请的港务监督机构商其他检查机关审查批准后，签发有效期不超过7天的定期出口岸许可证，在许可证有效期内对该船舶免办进口岸手续。

**Article 14** For vessels with a fixed route, fixed crew, and a round trip of one or more voyages within 24 hours, the vessel owner or its agent may submit a written application to the Harbor Superintendency Administration for periodic entry and exit formalities. After the Harbor Superintendency Administration, which accepts the application, consults with the other inspection authorities and grants approval upon examination, it shall issue a periodic exit permit with a validity period not exceeding seven days. During the validity period of the permit, the vessel shall be exempted from entry formalities.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　检查机关及其工作人员必须秉公执法，恪尽职守，及时实施检查和办理船舶进出口岸的申请。

**Article 15** The inspection authorities and their staff must enforce the law impartially, faithfully perform their duties, and promptly carry out inspections and process applications for vessel entry and exit.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法下列用语的含义：  
(一)国际航行船舶，是指进出中华人民共和国口岸的外国籍船舶和航行国际航线的中华人民共和国国籍船舶。  
(二)口岸，是指国家批准可以进出国际航行船舶的港口。  
(三)船方，是指船舶所有人或者经营人。

**Article 16** For the purpose of these Measures, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:  
(1) "International voyage vessel" means a foreign-registered vessel entering or leaving a port of the People's Republic of China, or a vessel registered in the People's Republic of China navigating international routes.  
(2) "Port" means a port approved by the State for the entry and exit of international voyage vessels.  
(3) "Vessel owner" means the owner or operator of the vessel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自发布之日起施行。经国务院批准，1961年10月24日由交通部、对外贸易部、公安部、卫生部发布的《进出口船舶联合检查通则》同时废止。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The General Rules for Joint Inspection of Import and Export Vessels, which were approved by the State Council and promulgated by the Ministry of Communications, the Ministry of Foreign Trade, the Ministry of Public Security, and the Ministry of Health on October 24, 1961, shall be abolished simultaneously.
