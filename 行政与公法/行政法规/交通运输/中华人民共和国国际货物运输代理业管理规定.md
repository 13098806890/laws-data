# 中华人民共和国国际货物运输代理业管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1995-06-29

> (1995年6月6日国务院批准　1995年6月29日对外贸易经济合作部令1995年第5号发布) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了规范国际货物运输代理行为，保障进出口货物收货人、发货人和国际货物运输代理企业的合法权益，促进对外贸易的发展，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定所称国际货物运输代理业，是指接受进出口货物收货人、发货人的委托，以委托人的名义或者以自己的名义，为委托人办理国际货物运输及相关业务并收取服务报酬的行业。 第三条　国际货物运输代理企业必须依法取得中华人民共和国企业法人资格。 第四条　国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门负责对全国的国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理。 省、自治区、直辖市和经济特区的人民政府对外经济贸易主管部门(以下简称地方对外贸易主管部门)依照本规定，在国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门授权的范围内，负责对本行政区域内的国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理。 第五条　对国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理，应当遵循下列原则： (一)适应对外贸易发展的需要，促进国际货物运输代理业的合理布局； (二)保护公平竞争，促进国际货物运输代理业服务质量的提高。 第六条　从事国际货物运输代理业务的企业，应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、行政法规、接受有关行业主管机关依照有关法律、行政法规规定实施的监督管理。 第二章　设立条件 第七条　设立国际货物运输代理企业，根据其行业特点，应当具备下列条件： (一)有与其从事的国际货物运输代理业务相适应的专业人员； (二)有固定的营业场所和必要的营业设施；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范国际货物运输代理行为，保障进出口货物收货人、发货人和国际货物运输代理企业的合法权益，促进对外贸易的发展，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated for the purpose of regulating the conduct of international freight forwarding agencies, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of consignees, consignors of import and export goods and international freight forwarding enterprises, and promoting the development of foreign trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称国际货物运输代理业，是指接受进出口货物收货人、发货人的委托，以委托人的名义或者以自己的名义，为委托人办理国际货物运输及相关业务并收取服务报酬的行业。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Provisions, the term "international freight forwarding industry" means the industry that, upon the entrustment of the consignee or consignor of import and export goods, handles international freight transportation and related business for the entrusting party in the name of the entrusting party or in its own name, and collects service fees.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国际货物运输代理企业必须依法取得中华人民共和国企业法人资格。

**Article 3** An international freight forwarding enterprise must obtain the status of an enterprise legal person of the People's Republic of China according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门负责对全国的国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理。  
省、自治区、直辖市和经济特区的人民政府对外经济贸易主管部门(以下简称地方对外贸易主管部门)依照本规定，在国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门授权的范围内，负责对本行政区域内的国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理。

**Article 4** The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the international freight forwarding industry nationwide.  
The competent departments of foreign trade and economic cooperation of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government and special economic zones (hereinafter referred to as the local competent departments of foreign trade), in accordance with these Provisions and within the scope authorized by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council, shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the international freight forwarding industry within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对国际货物运输代理业实施监督管理，应当遵循下列原则：  
(一)适应对外贸易发展的需要，促进国际货物运输代理业的合理布局；  
(二)保护公平竞争，促进国际货物运输代理业服务质量的提高。

**Article 5** The supervision and administration of the international freight forwarding industry shall follow the following principles:  
(1) meeting the needs of the development of foreign trade and promoting the rational distribution of the international freight forwarding industry;  
(2) protecting fair competition and promoting the improvement of the service quality of the international freight forwarding industry.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事国际货物运输代理业务的企业，应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、行政法规、接受有关行业主管机关依照有关法律、行政法规规定实施的监督管理。

**Article 6** Enterprises engaged in the business of international freight forwarding shall abide by the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China and accept the supervision and administration implemented by the relevant competent authorities in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第二章　设立条件

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　设立国际货物运输代理企业，根据其行业特点，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与其从事的国际货物运输代理业务相适应的专业人员；  
(二)有固定的营业场所和必要的营业设施；  
(三)有稳定的进出口货源市场。

**Article 7** The establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise shall, based on the characteristics of the industry, meet the following conditions:  
(1) having professional personnel commensurate with the international freight forwarding business it engages in;  
(2) having a fixed business site and necessary business facilities;  
(3) having a stable market for import and export goods.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国际货物运输代理企业的注册资本最低限额应当符合下列要求：  
(一)经营海上国际货物运输代理业务的，注册资本最低限额为500万元人民币；  
(二)经营航空国际货物运输代理业务的，注册资本最低限额为300万元人民币；  
(三)经营陆路国际货物运输代理业务或者国际快递业务的，注册资本最低限额为200万元人民币。  
经营前款两项以上业务的，注册资本最低限额为其中最高一项的限额。  
国际货物运输代理企业每设立一个从事国际货物运输代理业务的分支机构，应当增加注册资本50万元。

**Article 8** The minimum registered capital of an international freight forwarding enterprise shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) if operating maritime international freight forwarding business, the minimum registered capital shall be 5 million RMB;  
(2) if operating air international freight forwarding business, the minimum registered capital shall be 3 million RMB;  
(3) if operating land international freight forwarding business or international express business, the minimum registered capital shall be 2 million RMB.  
If operating two or more types of business as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the minimum registered capital shall be the highest of those limits.  
Each time an international freight forwarding enterprise establishes a branch engaged in international freight forwarding business, it shall increase its registered capital by 500,000 RMB.

### 第三章　审批程序

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立国际货物运输代理企业，申请人应当向拟设立国际货物运输代理企业所在地的地方对外贸易主管部门提出申请，由地方对外贸易主管部门提出意见后，转报国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门审查批准。  
国务院部门在北京的直属企业申请在北京设立国际货物运输代理企业的，可以直接向国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门提出申请，由国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门审查批准。

**Article 9** For an application for the establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise, the applicant shall submit an application to the local competent department of foreign trade of the place where the proposed international freight forwarding enterprise is to be located. The local competent department of foreign trade shall give its opinions and then forward the application to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council for examination and approval.  
If an enterprise directly under a department of the State Council in Beijing applies for the establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise in Beijing, it may directly submit an application to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council, which shall examine and approve the application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请设立国际货物运输代理企业，应当报送下列文件：  
(一)申请书；  
(二)企业章程草案；  
(三)负责人和主要业务人员的姓名、职务和身份证明；  
(四)资信证明和营业设施情况；  
(五)国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门规定的其他文件。

**Article 10** An application for the establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise shall submit the following documents:  
(1) a written application;  
(2) a draft of the enterprise's articles of association;  
(3) the names, positions and identity certificates of the responsible persons and main professional personnel;  
(4) a certificate of creditworthiness and a statement of business facilities;  
(5) other documents as prescribed by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　地方对外贸易主管部门应当自收到申请设立国际货物运输代理企业的申请书和其他文件之日起45天内提出意见，并转报国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门。  
国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门应当自收到申请设立国际货物运输代理企业的申请书和其他文件之日起45天内决定批准或者不批准；对批准设立的国际货物运输代理企业，颁发批准证书。

**Article 11** The local competent department of foreign trade shall give its opinions within 45 days from the date of receipt of the application and other documents for the establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise, and forward the same to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council.  
The competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall, within 45 days from the date of receipt of the application and other documents for the establishment of an international freight forwarding enterprise, decide whether to approve or disapprove the application; if it approves the establishment, it shall issue an approval certificate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国际货物运输代理企业应当凭国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门颁发的批准证书，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，办理企业登记、税务登记手续。

**Article 12** An international freight forwarding enterprise shall, on the strength of the approval certificate issued by the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council, handle the formalities for enterprise registration and tax registration in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请人自收到批准证书之日起180天内无正当理由未开始营业的，国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门应当撤销批准证书。

**Article 13** If an applicant, without justifiable reasons, fails to commence business within 180 days from the date of receipt of the approval certificate, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall revoke the approval certificate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　批准证书的有效期为3年。  
国际货物运输代理企业在批准证书有效期届满时，需要继续从事国际货物运输代理业务的，应当在批准证书有效期届满的30天前向国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门申请换领批准证书。  
国际货物运输代理企业未依照前款规定申请换领批准证书的，其从事国际货物运输代理业务的资格自批准证书有效期届满时自动丧失。

**Article 14** The validity period of an approval certificate shall be three years.  
If an international freight forwarding enterprise needs to continue engaging in international freight forwarding business upon the expiration of the validity period of its approval certificate, it shall apply to the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council for renewal of the approval certificate 30 days prior to the expiration of the validity period.  
If an international freight forwarding enterprise fails to apply for renewal of the approval certificate in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, its qualification to engage in international freight forwarding business shall be automatically forfeited upon the expiration of the validity period of the approval certificate.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国际货物运输代理企业终止营业，应当依照[本规定第九条](#art-9)规定的设立申请批准程序，报告所在地的地方对外贸易主管部门或者国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门并缴销批准证书。

**Article 15** If an international freight forwarding enterprise terminates its business, it shall, in accordance with the procedures for application and approval of establishment as prescribed in Article 9 of these Provisions, report to the local competent department of foreign trade or the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council and surrender its approval certificate for cancellation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国际货物运输代理企业申请设立从事国际货物运输代理业务的分支机构，应当依照本章规定的程序办理。

**Article 16** An international freight forwarding enterprise applying for the establishment of a branch engaged in international freight forwarding business shall handle the formalities in accordance with the procedures prescribed in this Chapter.

### 第四章　业务

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国际货物运输代理企业可以接受委托，代为办理下列部分或者全部业务：  
(一)订舱、仓储；  
(二)货物的监装、监卸，集装箱拼装拆箱；  
(三)国际多式联运；  
(四)国际快递，私人信函除外；  
(五)报关、报检、报验、保险；  
(六)缮制有关单证，交付运费，结算、交付杂费；  
(七)其他国际货物运输代理业务。  
国际货物运输代理企业应当在批准的业务经营范围内，从事经营活动。从事前款有关业务，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，需经有关主管机关注册的，还应当向有关主管机关注册。  
国际货物运输代理企业之间也可以相互委托办理本条第一款规定的业务。

**Article 17** An international freight forwarding enterprise may accept entrustment and handle, on behalf of the entrusting party, part or all of the following business:  
(1) booking space, warehousing;  
(2) supervising loading and unloading of goods, container stuffing and stripping;  
(3) international multimodal transport;  
(4) international express, excluding private letters;  
(5) customs declaration, inspection application, survey application, insurance;  
(6) preparing relevant documents, paying freight, settling and paying miscellaneous charges;  
(7) other international freight forwarding business.  
An international freight forwarding enterprise shall engage in business activities within the approved scope of business operation. If, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, engaging in the business mentioned in the preceding paragraph requires registration with the relevant competent authorities, it shall also register with such authorities.  
International freight forwarding enterprises may also entrust each other to handle the business specified in the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国际货物运输代理企业应当遵循安全、迅速、准确、节省、方便的经营方针，为进出口货物的收货人、发货人提供服务。

**Article 18** An international freight forwarding enterprise shall follow the business policy of safety, speed, accuracy, economy and convenience in providing services to the consignees and consignors of import and export goods.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国际货物运输代理企业，必须依照国家有关规定确定收费标准，并在其营业地点予以公布。

**Article 19** An international freight forwarding enterprise must determine its charging standards in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and make them public at its business premises.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国际货物运输代理企业从事国际货物运输代理业务，必须使用经税务机关核准的发票。

**Article 20** An international freight forwarding enterprise, when engaging in international freight forwarding business, must use invoices approved by tax authorities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国际货物运输代理企业应当于每年3月底前，向其所在地的地方对外贸易主管部门报送上一年度的经营情况资料。

**Article 21** An international freight forwarding enterprise shall, before the end of March each year, submit to the local competent department of foreign trade of its location the operational information of the preceding year.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国际货物运输代理企业不得有下列行为：  
(一)以不正当竞争手段从事经营活动；  
(二)出借、出租或者转让批准证书和有关国际货物运输代理业务单证。

**Article 22** An international freight forwarding enterprise shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) engaging in business activities by means of unfair competition;  
(2) lending, leasing or transferring the approval certificate and relevant documents of international freight forwarding business.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国际货物运输代理企业违反[本规定第十九条](#art-19)、第二十一条规定的，由国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门予以警告并责令限期改正；未在限期内改正的，可以撤销其批准证书。

**Article 23** If an international freight forwarding enterprise violates the provisions of Article 19 or Article 21 of these Provisions, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall give it a warning and order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, its approval certificate may be revoked.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国际货物运输代理企业违反[本规定第十七条](#art-17)第二款、第二十条、第二十二条规定的，由国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门予以警告、责令停业整顿直至撤销其批准证书；工商行政管理、海关、税务等有关主管机关并可依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 24** If an international freight forwarding enterprise violates the provisions of Article 17, paragraph 2, Article 20 or Article 22 of these Provisions, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall give it a warning, order it to suspend business for rectification, or even revoke its approval certificate; the relevant competent authorities, such as the administrative department for industry and commerce, customs, and tax authorities, may also impose punishment in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　违反本规定的规定，擅自从事[本规定第十七条](#art-17)规定的国际货物运输代理业务的，由国务院对外贸易经济合作主管部门取缔非法经营活动，并由工商行政管理机关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚。

**Article 25** If anyone, in violation of the provisions of these Provisions, engages without authorization in the international freight forwarding business as specified in Article 17 of these Provisions, the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council shall ban such illegal business activities, and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall impose punishment in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　违反本规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 26** If a violation of these Provisions constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国际货物运输代理企业可以依法设立国际货物运输代理业协会，协会依照其章程对会员进行协调指导，提供服务。

**Article 27** An international freight forwarding enterprise may, according to law, establish an association of the international freight forwarding industry, which shall, in accordance with its articles of association, coordinate and guide its members and provide services.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
