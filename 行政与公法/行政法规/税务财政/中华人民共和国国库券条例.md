### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了筹集社会资金，进行社会主义现代化建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of raising social funds to carry out socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国库券的发行对象是：居民个人、个体工商户、企业、事业单位、机关、社会团体和其他组织。

**Article 2** The targets for issuance of treasury bonds shall be: individual residents, individual industrial and commercial households, enterprises, institutions, state organs, social organizations, and other organizations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国库券以人民币元为计算单位。

**Article 3** The unit of calculation for treasury bonds shall be the Renminbi yuan.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　每年国库券的发行数额、利率、偿还期等，经国务院确定后，由财政部予以公告。

**Article 4** The amount issued, interest rate, term of repayment, etc. of treasury bonds each year shall be determined by the State Council, and then announced by the Ministry of Finance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国库券发行采取承购包销、认购等方式。  
国家下达的国库券发行计划，应当按期完成。

**Article 5** The issuance of treasury bonds shall adopt methods such as underwriting and subscription.  
The treasury bond issuance plan issued by the State shall be completed on schedule.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国库券按期偿还本金。国库券利息在偿还本金时一次付给，不计复利。

**Article 6** The principal of treasury bonds shall be repaid on schedule. The interest on treasury bonds shall be paid in a lump sum at the time of repayment of principal, without compound interest.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国库券的发行和还本付息事宜，在各级人民政府统一领导下，由财政部门和中国人民银行组织有关部门多渠道办理。

**Article 7** Matters concerning the issuance and repayment of principal and interest of treasury bonds shall, under the unified leadership of people's governments at all levels, be handled by the financial departments and the People's Bank of China by organizing relevant departments through multiple channels.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国库券可以用于抵押，但是不得作为货币流通。

**Article 8** Treasury bonds may be used for mortgage, but shall not be circulated as currency.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国库券可以转让，但是应当在国家批准的交易场所办理。

**Article 9** Treasury bonds may be transferred, but the transfer shall be conducted at a transaction venue approved by the State.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　发行国库券筹集的资金，由国务院统一安排使用。

**Article 10** The funds raised through the issuance of treasury bonds shall be arranged and used in a unified manner by the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对伪造国库券的，依法追究刑事责任。  
对倒卖国库券的，按照投机倒把论处。(2011年1月8日删除)

**Article 11** Whoever forges treasury bonds shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.  
Whoever resells treasury bonds shall be punished as speculation and profiteering. (deleted on January 8, 2011)

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国库券的利息收入享受免税待遇。

**Article 12** Interest income from treasury bonds shall enjoy tax exemption treatment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本条例由财政部负责解释。实施细则由财政部商中国人民银行制定。

**Article 13** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the Ministry of Finance. Implementing rules shall be formulated by the Ministry of Finance in consultation with the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 14** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
