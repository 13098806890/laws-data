# 中华人民共和国国家金库条例

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了统一组织国家财政收支，健全国家金库制度，特制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are enacted for the purpose of uniformly organizing the state's fiscal revenue and expenditure and perfecting the state treasury system.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家金库(以下简称国库)负责办理国家预算资金的收入和支出。在执行任务中，必须认真贯彻国家的方针、政策和财经制度，发挥国库的促进和监督作用。

**Article 2** The State Treasury (hereinafter referred to as the Treasury) shall be responsible for handling the receipt and disbursement of state budgetary funds. In the performance of its tasks, it must earnestly implement the state's principles, policies, and financial and economic systems, and give play to the Treasury's role in promotion and supervision.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国人民银行具体经理国库。组织管理国库工作是人民银行的一项重要职责。

**Article 3** The People's Bank of China shall specifically manage the Treasury. Organizing and managing the Treasury work is an important duty of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级国库库款的支配权，按照国家财政体制的规定，分别属于同级财政机关。

**Article 4** The power to control treasury funds at all levels shall, in accordance with the provisions of the state fiscal system, vest respectively in the fiscal authorities at the corresponding levels.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府应加强对同级国库的领导，监督所属部门、单位、不得超越国家规定的范围动用国库库款。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over the Treasury at the corresponding level, and supervise their subordinate departments and units not to use treasury funds beyond the scope prescribed by the state.

### 第二章　国库的组织机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国库机构按照国家财政管理体制设立，原则上一级财政设立一级国库。中央设立总库；省、自治区、直辖市设立分库；省辖市、自治州设立中心支库；县和相当于县的市、区设立支库。支库以下经收处的业务，由专业银行的基层机构代理。

**Article 6** The Treasury organization shall be established in accordance with the state fiscal management system, and in principle, one level of fiscal administration shall have one level of Treasury. The Central Treasury shall be established at the central level; branch treasuries shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; central sub-branches shall be established in cities under provincial jurisdiction and autonomous prefectures; and sub-branches shall be established in counties and cities or districts equivalent to counties. The business of receiving offices below the sub-branch level shall be handled by the grass-roots institutions of specialized banks on an agency basis.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级国库的主任，由各该级人民银行行长兼任，副主任由主管国库工作的副行长兼任。不设人民银行机构的地方，国库业务由人民银行委托当地专业银行办理，工作上受上级国库领导，受委托的专业银行行长兼国库主任。

**Article 7** The director of the Treasury at each level shall be concurrently held by the governor of the People's Bank of China at that level, and the deputy director shall be concurrently held by the deputy governor in charge of Treasury work. In localities where no People's Bank of China institution is established, the Treasury business shall be handled by the local specialized bank as entrusted by the People's Bank of China, and the specialized bank shall be subject to the leadership of the superior Treasury in its work, and the governor of the entrusted specialized bank shall concurrently serve as the director of the Treasury.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国库业务工作实行垂直领导。各省、自治区、直辖市分库及其所属各级支库，既是中央国库的分支机构，也是地方国库。

**Article 8** The Treasury business work shall be subject to vertical leadership. The branch treasuries of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and their subordinate sub-branches at all levels shall be both branch institutions of the Central Treasury and local treasuries.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　各级国库应当设立专门的工作机构办理国库业务。机构设置按照[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定，四级国库分别为司、处、科、股。人员应当稳定，编制单列。业务量不大的县支库，可不设专门机构，但要有专人办理国库业务。

**Article 9** Treasuries at all levels shall set up specialized working organs to handle Treasury business. The establishment of such organs shall be in accordance with the provisions of Article 6 of these Regulations, and the four-level treasuries shall be respectively at the department, division, section, and unit levels. The personnel shall be stable, and their staffing shall be listed separately. For county sub-branches with a small volume of business, a specialized organ may not be established, but there shall be dedicated personnel to handle Treasury business.

### 第三章　国库的职责权限

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国库的基本职责如下：  
(一)办理国家预算收入的收纳、划分和留解。  
(二)办理国家预算支出的拨付。  
(三)向上级国库和同级财政机关反映预算收支执行情况。  
(四)协助财政、税务机关督促企业和其他有经济收入的单位及时向国家缴纳应缴款项，对于屡催不缴的，应依照税法协助扣收入库。  
(五)组织管理和检查指导下级国库的工作。  
(六)办理国家交办的同国库有关的其他工作。

**Article 10** The basic duties of the Treasury are as follows:  
(1) To handle the receipt, division, and retention or remittance of state budgetary revenues.  
(2) To handle the disbursement of state budgetary expenditures.  
(3) To report to the superior Treasury and the fiscal authority at the same level on the execution of budgetary revenues and expenditures.  
(4) To assist fiscal and tax authorities in urging enterprises and other units with economic income to timely pay to the state the amounts due; for those who repeatedly fail to pay despite reminders, to assist in withholding and turning over to the Treasury in accordance with the tax law.  
(5) To organize, manage, and inspect and guide the work of lower-level treasuries.  
(6) To handle other tasks related to the Treasury as assigned by the state.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国库的主要权限如下：  
(一)督促检查各经收处和收入机关所收之款是否按规定全部缴入国库，发现违法不缴的，应及时查究处理。  
(二)对擅自变更各级财政之间收入划分范围、分成留解比例，以及随意调整库款账户之间存款余额的，国库有权拒绝执行。  
(三)对不符合国家规定要求办理退库的，国库有权拒绝办理。  
(四)监督财政存款的开户和财政库款的支拨。  
(五)任何单位和个人强令国库办理违反国家规定的事项，国库有权拒绝执行，并及时向上级报告。  
(六)对不符合规定的凭证，国库有权拒绝受理。

**Article 11** The main powers of the Treasury are as follows:  
(1) To supervise and inspect whether the funds collected by each receiving office and revenue authority are all turned over to the Treasury in accordance with the regulations; if any illegal non-payment is discovered, it shall promptly investigate and deal with it.  
(2) The Treasury shall have the power to refuse to execute any unauthorized changes in the scope of revenue division among fiscal levels, the proportion of revenue retention and remittance, and any arbitrary adjustment of deposit balances between treasury fund accounts.  
(3) The Treasury shall have the power to refuse to handle any tax refund that does not meet the requirements of state regulations.  
(4) To supervise the opening of fiscal deposit accounts and the disbursement of fiscal treasury funds.  
(5) If any entity or individual compels the Treasury to handle matters that violate state regulations, the Treasury shall have the power to refuse to execute and shall promptly report to the superior authority.  
(6) The Treasury shall have the power to refuse to accept any vouchers that do not conform to the regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级国库应加强会计核算工作，严密核算手续，健全账簿报表，保证各项预算收支数字完整、准确。

**Article 12** Treasuries at all levels shall strengthen their accounting work, tighten accounting procedures, improve account books and statements, and ensure the completeness and accuracy of all budgetary revenue and expenditure figures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国库工作人员要忠于职守，热爱本职工作，严格保守国家机密。对坚持执行国家方针、政策和财经制度，敢于同违反财经纪律行为作斗争的，要给予表扬和鼓励；对打击报复国库人员的，要严肃处理。

**Article 13** Treasury staff members shall be devoted to their duties, love their work, and strictly keep state secrets. Those who firmly implement the state's principles, policies, and financial and economic systems and dare to struggle against acts that violate financial discipline shall be commended and encouraged; those who retaliate against Treasury personnel shall be seriously dealt with.

### 第四章　库款的收纳与退付

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家的一切预算收入，应按照规定全部缴入国库，任何单位不得截留、坐支或自行保管。

**Article 14** All state budgetary revenues shall be fully turned over to the Treasury in accordance with the regulations, and no unit may withhold, use directly, or keep them on its own.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家各项预算收入，分别由各级财政机关、税务机关和海关负责管理，并监督缴入国库。缴库方式由财政部和中国人民银行总行另行规定。

**Article 15** All state budgetary revenues shall be managed respectively by fiscal authorities, tax authorities, and customs at all levels, and they shall supervise the payment into the Treasury. The methods of payment into the Treasury shall be separately prescribed by the Ministry of Finance and the head office of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国库收纳库款以人民币为限。以金银、外币等缴款，应当向当地银行兑换成人民币后缴纳。

**Article 16** The Treasury shall accept treasury funds only in Renminbi. Where payments are made in gold, silver, foreign currency, etc., they shall be converted into Renminbi at the local bank and then paid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　预算收入的退付，必须在国家统一规定的退库范围内办理。必须从收入中退库的，应严格按照财政管理体制的规定，从各该级预算收入的有关项目中退付。

**Article 17** Refunds of budgetary revenues shall be handled within the scope of tax refunds uniformly prescribed by the state. Where a refund must be made from revenues, it shall be strictly in accordance with the provisions of the fiscal management system and shall be refunded from the relevant items of budgetary revenues at the corresponding level.

### 第五章　库款的支拨

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家的一切预算支出，一律凭各级财政机关的拨款凭证，经国库统一办理拨付。

**Article 18** All state budgetary expenditures shall be disbursed uniformly through the Treasury on the strength of disbursement vouchers of fiscal authorities at all levels.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中央预算支出，采取实拨资金和限额管理两种方式。中央级行政事业经费，实行限额管理。地方预算支出，采用实拨资金的方式；如果采用限额管理，财政应随限额拨足资金，不由银行垫款。

**Article 19** Central budgetary expenditures shall adopt two methods: actual allocation of funds and quota management. Central-level administrative and institutional expenses shall be subject to quota management. Local budgetary expenditures shall adopt the method of actual allocation of funds; if quota management is adopted, the finance department shall allocate sufficient funds together with the quota, and banks shall not advance funds.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各级国库库款的支拨，必须在同级财政存款余额内支付。只办理转账，不支付现金。

**Article 20** The disbursement of treasury funds at all levels shall be made within the balance of fiscal deposits at the corresponding level. Only transfers shall be handled, and no cash shall be paid.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本条例实施细则，由财政部和中国人民银行总行共同制定。

**Article 21** The detailed rules for the implementation of these Regulations shall be formulated jointly by the Ministry of Finance and the head office of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　专业银行代办国库业务的具体办法，由中国人民银行总行另行制定。

**Article 22** The specific measures for specialized banks to handle Treasury business on an agency basis shall be separately formulated by the head office of the People's Bank of China.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例自发布之日起施行。一九五○年三月三日中央人民政府政务院公布的《中央金库条例》同时废止。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Central Treasury Regulations promulgated by the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government on March 3, 1950 shall be abolished simultaneously.
