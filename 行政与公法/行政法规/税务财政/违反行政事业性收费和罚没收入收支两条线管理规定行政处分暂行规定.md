# 违反行政事业性收费和罚没收入收支两条线管理规定行政处分暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2000-02-12  
**生效日期**：2000-02-12

> (2000年2月1日国务院第26次常务会议通过　2000年2月12日中华人民共和国国务院令第281号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了严肃财经纪律，加强廉政建设，落实行政事业性收费和罚没收入“收支两条线”管理，促进依法行政，根据法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，制定本规定。 第二条　国家公务员和法律、行政法规授权行使行政事业性收费或者罚没职能的事业单位的工作人员有违反“收支两条线”管理规定行为的，依照本规定给予行政处分。 第三条　本规定所称“行政事业性收费”，是指下列属于财政性资金的收入： (一)依据法律、行政法规、国务院有关规定、国务院财政部门与计划部门共同发布的规章或者规定以及省、自治区、直辖市的地方性法规、政府规章或者规定和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门与计划(物价)部门共同发布的规定所收取的各项收费； (二)法律、行政法规和国务院规定的以及国务院财政部门按照国家有关规定批准的政府性基金、附加。 事业单位因提供服务收取的经营服务性收费不属于行政事业性收费。 第四条　本规定所称“罚没收入”，是指法律、行政法规授权的执行处罚的部门依法实施处罚取得的罚没款和没收物品的折价收入。 第五条　违反规定，擅自设立行政事业性收费项目或者设置罚没处罚的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分。 第六条　违反规定，擅自变更行政事业性收费或者罚没范围、标准的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。 第七条　对行政事业性收费项目审批机关已经明令取消或者降低标准的收费项目，仍按原定项目或者标准收费的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。 第八条　下达或者变相下达罚没指标的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分。 第九条　违反《收费许可证》规定实施行政事业性收费的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告处分；情节严重的，给予记过或者记大过处分。 第十条　违反财政票据管理规定实施行政事业性收费、罚没的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分；以实施行政事业性收费、罚没的名义收取钱物，不出具任何票据的，给予开除处分。 第十一条　违反罚款决定与罚款收缴分离的规定收缴罚款的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过或者降级处分。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了严肃财经纪律，加强廉政建设，落实行政事业性收费和罚没收入“收支两条线”管理，促进依法行政，根据法律、行政法规和国家有关规定，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state regulations, with a view to strictly enforcing financial and economic discipline, strengthening the building of a clean government, implementing the "separate management of revenue and expenditure" for administrative fees and confiscated proceeds, and promoting law-based administration.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家公务员和法律、行政法规授权行使行政事业性收费或者罚没职能的事业单位的工作人员有违反“收支两条线”管理规定行为的，依照本规定给予行政处分。

**Article 2** Where state civil servants and staff members of public institutions authorized by laws and administrative regulations to exercise administrative fee collection or confiscation functions violate the regulations on "separate management of revenue and expenditure", they shall be given administrative sanctions in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本规定所称“行政事业性收费”，是指下列属于财政性资金的收入：  
(一)依据法律、行政法规、国务院有关规定、国务院财政部门与计划部门共同发布的规章或者规定以及省、自治区、直辖市的地方性法规、政府规章或者规定和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门与计划(物价)部门共同发布的规定所收取的各项收费；  
(二)法律、行政法规和国务院规定的以及国务院财政部门按照国家有关规定批准的政府性基金、附加。  
事业单位因提供服务收取的经营服务性收费不属于行政事业性收费。

**Article 3** The term "administrative fees" as used in these Provisions refers to the following income belonging to fiscal funds:  
(1) Various fees collected pursuant to laws, administrative regulations, relevant regulations of the State Council, rules or regulations jointly issued by the finance department and the planning department of the State Council, as well as local regulations, government rules or regulations of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and regulations jointly issued by the finance department and the planning (price) department of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) Government funds and surcharges stipulated by laws, administrative regulations and the State Council, as well as those approved by the finance department of the State Council in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
Operational service fees collected by public institutions for providing services shall not be considered administrative fees.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本规定所称“罚没收入”，是指法律、行政法规授权的执行处罚的部门依法实施处罚取得的罚没款和没收物品的折价收入。

**Article 4** The term "confiscated proceeds" as used in these Provisions refers to the fines and the converted value of confiscated articles obtained by departments authorized by laws and administrative regulations to impose penalties, in accordance with law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　违反规定，擅自设立行政事业性收费项目或者设置罚没处罚的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 5** Where anyone, in violation of regulations, establishes administrative fee items or sets confiscation penalties without authorization, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions of demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　违反规定，擅自变更行政事业性收费或者罚没范围、标准的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 6** Where anyone, in violation of regulations, alters the scope or standards of administrative fees or confiscation without authorization, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对行政事业性收费项目审批机关已经明令取消或者降低标准的收费项目，仍按原定项目或者标准收费的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 7** Where anyone still collects fees according to the original items or standards for fee items that have been explicitly abolished or reduced by the approval authority for administrative fee items, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　下达或者变相下达罚没指标的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 8** Where anyone assigns or indirectly assigns confiscation targets, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　违反《收费许可证》规定实施行政事业性收费的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告处分；情节严重的，给予记过或者记大过处分。

**Article 9** Where anyone implements administrative fee collection in violation of the provisions of the "Fee Collection Permit", the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a demerit record or a serious demerit record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　违反财政票据管理规定实施行政事业性收费、罚没的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职处分；以实施行政事业性收费、罚没的名义收取钱物，不出具任何票据的，给予开除处分。

**Article 10** Where anyone implements administrative fee collection or confiscation in violation of the regulations on financial receipts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given demotion or removal from office; if any money or goods are collected under the guise of administrative fee collection or confiscation without issuing any receipt, they shall be given expulsion.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　违反罚款决定与罚款收缴分离的规定收缴罚款的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过或者降级处分。

**Article 11** Where anyone collects fines in violation of the provisions on the separation of fine decision and fine collection, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record or demotion.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　不履行行政事业性收费、罚没职责，应收不收、应罚不罚，经批评教育仍不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告处分；情节严重的，给予记过或者记大过处分。

**Article 12** Where anyone fails to perform the duties of administrative fee collection or confiscation, neither collecting what should be collected nor fining what should be fined, and refuses to correct after criticism and education, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a warning; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a demerit record or a serious demerit record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　不按照规定将行政事业性收费纳入单位财务统一核算、管理的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记过处分；情节严重的，给予记大过或者降级处分。

**Article 13** Where anyone fails to incorporate administrative fees into the unified financial accounting and management of the unit in accordance with regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a serious demerit record or demotion.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　不按照规定将行政事业性收费缴入国库或者预算外资金财政专户的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。  
不按照规定将罚没收入上缴国库的，依照前款规定给予处分。

**Article 14** Where anyone fails to turn over administrative fees into the state treasury or the special fiscal account for extra-budgetary funds in accordance with regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given demotion or removal from office.  
Where anyone fails to turn over confiscated proceeds into the state treasury in accordance with regulations, sanctions shall be given in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反规定，擅自开设银行帐户的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 15** Where anyone, in violation of regulations, opens a bank account without authorization, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given demotion; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given removal from office or expulsion.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　截留、挪用、坐收坐支行政事业性收费、罚没收入的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 16** Where anyone intercepts, misappropriates, or directly collects and disburses administrative fees or confiscated proceeds, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given demotion; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given removal from office or expulsion.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反规定，将行政事业性收费、罚没收入用于提高福利补贴标准或者扩大福利补贴范围、滥发奖金实物、挥霍浪费或者有其他超标准支出行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 17** Where anyone, in violation of regulations, uses administrative fees or confiscated proceeds to raise the standards or expand the scope of welfare subsidies, excessively distribute bonuses or goods, engage in wasteful spending, or have other expenditures exceeding the standards, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a serious demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given demotion or removal from office.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　不按照规定编制预算外资金收支计划、单位财务收支计划和收支决算的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记过处分；情节严重的，给予记大过或者降级处分。

**Article 18** Where anyone fails to prepare the revenue and expenditure plan for extra-budgetary funds, the unit's financial revenue and expenditure plan, and revenue and expenditure final accounts in accordance with regulations, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a serious demerit record or demotion.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　不按照预算和批准的收支计划核拨财政资金，贻误核拨对象正常工作的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记过处分；情节严重的，给予记大过或者降级处分。

**Article 19** Where anyone fails to allocate fiscal funds in accordance with the budget and the approved revenue and expenditure plan, thereby delaying the normal work of the allocated target, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given a demerit record; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given a serious demerit record or demotion.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对坚持原则抵制违法违纪的行政事业性收费、罚没行为的单位或者个人打击报复的，给予降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 20** Where anyone retaliates against a unit or individual that persists in principle and resists illegal or disciplinary administrative fee collection or confiscation acts, they shall be given demotion; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given removal from office or expulsion.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　实施行政处分的权限以及不服行政处分的申诉，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 21** The authority to impose administrative sanctions and the petition against administrative sanctions shall be handled in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 22** Where anyone violates these Provisions and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 23** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
