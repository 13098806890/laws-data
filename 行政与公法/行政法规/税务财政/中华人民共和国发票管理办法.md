**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-20  
**生效日期**：2023-07-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强发票管理和财务监督，保障国家税收收入，维护经济秩序，根据《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection to strengthen invoice management and financial supervision, ensure national tax revenue, and maintain economic order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内印制、领用、开具、取得、保管、缴销发票的单位和个人（以下称印制、使用发票的单位和个人），必须遵守本办法。

**Article 2** All entities and individuals that print, receive, issue, obtain, keep, or cancel invoices within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as entities and individuals that print or use invoices) must comply with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法所称发票，是指在购销商品、提供或者接受服务以及从事其他经营活动中，开具、收取的收付款凭证。  
发票包括纸质发票和电子发票。电子发票与纸质发票具有同等法律效力。国家积极推广使用电子发票。

**Article 3** For the purposes of these Measures, invoices refer to receipts or payment vouchers issued or received in the purchase or sale of goods, provision or receipt of services, and other business activities.  
Invoices include paper invoices and electronic invoices. Electronic invoices have the same legal effect as paper invoices. The State actively promotes the use of electronic invoices.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　发票管理工作应当坚持和加强党的领导，为经济社会发展服务。  
国务院税务主管部门统一负责全国的发票管理工作。省、自治区、直辖市税务机关依据职责做好本行政区域内的发票管理工作。  
财政、审计、市场监督管理、公安等有关部门在各自的职责范围内，配合税务机关做好发票管理工作。

**Article 4** Invoice management shall adhere to and strengthen the leadership of the Party and serve economic and social development.  
The tax administration department under the State Council shall be responsible for invoice management nationwide. The tax authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with their functions, properly carry out invoice management within their respective administrative regions.  
The finance, audit, market regulation, public security, and other relevant departments shall, within their respective functions, cooperate with the tax authorities in invoice management.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　发票的种类、联次、内容、编码规则、数据标准、使用范围等具体管理办法由国务院税务主管部门规定。

**Article 5** Specific management measures such as the types, copies, content, coding rules, data standards, and scope of use of invoices shall be prescribed by the tax administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对违反发票管理法规的行为，任何单位和个人可以举报。税务机关应当为检举人保密，并酌情给予奖励。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual may report any act that violates the laws and regulations on invoice management. The tax authority shall keep the reporter's confidentiality and give a reward as appropriate.

### 第二章　发票的印制

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　增值税专用发票由国务院税务主管部门确定的企业印制；其他发票，按照国务院税务主管部门的规定，由省、自治区、直辖市税务机关确定的企业印制。禁止私自印制、伪造、变造发票。

**Article 7** Special VAT invoices shall be printed by enterprises designated by the tax administration department under the State Council; other invoices shall, in accordance with the provisions of the tax administration department under the State Council, be printed by enterprises designated by the tax authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. It is prohibited to print, forge, or alter invoices without authorization.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　印制发票的企业应当具备下列条件：  
（一）取得印刷经营许可证和营业执照；  
（二）设备、技术水平能够满足印制发票的需要；  
（三）有健全的财务制度和严格的质量监督、安全管理、保密制度。  
税务机关应当按照政府采购有关规定确定印制发票的企业。

**Article 8** An enterprise that prints invoices shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having obtained a printing operation permit and a business license;  
(2) having equipment and technical capabilities sufficient for printing invoices;  
(3) having a sound financial system and strict quality supervision, safety management, and confidentiality systems.  
The tax authority shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions on government procurement, determine the enterprises that print invoices.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　印制发票应当使用国务院税务主管部门确定的全国统一的发票防伪专用品。禁止非法制造发票防伪专用品。

**Article 9** The printing of invoices shall use the nationally unified special anti-counterfeiting products for invoices designated by the tax administration department under the State Council. It is prohibited to illegally manufacture special anti-counterfeiting products for invoices.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　发票应当套印全国统一发票监制章。全国统一发票监制章的式样和发票版面印刷的要求，由国务院税务主管部门规定。发票监制章由省、自治区、直辖市税务机关制作。禁止伪造发票监制章。  
发票实行不定期换版制度。

**Article 10** Invoices shall bear the nationally unified invoice supervision stamp. The pattern of the nationally unified invoice supervision stamp and the requirements for invoice layout printing shall be prescribed by the tax administration department under the State Council. The invoice supervision stamp shall be made by the tax authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. It is prohibited to forge the invoice supervision stamp.  
Invoices shall be subject to a system of irregular edition changes.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　印制发票的企业按照税务机关的统一规定，建立发票印制管理制度和保管措施。  
发票监制章和发票防伪专用品的使用和管理实行专人负责制度。

**Article 11** The enterprise printing invoices shall, in accordance with the uniform provisions of the tax authorities, establish an invoice printing management system and preservation measures.  
The use and management of invoice supervision stamps and invoice anti-counterfeiting special articles shall be subject to a system of specific personnel responsibility.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　印制发票的企业必须按照税务机关确定的式样和数量印制发票。

**Article 12** The enterprise printing invoices must print invoices in accordance with the patterns and quantities determined by the tax authorities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　发票应当使用中文印制。民族自治地方的发票，可以加印当地一种通用的民族文字。有实际需要的，也可以同时使用中外两种文字印制。

**Article 13** Invoices shall be printed in Chinese. Invoices in national autonomous areas may be printed with an additional commonly used local ethnic language where necessary. Where there is an actual need, invoices may also be printed in both Chinese and a foreign language.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各省、自治区、直辖市内的单位和个人使用的发票，除增值税专用发票外，应当在本省、自治区、直辖市内印制；确有必要到外省、自治区、直辖市印制的，应当由省、自治区、直辖市税务机关商印制地省、自治区、直辖市税务机关同意后确定印制发票的企业。  
禁止在境外印制发票。

**Article 14** Except for special VAT invoices, invoices used by units and individuals within each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall be printed within the said province, autonomous region, or municipality; where it is truly necessary to have invoices printed in another province, autonomous region, or municipality, the tax authority of the original province, autonomous region, or municipality shall, after consulting with and obtaining the consent of the tax authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality where the printing is to take place, determine the enterprise to print the invoices.  
Printing invoices outside China is prohibited.

### 第三章　发票的领用

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　需要领用发票的单位和个人，应当持设立登记证件或者税务登记证件，以及经办人身份证明，向主管税务机关办理发票领用手续。领用纸质发票的，还应当提供按照国务院税务主管部门规定式样制作的发票专用章的印模。主管税务机关根据领用单位和个人的经营范围、规模和风险等级，在5个工作日内确认领用发票的种类、数量以及领用方式。  
单位和个人领用发票时，应当按照税务机关的规定报告发票使用情况，税务机关应当按照规定进行查验。

**Article 15** Units and individuals that need to obtain invoices shall go through the invoice obtaining procedures with the competent tax authorities by presenting their establishment registration certificate or tax registration certificate, and the identity certificate of the handling person. For obtaining paper invoices, they shall also provide an imprint of the special invoice seal made in accordance with the pattern prescribed by the competent tax authority of the State Council. The competent tax authority shall, within 5 working days, confirm the type, quantity and method of obtaining invoices based on the business scope, scale and risk level of the unit or individual obtaining the invoices.  
When obtaining invoices, units and individuals shall report the use of invoices in accordance with the provisions of the tax authorities, and the tax authorities shall inspect the invoices in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　需要临时使用发票的单位和个人，可以凭购销商品、提供或者接受服务以及从事其他经营活动的书面证明、经办人身份证明，直接向经营地税务机关申请代开发票。依照税收法律、行政法规规定应当缴纳税款的，税务机关应当先征收税款，再开具发票。税务机关根据发票管理的需要，可以按照国务院税务主管部门的规定委托其他单位代开发票。  
禁止非法代开发票。

**Article 16** Units and individuals that need to temporarily use invoices may, by presenting written proof of the purchase or sale of goods, the provision or receipt of services, or engagement in other business activities, and the identity certificate of the handling person, directly apply to the tax authority at the place of business for the issuance of invoices on their behalf. Where taxes are payable in accordance with tax laws and administrative regulations, the tax authority shall first collect the taxes and then issue the invoices. Where necessary for invoice administration, the tax authority may, in accordance with the provisions of the competent tax authority of the State Council, entrust other units to issue invoices on its behalf.  
Illegal issuance of invoices on behalf of others is prohibited.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　临时到本省、自治区、直辖市以外从事经营活动的单位或者个人，应当凭所在地税务机关的证明，向经营地税务机关领用经营地的发票。  
临时在本省、自治区、直辖市以内跨市、县从事经营活动领用发票的办法，由省、自治区、直辖市税务机关规定。

**Article 17** Units or individuals that temporarily engage in business activities outside their own province, autonomous region, or municipality shall, by presenting a certificate from the tax authority at their place of domicile, obtain invoices from the tax authority at their place of business.  
The measures for obtaining invoices for those temporarily engaging in business activities across cities or counties within the same province, autonomous region, or municipality shall be prescribed by the tax authority of that province, autonomous region, or municipality.

### 第四章　发票的开具和保管

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　销售商品、提供服务以及从事其他经营活动的单位和个人，对外发生经营业务收取款项，收款方应当向付款方开具发票；特殊情况下，由付款方向收款方开具发票。

**Article 18** Units and individuals that sell goods, provide services, or engage in other business activities and receive payments for business transactions from outside parties shall, as the payees, issue invoices to the payors; under special circumstances, the payors shall issue invoices to the payees.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　所有单位和从事生产、经营活动的个人在购买商品、接受服务以及从事其他经营活动支付款项，应当向收款方取得发票。取得发票时，不得要求变更品名和金额。

**Article 19** All units and individuals engaged in production or business activities, when paying for the purchase of goods, receipt of services, or other business activities, shall obtain invoices from the payees. When obtaining invoices, they shall not request changes to the name or amount of the item.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　不符合规定的发票，不得作为财务报销凭证，任何单位和个人有权拒收。

**Article 20** Invoices that do not conform to the provisions shall not be used as financial reimbursement vouchers. Any unit or individual has the right to refuse to accept such invoices.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　开具发票应当按照规定的时限、顺序、栏目，全部联次一次性如实开具，开具纸质发票应当加盖发票专用章。  
任何单位和个人不得有下列虚开发票行为：  
（一）为他人、为自己开具与实际经营业务情况不符的发票；  
（二）让他人为自己开具与实际经营业务情况不符的发票；  
（三）介绍他人开具与实际经营业务情况不符的发票。

**Article 21** In issuing an invoice, all copies shall be issued truthfully at one time within the prescribed time limit, in the prescribed order and columns. For a paper invoice, the special invoice seal shall be affixed.  
No entity or individual shall commit any of the following acts of issuing false invoices:  
(1) issuing invoices that are inconsistent with the actual business operations for others or for oneself;  
(2) having others issue invoices that are inconsistent with the actual business operations for oneself;  
(3) introducing others to issue invoices that are inconsistent with the actual business operations.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　安装税控装置的单位和个人，应当按照规定使用税控装置开具发票，并按期向主管税务机关报送开具发票的数据。  
使用非税控电子器具开具发票的，应当将非税控电子器具使用的软件程序说明资料报主管税务机关备案，并按照规定保存、报送开具发票的数据。  
单位和个人开发电子发票信息系统自用或者为他人提供电子发票服务的，应当遵守国务院税务主管部门的规定。

**Article 22** Entities and individuals installing tax control devices shall use such devices to issue invoices in accordance with the provisions and shall periodically submit data on invoices issued to the competent tax authorities.  
Where non-tax-control electronic devices are used to issue invoices, the software program description materials for such devices shall be submitted to the competent tax authorities for record, and the data on invoices issued shall be preserved and submitted in accordance with the provisions.  
Entities and individuals developing electronic invoice information systems for self-use or providing electronic invoice services to others shall comply with the provisions of the tax regulatory authority under the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　任何单位和个人应当按照发票管理规定使用发票，不得有下列行为：  
（一）转借、转让、介绍他人转让发票、发票监制章和发票防伪专用品；  
（二）知道或者应当知道是私自印制、伪造、变造、非法取得或者废止的发票而受让、开具、存放、携带、邮寄、运输；  
（三）拆本使用发票；  
（四）扩大发票使用范围；  
（五）以其他凭证代替发票使用；  
（六）窃取、截留、篡改、出售、泄露发票数据。  
税务机关应当提供查询发票真伪的便捷渠道。

**Article 23** Any entity or individual shall use invoices in accordance with the invoice management provisions and shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) lending, transferring, or introducing others to transfer invoices, invoice supervision seals, or special anti-counterfeiting products for invoices;  
(2) accepting, issuing, storing, carrying, mailing, or transporting invoices that are known or should be known to be privately printed, forged, altered, illegally obtained, or abolished;  
(3) using invoices by detaching bound volumes;  
(4) expanding the scope of use of invoices;  
(5) using other documents as substitutes for invoices;  
(6) stealing, intercepting, tampering with, selling, or disclosing invoice data.  
Tax authorities shall provide convenient channels for verifying the authenticity of invoices.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　除国务院税务主管部门规定的特殊情形外，纸质发票限于领用单位和个人在本省、自治区、直辖市内开具。  
省、自治区、直辖市税务机关可以规定跨市、县开具纸质发票的办法。

**Article 24** Except for special circumstances prescribed by the tax regulatory authority under the State Council, paper invoices shall be limited to being issued by the receiving entity or individual within their province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The tax authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may prescribe measures for issuing paper invoices across cities and counties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　除国务院税务主管部门规定的特殊情形外，任何单位和个人不得跨规定的使用区域携带、邮寄、运输空白发票。  
禁止携带、邮寄或者运输空白发票出入境。

**Article 25** Except for special circumstances prescribed by the tax regulatory authority under the State Council, no entity or individual may carry, mail, or transport blank invoices across the prescribed use area.  
Carrying, mailing, or transporting blank invoices into or out of the country is prohibited.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　开具发票的单位和个人应当建立发票使用登记制度，配合税务机关进行身份验证，并定期向主管税务机关报告发票使用情况。

**Article 26** Entities and individuals issuing invoices shall establish an invoice use registration system, cooperate with tax authorities in identity verification, and periodically report the use of invoices to the competent tax authorities.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　开具发票的单位和个人应当在办理变更或者注销税务登记的同时，办理发票的变更、缴销手续。

**Article 27** Entities and individuals issuing invoices shall, at the same time as handling change or cancellation of tax registration, handle the formalities for change or cancellation of invoice registration.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　开具发票的单位和个人应当按照国家有关规定存放和保管发票，不得擅自损毁。已经开具的发票存根联，应当保存5年。

**Article 28** Entities and individuals issuing invoices shall store and preserve invoices in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and shall not damage them without authorization. The stub copies of issued invoices shall be preserved for 5 years.

### 第五章　发票的检查

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　税务机关在发票管理中有权进行下列检查：  
（一）检查印制、领用、开具、取得、保管和缴销发票的情况；  
（二）调出发票查验；  
（三）查阅、复制与发票有关的凭证、资料；  
（四）向当事各方询问与发票有关的问题和情况；  
（五）在查处发票案件时，对与案件有关的情况和资料，可以记录、录音、录像、照像和复制。

**Article 29** In invoice management, tax authorities shall have the authority to conduct the following inspections:  
(1) inspecting the printing, receiving, issuing, obtaining, preserving, and cancellation of invoices;  
(2) removing invoices for inspection;  
(3) consulting and copying certificates and materials related to invoices;  
(4) inquiring of the parties concerned about issues and circumstances related to invoices;  
(5) recording, audio-recording, video-recording, photographing, and copying the circumstances and materials related to the case when investigating and handling invoice cases.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　印制、使用发票的单位和个人，必须接受税务机关依法检查，如实反映情况，提供有关资料，不得拒绝、隐瞒。  
税务人员进行检查时，应当出示税务检查证。

**Article 30** Entities and individuals printing or using invoices must accept inspections conducted by tax authorities according to law, truthfully reflect the circumstances, and provide relevant materials, and shall not refuse or conceal.  
When tax officers conduct inspections, they shall present their tax inspection certificates.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　税务机关需要将已开具的发票调出查验时，应当向被查验的单位和个人开具发票换票证。发票换票证与所调出查验的发票有同等的效力。被调出查验发票的单位和个人不得拒绝接受。  
税务机关需要将空白发票调出查验时，应当开具收据；经查无问题的，应当及时返还。

**Article 31** When the tax authority needs to retrieve issued invoices for inspection, it shall issue an invoice exchange certificate to the unit or individual being inspected. The invoice exchange certificate shall have the same effect as the retrieved invoice for inspection. The unit or individual whose invoice is retrieved for inspection shall not refuse to accept it.  
When the tax authority needs to retrieve blank invoices for inspection, it shall issue a receipt; if no problem is found upon inspection, the invoices shall be returned promptly.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　单位和个人从中国境外取得的与纳税有关的发票或者凭证，税务机关在纳税审查时有疑义的，可以要求其提供境外公证机构或者注册会计师的确认证明，经税务机关审核认可后，方可作为记账核算的凭证。

**Article 32** Where a unit or individual obtains from outside China an invoice or voucher related to tax payment, and the tax authority has doubts during tax examination, it may require the unit or individual to provide a confirmation certificate from an overseas notarization institution or a certified public accountant. Such certificate may be used as a voucher for bookkeeping and accounting only after being verified and approved by the tax authority.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反本办法的规定，有下列情形之一的，由税务机关责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的予以没收：  
（一）应当开具而未开具发票，或者未按照规定的时限、顺序、栏目，全部联次一次性开具发票，或者未加盖发票专用章的；  
（二）使用税控装置开具发票，未按期向主管税务机关报送开具发票的数据的；  
（三）使用非税控电子器具开具发票，未将非税控电子器具使用的软件程序说明资料报主管税务机关备案，或者未按照规定保存、报送开具发票的数据的；  
（四）拆本使用发票的；  
（五）扩大发票使用范围的；  
（六）以其他凭证代替发票使用的；  
（七）跨规定区域开具发票的；  
（八）未按照规定缴销发票的；  
（九）未按照规定存放和保管发票的。

**Article 33** Where any of the following circumstances occurs in violation of these Measures, the tax authority shall order corrective action and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; any illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) failing to issue an invoice when one shall be issued, or failing to issue all copies of the invoice at one time in accordance with the prescribed time limit, sequence, and columns, or failing to affix the special invoice seal;  
(2) issuing invoices using tax control devices but failing to submit the invoice issuance data to the competent tax authority within the prescribed time limit;  
(3) issuing invoices using non-tax-control electronic devices but failing to report the software program description materials for the use of such devices to the competent tax authority for recordation, or failing to preserve and submit invoice issuance data in accordance with regulations;  
(4) using invoices by tearing them from the bound book;  
(5) expanding the scope of invoice use;  
(6) using other vouchers instead of invoices;  
(7) issuing invoices across prescribed regions;  
(8) failing to cancel and destroy invoices in accordance with regulations;  
(9) failing to store and keep invoices in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　跨规定的使用区域携带、邮寄、运输空白发票，以及携带、邮寄或者运输空白发票出入境的，由税务机关责令改正，可以处1万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处1万元以上3万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的予以没收。  
丢失发票或者擅自损毁发票的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 34** Where blank invoices are carried, mailed, or transported across prescribed usage areas, or where blank invoices are carried, mailed, or transported into or out of the country, the tax authority shall order corrective action and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan and not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where invoices are lost or intentionally damaged without authorization, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反本办法的规定虚开发票的，由税务机关没收违法所得；虚开金额在1万元以下的，可以并处5万元以下的罚款；虚开金额超过1万元的，并处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
非法代开发票的，依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 35** Where an invoice is fraudulently issued in violation of these Measures, the tax authority shall confiscate the illegal gains; if the amount fraudulently issued is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if the amount fraudulently issued exceeds 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where invoices are illegally issued on behalf of others, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　私自印制、伪造、变造发票，非法制造发票防伪专用品，伪造发票监制章，窃取、截留、篡改、出售、泄露发票数据的，由税务机关没收违法所得，没收、销毁作案工具和非法物品，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
前款规定的处罚，《中华人民共和国税收征收管理法》有规定的，依照其规定执行。

**Article 36** Where anyone privately prints, forges, or alters invoices; illegally manufactures special anti-counterfeiting products for invoices; forges invoice supervision seals; or steals, intercepts, alters, sells, or discloses invoice data, the tax authority shall confiscate illegal gains, confiscate and destroy tools and illegal articles, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan and not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection has provisions on the penalties prescribed in the preceding paragraph, those provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　有下列情形之一的，由税务机关处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处5万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的予以没收：  
（一）转借、转让、介绍他人转让发票、发票监制章和发票防伪专用品的；  
（二）知道或者应当知道是私自印制、伪造、变造、非法取得或者废止的发票而受让、开具、存放、携带、邮寄、运输的。

**Article 37** Where any of the following circumstances occurs, the tax authority shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan and not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan and not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; any illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) lending, transferring, or introducing others to transfer invoices, invoice supervision seals, or anti-counterfeiting special products for invoices;  
(2) acquiring, issuing, storing, carrying, mailing, or transporting invoices while knowing or should have known that such invoices are privately printed, forged, altered, illegally obtained, or abolished.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对违反发票管理规定2次以上或者情节严重的单位和个人，税务机关可以向社会公告。

**Article 38** Where a unit or individual violates the provisions on invoice administration two or more times or under serious circumstances, the tax authority may make a public announcement to the society.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反发票管理法规，导致其他单位或者个人未缴、少缴或者骗取税款的，由税务机关没收违法所得，可以并处未缴、少缴或者骗取的税款1倍以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where any person violates the laws and regulations on invoice administration, causing another unit or individual to fail to pay, underpay, or fraudulently obtain tax refunds, the tax authority shall confiscate the illegal gains and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than one time the amount of tax unpaid, underpaid, or fraudulently obtained.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人对税务机关的处罚决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 40** Where a party is dissatisfied with a penalty decision made by the tax authority, it may, according to law, apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative action before a People's Court.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　税务人员利用职权之便，故意刁难印制、使用发票的单位和个人，或者有违反发票管理法规行为的，依照国家有关规定给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Tax personnel who take advantage of their authority to deliberately create difficulties for entities and individuals printing or using invoices, or who violate invoice management laws and regulations, shall be given sanctions in accordance with relevant state provisions; if the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院税务主管部门可以根据有关行业特殊的经营方式和业务需求，会同国务院有关主管部门制定该行业的发票管理办法。  
国务院税务主管部门可以根据增值税专用发票管理的特殊需要，制定增值税专用发票的具体管理办法。

**Article 42** The competent tax department of the State Council may, based on the special business methods and operational needs of relevant industries, jointly with the relevant competent departments of the State Council, formulate invoice management measures for such industries.  
The competent tax department of the State Council may, based on the special needs for management of special VAT invoices, formulate specific management measures for special VAT invoices.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　本办法自发布之日起施行。财政部1986年发布的《全国发票管理暂行办法》和原国家税务局1991年发布的《关于对外商投资企业和外国企业发票管理的暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 43** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Interim Measures for Invoice Management Nationwide promulgated by the Ministry of Finance in 1986 and the Interim Provisions on Invoice Management for Enterprises with Foreign Investment and Foreign Enterprises promulgated by the former State Tax Bureau in 1991 shall be abolished simultaneously.
