# 中华人民共和国计算机信息网络国际联网管理暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-03-10  
**生效日期**：2024-05-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对计算机信息网络国际联网的管理，保障国际计算机信息交流的健康发展，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to strengthen the administration of international networking of computer information networks and to ensure the healthy development of international computer information exchange.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内的计算机信息网络进行国际联网，应当依照本规定办理。

**Article 2** For international networking of computer information networks within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the matter shall be handled in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本规定下列用语的含义是：  
（一）计算机信息网络国际联网（以下简称国际联网），是指中华人民共和国境内的计算机信息网络为实现信息的国际交流，同外国的计算机信息网络相联接。  
（二）互联网络，是指直接进行国际联网的计算机信息网络；互联单位，是指负责互联网络运行的单位。  
（三）接入网络，是指通过接入互联网络进行国际联网的计算机信息网络；接入单位，是指负责接入网络运行的单位。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Provisions, the following terms mean:  
(1) International networking of computer information networks (hereinafter referred to as international networking) means the connection of computer information networks within the territory of the People's Republic of China with foreign computer information networks for the purpose of international exchange of information.  
(2) Interconnection network means a computer information network that directly conducts international networking; interconnection unit means the unit responsible for the operation of an interconnection network.  
(3) Access network means a computer information network that conducts international networking through connection to an interconnection network; access unit means the unit responsible for the operation of an access network.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对国际联网实行统筹规划、统一标准、分级管理、促进发展的原则。

**Article 4** The State shall implement the principles of unified planning, unified standards, hierarchical administration, and promotion of development for international networking.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院信息化工作领导小组（以下简称领导小组），负责协调、解决有关国际联网工作中的重大问题。  
领导小组办公室按照本规定制定具体管理办法，明确国际出入口信道提供单位、互联单位、接入单位和用户的权利、义务和责任，并负责对国际联网工作的检查监督。

**Article 5** The Informatization Work Leading Group of the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the Leading Group) shall be responsible for coordinating and resolving major issues relating to international networking.  
The office of the Leading Group shall, in accordance with these Provisions, formulate specific administrative measures, specify the rights, obligations and responsibilities of international gateway channel providers, interconnection units, access units and users, and shall be responsible for the inspection and supervision of international networking.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　计算机信息网络直接进行国际联网，必须使用国家公用电信网提供的国际出入口信道。  
任何单位和个人不得自行建立或者使用其他信道进行国际联网。

**Article 6** For computer information networks to conduct direct international networking, they must use the international gateway channels provided by the national public telecommunications network.  
No unit or individual may establish or use other channels for international networking without authorization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　已经建立的互联网络，根据国务院有关规定调整后，分别由国务院电信主管部门、教育行政部门和中国科学院管理。  
新建互联网络，必须报经国务院批准。

**Article 7** The existing interconnection networks, after being adjusted in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council, shall be administered respectively by the telecommunications competent department of the State Council, the education administrative department, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences.  
New interconnection networks must be submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　接入网络必须通过互联网络进行国际联网。  
接入单位拟从事国际联网经营活动的，应当向有权受理从事国际联网经营活动申请的互联单位主管部门或者主管单位申请领取国际联网经营许可证；未取得国际联网经营许可证的，不得从事国际联网经营业务。  
接入单位拟从事非经营活动的，应当报经有权受理从事非经营活动申请的互联单位主管部门或者主管单位审批；未经批准的，不得接入互联网络进行国际联网。  
申请领取国际联网经营许可证或者办理审批手续时，应当提供其计算机信息网络的性质、应用范围和主机地址等资料。  
国际联网经营许可证的格式，由领导小组统一制定。

**Article 8** Access networks must conduct international networking through interconnection networks.  
An access unit that intends to engage in international networking operations shall apply to the competent department or responsible unit of the interconnection unit that is authorized to accept applications for engaging in international networking operations for an international networking operation permit; without obtaining the international networking operation permit, it shall not engage in international networking operations.  
An access unit that intends to engage in non-operational activities shall submit to the competent department or responsible unit of the interconnection unit that is authorized to accept applications for non-operational activities for examination and approval; without approval, it shall not connect to an interconnection network for international networking.  
When applying for an international networking operation permit or going through the examination and approval procedures, it shall provide information such as the nature, scope of application, and host addresses of its computer information network.  
The format of the international networking operation permit shall be uniformly formulated by the Leading Group.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事国际联网经营活动的和从事非经营活动的接入单位都必须具备下列条件：  
（一）是依法设立的企业法人或者事业法人；  
（二）具有相应的计算机信息网络、装备以及相应的技术人员和管理人员；  
（三）具有健全的安全保密管理制度和技术保护措施；  
（四）符合法律和国务院规定的其他条件。  
接入单位从事国际联网经营活动的，除必须具备本条前款规定条件外，还应当具备为用户提供长期服务的能力。  
从事国际联网经营活动的接入单位的情况发生变化，不再符合本条第一款、第二款规定条件的，其国际联网经营许可证由发证机构予以吊销；从事非经营活动的接入单位的情况发生变化，不再符合本条第一款规定条件的，其国际联网资格由审批机构予以取消。

**Article 9** All access units engaging in international networking operations and those engaging in non-operational activities must meet the following conditions:  
(1) It is an enterprise legal person or an institutional legal person established according to law;  
(2) It possesses the corresponding computer information network, equipment, and the corresponding technical and administrative personnel;  
(3) It has a sound security and confidentiality management system and technical protection measures;  
(4) It meets other conditions prescribed by law and the State Council.  
An access unit engaging in international networking operations shall, in addition to the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph of this Article, also have the capability to provide long-term services to users.  
If the circumstances of an access unit engaging in international networking operations change such that it no longer meets the conditions set out in the first and second paragraphs of this Article, its international networking operation permit shall be revoked by the issuing authority; if the circumstances of an access unit engaging in non-operational activities change such that it no longer meets the conditions set out in the first paragraph of this Article, its international networking qualification shall be cancelled by the examination and approval authority.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　个人、法人和其他组织（以下统称用户）使用的计算机或者计算机信息网络，需要进行国际联网的，必须通过接入网络进行国际联网。  
前款规定的计算机或者计算机信息网络，需要接入接入网络的，应当征得接入单位的同意，并办理登记手续。

**Article 10** Computers or computer information networks used by individuals, legal persons, and other organizations (hereinafter collectively referred to as users) that need to conduct international networking must do so through access networks.  
Computers or computer information networks as specified in the preceding paragraph that need to connect to access networks shall obtain the consent of the access unit and go through registration procedures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国际出入口信道提供单位、互联单位和接入单位，应当建立相应的网络管理中心，依照法律和国家有关规定加强对本单位及其用户的管理，做好网络信息安全管理工作，确保为用户提供良好、安全的服务。

**Article 11** International gateway channel providers, interconnection units, and access units shall establish corresponding network management centers, strengthen the administration of their own units and their users in accordance with laws and the relevant regulations of the State, perform network information security administration work, and ensure the provision of good and safe services to users.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　互联单位与接入单位，应当负责本单位及其用户有关国际联网的技术培训和管理教育工作。

**Article 12** Interconnection units and access units shall be responsible for the technical training and management education of their own units and their users relating to international networking.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　从事国际联网业务的单位和个人，应当遵守国家有关法律、行政法规，严格执行安全保密制度，不得利用国际联网从事危害国家安全、泄露国家秘密等违法犯罪活动，不得制作、查阅、复制和传播妨碍社会治安的信息和淫秽色情等信息。

**Article 13** Units and individuals engaged in international networking business shall abide by the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State, strictly implement the security and confidentiality system, and shall not use international networking to engage in illegal or criminal activities such as endangering national security or divulging State secrets, and shall not produce, browse, reproduce, or disseminate information that disrupts public order or obscene and pornographic information.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　违反[本规定第六条](#art-6)、第八条和第十条的规定的，由公安机关责令停止联网，给予警告，可以并处15000元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 14** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 6, Article 8, and Article 10 of these Provisions shall be ordered by the public security organ to stop networking, given a warning, and may also be imposed a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反本规定，同时触犯其他有关法律、行政法规的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 15** Whoever violates these Provisions and also contravenes other relevant laws and administrative regulations shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of such laws and administrative regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　与台湾、香港、澳门地区的计算机信息网络的联网，参照本规定执行。

**Article 16** For networking with computer information networks in the Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao regions, these Provisions shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 17** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
