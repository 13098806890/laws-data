# 互联网信息服务管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （2000年9月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第292号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范互联网信息服务活动，促进互联网信息服务健康有序发展，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of regulating Internet information service activities and promoting the healthy and orderly development of Internet information services.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事互联网信息服务活动，必须遵守本办法。  
本办法所称互联网信息服务，是指通过互联网向上网用户提供信息的服务活动。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in Internet information service activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China must comply with these Measures.  
Internet information service as mentioned in these Measures refers to the service activities of providing information to Internet users through the Internet.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　互联网信息服务分为经营性和非经营性两类。  
经营性互联网信息服务，是指通过互联网向上网用户有偿提供信息或者网页制作等服务活动。  
非经营性互联网信息服务，是指通过互联网向上网用户无偿提供具有公开性、共享性信息的服务活动。

**Article 3** Internet information services are classified into two categories: commercial and non-commercial.  
Commercial Internet information service refers to the service activities of providing information or webpage production to Internet users for a fee through the Internet.  
Non-commercial Internet information service refers to the service activities of providing information with openness and shareability to Internet users free of charge through the Internet.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对经营性互联网信息服务实行许可制度；对非经营性互联网信息服务实行备案制度。  
未取得许可或者未履行备案手续的，不得从事互联网信息服务。

**Article 4** The State implements a licensing system for commercial Internet information services and a filing system for non-commercial Internet information services.  
No person shall engage in Internet information services without obtaining a license or completing the filing procedures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事新闻、出版、教育等互联网信息服务，依照法律、行政法规以及国家有关规定须经有关主管部门审核同意的，在申请经营许可或者履行备案手续前，应当依法经有关主管部门审核同意。

**Article 5** Where engaging in Internet information services such as news, publishing, education, etc., which require examination and approval by the relevant competent authorities in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and relevant state provisions, the applicant shall, before applying for the business license or completing the filing procedures, obtain the examination and approval from the relevant competent authorities according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　从事经营性互联网信息服务，除应当符合《中华人民共和国电信条例》规定的要求外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有业务发展计划及相关技术方案；  
（二）有健全的网络与信息安全保障措施，包括网站安全保障措施、信息安全保密管理制度、用户信息安全管理制度；  
（三）服务项目属于[本办法第五条](#art-5)规定范围的，已取得有关主管部门同意的文件。

**Article 6** For engaging in commercial Internet information services, in addition to satisfying the requirements set forth in the Telecommunications Regulations of the People's Republic of China, the following conditions shall also be met:  
(1) having a business development plan and relevant technical scheme;  
(2) having sound network and information security protection measures, including website security protection measures, information security management system, and user information security management system;  
(3) where the service items fall within the scope as specified in Article 5 of these Measures, having obtained the documents of approval from relevant competent authorities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事经营性互联网信息服务，应当向省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构或者国务院信息产业主管部门申请办理互联网信息服务增值电信业务经营许可证（以下简称经营许可证）。  
省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构或者国务院信息产业主管部门应当自收到申请之日起60日内审查完毕，作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，颁发经营许可证；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
申请人取得经营许可证后，应当持经营许可证向企业登记机关办理登记手续。

**Article 7** For engaging in commercial Internet information services, an applicant shall apply to the telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the competent information industry department of the State Council for the Internet information service value-added telecommunications business operating license (hereinafter referred to as the operating license).  
The telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the competent information industry department of the State Council shall complete the review within 60 days from the date of receiving the application, and make a decision of approval or disapproval. If approval is granted, the operating license shall be issued; if disapproval is granted, a written notice shall be given to the applicant with reasons explained.  
After obtaining the operating license, the applicant shall, with the operating license, go through the registration formalities with the enterprise registration authority.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　从事非经营性互联网信息服务，应当向省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构或者国务院信息产业主管部门办理备案手续。办理备案时，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）主办单位和网站负责人的基本情况；  
（二）网站网址和服务项目；  
（三）服务项目属于[本办法第五条](#art-5)规定范围的，已取得有关主管部门的同意文件。  
省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构对备案材料齐全的，应当予以备案并编号。

**Article 8** For engaging in non-commercial Internet information services, an applicant shall go through the filing procedures with the telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or the competent information industry department of the State Council. When going through the filing, the following materials shall be submitted:  
(1) basic information of the sponsoring unit and the person in charge of the website;  
(2) website URL and service items;  
(3) where the service items fall within the scope as specified in Article 5 of these Measures, having obtained the documents of approval from relevant competent authorities.  
The telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, if the filing materials are complete, complete the filing and assign a filing number.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　从事互联网信息服务，拟开办电子公告服务的，应当在申请经营性互联网信息服务许可或者办理非经营性互联网信息服务备案时，按照国家有关规定提出专项申请或者专项备案。

**Article 9** Where engaging in Internet information services and intending to provide electronic bulletin board services, the applicant shall, when applying for a commercial Internet information service license or completing the non-commercial Internet information service filing, make a special application or special filing in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构和国务院信息产业主管部门应当公布取得经营许可证或者已履行备案手续的互联网信息服务提供者名单。

**Article 10** The telecommunications authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and the competent information industry department of the State Council shall publish the list of Internet information service providers that have obtained the operating license or have completed the filing procedures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　互联网信息服务提供者应当按照经许可或者备案的项目提供服务，不得超出经许可或者备案的项目提供服务。  
非经营性互联网信息服务提供者不得从事有偿服务。  
互联网信息服务提供者变更服务项目、网站网址等事项的，应当提前30日向原审核、发证或者备案机关办理变更手续。

**Article 11** An Internet information service provider shall provide services in accordance with the licensed or filed items, and shall not provide services beyond the licensed or filed items.  
Non-commercial Internet information service providers shall not engage in chargeable services.  
Where an Internet information service provider changes service items, website URL, or other matters, it shall go through the modification procedures with the original examination, licensing, or filing authority 30 days in advance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　互联网信息服务提供者应当在其网站主页的显著位置标明其经营许可证编号或者备案编号。

**Article 12** An Internet information service provider shall indicate its operating license number or filing number in a conspicuous position on its website homepage.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　互联网信息服务提供者应当向上网用户提供良好的服务，并保证所提供的信息内容合法。

**Article 13** An Internet information service provider shall provide good services to Internet users and ensure that the information content provided is lawful.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　从事新闻、出版以及电子公告等服务项目的互联网信息服务提供者，应当记录提供的信息内容及其发布时间、互联网地址或者域名；互联网接入服务提供者应当记录上网用户的上网时间、用户账号、互联网地址或者域名、主叫电话号码等信息。  
互联网信息服务提供者和互联网接入服务提供者的记录备份应当保存60日，并在国家有关机关依法查询时，予以提供。

**Article 14** Internet information service providers engaged in services such as news, publishing, and electronic bulletin boards shall record the information content provided, its release time, Internet address or domain name; Internet access service providers shall record the Internet user's online time, user account, Internet address or domain name, calling telephone number and other information.  
The record backups of Internet information service providers and Internet access service providers shall be kept for 60 days, and shall be provided when the relevant state authorities make inquiries according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　互联网信息服务提供者不得制作、复制、发布、传播含有下列内容的信息：  
（一）反对宪法所确定的基本原则的；  
（二）危害国家安全，泄露国家秘密，颠覆国家政权，破坏国家统一的；  
（三）损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
（四）煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结的；  
（五）破坏国家宗教政策，宣扬邪教和封建迷信的；  
（六）散布谣言，扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
（七）散布淫秽、色情、赌博、暴力、凶杀、恐怖或者教唆犯罪的；  
（八）侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
（九）含有法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容的。

**Article 15** No Internet information service provider shall produce, reproduce, publish, or disseminate any information containing the following content:  
(1) opposing the fundamental principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) endangering national security, leaking state secrets, subverting state power, or undermining national unity;  
(3) harming national honor and interests;  
(4) inciting ethnic hatred or discrimination, or undermining ethnic unity;  
(5) undermining state religious policies, or promoting cults and feudal superstitions;  
(6) spreading rumors, disrupting social order, or undermining social stability;  
(7) spreading obscenity, pornography, gambling, violence, murder, terror, or inciting crimes;  
(8) insulting or slandering others, or infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) containing other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　互联网信息服务提供者发现其网站传输的信息明显属于[本办法第十五条](#art-15)所列内容之一的，应当立即停止传输，保存有关记录，并向国家有关机关报告。

**Article 16** Where an Internet information service provider discovers that the information transmitted on its website obviously falls under any of the contents listed in Article 15 of these Measures, it shall immediately stop the transmission, preserve relevant records, and report to the relevant state authorities.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　经营性互联网信息服务提供者申请在境内境外上市或者同外商合资、合作，应当事先经国务院信息产业主管部门审查同意；其中，外商投资的比例应当符合有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 17** A commercial Internet information service provider that applies for listing domestically or overseas, or for joint venture or cooperation with foreign investors, shall obtain prior examination and approval from the competent information industry department of the State Council; the proportion of foreign investment shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院信息产业主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构，依法对互联网信息服务实施监督管理。  
新闻、出版、教育、卫生、药品监督管理、工商行政管理和公安、国家安全等有关主管部门，在各自职责范围内依法对互联网信息内容实施监督管理。

**Article 18** The competent information industry department of the State Council and the telecommunications authorities of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to law, exercise supervision and administration over Internet information services.  
The relevant competent authorities such as those for news, publishing, education, health, drug supervision and administration, industry and commerce administration, public security, and state security shall, within their respective functions and duties, exercise supervision and administration over Internet information content according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反本办法的规定，未取得经营许可证，擅自从事经营性互联网信息服务，或者超出许可的项目提供服务的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令限期改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，处违法所得3倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，处10万元以上100万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令关闭网站。  
违反本办法的规定，未履行备案手续，擅自从事非经营性互联网信息服务，或者超出备案的项目提供服务的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令限期改正；拒不改正的，责令关闭网站。

**Article 19** Where a person, in violation of these Measures, engages in commercial Internet information services without obtaining an operating license, or provides services beyond the licensed items, the telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order corrective action within a time limit; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the website shall be ordered to be closed down.  
Where a person, in violation of these Measures, engages in non-commercial Internet information services without completing the filing procedures, or provides services beyond the filed items, the telecommunications authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order corrective action within a time limit; if corrective action is refused, the website shall be ordered to be closed down.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　制作、复制、发布、传播[本办法第十五条](#art-15)所列内容之一的信息，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关、国家安全机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》、《计算机信息网络国际联网安全保护管理办法》等有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚；对经营性互联网信息服务提供者，并由发证机关责令停业整顿直至吊销经营许可证，通知企业登记机关；对非经营性互联网信息服务提供者，并由备案机关责令暂时关闭网站直至关闭网站。

**Article 20** Where a person produces, reproduces, publishes, or disseminates any information containing any of the contents listed in Article 15 of these Measures, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, the public security organ or the state security organ shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments, the Measures for the Security Protection and Management of Computer Information Networks and International Networking, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations; for a commercial Internet information service provider, the licensing authority shall also order suspension of business for rectification or revoke the operating license, and notify the enterprise registration authority; for a non-commercial Internet information service provider, the filing authority shall also order temporary closure of the website or closure of the website.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　未履行[本办法第十四条](#art-14)规定的义务的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者暂时关闭网站。

**Article 21** Any person who fails to fulfill the obligations prescribed in Article 14 of these Measures shall be ordered by the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the authority shall order suspension of business for rectification or temporary closure of the website.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本办法的规定，未在其网站主页上标明其经营许可证编号或者备案编号的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 22** Any person who violates the provisions of these Measures by failing to indicate the business license number or record-filing number on the homepage of its website shall be ordered by the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to make corrections and shall be imposed a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反[本办法第十六条](#art-16)规定的义务的，由省、自治区、直辖市电信管理机构责令改正；情节严重的，对经营性互联网信息服务提供者，并由发证机关吊销经营许可证，对非经营性互联网信息服务提供者，并由备案机关责令关闭网站。

**Article 23** Any person who violates the obligations prescribed in Article 16 of these Measures shall be ordered by the telecommunications administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, for a commercial Internet information service provider, the license-issuing authority shall also revoke the business license, and for a non-commercial Internet information service provider, the record-filing authority shall order closure of the website.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　互联网信息服务提供者在其业务活动中，违反其他法律、法规的，由新闻、出版、教育、卫生、药品监督管理和工商行政管理等有关主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处罚。

**Article 24** Where an Internet information service provider, in its business activities, violates other laws or regulations, the relevant competent authorities such as those for press and publication, education, health, drug supervision and administration, and industry and commerce administration shall impose penalties in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　电信管理机构和其他有关主管部门及其工作人员，玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，疏于对互联网信息服务的监督管理，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级、撤职直至开除的行政处分。

**Article 25** Where any telecommunications administration authority or other relevant competent authority and its staff members neglect their duties, abuse their powers, engage in malpractice for personal gain, or are negligent in the supervision and administration of Internet information services, causing serious consequences, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given administrative sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or even expulsion according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在本办法公布前从事互联网信息服务的，应当自本办法公布之日起60日内依照本办法的有关规定补办有关手续。

**Article 26** Any person who engaged in Internet information services prior to the promulgation of these Measures shall, within 60 days from the date of promulgation of these Measures, go through the relevant procedures retroactively in accordance with the relevant provisions of these Measures.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本办法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 27** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
