# 广播电台电视台播放录音制品支付报酬暂行办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：2010-01-01

> (2009年11月10日中华人民共和国国务院令第566号公布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订)

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障著作权人依法行使广播权，方便广播电台、电视台播放录音制品，根据《中华人民共和国著作权法》(以下称著作权法)第四十四条的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Copyright Law) for the purpose of protecting copyright owners' exercise of the right of broadcasting according to law and facilitating radio stations and television stations to broadcast sound recordings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　广播电台、电视台可以就播放已经发表的音乐作品向著作权人支付报酬的方式、数额等有关事项与管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织进行约定。  
广播电台、电视台播放已经出版的录音制品，已经与著作权人订立许可使用合同的，按照合同约定的方式和标准支付报酬。  
广播电台、电视台依照[著作权法第四十四条](../../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国著作权法.md#art-44)的规定，未经著作权人的许可播放已经出版的录音制品(以下称播放录音制品)的，依照本办法向著作权人支付报酬。

**Article 2** A radio station or television station may, regarding matters such as the methods and amounts of payment of remuneration to copyright owners for broadcasting published musical works, reach an agreement with the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights.  
Where a radio station or television station has concluded a license contract with the copyright owner for broadcasting published sound recordings, it shall pay remuneration in accordance with the methods and standards agreed upon in the contract.  
Where a radio station or television station, in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of the Copyright Law, broadcasts published sound recordings without obtaining the permission of the copyright owner (hereinafter referred to as broadcasting sound recordings), it shall pay remuneration to the copyright owner in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本办法所称播放，是指广播电台、电视台以无线或者有线的方式进行的首播、重播和转播。

**Article 3** For the purpose of these Measures, 'broadcasting' means first broadcast, rebroadcast, and retransmission made by a radio station or television station by wireless or wired means.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　广播电台、电视台播放录音制品，可以与管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织约定每年向著作权人支付固定数额的报酬；没有就固定数额进行约定或者约定不成的，广播电台、电视台与管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织可以以下列方式之一为基础，协商向著作权人支付报酬：  
(一)以本台或者本台各频道(频率)本年度广告收入扣除15%成本费用后的余额，乘以[本办法第五条](#art-5)或者第六条规定的付酬标准，计算支付报酬的数额；  
(二)以本台本年度播放录音制品的时间总量，乘以[本办法第七条](#art-7)规定的单位时间付酬标准，计算支付报酬的数额。

**Article 4** Where a radio station or television station broadcasts sound recordings, it may agree with the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights on a fixed amount of remuneration to be paid to the copyright owner each year; where no agreement on a fixed amount is reached or the agreement fails, the radio station or television station and the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights may negotiate the payment of remuneration to the copyright owner on the basis of one of the following methods:  
(1) Calculating the amount of remuneration payable by multiplying the balance of the current year's advertising revenue of such station or each of its channels (frequencies) after deducting 15% of the cost expenses by the remuneration standard specified in Article 5 or Article 6 of these Measures;  
(2) Calculating the amount of remuneration payable by multiplying the total duration of broadcasting sound recordings by such station in the current year by the unit time remuneration standard specified in Article 7 of these Measures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　以[本办法第四条](#art-4)第(一)项规定方式确定向著作权人支付报酬的数额的，自本办法施行之日起5年内，按照下列付酬标准协商支付报酬的数额：  
(一)播放录音制品的时间占本台或者本频道(频率)播放节目总时间的比例(以下称播放时间比例)不足1%的，付酬标准为0.01%；  
(二)播放时间比例为1%以上不足3%的，付酬标准为0.02%；  
(三)播放时间比例为3%以上不足6%的，相应的付酬标准为0.09%到0.15%，播放时间比例每增加1%，付酬标准相应增加0.03%；  
(四)播放时间比例为6%以上10%以下的，相应的付酬标准为0.24%到0.4%，播放时间比例每增加1%，付酬标准相应增加0.04%；  
(五)播放时间比例超过10%不足30%的，付酬标准为0.5%；  
(六)播放时间比例为30%以上不足50%的，付酬标准为0.6%；  
(七)播放时间比例为50%以上不足80%的，付酬标准为0.7%；  
(八)播放时间比例为80%以上的，付酬标准为0.8%。

**Article 5** Where the amount of remuneration payable to the copyright owner is determined by the method specified in subparagraph (1) of Article 4 of these Measures, within five years from the date on which these Measures come into force, the amount of remuneration payable shall be negotiated in accordance with the following remuneration standards:  
(1) Where the proportion of the time for broadcasting sound recordings to the total time of programs broadcast by such station or such channel (frequency) (hereinafter referred to as broadcasting time proportion) is less than 1%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.01%;  
(2) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 1% or more but less than 3%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.02%;  
(3) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 3% or more but less than 6%, the corresponding remuneration standard shall be from 0.09% to 0.15%, and for each additional 1% increase in the broadcasting time proportion, the remuneration standard shall increase by 0.03% correspondingly;  
(4) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 6% or more but not more than 10%, the corresponding remuneration standard shall be from 0.24% to 0.4%, and for each additional 1% increase in the broadcasting time proportion, the remuneration standard shall increase by 0.04% correspondingly;  
(5) Where the broadcasting time proportion exceeds 10% but is less than 30%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.5%;  
(6) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 30% or more but less than 50%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.6%;  
(7) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 50% or more but less than 80%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.7%;  
(8) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 80% or more, the remuneration standard shall be 0.8%.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　以[本办法第四条](#art-4)第(一)项规定方式确定向著作权人支付报酬的数额的，自本办法施行届满5年之日起，按照下列付酬标准协商支付报酬的数额：  
(一)播放时间比例不足1%的，付酬标准为0.02%；  
(二)播放时间比例为1%以上不足3%的，付酬标准为0.03%；  
(三)播放时间比例为3%以上不足6%的，相应的付酬标准为0.12%到0.2%，播放时间比例每增加1%，付酬标准相应增加0.04%；  
(四)播放时间比例为6%以上10%以下的，相应的付酬标准为0.3%到0.5%，播放时间比例每增加1%，付酬标准相应增加0.05%；  
(五)播放时间比例超过10%不足30%的，付酬标准为0.6%；  
(六)播放时间比例为30%以上不足50%的，付酬标准为0.7%；  
(七)播放时间比例为50%以上不足80%的，付酬标准为0.8%；  
(八)播放时间比例为80%以上的，付酬标准为0.9%。

**Article 6** Where the amount of remuneration payable to the copyright owner is determined by the method specified in subparagraph (1) of Article 4 of these Measures, from the date on which five years have elapsed since these Measures came into force, the amount of remuneration payable shall be negotiated in accordance with the following remuneration standards:  
(1) Where the broadcasting time proportion is less than 1%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.02%;  
(2) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 1% or more but less than 3%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.03%;  
(3) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 3% or more but less than 6%, the corresponding remuneration standard shall be from 0.12% to 0.2%, and for each additional 1% increase in the broadcasting time proportion, the remuneration standard shall increase by 0.04% correspondingly;  
(4) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 6% or more but not more than 10%, the corresponding remuneration standard shall be from 0.3% to 0.5%, and for each additional 1% increase in the broadcasting time proportion, the remuneration standard shall increase by 0.05% correspondingly;  
(5) Where the broadcasting time proportion exceeds 10% but is less than 30%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.6%;  
(6) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 30% or more but less than 50%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.7%;  
(7) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 50% or more but less than 80%, the remuneration standard shall be 0.8%;  
(8) Where the broadcasting time proportion is 80% or more, the remuneration standard shall be 0.9%.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　以[本办法第四条](#art-4)第(二)项规定的方式确定向著作权人支付报酬的数额的，按照下列付酬标准协商支付报酬的数额：  
(一)广播电台的单位时间付酬标准为每分钟0.30元；  
(二)电视台的单位时间付酬标准自本办法施行之日起5年内为每分钟1.50元，自本办法施行届满5年之日起为每分钟2元。

**Article 7** Where the amount of remuneration payable to the copyright owner is determined by the method specified in subparagraph (2) of Article 4 of these Measures, the amount of remuneration payable shall be negotiated in accordance with the following remuneration standards:  
(1) The unit time remuneration standard for a radio station shall be RMB 0.30 per minute;  
(2) The unit time remuneration standard for a television station shall be RMB 1.50 per minute within five years from the date on which these Measures come into force, and shall be RMB 2 per minute from the date on which five years have elapsed since these Measures came into force.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　广播电台、电视台播放录音制品，未能依照[本办法第四条](#art-4)的规定与管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织约定支付报酬的固定数额，也未能协商确定应支付报酬的，应当依照[本办法第四条](#art-4)第(一)项规定的方式和第五条、第六条规定的标准，确定向管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织支付报酬的数额。

**Article 8** Where a radio station or television station broadcasts sound recordings and fails to agree with the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights on a fixed amount of remuneration for payment in accordance with Article 4 of these Measures, and also fails to negotiate and determine the remuneration payable, it shall determine the amount of remuneration payable to the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights in accordance with the method specified in subparagraph (1) of Article 4 of these Measures and the standards specified in Articles 5 and 6.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　广播电台、电视台转播其他广播电台、电视台播放的录音制品的，其播放录音制品的时间按照实际播放时间的10%计算。

**Article 9** Where a radio station or television station retransmits sound recordings broadcast by another radio station or television station, the time for broadcasting sound recordings shall be calculated at 10% of the actual broadcasting time.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中部地区的广播电台、电视台依照本办法规定方式向著作权人支付报酬的数额，自本办法施行之日起5年内，按照依据本办法规定计算出的数额的50%计算。  
西部地区的广播电台、电视台以及全国专门对少年儿童、少数民族和农村地区等播出的专业频道(频率)，依照本办法规定方式向著作权人支付报酬的数额，自本办法施行之日起5年内，按照依据本办法规定计算出的数额的10%计算；自本办法施行届满5年之日起，按照依据本办法规定计算出的数额的50%计算。

**Article 10** The amount of remuneration paid by a radio station or television station in central regions to copyright owners in the manner provided for in these Measures shall, within five years from the date on which these Measures come into force, be calculated at 50% of the amount calculated in accordance with these Measures.  
The amount of remuneration paid by a radio station or television station in western regions and by national specialized channels (frequencies) broadcasting specifically to children, ethnic minorities, and rural areas, etc., to copyright owners in the manner provided for in these Measures shall, within five years from the date on which these Measures come into force, be calculated at 10% of the amount calculated in accordance with these Measures; from the date on which five years have elapsed since these Measures came into force, it shall be calculated at 50% of the amount calculated in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府财政部门将本级人民政府设立的广播电台、电视台播放录音制品向著作权人支付报酬的支出作为核定其收支的因素，根据本地区财政情况综合考虑，统筹安排。

**Article 11** The finance department of a people's government at or above the county level shall take the expenditure of a radio station or television station established by the people's government at the corresponding level for paying remuneration to copyright owners for broadcasting sound recordings as a factor in verifying its revenue and expenditure, and shall make comprehensive consideration and overall arrangement in light of the local financial situation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　广播电台、电视台向著作权人支付报酬，以年度为结算期。  
广播电台、电视台应当于每年度第一季度将其上年度应当支付的报酬交由著作权集体管理组织转付给著作权人。  
广播电台、电视台通过著作权集体管理组织向著作权人支付报酬时，应当提供其播放作品的名称、著作权人姓名或者名称、播放时间等情况，双方已有约定的除外。

**Article 12** A radio station or television station shall pay remuneration to copyright owners on an annual settlement basis.  
A radio station or television station shall, in the first quarter of each year, deliver the remuneration payable for the preceding year to the copyright collective administration organization for onward payment to copyright owners.  
Where a radio station or television station pays remuneration to a copyright owner through a copyright collective administration organization, it shall provide information such as the title of the work broadcast, the name of the copyright owner, and the broadcasting time, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　广播电台、电视台播放录音制品，未向管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织会员以外的著作权人支付报酬的，应当按照[本办法第十二条](#art-12)的规定将应支付的报酬送交管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织；管理相关权利的著作权集体管理组织应当向著作权人转付。

**Article 13** Where a radio station or television station broadcasts sound recordings but fails to pay remuneration to copyright owners other than those who are members of the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of these Measures, deliver the remuneration payable to the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights; and the copyright collective administration organization that administers the relevant rights shall forward the remuneration to the copyright owners.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　著作权集体管理组织向著作权人转付报酬，除本办法已有规定外，适用《著作权集体管理条例》的有关规定。

**Article 14** The forwarding of remuneration by a copyright collective administration organization to copyright owners shall, except as otherwise provided in these Measures, be governed by the relevant provisions of the Regulations on Collective Administration of Copyright.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　广播电台、电视台依照本办法规定将应当向著作权人支付的报酬交给著作权集体管理组织后，对著作权集体管理组织与著作权人之间的纠纷不承担责任。

**Article 15** After a radio station or television station has delivered the remuneration payable to the copyright owner to the copyright collective administration organization in accordance with these Measures, it shall not be liable for any dispute between the copyright collective administration organization and the copyright owner.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　广播电台、电视台与著作权人或者著作权集体管理组织因依照本办法规定支付报酬产生纠纷的，可以依法向人民法院提起民事诉讼，或者根据双方达成的书面仲裁协议向仲裁机构申请仲裁。

**Article 16** Where a dispute arises between a radio station or television station and a copyright owner or a copyright collective administration organization concerning the payment of remuneration in accordance with these Measures, the parties may, according to law, initiate a civil action in the People's Court, or apply for arbitration to an arbitration institution in accordance with a written arbitration agreement reached between them.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本办法自2010年1月1日起施行。

**Article 17** These Measures shall come into force on January 1, 2010.
