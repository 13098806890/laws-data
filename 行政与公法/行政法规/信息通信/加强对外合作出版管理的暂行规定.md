# 加强对外合作出版管理的暂行规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1981-10-12

> (1981年10月12日国务院批准) 为了加强对外合作出版工作的领导和管理，特制订本规定。 一、对外合作出版，必须符合我国的外交政策和对外方针，有利于加强对外宣传和文化交流；不得有损于我主权和国家利益；并严格遵守国家有关保密的规定。 二、对外合作出版，应注意了解国际图书市场的情况，维护我国作者和出版者的权益，在平等互利的基础上，在经济上有所收益。 三、确定对外合作项目，要根据自己的条件与特点，符合本社的出书方针和出书范围。 四、对外合作出版项目，应经双方充分协商确定。合作书刊的编辑方针、书稿内容以及最后定稿，均须经我方同意。凡经我方审定的书稿，未经我方同意，对方不得擅自增删或作其他改动。合作出版介绍我国情况的摄影画册或其他书刊中选用的照片，一般应由我方提供，不宜采取由对方派人拍摄或双方合拍的方式。 五、合作出版供我国读者使用的外国编辑的书稿，应是我国所急需的，必要时应根据我国的情况对其内容进行增删或改编，在经济上尽量做到不用外汇支付对方应得的收益。 六、对外合作出版，只能由国家正式批准的出版社进行。任何非出版单位和个人，均不得同国外进行合作出版。未经原出版社同意，任何出版社不得同国外合作出版其他出版社出版的书刊。出版社拟同国外合作出版的书稿，应事先征得原作者或原编辑单位的同意。 七、出版社可以直接对外洽谈合作出版业务，也可以委托国家批准的经营对外合作出版业务的专业公司或国外可靠的代理商进行。 八、同我进行合作出版的单位，应是对我友好并有可靠的信誉和资金，不要同情况不明的外商签订合同。 九、为了明确双方的权利和义务，每个合作项目都应签订有一定时限的合同。对于版权所有，一方转让或准许另一方使用的出版发行权和其他权利，支付报酬的数量、方式和时间，均应在合同中逐项加以规定。凡我方提供的书稿或图片，未经我方同意，对方不得转让版权，不得扩大使用权，不得扩大发行区域。 十、确定对外合作出版项目，应分别不同情况履行审批手续： 凡国内已公开出版的出版物，中央一级出版社由出版社自行决定，报上级主管部门备案，地方出版社报省、市、自治区出版局审批； 凡国内尚未出版的出版物，中央一级出版社报上级主管部门审批，地方出版社报省、市、自治区人民政府审批。内容涉及到全国的大型丛书，应事先征得国家出版局的同意； 凡涉及党和国家重大方针和外交政策的书稿，党和国家领导人的著作或传记，须经国家出版局会签后，报国务院或中央审批；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、对外合作出版，必须符合我国的外交政策和对外方针，有利于加强对外宣传和文化交流；不得有损于我主权和国家利益；并严格遵守国家有关保密的规定。

**Article 1** I. Cooperative publication with foreign parties must be in conformity with China's foreign policy and diplomatic guidelines, conducive to strengthening external propaganda and cultural exchange; shall not impair China's sovereignty and national interests; and must strictly comply with the state provisions on confidentiality.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、对外合作出版，应注意了解国际图书市场的情况，维护我国作者和出版者的权益，在平等互利的基础上，在经济上有所收益。

**Article 2** II. In cooperative publication with foreign parties, attention shall be paid to understanding the international book market, safeguarding the rights and interests of Chinese authors and publishers, and achieving economic gains on the basis of equality and mutual benefit.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、确定对外合作项目，要根据自己的条件与特点，符合本社的出书方针和出书范围。

**Article 3** III. The determination of foreign cooperative projects shall be based on one's own conditions and characteristics, and shall conform to the publishing policy and scope of the publishing house.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、对外合作出版项目，应经双方充分协商确定。合作书刊的编辑方针、书稿内容以及最后定稿，均须经我方同意。凡经我方审定的书稿，未经我方同意，对方不得擅自增删或作其他改动。合作出版介绍我国情况的摄影画册或其他书刊中选用的照片，一般应由我方提供，不宜采取由对方派人拍摄或双方合拍的方式。

**Article 4** IV. Foreign cooperative publication projects shall be determined through full consultation between both parties. The editorial policy, content of the manuscript, and the final version of the cooperative publication shall all be subject to the consent of our side. No manuscript that has been reviewed and approved by our side may be altered, added to, or deleted by the other party without our consent. Photographs used in photographic albums or other publications introducing China's situation under cooperative publication shall generally be provided by our side, and it shall be inappropriate to adopt the method of sending personnel from the other party to take photographs or joint photography.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、合作出版供我国读者使用的外国编辑的书稿，应是我国所急需的，必要时应根据我国的情况对其内容进行增删或改编，在经济上尽量做到不用外汇支付对方应得的收益。

**Article 5** V. Manuscripts edited by foreign parties for cooperative publication for use by Chinese readers shall be those urgently needed by China, and where necessary, the content thereof shall be added to, deleted from, or adapted in accordance with China's circumstances, and economically, efforts shall be made to avoid using foreign exchange to pay the remuneration due to the other party.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、对外合作出版，只能由国家正式批准的出版社进行。任何非出版单位和个人，均不得同国外进行合作出版。未经原出版社同意，任何出版社不得同国外合作出版其他出版社出版的书刊。出版社拟同国外合作出版的书稿，应事先征得原作者或原编辑单位的同意。

**Article 6** VI. Cooperative publication with foreign parties may only be carried out by publishing houses formally approved by the state. No non-publishing entity or individual may engage in cooperative publication with foreign parties. Without the consent of the original publishing house, no publishing house may cooperate with foreign parties to publish books or periodicals published by other publishing houses. For manuscripts that a publishing house intends to publish in cooperation with foreign parties, the consent of the original author or the original editorial unit shall be obtained in advance.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、出版社可以直接对外洽谈合作出版业务，也可以委托国家批准的经营对外合作出版业务的专业公司或国外可靠的代理商进行。

**Article 7** VII. Publishing houses may directly negotiate cooperative publication business with foreign parties, or may entrust specialized companies approved by the state to engage in foreign cooperative publication business, or reliable foreign agents, to carry out such business.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、同我进行合作出版的单位，应是对我友好并有可靠的信誉和资金，不要同情况不明的外商签订合同。

**Article 8** VIII. The entities engaging in cooperative publication with our side shall be friendly to us and have reliable reputation and funds, and contracts shall not be signed with foreign businessmen whose circumstances are unclear.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、为了明确双方的权利和义务，每个合作项目都应签订有一定时限的合同。对于版权所有，一方转让或准许另一方使用的出版发行权和其他权利，支付报酬的数量、方式和时间，均应在合同中逐项加以规定。凡我方提供的书稿或图片，未经我方同意，对方不得转让版权，不得扩大使用权，不得扩大发行区域。

**Article 9** IX. To clarify the rights and obligations of both parties, a contract with a definite term shall be signed for each cooperative project. The ownership of copyright, the transfer or licensing of the right to publish and distribute and other rights by one party to the other, and the amount, method, and time of payment of remuneration shall all be specified item by item in the contract. No manuscript or picture provided by our side may be transferred in copyright, have the right of use expanded, or have the distribution territory expanded by the other party without our consent.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、确定对外合作出版项目，应分别不同情况履行审批手续：  
凡国内已公开出版的出版物，中央一级出版社由出版社自行决定，报上级主管部门备案，地方出版社报省、市、自治区出版局审批；  
凡国内尚未出版的出版物，中央一级出版社报上级主管部门审批，地方出版社报省、市、自治区人民政府审批。内容涉及到全国的大型丛书，应事先征得国家出版局的同意；  
凡涉及党和国家重大方针和外交政策的书稿，党和国家领导人的著作或传记，须经国家出版局会签后，报国务院或中央审批；  
重要文物和其他珍品的摄影和使用，涉及国界线的地图的出版，应严格按照国家有关规定办理。  
所有对外合作出版的送审报告、中外文合同(或协议书)、样本，均应报国家出版局备案。

**Article 10** X. The determination of foreign cooperative publication projects shall go through the examination and approval formalities according to different circumstances:  
For publications already publicly published in China, central-level publishing houses may decide on their own and report to the competent higher authority for record, while local publishing houses shall report to the publishing bureau of the province, municipality, or autonomous region for examination and approval;  
For publications not yet publicly published in China, central-level publishing houses shall report to the competent higher authority for examination and approval, while local publishing houses shall report to the people's government of the province, municipality, or autonomous region for examination and approval. Where the content involves a large series of national scope, the consent of the National Publishing Administration shall be obtained in advance;  
For manuscripts involving major policies of the Party and the state and foreign policies, or the works or biographies of Party and state leaders, they shall, after countersignature by the National Publishing Administration, be reported to the State Council or the Central Committee for examination and approval;  
The photographing and use of important cultural relics and other treasures, and the publication of maps involving national boundaries shall be handled in strict accordance with the relevant state provisions.  
All reports submitted for examination, Chinese and foreign language contracts (or agreements), and sample copies for foreign cooperative publication shall be reported to the National Publishing Administration for record.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、对于开展对外合作出版所必需的业务人员同国外的往来，主管部门应简化审批手续，提供方便，以利于合同的顺利执行。

**Article 11** XI. For the necessary exchanges between business personnel and foreign parties in carrying out foreign cooperative publication, the competent authorities shall simplify the examination and approval formalities and provide convenience to facilitate the smooth implementation of contracts.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、合作出版书刊，应按国内现行稿酬制度，分别情况向作者支付报酬；  
首次出版的书稿，可按略高于国内稿酬标准支付报酬；  
使用国内已出版过的书稿，亦可支付适当的报酬，一般不超过原基本稿酬的百分之六十；  
使用外国人的作品，可按略高于国内作者稿酬支付。  
上述报酬，均以人民币支付，个别确属特别情况必须支付外汇者，经上级主管部门批准后，在本单位所得外汇中支付。

**Article 12** XII. In cooperative publication of books and periodicals, remuneration shall be paid to authors according to the current domestic remuneration system, depending on the circumstances:  
For manuscripts published for the first time, remuneration may be paid at a rate slightly higher than the domestic standard;  
For manuscripts already published domestically, appropriate remuneration may also be paid, generally not exceeding 60% of the original basic remuneration;  
For works by foreigners, remuneration may be paid at a rate slightly higher than that for domestic authors.  
All the above remuneration shall be paid in Renminbi, and where, in special circumstances, payment in foreign exchange is necessary, such payment shall, after approval by the competent higher authority, be made from the foreign exchange income of the unit.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、在对外合作出版工作中，各出版社之间要发扬社会主义协作风格，注意互通情报，对外协调一致，遇有分歧和矛盾，应内部协商或报上级机关协调解决。

**Article 13** XIII. In the work of foreign cooperative publication, publishing houses shall promote the style of socialist cooperation, pay attention to the exchange of information, maintain external coordination, and in case of differences and conflicts, they shall conduct internal consultation or report to the higher authority for coordination and resolution.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、在对外交往中，要严格遵守外事纪律，维护民族尊严和国家声誉。

**Article 14** XIV. In foreign exchanges, the foreign affairs discipline shall be strictly observed, and national dignity and state reputation shall be safeguarded.
