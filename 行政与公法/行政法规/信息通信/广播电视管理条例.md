# 广播电视管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强广播电视管理，发展广播电视事业，促进社会主义精神文明和物质文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of radio and television, developing the radio and television cause, and promoting the construction of socialist spiritual civilization and material civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于在中华人民共和国境内设立广播电台、电视台和采编、制作、播放、传输广播电视节目等活动。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to activities such as the establishment of radio stations and television stations, and the gathering, editing, producing, broadcasting and transmitting of radio and television programs within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　广播电视事业应当坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向，坚持正确的舆论导向。

**Article 3** The radio and television cause shall adhere to the orientation of serving the people and serving socialism, and shall adhere to correct guidance of public opinion.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家发展广播电视事业。县级以上人民政府应当将广播电视事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，并根据需要和财力逐步增加投入，提高广播电视覆盖率。  
国家支持农村广播电视事业的发展。  
国家扶持民族自治地方和边远贫困地区发展广播电视事业。

**Article 4** The State shall develop the radio and television cause. The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the radio and television cause into the national economic and social development plan, and gradually increase investment according to needs and financial resources, so as to raise the coverage rate of radio and television.  
The State shall support the development of the radio and television cause in rural areas.  
The State shall assist the development of the radio and television cause in national autonomous areas and remote and poverty-stricken areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院广播电视行政部门负责全国的广播电视管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责广播电视行政管理工作的部门或者机构（以下统称广播电视行政部门）负责本行政区域内的广播电视管理工作。

**Article 5** The administrative department of radio and television under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of radio and television throughout the country.  
The departments or organs in charge of the administration of radio and television under the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter collectively referred to as the administrative departments of radio and television) shall be responsible for the administration of radio and television within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国性广播电视行业的社会团体按照其章程，实行自律管理，并在国务院广播电视行政部门的指导下开展活动。

**Article 6** Social organizations of the nationwide radio and television industry shall, in accordance with their articles of association, practice self-discipline management, and shall carry out activities under the guidance of the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对为广播电视事业发展做出显著贡献的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** The State shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of the radio and television cause.

### 第二章　广播电台和电视台

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院广播电视行政部门负责制定全国广播电台、电视台的设立规划，确定广播电台、电视台的总量、布局和结构。  
本条例所称广播电台、电视台是指采编、制作并通过有线或者无线的方式播放广播电视节目的机构。

**Article 8** The administrative department of radio and television under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating the planning for the establishment of radio stations and television stations nationwide, and for determining the total number, layout and structure of radio stations and television stations.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "radio stations and television stations" means institutions that gather, edit and produce radio and television programs and broadcast them through wired or wireless means.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设立广播电台、电视台，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有符合国家规定的广播电视专业人员；  
（二）有符合国家规定的广播电视技术设备；  
（三）有必要的基本建设资金和稳定的资金保障；  
（四）有必要的场所。  
审批设立广播电台、电视台，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当符合国家的广播电视建设规划和技术发展规划。

**Article 9** To establish a radio station or television station, the following conditions shall be satisfied:  
(1) having radio and television professionals meeting the requirements prescribed by the State;  
(2) having radio and television technical equipment meeting the requirements prescribed by the State;  
(3) having necessary capital for capital construction and a stable financial guarantee; and  
(4) having necessary premises.  
In examining and approving the establishment of a radio station or television station, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the establishment shall also conform to the national radio and television construction plan and technical development plan.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　广播电台、电视台由县、不设区的市以上人民政府广播电视行政部门设立，其中教育电视台可以由设区的市、自治州以上人民政府教育行政部门设立。其他任何单位和个人不得设立广播电台、电视台。  
国家禁止设立外商投资的广播电台、电视台。

**Article 10** Radio stations and television stations shall be established by the administrative departments of radio and television of the people's governments at or above the county level or the city not divided into districts. Among them, educational television stations may be established by the administrative departments of education of the people's governments at or above the city divided into districts or the autonomous prefecture level. No other unit or individual shall establish a radio station or television station.  
The State prohibits the establishment of radio stations or television stations with foreign investment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　中央的广播电台、电视台由国务院广播电视行政部门设立。地方设立广播电台、电视台的，由县、不设区的市以上地方人民政府广播电视行政部门提出申请，本级人民政府审查同意后，逐级上报，经国务院广播电视行政部门审查批准后，方可筹建。  
中央的教育电视台由国务院教育行政部门设立，报国务院广播电视行政部门审查批准。地方设立教育电视台的，由设区的市、自治州以上地方人民政府教育行政部门提出申请，征得同级广播电视行政部门同意并经本级人民政府审查同意后，逐级上报，经国务院教育行政部门审核，由国务院广播电视行政部门审查批准后，方可筹建。

**Article 11** Central radio stations and television stations shall be established by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council. Where a local radio station or television station is to be established, the local administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level or the city not divided into districts shall file an application, and after the people's government at the same level examines and approves the application, it shall be reported level by level, and may be prepared for construction only after examination and approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.  
The central educational television station shall be established by the administrative department of education under the State Council, and shall be reported to the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council for examination and approval. Where a local educational television station is to be established, the local administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the city divided into districts or the autonomous prefecture level shall file an application, and after obtaining the consent of the administrative department of radio and television at the same level and the examination and approval of the people's government at the same level, it shall be reported level by level, and after examination and verification by the administrative department of education under the State Council, and examination and approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council, the station may be prepared for construction.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　经批准筹建的广播电台、电视台，应当按照国家规定的建设程序和广播电视技术标准进行工程建设。  
建成的广播电台、电视台，经国务院广播电视行政部门审查符合条件的，发给广播电台、电视台许可证。广播电台、电视台应当按照许可证载明的台名、台标、节目设置范围和节目套数等事项制作、播放节目。

**Article 12** Radio stations and television stations approved for preparation for construction shall carry out engineering construction in accordance with the construction procedures and radio and television technical standards prescribed by the State.  
Where a radio station or television station that has been completed, after being examined by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council, meets the requirements, a radio station or television station license shall be issued. A radio station or television station shall produce and broadcast programs in accordance with the matters specified in the license, such as the station name, station logo, scope of program offerings and number of program channels.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设区的市、自治州以上人民政府广播电视行政部门设立的广播电台、电视台或者设区的市、自治州以上人民政府教育行政部门设立的电视台变更台名、节目设置范围或者节目套数，省级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门设立的广播电台、电视台或者省级以上人民政府教育行政部门设立的电视台变更台标的，应当经国务院广播电视行政部门批准。县、不设区的市人民政府广播电视行政部门设立的广播电台、电视台变更台名、节目设置范围或者节目套数的，应当经省级人民政府广播电视行政部门批准。  
广播电台、电视台不得出租、转让播出时段。

**Article 13** Where a radio station or television station established by the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the city divided into districts or the autonomous prefecture level, or a television station established by the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the city divided into districts or the autonomous prefecture level, changes its station name, scope of program offerings or number of program channels, or where a radio station or television station established by the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the provincial level, or a television station established by the administrative department of education of the people's government at or above the provincial level, changes its station logo, the matter shall be subject to approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council. Where a radio station or television station established by the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at the county level or the city not divided into districts changes its station name, scope of program offerings or number of program channels, the matter shall be subject to approval by the administrative department of radio and television of the provincial people's government.  
No radio station or television station may lease or transfer broadcast time slots.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　广播电台、电视台终止，应当按照原审批程序申报，其许可证由国务院广播电视行政部门收回。  
广播电台、电视台因特殊情况需要暂时停止播出的，应当经省级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门同意；未经批准，连续停止播出超过30日的，视为终止，应当依照前款规定办理有关手续。

**Article 14** Where a radio station or television station is to be terminated, an application shall be submitted in accordance with the original examination and approval procedures, and its license shall be withdrawn by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.  
Where a radio station or television station needs to temporarily suspend broadcasting due to special circumstances, it shall obtain the consent of the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the provincial level; if, without approval, it suspends broadcasting for more than 30 consecutive days, it shall be deemed as terminated, and the relevant formalities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　乡、镇设立广播电视站的，由所在地县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门负责审核，并按照国务院广播电视行政部门的有关规定审批。  
机关、部队、团体、企业事业单位设立有线广播电视站的，按照国务院有关规定审批。

**Article 15** Where a radio and television station is to be established in a township or town, the administrative department of radio and television of the local people's government at or above the county level shall be responsible for examination, and shall examine and approve it in accordance with the relevant provisions of the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.  
Where an organ, army unit, organization, enterprise or institution establishes a cable radio and television station, it shall be subject to examination and approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　任何单位和个人不得冲击广播电台、电视台，不得损坏广播电台、电视台的设施，不得危害其安全播出。

**Article 16** No unit or individual may raid a radio station or television station, damage the facilities of a radio station or television station, or endanger their safe broadcasting.

### 第三章　广播电视传输覆盖网

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国务院广播电视行政部门应当对全国广播电视传输覆盖网按照国家的统一标准实行统一规划，并实行分级建设和开发。县级以上地方人民政府广播电视行政部门应当按照国家有关规定，组建和管理本行政区域内的广播电视传输覆盖网。  
组建广播电视传输覆盖网，包括充分利用国家现有的公用通信等各种网络资源，应当确保广播电视节目传输质量和畅通。  
本条例所称广播电视传输覆盖网，由广播电视发射台、转播台（包括差转台、收转台，下同）、广播电视卫星、卫星上行站、卫星收转站、微波站、监测台（站）及有线广播电视传输覆盖网等构成。

**Article 17** The administrative department of radio and television under the State Council shall make a unified plan for the national radio and television transmission and coverage network in accordance with the unified standards of the State, and shall carry out graded construction and development. The administrative departments of radio and television of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, set up and administer the radio and television transmission and coverage network within their respective administrative areas.  
The establishment of a radio and television transmission and coverage network, including the full utilization of various existing network resources such as public telecommunication networks of the State, shall ensure the quality and smoothness of transmission of radio and television programs.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "radio and television transmission and coverage network" consists of radio and television transmitting stations, relay stations (including transposer stations and receiving and retransmitting stations, the same below), radio and television satellites, satellite uplink stations, satellite receiving and retransmitting stations, microwave stations, monitoring stations (or monitoring posts), and cable radio and television transmission and coverage networks, etc.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院广播电视行政部门负责指配广播电视专用频段的频率，并核发频率专用指配证明。

**Article 18** The administrative department of radio and television under the State Council shall be responsible for assigning the frequencies of the frequency bands exclusively used for radio and television, and for issuing certificates of exclusive frequency assignment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　设立广播电视发射台、转播台、微波站、卫星上行站，应当按照国家有关规定，持国务院广播电视行政部门核发的频率专用指配证明，向国家的或者省、自治区、直辖市的无线电管理机构办理审批手续，领取无线电台执照。

**Article 19** To establish a radio and television transmitting station, relay station, microwave station or satellite uplink station, the unit concerned shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, hold the certificate of exclusive frequency assignment issued by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council, go through the examination and approval procedures with the national or the provincial, autonomous regional or municipal radio regulatory authority, and obtain a radio station license.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　广播电视发射台、转播台应当按照国务院广播电视行政部门的有关规定发射、转播广播电视节目。  
广播电视发射台、转播台经核准使用的频率、频段不得出租、转让，已经批准的各项技术参数不得擅自变更。

**Article 20** A radio and television transmitting station or relay station shall transmit or relay radio and television programs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.  
The frequencies and frequency bands approved for use by a radio and television transmitting station or relay station shall not be leased or transferred, and the technical parameters that have been approved shall not be altered without authorization.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　广播电视发射台、转播台不得擅自播放自办节目和插播广告。

**Article 21** A radio and television transmitting station or relay station shall not, without authorization, broadcast self-produced programs or insert advertisements.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　广播电视传输覆盖网的工程选址、设计、施工、安装，应当按照国家有关规定办理，并由依法取得相应资格证书的单位承担。  
广播电视传输覆盖网的工程建设和使用的广播电视技术设备，应当符合国家标准、行业标准。工程竣工后，由广播电视行政部门组织验收，验收合格的，方可投入使用。

**Article 22** The site selection, design, construction and installation of a radio and television transmission and coverage network project shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall be undertaken by units that have obtained corresponding qualification certificates according to law.  
The engineering construction of a radio and television transmission and coverage network and the radio and television technical equipment used therein shall conform to the national standards and industrial standards. After the completion of the project, the administrative department of radio and television shall organize the acceptance check; only after the project has passed the acceptance check may it be put into use.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　区域性有线广播电视传输覆盖网，由县级以上地方人民政府广播电视行政部门设立和管理。  
区域性有线广播电视传输覆盖网的规划、建设方案，由县级人民政府或者设区的市、自治州人民政府的广播电视行政部门报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政部门批准后实施，或者由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政部门报国务院广播电视行政部门批准后实施。  
同一行政区域只能设立一个区域性有线广播电视传输覆盖网。有线电视站应当按照规划与区域性有线电视传输覆盖网联网。

**Article 23** A regional cable radio and television transmission and coverage network shall be established and administered by the administrative department of radio and television of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The plan and construction program for a regional cable radio and television transmission and coverage network shall, after being submitted by the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at the county level or the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or an autonomous prefecture to the administrative department of radio and television of the provincial, autonomous regional or municipal people's government for approval, be implemented, or after being submitted by the administrative department of radio and television of the provincial, autonomous regional or municipal people's government to the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council for approval, be implemented.  
Only one regional cable radio and television transmission and coverage network may be established in a single administrative area. A cable television station shall, in accordance with the plan, be networked with the regional cable television transmission and coverage network.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　未经批准，任何单位和个人不得擅自利用有线广播电视传输覆盖网播放节目。

**Article 24** Without approval, no unit or individual may, without authorization, use the cable radio and television transmission and coverage network to broadcast programs.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　传输广播电视节目的卫星空间段资源的管理和使用，应当符合国家有关规定。  
广播电台、电视台利用卫星方式传输广播电视节目，应当符合国家规定的条件，并经国务院广播电视行政部门审核批准。

**Article 25** The management and use of satellite space segment resources for transmitting radio and television programs shall conform to the relevant provisions of the State.  
Where a radio station or television station uses satellite means to transmit radio and television programs, it shall meet the conditions prescribed by the State and undergo examination and approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　安装和使用卫星广播电视地面接收设施，应当按照国家有关规定向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政部门申领许可证。进口境外卫星广播电视节目解码器、解压器及其他卫星广播电视地面接收设施，应当经国务院广播电视行政部门审查同意。

**Article 26** For the installation and use of satellite radio and television ground receiving facilities, a license shall be applied for from the administrative department of radio and television of the provincial, autonomous regional or municipal people's government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The import of decoders, decompressors and other satellite radio and television ground receiving facilities for foreign satellite radio and television programs shall be subject to examination and consent by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　禁止任何单位和个人侵占、哄抢或者以其他方式破坏广播电视传输覆盖网的设施。

**Article 27** No unit or individual may encroach upon, loot or otherwise damage the facilities of a radio and television transmission and coverage network.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人不得侵占、干扰广播电视专用频率，不得擅自截传、干扰、解扰广播电视信号。

**Article 28** No unit or individual may encroach upon or interfere with the frequencies exclusively used for radio and television, or, without authorization, intercept, interfere with or descramble radio and television signals.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门应当采取卫星传送、无线转播、有线广播、有线电视等多种方式，提高农村广播电视覆盖率。

**Article 29** The administrative departments of radio and television of the people's governments at or above the county level shall adopt various means such as satellite transmission, wireless relay, wired broadcasting and cable television to raise the coverage rate of radio and television in rural areas.

### 第四章　广播电视节目

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　广播电台、电视台应当按照国务院广播电视行政部门批准的节目设置范围开办节目。

**Article 30** A radio station or television station shall launch programs within the scope of program offerings approved by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　广播电视节目由广播电台、电视台和省级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门批准设立的广播电视节目制作经营单位制作。广播电台、电视台不得播放未取得广播电视节目制作经营许可的单位制作的广播电视节目。

**Article 31** Radio and television programs shall be produced by radio stations, television stations, and radio and television program production and operation units approved for establishment by the administrative departments of radio and television of the people's governments at or above the provincial level. A radio station or television station shall not broadcast radio or television programs produced by a unit that has not obtained a license for radio and television program production and operation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　广播电台、电视台应当提高广播电视节目质量，增加国产优秀节目数量，禁止制作、播放载有下列内容的节目：  
（一）危害国家的统一、主权和领土完整的；  
（二）危害国家的安全、荣誉和利益的；  
（三）煽动民族分裂，破坏民族团结的；  
（四）泄露国家秘密的；  
（五）诽谤、侮辱他人的；  
（六）宣扬淫秽、迷信或者渲染暴力的；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定禁止的其他内容。

**Article 32** A radio station or television station shall improve the quality of radio and television programs, increase the number of excellent domestic programs, and shall prohibit the production and broadcasting of programs containing the following content:  
(1) content that endangers the unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the State;  
(2) content that endangers the security, honor and interests of the State;  
(3) content that incites ethnic division or undermines ethnic unity;  
(4) content that divulges State secrets;  
(5) content that slanders or insults others;  
(6) content that advocates obscenity, superstition or promotes violence; and  
(7) other content prohibited by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　广播电台、电视台对其播放的广播电视节目内容，应当依照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定进行播前审查，重播重审。

**Article 33** A radio station or television station shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of these Regulations, conduct pre-broadcast examination of the content of the radio and television programs it broadcasts, and shall conduct re-examination for rebroadcast.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　广播电视新闻应当真实、公正。

**Article 34** Radio and television news shall be truthful and impartial.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　电视剧的制作和播出管理办法，由国务院广播电视行政部门规定。

**Article 35** The administrative measures for the production and broadcasting of television plays shall be prescribed by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　广播电台、电视台应当使用规范的语言文字。  
广播电台、电视台应当推广全国通用的普通话。

**Article 36** A radio station or television station shall use standard spoken and written language.  
A radio station or television station shall promote Putonghua, the common language of the whole country.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　地方广播电台、电视台或者广播电视站，应当按照国务院广播电视行政部门的有关规定转播广播电视节目。  
乡、镇设立的广播电视站不得自办电视节目。

**Article 37** A local radio station, television station or radio and television station shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council, relay radio and television programs.  
No radio and television station established in a township or town may produce its own television programs.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　广播电台、电视台应当按照节目预告播放广播电视节目；确需更换、调整原预告节目的，应当提前向公众告示。

**Article 38** A radio station or television station shall broadcast radio and television programs in accordance with the program schedule; if it is really necessary to change or adjust the originally scheduled program, it shall give advance public notice.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　用于广播电台、电视台播放的境外电影、电视剧，必须经国务院广播电视行政部门审查批准。用于广播电台、电视台播放的境外其他广播电视节目，必须经国务院广播电视行政部门或者其授权的机构审查批准。  
向境外提供的广播电视节目，应当按照国家有关规定向省级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门备案。

**Article 39** Foreign films and television plays to be broadcast by radio stations or television stations must be subject to examination and approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council. Other foreign radio and television programs to be broadcast by radio stations or television stations must be subject to examination and approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council or an institution authorized by it.  
Radio and television programs provided to foreign countries shall be filed with the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the provincial level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　广播电台、电视台播放境外广播电视节目的时间与广播电视节目总播放时间的比例，由国务院广播电视行政部门规定。

**Article 40** The proportion of the time for broadcasting foreign radio and television programs to the total broadcasting time of radio and television programs by radio stations or television stations shall be prescribed by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　广播电台、电视台以卫星等传输方式进口、转播境外广播电视节目，必须经国务院广播电视行政部门批准。

**Article 41** Where a radio station or television station imports or relays foreign radio and television programs by means of satellite or other transmission means, it must obtain approval from the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　广播电台、电视台播放广告，不得超过国务院广播电视行政部门规定的时间。  
广播电台、电视台应当播放公益性广告。

**Article 42** The broadcasting of advertisements by a radio station or television station shall not exceed the time limit prescribed by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council.  
A radio station or television station shall broadcast public service advertisements.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院广播电视行政部门在特殊情况下，可以作出停止播出、更换特定节目或者指定转播特定节目的决定。

**Article 43** The administrative department of radio and television under the State Council may, under special circumstances, make a decision to stop broadcasting, replace specific programs or designate the relaying of specific programs.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　教育电视台应当按照国家有关规定播放各类教育教学节目，不得播放与教学内容无关的电影、电视片。

**Article 44** An educational television station shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, broadcast various kinds of educational and teaching programs, and shall not broadcast films or television films unrelated to the teaching content.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　举办国际性广播电视节目交流、交易活动，应当经国务院广播电视行政部门批准，并由指定的单位承办。举办国内区域性广播电视节目交流、交易活动，应当经举办地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政部门批准，并由指定的单位承办。

**Article 45** The holding of international exchange and trade activities of radio and television programs shall be subject to approval by the administrative department of radio and television under the State Council, and shall be undertaken by designated units. The holding of domestic regional exchange and trade activities of radio and television programs shall be subject to approval by the administrative department of radio and television of the provincial, autonomous regional or municipal people's government of the place where the activity is held, and shall be undertaken by designated units.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　对享有著作权的广播电视节目的播放和使用，依照《中华人民共和国著作权法》的规定办理。

**Article 46** The broadcasting and use of radio and television programs in which copyright is enjoyed shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China.

### 第五章　罚则

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，擅自设立广播电台、电视台、教育电视台、有线广播电视传输覆盖网、广播电视站的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门予以取缔，没收其从事违法活动的设备，并处投资总额1倍以上2倍以下的罚款。  
擅自设立广播电视发射台、转播台、微波站、卫星上行站的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门予以取缔，没收其从事违法活动的设备，并处投资总额1倍以上2倍以下的罚款；或者由无线电管理机构依照国家无线电管理的有关规定予以处罚。

**Article 47** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, without authorization establishes a radio station, television station, educational television station, cable radio and television transmission and coverage network or radio and television station, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall ban it, confiscate the equipment used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the total investment.  
Where anyone, without authorization, establishes a radio and television transmitting station, relay station, microwave station or satellite uplink station, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall ban it, confiscate the equipment used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the total investment; or the radio regulatory authority shall impose a penalty in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on radio regulation.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，擅自设立广播电视节目制作经营单位或者擅自制作广播电视节目的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门予以取缔，没收其从事违法活动的专用工具、设备和节目载体，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, without authorization establishes a radio and television program production and operation unit or, without authorization, produces radio and television programs, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall ban it, confiscate the special tools, equipment and program carriers used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，制作、播放、向境外提供含有[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)规定禁止内容的节目的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门责令停止制作、播放、向境外提供，收缴其节目载体，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原批准机关吊销许可证；违反治安管理规定的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, produces, broadcasts or provides to a foreign country programs containing content prohibited by Article 32 of these Regulations, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop production, broadcasting or provision to a foreign country, confiscate its program carriers, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original approving authority shall revoke the license; if the act violates the provisions on public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门责令停止违法活动，给予警告，没收违法所得，可以并处2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原批准机关吊销许可证：  
（一）未经批准，擅自变更台名、台标、节目设置范围或者节目套数的；  
（二）出租、转让播出时段的；  
（三）转播、播放广播电视节目违反规定的；  
（四）播放境外广播电视节目或者广告的时间超出规定的；  
（五）播放未取得广播电视节目制作经营许可的单位制作的广播电视节目的；  
（六）播放未经批准的境外电影、电视剧和其他广播电视节目的；  
（七）教育电视台播放[本条例第四十四条](#art-44)规定禁止播放的节目的；  
（八）未经批准，擅自举办广播电视节目交流、交易活动的。

**Article 50** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, commits one of the following acts, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop the illegal activities, give it a warning, confiscate its illegal gains, and may also impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original approving authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) altering, without authorization, the station name, station logo, scope of program offerings or number of program channels without approval;  
(2) leasing or transferring broadcast time slots;  
(3) relaying or broadcasting radio or television programs in violation of provisions;  
(4) broadcasting foreign radio or television programs or advertisements for a time exceeding the prescribed limits;  
(5) broadcasting radio or television programs produced by a unit that has not obtained a license for radio and television program production and operation;  
(6) broadcasting foreign films, television plays or other radio and television programs that have not been approved;  
(7) an educational television station broadcasting programs prohibited by Article 44 of these Regulations; or  
(8) holding, without authorization, exchange or trade activities of radio and television programs without approval.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门责令停止违法活动，给予警告，没收违法所得和从事违法活动的专用工具、设备，可以并处2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原批准机关吊销许可证：  
（一）出租、转让频率、频段，擅自变更广播电视发射台、转播台技术参数的；  
（二）广播电视发射台、转播台擅自播放自办节目、插播广告的；  
（三）未经批准，擅自利用卫星方式传输广播电视节目的；  
（四）未经批准，擅自以卫星等传输方式进口、转播境外广播电视节目的；  
（五）未经批准，擅自利用有线广播电视传输覆盖网播放节目的；  
（六）未经批准，擅自进行广播电视传输覆盖网的工程选址、设计、施工、安装的；  
（七）侵占、干扰广播电视专用频率，擅自截传、干扰、解扰广播电视信号的。

**Article 51** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, commits one of the following acts, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop the illegal activities, give it a warning, confiscate its illegal gains and the special tools and equipment used for the illegal activities, and may also impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original approving authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) leasing or transferring frequencies or frequency bands, or, without authorization, altering the technical parameters of a radio and television transmitting station or relay station;  
(2) a radio and television transmitting station or relay station broadcasting, without authorization, self-produced programs or inserting advertisements;  
(3) using satellite means to transmit radio and television programs without approval;  
(4) importing or relaying foreign radio and television programs by means of satellite or other transmission means without approval;  
(5) using the cable radio and television transmission and coverage network to broadcast programs without approval;  
(6) carrying out, without authorization, the site selection, design, construction or installation of a radio and television transmission and coverage network project without approval; or  
(7) encroaching upon or interfering with the frequencies exclusively used for radio and television, or, without authorization, intercepting, interfering with or descrambling radio and television signals.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，危害广播电台、电视台安全播出的，破坏广播电视设施的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政部门责令停止违法活动；情节严重的，处2万元以上5万元以下的罚款；造成损害的，侵害人应当依法赔偿损失；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, endangers the safe broadcasting of a radio station or television station, or damages radio and television facilities, the administrative department of radio and television of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to stop the illegal activities; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if damage is caused, the infringer shall compensate for the loss according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　广播电视行政部门及其工作人员在广播电视管理工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 53** Where an administrative department of radio and television or its functionary, in the administration of radio and television, abuses power, neglects duties or engages in malpractices for personal gain, if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not yet constitute a crime, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本条例施行前已经设立的广播电台、电视台、教育电视台、广播电视发射台、转播台、广播电视节目制作经营单位，自本条例施行之日起6个月内，应当依照本条例的规定重新办理审核手续；不符合本条例规定的，予以撤销；已有的县级教育电视台可以与县级电视台合并，开办教育节目频道。

**Article 54** Radio stations, television stations, educational television stations, radio and television transmitting stations, relay stations, and radio and television program production and operation units that have been established before these Regulations come into force shall, within six months from the date these Regulations come into force, undergo re-examination formalities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; those that do not conform to the provisions of these Regulations shall be abolished; existing county-level educational television stations may merge with county-level television stations to launch educational program channels.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自1997年9月1日起施行。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 1997.
