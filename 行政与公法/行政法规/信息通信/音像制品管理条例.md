# 音像制品管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （2001年12月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第341号公布　根据2011年3月19日《国务院关于修改〈音像制品管理条例〉的决定》第一次修订　根据2013年12月7日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订　根据2016年2月6日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第三次修订　根据2020年11月29日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第四次修订　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第五次修订）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强音像制品的管理，促进音像业的健康发展和繁荣，丰富人民群众的文化生活，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of audio-visual products, promoting the healthy development and prosperity of the audio-visual industry, enriching the cultural life of the people, and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于录有内容的录音带、录像带、唱片、激光唱盘和激光视盘等音像制品的出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售、出租等活动。  
音像制品用于广播电视播放的，适用广播电视法律、行政法规。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the activities of publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling, retailing, and renting of such audio-visual products as recorded audio tapes, video tapes, records, compact discs, and laser discs, and the like.  
Where audio-visual products are used for broadcasting and television transmission, the laws and administrative regulations on broadcasting and television shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售、出租音像制品，应当遵守宪法和有关法律、法规，坚持为人民服务和为社会主义服务的方向，传播有益于经济发展和社会进步的思想、道德、科学技术和文化知识。  
音像制品禁止载有下列内容：  
（一）反对宪法确定的基本原则的；  
（二）危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
（三）泄露国家秘密、危害国家安全或者损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
（四）煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结，或者侵害民族风俗、习惯的；  
（五）宣扬邪教、迷信的；  
（六）扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
（七）宣扬淫秽、赌博、暴力或者教唆犯罪的；  
（八）侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
（九）危害社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
（十）有法律、行政法规和国家规定禁止的其他内容的。

**Article 3** The publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling, retailing, and renting of audio-visual products shall comply with the Constitution and relevant laws and regulations, adhere to the orientation of serving the people and serving socialism, and disseminate thoughts, morals, science and technology, and cultural knowledge beneficial to economic development and social progress.  
The following contents shall be prohibited in audio-visual products:  
(1) those opposing the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) those endangering the unification, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the State;  
(3) those divulging State secrets, endangering national security or damaging national honor and interests;  
(4) those inciting ethnic hatred, ethnic discrimination, undermining ethnic unity, or infringing upon ethnic customs and habits;  
(5) those propagating cults and superstitions;  
(6) those disturbing social order and undermining social stability;  
(7) those propagating obscenity, gambling, violence or instigating crimes;  
(8) those insulting or slandering others, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) those endangering social morality or the excellent cultural traditions of the nation; or  
(10) other contents prohibited by laws, administrative regulations and State provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院出版行政主管部门负责全国音像制品的出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售和出租的监督管理工作；国务院其他有关行政部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责有关的音像制品经营活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府负责出版管理的行政主管部门（以下简称出版行政主管部门）负责本行政区域内音像制品的出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售和出租的监督管理工作；县级以上地方人民政府其他有关行政部门在各自的职责范围内负责有关的音像制品经营活动的监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent administrative department for publication under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling, retailing and renting of audio-visual products throughout the country; the other relevant administrative departments under the State Council shall, according to the division of responsibilities prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the relevant audio-visual product business activities.  
The competent administrative department for publication administration under the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the competent administrative department for publication) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling, retailing and renting of audio-visual products within its administrative region; the other relevant administrative departments under the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within the scope of their respective responsibilities, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the relevant audio-visual product business activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售音像制品，实行许可制度；未经许可，任何单位和个人不得从事音像制品的出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售等活动。  
依照本条例发放的许可证和批准文件，不得出租、出借、出售或者以其他任何形式转让。

**Article 5** The State shall implement a licensing system for the publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling and retailing of audio-visual products; without a license, no unit or individual may engage in such activities as publishing, producing, reproducing, importing, wholesaling and retailing of audio-visual products.  
The licenses and approval documents issued in accordance with these Regulations shall not be rented, lent, sold or transferred in any other form.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院出版行政主管部门负责制定音像业的发展规划，确定全国音像出版单位、音像复制单位的总量、布局和结构。

**Article 6** The competent administrative department for publication under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating development plans for the audio-visual industry and determining the total quantity, layout and structure of audio-visual publishing units and audio-visual reproduction units throughout the country.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　音像制品经营活动的监督管理部门及其工作人员不得从事或者变相从事音像制品经营活动，并不得参与或者变相参与音像制品经营单位的经营活动。

**Article 7** The supervision and administration departments for audio-visual product business activities and their staff members shall not engage in or in disguised form engage in audio-visual product business activities, and shall not participate or in disguised form participate in the business activities of audio-visual product business units.

### 第二章　出版

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立音像出版单位，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有音像出版单位的名称、章程；  
（二）有符合国务院出版行政主管部门认定的主办单位及其主管机关；  
（三）有确定的业务范围；  
（四）有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和符合国家规定的资格条件的音像出版专业人员；  
（五）有适应业务范围需要的资金、设备和工作场所；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
审批设立音像出版单位，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当符合音像出版单位总量、布局和结构的规划。

**Article 8** The establishment of an audio-visual publishing unit shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name and articles of association of the audio-visual publishing unit;  
(2) having a sponsoring unit and its competent authority that meet the requirements recognized by the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council;  
(3) having a definite scope of business;  
(4) having an organizational structure suitable for the needs of the business scope and audio-visual publishing professionals meeting the qualifications prescribed by the State;  
(5) having funds, equipment and work premises suitable for the needs of the business scope; and  
(6) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
In examining and approving the establishment of an audio-visual publishing unit, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the plans for the total quantity, layout and structure of audio-visual publishing units shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立音像出版单位，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核同意后，报国务院出版行政主管部门审批。国务院出版行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起60日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给《音像制品出版许可证》，由申请人持《音像制品出版许可证》到工商行政管理部门登记，依法领取营业执照；不批准的，应当说明理由。  
申请书应当载明下列内容：  
（一）音像出版单位的名称、地址；  
（二）音像出版单位的主办单位及其主管机关的名称、地址；  
（三）音像出版单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、住址、资格证明文件；  
（四）音像出版单位的资金来源和数额。

**Article 9** An application for the establishment of an audio-visual publishing unit shall, after examination and consent by the competent administrative department for publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the unit is located, be submitted to the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council for approval. The competent administrative department for publication under the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on approval or disapproval and notify the applicant. If approved, it shall issue the "Audio-Visual Product Publishing License", and the applicant shall, with the "Audio-Visual Product Publishing License", register with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license according to law; if not approved, it shall state the reasons.  
The application form shall contain the following contents:  
(1) the name and address of the audio-visual publishing unit;  
(2) the names and addresses of the sponsoring unit and its competent authority of the audio-visual publishing unit;  
(3) the name, address, and qualification certification documents of the legal representative or principal responsible person of the audio-visual publishing unit; and  
(4) the source and amount of funds of the audio-visual publishing unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　音像出版单位变更名称、主办单位或者其主管机关、业务范围，或者兼并其他音像出版单位，或者因合并、分立而设立新的音像出版单位的，应当依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定办理审批手续，并到原登记的工商行政管理部门办理相应的登记手续。  
音像出版单位变更地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人，或者终止出版经营活动的，应当到原登记的工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记，并向国务院出版行政主管部门备案。

**Article 10** Where an audio-visual publishing unit changes its name, sponsoring unit or its competent authority, or scope of business, or merges with other audio-visual publishing units, or establishes a new audio-visual publishing unit due to merger or division, it shall go through the examination and approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations and go through the corresponding registration formalities with the original registered administrative department for industry and commerce.  
Where an audio-visual publishing unit changes its address, legal representative or principal responsible person, or terminates its publishing business activities, it shall go through the formalities for modification registration or cancellation registration with the original registered administrative department for industry and commerce, and file with the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　音像出版单位的年度出版计划和涉及国家安全、社会安定等方面的重大选题，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核后报国务院出版行政主管部门备案；重大选题音像制品未在出版前报备案的，不得出版。

**Article 11** The annual publishing plan of an audio-visual publishing unit and major subject matters involving national security, social stability and the like shall, after examination by the competent administrative department for publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, be submitted to the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council for the record; where the audio-visual products on major subject matters have not been submitted for the record before publication, they shall not be published.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　音像出版单位应当在其出版的音像制品及其包装的明显位置，标明出版单位的名称、地址和音像制品的版号、出版时间、著作权人等事项；出版进口的音像制品，还应当标明进口批准文号。  
音像出版单位应当按照国家有关规定向国家图书馆、中国版本图书馆和国务院出版行政主管部门免费送交样本。

**Article 12** An audio-visual publishing unit shall, at the conspicuous position of the audio-visual products published by it and their packages, indicate such items as the name and address of the publishing unit, the edition number, publication time, copyright owner, etc. of the audio-visual products; where imported audio-visual products are published, the import approval number shall also be indicated.  
An audio-visual publishing unit shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, deliver free samples to the National Library, the China National Version Library and the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　音像出版单位不得向任何单位或者个人出租、出借、出售或者以其他任何形式转让本单位的名称，不得向任何单位或者个人出售或者以其他形式转让本单位的版号。

**Article 13** An audio-visual publishing unit shall not rent, lend, sell or transfer the name of the unit to any unit or individual in any other form, and shall not sell or transfer the edition number of the unit to any unit or individual in any other form.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位和个人不得以购买、租用、借用、擅自使用音像出版单位的名称或者购买、伪造版号等形式从事音像制品出版活动。  
图书出版社、报社、期刊社、电子出版物出版社，不得出版非配合本版出版物的音像制品；但是，可以按照国务院出版行政主管部门的规定，出版配合本版出版物的音像制品，并参照音像出版单位享有权利、承担义务。

**Article 14** No unit or individual shall engage in audio-visual product publishing activities by purchasing, renting, borrowing, or unauthorized use of the name of an audio-visual publishing unit, or by purchasing or forging edition numbers.  
Book publishing houses, newspapers, periodicals, and electronic publication publishing houses shall not publish audio-visual products that are not supplementary to their own publications; however, they may, in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council, publish audio-visual products that are supplementary to their own publications, and shall enjoy rights and assume obligations by reference to audio-visual publishing units.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　音像出版单位可以与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区或者外国的组织、个人合作制作音像制品。具体办法由国务院出版行政主管部门制定。

**Article 15** An audio-visual publishing unit may cooperate with organizations or individuals from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, Taiwan region or foreign countries in producing audio-visual products. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　音像出版单位实行编辑责任制度，保证音像制品的内容符合本条例的规定。

**Article 16** An audio-visual publishing unit shall implement an editorial responsibility system to ensure that the contents of audio-visual products comply with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　音像出版单位以外的单位设立的独立从事音像制品制作业务的单位（以下简称音像制作单位）申请从事音像制品制作业务，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审批。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起60日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给《音像制品制作许可证》；不批准的，应当说明理由。广播、电视节目制作经营单位的设立，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定办理。  
申请书应当载明下列内容：  
（一）音像制作单位的名称、地址；  
（二）音像制作单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、住址、资格证明文件；  
（三）音像制作单位的资金来源和数额。  
审批从事音像制品制作业务申请，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当兼顾音像制作单位总量、布局和结构。

**Article 17** If a unit other than an audio-visual publishing unit establishes a unit independently engaged in audio-visual product production business (hereinafter referred to as the audio-visual production unit) and applies for engaging in audio-visual product production business, it shall be examined and approved by the competent administrative department for publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located. The competent administrative department for publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 60 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision on approval or disapproval and notify the applicant. If approved, it shall issue the "Audio-Visual Product Production License"; if not approved, it shall state the reasons. The establishment of broadcasting and television program production and management units shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The application form shall contain the following contents:  
(1) the name and address of the audio-visual production unit;  
(2) the name, address, and qualification certification documents of the legal representative or principal responsible person of the audio-visual production unit; and  
(3) the source and amount of funds of the audio-visual production unit.  
In examining and approving an application for engaging in audio-visual product production business, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the total quantity, layout and structure of audio-visual production units shall also be taken into account.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　音像制作单位变更名称、业务范围，或者兼并其他音像制作单位，或者因合并、分立而设立新的音像制作单位的，应当依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定办理审批手续。  
音像制作单位变更地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人，或者终止制作经营活动的，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门备案。

**Article 18** Where an audio-visual production unit changes its name, scope of business, or merges with other audio-visual production units, or establishes a new audio-visual production unit due to merger or division, it shall go through the examination and approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations.  
Where an audio-visual production unit changes its address, legal representative or principal responsible person, or terminates its production business activities, it shall file with the competent administrative department for publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　音像出版单位不得委托未取得《音像制品制作许可证》的单位制作音像制品。  
音像制作单位接受委托制作音像制品的，应当按照国家有关规定，与委托的出版单位订立制作委托合同；验证委托的出版单位的《音像制品出版许可证》或者本版出版物的证明及由委托的出版单位盖章的音像制品制作委托书。  
音像制作单位不得出版、复制、批发、零售音像制品。

**Article 19** An audio-visual publishing unit shall not entrust a unit that has not obtained the "Audio-Visual Product Production License" to produce audio-visual products.  
Where an audio-visual production unit accepts an entrustment to produce audio-visual products, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, conclude a production entrustment contract with the entrusting publishing unit; verify the "Audio-Visual Product Publishing License" of the entrusting publishing unit or the certificate of its own publication and the audio-visual product production entrustment letter sealed by the entrusting publishing unit.  
An audio-visual production unit shall not publish, reproduce, wholesale or retail audio-visual products.

### 第三章　复制

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　申请从事音像制品复制业务应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有音像复制单位的名称、章程；  
（二）有确定的业务范围；  
（三）有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和人员；  
（四）有适应业务范围需要的资金、设备和复制场所；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
审批从事音像制品复制业务申请，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当符合音像复制单位总量、布局和结构的规划。

**Article 20** An application for engaging in audio-visual product reproduction business shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name and articles of association of the audio-visual reproduction unit;  
(2) having a definite scope of business;  
(3) having an organizational structure and personnel suitable for the needs of the business scope;  
(4) having funds, equipment and reproduction premises suitable for the needs of the business scope; and  
(5) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
In examining and approving an application for engaging in audio-visual product reproduction business, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the plans for the total quantity, layout and structure of audio-visual reproduction units shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请从事音像制品复制业务，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审批。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给《复制经营许可证》；不批准的，应当说明理由。  
申请书应当载明下列内容：  
（一）音像复制单位的名称、地址；  
（二）音像复制单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、住址；  
（三）音像复制单位的资金来源和数额。

**Article 21** An application for engaging in the reproduction business of audio-visual products shall be examined and approved by the competent publishing administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located. The competent publishing administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 20 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval and notify the applicant. If approved, a "Reproduction Business License" shall be issued; if disapproved, the reasons therefor shall be stated.  
The application shall specify the following:  
(1) the name and address of the audio-visual reproduction unit;  
(2) the name and domicile of the legal representative or the principal person in charge of the audio-visual reproduction unit;  
(3) the source and amount of funds of the audio-visual reproduction unit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　音像复制单位变更业务范围，或者兼并其他音像复制单位，或者因合并、分立而设立新的音像复制单位的，应当依照[本条例第二十一条](#art-21)的规定办理审批手续。  
音像复制单位变更名称、地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人，或者终止复制经营活动的，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门备案。

**Article 22** Where an audio-visual reproduction unit alters its business scope, merges with another audio-visual reproduction unit, or establishes a new audio-visual reproduction unit through merger or division, it shall go through the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of these Regulations.  
Where an audio-visual reproduction unit alters its name, address, legal representative or principal person in charge, or terminates its reproduction business activities, it shall report to the competent publishing administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for the record.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　音像复制单位接受委托复制音像制品的，应当按照国家有关规定，与委托的出版单位订立复制委托合同；验证委托的出版单位的《音像制品出版许可证》、营业执照副本、盖章的音像制品复制委托书以及出版单位取得的授权书；接受委托复制的音像制品属于非卖品的，应当验证委托单位的身份证明和委托单位出具的音像制品非卖品复制委托书。  
音像复制单位应当自完成音像制品复制之日起2年内，保存委托合同和所复制的音像制品的样本以及验证的有关证明文件的副本，以备查验。

**Article 23** Where an audio-visual reproduction unit accepts a commission to reproduce audio-visual products, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, enter into a reproduction commission contract with the commissioning publishing unit; verify the "Audio-Visual Product Publishing License", the duplicate of the business license, the sealed letter of commission for reproduction of audio-visual products, and the authorization certificate obtained by the publishing unit of the commissioning publishing unit; if the audio-visual products accepted for reproduction are non-commercial articles, it shall verify the identity certificate of the commissioning unit and the letter of commission for reproduction of non-commercial audio-visual products issued by the commissioning unit.  
The audio-visual reproduction unit shall, within two years from the date of completion of the reproduction of the audio-visual products, keep the commission contract, the samples of the reproduced audio-visual products, and the copies of the relevant supporting documents verified, for inspection.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　音像复制单位不得接受非音像出版单位或者个人的委托复制经营性的音像制品；不得自行复制音像制品；不得批发、零售音像制品。

**Article 24** An audio-visual reproduction unit shall not accept the commission of a non-audio-visual publishing unit or an individual to reproduce commercial audio-visual products; shall not reproduce audio-visual products on its own; and shall not wholesale or retail audio-visual products.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事光盘复制的音像复制单位复制光盘，必须使用蚀刻有国务院出版行政主管部门核发的激光数码储存片来源识别码的注塑模具。

**Article 25** An audio-visual reproduction unit engaged in the reproduction of discs must, when reproducing discs, use injection molds engraved with the source identification code of laser digital storage discs issued by the competent publishing administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　音像复制单位接受委托复制境外音像制品的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门批准，并持著作权人的授权书依法到著作权行政管理部门登记；复制的音像制品应当全部运输出境，不得在境内发行。

**Article 26** Where an audio-visual reproduction unit accepts a commission to reproduce audio-visual products from outside the territory, it shall obtain approval from the competent publishing administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall, with the authorization certificate of the copyright owner, go through registration at the copyright administration department according to law; all reproduced audio-visual products shall be transported out of the territory and shall not be distributed within the territory.

### 第四章　进口

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　音像制品成品进口业务由国务院出版行政主管部门批准的音像制品成品进口经营单位经营；未经批准，任何单位或者个人不得经营音像制品成品进口业务。

**Article 27** The import business of finished audio-visual products shall be operated by the business unit for import of finished audio-visual products approved by the competent publishing administration department of the State Council; without approval, no unit or individual may operate the import business of finished audio-visual products.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　进口用于出版的音像制品，以及进口用于批发、零售、出租等的音像制品成品，应当报国务院出版行政主管部门进行内容审查。  
国务院出版行政主管部门应当自收到音像制品内容审查申请书之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给批准文件；不批准的，应当说明理由。  
进口用于出版的音像制品的单位、音像制品成品进口经营单位应当持国务院出版行政主管部门的批准文件到海关办理进口手续。

**Article 28** The import of audio-visual products for publication, and the import of finished audio-visual products for wholesale, retail, rental, etc., shall be submitted to the competent publishing administration department of the State Council for content review.  
The competent publishing administration department of the State Council shall, within 30 days from the date of receiving the application for content review of audio-visual products, make a decision of approval or disapproval and notify the applicant. If approved, an approval document shall be issued; if disapproved, the reasons therefor shall be stated.  
The unit importing audio-visual products for publication and the business unit for import of finished audio-visual products shall, with the approval document of the competent publishing administration department of the State Council, go through the import formalities with the customs.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　进口用于出版的音像制品，其著作权事项应当向国务院著作权行政管理部门登记。

**Article 29** For audio-visual products imported for publication, their copyright matters shall be registered with the copyright administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　进口供研究、教学参考的音像制品，应当委托音像制品成品进口经营单位依照[本条例第二十八条](#art-28)的规定办理。  
进口用于展览、展示的音像制品，经国务院出版行政主管部门批准后，到海关办理临时进口手续。  
依照本条规定进口的音像制品，不得进行经营性复制、批发、零售、出租和放映。

**Article 30** The import of audio-visual products for research and teaching reference shall be handled by entrusting a business unit for import of finished audio-visual products in accordance with the provisions of Article 28 of these Regulations.  
The import of audio-visual products for exhibition or display shall, after approval by the competent publishing administration department of the State Council, go through the temporary import formalities with the customs.  
The audio-visual products imported in accordance with this Article shall not be reproduced, wholesaled, retailed, rented or shown for commercial purposes.

### 第五章　批发、零售和出租

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　申请从事音像制品批发、零售业务，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有音像制品批发、零售单位的名称、章程；  
（二）有确定的业务范围；  
（三）有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和人员；  
（四）有适应业务范围需要的资金和场所；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 31** An application for engaging in the wholesale or retail business of audio-visual products shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the name and articles of association of the wholesale or retail unit of audio-visual products;  
(2) having a definite business scope;  
(3) having organizational structure and personnel that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(4) having funds and premises that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(5) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　申请从事音像制品批发业务，应当报所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审批。申请从事音像制品零售业务，应当报县级地方人民政府出版行政主管部门审批。出版行政主管部门应当自受理申请书之日起30日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，应当发给《出版物经营许可证》；不批准的，应当说明理由。  
《出版物经营许可证》应当注明音像制品经营活动的种类。

**Article 32** An application for engaging in the wholesale business of audio-visual products shall be submitted to the competent publishing administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located for examination and approval. An application for engaging in the retail business of audio-visual products shall be submitted to the competent publishing administration department of the local people's government at the county level for examination and approval. The competent publishing administration department shall, within 30 days from the date of accepting the application, make a decision of approval or disapproval and notify the applicant. If approved, a "Publication Business License" shall be issued; if disapproved, the reasons therefor shall be stated.  
The "Publication Business License" shall indicate the type of audio-visual product business activities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　音像制品批发、零售单位变更名称、业务范围，或者兼并其他音像制品批发、零售单位，或者因合并、分立而设立新的音像制品批发、零售单位的，应当依照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定办理审批手续。  
音像制品批发、零售单位变更地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人或者终止经营活动，从事音像制品零售经营活动的个体工商户变更业务范围、地址或者终止经营活动的，应当向原批准的出版行政主管部门备案。

**Article 33** Where a wholesale or retail unit of audio-visual products alters its name or business scope, merges with another wholesale or retail unit of audio-visual products, or establishes a new wholesale or retail unit of audio-visual products through merger or division, it shall go through the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of these Regulations.  
Where a wholesale or retail unit of audio-visual products alters its address, legal representative or principal person in charge, or terminates its business activities, or where an individual industrial and commercial household engaged in audio-visual product retail business alters its business scope or address, or terminates its business activities, it shall report to the publishing administration department that originally approved the operation for the record.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　音像出版单位可以按照国家有关规定，批发、零售本单位出版的音像制品。从事非本单位出版的音像制品的批发、零售业务的，应当依照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定办理审批手续。

**Article 34** An audio-visual publishing unit may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, wholesale or retail the audio-visual products published by itself. Any unit engaging in the wholesale or retail business of audio-visual products not published by itself shall go through the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家允许设立从事音像制品发行业务的外商投资企业。

**Article 35** The State permits the establishment of foreign-invested enterprises engaged in the distribution business of audio-visual products.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　音像制品批发单位和从事音像制品零售、出租等业务的单位或者个体工商户，不得经营非音像出版单位出版的音像制品或者非音像复制单位复制的音像制品，不得经营未经国务院出版行政主管部门批准进口的音像制品，不得经营侵犯他人著作权的音像制品。

**Article 36** A wholesale unit of audio-visual products and a unit or individual industrial and commercial household engaged in retail, rental or other business of audio-visual products shall not deal in audio-visual products published by a non-audio-visual publishing unit or reproduced by a non-audio-visual reproduction unit, shall not deal in audio-visual products imported without the approval of the competent publishing administration department of the State Council, and shall not deal in audio-visual products that infringe upon others' copyright.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　出版行政主管部门或者其他有关行政部门及其工作人员，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处，批准不符合法定条件的申请人取得许可证、批准文件，或者不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予降级直至开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 37** Where the competent publishing administration department or any other relevant administrative department and its staff members, taking advantage of their positions, accept money or property or other benefits from others, approve applicants who do not meet the statutory conditions to obtain licenses or approval documents, or fail to perform their supervisory duties, or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, causing serious consequences, the persons in charge who are held responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions ranging from demotion to dismissal according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of acceptance of bribes, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty or other crimes.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　音像制品经营活动的监督管理部门的工作人员从事或者变相从事音像制品经营活动的，参与或者变相参与音像制品经营单位的经营活动的，依法给予撤职或者开除的处分。  
音像制品经营活动的监督管理部门有前款所列行为的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照前款规定处罚。

**Article 38** Where a staff member of the supervisory and administrative department for audio-visual product business activities engages in audio-visual product business activities or participates in the business activities of an audio-visual product business unit in a disguised form, he/she shall be given sanctions of removal from office or dismissal according to law.  
Where a supervisory and administrative department for audio-visual product business activities commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph, the persons in charge who are held responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　未经批准，擅自设立音像制品出版、进口单位，擅自从事音像制品出版、制作、复制业务或者进口、批发、零售经营活动的，由出版行政主管部门、工商行政管理部门依照法定职权予以取缔；依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，没收违法经营的音像制品和违法所得以及进行违法活动的专用工具、设备，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Anyone who, without approval, establishes a unit for publishing or importing audio-visual products without authorization, or engages in the publication, production, reproduction, import, wholesale or retail business of audio-visual products without authorization, shall be banned by the competent publishing administration department or the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with their statutory functions and powers; criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the audio-visual products illegally operated, the illegal gains, and the special tools and equipment used for the illegal activities shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed if the illegal gains amount to 50,000 yuan or more, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan shall be imposed if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　出版含有[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款禁止内容的音像制品，或者制作、复制、批发、零售、出租、放映明知或者应知含有[本条例第三条](#art-3)第二款禁止内容的音像制品的，依照刑法有关规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由出版行政主管部门、公安部门依据各自职权责令停业整顿，没收违法经营的音像制品和违法所得；违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 40** Anyone who publishes audio-visual products containing content prohibited by Paragraph 2 of Article 3 of these Regulations, or produces, reproduces, wholesales, retails, rents or shows audio-visual products that they know or should know contain content prohibited by Paragraph 2 of Article 3 of these Regulations, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the competent publishing administration department or the public security department shall, in accordance with their respective functions and powers, order the suspension of business for rectification, and confiscate the audio-visual products illegally operated and the illegal gains; and if the illegal business turnover is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover shall also be imposed; if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the license shall be revoked by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　走私音像制品的，依照刑法关于走私罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由海关依法给予行政处罚。

**Article 41** Whoever smuggles audio-visual products shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of smuggling; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the customs shall impose an administrative penalty according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法经营的音像制品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）音像出版单位向其他单位、个人出租、出借、出售或者以其他任何形式转让本单位的名称，出售或者以其他形式转让本单位的版号的；  
（二）音像出版单位委托未取得《音像制品制作许可证》的单位制作音像制品，或者委托未取得《复制经营许可证》的单位复制音像制品的；  
（三）音像出版单位出版未经国务院出版行政主管部门批准擅自进口的音像制品的；  
（四）音像制作单位、音像复制单位未依照本条例的规定验证音像出版单位的委托书、有关证明的；  
（五）音像复制单位擅自复制他人的音像制品，或者接受非音像出版单位、个人的委托复制经营性的音像制品，或者自行复制音像制品的。

**Article 42** If any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department of publication shall order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, confiscate the illegally operated audio-visual products and the illegal income; if the illegal income is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal income shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, the relevant production and business activities shall be restricted, a suspension of operations for rectification shall be ordered, or the original license-issuing authority shall reduce the relevant qualification grade until the license is revoked:  
(1) An audio-visual publishing unit leases, lends, sells, or transfers in any other form its name to another unit or individual, or sells or transfers in any other form its book number;  
(2) An audio-visual publishing unit entrusts a unit that has not obtained the "Audio-visual Product Production License" to produce audio-visual products, or entrusts a unit that has not obtained the "Reproduction Business License" to reproduce audio-visual products;  
(3) An audio-visual publishing unit publishes audio-visual products that have been imported without the approval of the administrative department of publication of the State Council;  
(4) An audio-visual production unit or audio-visual reproduction unit fails to verify the letter of entrustment and relevant certificates of the audio-visual publishing unit in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) An audio-visual reproduction unit reproduces another person's audio-visual products without authorization, or accepts the entrustment of a non-audio-visual publishing unit or individual to reproduce commercial audio-visual products, or reproduces audio-visual products on its own.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　音像出版单位违反国家有关规定与香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区或者外国的组织、个人合作制作音像制品，音像复制单位违反国家有关规定接受委托复制境外音像制品，未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核同意，或者未将复制的境外音像制品全部运输出境的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门责令改正，没收违法经营的音像制品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证。

**Article 43** If an audio-visual publishing unit, in violation of the relevant State regulations, cooperates with an organization or individual from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, the Taiwan Region, or a foreign country in producing audio-visual products, or an audio-visual reproduction unit, in violation of the relevant State regulations, accepts an entrustment to reproduce overseas audio-visual products without the examination and approval of the administrative department of publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or fails to transport all the reproduced overseas audio-visual products out of the country, the administrative department of publication of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall order correction, confiscate the illegally operated audio-visual products and the illegal income; if the illegal income is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal income shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, the relevant production and business activities shall be restricted, a suspension of operations for rectification shall be ordered, or the original license-issuing authority shall reduce the relevant qualification grade until the license is revoked.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，并责令停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
（一）音像出版单位未将其年度出版计划和涉及国家安全、社会安定等方面的重大选题报国务院出版行政主管部门备案的；  
（二）音像制品出版、制作、复制、批发、零售单位变更名称、地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人、业务范围等，未依照本条例规定办理审批、备案手续的；  
（三）音像出版单位未在其出版的音像制品及其包装的明显位置标明本条例规定的内容的；  
（四）音像出版单位未依照本条例的规定送交样本的；  
（五）音像复制单位未依照本条例的规定留存备查的材料的；  
（六）从事光盘复制的音像复制单位复制光盘，使用未蚀刻国务院出版行政主管部门核发的激光数码储存片来源识别码的注塑模具的。

**Article 44** If any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department of publication shall order correction and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a suspension of operations for rectification shall also be ordered or the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) An audio-visual publishing unit fails to report its annual publication plan and major topics involving national security, social stability, etc. to the administrative department of publication of the State Council for the record;  
(2) An audio-visual product publishing, production, reproduction, wholesale, or retail unit changes its name, address, legal representative or principal responsible person, business scope, etc., without going through the formalities of examination and approval or filing in accordance with these Regulations;  
(3) An audio-visual publishing unit fails to mark on the obvious position of its published audio-visual products and their packaging the contents as required by these Regulations;  
(4) An audio-visual publishing unit fails to submit samples in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) An audio-visual reproduction unit fails to retain the materials for reference as required by these Regulations;  
(6) An audio-visual reproduction unit engaged in disc reproduction uses an injection mold that is not etched with the source identification code of laser digital storage discs issued by the administrative department of publication of the State Council when reproducing discs.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法经营的音像制品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）批发、零售、出租、放映非音像出版单位出版的音像制品或者非音像复制单位复制的音像制品的；  
（二）批发、零售、出租或者放映未经国务院出版行政主管部门批准进口的音像制品的；  
（三）批发、零售、出租、放映供研究、教学参考或者用于展览、展示的进口音像制品的。

**Article 45** If any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department of publication shall order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, confiscate the illegally operated audio-visual products and the illegal income; if the illegal income is 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal income shall also be imposed; if there is no illegal income or the illegal income is less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, the relevant production and business activities shall be restricted, a suspension of operations for rectification shall be ordered, or the original license-issuing authority shall reduce the relevant qualification grade until the license is revoked:  
(1) Wholesaling, retailing, renting, or screening audio-visual products published by a non-audio-visual publishing unit or reproduced by a non-audio-visual reproduction unit;  
(2) Wholesaling, retailing, renting, or screening audio-visual products imported without the approval of the administrative department of publication of the State Council;  
(3) Wholesaling, retailing, renting, or screening imported audio-visual products intended for research, teaching reference, or for exhibition or display.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　单位违反本条例的规定，被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，其法定代表人或者主要负责人自许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得担任音像制品出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人。  
从事音像制品零售业务的个体工商户违反本条例的规定，被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，自许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得从事音像制品零售业务。

**Article 46** Where a unit violates the provisions of these Regulations and is subject to the administrative penalty of license revocation, its legal representative or principal responsible person shall not serve as the legal representative or principal responsible person of an audio-visual product publishing, production, reproduction, import, wholesale, or retail unit within 10 years from the date the license is revoked.  
Where an individual industrial or commercial household engaged in audio-visual product retail business violates the provisions of these Regulations and is subject to the administrative penalty of license revocation, it shall not engage in audio-visual product retail business within 10 years from the date the license is revoked.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款必须全部上缴国库。  
本条例所称违法所得，是指实施违法行为扣除成本后的获利数额，没有成本或者成本难以计算的，实施违法行为所取得的款项即为违法所得。

**Article 47** For an administrative penalty of fine imposed in accordance with these Regulations, the decision of fine and the collection of fine shall be separated in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; all fines collected must be turned over to the State Treasury in full.  
The illegal income as mentioned in these Regulations means the amount gained from the illegal act after deducting the costs; if there is no cost or the cost is difficult to calculate, the amount received from the illegal act shall be deemed as the illegal income.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　除[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)外，电子出版物的出版、制作、复制、进口、批发、零售等活动适用本条例。

**Article 48** Except for Article 35 of these Regulations, these Regulations shall apply to the publishing, production, reproduction, import, wholesale, and retail of electronic publications.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　依照本条例发放许可证，除按照法定标准收取成本费外，不得收取其他任何费用。

**Article 49** For the issuance of licenses in accordance with these Regulations, no other fees shall be charged except for the cost fees charged according to the statutory standards.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本条例自2002年2月1日起施行。1994年8月25日国务院发布的《音像制品管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 50** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2002. The "Regulations on the Administration of Audio-visual Products" promulgated by the State Council on August 25, 1994 shall be abolished simultaneously.
