# 广播电视设施保护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2000-11-05  
**生效日期**：2000-11-05

> (2000年11月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第295号公布　自公布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了维护广播电视设施的安全，确保广播电视信号顺利优质地播放和接收，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内依法设立的广播电视台、站(包括有线广播电视台、站，下同)和广播电视传输网的下列设施的保护，适用本条例： (一)广播电视信号发射设施，包括天线、馈线、塔桅(杆)、地网、卫星发射天线及其附属设备等； (二)广播电视信号专用传输设施，包括电缆线路、光缆线路(以下统称传输线路)、塔桅(杆)、微波等空中专用传输通路、微波站、卫星地面接收设施、转播设备及其附属设备等； (三)广播电视信号监测设施，包括监测接收天线、馈线、塔桅(杆)、测向场强室及其附属设备等。 传输广播电视信号所利用的公用通信等网络设施的保护和管理，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。 第三条　县级以上人民政府应当将广播电视设施的规划和保护纳入城乡建设总体规划，并加强广播电视设施保护的宣传教育工作。 县级以上人民政府负责广播电视行政管理工作的部门或者机构(以下统称广播电视行政管理部门)负责所管辖的广播电视设施的保护工作，并采取措施，确保广播电视设施的安全。 第四条　任何单位和个人均有保护广播电视设施的义务。 禁止任何单位和个人侵占、哄抢、私分、截留、破坏广播电视设施。 任何单位和个人对危害广播电视设施的行为，均有权制止并向有关部门报告。 第二章　保护措施 第五条　广播电视设施管理单位负责广播电视设施的维护和保养，保证其正常运行。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了维护广播电视设施的安全，确保广播电视信号顺利优质地播放和接收，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of maintaining the safety of radio and television facilities and ensuring the smooth and high-quality broadcast and reception of radio and television signals.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内依法设立的广播电视台、站(包括有线广播电视台、站，下同)和广播电视传输网的下列设施的保护，适用本条例：  
(一)广播电视信号发射设施，包括天线、馈线、塔桅(杆)、地网、卫星发射天线及其附属设备等；  
(二)广播电视信号专用传输设施，包括电缆线路、光缆线路(以下统称传输线路)、塔桅(杆)、微波等空中专用传输通路、微波站、卫星地面接收设施、转播设备及其附属设备等；  
(三)广播电视信号监测设施，包括监测接收天线、馈线、塔桅(杆)、测向场强室及其附属设备等。  
传输广播电视信号所利用的公用通信等网络设施的保护和管理，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the protection of the following facilities of broadcasting and television stations (including cable broadcasting and television stations, the same below) and broadcasting and television transmission networks lawfully established within the territory of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Radio and television signal transmission facilities, including antennas, feeders, towers (poles), ground nets, satellite transmitting antennas and their auxiliary equipment, etc.;  
(2) Radio and television signal dedicated transmission facilities, including cable lines, optical cable lines (hereinafter collectively referred to as transmission lines), towers (poles), microwave and other dedicated air transmission paths, microwave stations, satellite ground receiving facilities, relay equipment and their auxiliary equipment, etc.;  
(3) Radio and television signal monitoring facilities, including monitoring receiving antennas, feeders, towers (poles), direction-finding field strength rooms and their auxiliary equipment, etc.  
The protection and management of public communication and other network facilities used for transmitting radio and television signals shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当将广播电视设施的规划和保护纳入城乡建设总体规划，并加强广播电视设施保护的宣传教育工作。  
县级以上人民政府负责广播电视行政管理工作的部门或者机构(以下统称广播电视行政管理部门)负责所管辖的广播电视设施的保护工作，并采取措施，确保广播电视设施的安全。

**Article 3** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the planning and protection of radio and television facilities into the overall urban and rural construction plan, and strengthen the publicity and education on the protection of radio and television facilities.  
The departments or organs of the people's governments at or above the county level that are responsible for the administration of radio and television (hereinafter collectively referred to as the radio and television administrative departments) shall be responsible for the protection of radio and television facilities under their jurisdiction, and shall take measures to ensure the safety of radio and television facilities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　任何单位和个人均有保护广播电视设施的义务。  
禁止任何单位和个人侵占、哄抢、私分、截留、破坏广播电视设施。  
任何单位和个人对危害广播电视设施的行为，均有权制止并向有关部门报告。

**Article 4** Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to protect radio and television facilities.  
No unit or individual may occupy, loot, privately distribute, intercept or damage radio and television facilities.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to stop any act that endangers radio and television facilities and report it to the relevant department.

### 第二章　保护措施

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　广播电视设施管理单位负责广播电视设施的维护和保养，保证其正常运行。  
广播电视设施管理单位应当在广播电视设施周围设立保护标志，标明保护要求。

**Article 5** The unit managing radio and television facilities shall be responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of such facilities and ensure their normal operation.  
The unit managing radio and television facilities shall set up protection signs around the facilities, indicating the protection requirements.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　禁止危及广播电视信号发射设施的安全和损害其使用效能的下列行为：  
(一)拆除或者损坏天线、馈线、地网以及天线场地的围墙、围网及其附属设备、标志物；  
(二)在中波天线周围250米范围内建筑施工，或者以天线外250米为计算起点兴建高度超过仰角3度的高大建筑；  
(三)在短波天线前方500米范围内种植成林树木、堆放金属物品、穿越架空电力线路、建筑施工，或者以天线外500米为计算起点兴建高度超过仰角3度的高大建筑；  
(四)在功率300千瓦以上的定向天线前方1000米范围内建筑施工，或者以天线外1000米为计算起点兴建高度超过仰角3度的高大建筑；  
(五)在馈线两侧各3米范围内建筑施工，或者在馈线两侧各5米范围内种植树木、种植高杆作物；  
(六)在天线、塔桅(杆)周围5米或者可能危及拉锚安全的范围内挖沙、取土、钻探、打桩、倾倒腐蚀性物品。

**Article 6** The following acts that endanger the safety of radio and television signal transmission facilities or impair their service effectiveness are prohibited:  
(1) Removing or damaging antennas, feeders, ground nets, and the enclosing walls, fencing nets and their auxiliary equipment and markers of antenna sites;  
(2) Carrying out construction within a radius of 250 meters of a medium wave antenna, or building a tall structure with a height exceeding an elevation angle of 3 degrees starting from 250 meters outside the antenna;  
(3) Planting trees in a forest, stacking metal objects, crossing overhead power lines, or carrying out construction within a range of 500 meters in front of a short wave antenna, or building a tall structure with a height exceeding an elevation angle of 3 degrees starting from 500 meters outside the antenna;  
(4) Carrying out construction within a range of 1,000 meters in front of a directional antenna with a power of 300 kilowatts or more, or building a tall structure with a height exceeding an elevation angle of 3 degrees starting from 1,000 meters outside the antenna;  
(5) Carrying out construction within 3 meters on each side of a feeder, or planting trees or tall crops within 5 meters on each side of a feeder;  
(6) Digging sand, removing earth, drilling, piling, or dumping corrosive substances within 5 meters around an antenna or tower (pole), or within a range that may endanger the safety of anchor ropes.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　禁止危及广播电视信号专用传输设施的安全和损害其使用效能的下列行为：  
(一)在标志埋设地下传输线路两侧各5米和水下传输线路两侧各50米范围内进行铺设易燃易爆液(气)体主管道、抛锚、拖锚、挖沙等施工作业；  
(二)移动、损坏传输线路、终端杆、塔桅(杆)及其附属设备、标志物；  
(三)在标志埋设地下传输线路的地面周围1米范围内种植根茎可能缠绕传输线路的植物、倾倒腐蚀性物品；  
(四)树木的顶端与架空传输线路的间距小于2米；  
(五)在传输线路塔桅(杆)、拉线周围1米范围内挖沙、取土，或者在其周围5米范围内倾倒腐蚀性物品、堆放易燃易爆物品；  
(六)在传输线路塔桅(杆)、拉线上拴系牲畜、悬挂物品、攀附农作物。

**Article 7** The following acts that endanger the safety of radio and television signal dedicated transmission facilities or impair their service effectiveness are prohibited:  
(1) Laying main pipelines for flammable and explosive liquids (gases), anchoring, dragging anchors, digging sand, or other construction operations within 5 meters on each side of the marked underground transmission lines and within 50 meters on each side of underwater transmission lines;  
(2) Moving or damaging transmission lines, terminal poles, towers (poles) and their auxiliary equipment and markers;  
(3) Planting plants whose roots may entangle transmission lines or dumping corrosive substances within a radius of 1 meter on the ground surface around the marked underground transmission lines;  
(4) The distance between the top of trees and overhead transmission lines being less than 2 meters;  
(5) Digging sand or removing earth within a radius of 1 meter around transmission line towers (poles) or guy wires, or dumping corrosive substances or stacking flammable and explosive articles within a radius of 5 meters thereof;  
(6) Tying livestock, hanging objects, or attaching crops to transmission line towers (poles) or guy wires.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　禁止危及广播电视信号监测设施的安全和损害其使用效能的下列行为：  
(一)移动、损坏监测接收天线、塔桅(杆)及其附属设备、标志物；  
(二)在监测台、站周围违反国家标准架设架空电力线路，兴建电气化铁路、公路等产生电磁辐射的设施或者设置金属构件；  
(三)在监测台、站测向场强室周围150米范围内种植树木、高杆作物、进行对土地平坦有影响的挖掘、施工；  
(四)在监测天线周围1000米范围内建筑施工，或者以天线外1000米为计算起点修建高度超过仰角3度的建筑物、构筑物或者堆放超高的物品。

**Article 8** The following acts that endanger the safety of radio and television signal monitoring facilities or impair their service effectiveness are prohibited:  
(1) Moving or damaging monitoring receiving antennas, towers (poles) and their auxiliary equipment and markers;  
(2) Erecting overhead power lines, constructing electrified railways, highways or other facilities generating electromagnetic radiation, or installing metal components around monitoring stations or sites in violation of national standards;  
(3) Planting trees or tall crops, or carrying out excavation or construction that affects the flatness of the land within a radius of 150 meters around the direction-finding field strength room of a monitoring station or site;  
(4) Carrying out construction within a radius of 1,000 meters around a monitoring antenna, or building a structure or installation with a height exceeding an elevation angle of 3 degrees starting from 1,000 meters outside the antenna, or stacking articles exceeding the height limit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　禁止危及广播电视设施安全和损害其使用效能的下列行为：  
(一)在广播电视设施周围500米范围内进行爆破作业；  
(二)在天线、馈线、传输线路及其塔桅(杆)、拉线周围500米范围内进行烧荒；  
(三)在卫星天线前方50米范围内建筑施工，或者以天线前方50米为计算起点修建高度超过仰角5度的建筑物、构筑物或者堆放超高的物品；  
(四)在发射、监测台、站周围1500米范围内兴建有严重粉尘污染、严重腐蚀性化学气体溢出或者产生放射性物质的设施；  
(五)在发射、监测台、站周围500米范围内兴建油库、加油站、液化气站、煤气站等易燃易爆设施。

**Article 9** The following acts that endanger the safety of radio and television facilities or impair their service effectiveness are prohibited:  
(1) Carrying out blasting operations within a radius of 500 meters around radio and television facilities;  
(2) Burning wasteland within a radius of 500 meters around antennas, feeders, transmission lines and their towers (poles) or guy wires;  
(3) Carrying out construction within a range of 50 meters in front of a satellite antenna, or building a structure or installation with a height exceeding an elevation angle of 5 degrees starting from 50 meters in front of the antenna, or stacking articles exceeding the height limit;  
(4) Constructing facilities with serious dust pollution, serious corrosive chemical gas emission, or radioactive substance generation within a radius of 1,500 meters around transmitting or monitoring stations or sites;  
(5) Constructing flammable and explosive facilities such as oil depots, gas stations, liquefied gas stations, or gas stations within a radius of 500 meters around transmitting or monitoring stations or sites.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　新建、扩建广播电视设施，应当遵守城乡建设总体规划，按照国家有关规定选址，避开各种干扰源。

**Article 10** The construction or expansion of radio and television facilities shall comply with the overall urban and rural construction plan, and the site selection shall be made in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, avoiding various interference sources.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　广播电视信号发射设施的建设，应当符合国家有关电磁波防护和卫生标准；在已有发射设施的场强区内，兴建机关、工厂、学校、商店、居民住宅等设施的，除应当遵守本条例有关规定外，还应当符合国家有关电磁波防护和卫生标准。

**Article 11** The construction of radio and television signal transmission facilities shall conform to the national standards for electromagnetic wave protection and sanitation; within the field strength area of existing transmission facilities, the construction of facilities such as government offices, factories, schools, shops, and residential buildings shall not only comply with the relevant provisions of these Regulations but also conform to the national standards for electromagnetic wave protection and sanitation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在标志埋设广播电视传输线路两侧2米范围内堆放笨重物品、种植树木及平整土地的，应当事先征得广播电视设施管理单位的同意，并采取有效防范措施后，方可进行。

**Article 12** Where heavy articles are stacked, trees are planted, or land is leveled within a range of 2 meters on each side of the marked radio and television transmission lines, consent shall first be obtained from the unit managing the radio and television facilities, and effective preventive measures shall be taken before such activities may be carried out.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在天线、馈线周围500米范围外进行烧荒等活动，可能危及广播电视设施安全的，应当事先通知广播电视设施管理单位，并采取有效防范措施后，方可进行。

**Article 13** Where activities such as burning wasteland are carried out beyond a radius of 500 meters around antennas or feeders and may endanger the safety of radio and television facilities, the unit managing the radio and television facilities shall be notified in advance, and effective preventive measures shall be taken before such activities may be carried out.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在天线、馈线周围种植树木或者农作物的，应当确保巡视、维修车辆的通行；巡视、维修车辆通行，对树木或者农作物造成损失的，由广播电视设施管理单位按照国家有关规定给予补偿。  
对高度超越架空传输线路保护间距要求的树木，广播电视设施管理单位有权剪除其超越部分。

**Article 14** Where trees or crops are planted around antennas or feeders, the passage of inspection and maintenance vehicles shall be ensured; if the passage of inspection and maintenance vehicles causes damage to the trees or crops, the unit managing the radio and television facilities shall provide compensation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For trees whose height exceeds the protection clearance requirements for overhead transmission lines, the unit managing the radio and television facilities shall have the right to trim the parts that exceed the limit.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在广播电视传输线路上接挂收听、收视设备，调整、安装有线广播电视的光分配器、分支放大器等设备，或者在有线广播电视设备上插接分支分配器、其他线路的，应当经广播电视设施管理单位同意，并由专业人员安装。

**Article 15** Connecting listening or viewing devices to radio and television transmission lines, adjusting or installing optical splitters, branch amplifiers or other equipment for cable radio and television, or plugging in branch splitters or other lines onto cable radio and television equipment shall be subject to the consent of the unit managing the radio and television facilities and shall be installed by professional personnel.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在天线场地敷设电力、通讯线路或者在架空传输线路上附挂电力、通讯线路的，应当事先征得广播电视设施管理单位同意，并在专业人员的指导下进行施工。

**Article 16** Laying power or communication lines in an antenna site or attaching power or communication lines onto overhead transmission lines shall first subject to the consent of the unit managing the radio and television facilities, and the construction shall be carried out under the guidance of professional personnel.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　广播电视设施管理单位的专用供电、供水、通信等，有关部门或者单位应当予以保障。  
广播电视设施管理单位应当按照有关广播电视行政管理部门的要求，对重要的广播电视设施配备备用电源、水源等设施。

**Article 17** The relevant departments or units shall guarantee the dedicated power supply, water supply, and communication, etc., of the unit managing radio and television facilities.  
The unit managing radio and television facilities shall, in accordance with the requirements of the relevant radio and television administrative department, equip important radio and television facilities with backup power supply, water supply and other facilities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　进行工程建设，应当尽量避开广播电视设施；重大工程项目确实无法避开而需要搬迁广播电视设施的，城市规划行政主管部门在审批前，应当征得有关广播电视行政管理部门同意。  
迁建工作应当坚持先建设后拆除的原则。迁建所需费用由造成广播电视设施迁建的单位承担。迁建新址的技术参数应当按照国家有关规定报批。

**Article 18** Project construction shall avoid radio and television facilities as far as possible; where a major project cannot avoid them and relocation of such facilities is necessary, the department in charge of urban planning administration shall, before approval, obtain the consent of the relevant radio and television administrative department.  
The relocation shall adhere to the principle of constructing first and then demolishing. The costs required for the relocation shall be borne by the unit that causes the relocation of the radio and television facilities. The technical parameters of the new relocation site shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　确需在已有广播电视信号空中专用传输通路内兴建建设工程的，城市规划行政主管部门在审批前，应当征得有关广播电视行政管理部门同意。  
因建设工程阻挡空中专用传输通路，需要建立广播电视空中信号中继站的，建设单位应当承担所需费用并给予相应的经济补偿。

**Article 19** Where it is truly necessary to construct a project within an existing dedicated air transmission path for radio and television signals, the department in charge of urban planning administration shall, before approval, obtain the consent of the relevant radio and television administrative department.  
If the construction project blocks the dedicated air transmission path and requires the establishment of a radio and television air signal relay station, the construction unit shall bear the necessary costs and provide corresponding economic compensation.

### 第三章　罚则

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反本条例规定，在广播电视设施保护范围内进行建筑施工、兴建设施或者爆破作业、烧荒等活动的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政管理部门或者其授权的广播电视设施管理单位责令改正，限期拆除违章建筑、设施，对个人处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员及其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；违反治安管理规定的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Any unit or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, carries out construction, builds facilities, or conducts blasting operations, burns wasteland, or other activities within the protected range of radio and television facilities, shall be ordered by the radio and television administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the unit authorized by it to make corrections and demolish the illegal buildings and facilities within a specified time limit; a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on an individual, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on a unit; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act violates public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　违反本条例规定，损坏广播电视设施的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政管理部门或者其授权的广播电视设施管理单位责令改正，对个人处1000元以上1万元以下的罚款，对单位处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；对其直接负责的主管人员及其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；违反治安管理规定的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Any unit or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, damages radio and television facilities, shall be ordered by the radio and television administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the unit authorized by it to make corrections; a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed on an individual, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on a unit; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the act violates public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反本条例规定，在广播电视设施保护范围内有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政管理部门或者其授权的广播电视设施管理单位责令改正，给予警告，对个人可处以2000元以下的罚款，对单位可处以2万元以下的罚款：  
(一)种植树木、农作物的；  
(二)堆放金属物品、易燃易爆物品或者设置金属构件、倾倒腐蚀性物品的；  
(三)钻探、打桩、抛锚、拖锚、挖沙、取土的；  
(四)拴系牲畜、悬挂物品、攀附农作物的。

**Article 22** Any unit or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts within the protected range of radio and television facilities, shall be ordered by the radio and television administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the unit authorized by it to make corrections and given a warning; an individual may be fined not more than 2,000 yuan, and a unit may be fined not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) Planting trees or crops;  
(2) Stacking metal articles, flammable and explosive articles, or installing metal components, or dumping corrosive substances;  
(3) Drilling, piling, anchoring, dragging anchors, digging sand, or removing earth;  
(4) Tying livestock, hanging objects, or attaching crops.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反本条例规定，未经同意，擅自实施下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府广播电视行政管理部门或者其授权的广播电视设施管理单位责令改正，对个人可处以2000元以下的罚款，对单位可处以1万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在广播电视传输线路保护范围内堆放笨重物品、种植树木、平整土地的；  
(二)在天线、馈线保护范围外进行烧荒等的；  
(三)在广播电视传输线路上接挂、调整、安装、插接收听、收视设备的；  
(四)在天线场地敷设或者在架空传输线路上附挂电力、通信线路的。

**Article 23** Any unit or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, without consent, commits any of the following acts, shall be ordered by the radio and television administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the unit authorized by it to make corrections; an individual may be fined not more than 2,000 yuan, and a unit may be fined not more than 10,000 yuan:  
(1) Stacking heavy articles, planting trees, or leveling land within the protected range of radio and television transmission lines;  
(2) Burning wasteland, etc., outside the protected range of antennas or feeders;  
(3) Connecting, adjusting, installing, or plugging in listening or viewing devices on radio and television transmission lines;  
(4) Laying power or communication lines in an antenna site or attaching them onto overhead transmission lines.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反本条例规定，未经城市规划主管部门批准和广播电视行政管理部门同意，擅自进行建设工程的，由城市规划主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定给予处罚。

**Article 24** Any unit or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, constructs a project without the approval of the department in charge of urban planning administration and the consent of the radio and television administrative department, shall be subject to penalties imposed by the department in charge of urban planning administration in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　广播电视行政管理部门、城市规划主管部门、公安机关的工作人员违反本条例规定，滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊，造成广播电视设施严重损害或者严重影响其使用效能，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 25** Any staff member of the radio and television administrative department, the department in charge of urban planning administration, or the public security organ who, in violation of these Regulations, abuses power, neglects duty, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, causing serious damage to radio and television facilities or seriously affecting their service effectiveness, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　损坏广播电视设施无法恢复原状的，当事人应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 26** If radio and television facilities are damaged and cannot be restored to their original condition, the party concerned shall bear compensation liability according to law.

### 第四章　附则

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自公布之日起施行。1987年4月24日国务院发布的《广播电视设施保护条例》同时废止。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Regulations on the Protection of Radio and Television Facilities promulgated by the State Council on April 24, 1987 shall be abolished simultaneously.
