### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强印刷业管理，维护印刷业经营者的合法权益和社会公共利益，促进社会主义精神文明和物质文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of the printing industry, safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of printing industry operators and the public interest, and promoting the construction of socialist spiritual civilization and material civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于出版物、包装装潢印刷品和其他印刷品的印刷经营活动。  
本条例所称出版物，包括报纸、期刊、书籍、地图、年画、图片、挂历、画册及音像制品、电子出版物的装帧封面等。  
本条例所称包装装潢印刷品，包括商标标识、广告宣传品及作为产品包装装潢的纸、金属、塑料等的印刷品。  
本条例所称其他印刷品，包括文件、资料、图表、票证、证件、名片等。  
本条例所称印刷经营活动，包括经营性的排版、制版、印刷、装订、复印、影印、打印等活动。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the printing business activities of publications, packaging and decoration printed matters, and other printed matters.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "publications" include newspapers, periodicals, books, maps, New Year pictures, pictures, wall calendars, picture albums, and the binding covers of audio-visual products and electronic publications, etc.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "packaging and decoration printed matters" include trademark logos, advertising materials, and printed matters made of paper, metal, plastic, etc. used as product packaging and decoration.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "other printed matters" include documents, materials, charts, tickets, certificates, business cards, etc.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "printing business activities" include commercial typesetting, plate-making, printing, binding, copying, photo copying, printing, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　印刷业经营者必须遵守有关法律、法规和规章，讲求社会效益。  
禁止印刷含有反动、淫秽、迷信内容和国家明令禁止印刷的其他内容的出版物、包装装潢印刷品和其他印刷品。

**Article 3** Printing industry operators must comply with relevant laws, regulations and rules, and give due consideration to social benefits.  
It shall be prohibited to print publications, packaging and decoration printed matters and other printed matters which contain reactionary, obscene or superstitious content, or other content the printing of which is expressly prohibited by the State.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院出版行政部门主管全国的印刷业监督管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府负责出版管理的行政部门（以下简称出版行政部门）负责本行政区域内的印刷业监督管理工作。  
县级以上各级人民政府公安部门、工商行政管理部门及其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的印刷业监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The publishing administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of the supervision and administration of the printing industry nationwide. The administrative departments in charge of publishing under the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the publishing administrative departments) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the printing industry within their respective administrative regions.  
The public security departments, administrative departments for industry and commerce, and other relevant departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the printing industry within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　印刷业经营者应当建立、健全承印验证制度、承印登记制度、印刷品保管制度、印刷品交付制度、印刷活动残次品销毁制度等。具体办法由国务院出版行政部门制定。  
印刷业经营者在印刷经营活动中发现违法犯罪行为，应当及时向公安部门或者出版行政部门报告。

**Article 5** Printing industry operators shall establish and improve the system of verification of print orders, the system of registration of print orders, the system of safekeeping of printed matters, the system of delivery of printed matters, and the system of destruction of defective products from printing activities, among others. The specific measures shall be formulated by the publishing administrative department of the State Council.  
Where a printing industry operator discovers any illegal or criminal act during its printing business activities, it shall promptly report the matter to the public security department or the publishing administrative department.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　印刷行业的社会团体按照其章程，在出版行政部门的指导下，实行自律管理。

**Article 6** Social organizations of the printing industry shall, in accordance with their articles of association and under the guidance of the publishing administrative departments, exercise self-discipline management.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　印刷企业应当定期向出版行政部门报送年度报告。出版行政部门应当依法及时将年度报告中的有关内容向社会公示。

**Article 7** Printing enterprises shall periodically submit annual reports to the publishing administrative departments. The publishing administrative departments shall, in accordance with law, promptly disclose the relevant contents of the annual reports to the public.

### 第二章　印刷企业的设立

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实行印刷经营许可制度。未依照本条例规定取得印刷经营许可证的，任何单位和个人不得从事印刷经营活动。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a licensing system for printing business operations. No entity or individual may engage in printing business activities without obtaining a printing operation license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　企业从事印刷经营活动，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有企业的名称、章程；  
（二）有确定的业务范围；  
（三）有适应业务范围需要的生产经营场所和必要的资金、设备等生产经营条件；  
（四）有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和人员；  
（五）有关法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
审批从事印刷经营活动申请，除依照前款规定外，还应当符合国家有关印刷企业总量、结构和布局的规划。

**Article 9** An enterprise engaging in printing business activities shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Having a name and articles of association;  
(2) Having a defined business scope;  
(3) Having a place of production and business operation that meets the needs of the business scope, and the necessary production and business operation conditions such as funds and equipment;  
(4) Having an organizational structure and personnel that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
In examining and approving an application for engaging in printing business activities, in addition to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the application shall also comply with the State's planning regarding the total quantity, structure and layout of printing enterprises.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　企业申请从事出版物印刷经营活动，应当持营业执照向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门提出申请，经审核批准的，发给印刷经营许可证。  
企业申请从事包装装潢印刷品和其他印刷品印刷经营活动，应当持营业执照向所在地设区的市级人民政府出版行政部门提出申请，经审核批准的，发给印刷经营许可证。  
个人不得从事出版物、包装装潢印刷品印刷经营活动；个人从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的，依照本条第二款的规定办理审批手续。

**Article 10** Where an enterprise applies to engage in the printing business of publications, it shall submit an application, with its business license, to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located; upon examination and approval, a printing operation license shall be issued.  
Where an enterprise applies to engage in the printing business of packaging and decoration printed matters and other printed matters, it shall submit an application, with its business license, to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located; upon examination and approval, a printing operation license shall be issued.  
No individual may engage in the printing business of publications or packaging and decoration printed matters; where an individual engages in the printing business of other printed matters, the examination and approval formalities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　出版行政部门应当自收到依据[本条例第十条](#art-10)提出的申请之日起60日内作出批准或者不批准的决定。批准申请的，应当发给印刷经营许可证；不批准申请的，应当通知申请人并说明理由。  
印刷经营许可证应当注明印刷企业所从事的印刷经营活动的种类。  
印刷经营许可证不得出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让。

**Article 11** The publishing administrative department shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of an application submitted in accordance with Article 10 of these Regulations, make a decision to approve or disapprove the application. If the application is approved, a printing operation license shall be issued; if the application is not approved, the applicant shall be notified and the reasons shall be stated.  
The printing operation license shall specify the type of printing business activities engaged in by the printing enterprise.  
No printing operation license may be sold, leased, lent or transferred in any other form.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　印刷业经营者申请兼营或者变更从事出版物、包装装潢印刷品或者其他印刷品印刷经营活动，或者兼并其他印刷业经营者，或者因合并、分立而设立新的印刷业经营者，应当依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)的规定办理手续。  
印刷业经营者变更名称、法定代表人或者负责人、住所或者经营场所等主要登记事项，或者终止印刷经营活动，应当报原批准设立的出版行政部门备案。

**Article 12** Where a printing industry operator applies to concurrently engage in or change its printing business activities of publications, packaging and decoration printed matters or other printed matters, or to merge with another printing industry operator, or to establish a new printing industry operator through merger or division, it shall go through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 9 of these Regulations.  
Where a printing industry operator changes its name, legal representative or person in charge, domicile or business place, or other major registration items, or terminates its printing business activities, it shall report to the publishing administrative department that originally approved its establishment for archival filing.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　出版行政部门应当按照国家社会信用信息平台建设的总体要求，与公安部门、工商行政管理部门或者其他有关部门实现对印刷企业信息的互联共享。

**Article 13** The publishing administrative departments shall, in accordance with the overall requirements for the construction of the national social credit information platform, achieve interconnection and sharing of printing enterprise information with the public security departments, administrative departments for industry and commerce, or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家允许外国投资者与中国投资者共同投资设立从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业，允许设立从事包装装潢印刷品和其他印刷品印刷经营活动的外商投资企业。

**Article 14** The State permits foreign investors to invest jointly with Chinese investors in the establishment of enterprises engaged in the printing business of publications, and permits the establishment of foreign-invested enterprises engaged in the printing business of packaging and decoration printed matters and other printed matters.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　单位内部设立印刷厂（所），必须向所在地县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门办理登记手续；单位内部设立的印刷厂（所）印刷涉及国家秘密的印件的，还应当向保密工作部门办理登记手续。  
单位内部设立的印刷厂（所）不得从事印刷经营活动；从事印刷经营活动的，必须依照本章的规定办理手续。

**Article 15** Where a printing factory (shop) is established within an entity, it must go through registration formalities with the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; if such printing factory (shop) prints materials involving State secrets, it shall also go through registration formalities with the secrecy administrative department.  
A printing factory (shop) established within an entity may not engage in printing business activities; if it engages in printing business activities, it must go through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

### 第三章　出版物的印刷

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业及时印刷体现国内外新的优秀文化成果的出版物，重视印刷传统文化精品和有价值的学术著作。

**Article 16** The State encourages enterprises engaged in the printing business of publications to timely print publications that reflect outstanding new cultural achievements at home and abroad, and to attach importance to printing fine traditional cultural works and valuable academic works.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业不得印刷国家明令禁止出版的出版物和非出版单位出版的出版物。

**Article 17** An enterprise engaged in the printing business of publications may not print publications the publication of which is expressly prohibited by the State, or publications printed by non-publishing entities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　印刷出版物的，委托印刷单位和印刷企业应当按照国家有关规定签订印刷合同。

**Article 18** For the printing of publications, the entrusting printing entity and the printing enterprise shall sign a printing contract in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　印刷企业接受出版单位委托印刷图书、期刊的，必须验证并收存出版单位盖章的印刷委托书，并在印刷前报出版单位所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门备案；印刷企业接受所在地省、自治区、直辖市以外的出版单位的委托印刷图书、期刊的，印刷委托书还必须事先报印刷企业所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门备案。印刷委托书由国务院出版行政部门规定统一格式，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门统一印制。  
印刷企业接受出版单位委托印刷报纸的，必须验证报纸出版许可证；接受出版单位的委托印刷报纸、期刊的增版、增刊的，还必须验证主管的出版行政部门批准出版增版、增刊的文件。

**Article 19** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment of a publishing entity to print books or periodicals, it must verify and keep the printing entrustment letter sealed by the publishing entity, and report to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the publishing entity is located for archival filing before printing; where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment of a publishing entity from outside its own province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government to print books or periodicals, the printing entrustment letter must also be reported to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the printing enterprise is located for archival filing in advance. The uniform format of the printing entrustment letter shall be prescribed by the publishing administrative department of the State Council, and uniformly printed by the publishing administrative departments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment of a publishing entity to print newspapers, it must verify the newspaper publishing license; if it accepts the entrustment of a publishing entity to print supplements or extra issues of newspapers or periodicals, it must also verify the document approving the publication of such supplements or extra issues issued by the competent publishing administrative department.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　印刷企业接受委托印刷内部资料性出版物的，必须验证县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门核发的准印证。  
印刷企业接受委托印刷宗教内容的内部资料性出版物的，必须验证省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务管理部门的批准文件和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门核发的准印证。  
出版行政部门应当自收到印刷内部资料性出版物或者印刷宗教内容的内部资料性出版物的申请之日起30日内作出是否核发准印证的决定，并通知申请人；逾期不作出决定的，视为同意印刷。

**Article 20** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print an internal reference publication, it must verify the permit certificate issued by the local publishing administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level.  
Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print an internal reference publication with religious content, it must verify the approval document issued by the religious affairs administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government and the permit certificate issued by the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The publishing administrative department shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of an application for printing an internal reference publication or an internal reference publication with religious content, make a decision whether to issue a permit certificate and notify the applicant; if no decision is made within the time limit, the printing shall be deemed to have been approved.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　印刷企业接受委托印刷境外的出版物的，必须持有关著作权的合法证明文件，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门批准；印刷的境外出版物必须全部运输出境，不得在境内发行、散发。

**Article 21** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print overseas publications, it must hold lawful certification documents concerning copyright and obtain approval from the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; all printed overseas publications must be transported out of the country and may not be distributed or disseminated within the country.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　委托印刷单位必须按照国家有关规定在委托印刷的出版物上刊载出版单位的名称、地址，书号、刊号或者版号，出版日期或者刊期，接受委托印刷出版物的企业的真实名称和地址，以及其他有关事项。  
印刷企业应当自完成出版物的印刷之日起2年内，留存一份接受委托印刷的出版物样本备查。

**Article 22** The entrusting printing entity must, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, print on the entrusted publications the name and address of the publishing entity, the book number, periodical number or edition number, the publication date or periodical issue, the true name and address of the enterprise accepting the entrustment to print the publications, and other relevant information.  
The printing enterprise shall, within two years from the date of completion of the printing of the publications, retain one sample of the entrusted publications for future reference.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　印刷企业不得盗印出版物，不得销售、擅自加印或者接受第三人委托加印受委托印刷的出版物，不得将接受委托印刷的出版物纸型及印刷底片等出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让给其他单位或者个人。

**Article 23** A printing enterprise may not pirate publications, sell, print additional copies without authorization, or accept entrustment from a third party to print additional copies of the publications it is entrusted to print, nor may it sell, lease, lend or otherwise transfer the paper plates, printing negatives, etc. of the entrusted publications to any other entity or individual.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　印刷企业不得征订、销售出版物，不得假冒或者盗用他人名义印刷、销售出版物。

**Article 24** A printing enterprise may not solicit subscriptions for or sell publications, nor may it print or sell publications under the false or unauthorized name of another party.

### 第四章　包装装潢印刷品的印刷

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　从事包装装潢印刷品印刷的企业不得印刷假冒、伪造的注册商标标识，不得印刷容易对消费者产生误导的广告宣传品和作为产品包装装潢的印刷品。

**Article 25** An enterprise engaged in the printing of packaging and decoration printed matters may not print counterfeit or forged registered trademark logos, nor may it print advertising materials or printed matters used as product packaging and decoration that are likely to mislead consumers.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　印刷企业接受委托印刷注册商标标识的，应当验证商标注册人所在地县级工商行政管理部门签章的《商标注册证》复印件，并核查委托人提供的注册商标图样；接受注册商标被许可使用人委托，印刷注册商标标识的，印刷企业还应当验证注册商标使用许可合同。印刷企业应当保存其验证、核查的工商行政管理部门签章的《商标注册证》复印件、注册商标图样、注册商标使用许可合同复印件2年，以备查验。  
国家对注册商标标识的印刷另有规定的，印刷企业还应当遵守其规定。

**Article 26** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print registered trademark logos, it shall verify the copy of the "Trademark Registration Certificate" stamped by the administrative department for industry and commerce at the county level where the trademark registrant is located, and check the registered trademark pattern provided by the principal; where the printing enterprise accepts the entrustment of a licensee of the registered trademark to print the registered trademark logos, it shall also verify the trademark license contract. The printing enterprise shall keep the verified copy of the "Trademark Registration Certificate" stamped by the administrative department for industry and commerce, the registered trademark pattern, and the copy of the trademark license contract for two years for inspection.  
Where the State has other provisions concerning the printing of registered trademark logos, the printing enterprise shall also comply with such provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　印刷企业接受委托印刷广告宣传品、作为产品包装装潢的印刷品的，应当验证委托印刷单位的营业执照或者个人的居民身份证；接受广告经营者的委托印刷广告宣传品的，还应当验证广告经营资格证明。

**Article 27** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print advertising materials or printed matters used as product packaging and decoration, it shall verify the business license of the entrusting entity or the resident identity card of the individual; where it accepts the entrustment of an advertising operator to print advertising materials, it shall also verify the advertising business qualification certificate.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　印刷企业接受委托印刷包装装潢印刷品的，应当将印刷品的成品、半成品、废品和印板、纸型、底片、原稿等全部交付委托印刷单位或者个人，不得擅自留存。

**Article 28** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print packaging and decoration printed matters, it shall deliver all finished products, semi-finished products, waste products, printing plates, paper molds, negatives, original manuscripts, etc. to the entrusting entity or individual, and may not retain them without authorization.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　印刷企业接受委托印刷境外包装装潢印刷品的，必须事先向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门备案；印刷的包装装潢印刷品必须全部运输出境，不得在境内销售。

**Article 29** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print overseas packaging and decoration printed matters, it must report to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for archival filing in advance; all printed packaging and decoration printed matters must be transported out of the country and may not be sold within the country.

### 第五章　其他印刷品的印刷

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　印刷标有密级的文件、资料、图表等，按照国家有关法律、法规或者规章的规定办理。

**Article 30** The printing of documents, materials, charts, etc. marked with a classification level shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant State laws, regulations or rules.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　印刷布告、通告、重大活动工作证、通行证、在社会上流通使用的票证的，委托印刷单位必须向印刷企业出具主管部门的证明。印刷企业必须验证主管部门的证明，并保存主管部门的证明副本2年，以备查验；并且不得再委托他人印刷上述印刷品。  
印刷机关、团体、部队、企业事业单位内部使用的有价票证或者无价票证，或者印刷有单位名称的介绍信、工作证、会员证、出入证、学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书等专用证件的，委托印刷单位必须出具委托印刷证明。印刷企业必须验证委托印刷证明。  
印刷企业对前两款印件不得保留样本、样张；确因业务参考需要保留样本、样张的，应当征得委托印刷单位同意，在所保留印件上加盖“样本”、“样张”戳记，并妥善保管，不得丢失。

**Article 31** For printing notices, announcements, work passes for major events, passes, and negotiable instruments circulated in society, the entrusting printing entity must provide a certificate from the competent department to the printing enterprise. The printing enterprise must verify the certificate from the competent department and keep a copy of such certificate for two years for inspection; and it may not entrust another party to print the aforesaid printed matters.  
For printing valuable or non-valuable negotiable instruments used internally by state organs, organizations, military units, enterprises or institutions, or for printing special certificates such as letters of introduction, work passes, membership cards, entry permits, degree certificates, academic certificates or other academic credentials bearing the name of an entity, the entrusting printing entity must issue a printing entrustment certificate. The printing enterprise must verify the printing entrustment certificate.  
A printing enterprise may not retain samples or specimen sheets of the printed matters mentioned in the preceding two paragraphs; if it is necessary to retain samples or specimen sheets for business reference, it shall obtain the consent of the entrusting printing entity, stamp the retained printed matters with the mark of "sample" or "specimen sheet", and keep them properly without loss.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　印刷企业接受委托印刷宗教用品的，必须验证省、自治区、直辖市人民政府宗教事务管理部门的批准文件和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门核发的准印证；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门应当自收到印刷宗教用品的申请之日起10日内作出是否核发准印证的决定，并通知申请人；逾期不作出决定的，视为同意印刷。

**Article 32** Where a printing enterprise accepts the entrustment to print religious articles, it must verify the approval document issued by the religious affairs administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government and the permit certificate issued by the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of an application for printing religious articles, make a decision whether to issue a permit certificate and notify the applicant; if no decision is made within the time limit, the printing shall be deemed to have been approved.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的个人不得印刷标有密级的文件、资料、图表等，不得印刷布告、通告、重大活动工作证、通行证、在社会上流通使用的票证，不得印刷机关、团体、部队、企业事业单位内部使用的有价或者无价票证，不得印刷有单位名称的介绍信、工作证、会员证、出入证、学位证书、学历证书或者其他学业证书等专用证件，不得印刷宗教用品。

**Article 33** An individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters may not print documents, materials, charts, etc. marked with a classification level; may not print notices, announcements, work passes for major events, passes, or negotiable instruments circulated in society; may not print valuable or non-valuable negotiable instruments used internally by state organs, organizations, military units, enterprises or institutions; may not print special certificates such as letters of introduction, work passes, membership cards, entry permits, degree certificates, academic certificates or other academic credentials bearing the name of an entity; and may not print religious articles.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　接受委托印刷境外其他印刷品的，必须事先向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政部门备案；印刷的其他印刷品必须全部运输出境，不得在境内销售。

**Article 34** Where a party accepts the entrustment to print overseas other printed matters, it must report to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for archival filing in advance; all printed other printed matters must be transported out of the country and may not be sold within the country.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　印刷企业和从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的个人不得盗印他人的其他印刷品，不得销售、擅自加印或者接受第三人委托加印委托印刷的其他印刷品，不得将委托印刷的其他印刷品的纸型及印刷底片等出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让给其他单位或者个人。

**Article 35** A printing enterprise or an individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters may not pirate other printed matters of others, sell, print additional copies without authorization, or accept entrustment from a third party to print additional copies of the other printed matters it is entrusted to print, nor may it sell, lease, lend or otherwise transfer the paper plates, printing negatives, etc. of the other printed matters it is entrusted to print to any other entity or individual.

### 第六章　罚则

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本条例规定，擅自设立从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业或者擅自从事印刷经营活动的，由出版行政部门、工商行政管理部门依据法定职权予以取缔，没收印刷品和违法所得以及进行违法活动的专用工具、设备，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
单位内部设立的印刷厂（所）未依照本条例第二章的规定办理手续，从事印刷经营活动的，依照前款的规定处罚。

**Article 36** Where anyone, in violation of these Regulations, establishes an enterprise for the printing business of publications without authorization or engages in printing business activities without authorization, the publishing administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with its statutory functions and powers, close down the establishment, confiscate the printed matters, illegal gains, and the special tools and equipment used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a printing factory (shop) established within an entity engages in printing business activities without going through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of Chapter II of these Regulations, a penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　印刷业经营者违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门责令停止违法行为，限制开展相关生产经营活动或者责令停业整顿，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未取得出版行政部门的许可，擅自兼营或者变更从事出版物、包装装潢印刷品或者其他印刷品印刷经营活动，或者擅自兼并其他印刷业经营者的；  
（二）因合并、分立而设立新的印刷业经营者，未依照本条例的规定办理手续的；  
（三）出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让印刷经营许可证的。

**Article 37** Where a printing industry operator, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease the illegal act, restrict its relevant production and business activities or order it to suspend operations for rectification, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Without obtaining a permit from the publishing administrative department, engaging concurrently in or changing the printing business of publications, packaging and decoration printed matters or other printed matters without authorization, or merging with other printing industry operators without authorization;  
(2) Establishing a new printing industry operator through merger or division without going through the formalities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Selling, leasing, lending or otherwise transferring a printing operation license.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　印刷业经营者印刷明知或者应知含有[本条例第三条](#art-3)规定禁止印刷内容的出版物、包装装潢印刷品或者其他印刷品的，或者印刷国家明令禁止出版的出版物或者非出版单位出版的出版物的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门、公安部门依据法定职权责令停业整顿，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where a printing industry operator prints publications, packaging and decoration printed matters or other printed matters which it knows or should know contain content prohibited by Article 3 of these Regulations, or prints publications the publication of which is expressly prohibited by the State or publications printed by non-publishing entities, the publishing administrative department or the public security department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its statutory functions and powers, order it to suspend operations for rectification, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is not less than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　印刷业经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门、公安部门依据法定职权责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
（一）没有建立承印验证制度、承印登记制度、印刷品保管制度、印刷品交付制度、印刷活动残次品销毁制度等的；  
（二）在印刷经营活动中发现违法犯罪行为没有及时向公安部门或者出版行政部门报告的；  
（三）变更名称、法定代表人或者负责人、住所或者经营场所等主要登记事项，或者终止印刷经营活动，不向原批准设立的出版行政部门备案的；  
（四）未依照本条例的规定留存备查的材料的。  
单位内部设立印刷厂（所）违反本条例的规定，没有向所在地县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门、保密工作部门办理登记手续的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门、保密工作部门依据法定职权责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿。

**Article 39** Where a printing industry operator commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department or the public security department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its statutory functions and powers, order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, order it to suspend operations for rectification or the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) Failing to establish the system of verification of print orders, the system of registration of print orders, the system of safekeeping of printed matters, the system of delivery of printed matters, or the system of destruction of defective products from printing activities;  
(2) Failing to report promptly to the public security department or the publishing administrative department upon discovering any illegal or criminal act during its printing business activities;  
(3) Changing its name, legal representative or person in charge, domicile or business place, or other major registration items, or terminating its printing business activities, without reporting to the publishing administrative department that originally approved its establishment for archival filing;  
(4) Failing to retain materials for future reference in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.  
Where a printing factory (shop) established within an entity, in violation of these Regulations, fails to go through registration formalities with the local publishing administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level and the secrecy administrative department, the publishing administrative department or the secrecy administrative department at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its statutory functions and powers, order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, order it to suspend operations for rectification.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门给予警告，没收违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）接受他人委托印刷出版物，未依照本条例的规定验证印刷委托书、有关证明或者准印证，或者未将印刷委托书报出版行政部门备案的；  
（二）征订、销售出版物的；  
（三）未经批准，接受委托印刷境外出版物的，或者未将印刷的境外出版物全部运输出境的。  
从事出版物印刷经营活动的企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门给予警告，没收违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）假冒或者盗用他人名义，印刷出版物的；  
（二）盗印他人出版物的；  
（三）非法加印或者销售受委托印刷的出版物的；  
（四）擅自将出版单位委托印刷的出版物纸型及印刷底片等出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让的。

**Article 40** Where an enterprise engaged in the printing business of publications commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, restrict its relevant production and business activities, order it to suspend operations for rectification, or the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Accepting entrustment to print publications without verifying the printing entrustment letter, relevant certificates or permit certificate, or without reporting the printing entrustment letter to the publishing administrative department for archival filing in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Soliciting subscriptions for or selling publications;  
(3) Accepting entrustment to print overseas publications without approval, or failing to transport all printed overseas publications out of the country.  
Where an enterprise engaged in the printing business of publications commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is not less than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, restrict its relevant production and business activities, order it to suspend operations for rectification, or the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Printing publications under the false or unauthorized name of another party;  
(2) Pirating publications of others;  
(3) Illegally printing additional copies or selling the entrusted publications;  
(4) Without authorization, selling, leasing, lending or otherwise transferring the paper plates, printing negatives, etc. of the publications entrusted by the publishing entity.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　从事包装装潢印刷品印刷经营活动的企业有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门给予警告，没收违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）接受委托印刷注册商标标识，未依照本条例的规定验证、核查工商行政管理部门签章的《商标注册证》复印件、注册商标图样或者注册商标使用许可合同复印件的；  
（二）接受委托印刷广告宣传品、作为产品包装装潢的印刷品，未依照本条例的规定验证委托印刷单位的营业执照或者个人的居民身份证的，或者接受广告经营者的委托印刷广告宣传品，未验证广告经营资格证明的；  
（三）盗印他人包装装潢印刷品的；  
（四）接受委托印刷境外包装装潢印刷品未依照本条例的规定向出版行政部门备案的，或者未将印刷的境外包装装潢印刷品全部运输出境的。  
印刷企业接受委托印刷注册商标标识、广告宣传品，违反国家有关注册商标、广告印刷管理规定的，由工商行政管理部门给予警告，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where an enterprise engaged in the printing business of packaging and decoration printed matters commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, restrict its relevant production and business activities, order it to suspend operations for rectification, or the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Accepting entrustment to print registered trademark logos without verifying and checking the copy of the "Trademark Registration Certificate" stamped by the administrative department for industry and commerce, the registered trademark pattern, or the copy of the trademark license contract in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Accepting entrustment to print advertising materials or printed matters used as product packaging and decoration without verifying the business license of the entrusting entity or the resident identity card of the individual, or accepting the entrustment of an advertising operator to print advertising materials without verifying the advertising business qualification certificate;  
(3) Pirating packaging and decoration printed matters of others;  
(4) Accepting entrustment to print overseas packaging and decoration printed matters without reporting to the publishing administrative department for archival filing in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or failing to transport all printed overseas packaging and decoration printed matters out of the country.  
Where a printing enterprise, in accepting entrustment to print registered trademark logos or advertising materials, violates the State's provisions on the management of the printing of registered trademarks or advertisements, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall give a warning, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的企业和个人有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门给予警告，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）接受委托印刷其他印刷品，未依照本条例的规定验证有关证明的；  
（二）擅自将接受委托印刷的其他印刷品再委托他人印刷的；  
（三）将委托印刷的其他印刷品的纸型及印刷底片出售、出租、出借或者以其他形式转让的；  
（四）非法加印或者销售委托印刷的其他印刷品的；  
（五）接受委托印刷境外其他印刷品未依照本条例的规定向出版行政部门备案的，或者未将印刷的境外其他印刷品全部运输出境的；  
（六）从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的个人超范围经营的。  
伪造、变造学位证书、学历证书等国家机关公文、证件或者企业事业单位、人民团体公文、证件的，或者盗印他人的其他印刷品的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门给予警告，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where an enterprise or individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are not less than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, restrict its relevant production and business activities, order it to suspend operations for rectification, or the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Accepting entrustment to print other printed matters without verifying the relevant certificates in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Without authorization, re-entrusting the printing of the other printed matters it has accepted to another party;  
(3) Selling, leasing, lending or otherwise transferring the paper plates, printing negatives, etc. of the other printed matters it is entrusted to print;  
(4) Illegally printing additional copies or selling the other printed matters it is entrusted to print;  
(5) Accepting entrustment to print overseas other printed matters without reporting to the publishing administrative department for archival filing in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, or failing to transport all printed overseas other printed matters out of the country;  
(6) An individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters operating beyond the permitted scope.  
Where anyone forges or alters official documents or certificates of State organs, or official documents or certificates of enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, such as degree certificates or academic certificates, or pirates other printed matters of others, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is not less than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the circumstances, restrict its relevant production and business activities, order it to suspend operations for rectification, or the original license-issuing authority shall lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政部门给予警告，没收印刷品和违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者吊销印刷经营许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）印刷布告、通告、重大活动工作证、通行证、在社会上流通使用的票证，印刷企业没有验证主管部门的证明的，或者再委托他人印刷上述印刷品的；  
（二）印刷业经营者伪造、变造学位证书、学历证书等国家机关公文、证件或者企业事业单位、人民团体公文、证件的。  
印刷布告、通告、重大活动工作证、通行证、在社会上流通使用的票证，委托印刷单位没有取得主管部门证明的，由县级以上人民政府出版行政部门处以500元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 43** Where any of the following acts is committed, the publishing administrative department shall give a warning, confiscate the printed matters and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is not less than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, order it to suspend operations for rectification or revoke the printing operation license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) For printing notices, announcements, work passes for major events, passes, or negotiable instruments circulated in society, the printing enterprise fails to verify the certificate of the competent department, or re-entrusts another party to print the aforesaid printed matters;  
(2) A printing industry operator forges or alters official documents or certificates of State organs, or official documents or certificates of enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, such as degree certificates or academic certificates.  
Where the entrusting printing entity fails to obtain a certificate from the competent department for printing notices, announcements, work passes for major events, passes, or negotiable instruments circulated in society, the publishing administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　印刷业经营者违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府出版行政部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
（一）从事包装装潢印刷品印刷经营活动的企业擅自留存委托印刷的包装装潢印刷品的成品、半成品、废品和印板、纸型、印刷底片、原稿等的；  
（二）从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的企业和个人擅自保留其他印刷品的样本、样张的，或者在所保留的样本、样张上未加盖“样本”、“样张”戳记的。

**Article 44** Where a printing industry operator, in violation of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the publishing administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, order it to suspend operations for rectification or the original license-issuing authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) An enterprise engaged in the printing business of packaging and decoration printed matters retains without authorization the finished products, semi-finished products, waste products, printing plates, paper molds, printing negatives, original manuscripts, etc. of the entrusted packaging and decoration printed matters;  
(2) An enterprise or individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters retains without authorization the samples or specimen sheets of other printed matters, or fails to stamp the retained samples or specimen sheets with the mark of "sample" or "specimen sheet".

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　印刷企业被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，其法定代表人或者负责人自许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得担任印刷企业的法定代表人或者负责人。  
从事其他印刷品印刷经营活动的个人被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，自许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得从事印刷经营活动。

**Article 45** Where a printing enterprise is subject to the administrative penalty of revocation of its license, its legal representative or person in charge may not serve as the legal representative or person in charge of a printing enterprise within 10 years from the date of revocation of the license.  
Where an individual engaged in the printing business of other printed matters is subject to the administrative penalty of revocation of its license, such individual may not engage in printing business activities within 10 years from the date of revocation of the license.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款必须全部上缴国库。  
本条例所称违法所得，是指实施违法行为扣除成本后的获利数额，没有成本或者成本难以计算的，实施违法行为所取得的款项即为违法所得。

**Article 46** The administrative penalty of a fine imposed in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations shall be implemented with separation between the decision of the fine and the collection of the fine in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; all fines collected must be turned over to the State treasury.  
For the purpose of these Regulations, "illegal gains" means the amount of profit obtained from the illegal act after deducting costs; where there is no cost or the cost is difficult to calculate, the money obtained from the illegal act shall be the illegal gains.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　出版行政部门、工商行政管理部门或者其他有关部门违反本条例规定，擅自批准不符合法定条件的申请人取得许可证、批准文件，或者不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果的，对负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予降级或者撤职的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where the publishing administrative department, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or any other relevant department, in violation of these Regulations, approves without authorization an applicant who does not meet the statutory conditions to obtain a license or approval document, or fails to perform its supervisory duties, or fails to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences, the persons in charge who are directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be given the sanction of demotion or removal from office; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　本条例施行前已经依法设立的印刷企业，应当自本条例施行之日起180日内，到出版行政部门换领《印刷经营许可证》。  
依据本条例发放许可证，除按照法定标准收取成本费外，不得收取其他任何费用。

**Article 48** Printing enterprises that have been lawfully established before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within 180 days from the date on which these Regulations come into force, go to the publishing administrative department to exchange for a new "Printing Operation License".  
For the issuance of licenses in accordance with these Regulations, no fees other than the cost fees collected in accordance with the statutory standards may be charged.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　本条例自公布之日起施行。1997年3月8日国务院发布的《印刷业管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 49** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The "Regulations on the Administration of the Printing Industry" promulgated by the State Council on March 8, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
