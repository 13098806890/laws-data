# 卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送电视节目管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-18

> (1990年4月9日国务院批准　根据2018年9月18日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》修订) 第一条　为了加强对卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理，保障对外经济、科技和文化交流，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本办法。 第二条　本办法所称卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，是指单位利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收与本单位业务工作有直接关系的外国卫星传送的电视节目。 第三条　国务院广播电视行政管理部门负责全国卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理工作。 省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门负责本行政区内卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理工作。 第四条　教育、科研、新闻、金融、经贸以及其他确因业务工作需要的单位，可以按照本办法的规定，申请利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目。 常住外国人的涉外宾馆(饭店)、公寓确需提供国际金融、商情等经济信息服务的，可以按照本办法的规定，申请设置专门接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的卫星地面接收设施。 接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的卫星地面接收设施，由广播电视行政管理部门组织提供安装和维修服务。 第五条　申请利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者申请设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，应当具备下列条件： (一)本单位的业务工作确有接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的必要； (二)有确定的接收方位、接收内容和收视对象范围； (三)有符合国家标准的技术设备； (四)有合格的专职管理人员； (五)有健全的管理制度。 第六条　利用已有的或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的单位，应当向省级以上主管部门提出书面申请，经审查同意的，由申请单位报所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门审批。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门批准的，发给《卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目许可证》(以下简称《许可证》)，并由审批机关报国务院广播电视行政管理部门、公安部门、国家安全部门备案。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理，保障对外经济、科技和文化交流，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites through satellite ground receiving facilities, ensuring foreign economic, scientific, technological and cultural exchanges, and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本办法所称卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，是指单位利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收与本单位业务工作有直接关系的外国卫星传送的电视节目。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Measures, "receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites through satellite ground receiving facilities" refers to the use by a unit of its existing satellite ground receiving facilities or the installation of dedicated satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites that are directly related to the business work of such unit.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院广播电视行政管理部门负责全国卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门负责本行政区内卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理工作。

**Article 3** The administrative department for radio and television under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites through satellite ground receiving facilities nationwide.  
The administrative departments for radio and television of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be responsible for the administration of receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites through satellite ground receiving facilities within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　教育、科研、新闻、金融、经贸以及其他确因业务工作需要的单位，可以按照本办法的规定，申请利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目。  
常住外国人的涉外宾馆(饭店)、公寓确需提供国际金融、商情等经济信息服务的，可以按照本办法的规定，申请设置专门接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的卫星地面接收设施。  
接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的卫星地面接收设施，由广播电视行政管理部门组织提供安装和维修服务。

**Article 4** Units engaged in education, scientific research, news, finance, economic trade, and other units that genuinely need such reception for their business work may, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, apply to use their existing satellite ground receiving facilities or to install dedicated satellite ground receiving facilities for receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites.  
Foreign-related hotels (restaurants) and apartment buildings where foreigners permanently reside, which genuinely need to provide economic information services such as international finance and business conditions, may, in accordance with the provisions of these Measures, apply to install satellite ground receiving facilities dedicated to receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites.  
The installation and maintenance services for satellite ground receiving facilities used for receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites shall be organized and provided by the administrative departments for radio and television.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　申请利用已有的卫星地面接收设施或者申请设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)本单位的业务工作确有接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的必要；  
(二)有确定的接收方位、接收内容和收视对象范围；  
(三)有符合国家标准的技术设备；  
(四)有合格的专职管理人员；  
(五)有健全的管理制度。

**Article 5** An application for using existing satellite ground receiving facilities or for installing dedicated satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The business work of the unit shall have a genuine need to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites;  
(2) The receiving direction, receiving content, and the scope of the target audience shall be specified;  
(3) Technical equipment that complies with national standards shall be available;  
(4) Qualified full-time management personnel shall be available;  
(5) A sound management system shall be in place.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　利用已有的或者设置专门的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的单位，应当向省级以上主管部门提出书面申请，经审查同意的，由申请单位报所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门审批。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门批准的，发给《卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目许可证》(以下简称《许可证》)，并由审批机关报国务院广播电视行政管理部门、公安部门、国家安全部门备案。

**Article 6** A unit that intends to use existing or install dedicated satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites shall submit a written application to the competent authority at or above the provincial level. If approved after review, the applicant unit shall report to the administrative department for radio and television of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval. If approved by the said administrative department, it shall issue a "Satellite Ground Receiving Facility License for Receiving Television Programs Transmitted by Foreign Satellites" (hereinafter referred to as the "License"), and the approving authority shall report to the administrative department for radio and television under the State Council, the public security department, and the state security department for record.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　已有卫星地面接收设施的单位，未持有《许可证》的，不得接收外国卫星传送的电视节目；其他单位，未持有《许可证》的，不得设置卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目。

**Article 7** A unit that already has satellite ground receiving facilities but does not hold a License shall not receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites; other units that do not hold a License shall not install satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　持有《许可证》的单位，必须严格按照《许可证》载明的接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的接收目的、接收内容、接收方位、接收方式和收视对象的范围等要求，接收和使用外国电视节目。  
《许可证》不得涂改或者转让。因业务工作发生变化需要改变《许可证》规定的内容或者不再接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的，应当及时报请审批机关换发或者注销《许可证》，由审批机关按照[本办法第六条](#art-6)的规定，报有关机关备案。

**Article 8** A unit holding a License must strictly receive and use foreign television programs in accordance with the requirements specified in the License, such as the purpose of reception, content, direction, method, and scope of the target audience.  
The License shall not be altered or transferred. If the business work changes such that the contents specified in the License need to be modified or the unit no longer needs to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites, the unit shall promptly report to the approving authority for renewal or cancellation of the License, and the approving authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 6 of these Measures, report to the relevant authorities for record.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　持有《许可证》的单位，接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，只允许在本单位业务工作中使用。除本单位领导批准外，一律不得录制。严禁将所接收的外国卫星传送的电视节目在国内电视台、有线电视台、录像放映点播放或者以其他方式进行传播。  
经批准录制的音像资料目录，必须定期报所在地的广播电视行政管理部门、公安机关、国家安全机关备案。  
录制的音像资料必须指定专人严格保管。

**Article 9** A unit holding a License that receives television programs transmitted by foreign satellites may only use such programs for its own business work. No recording shall be made unless approved by the unit's leadership. It is strictly prohibited to broadcast the received television programs transmitted by foreign satellites on domestic television stations, cable television stations, or video projection sites, or to disseminate them by other means.  
A catalogue of audio-visual materials recorded with approval must be periodically reported to the local administrative department for radio and television, public security organ, and state security organ for record.  
The recorded audio-visual materials must be placed under strict custody by designated personnel.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　广播电视行政管理部门、公安机关、国家安全机关负责监督检查卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的管理工作，对违反本办法接收、录制、传播外国卫星传送的电视节目的行为有权予以制止。

**Article 10** The administrative departments for radio and television, public security organs, and state security organs shall be responsible for supervising and inspecting the administration of receiving television programs transmitted by foreign satellites through satellite ground receiving facilities, and shall have the authority to stop any act of receiving, recording, or disseminating television programs transmitted by foreign satellites in violation of these Measures.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　违反[本办法第八条](#art-8)、第九条规定的单位，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门会同公安机关、国家安全机关视情节轻重，给予警告、二万元以下的罚款、直至吊销《许可证》的处罚。吊销《许可证》的，可以同时没收其使用的卫星地面接收设施。对单位的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门、公安机关、国家安全机关可以建议其主管部门给予行政处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** A unit that violates the provisions of Articles 8 or 9 of these Measures shall, depending on the severity of the circumstances, be given a warning, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan, or even have its License revoked, by the administrative department for radio and television of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the public security organ and the state security organ. If the License is revoked, the satellite ground receiving facilities used by the unit may be confiscated at the same time. For the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit, the administrative department for radio and television, public security organ, and state security organ of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may recommend to the competent authority that administrative sanctions be imposed; if the circumstances are so serious as to constitute a crime, the judicial authorities shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　违反[本办法第七条](#art-7)的规定，未持有《许可证》而擅自设置卫星地面接收设施或者接收外国卫星传送的电视节目的单位，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府广播电视行政管理部门会同公安机关、国家安全机关可以没收其卫星地面接收设施，并处以五万元以下的罚款。对单位的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员可以建议其主管部门给予行政处分；有私自录制、传播行为，情节严重构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 12** A unit that, in violation of the provisions of Article 7 of these Measures, installs satellite ground receiving facilities or receives television programs transmitted by foreign satellites without holding a License, may be subject to confiscation of its satellite ground receiving facilities and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan, imposed by the administrative department for radio and television of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the public security organ and the state security organ. The directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit may be recommended to the competent authority for administrative sanctions; if there are acts of unauthorized recording or dissemination, and the circumstances are so serious as to constitute a crime, the judicial authorities shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人对罚款或者吊销《许可证》的处罚不服的，可以在收到处罚决定书之日起十五日内，向同级人民政府申请复议，也可以向人民法院起诉；对行政复议不服的，可以在收到复议决定书之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。逾期不申请复议或者不起诉的，吊销许可证的处罚生效；逾期不申请复议或者不起诉又不交纳罚款的，罚款的处罚由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 13** If a party is dissatisfied with the penalty of a fine or the revocation of a License, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the penalty decision, apply for reconsideration to the people's government at the same level, or institute legal proceedings with the People's Court; if dissatisfied with the administrative reconsideration decision, it may institute legal proceedings with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision. If no application for reconsideration or prosecution is filed within the time limit, the penalty of revocation of the License shall take effect; if no application for reconsideration or prosecution is filed and the fine is not paid within the time limit, the penalty of the fine shall be enforced by the People's Court upon application by the authority that made the penalty decision.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　军队以及公安机关、国家安全机关因国防、公安和国家安全工作需要利用已有的或者专门设置的卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，由军队有关部门、国务院公安部门、国务院国家安全部门分别制定措施进行管理。  
外国驻华使(领)馆，以及其他享有外交特权与豁免的机构设置卫星地面接收设施接收外国卫星传送的电视节目，通过外交途径办理。

**Article 14** Where the military, public security organs, or state security organs, for the needs of national defense, public security, and state security work, use existing or dedicated satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites, the relevant military departments, the public security department under the State Council, and the state security department under the State Council shall respectively formulate measures for administration.  
Foreign embassies (consulates) in China and other institutions enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunities that install satellite ground receiving facilities to receive television programs transmitted by foreign satellites shall handle the matter through diplomatic channels.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本办法由国务院广播电视行政管理部门解释。

**Article 15** These Measures shall be interpreted by the administrative department for radio and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。

**Article 16** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
