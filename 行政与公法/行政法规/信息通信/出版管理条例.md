# 出版管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对出版活动的管理，发展和繁荣有中国特色社会主义出版产业和出版事业，保障公民依法行使出版自由的权利，促进社会主义精神文明和物质文明建设，根据宪法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Constitution in order to strengthen the administration of publishing activities, develop and prosper the socialist publishing industry and publishing undertakings with Chinese characteristics, protect citizens' right to exercise the freedom of the press according to law, and promote the construction of socialist spiritual civilization and material civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内从事出版活动，适用本条例。  
本条例所称出版活动，包括出版物的出版、印刷或者复制、进口、发行。  
本条例所称出版物，是指报纸、期刊、图书、音像制品、电子出版物等。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to publishing activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, publishing activities include the publication, printing or reproduction, import, and distribution of publications.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, publications refer to newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic publications, and the like.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　出版活动必须坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向，坚持以马克思列宁主义、毛泽东思想、邓小平理论和“三个代表”重要思想为指导，贯彻落实科学发展观，传播和积累有益于提高民族素质、有益于经济发展和社会进步的科学技术和文化知识，弘扬民族优秀文化，促进国际文化交流，丰富和提高人民的精神生活。

**Article 3** Publishing activities must adhere to the orientation of serving the people and serving socialism, persist in taking Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, and the important thought of the "Three Represents" as the guide, implement the Scientific Outlook on Development, disseminate and accumulate scientific and technological knowledge and cultural knowledge beneficial to improving national quality, economic development, and social progress, promote the outstanding national culture, facilitate international cultural exchange, and enrich and enhance the spiritual life of the people.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　从事出版活动，应当将社会效益放在首位，实现社会效益与经济效益相结合。

**Article 4** When engaging in publishing activities, social benefits shall be placed in the first place, and the combination of social benefits and economic benefits shall be achieved.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公民依法行使出版自由的权利，各级人民政府应当予以保障。  
公民在行使出版自由的权利的时候，必须遵守宪法和法律，不得反对宪法确定的基本原则，不得损害国家的、社会的、集体的利益和其他公民的合法的自由和权利。

**Article 5** Citizens' right to exercise the freedom of the press according to law shall be guaranteed by the people's governments at all levels.  
When exercising the right to freedom of the press, citizens must abide by the Constitution and laws, shall not oppose the basic principles established by the Constitution, and shall not harm the interests of the State, society, or the collective, or the lawful freedoms and rights of other citizens.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院出版行政主管部门负责全国的出版活动的监督管理工作。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责有关的出版活动的监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府负责出版管理的部门（以下简称出版行政主管部门）负责本行政区域内出版活动的监督管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内，负责有关的出版活动的监督管理工作。

**Article 6** The administrative department for publishing under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of publishing activities nationwide. Other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of duties prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the supervision and administration of relevant publishing activities.  
The departments responsible for publishing administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the publishing administrative departments) shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of publishing activities within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of relevant publishing activities.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　出版行政主管部门根据已经取得的违法嫌疑证据或者举报，对涉嫌违法从事出版物出版、印刷或者复制、进口、发行等活动的行为进行查处时，可以检查与涉嫌违法活动有关的物品和经营场所；对有证据证明是与违法活动有关的物品，可以查封或者扣押。

**Article 7** When investigating and dealing with acts suspected of illegally engaging in the publication, printing or reproduction, import, or distribution of publications based on obtained evidence of illegal suspicion or reports, the publishing administrative department may inspect the articles and business premises related to the suspected illegal activities; and may seal up or detain articles for which there is evidence proving that they are related to illegal activities.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　出版行业的社会团体按照其章程，在出版行政主管部门的指导下，实行自律管理。

**Article 8** Social organizations in the publishing industry shall, in accordance with their articles of association and under the guidance of the publishing administrative department, implement self-discipline management.

### 第二章　出版单位的设立与管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　报纸、期刊、图书、音像制品和电子出版物等应当由出版单位出版。  
本条例所称出版单位，包括报社、期刊社、图书出版社、音像出版社和电子出版物出版社等。  
法人出版报纸、期刊，不设立报社、期刊社的，其设立的报纸编辑部、期刊编辑部视为出版单位。

**Article 9** Newspapers, periodicals, books, audio-visual products, electronic publications, and the like shall be published by publishing entities.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, publishing entities include newspaper offices, periodical offices, book publishing houses, audio-visual publishing houses, electronic publication publishing houses, and the like.  
If a legal person publishes a newspaper or periodical without establishing a newspaper office or periodical office, the editorial department of the newspaper or periodical established thereby shall be deemed a publishing entity.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院出版行政主管部门制定全国出版单位总量、结构、布局的规划，指导、协调出版产业和出版事业发展。

**Article 10** The administrative department for publishing under the State Council shall formulate plans on the total quantity, structure, and layout of publishing entities nationwide, and shall guide and coordinate the development of the publishing industry and publishing undertakings.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　设立出版单位，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有出版单位的名称、章程；  
（二）有符合国务院出版行政主管部门认定的主办单位及其主管机关；  
（三）有确定的业务范围；  
（四）有30万元以上的注册资本和固定的工作场所；  
（五）有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和符合国家规定的资格条件的编辑出版专业人员；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
审批设立出版单位，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当符合国家关于出版单位总量、结构、布局的规划。

**Article 11** The establishment of a publishing entity shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name and articles of association of the publishing entity;  
(2) having a sponsor that is recognized by the administrative department for publishing under the State Council and its controlling authority;  
(3) having a definite scope of business;  
(4) having a registered capital of not less than RMB 300,000 yuan and a fixed place of business;  
(5) having an organizational structure suitable for the needs of the business scope and editorial and publishing professionals who meet the qualifications prescribed by the State;  
(6) other conditions as prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
When examining and approving the establishment of a publishing entity, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the state's plans on the total quantity, structure, and layout of publishing entities shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　设立出版单位，由其主办单位向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门提出申请；省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核同意后，报国务院出版行政主管部门审批。设立的出版单位为事业单位的，还应当办理机构编制审批手续。

**Article 12** To establish a publishing entity, its sponsor shall submit an application to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located; after examination and consent by the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the application shall be submitted to the administrative department for publishing under the State Council for approval. If the publishing entity to be established is a public institution, the formalities for examination and approval of organizational establishment shall also be completed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　设立出版单位的申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）出版单位的名称、地址；  
（二）出版单位的主办单位及其主管机关的名称、地址；  
（三）出版单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、住址、资格证明文件；  
（四）出版单位的资金来源及数额。  
设立报社、期刊社或者报纸编辑部、期刊编辑部的，申请书还应当载明报纸或者期刊的名称、刊期、开版或者开本、印刷场所。  
申请书应当附具出版单位的章程和设立出版单位的主办单位及其主管机关的有关证明材料。

**Article 13** An application for the establishment of a publishing entity shall specify the following items:  
(1) the name and address of the publishing entity;  
(2) the names and addresses of the sponsor of the publishing entity and its controlling authority;  
(3) the name, address, and qualification certification documents of the legal representative or principal responsible person of the publishing entity;  
(4) the source and amount of funds of the publishing entity.  
If establishing a newspaper office, periodical office, or editorial department of a newspaper or periodical, the application shall also specify the name, periodicity, format or size, and printing premises of the newspaper or periodical.  
The application shall be accompanied by the articles of association of the publishing entity and the relevant supporting documents of the sponsor and its controlling authority.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院出版行政主管部门应当自受理设立出版单位的申请之日起60日内，作出批准或者不批准的决定，并由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门书面通知主办单位；不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 14** The administrative department for publishing under the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of accepting the application for the establishment of a publishing entity, make a decision on approval or disapproval, and the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall notify the sponsor in writing; if the application is disapproved, reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　设立出版单位的主办单位应当自收到批准决定之日起60日内，向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门登记，领取出版许可证。登记事项由国务院出版行政主管部门规定。  
出版单位领取出版许可证后，属于事业单位法人的，持出版许可证向事业单位登记管理机关登记，依法领取事业单位法人证书；属于企业法人的，持出版许可证向工商行政管理部门登记，依法领取营业执照。

**Article 15** The sponsor establishing a publishing entity shall, within 60 days from the date of receiving the decision on approval, register with the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and obtain a publishing license. The registration items shall be prescribed by the administrative department for publishing under the State Council.  
After obtaining the publishing license, if the publishing entity is a legal person as a public institution, it shall, with the publishing license, register with the registration administration authority for public institutions and obtain a certificate of legal person for public institutions according to law; if it is a legal person as an enterprise, it shall, with the publishing license, register with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　报社、期刊社、图书出版社、音像出版社和电子出版物出版社等应当具备法人条件，经核准登记后，取得法人资格，以其全部法人财产独立承担民事责任。  
依照[本条例第九条](#art-9)第三款的规定，视为出版单位的报纸编辑部、期刊编辑部不具有法人资格，其民事责任由其主办单位承担。

**Article 16** Newspaper offices, periodical offices, book publishing houses, audio-visual publishing houses, electronic publication publishing houses, and the like shall meet the conditions for a legal person, and after being approved and registered, shall obtain the status of a legal person and independently bear civil liability with all their legal person property.  
In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 9 of these Regulations, newspaper editorial departments and periodical editorial departments deemed as publishing entities shall not have the status of a legal person, and their civil liability shall be borne by their sponsors.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　出版单位变更名称、主办单位或者其主管机关、业务范围、资本结构，合并或者分立，设立分支机构，出版新的报纸、期刊，或者报纸、期刊变更名称的，应当依照[本条例第十二条](#art-12)、第十三条的规定办理审批手续。出版单位属于事业单位法人的，还应当持批准文件到事业单位登记管理机关办理相应的登记手续；属于企业法人的，还应当持批准文件到工商行政管理部门办理相应的登记手续。  
出版单位除前款所列变更事项外的其他事项的变更，应当经主办单位及其主管机关审查同意，向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门申请变更登记，并报国务院出版行政主管部门备案。出版单位属于事业单位法人的，还应当持批准文件到事业单位登记管理机关办理变更登记；属于企业法人的，还应当持批准文件到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记。

**Article 17** If a publishing entity changes its name, sponsor or its controlling authority, scope of business, capital structure, merges or divides, establishes a branch, publishes a new newspaper or periodical, or changes the name of a newspaper or periodical, it shall go through the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Articles 12 and 13 of these Regulations. If the publishing entity is a legal person as a public institution, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the corresponding registration procedures with the registration administration authority for public institutions; if it is a legal person as an enterprise, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the corresponding registration procedures with the administrative department for industry and commerce.  
Changes to items of a publishing entity other than those listed in the preceding paragraph shall be examined and consented to by the sponsor and its controlling authority, and an application for change of registration shall be submitted to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and shall be reported to the administrative department for publishing under the State Council for record. If the publishing entity is a legal person as a public institution, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the change of registration with the registration administration authority for public institutions; if it is a legal person as an enterprise, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the change of registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　出版单位中止出版活动的，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门备案并说明理由和期限；出版单位中止出版活动不得超过180日。  
出版单位终止出版活动的，由主办单位提出申请并经主管机关同意后，由主办单位向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门办理注销登记，并报国务院出版行政主管部门备案。出版单位属于事业单位法人的，还应当持批准文件到事业单位登记管理机关办理注销登记；属于企业法人的，还应当持批准文件到工商行政管理部门办理注销登记。

**Article 18** If a publishing entity suspends its publishing activities, it shall report to the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located for record and state the reasons and the time limit; the suspension of publishing activities by a publishing entity shall not exceed 180 days.  
If a publishing entity terminates its publishing activities, the sponsor shall file an application and, after obtaining consent from the controlling authority, the sponsor shall go through the cancellation of registration with the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and shall report to the administrative department for publishing under the State Council for record. If the publishing entity is a legal person as a public institution, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the cancellation of registration with the registration administration authority for public institutions; if it is a legal person as an enterprise, it shall also, with the approval document, go through the cancellation of registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　图书出版社、音像出版社和电子出版物出版社自登记之日起满180日未从事出版活动的，报社、期刊社自登记之日起满90日未出版报纸、期刊的，由原登记的出版行政主管部门注销登记，并报国务院出版行政主管部门备案。  
因不可抗力或者其他正当理由发生前款所列情形的，出版单位可以向原登记的出版行政主管部门申请延期。

**Article 19** If a book publishing house, audio-visual publishing house, or electronic publication publishing house has not engaged in publishing activities within 180 days from the date of registration, or if a newspaper office or periodical office has not published a newspaper or periodical within 90 days from the date of registration, the publishing administrative department with which it is originally registered shall cancel the registration and report to the administrative department for publishing under the State Council for record.  
If the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph occur due to force majeure or other legitimate reasons, the publishing entity may apply to the publishing administrative department with which it is originally registered for an extension.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　图书出版社、音像出版社和电子出版物出版社的年度出版计划及涉及国家安全、社会安定等方面的重大选题，应当经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核后报国务院出版行政主管部门备案；涉及重大选题，未在出版前报备案的出版物，不得出版。具体办法由国务院出版行政主管部门制定。  
期刊社的重大选题，应当依照前款规定办理备案手续。

**Article 20** The annual publishing plan of a book publishing house, audio-visual publishing house, or electronic publication publishing house, as well as major topics involving national security, social stability, and other aspects, shall be submitted to the administrative department for publishing under the State Council for record after examination by the publishing administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located; publications involving major topics that have not been reported for record before publication shall not be published. Specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department for publishing under the State Council.  
Major topics of a periodical office shall go through the record formalities in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　出版单位不得向任何单位或者个人出售或者以其他形式转让本单位的名称、书号、刊号或者版号、版面，并不得出租本单位的名称、刊号。  
出版单位及其从业人员不得利用出版活动谋取其他不正当利益。

**Article 21** A publishing unit shall not sell or transfer in any other form its name, book number, periodical number, edition number or layout to any unit or individual, and shall not lease its name or periodical number.  
A publishing unit and its employees shall not use publishing activities to seek other illegitimate benefits.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　出版单位应当按照国家有关规定向国家图书馆、中国版本图书馆和国务院出版行政主管部门免费送交样本。

**Article 22** A publishing unit shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, submit free samples to the National Library of China, the China National Library of Editions, and the competent department of publication administration under the State Council.

### 第三章　出版物的出版

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公民可以依照本条例规定，在出版物上自由表达自己对国家事务、经济和文化事业、社会事务的见解和意愿，自由发表自己从事科学研究、文学艺术创作和其他文化活动的成果。  
合法出版物受法律保护，任何组织和个人不得非法干扰、阻止、破坏出版物的出版。

**Article 23** Citizens may, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, freely express their opinions and wishes on state affairs, economic and cultural undertakings, and social affairs in publications, and freely publish the results of their scientific research, literary and artistic creation, and other cultural activities.  
Lawful publications shall be protected by law, and no organization or individual may illegally interfere with, prevent, or disrupt the publication of publications.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　出版单位实行编辑责任制度，保障出版物刊载的内容符合本条例的规定。

**Article 24** A publishing unit shall implement an editorial responsibility system to ensure that the contents published in publications comply with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何出版物不得含有下列内容：  
（一）反对宪法确定的基本原则的；  
（二）危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
（三）泄露国家秘密、危害国家安全或者损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
（四）煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结，或者侵害民族风俗、习惯的；  
（五）宣扬邪教、迷信的；  
（六）扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
（七）宣扬淫秽、赌博、暴力或者教唆犯罪的；  
（八）侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
（九）危害社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
（十）有法律、行政法规和国家规定禁止的其他内容的。

**Article 25** No publication may contain any of the following:  
(1) content opposing the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) content endangering national unity, sovereignty, and territorial integrity;  
(3) content disclosing state secrets, endangering national security, or harming national honor and interests;  
(4) content inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, undermining ethnic unity, or infringing upon ethnic customs and habits;  
(5) content propagating cults or superstition;  
(6) content disrupting social order or undermining social stability;  
(7) content propagating obscenity, gambling, violence, or instigating crime;  
(8) content insulting or slandering others, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) content endangering social morality or fine national cultural traditions;  
(10) other content prohibited by laws, administrative regulations, and state provisions.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　以未成年人为对象的出版物不得含有诱发未成年人模仿违反社会公德的行为和违法犯罪的行为的内容，不得含有恐怖、残酷等妨害未成年人身心健康的内容。

**Article 26** Publications intended for minors shall not contain content that induces minors to imitate acts violating social morality or acts violating the law and committing crimes, and shall not contain content such as horror or cruelty that impairs the physical and mental health of minors.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　出版物的内容不真实或者不公正，致使公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益受到侵害的，其出版单位应当公开更正，消除影响，并依法承担其他民事责任。  
报纸、期刊发表的作品内容不真实或者不公正，致使公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益受到侵害的，当事人有权要求有关出版单位更正或者答辩，有关出版单位应当在其近期出版的报纸、期刊上予以发表；拒绝发表的，当事人可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 27** If the content of a publication is untrue or unfair, thereby infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization, the publishing unit thereof shall make a public correction, eliminate the effects, and bear other civil liability according to law.  
If the content of a work published in a newspaper or periodical is untrue or unfair, thereby infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen, legal person, or other organization, the party concerned shall have the right to request the relevant publishing unit to make a correction or reply, and the relevant publishing unit shall publish it in its recently published newspaper or periodical; if the publishing unit refuses to publish it, the party may institute legal proceedings in the People's Court.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　出版物必须按照国家的有关规定载明作者、出版者、印刷者或者复制者、发行者的名称、地址，书号、刊号或者版号，在版编目数据，出版日期、刊期以及其他有关事项。  
出版物的规格、开本、版式、装帧、校对等必须符合国家标准和规范要求，保证出版物的质量。  
出版物使用语言文字必须符合国家法律规定和有关标准、规范。

**Article 28** A publication must indicate, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, the names and addresses of the author, publisher, printer or replicator, and distributor, the book number, periodical number, or edition number, the CIP (Cataloging in Publication) data, the date of publication, periodicity, and other relevant matters.  
The specifications, format, layout, binding, and proofreading of a publication must comply with national standards and norms, ensuring the quality of the publication.  
The language used in a publication must comply with the provisions of national laws and regulations and relevant standards and norms.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　任何单位和个人不得伪造、假冒出版单位名称或者报纸、期刊名称出版出版物。

**Article 29** No unit or individual may forge or counterfeit the name of a publishing unit or the name of a newspaper or periodical to publish a publication.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　中学小学教科书由国务院教育行政主管部门审定；其出版、发行单位应当具有适应教科书出版、发行业务需要的资金、组织机构和人员等条件，并取得国务院出版行政主管部门批准的教科书出版、发行资质。纳入政府采购范围的中学小学教科书，其发行单位按照《中华人民共和国政府采购法》的有关规定确定。其他任何单位或者个人不得从事中学小学教科书的出版、发行业务。

**Article 30** Secondary and primary school textbooks shall be examined and approved by the competent education administration department of the State Council; the publishing and distributing units thereof shall have such conditions as funds, organizational structure, and personnel that meet the needs of textbook publishing and distribution business, and shall obtain the qualification for textbook publishing and distribution approved by the competent department of publication administration under the State Council. For secondary and primary school textbooks that are included in the scope of government procurement, the distributing units thereof shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Government Procurement Law of the People's Republic of China. No other unit or individual may engage in the publishing or distribution of secondary and primary school textbooks.

### 第四章　出版物的印刷或者复制和发行

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　从事出版物印刷或者复制业务的单位，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门提出申请，经审核许可，并依照国家有关规定到工商行政管理部门办理相关手续后，方可从事出版物的印刷或者复制。  
未经许可并办理相关手续的，不得印刷报纸、期刊、图书，不得复制音像制品、电子出版物。

**Article 31** A unit engaged in printing or replicating publications shall submit an application to the competent department of publication administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and after being examined and licensed, and after going through the relevant formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, may engage in the printing or replicating of publications.  
Those that have not obtained the license and gone through the relevant formalities shall not print newspapers, periodicals, or books, and shall not replicate audio-visual products or electronic publications.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　出版单位不得委托未取得出版物印刷或者复制许可的单位印刷或者复制出版物。  
出版单位委托印刷或者复制单位印刷或者复制出版物的，必须提供符合国家规定的印刷或者复制出版物的有关证明，并依法与印刷或者复制单位签订合同。  
印刷或者复制单位不得接受非出版单位和个人的委托印刷报纸、期刊、图书或者复制音像制品、电子出版物，不得擅自印刷、发行报纸、期刊、图书或者复制、发行音像制品、电子出版物。

**Article 32** A publishing unit shall not entrust a unit that has not obtained a license for printing or replicating publications to print or replicate publications.  
If a publishing unit entrusts a printing or replicating unit to print or replicate publications, it must provide relevant certificates conforming to state provisions for printing or replicating the publications, and shall sign a contract with the printing or replicating unit according to law.  
A printing or replicating unit shall not accept the entrustment of a non-publishing unit or individual to print newspapers, periodicals, or books, or replicate audio-visual products or electronic publications, and shall not, without authorization, print or distribute newspapers, periodicals, or books, or replicate or distribute audio-visual products or electronic publications.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　印刷或者复制单位经所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门批准，可以承接境外出版物的印刷或者复制业务；但是，印刷或者复制的境外出版物必须全部运输出境，不得在境内发行。  
境外委托印刷或者复制的出版物的内容，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核。委托人应当持有著作权人授权书，并向著作权行政管理部门登记。

**Article 33** A printing or replicating unit may, upon approval by the competent department of publication administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, undertake the printing or replicating of overseas publications; however, the printed or replicated overseas publications must all be shipped out of the territory and shall not be distributed within the territory.  
The content of publications entrusted for printing or replicating from overseas shall be examined by the competent department of publication administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The entrusting party shall hold the copyright owner's authorization and shall register with the copyright administration department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　印刷或者复制单位应当自完成出版物的印刷或者复制之日起2年内，留存一份承接的出版物样本备查。

**Article 34** A printing or replicating unit shall, within two years from the date of completion of the printing or replicating of a publication, retain one sample of the undertaken publication for future reference.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　单位从事出版物批发业务的，须经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府出版行政主管部门审核许可，取得《出版物经营许可证》。  
单位和个体工商户从事出版物零售业务的，须经县级人民政府出版行政主管部门审核许可，取得《出版物经营许可证》。

**Article 35** If a unit engages in the wholesale business of publications, it must be examined and licensed by the competent department of publication administration of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and obtain a "Publication Distribution License".  
If a unit or an individual industrial and commercial household engages in the retail business of publications, it must be examined and licensed by the competent department of publication administration of the people's government at the county level, and obtain a "Publication Distribution License".

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　通过互联网等信息网络从事出版物发行业务的单位或者个体工商户，应当依照本条例规定取得《出版物经营许可证》。  
提供网络交易平台服务的经营者应当对申请通过网络交易平台从事出版物发行业务的单位或者个体工商户的经营主体身份进行审查，验证其《出版物经营许可证》。

**Article 36** Units or individual industrial and commercial households engaging in the distribution of publications through the internet or other information networks shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, obtain a "Publication Distribution License".  
Operators providing network trading platform services shall examine the business entity identity of units or individual industrial and commercial households applying to engage in the distribution of publications through the network trading platform, and verify their "Publication Distribution License".

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事出版物发行业务的单位和个体工商户变更《出版物经营许可证》登记事项，或者兼并、合并、分立的，应当依照[本条例第三十五条](#art-35)的规定办理审批手续。  
从事出版物发行业务的单位和个体工商户终止经营活动的，应当向原批准的出版行政主管部门备案。

**Article 37** Units and individual industrial and commercial households engaged in the distribution of publications that change the registered items of their "Publication Distribution License", or that undergo merger, consolidation, or division, shall go through the approval procedures in accordance with Article 35 of these Regulations.  
Units and individual industrial and commercial households engaged in the distribution of publications that cease their business activities shall file for the record with the original approving competent department of publication administration.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　出版单位可以发行本出版单位出版的出版物，不得发行其他出版单位出版的出版物。

**Article 38** A publishing unit may distribute the publications published by itself, and shall not distribute publications published by other publishing units.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家允许设立从事图书、报纸、期刊、电子出版物发行业务的外商投资企业。

**Article 39** The State permits the establishment of foreign-invested enterprises engaged in the distribution of books, newspapers, periodicals, and electronic publications.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　印刷或者复制单位、发行单位或者个体工商户不得印刷或者复制、发行有下列情形之一的出版物：  
（一）含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容的；  
（二）非法进口的；  
（三）伪造、假冒出版单位名称或者报纸、期刊名称的；  
（四）未署出版单位名称的；  
（五）中学小学教科书未经依法审定的；  
（六）侵犯他人著作权的。

**Article 40** A printing or replicating unit, distributing unit, or individual industrial and commercial household shall not print, replicate, or distribute any publication under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) containing content prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations;  
(2) illegally imported;  
(3) forging or counterfeiting the name of a publishing unit or the name of a newspaper or periodical;  
(4) failing to indicate the name of the publishing unit;  
(5) secondary or primary school textbooks that have not been examined and approved according to law;  
(6) infringing upon the copyright of others.

### 第五章　出版物的进口

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　出版物进口业务，由依照本条例设立的出版物进口经营单位经营；其他单位和个人不得从事出版物进口业务。

**Article 41** The import of publications shall be operated by entities established in accordance with these Regulations for such purpose; no other entity or individual may engage in the import of publications.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　设立出版物进口经营单位，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有出版物进口经营单位的名称、章程；  
（二）有符合国务院出版行政主管部门认定的主办单位及其主管机关；  
（三）有确定的业务范围；  
（四）具有进口出版物内容审查能力；  
（五）有与出版物进口业务相适应的资金；  
（六）有固定的经营场所；  
（七）法律、行政法规和国家规定的其他条件。

**Article 42** The establishment of an entity for the import of publications shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name and articles of association for the entity;  
(2) having a sponsoring unit and its competent authority recognized by the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council;  
(3) having a defined business scope;  
(4) having the capacity to review the content of imported publications;  
(5) having funds commensurate with the import business;  
(6) having a fixed business premises;  
(7) other conditions stipulated by laws, administrative regulations, and the State.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　设立出版物进口经营单位，应当向国务院出版行政主管部门提出申请，经审查批准，取得国务院出版行政主管部门核发的出版物进口经营许可证后，持证到工商行政管理部门依法领取营业执照。  
设立出版物进口经营单位，还应当依照对外贸易法律、行政法规的规定办理相应手续。

**Article 43** To establish an entity for the import of publications, an application shall be submitted to the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council. Upon examination and approval, and after obtaining the publication import license issued by the said department, the applicant shall, with the license, apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce for a business license in accordance with law.  
The establishment of an entity for the import of publications shall also go through the corresponding formalities in accordance with the provisions of foreign trade laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　出版物进口经营单位变更名称、业务范围、资本结构、主办单位或者其主管机关，合并或者分立，设立分支机构，应当依照[本条例第四十二条](#art-42)、第四十三条的规定办理审批手续，并持批准文件到工商行政管理部门办理相应的登记手续。

**Article 44** Where an entity for the import of publications changes its name, business scope, capital structure, sponsoring unit or its competent authority, merges or divides, or establishes a branch, it shall go through the examination and approval formalities in accordance with Articles 42 and 43 of these Regulations, and shall, with the approval documents, go through the corresponding registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　出版物进口经营单位进口的出版物，不得含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止的内容。  
出版物进口经营单位负责对其进口的出版物进行内容审查。省级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门可以对出版物进口经营单位进口的出版物直接进行内容审查。出版物进口经营单位无法判断其进口的出版物是否含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容的，可以请求省级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门进行内容审查。省级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门应出版物进口经营单位的请求，对其进口的出版物进行内容审查的，可以按照国务院价格主管部门批准的标准收取费用。  
国务院出版行政主管部门可以禁止特定出版物的进口。

**Article 45** Publications imported by an entity for the import of publications shall not contain any content prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations.  
The entity for the import of publications shall be responsible for reviewing the content of the publications it imports. The competent administrative department for publication of the people's government at or above the provincial level may directly review the content of publications imported by the entity. If the entity is unable to determine whether the publications it imports contain any content prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations, it may request the competent administrative department for publication at or above the provincial level to conduct a content review. If the said department conducts a content review at the request of the entity, it may charge fees in accordance with the standards approved by the competent department for pricing under the State Council.  
The competent administrative department for publication under the State Council may prohibit the import of specific publications.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　出版物进口经营单位应当在进口出版物前将拟进口的出版物目录报省级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门备案；省级以上人民政府出版行政主管部门发现有禁止进口的或者暂缓进口的出版物的，应当及时通知出版物进口经营单位并通报海关。对通报禁止进口或者暂缓进口的出版物，出版物进口经营单位不得进口，海关不得放行。  
出版物进口备案的具体办法由国务院出版行政主管部门制定。

**Article 46** Before importing publications, the entity for the import of publications shall submit the catalogue of publications to be imported to the competent administrative department for publication at or above the provincial level for recordation. If the said department discovers any publication whose import is prohibited or temporarily suspended, it shall promptly notify the entity and inform the Customs. For publications notified as prohibited or temporarily suspended, the entity shall not import them, and the Customs shall not release them.  
Specific measures for the recordation of imported publications shall be formulated by the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　发行进口出版物的，必须从依法设立的出版物进口经营单位进货。

**Article 47** Any entity distributing imported publications must purchase them from entities established in accordance with law for the import of publications.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　出版物进口经营单位在境内举办境外出版物展览，必须报经国务院出版行政主管部门批准。未经批准，任何单位和个人不得举办境外出版物展览。  
依照前款规定展览的境外出版物需要销售的，应当按照国家有关规定办理相关手续。

**Article 48** If an entity for the import of publications holds an exhibition of overseas publications within the territory, it must report to and obtain approval from the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council. Without such approval, no entity or individual may hold an exhibition of overseas publications.  
If the overseas publications exhibited in accordance with the preceding paragraph need to be sold, the relevant formalities shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　监督与管理

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　出版行政主管部门应当加强对本行政区域内出版单位出版活动的日常监督管理；出版单位的主办单位及其主管机关对所属出版单位出版活动负有直接管理责任，并应当配合出版行政主管部门督促所属出版单位执行各项管理规定。  
出版单位和出版物进口经营单位应当按照国务院出版行政主管部门的规定，将从事出版活动和出版物进口活动的情况向出版行政主管部门提出书面报告。

**Article 49** The competent administrative department for publication shall strengthen the routine supervision and administration of the publishing activities of publishing entities within its administrative region. The sponsoring unit and its competent authority of a publishing entity shall bear direct management responsibility for the publishing activities of the entity concerned, and shall cooperate with the competent administrative department for publication in supervising the entity to implement various management regulations.  
Publishing entities and entities for the import of publications shall, in accordance with the regulations of the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council, submit written reports to the said department on their publishing activities and import activities.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　出版行政主管部门履行下列职责：  
（一）对出版物的出版、印刷、复制、发行、进口单位进行行业监管，实施准入和退出管理；  
（二）对出版活动进行监管，对违反本条例的行为进行查处；  
（三）对出版物内容和质量进行监管；  
（四）根据国家有关规定对出版从业人员进行管理。

**Article 50** The competent administrative department for publication shall perform the following duties:  
(1) conducting industry supervision over entities engaged in publication, printing, reproduction, distribution, and import of publications, and implementing access and exit management;  
(2) supervising publishing activities, investigating and punishing acts in violation of these Regulations;  
(3) supervising the content and quality of publications;  
(4) managing publishing professionals in accordance with relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　出版行政主管部门根据有关规定和标准，对出版物的内容、编校、印刷或者复制、装帧设计等方面质量实施监督检查。

**Article 51** The competent administrative department for publication shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations and standards, implement supervision and inspection over the quality of publications in terms of content, editing and proofreading, printing or reproduction, and binding and layout design.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国务院出版行政主管部门制定出版单位综合评估办法，对出版单位分类实施综合评估。  
出版物的出版、印刷或者复制、发行和进口经营单位不再具备行政许可的法定条件的，由出版行政主管部门责令限期改正；逾期仍未改正的，由原发证机关撤销行政许可。

**Article 52** The competent administrative department for publication under the State Council shall formulate comprehensive evaluation measures for publishing entities, and implement comprehensive evaluation by categories.  
If an entity engaged in publication, printing or reproduction, distribution, or import of publications no longer meets the statutory conditions for an administrative license, the competent administrative department for publication shall order it to rectify within a specified time limit. If it fails to rectify within the time limit, the original licensing authority shall revoke the administrative license.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家对在出版单位从事出版专业技术工作的人员实行职业资格制度；出版专业技术人员通过国家专业技术人员资格考试取得专业技术资格。具体办法由国务院人力资源社会保障主管部门、国务院出版行政主管部门共同制定。

**Article 53** The State implements a professional qualification system for personnel engaged in publishing technical work in publishing entities. Publishing professionals shall obtain professional technical qualifications by passing the national professional and technical personnel qualification examination. Specific measures shall be jointly formulated by the competent department for human resources and social security under the State Council and the competent administrative department for publication under the State Council.

### 第七章　保障与奖励

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家制定有关政策，保障、促进出版产业和出版事业的发展与繁荣。

**Article 54** The State shall formulate relevant policies to safeguard and promote the development and prosperity of the publication industry and publication undertakings.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家支持、鼓励下列优秀的、重点的出版物的出版：  
（一）对阐述、传播宪法确定的基本原则有重大作用的；  
（二）对弘扬社会主义核心价值体系，在人民中进行爱国主义、集体主义、社会主义和民族团结教育以及弘扬社会公德、职业道德、家庭美德有重要意义的；  
（三）对弘扬民族优秀文化，促进国际文化交流有重大作用的；  
（四）对推进文化创新，及时反映国内外新的科学文化成果有重大贡献的；  
（五）对服务农业、农村和农民，促进公共文化服务有重大作用的；  
（六）其他具有重要思想价值、科学价值或者文化艺术价值的。

**Article 55** The State supports and encourages the publication of the following excellent and key publications:  
(1) those playing a significant role in elaborating and disseminating the fundamental principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) those of great significance in promoting the core socialist values, conducting education among the people in patriotism, collectivism, socialism and national unity, and advocating social morality, professional ethics and family virtues;  
(3) those playing a major role in promoting the excellent traditional culture of the nation and facilitating international cultural exchange;  
(4) those making significant contributions to advancing cultural innovation and promptly reflecting new scientific and cultural achievements at home and abroad;  
(5) those playing a vital role in serving agriculture, rural areas and farmers, and promoting public cultural services;  
(6) others having important ideological, scientific or cultural and artistic value.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家对教科书的出版发行，予以保障。  
国家扶持少数民族语言文字出版物和盲文出版物的出版发行。  
国家对在少数民族地区、边疆地区、经济不发达地区和在农村发行出版物，实行优惠政策。

**Article 56** The State shall guarantee the publication and distribution of textbooks.  
The State shall support the publication and distribution of publications in minority languages and Braille publications.  
The State shall implement preferential policies for the distribution of publications in minority regions, border areas, economically underdeveloped areas and rural areas.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　报纸、期刊交由邮政企业发行的，邮政企业应当保证按照合同约定及时、准确发行。  
承运出版物的运输企业，应当对出版物的运输提供方便。

**Article 57** Where newspapers and periodicals are distributed through postal enterprises, such enterprises shall ensure timely and accurate distribution in accordance with the contract.  
Transport enterprises that carry publications shall provide convenience for their transport.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　对为发展、繁荣出版产业和出版事业作出重要贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 58** Units and individuals that have made significant contributions to the development and prosperity of the publication industry and publication undertakings shall be rewarded in accordance with the relevant State regulations.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对非法干扰、阻止和破坏出版物出版、印刷或者复制、进口、发行的行为，县级以上各级人民政府出版行政主管部门及其他有关部门，应当及时采取措施，予以制止。

**Article 59** With respect to acts of unlawfully interfering with, obstructing or disrupting the publication, printing or reproduction, import or distribution of publications, the competent administrative departments for publication of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall promptly take measures to stop them.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　出版行政主管部门或者其他有关部门的工作人员，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处，批准不符合法定条件的申请人取得许可证、批准文件，或者不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果的，依法给予降级直至开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** If any staff member of the competent administrative department for publication or other relevant departments, by taking advantage of his or her position, accepts property or other benefits from others, approves applicants who do not meet the statutory conditions to obtain licenses or approval documents, or fails to perform supervisory duties, or fails to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences, he or she shall be given a sanction of demotion or even dismissal in accordance with law. If the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　未经批准，擅自设立出版物的出版、印刷或者复制、进口单位，或者擅自从事出版物的出版、印刷或者复制、进口、发行业务，假冒出版单位名称或者伪造、假冒报纸、期刊名称出版出版物的，由出版行政主管部门、工商行政管理部门依照法定职权予以取缔；依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，没收出版物、违法所得和从事违法活动的专用工具、设备，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；侵犯他人合法权益的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 61** Where, without approval, a unit is established for the publication, printing, reproduction or import of publications, or a person engages without approval in the publication, printing, reproduction, import or distribution of publications, or publications are published under the name of a counterfeit publishing unit or a forged or counterfeit name of a newspaper or periodical, the competent publication administration department and the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, in accordance with their statutory functions and powers, ban such act; criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the publications, illegal gains and special tools and equipment used for the illegal activities shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan shall be imposed concurrently if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the legitimate rights and interests of others are infringed upon, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　有下列行为之一，触犯刑律的，依照刑法有关规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由出版行政主管部门责令限期停业整顿，没收出版物、违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，可以处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
（一）出版、进口含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容的出版物的；  
（二）明知或者应知出版物含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容而印刷或者复制、发行的；  
（三）明知或者应知他人出版含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容的出版物而向其出售或者以其他形式转让本出版单位的名称、书号、刊号、版号、版面，或者出租本单位的名称、刊号的。

**Article 62** Where any of the following acts is committed and constitutes a criminal offence, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the competent publication administration department shall order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, confiscate the publications and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is more than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) Publishing or importing publications containing contents prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations;  
(2) Printing, reproducing or distributing publications while knowing or having reason to know that they contain contents prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations;  
(3) Selling or otherwise transferring the name, book number, periodical number, edition number, layout of the publishing unit, or leasing the name or periodical number of the unit to any person who publishes publications containing contents prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations while knowing or having reason to know of such circumstances.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收出版物、违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，违法经营额不足1万元的，并处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令限期停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）进口、印刷或者复制、发行国务院出版行政主管部门禁止进口的出版物的；  
（二）印刷或者复制走私的境外出版物的。  
发行进口出版物未从本条例规定的出版物进口经营单位进货的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收出版物、违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令限期停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证。

**Article 63** Where any of the following acts is committed, the competent publication administration department shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the publications and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is more than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the situation, it may restrict the development of relevant production and business activities, order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, or the original licensing authority may lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license:  
(1) Importing, printing, reproducing or distributing publications prohibited from import by the competent publication administration department of the State Council;  
(2) Printing or reproducing smuggled overseas publications.  
Where imported publications are distributed without purchasing from the publication import business units specified in these Regulations, the competent publication administration department shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the publications and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the situation, it may restrict the development of relevant production and business activities, order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, or the original licensing authority may lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　走私出版物的，依照刑法关于走私罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由海关依照海关法的规定给予行政处罚。

**Article 64** Where publications are smuggled, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on smuggling crimes; if the act is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the Customs shall impose administrative penalties in accordance with the provisions of the Customs Law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门没收出版物、违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令限期停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）出版单位委托未取得出版物印刷或者复制许可的单位印刷或者复制出版物的；  
（二）印刷或者复制单位未取得印刷或者复制许可而印刷或者复制出版物的；  
（三）印刷或者复制单位接受非出版单位和个人的委托印刷或者复制出版物的；  
（四）印刷或者复制单位未履行法定手续印刷或者复制境外出版物的，印刷或者复制的境外出版物没有全部运输出境的；  
（五）印刷或者复制单位、发行单位或者个体工商户印刷或者复制、发行未署出版单位名称的出版物的。  
有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门没收出版物、违法所得，违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，违法经营额不足1万元的，并处5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令限期停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）印刷或者复制单位、发行单位或者个体工商户印刷或者复制、发行伪造、假冒出版单位名称或者报纸、期刊名称的出版物的；  
（二）出版、印刷、发行单位出版、印刷、发行未经依法审定的中学小学教科书，或者非依照本条例规定确定的单位从事中学小学教科书的出版、发行业务的。

**Article 65** Where any of the following acts is committed, the competent publication administration department shall confiscate the publications and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the situation, it may restrict the development of relevant production and business activities, order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, or the original licensing authority may lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license:  
(1) A publishing unit entrusts the printing or reproduction of publications to a unit that has not obtained a license for printing or reproduction of publications;  
(2) A printing or reproduction unit prints or reproduces publications without obtaining a license for printing or reproduction;  
(3) A printing or reproduction unit accepts entrustment from a non-publishing unit or individual to print or reproduce publications;  
(4) A printing or reproduction unit prints or reproduces overseas publications without fulfilling the statutory procedures, or the printed or reproduced overseas publications are not all transported out of the territory;  
(5) A printing or reproduction unit, distribution unit, or individual industrial or commercial household prints, reproduces or distributes publications that do not bear the name of a publishing unit.  
Where any of the following acts is committed, the competent publication administration department shall confiscate the publications and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal business turnover if the illegal business turnover is more than 10,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the situation, it may restrict the development of relevant production and business activities, order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, or the original licensing authority may lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license:  
(1) A printing or reproduction unit, distribution unit, or individual industrial or commercial household prints, reproduces or distributes publications bearing forged or counterfeit names of publishing units or newspapers or periodicals;  
(2) A publishing, printing or distribution unit publishes, prints or distributes primary or secondary school textbooks that have not been examined and approved according to law, or a unit not determined in accordance with these Regulations engages in the publication or distribution of primary or secondary school textbooks.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　出版单位有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，给予警告，没收违法经营的出版物、违法所得，违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款，没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处25万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，视情形限制开展相关生产经营活动、责令限期停业整顿，或者由原发证机关降低相关资质等级直至吊销许可证：  
（一）出售或者以其他形式转让本出版单位的名称、书号、刊号、版号、版面，或者出租本单位的名称、刊号的；  
（二）利用出版活动谋取其他不正当利益的。

**Article 66** Where a publishing unit commits any of the following acts, the competent publication administration department shall order cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, confiscate the publications and illegal gains from the illegal operation, and impose a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the illegal gains if the illegal gains are more than 50,000 yuan, or a fine of not more than 250,000 yuan if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, depending on the situation, it may restrict the development of relevant production and business activities, order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit, or the original licensing authority may lower the relevant qualification grade or even revoke the license:  
(1) Selling or otherwise transferring the name, book number, periodical number, edition number or layout of the publishing unit, or leasing the name or periodical number of the unit;  
(2) Using publishing activities to seek other improper benefits.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　有下列行为之一的，由出版行政主管部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，责令限期停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
（一）出版单位变更名称、主办单位或者其主管机关、业务范围，合并或者分立，出版新的报纸、期刊，或者报纸、期刊改变名称，以及出版单位变更其他事项，未依照本条例的规定到出版行政主管部门办理审批、变更登记手续的；  
（二）出版单位未将其年度出版计划和涉及国家安全、社会安定等方面的重大选题备案的；  
（三）出版单位未依照本条例的规定送交出版物的样本的；  
（四）印刷或者复制单位未依照本条例的规定留存备查的材料的；  
（五）出版进口经营单位未将其进口的出版物目录报送备案的；  
（六）出版单位擅自中止出版活动超过180日的；  
（七）出版物发行单位、出版物进口经营单位未依照本条例的规定办理变更审批手续的；  
（八）出版物质量不符合有关规定和标准的。

**Article 67** Where any of the following acts is committed, the competent publication administration department shall order rectification and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit or the original licensing authority shall revoke the license:  
(1) A publishing unit changes its name, sponsors or competent authority, business scope, merges or splits, publishes a new newspaper or periodical, or changes the name of a newspaper or periodical, or changes other matters without going through the formalities of examination and approval or modification registration with the competent publication administration department in accordance with these Regulations;  
(2) A publishing unit fails to file for record its annual publication plan and major topics concerning national security or social stability;  
(3) A publishing unit fails to submit samples of publications in accordance with these Regulations;  
(4) A printing or reproduction unit fails to retain materials for future reference in accordance with these Regulations;  
(5) A publication import business unit fails to submit the catalogue of imported publications for record;  
(6) A publishing unit suspends its publishing activities without approval for more than 180 days;  
(7) A publication distribution unit or publication import business unit fails to go through modification approval formalities in accordance with these Regulations;  
(8) The quality of publications fails to meet relevant provisions and standards.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　未经批准，举办境外出版物展览的，由出版行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收出版物、违法所得；情节严重的，责令限期停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 68** Where an exhibition of overseas publications is held without approval, the competent publication administration department shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the publications and illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of business for rectification within a time limit or the original licensing authority shall revoke the license.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　印刷或者复制、批发、零售、出租、散发含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)、第二十六条禁止内容的出版物或者其他非法出版物的，当事人对非法出版物的来源作出说明、指认，经查证属实的，没收出版物、违法所得，可以减轻或者免除其他行政处罚。

**Article 69** Where a person prints, reproduces, wholesales, retails, rents or distributes publications containing contents prohibited by Articles 25 and 26 of these Regulations or other illegal publications, if the party gives an explanation and identification of the source of the illegal publications and such explanation is verified to be true, the publications and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and other administrative penalties may be mitigated or waived.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　单位违反本条例被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，其法定代表人或者主要负责人自许可证被吊销之日起10年内不得担任出版、印刷或者复制、进口、发行单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人。  
出版从业人员违反本条例规定，情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销其资格证书。

**Article 70** Where a unit violates these Regulations and is subjected to the administrative penalty of revocation of its license, its legal representative or principal responsible person shall not, within ten years from the date of revocation of the license, serve as the legal representative or principal responsible person of any unit engaged in publication, printing or reproduction, import or distribution of publications.  
Where a publication practitioner violates these Regulations, if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke his/her qualification certificate.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款必须全部上缴国库。  
本条例所称违法所得，是指实施违法行为扣除成本后的获利数额，没有成本或者成本难以计算的，实施违法行为所取得的款项即为违法所得。

**Article 71** Where an administrative penalty of a fine is imposed in accordance with these Regulations, the decision on the fine and the collection of the fine shall be separated in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; all fines collected must be turned over to the State Treasury.  
The term "illegal gains" as used in these Regulations means the profits obtained from the illegal act after deducting the costs. If there are no costs or the costs are difficult to calculate, the money obtained from the illegal act shall be deemed illegal gains.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　行政法规对音像制品和电子出版物的出版、复制、进口、发行另有规定的，适用其规定。  
接受境外机构或者个人赠送出版物的管理办法、订户订购境外出版物的管理办法、网络出版审批和管理办法，由国务院出版行政主管部门根据本条例的原则另行制定。

**Article 72** Where there are other provisions in administrative regulations on the publication, reproduction, import or distribution of audio-visual products and electronic publications, such provisions shall prevail.  
The administrative measures for accepting publications donated by overseas institutions or individuals, the administrative measures for subscriptions to overseas publications, and the administrative measures for the examination and approval and administration of online publications shall be formulated separately by the competent publication administration department of the State Council in accordance with the principles of these Regulations.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本条例自2002年2月1日起施行。1997年1月2日国务院发布的《出版管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 73** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2002. The Regulations on the Administration of Publications promulgated by the State Council on January 2, 1997 shall be abolished simultaneously.
