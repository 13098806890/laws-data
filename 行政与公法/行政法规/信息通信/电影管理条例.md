# 电影管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2001-12-25  
**生效日期**：2002-02-01

> (2001年12月12日国务院第50次常务会议通过　2001年12月25日中华人民共和国国务院令第342号公布　自2002年2月1日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对电影行业的管理，发展和繁荣电影事业，满足人民群众文化生活需要，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内的故事片、纪录片、科教片、美术片、专题片等电影片的制片、进口、出口、发行和放映等活动。 第三条　从事电影片的制片、进口、出口、发行和放映等活动，应当遵守宪法和有关法律、法规，坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向。 第四条　国务院广播电影电视行政部门主管全国电影工作。 县级以上地方人民政府管理电影的行政部门(以下简称电影行政部门)，依照本条例的规定负责本行政区域内的电影管理工作。 第五条　国家对电影摄制、进口、出口、发行、放映和电影片公映实行许可制度。未经许可，任何单位和个人不得从事电影片的摄制、进口、发行、放映活动，不得进口、出口、发行、放映未取得许可证的电影片。 依照本条例发放的许可证和批准文件，不得出租、出借、出售或者以其他任何形式转让。 第六条　全国性电影行业的社会团体按照其章程，在国务院广播电影电视行政部门指导下，实行自律管理。 第七条　国家对为电影事业发展做出显著贡献的单位和个人，给予奖励。 第二章　电影制片 第八条　设立电影制片单位，应当具备下列条件： (一)有电影制片单位的名称、章程； (二)有符合国务院广播电影电视行政部门认定的主办单位及其主管机关；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对电影行业的管理，发展和繁荣电影事业，满足人民群众文化生活需要，促进社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of the film industry, developing and prospering the film cause, meeting the cultural life needs of the people, and promoting the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于中华人民共和国境内的故事片、纪录片、科教片、美术片、专题片等电影片的制片、进口、出口、发行和放映等活动。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to such activities as production, import, export, distribution and projection of feature films, documentaries, science and education films, art films, special subject films and other films within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　从事电影片的制片、进口、出口、发行和放映等活动，应当遵守宪法和有关法律、法规，坚持为人民服务、为社会主义服务的方向。

**Article 3** Units and individuals engaged in such activities as production, import, export, distribution and projection of films shall comply with the Constitution and relevant laws and regulations, and shall adhere to the direction of serving the people and serving socialism.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院广播电影电视行政部门主管全国电影工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府管理电影的行政部门(以下简称电影行政部门)，依照本条例的规定负责本行政区域内的电影管理工作。

**Article 4** The administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall be in charge of the national film work.  
The administrative department in charge of films under the local people's governments at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the film administrative department) shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, be responsible for the film administration within their respective administrative areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对电影摄制、进口、出口、发行、放映和电影片公映实行许可制度。未经许可，任何单位和个人不得从事电影片的摄制、进口、发行、放映活动，不得进口、出口、发行、放映未取得许可证的电影片。  
依照本条例发放的许可证和批准文件，不得出租、出借、出售或者以其他任何形式转让。

**Article 5** The State shall apply a licensing system to film production, import, export, distribution, projection and public screening. Without a license, no unit or individual may engage in film production, import, distribution or projection activities, nor may they import, export, distribute or project films for which no license has been obtained.  
The licenses and approval documents issued in accordance with these Regulations shall not be rented, lent, sold or transferred in any other form.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　全国性电影行业的社会团体按照其章程，在国务院广播电影电视行政部门指导下，实行自律管理。

**Article 6** Social organizations of the national film industry shall, in accordance with their articles of association and under the guidance of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, practice self-discipline management.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对为电影事业发展做出显著贡献的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** The State shall give awards to units and individuals that have made remarkable contributions to the development of the film cause.

### 第二章　电影制片

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立电影制片单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有电影制片单位的名称、章程；  
(二)有符合国务院广播电影电视行政部门认定的主办单位及其主管机关；  
(三)有确定的业务范围；  
(四)有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和专业人员；  
(五)有适应业务范围需要的资金、场所和设备；  
(六)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
审批设立电影制片单位，除依照前款所列条件外，还应当符合国务院广播电影电视行政部门制定的电影制片单位总量、布局和结构的规划。

**Article 8** To establish a film production unit, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having a name and articles of association of the film production unit;  
(2) having sponsoring units and their competent authorities that meet the requirements recognized by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council;  
(3) having a definite business scope;  
(4) having the organizational structure and professional personnel that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(5) having the funds, premises and equipment that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(6) other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.  
When examining and approving the establishment of a film production unit, in addition to the conditions listed in the preceding paragraph, the plan for the total number, distribution and structure of film production units formulated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请设立电影制片单位，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影行政部门审核同意后，报国务院广播电影电视行政部门审批。  
申请书应当载明下列内容：  
(一)电影制片单位的名称、地址和经济性质；  
(二)电影制片单位的主办单位的名称、地址、性质及其主管机关；  
(三)电影制片单位的法定代表人的姓名、住址、资格证明文件；  
(四)电影制片单位的资金来源和数额。

**Article 9** An application to establish a film production unit shall be submitted to the film administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located for examination and consent, and then to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for approval.  
The application shall specify the following:  
(1) the name, address and economic nature of the film production unit;  
(2) the name, address and nature of the sponsoring unit of the film production unit and its competent authority;  
(3) the name, address and qualification certificate documents of the legal representative of the film production unit;  
(4) the source and amount of funds of the film production unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院广播电影电视行政部门应当自收到设立电影制片单位的申请书之日起90日内，作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门发给《摄制电影许可证》，申请人持《摄制电影许可证》到国务院工商行政管理部门办理登记手续，依法领取营业执照；不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 10** The administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall, within 90 days from the date of receipt of the application for establishing a film production unit, make a decision to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant. If approved, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall issue the License for Film Production to the applicant, and the applicant shall, with the License for Film Production, go through the registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council and obtain a business license according to law; if not approved, the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　电影制片单位以其全部法人财产，依法享有民事权利，承担民事责任。

**Article 11** A film production unit shall, with all its legal person property, enjoy civil rights and bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　电影制片单位变更、终止，应当报国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准，并依法到原登记的工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记。

**Article 12** The change or termination of a film production unit shall be reported to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for approval, and the unit shall go through the formalities for change registration or cancellation of registration with the original registered administrative department for industry and commerce according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　电影制片单位可以从事下列活动：  
(一)摄制电影片；  
(二)按照国家有关规定制作本单位摄制的电影片的复制品；  
(三)按照国家有关规定在全国范围发行本单位摄制并被许可公映的电影片及其复制品；  
(四)按照国家有关规定出口本单位摄制并被许可公映的电影片及其复制品。

**Article 13** A film production unit may engage in the following activities:  
(1) producing films;  
(2) making reproductions of films produced by itself in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(3) distributing, within the whole country, films produced by itself and permitted for public screening and reproductions thereof in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(4) exporting films produced by itself and permitted for public screening and reproductions thereof in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　电影制片单位应当建立、健全管理制度，保证电影片的质量。

**Article 14** A film production unit shall establish and perfect a management system to guarantee the quality of films.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　电影制片单位对其摄制的电影片，依法享有著作权。

**Article 15** A film production unit shall enjoy copyright in the films it produces according to law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　电影制片单位以外的单位独立从事电影摄制业务，须报经国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准，并持批准文件到工商行政管理部门办理相应的登记手续。  
电影制片单位以外的单位经批准后摄制电影片，应当事先到国务院广播电影电视行政部门领取一次性《摄制电影片许可证(单片)》，并参照电影制片单位享有权利、承担义务。具体办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门制定。

**Article 16** Where a unit other than a film production unit independently engages in film production business, it must submit an application to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for approval, and go through the corresponding registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce with the approval document.  
After approval, a unit other than a film production unit that produces films shall, in advance, obtain from the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council a one-time License for Film Production (Single Film), and shall enjoy rights and bear obligations by reference to a film production unit. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励企业、事业单位和其他社会组织以及个人以资助、投资的形式参与摄制电影片。具体办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门制定。

**Article 17** The State encourages enterprises, institutions and other social organizations as well as individuals to participate in film production in the form of financial assistance or investment. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　电影制片单位经国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准，可以与境外电影制片者合作摄制电影片；其他单位和个人不得与境外电影制片者合作摄制电影片。  
电影制片单位和持有《摄制电影片许可证(单片)》的单位经国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准，可以到境外从事电影片摄制活动。  
境外组织或者个人不得在中华人民共和国境内独立从事电影片摄制活动。

**Article 18** A film production unit may, with the approval of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, co-produce films with overseas film producers; no other unit or individual may co-produce films with overseas film producers.  
A film production unit and a unit holding the License for Film Production (Single Film) may, with the approval of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, engage in film production activities overseas.  
No overseas organization or individual may independently engage in film production activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　中外合作摄制电影片，应当由中方合作者事先向国务院广播电影电视行政部门提出立项申请。国务院广播电影电视行政部门征求有关部门的意见后，经审查符合规定的，发给申请人一次性《中外合作摄制电影片许可证》。申请人取得《中外合作摄制电影片许可证》后，应当按照国务院广播电影电视行政部门的规定签订中外合作摄制电影片合同。

**Article 19** For Sino-foreign co-production of films, the Chinese co-producer shall, in advance, submit a project application to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council. After soliciting opinions from relevant departments, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall, upon examination and finding it conforms to the provisions, issue a one-time License for Sino-Foreign Co-Production of Films to the applicant. After obtaining the License for Sino-Foreign Co-Production of Films, the applicant shall sign a contract for Sino-foreign co-production of films in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中外合作摄制电影片需要进口设备、器材、胶片、道具的，中方合作者应当持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的批准文件到海关办理进口或者临时进口手续。

**Article 20** Where equipment, apparatus, film stock or props need to be imported for Sino-foreign co-production of films, the Chinese co-producer shall, with the approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the formalities for import or temporary import with the Customs.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　境外电影制片者同中方合作者合作或者以其他形式在中华人民共和国境内摄制电影片，应当遵守中华人民共和国的法律、法规，尊重中华民族的风俗、习惯。

**Article 21** Overseas film producers that co-produce films with Chinese co-producers or produce films in the territory of the People's Republic of China in other forms shall comply with the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China and respect the customs and habits of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　电影底片、样片的冲洗及后期制作，应当在中华人民共和国境内完成。有特殊技术要求确需在境外完成的，应当单项申请，报经国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准后，按照批准文件载明的要求执行。

**Article 22** The development and post-production of film negatives and samples shall be completed within the territory of the People's Republic of China. Where special technical requirements necessitate completion overseas, a separate application shall be submitted, and after approval by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, the work shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements specified in the approval document.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　电影洗印单位不得洗印加工未取得《摄制电影许可证》或者《摄制电影片许可证(单片)》的单位摄制的电影底片、样片，不得洗印加工未取得《电影片公映许可证》的电影片拷贝。  
电影洗印单位接受委托洗印加工境外的电影底片、样片和电影片拷贝的，应当事先经国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准，并持批准文件依法向海关办理有关进口手续。洗印加工的电影底片、样片和电影片拷贝必须全部运输出境。

**Article 23** A film processing unit may not develop or process film negatives or samples produced by a unit that has not obtained the License for Film Production or the License for Film Production (Single Film), and may not develop or process film copies for which the License for Public Screening of Films has not been obtained.  
Where a film processing unit accepts entrustment to develop or process overseas film negatives, samples and film copies, it shall, in advance, obtain the approval of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, and with the approval document, go through the relevant import formalities with the Customs according to law. The developed or processed film negatives, samples and film copies must all be transported out of the country.

### 第三章　电影审查

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家实行电影审查制度。  
未经国务院广播电影电视行政部门的电影审查机构(以下简称电影审查机构)审查通过的电影片，不得发行、放映、进口、出口。  
供科学研究、教学参考的专题片进口和中国电影资料馆进口电影资料片，依照[本条例第三十二条](#art-32)的规定办理。

**Article 24** The State shall implement a film censorship system.  
Without examination and approval by the film censorship institution under the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the film censorship institution), no film may be distributed, projected, imported or exported.  
The import of special subject films for scientific research or teaching reference and the import of film archives by the China Film Archive shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　电影片禁止载有下列内容：  
(一)反对宪法确定的基本原则的；  
(二)危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
(三)泄露国家秘密、危害国家安全或者损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
(四)煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结，或者侵害民族风俗、习惯的；  
(五)宣扬邪教、迷信的；  
(六)扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
(七)宣扬淫秽、赌博、暴力或者教唆犯罪的；  
(八)侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
(九)危害社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
(十)有法律、行政法规和国家规定禁止的其他内容的。  
电影技术质量应当符合国家标准。

**Article 25** Films are prohibited from containing the following content:  
(1) that which opposes the basic principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) that which endangers the unity, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the State;  
(3) that which discloses State secrets, endangers State security or damages the honor and interests of the State;  
(4) that which incites ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, undermines ethnic unity, or infringes upon ethnic customs and habits;  
(5) that which advocates cults or superstitions;  
(6) that which disrupts social order or undermines social stability;  
(7) that which advocates obscenity, gambling, violence or instigates crimes;  
(8) that which insults or slanders others and infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(9) that which endangers social morality or fine ethnic cultural traditions;  
(10) other content prohibited by laws, administrative regulations and State provisions.  
The technical quality of films shall conform to national standards.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　电影制片单位应当依照[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)的规定，负责电影剧本投拍和电影片出厂前的审查。  
电影制片单位依照前款规定对其准备投拍的电影剧本审查后，应当报电影审查机构备案；电影审查机构可以对报备案的电影剧本进行审查，发现有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止内容的，应当及时通知电影制片单位不得投拍。具体办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门制定。

**Article 26** A film production unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 of these Regulations, be responsible for the censorship of film scripts for production and the censorship of films before leaving the factory.  
After censoring the film scripts it intends to produce in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, a film production unit shall report them to the film censorship institution for record; the film censorship institution may censor the film scripts so reported, and if it finds any content prohibited by Article 25 of these Regulations, it shall promptly notify the film production unit that it may not proceed with production. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　电影制片单位应当在电影片摄制完成后，报请电影审查机构审查；电影进口经营单位应当在办理电影片临时进口手续后，报请电影审查机构审查。  
电影审查收费标准由国务院价格主管部门会同国务院广播电影电视行政部门规定。

**Article 27** A film production unit shall, after completing the production of a film, submit it to the film censorship institution for censorship; a film import business unit shall, after going through the formalities for temporary import of the film, submit it to the film censorship institution for censorship.  
The fee standard for film censorship shall be prescribed by the competent department of price under the State Council jointly with the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　电影审查机构应当自收到报送审查的电影片之日起30日内，将审查决定书面通知送审单位。审查合格的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门发给《电影片公映许可证》。  
电影制片单位或者电影进口经营单位应当将《电影片公映许可证》证号印制在该电影片拷贝第一本片头处。  
审查不合格，经修改报送重审的，审查期限依照本条第一款的规定重新计算。

**Article 28** The film censorship institution shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the film submitted for censorship, notify the unit that submitted the film of the censorship decision in writing. If the film passes censorship, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall issue the License for Public Screening of Films.  
The film production unit or the film import business unit shall print the license number of the License for Public Screening of Films on the head of the first reel of the film copy.  
If the film fails censorship and is resubmitted after revision, the time limit for censorship shall be recalculated in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　电影制片单位和电影进口经营单位对电影片审查决定不服的，可以自收到审查决定之日起30日内向国务院广播电影电视行政部门的电影复审机构申请复审；复审合格的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门发给《电影片公映许可证》。

**Article 29** If a film production unit or a film import business unit disagrees with the censorship decision on a film, it may, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the censorship decision, apply to the film review institution under the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for review; if the film passes the review, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall issue the License for Public Screening of Films.

### 第四章　电影进口出口

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　电影进口业务由国务院广播电影电视行政部门指定电影进口经营单位经营；未经指定，任何单位或者个人不得经营电影进口业务。

**Article 30** The film import business shall be operated by film import business units designated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council; without such designation, no unit or individual may operate the film import business.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　进口供公映的电影片，进口前应当报送电影审查机构审查。  
报送电影审查机构审查的电影片，由指定的电影进口经营单位持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的临时进口批准文件到海关办理电影片临时进口手续；临时进口的电影片经电影审查机构审查合格并发给《电影片公映许可证》和进口批准文件后，由电影进口经营单位持进口批准文件到海关办理进口手续。

**Article 31** Imported films for public screening shall, before import, be submitted to the film censorship institution for censorship.  
For films submitted to the film censorship institution for censorship, the designated film import business unit shall, with the temporary import approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the formalities for temporary import of the films with the Customs; after the temporarily imported films pass the censorship of the film censorship institution and the License for Public Screening of Films and the import approval document are issued, the film import business unit shall, with the import approval document, go through the import formalities with the Customs.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　进口供科学研究、教学参考的专题片，进口单位应当报经国务院有关行政主管部门审查批准，持批准文件到海关办理进口手续，并于进口之日起30日内向国务院广播电影电视行政部门备案。但是，不得以科学研究、教学的名义进口故事片。  
中国电影资料馆进口电影资料片，可以直接到海关办理进口手续。中国电影资料馆应当将其进口的电影资料片按季度向国务院广播电影电视行政部门备案。  
除本条规定外，任何单位或者个人不得进口未经国务院广播电影电视行政部门审查合格的电影片。

**Article 32** For imported special subject films for scientific research or teaching reference, the importing unit shall submit an application to the relevant administrative department under the State Council for examination and approval, go through the import formalities with the Customs with the approval document, and file a record with the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council within 30 days from the date of import. However, no feature film may be imported under the pretext of scientific research or teaching.  
The China Film Archive may directly go through the import formalities with the Customs for imported film archives. The China Film Archive shall file a record with the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council on a quarterly basis regarding the film archives it imports.  
Except as provided in this Article, no unit or individual may import films that have not passed censorship by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　电影进口经营单位应当在取得电影作品著作权人使用许可后，在许可的范围内使用电影作品；未取得使用许可的，任何单位和个人不得使用进口电影作品。

**Article 33** A film import business unit shall, after obtaining the use permission from the copyright owner of the film work, use the film work within the scope of the permission; without obtaining such use permission, no unit or individual may use an imported film work.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　电影制片单位出口本单位制作的电影片的，应当持《电影片公映许可证》到海关办理电影片出口手续。  
中外合作摄制电影片出口的，中方合作者应当持《电影片公映许可证》到海关办理出口手续。中外合作摄制电影片素材出口的，中方合作者应当持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的批准文件到海关办理出口手续。  
中方协助摄制电影片或者电影片素材出境的，中方协助者应当持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的批准文件到海关办理出境手续。

**Article 34** Where a film production unit exports films produced by itself, it shall, with the License for Public Screening of Films, go through the export formalities for the films with the Customs.  
Where a film co-produced by Chinese and foreign parties is exported, the Chinese co-producer shall, with the License for Public Screening of Films, go through the export formalities with the Customs. Where materials of a film co-produced by Chinese and foreign parties are exported, the Chinese co-producer shall, with the approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the export formalities with the Customs.  
Where a Chinese party assists in the production of a film or film materials to be taken out of the country, the Chinese assistant shall, with the approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the exit formalities with the Customs.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　举办中外电影展、国际电影节，提供电影片参加境外电影展、电影节等，应当报国务院广播电影电视行政部门批准。  
参加前款规定的电影展、电影节的电影片，须报国务院广播电影电视行政部门审查批准。参加境外电影展、电影节的电影片经批准后，参展者应当持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的批准文件到海关办理电影片临时出口手续。参加在中国境内举办的中外电影展、国际电影节的境外电影片经批准后，举办者应当持国务院广播电影电视行政部门的批准文件到海关办理临时进口手续。

**Article 35** The holding of Chinese-foreign film exhibitions or international film festivals, and the provision of films for participation in overseas film exhibitions or film festivals, etc., shall be reported to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for approval.  
Films participating in the film exhibitions or film festivals specified in the preceding paragraph must be submitted to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council for examination and approval. After approval of films for participation in overseas film exhibitions or film festivals, the participants shall, with the approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the formalities for temporary export of the films with the Customs. After approval of overseas films for participation in Chinese-foreign film exhibitions or international film festivals held within the territory of China, the organizers shall, with the approval document of the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, go through the formalities for temporary import with the Customs.

### 第五章　电影发行和放映

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　设立电影发行单位、电影放映单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有电影发行单位、电影放映单位的名称、章程；  
(二)有确定的业务范围；  
(三)有适应业务范围需要的组织机构和专业人员；  
(四)有适应业务范围需要的资金、场所和设备；  
(五)法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 36** To establish a film distribution unit or a film projection unit, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) having a name and articles of association of the film distribution unit or film projection unit;  
(2) having a definite business scope;  
(3) having the organizational structure and professional personnel that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(4) having the funds, premises and equipment that meet the needs of the business scope;  
(5) other conditions as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　设立电影发行单位，应当向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影行政部门提出申请；设立跨省、自治区、直辖市的电影发行单位，应当向国务院广播电影电视行政部门提出申请。所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府电影行政部门或者国务院广播电影电视行政部门应当自收到申请书之日起60日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给《电影发行经营许可证》，申请人应当持《电影发行经营许可证》到工商行政管理部门登记，依法领取营业执照；不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 37** To establish a film distribution unit, an application shall be submitted to the film administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located; to establish a film distribution unit covering more than one province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, an application shall be submitted to the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council. The film administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government or the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant. If approved, the Film Distribution Operation License shall be issued, and the applicant shall, with the Film Distribution Operation License, go through the registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license according to law; if not approved, the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　设立电影放映单位，应当向所在地县或者设区的市人民政府电影行政部门提出申请。所在地县或者设区的市人民政府电影行政部门应当自收到申请书之日起60日内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并通知申请人。批准的，发给《电影放映经营许可证》，申请人持《电影放映经营许可证》到所在地工商行政管理部门登记，依法领取营业执照；不批准的，应当说明理由。

**Article 38** To establish a film projection unit, an application shall be submitted to the film administrative department of the people's government of the county or the city divided into districts where the applicant is located. The film administrative department of the people's government of the county or the city divided into districts shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, make a decision to approve or disapprove and notify the applicant. If approved, the Film Projection Operation License shall be issued, and the applicant shall, with the Film Projection Operation License, go through the registration with the local administrative department for industry and commerce and obtain a business license according to law; if not approved, the reasons shall be stated.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　电影发行单位、电影放映单位变更业务范围，或者兼并其他电影发行单位、电影放映单位，或者因合并、分立而设立新的电影发行单位、电影放映单位的，应当依照[本条例第三十七条](#art-37)或者第三十八条的规定办理审批手续，并到工商行政管理部门办理相应的登记手续。  
电影发行单位、电影放映单位变更名称、地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人，或者终止电影发行、放映经营活动的，应当到原登记的工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记，并向原审批的电影行政部门备案。

**Article 39** Where a film distribution unit or a film projection unit changes its business scope, or merges with another film distribution unit or film projection unit, or establishes a new film distribution unit or film projection unit through merger or division, it shall go through the approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 37 or Article 38 of these Regulations, and go through the corresponding registration formalities with the administrative department for industry and commerce.  
Where a film distribution unit or a film projection unit changes its name, address, legal representative or principal responsible person, or terminates its film distribution or projection business activities, it shall go through the formalities for change registration or cancellation of registration with the original registered administrative department for industry and commerce, and file a record with the original approving film administrative department.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　申请从事农村16毫米电影片发行、放映业务的单位或者个人，可以直接到所在地工商行政管理部门办理登记手续，并向所在地县级人民政府电影行政部门备案；备案后，可以在全国农村从事16毫米电影片发行、放映业务。

**Article 40** A unit or individual that applies to engage in the business of distributing or projecting 16mm films in rural areas may directly go through the registration formalities with the local administrative department for industry and commerce and file a record with the local film administrative department of the people's government at the county level; after such record, it may engage in the business of distributing and projecting 16mm films in rural areas throughout the country.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家允许企业、事业单位和其他社会组织以及个人投资建设、改造电影院。  
国家允许以中外合资或者中外合作的方式建设、改造电影院。具体办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门会同国务院文化行政部门、国务院对外经济贸易主管部门按照有关规定制定。

**Article 41** The State permits enterprises, institutions and other social organizations as well as individuals to invest in the construction and renovation of cinemas.  
The State permits the construction and renovation of cinemas by means of Sino-foreign equity joint ventures or Sino-foreign contractual joint ventures. The specific measures shall be formulated by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council jointly with the administrative department of culture under the State Council and the competent department of foreign trade and economic cooperation under the State Council in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　电影片依法取得国务院广播电影电视行政部门发给的《电影片公映许可证》后，方可发行、放映。  
已经取得《电影片公映许可证》的电影片，国务院广播电影电视行政部门在特殊情况下可以作出停止发行、放映或者经修改后方可发行、放映的决定；对决定经修改后方可发行、放映的电影片，著作权人拒绝修改的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门决定停止发行、放映。  
国务院广播电影电视行政部门作出的停止发行、放映的决定，电影发行单位、电影放映单位应当执行。

**Article 42** After a film has lawfully obtained the License for Public Screening of Films issued by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council, it may be distributed and projected.  
For a film that has already obtained the License for Public Screening of Films, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council may, under special circumstances, make a decision to stop its distribution and projection, or to allow its distribution and projection only after revision; if the copyright owner refuses to revise a film that has been decided to be distributed and projected only after revision, the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council shall decide to stop its distribution and projection.  
A film distribution unit or a film projection unit shall implement the decision made by the administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council to stop distribution and projection.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　利用电影片制作音像制品的，应当遵守国家有关音像制品管理的规定。  
任何单位和个人不得利用电影资料片从事或者变相从事经营性的发行、放映活动。

**Article 43** The use of films to produce audio-visual products shall comply with the provisions of the State on the administration of audio-visual products.  
No unit or individual may use film archives to engage in, or in disguised form, engage in commercial distribution or projection activities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　放映电影片，应当符合国家规定的国产电影片与进口电影片放映的时间比例。  
放映单位年放映国产电影片的时间不得低于年放映电影片时间总和的三分之二。

**Article 44** The projection of films shall conform to the time ratio between domestic films and imported films as prescribed by the State.  
The annual projection time of domestic films by a projection unit shall not be less than two-thirds of the total annual projection time of films.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　电影放映单位应当维护电影院的公共秩序和环境卫生，保证观众的安全与健康。

**Article 45** Film projection units shall maintain public order and environmental sanitation in cinemas, and guarantee the safety and health of the audience.

### 第六章　电影事业的保障

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家建立和完善适应社会主义市场经济体制的电影管理体制，发展电影事业。

**Article 46** The State shall establish and improve a film management system adapted to the socialist market economy system, and develop the film cause.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家保障电影创作自由，重视和培养电影专业人才，重视和加强电影理论研究，繁荣电影创作，提高电影质量。

**Article 47** The State shall guarantee freedom of film creation, attach importance to and train film professionals, attach importance to and strengthen film theory research, prosper film creation, and improve film quality.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家建立电影事业发展专项资金，并采取其他优惠措施，支持电影事业的发展。  
电影事业发展专项资金缴纳单位应当按照国家有关规定履行缴纳义务。

**Article 48** The State shall establish a special fund for the development of the film industry and adopt other preferential measures to support the development of the film industry.  
Units that pay into the special fund for the development of the film industry shall fulfill their payment obligations in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　电影事业发展专项资金扶持、资助下列项目：  
(一)国家倡导并确认的重点电影片的摄制和优秀电影剧本的征集；  
(二)重点制片基地的技术改造；  
(三)电影院的改造和放映设施的技术改造；  
(四)少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和农村地区的电影事业的发展；  
(五)需要资助的其他项目。

**Article 49** The special fund for the development of the film industry shall support and subsidize the following projects:  
(1) the production of key films advocated and recognized by the State and the solicitation of outstanding film scripts;  
(2) technical renovation of key production bases;  
(3) renovation of cinemas and technical renovation of projection facilities;  
(4) the development of the film industry in ethnic minority areas, remote poverty-stricken areas and rural areas;  
(5) other projects in need of subsidy.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家鼓励、扶持科学教育片、纪录片、美术片及儿童电影片的制片、发行和放映。

**Article 50** The State shall encourage and support the production, distribution and projection of science and education films, documentaries, art films and children's films.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家对少数民族地区、边远贫困地区和农村地区发行、放映电影实行优惠政策。  
国家对从事农村16毫米电影片发行、放映业务的单位和个人予以扶持。具体办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门、国务院文化行政部门会同国务院财政部门规定。

**Article 51** The State shall implement preferential policies for the distribution and projection of films in ethnic minority areas, remote and poverty-stricken areas, and rural areas.  
The State shall support entities and individuals engaged in the distribution and projection of 16mm films in rural areas. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council and the cultural administrative department of the State Council jointly with the finance department of the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上地方人民政府制定的本行政区域建设规划，应当包括电影院和放映设施的建设规划。  
改建、拆除电影院和放映设施，应当报经所在地县级以上地方人民政府电影行政部门审查批准，县级以上地方人民政府电影行政部门应当依据国家有关规定作出批准或者不批准的决定。

**Article 52** The construction plan for the administrative region formulated by the local people's government at or above the county level shall include the construction plan for cinemas and projection facilities.  
Alteration or demolition of cinemas and projection facilities shall be submitted to the local film administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level for examination and approval, and the film administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall make a decision of approval or disapproval in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上地方人民政府电影行政部门和其他有关行政部门，对干扰、阻止和破坏电影片的制片、发行、放映的行为，应当及时采取措施予以制止，并依法查处。  
大众传播媒体不得宣扬非法电影。

**Article 53** The film administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level and other relevant administrative departments shall promptly take measures to stop and investigate and deal with according to law acts that interfere with, obstruct, or sabotage the production, distribution, and projection of films.  
Mass media shall not promote illegal films.

### 第七章　罚则

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院广播电影电视行政部门和县级以上地方人民政府电影行政部门或者其他有关部门及其工作人员，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处，批准不符合法定设立条件的电影片的制片、发行和放映单位，或者不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，给予降级或者撤职的行政处分。

**Article 54** Where the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council, the film administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, or other relevant departments and their staff members, taking advantage of their position to accept money or property or other benefits from others, approve a film production, distribution, or projection entity that does not meet the statutory establishment conditions, or fail to perform their supervisory duties, or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, causing serious consequences, the persons in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of bribery, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the conduct is not serious enough for criminal punishment, administrative sanctions of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，擅自设立电影片的制片、发行、放映单位，或者擅自从事电影制片、进口、发行、放映活动的，由工商行政管理部门予以取缔；依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，没收违法经营的电影片和违法所得以及进行违法经营活动的专用工具、设备；违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 55** Anyone who, in violation of these Regulations, establishes a film production, distribution, or projection entity without authorization, or engages in film production, import, distribution, or projection activities without authorization, shall be banned by the administrative department for industry and commerce; criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operations; if the conduct is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the illegally operated films and illegal gains, as well as the specialized tools and equipment used for the illegal business activities, shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　摄制含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止内容的电影片，或者洗印加工、进口、发行、放映明知或者应知含有[本条例第二十五条](#art-25)禁止内容的电影片的，依照刑法有关规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由电影行政部门责令停业整顿，没收违法经营的电影片和违法所得；违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 56** Anyone who produces a film containing content prohibited under Article 25 of these Regulations, or develops, prints, imports, distributes, or projects a film that is known or should be known to contain content prohibited under Article 25 of these Regulations, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the conduct is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the film administrative department shall order the suspension of business for rectification, confiscate the illegally operated films and illegal gains; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the license shall be revoked by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　走私电影片，依照刑法关于走私罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由海关依法给予行政处罚。

**Article 57** Anyone who smuggles films shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of smuggling; if the conduct is not serious enough for criminal punishment, the Customs shall impose administrative penalties according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　出口、发行、放映未取得《电影片公映许可证》的电影片的，由电影行政部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法经营的电影片和违法所得；违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得10倍以上15倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并责令停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证。

**Article 58** Anyone who exports, distributes, or projects a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit shall be ordered by the film administrative department to cease the illegal act, and the illegally operated films and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 15 times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operation shall be ordered to suspend for rectification or the license shall be revoked by the original issuing authority.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有下列行为之一的，由电影行政部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法经营的电影片和违法所得；违法所得5万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足5万元的，并处10万元以上30万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并责令停业整顿或者由原发证机关吊销许可证：  
(一)未经批准，擅自与境外组织或者个人合作摄制电影，或者擅自到境外从事电影摄制活动的；  
(二)擅自到境外进行电影底片、样片的冲洗或者后期制作，或者未按照批准文件载明的要求执行的；  
(三)洗印加工未取得《摄制电影许可证》、《摄制电影片许可证(单片)》的单位摄制的电影底片、样片，或者洗印加工未取得《电影片公映许可证》的电影片拷贝的；  
(四)未经批准，接受委托洗印加工境外电影底片、样片或者电影片拷贝，或者未将洗印加工的境外电影底片、样片或者电影片拷贝全部运输出境的；  
(五)利用电影资料片从事或者变相从事经营性的发行、放映活动的；  
(六)未按照规定的时间比例放映电影片，或者不执行国务院广播电影电视行政部门停止发行、放映决定的。

**Article 59** Anyone who commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the film administrative department to cease the illegal act, and the illegally operated films and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 50,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 50,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the operation shall be ordered to suspend for rectification or the license shall be revoked by the original issuing authority:  
(1) without approval, cooperating with an overseas organization or individual in the production of a film without authorization, or engaging in film production activities abroad without authorization;  
(2) going abroad without authorization for the development or post-production of film negatives or positive prints, or failing to comply with the requirements specified in the approval document;  
(3) developing or printing film negatives or positive prints produced by an entity that has not obtained the Film Production Permit or the Film Production Permit (Single Film), or developing or printing copies of a film that has not obtained the Film Public Projection Permit;  
(4) without approval, accepting a commission to develop or print overseas film negatives, positive prints, or copies of films, or failing to transport all developed or printed overseas film negatives, positive prints, or copies out of the country;  
(5) using film reference materials to engage in or covertly engage in commercial distribution or projection activities;  
(6) failing to project films in accordance with the prescribed time ratio, or failing to comply with the decision of the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council to cease distribution or projection.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　境外组织、个人在中华人民共和国境内独立从事电影片摄制活动的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门责令停止违法活动，没收违法摄制的电影片和进行违法活动的专用工具、设备，并处30万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 60** Where an overseas organization or individual independently engages in film production activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal activities, confiscate the illegally produced films and the specialized tools and equipment used for the illegal activities, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　未经批准，擅自举办中外电影展、国际电影节，或者擅自提供电影片参加境外电影展、电影节的，由国务院广播电影电视行政部门责令停止违法活动，没收违法参展的电影片和违法所得；违法所得2万元以上的，并处违法所得5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足2万元的，并处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 61** Anyone who, without approval, holds a Chinese-foreign film exhibition or an international film festival without authorization, or provides films to participate in an overseas film exhibition or film festival without authorization, shall be ordered by the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council to cease the illegal activities, and the illegally exhibited films and illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 20,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 20,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　未经批准，擅自改建、拆除电影院或者放映设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府电影行政部门责令限期恢复电影院或者放映设施的原状，给予警告，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予纪律处分。

**Article 62** Anyone who, without approval, alters or demolishes a cinema or projection facilities without authorization shall be ordered by the film administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level to restore the cinema or projection facilities to their original condition within a time limit, given a warning, and disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed on the persons in charge directly responsible and other persons directly responsible according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　单位违反本条例，被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，应当按照国家有关规定到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记；逾期未办理的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 63** Where an entity violates these Regulations and is subject to the administrative penalty of license revocation, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, go through the formalities of change registration or cancellation of registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce; if it fails to do so within the time limit, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke its business license.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　单位违反本条例，被处以吊销许可证行政处罚的，其法定代表人或者主要负责人自吊销许可证之日起5年内不得担任电影片的制片、进口、出口、发行和放映单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人。  
个人违反本条例，未经批准擅自从事电影片的制片、进口、发行业务，或者擅自举办中外电影展、国际电影节或者擅自提供电影片参加境外电影展、电影节的，5年内不得从事相关电影业务。

**Article 64** Where an entity violates these Regulations and is subject to the administrative penalty of license revocation, its legal representative or principal responsible person shall not serve as the legal representative or principal responsible person of a film production, import, export, distribution, or projection entity within five years from the date of license revocation.  
Where an individual violates these Regulations and, without approval, engages in film production, import, or distribution business without authorization, or holds a Chinese-foreign film exhibition or an international film festival without authorization, or provides films to participate in an overseas film exhibition or film festival without authorization, the individual shall not engage in the relevant film business within five years.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　未按照国家有关规定履行电影事业发展专项资金缴纳义务的，由省级以上人民政府电影行政部门责令限期补交，并自欠缴之日起按日加收所欠缴金额万分之五的滞纳金。

**Article 65** Anyone who fails to fulfill the obligation to pay the special fund for the development of the film industry in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State shall be ordered by the film administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level to make up the payment within a time limit, and a late payment fee of 0.05% of the overdue amount per day shall be imposed from the date the payment becomes overdue.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款应当全部上缴国库。

**Article 66** The administrative penalty of a fine imposed in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations shall, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations, be implemented with the separation of the fine decision from the fine collection; all fines collected shall be turned over to the State treasury.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家实行《摄制电影许可证》和《电影发行经营许可证》、《电影放映经营许可证》年检制度。年检办法由国务院广播电影电视行政部门制定。

**Article 67** The State implements an annual inspection system for the Film Production Permit, the Film Distribution Operation Permit, and the Film Projection Operation Permit. The annual inspection measures shall be formulated by the radio, film and television administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例自2002年2月1日起施行。1996年6月19日国务院发布的《电影管理条例》同时废止。

**Article 68** These Regulations shall come into force as of February 1, 2002. The Regulations on the Administration of Films promulgated by the State Council on June 19, 1996 shall be abolished simultaneously.
