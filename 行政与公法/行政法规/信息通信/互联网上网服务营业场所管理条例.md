# 互联网上网服务营业场所管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对互联网上网服务营业场所的管理，规范经营者的经营行为，维护公众和经营者的合法权益，保障互联网上网服务经营活动健康发展，促进社会主义精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the administration of business premises for internet access services, regulate the operational conduct of operators, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of the public and operators, ensure the healthy development of internet access service operations, and promote the construction of socialist spiritual civilization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称互联网上网服务营业场所，是指通过计算机等装置向公众提供互联网上网服务的网吧、电脑休闲室等营业性场所。  
学校、图书馆等单位内部附设的为特定对象获取资料、信息提供上网服务的场所，应当遵守有关法律、法规，不适用本条例。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "business premises for internet access services" refer to commercial premises such as internet cafes and computer lounges that provide internet access services to the public through computers and other devices.  
Premises attached to schools, libraries and other entities that provide internet access services for specific persons to obtain materials and information shall comply with relevant laws and regulations, and these Regulations shall not apply to them.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当遵守有关法律、法规的规定，加强行业自律，自觉接受政府有关部门依法实施的监督管理，为上网消费者提供良好的服务。  
互联网上网服务营业场所的上网消费者，应当遵守有关法律、法规的规定，遵守社会公德，开展文明、健康的上网活动。

**Article 3** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, strengthen industry self-discipline, voluntarily accept the supervision and administration implemented by relevant government departments according to law, and provide good services to internet consumers.  
An internet consumer at internet access service business premises shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, observe social ethics, and engage in civilized and healthy online activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府文化行政部门负责互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的设立审批，并负责对依法设立的互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位经营活动的监督管理；公安机关负责对互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的信息网络安全、治安及消防安全的监督管理；工商行政管理部门负责对互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位登记注册和营业执照的管理，并依法查处无照经营活动；电信管理等其他有关部门在各自职责范围内，依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规的规定，对互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位分别实施有关监督管理。

**Article 4** The cultural administration departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the approval of establishment of business entities operating internet access service premises and for the supervision and administration of the business activities of business entities operating internet access service premises established according to law; the public security organs shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of information network security, public security and fire safety of business entities operating internet access service premises; the administrative departments for industry and commerce shall be responsible for the registration and business license administration of business entities operating internet access service premises and shall investigate and punish unlicensed business activities according to law; other relevant departments such as telecommunications administration shall, within their respective functions and duties, exercise relevant supervision and administration over business entities operating internet access service premises respectively in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　文化行政部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门和其他有关部门及其工作人员不得从事或者变相从事互联网上网服务经营活动，也不得参与或者变相参与互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的经营活动。

**Article 5** The cultural administration departments, public security organs, administrative departments for industry and commerce, other relevant departments and their staff members shall not engage in or covertly engage in internet access service operations, nor shall they participate in or covertly participate in the business activities of business entities operating internet access service premises.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织对互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的经营活动进行监督，并对有突出贡献的给予奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages citizens, legal persons and other organizations to supervise the business activities of business entities operating internet access service premises, and shall give rewards to those who make outstanding contributions.

### 第二章　设立

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的经营活动实行许可制度。未经许可，任何组织和个人不得从事互联网上网服务经营活动。

**Article 7** The State applies a licensing system to the business activities of business entities operating internet access service premises. Without a license, no organization or individual may engage in internet access service operations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位从事互联网上网服务经营活动，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有企业的名称、住所、组织机构和章程；  
（二）有与其经营活动相适应的资金；  
（三）有与其经营活动相适应并符合国家规定的消防安全条件的营业场所；  
（四）有健全、完善的信息网络安全管理制度和安全技术措施；  
（五）有固定的网络地址和与其经营活动相适应的计算机等装置及附属设备；  
（六）有与其经营活动相适应并取得从业资格的安全管理人员、经营管理人员、专业技术人员；  
（七）法律、行政法规和国务院有关部门规定的其他条件。  
互联网上网服务营业场所的最低营业面积、计算机等装置及附属设备数量、单机面积的标准，由国务院文化行政部门规定。  
审批从事互联网上网服务经营活动，除依照本条第一款、第二款规定的条件外，还应当符合国务院文化行政部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府文化行政部门规定的互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的总量和布局要求。

**Article 8** A business entity operating internet access service premises that engages in internet access service operations shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having a name, domicile, organizational structure and articles of association of the enterprise;  
(2) having funds commensurate with its business activities;  
(3) having business premises that are commensurate with its business activities and meet the fire safety conditions prescribed by the State;  
(4) having a sound and comprehensive information network security management system and security technical measures;  
(5) having a fixed network address and computers and other devices and ancillary equipment commensurate with its business activities;  
(6) having safety management personnel, operational management personnel and professional technical personnel who are commensurate with its business activities and have obtained the relevant qualifications;  
(7) other conditions prescribed by laws, administrative regulations and the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The minimum business area, the number of computers and other devices and ancillary equipment, and the standard for floor space per computer for internet access service business premises shall be prescribed by the cultural administration department of the State Council.  
When examining and approving engagement in internet access service operations, in addition to the conditions prescribed in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, the requirements for the total quantity and layout of business entities operating internet access service premises prescribed by the cultural administration department of the State Council and the cultural administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall also be met.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中学、小学校园周围200米范围内和居民住宅楼（院）内不得设立互联网上网服务营业场所。

**Article 9** No internet access service business premises may be established within a radius of 200 metres around junior or senior high school campuses or within residential buildings (courtyards).

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位申请从事互联网上网服务经营活动，应当向县级以上地方人民政府文化行政部门提出申请，并提交下列文件：  
（一）企业营业执照和章程；  
（二）法定代表人或者主要负责人的身份证明材料；  
（三）资金信用证明；  
（四）营业场所产权证明或者租赁意向书；  
（五）依法需要提交的其他文件。

**Article 10** A business entity operating internet access service premises that applies to engage in internet access service operations shall submit an application to the cultural administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level and shall submit the following documents:  
(1) the business licence and articles of association of the enterprise;  
(2) the identity certification documents of the legal representative or the main person in charge;  
(3) a certificate of capital credibility;  
(4) the property right certificate for the business premises or the letter of intent for lease;  
(5) other documents required to be submitted according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　文化行政部门应当自收到申请之日起20个工作日内作出决定；经审查，符合条件的，发给同意筹建的批准文件。  
申请人还应当依照有关消防管理法律法规的规定办理审批手续。  
申请人取得消防安全批准文件后，向文化行政部门申请最终审核。文化行政部门应当自收到申请之日起15个工作日内依据[本条例第八条](#art-8)的规定作出决定；经实地检查并审核合格的，发给《网络文化经营许可证》。  
对申请人的申请，有关部门经审查不符合条件的，或者经审核不合格的，应当分别向申请人书面说明理由。  
文化行政部门发放《网络文化经营许可证》的情况或互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位拟开展经营活动的情况，应当及时向同级公安机关通报或报备。

**Article 11** The cultural administration department shall make a decision within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application; upon examination, if the conditions are met, it shall issue an approval document agreeing to the preparation.  
The applicant shall also go through the examination and approval formalities in accordance with the provisions of relevant fire management laws and regulations.  
After obtaining the fire safety approval document, the applicant shall apply to the cultural administration department for the final review. The cultural administration department shall make a decision within 15 working days from the date of receipt of the application in accordance with Article 8 of these Regulations; upon on-site inspection and review, if qualified, it shall issue a "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations".  
If the relevant department finds, upon examination, that the applicant's application does not meet the conditions, or upon review, that it is not qualified, it shall state the reasons in writing to the applicant separately.  
The cultural administration department shall promptly notify or report to the public security organ at the same level of the issuance of the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" or of the intended commencement of business activities by the business entity operating internet access service premises.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位不得涂改、出租、出借或者以其他方式转让《网络文化经营许可证》。

**Article 12** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall not alter, lease, lend or transfer in any other manner the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations".

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位变更营业场所地址或者对营业场所进行改建、扩建，变更计算机数量或者其他重要事项的，应当经原审核机关同意。  
互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人、注册资本、网络地址或者终止经营活动的，应当依法到工商行政管理部门办理变更登记或者注销登记，并到文化行政部门、公安机关办理有关手续或者备案。

**Article 13** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises changes the address of the business premises, or carries out reconstruction or expansion of the business premises, or changes the number of computers or other significant matters, it shall obtain the consent of the original examining authority.  
Where a business entity operating internet access service premises changes its name, domicile, legal representative or main person in charge, registered capital, or network address, or terminates its business activities, it shall go through the formalities of change registration or cancellation of registration with the administrative department for industry and commerce according to law, and shall go through the relevant formalities or file a record with the cultural administration department and the public security organ.

### 第三章　经营

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位和上网消费者不得利用互联网上网服务营业场所制作、下载、复制、查阅、发布、传播或者以其他方式使用含有下列内容的信息：  
（一）反对宪法确定的基本原则的；  
（二）危害国家统一、主权和领土完整的；  
（三）泄露国家秘密，危害国家安全或者损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
（四）煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结，或者侵害民族风俗、习惯的；  
（五）破坏国家宗教政策，宣扬邪教、迷信的；  
（六）散布谣言，扰乱社会秩序，破坏社会稳定的；  
（七）宣传淫秽、赌博、暴力或者教唆犯罪的；  
（八）侮辱或者诽谤他人，侵害他人合法权益的；  
（九）危害社会公德或者民族优秀文化传统的；  
（十）含有法律、行政法规禁止的其他内容的。

**Article 14** Business entities operating internet access service premises and internet consumers shall not use the internet access service business premises to create, download, copy, browse, publish, disseminate, or use in any other manner information containing any of the following content:  
(1) opposing the fundamental principles established by the Constitution;  
(2) endangering national unification, sovereignty and territorial integrity;  
(3) divulging State secrets, endangering national security, or harming national honour and interests;  
(4) inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, undermining ethnic unity, or infringing upon ethnic customs or habits;  
(5) undermining the State's religious policy, or propagating cults or superstitions;  
(6) spreading rumours, disrupting social order, or undermining social stability;  
(7) promoting obscenity, gambling, violence, or inciting crime;  
(8) insulting or slandering others, or infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others;  
(9) endangering social ethics or the excellent cultural traditions of the nation;  
(10) containing other content prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位和上网消费者不得进行下列危害信息网络安全的活动：  
（一）故意制作或者传播计算机病毒以及其他破坏性程序的；  
（二）非法侵入计算机信息系统或者破坏计算机信息系统功能、数据和应用程序的；  
（三）进行法律、行政法规禁止的其他活动的。

**Article 15** Business entities operating internet access service premises and internet consumers shall not engage in any of the following activities that endanger information network security:  
(1) intentionally creating or disseminating computer viruses or other destructive programs;  
(2) illegally intruding into computer information systems or destroying the functions, data or application programs of computer information systems;  
(3) engaging in other activities prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当通过依法取得经营许可证的互联网接入服务提供者接入互联网，不得采取其他方式接入互联网。  
互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位提供上网消费者使用的计算机必须通过局域网的方式接入互联网，不得直接接入互联网。

**Article 16** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall connect to the internet through an internet access service provider that has obtained a business licence according to law, and shall not connect to the internet by other means.  
The computers provided by a business entity operating internet access service premises for use by internet consumers must be connected to the internet through a local area network, and shall not be directly connected to the internet.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位不得经营非网络游戏。

**Article 17** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall not operate non-online games.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位和上网消费者不得利用网络游戏或者其他方式进行赌博或者变相赌博活动。

**Article 18** Business entities operating internet access service premises and internet consumers shall not engage in gambling or disguised gambling activities through online games or by other means.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当实施经营管理技术措施，建立场内巡查制度，发现上网消费者有[本条例第十四条](#art-14)、第十五条、第十八条所列行为或者有其他违法行为的，应当立即予以制止并向文化行政部门、公安机关举报。

**Article 19** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall implement technical measures for operational management, establish an on-site patrol system, and if it discovers that an internet consumer has committed any act listed in Articles 14, 15, or 18 of these Regulations or has other illegal acts, it shall immediately stop such acts and report to the cultural administration department and the public security organ.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当在营业场所的显著位置悬挂《网络文化经营许可证》和营业执照。

**Article 20** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall display the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" and the business licence in a conspicuous position at the business premises.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位不得接纳未成年人进入营业场所。  
互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当在营业场所入口处的显著位置悬挂未成年人禁入标志。

**Article 21** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall not admit minors into the business premises.  
A business entity operating internet access service premises shall display a sign prohibiting minors from entering in a conspicuous position at the entrance of the business premises.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　互联网上网服务营业场所每日营业时间限于8时至24时。

**Article 22** The daily business hours of internet access service business premises shall be limited to from 8:00 to 24:00.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当对上网消费者的身份证等有效证件进行核对、登记，并记录有关上网信息。登记内容和记录备份保存时间不得少于60日，并在文化行政部门、公安机关依法查询时予以提供。登记内容和记录备份在保存期内不得修改或者删除。

**Article 23** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall check and register the valid identification documents such as identity cards of internet consumers, and record relevant internet access information. The retention period for the registration content and record backups shall be not less than 60 days, and they shall be provided when the cultural administration department or the public security organ makes a lawful inquiry. The registration content and record backups shall not be modified or deleted during the retention period.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位应当依法履行信息网络安全、治安和消防安全职责，并遵守下列规定：  
（一）禁止明火照明和吸烟并悬挂禁止吸烟标志；  
（二）禁止带入和存放易燃、易爆物品；  
（三）不得安装固定的封闭门窗栅栏；  
（四）营业期间禁止封堵或者锁闭门窗、安全疏散通道和安全出口；  
（五）不得擅自停止实施安全技术措施。

**Article 24** A business entity operating internet access service premises shall fulfill its duties of information network security, public security and fire safety according to law, and shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) prohibiting open flame lighting and smoking, and displaying a no-smoking sign;  
(2) prohibiting the bringing in and storage of flammable and explosive articles;  
(3) not installing fixed grilles that seal doors and windows;  
(4) during business hours, prohibiting the blocking or locking of doors and windows, safety evacuation passages and safety exits;  
(5) not suspending the implementation of safety technical measures without authorization.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　文化行政部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门或者其他有关部门及其工作人员，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物或者其他好处，违法批准不符合法定设立条件的互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位，或者不依法履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予依法查处，触犯刑律的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法关于受贿罪、滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者其他罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予降级、撤职或者开除的行政处分。

**Article 25** Where the cultural administration department, the public security organ, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or any other relevant department or any of its staff members, by taking advantage of their position, accepts money or property or other benefits from others, illegally approves a business entity operating internet access service premises that does not meet the statutory establishment conditions, or fails to perform supervisory duties according to law, or fails to investigate and punish illegal acts discovered according to law, and if the act constitutes a crime, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes, the crime of abuse of power, the crime of dereliction of duty, or other crimes; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions of demotion, removal from office or expulsion shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　文化行政部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门或者其他有关部门的工作人员，从事或者变相从事互联网上网服务经营活动的，参与或者变相参与互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的经营活动的，依法给予降级、撤职或者开除的行政处分。  
文化行政部门、公安机关、工商行政管理部门或者其他有关部门有前款所列行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照前款规定依法给予行政处分。

**Article 26** Where any staff member of the cultural administration department, the public security organ, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or any other relevant department engages in or covertly engages in internet access service operations, or participates in or covertly participates in the business activities of a business entity operating internet access service premises, administrative sanctions of demotion, removal from office or expulsion shall be imposed according to law.  
Where the cultural administration department, the public security organ, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or any other relevant department commits any act listed in the preceding paragraph, administrative sanctions shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons according to law in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违反本条例的规定，擅自从事互联网上网服务经营活动的，由文化行政部门或者由文化行政部门会同公安机关依法予以取缔，查封其从事违法经营活动的场所，扣押从事违法经营活动的专用工具、设备；触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于非法经营罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由文化行政部门没收违法所得及其从事违法经营活动的专用工具、设备；违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额5倍以上10倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款。

**Article 27** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, engages in internet access service operations without authorization, the cultural administration department, or the cultural administration department jointly with the public security organ, shall ban it according to law, seal up the premises used for the illegal business activities, and seize the special tools and equipment used for the illegal business activities; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of illegal business operation; if the act does not constitute a crime, the cultural administration department shall confiscate the illegal gains and the special tools and equipment used for the illegal business activities; if the illegal business turnover is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 5 times but not more than 10 times the illegal business turnover shall also be imposed; if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　文化行政部门应当建立互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的经营活动信用监管制度，建立健全信用约束机制，并及时公布行政处罚信息。

**Article 28** The cultural administration department shall establish a credit supervision system for the business activities of business entities operating internet access service premises, establish and perfect a credit restraint mechanism, and promptly publish information on administrative penalties.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，涂改、出租、出借或者以其他方式转让《网络文化经营许可证》，触犯刑律的，依照刑法关于伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪的规定，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由文化行政部门吊销《网络文化经营许可证》，没收违法所得；违法经营额5000元以上的，并处违法经营额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足5000元的，并处5000元以上1万元以下的罚款。

**Article 29** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, alters, leases, lends or transfers in any other manner the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations", if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of forging, altering, buying or selling official documents, certificates or seals of State organs; if the act does not constitute a crime, the cultural administration department shall revoke the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal business turnover is 5,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal business turnover shall also be imposed; if the illegal business turnover is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，利用营业场所制作、下载、复制、查阅、发布、传播或者以其他方式使用含有[本条例第十四条](#art-14)规定禁止含有的内容的信息，触犯刑律的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由公安机关给予警告，没收违法所得；违法经营额1万元以上的，并处违法经营额2倍以上5倍以下的罚款；违法经营额不足1万元的，并处1万元以上2万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至由文化行政部门吊销《网络文化经营许可证》。  
上网消费者有前款违法行为，触犯刑律的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由公安机关依照治安管理处罚法的规定给予处罚。

**Article 30** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, uses the business premises to create, download, copy, browse, publish, disseminate, or use in any other manner information containing content prohibited by Article 14 of these Regulations, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall give a warning and confiscate the illegal gains; if the illegal business turnover is 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than 2 times but not more than 5 times the illegal business turnover shall also be imposed; if the illegal business turnover is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the cultural administration department may even revoke the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations".  
Where an internet consumer commits the illegal act in the preceding paragraph, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由文化行政部门给予警告，可以并处15000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至吊销《网络文化经营许可证》：  
（一）在规定的营业时间以外营业的；  
（二）接纳未成年人进入营业场所的；  
（三）经营非网络游戏的；  
（四）擅自停止实施经营管理技术措施的；  
（五）未悬挂《网络文化经营许可证》或者未成年人禁入标志的。

**Article 31** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the cultural administration department shall give a warning and may also impose a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" may even be revoked:  
(1) operating outside the prescribed business hours;  
(2) admitting minors into the business premises;  
(3) operating non-online games;  
(4) suspending the implementation of technical measures for operational management without authorization;  
(5) failing to display the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" or the sign prohibiting minors from entering.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公安机关应当自互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位正式开展经营活动20个工作日内，对其依法履行信息网络安全职责情况进行实地检查。检查发现互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位未履行信息网络安全责任的，由公安机关给予警告，可以并处15000元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至由文化行政部门吊销《网络文化经营许可证》。

**Article 32** The public security organ shall, within 20 working days from the date the business entity operating internet access service premises formally commences business activities, conduct an on-site inspection of its performance of information network security duties according to law. If the inspection finds that the business entity operating internet access service premises has failed to perform its information network security responsibilities, the public security organ shall give a warning and may also impose a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" may even be revoked by the cultural administration department.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由文化行政部门、公安机关依据各自职权给予警告，可以并处15000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至由文化行政部门吊销《网络文化经营许可证》：  
（一）向上网消费者提供的计算机未通过局域网的方式接入互联网的；  
（二）未建立场内巡查制度，或者发现上网消费者的违法行为未予制止并向文化行政部门、公安机关举报的；  
（三）未按规定核对、登记上网消费者的有效身份证件或者记录有关上网信息的；  
（四）未按规定时间保存登记内容、记录备份，或者在保存期内修改、删除登记内容、记录备份的；  
（五）变更名称、住所、法定代表人或者主要负责人、注册资本、网络地址或者终止经营活动，未向文化行政部门、公安机关办理有关手续或者备案的。

**Article 33** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the cultural administration department or the public security organ shall, within their respective functions and powers, give a warning and may also impose a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" may even be revoked by the cultural administration department:  
(1) the computers provided to internet consumers are not connected to the internet through a local area network;  
(2) failing to establish an on-site patrol system, or failing to stop illegal acts of internet consumers discovered and report them to the cultural administration department or the public security organ;  
(3) failing to check and register the valid identification documents of internet consumers or record relevant internet access information as required;  
(4) failing to preserve the registration content and record backups for the prescribed period, or modifying or deleting the registration content or record backups during the retention period;  
(5) changing the name, domicile, legal representative or main person in charge, registered capital, or network address, or terminating business activities, without going through the relevant formalities or filing a record with the cultural administration department or the public security organ.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关给予警告，可以并处15000元以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，直至由文化行政部门吊销《网络文化经营许可证》：  
（一）利用明火照明或者发现吸烟不予制止，或者未悬挂禁止吸烟标志的；  
（二）允许带入或者存放易燃、易爆物品的；  
（三）在营业场所安装固定的封闭门窗栅栏的；  
（四）营业期间封堵或者锁闭门窗、安全疏散通道或者安全出口的；  
（五）擅自停止实施安全技术措施的。

**Article 34** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, commits any of the following acts, the public security organ shall give a warning and may also impose a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, it shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, and the "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" may even be revoked by the cultural administration department:  
(1) using open flame lighting, or failing to stop smoking when discovered, or failing to display a no-smoking sign;  
(2) allowing the bringing in or storage of flammable or explosive articles;  
(3) installing fixed grilles that seal doors and windows at the business premises;  
(4) blocking or locking doors and windows, safety evacuation passages or safety exits during business hours;  
(5) suspending the implementation of safety technical measures without authorization.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违反国家有关信息网络安全、治安管理、消防管理、工商行政管理、电信管理等规定，触犯刑律的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由公安机关、工商行政管理部门、电信管理机构依法给予处罚；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证件。

**Article 35** Where a person violates the provisions of the State concerning information network security, public security administration, fire administration, industrial and commercial administration, telecommunications administration, or other matters, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ, the administrative department for industry and commerce, or the telecommunications administration authority shall impose a penalty according to law; if the circumstances are serious, the original licensing authority shall revoke the licence.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位违反本条例的规定，被吊销《网络文化经营许可证》的，自被吊销《网络文化经营许可证》之日起5年内，其法定代表人或者主要负责人不得担任互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人。  
擅自设立的互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位被依法取缔的，自被取缔之日起5年内，其主要负责人不得担任互联网上网服务营业场所经营单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人。

**Article 36** Where a business entity operating internet access service premises has its "Permit for Internet Culture Business Operations" revoked for violating the provisions of these Regulations, within 5 years from the date of revocation, its legal representative or main person in charge shall not serve as the legal representative or main person in charge of any business entity operating internet access service premises.  
Where a business entity operating internet access service premises that was established without authorization is banned according to law, within 5 years from the date of being banned, its main person in charge shall not serve as the legal representative or main person in charge of any business entity operating internet access service premises.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　依照本条例的规定实施罚款的行政处罚，应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，实行罚款决定与罚款收缴分离；收缴的罚款和违法所得必须全部上缴国库。

**Article 37** Where an administrative penalty of a fine is imposed in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the separation of the decision on fines and the collection of fines shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; all fines collected and illegal gains confiscated must be turned over to the State treasury.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本条例自2002年11月15日起施行。2001年4月3日信息产业部、公安部、文化部、国家工商行政管理局发布的《互联网上网服务营业场所管理办法》同时废止。

**Article 38** These Regulations shall come into force as of November 15, 2002. The "Administrative Measures for Internet Access Service Business Premises" promulgated by the Ministry of Information Industry, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Culture, and the State Administration for Industry and Commerce on April 3, 2001 shall be abolished simultaneously.
