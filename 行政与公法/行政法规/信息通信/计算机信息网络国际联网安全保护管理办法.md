# 计算机信息网络国际联网安全保护管理办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2011-01-08  
**生效日期**：1997-12-30

> (1997年12月11日国务院批准　1997年12月16日公安部令第33号发布　根据2011年1月8日《国务院关于废止和修改部分行政法规的决定》修订)

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对计算机信息网络国际联网的安全保护，维护公共秩序和社会稳定，根据《中华人民共和国计算机信息系统安全保护条例》、《中华人民共和国计算机信息网络国际联网管理暂行规定》和其他法律、行政法规的规定，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Security Protection of Computer Information Systems, the Interim Provisions of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of International Networking of Computer Information Networks, and other laws and administrative regulations, for the purpose of strengthening the security protection of international networking of computer information networks and maintaining public order and social stability.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内的计算机信息网络国际联网安全保护管理，适用本办法。

**Article 2** These Measures shall apply to the security protection administration of international networking of computer information networks within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公安部计算机管理监察机构负责计算机信息网络国际联网的安全保护管理工作。  
公安机关计算机管理监察机构应当保护计算机信息网络国际联网的公共安全，维护从事国际联网业务的单位和个人的合法权益和公众利益。

**Article 3** The computer management and supervision institution of the Ministry of Public Security shall be responsible for the security protection administration of international networking of computer information networks.  
The computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ shall protect the public security of international networking of computer information networks and safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of entities and individuals engaged in international networking services as well as the public interest.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　任何单位和个人不得利用国际联网危害国家安全、泄露国家秘密，不得侵犯国家的、社会的、集体的利益和公民的合法权益，不得从事违法犯罪活动。

**Article 4** No entity or individual may use international networking to endanger national security, divulge state secrets, infringe upon the interests of the State, society, or collective, or the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, or engage in illegal or criminal activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人不得利用国际联网制作、复制、查阅和传播下列信息：  
(一)煽动抗拒、破坏宪法和法律、行政法规实施的；  
(二)煽动颠覆国家政权，推翻社会主义制度的；  
(三)煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一的；  
(四)煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视，破坏民族团结的；  
(五)捏造或者歪曲事实，散布谣言，扰乱社会秩序的；  
(六)宣扬封建迷信、淫秽、色情、赌博、暴力、凶杀、恐怖，教唆犯罪的；  
(七)公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人的；  
(八)损害国家机关信誉的；  
(九)其他违反宪法和法律、行政法规的。

**Article 5** No entity or individual may use international networking to produce, reproduce, browse, or disseminate the following information:  
(1) inciting resistance to or undermining the implementation of the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations;  
(2) inciting the overthrow of the state political power or the subversion of the socialist system;  
(3) inciting secession or undermining national unity;  
(4) inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, or undermining ethnic solidarity;  
(5) fabricating or distorting facts, spreading rumors, or disrupting social order;  
(6) promoting feudal superstition, obscenity, pornography, gambling, violence, murder, or terror, or instigating crimes;  
(7) publicly insulting others or fabricating facts to defame others;  
(8) damaging the credibility of state organs;  
(9) other information that violates the Constitution, laws, and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人不得从事下列危害计算机信息网络安全的活动：  
(一)未经允许，进入计算机信息网络或者使用计算机信息网络资源的；  
(二)未经允许，对计算机信息网络功能进行删除、修改或者增加的；  
(三)未经允许，对计算机信息网络中存储、处理或者传输的数据和应用程序进行删除、修改或者增加的；  
(四)故意制作、传播计算机病毒等破坏性程序的；  
(五)其他危害计算机信息网络安全的。

**Article 6** No entity or individual may engage in any of the following activities that endanger computer information network security:  
(1) entering a computer information network or using computer information network resources without permission;  
(2) deleting, modifying, or adding computer information network functions without permission;  
(3) deleting, modifying, or adding data and application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in a computer information network without permission;  
(4) intentionally creating or spreading destructive programs such as computer viruses;  
(5) other activities that endanger computer information network security.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　用户的通信自由和通信秘密受法律保护。任何单位和个人不得违反法律规定，利用国际联网侵犯用户的通信自由和通信秘密。

**Article 7** The freedom and secrecy of communication of users shall be protected by law. No entity or individual may violate legal provisions and use international networking to infringe upon the freedom and secrecy of communication of users.

### 第二章　安全保护责任

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　从事国际联网业务的单位和个人应当接受公安机关的安全监督、检查和指导，如实向公安机关提供有关安全保护的信息、资料及数据文件，协助公安机关查处通过国际联网的计算机信息网络的违法犯罪行为。

**Article 8** Entities and individuals engaged in international networking services shall accept the security supervision, inspection, and guidance of the public security organ, truthfully provide the public security organ with relevant security protection information, materials, and data files, and assist the public security organ in investigating and punishing illegal and criminal acts through computer information networks connected to international networking.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国际出入口信道提供单位、互联单位的主管部门或者主管单位，应当依照法律和国家有关规定负责国际出入口信道、所属互联网络的安全保护管理工作。

**Article 9** Entities providing international gateway channels, competent authorities or responsible units of internetworking units shall, in accordance with the law and relevant state provisions, be responsible for the security protection administration of international gateway channels and their respective internetworks.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　互联单位、接入单位及使用计算机信息网络国际联网的法人和其他组织应当履行下列安全保护职责：  
(一)负责本网络的安全保护管理工作，建立健全安全保护管理制度；  
(二)落实安全保护技术措施，保障本网络的运行安全和信息安全；  
(三)负责对本网络用户的安全教育和培训；  
(四)对委托发布信息的单位和个人进行登记，并对所提供的信息内容按照[本办法第五条](#art-5)进行审核；  
(五)建立计算机信息网络电子公告系统的用户登记和信息管理制度；  
(六)发现有[本办法第四条](#art-4)、第五条、第六条、第七条所列情形之一的，应当保留有关原始记录，并在24小时内向当地公安机关报告；  
(七)按照国家有关规定，删除本网络中含有[本办法第五条](#art-5)内容的地址、目录或者关闭服务器。

**Article 10** Internetworking units, access units, and legal persons and other organizations using international networking of computer information networks shall perform the following security protection duties:  
(1) being responsible for the security protection administration of their own networks, and establishing and improving security protection management systems;  
(2) implementing security protection technical measures to ensure the operational security and information security of their own networks;  
(3) being responsible for the security education and training of users of their own networks;  
(4) registering entities and individuals commissioning the release of information, and reviewing the content of the information provided in accordance with Article 5 of these Measures;  
(5) establishing user registration and information management systems for computer information network electronic bulletin board systems;  
(6) upon discovering any of the circumstances listed in Article 4, 5, 6, or 7 of these Measures, retaining the relevant original records and reporting to the local public security organ within 24 hours;  
(7) in accordance with relevant state provisions, deleting addresses or directories containing content specified in Article 5 of these Measures from their networks, or closing the servers.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　用户在接入单位办理入网手续时，应当填写用户备案表。备案表由公安部监制。

**Article 11** When a user goes through network access procedures at an access unit, the user shall fill in a user registration form. The registration form shall be produced under the supervision of the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　互联单位、接入单位、使用计算机信息网络国际联网的法人和其他组织(包括跨省、自治区、直辖市联网的单位和所属的分支机构)，应当自网络正式联通之日起30日内，到所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关指定的受理机关办理备案手续。  
前款所列单位应当负责将接入本网络的接入单位和用户情况报当地公安机关备案，并及时报告本网络中接入单位和用户的变更情况。

**Article 12** Internetworking units, access units, legal persons and other organizations using international networking of computer information networks (including entities connected across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government and their subordinate branches) shall, within 30 days from the date of formal network connection, go through registration formalities at the accepting organ designated by the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located.  
The units specified in the preceding paragraph shall report to the local public security organ for registration the information on access units and users accessing their networks, and shall promptly report any changes in access units and users within their networks.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　使用公用账号的注册者应当加强对公用账号的管理，建立账号使用登记制度。用户账号不得转借、转让。

**Article 13** The registrant of a shared account shall strengthen the management of the shared account and establish an account usage registration system. User accounts may not be lent or transferred.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　涉及国家事务、经济建设、国防建设、尖端科学技术等重要领域的单位办理备案手续时，应当出具其行政主管部门的审批证明。  
前款所列单位的计算机信息网络与国际联网，应当采取相应的安全保护措施。

**Article 14** When units involved in important fields such as state affairs, economic construction, national defense construction, and advanced science and technology go through registration formalities, they shall produce the approval certificate from their administrative competent department.  
Computer information networks of the units specified in the preceding paragraph that are connected to international networking shall adopt corresponding security protection measures.

### 第三章　安全监督

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　省、自治区、直辖市公安厅(局)，地(市)、县(市)公安局，应当有相应机构负责国际联网的安全保护管理工作。

**Article 15** The public security departments (bureaus) of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, as well as the public security bureaus of prefectures (cities) and counties (cities), shall have corresponding institutions responsible for the security protection administration of international networking.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公安机关计算机管理监察机构应当掌握互联单位、接入单位和用户的备案情况，建立备案档案，进行备案统计，并按照国家有关规定逐级上报。

**Article 16** The computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ shall keep abreast of the registration status of internetworking units, access units, and users, establish registration archives, conduct registration statistics, and report to higher levels level by level in accordance with relevant state provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公安机关计算机管理监察机构应当督促互联单位、接入单位及有关用户建立健全安全保护管理制度。监督、检查网络安全保护管理以及技术措施的落实情况。  
公安机关计算机管理监察机构在组织安全检查时，有关单位应当派人参加。公安机关计算机管理监察机构对安全检查发现的问题，应当提出改进意见，作出详细记录，存档备查。

**Article 17** The computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ shall urge internetworking units, access units, and relevant users to establish and improve security protection management systems, and supervise and inspect the implementation of network security protection management and technical measures.  
When the computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ organizes security inspections, relevant units shall send personnel to participate. The computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ shall propose improvement opinions on problems discovered during security inspections, make detailed records, and file them for future reference.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　公安机关计算机管理监察机构发现含有[本办法第五条](#art-5)所列内容的地址、目录或者服务器时，应当通知有关单位关闭或者删除。

**Article 18** When the computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ discovers addresses, directories, or servers containing content listed in Article 5 of these Measures, it shall notify the relevant units to close or delete them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公安机关计算机管理监察机构应当负责追踪和查处通过计算机信息网络的违法行为和针对计算机信息网络的犯罪案件，对违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)、第七条规定的违法犯罪行为，应当按照国家有关规定移送有关部门或者司法机关处理。

**Article 19** The computer management and supervision institution of the public security organ shall be responsible for tracking and investigating illegal acts through computer information networks and criminal cases targeting computer information networks. For illegal and criminal acts violating Articles 4 and 7 of these Measures, they shall be transferred to relevant departments or judicial organs for handling in accordance with relevant state provisions.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　违反法律、行政法规，有[本办法第五条](#art-5)、第六条所列行为之一的，由公安机关给予警告，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，对个人可以并处5000元以下的罚款，对单位可以并处1.5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并可以给予6个月以内停止联网、停机整顿的处罚，必要时可以建议原发证、审批机构吊销经营许可证或者取消联网资格；构成违反治安管理行为的，依照治安管理处罚法的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 20** Where any act listed in Article 5 or Article 6 of these Measures is committed in violation of laws and administrative regulations, the public security organ shall give a warning; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan may be imposed concurrently on an individual, and a fine of not more than 15,000 yuan may be imposed concurrently on a unit; if the circumstances are serious, a penalty of suspension of network connection or shutdown for rectification for not more than six months may also be imposed, and when necessary, the original licensing or approval authority may be recommended to revoke the business license or cancel the network connection qualification; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列行为之一的，由公安机关责令限期改正，给予警告，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；在规定的限期内未改正的，对单位的主管负责人员和其他直接责任人员可以并处5000元以下的罚款，对单位可以并处1.5万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并可以给予6个月以内的停止联网、停机整顿的处罚，必要时可以建议原发证、审批机构吊销经营许可证或者取消联网资格。  
(一)未建立安全保护管理制度的；  
(二)未采取安全技术保护措施的；  
(三)未对网络用户进行安全教育和培训的；  
(四)未提供安全保护管理所需信息、资料及数据文件，或者所提供内容不真实的；  
(五)对委托其发布的信息内容未进行审核或者对委托单位和个人未进行登记的；  
(六)未建立电子公告系统的用户登记和信息管理制度的；  
(七)未按照国家有关规定，删除网络地址、目录或者关闭服务器的；  
(八)未建立公用账号使用登记制度的；  
(九)转借、转让用户账号的。

**Article 21** Where any of the following acts is committed, the public security organ shall order corrections within a time limit, give a warning, and confiscate any illegal gains if any; if corrections are not made within the prescribed time limit, the person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit may be concurrently fined not more than 5,000 yuan, and the unit may be concurrently fined not more than 15,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the penalty of suspension of network connection or shutdown for rectification for not more than six months may also be imposed, and if necessary, the original issuing or approving authority may be recommended to revoke the business license or cancel the network connection qualification.  
(1) failing to establish a security protection management system;  
(2) failing to adopt security technical protection measures;  
(3) failing to conduct security education and training for network users;  
(4) failing to provide information, data and data files required for security protection management, or the content provided is untrue;  
(5) failing to review the information content entrusted to be published or failing to register the entrusting units and individuals;  
(6) failing to establish a user registration and information management system for electronic bulletin board systems;  
(7) failing to delete network addresses, directories or shut down servers in accordance with relevant state regulations;  
(8) failing to establish a registration system for the use of public accounts;  
(9) lending or transferring user accounts.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违反[本办法第四条](#art-4)、第七条规定的，依照有关法律、法规予以处罚。

**Article 22** Any violation of the provisions of Article 4 and Article 7 of these Measures shall be punished in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违反[本办法第十一条](#art-11)、第十二条规定，不履行备案职责的，由公安机关给予警告或者停机整顿不超过6个月的处罚。

**Article 23** Where any violation of the provisions of Article 11 and Article 12 of these Measures occurs, and the obligation to file for record is not performed, the public security organ shall impose a penalty of a warning or shutdown for rectification for not more than six months.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　与香港特别行政区和台湾、澳门地区联网的计算机信息网络的安全保护管理，参照本办法执行。

**Article 24** The security protection management of computer information networks connected to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the regions of Taiwan and Macao shall be implemented by reference to these Measures.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本办法自1997年12月30日起施行。

**Article 25** These Measures shall come into force as of December 30, 1997.
