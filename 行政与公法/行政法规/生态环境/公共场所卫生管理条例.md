# 公共场所卫生管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为创造良好的公共场所卫生条件，预防疾病，保障人体健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of creating good sanitary conditions in public places, preventing diseases, and protecting human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于下列公共场所：  
（一）宾馆、旅店、招待所；  
（二）公共浴室、理发店、美容店；  
（三）影剧院、录像厅（室）、游艺厅（室）、舞厅、音乐厅；  
（四）体育场（馆）、游泳场（馆）、公园；  
（五）展览馆、博物馆、美术馆、图书馆；  
（六）商场（店）、书店；  
（七）候诊室、候车（机、船）室、公共交通工具。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the following public places:  
(1) Hotels, inns, and hostels;  
(2) Public bathhouses, barber shops, and beauty salons;  
(3) Cinemas, video halls (rooms), amusement halls (rooms), dance halls, and concert halls;  
(4) Stadiums (gymnasiums), swimming pools (facilities), and parks;  
(5) Exhibition halls, museums, art galleries, and libraries;  
(6) Shopping malls (stores) and bookstores;  
(7) Waiting rooms, waiting areas for vehicles (airplanes, ships), and public transportation vehicles.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公共场所的下列项目应符合国家卫生标准和要求：  
（一）空气、微小气候（湿度、温度、风速）；  
（二）水质；  
（三）采光、照明；  
（四）噪音；  
（五）顾客用具和卫生设施。  
公共场所的卫生标准和要求，由国务院疾病预防控制部门负责制定。

**Article 3** The following items in public places shall meet the national hygienic standards and requirements:  
(1) Air and microclimate (humidity, temperature, wind speed);  
(2) Water quality;  
(3) Daylighting and illumination;  
(4) Noise;  
(5) Customer utensils and sanitary facilities.  
The hygienic standards and requirements for public places shall be formulated by the disease prevention and control department of the State Council.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对公共场所实行“卫生许可证”制度。  
“卫生许可证”由设区的市级、县级人民政府疾病预防控制部门签发。

**Article 4** The State shall implement a 'Sanitary Permit' system for public places.  
The 'Sanitary Permit' shall be issued by the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the city with districts or county level.

### 第二章　卫生管理

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公共场所的主管部门应当建立卫生管理制度，配备专职或者兼职卫生管理人员，对所属经营单位（包括个体经营者，下同）的卫生状况进行经常性检查，并提供必要的条件。

**Article 5** The competent departments in charge of public places shall establish hygienic management systems, assign full-time or part-time hygienic management personnel, conduct regular inspections of the sanitary conditions of the operating units under their jurisdiction (including individual operators, the same below), and provide necessary conditions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经营单位应当负责所经营的公共场所的卫生管理，建立卫生责任制度，对本单位的从业人员进行卫生知识的培训和考核工作。

**Article 6** Operating units shall be responsible for the sanitary management of the public places they operate, establish a sanitary responsibility system, and provide training and assessment on hygienic knowledge for their employees.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公共场所直接为顾客服务的人员，持有“健康合格证”方能从事本职工作。患有痢疾、伤寒、病毒性肝炎、活动期肺结核、化脓性或者渗出性皮肤病以及其他有碍公共卫生的疾病的，治愈前不得从事直接为顾客服务的工作。

**Article 7** Personnel who directly serve customers in public places shall hold a 'Health Certificate' before they may engage in their duties. Those who suffer from dysentery, typhoid fever, viral hepatitis, active pulmonary tuberculosis, suppurative or exudative skin diseases, or other diseases that are detrimental to public health shall not engage in work that directly serves customers before being cured.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　除公园、体育场（馆）、公共交通工具和本条第二款规定的实行卫生备案管理的公共场所外，经营单位应当在营业前向设区的市级、县级人民政府疾病预防控制部门申请办理“卫生许可证”。  
录像厅（室）、音乐厅、展览馆、博物馆、美术馆、图书馆、书店的经营单位应当在营业之日起30天内向设区的市级、县级人民政府疾病预防控制部门办理卫生备案。

**Article 8** Except for parks, stadiums (gymnasiums), public transportation vehicles, and public places subject to sanitary filing management as provided in the second paragraph of this Article, operating units shall, before commencing business, apply to the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the city with districts or county level for a 'Sanitary Permit'.  
The operating units of video halls (rooms), concert halls, exhibition halls, museums, art galleries, libraries, and bookstores shall, within 30 days from the date of commencing business, complete sanitary filing with the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the city with districts or county level.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　公共场所因不符合卫生标准和要求造成危害健康事故的，经营单位应妥善处理，并及时报告卫生防疫机构。

**Article 9** Where a public place causes an accident harmful to health due to non-compliance with hygienic standards and requirements, the operating unit shall properly handle it and promptly report it to the sanitary and anti-epidemic institution.

### 第三章　卫生监督

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级卫生防疫机构，负责管辖范围内的公共场所卫生监督工作。  
民航、铁路、交通、厂（场）矿卫生防疫机构对管辖范围内的公共场所，施行卫生监督，并接受当地卫生防疫机构的业务指导。

**Article 10** The sanitary and anti-epidemic institutions at all levels shall be responsible for the sanitary supervision of public places within their jurisdiction.  
The sanitary and anti-epidemic institutions of civil aviation, railways, transportation, and factories (mines) shall carry out sanitary supervision over the public places within their jurisdiction and shall accept the professional guidance of the local sanitary and anti-epidemic institutions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　卫生防疫机构根据需要设立公共场所卫生监督员，执行卫生防疫机构交给的任务。公共场所卫生监督员由同级人民政府发给证书。  
民航、铁路、交通、工矿企业卫生防疫机构的公共场所卫生监督员，由其上级主管部门发给证书。

**Article 11** Health and epidemic prevention agencies shall, as needed, appoint public place health supervisors to perform tasks assigned by such agencies. Public place health supervisors shall be issued certificates by the people's government at the same level.  
Public place health supervisors of health and epidemic prevention agencies in civil aviation, railways, transportation, industrial and mining enterprises shall be issued certificates by their superior competent departments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　卫生防疫机构对公共场所的卫生监督职责：  
（一）对公共场所进行卫生监测和卫生技术指导；  
（二）监督从业人员健康检查，指导有关部门对从业人员进行卫生知识的教育和培训。

**Article 12** The health supervision duties of health and epidemic prevention agencies over public places shall include:  
(1) conducting health monitoring and health technical guidance for public places;  
(2) supervising health examinations of employees and guiding relevant departments in educating and training employees on health knowledge.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　卫生监督员有权对公共场所进行现场检查，索取有关资料，经营单位不得拒绝或隐瞒。卫生监督员对所提供的技术资料有保密的责任。  
公共场所卫生监督员在执行任务时，应佩戴证章、出示证件。

**Article 13** Health supervisors shall have the right to conduct on-site inspections of public places and obtain relevant materials, and business operators shall not refuse or conceal such information. Health supervisors shall have the duty to maintain confidentiality of the technical materials provided.  
Public place health supervisors shall wear badges and present their credentials when performing tasks.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　凡有下列行为之一的单位或者个人，卫生防疫机构可以根据情节轻重，给予警告、罚款、停业整顿、吊销“卫生许可证”的行政处罚：  
（一）卫生质量不符合国家卫生标准和要求，而继续营业的；  
（二）未获得“健康合格证”，而从事直接为顾客服务的；  
（三）拒绝卫生监督的；  
（四）未取得“卫生许可证”擅自营业，或者未办理卫生备案的。  
罚款一律上交国库。

**Article 14** For any entity or individual that commits any of the following acts, the health and epidemic prevention agency may, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose administrative penalties such as a warning, a fine, suspension of business for rectification, or revocation of the "Health Permit":  
(1) continuing business operations when the health quality does not meet the national health standards and requirements;  
(2) engaging in direct service to customers without obtaining a "Health Certificate";  
(3) refusing health supervision;  
(4) operating without a "Health Permit" or failing to complete health record filing.  
All fines shall be turned over to the state treasury.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　违反本条例的规定造成严重危害公民健康的事故或中毒事故的单位或者个人，应当对受害人赔偿损失。  
违反本条例致人残疾或者死亡，构成犯罪的，应由司法机关依法追究直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 15** Any entity or individual that, in violation of these Regulations, causes an accident that seriously endangers the health of citizens or a poisoning accident, shall compensate the victims for losses.  
Where a violation of these Regulations causes disability or death and constitutes a crime, the judicial authorities shall, according to law, investigate the criminal liability of the persons directly responsible.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对罚款、停业整顿及吊销“卫生许可证”的行政处罚不服的，在接到处罚通知之日起15天内，可以向当地人民法院起诉。但对公共场所卫生质量控制的决定应立即执行。对处罚的决定不履行又逾期不起诉的，由卫生防疫机构向人民法院申请强制执行。

**Article 16** Any party that is dissatisfied with an administrative penalty of a fine, suspension of business for rectification, or revocation of the "Health Permit" may, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, initiate a prosecution with the local People's Court. However, decisions on the control of public place health quality shall be implemented immediately. Where a party fails to comply with the penalty decision and does not initiate a prosecution within the time limit, the health and epidemic prevention agency shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公共场所卫生监督机构和卫生监督员必须尽职尽责，依法办事。对玩忽职守，滥用职权，收取贿赂的，由上级主管部门给予直接责任人员行政处分。构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 17** Public place health supervision institutions and health supervisors shall perform their duties diligently and act according to law. Where they neglect their duties, abuse their powers, or accept bribes, the superior competent department shall impose administrative sanctions on the persons directly responsible. If a crime is constituted, the judicial authorities shall, according to law, investigate the criminal liability of the persons directly responsible.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本条例的实施细则由国务院卫生行政部门负责制定。

**Article 18** The detailed implementation rules for these Regulations shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 19** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
