# 放射性同位素与射线装置安全和防护条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-03-02  
**生效日期**：2005-12-01

> (2005年9月14日中华人民共和国国务院令第449号公布　根据2014年7月29日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2019年3月2日《国务院关于修改部分行政法规的决定》第二次修订) 第一章　总则 第一条　为了加强对放射性同位素、射线装置安全和防护的监督管理，促进放射性同位素、射线装置的安全应用，保障人体健康，保护环境，制定本条例。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置，以及转让、进出口放射性同位素的，应当遵守本条例。 本条例所称放射性同位素包括放射源和非密封放射性物质。 第三条　国务院生态环境主管部门对全国放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施统一监督管理。 国务院公安、卫生等部门按照职责分工和本条例的规定，对有关放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施监督管理。 县级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门，按照职责分工和本条例的规定，对本行政区域内放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施监督管理。 第四条　国家对放射源和射线装置实行分类管理。根据放射源、射线装置对人体健康和环境的潜在危害程度，从高到低将放射源分为Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类、Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类，具体分类办法由国务院生态环境主管部门制定；将射线装置分为Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类，具体分类办法由国务院生态环境主管部门商国务院卫生主管部门制定。 第二章　许可和备案 第五条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当依照本章规定取得许可证。 第六条　除医疗使用Ⅰ类放射源、制备正电子发射计算机断层扫描用放射性药物自用的单位外，生产放射性同位素、销售和使用Ⅰ类放射源、销售和使用Ⅰ类射线装置的单位的许可证，由国务院生态环境主管部门审批颁发。 除国务院生态环境主管部门审批颁发的许可证外，其他单位的许可证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门审批颁发。 国务院生态环境主管部门向生产放射性同位素的单位颁发许可证前，应当将申请材料印送其行业主管部门征求意见。 生态环境主管部门应当将审批颁发许可证的情况通报同级公安部门、卫生主管部门。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对放射性同位素、射线装置安全和防护的监督管理，促进放射性同位素、射线装置的安全应用，保障人体健康，保护环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening supervision and administration of the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, promoting the safe application of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, ensuring human health, and protecting the environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置，以及转让、进出口放射性同位素的，应当遵守本条例。  
本条例所称放射性同位素包括放射源和非密封放射性物质。

**Article 2** The production, sale, use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, and the transfer, import or export of radioactive isotopes within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall comply with these Regulations.  
For the purposes of these Regulations, radioactive isotopes include radioactive sources and unsealed radioactive substances.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院生态环境主管部门对全国放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施统一监督管理。  
国务院公安、卫生等部门按照职责分工和本条例的规定，对有关放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施监督管理。  
县级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门，按照职责分工和本条例的规定，对本行政区域内放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作实施监督管理。

**Article 3** The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and administration over the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices nationwide.  
The public security, health and other departments under the State Council shall, according to their respective functions and duties and the provisions of these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the safety and protection of relevant radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices.  
The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties and the provisions of these Regulations, exercise supervision and administration over the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对放射源和射线装置实行分类管理。根据放射源、射线装置对人体健康和环境的潜在危害程度，从高到低将放射源分为Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类、Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类，具体分类办法由国务院生态环境主管部门制定；将射线装置分为Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类，具体分类办法由国务院生态环境主管部门商国务院卫生主管部门制定。

**Article 4** The State shall implement classified administration of radioactive sources and radiation-emitting devices. According to the potential degree of harm to human health and the environment, radioactive sources shall be classified from high to low as Category I, Category II, Category III, Category IV and Category V, and the specific classification measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council; radiation-emitting devices shall be classified as Category I, Category II and Category III, and the specific classification measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council in consultation with the competent health department under the State Council.

### 第二章　许可和备案

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当依照本章规定取得许可证。

**Article 5** The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall obtain a license in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　除医疗使用Ⅰ类放射源、制备正电子发射计算机断层扫描用放射性药物自用的单位外，生产放射性同位素、销售和使用Ⅰ类放射源、销售和使用Ⅰ类射线装置的单位的许可证，由国务院生态环境主管部门审批颁发。  
除国务院生态环境主管部门审批颁发的许可证外，其他单位的许可证，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门审批颁发。  
国务院生态环境主管部门向生产放射性同位素的单位颁发许可证前，应当将申请材料印送其行业主管部门征求意见。  
生态环境主管部门应当将审批颁发许可证的情况通报同级公安部门、卫生主管部门。

**Article 6** Except for units that use Category I radioactive sources for medical purposes and prepare radiopharmaceuticals for positron emission computed tomography for their own use, the licenses for units that produce radioactive isotopes, sell and use Category I radioactive sources, and sell and use Category I radiation-emitting devices shall be examined, approved and issued by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council.  
Except for the licenses examined, approved and issued by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council, the licenses for other units shall be examined, approved and issued by the competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Before issuing a license to a unit that produces radioactive isotopes, the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall send the application materials to the competent department of the industry for comments.  
The competent department of ecological environment shall notify the public security department and the competent health department at the same level of the examination, approval and issuance of the license.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位申请领取许可证，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有与所从事的生产、销售、使用活动规模相适应的，具备相应专业知识和防护知识及健康条件的专业技术人员；  
(二)有符合国家环境保护标准、职业卫生标准和安全防护要求的场所、设施和设备；  
(三)有专门的安全和防护管理机构或者专职、兼职安全和防护管理人员，并配备必要的防护用品和监测仪器；  
(四)有健全的安全和防护管理规章制度、辐射事故应急措施；  
(五)产生放射性废气、废液、固体废物的，具有确保放射性废气、废液、固体废物达标排放的处理能力或者可行的处理方案。

**Article 7** A unit applying for a license to produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having professional and technical personnel commensurate with the scale of the production, sale or use activities engaged in, who possess corresponding professional knowledge, protection knowledge and health conditions;  
(2) having premises, facilities and equipment that conform to the national environmental protection standards, occupational health standards and safety protection requirements;  
(3) having a specialized safety and protection management institution or full-time or part-time safety and protection management personnel, and being equipped with necessary protective supplies and monitoring instruments;  
(4) having sound safety and protection management rules and regulations, and radiation accident emergency measures;  
(5) if generating radioactive waste gas, waste liquid or solid waste, having the treatment capacity or feasible treatment plan to ensure that the radioactive waste gas, waste liquid and solid waste are discharged up to the standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当事先向有审批权的生态环境主管部门提出许可申请，并提交符合[本条例第七条](#art-7)规定条件的证明材料。  
使用放射性同位素和射线装置进行放射诊疗的医疗卫生机构，还应当获得放射源诊疗技术和医用辐射机构许可。

**Article 8** A unit that produces, sells or uses radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall, in advance, submit a license application to the competent department of ecological environment with the approval authority, and submit evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the conditions specified in Article 7 of these Regulations.  
A medical or health institution that uses radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices for radiological diagnosis and treatment shall also obtain a license for radiological source diagnosis and treatment techniques and medical radiation institutions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　生态环境主管部门应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，符合条件的，颁发许可证，并予以公告；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 9** The competent department of ecological environment shall complete the review within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, it shall issue a license and make an announcement; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　许可证包括下列主要内容：  
(一)单位的名称、地址、法定代表人；  
(二)所从事活动的种类和范围；  
(三)有效期限；  
(四)发证日期和证书编号。

**Article 10** A license shall include the following main contents:  
(1) the name, address and legal representative of the unit;  
(2) the type and scope of the activities engaged in;  
(3) the period of validity;  
(4) the date of issuance and the license number.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　持证单位变更单位名称、地址、法定代表人的，应当自变更登记之日起20日内，向原发证机关申请办理许可证变更手续。

**Article 11** If a licensee changes its name, address or legal representative, it shall apply to the original issuing authority for modification of the license within 20 days from the date of change registration.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，持证单位应当按照原申请程序，重新申请领取许可证：  
(一)改变所从事活动的种类或者范围的；  
(二)新建或者改建、扩建生产、销售、使用设施或者场所的。

**Article 12** In any of the following circumstances, the licensee shall re-apply for a license in accordance with the original application procedures:  
(1) changing the type or scope of the activities engaged in;  
(2) constructing new production, sale or use facilities or premises, or altering or expanding existing ones.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　许可证有效期为5年。有效期届满，需要延续的，持证单位应当于许可证有效期届满30日前，向原发证机关提出延续申请。原发证机关应当自受理延续申请之日起，在许可证有效期届满前完成审查，符合条件的，予以延续；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 13** The term of validity of a license shall be five years. If renewal is needed upon expiry, the licensee shall submit a renewal application to the original issuing authority 30 days before the expiry of the license term. The original issuing authority shall complete the review from the date of accepting the renewal application before the expiry of the license term. If the conditions are met, it shall renew the license; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　持证单位部分终止或者全部终止生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置活动的，应当向原发证机关提出部分变更或者注销许可证申请，由原发证机关核查合格后，予以变更或者注销许可证。

**Article 14** If a licensee partially or completely terminates the production, sale or use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, it shall apply to the original issuing authority for partial modification or cancellation of the license. After verification by the original issuing authority and finding it satisfactory, the license shall be modified or cancelled.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　禁止无许可证或者不按照许可证规定的种类和范围从事放射性同位素和射线装置的生产、销售、使用活动。  
禁止伪造、变造、转让许可证。

**Article 15** It is prohibited to engage in the production, sale or use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices without a license or not in accordance with the type and scope specified in the license.  
It is prohibited to forge, alter or transfer a license.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院对外贸易主管部门会同国务院生态环境主管部门、海关总署和生产放射性同位素的单位的行业主管部门制定并公布限制进出口放射性同位素目录和禁止进出口放射性同位素目录。  
进口列入限制进出口目录的放射性同位素，应当在国务院生态环境主管部门审查批准后，由国务院对外贸易主管部门依据国家对外贸易的有关规定签发进口许可证。进口限制进出口目录和禁止进出口目录之外的放射性同位素，依据国家对外贸易的有关规定办理进口手续。

**Article 16** The competent department of foreign trade under the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council, the General Administration of Customs, and the competent department of the industry of the units that produce radioactive isotopes, formulate and publish a list of radioactive isotopes whose import and export are restricted and a list of radioactive isotopes whose import and export are prohibited.  
For the import of radioactive isotopes included in the list of restricted import and export, after examination and approval by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council, the competent department of foreign trade under the State Council shall issue an import license in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on foreign trade. For the import of radioactive isotopes not included in the lists of restricted import and export or prohibited import and export, the import formalities shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State on foreign trade.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请进口列入限制进出口目录的放射性同位素，应当符合下列要求：  
(一)进口单位已经取得与所从事活动相符的许可证；  
(二)进口单位具有进口放射性同位素使用期满后的处理方案，其中，进口Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源的，应当具有原出口方负责回收的承诺文件；  
(三)进口的放射源应当有明确标号和必要说明文件，其中，Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源的标号应当刻制在放射源本体或者密封包壳体上，Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类放射源的标号应当记录在相应说明文件中；  
(四)将进口的放射性同位素销售给其他单位使用的，还应当具有与使用单位签订的书面协议以及使用单位取得的许可证复印件。

**Article 17** The application for import of radioactive isotopes listed in the list of restricted import and export shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) the importing unit has already obtained a license consistent with the activities it engages in;  
(2) the importing unit has a plan for the disposal of the imported radioactive isotopes after their period of use expires; provided that where importing Category I, II or III radioactive sources, it shall have a commitment document from the original exporter to take them back;  
(3) the imported radioactive sources shall have clear markings and necessary explanatory documents; for Category I, II and III radioactive sources, the markings shall be engraved on the source body or sealed capsule; for Category IV and V radioactive sources, the markings shall be recorded in the corresponding explanatory documents;  
(4) if the imported radioactive isotopes are to be sold to other units for use, the importing unit shall also have a written agreement signed with the using unit and a copy of the using unit's obtained license.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　进口列入限制进出口目录的放射性同位素的单位，应当向国务院生态环境主管部门提出进口申请，并提交符合[本条例第十七条](#art-17)规定要求的证明材料。  
国务院生态环境主管部门应当自受理申请之日起10个工作日内完成审查，符合条件的，予以批准；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
海关验凭放射性同位素进口许可证办理有关进口手续。进口放射性同位素的包装材料依法需要实施检疫的，依照国家有关检疫法律、法规的规定执行。  
对进口的放射源，国务院生态环境主管部门还应当同时确定与其标号相对应的放射源编码。

**Article 18** A unit importing radioactive isotopes listed in the list of restricted import and export shall submit an import application to the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council and submit evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the requirements specified in Article 17 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall complete the review within 10 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, it shall approve the application; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.  
The Customs shall handle the relevant import formalities upon verification of the radioactive isotope import license. If the packaging materials of the imported radioactive isotopes are required to undergo quarantine according to law, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant national quarantine laws and regulations.  
For imported radioactive sources, the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall also determine the radioactive source code corresponding to their markings at the same time.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请转让放射性同位素，应当符合下列要求：  
(一)转出、转入单位持有与所从事活动相符的许可证；  
(二)转入单位具有放射性同位素使用期满后的处理方案；  
(三)转让双方已经签订书面转让协议。

**Article 19** The application for transfer of radioactive isotopes shall meet the following requirements:  
(1) the transferring unit and the receiving unit hold licenses consistent with the activities they engage in;  
(2) the receiving unit has a plan for the disposal of the radioactive isotopes after their period of use expires;  
(3) the transferor and the transferee have signed a written transfer agreement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　转让放射性同位素，由转入单位向其所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门提出申请，并提交符合[本条例第十九条](#art-19)规定要求的证明材料。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当自受理申请之日起15个工作日内完成审查，符合条件的，予以批准；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 20** For the transfer of radioactive isotopes, the receiving unit shall submit an application to the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located, and submit evidentiary materials demonstrating compliance with the requirements specified in Article 19 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall complete the review within 15 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the conditions are met, it shall approve the application; if the conditions are not met, it shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　放射性同位素的转出、转入单位应当在转让活动完成之日起20日内，分别向其所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 21** The transferring unit and the receiving unit of radioactive isotopes shall, within 20 days from the date of completion of the transfer activity, separately file a record with the competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government where they are located.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　生产放射性同位素的单位，应当建立放射性同位素产品台账，并按照国务院生态环境主管部门制定的编码规则，对生产的放射源统一编码。放射性同位素产品台账和放射源编码清单应当报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。  
生产的放射源应当有明确标号和必要说明文件。其中，Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源的标号应当刻制在放射源本体或者密封包壳体上，Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类放射源的标号应当记录在相应说明文件中。  
国务院生态环境主管部门负责建立放射性同位素备案信息管理系统，与有关部门实行信息共享。  
未列入产品台账的放射性同位素和未编码的放射源，不得出厂和销售。

**Article 22** A unit that produces radioactive isotopes shall establish a product ledger of radioactive isotopes and uniformly code the produced radioactive sources according to the coding rules formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council. The product ledger of radioactive isotopes and the list of radioactive source codes shall be filed with the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council for record.  
The produced radioactive sources shall have clear markings and necessary explanatory documents. For Category I, II and III radioactive sources, the markings shall be engraved on the source body or sealed capsule; for Category IV and V radioactive sources, the markings shall be recorded in the corresponding explanatory documents.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall be responsible for establishing an information management system for the recordation of radioactive isotopes and share information with relevant departments.  
Radioactive isotopes not listed in the product ledger and radioactive sources without coding shall not be delivered from the factory or sold.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　持有放射源的单位将废旧放射源交回生产单位、返回原出口方或者送交放射性废物集中贮存单位贮存的，应当在该活动完成之日起20日内向其所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 23** A unit holding radioactive sources that returns spent radioactive sources to the producer, returns them to the original exporter, or delivers them to a centralized radioactive waste storage unit for storage shall, within 20 days from the date of completion of such activity, file a record with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例施行前生产和进口的放射性同位素，由放射性同位素持有单位在本条例施行之日起6个月内，到其所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门办理备案手续，省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门应当对放射源进行统一编码。

**Article 24** For radioactive isotopes produced or imported before the coming into force of these Regulations, the units holding such radioactive isotopes shall, within six months from the date on which these Regulations come into force, go through the recordation formalities with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located, and the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall uniformly code the radioactive sources.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　使用放射性同位素的单位需要将放射性同位素转移到外省、自治区、直辖市使用的，应当持许可证复印件向使用地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门备案，并接受当地生态环境主管部门的监督管理。

**Article 25** If a unit using radioactive isotopes needs to transfer the radioactive isotopes to another province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for use, it shall file a record with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the use will take place by presenting a copy of its license, and shall accept the supervision and administration of the local competent department of ecological environment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　出口列入限制进出口目录的放射性同位素，应当提供进口方可以合法持有放射性同位素的证明材料，并由国务院生态环境主管部门依照有关法律和我国缔结或者参加的国际条约、协定的规定，办理有关手续。  
出口放射性同位素应当遵守国家对外贸易的有关规定。

**Article 26** For the export of radioactive isotopes listed in the list of restricted import and export, the exporting unit shall provide evidentiary materials that the importing party may lawfully hold the radioactive isotopes, and the relevant formalities shall be handled by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council in accordance with the relevant laws and the provisions of the international treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by China.  
The export of radioactive isotopes shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on foreign trade.

### 第三章　安全和防护

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当对本单位的放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护工作负责，并依法对其造成的放射性危害承担责任。  
生产放射性同位素的单位的行业主管部门，应当加强对生产单位安全和防护工作的管理，并定期对其执行法律、法规和国家标准的情况进行监督检查。

**Article 27** The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall be responsible for the safety and protection of their own radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, and shall bear liability for the radioactive hazards caused by them according to law.  
The competent department of the industry of the units that produce radioactive isotopes shall strengthen the management of the safety and protection work of the producing units, and shall regularly conduct supervision and inspection on their compliance with laws, regulations and national standards.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当对直接从事生产、销售、使用活动的工作人员进行安全和防护知识教育培训，并进行考核；考核不合格的，不得上岗。  
辐射安全关键岗位应当由注册核安全工程师担任。辐射安全关键岗位名录由国务院生态环境主管部门商国务院有关部门制定并公布。

**Article 28** The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall conduct education and training on safety and protection knowledge for the workers directly engaged in production, sale or use activities, and shall assess them; those who fail the assessment shall not take up their posts.  
The key positions in radiation safety shall be held by registered nuclear safety engineers. The catalogue of key positions in radiation safety shall be formulated and published by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council in consultation with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当严格按照国家关于个人剂量监测和健康管理的规定，对直接从事生产、销售、使用活动的工作人员进行个人剂量监测和职业健康检查，建立个人剂量档案和职业健康监护档案。

**Article 29** The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall strictly in accordance with the national provisions on individual dose monitoring and health management, conduct individual dose monitoring and occupational health examinations for the workers directly engaged in production, sale or use activities, and establish individual dose files and occupational health surveillance files.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当对本单位的放射性同位素、射线装置的安全和防护状况进行年度评估。发现安全隐患的，应当立即进行整改。

**Article 30** The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall conduct an annual assessment on the safety and protection status of their own radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices. If any safety hazard is discovered, they shall immediately carry out rectification.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位需要终止的，应当事先对本单位的放射性同位素和放射性废物进行清理登记，作出妥善处理，不得留有安全隐患。生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位发生变更的，由变更后的单位承担处理责任。变更前当事人对此另有约定的，从其约定；但是，约定中不得免除当事人的处理义务。  
在本条例施行前已经终止的生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，其未安全处理的废旧放射源和放射性废物，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门提出处理方案，及时进行处理。所需经费由省级以上人民政府承担。

**Article 31** Where a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices needs to terminate its operations, it shall, beforehand, make an inventory and register of its radioactive isotopes and radioactive waste, and dispose of them properly, leaving no safety hazards. Where a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices undergoes a change, the unit after the change shall assume the responsibility for disposal. If the parties before the change have otherwise agreed, such agreement shall prevail; however, the agreement may not relieve the parties of their disposal obligations.  
For units that produced, sold or used radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices but have already been terminated before the coming into force of these Regulations, the spent radioactive sources and radioactive waste that have not been safely disposed of shall be handled by the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located, which shall propose a disposal plan and carry out timely disposal. The necessary funds shall be borne by the people's governments at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　生产、进口放射源的单位销售Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源给其他单位使用的，应当与使用放射源的单位签订废旧放射源返回协议；使用放射源的单位应当按照废旧放射源返回协议规定将废旧放射源交回生产单位或者返回原出口方。确实无法交回生产单位或者返回原出口方的，送交有相应资质的放射性废物集中贮存单位贮存。  
使用放射源的单位应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定，将Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类废旧放射源进行包装整备后送交有相应资质的放射性废物集中贮存单位贮存。

**Article 32** Where a unit that produces or imports radioactive sources sells Category I, II or III radioactive sources to other units for use, it shall enter into a spent radioactive source return agreement with the unit using the radioactive sources; the unit using the radioactive sources shall return the spent radioactive sources to the producer or the original exporter in accordance with the provisions of the spent radioactive source return agreement. If it is truly impossible to return them to the producer or the original exporter, they shall be delivered to a centralized radioactive waste storage unit with corresponding qualifications for storage.  
The unit using radioactive sources shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council, package and prepare Category IV and V spent radioactive sources and deliver them to a centralized radioactive waste storage unit with corresponding qualifications for storage.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　使用Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源的场所和生产放射性同位素的场所，以及终结运行后产生放射性污染的射线装置，应当依法实施退役。

**Article 33** The premises where Category I, II or III radioactive sources are used and the premises where radioactive isotopes are produced, as well as radiation-emitting devices that generate radioactive contamination after their final shutdown, shall be decommissioned according to law.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　生产、销售、使用、贮存放射性同位素和射线装置的场所，应当按照国家有关规定设置明显的放射性标志，其入口处应当按照国家有关安全和防护标准的要求，设置安全和防护设施以及必要的防护安全联锁、报警装置或者工作信号。射线装置的生产调试和使用场所，应当具有防止误操作、防止工作人员和公众受到意外照射的安全措施。  
放射性同位素的包装容器、含放射性同位素的设备和射线装置，应当设置明显的放射性标识和中文警示说明；放射源上能够设置放射性标识的，应当一并设置。运输放射性同位素和含放射源的射线装置的工具，应当按照国家有关规定设置明显的放射性标志或者显示危险信号。

**Article 34** The premises where radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices are produced, sold, used or stored shall be provided with conspicuous radioactive signs in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and at their entrances, safety and protection facilities as well as necessary protective safety interlocking devices, alarm devices or working signals shall be installed in accordance with the requirements of the national safety and protection standards. The premises where radiation-emitting devices are produced, debugged or used shall have safety measures to prevent misoperation and prevent accidental exposure of workers and the public.  
The packaging containers of radioactive isotopes, equipment containing radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall be provided with conspicuous radioactive identification and warning instructions in Chinese; if radioactive identification can be placed on the radioactive source, it shall also be provided. The means of transport for radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices containing radioactive sources shall be provided with conspicuous radioactive signs or danger signals in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　放射性同位素应当单独存放，不得与易燃、易爆、腐蚀性物品等一起存放，并指定专人负责保管。贮存、领取、使用、归还放射性同位素时，应当进行登记、检查，做到账物相符。对放射性同位素贮存场所应当采取防火、防水、防盗、防丢失、防破坏、防射线泄漏的安全措施。  
对放射源还应当根据其潜在危害的大小，建立相应的多层防护和安全措施，并对可移动的放射源定期进行盘存，确保其处于指定位置，具有可靠的安全保障。

**Article 35** Radioactive isotopes shall be stored separately, and shall not be stored together with flammable, explosive or corrosive articles, and a specially assigned person shall be responsible for their custody. When storing, collecting, using or returning radioactive isotopes, registration and checking shall be carried out to ensure that the accounts match the actual items. Fire prevention, waterproof, anti-theft, anti-loss, anti-damage and anti-radiation leakage safety measures shall be taken for the storage premises of radioactive isotopes.  
For radioactive sources, corresponding multi-layer protection and safety measures shall also be established according to their potential harm, and movable radioactive sources shall be regularly inventoried to ensure that they are at designated locations and have reliable safety guarantees.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在室外、野外使用放射性同位素和射线装置的，应当按照国家安全和防护标准的要求划出安全防护区域，设置明显的放射性标志，必要时设专人警戒。  
在野外进行放射性同位素示踪试验的，应当经省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门商同级有关部门批准方可进行。

**Article 36** Where radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices are used outdoors or in the field, a safety protection area shall be demarcated in accordance with the requirements of the national safety and protection standards, conspicuous radioactive signs shall be set up, and when necessary, specially assigned persons shall be posted for warning.  
The performance of radioactive isotope tracer tests in the field shall be subject to approval by the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level in consultation with the relevant departments at the same level before such tests may be conducted.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　辐射防护器材、含放射性同位素的设备和射线装置，以及含有放射性物质的产品和伴有产生X射线的电器产品，应当符合辐射防护要求。不合格的产品不得出厂和销售。

**Article 37** Radiation protection equipment, equipment containing radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, as well as products containing radioactive substances and electrical products that are accompanied by the generation of X-rays, shall comply with radiation protection requirements. Non-conforming products shall not be delivered from the factory or sold.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　使用放射性同位素和射线装置进行放射诊疗的医疗卫生机构，应当依据国务院卫生主管部门有关规定和国家标准，制定与本单位从事的诊疗项目相适应的质量保证方案，遵守质量保证监测规范，按照医疗照射正当化和辐射防护最优化的原则，避免一切不必要的照射，并事先告知患者和受检者辐射对健康的潜在影响。

**Article 38** A medical or health institution that uses radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices for radiological diagnosis and treatment shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent health department under the State Council and national standards, formulate a quality assurance program suitable for the diagnosis and treatment items it engages in, comply with quality assurance monitoring specifications, adhere to the principles of justification of medical exposure and optimization of radiation protection, avoid all unnecessary exposures, and inform patients and examinees in advance of the potential health effects of radiation.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　金属冶炼厂回收冶炼废旧金属时，应当采取必要的监测措施，防止放射性物质熔入产品中。监测中发现问题的，应当及时通知所在地设区的市级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门。

**Article 39** When a metal smelting plant recovers and smelts scrap metal, it shall take necessary monitoring measures to prevent radioactive substances from being melted into the products. If problems are discovered during monitoring, it shall promptly notify the competent department of ecological environment of the local people's government at or above the districted city level.

### 第四章　辐射事故应急处理

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　根据辐射事故的性质、严重程度、可控性和影响范围等因素，从重到轻将辐射事故分为特别重大辐射事故、重大辐射事故、较大辐射事故和一般辐射事故四个等级。  
特别重大辐射事故，是指Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类放射源丢失、被盗、失控造成大范围严重辐射污染后果，或者放射性同位素和射线装置失控导致3人以上(含3人)急性死亡。  
重大辐射事故，是指Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类放射源丢失、被盗、失控，或者放射性同位素和射线装置失控导致2人以下(含2人)急性死亡或者10人以上(含10人)急性重度放射病、局部器官残疾。  
较大辐射事故，是指Ⅲ类放射源丢失、被盗、失控，或者放射性同位素和射线装置失控导致9人以下(含9人)急性重度放射病、局部器官残疾。  
一般辐射事故，是指Ⅳ类、Ⅴ类放射源丢失、被盗、失控，或者放射性同位素和射线装置失控导致人员受到超过年剂量限值的照射。

**Article 40** According to factors such as the nature, severity, controllability and scope of impact of a radiation accident, radiation accidents are classified from the most serious to the least serious into four grades: especially major radiation accident, major radiation accident, relatively major radiation accident and general radiation accident.  
An especially major radiation accident refers to the loss, theft or loss of control of Category I or II radioactive sources causing large-scale severe radiation pollution, or the loss of control of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices resulting in acute death of three or more persons (including three persons).  
A major radiation accident refers to the loss, theft or loss of control of Category I or II radioactive sources, or the loss of control of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices resulting in acute death of two or fewer persons (including two persons) or acute severe radiation sickness or partial organ disability in ten or more persons (including ten persons).  
A relatively major radiation accident refers to the loss, theft or loss of control of Category III radioactive sources, or the loss of control of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices resulting in acute severe radiation sickness or partial organ disability in nine or fewer persons (including nine persons).  
A general radiation accident refers to the loss, theft or loss of control of Category IV or V radioactive sources, or the loss of control of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices resulting in exposure of personnel exceeding the annual dose limit.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同同级公安、卫生、财政等部门编制辐射事故应急预案，报本级人民政府批准。辐射事故应急预案应当包括下列内容：  
(一)应急机构和职责分工；  
(二)应急人员的组织、培训以及应急和救助的装备、资金、物资准备；  
(三)辐射事故分级与应急响应措施；  
(四)辐射事故调查、报告和处理程序。  
生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当根据可能发生的辐射事故的风险，制定本单位的应急方案，做好应急准备。

**Article 41** The competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the public security, health, finance and other departments at the same level, formulate radiation accident emergency response plans and submit them to the people's government at the same level for approval. The radiation accident emergency response plan shall include the following contents:  
(1) the emergency organization and the division of duties and responsibilities;  
(2) the organization and training of emergency personnel, and the preparation of equipment, funds and materials for emergency response and rescue;  
(3) the classification of radiation accidents and the emergency response measures;  
(4) the procedures for investigation, reporting and handling of radiation accidents.  
The units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall, based on the risks of possible radiation accidents, formulate their own emergency plans and make emergency preparations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　发生辐射事故时，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位应当立即启动本单位的应急方案，采取应急措施，并立即向当地生态环境主管部门、公安部门、卫生主管部门报告。  
生态环境主管部门、公安部门、卫生主管部门接到辐射事故报告后，应当立即派人赶赴现场，进行现场调查，采取有效措施，控制并消除事故影响，同时将辐射事故信息报告本级人民政府和上级人民政府生态环境主管部门、公安部门、卫生主管部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门接到辐射事故报告后，应当按照事故分级报告的规定及时将辐射事故信息报告上级人民政府及其有关部门。发生特别重大辐射事故和重大辐射事故后，事故发生地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府和国务院有关部门应当在4小时内报告国务院；特殊情况下，事故发生地人民政府及其有关部门可以直接向国务院报告，并同时报告上级人民政府及其有关部门。  
禁止缓报、瞒报、谎报或者漏报辐射事故。

**Article 42** When a radiation accident occurs, the unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall immediately activate its own emergency plan, take emergency measures, and immediately report to the local competent department of ecological environment, public security department and competent health department.  
Upon receiving a report of a radiation accident, the competent department of ecological environment, the public security department and the competent health department shall immediately send personnel to the scene, conduct on-site investigations, take effective measures to control and eliminate the impact of the accident, and at the same time report the information on the radiation accident to the people's government at the same level and to the competent departments of ecological environment, public security departments and competent health departments of the people's government at a higher level.  
Upon receiving a report of a radiation accident, the local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall promptly report the information on the radiation accident to the people's government at a higher level and its relevant departments in accordance with the provisions on graded reporting of accidents. After an especially major radiation accident or a major radiation accident occurs, the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the accident occurred and the relevant departments under the State Council shall report to the State Council within four hours; under special circumstances, the people's government where the accident occurred and its relevant departments may directly report to the State Council and simultaneously report to the people's government at a higher level and its relevant departments.  
It is prohibited to delay reporting, conceal, make false reports or omit reports of radiation accidents.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在发生辐射事故或者有证据证明辐射事故可能发生时，县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门有权采取下列临时控制措施：  
(一)责令停止导致或者可能导致辐射事故的作业；  
(二)组织控制事故现场。

**Article 43** When a radiation accident occurs or there is evidence proving that a radiation accident may occur, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall have the power to take the following temporary control measures:  
(1) ordering the cessation of operations that cause or may cause a radiation accident;  
(2) organizing the control of the accident scene.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　辐射事故发生后，有关县级以上人民政府应当按照辐射事故的等级，启动并组织实施相应的应急预案。  
县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门、公安部门、卫生主管部门，按照职责分工做好相应的辐射事故应急工作：  
(一)生态环境主管部门负责辐射事故的应急响应、调查处理和定性定级工作，协助公安部门监控追缴丢失、被盗的放射源；  
(二)公安部门负责丢失、被盗放射源的立案侦查和追缴；  
(三)卫生主管部门负责辐射事故的医疗应急。  
生态环境主管部门、公安部门、卫生主管部门应当及时相互通报辐射事故应急响应、调查处理、定性定级、立案侦查和医疗应急情况。国务院指定的部门根据生态环境主管部门确定的辐射事故的性质和级别，负责有关国际信息通报工作。

**Article 44** After a radiation accident occurs, the relevant people's government at or above the county level shall, according to the grade of the radiation accident, activate and organize the implementation of the corresponding emergency response plan.  
The competent departments of ecological environment, public security departments and competent health departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, properly carry out the corresponding radiation accident emergency work:  
(1) the competent department of ecological environment shall be responsible for the emergency response, investigation, handling, characterization and grading of the radiation accident, and assist the public security department in monitoring and recovering lost or stolen radioactive sources;  
(2) the public security department shall be responsible for filing cases, investigating and recovering lost or stolen radioactive sources;  
(3) the competent health department shall be responsible for the medical emergency of the radiation accident.  
The competent departments of ecological environment, public security departments and competent health departments shall promptly inform each other of the emergency response, investigation, handling, characterization, grading, case filing, investigation and medical emergency of the radiation accident. The department designated by the State Council shall be responsible for relevant international information notification based on the nature and grade of the radiation accident determined by the competent department of ecological environment.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　发生辐射事故的单位应当立即将可能受到辐射伤害的人员送至当地卫生主管部门指定的医院或者有条件救治辐射损伤病人的医院，进行检查和治疗，或者请求医院立即派人赶赴事故现场，采取救治措施。

**Article 45** The unit in which a radiation accident occurs shall immediately send persons who may have been injured by radiation to a hospital designated by the local competent health department or a hospital capable of treating patients with radiation injuries for examination and treatment, or request a hospital to immediately send personnel to the accident scene to take treatment measures.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门应当按照各自职责对生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位进行监督检查。  
被检查单位应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供必要的资料，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 46** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, conduct supervision and inspection of the units that produce, sell or use radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices.  
The unit under inspection shall cooperate, truthfully reflect the situation, provide necessary materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门应当配备辐射防护安全监督员。辐射防护安全监督员由从事辐射防护工作，具有辐射防护安全知识并经省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门认可的专业人员担任。辐射防护安全监督员应当定期接受专业知识培训和考核。

**Article 47** The competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be staffed with radiation protection safety supervisors. Radiation protection safety supervisors shall be professional personnel engaged in radiation protection work, possessing knowledge of radiation protection safety and recognized by the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level. Radiation protection safety supervisors shall regularly receive professional knowledge training and assessment.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门在监督检查中发现生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位有不符合原发证条件的情形的，应当责令其限期整改。  
监督检查人员依法进行监督检查时，应当出示证件，并为被检查单位保守技术秘密和业务秘密。

**Article 48** If, during supervision and inspection, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level discovers that a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices does not meet the original licensing conditions, it shall order the unit to make rectification within a time limit.  
When conducting supervision and inspection according to law, the supervision and inspection personnel shall present their credentials and shall keep the technical secrets and business secrets of the unit under inspection confidential.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例的行为，有权向生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门检举；对生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门未依法履行监督管理职责的行为，有权向本级人民政府、上级人民政府有关部门检举。接到举报的有关人民政府、生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门对有关举报应当及时核实、处理。

**Article 49** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report any act in violation of these Regulations to the competent department of ecological environment and other relevant departments; and shall have the right to report any act of the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments that fails to perform its supervision and administration duties according to law to the people's government at the same level or the relevant departments of a people's government at a higher level. The relevant people's government, competent department of ecological environment or other relevant department receiving such report shall promptly verify and handle the report.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)向不符合本条例规定条件的单位颁发许可证或者批准不符合本条例规定条件的单位进口、转让放射性同位素的；  
(二)发现未依法取得许可证的单位擅自生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置，不予查处或者接到举报后不依法处理的；  
(三)发现未经依法批准擅自进口、转让放射性同位素，不予查处或者接到举报后不依法处理的；  
(四)对依法取得许可证的单位不履行监督管理职责或者发现违反本条例规定的行为不予查处的；  
(五)在放射性同位素、射线装置安全和防护监督管理工作中有其他渎职行为的。

**Article 50** If, in violation of these Regulations, the competent department of ecological environment of a people's government at or above the county level commits any of the following acts, the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing a license to a unit that does not meet the conditions specified in these Regulations, or approving the import or transfer of radioactive isotopes by a unit that does not meet the conditions specified in these Regulations;  
(2) discovering that a unit without a license obtained according to law has produced, sold or used radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices without authorization, but failing to investigate and deal with the matter, or failing to handle it according to law after receiving a report;  
(3) discovering the unauthorized import or transfer of radioactive isotopes without approval according to law, but failing to investigate and deal with the matter, or failing to handle it according to law after receiving a report;  
(4) failing to perform supervision and administration duties over a unit that has obtained a license according to law, or discovering an act in violation of these Regulations but failing to investigate and deal with the matter;  
(5) engaging in other acts of dereliction of duty in the supervision and administration of the safety and protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)缓报、瞒报、谎报或者漏报辐射事故的；  
(二)未按照规定编制辐射事故应急预案或者不依法履行辐射事故应急职责的。

**Article 51** If, in violation of these Regulations, the competent department of ecological environment of a people's government at or above the county level or other relevant department commits any of the following acts, the person directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) delaying reporting, concealing, making false reports or omitting reports of a radiation accident;  
(2) failing to formulate a radiation accident emergency response plan in accordance with the provisions, or failing to perform the radiation accident emergency duties according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业或者由原发证机关吊销许可证；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得10万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足10万元的，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)无许可证从事放射性同位素和射线装置生产、销售、使用活动的；  
(二)未按照许可证的规定从事放射性同位素和射线装置生产、销售、使用活动的；  
(三)改变所从事活动的种类或者范围以及新建、改建或者扩建生产、销售、使用设施或者场所，未按照规定重新申请领取许可证的；  
(四)许可证有效期届满，需要延续而未按照规定办理延续手续的；  
(五)未经批准，擅自进口或者转让放射性同位素的。

**Article 52** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the unit shall be ordered to suspend production or business, or the license shall be revoked by the original issuing authority; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) engaging in the production, sale or use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices without a license;  
(2) engaging in the production, sale or use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices not in accordance with the provisions of the license;  
(3) changing the type or scope of the activities engaged in, or constructing new, altering or expanding production, sale or use facilities or premises, without re-applying for a license in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) where the term of validity of the license expires and renewal is needed, failing to go through the renewal formalities in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) importing or transferring radioactive isotopes without authorization and without approval.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位变更单位名称、地址、法定代表人，未依法办理许可证变更手续的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销许可证。

**Article 53** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices changes its name, address or legal representative but fails to go through the license modification formalities according to law, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the original issuing authority shall suspend or revoke the license.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位部分终止或者全部终止生产、销售、使用活动，未按照规定办理许可证变更或者注销手续的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成辐射事故，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices partially or completely terminates its production, sale or use activities but fails to go through the license modification or cancellation formalities in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a radiation accident is caused and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，伪造、变造、转让许可证的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门收缴伪造、变造的许可证或者由原发证机关吊销许可证，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，伪造、变造、转让放射性同位素进口和转让批准文件的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门收缴伪造、变造的批准文件或者由原批准机关撤销批准文件，并处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证机关吊销许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** If, in violation of these Regulations, a license is forged, altered or transferred, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the forged or altered license, or the original issuing authority shall revoke the license, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If, in violation of these Regulations, the approval documents for the import or transfer of radioactive isotopes are forged, altered or transferred, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the forged or altered approval documents, or the original approving authority shall revoke the approval documents, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the license may be revoked by the original issuing authority; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销许可证：  
(一)转入、转出放射性同位素未按照规定备案的；  
(二)将放射性同位素转移到外省、自治区、直辖市使用，未按照规定备案的；  
(三)将废旧放射源交回生产单位、返回原出口方或者送交放射性废物集中贮存单位贮存，未按照规定备案的。

**Article 56** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the original issuing authority shall suspend or revoke the license:  
(1) failing to file a record as required for the transfer-in or transfer-out of radioactive isotopes;  
(2) failing to file a record as required for the transfer of radioactive isotopes to another province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for use;  
(3) failing to file a record as required for returning spent radioactive sources to the producer, returning them to the original exporter, or delivering them to a centralized radioactive waste storage unit for storage.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)在室外、野外使用放射性同位素和射线装置，未按照国家有关安全和防护标准的要求划出安全防护区域和设置明显的放射性标志的；  
(二)未经批准擅自在野外进行放射性同位素示踪试验的。

**Article 57** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices outdoors or in the field without demarcating a safety protection area or setting up conspicuous radioactive signs in accordance with the requirements of the national safety and protection standards;  
(2) conducting radioactive isotope tracer tests in the field without authorization and without approval.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本条例规定，生产放射性同位素的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，依法收缴其未备案的放射性同位素和未编码的放射源，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款，并可以由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销许可证：  
(一)未建立放射性同位素产品台账的；  
(二)未按照国务院生态环境主管部门制定的编码规则，对生产的放射源进行统一编码的；  
(三)未将放射性同位素产品台账和放射源编码清单报国务院生态环境主管部门备案的；  
(四)出厂或者销售未列入产品台账的放射性同位素和未编码的放射源的。

**Article 58** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing radioactive isotopes commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit and give a warning; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the unregistered radioactive isotopes and uncoded radioactive sources shall be confiscated according to law, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the original issuing authority may also suspend or revoke the license:  
(1) failing to establish a product ledger of radioactive isotopes;  
(2) failing to uniformly code the produced radioactive sources in accordance with the coding rules formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council;  
(3) failing to file the product ledger of radioactive isotopes and the list of radioactive source codes with the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council for record;  
(4) delivering from the factory or selling radioactive isotopes not listed in the product ledger or uncoded radioactive sources.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，由原发证机关指定有处理能力的单位代为处理或者实施退役，费用由生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位承担，并处1万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)未按照规定对废旧放射源进行处理的；  
(二)未按照规定对使用Ⅰ类、Ⅱ类、Ⅲ类放射源的场所和生产放射性同位素的场所，以及终结运行后产生放射性污染的射线装置实施退役的。

**Article 59** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, the original issuing authority shall designate a unit with disposal capability to handle the matter or carry out decommissioning on behalf of the violating unit, with the costs borne by the unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) failing to dispose of spent radioactive sources in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to decommission the premises where Category I, II or III radioactive sources are used and the premises where radioactive isotopes are produced, as well as radiation-emitting devices that generate radioactive contamination after their final shutdown, in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本条例规定，生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业，并处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照规定对本单位的放射性同位素、射线装置安全和防护状况进行评估或者发现安全隐患不及时整改的；  
(二)生产、销售、使用、贮存放射性同位素和射线装置的场所未按照规定设置安全和防护设施以及放射性标志的。

**Article 60** If, in violation of these Regulations, a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act and rectification within a time limit; if it fails to rectify within the time limit, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to conduct an assessment on the safety and protection status of its own radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices in accordance with the provisions, or failing to promptly rectify safety hazards after discovery;  
(2) failing to install safety and protection facilities and radioactive signs in the premises where radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices are produced, sold, used or stored in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本条例规定，造成辐射事故的，由原发证机关责令限期改正，并处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由原发证机关吊销许可证；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以治安处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
因辐射事故造成他人损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 61** If, in violation of these Regulations, a radiation accident is caused, the original issuing authority shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the license; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If damage is caused to others due to a radiation accident, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位被责令限期整改，逾期不整改或者经整改仍不符合原发证条件的，由原发证机关暂扣或者吊销许可证。

**Article 62** If a unit producing, selling or using radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices is ordered to make rectification within a time limit but fails to do so within the time limit or still does not meet the original licensing conditions after rectification, the original issuing authority shall suspend or revoke the license.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本条例规定，被依法吊销许可证的单位或者伪造、变造许可证的单位，5年内不得申请领取许可证。

**Article 63** A unit whose license has been revoked according to law or a unit that has forged or altered a license in violation of these Regulations shall not apply for a license within five years.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门的行政处罚权限的划分，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 64** The division of the authority for administrative penalties among the competent departments of ecological environment of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　军用放射性同位素、射线装置安全和防护的监督管理，依照[《中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法》第六十条](../../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法.md#art-60)的规定执行。

**Article 65** The supervision and administration of the safety and protection of military radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Article 60 of the Law on Prevention and Control of Radioactive Pollution of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　劳动者在职业活动中接触放射性同位素和射线装置造成的职业病的防治，依照《中华人民共和国职业病防治法》和国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 66** The prevention and control of occupational diseases caused by workers' exposure to radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices in their occupational activities shall be carried out in accordance with the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　放射性同位素的运输，放射性同位素和射线装置生产、销售、使用过程中产生的放射性废物的处置，依照国务院有关规定执行。

**Article 67** The transport of radioactive isotopes and the disposal of radioactive waste generated during the production, sale or use of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本条例中下列用语的含义：  
放射性同位素，是指某种发生放射性衰变的元素中具有相同原子序数但质量不同的核素。  
放射源，是指除研究堆和动力堆核燃料循环范畴的材料以外，永久密封在容器中或者有严密包层并呈固态的放射性材料。  
射线装置，是指X线机、加速器、中子发生器以及含放射源的装置。  
非密封放射性物质，是指非永久密封在包壳里或者紧密地固结在覆盖层里的放射性物质。  
转让，是指除进出口、回收活动之外，放射性同位素所有权或者使用权在不同持有者之间的转移。  
伴有产生X射线的电器产品，是指不以产生X射线为目的，但在生产或者使用过程中产生X射线的电器产品。  
辐射事故，是指放射源丢失、被盗、失控，或者放射性同位素和射线装置失控导致人员受到意外的异常照射。

**Article 68** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
"Radioactive isotopes" means nuclides of an element undergoing radioactive decay that have the same atomic number but different masses.  
"Radioactive source" means radioactive material that is permanently sealed in a container or tightly encapsulated in a solid form, excluding materials within the nuclear fuel cycle of research reactors and power reactors.  
"Radiation-emitting device" means X-ray machines, accelerators, neutron generators, and devices containing radioactive sources.  
"Unsealed radioactive substance" means radioactive substance that is not permanently sealed in a capsule or tightly bound in a coating.  
"Transfer" means the transfer of ownership or right to use radioactive isotopes between different holders, excluding import, export and recovery activities.  
"Electrical product accompanied by the generation of X-rays" means an electrical product that is not intended to produce X-rays but generates X-rays during production or use.  
"Radiation accident" means the loss, theft or loss of control of a radioactive source, or the loss of control of radioactive isotopes and radiation-emitting devices resulting in unexpected abnormal exposure of persons.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　本条例自2005年12月1日起施行。1989年10月24日国务院发布的《放射性同位素与射线装置放射防护条例》同时废止。

**Article 69** These Regulations shall come into force as of December 1, 2005. The Regulations on the Radiological Protection of Radioactive Isotopes and Radiation-Emitting Devices promulgated by the State Council on October 24, 1989 shall be abolished simultaneously.
