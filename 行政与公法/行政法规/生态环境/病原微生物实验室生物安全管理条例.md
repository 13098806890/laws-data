### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强病原微生物实验室（以下称实验室）生物安全管理，保护实验室工作人员和公众的健康，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purpose of strengthening the biosafety management of pathogenic microorganism laboratories (hereinafter referred to as laboratories) and protecting the health of laboratory workers and the public.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对中华人民共和国境内的实验室及其从事实验活动的生物安全管理，适用本条例。  
本条例所称病原微生物，是指能够使人或者动物致病的微生物。  
本条例所称实验活动，是指实验室从事与病原微生物菌（毒）种、样本有关的研究、教学、检测、诊断等活动。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the biosafety management of laboratories and their experimental activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Pathogenic microorganisms as mentioned in these Regulations refer to microorganisms that may cause diseases in humans or animals.  
Experimental activities as mentioned in these Regulations refer to the research, teaching, testing, diagnosis and other activities conducted by laboratories related to pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院卫生主管部门主管与人体健康有关的实验室及其实验活动的生物安全监督工作。  
国务院兽医主管部门主管与动物有关的实验室及其实验活动的生物安全监督工作。  
国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责实验室及其实验活动的生物安全管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门在各自职责范围内负责实验室及其实验活动的生物安全管理工作。

**Article 3** The competent health department of the State Council shall be in charge of biosafety supervision over laboratories related to human health and their experimental activities.  
The competent veterinary department of the State Council shall be in charge of biosafety supervision over laboratories related to animals and their experimental activities.  
Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for biosafety management of laboratories and their experimental activities within their respective functions and duties.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall be responsible for biosafety management of laboratories and their experimental activities within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家对病原微生物实行分类管理，对实验室实行分级管理。

**Article 4** The State shall implement classified management of pathogenic microorganisms and graded management of laboratories.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家实行统一的实验室生物安全标准。实验室应当符合国家标准和要求。

**Article 5** The State shall implement uniform laboratory biosafety standards. A laboratory shall comply with the national standards and requirements.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　实验室的设立单位及其主管部门负责实验室日常活动的管理，承担建立健全安全管理制度，检查、维护实验设施、设备，控制实验室感染的职责。

**Article 6** The establishing entity of a laboratory and its competent department shall be responsible for the management of daily activities of the laboratory, and shall assume the duties of establishing and improving safety management systems, inspecting and maintaining experimental facilities and equipment, and controlling laboratory infections.

### 第二章　病原微生物的分类和管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家根据病原微生物的传染性、感染后对个体或者群体的危害程度，将病原微生物分为四类：  
第一类病原微生物，是指能够引起人类或者动物非常严重疾病的微生物，以及我国尚未发现或者已经宣布消灭的微生物。  
第二类病原微生物，是指能够引起人类或者动物严重疾病，比较容易直接或者间接在人与人、动物与人、动物与动物间传播的微生物。  
第三类病原微生物，是指能够引起人类或者动物疾病，但一般情况下对人、动物或者环境不构成严重危害，传播风险有限，实验室感染后很少引起严重疾病，并且具备有效治疗和预防措施的微生物。  
第四类病原微生物，是指在通常情况下不会引起人类或者动物疾病的微生物。  
第一类、第二类病原微生物统称为高致病性病原微生物。

**Article 7** The State classifies pathogenic microorganisms into four categories according to their infectivity and the degree of harm to individuals or groups after infection:  
Category I pathogenic microorganisms refer to microorganisms that may cause very serious diseases in humans or animals, as well as microorganisms that have not yet been discovered or have been declared eradicated in China.  
Category II pathogenic microorganisms refer to microorganisms that may cause serious diseases in humans or animals and are relatively easy to transmit directly or indirectly between humans, between animals and humans, or between animals.  
Category III pathogenic microorganisms refer to microorganisms that may cause diseases in humans or animals, but generally do not pose serious harm to humans, animals or the environment, have limited transmission risk, rarely cause serious diseases after laboratory infection, and for which effective treatment and prevention measures exist.  
Category IV pathogenic microorganisms refer to microorganisms that do not normally cause diseases in humans or animals.  
Category I and Category II pathogenic microorganisms are collectively referred to as highly pathogenic microorganisms.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人间传染的病原微生物目录由国务院卫生主管部门商国务院有关部门后制定、调整并予以公布；动物间传染的病原微生物目录由国务院兽医主管部门商国务院有关部门后制定、调整并予以公布。

**Article 8** The catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms transmissible among humans shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent health department of the State Council after consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council; the catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms transmissible among animals shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent veterinary department of the State Council after consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　采集病原微生物样本应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有与采集病原微生物样本所需要的生物安全防护水平相适应的设备；  
（二）具有掌握相关专业知识和操作技能的工作人员；  
（三）具有有效的防止病原微生物扩散和感染的措施；  
（四）具有保证病原微生物样本质量的技术方法和手段。  
采集高致病性病原微生物样本的工作人员在采集过程中应当防止病原微生物扩散和感染，并对样本的来源、采集过程和方法等作详细记录。

**Article 9** Collection of pathogenic microorganism samples shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having equipment commensurate with the biosafety protection level required for collecting pathogenic microorganism samples;  
(2) having workers who master relevant professional knowledge and operational skills;  
(3) having effective measures to prevent the spread and infection of pathogenic microorganisms;  
(4) having technical methods and means to ensure the quality of pathogenic microorganism samples.  
Workers collecting highly pathogenic microorganism samples shall, during the collection process, prevent the spread and infection of pathogenic microorganisms and make detailed records of the source, collection process and methods of the samples.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本，应当通过陆路运输；没有陆路通道，必须经水路运输的，可以通过水路运输；紧急情况下或者需要将高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本运往国外的，可以通过民用航空运输。

**Article 10** Transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall be conducted by land transportation; if there is no land route and water transportation must be used, water transportation may be used; in emergency situations or when highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples need to be transported abroad, civil aviation transportation may be used.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）运输目的、高致病性病原微生物的用途和接收单位符合国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定；  
（二）高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的容器应当密封，容器或者包装材料还应当符合防水、防破损、防外泄、耐高（低）温、耐高压的要求；  
（三）容器或者包装材料上应当印有国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门规定的生物危险标识、警告用语和提示用语。  
运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本，应当经省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门批准。在省、自治区、直辖市行政区域内运输的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门批准；需要跨省、自治区、直辖市运输或者运往国外的，由出发地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门进行初审后，分别报国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门批准。  
出入境检验检疫机构在检验检疫过程中需要运输病原微生物样本的，由国务院出入境检验检疫部门批准，并同时向国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门通报。  
通过民用航空运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的，除依照本条第二款、第三款规定取得批准外，还应当经国务院民用航空主管部门批准。  
有关主管部门应当对申请人提交的关于运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的申请材料进行审查，对符合本条第一款规定条件的，应当即时批准。

**Article 11** Transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) the purpose of transportation, the use of the highly pathogenic microorganisms, and the receiving entity comply with the provisions of the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council;  
(2) the container for highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall be sealed, and the container or packaging materials shall also meet the requirements for waterproofing, breakage prevention, leakage prevention, resistance to high (low) temperature and high pressure;  
(3) the container or packaging materials shall be printed with the biohazard signs, warning words and prompting words prescribed by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council.  
Transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall be approved by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the provincial level. For transportation within the administrative region of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, approval shall be granted by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government of that province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; if transportation across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government or abroad is needed, after preliminary examination by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government of the place of departure, the matter shall be submitted to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council for approval respectively.  
Where an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution needs to transport pathogenic microorganism samples during inspection and quarantine, approval shall be obtained from the entry-exit inspection and quarantine department of the State Council, and the matter shall be reported simultaneously to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council.  
Where highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples are transported by civil aviation, in addition to obtaining approval in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article, approval from the competent civil aviation department of the State Council shall also be obtained.  
The relevant competent departments shall examine the application materials submitted by the applicant concerning the transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples, and shall grant approval promptly if the conditions specified in paragraph 1 of this Article are met.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本，应当由不少于2人的专人护送，并采取相应的防护措施。  
有关单位或者个人不得通过公共电（汽）车和城市铁路运输病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本。

**Article 12** Transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall be escorted by at least two dedicated persons, and corresponding protective measures shall be taken.  
No relevant entity or individual shall transport pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples by public tram (bus) or urban railway.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　需要通过铁路、公路、民用航空等公共交通工具运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的，承运单位应当凭[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定的批准文件予以运输。  
承运单位应当与护送人共同采取措施，确保所运输的高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的安全，严防发生被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏事件。

**Article 13** Where highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples need to be transported by public transport such as railway, highway or civil aviation, the carrier entity shall transport them on the strength of the approval documents prescribed in Article 11 of these Regulations.  
The carrier entity shall take measures jointly with the escort to ensure the safety of the transported highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples, and strictly prevent the occurrence of theft, robbery, loss or leakage.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门指定的菌（毒）种保藏中心或者专业实验室（以下称保藏机构），承担集中储存病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本的任务。  
保藏机构应当依照国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定，储存实验室送交的病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本，并向实验室提供病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本。  
保藏机构应当制定严格的安全保管制度，作好病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本进出和储存的记录，建立档案制度，并指定专人负责。对高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本应当设专库或者专柜单独储存。  
保藏机构储存、提供病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本，不得收取任何费用，其经费由同级财政在单位预算中予以保障。  
保藏机构的管理办法由国务院卫生主管部门会同国务院兽医主管部门制定。

**Article 14** The strain (virus) preservation centers or specialized laboratories (hereinafter referred to as preservation institutions) designated by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council shall undertake the task of centralized storage of pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples.  
A preservation institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council, store pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples delivered by laboratories, and provide pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples to laboratories.  
A preservation institution shall formulate a strict safety storage system, keep records of the entry, exit and storage of pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples, establish an archive system, and designate specially assigned persons to be responsible. For highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples, dedicated warehouses or cabinets shall be set up for separate storage.  
A preservation institution shall not charge any fee for storing or providing pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples, and its funds shall be guaranteed in the unit budget by the finance department at the same level.  
The administrative measures for preservation institutions shall be formulated by the competent health department of the State Council jointly with the competent veterinary department of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　保藏机构应当凭实验室依照本条例的规定取得的从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的批准文件或者设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门发放的实验室备案凭证，向实验室提供高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本，并予以登记。

**Article 15** A preservation institution shall, on the strength of the approval documents obtained by a laboratory for conducting experimental activities related to highly pathogenic microorganisms in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations or the laboratory filing certificate issued by the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts, provide the laboratory with highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples, and register such provision.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　实验室在相关实验活动结束后，应当依照国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定，及时将病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本就地销毁或者送交保藏机构保管。  
保藏机构接受实验室送交的病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本，应当予以登记，并开具接收证明。

**Article 16** After the completion of relevant experimental activities, a laboratory shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council, promptly destroy the pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples on site or deliver them to the preservation institution for custody.  
A preservation institution that accepts pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples delivered by a laboratory shall register them and issue a receipt.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本在运输、储存中被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏的，承运单位、护送人、保藏机构应当采取必要的控制措施，并在2小时内分别向承运单位的主管部门、护送人所在单位和保藏机构的主管部门报告，同时向所在地的县级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门报告，发生被盗、被抢、丢失的，还应当向公安机关报告；接到报告的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当在2小时内向本级人民政府报告，并同时向上级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门和国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门报告。  
县级人民政府应当在接到报告后2小时内向设区的市级人民政府或者上一级人民政府报告；设区的市级人民政府应当在接到报告后2小时内向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府报告。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当在接到报告后1小时内，向国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门报告。  
任何单位和个人发现高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本的容器或者包装材料，应当及时向附近的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门报告；接到报告的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当及时组织调查核实，并依法采取必要的控制措施。

**Article 17** Where highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples are stolen, robbed, lost or leaked during transportation or storage, the carrier entity, the escort and the preservation institution shall take necessary control measures, and shall report to the competent department of the carrier entity, the entity to which the escort belongs and the competent department of the preservation institution respectively within 2 hours, and simultaneously report to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the local people's government at the county level; if they are stolen, robbed or lost, they shall also report to the public security organ; the health department or the veterinary department that receives the report shall report to the people's government at the same level within 2 hours, and simultaneously report to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government at the higher level and to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council.  
The people's government at the county level shall, within 2 hours after receiving the report, report to the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts or to the people's government at the next higher level; the people's government at the level of a city divided into districts shall, within 2 hours after receiving the report, report to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. The people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, within 1 hour after receiving the report, report to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the State Council.  
Any entity or individual that discovers a container or packaging material of highly pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) or samples shall promptly report to the nearby health department or veterinary department; the health department or veterinary department that receives the report shall promptly organize an investigation and verification, and take necessary control measures according to law.

### 第三章　实验室的设立与管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家根据实验室对病原微生物的生物安全防护水平，并依照实验室生物安全国家标准的规定，将实验室分为一级、二级、三级、四级。

**Article 18** The State shall classify laboratories into levels one, two, three and four according to the biosafety protection level of the laboratories against pathogenic microorganisms and in accordance with the provisions of the national standards on laboratory biosafety.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　新建、改建、扩建三级、四级实验室或者生产、进口移动式三级、四级实验室应当遵守下列规定：  
（一）符合国家生物安全实验室体系规划并依法履行有关审批手续；  
（二）经国务院卫生主管部门审查同意；  
（三）符合国家生物安全实验室建筑技术规范；  
（四）依照《中华人民共和国环境影响评价法》的规定进行环境影响评价并经环境保护主管部门审查批准；  
（五）生物安全防护级别与其拟从事的实验活动相适应。  
前款规定所称国家生物安全实验室体系规划，由国务院投资主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。制定国家生物安全实验室体系规划应当遵循总量控制、合理布局、资源共享的原则，并应当召开听证会或者论证会，听取公共卫生、环境保护、投资管理和实验室管理等方面专家的意见。

**Article 19** The construction of new, renovation, expansion of level three or four laboratories, or the production or import of mobile level three or four laboratories shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) conforming to the national biosafety laboratory system plan and performing the relevant approval formalities according to law;  
(2) having been examined and approved by the competent health department of the State Council;  
(3) conforming to the national technical specifications for biosafety laboratory construction;  
(4) conducting environmental impact assessment in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Impact Assessment Law of the People's Republic of China and having been examined and approved by the competent environmental protection department;  
(5) the biosafety protection level being compatible with the experimental activities to be conducted.  
The national biosafety laboratory system plan as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the competent investment department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council. The formulation of the national biosafety laboratory system plan shall follow the principles of total quantity control, rational layout and resource sharing, and a hearing or appraisal meeting shall be held to solicit opinions from experts in public health, environmental protection, investment management and laboratory management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　三级、四级实验室应当通过实验室国家认可。  
国务院认证认可监督管理部门确定的认可机构应当依照实验室生物安全国家标准以及本条例的有关规定，对三级、四级实验室进行认可；实验室通过认可的，颁发相应级别的生物安全实验室证书。证书有效期为5年。

**Article 20** Level three and four laboratories shall pass national laboratory accreditation.  
The accreditation body designated by the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council shall, in accordance with the national standards on laboratory biosafety and the relevant provisions of these Regulations, accredit level three and four laboratories; if a laboratory passes the accreditation, a biosafety laboratory certificate of the corresponding level shall be issued. The certificate shall be valid for five years.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事病原微生物实验活动应当在相应级别的实验室进行。实验室从事病原微生物实验活动，其级别应当不低于病原微生物目录规定的该项实验活动所需的实验室级别。  
一级、二级实验室仅可从事病原微生物目录规定的可以在一级、二级实验室进行的高致病性病原微生物实验活动。三级、四级实验室从事高致病性病原微生物实验活动，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）实验目的和拟从事的实验活动符合国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定；  
（二）通过实验室国家认可；  
（三）具有与拟从事的实验活动相适应的工作人员；  
（四）工程质量经建筑主管部门依法检测验收合格。

**Article 21** Pathogenic microorganism experiment activities shall be conducted in laboratories of corresponding levels. When conducting pathogenic microorganism experiment activities in a laboratory, the level of the laboratory shall not be lower than the laboratory level required for such experiment activity as specified in the catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms.  
Level I and Level II laboratories may only conduct experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms that, according to the catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms, may be conducted in Level I and Level II laboratories. Level III and Level IV laboratories conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The purpose of the experiment and the experiment activity to be conducted comply with the provisions of the competent health department or competent veterinary department under the State Council;  
(2) National accreditation for the laboratory has been obtained;  
(3) There are staff members suitable for the experiment activity to be conducted; and  
(4) The engineering quality has passed inspection and acceptance according to law by the competent construction department.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　三级、四级实验室，需要从事病原微生物目录规定的应当在三级、四级实验室进行的高致病性病原微生物实验活动或者疑似高致病性病原微生物实验活动的，应当依照国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定报省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门批准。实验活动结果以及工作情况应当向原批准部门报告。  
实验室申报或者接受与高致病性病原微生物有关的科研项目，应当符合科研需要和生物安全要求，具有相应的生物安全防护水平。与动物间传染的高致病性病原微生物有关的科研项目，应当经国务院兽医主管部门同意；与人体健康有关的高致病性病原微生物科研项目，实验室应当将立项结果告知省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门。

**Article 22** Where Level III and Level IV laboratories need to conduct experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms or suspected highly pathogenic microorganisms which, according to the catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms, shall be conducted in Level III and Level IV laboratories, they shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department or competent veterinary department under the State Council, report to and obtain approval from the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the provincial level. The results of the experiment activities and the work situation shall be reported to the original approving department.  
When a laboratory applies for or accepts a scientific research project related to highly pathogenic microorganisms, it shall meet the scientific research needs and biosafety requirements, and shall have the corresponding level of biosafety protection. Scientific research projects related to highly pathogenic microorganisms that are transmitted between animals shall be subject to the consent of the competent veterinary department under the State Council; for scientific research projects involving highly pathogenic microorganisms related to human health, the laboratory shall inform the competent health department of the people's government at or above the provincial level of the project establishment results.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　出入境检验检疫机构、医疗卫生机构、动物疫病预防控制机构在实验室开展检测、诊断工作时，发现高致病性病原微生物或者疑似高致病性病原微生物，需要进一步从事这类高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的，应当在具备相应条件的实验室中进行；依照本条例的规定需要经过批准的，应当取得批准。  
专门从事检测、诊断的实验室应当严格依照国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门的规定，建立健全规章制度，保证实验室生物安全。

**Article 23** Where entry-exit inspection and quarantine institutions, medical and health institutions, and animal epidemic prevention and control institutions, when carrying out testing and diagnostic work in a laboratory, discover highly pathogenic microorganisms or suspected highly pathogenic microorganisms and need to further conduct relevant experiment activities involving such highly pathogenic microorganisms, such activities shall be carried out in a laboratory with corresponding conditions; where approval is required in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, approval shall be obtained.  
Laboratories specialized in testing and diagnosis shall strictly in accordance with the provisions of the competent health department or competent veterinary department under the State Council, establish and improve rules and regulations to ensure laboratory biosafety.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当自收到需要从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的申请之日起15日内作出是否批准的决定。  
对出入境检验检疫机构为了检验检疫工作的紧急需要，申请在实验室对高致病性病原微生物或者疑似高致病性病原微生物开展进一步实验活动的，省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当自收到申请之时起2小时内作出是否批准的决定；2小时内未作出决定的，实验室可以从事相应的实验活动。  
省级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当为申请人通过电报、电传、传真、电子数据交换和电子邮件等方式提出申请提供方便。

**Article 24** The competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, within 15 days from receipt of an application for conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms, make a decision on whether to grant approval.  
Where an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution, due to urgent needs in inspection and quarantine work, applies to conduct further experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms or suspected highly pathogenic microorganisms in a laboratory, the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, within 2 hours from receipt of the application, make a decision on whether to grant approval; if no decision is made within 2 hours, the laboratory may conduct the corresponding experiment activities.  
The competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall facilitate the applicant submitting applications by way of telegram, telex, facsimile, electronic data interchange, and email.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　新建、改建或者扩建一级、二级实验室，应当向设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门备案。设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当每年将备案情况汇总后报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门。

**Article 25** The construction, renovation, or expansion of Level I and Level II laboratories shall be filed for record with the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at the city with districts level. The competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at the city with districts level shall, each year, summarize the record-filing situations and report them to the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门应当定期汇总并互相通报实验室数量和实验室设立、分布情况，以及三级、四级实验室从事高致病性病原微生物实验活动的情况。

**Article 26** The competent health department and the competent veterinary department under the State Council shall regularly summarize and mutually inform each other of the number of laboratories and the establishment and distribution of laboratories, as well as the situation of Level III and Level IV laboratories conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　已经建成并通过实验室国家认可的三级、四级实验室应当向所在地的县级人民政府环境保护主管部门备案。环境保护主管部门依照法律、行政法规的规定对实验室排放的废水、废气和其他废物处置情况进行监督检查。

**Article 27** Level III and Level IV laboratories that have been constructed and have passed national laboratory accreditation shall file for record with the competent environmental protection department of the people's government at the county level where they are located. The competent environmental protection department shall, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations, supervise and inspect the disposal of wastewater, waste gas, and other wastes discharged by the laboratories.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对我国尚未发现或者已经宣布消灭的病原微生物，任何单位和个人未经批准不得从事相关实验活动。  
为了预防、控制传染病，需要从事前款所指病原微生物相关实验活动的，应当经国务院卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门批准，并在批准部门指定的专业实验室中进行。

**Article 28** No unit or individual may, without approval, conduct relevant experiment activities involving pathogenic microorganisms that have not yet been discovered in China or have been declared eliminated.  
Where, for the purpose of preventing or controlling infectious diseases, it is necessary to conduct experiment activities involving the pathogenic microorganisms referred to in the preceding paragraph, approval shall be obtained from the competent health department or competent veterinary department under the State Council, and the activities shall be carried out in a specialized laboratory designated by the approving department.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　实验室使用新技术、新方法从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的，应当符合防止高致病性病原微生物扩散、保证生物安全和操作者人身安全的要求，并经国家病原微生物实验室生物安全专家委员会论证；经论证可行的，方可使用。

**Article 29** Where a laboratory uses new technologies or new methods to conduct experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms, it shall meet the requirements for preventing the spread of highly pathogenic microorganisms, ensuring biosafety, and ensuring the personal safety of operators, and shall be subject to evaluation by the National Expert Committee on Biosafety of Pathogenic Microorganism Laboratories; only after such evaluation demonstrates feasibility may they be used.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　需要在动物体上从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的，应当按照病原微生物目录的规定，在符合动物实验室生物安全国家标准的相应级别的实验室进行。

**Article 30** Where it is necessary to conduct experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms on animals, such activities shall, in accordance with the provisions of the catalogue of pathogenic microorganisms, be carried out in a laboratory of the corresponding level that meets the national standards for biosafety of animal laboratories.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　实验室的设立单位负责实验室的生物安全管理。  
实验室的设立单位应当依照本条例的规定制定科学、严格的管理制度，并定期对有关生物安全规定的落实情况进行检查，定期对实验室设施、设备、材料等进行检查、维护和更新，以确保其符合国家标准。  
实验室的设立单位及其主管部门应当加强对实验室日常活动的管理。

**Article 31** The entity that establishes the laboratory shall be responsible for the biosafety management of the laboratory.  
The entity that establishes the laboratory shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, formulate scientific and strict management systems, and regularly inspect the implementation of relevant biosafety provisions, and regularly inspect, maintain, and update laboratory facilities, equipment, materials, etc., to ensure that they meet national standards.  
The entity that establishes the laboratory and its competent department shall strengthen the management of the daily activities of the laboratory.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　实验室负责人为实验室生物安全的第一责任人。  
实验室从事实验活动应当严格遵守有关国家标准和实验室技术规范、操作规程。实验室负责人应当指定专人监督检查实验室技术规范和操作规程的落实情况。

**Article 32** The person in charge of the laboratory shall be the primary person responsible for the biosafety of the laboratory.  
When conducting experiment activities, the laboratory shall strictly observe relevant national standards and laboratory technical specifications and operating procedures. The person in charge of the laboratory shall designate a specific person to supervise and inspect the implementation of the laboratory technical specifications and operating procedures.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室的设立单位，应当建立健全安全保卫制度，采取安全保卫措施，严防高致病性病原微生物被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏，保障实验室及其病原微生物的安全。实验室发生高致病性病原微生物被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏的，实验室的设立单位应当依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定进行报告。  
从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室应当向当地公安机关备案，并接受公安机关有关实验室安全保卫工作的监督指导。

**Article 33** The entity that establishes a laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall establish and improve a security system, take security measures, strictly prevent the theft, robbery, loss, or leakage of highly pathogenic microorganisms, and ensure the safety of the laboratory and its pathogenic microorganisms. In the event of theft, robbery, loss, or leakage of highly pathogenic microorganisms in the laboratory, the entity that establishes the laboratory shall make a report in accordance with the provisions of Article 17 of these Regulations.  
A laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall file for record with the local public security organ and accept the supervision and guidance of the public security organ concerning the laboratory's security work.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　实验室或者实验室的设立单位应当每年定期对工作人员进行培训，保证其掌握实验室技术规范、操作规程、生物安全防护知识和实际操作技能，并进行考核。工作人员经考核合格的，方可上岗。  
从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室，应当每半年将培训、考核其工作人员的情况和实验室运行情况向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门报告。

**Article 34** The laboratory or the entity that establishes the laboratory shall conduct regular training for staff members each year to ensure that they master the laboratory technical specifications, operating procedures, biosafety protection knowledge, and practical operational skills, and shall conduct assessments. Staff members may only take up their posts after passing the assessments.  
A laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall, every six months, report the training and assessment of its staff members and the operation of the laboratory to the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动应当有2名以上的工作人员共同进行。  
进入从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室的工作人员或者其他有关人员，应当经实验室负责人批准。实验室应当为其提供符合防护要求的防护用品并采取其他职业防护措施。从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室，还应当对实验室工作人员进行健康监测，每年组织对其进行体检，并建立健康档案；必要时，应当对实验室工作人员进行预防接种。

**Article 35** Experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall be conducted jointly by two or more staff members.  
Staff members or other relevant persons entering a laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall obtain approval from the person in charge of the laboratory. The laboratory shall provide them with protective articles meeting the protection requirements and take other occupational protection measures. A laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall also conduct health monitoring for laboratory staff members, arrange annual physical examinations for them, and establish health records; when necessary, shall provide preventive vaccinations for laboratory staff members.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在同一个实验室的同一个独立安全区域内，只能同时从事一种高致病性病原微生物的相关实验活动。

**Article 36** Only one type of experiment activity involving highly pathogenic microorganisms may be conducted at the same time within the same independent safety area of the same laboratory.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实验室应当建立实验档案，记录实验室使用情况和安全监督情况。实验室从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验档案保存期，不得少于20年。

**Article 37** The laboratory shall establish experiment archives to record the use of the laboratory and the safety supervision situation. The retention period of experiment archives for laboratories conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall not be less than 20 years.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　实验室应当依照环境保护的有关法律、行政法规和国务院有关部门的规定，对废水、废气以及其他废物进行处置，并制定相应的环境保护措施，防止环境污染。

**Article 38** The laboratory shall, in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations on environmental protection and the provisions of the relevant departments under the State Council, dispose of wastewater, waste gas, and other wastes, and formulate corresponding environmental protection measures to prevent environmental pollution.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　三级、四级实验室应当在明显位置标示国务院卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门规定的生物危险标识和生物安全实验室级别标志。

**Article 39** Level III and Level IV laboratories shall display, in prominent locations, the biohazard signs and biosafety laboratory level markings as prescribed by the competent health department and competent veterinary department under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室应当制定实验室生物安全事件应急处置预案，并向该实验室所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门备案。

**Article 40** A laboratory conducting experiment activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms shall formulate an emergency response plan for biosafety incidents and file it for record with the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the laboratory is located.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门会同国务院有关部门组织病原学、免疫学、检验医学、流行病学、预防兽医学、环境保护和实验室管理等方面的专家，组成国家病原微生物实验室生物安全专家委员会。该委员会承担从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室的设立与运行的生物安全评估和技术咨询、论证工作。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门会同同级人民政府有关部门组织病原学、免疫学、检验医学、流行病学、预防兽医学、环境保护和实验室管理等方面的专家，组成本地区病原微生物实验室生物安全专家委员会。该委员会承担本地区实验室设立和运行的技术咨询工作。

**Article 41** The competent health department and the competent veterinary department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize experts in etiology, immunology, laboratory medicine, epidemiology, preventive veterinary medicine, environmental protection, and laboratory management to form the National Biosafety Expert Committee for Pathogenic Microorganism Laboratories. This committee shall undertake the biosafety assessment and technical consultation and demonstration work for the establishment and operation of laboratories engaged in experimental activities related to highly pathogenic microorganisms.  
The competent health department and the competent veterinary department of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, organize experts in etiology, immunology, laboratory medicine, epidemiology, preventive veterinary medicine, environmental protection, and laboratory management to form the local biosafety expert committee for pathogenic microorganism laboratories. This committee shall undertake the technical consultation work for the establishment and operation of local laboratories.

### 第四章　实验室感染控制

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　实验室的设立单位应当指定专门的机构或者人员承担实验室感染控制工作，定期检查实验室的生物安全防护、病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本保存与使用、安全操作、实验室排放的废水和废气以及其他废物处置等规章制度的实施情况。  
负责实验室感染控制工作的机构或者人员应当具有与该实验室中的病原微生物有关的传染病防治知识，并定期调查、了解实验室工作人员的健康状况。

**Article 42** The entity establishing a laboratory shall designate a specialized institution or personnel to be responsible for laboratory infection control, and shall regularly inspect the implementation of rules and regulations concerning biosafety protection, storage and use of pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples, safe operation, disposal of wastewater, exhaust gas, and other wastes discharged from the laboratory, etc.  
The institution or personnel responsible for laboratory infection control shall possess knowledge of the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases related to the pathogenic microorganisms in the laboratory, and shall regularly survey and ascertain the health status of laboratory staff.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　实验室工作人员出现与本实验室从事的高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动有关的感染临床症状或者体征时，实验室负责人应当向负责实验室感染控制工作的机构或者人员报告，同时派专人陪同及时就诊；实验室工作人员应当将近期所接触的病原微生物的种类和危险程度如实告知诊治医疗机构。接诊的医疗机构应当及时救治；不具备相应救治条件的，应当依照规定将感染的实验室工作人员转诊至具备相应传染病救治条件的医疗机构；具备相应传染病救治条件的医疗机构应当接诊治疗，不得拒绝救治。

**Article 43** When laboratory staff develop clinical symptoms or signs of infection related to the experimental activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms conducted in the laboratory, the person in charge of the laboratory shall report to the institution or personnel responsible for laboratory infection control, and shall at the same time arrange for someone to accompany the staff to seek medical treatment promptly. The laboratory staff shall truthfully inform the medical institution providing diagnosis and treatment of the types and degree of danger of the pathogenic microorganisms they have recently been exposed to. The medical institution receiving the patient shall provide timely treatment. If the medical institution does not have the appropriate conditions for treatment, it shall transfer the infected laboratory staff to a medical institution with the appropriate conditions for treating infectious diseases in accordance with regulations. A medical institution with the appropriate conditions for treating infectious diseases shall accept and treat the patient and shall not refuse treatment.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　实验室发生高致病性病原微生物泄漏时，实验室工作人员应当立即采取控制措施，防止高致病性病原微生物扩散，并同时向负责实验室感染控制工作的机构或者人员报告。

**Article 44** When a leakage of highly pathogenic microorganisms occurs in a laboratory, laboratory staff shall immediately take control measures to prevent the spread of the highly pathogenic microorganisms, and shall at the same time report to the institution or personnel responsible for laboratory infection control.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　负责实验室感染控制工作的机构或者人员接到[本条例第四十三条](#art-43)、第四十四条规定的报告后，应当立即启动实验室生物安全事件应急处置预案，并组织人员对该实验室生物安全状况等情况进行调查；确认发生实验室感染或者高致病性病原微生物泄漏的，应当依照[本条例第十七条](#art-17)的规定进行报告，并同时采取控制措施，对有关人员进行医学观察或者隔离治疗，封闭实验室，防止扩散。

**Article 45** Upon receiving a report as stipulated in Article 43 and Article 44 of these Regulations, the institution or personnel responsible for laboratory infection control shall immediately initiate the emergency response plan for laboratory biosafety incidents, and shall organize personnel to investigate the biosafety conditions of the laboratory, among other matters. If it is confirmed that a laboratory infection or leakage of highly pathogenic microorganisms has occurred, they shall report in accordance with Article 17 of these Regulations, and shall at the same time take control measures, place relevant personnel under medical observation or isolation treatment, seal the laboratory, and prevent spread.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门接到关于实验室发生工作人员感染事故或者病原微生物泄漏事件的报告，或者发现实验室从事病原微生物相关实验活动造成实验室感染事故的，应当立即组织疾病预防控制机构、动物疫病预防控制机构和医疗机构以及其他有关机构依法采取下列预防、控制措施：  
（一）封闭被病原微生物污染的实验室或者可能造成病原微生物扩散的场所；  
（二）开展流行病学调查；  
（三）对病人进行隔离治疗，对相关人员进行医学检查；  
（四）对密切接触者进行医学观察；  
（五）进行现场消毒；  
（六）对染疫或者疑似染疫的动物采取隔离、扑杀等措施；  
（七）其他需要采取的预防、控制措施。

**Article 46** Upon receiving a report of a laboratory staff infection accident or a pathogenic microorganism leakage incident, or upon discovering that a laboratory conducting experimental activities related to pathogenic microorganisms has caused a laboratory infection accident, the competent health department or the competent veterinary department shall immediately organize disease prevention and control institutions, animal epidemic prevention and control institutions, medical institutions, and other relevant institutions to take the following preventive and control measures according to law:  
(1) Seal off the laboratory contaminated by pathogenic microorganisms or the place that may cause spread of pathogenic microorganisms;  
(2) Conduct epidemiological investigation;  
(3) Isolate and treat patients, and conduct medical examinations on relevant personnel;  
(4) Conduct medical observation on close contacts;  
(5) Carry out on-site disinfection;  
(6) Take measures such as isolation and culling of animals that are infected or suspected of being infected;  
(7) Other preventive and control measures that need to be taken.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　医疗机构或者兽医医疗机构及其执行职务的医务人员发现由于实验室感染而引起的与高致病性病原微生物相关的传染病病人、疑似传染病病人或者患有疫病、疑似患有疫病的动物，诊治的医疗机构或者兽医医疗机构应当在2小时内报告所在地的县级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门；接到报告的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当在2小时内通报实验室所在地的县级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门。接到通报的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当依照[本条例第四十六条](#art-46)的规定采取预防、控制措施。

**Article 47** When a medical institution or veterinary medical institution and its practicing medical staff discover a patient with an infectious disease related to highly pathogenic microorganisms caused by laboratory infection, a suspected patient with such an infectious disease, or an animal suffering from or suspected of suffering from an epidemic disease, the medical institution or veterinary medical institution providing diagnosis and treatment shall report to the competent health department or the competent veterinary department of the people's government at the county level where it is located within 2 hours. The competent health department or competent veterinary department that receives the report shall notify the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at the county level where the laboratory is located within 2 hours. The competent health department or competent veterinary department that receives the notification shall take preventive and control measures in accordance with Article 46 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　发生病原微生物扩散，有可能造成传染病暴发、流行时，县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定以及实验室生物安全事件应急处置预案进行处理。

**Article 48** When a spread of pathogenic microorganisms occurs that may cause an outbreak or epidemic of an infectious disease, the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at or above the county level shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations and the emergency response plan for laboratory biosafety incidents.

### 第五章　监督管理

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自分工，履行下列职责：  
（一）对病原微生物菌（毒）种、样本的采集、运输、储存进行监督检查；  
（二）对从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室是否符合本条例规定的条件进行监督检查；  
（三）对实验室或者实验室的设立单位培训、考核其工作人员以及上岗人员的情况进行监督检查；  
（四）对实验室是否按照有关国家标准、技术规范和操作规程从事病原微生物相关实验活动进行监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门，应当主要通过检查反映实验室执行国家有关法律、行政法规以及国家标准和要求的记录、档案、报告，切实履行监督管理职责。

**Article 49** The competent health department and the competent veterinary department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective division of responsibilities, perform the following duties:  
(1) Supervise and inspect the collection, transport, and storage of pathogenic microorganism strains (viruses) and samples;  
(2) Supervise and inspect whether laboratories engaged in experimental activities related to highly pathogenic microorganisms meet the conditions stipulated in these Regulations;  
(3) Supervise and inspect the training and assessment of their staff and personnel on duty by laboratories or entities establishing laboratories;  
(4) Supervise and inspect whether laboratories conduct experimental activities related to pathogenic microorganisms in accordance with relevant national standards, technical specifications, and operational procedures.  
The competent health department and the competent veterinary department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall effectively perform their supervision and management duties mainly by inspecting records, archives, and reports that reflect the implementation by laboratories of relevant national laws, administrative regulations, and national standards and requirements.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门在履行监督检查职责时，有权进入被检查单位和病原微生物泄漏或者扩散现场调查取证、采集样品，查阅复制有关资料。需要进入从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室调查取证、采集样品的，应当指定或者委托专业机构实施。被检查单位应当予以配合，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 50** When performing their supervision and inspection duties, the competent health department, competent veterinary department, and competent environmental protection department of the people's government at or above the county level shall have the authority to enter the unit being inspected and the site of pathogenic microorganism leakage or spread to investigate and collect evidence, collect samples, and consult and copy relevant materials. If it is necessary to enter a laboratory engaged in experimental activities related to highly pathogenic microorganisms to investigate and collect evidence or collect samples, a specialized institution shall be designated or commissioned to carry out the work. The unit being inspected shall cooperate and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院认证认可监督管理部门依照《中华人民共和国认证认可条例》的规定对实验室认可活动进行监督检查。

**Article 51** The certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council shall supervise and inspect laboratory accreditation activities in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Certification and Accreditation.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门应当依据法定的职权和程序履行职责，做到公正、公平、公开、文明、高效。

**Article 52** The competent health department, competent veterinary department, and competent environmental protection department shall perform their duties in accordance with statutory authorities and procedures, and shall be fair, just, open, civilized, and efficient.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门的执法人员执行职务时，应当有2名以上执法人员参加，出示执法证件，并依照规定填写执法文书。  
现场检查笔录、采样记录等文书经核对无误后，应当由执法人员和被检查人、被采样人签名。被检查人、被采样人拒绝签名的，执法人员应当在自己签名后注明情况。

**Article 53** When law enforcement personnel of the competent health department, competent veterinary department, and competent environmental protection department perform their duties, there shall be at least two law enforcement personnel present, they shall produce their law enforcement credentials, and they shall fill out law enforcement documents in accordance with regulations.  
After verification, documents such as on-site inspection records and sampling records shall be signed by the law enforcement personnel and the person being inspected or the person from whom samples are taken. If the person being inspected or the person from whom samples are taken refuses to sign, the law enforcement personnel shall sign and indicate the circumstances.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门及其执法人员执行职务，应当自觉接受社会和公民的监督。公民、法人和其他组织有权向上级人民政府及其卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门举报地方人民政府及其有关主管部门不依照规定履行职责的情况。接到举报的有关人民政府或者其卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门，应当及时调查处理。

**Article 54** The competent health department, competent veterinary department, and competent environmental protection department and their law enforcement personnel shall consciously accept supervision from society and citizens when performing their duties. Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the right to report to the superior people's government and its competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department about the failure of the local people's government or its relevant competent departments to perform their duties in accordance with regulations. The relevant people's government or its competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle it.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　上级人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门发现属于下级人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门职责范围内需要处理的事项的，应当及时告知该部门处理；下级人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门不及时处理或者不积极履行本部门职责的，上级人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门应当责令其限期改正；逾期不改正的，上级人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门、环境保护主管部门有权直接予以处理。

**Article 55** If the competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department of a higher people's government discovers matters that fall within the responsibilities of the competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department of a lower people's government and need to be handled, it shall promptly notify that department to handle them. If the competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department of the lower people's government fails to handle them in a timely manner or fails to actively perform its departmental duties, the competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department of the higher people's government shall order it to make corrections within a time limit. If it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the competent health department, competent veterinary department, or competent environmental protection department of the higher people's government shall have the authority to handle the matter directly.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　三级、四级实验室未经批准从事病原微生物目录规定的应当在三级、四级实验室进行的高致病性病原微生物实验活动或者疑似高致病性病原微生物实验活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令停止有关活动，监督其将用于实验活动的病原微生物销毁或者送交保藏机构，并给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由实验室的设立单位对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** If a BSL-3 or BSL-4 laboratory, without approval, engages in experimental activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms or suspected highly pathogenic microorganisms that are specified in the catalog of pathogenic microorganisms and that shall be conducted in BSL-3 or BSL-4 laboratories, the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, order the cessation of the relevant activities, supervise the destruction or delivery of the pathogenic microorganisms used in the experimental activities to a preservation institution, and issue a warning. If this causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences, the entity establishing the laboratory shall, in accordance with law, impose sanctions of removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门违反本条例的规定，准予不符合本条例规定条件的实验室从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的，由作出批准决定的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门撤销原批准决定，责令有关实验室立即停止有关活动，并监督其将用于实验活动的病原微生物销毁或者送交保藏机构，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
因违法作出批准决定给当事人的合法权益造成损害的，作出批准决定的卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门应当依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 57** If the competent health department or the competent veterinary department, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, approves a laboratory that does not meet the conditions stipulated in these Regulations to engage in experimental activities related to highly pathogenic microorganisms, the competent health department or competent veterinary department that made the approval decision shall revoke the original approval decision, order the relevant laboratory to immediately cease the relevant activities, and supervise the destruction or delivery of the pathogenic microorganisms used in the experimental activities to a preservation institution, and shall impose administrative sanctions on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons according to law. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If the illegal approval decision causes damage to the lawful rights and interests of the party, the competent health department or competent veterinary department that made the approval decision shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门对出入境检验检疫机构为了检验检疫工作的紧急需要，申请在实验室对高致病性病原微生物或者疑似高致病性病原微生物开展进一步检测活动，不在法定期限内作出是否批准决定的，由其上级行政机关或者监察机关责令改正，给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予撤职、开除的行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** If the competent health department or the competent veterinary department fails to make a decision on whether to approve within the statutory time limit an application submitted by an entry-exit inspection and quarantine institution for carrying out further testing activities on highly pathogenic microorganisms or suspected highly pathogenic microorganisms in a laboratory due to urgent needs for inspection and quarantine work, the superior administrative organ or supervisory organ shall order it to make corrections and issue a warning. If this causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences, administrative sanctions of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons according to law. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，在不符合相应生物安全要求的实验室从事病原微生物相关实验活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令停止有关活动，监督其将用于实验活动的病原微生物销毁或者送交保藏机构，并给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由实验室的设立单位对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** If, in violation of the provisions of these Regulations, a laboratory that does not meet the corresponding biosafety requirements engages in experimental activities related to pathogenic microorganisms, the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, order the cessation of the relevant activities, supervise the destruction or delivery of the pathogenic microorganisms used in the experimental activities to a preservation institution, and issue a warning. If this causes the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences, the entity establishing the laboratory shall, in accordance with law, impose sanctions of removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons. If a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　实验室有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令限期改正，给予警告；逾期不改正的，由实验室的设立单位对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；有许可证件的，并由原发证部门吊销有关许可证件：  
（一）未依照规定在明显位置标示国务院卫生主管部门和兽医主管部门规定的生物危险标识和生物安全实验室级别标志的；  
（二）未向原批准部门报告实验活动结果以及工作情况的；  
（三）未依照规定采集病原微生物样本，或者对所采集样本的来源、采集过程和方法等未作详细记录的；  
（四）新建、改建或者扩建一级、二级实验室未向设区的市级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门备案的；  
（五）未依照规定定期对工作人员进行培训，或者工作人员考核不合格允许其上岗，或者批准未采取防护措施的人员进入实验室的；  
（六）实验室工作人员未遵守实验室生物安全技术规范和操作规程的；  
（七）未依照规定建立或者保存实验档案的；  
（八）未依照规定制定实验室生物安全事件应急处置预案并备案的。

**Article 60** Where a laboratory commits any of the following acts, the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective responsibilities, order a correction within a time limit and issue a warning. If the laboratory fails to make corrections within the time limit, the entity establishing the laboratory shall, in accordance with law, impose sanctions of removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible supervisor, and other directly responsible persons. If the laboratory holds a license, the original licensing authority shall revoke the relevant license:  
(1) Failing to display, in a conspicuous location, the biological hazard symbol and the biosafety laboratory level sign as prescribed by the competent health department and the competent veterinary department of the State Council in accordance with regulations;  
(2) Failing to report the results of experimental activities and work conditions to the original approving department;  
(3) Failing to collect pathogenic microorganism samples in accordance with regulations, or failing to make detailed records of the source, collection process, and methods of the collected samples;  
(4) Failing to file records with the competent health department or competent veterinary department of the people's government at the city with districts level when constructing, renovating, or expanding a BSL-1 or BSL-2 laboratory;  
(5) Failing to provide regular training to staff in accordance with regulations, or allowing staff who have failed assessment to work, or permitting personnel who have not taken protective measures to enter the laboratory;  
(6) Laboratory staff failing to comply with laboratory biosafety technical specifications and operational procedures;  
(7) Failing to establish or preserve experimental archives in accordance with regulations;  
(8) Failing to formulate and file an emergency response plan for laboratory biosafety incidents in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动的实验室的设立单位未建立健全安全保卫制度，或者未采取安全保卫措施的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令限期改正；逾期不改正，导致高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种、样本被盗、被抢或者造成其他严重后果的，责令停止该项实验活动，该实验室2年内不得从事高致病性病原微生物实验活动；造成传染病传播、流行的，该实验室设立单位的主管部门还应当对该实验室的设立单位的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where the establishment entity of a laboratory engaged in experimental activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms fails to establish and improve a security protection system or fails to take security protection measures, the health authority or veterinary authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the prescribed time limit, causing the theft, robbery, or other serious consequences of highly pathogenic microorganism bacterial (viral) strains or samples, the laboratory shall be ordered to cease such experimental activities, and the laboratory shall not engage in experimental activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms for two years; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease is caused, the department in charge of the establishment entity of the laboratory shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as demotion, removal from office, or dismissal on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons of the establishment entity; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　未经批准运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本，或者承运单位经批准运输高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本未履行保护义务，导致高致病性病原微生物菌（毒）种或者样本被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令采取措施，消除隐患，给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由托运单位和承运单位的主管部门对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where the transportation of highly pathogenic microorganism bacterial (viral) strains or samples is carried out without approval, or the carrier entity, after being approved to transport such strains or samples, fails to fulfill its protection obligations, resulting in the theft, robbery, loss, or leakage of the strains or samples, the health authority or veterinary authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order the entity to take measures to eliminate hazards and give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the department in charge of the consignor entity and the carrier entity shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　有下列行为之一的，由实验室所在地的设区的市级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令有关单位立即停止违法活动，监督其将病原微生物销毁或者送交保藏机构；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由其所在单位或者其上级主管部门对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；有许可证件的，并由原发证部门吊销有关许可证件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）实验室在相关实验活动结束后，未依照规定及时将病原微生物菌（毒）种和样本就地销毁或者送交保藏机构保管的；  
（二）实验室使用新技术、新方法从事高致病性病原微生物相关实验活动未经国家病原微生物实验室生物安全专家委员会论证的；  
（三）未经批准擅自从事在我国尚未发现或者已经宣布消灭的病原微生物相关实验活动的；  
（四）在未经指定的专业实验室从事在我国尚未发现或者已经宣布消灭的病原微生物相关实验活动的；  
（五）在同一个实验室的同一个独立安全区域内同时从事两种或者两种以上高致病性病原微生物的相关实验活动的。

**Article 63** Where any of the following acts is committed, the health authority or veterinary authority of the local people's government at or above the city divided into districts where the laboratory is located shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order the relevant entity to immediately cease the illegal activity, and supervise the destruction of the pathogenic microorganisms or their delivery to a preservation institution; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the entity to which the person belongs or its superior department in charge shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons; if the entity holds a license, the original issuing department shall revoke the relevant license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) After the completion of relevant experimental activities, the laboratory fails to destroy the pathogenic microorganism bacterial (viral) strains and samples on site or deliver them to a preservation institution for storage in a timely manner in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) The laboratory uses new technologies or new methods to engage in experimental activities involving highly pathogenic microorganisms without the assessment of the National Biosafety Expert Committee for Pathogenic Microorganism Laboratories;  
(3) Without approval, the laboratory engages in experimental activities involving pathogenic microorganisms that have not yet been discovered in China or have been declared eradicated;  
(4) Engaging in experimental activities involving pathogenic microorganisms that have not yet been discovered in China or have been declared eradicated in a professional laboratory that has not been designated;  
(5) Engaging in experimental activities involving two or more types of highly pathogenic microorganisms simultaneously in the same independent safety area of the same laboratory.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　认可机构对不符合实验室生物安全国家标准以及本条例规定条件的实验室予以认可，或者对符合实验室生物安全国家标准以及本条例规定条件的实验室不予认可的，由国务院认证认可监督管理部门责令限期改正，给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由国务院认证认可监督管理部门撤销其认可资格，有上级主管部门的，由其上级主管部门对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 64** Where an accreditation body grants accreditation to a laboratory that does not meet the national standards for laboratory biosafety and the conditions prescribed in these Regulations, or fails to grant accreditation to a laboratory that meets such standards and conditions, the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit and give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the certification and accreditation supervision and administration department of the State Council shall revoke its accreditation qualification; if it has a superior department in charge, the superior department shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　实验室工作人员出现该实验室从事的病原微生物相关实验活动有关的感染临床症状或者体征，以及实验室发生高致病性病原微生物泄漏时，实验室负责人、实验室工作人员、负责实验室感染控制的专门机构或者人员未依照规定报告，或者未依照规定采取控制措施的，由县级以上地方人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令限期改正，给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由其设立单位对实验室主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；有许可证件的，并由原发证部门吊销有关许可证件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where a laboratory worker presents clinical symptoms or signs of infection related to the experimental activities involving pathogenic microorganisms conducted in the laboratory, or where a leakage of highly pathogenic microorganisms occurs in the laboratory, and the laboratory principal, laboratory workers, or the specialized institution or personnel responsible for laboratory infection control fail to report the situation in accordance with the provisions or fail to take control measures in accordance with the provisions, the health authority or veterinary authority of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order corrections within a prescribed time limit and give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the establishment entity of the laboratory shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons of the laboratory; if the laboratory holds a license, the original issuing department shall revoke the relevant license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　拒绝接受卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依法开展有关高致病性病原微生物扩散的调查取证、采集样品等活动或者依照本条例规定采取有关预防、控制措施的，由县级以上人民政府卫生主管部门、兽医主管部门依照各自职责，责令改正，给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行以及其他严重后果的，由实验室的设立单位对实验室主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予降级、撤职、开除的处分；有许可证件的，并由原发证部门吊销有关许可证件；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 66** Where a party refuses to accept the investigation, evidence collection, sample collection, or other activities conducted by the health authority or veterinary authority according to law concerning the spread of highly pathogenic microorganisms, or refuses to take relevant prevention and control measures in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the health authority or veterinary authority of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, order corrections and give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the establishment entity of the laboratory shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as demotion, removal from office, or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons of the laboratory; if the laboratory holds a license, the original issuing department shall revoke the relevant license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　发生病原微生物被盗、被抢、丢失、泄漏，承运单位、护送人、保藏机构和实验室的设立单位未依照本条例的规定报告的，由所在地的县级人民政府卫生主管部门或者兽医主管部门给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由实验室的设立单位或者承运单位、保藏机构的上级主管部门对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 67** Where pathogenic microorganisms are stolen, robbed, lost, or leaked, and the carrier entity, escort, preservation institution, or establishment entity of the laboratory fails to report in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the health authority or veterinary authority of the local people's government at the county level shall give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the superior department in charge of the establishment entity of the laboratory, the carrier entity, or the preservation institution shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　保藏机构未依照规定储存实验室送交的菌（毒）种和样本，或者未依照规定提供菌（毒）种和样本的，由其指定部门责令限期改正，收回违法提供的菌（毒）种和样本，并给予警告；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，由其所在单位或者其上级主管部门对主要负责人、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予撤职、开除的处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 68** Where a preservation institution fails to store the bacterial (viral) strains and samples delivered by a laboratory in accordance with the provisions, or fails to provide bacterial (viral) strains and samples in accordance with the provisions, the department that designated it shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit, recover the illegally provided bacterial (viral) strains and samples, and give a warning; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the entity to which it belongs or its superior department in charge shall, according to law, impose sanctions such as removal from office or dismissal on the principal responsible person, the directly responsible person in charge, and other directly liable persons; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　县级以上人民政府有关主管部门，未依照本条例的规定履行实验室及其实验活动监督检查职责的，由有关人民政府在各自职责范围内责令改正，通报批评；造成传染病传播、流行或者其他严重后果的，对直接负责的主管人员，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** Where the relevant competent departments of the people's government at or above the county level fail to perform their duties of supervision and inspection of laboratories and their experimental activities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant people's government shall, within its respective duties, order corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; if the spread or epidemic of an infectious disease or other serious consequences are caused, the directly responsible person in charge shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　军队实验室由中国人民解放军卫生主管部门参照本条例负责监督管理。

**Article 70** Military laboratories shall be supervised and managed by the health authority of the Chinese People's Liberation Army by reference to these Regulations.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本条例施行前设立的实验室，应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内，依照本条例的规定，办理有关手续。

**Article 71** Laboratories established before the coming into force of these Regulations shall, within six months from the date on which these Regulations come into force, go through the relevant formalities in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 72** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
