# 城市市容和环境卫生管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-03-01  
**生效日期**：1992-08-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城市市容和环境卫生管理，创造清洁、优美的城市工作、生活环境，促进城市社会主义物质文明和精神文明建设，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of urban appearance and environmental sanitation, create clean and beautiful urban working and living environments, and promote the construction of socialist material civilization and spiritual civilization in cities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国城市内，一切单位和个人都必须遵守本条例。

**Article 2** Within cities of the People's Republic of China, all entities and individuals must comply with these Regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市市容和环境卫生工作，实行统一领导、分区负责、专业人员管理与群众管理相结合的原则。

**Article 3** The work of urban appearance and environmental sanitation shall implement the principle of unified leadership, division of responsibilities by area, and combination of professional management with public management.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院城市建设行政主管部门主管全国城市市容和环境卫生工作。  
省、自治区人民政府城市建设行政主管部门负责本行政区域的城市市容和环境卫生管理工作。  
城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门负责本行政区域的城市市容和环境卫生管理工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of urban construction under the State Council shall be in charge of the work of urban appearance and environmental sanitation nationwide.  
The competent department of urban construction under the people's government of a province or autonomous region shall be responsible for the management of urban appearance and environmental sanitation within its administrative region.  
The competent department of urban appearance and environmental sanitation under the people's government of a city shall be responsible for the management of urban appearance and environmental sanitation within its administrative region.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　城市人民政府应当把城市市容和环境卫生事业纳入国民经济和社会发展计划，并组织实施。  
城市人民政府应当结合本地的实际情况，积极推行环境卫生用工制度的改革，并采取措施，逐步提高环境卫生工作人员的工资福利待遇。

**Article 5** The people's government of a city shall incorporate the undertakings of urban appearance and environmental sanitation into the national economic and social development plan, and organize its implementation.  
The people's government of a city shall, in light of local actual conditions, actively promote the reform of the employment system for environmental sanitation, and adopt measures to gradually improve the wages and welfare benefits of environmental sanitation workers.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　城市人民政府应当加强城市市容和环境卫生科学知识的宣传，提高公民的环境卫生意识，养成良好的卫生习惯。  
一切单位和个人，都应当尊重市容和环境卫生工作人员的劳动，不得妨碍、阻挠市容和环境卫生工作人员履行职务。

**Article 6** The people's government of a city shall strengthen the publicity of scientific knowledge on urban appearance and environmental sanitation, enhance citizens' awareness of environmental sanitation, and foster good hygiene habits.  
All entities and individuals shall respect the labor of personnel engaged in urban appearance and environmental sanitation work, and shall not obstruct or impede such personnel in the performance of their duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励城市市容和环境卫生的科学技术研究，推广先进技术，提高城市市容和环境卫生水平。

**Article 7** The State shall encourage scientific and technological research on urban appearance and environmental sanitation, promote advanced technologies, and improve the level of urban appearance and environmental sanitation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对在城市市容和环境卫生工作中成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 8** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of urban appearance and environmental sanitation shall be rewarded by the people's government.

### 第二章　城市市容管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市中的建筑物和设施，应当符合国家规定的城市容貌标准。对外开放城市、风景旅游城市和有条件的其他城市，可以结合本地具体情况，制定严于国家规定的城市容貌标准；建制镇可以参照国家规定的城市容貌标准执行。

**Article 9** Buildings and facilities in cities shall conform to the national standards for urban appearance. Open cities, scenic and tourist cities, and other cities with appropriate conditions may, in light of their specific local circumstances, formulate standards for urban appearance that are stricter than the national standards; towns with organizational systems may implement the national standards for urban appearance by reference.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　一切单位和个人都应当保持建筑物的整洁、美观。在城市人民政府规定的街道的临街建筑物的阳台和窗外，不得堆放、吊挂有碍市容的物品。搭建或者封闭阳台必须符合城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门的有关规定。

**Article 10** All entities and individuals shall keep buildings clean and tidy. On the balconies and outside windows of buildings facing streets designated by the people's government of a city, no articles that impair urban appearance shall be piled up or hung up. Any construction or enclosure of balconies must comply with the relevant provisions of the competent department of urban appearance and environmental sanitation under the people's government of the city.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在城市中设置户外广告、标语牌、画廊、橱窗等，应当内容健康、外型美观，并定期维修、油饰或者拆除。  
大型户外广告的设置必须征得城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门同意后，按照有关规定办理审批手续。

**Article 11** The installation of outdoor advertisements, billboards, galleries, show windows, etc. in cities shall be healthy in content, pleasant in appearance, and shall be regularly maintained, painted or removed.  
The installation of large outdoor advertisements must obtain the consent of the administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the people's government of the city, and then go through the approval procedures in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　城市中的市政公用设施，应当与周围环境相协调，并维护和保持设施完好、整洁。

**Article 12** The municipal public facilities in cities shall be coordinated with the surrounding environment, and the facilities shall be maintained and kept intact and clean.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　主要街道两侧的建筑物前，应当根据需要与可能，选用透景、半透景的围墙、栅栏或者绿篱、花坛(池)、草坪等作为分界。  
临街树木、绿篱、花坛(池)、草坪等，应当保持整洁、美观。栽培、整修或者其他作业留下的渣土、枝叶等，管理单位、个人或者作业者应当及时清除。

**Article 13** In front of buildings on both sides of main streets, transparent or semi-transparent walls, fences, or hedges, flower beds (pools), lawns, etc. shall be used as boundaries according to needs and possibilities.  
The trees, hedges, flower beds (pools), lawns, etc. along the street shall be kept clean and beautiful. The residue soil, branches and leaves left from cultivation, trimming or other operations shall be promptly removed by the managing unit, individual or the operator.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　任何单位和个人都不得在街道两侧和公共场地堆放物料，搭建建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施。因建设等特殊需要，在街道两侧和公共场地临时堆放物料，搭建非永久性建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施的，必须征得城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门同意后，按照有关规定办理审批手续。

**Article 14** No unit or individual shall pile up materials, erect buildings, structures or other facilities on both sides of streets or in public places. Where it is necessary for special purposes such as construction to temporarily pile up materials, erect non-permanent buildings, structures or other facilities on both sides of streets or in public places, the consent of the administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the people's government of the city must be obtained, and the approval procedures shall be gone through in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　在市区运行的交通运输工具，应当保持外型完好、整洁，货运车辆运输的液体、散装货物，应当密封、包扎、覆盖，避免泄漏、遗撒。

**Article 15** Means of transport operating in urban areas shall be kept intact and clean in appearance, and the liquid and bulk goods transported by freight vehicles shall be sealed, wrapped or covered to avoid leakage and spillage.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城市的工程施工现场的材料、机具应当堆放整齐，渣土应当及时清运；临街工地应当设置护栏或者围布遮挡；停工场地应当及时整理并作必要的覆盖；竣工后，应当及时清理和平整场地。

**Article 16** Materials and equipment on construction sites in cities shall be stacked in order, and spoil shall be promptly removed; sites adjacent to streets shall be provided with guardrails or covered with cloth screens; idle sites shall be promptly tidied up and properly covered; after completion of the project, the site shall be promptly cleaned and leveled.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　一切单位和个人，都不得在城市建筑物、设施以及树木上涂写、刻画。  
单位和个人在城市建筑物、设施上张挂、张贴宣传品等，须经城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者其他有关部门批准。

**Article 17** No unit or individual shall write or carve on urban buildings, facilities or trees.  
Any unit or individual that hangs or posts publicity materials, etc. on urban buildings or facilities shall obtain the approval of the administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the people's government of the city or other relevant departments.

### 第三章　城市环境卫生管理

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　城市中的环境卫生设施，应当符合国家规定的城市环境卫生标准。

**Article 18** The environmental sanitation facilities in cities shall conform to the national standards for urban environmental sanitation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　城市人民政府在进行城市新区开发或者旧区改造时，应当依照国家有关规定，建设生活废弃物的清扫、收集、运输和处理等环境卫生设施，所需经费应当纳入建设工程概算。

**Article 19** When developing new urban areas or renovating old areas, the people's government of the city shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, construct environmental sanitation facilities such as those for the cleaning, collection, transportation and disposal of domestic waste, and the necessary funds shall be included in the budget estimate of the construction project.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门，应当根据城市居住人口密度和流动人口数量以及公共场所等特定地区的需要，制定公共厕所建设规划，并按照规定的标准，建设、改造或者支持有关单位建设、改造公共厕所。  
城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门，应当配备专业人员或者委托有关单位和个人负责公共厕所的保洁和管理；有关单位和个人也可以承包公共厕所的保洁和管理。公共厕所的管理者可以适当收费，具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。  
对不符合规定标准的公共厕所，城市人民政府应当责令有关单位限期改造。  
公共厕所的粪便应当排入贮(化)粪池或者城市污水系统。

**Article 20** The administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the people's government of the city shall, based on the density of urban residential population, the number of floating population, and the needs of specific areas such as public places, formulate plans for the construction of public toilets, and construct, renovate or support relevant units in constructing or renovating public toilets in accordance with the prescribed standards.  
The administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the people's government of the city shall assign professional personnel or entrust relevant units or individuals to be responsible for the cleaning and management of public toilets; relevant units or individuals may also contract the cleaning and management of public toilets. The manager of a public toilet may charge appropriate fees, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For public toilets that do not meet the prescribed standards, the people's government of the city shall order the relevant units to renovate them within a time limit.  
The night soil from public toilets shall be discharged into septic tanks or urban sewage systems.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　多层和高层建筑应当设置封闭式垃圾通道或者垃圾贮存设施，并修建清运车辆通道。  
城市街道两侧、居住区或者人流密集地区，应当设置封闭式垃圾容器、果皮箱等设施。

**Article 21** Multi-story and high-rise buildings shall be equipped with enclosed garbage chutes or garbage storage facilities, and passages for garbage collection vehicles shall be constructed.  
On both sides of urban streets, in residential areas, or in densely populated areas, enclosed garbage containers, litter bins, and other facilities shall be installed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　一切单位和个人都不得擅自拆除环境卫生设施；因建设需要必须拆除的，建设单位必须事先提出拆迁方案，报城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门批准。

**Article 22** No entity or individual may dismantle environmental sanitation facilities without authorization; if dismantlement is necessary due to construction needs, the construction entity must submit a dismantling plan in advance and report it to the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government for approval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　按国家行政建制设立的市的主要街道、广场和公共水域的环境卫生，由环境卫生专业单位负责。  
居住区、街巷等地方，由街道办事处负责组织专人清扫保洁。

**Article 23** The environmental sanitation of main streets, squares, and public waters in cities established according to national administrative divisions shall be the responsibility of specialized environmental sanitation units.  
In residential areas, streets, and alleys, the subdistrict office shall be responsible for organizing dedicated personnel for cleaning and sanitation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　飞机场、火车站、公共汽车始末站、港口、影剧院、博物馆、展览馆、纪念馆、体育馆(场)和公园等公共场所，由本单位负责清扫保洁。

**Article 24** Public places such as airports, railway stations, bus terminals, ports, theaters, museums, exhibition halls, memorial halls, stadiums (gymnasiums), and parks shall be cleaned and maintained by the respective entities.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　机关、团体、部队、企事业单位，应当按照城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门划分的卫生责任区负责清扫保洁。

**Article 25** Government organs, organizations, military units, enterprises, and institutions shall be responsible for cleaning and maintaining the sanitation responsibility zones designated by the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　城市集贸市场，由主管部门负责组织专人清扫保洁。  
各种摊点，由从业者负责清扫保洁。

**Article 26** Urban markets shall be cleaned and maintained by the competent department organizing dedicated personnel.  
Various stalls shall be cleaned and maintained by the operators.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城市港口客货码头作业范围内的水面，由港口客货码头经营单位责成作业者清理保洁。  
在市区水域行驶或者停泊的各类船舶上的垃圾、粪便，由船上负责人依照规定处理。

**Article 27** The water surface within the operation scope of urban passenger and cargo terminals shall be cleaned and maintained by the terminal operating entity, which shall instruct the operators to do so.  
Garbage and excrement on various vessels navigating or mooring in urban waters shall be disposed of by the person in charge on board in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门对城市生活废弃物的收集、运输和处理实施监督管理。  
一切单位和个人，都应当依照城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门规定的时间、地点、方式，倾倒垃圾、粪便。  
对垃圾、粪便应当及时清运，并逐步做到垃圾、粪便的无害化处理和综合利用。  
对城市生活废弃物应当逐步做到分类收集、运输和处理。

**Article 28** The competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government shall exercise supervision and management over the collection, transportation, and treatment of urban domestic waste.  
All entities and individuals shall dump garbage and excrement at the times, places, and in the manner prescribed by the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government.  
Garbage and excrement shall be promptly collected and transported, and efforts shall be made step by step to achieve harmless treatment and comprehensive utilization of garbage and excrement.  
Urban domestic waste shall be gradually collected, transported, and treated by classification.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　环境卫生管理应当逐步实行社会化服务。有条件的城市，可以成立环境卫生服务公司。  
凡委托环境卫生专业单位清扫、收集、运输和处理废弃物的，应当交纳服务费。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 29** Environmental sanitation management shall gradually implement socialized services. Cities with the necessary conditions may establish environmental sanitation service companies.  
Any entity or individual that entrusts specialized environmental sanitation units with the cleaning, collection, transportation, and treatment of waste shall pay service fees. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城市人民政府应当有计划地发展城市煤气、天然气、液化气，改变燃料结构；鼓励和支持有关部门组织净菜进城和回收利用废旧物资，减少城市垃圾。

**Article 30** The city people's government shall systematically develop urban coal gas, natural gas, and liquefied petroleum gas to change the fuel structure; and shall encourage and support relevant departments in organizing the delivery of clean vegetables to the city and the recycling of waste materials, so as to reduce urban garbage.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　医院、疗养院、屠宰场、生物制品厂产生的废弃物，必须依照有关规定处理。

**Article 31** Waste generated by hospitals, sanatoriums, slaughterhouses, and biological product factories must be disposed of in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公民应当爱护公共卫生环境，不随地吐痰、便溺，不乱扔果皮、纸屑和烟头等废弃物。

**Article 32** Citizens shall protect the public health environment and shall not spit or defecate at will, nor discard waste such as fruit peels, paper scraps, and cigarette butts indiscriminately.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　按国家行政建制设立的市的市区内，禁止饲养鸡、鸭、鹅、兔、羊、猪等家畜家禽；因教学、科研以及其他特殊需要饲养的除外。

**Article 33** In the urban areas of cities established according to the national administrative structure, the raising of livestock and poultry such as chickens, ducks, geese, rabbits, sheep, and pigs is prohibited, except for those raised for teaching, scientific research, or other special needs.

### 第四章　罚则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　有下列行为之一者，城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者其委托的单位除责令其纠正违法行为、采取补救措施外，可以并处警告、罚款：  
(一)随地吐痰、便溺，乱扔果皮、纸屑和烟头等废弃物的；  
(二)在城市建筑物、设施以及树木上涂写、刻画或者未经批准张挂、张贴宣传品等的；  
(三)在城市人民政府规定的街道的临街建筑物的阳台和窗外，堆放、吊挂有碍市容的物品的；  
(四)不按规定的时间、地点、方式，倾倒垃圾、粪便的；  
(五)不履行卫生责任区清扫保洁义务或者不按规定清运、处理垃圾和粪便的；  
(六)运输液体、散装货物不作密封、包扎、覆盖，造成泄漏、遗撒的；  
(七)临街工地不设置护栏或者不作遮挡、停工场地不及时整理并作必要覆盖或者竣工后不及时清理和平整场地，影响市容和环境卫生的。

**Article 34** Anyone who commits any of the following acts shall, in addition to being ordered by the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government or its authorized entity to correct the illegal act and take remedial measures, be subject to a warning and a fine:  
(1) spitting or defecating at will, discarding waste such as fruit peels, paper scraps, and cigarette butts indiscriminately;  
(2) scribbling, carving, or hanging or posting promotional materials without approval on city buildings, facilities, or trees;  
(3) stacking or hanging objects that obstruct city appearance on balconies or outside windows of buildings along streets specified by the city people's government;  
(4) dumping garbage or excrement not in accordance with the prescribed time, place, or manner;  
(5) failing to fulfill the obligation of cleaning and maintaining the sanitation responsibility area, or failing to transport and dispose of garbage and excrement in accordance with regulations;  
(6) failing to seal, wrap, or cover liquid or bulk goods during transport, causing leakage or spillage;  
(7) failing to set up guardrails or coverings at construction sites along streets, failing to arrange and cover idle sites in a timely manner, or failing to clean and level the site after completion, thus affecting city appearance and environmental sanitation.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　饲养家畜家禽影响市容和环境卫生的，由城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者其委托的单位，责令其限期处理或者予以没收，并可处以罚款。

**Article 35** Where the raising of livestock and poultry affects city appearance and environmental sanitation, the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government or its authorized entity shall order the party to dispose of them within a specified time limit or confiscate them, and may impose a fine.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有下列行为之一者，由城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者其委托的单位责令其停止违法行为，限期清理、拆除或者采取其他补救措施，并可处以罚款：  
(一)未经城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门同意，擅自设置大型户外广告，影响市容的；  
(二)未经城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门批准，擅自在街道两侧和公共场地堆放物料，搭建建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施，影响市容的；  
(三)未经批准擅自拆除环境卫生设施或者未按批准的拆迁方案进行拆迁的。

**Article 36** Anyone who commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government or its authorized entity to cease the illegal act, clean up, remove, or take other remedial measures within a specified time limit, and may be subject to a fine:  
(1) setting up large outdoor advertisements without the consent of the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government, thus affecting city appearance;  
(2) stacking materials or constructing buildings, structures, or other facilities on both sides of streets or in public places without the approval of the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government, thus affecting city appearance;  
(3) demolishing environmental sanitation facilities without authorization or carrying out demolition not in accordance with the approved demolition plan.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　凡不符合城市容貌标准、环境卫生标准的建筑物或者设施，由城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门会同城市规划行政主管部门，责令有关单位和个人限期改造或者拆除；逾期未改造或者未拆除的，经县级以上人民政府批准，由城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者城市规划行政主管部门组织强制拆除，并可处以罚款。

**Article 37** Where any building or facility fails to meet the city appearance standards and environmental sanitation standards, the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government, together with the competent administrative department of urban planning, shall order the relevant entity or individual to renovate or demolish it within a specified time limit; if renovation or demolition is not completed within the time limit, with the approval of the people's government at or above the county level, the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government or the competent administrative department of urban planning shall organize compulsory demolition, and may impose a fine.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　损坏各类环境卫生设施及其附属设施的，城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门或者其委托的单位除责令其恢复原状外，可以并处罚款；盗窃、损坏各类环境卫生设施及其附属设施，应当给予治安管理处罚的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Where any person damages various types of environmental sanitation facilities and their ancillary facilities, the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of the city people's government or its authorized entity shall, in addition to ordering the person to restore them to their original condition, impose a fine concurrently; if the theft or damage of various types of environmental sanitation facilities and their ancillary facilities is subject to public security administration penalties, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Penalties; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　侮辱、殴打市容和环境卫生工作人员或者阻挠其执行公务的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where any person insults, assaults, or obstructs the city appearance and environmental sanitation staff in the execution of their official duties, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Penalties; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服的，可以自接到处罚通知之日起15日内，向作出处罚决定机关的上一级机关申请复议；对复议决定不服的，可以自接到复议决定书之日起15日内向人民法院起诉。当事人也可以自接到处罚通知之日起15日内直接向人民法院起诉。期满不申请复议、也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，由作出处罚决定的机关申请人民法院强制执行。  
对治安管理处罚不服的，依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定办理。

**Article 40** Where a party disagrees with the decision on administrative penalty, it may apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the penalty decision within 15 days from the date of receiving the penalty notice; if it disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receiving the reconsideration decision. The party may also directly file a prosecution with the People's Court within 15 days from the date of receiving the penalty notice. If the party neither applies for reconsideration nor files a prosecution with the People's Court within the time limit, nor performs the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.  
Where a party disagrees with a public security administration penalty, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Penalties.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　城市人民政府市容环境卫生行政主管部门工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊的，由其所在单位或者上级主管机关给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** If any staff member of the competent administrative department of city appearance and environmental sanitation of a city people's government neglects their duty, abuses their power, or engages in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given an administrative sanction by their unit or the competent authority at a higher level; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　未设镇建制的城市型居民区可以参照本条例执行。

**Article 42** Urban residential areas without the organizational system of a town may implement these Regulations by reference.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以根据本条例制定实施办法。

**Article 43** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate implementation measures in accordance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本条例由国务院城市建设行政主管部门负责解释。

**Article 44** These Regulations shall be interpreted by the competent administrative department of urban construction under the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自1992年8月1日起施行。

**Article 45** These Regulations shall come into force on August 1, 1992.
