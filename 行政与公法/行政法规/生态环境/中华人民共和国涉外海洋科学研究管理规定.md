# 中华人民共和国涉外海洋科学研究管理规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1996-06-18  
**生效日期**：1996-10-01

> (1996年6月18日中华人民共和国国务院令第199号发布　自1996年10月1日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的管理，促进海洋科学研究的国际交流与合作，维护国家安全和海洋权益，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定适用于国际组织、外国的组织和个人(以下简称外方)为和平目的，单独或者与中华人民共和国的组织(以下简称中方)合作，使用船舶或者其他运载工具、设施，在中华人民共和国内海、领海以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内进行的对海洋环境和海洋资源等的调查研究活动。但是，海洋矿产资源(包括海洋石油资源)勘查、海洋渔业资源调查和国家重点保护的海洋野生动物考察等活动，适用中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定。 第三条　中华人民共和国国家海洋行政主管部门(以下简称国家海洋行政主管部门)及其派出机构或者其委托的机构，对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行的涉外海洋科学研究活动，依照本规定实施管理。 国务院其他有关部门根据国务院规定的职责，协同国家海洋行政主管部门对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行的涉外海洋科学研究活动实施管理。 第四条　在中华人民共和国内海、领海内，外方进行海洋科学研究活动，应当采用与中方合作的方式。在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内，外方可以单独或者与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动。 外方单独或者与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动，须经国家海洋行政主管部门批准或者由国家海洋行政主管部门报请国务院批准，并遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规。 第五条　外方与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动的，中方应当在海洋科学研究计划预定开始日期6个月前，向国家海洋行政主管部门提出书面申请，并按照规定提交海洋科学研究计划和其他有关说明材料。 外方单独进行海洋科学研究活动的，应当在海洋科学研究计划预定开始日期6个月前，通过外交途径向国家海洋行政主管部门提出书面申请，并按照规定提交海洋科学研究计划和其他有关说明材料。 国家海洋行政主管部门收到海洋科学研究申请后，应当会同外交部、军事主管部门以及国务院其他有关部门进行审查，在4个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定，或者提出审查意见报请国务院决定。 第六条　经批准进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的，申请人应当在各航次开始之日2个月前，将海上船只活动计划报国家海洋行政主管部门审批。国家海洋行政主管部门应当自收到海上船只活动计划之日起1个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并书面通知申请人，同时通报国务院有关部门。 第七条　有关中外双方或者外方应当按照经批准的海洋科学研究计划和海上船只活动计划进行海洋科学研究活动；海洋科学研究计划或者海上船只活动计划在执行过程中需要作重大修改的，应当征得国家海洋行政主管部门同意。 因不可抗力不能执行经批准的海洋科学研究计划或者海上船只活动计划的，有关中外双方或者外方应当及时报告国家海洋行政主管部门；在不可抗力消失后，可以恢复执行、修改计划或者中止执行计划。 第八条　进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的，不得将有害物质引入海洋环境，不得擅自钻探或者使用炸药作业。 第九条　中外合作使用外国籍调查船在中华人民共和国内海、领海内进行海洋科学研究活动的，作业船舶应当于格林威治时间每天00时和08时，向国家海洋行政主管部门报告船位及船舶活动情况。外方单独或者中外合作使用外国籍调查船在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内进行海洋科学研究活动的，作业船舶应当于格林威治时间每天02时，向国家海洋行政主管部门报告船位及船舶活动情况。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的管理，促进海洋科学研究的国际交流与合作，维护国家安全和海洋权益，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to strengthen the management of foreign-related marine scientific research activities conducted within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, to promote international exchanges and cooperation in marine scientific research, and to safeguard national security and marine rights and interests.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定适用于国际组织、外国的组织和个人(以下简称外方)为和平目的，单独或者与中华人民共和国的组织(以下简称中方)合作，使用船舶或者其他运载工具、设施，在中华人民共和国内海、领海以及中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内进行的对海洋环境和海洋资源等的调查研究活动。但是，海洋矿产资源(包括海洋石油资源)勘查、海洋渔业资源调查和国家重点保护的海洋野生动物考察等活动，适用中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall apply to survey and research activities on the marine environment and marine resources conducted by international organizations, foreign organizations and individuals (hereinafter referred to as the foreign party) for peaceful purposes, either independently or in cooperation with organizations of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Chinese party), using vessels or other means of transport or facilities, within the internal waters, territorial sea, and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China. However, activities such as exploration of marine mineral resources (including offshore oil resources), surveys of marine fishery resources, and investigations of marine wild animals under special state protection shall be governed by the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国国家海洋行政主管部门(以下简称国家海洋行政主管部门)及其派出机构或者其委托的机构，对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行的涉外海洋科学研究活动，依照本规定实施管理。  
国务院其他有关部门根据国务院规定的职责，协同国家海洋行政主管部门对在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行的涉外海洋科学研究活动实施管理。

**Article 3** The national administrative department of marine affairs of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the national administrative department of marine affairs) and its dispatched agencies or agencies entrusted by it shall, in accordance with these Provisions, exercise administration over foreign-related marine scientific research activities conducted within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.  
Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the functions and duties prescribed by the State Council, cooperate with the national administrative department of marine affairs in exercising administration over foreign-related marine scientific research activities conducted within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　在中华人民共和国内海、领海内，外方进行海洋科学研究活动，应当采用与中方合作的方式。在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内，外方可以单独或者与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动。  
外方单独或者与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动，须经国家海洋行政主管部门批准或者由国家海洋行政主管部门报请国务院批准，并遵守中华人民共和国的有关法律、法规。

**Article 4** Within the internal waters and territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, the foreign party shall conduct marine scientific research activities in a cooperative manner with the Chinese party. Within other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the foreign party may conduct marine scientific research activities independently or in cooperation with the Chinese party.  
The foreign party conducting marine scientific research activities independently or in cooperation with the Chinese party must obtain approval from the national administrative department of marine affairs, or from the State Council upon submission by the national administrative department of marine affairs, and shall abide by the relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　外方与中方合作进行海洋科学研究活动的，中方应当在海洋科学研究计划预定开始日期6个月前，向国家海洋行政主管部门提出书面申请，并按照规定提交海洋科学研究计划和其他有关说明材料。  
外方单独进行海洋科学研究活动的，应当在海洋科学研究计划预定开始日期6个月前，通过外交途径向国家海洋行政主管部门提出书面申请，并按照规定提交海洋科学研究计划和其他有关说明材料。  
国家海洋行政主管部门收到海洋科学研究申请后，应当会同外交部、军事主管部门以及国务院其他有关部门进行审查，在4个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定，或者提出审查意见报请国务院决定。

**Article 5** Where the foreign party conducts marine scientific research activities in cooperation with the Chinese party, the Chinese party shall submit a written application to the national administrative department of marine affairs six months before the scheduled start date of the marine scientific research plan, and shall submit the marine scientific research plan and other relevant explanatory materials in accordance with the regulations.  
Where the foreign party conducts marine scientific research activities independently, it shall submit a written application to the national administrative department of marine affairs through diplomatic channels six months before the scheduled start date of the marine scientific research plan, and shall submit the marine scientific research plan and other relevant explanatory materials in accordance with the regulations.  
Upon receipt of the application for marine scientific research, the national administrative department of marine affairs shall, in conjunction with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the competent military department, and other relevant departments of the State Council, conduct a review, and shall make a decision on approval or disapproval within four months, or submit a review opinion to the State Council for a decision.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　经批准进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的，申请人应当在各航次开始之日2个月前，将海上船只活动计划报国家海洋行政主管部门审批。国家海洋行政主管部门应当自收到海上船只活动计划之日起1个月内作出批准或者不批准的决定，并书面通知申请人，同时通报国务院有关部门。

**Article 6** Where foreign-related marine scientific research activities have been approved, the applicant shall, two months before the start date of each voyage, submit the plan of vessel activities at sea to the national administrative department of marine affairs for examination and approval. The national administrative department of marine affairs shall, within one month from the date of receipt of the plan of vessel activities at sea, make a decision on approval or disapproval, notify the applicant in writing, and simultaneously notify the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　有关中外双方或者外方应当按照经批准的海洋科学研究计划和海上船只活动计划进行海洋科学研究活动；海洋科学研究计划或者海上船只活动计划在执行过程中需要作重大修改的，应当征得国家海洋行政主管部门同意。  
因不可抗力不能执行经批准的海洋科学研究计划或者海上船只活动计划的，有关中外双方或者外方应当及时报告国家海洋行政主管部门；在不可抗力消失后，可以恢复执行、修改计划或者中止执行计划。

**Article 7** The relevant Chinese and foreign parties or the foreign party shall conduct marine scientific research activities in accordance with the approved marine scientific research plan and the plan of vessel activities at sea; if major modifications to the marine scientific research plan or the plan of vessel activities at sea are necessary during execution, consent from the national administrative department of marine affairs shall be obtained.  
Where the approved marine scientific research plan or the plan of vessel activities at sea cannot be executed due to force majeure, the relevant Chinese and foreign parties or the foreign party shall promptly report to the national administrative department of marine affairs; after force majeure ceases, execution may be resumed, the plan may be modified, or execution may be terminated.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的，不得将有害物质引入海洋环境，不得擅自钻探或者使用炸药作业。

**Article 8** Those conducting foreign-related marine scientific research activities shall not introduce harmful substances into the marine environment, and shall not engage in drilling or use explosives without authorization.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中外合作使用外国籍调查船在中华人民共和国内海、领海内进行海洋科学研究活动的，作业船舶应当于格林威治时间每天00时和08时，向国家海洋行政主管部门报告船位及船舶活动情况。外方单独或者中外合作使用外国籍调查船在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内进行海洋科学研究活动的，作业船舶应当于格林威治时间每天02时，向国家海洋行政主管部门报告船位及船舶活动情况。  
国家海洋行政主管部门或者其派出机构、其委托的机构可以对前款外国籍调查船进行海上监视或者登船检查。

**Article 9** Where Chinese-foreign cooperation uses a foreign survey vessel to conduct marine scientific research activities within the internal waters and territorial sea of the People's Republic of China, the operating vessel shall report its position and vessel activity to the national administrative department of marine affairs at 00:00 and 08:00 hours Greenwich Mean Time each day. Where the foreign party independently or Chinese-foreign cooperation uses a foreign survey vessel to conduct marine scientific research activities within other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the operating vessel shall report its position and vessel activity to the national administrative department of marine affairs at 02:00 hours Greenwich Mean Time each day.  
The national administrative department of marine affairs or its dispatched agencies or agencies entrusted by it may conduct maritime surveillance or board and inspect the foreign survey vessel referred to in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中外合作在中华人民共和国内海、领海内进行海洋科学研究活动所获得的原始资料和样品，归中华人民共和国所有，参加合作研究的外方可以依照合同约定无偿使用。  
中外合作在中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域内进行海洋科学研究活动所获得的原始资料和样品，在不违反中华人民共和国有关法律、法规和有关规定的前提下，由中外双方按照协议分享，都可以无偿使用。  
外方单独进行海洋科学研究活动所获得的原始资料和样品，中华人民共和国的有关组织可以无偿使用；外方应当向国家海洋行政主管部门无偿提供所获得的资料的复制件和可分样品。  
未经国家海洋行政主管部门以及国务院其他有关部门同意，有关中外双方或者外方不得公开发表或者转让在中华人民共和国管辖海域内进行海洋科学研究活动所获得的原始资料和样品。

**Article 10** Original data and samples obtained from Chinese-foreign cooperative marine scientific research activities conducted within the internal waters and territorial sea of the People's Republic of China shall be owned by the People's Republic of China, and the foreign party participating in the cooperative research may use them free of charge in accordance with the contract.  
Original data and samples obtained from Chinese-foreign cooperative marine scientific research activities conducted within other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, provided that they do not violate the relevant laws, regulations, and provisions of the People's Republic of China, shall be shared by the Chinese and foreign parties in accordance with the agreement, and both parties may use them free of charge.  
Original data and samples obtained from marine scientific research activities conducted independently by the foreign party may be used free of charge by the relevant organizations of the People's Republic of China; the foreign party shall provide the national administrative department of marine affairs with copies of the obtained data and divisible samples free of charge.  
Without the consent of the national administrative department of marine affairs and other relevant departments of the State Council, the relevant Chinese and foreign parties or the foreign party shall not publicly publish or transfer the original data and samples obtained from marine scientific research activities conducted within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　外方单独或者中外合作进行的海洋科学研究活动结束后，所使用的外国籍调查船应当接受国家海洋行政主管部门或者其派出机构、其委托的机构检查。

**Article 11** After the completion of marine scientific research activities conducted independently by the foreign party or in Chinese-foreign cooperation, the foreign survey vessel used shall be subject to inspection by the national administrative department of marine affairs, its dispatched agencies, or agencies entrusted by it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　中外合作进行的海洋科学研究活动结束后，中方应当将研究成果和资料目录抄报国家海洋行政主管部门和国务院有关部门。  
外方单独进行的海洋科学研究活动结束后，应当向国家海洋行政主管部门提供该项活动所获得的资料或者复制件和样品或者可分样品，并及时提供有关阶段性研究成果以及最后研究成果和结论。

**Article 12** After the completion of Chinese-foreign cooperative marine scientific research activities, the Chinese party shall submit a copy of the research results and a list of data to the national administrative department of marine affairs and the relevant departments of the State Council.  
After the completion of marine scientific research activities conducted independently by the foreign party, it shall provide the national administrative department of marine affairs with the data or copies and samples or divisible samples obtained from the activities, and shall promptly provide relevant interim research results as well as final research results and conclusions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　违反本规定进行涉外海洋科学研究活动的，由国家海洋行政主管部门或者其派出机构、其委托的机构责令停止该项活动，可以没收违法活动器具、没收违法获得的资料和样品，可以单处或者并处5万元人民币以下的罚款。  
违反本规定造成重大损失或者引起严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where a party conducts foreign-related marine scientific research activities in violation of these Provisions, the national administrative department of marine affairs, its dispatched agencies, or agencies entrusted by it shall order the cessation of such activities, may confiscate the instruments used in the illegal activities, may confiscate the illegally obtained data and samples, and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan RMB alone or in combination with the confiscation.  
Where the violation of these Provisions causes significant losses or leads to serious consequences, constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约与本规定有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定；但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 14** Where an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions differing from those of these Provisions, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply; however, the provisions on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations shall be excepted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定自1996年10月1日起施行。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 1996.
