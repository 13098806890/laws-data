### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对民用核安全设备的监督管理，保证民用核设施的安全运行，预防核事故，保障工作人员和公众的健康，保护环境，促进核能事业的顺利发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the supervision and administration of civil nuclear safety equipment, ensuring the safe operation of civil nuclear installations, preventing nuclear accidents, protecting the health of workers and the public, protecting the environment, and promoting the smooth development of the nuclear energy industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称民用核安全设备，是指在民用核设施中使用的执行核安全功能的设备，包括核安全机械设备和核安全电气设备。  
民用核安全设备目录由国务院核安全监管部门商国务院有关部门制定并发布。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Regulations, "civil nuclear safety equipment" means equipment used in civil nuclear installations that performs nuclear safety functions, including nuclear safety mechanical equipment and nuclear safety electrical equipment.  
The catalogue of civil nuclear safety equipment shall be formulated and published by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council after consultation with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动适用本条例。  
民用核安全设备运离民用核设施现场进行的维修活动，适用民用核安全设备制造活动的有关规定。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.  
Repair activities carried out after civil nuclear safety equipment is removed from the civil nuclear installation site shall be governed by the relevant provisions on the manufacture of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院核安全监管部门对民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动实施监督管理。  
国务院核行业主管部门和其他有关部门依照本条例和国务院规定的职责分工负责有关工作。

**Article 4** The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall exercise supervision and administration over the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.  
The competent nuclear industry department of the State Council and other relevant departments shall be responsible for relevant work in accordance with these Regulations and the division of duties prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位，应当建立健全责任制度，加强质量管理，并对其所从事的民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动承担全面责任。  
民用核设施营运单位，应当对在役的民用核安全设备进行检查、试验、检验和维修，并对民用核安全设备的使用和运行安全承担全面责任。

**Article 5** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall establish and improve a responsibility system, strengthen quality management, and bear full responsibility for the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities they carry out.  
Operators of civil nuclear installations shall inspect, test, examine and repair in-service civil nuclear safety equipment, and bear full responsibility for the use and operational safety of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动应当符合国家有关产业政策。  
国家鼓励民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验的科学技术研究，提高安全水平。

**Article 6** The design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment shall comply with the relevant industrial policies of the State.  
The State encourages scientific and technological research on the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment to improve the level of safety.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向国务院核安全监管部门举报。国务院核安全监管部门接到举报，应当及时调查处理，并为举报人保密。

**Article 7** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations. Upon receiving a report, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall promptly investigate and deal with it, and shall keep the reporter confidential.

### 第二章　标准

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　民用核安全设备标准是从事民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的技术依据。

**Article 8** Civil nuclear safety equipment standards are the technical basis for carrying out design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家建立健全民用核安全设备标准体系。制定民用核安全设备标准，应当充分考虑民用核安全设备的技术发展和使用要求，结合我国的工业基础和技术水平，做到安全可靠、技术成熟、经济合理。  
民用核安全设备标准包括国家标准、行业标准和企业标准。

**Article 9** The State shall establish and improve the civil nuclear safety equipment standard system. In formulating civil nuclear safety equipment standards, full consideration shall be given to the technological development and usage requirements of civil nuclear safety equipment, and the industrial base and technology level of the country shall be taken into account, so as to achieve safety, reliability, technological maturity, and economic reasonableness.  
Civil nuclear safety equipment standards include national standards, industry standards and enterprise standards.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　涉及核安全基本原则和技术要求的民用核安全设备国家标准，由国务院核安全监管部门组织拟定，由国务院标准化主管部门和国务院核安全监管部门联合发布；其他的民用核安全设备国家标准，由国务院核行业主管部门组织拟定，经国务院核安全监管部门认可，由国务院标准化主管部门发布。  
民用核安全设备行业标准，由国务院核行业主管部门组织拟定，经国务院核安全监管部门认可，由国务院核行业主管部门发布，并报国务院标准化主管部门备案。  
制定民用核安全设备国家标准和行业标准，应当充分听取有关部门和专家的意见。

**Article 10** National standards for civil nuclear safety equipment concerning the basic principles and technical requirements of nuclear safety shall be formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council, and jointly published by the standardization administration department of the State Council and the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council; other national standards for civil nuclear safety equipment shall be formulated by the competent nuclear industry department of the State Council, recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council, and published by the standardization administration department of the State Council.  
Industry standards for civil nuclear safety equipment shall be formulated by the competent nuclear industry department of the State Council, recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council, published by the competent nuclear industry department of the State Council, and submitted to the standardization administration department of the State Council for recordation.  
The opinions of relevant departments and experts shall be fully heard in the formulation of national standards and industry standards for civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　尚未制定相应国家标准和行业标准的，民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位应当采用经国务院核安全监管部门认可的标准。

**Article 11** Where no relevant national standards or industry standards have been formulated, units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall adopt standards recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第三章　许可

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位应当依照本条例规定申请领取许可证。

**Article 12** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall apply for and obtain a license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请领取民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装或者无损检验许可证的单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)具有法人资格；  
(二)有与拟从事活动相关或者相近的工作业绩，并且满5年以上；  
(三)有与拟从事活动相适应的、经考核合格的专业技术人员，其中从事民用核安全设备焊接和无损检验活动的专业技术人员应当取得相应的资格证书；  
(四)有与拟从事活动相适应的工作场所、设施和装备；  
(五)有健全的管理制度和完善的质量保证体系，以及符合核安全监督管理规定的质量保证大纲。  
申请领取民用核安全设备制造许可证或者安装许可证的单位，还应当制作有代表性的模拟件。

**Article 13** A unit applying for a license for the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having work performance related or similar to the intended activities, for a period of not less than five years;  
(3) having professional technical personnel who are suitable for the intended activities and have passed the required examinations, among whom the professional technical personnel engaged in welding and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall have obtained corresponding qualification certificates;  
(4) having workplaces, facilities and equipment appropriate for the intended activities;  
(5) having a sound management system and a complete quality assurance system, as well as a quality assurance program that complies with the provisions on nuclear safety supervision and administration.  
A unit applying for a license for the manufacture or installation of civil nuclear safety equipment shall also manufacture representative mock-ups.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请领取民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装或者无损检验许可证的单位，应当向国务院核安全监管部门提出书面申请，并提交符合[本条例第十三条](#art-13)规定条件的证明材料。

**Article 14** A unit applying for a license for the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall submit a written application to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council, and provide evidentiary materials proving compliance with the conditions specified in Article 13 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院核安全监管部门应当自受理申请之日起45个工作日内完成审查，并对符合条件的颁发许可证，予以公告；对不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
国务院核安全监管部门在审查过程中，应当组织专家进行技术评审，并征求国务院核行业主管部门和其他有关部门的意见。技术评审所需时间不计算在前款规定的期限内。

**Article 15** The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall complete the review within 45 working days from the date of acceptance of the application, and shall issue a license to those that meet the conditions and make an announcement; for those that do not meet the conditions, it shall give written notice to the applicant unit stating the reasons.  
During the review process, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall organize experts to conduct a technical review, and shall solicit the opinions of the competent nuclear industry department of the State Council and other relevant departments. The time required for the technical review shall not be counted within the period specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验许可证应当载明下列内容：  
(一)单位名称、地址和法定代表人；  
(二)准予从事的活动种类和范围；  
(三)有效期限；  
(四)发证机关、发证日期和证书编号。

**Article 16** A license for the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall state the following:  
(1) the name, address and legal representative of the unit;  
(2) the types and scope of activities permitted;  
(3) the term of validity;  
(4) the issuing authority, date of issuance and certificate number.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位变更单位名称、地址或者法定代表人的，应当自变更工商登记之日起20日内，向国务院核安全监管部门申请办理许可证变更手续。  
民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位变更许可证规定的活动种类或者范围的，应当按照原申请程序向国务院核安全监管部门重新申请领取许可证。

**Article 17** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment changes its name, address or legal representative, it shall, within 20 days from the date of the change in industrial and commercial registration, apply to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council for the modification of the license.  
Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment changes the types or scope of activities specified in the license, it shall re-apply for a license to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the original application procedures.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验许可证有效期为5年。  
许可证有效期届满，民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位需要继续从事相关活动的，应当于许可证有效期届满6个月前，向国务院核安全监管部门提出延续申请。  
国务院核安全监管部门应当在许可证有效期届满前作出是否准予延续的决定；逾期未作决定的，视为准予延续。

**Article 18** The term of validity of a license for the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall be five years.  
If, upon the expiration of the term of validity of the license, a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment needs to continue the relevant activities, it shall submit an application for renewal to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council six months before the expiration of the term of validity of the license.  
The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall make a decision on whether to approve the renewal before the expiration of the term of validity of the license; if no decision is made within the prescribed time, the renewal shall be deemed to have been granted.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　禁止无许可证擅自从事或者不按照许可证规定的活动种类和范围从事民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动。  
禁止委托未取得相应许可证的单位进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动。  
禁止伪造、变造、转让许可证。

**Article 19** It shall be prohibited to engage in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment without a license or not in accordance with the types and scope of activities specified in the license.  
It shall be prohibited to entrust a unit that has not obtained the corresponding license to carry out the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment.  
It shall be prohibited to forge, alter or transfer a license.

### 第四章　设计、制造、安装和无损检验

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位，应当提高核安全意识，建立完善的质量保证体系，确保民用核安全设备的质量和可靠性。  
民用核设施营运单位，应当对民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动进行质量管理和过程控制，做好监造和验收工作。

**Article 20** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall enhance nuclear safety awareness, establish a complete quality assurance system, and ensure the quality and reliability of civil nuclear safety equipment.  
Operators of civil nuclear installations shall carry out quality management and process control over the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment, and perform supervision over manufacturing and acceptance inspection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位，应当根据其质量保证大纲和民用核设施营运单位的要求，在民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动开始前编制项目质量保证分大纲，并经民用核设施营运单位审查同意。

**Article 21** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall, based on their quality assurance program and the requirements of the operator of the civil nuclear installation, prepare a project-specific quality assurance sub-program before commencing the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment, and shall have it reviewed and approved by the operator of the civil nuclear installation.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　民用核安全设备设计单位，应当在设计活动开始30日前，将下列文件报国务院核安全监管部门备案：  
(一)项目设计质量保证分大纲和程序清单；  
(二)设计内容和设计进度计划；  
(三)设计遵循的标准和规范目录清单，设计中使用的计算机软件清单；  
(四)设计验证活动清单。

**Article 22** A civil nuclear safety equipment design unit shall, 30 days before the commencement of the design activities, submit the following documents to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) the project design quality assurance sub-program and a list of procedures;  
(2) the design content and design schedule;  
(3) a list of standards and specifications to be followed in the design, and a list of computer software used in the design;  
(4) a list of design verification activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　民用核安全设备制造、安装单位，应当在制造、安装活动开始30日前，将下列文件报国务院核安全监管部门备案：  
(一)项目制造、安装质量保证分大纲和程序清单；  
(二)制造、安装技术规格书；  
(三)分包项目清单；  
(四)制造、安装质量计划。

**Article 23** A civil nuclear safety equipment manufacture or installation unit shall, 30 days before the commencement of the manufacture or installation activities, submit the following documents to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) the project manufacture or installation quality assurance sub-program and a list of procedures;  
(2) the manufacture or installation technical specifications;  
(3) a list of subcontracted items;  
(4) the manufacture or installation quality plan.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位，不得将国务院核安全监管部门确定的关键工艺环节分包给其他单位。

**Article 24** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall not subcontract the key process steps identified by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council to other units.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　民用核安全设备制造、安装、无损检验单位和民用核设施营运单位，应当聘用取得民用核安全设备焊工、焊接操作工和无损检验人员资格证书的人员进行民用核安全设备焊接和无损检验活动。  
民用核安全设备焊工、焊接操作工和无损检验人员由国务院核安全监管部门核准颁发资格证书。  
民用核安全设备焊工、焊接操作工和无损检验人员在民用核安全设备焊接和无损检验活动中，应当严格遵守操作规程。

**Article 25** Units engaged in the manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment and operators of civil nuclear installations shall employ personnel who have obtained qualification certificates for welders, welding operators or non-destructive testing personnel of civil nuclear safety equipment to carry out welding and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.  
The qualification certificates for welders, welding operators and non-destructive testing personnel of civil nuclear safety equipment shall be approved and issued by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council.  
Welders, welding operators and non-destructive testing personnel of civil nuclear safety equipment shall strictly observe the operating procedures in the welding and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　民用核安全设备无损检验单位应当客观、准确地出具无损检验结果报告。无损检验结果报告经取得相应资格证书的无损检验人员签字方为有效。  
民用核安全设备无损检验单位和无损检验人员对无损检验结果报告负责。

**Article 26** A civil nuclear safety equipment non-destructive testing unit shall issue non-destructive testing result reports objectively and accurately. A non-destructive testing result report shall be valid only after being signed by a non-destructive testing person who has obtained the corresponding qualification certificate.  
The civil nuclear safety equipment non-destructive testing unit and the non-destructive testing personnel shall be responsible for the non-destructive testing result reports.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　民用核安全设备设计单位应当对其设计进行设计验证。设计验证由未参与原设计的专业人员进行。  
设计验证可以采用设计评审、鉴定试验或者不同于设计中使用的计算方法的其他计算方法等形式。

**Article 27** A civil nuclear safety equipment design unit shall perform design verification on its design. The design verification shall be carried out by professionals who did not participate in the original design.  
Design verification may take the form of design review, qualification testing, or other calculation methods different from those used in the design.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　民用核安全设备制造、安装单位应当对民用核安全设备的制造、安装质量进行检验。未经检验或者经检验不合格的，不得交付验收。

**Article 28** A civil nuclear safety equipment manufacture or installation unit shall inspect the quality of the manufacture or installation of civil nuclear safety equipment. No equipment that has not been inspected or has been found unqualified upon inspection shall be delivered for acceptance.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　民用核设施营运单位应当对民用核安全设备质量进行验收。有下列情形之一的，不得验收通过：  
(一)不能按照质量保证要求证明质量受控的；  
(二)出现重大质量问题未处理完毕的。

**Article 29** The operator of a civil nuclear installation shall accept the quality of civil nuclear safety equipment. In any of the following circumstances, the acceptance inspection shall not be passed:  
(1) the quality cannot be demonstrated to be under control in accordance with the quality assurance requirements;  
(2) major quality problems have occurred and have not been completely resolved.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位，应当对本单位所从事的民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动进行年度评估，并于每年4月1日前向国务院核安全监管部门提交上一年度的评估报告。  
评估报告应当包括本单位工作场所、设施、装备和人员等变动情况，质量保证体系实施情况，重大质量问题处理情况以及国务院核安全监管部门和民用核设施营运单位提出的整改要求落实情况等内容。  
民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位对本单位在民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动中出现的重大质量问题，应当立即采取处理措施，并向国务院核安全监管部门报告。

**Article 30** Units engaged in the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall conduct an annual evaluation of the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities they carry out, and shall submit an evaluation report for the preceding year to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council before April 1 each year.  
The evaluation report shall include the changes in the unit's workplaces, facilities, equipment, personnel, etc., the implementation of the quality assurance system, the handling of major quality problems, and the implementation of corrective action requirements raised by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council and the operator of the civil nuclear installation.  
Where a major quality problem occurs in the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities carried out by a unit, the unit shall immediately take corrective measures and report to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council.

### 第五章　进出口

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　为中华人民共和国境内民用核设施进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的境外单位，应当具备下列条件：  
(一)遵守中华人民共和国的法律、行政法规和核安全监督管理规定；  
(二)已取得所在国核安全监管部门规定的相应资质；  
(三)使用的民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验技术是成熟的或者经过验证的；  
(四)采用中华人民共和国的民用核安全设备国家标准、行业标准或者国务院核安全监管部门认可的标准。

**Article 31** An overseas unit that carries out civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities for a civil nuclear installation within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) complying with the laws, administrative regulations and nuclear safety supervision and management provisions of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) having obtained the corresponding qualifications prescribed by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the country where it is located;  
(3) the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing technology used being mature or validated;  
(4) adopting the national standards or industry standards of the People's Republic of China for civil nuclear safety equipment, or the standards recognized by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　为中华人民共和国境内民用核设施进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的境外单位，应当事先到国务院核安全监管部门办理注册登记手续。国务院核安全监管部门应当将境外单位注册登记情况抄送国务院核行业主管部门和其他有关部门。  
注册登记的具体办法由国务院核安全监管部门制定。

**Article 32** An overseas unit that carries out civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities for a civil nuclear installation within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall, in advance, go through the registration procedures with the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council. The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall send a copy of the registration status of the overseas unit to the competent nuclear industry department of the State Council and other relevant departments.  
The specific measures for registration shall be formulated by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院核安全监管部门及其所属的检验机构应当依法对进口的民用核安全设备进行安全检验。  
进口的民用核安全设备在安全检验合格后，由海关进行商品检验。

**Article 33** The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council and its subordinate inspection bodies shall, according to law, conduct safety inspection of imported civil nuclear safety equipment.  
After an imported civil nuclear safety equipment passes the safety inspection, the customs shall conduct commodity inspection.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院核安全监管部门根据需要，可以对境外单位为中华人民共和国境内民用核设施进行的民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动实施核安全监督检查。

**Article 34** The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council may, as necessary, conduct nuclear safety supervision and inspection over the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities carried out by an overseas unit for a civil nuclear installation within the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　民用核设施营运单位应当在对外贸易合同中约定有关民用核安全设备监造、装运前检验和监装等方面的要求。

**Article 35** The operator of a civil nuclear installation shall stipulate in foreign trade contracts the requirements for supervision over manufacturing, pre-shipment inspection, and supervision over loading of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　民用核安全设备的出口管理依照有关法律、行政法规的规定执行。

**Article 36** The export administration of civil nuclear safety equipment shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院核安全监管部门及其派出机构，依照本条例规定对民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动进行监督检查。监督检查分为例行检查和非例行检查。

**Article 37** The nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council and its local offices shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, conduct supervision and inspection over the design, manufacture, installation and non-destructive testing activities of civil nuclear safety equipment. The supervision and inspection shall be divided into routine inspection and non-routine inspection.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院核安全监管部门及其派出机构在进行监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)向被检查单位的法定代表人和其他有关人员调查、了解情况；  
(二)进入被检查单位进行现场调查或者核查；  
(三)查阅、复制相关文件、记录以及其他有关资料；  
(四)要求被检查单位提交有关情况说明或者后续处理报告；  
(五)对有证据表明可能存在重大质量问题的民用核安全设备或者其主要部件，予以暂时封存。  
被检查单位应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供必要资料，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 38** When conducting supervision and inspection, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council and its local offices shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) investigating and learning about the situation from the legal representative and other relevant persons of the unit being inspected;  
(2) entering the unit being inspected to conduct on-site investigation or verification;  
(3) consulting and copying relevant documents, records and other relevant materials;  
(4) requiring the unit being inspected to submit explanations of the situation or follow-up treatment reports;  
(5) temporarily sealing up civil nuclear safety equipment or major components thereof where there is evidence indicating possible major quality problems.  
The unit being inspected shall cooperate, truthfully reflect the situation, provide necessary materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院核安全监管部门及其派出机构在进行监督检查时，应当对检查的内容、发现的问题以及处理情况作出记录，并由监督检查人员和被检查单位的有关负责人签字确认。被检查单位的有关负责人拒绝签字的，监督检查人员应当将有关情况记录在案。

**Article 39** When conducting supervision and inspection, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council and its local offices shall make records of the contents of the inspection, the problems discovered, and the handling situation, and shall have them signed for confirmation by the supervision and inspection personnel and the relevant responsible persons of the unit being inspected. If the relevant responsible person of the unit being inspected refuses to sign, the supervision and inspection personnel shall record the relevant situation in the file.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　民用核安全设备监督检查人员在进行监督检查时，应当出示证件，并为被检查单位保守技术秘密和业务秘密。  
民用核安全设备监督检查人员不得滥用职权侵犯企业的合法权益，或者利用职务上的便利索取、收受财物。  
民用核安全设备监督检查人员不得从事或者参与民用核安全设备经营活动。

**Article 40** Civil nuclear safety equipment supervision and inspection personnel shall, when conducting supervision and inspection, present their credentials and keep confidential the technical secrets and business secrets of the unit being inspected.  
Civil nuclear safety equipment supervision and inspection personnel shall not abuse their powers to infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of enterprises, or take advantage of their positions to extort or accept property.  
Civil nuclear safety equipment supervision and inspection personnel shall not engage in or participate in the business activities of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院核安全监管部门发现民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位有不符合发证条件的情形的，应当责令其限期整改。

**Article 41** Where the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council discovers that a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment is in a situation that does not meet the licensing conditions, it shall order the unit to make corrections within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院核行业主管部门应当加强对本行业民用核设施营运单位的管理，督促本行业民用核设施营运单位遵守法律、行政法规和核安全监督管理规定。

**Article 42** The competent nuclear industry department of the State Council shall strengthen the management of operators of civil nuclear installations in its industry, and urge operators of civil nuclear installations in its industry to comply with laws, administrative regulations and nuclear safety supervision and management provisions.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院核安全监管部门及其民用核安全设备监督检查人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)不依照本条例规定颁发许可证的；  
(二)发现违反本条例规定的行为不予查处，或者接到举报后不依法处理的；  
(三)滥用职权侵犯企业的合法权益，或者利用职务上的便利索取、收受财物的；  
(四)从事或者参与民用核安全设备经营活动的；  
(五)在民用核安全设备监督管理工作中有其他违法行为的。

**Article 43** Where the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council or its civil nuclear safety equipment supervision and inspection personnel commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if such persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to issue a license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) failing to investigate and deal with acts violating the provisions of these Regulations when discovered, or failing to handle reports received according to law;  
(3) abusing powers to infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of enterprises, or taking advantage of their positions to extort or accept property;  
(4) engaging in or participating in the business activities of civil nuclear safety equipment;  
(5) committing other illegal acts in the supervision and administration of civil nuclear safety equipment.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　无许可证擅自从事民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 44** Whoever, without a license, engages in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment without authorization shall be ordered by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council to cease the illegal act, and shall be fined not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位不按照许可证规定的活动种类和范围从事民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 45** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment carries out such activities not in accordance with the types and scope of activities specified in the license, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to cease the illegal act, make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be suspended or revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位变更单位名称、地址或者法定代表人，未依法办理许可证变更手续的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证。

**Article 46** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment changes its name, address or legal representative but fails to undergo the formalities for modification of the license according to law, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　单位伪造、变造、转让许可证的，由国务院核安全监管部门收缴伪造、变造的许可证或者吊销许可证，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法予以治安处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where a unit forges, alters or transfers a license, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall confiscate the forged or altered license or revoke the license, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位未按照民用核安全设备标准进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，禁止使用相关设计、设备，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment fails to carry out the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing in accordance with the civil nuclear safety equipment standards, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to cease the illegal act, make corrections within a specified time limit, prohibit the use of the relevant design or equipment, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be suspended or revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)委托未取得相应许可证的单位进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的；  
(二)聘用未取得相应资格证书的人员进行民用核安全设备焊接和无损检验活动的；  
(三)将国务院核安全监管部门确定的关键工艺环节分包给其他单位的。

**Article 49** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to cease the illegal act, make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be suspended or revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000:  
(1) entrusting a unit that has not obtained the corresponding license to carry out the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment;  
(2) employing persons who have not obtained corresponding qualification certificates to carry out welding and non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment;  
(3) subcontracting the key process steps identified by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council to other units.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位对本单位在民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动中出现的重大质量问题，未按照规定采取处理措施并向国务院核安全监管部门报告的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动，限期改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，暂扣或者吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment fails to take corrective measures and report to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions with respect to a major quality problem occurring in its civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to cease the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities, make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be suspended or revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令停止民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动，限期改正；逾期不改正的，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款，暂扣或者吊销许可证：  
(一)未按照规定编制项目质量保证分大纲并经民用核设施营运单位审查同意的；  
(二)在民用核安全设备设计、制造和安装活动开始前，未按照规定将有关文件报国务院核安全监管部门备案的；  
(三)未按照规定进行年度评估并向国务院核安全监管部门提交评估报告的。

**Article 51** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to cease the civil nuclear safety equipment design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing activities and make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be fined not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000, and the license shall be suspended or revoked:  
(1) failing to prepare a project-specific quality assurance sub-program and have it reviewed and approved by the operator of the civil nuclear installation in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to submit relevant documents to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council for recordation before the commencement of the design, manufacture or installation activities of civil nuclear safety equipment in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to conduct an annual evaluation and submit an evaluation report to the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民用核安全设备无损检验单位出具虚假无损检验结果报告的，由国务院核安全监管部门处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，吊销许可证；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where a civil nuclear safety equipment non-destructive testing unit issues a false non-destructive testing result report, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000, and revoke the license; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民用核安全设备焊工、焊接操作工违反操作规程导致严重焊接质量问题的，由国务院核安全监管部门吊销其资格证书。

**Article 53** Where a welder or welding operator of civil nuclear safety equipment violates the operating procedures and causes serious welding quality problems, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall revoke his or her qualification certificate.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　民用核安全设备无损检验人员违反操作规程导致无损检验结果报告严重错误的，由国务院核安全监管部门吊销其资格证书。

**Article 54** Where a non-destructive testing person of civil nuclear safety equipment violates the operating procedures and causes serious errors in the non-destructive testing result report, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall revoke his or her qualification certificate.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　民用核安全设备设计单位未按照规定进行设计验证，或者民用核安全设备制造、安装单位未按照规定进行质量检验以及经检验不合格即交付验收的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；逾期不改正的，吊销许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款。

**Article 55** Where a civil nuclear safety equipment design unit fails to perform design verification in accordance with the provisions, or a civil nuclear safety equipment manufacture or installation unit fails to conduct quality inspection in accordance with the provisions or delivers equipment for acceptance despite failing the quality inspection, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the license shall be revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　民用核设施营运单位有下列行为之一的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正，处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，吊销其核设施建造许可证或者核设施运行许可证，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处2万元以上10万元以下的罚款：  
(一)委托未取得相应许可证的单位进行民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验活动的；  
(二)对不能按照质量保证要求证明质量受控，或者出现重大质量问题未处理完毕的民用核安全设备予以验收通过的。

**Article 56** Where an operator of a civil nuclear installation commits any of the following acts, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall order it to make corrections within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, its nuclear installation construction license or nuclear installation operation license shall be revoked, and the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 100,000:  
(1) entrusting a unit that has not obtained the corresponding license to carry out the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment;  
(2) passing the acceptance inspection of civil nuclear safety equipment that cannot demonstrate that its quality is under control in accordance with the quality assurance requirements, or for which major quality problems have not been completely resolved.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装和无损检验单位被责令限期整改，逾期不整改或者经整改仍不符合发证条件的，由国务院核安全监管部门暂扣或者吊销许可证。

**Article 57** Where a unit engaged in the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment is ordered to make corrections within a specified time limit, but fails to do so within the time limit or still does not meet the licensing conditions after corrections, the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council shall suspend or revoke its license.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　拒绝或者阻碍国务院核安全监管部门及其派出机构监督检查的，由国务院核安全监管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，暂扣或者吊销许可证。

**Article 58** Whoever refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection conducted by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council or its local offices shall be ordered by the nuclear safety regulatory authority of the State Council to make corrections within a specified time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit or practices fraud during the supervision and inspection, the license shall be suspended or revoked.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本条例规定，被依法吊销许可证的单位，自吊销许可证之日起1年内不得重新申请领取许可证。

**Article 59** A unit whose license has been revoked according to law for violating the provisions of these Regulations shall not re-apply for a license within one year from the date of revocation.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　申请领取民用核安全设备设计、制造、安装或者无损检验许可证的单位，应当按照国家有关规定缴纳技术评审的费用。

**Article 60** A unit applying for a license for the design, manufacture, installation or non-destructive testing of civil nuclear safety equipment shall pay the fees for technical review in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)核安全机械设备，包括执行核安全功能的压力容器、钢制安全壳(钢衬里)、储罐、热交换器、泵、风机和压缩机、阀门、闸门、管道(含热交换器传热管)和管配件、膨胀节、波纹管、法兰、堆内构件、控制棒驱动机构、支承件、机械贯穿件以及上述设备的铸锻件等。  
(二)核安全电气设备，包括执行核安全功能的传感器(包括探测器和变送器)、电缆、机柜(包括机箱和机架)、控制台屏、显示仪表、应急柴油发电机组、蓄电池(组)、电动机、阀门驱动装置、电气贯穿件等。

**Article 61** For the purpose of these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Nuclear safety mechanical equipment" includes pressure vessels, steel containment vessels (steel liners), storage tanks, heat exchangers, pumps, fans and compressors, valves, gates, pipes (including heat exchanger tubes) and pipe fittings, expansion joints, bellows, flanges, reactor internals, control rod drive mechanisms, support members, mechanical penetrations, and castings and forgings for the aforesaid equipment, which perform nuclear safety functions.  
(2) "Nuclear safety electrical equipment" includes sensors (including detectors and transmitters), cables, cabinets (including chassis and racks), control consoles and panels, display instruments, emergency diesel generator sets, batteries (banks), electric motors, valve actuators, electrical penetrations, etc., which perform nuclear safety functions.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本条例自2008年1月1日起施行。

**Article 62** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2008.
