# 中华人民共和国核材料管制条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：1987-06-14  
**生效日期**：1987-06-15

> (1987年6月15日国务院发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一章　总则 第一条　为保证核材料的安全与合法利用，防止被盗、破坏、丢失、非法转让和非法使用，保护国家和人民群众的安全，促进核能事业的发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例管制的核材料是： (一)铀—235，含铀—235的材料和制品； (二)铀—233，含铀—233的材料和制品； (三)钚—239，含钚—239的材料和制品； (四)氚，含氚的材料和制品； (五)锂—6，含锂—6的材料和制品； (六)其他需要管制的核材料。 铀矿石及其初级产品，不属于本条例管制范围。已移交给军队的核制品的管制办法由国防部门制定。 第三条　国家对核材料实行许可证制度。 第四条　核材料管制的基本要求是： (一)保证符合国家利益及法律的规定； (二)保证国家和人民群众的安全；

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保证核材料的安全与合法利用，防止被盗、破坏、丢失、非法转让和非法使用，保护国家和人民群众的安全，促进核能事业的发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to ensure the safety and lawful utilization of nuclear materials, to prevent theft, sabotage, loss, illegal transfer and illegal use, to protect the safety of the State and the people, and to promote the development of nuclear energy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例管制的核材料是：  
(一)铀—235，含铀—235的材料和制品；  
(二)铀—233，含铀—233的材料和制品；  
(三)钚—239，含钚—239的材料和制品；  
(四)氚，含氚的材料和制品；  
(五)锂—6，含锂—6的材料和制品；  
(六)其他需要管制的核材料。  
铀矿石及其初级产品，不属于本条例管制范围。已移交给军队的核制品的管制办法由国防部门制定。

**Article 2** The nuclear materials subject to control under these Regulations are:  
(1) Uranium-235, materials and products containing Uranium-235;  
(2) Uranium-233, materials and products containing Uranium-233;  
(3) Plutonium-239, materials and products containing Plutonium-239;  
(4) Tritium, materials and products containing Tritium;  
(5) Lithium-6, materials and products containing Lithium-6;  
(6) Other nuclear materials that require control.  
Uranium ores and their primary products shall not be within the scope of control under these Regulations. The control measures for nuclear products that have been transferred to the military shall be formulated by the national defense department.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对核材料实行许可证制度。

**Article 3** The State shall implement a licensing system for nuclear materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　核材料管制的基本要求是：  
(一)保证符合国家利益及法律的规定；  
(二)保证国家和人民群众的安全；  
(三)保证国家对核材料的控制，在必要时国家可以征收所有核材料。

**Article 4** The basic requirements for nuclear materials control are:  
(1) to ensure compliance with the provisions of national interests and laws;  
(2) to ensure the safety of the State and the people;  
(3) to ensure the State's control over nuclear materials, and the State may expropriate all nuclear materials when necessary.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　一切持有、使用、生产、储存、运输和处置第二条所列核材料的部门和单位必须遵守本条例。

**Article 5** All departments and entities that possess, use, produce, store, transport and dispose of the nuclear materials listed in Article 2 must comply with these Regulations.

### 第二章　监督管理职责

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家核安全局负责民用核材料的安全监督，在核材料管制方面的主要职责是：  
(一)拟订核材料管制法规；  
(二)监督民用核材料管制法规的实施；  
(三)核准核材料许可证。

**Article 6** The National Nuclear Safety Administration shall be responsible for the safety supervision of civil nuclear materials, and its main responsibilities in nuclear materials control are:  
(1) to formulate regulations on nuclear materials control;  
(2) to supervise the implementation of regulations on civil nuclear materials control;  
(3) to approve nuclear materials licenses.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　核工业部负责管理全国的核材料，在核材料管制方面的主要职责是：  
(一)负责实施全国核材料管制；  
(二)负责审查、颁发核材料许可证；  
(三)拟订核材料管制规章制度；  
(四)负责全国核材料帐务系统的建立和检查。

**Article 7** The Ministry of Nuclear Industry shall be responsible for managing nuclear materials throughout the country, and its main responsibilities in nuclear materials control are:  
(1) to implement national nuclear materials control;  
(2) to examine and issue nuclear materials licenses;  
(3) to formulate rules and regulations on nuclear materials control;  
(4) to establish and inspect the national nuclear materials accounting system.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国防科学技术工业委员会负责涉及国防的核材料的安全监督和核准核材料许可证。

**Article 8** The Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense shall be responsible for the safety supervision of nuclear materials involving national defense and for approving nuclear materials licenses.

### 第三章　核材料管制办法

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　持有核材料数量达到下列限额的单位，必须申请核材料许可证：  
(一)累计的调入量或生产量大于或等于0.01有效公斤的铀、含铀材料和制品(以铀的有效公斤量计)；  
(二)任何量的钚—239、含钚—239的材料和制品；  
(三)累计的调入量或生产量大于或等于3.7×1013贝可(1000居里)的氚、含氚材料和制品(以氚量计)；  
(四)累计的调入量或生产量大于或等于1公斤的浓缩锂、含浓缩锂材料和制品(以锂—6量计)。  
累计调入或生产核材料数量小于上列限额者，可免予办理许可证，但必须向核工业部办理核材料登记手续。  
对不致危害国家和人民群众安全的少量的核材料制品可免予登记，其品种和数量限额由核工业部规定。

**Article 9** Any entity that holds nuclear materials reaching the following limits must apply for a nuclear materials license:  
(1) cumulative receipt or production of uranium, uranium-containing materials and products (calculated by effective kilograms of uranium) greater than or equal to 0.01 effective kilogram;  
(2) any quantity of Plutonium-239, materials and products containing Plutonium-239;  
(3) cumulative receipt or production of tritium, tritium-containing materials and products (calculated by amount of tritium) greater than or equal to 3.7×10^13 Becquerels (1000 Curies);  
(4) cumulative receipt or production of enriched lithium, enriched lithium-containing materials and products (calculated by amount of Lithium-6) greater than or equal to 1 kilogram.  
Where the cumulative receipt or production of nuclear materials is less than the above limits, the entity may be exempted from applying for a license, but must register the nuclear materials with the Ministry of Nuclear Industry.  
Small quantities of nuclear material products that would not endanger the safety of the State and the people may be exempted from registration, and their types and quantity limits shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Nuclear Industry.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　核材料许可证的申请程序是：  
(一)核材料许可证申请单位向核工业部提交许可证申请书以及申请单位的上级领导部门的审核批准文件；  
(二)核工业部审查并报国家核安全局或国防科学技术工业委员会核准；  
(三)核工业部颁发核材料许可证。

**Article 10** The application procedure for a nuclear materials license is:  
(1) the unit applying for a nuclear materials license shall submit a license application form and the examination and approval documents from the superior leading department of the applying unit to the Ministry of Nuclear Industry;  
(2) the Ministry of Nuclear Industry shall examine and submit to the National Nuclear Safety Administration or the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense for approval;  
(3) the Ministry of Nuclear Industry shall issue the nuclear materials license.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　核材料许可证持有单位必须建立专职机构或指定专人负责保管核材料，严格交接手续，建立帐目与报告制度，保证帐物相符。  
许可证持有单位必须建立核材料衡算制度和分析测量系统，应用批准的分析测量方法和标准，达到规定的衡算误差要求，保持核材料收支平衡。

**Article 11** The unit holding a nuclear materials license must establish a special organization or designate specific personnel to be responsible for the safekeeping of nuclear materials, strictly implement handover procedures, establish an accounting and reporting system, and ensure that accounts correspond to physical inventory.  
The license-holding unit must establish a nuclear materials accounting system and an analytical measurement system, apply approved analytical measurement methods and standards, meet prescribed accounting error requirements, and maintain a balance of nuclear materials receipts and expenditures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　许可证持有单位应当在当地公安部门的指导下，对生产、使用、贮存和处置核材料的场所，建立严格的安全保卫制度，采用可靠的安全防范措施，严防盗窃、破坏、火灾等事故的发生。

**Article 12** The license-holding unit shall, under the guidance of the local public security organ, establish strict security protection systems for the sites where nuclear materials are produced, used, stored and disposed of, and adopt reliable security precautions to strictly prevent the occurrence of theft, sabotage, fire and other incidents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　运输核材料必须遵守国家的有关规定，核材料托运单位负责与有关部门制定运输保卫方案，落实保卫措施。运输部门、公安部门和其他有关部门要密切配合，确保核材料运输途中安全。

**Article 13** The transport of nuclear materials must comply with the relevant State provisions. The consigning unit of nuclear materials shall be responsible for formulating transport security plans together with relevant departments and implementing security measures. The transport department, public security department and other relevant departments shall cooperate closely to ensure the safety of nuclear materials during transport.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　核材料持有单位必须切实做好核材料及其有关文件、资料的安全保密工作。凡涉及国家秘密的文件、资料，要按照国家保密规定，准确划定密级，制定严格的保密制度，防止失密、泄密和窃密。  
对接触核材料及其秘密的人员，应当按照国家有关规定进行审查。

**Article 14** The unit holding nuclear materials must effectively ensure the security and confidentiality of nuclear materials and related documents and materials. Any documents and materials involving State secrets shall, in accordance with State confidentiality provisions, be accurately classified and strict confidentiality systems shall be established to prevent leakage, divulgement and theft of secrets.  
Personnel who have access to nuclear materials and their secrets shall be examined in accordance with the relevant State provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　发现核材料被盗、破坏、丢失、非法转让和非法使用的事件，当事单位必须立即追查原因、追回核材料，并迅速报告其上级领导部门、核工业部、国防科学技术工业委员会和国家核安全局。对核材料被盗、破坏、丢失等事件，必须迅速报告当地公安机关。

**Article 15** Where incidents of theft, sabotage, loss, illegal transfer or illegal use of nuclear materials are discovered, the unit concerned must immediately investigate the cause, recover the nuclear materials, and promptly report to its superior leading department, the Ministry of Nuclear Industry, the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense, and the National Nuclear Safety Administration. For incidents such as theft, sabotage or loss of nuclear materials, the unit must promptly report to the local public security organ.

### 第四章　许可证持有单位及其上级领导部门的责任

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　核材料许可证持有单位的责任是：  
(一)遵守国家的法律和法规；  
(二)对所持有的核材料负全面安全责任，直至核材料安全责任合法转移为止；  
(三)接受管理和监督。

**Article 16** The responsibilities of the unit holding a nuclear materials license are:  
(1) to comply with the laws and regulations of the State;  
(2) to bear full safety responsibility for the nuclear materials it holds until the safety responsibility for the nuclear materials is legally transferred;  
(3) to accept management and supervision.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　核材料许可证持有单位的上级领导部门应当给所属持有单位以必要的支持和督促检查，并承担领导责任。

**Article 17** The superior leading department of the unit holding a nuclear materials license shall provide necessary support and supervision and inspection to the subordinate holding unit, and shall bear leadership responsibility.

### 第五章　奖励和处罚

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对核材料管制工作做出显著成绩的单位、个人，由国家核安全局、国防科学技术工业委员会或核工业部给予表扬和奖励。

**Article 18** Units and individuals that have made significant achievements in nuclear materials control work shall be commended and rewarded by the National Nuclear Safety Administration, the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense, or the Ministry of Nuclear Industry.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　凡违反本条例的规定，有下列行为之一的，国家核安全局可依其情节轻重，给予警告、限期改进、罚款和吊销许可证的处罚，但吊销许可证的处罚需经核工业部同意。  
(一)未经批准或违章从事核材料生产、使用、贮存和处置的；  
(二)不按照规定报告或谎报有关事实和资料的；  
(三)拒绝监督检查的；  
(四)不按照规定管理，造成事故的。

**Article 19** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the National Nuclear Safety Administration may, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances, impose a warning, order rectification within a time limit, impose a fine, or revoke the license; provided that revocation of the license shall require the consent of the Ministry of Nuclear Industry.  
(1) engaging in the production, use, storage or disposal of nuclear materials without approval or in violation of regulations;  
(2) failing to report in accordance with regulations or making false reports concerning relevant facts and data;  
(3) refusing supervision and inspection;  
(4) failing to manage in accordance with regulations, resulting in an accident.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人对行政处罚不服的，可在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。但是，对吊销许可证的决定应当立即执行。对处罚决定不履行逾期又不起诉的，由国家核安全局申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 20** A party that disagrees with an administrative penalty may file a prosecution with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty. However, the decision to revoke a license shall be executed immediately. If the party neither complies with the penalty decision nor files a prosecution within the time limit, the National Nuclear Safety Administration shall apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对于不服从核材料管制、违反规章制度，因而发生重大事故，造成严重后果的，或者盗窃、抢劫、破坏本条例管制的核材料，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** Where a person refuses to submit to nuclear materials control or violates rules and regulations, thereby causing a major accident with serious consequences, or steals, robs or sabotages nuclear materials controlled under these Regulations, which constitutes a crime, the judicial organ shall investigate criminal liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本条例下列用语的含义：  
(一)“浓缩锂”——指锂—6同位素原子百分含量大于天然锂的；  
(二)“铀的有效公斤”——指铀(包括加浓铀、天然铀、贫化铀)按如下方法计算的有效公斤：  
1．对于铀—235同位素原子百分含量不小于1%的铀，以公斤为单位的铀的实际量乘以铀—235同位素原子百分含量的平方。  
2．对于铀—235同位素原子百分含量小于1%，大于0.5%的铀，以公斤为单位的铀的实际重量乘以0.0001。  
3．对于铀—235同位素原子百分含量不大于0.5%的铀，以公斤为单位的铀的实际重量乘以0.00005。  
4．对于铀—233，其有效公斤计算方法与铀—235相同。

**Article 22** For the purposes of these Regulations, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(1) "Enriched lithium" means lithium in which the atomic percentage of the Lithium-6 isotope is greater than that in natural lithium;  
(2) "Effective kilogram of uranium" means the effective kilogram of uranium (including enriched uranium, natural uranium, and depleted uranium) calculated by the following methods:  
1. For uranium with an atomic percentage of Uranium-235 isotope not less than 1%, the actual quantity of uranium in kilograms multiplied by the square of the atomic percentage of Uranium-235 isotope.  
2. For uranium with an atomic percentage of Uranium-235 isotope less than 1% but greater than 0.5%, the actual weight of uranium in kilograms multiplied by 0.0001.  
3. For uranium with an atomic percentage of Uranium-235 isotope not greater than 0.5%, the actual weight of uranium in kilograms multiplied by 0.00005.  
4. For Uranium-233, the method for calculating its effective kilogram is the same as that for Uranium-235.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本条例由国家核安全局负责解释；本条例的实施细则由国家核安全局会同国防科学技术工业委员会、核工业部制定。

**Article 23** The interpretation of these Regulations shall be vested in the National Nuclear Safety Administration; the detailed rules for the implementation of these Regulations shall be formulated by the National Nuclear Safety Administration jointly with the Commission of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense and the Ministry of Nuclear Industry.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 24** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
