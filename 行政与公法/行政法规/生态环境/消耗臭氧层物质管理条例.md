# 消耗臭氧层物质管理条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-12-29  
**生效日期**：2024-03-01

> （2010年4月8日中华人民共和国国务院令第573号公布　根据2018年3月19日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》第一次修订　根据2023年12月29日《国务院关于修改〈消耗臭氧层物质管理条例〉的决定》第二次修订） 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强对消耗臭氧层物质的管理，履行《保护臭氧层维也纳公约》和《关于消耗臭氧层物质的蒙特利尔议定书》规定的义务，保护臭氧层和生态环境，保障人体健康，根据《中华人民共和国大气污染防治法》，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例所称消耗臭氧层物质，是指列入《中国受控消耗臭氧层物质清单》的化学品。 《中国受控消耗臭氧层物质清单》由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整和公布。 第三条　在中华人民共和国境内从事消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售、使用和进出口等活动，适用本条例。 前款所称生产，是指制造消耗臭氧层物质的活动。前款所称使用，是指利用消耗臭氧层物质进行的生产经营等活动，不包括使用含消耗臭氧层物质的产品的活动。 第四条　消耗臭氧层物质的管理工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻党和国家路线方针政策和决策部署。 国务院生态环境主管部门统一负责全国消耗臭氧层物质的监督管理工作。 国务院商务主管部门、海关总署等有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责消耗臭氧层物质的有关监督管理工作。 地方人民政府生态环境主管部门和商务等有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责本行政区域消耗臭氧层物质的有关监督管理工作。 第五条　国家逐步削减并最终淘汰作为制冷剂、发泡剂、灭火剂、溶剂、清洗剂、加工助剂、杀虫剂、气雾剂、膨胀剂等用途的消耗臭氧层物质。 禁止将国家已经淘汰的消耗臭氧层物质用于前款规定的用途。 国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门拟订《中国履行〈关于消耗臭氧层物质的蒙特利尔议定书〉国家方案》（以下简称国家方案），报国务院批准后实施。 第六条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据国家方案和消耗臭氧层物质淘汰进展情况，会同国务院有关部门确定并公布限制或者禁止新建、改建、扩建生产、使用消耗臭氧层物质建设项目的类别，制定并公布限制或者禁止生产、使用、进出口消耗臭氧层物质的名录。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对消耗臭氧层物质的管理，履行《保护臭氧层维也纳公约》和《关于消耗臭氧层物质的蒙特利尔议定书》规定的义务，保护臭氧层和生态环境，保障人体健康，根据《中华人民共和国大气污染防治法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Atmospheric Pollution for the purposes of strengthening the management of ozone-depleting substances, fulfilling the obligations provided for in the Vienna Convention for the Protection of the Ozone Layer and the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer, protecting the ozone layer and the ecological environment, and safeguarding human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例所称消耗臭氧层物质，是指列入《中国受控消耗臭氧层物质清单》的化学品。  
《中国受控消耗臭氧层物质清单》由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整和公布。

**Article 2** For the purposes of these Regulations, "ozone-depleting substances" means the chemicals listed in the Inventory of Controlled Ozone-depleting Substances of China.  
The Inventory of Controlled Ozone-depleting Substances of China shall be formulated, adjusted and published by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在中华人民共和国境内从事消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售、使用和进出口等活动，适用本条例。  
前款所称生产，是指制造消耗臭氧层物质的活动。前款所称使用，是指利用消耗臭氧层物质进行的生产经营等活动，不包括使用含消耗臭氧层物质的产品的活动。

**Article 3** These Regulations shall apply to the production, sale, use, import and export of ozone-depleting substances within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
"Production" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means the activity of manufacturing ozone-depleting substances. "Use" as mentioned in the preceding paragraph means the activities of production and business operations conducted by utilizing ozone-depleting substances, excluding the activity of using products containing ozone-depleting substances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　消耗臭氧层物质的管理工作应当坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻党和国家路线方针政策和决策部署。  
国务院生态环境主管部门统一负责全国消耗臭氧层物质的监督管理工作。  
国务院商务主管部门、海关总署等有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责消耗臭氧层物质的有关监督管理工作。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门和商务等有关部门依照本条例的规定和各自的职责负责本行政区域消耗臭氧层物质的有关监督管理工作。

**Article 4** The management of ozone-depleting substances shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China and implement the Party and the state's lines, principles, policies and decisions.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall be uniformly responsible for the supervision and management of ozone-depleting substances nationwide.  
The competent department of commerce of the State Council, the General Administration of Customs and other relevant departments shall be responsible for the relevant supervision and management of ozone-depleting substances in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties.  
The competent departments of ecological environment, commerce and other relevant departments of local people's governments shall be responsible for the relevant supervision and management of ozone-depleting substances within their respective administrative regions in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家逐步削减并最终淘汰作为制冷剂、发泡剂、灭火剂、溶剂、清洗剂、加工助剂、杀虫剂、气雾剂、膨胀剂等用途的消耗臭氧层物质。  
禁止将国家已经淘汰的消耗臭氧层物质用于前款规定的用途。  
国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门拟订《中国履行〈关于消耗臭氧层物质的蒙特利尔议定书〉国家方案》（以下简称国家方案），报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 5** The state shall gradually reduce and eventually phase out ozone-depleting substances used as refrigerants, foaming agents, fire extinguishing agents, solvents, cleaning agents, processing aids, pesticides, aerosol propellants, expansion agents, and the like.  
It shall be prohibited to use ozone-depleting substances that have already been phased out by the state for the purposes specified in the preceding paragraph.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, draft the National Program for China's Implementation of the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (hereinafter referred to as the National Program) and submit it to the State Council for approval before implementation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据国家方案和消耗臭氧层物质淘汰进展情况，会同国务院有关部门确定并公布限制或者禁止新建、改建、扩建生产、使用消耗臭氧层物质建设项目的类别，制定并公布限制或者禁止生产、使用、进出口消耗臭氧层物质的名录。  
因特殊用途确需生产、使用前款规定禁止生产、使用的消耗臭氧层物质的，按照《关于消耗臭氧层物质的蒙特利尔议定书》有关允许用于特殊用途的规定，由国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门批准。

**Article 6** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in light of the National Program and the progress of the phase-out of ozone-depleting substances, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, determine and publish the categories of construction projects for which the construction, renovation or expansion for the production or use of ozone-depleting substances is restricted or prohibited, and formulate and publish the list of ozone-depleting substances whose production, use, import or export is restricted or prohibited.  
Where it is truly necessary to produce or use ozone-depleting substances whose production or use is prohibited under the preceding paragraph for special purposes, such production or use shall be approved by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer that permit the use for special purposes.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用、进出口实行总量控制和配额管理。国务院生态环境主管部门根据国家方案和消耗臭氧层物质淘汰进展情况，商国务院有关部门确定国家消耗臭氧层物质的年度生产、使用和进出口配额总量，并予以公告。

**Article 7** The state shall implement total quantity control and quota management for the production, use, import and export of ozone-depleting substances. The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in light of the National Program and the progress of the phase-out of ozone-depleting substances, consult the relevant departments of the State Council to determine the total annual production, use, import and export quotas for ozone-depleting substances of the state, and shall announce such quotas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励、支持消耗臭氧层物质替代品和替代技术的科学研究、技术开发和推广应用。  
国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定、调整和公布《中国消耗臭氧层物质替代品推荐名录》。  
开发、生产、使用消耗臭氧层物质替代品，应当符合国家产业政策，并按照国家有关规定享受优惠政策。对在消耗臭氧层物质淘汰工作中做出突出成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 8** The state shall encourage and support scientific research, technological development and the promotion and application of ozone-depleting substance alternatives and alternative technologies.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate, adjust and publish the Recommended List of Ozone-depleting Substance Alternatives of China.  
The development, production and use of ozone-depleting substance alternatives shall comply with the national industrial policies and shall enjoy preferential policies in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state. Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of phasing out ozone-depleting substances shall be rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人对违反本条例规定的行为，有权向生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门举报。接到举报的部门应当及时调查处理，并为举报人保密；经调查情况属实的，对举报人给予奖励。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations. The department that receives the report shall promptly investigate and handle the matter, and shall keep the whistleblower confidential; if the reported matter is verified upon investigation, the whistleblower shall be rewarded.

### 第二章　生产、销售和使用

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位，应当依照本条例的规定申请领取生产或者使用配额许可证。但是，使用单位有下列情形之一的，不需要申请领取使用配额许可证：  
（一）维修单位为了维修制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统使用消耗臭氧层物质的；  
（二）实验室为了实验分析少量使用消耗臭氧层物质的；  
（三）海关为了防止有害生物传入传出使用消耗臭氧层物质实施检疫的；  
（四）国务院生态环境主管部门规定的不需要申请领取使用配额许可证的其他情形。

**Article 10** Units that produce or use ozone-depleting substances shall apply for and obtain a production or use quota license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations. However, a using unit shall not be required to apply for a use quota license under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) A maintenance unit uses ozone-depleting substances for the purpose of repairing refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems;  
(2) A laboratory uses a small amount of ozone-depleting substances for experimental analysis;  
(3) The customs uses ozone-depleting substances to carry out quarantine to prevent the entry or exit of harmful organisms; and  
(4) Other circumstances specified by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council where an application for a use quota license is not required.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位除具备法律、行政法规规定的条件外，还应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有合法生产或者使用相应消耗臭氧层物质的业绩；  
（二）有生产或者使用相应消耗臭氧层物质的场所、设施、设备和专业技术人员；  
（三）有经验收合格的环境保护设施；  
（四）有健全完善的生产经营管理制度。  
将消耗臭氧层物质用于[本条例第六条](#art-6)规定的特殊用途的单位，不适用前款第（一）项的规定。

**Article 11** In addition to the conditions provided for in laws and administrative regulations, units that produce or use ozone-depleting substances shall also meet the following conditions:  
(1) They have a record of legally producing or using the corresponding ozone-depleting substances;  
(2) They have the premises, facilities, equipment and professional technical personnel for producing or using the corresponding ozone-depleting substances;  
(3) They have environmental protection facilities that have passed inspection; and  
(4) They have a sound and complete production and operation management system.  
The provisions of subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to units that use ozone-depleting substances for the special purposes specified in Article 6 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位应当于每年10月31日前向国务院生态环境主管部门书面申请下一年度的生产配额或者使用配额，并提交其符合[本条例第十一条](#art-11)规定条件的证明材料。  
国务院生态环境主管部门根据国家消耗臭氧层物质的年度生产、使用配额总量和申请单位生产、使用相应消耗臭氧层物质的业绩情况，核定申请单位下一年度的生产配额或者使用配额，并于每年12月20日前完成审查，符合条件的，核发下一年度的生产或者使用配额许可证，予以公告，并抄送国务院有关部门和申请单位所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 12** Units that produce or use ozone-depleting substances shall, before October 31 of each year, submit a written application to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for the production quota or use quota for the following year, and submit supporting materials demonstrating that they meet the conditions specified in Article 11 of these Regulations.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, based on the total annual production and use quotas for ozone-depleting substances of the state and the record of the applicant unit in producing or using the corresponding ozone-depleting substances, verify the production quota or use quota for the applicant unit for the following year, and shall complete the review before December 20 of each year. If the applicant meets the conditions, the department shall issue the production or use quota license for the following year, announce it, and send a copy to the relevant departments of the State Council and the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located; if the applicant does not meet the conditions, the department shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产或者使用配额许可证应当载明下列内容：  
（一）生产或者使用单位的名称、地址、法定代表人或者负责人；  
（二）准予生产或者使用的消耗臭氧层物质的品种、用途及其数量；  
（三）有效期限；  
（四）发证机关、发证日期和证书编号。

**Article 13** A production or use quota license for ozone-depleting substances shall specify the following contents:  
(1) The name, address, legal representative or responsible person of the producing or using unit;  
(2) The types, uses and quantities of ozone-depleting substances authorized for production or use;  
(3) The period of validity; and  
(4) The issuing authority, date of issuance and certificate number.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位需要调整其配额的，应当向国务院生态环境主管部门申请办理配额变更手续。  
国务院生态环境主管部门应当依照[本条例第十一条](#art-11)、第十二条规定的条件和依据进行审查，并在受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，符合条件的，对申请单位的配额进行调整，并予以公告；不符合条件的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。

**Article 14** If a unit that produces or uses ozone-depleting substances needs to adjust its quota, it shall apply to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for quota modification procedures.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall conduct a review in accordance with the conditions and bases specified in Articles 11 and 12 of these Regulations, and shall complete the review within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application. If the applicant meets the conditions, the department shall adjust the quota of the applicant unit and announce it; if the applicant does not meet the conditions, the department shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产单位不得超出生产配额许可证规定的品种、数量、期限生产消耗臭氧层物质，不得超出生产配额许可证规定的用途生产、销售消耗臭氧层物质。  
禁止无生产配额许可证生产消耗臭氧层物质。

**Article 15** A unit that produces ozone-depleting substances shall not produce ozone-depleting substances beyond the types, quantities or validity period specified in the production quota license, and shall not produce or sell ozone-depleting substances beyond the uses specified in the production quota license.  
It shall be prohibited to produce ozone-depleting substances without a production quota license.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　依照本条例规定领取使用配额许可证的单位，不得超出使用配额许可证规定的品种、用途、数量、期限使用消耗臭氧层物质。  
除[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的不需要申请领取使用配额许可证的情形外，禁止无使用配额许可证使用消耗臭氧层物质。

**Article 16** A unit that has obtained a use quota license in accordance with these Regulations shall not use ozone-depleting substances beyond the types, uses, quantities or validity period specified in the use quota license.  
Except for the circumstances specified in Article 10 of these Regulations where an application for a use quota license is not required, it shall be prohibited to use ozone-depleting substances without a use quota license.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　下列单位应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定办理备案手续：  
（一）消耗臭氧层物质的销售单位；  
（二）从事含消耗臭氧层物质的制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统的维修、报废处理等经营活动的单位；  
（三）从事消耗臭氧层物质回收、再生利用或者销毁等经营活动的单位；  
（四）国务院生态环境主管部门规定的不需要申请领取使用配额许可证的消耗臭氧层物质的使用单位。  
前款第（一）项、第（二）项、第（四）项规定的单位向所在地设区的市级人民政府生态环境主管部门备案，第（三）项规定的单位向所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 17** The following units shall go through the record-filing formalities in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council:  
(1) Units that sell ozone-depleting substances;  
(2) Units engaged in business activities such as the maintenance and disposal of refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems containing ozone-depleting substances;  
(3) Units engaged in business activities such as the recovery, recycling or destruction of ozone-depleting substances; and  
(4) Units that use ozone-depleting substances and are not required to apply for a use quota license as specified by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.  
The units specified in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (4) of the preceding paragraph shall file records with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government at the city divided into districts level where they are located; the units specified in subparagraph (3) of the preceding paragraph shall file records with the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　除依照本条例规定进出口外，消耗臭氧层物质的购买和销售行为只能在符合本条例规定的消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售和使用单位之间进行。

**Article 18** Except for import and export conducted in accordance with these Regulations, the purchase and sale of ozone-depleting substances shall only be carried out among units that produce, sell or use ozone-depleting substances in compliance with these Regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位，应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定采取必要的措施，防止或者减少消耗臭氧层物质的泄漏和排放。  
从事含消耗臭氧层物质的制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统的维修、报废处理等经营活动的单位，应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定对消耗臭氧层物质进行回收、循环利用或者交由从事消耗臭氧层物质回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位进行无害化处置。  
从事消耗臭氧层物质回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位，以及生产过程中附带产生消耗臭氧层物质的单位，应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定对消耗臭氧层物质进行无害化处置，不得直接排放。

**Article 19** Units that produce or use ozone-depleting substances shall take necessary measures in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council to prevent or reduce the leakage and emission of ozone-depleting substances.  
Units engaged in business activities such as the maintenance and disposal of refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems containing ozone-depleting substances shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council, recover, recycle or hand over ozone-depleting substances to units engaged in business activities such as recovery, recycling or destruction for harmless disposal.  
Units engaged in business activities such as the recovery, recycling or destruction of ozone-depleting substances, as well as units that produce ozone-depleting substances incidentally during production processes, shall carry out harmless disposal of ozone-depleting substances in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council, and shall not discharge them directly.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　从事消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售、使用、回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位，以及从事含消耗臭氧层物质的制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统的维修、报废处理等经营活动的单位，应当完整保存有关生产经营活动的原始资料至少3年，并按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定报送相关数据。  
生产、使用消耗臭氧层物质数量较大，以及生产过程中附带产生消耗臭氧层物质数量较大的单位，应当安装自动监测设备，与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网，并保证监测设备正常运行，确保监测数据的真实性和准确性。具体办法由国务院生态环境主管部门规定。

**Article 20** Units engaged in business activities such as the production, sale, use, recovery, recycling or destruction of ozone-depleting substances, as well as units engaged in business activities such as the maintenance and disposal of refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems containing ozone-depleting substances, shall completely preserve the original materials related to their production and business activities for at least three years, and shall submit relevant data in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.  
Units that produce or use relatively large quantities of ozone-depleting substances, and units that produce relatively large quantities of ozone-depleting substances incidentally during production processes, shall install automatic monitoring equipment, connect it to the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment, and ensure the normal operation of the monitoring equipment to guarantee the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.

### 第三章　进出口

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家对进出口消耗臭氧层物质予以控制，并实行名录管理。国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院商务主管部门、海关总署制定、调整和公布《中国进出口受控消耗臭氧层物质名录》。  
进出口列入《中国进出口受控消耗臭氧层物质名录》的消耗臭氧层物质的单位，应当依照本条例的规定向国家消耗臭氧层物质进出口管理机构申请进出口配额，领取进出口审批单，并提交拟进出口的消耗臭氧层物质的品种、数量、来源、用途等情况的材料。

**Article 21** The state shall exercise control over the import and export of ozone-depleting substances and implement list management. The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of commerce of the State Council and the General Administration of Customs, formulate, adjust and publish the List of Controlled Ozone-depleting Substances for Import and Export of China.  
Units that import or export ozone-depleting substances included in the List of Controlled Ozone-depleting Substances for Import and Export of China shall, in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations, apply to the state import and export administration authority for ozone-depleting substances for import and export quotas, obtain import or export approval sheets, and submit materials on the types, quantities, sources, uses and other information of the ozone-depleting substances to be imported or exported.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家消耗臭氧层物质进出口管理机构应当自受理申请之日起20个工作日内完成审查，作出是否批准的决定。予以批准的，向申请单位核发进出口审批单；未予批准的，书面通知申请单位并说明理由。  
进出口审批单的有效期最长为90日，不得超期或者跨年度使用。

**Article 22** The state import and export administration authority for ozone-depleting substances shall complete the review within 20 working days from the date of accepting the application and make a decision on whether to approve it. If approval is granted, the authority shall issue an import or export approval sheet to the applicant unit; if approval is not granted, the authority shall notify the applicant unit in writing and state the reasons.  
The validity period of an import or export approval sheet shall be up to 90 days, and it shall not be used beyond the validity period or across calendar years.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　取得消耗臭氧层物质进出口审批单的单位，应当按照国务院商务主管部门的规定申请领取进出口许可证，持进出口许可证向海关办理通关手续。列入必须实施检验的进出口商品目录的消耗臭氧层物质，由海关依法实施检验。  
消耗臭氧层物质在中华人民共和国境内的海关特殊监管区域、保税监管场所与境外之间进出的，进出口单位应当依照本条例的规定申请领取进出口审批单、进出口许可证；消耗臭氧层物质在中华人民共和国境内的海关特殊监管区域、保税监管场所与境内其他区域之间进出的，或者在上述海关特殊监管区域、保税监管场所之间进出的，不需要申请领取进出口审批单、进出口许可证。

**Article 23** A unit that has obtained an import or export approval sheet for ozone-depleting substances shall apply for an import or export license in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of commerce of the State Council, and shall go through customs clearance procedures with the customs by presenting the import or export license. For ozone-depleting substances included in the list of imported and exported commodities subject to compulsory inspection, the customs shall carry out inspection according to law.  
Where ozone-depleting substances are shipped between a customs special supervision area or bonded supervision site within the territory of the People's Republic of China and outside the territory, the importing or exporting unit shall apply for an import or export approval sheet and an import or export license in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations; where ozone-depleting substances are shipped between a customs special supervision area or bonded supervision site within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other areas within the territory, or between such customs special supervision areas or bonded supervision sites, no application for an import or export approval sheet or an import or export license shall be required.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门，依照本条例的规定和各自的职责对消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售、使用和进出口等活动进行监督检查。

**Article 24** The competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall conduct supervision and inspection over the production, sale, use, import and export of ozone-depleting substances in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations and their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门进行监督检查，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位提供有关资料；  
（二）要求被检查单位就执行本条例规定的有关情况作出说明；  
（三）进入被检查单位的生产、经营、储存场所进行调查和取证；  
（四）责令被检查单位停止违反本条例规定的行为，履行法定义务；  
（五）扣押、查封违法生产、销售、使用、进出口的消耗臭氧层物质及其生产设备、设施、原料及产品。  
被检查单位应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供必要资料，不得拒绝和阻碍。

**Article 25** When conducting supervision and inspection, the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) Requiring the unit under inspection to provide relevant materials;  
(2) Requiring the unit under inspection to make explanations concerning the implementation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Entering the production, business operation or storage premises of the unit under inspection to conduct investigation and collect evidence;  
(4) Ordering the unit under inspection to cease acts violating the provisions of these Regulations and to perform statutory obligations; and  
(5) Seizing and sealing up ozone-depleting substances illegally produced, sold, used, imported or exported, as well as their production equipment, facilities, raw materials and products.  
The unit under inspection shall cooperate, truthfully report the situation, provide necessary materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门进行监督检查，监督检查人员不得少于2人，并应当出示有效的行政执法证件。  
生态环境主管部门和其他有关部门的工作人员，对监督检查中知悉的商业秘密负有保密义务。

**Article 26** When conducting supervision and inspection, the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall have no fewer than two inspection personnel, who shall present valid law enforcement credentials.  
The staff members of the competent departments of ecological environment and other relevant departments shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets learned during supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当建立健全消耗臭氧层物质的数据信息管理系统，收集、汇总和发布消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用、进出口等数据信息。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当将监督检查中发现的违反本条例规定的行为及处理情况逐级上报至国务院生态环境主管部门。  
县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门应当将监督检查中发现的违反本条例规定的行为及处理情况逐级上报至国务院有关部门，国务院有关部门应当及时抄送国务院生态环境主管部门。

**Article 27** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall establish and improve a data information management system for ozone-depleting substances, and collect, summarize and release data and information on the production, use, import and export of ozone-depleting substances.  
The competent departments of ecological environment of local people's governments shall report levels by level to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council the acts violating the provisions of these Regulations discovered during supervision and inspection and the handling thereof.  
Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall report levels by level to the relevant departments of the State Council the acts violating the provisions of these Regulations discovered during supervision and inspection and the handling thereof, and the relevant departments of the State Council shall promptly send a copy to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门对违反本条例规定的行为不查处的，其上级主管部门有权责令其依法查处或者直接进行查处。

**Article 28** Where the competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government or any other relevant department fails to investigate and handle any act that violates the provisions of these Regulations, the competent department at a higher level shall have the authority to order it to investigate and handle according to law, or to directly investigate and handle the matter.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　负有消耗臭氧层物质监督管理职责的部门及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）违反本条例规定核发消耗臭氧层物质生产、使用配额许可证的；  
（二）违反本条例规定核发消耗臭氧层物质进出口审批单或者进出口许可证的；  
（三）对发现的违反本条例的行为不依法查处的；  
（四）在办理消耗臭氧层物质生产、使用、进出口等行政许可以及实施监督检查的过程中，索取、收受他人财物或者谋取其他利益的；  
（五）有其他徇私舞弊、滥用职权、玩忽职守行为的。

**Article 29** Where a department responsible for the supervision and management of ozone-depleting substances and its staff members commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if such persons constitute a crime, they shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law:  
(1) Issuing a production or use quota license for ozone-depleting substances in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(2) Issuing an import or export approval sheet or an import or export license for ozone-depleting substances in violation of the provisions of these Regulations;  
(3) Failing to investigate and handle discovered acts violating these Regulations according to law;  
(4) Soliciting or accepting property from others or seeking other benefits in the process of handling administrative licenses for the production, use, import or export of ozone-depleting substances or conducting supervision and inspection; or  
(5) Engaging in other acts of engaging in malpractices for personal gain, abusing power or neglecting duties.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　无生产配额许可证生产消耗臭氧层物质的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收用于违法生产消耗臭氧层物质的原料、违法生产的消耗臭氧层物质和违法所得，拆除、销毁用于违法生产消耗臭氧层物质的设备、设施，并处100万元以上500万元以下的罚款。

**Article 30** Where any person produces ozone-depleting substances without a production quota license, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the raw materials used for the illegal production of ozone-depleting substances, the illegally produced ozone-depleting substances and the illegal gains, dismantle and destroy the equipment and facilities used for the illegal production of ozone-depleting substances, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 1,000,000 but not more than RMB 5,000,000.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　依照本条例规定应当申请领取使用配额许可证的单位无使用配额许可证使用消耗臭氧层物质，或者违反本条例规定将已淘汰的消耗臭氧层物质用于制冷剂、发泡剂、灭火剂、溶剂、清洗剂、加工助剂、杀虫剂、气雾剂、膨胀剂等用途的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法使用的消耗臭氧层物质、违法使用消耗臭氧层物质生产的产品和违法所得，并处20万元以上50万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，拆除、销毁用于违法使用消耗臭氧层物质的设备、设施。

**Article 31** Where a unit that is required to apply for a use quota license under these Regulations uses ozone-depleting substances without a use quota license, or in violation of these Regulations uses phased-out ozone-depleting substances as refrigerants, foaming agents, fire extinguishing agents, solvents, cleaning agents, processing aids, pesticides, aerosol propellants, expansion agents, or the like, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally used ozone-depleting substances, the products produced from the illegal use of ozone-depleting substances and the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 200,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall also be imposed, and the equipment and facilities used for the illegal use of ozone-depleting substances shall be dismantled and destroyed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位有下列行为之一的，由所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法生产、使用的消耗臭氧层物质、违法使用消耗臭氧层物质生产的产品和违法所得，并处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，报国务院生态环境主管部门核减其生产、使用配额数量；情节严重的，并处50万元以上100万元以下的罚款，报国务院生态环境主管部门吊销其生产、使用配额许可证：  
（一）超出生产配额许可证规定的品种、数量、期限生产消耗臭氧层物质的；  
（二）超出生产配额许可证规定的用途生产或者销售消耗臭氧层物质的；  
（三）超出使用配额许可证规定的品种、数量、用途、期限使用消耗臭氧层物质的。

**Article 32** Where a unit that produces or uses ozone-depleting substances commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government at the place where the violation occurs shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegally produced or used ozone-depleting substances, the products produced from the illegal use of ozone-depleting substances and the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000, and report to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council to reduce its production or use quota; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 but not more than RMB 1,000,000 shall also be imposed, and the matter shall be reported to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council to revoke its production or use quota license:  
(1) Producing ozone-depleting substances beyond the types, quantities or validity period specified in the production quota license;  
(2) Producing or selling ozone-depleting substances beyond the uses specified in the production quota license; or  
(3) Using ozone-depleting substances beyond the types, quantities, uses or validity period specified in the use quota license.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、销售、使用单位向不符合本条例规定的单位销售或者购买消耗臭氧层物质的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，没收违法销售或者购买的消耗臭氧层物质和违法所得，处以所销售或者购买的消耗臭氧层物质市场总价3倍的罚款；对取得生产、使用配额许可证的单位，报国务院生态环境主管部门核减其生产、使用配额数量。

**Article 33** Where a unit that produces, sells or uses ozone-depleting substances sells or purchases ozone-depleting substances to or from a unit that does not meet the requirements of these Regulations, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification, confiscate the illegally sold or purchased ozone-depleting substances and the illegal gains, and impose a fine equal to three times the total market value of the ozone-depleting substances sold or purchased; for a unit that has obtained a production or use quota license, the matter shall be reported to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council to reduce its production or use quota.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　消耗臭氧层物质的生产、使用单位未按照规定采取必要的措施防止或者减少消耗臭氧层物质的泄漏和排放的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款，报国务院生态环境主管部门核减其生产、使用配额数量。

**Article 34** Where a unit that produces or uses ozone-depleting substances fails to take necessary measures as required to prevent or reduce the leakage and emission of ozone-depleting substances, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000; if the unit refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed, and the matter shall be reported to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council to reduce its production or use quota.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从事含消耗臭氧层物质的制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统的维修、报废处理等经营活动的单位，未按照规定对消耗臭氧层物质进行回收、循环利用或者交由从事消耗臭氧层物质回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位进行无害化处置的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，处5万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产整治或者停业整治。

**Article 35** Where a unit engaged in business activities such as the maintenance and disposal of refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems containing ozone-depleting substances fails to recover, recycle or hand over the ozone-depleting substances to a unit engaged in business activities such as recovery, recycling or destruction for harmless disposal as required, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if the unit refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification or suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　从事消耗臭氧层物质回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位，以及生产过程中附带产生消耗臭氧层物质的单位，未按照规定对消耗臭氧层物质进行无害化处置而直接排放的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产整治或者停业整治。

**Article 36** Where a unit engaged in business activities such as the recovery, recycling or destruction of ozone-depleting substances, or a unit that produces ozone-depleting substances incidentally during production processes, fails to carry out harmless disposal of ozone-depleting substances as required and directly discharges them, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000; if the unit refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification or suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　从事消耗臭氧层物质生产、销售、使用、进出口、回收、再生利用、销毁等经营活动的单位，以及从事含消耗臭氧层物质的制冷设备、制冷系统或者灭火系统的维修、报废处理等经营活动的单位有下列行为之一的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，处5000元以上2万元以下的罚款：  
（一）依照本条例规定应当向生态环境主管部门备案而未备案的；  
（二）未按照规定完整保存有关生产经营活动的原始资料的；  
（三）未按时申报或者谎报、瞒报有关经营活动的数据资料的；  
（四）未按照监督检查人员的要求提供必要的资料的。

**Article 37** Where a unit engaged in business activities such as the production, sale, use, import and export, recovery, recycling or destruction of ozone-depleting substances, or a unit engaged in business activities such as the maintenance and disposal of refrigeration equipment, refrigeration systems or fire extinguishing systems containing ozone-depleting substances, commits any of the following acts, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 20,000:  
(1) Failing to file records with the competent department of ecological environment as required under these Regulations;  
(2) Failing to completely preserve the original materials related to production and business activities as required;  
(3) Failing to declare on time, or making false declarations or concealing data and materials related to business activities; or  
(4) Failing to provide necessary materials as required by the inspection personnel.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　生产、使用消耗臭氧层物质数量较大，以及生产过程中附带产生消耗臭氧层物质数量较大的单位，未按照规定安装自动监测设备并与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网，或者未保证监测设备正常运行导致监测数据不真实、不准确的，由所在地生态环境主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产整治或者停业整治。

**Article 38** Where a unit that produces or uses relatively large quantities of ozone-depleting substances, or a unit that produces relatively large quantities of ozone-depleting substances incidentally during production processes, fails to install automatic monitoring equipment and connect it to the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment as required, or fails to ensure the normal operation of the monitoring equipment, resulting in untrue or inaccurate monitoring data, the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if the unit refuses to rectify, it shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification or suspend business for rectification.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　进出口单位无进出口许可证或者超出进出口许可证的规定进出口消耗臭氧层物质的，由海关依照有关法律、行政法规的规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
以欺骗、贿赂等不正当手段取得消耗臭氧层物质进出口配额、进出口审批单、进出口许可证的，由国家消耗臭氧层物质进出口管理机构、国务院商务主管部门依据职责撤销其进出口配额、进出口审批单、进出口许可证，3年内不得再次申请，并由所在地生态环境主管部门处10万元以上50万元以下的罚款。

**Article 39** Where an importing or exporting unit imports or exports ozone-depleting substances without an import or export license, or beyond the provisions of the import or export license, the customs shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where any person obtains an import or export quota, import or export approval sheet or import or export license for ozone-depleting substances by fraudulent, bribery or other illegitimate means, the state import and export administration authority for ozone-depleting substances or the competent department of commerce of the State Council shall, within its functions and duties, revoke the import or export quota, import or export approval sheet or import or export license, and the person shall not apply again within three years, and the competent department of ecological environment at the place where the violation occurs shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 500,000.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　拒绝、阻碍生态环境主管部门或者其他有关部门的监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由监督检查部门责令改正，处2万元以上20万元以下的罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 40** Where any person refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection conducted by the competent department of ecological environment or other relevant departments, or practices fraud when undergoing supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection department shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　因违反本条例规定受到行政处罚的，按照国家有关规定记入信用记录，并向社会公布。

**Article 41** Any person who has been subjected to an administrative penalty for violating the provisions of these Regulations shall have it recorded in the credit record in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, and shall be disclosed to the public.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本条例自2010年6月1日起施行。

**Article 42** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2010.
