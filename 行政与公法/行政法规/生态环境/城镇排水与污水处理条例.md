**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2013-10-02  
**生效日期**：2014-01-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对城镇排水与污水处理的管理，保障城镇排水与污水处理设施安全运行，防治城镇水污染和内涝灾害，保障公民生命、财产安全和公共安全，保护环境，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to strengthen the management of urban drainage and sewage treatment, ensure the safe operation of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, prevent and control urban water pollution and waterlogging disasters, protect citizens' life, property safety and public safety, and protect the environment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　城镇排水与污水处理的规划，城镇排水与污水处理设施的建设、维护与保护，向城镇排水设施排水与污水处理，以及城镇内涝防治，适用本条例。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to the planning of urban drainage and sewage treatment, the construction, maintenance and protection of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, the drainage and sewage treatment into urban drainage facilities, and the prevention and control of urban waterlogging.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当加强对城镇排水与污水处理工作的领导，并将城镇排水与污水处理工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 3** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen leadership over urban drainage and sewage treatment work and incorporate urban drainage and sewage treatment work into the national economic and social development plan.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　城镇排水与污水处理应当遵循尊重自然、统筹规划、配套建设、保障安全、综合利用的原则。

**Article 4** Urban drainage and sewage treatment shall follow the principles of respecting nature, overall planning, supporting construction, ensuring safety, and comprehensive utilization.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院住房城乡建设主管部门指导监督全国城镇排水与污水处理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府城镇排水与污水处理主管部门(以下称城镇排水主管部门)负责本行政区域内城镇排水与污水处理的监督管理工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照本条例和其他有关法律、法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内负责城镇排水与污水处理监督管理的相关工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of housing and urban-rural construction under the State Council shall guide and supervise the national urban drainage and sewage treatment work.  
The competent department of urban drainage and sewage treatment of the local people's government at or above the county level (hereinafter referred to as the competent urban drainage department) shall be responsible for the supervision and management of urban drainage and sewage treatment within its administrative region.  
Other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with these Regulations and other relevant laws and regulations, be responsible for the relevant work of supervision and management of urban drainage and sewage treatment within their respective duties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励采取特许经营、政府购买服务等多种形式，吸引社会资金参与投资、建设和运营城镇排水与污水处理设施。  
县级以上人民政府鼓励、支持城镇排水与污水处理科学技术研究，推广应用先进适用的技术、工艺、设备和材料，促进污水的再生利用和污泥、雨水的资源化利用，提高城镇排水与污水处理能力。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage the adoption of various forms such as franchising and government purchase of services to attract social capital to participate in the investment, construction and operation of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall encourage and support scientific and technological research on urban drainage and sewage treatment, promote and apply advanced and applicable technologies, processes, equipment and materials, facilitate the reuse of wastewater and the resource utilization of sludge and rainwater, and improve the capacity of urban drainage and sewage treatment.

### 第二章　规划与建设

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院有关部门，编制全国的城镇排水与污水处理规划，明确全国城镇排水与污水处理的中长期发展目标、发展战略、布局、任务以及保障措施等。  
城镇排水主管部门会同有关部门，根据当地经济社会发展水平以及地理、气候特征，编制本行政区域的城镇排水与污水处理规划，明确排水与污水处理目标与标准，排水量与排水模式，污水处理与再生利用、污泥处理处置要求，排涝措施，城镇排水与污水处理设施的规模、布局、建设时序和建设用地以及保障措施等；易发生内涝的城市、镇，还应当编制城镇内涝防治专项规划，并纳入本行政区域的城镇排水与污水处理规划。

**Article 7** The competent department of housing and urban-rural construction under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate a national plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment, specifying the medium and long-term development goals, development strategies, layout, tasks and safeguard measures for national urban drainage and sewage treatment.  
The competent urban drainage department shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate the plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment within its administrative region based on the local economic and social development level and geographical and climatic characteristics, specifying the objectives and standards for drainage and sewage treatment, the amount and mode of drainage, the requirements for sewage treatment and reuse, sludge treatment and disposal, waterlogging prevention measures, the scale, layout, construction sequence and construction land of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, and safeguard measures; for cities and towns prone to waterlogging, a special plan for the prevention and control of urban waterlogging shall also be formulated and included in the plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment within the administrative region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　城镇排水与污水处理规划的编制，应当依据国民经济和社会发展规划、城乡规划、土地利用总体规划、水污染防治规划和防洪规划，并与城镇开发建设、道路、绿地、水系等专项规划相衔接。  
城镇内涝防治专项规划的编制，应当根据城镇人口与规模、降雨规律、暴雨内涝风险等因素，合理确定内涝防治目标和要求，充分利用自然生态系统，提高雨水滞渗、调蓄和排放能力。

**Article 8** The formulation of plans for urban drainage and sewage treatment shall be based on the national economic and social development plan, urban and rural plan, overall land use plan, water pollution prevention plan and flood control plan, and shall be aligned with special plans for urban development and construction, roads, green spaces, water systems, etc.  
The formulation of a special plan for the prevention and control of urban waterlogging shall, based on factors such as urban population and scale, rainfall patterns, and rainstorm waterlogging risks, reasonably determine the objectives and requirements for waterlogging prevention and control, fully utilize natural ecosystems, and improve the capacity for rainwater infiltration, storage and discharge.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城镇排水主管部门应当将编制的城镇排水与污水处理规划报本级人民政府批准后组织实施，并报上一级人民政府城镇排水主管部门备案。  
城镇排水与污水处理规划一经批准公布，应当严格执行；因经济社会发展确需修改的，应当按照原审批程序报送审批。

**Article 9** The competent urban drainage department shall submit the formulated plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment to the people's government at the same level for approval before organizing its implementation, and shall report it to the competent urban drainage department of the people's government at the next higher level for record.  
Once approved and published, the plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment shall be strictly implemented; if modification is truly necessary due to economic and social development, it shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the original approval procedures.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据城镇排水与污水处理规划的要求，加大对城镇排水与污水处理设施建设和维护的投入。

**Article 10** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the requirements of the plan for urban drainage and sewage treatment, increase investment in the construction and maintenance of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城乡规划和城镇排水与污水处理规划确定的城镇排水与污水处理设施建设用地，不得擅自改变用途。

**Article 11** The land designated for the construction of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities as determined by urban and rural planning and urban drainage and sewage treatment planning shall not be changed in use without authorization.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照先规划后建设的原则，依据城镇排水与污水处理规划，合理确定城镇排水与污水处理设施建设标准，统筹安排管网、泵站、污水处理厂以及污泥处理处置、再生水利用、雨水调蓄和排放等排水与污水处理设施建设和改造。  
城镇新区的开发和建设，应当按照城镇排水与污水处理规划确定的建设时序，优先安排排水与污水处理设施建设；未建或者已建但未达到国家有关标准的，应当按照年度改造计划进行改造，提高城镇排水与污水处理能力。

**Article 12** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principle of planning before construction and pursuant to the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning, reasonably determine the construction standards for urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, and make overall arrangements for the construction and renovation of drainage and sewage treatment facilities, including pipe networks, pumping stations, sewage treatment plants, as well as sludge treatment and disposal, reclaimed water utilization, rainwater regulation and storage, and discharge.  
The development and construction of new urban districts shall, in accordance with the construction schedule determined by the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning, give priority to the construction of drainage and sewage treatment facilities; where such facilities have not been built or, having been built, do not meet the relevant national standards, they shall be renovated in accordance with the annual renovation plan to enhance the capacity of urban drainage and sewage treatment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照城镇排涝要求，结合城镇用地性质和条件，加强雨水管网、泵站以及雨水调蓄、超标雨水径流排放等设施建设和改造。  
新建、改建、扩建市政基础设施工程应当配套建设雨水收集利用设施，增加绿地、砂石地面、可渗透路面和自然地面对雨水的滞渗能力，利用建筑物、停车场、广场、道路等建设雨水收集利用设施，削减雨水径流，提高城镇内涝防治能力。  
新区建设与旧城区改建，应当按照城镇排水与污水处理规划确定的雨水径流控制要求建设相关设施。

**Article 13** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the urban waterlogging prevention requirements and in light of the nature and conditions of urban land use, strengthen the construction and renovation of facilities such as rainwater pipe networks, pumping stations, rainwater regulation and storage, and discharge of excessive rainwater runoff.  
New construction, reconstruction, or expansion of municipal infrastructure projects shall be accompanied by the construction of rainwater collection and utilization facilities, enhance the capacity of green spaces, gravel surfaces, permeable pavements, and natural surfaces to retain and infiltrate rainwater, and utilize buildings, parking lots, squares, roads, etc. to build rainwater collection and utilization facilities, so as to reduce rainwater runoff and improve the urban waterlogging prevention capability.  
The construction of new districts and the renovation of old urban areas shall, in accordance with the rainwater runoff control requirements determined by the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning, construct relevant facilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　城镇排水与污水处理规划范围内的城镇排水与污水处理设施建设项目以及需要与城镇排水与污水处理设施相连接的新建、改建、扩建建设工程，城乡规划主管部门在依法核发建设用地规划许可证时，应当征求城镇排水主管部门的意见。城镇排水主管部门应当就排水设计方案是否符合城镇排水与污水处理规划和相关标准提出意见。  
建设单位应当按照排水设计方案建设连接管网等设施；未建设连接管网等设施的，不得投入使用。城镇排水主管部门或者其委托的专门机构应当加强指导和监督。

**Article 14** For construction projects of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities within the scope of the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning, as well as new construction, reconstruction, or expansion projects that need to be connected to urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall, when lawfully issuing a permit for the planned use of construction land, solicit the opinions of the competent department of urban drainage. The competent department of urban drainage shall provide opinions on whether the drainage design scheme complies with the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning and relevant standards.  
The construction unit shall, in accordance with the drainage design scheme, construct connecting pipe networks and other facilities; where connecting pipe networks or other facilities have not been constructed, the project shall not be put into use. The competent department of urban drainage or the specialized agency entrusted by it shall strengthen guidance and supervision.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　城镇排水与污水处理设施建设工程竣工后，建设单位应当依法组织竣工验收。竣工验收合格的，方可交付使用，并自竣工验收合格之日起15日内，将竣工验收报告及相关资料报城镇排水主管部门备案。

**Article 15** After the completion of a construction project of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, the construction unit shall organize the completion acceptance according to law. Only upon passing the completion acceptance may the project be delivered for use, and within 15 days from the date of passing the completion acceptance, the completion acceptance report and relevant materials shall be submitted to the competent department of urban drainage for recordation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　城镇排水与污水处理设施竣工验收合格后，由城镇排水主管部门通过招标投标、委托等方式确定符合条件的设施维护运营单位负责管理。特许经营合同、委托运营合同涉及污染物削减和污水处理运营服务费的，城镇排水主管部门应当征求环境保护主管部门、价格主管部门的意见。国家鼓励实施城镇污水处理特许经营制度。具体办法由国务院住房城乡建设主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
城镇排水与污水处理设施维护运营单位应当具备下列条件：  
(一)有法人资格；  
(二)有与从事城镇排水与污水处理设施维护运营活动相适应的资金和设备；  
(三)有完善的运行管理和安全管理制度；  
(四)技术负责人和关键岗位人员经专业培训并考核合格；  
(五)有相应的良好业绩和维护运营经验；  
(六)法律、法规规定的其他条件。

**Article 16** After the completion acceptance of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities has passed, the competent department of urban drainage shall, through methods such as tender or entrustment, determine a qualified facility maintenance and operation entity to be responsible for management. Where a concession contract or an entrusted operation contract involves pollutant reduction and sewage treatment operation service fees, the competent department of urban drainage shall solicit the opinions of the competent department of environmental protection and the competent department of price. The State encourages the implementation of a concession system for urban sewage treatment. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
A maintenance and operation entity of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) have the status of a legal person;  
(2) have funds and equipment commensurate with the maintenance and operation activities of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(3) have a sound operation management and safety management system;  
(4) the technical person in charge and key position personnel have received professional training and passed assessments;  
(5) have corresponding good performance and maintenance and operation experience;  
(6) other conditions as prescribed by laws and regulations.

### 第三章　排水

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据当地降雨规律和暴雨内涝风险情况，结合气象、水文资料，建立排水设施地理信息系统，加强雨水排放管理，提高城镇内涝防治水平。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门、单位采取相应的预防治理措施，建立城镇内涝防治预警、会商、联动机制，发挥河道行洪能力和水库、洼淀、湖泊调蓄洪水的功能，加强对城镇排水设施的管理和河道防护、整治，因地制宜地采取定期清淤疏浚等措施，确保雨水排放畅通，共同做好城镇内涝防治工作。

**Article 17** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on local rainfall patterns and stormwater waterlogging risk conditions, and in conjunction with meteorological and hydrological data, establish a geographic information system for drainage facilities, strengthen the management of rainwater discharge, and improve the level of urban waterlogging prevention.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments and entities to adopt corresponding preventive and control measures, establish mechanisms for early warning, consultation, and joint action for urban waterlogging prevention, give full play to the flood discharge capacity of rivers and the flood regulation and storage functions of reservoirs, depressions, and lakes, strengthen the management of urban drainage facilities and the protection and remediation of rivers, and take measures such as regular desilting and dredging in light of local conditions to ensure the smooth discharge of rainwater, and jointly accomplish the work of urban waterlogging prevention.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　城镇排水主管部门应当按照城镇内涝防治专项规划的要求，确定雨水收集利用设施建设标准，明确雨水的排水分区和排水出路，合理控制雨水径流。

**Article 18** The competent department of urban drainage shall, in accordance with the requirements of the special plan for urban waterlogging prevention, determine the construction standards for rainwater collection and utilization facilities, specify the drainage zones and drainage outlets for rainwater, and reasonably control rainwater runoff.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　除干旱地区外，新区建设应当实行雨水、污水分流；对实行雨水、污水合流的地区，应当按照城镇排水与污水处理规划要求，进行雨水、污水分流改造。雨水、污水分流改造可以结合旧城区改建和道路建设同时进行。  
在雨水、污水分流地区，新区建设和旧城区改建不得将雨水管网、污水管网相互混接。  
在有条件的地区，应当逐步推进初期雨水收集与处理，合理确定截流倍数，通过设置初期雨水贮存池、建设截流干管等方式，加强对初期雨水的排放调控和污染防治。

**Article 19** Except for arid areas, new district construction shall adopt separate systems for rainwater and sewage; in areas where combined systems for rainwater and sewage are used, conversion to separate systems for rainwater and sewage shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the urban drainage and sewage treatment planning. The conversion to separate systems for rainwater and sewage may be carried out simultaneously with the renovation of old urban areas and road construction.  
In areas with separate systems for rainwater and sewage, the rainwater pipe network and the sewage pipe network shall not be mixed and connected in new district construction or old urban area renovation.  
In areas where conditions permit, the collection and treatment of initial rainwater shall be progressively promoted, the interception ratio shall be reasonably determined, and measures such as setting up initial rainwater storage tanks and constructing interception main pipes shall be adopted to strengthen the regulation of initial rainwater discharge and pollution prevention.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　城镇排水设施覆盖范围内的排水单位和个人，应当按照国家有关规定将污水排入城镇排水设施。  
在雨水、污水分流地区，不得将污水排入雨水管网。

**Article 20** Entities and individuals that discharge wastewater within the coverage of urban drainage facilities shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, discharge wastewater into the urban drainage facilities.  
In areas with separate systems for rainwater and sewage, sewage shall not be discharged into the rainwater pipe network.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　从事工业、建筑、餐饮、医疗等活动的企业事业单位、个体工商户(以下称排水户)向城镇排水设施排放污水的，应当向城镇排水主管部门申请领取污水排入排水管网许可证。城镇排水主管部门应当按照国家有关标准，重点对影响城镇排水与污水处理设施安全运行的事项进行审查。  
排水户应当按照污水排入排水管网许可证的要求排放污水。

**Article 21** Enterprises, institutions, and individual businesses (hereinafter referred to as drainage users) engaged in industries, construction, catering, medical treatment, etc., that discharge sewage into urban drainage facilities shall apply to the urban drainage competent department for a permit for the discharge of sewage into the drainage network. The urban drainage competent department shall, in accordance with the relevant national standards, focus on examining matters affecting the safe operation of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities.  
Drainage users shall discharge sewage in accordance with the requirements of the permit for the discharge of sewage into the drainage network.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　排水户申请领取污水排入排水管网许可证应当具备下列条件：  
(一)排放口的设置符合城镇排水与污水处理规划的要求；  
(二)按照国家有关规定建设相应的预处理设施和水质、水量检测设施；  
(三)排放的污水符合国家或者地方规定的有关排放标准；  
(四)法律、法规规定的其他条件。  
符合前款规定条件的，由城镇排水主管部门核发污水排入排水管网许可证；具体办法由国务院住房城乡建设主管部门制定。

**Article 22** A drainage user applying for a permit for the discharge of sewage into the drainage network shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The setting of the discharge outlet complies with the requirements of the urban drainage and sewage treatment plan;  
(2) The corresponding pretreatment facilities and water quality and quantity monitoring facilities are constructed in accordance with the relevant state regulations;  
(3) The discharged sewage meets the relevant discharge standards prescribed by the state or local authorities;  
(4) Other conditions prescribed by laws and regulations.  
Where the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph are met, the urban drainage competent department shall issue a permit for the discharge of sewage into the drainage network; the specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the State Council.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　城镇排水主管部门应当加强对排放口设置以及预处理设施和水质、水量检测设施建设的指导和监督；对不符合规划要求或者国家有关规定的，应当要求排水户采取措施，限期整改。

**Article 23** The urban drainage competent department shall strengthen the guidance and supervision over the setting of discharge outlets and the construction of pretreatment facilities and water quality and quantity monitoring facilities; if any non-compliance with planning requirements or relevant national regulations is found, it shall require the drainage user to take measures and rectify within a time limit.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　城镇排水主管部门委托的排水监测机构，应当对排水户排放污水的水质和水量进行监测，并建立排水监测档案。排水户应当接受监测，如实提供有关资料。  
列入重点排污单位名录的排水户安装的水污染物排放自动监测设备，应当与环境保护主管部门的监控设备联网。环境保护主管部门应当将监测数据与城镇排水主管部门共享。

**Article 24** The drainage monitoring agency entrusted by the urban drainage competent department shall monitor the water quality and quantity of sewage discharged by drainage users, and establish drainage monitoring files. Drainage users shall accept the monitoring and truthfully provide relevant materials.  
Where drainage users included in the list of key pollutant-discharging units install automatic monitoring equipment for water pollutant discharge, such equipment shall be connected to the monitoring equipment of the competent environmental protection department. The competent environmental protection department shall share the monitoring data with the urban drainage competent department.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　因城镇排水设施维护或者检修可能对排水造成影响的，城镇排水设施维护运营单位应当提前24小时通知相关排水户；可能对排水造成严重影响的，应当事先向城镇排水主管部门报告，采取应急处理措施，并向社会公告。

**Article 25** Where the maintenance or repair of urban drainage facilities may affect drainage, the maintenance and operation unit of urban drainage facilities shall notify the relevant drainage users 24 hours in advance; where it may cause a serious impact on drainage, it shall report to the urban drainage competent department in advance, take emergency measures, and announce to the public.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　设置于机动车道路上的窨井，应当按照国家有关规定进行建设，保证其承载力和稳定性等符合相关要求。  
排水管网窨井盖应当具备防坠落和防盗窃功能，满足结构强度要求。

**Article 26** Manholes installed on motor vehicle roads shall be constructed in accordance with the relevant state regulations to ensure that their bearing capacity and stability meet the relevant requirements.  
The manhole covers of the drainage network shall have anti-falling and anti-theft functions and meet the structural strength requirements.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　城镇排水主管部门应当按照国家有关规定建立城镇排涝风险评估制度和灾害后评估制度，在汛前对城镇排水设施进行全面检查，对发现的问题，责成有关单位限期处理，并加强城镇广场、立交桥下、地下构筑物、棚户区等易涝点的治理，强化排涝措施，增加必要的强制排水设施和装备。  
城镇排水设施维护运营单位应当按照防汛要求，对城镇排水设施进行全面检查、维护、清疏，确保设施安全运行。  
在汛期，有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥机构应当加强对易涝点的巡查，发现险情，立即采取措施。有关单位和个人在汛期应当服从有管辖权的人民政府防汛指挥机构的统一调度指挥或者监督。

**Article 27** The urban drainage competent department shall, in accordance with the relevant state regulations, establish an urban waterlogging risk assessment system and a post-disaster assessment system, conduct a comprehensive inspection of urban drainage facilities before the flood season, order relevant units to deal with the problems found within a time limit, and strengthen the management of waterlogging-prone areas such as urban squares, underpasses, underground structures, and shantytowns, enhance drainage measures, and add necessary forced drainage facilities and equipment.  
The maintenance and operation unit of urban drainage facilities shall, in accordance with the flood control requirements, conduct comprehensive inspection, maintenance, and dredging of urban drainage facilities to ensure their safe operation.  
During the flood season, the flood control command of the people's government with jurisdiction shall strengthen inspections of waterlogging-prone areas, and take immediate measures upon discovering any danger. Relevant entities and individuals shall, during the flood season, comply with the unified dispatch, command, or supervision of the flood control command of the people's government with jurisdiction.

### 第四章　污水处理

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　城镇排水主管部门应当与城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位签订维护运营合同，明确双方权利义务。  
城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位应当依照法律、法规和有关规定以及维护运营合同进行维护运营，定期向社会公开有关维护运营信息，并接受相关部门和社会公众的监督。

**Article 28** The urban drainage competent department shall enter into a maintenance and operation contract with the maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities, specifying the rights and obligations of both parties.  
The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities shall perform maintenance and operation in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant provisions, and the maintenance and operation contract, regularly disclose relevant maintenance and operation information to the public, and accept supervision from relevant departments and the public.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位应当保证出水水质符合国家和地方规定的排放标准，不得排放不达标污水。  
城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位应当按照国家有关规定检测进出水水质，向城镇排水主管部门、环境保护主管部门报送污水处理水质和水量、主要污染物削减量等信息，并按照有关规定和维护运营合同，向城镇排水主管部门报送生产运营成本等信息。  
城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位应当按照国家有关规定向价格主管部门提交相关成本信息。  
城镇排水主管部门核定城镇污水处理运营成本，应当考虑主要污染物削减情况。

**Article 29** The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities shall ensure that the effluent water quality meets the discharge standards prescribed by the state and local authorities, and shall not discharge substandard sewage.  
The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities shall, in accordance with the relevant state regulations, test the influent and effluent water quality, submit information such as the quality and quantity of treated sewage and the reduction of major pollutants to the urban drainage competent department and the competent environmental protection department, and, in accordance with the relevant regulations and the maintenance and operation contract, submit information such as production and operation costs to the urban drainage competent department.  
The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities shall, in accordance with the relevant state regulations, submit relevant cost information to the competent price department.  
The urban drainage competent department, when reviewing the operation costs of urban sewage treatment, shall consider the reduction of major pollutants.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位或者污泥处理处置单位应当安全处理处置污泥，保证处理处置后的污泥符合国家有关标准，对产生的污泥以及处理处置后的污泥去向、用途、用量等进行跟踪、记录，并向城镇排水主管部门、环境保护主管部门报告。任何单位和个人不得擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒污泥。

**Article 30** The maintenance and operation unit of urban sewage treatment facilities or the sludge treatment and disposal unit shall safely treat and dispose of sludge, ensure that the sludge after treatment and disposal meets the relevant national standards, track and record the generated sludge and the whereabouts, use, and quantity of the sludge after treatment and disposal, and report to the urban drainage competent department and the competent environmental protection department. No entity or individual shall arbitrarily dump, pile up, discard, or spill sludge.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位不得擅自停运城镇污水处理设施，因检修等原因需要停运或者部分停运城镇污水处理设施的，应当在90个工作日前向城镇排水主管部门、环境保护主管部门报告。  
城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位在出现进水水质和水量发生重大变化可能导致出水水质超标，或者发生影响城镇污水处理设施安全运行的突发情况时，应当立即采取应急处理措施，并向城镇排水主管部门、环境保护主管部门报告。  
城镇排水主管部门或者环境保护主管部门接到报告后，应当及时核查处理。

**Article 31** The operation and maintenance entity of an urban sewage treatment facility shall not suspend the operation of the urban sewage treatment facility without authorization. Where it is necessary to suspend or partially suspend the operation of the urban sewage treatment facility due to maintenance or other reasons, it shall report to the competent urban drainage department and the competent environmental protection department 90 working days in advance.  
Where the quality or quantity of incoming water undergoes significant changes which may cause the effluent quality to exceed standards, or where an emergency occurs that affects the safe operation of the urban sewage treatment facility, the operation and maintenance entity of the urban sewage treatment facility shall immediately take emergency response measures and report to the competent urban drainage department and the competent environmental protection department.  
Upon receiving the report, the competent urban drainage department or the competent environmental protection department shall promptly verify and handle the matter.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　排水单位和个人应当按照国家有关规定缴纳污水处理费。  
向城镇污水处理设施排放污水、缴纳污水处理费的，不再缴纳排污费。  
排水监测机构接受城镇排水主管部门委托从事有关监测活动，不得向城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位和排水户收取任何费用。

**Article 32** Entities and individuals that discharge sewage shall pay sewage treatment fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Those who discharge sewage into an urban sewage treatment facility and pay sewage treatment fees shall no longer pay pollutant discharge fees.  
A drainage monitoring agency entrusted by the competent urban drainage department to engage in relevant monitoring activities shall not charge any fees from the operation and maintenance entity of the urban sewage treatment facility or from drainage entities.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　污水处理费应当纳入地方财政预算管理，专项用于城镇污水处理设施的建设、运行和污泥处理处置，不得挪作他用。污水处理费的收费标准不应低于城镇污水处理设施正常运营的成本。因特殊原因，收取的污水处理费不足以支付城镇污水处理设施正常运营的成本的，地方人民政府给予补贴。  
污水处理费的收取、使用情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 33** Sewage treatment fees shall be incorporated into the local fiscal budget management and shall be used exclusively for the construction, operation, and sludge treatment and disposal of urban sewage treatment facilities, and shall not be misappropriated for other purposes. The charging standard for sewage treatment fees shall not be lower than the cost of the normal operation of the urban sewage treatment facility. If, due to special reasons, the collected sewage treatment fees are insufficient to cover the cost of the normal operation of the urban sewage treatment facility, the local people's government shall provide subsidies.  
The collection and use of sewage treatment fees shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上地方人民政府环境保护主管部门应当依法对城镇污水处理设施的出水水质和水量进行监督检查。  
城镇排水主管部门应当对城镇污水处理设施运营情况进行监督和考核，并将监督考核情况向社会公布。有关单位和个人应当予以配合。  
城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位应当为进出水在线监测系统的安全运行提供保障条件。

**Article 34** The competent environmental protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, supervise and inspect the quality and quantity of effluent from urban sewage treatment facilities.  
The competent urban drainage department shall supervise and assess the operation of urban sewage treatment facilities and shall disclose the supervision and assessment results to the public. Relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.  
The operation and maintenance entity of an urban sewage treatment facility shall provide guarantee conditions for the safe operation of the online monitoring system for influent and effluent.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　城镇排水主管部门应当根据城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位履行维护运营合同的情况以及环境保护主管部门对城镇污水处理设施出水水质和水量的监督检查结果，核定城镇污水处理设施运营服务费。地方人民政府有关部门应当及时、足额拨付城镇污水处理设施运营服务费。

**Article 35** The competent urban drainage department shall, based on the performance of the operation and maintenance contract by the operation and maintenance entity of the urban sewage treatment facility and the results of the supervision and inspection by the competent environmental protection department of the effluent quality and quantity of the urban sewage treatment facility, determine the operation service fee for the urban sewage treatment facility. The relevant departments of the local people's government shall promptly and in full allocate the operation service fee for the urban sewage treatment facility.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　城镇排水主管部门在监督考核中，发现城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位存在未依照法律、法规和有关规定以及维护运营合同进行维护运营，擅自停运或者部分停运城镇污水处理设施，或者其他无法安全运行等情形的，应当要求城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位采取措施，限期整改；逾期不整改的，或者整改后仍无法安全运行的，城镇排水主管部门可以终止维护运营合同。  
城镇排水主管部门终止与城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位签订的维护运营合同的，应当采取有效措施保障城镇污水处理设施的安全运行。

**Article 36** Where, during supervision and assessment, the competent urban drainage department finds that the operation and maintenance entity of an urban sewage treatment facility fails to conduct operation and maintenance in accordance with laws, regulations, relevant provisions, and the operation and maintenance contract, suspends or partially suspends the operation of the urban sewage treatment facility without authorization, or has other circumstances that render safe operation impossible, the competent urban drainage department shall require the operation and maintenance entity to take measures and rectify within a specified period; if the entity fails to rectify within the specified period, or if safe operation remains impossible after rectification, the competent urban drainage department may terminate the operation and maintenance contract.  
Where the competent urban drainage department terminates the operation and maintenance contract entered into with the operation and maintenance entity of the urban sewage treatment facility, it shall take effective measures to ensure the safe operation of the urban sewage treatment facility.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家鼓励城镇污水处理再生利用，工业生产、城市绿化、道路清扫、车辆冲洗、建筑施工以及生态景观等，应当优先使用再生水。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据当地水资源和水环境状况，合理确定再生水利用的规模，制定促进再生水利用的保障措施。  
再生水纳入水资源统一配置，县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门应当依法加强指导。

**Article 37** The State encourages the reuse of treated urban sewage. Industrial production, urban greening, road cleaning, vehicle washing, construction, and ecological landscaping, among others, shall give priority to the use of reclaimed water.  
The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the local water resources and water environment conditions, reasonably determine the scale of reclaimed water utilization and formulate safeguard measures to promote the utilization of reclaimed water.  
Reclaimed water shall be incorporated into the unified allocation of water resources, and the competent water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen guidance.

### 第五章　设施维护与保护

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　城镇排水与污水处理设施维护运营单位应当建立健全安全生产管理制度，加强对窨井盖等城镇排水与污水处理设施的日常巡查、维修和养护，保障设施安全运行。  
从事管网维护、应急排水、井下及有限空间作业的，设施维护运营单位应当安排专门人员进行现场安全管理，设置醒目警示标志，采取有效措施避免人员坠落、车辆陷落，并及时复原窨井盖，确保操作规程的遵守和安全措施的落实。相关特种作业人员，应当按照国家有关规定取得相应的资格证书。

**Article 38** The operation and maintenance entity of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities shall establish and improve a safety production management system, strengthen daily patrol, repair, and maintenance of manhole covers and other urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, and ensure the safe operation of the facilities.  
Where personnel engage in pipeline network maintenance, emergency drainage, underground and confined space operations, the entity operating and maintaining the facilities shall assign specialized personnel for on-site safety management, set up conspicuous warning signs, take effective measures to prevent personnel from falling and vehicles from falling in, and promptly restore manhole covers, ensuring compliance with operating procedures and implementation of safety measures. Relevant special operations personnel shall obtain corresponding qualification certificates in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上地方人民政府应当根据实际情况，依法组织编制城镇排水与污水处理应急预案，统筹安排应对突发事件以及城镇排涝所必需的物资。  
城镇排水与污水处理设施维护运营单位应当制定本单位的应急预案，配备必要的抢险装备、器材，并定期组织演练。

**Article 39** The local people's government at or above the county level shall, based on actual circumstances and according to law, organize the formulation of emergency plans for urban drainage and sewage treatment, and make overall arrangements for the materials necessary to respond to emergencies and urban flood control and drainage.  
The operation and maintenance entity of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities shall formulate its own emergency plan, equip necessary emergency rescue equipment and apparatus, and organize regular drills.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　排水户因发生事故或者其他突发事件，排放的污水可能危及城镇排水与污水处理设施安全运行的，应当立即采取措施消除危害，并及时向城镇排水主管部门和环境保护主管部门等有关部门报告。  
城镇排水与污水处理安全事故或者突发事件发生后，设施维护运营单位应当立即启动本单位应急预案，采取防护措施、组织抢修，并及时向城镇排水主管部门和有关部门报告。

**Article 40** Where, due to an accident or other emergency, the sewage discharged by a drainage entity may jeopardize the safe operation of an urban drainage and sewage treatment facility, the entity shall immediately take measures to eliminate the harm and promptly report to the competent urban drainage department, the competent environmental protection department, and other relevant departments.  
After an urban drainage and sewage treatment safety accident or emergency occurs, the facility operation and maintenance entity shall immediately activate its own emergency plan, take protective measures, organize emergency repairs, and promptly report to the competent urban drainage department and relevant departments.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　城镇排水主管部门应当会同有关部门，按照国家有关规定划定城镇排水与污水处理设施保护范围，并向社会公布。  
在保护范围内，有关单位从事爆破、钻探、打桩、顶进、挖掘、取土等可能影响城镇排水与污水处理设施安全的活动的，应当与设施维护运营单位等共同制定设施保护方案，并采取相应的安全防护措施。

**Article 41** The competent urban drainage department shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, delimit the protection scope of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and shall make it known to the public.  
Within the protection scope, if relevant entities engage in activities such as blasting, drilling, piling, pipe jacking, excavation, earth taking, etc., which may affect the safety of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, they shall jointly formulate a facility protection plan with the facility maintenance and operation entity, etc., and shall adopt corresponding safety protection measures.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　禁止从事下列危及城镇排水与污水处理设施安全的活动：  
(一)损毁、盗窃城镇排水与污水处理设施；  
(二)穿凿、堵塞城镇排水与污水处理设施；  
(三)向城镇排水与污水处理设施排放、倾倒剧毒、易燃易爆、腐蚀性废液和废渣；  
(四)向城镇排水与污水处理设施倾倒垃圾、渣土、施工泥浆等废弃物；  
(五)建设占压城镇排水与污水处理设施的建筑物、构筑物或者其他设施；  
(六)其他危及城镇排水与污水处理设施安全的活动。

**Article 42** The following activities that endanger the safety of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities are prohibited:  
(1) damaging or stealing urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(2) cutting through or blocking urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(3) discharging or dumping highly toxic, flammable, explosive, or corrosive waste liquids and residues into urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(4) dumping garbage, debris, construction mud, and other wastes into urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(5) constructing buildings, structures, or other facilities that press on urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities;  
(6) other activities that endanger the safety of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　新建、改建、扩建建设工程，不得影响城镇排水与污水处理设施安全。  
建设工程开工前，建设单位应当查明工程建设范围内地下城镇排水与污水处理设施的相关情况。城镇排水主管部门及其他相关部门和单位应当及时提供相关资料。  
建设工程施工范围内有排水管网等城镇排水与污水处理设施的，建设单位应当与施工单位、设施维护运营单位共同制定设施保护方案，并采取相应的安全保护措施。  
因工程建设需要拆除、改动城镇排水与污水处理设施的，建设单位应当制定拆除、改动方案，报城镇排水主管部门审核，并承担重建、改建和采取临时措施的费用。

**Article 43** For new construction, reconstruction, or expansion of construction projects, the safety of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities shall not be affected.  
Before the commencement of a construction project, the construction entity shall ascertain the relevant information on underground urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities within the project scope. The competent urban drainage department and other relevant departments and entities shall provide relevant materials in a timely manner.  
If there are drainage pipe networks and other urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities within the construction scope, the construction entity shall jointly formulate a facility protection plan with the construction unit and the facility maintenance and operation entity, and shall adopt corresponding safety protection measures.  
If it is necessary to remove or alter urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities due to project construction, the construction entity shall formulate a removal or alteration plan, submit it to the competent urban drainage department for review, and shall bear the costs of reconstruction, alteration, and adoption of interim measures.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府城镇排水主管部门应当会同有关部门，加强对城镇排水与污水处理设施运行维护和保护情况的监督检查，并将检查情况及结果向社会公开。实施监督检查时，有权采取下列措施：  
(一)进入现场进行检查、监测；  
(二)查阅、复制有关文件和资料；  
(三)要求被监督检查的单位和个人就有关问题作出说明。  
被监督检查的单位和个人应当予以配合，不得妨碍和阻挠依法进行的监督检查活动。

**Article 44** The competent urban drainage department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in conjunction with relevant departments, strengthen supervision and inspection of the operation, maintenance, and protection of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, and shall make the inspection situation and results known to the public. When conducting supervision and inspection, it has the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) entering the site for inspection and monitoring;  
(2) reviewing and copying relevant documents and materials;  
(3) requiring the entities and individuals under supervision and inspection to provide explanations on relevant issues.  
The entities and individuals under supervision and inspection shall cooperate and shall not obstruct or impede the supervision and inspection activities conducted according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　审计机关应当加强对城镇排水与污水处理设施建设、运营、维护和保护等资金筹集、管理和使用情况的监督，并公布审计结果。

**Article 45** The audit authority shall strengthen supervision over the fundraising, management, and use of funds for the construction, operation, maintenance, and protection of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities, and shall publish the audit results.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本条例规定，县级以上地方人民政府及其城镇排水主管部门和其他有关部门，不依法作出行政许可或者办理批准文件的，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不予查处的，或者有其他未依照本条例履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的行为构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，核发污水排入排水管网许可证、排污许可证后不实施监督检查的，对核发许可证的部门及其工作人员依照前款规定处理。

**Article 46** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the local people's government at or above the county level and its competent urban drainage department and other relevant departments fail to make an administrative license or handle approval documents according to law, fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts found or upon receipt of a report on illegal acts, or have other acts of failing to perform their duties as provided in these Regulations, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the acts of the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, after issuing a permit for discharging sewage into drainage pipe networks or a pollutant discharge permit, the department and its staff fail to conduct supervision and inspection, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本条例规定，城镇排水主管部门对不符合法定条件的排水户核发污水排入排水管网许可证的，或者对符合法定条件的排水户不予核发污水排入排水管网许可证的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的行为构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Where, in violation of these Regulations, the competent urban drainage department issues a permit for discharging sewage into drainage pipe networks to a drainage user that does not meet the statutory conditions, or fails to issue such permit to a drainage user that meets the statutory conditions, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if the acts of the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本条例规定，在雨水、污水分流地区，建设单位、施工单位将雨水管网、污水管网相互混接的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，处5万元以上10万元以下的罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 48** Where, in violation of these Regulations, in a rainwater and sewage diversion area, a construction entity or construction unit mixes the rainwater pipe network with the sewage pipe network, the competent urban drainage department shall order it to make corrections and impose a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本条例规定，城镇排水与污水处理设施覆盖范围内的排水单位和个人，未按照国家有关规定将污水排入城镇排水设施，或者在雨水、污水分流地区将污水排入雨水管网的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，给予警告；逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，对单位处10万元以上20万元以下罚款，对个人处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 49** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a drainage entity or individual within the coverage area of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities fails to discharge sewage into urban drainage facilities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, or in a rainwater and sewage diversion area, discharges sewage into the rainwater pipe network, the competent urban drainage department shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit or causes serious consequences, the entity shall be fined not less than RMB 100,000 yuan but not more than RMB 200,000 yuan, and the individual shall be fined not less than RMB 20,000 yuan but not more than RMB 100,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本条例规定，排水户未取得污水排入排水管网许可证向城镇排水设施排放污水的，由城镇排水主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期采取治理措施，补办污水排入排水管网许可证，可以处50万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，排水户不按照污水排入排水管网许可证的要求排放污水的，由城镇排水主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，可以处5万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，吊销污水排入排水管网许可证，并处5万元以上50万元以下罚款，可以向社会予以通报；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a drainage user discharges sewage into urban drainage facilities without obtaining a permit for discharging sewage into drainage pipe networks, the competent urban drainage department shall order it to cease the illegal act, take remedial measures within a time limit, and retroactively apply for the permit; it may impose a fine of not more than RMB 500,000 yuan; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a drainage user fails to discharge sewage in accordance with the requirements of the permit for discharging sewage into drainage pipe networks, the competent urban drainage department shall order it to cease the illegal act and make corrections within a time limit; it may impose a fine of not more than RMB 50,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, the permit shall be revoked, and a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 yuan but not more than RMB 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and it may be notified to the public; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本条例规定，因城镇排水设施维护或者检修可能对排水造成影响或者严重影响，城镇排水设施维护运营单位未提前通知相关排水户的，或者未事先向城镇排水主管部门报告，采取应急处理措施的，或者未按照防汛要求对城镇排水设施进行全面检查、维护、清疏，影响汛期排水畅通的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，给予警告；逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 51** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit maintaining and operating urban drainage facilities fails to notify in advance the relevant drainage users where urban drainage facility maintenance or repair may affect or seriously affect drainage, or fails to report to the competent urban drainage department in advance and take emergency response measures, or fails to conduct comprehensive inspection, maintenance, and dredging of urban drainage facilities as required by flood control requirements, thereby affecting smooth drainage during the flood season, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action and give a warning; if corrective action is not taken within the time limit or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本条例规定，城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位未按照国家有关规定检测进出水水质的，或者未报送污水处理水质和水量、主要污染物削减量等信息和生产运营成本等信息的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，可以处5万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位擅自停运城镇污水处理设施，未按照规定事先报告或者采取应急处理措施的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，给予警告；逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 52** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit maintaining and operating urban sewage treatment facilities fails to test the quality of influent and effluent in accordance with relevant state provisions, or fails to submit information such as sewage treatment quality and quantity, reduction amount of major pollutants, and production operation costs, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action and may impose a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit maintaining and operating urban sewage treatment facilities suspends the operation of urban sewage treatment facilities without authorization, or fails to report in advance or take emergency response measures as required, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action and give a warning; if corrective action is not taken within the time limit or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本条例规定，城镇污水处理设施维护运营单位或者污泥处理处置单位对产生的污泥以及处理处置后的污泥的去向、用途、用量等未进行跟踪、记录的，或者处理处置后的污泥不符合国家有关标准的，由城镇排水主管部门责令限期采取治理措施，给予警告；造成严重后果的，处10万元以上20万元以下罚款；逾期不采取治理措施的，城镇排水主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由当事人承担；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
违反本条例规定，擅自倾倒、堆放、丢弃、遗撒污泥的，由城镇排水主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期采取治理措施，给予警告；造成严重后果的，对单位处10万元以上50万元以下罚款，对个人处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；逾期不采取治理措施的，城镇排水主管部门可以指定有治理能力的单位代为治理，所需费用由当事人承担；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit maintaining and operating urban sewage treatment facilities or a unit treating and disposing of sludge fails to track and record the whereabouts, use, quantity, etc. of the sludge generated and the sludge after treatment and disposal, or the sludge after treatment and disposal does not meet relevant national standards, the competent urban drainage department shall order remedial action to be taken within a time limit and give a warning; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; if remedial action is not taken within the time limit, the competent urban drainage department may designate a unit with the capacity to take remedial action on its behalf, and the expenses thus incurred shall be borne by the party; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, sludge is dumped, piled, discarded, or scattered without authorization, the competent urban drainage department shall order cessation of the illegal act, take remedial action within a time limit, and give a warning; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if remedial action is not taken within the time limit, the competent urban drainage department may designate a unit with the capacity to take remedial action on its behalf, and the expenses thus incurred shall be borne by the party; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本条例规定，排水单位或者个人不缴纳污水处理费的，由城镇排水主管部门责令限期缴纳，逾期拒不缴纳的，处应缴纳污水处理费数额1倍以上3倍以下罚款。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a drainage unit or individual fails to pay sewage treatment fees, the competent urban drainage department shall order payment within a time limit; if payment is refused beyond the time limit, a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the amount of sewage treatment fees payable shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本条例规定，城镇排水与污水处理设施维护运营单位有下列情形之一的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，给予警告；逾期不改正或者造成严重后果的，处10万元以上50万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)未按照国家有关规定履行日常巡查、维修和养护责任，保障设施安全运行的；  
(二)未及时采取防护措施、组织事故抢修的；  
(三)因巡查、维护不到位，导致窨井盖丢失、损毁，造成人员伤亡和财产损失的。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a unit maintaining and operating urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities commits any of the following acts, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action and give a warning; if corrective action is not taken within the time limit or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Failing to perform the duties of daily inspection, repair and maintenance in accordance with relevant state provisions to ensure safe operation of the facilities;  
(2) Failing to take protective measures in a timely manner or to organize emergency repair of accidents;  
(3) Causing personal injury or property loss due to inadequate inspection or maintenance resulting in loss or damage of manhole covers.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本条例规定，从事危及城镇排水与污水处理设施安全的活动的，由城镇排水主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，给予警告；逾期不采取补救措施或者造成严重后果的，对单位处10万元以上30万元以下罚款，对个人处2万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any activity endangering the safety of urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities is engaged in, the competent urban drainage department shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration to the original condition within a time limit or taking of other remedial measures, and give a warning; if remedial measures are not taken within the time limit or serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed on the individual; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本条例规定，有关单位未与施工单位、设施维护运营单位等共同制定设施保护方案，并采取相应的安全防护措施的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，处2万元以上5万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本条例规定，擅自拆除、改动城镇排水与污水处理设施的，由城镇排水主管部门责令改正，恢复原状或者采取其他补救措施，处5万元以上10万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处10万元以上30万元以下罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of these Regulations, a relevant unit fails to jointly formulate a facility protection plan with the construction unit, facility maintenance and operation unit, etc., and to take corresponding safety protection measures, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of these Regulations, urban drainage and sewage treatment facilities are demolished or altered without authorization, the competent urban drainage department shall order corrective action, restoration to the original condition or taking of other remedial measures, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　依照《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定，排水户需要取得排污许可证的，由环境保护主管部门核发；违反《中华人民共和国水污染防治法》的规定排放污水的，由环境保护主管部门处罚。

**Article 58** Where, in accordance with the provisions of the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China, a drainage user is required to obtain a pollutant discharge permit, the environmental protection department shall issue such permit; where sewage is discharged in violation of the provisions of the Water Pollution Prevention and Control Law of the People's Republic of China, the environmental protection department shall impose penalties.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本条例自2014年1月1日起施行。

**Article 59** These Regulations shall come into force as of January 1, 2014.
