# 中华人民共和国枪支管理法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2015-04-24  
**生效日期**：2015-04-24

> （1996年7月5日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国港口法〉等七部法律的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　枪支的配备和配置 第三章　枪支的制造和民用枪支的配售 第四章　枪支的日常管理 第五章　枪支的运输 第六章　枪支的入境和出境 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强枪支管理，维护社会治安秩序，保障公共安全，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国境内的枪支管理，适用本法。 对中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵装备枪支的管理，国务院、中央军事委员会另有规定的，适用有关规定。 第三条　国家严格管制枪支。禁止任何单位或者个人违反法律规定持有、制造（包括变造、装配）、买卖、运输、出租、出借枪支。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强枪支管理，维护社会治安秩序，保障公共安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of strengthening the control of firearms, maintaining public security and order, and ensuring public safety.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国境内的枪支管理，适用本法。  
对中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队和民兵装备枪支的管理，国务院、中央军事委员会另有规定的，适用有关规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the control of firearms within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the State Council and the Central Military Commission have otherwise provided for the control of firearms equipped by the Chinese People's Liberation Army, the Chinese People's Armed Police Force and the militia, the relevant provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家严格管制枪支。禁止任何单位或者个人违反法律规定持有、制造（包括变造、装配）、买卖、运输、出租、出借枪支。  
国家严厉惩处违反枪支管理的违法犯罪行为。任何单位和个人对违反枪支管理的行为有检举的义务。国家对检举人给予保护，对检举违反枪支管理犯罪活动有功的人员，给予奖励。

**Article 3** The State strictly controls firearms. No unit or individual shall possess, manufacture (including alteration and assembly), trade, transport, lease or lend firearms in violation of the provisions of laws.  
The State shall severely punish illegal and criminal acts that violate firearms control. Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to report acts that violate firearms control. The State shall protect the informants and give rewards to persons who have rendered meritorious service in reporting criminal activities that violate firearms control.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院公安部门主管全国的枪支管理工作。县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关主管本行政区域内的枪支管理工作。上级人民政府公安机关监督下级人民政府公安机关的枪支管理工作。

**Article 4** The public security department under the State Council shall be responsible for the firearms control work throughout the country. The public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the firearms control work within their respective administrative areas. The public security organs of the people's governments at a higher level shall supervise the firearms control work of the public security organs of the people's governments at a lower level.

### 第二章　枪支的配备和配置

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱、劳动教养机关的人民警察，人民法院的司法警察，人民检察院的司法警察和担负案件侦查任务的检察人员，海关的缉私人员，在依法履行职责时确有必要使用枪支的，可以配备公务用枪。  
国家重要的军工、金融、仓储、科研等单位的专职守护、押运人员在执行守护、押运任务时确有必要使用枪支的，可以配备公务用枪。  
配备公务用枪的具体办法，由国务院公安部门会同其他有关国家机关按照严格控制的原则制定，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 5** The people's police of the public security organs, State security organs, prisons and reeducation-through-labor institutions, the judicial police of the People's Courts, the judicial police of the People's Procuratorates and procuratorial personnel undertaking investigation tasks, and anti-smuggling personnel of the Customs may be equipped with firearms for official use when it is truly necessary for them to use firearms in performing their duties according to law.  
The full-time guards and escorts of important military, financial, warehousing, scientific research and other units of the State may be equipped with firearms for official use when it is truly necessary for them to use firearms in performing their guarding and escorting tasks.  
The specific measures for equipping firearms for official use shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council together with other relevant State organs in accordance with the principle of strict control, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　下列单位可以配置民用枪支：  
（一）经省级人民政府体育行政主管部门批准专门从事射击竞技体育运动的单位、经省级人民政府公安机关批准的营业性射击场，可以配置射击运动枪支；  
（二）经省级以上人民政府林业行政主管部门批准的狩猎场，可以配置猎枪；  
（三）野生动物保护、饲养、科研单位因业务需要，可以配置猎枪、麻醉注射枪。  
猎民在猎区、牧民在牧区，可以申请配置猎枪。猎区和牧区的区域由省级人民政府划定。  
配置民用枪支的具体办法，由国务院公安部门按照严格控制的原则制定，报国务院批准后施行。

**Article 6** The following units may be equipped with civilian firearms:  
(1) Units duly approved by the administrative department of sports under the provincial people's government to be exclusively engaged in shooting competitive sports, and commercial shooting ranges duly approved by the public security organ under the provincial people's government, may be equipped with firearms for shooting sports;  
(2) Hunting grounds duly approved by the administrative department of forestry under the people's government at or above the provincial level may be equipped with hunting guns;  
(3) Units for wildlife protection, breeding and scientific research may, as required by their business, be equipped with hunting guns and anesthetic injection guns.  
Hunters in hunting areas and herdsmen in pastoral areas may apply to be equipped with hunting guns. The boundaries of hunting areas and pastoral areas shall be delimited by the provincial people's governments.  
The specific measures for equipping civilian firearms shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council in accordance with the principle of strict control, and shall come into force after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　配备公务用枪，由国务院公安部门或者省级人民政府公安机关审批。  
配备公务用枪时，由国务院公安部门或者省级人民政府公安机关发给公务用枪持枪证件。

**Article 7** The equipping of firearms for official use shall be subject to the examination and approval by the public security department under the State Council or the public security organ under the provincial people's government.  
When firearms for official use are equipped, the public security department under the State Council or the public security organ under the provincial people's government shall issue the firearm license for official use.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　专门从事射击竞技体育运动的单位配置射击运动枪支，由国务院体育行政主管部门提出，由国务院公安部门审批。营业性射击场配置射击运动枪支，由省级人民政府公安机关报国务院公安部门批准。  
配置射击运动枪支时，由省级人民政府公安机关发给民用枪支持枪证件。

**Article 8** The equipping of firearms for shooting sports by a unit exclusively engaged in shooting competitive sports shall be proposed by the administrative department of sports under the State Council and subject to the examination and approval by the public security department under the State Council. The equipping of firearms for shooting sports by a commercial shooting range shall be reported by the public security organ under the provincial people's government to the public security department under the State Council for approval.  
When firearms for shooting sports are equipped, the public security organ under the provincial people's government shall issue a civilian firearm license.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　狩猎场配置猎枪，凭省级以上人民政府林业行政主管部门的批准文件，报省级以上人民政府公安机关审批，由设区的市级人民政府公安机关核发民用枪支配购证件。

**Article 9** For equipping hunting guns by a hunting ground, on the strength of the approval document of the administrative department of forestry under the people's government at or above the provincial level, a report shall be submitted to the public security organ under the people's government at or above the provincial level for examination and approval, and the public security organ under the people's government at the city with districts shall issue a civilian firearm purchase permit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　野生动物保护、饲养、科研单位申请配置猎枪、麻醉注射枪的，应当凭其所在地的县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门核发的狩猎证或者特许猎捕证和单位营业执照，向所在地的县级人民政府公安机关提出；猎民申请配置猎枪的，应当凭其所在地的县级人民政府野生动物行政主管部门核发的狩猎证和个人身份证件，向所在地的县级人民政府公安机关提出；牧民申请配置猎枪的，应当凭个人身份证件，向所在地的县级人民政府公安机关提出。  
受理申请的公安机关审查批准后，应当报请设区的市级人民政府公安机关核发民用枪支配购证件。

**Article 10** A unit for wildlife protection, breeding or scientific research that applies to be equipped with hunting guns or anesthetic injection guns shall, on the strength of the hunting permit or special hunting license issued by the administrative department of wildlife under the people's government at the county level of its locality and its business license, submit the application to the public security organ under the people's government at the county level of its locality; a hunter who applies to be equipped with a hunting gun shall, on the strength of the hunting permit issued by the administrative department of wildlife under the people's government at the county level of his locality and his personal identification document, submit the application to the public security organ under the people's government at the county level of his locality; a herdsman who applies to be equipped with a hunting gun shall, on the strength of his personal identification document, submit the application to the public security organ under the people's government at the county level of his locality.  
After the public security organ that accepts the application examines and approves it, it shall request the public security organ under the people's government at the city with districts to issue a civilian firearm purchase permit.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　配购猎枪、麻醉注射枪的单位和个人，必须在配购枪支后三十日内向核发民用枪支配购证件的公安机关申请领取民用枪支持枪证件。

**Article 11** A unit or individual that purchases hunting guns or anesthetic injection guns must, within 30 days after the purchase of the firearms, apply to the public security organ that issued the civilian firearm purchase permit for a civilian firearm license.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　营业性射击场、狩猎场配置的民用枪支不得携带出营业性射击场、狩猎场。  
猎民、牧民配置的猎枪不得携带出猎区、牧区。

**Article 12** Civilian firearms equipped by commercial shooting ranges or hunting grounds shall not be carried out of such commercial shooting ranges or hunting grounds.  
Hunting guns equipped by hunters or herdsmen shall not be carried out of hunting areas or pastoral areas.

### 第三章　枪支的制造和民用枪支的配售

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对枪支的制造、配售实行特别许可制度。未经许可，任何单位或者个人不得制造、买卖枪支。

**Article 13** The State applies a special licensing system to the manufacture and distribution of firearms. No unit or individual may manufacture or trade firearms without permission.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公务用枪，由国家指定的企业制造。

**Article 14** Firearms for official use shall be manufactured by enterprises designated by the State.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　制造民用枪支的企业，由国务院有关主管部门提出，由国务院公安部门确定。  
配售民用枪支的企业，由省级人民政府公安机关确定。  
制造民用枪支的企业，由国务院公安部门核发民用枪支制造许可证件。配售民用枪支的企业，由省级人民政府公安机关核发民用枪支配售许可证件。民用枪支制造许可证件、配售许可证件的有效期为三年；有效期届满，需要继续制造、配售民用枪支的，应当重新申请领取许可证件。

**Article 15** Enterprises that manufacture civilian firearms shall be proposed by the relevant competent department under the State Council and determined by the public security department under the State Council.  
Enterprises that distribute civilian firearms shall be determined by the public security organ under the provincial people's government.  
The public security department under the State Council shall issue a civilian firearm manufacturing license to an enterprise that manufactures civilian firearms. The public security organ under the provincial people's government shall issue a civilian firearm distribution license to an enterprise that distributes civilian firearms. The validity period of the civilian firearm manufacturing license and the distribution license shall be three years; if the license expires and the enterprise needs to continue manufacturing or distributing civilian firearms, it shall re-apply for the license.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对制造、配售民用枪支的数量，实行限额管理。  
制造民用枪支的年度限额，由国务院林业、体育等有关主管部门、省级人民政府公安机关提出，由国务院公安部门确定并统一编制民用枪支序号，下达到民用枪支制造企业。  
配售民用枪支的年度限额，由国务院林业、体育等有关主管部门、省级人民政府公安机关提出，由国务院公安部门确定并下达到民用枪支配售企业。

**Article 16** The State exercises quota management over the quantity of civilian firearms manufactured and distributed.  
The annual quota for manufacturing civilian firearms shall be proposed by the relevant competent departments such as forestry and sports under the State Council and the public security organ under the provincial people's government, determined by the public security department under the State Council, and the public security department under the State Council shall uniformly compile serial numbers for civilian firearms and assign them to the civilian firearm manufacturing enterprises.  
The annual quota for distributing civilian firearms shall be proposed by the relevant competent departments such as forestry and sports under the State Council and the public security organ under the provincial people's government, determined by the public security department under the State Council, and assigned to the civilian firearm distribution enterprises.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　制造民用枪支的企业不得超过限额制造民用枪支，所制造的民用枪支必须全部交由指定的民用枪支配售企业配售，不得自行销售。配售民用枪支的企业应当在配售限额内，配售指定的企业制造的民用枪支。

**Article 17** An enterprise manufacturing civilian firearms shall not manufacture civilian firearms in excess of the quota, and all civilian firearms manufactured must be turned over to the designated civilian firearm distribution enterprises for distribution, and shall not be sold by itself. A civilian firearm distribution enterprise shall, within the distribution quota, distribute the civilian firearms manufactured by the designated enterprises.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　制造民用枪支的企业，必须严格按照国家规定的技术标准制造民用枪支，不得改变民用枪支的性能和结构；必须在民用枪支指定部位铸印制造厂的厂名、枪种代码和国务院公安部门统一编制的枪支序号，不得制造无号、重号、假号的民用枪支。  
制造民用枪支的企业必须实行封闭式管理，采取必要的安全保卫措施，防止民用枪支以及民用枪支零部件丢失。

**Article 18** An enterprise manufacturing civilian firearms must strictly manufacture civilian firearms in accordance with the technical standards prescribed by the State, and shall not alter the performance or structure of civilian firearms; it must cast or print on the specified parts of the civilian firearms the name of the manufacturing factory, the code of the type of firearm, and the firearm serial number uniformly compiled by the public security department under the State Council, and shall not manufacture civilian firearms without a serial number, with a duplicate serial number or with a false serial number.  
An enterprise manufacturing civilian firearms must implement closed management and take necessary security measures to prevent the loss of civilian firearms and parts thereof.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　配售民用枪支，必须核对配购证件，严格按照配购证件载明的品种、型号和数量配售；配售弹药，必须核对持枪证件。民用枪支配售企业必须按照国务院公安部门的规定建立配售帐册，长期保管备查。

**Article 19** When distributing civilian firearms, the purchase permit must be checked, and distribution must be strictly in accordance with the type, model and quantity specified in the purchase permit; when distributing ammunition, the firearm license must be checked. A civilian firearm distribution enterprise must establish distribution account books in accordance with the provisions of the public security department under the State Council, and keep them for a long period for reference.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公安机关对制造、配售民用枪支的企业制造、配售、储存和帐册登记等情况，必须进行定期检查；必要时，可以派专人驻厂对制造企业进行监督、检查。

**Article 20** The public security organs must conduct regular inspections on the manufacturing, distribution, storage and account book records of enterprises manufacturing or distributing civilian firearms; where necessary, they may assign special personnel to station at the manufacturing enterprise for supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　民用枪支的研制和定型，由国务院有关业务主管部门会同国务院公安部门组织实施。

**Article 21** The research, development and finalization of civilian firearms shall be organized and implemented by the relevant competent department under the State Council together with the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　禁止制造、销售仿真枪。

**Article 22** The manufacture or sale of imitation firearms is prohibited.

### 第四章　枪支的日常管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　配备、配置枪支的单位和个人必须妥善保管枪支，确保枪支安全。  
配备、配置枪支的单位，必须明确枪支管理责任，指定专人负责，应当有牢固的专用保管设施，枪支、弹药应当分开存放。对交由个人使用的枪支，必须建立严格的枪支登记、交接、检查、保养等管理制度，使用完毕，及时收回。  
配备、配置给个人使用的枪支，必须采取有效措施，严防被盗、被抢、丢失或者发生其他事故。

**Article 23** Units and individuals that are equipped with or configured with firearms must keep the firearms safely to ensure the safety of the firearms.  
Units that are equipped with or configured with firearms must clearly define the responsibility for firearms control, designate special persons to be in charge, have sturdy special storage facilities, and firearms and ammunition must be stored separately. For firearms that are assigned to individuals for use, strict management systems for registration, handover, inspection, and maintenance of firearms must be established, and the firearms must be taken back in time after use.  
For firearms that are equipped with or configured to individuals for use, effective measures must be taken to strictly prevent theft, robbery, loss or other accidents.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　使用枪支的人员，必须掌握枪支的性能，遵守使用枪支的有关规定，保证枪支的合法、安全使用。使用公务用枪的人员，必须经过专门培训。

**Article 24** Persons who use firearms must master the performance of the firearms, comply with the relevant provisions on the use of firearms, and ensure the lawful and safe use of firearms. Persons who use firearms for official use must undergo special training.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　配备、配置枪支的单位和个人必须遵守下列规定：  
（一）携带枪支必须同时携带持枪证件，未携带持枪证件的，由公安机关扣留枪支；  
（二）不得在禁止携带枪支的区域、场所携带枪支；  
（三）枪支被盗、被抢或者丢失的，立即报告公安机关。

**Article 25** Units and individuals that are equipped with or configured with firearms must comply with the following provisions:  
(1) When carrying a firearm, a firearm license must be carried at the same time; if the firearm license is not carried, the public security organ shall impound the firearm;  
(2) Firearms shall not be carried in areas or places where carrying firearms is prohibited;  
(3) If a firearm is stolen, robbed or lost, it shall be reported immediately to the public security organ.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　配备公务用枪的人员不再符合持枪条件时，由所在单位收回枪支和持枪证件。  
配置民用枪支的单位和个人不再符合持枪条件时，必须及时将枪支连同持枪证件上缴核发持枪证件的公安机关；未及时上缴的，由公安机关收缴。

**Article 26** When a person equipped with a firearm for official use no longer meets the conditions for holding a firearm, the unit to which he belongs shall take back the firearm and the firearm license.  
When a unit or individual configured with a civilian firearm no longer meets the conditions for holding a firearm, it or he must promptly surrender the firearm together with the firearm license to the public security organ that issued the firearm license; if it or he fails to surrender promptly, the public security organ shall confiscate it.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　不符合国家技术标准、不能安全使用的枪支，应当报废。配备、持有枪支的单位和个人应当将报废的枪支连同持枪证件上缴核发持枪证件的公安机关；未及时上缴的，由公安机关收缴。报废的枪支应当及时销毁。  
销毁枪支，由省级人民政府公安机关负责组织实施。

**Article 27** Firearms that do not conform to the national technical standards or cannot be used safely shall be scrapped. Units and individuals equipped with or holding firearms shall surrender the scrapped firearms together with the firearm licenses to the public security organ that issued the firearm license; if they fail to surrender promptly, the public security organ shall confiscate them. Scrapped firearms shall be destroyed promptly.  
The destruction of firearms shall be organized and implemented by the public security organ under the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家对枪支实行查验制度。持有枪支的单位和个人，应当在公安机关指定的时间、地点接受查验。公安机关在查验时，必须严格审查持枪单位和个人是否符合本法规定的条件，检查枪支状况及使用情况；对违法使用枪支、不符合持枪条件或者枪支应当报废的，必须收缴枪支和持枪证件。拒不接受查验的，枪支和持枪证件由公安机关收缴。

**Article 28** The State applies an inspection system for firearms. Units and individuals holding firearms shall undergo inspection at the time and place designated by the public security organ. When conducting an inspection, the public security organ must strictly examine whether the unit or individual holding the firearm meets the conditions prescribed by this Law, and inspect the condition and use of the firearm; if the firearm is used illegally, the holder does not meet the conditions for holding a firearm, or the firearm should be scrapped, the firearm and the firearm license must be confiscated. If a person refuses to undergo the inspection, the public security organ shall confiscate the firearm and the firearm license.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　为了维护社会治安秩序的特殊需要，经国务院公安部门批准，县级以上地方各级人民政府公安机关可以对局部地区合法配备、配置的枪支采取集中保管等特别管制措施。

**Article 29** For special needs of maintaining public security and order, with the approval of the public security department under the State Council, the public security organs of the local people's governments at or above the county level may take special control measures such as centralized storage of firearms that are lawfully equipped or configured in a certain area.

### 第五章　枪支的运输

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　任何单位或者个人未经许可，不得运输枪支。需要运输枪支的，必须向公安机关如实申报运输枪支的品种、数量和运输的路线、方式，领取枪支运输许可证件。在本省、自治区、直辖市内运输的，向运往地设区的市级人民政府公安机关申请领取枪支运输许可证件；跨省、自治区、直辖市运输的，向运往地省级人民政府公安机关申请领取枪支运输许可证件。  
没有枪支运输许可证件的，任何单位和个人都不得承运，并应当立即报告所在地公安机关。  
公安机关对没有枪支运输许可证件或者没有按照枪支运输许可证件的规定运输枪支的，应当扣留运输的枪支。

**Article 30** No unit or individual may transport firearms without permission. Where it is necessary to transport firearms, the unit or individual must truthfully declare to the public security organ the types and quantity of the firearms to be transported, the route and means of transportation, and obtain a firearm transportation permit. For transportation within a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, an application shall be made to the public security organ under the people's government at the city with districts of the destination for a firearm transportation permit; for transportation across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, an application shall be made to the public security organ under the people's government at the provincial level of the destination for a firearm transportation permit.  
No unit or individual may undertake the transportation of firearms without a firearm transportation permit, and shall immediately report to the public security organ of the locality.  
The public security organ shall impound the firearms being transported if there is no firearm transportation permit or if the firearms are not transported in accordance with the provisions of the firearm transportation permit.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　运输枪支必须依照规定使用安全可靠的封闭式运输设备，由专人押运；途中停留住宿的，必须报告当地公安机关。  
运输枪支、弹药必须依照规定分开运输。

**Article 31** The transportation of firearms must use safe and reliable closed transport equipment in accordance with the provisions, and be escorted by specially assigned personnel; if a stop or overnight stay occurs during the journey, it must be reported to the local public security organ.  
Transportation of firearms and ammunition must be carried out separately in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　严禁邮寄枪支，或者在邮寄的物品中夹带枪支。

**Article 21** The mailing of firearms, or the inclusion of firearms in mailed items, is strictly prohibited.

### 第六章　枪支的入境和出境

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家严格管理枪支的入境和出境。任何单位或者个人未经许可，不得私自携带枪支入境、出境。

**Article 33** The State strictly controls the entry into and exit from the country of firearms. No unit or individual may bring firearms into or out of the country without permission.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　外国驻华外交代表机构、领事机构的人员携带枪支入境，必须事先报经中华人民共和国外交部批准；携带枪支出境，应当事先照会中华人民共和国外交部，办理有关手续。  
依照前款规定携带入境的枪支，不得携带出所在的驻华机构。

**Article 34** Personnel of foreign diplomatic missions and consular posts in China who bring firearms into the country must obtain prior approval from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China; to bring firearms out of the country, they shall notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China in advance and go through the relevant formalities.  
Firearms brought into the country in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall not be carried out of the diplomatic missions or consular posts in China where they are located.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　外国体育代表团入境参加射击竞技体育活动，或者中国体育代表团出境参加射击竞技体育活动，需要携带射击运动枪支入境、出境的，必须经国务院体育行政主管部门批准。

**Article 35** Where a foreign sports delegation enters China to participate in shooting competitive sports activities, or a Chinese sports delegation leaves China to participate in shooting competitive sports activities, and it is necessary to bring firearms for shooting sports into or out of the country, the matter must be approved by the administrative department of sports under the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　[本法第三十四条](#art-34)、第三十五条规定以外的其他人员携带枪支入境、出境，应当事先经国务院公安部门批准。

**Article 36** Other persons other than those provided for in Articles 34 and 35 of this Law who bring firearms into or out of the country shall obtain prior approval from the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　经批准携带枪支入境的，入境时，应当凭批准文件在入境地边防检查站办理枪支登记，申请领取枪支携运许可证件，向海关申报，海关凭枪支携运许可证件放行；到达目的地后，凭枪支携运许可证件向设区的市级人民政府公安机关申请换发持枪证件。  
经批准携带枪支出境的，出境时，应当凭批准文件向出境地海关申报，边防检查站凭批准文件放行。

**Article 37** Where a person is approved to bring firearms into the country, when entering the country, he shall, on the strength of the approval document, undergo firearm registration at the border inspection station at the port of entry, apply for a firearm transportation permit, and declare to the Customs; the Customs shall release the firearms upon presentation of the firearm transportation permit; after arriving at the destination, he shall, on the strength of the firearm transportation permit, apply to the public security organ under the people's government at the city with districts for a firearm license.  
Where a person is approved to bring firearms out of the country, when leaving the country, he shall, on the strength of the approval document, make a declaration to the Customs at the port of exit, and the border inspection station shall release the firearms upon presentation of the approval document.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　外国交通运输工具携带枪支入境或者过境的，交通运输工具负责人必须向边防检查站申报，由边防检查站加封，交通运输工具出境时予以启封。

**Article 38** Where foreign means of transportation bring firearms into or pass through the country, the person in charge of the means of transportation must declare to the border inspection station, which shall seal them; the seal shall be unsealed when the means of transportation leaves the country.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本法规定，未经许可制造、买卖或者运输枪支的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
单位有前款行为的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, manufactures, trades or transports firearms without permission shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where a unit commits the act mentioned in the preceding paragraph, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　依法被指定、确定的枪支制造企业、销售企业，违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；公安机关可以责令其停业整顿或者吊销其枪支制造许可证件、枪支配售许可证件：  
（一）超过限额或者不按照规定的品种制造、配售枪支的；  
（二）制造无号、重号、假号的枪支的；  
（三）私自销售枪支或者在境内销售为出口制造的枪支的。

**Article 40** Where a firearm manufacturing or selling enterprise that is designated or determined according to law, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits one of the following acts, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; the public security organ may order it to suspend business for rectification or revoke its firearm manufacturing license or firearm distribution license:  
(1) Manufacturing or distributing firearms in excess of the quota or not in accordance with the prescribed types;  
(2) Manufacturing firearms without a serial number, with a duplicate serial number or with a false serial number;  
(3) Selling firearms privately or selling firearms manufactured for export within the country.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本法规定，非法持有、私藏枪支的，非法运输、携带枪支入境、出境的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 41** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, illegally possesses or conceals firearms, or illegally transports or brings firearms into or out of the country, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本法规定，运输枪支未使用安全可靠的运输设备、不设专人押运、枪支弹药未分开运输或者运输途中停留住宿不报告公安机关，情节严重的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任；未构成犯罪的，由公安机关对直接责任人员处十五日以下拘留。

**Article 42** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, transports firearms without using safe and reliable transport equipment, without assigning special escorts, without separating the transportation of firearms and ammunition, or fails to report to the public security organ when making a stop or overnight stay during transportation, if the circumstances are serious, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose on the person directly responsible a detention of not more than 15 days.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　违反枪支管理规定，出租、出借公务用枪的，依照刑法有关规定处罚。  
单位有前款行为的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照前款规定处罚。  
配置民用枪支的单位，违反枪支管理规定，出租、出借枪支，造成严重后果或者有其他严重情节的，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法有关规定处罚。  
配置民用枪支的个人，违反枪支管理规定，出租、出借枪支，造成严重后果的，依照刑法有关规定处罚。  
违反枪支管理规定，出租、出借枪支，情节轻微未构成犯罪的，由公安机关对个人或者单位负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处十五日以下拘留，可以并处五千元以下罚款；对出租、出借的枪支，应当予以没收。

**Article 43** Whoever, in violation of the firearms control provisions, leases or lends firearms for official use shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where a unit commits the act mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
Where a unit configured with civilian firearms, in violation of the firearms control provisions, leases or lends firearms, causing serious consequences or if there are other serious circumstances, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where an individual configured with civilian firearms, in violation of the firearms control provisions, leases or lends firearms, causing serious consequences, he shall be punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
Where the act of leasing or lending firearms in violation of the firearms control provisions is minor and does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose on the individual or on the person directly in charge of the unit and other persons directly responsible a detention of not more than 15 days, and may also impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan; the firearms that are leased or lent shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由公安机关对个人或者单位负有直接责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处警告或者十五日以下拘留；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未按照规定的技术标准制造民用枪支的；  
（二）在禁止携带枪支的区域、场所携带枪支的；  
（三）不上缴报废枪支的；  
（四）枪支被盗、被抢或者丢失，不及时报告的；  
（五）制造、销售仿真枪的。  
有前款第（一）项至第（三）项所列行为的，没收其枪支，可以并处五千元以下罚款；有前款第（五）项所列行为的，由公安机关、工商行政管理部门按照各自职责范围没收其仿真枪，可以并处制造、销售金额五倍以下的罚款，情节严重的，由工商行政管理部门吊销营业执照。

**Article 44** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits one of the following acts shall be given a warning or be detained for not more than 15 days by the public security organ, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Manufacturing civilian firearms not in accordance with the prescribed technical standards;  
(2) Carrying firearms in an area or place where carrying firearms is prohibited;  
(3) Failing to surrender scrapped firearms;  
(4) Failing to report promptly when a firearm is stolen, robbed or lost;  
(5) Manufacturing or selling imitation firearms.  
Where a person commits any of the acts listed in sub-paragraphs (1) to (3) of the preceding paragraph, the firearms shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan may also be imposed; where a person commits the act listed in sub-paragraph (5) of the preceding paragraph, the public security organ or the administrative department for industry and commerce shall, within its respective scope of functions and duties, confiscate the imitation firearms, and may also impose a fine of not more than five times the value of the manufactured or sold imitation firearms; if the circumstances are serious, the administrative department for industry and commerce shall revoke the business license.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　公安机关工作人员有下列行为之一的，依法追究刑事责任；未构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分：  
（一）向[本法第五条](#art-5)、第六条规定以外的单位和个人配备、配置枪支的；  
（二）违法发给枪支管理证件的；  
（三）将没收的枪支据为己有的；  
（四）不履行枪支管理职责，造成后果的。

**Article 45** Where a staff member of a public security organ commits one of the following acts, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be given according to law:  
(1) Equipping or configuring firearms to units or individuals other than those provided for in Articles 5 and 6 of this Law;  
(2) Illegally issuing firearms control certificates;  
(3) Appropriating confiscated firearms;  
(4) Failing to perform the duty of firearms control, causing consequences.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本法所称枪支，是指以火药或者压缩气体等为动力，利用管状器具发射金属弹丸或者其他物质，足以致人伤亡或者丧失知觉的各种枪支。

**Article 46** For the purposes of this Law, "firearms" means all kinds of guns that use gunpowder or compressed gas as propellant and use tubular devices to launch metal projectiles or other substances, and are capable of causing casualties or loss of consciousness.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　单位和个人为开展游艺活动，可以配置口径不超过4.5毫米的气步枪。具体管理办法由国务院公安部门制定。  
制作影视剧使用的道具枪支的管理办法，由国务院公安部门会同国务院广播电影电视行政主管部门制定。  
博物馆、纪念馆、展览馆保存或者展览枪支的管理办法，由国务院公安部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门制定。

**Article 47** Units and individuals may be equipped with air rifles with a caliber not exceeding 4.5 mm for recreational activities. The specific control measures shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council.  
The control measures for prop guns used in the production of films and television dramas shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council together with the competent administrative department of radio, film and television under the State Council.  
The control measures for firearms preserved or exhibited by museums, memorial halls and exhibition halls shall be formulated by the public security department under the State Council together with the relevant competent administrative departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　制造、配售、运输枪支的主要零部件和用于枪支的弹药，适用本法的有关规定。

**Article 48** The manufacture, distribution and transportation of main parts of firearms and ammunition for firearms shall be governed by the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　枪支管理证件由国务院公安部门制定。

**Article 49** The forms of firearms control certificates shall be prescribed by the public security department under the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自1996年10月1日起施行。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 1996.
