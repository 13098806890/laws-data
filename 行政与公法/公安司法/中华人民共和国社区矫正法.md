# 中华人民共和国社区矫正法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-12-28  
**生效日期**：2020-07-01

> （2019年12月28日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　机构、人员和职责 第三章　决定和接收 第四章　监督管理 第五章　教育帮扶 第六章　解除和终止 第七章　未成年人社区矫正特别规定 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了推进和规范社区矫正工作，保障刑事判决、刑事裁定和暂予监外执行决定的正确执行，提高教育矫正质量，促进社区矫正对象顺利融入社会，预防和减少犯罪，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　对被判处管制、宣告缓刑、假释和暂予监外执行的罪犯，依法实行社区矫正。 对社区矫正对象的监督管理、教育帮扶等活动，适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了推进和规范社区矫正工作，保障刑事判决、刑事裁定和暂予监外执行决定的正确执行，提高教育矫正质量，促进社区矫正对象顺利融入社会，预防和减少犯罪，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution with a view to advancing and regulating community correction work, ensuring the correct enforcement of criminal judgments, criminal rulings, and decisions on temporary service of sentence outside prison, improving the quality of education and correction, facilitating the smooth integration of community correction recipients into society, and preventing and reducing crimes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对被判处管制、宣告缓刑、假释和暂予监外执行的罪犯，依法实行社区矫正。  
对社区矫正对象的监督管理、教育帮扶等活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** Community correction shall be applied according to law to criminals who are sentenced to public surveillance, granted probation, released on parole, or allowed to temporarily serve sentence outside prison.  
The supervision, management, education, assistance, and other activities concerning community correction recipients shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　社区矫正工作坚持监督管理与教育帮扶相结合，专门机关与社会力量相结合，采取分类管理、个别化矫正，有针对性地消除社区矫正对象可能重新犯罪的因素，帮助其成为守法公民。

**Article 3** Community correction work shall adhere to the combination of supervision and management with education and assistance, and the combination of specialized agencies with social forces, adopt classified management and individualized correction, and target the elimination of factors that may lead community correction recipients to re-offend, so as to help them become law-abiding citizens.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　社区矫正对象应当依法接受社区矫正，服从监督管理。  
社区矫正工作应当依法进行，尊重和保障人权。社区矫正对象依法享有的人身权利、财产权利和其他权利不受侵犯，在就业、就学和享受社会保障等方面不受歧视。

**Article 4** Community correction recipients shall accept community correction according to law and submit to supervision and management.  
Community correction work shall be carried out according to law, respecting and safeguarding human rights. The personal rights, property rights, and other rights enjoyed by community correction recipients according to law shall not be infringed upon, and they shall not be discriminated against in employment, education, access to social security, and other aspects.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家支持社区矫正机构提高信息化水平，运用现代信息技术开展监督管理和教育帮扶。社区矫正工作相关部门之间依法进行信息共享。

**Article 5** The State supports community correction institutions in raising the level of informatization and using modern information technology to carry out supervision, management, education, and assistance. Relevant departments for community correction work shall share information with each other according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当将社区矫正经费列入本级政府预算。  
居民委员会、村民委员会和其他社会组织依法协助社区矫正机构开展工作所需的经费应当按照规定列入社区矫正机构本级政府预算。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall include community correction expenses in their respective government budgets.  
The expenses necessary for residents' committees, villagers' committees, and other social organizations to assist community correction institutions in carrying out their work according to law shall be included in the budget of the government at the same level as the community correction institution in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对在社区矫正工作中做出突出贡献的组织、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions to community correction work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant State regulations.

### 第二章　机构、人员和职责

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院司法行政部门主管全国的社区矫正工作。县级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门主管本行政区域内的社区矫正工作。  
人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关和其他有关部门依照各自职责，依法做好社区矫正工作。人民检察院依法对社区矫正工作实行法律监督。  
地方人民政府根据需要设立社区矫正委员会，负责统筹协调和指导本行政区域内的社区矫正工作。

**Article 8** The judicial administration department of the State Council shall be in charge of community correction work throughout the country. The judicial administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of community correction work within their respective administrative regions.  
The People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, public security organs, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly carry out community correction work according to law. The People's Procuratorates shall exercise legal supervision over community correction work according to law.  
Local people's governments may, as necessary, establish community correction committees to be responsible for the overall coordination and guidance of community correction work within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　县级以上地方人民政府根据需要设置社区矫正机构，负责社区矫正工作的具体实施。社区矫正机构的设置和撤销，由县级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门提出意见，按照规定的权限和程序审批。  
司法所根据社区矫正机构的委托，承担社区矫正相关工作。

**Article 9** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, as necessary, establish community correction institutions to be responsible for the specific implementation of community correction work. The establishment and abolition of community correction institutions shall be proposed by the judicial administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level and approved in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures.  
Judicial offices shall, upon entrustment by community correction institutions, undertake relevant community correction work.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　社区矫正机构应当配备具有法律等专业知识的专门国家工作人员（以下称社区矫正机构工作人员），履行监督管理、教育帮扶等执法职责。

**Article 10** Community correction institutions shall be staffed with specialized State personnel with professional knowledge in law and other fields (hereinafter referred to as community correction institution staff), who shall perform law enforcement duties such as supervision, management, education, and assistance.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　社区矫正机构根据需要，组织具有法律、教育、心理、社会工作等专业知识或者实践经验的社会工作者开展社区矫正相关工作。

**Article 11** Community correction institutions may, as necessary, organize social workers with professional knowledge or practical experience in law, education, psychology, social work, and other fields to carry out relevant community correction work.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　居民委员会、村民委员会依法协助社区矫正机构做好社区矫正工作。  
社区矫正对象的监护人、家庭成员，所在单位或者就读学校应当协助社区矫正机构做好社区矫正工作。

**Article 12** Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall, according to law, assist community correction institutions in properly carrying out community correction work.  
The guardians, family members, employers, or schools of community correction recipients shall assist community correction institutions in properly carrying out community correction work.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励、支持企业事业单位、社会组织、志愿者等社会力量依法参与社区矫正工作。

**Article 13** The State encourages and supports enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, volunteers, and other social forces to participate in community correction work according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　社区矫正机构工作人员应当严格遵守宪法和法律，忠于职守，严守纪律，清正廉洁。

**Article 14** Community correction institution staff shall strictly abide by the Constitution and laws, be devoted to their duties, strictly observe discipline, and be honest and clean.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　社区矫正机构工作人员和其他参与社区矫正工作的人员依法开展社区矫正工作，受法律保护。

**Article 15** Community correction institution staff and other personnel participating in community correction work shall carry out community correction work according to law and shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家推进高素质的社区矫正工作队伍建设。社区矫正机构应当加强对社区矫正工作人员的管理、监督、培训和职业保障，不断提高社区矫正工作的规范化、专业化水平。

**Article 16** The State shall promote the building of a high-quality community correction workforce. Community correction institutions shall strengthen the management, supervision, training, and occupational protection of community correction workers, and continuously improve the standardization and specialization of community correction work.

### 第三章　决定和接收

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　社区矫正决定机关判处管制、宣告缓刑、裁定假释、决定或者批准暂予监外执行时应当确定社区矫正执行地。  
社区矫正执行地为社区矫正对象的居住地。社区矫正对象在多个地方居住的，可以确定经常居住地为执行地。  
社区矫正对象的居住地、经常居住地无法确定或者不适宜执行社区矫正的，社区矫正决定机关应当根据有利于社区矫正对象接受矫正、更好地融入社会的原则，确定执行地。  
本法所称社区矫正决定机关，是指依法判处管制、宣告缓刑、裁定假释、决定暂予监外执行的人民法院和依法批准暂予监外执行的监狱管理机关、公安机关。

**Article 17** When imposing public surveillance, granting probation, ruling parole, or deciding or approving temporary service of sentence outside prison, a community correction decision-making authority shall determine the place of execution of community correction.  
The place of execution of community correction shall be the place of residence of the community correction recipient. If the community correction recipient resides in multiple places, the place of habitual residence may be determined as the place of execution.  
If the place of residence or habitual residence of the community correction recipient cannot be determined or is not suitable for community correction, the community correction decision-making authority shall determine the place of execution based on the principle of facilitating the community correction recipient's acceptance of correction and better integration into society.  
For the purposes of this Law, "community correction decision-making authority" refers to the People's Court that imposes public surveillance, grants probation, rules parole, or decides temporary service of sentence outside prison according to law, and the prison administration authority or public security organ that approves temporary service of sentence outside prison according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　社区矫正决定机关根据需要，可以委托社区矫正机构或者有关社会组织对被告人或者罪犯的社会危险性和对所居住社区的影响，进行调查评估，提出意见，供决定社区矫正时参考。居民委员会、村民委员会等组织应当提供必要的协助。

**Article 18** The community correction decision-making authority may, as necessary, entrust a community correction institution or relevant social organization to investigate and assess the social dangerousness of the defendant or criminal and the impact on the community where they reside, and provide opinions for reference in deciding community correction. Residents' committees, villagers' committees, and other organizations shall provide necessary assistance.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　社区矫正决定机关判处管制、宣告缓刑、裁定假释、决定或者批准暂予监外执行，应当按照刑法、刑事诉讼法等法律规定的条件和程序进行。  
社区矫正决定机关应当对社区矫正对象进行教育，告知其在社区矫正期间应当遵守的规定以及违反规定的法律后果，责令其按时报到。

**Article 19** The community correction decision-making authority shall impose public surveillance, grant probation, rule parole, decide or approve temporary service of sentence outside prison in accordance with the conditions and procedures prescribed by the Criminal Law, Criminal Procedure Law, and other laws.  
The community correction decision-making authority shall educate the community correction recipient, inform him/her of the rules to be observed during community correction and the legal consequences of violating such rules, and order him/her to report on time.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　社区矫正决定机关应当自判决、裁定或者决定生效之日起五日内通知执行地社区矫正机构，并在十日内送达有关法律文书，同时抄送人民检察院和执行地公安机关。社区矫正决定地与执行地不在同一地方的，由执行地社区矫正机构将法律文书转送所在地的人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 20** The community correction decision-making authority shall, within five days from the date the judgment, ruling, or decision takes effect, notify the community correction institution at the place of execution, and within ten days, serve the relevant legal documents, while simultaneously copying them to the People's Procuratorate and the public security organ at the place of execution. If the place where community correction is decided is not the same as the place of execution, the community correction institution at the place of execution shall forward the legal documents to the local People's Procuratorate and public security organ.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院判处管制、宣告缓刑、裁定假释的社区矫正对象，应当自判决、裁定生效之日起十日内到执行地社区矫正机构报到。  
人民法院决定暂予监外执行的社区矫正对象，由看守所或者执行取保候审、监视居住的公安机关自收到决定之日起十日内将社区矫正对象移送社区矫正机构。  
监狱管理机关、公安机关批准暂予监外执行的社区矫正对象，由监狱或者看守所自收到批准决定之日起十日内将社区矫正对象移送社区矫正机构。

**Article 21** A community correction recipient who is sentenced to public surveillance, granted probation, or ruled parole by a People's Court shall report to the community correction institution at the place of execution within ten days from the date the judgment or ruling takes effect.  
For a community correction recipient whose temporary service of sentence outside prison is decided by a People's Court, the detention house or the public security organ executing release on bail pending investigation or residential surveillance shall transfer the community correction recipient to the community correction institution within ten days from the date of receiving the decision.  
For a community correction recipient whose temporary service of sentence outside prison is approved by a prison administration authority or public security organ, the prison or detention house shall transfer the community correction recipient to the community correction institution within ten days from the date of receiving the approval decision.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　社区矫正机构应当依法接收社区矫正对象，核对法律文书、核实身份、办理接收登记、建立档案，并宣告社区矫正对象的犯罪事实、执行社区矫正的期限以及应当遵守的规定。

**Article 22** A community correction institution shall accept community correction recipients according to law, verify the legal documents, confirm identities, handle acceptance registration, establish files, and announce the criminal facts of the community correction recipient, the term of community correction, and the rules to be observed.

### 第四章　监督管理

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　社区矫正对象在社区矫正期间应当遵守法律、行政法规，履行判决、裁定、暂予监外执行决定等法律文书确定的义务，遵守国务院司法行政部门关于报告、会客、外出、迁居、保外就医等监督管理规定，服从社区矫正机构的管理。

**Article 23** During community correction, community correction recipients shall abide by laws and administrative regulations, fulfill the obligations specified in legal documents such as judgments, rulings, and decisions on temporary service of sentence outside prison, comply with the supervision and management regulations of the judicial administration department of the State Council concerning reporting, meeting visitors, leaving the area, changing residence, medical release, and other matters, and submit to the management of the community correction institution.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　社区矫正机构应当根据裁判内容和社区矫正对象的性别、年龄、心理特点、健康状况、犯罪原因、犯罪类型、犯罪情节、悔罪表现等情况，制定有针对性的矫正方案，实现分类管理、个别化矫正。矫正方案应当根据社区矫正对象的表现等情况相应调整。

**Article 24** A community correction institution shall formulate targeted correction plans based on the content of the judgment and the gender, age, psychological characteristics, health status, causes of crime, type of crime, circumstances of crime, and repentance of the community correction recipient, so as to achieve classified management and individualized correction. The correction plan shall be adjusted accordingly based on the performance of the community correction recipient and other circumstances.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　社区矫正机构应当根据社区矫正对象的情况，为其确定矫正小组，负责落实相应的矫正方案。  
根据需要，矫正小组可以由司法所、居民委员会、村民委员会的人员，社区矫正对象的监护人、家庭成员，所在单位或者就读学校的人员以及社会工作者、志愿者等组成。社区矫正对象为女性的，矫正小组中应有女性成员。

**Article 25** A community correction institution shall, based on the circumstances of the community correction recipient, designate a correction team to be responsible for implementing the corresponding correction plan.  
As necessary, the correction team may be composed of personnel from judicial offices, residents' committees, or villagers' committees; the guardian, family members, or personnel from the employer or school of the community correction recipient; as well as social workers and volunteers. If the community correction recipient is female, the correction team shall include female members.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　社区矫正机构应当了解掌握社区矫正对象的活动情况和行为表现。社区矫正机构可以通过通信联络、信息化核查、实地查访等方式核实有关情况，有关单位和个人应当予以配合。  
社区矫正机构开展实地查访等工作时，应当保护社区矫正对象的身份信息和个人隐私。

**Article 26** A community correction institution shall keep informed of the activities and behavior of community correction recipients. The community correction institution may verify relevant information through communication, digital checks, field visits, and other methods, and the relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.  
When conducting field visits and other work, the community correction institution shall protect the identity information and personal privacy of community correction recipients.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　社区矫正对象离开所居住的市、县或者迁居，应当报经社区矫正机构批准。社区矫正机构对于有正当理由的，应当批准；对于因正常工作和生活需要经常性跨市、县活动的，可以根据情况，简化批准程序和方式。  
因社区矫正对象迁居等原因需要变更执行地的，社区矫正机构应当按照有关规定作出变更决定。社区矫正机构作出变更决定后，应当通知社区矫正决定机关和变更后的社区矫正机构，并将有关法律文书抄送变更后的社区矫正机构。变更后的社区矫正机构应当将法律文书转送所在地的人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 27** If a community correction recipient needs to leave the city or county where he/she resides or change his/her residence, he/she shall report to and obtain approval from the community correction institution. The community correction institution shall grant approval if there is a legitimate reason; for frequent cross-city or cross-county activities due to normal work and life needs, it may simplify the approval procedures and methods based on the circumstances.  
If the place of execution needs to be changed due to the change of residence of the community correction recipient or other reasons, the community correction institution shall make a decision on the change in accordance with relevant regulations. After making the change decision, the community correction institution shall notify the community correction decision-making authority and the community correction institution at the new place of execution, and send copies of the relevant legal documents to the community correction institution at the new place of execution. The community correction institution at the new place of execution shall forward the legal documents to the local People's Procuratorate and public security organ.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　社区矫正机构根据社区矫正对象的表现，依照有关规定对其实施考核奖惩。社区矫正对象认罪悔罪、遵守法律法规、服从监督管理、接受教育表现突出的，应当给予表扬。社区矫正对象违反法律法规或者监督管理规定的，应当视情节依法给予训诫、警告、提请公安机关予以治安管理处罚，或者依法提请撤销缓刑、撤销假释、对暂予监外执行的收监执行。  
对社区矫正对象的考核结果，可以作为认定其是否确有悔改表现或者是否严重违反监督管理规定的依据。

**Article 28** A community correction institution shall, based on the performance of the community correction recipient and in accordance with relevant regulations, conduct assessment and impose rewards or sanctions. If a community correction recipient admits guilt and shows repentance, observes laws and regulations, submits to supervision and management, and demonstrates outstanding performance in receiving education, he/she shall be given a commendation. If a community correction recipient violates laws, regulations, or supervision and management rules, he/she shall, depending on the circumstances, be given a reprimand, a warning, be reported to the public security organ for a public security penalty, or be reported according to law for revocation of probation, revocation of parole, or execution of the original sentence for those on temporary service of sentence outside prison.  
The assessment results of a community correction recipient may serve as a basis for determining whether he/she truly shows repentance or has seriously violated supervision and management rules.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　社区矫正对象有下列情形之一的，经县级司法行政部门负责人批准，可以使用电子定位装置，加强监督管理：  
（一）违反人民法院禁止令的；  
（二）无正当理由，未经批准离开所居住的市、县的；  
（三）拒不按照规定报告自己的活动情况，被给予警告的；  
（四）违反监督管理规定，被给予治安管理处罚的；  
（五）拟提请撤销缓刑、假释或者暂予监外执行收监执行的。  
前款规定的使用电子定位装置的期限不得超过三个月。对于不需要继续使用的，应当及时解除；对于期限届满后，经评估仍有必要继续使用的，经过批准，期限可以延长，每次不得超过三个月。  
社区矫正机构对通过电子定位装置获得的信息应当严格保密，有关信息只能用于社区矫正工作，不得用于其他用途。

**Article 29** In any of the following circumstances, with the approval of the principal of the county-level judicial administration department, an electronic positioning device may be used to strengthen supervision and management:  
(1) violating a prohibition order of a People's Court;  
(2) leaving the city or county of residence without approval without a legitimate reason;  
(3) refusing to report his/her activities in accordance with regulations and having been given a warning;  
(4) having been given a public security penalty for violating supervision and management rules;  
(5) being proposed for revocation of probation, revocation of parole, or execution of the original sentence for temporary service of sentence outside prison.  
The period for using an electronic positioning device as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall not exceed three months. If continued use is no longer necessary, the device shall be promptly removed; if, after the expiration of the period, it is assessed that continued use is still necessary, the period may be extended upon approval, each time not exceeding three months.  
A community correction institution shall strictly keep confidential the information obtained through the electronic positioning device, and such information may only be used for community correction work and shall not be used for other purposes.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　社区矫正对象失去联系的，社区矫正机构应当立即组织查找，公安机关等有关单位和人员应当予以配合协助。查找到社区矫正对象后，应当区别情形依法作出处理。

**Article 30** If a community correction recipient loses contact, the community correction institution shall immediately organize a search, and the public security organ and other relevant entities and personnel shall cooperate and provide assistance. After the community correction recipient is found, the matter shall be handled according to law depending on the circumstances.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　社区矫正机构发现社区矫正对象正在实施违反监督管理规定的行为或者违反人民法院禁止令等违法行为的，应当立即制止；制止无效的，应当立即通知公安机关到场处置。

**Article 31** If a community correction institution discovers that a community correction recipient is committing acts that violate supervision and management rules or violate a prohibition order of a People's Court or other illegal acts, it shall immediately stop such acts; if the stopping is ineffective, it shall immediately notify the public security organ to come to the scene and handle the matter.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　社区矫正对象有被依法决定拘留、强制隔离戒毒、采取刑事强制措施等限制人身自由情形的，有关机关应当及时通知社区矫正机构。

**Article 32** If a community correction recipient is in a situation where he/she is subject to restrictions on personal freedom, such as being decided for detention, compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, or the adoption of criminal compulsory measures, the relevant authority shall promptly notify the community correction institution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　社区矫正对象符合刑法规定的减刑条件的，社区矫正机构应当向社区矫正执行地的中级以上人民法院提出减刑建议，并将减刑建议书抄送同级人民检察院。  
人民法院应当在收到社区矫正机构的减刑建议书后三十日内作出裁定，并将裁定书送达社区矫正机构，同时抄送人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 33** If a community correction recipient meets the conditions for commutation of punishment prescribed by the Criminal Law, the community correction institution shall submit a commutation proposal to the intermediate People's Court or higher at the place of execution of community correction, and send a copy of the commutation proposal to the People's Procuratorate at the same level.  
The People's Court shall, within thirty days of receiving the commutation proposal from the community correction institution, render a ruling and serve the ruling on the community correction institution, while simultaneously sending copies to the People's Procuratorate and the public security organ.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　开展社区矫正工作，应当保障社区矫正对象的合法权益。社区矫正的措施和方法应当避免对社区矫正对象的正常工作和生活造成不必要的影响；非依法律规定，不得限制或者变相限制社区矫正对象的人身自由。  
社区矫正对象认为其合法权益受到侵害的，有权向人民检察院或者有关机关申诉、控告和检举。受理机关应当及时办理，并将办理结果告知申诉人、控告人和检举人。

**Article 34** In carrying out community correction work, the lawful rights and interests of community correction recipients shall be protected. Measures and methods of community correction shall avoid causing unnecessary impact on the normal work and life of community correction recipients; personal freedom of community correction recipients shall not be restricted or indirectly restricted except in accordance with law.  
If a community correction recipient believes that his/her lawful rights and interests have been infringed upon, he/she has the right to petition, file a complaint, or report to the People's Procuratorate or relevant authorities. The receiving authority shall handle the matter promptly and inform the petitioner, complainant, or reporter of the result.

### 第五章　教育帮扶

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当通过多种形式为教育帮扶社区矫正对象提供必要的场所和条件，组织动员社会力量参与教育帮扶工作。  
有关人民团体应当依法协助社区矫正机构做好教育帮扶工作。

**Article 35** Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, through various forms, provide necessary premises and conditions for the education and assistance of community correction recipients, and organize and mobilize social forces to participate in education and assistance work.  
Relevant people's organizations shall, according to law, assist community correction institutions in properly carrying out education and assistance work.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　社区矫正机构根据需要，对社区矫正对象进行法治、道德等教育，增强其法治观念，提高其道德素质和悔罪意识。  
对社区矫正对象的教育应当根据其个体特征、日常表现等实际情况，充分考虑其工作和生活情况，因人施教。

**Article 36** A community correction institution shall, as necessary, provide education on the rule of law, morality, and other aspects to community correction recipients, strengthen their awareness of the rule of law, and improve their moral quality and sense of guilt.  
Education for community correction recipients shall be tailored to individual characteristics, daily performance, and other actual circumstances, taking full account of their work and life conditions, and be taught according to their aptitude.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　社区矫正机构可以协调有关部门和单位，依法对就业困难的社区矫正对象开展职业技能培训、就业指导，帮助社区矫正对象中的在校学生完成学业。

**Article 37** A community correction institution may coordinate with relevant departments and entities to carry out vocational skills training and employment guidance according to law for community correction recipients who have difficulty in finding employment, and help school students among community correction recipients complete their studies.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　居民委员会、村民委员会可以引导志愿者和社区群众，利用社区资源，采取多种形式，对有特殊困难的社区矫正对象进行必要的教育帮扶。

**Article 38** Residents' committees and villagers' committees may guide volunteers and community members, use community resources, and adopt various forms to provide necessary education and assistance to community correction recipients with special difficulties.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　社区矫正对象的监护人、家庭成员，所在单位或者就读学校应当协助社区矫正机构做好对社区矫正对象的教育。

**Article 39** The guardians, family members, employers, or schools of community correction recipients shall assist community correction institutions in properly carrying out education for community correction recipients.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　社区矫正机构可以通过公开择优购买社区矫正社会工作服务或者其他社会服务，为社区矫正对象在教育、心理辅导、职业技能培训、社会关系改善等方面提供必要的帮扶。  
社区矫正机构也可以通过项目委托社会组织等方式开展上述帮扶活动。国家鼓励有经验和资源的社会组织跨地区开展帮扶交流和示范活动。

**Article 40** A community correction institution may purchase community correction social work services or other social services through open and merit-based selection, so as to provide necessary assistance to community correction recipients in education, psychological counseling, vocational skills training, improvement of social relations, and other aspects.  
A community correction institution may also carry out the above-mentioned assistance activities through project commissioning to social organizations and other means. The State encourages social organizations with experience and resources to carry out cross-regional assistance exchange and demonstration activities.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家鼓励企业事业单位、社会组织为社区矫正对象提供就业岗位和职业技能培训。招用符合条件的社区矫正对象的企业，按照规定享受国家优惠政策。

**Article 41** The State encourages enterprises, public institutions, and social organizations to provide job positions and vocational skills training for community correction recipients. Enterprises that employ eligible community correction recipients shall enjoy national preferential policies in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　社区矫正机构可以根据社区矫正对象的个人特长，组织其参加公益活动，修复社会关系，培养社会责任感。

**Article 42** A community correction institution may, based on the personal strengths of the community correction recipient, organize him/her to participate in public welfare activities to repair social relations and cultivate a sense of social responsibility.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　社区矫正对象可以按照国家有关规定申请社会救助、参加社会保险、获得法律援助，社区矫正机构应当给予必要的协助。

**Article 43** A community correction recipient may apply for social assistance, participate in social insurance, and obtain legal aid in accordance with relevant State regulations, and the community correction institution shall provide necessary assistance.

### 第六章　解除和终止

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　社区矫正对象矫正期满或者被赦免的，社区矫正机构应当向社区矫正对象发放解除社区矫正证明书，并通知社区矫正决定机关、所在地的人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 44** If a community correction recipient completes the correction period or is pardoned, the community correction institution shall issue a certificate of removal from community correction to the community correction recipient, and notify the community correction decision-making authority and the local People's Procuratorate and public security organ.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　社区矫正对象被裁定撤销缓刑、假释，被决定收监执行，或者社区矫正对象死亡的，社区矫正终止。

**Article 45** Community correction shall terminate if the community correction recipient is ruled to have his/her probation or parole revoked, is decided to be taken back into custody for execution, or if the community correction recipient dies.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　社区矫正对象具有刑法规定的撤销缓刑、假释情形的，应当由人民法院撤销缓刑、假释。  
对于在考验期限内犯新罪或者发现判决宣告以前还有其他罪没有判决的，应当由审理该案件的人民法院撤销缓刑、假释，并书面通知原审人民法院和执行地社区矫正机构。  
对于有第二款规定以外的其他需要撤销缓刑、假释情形的，社区矫正机构应当向原审人民法院或者执行地人民法院提出撤销缓刑、假释建议，并将建议书抄送人民检察院。社区矫正机构提出撤销缓刑、假释建议时，应当说明理由，并提供有关证据材料。

**Article 46** If a community correction recipient has circumstances that require revocation of probation or parole as prescribed by the Criminal Law, the People's Court shall revoke the probation or parole.  
If a new crime is committed during the probation or parole period, or if it is discovered that there is another crime for which no judgment has been made before the pronouncement of the judgment, the People's Court hearing the case shall revoke the probation or parole and notify in writing the original trial court and the community correction institution at the place of execution.  
For circumstances other than those specified in paragraph 2 that require revocation of probation or parole, the community correction institution shall submit a proposal for revocation of probation or parole to the original trial court or the People's Court at the place of execution, and send a copy of the proposal to the People's Procuratorate. When submitting a proposal for revocation of probation or parole, the community correction institution shall state the reasons and provide relevant evidence.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　被提请撤销缓刑、假释的社区矫正对象可能逃跑或者可能发生社会危险的，社区矫正机构可以在提出撤销缓刑、假释建议的同时，提请人民法院决定对其予以逮捕。  
人民法院应当在四十八小时内作出是否逮捕的决定。决定逮捕的，由公安机关执行。逮捕后的羁押期限不得超过三十日。

**Article 47** If a community correction recipient who is proposed for revocation of probation or parole may abscond or may pose a social danger, the community correction institution may, at the same time as submitting the proposal for revocation of probation or parole, request the People's Court to decide on his/her arrest.  
The People's Court shall decide whether to arrest within forty-eight hours. If a decision is made to arrest, the public security organ shall enforce it. The period of custody after arrest shall not exceed thirty days.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　人民法院应当在收到社区矫正机构撤销缓刑、假释建议书后三十日内作出裁定，将裁定书送达社区矫正机构和公安机关，并抄送人民检察院。  
人民法院拟撤销缓刑、假释的，应当听取社区矫正对象的申辩及其委托的律师的意见。  
人民法院裁定撤销缓刑、假释的，公安机关应当及时将社区矫正对象送交监狱或者看守所执行。执行以前被逮捕的，羁押一日折抵刑期一日。  
人民法院裁定不予撤销缓刑、假释的，对被逮捕的社区矫正对象，公安机关应当立即予以释放。

**Article 48** The People's Court shall, within thirty days of receiving the proposal for revocation of probation or parole from the community correction institution, render a ruling, serve the ruling on the community correction institution and the public security organ, and send a copy to the People's Procuratorate.  
If the People's Court intends to revoke the probation or parole, it shall hear the defense of the community correction recipient and the opinions of the lawyer entrusted by him/her.  
If the People's Court rules to revoke the probation or parole, the public security organ shall promptly deliver the community correction recipient to a prison or detention house for execution. If the community correction recipient had been arrested before execution, one day in custody shall be counted as one day of the sentence.  
If the People's Court rules not to revoke the probation or parole, the public security organ shall immediately release the community correction recipient who has been arrested.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　暂予监外执行的社区矫正对象具有刑事诉讼法规定的应当予以收监情形的，社区矫正机构应当向执行地或者原社区矫正决定机关提出收监执行建议，并将建议书抄送人民检察院。  
社区矫正决定机关应当在收到建议书后三十日内作出决定，将决定书送达社区矫正机构和公安机关，并抄送人民检察院。  
人民法院、公安机关对暂予监外执行的社区矫正对象决定收监执行的，由公安机关立即将社区矫正对象送交监狱或者看守所收监执行。  
监狱管理机关对暂予监外执行的社区矫正对象决定收监执行的，监狱应当立即将社区矫正对象收监执行。

**Article 49** If a community correction recipient on temporary service of sentence outside prison has circumstances that require execution of the original sentence as prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law, the community correction institution shall submit a proposal for execution of the original sentence to the place of execution or the original community correction decision-making authority, and send a copy of the proposal to the People's Procuratorate.  
The community correction decision-making authority shall, within thirty days of receiving the proposal, make a decision, serve the decision on the community correction institution and the public security organ, and send a copy to the People's Procuratorate.  
If a People's Court or a public security organ decides to execute the original sentence for a community correction recipient on temporary service of sentence outside prison, the public security organ shall immediately deliver the community correction recipient to a prison or detention house for execution.  
If a prison administration authority decides to execute the original sentence for a community correction recipient on temporary service of sentence outside prison, the prison shall immediately execute the original sentence on the community correction recipient.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　被裁定撤销缓刑、假释和被决定收监执行的社区矫正对象逃跑的，由公安机关追捕，社区矫正机构、有关单位和个人予以协助。

**Article 50** If a community correction recipient whose probation or parole has been revoked by ruling or who has been decided to be taken back into custody escapes, the public security organ shall pursue and capture him/her, and the community correction institution, relevant entities, and individuals shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　社区矫正对象在社区矫正期间死亡的，其监护人、家庭成员应当及时向社区矫正机构报告。社区矫正机构应当及时通知社区矫正决定机关、所在地的人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 51** Where a community corrections object dies during community corrections, his or her guardian or family member shall promptly report to the community corrections institution. The community corrections institution shall promptly notify the community corrections decision-making organ, the local People's Procuratorate, and the public security organ.

### 第七章　未成年人社区矫正特别规定

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　社区矫正机构应当根据未成年社区矫正对象的年龄、心理特点、发育需要、成长经历、犯罪原因、家庭监护教育条件等情况，采取针对性的矫正措施。  
社区矫正机构为未成年社区矫正对象确定矫正小组，应当吸收熟悉未成年人身心特点的人员参加。  
对未成年人的社区矫正，应当与成年人分别进行。

**Article 52** The community corrections institution shall, based on the age, psychological characteristics, developmental needs, growth experience, causes of crime, and family guardianship and education conditions of a minor community corrections object, adopt targeted correction measures.  
The community corrections institution, when determining a correction team for a minor community corrections object, shall include personnel familiar with the physical and psychological characteristics of minors.  
Community corrections for minors shall be conducted separately from adults.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　未成年社区矫正对象的监护人应当履行监护责任，承担抚养、管教等义务。  
监护人怠于履行监护职责的，社区矫正机构应当督促、教育其履行监护责任。监护人拒不履行监护职责的，通知有关部门依法作出处理。

**Article 53** The guardian of a minor community corrections object shall fulfill guardianship duties and assume obligations such as upbringing and discipline.  
If the guardian neglects to perform guardianship duties, the community corrections institution shall urge and educate the guardian to fulfill the guardianship responsibility. If the guardian refuses to perform guardianship duties, the community corrections institution shall notify the relevant departments to handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　社区矫正机构工作人员和其他依法参与社区矫正工作的人员对履行职责过程中获得的未成年人身份信息应当予以保密。  
除司法机关办案需要或者有关单位根据国家规定查询外，未成年社区矫正对象的档案信息不得提供给任何单位或者个人。依法进行查询的单位，应当对获得的信息予以保密。

**Article 54** The staff of the community corrections institution and other persons who participate in community corrections work according to law shall keep confidential the identity information of minors obtained in the course of performing their duties.  
Except for the needs of judicial authorities in handling cases or for inquiries by relevant units in accordance with state provisions, the file information of minor community corrections objects shall not be provided to any unit or individual. Units making inquiries according to law shall keep the obtained information confidential.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对未完成义务教育的未成年社区矫正对象，社区矫正机构应当通知并配合教育部门为其完成义务教育提供条件。未成年社区矫正对象的监护人应当依法保证其按时入学接受并完成义务教育。  
年满十六周岁的社区矫正对象有就业意愿的，社区矫正机构可以协调有关部门和单位为其提供职业技能培训，给予就业指导和帮助。

**Article 55** For a minor community corrections object who has not completed compulsory education, the community corrections institution shall notify and cooperate with the education department to provide conditions for the completion of compulsory education. The guardian of the minor community corrections object shall ensure according to law that the minor attends school on time and receives and completes compulsory education.  
If a community corrections object who has reached the age of sixteen has the will to be employed, the community corrections institution may coordinate with relevant departments and units to provide vocational skills training and offer employment guidance and assistance.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、未成年人保护组织应当依法协助社区矫正机构做好未成年人社区矫正工作。  
国家鼓励其他未成年人相关社会组织参与未成年人社区矫正工作，依法给予政策支持。

**Article 56** The Communist Youth League, the Women's Federation, and the minors protection organization shall assist the community corrections institution in conducting community corrections work for minors according to law.  
The State encourages other social organizations related to minors to participate in community corrections work for minors and provides policy support according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　未成年社区矫正对象在复学、升学、就业等方面依法享有与其他未成年人同等的权利，任何单位和个人不得歧视。有歧视行为的，应当由教育、人力资源和社会保障等部门依法作出处理。

**Article 57** A minor community corrections object shall enjoy equal rights with other minors according to law in respect of resumption of schooling, advancement of education, employment, etc., and no unit or individual shall discriminate against him or her. If there is discriminatory conduct, the matter shall be handled by the departments of education, human resources and social security, among others, according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　未成年社区矫正对象在社区矫正期间年满十八周岁的，继续按照未成年人社区矫正有关规定执行。

**Article 58** If a minor community corrections object reaches the age of eighteen during the period of community corrections, the provisions on community corrections for minors shall continue to apply.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　社区矫正对象在社区矫正期间有违反监督管理规定行为的，由公安机关依照《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》的规定给予处罚；具有撤销缓刑、假释或者暂予监外执行收监情形的，应当依法作出处理。

**Article 59** If a community corrections object violates the supervision and management regulations during the period of community corrections, the public security organ shall impose a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments; if there are circumstances requiring revocation of probation, parole, or commutation of sentence for temporary service outside prison, the matter shall be handled according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　社区矫正对象殴打、威胁、侮辱、骚扰、报复社区矫正机构工作人员和其他依法参与社区矫正工作的人员及其近亲属，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 60** If a community corrections object assaults, threatens, insults, harasses, or retaliates against the staff of the community corrections institution or other persons who participate in community corrections work according to law, or their close relatives, and the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the conduct does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　社区矫正机构工作人员和其他国家工作人员有下列行为之一的，应当给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）利用职务或者工作便利索取、收受贿赂的；  
（二）不履行法定职责的；  
（三）体罚、虐待社区矫正对象，或者违反法律规定限制或者变相限制社区矫正对象的人身自由的；  
（四）泄露社区矫正工作秘密或者其他依法应当保密的信息的；  
（五）对依法申诉、控告或者检举的社区矫正对象进行打击报复的；  
（六）有其他违纪违法行为的。

**Article 61** If a staff member of the community corrections institution or any other state functionary commits any of the following acts, he or she shall be given a disciplinary sanction; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) soliciting or accepting bribes by taking advantage of their position or work convenience;  
(2) failing to perform statutory duties;  
(3) physically punishing or abusing a community corrections object, or restricting or indirectly restricting the personal freedom of a community corrections object in violation of legal provisions;  
(4) disclosing secrets of community corrections work or other information that shall be kept confidential according to law;  
(5) retaliating against a community corrections object who has lawfully lodged a petition, accusation, or report;  
(6) committing other violations of discipline or law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　人民检察院发现社区矫正工作违反法律规定的，应当依法提出纠正意见、检察建议。有关单位应当将采纳纠正意见、检察建议的情况书面回复人民检察院，没有采纳的应当说明理由。

**Article 62** If the People's Procuratorate finds that community corrections work violates legal provisions, it shall propose corrective opinions and procuratorial suggestions according to law. The relevant unit shall reply in writing to the People's Procuratorate on the adoption of the corrective opinions and procuratorial suggestions; if not adopted, it shall state the reasons.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2020年7月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force on July 1, 2020.
