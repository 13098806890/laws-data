# 中华人民共和国人民防空法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1996年10月29日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　防护重点 第三章　人民防空工程 第四章　通信和警报 第五章　疏　　散 第六章　群众防空组织 第七章　人民防空教育 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了有效地组织人民防空，保护人民的生命和财产安全，保障社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，制定本法。 第二条　人民防空是国防的组成部分。国家根据国防需要，动员和组织群众采取防护措施，防范和减轻空袭危害。 人民防空实行长期准备、重点建设、平战结合的方针，贯彻与经济建设协调发展、与城市建设相结合的原则。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了有效地组织人民防空，保护人民的生命和财产安全，保障社会主义现代化建设的顺利进行，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of effectively organizing civil air defense, protecting the safety of people's lives and property, and ensuring the smooth progress of socialist modernization.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民防空是国防的组成部分。国家根据国防需要，动员和组织群众采取防护措施，防范和减轻空袭危害。  
人民防空实行长期准备、重点建设、平战结合的方针，贯彻与经济建设协调发展、与城市建设相结合的原则。

**Article 2** Civil air defense is a component of national defense. The state, in light of the needs of national defense, mobilizes and organizes the masses to take protective measures to prevent and mitigate the hazards of air raids.  
Civil air defense shall implement the policy of long-term preparation, key construction, and combination of peacetime and wartime, and shall adhere to the principle of coordinated development with economic construction and integration with urban construction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当将人民防空建设纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 3** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate civil air defense construction into the national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民防空经费由国家和社会共同负担。  
中央负担的人民防空经费，列入中央预算；县级以上地方各级人民政府负担的人民防空经费，列入地方各级预算。  
有关单位应当按照国家规定负担人民防空费用。

**Article 4** Civil air defense funds shall be borne jointly by the state and society.  
The civil air defense funds borne by the central government shall be included in the central budget; the civil air defense funds borne by local people's governments at or above the county level shall be included in the local budgets at corresponding levels.  
Relevant units shall bear civil air defense expenses in accordance with state regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家对人民防空设施建设按照有关规定给予优惠。  
国家鼓励、支持企业事业组织、社会团体和个人，通过多种途径，投资进行人民防空工程建设；人民防空工程平时由投资者使用管理，收益归投资者所有。

**Article 5** The state shall grant preferential treatment to civil air defense facility construction in accordance with relevant regulations.  
The state encourages and supports enterprises, institutions, social organizations, and individuals to invest in civil air defense engineering construction through various channels; civil air defense projects shall be used and managed by investors during peacetime, and the proceeds shall belong to the investors.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院、中央军事委员会领导全国的人民防空工作。  
大军区根据国务院、中央军事委员会的授权领导本区域的人民防空工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府和同级军事机关领导本行政区域的人民防空工作。

**Article 6** The State Council and the Central Military Commission shall lead the national civil air defense work.  
The greater military regions shall lead civil air defense work within their respective regions under the authorization of the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level and the military organs at the same level shall lead civil air defense work within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家人民防空主管部门管理全国的人民防空工作。  
大军区人民防空主管部门管理本区域的人民防空工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府人民防空主管部门管理本行政区域的人民防空工作。  
中央国家机关人民防空主管部门管理中央国家机关的人民防空工作。  
人民防空主管部门的设置、职责和任务，由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。  
县级以上人民政府的计划、规划、建设等有关部门在各自的职责范围内负责有关的人民防空工作。

**Article 7** The competent department of civil air defense under the State Council shall administer the national civil air defense work.  
The competent departments of civil air defense of the greater military regions shall administer civil air defense work within their respective regions.  
The competent departments of civil air defense of local people's governments at or above the county level shall administer civil air defense work within their respective administrative regions.  
The competent department of civil air defense of the central state organs shall administer the civil air defense work of the central state organs.  
The establishment, functions, and tasks of the competent departments of civil air defense shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.  
The relevant departments in charge of planning, programming, and construction of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant civil air defense work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　一切组织和个人都有得到人民防空保护的权利，都必须依法履行人民防空的义务。

**Article 8** All organizations and individuals shall have the right to obtain civil air defense protection, and must fulfill civil air defense obligations according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家保护人民防空设施不受侵害。禁止任何组织或者个人破坏、侵占人民防空设施。

**Article 9** The state shall protect civil air defense facilities from infringement. No organization or individual may damage or occupy civil air defense facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府和军事机关对在人民防空工作中做出显著成绩的组织或者个人，给予奖励。

**Article 10** People's governments at or above the county level and military organs shall give awards to organizations or individuals that have made outstanding achievements in civil air defense work.

### 第二章　防护重点

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　城市是人民防空的重点。国家对城市实行分类防护。  
城市的防护类别、防护标准，由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 11** Cities are the key points of civil air defense. The state shall implement classified protection for cities.  
The protection categories and standards for cities shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　城市人民政府应当制定防空袭方案及实施计划，必要时可以组织演习。

**Article 12** People's governments of cities shall formulate air raid defense plans and implementation plans, and may organize exercises when necessary.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　城市人民政府应当制定人民防空工程建设规划，并纳入城市总体规划。

**Article 13** People's governments of cities shall formulate plans for civil air defense engineering construction and incorporate them into the overall urban plans.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　城市的地下交通干线以及其他地下工程的建设，应当兼顾人民防空需要。

**Article 14** The construction of underground traffic arteries and other underground projects in cities shall take into account the needs of civil air defense.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　为战时储备粮食、医药、油料和其他必需物资的工程，应当建在地下或者其他隐蔽地点。

**Article 15** Projects for storing grain, medicine, oil, and other necessary materials for wartime shall be constructed underground or in other concealed locations.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对重要的经济目标，有关部门必须采取有效防护措施，并制定应急抢险抢修方案。  
前款所称重要的经济目标，包括重要的工矿企业、科研基地、交通枢纽、通信枢纽、桥梁、水库、仓库、电站等。

**Article 16** Relevant departments must take effective protective measures for important economic targets and formulate emergency rescue and repair plans.  
The important economic targets referred to in the preceding paragraph include important industrial and mining enterprises, scientific research bases, transportation hubs, communication hubs, bridges, reservoirs, warehouses, power stations, etc.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民防空主管部门应当依照规定对城市和经济目标的人民防空建设进行监督检查。被检查单位应当如实提供情况和必要的资料。

**Article 17** The competent departments of civil air defense shall supervise and inspect civil air defense construction of cities and economic targets in accordance with regulations. The units being inspected shall truthfully provide information and necessary materials.

### 第三章　人民防空工程

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民防空工程包括为保障战时人员与物资掩蔽、人民防空指挥、医疗救护等而单独修建的地下防护建筑，以及结合地面建筑修建的战时可用于防空的地下室。

**Article 18** Civil air defense projects include underground protective buildings constructed separately to ensure the shelter of personnel and materials, civil air defense command, medical rescue, etc., as well as basements built in conjunction with above-ground buildings that can be used for air defense in wartime.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家对人民防空工程建设，按照不同的防护要求，实行分类指导。  
国家根据国防建设的需要，结合城市建设和经济发展水平，制定人民防空工程建设规划。

**Article 19** The state shall implement classified guidance for civil air defense engineering construction according to different protection requirements.  
The state shall formulate plans for civil air defense engineering construction based on the needs of national defense construction, taking into account urban construction and economic development levels.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　建设人民防空工程，应当在保证战时使用效能的前提下，有利于平时的经济建设、群众的生产生活和工程的开发利用。

**Article 20** In constructing civil air defense projects, under the premise of ensuring wartime operational effectiveness, they shall be conducive to peacetime economic construction, the production and livelihood of the masses, and the development and utilization of the projects.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民防空指挥工程、公用的人员掩蔽工程和疏散干道工程由人民防空主管部门负责组织修建；医疗救护、物资储备等专用工程由其他有关部门负责组织修建。  
有关单位负责修建本单位的人员与物资掩蔽工程。

**Article 21** Civil air defense command projects, public personnel shelter projects, and evacuation trunk road projects shall be organized and constructed by the competent departments of civil air defense; special projects such as medical rescue and material storage shall be organized and constructed by other relevant departments.  
The relevant units shall be responsible for constructing personnel and material shelter projects within their own units.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　城市新建民用建筑，按照国家有关规定修建战时可用于防空的地下室。

**Article 22** For new civil buildings in cities, basements that can be used for air defense in wartime shall be constructed in accordance with relevant state regulations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民防空工程建设的设计、施工、质量必须符合国家规定的防护标准和质量标准。  
人民防空工程专用设备的定型、生产必须符合国家规定的标准。

**Article 23** The design, construction, and quality of civil air defense engineering construction must comply with the protection and quality standards prescribed by the state.  
The type approval and production of special equipment for civil air defense projects must comply with the standards prescribed by the state.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府有关部门对人民防空工程所需的建设用地应当依法予以保障；对人民防空工程连接城市的道路、供电、供热、供水、排水、通信等系统的设施建设，应当提供必要的条件。

**Article 24** Relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall ensure the land required for civil air defense engineering construction according to law; they shall provide necessary conditions for the construction of facilities connecting civil air defense projects to urban road, power supply, heat supply, water supply, drainage, communication, and other systems.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民防空主管部门对人民防空工程的维护管理进行监督检查。  
公用的人民防空工程的维护管理由人民防空主管部门负责。  
有关单位应当按照国家规定对已经修建或者使用的人民防空工程进行维护管理，使其保持良好使用状态。

**Article 25** The competent departments of civil air defense shall supervise and inspect the maintenance and management of civil air defense projects.  
The maintenance and management of public civil air defense projects shall be the responsibility of the competent departments of civil air defense.  
Relevant units shall maintain and manage civil air defense projects that have been constructed or are in use in accordance with state regulations, so as to keep them in good working condition.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家鼓励平时利用人民防空工程为经济建设和人民生活服务。平时利用人民防空工程，不得影响其防空效能。

**Article 26** The state encourages the use of civil air defense projects during peacetime to serve economic construction and people's lives. The use of civil air defense projects during peacetime shall not affect their air defense effectiveness.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　任何组织或者个人不得进行影响人民防空工程使用或者降低人民防空工程防护能力的作业，不得向人民防空工程内排入废水、废气和倾倒废弃物，不得在人民防空工程内生产、储存爆炸、剧毒、易燃、放射性和腐蚀性物品。

**Article 27** No organization or individual may carry out operations that affect the use of civil air defense projects or reduce the protection capability of civil air defense projects, may not discharge waste water, waste gas, or dump waste into civil air defense projects, and may not produce or store explosive, highly toxic, flammable, radioactive, and corrosive substances in civil air defense projects.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何组织或者个人不得擅自拆除[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定的人民防空工程；确需拆除的，必须报经人民防空主管部门批准，并由拆除单位负责补建或者补偿。

**Article 28** No organization or individual may dismantle civil air defense projects as specified in Article 21 of this Law without authorization; if dismantling is truly necessary, approval must be obtained from the competent department of civil air defense, and the dismantling unit shall be responsible for reconstruction or compensation.

### 第四章　通信和警报

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家保障人民防空通信、警报的畅通，以迅速准确地传递、发放防空警报信号，有效地组织、指挥人民防空。

**Article 29** The state shall ensure the unimpeded flow of civil air defense communications and alarms, so as to transmit and issue air defense alarm signals quickly and accurately, and effectively organize and direct civil air defense.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家人民防空主管部门负责制定全国的人民防空通信、警报建设规划，组织全国的人民防空通信、警报网的建设和管理。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府人民防空主管部门负责制定本行政区域的人民防空通信、警报建设规划，组织本行政区域人民防空通信、警报网的建设和管理。

**Article 30** The competent department of civil air defense under the State Council shall be responsible for formulating national plans for civil air defense communications and alarm construction, and organizing the construction and management of the national civil air defense communications and alarm network.  
The competent departments of civil air defense of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for formulating plans for civil air defense communications and alarm construction within their respective administrative regions, and organizing the construction and management of the civil air defense communications and alarm network within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　邮电部门、军队通信部门和人民防空主管部门应当按照国家规定的任务和人民防空通信、警报建设规划，对人民防空通信实施保障。

**Article 31** The postal and telecommunications departments, military communication departments, and competent departments of civil air defense shall ensure the implementation of civil air defense communications in accordance with the tasks prescribed by the state and plans for civil air defense communications and alarm construction.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民防空主管部门建设通信、警报网所需的电路、频率，邮电部门、军队通信部门、无线电管理机构应当予以保障；安装人民防空通信、警报设施，有关单位或者个人应当提供方便条件，不得阻挠。  
国家用于人民防空通信的专用频率和防空警报音响信号，任何组织或者个人不得占用、混同。

**Article 32** The circuits and frequencies required by the competent departments of civil air defense for constructing communications and alarm networks shall be ensured by the postal and telecommunications departments, military communication departments, and radio regulatory authorities; when installing civil air defense communications and alarm facilities, relevant units or individuals shall provide convenient conditions and may not obstruct.  
No organization or individual may occupy or confuse the dedicated frequencies used by the state for civil air defense communications and the audio signals of air defense alarms.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　通信、广播、电视系统，战时必须优先传递、发放防空警报信号。

**Article 33** Communication, broadcasting, and television systems must prioritize the transmission and issuance of air defense alarm signals during wartime.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　军队有关部门应当向人民防空主管部门通报空中情报，协助训练有关专业人员。

**Article 34** Relevant military departments shall notify the competent departments of civil air defense of air intelligence and assist in training relevant professionals.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人民防空通信、警报设施必须保持良好使用状态。  
设置在有关单位的人民防空警报设施，由其所在单位维护管理，不得擅自拆除。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府根据需要可以组织试鸣防空警报；并在试鸣的五日以前发布公告。

**Article 35** Civil air defense communications and alarm facilities must be kept in good working condition.  
Civil air defense alarm facilities installed in relevant units shall be maintained and managed by the units where they are located, and may not be dismantled without authorization.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level may organize trial sounding of air defense alarms as needed; and shall issue a public notice five days before the trial sounding.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民防空通信、警报设施平时应当为抢险救灾服务。

**Article 36** Civil air defense communications and alarm facilities shall serve emergency rescue and disaster relief during peacetime.

### 第五章　疏散

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民防空疏散由县级以上人民政府统一组织。  
人民防空疏散必须根据国家发布的命令实施，任何组织不得擅自行动。

**Article 37** Civil air defense evacuation shall be uniformly organized by people's governments at or above the county level.  
Civil air defense evacuation must be implemented in accordance with orders issued by the state, and no organization may act without authorization.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　城市人民防空疏散计划，由县级以上人民政府根据需要组织有关部门制定。  
预定的疏散地区，在本行政区域内的，由本级人民政府确定；跨越本行政区域的，由上一级人民政府确定。

**Article 38** Plans for urban civil air defense evacuation shall be formulated by relevant departments organized by people's governments at or above the county level as needed.  
The predetermined evacuation areas, if within the same administrative region, shall be determined by the people's government at that level; if crossing administrative regions, shall be determined by the people's government at the next higher level.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府应当组织有关部门和单位，做好城市疏散人口安置和物资储运、供应的准备工作。

**Article 39** People's governments at or above the county level shall organize relevant departments and units to do a good job in preparing for the placement of evacuated urban population and the storage, transportation, and supply of materials.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　农村人口在有必要疏散时，由当地人民政府按照就近的原则组织实施。

**Article 40** When it is necessary to evacuate the rural population, the local people's government shall organize and implement the evacuation in accordance with the principle of proximity.

### 第六章　群众防空组织

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　县级以上地方各级人民政府应当根据人民防空的需要，组织有关部门建立群众防空组织。  
群众防空组织战时担负抢险抢修、医疗救护、防火灭火、防疫灭菌、消毒和消除沾染、保障通信联络、抢救人员和抢运物资、维护社会治安等任务，平时应当协助防汛、防震等部门担负抢险救灾任务。

**Article 41** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in light of the needs of civil air defense, organize relevant departments to establish civil air defense organizations of the masses.  
Civil air defense organizations of the masses shall undertake tasks such as emergency rescue and repair, medical rescue, fire fighting, epidemic prevention and sterilization, disinfection and decontamination, ensuring communication links, rescuing personnel and rushing materials, maintaining social order during wartime, and shall assist flood control, earthquake prevention, and other departments in undertaking emergency rescue and disaster relief tasks during peacetime.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　群众防空组织由下列部门负责组建：  
（一）城建、公用、电力等部门组建抢险抢修队；  
（二）卫生、医药部门组建医疗救护队；  
（三）公安部门组建消防队、治安队；  
（四）卫生、化工、环保等部门组建防化防疫队；  
（五）邮电部门组建通信队；  
（六）交通运输部门组建运输队。  
红十字会组织依法进行救护工作。

**Article 42** Civil air defense organizations of the masses shall be formed by the following departments:  
(1) Departments of urban construction, public utilities, and electric power shall form emergency rescue and repair teams;  
(2) Departments of health and medicine shall form medical rescue teams;  
(3) Public security departments shall form fire brigades and public security teams;  
(4) Departments of health, chemical industry, and environmental protection shall form chemical defense and epidemic prevention teams;  
(5) Postal and telecommunications departments shall form communication teams;  
(6) Transportation departments shall form transport teams.  
Red Cross organizations shall carry out rescue work according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　群众防空组织所需装备、器材和经费由人民防空主管部门和组建单位提供。

**Article 43** The equipment, materials, and funds required by civil air defense organizations of the masses shall be provided by the competent departments of civil air defense and the forming units.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　群众防空组织应当根据人民防空主管部门制定的训练大纲和训练计划进行专业训练。

**Article 44** Civil air defense organizations of the masses shall conduct professional training in accordance with the training outlines and plans formulated by the competent departments of civil air defense.

### 第七章　人民防空教育

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家开展人民防空教育，使公民增强国防观念，掌握人民防空的基本知识和技能。

**Article 45** The state shall carry out civil air defense education to enable citizens to strengthen their awareness of national defense and master the basic knowledge and skills of civil air defense.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家人民防空主管部门负责组织制定人民防空教育计划，规定教育内容。  
在校学生的人民防空教育，由各级教育主管部门和人民防空主管部门组织实施。  
国家机关、社会团体、企业事业组织人员的人民防空教育，由所在单位组织实施；其他人员的人民防空教育，由城乡基层人民政府组织实施。

**Article 46** The competent department of civil air defense under the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the formulation of civil air defense education plans and prescribing the education content.  
Civil air defense education for school students shall be organized and implemented by the competent education departments and competent civil air defense departments at all levels.  
Civil air defense education for personnel of state organs, social organizations, enterprises, and institutions shall be organized and implemented by the units they belong to; civil air defense education for other personnel shall be organized and implemented by the grassroots people's governments in urban and rural areas.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　新闻、出版、广播、电影、电视、文化等有关部门应当协助开展人民防空教育。

**Article 47** Relevant departments such as news, publishing, broadcasting, film, television, and culture shall assist in carrying out civil air defense education.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　城市新建民用建筑，违反国家有关规定不修建战时可用于防空的地下室的，由县级以上人民政府人民防空主管部门对当事人给予警告，并责令限期修建，可以并处十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Where new civil buildings in cities, in violation of relevant state regulations, do not construct basements that can be used for air defense in wartime, the competent department of civil air defense of the people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning to the party and order rectification within a specified time limit, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府人民防空主管部门对当事人给予警告，并责令限期改正违法行为，可以对个人并处五千元以下的罚款、对单位并处一万元至五万元的罚款；造成损失的，应当依法赔偿损失：  
（一）侵占人民防空工程的；  
（二）不按照国家规定的防护标准和质量标准修建人民防空工程的；  
（三）违反国家有关规定，改变人民防空工程主体结构、拆除人民防空工程设备设施或者采用其他方法危害人民防空工程的安全和使用效能的；  
（四）拆除人民防空工程后拒不补建的；  
（五）占用人民防空通信专用频率、使用与防空警报相同的音响信号或者擅自拆除人民防空通信、警报设备设施的；  
（六）阻挠安装人民防空通信、警报设施，拒不改正的；  
（七）向人民防空工程内排入废水、废气或者倾倒废弃物的。

**Article 49** Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be given a warning by the competent department of civil air defense of the people's government at or above the county level and ordered to correct the illegal act within a specified time limit; a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan may be imposed on the individual, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan may be imposed on the unit; if losses are caused, compensation shall be paid according to law:  
(1) Encroaching upon civil air defense projects;  
(2) Constructing civil air defense projects not in accordance with the protection standards and quality standards prescribed by the state;  
(3) Violating relevant state regulations by altering the main structure of civil air defense projects, dismantling equipment and facilities of civil air defense projects, or adopting other methods to endanger the safety and use effectiveness of civil air defense projects;  
(4) Refusing to reconstruct after dismantling civil air defense projects;  
(5) Occupying dedicated frequencies for civil air defense communications, using sound signals identical to air defense alarms, or dismantling civil air defense communications and alarm equipment and facilities without authorization;  
(6) Obstructing the installation of civil air defense communications and alarm facilities and refusing to correct;  
(7) Discharging waste water, waste gas, or dumping waste into civil air defense projects.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，故意损坏人民防空设施或者在人民防空工程内生产、储存爆炸、剧毒、易燃、放射性等危险品，尚不构成犯罪的，依照治安管理处罚法的有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, intentionally damages civil air defense facilities or produces or stores explosive, highly toxic, flammable, radioactive, and other dangerous substances in civil air defense projects, if the act does not constitute a crime, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民防空主管部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊或者有其他违法、失职行为构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 51** Where staff members of the competent departments of civil air defense, through dereliction of duty, abuse of power, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, or other illegal or derelict acts, constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会可以根据本法制定实施办法。

**Article 52** The standing committees of the people's congresses of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with this Law, formulate measures for implementation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本法自1997年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1997.
