### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了正确执行刑罚，惩罚和改造罪犯，预防和减少犯罪，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purpose of correctly carrying out punishments, punishing and reforming criminals, and preventing and reducing crimes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　监狱是国家的刑罚执行机关。  
依照刑法和刑事诉讼法的规定，被判处死刑缓期二年执行、无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，在监狱内执行刑罚。

**Article 2** A prison is the State's organ for carrying out punishments.  
In accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law and the Criminal Procedure Law, criminals sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment shall serve their sentences in prison.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　监狱对罪犯实行惩罚和改造相结合、教育和劳动相结合的原则，将罪犯改造成为守法公民。

**Article 3** A prison shall, with respect to criminals, implement the principle of combining punishment with reform and combining education with labor, so as to reform them into law-abiding citizens.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　监狱对罪犯应当依法监管，根据改造罪犯的需要，组织罪犯从事生产劳动，对罪犯进行思想教育、文化教育、技术教育。

**Article 4** A prison shall supervise criminals according to law, and organize them to engage in productive labor and conduct ideological, cultural and technical education among them in light of the need for reforming them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　监狱的人民警察依法管理监狱、执行刑罚、对罪犯进行教育改造等活动，受法律保护。

**Article 5** The people's police of a prison shall, according to law, administer the prison, carry out punishments, and conduct educational reform of criminals, and such activities shall be protected by law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民检察院对监狱执行刑罚的活动是否合法，依法实行监督。

**Article 6** The People's Procuratorate shall, according to law, exercise supervision over whether the activities of the prison in carrying out punishments are legal.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　罪犯的人格不受侮辱，其人身安全、合法财产和辩护、申诉、控告、检举以及其他未被依法剥夺或者限制的权利不受侵犯。  
罪犯必须严格遵守法律、法规和监规纪律，服从管理，接受教育，参加劳动。

**Article 7** The personal dignity of a criminal shall not be humiliated, and his personal safety, lawful property, and the rights of defense, petition, accusation, impeachment, and other rights not yet deprived or restricted according to law shall not be violated.  
A criminal must strictly observe laws, regulations, and prison discipline, submit to administration, receive education, and participate in labor.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家保障监狱改造罪犯所需经费。监狱的人民警察经费、罪犯改造经费、罪犯生活费、狱政设施经费及其他专项经费，列入国家预算。  
国家提供罪犯劳动必需的生产设施和生产经费。

**Article 8** The State shall guarantee the funds necessary for the prison to reform criminals. The funds for the people's police of the prison, for reforming criminals, for the living expenses of criminals, for prison administration facilities, and other special funds shall be included in the State budget.  
The State shall provide the production facilities and production funds necessary for criminal labor.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　监狱依法使用的土地、矿产资源和其他自然资源以及监狱的财产，受法律保护，任何组织或者个人不得侵占、破坏。

**Article 9** Land, mineral resources, other natural resources, and property used by the prison according to law shall be protected by law, and no organization or individual may encroach upon or damage them.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院司法行政部门主管全国的监狱工作。

**Article 10** The judicial administration department under the State Council shall be in charge of prison work nationwide.

### 第二章　监狱

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　监狱的设置、撤销、迁移，由国务院司法行政部门批准。

**Article 11** The establishment, dissolution, or relocation of a prison shall be approved by the judicial administration department under the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　监狱设监狱长一人、副监狱长若干人，并根据实际需要设置必要的工作机构和配备其他监狱管理人员。  
监狱的管理人员是人民警察。

**Article 12** A prison shall have one warden and a number of deputy wardens, and shall, in light of actual needs, establish necessary working organs and equip other prison administrators.  
The administrators of a prison shall be people's police.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　监狱的人民警察应当严格遵守宪法和法律，忠于职守，秉公执法，严守纪律，清正廉洁。

**Article 13** The people's police of a prison shall strictly observe the Constitution and laws, be faithful to their duties, enforce the law impartially, strictly observe discipline, and be honest and incorruptible.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　监狱的人民警察不得有下列行为：  
（一）索要、收受、侵占罪犯及其亲属的财物；  
（二）私放罪犯或者玩忽职守造成罪犯脱逃；  
（三）刑讯逼供或者体罚、虐待罪犯；  
（四）侮辱罪犯的人格；  
（五）殴打或者纵容他人殴打罪犯；  
（六）为谋取私利，利用罪犯提供劳务；  
（七）违反规定，私自为罪犯传递信件或者物品；  
（八）非法将监管罪犯的职权交予他人行使；  
（九）其他违法行为。  
监狱的人民警察有前款所列行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚未构成犯罪的，应当予以行政处分。

**Article 14** The people's police of a prison shall not commit any of the following acts:  
(1) asking for, accepting, or embezzling property of criminals or their relatives;  
(2) releasing a criminal without authorization, or causing the escape of a criminal due to dereliction of duty;  
(3) extorting confessions by torture, or subjecting criminals to corporal punishment or maltreatment;  
(4) humiliating the personal dignity of criminals;  
(5) beating or conniving at others' beating of criminals;  
(6) using criminal labor for personal gain;  
(7) privately delivering letters or articles to criminals in violation of regulations;  
(8) illegally transferring the power of supervising criminals to others for exercise;  
(9) other illegal acts.  
If a person's police of a prison commits any act listed in the preceding paragraph, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not yet constitute a crime, he shall be subject to administrative sanctions.

### 第三章　刑罚的执行

#### 第一节　收监

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院对被判处死刑缓期二年执行、无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，应当将执行通知书、判决书送达羁押该罪犯的公安机关，公安机关应当自收到执行通知书、判决书之日起一个月内将该罪犯送交监狱执行刑罚。  
罪犯在被交付执行刑罚前，剩余刑期在三个月以下的，由看守所代为执行。

**Article 15** With respect to a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment, the People's Court shall serve the execution notice and the judgment document on the public security organ that has the criminal in custody, and the public security organ shall, within one month from the date of receiving the execution notice and judgment document, send the criminal to a prison to execute the punishment.  
If, before a criminal is delivered for execution of punishment, the remaining term of sentence is less than three months, the detention house shall execute the punishment on behalf of the prison.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　罪犯被交付执行刑罚时，交付执行的人民法院应当将人民检察院的起诉书副本、人民法院的判决书、执行通知书、结案登记表同时送达监狱。监狱没有收到上述文件的，不得收监；上述文件不齐全或者记载有误的，作出生效判决的人民法院应当及时补充齐全或者作出更正；对其中可能导致错误收监的，不予收监。

**Article 16** When a criminal is delivered for execution of punishment, the People's Court that delivers for execution shall simultaneously serve on the prison the copy of the indictment of the People's Procuratorate, the judgment document of the People's Court, the execution notice, and the case closure registration form. If the prison has not received the above documents, it shall not admit the criminal; if the above documents are incomplete or contain errors, the People's Court that rendered the effective judgment shall promptly complete them or make corrections; if any of them may lead to erroneous admission, the criminal shall not be admitted.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　罪犯被交付执行刑罚，符合[本法第十六条](#art-16)规定的，应当予以收监。罪犯收监后，监狱应当对其进行身体检查。经检查，对于具有暂予监外执行情形的，监狱可以提出书面意见，报省级以上监狱管理机关批准。

**Article 17** If a criminal delivered for execution of punishment meets the requirements of Article 16 of this Law, he shall be admitted to the prison. After a criminal is admitted, the prison shall conduct a physical examination of him. Where, through examination, the criminal falls under the circumstances for temporary service of sentence outside prison, the prison may submit a written opinion and report it to the prison administration authority at or above the provincial level for approval.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　罪犯收监，应当严格检查其人身和所携带的物品。非生活必需品，由监狱代为保管或者征得罪犯同意退回其家属，违禁品予以没收。  
女犯由女性人民警察检查。

**Article 18** When a criminal is admitted, a strict inspection shall be conducted on his person and the articles he carries. Non-essential daily necessities shall be kept by the prison on behalf of the criminal or returned to his family with his consent, and contraband shall be confiscated.  
Female criminals shall be inspected by female people's police.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　罪犯不得携带子女在监内服刑。

**Article 19** A criminal may not bring his child into the prison to serve a sentence.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　罪犯收监后，监狱应当通知罪犯家属。通知书应当自收监之日起五日内发出。

**Article 20** After a criminal is admitted, the prison shall notify his family. The notice shall be sent within five days from the date of admission.

#### 第二节　对罪犯提出的申诉、控告、检举的处理

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　罪犯对生效的判决不服的，可以提出申诉。  
对于罪犯的申诉，人民检察院或者人民法院应当及时处理。

**Article 21** If a criminal refuses to accept an effective judgment, he may file a petition.  
With respect to a criminal's petition, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall promptly handle it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对罪犯提出的控告、检举材料，监狱应当及时处理或者转送公安机关或者人民检察院处理，公安机关或者人民检察院应当将处理结果通知监狱。

**Article 22** The prison shall promptly handle the materials of accusation or impeachment submitted by a criminal, or transfer them to the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate for handling, and the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate shall notify the prison of the results of handling.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　罪犯的申诉、控告、检举材料，监狱应当及时转递，不得扣压。

**Article 23** The prison shall promptly transmit the materials of a criminal's petition, accusation, or impeachment and shall not withhold them.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　监狱在执行刑罚过程中，根据罪犯的申诉，认为判决可能有错误的，应当提请人民检察院或者人民法院处理，人民检察院或者人民法院应当自收到监狱提请处理意见书之日起六个月内将处理结果通知监狱。

**Article 24** Where, in the process of executing a punishment, a prison, based on a criminal's petition, believes that the judgment may be erroneous, it shall refer the matter to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court for handling, and the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall, within six months from the date of receiving the written opinion for handling submitted by the prison, notify the prison of the result.

#### 第三节　监外执行

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对于被判处无期徒刑、有期徒刑在监内服刑的罪犯，符合刑事诉讼法规定的监外执行条件的，可以暂予监外执行。

**Article 25** For a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment serving his sentence in prison, if he meets the conditions for serving sentence outside prison as prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law, he may temporarily serve his sentence outside prison.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　暂予监外执行，由监狱提出书面意见，报省、自治区、直辖市监狱管理机关批准。批准机关应当将批准的暂予监外执行决定通知公安机关和原判人民法院，并抄送人民检察院。  
人民检察院认为对罪犯适用暂予监外执行不当的，应当自接到通知之日起一个月内将书面意见送交批准暂予监外执行的机关，批准暂予监外执行的机关接到人民检察院的书面意见后，应当立即对该决定进行重新核查。

**Article 26** Temporary service of sentence outside prison shall be submitted by the prison as a written opinion to the prison administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval. The approving authority shall notify the public security organ and the original sentencing People's Court of the approved decision for temporary service of sentence outside prison, and send a copy to the People's Procuratorate.  
If the People's Procuratorate considers the application of temporary service of sentence outside prison to a criminal improper, it shall, within one month from the date of receiving the notice, deliver a written opinion to the authority that approved temporary service of sentence outside prison. After receiving the written opinion of the People's Procuratorate, the approving authority shall immediately re-examine the decision.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对暂予监外执行的罪犯，依法实行社区矫正，由社区矫正机构负责执行。原关押监狱应当及时将罪犯在监内改造情况通报负责执行的社区矫正机构。

**Article 27** For a criminal temporarily serving his sentence outside prison, community correction shall be implemented according to law, and the community correction institution shall be responsible for its execution. The original prison that had the criminal in custody shall promptly notify the community correction institution responsible for execution of the criminal's reform situation in the prison.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　暂予监外执行的罪犯具有刑事诉讼法规定的应当收监的情形的，社区矫正机构应当及时通知监狱收监；刑期届满的，由原关押监狱办理释放手续。罪犯在暂予监外执行期间死亡的，社区矫正机构应当及时通知原关押监狱。

**Article 28** If a criminal temporarily serving his sentence outside prison falls under the circumstances prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law under which he shall be re-admitted to the prison, the community correction institution shall promptly notify the prison to take him back; if the sentence has expired, the original prison that had the criminal in custody shall handle the release procedures. If a criminal dies during the period of temporary service of sentence outside prison, the community correction institution shall promptly notify the original prison that had him in custody.

#### 第四节　减刑、假释

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　被判处无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，在服刑期间确有悔改或者立功表现的，根据监狱考核的结果，可以减刑。有下列重大立功表现之一的，应当减刑：  
（一）阻止他人重大犯罪活动的；  
（二）检举监狱内外重大犯罪活动，经查证属实的；  
（三）有发明创造或者重大技术革新的；  
（四）在日常生产、生活中舍己救人的；  
（五）在抗御自然灾害或者排除重大事故中，有突出表现的；  
（六）对国家和社会有其他重大贡献的。

**Article 29** A criminal sentenced to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment who truly demonstrates repentance or meritorious service during the period of serving his sentence may, based on the results of the prison's assessment, have his punishment commuted. If he has any of the following major meritorious performances, his punishment shall be commuted:  
(1) preventing others from committing major criminal activities;  
(2) reporting major criminal activities inside or outside the prison, which has been verified;  
(3) having inventions or major technical innovations;  
(4) sacrificing himself to save others in daily production or life;  
(5) having outstanding performance in resisting natural disasters or eliminating major accidents;  
(6) making other major contributions to the State and society.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　减刑建议由监狱向人民法院提出，人民法院应当自收到减刑建议书之日起一个月内予以审核裁定；案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月。减刑裁定的副本应当抄送人民检察院。

**Article 30** A proposal for commutation of punishment shall be submitted by the prison to the People's Court, and the People's Court shall, within one month from the date of receiving the written proposal for commutation, examine and render a ruling; if the case is complicated or special circumstances exist, the time limit may be extended by one month. A copy of the commutation ruling shall be sent to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　被判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期间，符合法律规定的减为无期徒刑、有期徒刑条件的，二年期满时，所在监狱应当及时提出减刑建议，报经省、自治区、直辖市监狱管理机关审核后，提请高级人民法院裁定。

**Article 31** For a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, if during the period of reprieve he meets the conditions prescribed by law for commutation to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, when the two-year period expires, the prison where he is held shall promptly submit a proposal for commutation of punishment, and after being examined by the prison administration authority of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, submit the matter to the Higher People's Court for a ruling.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　被判处无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，符合法律规定的假释条件的，由监狱根据考核结果向人民法院提出假释建议，人民法院应当自收到假释建议书之日起一个月内予以审核裁定；案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月。假释裁定的副本应当抄送人民检察院。

**Article 32** For a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment who meets the conditions for parole prescribed by law, the prison shall, based on the assessment results, submit a proposal for parole to the People's Court. The People's Court shall, within one month from the date of receiving the written proposal for parole, examine and render a ruling; if the case is complicated or special circumstances exist, the time limit may be extended by one month. A copy of the parole ruling shall be sent to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民法院裁定假释的，监狱应当按期假释并发给假释证明书。  
对被假释的罪犯，依法实行社区矫正，由社区矫正机构负责执行。被假释的罪犯，在假释考验期限内有违反法律、行政法规或者国务院有关部门关于假释的监督管理规定的行为，尚未构成新的犯罪的，社区矫正机构应当向人民法院提出撤销假释的建议，人民法院应当自收到撤销假释建议书之日起一个月内予以审核裁定。人民法院裁定撤销假释的，由公安机关将罪犯送交监狱收监。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于废止1980年1月1日至1997年6月30日期间发布的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件（第九批）的决定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于废止1980年1月1日至1997年6月30日期间发布的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件（第九批）的决定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** If the People's Court rules to grant parole, the prison shall release on parole on schedule and issue a certificate of parole.  
For a criminal released on parole, community correction shall be implemented according to law, and the community correction institution shall be responsible for its execution. If a criminal released on parole, during the probation period for parole, violates laws, administrative regulations, or the provisions of the relevant departments under the State Council concerning the supervision and administration of parole, and such act does not yet constitute a new crime, the community correction institution shall propose to the People's Court to revoke the parole, and the People's Court shall, within one month from the date of receiving the written proposal for revocation of parole, examine and render a ruling. If the People's Court rules to revoke the parole, the public security organ shall send the criminal to the prison for re-admission.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对不符合法律规定的减刑、假释条件的罪犯，不得以任何理由将其减刑、假释。  
人民检察院认为人民法院减刑、假释的裁定不当，应当依照刑事诉讼法规定的期间向人民法院提出书面纠正意见。对于人民检察院提出书面纠正意见的案件，人民法院应当重新审理。

**Article 34** A criminal who does not meet the conditions for commutation or parole prescribed by law may not be granted commutation or parole on any grounds.  
If the People's Procuratorate considers a ruling on commutation or parole rendered by the People's Court improper, it shall submit a written opinion for correction to the People's Court within the time limit prescribed by the Criminal Procedure Law. With respect to a case in which the People's Procuratorate has submitted a written opinion for correction, the People's Court shall retry the case.

#### 第五节　释放和安置

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　罪犯服刑期满，监狱应当按期释放并发给释放证明书。

**Article 35** When a criminal has served his full sentence, the prison shall release him on schedule and issue a certificate of release.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　罪犯释放后，公安机关凭释放证明书办理户籍登记。

**Article 36** After a criminal is released, the public security organ shall, on the basis of the certificate of release, handle the household registration.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对刑满释放人员，当地人民政府帮助其安置生活。  
刑满释放人员丧失劳动能力又无法定赡养人、扶养人和基本生活来源的，由当地人民政府予以救济。

**Article 37** For persons released after serving their full sentences, the local people's government shall assist them in arranging their livelihood.  
If a person released after serving his full sentence has lost the ability to work and has no statutory supporter, caregiver, or basic source of living, the local people's government shall provide relief to him.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　刑满释放人员依法享有与其他公民平等的权利。

**Article 38** A person released after serving his full sentence shall, according to law, enjoy rights equal to those of other citizens.

### 第四章　狱政管理

#### 第一节　分押分管

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　监狱对成年男犯、女犯和未成年犯实行分开关押和管理，对未成年犯和女犯的改造，应当照顾其生理、心理特点。  
监狱根据罪犯的犯罪类型、刑罚种类、刑期、改造表现等情况，对罪犯实行分别关押，采取不同方式管理。

**Article 39** A prison shall separately detain and manage adult male criminals, female criminals, and juvenile criminals, and shall, in reforming juvenile and female criminals, take into account their physiological and psychological characteristics.  
A prison shall, based on the types of crimes committed by criminals, the types of punishments, the terms of sentences, and their reform performance, separately detain criminals and adopt different methods of administration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　女犯由女性人民警察直接管理。

**Article 40** Female criminals shall be directly administered by female people's police.

#### 第二节　警戒

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　监狱的武装警戒由人民武装警察部队负责，具体办法由国务院、中央军事委员会规定。

**Article 41** The armed guard of a prison shall be the responsibility of the People's Armed Police Force. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　监狱发现在押罪犯脱逃，应当即时将其抓获，不能即时抓获的，应当立即通知公安机关，由公安机关负责追捕，监狱密切配合。

**Article 42** If a prison finds that a criminal in custody has escaped, it shall immediately capture him; if it cannot capture him immediately, it shall immediately notify the public security organ, which shall be responsible for pursuing and apprehending the criminal, and the prison shall closely cooperate.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　监狱根据监管需要，设立警戒设施。监狱周围设警戒隔离带，未经准许，任何人不得进入。

**Article 43** A prison shall set up guard facilities according to the needs of supervision. A guard isolation belt shall be set up around the prison, and no person may enter without permission.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　监区、作业区周围的机关、团体、企业事业单位和基层组织，应当协助监狱做好安全警戒工作。

**Article 44** The organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and grassroots organizations around the prison area and the work area shall assist the prison in doing a good job of security guard.

#### 第三节　戒具和武器的使用

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　监狱遇有下列情形之一的，可以使用戒具：  
（一）罪犯有脱逃行为的；  
（二）罪犯有使用暴力行为的；  
（三）罪犯正在押解途中的；  
（四）罪犯有其他危险行为需要采取防范措施的。  
前款所列情形消失后，应当停止使用戒具。

**Article 45** A prison may use restraint implements in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) a criminal has an escape act;  
(2) a criminal has an act of using violence;  
(3) a criminal is being escorted;  
(4) a criminal has other dangerous acts requiring preventive measures.  
After the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph disappear, the use of restraint implements shall cease.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　人民警察和人民武装警察部队的执勤人员遇有下列情形之一，非使用武器不能制止的，按照国家有关规定，可以使用武器：  
（一）罪犯聚众骚乱、暴乱的；  
（二）罪犯脱逃或者拒捕的；  
（三）罪犯持有凶器或者其他危险物，正在行凶或者破坏，危及他人生命、财产安全的；  
（四）劫夺罪犯的；  
（五）罪犯抢夺武器的。  
使用武器的人员，应当按照国家有关规定报告情况。

**Article 46** If a people's police officer or a person on duty of the People's Armed Police Force encounters any of the following circumstances, and the situation cannot be stopped without using weapons, he may use weapons in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State:  
(1) criminals gathering together to riot or mutiny;  
(2) criminals escaping or resisting arrest;  
(3) criminals holding lethal weapons or other dangerous objects, committing violence or sabotage, endangering the life or property of others;  
(4) seizing criminals;  
(5) criminals seizing weapons.  
A person who uses weapons shall report the situation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

#### 第四节　通信、会见

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　罪犯在服刑期间可以与他人通信，但是来往信件应当经过监狱检查。监狱发现有碍罪犯改造内容的信件，可以扣留。罪犯写给监狱的上级机关和司法机关的信件，不受检查。

**Article 47** A criminal may correspond with others during the period of serving his sentence, but incoming and outgoing letters shall be subject to prison inspection. If a prison finds that a letter contains content harmful to the criminal's reform, it may detain it. Letters written by a criminal to the superior organ of the prison or to the judicial authorities shall not be inspected.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　罪犯在监狱服刑期间，按照规定，可以会见亲属、监护人。

**Article 48** During the period of serving his sentence in prison, a criminal may, in accordance with regulations, meet with relatives or guardians.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　罪犯收受物品和钱款，应当经监狱批准、检查。

**Article 49** The receipt of articles and money by a criminal shall be approved and inspected by the prison.

#### 第五节　生活、卫生

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　罪犯的生活标准按实物量计算，由国家规定。

**Article 50** The living standards of criminals shall be calculated in kind and prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　罪犯的被服由监狱统一配发。

**Article 51** The bedding and clothing of criminals shall be uniformly issued by the prison.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对少数民族罪犯的特殊生活习惯，应当予以照顾。

**Article 52** The special living habits of criminals of ethnic minorities shall be given consideration.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　罪犯居住的监舍应当坚固、通风、透光、清洁、保暖。

**Article 53** The prison cells in which criminals reside shall be solid, ventilated, well-lit, clean, and warm.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　监狱应当设立医疗机构和生活、卫生设施，建立罪犯生活、卫生制度。罪犯的医疗保健列入监狱所在地区的卫生、防疫计划。

**Article 54** A prison shall establish medical institutions and living and sanitation facilities, and set up living and sanitation systems for criminals. The medical care of criminals shall be included in the health and epidemic prevention plans of the area where the prison is located.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　罪犯在服刑期间死亡的，监狱应当立即通知罪犯家属和人民检察院、人民法院。罪犯因病死亡的，由监狱作出医疗鉴定。人民检察院对监狱的医疗鉴定有疑义的，可以重新对死亡原因作出鉴定。罪犯家属有疑义的，可以向人民检察院提出。罪犯非正常死亡的，人民检察院应当立即检验，对死亡原因作出鉴定。

**Article 55** If a criminal dies during the period of serving his sentence, the prison shall immediately notify the criminal's family, the People's Procuratorate, and the People's Court. If the criminal dies of illness, the prison shall make a medical evaluation. If the People's Procuratorate has doubts about the prison's medical evaluation, it may re-evaluate the cause of death. If the criminal's family has doubts, it may raise the matter with the People's Procuratorate. If the criminal dies an unnatural death, the People's Procuratorate shall immediately conduct an examination and make a determination of the cause of death.

#### 第六节　奖惩

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　监狱应当建立罪犯的日常考核制度，考核的结果作为对罪犯奖励和处罚的依据。

**Article 56** A prison shall establish a system of daily assessment of criminals, and the results of the assessment shall serve as the basis for rewards and punishment of criminals.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　罪犯有下列情形之一的，监狱可以给予表扬、物质奖励或者记功：  
（一）遵守监规纪律，努力学习，积极劳动，有认罪服法表现的；  
（二）阻止违法犯罪活动的；  
（三）超额完成生产任务的；  
（四）节约原材料或者爱护公物，有成绩的；  
（五）进行技术革新或者传授生产技术，有一定成效的；  
（六）在防止或者消除灾害事故中作出一定贡献的；  
（七）对国家和社会有其他贡献的。  
被判处有期徒刑的罪犯有前款所列情形之一，执行原判刑期二分之一以上，在服刑期间一贯表现好，离开监狱不致再危害社会的，监狱可以根据情况准其离监探亲。

**Article 57** If a criminal has any of the following circumstances, the prison may commend him with praise, material reward, or record a merit:  
(1) observing prison discipline, studying diligently, actively laboring, and demonstrating admission of guilt and submission to the law;  
(2) preventing illegal or criminal activities;  
(3) overfulfilling production tasks;  
(4) saving raw materials or taking good care of public property with achievements;  
(5) carrying out technical innovations or imparting production skills with certain results;  
(6) making certain contributions in preventing or eliminating disasters or accidents;  
(7) making other contributions to the State and society.  
If a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment has one of the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph, has served more than half of the original sentence, has consistently behaved well during the period of serving his sentence, and would not endanger society again if released, the prison may, depending on the circumstances, allow him to leave the prison to visit his family.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　罪犯有下列破坏监管秩序情形之一的，监狱可以给予警告、记过或者禁闭：  
（一）聚众哄闹监狱，扰乱正常秩序的；  
（二）辱骂或者殴打人民警察的；  
（三）欺压其他罪犯的；  
（四）偷窃、赌博、打架斗殴、寻衅滋事的；  
（五）有劳动能力拒不参加劳动或者消极怠工，经教育不改的；  
（六）以自伤、自残手段逃避劳动的；  
（七）在生产劳动中故意违反操作规程，或者有意损坏生产工具的；  
（八）有违反监规纪律的其他行为的。  
依照前款规定对罪犯实行禁闭的期限为七天至十五天。  
罪犯在服刑期间有第一款所列行为，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** If a criminal commits any of the following acts disrupting the supervision order, the prison may impose a warning, a demerit, or solitary confinement:  
(1) gathering to make trouble in the prison, disturbing normal order;  
(2) insulting or beating people's police;  
(3) bullying other criminals;  
(4) stealing, gambling, fighting, or picking quarrels and making trouble;  
(5) refusing to participate in labor despite having the ability to work, or being slack in work, and not correcting after education;  
(6) evading labor by self-injury or self-mutilation;  
(7) intentionally violating operating procedures in production labor, or intentionally damaging production tools;  
(8) other acts violating prison discipline.  
The period of solitary confinement imposed on a criminal in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be from seven to fifteen days.  
If a criminal commits any act listed in the first paragraph during the period of serving his sentence, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

#### 第七节　对罪犯服刑期间犯罪的处理

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　罪犯在服刑期间故意犯罪的，依法从重处罚。

**Article 59** If a criminal intentionally commits a crime during the period of serving his sentence, he shall be given a severe punishment according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对罪犯在监狱内犯罪的案件，由监狱进行侦查。侦查终结后，写出起诉意见书，连同案卷材料、证据一并移送人民检察院。

**Article 60** With respect to a case of a crime committed by a criminal within the prison, the prison shall conduct an investigation. After the investigation is concluded, the prison shall prepare a written opinion for prosecution and transfer it together with the case file materials and evidence to the People's Procuratorate.

### 第五章　对罪犯的教育改造

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　教育改造罪犯，实行因人施教、分类教育，以理服人的原则，采取集体教育与个别教育相结合、狱内教育与社会教育相结合的方法。

**Article 61** The education and reform of criminals shall implement the principles of teaching according to individual characteristics, classified education, and convincing people by reasoning, and adopt the methods of combining collective education with individual education, and combining in-prison education with social education.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　监狱应当对罪犯进行法制、道德、形势、政策、前途等内容的思想教育。

**Article 62** A prison shall conduct ideological education for criminals on such contents as legality, morality, current situation, policy, and future prospects.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　监狱应当根据不同情况，对罪犯进行扫盲教育、初等教育和初级中等教育，经考试合格的，由教育部门发给相应的学业证书。

**Article 63** A prison shall, according to different circumstances, conduct literacy education, primary education, and junior secondary education for criminals. Those who pass the examination shall be issued corresponding academic certificates by the education department.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　监狱应当根据监狱生产和罪犯释放后就业的需要，对罪犯进行职业技术教育，经考核合格的，由劳动部门发给相应的技术等级证书。

**Article 64** A prison shall, based on the needs of prison production and the employment of criminals after release, conduct vocational and technical education for criminals. Those who pass the examination shall be issued corresponding technical grade certificates by the labor department.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　监狱鼓励罪犯自学，经考试合格的，由有关部门发给相应的证书。

**Article 65** A prison shall encourage criminals to study on their own. Those who pass the examination shall be issued corresponding certificates by the relevant departments.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　罪犯的文化和职业技术教育，应当列入所在地区教育规划。监狱应当设立教室、图书阅览室等必要的教育设施。

**Article 66** The cultural and vocational-technical education of criminals shall be included in the educational plan of the area where they are located. A prison shall set up necessary educational facilities such as classrooms and reading rooms.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　监狱应当组织罪犯开展适当的体育活动和文化娱乐活动。

**Article 67** A prison shall organize criminals to engage in appropriate sports activities and cultural and recreational activities.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国家机关、社会团体、部队、企业事业单位和社会各界人士以及罪犯的亲属，应当协助监狱做好对罪犯的教育改造工作。

**Article 68** State organs, social organizations, armed forces, enterprises, institutions, persons from all walks of life, and the family members of criminals shall assist the prison in doing a good job of education and reform of criminals.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有劳动能力的罪犯，必须参加劳动。

**Article 69** Criminals capable of labor must participate in labor.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　监狱根据罪犯的个人情况，合理组织劳动，使其矫正恶习，养成劳动习惯，学会生产技能，并为释放后就业创造条件。

**Article 70** A prison shall, based on the individual circumstances of criminals, reasonably organize labor, so as to enable them to correct bad habits, develop labor habits, learn production skills, and create conditions for employment after release.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　监狱对罪犯的劳动时间，参照国家有关劳动工时的规定执行；在季节性生产等特殊情况下，可以调整劳动时间。  
罪犯有在法定节日和休息日休息的权利。

**Article 71** A prison shall, with respect to the labor time of criminals, implement it by reference to the State provisions on working hours; under special circumstances such as seasonal production, the labor time may be adjusted.  
Criminals shall have the right to rest on statutory holidays and rest days.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　监狱对参加劳动的罪犯，应当按照有关规定给予报酬并执行国家有关劳动保护的规定。

**Article 72** A prison shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, give remuneration to criminals participating in labor and implement the State provisions on labor protection.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　罪犯在劳动中致伤、致残或者死亡的，由监狱参照国家劳动保险的有关规定处理。

**Article 73** If a criminal is injured, disabled, or dies in the course of labor, the prison shall handle the matter by reference to the relevant provisions of the State on labor insurance.

### 第六章　对未成年犯的教育改造

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对未成年犯应当在未成年犯管教所执行刑罚。

**Article 74** Juvenile offenders shall serve their sentences in a juvenile correction facility.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　对未成年犯执行刑罚应当以教育改造为主。未成年犯的劳动，应当符合未成年人的特点，以学习文化和生产技能为主。  
监狱应当配合国家、社会、学校等教育机构，为未成年犯接受义务教育提供必要的条件。

**Article 75** The execution of punishment on juvenile offenders shall primarily focus on education and reform. The labor of juvenile offenders shall conform to the characteristics of juveniles, focusing on learning culture and production skills.  
A prison shall, in cooperation with the State, society, schools, and other educational institutions, provide necessary conditions for juvenile offenders to receive compulsory education.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　未成年犯年满十八周岁时，剩余刑期不超过二年的，仍可以留在未成年犯管教所执行剩余刑期。

**Article 76** When a juvenile offender reaches the age of eighteen and the remaining sentence does not exceed two years, he may still stay in the juvenile correction facility to serve the remaining sentence.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　对未成年犯的管理和教育改造，本章未作规定的，适用本法的有关规定。

**Article 77** With respect to the management and educational reform of juvenile offenders, matters not provided for in this Chapter shall be governed by the relevant provisions of this Law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　本法自公布之日起施行。

**Article 78** This Law shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
