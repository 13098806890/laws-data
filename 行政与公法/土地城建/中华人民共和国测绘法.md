# 中华人民共和国测绘法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-04-27  
**生效日期**：2017-07-01

> （1992年12月28日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过　2002年8月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议第一次修订　2017年4月27日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十七次会议第二次修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　测绘基准和测绘系统 第三章　基础测绘 第四章　界线测绘和其他测绘 第五章　测绘资质资格 第六章　测绘成果 第七章　测量标志保护 第八章　监督管理 第九章　法律责任 第十章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强测绘管理，促进测绘事业发展，保障测绘事业为经济建设、国防建设、社会发展和生态保护服务，维护国家地理信息安全，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事测绘活动，应当遵守本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强测绘管理，促进测绘事业发展，保障测绘事业为经济建设、国防建设、社会发展和生态保护服务，维护国家地理信息安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening the administration of surveying and mapping, promoting the development of surveying and mapping, ensuring that surveying and mapping serves economic construction, national defense construction, social development and ecological protection, and safeguarding the security of national geographic information.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事测绘活动，应当遵守本法。  
本法所称测绘，是指对自然地理要素或者地表人工设施的形状、大小、空间位置及其属性等进行测定、采集、表述，以及对获取的数据、信息、成果进行处理和提供的活动。

**Article 2** Any person engaging in surveying and mapping activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall comply with this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "surveying and mapping" means the activities of determining, collecting and representing the shape, size, spatial position and attributes of natural geographical elements or man-made surface structures, as well as processing and providing the data, information and results obtained.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　测绘事业是经济建设、国防建设、社会发展的基础性事业。各级人民政府应当加强对测绘工作的领导。

**Article 3** Surveying and mapping is a fundamental undertaking for economic construction, national defense construction and social development. People's governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over surveying and mapping work.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院测绘地理信息主管部门负责全国测绘工作的统一监督管理。国务院其他有关部门按照国务院规定的职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门负责本行政区域测绘工作的统一监督管理。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门按照本级人民政府规定的职责分工，负责本部门有关的测绘工作。  
军队测绘部门负责管理军事部门的测绘工作，并按照国务院、中央军事委员会规定的职责分工负责管理海洋基础测绘工作。

**Article 4** The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of surveying and mapping nationwide. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for the surveying and mapping work related to their respective functions in accordance with the division of functions prescribed by the State Council.  
The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of surveying and mapping within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the surveying and mapping work related to their respective functions in accordance with the division of functions prescribed by the people's governments at the corresponding level.  
The surveying and mapping department of the military shall be responsible for the administration of surveying and mapping of the military departments, and shall be responsible for the administration of marine basic surveying and mapping in accordance with the division of functions prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　从事测绘活动，应当使用国家规定的测绘基准和测绘系统，执行国家规定的测绘技术规范和标准。

**Article 5** Any person engaging in surveying and mapping activities shall use the surveying and mapping reference systems and coordinate systems prescribed by the State, and shall implement the technical specifications and standards for surveying and mapping prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励测绘科学技术的创新和进步，采用先进的技术和设备，提高测绘水平，推动军民融合，促进测绘成果的应用。国家加强测绘科学技术的国际交流与合作。  
对在测绘科学技术的创新和进步中做出重要贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages innovation and progress in surveying and mapping science and technology, adopts advanced technologies and equipment, improves the level of surveying and mapping, promotes civil-military integration, and facilitates the application of surveying and mapping results. The State shall strengthen international exchange and cooperation in surveying and mapping science and technology.  
Units and individuals that have made significant contributions to the innovation and progress of surveying and mapping science and technology shall be rewarded in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府和有关部门应当加强对国家版图意识的宣传教育，增强公民的国家版图意识。新闻媒体应当开展国家版图意识的宣传。教育行政部门、学校应当将国家版图意识教育纳入中小学教学内容，加强爱国主义教育。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall strengthen publicity and education on national territory awareness, and enhance citizens' awareness of national territory. News media shall carry out publicity on national territory awareness. Education administrative departments and schools shall incorporate education on national territory awareness into primary and secondary school teaching content, and strengthen patriotic education.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域从事测绘活动，应当经国务院测绘地理信息主管部门会同军队测绘部门批准，并遵守中华人民共和国有关法律、行政法规的规定。  
外国的组织或者个人在中华人民共和国领域从事测绘活动，应当与中华人民共和国有关部门或者单位合作进行，并不得涉及国家秘密和危害国家安全。

**Article 8** Any foreign organization or individual that engages in surveying and mapping activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and in other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall obtain approval from the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council jointly with the surveying and mapping department of the military, and shall comply with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China.  
Any foreign organization or individual that engages in surveying and mapping activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall cooperate with the relevant departments or units of the People's Republic of China, and shall not involve State secrets or endanger national security.

### 第二章　测绘基准和测绘系统

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家设立和采用全国统一的大地基准、高程基准、深度基准和重力基准，其数据由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门审核，并与国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘部门会商后，报国务院批准。

**Article 9** The State establishes and adopts a unified national geodetic datum, vertical datum, depth datum and gravity datum. The data thereof shall be examined by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council, and shall be reported to the State Council for approval after consultation with other relevant departments of the State Council and the surveying and mapping department of the military.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家建立全国统一的大地坐标系统、平面坐标系统、高程系统、地心坐标系统和重力测量系统，确定国家大地测量等级和精度以及国家基本比例尺地图的系列和基本精度。具体规范和要求由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘部门制定。

**Article 10** The State establishes a unified national geodetic coordinate system, plane coordinate system, vertical system, geocentric coordinate system and gravity measurement system, and determines the grades and accuracy of national geodetic surveys as well as the series and basic accuracy of national basic scale maps. The specific specifications and requirements shall be formulated by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council and the surveying and mapping department of the military.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因建设、城市规划和科学研究的需要，国家重大工程项目和国务院确定的大城市确需建立相对独立的平面坐标系统的，由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门批准；其他确需建立相对独立的平面坐标系统的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门批准。  
建立相对独立的平面坐标系统，应当与国家坐标系统相联系。

**Article 11** Where major national projects and large cities determined by the State Council need to establish a relatively independent plane coordinate system due to the needs of construction, urban planning and scientific research, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council; where other entities need to establish such a system, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The establishment of a relatively independent plane coordinate system shall be linked to the national coordinate system.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院测绘地理信息主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当会同本级人民政府其他有关部门，按照统筹建设、资源共享的原则，建立统一的卫星导航定位基准服务系统，提供导航定位基准信息公共服务。

**Article 12** The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council and the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, establish a unified satellite navigation and positioning reference service system in accordance with the principle of coordinated construction and resource sharing, and provide public services for navigation and positioning reference information.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　建设卫星导航定位基准站的，建设单位应当按照国家有关规定报国务院测绘地理信息主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门备案。国务院测绘地理信息主管部门应当汇总全国卫星导航定位基准站建设备案情况，并定期向军队测绘部门通报。  
本法所称卫星导航定位基准站，是指对卫星导航信号进行长期连续观测，并通过通信设施将观测数据实时或者定时传送至数据中心的地面固定观测站。

**Article 13** Where a satellite navigation and positioning reference station is to be constructed, the construction entity shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, report it for recordation to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council or to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council shall compile the recordation information of the construction of satellite navigation and positioning reference stations nationwide, and shall periodically notify the surveying and mapping department of the military.  
For the purposes of this Law, "satellite navigation and positioning reference station" means a fixed ground observation station that continuously monitors satellite navigation signals for a long period of time and transmits the observation data to a data center in real time or at regular intervals through communication facilities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　卫星导航定位基准站的建设和运行维护应当符合国家标准和要求，不得危害国家安全。  
卫星导航定位基准站的建设和运行维护单位应当建立数据安全保障制度，并遵守保密法律、行政法规的规定。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当会同本级人民政府其他有关部门，加强对卫星导航定位基准站建设和运行维护的规范和指导。

**Article 14** The construction, operation and maintenance of satellite navigation and positioning reference stations shall comply with national standards and requirements, and shall not endanger national security.  
The entities responsible for the construction, operation and maintenance of satellite navigation and positioning reference stations shall establish a data security protection system, and shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on confidentiality.  
The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, strengthen the regulation and guidance on the construction, operation and maintenance of satellite navigation and positioning reference stations.

### 第三章　基础测绘

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　基础测绘是公益性事业。国家对基础测绘实行分级管理。  
本法所称基础测绘，是指建立全国统一的测绘基准和测绘系统，进行基础航空摄影，获取基础地理信息的遥感资料，测制和更新国家基本比例尺地图、影像图和数字化产品，建立、更新基础地理信息系统。

**Article 15** Basic surveying and mapping is a public welfare undertaking. The State implements hierarchical administration of basic surveying and mapping.  
For the purposes of this Law, "basic surveying and mapping" means the establishment of a unified national surveying and mapping datum and surveying and mapping system, the conduct of basic aerial photography, the acquisition of remote sensing data for basic geographic information, the production and updating of national basic scale maps, image maps and digital products, and the establishment and updating of basic geographic information systems.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院测绘地理信息主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘部门组织编制全国基础测绘规划，报国务院批准后组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门会同本级人民政府其他有关部门，根据国家和上一级人民政府的基础测绘规划及本行政区域的实际情况，组织编制本行政区域的基础测绘规划，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 16** The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the State Council and the surveying and mapping department of the military, organize the formulation of the national basic surveying and mapping plan, which shall be implemented after being reported to the State Council for approval.  
The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, organize the formulation of the basic surveying and mapping plan for their respective administrative regions based on the national basic surveying and mapping plan, the basic surveying and mapping plan of the people's government at the next higher level, and the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, which shall be implemented after being reported to the people's governments at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　军队测绘部门负责编制军事测绘规划，按照国务院、中央军事委员会规定的职责分工负责编制海洋基础测绘规划，并组织实施。

**Article 17** The surveying and mapping department of the military shall be responsible for formulating the military surveying and mapping plan, and shall be responsible for formulating the marine basic surveying and mapping plan in accordance with the division of functions prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission, and shall organize the implementation thereof.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上人民政府应当将基础测绘纳入本级国民经济和社会发展年度计划，将基础测绘工作所需经费列入本级政府预算。  
国务院发展改革部门会同国务院测绘地理信息主管部门，根据全国基础测绘规划编制全国基础测绘年度计划。  
县级以上地方人民政府发展改革部门会同本级人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门，根据本行政区域的基础测绘规划编制本行政区域的基础测绘年度计划，并分别报上一级部门备案。

**Article 18** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate basic surveying and mapping into the annual plans for national economic and social development at the corresponding level, and shall include the funds required for basic surveying and mapping work in the budgets of the people's governments at the corresponding level.  
The development and reform department of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council, formulate the national annual plan for basic surveying and mapping based on the national basic surveying and mapping plan.  
The development and reform departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at the same level, formulate the annual plans for basic surveying and mapping for their respective administrative regions based on the basic surveying and mapping plans for those regions, and shall report them separately to the departments at the next higher level for recordation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　基础测绘成果应当定期更新，经济建设、国防建设、社会发展和生态保护急需的基础测绘成果应当及时更新。  
基础测绘成果的更新周期根据不同地区国民经济和社会发展的需要确定。

**Article 19** Basic surveying and mapping results shall be updated periodically, and basic surveying and mapping results urgently needed for economic construction, national defense construction, social development and ecological protection shall be updated promptly.  
The update cycle for basic surveying and mapping results shall be determined according to the needs of national economic and social development in different regions.

### 第四章　界线测绘和其他测绘

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　中华人民共和国国界线的测绘，按照中华人民共和国与相邻国家缔结的边界条约或者协定执行，由外交部组织实施。中华人民共和国地图的国界线标准样图，由外交部和国务院测绘地理信息主管部门拟定，报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 20** The surveying and mapping of the boundary lines of the People's Republic of China shall be carried out in accordance with the boundary treaties or agreements concluded between the People's Republic of China and neighboring countries, and shall be organized and implemented by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The standard sample maps of the boundary lines of the maps of the People's Republic of China shall be drawn up by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council, and shall be published after being reported to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　行政区域界线的测绘，按照国务院有关规定执行。省、自治区、直辖市和自治州、县、自治县、市行政区域界线的标准画法图，由国务院民政部门和国务院测绘地理信息主管部门拟定，报国务院批准后公布。

**Article 21** The surveying and mapping of administrative area boundary lines shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council. The standard delineation maps of the administrative area boundaries of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, autonomous prefectures, counties, autonomous counties and cities shall be drawn up by the civil affairs department of the State Council and the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council, and shall be published after being reported to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当会同本级人民政府不动产登记主管部门，加强对不动产测绘的管理。  
测量土地、建筑物、构筑物和地面其他附着物的权属界址线，应当按照县级以上人民政府确定的权属界线的界址点、界址线或者提供的有关登记资料和附图进行。权属界址线发生变化的，有关当事人应当及时进行变更测绘。

**Article 22** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with the competent departments of real estate registration of the people's governments at the same level, strengthen the administration of surveying and mapping of real estate.  
The measurement of the boundary lines of the ownership of land, buildings, structures and other attachments on the ground shall be carried out in accordance with the boundary points and boundary lines of the ownership boundaries determined by the people's governments at or above the county level, or the relevant registration materials and attached drawings provided. Where the boundary lines of ownership change, the parties concerned shall promptly carry out alteration surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　城乡建设领域的工程测量活动，与房屋产权、产籍相关的房屋面积的测量，应当执行由国务院住房城乡建设主管部门、国务院测绘地理信息主管部门组织编制的测量技术规范。  
水利、能源、交通、通信、资源开发和其他领域的工程测量活动，应当执行国家有关的工程测量技术规范。

**Article 23** Engineering surveying activities in the field of urban and rural construction, and the measurement of building areas related to building property rights and property register, shall implement the surveying technical specifications formulated by the competent department of housing and urban-rural construction under the State Council and the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council.  
Engineering surveying activities in the fields of water conservancy, energy, transportation, communications, resource development and other fields shall implement the relevant national technical specifications for engineering surveying.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　建立地理信息系统，应当采用符合国家标准的基础地理信息数据。

**Article 24** The establishment of a geographic information system shall adopt basic geographic information data that comply with national standards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当根据突发事件应对工作需要，及时提供地图、基础地理信息数据等测绘成果，做好遥感监测、导航定位等应急测绘保障工作。

**Article 25** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the needs of emergency response work, promptly provide surveying and mapping results such as maps and basic geographic information data, and shall ensure emergency surveying and mapping support services such as remote sensing monitoring, navigation and positioning.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当会同本级人民政府其他有关部门依法开展地理国情监测，并按照国家有关规定严格管理、规范使用地理国情监测成果。  
各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，发挥地理国情监测成果在政府决策、经济社会发展和社会公众服务中的作用。

**Article 26** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, carry out geographical conditions monitoring according to law, and shall strictly manage and standardize the use of geographical conditions monitoring results in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.  
People's governments at all levels shall take effective measures to give play to the role of geographical conditions monitoring results in government decision-making, economic and social development and public services.

### 第五章　测绘资质资格

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家对从事测绘活动的单位实行测绘资质管理制度。  
从事测绘活动的单位应当具备下列条件，并依法取得相应等级的测绘资质证书，方可从事测绘活动：  
（一）有法人资格；  
（二）有与从事的测绘活动相适应的专业技术人员；  
（三）有与从事的测绘活动相适应的技术装备和设施；  
（四）有健全的技术和质量保证体系、安全保障措施、信息安全保密管理制度以及测绘成果和资料档案管理制度。

**Article 27** The State implements a system of surveying and mapping qualification administration for units engaged in surveying and mapping activities.  
A unit engaging in surveying and mapping activities shall meet the following conditions and shall obtain a surveying and mapping qualification certificate of the corresponding grade according to law before it may engage in surveying and mapping activities:  
(1) Having the status of a legal person;  
(2) Having professional technical personnel suitable for the surveying and mapping activities to be engaged in;  
(3) Having technical equipment and facilities suitable for the surveying and mapping activities to be engaged in;  
(4) Having a sound technical and quality assurance system, safety assurance measures, an information security and confidentiality management system, and a management system for surveying and mapping results and data archives.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院测绘地理信息主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门按照各自的职责负责测绘资质审查、发放测绘资质证书。具体办法由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门商国务院其他有关部门规定。  
军队测绘部门负责军事测绘单位的测绘资质审查。

**Article 28** The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council and the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in accordance with their respective functions, be responsible for the examination and issuance of surveying and mapping qualification certificates. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council in consultation with other relevant departments of the State Council.  
The surveying and mapping department of the military shall be responsible for the examination of surveying and mapping qualifications of military surveying and mapping units.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　测绘单位不得超越资质等级许可的范围从事测绘活动，不得以其他测绘单位的名义从事测绘活动，不得允许其他单位以本单位的名义从事测绘活动。  
测绘项目实行招投标的，测绘项目的招标单位应当依法在招标公告或者投标邀请书中对测绘单位资质等级作出要求，不得让不具有相应测绘资质等级的单位中标，不得让测绘单位低于测绘成本中标。  
中标的测绘单位不得向他人转让测绘项目。

**Article 29** A surveying and mapping unit shall not engage in surveying and mapping activities beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade, shall not engage in surveying and mapping activities in the name of another surveying and mapping unit, and shall not permit another unit to engage in surveying and mapping activities in its name.  
Where a surveying and mapping project is subject to tender, the tenderee of the surveying and mapping project shall, according to law, specify the requirements for the qualification grade of surveying and mapping units in the tender announcement or the invitation to bid, and shall not allow a unit without the corresponding surveying and mapping qualification grade to win the bid, and shall not allow a surveying and mapping unit to win the bid at a price lower than the cost of surveying and mapping.  
The surveying and mapping unit that wins the bid shall not assign the surveying and mapping project to another person.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从事测绘活动的专业技术人员应当具备相应的执业资格条件。具体办法由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门会同国务院人力资源社会保障主管部门规定。

**Article 30** Professional technical personnel engaging in surveying and mapping activities shall meet the corresponding professional qualification requirements. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council jointly with the competent department of human resources and social security under the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　测绘人员进行测绘活动时，应当持有测绘作业证件。  
任何单位和个人不得阻碍测绘人员依法进行测绘活动。

**Article 31** When conducting surveying and mapping activities, a surveying and mapping worker shall hold a surveying and mapping operation certificate.  
No unit or individual may obstruct a surveying and mapping worker from carrying out surveying and mapping activities according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　测绘单位的测绘资质证书、测绘专业技术人员的执业证书和测绘人员的测绘作业证件的式样，由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门统一规定。

**Article 32** The formats of the surveying and mapping qualification certificate of a surveying and mapping unit, the practice certificate of a surveying and mapping professional technical personnel, and the surveying and mapping operation certificate of a surveying and mapping worker shall be uniformly prescribed by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council.

### 第六章　测绘成果

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家实行测绘成果汇交制度。国家依法保护测绘成果的知识产权。  
测绘项目完成后，测绘项目出资人或者承担国家投资的测绘项目的单位，应当向国务院测绘地理信息主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门汇交测绘成果资料。属于基础测绘项目的，应当汇交测绘成果副本；属于非基础测绘项目的，应当汇交测绘成果目录。负责接收测绘成果副本和目录的测绘地理信息主管部门应当出具测绘成果汇交凭证，并及时将测绘成果副本和目录移交给保管单位。测绘成果汇交的具体办法由国务院规定。  
国务院测绘地理信息主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当及时编制测绘成果目录，并向社会公布。

**Article 33** The State implements a system for the submission of surveying and mapping results. The State protects the intellectual property rights of surveying and mapping results according to law.  
After the completion of a surveying and mapping project, the sponsor of the surveying and mapping project or the unit undertaking the surveying and mapping project funded by the State shall submit the surveying and mapping result data to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council or to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government. If the project is a basic surveying and mapping project, a copy of the surveying and mapping results shall be submitted; if the project is a non-basic surveying and mapping project, a catalogue of the surveying and mapping results shall be submitted. The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation responsible for receiving copies and catalogues of surveying and mapping results shall issue a certificate of submission of surveying and mapping results, and shall promptly transfer the copies and catalogues to the custodian. The specific measures for the submission of surveying and mapping results shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council and the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall promptly compile catalogues of surveying and mapping results and make them public.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当积极推进公众版测绘成果的加工和编制工作，通过提供公众版测绘成果、保密技术处理等方式，促进测绘成果的社会化应用。  
测绘成果保管单位应当采取措施保障测绘成果的完整和安全，并按照国家有关规定向社会公开和提供利用。  
测绘成果属于国家秘密的，适用保密法律、行政法规的规定；需要对外提供的，按照国务院和中央军事委员会规定的审批程序执行。  
测绘成果的秘密范围和秘密等级，应当依照保密法律、行政法规的规定，按照保障国家秘密安全、促进地理信息共享和应用的原则确定并及时调整、公布。

**Article 34** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall actively promote the processing and compilation of public version surveying and mapping results, and shall facilitate the social application of surveying and mapping results by means such as providing public version surveying and mapping results and conducting confidential technical treatment.  
The units responsible for the custody of surveying and mapping results shall take measures to ensure the integrity and security of the surveying and mapping results, and shall make them public and provide them for use in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.  
Where surveying and mapping results are State secrets, the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on confidentiality shall apply; where they need to be provided to foreign parties, the examination and approval procedures prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission shall be followed.  
The scope and classification of secrets of surveying and mapping results shall be determined, promptly adjusted and made public in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on confidentiality, based on the principles of safeguarding State secrets and promoting the sharing and application of geographic information.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　使用财政资金的测绘项目和涉及测绘的其他使用财政资金的项目，有关部门在批准立项前应当征求本级人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门的意见；有适宜测绘成果的，应当充分利用已有的测绘成果，避免重复测绘。

**Article 35** For surveying and mapping projects using fiscal funds and other projects involving surveying and mapping using fiscal funds, the relevant departments shall, before approving the project, seek the opinions of the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government at the same level; if suitable surveying and mapping results exist, they shall fully utilize the existing surveying and mapping results to avoid redundant surveying and mapping.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　基础测绘成果和国家投资完成的其他测绘成果，用于政府决策、国防建设和公共服务的，应当无偿提供。  
除前款规定情形外，测绘成果依法实行有偿使用制度。但是，各级人民政府及有关部门和军队因防灾减灾、应对突发事件、维护国家安全等公共利益的需要，可以无偿使用。  
测绘成果使用的具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 36** Basic surveying and mapping results and other surveying and mapping results completed with State investment shall be provided free of charge if they are used for government decision-making, national defense construction and public services.  
Except for the circumstances provided for in the preceding paragraph, surveying and mapping results shall be subject to a paid use system according to law. However, people's governments at all levels, relevant departments and the military may use them free of charge for the needs of public interests such as disaster prevention and mitigation, emergency response and safeguarding national security.  
The specific measures for the use of surveying and mapping results shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域的位置、高程、深度、面积、长度等重要地理信息数据，由国务院测绘地理信息主管部门审核，并与国务院其他有关部门、军队测绘部门会商后，报国务院批准，由国务院或者国务院授权的部门公布。

**Article 37** Important geographic information data such as the position, elevation, depth, area and length of the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be examined by the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation under the State Council, and after consultation with other relevant departments of the State Council and the surveying and mapping department of the military, shall be reported to the State Council for approval, and shall be published by the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　地图的编制、出版、展示、登载及更新应当遵守国家有关地图编制标准、地图内容表示、地图审核的规定。  
互联网地图服务提供者应当使用经依法审核批准的地图，建立地图数据安全管理制度，采取安全保障措施，加强对互联网地图新增内容的核校，提高服务质量。  
县级以上人民政府和测绘地理信息主管部门、网信部门等有关部门应当加强对地图编制、出版、展示、登载和互联网地图服务的监督管理，保证地图质量，维护国家主权、安全和利益。  
地图管理的具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 38** The compilation, publication, display, loading and updating of maps shall comply with the national regulations on map compilation standards, map content representation and map review.  
Providers of Internet map services shall use maps that have been legally reviewed and approved, establish a map data security management system, take security measures, strengthen the verification of new content on Internet maps, and improve service quality.  
People's governments at or above the county level, the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation, the network information departments and other relevant departments shall strengthen the supervision and administration of map compilation, publication, display, loading and Internet map services, ensure the quality of maps, and safeguard national sovereignty, security and interests.  
The specific measures for the administration of maps shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　测绘单位应当对完成的测绘成果质量负责。县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当加强对测绘成果质量的监督管理。

**Article 39** A surveying and mapping unit shall be responsible for the quality of the surveying and mapping results it has completed. The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and administration of the quality of surveying and mapping results.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家鼓励发展地理信息产业，推动地理信息产业结构调整和优化升级，支持开发各类地理信息产品，提高产品质量，推广使用安全可信的地理信息技术和设备。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立健全政府部门间地理信息资源共建共享机制，引导和支持企业提供地理信息社会化服务，促进地理信息广泛应用。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当及时获取、处理、更新基础地理信息数据，通过地理信息公共服务平台向社会提供地理信息公共服务，实现地理信息数据开放共享。

**Article 40** The State encourages the development of the geographic information industry, promotes the adjustment, optimization and upgrading of the geographic information industry structure, supports the development of various geographic information products, improves product quality, and promotes the use of safe and reliable geographic information technologies and equipment.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve the mechanism for the joint construction and sharing of geographic information resources among government departments, guide and support enterprises in providing socialized geographic information services, and promote the wide application of geographic information.  
The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall promptly acquire, process and update basic geographic information data, provide geographic information public services to the public through the geographic information public service platform, and achieve the openness and sharing of geographic information data.

### 第七章　测量标志保护

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　任何单位和个人不得损毁或者擅自移动永久性测量标志和正在使用中的临时性测量标志，不得侵占永久性测量标志用地，不得在永久性测量标志安全控制范围内从事危害测量标志安全和使用效能的活动。  
本法所称永久性测量标志，是指各等级的三角点、基线点、导线点、军用控制点、重力点、天文点、水准点和卫星定位点的觇标和标石标志，以及用于地形测图、工程测量和形变测量的固定标志和海底大地点设施。

**Article 41** No unit or individual may damage or arbitrarily remove permanent surveying markers or temporary surveying markers in use, encroach upon the land used for permanent surveying markers, or engage in activities within the safety control range of permanent surveying markers that endanger the safety or effectiveness of such markers.  
For the purposes of this Law, "permanent surveying markers" means the observation pillars and mark stones of triangulation points, baseline points, traverse points, military control points, gravity points, astronomical points, leveling points and satellite positioning points of various grades, as well as fixed markers used for topographic mapping, engineering surveying and deformation surveying, and seabed geodetic point facilities.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　永久性测量标志的建设单位应当对永久性测量标志设立明显标记，并委托当地有关单位指派专人负责保管。

**Article 42** The construction entity of permanent surveying markers shall set up conspicuous marks on those markers, and shall entrust the relevant local unit to assign a person to be responsible for their custody.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　进行工程建设，应当避开永久性测量标志；确实无法避开，需要拆迁永久性测量标志或者使永久性测量标志失去使用效能的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门批准；涉及军用控制点的，应当征得军队测绘部门的同意。所需迁建费用由工程建设单位承担。

**Article 43** When carrying out engineering construction, permanent surveying markers shall be avoided; if they cannot be avoided and it is necessary to relocate the permanent surveying markers or render them ineffective, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government of the relevant province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; if military control points are involved, the consent of the surveying and mapping department of the military shall be obtained. The costs for relocation and reconstruction shall be borne by the engineering construction entity.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　测绘人员使用永久性测量标志，应当持有测绘作业证件，并保证测量标志的完好。  
保管测量标志的人员应当查验测量标志使用后的完好状况。

**Article 44** A surveying and mapping worker using a permanent surveying marker shall hold a surveying and mapping operation certificate and shall ensure the integrity of the marker.  
The person in charge of the custody of surveying markers shall inspect the condition of the marker after use.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府应当采取有效措施加强测量标志的保护工作。  
县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当按照规定检查、维护永久性测量标志。  
乡级人民政府应当做好本行政区域内的测量标志保护工作。

**Article 45** People's governments at or above the county level shall take effective measures to strengthen the protection of surveying markers.  
The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the regulations, inspect and maintain permanent surveying markers.  
Township people's governments shall do a good job in the protection of surveying markers within their respective administrative regions.

### 第八章　监督管理

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当会同本级人民政府其他有关部门建立地理信息安全管理制度和技术防控体系，并加强对地理信息安全的监督管理。

**Article 46** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, jointly with other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, establish a geographic information security management system and a technical prevention and control system, and shall strengthen the supervision and administration of geographic information security.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　地理信息生产、保管、利用单位应当对属于国家秘密的地理信息的获取、持有、提供、利用情况进行登记并长期保存，实行可追溯管理。  
从事测绘活动涉及获取、持有、提供、利用属于国家秘密的地理信息，应当遵守保密法律、行政法规和国家有关规定。  
地理信息生产、利用单位和互联网地图服务提供者收集、使用用户个人信息的，应当遵守法律、行政法规关于个人信息保护的规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国测绘成果管理条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国测绘成果管理条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 47** Units that produce, store or use geographic information shall register and keep for a long term the records of the acquisition, possession, provision and use of geographic information that is classified as State secrets, and shall implement traceable management.  
Where surveying and mapping activities involve the acquisition, possession, provision or use of geographic information that is classified as State secrets, the provisions of laws, administrative regulations and relevant regulations of the State on confidentiality shall be complied with.  
Where geographic information production and utilization units and Internet map service providers collect and use users' personal information, they shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the protection of personal information.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当对测绘单位实行信用管理，并依法将其信用信息予以公示。

**Article 48** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall implement credit management over surveying and mapping units, and shall publicize their credit information according to law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门应当建立健全随机抽查机制，依法履行监督检查职责，发现涉嫌违反本法规定行为的，可以依法采取下列措施：  
（一）查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿、登记台账以及其他有关文件、资料；  
（二）查封、扣押与涉嫌违法测绘行为直接相关的设备、工具、原材料、测绘成果资料等。  
被检查的单位和个人应当配合，如实提供有关文件、资料，不得隐瞒、拒绝和阻碍。  
任何单位和个人对违反本法规定的行为，有权向县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门举报。接到举报的测绘地理信息主管部门应当及时依法处理。

**Article 49** The competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a random inspection mechanism, perform supervision and inspection duties according to law, and where they discover conduct suspected of violating the provisions of this Law, they may take the following measures according to law:  
(1) Inspecting and copying relevant contracts, invoices, account books, registration ledgers and other related documents and materials;  
(2) Sealing up or seizing equipment, tools, raw materials, surveying and mapping result data, etc., directly related to the suspected illegal surveying and mapping.  
The units and individuals subject to inspection shall cooperate and truthfully provide relevant documents and materials, and shall not conceal, refuse or obstruct.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report to the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's government at or above the county level any conduct that violates the provisions of this Law. The competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation that receives a report shall promptly handle it according to law.

### 第九章　法律责任

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门或者其他有关部门工作人员利用职务上的便利收受他人财物、其他好处或者玩忽职守，对不符合法定条件的单位核发测绘资质证书，不依法履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a staff member of the competent department of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of a people's government at or above the county level or of another relevant department takes advantage of his or her position to accept property or other benefits from others, or neglects his or her duties, issues a surveying and mapping qualification certificate to a unit that does not meet the statutory conditions, fails to perform supervision and administration duties according to law, or fails to investigate and deal with illegal conduct discovered, the person in charge with responsibility and the directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本法规定，外国的组织或者个人未经批准，或者未与中华人民共和国有关部门、单位合作，擅自从事测绘活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得、测绘成果和测绘工具，并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，并处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，限期出境或者驱逐出境；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a foreign organization or individual engages in surveying and mapping activities without approval or without cooperating with the relevant departments or units of the People's Republic of China, the illegal conduct shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains, surveying and mapping results and surveying and mapping tools shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall also be imposed, and the person shall be ordered to leave the country within a specified period or be deported; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本法规定，未经批准擅自建立相对独立的平面坐标系统，或者采用不符合国家标准的基础地理信息数据建立地理信息系统的，给予警告，责令改正，可以并处五十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 52** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a relatively independent plane coordinate system is established without approval, or a geographic information system is established using basic geographic information data that do not comply with national standards, a warning shall be given, the entity shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan may also be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本法规定，卫星导航定位基准站建设单位未报备案的，给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处十万元以上三十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 53** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the construction entity of a satellite navigation and positioning reference station fails to report for recordation, a warning shall be given, and the entity shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 300,000 yuan shall be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，卫星导航定位基准站的建设和运行维护不符合国家标准、要求的，给予警告，责令限期改正，没收违法所得和测绘成果，并处三十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；逾期不改正的，没收相关设备；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 54** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the construction, operation and maintenance of a satellite navigation and positioning reference station do not comply with national standards and requirements, a warning shall be given, the entity shall be ordered to make corrections within a specified period, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 300,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, the relevant equipment shall be confiscated; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，未取得测绘资质证书，擅自从事测绘活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和测绘成果，并处测绘约定报酬一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，没收测绘工具。  
以欺骗手段取得测绘资质证书从事测绘活动的，吊销测绘资质证书，没收违法所得和测绘成果，并处测绘约定报酬一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，没收测绘工具。

**Article 55** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a unit engages in surveying and mapping activities without obtaining a surveying and mapping qualification certificate, the illegal conduct shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed remuneration for surveying and mapping shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping tools shall be confiscated.  
Where a unit obtains a surveying and mapping qualification certificate by fraudulent means and engages in surveying and mapping activities, the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed remuneration for surveying and mapping shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping tools shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，测绘单位有下列行为之一的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和测绘成果，处测绘约定报酬一倍以上二倍以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿或者降低测绘资质等级；情节严重的，吊销测绘资质证书：  
（一）超越资质等级许可的范围从事测绘活动；  
（二）以其他测绘单位的名义从事测绘活动；  
（三）允许其他单位以本单位的名义从事测绘活动。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a surveying and mapping unit commits any of the following acts, it shall be ordered to cease the illegal conduct, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed remuneration for surveying and mapping shall be imposed, and it may also be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or its surveying and mapping qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) Engaging in surveying and mapping activities beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade;  
(2) Engaging in surveying and mapping activities in the name of another surveying and mapping unit;  
(3) Permitting another unit to engage in surveying and mapping activities in its name.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，测绘项目的招标单位让不具有相应资质等级的测绘单位中标，或者让测绘单位低于测绘成本中标的，责令改正，可以处测绘约定报酬二倍以下的罚款。招标单位的工作人员利用职务上的便利，索取他人财物，或者非法收受他人财物为他人谋取利益的，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the tenderee of a surveying and mapping project allows a surveying and mapping unit without the corresponding qualification grade to win the bid, or allows a surveying and mapping unit to win the bid at a price lower than the cost of surveying and mapping, the tenderee shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than two times the agreed remuneration for surveying and mapping may be imposed. Where a staff member of the tenderee takes advantage of his or her position to solicit property from others, or illegally accepts property from others and seeks benefits for them, sanctions shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，中标的测绘单位向他人转让测绘项目的，责令改正，没收违法所得，处测绘约定报酬一倍以上二倍以下的罚款，并可以责令停业整顿或者降低测绘资质等级；情节严重的，吊销测绘资质证书。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the surveying and mapping unit that wins the bid assigns the surveying and mapping project to another person, the unit shall be ordered to make corrections, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the agreed remuneration for surveying and mapping shall be imposed, and it may also be ordered to suspend operations for rectification or its surveying and mapping qualification grade may be lowered; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，未取得测绘执业资格，擅自从事测绘活动的，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得和测绘成果，对其所在单位可以处违法所得二倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，没收测绘工具；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a person engages in surveying and mapping activities without obtaining a surveying and mapping practice qualification, the illegal conduct shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains and surveying and mapping results shall be confiscated, and the unit to which the person belongs may be fined not more than two times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, the surveying and mapping tools shall be confiscated; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，不汇交测绘成果资料的，责令限期汇交；测绘项目出资人逾期不汇交的，处重测所需费用一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；承担国家投资的测绘项目的单位逾期不汇交的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，并处暂扣测绘资质证书，自暂扣测绘资质证书之日起六个月内仍不汇交的，吊销测绘资质证书；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 60** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, surveying and mapping result data are not submitted, the entity shall be ordered to submit them within a specified period; if the sponsor of the surveying and mapping project fails to submit them within the specified period, a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the cost required for re-surveying and mapping shall be imposed; if the unit undertaking the surveying and mapping project funded by the State fails to submit them within the specified period, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be temporarily withheld; if the data are still not submitted within six months from the date of temporary withholding, the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本法规定，擅自发布中华人民共和国领域和中华人民共和国管辖的其他海域的重要地理信息数据的，给予警告，责令改正，可以并处五十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, important geographic information data of the territory of the People's Republic of China or other sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China are published without authorization, a warning shall be given, the entity shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 500,000 yuan may also be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，编制、出版、展示、登载、更新的地图或者互联网地图服务不符合国家有关地图管理规定的，依法给予行政处罚、处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the compilation, publication, display, loading or updating of maps, or Internet map services do not comply with the relevant national regulations on map administration, administrative penalties or sanctions shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，测绘成果质量不合格的，责令测绘单位补测或者重测；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处降低测绘资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the quality of surveying and mapping results is substandard, the surveying and mapping unit shall be ordered to carry out supplementary survey or re-survey; if the circumstances are serious, the unit shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and its surveying and mapping qualification grade shall be lowered or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，给予警告，责令改正，可以并处二十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）损毁、擅自移动永久性测量标志或者正在使用中的临时性测量标志；  
（二）侵占永久性测量标志用地；  
（三）在永久性测量标志安全控制范围内从事危害测量标志安全和使用效能的活动；  
（四）擅自拆迁永久性测量标志或者使永久性测量标志失去使用效能，或者拒绝支付迁建费用；  
（五）违反操作规程使用永久性测量标志，造成永久性测量标志毁损。

**Article 64** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, a warning shall be given, the person shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan may also be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Damaging or arbitrarily removing permanent surveying markers or temporary surveying markers in use;  
(2) Encroaching upon the land used for permanent surveying markers;  
(3) Engaging in activities within the safety control range of permanent surveying markers that endanger the safety or effectiveness of such markers;  
(4) Arbitrarily relocating permanent surveying markers or rendering them ineffective, or refusing to pay the costs for relocation and reconstruction;  
(5) Using permanent surveying markers in violation of operational procedures, causing damage or destruction to the markers.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，地理信息生产、保管、利用单位未对属于国家秘密的地理信息的获取、持有、提供、利用情况进行登记、长期保存的，给予警告，责令改正，可以并处二十万元以下的罚款；泄露国家秘密的，责令停业整顿，并处降低测绘资质等级或者吊销测绘资质证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本法规定，获取、持有、提供、利用属于国家秘密的地理信息的，给予警告，责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得二倍以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 65** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a unit that produces, stores or uses geographic information fails to register and keep for a long term the records of the acquisition, possession, provision or use of geographic information that is classified as State secrets, a warning shall be given, the unit shall be ordered to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan may also be imposed; if State secrets are divulged, the unit shall be ordered to suspend operations for rectification, and its surveying and mapping qualification grade shall be lowered or its surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be revoked; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a person acquires, possesses, provides or uses geographic information that is classified as State secrets, a warning shall be given, the illegal conduct shall be ordered to cease, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than two times the illegal gains may also be imposed; the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if losses are caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　本法规定的降低测绘资质等级、暂扣测绘资质证书、吊销测绘资质证书的行政处罚，由颁发测绘资质证书的部门决定；其他行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府测绘地理信息主管部门决定。  
[本法第五十一条](#art-51)规定的限期出境和驱逐出境由公安机关依法决定并执行。

**Article 66** The administrative penalties provided for in this Law of lowering the surveying and mapping qualification grade, temporarily withholding the surveying and mapping qualification certificate and revoking the surveying and mapping qualification certificate shall be decided by the department that issued the certificate; other administrative penalties shall be decided by the competent departments of surveying, mapping and geoinformation of the people's governments at or above the county level.  
The orders to leave the country within a specified period and deportation provided for in Article 51 of this Law shall be decided and enforced by the public security organs according to law.

### 第十章　附则

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　军事测绘管理办法由中央军事委员会根据本法规定。

**Article 67** The measures for the administration of military surveying and mapping shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　本法自2017年7月1日起施行。

**Article 68** This Law shall come into force as of July 1, 2017.
