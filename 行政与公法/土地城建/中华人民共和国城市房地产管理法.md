### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对城市房地产的管理，维护房地产市场秩序，保障房地产权利人的合法权益，促进房地产业的健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated with a view to strengthening the administration of urban real estate, maintaining the order of the real estate market, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of real estate right holders, and promoting the healthy development of the real estate industry.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国城市规划区国有土地（以下简称国有土地）范围内取得房地产开发用地的土地使用权，从事房地产开发、房地产交易，实施房地产管理，应当遵守本法。  
本法所称房屋，是指土地上的房屋等建筑物及构筑物。  
本法所称房地产开发，是指在依据本法取得国有土地使用权的土地上进行基础设施、房屋建设的行为。  
本法所称房地产交易，包括房地产转让、房地产抵押和房屋租赁。

**Article 2** Any person who obtains the right to the use of land for real estate development, engages in real estate development or real estate transactions, or conducts real estate administration within the area of State-owned land (hereinafter referred to as State-owned land) in the urban planning zones of the People's Republic of China shall comply with this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "houses" means buildings and structures on land.  
For the purposes of this Law, "real estate development" means the conduct of infrastructure and house construction on the land for which the right to the use of State-owned land has been obtained in accordance with this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "real estate transactions" include the transfer of real estate, the mortgage of real estate, and the lease of houses.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家依法实行国有土地有偿、有限期使用制度。但是，国家在本法规定的范围内划拨国有土地使用权的除外。

**Article 3** The State shall, according to law, implement a system of compensated and time-limited use of State-owned land, except where the State allocates the right to the use of State-owned land within the scope provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家根据社会、经济发展水平，扶持发展居民住宅建设，逐步改善居民的居住条件。

**Article 4** The State shall, in light of the level of social and economic development, support and develop the construction of residential houses for residents and gradually improve the housing conditions of residents.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　房地产权利人应当遵守法律和行政法规，依法纳税。房地产权利人的合法权益受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。

**Article 5** Real estate right holders shall abide by laws and administrative regulations and pay taxes according to law. The legitimate rights and interests of real estate right holders shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　为了公共利益的需要，国家可以征收国有土地上单位和个人的房屋，并依法给予拆迁补偿，维护被征收人的合法权益；征收个人住宅的，还应当保障被征收人的居住条件。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 6** For the needs of the public interest, the State may expropriate houses owned by units or individuals on State-owned land, and shall provide demolition compensation according to law, protecting the legitimate rights and interests of the persons whose houses are expropriated. Where individual residential houses are expropriated, the State shall also ensure the housing conditions of the persons whose houses are expropriated. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院建设行政主管部门、土地管理部门依照国务院规定的职权划分，各司其职，密切配合，管理全国房地产工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府房产管理、土地管理部门的机构设置及其职权由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定。

**Article 7** The administrative department of construction and the land administrative department under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of functions and powers prescribed by the State Council, perform their respective duties, cooperate closely, and administer the real estate work nationwide.  
The institutional setup and functions of the real estate administrative departments and the land administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第二章　房地产开发用地

#### 第一节　土地使用权出让

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　土地使用权出让，是指国家将国有土地使用权（以下简称土地使用权）在一定年限内出让给土地使用者，由土地使用者向国家支付土地使用权出让金的行为。

**Article 8** The assignment of land-use right refers to the act whereby the State assigns the right to use State-owned land (hereinafter referred to as the land-use right) to a land user for a certain number of years, and the land user shall pay the State a grant fee for the land-use right.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市规划区内的集体所有的土地，经依法征收转为国有土地后，该幅国有土地的使用权方可有偿出让，但法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 9** The right to use collectively-owned land within urban planning areas may be assigned for a consideration only after it has been lawfully expropriated and converted into State-owned land, except where this Law provides otherwise.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　土地使用权出让，必须符合土地利用总体规划、城市规划和年度建设用地计划。

**Article 10** The assignment of land-use right must conform to the overall plan for land utilization, urban planning, and the annual plan for land used for construction purposes.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府出让土地使用权用于房地产开发的，须根据省级以上人民政府下达的控制指标拟订年度出让土地使用权总面积方案，按照国务院规定，报国务院或者省级人民政府批准。

**Article 11** Where local people's governments at or above the county level assign land-use rights for real estate development, they must, according to the control quotas issued by people's governments at or above the provincial level, draw up a plan of the total area of annual land-use right assignment, and submit it for approval to the State Council or the people's government at the provincial level in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地使用权出让，由市、县人民政府有计划、有步骤地进行。出让的每幅地块、用途、年限和其他条件，由市、县人民政府土地管理部门会同城市规划、建设、房产管理部门共同拟定方案，按照国务院规定，报经有批准权的人民政府批准后，由市、县人民政府土地管理部门实施。  
直辖市的县人民政府及其有关部门行使前款规定的权限，由直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 12** The assignment of land-use right shall be conducted in a planned and step-by-step manner by the people's governments of cities and counties. The plan for each parcel of land to be assigned, including its purpose, duration and other conditions, shall be drawn up jointly by the land administration departments of the people's governments of cities and counties, together with the departments of urban planning, construction, and real estate administration. The plan shall then be implemented by the land administration departments of the people's governments of cities and counties after reporting to and receiving approval from the people's government at the level with the power of approval, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
The people's governments and their relevant departments of counties directly under a municipality shall exercise the powers provided in the preceding paragraph as prescribed by the people's government of the municipality.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　土地使用权出让，可以采取拍卖、招标或者双方协议的方式。  
商业、旅游、娱乐和豪华住宅用地，有条件的，必须采取拍卖、招标方式；没有条件，不能采取拍卖、招标方式的，可以采取双方协议的方式。  
采取双方协议方式出让土地使用权的出让金不得低于按国家规定所确定的最低价。

**Article 13** The assignment of land-use right may be carried out by means of auction, invitation to tender, or agreement between the two parties.  
For land used for commercial, tourist, recreational, and luxury residential purposes, where conditions permit, the assignment must be carried out by means of auction or invitation to tender; where conditions do not permit and neither auction nor invitation to tender can be adopted, the assignment may be carried out by means of agreement between the two parties.  
The grant fee for the assignment of land-use right carried out by means of agreement between the two parties shall not be lower than the minimum price determined in accordance with the provisions of the State.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　土地使用权出让最高年限由国务院规定。

**Article 14** The maximum duration for the assignment of land-use right shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　土地使用权出让，应当签订书面出让合同。  
土地使用权出让合同由市、县人民政府土地管理部门与土地使用者签订。

**Article 15** A written assignment contract shall be signed for the assignment of land-use right.  
The land-use right assignment contract shall be signed between the land administration department of the people's government of a city or county and the land user.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　土地使用者必须按照出让合同约定，支付土地使用权出让金；未按照出让合同约定支付土地使用权出让金的，土地管理部门有权解除合同，并可以请求违约赔偿。

**Article 16** A land user must pay the grant fee for land-use right as agreed upon in the assignment contract; where the land user fails to pay the grant fee as agreed upon in the assignment contract, the land administration department shall have the right to dissolve the contract and may claim damages for breach of contract.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　土地使用者按照出让合同约定支付土地使用权出让金的，市、县人民政府土地管理部门必须按照出让合同约定，提供出让的土地；未按照出让合同约定提供出让的土地的，土地使用者有权解除合同，由土地管理部门返还土地使用权出让金，土地使用者并可以请求违约赔偿。

**Article 17** Where a land user has paid the grant fee for land-use right as agreed upon in the assignment contract, the land administration department of the people's government of the city or county must provide the assigned land as agreed upon in the assignment contract; where the land administration department fails to provide the assigned land as agreed upon, the land user shall have the right to dissolve the contract, the land administration department shall return the grant fee for land-use right, and the land user may claim damages for breach of contract.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　土地使用者需要改变土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途的，必须取得出让方和市、县人民政府城市规划行政主管部门的同意，签订土地使用权出让合同变更协议或者重新签订土地使用权出让合同，相应调整土地使用权出让金。

**Article 18** Where a land user needs to change the land use purpose agreed upon in the land-use right assignment contract, the land user must obtain the consent of the assignor and the competent administrative department of urban planning of the people's government of the city or county, and sign a supplementary agreement to modify the land-use right assignment contract or enter into a new land-use right assignment contract, and correspondingly adjust the grant fee for land-use right.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　土地使用权出让金应当全部上缴财政，列入预算，用于城市基础设施建设和土地开发。土地使用权出让金上缴和使用的具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 19** All grant fees for land-use right shall be turned over to the State Treasury, included in the budget, and used for urban infrastructure construction and land development. The specific measures for the turning over and use of the grant fees for land-use right shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家对土地使用者依法取得的土地使用权，在出让合同约定的使用年限届满前不收回；在特殊情况下，根据社会公共利益的需要，可以依照法律程序提前收回，并根据土地使用者使用土地的实际年限和开发土地的实际情况给予相应的补偿。

**Article 20** The State shall not withdraw the land-use right lawfully obtained by a land user before the expiry of the term of use agreed upon in the assignment contract; under special circumstances, where the needs of social and public interests require such withdrawal, the State may withdraw the land-use right before the expiry of the term in accordance with legal procedures, and shall give the land user corresponding compensation based on the actual years of use and the actual development of the land.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　土地使用权因土地灭失而终止。

**Article 21** The land-use right shall be terminated when the land ceases to exist.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　土地使用权出让合同约定的使用年限届满，土地使用者需要继续使用土地的，应当至迟于届满前一年申请续期，除根据社会公共利益需要收回该幅土地的，应当予以批准。经批准准予续期的，应当重新签订土地使用权出让合同，依照规定支付土地使用权出让金。  
土地使用权出让合同约定的使用年限届满，土地使用者未申请续期或者虽申请续期但依照前款规定未获批准的，土地使用权由国家无偿收回。

**Article 22** Upon the expiry of the term of use agreed upon in the land-use right assignment contract, where the land user needs to continue using the land, the land user shall apply for an extension not later than one year before the expiry of the term; unless the land parcel is to be withdrawn due to the needs of social and public interests, the application shall be approved. Where the application for extension is approved upon examination, a new land-use right assignment contract shall be signed, and the grant fee for land-use right shall be paid in accordance with the provisions.  
Where, upon the expiry of the term of use agreed upon in the land-use right assignment contract, the land user neither applies for an extension nor obtains approval for the application for extension in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the land-use right shall be withdrawn by the State without compensation.

#### 第二节　土地使用权划拨

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　土地使用权划拨，是指县级以上人民政府依法批准，在土地使用者缴纳补偿、安置等费用后将该幅土地交付其使用，或者将土地使用权无偿交付给土地使用者使用的行为。  
依照本法规定以划拨方式取得土地使用权的，除法律、行政法规另有规定外，没有使用期限的限制。

**Article 23** The allocation of land-use right refers to the act whereby the people's government at or above the county level, upon approval in accordance with law, delivers the land to a land user for use after the land user has paid compensation, resettlement, and other costs, or grants the land-use right to a land user without compensation.  
Where a land-use right is obtained by means of allocation in accordance with the provisions of this Law, no limitation on the term of use shall be imposed, except as otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　下列建设用地的土地使用权，确属必需的，可以由县级以上人民政府依法批准划拨：  
（一）国家机关用地和军事用地；  
（二）城市基础设施用地和公益事业用地；  
（三）国家重点扶持的能源、交通、水利等项目用地；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他用地。

**Article 24** The right to use land for the following types of construction purposes may be allocated upon approval by the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with law where such allocation is truly necessary:  
(1) Land used for State organs and military purposes;  
(2) Land used for urban infrastructure and public welfare undertakings;  
(3) Land used for key energy, transportation, water conservancy, and other projects supported by the State;  
(4) Other land uses as provided for by laws and administrative regulations.

### 第三章　房地产开发

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　房地产开发必须严格执行城市规划，按照经济效益、社会效益、环境效益相统一的原则，实行全面规划、合理布局、综合开发、配套建设。

**Article 25** Real estate development shall strictly comply with urban planning, and shall be carried out in accordance with the principle of integrating economic, social, and environmental benefits, by implementing comprehensive planning, rational layout, integrated development, and coordinated construction.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　以出让方式取得土地使用权进行房地产开发的，必须按照土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途、动工开发期限开发土地。超过出让合同约定的动工开发日期满一年未动工开发的，可以征收相当于土地使用权出让金百分之二十以下的土地闲置费；满二年未动工开发的，可以无偿收回土地使用权；但是，因不可抗力或者政府、政府有关部门的行为或者动工开发必需的前期工作造成动工开发迟延的除外。

**Article 26** Where the right to the use of land is obtained by means of concession for real estate development, the land shall be developed in accordance with the land use purpose and the development commencement period agreed upon in the contract for the concession of the right to the use of land. Where development has not commenced within one year after the expiration of the development commencement period agreed upon in the concession contract, a land idle fee of not more than 20 percent of the land concession price may be levied; where development has not commenced within two years, the right to the use of land may be taken back without compensation, except where the delay in commencing development is caused by force majeure, or by acts of the government or relevant government departments, or by the necessary preliminary work for commencing development.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　房地产开发项目的设计、施工，必须符合国家的有关标准和规范。  
房地产开发项目竣工，经验收合格后，方可交付使用。

**Article 27** The design and construction of real estate development projects shall conform to the relevant national standards and specifications.  
A real estate development project may be delivered for use only after it has been completed and has passed inspection.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　依法取得的土地使用权，可以依照本法和有关法律、行政法规的规定，作价入股，合资、合作开发经营房地产。

**Article 28** The lawfully obtained right to the use of land may, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant laws and administrative regulations, be invested as shares, or used for joint ventures or cooperative development and operation of real estate.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家采取税收等方面的优惠措施鼓励和扶持房地产开发企业开发建设居民住宅。

**Article 29** The State shall adopt preferential measures such as tax incentives to encourage and support real estate development enterprises in developing and constructing residential houses.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　房地产开发企业是以营利为目的，从事房地产开发和经营的企业。设立房地产开发企业，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有自己的名称和组织机构；  
（二）有固定的经营场所；  
（三）有符合国务院规定的注册资本；  
（四）有足够的专业技术人员；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
设立房地产开发企业，应当向工商行政管理部门申请设立登记。工商行政管理部门对符合本法规定条件的，应当予以登记，发给营业执照；对不符合本法规定条件的，不予登记。  
设立有限责任公司、股份有限公司，从事房地产开发经营的，还应当执行公司法的有关规定。  
房地产开发企业在领取营业执照后的一个月内，应当到登记机关所在地的县级以上地方人民政府规定的部门备案。

**Article 30** A real estate development enterprise is an enterprise engaged in real estate development and operation for profit. The establishment of a real estate development enterprise shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having its own name and organizational structure;  
(2) having a fixed place of business;  
(3) having a registered capital conforming to the regulations of the State Council;  
(4) having sufficient professional and technical personnel;  
(5) other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
To establish a real estate development enterprise, an application for registration of establishment shall be submitted to the administrative department for industry and commerce. The administrative department for industry and commerce shall register those that meet the conditions prescribed by this Law and issue a business license; for those that do not meet the conditions, registration shall be denied.  
Where a limited liability company or a company limited by shares is established to engage in real estate development and operation, the relevant provisions of the Company Law shall also be complied with.  
Within one month after obtaining the business license, a real estate development enterprise shall file a record with the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level in the place where the registration authority is located.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　房地产开发企业的注册资本与投资总额的比例应当符合国家有关规定。  
房地产开发企业分期开发房地产的，分期投资额应当与项目规模相适应，并按照土地使用权出让合同的约定，按期投入资金，用于项目建设。

**Article 31** The ratio of registered capital to total investment of a real estate development enterprise shall conform to the relevant regulations of the State.  
Where a real estate development enterprise develops real estate in phases, the phased investment amount shall be commensurate with the project scale, and funds shall be invested on schedule in accordance with the contract for the concession of the right to the use of land for project construction.

### 第四章　房地产交易

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　房地产转让、抵押时，房屋的所有权和该房屋占用范围内的土地使用权同时转让、抵押。

**Article 32** When real estate is transferred or mortgaged, the ownership of the houses and the land-use right within the area occupied by the houses shall be transferred or mortgaged simultaneously.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　基准地价、标定地价和各类房屋的重置价格应当定期确定并公布。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 33** The standard land price, the benchmark land price, and the replacement cost of various types of houses shall be determined and announced regularly. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家实行房地产价格评估制度。  
房地产价格评估，应当遵循公正、公平、公开的原则，按照国家规定的技术标准和评估程序，以基准地价、标定地价和各类房屋的重置价格为基础，参照当地的市场价格进行评估。

**Article 34** The State implements a system of real estate price appraisal.  
Real estate price appraisal shall follow the principles of fairness, impartiality, and openness. Such appraisal shall be based on the standard land price, the benchmark land price, and the replacement cost of various types of houses, with reference to the local market prices, and shall be conducted in accordance with the technical standards and appraisal procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家实行房地产成交价格申报制度。  
房地产权利人转让房地产，应当向县级以上地方人民政府规定的部门如实申报成交价，不得瞒报或者作不实的申报。

**Article 35** The State shall implement a system for declaring the transaction prices of real estate.  
A real estate right holder who transfers real estate shall truthfully declare the transaction price to the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall not conceal or make a false declaration.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　房地产转让、抵押，当事人应当依照本法第五章的规定办理权属登记。

**Article 36** For the transfer or mortgage of real estate, the parties shall undergo the registration of ownership in accordance with the provisions of Chapter V of this Law.

#### 第二节　房地产转让

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　房地产转让，是指房地产权利人通过买卖、赠与或者其他合法方式将其房地产转移给他人的行为。

**Article 37** The transfer of real estate refers to the act by which a real estate right holder transfers his real estate to another person through sale, donation, or other lawful means.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　下列房地产，不得转让：  
（一）以出让方式取得土地使用权的，不符合[本法第三十九条](#art-39)规定的条件的；  
（二）司法机关和行政机关依法裁定、决定查封或者以其他形式限制房地产权利的；  
（三）依法收回土地使用权的；  
（四）共有房地产，未经其他共有人书面同意的；  
（五）权属有争议的；  
（六）未依法登记领取权属证书的；  
（七）法律、行政法规规定禁止转让的其他情形。

**Article 38** The following real estate shall not be transferred:  
(1) Where the land-use right has been acquired by means of concession, the conditions specified in Article 39 of this Law are not met;  
(2) The real estate right is restricted by a ruling or decision of a judicial organ or administrative organ to seal up or in other forms according to law;  
(3) The land-use right has been recovered according to law;  
(4) Co-owned real estate, without the written consent of the other co-owners;  
(5) The ownership is disputed;  
(6) The ownership certificate has not been obtained by registration according to law;  
(7) Other circumstances where transfer is prohibited by laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　以出让方式取得土地使用权的，转让房地产时，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）按照出让合同约定已经支付全部土地使用权出让金，并取得土地使用权证书；  
（二）按照出让合同约定进行投资开发，属于房屋建设工程的，完成开发投资总额的百分之二十五以上，属于成片开发土地的，形成工业用地或者其他建设用地条件。  
转让房地产时房屋已经建成的，还应当持有房屋所有权证书。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/城市房地产开发经营管理条例.md#art-19" title="被《城市房地产开发经营管理条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** When transferring real estate for which the land-use right has been acquired by means of concession, the following conditions shall be met:  
(1) The full payment of the land-use right transfer fee has been made in accordance with the provisions of the concession contract, and the land-use right certificate has been obtained;  
(2) Investment and development have been carried out in accordance with the provisions of the concession contract: for housing construction projects, the investment completed shall account for more than 25 percent of the total investment for development; for land to be developed in tracts, conditions for industrial land or other construction land shall have been formed.  
Where the house has already been completed at the time of transfer of the real estate, the house ownership certificate shall also be held.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　以划拨方式取得土地使用权的，转让房地产时，应当按照国务院规定，报有批准权的人民政府审批。有批准权的人民政府准予转让的，应当由受让方办理土地使用权出让手续，并依照国家有关规定缴纳土地使用权出让金。  
以划拨方式取得土地使用权的，转让房地产报批时，有批准权的人民政府按照国务院规定决定可以不办理土地使用权出让手续的，转让方应当按照国务院规定将转让房地产所获收益中的土地收益上缴国家或者作其他处理。

**Article 40** When transferring real estate for which the land-use right has been acquired by means of allocation, the matter shall be submitted to the people's government with the power of approval for examination and approval in accordance with the regulations of the State Council. Where the people's government with the power of approval approves the transfer, the transferee shall undergo the procedures for the concession of the land-use right and pay the land-use right transfer fee in accordance with relevant State regulations.  
Where, upon submission for approval of the transfer of real estate for which the land-use right has been acquired by means of allocation, the people's government with the power of approval decides, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, that the procedures for the concession of the land-use right may not be undergone, the transferor shall, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, turn over to the State the land proceeds from the proceeds derived from the transfer of the real estate or dispose of them otherwise.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　房地产转让，应当签订书面转让合同，合同中应当载明土地使用权取得的方式。

**Article 41** The transfer of real estate shall be effected by a written transfer contract, which shall state the mode of acquisition of the land-use right.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　房地产转让时，土地使用权出让合同载明的权利、义务随之转移。

**Article 42** When real estate is transferred, the rights and obligations stated in the land-use right concession contract shall be transferred accordingly.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　以出让方式取得土地使用权的，转让房地产后，其土地使用权的使用年限为原土地使用权出让合同约定的使用年限减去原土地使用者已经使用年限后的剩余年限。

**Article 43** Where the land-use right has been acquired by means of concession, after the transfer of real estate, the term of use of the land-use right shall be the remaining term after deducting the years that the original land user has already used from the term of use stipulated in the original land-use right concession contract.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　以出让方式取得土地使用权的，转让房地产后，受让人改变原土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途的，必须取得原出让方和市、县人民政府城市规划行政主管部门的同意，签订土地使用权出让合同变更协议或者重新签订土地使用权出让合同，相应调整土地使用权出让金。

**Article 44** Where the land-use right has been acquired by means of concession, after the transfer of real estate, if the transferee changes the land use stipulated in the original land-use right concession contract, he must obtain the consent of the original grantor and the administrative department of urban planning of the city or county people's government, and sign an agreement to amend the land-use right concession contract or sign a new land-use right concession contract, and correspondingly adjust the land-use right transfer fee.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　商品房预售，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）已交付全部土地使用权出让金，取得土地使用权证书；  
（二）持有建设工程规划许可证；  
（三）按提供预售的商品房计算，投入开发建设的资金达到工程建设总投资的百分之二十五以上，并已经确定施工进度和竣工交付日期；  
（四）向县级以上人民政府房产管理部门办理预售登记，取得商品房预售许可证明。  
商品房预售人应当按照国家有关规定将预售合同报县级以上人民政府房产管理部门和土地管理部门登记备案。  
商品房预售所得款项，必须用于有关的工程建设。

**Article 45** Pre-sale of commercial housing shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The full amount of the land-use right transfer fee has been paid, and the land-use right certificate has been obtained;  
(2) A construction project planning permit is held;  
(3) Based on the commercial housing offered for pre-sale, the funds invested in development and construction account for more than 25 percent of the total investment in the project construction, and the construction progress and the date of completion and delivery have been determined;  
(4) Pre-sale registration has been carried out with the real estate administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level, and a pre-sale permit for commercial housing has been obtained.  
The pre-seller of commercial housing shall, in accordance with relevant State regulations, submit the pre-sale contract to the real estate administrative department and the land administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level for registration and record.  
The proceeds from the pre-sale of commercial housing must be used for the relevant project construction.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　商品房预售的，商品房预购人将购买的未竣工的预售商品房再行转让的问题，由国务院规定。

**Article 46** The issue of a pre-purchaser of commercial housing re-transferring the purchased unfinished pre-sold commercial housing shall be regulated by the State Council.

#### 第三节　房地产抵押

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　房地产抵押，是指抵押人以其合法的房地产以不转移占有的方式向抵押权人提供债务履行担保的行为。债务人不履行债务时，抵押权人有权依法以抵押的房地产拍卖所得的价款优先受偿。

**Article 47** The mortgage of real estate refers to the act by which a mortgagor provides his lawful real estate to the mortgagee as security for the performance of a debt without transferring possession. Where the debtor fails to perform the debt, the mortgagee has the right to receive priority payment from the proceeds from the auction of the mortgaged real estate according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　依法取得的房屋所有权连同该房屋占用范围内的土地使用权，可以设定抵押权。  
以出让方式取得的土地使用权，可以设定抵押权。

**Article 48** The ownership of a house obtained according to law together with the land-use right within the area occupied by the house may be mortgaged.  
A land-use right acquired by means of concession may be mortgaged.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　房地产抵押，应当凭土地使用权证书、房屋所有权证书办理。

**Article 49** The mortgage of real estate shall be handled on the strength of the land-use right certificate and the house ownership certificate.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　房地产抵押，抵押人和抵押权人应当签订书面抵押合同。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 50** For the mortgage of real estate, the mortgagor and the mortgagee shall sign a written mortgage contract.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　设定房地产抵押权的土地使用权是以划拨方式取得的，依法拍卖该房地产后，应当从拍卖所得的价款中缴纳相当于应缴纳的土地使用权出让金的款额后，抵押权人方可优先受偿。

**Article 51** Where the land-use right on which a real estate mortgage is established is acquired by means of allocation, after the real estate is auctioned according to law, the mortgagee may only receive priority payment after the amount equivalent to the land-use right transfer fee payable has been paid from the auction proceeds.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　房地产抵押合同签订后，土地上新增的房屋不属于抵押财产。需要拍卖该抵押的房地产时，可以依法将土地上新增的房屋与抵押财产一同拍卖，但对拍卖新增房屋所得，抵押权人无权优先受偿。

**Article 52** After the conclusion of a real estate mortgage contract, the newly built houses on the land shall not be part of the mortgaged property. Where it is necessary to auction the mortgaged real estate, the newly built houses on the land may be auctioned together with the mortgaged property according to law, but the mortgagee shall have no right to priority payment from the proceeds from the auction of the newly built houses.

#### 第四节　房屋租赁

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　房屋租赁，是指房屋所有权人作为出租人将其房屋出租给承租人使用，由承租人向出租人支付租金的行为。

**Article 53** House lease refers to the act by which the owner of a house, as the lessor, leases his house to a lessee for use, and the lessee pays rent to the lessor.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　房屋租赁，出租人和承租人应当签订书面租赁合同，约定租赁期限、租赁用途、租赁价格、修缮责任等条款，以及双方的其他权利和义务，并向房产管理部门登记备案。

**Article 54** For house lease, the lessor and the lessee shall sign a written lease contract stipulating the lease term, purpose of use, rent, responsibility for repairs, and other rights and obligations of the parties, and shall register and file it with the real estate administrative department.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　住宅用房的租赁，应当执行国家和房屋所在城市人民政府规定的租赁政策。租用房屋从事生产、经营活动的，由租赁双方协商议定租金和其他租赁条款。

**Article 55** The lease of residential housing shall implement the lease policies prescribed by the State and the people's government of the city where the housing is located. Where houses are leased for production or business operations, the rent and other lease terms shall be negotiated and agreed upon by the lessor and lessee.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　以营利为目的，房屋所有权人将以划拨方式取得使用权的国有土地上建成的房屋出租的，应当将租金中所含土地收益上缴国家。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 56** Where a house owner leases, for profit, a house built on State-owned land with the right to use obtained through allocation, the land proceeds contained in the rent shall be turned over to the State. The specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

#### 第五节　中介服务机构

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　房地产中介服务机构包括房地产咨询机构、房地产价格评估机构、房地产经纪机构等。

**Article 57** Real estate intermediary service agencies include real estate consulting agencies, real estate price appraisal agencies, real estate brokerage agencies, etc.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　房地产中介服务机构应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有自己的名称和组织机构；  
（二）有固定的服务场所；  
（三）有必要的财产和经费；  
（四）有足够数量的专业人员；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定的其他条件。  
设立房地产中介服务机构，应当向工商行政管理部门申请设立登记，领取营业执照后，方可开业。

**Article 58** A real estate intermediary service agency shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Have its own name and organizational structure;  
(2) Have a fixed place of service;  
(3) Have the necessary property and funds;  
(4) Have a sufficient number of professional personnel;  
(5) Other conditions prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
The establishment of a real estate intermediary service agency shall apply to the administrative department for industry and commerce for establishment registration, and may commence business only after obtaining a business license.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家实行房地产价格评估人员资格认证制度。

**Article 59** The State implements a qualification certification system for real estate price appraisers.

### 第五章　房地产权属登记管理

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家实行土地使用权和房屋所有权登记发证制度。

**Article 60** The State implements a system of registration and certification for the right to the use of land and the ownership of houses.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　以出让或者划拨方式取得土地使用权，应当向县级以上地方人民政府土地管理部门申请登记，经县级以上地方人民政府土地管理部门核实，由同级人民政府颁发土地使用权证书。  
在依法取得的房地产开发用地上建成房屋的，应当凭土地使用权证书向县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门申请登记，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门核实并颁发房屋所有权证书。  
房地产转让或者变更时，应当向县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门申请房产变更登记，并凭变更后的房屋所有权证书向同级人民政府土地管理部门申请土地使用权变更登记，经同级人民政府土地管理部门核实，由同级人民政府更换或者更改土地使用权证书。  
法律另有规定的，依照有关法律的规定办理。

**Article 61** Where the right to the use of land is obtained by means of concession or allocation, an application for registration shall be submitted to the land administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level; upon verification by the land administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, the people's government at the same level shall issue a certificate of the right to the use of land.  
Where houses are built on the land obtained according to law for real estate development, an application for registration shall be submitted to the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level on the basis of the certificate of the right to the use of land; upon verification by the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, a certificate of ownership of the house shall be issued.  
Where real estate is transferred or changed, an application for registration of the change of the real estate shall be submitted to the real estate administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and on the basis of the changed certificate of ownership of the house, an application for registration of the change of the right to the use of land shall be submitted to the land administrative department of the people's government at the same level; upon verification by the land administrative department of the people's government at the same level, the certificate of the right to the use of land shall be replaced or altered by the people's government at the same level.  
Where laws provide otherwise, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　房地产抵押时，应当向县级以上地方人民政府规定的部门办理抵押登记。  
因处分抵押房地产而取得土地使用权和房屋所有权的，应当依照本章规定办理过户登记。

**Article 62** Where a real estate is mortgaged, an application for mortgage registration shall be submitted to the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level.  
Where the right to the use of land and the ownership of houses are obtained due to the disposal of the mortgaged real estate, the transfer registration shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定，县级以上地方人民政府由一个部门统一负责房产管理和土地管理工作的，可以制作、颁发统一的房地产权证书，依照[本法第六十一条](#art-61)的规定，将房屋的所有权和该房屋占用范围内的土地使用权的确认和变更，分别载入房地产权证书。

**Article 63** Where, as determined by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the local people's government at or above the county level has one department responsible for both the administration of real estate and the administration of land, such department may prepare and issue a unified real estate title certificate, and, in accordance with the provisions of Article 61 of this Law, shall record respectively in the real estate title certificate the confirmation and change of the ownership of the house and the right to the use of the land within the area occupied by such house.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　违反[本法第十一条](#art-11)、第十二条的规定，擅自批准出让或者擅自出让土地使用权用于房地产开发的，由上级机关或者所在单位给予有关责任人员行政处分。

**Article 64** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Articles 11 and 12 of this Law, approves without authorization the concession of land or the concession of the right to the use of land for real estate development, the superior organ or the unit in which the person works shall impose administrative sanctions on the responsible person.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反[本法第三十条](#art-30)的规定，未取得营业执照擅自从事房地产开发业务的，由县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令停止房地产开发业务活动，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 65** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 30 of this Law, engages in real estate development without having obtained a business license, the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the real estate development business activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反[本法第三十九条](#art-39)第一款的规定转让土地使用权的，由县级以上人民政府土地管理部门没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 66** Whoever transfers the land use right in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 39 of this Law shall, by the land administration department of the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the illegal gains and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反[本法第四十条](#art-40)第一款的规定转让房地产的，由县级以上人民政府土地管理部门责令缴纳土地使用权出让金，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 67** Whoever transfers the real estate in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 40 of this Law shall, by the land administration department of the people's government at or above the county level, be ordered to pay the land use right granting fee, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反[本法第四十五条](#art-45)第一款的规定预售商品房的，由县级以上人民政府房产管理部门责令停止预售活动，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 68** Whoever pre-sells commercial housing in violation of the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 45 of this Law shall, by the real estate administration department of the people's government at or above the county level, be ordered to stop the pre-sale activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反[本法第五十八条](#art-58)的规定，未取得营业执照擅自从事房地产中介服务业务的，由县级以上人民政府工商行政管理部门责令停止房地产中介服务业务活动，没收违法所得，可以并处罚款。

**Article 69** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of Article 58 of this Law, engages in real estate intermediary services without obtaining a business license shall, by the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level, be ordered to stop the real estate intermediary service activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also impose a fine.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　没有法律、法规的依据，向房地产开发企业收费的，上级机关应当责令退回所收取的钱款；情节严重的，由上级机关或者所在单位给予直接责任人员行政处分。

**Article 70** Where fees are charged to real estate development enterprises without the basis of laws or regulations, the competent authority at a higher level shall order the return of the money collected; if the circumstances are serious, the competent authority at a higher level or the entity to which the person belongs shall impose administrative sanctions on the person directly responsible.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　房产管理部门、土地管理部门工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。  
房产管理部门、土地管理部门工作人员利用职务上的便利，索取他人财物，或者非法收受他人财物为他人谋取利益，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。

**Article 71** Where any staff member of the real estate administration department or land administration department neglects his duty or abuses his power, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed.  
Where any staff member of the real estate administration department or land administration department takes advantage of his position to demand property from others, or illegally accepts property from others to seek benefits for them, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　在城市规划区外的国有土地范围内取得房地产开发用地的土地使用权，从事房地产开发、交易活动以及实施房地产管理，参照本法执行。

**Article 72** The acquisition of land use right for real estate development on state-owned land outside the urban planning area, the carrying out of real estate development and transaction activities, and the implementation of real estate administration shall be handled by reference to this Law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　本法自1995年1月1日起施行。

**Article 73** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1995.
