# 中华人民共和国海域使用管理法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2001-10-27  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

> （2001年10月27日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　海洋功能区划 第三章　海域使用的申请与审批 第四章　海域使用权 第五章　海域使用金 第六章　监督检查 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强海域使用管理，维护国家海域所有权和海域使用权人的合法权益，促进海域的合理开发和可持续利用，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称海域，是指中华人民共和国内水、领海的水面、水体、海床和底土。 本法所称内水，是指中华人民共和国领海基线向陆地一侧至海岸线的海域。 在中华人民共和国内水、领海持续使用特定海域三个月以上的排他性用海活动，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强海域使用管理，维护国家海域所有权和海域使用权人的合法权益，促进海域的合理开发和可持续利用，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of strengthening the administration of the use of sea areas, safeguarding the ownership of the State over sea areas and the lawful rights and interests of the holders of the right to the use of sea areas, and promoting the rational development and sustainable utilization of sea areas.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称海域，是指中华人民共和国内水、领海的水面、水体、海床和底土。  
本法所称内水，是指中华人民共和国领海基线向陆地一侧至海岸线的海域。  
在中华人民共和国内水、领海持续使用特定海域三个月以上的排他性用海活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** For the purpose of this Law, "sea areas" refer to the water surface, water body, sea-bed and subsoil of the internal waters and the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purpose of this Law, "internal waters" refer to the sea areas on the landward side of the baseline of the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China up to the coastline.  
This Law shall apply to the exclusive use of sea areas, which lasts for more than three months, in the internal waters and the territorial sea of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　海域属于国家所有，国务院代表国家行使海域所有权。任何单位或者个人不得侵占、买卖或者以其他形式非法转让海域。  
单位和个人使用海域，必须依法取得海域使用权。

**Article 3** Sea areas shall be owned by the State. The State Council shall exercise on behalf of the State the ownership of sea areas. No units or individuals may encroach upon, buy, sell or illegally transfer sea areas in any other forms.  
Units and individuals that use sea areas must obtain the right to the use of sea areas according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行海洋功能区划制度。海域使用必须符合海洋功能区划。  
国家严格管理填海、围海等改变海域自然属性的用海活动。

**Article 4** The State shall institute a system of marine functional zoning. The use of sea areas must be in conformity with the marine functional zonation.  
The State shall strictly control such activities as filling and enclosing of sea areas that change the natural attributes of sea areas.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立海域使用管理信息系统，对海域使用状况实施监视、监测。

**Article 5** The State shall establish an information system for the administration of the use of sea areas to monitor and inspect the conditions of the use of sea areas.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家建立海域使用权登记制度，依法登记的海域使用权受法律保护。  
国家建立海域使用统计制度，定期发布海域使用统计资料。

**Article 6** The State shall establish a registration system for the right to the use of sea areas. The right to the use of sea areas registered according to law shall be protected by law.  
The State shall establish a statistical system for the use of sea areas and regularly publish the statistical data concerning the use of sea areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院海洋行政主管部门负责全国海域使用的监督管理。沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋行政主管部门根据授权，负责本行政区毗邻海域使用的监督管理。  
渔业行政主管部门依照《中华人民共和国渔业法》，对海洋渔业实施监督管理。  
海事管理机构依照《中华人民共和国海上交通安全法》，对海上交通安全实施监督管理。

**Article 7** The administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the use of sea areas nationwide. The administrative departments in charge of marine affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level in the coastal areas shall, within the limits of their functions and powers as authorized, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the use of the sea areas adjacent to their own administrative regions.  
The administrative department in charge of fisheries shall, in accordance with the Fisheries Law of the People's Republic of China, exercise supervision and administration over marine fisheries.  
The maritime administration agency shall, in accordance with the Maritime Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, exercise supervision and administration over maritime traffic safety.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　任何单位和个人都有遵守海域使用管理法律、法规的义务，并有权对违反海域使用管理法律、法规的行为提出检举和控告。

**Article 8** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to abide by the laws and regulations governing the administration of the use of sea areas and shall have the right to report and bring charges against any acts that violate such laws and regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在保护和合理利用海域以及进行有关的科学研究等方面成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 9** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in protecting and rationally utilizing sea areas and in conducting scientific research in this regard shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

### 第二章　海洋功能区划

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院海洋行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，编制全国海洋功能区划。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋行政主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门，依据上一级海洋功能区划，编制地方海洋功能区划。

**Article 10** The administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments under the State Council and the people's governments of the coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, formulate the national marine functional zonation.  
The administrative departments in charge of marine affairs under the local people's governments at or above the county level in the coastal areas shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments under the same people's governments and in accordance with the marine functional zonation at the next higher level, formulate the local marine functional zonation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海洋功能区划按照下列原则编制：  
（一）按照海域的区位、自然资源和自然环境等自然属性，科学确定海域功能；  
（二）根据经济和社会发展的需要，统筹安排各有关行业用海；  
（三）保护和改善生态环境，保障海域可持续利用，促进海洋经济的发展；  
（四）保障海上交通安全；  
（五）保障国防安全，保证军事用海需要。

**Article 11** The marine functional zonation shall be formulated in light of the following principles:  
(1) scientifically determining the functions of sea areas in the light of the location, natural resources, natural environment and other natural attributes of the sea areas;  
(2) making overall arrangements for the use of sea areas by all the trades and industries concerned in accordance with the needs of economic and social development;  
(3) protecting and improving the ecological environment, ensuring the sustainable utilization of sea areas and promoting the development of marine economy;  
(4) ensuring the safety of maritime traffic; and  
(5) ensuring the security of national defense and meeting the needs of sea areas for military use.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海洋功能区划实行分级审批。  
全国海洋功能区划，报国务院批准。  
沿海省、自治区、直辖市海洋功能区划，经该省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审核同意后，报国务院批准。  
沿海市、县海洋功能区划，经该市、县人民政府审核同意后，报所在的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，报国务院海洋行政主管部门备案。

**Article 12** The marine functional zonation shall be subject to examination and approval at different levels.  
The national marine functional zonation shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The marine functional zonation of a coastal province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, after being examined and approved by the people's government of the said province, autonomous region or municipality, be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The marine functional zonation of a coastal city or county shall, after being examined and approved by the people's government of the said city or county, be submitted to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality to which it belongs for approval and be reported to the administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the State Council for the record.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　海洋功能区划的修改，由原编制机关会同同级有关部门提出修改方案，报原批准机关批准；未经批准，不得改变海洋功能区划确定的海域功能。  
经国务院批准，因公共利益、国防安全或者进行大型能源、交通等基础设施建设，需要改变海洋功能区划的，根据国务院的批准文件修改海洋功能区划。

**Article 13** Any modification to the marine functional zonation shall be subject to a modification plan put forward by the department that originally formulated the zonation in conjunction with the relevant departments at the same level and submitted to the original approving authority for approval; without approval, no changes shall be made to the functions of the sea areas as defined in the marine functional zonation.  
Where the marine functional zonation needs to be modified due to public interests, national defense security or construction of large energy, transport and other infrastructure, with the approval of the State Council, such modification shall be made in accordance with the approval documents of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　海洋功能区划经批准后，应当向社会公布；但是，涉及国家秘密的部分除外。

**Article 14** The marine functional zonation shall, after being approved, be made known to the public, except for the parts that involve State secrets.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　养殖、盐业、交通、旅游等行业规划涉及海域使用的，应当符合海洋功能区划。  
沿海土地利用总体规划、城市规划、港口规划涉及海域使用的，应当与海洋功能区划衔接。

**Article 15** The plans for such trades as aquaculture, salt industry, transport and tourism that involve the use of sea areas shall be in conformity with the marine functional zonation.  
The overall plans for land use, urban plans and port plans in the coastal areas that involve the use of sea areas shall be dovetailed with the marine functional zonation.

### 第三章　海域使用的申请与审批

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　单位和个人可以向县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门申请使用海域。  
申请使用海域的，申请人应当提交下列书面材料：  
（一）海域使用申请书；  
（二）海域使用论证材料；  
（三）相关的资信证明材料；  
（四）法律、法规规定的其他书面材料。

**Article 16** Any unit or individual may apply to the administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the people's government at or above the county level for using a sea area.  
An applicant applying for using a sea area shall submit the following written materials:  
(1) an application for the use of a sea area;  
(2) materials demonstrating the necessity of such use;  
(3) relevant materials testifying the applicant's financial standing; and  
(4) other written materials as provided for by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门依据海洋功能区划，对海域使用申请进行审核，并依照本法和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，报有批准权的人民政府批准。  
海洋行政主管部门审核海域使用申请，应当征求同级有关部门的意见。

**Article 17** The administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the people's government at or above the county level shall, on the basis of the marine functional zonation, examine the application for the use of a sea area and, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, submit it to the people's government that has the power of approval for approval.  
When examining an application for the use of a sea area, the administrative department in charge of marine affairs shall solicit the opinions of the relevant departments at the same level.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　下列项目用海，应当报国务院审批：  
（一）填海五十公顷以上的项目用海；  
（二）围海一百公顷以上的项目用海；  
（三）不改变海域自然属性的用海七百公顷以上的项目用海；  
（四）国家重大建设项目用海；  
（五）国务院规定的其他项目用海。  
前款规定以外的项目用海的审批权限，由国务院授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府规定。

**Article 18** The use of sea areas for the following items shall be submitted to the State Council for examination and approval:  
(1) reclamation of 50 hectares or more of sea areas;  
(2) enclosure of 100 hectares or more of sea areas;  
(3) use of 700 hectares or more of sea areas without changing the natural attributes of the sea areas;  
(4) use of sea areas for major national construction projects; and  
(5) use of sea areas for other items as prescribed by the State Council.  
The power of examination and approval for the use of sea areas for items other than those provided for in the preceding paragraph shall be defined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government as authorized by the State Council.

### 第四章　海域使用权

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海域使用申请经依法批准后，国务院批准用海的，由国务院海洋行政主管部门登记造册，向海域使用申请人颁发海域使用权证书；地方人民政府批准用海的，由地方人民政府登记造册，向海域使用申请人颁发海域使用权证书。海域使用申请人自领取海域使用权证书之日起，取得海域使用权。

**Article 19** After an application for the use of a sea area is approved according to law, where the use is approved by the State Council, the administrative department in charge of marine affairs under the State Council shall make registration and issue a certificate for the right to the use of the sea area to the applicant; where the use is approved by a local people's government, the local people's government shall make registration and issue a certificate for the right to the use of the sea area to the applicant. The applicant shall acquire the right to the use of the sea area as of the date when it receives the certificate.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　海域使用权除依照[本法第十九条](#art-19)规定的方式取得外，也可以通过招标或者拍卖的方式取得。招标或者拍卖方案由海洋行政主管部门制订，报有审批权的人民政府批准后组织实施。海洋行政主管部门制订招标或者拍卖方案，应当征求同级有关部门的意见。  
招标或者拍卖工作完成后，依法向中标人或者买受人颁发海域使用权证书。中标人或者买受人自领取海域使用权证书之日起，取得海域使用权。

**Article 20** The right to the use of a sea area may be acquired through bid invitation or auction in addition to the method provided for in Article 19 of this Law. The plan for bid invitation or auction shall be formulated by the administrative department in charge of marine affairs and, after being approved by the people's government that has the power of approval, be organized for implementation. The administrative department in charge of marine affairs shall, when formulating the plan for bid invitation or auction, solicit the opinions of the relevant departments at the same level.  
After the completion of the bid invitation or auction, the certificate for the right to the use of the sea area shall be issued according to law to the bid winner or the buyer. The bid winner or the buyer shall acquire the right to the use of the sea area as of the date when it receives the certificate.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　颁发海域使用权证书，应当向社会公告。  
颁发海域使用权证书，除依法收取海域使用金外，不得收取其他费用。  
海域使用权证书的发放和管理办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 21** The issuance of sea area use certificates shall be publicly announced.  
For the issuance of sea area use certificates, no fees other than the sea area use fees collected according to law shall be charged.  
The measures for the issuance and management of sea area use certificates shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本法施行前，已经由农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会经营、管理的养殖用海，符合海洋功能区划的，经当地县级人民政府核准，可以将海域使用权确定给该农村集体经济组织或者村民委员会，由本集体经济组织的成员承包，用于养殖生产。

**Article 22** For aquaculture sea areas that have been operated and managed by rural collective economic organizations or villagers' committees prior to the coming into force of this Law, if they conform to the marine functional zoning, with the approval of the local people's government at the county level, the sea area use right may be determined to such rural collective economic organization or villagers' committee, and shall be contracted by members of this collective economic organization for aquaculture production.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　海域使用权人依法使用海域并获得收益的权利受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。  
海域使用权人有依法保护和合理使用海域的义务；海域使用权人对不妨害其依法使用海域的非排他性用海活动，不得阻挠。

**Article 23** The right of a sea area use right holder to use the sea area according to law and obtain proceeds therefrom shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon it.  
A sea area use right holder has the obligation to protect and rationally use the sea area according to law; a sea area use right holder shall not obstruct any non-exclusive sea area use activities that do not harm its lawful use of the sea area.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海域使用权人在使用海域期间，未经依法批准，不得从事海洋基础测绘。  
海域使用权人发现所使用海域的自然资源和自然条件发生重大变化时，应当及时报告海洋行政主管部门。

**Article 24** During the period of using the sea area, a sea area use right holder shall not engage in marine basic surveying and mapping without approval according to law.  
Where a sea area use right holder discovers significant changes in the natural resources and natural conditions of the sea area being used, it shall promptly report to the marine administrative department.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　海域使用权最高期限，按照下列用途确定：  
（一）养殖用海十五年；  
（二）拆船用海二十年；  
（三）旅游、娱乐用海二十五年；  
（四）盐业、矿业用海三十年；  
（五）公益事业用海四十年；  
（六）港口、修造船厂等建设工程用海五十年。

**Article 25** The maximum term of sea area use rights shall be determined according to the following purposes:  
(1) fifteen years for aquaculture sea areas;  
(2) twenty years for shipbreaking sea areas;  
(3) twenty-five years for tourism and recreation sea areas;  
(4) thirty years for salt industry and mining sea areas;  
(5) forty years for public welfare undertakings sea areas;  
(6) fifty years for construction projects such as ports and ship repair yards.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　海域使用权期限届满，海域使用权人需要继续使用海域的，应当至迟于期限届满前二个月向原批准用海的人民政府申请续期。除根据公共利益或者国家安全需要收回海域使用权的外，原批准用海的人民政府应当批准续期。准予续期的，海域使用权人应当依法缴纳续期的海域使用金。

**Article 26** Where the term of a sea area use right expires and the sea area use right holder needs to continue using the sea area, it shall apply to the original people's government that approved the sea area use for renewal no later than two months before the expiration of the term. Unless the sea area use right is required to be reclaimed in the public interest or for national security, the original people's government that approved the sea area use shall approve the renewal. Where renewal is approved, the sea area use right holder shall pay the sea area use fee for the renewed period according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　因企业合并、分立或者与他人合资、合作经营，变更海域使用权人的，需经原批准用海的人民政府批准。  
海域使用权可以依法转让。海域使用权转让的具体办法，由国务院规定。  
海域使用权可以依法继承。

**Article 27** Where a sea area use right holder is changed due to merger, division, or joint venture or cooperative operation with others, the change shall be subject to the approval of the original people's government that approved the sea area use.  
Sea area use rights may be transferred according to law. The specific measures for the transfer of sea area use rights shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Sea area use rights may be succeeded according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　海域使用权人不得擅自改变经批准的海域用途；确需改变的，应当在符合海洋功能区划的前提下，报原批准用海的人民政府批准。

**Article 28** A sea area use right holder shall not change the approved sea area use without authorization; if a change is indeed necessary, it shall, on the premise of conforming to the marine functional zoning, report to the original people's government that approved the sea area use for approval.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　海域使用权期满，未申请续期或者申请续期未获批准的，海域使用权终止。  
海域使用权终止后，原海域使用权人应当拆除可能造成海洋环境污染或者影响其他用海项目的用海设施和构筑物。

**Article 29** Upon the expiration of the term of a sea area use right, if no renewal is applied for or the renewal application is not approved, the sea area use right shall terminate.  
After the termination of the sea area use right, the original sea area use right holder shall dismantle any sea area use facilities and structures that may cause marine environmental pollution or affect other sea area use projects.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　因公共利益或者国家安全的需要，原批准用海的人民政府可以依法收回海域使用权。  
依照前款规定在海域使用权期满前提前收回海域使用权的，对海域使用权人应当给予相应的补偿。

**Article 30** For the needs of public interest or national security, the original people's government that approved the sea area use may reclaim the sea area use right according to law.  
Where the sea area use right is reclaimed before the expiration of its term in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the sea area use right holder shall be given corresponding compensation.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　因海域使用权发生争议，当事人协商解决不成的，由县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门调解；当事人也可以直接向人民法院提起诉讼。  
在海域使用权争议解决前，任何一方不得改变海域使用现状。

**Article 31** Where a dispute over a sea area use right arises and the parties cannot resolve it through consultation, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall conduct mediation; the parties may also directly initiate a prosecution with the People's Court.  
Before the dispute over the sea area use right is resolved, neither party shall change the status quo of the sea area use.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　填海项目竣工后形成的土地，属于国家所有。  
海域使用权人应当自填海项目竣工之日起三个月内，凭海域使用权证书，向县级以上人民政府土地行政主管部门提出土地登记申请，由县级以上人民政府登记造册，换发国有土地使用权证书，确认土地使用权。

**Article 32** Land formed after the completion of a sea reclamation project shall belong to the State.  
The sea area use right holder shall, within three months from the date of completion of the sea reclamation project, submit an application for land registration to the land administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level by presenting the sea area use right certificate, and the people's government at or above the county level shall register it in the register, issue a new State-owned land use right certificate, and confirm the land use right.

### 第五章　海域使用金

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家实行海域有偿使用制度。  
单位和个人使用海域，应当按照国务院的规定缴纳海域使用金。海域使用金应当按照国务院的规定上缴财政。  
对渔民使用海域从事养殖活动收取海域使用金的具体实施步骤和办法，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 33** The State shall implement a system of paid use of sea areas.  
Units and individuals using sea areas shall pay sea area use fees in accordance with the provisions of the State Council. The sea area use fees shall be turned over to the state treasury in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
The specific implementation steps and measures for collecting sea area use fees from fishermen using sea areas for aquaculture activities shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　根据不同的用海性质或者情形，海域使用金可以按照规定一次缴纳或者按年度逐年缴纳。

**Article 34** Depending on different natures or circumstances of sea area use, the sea area use fee may be paid in a lump sum or on an annual basis according to the provisions.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　下列用海，免缴海域使用金：  
（一）军事用海；  
（二）公务船舶专用码头用海；  
（三）非经营性的航道、锚地等交通基础设施用海；  
（四）教学、科研、防灾减灾、海难搜救打捞等非经营性公益事业用海。

**Article 35** The following sea area uses shall be exempted from payment of sea area use fees:  
(1) military sea area use;  
(2) sea area use for special-purpose docks of government vessels;  
(3) sea area use for non-operational transportation infrastructure such as waterways and anchorage grounds;  
(4) sea area use for non-operational public welfare undertakings such as education, scientific research, disaster prevention and mitigation, and maritime search, rescue and salvage.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　下列用海，按照国务院财政部门和国务院海洋行政主管部门的规定，经有批准权的人民政府财政部门和海洋行政主管部门审查批准，可以减缴或者免缴海域使用金：  
（一）公用设施用海；  
（二）国家重大建设项目用海；  
（三）养殖用海。

**Article 36** For the following sea area uses, subject to examination and approval by the financial department and the marine administrative department of the people's government with approval authority, in accordance with the provisions of the financial department of the State Council and the marine administrative department of the State Council, the sea area use fee may be reduced or exempted:  
(1) sea area use for public facilities;  
(2) sea area use for major national construction projects;  
(3) sea area use for aquaculture.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门应当加强对海域使用的监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府财政部门应当加强对海域使用金缴纳情况的监督检查。

**Article 37** The marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of sea area use.  
The financial department of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and inspection of the payment of sea area use fees.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　海洋行政主管部门应当加强队伍建设，提高海域使用管理监督检查人员的政治、业务素质。海域使用管理监督检查人员必须秉公执法，忠于职守，清正廉洁，文明服务，并依法接受监督。  
海洋行政主管部门及其工作人员不得参与和从事与海域使用有关的生产经营活动。

**Article 38** The marine administrative department shall strengthen team building and improve the political and professional qualities of the supervision and inspection personnel for sea area use management. The supervision and inspection personnel for sea area use management must enforce the law impartially, be loyal to their duties, be honest and upright, provide civilized services, and accept supervision according to law.  
The marine administrative department and its staff shall not participate in or engage in any production or business activities related to sea area use.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位或者个人提供海域使用的有关文件和资料；  
（二）要求被检查单位或者个人就海域使用的有关问题作出说明；  
（三）进入被检查单位或者个人占用的海域现场进行勘查；  
（四）责令当事人停止正在进行的违法行为。

**Article 39** When performing the duties of supervision and inspection, the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) to require the unit or individual under inspection to provide relevant documents and materials concerning the sea area use;  
(2) to require the unit or individual under inspection to make explanations on relevant issues concerning the sea area use;  
(3) to enter the sea area site occupied by the unit or individual under inspection for survey;  
(4) to order the party to cease the ongoing illegal act.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　海域使用管理监督检查人员履行监督检查职责时，应当出示有效执法证件。  
有关单位和个人对海洋行政主管部门的监督检查应当予以配合，不得拒绝、妨碍监督检查人员依法执行公务。

**Article 40** When performing the duties of supervision and inspection, the supervision and inspection personnel for sea area use management shall present valid law enforcement credentials.  
Relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted by the marine administrative department, and shall not refuse or obstruct the supervision and inspection personnel from performing their official duties according to law.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　依照法律规定行使海洋监督管理权的有关部门在海上执法时应当密切配合，互相支持，共同维护国家海域所有权和海域使用权人的合法权益。

**Article 41** The relevant departments exercising the power of marine supervision and administration according to law shall closely cooperate with and support each other in law enforcement at sea, and jointly safeguard the ownership of the State's sea areas and the lawful rights and interests of holders of the right to the use of sea areas.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　未经批准或者骗取批准，非法占用海域的，责令退还非法占用的海域，恢复海域原状，没收违法所得，并处非法占用海域期间内该海域面积应缴纳的海域使用金五倍以上十五倍以下的罚款；对未经批准或者骗取批准，进行围海、填海活动的，并处非法占用海域期间内该海域面积应缴纳的海域使用金十倍以上二十倍以下的罚款。

**Article 42** Where anyone, without approval or by obtaining approval through fraud, illegally occupies a sea area, the sea area illegally occupied shall be ordered to be returned and restored to its original state, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than five times but not more than fifteen times the amount of the sea area use fee payable for the area of the sea area during the period of illegal occupation shall be imposed; where anyone, without approval or by obtaining approval through fraud, carries out activities of enclosing or reclaiming the sea area, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the amount of the sea area use fee payable for the area of the sea area during the period of illegal occupation shall be additionally imposed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　无权批准使用海域的单位非法批准使用海域的，超越批准权限非法批准使用海域的，或者不按海洋功能区划批准使用海域的，批准文件无效，收回非法使用的海域；对非法批准使用海域的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 43** Where a unit without the authority to approve the use of sea areas illegally approves the use of sea areas, or exceeds its approval authority in illegally approving the use of sea areas, or approves the use of sea areas not in accordance with the marine functional zoning, the approval document shall be null and void, and the illegally used sea area shall be recovered; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible for the illegal approval of the use of sea areas shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)规定，阻挠、妨害海域使用权人依法使用海域的，海域使用权人可以请求海洋行政主管部门排除妨害，也可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼；造成损失的，可以依法请求损害赔偿。

**Article 44** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 23 of this Law, obstructs or hinders the holder of the right to the use of a sea area from using the sea area according to law, the holder of the right to the use of the sea area may request the marine administrative department to remove the obstruction, or may institute a prosecution in the People's Court according to law; if losses are caused, the holder may claim damages according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反[本法第二十六条](#art-26)规定，海域使用权期满，未办理有关手续仍继续使用海域的，责令限期办理，可以并处一万元以下的罚款；拒不办理的，以非法占用海域论处。

**Article 45** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 26 of this Law, after the expiration of the right to the use of a sea area, continues to use the sea area without going through the relevant formalities, the party shall be ordered to go through the formalities within a time limit, and may also be fined not more than 10,000 yuan; if the party refuses to do so, the matter shall be dealt with as illegal occupation of the sea area.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)规定，擅自改变海域用途的，责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处非法改变海域用途的期间内该海域面积应缴纳的海域使用金五倍以上十五倍以下的罚款；对拒不改正的，由颁发海域使用权证书的人民政府注销海域使用权证书，收回海域使用权。

**Article 46** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of Article 28 of this Law, alters the purpose of use of a sea area without authorization, the party shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than five times but not more than fifteen times the amount of the sea area use fee payable for the area of the sea area during the period of illegally altering the purpose of use shall be imposed; if the party refuses to make corrections, the people's government that issued the certificate of the right to the use of the sea area shall cancel the certificate and withdraw the right to the use of the sea area.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定，海域使用权终止，原海域使用权人不按规定拆除用海设施和构筑物的，责令限期拆除；逾期拒不拆除的，处五万元以下的罚款，并由县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门委托有关单位代为拆除，所需费用由原海域使用权人承担。

**Article 47** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 29 of this Law, after the termination of the right to the use of a sea area, fails to dismantle the sea-use facilities and structures as required, the party shall be ordered to dismantle them within a time limit; if the party refuses to dismantle them after the expiration of the time limit, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall entrust a relevant unit to dismantle them on its behalf, and the expenses thus incurred shall be borne by the original holder of the right to the use of the sea area.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本法规定，按年度逐年缴纳海域使用金的海域使用权人不按期缴纳海域使用金的，限期缴纳；在限期内仍拒不缴纳的，由颁发海域使用权证书的人民政府注销海域使用权证书，收回海域使用权。

**Article 48** Where a holder of the right to the use of a sea area who pays the sea area use fee on an annual basis fails to pay the fee on time in violation of the provisions of this Law, the holder shall be ordered to pay within a time limit; if the holder still refuses to pay within the time limit, the people's government that issued the certificate of the right to the use of the sea area shall cancel the certificate and withdraw the right to the use of the sea area.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本法规定，拒不接受海洋行政主管部门监督检查、不如实反映情况或者不提供有关资料的，责令限期改正，给予警告，可以并处二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 49** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, refuses to accept the supervision and inspection of the marine administrative department, fails to truthfully report the situation, or fails to provide relevant materials, the party shall be ordered to make corrections within a time limit, given a warning, and may also be fined not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法规定的行政处罚，由县级以上人民政府海洋行政主管部门依据职权决定。但是，本法已对处罚机关作出规定的除外。

**Article 50** The administrative penalties provided for in this Law shall be decided by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with its functions and powers, except where this Law has otherwise provided for the punishing authority.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国务院海洋行政主管部门和县级以上地方人民政府违反本法规定颁发海域使用权证书，或者颁发海域使用权证书后不进行监督管理，或者发现违法行为不予查处的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分；徇私舞弊、滥用职权或者玩忽职守构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 51** Where the marine administrative department of the State Council or a local people's government at or above the county level, in violation of the provisions of this Law, issues a certificate of the right to the use of a sea area, or fails to carry out supervision and administration after issuing such a certificate, or fails to investigate and deal with the illegal acts discovered, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law; if the circumstances constitute a crime due to engaging in malpractices for personal gain, abusing power, or neglecting duties, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在中华人民共和国内水、领海使用特定海域不足三个月，可能对国防安全、海上交通安全和其他用海活动造成重大影响的排他性用海活动，参照本法有关规定办理临时海域使用证。

**Article 52** For an exclusive sea-use activity that uses a specific sea area in the inland waters or territorial sea of the People's Republic of China for less than three months and may have a significant impact on national defense security, maritime traffic safety, and other sea-use activities, a temporary sea area use permit shall be handled with reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　军事用海的管理办法，由国务院、中央军事委员会依据本法制定。

**Article 53** The measures for the administration of military use of sea areas shall be formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　本法自2002年1月1日起施行。

**Article 54** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
