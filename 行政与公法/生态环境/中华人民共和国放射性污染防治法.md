# 中华人民共和国放射性污染防治法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2003-06-28  
**生效日期**：2003-10-01

> （2003年6月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　放射性污染防治的监督管理 第三章　核设施的放射性污染防治 第四章　核技术利用的放射性污染防治 第五章　铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用的放射性污染防治 第六章　放射性废物管理 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了防治放射性污染，保护环境，保障人体健康，促进核能、核技术的开发与和平利用，制定本法。 第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域在核设施选址、建造、运行、退役和核技术、铀（钍）矿、伴生放射性矿开发利用过程中发生的放射性污染的防治活动。 第三条　国家对放射性污染的防治，实行预防为主、防治结合、严格管理、安全第一的方针。 第四条　国家鼓励、支持放射性污染防治的科学研究和技术开发利用，推广先进的放射性污染防治技术。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治放射性污染，保护环境，保障人体健康，促进核能、核技术的开发与和平利用，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of preventing and controlling radioactive pollution, protecting the environment, safeguarding human health, and promoting the development and peaceful use of nuclear energy and nuclear technology.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于中华人民共和国领域和管辖的其他海域在核设施选址、建造、运行、退役和核技术、铀（钍）矿、伴生放射性矿开发利用过程中发生的放射性污染的防治活动。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the prevention and control of radioactive pollution arising from the siting, construction, operation and decommissioning of nuclear installations, and from the development and utilization of nuclear technology, uranium (thorium) mines, and associated radioactive mines within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对放射性污染的防治，实行预防为主、防治结合、严格管理、安全第一的方针。

**Article 3** The State shall, in the prevention and control of radioactive pollution, implement the principles of putting prevention first, combining prevention with control, exercising strict management, and prioritizing safety.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家鼓励、支持放射性污染防治的科学研究和技术开发利用，推广先进的放射性污染防治技术。  
国家支持开展放射性污染防治的国际交流与合作。

**Article 4** The State shall encourage and support scientific research and the development and utilization of technologies for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution, and shall promote advanced technologies for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution.  
The State shall support international exchanges and cooperation in the prevention and control of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将放射性污染防治工作纳入环境保护规划。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织开展有针对性的放射性污染防治宣传教育，使公众了解放射性污染防治的有关情况和科学知识。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work of preventing and controlling radioactive pollution into their environmental protection plans.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out targeted publicity and education on the prevention and control of radioactive pollution, so that the public may understand the relevant circumstances and scientific knowledge concerning the prevention and control of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何单位和个人有权对造成放射性污染的行为提出检举和控告。

**Article 6** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report or file charges against any act that causes radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在放射性污染防治工作中作出显著成绩的单位和个人，由县级以上人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 7** Entities and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in the work of preventing and controlling radioactive pollution shall be rewarded by the people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门对全国放射性污染防治工作依法实施统一监督管理。  
国务院卫生行政部门和其他有关部门依据国务院规定的职责，对有关的放射性污染防治工作依法实施监督管理。

**Article 8** The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall, according to law, exercise unified supervision and administration of the nationwide work of preventing and controlling radioactive pollution.  
The competent administrative department of health under the State Council and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with the duties prescribed by the State Council, exercise supervision and administration of the relevant work of preventing and controlling radioactive pollution according to law.

### 第二章　放射性污染防治的监督管理

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家放射性污染防治标准由国务院环境保护行政主管部门根据环境安全要求、国家经济技术条件制定。国家放射性污染防治标准由国务院环境保护行政主管部门和国务院标准化行政主管部门联合发布。

**Article 9** The national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution shall be formulated by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council on the basis of environmental safety requirements and the national economic and technological conditions. Such standards shall be jointly issued by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent administrative department of standardization under the State Council.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家建立放射性污染监测制度。国务院环境保护行政主管部门会同国务院其他有关部门组织环境监测网络，对放射性污染实施监测管理。

**Article 10** The State shall establish a monitoring system for radioactive pollution. The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall, together with other relevant departments under the State Council, organize an environmental monitoring network to exercise monitoring and administration of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院环境保护行政主管部门和国务院其他有关部门，按照职责分工，各负其责，互通信息，密切配合，对核设施、铀（钍）矿开发利用中的放射性污染防治进行监督检查。  
县级以上地方人民政府环境保护行政主管部门和同级其他有关部门，按照职责分工，各负其责，互通信息，密切配合，对本行政区域内核技术利用、伴生放射性矿开发利用中的放射性污染防治进行监督检查。  
监督检查人员进行现场检查时，应当出示证件。被检查的单位必须如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。监督检查人员应当为被检查单位保守技术秘密和业务秘密。对涉及国家秘密的单位和部位进行检查时，应当遵守国家有关保守国家秘密的规定，依法办理有关审批手续。

**Article 11** The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and other relevant departments under the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of their duties, assume their respective responsibilities, exchange information, and closely cooperate with each other to exercise supervision and inspection over the prevention and control of radioactive pollution in the development and utilization of nuclear installations and uranium (thorium) mines.  
The competent administrative departments of environmental protection of the local people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments at the same level shall, in accordance with the division of their duties, assume their respective responsibilities, exchange information, and closely cooperate with each other to exercise supervision and inspection over the prevention and control of radioactive pollution in the utilization of nuclear technology and the development and utilization of associated radioactive mines within their respective administrative regions.  
When conducting on-site inspections, the inspectors shall present their credentials. The entity subject to inspection must truthfully provide information and necessary materials. The inspectors shall keep confidential the technical secrets and business secrets of the entity subject to inspection. When inspecting an entity or part involving State secrets, they shall comply with the relevant State provisions on the protection of State secrets and go through the relevant approval procedures according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位、铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用单位，负责本单位放射性污染的防治，接受环境保护行政主管部门和其他有关部门的监督管理，并依法对其造成的放射性污染承担责任。

**Article 12** Operators of nuclear installations, entities utilizing nuclear technology, and entities developing and utilizing uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines shall be responsible for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution within their own units, accept the supervision and administration of the competent administrative department of environmental protection and other relevant departments, and shall bear liability according to law for the radioactive pollution they cause.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位、铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用单位，必须采取安全与防护措施，预防发生可能导致放射性污染的各类事故，避免放射性污染危害。  
核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位、铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用单位，应当对其工作人员进行放射性安全教育、培训，采取有效的防护安全措施。

**Article 13** Operators of nuclear installations, entities utilizing nuclear technology, and entities developing and utilizing uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines must adopt safety and protection measures to prevent the occurrence of various incidents that may lead to radioactive pollution, and to avoid harm from radioactive pollution.  
Operators of nuclear installations, entities utilizing nuclear technology, and entities developing and utilizing uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines shall provide their staff with education and training on radiation safety, and shall adopt effective protective safety measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家对从事放射性污染防治的专业人员实行资格管理制度；对从事放射性污染监测工作的机构实行资质管理制度。

**Article 14** The State shall implement a qualification management system for professionals engaged in the prevention and control of radioactive pollution; and shall implement a qualification management system for institutions engaged in the monitoring of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　运输放射性物质和含放射源的射线装置，应当采取有效措施，防止放射性污染。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 15** Effective measures shall be taken to prevent radioactive pollution during the transport of radioactive substances and radiation devices containing radiation sources. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　放射性物质和射线装置应当设置明显的放射性标识和中文警示说明。生产、销售、使用、贮存、处置放射性物质和射线装置的场所，以及运输放射性物质和含放射源的射线装置的工具，应当设置明显的放射性标志。

**Article 16** Radioactive substances and radiation devices shall be provided with conspicuous radioactive signs and warning instructions in Chinese. Places where radioactive substances and radiation devices are produced, sold, used, stored or disposed of, and means of transport for radioactive substances and radiation devices containing radiation sources, shall be provided with conspicuous radioactive symbols.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　含有放射性物质的产品，应当符合国家放射性污染防治标准；不符合国家放射性污染防治标准的，不得出厂和销售。  
使用伴生放射性矿渣和含有天然放射性物质的石材做建筑和装修材料，应当符合国家建筑材料放射性核素控制标准。

**Article 17** Products containing radioactive substances shall conform to the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution; those not conforming to such standards shall not be released from factories or sold.  
The use of associated radioactive mine slag and stone containing natural radioactive substances as building and decoration materials shall conform to the national standards for the control of radionuclides in building materials.

### 第三章　核设施的放射性污染防治

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　核设施选址，应当进行科学论证，并按照国家有关规定办理审批手续。在办理核设施选址审批手续前，应当编制环境影响报告书，报国务院环境保护行政主管部门审查批准；未经批准，有关部门不得办理核设施选址批准文件。

**Article 18** The siting of nuclear installations shall be subject to scientific demonstration, and the approval formalities shall be gone through in accordance with the relevant State provisions. Before going through the approval formalities for the siting of a nuclear installation, an environmental impact report shall be prepared and submitted to the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council for examination and approval; without approval, the relevant department shall not issue the document for approval of the siting of the nuclear installation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　核设施营运单位在进行核设施建造、装料、运行、退役等活动前，必须按照国务院有关核设施安全监督管理的规定，申请领取核设施建造、运行许可证和办理装料、退役等审批手续。  
核设施营运单位领取有关许可证或者批准文件后，方可进行相应的建造、装料、运行、退役等活动。

**Article 19** Before carrying out activities such as construction, fuel loading, operation, or decommissioning of a nuclear installation, the operator of the nuclear installation must, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the safety supervision and administration of nuclear installations, apply for and obtain the construction and operation permits for the nuclear installation and go through the approval formalities for fuel loading and decommissioning.  
Only after the operator of the nuclear installation obtains the relevant permit or document of approval may it carry out the corresponding activities of construction, fuel loading, operation, or decommissioning.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　核设施营运单位应当在申请领取核设施建造、运行许可证和办理退役审批手续前编制环境影响报告书，报国务院环境保护行政主管部门审查批准；未经批准，有关部门不得颁发许可证和办理批准文件。

**Article 20** The operator of a nuclear installation shall prepare an environmental impact report before applying for the construction and operation permits for the nuclear installation and going through the approval formalities for decommissioning, and submit it to the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council for examination and approval; without approval, the relevant department shall not issue the permits or process the documents of approval.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　与核设施相配套的放射性污染防治设施，应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。  
放射性污染防治设施应当与主体工程同时验收；验收合格的，主体工程方可投入生产或者使用。

**Article 21** The facilities for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution that are ancillary to a nuclear installation shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.  
The facilities for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution shall be accepted for inspection simultaneously with the main project; only after they pass the acceptance inspection may the main project be put into production or use.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　进口核设施，应当符合国家放射性污染防治标准；没有相应的国家放射性污染防治标准的，采用国务院环境保护行政主管部门指定的国外有关标准。

**Article 22** Imported nuclear installations shall conform to the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution; where there are no corresponding national standards, the relevant foreign standards designated by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall be adopted.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　核动力厂等重要核设施外围地区应当划定规划限制区。规划限制区的划定和管理办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 23** Planned restricted zones shall be delimited in the peripheral areas of important nuclear installations such as nuclear power plants. The measures for the delimitation and administration of planned restricted zones shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　核设施营运单位应当对核设施周围环境中所含的放射性核素的种类、浓度以及核设施流出物中的放射性核素总量实施监测，并定期向国务院环境保护行政主管部门和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门报告监测结果。  
国务院环境保护行政主管部门负责对核动力厂等重要核设施实施监督性监测，并根据需要对其他核设施的流出物实施监测。监督性监测系统的建设、运行和维护费用由财政预算安排。

**Article 24** The operator of a nuclear installation shall monitor the types and concentrations of radionuclides contained in the environment surrounding the nuclear installation and the total amount of radionuclides in the effluents of the nuclear installation, and shall periodically report the monitoring results to the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where it is located.  
The competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall be responsible for carrying out supervisory monitoring of important nuclear installations such as nuclear power plants, and, as needed, monitor the effluents of other nuclear installations. The expenses for the construction, operation, and maintenance of the supervisory monitoring system shall be arranged from the financial budget.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　核设施营运单位应当建立健全安全保卫制度，加强安全保卫工作，并接受公安部门的监督指导。  
核设施营运单位应当按照核设施的规模和性质制定核事故场内应急计划，做好应急准备。  
出现核事故应急状态时，核设施营运单位必须立即采取有效的应急措施控制事故，并向核设施主管部门和环境保护行政主管部门、卫生行政部门、公安部门以及其他有关部门报告。

**Article 25** The operator of a nuclear installation shall establish and improve a security system, strengthen security work, and accept the supervision and guidance of the public security department.  
The operator of a nuclear installation shall, according to the scale and nature of the nuclear installation, formulate an on-site emergency plan for nuclear accidents and make emergency preparations.  
When a nuclear accident emergency state occurs, the operator of the nuclear installation must immediately take effective emergency measures to control the accident, and report to the competent department of nuclear installations, the competent administrative department of environmental protection, the administrative department of health, the public security department, and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立健全核事故应急制度。  
核设施主管部门、环境保护行政主管部门、卫生行政部门、公安部门以及其他有关部门，在本级人民政府的组织领导下，按照各自的职责依法做好核事故应急工作。  
中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队按照国务院、中央军事委员会的有关规定在核事故应急中实施有效的支援。

**Article 26** The State shall establish and improve a nuclear accident emergency system.  
The competent department of nuclear installations, the competent administrative department of environmental protection, the administrative department of health, the public security department, and other relevant departments shall, under the organization and leadership of the people's governments at the corresponding level, perform their respective duties according to law in carrying out nuclear accident emergency work well.  
The Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council and the Central Military Commission, provide effective support in nuclear accident emergencies.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　核设施营运单位应当制定核设施退役计划。  
核设施的退役费用和放射性废物处置费用应当预提，列入投资概算或者生产成本。核设施的退役费用和放射性废物处置费用的提取和管理办法，由国务院财政部门、价格主管部门会同国务院环境保护行政主管部门、核设施主管部门规定。

**Article 27** The operator of a nuclear installation shall formulate a decommissioning plan for the nuclear installation.  
The costs of decommissioning the nuclear installation and the costs of disposing of radioactive waste shall be set aside in advance and included in the investment estimate or production costs. The measures for the setting aside and administration of the costs of decommissioning nuclear installations and the costs of disposing of radioactive waste shall be prescribed by the competent financial department and the competent price department under the State Council, together with the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent department of nuclear installations.

### 第四章　核技术利用的放射性污染防治

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当按照国务院有关放射性同位素与射线装置放射防护的规定申请领取许可证，办理登记手续。  
转让、进口放射性同位素和射线装置的单位以及装备有放射性同位素的仪表的单位，应当按照国务院有关放射性同位素与射线装置放射防护的规定办理有关手续。

**Article 28** Entities that produce, sell, or use radioactive isotopes and radiation devices shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the radiological protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation devices, apply for and obtain a permit and go through registration formalities.  
Entities that transfer or import radioactive isotopes and radiation devices, and entities that have instruments equipped with radioactive isotopes, shall go through the relevant formalities in accordance with the provisions of the State Council on the radiological protection of radioactive isotopes and radiation devices.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生产、销售、使用放射性同位素和加速器、中子发生器以及含放射源的射线装置的单位，应当在申请领取许可证前编制环境影响评价文件，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门审查批准；未经批准，有关部门不得颁发许可证。  
国家建立放射性同位素备案制度。具体办法由国务院规定。

**Article 29** Entities that produce, sell, or use radioactive isotopes, accelerators, neutron generators, and radiation devices containing radiation sources shall, before applying for a permit, prepare an environmental impact assessment document and submit it to the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for examination and approval; without approval, the relevant department shall not issue a permit.  
The State shall establish a filing system for radioactive isotopes. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　新建、改建、扩建放射工作场所的放射防护设施，应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。  
放射防护设施应当与主体工程同时验收；验收合格的，主体工程方可投入生产或者使用。

**Article 30** The radiation protection facilities for new, expanded, or renovated radiation workplaces shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.  
The radiation protection facilities shall be accepted for inspection simultaneously with the main project; only after they pass the acceptance inspection may the main project be put into production or use.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　放射性同位素应当单独存放，不得与易燃、易爆、腐蚀性物品等一起存放，其贮存场所应当采取有效的防火、防盗、防射线泄漏的安全防护措施，并指定专人负责保管。贮存、领取、使用、归还放射性同位素时，应当进行登记、检查，做到账物相符。

**Article 31** Radioactive isotopes shall be stored separately and shall not be stored together with flammable, explosive, corrosive, or other similar articles. In their storage places, effective safety protection measures against fire, theft, and radiation leakage shall be taken, and special personnel shall be designated to be responsible for their custody. When storing, collecting, using, or returning radioactive isotopes, registration and inspection shall be carried out so that the accounts and the materials are consistent.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　生产、使用放射性同位素和射线装置的单位，应当按照国务院环境保护行政主管部门的规定对其产生的放射性废物进行收集、包装、贮存。  
生产放射源的单位，应当按照国务院环境保护行政主管部门的规定回收和利用废旧放射源；使用放射源的单位，应当按照国务院环境保护行政主管部门的规定将废旧放射源交回生产放射源的单位或者送交专门从事放射性固体废物贮存、处置的单位。

**Article 32** Entities that produce or use radioactive isotopes and radiation devices shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council, collect, package, and store the radioactive waste they generate.  
Entities that produce radiation sources shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council, recover and utilize spent radiation sources; entities that use radiation sources shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council, return spent radiation sources to the entities that produced them, or deliver them to entities specializing in the storage and disposal of solid radioactive waste.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　生产、销售、使用、贮存放射源的单位，应当建立健全安全保卫制度，指定专人负责，落实安全责任制，制定必要的事故应急措施。发生放射源丢失、被盗和放射性污染事故时，有关单位和个人必须立即采取应急措施，并向公安部门、卫生行政部门和环境保护行政主管部门报告。  
公安部门、卫生行政部门和环境保护行政主管部门接到放射源丢失、被盗和放射性污染事故报告后，应当报告本级人民政府，并按照各自的职责立即组织采取有效措施，防止放射性污染蔓延，减少事故损失。当地人民政府应当及时将有关情况告知公众，并做好事故的调查、处理工作。

**Article 33** Entities that produce, sell, use, or store radiation sources shall establish and improve a security system, designate special personnel to be responsible, implement the safety responsibility system, and formulate necessary accident emergency measures. When incidents of loss, theft of radiation sources, or radioactive pollution incidents occur, the relevant entities and individuals must immediately take emergency measures and report to the public security department, the administrative department of health, and the competent administrative department of environmental protection.  
After receiving reports of loss, theft of radiation sources, or radioactive pollution incidents, the public security department, the administrative department of health, and the competent administrative department of environmental protection shall report to the people's government at the corresponding level, and immediately organize the adoption of effective measures in accordance with their respective duties to prevent the spread of radioactive pollution and reduce accident losses. The local people's government shall promptly inform the public of the relevant situation, and shall properly investigate and handle the accident.

### 第五章　铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用的放射性污染防治

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　开发利用或者关闭铀（钍）矿的单位，应当在申请领取采矿许可证或者办理退役审批手续前编制环境影响报告书，报国务院环境保护行政主管部门审查批准。  
开发利用伴生放射性矿的单位，应当在申请领取采矿许可证前编制环境影响报告书，报省级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门审查批准。

**Article 34** Entities that develop, utilize, or close uranium (thorium) mines shall, before applying for a mining permit or going through decommissioning approval formalities, prepare an environmental impact report and submit it to the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council for examination and approval.  
Entities that develop and utilize associated radioactive mines shall, before applying for a mining permit, prepare an environmental impact report and submit it to the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the provincial level for examination and approval.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　与铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用建设项目相配套的放射性污染防治设施，应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投入使用。  
放射性污染防治设施应当与主体工程同时验收；验收合格的，主体工程方可投入生产或者使用。

**Article 35** The facilities for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution that are ancillary to construction projects for the development and utilization of uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines shall be designed, constructed, and put into use simultaneously with the main project.  
The facilities for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution shall be accepted for inspection simultaneously with the main project; only after they pass the acceptance inspection may the main project be put into production or use.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　铀（钍）矿开发利用单位应当对铀（钍）矿的流出物和周围的环境实施监测，并定期向国务院环境保护行政主管部门和所在地省、自治区、直辖市人民政府环境保护行政主管部门报告监测结果。

**Article 36** Entities that develop and utilize uranium (thorium) mines shall monitor the effluents of the uranium (thorium) mines and the surrounding environment, and shall periodically report the monitoring results to the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where they are located.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用过程中产生的尾矿，应当建造尾矿库进行贮存、处置；建造的尾矿库应当符合放射性污染防治的要求。

**Article 37** Tailings generated in the process of developing and utilizing uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines shall be stored and disposed of in tailings ponds constructed for that purpose; the tailings ponds constructed shall meet the requirements for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　铀（钍）矿开发利用单位应当制定铀（钍）矿退役计划。铀矿退役费用由国家财政预算安排。

**Article 38** Entities that develop and utilize uranium (thorium) mines shall formulate decommissioning plans for the uranium (thorium) mines. The costs of decommissioning uranium mines shall be arranged from the State financial budget.

### 第六章　放射性废物管理

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　核设施营运单位、核技术利用单位、铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿开发利用单位，应当合理选择和利用原材料，采用先进的生产工艺和设备，尽量减少放射性废物的产生量。

**Article 39** Operators of nuclear installations, entities utilizing nuclear technology, and entities developing and utilizing uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines shall rationally select and utilize raw materials, adopt advanced production processes and equipment, and minimize the generation of radioactive waste.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　向环境排放放射性废气、废液，必须符合国家放射性污染防治标准。

**Article 40** Discharge of radioactive waste gas and waste liquid into the environment must conform to the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　产生放射性废气、废液的单位向环境排放符合国家放射性污染防治标准的放射性废气、废液，应当向审批环境影响评价文件的环境保护行政主管部门申请放射性核素排放量，并定期报告排放计量结果。

**Article 41** Entities that generate radioactive waste gas and waste liquid and discharge radioactive waste gas and waste liquid that conform to the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution into the environment shall apply to the competent administrative department of environmental protection that examined and approved the environmental impact assessment document for the amount of radionuclide discharge, and shall periodically report the discharge measurement results.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　产生放射性废液的单位，必须按照国家放射性污染防治标准的要求，对不得向环境排放的放射性废液进行处理或者贮存。  
产生放射性废液的单位，向环境排放符合国家放射性污染防治标准的放射性废液，必须采用符合国务院环境保护行政主管部门规定的排放方式。  
禁止利用渗井、渗坑、天然裂隙、溶洞或者国家禁止的其他方式排放放射性废液。

**Article 42** Entities that generate radioactive waste liquid must, in accordance with the requirements of the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution, treat or store radioactive waste liquid that shall not be discharged into the environment.  
Entities that generate radioactive waste liquid and discharge radioactive waste liquid that conforms to the national standards for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution into the environment must adopt discharge methods that conform to the provisions of the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council.  
It is prohibited to discharge radioactive waste liquid by means of seepage wells, seepage pits, natural fissures, karst caves, or other means prohibited by the State.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　低、中水平放射性固体废物在符合国家规定的区域实行近地表处置。  
高水平放射性固体废物实行集中的深地质处置。  
α放射性固体废物依照前款规定处置。  
禁止在内河水域和海洋上处置放射性固体废物。

**Article 43** Low and intermediate level solid radioactive waste shall be disposed of in near-surface disposal facilities in areas conforming to the State regulations.  
High level solid radioactive waste shall be disposed of in concentrated deep geological disposal facilities.  
α solid radioactive waste shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
It is prohibited to dispose of solid radioactive waste in inland waters or in the sea.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院核设施主管部门会同国务院环境保护行政主管部门根据地质条件和放射性固体废物处置的需要，在环境影响评价的基础上编制放射性固体废物处置场所选址规划，报国务院批准后实施。  
有关地方人民政府应当根据放射性固体废物处置场所选址规划，提供放射性固体废物处置场所的建设用地，并采取有效措施支持放射性固体废物的处置。

**Article 44** The competent department of nuclear installations under the State Council shall, together with the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council, prepare a siting plan for solid radioactive waste disposal facilities based on geological conditions and the needs for solid radioactive waste disposal, and on the basis of environmental impact assessment, which shall be implemented after being approved by the State Council.  
The relevant local people's governments shall, in accordance with the siting plan for solid radioactive waste disposal facilities, provide the construction land for such facilities, and take effective measures to support the disposal of solid radioactive waste.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　产生放射性固体废物的单位，应当按照国务院环境保护行政主管部门的规定，对其产生的放射性固体废物进行处理后，送交放射性固体废物处置单位处置，并承担处置费用。  
放射性固体废物处置费用收取和使用管理办法，由国务院财政部门、价格主管部门会同国务院环境保护行政主管部门规定。

**Article 45** Entities that generate solid radioactive waste shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council, treat the solid radioactive waste they generate and then deliver it to solid radioactive waste disposal entities for disposal, and shall bear the disposal costs.  
The measures for the collection and use administration of solid radioactive waste disposal costs shall be prescribed by the competent financial department and the competent price department under the State Council, together with the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　设立专门从事放射性固体废物贮存、处置的单位，必须经国务院环境保护行政主管部门审查批准，取得许可证。具体办法由国务院规定。  
禁止未经许可或者不按照许可的有关规定从事贮存和处置放射性固体废物的活动。  
禁止将放射性固体废物提供或者委托给无许可证的单位贮存和处置。

**Article 46** Entities established to specialize in the storage and disposal of solid radioactive waste must be examined and approved by the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council and obtain a permit. Specific measures shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
It is prohibited to engage in the storage and disposal of solid radioactive waste without a permit or not in accordance with the relevant provisions of the permit.  
It is prohibited to provide or entrust solid radioactive waste for storage and disposal to an entity without a permit.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　禁止将放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品输入中华人民共和国境内或者经中华人民共和国境内转移。

**Article 47** It is prohibited to import radioactive waste and articles contaminated with radioactive substances into the territory of the People's Republic of China, or to transfer them through the territory of the People's Republic of China.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　放射性污染防治监督管理人员违反法律规定，利用职务上的便利收受他人财物、谋取其他利益，或者玩忽职守，有下列行为之一的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）对不符合法定条件的单位颁发许可证和办理批准文件的；  
（二）不依法履行监督管理职责的；  
（三）发现违法行为不予查处的。

**Article 48** Where any supervisor or administrator of the prevention and control of radioactive pollution violates the provisions of law, takes advantage of his position to accept property from others or seek other interests, or neglects his duties, and commits any of the following acts, he shall be given an administrative sanction according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing permits or handling documents of approval to an entity that does not meet the statutory requirements;  
(2) failing to perform the duties of supervision and administration according to law;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职权责令限期改正，可以处二万元以下罚款：  
（一）不按照规定报告有关环境监测结果的；  
（二）拒绝环境保护行政主管部门和其他有关部门进行现场检查，或者被检查时不如实反映情况和提供必要资料的。

**Article 49** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level or another relevant department shall, within the scope of its authority, order it to make corrections within a specified period, and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to report the relevant environmental monitoring results as required;  
(2) refusing to accept on-site inspection by the competent administrative department of environmental protection or other relevant departments, or failing to truthfully reflect the situation and provide necessary materials when being inspected.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，未编制环境影响评价文件，或者环境影响评价文件未经环境保护行政主管部门批准，擅自进行建造、运行、生产和使用等活动的，由审批环境影响评价文件的环境保护行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期补办手续或者恢复原状，并处一万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 50** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to prepare an environmental impact assessment document, or carries out activities such as construction, operation, production, or use without the environmental impact assessment document having been approved by the competent administrative department of environmental protection, the competent administrative department of environmental protection that examined and approved the environmental impact assessment document shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and require it to go through the formalities retroactively or restore the original state within a specified period, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本法规定，未建造放射性污染防治设施、放射防护设施，或者防治防护设施未经验收合格，主体工程即投入生产或者使用的，由审批环境影响评价文件的环境保护行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，并处五万元以上二十万元以下罚款。

**Article 51** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, fails to construct facilities for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution or radiation protection facilities, or puts the main project into production or use before such facilities have passed acceptance inspection, the competent administrative department of environmental protection that examined and approved the environmental impact assessment document shall order the cessation of the illegal act and make corrections within a specified period, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反本法规定，未经许可或者批准，核设施营运单位擅自进行核设施的建造、装料、运行、退役等活动的，由国务院环境保护行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，并处二十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 52** Where an operator of a nuclear installation, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in activities such as construction, fuel loading, operation, or decommissioning of a nuclear installation without a permit or approval, the competent administrative department of environmental protection under the State Council shall order the cessation of the illegal act and make corrections within a specified period, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反本法规定，生产、销售、使用、转让、进口、贮存放射性同位素和射线装置以及装备有放射性同位素的仪表的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职权责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业或者吊销许可证；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, produces, sells, uses, transfers, imports, or stores radioactive isotopes, radiation devices, or instruments equipped with radioactive isotopes, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level or another relevant department shall, within the scope of its authority, order the cessation of the illegal act and make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business operations, or its permit shall be revoked; if there are any illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，处以罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未建造尾矿库或者不按照放射性污染防治的要求建造尾矿库，贮存、处置铀（钍）矿和伴生放射性矿的尾矿的；  
（二）向环境排放不得排放的放射性废气、废液的；  
（三）不按照规定的方式排放放射性废液，利用渗井、渗坑、天然裂隙、溶洞或者国家禁止的其他方式排放放射性废液的；  
（四）不按照规定处理或者贮存不得向环境排放的放射性废液的；  
（五）将放射性固体废物提供或者委托给无许可证的单位贮存和处置的。  
有前款第（一）项、第（二）项、第（三）项、第（五）项行为之一的，处十万元以上二十万元以下罚款；有前款第（四）项行为的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 54** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, make corrections within a specified period, and impose a fine; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to construct a tailings pond, or constructing a tailings pond that does not meet the requirements for the prevention and control of radioactive pollution, for storing and disposing of tailings from uranium (thorium) mines and associated radioactive mines;  
(2) discharging into the environment radioactive waste gas or waste liquid that is prohibited from being discharged;  
(3) discharging radioactive waste liquid not in the prescribed manner, or discharging it through seepage wells, seepage pits, natural fissures, karst caves, or other means prohibited by the State;  
(4) failing to treat or store radioactive waste liquid that shall not be discharged into the environment as required;  
(5) providing or entrusting solid radioactive waste for storage and disposal to an entity without a permit.  
Where any of the acts specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), or (5) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; where the act specified in subparagraph (4) of the preceding paragraph is committed, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门或者其他有关部门依据职权责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令停产停业，并处二万元以上十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）不按照规定设置放射性标识、标志、中文警示说明的；  
（二）不按照规定建立健全安全保卫制度和制定事故应急计划或者应急措施的；  
（三）不按照规定报告放射源丢失、被盗情况或者放射性污染事故的。

**Article 55** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the county level or another relevant department shall, within the scope of its authority, order it to make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, it shall be ordered to suspend production or business operations, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) failing to set up radioactive signs, symbols, or warning instructions in Chinese as required;  
(2) failing to establish and improve a security system and formulate accident emergency plans or measures as required;  
(3) failing to report the loss or theft of radiation sources or radioactive pollution incidents as required.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　产生放射性固体废物的单位，不按照[本法第四十五条](#art-45)的规定对其产生的放射性固体废物进行处置的，由审批该单位立项环境影响评价文件的环境保护行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正；逾期不改正的，指定有处置能力的单位代为处置，所需费用由产生放射性固体废物的单位承担，可以并处二十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Where an entity that generates solid radioactive waste fails to dispose of the solid radioactive waste it generates in accordance with the provisions of Article 45 of this Law, the competent administrative department of environmental protection that examined and approved the environmental impact assessment document for the project initiation of that entity shall order the cessation of the illegal act and make corrections within a specified period; if it fails to make corrections within the specified period, the department shall designate an entity with disposal capacity to dispose of the waste on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the entity that generated the solid radioactive waste, and a fine of not more than 200,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由省级以上人民政府环境保护行政主管部门责令停产停业或者吊销许可证；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足十万元的，并处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）未经许可，擅自从事贮存和处置放射性固体废物活动的；  
（二）不按照许可的有关规定从事贮存和处置放射性固体废物活动的。

**Article 57** Where any entity or individual, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, the competent administrative department of environmental protection of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall order the suspension of production or business operations or revoke the permit; if there are any illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains shall be concurrently imposed; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) engaging in the storage and disposal of solid radioactive waste without a permit;  
(2) engaging in the storage and disposal of solid radioactive waste not in accordance with the relevant provisions of the permit.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　向中华人民共和国境内输入放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品，或者经中华人民共和国境内转移放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品的，由海关责令退运该放射性废物和被放射性污染的物品，并处五十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Where any entity or individual imports radioactive waste and articles contaminated with radioactive substances into the territory of the People's Republic of China, or transfers them through the territory of the People's Republic of China, the Customs shall order the return of such radioactive waste and articles, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　因放射性污染造成他人损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。

**Article 59** Where damage is caused to another person by radioactive pollution, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　军用设施、装备的放射性污染防治，由国务院和军队的有关主管部门依照本法规定的原则和国务院、中央军事委员会规定的职责实施监督管理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/生态环境/放射性同位素与射线装置安全和防护条例.md#art-65" title="被《放射性同位素与射线装置安全和防护条例》第65条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/生态环境/放射性废物安全管理条例.md#art-44" title="被《放射性废物安全管理条例》第44条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/交通运输/放射性物品运输安全管理条例.md#art-67" title="被《放射性物品运输安全管理条例》第67条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 60** The prevention and control of radioactive pollution of military installations and equipment shall be subject to supervision and administration by the relevant competent departments of the State Council and the military in accordance with the principles stipulated in this Law and the duties prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　劳动者在职业活动中接触放射性物质造成的职业病的防治，依照《中华人民共和国职业病防治法》的规定执行。

**Article 61** The prevention and control of occupational diseases caused by exposure to radioactive substances in the course of occupational activities of workers shall be governed by the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）放射性污染，是指由于人类活动造成物料、人体、场所、环境介质表面或者内部出现超过国家标准的放射性物质或者射线。  
（二）核设施，是指核动力厂（核电厂、核热电厂、核供汽供热厂等）和其他反应堆（研究堆、实验堆、临界装置等）；核燃料生产、加工、贮存和后处理设施；放射性废物的处理和处置设施等。  
（三）核技术利用，是指密封放射源、非密封放射源和射线装置在医疗、工业、农业、地质调查、科学研究和教学等领域中的使用。  
（四）放射性同位素，是指某种发生放射性衰变的元素中具有相同原子序数但质量不同的核素。  
（五）放射源，是指除研究堆和动力堆核燃料循环范畴的材料以外，永久密封在容器中或者有严密包层并呈固态的放射性材料。  
（六）射线装置，是指X线机、加速器、中子发生器以及含放射源的装置。  
（七）伴生放射性矿，是指含有较高水平天然放射性核素浓度的非铀矿（如稀土矿和磷酸盐矿等）。  
（八）放射性废物，是指含有放射性核素或者被放射性核素污染，其浓度或者比活度大于国家确定的清洁解控水平，预期不再使用的废弃物。

**Article 62** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms mean:  
(1) "Radioactive pollution" means the presence of radioactive substances or radiation in or on the surface of materials, the human body, premises, or environmental media exceeding the national standards as a result of human activities.  
(2) "Nuclear installations" means nuclear power plants (nuclear power plants, nuclear heating plants, nuclear steam and heat supply plants, etc.) and other reactors (research reactors, experimental reactors, critical assemblies, etc.); facilities for the production, processing, storage and reprocessing of nuclear fuel; facilities for the treatment and disposal of radioactive waste, etc.  
(3) "Utilization of nuclear technology" means the use of sealed radiation sources, unsealed radiation sources, and radiation devices in the fields of medicine, industry, agriculture, geological survey, scientific research, and teaching, etc.  
(4) "Radioactive isotopes" means nuclides of an element undergoing radioactive decay that have the same atomic number but different masses.  
(5) "Radiation sources" means radioactive materials that are permanently sealed in a container or have a tight cladding and are in a solid state, except materials within the nuclear fuel cycle of research reactors and power reactors.  
(6) "Radiation devices" means X-ray machines, accelerators, neutron generators, and devices containing radiation sources.  
(7) "Associated radioactive mines" means non-uranium mines (such as rare earth mines and phosphate mines, etc.) containing relatively high concentrations of natural radionuclides.  
(8) "Radioactive waste" means waste that contains radionuclides or is contaminated by radionuclides, with a concentration or specific activity greater than the clearance level determined by the State, and that is not expected to be used further.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2003年10月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2003.
