### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护、建设和合理利用草原，改善生态环境，维护生物多样性，发展现代畜牧业，促进经济和社会的可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of protecting, constructing and rationally utilizing grasslands, improving the ecological environment, maintaining biodiversity, developing modern animal husbandry, and promoting the sustainable development of the economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内从事草原规划、保护、建设、利用和管理活动，适用本法。  
本法所称草原，是指天然草原和人工草地。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as grassland planning, protection, construction, utilization and management within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, "grasslands" means natural grasslands and artificial grasslands.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对草原实行科学规划、全面保护、重点建设、合理利用的方针，促进草原的可持续利用和生态、经济、社会的协调发展。

**Article 3** The State shall adopt the policy of scientific planning, comprehensive protection, prioritized construction and rational utilization of grasslands, so as to promote the sustainable utilization of grasslands and the coordinated development of ecology, economy and society.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　各级人民政府应当加强对草原保护、建设和利用的管理，将草原的保护、建设和利用纳入国民经济和社会发展计划。  
各级人民政府应当加强保护、建设和合理利用草原的宣传教育。

**Article 4** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the administration of grassland protection, construction and utilization, and incorporate grassland protection, construction and utilization into the plans for national economic and social development.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen publicity and education on the protection, construction and rational utilization of grasslands.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　任何单位和个人都有遵守草原法律法规、保护草原的义务，同时享有对违反草原法律法规、破坏草原的行为进行监督、检举和控告的权利。

**Article 5** Any unit or individual shall have the obligation to abide by the laws and regulations on grasslands and to protect grasslands, and shall at the same time have the right to supervise, report and accuse any act that violates the laws and regulations on grasslands or damages grasslands.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励与支持开展草原保护、建设、利用和监测方面的科学研究，推广先进技术和先进成果，培养科学技术人才。

**Article 6** The State shall encourage and support scientific research on grassland protection, construction, utilization and monitoring, promote advanced technologies and achievements, and train scientific and technological personnel.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对在草原管理、保护、建设、合理利用和科学研究等工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，给予奖励。

**Article 7** The State shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in grassland management, protection, construction, rational utilization and scientific research.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院草原行政主管部门主管全国草原监督管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门主管本行政区域内草原监督管理工作。  
乡（镇）人民政府应当加强对本行政区域内草原保护、建设和利用情况的监督检查，根据需要可以设专职或者兼职人员负责具体监督检查工作。

**Article 8** The grassland administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of grasslands nationwide.  
The grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of grasslands within their respective administrative regions.  
The people's government of a township (town) shall strengthen the supervision and inspection of grassland protection, construction and utilization within its administrative region, and may, where necessary, assign full-time or part-time personnel to be responsible for specific supervision and inspection work.

### 第二章　草原权属

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　草原属于国家所有，由法律规定属于集体所有的除外。国家所有的草原，由国务院代表国家行使所有权。  
任何单位或者个人不得侵占、买卖或者以其他形式非法转让草原。

**Article 9** Grasslands shall be owned by the State, except those which are owned by collectives as prescribed by law. Grasslands owned by the State shall have their ownership exercised by the State Council on behalf of the State.  
No unit or individual may encroach upon, buy, sell or illegally transfer grasslands in any other form.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家所有的草原，可以依法确定给全民所有制单位、集体经济组织等使用。  
使用草原的单位，应当履行保护、建设和合理利用草原的义务。

**Article 10** Grasslands owned by the State may be allocated according to law to units under ownership by the whole people, collective economic organizations, etc. for use.  
Units that use grasslands shall fulfill the obligations of protecting, constructing and rationally utilizing the grasslands.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　依法确定给全民所有制单位、集体经济组织等使用的国家所有的草原，由县级以上人民政府登记，核发使用权证，确认草原使用权。  
未确定使用权的国家所有的草原，由县级以上人民政府登记造册，并负责保护管理。  
集体所有的草原，由县级人民政府登记，核发所有权证，确认草原所有权。  
依法改变草原权属的，应当办理草原权属变更登记手续。

**Article 11** For grasslands owned by the State that are allocated according to law to units under ownership by the whole people, collective economic organizations, etc. for use, the people's governments at or above the county level shall register, issue certificates of use rights, and confirm the rights to use the grasslands.  
For grasslands owned by the State for which no use rights have been determined, the people's governments at or above the county level shall register them in a register and be responsible for their protection and management.  
For grasslands owned by collectives, the people's governments at the county level shall register, issue certificates of ownership, and confirm the ownership of the grasslands.  
Where the ownership or use rights of grasslands are changed according to law, the procedures for alteration registration of grassland rights shall be completed.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　依法登记的草原所有权和使用权受法律保护，任何单位或者个人不得侵犯。

**Article 12** The ownership and use rights of grasslands registered according to law shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　集体所有的草原或者依法确定给集体经济组织使用的国家所有的草原，可以由本集体经济组织内的家庭或者联户承包经营。  
在草原承包经营期内，不得对承包经营者使用的草原进行调整；个别确需适当调整的，必须经本集体经济组织成员的村（牧）民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村（牧）民代表的同意，并报乡（镇）人民政府和县级人民政府草原行政主管部门批准。  
集体所有的草原或者依法确定给集体经济组织使用的国家所有的草原由本集体经济组织以外的单位或者个人承包经营的，必须经本集体经济组织成员的村（牧）民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村（牧）民代表的同意，并报乡（镇）人民政府批准。

**Article 13** Grasslands owned by collectives or grasslands owned by the State that are allocated according to law to collective economic organizations for use may be contracted for operation by households or joint households within such collective economic organizations.  
During the term of grassland contractual operation, the grasslands used by the contract operator shall not be readjusted; where individual readjustment is truly necessary, it must be approved by more than two-thirds of the members of the village (herdsmen) meeting of the said collective economic organization or by more than two-thirds of the village (herdsmen) representatives, and shall be reported to the people's government of the township (town) and the grassland administrative department of the people's government at the county level for approval.  
Where grasslands owned by collectives or grasslands owned by the State that are allocated according to law to collective economic organizations for use are contracted to units or individuals outside the said collective economic organization, it must be approved by more than two-thirds of the members of the village (herdsmen) meeting of the said collective economic organization or by more than two-thirds of the village (herdsmen) representatives, and shall be reported to the people's government of the township (town) for approval.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　承包经营草原，发包方和承包方应当签订书面合同。草原承包合同的内容应当包括双方的权利和义务、承包草原四至界限、面积和等级、承包期和起止日期、承包草原用途和违约责任等。承包期届满，原承包经营者在同等条件下享有优先承包权。  
承包经营草原的单位和个人，应当履行保护、建设和按照承包合同约定的用途合理利用草原的义务。

**Article 14** For contractual operation of grasslands, the party offering the contract and the contractor shall conclude a written contract. The contents of a grassland contract shall include the rights and obligations of both parties, the boundaries, area and grade of the contracted grassland, the contract term and its start and end dates, the purpose of use of the contracted grassland, and liability for breach of contract, among others. Upon expiry of the contract term, the original contract operator shall have the preemptive right to contract under the same conditions.  
Units and individuals that operate grasslands under contract shall fulfill the obligations of protecting, constructing and rationally utilizing the grasslands in accordance with the purposes agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　草原承包经营权受法律保护，可以按照自愿、有偿的原则依法转让。  
草原承包经营权转让的受让方必须具有从事畜牧业生产的能力，并应当履行保护、建设和按照承包合同约定的用途合理利用草原的义务。  
草原承包经营权转让应当经发包方同意。承包方与受让方在转让合同中约定的转让期限，不得超过原承包合同剩余的期限。

**Article 15** The right to contractual operation of grasslands shall be protected by law and may be transferred according to law on the principles of voluntariness and compensation.  
The transferee of the transfer of the right to contractual operation of grasslands must have the capability to engage in animal husbandry production, and shall fulfill the obligations of protecting, constructing and rationally utilizing the grasslands in accordance with the purposes agreed in the contract.  
The transfer of the right to contractual operation of grasslands shall be subject to the consent of the party offering the contract. The transfer term agreed in the transfer contract between the contractor and the transferee shall not exceed the remaining term of the original contract.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　草原所有权、使用权的争议，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由有关人民政府处理。  
单位之间的争议，由县级以上人民政府处理；个人之间、个人与单位之间的争议，由乡（镇）人民政府或者县级以上人民政府处理。  
当事人对有关人民政府的处理决定不服的，可以依法向人民法院起诉。  
在草原权属争议解决前，任何一方不得改变草原利用现状，不得破坏草原和草原上的设施。

**Article 16** Disputes over the ownership or use rights of grasslands shall be resolved by the parties through consultation; where consultation fails, the relevant people's government shall handle the matter.  
Disputes between units shall be handled by the people's government at or above the county level; disputes between individuals or between an individual and a unit shall be handled by the people's government of a township (town) or the people's government at or above the county level.  
If a party is not satisfied with the decision made by the relevant people's government, it may initiate a prosecution in the People's Court according to law.  
Before a dispute over grassland rights is resolved, neither party may change the current status of grassland utilization or damage the grasslands or the facilities on the grasslands.

### 第三章　规划

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家对草原保护、建设、利用实行统一规划制度。国务院草原行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门编制全国草原保护、建设、利用规划，报国务院批准后实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门会同同级有关部门依据上一级草原保护、建设、利用规划编制本行政区域的草原保护、建设、利用规划，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
经批准的草原保护、建设、利用规划确需调整或者修改时，须经原批准机关批准。

**Article 17** The State shall practice a unified planning system for grassland protection, construction and utilization. The grassland administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a national plan for grassland protection, construction and utilization, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the State Council.  
The grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, formulate plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization for their respective administrative regions on the basis of the plan of the next higher level for grassland protection, construction and utilization, which shall be implemented after being submitted to and approved by the people's governments at the same level.  
Where an approved plan for grassland protection, construction and utilization truly needs to be adjusted or modified, it must be approved by the original approving authority.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　编制草原保护、建设、利用规划，应当依据国民经济和社会发展规划并遵循下列原则：  
（一）改善生态环境，维护生物多样性，促进草原的可持续利用；  
（二）以现有草原为基础，因地制宜，统筹规划，分类指导；  
（三）保护为主、加强建设、分批改良、合理利用；  
（四）生态效益、经济效益、社会效益相结合。

**Article 18** The formulation of plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization shall be based on the plan for national economic and social development and shall follow the following principles:  
(1) improving the ecological environment, maintaining biodiversity, and promoting the sustainable utilization of grasslands;  
(2) taking existing grasslands as the basis, adapting measures to local conditions, making overall plans, and providing guidance by category;  
(3) giving priority to protection, strengthening construction, improving in batches, and utilizing rationally; and  
(4) combining ecological, economic and social benefits.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　草原保护、建设、利用规划应当包括：草原保护、建设、利用的目标和措施，草原功能分区和各项建设的总体部署，各项专业规划等。

**Article 19** Plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization shall include: the objectives and measures for grassland protection, construction and utilization; the functional zoning of grasslands and the overall deployment of various constructions; and various specialized plans.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　草原保护、建设、利用规划应当与土地利用总体规划相衔接，与环境保护规划、水土保持规划、防沙治沙规划、水资源规划、林业长远规划、城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划以及其他有关规划相协调。

**Article 20** Plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization shall be linked with the overall plan for land utilization, and shall be coordinated with the plan for environmental protection, the plan for water and soil conservation, the plan for sand prevention and control, the plan for water resources, the long-term plan for forestry, the overall plan for cities, the plans for villages and towns, and other relevant plans.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　草原保护、建设、利用规划一经批准，必须严格执行。

**Article 21** Once approved, the plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization must be strictly implemented.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家建立草原调查制度。  
县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门会同同级有关部门定期进行草原调查；草原所有者或者使用者应当支持、配合调查，并提供有关资料。

**Article 22** The State shall establish a grassland survey system.  
The grassland administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments at the same level, conduct regular surveys of grasslands; the owners or users of grasslands shall support and cooperate with the surveys and provide relevant data.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院草原行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定全国草原等级评定标准。  
县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门根据草原调查结果、草原的质量，依据草原等级评定标准，对草原进行评等定级。

**Article 23** The grassland administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national standards for grading grasslands.  
The grassland administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the results of grassland surveys and the quality of grasslands, grade and classify the grasslands in accordance with the standards for grading grasslands.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家建立草原统计制度。  
县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门和同级统计部门共同制定草原统计调查办法，依法对草原的面积、等级、产草量、载畜量等进行统计，定期发布草原统计资料。  
草原统计资料是各级人民政府编制草原保护、建设、利用规划的依据。

**Article 24** The State shall establish a grassland statistical system.  
The grassland administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and the statistical departments at the same level shall jointly formulate grassland statistical survey measures, and shall, according to law, conduct statistics on the area, grade, grass yield, livestock carrying capacity, etc. of grasslands, and regularly publish grassland statistical data.  
Grassland statistical data shall be the basis for the people's governments at all levels to formulate plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家建立草原生产、生态监测预警系统。  
县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门对草原的面积、等级、植被构成、生产能力、自然灾害、生物灾害等草原基本状况实行动态监测，及时为本级政府和有关部门提供动态监测和预警信息服务。

**Article 25** The State shall establish a monitoring and early warning system for grassland production and ecology.  
The grassland administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct dynamic monitoring of the basic conditions of grasslands, including area, grade, vegetation composition, production capacity, natural disasters and biological disasters, and shall provide in a timely manner dynamic monitoring and early warning information services to the people's governments at the same level and relevant departments.

### 第四章　建设

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　县级以上人民政府应当增加草原建设的投入，支持草原建设。  
国家鼓励单位和个人投资建设草原，按照谁投资、谁受益的原则保护草原投资建设者的合法权益。

**Article 26** People's governments at or above the county level shall increase investment in grassland construction and support grassland construction.  
The State shall encourage units and individuals to invest in grassland construction, and shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of grassland investors and constructors in accordance with the principle of whoever invests shall benefit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励与支持人工草地建设、天然草原改良和饲草饲料基地建设，稳定和提高草原生产能力。

**Article 27** The State shall encourage and support the construction of artificial grasslands, the improvement of natural grasslands and the construction of forage grass and feed bases, so as to stabilize and enhance grassland production capacity.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府应当支持、鼓励和引导农牧民开展草原围栏、饲草饲料储备、牲畜圈舍、牧民定居点等生产生活设施的建设。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当支持草原水利设施建设，发展草原节水灌溉，改善人畜饮水条件。

**Article 28** People's governments at or above the county level shall support, encourage and guide farmers and herdsmen to carry out the construction of production and living facilities such as grassland fencing, forage and feed storage, livestock sheds, and settled herdsmen settlements.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall support the construction of water conservancy facilities on grasslands, develop water-saving irrigation on grasslands, and improve the conditions for drinking water for humans and livestock.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　县级以上人民政府应当按照草原保护、建设、利用规划加强草种基地建设，鼓励选育、引进、推广优良草品种。  
新草品种必须经全国草品种审定委员会审定，由国务院草原行政主管部门公告后方可推广。从境外引进草种必须依法进行审批。  
县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门应当依法加强对草种生产、加工、检疫、检验的监督管理，保证草种质量。

**Article 29** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization, strengthen the construction of grass seed bases, and encourage the selection, breeding, introduction and promotion of fine grass varieties.  
New grass varieties must be examined and approved by the National Grass Variety Approval Committee, and may be promoted only after being announced by the grassland administrative department of the State Council. The introduction of grass seeds from abroad must be examined and approved according to law.  
The grassland administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and administration of the production, processing, quarantine and inspection of grass seeds to ensure the quality of grass seeds.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府应当有计划地进行火情监测、防火物资储备、防火隔离带等草原防火设施的建设，确保防火需要。

**Article 30** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in a planned manner, carry out the construction of grassland fire prevention facilities such as fire monitoring, fire prevention material reserves and firebreaks, so as to ensure the needs for fire prevention.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　对退化、沙化、盐碱化、石漠化和水土流失的草原，地方各级人民政府应当按照草原保护、建设、利用规划，划定治理区，组织专项治理。  
大规模的草原综合治理，列入国家国土整治计划。

**Article 31** For grasslands that are degraded, desertified, salinized, rocky or suffer from water and soil erosion, local people's governments at all levels shall, in accordance with the plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization, delimit treatment areas and organize special treatment.  
Large-scale comprehensive treatment of grasslands shall be included in the national land remediation plan.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府应当根据草原保护、建设、利用规划，在本级国民经济和社会发展计划中安排资金用于草原改良、人工种草和草种生产，任何单位或者个人不得截留、挪用；县级以上人民政府财政部门和审计部门应当加强监督管理。

**Article 32** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the plans for grassland protection, construction and utilization, allocate funds in their plans for national economic and social development for grassland improvement, artificial grass planting and grass seed production, and no unit or individual may withhold or misappropriate such funds; the financial departments and audit departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and administration.

### 第五章　利用

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　草原承包经营者应当合理利用草原，不得超过草原行政主管部门核定的载畜量；草原承包经营者应当采取种植和储备饲草饲料、增加饲草饲料供应量、调剂处理牲畜、优化畜群结构、提高出栏率等措施，保持草畜平衡。  
草原载畜量标准和草畜平衡管理办法由国务院草原行政主管部门规定。

**Article 33** Grassland contract operators shall rationally utilize grasslands and shall not exceed the livestock carrying capacity verified by the grassland administrative department; grassland contract operators shall take measures such as planting and storing forage and feed, increasing the supply of forage and feed, adjusting and disposing of livestock, optimizing herd structure, and increasing the rate of slaughter, so as to maintain the balance between grass and livestock.  
The standards for grassland livestock carrying capacity and the measures for the administration of grass-livestock balance shall be formulated by the grassland administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　牧区的草原承包经营者应当实行划区轮牧，合理配置畜群，均衡利用草原。

**Article 34** Grassland contract operators in pastoral areas shall practice rotational grazing in designated areas, rationally allocate herds, and utilize grasslands in a balanced manner.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家提倡在农区、半农半牧区和有条件的牧区实行牲畜圈养。草原承包经营者应当按照饲养牲畜的种类和数量，调剂、储备饲草饲料，采用青贮和饲草饲料加工等新技术，逐步改变依赖天然草地放牧的生产方式。  
在草原禁牧、休牧、轮牧区，国家对实行舍饲圈养的给予粮食和资金补助，具体办法由国务院或者国务院授权的有关部门规定。

**Article 35** The State shall advocate the pen-feeding of livestock in agricultural areas, semi-agricultural and semi-pastoral areas, and pastoral areas where conditions permit. Grassland contract operators shall, in accordance with the types and numbers of livestock raised, adjust and reserve forage and feed, adopt new technologies such as silage and feed processing, and gradually change the production mode of relying on natural grasslands for grazing.  
In areas where grazing on grasslands is prohibited, rested or rotated, the State shall provide grain and financial subsidies to those who practice pen-feeding, and the specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council or the relevant departments authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门对割草场和野生草种基地应当规定合理的割草期、采种期以及留茬高度和采割强度，实行轮割轮采。

**Article 36** The grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall prescribe reasonable mowing periods, seed collection periods, stubble heights and mowing intensities for mowing pastures and wild grass seed bases, and shall practice rotational mowing and rotational collection.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　遇到自然灾害等特殊情况，需要临时调剂使用草原的，按照自愿互利的原则，由双方协商解决；需要跨县临时调剂使用草原的，由有关县级人民政府或者共同的上级人民政府组织协商解决。

**Article 37** Where temporary adjustment of the use of grasslands is needed due to special circumstances such as natural disasters, the matter shall be resolved through consultation between the two parties on the principle of voluntariness and mutual benefit; where temporary adjustment of the use of grasslands across counties is needed, the matter shall be resolved through consultation organized by the relevant people's governments at the county level or by the common people's government at a higher level.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　进行矿藏开采和工程建设，应当不占或者少占草原；确需征收、征用或者使用草原的，必须经省级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门审核同意后，依照有关土地管理的法律、行政法规办理建设用地审批手续。

**Article 38** For mining and construction projects, grasslands shall not be occupied or shall be occupied as little as possible; where it is truly necessary to requisition, expropriate or use grasslands, the matter must be examined and approved by the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the provincial level, and then the procedures for examination and approval of land for construction purposes shall be handled in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations on land administration.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　因建设征收、征用集体所有的草原的，应当依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》的规定给予补偿；因建设使用国家所有的草原的，应当依照国务院有关规定对草原承包经营者给予补偿。  
因建设征收、征用或者使用草原的，应当交纳草原植被恢复费。草原植被恢复费专款专用，由草原行政主管部门按照规定用于恢复草原植被，任何单位和个人不得截留、挪用。草原植被恢复费的征收、使用和管理办法，由国务院价格主管部门和国务院财政部门会同国务院草原行政主管部门制定。

**Article 39** Where grasslands owned by collectives are requisitioned or expropriated for construction, compensation shall be given in accordance with the provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China; where grasslands owned by the State are used for construction, compensation shall be given to grassland contract operators in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Where grasslands are requisitioned, expropriated or used for construction, a fee for grassland vegetation restoration shall be paid. The fee for grassland vegetation restoration shall be used for a specific purpose, and the grassland administrative department shall use it for restoring grassland vegetation in accordance with the provisions; no unit or individual may withhold or misappropriate such fee. The measures for the collection, use and administration of the fee for grassland vegetation restoration shall be formulated by the competent pricing department of the State Council and the financial department of the State Council jointly with the grassland administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　需要临时占用草原的，应当经县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门审核同意。  
临时占用草原的期限不得超过二年，并不得在临时占用的草原上修建永久性建筑物、构筑物；占用期满，用地单位必须恢复草原植被并及时退还。

**Article 40** Where grasslands need to be temporarily occupied, the matter shall be subject to examination and approval by the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level.  
The period of temporary occupation of grasslands shall not exceed two years, and no permanent buildings or structures may be constructed on the temporarily occupied grasslands; upon expiry of the occupation period, the land-using unit must restore the grassland vegetation and promptly return the land.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在草原上修建直接为草原保护和畜牧业生产服务的工程设施，需要使用草原的，由县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门批准；修筑其他工程，需要将草原转为非畜牧业生产用地的，必须依法办理建设用地审批手续。  
前款所称直接为草原保护和畜牧业生产服务的工程设施，是指：  
（一）生产、贮存草种和饲草饲料的设施；  
（二）牲畜圈舍、配种点、剪毛点、药浴池、人畜饮水设施；  
（三）科研、试验、示范基地；  
（四）草原防火和灌溉设施。

**Article 41** Where engineering facilities that directly serve grassland protection and animal husbandry production need to be constructed on grasslands and require the use of grasslands, the matter shall be approved by the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level; where other projects need to be constructed and require the conversion of grasslands into land for non-animal husbandry production, the procedures for examination and approval of land for construction purposes must be handled according to law.  
The "engineering facilities that directly serve grassland protection and animal husbandry production" mentioned in the preceding paragraph refer to:  
(1) facilities for producing and storing grass seeds and forage and feed;  
(2) livestock sheds, breeding stations, shearing stations, dipping baths, and drinking water facilities for humans and livestock;  
(3) scientific research, experimental and demonstration bases; and  
(4) grassland fire prevention and irrigation facilities.

### 第六章　保护

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家实行基本草原保护制度。下列草原应当划为基本草原，实施严格管理：  
（一）重要放牧场；  
（二）割草地；  
（三）用于畜牧业生产的人工草地、退耕还草地以及改良草地、草种基地；  
（四）对调节气候、涵养水源、保持水土、防风固沙具有特殊作用的草原；  
（五）作为国家重点保护野生动植物生存环境的草原；  
（六）草原科研、教学试验基地；  
（七）国务院规定应当划为基本草原的其他草原。  
基本草原的保护管理办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 42** The State shall practice a basic grassland protection system. The following grasslands shall be designated as basic grasslands and be subject to strict management:  
(1) important grazing pastures;  
(2) mowing pastures;  
(3) artificial grasslands used for animal husbandry production, grasslands returned from farmland, improved grasslands, and grass seed bases;  
(4) grasslands that have special functions in regulating climate, conserving water sources, retaining water and soil, and preventing wind and fixing sand;  
(5) grasslands that serve as living environments for wild animals and plants under key State protection;  
(6) grassland scientific research and teaching experiment bases; and  
(7) other grasslands that shall be designated as basic grasslands as prescribed by the State Council.  
The measures for the protection and management of basic grasslands shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院草原行政主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以按照自然保护区管理的有关规定在下列地区建立草原自然保护区：  
（一）具有代表性的草原类型；  
（二）珍稀濒危野生动植物分布区；  
（三）具有重要生态功能和经济科研价值的草原。

**Article 43** The grassland administrative department of the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in accordance with the relevant provisions on the administration of nature reserves, establish grassland nature reserves in the following areas:  
(1) areas with representative types of grasslands;  
(2) areas where rare and endangered wild animals and plants are distributed; and  
(3) grasslands with important ecological functions and economic and scientific research value.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　县级以上人民政府应当依法加强对草原珍稀濒危野生植物和种质资源的保护、管理。

**Article 44** People's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, strengthen the protection and management of rare and endangered wild plants and germplasm resources on grasslands.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家对草原实行以草定畜、草畜平衡制度。县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门应当按照国务院草原行政主管部门制定的草原载畜量标准，结合当地实际情况，定期核定草原载畜量。各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，防止超载过牧。

**Article 45** The State shall practice the systems of determining livestock numbers based on grass availability and of grass-livestock balance on grasslands. The grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the standards for grassland livestock carrying capacity formulated by the grassland administrative department of the State Council and in light of the local actual conditions, regularly verify the livestock carrying capacity of grasslands. People's governments at all levels shall take effective measures to prevent overgrazing.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　禁止开垦草原。对水土流失严重、有沙化趋势、需要改善生态环境的已垦草原，应当有计划、有步骤地退耕还草；已造成沙化、盐碱化、石漠化的，应当限期治理。

**Article 46** The reclamation of grasslands is prohibited. For reclaimed grasslands that suffer from serious water and soil erosion, have a tendency of desertification, or need ecological environment improvement, the conversion of farmland to grassland shall be carried out in a planned and step-by-step manner; where desertification, salinization or rocky desertification has been caused, treatment shall be completed within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对严重退化、沙化、盐碱化、石漠化的草原和生态脆弱区的草原，实行禁牧、休牧制度。

**Article 47** The systems of prohibiting grazing and resting grazing shall be practiced on grasslands that are severely degraded, desertified, salinized or rocky, and on grasslands in ecologically fragile areas.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家支持依法实行退耕还草和禁牧、休牧。具体办法由国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。  
对在国务院批准规划范围内实施退耕还草的农牧民，按照国家规定给予粮食、现金、草种费补助。退耕还草完成后，由县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门核实登记，依法履行土地用途变更手续，发放草原权属证书。

**Article 48** The State shall support the implementation of the conversion of farmland to grassland and the prohibition and resting of grazing according to law. The specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council or the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Farmers and herdsmen who carry out the conversion of farmland to grassland within the scope of the plan approved by the State Council shall be given subsidies in grain, cash and grass seed expenses in accordance with the provisions of the State. After the completion of the conversion of farmland to grassland, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall verify and register, handle the procedures for alteration of land use according to law, and issue certificates of grassland rights.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　禁止在荒漠、半荒漠和严重退化、沙化、盐碱化、石漠化、水土流失的草原以及生态脆弱区的草原上采挖植物和从事破坏草原植被的其他活动。

**Article 49** It is prohibited to dig up plants or engage in other activities that damage grassland vegetation in deserts, semi-deserts, grasslands that are severely degraded, desertified, salinized, rocky or suffer from water and soil erosion, and grasslands in ecologically fragile areas.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在草原上从事采土、采砂、采石等作业活动，应当报县级人民政府草原行政主管部门批准；开采矿产资源的，并应当依法办理有关手续。  
经批准在草原上从事本条第一款所列活动的，应当在规定的时间、区域内，按照准许的采挖方式作业，并采取保护草原植被的措施。  
在他人使用的草原上从事本条第一款所列活动的，还应当事先征得草原使用者的同意。

**Article 50** For operations such as extracting soil, sand or stone on grasslands, approval shall be obtained from the grassland administrative department of the people's government at the county level; for mining mineral resources, the relevant procedures shall also be handled according to law.  
Those who are approved to engage in the activities listed in the first paragraph of this Article on grasslands shall operate within the prescribed time and area, in the approved manner of extraction, and shall take measures to protect grassland vegetation.  
Where the activities listed in the first paragraph of this Article are carried out on grasslands used by others, the consent of the grassland user shall also be obtained in advance.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　在草原上种植牧草或者饲料作物，应当符合草原保护、建设、利用规划；县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门应当加强监督管理，防止草原沙化和水土流失。

**Article 51** The planting of pasture grass or feed crops on grassland shall conform to the plans for the protection, construction and utilization of grasslands; the grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and administration to prevent desertification of grasslands and water and soil erosion.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在草原上开展经营性旅游活动，应当符合有关草原保护、建设、利用规划，并不得侵犯草原所有者、使用者和承包经营者的合法权益，不得破坏草原植被。

**Article 52** Business tourism activities on grassland shall conform to the relevant plans for the protection, construction and utilization of grasslands, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the owners, users and contractors of grasslands, nor destroy grassland vegetation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　草原防火工作贯彻预防为主、防消结合的方针。  
各级人民政府应当建立草原防火责任制，规定草原防火期，制定草原防火扑火预案，切实做好草原火灾的预防和扑救工作。

**Article 53** The work of grassland fire prevention shall follow the principle of putting prevention first and combining prevention with extinguishing.  
People's governments at all levels shall establish a grassland fire prevention responsibility system, designate a grassland fire prevention period, formulate grassland fire prevention and extinguishing plans, and earnestly do a good job in preventing and extinguishing grassland fires.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上地方人民政府应当做好草原鼠害、病虫害和毒害草防治的组织管理工作。县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门应当采取措施，加强草原鼠害、病虫害和毒害草监测预警、调查以及防治工作，组织研究和推广综合防治的办法。  
禁止在草原上使用剧毒、高残留以及可能导致二次中毒的农药。

**Article 54** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall do a good job in the organization and management of the prevention and control of rodent infestations, plant diseases and insect pests, and poisonous and harmful plants on grasslands. The grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen the monitoring, early warning, investigation, and prevention and control of rodent infestations, plant diseases and insect pests, and poisonous and harmful plants on grasslands, and organize research and promotion of integrated control methods.  
The use of pesticides that are highly toxic, leave high residues, or may cause secondary poisoning on grasslands is prohibited.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　除抢险救灾和牧民搬迁的机动车辆外，禁止机动车辆离开道路在草原上行驶，破坏草原植被；因从事地质勘探、科学考察等活动确需离开道路在草原上行驶的，应当事先向所在地县级人民政府草原行政主管部门报告行驶区域和行驶路线，并按照报告的行驶区域和行驶路线在草原上行驶。

**Article 55** Except for motor vehicles used for emergency rescue, disaster relief, and the relocation of herders, motor vehicles are prohibited from leaving roads and driving on grasslands, thereby destroying grassland vegetation; where, due to activities such as geological prospecting and scientific investigation, it is truly necessary to leave roads and drive on grasslands, the driving area and route shall be reported in advance to the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at the county level, and the vehicle shall drive on the grassland in accordance with the reported driving area and route.

### 第七章　监督检查

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院草原行政主管部门和草原面积较大的省、自治区的县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门设立草原监督管理机构，负责草原法律、法规执行情况的监督检查，对违反草原法律、法规的行为进行查处。  
草原行政主管部门和草原监督管理机构应当加强执法队伍建设，提高草原监督检查人员的政治、业务素质。草原监督检查人员应当忠于职守，秉公执法。

**Article 56** The grassland administrative department of the State Council and the grassland administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in provinces and autonomous regions with large grassland areas shall establish grassland supervision and administration organs to be responsible for the supervision and inspection of the implementation of grassland laws and regulations, and to investigate and deal with acts violating grassland laws and regulations.  
The grassland administrative departments and the grassland supervision and administration organs shall strengthen the building of law enforcement teams and improve the political and professional qualities of grassland supervision and inspection personnel. Grassland supervision and inspection personnel shall be devoted to their duties and enforce the law impartially.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　草原监督检查人员履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位或者个人提供有关草原权属的文件和资料，进行查阅或者复制；  
（二）要求被检查单位或者个人对草原权属等问题作出说明；  
（三）进入违法现场进行拍照、摄像和勘测；  
（四）责令被检查单位或者个人停止违反草原法律、法规的行为，履行法定义务。

**Article 57** When performing their duties of supervision and inspection, grassland supervision and inspection personnel shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) to require the unit or individual under inspection to provide documents and data concerning the ownership of grasslands, and to examine or copy them;  
(2) to require the unit or individual under inspection to provide explanations on issues such as the ownership of grasslands;  
(3) to enter the site of the illegal act to take photographs, make video recordings, and conduct surveys;  
(4) to order the unit or individual under inspection to cease acts violating grassland laws and regulations and to perform legal obligations.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国务院草原行政主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府草原行政主管部门，应当加强对草原监督检查人员的培训和考核。

**Article 58** The grassland administrative department of the State Council and the grassland administrative departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strengthen the training and assessment of grassland supervision and inspection personnel.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　有关单位和个人对草原监督检查人员的监督检查工作应当给予支持、配合，不得拒绝或者阻碍草原监督检查人员依法执行职务。  
草原监督检查人员在履行监督检查职责时，应当向被检查单位和个人出示执法证件。

**Article 59** The relevant units and individuals shall support and cooperate with the supervision and inspection work of grassland supervision and inspection personnel, and shall not refuse or obstruct grassland supervision and inspection personnel in the lawful performance of their duties.  
When performing their duties of supervision and inspection, grassland supervision and inspection personnel shall present their law enforcement credentials to the units and individuals under inspection.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对违反草原法律、法规的行为，应当依法作出行政处理，有关草原行政主管部门不作出行政处理决定的，上级草原行政主管部门有权责令有关草原行政主管部门作出行政处理决定或者直接作出行政处理决定。

**Article 60** Administrative handling shall be imposed according to law for acts violating grassland laws and regulations. If the relevant grassland administrative department fails to make an administrative handling decision, the grassland administrative department at the higher level shall have the power to order the relevant grassland administrative department to make an administrative handling decision or directly make an administrative handling decision.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　草原行政主管部门工作人员及其他国家机关有关工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权，不依法履行监督管理职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 61** If staff members of the grassland administrative department or other relevant staff members of State organs, through neglect of duty or abuse of power, fail to perform their supervision and management duties according to law, or fail to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　截留、挪用草原改良、人工种草和草种生产资金或者草原植被恢复费，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 62** If funds for grassland improvement, artificial grass planting, and grass seed production, or charges for the restoration of grassland vegetation are withheld or misappropriated, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　无权批准征收、征用、使用草原的单位或者个人非法批准征收、征用、使用草原的，超越批准权限非法批准征收、征用、使用草原的，或者违反法律规定的程序批准征收、征用、使用草原，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。非法批准征收、征用、使用草原的文件无效。非法批准征收、征用、使用的草原应当收回，当事人拒不归还的，以非法使用草原论处。  
非法批准征收、征用、使用草原，给当事人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 63** If a unit or individual without the authority to approve the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands illegally approves the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands, exceeds the limits of authority in illegally approving the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands, or approves the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands in violation of the procedures prescribed by law, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law. Documents illegally approving the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands shall be null and void. The grasslands illegally approved for expropriation, requisition, or use shall be recovered; if the party concerned refuses to return them, the matter shall be dealt with as illegal use of grasslands.  
If damage is caused to a party by the illegal approval of the expropriation, requisition, or use of grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　买卖或者以其他形式非法转让草原，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 64** If grasslands are sold or illegally transferred in any other form, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order corrections within a specified time limit, confiscate the illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　未经批准或者采取欺骗手段骗取批准，非法使用草原，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令退还非法使用的草原，对违反草原保护、建设、利用规划擅自将草原改为建设用地的，限期拆除在非法使用的草原上新建的建筑物和其他设施，恢复草原植被，并处草原被非法使用前三年平均产值六倍以上十二倍以下的罚款。

**Article 65** If grasslands are illegally used without approval or by fraudulent means to obtain approval, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order the return of the illegally used grasslands; if grasslands are converted into construction land in violation of the plans for the protection, construction and utilization of grasslands, it shall order the demolition, within a specified time limit, of buildings and other structures newly built on the illegally used grasslands, the restoration of grassland vegetation, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than six times but not more than twelve times the average output value of the grasslands in the three years preceding the illegal use.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　非法开垦草原，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由县级以上人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令停止违法行为，限期恢复植被，没收非法财物和违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，并处五万元以下的罚款；给草原所有者或者使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 66** If grasslands are illegally reclaimed, and if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order cessation of the illegal act, restoration of vegetation within a specified time limit, confiscation of illegal property and illegal gains, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if losses are caused to the owners or users of the grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在荒漠、半荒漠和严重退化、沙化、盐碱化、石漠化、水土流失的草原，以及生态脆弱区的草原上采挖植物或者从事破坏草原植被的其他活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令停止违法行为，没收非法财物和违法所得，可以并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以并处五万元以下的罚款；给草原所有者或者使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 67** If plants are dug up or other activities that destroy grassland vegetation are engaged in on grasslands that are desert, semi-desert, or suffer from severe degradation, desertification, salinization, rocky desertification, or water and soil erosion, as well as on grasslands in ecologically fragile areas, the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate illegal property and illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if losses are caused to the owners or users of the grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　未经批准或者未按照规定的时间、区域和采挖方式在草原上进行采土、采砂、采石等活动的，由县级人民政府草原行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复植被，没收非法财物和违法所得，可以并处违法所得一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以并处二万元以下的罚款；给草原所有者或者使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 68** If activities such as quarrying earth, sand, or stone are carried out on grasslands without approval or not in accordance with the prescribed time, area, and method of extraction, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration of vegetation within a specified time limit, confiscation of illegal property and illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan may be concurrently imposed; if losses are caused to the owners or users of the grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反[本法第五十二条](#art-52)规定，在草原上开展经营性旅游活动，破坏草原植被的，由县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令停止违法行为，限期恢复植被，没收违法所得，可以并处违法所得一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得的，可以并处草原被破坏前三年平均产值六倍以上十二倍以下的罚款；给草原所有者或者使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 69** If, in violation of the provisions of Article 52 of this Law, business tourism activities are carried out on grasslands, destroying grassland vegetation, the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order cessation of the illegal act, restoration of vegetation within a specified time limit, confiscation of illegal gains, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains, a fine of not less than six times but not more than twelve times the average output value of the grasslands in the three years preceding the destruction may be concurrently imposed; if losses are caused to the owners or users of the grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　非抢险救灾和牧民搬迁的机动车辆离开道路在草原上行驶，或者从事地质勘探、科学考察等活动，未事先向所在地县级人民政府草原行政主管部门报告或者未按照报告的行驶区域和行驶路线在草原上行驶，破坏草原植被的，由县级人民政府草原行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复植被，可以并处草原被破坏前三年平均产值三倍以上九倍以下的罚款；给草原所有者或者使用者造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 70** If motor vehicles other than those used for emergency rescue, disaster relief, and the relocation of herders leave roads and drive on grasslands, or if, when engaging in activities such as geological prospecting and scientific investigation, no prior report is made to the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at the county level, or the vehicle does not drive on the grassland in accordance with the reported driving area and route, thereby destroying grassland vegetation, the grassland administrative department of the people's government at the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, restoration of vegetation within a specified time limit, and may concurrently impose a fine of not less than three times but not more than nine times the average output value of the grasslands in the three years preceding the destruction; if losses are caused to the owners or users of the grasslands, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　在临时占用的草原上修建永久性建筑物、构筑物的，由县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令限期拆除；逾期不拆除的，依法强制拆除，所需费用由违法者承担。  
临时占用草原，占用期届满，用地单位不予恢复草原植被的，由县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门依据职权责令限期恢复；逾期不恢复的，由县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门代为恢复，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 71** If permanent buildings or structures are constructed on temporarily occupied grasslands, the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order their demolition within a specified time limit; if they are not demolished within the time limit, compulsory demolition shall be carried out according to law, and the expenses required shall be borne by the violator.  
If, upon expiration of the period of temporary occupation of grasslands, the land-using unit fails to restore the grassland vegetation, the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within its authority, order restoration within a specified time limit; if restoration is not carried out within the time limit, the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall carry out the restoration on behalf of the violator, and the expenses required shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　未经批准，擅自改变草原保护、建设、利用规划的，由县级以上人民政府责令限期改正；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 72** If the plans for the protection, construction and utilization of grasslands are modified without approval, the people's government at or above the county level shall order corrections within a specified time limit; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　对违反本法有关草畜平衡制度的规定，牲畜饲养量超过县级以上地方人民政府草原行政主管部门核定的草原载畜量标准的纠正或者处罚措施，由省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会或者其常务委员会规定。

**Article 73** The corrective or punitive measures for violation of the provisions of this Law concerning the forage-livestock balance system, where the number of livestock raised exceeds the grassland livestock carrying capacity standard verified by the grassland administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level, shall be prescribed by the people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or by its standing committee.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　[本法第二条](#art-2)第二款中所称的天然草原包括草地、草山和草坡，人工草地包括改良草地和退耕还草地，不包括城镇草地。

**Article 74** The term "natural grasslands" as used in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of this Law includes grasslands, grass mountains, and grass slopes; "artificial grasslands" includes improved grasslands and land returned from cultivation to grasslands, but excludes urban grasslands.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　本法自2003年3月1日起施行。

**Article 75** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2003.
