# 中华人民共和国噪声污染防治法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2021-12-24  
**生效日期**：2022-06-05

> （2021年12月24日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　噪声污染防治标准和规划 第三章　噪声污染防治的监督管理 第四章　工业噪声污染防治 第五章　建筑施工噪声污染防治 第六章　交通运输噪声污染防治 第七章　社会生活噪声污染防治 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了防治噪声污染，保障公众健康，保护和改善生活环境，维护社会和谐，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称噪声，是指在工业生产、建筑施工、交通运输和社会生活中产生的干扰周围生活环境的声音。 本法所称噪声污染，是指超过噪声排放标准或者未依法采取防控措施产生噪声，并干扰他人正常生活、工作和学习的现象。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了防治噪声污染，保障公众健康，保护和改善生活环境，维护社会和谐，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of preventing and controlling noise pollution, safeguarding public health, protecting and improving the living environment, maintaining social harmony, advancing ecological civilization, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称噪声，是指在工业生产、建筑施工、交通运输和社会生活中产生的干扰周围生活环境的声音。  
本法所称噪声污染，是指超过噪声排放标准或者未依法采取防控措施产生噪声，并干扰他人正常生活、工作和学习的现象。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, 'noise' means sounds generated in industrial production, construction, transportation, and social life that interfere with the surrounding living environment.  
For the purposes of this Law, 'noise pollution' means the phenomenon where noise exceeds noise emission standards or noise is generated without taking prevention and control measures according to law, and such noise interferes with the normal life, work, and study of others.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　噪声污染的防治，适用本法。  
因从事本职生产经营工作受到噪声危害的防治，适用劳动保护等其他有关法律的规定。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to the prevention and control of noise pollution.  
The prevention and control of noise hazards caused by engaging in one's own production and operation work shall be governed by the provisions of other relevant laws, such as labor protection.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　噪声污染防治应当坚持统筹规划、源头防控、分类管理、社会共治、损害担责的原则。

**Article 4** The prevention and control of noise pollution shall adhere to the principles of overall planning, source prevention and control, classified management, social co-governance, and liability for damage.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当将噪声污染防治工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、生态环境保护规划，将噪声污染防治工作经费纳入本级政府预算。  
生态环境保护规划应当明确噪声污染防治目标、任务、保障措施等内容。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate noise pollution prevention and control work into national economic and social development plans and ecological environment protection plans, and shall incorporate the funds for noise pollution prevention and control work into the budgets of the people's governments at the same level.  
Ecological environment protection plans shall specify the objectives, tasks, and safeguard measures for noise pollution prevention and control, among other contents.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　地方各级人民政府对本行政区域声环境质量负责，采取有效措施，改善声环境质量。  
国家实行噪声污染防治目标责任制和考核评价制度，将噪声污染防治目标完成情况纳入考核评价内容。

**Article 6** Local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible for the acoustic environment quality in their respective administrative regions and shall take effective measures to improve the acoustic environment quality.  
The State shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for noise pollution prevention and control, and shall incorporate the completion of noise pollution prevention and control targets into the assessment and evaluation content.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当依照本法和国务院的规定，明确有关部门的噪声污染防治监督管理职责，根据需要建立噪声污染防治工作协调联动机制，加强部门协同配合、信息共享，推进本行政区域噪声污染防治工作。

**Article 7** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law and the regulations of the State Council, clarify the noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management responsibilities of the relevant departments, establish a coordination and linkage mechanism for noise pollution prevention and control work as needed, strengthen departmental coordination, cooperation, and information sharing, and promote noise pollution prevention and control work in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院生态环境主管部门对全国噪声污染防治实施统一监督管理。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门对本行政区域噪声污染防治实施统一监督管理。  
各级住房和城乡建设、公安、交通运输、铁路监督管理、民用航空、海事等部门，在各自职责范围内，对建筑施工、交通运输和社会生活噪声污染防治实施监督管理。  
基层群众性自治组织应当协助地方人民政府及其有关部门做好噪声污染防治工作。

**Article 8** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall exercise unified supervision and management over noise pollution prevention and control nationwide.  
The competent departments of ecological environment of local people's governments shall exercise unified supervision and management over noise pollution prevention and control in their respective administrative regions.  
The departments of housing and urban-rural development, public security, transportation, railway supervision and administration, civil aviation, maritime affairs, and other relevant departments at all levels shall, within their respective functions and duties, exercise supervision and management over the prevention and control of noise pollution from construction, transportation, and social life.  
Grassroots mass autonomous organizations shall assist local people's governments and their relevant departments in carrying out noise pollution prevention and control work.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都有保护声环境的义务，同时依法享有获取声环境信息、参与和监督噪声污染防治的权利。  
排放噪声的单位和个人应当采取有效措施，防止、减轻噪声污染。

**Article 9** All units and individuals shall have the obligation to protect the acoustic environment, and at the same time, they shall enjoy the right to obtain acoustic environment information, participate in and supervise noise pollution prevention and control according to law.  
Units and individuals that emit noise shall take effective measures to prevent and mitigate noise pollution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当加强噪声污染防治法律法规和知识的宣传教育普及工作，增强公众噪声污染防治意识，引导公众依法参与噪声污染防治工作。  
新闻媒体应当开展噪声污染防治法律法规和知识的公益宣传，对违反噪声污染防治法律法规的行为进行舆论监督。  
国家鼓励基层群众性自治组织、社会组织、公共场所管理者、业主委员会、物业服务人、志愿者等开展噪声污染防治法律法规和知识的宣传。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity, education, and popularization of laws, regulations, and knowledge on noise pollution prevention and control, enhance the public's awareness of noise pollution prevention and control, and guide the public to participate in noise pollution prevention and control work according to law.  
News media shall conduct public interest publicity on laws, regulations, and knowledge on noise pollution prevention and control, and exercise public opinion supervision over acts that violate laws and regulations on noise pollution prevention and control.  
The State encourages grassroots mass autonomous organizations, social organizations, managers of public places, owners' committees, property service providers, volunteers, and others to carry out publicity on laws, regulations, and knowledge on noise pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励、支持噪声污染防治科学技术研究开发、成果转化和推广应用，加强噪声污染防治专业技术人才培养，促进噪声污染防治科学技术进步和产业发展。

**Article 11** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research and development, transformation of achievements, and popularization and application of noise pollution prevention and control, strengthens the cultivation of professional and technical personnel for noise pollution prevention and control, and promotes scientific and technological progress and industrial development in noise pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对在噪声污染防治工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** Units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in noise pollution prevention and control work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with state regulations.

### 第二章　噪声污染防治标准和规划

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家推进噪声污染防治标准体系建设。  
国务院生态环境主管部门和国务院其他有关部门，在各自职责范围内，制定和完善噪声污染防治相关标准，加强标准之间的衔接协调。

**Article 13** The State shall promote the development of a standards system for noise pollution prevention and control.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, formulate and improve relevant standards for noise pollution prevention and control, and strengthen the connection and coordination between standards.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门制定国家声环境质量标准。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据国家声环境质量标准和国土空间规划以及用地现状，划定本行政区域各类声环境质量标准的适用区域；将以用于居住、科学研究、医疗卫生、文化教育、机关团体办公、社会福利等的建筑物为主的区域，划定为噪声敏感建筑物集中区域，加强噪声污染防治。  
声环境质量标准适用区域范围和噪声敏感建筑物集中区域范围应当向社会公布。

**Article 14** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall formulate national acoustic environment quality standards.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the national acoustic environment quality standards, territorial space planning, and current land use, delineate the applicable areas for various types of acoustic environment quality standards in their respective administrative regions; they shall delineate areas predominantly consisting of buildings used for residence, scientific research, medical and health care, cultural education, government offices, social welfare, etc., as concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, and strengthen noise pollution prevention and control.  
The scope of applicable areas for acoustic environment quality standards and the scope of concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings shall be announced to the public.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据国家声环境质量标准和国家经济、技术条件，制定国家噪声排放标准以及相关的环境振动控制标准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府对尚未制定国家噪声排放标准的，可以制定地方噪声排放标准；对已经制定国家噪声排放标准的，可以制定严于国家噪声排放标准的地方噪声排放标准。地方噪声排放标准应当报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 15** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, based on the national acoustic environment quality standards and the national economic and technological conditions, formulate national noise emission standards and relevant environmental vibration control standards.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may formulate local noise emission standards for noise emission standards that have not yet been formulated nationally; for those that have already been formulated nationally, they may formulate local noise emission standards that are stricter than the national noise emission standards. Local noise emission standards shall be submitted to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院标准化主管部门会同国务院发展改革、生态环境、工业和信息化、住房和城乡建设、交通运输、铁路监督管理、民用航空、海事等部门，对可能产生噪声污染的工业设备、施工机械、机动车、铁路机车车辆、城市轨道交通车辆、民用航空器、机动船舶、电气电子产品、建筑附属设备等产品，根据声环境保护的要求和国家经济、技术条件，在其技术规范或者产品质量标准中规定噪声限值。  
前款规定的产品使用时产生噪声的限值，应当在有关技术文件中注明。禁止生产、进口或者销售不符合噪声限值的产品。  
县级以上人民政府市场监督管理等部门对生产、销售的有噪声限值的产品进行监督抽查，对电梯等特种设备使用时发出的噪声进行监督抽测，生态环境主管部门予以配合。

**Article 16** The competent department of standardization of the State Council shall, together with the departments of development and reform, ecological environment, industry and information technology, housing and urban-rural development, transportation, railway supervision and administration, civil aviation, maritime affairs, and other relevant departments of the State Council, prescribe noise limits in the technical specifications or product quality standards for products that may generate noise pollution, such as industrial equipment, construction machinery, motor vehicles, railway locomotives and rolling stock, urban rail transit vehicles, civil aircraft, motor vessels, electrical and electronic products, and building ancillary equipment, based on the requirements for acoustic environment protection and the national economic and technological conditions.  
The noise limits generated when using the products prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be indicated in the relevant technical documents. The production, import, or sale of products that do not meet the noise limits shall be prohibited.  
The market supervision and administration departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervisory spot checks on products with noise limits that are produced or sold, and shall conduct supervisory spot tests on noise generated by special equipment such as elevators when in use, and the competent departments of ecological environment shall provide coordination.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　声环境质量标准、噪声排放标准和其他噪声污染防治相关标准应当定期评估，并根据评估结果适时修订。

**Article 17** Acoustic environment quality standards, noise emission standards, and other relevant standards for noise pollution prevention and control shall be periodically assessed, and shall be revised in a timely manner based on the assessment results.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各级人民政府及其有关部门制定、修改国土空间规划和相关规划，应当依法进行环境影响评价，充分考虑城乡区域开发、改造和建设项目产生的噪声对周围生活环境的影响，统筹规划，合理安排土地用途和建设布局，防止、减轻噪声污染。有关环境影响篇章、说明或者报告书中应当包括噪声污染防治内容。

**Article 18** When formulating and modifying territorial space plans and relevant plans, people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall conduct environmental impact assessments according to law, fully consider the impact of noise generated by urban and rural regional development, renovation, and construction projects on the surrounding living environment, make overall plans, rationally arrange land use and construction layout, and prevent and mitigate noise pollution. The relevant environmental impact chapters, descriptions, or reports shall include contents on noise pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　确定建设布局，应当根据国家声环境质量标准和民用建筑隔声设计相关标准，合理划定建筑物与交通干线等的防噪声距离，并提出相应的规划设计要求。

**Article 19** When determining construction layout, the noise prevention distance between buildings and traffic trunks, etc., shall be reasonably delineated based on the national acoustic environment quality standards and relevant standards for sound insulation design of civil buildings, and corresponding planning and design requirements shall be proposed.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　未达到国家声环境质量标准的区域所在的设区的市、县级人民政府，应当及时编制声环境质量改善规划及其实施方案，采取有效措施，改善声环境质量。  
声环境质量改善规划及其实施方案应当向社会公开。

**Article 20** The people's governments of cities divided into districts and counties where the areas have not reached the national acoustic environment quality standards shall promptly formulate acoustic environment quality improvement plans and their implementation plans, and take effective measures to improve the acoustic environment quality.  
The acoustic environment quality improvement plans and their implementation plans shall be made public to the society.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　编制声环境质量改善规划及其实施方案，制定、修订噪声污染防治相关标准，应当征求有关行业协会、企业事业单位、专家和公众等的意见。

**Article 21** When formulating acoustic environment quality improvement plans and their implementation plans, and formulating or revising relevant standards for noise pollution prevention and control, opinions from relevant industry associations, enterprises, institutions, experts, and the public, among others, shall be solicited.

### 第三章　噪声污染防治的监督管理

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　排放噪声、产生振动，应当符合噪声排放标准以及相关的环境振动控制标准和有关法律、法规、规章的要求。  
排放噪声的单位和公共场所管理者，应当建立噪声污染防治责任制度，明确负责人和相关人员的责任。

**Article 22** Emitting noise or generating vibration shall comply with the noise emission standards and relevant environmental vibration control standards, as well as the requirements of relevant laws, regulations, and rules.  
Units that emit noise and managers of public places shall establish a responsibility system for noise pollution prevention and control, and specify the responsibilities of the person in charge and relevant personnel.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责制定噪声监测和评价规范，会同国务院有关部门组织声环境质量监测网络，规划国家声环境质量监测站（点）的设置，组织开展全国声环境质量监测，推进监测自动化，统一发布全国声环境质量状况信息。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门会同有关部门按照规定设置本行政区域声环境质量监测站（点），组织开展本行政区域声环境质量监测，定期向社会公布声环境质量状况信息。  
地方人民政府生态环境等部门应当加强对噪声敏感建筑物周边等重点区域噪声排放情况的调查、监测。

**Article 23** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating specifications for noise monitoring and assessment, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize a monitoring network for acoustic environment quality, plan the setup of national acoustic environment quality monitoring stations (points), organize and carry out national acoustic environment quality monitoring, promote monitoring automation, and uniformly release national acoustic environment quality information.  
Local people's governments' competent departments of ecological environment shall, together with relevant departments, set up acoustic environment quality monitoring stations (points) in their respective administrative regions as prescribed, organize and carry out acoustic environment quality monitoring in their respective administrative regions, and regularly publish acoustic environment quality information to the society.  
Local people's governments' ecological environment and other departments shall strengthen the investigation and monitoring of noise emissions in key areas such as the surroundings of noise-sensitive buildings.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　新建、改建、扩建可能产生噪声污染的建设项目，应当依法进行环境影响评价。

**Article 24** Construction projects that are newly built, renovated, or expanded and may generate noise pollution shall undergo environmental impact assessment according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　建设项目的噪声污染防治设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产使用。  
建设项目在投入生产或者使用之前，建设单位应当依照有关法律法规的规定，对配套建设的噪声污染防治设施进行验收，编制验收报告，并向社会公开。未经验收或者验收不合格的，该建设项目不得投入生产或者使用。

**Article 25** Noise pollution prevention and control facilities of a construction project shall be designed, constructed, and put into operation and use simultaneously with the main project.  
Before a construction project is put into production or use, the construction unit shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, carry out acceptance inspection of the supporting noise pollution prevention and control facilities, prepare an acceptance inspection report, and make it public to the society. If the acceptance inspection has not been carried out or has failed, the construction project shall not be put into production or use.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　建设噪声敏感建筑物，应当符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求，不符合标准要求的，不得通过验收、交付使用；在交通干线两侧、工业企业周边等地方建设噪声敏感建筑物，还应当按照规定间隔一定距离，并采取减少振动、降低噪声的措施。

**Article 26** The construction of noise-sensitive buildings shall meet the requirements of relevant standards for sound insulation design of civil buildings; if the standards requirements are not met, they shall not pass acceptance inspection or be delivered for use. When constructing noise-sensitive buildings in places such as on both sides of traffic trunks or around industrial enterprises, a certain distance shall be kept as prescribed, and measures to reduce vibration and lower noise shall be taken.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励、支持低噪声工艺和设备的研究开发和推广应用，实行噪声污染严重的落后工艺和设备淘汰制度。  
国务院发展改革部门会同国务院有关部门确定噪声污染严重的工艺和设备淘汰期限，并纳入国家综合性产业政策目录。  
生产者、进口者、销售者或者使用者应当在规定期限内停止生产、进口、销售或者使用列入前款规定目录的设备。工艺的采用者应当在规定期限内停止采用列入前款规定目录的工艺。

**Article 27** The State encourages and supports the research, development, and popularization and application of low-noise processes and equipment, and implements a phasing-out system for outdated processes and equipment that cause serious noise pollution.  
The development and reform department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, determine the phasing-out period for processes and equipment that cause serious noise pollution, and include them in the national comprehensive industrial policy catalog.  
Producers, importers, sellers, or users shall stop producing, importing, selling, or using the equipment included in the catalog prescribed in the preceding paragraph within the prescribed period. Adopters of processes shall stop adopting the processes included in the catalog prescribed in the preceding paragraph within the prescribed period.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对未完成声环境质量改善规划设定目标的地区以及噪声污染问题突出、群众反映强烈的地区，省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门会同其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门约谈该地区人民政府及其有关部门的主要负责人，要求其采取有效措施及时整改。约谈和整改情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 28** For areas that have not completed the targets set in the acoustic environment quality improvement plans and areas with prominent noise pollution problems and strong public complaints, the competent departments of ecological environment of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, together with other departments responsible for noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management, conduct interviews with the principal responsible persons of the people's governments and their relevant departments in those areas, and require them to take effective measures to rectify the situation in a timely manner. The interviews and rectification situations shall be made public to the society.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　生态环境主管部门和其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门，有权对排放噪声的单位或者场所进行现场检查。被检查者应当如实反映情况，提供必要的资料，不得拒绝或者阻挠。实施检查的部门、人员对现场检查中知悉的商业秘密应当保密。  
检查人员进行现场检查，不得少于两人，并应当主动出示执法证件。

**Article 29** The competent departments of ecological environment and other departments responsible for noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management shall have the power to conduct on-site inspections of units or places that emit noise. The inspected party shall truthfully reflect the situation and provide necessary materials, and shall not refuse or obstruct. The departments and personnel conducting the inspections shall keep confidential the trade secrets learned during the on-site inspections.  
When conducting on-site inspections, the inspectors shall be no fewer than two persons, and shall proactively show their law enforcement credentials.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　排放噪声造成严重污染，被责令改正拒不改正的，生态环境主管部门或者其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门，可以查封、扣押排放噪声的场所、设施、设备、工具和物品。

**Article 30** Where noise emissions cause serious pollution and the unit or individual is ordered to make corrections but refuses to do so, the competent department of ecological environment or other department responsible for noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management may seal up or seize the places, facilities, equipment, tools, and articles that emit noise.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人都有权向生态环境主管部门或者其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门举报造成噪声污染的行为。  
生态环境主管部门和其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门应当公布举报电话、电子邮箱等，方便公众举报。  
接到举报的部门应当及时处理并对举报人的相关信息保密。举报事项属于其他部门职责的，接到举报的部门应当及时移送相关部门并告知举报人。举报人要求答复并提供有效联系方式的，处理举报事项的部门应当反馈处理结果等情况。

**Article 31** All units and individuals shall have the right to report to the competent department of ecological environment or other departments responsible for noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management any acts that cause noise pollution.  
The competent department of ecological environment and other departments responsible for noise pollution prevention and control supervision and management shall publish reporting telephone numbers, email addresses, etc., to facilitate public reporting.  
The department that receives a report shall handle it promptly and keep confidential the relevant information of the reporter. If the reported matter falls within the functions of another department, the department that received the report shall promptly transfer it to the relevant department and inform the reporter thereof. If the reporter requests a reply and provides valid contact information, the department handling the reported matter shall provide feedback on the handling results and other information.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励开展宁静小区、静音车厢等宁静区域创建活动，共同维护生活环境和谐安宁。

**Article 32** The State encourages the creation of quiet areas such as quiet residential communities and quiet carriage compartments, to jointly maintain the harmony and tranquility of the living environment.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在举行中等学校招生考试、高等学校招生统一考试等特殊活动期间，地方人民政府或者其指定的部门可以对可能产生噪声影响的活动，作出时间和区域的限制性规定，并提前向社会公告。

**Article 33** During special activities such as the secondary school entrance examination and the unified higher education entrance examination, local people's governments or the departments designated by them may impose restrictive provisions on the time and area for activities that may cause noise impact, and shall announce such provisions to the society in advance.

### 第四章　工业噪声污染防治

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　本法所称工业噪声，是指在工业生产活动中产生的干扰周围生活环境的声音。

**Article 34** For the purposes of this Law, 'industrial noise' means sounds generated in industrial production activities that interfere with the surrounding living environment.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　工业企业选址应当符合国土空间规划以及相关规划要求，县级以上地方人民政府应当按照规划要求优化工业企业布局，防止工业噪声污染。  
在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域，禁止新建排放噪声的工业企业，改建、扩建工业企业的，应当采取有效措施防止工业噪声污染。

**Article 35** The site selection of industrial enterprises shall meet the requirements of territorial space planning and relevant plans. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall optimize the layout of industrial enterprises in accordance with planning requirements to prevent industrial noise pollution.  
In concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, the construction of new industrial enterprises that emit noise shall be prohibited. If an industrial enterprise is renovated or expanded, effective measures shall be taken to prevent industrial noise pollution.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　排放工业噪声的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者，应当采取有效措施，减少振动、降低噪声，依法取得排污许可证或者填报排污登记表。  
实行排污许可管理的单位，不得无排污许可证排放工业噪声，并应当按照排污许可证的要求进行噪声污染防治。

**Article 36** Enterprises, institutions, and other business operators that emit industrial noise shall take effective measures to reduce vibration and lower noise, and shall obtain a pollutant discharge permit or fill in a pollutant discharge registration form according to law.  
Units that implement pollutant discharge permit management shall not discharge industrial noise without a pollutant discharge permit, and shall carry out noise pollution prevention and control in accordance with the requirements of the pollutant discharge permit.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定，根据噪声排放、声环境质量改善要求等情况，制定本行政区域噪声重点排污单位名录，向社会公开并适时更新。

**Article 37** The competent departments of ecological environment of local people's governments at or above the city divided into districts level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council, and based on factors such as noise emissions and requirements for acoustic environment quality improvement, formulate a list of key noise pollutant-discharging units in their respective administrative regions, make it public to the society, and update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　实行排污许可管理的单位应当按照规定，对工业噪声开展自行监测，保存原始监测记录，向社会公开监测结果，对监测数据的真实性和准确性负责。  
噪声重点排污单位应当按照国家规定，安装、使用、维护噪声自动监测设备，与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网。

**Article 38** Units that implement pollutant discharge permit management shall, as prescribed, conduct self-monitoring of industrial noise, keep original monitoring records, make monitoring results public to the society, and be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data.  
Key noise pollutant-discharging units shall, in accordance with state regulations, install, use, and maintain noise automatic monitoring equipment, and connect it to the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecological environment.

### 第五章　建筑施工噪声污染防治

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本法所称建筑施工噪声，是指在建筑施工过程中产生的干扰周围生活环境的声音。

**Article 39** For the purposes of this Law, 'construction noise' means sounds generated during the construction process that interfere with the surrounding living environment.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　建设单位应当按照规定将噪声污染防治费用列入工程造价，在施工合同中明确施工单位的噪声污染防治责任。  
施工单位应当按照规定制定噪声污染防治实施方案，采取有效措施，减少振动、降低噪声。建设单位应当监督施工单位落实噪声污染防治实施方案。

**Article 40** The construction unit shall, as prescribed, include the expenses for noise pollution prevention and control in the project cost, and specify the noise pollution prevention and control responsibilities of the construction contractor in the construction contract.  
The construction contractor shall, as prescribed, formulate an implementation plan for noise pollution prevention and control, and take effective measures to reduce vibration and lower noise. The construction unit shall supervise the construction contractor in implementing the implementation plan for noise pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域施工作业，应当优先使用低噪声施工工艺和设备。  
国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院生态环境、住房和城乡建设、市场监督管理等部门，公布低噪声施工设备指导名录并适时更新。

**Article 41** When conducting construction operations in concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, low-noise construction processes and equipment shall be given priority use.  
The competent department of industry and information technology of the State Council shall, together with the departments of ecological environment, housing and urban-rural development, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments of the State Council, publish a guidance list of low-noise construction equipment and update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域施工作业，建设单位应当按照国家规定，设置噪声自动监测系统，与监督管理部门联网，保存原始监测记录，对监测数据的真实性和准确性负责。

**Article 42** When conducting construction operations in concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, the construction unit shall, in accordance with state regulations, set up a noise automatic monitoring system, connect it to the supervision and management department, keep original monitoring records, and be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域，禁止夜间进行产生噪声的建筑施工作业，但抢修、抢险施工作业，因生产工艺要求或者其他特殊需要必须连续施工作业的除外。  
因特殊需要必须连续施工作业的，应当取得地方人民政府住房和城乡建设、生态环境主管部门或者地方人民政府指定的部门的证明，并在施工现场显著位置公示或者以其他方式公告附近居民。

**Article 43** In concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, construction operations that generate noise during nighttime shall be prohibited, except for emergency repair and rescue construction operations, and operations that must be carried out continuously due to production process requirements or other special needs.  
If continuous construction operations are necessary due to special needs, a certificate shall be obtained from the department of housing and urban-rural development, the competent department of ecological environment, or the department designated by the local people's government, and shall be displayed at a conspicuous location on the construction site or announced to nearby residents by other means.

### 第六章　交通运输噪声污染防治

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　本法所称交通运输噪声，是指机动车、铁路机车车辆、城市轨道交通车辆、机动船舶、航空器等交通运输工具在运行时产生的干扰周围生活环境的声音。

**Article 44** For the purposes of this Law, 'transportation noise' means sounds generated during the operation of transportation vehicles such as motor vehicles, railway locomotives and rolling stock, urban rail transit vehicles, motor vessels, and aircraft that interfere with the surrounding living environment.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　各级人民政府及其有关部门制定、修改国土空间规划和交通运输等相关规划，应当综合考虑公路、城市道路、铁路、城市轨道交通线路、水路、港口和民用机场及其起降航线对周围声环境的影响。  
新建公路、铁路线路选线设计，应当尽量避开噪声敏感建筑物集中区域。  
新建民用机场选址与噪声敏感建筑物集中区域的距离应当符合标准要求。

**Article 45** When formulating and modifying territorial space plans and relevant plans such as transportation plans, people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall comprehensively consider the impact of highways, urban roads, railways, urban rail transit lines, waterways, ports, and civil airports and their takeoff and landing flight paths on the surrounding acoustic environment.  
The route selection and design of newly built highways and railway lines shall try to avoid concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings.  
The distance between the site selection of a newly built civil airport and concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings shall meet the standard requirements.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　制定交通基础设施工程技术规范，应当明确噪声污染防治要求。  
新建、改建、扩建经过噪声敏感建筑物集中区域的高速公路、城市高架、铁路和城市轨道交通线路等的，建设单位应当在可能造成噪声污染的重点路段设置声屏障或者采取其他减少振动、降低噪声的措施，符合有关交通基础设施工程技术规范以及标准要求。  
建设单位违反前款规定的，由县级以上人民政府指定的部门责令制定、实施治理方案。

**Article 46** When formulating technical specifications for transportation infrastructure engineering, noise pollution prevention and control requirements shall be specified.  
When newly building, renovating, or expanding highways, urban viaducts, railways, and urban rail transit lines that pass through concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, the construction unit shall set up sound barriers or take other measures to reduce vibration and lower noise on the key sections that may cause noise pollution, and shall meet the requirements of relevant technical specifications and standards for transportation infrastructure engineering.  
If the construction unit violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the department designated by the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to formulate and implement a remediation plan.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　机动车的消声器和喇叭应当符合国家规定。禁止驾驶拆除或者损坏消声器、加装排气管等擅自改装的机动车以轰鸣、疾驶等方式造成噪声污染。  
使用机动车音响器材，应当控制音量，防止噪声污染。  
机动车应当加强维修和保养，保持性能良好，防止噪声污染。

**Article 47** The mufflers and horns of motor vehicles shall comply with state regulations. It shall be prohibited to drive a motor vehicle that has been modified without authorization, such as by removing or damaging the muffler or adding an exhaust pipe, in a manner that causes noise pollution through roaring, speeding, or other means.  
When using audio equipment on motor vehicles, the volume shall be controlled to prevent noise pollution.  
Motor vehicles shall be subject to enhanced repair and maintenance to keep good performance and prevent noise pollution.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　机动车、铁路机车车辆、城市轨道交通车辆、机动船舶等交通运输工具运行时，应当按照规定使用喇叭等声响装置。  
警车、消防救援车、工程救险车、救护车等机动车安装、使用警报器，应当符合国务院公安等部门的规定；非执行紧急任务，不得使用警报器。

**Article 48** When motor vehicles, railway locomotives and rolling stock, urban rail transit vehicles, motor vessels, and other transportation vehicles are in operation, they shall use horns and other sound devices as prescribed.  
The installation and use of sirens on motor vehicles such as police cars, fire rescue vehicles, engineering rescue vehicles, and ambulances shall comply with the regulations of the public security department of the State Council; they shall not use sirens when not performing emergency tasks.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　地方人民政府生态环境主管部门会同公安机关根据声环境保护的需要，可以划定禁止机动车行驶和使用喇叭等声响装置的路段和时间，向社会公告，并由公安机关交通管理部门依法设置相关标志、标线。

**Article 49** The competent departments of ecological environment of local people's governments, together with the public security organs, may, based on the needs of acoustic environment protection, designate road sections and time periods where motor vehicles are prohibited from driving and using horns and other sound devices, announce them to the society, and the traffic management departments of the public security organs shall set up relevant signs and markings according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在车站、铁路站场、港口等地指挥作业时使用广播喇叭的，应当控制音量，减轻噪声污染。

**Article 50** When using loudspeakers for directing operations at stations, railway yards, ports, and other places, the volume shall be controlled to mitigate noise pollution.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　公路养护管理单位、城市道路养护维修单位应当加强对公路、城市道路的维护和保养，保持减少振动、降低噪声设施正常运行。  
城市轨道交通运营单位、铁路运输企业应当加强对城市轨道交通线路和城市轨道交通车辆、铁路线路和铁路机车车辆的维护和保养，保持减少振动、降低噪声设施正常运行，并按照国家规定进行监测，保存原始监测记录，对监测数据的真实性和准确性负责。

**Article 51** The highway maintenance management unit and the urban road maintenance and repair unit shall strengthen the maintenance and upkeep of highways and urban roads, and ensure the normal operation of facilities for vibration reduction and noise reduction.  
The urban rail transit operation unit and the railway transport enterprise shall strengthen the maintenance and upkeep of urban rail transit lines and urban rail transit vehicles, railway lines and railway locomotives and vehicles, ensure the normal operation of facilities for vibration reduction and noise reduction, conduct monitoring in accordance with national provisions, preserve original monitoring records, and be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　民用机场所在地人民政府，应当根据环境影响评价以及监测结果确定的民用航空器噪声对机场周围生活环境产生影响的范围和程度，划定噪声敏感建筑物禁止建设区域和限制建设区域，并实施控制。  
在禁止建设区域禁止新建与航空无关的噪声敏感建筑物。  
在限制建设区域确需建设噪声敏感建筑物的，建设单位应当对噪声敏感建筑物进行建筑隔声设计，符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求。

**Article 52** The people's government of the place where the civil airport is located shall, based on the scope and degree of impact of civil aircraft noise on the living environment around the airport as determined by the environmental impact assessment and monitoring results, designate areas where the construction of noise-sensitive buildings is prohibited and areas where construction is restricted, and implement control measures.  
In the prohibited construction area, the construction of noise-sensitive buildings unrelated to aviation is prohibited.  
If the construction of a noise-sensitive building is truly necessary in the restricted construction area, the construction entity shall perform architectural sound insulation design for the noise-sensitive building, which shall comply with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　民用航空器应当符合国务院民用航空主管部门规定的适航标准中的有关噪声要求。

**Article 53** Civil aircraft shall comply with the relevant noise requirements in the airworthiness standards prescribed by the competent civil aviation department of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　民用机场管理机构负责机场起降航空器噪声的管理，会同航空运输企业、通用航空企业、空中交通管理部门等单位，采取低噪声飞行程序、起降跑道优化、运行架次和时段控制、高噪声航空器运行限制或者周围噪声敏感建筑物隔声降噪等措施，防止、减轻民用航空器噪声污染。  
民用机场管理机构应当按照国家规定，对机场周围民用航空器噪声进行监测，保存原始监测记录，对监测数据的真实性和准确性负责，监测结果定期向民用航空、生态环境主管部门报送。

**Article 54** The civil airport management agency shall be responsible for the management of noise from aircraft taking off and landing at the airport, and in conjunction with air transport enterprises, general aviation enterprises, air traffic control departments and other entities, shall adopt measures such as low-noise flight procedures, optimization of takeoff and landing runways, control of flight sorties and time periods, restrictions on the operation of high-noise aircraft, or sound insulation and noise reduction for surrounding noise-sensitive buildings, to prevent and mitigate civil aircraft noise pollution.  
The civil airport management agency shall, in accordance with national provisions, monitor civil aircraft noise around the airport, preserve original monitoring records, be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data, and periodically submit the monitoring results to the competent departments of civil aviation and ecology and environment.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　因公路、城市道路和城市轨道交通运行排放噪声造成严重污染的，设区的市、县级人民政府应当组织有关部门和其他有关单位对噪声污染情况进行调查评估和责任认定，制定噪声污染综合治理方案。  
噪声污染责任单位应当按照噪声污染综合治理方案的要求采取管理或者工程措施，减轻噪声污染。

**Article 55** If the noise emitted by the operation of highways, urban roads and urban rail transit causes severe pollution, the people's government of a city with districts or at the county level shall organize the relevant departments and other relevant entities to investigate and assess the noise pollution situation and determine the responsibility, and formulate a comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan.  
The unit responsible for noise pollution shall, according to the requirements of the comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan, take management or engineering measures to mitigate the noise pollution.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　因铁路运行排放噪声造成严重污染的，铁路运输企业和设区的市、县级人民政府应当对噪声污染情况进行调查，制定噪声污染综合治理方案。  
铁路运输企业和设区的市、县级人民政府有关部门和其他有关单位应当按照噪声污染综合治理方案的要求采取有效措施，减轻噪声污染。

**Article 56** If the noise emitted by railway operations causes severe pollution, the railway transport enterprise and the people's government of a city with districts or at the county level shall investigate the noise pollution situation and formulate a comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan.  
The railway transport enterprise, the relevant departments of the people's government of a city with districts or at the county level, and other relevant entities shall, according to the requirements of the comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan, take effective measures to mitigate the noise pollution.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　因民用航空器起降排放噪声造成严重污染的，民用机场所在地人民政府应当组织有关部门和其他有关单位对噪声污染情况进行调查，综合考虑经济、技术和管理措施，制定噪声污染综合治理方案。  
民用机场管理机构、地方各级人民政府和其他有关单位应当按照噪声污染综合治理方案的要求采取有效措施，减轻噪声污染。

**Article 57** If the noise emitted by civil aircraft taking off and landing causes severe pollution, the people's government of the place where the civil airport is located shall organize the relevant departments and other relevant entities to investigate the noise pollution situation, comprehensively consider economic, technical and management measures, and formulate a comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan.  
The civil airport management agency, the local people's governments at all levels and other relevant entities shall, according to the requirements of the comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan, take effective measures to mitigate the noise pollution.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　制定噪声污染综合治理方案，应当征求有关专家和公众等的意见。

**Article 58** In formulating a comprehensive noise pollution treatment plan, the opinions of relevant experts and the public shall be solicited.

### 第七章　社会生活噪声污染防治

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　本法所称社会生活噪声，是指人为活动产生的除工业噪声、建筑施工噪声和交通运输噪声之外的干扰周围生活环境的声音。

**Article 59** For the purposes of this Law, social life noise means sounds generated by human activities, other than industrial noise, construction noise and transportation noise, that interfere with the surrounding living environment.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　全社会应当增强噪声污染防治意识，自觉减少社会生活噪声排放，积极开展噪声污染防治活动，形成人人有责、人人参与、人人受益的良好噪声污染防治氛围，共同维护生活环境和谐安宁。

**Article 60** The whole society shall enhance the awareness of noise pollution prevention and control, consciously reduce the emission of social life noise, actively carry out noise pollution prevention and control activities, form a good atmosphere for noise pollution prevention and control in which everyone has a responsibility, everyone participates, and everyone benefits, and jointly maintain a harmonious and peaceful living environment.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　文化娱乐、体育、餐饮等场所的经营管理者应当采取有效措施，防止、减轻噪声污染。

**Article 61** The operators and managers of cultural entertainment, sports, catering and other places shall take effective measures to prevent and mitigate noise pollution.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　使用空调器、冷却塔、水泵、油烟净化器、风机、发电机、变压器、锅炉、装卸设备等可能产生社会生活噪声污染的设备、设施的企业事业单位和其他经营管理者等，应当采取优化布局、集中排放等措施，防止、减轻噪声污染。

**Article 62** Enterprises, institutions and other operators and managers that use equipment and facilities such as air conditioners, cooling towers, water pumps, oil fume purifiers, fans, generators, transformers, boilers, loading and unloading equipment that may generate social life noise pollution shall take measures such as optimizing layout and centralized discharge to prevent and mitigate noise pollution.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　禁止在商业经营活动中使用高音广播喇叭或者采用其他持续反复发出高噪声的方法进行广告宣传。  
对商业经营活动中产生的其他噪声，经营者应当采取有效措施，防止噪声污染。

**Article 63** The use of high-pitched loudspeakers or other methods that continuously and repeatedly emit high noise for advertising in commercial business activities is prohibited.  
For other noise generated in commercial business activities, the operator shall take effective measures to prevent noise pollution.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　禁止在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域使用高音广播喇叭，但紧急情况以及地方人民政府规定的特殊情形除外。  
在街道、广场、公园等公共场所组织或者开展娱乐、健身等活动，应当遵守公共场所管理者有关活动区域、时段、音量等规定，采取有效措施，防止噪声污染；不得违反规定使用音响器材产生过大音量。  
公共场所管理者应当合理规定娱乐、健身等活动的区域、时段、音量，可以采取设置噪声自动监测和显示设施等措施加强管理。

**Article 64** The use of high-pitched loudspeakers in concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings is prohibited, except in emergencies and special circumstances prescribed by the local people's government.  
When organizing or conducting entertainment, fitness and other activities in public places such as streets, squares and parks, the provisions of the public place manager on activity areas, time periods, volume, etc. shall be observed, and effective measures shall be taken to prevent noise pollution; the use of audio equipment in violation of provisions to generate excessive volume shall not be allowed.  
The manager of a public place shall reasonably regulate the areas, time periods and volume for entertainment, fitness and other activities, and may take measures such as installing automatic noise monitoring and display facilities to strengthen management.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　家庭及其成员应当培养形成减少噪声产生的良好习惯，乘坐公共交通工具、饲养宠物和其他日常活动尽量避免产生噪声对周围人员造成干扰，互谅互让解决噪声纠纷，共同维护声环境质量。  
使用家用电器、乐器或者进行其他家庭场所活动，应当控制音量或者采取其他有效措施，防止噪声污染。

**Article 65** Families and their members shall cultivate good habits of reducing noise generation, and in taking public transport, keeping pets and other daily activities, shall try to avoid causing interference to surrounding persons by noise, resolve noise disputes through mutual understanding and mutual accommodation, and jointly maintain the quality of the acoustic environment.  
When using household appliances, musical instruments or conducting other activities in family premises, the volume shall be controlled or other effective measures shall be taken to prevent noise pollution.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对已竣工交付使用的住宅楼、商铺、办公楼等建筑物进行室内装修活动，应当按照规定限定作业时间，采取有效措施，防止、减轻噪声污染。

**Article 66** Indoor decoration activities on buildings such as residential buildings, shops and office buildings that have been completed and delivered for use shall be conducted within the limited working hours as prescribed, and effective measures shall be taken to prevent and mitigate noise pollution.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　新建居民住房的房地产开发经营者应当在销售场所公示住房可能受到噪声影响的情况以及采取或者拟采取的防治措施，并纳入买卖合同。  
新建居民住房的房地产开发经营者应当在买卖合同中明确住房的共用设施设备位置和建筑隔声情况。

**Article 67** The real estate development operator of newly built residential housing shall, at the sales location, disclose the circumstances under which the housing may be affected by noise and the prevention and control measures that have been taken or are proposed to be taken, and include them in the sales contract.  
The real estate development operator of newly built residential housing shall specify in the sales contract the locations of the shared facilities and equipment of the housing and the sound insulation conditions of the building.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　居民住宅区安装电梯、水泵、变压器等共用设施设备的，建设单位应当合理设置，采取减少振动、降低噪声的措施，符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求。  
已建成使用的居民住宅区电梯、水泵、变压器等共用设施设备由专业运营单位负责维护管理，符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求。

**Article 68** Where shared facilities and equipment such as elevators, water pumps and transformers are installed in residential areas, the construction entity shall install them reasonably and take measures to reduce vibration and noise, complying with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design.  
The shared facilities and equipment such as elevators, water pumps and transformers in residential areas that have been constructed and are in use shall be maintained and managed by professional operation units, complying with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　基层群众性自治组织指导业主委员会、物业服务人、业主通过制定管理规约或者其他形式，约定本物业管理区域噪声污染防治要求，由业主共同遵守。

**Article 69** The grassroots self-governing organization shall guide the owners' committee, the property service provider and the owners to stipulate the requirements for noise pollution prevention and control in the property management area through the formulation of management regulations or other forms, which shall be jointly observed by the owners.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　对噪声敏感建筑物集中区域的社会生活噪声扰民行为，基层群众性自治组织、业主委员会、物业服务人应当及时劝阻、调解；劝阻、调解无效的，可以向负有社会生活噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门或者地方人民政府指定的部门报告或者投诉，接到报告或者投诉的部门应当依法处理。

**Article 70** For acts of social life noise that disturb residents in concentrated areas of noise-sensitive buildings, the grassroots self-governing organization, the owners' committee and the property service provider shall promptly dissuade and mediate; if dissuasion and mediation are ineffective, they may report or complain to the department responsible for supervision and management of social life noise pollution prevention and control or the department designated by the local people's government, and the department that receives the report or complaint shall handle it according to law.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，拒绝、阻挠监督检查，或者在接受监督检查时弄虚作假的，由生态环境主管部门或者其他负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection, or practises fraud during supervision and inspection, shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent department of ecology and environment or other department with the responsibility for supervision and management of noise pollution prevention and control, and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本法规定，生产、进口、销售超过噪声限值的产品的，由县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门、海关按照职责责令改正，没收违法所得，并处货值金额一倍以上三倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭。  
违反本法规定，生产、进口、销售、使用淘汰的设备，或者采用淘汰的工艺的，由县级以上人民政府指定的部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处货值金额一倍以上三倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭。

**Article 72** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, produces, imports or sells products that exceed the noise limit values shall be ordered to make corrections by the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level or the customs according to their respective functions, confiscate the illegal gains, and shall be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the goods; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the entity shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.  
Anyone who, in violation of this Law, produces, imports, sells or uses eliminated equipment, or adopts eliminated techniques, shall be ordered to make corrections by the department designated by the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the illegal gains, and shall be fined not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the goods; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and the entity shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，建设单位建设噪声敏感建筑物不符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求的，由县级以上地方人民政府住房和城乡建设主管部门责令改正，处建设工程合同价款百分之二以上百分之四以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，建设单位在噪声敏感建筑物禁止建设区域新建与航空无关的噪声敏感建筑物的，由地方人民政府指定的部门责令停止违法行为，处建设工程合同价款百分之二以上百分之十以下的罚款，并报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令拆除。

**Article 73** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, constructs a noise-sensitive building that does not comply with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent department of housing and urban-rural development of the local people's government at or above the county level, and shall be fined not less than two percent but not more than four percent of the contract price of the construction project.  
Anyone who, in violation of this Law, constructs a new noise-sensitive building unrelated to aviation in an area where the construction of noise-sensitive buildings is prohibited shall be ordered to cease the illegal act by the department designated by the local people's government, fined not less than two percent but not more than ten percent of the contract price of the construction project, and, upon reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, be ordered to demolish the building.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　违反本法规定，在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域新建排放噪声的工业企业的，由生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款，并报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。  
违反本法规定，在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域改建、扩建工业企业，未采取有效措施防止工业噪声污染的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。

**Article 74** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, establishes a new industrial enterprise that emits noise in a concentrated area of noise-sensitive buildings shall be ordered to cease the illegal act by the competent department of ecology and environment, fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan, and, upon reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, be ordered to close down.  
Anyone who, in violation of this Law, renovates or expands an industrial enterprise in a concentrated area of noise-sensitive buildings without taking effective measures to prevent industrial noise pollution shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent department of ecology and environment and fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if correction is refused, upon reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, the enterprise shall be ordered to close down.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，无排污许可证或者超过噪声排放标准排放工业噪声的，由生态环境主管部门责令改正或者限制生产、停产整治，并处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业、关闭。

**Article 75** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, emits industrial noise without a pollutant discharge permit or in excess of the noise emission standards shall be ordered to make corrections, or be subject to production restrictions or suspension of production for remediation by the competent department of ecology and environment, and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, upon reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, the entity shall be ordered to suspend business or close down.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由生态环境主管部门责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令限制生产、停产整治：  
（一）实行排污许可管理的单位未按照规定对工业噪声开展自行监测，未保存原始监测记录，或者未向社会公开监测结果的；  
（二）噪声重点排污单位未按照国家规定安装、使用、维护噪声自动监测设备，或者未与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网的。

**Article 76** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent department of ecology and environment and fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity shall be ordered to restrict production or suspend production for remediation:  
(1) A unit subject to pollutant discharge permit management fails to conduct self-monitoring of industrial noise in accordance with the provisions, fails to preserve original monitoring records, or fails to disclose the monitoring results to the public;  
(2) A key noise-discharging unit fails to install, use or maintain automatic noise monitoring equipment in accordance with national provisions, or fails to connect with the monitoring equipment of the competent department of ecology and environment.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　违反本法规定，建设单位、施工单位有下列行为之一，由工程所在地人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，可以责令暂停施工：  
（一）超过噪声排放标准排放建筑施工噪声的；  
（二）未按照规定取得证明，在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域夜间进行产生噪声的建筑施工作业的。

**Article 77** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, as a construction entity or construction unit, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the department designated by the people's government where the project is located, and fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity may be ordered to suspend construction:  
(1) Emitting construction noise in excess of the noise emission standards;  
(2) Conducting construction operations that generate noise at night in a concentrated area of noise-sensitive buildings without obtaining a certificate as prescribed.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由工程所在地人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）建设单位未按照规定将噪声污染防治费用列入工程造价的；  
（二）施工单位未按照规定制定噪声污染防治实施方案，或者未采取有效措施减少振动、降低噪声的；  
（三）在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域施工作业的建设单位未按照国家规定设置噪声自动监测系统，未与监督管理部门联网，或者未保存原始监测记录的；  
（四）因特殊需要必须连续施工作业，建设单位未按照规定公告附近居民的。

**Article 78** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the department designated by the people's government where the project is located, and fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) The construction entity fails to include the costs of noise pollution prevention and control in the project cost as prescribed;  
(2) The construction unit fails to formulate a noise pollution prevention and control implementation plan as prescribed, or fails to take effective measures to reduce vibration and noise;  
(3) The construction entity conducting construction operations in a concentrated area of noise-sensitive buildings fails to install an automatic noise monitoring system in accordance with national provisions, fails to connect with the supervision and management department, or fails to preserve original monitoring records;  
(4) When continuous construction operations are necessary due to special needs, the construction entity fails to notify nearby residents as prescribed.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，驾驶拆除或者损坏消声器、加装排气管等擅自改装的机动车轰鸣、疾驶，机动车运行时未按照规定使用声响装置，或者违反禁止机动车行驶和使用声响装置的路段和时间规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府公安机关交通管理部门依照有关道路交通安全的法律法规处罚。  
违反本法规定，铁路机车车辆、城市轨道交通车辆、机动船舶等交通运输工具运行时未按照规定使用声响装置的，由交通运输、铁路监督管理、海事等部门或者地方人民政府指定的城市轨道交通有关部门按照职责责令改正，处五千元以上一万元以下的罚款。

**Article 79** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, drives a motor vehicle that has been illegally modified by removing or damaging the muffler, adding an exhaust pipe, etc., causing it to roar or speed, fails to use sound devices as prescribed when the motor vehicle is running, or violates the provisions on road sections and time periods prohibiting the driving of motor vehicles and the use of sound devices, shall be penalized by the traffic management department of the public security organ of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the laws and regulations on road traffic safety.  
Anyone who, in violation of this Law, fails to use sound devices as prescribed when operating railway locomotives and vehicles, urban rail transit vehicles, motor vessels and other means of transportation, shall be ordered to make corrections by the departments of transport, railway supervision and administration, maritime affairs, or the relevant department of urban rail transit designated by the local people's government according to their respective functions, and shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由交通运输、铁路监督管理、民用航空等部门或者地方人民政府指定的城市道路、城市轨道交通有关部门，按照职责责令改正，处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）公路养护管理单位、城市道路养护维修单位、城市轨道交通运营单位、铁路运输企业未履行维护和保养义务，未保持减少振动、降低噪声设施正常运行的；  
（二）城市轨道交通运营单位、铁路运输企业未按照国家规定进行监测，或者未保存原始监测记录的；  
（三）民用机场管理机构、航空运输企业、通用航空企业未采取措施防止、减轻民用航空器噪声污染的；  
（四）民用机场管理机构未按照国家规定对机场周围民用航空器噪声进行监测，未保存原始监测记录，或者监测结果未定期报送的。

**Article 80** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the departments of transport, railway supervision and administration, civil aviation, or the relevant departments of urban roads or urban rail transit designated by the local people's government according to their respective functions, and shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) The highway maintenance management unit, urban road maintenance and repair unit, urban rail transit operation unit, or railway transport enterprise fails to perform maintenance and upkeep obligations, or fails to ensure the normal operation of facilities for vibration reduction and noise reduction;  
(2) The urban rail transit operation unit or railway transport enterprise fails to conduct monitoring in accordance with national provisions, or fails to preserve original monitoring records;  
(3) The civil airport management agency, air transport enterprise, or general aviation enterprise fails to take measures to prevent and mitigate civil aircraft noise pollution;  
(4) The civil airport management agency fails to monitor civil aircraft noise around the airport in accordance with national provisions, fails to preserve original monitoring records, or fails to periodically submit monitoring results.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由地方人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款，并可以报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令停业：  
（一）超过噪声排放标准排放社会生活噪声的；  
（二）在商业经营活动中使用高音广播喇叭或者采用其他持续反复发出高噪声的方法进行广告宣传的；  
（三）未对商业经营活动中产生的其他噪声采取有效措施造成噪声污染的。

**Article 81** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the department designated by the local people's government, and fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan, and may, upon reporting to and obtaining approval from the people's government with approval authority, be ordered to suspend business:  
(1) Emitting social life noise in excess of the noise emission standards;  
(2) Using high-pitched loudspeakers or other methods that continuously and repeatedly emit high noise for advertising in commercial business activities;  
(3) Failing to take effective measures against other noise generated in commercial business activities, resulting in noise pollution.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由地方人民政府指定的部门说服教育，责令改正；拒不改正的，给予警告，对个人可以处二百元以上一千元以下的罚款，对单位可以处二千元以上二万元以下的罚款：  
（一）在噪声敏感建筑物集中区域使用高音广播喇叭的；  
（二）在公共场所组织或者开展娱乐、健身等活动，未遵守公共场所管理者有关活动区域、时段、音量等规定，未采取有效措施造成噪声污染，或者违反规定使用音响器材产生过大音量的；  
（三）对已竣工交付使用的建筑物进行室内装修活动，未按照规定在限定的作业时间内进行，或者未采取有效措施造成噪声污染的；  
（四）其他违反法律规定造成社会生活噪声污染的。

**Article 82** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be persuaded and educated by the department designated by the local people's government and ordered to make corrections; if correction is refused, a warning shall be given, and the individual may be fined not less than 200 yuan but not more than 1,000 yuan, and the entity may be fined not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) Using high-pitched loudspeakers in a concentrated area of noise-sensitive buildings;  
(2) Organizing or conducting entertainment, fitness and other activities in public places, failing to comply with the provisions of the public place manager on activity areas, time periods, volume, etc., failing to take effective measures resulting in noise pollution, or using audio equipment in violation of provisions to generate excessive volume;  
(3) Conducting indoor decoration activities on buildings that have been completed and delivered for use, failing to carry them out within the limited working hours as prescribed, or failing to take effective measures resulting in noise pollution;  
(4) Other acts that violate legal provisions and cause social life noise pollution.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由县级以上地方人民政府房产管理部门责令改正，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，责令暂停销售：  
（一）新建居民住房的房地产开发经营者未在销售场所公示住房可能受到噪声影响的情况以及采取或者拟采取的防治措施，或者未纳入买卖合同的；  
（二）新建居民住房的房地产开发经营者未在买卖合同中明确住房的共用设施设备位置或者建筑隔声情况的。

**Article 83** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the real estate management department of the local people's government at or above the county level, and fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if correction is refused, sales shall be suspended:  
(1) The real estate development operator of newly built residential housing fails to disclose at the sales location the circumstances under which the housing may be affected by noise and the prevention and control measures that have been taken or are proposed to be taken, or fails to include them in the sales contract;  
(2) The real estate development operator of newly built residential housing fails to specify in the sales contract the locations of the shared facilities and equipment of the housing or the sound insulation conditions of the building.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一，由地方人民政府指定的部门责令改正，处五千元以上五万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处五万元以上二十万元以下的罚款：  
（一）居民住宅区安装共用设施设备，设置不合理或者未采取减少振动、降低噪声的措施，不符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求的；  
（二）对已建成使用的居民住宅区共用设施设备，专业运营单位未进行维护管理，不符合民用建筑隔声设计相关标准要求的。

**Article 84** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered to make corrections by the department designated by the local people's government, and fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if correction is refused, the entity shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan:  
(1) The installation of shared facilities and equipment in a residential area is unreasonable or fails to take measures to reduce vibration and noise, not complying with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design;  
(2) For shared facilities and equipment in a residential area that has been constructed and is in use, the professional operation unit fails to perform maintenance and management, and they do not comply with the requirements of the relevant standards for civil building sound insulation design.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　噪声污染防治监督管理人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，由监察机关或者任免机关、单位依法给予处分。

**Article 85** Where a supervisor or manager of noise pollution prevention and control abuses his power, neglects his duty or engages in malpractice for personal gain, he shall be given a sanction by the supervisory organ or the appointing and dismissing organ or unit according to law.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　受到噪声侵害的单位和个人，有权要求侵权人依法承担民事责任。  
对赔偿责任和赔偿金额纠纷，可以根据当事人的请求，由相应的负有噪声污染防治监督管理职责的部门、人民调解委员会调解处理。  
国家鼓励排放噪声的单位、个人和公共场所管理者与受到噪声侵害的单位和个人友好协商，通过调整生产经营时间、施工作业时间，采取减少振动、降低噪声措施，支付补偿金、异地安置等方式，妥善解决噪声纠纷。

**Article 86** Any unit or individual that suffers from noise infringement shall have the right to demand that the tortfeasor bear civil liability according to law.  
For disputes over liability for compensation and the amount of compensation, the corresponding department with the responsibility for supervision and management of noise pollution prevention and control or the people's mediation committee may conduct mediation upon request of the parties.  
The State encourages units and individuals that emit noise and managers of public places to engage in friendly consultation with the units and individuals that suffer from noise infringement, to properly resolve noise disputes by adjusting production and operation time or construction operation time, taking measures to reduce vibration and noise, paying compensation, providing resettlement in other places, or other means.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，产生社会生活噪声，经劝阻、调解和处理未能制止，持续干扰他人正常生活、工作和学习，或者有其他扰乱公共秩序、妨害社会管理等违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。  
违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 87** Anyone who, in violation of this Law, generates social life noise, and after being dissuaded, mediated and handled, fails to stop such act, continuously interferes with the normal life, work and study of others, or commits other acts of violating public security administration such as disrupting public order or obstructing social management, shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law.  
Anyone who, in violation of this Law, commits a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）噪声排放，是指噪声源向周围生活环境辐射噪声；  
（二）夜间，是指晚上十点至次日早晨六点之间的期间，设区的市级以上人民政府可以另行规定本行政区域夜间的起止时间，夜间时段长度为八小时；  
（三）噪声敏感建筑物，是指用于居住、科学研究、医疗卫生、文化教育、机关团体办公、社会福利等需要保持安静的建筑物；  
（四）交通干线，是指铁路、高速公路、一级公路、二级公路、城市快速路、城市主干路、城市次干路、城市轨道交通线路、内河高等级航道。

**Article 88** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings specified below:  
(1) "Noise emission" means the radiation of noise from a noise source to the surrounding living environment;  
(2) "Night time" means the period from 10:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. the next day; the people's government at or above the level of a city with districts may separately prescribe the starting and ending times of night time in its administrative region, provided that the length of the night time period shall be eight hours;  
(3) "Noise-sensitive buildings" means buildings that need to be kept quiet, such as those used for residential purposes, scientific research, medical and health care, culture and education, government office, social welfare, etc.;  
(4) "Traffic arteries" mean railways, expressways, Class I highways, Class II highways, urban expressways, urban main roads, urban secondary roads, urban rail transit lines, and inland high-grade waterways.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市、自治州根据实际情况，制定本地方噪声污染防治具体办法。

**Article 89** A province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or a city with districts or an autonomous prefecture shall, based on the actual circumstances, formulate specific measures for noise pollution prevention and control in its locality.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　本法自2022年6月5日起施行。《中华人民共和国环境噪声污染防治法》同时废止。

**Article 90** This Law shall come into force on June 5, 2022. The Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Noise Pollution shall be abolished simultaneously.
