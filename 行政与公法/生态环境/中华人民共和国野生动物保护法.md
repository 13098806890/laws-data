# 中华人民共和国野生动物保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-12-30

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护野生动物，拯救珍贵、濒危野生动物，维护生物多样性和生态平衡，推进生态文明建设，促进人与自然和谐共生，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of protecting wildlife, saving precious and endangered wildlife, maintaining biodiversity and ecological balance, advancing ecological civilization, and promoting harmonious coexistence between human and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域，从事野生动物保护及相关活动，适用本法。  
本法规定保护的野生动物，是指珍贵、濒危的陆生、水生野生动物和有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物。  
本法规定的野生动物及其制品，是指野生动物的整体（含卵、蛋）、部分及衍生物。  
珍贵、濒危的水生野生动物以外的其他水生野生动物的保护，适用《中华人民共和国渔业法》等有关法律的规定。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to wildlife protection and related activities within the territory of the People's Republic of China and under other sea areas under its jurisdiction.  
The wildlife protected under this Law refers to precious and endangered terrestrial and aquatic wildlife and terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value.  
The wildlife and their products as mentioned in this Law refer to the whole body (including eggs and spawn), parts, and derivatives of wildlife.  
The protection of aquatic wildlife other than precious and endangered aquatic wildlife shall be governed by the provisions of the Fisheries Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　野生动物资源属于国家所有。  
国家保障依法从事野生动物科学研究、人工繁育等保护及相关活动的组织和个人的合法权益。

**Article 3** Wildlife resources shall be owned by the State.  
The State shall guarantee the lawful rights and interests of organizations and individuals engaged in scientific research, artificial propagation, and other protection-related activities concerning wildlife in accordance with law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家加强重要生态系统保护和修复，对野生动物实行保护优先、规范利用、严格监管的原则，鼓励和支持开展野生动物科学研究与应用，秉持生态文明理念，推动绿色发展。

**Article 4** The State shall strengthen the protection and restoration of important ecosystems, implement the principles of prioritizing protection, regulating utilization, and strictly supervising wildlife, encourage and support scientific research and application in wildlife, uphold the concept of ecological civilization, and promote green development.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家保护野生动物及其栖息地。县级以上人民政府应当制定野生动物及其栖息地相关保护规划和措施，并将野生动物保护经费纳入预算。  
国家鼓励公民、法人和其他组织依法通过捐赠、资助、志愿服务等方式参与野生动物保护活动，支持野生动物保护公益事业。  
本法规定的野生动物栖息地，是指野生动物野外种群生息繁衍的重要区域。

**Article 5** The State shall protect wildlife and their habitats. People's governments at or above the county level shall formulate relevant protection plans and measures for wildlife and their habitats, and incorporate wildlife protection funds into their budgets.  
The State shall encourage citizens, legal persons, and other organizations to participate in wildlife protection activities by means of donation, funding, volunteer service, and other lawful means, and support public welfare undertakings for wildlife protection.  
The wildlife habitats as mentioned in this Law refer to important areas where wild populations of wildlife breed and inhabit.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　任何组织和个人有保护野生动物及其栖息地的义务。禁止违法猎捕、运输、交易野生动物，禁止破坏野生动物栖息地。  
社会公众应当增强保护野生动物和维护公共卫生安全的意识，防止野生动物源性传染病传播，抵制违法食用野生动物，养成文明健康的生活方式。  
任何组织和个人有权举报违反本法的行为，接到举报的县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和其他有关部门应当及时依法处理。

**Article 6** Any organization or individual shall have the obligation to protect wildlife and their habitats. Illegal hunting, transportation, and trading of wildlife shall be prohibited, and destruction of wildlife habitats shall be prohibited.  
The public shall enhance their awareness of protecting wildlife and maintaining public health safety, prevent the spread of wildlife-borne infectious diseases, resist illegal consumption of wildlife, and cultivate a civilized and healthy lifestyle.  
Any organization or individual shall have the right to report acts violating this Law. The competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level and other relevant departments that receive such reports shall handle them in a timely manner in accordance with law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院林业草原、渔业主管部门分别主管全国陆生、水生野生动物保护工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域内野生动物保护工作负责，其林业草原、渔业主管部门分别主管本行政区域内陆生、水生野生动物保护工作。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门按照职责分工，负责野生动物保护相关工作。

**Article 7** The competent departments of forestry, grassland, and fisheries under the State Council shall be respectively responsible for the protection of terrestrial and aquatic wildlife nationwide.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for wildlife protection within their respective administrative regions, and their competent departments of forestry, grassland, and fisheries shall be respectively responsible for the protection of terrestrial and aquatic wildlife within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for wildlife protection related work according to their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级人民政府应当加强野生动物保护的宣传教育和科学知识普及工作，鼓励和支持基层群众性自治组织、社会组织、企业事业单位、志愿者开展野生动物保护法律法规、生态保护等知识的宣传活动；组织开展对相关从业人员法律法规和专业知识培训；依法公开野生动物保护和管理信息。  
教育行政部门、学校应当对学生进行野生动物保护知识教育。  
新闻媒体应当开展野生动物保护法律法规和保护知识的宣传，并依法对违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 8** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen publicity, education, and popularization of scientific knowledge on wildlife protection, encourage and support grassroots autonomous organizations, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and volunteers to carry out publicity activities on laws, regulations, and ecological protection knowledge related to wildlife protection; organize training on laws, regulations, and professional knowledge for relevant practitioners; and disclose wildlife protection and management information in accordance with law.  
Education administrative departments and schools shall educate students on wildlife protection knowledge.  
News media shall publicize laws, regulations, and protection knowledge on wildlife protection, and exercise public opinion supervision over illegal acts in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在野生动物保护和科学研究方面成绩显著的组织和个人，由县级以上人民政府按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 9** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding achievements in wildlife protection and scientific research shall be commended and rewarded by the people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　野生动物及其栖息地保护

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家对野生动物实行分类分级保护。  
国家对珍贵、濒危的野生动物实行重点保护。国家重点保护的野生动物分为一级保护野生动物和二级保护野生动物。国家重点保护野生动物名录，由国务院野生动物保护主管部门组织科学论证评估后，报国务院批准公布。  
有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录，由国务院野生动物保护主管部门征求国务院农业农村、自然资源、科学技术、生态环境、卫生健康等部门意见，组织科学论证评估后制定并公布。  
地方重点保护野生动物，是指国家重点保护野生动物以外，由省、自治区、直辖市重点保护的野生动物。地方重点保护野生动物名录，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府组织科学论证评估，征求国务院野生动物保护主管部门意见后制定、公布。  
对本条规定的名录，应当每五年组织科学论证评估，根据论证评估情况进行调整，也可以根据野生动物保护的实际需要及时进行调整。

**Article 10** The State shall implement classified and graded protection for wildlife.  
The State shall provide prioritized protection for precious and endangered wildlife. The wildlife under prioritized protection of the State shall be divided into first-class protected wildlife and second-class protected wildlife. The list of wildlife under prioritized protection of the State shall be determined by the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council after organizing scientific demonstration and evaluation, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval and publication.  
The list of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value shall be formulated and published by the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council after soliciting opinions from the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, science and technology, ecology and environment, and health under the State Council, and organizing scientific demonstration and evaluation.  
Local prioritized protected wildlife refers to wildlife that is prioritized for protection by provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, other than those under prioritized protection of the State. The list of local prioritized protected wildlife shall be formulated and published by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government after organizing scientific demonstration and evaluation and soliciting opinions from the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council.  
Scientific demonstration and evaluation shall be conducted every five years for the lists specified in this Article, which may be adjusted based on the evaluation results, or may be adjusted in a timely manner according to the actual needs of wildlife protection.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门应当加强信息技术应用，定期组织或者委托有关科学研究机构对野生动物及其栖息地状况进行调查、监测和评估，建立健全野生动物及其栖息地档案。  
对野生动物及其栖息地状况的调查、监测和评估应当包括下列内容：  
（一）野生动物野外分布区域、种群数量及结构；  
（二）野生动物栖息地的面积、生态状况；  
（三）野生动物及其栖息地的主要威胁因素；  
（四）野生动物人工繁育情况等其他需要调查、监测和评估的内容。

**Article 11** The competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall strengthen the application of information technology, regularly organize or entrust relevant scientific research institutions to survey, monitor, and evaluate the status of wildlife and their habitats, and establish and improve archives of wildlife and their habitats.  
The survey, monitoring, and evaluation of the status of wildlife and their habitats shall include the following contents:  
(1) Wild distribution areas, population size, and structure of wildlife;  
(2) Area and ecological status of wildlife habitats;  
(3) Main threatening factors for wildlife and their habitats;  
(4) Other contents that need to be surveyed, monitored, and evaluated, such as the status of artificial propagation of wildlife.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院野生动物保护主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门，根据野生动物及其栖息地状况的调查、监测和评估结果，确定并发布野生动物重要栖息地名录。  
省级以上人民政府依法将野生动物重要栖息地划入国家公园、自然保护区等自然保护地，保护、恢复和改善野生动物生存环境。对不具备划定自然保护地条件的，县级以上人民政府可以采取划定禁猎（渔）区、规定禁猎（渔）期等措施予以保护。  
禁止或者限制在自然保护地内引入外来物种、营造单一纯林、过量施洒农药等人为干扰、威胁野生动物生息繁衍的行为。  
自然保护地依照有关法律法规的规定划定和管理，野生动物保护主管部门依法加强对野生动物及其栖息地的保护。

**Article 12** The competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, determine and publish a list of important wildlife habitats based on the results of survey, monitoring, and evaluation of the status of wildlife and their habitats.  
People's governments at or above the provincial level shall, in accordance with law, designate important wildlife habitats into natural protected areas such as national parks and nature reserves, to protect, restore, and improve the living environment of wildlife. Where conditions for designating natural protected areas do not exist, the people's government at or above the county level may adopt measures such as designating hunting or fishing prohibition areas, and specifying hunting or fishing prohibition periods for protection.  
Acts that artificially disturb or threaten the breeding and inhabitation of wildlife, such as introducing alien species, planting pure stands, or excessively applying pesticides, shall be prohibited or restricted within natural protected areas.  
Natural protected areas shall be designated and managed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, and the competent department of wildlife protection shall, in accordance with law, strengthen the protection of wildlife and their habitats.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府及其有关部门在编制有关开发利用规划时，应当充分考虑野生动物及其栖息地保护的需要，分析、预测和评估规划实施可能对野生动物及其栖息地保护产生的整体影响，避免或者减少规划实施可能造成的不利后果。  
禁止在自然保护地建设法律法规规定不得建设的项目。机场、铁路、公路、航道、水利水电、风电、光伏发电、围堰、围填海等建设项目的选址选线，应当避让自然保护地以及其他野生动物重要栖息地、迁徙洄游通道；确实无法避让的，应当采取修建野生动物通道、过鱼设施等措施，消除或者减少对野生动物的不利影响。  
建设项目可能对自然保护地以及其他野生动物重要栖息地、迁徙洄游通道产生影响的，环境影响评价文件的审批部门在审批环境影响评价文件时，涉及国家重点保护野生动物的，应当征求国务院野生动物保护主管部门意见；涉及地方重点保护野生动物的，应当征求省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门意见。

**Article 13** When formulating relevant development and utilization plans, the people's government at or above the county level and its relevant departments shall fully consider the needs for the protection of wildlife and their habitats, analyze, predict, and evaluate the overall impact of the implementation of the plans on the protection of wildlife and their habitats, and avoid or mitigate the adverse consequences that may be caused by the implementation of the plans.  
Construction of projects that are not permitted by laws and regulations shall be prohibited in natural protected areas. The site selection and route selection of construction projects such as airports, railways, highways, waterways, water conservancy and hydropower, wind power, photovoltaic power generation, cofferdams, and sea reclamation shall avoid natural protected areas and other important wildlife habitats, migration and migration channels; where it is truly impossible to avoid, measures such as building wildlife passages and fish passage facilities shall be taken to eliminate or reduce adverse effects on wildlife.  
Where a construction project may affect natural protected areas and other important wildlife habitats, migration and migration channels, the department approving the environmental impact assessment document shall, when examining and approving the environmental impact assessment document, consult the opinion of the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council if the project involves wildlife under prioritized protection of the State; and consult the opinion of the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government if the project involves local prioritized protected wildlife.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级野生动物保护主管部门应当监测环境对野生动物的影响，发现环境影响对野生动物造成危害时，应当会同有关部门及时进行调查处理。

**Article 14** The competent departments of wildlife protection at all levels shall monitor the impact of the environment on wildlife. When discovering that the environmental impact causes harm to wildlife, they shall, together with the relevant departments, promptly conduct investigation and handling.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家重点保护野生动物和有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者地方重点保护野生动物受到自然灾害、重大环境污染事故等突发事件威胁时，当地人民政府应当及时采取应急救助措施。  
国家加强野生动物收容救护能力建设。县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门应当按照国家有关规定组织开展野生动物收容救护工作，加强对社会组织开展野生动物收容救护工作的规范和指导。  
收容救护机构应当根据野生动物收容救护的实际需要，建立收容救护场所，配备相应的专业技术人员、救护工具、设备和药品等。  
禁止以野生动物收容救护为名买卖野生动物及其制品。

**Article 15** Where wildlife under prioritized protection of the State, terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value, or local prioritized protected wildlife is threatened by natural disasters, major environmental pollution accidents, or other emergencies, the local people's government shall promptly take emergency rescue measures.  
The State shall strengthen the capacity building for wildlife shelter and rescue. The competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, organize and carry out wildlife shelter and rescue work, and strengthen the regulation and guidance of social organizations in carrying out wildlife shelter and rescue work.  
Shelter and rescue institutions shall, based on the actual needs of wildlife shelter and rescue, establish shelter and rescue sites and equip them with corresponding professional and technical personnel, rescue tools, equipment, and medicines.  
Buying or selling wildlife and their products under the pretext of wildlife shelter and rescue shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　野生动物疫源疫病监测、检疫和与人畜共患传染病有关的动物传染病的防治管理，适用《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》等有关法律法规的规定。

**Article 16** The monitoring and quarantine of animal epidemic sources and the prevention and control of animal infectious diseases related to zoonotic diseases shall be governed by the provisions of the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家加强对野生动物遗传资源的保护，对濒危野生动物实施抢救性保护。  
国务院野生动物保护主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门制定有关野生动物遗传资源保护和利用规划，建立国家野生动物遗传资源基因库，对原产我国的珍贵、濒危野生动物遗传资源实行重点保护。

**Article 17** The State shall strengthen the protection of wildlife genetic resources and implement rescue protection for endangered wildlife.  
The competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, formulate relevant plans for the protection and utilization of wildlife genetic resources, establish a national gene bank for wildlife genetic resources, and implement prioritized protection for the genetic resources of precious and endangered wildlife originating from China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有关地方人民政府应当根据实际情况和需要建设隔离防护设施、设置安全警示标志等，预防野生动物可能造成的危害。  
县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门根据野生动物及其栖息地调查、监测和评估情况，对种群数量明显超过环境容量的物种，可以采取迁地保护、猎捕等种群调控措施，保障人身财产安全、生态安全和农业生产。对种群调控猎捕的野生动物按照国家有关规定进行处理和综合利用。种群调控的具体办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 18** The relevant local people's government shall, according to the actual situation and needs, construct isolation and protection facilities, set up safety warning signs, etc., to prevent harm that may be caused by wildlife.  
The competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level may, based on the survey, monitoring, and evaluation of wildlife and their habitats, take population regulation measures such as ex situ protection and hunting for species whose population significantly exceeds the environmental capacity, to ensure personal and property safety, ecological safety, and agricultural production. The wildlife hunted for population regulation shall be disposed of and comprehensively utilized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The specific measures for population regulation shall be formulated by the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因保护本法规定保护的野生动物，造成人员伤亡、农作物或者其他财产损失的，由当地人民政府给予补偿。具体办法由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。有关地方人民政府可以推动保险机构开展野生动物致害赔偿保险业务。  
有关地方人民政府采取预防、控制国家重点保护野生动物和其他致害严重的陆生野生动物造成危害的措施以及实行补偿所需经费，由中央财政予以补助。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定。  
在野生动物危及人身安全的紧急情况下，采取措施造成野生动物损害的，依法不承担法律责任。

**Article 19** Where casualties, damage to crops or other property are caused due to the protection of wildlife protected under this Law, the local people's government shall provide compensation. The specific measures shall be formulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. The relevant local people's government may promote insurance institutions to carry out insurance business for compensation for wildlife damage.  
The expenses required for the measures taken by the relevant local people's government to prevent and control harm caused by wildlife under prioritized protection of the State and other terrestrial wildlife that cause serious damage, as well as for compensation, shall be subsidized by the central finance. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of finance under the State Council together with the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council.  
In emergency situations where wildlife endangers personal safety, the damage caused by measures taken shall not bear legal liability in accordance with law.

### 第三章　野生动物管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　在自然保护地和禁猎（渔）区、禁猎（渔）期内，禁止猎捕以及其他妨碍野生动物生息繁衍的活动，但法律法规另有规定的除外。  
野生动物迁徙洄游期间，在前款规定区域外的迁徙洄游通道内，禁止猎捕并严格限制其他妨碍野生动物生息繁衍的活动。县级以上人民政府或者其野生动物保护主管部门应当规定并公布迁徙洄游通道的范围以及妨碍野生动物生息繁衍活动的内容。

**Article 20** Within natural protected areas and hunting or fishing prohibition areas, and hunting or fishing prohibition periods, hunting and other activities that hinder the breeding and inhabitation of wildlife shall be prohibited, unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations.  
During the migration and migration of wildlife, within the migration and migration channels outside the areas specified in the preceding paragraph, hunting shall be prohibited and other activities that hinder the breeding and inhabitation of wildlife shall be strictly restricted. The people's government at or above the county level or its competent department of wildlife protection shall specify and publish the scope of migration and migration channels and the content of activities that hinder the breeding and inhabitation of wildlife.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　禁止猎捕、杀害国家重点保护野生动物。  
因科学研究、种群调控、疫源疫病监测或者其他特殊情况，需要猎捕国家一级保护野生动物的，应当向国务院野生动物保护主管部门申请特许猎捕证；需要猎捕国家二级保护野生动物的，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门申请特许猎捕证。

**Article 21** It shall be prohibited to hunt or kill wildlife under special state protection.  
If, due to scientific research, population regulation, epidemic source and disease monitoring, or other special circumstances, it is necessary to hunt wildlife under Grade I state protection, the hunter shall apply to the wildlife protection department under the State Council for a special hunting permit; if it is necessary to hunt wildlife under Grade II state protection, the hunter shall apply to the wildlife protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for a special hunting permit.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　猎捕有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物和地方重点保护野生动物的，应当依法取得县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门核发的狩猎证，并服从猎捕量限额管理。

**Article 22** Hunting of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value and local key protected wildlife shall be subject to obtaining a hunting permit issued by the wildlife protection department of the local people's government at or above the county level according to law, and the hunting quantity limit management shall be complied with.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　猎捕者应当严格按照特许猎捕证、狩猎证规定的种类、数量或者限额、地点、工具、方法和期限进行猎捕。猎捕作业完成后，应当将猎捕情况向核发特许猎捕证、狩猎证的野生动物保护主管部门备案。具体办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定。猎捕国家重点保护野生动物应当由专业机构和人员承担；猎捕有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物，有条件的地方可以由专业机构有组织开展。  
持枪猎捕的，应当依法取得公安机关核发的持枪证。

**Article 23** A hunter shall hunt strictly in accordance with the species, quantity or limit, location, tools, methods, and time limit specified in the special hunting permit or hunting permit. After completing the hunting operation, the hunter shall file the hunting situation with the wildlife protection department that issued the special hunting permit or hunting permit. The specific measures shall be formulated by the wildlife protection department under the State Council. Hunting of wildlife under special state protection shall be carried out by professional institutions and personnel; hunting of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value may, where conditions permit, be organized professionally by professional institutions.  
If hunting with a firearm, the hunter shall obtain a firearm permit issued by the public security organ according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　禁止使用毒药、爆炸物、电击或者电子诱捕装置以及猎套、猎夹、捕鸟网、地枪、排铳等工具进行猎捕，禁止使用夜间照明行猎、歼灭性围猎、捣毁巢穴、火攻、烟熏、网捕等方法进行猎捕，但因物种保护、科学研究确需网捕、电子诱捕以及植保作业等除外。  
前款规定以外的禁止使用的猎捕工具和方法，由县级以上地方人民政府规定并公布。

**Article 24** It shall be prohibited to use poisons, explosives, electric shocks or electronic trapping devices, as well as snares, traps, bird nets, ground guns, multi-barrel shotguns, or other tools for hunting; it shall be prohibited to use methods such as nighttime illumination hunting, extermination-campaign encircling, nest destruction, fire attack, smoking out, or netting for hunting, except where netting, electronic trapping, or plant protection operations are truly necessary for species protection or scientific research.  
Hunting tools and methods prohibited in addition to those specified in the preceding paragraph shall be prescribed and published by the local people's government at or above the county level.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人工繁育野生动物实行分类分级管理，严格保护和科学利用野生动物资源。国家支持有关科学研究机构因物种保护目的人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物。  
人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物实行许可制度。人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门批准，取得人工繁育许可证，但国务院对批准机关另有规定的除外。  
人工繁育有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物的，应当向县级人民政府野生动物保护主管部门备案。  
人工繁育野生动物应当使用人工繁育子代种源，建立物种系谱、繁育档案和个体数据。因物种保护目的确需采用野外种源的，应当遵守本法有关猎捕野生动物的规定。  
本法所称人工繁育子代，是指人工控制条件下繁殖出生的子代个体且其亲本也在人工控制条件下出生。  
人工繁育野生动物的具体管理办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定。

**Article 25** The artificial breeding of wildlife shall be subject to classified and graded management, and wildlife resources shall be strictly protected and scientifically utilized. The State supports relevant scientific research institutions in artificially breeding wildlife under special state protection for the purpose of species conservation.  
A license system shall be implemented for artificial breeding of wildlife under special state protection. Any person who artificially breeds wildlife under special state protection shall obtain approval from the wildlife protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government and obtain an artificial breeding license, except where the State Council provides otherwise regarding the approving authority.  
Any person who artificially breeds terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value shall file a record with the wildlife protection department of the people's government at the county level.  
Artificial breeding of wildlife shall use artificially bred offspring as sources, and establish species pedigrees, breeding archives, and individual data. If it is truly necessary to use wild sources for the purpose of species conservation, the provisions of this Law concerning hunting of wildlife shall be complied with.  
For the purposes of this Law, "artificially bred offspring" means individuals that are born through reproduction under artificial control conditions and whose parents were also born under artificial control conditions.  
The specific management measures for artificial breeding of wildlife shall be formulated by the wildlife protection department under the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人工繁育野生动物应当有利于物种保护及其科学研究，不得违法猎捕野生动物，破坏野外种群资源，并根据野生动物习性确保其具有必要的活动空间和生息繁衍、卫生健康条件，具备与其繁育目的、种类、发展规模相适应的场所、设施、技术，符合有关技术标准和防疫要求，不得虐待野生动物。  
省级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门可以根据保护国家重点保护野生动物的需要，组织开展国家重点保护野生动物放归野外环境工作。  
前款规定以外的人工繁育的野生动物放归野外环境的，适用本法有关放生野生动物管理的规定。

**Article 26** Artificial breeding of wildlife shall be conducive to species conservation and its scientific research; it shall not illegally hunt wildlife or destroy wild population resources. According to the habits of the wildlife, it shall ensure that they have the necessary space for activities and conditions for survival, reproduction, health and hygiene, and have venues, facilities, and technologies that are appropriate for the purpose, species, and development scale of the breeding, comply with relevant technical standards and epidemic prevention requirements, and shall not mistreat wildlife.  
The wildlife protection department of the people's government at or above the provincial level may, based on the need to protect wildlife under special state protection, organize and carry out the release of wildlife under special state protection into the wild environment.  
Where artificially bred wildlife other than that specified in the preceding paragraph is released into the wild environment, the provisions of this Law concerning the management of released wildlife shall apply.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人工繁育野生动物应当采取安全措施，防止野生动物伤人和逃逸。人工繁育的野生动物造成他人损害、危害公共安全或者破坏生态的，饲养人、管理人等应当依法承担法律责任。

**Article 27** Artificial breeding of wildlife shall adopt safety measures to prevent wildlife from injuring people and escaping. If artificially bred wildlife causes harm to others, endangers public safety, or damages the ecology, the keeper, manager, or other persons shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　禁止出售、购买、利用国家重点保护野生动物及其制品。  
因科学研究、人工繁育、公众展示展演、文物保护或者其他特殊情况，需要出售、购买、利用国家重点保护野生动物及其制品的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门批准，并按照规定取得和使用专用标识，保证可追溯，但国务院对批准机关另有规定的除外。  
出售、利用有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物和地方重点保护野生动物及其制品的，应当提供狩猎、人工繁育、进出口等合法来源证明。  
实行国家重点保护野生动物和有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物及其制品专用标识的范围和管理办法，由国务院野生动物保护主管部门规定。  
出售本条第二款、第三款规定的野生动物的，还应当依法附有检疫证明。  
利用野生动物进行公众展示展演应当采取安全管理措施，并保障野生动物健康状态，具体管理办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 28** It shall be prohibited to sell, purchase, or utilize wildlife under special state protection or their products.  
If, due to scientific research, artificial breeding, public display or exhibition, cultural relic preservation, or other special circumstances, it is necessary to sell, purchase, or utilize wildlife under special state protection or their products, approval shall be obtained from the wildlife protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and special identification marks shall be obtained and used in accordance with the provisions to ensure traceability, except where the State Council provides otherwise regarding the approving authority.  
Where terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value and local key protected wildlife or their products are sold or utilized, lawful source certificates such as hunting, artificial breeding, import or export documents shall be provided.  
The scope and management measures for implementing special identification marks for wildlife under special state protection and terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value and their products shall be prescribed by the wildlife protection department under the State Council.  
Where wildlife specified in the second or third paragraph of this Article is sold, a quarantine certificate shall also be attached according to law.  
Where wildlife is used for public display or exhibition, safety management measures shall be adopted and the health status of the wildlife shall be guaranteed. Specific management measures shall be formulated by the wildlife protection department under the State Council jointly with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对人工繁育技术成熟稳定的国家重点保护野生动物或者有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物，经科学论证评估，纳入国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定的人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物名录或者有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录，并适时调整。对列入名录的野生动物及其制品，可以凭人工繁育许可证或者备案，按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门或者其授权的部门核验的年度生产数量直接取得专用标识，凭专用标识出售和利用，保证可追溯。  
对[本法第十条](#art-10)规定的国家重点保护野生动物名录和有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录进行调整时，根据有关野外种群保护情况，可以对前款规定的有关人工繁育技术成熟稳定野生动物的人工种群，不再列入国家重点保护野生动物名录和有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录，实行与野外种群不同的管理措施，但应当依照[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第二款、第三款和本条第一款的规定取得人工繁育许可证或者备案和专用标识。  
对符合[《中华人民共和国畜牧法》第十二条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国畜牧法.md#art-12)第二款规定的陆生野生动物人工繁育种群，经科学论证评估，可以列入畜禽遗传资源目录。

**Article 29** For wildlife under special state protection or terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value that has mature and stable artificial breeding techniques, after scientific demonstration and evaluation, it shall be included in the list of artificially bred wildlife under special state protection or the list of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value formulated by the wildlife protection department under the State Council, and shall be adjusted in a timely manner. For wildlife and their products included in such lists, on the basis of the artificial breeding license or record, special identification marks may be directly obtained according to the annual production quantity verified by the wildlife protection department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government or its authorized department, and such wildlife and their products may be sold and utilized with such special identification marks to ensure traceability.  
When adjusting the list of wildlife under special state protection and the list of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value as specified in Article 10 of this Law, based on the protection status of relevant wild populations, the artificial populations of wildlife with mature and stable artificial breeding techniques as specified in the preceding paragraph may no longer be included in the list of wildlife under special state protection and the list of terrestrial wildlife of significant ecological, scientific, or social value, and different management measures from those for wild populations shall be implemented; however, the artificial breeding license or record and the special identification marks shall be obtained in accordance with the provisions of Article 25, paragraphs 2 and 3 and paragraph 1 of this Article.  
For artificial breeding populations of terrestrial wildlife that meet the provisions of Article 12, paragraph 2 of the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China, after scientific demonstration and evaluation, they may be included in the catalog of livestock and poultry genetic resources.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　利用野生动物及其制品的，应当以人工繁育种群为主，有利于野外种群养护，符合生态文明建设的要求，尊重社会公德，遵守法律法规和国家有关规定。  
野生动物及其制品作为药品等经营和利用的，还应当遵守《中华人民共和国药品管理法》等有关法律法规的规定。

**Article 30** Utilization of wildlife and their products shall primarily rely on artificially bred populations, shall be conducive to the conservation of wild populations, shall meet the requirements of ecological civilization construction, shall respect social ethics, and shall comply with laws, regulations, and relevant provisions of the State.  
Where wildlife and their products are operated and utilized as drugs or for other purposes, the provisions of the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations shall also be complied with.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　禁止食用国家重点保护野生动物和国家保护的有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物以及其他陆生野生动物。  
禁止以食用为目的猎捕、交易、运输在野外环境自然生长繁殖的前款规定的野生动物。  
禁止生产、经营使用本条第一款规定的野生动物及其制品制作的食品。  
禁止为食用非法购买本条第一款规定的野生动物及其制品。

**Article 31** The consumption of terrestrial wild animals under special state protection, terrestrial wild animals under state protection with important ecological, scientific, or social value, and other terrestrial wild animals shall be prohibited.  
Hunting, trading, transporting, or purchasing for the purpose of consumption wild animals that naturally grow and breed in the wild environment as specified in the preceding paragraph shall be prohibited.  
The production or operation of food made from the wild animals and their products specified in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be prohibited.  
The illegal purchase for consumption of the wild animals and their products specified in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　禁止为出售、购买、利用野生动物或者禁止使用的猎捕工具发布广告。禁止为违法出售、购买、利用野生动物制品发布广告。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-33" title="被《中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例》第33条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** The publication of advertisements for the sale, purchase, or utilization of wild animals or for hunting tools the use of which is prohibited shall be prohibited. The publication of advertisements for the illegal sale, purchase, or utilization of wild animal products shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　禁止网络平台、商品交易市场、餐饮场所等，为违法出售、购买、食用及利用野生动物及其制品或者禁止使用的猎捕工具提供展示、交易、消费服务。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-34" title="被《中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例》第34条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** Network platforms, commodity trading markets, catering establishments, and other such venues shall be prohibited from providing display, trading, or consumption services for the illegal sale, purchase, consumption, or utilization of wild animals and their products or for hunting tools the use of which is prohibited.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　运输、携带、寄递国家重点保护野生动物及其制品，或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出国家重点保护野生动物名录的野生动物及其制品出县境的，应当持有或者附有[本法第二十一条](#art-21)、第二十五条、第二十八条或者第二十九条规定的许可证、批准文件的副本或者专用标识。  
运输、携带、寄递有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物和地方重点保护野生动物，或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录的野生动物出县境的，应当持有狩猎、人工繁育、进出口等合法来源证明或者专用标识。  
运输、携带、寄递前两款规定的野生动物出县境的，还应当依照《中华人民共和国动物防疫法》的规定附有检疫证明。  
铁路、道路、水运、民航、邮政、快递等企业对托运、携带、交寄野生动物及其制品的，应当查验其相关证件、文件副本或者专用标识，对不符合规定的，不得承运、寄递。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国水生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国水生野生动物保护实施条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-35" title="被《中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例》第35条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 34** When transporting, carrying, or sending by post wild animals under special state protection and their products, or wild animals that have been removed from the list of wild animals under special state protection in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 29 of this Law and their products, across county boundaries, a license, a copy of the approval document, or a special identification mark as specified in Article 21, 25, 28, or 29 of this Law shall be held or attached.  
When transporting, carrying, or sending by post terrestrial wild animals with important ecological, scientific, or social value or locally key protected wild animals, or wild animals that have been removed from the list of terrestrial wild animals with important ecological, scientific, or social value in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 29 of this Law and their products, across county boundaries, a certificate of legal source, such as a hunting, captive breeding, or import/export certificate, or a special identification mark shall be held.  
When transporting, carrying, or sending by post the wild animals specified in the preceding two paragraphs across county boundaries, a quarantine certificate shall also be attached in accordance with the provisions of the Animal Epidemic Prevention Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Railway, road, waterway, civil aviation, postal, and express delivery enterprises shall, when accepting consignment, carrying, or sending by post of wild animals and their products, check the relevant certificates, copies of documents, or special identification marks; those that do not comply with the provisions shall not be carried or sent by post.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门应当对科学研究、人工繁育、公众展示展演等利用野生动物及其制品的活动进行规范和监督管理。  
市场监督管理、海关、铁路、道路、水运、民航、邮政等部门应当按照职责分工对野生动物及其制品交易、利用、运输、携带、寄递等活动进行监督检查。  
国家建立由国务院林业草原、渔业主管部门牵头，各相关部门配合的野生动物联合执法工作协调机制。地方人民政府建立相应联合执法工作协调机制。  
县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和其他负有野生动物保护职责的部门发现违法事实涉嫌犯罪的，应当将犯罪线索移送具有侦查、调查职权的机关。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院在办理野生动物保护犯罪案件过程中认为没有犯罪事实，或者犯罪事实显著轻微，不需要追究刑事责任，但应当予以行政处罚的，应当及时将案件移送县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和其他负有野生动物保护职责的部门，有关部门应当依法处理。

**Article 35** The competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level shall regulate, supervise, and manage the utilization of wild animals and their products in scientific research, artificial breeding, public exhibitions, and other activities.  
The departments for market regulation, customs, railway, road, waterway, civil aviation, postal, and other relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, supervise and inspect activities such as the trading, utilization, transportation, carrying, and sending by post of wild animals and their products.  
The state shall establish a coordination mechanism for joint law enforcement on wildlife, led by the competent departments of forestry, grassland, and fisheries under the State Council, with the cooperation of relevant departments. The local people's governments shall establish corresponding coordination mechanisms for joint law enforcement.  
Where the competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level and other departments responsible for wildlife protection discover illegal facts suspected of constituting a crime, they shall transfer the crime clues to the authorities with the power of investigation or inquiry.  
Where, in the process of handling criminal cases concerning wildlife protection, the public security organs, people's procuratorates, or people's courts consider that there is no criminal fact, or the criminal fact is so minor that it does not need to be investigated for criminal liability but an administrative penalty shall be imposed, they shall promptly transfer the case to the competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level and other departments responsible for wildlife protection, and the relevant departments shall handle it according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和其他负有野生动物保护职责的部门，在履行本法规定的职责时，可以采取下列措施：  
（一）进入与违反野生动物保护管理行为有关的场所进行现场检查、调查；  
（二）对野生动物进行检验、检测、抽样取证；  
（三）查封、复制有关文件、资料，对可能被转移、销毁、隐匿或者篡改的文件、资料予以封存；  
（四）查封、扣押无合法来源证明的野生动物及其制品，查封、扣押涉嫌非法猎捕野生动物或者非法收购、出售、加工、运输猎捕野生动物及其制品的工具、设备或者财物。

**Article 36** When performing the duties prescribed in this Law, the competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level and other departments responsible for wildlife protection may take the following measures:  
(1) Entering places relevant to acts violating wildlife protection management for on-site inspection and investigation;  
(2) Conducting inspection, testing, and sampling for evidence of wild animals;  
(3) Sealing up and making copies of relevant documents and materials, and sealing up documents and materials that may be removed, destroyed, concealed, or altered;  
(4) Sealing up or seizing wild animals and their products without certificates of legal source, and sealing up or seizing tools, equipment, or property suspected of being used for illegally hunting wild animals or illegally purchasing, selling, processing, or transporting hunted wild animals and their products.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际公约禁止或者限制贸易的野生动物或者其制品名录，由国家濒危物种进出口管理机构制定、调整并公布。  
进出口列入前款名录的野生动物或者其制品，或者出口国家重点保护野生动物或者其制品的，应当经国务院野生动物保护主管部门或者国务院批准，并取得国家濒危物种进出口管理机构核发的允许进出口证明书。海关凭允许进出口证明书办理进出境检疫，并依法办理其他海关手续。  
涉及科学技术保密的野生动物物种的出口，按照国务院有关规定办理。  
列入本条第一款名录的野生动物，经国务院野生动物保护主管部门核准，按照本法有关规定进行管理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国水生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-29" title="被《中华人民共和国水生野生动物保护实施条例》第29条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例.md#art-37" title="被《中华人民共和国陆生野生动物保护实施条例》第37条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 37** The list of wild animals or their products the trade of which is prohibited or restricted by international conventions concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China shall be formulated, adjusted, and published by the national authority for the administration of import and export of endangered species.  
The import or export of wild animals or their products included in the list specified in the preceding paragraph, or the export of wild animals under special state protection or their products, shall be approved by the competent department for wildlife protection under the State Council or the State Council, and an import or export permit certificate issued by the national authority for the administration of import and export of endangered species shall be obtained. Customs shall handle entry and exit quarantine on the strength of the import or export permit certificate and handle other customs formalities according to law.  
The export of wild animal species involving scientific and technological secrets shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.  
Wild animals included in the list specified in paragraph 1 of this Article shall, upon verification by the competent department for wildlife protection under the State Council, be managed in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　禁止向境外机构或者人员提供我国特有的野生动物遗传资源。开展国际科学研究合作的，应当依法取得批准，有我国科研机构、高等学校、企业及其研究人员实质性参与研究，按照规定提出国家共享惠益的方案，并遵守我国法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 38** The provision of wildlife genetic resources unique to our country to overseas institutions or individuals shall be prohibited. Where international scientific research cooperation is carried out, approval shall be obtained according to law, with substantive participation in the research by our country's scientific research institutions, institutions of higher learning, enterprises, and their researchers, a plan for national benefit-sharing shall be proposed in accordance with regulations, and the provisions of our country's laws and administrative regulations shall be complied with.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家组织开展野生动物保护及相关执法活动的国际合作与交流，加强与毗邻国家的协作，保护野生动物迁徙通道；建立防范、打击野生动物及其制品的走私和非法贸易的部门协调机制，开展防范、打击走私和非法贸易行动。

**Article 39** The state shall organize international cooperation and exchanges in wildlife protection and related law enforcement activities, strengthen collaboration with neighboring countries to protect wildlife migration corridors; establish a departmental coordination mechanism for preventing and combating smuggling of and illegal trade in wild animals and their products, and carry out actions to prevent and combat smuggling and illegal trade.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　从境外引进野生动物物种的，应当经国务院野生动物保护主管部门批准。从境外引进列入[本法第三十七条](#art-37)第一款名录的野生动物，还应当依法取得允许进出口证明书。海关凭进口批准文件或者允许进出口证明书办理进境检疫，并依法办理其他海关手续。  
从境外引进野生动物物种的，应当采取安全可靠的防范措施，防止其进入野外环境，避免对生态系统造成危害；不得违法放生、丢弃，确需将其放生至野外环境的，应当遵守有关法律法规的规定。  
发现来自境外的野生动物对生态系统造成危害的，县级以上人民政府野生动物保护等有关部门应当采取相应的安全控制措施。

**Article 40** The introduction of wild animal species from outside the territory shall be approved by the competent department for wildlife protection under the State Council. The introduction of wild animals included in the list specified in paragraph 1 of Article 37 of this Law from outside the territory shall also require an import or export permit certificate obtained according to law. Customs shall handle entry quarantine on the strength of the import approval document or the import or export permit certificate and handle other customs formalities according to law.  
When introducing wild animal species from outside the territory, safe and reliable preventive measures shall be taken to prevent them from entering the wild environment and to avoid harm to the ecosystem; release or abandonment in violation of law shall not be allowed. Where release into the wild environment is truly necessary, the provisions of relevant laws and regulations shall be complied with.  
Where wild animals from outside the territory are found to have caused harm to the ecosystem, the competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments shall take corresponding safety control measures.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院野生动物保护主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门加强对放生野生动物活动的规范、引导。任何组织和个人将野生动物放生至野外环境，应当选择适合放生地野外生存的当地物种，不得干扰当地居民的正常生活、生产，避免对生态系统造成危害。具体办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定。随意放生野生动物，造成他人人身、财产损害或者危害生态系统的，依法承担法律责任。

**Article 41** The competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, strengthen the regulation and guidance of the release of wildlife into the wild. Any organization or individual that releases wildlife into the wild environment shall select local species suitable for survival in the wild at the release site, and shall not interfere with the normal life and production of local residents, and shall avoid causing harm to the ecosystem. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of wildlife protection under the State Council. Whoever arbitrarily releases wildlife, causing harm to another person's person or property or endangering the ecosystem, shall bear legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　禁止伪造、变造、买卖、转让、租借特许猎捕证、狩猎证、人工繁育许可证及专用标识，出售、购买、利用国家重点保护野生动物及其制品的批准文件，或者允许进出口证明书、进出口等批准文件。  
前款规定的有关许可证书、专用标识、批准文件的发放有关情况，应当依法公开。

**Article 42** It is forbidden to forge, alter, buy, sell, transfer, or lend special hunting permits, hunting licenses, artificial breeding licenses and special marks, approval documents for the sale, purchase, or use of state key protected wildlife and their products, or approval documents such as permits for import and export, or import and export certificates.  
The issuance of relevant permits, special marks, and approval documents as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be disclosed according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　外国人在我国对国家重点保护野生动物进行野外考察或者在野外拍摄电影、录像，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府野生动物保护主管部门或者其授权的单位批准，并遵守有关法律法规的规定。

**Article 43** Foreigners conducting field surveys of state key protected wildlife or filming movies or videos in the wild in China shall obtain approval from the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council, or the unit authorized by it, and shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会或者其常务委员会可以根据地方实际情况制定对地方重点保护野生动物等的管理办法。

**Article 44** The people's congress of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council, or its standing committee, may formulate management measures for local key protected wildlife, among others, in light of local actual conditions.

### 第四章　法律责任

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　野生动物保护主管部门或者其他有关部门不依法作出行政许可决定，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报不依法处理，或者有其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊等不依法履行职责的行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 45** Where the competent department of wildlife protection or other relevant departments fail to make an administrative licensing decision according to law, fail to handle discovered illegal acts or reports of illegal acts according to law, or have other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain, failing to perform their duties according to law, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反[本法第十二条](#art-12)第三款、第十三条第二款规定的，依照有关法律法规的规定处罚。

**Article 46** Whoever violates the provisions of Article 12, paragraph 3, or Article 13, paragraph 2 of this Law shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反[本法第十五条](#art-15)第四款规定，以收容救护为名买卖野生动物及其制品的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门没收野生动物及其制品、违法所得，并处野生动物及其制品价值二倍以上二十倍以下罚款，将有关违法信息记入社会信用记录，并向社会公布；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 47** Whoever, in violation of Article 15, paragraph 4 of this Law, buys or sells wildlife and their products under the guise of shelter and rescue shall be ordered by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level to confiscate the wildlife and their products and illegal gains, and shall be fined not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the wildlife and their products; the relevant illegal information shall be recorded in the social credit record and shall be made public; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)、第二十一条、第二十三条第一款、第二十四条第一款规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门、海警机构和有关自然保护地管理机构按照职责分工没收猎获物、猎捕工具和违法所得，吊销特许猎捕证，并处猎获物价值二倍以上二十倍以下罚款；没有猎获物或者猎获物价值不足五千元的，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）在自然保护地、禁猎（渔）区、禁猎（渔）期猎捕国家重点保护野生动物；  
（二）未取得特许猎捕证、未按照特许猎捕证规定猎捕、杀害国家重点保护野生动物；  
（三）使用禁用的工具、方法猎捕国家重点保护野生动物。  
违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)第一款规定，未将猎捕情况向野生动物保护主管部门备案的，由核发特许猎捕证、狩猎证的野生动物保护主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销特许猎捕证、狩猎证。

**Article 48** Whoever, in violation of Article 20, Article 21, Article 23, paragraph 1, or Article 24, paragraph 1 of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level, the coast guard agency, and the relevant natural protected area management authority according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the hunted items, hunting tools, and illegal gains, revoke the special hunting permit, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the hunted items; if there are no hunted items or the value of the hunted items is less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Hunting state key protected wildlife in natural protected areas, areas where hunting (fishing) is prohibited, or during the period when hunting (fishing) is prohibited;  
(2) Hunting or killing state key protected wildlife without obtaining a special hunting permit or not in accordance with the provisions of the special hunting permit;  
(3) Using prohibited tools or methods to hunt state key protected wildlife.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 23, paragraph 1 of this Law, fails to file hunting information with the competent department of wildlife protection shall be ordered by the competent department of wildlife protection that issued the special hunting permit or hunting license to make corrections within a specified period; if no correction is made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the special hunting permit or hunting license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)、第二十二条、第二十三条第一款、第二十四条第一款规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和有关自然保护地管理机构按照职责分工没收猎获物、猎捕工具和违法所得，吊销狩猎证，并处猎获物价值一倍以上十倍以下罚款；没有猎获物或者猎获物价值不足二千元的，并处二千元以上二万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）在自然保护地、禁猎（渔）区、禁猎（渔）期猎捕有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者地方重点保护野生动物；  
（二）未取得狩猎证、未按照狩猎证规定猎捕有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者地方重点保护野生动物；  
（三）使用禁用的工具、方法猎捕有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者地方重点保护野生动物。  
违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)、第二十四条第一款规定，在自然保护地、禁猎区、禁猎期或者使用禁用的工具、方法猎捕其他陆生野生动物，破坏生态的，由县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和有关自然保护地管理机构按照职责分工没收猎获物、猎捕工具和违法所得，并处猎获物价值一倍以上三倍以下罚款；没有猎获物或者猎获物价值不足一千元的，并处一千元以上三千元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本法第二十三条](#art-23)第二款规定，未取得持枪证持枪猎捕野生动物，构成违反治安管理行为的，还应当由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 49** Whoever, in violation of Article 20, Article 22, Article 23, paragraph 1, or Article 24, paragraph 1 of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level and the relevant natural protected area management authority according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the hunted items, hunting tools, and illegal gains, revoke the hunting license, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the value of the hunted items; if there are no hunted items or the value of the hunted items is less than 2,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Hunting terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value or local key protected wildlife in natural protected areas, areas where hunting (fishing) is prohibited, or during the period when hunting (fishing) is prohibited;  
(2) Hunting terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value or local key protected wildlife without obtaining a hunting license or not in accordance with the provisions of the hunting license;  
(3) Using prohibited tools or methods to hunt terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value or local key protected wildlife.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 20 or Article 24, paragraph 1 of this Law, hunts other terrestrial wildlife in natural protected areas, areas where hunting is prohibited, during the period when hunting is prohibited, or using prohibited tools or methods, thereby damaging the ecology, shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level and the relevant natural protected area management authority according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the hunted items, hunting tools, and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the hunted items; if there are no hunted items or the value of the hunted items is less than 1,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 3,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 23, paragraph 2 of this Law, hunts wildlife with a firearm without obtaining a firearm permit, and such act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall also be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第二款规定，以食用为目的猎捕、交易、运输在野外环境自然生长繁殖的国家重点保护野生动物或者有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物的，依照[本法第四十八条](#art-48)、第四十九条、第五十二条的规定从重处罚。  
违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第二款规定，以食用为目的猎捕在野外环境自然生长繁殖的其他陆生野生动物的，由县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和有关自然保护地管理机构按照职责分工没收猎获物、猎捕工具和违法所得；情节严重的，并处猎获物价值一倍以上五倍以下罚款，没有猎获物或者猎获物价值不足二千元的，并处二千元以上一万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第二款规定，以食用为目的交易、运输在野外环境自然生长繁殖的其他陆生野生动物的，由县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工没收野生动物；情节严重的，并处野生动物价值一倍以上五倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 50** Whoever, in violation of Article 31, paragraph 2 of this Law, hunts, trades, or transports state key protected wildlife or terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value that grows and breeds naturally in the wild environment for the purpose of consumption shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with Articles 48, 49, and 52 of this Law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 31, paragraph 2 of this Law, hunts other terrestrial wildlife that grows and breeds naturally in the wild environment for the purpose of consumption shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level and the relevant natural protected area management authority according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the hunted items, hunting tools, and illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the hunted items shall also be imposed, and if there are no hunted items or the value of the hunted items is less than 2,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 31, paragraph 2 of this Law, trades or transports other terrestrial wildlife that grows and breeds naturally in the wild environment for the purpose of consumption shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level and the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the wildlife; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the wildlife shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第二款规定，未取得人工繁育许可证，繁育国家重点保护野生动物或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出国家重点保护野生动物名录的野生动物的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门没收野生动物及其制品，并处野生动物及其制品价值一倍以上十倍以下罚款。  
违反[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第三款规定，人工繁育有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录的野生动物未备案的，由县级人民政府野生动物保护主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五百元以上二千元以下罚款。

**Article 51** Whoever, in violation of Article 25, paragraph 2 of this Law, breeds state key protected wildlife or wildlife that has been removed from the list of state key protected wildlife in accordance with Article 29, paragraph 2 of this Law without obtaining an artificial breeding license shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the wildlife and their products, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the value of the wildlife and their products.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 25, paragraph 3 of this Law, artificially breeds terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value or wildlife that has been removed from the list of terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value in accordance with Article 29, paragraph 2 of this Law without filing a record shall be ordered by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at the county level to make corrections within a specified period; if no correction is made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第一款和第二款、第二十九条第一款、第三十四条第一款规定，未经批准、未取得或者未按照规定使用专用标识，或者未持有、未附有人工繁育许可证、批准文件的副本或者专用标识出售、购买、利用、运输、携带、寄递国家重点保护野生动物及其制品或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出国家重点保护野生动物名录的野生动物及其制品的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工没收野生动物及其制品和违法所得，责令关闭违法经营场所，并处野生动物及其制品价值二倍以上二十倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销人工繁育许可证、撤销批准文件、收回专用标识；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第三款、第二十九条第一款、第三十四条第二款规定，未持有合法来源证明或者专用标识出售、利用、运输、携带、寄递有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物、地方重点保护野生动物或者依照[本法第二十九条](#art-29)第二款规定调出有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物名录的野生动物及其制品的，由县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工没收野生动物，并处野生动物价值一倍以上十倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本法第三十四条](#art-34)第四款规定，铁路、道路、水运、民航、邮政、快递等企业未按照规定查验或者承运、寄递野生动物及其制品的，由交通运输、铁路监督管理、民用航空、邮政管理等相关主管部门按照职责分工没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销经营许可证。

**Article 52** Whoever, in violation of Article 28, paragraphs 1 and 2, Article 29, paragraph 1, or Article 34, paragraph 1 of this Law, without approval, without obtaining or without using special marks as required, or without holding or attaching a copy of an artificial breeding license, approval document, or special mark, sells, purchases, uses, transports, carries, or sends by mail state key protected wildlife and their products, or wildlife and their products that have been removed from the list of state key protected wildlife in accordance with Article 29, paragraph 2 of this Law, shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level and the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the wildlife and their products and illegal gains, order the closure of the illegal business premises, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the wildlife and their products; if the circumstances are serious, the artificial breeding license shall be revoked, the approval document shall be withdrawn, and the special marks shall be recalled; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 28, paragraph 3, Article 29, paragraph 1, or Article 34, paragraph 2 of this Law, without holding a legal source certificate or special mark, sells, uses, transports, carries, or sends by mail terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value, local key protected wildlife, or wildlife and their products that have been removed from the list of terrestrial wildlife of important ecological, scientific, or social value in accordance with Article 29, paragraph 2 of this Law, shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level and the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the wildlife, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the value of the wildlife; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 34, paragraph 4 of this Law, being an enterprise such as railway, road, water transport, civil aviation, postal service, or express delivery service, fails to inspect or fails to carry or send by mail wildlife and their products as required, shall, by the relevant competent departments such as transportation, railway supervision and administration, civil aviation, and postal administration according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the illegal gains; if the circumstances are serious, the business license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第一款、第四款规定，食用或者为食用非法购买本法规定保护的野生动物及其制品的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工责令停止违法行为，没收野生动物及其制品，并处野生动物及其制品价值二倍以上二十倍以下罚款；食用或者为食用非法购买其他陆生野生动物及其制品的，责令停止违法行为，给予批评教育，没收野生动物及其制品，情节严重的，并处野生动物及其制品价值一倍以上五倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反[本法第三十一条](#art-31)第三款规定，生产、经营使用本法规定保护的野生动物及其制品制作的食品的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和市场监督管理部门按照职责分工责令停止违法行为，没收野生动物及其制品和违法所得，责令关闭违法经营场所，并处违法所得十五倍以上三十倍以下罚款；生产、经营使用其他陆生野生动物及其制品制作的食品的，给予批评教育，没收野生动物及其制品和违法所得，情节严重的，并处违法所得一倍以上十倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 53** Whoever, in violation of Article 31, paragraphs 1 and 4 of this Law, consumes or illegally purchases for consumption wildlife and their products protected under this Law shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level and the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties, be ordered to cease the illegal act, confiscate the wildlife and their products, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the wildlife and their products; if the consumption or illegal purchase for consumption concerns other terrestrial wildlife and their products, the illegal act shall be ordered to cease, a reprimand shall be given, the wildlife and their products shall be confiscated, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the wildlife and their products shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Whoever, in violation of Article 31, paragraph 3 of this Law, produces or operates food made from wildlife and their products protected under this Law shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level and the market supervision and administration department according to their respective functions and duties, be ordered to cease the illegal act, confiscate the wildlife and their products and illegal gains, order the closure of the illegal business premises, and impose a fine of not less than fifteen times but not more than thirty times the illegal gains; if the production or operation concerns food made from other terrestrial wildlife and their products, a reprimand shall be given, the wildlife and their products and illegal gains shall be confiscated, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than one time but not more than ten times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反[本法第三十二条](#art-32)规定，为出售、购买、利用野生动物及其制品或者禁止使用的猎捕工具发布广告的，依照《中华人民共和国广告法》的规定处罚。

**Article 54** Whoever, in violation of Article 32 of this Law, publishes advertisements for the sale, purchase, or use of wildlife and their products or prohibited hunting tools shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反[本法第三十三条](#art-33)规定，为违法出售、购买、食用及利用野生动物及其制品或者禁止使用的猎捕工具提供展示、交易、消费服务的，由县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门责令停止违法行为，限期改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得二倍以上十倍以下罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足五千元的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 55** Whoever, in violation of Article 33 of this Law, provides display, trading, or consumption services for the illegal sale, purchase, consumption, or use of wildlife and their products or prohibited hunting tools shall, by the market supervision and administration department of the people's government at or above the county level, be ordered to cease the illegal act, make corrections within a specified period, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than ten times the illegal gains; if there are no illegal gains or the illegal gains are less than 5,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反[本法第三十七条](#art-37)规定，进出口野生动物及其制品的，由海关、公安机关、海警机构依照法律、行政法规和国家有关规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 56** Whoever, in violation of Article 37 of this Law, imports or exports wildlife and their products shall be penalized by the customs, the public security organ, and the coast guard agency in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, and the relevant provisions of the State; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反[本法第三十八条](#art-38)规定，向境外机构或者人员提供我国特有的野生动物遗传资源的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门没收野生动物及其制品和违法所得，并处野生动物及其制品价值或者违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 57** Whoever, in violation of Article 38 of this Law, provides wildlife genetic resources unique to China to overseas institutions or individuals shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the wildlife and their products and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the value of the wildlife and their products or the illegal gains; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反[本法第四十条](#art-40)第一款规定，从境外引进野生动物物种的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门没收所引进的野生动物，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；未依法实施进境检疫的，依照《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》的规定处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 58** Whoever, in violation of Article 40, paragraph 1 of this Law, introduces wildlife species from overseas shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the introduced wildlife, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the entry quarantine is not carried out according to law, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Quarantine of Animals and Plants Entering and Exiting the Country; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反[本法第四十条](#art-40)第二款规定，将从境外引进的野生动物放生、丢弃的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门责令限期捕回，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；逾期不捕回的，由有关野生动物保护主管部门代为捕回或者采取降低影响的措施，所需费用由被责令限期捕回者承担；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 59** Whoever, in violation of Article 40, paragraph 2 of this Law, releases or discards wildlife introduced from overseas shall be ordered by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level to recapture the wildlife within a specified period, and shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the wildlife is not recaptured within the specified period, the relevant competent department of wildlife protection shall recapture them on behalf of the offender or take measures to reduce the impact, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the person ordered to recapture within the specified period; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反[本法第四十二条](#art-42)第一款规定，伪造、变造、买卖、转让、租借有关证件、专用标识或者有关批准文件的，由县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门没收违法证件、专用标识、有关批准文件和违法所得，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Whoever, in violation of Article 42, paragraph 1 of this Law, forges, alters, buys, sells, transfers, or lends relevant certificates, special marks, or relevant approval documents shall, by the competent department of wildlife protection of the people's government at or above the county level, confiscate the illegal certificates, special marks, relevant approval documents, and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门和其他负有野生动物保护职责的部门、机构应当按照有关规定处理罚没的野生动物及其制品，具体办法由国务院野生动物保护主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 61** The competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level and other departments and institutions with wildlife protection duties shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, dispose of confiscated wildlife and their products. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department for wildlife protection of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　县级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门应当加强对野生动物及其制品鉴定、价值评估工作的规范、指导。本法规定的猎获物价值、野生动物及其制品价值的评估标准和方法，由国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定。

**Article 62** The competent departments for wildlife protection of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the regulation and guidance of the expert evaluation and value assessment of wildlife and their products. The evaluation standards and methods for the value of prey and the value of wildlife and their products prescribed in this Law shall be formulated by the competent department for wildlife protection of the State Council.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　对违反本法规定破坏野生动物资源、生态环境，损害社会公共利益的行为，可以依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 63** For acts that violate the provisions of this Law, damage wildlife resources and ecological environment, and harm the public interest, a civil action may be filed with the People's Court in accordance with the provisions of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws.

### 第五章　附则

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　本法自2023年5月1日起施行。

**Article 64** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2023.
