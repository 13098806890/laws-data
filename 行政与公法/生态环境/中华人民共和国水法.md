### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了合理开发、利用、节约和保护水资源，防治水害，实现水资源的可持续利用，适应国民经济和社会发展的需要，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of rationally developing, utilizing, conserving and protecting water resources, preventing and controlling water disasters, achieving sustainable utilization of water resources, and meeting the needs of national economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域内开发、利用、节约、保护、管理水资源，防治水害，适用本法。  
本法所称水资源，包括地表水和地下水。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the development, utilization, conservation, protection and management of water resources, and the prevention and control of water disasters within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
Water resources as mentioned in this Law include surface water and groundwater.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　水资源属于国家所有。水资源的所有权由国务院代表国家行使。农村集体经济组织的水塘和由农村集体经济组织修建管理的水库中的水，归各该农村集体经济组织使用。

**Article 3** Water resources shall be owned by the State. The ownership of water resources shall be exercised by the State Council on behalf of the State. Water in ponds and reservoirs constructed and managed by rural collective economic organizations shall be used by such rural collective economic organizations.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　开发、利用、节约、保护水资源和防治水害，应当全面规划、统筹兼顾、标本兼治、综合利用、讲求效益，发挥水资源的多种功能，协调好生活、生产经营和生态环境用水。

**Article 4** The development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters shall be carried out in a comprehensive planning manner, taking into full consideration all relevant factors, addressing both symptoms and root causes, pursuing comprehensive utilization, and aiming for benefits, so as to bring into play the multiple functions of water resources and coordinate the use of water for domestic purposes, production and business operations, and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强水利基础设施建设，并将其纳入本级国民经济和社会发展计划。

**Article 5** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of water conservancy infrastructure and incorporate it into their respective national economic and social development plans.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励单位和个人依法开发、利用水资源，并保护其合法权益。开发、利用水资源的单位和个人有依法保护水资源的义务。

**Article 6** The State encourages entities and individuals to develop and utilize water resources according to law and protects their lawful rights and interests. Entities and individuals that develop and utilize water resources shall have the obligation to protect water resources according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家对水资源依法实行取水许可制度和有偿使用制度。但是，农村集体经济组织及其成员使用本集体经济组织的水塘、水库中的水的除外。国务院水行政主管部门负责全国取水许可制度和水资源有偿使用制度的组织实施。

**Article 7** The State implements a water-taking permit system and a paid-use system for water resources according to law, except where rural collective economic organizations and their members use water from ponds and reservoirs of such organizations. The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of the water-taking permit system and the paid-use system for water resources nationwide.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家厉行节约用水，大力推行节约用水措施，推广节约用水新技术、新工艺，发展节水型工业、农业和服务业，建立节水型社会。  
各级人民政府应当采取措施，加强对节约用水的管理，建立节约用水技术开发推广体系，培育和发展节约用水产业。  
单位和个人有节约用水的义务。

**Article 8** The State practices economy in water use, vigorously promotes water-conservation measures, disseminates new technologies and processes for water conservation, develops water-saving industries, agriculture and service sectors, and establishes a water-saving society.  
People's governments at all levels shall take measures to strengthen the management of water conservation, establish a system for the development and dissemination of water-saving technologies, and foster and develop the water-saving industry.  
Entities and individuals shall have the obligation to conserve water.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家保护水资源，采取有效措施，保护植被，植树种草，涵养水源，防治水土流失和水体污染，改善生态环境。

**Article 9** The State shall protect water resources, take effective measures to protect vegetation, plant trees and grass, conserve water sources, prevent and control soil erosion and water pollution, and improve the ecological environment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持开发、利用、节约、保护、管理水资源和防治水害的先进科学技术的研究、推广和应用。

**Article 10** The State encourages and supports the research, dissemination and application of advanced science and technology for the development, utilization, conservation, protection and management of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在开发、利用、节约、保护、管理水资源和防治水害等方面成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 11** Entities and individuals that have achieved outstanding results in the development, utilization, conservation, protection and management of water resources and in the prevention and control of water disasters shall be rewarded by the people's governments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家对水资源实行流域管理与行政区域管理相结合的管理体制。  
国务院水行政主管部门负责全国水资源的统一管理和监督工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构（以下简称流域管理机构），在所管辖的范围内行使法律、行政法规规定的和国务院水行政主管部门授予的水资源管理和监督职责。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照规定的权限，负责本行政区域内水资源的统一管理和监督工作。

**Article 12** The State implements a management system for water resources that combines river basin management with administrative regional management.  
The water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the unified management and supervision of water resources nationwide.  
The river basin management agencies established by the water administrative department of the State Council in major rivers and lakes designated by the State (hereinafter referred to as river basin management agencies) shall, within their respective jurisdictions, exercise the functions of water resources management and supervision as provided for by laws and administrative regulations and as delegated by the water administrative department of the State Council.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their prescribed functions, be responsible for the unified management and supervision of water resources within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院有关部门按照职责分工，负责水资源开发、利用、节约和保护的有关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工，负责本行政区域内水资源开发、利用、节约和保护的有关工作。

**Article 13** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the work relating to the development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources.  
The relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the work relating to the development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　水资源规划

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家制定全国水资源战略规划。  
开发、利用、节约、保护水资源和防治水害，应当按照流域、区域统一制定规划。规划分为流域规划和区域规划。流域规划包括流域综合规划和流域专业规划；区域规划包括区域综合规划和区域专业规划。  
前款所称综合规划，是指根据经济社会发展需要和水资源开发利用现状编制的开发、利用、节约、保护水资源和防治水害的总体部署。前款所称专业规划，是指防洪、治涝、灌溉、航运、供水、水力发电、竹木流放、渔业、水资源保护、水土保持、防沙治沙、节约用水等规划。

**Article 14** The State shall formulate a national strategic plan for water resources.  
For the development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters, plans shall be formulated in a unified manner by river basin and region. Plans shall be divided into river basin plans and regional plans. River basin plans include comprehensive river basin plans and specialized river basin plans; regional plans include comprehensive regional plans and specialized regional plans.  
The comprehensive plans referred to in the preceding paragraph mean the overall arrangements for the development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters formulated on the basis of the needs of economic and social development and the current status of water resources development and utilization. The specialized plans referred to in the preceding paragraph mean plans for flood control, waterlogging control, irrigation, navigation, water supply, hydropower generation, bamboo and log floating, fisheries, water resources protection, water and soil conservation, sand prevention and control, water conservation, etc.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　流域范围内的区域规划应当服从流域规划，专业规划应当服从综合规划。  
流域综合规划和区域综合规划以及与土地利用关系密切的专业规划，应当与国民经济和社会发展规划以及土地利用总体规划、城市总体规划和环境保护规划相协调，兼顾各地区、各行业的需要。

**Article 15** Regional plans within the scope of a river basin shall be subordinate to the river basin plan, and specialized plans shall be subordinate to comprehensive plans.  
Comprehensive river basin plans and comprehensive regional plans, as well as specialized plans closely related to land use, shall be coordinated with national economic and social development plans, overall land use plans, overall urban plans and environmental protection plans, and shall take into account the needs of various regions and industries.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　制定规划，必须进行水资源综合科学考察和调查评价。水资源综合科学考察和调查评价，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级有关部门组织进行。  
县级以上人民政府应当加强水文、水资源信息系统建设。县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和流域管理机构应当加强对水资源的动态监测。  
基本水文资料应当按照国家有关规定予以公开。

**Article 16** In formulating plans, comprehensive scientific investigation and survey assessment of water resources must be conducted. The comprehensive scientific investigation and survey assessment of water resources shall be organized by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level jointly with the relevant departments at the same level.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of hydrological and water resources information systems. The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and river basin management agencies shall strengthen the dynamic monitoring of water resources.  
Basic hydrological data shall be made public in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的流域综合规划，由国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制，报国务院批准。跨省、自治区、直辖市的其他江河、湖泊的流域综合规划和区域综合规划，由有关流域管理机构会同江河、湖泊所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门和有关部门编制，分别经有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查提出意见后，报国务院水行政主管部门审核；国务院水行政主管部门征求国务院有关部门意见后，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准。  
前款规定以外的其他江河、湖泊的流域综合规划和区域综合规划，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级有关部门和有关地方人民政府编制，报本级人民政府或者其授权的部门批准，并报上一级水行政主管部门备案。  
专业规划由县级以上人民政府有关部门编制，征求同级其他有关部门意见后，报本级人民政府批准。其中，防洪规划、水土保持规划的编制、批准，依照防洪法、水土保持法的有关规定执行。

**Article 17** The comprehensive river basin plans for major rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval. The comprehensive river basin plans and comprehensive regional plans for other rivers and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the relevant river basin management agencies jointly with the water administrative departments and relevant departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the rivers and lakes are located, and after being examined and comments submitted by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for review; after consulting the relevant departments of the State Council, the water administrative department of the State Council shall submit them to the State Council or its authorized department for approval.  
The comprehensive river basin plans and comprehensive regional plans for other rivers and lakes not covered by the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level jointly with the relevant departments at the same level and the relevant local people's governments, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level or its authorized department for approval, and shall be reported to the water administrative department at the next higher level for record.  
Specialized plans shall be formulated by the relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and after consulting the opinions of other relevant departments at the same level, shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval. Among these, the formulation and approval of flood control plans and water and soil conservation plans shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Flood Control Law and the Law on Water and Soil Conservation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　规划一经批准，必须严格执行。  
经批准的规划需要修改时，必须按照规划编制程序经原批准机关批准。

**Article 18** Once a plan is approved, it must be strictly implemented.  
If an approved plan needs to be revised, it must be approved by the original approving authority in accordance with the planning procedure.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　建设水工程，必须符合流域综合规划。在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊和跨省、自治区、直辖市的江河、湖泊上建设水工程，未取得有关流域管理机构签署的符合流域综合规划要求的规划同意书的，建设单位不得开工建设；在其他江河、湖泊上建设水工程，未取得县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门按照管理权限签署的符合流域综合规划要求的规划同意书的，建设单位不得开工建设。水工程建设涉及防洪的，依照防洪法的有关规定执行；涉及其他地区和行业的，建设单位应当事先征求有关地区和部门的意见。

**Article 19** Any construction of water projects must conform to the comprehensive river basin plan. For the construction of water projects on major rivers and lakes designated by the State or on rivers and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, if the relevant river basin management agency has not issued a planning consent letter confirming compliance with the comprehensive river basin plan, the construction entity shall not commence construction; for the construction of water projects on other rivers and lakes, if the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level has not issued a planning consent letter confirming compliance with the comprehensive river basin plan within its management authority, the construction entity shall not commence construction. Where the construction of a water project involves flood control, the relevant provisions of the Flood Control Law shall apply; where it involves other regions or industries, the construction entity shall consult in advance with the relevant regions and departments.

### 第三章　水资源开发利用

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　开发、利用水资源，应当坚持兴利与除害相结合，兼顾上下游、左右岸和有关地区之间的利益，充分发挥水资源的综合效益，并服从防洪的总体安排。

**Article 20** In developing and utilizing water resources, the principle of combining beneficial uses with disaster prevention shall be adhered to, taking into account the interests of upstream and downstream, left and right banks, and relevant regions, giving full play to the comprehensive benefits of water resources, and subjecting it to the overall arrangements for flood control.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　开发、利用水资源，应当首先满足城乡居民生活用水，并兼顾农业、工业、生态环境用水以及航运等需要。  
在干旱和半干旱地区开发、利用水资源，应当充分考虑生态环境用水需要。

**Article 21** In developing and utilizing water resources, priority shall be given to meeting the domestic water needs of urban and rural residents, while also taking into account the needs of agriculture, industry, ecological environment, and navigation.  
In developing and utilizing water resources in arid and semi-arid areas, full consideration shall be given to the water needs of the ecological environment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　跨流域调水，应当进行全面规划和科学论证，统筹兼顾调出和调入流域的用水需要，防止对生态环境造成破坏。

**Article 22** Inter-basin water transfer shall be subject to comprehensive planning and scientific demonstration, taking into overall consideration the water needs of both the exporting and importing basins, and preventing damage to the ecological environment.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　地方各级人民政府应当结合本地区水资源的实际情况，按照地表水与地下水统一调度开发、开源与节流相结合、节流优先和污水处理再利用的原则，合理组织开发、综合利用水资源。  
国民经济和社会发展规划以及城市总体规划的编制、重大建设项目的布局，应当与当地水资源条件和防洪要求相适应，并进行科学论证；在水资源不足的地区，应当对城市规模和建设耗水量大的工业、农业和服务业项目加以限制。

**Article 23** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in light of the actual conditions of local water resources, rationally organize the development and comprehensive utilization of water resources in accordance with the principles of unified scheduling and development of surface water and groundwater, combining the development of new sources with conservation, giving priority to conservation, and reusing treated sewage.  
The formulation of national economic and social development plans and overall urban plans, as well as the layout of major construction projects, shall be compatible with local water resource conditions and flood control requirements, and shall be subject to scientific demonstration; in areas with insufficient water resources, restrictions shall be imposed on the scale of cities and on industrial, agricultural and service projects that consume large amounts of water.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在水资源短缺的地区，国家鼓励对雨水和微咸水的收集、开发、利用和对海水的利用、淡化。

**Article 24** In areas where water resources are scarce, the State encourages the collection, development and utilization of rainwater and slightly saline water, as well as the utilization and desalination of seawater.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　地方各级人民政府应当加强对灌溉、排涝、水土保持工作的领导，促进农业生产发展；在容易发生盐碱化和渍害的地区，应当采取措施，控制和降低地下水的水位。  
农村集体经济组织或者其成员依法在本集体经济组织所有的集体土地或者承包土地上投资兴建水工程设施的，按照谁投资建设谁管理和谁受益的原则，对水工程设施及其蓄水进行管理和合理使用。  
农村集体经济组织修建水库应当经县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门批准。

**Article 25** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over irrigation, drainage and water and soil conservation work to promote agricultural production; in areas prone to salinization and waterlogging, measures shall be taken to control and lower the groundwater table.  
Where a rural collective economic organization or its members invest in the construction of water project facilities on collectively owned land or contracted land belonging to that organization according to law, they shall manage and reasonably use the water project facilities and the impounded water in accordance with the principle that whoever invests in construction manages it and benefits from it.  
A rural collective economic organization shall obtain approval from the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level before building a reservoir.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家鼓励开发、利用水能资源。在水能丰富的河流，应当有计划地进行多目标梯级开发。  
建设水力发电站，应当保护生态环境，兼顾防洪、供水、灌溉、航运、竹木流放和渔业等方面的需要。

**Article 26** The State encourages the development and utilization of hydropower resources. On rivers rich in hydropower resources, multi-purpose cascade development shall be carried out in a planned manner.  
In constructing hydropower stations, the ecological environment shall be protected, and the needs of flood control, water supply, irrigation, navigation, bamboo and log floating, and fisheries shall be taken into account.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家鼓励开发、利用水运资源。在水生生物洄游通道、通航或者竹木流放的河流上修建永久性拦河闸坝，建设单位应当同时修建过鱼、过船、过木设施，或者经国务院授权的部门批准采取其他补救措施，并妥善安排施工和蓄水期间的水生生物保护、航运和竹木流放，所需费用由建设单位承担。  
在不通航的河流或者人工水道上修建闸坝后可以通航的，闸坝建设单位应当同时修建过船设施或者预留过船设施位置。

**Article 27** The State encourages the development and utilization of water transport resources. Where a permanent barrage or dam is built on a river that serves as a migration passage for aquatic organisms, or is navigable or used for bamboo and log floating, the construction entity shall simultaneously build fish passes, ship locks, and log passes, or take other remedial measures approved by the department authorized by the State Council, and shall properly arrange for the protection of aquatic organisms, navigation, and bamboo and log floating during construction and impoundment; the costs involved shall be borne by the construction entity.  
Where a dam is built on a non-navigable river or artificial watercourse that may become navigable thereafter, the dam construction entity shall simultaneously build ship lock facilities or reserve space for such facilities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人引水、截（蓄）水、排水，不得损害公共利益和他人的合法权益。

**Article 28** No entity or individual shall, when diverting, impounding (storing) or draining water, harm the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家对水工程建设移民实行开发性移民的方针，按照前期补偿、补助与后期扶持相结合的原则，妥善安排移民的生产和生活，保护移民的合法权益。  
移民安置应当与工程建设同步进行。建设单位应当根据安置地区的环境容量和可持续发展的原则，因地制宜，编制移民安置规划，经依法批准后，由有关地方人民政府组织实施。所需移民经费列入工程建设投资计划。

**Article 29** The State implements a developmental resettlement policy for water project construction resettlement, based on the principle of combining preliminary compensation and subsidies with post-relocation support, and shall properly arrange for the production and livelihood of resettled persons and protect their lawful rights and interests.  
Resettlement shall be carried out simultaneously with project construction. The construction entity shall, based on the environmental capacity of the resettlement area and the principle of sustainable development, adapt measures to local conditions, formulate a resettlement plan, and after approval according to law, the relevant local people's government shall organize its implementation. The funds required for resettlement shall be included in the project construction investment plan.

### 第四章　水资源、水域和水工程的保护

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、流域管理机构以及其他有关部门在制定水资源开发、利用规划和调度水资源时，应当注意维持江河的合理流量和湖泊、水库以及地下水的合理水位，维护水体的自然净化能力。

**Article 30** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level, river basin management agencies, and other relevant departments shall, when formulating plans for the development and utilization of water resources and scheduling water resources, pay attention to maintaining reasonable flow rates in rivers and reasonable water levels in lakes, reservoirs, and groundwater, and preserve the natural purification capacity of water bodies.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　从事水资源开发、利用、节约、保护和防治水害等水事活动，应当遵守经批准的规划；因违反规划造成江河和湖泊水域使用功能降低、地下水超采、地面沉降、水体污染的，应当承担治理责任。  
开采矿藏或者建设地下工程，因疏干排水导致地下水水位下降、水源枯竭或者地面塌陷，采矿单位或者建设单位应当采取补救措施；对他人生活和生产造成损失的，依法给予补偿。

**Article 31** Those engaged in water-related activities such as the development, utilization, conservation and protection of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters shall comply with the approved plans; where the use functions of river or lake waters are reduced, groundwater is over-extracted, land subsidence occurs, or water bodies are polluted due to violation of the plans, the responsible party shall bear the liability for remediation.  
Where mining or underground construction causes a drop in the groundwater table, depletion of water sources, or ground collapse due to dewatering and drainage, the mining unit or construction entity shall take remedial measures; if losses are caused to the life or production of others, compensation shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院环境保护行政主管部门、有关部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，按照流域综合规划、水资源保护规划和经济社会发展要求，拟定国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的水功能区划，报国务院批准。跨省、自治区、直辖市的其他江河、湖泊的水功能区划，由有关流域管理机构会同江河、湖泊所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门、环境保护行政主管部门和其他有关部门拟定，分别经有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审查提出意见后，由国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院环境保护行政主管部门审核，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准。  
前款规定以外的其他江河、湖泊的水功能区划，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级人民政府环境保护行政主管部门和有关部门拟定，报同级人民政府或者其授权的部门批准，并报上一级水行政主管部门和环境保护行政主管部门备案。  
县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构应当按照水功能区对水质的要求和水体的自然净化能力，核定该水域的纳污能力，向环境保护行政主管部门提出该水域的限制排污总量意见。  
县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和流域管理机构应当对水功能区的水质状况进行监测，发现重点污染物排放总量超过控制指标的，或者水功能区的水质未达到水域使用功能对水质的要求的，应当及时报告有关人民政府采取治理措施，并向环境保护行政主管部门通报。

**Article 32** The water administrative department of the State Council, together with the environmental protection administrative department of the State Council, relevant departments, and the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall, in accordance with comprehensive river basin plans, water resource protection plans and the requirements of economic and social development, formulate water function zoning for major rivers and lakes designated by the State, and submit it to the State Council for approval. The water function zoning for other rivers and lakes that cross provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the relevant river basin management agencies jointly with the water administrative departments, environmental protection administrative departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government where the rivers and lakes are located, and after being examined and comments submitted by the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, shall be reviewed by the water administrative department of the State Council together with the environmental protection administrative department of the State Council, and submitted to the State Council or its authorized department for approval.  
The water function zoning for other rivers and lakes not covered by the preceding paragraph shall be formulated by the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level jointly with the environmental protection administrative departments and other relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, and shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level or its authorized department for approval, and shall be reported to the water administrative department and the environmental protection administrative department at the next higher level for record.  
The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level or the river basin management agencies shall, in accordance with the water quality requirements of the water function zones and the natural purification capacity of the water bodies, verify the pollution-carrying capacity of such waters and submit to the environmental protection administrative department opinions on the total discharge limits of pollutants for such waters.  
The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level and the river basin management agencies shall monitor the water quality conditions of water function zones; if they find that the total discharge of key pollutants exceeds the control targets, or that the water quality of a water function zone fails to meet the water quality requirements for the use function of the waters, they shall promptly report to the relevant people's government for the adoption of remedial measures and shall notify the environmental protection administrative department.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立饮用水水源保护区制度。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当划定饮用水水源保护区，并采取措施，防止水源枯竭和水体污染，保证城乡居民饮用水安全。

**Article 33** The State establishes a system for the protection of drinking water source reserves. The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall designate drinking water source reserves and take measures to prevent water source depletion and water pollution, and ensure the safety of drinking water for urban and rural residents.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　禁止在饮用水水源保护区内设置排污口。  
在江河、湖泊新建、改建或者扩大排污口，应当经过有管辖权的水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构同意，由环境保护行政主管部门负责对该建设项目的环境影响报告书进行审批。

**Article 34** No sewage outlets shall be set up within drinking water source reserves.  
The establishment, modification or expansion of sewage outlets in rivers or lakes shall be subject to the consent of the water administrative department or river basin management agency having jurisdiction, and the environmental protection administrative department shall be responsible for examining and approving the environmental impact report for the construction project.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　从事工程建设，占用农业灌溉水源、灌排工程设施，或者对原有灌溉用水、供水水源有不利影响的，建设单位应当采取相应的补救措施；造成损失的，依法给予补偿。

**Article 35** Where engineering construction occupies agricultural irrigation water sources or irrigation and drainage engineering facilities, or has an adverse effect on existing irrigation water or water supply sources, the construction entity shall take corresponding remedial measures; if losses are caused, compensation shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　在地下水超采地区，县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，严格控制开采地下水。在地下水严重超采地区，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以划定地下水禁止开采或者限制开采区。在沿海地区开采地下水，应当经过科学论证，并采取措施，防止地面沉降和海水入侵。

**Article 36** In areas where groundwater is over-exploited, local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strictly control the extraction of groundwater. In areas where groundwater is severely over-exploited, with the approval of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, areas where groundwater extraction is prohibited or restricted may be designated. In coastal areas, the extraction of groundwater shall be subject to scientific demonstration, and measures shall be taken to prevent land subsidence and seawater intrusion.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　禁止在江河、湖泊、水库、运河、渠道内弃置、堆放阻碍行洪的物体和种植阻碍行洪的林木及高秆作物。  
禁止在河道管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物以及从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动。

**Article 37** It shall be prohibited to discard or pile up objects that obstruct flood discharge, or to plant trees or tall crops that obstruct flood discharge, in rivers, lakes, reservoirs, canals or channels.  
It shall be prohibited, within the scope of river course management, to construct buildings or structures that obstruct flood discharge, or to engage in activities that affect the stability of river regimes, endanger the safety of river banks and levees, or otherwise obstruct flood discharge in river courses.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在河道管理范围内建设桥梁、码头和其他拦河、跨河、临河建筑物、构筑物，铺设跨河管道、电缆，应当符合国家规定的防洪标准和其他有关的技术要求，工程建设方案应当依照防洪法的有关规定报经有关水行政主管部门审查同意。  
因建设前款工程设施，需要扩建、改建、拆除或者损坏原有水工程设施的，建设单位应当负担扩建、改建的费用和损失补偿。但是，原有工程设施属于违法工程的除外。

**Article 38** The construction of bridges, wharves and other barrage, river-crossing or riverside buildings and structures, and the laying of river-crossing pipelines and cables within the river course management scope shall comply with the flood control standards and other relevant technical requirements prescribed by the State. Construction plans shall be submitted to the relevant water administrative department for review and approval in accordance with the provisions of the Flood Control Law.  
Where the construction of the engineering facilities mentioned in the preceding paragraph necessitates the expansion, modification, removal or damage of existing water engineering facilities, the construction entity shall bear the costs of expansion or modification and compensation for losses, except where the existing facilities are illegal.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家实行河道采砂许可制度。河道采砂许可制度实施办法，由国务院规定。  
在河道管理范围内采砂，影响河势稳定或者危及堤防安全的，有关县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门应当划定禁采区和规定禁采期，并予以公告。

**Article 39** The State implements a permit system for sand mining in river courses. The measures for implementing the permit system for sand mining in river courses shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
Where sand mining within the scope of river course management affects the stability of the river regime or endangers the safety of levees, the relevant water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall designate prohibited mining areas and periods for prohibition, and shall make an announcement.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　禁止围湖造地。已经围垦的，应当按照国家规定的防洪标准有计划地退地还湖。  
禁止围垦河道。确需围垦的，应当经过科学论证，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府水行政主管部门或者国务院水行政主管部门同意后，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 40** It shall be prohibited to reclaim land from lakes by enclosure. Where land has already been reclaimed, it shall be returned to the lake in a planned manner in accordance with the flood control standards prescribed by the State.  
It shall be prohibited to reclaim land by enclosing river courses. Where reclamation is truly necessary, it shall be subject to scientific demonstration and, after obtaining the consent of the water administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, or the water administrative department of the State Council, shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　单位和个人有保护水工程的义务，不得侵占、毁坏堤防、护岸、防汛、水文监测、水文地质监测等工程设施。

**Article 41** Entities and individuals shall have the obligation to protect water engineering facilities and shall not occupy or damage embankments, revetments, flood control, hydrological monitoring, hydrogeological monitoring and other engineering facilities.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，保障本行政区域内水工程，特别是水坝和堤防的安全，限期消除险情。水行政主管部门应当加强对水工程安全的监督管理。

**Article 42** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to ensure the safety of water projects within their respective administrative regions, especially dams and embankments, and shall eliminate dangers within a prescribed time limit. The water administrative departments shall strengthen the supervision and management of water project safety.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家对水工程实施保护。国家所有的水工程应当按照国务院的规定划定工程管理和保护范围。  
国务院水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构管理的水工程，由主管部门或者流域管理机构商有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府划定工程管理和保护范围。  
前款规定以外的其他水工程，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市人民政府的规定，划定工程保护范围和保护职责。  
在水工程保护范围内，禁止从事影响水工程运行和危害水工程安全的爆破、打井、采石、取土等活动。

**Article 43** The State protects water projects. For water projects owned by the State, the scope of project management and protection shall be delimited in accordance with the provisions of the State Council.  
For water projects managed by the water administrative department of the State Council or river basin management agencies, the competent department or the river basin management agency shall, in consultation with the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, delimit the scope of project management and protection.  
For other water projects not covered by the preceding paragraph, the scope of project protection and protection responsibilities shall be delimited in accordance with the provisions of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
Within the scope of water project protection, activities such as blasting, well drilling, quarrying, and earth extraction that affect the operation of water projects or endanger the safety of water projects shall be prohibited.

### 第五章　水资源配置和节约使用

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院发展计划主管部门和国务院水行政主管部门负责全国水资源的宏观调配。全国的和跨省、自治区、直辖市的水中长期供求规划，由国务院水行政主管部门会同有关部门制订，经国务院发展计划主管部门审查批准后执行。地方的水中长期供求规划，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同同级有关部门依据上一级水中长期供求规划和本地区的实际情况制订，经本级人民政府发展计划主管部门审查批准后执行。  
水中长期供求规划应当依据水的供求现状、国民经济和社会发展规划、流域规划、区域规划，按照水资源供需协调、综合平衡、保护生态、厉行节约、合理开源的原则制定。

**Article 44** The development planning department of the State Council and the water administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the macro-allocation of water resources nationwide. The medium and long-term water supply and demand plans for the whole country and for cross-province, autonomous region and municipality directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the water administrative department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments, and after being examined and approved by the development planning department of the State Council, shall be implemented. Local medium and long-term water supply and demand plans shall be formulated by the water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level jointly with the relevant departments at the same level, based on the medium and long-term water supply and demand plan at the next higher level and the actual local conditions, and after being examined and approved by the development planning department of the people's government at the same level, shall be implemented.  
Medium and long-term water supply and demand plans shall be formulated in accordance with the principles of coordinating water supply and demand, comprehensive balance, ecological protection, strict economy, and reasonable development of new sources, based on the current status of water supply and demand, national economic and social development plans, river basin plans, and regional plans.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　调蓄径流和分配水量，应当依据流域规划和水中长期供求规划，以流域为单元制定水量分配方案。  
跨省、自治区、直辖市的水量分配方案和旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案，由流域管理机构商有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制订，报国务院或者其授权的部门批准后执行。其他跨行政区域的水量分配方案和旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案，由共同的上一级人民政府水行政主管部门商有关地方人民政府制订，报本级人民政府批准后执行。  
水量分配方案和旱情紧急情况下的水量调度预案经批准后，有关地方人民政府必须执行。  
在不同行政区域之间的边界河流上建设水资源开发、利用项目，应当符合该流域经批准的水量分配方案，由有关县级以上地方人民政府报共同的上一级人民政府水行政主管部门或者有关流域管理机构批准。

**Article 45** The regulation of runoff and the allocation of water quantities shall be based on river basin plans and medium and long-term water supply and demand plans, and water allocation plans shall be formulated with the river basin as a unit.  
Water allocation plans and water scheduling contingency plans for drought emergencies for cross-province, autonomous region and municipality directly under the Central Government shall be formulated by the river basin management agencies in consultation with the people's governments of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and after being approved by the State Council or its authorized department, shall be implemented. Water allocation plans and water scheduling contingency plans for drought emergencies for other cross-administrative regions shall be formulated by the common water administrative department at the next higher level in consultation with the relevant local people's governments, and after being approved by the people's government at the same level, shall be implemented.  
Once the water allocation plans and water scheduling contingency plans for drought emergencies are approved, the relevant local people's governments must implement them.  
The construction of water resources development and utilization projects on boundary rivers between different administrative regions shall conform to the approved water allocation plan for the basin, and shall be approved by the common water administrative department at the next higher level of the relevant people's governments at or above the county level or the relevant river basin management agency.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构应当根据批准的水量分配方案和年度预测来水量，制定年度水量分配方案和调度计划，实施水量统一调度；有关地方人民政府必须服从。  
国家确定的重要江河、湖泊的年度水量分配方案，应当纳入国家的国民经济和社会发展年度计划。

**Article 46** The water administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level or the river basin management agencies shall, based on the approved water allocation plans and the forecasted incoming water volume for the year, formulate annual water allocation plans and scheduling plans, and implement unified water scheduling; the relevant local people's governments must comply.  
The annual water allocation plans for major rivers and lakes designated by the State shall be incorporated into the national annual plan for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家对用水实行总量控制和定额管理相结合的制度。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府有关行业主管部门应当制订本行政区域内行业用水定额，报同级水行政主管部门和质量监督检验行政主管部门审核同意后，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公布，并报国务院水行政主管部门和国务院质量监督检验行政主管部门备案。  
县级以上地方人民政府发展计划主管部门会同同级水行政主管部门，根据用水定额、经济技术条件以及水量分配方案确定的可供本行政区域使用的水量，制定年度用水计划，对本行政区域内的年度用水实行总量控制。

**Article 47** The State implements a system combining total volume control and quota management for water use.  
The relevant competent departments of industry of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate water use quotas for their respective administrative regions, and after being examined and consented to by the water administrative department and the quality supervision and inspection administrative department at the same level, shall be published by the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and shall be reported to the water administrative department of the State Council and the quality supervision and inspection administrative department of the State Council for record.  
The development planning departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, together with the water administrative departments at the same level, shall, based on the water use quotas, economic and technical conditions, and the water quantity available for use in the administrative region as determined by the water allocation plan, formulate annual water use plans and implement total volume control over annual water use within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　直接从江河、湖泊或者地下取用水资源的单位和个人，应当按照国家取水许可制度和水资源有偿使用制度的规定，向水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构申请领取取水许可证，并缴纳水资源费，取得取水权。但是，家庭生活和零星散养、圈养畜禽饮用等少量取水的除外。  
实施取水许可制度和征收管理水资源费的具体办法，由国务院规定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于废止1997年7月1日至2011年12月31日期间发布的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件（第十批）的决定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于废止1997年7月1日至2011年12月31日期间发布的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件（第十批）的决定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 48** Entities and individuals that directly extract water from rivers, lakes or groundwater shall, in accordance with the provisions of the national water-taking permit system and the paid-use system for water resources, apply to the water administrative department or the river basin management agency for a water-taking permit and pay water resource fees, thereby obtaining the water-taking right, except for small amounts of water taken for household use, and for drinking water for scattered and penned livestock and poultry.  
The specific measures for implementing the water-taking permit system and for collecting and managing water resource fees shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　用水应当计量，并按照批准的用水计划用水。  
用水实行计量收费和超定额累进加价制度。

**Article 49** Water use shall be metered and shall be in accordance with the approved water use plan.  
A system of metered charging for water use and progressive surcharges for exceeding quotas shall be implemented.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　各级人民政府应当推行节水灌溉方式和节水技术，对农业蓄水、输水工程采取必要的防渗漏措施，提高农业用水效率。

**Article 50** People's governments at all levels shall promote water-saving irrigation methods and water-saving technologies, adopt necessary anti-seepage measures for agricultural water storage and conveyance projects, and improve agricultural water use efficiency.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　工业用水应当采用先进技术、工艺和设备，增加循环用水次数，提高水的重复利用率。  
国家逐步淘汰落后的、耗水量高的工艺、设备和产品，具体名录由国务院经济综合主管部门会同国务院水行政主管部门和有关部门制定并公布。生产者、销售者或者生产经营中的使用者应当在规定的时间内停止生产、销售或者使用列入名录的工艺、设备和产品。

**Article 51** Industrial water use shall adopt advanced technologies, processes and equipment, increase the number of water recycling cycles, and improve the rate of water reuse.  
The State shall gradually eliminate outdated technologies, equipment and products with high water consumption. The specific catalogues shall be formulated and published by the comprehensive economic department of the State Council jointly with the water administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments. Producers, sellers or users in the course of production or business operations shall stop producing, selling or using the technologies, equipment and products listed in the catalogues within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　城市人民政府应当因地制宜采取有效措施，推广节水型生活用水器具，降低城市供水管网漏失率，提高生活用水效率；加强城市污水集中处理，鼓励使用再生水，提高污水再生利用率。

**Article 52** Municipal people's governments shall take effective measures according to local conditions, promote water-saving household water appliances, reduce the leakage rate of urban water supply networks, and improve domestic water use efficiency; they shall strengthen the centralized treatment of urban sewage, encourage the use of reclaimed water, and improve the rate of sewage reuse.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　新建、扩建、改建建设项目，应当制订节水措施方案，配套建设节水设施。节水设施应当与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产。  
供水企业和自建供水设施的单位应当加强供水设施的维护管理，减少水的漏失。

**Article 53** For new construction, expansion or renovation projects, water conservation measures shall be formulated and water conservation facilities shall be built concurrently. Water conservation facilities shall be designed, constructed and put into operation simultaneously with the main project.  
Water supply enterprises and entities that build their own water supply facilities shall strengthen the maintenance and management of water supply facilities to reduce water leakage.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　各级人民政府应当积极采取措施，改善城乡居民的饮用水条件。

**Article 54** People's governments at all levels shall actively take measures to improve the drinking water conditions of urban and rural residents.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　使用水工程供应的水，应当按照国家规定向供水单位缴纳水费。供水价格应当按照补偿成本、合理收益、优质优价、公平负担的原则确定。具体办法由省级以上人民政府价格主管部门会同同级水行政主管部门或者其他供水行政主管部门依据职权制定。

**Article 55** Entities and individuals using water supplied by water projects shall pay water fees to the water supply entity in accordance with State regulations. The water supply price shall be determined in accordance with the principles of compensating costs, reasonable profit, high quality with high price, and fair burden. The specific measures shall be formulated by the price departments of people's governments at or above the provincial level jointly with the water administrative departments at the same level or other water supply competent departments according to their functions.

### 第六章　水事纠纷处理与执法监督检查

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　不同行政区域之间发生水事纠纷的，应当协商处理；协商不成的，由上一级人民政府裁决，有关各方必须遵照执行。在水事纠纷解决前，未经各方达成协议或者共同的上一级人民政府批准，在行政区域交界线两侧一定范围内，任何一方不得修建排水、阻水、取水和截（蓄）水工程，不得单方面改变水的现状。

**Article 56** Where a water dispute arises between different administrative regions, it shall be resolved through consultation; if consultation fails, the people's government at the next higher level shall make a ruling, and all parties concerned must comply. Before the water dispute is resolved, and without an agreement among the parties or approval from the common people's government at the next higher level, no party shall construct drainage, water-blocking, water-drawing or water-impounding (storage) projects within a certain range on both sides of the boundary line between the administrative regions, or unilaterally change the existing state of water.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　单位之间、个人之间、单位与个人之间发生的水事纠纷，应当协商解决；当事人不愿协商或者协商不成的，可以申请县级以上地方人民政府或者其授权的部门调解，也可以直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼。县级以上地方人民政府或者其授权的部门调解不成的，当事人可以向人民法院提起民事诉讼。  
在水事纠纷解决前，当事人不得单方面改变现状。

**Article 57** Where a water dispute arises between entities, between individuals, or between an entity and an individual, it shall be resolved through consultation; if the parties are unwilling to consult or consultation fails, they may apply to the local people's government at or above the county level or its authorized department for mediation, or may directly file a civil action in the People's Court. If mediation by the local people's government at or above the county level or its authorized department fails, the parties may file a civil action in the People's Court.  
Before the water dispute is resolved, the parties shall not unilaterally change the existing state of affairs.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上人民政府或者其授权的部门在处理水事纠纷时，有权采取临时处置措施，有关各方或者当事人必须服从。

**Article 58** When handling a water dispute, the people's government at or above the county level or its authorized department shall have the power to take interim measures, and all parties concerned or the parties must comply.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门和流域管理机构应当对违反本法的行为加强监督检查并依法进行查处。  
水政监督检查人员应当忠于职守，秉公执法。

**Article 59** The water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level and river basin management agencies shall strengthen supervision and inspection of violations of this Law and shall investigate and deal with them according to law.  
Water administration supervisory personnel shall be devoted to their duties and enforce the law impartially.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门、流域管理机构及其水政监督检查人员履行本法规定的监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查单位提供有关文件、证照、资料；  
（二）要求被检查单位就执行本法的有关问题作出说明；  
（三）进入被检查单位的生产场所进行调查；  
（四）责令被检查单位停止违反本法的行为，履行法定义务。

**Article 60** When performing the supervision and inspection functions provided for in this Law, the water administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level, river basin management agencies and their water administration supervisory personnel shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) to require the entity under inspection to provide relevant documents, licenses and materials;  
(2) to require the entity under inspection to make explanations on issues relating to the implementation of this Law;  
(3) to enter the production premises of the entity under inspection for investigation;  
(4) to order the entity under inspection to cease acts violating this Law and to perform legal obligations.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　有关单位或者个人对水政监督检查人员的监督检查工作应当给予配合，不得拒绝或者阻碍水政监督检查人员依法执行职务。

**Article 61** The relevant entities or individuals shall cooperate with the supervision and inspection work of water administration supervisory personnel and shall not refuse or obstruct water administration supervisory personnel from performing their duties according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　水政监督检查人员在履行监督检查职责时，应当向被检查单位或者个人出示执法证件。

**Article 62** When performing supervision and inspection duties, water administration supervisory personnel shall present law enforcement identification documents to the entity or individual under inspection.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　县级以上人民政府或者上级水行政主管部门发现本级或者下级水行政主管部门在监督检查工作中有违法或者失职行为的，应当责令其限期改正。

**Article 63** Where a people's government at or above the county level or a water administrative department at a higher level discovers that the water administrative department at the same level or at a lower level has engaged in illegal or derelict conduct during supervision and inspection, it shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　水行政主管部门或者其他有关部门以及水工程管理单位及其工作人员，利用职务上的便利收取他人财物、其他好处或者玩忽职守，对不符合法定条件的单位或者个人核发许可证、签署审查同意意见，不按照水量分配方案分配水量，不按照国家有关规定收取水资源费，不履行监督职责，或者发现违法行为不予查处，造成严重后果，构成犯罪的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依照刑法的有关规定追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，依法给予行政处分。

**Article 64** Where a water administrative department or other relevant department, or a water project management entity and its staff members, take advantage of their positions to accept property or other benefits from others, or neglect their duties, by issuing permits or signing review consent opinions to entities or individuals that do not meet the statutory conditions, failing to allocate water in accordance with water allocation plans, failing to collect water resource fees in accordance with State regulations, failing to perform supervision duties, or failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered, thereby causing serious consequences that constitute a crime, the persons in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the conduct does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在河道管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物，或者从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，限期拆除违法建筑物、构筑物，恢复原状；逾期不拆除、不恢复原状的，强行拆除，所需费用由违法单位或者个人负担，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
未经水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构同意，擅自修建水工程，或者建设桥梁、码头和其他拦河、跨河、临河建筑物、构筑物，铺设跨河管道、电缆，且防洪法未作规定的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，限期补办有关手续；逾期不补办或者补办未被批准的，责令限期拆除违法建筑物、构筑物；逾期不拆除的，强行拆除，所需费用由违法单位或者个人负担，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
虽经水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构同意，但未按照要求修建前款所列工程设施的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令限期改正，按照情节轻重，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 65** Where buildings or structures that obstruct flood discharge are constructed within the scope of river course management, or activities are engaged in that affect the stability of river regimes, endanger the safety of river banks and levees, or otherwise obstruct flood discharge in river courses, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act, and order removal of the illegal buildings or structures and restoration to the original condition within a prescribed time limit; if removal and restoration are not carried out within the prescribed time limit, forcible removal shall be carried out, with the costs borne by the illegal entity or individual, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a water project is constructed without the consent of the water administrative department or river basin management agency, or bridges, wharves and other barrage, river-crossing or riverside buildings and structures are constructed, or river-crossing pipelines and cables are laid, without such consent, and the Flood Control Law does not provide otherwise, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act and completion of the relevant procedures within a prescribed time limit; if the procedures are not completed within the prescribed time limit or are not approved, it shall order removal of the illegal buildings or structures within a prescribed time limit; if removal is not carried out within the prescribed time limit, forcible removal shall be carried out, with the costs borne by the illegal entity or individual, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where the construction of the engineering facilities listed in the preceding paragraph has been consented to by the water administrative department or river basin management agency, but the construction fails to meet the requirements, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order corrections within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, depending on the seriousness of the circumstances.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有下列行为之一，且防洪法未作规定的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，限期清除障碍或者采取其他补救措施，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）在江河、湖泊、水库、运河、渠道内弃置、堆放阻碍行洪的物体和种植阻碍行洪的林木及高秆作物的；  
（二）围湖造地或者未经批准围垦河道的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/太湖流域管理条例.md#art-67" title="被《太湖流域管理条例》第67条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 66** Where any of the following acts is committed, and the Flood Control Law does not provide otherwise, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act, and order removal of the obstacle or adoption of other remedial measures within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) discarding or piling up objects that obstruct flood discharge, or planting trees or tall crops that obstruct flood discharge, in rivers, lakes, reservoirs, canals or channels;  
(2) reclaiming land from lakes by enclosure, or reclaiming land by enclosing river courses without approval.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　在饮用水水源保护区内设置排污口的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令限期拆除、恢复原状；逾期不拆除、不恢复原状的，强行拆除、恢复原状，并处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
未经水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构审查同意，擅自在江河、湖泊新建、改建或者扩大排污口的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 67** Where a sewage outlet is set up within a drinking water source reserve, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order removal within a prescribed time limit and restoration to the original condition; if removal and restoration are not carried out within the prescribed time limit, forcible removal and restoration shall be carried out, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
Where a sewage outlet is established, modified or expanded in a river or lake without review and consent by the water administrative department or river basin management agency, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act and restoration to the original condition within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　生产、销售或者在生产经营中使用国家明令淘汰的落后的、耗水量高的工艺、设备和产品的，由县级以上地方人民政府经济综合主管部门责令停止生产、销售或者使用，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 68** Where any person produces, sells or, in the course of production or business operations, uses outdated technologies, equipment or products with high water consumption that are expressly eliminated by the State, the comprehensive economic department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of production, sale or use, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施，处二万元以上十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，吊销其取水许可证：  
（一）未经批准擅自取水的；  
（二）未依照批准的取水许可规定条件取水的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例.md#art-48" title="被《取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 69** Where any of the following acts is committed, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act, order adoption of remedial measures within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the water-taking permit shall be revoked:  
(1) extracting water without approval;  
(2) extracting water not in accordance with the conditions specified in the approved water-taking permit.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　拒不缴纳、拖延缴纳或者拖欠水资源费的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令限期缴纳；逾期不缴纳的，从滞纳之日起按日加收滞纳部分千分之二的滞纳金，并处应缴或者补缴水资源费一倍以上五倍以下的罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/自然资源/取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例.md#art-54" title="被《取水许可和水资源费征收管理条例》第54条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 70** Where any person refuses to pay, delays payment, or is in arrears with water resource fees, the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order payment within a prescribed time limit; if payment is not made within the prescribed time limit, a late payment penalty of 2/1,000 of the overdue amount per day shall be imposed from the date of default, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of water resource fees payable or supplementary payable shall be imposed.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　建设项目的节水设施没有建成或者没有达到国家规定的要求，擅自投入使用的，由县级以上人民政府有关部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止使用，限期改正，处五万元以上十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 71** Where water conservation facilities for a construction project are not built or do not meet the requirements prescribed by the State, and the project is put into use without authorization, the relevant department of the people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of use and corrections within a prescribed time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　有下列行为之一，构成犯罪的，依照刑法的有关规定追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚，且防洪法未作规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者流域管理机构依据职权，责令停止违法行为，采取补救措施，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；违反治安管理处罚法的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；给他人造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）侵占、毁坏水工程及堤防、护岸等有关设施，毁坏防汛、水文监测、水文地质监测设施的；  
（二）在水工程保护范围内，从事影响水工程运行和危害水工程安全的爆破、打井、采石、取土等活动的。

**Article 72** Where any of the following acts is committed, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the conduct does not constitute a crime and the Flood Control Law does not provide otherwise, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the river basin management agency shall, according to its functions, order cessation of the illegal act, adoption of remedial measures, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the conduct violates the Public Security Administration Punishments Law, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration punishment according to law; if losses are caused to others, compensation liability shall be borne according to law:  
(1) occupying or damaging water projects and embankments, revetments and other related facilities, or damaging flood control, hydrological monitoring or hydrogeological monitoring facilities;  
(2) engaging in activities such as blasting, well drilling, quarrying or earth extraction within the scope of water project protection, which affect the operation of water projects or endanger the safety of water projects.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　侵占、盗窃或者抢夺防汛物资，防洪排涝、农田水利、水文监测和测量以及其他水工程设备和器材，贪污或者挪用国家救灾、抢险、防汛、移民安置和补偿及其他水利建设款物，构成犯罪的，依照刑法的有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 73** Where any person occupies, steals or robs flood control materials, flood control and drainage, farmland water conservancy, hydrological monitoring and surveying, and other water project equipment and materials, or embezzles or misappropriates State funds for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, resettlement and compensation, and other water conservancy construction funds and materials, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　在水事纠纷发生及其处理过程中煽动闹事、结伙斗殴、抢夺或者损坏公私财物、非法限制他人人身自由，构成犯罪的，依照刑法的有关规定追究刑事责任；尚不够刑事处罚的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 74** Where any person incites disturbances, engages in affrays, robs or damages public or private property, or unlawfully restricts the personal freedom of others during the occurrence and handling of a water dispute, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law; if the conduct does not constitute a crime, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration punishment according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　不同行政区域之间发生水事纠纷，有下列行为之一的，对负有责任的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予行政处分：  
（一）拒不执行水量分配方案和水量调度预案的；  
（二）拒不服从水量统一调度的；  
（三）拒不执行上一级人民政府的裁决的；  
（四）在水事纠纷解决前，未经各方达成协议或者上一级人民政府批准，单方面违反本法规定改变水的现状的。

**Article 75** Where a water dispute arises between different administrative regions and any of the following acts is committed, administrative sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the persons in charge and other directly responsible personnel:  
(1) refusing to implement the water allocation plan or the water scheduling contingency plan;  
(2) refusing to comply with unified water scheduling;  
(3) refusing to implement the ruling of the people's government at the next higher level;  
(4) before the water dispute is resolved, without an agreement among the parties or approval from the people's government at the next higher level, unilaterally changing the existing state of water in violation of the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　引水、截（蓄）水、排水，损害公共利益或者他人合法权益的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 76** Where any person diverts, impounds (stores) or drains water, thereby harming the public interest or the lawful rights and interests of others, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　对违反[本法第三十九条](#art-39)有关河道采砂许可制度规定的行政处罚，由国务院规定。

**Article 77** The administrative penalties for violating the provisions of Article 39 of this Law on the permit system for sand mining in river courses shall be prescribed by the State Council.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的与国际或者国境边界河流、湖泊有关的国际条约、协定与中华人民共和国法律有不同规定的，适用国际条约、协定的规定。但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 78** Where an international treaty or agreement concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China relating to international or boundary rivers or lakes contains provisions differing from those of the laws of the People's Republic of China, the provisions of the international treaty or agreement shall apply, except for clauses to which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　本法所称水工程，是指在江河、湖泊和地下水源上开发、利用、控制、调配和保护水资源的各类工程。

**Article 79** For the purposes of this Law, water projects refer to various types of projects for the development, utilization, control, allocation and protection of water resources in rivers, lakes and groundwater sources.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　海水的开发、利用、保护和管理，依照有关法律的规定执行。

**Article 80** The development, utilization, protection and management of seawater shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　从事防洪活动，依照防洪法的规定执行。  
水污染防治，依照水污染防治法的规定执行。

**Article 81** Flood control activities shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Flood Control Law.  
Water pollution prevention and control shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本法自2002年10月1日起施行。

**Article 82** This Law shall come into force as of October 1, 2002.
