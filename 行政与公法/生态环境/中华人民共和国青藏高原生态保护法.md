# 中华人民共和国青藏高原生态保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-04-26  
**生效日期**：2023-09-01

> （2023年4月26日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　生态安全布局 第三章　生态保护修复 第四章　生态风险防控 第五章　保障与监督 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强青藏高原生态保护，防控生态风险，保障生态安全，建设国家生态文明高地，促进经济社会可持续发展，实现人与自然和谐共生，制定本法。 第二条　从事或者涉及青藏高原生态保护相关活动，适用本法；本法未作规定的，适用其他有关法律的规定。 本法所称青藏高原，是指西藏自治区、青海省的全部行政区域和新疆维吾尔自治区、四川省、甘肃省、云南省的相关县级行政区域。 第三条　青藏高原生态保护应当尊重自然、顺应自然、保护自然；坚持生态保护第一，自然恢复为主，守住自然生态安全边界；坚持统筹协调、分类施策、科学防控、系统治理。 第四条　国家建立青藏高原生态保护协调机制，统筹指导、综合协调青藏高原生态保护工作，审议青藏高原生态保护重大政策、重大规划、重大项目，协调跨地区跨部门重大问题，督促检查相关重要工作的落实情况。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强青藏高原生态保护，防控生态风险，保障生态安全，建设国家生态文明高地，促进经济社会可持续发展，实现人与自然和谐共生，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of strengthening ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, preventing and controlling ecological risks, ensuring ecological security, building a national ecological civilization highland, promoting sustainable economic and social development, and achieving harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事或者涉及青藏高原生态保护相关活动，适用本法；本法未作规定的，适用其他有关法律的规定。  
本法所称青藏高原，是指西藏自治区、青海省的全部行政区域和新疆维吾尔自治区、四川省、甘肃省、云南省的相关县级行政区域。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities engaged in or involving ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau; matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of other relevant laws.  
For the purposes of this Law, the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau refers to the entire administrative areas of Xizang Autonomous Region and Qinghai Province, as well as the relevant county-level administrative areas of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, Sichuan Province, Gansu Province, and Yunnan Province.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　青藏高原生态保护应当尊重自然、顺应自然、保护自然；坚持生态保护第一，自然恢复为主，守住自然生态安全边界；坚持统筹协调、分类施策、科学防控、系统治理。

**Article 3** Ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall respect nature, follow its laws, and protect nature; adhere to giving priority to ecological protection and natural restoration as the main approach, and guard the boundary of natural ecological security; adhere to overall coordination, classified measures, scientific prevention and control, and systematic governance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家建立青藏高原生态保护协调机制，统筹指导、综合协调青藏高原生态保护工作，审议青藏高原生态保护重大政策、重大规划、重大项目，协调跨地区跨部门重大问题，督促检查相关重要工作的落实情况。  
国务院有关部门按照职责分工，负责青藏高原生态保护相关工作。

**Article 4** The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for the ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau to provide overall guidance and comprehensive coordination for the ecological protection work of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, review major policies, major plans, and major projects concerning ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, coordinate major cross-regional and cross-departmental issues, and supervise and inspect the implementation of relevant important work.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the relevant work concerning ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　青藏高原地方各级人民政府应当落实本行政区域的生态保护修复、生态风险防控、优化产业结构和布局、维护青藏高原生态安全等责任。  
青藏高原相关地方根据需要在地方性法规和地方政府规章制定、规划编制、监督执法等方面加强协作，协同推进青藏高原生态保护。

**Article 5** The local people's governments at all levels in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall fulfill responsibilities such as ecological protection and restoration, ecological risk prevention and control, optimization of industrial structure and layout, and maintenance of ecological security of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau within their respective administrative areas.  
Relevant localities in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, as needed, strengthen collaboration in formulating local regulations and local government rules, planning, supervision and law enforcement, and jointly promote ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当将青藏高原生态保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国务院有关部门按照职责分工，组织编制青藏高原生态保护修复等相关专项规划，组织实施重大生态修复等工程，统筹推进青藏高原生态保护修复等工作。青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府按照国家有关规定，在本行政区域组织实施青藏高原生态保护修复等相关专项规划。编制青藏高原生态保护修复等相关专项规划，应当进行科学论证评估。

**Article 6** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall incorporate the ecological protection work of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau into the national economic and social development plans.  
The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize the formulation of relevant special plans such as ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, organize the implementation of major ecological restoration projects, and coordinate the promotion of ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, organize the implementation of relevant special plans such as ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau within their administrative areas. Scientific demonstration and evaluation shall be carried out when formulating relevant special plans such as ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家加强青藏高原土地、森林、草原、河流、湖泊、湿地、冰川、荒漠、野生动植物等自然资源状况和生态环境状况调查，开展区域资源环境承载能力和国土空间开发适宜性评价，健全青藏高原生态环境、自然资源、生物多样性、水文、气象、地质、水土保持、自然灾害等监测网络体系，推进综合监测、协同监测和常态化监测。调查、评价和监测信息应当按照国家有关规定共享。

**Article 7** The State shall strengthen surveys on the status of natural resources such as land, forests, grasslands, rivers, lakes, wetlands, glaciers, deserts, and wildlife, and on the ecological environment of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, carry out assessments of regional resource and environmental carrying capacity and suitability of territorial space development, improve the monitoring network system for ecological environment, natural resources, biodiversity, hydrology, meteorology, geology, soil and water conservation, natural disasters, etc., of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and promote comprehensive, collaborative, and routine monitoring. The survey, assessment, and monitoring information shall be shared in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持开展青藏高原科学考察与研究，加强青藏高原气候变化、生物多样性、生态保护修复、水文水资源、雪山冰川冻土、水土保持、荒漠化防治、河湖演变、地质环境、自然灾害监测预警与防治、能源和气候资源开发利用与保护、生态系统碳汇等领域的重大科技问题研究和重大科技基础设施建设，推动长期研究工作，掌握青藏高原生态本底及其变化。  
国家统筹布局青藏高原生态保护科技创新平台，加大科技专业人才培养力度，充分运用青藏高原科学考察与研究成果，推广应用先进适用技术，促进科技成果转化，发挥科技在青藏高原生态保护中的支撑作用。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support scientific expedition and research on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, strengthen research on major scientific and technological issues and construction of major scientific and technological infrastructure in fields such as climate change, biodiversity, ecological protection and restoration, hydrology and water resources, snow mountains, glaciers and permafrost, soil and water conservation, desertification control, river and lake evolution, geological environment, monitoring, early warning and prevention of natural disasters, development, utilization and protection of energy and climate resources, and ecosystem carbon sinks, promote long-term research work, and grasp the ecological background and its changes of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The State shall make overall arrangements for scientific and technological innovation platforms for ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, increase training efforts for scientific and technological professionals, fully utilize the results of scientific expedition and research on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, promote and apply advanced and applicable technologies, facilitate the transformation of scientific and technological achievements, and give play to the supporting role of science and technology in ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，保护青藏高原传统生态文化遗产，弘扬青藏高原优秀生态文化。  
国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府应当加强青藏高原生态保护宣传教育和科学普及，传播生态文明理念，倡导绿色低碳生活方式，提高全民生态文明素养，鼓励和支持单位和个人参与青藏高原生态保护相关活动。  
新闻媒体应当采取多种形式开展青藏高原生态保护宣传报道，并依法对违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 9** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels shall take effective measures to protect the traditional ecological cultural heritage of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and promote the excellent ecological culture of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen public education and scientific popularization on ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, disseminate the concept of ecological civilization, advocate green and low-carbon lifestyles, improve the ecological civilization literacy of the whole people, and encourage and support entities and individuals to participate in activities related to ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The news media shall conduct publicity and reporting on ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau in various forms, and shall exercise public opinion supervision over illegal acts in accordance with law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对在青藏高原生态保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 10** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the work of ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　生态安全布局

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家统筹青藏高原生态安全布局，推进山水林田湖草沙冰综合治理、系统治理、源头治理，实施重要生态系统保护修复重大工程，优化以水源涵养、生物多样性保护、水土保持、防风固沙、生态系统碳汇等为主要生态功能的青藏高原生态安全屏障体系，提升生态系统质量和多样性、稳定性、持续性，增强生态产品供给能力和生态系统服务功能，建设国家生态安全屏障战略地。

**Article 11** The State shall make overall arrangements for the ecological security layout of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, promote comprehensive, systematic, and source-oriented governance of mountains, rivers, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands, deserts, and glaciers, implement major projects for protection and restoration of important ecosystems, optimize the ecological security barrier system of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau with main ecological functions such as water conservation, biodiversity protection, soil and water conservation, windbreak and sand fixation, and ecosystem carbon sinks, improve the quality, diversity, stability, and sustainability of ecosystems, enhance the supply capacity of ecological products and ecosystem service functions, and build a strategic area for national ecological security barrier.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府组织编制本行政区域的国土空间规划，应当落实国家对青藏高原国土空间开发保护的有关要求，细化安排农业、生态、城镇等功能空间，统筹划定耕地和永久基本农田、生态保护红线、城镇开发边界。涉及青藏高原国土空间利用的专项规划应当与国土空间规划相衔接。

**Article 12** When organizing the formulation of territorial space plans for their respective administrative areas, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall implement the relevant requirements of the State regarding the development and protection of territorial space of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, make detailed arrangements for functional spaces such as agriculture, ecology, and urban areas, and coordinate the delimitation of arable land and permanent basic farmland, ecological protection red lines, and urban development boundaries. Special plans involving the use of territorial space of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall be coordinated with the territorial space plans.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　青藏高原国土空间开发利用活动应当符合国土空间用途管制要求。青藏高原生态空间内的用途转换，应当有利于增强森林、草原、河流、湖泊、湿地、冰川、荒漠等生态系统的生态功能。  
青藏高原省级人民政府应当加强对生态保护红线内人类活动的监督管理，定期评估生态保护成效。

**Article 13** Activities for the development and utilization of territorial space of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall comply with the requirements of territorial space use regulation. The conversion of uses within ecological spaces of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall be conducive to enhancing the ecological functions of ecosystems such as forests, grasslands, rivers, lakes, wetlands, glaciers, and deserts.  
The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall strengthen supervision and management of human activities within the ecological protection red lines, and regularly assess the effectiveness of ecological protection.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　青藏高原省级人民政府根据本行政区域的生态环境和资源利用状况，按照生态保护红线、环境质量底线、资源利用上线的要求，从严制定生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案后实施。生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单应当与国土空间规划相衔接。

**Article 14** The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, based on the ecological environment and resource utilization conditions of their respective administrative areas and in accordance with the requirements of ecological protection red lines, environmental quality bottom lines, and resource utilization upper lines, strictly formulate ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists, and submit them to the competent ecological environment department of the State Council for record before implementation. The ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists shall be coordinated with the territorial space plans.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家加强对青藏高原森林、高寒草甸、草原、河流、湖泊、湿地、雪山冰川、高原冻土、荒漠、泉域等生态系统的保护，巩固提升三江源（长江、黄河、澜沧江发源地）草原草甸湿地生态功能区、若尔盖草原湿地生态功能区、甘南黄河重要水源补给生态功能区、祁连山冰川与水源涵养生态功能区、阿尔金草原荒漠化防治生态功能区、川滇森林及生物多样性生态功能区、藏东南高原边缘森林生态功能区、藏西北羌塘高原荒漠生态功能区、珠穆朗玛峰生物多样性保护与水源涵养生态功能区等国家重点生态功能区的水源涵养、生物多样性保护、水土保持、防风固沙等生态功能。

**Article 15** The State shall strengthen the protection of ecosystems such as forests, alpine meadows, grasslands, rivers, lakes, wetlands, snow mountains and glaciers, plateau permafrost, deserts, and spring areas on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, consolidate and enhance the ecological functions such as water conservation, biodiversity protection, soil and water conservation, windbreak and sand fixation of national key ecological function zones including the Sanjiangyuan (source of the Yangtze River, Yellow River, and Lancang River) grassland meadow wetland ecological function zone, the Zoige grassland wetland ecological function zone, the Gannan Yellow River important water supply ecological function zone, the Qilian Mountain glacier and water conservation ecological function zone, the Altun grassland desertification prevention ecological function zone, the Sichuan-Yunnan forest and biodiversity ecological function zone, the southeastern Xizang plateau edge forest ecological function zone, the northwestern Xizang Qiangtang plateau desert ecological function zone, and the Mount Everest biodiversity protection and water conservation ecological function zone.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家支持青藏高原自然保护地体系建设。国务院和青藏高原省级人民政府在青藏高原重要典型生态系统的完整分布区、生态环境敏感区以及珍贵濒危或者特有野生动植物天然集中分布区和重要栖息地、重要自然遗迹、重要自然景观分布区等区域，依法设立国家公园、自然保护区、自然公园等自然保护地，推进三江源、祁连山、羌塘、珠穆朗玛峰、高黎贡山、贡嘎山等自然保护地建设，保持重要自然生态系统原真性和完整性。

**Article 16** The State shall support the construction of a system of protected areas in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with law, establish national parks, nature reserves, and natural parks and other protected areas in areas such as the complete distribution areas of important typical ecosystems, ecologically sensitive areas, and areas of natural concentration and important habitats of precious, endangered or endemic wild animals and plants, important natural relics, and important natural landscapes, advance the construction of protected areas such as Sanjiangyuan, Qilian Mountain, Qiangtang, Mount Everest, Gaoligong Mountain, and Gongga Mountain, and maintain the authenticity and integrity of important natural ecosystems.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　青藏高原产业结构和布局应当与青藏高原生态系统和资源环境承载能力相适应。国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国土空间规划要求，调整产业结构，优化生产力布局，优先发展资源节约型、环境友好型产业，适度发展生态旅游、特色文化、特色农牧业、民族特色手工业等区域特色生态产业，建立健全绿色、低碳、循环经济体系。  
在青藏高原新建、扩建产业项目应当符合区域主体功能定位和国家产业政策要求，严格执行自然资源开发、产业准入及退出规定。

**Article 17** The industrial structure and layout of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall be compatible with the carrying capacity of the ecosystem and resources and environment of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with the requirements of territorial space plans, adjust the industrial structure, optimize the distribution of productive forces, give priority to the development of resource-saving and environment-friendly industries, appropriately develop regional characteristic ecological industries such as eco-tourism, characteristic culture, characteristic agriculture and animal husbandry, and ethnic characteristic handicrafts, and establish and improve a green, low-carbon, and circular economic system.  
New or expanded industrial projects in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall conform to the regional main function positioning and the national industrial policy requirements, and strictly implement the provisions on natural resources development, industrial access and withdrawal.

### 第三章　生态保护修复

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家加强青藏高原生态保护修复，坚持山水林田湖草沙冰一体化保护修复，实行自然恢复为主、自然恢复与人工修复相结合的系统治理。

**Article 18** The State shall strengthen ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, adhere to integrated protection and restoration of mountains, rivers, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands, deserts, and glaciers, and implement systematic governance that gives priority to natural restoration and combines natural restoration with artificial restoration.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院有关部门和有关地方人民政府加强三江源地区的生态保护修复工作，对依法设立的国家公园进行系统保护和分区分类管理，科学采取禁牧封育等措施，加大退化草原、退化湿地、沙化土地治理和水土流失防治的力度，综合整治重度退化土地；严格禁止破坏生态功能或者不符合差别化管控要求的各类资源开发利用活动。

**Article 19** The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant local people's governments shall strengthen ecological protection and restoration work in the Sanjiangyuan region, carry out systematic protection and zoning and classification management of national parks established in accordance with law, scientifically adopt measures such as grazing prohibition and enclosure, intensify efforts in the control of degraded grasslands, degraded wetlands, and desertified land, and in the prevention of soil erosion, comprehensively remediate severely degraded land; and strictly prohibit all kinds of resource development and utilization activities that damage ecological functions or do not meet the requirements of differentiated control.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当建立健全青藏高原雪山冰川冻土保护制度，加强对雪山冰川冻土的监测预警和系统保护。  
青藏高原省级人民政府应当将大型冰帽冰川、小规模冰川群等划入生态保护红线，对重要雪山冰川实施封禁保护，采取有效措施，严格控制人为扰动。  
青藏高原省级人民政府应当划定冻土区保护范围，加强对多年冻土区和中深季节冻土区的保护，严格控制多年冻土区资源开发，严格审批多年冻土区城镇规划和交通、管线、输变电等重大工程项目。  
青藏高原省级人民政府应当开展雪山冰川冻土与周边生态系统的协同保护，维持有利于雪山冰川冻土保护的自然生态环境。

**Article 20** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall establish and improve a protection system for snow mountains, glaciers, and permafrost on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and strengthen monitoring, early warning, and systematic protection of snow mountains, glaciers, and permafrost.  
The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall incorporate large ice cap glaciers and small-scale glacier groups into the ecological protection red lines, implement closed protection for important snow mountains and glaciers, and take effective measures to strictly control human disturbance.  
The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall delimit the protection scope of permafrost areas, strengthen protection of permafrost areas and deep and medium seasonally frozen ground areas, strictly control resource development in permafrost areas, and strictly approve urban planning and major engineering projects such as transportation, pipelines, and power transmission in permafrost areas.  
The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall carry out coordinated protection of snow mountains, glaciers, and permafrost with surrounding ecosystems, and maintain a natural ecological environment conducive to the protection of snow mountains, glaciers, and permafrost.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原地方各级人民政府建立健全青藏高原江河、湖泊管理和保护制度，完善河湖长制，加大对长江、黄河、澜沧江、雅鲁藏布江、怒江等重点河流和青海湖、扎陵湖、鄂陵湖、色林错、纳木错、羊卓雍错、玛旁雍错等重点湖泊的保护力度。  
青藏高原河道、湖泊管理范围由有关县级以上地方人民政府依法科学划定并公布。禁止违法利用、占用青藏高原河道、湖泊水域和岸线。

**Article 21** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall establish and improve the management and protection systems for rivers and lakes on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, improve the river and lake chief system, and strengthen the protection of key rivers such as the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Lancang River, Yarlung Zangbo River, and Nujiang River, and key lakes such as Qinghai Lake, Zhaling Lake, Eling Lake, Serling Tso, Namtso, Yamdrok Yumtso, and Mapam Yumtso.  
The management scope of rivers and lakes on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall be scientifically delimited and announced by the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level in accordance with law. The illegal use or occupation of river and lake waters and shorelines on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau is prohibited.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　青藏高原水资源开发利用，应当符合流域综合规划，坚持科学开发、合理利用，统筹各类用水需求，兼顾上下游、干支流、左右岸利益，充分发挥水资源的综合效益，保障用水安全和生态安全。

**Article 22** The development and utilization of water resources on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall comply with the comprehensive basin plans, adhere to scientific development and rational utilization, coordinate various water use needs, take into account the interests of upstream and downstream, main streams and tributaries, left and right banks, give full play to the comprehensive benefits of water resources, and ensure water use security and ecological security.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家严格保护青藏高原大江大河源头等重要生态区位的天然草原，依法将维护国家生态安全、保障草原畜牧业健康发展发挥最基本、最重要作用的草原划为基本草原。青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当加强青藏高原草原保护，对基本草原实施更加严格的保护和管理，确保面积不减少、质量不下降、用途不改变。  
国家加强青藏高原高寒草甸、草原生态保护修复。青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当优化草原围栏建设，采取有效措施保护草原原生植被，科学推进退化草原生态修复工作，实施黑土滩等退化草原综合治理。

**Article 23** The State shall strictly protect natural grasslands in important ecological locations such as the sources of major rivers on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and in accordance with law, designate the grasslands that play the most fundamental and important role in maintaining national ecological security and ensuring the healthy development of grassland animal husbandry as basic grasslands. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall strengthen the protection of grasslands on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, implement stricter protection and management of basic grasslands, and ensure that the area is not reduced, the quality is not degraded, and the use is not changed.  
The State shall strengthen ecological protection and restoration of alpine meadows and grasslands on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall optimize the construction of grassland fences, take effective measures to protect the native vegetation of grasslands, scientifically promote the ecological restoration of degraded grasslands, and implement comprehensive treatment of degraded grasslands such as black soil type degraded grassland.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当统筹协调草原生态保护和畜牧业发展，结合当地实际情况，定期核定草原载畜量，落实草畜平衡，科学划定禁牧区，防止超载过牧。对严重退化、沙化、盐碱化、石漠化的草原和生态脆弱区的草原，实行禁牧、休牧制度。  
草原承包经营者应当合理利用草原，不得超过核定的草原载畜量；采取种植和储备饲草饲料、增加饲草饲料供应量、调剂处理牲畜、优化畜群结构等措施，保持草畜平衡。

**Article 24** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their relevant departments shall coordinate the ecological protection of grasslands and the development of animal husbandry, periodically verify the livestock carrying capacity of grasslands in light of local actual conditions, implement the balance between grass and livestock, scientifically delimit grazing prohibited areas, and prevent overgrazing. A system of grazing prohibition and rest grazing shall be implemented for grasslands that are severely degraded, desertified, salinized, or rocky, and for grasslands in ecologically fragile areas.  
The grassland contractors shall rationally utilize grasslands and shall not exceed the verified livestock carrying capacity; they shall take measures such as planting and storing forage and feed, increasing the supply of forage and feed, adjusting and disposing of livestock, and optimizing the herd structure to maintain the balance between grass and livestock.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家全面加强青藏高原天然林保护，严格限制采伐天然林，加强原生地带性植被保护，优化森林生态系统结构，健全重要流域防护林体系。国务院和青藏高原省级人民政府应当依法在青藏高原重要生态区、生态状况脆弱区划定公益林，实施严格管理。  
青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当科学实施国土绿化，因地制宜，合理配置乔灌草植被，优先使用乡土树种草种，提升绿化质量，加强有害生物防治和森林草原火灾防范。

**Article 25** The State shall comprehensively strengthen the protection of natural forests on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, strictly restrict the felling of natural forests, strengthen the protection of native zonal vegetation, optimize the structure of forest ecosystems, and improve the shelter forest system of important watersheds. The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with law, designate public welfare forests in important ecological areas and ecologically fragile areas on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and implement strict management.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their relevant departments shall scientifically implement territorial greening, adapt measures to local conditions, rationally allocate trees, shrubs, and grasses, give priority to the use of native tree and grass species, improve the quality of greening, and strengthen the prevention and control of harmful organisms and the prevention of forest and grassland fires.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家加强青藏高原湿地保护修复，增强湿地水源涵养、气候调节、生物多样性保护等生态功能，提升湿地固碳能力。  
青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当加强湿地保护协调工作，采取有效措施，落实湿地面积总量管控目标的要求，优化湿地保护空间布局，强化江河源头、上中游和泥炭沼泽湿地整体保护，对生态功能严重退化的湿地进行综合整治和修复。  
禁止在星宿海、扎陵湖、鄂陵湖、若尔盖等泥炭沼泽湿地开采泥炭。禁止开（围）垦、排干自然湿地等破坏湿地及其生态功能的行为。

**Article 26** The State shall strengthen the protection and restoration of wetlands on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, enhance the ecological functions of wetlands such as water conservation, climate regulation, and biodiversity protection, and improve the carbon sequestration capacity of wetlands.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall strengthen coordination for wetland protection, take effective measures, fulfill the requirements for the total area control targets of wetlands, optimize the spatial layout of wetland protection, strengthen the overall protection of river sources, upper and middle reaches, and peatland wetlands, and carry out comprehensive improvement and restoration of wetlands with severely degraded ecological functions.  
The exploitation of peat in peatland wetlands such as Xingxiu Sea, Zhaling Lake, Eling Lake, and Zoige is prohibited. Acts such as reclamation (enclosure), drainage of natural wetlands that damage wetlands and their ecological functions are prohibited.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　青藏高原地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当落实最严格耕地保护制度，采取有效措施提升耕地基础地力，增强耕地生态功能，保护和改善耕地生态环境；鼓励和支持农业生产经营者采取养用结合、盐碱地改良、生态循环、废弃物综合利用等方式，科学利用耕地，推广使用绿色、高效农业生产技术，严格控制化肥、农药施用，科学处置农用薄膜、农作物秸秆等农业废弃物。

**Article 27** The local people's governments at all levels in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their relevant departments shall implement the strictest arable land protection system, take effective measures to improve the basic fertility of arable land, enhance the ecological functions of arable land, and protect and improve the ecological environment of arable land; encourage and support agricultural producers and operators to adopt methods such as combining use with conservation, improvement of saline-alkali land, ecological circulation, and comprehensive utilization of waste, to scientifically utilize arable land, promote the use of green and efficient agricultural production technologies, strictly control the application of chemical fertilizers and pesticides, and scientifically dispose of agricultural wastes such as agricultural films and crop straw.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国务院林业草原、农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门和青藏高原省级人民政府按照职责分工，开展野生动植物物种调查，根据调查情况提出实施保护措施的意见，完善相关名录制度，加强野生动物重要栖息地、迁徙洄游通道和野生植物原生境保护，对野牦牛、藏羚、普氏原羚、雪豹、大熊猫、高黎贡白眉长臂猿、黑颈鹤、川陕哲罗鲑、骨唇黄河鱼、黑斑原鮡、扁吻鱼、尖裸鲤和大花红景天、西藏杓兰、雪兔子等青藏高原珍贵濒危或者特有野生动植物物种实行重点保护。  
国家支持开展野生动物救护繁育野化基地以及植物园、高原生物种质资源库建设，加强对青藏高原珍贵濒危或者特有野生动植物物种的救护和迁地保护。  
青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关单位和个人积极开展野生动物致害综合防控。对野生动物造成人员伤亡，牲畜、农作物或者其他财产损失的，依法给予补偿。

**Article 28** The competent departments of forestry and grassland, and agriculture and rural areas of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, carry out surveys of wildlife species, put forward opinions on implementing protection measures based on the survey results, improve the relevant list system, strengthen the protection of important habitats, migration and migration routes of wild animals, and native habitats of wild plants, and implement key protection for precious, endangered or endemic wildlife species on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau such as wild yak, Tibetan antelope, Przewalski's gazelle, snow leopard, giant panda, Gaoligong hoolock gibbon, black-necked crane, Sichuan taimen, Gymnocypris eckloni, Glyptothorax maculatus, Aspiorhynchus laticeps, Oxygymnocypris stewartii, Rhodiola crenulata, Cypripedium tibeticum, and Saussurea involucrata.  
The State shall support the construction of wildlife rescue, breeding and rewilding bases, botanical gardens, and highland biological germplasm resource banks, and strengthen the rescue and ex situ conservation of precious, endangered or endemic wildlife species on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall organize relevant entities and individuals to actively carry out comprehensive prevention and control of damage caused by wild animals. Where wild animals cause personal injury or death, or damage to livestock, crops or other property, compensation shall be given in accordance with law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家加强青藏高原生物多样性保护，实施生物多样性保护重大工程，防止对生物多样性的破坏。  
国务院有关部门和青藏高原地方各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，建立完善生态廊道，提升生态系统完整性和连通性。

**Article 29** The State shall strengthen the protection of biodiversity on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, implement major projects for biodiversity protection, and prevent damage to biodiversity.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall take effective measures to establish and improve ecological corridors, and enhance the integrity and connectivity of ecosystems.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府及其林业草原主管部门，应当采取荒漠化土地封禁保护、植被保护与恢复等措施，加强荒漠生态保护与荒漠化土地综合治理。

**Article 30** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their competent departments of forestry and grassland shall take measures such as closed protection of desertified land, vegetation protection and restoration, to strengthen ecological protection of deserts and comprehensive treatment of desertified land.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　青藏高原省级人民政府应当采取封禁抚育、轮封轮牧、移民搬迁等措施，实施高原山地以及农田风沙地带、河岸地带、生态防护带等重点治理工程，提升水土保持功能。

**Article 31** The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall take measures such as closed tending, rotational closure and rotational grazing, and relocation of residents, implement key treatment projects such as plateau mountains and farmland wind-sand areas, riparian areas, and ecological protection belts, and improve soil and water conservation functions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院水行政主管部门和青藏高原省级人民政府应当采取有效措施，加强对三江源、祁连山黑河流域、金沙江和岷江上游、雅鲁藏布江以及金沙江、澜沧江、怒江三江并流地区等重要江河源头区和水土流失重点预防区、治理区，人口相对密集高原河谷区的水土流失防治。  
禁止在青藏高原水土流失严重、生态脆弱的区域开展可能造成水土流失的生产建设活动。确因国家发展战略和国计民生需要建设的，应当经科学论证，并依法办理审批手续，严格控制扰动范围。

**Article 32** The competent water administrative department of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall take effective measures to strengthen the prevention and control of soil erosion in important river source areas such as the Sanjiangyuan, Heihe River Basin of Qilian Mountain, upper reaches of Jinsha River and Minjiang River, Yarlung Zangbo River, and the Three Parallel Rivers area of Jinsha River, Lancang River, and Nujiang River, key prevention and control areas of soil erosion, and relatively densely populated plateau valley areas.  
It is prohibited to carry out production and construction activities that may cause soil erosion in areas with severe soil erosion and fragile ecology on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. Where construction is truly necessary for the national development strategy and national economy and people's livelihood, scientific demonstration shall be conducted, and approval procedures shall be handled in accordance with law, and the scope of disturbance shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　在青藏高原设立探矿权、采矿权应当符合国土空间规划和矿产资源规划要求。依法禁止在长江、黄河、澜沧江、雅鲁藏布江、怒江等江河源头自然保护地内从事不符合生态保护管控要求的采砂、采矿活动。  
在青藏高原从事矿产资源勘查、开采活动，探矿权人、采矿权人应当采用先进适用的工艺、设备和产品，选择环保、安全的勘探、开采技术和方法，避免或者减少对矿产资源和生态环境的破坏；禁止使用国家明令淘汰的工艺、设备和产品。在生态环境敏感区从事矿产资源勘查、开采活动，应当符合相关管控要求，采取避让、减缓和及时修复重建等保护措施，防止造成环境污染和生态破坏。

**Article 33** The establishment of prospecting rights and mining rights on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall comply with the requirements of territorial space plans and mineral resource plans. In accordance with law, sand mining and mining activities that do not meet the requirements of ecological protection and control are prohibited in natural protected areas of river sources such as the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Lancang River, Yarlung Zangbo River, and Nujiang River.  
Where carrying out mineral resource exploration and mining activities on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, the holders of prospecting rights and mining rights shall adopt advanced and applicable technologies, equipment, and products, select environmentally friendly and safe exploration and mining techniques and methods, to avoid or reduce damage to mineral resources and the ecological environment; the use of technologies, equipment, and products eliminated by the State is prohibited. Where carrying out mineral resource exploration and mining activities in ecologically sensitive areas, they shall comply with the relevant control requirements, and take protection measures such as avoidance, mitigation, and timely restoration and reconstruction to prevent environmental pollution and ecological damage.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜采取消除地质灾害隐患、土地复垦、恢复植被、防治污染等措施，加快历史遗留矿山生态修复工作，加强对在建和运行中矿山的监督管理，督促采矿权人依法履行矿山污染防治和生态修复责任。  
在青藏高原开采矿产资源应当科学编制矿产资源开采方案和矿区生态修复方案。新建矿山应当严格按照绿色矿山建设标准规划设计、建设和运营管理。生产矿山应当实施绿色化升级改造，加强尾矿库运行管理，防范和化解环境和安全风险。

**Article 34** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall take measures such as eliminating geological hazard risks, land reclamation, vegetation restoration, and pollution prevention according to local conditions, accelerate the ecological restoration of historical legacy mines, strengthen the supervision and management of mines under construction and in operation, and urge mining right holders to fulfill their responsibilities for mine pollution prevention and ecological restoration in accordance with law.  
When mining mineral resources on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, mineral resource mining plans and mining area ecological restoration plans shall be scientifically formulated. New mines shall be designed, constructed, operated and managed strictly in accordance with the construction standards of green mines. Existing mines shall implement green upgrading and transformation, strengthen the operation and management of tailings ponds, and prevent and mitigate environmental and safety risks.

### 第四章　生态风险防控

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家建立健全青藏高原生态风险防控体系，采取有效措施提高自然灾害防治、气候变化应对等生态风险防控能力和水平，保障青藏高原生态安全。

**Article 35** The State shall establish and improve a system for ecological risk prevention and control on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, take effective measures to improve the capacity and level of ecological risk prevention and control such as natural disaster prevention and climate change response, and ensure the ecological security of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家加强青藏高原自然灾害调查评价和监测预警。  
国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强对地震、雪崩、冰崩、山洪、山体崩塌、滑坡、泥石流、冰湖溃决、冻土消融、森林草原火灾、暴雨（雪）、干旱等自然灾害的调查评价和监测预警。  
在地质灾害易发区进行工程建设时，应当按照有关规定进行地质灾害危险性评估，及时采取工程治理或者搬迁避让等措施。

**Article 36** The State shall strengthen the investigation, assessment, monitoring and early warning of natural disasters on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their relevant departments shall strengthen the investigation, assessment, monitoring and early warning of natural disasters such as earthquakes, avalanches, ice avalanches, flash floods, landslides, debris flows, glacial lake outbursts, permafrost thawing, forest and grassland fires, heavy rain (snow), and droughts.  
When carrying out engineering construction in areas prone to geological disasters, a geological hazard risk assessment shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions, and measures such as engineering treatment or relocation and avoidance shall be taken in a timely manner.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当加强自然灾害综合治理，提高地震、山洪、冰湖溃决、地质灾害等自然灾害防御工程标准，建立与青藏高原生态保护相适应的自然灾害防治工程和非工程体系。  
交通、水利、电力、市政、边境口岸等基础设施工程建设、运营单位应当依法承担自然灾害防治义务，采取综合治理措施，加强工程建设、运营期间的自然灾害防治，保障人民群众生命财产安全。

**Article 37** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall strengthen comprehensive management of natural disasters, raise the standards of defense projects against natural disasters such as earthquakes, flash floods, glacial lake outbursts, and geological disasters, and establish a system of engineering and non-engineering measures for natural disaster prevention and control that is compatible with ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The construction and operation units of infrastructure projects such as transportation, water conservancy, electric power, municipal facilities, and border ports shall, in accordance with law, assume the obligation of natural disaster prevention and control, take comprehensive management measures, strengthen the prevention and control of natural disasters during the construction and operation of projects, and ensure the safety of people's lives and property.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　重大工程建设可能造成生态和地质环境影响的，建设单位应当根据工程沿线生态和地质环境敏感脆弱区域状况，制定沿线生态和地质环境监测方案，开展生态和地质环境影响的全生命周期监测，包括工程开工前的本底监测、工程建设中的生态和地质环境影响监测、工程运营期的生态和地质环境变化与保护修复跟踪监测。  
重大工程建设应当避让野生动物重要栖息地、迁徙洄游通道和国家重点保护野生植物的天然集中分布区；无法避让的，应当采取修建野生动物通道、迁地保护等措施，避免或者减少对自然生态系统与野生动植物的影响。

**Article 38** Where a major engineering project may cause ecological and geological environmental impacts, the construction unit shall, based on the conditions of ecologically and geologically sensitive and fragile areas along the project route, formulate a monitoring plan for the ecological and geological environment along the route, and carry out full-life-cycle monitoring of the ecological and geological environmental impacts, including baseline monitoring before the start of the project, monitoring of ecological and geological environmental impacts during construction, and tracking monitoring of ecological and geological environment changes, protection and restoration during the operation period.  
Major engineering projects shall avoid important wildlife habitats, migration and migration routes, and natural concentrated distribution areas of national key protected wild plants; where avoidance is not possible, measures such as constructing wildlife passages and ex situ conservation shall be taken to avoid or reduce impacts on natural ecosystems and wildlife.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对青藏高原种质资源的保护和管理，组织开展种质资源调查与收集，完善相关资源保护设施和数据库。  
禁止在青藏高原采集或者采伐国家重点保护的天然种质资源。因科研、有害生物防治、自然灾害防治等需要采集或者采伐的，应当依法取得批准。

**Article 39** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall strengthen the protection and management of germplasm resources on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, organize and carry out surveys and collection of germplasm resources, and improve relevant resource protection facilities and databases.  
The collection or felling of natural germplasm resources under national key protection on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau is prohibited. Where collection or felling is necessary for scientific research, pest control, natural disaster prevention, etc., approval shall be obtained in accordance with law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原省级人民政府按照职责分工，统筹推进区域外来入侵物种防控，实行外来物种引入审批管理，强化入侵物种口岸防控，加强外来入侵物种调查、监测、预警、控制、评估、清除、生态修复等工作。  
任何单位和个人未经批准，不得擅自引进、释放或者丢弃外来物种。

**Article 40** The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, coordinate the promotion of prevention and control of alien invasive species in the region, implement approval management for the introduction of alien species, strengthen port control of invasive species, and enhance the survey, monitoring, early warning, control, assessment, removal, and ecological restoration of alien invasive species.  
No entity or individual may, without approval, introduce, release, or discard alien species without authorization.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家加强对气候变化及其综合影响的监测，建立气候变化对青藏高原生态系统、气候系统、水资源、珍贵濒危或者特有野生动植物、雪山冰川冻土和自然灾害影响的预测体系，完善生态风险报告和预警机制，强化气候变化对青藏高原影响和高原生态系统演变的评估。  
青藏高原省级人民政府应当开展雪山冰川冻土消融退化对区域生态系统影响的监测与风险评估。

**Article 41** The State shall strengthen monitoring of climate change and its comprehensive impacts, establish a prediction system for the impacts of climate change on the ecosystems, climate system, water resources, precious, endangered or endemic wildlife, snow mountains, glaciers, permafrost, and natural disasters of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, improve the ecological risk reporting and early warning mechanism, and strengthen the assessment of the impacts of climate change on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and the evolution of plateau ecosystems.  
The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall carry out monitoring and risk assessment of the impacts of the melting and degradation of snow mountains, glaciers, and permafrost on regional ecosystems.

### 第五章　保障与监督

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家加大对青藏高原生态保护修复的财政投入，中央财政安排专项资金用于青藏高原生态保护修复、生态风险防控等。中央预算内投资对青藏高原基础设施和基本公共服务设施建设予以倾斜。  
青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当加大资金投入力度，重点支持青藏高原生态保护修复工程建设。

**Article 42** The State shall increase financial investment in ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and the central finance shall arrange special funds for ecological protection and restoration, ecological risk prevention and control, etc., of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau. The central budget investment shall give preference to the construction of infrastructure and basic public service facilities on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall increase capital investment, with priority given to supporting the construction of ecological protection and restoration projects on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家加大财政转移支付力度，通过提高转移支付系数、加计生态环保支出等方式，对青藏高原生态功能重要区域予以补偿。青藏高原省级人民政府应当将生态功能重要区域全面纳入省级对下生态保护补偿转移支付范围，促进生态保护同民生改善相结合。  
国家通过开展自然资源统一确权登记，探索确定青藏高原生态产品权责归属，健全生态产品经营开发机制，鼓励青藏高原特色生态产品区域公用品牌创建，形成多元化的生态产品价值实现路径。

**Article 43** The State shall increase fiscal transfer payments, and provide compensation to areas with important ecological functions on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau by means such as increasing transfer payment coefficients and adding ecological and environmental protection expenditures. The provincial people's governments in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall comprehensively include areas with important ecological functions into the scope of provincial-level ecological protection compensation transfer payments to lower levels, and promote the integration of ecological protection with the improvement of people's livelihood.  
The State shall, through conducting unified confirmation and registration of natural resources, explore the determination of the attribution of rights and responsibilities of ecological products on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, improve the operation and development mechanism of ecological products, encourage the creation of regional public brands for characteristic ecological products on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and form diversified paths for realizing the value of ecological products.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家为青藏高原生态保护提供支持，实行有利于节水、节能、水土保持、环境保护和资源综合利用的金融、税收政策，鼓励发展绿色信贷、绿色债券、绿色保险等金融产品。  
国家鼓励和支持公益组织、社会资本参与青藏高原生态保护修复工作，开展生态产品开发、产业发展、科技创新、技术服务等活动。

**Article 44** The State shall provide support for ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, implement financial and tax policies conducive to water conservation, energy conservation, soil and water conservation, environmental protection, and comprehensive utilization of resources, and encourage the development of green credit, green bonds, green insurance and other financial products.  
The State shall encourage and support public welfare organizations and social capital to participate in ecological protection and restoration of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and carry out activities such as ecological product development, industrial development, scientific and technological innovation, and technical services.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家支持在青藏高原因地制宜建设以风电、光伏发电、水电、水风光互补发电、光热、地热等清洁能源为主体的能源体系，加强清洁能源输送通道建设，推进能源绿色低碳转型。  
除保障居民用电和巩固边防需要外，禁止在青藏高原新建小水电项目。

**Article 45** The State shall support the construction of an energy system with clean energy such as wind power, photovoltaic power, hydropower, water-wind-solar complementary power generation, solar thermal power, and geothermal power as the main body, strengthen the construction of clean energy transmission channels, and promote the green and low-carbon transformation of energy.  
Except for the need to ensure residential electricity supply and consolidate border defense, the construction of new small hydropower projects on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau is prohibited.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　在青藏高原发展生态旅游应当符合资源和生态保护要求，尊重和维护当地传统文化和习俗，保护和合理利用旅游资源。  
地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当按照国家有关规定，科学开发青藏高原生态旅游产品、设计旅游路线，合理控制游客数量和相关基础设施建设规模。  
组织或者参加青藏高原旅游、山地户外运动等活动，应当遵守安全规定和文明行为规范，符合区域生态旅游、山地户外运动等管控和规范要求；禁止破坏自然景观和草原植被、猎捕和采集野生动植物。  
组织或者参加青藏高原旅游、山地户外运动等活动，应当自行带走产生的垃圾或者在指定地点投放；禁止随意倾倒、抛撒生活垃圾。

**Article 46** The development of eco-tourism on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall meet the requirements of resource and ecological protection, respect and maintain local traditional culture and customs, and protect and rationally utilize tourism resources.  
The local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, scientifically develop eco-tourism products and design tourist routes on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and reasonably control the number of tourists and the scale of construction of relevant infrastructure.  
When organizing or participating in activities such as tourism and mountain outdoor sports on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, the safety regulations and codes of civilized conduct shall be observed, and the control and normative requirements for regional eco-tourism and mountain outdoor sports shall be complied with; damaging natural landscapes and grassland vegetation, hunting and collecting wildlife are prohibited.  
When organizing or participating in activities such as tourism and mountain outdoor sports on the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, the waste generated shall be taken away by oneself or deposited in designated places; randomly dumping or discarding domestic waste is prohibited.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当根据区域资源环境承载能力，统筹推进交通、水利、能源等重大基础设施建设和生活污水、垃圾收集处理等环境基础设施建设，加强城市内部以及周边毗邻地带生态保护修复，统筹规划城乡社区综合服务设施建设，加快推进基本公共服务均等化。  
青藏高原地方各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，推进农村生活污水和垃圾治理，推进农村卫生厕所改造和乡村绿化，持续改善农村人居环境，塑造乡村风貌，建设生态宜居美丽乡村。

**Article 47** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, based on the regional resource and environmental carrying capacity, coordinate the promotion of the construction of major infrastructure such as transportation, water conservancy, and energy, and the construction of environmental infrastructure such as domestic sewage and garbage collection and treatment, strengthen ecological protection and restoration within cities and adjacent surrounding areas, coordinate the planning of comprehensive service facilities for urban and rural communities, and accelerate the equalization of basic public services.  
The local people's governments at all levels in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall take effective measures to promote the treatment of rural domestic sewage and garbage, promote the transformation of rural sanitary toilets and rural greening, continuously improve the rural living environment, shape the rural landscape, and build ecologically livable and beautiful villages.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工，对青藏高原生态保护各类活动进行监督检查，查处违法行为，依法公开青藏高原生态保护工作相关信息，完善公众参与程序。  
单位和个人有权依法举报和控告污染青藏高原环境、破坏青藏高原生态的违法行为。

**Article 48** The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, supervise and inspect various activities concerning ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, investigate and deal with illegal acts, publicly disclose relevant information on ecological protection work of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau in accordance with law, and improve the procedures for public participation.  
Entities and individuals have the right to report and accuse, in accordance with law, illegal acts that pollute the environment or damage the ecology of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院有关部门和青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强青藏高原生态保护监督管理能力建设，提高科技化、信息化水平，建立执法协调机制，对重大违法案件和跨行政区域、生态敏感区域的违法案件，依法开展联合执法。

**Article 49** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau and their relevant departments shall strengthen the capacity building for supervision and management of ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, improve the level of science, technology, and informatization, establish a law enforcement coordination mechanism, and conduct joint law enforcement in accordance with law for major illegal cases and illegal cases across administrative regions and ecologically sensitive areas.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家实行青藏高原生态保护绩效评价考核制度，将环境质量提升、生态保护成效、生态产品供给能力等纳入指标体系。

**Article 50** The State shall implement a performance evaluation and assessment system for ecological protection of the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau, and incorporate environmental quality improvement, ecological protection effectiveness, and ecological product supply capacity into the indicator system.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家加强青藏高原生态保护司法保障建设，鼓励有关单位为青藏高原生态保护提供法律服务。  
青藏高原各级行政执法机关、人民法院、人民检察院在依法查处青藏高原生态保护违法行为或者办理自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼、公益诉讼等过程中，发现存在涉嫌犯罪行为的，应当将犯罪线索移送具有侦查、调查职权的机关。

**Article 51** The State shall strengthen the construction of judicial guarantees for ecological protection in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau and encourage relevant entities to provide legal services for the ecological protection of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau.  
Administrative law enforcement organs, People's Courts, and People's Procuratorates at all levels in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, when investigating and dealing with illegal acts in Qinghai-Tibet Plateau ecological protection according to law or handling lawsuits for damages to natural resources and the ecological environment, public interest litigation, and other proceedings, shall, if finding acts suspected of constituting crimes, transfer the criminal leads to the organs with investigation and inquiry powers.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　青藏高原县级以上地方人民政府应当定期向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告本级人民政府青藏高原生态保护工作情况。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at or above the county level in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau shall periodically report to the people's congress at the corresponding level or its standing committee on the work of ecological protection of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau conducted by the said people's governments.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国务院有关部门和地方各级人民政府及其有关部门违反本法规定，在履行相关职责中有玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊行为的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予警告、记过、记大过或者降级处分；造成严重后果的，给予撤职或者开除处分，其主要负责人应当引咎辞职。

**Article 53** Where the relevant departments of the State Council and local people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments violate the provisions of this Law and are found guilty of dereliction of duty, abuse of power, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain in the performance of their relevant duties, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning, a demerit, a major demerit, or a demotion sanction according to law; if serious consequences are caused, they shall be given a sanction of removal from office or expulsion, and the principal responsible persons shall take the blame and resign.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，在青藏高原有下列行为之一的，依照有关法律法规的规定从重处罚：  
（一）在国家公园内从事资源开发利用活动造成生态破坏；  
（二）在星宿海、扎陵湖、鄂陵湖、若尔盖等泥炭沼泽湿地开采泥炭或者开（围）垦、排干自然湿地；  
（三）在水土流失严重、生态脆弱的区域开展可能造成水土流失的生产建设活动；  
（四）采集或者采伐国家重点保护的天然种质资源；  
（五）擅自引进、释放或者丢弃外来物种；  
（六）破坏自然景观或者草原植被；  
（七）猎捕、采集国家或者地方重点保护野生动植物。

**Article 54** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations:  
(1) engaging in resource development and utilization activities in national parks, causing ecological damage;  
(2) extracting peat or opening up (enclosing) and cultivating, or draining natural wetlands in peat swamp wetlands such as Xingxuhai, Zhaling Lake, Eling Lake, Zoige, etc.;  
(3) carrying out production and construction activities that may cause soil erosion in areas with severe soil erosion and fragile ecological conditions;  
(4) collecting or cutting natural germplasm resources that are under special State protection;  
(5) introducing, releasing, or discarding alien species without authorization;  
(6) damaging natural landscapes or grassland vegetation;  
(7) hunting and capturing or collecting wild animals or plants that are under State or local key protection.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，利用、占用青藏高原河道、湖泊水域和岸线的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，限期拆除并恢复原状，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；逾期不拆除或者不恢复原状的，强制拆除或者代为恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 55** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, uses or occupies the river courses, lake waters, and shorelines in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau shall be ordered by the water administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, dismantle the structures within a specified time limit and restore the original condition, and shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the structures are not dismantled or the original condition is not restored within the specified time limit, compulsory dismantlement or restoration on behalf of the violator shall be carried out, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，在长江、黄河、澜沧江、雅鲁藏布江、怒江等江河源头自然保护地内从事不符合生态保护管控要求的采矿活动的，由自然资源、生态环境主管部门按照职责分工，责令改正，没收违法所得和直接用于违法开采的设备、工具；违法所得十万元以上的，并处违法所得十倍以上二十倍以下罚款；违法所得不足十万元的，并处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 56** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in mining activities that do not meet the ecological protection control requirements within the nature reserves at the headwaters of rivers such as the Yangtze River, Yellow River, Lancang River, Yarlung Tsangpo River, and Nujiang River shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent departments of natural resources and ecological environment in accordance with their respective responsibilities; the illegal gains and the equipment and tools directly used for illegal mining shall be confiscated; if the illegal gains are 100,000 yuan or more, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the illegal gains shall also be imposed; if the illegal gains are less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，建设单位新建小水电项目的，由县级以上地方人民政府责令停止建设，根据违法情节和危害后果，责令恢复原状，处建设项目总投资额百分之一以上百分之五以下罚款。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a construction entity builds a new small hydropower project, the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of construction, and, based on the circumstances of the violation and the harmful consequences, order restoration to the original condition, and impose a fine of not less than one percent but not more than five percent of the total investment in the construction project.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，在旅游、山地户外运动中随意倾倒、抛撒生活垃圾的，由环境卫生主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府指定的部门责令改正，对个人处一百元以上五百元以下罚款，情节严重的，处五百元以上一万元以下罚款；对单位处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 58** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, randomly dumps or discards domestic waste during tourism or mountain outdoor sports shall be ordered to make corrections by the competent department of environmental sanitation or the department designated by the local people's government at or above the county level; if the violator is an individual, a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 500 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the violator is an entity, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　污染青藏高原环境、破坏青藏高原生态造成他人损害的，侵权人应当承担侵权责任。  
违反国家规定造成青藏高原生态环境损害的，国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织有权请求侵权人承担修复责任、赔偿损失和相关费用。

**Article 59** Where pollution of the environment of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau or damage to the ecology of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau causes harm to others, the tortfeasor shall bear tort liability.  
Where damage is caused to the ecological environment of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau in violation of State provisions, the organs specified by the State or the organizations specified by law shall have the right to request the tortfeasor to bear the liability for restoration, compensate for losses, and pay related expenses.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 60** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　[本法第二条](#art-2)第二款规定的相关县级行政区域，由国务院授权的部门确定。

**Article 61** The relevant county-level administrative regions as provided for in the second paragraph of Article 2 of this Law shall be determined by the department authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　青藏高原省、自治区和设区的市、自治州可以结合本地实际，制定青藏高原生态保护具体办法。

**Article 62** The provinces, autonomous regions, and cities with districts, autonomous prefectures in the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau may formulate specific measures for ecological protection of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau in light of local conditions.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　本法自2023年9月1日起施行。

**Article 63** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2023.
