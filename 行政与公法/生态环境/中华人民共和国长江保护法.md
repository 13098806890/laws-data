# 中华人民共和国长江保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-12-26  
**生效日期**：2021-03-01

> （2020年12月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　规划与管控 第三章　资源保护 第四章　水污染防治 第五章　生态环境修复 第六章　绿色发展 第七章　保障与监督 第八章　法律责任 第九章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强长江流域生态环境保护和修复，促进资源合理高效利用，保障生态安全，实现人与自然和谐共生、中华民族永续发展，制定本法。 第二条　在长江流域开展生态环境保护和修复以及长江流域各类生产生活、开发建设活动，应当遵守本法。 本法所称长江流域，是指由长江干流、支流和湖泊形成的集水区域所涉及的青海省、四川省、西藏自治区、云南省、重庆市、湖北省、湖南省、江西省、安徽省、江苏省、上海市，以及甘肃省、陕西省、河南省、贵州省、广西壮族自治区、广东省、浙江省、福建省的相关县级行政区域。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强长江流域生态环境保护和修复，促进资源合理高效利用，保障生态安全，实现人与自然和谐共生、中华民族永续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening the protection and restoration of the ecological environment in the Yangtze River Basin, promoting the rational and efficient utilization of resources, ensuring ecological security, and achieving harmonious coexistence between humans and nature as well as the sustainable development of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在长江流域开展生态环境保护和修复以及长江流域各类生产生活、开发建设活动，应当遵守本法。  
本法所称长江流域，是指由长江干流、支流和湖泊形成的集水区域所涉及的青海省、四川省、西藏自治区、云南省、重庆市、湖北省、湖南省、江西省、安徽省、江苏省、上海市，以及甘肃省、陕西省、河南省、贵州省、广西壮族自治区、广东省、浙江省、福建省的相关县级行政区域。

**Article 2** The protection and restoration of the ecological environment in the Yangtze River Basin, as well as various production, living, development, and construction activities within the Yangtze River Basin, shall comply with this Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, the Yangtze River Basin refers to the relevant county-level administrative areas in Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Tibet Autonomous Region, Yunnan Province, Chongqing Municipality, Hubei Province, Hunan Province, Jiangxi Province, Anhui Province, Jiangsu Province, Shanghai Municipality, as well as Gansu Province, Shaanxi Province, Henan Province, Guizhou Province, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, Guangdong Province, Zhejiang Province, and Fujian Province, which are covered by the catchment area formed by the main stream, tributaries, and lakes of the Yangtze River.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　长江流域经济社会发展，应当坚持生态优先、绿色发展，共抓大保护、不搞大开发；长江保护应当坚持统筹协调、科学规划、创新驱动、系统治理。

**Article 3** The economic and social development of the Yangtze River Basin shall adhere to the principles of giving priority to ecological protection and pursuing green development, jointly promoting comprehensive protection and refraining from large-scale development; the protection of the Yangtze River shall adhere to the principles of overall coordination, scientific planning, innovation-driven development, and systematic governance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家建立长江流域协调机制，统一指导、统筹协调长江保护工作，审议长江保护重大政策、重大规划，协调跨地区跨部门重大事项，督促检查长江保护重要工作的落实情况。

**Article 4** The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin to provide unified guidance and overall coordination for the protection of the Yangtze River, review major policies and plans for the protection of the Yangtze River, coordinate major cross-regional and cross-departmental matters, and supervise and inspect the implementation of key tasks in the protection of the Yangtze River.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府负责落实国家长江流域协调机制的决策，按照职责分工负责长江保护相关工作。  
长江流域地方各级人民政府应当落实本行政区域的生态环境保护和修复、促进资源合理高效利用、优化产业结构和布局、维护长江流域生态安全的责任。  
长江流域各级河湖长负责长江保护相关工作。

**Article 5** The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall be responsible for implementing the decisions of the national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin, and shall be responsible for the relevant work of the protection of the Yangtze River in accordance with their respective functions and duties.  
The local people's governments at all levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall fulfill their responsibilities for the protection and restoration of the ecological environment, promoting the rational and efficient utilization of resources, optimizing the industrial structure and layout, and maintaining the ecological security of the Yangtze River Basin within their respective administrative regions.  
The river and lake chiefs at all levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall be responsible for the relevant work of the protection of the Yangtze River.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　长江流域相关地方根据需要在地方性法规和政府规章制定、规划编制、监督执法等方面建立协作机制，协同推进长江流域生态环境保护和修复。

**Article 6** The relevant localities within the Yangtze River Basin shall, as needed, establish collaborative mechanisms in the formulation of local regulations and government rules, planning, supervision and law enforcement, among others, to jointly advance the protection and restoration of the ecological environment in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院生态环境、自然资源、水行政、农业农村和标准化等有关主管部门按照职责分工，建立健全长江流域水环境质量和污染物排放、生态环境修复、水资源节约集约利用、生态流量、生物多样性保护、水产养殖、防灾减灾等标准体系。

**Article 7** The competent departments of the State Council for ecological environment, natural resources, water administration, agriculture and rural affairs, and standardization shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, establish and improve the standard system for the Yangtze River Basin concerning water environment quality and pollutant discharge, ecological environment restoration, water conservation and intensive utilization, ecological flow, biodiversity protection, aquaculture, disaster prevention and reduction, among others.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门定期组织长江流域土地、矿产、水流、森林、草原、湿地等自然资源状况调查，建立资源基础数据库，开展资源环境承载能力评价，并向社会公布长江流域自然资源状况。  
国务院野生动物保护主管部门应当每十年组织一次野生动物及其栖息地状况普查，或者根据需要组织开展专项调查，建立野生动物资源档案，并向社会公布长江流域野生动物资源状况。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门对水生生物产卵场、索饵场、越冬场和洄游通道等重要栖息地开展生物多样性调查。

**Article 8** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, regularly organize surveys of the conditions of natural resources such as land, minerals, water, forests, grasslands, and wetlands in the Yangtze River Basin, establish a basic resource database, conduct assessments of resource and environmental carrying capacity, and disclose to the public the conditions of natural resources in the Yangtze River Basin.  
The competent department of wildlife protection of the State Council shall organize a general survey of wildlife and their habitat conditions every ten years, or organize special surveys as needed, establish wildlife resource archives, and disclose to the public the conditions of wildlife resources in the Yangtze River Basin.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, conduct biodiversity surveys on important habitats such as spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, and migration corridors of aquatic organisms.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家长江流域协调机制应当统筹协调国务院有关部门在已经建立的台站和监测项目基础上，健全长江流域生态环境、资源、水文、气象、航运、自然灾害等监测网络体系和监测信息共享机制。  
国务院有关部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门按照职责分工，组织完善生态环境风险报告和预警机制。

**Article 9** The national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate and integrate the relevant departments of the State Council to improve the monitoring network system for ecological environment, resources, hydrology, meteorology, shipping, natural disasters, etc., and the monitoring information sharing mechanism, based on the existing stations and monitoring projects.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize and improve the ecological environment risk reporting and early warning mechanism.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府建立健全长江流域突发生态环境事件应急联动工作机制，与国家突发事件应急体系相衔接，加强对长江流域船舶、港口、矿山、化工厂、尾矿库等发生的突发生态环境事件的应急管理。

**Article 10** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin, establish and improve an emergency response linkage mechanism for sudden ecological and environmental incidents in the Yangtze River Basin, which shall be connected with the national emergency response system, and shall strengthen the emergency management of sudden ecological and environmental incidents occurring in ships, ports, mines, chemical plants, tailings ponds, etc., in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强长江流域洪涝干旱、森林草原火灾、地质灾害、地震等灾害的监测预报预警、防御、应急处置与恢复重建体系建设，提高防灾、减灾、抗灾、救灾能力。

**Article 11** The State shall strengthen the construction of systems for monitoring, forecasting, early warning, prevention, emergency response, and recovery and reconstruction regarding disasters such as floods, droughts, forest and grassland fires, geological disasters, and earthquakes in the Yangtze River Basin, so as to improve the capacity for disaster prevention, mitigation, resistance, and relief.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家长江流域协调机制设立专家咨询委员会，组织专业机构和人员对长江流域重大发展战略、政策、规划等开展科学技术等专业咨询。  
国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府及其有关部门按照职责分工，组织开展长江流域建设项目、重要基础设施和产业布局相关规划等对长江流域生态系统影响的第三方评估、分析、论证等工作。

**Article 12** The national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin shall establish an expert advisory committee, and organize professional institutions and personnel to provide scientific and technical advice on major development strategies, policies, plans, etc., for the Yangtze River Basin.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize third-party assessment, analysis, and demonstration of the impact on the ecosystem of the Yangtze River Basin arising from construction projects, important infrastructure, and plans related to industrial layout in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家长江流域协调机制统筹协调国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府建立健全长江流域信息共享系统。国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府及其有关部门应当按照规定，共享长江流域生态环境、自然资源以及管理执法等信息。

**Article 13** The national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate and integrate the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin to establish and improve an information sharing system for the Yangtze River Basin. The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall share information on the ecological environment, natural resources, and administrative and law enforcement matters in the Yangtze River Basin in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院有关部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强长江流域生态环境保护和绿色发展的宣传教育。  
新闻媒体应当采取多种形式开展长江流域生态环境保护和绿色发展的宣传教育，并依法对违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 14** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity and education on ecological environment protection and green development in the Yangtze River Basin.  
The news media shall carry out publicity and education on ecological environment protection and green development in the Yangtze River Basin through various forms, and shall conduct public opinion supervision over illegal acts in accordance with law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院有关部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施，保护长江流域历史文化名城名镇名村，加强长江流域文化遗产保护工作，继承和弘扬长江流域优秀特色文化。

**Article 15** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall take measures to protect the famous historical and cultural cities, towns, and villages in the Yangtze River Basin, strengthen the protection of cultural heritage in the Yangtze River Basin, and inherit and promote the outstanding and distinctive culture of the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励、支持单位和个人参与长江流域生态环境保护和修复、资源合理利用、促进绿色发展的活动。  
对在长江保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 16** The State shall encourage and support entities and individuals to participate in activities concerning the protection and restoration of the ecological environment, rational utilization of resources, and promotion of green development in the Yangtze River Basin.  
Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the protection of the Yangtze River shall be commended and rewarded by the people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　规划与管控

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家建立以国家发展规划为统领，以空间规划为基础，以专项规划、区域规划为支撑的长江流域规划体系，充分发挥规划对推进长江流域生态环境保护和绿色发展的引领、指导和约束作用。

**Article 17** The State shall establish a planning system for the Yangtze River Basin, which shall be led by the national development plan, based on spatial plans, and supported by special plans and regional plans, so as to give full play to the guiding, directing, and constraining role of planning in promoting ecological environment protection and green development in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当将长江保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国务院发展改革部门会同国务院有关部门编制长江流域发展规划，科学统筹长江流域上下游、左右岸、干支流生态环境保护和绿色发展，报国务院批准后实施。  
长江流域水资源规划、生态环境保护规划等依照有关法律、行政法规的规定编制。

**Article 18** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall incorporate the protection of the Yangtze River into the national economic and social development plans.  
The development and reform department of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the development plan for the Yangtze River Basin, which shall scientifically coordinate the ecological environment protection and green development of the upper, middle, and lower reaches, the left and right banks, and the main stream and tributaries of the Yangtze River, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation.  
The water resources plan, ecological environment protection plan, etc., for the Yangtze River Basin shall be formulated in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门组织编制长江流域国土空间规划，科学有序统筹安排长江流域生态、农业、城镇等功能空间，划定生态保护红线、永久基本农田、城镇开发边界，优化国土空间结构和布局，统领长江流域国土空间利用任务，报国务院批准后实施。涉及长江流域国土空间利用的专项规划应当与长江流域国土空间规划相衔接。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府组织编制本行政区域的国土空间规划，按照规定的程序报经批准后实施。

**Article 19** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the formulation of the territorial space plan for the Yangtze River Basin, which shall scientifically and orderly arrange the functional spaces such as ecological, agricultural, and urban spaces in the Yangtze River Basin, delineate the ecological protection redlines, permanent basic farmland, and urban development boundaries, optimize the structure and layout of territorial space, and guide the tasks of territorial space utilization in the Yangtze River Basin, and shall be submitted to the State Council for approval before implementation. Special plans involving territorial space utilization in the Yangtze River Basin shall be aligned with the territorial space plan for the Yangtze River Basin.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall organize the formulation of territorial space plans for their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures before implementation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家对长江流域国土空间实施用途管制。长江流域县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门依照国土空间规划，对所辖长江流域国土空间实施分区、分类用途管制。  
长江流域国土空间开发利用活动应当符合国土空间用途管制要求，并依法取得规划许可。对不符合国土空间用途管制要求的，县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门不得办理规划许可。

**Article 20** The State shall implement use regulation on the territorial space in the Yangtze River Basin. The competent departments of natural resources of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall implement use regulation by zones and categories on the territorial space of the Yangtze River Basin under their jurisdiction in accordance with the territorial space plan.  
The development and utilization activities of territorial space in the Yangtze River Basin shall comply with the requirements of territorial space use regulation and shall obtain planning permits in accordance with law. Where the requirements of territorial space use regulation are not met, the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall not handle the planning permits.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院水行政主管部门统筹长江流域水资源合理配置、统一调度和高效利用，组织实施取用水总量控制和消耗强度控制管理制度。  
国务院生态环境主管部门根据水环境质量改善目标和水污染防治要求，确定长江流域各省级行政区域重点污染物排放总量控制指标。长江流域水质超标的水功能区，应当实施更严格的污染物排放总量削减要求。企业事业单位应当按照要求，采取污染物排放总量控制措施。  
国务院自然资源主管部门负责统筹长江流域新增建设用地总量控制和计划安排。

**Article 21** The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall coordinate the rational allocation, unified scheduling, and efficient utilization of water resources in the Yangtze River Basin, and organize the implementation of the management systems for total water withdrawal control and water consumption intensity control.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, in accordance with the targets for improving water environment quality and the requirements for preventing and controlling water pollution, determine the total discharge control targets for key pollutants in each provincial-level administrative region in the Yangtze River Basin. For water functional areas in the Yangtze River Basin where water quality exceeds the standards, more stringent requirements for total pollutant discharge reduction shall be implemented. Enterprises and institutions shall take measures for total pollutant discharge control in accordance with the requirements.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall be responsible for coordinating the total amount control and planning arrangement of newly added construction land in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　长江流域省级人民政府根据本行政区域的生态环境和资源利用状况，制定生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案后实施。生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单应当与国土空间规划相衔接。  
长江流域产业结构和布局应当与长江流域生态系统和资源环境承载能力相适应。禁止在长江流域重点生态功能区布局对生态系统有严重影响的产业。禁止重污染企业和项目向长江中上游转移。

**Article 22** The provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, based on the ecological environment and resource utilization conditions of their respective administrative regions, formulate ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists, which shall be submitted to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation before implementation. The ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists shall be aligned with the territorial space plan.  
The industrial structure and layout in the Yangtze River Basin shall be compatible with the carrying capacity of the ecosystem and resource environment of the Yangtze River Basin. It is prohibited to locate industries that have a serious impact on the ecosystem in key ecological function areas of the Yangtze River Basin. It is prohibited to transfer heavily polluting enterprises and projects to the middle and upper reaches of the Yangtze River.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家加强对长江流域水能资源开发利用的管理。因国家发展战略和国计民生需要，在长江流域新建大中型水电工程，应当经科学论证，并报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准。  
对长江流域已建小水电工程，不符合生态保护要求的，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织分类整改或者采取措施逐步退出。

**Article 23** The State shall strengthen the management of the development and utilization of water energy resources in the Yangtze River Basin. The construction of new large and medium-sized hydropower projects in the Yangtze River Basin shall be subject to scientific demonstration and shall be submitted to the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council for approval, when necessary for the national development strategy and the national economy and people's livelihood.  
For existing small hydropower projects in the Yangtze River Basin that do not meet the requirements of ecological protection, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize classified rectification or take measures to phase them out gradually.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家对长江干流和重要支流源头实行严格保护，设立国家公园等自然保护地，保护国家生态安全屏障。

**Article 24** The State shall implement strict protection on the sources of the main stream and important tributaries of the Yangtze River, establish national parks and other protected natural areas, and protect the national ecological security barrier.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国务院水行政主管部门加强长江流域河道、湖泊保护工作。长江流域县级以上地方人民政府负责划定河道、湖泊管理范围，并向社会公告，实行严格的河湖保护，禁止非法侵占河湖水域。

**Article 25** The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall strengthen the protection of river courses and lakes in the Yangtze River Basin. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall be responsible for delineating the management scope of river courses and lakes, and shall announce it to the public, implement strict protection of rivers and lakes, and prohibit the illegal occupation of river and lake waters.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家对长江流域河湖岸线实施特殊管制。国家长江流域协调机制统筹协调国务院自然资源、水行政、生态环境、住房和城乡建设、农业农村、交通运输、林业和草原等部门和长江流域省级人民政府划定河湖岸线保护范围，制定河湖岸线保护规划，严格控制岸线开发建设，促进岸线合理高效利用。  
禁止在长江干支流岸线一公里范围内新建、扩建化工园区和化工项目。  
禁止在长江干流岸线三公里范围内和重要支流岸线一公里范围内新建、改建、扩建尾矿库；但是以提升安全、生态环境保护水平为目的的改建除外。

**Article 26** The State shall implement special regulation on the shorelines of rivers and lakes in the Yangtze River Basin. The national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate the departments of natural resources, water administration, ecological environment, housing and urban-rural development, agriculture and rural affairs, transport, forestry and grassland of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin to delineate the protection scope of river and lake shorelines, formulate plans for the protection of river and lake shorelines, strictly control the development and construction of shorelines, and promote the rational and efficient utilization of shorelines.  
It is prohibited to construct or expand chemical industrial parks and chemical projects within a distance of one kilometer from the shorelines of the main stream and tributaries of the Yangtze River.  
It is prohibited to construct, reconstruct, or expand tailings ponds within a distance of three kilometers from the shoreline of the main stream of the Yangtze River and within a distance of one kilometer from the shorelines of important tributaries; however, reconstruction for the purposes of improving safety and ecological environment protection levels shall be excluded.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院自然资源、水行政、生态环境、农业农村、林业和草原主管部门在长江流域水生生物重要栖息地科学划定禁止航行区域和限制航行区域。  
禁止船舶在划定的禁止航行区域内航行。因国家发展战略和国计民生需要，在水生生物重要栖息地禁止航行区域内航行的，应当由国务院交通运输主管部门商国务院农业农村主管部门同意，并应当采取必要措施，减少对重要水生生物的干扰。  
严格限制在长江流域生态保护红线、自然保护地、水生生物重要栖息地水域实施航道整治工程；确需整治的，应当经科学论证，并依法办理相关手续。

**Article 27** The competent department of transport of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent departments of natural resources, water administration, ecological environment, agriculture and rural affairs, and forestry and grassland of the State Council, scientifically designate no-navigation areas and restricted navigation areas in important habitats of aquatic organisms in the Yangtze River Basin.  
It is prohibited for vessels to navigate in the designated no-navigation areas. Where navigation in the no-navigation areas of important habitats of aquatic organisms is necessary for the national development strategy and the national economy and people's livelihood, the competent department of transport of the State Council shall consult with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for consent, and shall take necessary measures to reduce interference with important aquatic organisms.  
The implementation of waterway regulation projects in waters within the ecological protection redlines, protected natural areas, and important habitats of aquatic organisms in the Yangtze River Basin shall be strictly restricted; where regulation is truly necessary, scientific demonstration shall be conducted, and relevant procedures shall be handled in accordance with law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家建立长江流域河道采砂规划和许可制度。长江流域河道采砂应当依法取得国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门的许可。  
国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府依法划定禁止采砂区和禁止采砂期，严格控制采砂区域、采砂总量和采砂区域内的采砂船舶数量。禁止在长江流域禁止采砂区和禁止采砂期从事采砂活动。  
国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门组织长江流域有关地方人民政府及其有关部门开展长江流域河道非法采砂联合执法工作。

**Article 28** The State shall establish a planning and licensing system for sand extraction in river channels in the Yangtze River Basin. Sand extraction in river channels of the Yangtze River Basin shall obtain a permit from the relevant basin management agency under the competent department of water administration of the State Council or the competent department of water administration of the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with law.  
The relevant basin management agency under the competent department of water administration of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with law, designate areas and periods where sand extraction is prohibited, and strictly control the sand extraction areas, total amount of sand extraction, and the number of sand extraction vessels within the sand extraction areas. It is prohibited to engage in sand extraction activities in the prohibited sand extraction areas and during the prohibited sand extraction periods in the Yangtze River Basin.  
The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the relevant local people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments to carry out joint law enforcement against illegal sand extraction in river channels in the Yangtze River Basin.

### 第三章　资源保护

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　长江流域水资源保护与利用，应当根据流域综合规划，优先满足城乡居民生活用水，保障基本生态用水，并统筹农业、工业用水以及航运等需要。

**Article 29** The protection and utilization of water resources in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the comprehensive basin plan, give priority to meeting domestic water use of urban and rural residents, ensure basic ecological water use, and coordinate agricultural and industrial water use as well as navigation needs.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构商长江流域省级人民政府依法制定跨省河流水量分配方案，报国务院或者国务院授权的部门批准后实施。制定长江流域跨省河流水量分配方案应当征求国务院有关部门的意见。长江流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门制定本行政区域的长江流域水量分配方案，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构或者长江流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门依据批准的水量分配方案，编制年度水量分配方案和调度计划，明确相关河段和控制断面流量水量、水位管控要求。

**Article 30** The relevant basin management agency under the competent department of water administration of the State Council shall, in consultation with the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin, formulate water allocation plans for trans-provincial rivers in accordance with law, which shall be submitted to the State Council or the department authorized by the State Council for approval before implementation. The formulation of water allocation plans for trans-provincial rivers in the Yangtze River Basin shall seek opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council. The competent departments of water administration of the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall formulate water allocation plans for the Yangtze River Basin within their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the people's governments at the same level for approval before implementation.  
The relevant basin management agency under the competent department of water administration of the State Council or the competent departments of water administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, based on the approved water allocation plans, prepare annual water allocation plans and scheduling plans, and specify the flow, water volume, and water level control requirements for relevant river sections and control sections.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家加强长江流域生态用水保障。国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门提出长江干流、重要支流和重要湖泊控制断面的生态流量管控指标。其他河湖生态流量管控指标由长江流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门确定。  
国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构应当将生态水量纳入年度水量调度计划，保证河湖基本生态用水需求，保障枯水期和鱼类产卵期生态流量、重要湖泊的水量和水位，保障长江河口咸淡水平衡。  
长江干流、重要支流和重要湖泊上游的水利水电、航运枢纽等工程应当将生态用水调度纳入日常运行调度规程，建立常规生态调度机制，保证河湖生态流量；其下泄流量不符合生态流量泄放要求的，由县级以上人民政府水行政主管部门提出整改措施并监督实施。

**Article 31** The State shall strengthen the guarantee of ecological water use in the Yangtze River Basin. The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, propose ecological flow control indicators for the control sections of the main stream, important tributaries, and important lakes of the Yangtze River. The ecological flow control indicators for other rivers and lakes shall be determined by the competent departments of water administration of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin jointly with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level.  
The relevant basin management agency under the competent department of water administration of the State Council shall incorporate ecological water volume into the annual water scheduling plan, ensure the basic ecological water needs of rivers and lakes, guarantee the ecological flow during dry seasons and fish spawning periods, the water volume and water level of important lakes, and ensure the saltwater-freshwater balance at the Yangtze River estuary.  
Projects such as water conservancy and hydropower projects and navigation hubs located upstream of the main stream, important tributaries, and important lakes of the Yangtze River shall incorporate ecological water scheduling into their daily operation scheduling procedures, establish a routine ecological scheduling mechanism, and ensure the ecological flow of rivers and lakes; where the discharge flow of such projects fails to meet the ecological flow discharge requirements, the competent departments of water administration of the people's governments at or above the county level shall propose rectification measures and supervise their implementation.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国务院有关部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，加快病险水库除险加固，推进堤防和蓄滞洪区建设，提升洪涝灾害防御工程标准，加强水工程联合调度，开展河道泥沙观测和河势调查，建立与经济社会发展相适应的防洪减灾工程和非工程体系，提高防御水旱灾害的整体能力。

**Article 32** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall take measures to accelerate the reinforcement of dangerous reservoirs, promote the construction of embankments and flood storage and detention areas, raise the standards of flood and waterlogging disaster prevention projects, strengthen joint scheduling of water projects, conduct river sediment observation and river regime surveys, and establish flood prevention and disaster reduction engineering and non-engineering systems that are compatible with economic and social development, so as to enhance the overall capacity to prevent flood and drought disasters.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家对跨长江流域调水实行科学论证，加强控制和管理。实施跨长江流域调水应当优先保障调出区域及其下游区域的用水安全和生态安全，统筹调出区域和调入区域用水需求。

**Article 33** The State shall conduct scientific demonstration on water diversion across the Yangtze River Basin, and strengthen control and management. The implementation of water diversion across the Yangtze River Basin shall give priority to ensuring the water security and ecological security of the water-exporting area and its downstream areas, and coordinate the water needs of the water-exporting area and the water-importing area.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家加强长江流域饮用水水源地保护。国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定长江流域饮用水水源地名录。长江流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门制定本行政区域的其他饮用水水源地名录。  
长江流域省级人民政府组织划定饮用水水源保护区，加强饮用水水源保护，保障饮用水安全。

**Article 34** The State shall strengthen the protection of drinking water sources in the Yangtze River Basin. The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a list of drinking water sources in the Yangtze River Basin. The competent departments of water administration of the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, formulate lists of other drinking water sources within their respective administrative regions.  
The provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall organize the delineation of drinking water source protection zones, strengthen the protection of drinking water sources, and ensure the safety of drinking water.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当合理布局饮用水水源取水口，制定饮用水安全突发事件应急预案，加强饮用水备用应急水源建设，对饮用水水源的水环境质量进行实时监测。

**Article 35** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall reasonably arrange the intake points of drinking water sources, formulate emergency response plans for drinking water safety incidents, strengthen the construction of emergency standby drinking water sources, and conduct real-time monitoring of the water environment quality of drinking water sources.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　丹江口库区及其上游所在地县级以上地方人民政府应当按照饮用水水源地安全保障区、水质影响控制区、水源涵养生态建设区管理要求，加强山水林田湖草整体保护，增强水源涵养能力，保障水质稳定达标。

**Article 36** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Danjiangkou Reservoir area and its upstream areas shall, in accordance with the management requirements for the drinking water source security protection area, water quality impact control area, and water conservation ecological construction area, strengthen the overall protection of mountains, waters, forests, farmlands, lakes, and grasslands, enhance water conservation capacity, and ensure that water quality stably meets the standards.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家加强长江流域地下水资源保护。长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当定期调查评估地下水资源状况，监测地下水水量、水位、水环境质量，并采取相应风险防范措施，保障地下水资源安全。

**Article 37** The State shall strengthen the protection of groundwater resources in the Yangtze River Basin. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall regularly investigate and assess the conditions of groundwater resources, monitor the groundwater volume, water level, and water environment quality, and take corresponding risk prevention measures to ensure the security of groundwater resources.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门确定长江流域农业、工业用水效率目标，加强用水计量和监测设施建设；完善规划和建设项目水资源论证制度；加强对高耗水行业、重点用水单位的用水定额管理，严格控制高耗水项目建设。

**Article 38** The competent department of water administration of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, determine the targets for water use efficiency in agriculture and industry in the Yangtze River Basin, strengthen the construction of water metering and monitoring facilities; improve the water resources demonstration system for plans and construction projects; strengthen the management of water use quotas for high water-consuming industries and key water users, and strictly control the construction of high water-consuming projects.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家统筹长江流域自然保护地体系建设。国务院和长江流域省级人民政府在长江流域重要典型生态系统的完整分布区、生态环境敏感区以及珍贵野生动植物天然集中分布区和重要栖息地、重要自然遗迹分布区等区域，依法设立国家公园、自然保护区、自然公园等自然保护地。

**Article 39** The State shall coordinate the construction of a protected natural area system in the Yangtze River Basin. The State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with law, establish national parks, nature reserves, natural parks, and other protected natural areas in areas such as the complete distribution areas of important and typical ecosystems, ecologically sensitive areas, natural concentration areas and important habitats of precious wild animals and plants, and important natural relic distribution areas in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院和长江流域省级人民政府应当依法在长江流域重要生态区、生态状况脆弱区划定公益林，实施严格管理。国家对长江流域天然林实施严格保护，科学划定天然林保护重点区域。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对长江流域草原资源的保护，对具有调节气候、涵养水源、保持水土、防风固沙等特殊作用的基本草原实施严格管理。  
国务院林业和草原主管部门和长江流域省级人民政府林业和草原主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门，根据不同生态区位、生态系统功能和生物多样性保护的需要，发布长江流域国家重要湿地、地方重要湿地名录及保护范围，加强对长江流域湿地的保护和管理，维护湿地生态功能和生物多样性。

**Article 40** The State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with law, designate public welfare forests in important ecological areas and areas with fragile ecological conditions in the Yangtze River Basin, and implement strict management. The State shall implement strict protection of natural forests in the Yangtze River Basin, and scientifically designate key areas for natural forest protection.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall strengthen the protection of grassland resources in the Yangtze River Basin, and implement strict management of basic grasslands that have special functions such as regulating climate, conserving water, retaining soil and water, and preventing wind and sand.  
The competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council and the competent departments of forestry and grassland of the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, publish lists and protection scopes of nationally important wetlands and locally important wetlands in the Yangtze River Basin based on different ecological zones, ecosystem functions, and the needs of biodiversity protection, and strengthen the protection and management of wetlands in the Yangtze River Basin, and maintain the ecological functions and biodiversity of wetlands.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府建立长江流域水生生物完整性指数评价体系，组织开展长江流域水生生物完整性评价，并将结果作为评估长江流域生态系统总体状况的重要依据。长江流域水生生物完整性指数应当与长江流域水环境质量标准相衔接。

**Article 41** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin, establish an evaluation system for the aquatic organism integrity index in the Yangtze River Basin, organize and carry out evaluations of the aquatic organism integrity in the Yangtze River Basin, and use the results as an important basis for assessing the overall condition of the ecosystem in the Yangtze River Basin. The aquatic organism integrity index for the Yangtze River Basin shall be aligned with the water environment quality standards for the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国务院农业农村主管部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当制定长江流域珍贵、濒危水生野生动植物保护计划，对长江流域珍贵、濒危水生野生动植物实行重点保护。  
国家鼓励有条件的单位开展对长江流域江豚、白鱀豚、白鲟、中华鲟、长江鲟、鯮、鲥、四川白甲鱼、川陕哲罗鲑、胭脂鱼、鳤、圆口铜鱼、多鳞白甲鱼、华鲮、鲈鲤和葛仙米、弧形藻、眼子菜、水菜花等水生野生动植物生境特征和种群动态的研究，建设人工繁育和科普教育基地，组织开展水生生物救护。  
禁止在长江流域开放水域养殖、投放外来物种或者其他非本地物种种质资源。

**Article 42** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall formulate protection plans for precious and endangered species of aquatic wild animals and plants in the Yangtze River Basin, and implement key protection for such species.  
The State encourages qualified entities to conduct research on the habitat characteristics and population dynamics of aquatic wild animals and plants in the Yangtze River Basin, such as the Yangtze finless porpoise, Chinese river dolphin, Chinese paddlefish, Chinese sturgeon, Yangtze sturgeon, yellowfin, Reeves shad, Sichuan white fish, Sichuan-Shaanxi taimen, Chinese sucker, darkbarbel catfish, large-scale loach, Chinese bream, barbel chub, and Ge Xian Mi (Nostoc sphaeroides), arc-shaped algae, pondweed, water cabbage, etc., to build artificial breeding and science education bases, and to organize and carry out rescue of aquatic organisms.  
It is prohibited to farm or release alien species or other non-native species germplasm resources in open waters of the Yangtze River Basin.

### 第四章　水污染防治

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院生态环境主管部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府应当采取有效措施，加大对长江流域的水污染防治、监管力度，预防、控制和减少水环境污染。

**Article 43** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall take effective measures to strengthen the prevention, control, and supervision of water pollution in the Yangtze River Basin, and to prevent, control, and reduce water environment pollution.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国务院生态环境主管部门负责制定长江流域水环境质量标准，对国家水环境质量标准中未作规定的项目可以补充规定；对国家水环境质量标准中已经规定的项目，可以作出更加严格的规定。制定长江流域水环境质量标准应当征求国务院有关部门和有关省级人民政府的意见。长江流域省级人民政府可以制定严于长江流域水环境质量标准的地方水环境质量标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 44** The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating water environment quality standards for the Yangtze River Basin. It may supplement provisions for items not specified in the national water environment quality standards; and may make more stringent provisions for items already specified in the national water environment quality standards. The formulation of water environment quality standards for the Yangtze River Basin shall seek opinions from the relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant provincial people's governments. The provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin may formulate local water environment quality standards that are more stringent than the water environment quality standards for the Yangtze River Basin, and shall report them to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　长江流域省级人民政府应当对没有国家水污染物排放标准的特色产业、特有污染物，或者国家有明确要求的特定水污染源或者水污染物，补充制定地方水污染物排放标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。  
有下列情形之一的，长江流域省级人民政府应当制定严于国家水污染物排放标准的地方水污染物排放标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案：  
（一）产业密集、水环境问题突出的；  
（二）现有水污染物排放标准不能满足所辖长江流域水环境质量要求的；  
（三）流域或者区域水环境形势复杂，无法适用统一的水污染物排放标准的。

**Article 45** The provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall, for characteristic industries with no national water pollutant discharge standards, unique pollutants, or specific water pollution sources or water pollutants for which the State has explicit requirements, supplement the formulation of local water pollutant discharge standards, and report them to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation.  
In any of the following circumstances, the provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall formulate local water pollutant discharge standards that are more stringent than the national water pollutant discharge standards, and report them to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) where industries are dense and water environment problems are prominent;  
(2) where the existing water pollutant discharge standards cannot meet the water environment quality requirements of their respective administrative regions within the Yangtze River Basin; or  
(3) where the water environment situation of the basin or region is complex, making it impossible to apply uniform water pollutant discharge standards.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　长江流域省级人民政府制定本行政区域的总磷污染控制方案，并组织实施。对磷矿、磷肥生产集中的长江干支流，有关省级人民政府应当制定更加严格的总磷排放管控要求，有效控制总磷排放总量。  
磷矿开采加工、磷肥和含磷农药制造等企业，应当按照排污许可要求，采取有效措施控制总磷排放浓度和排放总量；对排污口和周边环境进行总磷监测，依法公开监测信息。

**Article 46** The provincial people's governments of the Yangtze River Basin shall formulate total phosphorus pollution control plans for their respective administrative regions, and organize their implementation. For the main stream and tributaries of the Yangtze River where phosphate rock and phosphate fertilizer production are concentrated, the relevant provincial people's governments shall formulate more stringent total phosphorus discharge control requirements to effectively control the total amount of total phosphorus discharge.  
Enterprises engaged in phosphate rock mining and processing, phosphate fertilizer and phosphorus-containing pesticide manufacturing, etc., shall, in accordance with the requirements of pollutant discharge permits, take effective measures to control the concentration and total amount of total phosphorus discharge; monitor total phosphorus at the discharge outlets and the surrounding environment, and disclose monitoring information in accordance with law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹长江流域城乡污水集中处理设施及配套管网建设，并保障其正常运行，提高城乡污水收集处理能力。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织对本行政区域的江河、湖泊排污口开展排查整治，明确责任主体，实施分类管理。  
在长江流域江河、湖泊新设、改设或者扩大排污口，应当按照国家有关规定报经有管辖权的生态环境主管部门或者长江流域生态环境监督管理机构同意。对未达到水质目标的水功能区，除污水集中处理设施排污口外，应当严格控制新设、改设或者扩大排污口。

**Article 47** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate the construction of centralized urban and rural sewage treatment facilities and supporting pipeline networks in the Yangtze River Basin, ensure their normal operation, and improve the capacity for urban and rural sewage collection and treatment.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall organize the investigation and remediation of discharge outlets into rivers and lakes within their respective administrative regions, clarify the responsible entities, and implement classified management.  
The establishment, modification, or expansion of discharge outlets into rivers and lakes in the Yangtze River Basin shall be subject to approval by the competent department of ecological environment with jurisdiction or the ecological environment supervision and management agency for the Yangtze River Basin in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. For water functional areas that have not met the water quality targets, the establishment, modification, or expansion of discharge outlets, except for those of centralized sewage treatment facilities, shall be strictly controlled.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家加强长江流域农业面源污染防治。长江流域农业生产应当科学使用农业投入品，减少化肥、农药施用，推广有机肥使用，科学处置农用薄膜、农作物秸秆等农业废弃物。

**Article 48** The State shall strengthen the prevention and control of agricultural non-point source pollution in the Yangtze River Basin. Agricultural production in the Yangtze River Basin shall make scientific use of agricultural inputs, reduce the application of chemical fertilizers and pesticides, promote the use of organic fertilizers, and scientifically dispose of agricultural wastes such as agricultural film and crop straw.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　禁止在长江流域河湖管理范围内倾倒、填埋、堆放、弃置、处理固体废物。长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对固体废物非法转移和倾倒的联防联控。

**Article 49** It is prohibited to dump, landfill, pile up, abandon, or dispose of solid waste within the management scope of rivers and lakes in the Yangtze River Basin. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall strengthen joint prevention and control of illegal transfer and dumping of solid waste.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织对沿河湖垃圾填埋场、加油站、矿山、尾矿库、危险废物处置场、化工园区和化工项目等地下水重点污染源及周边地下水环境风险隐患开展调查评估，并采取相应风险防范和整治措施。

**Article 50** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall organize investigation and assessment of key groundwater pollution sources such as landfills along rivers and lakes, gas stations, mines, tailings ponds, hazardous waste disposal sites, chemical industrial parks and chemical projects, as well as the surrounding groundwater environmental risks and hazards, and shall take corresponding risk prevention and remediation measures.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家建立长江流域危险货物运输船舶污染责任保险与财务担保相结合机制。具体办法由国务院交通运输主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
禁止在长江流域水上运输剧毒化学品和国家规定禁止通过内河运输的其他危险化学品。长江流域县级以上地方人民政府交通运输主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门加强对长江流域危险化学品运输的管控。

**Article 51** The State shall establish a mechanism combining pollution liability insurance with financial guarantees for ships transporting dangerous goods in the Yangtze River Basin. Specific measures shall be formulated by the transport department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The transport of acute toxic chemicals and other dangerous chemicals that are prohibited from being transported through inland waters by State regulations on the waters of the Yangtze River Basin is prohibited. The transport departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level, shall strengthen the control over the transport of dangerous chemicals in the Yangtze River Basin.

### 第五章　生态环境修复

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家对长江流域生态系统实行自然恢复为主、自然恢复与人工修复相结合的系统治理。国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院有关部门编制长江流域生态环境修复规划，组织实施重大生态环境修复工程，统筹推进长江流域各项生态环境修复工作。

**Article 52** The State shall implement systematic governance of the ecosystem of the Yangtze River Basin, giving priority to natural restoration and combining natural restoration with artificial restoration. The natural resources department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall prepare a plan for the ecological environment restoration of the Yangtze River Basin, organize the implementation of major ecological environment restoration projects, and coordinate the promotion of various ecological environment restoration efforts in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家对长江流域重点水域实行严格捕捞管理。在长江流域水生生物保护区全面禁止生产性捕捞；在国家规定的期限内，长江干流和重要支流、大型通江湖泊、长江河口规定区域等重点水域全面禁止天然渔业资源的生产性捕捞。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府加强长江流域禁捕执法工作，严厉查处电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等破坏渔业资源和生态环境的捕捞行为。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定做好长江流域重点水域退捕渔民的补偿、转产和社会保障工作。  
长江流域其他水域禁捕、限捕管理办法由县级以上地方人民政府制定。

**Article 53** The State shall implement strict fishing management in key waters of the Yangtze River Basin. Productive fishing shall be completely prohibited in aquatic biological reserves in the Yangtze River Basin; within the period prescribed by the State, productive fishing of natural fishery resources shall be completely prohibited in key waters such as the main stream and important tributaries of the Yangtze River, large river-connected lakes, and prescribed areas of the Yangtze River estuary. Specific measures shall be formulated by the agriculture and rural affairs department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The agriculture and rural affairs department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin, shall strengthen the enforcement work on fishing bans in the Yangtze River Basin, and strictly investigate and punish fishing acts that damage fishery resources and the ecological environment, such as electric fishing, poison fishing, and explosive fishing.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State, properly handle the compensation, job transfer, and social security for fishermen who withdraw from fishing in key waters of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The measures for the management of fishing bans and restrictions in other waters of the Yangtze River Basin shall be formulated by the local people's governments at or above the county level.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定并组织实施长江干流和重要支流的河湖水系连通修复方案，长江流域省级人民政府制定并组织实施本行政区域的长江流域河湖水系连通修复方案，逐步改善长江流域河湖连通状况，恢复河湖生态流量，维护河湖水系生态功能。

**Article 54** The water administrative department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, shall formulate and implement a plan for the restoration of river-lake water system connectivity of the main stream and important tributaries of the Yangtze River; the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin shall formulate and implement a plan for the restoration of river-lake water system connectivity of the Yangtze River Basin within their respective administrative regions, gradually improving the connectivity of rivers and lakes in the Yangtze River Basin, restoring the ecological flow of rivers and lakes, and maintaining the ecological functions of the river-lake water system.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家长江流域协调机制统筹协调国务院自然资源、水行政、生态环境、住房和城乡建设、农业农村、交通运输、林业和草原等部门和长江流域省级人民政府制定长江流域河湖岸线修复规范，确定岸线修复指标。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府按照长江流域河湖岸线保护规划、修复规范和指标要求，制定并组织实施河湖岸线修复计划，保障自然岸线比例，恢复河湖岸线生态功能。  
禁止违法利用、占用长江流域河湖岸线。

**Article 55** The national coordination mechanism for the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate the natural resources, water administration, ecological environment, housing and urban-rural construction, agriculture and rural affairs, transport, forestry and grassland departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin to formulate specifications for the restoration of river-lake shorelines in the Yangtze River Basin and determine the indicators for shoreline restoration.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the river-lake shoreline protection plan, restoration specifications, and indicator requirements of the Yangtze River Basin, formulate and implement a river-lake shoreline restoration plan, ensure the proportion of natural shorelines, and restore the ecological functions of river-lake shorelines.  
It is prohibited to illegally use or occupy river-lake shorelines in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院有关部门会同长江流域有关省级人民政府加强对三峡库区、丹江口库区等重点库区消落区的生态环境保护和修复，因地制宜实施退耕还林还草还湿，禁止施用化肥、农药，科学调控水库水位，加强库区水土保持和地质灾害防治工作，保障消落区良好生态功能。

**Article 56** The relevant departments of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin, shall strengthen the ecological environment protection and restoration of the fluctuating zones of key reservoirs such as the Three Gorges Reservoir area and the Danjiangkou Reservoir area, implement the conversion of farmland to forests, grasslands, and wetlands as appropriate to local conditions, prohibit the application of chemical fertilizers and pesticides, scientifically regulate the water level of reservoirs, strengthen soil and water conservation and geological disaster prevention in the reservoir areas, and ensure the good ecological functions of the fluctuating zones.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府林业和草原主管部门负责组织实施长江流域森林、草原、湿地修复计划，科学推进森林、草原、湿地修复工作，加大退化天然林、草原和受损湿地修复力度。

**Article 57** The forestry and grassland departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of plans for the restoration of forests, grasslands, and wetlands in the Yangtze River Basin, scientifically promote the restoration of forests, grasslands, and wetlands, and intensify the restoration of degraded natural forests, grasslands, and damaged wetlands.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家加大对太湖、鄱阳湖、洞庭湖、巢湖、滇池等重点湖泊实施生态环境修复的支持力度。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织开展富营养化湖泊的生态环境修复，采取调整产业布局规模、实施控制性水工程统一调度、生态补水、河湖连通等综合措施，改善和恢复湖泊生态系统的质量和功能；对氮磷浓度严重超标的湖泊，应当在影响湖泊水质的汇水区，采取措施削减化肥用量，禁止使用含磷洗涤剂，全面清理投饵、投肥养殖。

**Article 58** The State shall increase support for the ecological environment restoration of key lakes such as Taihu Lake, Poyang Lake, Dongting Lake, Chaohu Lake, and Dianchi Lake.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall organize the ecological environment restoration of eutrophic lakes and take comprehensive measures such as adjusting the industrial layout and scale, implementing unified regulation of control water projects, ecological water replenishment, and river-lake connectivity to improve and restore the quality and functions of the lake ecosystem; for lakes with severely excessive concentrations of nitrogen and phosphorus, measures shall be taken in the catchment areas affecting the lake water quality to reduce the amount of chemical fertilizers used, prohibit the use of phosphorus-containing detergents, and comprehensively clean up bait-feeding and fertilizer-feeding aquaculture.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院林业和草原、农业农村主管部门应当对长江流域数量急剧下降或者极度濒危的野生动植物和受到严重破坏的栖息地、天然集中分布区、破碎化的典型生态系统制定修复方案和行动计划，修建迁地保护设施，建立野生动植物遗传资源基因库，进行抢救性修复。  
在长江流域水生生物产卵场、索饵场、越冬场和洄游通道等重要栖息地应当实施生态环境修复和其他保护措施。对鱼类等水生生物洄游产生阻隔的涉水工程应当结合实际采取建设过鱼设施、河湖连通、生态调度、灌江纳苗、基因保存、增殖放流、人工繁育等多种措施，充分满足水生生物的生态需求。

**Article 59** The forestry and grassland department and the agriculture and rural affairs department of the State Council shall formulate restoration plans and action plans for wild animals and plants whose numbers have declined sharply or are extremely endangered in the Yangtze River Basin, as well as severely damaged habitats, naturally concentrated distribution areas, and fragmented typical ecosystems, construct ex-situ protection facilities, establish a gene bank of wild animal and plant genetic resources, and carry out rescue restoration.  
In important habitats such as spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, and migration channels of aquatic organisms in the Yangtze River Basin, ecological environment restoration and other protection measures shall be implemented. For water-related projects that block the migration of fish and other aquatic organisms, a variety of measures such as constructing fish passage facilities, river-lake connectivity, ecological regulation, connecting rivers with lakes for fish introduction, gene preservation, stock enhancement and release, and artificial breeding shall be taken according to the actual situation to fully meet the ecological needs of aquatic organisms.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国务院水行政主管部门会同国务院有关部门和长江河口所在地人民政府按照陆海统筹、河海联动的要求，制定实施长江河口生态环境修复和其他保护措施方案，加强对水、沙、盐、潮滩、生物种群的综合监测，采取有效措施防止海水入侵和倒灌，维护长江河口良好生态功能。

**Article 60** The water administrative department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's government at the Yangtze River estuary, shall, in accordance with the requirements of land-sea coordination and river-sea linkage, formulate and implement plans for the ecological environment restoration and other protection measures of the Yangtze River estuary, strengthen comprehensive monitoring of water, sediment, salt, tidal flats, and biological populations, and take effective measures to prevent seawater intrusion and backflow, and maintain good ecological functions of the Yangtze River estuary.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　长江流域水土流失重点预防区和重点治理区的县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，防治水土流失。生态保护红线范围内的水土流失地块，以自然恢复为主，按照规定有计划地实施退耕还林还草还湿；划入自然保护地核心保护区的永久基本农田，依法有序退出并予以补划。  
禁止在长江流域水土流失严重、生态脆弱的区域开展可能造成水土流失的生产建设活动。确因国家发展战略和国计民生需要建设的，应当经科学论证，并依法办理审批手续。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当对石漠化的土地因地制宜采取综合治理措施，修复生态系统，防止土地石漠化蔓延。

**Article 61** The local people's governments at or above the county level in key areas for water and soil erosion prevention and key treatment areas in the Yangtze River Basin shall take measures to prevent and control water and soil erosion. For water and soil erosion plots within the ecological protection redline, natural restoration shall be the main approach, and the conversion of farmland to forests, grasslands, and wetlands shall be implemented in a planned manner according to regulations; permanent basic farmland designated as core protection areas of nature reserves shall be legally and orderly withdrawn and reallocated.  
It is prohibited to carry out production and construction activities that may cause water and soil erosion in areas with severe water and soil erosion and fragile ecology in the Yangtze River Basin. If construction is truly necessary due to national development strategies and the national economy and people's livelihood, it shall be subject to scientific demonstration and approval procedures shall be handled according to law.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, as appropriate to local conditions, take comprehensive treatment measures for rocky desertification land, restore the ecosystem, and prevent the spread of rocky desertification.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜采取消除地质灾害隐患、土地复垦、恢复植被、防治污染等措施，加快历史遗留矿山生态环境修复工作，并加强对在建和运行中矿山的监督管理，督促采矿权人切实履行矿山污染防治和生态环境修复责任。

**Article 62** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, as appropriate to local conditions, take measures such as eliminating geological hazard risks, land reclamation, restoring vegetation, and preventing and controlling pollution, accelerate the ecological environment restoration of historical legacy mines, and strengthen the supervision and management of mines under construction and in operation, urging mining rights holders to effectively fulfill their responsibilities for mine pollution prevention and ecological environment restoration.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　长江流域中下游地区县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜在项目、资金、人才、管理等方面，对长江流域江河源头和上游地区实施生态环境修复和其他保护措施给予支持，提升长江流域生态脆弱区实施生态环境修复和其他保护措施的能力。  
国家按照政策支持、企业和社会参与、市场化运作的原则，鼓励社会资本投入长江流域生态环境修复。

**Article 63** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River Basin shall, as appropriate to local conditions, provide support in terms of projects, funds, talents, management, etc., for the implementation of ecological environment restoration and other protection measures in the river source and upstream areas of the Yangtze River Basin, and enhance the capacity of ecologically fragile areas in the Yangtze River Basin to implement ecological environment restoration and other protection measures.  
The State, in accordance with the principles of policy support, enterprise and social participation, and market-oriented operation, encourages social capital to invest in the ecological environment restoration of the Yangtze River Basin.

### 第六章　绿色发展

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　国务院有关部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府应当按照长江流域发展规划、国土空间规划的要求，调整产业结构，优化产业布局，推进长江流域绿色发展。

**Article 64** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the requirements of the Yangtze River Basin development plan and the national spatial plan, adjust the industrial structure, optimize the industrial layout, and promote the green development of the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　国务院和长江流域地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当协同推进乡村振兴战略和新型城镇化战略的实施，统筹城乡基础设施建设和产业发展，建立健全全民覆盖、普惠共享、城乡一体的基本公共服务体系，促进长江流域城乡融合发展。

**Article 65** The State Council and the local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall coordinate the implementation of the rural revitalization strategy and the new urbanization strategy, coordinate urban and rural infrastructure construction and industrial development, establish and improve a basic public service system covering all people, benefiting all, and integrating urban and rural areas, and promote the integrated development of urban and rural areas in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当推动钢铁、石油、化工、有色金属、建材、船舶等产业升级改造，提升技术装备水平；推动造纸、制革、电镀、印染、有色金属、农药、氮肥、焦化、原料药制造等企业实施清洁化改造。企业应当通过技术创新减少资源消耗和污染物排放。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施加快重点地区危险化学品生产企业搬迁改造。

**Article 66** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall promote the upgrading and transformation of industries such as steel, petroleum, chemicals, non-ferrous metals, building materials, and ships, and improve the level of technical equipment; promote the clean transformation of enterprises such as papermaking, leather making, electroplating, printing and dyeing, non-ferrous metals, pesticides, nitrogen fertilizers, coking, and raw material drug manufacturing. Enterprises shall reduce resource consumption and pollutant emissions through technological innovation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall take measures to accelerate the relocation and transformation of hazardous chemical production enterprises in key areas.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国务院有关部门会同长江流域省级人民政府建立开发区绿色发展评估机制，并组织对各类开发区的资源能源节约集约利用、生态环境保护等情况开展定期评估。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当根据评估结果对开发区产业产品、节能减排措施等进行优化调整。

**Article 67** The relevant departments of the State Council, in conjunction with the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin, shall establish a green development evaluation mechanism for development zones, and organize regular evaluations of the resource and energy conservation and intensive utilization, ecological environment protection, etc., of various development zones.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, based on the evaluation results, optimize and adjust the industrial products, energy-saving and emission reduction measures, etc., of the development zones.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国家鼓励和支持在长江流域实施重点行业和重点用水单位节水技术改造，提高水资源利用效率。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强节水型城市和节水型园区建设，促进节水型行业产业和企业发展，并加快建设雨水自然积存、自然渗透、自然净化的海绵城市。

**Article 68** The State encourages and supports the implementation of water-saving technological transformation in key industries and key water-using units in the Yangtze River Basin to improve the efficiency of water resource utilization.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall strengthen the construction of water-saving cities and water-saving parks, promote the development of water-saving industries and enterprises, and accelerate the construction of sponge cities that naturally accumulate, infiltrate, and purify rainwater.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照绿色发展的要求，统筹规划、建设与管理，提升城乡人居环境质量，建设美丽城镇和美丽乡村。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照生态、环保、经济、实用的原则因地制宜组织实施厕所改造。  
国务院有关部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强对城市新区、各类开发区等使用建筑材料的管理，鼓励使用节能环保、性能高的建筑材料，建设地下综合管廊和管网。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当建设废弃土石渣综合利用信息平台，加强对生产建设活动废弃土石渣收集、清运、集中堆放的管理，鼓励开展综合利用。

**Article 69** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the requirements of green development, coordinate planning, construction, and management, improve the quality of the urban and rural living environment, and build beautiful towns and beautiful villages.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the principles of ecology, environmental protection, economy, and practicality, organize the implementation of toilet renovation as appropriate to local conditions.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen the management of building materials used in new urban areas, various development zones, etc., encourage the use of energy-saving, environmentally friendly, and high-performance building materials, and build underground comprehensive pipe galleries and pipe networks.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall build an information platform for the comprehensive utilization of waste earth, stone, and slag, strengthen the management of the collection, removal, and centralized storage of waste earth, stone, and slag from production and construction activities, and encourage comprehensive utilization.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当编制并组织实施养殖水域滩涂规划，合理划定禁养区、限养区、养殖区，科学确定养殖规模和养殖密度；强化水产养殖投入品管理，指导和规范水产养殖、增殖活动。

**Article 70** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall prepare and implement plans for aquaculture waters and tidal flats, reasonably designate prohibited aquaculture areas, restricted aquaculture areas, and aquaculture areas, scientifically determine the scale and density of aquaculture; strengthen the management of inputs for aquaculture, and guide and regulate aquaculture and enhancement activities.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家加强长江流域综合立体交通体系建设，完善港口、航道等水运基础设施，推动交通设施互联互通，实现水陆有机衔接、江海直达联运，提升长江黄金水道功能。

**Article 71** The State shall strengthen the construction of a comprehensive three-dimensional transportation system in the Yangtze River Basin, improve water transport infrastructure such as ports and waterways, promote the interconnection of transportation facilities, achieve organic connection between water and land, direct combined transport between rivers and the sea, and enhance the function of the Yangtze River Golden Waterway.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当统筹建设船舶污染物接收转运处置设施、船舶液化天然气加注站，制定港口岸电设施、船舶受电设施建设和改造计划，并组织实施。具备岸电使用条件的船舶靠港应当按照国家有关规定使用岸电，但使用清洁能源的除外。

**Article 72** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall coordinate the construction of facilities for the reception, transfer, and disposal of ship pollutants and liquefied natural gas filling stations for ships, formulate plans for the construction and renovation of port shore power facilities and ship shore receiving facilities, and organize their implementation. Ships with conditions for using shore power shall use shore power in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State when docking, except for those using clean energy.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府对长江流域港口、航道和船舶升级改造，液化天然气动力船舶等清洁能源或者新能源动力船舶建造，港口绿色设计等按照规定给予资金支持或者政策扶持。  
国务院和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府对长江流域港口岸电设施、船舶受电设施的改造和使用按照规定给予资金补贴、电价优惠等政策扶持。

**Article 73** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with regulations, provide financial support or policy support for the upgrading of ports, waterways, and ships in the Yangtze River Basin, the construction of clean energy or new energy-powered ships such as liquefied natural gas-powered ships, and green port design.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with regulations, provide policy support such as financial subsidies and electricity price concessions for the renovation and use of port shore power facilities and ship shore receiving facilities in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　长江流域地方各级人民政府加强对城乡居民绿色消费的宣传教育，并采取有效措施，支持、引导居民绿色消费。  
长江流域地方各级人民政府按照系统推进、广泛参与、突出重点、分类施策的原则，采取回收押金、限制使用易污染不易降解塑料用品、绿色设计、发展公共交通等措施，提倡简约适度、绿色低碳的生活方式。

**Article 74** The local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall strengthen the publicity and education of urban and rural residents on green consumption, and take effective measures to support and guide residents in green consumption.  
The local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the principles of systematic promotion, broad participation, highlighting key points, and classified measures, adopt measures such as deposit refunds, restrictions on the use of easily polluting and non-degradable plastic products, green design, and the development of public transportation, to promote a simple, moderate, green, and low-carbon lifestyle.

### 第七章　保障与监督

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国务院和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加大长江流域生态环境保护和修复的财政投入。  
国务院和长江流域省级人民政府按照中央与地方财政事权和支出责任划分原则，专项安排长江流域生态环境保护资金，用于长江流域生态环境保护和修复。国务院自然资源主管部门会同国务院财政、生态环境等有关部门制定合理利用社会资金促进长江流域生态环境修复的政策措施。  
国家鼓励和支持长江流域生态环境保护和修复等方面的科学技术研究开发和推广应用。  
国家鼓励金融机构发展绿色信贷、绿色债券、绿色保险等金融产品，为长江流域生态环境保护和绿色发展提供金融支持。

**Article 75** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall increase financial investment in the ecological environment protection and restoration of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the principle of division of financial powers and expenditure responsibilities between the central and local governments, specially allocate funds for ecological environment protection in the Yangtze River Basin, to be used for the ecological environment protection and restoration of the Yangtze River Basin. The natural resources department of the State Council, in conjunction with the relevant departments such as the finance and ecological environment departments of the State Council, shall formulate policy measures for the reasonable use of social funds to promote the ecological environment restoration of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research, development, and promotion and application in the field of ecological environment protection and restoration of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The State encourages financial institutions to develop financial products such as green credit, green bonds, and green insurance to provide financial support for the ecological environment protection and green development of the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国家建立长江流域生态保护补偿制度。  
国家加大财政转移支付力度，对长江干流及重要支流源头和上游的水源涵养地等生态功能重要区域予以补偿。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
国家鼓励长江流域上下游、左右岸、干支流地方人民政府之间开展横向生态保护补偿。  
国家鼓励社会资金建立市场化运作的长江流域生态保护补偿基金；鼓励相关主体之间采取自愿协商等方式开展生态保护补偿。

**Article 76** The State shall establish an ecological protection compensation system for the Yangtze River Basin.  
The State shall increase financial transfer payments and provide compensation to ecologically important areas such as water source conservation areas at the source and upstream of the main stream and important tributaries of the Yangtze River. Specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The State encourages horizontal ecological protection compensation between the local people's governments of the upstream and downstream, left and right banks, and main and tributary streams in the Yangtze River Basin.  
The State encourages social funds to establish market-oriented ecological protection compensation funds for the Yangtze River Basin; encourages relevant entities to conduct ecological protection compensation through voluntary consultation and other means.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　国家加强长江流域司法保障建设，鼓励有关单位为长江流域生态环境保护提供法律服务。  
长江流域各级行政执法机关、人民法院、人民检察院在依法查处长江保护违法行为或者办理相关案件过程中，发现存在涉嫌犯罪行为的，应当将犯罪线索移送具有侦查、调查职权的机关。

**Article 77** The State shall strengthen the construction of judicial guarantees in the Yangtze River Basin, and encourages relevant entities to provide legal services for the ecological environment protection of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The administrative law enforcement organs, people's courts, and people's procuratorates at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in the process of investigating and punishing illegal acts in the Yangtze River protection or handling related cases according to law, if they find that there are suspected criminal acts, transfer the criminal clues to the organs with investigation and inquiry functions.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国家实行长江流域生态环境保护责任制和考核评价制度。上级人民政府应当对下级人民政府生态环境保护和修复目标完成情况等进行考核。

**Article 78** The State shall implement a responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for ecological environment protection in the Yangtze River Basin. The people's government at a higher level shall assess the completion of ecological environment protection and restoration targets by the people's government at a lower level.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　国务院有关部门和长江流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当依照本法规定和职责分工，对长江流域各类保护、开发、建设活动进行监督检查，依法查处破坏长江流域自然资源、污染长江流域环境、损害长江流域生态系统等违法行为。  
公民、法人和非法人组织有权依法获取长江流域生态环境保护相关信息，举报和控告破坏长江流域自然资源、污染长江流域环境、损害长江流域生态系统等违法行为。  
国务院有关部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府及其有关部门应当依法公开长江流域生态环境保护相关信息，完善公众参与程序，为公民、法人和非法人组织参与和监督长江流域生态环境保护提供便利。

**Article 79** The relevant departments of the State Council and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and their respective responsibilities, supervise and inspect various protection, development, and construction activities in the Yangtze River Basin, and investigate and punish illegal acts that destroy natural resources, pollute the environment, or damage the ecosystem of the Yangtze River Basin according to law.  
Citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations have the right to obtain relevant information on the ecological environment protection of the Yangtze River Basin according to law, and to report and accuse illegal acts that destroy natural resources, pollute the environment, or damage the ecosystem of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall disclose relevant information on the ecological environment protection of the Yangtze River Basin according to law, improve public participation procedures, and provide convenience for citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to participate in and supervise the ecological environment protection of the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国务院有关部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府及其有关部门对长江流域跨行政区域、生态敏感区域和生态环境违法案件高发区域以及重大违法案件，依法开展联合执法。

**Article 80** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with law, carry out joint law enforcement in areas with cross-administrative regions, ecologically sensitive areas, areas with high incidence of ecological environment law cases, and major illegal cases in the Yangtze River Basin.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　国务院有关部门和长江流域省级人民政府对长江保护工作不力、问题突出、群众反映集中的地区，可以约谈所在地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门主要负责人，要求其采取措施及时整改。

**Article 81** The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yangtze River Basin may, for areas where the Yangtze River protection work is ineffective, problems are prominent, and the masses are concentrated in reporting, interview the main responsible persons of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the area and their relevant departments, requiring them to take measures for timely rectification.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国务院应当定期向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告长江流域生态环境状况及保护和修复工作等情况。  
长江流域县级以上地方人民政府应当定期向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告本级人民政府长江流域生态环境保护和修复工作等情况。

**Article 82** The State Council shall periodically report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the ecological environment status, protection, and restoration of the Yangtze River Basin.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yangtze River Basin shall periodically report to the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee on the ecological environment protection and restoration work of the Yangtze River Basin by the people's government at the same level.

### 第八章　法律责任

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　国务院有关部门和长江流域地方各级人民政府及其有关部门违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予警告、记过、记大过或者降级处分；造成严重后果的，给予撤职或者开除处分，其主要负责人应当引咎辞职：  
（一）不符合行政许可条件准予行政许可的；  
（二）依法应当作出责令停业、关闭等决定而未作出的；  
（三）发现违法行为或者接到举报不依法查处的；  
（四）有其他玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊行为的。

**Article 83** Where the relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at various levels in the Yangtze River Basin and their relevant departments violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be given warnings, demerits, major demerits, or demotion in accordance with law; if serious consequences are caused, they shall be given removal from office or expulsion, and the main responsible person shall resign to assume responsibility:  
(1) granting an administrative license that does not meet the conditions for an administrative license;  
(2) failing to make a decision that should be made such as ordering suspension of business or closure in accordance with law;  
(3) failing to investigate and punish illegal acts found or reported in accordance with law;  
(4) other acts of dereliction of duty, abuse of power, or malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由有关主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为，给予警告，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款：  
（一）船舶在禁止航行区域内航行的；  
（二）经同意在水生生物重要栖息地禁止航行区域内航行，未采取必要措施减少对重要水生生物干扰的；  
（三）水利水电、航运枢纽等工程未将生态用水调度纳入日常运行调度规程的；  
（四）具备岸电使用条件的船舶未按照国家有关规定使用岸电的。

**Article 84** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, the relevant competent department shall, in accordance with its division of responsibilities, order the cessation of the illegal act, give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed:  
(1) a ship navigating in a prohibited navigation area;  
(2) after being allowed to navigate in a prohibited navigation area in an important habitat of aquatic organisms, failing to take necessary measures to reduce interference with important aquatic organisms;  
(3) a project such as a water conservancy and hydropower project or a shipping hub failing to incorporate ecological water use regulation into the daily operation regulation procedures;  
(4) a ship with conditions for using shore power failing to use shore power in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　违反本法规定，在长江流域开放水域养殖、投放外来物种或者其他非本地物种种质资源的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期捕回，处十万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；逾期不捕回的，由有关人民政府农业农村主管部门代为捕回或者采取降低负面影响的措施，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 85** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and breeds or releases alien species or other species germplasm resources in open waters of the Yangtze River Basin, the agriculture and rural affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the capture to be completed within a time limit and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the capture is not completed within the time limit, the relevant agriculture and rural affairs department of the people's government shall carry out the capture or take measures to reduce the negative impact, and the expenses shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，在长江流域水生生物保护区内从事生产性捕捞，或者在长江干流和重要支流、大型通江湖泊、长江河口规定区域等重点水域禁捕期间从事天然渔业资源的生产性捕捞的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门没收渔获物、违法所得以及用于违法活动的渔船、渔具和其他工具，并处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；采取电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等方式捕捞，或者有其他严重情节的，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。  
收购、加工、销售前款规定的渔获物的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村、市场监督管理等部门按照职责分工，没收渔获物及其制品和违法所得，并处货值金额十倍以上二十倍以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销相关生产经营许可证或者责令关闭。

**Article 86** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and engages in productive fishing in aquatic biological reserves of the Yangtze River Basin, or engages in productive fishing of natural fishery resources during the fishing ban period in key waters such as the main stream and important tributaries of the Yangtze River, large river-connected lakes, and prescribed areas of the Yangtze River estuary, the agriculture and rural affairs department of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the catch, illegal gains, and the fishing vessels, fishing gear, and other tools used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the fishing is carried out by means of electric fishing, poison fishing, explosive fishing, or if there are other serious circumstances, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
Where anyone purchases, processes, or sells the catch mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the agriculture and rural affairs department or the market supervision and management department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their division of responsibilities, confiscate the catch, its products, and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods; if the circumstances are serious, the relevant production and operation license shall be revoked or the entity shall be ordered to close.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，非法侵占长江流域河湖水域，或者违法利用、占用河湖岸线的，由县级以上人民政府水行政、自然资源等主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为，限期拆除并恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担，没收违法所得，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 87** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and illegally encroaches upon the river-lake waters of the Yangtze River Basin, or illegally uses or occupies river-lake shorelines, the water administrative department or the natural resources department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their division of responsibilities, order the cessation of the illegal act, order the removal and restoration to the original condition within a time limit, the expenses shall be borne by the violator, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境、自然资源等主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为，限期拆除并恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担，没收违法所得，并处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭：  
（一）在长江干支流岸线一公里范围内新建、扩建化工园区和化工项目的；  
（二）在长江干流岸线三公里范围内和重要支流岸线一公里范围内新建、改建、扩建尾矿库的；  
（三）违反生态环境准入清单的规定进行生产建设活动的。

**Article 88** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts, the ecological environment department or the natural resources department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their division of responsibilities, order the cessation of the illegal act, order the removal and restoration to the original condition within a time limit, the expenses shall be borne by the violator, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval to order closure:  
(1) constructing new or expanding chemical industry parks and chemical projects within one kilometer of the shoreline of the main and tributary streams of the Yangtze River;  
(2) constructing new, reconstructing, or expanding tailings ponds within three kilometers of the shoreline of the main stream of the Yangtze River and within one kilometer of the shoreline of important tributaries;  
(3) conducting production and construction activities in violation of the provisions of the ecological environment access list.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　长江流域磷矿开采加工、磷肥和含磷农药制造等企业违反本法规定，超过排放标准或者总量控制指标排放含磷水污染物的，由县级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门责令停止违法行为，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停产整顿，或者报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭。

**Article 89** Where an enterprise such as a phosphorus ore mining and processing enterprise, a phosphate fertilizer manufacturing enterprise, or a phosphorus-containing pesticide manufacturing enterprise in the Yangtze River Basin violates the provisions of this Law and discharges phosphorus-containing water pollutants exceeding the discharge standard or the total discharge control indicator, the ecological environment department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the enterprise shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification, or the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval to order closure.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　违反本法规定，在长江流域水上运输剧毒化学品和国家规定禁止通过内河运输的其他危险化学品的，由县级以上人民政府交通运输主管部门或者海事管理机构责令改正，没收违法所得，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，或者吊销相关许可证。

**Article 90** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and transports acute toxic chemicals or other dangerous chemicals prohibited from being transported through inland waters by State regulations on the waters of the Yangtze River Basin, the transport department of the people's government at or above the county level or the maritime administration agency shall order correction, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, the entity shall be ordered to suspend business for rectification, or the relevant license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　违反本法规定，在长江流域未依法取得许可从事采砂活动，或者在禁止采砂区和禁止采砂期从事采砂活动的，由国务院水行政主管部门有关流域管理机构或者县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得以及用于违法活动的船舶、设备、工具，并处货值金额二倍以上二十倍以下罚款；货值金额不足十万元的，并处二十万元以上二百万元以下罚款；已经取得河道采砂许可证的，吊销河道采砂许可证。

**Article 91** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and engages in sand mining activities without obtaining a permit according to law in the Yangtze River Basin, or engages in sand mining activities in prohibited sand mining areas or during prohibited sand mining periods, the relevant river basin management agency of the water administrative department of the State Council or the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, confiscate illegal gains and the ships, equipment, and tools used for the illegal activities, and impose a fine of not less than two times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods; if the value of the goods is less than 100,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a river sand mining permit has been obtained, the river sand mining permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　对破坏长江流域自然资源、污染长江流域环境、损害长江流域生态系统等违法行为，本法未作行政处罚规定的，适用有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 92** Where this Law does not provide for administrative penalties for illegal acts such as destroying natural resources, polluting the environment, or damaging the ecosystem of the Yangtze River Basin, the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations shall apply.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　因污染长江流域环境、破坏长江流域生态造成他人损害的，侵权人应当承担侵权责任。  
违反国家规定造成长江流域生态环境损害的，国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织有权请求侵权人承担修复责任、赔偿损失和有关费用。

**Article 93** Where damage is caused to others due to pollution of the environment or destruction of the ecology of the Yangtze River Basin, the infringer shall bear tort liability.  
Where damage to the ecological environment of the Yangtze River Basin is caused in violation of State regulations, the organs prescribed by the State or the organizations prescribed by law have the right to request the infringer to bear restoration liability, compensate for losses, and pay relevant expenses.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 94** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第九章　附则

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）本法所称长江干流，是指长江源头至长江河口，流经青海省、四川省、西藏自治区、云南省、重庆市、湖北省、湖南省、江西省、安徽省、江苏省、上海市的长江主河段；  
（二）本法所称长江支流，是指直接或者间接流入长江干流的河流，支流可以分为一级支流、二级支流等；  
（三）本法所称长江重要支流，是指流域面积一万平方公里以上的支流，其中流域面积八万平方公里以上的一级支流包括雅砻江、岷江、嘉陵江、乌江、湘江、沅江、汉江和赣江等。

**Article 95** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings specified:  
(1) The main stream of the Yangtze River referred to in this Law means the main channel of the Yangtze River from the source of the Yangtze River to the Yangtze River estuary, flowing through Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Tibet Autonomous Region, Yunnan Province, Chongqing Municipality, Hubei Province, Hunan Province, Jiangxi Province, Anhui Province, Jiangsu Province, and Shanghai Municipality;  
(2) The tributaries of the Yangtze River referred to in this Law mean rivers that flow directly or indirectly into the main stream of the Yangtze River, and tributaries can be divided into first-level tributaries, second-level tributaries, etc.;  
(3) The important tributaries of the Yangtze River referred to in this Law mean tributaries with a basin area of more than 10,000 square kilometers, among which the first-level tributaries with a basin area of more than 80,000 square kilometers include the Yalong River, Min River, Jialing River, Wu River, Xiang River, Yuan River, Han River, and Gan River, etc.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　本法自2021年3月1日起施行。

**Article 96** This Law shall come into force on March 1, 2021.
