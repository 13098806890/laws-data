# 中华人民共和国黄河保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-10-30  
**生效日期**：2023-04-01

> （2022年10月30日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十七次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　规划与管控 第三章　生态保护与修复 第四章　水资源节约集约利用 第五章　水沙调控与防洪安全 第六章　污染防治 第七章　促进高质量发展 第八章　黄河文化保护传承弘扬 第九章　保障与监督 第十章　法律责任 第十一章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强黄河流域生态环境保护，保障黄河安澜，推进水资源节约集约利用，推动高质量发展，保护传承弘扬黄河文化，实现人与自然和谐共生、中华民族永续发展，制定本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强黄河流域生态环境保护，保障黄河安澜，推进水资源节约集约利用，推动高质量发展，保护传承弘扬黄河文化，实现人与自然和谐共生、中华民族永续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purposes of strengthening the ecological protection of the Yellow River Basin, ensuring the safety of the Yellow River, promoting the conservation and intensive use of water resources, advancing high-quality development, protecting, inheriting, and promoting the Yellow River culture, and achieving harmony between humans and nature as well as the sustainable development of the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展各类活动，适用本法；本法未作规定的，适用其他有关法律的规定。  
本法所称黄河流域，是指黄河干流、支流和湖泊的集水区域所涉及的青海省、四川省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、山西省、陕西省、河南省、山东省的相关县级行政区域。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to all activities related to ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin. Matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of other relevant laws.  
For the purposes of this Law, the Yellow River Basin refers to the relevant county-level administrative regions of Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Gansu Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Shanxi Province, Shaanxi Province, Henan Province, and Shandong Province covered by the catchment area of the main stream, tributaries, and lakes of the Yellow River.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展，坚持中国共产党的领导，落实重在保护、要在治理的要求，加强污染防治，贯彻生态优先、绿色发展，量水而行、节水为重，因地制宜、分类施策，统筹谋划、协同推进的原则。

**Article 3** Ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the requirements of focusing on protection and emphasizing governance, strengthen pollution prevention and control, and follow the principles of ecological priority and green development, acting within the limits of water resources and prioritizing water conservation, tailoring measures to local conditions and implementing categorized strategies, and carrying out coordinated planning and synergistic promotion.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家建立黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展统筹协调机制（以下简称黄河流域统筹协调机制），全面指导、统筹协调黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作，审议黄河流域重大政策、重大规划、重大项目等，协调跨地区跨部门重大事项，督促检查相关重要工作的落实情况。  
黄河流域省、自治区可以根据需要，建立省级协调机制，组织、协调推进本行政区域黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作。

**Article 4** The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin (hereinafter referred to as the Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism) to comprehensively guide, coordinate, and oversee the work of ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin, review major policies, plans, and projects in the Yellow River Basin, coordinate major cross-regional and cross-departmental matters, and supervise and inspect the implementation of relevant important work.  
Provinces and autonomous regions in the Yellow River Basin may, as needed, establish coordination mechanisms at the provincial level to organize, coordinate, and promote the work of ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院有关部门按照职责分工，负责黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展相关工作。  
国务院水行政主管部门黄河水利委员会（以下简称黄河流域管理机构）及其所属管理机构，依法行使流域水行政监督管理职责，为黄河流域统筹协调机制相关工作提供支撑保障。  
国务院生态环境主管部门黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构（以下简称黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构）依法开展流域生态环境监督管理相关工作。

**Article 5** The relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the work related to ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin.  
The Yellow River Water Conservancy Commission (hereinafter referred to as the Yellow River Basin Management Agency) of the water administrative department of the State Council and its affiliated management agencies shall, according to law, exercise the functions and duties of water administrative supervision and management of the river basin, and provide support and guarantee for the relevant work of the Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism.  
The Yellow River Basin Ecological and Environmental Supervision and Management Agency (hereinafter referred to as the Yellow River Basin Ecological and Environmental Supervision and Management Agency) of the ecological and environmental authority of the State Council shall, according to law, carry out relevant work on ecological and environmental supervision and management of the river basin.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府负责本行政区域黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工，负责本行政区域黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展相关工作。  
黄河流域相关地方根据需要在地方性法规和地方政府规章制定、规划编制、监督执法等方面加强协作，协同推进黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展。  
黄河流域建立省际河湖长联席会议制度。各级河湖长负责河道、湖泊管理和保护相关工作。

**Article 6** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall be responsible for the work of ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin within their respective administrative regions.  
The relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the work related to ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin within their respective administrative regions.  
Relevant localities in the Yellow River Basin shall, as needed, strengthen collaboration in formulating local regulations and government rules, planning, supervision, and law enforcement, and jointly promote ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin.  
A joint meeting system for provincial-level river and lake chiefs shall be established in the Yellow River Basin. River and lake chiefs at all levels shall be responsible for the management and protection of rivers, channels, and lakes.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院水行政、生态环境、自然资源、住房和城乡建设、农业农村、发展改革、应急管理、林业和草原、文化和旅游、标准化等主管部门按照职责分工，建立健全黄河流域水资源节约集约利用、水沙调控、防汛抗旱、水土保持、水文、水环境质量和污染物排放、生态保护与修复、自然资源调查监测评价、生物多样性保护、文化遗产保护等标准体系。

**Article 7** The competent departments of water administration, ecological environment, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, agriculture and rural affairs, development and reform, emergency management, forestry and grassland, culture and tourism, and standardization of the State Council shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, establish and improve the standards system for the conservation and intensive use of water resources, water and sediment regulation, flood prevention and drought relief, soil and water conservation, hydrology, water environment quality and pollutant discharge, ecological protection and restoration, natural resource survey, monitoring, and evaluation, biodiversity conservation, and cultural heritage protection in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家在黄河流域实行水资源刚性约束制度，坚持以水定城、以水定地、以水定人、以水定产，优化国土空间开发保护格局，促进人口和城市科学合理布局，构建与水资源承载能力相适应的现代产业体系。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府按照国家有关规定，在本行政区域组织实施水资源刚性约束制度。

**Article 8** The State shall implement a rigid water resource constraint system in the Yellow River Basin, adhere to the principles of determining urban development, land use, population, and industrial structure based on water availability, optimize the pattern of territorial space development and protection, promote a scientific and rational distribution of population and cities, and build a modern industrial system compatible with the carrying capacity of water resources.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, organize the implementation of the rigid water resource constraint system within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家在黄河流域强化农业节水增效、工业节水减排和城镇节水降损措施，鼓励、推广使用先进节水技术，加快形成节水型生产、生活方式，有效实现水资源节约集约利用，推进节水型社会建设。

**Article 9** The State shall strengthen measures for agricultural water-saving and efficiency improvement, industrial water-saving and emission reduction, and urban water-saving and loss reduction in the Yellow River Basin, encourage and promote the use of advanced water-saving technologies, accelerate the formation of water-saving production and lifestyles, effectively achieve the conservation and intensive use of water resources, and promote the construction of a water-saving society.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家统筹黄河干支流防洪体系建设，加强流域及流域间防洪体系协同，推进黄河上中下游防汛抗旱、防凌联动，构建科学高效的综合性防洪减灾体系，并适时组织评估，有效提升黄河流域防治洪涝等灾害的能力。

**Article 10** The State shall coordinate the construction of flood control systems for the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River, strengthen coordination of flood control systems within and between river basins, promote joint efforts in flood prevention, drought relief, and ice prevention in the upper, middle, and lower reaches of the Yellow River, build a scientific and efficient comprehensive flood prevention and disaster reduction system, and organize timely assessments to effectively enhance the capacity to prevent and control floods and other disasters in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院自然资源主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门定期组织开展黄河流域土地、矿产、水流、森林、草原、湿地等自然资源状况调查，建立资源基础数据库，开展资源环境承载能力评价，并向社会公布黄河流域自然资源状况。  
国务院野生动物保护主管部门应当定期组织开展黄河流域野生动物及其栖息地状况普查，或者根据需要组织开展专项调查，建立野生动物资源档案，并向社会公布黄河流域野生动物资源状况。  
国务院生态环境主管部门应当定期组织开展黄河流域生态状况评估，并向社会公布黄河流域生态状况。  
国务院林业和草原主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门组织开展黄河流域土地荒漠化、沙化调查监测，并定期向社会公布调查监测结果。  
国务院水行政主管部门应当组织开展黄河流域水土流失调查监测，并定期向社会公布调查监测结果。

**Article 11** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, periodically organize surveys on the status of natural resources such as land, minerals, water flow, forests, grasslands, and wetlands in the Yellow River Basin, establish a basic resource database, conduct assessments of resource and environmental carrying capacity, and make public the status of natural resources in the Yellow River Basin.  
The competent department of wildlife protection of the State Council shall periodically organize surveys on the status of wild animals and their habitats in the Yellow River Basin, or conduct special surveys as needed, establish wildlife resource archives, and make public the status of wildlife resources in the Yellow River Basin.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall periodically organize assessments of the ecological status of the Yellow River Basin and make public the ecological status of the Yellow River Basin.  
The competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize surveys and monitoring of land desertification and sandification in the Yellow River Basin, and periodically make public the results of the surveys and monitoring.  
The water administrative department of the State Council shall organize surveys and monitoring of soil erosion in the Yellow River Basin and periodically make public the results of the surveys and monitoring.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　黄河流域统筹协调机制统筹协调国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，在已经建立的台站和监测项目基础上，健全黄河流域生态环境、自然资源、水文、泥沙、荒漠化和沙化、水土保持、自然灾害、气象等监测网络体系。  
国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门按照职责分工，健全完善生态环境风险报告和预警机制。

**Article 12** The Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism shall coordinate the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin to improve the monitoring network system for ecology and environment, natural resources, hydrology, sediment, desertification and sandification, soil and water conservation, natural disasters, and meteorology in the Yellow River Basin, on the basis of established stations and monitoring projects.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, improve the ecological and environmental risk reporting and early warning mechanism.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家加强黄河流域自然灾害的预防与应急准备、监测与预警、应急处置与救援、事后恢复与重建体系建设，维护相关工程和设施安全，控制、减轻和消除自然灾害引起的危害。  
国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，建立健全黄河流域突发生态环境事件应急联动工作机制，与国家突发事件应急体系相衔接，加强对黄河流域突发生态环境事件的应对管理。  
出现严重干旱、省际或者重要控制断面流量降至预警流量、水库运行故障、重大水污染事故等情形，可能造成供水危机、黄河断流时，黄河流域管理机构应当组织实施应急调度。

**Article 13** The State shall strengthen the construction of systems for natural disaster prevention and emergency preparedness, monitoring and early warning, emergency response and rescue, and post-disaster recovery and reconstruction in the Yellow River Basin, ensure the safety of relevant projects and facilities, and control, mitigate, and eliminate hazards caused by natural disasters.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, establish and improve an emergency linkage mechanism for sudden ecological and environmental incidents in the Yellow River Basin, which shall be connected with the national emergency response system, and strengthen the response management of sudden ecological and environmental incidents in the Yellow River Basin.  
In circumstances such as severe drought, the flow of inter-provincial or important control sections dropping to the early warning flow, reservoir operation failure, or major water pollution incidents, which may lead to water supply crisis or the Yellow River running dry, the Yellow River Basin Management Agency shall organize and implement emergency dispatch measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　黄河流域统筹协调机制设立黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展专家咨询委员会，对黄河流域重大政策、重大规划、重大项目和重大科技问题等提供专业咨询。  
国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府及其有关部门按照职责分工，组织开展黄河流域建设项目、重要基础设施和产业布局相关规划等对黄河流域生态系统影响的第三方评估、分析、论证等工作。

**Article 14** The Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism shall establish an expert advisory committee on ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin to provide professional consultation on major policies, plans, projects, and scientific and technological issues in the Yellow River Basin.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize third-party assessments, analyses, and demonstrations of the impact of construction projects, important infrastructure, and industrial layout-related plans in the Yellow River Basin on the Yellow River Basin ecosystem.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　黄河流域统筹协调机制统筹协调国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，建立健全黄河流域信息共享系统，组织建立智慧黄河信息共享平台，提高科学化水平。国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府及其有关部门应当按照国家有关规定，共享黄河流域生态环境、自然资源、水土保持、防洪安全以及管理执法等信息。

**Article 15** The Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism shall coordinate the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin to establish and improve the information sharing system for the Yellow River Basin, and organize the establishment of a smart Yellow River information sharing platform to enhance the scientific level. The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, share information on ecological environment, natural resources, soil and water conservation, flood control safety, and management and law enforcement in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家鼓励、支持开展黄河流域生态保护与修复、水资源节约集约利用、水沙运动与调控、防沙治沙、泥沙综合利用、河流动力与河床演变、水土保持、水文、气候、污染防治等方面的重大科技问题研究，加强协同创新，推动关键性技术研究，推广应用先进适用技术，提升科技创新支撑能力。

**Article 16** The State shall encourage and support research on major scientific and technological issues in the Yellow River Basin, such as ecological protection and restoration, conservation and intensive use of water resources, water and sediment movement and regulation, sand prevention and control, comprehensive utilization of sediment, river dynamics and riverbed evolution, soil and water conservation, hydrology, climate, and pollution prevention, strengthen collaborative innovation, promote research on key technologies, and promote the application of advanced and applicable technologies to enhance the supporting capacity of scientific and technological innovation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家加强黄河文化保护传承弘扬，系统保护黄河文化遗产，研究黄河文化发展脉络，阐发黄河文化精神内涵和时代价值，铸牢中华民族共同体意识。

**Article 17** The State shall strengthen the protection, inheritance, and promotion of the Yellow River culture, systematically protect the cultural heritage of the Yellow River, study the development context of the Yellow River culture, elucidate the spiritual connotation and contemporary value of the Yellow River culture, and forge a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展的宣传教育。  
新闻媒体应当采取多种形式开展黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展的宣传报道，并依法对违法行为进行舆论监督。

**Article 18** The relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity and education on ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin.  
News media shall carry out publicity and reporting on ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin through various forms, and conduct public opinion supervision over illegal acts according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家鼓励、支持单位和个人参与黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展相关活动。  
对在黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定予以表彰和奖励。

**Article 19** The State shall encourage and support units and individuals to participate in activities related to ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the work of ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　规划与管控

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家建立以国家发展规划为统领，以空间规划为基础，以专项规划、区域规划为支撑的黄河流域规划体系，发挥规划对推进黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展的引领、指导和约束作用。

**Article 20** The State shall establish a planning system for the Yellow River Basin that takes national development plans as the guide, spatial plans as the foundation, and special plans and regional plans as support, so that the plans play a leading, guiding, and constraining role in promoting ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国务院和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当将黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国务院发展改革部门应当会同国务院有关部门编制黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展规划，报国务院批准后实施。

**Article 21** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall incorporate the work of ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin into the national economic and social development plans.  
The development and reform department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, prepare a plan for ecological protection and high-quality development in the Yellow River Basin, which shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院自然资源主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门组织编制黄河流域国土空间规划，科学有序统筹安排黄河流域农业、生态、城镇等功能空间，划定永久基本农田、生态保护红线、城镇开发边界，优化国土空间结构和布局，统领黄河流域国土空间利用任务，报国务院批准后实施。涉及黄河流域国土空间利用的专项规划应当与黄河流域国土空间规划相衔接。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府组织编制本行政区域的国土空间规划，按照规定的程序报经批准后实施。

**Article 22** The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, organize the preparation of a territorial space plan for the Yellow River Basin, scientifically and orderly arrange functional spaces such as agriculture, ecology, and towns in the Yellow River Basin, designate permanent basic farmland, ecological protection red lines, and urban development boundaries, optimize the structure and layout of territorial space, and guide the tasks of territorial space utilization in the Yellow River Basin. The plan shall be implemented after being submitted to the State Council for approval. Special plans involving the utilization of territorial space in the Yellow River Basin shall be consistent with the territorial space plan for the Yellow River Basin.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall organize the preparation of territorial space plans for their respective administrative regions, which shall be implemented after being submitted for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，按照统一规划、统一管理、统一调度的原则，依法编制黄河流域综合规划、水资源规划、防洪规划等，对节约、保护、开发、利用水资源和防治水害作出部署。  
黄河流域生态环境保护等规划依照有关法律、行政法规的规定编制。

**Article 23** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, prepare comprehensive plans, water resource plans, flood control plans, etc., for the Yellow River Basin according to law, in accordance with the principles of unified planning, unified management, and unified dispatch, and make arrangements for the conservation, protection, development, and utilization of water resources and the prevention and control of water disasters.  
Plans for ecological and environmental protection in the Yellow River Basin shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国民经济和社会发展规划、国土空间总体规划的编制以及重大产业政策的制定，应当与黄河流域水资源条件和防洪要求相适应，并进行科学论证。  
黄河流域工业、农业、畜牧业、林草业、能源、交通运输、旅游、自然资源开发等专项规划和开发区、新区规划等，涉及水资源开发利用的，应当进行规划水资源论证。未经论证或者经论证不符合水资源强制性约束控制指标的，规划审批机关不得批准该规划。

**Article 24** The preparation of national economic and social development plans, overall territorial space plans, and the formulation of major industrial policies shall be compatible with the water resource conditions and flood control requirements of the Yellow River Basin, and scientific demonstration shall be carried out.  
Special plans for industry, agriculture, animal husbandry, forestry and grass, energy, transportation, tourism, natural resource development, etc., as well as plans for development zones and new areas in the Yellow River Basin, that involve the development and utilization of water resources, shall undergo planning water resources demonstration. If no demonstration has been conducted or the demonstration does not meet the mandatory water resource constraint control indicators, the planning approval authority shall not approve the plan.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家对黄河流域国土空间严格实行用途管制。黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门依据国土空间规划，对本行政区域黄河流域国土空间实行分区、分类用途管制。  
黄河流域国土空间开发利用活动应当符合国土空间用途管制要求，并依法取得规划许可。  
禁止违反国家有关规定、未经国务院批准，占用永久基本农田。禁止擅自占用耕地进行非农业建设，严格控制耕地转为林地、草地、园地等其他农用地。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当严格控制黄河流域以人工湖、人工湿地等形式新建人造水景观，黄河流域统筹协调机制应当组织有关部门加强监督管理。

**Article 25** The State shall strictly implement use control over territorial space in the Yellow River Basin. The competent departments of natural resources of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall, based on territorial space plans, implement zonal and categorized use control over territorial space in the Yellow River Basin within their respective administrative regions.  
Activities for the development and utilization of territorial space in the Yellow River Basin shall comply with the requirements for territorial space use control and shall obtain planning permits according to law.  
It shall be prohibited to occupy permanent basic farmland without the approval of the State Council in violation of the relevant provisions of the State. It shall be prohibited to occupy farmland for non-agricultural construction without authorization, and the conversion of farmland into other agricultural land such as forestland, grassland, and garden land shall be strictly controlled.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall strictly control the construction of new man-made water landscapes, such as artificial lakes and constructed wetlands, in the Yellow River Basin. The Yellow River Basin Coordination Mechanism shall organize the relevant departments to strengthen supervision and management.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　黄河流域省级人民政府根据本行政区域的生态环境和资源利用状况，按照生态保护红线、环境质量底线、资源利用上线的要求，制定生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案后实施。生态环境分区管控方案和生态环境准入清单应当与国土空间规划相衔接。  
禁止在黄河干支流岸线管控范围内新建、扩建化工园区和化工项目。禁止在黄河干流岸线和重要支流岸线的管控范围内新建、改建、扩建尾矿库；但是以提升安全水平、生态环境保护水平为目的的改建除外。  
干支流目录、岸线管控范围由国务院水行政、自然资源、生态环境主管部门按照职责分工，会同黄河流域省级人民政府确定并公布。

**Article 26** The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, based on the ecological environment and resource utilization conditions of their respective administrative regions and in accordance with the requirements of ecological protection red lines, environmental quality bottom lines, and resource utilization upper lines, formulate ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists, which shall be implemented after being submitted to the competent department of ecological environment of the State Council for recordation. The ecological environment zoning control plans and ecological environment access lists shall be consistent with the territorial space plans.  
It shall be prohibited to build new or expand chemical industrial parks and chemical projects within the control scope of the main stream and tributary banks of the Yellow River. It shall be prohibited to build new, reconstruct, or expand tailings ponds within the control scope of the main stream bank and important tributary banks of the Yellow River, except for reconstruction aimed at improving safety and ecological environmental protection levels.  
The catalogs of main streams and tributaries and the scope of bank control shall be determined and published by the water administrative, natural resources, and ecological environment competent departments of the State Council, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, together with the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　黄河流域水电开发，应当进行科学论证，符合国家发展规划、流域综合规划和生态保护要求。对黄河流域已建小水电工程，不符合生态保护要求的，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织分类整改或者采取措施逐步退出。

**Article 27** The development of hydropower in the Yellow River Basin shall undergo scientific demonstration and comply with national development plans, comprehensive river basin plans, and ecological protection requirements. For existing small hydropower projects in the Yellow River Basin that do not meet ecological protection requirements, the local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize classified rectification or take measures to gradually phase them out.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　黄河流域管理机构统筹防洪减淤、城乡供水、生态保护、灌溉用水、水力发电等目标，建立水资源、水沙、防洪防凌综合调度体系，实施黄河干支流控制性水工程统一调度，保障流域水安全，发挥水资源综合效益。

**Article 28** The Yellow River Basin Management Agency shall coordinate the objectives of flood control and sediment reduction, urban and rural water supply, ecological protection, irrigation water use, and hydropower generation, establish a comprehensive dispatch system for water resources, water and sediment, flood control and ice prevention, implement unified dispatch of control water projects on the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River, ensure the water security of the river basin, and maximize the comprehensive benefits of water resources.

### 第三章　生态保护与修复

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家加强黄河流域生态保护与修复，坚持山水林田湖草沙一体化保护与修复，实行自然恢复为主、自然恢复与人工修复相结合的系统治理。  
国务院自然资源主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门编制黄河流域国土空间生态修复规划，组织实施重大生态修复工程，统筹推进黄河流域生态保护与修复工作。

**Article 29** The State shall strengthen ecological protection and restoration in the Yellow River Basin, adhere to the integrated protection and restoration of mountains, waters, forests, farmlands, lakes, grasslands, and deserts, and implement systematic governance that focuses on natural restoration and combines natural restoration with artificial restoration.  
The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, prepare a territorial space ecological restoration plan for the Yellow River Basin, organize the implementation of major ecological restoration projects, and coordinate the promotion of ecological protection and restoration work in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家加强对黄河水源涵养区的保护，加大对黄河干流和支流源头、水源涵养区的雪山冰川、高原冻土、高寒草甸、草原、湿地、荒漠、泉域等的保护力度。  
禁止在黄河上游约古宗列曲、扎陵湖、鄂陵湖、玛多河湖群等河道、湖泊管理范围内从事采矿、采砂、渔猎等活动，维持河道、湖泊天然状态。

**Article 30** The State shall strengthen the protection of water source conservation areas in the Yellow River, and increase the protection of snow-capped mountains and glaciers, plateau permafrost, alpine meadows, grasslands, wetlands, deserts, and spring areas in the sources and water source conservation areas of the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River.  
It shall be prohibited to engage in mining, sand mining, fishing, and hunting activities within the management scope of river channels and lakes, such as the Yueguzongliequ, Zhaling Lake, Eling Lake, and Maduo River-Lake Group in the upper reaches of the Yellow River, and the natural state of the river channels and lakes shall be maintained.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国务院和黄河流域省级人民政府应当依法在重要生态功能区域、生态脆弱区域划定公益林，实施严格管护；需要补充灌溉的，在水资源承载能力范围内合理安排灌溉用水。  
国务院林业和草原主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门、黄河流域省级人民政府，加强对黄河流域重要生态功能区域天然林、湿地、草原保护与修复和荒漠化、沙化土地治理工作的指导。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当采取防护林建设、禁牧封育、锁边防风固沙工程、沙化土地封禁保护、鼠害防治等措施，加强黄河流域重要生态功能区域天然林、湿地、草原保护与修复，开展规模化防沙治沙，科学治理荒漠化、沙化土地，在河套平原区、内蒙古高原湖泊萎缩退化区、黄土高原土地沙化区、汾渭平原区等重点区域实施生态修复工程。

**Article 31** The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, according to law, designate ecological public welfare forests in important ecological function areas and ecologically fragile areas, and implement strict management and protection; if supplementary irrigation is needed, irrigation water shall be reasonably arranged within the carrying capacity of water resources.  
The competent department of forestry and grassland of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, strengthen guidance for the protection and restoration of natural forests, wetlands, and grasslands in important ecological function areas of the Yellow River Basin, and for the control of desertification and sandification of land.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall take measures such as building shelter forests, prohibiting grazing and sealing off for afforestation, implementing border windbreak and sand fixation projects, sealing off and protecting sandified land, and preventing and controlling rodent damage, to strengthen the protection and restoration of natural forests, wetlands, and grasslands in important ecological function areas of the Yellow River Basin, carry out large-scale sand prevention and control, scientifically control desertified and sandified land, and implement ecological restoration projects in key areas such as the Hetao Plain, the Lake Shrinkage and Degradation Area of the Inner Mongolia Plateau, the Land Sandification Area of the Loess Plateau, and the Fenwei Plain.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家加强对黄河流域子午岭—六盘山、秦岭北麓、贺兰山、白于山、陇中等水土流失重点预防区、治理区和渭河、洮河、汾河、伊洛河等重要支流源头区的水土流失防治。水土流失防治应当根据实际情况，科学采取生物措施和工程措施。  
禁止在二十五度以上陡坡地开垦种植农作物。黄河流域省级人民政府根据本行政区域的实际情况，可以规定小于二十五度的禁止开垦坡度。禁止开垦的陡坡地范围由所在地县级人民政府划定并公布。

**Article 32** The State shall strengthen soil erosion prevention and control in key prevention and control areas of soil erosion such as Ziwuling-Liupan Mountain, the northern foot of the Qinling Mountains, Helan Mountain, Baiyu Mountain, and Longzhong, as well as in the source areas of important tributaries such as the Weihe River, Taohe River, Fenhe River, and Yiluo River in the Yellow River Basin. Soil erosion prevention and control shall be carried out by taking biological and engineering measures scientifically based on actual conditions.  
It shall be prohibited to reclaim and plant crops on slopes with a gradient of 25 degrees or more. The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin may, based on the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, stipulate a prohibited reclamation slope gradient of less than 25 degrees. The scope of steep slopes where reclamation is prohibited shall be delineated and published by the local people's governments at the county level.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门加强黄河流域砒砂岩区、多沙粗沙区、水蚀风蚀交错区和沙漠入河区等生态脆弱区域保护和治理，开展土壤侵蚀和水土流失状况评估，实施重点防治工程。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织推进小流域综合治理、坡耕地综合整治、黄土高原塬面治理保护、适地植被建设等水土保持重点工程，采取塬面、沟头、沟坡、沟道防护等措施，加强多沙粗沙区治理，开展生态清洁流域建设。  
国家支持在黄河流域上中游开展整沟治理。整沟治理应当坚持规划先行、系统修复、整体保护、因地制宜、综合治理、一体推进。

**Article 33** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, strengthen the protection and governance of ecologically fragile areas in the Yellow River Basin, such as the sandstone area, the area with high sediment content and coarse sand, the interleaving area of water erosion and wind erosion, and the area where deserts enter the river, conduct assessments of soil erosion and water loss conditions, and implement key prevention and control projects.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall organize and promote key soil and water conservation projects such as comprehensive treatment of small watersheds, comprehensive improvement of sloping farmland, treatment and protection of loess tableland surfaces, and vegetation construction suitable for local conditions, adopt measures such as protection of tableland surfaces, gully heads, gully slopes, and gully channels, strengthen the treatment of areas with high sediment content and coarse sand, and carry out the construction of ecologically clean watersheds.  
The State shall support the implementation of whole-gully treatment in the upper and middle reaches of the Yellow River. Whole-gully treatment shall adhere to the principles of planning first, systematic restoration, overall protection, adaptation to local conditions, comprehensive treatment, and integrated promotion.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门制定淤地坝建设、养护标准或者技术规范，健全淤地坝建设、管理、安全运行制度。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当因地制宜组织开展淤地坝建设，加快病险淤地坝除险加固和老旧淤地坝提升改造，建设安全监测和预警设施，将淤地坝工程防汛纳入地方防汛责任体系，落实管护责任，提高养护水平，减少下游河道淤积。  
禁止损坏、擅自占用淤地坝。

**Article 34** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate construction and maintenance standards or technical specifications for check dams, and improve the construction, management, and safe operation systems for check dams.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall, according to local conditions, organize the construction of check dams, accelerate the reinforcement of dangerous and defective check dams and the upgrading and renovation of old check dams, construct safety monitoring and early warning facilities, incorporate the flood prevention of check dam projects into the local flood prevention responsibility system, implement management and protection responsibilities, improve maintenance levels, and reduce sedimentation in downstream river channels.  
It shall be prohibited to damage or occupy check dams without authorization.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　禁止在黄河流域水土流失严重、生态脆弱区域开展可能造成水土流失的生产建设活动。确因国家发展战略和国计民生需要建设的，应当进行科学论证，并依法办理审批手续。  
生产建设单位应当依法编制并严格执行经批准的水土保持方案。  
从事生产建设活动造成水土流失的，应当按照国家规定的水土流失防治相关标准进行治理。

**Article 35** It shall be prohibited to carry out production and construction activities that may cause soil erosion in areas with severe soil erosion and ecologically fragile areas in the Yellow River Basin. If construction is truly necessary for national development strategies and the national economy and people's livelihood, scientific demonstration shall be conducted, and approval procedures shall be completed according to law.  
Production and construction units shall prepare and strictly implement approved soil and water conservation plans according to law.  
Where soil erosion is caused by production and construction activities, the unit shall carry out control in accordance with the relevant national standards for soil erosion prevention and control.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和山东省人民政府，编制并实施黄河入海河口整治规划，合理布局黄河入海流路，加强河口治理，保障入海河道畅通和河口防洪防凌安全，实施清水沟、刁口河生态补水，维护河口生态功能。  
国务院自然资源、林业和草原主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和山东省人民政府，组织开展黄河三角洲湿地生态保护与修复，有序推进退塘还河、退耕还湿、退田还滩，加强外来入侵物种防治，减少油气开采、围垦养殖、港口航运等活动对河口生态系统的影响。  
禁止侵占刁口河等黄河备用入海流路。

**Article 36** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's government of Shandong Province, prepare and implement a plan for the regulation of the Yellow River estuary, rationally arrange the flow paths of the Yellow River into the sea, strengthen estuary regulation, ensure the smooth flow of the river channel into the sea and the safety of flood control and ice prevention at the estuary, implement ecological water replenishment for the Qingshuigou and Diaokou rivers, and maintain estuary ecological functions.  
The competent departments of natural resources, forestry and grassland of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the people's government of Shandong Province, organize ecological protection and restoration of the Yellow River Delta wetlands, orderly promote the conversion of ponds into rivers, reclaimed land into wetlands, and farmland into shallows, strengthen the prevention and control of invasive alien species, and reduce the impact of activities such as oil and gas extraction, reclamation and aquaculture, and port shipping on the estuary ecosystem.  
It shall be prohibited to encroach upon the Diaokou River and other backup flow paths of the Yellow River into the sea.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院水行政主管部门确定黄河干流、重要支流控制断面生态流量和重要湖泊生态水位的管控指标，应当征求并研究国务院生态环境、自然资源等主管部门的意见。黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门确定其他河流生态流量和其他湖泊生态水位的管控指标，应当征求并研究同级人民政府生态环境、自然资源等主管部门的意见，报黄河流域管理机构、黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构备案。确定生态流量和生态水位的管控指标，应当进行科学论证，综合考虑水资源条件、气候状况、生态环境保护要求、生活生产用水状况等因素。  
黄河流域管理机构和黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门按照职责分工，组织编制和实施生态流量和生态水位保障实施方案。  
黄河干流、重要支流水工程应当将生态用水调度纳入日常运行调度规程。

**Article 37** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, when determining the control indicators for ecological flow of control sections on the main stream and important tributaries of the Yellow River and ecological water level of important lakes, solicit and study the opinions of the competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources of the State Council. The water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, when determining the control indicators for ecological flow of other rivers and ecological water level of other lakes, solicit and study the opinions of the competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources of the same level people's government, and submit them to the Yellow River Basin Management Agency and the Yellow River Basin Ecological and Environmental Supervision and Management Agency for recordation. The determination of control indicators for ecological flow and ecological water level shall be based on scientific demonstration, taking into comprehensive consideration factors such as water resource conditions, climate conditions, ecological and environmental protection requirements, and domestic and production water use conditions.  
The Yellow River Basin Management Agency and the water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, organize the preparation and implementation of implementation plans for ensuring ecological flow and ecological water level.  
Water projects on the main stream and important tributaries of the Yellow River shall incorporate ecological water dispatch into the routine operation dispatch procedures.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家统筹黄河流域自然保护地体系建设。国务院和黄河流域省级人民政府在黄河流域重要典型生态系统的完整分布区、生态环境敏感区以及珍贵濒危野生动植物天然集中分布区和重要栖息地、重要自然遗迹分布区等区域，依法设立国家公园、自然保护区、自然公园等自然保护地。  
自然保护地建设、管理涉及河道、湖泊管理范围的，应当统筹考虑河道、湖泊保护需要，满足防洪要求，并保障防洪工程建设和管理活动的开展。

**Article 38** The State shall coordinate the construction of the natural protected area system in the Yellow River Basin. The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, according to law, establish natural protected areas such as national parks, nature reserves, and natural parks in the complete distribution areas of important and typical ecosystems, ecologically sensitive areas, natural concentrated distribution areas and important habitats of rare and endangered wild animals and plants, and important natural relic distribution areas in the Yellow River Basin.  
Where the construction and management of natural protected areas involve the management scope of river channels and lakes, the needs for river channel and lake protection shall be taken into overall consideration, the flood control requirements shall be met, and the construction and management activities of flood control projects shall be guaranteed.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院林业和草原、农业农村主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府按照职责分工，对黄河流域数量急剧下降或者极度濒危的野生动植物和受到严重破坏的栖息地、天然集中分布区、破碎化的典型生态系统开展保护与修复，修建迁地保护设施，建立野生动植物遗传资源基因库，进行抢救性修复。  
国务院生态环境主管部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府组织开展黄河流域生物多样性保护管理，定期评估生物受威胁状况以及生物多样性恢复成效。

**Article 39** The competent departments of forestry and grassland, and agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, carry out protection and restoration of wild animals and plants that have sharply declined in number or are extremely endangered, and severely damaged habitats, natural concentrated distribution areas, and fragmented typical ecosystems in the Yellow River Basin, construct ex-situ conservation facilities, establish gene banks for wildlife genetic resources, and carry out rescue restoration.  
The competent department of ecological environment of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall organize the management of biodiversity conservation in the Yellow River Basin, and periodically assess the threat status of organisms and the effectiveness of biodiversity restoration.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，建立黄河流域水生生物完整性指数评价体系，组织开展黄河流域水生生物完整性评价，并将评价结果作为评估黄河流域生态系统总体状况的重要依据。黄河流域水生生物完整性指数应当与黄河流域水环境质量标准相衔接。

**Article 40** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, establish an aquatic biological integrity index evaluation system for the Yellow River Basin, organize the evaluation of aquatic biological integrity in the Yellow River Basin, and use the evaluation results as an important basis for assessing the overall status of the Yellow River Basin ecosystem. The aquatic biological integrity index for the Yellow River Basin shall be consistent with the water environment quality standards for the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家保护黄河流域水产种质资源和珍贵濒危物种，支持开展水产种质资源保护区、国家重点保护野生动物人工繁育基地建设。  
禁止在黄河流域开放水域养殖、投放外来物种和其他非本地物种种质资源。

**Article 41** The State shall protect aquatic germplasm resources and rare and endangered species in the Yellow River Basin, and support the construction of aquatic germplasm resource conservation areas and artificial breeding bases for key protected wild animals under State priority.  
It shall be prohibited to cultivate or release alien species and germplasm resources of non-native species in open waters of the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家加强黄河流域水生生物产卵场、索饵场、越冬场、洄游通道等重要栖息地的生态保护与修复。对鱼类等水生生物洄游产生阻隔的涉水工程应当结合实际采取建设过鱼设施、河湖连通、增殖放流、人工繁育等多种措施，满足水生生物的生态需求。  
国家实行黄河流域重点水域禁渔期制度，禁渔期内禁止在黄河流域重点水域从事天然渔业资源生产性捕捞，具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定做好禁渔期渔民的生活保障工作。  
禁止电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等破坏渔业资源和水域生态的捕捞行为。

**Article 42** The State shall strengthen the ecological protection and restoration of important habitats such as spawning grounds, feeding grounds, wintering grounds, and migration channels of aquatic organisms in the Yellow River Basin. For water-related projects that block the migration of fish and other aquatic organisms, multiple measures such as constructing fish passage facilities, connecting rivers and lakes, stocking and releasing, and artificial breeding shall be taken based on actual conditions to meet the ecological needs of aquatic organisms.  
The State shall implement a fishing ban period system for key waters in the Yellow River Basin. During the fishing ban period, it shall be prohibited to engage in productive fishing of natural fishery resources in key waters of the Yellow River Basin. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall do a good job in providing livelihood security for fishermen during the fishing ban period in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
It shall be prohibited to engage in fishing practices that destroy fishery resources and aquatic ecology, such as electric fishing, poisoning, and explosives fishing.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源主管部门组织划定并公布黄河流域地下水超采区。  
黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门编制本行政区域地下水超采综合治理方案，经省级人民政府批准后，报国务院水行政主管部门备案。

**Article 43** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, organize the delineation and publication of areas of groundwater over-extraction in the Yellow River Basin.  
The water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, together with the relevant departments of the same level people's government, prepare comprehensive treatment plans for groundwater over-extraction in their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council for recordation after approval by the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织开展退化农用地生态修复，实施农田综合整治。  
黄河流域生产建设活动损毁的土地，由生产建设者负责复垦。因历史原因无法确定土地复垦义务人以及因自然灾害损毁的土地，由黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府负责组织复垦。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对矿山的监督管理，督促采矿权人履行矿山污染防治和生态修复责任，并因地制宜采取消除地质灾害隐患、土地复垦、恢复植被、防治污染等措施，组织开展历史遗留矿山生态修复工作。

**Article 44** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall organize ecological restoration of degraded agricultural land and implement comprehensive improvement of farmland.  
Land damaged by production and construction activities in the Yellow River Basin shall be reclaimed by the production and construction entity. Where the obligation to reclaim land cannot be determined due to historical reasons, or land is damaged by natural disasters, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall be responsible for organizing reclamation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall strengthen the supervision and management of mines, urge mining rights holders to fulfill their obligations of mine pollution prevention and ecological restoration, and take measures such as eliminating geological hazard risks, land reclamation, restoring vegetation, and preventing pollution according to local conditions, and organize ecological restoration of historical abandoned mines.

### 第四章　水资源节约集约利用

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　黄河流域水资源利用，应当坚持节水优先、统筹兼顾、集约使用、精打细算，优先满足城乡居民生活用水，保障基本生态用水，统筹生产用水。

**Article 45** The utilization of water resources in the Yellow River Basin shall adhere to the principles of prioritizing water conservation, balancing various needs, intensive use, and careful calculation, prioritize meeting the domestic water needs of urban and rural residents, ensure basic ecological water use, and coordinate production water use.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家对黄河水量实行统一配置。制定和调整黄河水量分配方案，应当充分考虑黄河流域水资源条件、生态环境状况、区域用水状况、节水水平、洪水资源化利用等，统筹当地水和外调水、常规水和非常规水，科学确定水资源可利用总量和河道输沙入海水量，分配区域地表水取用水总量。  
黄河流域管理机构商黄河流域省级人民政府制定和调整黄河水量分配方案和跨省支流水量分配方案。黄河水量分配方案经国务院发展改革部门、水行政主管部门审查后，报国务院批准。跨省支流水量分配方案报国务院授权的部门批准。  
黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门根据黄河水量分配方案和跨省支流水量分配方案，制定和调整本行政区域水量分配方案，经省级人民政府批准后，报黄河流域管理机构备案。

**Article 46** The State shall implement unified allocation of the Yellow River water volume. When formulating and adjusting the water allocation plan for the Yellow River, full consideration shall be given to the water resource conditions, ecological and environmental conditions, regional water use conditions, water conservation levels, and flood resource utilization in the Yellow River Basin, and local water and transferred water, conventional water and unconventional water shall be coordinated, the total available water resources and the water volume for sediment transport into the sea shall be scientifically determined, and total water withdrawal from surface water in the region shall be allocated.  
The Yellow River Basin Management Agency shall, after consulting with the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin, formulate and adjust the water allocation plan for the Yellow River and the water allocation plans for inter-provincial tributaries. The water allocation plan for the Yellow River shall be submitted to the State Council for approval after review by the development and reform department and the water administrative department of the State Council. The water allocation plans for inter-provincial tributaries shall be submitted to the department authorized by the State Council for approval.  
The water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, based on the water allocation plan for the Yellow River and the water allocation plans for inter-provincial tributaries, formulate and adjust the water allocation plans for their respective administrative regions, which shall be submitted to the Yellow River Basin Management Agency for recordation after approval by the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家对黄河流域水资源实行统一调度，遵循总量控制、断面流量控制、分级管理、分级负责的原则，根据水情变化进行动态调整。  
国务院水行政主管部门依法组织黄河流域水资源统一调度的实施和监督管理。

**Article 47** The State shall implement unified dispatch of water resources in the Yellow River Basin, following the principles of total quantity control, cross-section flow control, graded management, and graded responsibility, and make dynamic adjustments based on changes in water conditions.  
The water administrative department of the State Council shall, according to law, organize the implementation and supervision and management of unified dispatch of water resources in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源主管部门制定黄河流域省级行政区域地下水取水总量控制指标。  
黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门，根据本行政区域地下水取水总量控制指标，制定设区的市、县级行政区域地下水取水总量控制指标和地下水水位控制指标，经省级人民政府批准后，报国务院水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构备案。

**Article 48** The water administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the competent department of natural resources of the State Council, formulate the total groundwater withdrawal control indicators for the provincial-level administrative regions in the Yellow River Basin.  
The water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin shall, together with the relevant departments of the same level people's government, formulate the total groundwater withdrawal control indicators and groundwater level control indicators for the city-level and county-level administrative regions within their respective administrative regions, based on the total groundwater withdrawal control indicators for their respective administrative regions. These indicators shall be submitted to the water administrative department of the State Council or the Yellow River Basin Management Agency for recordation after approval by the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　黄河流域县级以上行政区域的地表水取用水总量不得超过水量分配方案确定的控制指标，并符合生态流量和生态水位的管控指标要求；地下水取水总量不得超过本行政区域地下水取水总量控制指标，并符合地下水水位控制指标要求。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本行政区域取用水总量控制指标，统筹考虑经济社会发展用水需求、节水标准和产业政策，制定本行政区域农业、工业、生活及河道外生态等用水量控制指标。

**Article 49** The total surface water withdrawal and consumption in the county-level and above administrative regions in the Yellow River Basin shall not exceed the control indicators determined in the water allocation plan, and shall comply with the control indicator requirements for ecological flow and ecological water level; the total groundwater withdrawal shall not exceed the total groundwater withdrawal control indicators for the respective administrative regions, and shall comply with the groundwater level control indicator requirements.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall, based on the total water withdrawal and consumption control indicators for their respective administrative regions, taking into comprehensive consideration the water use needs for economic and social development, water conservation standards, and industrial policies, formulate water use control indicators for agriculture, industry, domestic use, and ecology outside river channels in their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在黄河流域取用水资源，应当依法取得取水许可。  
黄河干流取水，以及跨省重要支流指定河段限额以上取水，由黄河流域管理机构负责审批取水申请，审批时应当研究取水口所在地的省级人民政府水行政主管部门的意见；其他取水由黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门负责审批取水申请。指定河段和限额标准由国务院水行政主管部门确定公布、适时调整。

**Article 50** Water withdrawal from the Yellow River Basin shall be subject to obtaining a water withdrawal permit according to law.  
Water withdrawal from the main stream of the Yellow River, and water withdrawal exceeding the quota from designated sections of important inter-provincial tributaries, shall be subject to approval of the water withdrawal application by the Yellow River Basin Management Agency. When approving, the agency shall study the opinions of the water administrative department of the provincial people's government where the water intake is located. Other water withdrawals shall be subject to approval of the water withdrawal application by the water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin. The designated sections and quota standards shall be determined, published, and adjusted in a timely manner by the water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家在黄河流域实行水资源差别化管理。国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院自然资源主管部门定期组织开展黄河流域水资源评价和承载能力调查评估。评估结果作为划定水资源超载地区、临界超载地区、不超载地区的依据。  
水资源超载地区县级以上地方人民政府应当制定水资源超载治理方案，采取产业结构调整、强化节水等措施，实施综合治理。水资源临界超载地区县级以上地方人民政府应当采取限制性措施，防止水资源超载。  
除生活用水等民生保障用水外，黄河流域水资源超载地区不得新增取水许可；水资源临界超载地区应当严格限制新增取水许可。

**Article 51** The State shall implement differentiated management of water resources in the Yellow River basin. The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the competent natural resources department of the State Council, regularly organize the evaluation of water resources and investigation and assessment of carrying capacity in the Yellow River basin. The assessment results shall serve as the basis for delineating areas with water resource overload, critical overload, and non-overload.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in areas with water resource overload shall formulate water resource overload management plans and adopt measures such as industrial structure adjustment and enhanced water conservation to implement comprehensive management. The local people's governments at or above the county level in areas with critical water resource overload shall adopt restrictive measures to prevent water resource overload.  
Except for livelihood-guaranteed water uses such as domestic water, new water intake permits shall not be granted in areas with water resource overload in the Yellow River basin; in areas with critical water resource overload, new water intake permits shall be strictly restricted.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家在黄河流域实行强制性用水定额管理制度。国务院水行政、标准化主管部门应当会同国务院发展改革部门组织制定黄河流域高耗水工业和服务业强制性用水定额。制定强制性用水定额应当征求国务院有关部门、黄河流域省级人民政府、企业事业单位和社会公众等方面的意见，并依照《中华人民共和国标准化法》的有关规定执行。  
黄河流域省级人民政府按照深度节水控水要求，可以制定严于国家用水定额的地方用水定额；国家用水定额未作规定的，可以补充制定地方用水定额。  
黄河流域以及黄河流经省、自治区其他黄河供水区相关县级行政区域的用水单位，应当严格执行强制性用水定额；超过强制性用水定额的，应当限期实施节水技术改造。

**Article 52** The State shall implement a mandatory water quota management system in the Yellow River basin. The competent water administrative and standardization departments of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the development and reform department of the State Council, organize the formulation of mandatory water quotas for high water-consuming industries and service sectors in the Yellow River basin. In formulating mandatory water quotas, opinions shall be solicited from relevant departments of the State Council, provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin, enterprises, institutions, and the public, and shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Standardization Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin may, in accordance with the requirements for in-depth water conservation and consumption control, formulate local water quotas stricter than the national water quotas; where national water quotas have not provided for certain matters, local water quotas may be supplemented.  
Water-using units in the Yellow River basin and the relevant county-level administrative areas within the other Yellow River water supply areas of provinces and autonomous regions through which the Yellow River flows shall strictly implement the mandatory water quotas; where mandatory water quotas are exceeded, they shall implement water conservation technological transformation within a specified period.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　黄河流域以及黄河流经省、自治区其他黄河供水区相关县级行政区域的县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门和黄河流域管理机构核定取水单位的取水量，应当符合用水定额的要求。  
黄河流域以及黄河流经省、自治区其他黄河供水区相关县级行政区域取水量达到取水规模以上的单位，应当安装合格的在线计量设施，保证设施正常运行，并将计量数据传输至有管理权限的水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构。取水规模标准由国务院水行政主管部门制定。

**Article 53** The competent water administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level and the Yellow River basin management authority in the Yellow River basin and the relevant county-level administrative areas within the other Yellow River water supply areas of provinces and autonomous regions through which the Yellow River flows shall, when verifying the water intake volume of water intake units, comply with the water quota requirements.  
Units in the Yellow River basin and the relevant county-level administrative areas within the other Yellow River water supply areas of provinces and autonomous regions through which the Yellow River flows whose water intake volume reaches the scale of water intake shall install qualified online metering facilities, ensure their normal operation, and transmit the metering data to the competent water administrative department or the Yellow River basin management authority with management authority. The standard for the scale of water intake shall be formulated by the competent water administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家在黄河流域实行高耗水产业准入负面清单和淘汰类高耗水产业目录制度。列入高耗水产业准入负面清单和淘汰类高耗水产业目录的建设项目，取水申请不予批准。高耗水产业准入负面清单和淘汰类高耗水产业目录由国务院发展改革部门会同国务院水行政主管部门制定并发布。  
严格限制从黄河流域向外流域扩大供水量，严格限制新增引黄灌溉用水量。因实施国家重大战略确需新增用水量的，应当严格进行水资源论证，并取得黄河流域管理机构批准的取水许可。

**Article 54** The State shall implement a negative list for access to high water-consuming industries and a directory of eliminated high water-consuming industries in the Yellow River basin. For construction projects included in the negative list for access to high water-consuming industries or the directory of eliminated high water-consuming industries, the water intake application shall not be approved. The negative list for access to high water-consuming industries and the directory of eliminated high water-consuming industries shall be formulated and published by the development and reform department of the State Council in conjunction with the competent water administrative department of the State Council.  
The expansion of water supply from the Yellow River to external river basins shall be strictly restricted, and the increase in water use for irrigation diverting water from the Yellow River shall be strictly restricted. Where an increase in water use is truly necessary for the implementation of major national strategies, a water resources argumentation shall be strictly conducted, and a water intake permit approved by the Yellow River basin management authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织发展高效节水农业，加强农业节水设施和农业用水计量设施建设，选育推广低耗水、高耐旱农作物，降低农业耗水量。禁止取用深层地下水用于农业灌溉。  
黄河流域工业企业应当优先使用国家鼓励的节水工艺、技术和装备。国家鼓励的工业节水工艺、技术和装备目录由国务院工业和信息化主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定并发布。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织推广应用先进适用的节水工艺、技术、装备、产品和材料，推进工业废水资源化利用，支持企业用水计量和节水技术改造，支持工业园区企业发展串联用水系统和循环用水系统，促进能源、化工、建材等高耗水产业节水。高耗水工业企业应当实施用水计量和节水技术改造。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织实施城乡老旧供水设施和管网改造，推广普及节水型器具，开展公共机构节水技术改造，控制高耗水服务业用水，完善农村集中供水和节水配套设施。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强节水宣传教育和科学普及，提高公众节水意识，营造良好节水氛围。

**Article 55** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the development of efficient water-saving agriculture, strengthen the construction of agricultural water-saving facilities and agricultural water metering facilities, select, breed, and promote low water-consuming and high drought-tolerant crops, and reduce agricultural water consumption. It shall be prohibited to extract deep groundwater for agricultural irrigation.  
Industrial enterprises in the Yellow River basin shall give priority to using water-saving processes, technologies, and equipment encouraged by the State. The catalog of industrial water-saving processes, technologies, and equipment encouraged by the State shall be formulated and published by the competent industry and information technology department of the State Council in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the promotion and application of advanced and applicable water-saving processes, technologies, equipment, products, and materials, promote the resource utilization of industrial wastewater, support enterprise water metering and water conservation technological transformation, support industrial parks and enterprises in developing series water systems and circulating water systems, and promote water conservation in high water-consuming industries such as energy, chemicals, and building materials. High water-consuming industrial enterprises shall implement water metering and water conservation technological transformation.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the implementation of the renovation of old urban and rural water supply facilities and pipelines, promote and popularize water-saving appliances, carry out water conservation technological transformation of public institutions, control water use in high water-consuming service industries, and improve rural centralized water supply and water conservation supporting facilities.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen water conservation publicity, education, and scientific popularization, enhance public awareness of water conservation, and create a good atmosphere for water conservation.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家在黄河流域建立促进节约用水的水价体系。城镇居民生活用水和具备条件的农村居民生活用水实行阶梯水价，高耗水工业和服务业水价实行高额累进加价，非居民用水水价实行超定额累进加价，推进农业水价综合改革。  
国家在黄河流域对节水潜力大、使用面广的用水产品实行水效标识管理，限期淘汰水效等级较低的用水产品，培育合同节水等节水市场。

**Article 56** The State shall establish a water pricing system that promotes water conservation in the Yellow River basin. Tiered water pricing shall be implemented for domestic water use of urban residents and rural residents where conditions permit; high progressive surcharge pricing shall be implemented for high water-consuming industrial and service water use; super-quota progressive surcharge pricing shall be implemented for non-residential water use; and comprehensive reform of agricultural water pricing shall be promoted.  
In the Yellow River basin, the State shall implement water efficiency labeling management for water-using products with high water-saving potential and wide application, phase out water-using products with low water efficiency grades within a specified period, and cultivate water-saving markets such as contract water conservation.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国务院水行政主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门制定黄河流域重要饮用水水源地名录。黄河流域省级人民政府水行政主管部门应当会同本级人民政府有关部门制定本行政区域的其他饮用水水源地名录。  
黄河流域省级人民政府组织划定饮用水水源保护区，加强饮用水水源保护，保障饮用水安全。黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当合理布局饮用水水源取水口，加强饮用水应急水源、备用水源建设。

**Article 57** The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a directory of important drinking water source areas in the Yellow River basin. The competent water administrative departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, formulate directories of other drinking water source areas within their respective administrative regions.  
The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall organize the delineation of drinking water source protection zones, strengthen the protection of drinking water sources, and ensure the safety of drinking water. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall reasonably arrange the water intake points of drinking water sources and strengthen the construction of emergency drinking water sources and backup water sources.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家综合考虑黄河流域水资源条件、经济社会发展需要和生态环境保护要求，统筹调出区和调入区供水安全和生态安全，科学论证、规划和建设跨流域调水和重大水源工程，加快构建国家水网，优化水资源配置，提高水资源承载能力。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织实施区域水资源配置工程建设，提高城乡供水保障程度。

**Article 58** The State, taking into comprehensive consideration the water resource conditions, economic and social development needs, and ecological environmental protection requirements of the Yellow River basin, coordinating the water supply safety and ecological safety of the water transfer-out and water transfer-in areas, shall scientifically demonstrate, plan, and construct inter-basin water transfer and major water source projects, accelerate the construction of the national water network, optimize the allocation of water resources, and improve the water resource carrying capacity.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the implementation of regional water resource allocation projects to improve the guarantee level of urban and rural water supply.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当推进污水资源化利用，国家对相关设施建设予以支持。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当将再生水、雨水、苦咸水、矿井水等非常规水纳入水资源统一配置，提高非常规水利用比例。景观绿化、工业生产、建筑施工等用水，应当优先使用符合要求的再生水。

**Article 59** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall promote the utilization of wastewater as a resource, and the State shall provide support for the construction of relevant facilities.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall incorporate unconventional water sources such as reclaimed water, rainwater, brackish water, and mine water into the unified allocation of water resources, and increase the proportion of unconventional water use. For water uses such as landscape greening, industrial production, and building construction, reclaimed water meeting the requirements shall be given priority.

### 第五章　水沙调控与防洪安全

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家依据黄河流域综合规划、防洪规划，在黄河流域组织建设水沙调控和防洪减灾工程体系，完善水沙调控和防洪防凌调度机制，加强水文和气象监测预报预警、水沙观测和河势调查，实施重点水库和河段清淤疏浚、滩区放淤，提高河道行洪输沙能力，塑造河道主槽，维持河势稳定，保障防洪安全。

**Article 60** The State, in accordance with the comprehensive plan and flood control plan for the Yellow River basin, shall organize the construction of a water-sediment regulation and flood control and disaster reduction engineering system in the Yellow River basin, improve the water-sediment regulation and flood control and ice prevention dispatching mechanism, strengthen hydrological and meteorological monitoring, forecasting, early warning, water and sediment observation, and river regime survey, implement dredging and desilting of key reservoirs and river sections, and siltation in floodplain areas, enhance the flood discharge and sediment transport capacity of river channels, shape the main channel of the river, maintain river regime stability, and ensure flood control safety.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家完善以骨干水库等重大水工程为主的水沙调控体系，采取联合调水调沙、泥沙综合处理利用等措施，提高拦沙输沙能力。纳入水沙调控体系的工程名录由国务院水行政主管部门制定。  
国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府应当加强黄河干支流控制性水工程、标准化堤防、控制引导河水流向工程等防洪工程体系建设和管理，实施病险水库除险加固和山洪、泥石流灾害防治。  
黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强防洪工程的运行管护，保障工程安全稳定运行。

**Article 61** The State shall improve the water-sediment regulation system with major water projects such as backbone reservoirs as the mainstay, and take measures such as joint water and sediment regulation and comprehensive treatment and utilization of sediment to enhance the capacity for sediment trapping and transport. The catalog of projects included in the water-sediment regulation system shall be formulated by the competent water administrative department of the State Council.  
Relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen the construction and management of flood control engineering systems such as controlling water projects on the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River, standardized levees, and projects for controlling and guiding river flow direction, and carry out reinforcement of dangerous reservoirs and prevention of mountain torrents and debris flow disasters.  
The Yellow River basin management authority and its subordinate management agencies, and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen the operation and maintenance of flood control projects to ensure their safe and stable operation.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　国家实行黄河流域水沙统一调度制度。黄河流域管理机构应当组织实施黄河干支流水库群统一调度，编制水沙调控方案，确定重点水库水沙调控运用指标、运用方式、调控起止时间，下达调度指令。水沙调控应当采取措施尽量减少对水生生物及其栖息地的影响。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府、水库主管部门和管理单位应当执行黄河流域管理机构的调度指令。

**Article 62** The State shall implement a unified water-sediment dispatch system for the Yellow River basin. The Yellow River basin management authority shall organize the implementation of unified dispatch of reservoir groups on the main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River, formulate water-sediment regulation plans, determine the operation targets, operation methods, and start and end times for water-sediment regulation of key reservoirs, and issue dispatch instructions. Water-sediment regulation shall take measures to minimize impacts on aquatic organisms and their habitats.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin, and the competent departments and management units of reservoirs shall execute the dispatch instructions of the Yellow River basin management authority.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国务院水行政主管部门组织编制黄河防御洪水方案，经国家防汛抗旱指挥机构审核后，报国务院批准。  
黄河流域管理机构应当会同黄河流域省级人民政府根据批准的黄河防御洪水方案，编制黄河干流和重要支流、重要水工程的洪水调度方案，报国务院水行政主管部门批准并抄送国家防汛抗旱指挥机构和国务院应急管理部门，按照职责组织实施。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府组织编制和实施黄河其他支流、水工程的洪水调度方案，并报上一级人民政府防汛抗旱指挥机构和有关主管部门备案。

**Article 63** The competent water administrative department of the State Council shall organize the formulation of the Yellow River flood prevention plan, which, after review by the national flood control and drought relief headquarters, shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The Yellow River basin management authority shall, in conjunction with the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin, formulate flood dispatch plans for the Yellow River main stream, important tributaries, and important water projects based on the approved Yellow River flood prevention plan, submit them to the competent water administrative department of the State Council for approval, and send copies to the national flood control and drought relief headquarters and the emergency management department of the State Council, and organize implementation according to their functions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the formulation and implementation of flood dispatch plans for other tributaries and water projects of the Yellow River, and report them to the flood control and drought relief headquarters of the people's government at the next higher level and the relevant competent departments for recordation.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　黄河流域管理机构制定年度防凌调度方案，报国务院水行政主管部门备案，按照职责组织实施。  
黄河流域有防凌任务的县级以上地方人民政府应当把防御凌汛纳入本行政区域的防洪规划。

**Article 64** The Yellow River basin management authority shall formulate an annual ice prevention dispatch plan, report it to the competent water administrative department of the State Council for recordation, and organize implementation according to its functions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin with ice prevention tasks shall incorporate ice flood prevention into the flood control plan of their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　黄河防汛抗旱指挥机构负责指挥黄河流域防汛抗旱工作，其办事机构设在黄河流域管理机构，承担黄河防汛抗旱指挥机构的日常工作。

**Article 65** The Yellow River flood control and drought relief headquarters shall be responsible for commanding the flood control and drought relief work in the Yellow River basin. Its office shall be established within the Yellow River basin management authority and shall undertake the daily work of the Yellow River flood control and drought relief headquarters.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　黄河流域管理机构应当会同黄河流域省级人民政府依据黄河流域防洪规划，制定黄河滩区名录，报国务院水行政主管部门批准。黄河流域省级人民政府应当有序安排滩区居民迁建，严格控制向滩区迁入常住人口，实施滩区综合提升治理工程。  
黄河滩区土地利用、基础设施建设和生态保护与修复应当满足河道行洪需要，发挥滩区滞洪、沉沙功能。  
在黄河滩区内，不得新规划城镇建设用地、设立新的村镇，已经规划和设立的，不得扩大范围；不得新划定永久基本农田，已经划定为永久基本农田、影响防洪安全的，应当逐步退出；不得新开垦荒地、新建生产堤，已建生产堤影响防洪安全的应当及时拆除，其他生产堤应当逐步拆除。  
因黄河滩区自然行洪、蓄滞洪水等导致受淹造成损失的，按照国家有关规定予以补偿。

**Article 66** The Yellow River basin management authority shall, in conjunction with the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin, formulate a directory of Yellow River floodplain areas based on the Yellow River basin flood control plan, and submit it to the competent water administrative department of the State Council for approval. The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall arrange for the relocation of residents in floodplain areas in an orderly manner, strictly control the migration of permanent residents into floodplain areas, and implement comprehensive improvement and upgrading projects in floodplain areas.  
The utilization of land, construction of infrastructure, and ecological protection and restoration in Yellow River floodplain areas shall meet the requirements for river channel flood discharge and play the functions of flood detention and sediment storage in the floodplain area.  
In Yellow River floodplain areas, new planning of urban construction land or establishment of new villages and towns shall not be allowed; for those already planned or established, the scope shall not be expanded; new permanent basic farmland shall not be designated; for those already designated as permanent basic farmland that affect flood control safety, they shall be gradually withdrawn; new reclamation of wasteland or construction of new production levees shall not be allowed; production levees that have been built and affect flood control safety shall be promptly demolished, and other production levees shall be gradually demolished.  
Compensation shall be provided in accordance with relevant state regulations for losses caused by flooding due to natural flood discharge, flood storage, or detention in Yellow River floodplain areas.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　国家加强黄河流域河道、湖泊管理和保护。禁止在河道、湖泊管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物以及从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动。禁止违法利用、占用河道、湖泊水域和岸线。河道、湖泊管理范围由黄河流域管理机构和有关县级以上地方人民政府依法科学划定并公布。  
建设跨河、穿河、穿堤、临河的工程设施，应当符合防洪标准等要求，不得威胁堤防安全、影响河势稳定、擅自改变水域和滩地用途、降低行洪和调蓄能力、缩小水域面积；确实无法避免降低行洪和调蓄能力、缩小水域面积的，应当同时建设等效替代工程或者采取其他功能补救措施。

**Article 67** The State shall strengthen the management and protection of river channels and lakes in the Yellow River basin. It shall be prohibited to construct buildings or structures that obstruct flood discharge within the management scope of river channels and lakes, or engage in activities that affect river regime stability, endanger the safety of river banks and levees, or otherwise obstruct river channel flood discharge. It shall be prohibited to illegally use or occupy river channel and lake waters and shorelines. The management scope of river channels and lakes shall be scientifically delineated and published by the Yellow River basin management authority and the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level according to law.  
The construction of engineering facilities crossing, piercing, or adjacent to river channels, levees, or lakes shall meet flood control standards and other requirements, and shall not threaten levee safety, affect river regime stability, arbitrarily change the use of waters and floodplains, reduce flood discharge and regulation capacity, or reduce water area; where it is truly unavoidable to reduce flood discharge and regulation capacity or water area, equivalent alternative projects shall be constructed simultaneously or other functional remedial measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　黄河流域河道治理，应当因地制宜采取河道清障、清淤疏浚、岸坡整治、堤防加固、水源涵养与水土保持、河湖管护等治理措施，加强悬河和游荡性河道整治，增强河道、湖泊、水库防御洪水能力。  
国家支持黄河流域有关地方人民政府以稳定河势、规范流路、保障行洪能力为前提，统筹河道岸线保护修复、退耕还湿，建设集防洪、生态保护等功能于一体的绿色生态走廊。

**Article 68** The regulation of river channels in the Yellow River basin shall, based on local conditions, adopt measures such as removal of obstacles from river channels, dredging and desilting, bank slope remediation, levee reinforcement, water source conservation and soil and water conservation, and river and lake management and protection, strengthen the regulation of suspended rivers and wandering rivers, and enhance the flood defense capability of rivers, lakes, and reservoirs.  
The State shall support the relevant local people's governments in the Yellow River basin, on the premise of stabilizing river regime, standardizing flow paths, and ensuring flood discharge capacity, to coordinate the protection and restoration of river channel shorelines and the conversion of farmland to wetlands, and build a green ecological corridor integrating flood control, ecological protection, and other functions.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家实行黄河流域河道采砂规划和许可制度。黄河流域河道采砂应当依法取得采砂许可。  
黄河流域管理机构和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府依法划定禁采区，规定禁采期，并向社会公布。禁止在黄河流域禁采区和禁采期从事河道采砂活动。

**Article 69** The State shall implement a planning and licensing system for sand mining in the Yellow River basin. Sand mining in the Yellow River basin shall obtain a sand mining license according to law.  
The Yellow River basin management authority and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall, according to law, delineate prohibited mining areas, specify prohibited mining periods, and announce them to the public. It shall be prohibited to engage in sand mining activities in the prohibited mining areas and during the prohibited mining periods in the Yellow River basin.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国务院有关部门应当会同黄河流域省级人民政府加强对龙羊峡、刘家峡、三门峡、小浪底、故县、陆浑、河口村等干支流骨干水库库区的管理，科学调控水库水位，加强库区水土保持、生态保护和地质灾害防治工作。  
在三门峡、小浪底、故县、陆浑、河口村水库库区养殖，应当满足水沙调控和防洪要求，禁止采用网箱、围网和拦河拉网方式养殖。

**Article 70** Relevant departments of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin, strengthen the management of the reservoir areas of backbone reservoirs on the main stream and tributaries, such as Longyangxia, Liujiaxia, Sanmenxia, Xiaolangdi, Guxian, Luhun, and Hekoucun, scientifically regulate reservoir water levels, and strengthen soil and water conservation, ecological protection, and geological disaster prevention in the reservoir areas.  
Aquaculture in the reservoir areas of Sanmenxia, Xiaolangdi, Guxian, Luhun, and Hekoucun shall meet the requirements for water-sediment regulation and flood control, and the use of cage culture, enclosure culture, and net fishing across rivers shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　黄河流域城市人民政府应当统筹城市防洪和排涝工作，加强城市防洪排涝设施建设和管理，完善城市洪涝灾害监测预警机制，健全城市防灾减灾体系，提升城市洪涝灾害防御和应对能力。  
黄河流域城市人民政府及其有关部门应当加强洪涝灾害防御宣传教育和社会动员，定期组织开展应急演练，增强社会防范意识。

**Article 71** The people's governments of cities in the Yellow River basin shall coordinate urban flood control and waterlogging drainage work, strengthen the construction and management of urban flood control and waterlogging drainage facilities, improve the urban flood and waterlogging disaster monitoring and early warning mechanism, perfect the urban disaster prevention and mitigation system, and enhance the capacity to prevent and respond to urban flood and waterlogging disasters.  
The people's governments of cities in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen publicity, education, and social mobilization for flood and waterlogging disaster prevention, regularly organize emergency drills, and enhance social awareness of prevention.

### 第六章　污染防治

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国家加强黄河流域农业面源污染、工业污染、城乡生活污染等的综合治理、系统治理、源头治理，推进重点河湖环境综合整治。

**Article 72** The State shall strengthen comprehensive, systematic, and source management of agricultural non-point source pollution, industrial pollution, and urban and rural domestic pollution in the Yellow River basin, and promote comprehensive environmental improvement of key rivers and lakes.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国务院生态环境主管部门制定黄河流域水环境质量标准，对国家水环境质量标准中未作规定的项目，可以作出补充规定；对国家水环境质量标准中已经规定的项目，可以作出更加严格的规定。制定黄河流域水环境质量标准应当征求国务院有关部门和有关省级人民政府的意见。  
黄河流域省级人民政府可以制定严于黄河流域水环境质量标准的地方水环境质量标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 73** The competent ecological environment department of the State Council shall formulate water environment quality standards for the Yellow River basin; for items not provided for in the national water environment quality standards, supplementary provisions may be made; for items already provided for in the national water environment quality standards, stricter provisions may be made. The formulation of water environment quality standards for the Yellow River basin shall solicit opinions from relevant departments of the State Council and relevant provincial people's governments.  
The provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin may formulate local water environment quality standards stricter than those for the Yellow River basin, and report them to the competent ecological environment department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　对没有国家水污染物排放标准的特色产业、特有污染物，以及国家有明确要求的特定水污染源或者水污染物，黄河流域省级人民政府应当补充制定地方水污染物排放标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。  
有下列情形之一的，黄河流域省级人民政府应当制定严于国家水污染物排放标准的地方水污染物排放标准，报国务院生态环境主管部门备案：  
（一）产业密集、水环境问题突出；  
（二）现有水污染物排放标准不能满足黄河流域水环境质量要求；  
（三）流域或者区域水环境形势复杂，无法适用统一的水污染物排放标准。

**Article 74** For characteristic industries and unique pollutants for which there are no national pollutant discharge standards for water, or for specific sources or pollutants of water for which the State has explicit requirements, the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall supplement local pollutant discharge standards for water and report them to the competent ecological environment department of the State Council for recordation.  
Where any of the following circumstances exist, the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall formulate local pollutant discharge standards for water stricter than the national standards and report them to the competent ecological environment department of the State Council for recordation:  
(1) The industries are dense and water environment problems are prominent;  
(2) Existing water pollutant discharge standards cannot meet the water environment quality requirements of the Yellow River basin;  
(3) The water environment situation in the basin or region is complex and uniform water pollutant discharge standards cannot be applied.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据水环境质量改善目标和水污染防治要求，确定黄河流域各省级行政区域重点水污染物排放总量控制指标。黄河流域水环境质量不达标的水功能区，省级人民政府生态环境主管部门应当实施更加严格的水污染物排放总量削减措施，限期实现水环境质量达标。排放水污染物的企业事业单位应当按照要求，采取水污染物排放总量控制措施。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强和统筹污水、固体废物收集处理处置等环境基础设施建设，保障设施正常运行，因地制宜推进农村厕所改造、生活垃圾处理和污水治理，消除黑臭水体。

**Article 75** The competent ecological environment department of the State Council shall, based on the objectives for improving water environment quality and the requirements for water pollution prevention and control, determine the total discharge control targets for key water pollutants in each provincial administrative region of the Yellow River basin. For water function zones in the Yellow River basin where water environment quality is not up to standard, the competent ecological environment departments of the provincial people's governments shall implement stricter measures for reducing the total discharge of water pollutants, and achieve water environment quality standards within a specified period. Enterprises and institutions that discharge water pollutants shall take measures to control the total discharge of water pollutants as required.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen and coordinate the construction of environmental infrastructure such as sewage and solid waste collection, treatment, and disposal facilities, ensure their normal operation, promote the renovation of rural toilets, domestic waste treatment, and sewage treatment according to local conditions, and eliminate black and odorous water bodies.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　在黄河流域河道、湖泊新设、改设或者扩大排污口，应当报经有管辖权的生态环境主管部门或者黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构批准。新设、改设或者扩大可能影响防洪、供水、堤防安全、河势稳定的排污口的，审批时应当征求县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构的意见。  
黄河流域水环境质量不达标的水功能区，除城乡污水集中处理设施等重要民生工程的排污口外，应当严格控制新设、改设或者扩大排污口。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当对本行政区域河道、湖泊的排污口组织开展排查整治，明确责任主体，实施分类管理。

**Article 76** The establishment, alteration, or expansion of sewage outlets in the river channels and lakes of the Yellow River basin shall be approved by the competent ecological environment department with jurisdiction or the Yellow River basin ecological environment supervision and management institution. For the establishment, alteration, or expansion of sewage outlets that may affect flood control, water supply, levee safety, or river regime stability, the opinions of the competent water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River basin management authority shall be solicited during approval.  
For water function zones in the Yellow River basin where water environment quality is not up to standard, except for sewage outlets of important livelihood projects such as centralized urban and rural sewage treatment facilities, the establishment, alteration, or expansion of sewage outlets shall be strictly controlled.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the investigation and remediation of sewage outlets in the river channels and lakes within their administrative regions, clarify the responsible entities, and implement classified management.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当对沿河道、湖泊的垃圾填埋场、加油站、储油库、矿山、尾矿库、危险废物处置场、化工园区和化工项目等地下水重点污染源及周边地下水环境风险隐患组织开展调查评估，采取风险防范和整治措施。  
黄河流域设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门商本级人民政府有关部门，制定并发布地下水污染防治重点排污单位名录。地下水污染防治重点排污单位应当依法安装水污染物排放自动监测设备，与生态环境主管部门的监控设备联网，并保证监测设备正常运行。

**Article 77** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the investigation and assessment of environmental risk hazards of key groundwater pollution sources along river channels and lakes, such as garbage landfills, gas stations, oil depots, mines, tailings ponds, hazardous waste disposal sites, chemical industrial parks, and chemical projects, and the surrounding groundwater environment, and take risk prevention and remediation measures.  
The competent ecological environment departments of the local people's governments at or above the city with districts level in the Yellow River basin shall, in consultation with relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, formulate and publish a directory of key discharge units for groundwater pollution prevention and control. Key discharge units for groundwater pollution prevention and control shall install automatic monitoring equipment for water pollutant discharge according to law, connect it with the monitoring equipment of the competent ecological environment department, and ensure the normal operation of the monitoring equipment.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　黄河流域省级人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同本级人民政府水行政、自然资源等主管部门，根据本行政区域地下水污染防治需要，划定地下水污染防治重点区，明确环境准入、隐患排查、风险管控等管理要求。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强油气开采区等地下水污染防治监督管理。在黄河流域开发煤层气、致密气等非常规天然气的，应当对其产生的压裂液、采出水进行处理处置，不得污染土壤和地下水。

**Article 78** The competent ecological environment departments of the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall, in conjunction with the competent water administrative, natural resources, and other departments of the people's government at the same level, delineate key areas for groundwater pollution prevention and control based on the needs for groundwater pollution prevention and control in their respective administrative regions, and specify management requirements such as environmental access, hazard investigation, and risk control.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen the supervision and management of groundwater pollution prevention and control in oil and gas extraction areas and other areas. The development of unconventional natural gas such as coalbed methane and tight gas in the Yellow River basin shall involve the treatment and disposal of the fracturing fluid and produced water generated, which shall not pollute the soil and groundwater.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强黄河流域土壤生态环境保护，防止新增土壤污染，因地制宜分类推进土壤污染风险管控与修复。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强黄河流域固体废物污染环境防治，组织开展固体废物非法转移和倾倒的联防联控。

**Article 79** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen the protection of the soil ecological environment in the Yellow River basin, prevent the addition of new soil pollution, and promote the risk control and remediation of soil pollution in a classified manner according to local conditions.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen the prevention and control of environmental pollution caused by solid waste in the Yellow River basin, and organize joint prevention and control of illegal transfer and dumping of solid waste.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当在黄河流域定期组织开展大气、水体、土壤、生物中有毒有害化学物质调查监测，并会同国务院卫生健康等主管部门开展黄河流域有毒有害化学物质环境风险评估与管控。  
国务院生态环境等主管部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强对持久性有机污染物等新污染物的管控、治理。

**Article 80** The competent ecological environment department of the State Council shall regularly organize the investigation and monitoring of toxic and hazardous chemicals in the atmosphere, water bodies, soil, and organisms in the Yellow River basin, and shall, in conjunction with the competent health and other departments of the State Council, conduct environmental risk assessment and control of toxic and hazardous chemicals in the Yellow River basin.  
The competent ecological environment and other departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen the control and treatment of new pollutants such as persistent organic pollutants.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当加强农药、化肥等农业投入品使用总量控制、使用指导和技术服务，推广病虫害绿色防控等先进适用技术，实施灌区农田退水循环利用，加强对农业污染源的监测预警。  
黄河流域农业生产经营者应当科学合理使用农药、化肥、兽药等农业投入品，科学处理、处置农业投入品包装废弃物、农用薄膜等农业废弃物，综合利用农作物秸秆，加强畜禽、水产养殖污染防治。

**Article 81** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall strengthen the total use control, use guidance, and technical services for agricultural inputs such as pesticides and fertilizers, promote advanced and applicable technologies such as green pest prevention and control, implement recycling of return flow from irrigation areas, and strengthen monitoring and early warning of agricultural pollution sources.  
Agricultural producers and operators in the Yellow River basin shall scientifically and rationally use agricultural inputs such as pesticides, fertilizers, and veterinary drugs, scientifically treat and dispose of agricultural waste such as packaging waste of agricultural inputs and agricultural plastic film, comprehensively utilize crop straw, and strengthen the prevention and control of pollution from livestock, poultry, and aquaculture.

### 第七章　促进高质量发展

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　促进黄河流域高质量发展应当坚持新发展理念，加快发展方式绿色转型，以生态保护为前提优化调整区域经济和生产力布局。

**Article 82** To promote high-quality development in the Yellow River basin, the new development philosophy shall be adhered to, the pace of green transformation of development modes shall be accelerated, and the regional economic and productivity layout shall be optimized and adjusted on the premise of ecological protection.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当协同推进黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展战略与乡村振兴战略、新型城镇化战略和中部崛起、西部大开发等区域协调发展战略的实施，统筹城乡基础设施建设和产业发展，改善城乡人居环境，健全基本公共服务体系，促进城乡融合发展。

**Article 83** Relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall coordinate the implementation of the strategy for ecological protection and high-quality development of the Yellow River basin with the rural revitalization strategy, the new urbanization strategy, and regional coordinated development strategies such as the rise of the central region and the development of the western region, coordinate urban and rural infrastructure construction and industrial development, improve urban and rural living environments, perfect the basic public service system, and promote integrated urban and rural development.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当强化生态环境、水资源等约束和城镇开发边界管控，严格控制黄河流域上中游地区新建各类开发区，推进节水型城市、海绵城市建设，提升城市综合承载能力和公共服务能力。

**Article 84** Relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen constraints on ecology, environment, water resources, etc., and control of urban development boundaries, strictly control the establishment of new development zones in the upper and middle reaches of the Yellow River basin, and promote the construction of water-saving cities and sponge cities, enhancing the comprehensive carrying capacity of cities and their public service capabilities.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当科学规划乡村布局，统筹生态保护与乡村发展，加强农村基础设施建设，推进农村产业融合发展，鼓励使用绿色低碳能源，加快推进农房和村庄建设现代化，塑造乡村风貌，建设生态宜居美丽乡村。

**Article 85** Relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall scientifically plan the layout of villages, coordinate ecological protection and rural development, strengthen the construction of rural infrastructure, promote the integrated development of rural industries, encourage the use of green and low-carbon energy, accelerate the modernization of rural housing and village construction, shape the rural landscape, and build ecologically livable and beautiful villages.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　黄河流域产业结构和布局应当与黄河流域生态系统和资源环境承载能力相适应。严格限制在黄河流域布局高耗水、高污染或者高耗能项目。  
黄河流域煤炭、火电、钢铁、焦化、化工、有色金属等行业应当开展清洁生产，依法实施强制性清洁生产审核。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，推动企业实施清洁化改造，组织推广应用工业节能、资源综合利用等先进适用的技术装备，完善绿色制造体系。

**Article 86** The industrial structure and layout of the Yellow River basin shall be compatible with the carrying capacity of the ecosystem, resources, and environment in the basin. The layout of high water-consuming, highly polluting, or high energy-consuming projects in the Yellow River basin shall be strictly restricted.  
Industries such as coal, thermal power, steel, coking, chemicals, and non-ferrous metals in the Yellow River basin shall carry out cleaner production and implement mandatory cleaner production audits according to law.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall take measures to promote the implementation of cleaner transformation by enterprises, organize the promotion and application of advanced and applicable technical equipment such as industrial energy conservation and comprehensive utilization of resources, and improve the green manufacturing system.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　国家鼓励黄河流域开展新型基础设施建设，完善交通运输、水利、能源、防灾减灾等基础设施网络。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当推动制造业高质量发展和资源型产业转型，因地制宜发展特色优势现代产业和清洁低碳能源，推动产业结构、能源结构、交通运输结构等优化调整，推进碳达峰碳中和工作。

**Article 87** The State shall encourage the development of new infrastructure in the Yellow River basin, improving the infrastructure network for transportation, water conservancy, energy, disaster prevention and mitigation, etc.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall promote high-quality development of the manufacturing industry and transformation of resource-based industries, develop characteristic and advantageous modern industries and clean and low-carbon energy according to local conditions, promote the optimization and adjustment of industrial structure, energy structure, and transportation structure, and advance the work on peaking carbon emissions and achieving carbon neutrality.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　国家鼓励、支持黄河流域建设高标准农田、现代畜牧业生产基地以及种质资源和制种基地，因地制宜开展盐碱地农业技术研究、开发和应用，支持地方品种申请地理标志产品保护，发展现代农业服务业。  
国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当组织调整农业产业结构，优化农业产业布局，发展区域优势农业产业，服务国家粮食安全战略。

**Article 88** The State shall encourage and support the construction of high-standard farmland, modern animal husbandry production bases, and germplasm resources and seed production bases in the Yellow River basin, carry out research, development, and application of saline-alkali land agricultural technology according to local conditions, support the application of local varieties for protection as geographical indication products, and develop modern agricultural service industries.  
Relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall organize the adjustment of the agricultural industrial structure, optimize the agricultural industrial layout, develop regional advantageous agricultural industries, and serve the national food security strategy.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当鼓励、支持黄河流域科技创新，引导社会资金参与科技成果开发和推广应用，提升黄河流域科技创新能力。  
国家支持社会资金设立黄河流域科技成果转化基金，完善科技投融资体系，综合运用政府采购、技术标准、激励机制等促进科技成果转化。

**Article 89** Relevant departments of the State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall encourage and support scientific and technological innovation in the Yellow River basin, guide social funds to participate in the development, promotion, and application of scientific and technological achievements, and enhance the capacity for scientific and technological innovation in the Yellow River basin.  
The State shall support the establishment of a Yellow River basin science and technology achievement transformation fund with social funds, improve the science and technology investment and financing system, and comprehensively utilize government procurement, technical standards, incentive mechanisms, etc., to promote the transformation of scientific and technological achievements.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取有效措施，提高城乡居民对本行政区域生态环境、资源禀赋的认识，支持、引导居民形成绿色低碳的生活方式。

**Article 90** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall take effective measures to improve the understanding of urban and rural residents regarding the ecological environment and resource endowment of their respective administrative regions, and support and guide residents to form green and low-carbon lifestyles.

### 第八章　黄河文化保护传承弘扬

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　国务院文化和旅游主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门编制并实施黄河文化保护传承弘扬规划，加强统筹协调，推动黄河文化体系建设。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其文化和旅游等主管部门应当加强黄河文化保护传承弘扬，提供优质公共文化服务，丰富城乡居民精神文化生活。

**Article 91** The competent cultural and tourism department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council, formulate and implement a plan for the protection, inheritance, and promotion of the Yellow River culture, strengthen overall coordination, and promote the construction of the Yellow River cultural system.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their competent cultural and tourism and other departments shall strengthen the protection, inheritance, and promotion of the Yellow River culture, provide high-quality public cultural services, and enrich the spiritual and cultural life of urban and rural residents.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　国务院文化和旅游主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府，组织开展黄河文化和治河历史研究，推动黄河文化创造性转化和创新性发展。

**Article 92** The competent cultural and tourism department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin, organize the conduct of research on Yellow River culture and the history of river management, and promote the creative transformation and innovative development of Yellow River culture.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　国务院文化和旅游主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门组织指导黄河文化资源调查和认定，对文物古迹、非物质文化遗产、古籍文献等重要文化遗产进行记录、建档，建立黄河文化资源基础数据库，推动黄河文化资源整合利用和公共数据开放共享。

**Article 93** The competent cultural and tourism department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council, organize guidance for the investigation and identification of Yellow River cultural resources, record and file important cultural heritage such as cultural relics and monuments, intangible cultural heritage, and ancient books and documents, establish a basic database of Yellow River cultural resources, and promote the integrated utilization of Yellow River cultural resources and the open sharing of public data.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　国家加强黄河流域历史文化名城名镇名村、历史文化街区、文物、历史建筑、传统村落、少数民族特色村寨和古河道、古堤防、古灌溉工程等水文化遗产以及农耕文化遗产、地名文化遗产等的保护。国务院住房和城乡建设、文化和旅游、文物等主管部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门按照职责分工和分级保护、分类实施的原则，加强监督管理。  
国家加强黄河流域非物质文化遗产保护。国务院文化和旅游等主管部门和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门应当完善黄河流域非物质文化遗产代表性项目名录体系，推进传承体验设施建设，加强代表性项目保护传承。

**Article 94** The State shall strengthen the protection of historical and cultural cities, towns, villages, historical and cultural blocks, cultural relics, historical buildings, traditional villages, ethnic minority characteristic villages, and water cultural heritage such as ancient river channels, ancient levees, ancient irrigation projects, as well as agricultural cultural heritage and place-name cultural heritage in the Yellow River basin. The competent departments of housing and urban-rural development, culture and tourism, cultural relics, etc., of the State Council and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen supervision and management in accordance with the principle of graded protection and classified implementation based on their respective responsibilities.  
The State shall strengthen the protection of intangible cultural heritage in the Yellow River basin. The competent departments of culture and tourism, cultural relics, etc., of the State Council and the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall improve the system of representative items of intangible cultural heritage in the Yellow River basin, promote the construction of inheritance and experience facilities, and strengthen the protection and inheritance of representative items.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　国家加强黄河流域具有革命纪念意义的文物和遗迹保护，建设革命传统教育、爱国主义教育基地，传承弘扬黄河红色文化。

**Article 95** The State shall strengthen the protection of cultural relics and sites with revolutionary commemorative significance in the Yellow River basin, build bases for education on revolutionary traditions and patriotism, and inherit and promote the red culture of the Yellow River.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　国家建设黄河国家文化公园，统筹利用文化遗产地以及博物馆、纪念馆、展览馆、教育基地、水工程等资源，综合运用信息化手段，系统展示黄河文化。  
国务院发展改革部门、文化和旅游主管部门组织开展黄河国家文化公园建设。

**Article 96** The State shall construct the Yellow River National Cultural Park, comprehensively utilize resources such as cultural heritage sites, museums, memorial halls, exhibition halls, education bases, and water projects, and comprehensively display Yellow River culture using information technology.  
The development and reform department and the competent cultural and tourism department of the State Council shall organize the construction of the Yellow River National Cultural Park.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　国家采取政府购买服务等措施，支持单位和个人参与提供反映黄河流域特色、体现黄河文化精神、适宜普及推广的公共文化服务。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当组织将黄河文化融入城乡建设和水利工程等基础设施建设。

**Article 97** The State shall adopt measures such as government procurement of services to support units and individuals in participating in the provision of public cultural services that reflect the characteristics of the Yellow River, embody the spirit of Yellow River culture, and are suitable for popularization.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin and their relevant departments shall organize the integration of Yellow River culture into urban and rural construction and the construction of infrastructure such as water conservancy projects.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当以保护传承弘扬黄河文化为重点，推动文化产业发展，促进文化产业与农业、水利、制造业、交通运输业、服务业等深度融合。  
国务院文化和旅游主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门统筹黄河文化、流域水景观和水工程等资源，建设黄河文化旅游带。黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府文化和旅游主管部门应当结合当地实际，推动本行政区域旅游业发展，展示和弘扬黄河文化。  
黄河流域旅游活动应当符合黄河防洪和河道、湖泊管理要求，避免破坏生态环境和文化遗产。

**Article 98** The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall, with the focus on protecting, inheriting, and promoting Yellow River culture, promote the development of the cultural industry and facilitate the deep integration of the cultural industry with agriculture, water conservancy, manufacturing, transportation, service industries, etc.  
The competent cultural and tourism department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments of the State Council, coordinate resources such as Yellow River culture, river landscapes, and water projects to build a Yellow River cultural tourism belt. The competent cultural and tourism departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall, in light of local conditions, promote the development of the tourism industry in their respective administrative regions, and display and promote Yellow River culture.  
Tourism activities in the Yellow River basin shall comply with the requirements for flood control, river channel and lake management, and avoid damaging the ecological environment and cultural heritage.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　国家鼓励开展黄河题材文艺作品创作。黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加强对黄河题材文艺作品创作的支持和保护。  
国家加强黄河文化宣传，促进黄河文化国际传播，鼓励、支持举办黄河文化交流、合作等活动，提高黄河文化影响力。

**Article 99** The State shall encourage the creation of literary and artistic works on Yellow River themes. The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall strengthen support and protection for the creation of literary and artistic works on Yellow River themes.  
The State shall strengthen the publicity of Yellow River culture, promote the international dissemination of Yellow River culture, and encourage and support the holding of activities such as Yellow River cultural exchanges and cooperation to enhance the influence of Yellow River culture.

### 第九章　保障与监督

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　国务院和黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当加大对黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展的财政投入。  
国务院和黄河流域省级人民政府按照中央与地方财政事权和支出责任划分原则，安排资金用于黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展。  
国家支持设立黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展基金，专项用于黄河流域生态保护与修复、资源能源节约集约利用、战略性新兴产业培育、黄河文化保护传承弘扬等。

**Article 100** The State Council and the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River basin shall increase financial investment in ecological protection and high-quality development of the Yellow River basin.  
The State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River basin shall allocate funds for ecological protection and high-quality development of the Yellow River basin in accordance with the principle of division of financial powers and expenditure responsibilities between the central and local governments.  
The State shall support the establishment of a fund for ecological protection and high-quality development of the Yellow River basin, specifically used for ecological protection and restoration, conservation and intensive use of resources and energy, cultivation of strategic emerging industries, and protection, inheritance, and promotion of Yellow River culture in the basin.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　国家实行有利于节水、节能、生态环境保护和资源综合利用的税收政策，鼓励发展绿色信贷、绿色债券、绿色保险等金融产品，为黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展提供支持。  
国家在黄河流域建立有利于水、电、气等资源性产品节约集约利用的价格机制，对资源高消耗行业中的限制类项目，实行限制性价格政策。

**Article 101** The State shall implement tax policies conducive to water conservation, energy conservation, ecological environmental protection, and comprehensive utilization of resources, encourage the development of green credit, green bonds, green insurance and other financial products, and provide support for ecological conservation and high-quality development of the Yellow River Basin.  
The State shall establish in the Yellow River Basin a pricing mechanism conducive to the economical and intensive use of water, electricity, gas and other resource-based products, and implement restrictive pricing policies for projects of restricted categories in industries with high resource consumption.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　国家建立健全黄河流域生态保护补偿制度。  
国家加大财政转移支付力度，对黄河流域生态功能重要区域予以补偿。具体办法由国务院财政部门会同国务院有关部门制定。  
国家加强对黄河流域行政区域间生态保护补偿的统筹指导、协调，引导和支持黄河流域上下游、左右岸、干支流地方人民政府之间通过协商或者按照市场规则，采用资金补偿、产业扶持等多种形式开展横向生态保护补偿。  
国家鼓励社会资金设立市场化运作的黄河流域生态保护补偿基金。国家支持在黄河流域开展用水权市场化交易。

**Article 102** The State shall establish and improve the ecological protection compensation system for the Yellow River Basin.  
The State shall increase financial transfer payments and provide compensation to areas with important ecological functions in the Yellow River Basin. The specific measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council.  
The State shall strengthen overall guidance and coordination of ecological protection compensation among administrative regions in the Yellow River Basin, and guide and support the local people's governments in the upstream, downstream, left bank, right bank, main stream and tributaries of the Yellow River Basin to carry out horizontal ecological protection compensation through consultation or in accordance with market rules, adopting multiple forms such as monetary compensation and industrial support.  
The State shall encourage social funds to establish market-oriented ecological protection compensation funds for the Yellow River Basin. The State shall support the market-oriented trading of water use rights in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　国家实行黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展责任制和考核评价制度。上级人民政府应当对下级人民政府水资源、水土保持强制性约束控制指标落实情况等生态保护和高质量发展目标完成情况进行考核。

**Article 103** The State shall implement a responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for ecological conservation and high-quality development of the Yellow River Basin. The people's government at a higher level shall assess the completion of ecological conservation and high-quality development targets of the people's government at a lower level, including the implementation of mandatory binding control indicators for water resources and soil and water conservation.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　国务院有关部门、黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府有关部门、黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构、黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构按照职责分工，对黄河流域各类生产生活、开发建设等活动进行监督检查，依法查处违法行为，公开黄河保护工作相关信息，完善公众参与程序，为单位和个人参与和监督黄河保护工作提供便利。  
单位和个人有权依法获取黄河保护工作相关信息，举报和控告违法行为。

**Article 104** The relevant departments of the State Council, the relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin, the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities, and the Yellow River Basin ecological environment supervision and management authority shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, supervise and inspect various production, living, development and construction activities in the Yellow River Basin, investigate and deal with illegal acts according to law, disclose information related to the Yellow River protection work, improve public participation procedures, and facilitate the participation and supervision of entities and individuals in the Yellow River protection work.  
Entities and individuals have the right to obtain information related to the Yellow River protection work according to law, and to report and accuse illegal acts.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　国务院有关部门、黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构、黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构应当加强黄河保护监督管理能力建设，提高科技化、信息化水平，建立执法协调机制，对跨行政区域、生态敏感区域以及重大违法案件，依法开展联合执法。  
国家加强黄河流域司法保障建设，组织开展黄河流域司法协作，推进行政执法机关与司法机关协同配合，鼓励有关单位为黄河流域生态环境保护提供法律服务。

**Article 105** The relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments, the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities, and the Yellow River Basin ecological environment supervision and management authority shall strengthen the capacity building for supervision and management of Yellow River protection, enhance the level of science, technology and information technology, establish a law enforcement coordination mechanism, and carry out joint law enforcement according to law for cases involving cross-administrative regions, ecologically sensitive areas, and major illegal acts.  
The State shall strengthen the judicial guarantee construction for the Yellow River Basin, organize and carry out judicial cooperation in the Yellow River Basin, promote the coordination and cooperation between administrative law enforcement organs and judicial organs, and encourage relevant entities to provide legal services for ecological and environmental protection in the Yellow River Basin.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　国务院有关部门和黄河流域省级人民政府对黄河保护不力、问题突出、群众反映集中的地区，可以约谈该地区县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门主要负责人，要求其采取措施及时整改。约谈和整改情况应当向社会公布。

**Article 106** The relevant departments of the State Council and the provincial people's governments in the Yellow River Basin may, for areas where Yellow River protection is inadequate, problems are prominent, and the public has concentrated complaints, conduct interviews with the principal leading persons of the local people's governments at or above the county level in such areas and their relevant departments, and require them to take measures to make timely rectifications. The interviews and rectifications shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　国务院应当定期向全国人民代表大会常务委员会报告黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作情况。  
黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府应当定期向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告本级人民政府黄河流域生态保护和高质量发展工作情况。

**Article 107** The State Council shall periodically report to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the work of ecological conservation and high-quality development of the Yellow River Basin.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin shall periodically report to the people's congress at the corresponding level or its standing committee on the work of ecological conservation and high-quality development of the Yellow River Basin carried out by the people's government at the corresponding level.

### 第十章　法律责任

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　国务院有关部门、黄河流域县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门、黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构、黄河流域生态环境监督管理机构违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予警告、记过、记大过或者降级处分；造成严重后果的，给予撤职或者开除处分，其主要负责人应当引咎辞职：  
（一）不符合行政许可条件准予行政许可；  
（二）依法应当作出责令停业、关闭等决定而未作出；  
（三）发现违法行为或者接到举报不依法查处；  
（四）有其他玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊行为。

**Article 108** Where the relevant departments of the State Council, the local people's governments at or above the county level in the Yellow River Basin and their relevant departments, the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities, or the Yellow River Basin ecological environment supervision and management authority violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given a warning, a demerit, a major demerit or demotion according to law; if serious consequences are caused, they shall be given removal from office or expulsion, and the principal leading person shall resign by taking the blame:  
(1) granting an administrative license where the conditions for the administrative license are not met;  
(2) failing to make a decision such as ordering suspension of business or closure as legally required;  
(3) failing to investigate and deal with illegal acts discovered or reported in accordance with law;  
(4) engaging in other acts of dereliction of duty, abuse of power, or engaging in malpractice for personal gain.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由地方人民政府生态环境、自然资源等主管部门按照职责分工，责令停止违法行为，限期拆除或者恢复原状，处五十万元以上五百万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五万元以上十万元以下罚款；逾期不拆除或者不恢复原状的，强制拆除或者代为恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担；情节严重的，报经有批准权的人民政府批准，责令关闭：  
（一）在黄河干支流岸线管控范围内新建、扩建化工园区或者化工项目；  
（二）在黄河干流岸线或者重要支流岸线的管控范围内新建、改建、扩建尾矿库；  
（三）违反生态环境准入清单规定进行生产建设活动。

**Article 109** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent departments of the local people's governments, such as ecology and environment and natural resources, shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, order the cessation of the illegal act, order removal within a time limit or restoration to the original condition, impose a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan on the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons; if the removal or restoration to the original condition is not completed within the time limit, compulsory removal or restoration to the original condition shall be carried out, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator; if the circumstances are serious, the matter shall be reported to the people's government with approval authority for approval, and closure shall be ordered:  
(1) constructing or expanding a chemical industrial park or chemical project within the control range of the shoreline of the main stream or tributaries of the Yellow River;  
(2) constructing, rebuilding or expanding a tailings pond within the control range of the shoreline of the main stream of the Yellow River or important tributaries;  
(3) carrying out production and construction activities in violation of the ecological environment access list.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　违反本法规定，在黄河流域禁止开垦坡度以上陡坡地开垦种植农作物的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令停止违法行为，采取退耕、恢复植被等补救措施；按照开垦面积，可以对单位处每平方米一百元以下罚款、对个人处每平方米二十元以下罚款。  
违反本法规定，在黄河流域损坏、擅自占用淤地坝的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令停止违法行为，限期治理或者采取补救措施，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；逾期不治理或者不采取补救措施的，代为治理或者采取补救措施，所需费用由违法者承担。  
违反本法规定，在黄河流域从事生产建设活动造成水土流失未进行治理，或者治理不符合国家规定的相关标准的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令限期治理，对单位处二万元以上二十万元以下罚款，对个人可以处二万元以下罚款；逾期不治理的，代为治理，所需费用由违法者承担。

**Article 110** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, crops are cultivated on steep slopes with a gradient above the prohibited reclamation gradient in the Yellow River Basin, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order the cessation of the illegal act and require remedial measures such as returning farmland and restoring vegetation; according to the reclaimed area, a fine of not more than 100 yuan per square meter may be imposed on a unit, and a fine of not more than 20 yuan per square meter may be imposed on an individual.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a silt dam is damaged or occupied without authorization in the Yellow River Basin, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order the cessation of the illegal act, require treatment within a time limit or the taking of remedial measures, and impose a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if treatment or remedial measures are not taken within the time limit, the treatment or remedial measures shall be carried out on behalf of the violator, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, production and construction activities in the Yellow River Basin cause soil erosion without treatment, or the treatment does not meet the relevant standards prescribed by the State, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order treatment within a time limit, impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan on a unit, and may impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan on an individual; if treatment is not carried out within the time limit, treatment shall be carried out on behalf of the violator, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　违反本法规定，黄河干流、重要支流水工程未将生态用水调度纳入日常运行调度规程的，由有关主管部门按照职责分工，责令改正，给予警告，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款。

**Article 111** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the ecological water dispatch is not incorporated into the daily operation dispatch procedures for water projects on the main stream or important tributaries of the Yellow River, the relevant competent department shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order correction, give a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　违反本法规定，禁渔期内在黄河流域重点水域从事天然渔业资源生产性捕捞的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门没收渔获物、违法所得以及用于违法活动的渔船、渔具和其他工具，并处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；采用电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等方式捕捞，或者有其他严重情节的，并处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款。  
违反本法规定，在黄河流域开放水域养殖、投放外来物种或者其他非本地物种种质资源的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期捕回，处十万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款；逾期不捕回的，代为捕回或者采取降低负面影响的措施，所需费用由违法者承担。  
违反本法规定，在三门峡、小浪底、故县、陆浑、河口村水库库区采用网箱、围网或者拦河拉网方式养殖，妨碍水沙调控和防洪的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，拆除网箱、围网或者拦河拉网，处十万元以下罚款；造成严重后果的，处十万元以上一百万元以下罚款。

**Article 112** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, productive fishing of natural fishery resources is carried out in key waters of the Yellow River Basin during the closed fishing season, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the catch, illegal gains, and the fishing vessels, fishing gear and other tools used for the illegal activities, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if fishing is carried out by means such as electric fishing, poison fishing, or explosive fishing, or if there are other serious circumstances, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, alien species or other non-local species germplasm resources are cultivated or released into open waters of the Yellow River Basin, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the recovery of such species by catching within a time limit, and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the recovery is not completed within the time limit, the species shall be recovered on behalf of the violator or measures to reduce negative impacts shall be taken, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, aquaculture is carried out by means of cages, pen enclosures or barrier nets in the reservoir areas of Sanmenxia, Xiaolangdi, Guxian, Luhun, and Hekoucun, obstructing water-sediment regulation and flood control, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order the cessation of the illegal act, dismantle the cages, pen enclosures or barrier nets, and impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if serious consequences are caused, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　违反本法规定，未经批准擅自取水，或者未依照批准的取水许可规定条件取水的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令停止违法行为，限期采取补救措施，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 113** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, water is taken without approval, or water is taken not in accordance with the conditions specified in the approved water-taking permit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order the cessation of the illegal act, require remedial measures within a time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the water-taking permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　违反本法规定，黄河流域以及黄河流经省、自治区其他黄河供水区相关县级行政区域的用水单位用水超过强制性用水定额，未按照规定期限实施节水技术改造的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令限期整改，可以处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 114** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a water user in the Yellow River Basin or in other relevant county-level administrative areas of the Yellow River water supply areas of the provinces and autonomous regions through which the Yellow River flows exceeds the mandatory water use quota and fails to implement water-saving technology transformation within the prescribed time limit, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order rectification within a time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the water-taking permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　违反本法规定，黄河流域以及黄河流经省、自治区其他黄河供水区相关县级行政区域取水量达到取水规模以上的单位未安装在线计量设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令限期安装，并按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量计征相关费用，处二万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，吊销取水许可证。  
违反本法规定，在线计量设施不合格或者运行不正常的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令限期更换或者修复；逾期不更换或者不修复的，按照日最大取水能力计算的取水量计征相关费用，处五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 115** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a unit whose water intake reaches or exceeds the water intake scale in the Yellow River Basin or in other relevant county-level administrative areas of the Yellow River water supply areas of the provinces and autonomous regions through which the Yellow River flows fails to install online metering facilities, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order installation within a time limit, levy relevant fees based on the water intake calculated according to the maximum daily water intake capacity, and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the water-taking permit shall be revoked.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the online metering facilities are substandard or operate abnormally, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order replacement or repair within a time limit; if replacement or repair is not completed within the time limit, relevant fees shall be levied based on the water intake calculated according to the maximum daily water intake capacity, and a fine of not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the water-taking permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　违反本法规定，黄河流域农业灌溉取用深层地下水的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令限期整改，可以处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款，吊销取水许可证。

**Article 116** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, deep groundwater is taken for agricultural irrigation in the Yellow River Basin, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order rectification within a time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the water-taking permit shall be revoked.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　违反本法规定，黄河流域水库管理单位不执行黄河流域管理机构的水沙调度指令的，由黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令改正，给予警告，并处二万元以上十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，并处十万元以上五十万元以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 117** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a reservoir management unit in the Yellow River Basin fails to implement the water and sediment dispatch instructions of the Yellow River Basin management authority, the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order correction, give a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府水行政主管部门或者黄河流域管理机构及其所属管理机构责令停止违法行为，限期拆除违法建筑物、构筑物或者恢复原状，处五万元以上五十万元以下罚款；逾期不拆除或者不恢复原状的，强制拆除或者代为恢复原状，所需费用由违法者承担：  
（一）在河道、湖泊管理范围内建设妨碍行洪的建筑物、构筑物或者从事影响河势稳定、危害河岸堤防安全和其他妨碍河道行洪的活动；  
（二）违法利用、占用黄河流域河道、湖泊水域和岸线；  
（三）建设跨河、穿河、穿堤、临河的工程设施，降低行洪和调蓄能力或者缩小水域面积，未建设等效替代工程或者采取其他功能补救措施；  
（四）侵占黄河备用入海流路。

**Article 118** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the water administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level or the Yellow River Basin management authority and its subordinate management authorities shall order the cessation of the illegal act, order removal of the illegal buildings or structures within a time limit or restoration to the original condition, and impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if removal or restoration to the original condition is not completed within the time limit, compulsory removal or restoration to the original condition shall be carried out, and the costs incurred shall be borne by the violator:  
(1) constructing buildings or structures that obstruct flood discharge within the management scope of rivers or lakes, or engaging in activities that affect the stability of river regimes, endanger the safety of riverbanks and levees, or otherwise obstruct flood discharge in rivers;  
(2) illegally using or occupying the waters and shorelines of rivers and lakes in the Yellow River Basin;  
(3) constructing engineering facilities crossing, passing through, passing through levees, or adjacent to rivers, reducing flood discharge and regulation and storage capacity, or narrowing water area, without constructing equivalent replacement projects or taking other functional remediation measures;  
(4) encroaching upon the reserve seaward flow path of the Yellow River.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　违反本法规定，在黄河流域破坏自然资源和生态、污染环境、妨碍防洪安全、破坏文化遗产等造成他人损害的，侵权人应当依法承担侵权责任。  
违反本法规定，造成黄河流域生态环境损害的，国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织有权请求侵权人承担修复责任、赔偿损失和相关费用。

**Article 119** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, damage is caused to others by destroying natural resources and ecology, polluting the environment, obstructing flood control safety, damaging cultural heritage, or otherwise in the Yellow River Basin, the tortfeasor shall bear tort liability according to law.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, damage is caused to the ecological environment of the Yellow River Basin, the authorities prescribed by the State or the organizations prescribed by law shall have the right to request the tortfeasor to bear the liability for restoration, compensation for losses, and related expenses.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 120** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十一章　附则

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）黄河干流，是指黄河源头至黄河河口，流经青海省、四川省、甘肃省、宁夏回族自治区、内蒙古自治区、山西省、陕西省、河南省、山东省的黄河主河段（含入海流路）；  
（二）黄河支流，是指直接或者间接流入黄河干流的河流，支流可以分为一级支流、二级支流等；  
（三）黄河重要支流，是指湟水、洮河、祖厉河、清水河、大黑河、皇甫川、窟野河、无定河、汾河、渭河、伊洛河、沁河、大汶河等一级支流；  
（四）黄河滩区，是指黄河流域河道管理范围内具有行洪、滞洪、沉沙功能，由于历史原因形成的有群众居住、耕种的滩地。

**Article 121** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms shall have the meanings as defined below:  
(1) "Main stream of the Yellow River" means the main river section of the Yellow River from its source to its estuary, flowing through Qinghai Province, Sichuan Province, Gansu Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Shanxi Province, Shaanxi Province, Henan Province, and Shandong Province (including the seaward flow path);  
(2) "Tributary of the Yellow River" means a river that flows directly or indirectly into the main stream of the Yellow River, and tributaries may be classified as primary tributaries, secondary tributaries, etc.;  
(3) "Important tributaries of the Yellow River" means the primary tributaries such as the Huangshui River, Tao River, Zuli River, Qingshui River, Dahei River, Huangfuchuan River, Kuye River, Wuding River, Fen River, Wei River, Yiluo River, Qin River, and Dawen River;  
(4) "Yellow River floodplain area" means the floodplain area within the river course management scope of the Yellow River Basin that has functions of flood discharge, flood detention, and sediment deposition, and where there are historically formed settlements and cultivated lands inhabited and farmed by the masses.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　本法自2023年4月1日起施行。

**Article 122** This Law shall come into force on April 1, 2023.
