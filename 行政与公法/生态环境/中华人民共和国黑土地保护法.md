# 中华人民共和国黑土地保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-06-24  
**生效日期**：2022-08-01

> （2022年6月24日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十五次会议通过） 第一条　为了保护黑土地资源，稳步恢复提升黑土地基础地力，促进资源可持续利用，维护生态平衡，保障国家粮食安全，制定本法。 第二条　从事黑土地保护、利用和相关治理、修复等活动，适用本法。本法没有规定的，适用土地管理等有关法律的规定。 本法所称黑土地，是指黑龙江省、吉林省、辽宁省、内蒙古自治区（以下简称四省区）的相关区域范围内具有黑色或者暗黑色腐殖质表土层，性状好、肥力高的耕地。 第三条　国家实行科学、有效的黑土地保护政策，保障黑土地保护财政投入，综合采取工程、农艺、农机、生物等措施，保护黑土地的优良生产能力，确保黑土地总量不减少、功能不退化、质量有提升、产能可持续。 第四条　黑土地保护应当坚持统筹规划、因地制宜、用养结合、近期目标与远期目标结合、突出重点、综合施策的原则，建立健全政府主导、农业生产经营者实施、社会参与的保护机制。 国务院农业农村主管部门会同自然资源、水行政等有关部门，综合考虑黑土地开垦历史和利用现状，以及黑土层厚度、土壤性状、土壤类型等，按照最有利于全面保护、综合治理和系统修复的原则，科学合理确定黑土地保护范围并适时调整，有计划、分步骤、分类别地推进黑土地保护工作。历史上属黑土地的，除确无法修复的外，原则上都应列入黑土地保护范围进行修恢复。 第五条　黑土地应当用于粮食和油料作物、糖料作物、蔬菜等农产品生产。 黑土层深厚、土壤性状良好的黑土地应当按照规定的标准划入永久基本农田，重点用于粮食生产，实行严格保护，确保数量和质量长期稳定。 第六条　国务院和四省区人民政府加强对黑土地保护工作的领导、组织、协调、监督管理，统筹制定黑土地保护政策。四省区人民政府对本行政区域内的黑土地数量、质量、生态环境负责。 县级以上地方人民政府应当建立农业农村、自然资源、水行政、发展改革、财政、生态环境等有关部门组成的黑土地保护协调机制，加强协调指导，明确工作责任，推动黑土地保护工作落实。 乡镇人民政府应当协助组织实施黑土地保护工作，向农业生产经营者推广适宜其所经营耕地的保护、治理、修复和利用措施，督促农业生产经营者履行黑土地保护义务。 第七条　各级人民政府应当加强黑土地保护宣传教育，提高全社会的黑土地保护意识。 对在黑土地保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。 第八条　国务院标准化主管部门和农业农村、自然资源、水行政等主管部门按照职责分工，制定和完善黑土地质量和其他保护标准。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护黑土地资源，稳步恢复提升黑土地基础地力，促进资源可持续利用，维护生态平衡，保障国家粮食安全，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of protecting black soil resources, steadily restoring and enhancing the basic fertility of black soil, promoting sustainable utilization of resources, maintaining ecological balance, and ensuring national food security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事黑土地保护、利用和相关治理、修复等活动，适用本法。本法没有规定的，适用土地管理等有关法律的规定。  
本法所称黑土地，是指黑龙江省、吉林省、辽宁省、内蒙古自治区（以下简称四省区）的相关区域范围内具有黑色或者暗黑色腐殖质表土层，性状好、肥力高的耕地。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/妨害社会管理秩序/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理破坏黑土地资源刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理破坏黑土地资源刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities such as the protection, utilization, and related treatment and restoration of black soil. Matters not provided for in this Law shall be governed by the provisions of relevant laws such as the Land Administration Law.  
For the purposes of this Law, "black soil" means cultivated land with a black or dark black humus topsoil layer, good properties and high fertility, within the relevant areas of Heilongjiang Province, Jilin Province, Liaoning Province, and the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region (hereinafter referred to as the four provinces/regions).

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家实行科学、有效的黑土地保护政策，保障黑土地保护财政投入，综合采取工程、农艺、农机、生物等措施，保护黑土地的优良生产能力，确保黑土地总量不减少、功能不退化、质量有提升、产能可持续。

**Article 3** The State shall implement scientific and effective policies for the protection of black soil, ensure financial investment for black soil protection, and comprehensively adopt engineering, agronomic, agricultural machinery, biological and other measures to protect the excellent production capacity of black soil, and ensure that the total quantity of black soil does not decrease, its functions do not degrade, its quality improves, and its productivity is sustainable.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　黑土地保护应当坚持统筹规划、因地制宜、用养结合、近期目标与远期目标结合、突出重点、综合施策的原则，建立健全政府主导、农业生产经营者实施、社会参与的保护机制。  
国务院农业农村主管部门会同自然资源、水行政等有关部门，综合考虑黑土地开垦历史和利用现状，以及黑土层厚度、土壤性状、土壤类型等，按照最有利于全面保护、综合治理和系统修复的原则，科学合理确定黑土地保护范围并适时调整，有计划、分步骤、分类别地推进黑土地保护工作。历史上属黑土地的，除确无法修复的外，原则上都应列入黑土地保护范围进行修恢复。

**Article 4** The protection of black soil shall adhere to the principles of overall planning, adaptation to local conditions, combination of utilization and nurturing, integration of short-term and long-term goals, highlighting priorities, and comprehensive implementation of measures, and shall establish and improve a protection mechanism featuring government leadership, implementation by agricultural producers and operators, and social participation.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of natural resources, water administration and other relevant departments, comprehensively consider the history of reclamation and current utilization of black soil, as well as the thickness of the black soil layer, soil properties, soil types, etc., scientifically and reasonably determine the scope of black soil protection and make timely adjustments in accordance with the principle of being most conducive to comprehensive protection, integrated treatment and systematic restoration, and promote black soil protection work in a planned, step-by-step and categorized manner. Land that was historically black soil shall, except where restoration is truly impossible, in principle be included in the black soil protection scope for restoration.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　黑土地应当用于粮食和油料作物、糖料作物、蔬菜等农产品生产。  
黑土层深厚、土壤性状良好的黑土地应当按照规定的标准划入永久基本农田，重点用于粮食生产，实行严格保护，确保数量和质量长期稳定。

**Article 5** Black soil shall be used for the production of agricultural products such as grain, oil crops, sugar crops, and vegetables.  
Black soil with a deep black soil layer and good soil properties shall, in accordance with the prescribed standards, be designated as permanent basic farmland, mainly used for grain production, and subject to strict protection to ensure long-term stability in quantity and quality.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院和四省区人民政府加强对黑土地保护工作的领导、组织、协调、监督管理，统筹制定黑土地保护政策。四省区人民政府对本行政区域内的黑土地数量、质量、生态环境负责。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当建立农业农村、自然资源、水行政、发展改革、财政、生态环境等有关部门组成的黑土地保护协调机制，加强协调指导，明确工作责任，推动黑土地保护工作落实。  
乡镇人民政府应当协助组织实施黑土地保护工作，向农业生产经营者推广适宜其所经营耕地的保护、治理、修复和利用措施，督促农业生产经营者履行黑土地保护义务。

**Article 6** The State Council and the people's governments of the four provinces/regions shall strengthen the leadership, organization, coordination, supervision and management of black soil protection work, and coordinate the formulation of black soil protection policies. The people's governments of the four provinces/regions shall be responsible for the quantity, quality and ecological environment of black soil within their respective administrative regions.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism for black soil protection composed of the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, water administration, development and reform, finance, ecological environment, and other relevant departments, strengthen coordination and guidance, clarify work responsibilities, and promote the implementation of black soil protection work.  
Township people's governments shall assist in organizing the implementation of black soil protection work, promote to agricultural producers and operators the measures suitable for the protection, treatment, restoration and utilization of the cultivated land they operate, and urge agricultural producers and operators to fulfill their obligations for black soil protection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　各级人民政府应当加强黑土地保护宣传教育，提高全社会的黑土地保护意识。  
对在黑土地保护工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 7** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen publicity and education on black soil protection, and raise the awareness of black soil protection throughout society.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to black soil protection shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院标准化主管部门和农业农村、自然资源、水行政等主管部门按照职责分工，制定和完善黑土地质量和其他保护标准。

**Article 8** The competent department of standardization of the State Council and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, water administration, etc. shall, in accordance with their respective functions and duties, formulate and improve the quality and other protection standards for black soil.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家建立健全黑土地调查和监测制度。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同有关部门开展土地调查时，同步开展黑土地类型、分布、数量、质量、保护和利用状况等情况的调查，建立黑土地档案。  
国务院农业农村、水行政等主管部门会同四省区人民政府建立健全黑土地质量监测网络，加强对黑土地土壤性状、黑土层厚度、水蚀、风蚀等情况的常态化监测，建立黑土地质量动态变化数据库，并做好信息共享工作。

**Article 9** The State shall establish and improve the investigation and monitoring system for black soil.  
When the competent department of natural resources of a people's government at or above the county level, together with relevant departments, conducts land surveys, it shall simultaneously carry out investigations into the types, distribution, quantity, quality, protection and utilization status of black soil, and establish black soil archives.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, water administration, etc. of the State Council shall, together with the people's governments of the four provinces/regions, establish and improve a black soil quality monitoring network, strengthen routine monitoring of black soil properties, black soil layer thickness, water erosion, wind erosion, etc., establish a dynamic change database of black soil quality, and ensure information sharing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府应当将黑土地保护工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。  
国土空间规划应当充分考虑保护黑土地及其周边生态环境，合理布局各类用途土地，以利于黑土地水蚀、风蚀等的预防和治理。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门会同有关部门以调查和监测为基础、体现整体集中连片治理，编制黑土地保护规划，明确保护范围、目标任务、技术模式、保障措施等，遏制黑土地退化趋势，提升黑土地质量，改善黑土地生态环境。县级黑土地保护规划应当与国土空间规划相衔接，落实到黑土地具体地块，并向社会公布。

**Article 10** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate black soil protection work into national economic and social development plans.  
Territorial spatial plans shall fully consider the protection of black soil and its surrounding ecological environment, rationally allocate land for various purposes, so as to facilitate the prevention and control of water erosion, wind erosion, etc. of black soil.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of a people's government at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, based on investigation and monitoring and reflecting overall concentrated contiguous treatment, formulate black soil protection plans, specify the scope of protection, goals and tasks, technical models, safeguard measures, etc., curb the degradation trend of black soil, improve the quality of black soil, and improve the ecological environment of black soil. County-level black soil protection plans shall be aligned with territorial spatial plans, be implemented on specific plots of black soil, and be made public.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家采取措施加强黑土地保护的科技支撑能力建设，将黑土地保护、治理、修复和利用的科技创新作为重点支持领域；鼓励高等学校、科研机构和农业技术推广机构等协同开展科技攻关。县级以上人民政府应当鼓励和支持水土保持、防风固沙、土壤改良、地力培肥、生态保护等科学研究和科研成果推广应用。  
有关耕地质量监测保护和农业技术推广机构应当对农业生产经营者保护黑土地进行技术培训、提供指导服务。  
国家鼓励企业、高等学校、职业学校、科研机构、科学技术社会团体、农民专业合作社、农业社会化服务组织、农业科技人员等开展黑土地保护相关技术服务。  
国家支持开展黑土地保护国际合作与交流。

**Article 11** The State shall take measures to strengthen the construction of scientific and technological support capabilities for black soil protection, and designate scientific and technological innovation in black soil protection, treatment, restoration and utilization as a key support area; encourage higher education institutions, scientific research institutions and agricultural technology extension agencies to jointly carry out scientific and technological research. People's governments at or above the county level shall encourage and support scientific research and the promotion and application of scientific research results in soil and water conservation, windbreak and sand fixation, soil improvement, fertility cultivation, ecological protection, etc.  
Relevant agencies for cultivated land quality monitoring and protection and agricultural technology extension shall provide technical training and guidance services to agricultural producers and operators for the protection of black soil.  
The State shall encourage enterprises, higher education institutions, vocational schools, scientific research institutions, scientific and technological societies, specialized farmers' cooperatives, agricultural社会化 service organizations, agricultural science and technology personnel, etc. to carry out technical services related to black soil protection.  
The State supports international cooperation and exchanges on black soil protection.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府应当采取以下措施加强黑土地农田基础设施建设：  
（一）加强农田水利工程建设，完善水田、旱地灌排体系；  
（二）加强田块整治，修复沟毁耕地，合理划分适宜耕作田块；  
（三）加强坡耕地、侵蚀沟水土保持工程建设；  
（四）合理规划修建机耕路、生产路；  
（五）建设农田防护林网；  
（六）其他黑土地保护措施。

**Article 12** People's governments at or above the county level shall take the following measures to strengthen the construction of farmland infrastructure for black soil:  
(1) Strengthen the construction of farmland water conservancy projects, and improve the irrigation and drainage systems for paddy fields and dry land;  
(2) Strengthen plot consolidation, restore ditches and damaged cultivated land, and reasonably divide suitable farming plots;  
(3) Strengthen the construction of soil and water conservation projects for sloping farmland and erosion gullies;  
(4) Rationally plan and build tractor roads and production roads;  
(5) Build farmland shelterbelt networks;  
(6) Other measures for black soil protection.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级以上人民政府应当推广科学的耕作制度，采取以下措施提高黑土地质量：  
（一）因地制宜实行轮作等用地养地相结合的种植制度，按照国家有关规定推广适度休耕；  
（二）因地制宜推广免（少）耕、深松等保护性耕作技术，推广适宜的农业机械；  
（三）因地制宜推广秸秆覆盖、粉碎深（翻）埋、过腹转化等还田方式；  
（四）组织实施测土配方施肥，科学减少化肥施用量，鼓励增施有机肥料，推广土壤生物改良等技术；  
（五）推广生物技术或者生物制剂防治病虫害等绿色防控技术，科学减少化学农药、除草剂使用量，合理使用农用薄膜等农业生产资料；  
（六）其他黑土地质量提升措施。

**Article 13** People's governments at or above the county level shall promote scientific farming systems and take the following measures to improve the quality of black soil:  
(1) Implement planting systems combining land use and nurturing, such as crop rotation, according to local conditions, and promote moderate fallowing in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State;  
(2) Promote conservation tillage techniques such as no-tillage (minimum tillage) and subsoiling according to local conditions, and promote suitable agricultural machinery;  
(3) Promote methods of returning straw to the field, such as straw mulching, chopping and deep burial (deep plowing), and conversion through livestock, according to local conditions;  
(4) Organize and implement soil testing and formula fertilization, scientifically reduce the amount of chemical fertilizer application, encourage the increased application of organic fertilizers, and promote technologies such as soil biological improvement;  
(5) Promote green prevention and control technologies such as biological techniques or biological agents for pest and disease control, scientifically reduce the use of chemical pesticides and herbicides, and rationally use agricultural production materials such as agricultural films;  
(6) Other measures to improve the quality of black soil.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家鼓励采取综合性措施，预防和治理水土流失，防止黑土地土壤侵蚀、土地沙化和盐渍化，改善和修复农田生态环境。  
县级以上人民政府应当开展侵蚀沟治理，实施沟头沟坡沟底加固防护，因地制宜组织在侵蚀沟的沟坡和沟岸、黑土地周边河流两岸、湖泊和水库周边等区域营造植物保护带或者采取其他措施，防止侵蚀沟变宽变深变长。  
县级以上人民政府应当按照因害设防、合理管护、科学布局的原则，制定农田防护林建设计划，组织沿农田道路、沟渠等种植农田防护林，防止违背自然规律造林绿化。农田防护林只能进行抚育、更新性质的采伐，确保防护林功能不减退。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织开展防沙治沙，加强黑土地周边的沙漠和沙化土地治理，防止黑土地沙化。

**Article 14** The State encourages the adoption of comprehensive measures to prevent and control soil erosion, prevent soil erosion, desertification and salinization of black soil, and improve and restore the ecological environment of farmland.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall carry out erosion gully treatment, implement reinforcement and protection of gully heads, slopes and bottoms, and organize, according to local conditions, the creation of plant protection belts or other measures in areas such as the slopes and banks of erosion gullies, the banks of rivers around black soil, and around lakes and reservoirs, to prevent erosion gullies from becoming wider, deeper and longer.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the principles of establishing defense according to hazards, rational management and protection, and scientific layout, formulate plans for the construction of farmland shelterbelts, organize the planting of farmland shelterbelts along farmland roads, ditches, etc., and prevent afforestation in violation of natural laws. Farmland shelterbelts may only be felled for tending or regeneration purposes to ensure that the function of shelterbelts does not diminish.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out sand prevention and control, strengthen the treatment of deserts and desertified land around black soil, and prevent desertification of black soil.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县级以上人民政府应当加强黑土地生态保护和黑土地周边林地、草原、湿地的保护修复，推动荒山荒坡治理，提升自然生态系统涵养水源、保持水土、防风固沙、维护生物多样性等生态功能，维持有利于黑土地保护的自然生态环境。

**Article 15** People's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the ecological protection of black soil and the protection and restoration of forest land, grassland, and wetlands around black soil, promote the treatment of barren hills and slopes, enhance the ecological functions of natural ecosystems such as water conservation, soil and water conservation, windbreak and sand fixation, and maintenance of biodiversity, and maintain a natural ecological environment conducive to the protection of black soil.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　县级人民政府应当依据黑土地调查和监测数据，并结合土壤类型和质量等级、气候特点、环境状况等实际情况，对本行政区域内的黑土地进行科学分区，制定并组织实施黑土地质量提升计划，因地制宜合理采取保护、治理、修复和利用的精细化措施。

**Article 16** People's governments at the county level shall, based on black soil survey and monitoring data, and taking into account actual conditions such as soil type and quality grade, climatic characteristics, and environmental conditions, scientifically divide the black soil within their administrative regions into zones, formulate and organize the implementation of black soil quality improvement plans, and adopt refined measures for protection, treatment, restoration and utilization according to local conditions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国有农场应当对其经营管理范围内的黑土地加强保护，充分发挥示范作用，并依法接受监督检查。  
农村集体经济组织、村民委员会和村民小组应当依法发包农村土地，监督承包方依照承包合同约定的用途合理利用和保护黑土地，制止承包方损害黑土地等行为。  
农村集体经济组织、农业企业、农民专业合作社、农户等应当十分珍惜和合理利用黑土地，加强农田基础设施建设，因地制宜应用保护性耕作等技术，积极采取提升黑土地质量和改善农田生态环境的养护措施，依法保护黑土地。

**Article 17** State-owned farms shall strengthen the protection of black soil within their management scope, give full play to their demonstration role, and accept supervision and inspection according to law.  
Rural collective economic organizations, villagers' committees and villagers' groups shall contract out rural land according to law, supervise the contractors to rationally use and protect black soil in accordance with the purposes stipulated in the contract, and stop acts such as damage to black soil by the contractors.  
Rural collective economic organizations, agricultural enterprises, specialized farmers' cooperatives, farmers, etc. shall cherish and rationally use black soil, strengthen the construction of farmland infrastructure, apply conservation tillage and other techniques according to local conditions, actively take maintenance measures to improve the quality of black soil and improve the ecological environment of farmland, and protect black soil according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农业投入品生产者、经营者和使用者应当依法对农药、肥料、农用薄膜等农业投入品的包装物、废弃物进行回收以及资源化利用或者无害化处理，不得随意丢弃，防止黑土地污染。  
县级人民政府应当采取措施，支持农药、肥料、农用薄膜等农业投入品包装物、废弃物的回收以及资源化利用或者无害化处理。

**Article 18** Producers, operators and users of agricultural inputs shall, according to law, recycle the packaging and waste of agricultural inputs such as pesticides, fertilizers, and agricultural films, and carry out resource utilization or harmless treatment, and shall not discard them at will, to prevent pollution of black soil.  
People's governments at the county level shall take measures to support the recycling and resource utilization or harmless treatment of packaging and waste of agricultural inputs such as pesticides, fertilizers, and agricultural films.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事畜禽养殖的单位和个人，应当科学开展畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用，以畜禽粪污就地就近还田利用为重点，促进黑土地绿色种养循环农业发展。  
县级以上人民政府应当支持开展畜禽粪污无害化处理和资源化利用。

**Article 19** Units and individuals engaged in livestock and poultry breeding shall scientifically carry out harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure, focusing on the use of livestock and poultry manure returned to the field locally or nearby, to promote the development of green planting and breeding circular agriculture for black soil.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall support the harmless treatment and resource utilization of livestock and poultry manure.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　任何组织和个人不得破坏黑土地资源和生态环境。禁止盗挖、滥挖和非法买卖黑土。国务院自然资源主管部门会同农业农村、水行政、公安、交通运输、市场监督管理等部门应当建立健全保护黑土地资源监督管理制度，提高对盗挖、滥挖、非法买卖黑土和其他破坏黑土地资源、生态环境行为的综合治理能力。

**Article 20** No organization or individual may destroy black soil resources or the ecological environment. It is prohibited to illegally excavate, excessively excavate, or illegally trade black soil. The competent department of natural resources of the State Council shall, together with the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, water administration, public security, transport, market supervision and administration, etc., establish and improve the supervision and management system for the protection of black soil resources, and enhance the comprehensive governance capacity against illegal excavation, excessive excavation, illegal trading of black soil, and other acts that damage black soil resources and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　建设项目不得占用黑土地；确需占用的，应当依法严格审批，并补充数量和质量相当的耕地。  
建设项目占用黑土地的，应当按照规定的标准对耕作层的土壤进行剥离。剥离的黑土应当就近用于新开垦耕地和劣质耕地改良、被污染耕地的治理、高标准农田建设、土地复垦等。建设项目主体应当制定剥离黑土的再利用方案，报自然资源主管部门备案。具体办法由四省区人民政府分别制定。

**Article 21** Construction projects may not occupy black soil; if occupation is truly necessary, it shall be subject to strict approval according to law, and cultivated land of equivalent quantity and quality shall be supplemented.  
Where a construction project occupies black soil, the soil of the plow layer shall be stripped in accordance with the prescribed standards. The stripped black soil shall be used locally for newly reclaimed cultivated land and the improvement of low-quality cultivated land, treatment of contaminated cultivated land, construction of high-standard farmland, land reclamation, etc. The construction project entity shall formulate a plan for the reuse of the stripped black soil and report it to the competent department of natural resources for recordation. Specific measures shall be formulated by the people's governments of the four provinces/regions respectively.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家建立健全黑土地保护财政投入保障制度。县级以上人民政府应当将黑土地保护资金纳入本级预算。  
国家加大对黑土地保护措施奖补资金的倾斜力度，建立长期稳定的奖励补助机制。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将黑土地保护作为土地使用权出让收入用于农业农村投入的重点领域，并加大投入力度。  
国家组织开展高标准农田、农田水利、水土保持、防沙治沙、农田防护林、土地复垦等建设活动，在项目资金安排上积极支持黑土地保护需要。县级人民政府可以按照国家有关规定统筹使用涉农资金用于黑土地保护，提高财政资金使用效益。

**Article 22** The State shall establish and improve the financial input guarantee system for black soil protection. People's governments at or above the county level shall include black soil protection funds in their budgets.  
The State shall increase the allocation of awards and subsidies for black soil protection measures, and establish a long-term and stable reward and subsidy mechanism.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall take black soil protection as a key area for the use of land use right transfer income for agricultural and rural input, and increase input efforts.  
The State shall organize the construction of high-standard farmland, farmland water conservancy, soil and water conservation, sand prevention and control, farmland shelterbelts, land reclamation, etc., and actively support the needs of black soil protection in the arrangement of project funds. People's governments at the county level may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, use agricultural-related funds in a coordinated manner for black soil protection to improve the efficiency of financial funds.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家实行用养结合、保护效果导向的激励政策，对采取黑土地保护和治理修复措施的农业生产经营者按照国家有关规定给予奖励补助。

**Article 23** The State shall implement incentive policies combining utilization and nurturing and oriented towards protection effects, and shall give rewards and subsidies to agricultural producers and operators who adopt measures for black soil protection, treatment and restoration in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励粮食主销区通过资金支持、与四省区建立稳定粮食购销关系等经济合作方式参与黑土地保护，建立健全黑土地跨区域投入保护机制。

**Article 24** The State encourages major grain marketing areas to participate in black soil protection through economic cooperation methods such as financial support and the establishment of stable grain purchase and sale relations with the four provinces/regions, and to establish and improve a cross-regional investment protection mechanism for black soil.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家按照政策支持、社会参与、市场化运作的原则，鼓励社会资本投入黑土地保护活动，并保护投资者的合法权益。  
国家鼓励保险机构开展黑土地保护相关保险业务。  
国家支持农民专业合作社、企业等以多种方式与农户建立利益联结机制和社会化服务机制，发展适度规模经营，推动农产品品质提升、品牌打造和标准化生产，提高黑土地产出效益。

**Article 25** The State, in accordance with the principles of policy support, social participation and market-oriented operation, encourages social capital to invest in black soil protection activities, and protects the legitimate rights and interests of investors.  
The State encourages insurance institutions to carry out insurance business related to black soil protection.  
The State supports specialized farmers' cooperatives, enterprises, etc. to establish interest linkage mechanisms and socialized service mechanisms with farmers in various ways, develop moderate-scale operations, promote the improvement of agricultural product quality, brand building and standardized production, and increase the output efficiency of black soil.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院对四省区人民政府黑土地保护责任落实情况进行考核，将黑土地保护情况纳入耕地保护责任目标。

**Article 26** The State Council shall assess the implementation of black soil protection responsibilities by the people's governments of the four provinces/regions, and incorporate the status of black soil protection into the responsibility targets for cultivated land protection.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府自然资源、农业农村、水行政等有关部门按照职责，依法对黑土地保护和质量建设情况联合开展监督检查。

**Article 27** The competent departments of natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, water administration, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their functions and duties, jointly conduct supervision and inspection on black soil protection and quality construction according to law.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　县级以上人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告黑土地保护情况，依法接受监督。

**Article 28** People's governments at or above the county level shall report on the status of black soil protection to the people's congresses at the same level or their standing committees, and accept supervision according to law.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　违反本法规定，国务院农业农村、自然资源等有关部门、县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）截留、挪用或者未按照规定使用黑土地保护资金；  
（二）对破坏黑土地的行为，发现或者接到举报未及时查处；  
（三）其他不依法履行黑土地保护职责导致黑土地资源和生态环境遭受破坏的行为。

**Article 29** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the relevant departments of the State Council such as agriculture and rural affairs and natural resources, local people's governments at or above the county level or their relevant departments commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given a warning, a demerit or a major demerit; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given a demotion or dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given expulsion:  
(1) Intercepting, misappropriating, or failing to use black soil protection funds in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) Failing to investigate and deal with acts of damaging black soil in a timely manner upon discovery or upon receiving a report;  
(3) Other acts of failing to perform black soil protection duties according to law, resulting in damage to black soil resources and the ecological environment.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　非法占用或者损毁黑土地农田基础设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、水行政等部门责令停止违法行为，限期恢复原状，处恢复费用一倍以上三倍以下罚款。

**Article 30** Where anyone illegally occupies or damages farmland infrastructure of black soil, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, water administration, etc. of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, restore the original condition within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the restoration cost.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　违法将黑土地用于非农建设的，依照土地管理等有关法律法规的规定从重处罚。  
违反法律法规规定，造成黑土地面积减少、质量下降、功能退化或者生态环境损害的，应当依法治理修复、赔偿损失。  
农业生产经营者未尽到黑土地保护义务，经批评教育仍不改正的，可以不予发放耕地保护相关补贴。

**Article 31** Where black soil is illegally used for non-agricultural construction, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations such as the Land Administration Law.  
Where, in violation of laws and regulations, black soil area is reduced, quality is degraded, functions are impaired, or ecological environment is damaged, the violator shall carry out treatment and restoration and compensate for losses according to law.  
Where an agricultural producer or operator fails to fulfill the duty of protecting black soil and does not correct the mistake after criticism and education, the relevant subsidies for cultivated land protection may not be granted.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)规定，盗挖、滥挖黑土的，依照土地管理等有关法律法规的规定从重处罚。  
非法出售黑土的，由县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理、农业农村、自然资源等部门按照职责分工没收非法出售的黑土和违法所得，并处每立方米五百元以上五千元以下罚款；明知是非法出售的黑土而购买的，没收非法购买的黑土，并处货值金额一倍以上三倍以下罚款。

**Article 32** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 20 of this Law, black soil is illegally excavated or excessively excavated, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations such as the Land Administration Law.  
Where black soil is illegally sold, the competent departments of market supervision and administration, agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, etc. of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, confiscate the illegally sold black soil and the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 500 yuan but not more than RMB 5,000 yuan per cubic meter; where black soil is purchased with knowledge that it is illegally sold, the illegally purchased black soil shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the value of the goods shall be imposed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定，建设项目占用黑土地未对耕作层的土壤实施剥离的，由县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门处每平方米一百元以上二百元以下罚款；未按照规定的标准对耕作层的土壤实施剥离的，处每平方米五十元以上一百元以下罚款。

**Article 33** Where, in violation of the provisions of Article 21 of this Law, a construction project occupies black soil without stripping the soil of the plow layer, the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose a fine of not less than RMB 100 yuan but not more than RMB 200 yuan per square meter; where the soil of the plow layer is not stripped in accordance with the prescribed standards, a fine of not less than RMB 50 yuan but not more than RMB 100 yuan per square meter shall be imposed.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　拒绝、阻碍对黑土地保护情况依法进行监督检查的，由县级以上地方人民政府有关部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款。

**Article 34** Where anyone refuses or obstructs the supervision and inspection of black soil protection carried out according to law, the relevant department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 yuan but not more than RMB 20,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　造成黑土地污染、水土流失的，分别依照污染防治、水土保持等有关法律法规的规定从重处罚。

**Article 35** Where pollution of black soil or soil erosion is caused, a severe punishment shall be imposed respectively in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations such as the Law on the Prevention and Control of Environmental Pollution and the Law on Soil and Water Conservation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 36** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　林地、草原、湿地、河湖等范围内黑土的保护，适用《中华人民共和国森林法》、《中华人民共和国草原法》、《中华人民共和国湿地保护法》、《中华人民共和国水法》等有关法律；有关法律对盗挖、滥挖、非法买卖黑土未作规定的，参照[本法第三十二条](#art-32)的规定处罚。

**Article 37** The protection of black soil within forest land, grassland, wetlands, rivers and lakes shall be governed by relevant laws such as the Forest Law of the People's Republic of China, the Grassland Law of the People's Republic of China, the Wetland Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Water Law of the People's Republic of China; where the relevant laws do not provide for the illegal excavation, excessive excavation, or illegal trading of black soil, the punishment shall be imposed by reference to the provisions of Article 32 of this Law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　本法自2022年8月1日起施行。

**Article 38** This Law shall come into force as of August 1, 2022.
