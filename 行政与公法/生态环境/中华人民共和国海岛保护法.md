# 中华人民共和国海岛保护法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-12-26  
**生效日期**：2010-03-01

> （2009年12月26日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　海岛保护规划 第三章　海岛的保护 第一节　一般规定 第二节　有居民海岛生态系统的保护 第三节　无居民海岛的保护 第四节　特殊用途海岛的保护 第四章　监督检查 第五章　法律责任 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保护海岛及其周边海域生态系统，合理开发利用海岛自然资源，维护国家海洋权益，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　从事中华人民共和国所属海岛的保护、开发利用及相关管理活动，适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护海岛及其周边海域生态系统，合理开发利用海岛自然资源，维护国家海洋权益，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of protecting the ecosystem of islands and their surrounding waters, rationally developing and utilizing the natural resources of islands, safeguarding national maritime rights and interests, and promoting sustainable economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事中华人民共和国所属海岛的保护、开发利用及相关管理活动，适用本法。  
本法所称海岛，是指四面环海水并在高潮时高于水面的自然形成的陆地区域，包括有居民海岛和无居民海岛。  
本法所称海岛保护，是指海岛及其周边海域生态系统保护，无居民海岛自然资源保护和特殊用途海岛保护。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the protection, development, utilization and relevant management activities of islands belonging to the People's Republic of China.  
For the purposes of this Law, "island" means a naturally formed area of land surrounded by seawater, which is above water at high tide, including inhabited islands and uninhabited islands.  
For the purposes of this Law, "island protection" means the protection of the ecosystem of islands and their surrounding waters, the protection of natural resources of uninhabited islands, and the protection of islands for special purposes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国家对海岛实行科学规划、保护优先、合理开发、永续利用的原则。  
国务院和沿海地方各级人民政府应当将海岛保护和合理开发利用纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，采取有效措施，加强对海岛的保护和管理，防止海岛及其周边海域生态系统遭受破坏。

**Article 3** The State shall apply the principles of scientific planning, priority on protection, rational development, and sustainable utilization to islands.  
The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall incorporate island protection and rational development and utilization into the national economic and social development plans, adopt effective measures to strengthen the protection and management of islands, and prevent damage to the ecosystem of islands and their surrounding waters.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无居民海岛属于国家所有，国务院代表国家行使无居民海岛所有权。

**Article 4** Uninhabited islands shall be owned by the State, and the State Council shall exercise the ownership of uninhabited islands on behalf of the State.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院海洋主管部门和国务院其他有关部门依照法律和国务院规定的职责分工，负责全国有居民海岛及其周边海域生态保护工作。沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门和其他有关部门按照各自的职责，负责本行政区域内有居民海岛及其周边海域生态保护工作。  
国务院海洋主管部门负责全国无居民海岛保护和开发利用的管理工作。沿海县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门负责本行政区域内无居民海岛保护和开发利用管理的有关工作。

**Article 5** The marine administrative department of the State Council and other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with the division of duties as provided by law and as prescribed by the State Council, be responsible for the ecological protection of inhabited islands and their surrounding waters nationwide. The marine administrative departments and other relevant departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, in accordance with their respective duties, be responsible for the ecological protection of inhabited islands and their surrounding waters within their respective administrative regions.  
The marine administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of the protection and development and utilization of uninhabited islands nationwide. The marine administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall be responsible for the relevant work concerning the protection and development and utilization of uninhabited islands within their respective administrative regions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　海岛的名称，由国家地名管理机构和国务院海洋主管部门按照国务院有关规定确定和发布。  
沿海县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家规定，在需要设置海岛名称标志的海岛设置海岛名称标志。  
禁止损毁或者擅自移动海岛名称标志。

**Article 6** The names of islands shall be determined and published by the national administrative agency for place names and the marine administrative department of the State Council in accordance with the relevant regulations of the State Council.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level in coastal areas shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, set up island name signs on the islands where such signs are required.  
It shall be prohibited to damage or remove island name signs without authorization.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院和沿海地方各级人民政府应当加强对海岛保护的宣传教育工作，增强公民的海岛保护意识，并对在海岛保护以及有关科学研究工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人予以奖励。  
任何单位和个人都有遵守海岛保护法律的义务，并有权向海洋主管部门或者其他有关部门举报违反海岛保护法律、破坏海岛生态的行为。

**Article 7** The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall strengthen the publicity and education on island protection, enhance the citizens' awareness of island protection, and award the units and individuals that have made remarkable achievements in island protection and relevant scientific research.  
Every unit and individual shall have the obligation to abide by the laws on island protection and shall have the right to report to the marine administrative department or other relevant departments any act that violates the laws on island protection and damages the island ecology.

### 第二章　海岛保护规划

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家实行海岛保护规划制度。海岛保护规划是从事海岛保护、利用活动的依据。  
制定海岛保护规划应当遵循有利于保护和改善海岛及其周边海域生态系统，促进海岛经济社会可持续发展的原则。  
海岛保护规划报送审批前，应当征求有关专家和公众的意见，经批准后应当及时向社会公布。但是，涉及国家秘密的除外。

**Article 8** The State shall apply a system of plans for island protection. The island protection plan shall be the basis for carrying out island protection and utilization activities.  
When formulating an island protection plan, the principle of being conducive to protecting and improving the ecosystem of islands and their surrounding waters and promoting the sustainable economic and social development of islands shall be followed.  
Before an island protection plan is submitted for approval, opinions of relevant experts and the public shall be sought, and after approval, it shall be promptly published to the public, except for those involving State secrets.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院海洋主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门、军事机关，依据国民经济和社会发展规划、全国海洋功能区划，组织编制全国海岛保护规划，报国务院审批。  
全国海岛保护规划应当按照海岛的区位、自然资源、环境等自然属性及保护、利用状况，确定海岛分类保护的原则和可利用的无居民海岛，以及需要重点修复的海岛等。  
全国海岛保护规划应当与全国城镇体系规划和全国土地利用总体规划相衔接。

**Article 9** The marine administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and military authorities, organize the compilation of a national island protection plan based on the national economic and social development plan and the national marine functional zoning, and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The national island protection plan shall, in accordance with the natural attributes such as the location, natural resources and environment of islands and the status of their protection and utilization, determine the principles for classified protection of islands, the available uninhabited islands, and the islands requiring key restoration, etc.  
The national island protection plan shall be coordinated with the national urban system plan and the national general land use plan.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　沿海省、自治区人民政府海洋主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门、军事机关，依据全国海岛保护规划、省域城镇体系规划和省、自治区土地利用总体规划，组织编制省域海岛保护规划，报省、自治区人民政府审批，并报国务院备案。  
沿海直辖市人民政府组织编制的城市总体规划，应当包括本行政区域内海岛保护专项规划。  
省域海岛保护规划和直辖市海岛保护专项规划，应当规定海岛分类保护的具体措施。

**Article 10** The marine administrative departments of the people's governments of coastal provinces and autonomous regions shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's governments at the same level and military authorities, organize the compilation of provincial island protection plans based on the national island protection plan, the provincial urban system plan, and the general land use plan of the province or autonomous region, submit them to the people's governments of the provinces or autonomous regions for approval, and report them to the State Council for recordation.  
The overall urban plan compiled by the people's government of a coastal municipality directly under the Central Government shall include a special plan for island protection within its administrative region.  
The provincial island protection plan and the special plan for island protection of a municipality directly under the Central Government shall provide specific measures for classified protection of islands.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　省、自治区人民政府根据实际情况，可以要求本行政区域内的沿海城市、县、镇人民政府组织编制海岛保护专项规划，并纳入城市总体规划、镇总体规划；可以要求沿海县人民政府组织编制县域海岛保护规划。  
沿海城市、镇海岛保护专项规划和县域海岛保护规划，应当符合全国海岛保护规划和省域海岛保护规划。  
编制沿海城市、镇海岛保护专项规划，应当征求上一级人民政府海洋主管部门的意见。  
县域海岛保护规划报省、自治区人民政府审批，并报国务院海洋主管部门备案。

**Article 11** The people's governments of provinces and autonomous regions may, based on actual circumstances, require the people's governments of coastal cities, counties and towns within their administrative regions to organize the compilation of special plans for island protection, which shall be incorporated into the overall urban plan and the overall town plan; and may require the people's governments of coastal counties to organize the compilation of county island protection plans.  
The special plans for island protection of coastal cities and towns and the county island protection plans shall comply with the national island protection plan and the provincial island protection plan.  
When compiling a special plan for island protection of a coastal city or town, opinions shall be sought from the marine administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level.  
A county island protection plan shall be submitted to the people's government of the province or autonomous region for approval and shall be reported to the marine administrative department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　沿海县级人民政府可以组织编制全国海岛保护规划确定的可利用无居民海岛的保护和利用规划。

**Article 12** The people's governments of coastal counties may organize the compilation of plans for the protection and utilization of available uninhabited islands as determined in the national island protection plan.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　修改海岛保护规划，应当依照[本法第九条](#art-9)、第十条、第十一条规定的审批程序报经批准。

**Article 13** The amendment of an island protection plan shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the approval procedures provided for in Articles 9, 10 and 11 of this Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立完善海岛统计调查制度。国务院海洋主管部门会同有关部门拟定海岛综合统计调查计划，依法经批准后组织实施，并发布海岛统计调查公报。

**Article 14** The State shall establish and improve the statistical survey system for islands. The marine administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate a comprehensive statistical survey plan for islands, which shall, after being approved according to law, be organized for implementation, and shall publish the bulletin on the statistical survey of islands.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家建立海岛管理信息系统，开展海岛自然资源的调查评估，对海岛的保护与利用等状况实施监视、监测。

**Article 15** The State shall establish an island management information system, carry out investigation and assessment of island natural resources, and implement surveillance and monitoring on the status of island protection and utilization.

### 第三章　海岛的保护

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国务院和沿海地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，保护海岛的自然资源、自然景观以及历史、人文遗迹。  
禁止改变自然保护区内海岛的海岸线。禁止采挖、破坏珊瑚和珊瑚礁。禁止砍伐海岛周边海域的红树林。

**Article 16** The State Council and the local people's governments at all levels in coastal areas shall take measures to protect the natural resources, natural landscapes and historical and human remains of islands.  
It shall be prohibited to change the coastline of islands in nature reserves. It shall be prohibited to mine or damage corals and coral reefs. It shall be prohibited to cut down mangroves in the waters surrounding islands.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家保护海岛植被，促进海岛淡水资源的涵养；支持有居民海岛淡水储存、海水淡化和岛外淡水引入工程设施的建设。

**Article 17** The State shall protect island vegetation and promote the conservation of island freshwater resources; and shall support the construction of projects and facilities for freshwater storage, seawater desalination and introduction of freshwater from outside the island on inhabited islands.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家支持利用海岛开展科学研究活动。在海岛从事科学研究活动不得造成海岛及其周边海域生态系统破坏。

**Article 18** The State shall support the use of islands for scientific research activities. Scientific research activities on islands shall not cause damage to the ecosystem of the islands and their surrounding waters.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家开展海岛物种登记，依法保护和管理海岛生物物种。

**Article 19** The State shall carry out registration of island species and protect and manage island biological species according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家支持在海岛建立可再生能源开发利用、生态建设等实验基地。

**Article 20** The State shall support the establishment on islands of experimental bases for the development and utilization of renewable energy, ecological construction, etc.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家安排海岛保护专项资金，用于海岛的保护、生态修复和科学研究活动。

**Article 21** The State shall arrange special funds for island protection, which shall be used for island protection, ecological restoration and scientific research activities.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家保护设置在海岛的军事设施，禁止破坏、危害军事设施的行为。  
国家保护依法设置在海岛的助航导航、测量、气象观测、海洋监测和地震监测等公益设施，禁止损毁或者擅自移动，妨碍其正常使用。

**Article 22** The State shall protect the military installations set up on islands, and prohibit any act that damages or endangers such military installations.  
The State shall protect the public facilities set up on islands according to law, such as aids to navigation, surveying, meteorological observation, marine monitoring and earthquake monitoring facilities, and prohibit any act that damages or removes them without authorization or hinders their normal use.

#### 第二节　有居民海岛生态系统的保护

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有居民海岛的开发、建设应当遵守有关城乡规划、环境保护、土地管理、海域使用管理、水资源和森林保护等法律、法规的规定，保护海岛及其周边海域生态系统。

**Article 23** The development and construction of inhabited islands shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on urban and rural planning, environmental protection, land administration, sea area use administration, water resources and forest protection, and shall protect the ecosystem of the islands and their surrounding waters.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有居民海岛的开发、建设应当对海岛土地资源、水资源及能源状况进行调查评估，依法进行环境影响评价。海岛的开发、建设不得超出海岛的环境容量。新建、改建、扩建建设项目，必须符合海岛主要污染物排放、建设用地和用水总量控制指标的要求。  
有居民海岛的开发、建设应当优先采用风能、海洋能、太阳能等可再生能源和雨水集蓄、海水淡化、污水再生利用等技术。  
有居民海岛及其周边海域应当划定禁止开发、限制开发区域，并采取措施保护海岛生物栖息地，防止海岛植被退化和生物多样性降低。

**Article 24** The development and construction of inhabited islands shall include investigation and assessment of the island's land resources, water resources and energy conditions, and shall carry out environmental impact assessment according to law. The development and construction of an island shall not exceed the island's environmental carrying capacity. For new construction, reconstruction or expansion of construction projects, the requirements on the total control indicators for the discharge of major pollutants, land for construction and total water consumption of the island must be met.  
The development and construction of inhabited islands shall give priority to the use of renewable energy such as wind energy, ocean energy and solar energy, and technologies such as rainwater harvesting, seawater desalination and wastewater recycling.  
On inhabited islands and in their surrounding waters, areas where development is prohibited and areas where development is restricted shall be delimited, and measures shall be taken to protect the habitats of island organisms and prevent the degradation of island vegetation and the reduction of biodiversity.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在有居民海岛进行工程建设，应当坚持先规划后建设、生态保护设施优先建设或者与工程项目同步建设的原则。  
进行工程建设造成生态破坏的，应当负责修复；无力修复的，由县级以上人民政府责令停止建设，并可以指定有关部门组织修复，修复费用由造成生态破坏的单位、个人承担。

**Article 25** For engineering construction on inhabited islands, the principles of planning before construction, priority of construction of ecological protection facilities or simultaneous construction with the project shall be adhered to.  
If ecological damage is caused by engineering construction, the entity or individual responsible shall be liable for restoration; if they are unable to restore, the people's government at or above the county level shall order the suspension of construction, and may designate relevant departments to organize the restoration, and the restoration costs shall be borne by the unit or individual that caused the ecological damage.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　严格限制在有居民海岛沙滩建造建筑物或者设施；确需建造的，应当依照有关城乡规划、土地管理、环境保护等法律、法规的规定执行。未经依法批准在有居民海岛沙滩建造的建筑物或者设施，对海岛及其周边海域生态系统造成严重破坏的，应当依法拆除。  
严格限制在有居民海岛沙滩采挖海砂；确需采挖的，应当依照有关海域使用管理、矿产资源的法律、法规的规定执行。

**Article 26** The construction of buildings or facilities on the beaches of inhabited islands shall be strictly restricted; where construction is truly necessary, it shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations on urban and rural planning, land administration, environmental protection, etc. If buildings or facilities constructed on the beaches of inhabited islands without approval according to law cause serious damage to the ecosystem of the islands and their surrounding waters, they shall be demolished according to law.  
The extraction of sea sand from the beaches of inhabited islands shall be strictly restricted; where extraction is truly necessary, it shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations on administration of sea area use and mineral resources.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　严格限制填海、围海等改变有居民海岛海岸线的行为，严格限制填海连岛工程建设；确需填海、围海改变海岛海岸线，或者填海连岛的，项目申请人应当提交项目论证报告、经批准的环境影响评价报告等申请文件，依照《中华人民共和国海域使用管理法》的规定报经批准。  
本法施行前在有居民海岛建设的填海连岛工程，对海岛及其周边海域生态系统造成严重破坏的，由海岛所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门会同本级人民政府有关部门制定生态修复方案，报本级人民政府批准后组织实施。

**Article 27** Acts such as sea reclamation and enclosure that change the coastline of inhabited islands shall be strictly restricted, and the construction of land reclamation projects connecting islands to the mainland or to other islands shall be strictly restricted; where it is truly necessary to change the island coastline by sea reclamation or enclosure, or to connect islands to the mainland or to other islands by land reclamation, the project applicant shall submit application documents including a project feasibility study report and an approved environmental impact assessment report, and submit them for approval in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Sea Area Use.  
For land reclamation projects connecting islands to the mainland or to other islands constructed on inhabited islands before the coming into force of this Law, if they have caused serious damage to the ecosystem of the islands and their surrounding waters, the marine administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the island is located shall, together with the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, formulate an ecological restoration plan, which shall be implemented after being approved by the people's government at the same level.

#### 第三节　无居民海岛的保护

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　未经批准利用的无居民海岛，应当维持现状；禁止采石、挖海砂、采伐林木以及进行生产、建设、旅游等活动。

**Article 28** For uninhabited islands that have not been approved for utilization, the status quo shall be maintained; quarrying, sea sand excavation, tree felling, as well as production, construction, tourism and other activities shall be prohibited.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　严格限制在无居民海岛采集生物和非生物样本；因教学、科学研究确需采集的，应当报经海岛所在县级以上地方人民政府海洋主管部门批准。

**Article 29** The collection of biological and non-biological samples on uninhabited islands shall be strictly restricted; where collection is truly necessary for teaching or scientific research, approval shall be obtained from the marine administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level where the island is located.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　从事全国海岛保护规划确定的可利用无居民海岛的开发利用活动，应当遵守可利用无居民海岛保护和利用规划，采取严格的生态保护措施，避免造成海岛及其周边海域生态系统破坏。  
开发利用前款规定的可利用无居民海岛，应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府海洋主管部门提出申请，并提交项目论证报告、开发利用具体方案等申请文件，由海洋主管部门组织有关部门和专家审查，提出审查意见，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府审批。  
无居民海岛的开发利用涉及利用特殊用途海岛，或者确需填海连岛以及其他严重改变海岛自然地形、地貌的，由国务院审批。  
无居民海岛开发利用审查批准的具体办法，由国务院规定。

**Article 30** The development and utilization activities of available uninhabited islands as determined in the national island protection plan shall comply with the protection and utilization plans for available uninhabited islands, adopt strict ecological protection measures, and avoid causing damage to the ecosystem of the islands and their surrounding waters.  
For the development and utilization of the available uninhabited islands as prescribed in the preceding paragraph, an application shall be submitted to the marine administrative department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, together with application documents such as a project feasibility study report and a specific plan for development and utilization. The marine administrative department shall organize relevant departments and experts to review the application, put forward review opinions, and submit the application to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval.  
Where the development and utilization of an uninhabited island involves the use of an island for special purposes, or if it is truly necessary to connect the island to the mainland or to other islands by land reclamation or to otherwise seriously change the natural topography and landform of the island, the matter shall be subject to the approval of the State Council.  
The specific measures for the review and approval of the development and utilization of uninhabited islands shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　经批准开发利用无居民海岛的，应当依法缴纳使用金。但是，因国防、公务、教学、防灾减灾、非经营性公用基础设施建设和基础测绘、气象观测等公益事业使用无居民海岛的除外。  
无居民海岛使用金征收使用管理办法，由国务院财政部门会同国务院海洋主管部门规定。

**Article 31** Entities or individuals that are approved to develop and utilize uninhabited islands shall pay usage fees according to law, except where the uninhabited islands are used for public welfare undertakings such as national defense, official business, teaching, disaster prevention and mitigation, non-profit public infrastructure construction, basic surveying and mapping, and meteorological observation.  
The measures for the collection and use of usage fees for uninhabited islands shall be prescribed by the finance department of the State Council together with the marine administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经批准在可利用无居民海岛建造建筑物或者设施，应当按照可利用无居民海岛保护和利用规划限制建筑物、设施的建设总量、高度以及与海岸线的距离，使其与周围植被和景观相协调。

**Article 32** Where buildings or facilities are to be constructed on an available uninhabited island with approval, the total amount, height and distance from the coastline of such buildings and facilities shall be restricted in accordance with the protection and utilization plan for the available uninhabited island, so as to make them harmonious with the surrounding vegetation and landscape.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　无居民海岛利用过程中产生的废水，应当按照规定进行处理和排放。  
无居民海岛利用过程中产生的固体废物，应当按照规定进行无害化处理、处置，禁止在无居民海岛弃置或者向其周边海域倾倒。

**Article 33** Wastewater generated during the utilization of an uninhabited island shall be treated and discharged in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
Solid waste generated during the utilization of an uninhabited island shall be subject to harmless treatment and disposal in accordance with the relevant provisions, and it shall be prohibited to discard such waste on the uninhabited island or dump it into the surrounding waters.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　临时性利用无居民海岛的，不得在所利用的海岛建造永久性建筑物或者设施。

**Article 34** Where an uninhabited island is temporarily utilized, no permanent buildings or facilities shall be constructed on the island utilized.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　在依法确定为开展旅游活动的可利用无居民海岛及其周边海域，不得建造居民定居场所，不得从事生产性养殖活动；已经存在生产性养殖活动的，应当在编制可利用无居民海岛保护和利用规划中确定相应的污染防治措施。

**Article 35** On an available uninhabited island lawfully designated for tourism activities and in its surrounding waters, no residential settlements shall be constructed and no productive aquaculture activities shall be carried out; where productive aquaculture activities already exist, corresponding pollution prevention and control measures shall be determined when compiling the protection and utilization plan for the available uninhabited island.

#### 第四节　特殊用途海岛的保护

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国家对领海基点所在海岛、国防用途海岛、海洋自然保护区内的海岛等具有特殊用途或者特殊保护价值的海岛，实行特别保护。

**Article 36** The State shall provide special protection to islands with special purposes or special protection value, such as islands where the base points of the territorial sea are located, islands used for national defense, and islands in marine nature reserves.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　领海基点所在的海岛，应当由海岛所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府划定保护范围，报国务院海洋主管部门备案。领海基点及其保护范围周边应当设置明显标志。  
禁止在领海基点保护范围内进行工程建设以及其他可能改变该区域地形、地貌的活动。确需进行以保护领海基点为目的的工程建设的，应当经过科学论证，报国务院海洋主管部门同意后依法办理审批手续。  
禁止损毁或者擅自移动领海基点标志。  
县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门应当按照国家规定，对领海基点所在海岛及其周边海域生态系统实施监视、监测。  
任何单位和个人都有保护海岛领海基点的义务。发现领海基点以及领海基点保护范围内的地形、地貌受到破坏的，应当及时向当地人民政府或者海洋主管部门报告。

**Article 37** For an island where a base point of the territorial sea is located, the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the island is located shall delimit a protection zone and report it to the marine administrative department of the State Council for recordation. Obvious signs shall be set up around the base point and its protection zone.  
It shall be prohibited to carry out engineering construction or other activities that may change the topography or landform of the area within the protection zone of a territorial sea base point. Where engineering construction is truly necessary for the purpose of protecting the base point, scientific justification shall be carried out, and after obtaining the consent of the marine administrative department of the State Council, the approval formalities shall be gone through according to law.  
It shall be prohibited to damage or remove the signs of territorial sea base points without authorization.  
The marine administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State, implement surveillance and monitoring of the ecosystem of the island where a territorial sea base point is located and its surrounding waters.  
Every unit and individual shall have the obligation to protect the territorial sea base points on islands. If any damage to the topography or landform of a territorial sea base point or the area within its protection zone is discovered, the local people's government or the marine administrative department shall be promptly reported.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　禁止破坏国防用途无居民海岛的自然地形、地貌和有居民海岛国防用途区域及其周边的地形、地貌。  
禁止将国防用途无居民海岛用于与国防无关的目的。国防用途终止时，经军事机关批准后，应当将海岛及其有关生态保护的资料等一并移交该海岛所在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府。

**Article 38** It shall be prohibited to damage the natural topography and landform of uninhabited islands used for national defense purposes and the topography and landform of areas used for national defense purposes on inhabited islands and their surroundings.  
It shall be prohibited to use an uninhabited island for national defense purposes for any purpose unrelated to national defense. When the use for national defense purposes terminates, after approval by the military authority, the island and the relevant ecological protection data shall be handed over to the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the island is located.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国务院、国务院有关部门和沿海省、自治区、直辖市人民政府，根据海岛自然资源、自然景观以及历史、人文遗迹保护的需要，对具有特殊保护价值的海岛及其周边海域，依法批准设立海洋自然保护区或者海洋特别保护区。

**Article 39** The State Council, the relevant departments of the State Council, and the people's governments of coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to the needs of protecting the natural resources, natural landscapes and historical and human remains of islands, approve the establishment of marine nature reserves or special marine protected areas for islands with special protection value and their surrounding waters according to law.

### 第四章　监督检查

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府有关部门应当依法对有居民海岛保护和开发、建设进行监督检查。

**Article 40** The relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection over the protection, development and construction of inhabited islands.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　海洋主管部门应当依法对无居民海岛保护和合理利用情况进行监督检查。  
海洋主管部门及其海监机构依法对海岛周边海域生态系统保护情况进行监督检查。

**Article 41** The marine administrative department shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection over the protection and rational utilization of uninhabited islands.  
The marine administrative departments and their marine surveillance agencies shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection over the protection of the ecosystem of the waters surrounding islands.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　海洋主管部门依法履行监督检查职责，有权要求被检查单位和个人就海岛利用的有关问题作出说明，提供海岛利用的有关文件和资料；有权进入被检查单位和个人所利用的海岛实施现场检查。  
检查人员在履行检查职责时，应当出示有效的执法证件。有关单位和个人对检查工作应当予以配合，如实反映情况，提供有关文件和资料等；不得拒绝或者阻碍检查工作。

**Article 42** When performing the duty of supervision and inspection according to law, the marine administrative department shall have the power to require the units and individuals under inspection to make explanations on relevant issues concerning island utilization and provide relevant documents and materials on island utilization; and shall have the power to enter the islands utilized by the units and individuals under inspection to conduct on-site inspection.  
When performing inspection duties, inspectors shall present valid law enforcement certificates. The relevant units and individuals shall cooperate with the inspection, truthfully reflect the situation, and provide relevant documents and materials; and shall not refuse or obstruct the inspection.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　检查人员必须忠于职守、秉公执法、清正廉洁、文明服务，并依法接受监督。在依法查处违反本法规定的行为时，发现国家机关工作人员有违法行为应当给予处分的，应当向其任免机关或者监察机关提出处分建议。

**Article 43** Inspectors must be faithful to their duties, enforce the law impartially, be honest and clean, provide civil service, and accept supervision according to law. When investigating and punishing acts violating the provisions of this Law according to law, if it is discovered that a State functionary has committed an illegal act for which a sanction shall be imposed, the inspector shall propose a sanction to the authority that appointed or removed the functionary or to the supervisory authority.

### 第五章　法律责任

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　海洋主管部门或者其他对海岛保护负有监督管理职责的部门，发现违法行为或者接到对违法行为的举报后不依法予以查处，或者有其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府有关主管部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 44** Where the marine administrative department or any other department responsible for the supervision and administration of island protection, upon discovering an illegal act or receiving a report of an illegal act, fails to investigate and punish it according to law, or commits any other act of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the people's government at the same level or the relevant competent department of the people's government at the next higher level shall order it to make corrections, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　违反本法规定，改变自然保护区内海岛的海岸线，填海、围海改变海岛海岸线，或者进行填海连岛的，依照《中华人民共和国海域使用管理法》的规定处罚。

**Article 45** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, changes the coastline of an island in a nature reserve, changes the coastline of an island by sea reclamation or enclosure, or connects an island to the mainland or to another island by land reclamation shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Sea Area Use.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　违反本法规定，采挖、破坏珊瑚、珊瑚礁，或者砍伐海岛周边海域红树林的，依照《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》的规定处罚。

**Article 46** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, mines or damages corals or coral reefs, or cuts down mangroves in the waters surrounding an island shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　违反本法规定，在无居民海岛采石、挖海砂、采伐林木或者采集生物、非生物样本的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，可以并处二万元以下的罚款。  
违反本法规定，在无居民海岛进行生产、建设活动或者组织开展旅游活动的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 47** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, quarries, excavates sea sand, fells trees, or collects biological or non-biological samples on an uninhabited island shall be ordered by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, and the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan may also be imposed.  
Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, carries out production or construction activities or organizes tourism activities on an uninhabited island shall be ordered by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　违反本法规定，进行严重改变无居民海岛自然地形、地貌的活动的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，处以五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 48** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, engages in activities that seriously change the natural topography or landform of an uninhabited island shall be ordered by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, and a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　在海岛及其周边海域违法排放污染物的，依照有关环境保护法律的规定处罚。

**Article 49** Whoever illegally discharges pollutants on an island or into its surrounding waters shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant environmental protection laws.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，在领海基点保护范围内进行工程建设或者其他可能改变该区域地形、地貌活动，在临时性利用的无居民海岛建造永久性建筑物或者设施，或者在依法确定为开展旅游活动的可利用无居民海岛建造居民定居场所的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令停止违法行为，处以二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 50** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, carries out engineering construction or other activities that may change the topography or landform of the area within the protection zone of a territorial sea base point, constructs permanent buildings or facilities on a temporarily utilized uninhabited island, or constructs residential settlements on an available uninhabited island lawfully designated for tourism activities shall be ordered by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to cease the illegal act, and a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　损毁或者擅自移动领海基点标志的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 51** Whoever damages or removes without authorization the signs of a territorial sea base point shall be given a public security penalty according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　破坏、危害设置在海岛的军事设施，或者损毁、擅自移动设置在海岛的助航导航、测量、气象观测、海洋监测和地震监测等公益设施的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处罚。

**Article 52** Whoever damages or endangers military installations set up on islands, or damages or removes without authorization public facilities set up on islands such as aids to navigation, surveying, meteorological observation, marine monitoring and earthquake monitoring facilities shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　无权批准开发利用无居民海岛而批准，超越批准权限批准开发利用无居民海岛，或者违反海岛保护规划批准开发利用无居民海岛的，批准文件无效；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 53** Where a person without the authority to approve the development and utilization of an uninhabited island grants approval, approves the development and utilization of an uninhabited island beyond the scope of his authority, or approves the development and utilization of an uninhabited island in violation of the island protection plan, the approval document shall be null and void; and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　违反本法规定，拒绝海洋主管部门监督检查，在接受监督检查时弄虚作假，或者不提供有关文件和资料的，由县级以上人民政府海洋主管部门责令改正，可以处二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 54** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, refuses supervision and inspection by the marine administrative department, practices fraud during supervision and inspection, or fails to provide relevant documents and materials shall be ordered by the marine administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections, and a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
造成海岛及其周边海域生态系统破坏的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 55** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.  
Whoever causes damage to the ecosystem of an island and its surrounding waters shall bear civil liability according to law.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　低潮高地的保护及相关管理活动，比照本法有关规定执行。

**Article 56** The protection of low-tide elevations and related management activities shall be carried out by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　本法中下列用语的含义：  
（一）海岛及其周边海域生态系统，是指由维持海岛存在的岛体、海岸线、沙滩、植被、淡水和周边海域等生物群落和非生物环境组成的有机复合体。  
（二）无居民海岛，是指不属于居民户籍管理的住址登记地的海岛。  
（三）低潮高地，是指在低潮时四面环海水并高于水面但在高潮时没入水中的自然形成的陆地区域。  
（四）填海连岛，是指通过填海造地等方式将海岛与陆地或者海岛与海岛连接起来的行为。  
（五）临时性利用无居民海岛，是指因公务、教学、科学调查、救灾、避险等需要而短期登临、停靠无居民海岛的行为。

**Article 57** For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) "Ecosystem of islands and their surrounding waters" means the organic complex composed of biological communities and non-biological environments, including the island body, coastline, beach, vegetation, freshwater and surrounding waters that sustain the existence of the island.  
(2) "Uninhabited island" means an island that is not a residential address registered under the administration of household registration.  
(3) "Low-tide elevation" means a naturally formed area of land that is surrounded by seawater at low tide and above water but submerged at high tide.  
(4) "Land reclamation connecting islands" means the act of connecting an island to the mainland or connecting islands to each other by means such as reclamation.  
(5) "Temporary utilization of an uninhabited island" means the act of temporarily landing on or berthing at an uninhabited island for purposes such as official business, teaching, scientific investigation, disaster relief, or shelter.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　本法自2010年3月1日起施行。

**Article 58** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2010.
