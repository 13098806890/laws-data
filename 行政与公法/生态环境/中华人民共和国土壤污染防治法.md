# 中华人民共和国土壤污染防治法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-08-31  
**生效日期**：2019-01-01

> （2018年8月31日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第五次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　规划、标准、普查和监测 第三章　预防和保护 第四章　风险管控和修复 第一节　一般规定 第二节　农用地 第三节　建设用地 第五章　保障和监督 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保护和改善生态环境，防治土壤污染，保障公众健康，推动土壤资源永续利用，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事土壤污染防治及相关活动，适用本法。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护和改善生态环境，防治土壤污染，保障公众健康，推动土壤资源永续利用，推进生态文明建设，促进经济社会可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of protecting and improving the ecological environment, preventing and controlling soil pollution, safeguarding public health, promoting the sustainable use of soil resources, advancing ecological civilization, and facilitating the sustainable development of the economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国领域及管辖的其他海域从事土壤污染防治及相关活动，适用本法。  
本法所称土壤污染，是指因人为因素导致某种物质进入陆地表层土壤，引起土壤化学、物理、生物等方面特性的改变，影响土壤功能和有效利用，危害公众健康或者破坏生态环境的现象。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to the prevention and control of soil pollution and related activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China and other sea areas under its jurisdiction.  
For the purposes of this Law, "soil pollution" means the phenomenon whereby, due to human factors, certain substances enter the surface soil layer, causing changes in the chemical, physical, biological or other properties of the soil, thereby affecting the functions and effective use of the soil, endangering public health, or damaging the ecological environment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　土壤污染防治应当坚持预防为主、保护优先、分类管理、风险管控、污染担责、公众参与的原则。

**Article 3** The prevention and control of soil pollution shall adhere to the principles of giving priority to prevention, protecting the environment first, classified management, risk control, the polluter bearing responsibility, and public participation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　任何组织和个人都有保护土壤、防止土壤污染的义务。  
土地使用权人从事土地开发利用活动，企业事业单位和其他生产经营者从事生产经营活动，应当采取有效措施，防止、减少土壤污染，对所造成的土壤污染依法承担责任。

**Article 4** Every organization and individual has the obligation to protect the soil and prevent soil pollution.  
Land use right holders engaged in land development and utilization activities, and enterprises, public institutions, and other producers and business operators engaged in production and business activities, shall take effective measures to prevent and reduce soil pollution, and assume liability according to law for the soil pollution caused by them.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　地方各级人民政府应当对本行政区域土壤污染防治和安全利用负责。  
国家实行土壤污染防治目标责任制和考核评价制度，将土壤污染防治目标完成情况作为考核评价地方各级人民政府及其负责人、县级以上人民政府负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门及其负责人的内容。

**Article 5** Local people's governments at all levels shall be responsible for the prevention, control, and safe utilization of soil pollution within their respective administrative regions.  
The State implements a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for the prevention and control of soil pollution, and shall take the fulfillment of targets for the prevention and control of soil pollution as part of the assessment and evaluation of local people's governments at all levels and their persons in charge, and of the departments under the people's governments at or above the county level that are responsible for the supervision and administration of soil pollution prevention and control and their persons in charge.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当加强对土壤污染防治工作的领导，组织、协调、督促有关部门依法履行土壤污染防治监督管理职责。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen their leadership over the work of soil pollution prevention and control, and organize, coordinate, and urge the relevant departments to perform their duties of supervision and administration over soil pollution prevention and control according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国务院生态环境主管部门对全国土壤污染防治工作实施统一监督管理；国务院农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、林业草原等主管部门在各自职责范围内对土壤污染防治工作实施监督管理。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门对本行政区域土壤污染防治工作实施统一监督管理；地方人民政府农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、林业草原等主管部门在各自职责范围内对土壤污染防治工作实施监督管理。

**Article 7** The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of the work of soil pollution prevention and control nationwide; the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, forestry and grassland under the State Council shall, within the scope of their respective duties, supervise and administer the work of soil pollution prevention and control.  
The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government shall be responsible for the unified supervision and administration of the work of soil pollution prevention and control within its administrative region; the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, forestry and grassland of a local people's government shall, within the scope of their respective duties, supervise and administer the work of soil pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家建立土壤环境信息共享机制。  
国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、水利、卫生健康、林业草原等主管部门建立土壤环境基础数据库，构建全国土壤环境信息平台，实行数据动态更新和信息共享。

**Article 8** The State shall establish a mechanism for sharing soil environmental information.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, water resources, health, forestry and grassland under the State Council, establish a basic database of soil environment, build a national soil environmental information platform, and implement dynamic data update and information sharing.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家支持土壤污染风险管控和修复、监测等污染防治科学技术研究开发、成果转化和推广应用，鼓励土壤污染防治产业发展，加强土壤污染防治专业技术人才培养，促进土壤污染防治科学技术进步。  
国家支持土壤污染防治国际交流与合作。

**Article 9** The State shall support the research and development, transformation of achievements, and popularization and application of science and technology for the prevention and control of soil pollution, such as risk control, remediation, and monitoring of soil pollution, encourage the development of the soil pollution prevention and control industry, strengthen the training of specialized technical personnel for soil pollution prevention and control, and promote scientific and technological progress in soil pollution prevention and control.  
The State shall support international exchanges and cooperation on soil pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　各级人民政府及其有关部门、基层群众性自治组织和新闻媒体应当加强土壤污染防治宣传教育和科学普及，增强公众土壤污染防治意识，引导公众依法参与土壤污染防治工作。

**Article 10** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments, grassroots autonomous organizations, and news media shall strengthen publicity, education, and popularization of science on soil pollution prevention and control, enhance the public's awareness of soil pollution prevention and control, and guide the public to participate in the work of soil pollution prevention and control according to law.

### 第二章　规划、标准、普查和监测

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上人民政府应当将土壤污染防治工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、环境保护规划。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同发展改革、农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、林业草原等主管部门，根据环境保护规划要求、土地用途、土壤污染状况普查和监测结果等，编制土壤污染防治规划，报本级人民政府批准后公布实施。

**Article 11** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work of soil pollution prevention and control into the national economic and social development plans and environmental protection plans.  
The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall, together with the competent departments of development and reform, agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, forestry and grassland, formulate a plan for soil pollution prevention and control based on the requirements of environmental protection plans, land use, general survey and monitoring results of soil pollution conditions, and submit it to the people's government at the same level for approval before promulgating and implementing it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院生态环境主管部门根据土壤污染状况、公众健康风险、生态风险和科学技术水平，并按照土地用途，制定国家土壤污染风险管控标准，加强土壤污染防治标准体系建设。  
省级人民政府对国家土壤污染风险管控标准中未作规定的项目，可以制定地方土壤污染风险管控标准；对国家土壤污染风险管控标准中已作规定的项目，可以制定严于国家土壤污染风险管控标准的地方土壤污染风险管控标准。地方土壤污染风险管控标准应当报国务院生态环境主管部门备案。  
土壤污染风险管控标准是强制性标准。  
国家支持对土壤环境背景值和环境基准的研究。

**Article 12** The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall, based on soil pollution conditions, public health risks, ecological risks, and the level of science and technology, and in accordance with land use, formulate national standards for risk control of soil pollution and strengthen the construction of a system of standards for soil pollution prevention and control.  
Provincial people's governments may formulate local standards for risk control of soil pollution for items not specified in the national standards for risk control of soil pollution; for items already specified in the national standards for risk control of soil pollution, they may formulate local standards for risk control of soil pollution that are stricter than the national standards. Local standards for risk control of soil pollution shall be submitted to the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council for recordation.  
Standards for risk control of soil pollution are mandatory standards.  
The State supports research on soil environmental background values and environmental baselines.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　制定土壤污染风险管控标准，应当组织专家进行审查和论证，并征求有关部门、行业协会、企业事业单位和公众等方面的意见。  
土壤污染风险管控标准的执行情况应当定期评估，并根据评估结果对标准适时修订。  
省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门应当在其网站上公布土壤污染风险管控标准，供公众免费查阅、下载。

**Article 13** When formulating standards for risk control of soil pollution, experts shall be organized to conduct review and demonstration, and opinions from relevant departments, industry associations, enterprises, institutions, and the public shall be solicited.  
The implementation of standards for risk control of soil pollution shall be regularly evaluated, and the standards shall be revised in a timely manner based on the evaluation results.  
The competent department of ecological environment of a people's government at or above the provincial level shall publish the standards for risk control of soil pollution on its website for free public inspection and download.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国务院统一领导全国土壤污染状况普查。国务院生态环境主管部门会同国务院农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、林业草原等主管部门，每十年至少组织开展一次全国土壤污染状况普查。  
国务院有关部门、设区的市级以上地方人民政府可以根据本行业、本行政区域实际情况组织开展土壤污染状况详查。

**Article 14** The State Council shall exercise unified leadership over the general survey of nationwide soil pollution conditions. The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall, together with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, forestry and grassland under the State Council, organize and carry out a general survey of nationwide soil pollution conditions at least once every ten years.  
The relevant departments under the State Council and local people's governments at or above the level of a city divided into districts may, based on the actual conditions of their respective industries or administrative regions, organize and carry out detailed surveys of soil pollution conditions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家实行土壤环境监测制度。  
国务院生态环境主管部门制定土壤环境监测规范，会同国务院农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、水利、卫生健康、林业草原等主管部门组织监测网络，统一规划国家土壤环境监测站（点）的设置。

**Article 15** The State shall implement a soil environmental monitoring system.  
The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall formulate specifications for soil environmental monitoring, and together with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, water resources, health, forestry and grassland under the State Council, organize a monitoring network and uniformly plan the establishment of national soil environmental monitoring stations (points).

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当会同生态环境、自然资源主管部门对下列农用地地块进行重点监测：  
（一）产出的农产品污染物含量超标的；  
（二）作为或者曾作为污水灌溉区的；  
（三）用于或者曾用于规模化养殖，固体废物堆放、填埋的；  
（四）曾作为工矿用地或者发生过重大、特大污染事故的；  
（五）有毒有害物质生产、贮存、利用、处置设施周边的；  
（六）国务院农业农村、林业草原、生态环境、自然资源主管部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 16** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of local people's governments shall, together with the competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources, conduct key monitoring of the following agricultural land plots:  
(1) Those where the content of pollutants in the agricultural products produced exceeds the standards;  
(2) Those that are or have been used as sewage irrigation areas;  
(3) Those that are or have been used for large-scale breeding, stacking, or landfilling of solid waste;  
(4) Those that have been used as industrial or mining land or where major or extraordinarily serious pollution accidents have occurred;  
(5) Those surrounding facilities for the production, storage, utilization, or disposal of toxic and hazardous substances;  
(6) Other circumstances specified by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, forestry and grassland, ecological environment, and natural resources under the State Council.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同自然资源主管部门对下列建设用地地块进行重点监测：  
（一）曾用于生产、使用、贮存、回收、处置有毒有害物质的；  
（二）曾用于固体废物堆放、填埋的；  
（三）曾发生过重大、特大污染事故的；  
（四）国务院生态环境、自然资源主管部门规定的其他情形。

**Article 17** The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government shall, together with the competent department of natural resources, conduct key monitoring of the following construction land plots:  
(1) Those that have been used for the production, use, storage, recovery, or disposal of toxic and hazardous substances;  
(2) Those that have been used for stacking or landfilling of solid waste;  
(3) Those where major or extraordinarily serious pollution accidents have occurred;  
(4) Other circumstances specified by the competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources under the State Council.

### 第三章　预防和保护

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　各类涉及土地利用的规划和可能造成土壤污染的建设项目，应当依法进行环境影响评价。环境影响评价文件应当包括对土壤可能造成的不良影响及应当采取的相应预防措施等内容。

**Article 18** Various types of plans involving land use and construction projects that may cause soil pollution shall undergo environmental impact assessment according to law. The environmental impact assessment documents shall include information on the adverse effects that may be caused to the soil and the corresponding preventive measures that shall be taken.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　生产、使用、贮存、运输、回收、处置、排放有毒有害物质的单位和个人，应当采取有效措施，防止有毒有害物质渗漏、流失、扬散，避免土壤受到污染。

**Article 19** Units and individuals that produce, use, store, transport, recover, dispose of, or discharge toxic and hazardous substances shall take effective measures to prevent the leakage, loss, or spillage of toxic and hazardous substances and avoid soil pollution.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院生态环境主管部门应当会同国务院卫生健康等主管部门，根据对公众健康、生态环境的危害和影响程度，对土壤中有毒有害物质进行筛查评估，公布重点控制的土壤有毒有害物质名录，并适时更新。

**Article 20** The competent department of ecological environment under the State Council shall, together with the competent department of health under the State Council and other relevant departments, conduct screening and assessment of toxic and hazardous substances in the soil based on the degree of harm and impact on public health and the ecological environment, publish a list of key controlled toxic and hazardous substances in the soil, and update it in a timely manner.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定，根据有毒有害物质排放等情况，制定本行政区域土壤污染重点监管单位名录，向社会公开并适时更新。  
土壤污染重点监管单位应当履行下列义务：  
（一）严格控制有毒有害物质排放，并按年度向生态环境主管部门报告排放情况；  
（二）建立土壤污染隐患排查制度，保证持续有效防止有毒有害物质渗漏、流失、扬散；  
（三）制定、实施自行监测方案，并将监测数据报生态环境主管部门。  
前款规定的义务应当在排污许可证中载明。  
土壤污染重点监管单位应当对监测数据的真实性和准确性负责。生态环境主管部门发现土壤污染重点监管单位监测数据异常，应当及时进行调查。  
设区的市级以上地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当定期对土壤污染重点监管单位周边土壤进行监测。

**Article 21** The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council and based on factors such as the discharge of toxic and hazardous substances, formulate a list of key units for the supervision of soil pollution within its administrative region, make it public to the society, and update it in a timely manner.  
Key units for the supervision of soil pollution shall fulfill the following obligations:  
(1) Strictly control the discharge of toxic and hazardous substances and report the discharge situation annually to the competent department of ecological environment;  
(2) Establish a system for investigating potential soil pollution hazards to ensure continuous and effective prevention of the leakage, loss, or spillage of toxic and hazardous substances;  
(3) Formulate and implement self-monitoring plans and submit monitoring data to the competent department of ecological environment.  
The obligations specified in the preceding paragraph shall be stated in the pollutant discharge permit.  
Key units for the supervision of soil pollution shall be responsible for the authenticity and accuracy of the monitoring data. Where the competent department of ecological environment finds that the monitoring data of a key unit for the supervision of soil pollution is abnormal, it shall promptly conduct an investigation.  
The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government at or above the level of a city divided into districts shall regularly monitor the soil around the key units for the supervision of soil pollution.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　企业事业单位拆除设施、设备或者建筑物、构筑物的，应当采取相应的土壤污染防治措施。  
土壤污染重点监管单位拆除设施、设备或者建筑物、构筑物的，应当制定包括应急措施在内的土壤污染防治工作方案，报地方人民政府生态环境、工业和信息化主管部门备案并实施。

**Article 22** Where enterprises and institutions dismantle facilities, equipment, or buildings or structures, they shall take corresponding soil pollution prevention and control measures.  
Where a key unit for the supervision of soil pollution dismantles facilities, equipment, or buildings or structures, it shall formulate a work plan for soil pollution prevention and control that includes emergency measures, and submit it to the competent departments of ecological environment and industry and information technology of the local people's government for recordation and implementation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府生态环境、自然资源主管部门应当依法加强对矿产资源开发区域土壤污染防治的监督管理，按照相关标准和总量控制的要求，严格控制可能造成土壤污染的重点污染物排放。  
尾矿库运营、管理单位应当按照规定，加强尾矿库的安全管理，采取措施防止土壤污染。危库、险库、病库以及其他需要重点监管的尾矿库的运营、管理单位应当按照规定，进行土壤污染状况监测和定期评估。

**Article 23** The competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources of people's governments at all levels shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and administration of soil pollution prevention and control in mineral resource development areas, and strictly control the discharge of key pollutants that may cause soil pollution in accordance with relevant standards and total amount control requirements.  
The operation and management units of tailings ponds shall, in accordance with regulations, strengthen the safety management of tailings ponds and take measures to prevent soil pollution. The operation and management units of dangerous, risky, or defective tailings ponds, and other tailings ponds requiring key supervision, shall, according to regulations, conduct monitoring and regular assessment of soil pollution conditions.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励在建筑、通信、电力、交通、水利等领域的信息、网络、防雷、接地等建设工程中采用新技术、新材料，防止土壤污染。  
禁止在土壤中使用重金属含量超标的降阻产品。

**Article 24** The State encourages the adoption of new technologies and new materials in information, network, lightning protection, grounding, and other construction projects in the fields of construction, communications, electric power, transportation, and water resources to prevent soil pollution.  
The use of resistivity reduction products with heavy metal content exceeding the standards in the soil is prohibited.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　建设和运行污水集中处理设施、固体废物处置设施，应当依照法律法规和相关标准的要求，采取措施防止土壤污染。  
地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当定期对污水集中处理设施、固体废物处置设施周边土壤进行监测；对不符合法律法规和相关标准要求的，应当根据监测结果，要求污水集中处理设施、固体废物处置设施运营单位采取相应改进措施。  
地方各级人民政府应当统筹规划、建设城乡生活污水和生活垃圾处理、处置设施，并保障其正常运行，防止土壤污染。

**Article 25** The construction and operation of centralized sewage treatment facilities and solid waste disposal facilities shall, in accordance with the requirements of laws, regulations, and relevant standards, take measures to prevent soil pollution.  
The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government shall regularly monitor the soil around centralized sewage treatment facilities and solid waste disposal facilities; if it is found that the facilities do not comply with laws, regulations, and relevant standards, it shall, based on the monitoring results, require the operation units of the centralized sewage treatment facilities or solid waste disposal facilities to take corresponding improvement measures.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall make overall plans and construct urban and rural domestic sewage and domestic waste treatment and disposal facilities, ensure their normal operation, and prevent soil pollution.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当制定规划，完善相关标准和措施，加强农用地农药、化肥使用指导和使用总量控制，加强农用薄膜使用控制。  
国务院农业农村主管部门应当加强农药、肥料登记，组织开展农药、肥料对土壤环境影响的安全性评价。  
制定农药、兽药、肥料、饲料、农用薄膜等农业投入品及其包装物标准和农田灌溉用水水质标准，应当适应土壤污染防治的要求。

**Article 26** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland under the State Council shall formulate plans, improve relevant standards and measures, strengthen guidance on the use of pesticides and chemical fertilizers in agricultural land and control over the total amount used, and strengthen control over the use of agricultural films.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council shall strengthen the registration of pesticides and fertilizers and organize safety assessments of the impact of pesticides and fertilizers on the soil environment.  
When formulating standards for agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, feeds, and agricultural films and their packaging materials, as well as standards for the quality of farmland irrigation water, the requirements for soil pollution prevention and control shall be taken into account.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当开展农用地土壤污染防治宣传和技术培训活动，扶持农业生产专业化服务，指导农业生产者合理使用农药、兽药、肥料、饲料、农用薄膜等农业投入品，控制农药、兽药、化肥等的使用量。  
地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当鼓励农业生产者采取有利于防止土壤污染的种养结合、轮作休耕等农业耕作措施；支持采取土壤改良、土壤肥力提升等有利于土壤养护和培育的措施；支持畜禽粪便处理、利用设施的建设。

**Article 27** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of local people's governments shall carry out publicity and technical training activities on soil pollution prevention and control in agricultural land, support specialized agricultural production services, guide agricultural producers in the rational use of agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, feeds, and agricultural films, and control the amounts of pesticides, veterinary drugs, and chemical fertilizers used.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of a local people's government shall encourage agricultural producers to adopt agricultural cultivation measures such as crop-livestock integration, crop rotation, and fallow that are conducive to preventing soil pollution; support measures that are conducive to soil maintenance and cultivation, such as soil improvement and soil fertility enhancement; and support the construction of facilities for the treatment and utilization of livestock and poultry manure.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　禁止向农用地排放重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污水、污泥，以及可能造成土壤污染的清淤底泥、尾矿、矿渣等。  
县级以上人民政府有关部门应当加强对畜禽粪便、沼渣、沼液等收集、贮存、利用、处置的监督管理，防止土壤污染。  
农田灌溉用水应当符合相应的水质标准，防止土壤、地下水和农产品污染。地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同农业农村、水利主管部门加强对农田灌溉用水水质的管理，对农田灌溉用水水质进行监测和监督检查。

**Article 28** The discharge of sewage and sludge with heavy metal or other toxic and hazardous substance content exceeding the standards, as well as dredging sediment, tailings, slag, and the like that may cause soil pollution, onto agricultural land is prohibited.  
The relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision and administration of the collection, storage, utilization, and disposal of livestock and poultry manure, biogas residue, biogas slurry, and the like to prevent soil pollution.  
Water used for irrigation of farmland shall meet the corresponding water quality standards to prevent pollution of soil, groundwater, and agricultural products. The competent department of ecological environment of a local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and water resources, strengthen the management of the quality of farmland irrigation water, and monitor and supervise the quality of farmland irrigation water.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家鼓励和支持农业生产者采取下列措施：  
（一）使用低毒、低残留农药以及先进喷施技术；  
（二）使用符合标准的有机肥、高效肥；  
（三）采用测土配方施肥技术、生物防治等病虫害绿色防控技术；  
（四）使用生物可降解农用薄膜；  
（五）综合利用秸秆、移出高富集污染物秸秆；  
（六）按照规定对酸性土壤等进行改良。

**Article 29** The State encourages and supports agricultural producers in taking the following measures:  
(1) Using low-toxicity, low-residue pesticides and advanced spraying technologies;  
(2) Using organic fertilizers and high-efficiency fertilizers that meet the standards;  
(3) Adopting green prevention and control technologies for diseases, pests, and weeds, such as soil testing and formulated fertilization technology and biological control;  
(4) Using biodegradable agricultural films;  
(5) Comprehensively utilizing crop straw and removing crop straw with high accumulations of pollutants;  
(6) Improving acidic soil and the like in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　禁止生产、销售、使用国家明令禁止的农业投入品。  
农业投入品生产者、销售者和使用者应当及时回收农药、肥料等农业投入品的包装废弃物和农用薄膜，并将农药包装废弃物交由专门的机构或者组织进行无害化处理。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院生态环境等主管部门制定。  
国家采取措施，鼓励、支持单位和个人回收农业投入品包装废弃物和农用薄膜。

**Article 30** The production, sale, and use of agricultural inputs explicitly prohibited by the State are prohibited.  
Producers, sellers, and users of agricultural inputs shall promptly recover waste packaging of agricultural inputs such as pesticides and fertilizers and agricultural films, and deliver the waste packaging of pesticides to specialized institutions or organizations for harmless treatment. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs under the State Council together with the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council and other relevant departments.  
The State takes measures to encourage and support entities and individuals in recovering waste packaging of agricultural inputs and agricultural films.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家加强对未污染土壤的保护。  
地方各级人民政府应当重点保护未污染的耕地、林地、草地和饮用水水源地。  
各级人民政府应当加强对国家公园等自然保护地的保护，维护其生态功能。  
对未利用地应当予以保护，不得污染和破坏。

**Article 31** The State strengthens the protection of unpolluted soil.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall focus on protecting unpolluted farmland, forestland, grassland, and drinking water source areas.  
People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the protection of natural protected areas such as national parks and maintain their ecological functions.  
Unused land shall be protected and shall not be polluted or damaged.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当按照土地利用总体规划和城乡规划，严格执行相关行业企业布局选址要求，禁止在居民区和学校、医院、疗养院、养老院等单位周边新建、改建、扩建可能造成土壤污染的建设项目。

**Article 32** Local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall, in accordance with the overall land use plan and urban and rural planning, strictly implement the requirements for the layout and site selection of relevant industries and enterprises, and prohibit the construction, expansion, or reconstruction of construction projects that may cause soil pollution in the vicinity of residential areas, schools, hospitals, sanatoriums, nursing homes, and other such units.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家加强对土壤资源的保护和合理利用。对开发建设过程中剥离的表土，应当单独收集和存放，符合条件的应当优先用于土地复垦、土壤改良、造地和绿化等。  
禁止将重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的工业固体废物、生活垃圾或者污染土壤用于土地复垦。

**Article 33** The State strengthens the protection and rational use of soil resources. Topsoil stripped during development and construction shall be collected and stored separately, and if it meets the conditions, it shall be given priority for use in land reclamation, soil improvement, land creation, and greening.  
The use of industrial solid waste, domestic waste, or contaminated soil with heavy metal or other toxic and hazardous substance content exceeding the standards for land reclamation is prohibited.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　因科学研究等特殊原因，需要进口土壤的，应当遵守国家出入境检验检疫的有关规定。

**Article 34** If, for special reasons such as scientific research, it is necessary to import soil, the relevant provisions of the State on entry-exit inspection and quarantine shall be complied with.

### 第四章　风险管控和修复

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　土壤污染风险管控和修复，包括土壤污染状况调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控、修复、风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估、后期管理等活动。

**Article 35** Risk control and remediation of soil pollution include activities such as investigation of soil pollution conditions, risk assessment of soil pollution, risk control, remediation, assessment of risk control effectiveness, assessment of remediation effectiveness, and post-remediation management.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　实施土壤污染状况调查活动，应当编制土壤污染状况调查报告。  
土壤污染状况调查报告应当主要包括地块基本信息、污染物含量是否超过土壤污染风险管控标准等内容。污染物含量超过土壤污染风险管控标准的，土壤污染状况调查报告还应当包括污染类型、污染来源以及地下水是否受到污染等内容。

**Article 36** Where an investigation of soil pollution conditions is conducted, an investigation report on soil pollution conditions shall be prepared.  
The investigation report on soil pollution conditions shall mainly include basic information on the plot, whether the content of pollutants exceeds the standards for risk control of soil pollution, and other such matters. If the content of pollutants exceeds the standards for risk control of soil pollution, the investigation report on soil pollution conditions shall also include information on the type of pollution, the source of pollution, and whether the groundwater is contaminated.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实施土壤污染风险评估活动，应当编制土壤污染风险评估报告。  
土壤污染风险评估报告应当主要包括下列内容：  
（一）主要污染物状况；  
（二）土壤及地下水污染范围；  
（三）农产品质量安全风险、公众健康风险或者生态风险；  
（四）风险管控、修复的目标和基本要求等。

**Article 37** Where a risk assessment of soil pollution is conducted, a risk assessment report on soil pollution shall be prepared.  
The risk assessment report on soil pollution shall mainly include the following:  
(1) The status of major pollutants;  
(2) The scope of soil and groundwater contamination;  
(3) Risks to the quality and safety of agricultural products, public health risks, or ecological risks;  
(4) The objectives and basic requirements for risk control and remediation.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　实施风险管控、修复活动，应当因地制宜、科学合理，提高针对性和有效性。  
实施风险管控、修复活动，不得对土壤和周边环境造成新的污染。

**Article 38** Risk control and remediation activities shall be conducted in a manner that is tailored to local conditions, scientifically sound, and with enhanced targeting and effectiveness.  
Risk control and remediation activities shall not cause new pollution to the soil or the surrounding environment.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　实施风险管控、修复活动前，地方人民政府有关部门有权根据实际情况，要求土壤污染责任人、土地使用权人采取移除污染源、防止污染扩散等措施。

**Article 39** Before the implementation of risk control and remediation activities, the relevant departments of the local people's government have the authority, based on the actual circumstances, to require the persons responsible for soil pollution or the land use right holders to take measures such as removing pollution sources and preventing the spread of pollution.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　实施风险管控、修复活动中产生的废水、废气和固体废物，应当按照规定进行处理、处置，并达到相关环境保护标准。  
实施风险管控、修复活动中产生的固体废物以及拆除的设施、设备或者建筑物、构筑物属于危险废物的，应当依照法律法规和相关标准的要求进行处置。  
修复施工期间，应当设立公告牌，公开相关情况和环境保护措施。

**Article 40** Wastewater, waste gas, and solid waste generated during risk control and remediation activities shall be treated and disposed of in accordance with regulations and shall meet the relevant environmental protection standards.  
Where solid waste generated during risk control and remediation activities, as well as dismantled facilities, equipment, or buildings or structures, constitute hazardous waste, they shall be disposed of in accordance with the requirements of laws, regulations, and relevant standards.  
During the remediation construction period, a bulletin board shall be erected to make public the relevant information and environmental protection measures.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　修复施工单位转运污染土壤的，应当制定转运计划，将运输时间、方式、线路和污染土壤数量、去向、最终处置措施等，提前报所在地和接收地生态环境主管部门。  
转运的污染土壤属于危险废物的，修复施工单位应当依照法律法规和相关标准的要求进行处置。

**Article 41** Where a remediation construction unit transports contaminated soil, it shall formulate a transport plan and report the transport time, method, route, and the quantity, destination, and final disposal measures of the contaminated soil in advance to the competent departments of ecological environment of the place of origin and the place of reception.  
Where the transported contaminated soil constitutes hazardous waste, the remediation construction unit shall dispose of it in accordance with the requirements of laws, regulations, and relevant standards.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　实施风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估活动，应当编制效果评估报告。  
效果评估报告应当主要包括是否达到土壤污染风险评估报告确定的风险管控、修复目标等内容。  
风险管控、修复活动完成后，需要实施后期管理的，土壤污染责任人应当按照要求实施后期管理。

**Article 42** Where an assessment of risk control effectiveness or remediation effectiveness is conducted, an effectiveness assessment report shall be prepared.  
The effectiveness assessment report shall mainly include whether the risk control and remediation objectives determined in the risk assessment report on soil pollution have been achieved, and other such matters.  
Where post-remediation management is required after the completion of risk control and remediation activities, the person responsible for soil pollution shall carry out the post-remediation management in accordance with the requirements.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　从事土壤污染状况调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控、修复、风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估、后期管理等活动的单位，应当具备相应的专业能力。  
受委托从事前款活动的单位对其出具的调查报告、风险评估报告、风险管控效果评估报告、修复效果评估报告的真实性、准确性、完整性负责，并按照约定对风险管控、修复、后期管理等活动结果负责。

**Article 43** Units engaged in activities such as investigation of soil pollution conditions, risk assessment of soil pollution, risk control, remediation, assessment of risk control effectiveness, assessment of remediation effectiveness, and post-remediation management shall possess the corresponding professional capabilities.  
Units entrusted to carry out the activities mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be responsible for the authenticity, accuracy, and completeness of the investigation reports, risk assessment reports, risk control effectiveness assessment reports, and remediation effectiveness assessment reports they issue, and shall be responsible for the results of activities such as risk control, remediation, and post-remediation management in accordance with the agreement.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　发生突发事件可能造成土壤污染的，地方人民政府及其有关部门和相关企业事业单位以及其他生产经营者应当立即采取应急措施，防止土壤污染，并依照本法规定做好土壤污染状况监测、调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控、修复等工作。

**Article 44** Where an emergency event may cause soil pollution, local people's governments and their relevant departments, as well as the relevant enterprises, institutions, and other producers and business operators, shall immediately take emergency measures to prevent soil pollution, and carry out work such as monitoring and investigation of soil pollution conditions, risk assessment of soil pollution, risk control, and remediation in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　土壤污染责任人负有实施土壤污染风险管控和修复的义务。土壤污染责任人无法认定的，土地使用权人应当实施土壤污染风险管控和修复。  
地方人民政府及其有关部门可以根据实际情况组织实施土壤污染风险管控和修复。  
国家鼓励和支持有关当事人自愿实施土壤污染风险管控和修复。

**Article 45** The person responsible for soil pollution has the obligation to implement risk control and remediation of soil pollution. Where the person responsible for soil pollution cannot be identified, the land use right holder shall implement risk control and remediation of soil pollution.  
Local people's governments and their relevant departments may, based on the actual circumstances, organize the implementation of risk control and remediation of soil pollution.  
The State encourages and supports the parties concerned to voluntarily implement risk control and remediation of soil pollution.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　因实施或者组织实施土壤污染状况调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控、修复、风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估、后期管理等活动所支出的费用，由土壤污染责任人承担。

**Article 46** The expenses incurred for conducting or organizing the conducting of activities such as investigation of soil pollution conditions, risk assessment of soil pollution, risk control, remediation, assessment of risk control effectiveness, assessment of remediation effectiveness, and post-remediation management shall be borne by the person responsible for soil pollution.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　土壤污染责任人变更的，由变更后承继其债权、债务的单位或者个人履行相关土壤污染风险管控和修复义务并承担相关费用。

**Article 47** Where the person responsible for soil pollution changes, the entity or individual that succeeds to its creditor's rights and debts after the change shall fulfill the relevant obligations for risk control and remediation of soil pollution and bear the relevant expenses.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　土壤污染责任人不明确或者存在争议的，农用地由地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门会同生态环境、自然资源主管部门认定，建设用地由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门会同自然资源主管部门认定。认定办法由国务院生态环境主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 48** Where the person responsible for soil pollution is unclear or disputed, for agricultural land, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of ecological environment and natural resources, determine the responsible person; for construction land, the competent department of ecological environment of the local people's government shall, together with the competent department of natural resources, determine the responsible person. The determination measures shall be formulated by the competent department of ecological environment under the State Council together with the relevant departments.

#### 第二节　农用地

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家建立农用地分类管理制度。按照土壤污染程度和相关标准，将农用地划分为优先保护类、安全利用类和严格管控类。

**Article 49** The State shall establish a classified management system for agricultural land. According to the degree of soil pollution and relevant standards, agricultural land shall be divided into priority protection categories, safe utilization categories, and strict control categories.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上地方人民政府应当依法将符合条件的优先保护类耕地划为永久基本农田，实行严格保护。  
在永久基本农田集中区域，不得新建可能造成土壤污染的建设项目；已经建成的，应当限期关闭拆除。

**Article 50** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, designate priority protection class farmland that meets the conditions as permanent basic farmland and implement strict protection.  
Within the concentrated areas of permanent basic farmland, no construction projects that may cause soil pollution shall be constructed; those already constructed shall be closed and dismantled within a specified time limit.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　未利用地、复垦土地等拟开垦为耕地的，地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当会同生态环境、自然资源主管部门进行土壤污染状况调查，依法进行分类管理。

**Article 51** For unused land, reclaimed land, or the like that is proposed to be reclaimed as cropland, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of ecology and environment and natural resources, conduct a soil pollution status survey and carry out classified management according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对土壤污染状况普查、详查和监测、现场检查表明有土壤污染风险的农用地地块，地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当会同生态环境、自然资源主管部门进行土壤污染状况调查。  
对土壤污染状况调查表明污染物含量超过土壤污染风险管控标准的农用地地块，地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当会同生态环境、自然资源主管部门组织进行土壤污染风险评估，并按照农用地分类管理制度管理。

**Article 52** For agricultural land plots that are indicated by general surveys, detailed surveys, monitoring, and on-site inspections of soil pollution status to have soil pollution risks, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of ecology and environment and natural resources, conduct a soil pollution status survey.  
For agricultural land plots for which the soil pollution status survey shows that the pollutant content exceeds the soil pollution risk control standards, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of ecology and environment and natural resources, organize a soil pollution risk assessment and manage such plots in accordance with the classified management system for agricultural land.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对安全利用类农用地地块，地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门，应当结合主要作物品种和种植习惯等情况，制定并实施安全利用方案。  
安全利用方案应当包括下列内容：  
（一）农艺调控、替代种植；  
（二）定期开展土壤和农产品协同监测与评价；  
（三）对农民、农民专业合作社及其他农业生产经营主体进行技术指导和培训；  
（四）其他风险管控措施。

**Article 53** For agricultural land plots of the safe utilization type, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government shall, taking into account the situation of major crop varieties and planting habits, formulate and implement a safe utilization plan.  
The safe utilization plan shall include the following:  
(1) agronomic regulation and alternative planting;  
(2) regularly carrying out coordinated monitoring and evaluation of soil and agricultural products;  
(3) providing technical guidance and training to farmers, specialized farmer cooperatives, and other agricultural production and operation entities; and  
(4) other risk control measures.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对严格管控类农用地地块，地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门应当采取下列风险管控措施：  
（一）提出划定特定农产品禁止生产区域的建议，报本级人民政府批准后实施；  
（二）按照规定开展土壤和农产品协同监测与评价；  
（三）对农民、农民专业合作社及其他农业生产经营主体进行技术指导和培训；  
（四）其他风险管控措施。  
各级人民政府及其有关部门应当鼓励对严格管控类农用地采取调整种植结构、退耕还林还草、退耕还湿、轮作休耕、轮牧休牧等风险管控措施，并给予相应的政策支持。

**Article 54** For agricultural land plots of the strict control type, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government shall take the following risk control measures:  
(1) proposing suggestions on delimiting the areas where the production of specific agricultural products is prohibited, and implementing them after obtaining approval from the people's government at the same level;  
(2) carrying out coordinated monitoring and evaluation of soil and agricultural products in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) providing technical guidance and training to farmers, specialized farmer cooperatives, and other agricultural production and operation entities; and  
(4) other risk control measures.  
People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof shall encourage the adoption of risk control measures such as adjusting the planting structure, returning cropland to forest or grassland, returning cropland to wetland, rotation and fallow, and rotational grazing and rest grazing for agricultural land plots of the strict control type, and shall grant corresponding policy support.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　安全利用类和严格管控类农用地地块的土壤污染影响或者可能影响地下水、饮用水水源安全的，地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同农业农村、林业草原等主管部门制定防治污染的方案，并采取相应的措施。

**Article 55** Where soil pollution on agricultural land plots of the safe utilization type or strict control type affects or may affect the safety of groundwater or drinking water sources, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government shall, together with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland, formulate a pollution prevention plan and take corresponding measures.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　对安全利用类和严格管控类农用地地块，土壤污染责任人应当按照国家有关规定以及土壤污染风险评估报告的要求，采取相应的风险管控措施，并定期向地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门报告。

**Article 56** For agricultural land plots of the safe utilization type or strict control type, the soil pollution liable person shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the requirements of the soil pollution risk assessment report, take corresponding risk control measures and regularly report to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　对产出的农产品污染物含量超标，需要实施修复的农用地地块，土壤污染责任人应当编制修复方案，报地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门备案并实施。修复方案应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。  
修复活动应当优先采取不影响农业生产、不降低土壤生产功能的生物修复措施，阻断或者减少污染物进入农作物食用部分，确保农产品质量安全。  
风险管控、修复活动完成后，土壤污染责任人应当另行委托有关单位对风险管控效果、修复效果进行评估，并将效果评估报告报地方人民政府农业农村、林业草原主管部门备案。  
农村集体经济组织及其成员、农民专业合作社及其他农业生产经营主体等负有协助实施土壤污染风险管控和修复的义务。

**Article 57** For agricultural land plots where the pollutant content in the agricultural products produced exceeds the standards and where remediation is required, the soil pollution liable person shall prepare a remediation plan, report it to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government for filing, and implement it. The remediation plan shall include contents on groundwater pollution prevention.  
Remediation activities shall give priority to bioremediation measures that do not affect agricultural production or reduce the production function of the soil, block or reduce the entry of pollutants into the edible parts of crops, and ensure the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Upon completion of the risk control and remediation activities, the soil pollution liable person shall separately entrust a relevant unit to evaluate the effectiveness of risk control and remediation, and shall submit the effectiveness evaluation report to the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and forestry and grassland of the local people's government for filing.  
Rural collective economic organizations and their members, specialized farmer cooperatives, and other agricultural production and operation entities shall have the obligation to assist in the implementation of soil pollution risk control and remediation.

#### 第三节　建设用地

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　国家实行建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录制度。  
建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录由省级人民政府生态环境主管部门会同自然资源等主管部门制定，按照规定向社会公开，并根据风险管控、修复情况适时更新。

**Article 58** The State shall implement a list system for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land.  
The list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land shall be formulated by the competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government together with the competent department of natural resources and other relevant departments, disclosed to the public in accordance with the provisions, and updated in a timely manner based on the situation of risk control and remediation.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对土壤污染状况普查、详查和监测、现场检查表明有土壤污染风险的建设用地地块，地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当要求土地使用权人按照规定进行土壤污染状况调查。  
用途变更为住宅、公共管理与公共服务用地的，变更前应当按照规定进行土壤污染状况调查。  
前两款规定的土壤污染状况调查报告应当报地方人民政府生态环境主管部门，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门会同自然资源主管部门组织评审。

**Article 59** For construction land plots that are indicated by general surveys, detailed surveys, monitoring, and on-site inspections of soil pollution status to have soil pollution risks, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government shall require the land user to conduct a soil pollution status survey in accordance with the provisions.  
Where the use of land is changed to residential land or land for public administration and public services, a soil pollution status survey shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions before the change.  
The soil pollution status survey report specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall be submitted to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government, which shall organize an appraisal together with the competent department of natural resources.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对土壤污染状况调查报告评审表明污染物含量超过土壤污染风险管控标准的建设用地地块，土壤污染责任人、土地使用权人应当按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定进行土壤污染风险评估，并将土壤污染风险评估报告报省级人民政府生态环境主管部门。

**Article 60** For construction land plots for which the appraisal of the soil pollution status survey report indicates that the pollutant content exceeds the soil pollution risk control standards, the soil pollution liable person and the land user shall conduct a soil pollution risk assessment in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecology and environment of the State Council, and shall submit the soil pollution risk assessment report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　省级人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同自然资源等主管部门按照国务院生态环境主管部门的规定，对土壤污染风险评估报告组织评审，及时将需要实施风险管控、修复的地块纳入建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录，并定期向国务院生态环境主管部门报告。  
列入建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录的地块，不得作为住宅、公共管理与公共服务用地。

**Article 61** The competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government shall, together with the competent department of natural resources and other relevant departments, organize an appraisal of the soil pollution risk assessment report in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of ecology and environment of the State Council, promptly include the plots requiring risk control and remediation into the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land, and regularly report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the State Council.  
Plots included in the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land shall not be used as land for residential, public administration, or public services purposes.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　对建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录中的地块，土壤污染责任人应当按照国家有关规定以及土壤污染风险评估报告的要求，采取相应的风险管控措施，并定期向地方人民政府生态环境主管部门报告。风险管控措施应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。

**Article 62** For plots in the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land, the soil pollution liable person shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and the requirements of the soil pollution risk assessment report, take corresponding risk control measures and regularly report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government. The risk control measures shall include contents on groundwater pollution prevention.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　对建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录中的地块，地方人民政府生态环境主管部门可以根据实际情况采取下列风险管控措施：  
（一）提出划定隔离区域的建议，报本级人民政府批准后实施；  
（二）进行土壤及地下水污染状况监测；  
（三）其他风险管控措施。

**Article 63** For plots in the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government may, based on the actual circumstances, take the following risk control measures:  
(1) proposing suggestions on delimiting isolation areas and implementing them after obtaining approval from the people's government at the same level;  
(2) monitoring the status of soil and groundwater pollution; and  
(3) other risk control measures.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　对建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录中需要实施修复的地块，土壤污染责任人应当结合土地利用总体规划和城乡规划编制修复方案，报地方人民政府生态环境主管部门备案并实施。修复方案应当包括地下水污染防治的内容。

**Article 64** For plots in the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land that require remediation, the soil pollution liable person shall, by taking into account the overall land use plan and urban-rural plan, prepare a remediation plan, report it to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government for filing, and implement it. The remediation plan shall include contents on groundwater pollution prevention.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　风险管控、修复活动完成后，土壤污染责任人应当另行委托有关单位对风险管控效果、修复效果进行评估，并将效果评估报告报地方人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 65** Upon completion of the risk control and remediation activities, the soil pollution liable person shall separately entrust a relevant unit to evaluate the effectiveness of risk control and remediation, and shall submit the effectiveness evaluation report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government for filing.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　对达到土壤污染风险评估报告确定的风险管控、修复目标的建设用地地块，土壤污染责任人、土地使用权人可以申请省级人民政府生态环境主管部门移出建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录。  
省级人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同自然资源等主管部门对风险管控效果评估报告、修复效果评估报告组织评审，及时将达到土壤污染风险评估报告确定的风险管控、修复目标且可以安全利用的地块移出建设用地土壤污染风险管控和修复名录，按照规定向社会公开，并定期向国务院生态环境主管部门报告。  
未达到土壤污染风险评估报告确定的风险管控、修复目标的建设用地地块，禁止开工建设任何与风险管控、修复无关的项目。

**Article 66** For construction land plots that have achieved the risk control and remediation targets determined in the soil pollution risk assessment report, the soil pollution liable person or the land user may apply to the competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government to remove such plots from the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land.  
The competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government shall, together with the competent department of natural resources and other relevant departments, organize an appraisal of the effectiveness evaluation reports on risk control and remediation, promptly remove the plots that have achieved the risk control and remediation targets determined in the soil pollution risk assessment report and can be safely used from the list for risk control and remediation of soil pollution on construction land, disclose the information to the public in accordance with the provisions, and regularly report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the State Council.  
For construction land plots that have not achieved the risk control and remediation targets determined in the soil pollution risk assessment report, no project unrelated to risk control and remediation may commence construction.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　土壤污染重点监管单位生产经营用地的用途变更或者在其土地使用权收回、转让前，应当由土地使用权人按照规定进行土壤污染状况调查。土壤污染状况调查报告应当作为不动产登记资料送交地方人民政府不动产登记机构，并报地方人民政府生态环境主管部门备案。

**Article 67** Where the use of the land for production and operation of a key entity for soil pollution supervision is changed, or before the land use right of such entity is withdrawn or transferred, the land user shall conduct a soil pollution status survey in accordance with the provisions. The soil pollution status survey report shall be submitted to the real estate registration authority of the local people's government as part of the real estate registration materials and filed with the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　土地使用权已经被地方人民政府收回，土壤污染责任人为原土地使用权人的，由地方人民政府组织实施土壤污染风险管控和修复。

**Article 68** Where the land use right has been withdrawn by the local people's government and the soil pollution liable person is the original land user, the local people's government shall organize the implementation of soil pollution risk control and remediation.

### 第五章　保障和监督

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家采取有利于土壤污染防治的财政、税收、价格、金融等经济政策和措施。

**Article 69** The State shall adopt economic policies and measures such as fiscal, tax, price, and financial policies that are conducive to soil pollution prevention and control.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　各级人民政府应当加强对土壤污染的防治，安排必要的资金用于下列事项：  
（一）土壤污染防治的科学技术研究开发、示范工程和项目；  
（二）各级人民政府及其有关部门组织实施的土壤污染状况普查、监测、调查和土壤污染责任人认定、风险评估、风险管控、修复等活动；  
（三）各级人民政府及其有关部门对涉及土壤污染的突发事件的应急处置；  
（四）各级人民政府规定的涉及土壤污染防治的其他事项。  
使用资金应当加强绩效管理和审计监督，确保资金使用效益。

**Article 70** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen the prevention and control of soil pollution and allocate necessary funds for the following matters:  
(1) scientific and technological research and development, demonstration projects, and programs on soil pollution prevention and control;  
(2) activities such as general surveys, monitoring, investigations, identification of soil pollution liable persons, risk assessment, risk control, and remediation of soil pollution organized and implemented by people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof;  
(3) emergency response by people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments thereof to emergencies involving soil pollution; and  
(4) other matters concerning soil pollution prevention and control as prescribed by people's governments at all levels.  
The use of funds shall be subject to enhanced performance management and audit supervision to ensure the efficiency of fund use.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家加大土壤污染防治资金投入力度，建立土壤污染防治基金制度。设立中央土壤污染防治专项资金和省级土壤污染防治基金，主要用于农用地土壤污染防治和土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人无法认定的土壤污染风险管控和修复以及政府规定的其他事项。  
对本法实施之前产生的，并且土壤污染责任人无法认定的污染地块，土地使用权人实际承担土壤污染风险管控和修复的，可以申请土壤污染防治基金，集中用于土壤污染风险管控和修复。  
土壤污染防治基金的具体管理办法，由国务院财政主管部门会同国务院生态环境、农业农村、自然资源、住房城乡建设、林业草原等主管部门制定。

**Article 71** The State shall increase the investment of funds in soil pollution prevention and control and establish a soil pollution prevention and control fund system. A central government special fund for soil pollution prevention and control and provincial soil pollution prevention and control funds shall be established, mainly used for soil pollution prevention and control on agricultural land, risk control and remediation of soil pollution where the soil pollution liable person or the land user cannot be identified, and other matters as prescribed by the government.  
For polluted plots arising before the implementation of this Law for which the soil pollution liable person cannot be identified, where the land user actually undertakes soil pollution risk control and remediation, it may apply to the soil pollution prevention and control fund for centralized use in soil pollution risk control and remediation.  
The specific measures for the management of the soil pollution prevention and control fund shall be formulated by the competent department of finance of the State Council together with the competent departments of ecology and environment, agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, housing and urban-rural development, and forestry and grassland of the State Council.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国家鼓励金融机构加大对土壤污染风险管控和修复项目的信贷投放。  
国家鼓励金融机构在办理土地权利抵押业务时开展土壤污染状况调查。

**Article 72** The State shall encourage financial institutions to increase credit supply for soil pollution risk control and remediation projects.  
The State shall encourage financial institutions to conduct soil pollution status surveys when handling land right mortgage business.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　从事土壤污染风险管控和修复的单位依照法律、行政法规的规定，享受税收优惠。

**Article 73** Entities engaged in soil pollution risk control and remediation shall enjoy tax incentives in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　国家鼓励并提倡社会各界为防治土壤污染捐赠财产，并依照法律、行政法规的规定，给予税收优惠。

**Article 74** The State shall encourage and advocate donations of property by all sectors of society for soil pollution prevention and control, and shall grant tax incentives in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　县级以上人民政府应当将土壤污染防治情况纳入环境状况和环境保护目标完成情况年度报告，向本级人民代表大会或者人民代表大会常务委员会报告。

**Article 75** The people's government at or above the county level shall include the status of soil pollution prevention and control in the annual report on environmental conditions and the accomplishment of environmental protection targets, and shall report to the people's congress at the same level or the standing committee thereof.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门应当会同有关部门对土壤污染问题突出、防治工作不力、群众反映强烈的地区，约谈设区的市级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门主要负责人，要求其采取措施及时整改。约谈整改情况应当向社会公开。

**Article 76** The competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level shall, together with the relevant departments, hold talks with the principal responsible persons of the local people's government at or above the level of a city with districts and the relevant departments thereof in areas where soil pollution problems are prominent, prevention and control efforts are weak, and public opinion is strong, and shall require them to take measures to rectify the situation in a timely manner. The status of the talks and rectification shall be disclosed to the public.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　生态环境主管部门及其环境执法机构和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门，有权对从事可能造成土壤污染活动的企业事业单位和其他生产经营者进行现场检查、取样，要求被检查者提供有关资料、就有关问题作出说明。  
被检查者应当配合检查工作，如实反映情况，提供必要的资料。  
实施现场检查的部门、机构及其工作人员应当为被检查者保守商业秘密。

**Article 77** The competent department of ecology and environment and the environmental law enforcement agencies thereof, and other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control, shall have the power to conduct on-site inspections and sampling of enterprises, public institutions, and other producers and operators that may engage in activities causing soil pollution, require those under inspection to provide relevant materials, and make explanations on relevant issues.  
Those under inspection shall cooperate with the inspection, truthfully reflect the situation, and provide necessary materials.  
The departments, agencies, and their staff carrying out on-site inspections shall maintain the confidentiality of the trade secrets of those under inspection.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　企业事业单位和其他生产经营者违反法律法规规定排放有毒有害物质，造成或者可能造成严重土壤污染的，或者有关证据可能灭失或者被隐匿的，生态环境主管部门和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门，可以查封、扣押有关设施、设备、物品。

**Article 78** Where an enterprise, public institution, or other producer or operator discharges toxic and harmful substances in violation of laws and regulations, causing or likely to cause serious soil pollution, or where relevant evidence may be destroyed or concealed, the competent department of ecology and environment and other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control may seal or seize the relevant facilities, equipment, and articles.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　地方人民政府安全生产监督管理部门应当监督尾矿库运营、管理单位履行防治土壤污染的法定义务，防止其发生可能污染土壤的事故；地方人民政府生态环境主管部门应当加强对尾矿库土壤污染防治情况的监督检查和定期评估，发现风险隐患的，及时督促尾矿库运营、管理单位采取相应措施。  
地方人民政府及其有关部门应当依法加强对向沙漠、滩涂、盐碱地、沼泽地等未利用地非法排放有毒有害物质等行为的监督检查。

**Article 79** The competent department of work safety supervision and administration of the local people's government shall supervise the operation and management entities of tailings ponds in fulfilling their statutory obligations for soil pollution prevention and control and preventing accidents that may pollute the soil; the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government shall strengthen the supervision and inspection and regular assessment of soil pollution prevention and control in tailings ponds, and upon discovering risks and hidden dangers, shall promptly urge the operation and management entities of tailings ponds to take corresponding measures.  
The local people's government and the relevant departments thereof shall, according to law, strengthen the supervision and inspection of activities such as illegally discharging toxic and harmful substances to unused land such as deserts, tidal flats, saline-alkali land, and swamps.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　省级以上人民政府生态环境主管部门和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门应当将从事土壤污染状况调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控、修复、风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估、后期管理等活动的单位和个人的执业情况，纳入信用系统建立信用记录，将违法信息记入社会诚信档案，并纳入全国信用信息共享平台和国家企业信用信息公示系统向社会公布。

**Article 80** The competent department of ecology and environment of the people's government at or above the provincial level and other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control shall include the practice status of entities and individuals engaged in activities such as soil pollution status surveys, soil pollution risk assessments, risk control, remediation, risk control effectiveness assessments, remediation effectiveness assessments, and post-management into a credit system, establish credit records, record illegal information in the social credit file, and include such information in the national credit information sharing platform and the national enterprise credit information publicity system for public disclosure.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　生态环境主管部门和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门应当依法公开土壤污染状况和防治信息。  
国务院生态环境主管部门负责统一发布全国土壤环境信息；省级人民政府生态环境主管部门负责统一发布本行政区域土壤环境信息。生态环境主管部门应当将涉及主要食用农产品生产区域的重大土壤环境信息，及时通报同级农业农村、卫生健康和食品安全主管部门。  
公民、法人和其他组织享有依法获取土壤污染状况和防治信息、参与和监督土壤污染防治的权利。

**Article 81** The competent department of ecology and environment and other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control shall, according to law, disclose information on soil pollution status and prevention and control.  
The competent department of ecology and environment of the State Council shall be responsible for uniformly publishing national soil environmental information; the competent department of ecology and environment of the provincial people's government shall be responsible for uniformly publishing soil environmental information within its administrative region. The competent department of ecology and environment shall promptly notify the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, health, and food safety at the same level of major soil environmental information concerning areas for the production of major edible agricultural products.  
Citizens, legal persons, and other organizations shall have the right to obtain information on soil pollution status and prevention and control, participate in and supervise soil pollution prevention and control in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　土壤污染状况普查报告、监测数据、调查报告和土壤污染风险评估报告、风险管控效果评估报告、修复效果评估报告等，应当及时上传全国土壤环境信息平台。

**Article 82** General survey reports on soil pollution status, monitoring data, survey reports, soil pollution risk assessment reports, risk control effectiveness assessment reports, and remediation effectiveness assessment reports shall be uploaded to the national soil environmental information platform in a timely manner.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　新闻媒体对违反土壤污染防治法律法规的行为享有舆论监督的权利，受监督的单位和个人不得打击报复。

**Article 83** News media shall have the right of public opinion supervision over acts violating the laws and regulations on soil pollution prevention and control, and the entities and individuals under supervision shall not retaliate.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　任何组织和个人对污染土壤的行为，均有向生态环境主管部门和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门报告或者举报的权利。  
生态环境主管部门和其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门应当将土壤污染防治举报方式向社会公布，方便公众举报。  
接到举报的部门应当及时处理并对举报人的相关信息予以保密；对实名举报并查证属实的，给予奖励。  
举报人举报所在单位的，该单位不得以解除、变更劳动合同或者其他方式对举报人进行打击报复。

**Article 84** Any organization or individual shall have the right to report or inform the competent department of ecology and environment or other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control of acts polluting the soil.  
The competent department of ecology and environment and other departments with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control shall disclose the reporting methods for soil pollution prevention and control to the public to facilitate public reporting.  
The department receiving the report shall handle it in a timely manner and keep the relevant information of the informant confidential; where a report is made with the real name and verified to be truthful, a reward shall be given.  
Where the informant reports the entity where it works, such entity shall not retaliate against the informant by terminating or modifying the labor contract or by other means.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　地方各级人民政府、生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门未依照本法规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。  
依照本法规定应当作出行政处罚决定而未作出的，上级主管部门可以直接作出行政处罚决定。

**Article 85** Where a local people's government at any level, the competent department of ecology and environment, or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control fails to perform its duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be given disciplinary sanctions according to law.  
Where an administrative penalty decision should be made in accordance with the provisions of this Law but is not made, the competent department at a higher level may directly make the administrative penalty decision.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处以罚款；拒不改正的，责令停产整治：  
（一）土壤污染重点监管单位未制定、实施自行监测方案，或者未将监测数据报生态环境主管部门的；  
（二）土壤污染重点监管单位篡改、伪造监测数据的；  
（三）土壤污染重点监管单位未按年度报告有毒有害物质排放情况，或者未建立土壤污染隐患排查制度的；  
（四）拆除设施、设备或者建筑物、构筑物，企业事业单位未采取相应的土壤污染防治措施或者土壤污染重点监管单位未制定、实施土壤污染防治工作方案的；  
（五）尾矿库运营、管理单位未按照规定采取措施防止土壤污染的；  
（六）尾矿库运营、管理单位未按照规定进行土壤污染状况监测的；  
（七）建设和运行污水集中处理设施、固体废物处置设施，未依照法律法规和相关标准的要求采取措施防止土壤污染的。  
有前款规定行为之一的，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；有前款第二项、第四项、第五项、第七项规定行为之一，造成严重后果的，处二十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款。

**Article 86** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control to make corrections and shall be fined; if the person refuses to make corrections, the person shall be ordered to suspend production for rectification:  
(1) a key entity for soil pollution supervision fails to formulate or implement a self-monitoring plan, or fails to report monitoring data to the competent department of ecology and environment;  
(2) a key entity for soil pollution supervision falsifies or forges monitoring data;  
(3) a key entity for soil pollution supervision fails to report the discharge of toxic and harmful substances on an annual basis, or fails to establish a soil pollution hidden danger investigation system;  
(4) when dismantling facilities, equipment, buildings, or structures, the enterprise or public institution fails to take corresponding soil pollution prevention and control measures, or a key entity for soil pollution supervision fails to formulate or implement a soil pollution prevention and control work plan;  
(5) the operation and management entity of a tailings pond fails to take measures in accordance with the provisions to prevent soil pollution;  
(6) the operation and management entity of a tailings pond fails to monitor soil pollution status in accordance with the provisions; or  
(7) when constructing and operating a centralized sewage treatment facility or a solid waste disposal facility, the entity fails to take measures to prevent soil pollution in accordance with the requirements of laws, regulations, and relevant standards.  
For any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan shall be imposed; for any of the acts specified in items (2), (4), (5), and (7) of the preceding paragraph that causes serious consequences, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　违反本法规定，向农用地排放重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的污水、污泥，以及可能造成土壤污染的清淤底泥、尾矿、矿渣等的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上二百万元以下的罚款，并可以将案件移送公安机关，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以上十五日以下的拘留；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 87** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, discharges sewage or sludge with heavy metal or other toxic and harmful substances content exceeding the standards, or discharges dredged sediment, tailings, slag, or the like that may cause soil pollution, to agricultural land, shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 2,000,000 yuan, and the case may be transferred to the public security organ, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be detained for not less than 5 days but not more than 15 days; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　违反本法规定，农业投入品生产者、销售者、使用者未按照规定及时回收肥料等农业投入品的包装废弃物或者农用薄膜，或者未按照规定及时回收农药包装废弃物交由专门的机构或者组织进行无害化处理的，由地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款；农业投入品使用者为个人的，可以处二百元以上二千元以下的罚款。

**Article 88** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, being a producer, seller, or user of agricultural inputs, fails to timely recycle the packaging waste of agricultural inputs such as fertilizers or agricultural mulch film, or fails to timely recycle the packaging waste of pesticides and deliver it to a specialized agency or organization for harmless treatment, shall be ordered by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan; if the user of agricultural inputs is an individual, a fine of not less than 200 yuan but not more than 2,000 yuan may be imposed.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　违反本法规定，将重金属或者其他有毒有害物质含量超标的工业固体废物、生活垃圾或者污染土壤用于土地复垦的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 89** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, uses industrial solid waste, domestic waste, or polluted soil with heavy metal or other toxic and harmful substances content exceeding the standards for land reclamation shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　违反本法规定，受委托从事土壤污染状况调查和土壤污染风险评估、风险管控效果评估、修复效果评估活动的单位，出具虚假调查报告、风险评估报告、风险管控效果评估报告、修复效果评估报告的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，禁止从事上述业务，并处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
前款规定的单位出具虚假报告的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，十年内禁止从事前款规定的业务；构成犯罪的，终身禁止从事前款规定的业务。  
本条第一款规定的单位和委托人恶意串通，出具虚假报告，造成他人人身或者财产损害的，还应当与委托人承担连带责任。

**Article 90** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, being an entity entrusted to engage in activities such as soil pollution status surveys, soil pollution risk assessments, risk control effectiveness assessments, or remediation effectiveness assessments, issues a false survey report, risk assessment report, risk control effectiveness assessment report, or remediation effectiveness assessment report, shall be fined by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the entity shall be prohibited from engaging in the aforementioned business and shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; any illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
If the entity specified in the preceding paragraph issues a false report, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be fined by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, such persons shall be prohibited from engaging in the business specified in the preceding paragraph for ten years; if a crime is constituted, such persons shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in the business specified in the preceding paragraph.  
If the entity specified in the first paragraph of this Article colludes maliciously with the principal to issue a false report, causing personal injury or property damage to others, the entity shall also bear joint and several liability with the principal.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门责令改正，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五千元以上二万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未单独收集、存放开发建设过程中剥离的表土的；  
（二）实施风险管控、修复活动对土壤、周边环境造成新的污染的；  
（三）转运污染土壤，未将运输时间、方式、线路和污染土壤数量、去向、最终处置措施等提前报所在地和接收地生态环境主管部门的；  
（四）未达到土壤污染风险评估报告确定的风险管控、修复目标的建设用地地块，开工建设与风险管控、修复无关的项目的。

**Article 91** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be fined not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to separately collect and store topsoil stripped during the development and construction process;  
(2) carrying out risk control or remediation activities that cause new pollution to the soil or the surrounding environment;  
(3) transporting polluted soil without reporting in advance the time, method, route, and the quantity, destination, and final disposal measures of the polluted soil to the competent departments of ecology and environment of the place of origin and the place of reception; or  
(4) commencing construction of a project unrelated to risk control and remediation on a construction land plot that has not achieved the risk control and remediation targets determined in the soil pollution risk assessment report.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　违反本法规定，土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人未按照规定实施后期管理的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款。

**Article 92** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, being the soil pollution liable person or the land user, fails to carry out post-management in accordance with the provisions, shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be fined not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　违反本法规定，被检查者拒不配合检查，或者在接受检查时弄虚作假的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五千元以上二万元以下的罚款。

**Article 93** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, being a person under inspection, refuses to cooperate with the inspection or practices fraud during the inspection, shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人有下列行为之一的，由地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门责令改正，处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款；拒不改正的，处二十万元以上一百万元以下的罚款，并委托他人代为履行，所需费用由土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人承担；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五千元以上二万元以下的罚款：  
（一）未按照规定进行土壤污染状况调查的；  
（二）未按照规定进行土壤污染风险评估的；  
（三）未按照规定采取风险管控措施的；  
（四）未按照规定实施修复的；  
（五）风险管控、修复活动完成后，未另行委托有关单位对风险管控效果、修复效果进行评估的。  
土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人有前款第三项、第四项规定行为之一，情节严重的，地方人民政府生态环境主管部门或者其他负有土壤污染防治监督管理职责的部门可以将案件移送公安机关，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以上十五日以下的拘留。

**Article 94** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, being the soil pollution liable person or the land user, commits any of the following acts, shall be ordered by the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control to make corrections and shall be fined not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 200,000 yuan; if the person refuses to make corrections, the person shall be fined not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan, and another person shall be entrusted to perform the obligations, with the expenses borne by the soil pollution liable person or the land user; the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be fined not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 20,000 yuan:  
(1) failing to conduct a soil pollution status survey in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) failing to conduct a soil pollution risk assessment in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) failing to take risk control measures in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) failing to carry out remediation in accordance with the provisions; or  
(5) after completion of risk control and remediation activities, failing to separately entrust a relevant unit to evaluate the effectiveness of risk control and remediation.  
If the soil pollution liable person or the land user commits any of the acts specified in items (3) or (4) of the preceding paragraph and the circumstances are serious, the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government or any other department with supervisory and administrative duties over soil pollution prevention and control may transfer the case to the public security organ, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be detained for not less than 5 days but not more than 15 days.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由地方人民政府有关部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款：  
（一）土壤污染重点监管单位未按照规定将土壤污染防治工作方案报地方人民政府生态环境、工业和信息化主管部门备案的；  
（二）土壤污染责任人或者土地使用权人未按照规定将修复方案、效果评估报告报地方人民政府生态环境、农业农村、林业草原主管部门备案的；  
（三）土地使用权人未按照规定将土壤污染状况调查报告报地方人民政府生态环境主管部门备案的。

**Article 95** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits any of the following acts, shall be ordered by the relevant department of the local people's government to make corrections; if the person refuses to make corrections, the person shall be fined not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan:  
(1) a key entity for soil pollution supervision fails to report the soil pollution prevention and control work plan to the competent departments of ecology and environment and industry and information technology of the local people's government for filing in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) the soil pollution liable person or the land user fails to report the remediation plan or effectiveness evaluation report to the competent departments of ecology and environment, agriculture and rural affairs, or forestry and grassland of the local people's government for filing in accordance with the provisions; or  
(3) the land user fails to report the soil pollution status survey report to the competent department of ecology and environment of the local people's government for filing in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　污染土壤造成他人人身或者财产损害的，应当依法承担侵权责任。  
土壤污染责任人无法认定，土地使用权人未依照本法规定履行土壤污染风险管控和修复义务，造成他人人身或者财产损害的，应当依法承担侵权责任。  
土壤污染引起的民事纠纷，当事人可以向地方人民政府生态环境等主管部门申请调解处理，也可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 96** Any person who pollutes the soil, causing personal injury or property damage to others, shall bear tort liability according to law.  
If the soil pollution liable person cannot be identified and the land user fails to perform the obligation of soil pollution risk control and remediation in accordance with the provisions of this Law, causing personal injury or property damage to others, the land user shall bear tort liability according to law.  
For civil disputes arising from soil pollution, the parties may apply to the competent department of ecology and environment or other relevant departments of the local people's government for mediation, or may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　污染土壤损害国家利益、社会公共利益的，有关机关和组织可以依照《中华人民共和国环境保护法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 97** Where soil pollution harms the national interest or the public interest, the relevant authorities and organizations may initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court in accordance with the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 98** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of this Law, commits an act constituting a violation of public security administration, shall be given a public security administration penalty by the public security organ according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　本法自2019年1月1日起施行。

**Article 99** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2019.
