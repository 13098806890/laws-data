# 行政机关公务员处分条例

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2007-04-04  
**生效日期**：2007-06-01

> （2007年4月4日国务院第173次常务会议通过 2007年4月22日中华人民共和国国务院令第495号公布 自2007年6月1日起施行） 第一章　总则 第一条　为了严肃行政机关纪律，规范行政机关公务员的行为，保证行政机关及其公务员依法履行职责，根据《中华人民共和国公务员法》和《中华人民共和国行政监察法》，制定本条例。 第二条　行政机关公务员违反法律、法规、规章以及行政机关的决定和命令，应当承担纪律责任的，依照本条例给予处分。 法律、其他行政法规、国务院决定对行政机关公务员处分有规定的，依照该法律、行政法规、国务院决定的规定执行；法律、其他行政法规、国务院决定对行政机关公务员应当受到处分的违法违纪行为做了规定，但是未对处分幅度做规定的，适用本条例第三章与其最相类似的条款有关处分幅度的规定。 地方性法规、部门规章、地方政府规章可以补充规定本条例第三章未作规定的应当给予处分的违法违纪行为以及相应的处分幅度。除国务院监察机关、国务院人事部门外，国务院其他部门制定处分规章，应当与国务院监察机关、国务院人事部门联合制定。 除法律、法规、规章以及国务院决定外，行政机关不得以其他形式设定行政机关公务员处分事项。 第三条　行政机关公务员依法履行职务的行为受法律保护，非因法定事由，非经法定程序，不受处分。 第四条　给予行政机关公务员处分，应当坚持公正、公平和教育与惩处相结合的原则。 给予行政机关公务员处分，应当与其违法违纪行为的性质、情节、危害程度相适应。 给予行政机关公务员处分，应当事实清楚、证据确凿、定性准确、处理恰当、程序合法、手续完备。 第五条　行政机关公务员违法违纪涉嫌犯罪的，应当移送司法机关依法追究刑事责任。 第二章　处分的种类和适用 第六条　行政机关公务员处分的种类为： （一）警告；

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了严肃行政机关纪律，规范行政机关公务员的行为，保证行政机关及其公务员依法履行职责，根据《中华人民共和国公务员法》和《中华人民共和国行政监察法》，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated in accordance with the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China for the purposes of strictly enforcing discipline in administrative organs, regulating the conduct of civil servants of administrative organs, and ensuring that administrative organs and their civil servants perform their duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　行政机关公务员违反法律、法规、规章以及行政机关的决定和命令，应当承担纪律责任的，依照本条例给予处分。  
法律、其他行政法规、国务院决定对行政机关公务员处分有规定的，依照该法律、行政法规、国务院决定的规定执行；法律、其他行政法规、国务院决定对行政机关公务员应当受到处分的违法违纪行为做了规定，但是未对处分幅度做规定的，适用本条例第三章与其最相类似的条款有关处分幅度的规定。  
地方性法规、部门规章、地方政府规章可以补充规定本条例第三章未作规定的应当给予处分的违法违纪行为以及相应的处分幅度。除国务院监察机关、国务院人事部门外，国务院其他部门制定处分规章，应当与国务院监察机关、国务院人事部门联合制定。  
除法律、法规、规章以及国务院决定外，行政机关不得以其他形式设定行政机关公务员处分事项。

**Article 2** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ violates laws, regulations, rules, as well as decisions and orders of the administrative organ, and shall bear disciplinary liability, a sanction shall be imposed in accordance with these Regulations.  
Where laws, other administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council provide for sanctions against civil servants of administrative organs, such sanctions shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of such laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council; where laws, other administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council provide for the illegal or disciplinary acts for which civil servants of administrative organs shall be sanctioned but do not provide for the range of sanctions, the provisions on the range of sanctions in the most similar clauses in Chapter III of these Regulations shall apply.  
Local regulations, departmental rules, and local government rules may supplement the provisions on illegal or disciplinary acts for which sanctions shall be imposed and the corresponding range of sanctions not provided for in Chapter III of these Regulations. Except for the supervisory organ of the State Council and the personnel department of the State Council, other departments of the State Council that formulate rules on sanctions shall do so jointly with the supervisory organ of the State Council and the personnel department of the State Council.  
Except for laws, regulations, rules, and decisions of the State Council, no administrative organ may establish matters concerning sanctions against civil servants of administrative organs in any other form.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　行政机关公务员依法履行职务的行为受法律保护，非因法定事由，非经法定程序，不受处分。

**Article 3** The acts of a civil servant of an administrative organ in performing his or her duties according to law shall be protected by law, and no sanction shall be imposed on him or her without statutory causes and without going through statutory procedures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　给予行政机关公务员处分，应当坚持公正、公平和教育与惩处相结合的原则。  
给予行政机关公务员处分，应当与其违法违纪行为的性质、情节、危害程度相适应。  
给予行政机关公务员处分，应当事实清楚、证据确凿、定性准确、处理恰当、程序合法、手续完备。

**Article 4** When imposing a sanction on a civil servant of an administrative organ, the principles of fairness, impartiality, and combining education with punishment shall be adhered to.  
When imposing a sanction on a civil servant of an administrative organ, the sanction shall be commensurate with the nature, circumstances, and degree of harm of the illegal or disciplinary act.  
When imposing a sanction on a civil servant of an administrative organ, the facts shall be clear, the evidence shall be conclusive, the characterization shall be accurate, the handling shall be appropriate, the procedures shall be lawful, and the formalities shall be complete.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行政机关公务员违法违纪涉嫌犯罪的，应当移送司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 5** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ is suspected of committing a crime due to his or her illegal or disciplinary act, he or she shall be transferred to the judicial organ to be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

### 第二章　处分的种类和适用

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行政机关公务员处分的种类为：  
（一）警告；  
（二）记过；  
（三）记大过；  
（四）降级；  
（五）撤职；  
（六）开除。

**Article 6** The types of sanctions against civil servants of administrative organs are:  
(1) Warning;  
(2) Demerit;  
(3) Serious demerit;  
(4) Demotion;  
(5) Removal from office;  
(6) Dismissal.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　行政机关公务员受处分的期间为：  
（一）警告，6个月；  
（二）记过，12个月；  
（三）记大过，18个月；  
（四）降级、撤职，24个月。

**Article 7** The duration of a sanction imposed on a civil servant of an administrative organ is:  
(1) Warning: 6 months;  
(2) Demerit: 12 months;  
(3) Serious demerit: 18 months;  
(4) Demotion and removal from office: 24 months.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行政机关公务员在受处分期间不得晋升职务和级别，其中，受记过、记大过、降级、撤职处分的，不得晋升工资档次；受撤职处分的，应当按照规定降低级别。

**Article 8** A civil servant of an administrative organ shall not be promoted in position or rank during the period of sanction; if he or she is subject to a sanction of demerit, serious demerit, demotion, or removal from office, he or she shall not be promoted in salary grade; if he or she is subject to a sanction of removal from office, his or her rank shall be lowered in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政机关公务员受开除处分的，自处分决定生效之日起，解除其与单位的人事关系，不得再担任公务员职务。  
行政机关公务员受开除以外的处分，在受处分期间有悔改表现，并且没有再发生违法违纪行为的，处分期满后，应当解除处分。解除处分后，晋升工资档次、级别和职务不再受原处分的影响。但是，解除降级、撤职处分的，不视为恢复原级别、原职务。

**Article 9** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ is subject to a sanction of dismissal, the personnel relationship with the unit shall be terminated as of the date on which the sanction decision takes effect, and he or she shall no longer hold a civil servant post.  
Where a civil servant of an administrative organ is subject to a sanction other than dismissal, if he or she shows repentance during the period of sanction and does not engage in any further illegal or disciplinary act, the sanction shall be lifted upon expiration of the period. After the sanction is lifted, promotion in salary grade, rank, and position shall no longer be affected by the original sanction. However, the lifting of a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall not be deemed as restoration of the original rank or position.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　行政机关公务员同时有两种以上需要给予处分的行为的，应当分别确定其处分。应当给予的处分种类不同的，执行其中最重的处分；应当给予撤职以下多个相同种类处分的，执行该处分，并在一个处分期以上、多个处分期之和以下，决定处分期。  
行政机关公务员在受处分期间受到新的处分的，其处分期为原处分期尚未执行的期限与新处分期限之和。  
处分期最长不得超过48个月。

**Article 10** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ commits two or more acts for which sanctions shall be imposed, the sanctions for each act shall be determined separately. If the types of sanctions to be imposed are different, the heaviest sanction shall be executed; if multiple sanctions of the same type below removal from office shall be imposed, such sanction shall be executed, and the period of the sanction shall be determined as not less than one such period and not more than the sum of multiple periods.  
Where a civil servant of an administrative organ is subject to a new sanction during the period of an existing sanction, the period of the sanction shall be the sum of the unexecuted portion of the original period and the period of the new sanction.  
The maximum period of a sanction shall not exceed 48 months.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政机关公务员2人以上共同违法违纪，需要给予处分的，根据各自应当承担的纪律责任，分别给予处分。

**Article 11** Where two or more civil servants of an administrative organ jointly commit an illegal or disciplinary act and sanctions need to be imposed, sanctions shall be imposed on each of them separately in accordance with the disciplinary liability they shall bear.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，应当从重处分：  
（一）在2人以上的共同违法违纪行为中起主要作用的；  
（二）隐匿、伪造、销毁证据的；  
（三）串供或者阻止他人揭发检举、提供证据材料的；  
（四）包庇同案人员的；  
（五）法律、法规、规章规定的其他从重情节。

**Article 12** In any of the following circumstances, a heavier sanction shall be imposed:  
(1) Playing a major role in a joint illegal or disciplinary act committed by two or more persons;  
(2) Concealing, forging, or destroying evidence;  
(3) Colluding in the making of confessions or preventing others from exposing or reporting, or providing evidence materials;  
(4) Covering up for other persons involved in the same case;  
(5) Other circumstances for heavier sanctions as provided for by laws, regulations, or rules.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　有下列情形之一的，应当从轻处分：  
（一）主动交代违法违纪行为的；  
（二）主动采取措施，有效避免或者挽回损失的；  
（三）检举他人重大违法违纪行为，情况属实的。

**Article 13** In any of the following circumstances, a lighter sanction shall be imposed:  
(1) Voluntarily confessing the illegal or disciplinary act;  
(2) Voluntarily taking measures to effectively avoid or retrieve losses;  
(3) Reporting another person's major illegal or disciplinary act, which is verified to be true.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　行政机关公务员主动交代违法违纪行为，并主动采取措施有效避免或者挽回损失的，应当减轻处分。  
行政机关公务员违纪行为情节轻微，经过批评教育后改正的，可以免予处分。

**Article 14** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ voluntarily confesses the illegal or disciplinary act and voluntarily takes measures to effectively avoid or retrieve losses, the sanction shall be mitigated.  
Where the disciplinary act of a civil servant of an administrative organ is minor and he or she corrects the act after criticism and education, the sanction may be exempted.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　行政机关公务员有[本条例第十二条](#art-12)、第十三条规定情形之一的，应当在本条例第三章规定的处分幅度以内从重或者从轻给予处分。  
行政机关公务员有[本条例第十四条](#art-14)第一款规定情形的，应当在本条例第三章规定的处分幅度以外，减轻一个处分的档次给予处分。应当给予警告处分，又有减轻处分的情形的，免予处分。

**Article 15** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ falls under any of the circumstances specified in Articles 12 and 13 of these Regulations, a heavier or lighter sanction shall be imposed within the range of sanctions provided for in Chapter III of these Regulations.  
Where a civil servant of an administrative organ falls under the circumstance specified in paragraph 1 of Article 14 of these Regulations, the sanction shall be imposed by mitigating one grade of sanction outside the range of sanctions provided for in Chapter III of these Regulations. Where a sanction of warning shall be imposed and there is also a circumstance for mitigation, the sanction shall be exempted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　行政机关经人民法院、监察机关、行政复议机关或者上级行政机关依法认定有行政违法行为或者其他违法违纪行为，需要追究纪律责任的，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员给予处分。

**Article 16** Where an administrative organ is determined by a People's Court, a supervisory organ, an administrative reconsideration organ, or a superior administrative organ according to law to have committed an administrative illegal act or other illegal or disciplinary act, and disciplinary liability needs to be investigated, sanctions shall be imposed on the responsible leading persons and the persons directly liable.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违法违纪的行政机关公务员在行政机关对其作出处分决定前，已经依法被判处刑罚、罢免、免职或者已经辞去领导职务，依法应当给予处分的，由行政机关根据其违法违纪事实，给予处分。  
行政机关公务员依法被判处刑罚的，给予开除处分。

**Article 17** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ who has committed an illegal or disciplinary act has already been sentenced to a criminal punishment, removed from office, dismissed from office, or resigned from a leading position according to law before the administrative organ makes a sanction decision against him or her, and a sanction shall be imposed according to law, the administrative organ shall impose a sanction based on the facts of the illegal or disciplinary act.  
Where a civil servant of an administrative organ is sentenced to a criminal punishment according to law, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

### 第三章　违法违纪行为及其适用的处分

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　有下列行为之一的，给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）散布有损国家声誉的言论，组织或者参加旨在反对国家的集会、游行、示威等活动的；  
（二）组织或者参加非法组织，组织或者参加罢工的；  
（三）违反国家的民族宗教政策，造成不良后果的；  
（四）以暴力、威胁、贿赂、欺骗等手段，破坏选举的；  
（五）在对外交往中损害国家荣誉和利益的；  
（六）非法出境，或者违反规定滞留境外不归的；  
（七）未经批准获取境外永久居留资格，或者取得外国国籍的；  
（八）其他违反政治纪律的行为。  
有前款第（六）项规定行为的，给予开除处分；有前款第（一）项、第（二）项或者第（三）项规定的行为，属于不明真相被裹挟参加，经批评教育后确有悔改表现的，可以减轻或者免予处分。

**Article 18** For any of the following acts, a sanction of serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed:  
(1) Spreading remarks that are detrimental to the reputation of the State, or organizing or participating in activities such as assemblies, processions, or demonstrations aimed at opposing the State;  
(2) Organizing or participating in illegal organizations, or organizing or participating in strikes;  
(3) Violating the State's policies on ethnic groups and religions, causing adverse consequences;  
(4) Undermining elections by means of violence, threats, bribery, deception, etc.;  
(5) Damaging the honor and interests of the State in foreign exchanges;  
(6) Illegally leaving the country, or staying abroad without returning in violation of provisions;  
(7) Obtaining the right of permanent residence abroad or acquiring foreign nationality without approval;  
(8) Other acts that violate political discipline.  
For an act specified in subparagraph (6) of the preceding paragraph, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed; for an act specified in subparagraph (1), (2), or (3) of the preceding paragraph, if the person participated under duress without knowing the truth and shows true repentance after criticism and education, the sanction may be mitigated or exempted.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　有下列行为之一的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）负有领导责任的公务员违反议事规则，个人或者少数人决定重大事项，或者改变集体作出的重大决定的；  
（二）拒绝执行上级依法作出的决定、命令的；  
（三）拒不执行机关的交流决定的；  
（四）拒不执行人民法院对行政案件的判决、裁定或者监察机关、审计机关、行政复议机关作出的决定的；  
（五）违反规定应当回避而不回避，影响公正执行公务，造成不良后果的；  
（六）离任、辞职或者被辞退时，拒不办理公务交接手续或者拒不接受审计的；  
（七）旷工或者因公外出、请假期满无正当理由逾期不归，造成不良影响的；  
（八）其他违反组织纪律的行为。

**Article 19** For any of the following acts, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed:  
(1) A civil servant with leading responsibility violating rules of procedure, making decisions on major matters individually or by a small number of persons, or altering major decisions made collectively;  
(2) Refusing to implement decisions or orders made legally by a superior;  
(3) Refusing to implement an exchange decision made by the organ;  
(4) Refusing to implement a judgment or ruling of a People's Court on an administrative case or a decision made by a supervisory organ, an audit organ, or an administrative reconsideration organ;  
(5) Failing to recuse oneself as required by provisions when recusal is necessary, thereby affecting impartial execution of official duties and causing adverse consequences;  
(6) Refusing to go through the procedures for handing over official duties or refusing to accept audit when leaving office, resigning, or being dismissed;  
(7) Being absent from work without leave or, after going on a business trip or taking leave, failing to return without justifiable reasons after the expiration of the period, causing adverse effects;  
(8) Other acts that violate organizational discipline.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列行为之一的，给予记过、记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）不依法履行职责，致使可以避免的爆炸、火灾、传染病传播流行、严重环境污染、严重人员伤亡等重大事故或者群体性事件发生的；  
（二）发生重大事故、灾害、事件或者重大刑事案件、治安案件，不按规定报告、处理的；  
（三）对救灾、抢险、防汛、防疫、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济、社会保险、征地补偿等专项款物疏于管理，致使款物被贪污、挪用，或者毁损、灭失的；  
（四）其他玩忽职守、贻误工作的行为。

**Article 20** For any of the following acts, a sanction of demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed:  
(1) Failing to perform duties according to law, thereby causing the occurrence of avoidable major accidents or mass incidents such as explosions, fires, spread of infectious diseases, serious environmental pollution, or heavy casualties;  
(2) Failing to report or handle major accidents, disasters, incidents, or major criminal cases or public security cases in accordance with provisions;  
(3) Being negligent in the management of special funds and goods for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, epidemic prevention, special care, poverty alleviation, resettlement, relief, social insurance, land acquisition compensation, etc., resulting in the embezzlement, misappropriation, damage, or loss of such funds and goods;  
(4) Other acts of dereliction of duty or neglect of work.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有下列行为之一的，给予警告或者记过处分；情节较重的，给予记大过或者降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职处分：  
（一）在行政许可工作中违反法定权限、条件和程序设定或者实施行政许可的；  
（二）违法设定或者实施行政强制措施的；  
（三）违法设定或者实施行政处罚的；  
（四）违反法律、法规规定进行行政委托的；  
（五）对需要政府、政府部门决定的招标投标、征收征用、城市房屋拆迁、拍卖等事项违反规定办理的。

**Article 21** For any of the following acts, a sanction of warning or demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of serious demerit or demotion shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of removal from office shall be imposed:  
(1) Establishing or implementing an administrative license in violation of statutory authority, conditions, and procedures in administrative licensing work;  
(2) Illegally establishing or implementing compulsory administrative measures;  
(3) Illegally establishing or implementing administrative penalties;  
(4) Making an administrative entrustment in violation of the provisions of laws or regulations;  
(5) Handling matters such as bidding, requisition and expropriation, urban house demolition, auction, etc., that require decisions by the government or government departments, in violation of provisions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　弄虚作假，误导、欺骗领导和公众，造成不良后果的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 22** Where any person engages in fraud, misleads or deceives leaders or the public, and causes adverse consequences, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　有贪污、索贿、受贿、行贿、介绍贿赂、挪用公款、利用职务之便为自己或者他人谋取私利、巨额财产来源不明等违反廉政纪律行为的，给予记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 23** For acts violating discipline on integrity such as embezzlement, solicitation of bribes, acceptance of bribes, offering of bribes, introduction of bribes, misappropriation of public funds, taking advantage of one's position to seek private gains for oneself or others, or having a large amount of property with unidentified sources, a sanction of demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　违反财经纪律，挥霍浪费国家资财的，给予警告处分；情节较重的，给予记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予降级或者撤职处分。

**Article 24** For violating financial and economic discipline by squandering and wasting State assets, a sanction of warning shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列行为之一的，给予记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）以殴打、体罚、非法拘禁等方式侵犯公民人身权利的；  
（二）压制批评，打击报复，扣压、销毁举报信件，或者向被举报人透露举报情况的；  
（三）违反规定向公民、法人或者其他组织摊派或者收取财物的；  
（四）妨碍执行公务或者违反规定干预执行公务的；  
（五）其他滥用职权，侵害公民、法人或者其他组织合法权益的行为。

**Article 25** For any of the following acts, a sanction of demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed:  
(1) Infringing upon citizens' personal rights by means of beating, corporal punishment, illegal detention, etc.;  
(2) Suppressing criticism, retaliating, withholding or destroying letters of reports, or disclosing the report information to the reported person;  
(3) Illegally apportioning or collecting money or property from citizens, legal persons, or other organizations in violation of provisions;  
(4) Obstructing the execution of official duties or interfering with the execution of official duties in violation of provisions;  
(5) Other acts of abusing power and infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　泄露国家秘密、工作秘密，或者泄露因履行职责掌握的商业秘密、个人隐私，造成不良后果的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 26** For leaking State secrets or work secrets, or leaking trade secrets or personal privacy obtained in the course of performing duties, causing adverse consequences, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　从事或者参与营利性活动，在企业或者其他营利性组织中兼任职务的，给予记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 27** For engaging in or participating in for-profit activities, or holding concurrent posts in enterprises or other for-profit organizations, a sanction of demerit or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　严重违反公务员职业道德，工作作风懈怠、工作态度恶劣，造成不良影响的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分。

**Article 28** For seriously violating the professional ethics of civil servants, being slack in work style, or having a bad work attitude, causing adverse effects, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　有下列行为之一的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分：  
（一）拒不承担赡养、抚养、扶养义务的；  
（二）虐待、遗弃家庭成员的；  
（三）包养情人的；  
（四）严重违反社会公德的行为。  
有前款第（三）项行为的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 29** For any of the following acts, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed:  
(1) Refusing to assume the duty of support, maintenance, or child-rearing;  
(2) Abusing or abandoning family members;  
(3) Keeping a mistress or lover;  
(4) Acts seriously violating social morality.  
For an act specified in subparagraph (3) of the preceding paragraph, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　参与迷信活动，造成不良影响的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；组织迷信活动的，给予降级或者撤职处分，情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 30** For participating in superstitious activities, causing adverse effects, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; for organizing superstitious activities, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　吸食、注射毒品或者组织、支持、参与卖淫、嫖娼、色情淫乱活动的，给予撤职或者开除处分。

**Article 31** For ingesting or injecting drugs, or organizing, supporting, or participating in prostitution, whoring, or pornographic and promiscuous activities, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　参与赌博的，给予警告或者记过处分；情节较重的，给予记大过或者降级处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。  
为赌博活动提供场所或者其他便利条件的，给予警告、记过或者记大过处分；情节严重的，给予撤职或者开除处分。  
在工作时间赌博的，给予记过、记大过或者降级处分；屡教不改的，给予撤职或者开除处分。  
挪用公款赌博的，给予撤职或者开除处分。  
利用赌博索贿、受贿或者行贿的，依照[本条例第二十三条](#art-23)的规定给予处分。

**Article 32** For participating in gambling, a sanction of warning or demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are relatively serious, a sanction of serious demerit or demotion shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.  
For providing premises or other convenience for gambling activities, a sanction of warning, demerit, or serious demerit shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.  
For gambling during working hours, a sanction of demerit, serious demerit, or demotion shall be imposed; if the person refuses to correct after repeated education, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.  
For gambling with misappropriated public funds, a sanction of removal from office or dismissal shall be imposed.  
For using gambling to solicit bribes, accept bribes, or offer bribes, a sanction shall be imposed in accordance with Article 23 of these Regulations.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违反规定超计划生育的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分。

**Article 33** For violating provisions by exceeding the birth plan, a sanction of demotion or removal from office shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a sanction of dismissal shall be imposed.

### 第四章　处分的权限

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　对行政机关公务员给予处分，由任免机关或者监察机关（以下统称处分决定机关）按照管理权限决定。

**Article 34** The sanction against a civil servant of an administrative organ shall be decided by the appointing and removing organ or the supervisory organ (hereinafter collectively referred to as the sanction decision organ) in accordance with the management authority.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　对经全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会决定任命的国务院组成人员给予处分，由国务院决定。其中，拟给予撤职、开除处分的，由国务院向全国人民代表大会提出罢免建议，或者向全国人民代表大会常务委员会提出免职建议。罢免或者免职前，国务院可以决定暂停其履行职务。

**Article 35** A sanction against a member of the State Council appointed by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee shall be decided by the State Council. Where a sanction of removal from office or dismissal is to be imposed, the State Council shall propose a motion of recall to the National People's Congress or a motion of removal from office to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. Before the recall or removal, the State Council may decide to suspend him or her from performing duties.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　对经地方各级人民代表大会及其常务委员会选举或者决定任命的地方各级人民政府领导人员给予处分，由上一级人民政府决定。  
拟给予经县级以上地方人民代表大会及其常务委员会选举或者决定任命的县级以上地方人民政府领导人员撤职、开除处分的，应当先由本级人民政府向同级人民代表大会提出罢免建议。其中，拟给予县级以上地方人民政府副职领导人员撤职、开除处分的，也可以向同级人民代表大会常务委员会提出撤销职务的建议。拟给予乡镇人民政府领导人员撤职、开除处分的，应当先由本级人民政府向同级人民代表大会提出罢免建议。罢免或者撤销职务前，上级人民政府可以决定暂停其履行职务；遇有特殊紧急情况，省级以上人民政府认为必要时，也可以对其作出撤职或者开除的处分，同时报告同级人民代表大会常务委员会，并通报下级人民代表大会常务委员会。

**Article 36** A sanction against a leading person of a local people's government elected or appointed by the local people's congress or its standing committee at the corresponding level shall be decided by the people's government at the next higher level.  
Where a sanction of removal from office or dismissal is to be imposed on a leading person of a local people's government at or above the county level elected or appointed by the people's congress or its standing committee at the corresponding level, the people's government at the same level shall first propose a motion of recall to the people's congress at the same level. Where a sanction of removal from office or dismissal is to be imposed on a deputy leading person of a local people's government at or above the county level, a proposal for removal from office may also be made to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level. Where a sanction of removal from office or dismissal is to be imposed on a leading person of a township people's government, the people's government at the same level shall first propose a motion of recall to the people's congress at the same level. Before the recall or removal from office, the people's government at the next higher level may decide to suspend him or her from performing duties; in special emergency circumstances, where the people's government at or above the provincial level deems it necessary, it may also impose a sanction of removal from office or dismissal on him or her, and shall simultaneously report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level and notify the standing committee of the people's congress at the lower level.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对地方各级人民政府工作部门正职领导人员给予处分，由本级人民政府决定。其中，拟给予撤职、开除处分的，由本级人民政府向同级人民代表大会常务委员会提出免职建议。免去职务前，本级人民政府或者上级人民政府可以决定暂停其履行职务。

**Article 37** A sanction against a principal leading person of a department of a local people's government shall be decided by the people's government at the same level. Where a sanction of removal from office or dismissal is to be imposed, the people's government at the same level shall propose a motion of removal from office to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level. Before the removal from office, the people's government at the same level or at the next higher level may decide to suspend him or her from performing duties.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　行政机关公务员违法违纪，已经被立案调查，不宜继续履行职责的，任免机关可以决定暂停其履行职务。  
被调查的公务员在违法违纪案件立案调查期间，不得交流、出境、辞去公职或者办理退休手续。

**Article 38** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ commits an illegal or disciplinary act and has been placed on file for investigation, and it is inappropriate for him or her to continue performing duties, the appointing and removing organ may decide to suspend him or her from performing duties.  
A civil servant under investigation shall not be transferred, exit the country, resign from public office, or go through retirement procedures during the period of investigation of the illegal or disciplinary case.

### 第五章　处分的程序

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　任免机关对涉嫌违法违纪的行政机关公务员的调查、处理，按照下列程序办理：  
（一）经任免机关负责人同意，由任免机关有关部门对需要调查处理的事项进行初步调查；  
（二）任免机关有关部门经初步调查认为该公务员涉嫌违法违纪，需要进一步查证的，报任免机关负责人批准后立案；  
（三）任免机关有关部门负责对该公务员违法违纪事实做进一步调查，包括收集、查证有关证据材料，听取被调查的公务员所在单位的领导成员、有关工作人员以及所在单位监察机构的意见，向其他有关单位和人员了解情况，并形成书面调查材料，向任免机关负责人报告；  
（四）任免机关有关部门将调查认定的事实及拟给予处分的依据告知被调查的公务员本人，听取其陈述和申辩，并对其所提出的事实、理由和证据进行复核，记录在案。被调查的公务员提出的事实、理由和证据成立的，应予采信；  
（五）经任免机关领导成员集体讨论，作出对该公务员给予处分、免予处分或者撤销案件的决定；  
（六）任免机关应当将处分决定以书面形式通知受处分的公务员本人，并在一定范围内宣布；  
（七）任免机关有关部门应当将处分决定归入受处分的公务员本人档案，同时汇集有关材料形成该处分案件的工作档案。  
受处分的行政机关公务员处分期满解除处分的程序，参照前款第（五）项、第（六）项和第（七）项的规定办理。  
任免机关应当按照管理权限，及时将处分决定或者解除处分决定报公务员主管部门备案。

**Article 39** The investigation and handling of a civil servant of an administrative organ suspected of an illegal or disciplinary act by the appointing and removing organ shall be carried out in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) With the consent of the responsible person of the appointing and removing organ, the relevant department of the appointing and removing organ shall conduct a preliminary investigation into the matters requiring investigation and handling;  
(2) Where, after preliminary investigation, the relevant department of the appointing and removing organ believes that the civil servant is suspected of an illegal or disciplinary act and further verification is needed, it shall report to the responsible person of the appointing and removing organ for approval and then place the case on file;  
(3) The relevant department of the appointing and removing organ shall be responsible for further investigating the facts of the illegal or disciplinary act of the civil servant, including collecting and verifying relevant evidence materials, soliciting opinions from the leading members, relevant staff, and the supervisory body of the unit where the civil servant under investigation works, obtaining information from other relevant units and persons, forming written investigation materials, and reporting to the responsible person of the appointing and removing organ;  
(4) The relevant department of the appointing and removing organ shall inform the civil servant under investigation of the facts determined through investigation and the basis for the proposed sanction, hear his or her statements and defenses, review the facts, reasons, and evidence he or she presents, and record them. If the facts, reasons, and evidence presented by the civil servant under investigation are established, they shall be accepted;  
(5) After collective discussion by the leading members of the appointing and removing organ, a decision on imposing a sanction, exempting from sanction, or dismissing the case against the civil servant shall be made;  
(6) The appointing and removing organ shall notify the sanctioned civil servant in writing of the sanction decision and announce it within a certain scope;  
(7) The relevant department of the appointing and removing organ shall place the sanction decision in the file of the sanctioned civil servant and, at the same time, gather relevant materials to form a working file for the sanction case.  
The procedures for lifting the sanction upon expiration of the sanction period for a sanctioned civil servant of an administrative organ shall be handled by reference to the provisions of subparagraphs (5), (6), and (7) of the preceding paragraph.  
The appointing and removing organ shall, in accordance with the management authority, promptly report the sanction decision or the decision to lift the sanction to the competent department of civil servants for recordation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　监察机关对违法违纪的行政机关公务员的调查、处理，依照《中华人民共和国行政监察法》规定的程序办理。

**Article 40** The investigation and handling of a civil servant of an administrative organ who commits an illegal or disciplinary act by the supervisory organ shall be carried out in accordance with the procedures provided for in the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对行政机关公务员违法违纪案件进行调查，应当由2名以上办案人员进行；接受调查的单位和个人应当如实提供情况。  
严禁以暴力、威胁、引诱、欺骗等非法方式收集证据；非法收集的证据不得作为定案的依据。

**Article 41** An investigation into a case involving an illegal or disciplinary act of a civil servant of an administrative organ shall be conducted by two or more case handlers; the units and individuals under investigation shall truthfully provide information.  
It is strictly forbidden to collect evidence by illegal means such as violence, threats, inducement, or fraud; evidence collected illegally shall not be used as the basis for determining the case.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　参与行政机关公务员违法违纪案件调查、处理的人员有下列情形之一的，应当提出回避申请；被调查的公务员以及与案件有利害关系的公民、法人或者其他组织有权要求其回避：  
（一）与被调查的公务员是近亲属关系的；  
（二）与被调查的案件有利害关系的；  
（三）与被调查的公务员有其他关系，可能影响案件公正处理的。

**Article 42** Any person participating in the investigation or handling of a case involving an illegal or disciplinary act of a civil servant of an administrative organ who falls under any of the following circumstances shall apply for recusal; the civil servant under investigation and any citizen, legal person, or other organization with an interest in the case shall have the right to request his or her recusal:  
(1) Being a close relative of the civil servant under investigation;  
(2) Having an interest in the case under investigation;  
(3) Having another relationship with the civil servant under investigation that may affect the impartial handling of the case.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　处分决定机关负责人的回避，由处分决定机关的上一级行政机关负责人决定；其他违法违纪案件调查、处理人员的回避，由处分决定机关负责人决定。  
处分决定机关或者处分决定机关的上一级行政机关，发现违法违纪案件调查、处理人员有应当回避的情形，可以直接决定该人员回避。

**Article 43** The recusal of the responsible person of the sanction decision organ shall be decided by the responsible person of the administrative organ at the next higher level of the sanction decision organ; the recusal of other personnel investigating or handling the illegal or disciplinary case shall be decided by the responsible person of the sanction decision organ.  
Where the sanction decision organ or the administrative organ at the next higher level of the sanction decision organ finds that any personnel investigating or handling the illegal or disciplinary case has a circumstance that warrants recusal, it may directly decide on the recusal of such personnel.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　给予行政机关公务员处分，应当自批准立案之日起6个月内作出决定；案情复杂或者遇有其他特殊情形的，办案期限可以延长，但是最长不得超过12个月。

**Article 44** A decision on imposing a sanction on a civil servant of an administrative organ shall be made within six months from the date of approval to place the case on file; where the case is complex or other special circumstances exist, the case-handling period may be extended, but the extension shall not exceed twelve months at most.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　处分决定应当包括下列内容：  
（一）被处分人员的姓名、职务、级别、工作单位等基本情况；  
（二）经查证的违法违纪事实；  
（三）处分的种类和依据；  
（四）不服处分决定的申诉途径和期限；  
（五）处分决定机关的名称、印章和作出决定的日期。  
解除处分决定除包括前款第（一）项、第（二）项和第（五）项规定的内容外，还应当包括原处分的种类和解除处分的依据，以及受处分的行政机关公务员在受处分期间的表现情况。

**Article 45** A sanction decision shall include the following contents:  
(1) Basic information such as the name, position, rank, and work unit of the person sanctioned;  
(2) The verified facts of the illegal or disciplinary act;  
(3) The type and basis of the sanction;  
(4) The channels and time limit for petitioning against the sanction decision;  
(5) The name, seal, and date of decision of the sanction decision organ.  
A decision to lift the sanction shall, in addition to the contents specified in subparagraphs (1), (2), and (5) of the preceding paragraph, also include the type of the original sanction, the basis for lifting the sanction, and the performance of the sanctioned civil servant of the administrative organ during the period of sanction.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　处分决定、解除处分决定自作出之日起生效。

**Article 46** The sanction decision and the decision to lift the sanction shall take effect as of the date on which they are made.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　行政机关公务员受到开除处分后，有新工作单位的，其本人档案转由新工作单位管理；没有新工作单位的，其本人档案转由其户籍所在地人事部门所属的人才服务机构管理。

**Article 47** After a civil servant of an administrative organ is subject to a sanction of dismissal, if he or she has a new work unit, his or her personal file shall be transferred to the new work unit for management; if he or she does not have a new work unit, his or her personal file shall be transferred to the talent service institution under the personnel department of the place of his or her domicile for management.

### 第六章　不服处分的申诉

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　受到处分的行政机关公务员对处分决定不服的，依照《中华人民共和国公务员法》和《中华人民共和国行政监察法》的有关规定，可以申请复核或者申诉。  
复核、申诉期间不停止处分的执行。  
行政机关公务员不因提出复核、申诉而被加重处分。

**Article 48** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ subject to a sanction refuses to accept the sanction decision, he or she may apply for review or lodge a petition in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The execution of the sanction shall not be suspended during the period of review or petition.  
A civil servant of an administrative organ shall not be given a heavier sanction because he or she has submitted a request for review or lodged a petition.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　有下列情形之一的，受理公务员复核、申诉的机关应当撤销处分决定，重新作出决定或者责令原处分决定机关重新作出决定：  
（一）处分所依据的违法违纪事实证据不足的；  
（二）违反法定程序，影响案件公正处理的；  
（三）作出处分决定超越职权或者滥用职权的。

**Article 49** In any of the following circumstances, the organ that accepts the review or petition of a civil servant shall revoke the sanction decision and make a new decision or order the original sanction decision organ to make a new decision:  
(1) There is insufficient evidence for the facts of the illegal or disciplinary act on which the sanction is based;  
(2) The statutory procedures are violated, affecting the impartial handling of the case;  
(3) The sanction decision is made beyond the authority or by abusing power.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　有下列情形之一的，受理公务员复核、申诉的机关应当变更处分决定，或者责令原处分决定机关变更处分决定：  
（一）适用法律、法规、规章或者国务院决定错误的；  
（二）对违法违纪行为的情节认定有误的；  
（三）处分不当的。

**Article 50** In any of the following circumstances, the organ that accepts the review or petition of a civil servant shall modify the sanction decision, or order the original sanction decision organ to modify the sanction decision:  
(1) The application of laws, regulations, rules, or decisions of the State Council is incorrect;  
(2) There is an error in the determination of the circumstances of the illegal or disciplinary act;  
(3) The sanction is inappropriate.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　行政机关公务员的处分决定被变更，需要调整该公务员的职务、级别或者工资档次的，应当按照规定予以调整；行政机关公务员的处分决定被撤销的，应当恢复该公务员的级别、工资档次，按照原职务安排相应的职务，并在适当范围内为其恢复名誉。  
被撤销处分或者被减轻处分的行政机关公务员工资福利受到损失的，应当予以补偿。

**Article 51** Where the decision on a sanction against a civil servant of an administrative organ is modified, and it is necessary to adjust the post, rank or wage grade of such civil servant, an adjustment shall be made in accordance with the provisions; where the decision on a sanction against a civil servant of an administrative organ is revoked, the rank and wage grade of such civil servant shall be restored, a corresponding post shall be arranged according to the original post, and his/her reputation shall be rehabilitated within an appropriate scope.  
Where a civil servant of an administrative organ whose sanction is revoked or mitigated suffers losses in wages and benefits, compensation shall be made.

### 第七章　附 则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　有违法违纪行为应当受到处分的行政机关公务员，在处分决定机关作出处分决定前已经退休的，不再给予处分；但是，依法应当给予降级、撤职、开除处分的，应当按照规定相应降低或者取消其享受的待遇。

**Article 52** Where a civil servant of an administrative organ who has committed illegal or disciplinary violations and should be subject to a sanction has already retired before the sanction decision authority makes the sanction decision, no sanction shall be imposed; however, where a sanction of demotion, removal from office or expulsion should be imposed according to law, the treatment he/she enjoys shall be correspondingly lowered or cancelled in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　行政机关公务员违法违纪取得的财物和用于违法违纪的财物，除依法应当由其他机关没收、追缴或者责令退赔的，由处分决定机关没收、追缴或者责令退赔。违法违纪取得的财物应当退还原所有人或者原持有人的，退还原所有人或者原持有人；属于国家财产以及不应当退还或者无法退还原所有人或者原持有人的，上缴国库。

**Article 53** The property acquired through illegal or disciplinary violations by a civil servant of an administrative organ and the property used for illegal or disciplinary violations shall be confiscated, recovered or ordered to be returned by the sanction decision authority, unless it shall be confiscated, recovered or ordered to be returned by another authority according to law. Where the property acquired through illegal or disciplinary violations shall be returned to the original owner or original holder, it shall be returned to the original owner or original holder; where it is state property or should not be returned or cannot be returned to the original owner or original holder, it shall be turned over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　对法律、法规授权的具有公共事务管理职能的事业单位中经批准参照《中华人民共和国公务员法》管理的工作人员给予处分，参照本条例的有关规定办理。

**Article 54** For the sanction against the staff of a public institution authorized by laws and regulations to have public affairs management functions, who are approved to be managed with reference to the Public Servants Law of the People's Republic of China, the relevant provisions of these Regulations shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　本条例自2007年6月1日起施行。1988年9月13日国务院发布的《国家行政机关工作人员贪污贿赂行政处分暂行规定》同时废止。

**Article 55** These Regulations shall come into force as of June 1, 2007. The Interim Provisions on Administrative Sanctions for Corruption and Bribery of Staff of State Administrative Organs promulgated by the State Council on September 13, 1988 shall be abolished simultaneously.
