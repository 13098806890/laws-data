# 中华人民共和国红十字会法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2017-02-24  
**生效日期**：2017-05-08

> （1993年10月31日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第四次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正　2017年2月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　组　　织 第三章　职　　责 第四章　标志与名称 第五章　财产与监管 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保护人的生命和健康，维护人的尊严，发扬人道主义精神，促进和平进步事业，保障和规范红十字会依法履行职责，制定本法。 第二条　中国红十字会是中华人民共和国统一的红十字组织，是从事人道主义工作的社会救助团体。 第三条　中华人民共和国公民，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、宗教信仰、教育程度，承认中国红十字会章程并缴纳会费的，可以自愿参加中国红十字会。 企业、事业单位及有关团体通过申请可以成为红十字会的团体会员。 国家鼓励自然人、法人以及其他组织参与红十字志愿服务。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保护人的生命和健康，维护人的尊严，发扬人道主义精神，促进和平进步事业，保障和规范红十字会依法履行职责，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of protecting people's lives and health, safeguarding human dignity, promoting humanitarianism, advancing the cause of peace and progress, and ensuring and regulating the Red Cross Society to perform its duties according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中国红十字会是中华人民共和国统一的红十字组织，是从事人道主义工作的社会救助团体。

**Article 2** The Red Cross Society of China is a unified national Red Cross organization of the People's Republic of China and a social relief organization engaged in humanitarian work.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中华人民共和国公民，不分民族、种族、性别、职业、宗教信仰、教育程度，承认中国红十字会章程并缴纳会费的，可以自愿参加中国红十字会。  
企业、事业单位及有关团体通过申请可以成为红十字会的团体会员。  
国家鼓励自然人、法人以及其他组织参与红十字志愿服务。  
国家支持在学校开展红十字青少年工作。

**Article 3** Citizens of the People's Republic of China, regardless of nationality, race, sex, occupation, religious belief or education level, who recognize the Constitution of the Red Cross Society of China and pay membership dues, may voluntarily join the Red Cross Society of China.  
Enterprises, public institutions and relevant organizations may, through application, become group members of the Red Cross Society.  
The State encourages natural persons, legal persons and other organizations to participate in Red Cross volunteer services.  
The State supports the development of Red Cross youth work in schools.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　中国红十字会应当遵守宪法和法律，遵循国际红十字和红新月运动确立的基本原则，依照中国批准或者加入的日内瓦公约及其附加议定书和中国红十字会章程，独立自主地开展工作。  
中国红十字会全国会员代表大会依法制定或者修改中国红十字会章程，章程不得与宪法和法律相抵触。

**Article 4** The Red Cross Society of China shall abide by the Constitution and laws, follow the Fundamental Principles of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, and carry out its work independently in accordance with the Geneva Conventions and their Additional Protocols ratified or acceded to by China and the Constitution of the Red Cross Society of China.  
The National Congress of the Red Cross Society of China shall, according to law, formulate or amend the Constitution of the Red Cross Society of China, which shall not contravene the Constitution and laws.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　各级人民政府对红十字会给予支持和资助，保障红十字会依法履行职责，并对其活动进行监督。

**Article 5** People's governments at all levels shall provide support and financial assistance to the Red Cross Society, guarantee it to perform its duties according to law, and supervise its activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　中国红十字会根据独立、平等、互相尊重的原则，发展同各国红十字会和红新月会的友好合作关系。

**Article 6** The Red Cross Society of China shall develop friendly cooperative relations with the Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies of other countries on the principles of independence, equality and mutual respect.

### 第二章　组织

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全国建立中国红十字会总会。中国红十字会总会对外代表中国红十字会。  
县级以上地方按行政区域建立地方各级红十字会，根据实际工作需要配备专职工作人员。  
全国性行业根据需要可以建立行业红十字会。  
上级红十字会指导下级红十字会工作。

**Article 7** The Red Cross Society of China shall establish its headquarters nationwide. The headquarters of the Red Cross Society of China shall represent the Red Cross Society of China externally.  
Local Red Cross Societies at various levels shall be established at or above the county level according to administrative divisions, and full-time staff shall be allocated based on actual work needs.  
National trade unions may, as needed, establish trade Red Cross Societies.  
Red Cross Societies at higher levels shall guide the work of Red Cross Societies at lower levels.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　各级红十字会设立理事会、监事会。理事会、监事会由会员代表大会选举产生，向会员代表大会负责并报告工作，接受其监督。  
理事会民主选举产生会长和副会长。理事会执行会员代表大会的决议。  
执行委员会是理事会的常设执行机构，其人员组成由理事会决定，向理事会负责并报告工作。  
监事会民主推选产生监事长和副监事长。理事会、执行委员会工作受监事会监督。

**Article 8** Red Cross Societies at all levels shall establish a council and a board of supervisors. The council and the board of supervisors shall be elected by the congress of members, be responsible to and report on their work to the congress of members, and be subject to its supervision.  
The council shall democratically elect a president and vice-presidents. The council shall implement the resolutions of the congress of members.  
The executive committee shall be the permanent executive body of the council, whose composition shall be decided by the council, and which shall be responsible to and report on its work to the council.  
The board of supervisors shall democratically elect a chairperson and vice-chairpersons. The work of the council and the executive committee shall be subject to the supervision of the board of supervisors.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　中国红十字会总会可以设名誉会长和名誉副会长。名誉会长和名誉副会长由中国红十字会总会理事会聘请。

**Article 9** The Red Cross Society of China may have honorary presidents and honorary vice-presidents. The honorary presidents and honorary vice-presidents shall be invited by the Council of the Red Cross Society of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国红十字会总会具有社会团体法人资格；地方各级红十字会、行业红十字会依法取得社会团体法人资格。

**Article 10** The Red Cross Society of China has the status of a social organization legal person; local Red Cross Societies at various levels and trade Red Cross Societies shall obtain the status of a social organization legal person according to law.

### 第三章　职责

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　红十字会履行下列职责：  
（一）开展救援、救灾的相关工作，建立红十字应急救援体系。在战争、武装冲突和自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件中，对伤病人员和其他受害者提供紧急救援和人道救助；  
（二）开展应急救护培训，普及应急救护、防灾避险和卫生健康知识，组织志愿者参与现场救护；  
（三）参与、推动无偿献血、遗体和人体器官捐献工作，参与开展造血干细胞捐献的相关工作；  
（四）组织开展红十字志愿服务、红十字青少年工作；  
（五）参加国际人道主义救援工作；  
（六）宣传国际红十字和红新月运动的基本原则和日内瓦公约及其附加议定书；  
（七）依照国际红十字和红新月运动的基本原则，完成人民政府委托事宜；  
（八）依照日内瓦公约及其附加议定书的有关规定开展工作；  
（九）协助人民政府开展与其职责相关的其他人道主义服务活动。

**Article 11** The Red Cross Society shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Carry out work related to rescue and disaster relief and establish a Red Cross emergency rescue system. Provide emergency rescue and humanitarian relief to the injured, sick and other victims in wars, armed conflicts and emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters and public health incidents;  
(2) Carry out emergency first aid training, popularize knowledge on emergency first aid, disaster prevention and avoidance, and health, and organize volunteers to participate in on-site first aid;  
(3) Participate in and promote unpaid blood donation, body organ donation, and participate in the relevant work of hematopoietic stem cell donation;  
(4) Organize and carry out Red Cross voluntary services and Red Cross youth work;  
(5) Participate in international humanitarian relief work;  
(6) Publicize the Fundamental Principles of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement and the Geneva Conventions and their Additional Protocols;  
(7) Complete tasks entrusted by the people's government in accordance with the Fundamental Principles of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement;  
(8) Carry out work in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Geneva Conventions and their Additional Protocols;  
(9) Assist the people's government in carrying out other humanitarian service activities related to its functions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在战争、武装冲突和自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件等突发事件中，执行救援、救助任务并标有红十字标志的人员、物资和交通工具有优先通行的权利。

**Article 12** In wars, armed conflicts and emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters and public health incidents, personnel, materials and means of transport performing rescue and relief tasks and bearing the Red Cross emblem shall have the right of priority passage.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　任何组织和个人不得阻碍红十字会工作人员依法履行救援、救助、救护职责。

**Article 13** No organization or individual may obstruct Red Cross staff from performing their duties of rescue, relief and first aid according to law.

### 第四章　标志与名称

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　中国红十字会使用白底红十字标志。  
红十字标志具有保护作用和标明作用。  
红十字标志的保护使用，是标示在战争、武装冲突中必须受到尊重和保护的人员和设备、设施。其使用办法，依照日内瓦公约及其附加议定书的有关规定执行。  
红十字标志的标明使用，是标示与红十字活动有关的人或者物。其使用办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会依据本法规定。

**Article 14** The Red Cross Society of China shall use the red cross emblem on a white ground.  
The Red Cross emblem has both a protective function and an indicative function.  
The protective use of the Red Cross emblem is to mark personnel, equipment and facilities that must be respected and protected in wars and armed conflicts. The methods of such use shall be carried out in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Geneva Conventions and their Additional Protocols.  
The indicative use of the Red Cross emblem is to mark persons or objects related to Red Cross activities. The methods of such use shall be prescribed by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家武装力量的医疗卫生机构使用红十字标志，应当符合日内瓦公约及其附加议定书的有关规定。

**Article 15** Medical and health institutions of the national armed forces using the Red Cross emblem shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Geneva Conventions and their Additional Protocols.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　红十字标志和名称受法律保护。禁止利用红十字标志和名称牟利，禁止以任何形式冒用、滥用、篡改红十字标志和名称。

**Article 16** The Red Cross emblem and name shall be protected by law. It is prohibited to use the Red Cross emblem and name for profit-making purposes, or to impersonate, abuse or alter the Red Cross emblem and name in any form.

### 第五章　财产与监管

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　红十字会财产的主要来源：  
（一）红十字会会员缴纳的会费；  
（二）境内外组织和个人捐赠的款物；  
（三）动产和不动产的收入；  
（四）人民政府的拨款；  
（五）其他合法收入。

**Article 17** The principal sources of property of the Red Cross Society are:  
(1) Membership dues paid by Red Cross members;  
(2) Money and goods donated by domestic and foreign organizations and individuals;  
(3) Income from movable and immovable property;  
(4) Appropriations by the people's government;  
(5) Other lawful income.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家对红十字会兴办的与其宗旨相符的公益事业给予扶持。

**Article 18** The State shall provide support for the public welfare undertakings run by the Red Cross Society that are consistent with its purposes.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　红十字会可以依法进行募捐活动。募捐活动应当符合《中华人民共和国慈善法》的有关规定。

**Article 19** The Red Cross Society may conduct fundraising activities according to law. Such activities shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Charity Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　红十字会依法接受自然人、法人以及其他组织捐赠的款物，应当向捐赠人开具由财政部门统一监（印）制的公益事业捐赠票据。捐赠人匿名或者放弃接受捐赠票据的，红十字会应当做好相关记录。  
捐赠人依法享受税收优惠。

**Article 20** The Red Cross Society, when accepting donations of money or goods from natural persons, legal persons or other organizations according to law, shall issue to the donors donation receipts for public welfare undertakings uniformly supervised (printed) by the financial department. If a donor does not disclose his/her identity or waives acceptance of the donation receipt, the Red Cross Society shall keep relevant records.  
Donors shall enjoy tax preferences according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　红十字会应当按照募捐方案、捐赠人意愿或者捐赠协议处分其接受的捐赠款物。  
捐赠人有权查询、复制其捐赠财产管理使用的有关资料，红十字会应当及时主动向捐赠人反馈有关情况。  
红十字会违反募捐方案、捐赠人意愿或者捐赠协议约定的用途，滥用捐赠财产的，捐赠人有权要求其改正；拒不改正的，捐赠人可以向人民政府民政部门投诉、举报或者向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 21** The Red Cross Society shall dispose of the donated money and goods it receives in accordance with the fundraising plan, the donor's wishes or the donation agreement.  
Donors shall have the right to inquire about and copy relevant materials concerning the management and use of their donated property, and the Red Cross Society shall promptly and proactively provide feedback to the donors on the relevant circumstances.  
If the Red Cross Society violates the fundraising plan, the donor's wishes or the purpose agreed in the donation agreement and abuses donated property, the donor shall have the right to demand correction; if the Red Cross Society refuses to correct, the donor may complain or report to the civil affairs department of the people's government or institute proceedings in a People's Court.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　红十字会应当建立财务管理、内部控制、审计公开和监督检查制度。  
红十字会的财产使用应当与其宗旨相一致。  
红十字会对接受的境外捐赠款物，应当建立专项审查监督制度。  
红十字会应当及时聘请依法设立的独立第三方机构，对捐赠款物的收入和使用情况进行审计，将审计结果向红十字会理事会和监事会报告，并向社会公布。

**Article 22** The Red Cross Society shall establish systems for financial management, internal control, audit disclosure and supervision and inspection.  
The use of property of the Red Cross Society shall be consistent with its purposes.  
The Red Cross Society shall establish a special review and supervision system for donated money and goods received from outside the country.  
The Red Cross Society shall promptly engage an independent third-party institution established according to law to audit the income and use of donated money and goods, report the audit results to its Council and Board of Supervisors, and make them public.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　红十字会应当建立健全信息公开制度，规范信息发布，在统一的信息平台及时向社会公布捐赠款物的收入和使用情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 23** The Red Cross Society shall establish and improve the information disclosure system, standardize information release, and timely disclose to the public the income and use of donated money and goods on a unified information platform, subject to public supervision.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　红十字会财产的收入和使用情况依法接受人民政府审计等部门的监督。  
红十字会接受社会捐赠及其使用情况，依法接受人民政府民政部门的监督。

**Article 24** The income and use of property of the Red Cross Society shall be subject to supervision by the auditing and other departments of the people's government according to law.  
The acceptance and use of public donations by the Red Cross Society shall be subject to supervision by the civil affairs department of the people's government according to law.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　任何组织和个人不得私分、挪用、截留或者侵占红十字会的财产。

**Article 25** No organization or individual may distribute, misappropriate, intercept or embezzle the property of the Red Cross Society.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　红十字会及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，由同级人民政府审计、民政等部门责令改正；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；造成损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）违背募捐方案、捐赠人意愿或者捐赠协议，擅自处分其接受的捐赠款物的；  
（二）私分、挪用、截留或者侵占财产的；  
（三）未依法向捐赠人反馈情况或者开具捐赠票据的；  
（四）未依法对捐赠款物的收入和使用情况进行审计的；  
（五）未依法公开信息的；  
（六）法律、法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 26** Where the Red Cross Society or its staff members commit any of the following acts, the auditing, civil affairs and other departments of the people's government at the same level shall order correction; if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if damage is caused, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Violating the fundraising plan, the donor's wishes or the donation agreement by disposing of the donated money and goods received without authorization;  
(2) Distributing, misappropriating, intercepting or embezzling property;  
(3) Failing to provide feedback to donors or issue donation receipts according to law;  
(4) Failing to audit the income and use of donated money and goods according to law;  
(5) Failing to disclose information according to law;  
(6) Other circumstances provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　自然人、法人或者其他组织有下列情形之一，造成损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）冒用、滥用、篡改红十字标志和名称的；  
（二）利用红十字标志和名称牟利的；  
（三）制造、发布、传播虚假信息，损害红十字会名誉的；  
（四）盗窃、损毁或者以其他方式侵害红十字会财产的；  
（五）阻碍红十字会工作人员依法履行救援、救助、救护职责的；  
（六）法律、法规规定的其他情形。  
红十字会及其工作人员有前款第一项、第二项所列行为的，按照前款规定处罚。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/社会民政/中华人民共和国红十字标志使用办法.md#art-23" title="被《中华人民共和国红十字标志使用办法》第23条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Where a natural person, legal person or other organization commits any of the following acts, causing damage, civil liability shall be borne according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Impersonating, abusing or altering the Red Cross emblem and name;  
(2) Using the Red Cross emblem and name for profit-making purposes;  
(3) Fabricating, publishing or disseminating false information, damaging the reputation of the Red Cross Society;  
(4) Stealing, damaging or otherwise infringing upon the property of the Red Cross Society;  
(5) Obstructing Red Cross staff from performing their duties of rescue, relief and first aid according to law;  
(6) Other circumstances provided by laws and regulations.  
Where the Red Cross Society or its staff members commit acts listed in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, they shall be penalized in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　各级人民政府有关部门及其工作人员在实施监督管理中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 28** Where the relevant departments of the people's government at various levels and their staff members abuse their powers, neglect their duties or engage in malpractice for personal gain in the exercise of supervision and management, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本法所称“国际红十字和红新月运动确立的基本原则”，是指一九八六年十月日内瓦国际红十字大会第二十五次会议通过的“国际红十字和红新月运动章程”中确立的人道、公正、中立、独立、志愿服务、统一和普遍七项基本原则。  
本法所称“日内瓦公约”，是指中国批准的于一九四九年八月十二日订立的日内瓦四公约，即：《改善战地武装部队伤者病者境遇之日内瓦公约》、《改善海上武装部队伤者病者及遇船难者境遇之日内瓦公约》、《关于战俘待遇之日内瓦公约》和《关于战时保护平民之日内瓦公约》。  
本法所称日内瓦公约“附加议定书”，是指中国加入的于一九七七年六月八日订立的《一九四九年八月十二日日内瓦四公约关于保护国际性武装冲突受难者的附加议定书》和《一九四九年八月十二日日内瓦四公约关于保护非国际性武装冲突受难者的附加议定书》。

**Article 29** For the purposes of this Law, the term "Fundamental Principles of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement" refers to the seven fundamental principles of humanity, impartiality, neutrality, independence, voluntary service, unity and universality established in the "Statutes of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement" adopted at the 25th International Conference of the Red Cross in Geneva in October 1986.  
For the purposes of this Law, the term "Geneva Conventions" refers to the four Geneva Conventions concluded on August 12, 1949, which China has ratified, namely: the Geneva Convention for the Amelioration of the Condition of the Wounded and Sick in Armed Forces in the Field, the Geneva Convention for the Amelioration of the Condition of Wounded, Sick and Shipwrecked Members of Armed Forces at Sea, the Geneva Convention relative to the Treatment of Prisoners of War, and the Geneva Convention relative to the Protection of Civilian Persons in Time of War.  
For the purposes of this Law, the term "Additional Protocols" to the Geneva Conventions refers to the Protocol Additional to the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949, and relating to the Protection of Victims of International Armed Conflicts (Protocol I) and the Protocol Additional to the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949, and relating to the Protection of Victims of Non-International Armed Conflicts (Protocol II), concluded on June 8, 1977, which China has acceded to.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本法自2017年5月8日起施行。

**Article 30** This Law shall come into force as of May 8, 2017.
