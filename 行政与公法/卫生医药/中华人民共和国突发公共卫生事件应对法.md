# 中华人民共和国突发公共卫生事件应对法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2025-09-12  
**生效日期**：2025-11-01

> （2025年9月12日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十七次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　管理与指挥体制 第三章　预防与应急准备 第四章　监测预警 第五章　应急处置 第六章　保障措施 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了预防和减少突发公共卫生事件的发生，控制、减轻和消除突发公共卫生事件的危害，提高突发公共卫生事件应对能力，规范突发公共卫生事件应对活动，保障公众生命安全和身体健康，维护国家安全、公共安全和社会秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称突发公共卫生事件，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成公众生命安全和身体健康严重损害，需要采取应急处置措施予以应对的重大传染病疫情、群体性不明原因疾病、群体性中毒以及其他严重影响公众生命安全和身体健康的事件。 突发公共卫生事件的应对，适用本法；《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》对重大传染病疫情的应对作出专门规定的，适用其规定。上述法律没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定。 突发公共卫生事件的分级标准由国务院卫生健康主管部门会同国务院疾病预防控制等部门制定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了预防和减少突发公共卫生事件的发生，控制、减轻和消除突发公共卫生事件的危害，提高突发公共卫生事件应对能力，规范突发公共卫生事件应对活动，保障公众生命安全和身体健康，维护国家安全、公共安全和社会秩序，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution for the purposes of preventing and reducing the occurrence of public health emergencies, controlling, mitigating and eliminating the hazards of public health emergencies, enhancing the capacity to respond to public health emergencies, regulating response activities for public health emergencies, and safeguarding public life safety and health, and maintaining national security, public security and social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称突发公共卫生事件，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成公众生命安全和身体健康严重损害，需要采取应急处置措施予以应对的重大传染病疫情、群体性不明原因疾病、群体性中毒以及其他严重影响公众生命安全和身体健康的事件。  
突发公共卫生事件的应对，适用本法；《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》对重大传染病疫情的应对作出专门规定的，适用其规定。上述法律没有规定的，适用《中华人民共和国突发事件应对法》的规定。  
突发公共卫生事件的分级标准由国务院卫生健康主管部门会同国务院疾病预防控制等部门制定。

**Article 2** "Public health emergency" as used in this Law means the outbreak of a major infectious disease, a mass disease of unknown cause, mass poisoning, or any other event that seriously endangers or may seriously endanger public life safety and health, which requires the adoption of emergency response measures.  
Response to public health emergencies shall be governed by this Law; where the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases provides special provisions on the response to major infectious disease epidemics, those provisions shall apply. Where no provisions are provided in the said Law, the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Emergency Response shall apply.  
The classification standards for public health emergencies shall be formulated by the competent health department of the State Council jointly with the disease prevention and control department of the State Council and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　突发公共卫生事件应对工作坚持中国共产党的领导，贯彻总体国家安全观，统筹发展和安全，坚持人民至上、生命至上。  
突发公共卫生事件应对工作遵循预防为主、预防与应急相结合的原则，坚持依法应对、科学应对。

**Article 3** Work on responding to public health emergencies shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, implement the holistic approach to national security, coordinate development and security, and persist in putting the people and life above all else.  
Work on responding to public health emergencies shall follow the principle of prevention first, combining prevention with emergency response, and adhere to response based on law and science.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将突发公共卫生事件应对工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，加强公共卫生风险防控和治理体系建设，提高应对突发公共卫生事件的能力。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate work on responding to public health emergencies into national economic and social development plans, strengthen the construction of public health risk prevention and control and governance systems, and improve the capacity to respond to public health emergencies.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家建立应对突发公共卫生事件的社会动员机制，组织动员单位和个人依法有序参与突发公共卫生事件应对工作。  
国家开展突发公共卫生事件应对知识和相关法律法规宣传教育工作，增强公众公共卫生风险防范意识、提高防范能力。

**Article 5** The State shall establish a social mobilization mechanism for responding to public health emergencies, and organize and mobilize entities and individuals to participate in work on responding to public health emergencies according to law and in an orderly manner.  
The State shall conduct publicity and education on knowledge of public health emergency response and relevant laws and regulations to enhance the public's awareness of public health risk prevention and improve prevention capability.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家坚持中西医并重，加强中西医结合，充分发挥中医药在突发公共卫生事件应对中的作用。

**Article 6** The State shall adhere to the equal importance of traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, strengthen the integration of traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine, and give full play to the role of traditional Chinese medicine in responding to public health emergencies.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家支持和鼓励开展公共卫生应急领域的科学研究和技术创新，加强公共卫生应急科研攻关体系和能力建设，推广应用相关科研成果和先进技术。  
国家支持和鼓励在突发公共卫生事件应对中运用现代信息技术等先进技术。

**Article 7** The State shall support and encourage scientific research and technological innovation in the field of public health emergency response, strengthen the construction of public health emergency scientific research and technical system and capacity building, and promote the application of relevant scientific research results and advanced technologies.  
The State shall support and encourage the use of modern information technology and other advanced technologies in responding to public health emergencies.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　突发公共卫生事件应对中，收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供、公开、删除个人信息的，应当遵守《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》等法律、行政法规的规定，遵循合法、正当、必要和诚信原则，采取必要措施确保个人信息安全，不得非法收集、使用、加工、传输他人个人信息，不得非法买卖、提供或者公开他人个人信息；涉及敏感个人信息的，应当采取严格的保护措施。突发公共卫生事件应对结束后，应当及时删除为应对突发公共卫生事件所处理的个人信息。

**Article 8** In the response to public health emergencies, the collection, storage, use, processing, transmission, provision, disclosure, and deletion of personal information shall comply with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations such as the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, follow the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity, and good faith, take necessary measures to ensure the security of personal information, and shall not illegally collect, use, process, or transmit others' personal information, nor illegally trade, provide, or disclose others' personal information; where sensitive personal information is involved, strict protective measures shall be taken. After the public health emergency response ends, the personal information processed for the purpose of responding to the public health emergency shall be deleted in a timely manner.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　采取突发公共卫生事件应对措施，应当依照法定权限和程序，与突发公共卫生事件的性质和可能造成危害的程度、范围等相适应；有多种措施可供选择的，应当选择有利于最大程度保护单位和个人合法权益，且对他人权益损害和生产生活影响较小的措施，并根据情况变化及时调整。

**Article 9** When adopting measures to respond to a public health emergency, the measures shall be taken in accordance with statutory authority and procedures and shall be commensurate with the nature of the public health emergency and the degree and extent of harm it may cause; where multiple measures are available, the measure that is most conducive to protecting the lawful rights and interests of entities and individuals and that causes less damage to the rights and interests of others and less impact on production and life shall be chosen, and adjustments shall be made in a timely manner according to changes in circumstances.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持和鼓励开展应对突发公共卫生事件的国际交流与合作。

**Article 10** The State shall support and encourage international exchanges and cooperation in responding to public health emergencies.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在突发公共卫生事件应对工作中做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to work on responding to public health emergencies shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　管理与指挥体制

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　突发公共卫生事件应对工作实行党委领导、政府负责、部门协同联动、社会共同参与、属地管理和分类管理相结合的管理体制，明确属地、部门、单位和个人责任，加强公共卫生应急指挥能力建设。

**Article 12** The management system for work on responding to public health emergencies shall implement the combination of Party committee leadership, government responsibility, departmental coordination and linkage, social participation, territorial management, and classification management, clarify the responsibilities of territories, departments, entities, and individuals, and strengthen the construction of public health emergency command capacity.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　县级人民政府对本行政区域突发公共卫生事件应对工作负责；涉及两个以上行政区域的，由有关行政区域共同的上一级人民政府负责，或者由各有关行政区域的上一级人民政府共同负责。  
突发公共卫生事件发生后，发生地县级人民政府应当立即开展应对工作，同时向上一级人民政府报告，必要时可以越级上报。突发公共卫生事件发生地县级人民政府不能消除或者不能有效控制突发公共卫生事件危害的，上级人民政府应当及时采取支持措施，必要时统一领导应对工作。  
行政区域毗邻的县级以上地方人民政府应当建立应对突发公共卫生事件的协同机制，加强信息通报，开展风险会商，协调应对措施。

**Article 13** The people's government at the county level shall be responsible for work on responding to public health emergencies within its administrative region; where two or more administrative regions are involved, the common higher-level people's government of the relevant administrative regions shall be responsible, or the respective higher-level people's governments of the relevant administrative regions shall be jointly responsible.  
After a public health emergency occurs, the people's government at the county level where the emergency occurs shall immediately carry out response work and simultaneously report to the higher-level people's government, and may skip levels to report if necessary. Where the people's government at the county level where the public health emergency occurs cannot eliminate or effectively control the hazards of the public health emergency, the higher-level people's government shall take supporting measures in a timely manner and, if necessary, uniformly lead the response work.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level with adjacent administrative regions shall establish a coordination mechanism for responding to public health emergencies, strengthen information notification, conduct risk consultations, and coordinate response measures.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　县级以上地方人民政府设立由本级人民政府主要负责人、有关部门负责人、参与突发公共卫生事件应对的中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队有关单位负责人等组成的突发公共卫生事件应急指挥机构，组织、协调、指挥本级人民政府各有关部门和下级人民政府开展突发公共卫生事件应对工作。  
国务院根据应对突发公共卫生事件的需要设立国家突发公共卫生事件应急指挥机构，领导突发公共卫生事件应对工作；必要时，国务院可以派出工作组指导有关工作。  
突发公共卫生事件应急指挥机构开展突发公共卫生事件应对工作，可以依照本级人民政府的权限发布有关突发公共卫生事件应对的决定、命令、措施。  
突发公共卫生事件应急指挥机构根据工作需要组建专家组，为突发公共卫生事件的形势研判、应急处置等提供咨询意见。

**Article 14** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a public health emergency command agency composed of the principal person in charge of the people's government at the same level, the person in charge of relevant departments, and the person in charge of relevant units of the People's Liberation Army and the People's Armed Police Force participating in the response to the public health emergency, to organize, coordinate, and direct the relevant departments of the people's government at the same level and the lower-level people's government to carry out work on responding to the public health emergency.  
The State Council shall establish a national public health emergency command agency according to the needs of responding to public health emergencies to lead the work on responding to public health emergencies; if necessary, the State Council may send a working group to guide relevant work.  
When carrying out work on responding to a public health emergency, the public health emergency command agency may, within the authority of the people's government at the same level, issue decisions, orders, and measures concerning the response to the public health emergency.  
The public health emergency command agency shall, based on work needs, form an expert group to provide advisory opinions on the assessment of the situation and emergency response to the public health emergency.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院卫生健康主管部门牵头组织协调全国重大传染病疫情应对工作，负责全国其他突发公共卫生事件应对工作。国务院疾病预防控制部门负责全国重大传染病疫情应对相关工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责突发公共卫生事件应对有关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康主管部门牵头组织协调本行政区域重大传染病疫情应对工作，负责本行政区域其他突发公共卫生事件应对工作。县级以上地方人民政府疾病预防控制部门负责本行政区域重大传染病疫情应对相关工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内负责突发公共卫生事件应对有关工作。

**Article 15** The competent health department of the State Council shall take the lead in organizing and coordinating nationwide response work for major infectious disease epidemics and shall be responsible for nationwide response work for other public health emergencies. The disease prevention and control department of the State Council shall be responsible for relevant work for nationwide response to major infectious disease epidemics. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall be responsible for relevant work on responding to public health emergencies within their respective duties.  
The competent health departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall take the lead in organizing and coordinating response work for major infectious disease epidemics within their respective administrative regions and shall be responsible for response work for other public health emergencies within their respective administrative regions. The disease prevention and control departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant work for response to major infectious disease epidemics within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant work on responding to public health emergencies within their respective duties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　街道办事处、乡镇人民政府应当指导居民委员会、村民委员会协助开展突发公共卫生事件应对有关工作，推动居民委员会、村民委员会与基层医疗卫生机构协同联动。  
居民委员会、村民委员会协助做好应对突发公共卫生事件的宣传教育和健康提示，组织城乡居民依法有序参与城乡社区的突发公共卫生事件应对活动。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当为城乡社区开展突发公共卫生事件应对有关工作提供必要的支持和保障，加强基层公共卫生应急能力建设。

**Article 16** Sub-district offices and township people's governments shall guide residents' committees and villagers' committees to assist in carrying out relevant work on responding to public health emergencies, and promote coordination and linkage between residents' committees, villagers' committees, and primary-level medical and health institutions.  
Residents' committees and villagers' committees shall assist in carrying out publicity and education and health tips for responding to public health emergencies, and organize urban and rural residents to participate in public health emergency response activities in urban and rural communities according to law and in an orderly manner.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall provide necessary support and guarantees for urban and rural communities to carry out relevant work on responding to public health emergencies, and strengthen the construction of primary-level public health emergency response capacity.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　单位和个人应当支持突发公共卫生事件应对工作，执行依法采取的突发公共卫生事件应对措施。  
各级人民政府应当提供便利措施，引导单位和个人依法有序参与应对突发公共卫生事件的宣传教育、信息报告、志愿服务等活动。  
志愿服务组织等应当在县级以上地方人民政府的统筹协调、有序引导下依法开展应对突发公共卫生事件的救助、志愿服务等活动。

**Article 17** Entities and individuals shall support work on responding to public health emergencies and implement measures adopted for responding to public health emergencies according to law.  
People's governments at all levels shall provide convenient measures to guide entities and individuals to participate in an orderly manner according to law in activities such as publicity and education, information reporting, and voluntary services for responding to public health emergencies.  
Voluntary service organizations and others shall, under the overall coordination and orderly guidance of local people's governments at or above the county level, carry out relief and voluntary services for responding to public health emergencies according to law.

### 第三章　预防与应急准备

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家加强突发公共卫生事件风险源头管理，减少突发公共卫生事件的发生，减轻突发公共卫生事件的危害。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当组织卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和其他有关部门对本行政区域可能引发突发公共卫生事件的风险源进行调查、登记和风险评估，对风险源的管理使用情况开展监督检查。  
可能引发突发公共卫生事件的风险源的管理使用单位应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定加强风险管理，采取防范措施，消除引发突发公共卫生事件的隐患。

**Article 18** The State shall strengthen the management of the sources of public health emergency risks, reduce the occurrence of public health emergencies, and mitigate the hazards of public health emergencies.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the competent health departments, disease prevention and control departments, and other relevant departments to investigate, register, and assess the risks of sources that may trigger public health emergencies within their administrative regions, and conduct supervision and inspection on the management and use of the risk sources.  
Entities that manage and use risk sources that may trigger public health emergencies shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, strengthen risk management, take preventive measures, and eliminate hidden dangers that may trigger public health emergencies.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家建立健全突发公共卫生事件应急预案体系。  
国务院组织制定国家突发公共卫生事件应急预案。县级以上地方人民政府根据上级人民政府的突发公共卫生事件应急预案和本行政区域的实际情况，组织制定本行政区域的突发公共卫生事件应急预案，并按照国家有关规定报送备案。县级以上人民政府卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门依据各自职责制定本部门的突发公共卫生事件应急预案，并按照国家有关规定报送备案。  
突发公共卫生事件应急预案应当根据本法和其他有关法律、法规的规定，针对突发公共卫生事件的性质、可能造成的危害，规定突发公共卫生事件应对的组织指挥体系、监测预警、信息报告和通报、应急处置、人员调集、物资储备和调用、信息发布等内容。应急预案制定机关应当广泛听取有关部门、单位、专家和社会各方面意见，增强应急预案的科学性、针对性和可操作性，并根据实际需要、情势变化、应急演练中发现的问题等及时修订。  
县级以上人民政府应当根据突发公共卫生事件应急预案组织开展演练。

**Article 19** The State shall establish and improve the public health emergency response plan system.  
The State Council shall organize the formulation of a national public health emergency response plan. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the public health emergency response plan of the higher-level people's government and the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, organize the formulation of public health emergency response plans for their administrative regions and file them for record in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State. The health, disease prevention and control, and other relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall, based on their respective duties, formulate public health emergency response plans for their departments and file them for record in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Public health emergency response plans shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws and regulations, and in view of the nature of the public health emergency and the harm it may cause, stipulate the organizational command system, monitoring and early warning, information reporting and notification, emergency response, personnel mobilization, material reserves and deployment, and information release for responding to public health emergencies. The entity formulating the plan shall extensively solicit opinions from relevant departments, entities, experts, and all sectors of society to enhance the scientific nature, targetedness, and operability of the plan, and shall revise it in a timely manner based on actual needs, changes in circumstances, and problems discovered during emergency drills.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize drills in accordance with public health emergency response plans.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　下列单位应当评估本单位发生突发公共卫生事件的风险，针对可能发生的突发公共卫生事件，制定相应的应急预案，并组织开展演练：  
（一）医疗卫生机构；  
（二）学校、托育机构；  
（三）养老机构、康复机构、福利机构、未成年人救助保护机构、救助管理机构；  
（四）大型体育场馆；  
（五）监管场所；  
（六）车站、港口、机场；  
（七）可能引发突发公共卫生事件的风险源的管理使用单位；  
（八）法律、行政法规以及国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门规定的其他应当制定突发公共卫生事件应急预案的单位。  
鼓励前款规定以外的其他单位根据本单位的实际情况制定突发公共卫生事件应急预案，并组织开展演练。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门应当对有关单位制定突发公共卫生事件应急预案、组织开展演练予以指导。

**Article 20** The following entities shall assess the risks of a public health emergency occurring within their entities, formulate corresponding emergency response plans for possible public health emergencies, and organize drills:  
(1) medical and health institutions;  
(2) schools and childcare institutions;  
(3) elderly care institutions, rehabilitation institutions, welfare institutions, juvenile rescue and protection institutions, and rescue management institutions;  
(4) large sports venues;  
(5) places of supervision;  
(6) stations, ports, and airports;  
(7) entities that manage and use risk sources that may trigger public health emergencies;  
(8) other entities that shall formulate public health emergency response plans as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and the competent health department and disease prevention and control department of the State Council.  
Entities other than those specified in the preceding paragraph are encouraged to formulate public health emergency response plans based on their actual conditions and organize drills.  
The competent health departments and disease prevention and control departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide guidance to relevant entities on formulating public health emergency response plans and organizing drills.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将突发公共卫生事件临时应急处置场所建设纳入国土空间规划等相关规划，依法推进公共设施平急两用改造。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当完善大型体育场馆、会展场馆、市民活动中心、学校等公共设施，根据公共卫生应急需要预留改造条件，便于紧急需要时迅速改造用于突发公共卫生事件应急处置。

**Article 21** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of temporary public health emergency response sites into relevant plans such as territorial spatial planning, and promote the dual-use (peacetime and emergency) transformation of public facilities according to law.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall improve public facilities such as large sports venues, exhibition centers, civic activity centers, and schools, and reserve renovation conditions based on public health emergency needs to facilitate rapid renovation for public health emergency response when urgently needed.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　疾病预防控制机构应当提高突发公共卫生事件监测预警、流行病学调查、检验检测和应急处置能力，配备与其职责相适应的专职人员。

**Article 22** Disease prevention and control institutions shall improve their capabilities in public health emergency monitoring and early warning, epidemiological investigation, inspection and testing, and emergency response, and shall be staffed with full-time personnel commensurate with their duties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　二级以上医疗机构应当指定科室、配备专职人员承担卫生应急工作，组建本机构的卫生应急队伍。基层医疗卫生机构应当指定人员负责卫生应急工作。  
医疗机构应当优化诊区布局和诊疗流程，科学规划应急医疗救治床位和设施、设备、物资储备，做好突发公共卫生事件应急医疗救治准备工作。

**Article 23** Medical institutions at or above the second level shall designate departments and be staffed with full-time personnel to undertake health emergency work and establish their own health emergency teams. Primary-level medical and health institutions shall designate personnel to be responsible for health emergency work.  
Medical institutions shall optimize the layout of consultation areas and diagnostic and treatment processes, scientifically plan emergency medical treatment beds, facilities, equipment, and material reserves, and make preparations for emergency medical treatment for public health emergencies.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家建立健全突发公共卫生事件应急医疗救治体系。县级以上地方人民政府应当确定各类突发公共卫生事件的定点救治医院，形成由定点救治医院、其他医院和基层医疗卫生机构、院前急救机构、临时性救治场所、血站等组成的突发公共卫生事件应急医疗救治网络。

**Article 24** The State shall establish and improve the emergency medical treatment system for public health emergencies. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall designate designated treatment hospitals for various types of public health emergencies, and form an emergency medical treatment network for public health emergencies composed of designated treatment hospitals, other hospitals, primary-level medical and health institutions, pre-hospital first aid institutions, temporary treatment sites, and blood stations.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门、中医药管理部门应当加强卫生应急队伍建设，组建包括形势研判、流行病学调查、医疗救治、检验检测、消毒处理、社区指导、心理援助、物资调配等领域人员的卫生应急队伍，定期组织开展演练。

**Article 25** The competent health departments, disease prevention and control departments, and traditional Chinese medicine administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of health emergency teams, form health emergency teams including personnel in fields such as situation assessment, epidemiological investigation, medical treatment, inspection and testing, disinfection treatment, community guidance, psychological assistance, and material deployment, and regularly organize drills.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家采取措施，加强公共卫生应急领域人才培养。  
县级以上人民政府应当组织开展突发公共卫生事件应对的培训工作，提高人民政府及其有关部门负有突发公共卫生事件应对职责的工作人员以及居民委员会、村民委员会有关人员突发公共卫生事件应对能力。  
县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门、中医药管理部门应当将突发公共卫生事件应对理论、知识和技能纳入医疗卫生人员继续教育内容。

**Article 26** The State shall take measures to strengthen the training of personnel in the field of public health emergency response.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall organize training on public health emergency response to improve the public health emergency response capability of personnel of the people's government and its relevant departments responsible for public health emergency response duties, as well as relevant personnel of residents' committees and villagers' committees.  
The competent health departments, disease prevention and control departments, and traditional Chinese medicine administration departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate public health emergency response theories, knowledge, and skills into the continuing education content of medical and health personnel.

### 第四章　监测预警

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　国家建立健全多点触发、反应快速、权威高效的突发公共卫生事件监测预警体系。

**Article 27** The State shall establish and improve a multi-point triggering, rapid response, authoritative and efficient public health emergency monitoring and early warning system.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家加强突发公共卫生事件监测，建立突发公共卫生事件监测哨点，多途径、多渠道开展监测，建立智慧化多点触发机制，提高监测的敏感性和准确性，及时发现突发公共卫生事件。  
县级以上地方人民政府组织建立覆盖医疗卫生机构、学校、托育机构、养老机构、检验检测机构、药品经营企业、互联网健康服务企业、车站、港口、机场、饮用水供水单位、食品集中交易市场、物流仓储中心、医疗和生活污水处理单位、医疗废物处置单位等的突发公共卫生事件监测哨点网络，收集患者就医症状、群体性不明原因疾病、异常健康事件等突发公共卫生事件相关信息。监测哨点应当按照规定报告信息，配合疾病预防控制机构开展突发公共卫生事件监测工作。  
疾病预防控制机构负责开展突发公共卫生事件监测工作，收集、分析、报告突发公共卫生事件监测信息。

**Article 28** The State shall strengthen the monitoring of public health emergencies, establish monitoring sentinel points for public health emergencies, conduct monitoring through multiple channels and methods, establish an intelligent multi-point triggering mechanism, improve the sensitivity and accuracy of monitoring, and promptly detect public health emergencies.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize the establishment of a monitoring sentinel point network covering medical and health institutions, schools, childcare institutions, elderly care institutions, inspection and testing institutions, pharmaceutical trading enterprises, internet health service enterprises, stations, ports, airports, drinking water supply units, centralized food trading markets, logistics and warehousing centers, medical and domestic sewage treatment units, and medical waste disposal units, to collect information related to public health emergencies such as patient symptoms, mass diseases of unknown cause, and abnormal health events. Monitoring sentinel points shall report information as required and cooperate with disease prevention and control institutions in carrying out public health emergency monitoring.  
Disease prevention and control institutions shall be responsible for carrying out public health emergency monitoring, collecting, analyzing, and reporting public health emergency monitoring information.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家建立跨部门、跨地域的突发公共卫生事件监测信息共享机制，加强卫生健康、疾病预防控制、生态环境、农业农村、海关、市场监督管理、移民管理、林业草原等部门的联动监测和信息共享。

**Article 29** The State shall establish a cross-departmental and cross-regional information sharing mechanism for public health emergency monitoring, and strengthen linked monitoring and information sharing among departments such as health, disease prevention and control, ecological environment, agriculture and rural affairs, customs, market supervision and administration, immigration management, and forestry and grassland.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家建立健全突发公共卫生事件报告制度。国务院卫生健康主管部门会同国务院疾病预防控制部门制定突发公共卫生事件报告规范。  
医疗卫生机构及其执行职务的人员发现突发公共卫生事件报告规范规定应当报告的有关情形，以及发现发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件时，应当于两小时内通过网络直报系统进行报告；不具备网络直报条件的，应当通过电话、传真等方式向所在地疾病预防控制机构报告。  
检验检测机构发现突发公共卫生事件报告规范规定应当报告的有关情形，以及发现发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件时，应当于两小时内向所在地疾病预防控制机构报告。  
有关单位发现本单位发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件时，应当立即采取措施防止危害扩大，并立即向所在地疾病预防控制机构和有关行业主管部门报告。

**Article 30** The State shall establish and improve a public health emergency reporting system. The competent health department of the State Council, together with the disease prevention and control department of the State Council, shall formulate specifications for public health emergency reporting.  
Medical and health institutions and their personnel on duty who discover the relevant circumstances that should be reported as specified in the public health emergency reporting specifications, or who discover that a public health emergency has occurred or may occur, shall report through the online direct reporting system within two hours; where online direct reporting conditions are not available, they shall report to the local disease prevention and control institution by telephone, fax, or other means.  
Inspection and testing institutions that discover the relevant circumstances that should be reported as specified in the public health emergency reporting specifications, or who discover that a public health emergency has occurred or may occur, shall report to the local disease prevention and control institution within two hours.  
Relevant entities that discover that a public health emergency has occurred or may occur within their entities shall immediately take measures to prevent the expansion of harm and immediately report to the local disease prevention and control institution and relevant industry competent department.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　任何单位和个人发现发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件时，应当立即通过热线电话等渠道向所在地人民政府、有关主管部门或者疾病预防控制机构报告。

**Article 31** Any entity or individual that discovers that a public health emergency has occurred or may occur shall immediately report to the local people's government, relevant competent department, or disease prevention and control institution through hotlines or other channels.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　对单位和个人的报告，经调查不属于突发公共卫生事件的，报告的单位和个人不承担法律责任。

**Article 32** Where a report made by an entity or individual is determined after investigation not to be a public health emergency, the reporting entity or individual shall not bear legal liability.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　疾病预防控制机构接到突发公共卫生事件报告后，应当立即进行调查、核实。  
疾病预防控制机构在监测中发现或者接到报告并开展调查、核实后发现发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件的，应当立即向同级卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和上级疾病预防控制机构报告。卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门接到报告后，应当立即报告本级人民政府，同时报告上一级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门和国务院卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门，并通报可能受到影响地区的同级人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门。

**Article 33** Upon receiving a report of a public health emergency, a disease prevention and control institution shall immediately conduct investigation and verification.  
Where a disease prevention and control institution discovers, during monitoring or upon receiving a report and after investigation and verification, that a public health emergency has occurred or may occur, it shall immediately report to the competent health department, disease prevention and control department at the same level, and the higher-level disease prevention and control institution. The competent health department or disease prevention and control department, upon receiving the report, shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level, and simultaneously report to the competent health department and disease prevention and control department of the higher-level people's government, and to the competent health department and disease prevention and control department of the State Council, and notify the competent health department and disease prevention and control department of the people's government at the same level of the areas that may be affected.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门，医疗卫生机构及其执行职务的人员、检验检测机构以及发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件的单位，应当依照本法、突发公共卫生事件报告规范及时、准确报告。任何单位或者个人不得干预突发公共卫生事件报告。

**Article 34** The competent health departments and disease prevention and control departments, medical and health institutions and their personnel on duty, inspection and testing institutions, and entities where a public health emergency has occurred or may occur shall report promptly and accurately in accordance with this Law and the public health emergency reporting specifications. No entity or individual may interfere with public health emergency reporting.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　疾病预防控制机构发现可能发生突发公共卫生事件，经评估认为需要发布预警的，向同级卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门报告时应当同时提出发布预警的建议。卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门收到建议后，应当立即组织专家进行分析研判；需要发布预警的，应当立即报告本级人民政府。人民政府应当及时决定是否发布预警；发布预警的，应当同时报告上一级人民政府，必要时可以越级上报，并通报可能受到影响地区的同级人民政府；需要上级人民政府采取防范措施的，应当同时提出建议。

**Article 35** Where a disease prevention and control institution finds that a public health emergency may occur and, after assessment, considers it necessary to issue a warning, it shall, when reporting to the competent health department and disease prevention and control department at the same level, also propose to issue a warning. Upon receiving the proposal, the competent health department and disease prevention and control department shall immediately organize experts to conduct analysis and assessment; where it is necessary to issue a warning, they shall immediately report to the people's government at the same level. The people's government shall decide in a timely manner whether to issue a warning; if a warning is issued, it shall simultaneously report to the higher-level people's government, may skip levels to report if necessary, and notify the people's government at the same level of the areas that may be affected; where it is necessary for the higher-level people's government to take preventive measures, it shall also propose accordingly.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　县级以上人民政府发布预警，应当根据可能发生的突发公共卫生事件的性质、可能造成的危害，采取下列一项或者多项措施：  
（一）启动应急预案；  
（二）组织有关部门、疾病预防控制机构和专家对事态发展进行监测、分析，研判突发公共卫生事件发生的可能性、影响范围、危害和级别；  
（三）责令医疗卫生机构做好应对突发公共卫生事件的准备，责令卫生应急队伍进入待命状态；  
（四）准备临时应急处置场所，调集应急处置所需的设备、物资、交通工具；  
（五）向社会发布突发公共卫生事件预测信息以及避免或者减轻危害的建议，公布咨询或者求助电话等联络方式和渠道；  
（六）疏散、转移并妥善安置易受突发公共卫生事件危害的人员，关闭可能发生突发公共卫生事件的公共场所或者采取限制人员聚集的措施；  
（七）法律、法规、规章规定的其他必要的防范性、保护性措施。  
县级以上人民政府应当根据事态发展，适时调整预警措施；发生突发公共卫生事件的风险消除后，应当及时宣布解除预警，并解除已经采取的预警措施；发生突发公共卫生事件的，应当立即启动应急响应。

**Article 36** Where a people's government at or above the county level issues a warning, it may, based on the nature of the possible public health emergency and the harm it may cause, take one or more of the following measures:  
(1) initiate an emergency response plan;  
(2) organize relevant departments, disease prevention and control institutions, and experts to monitor and analyze the development of the situation, and assess the possibility, scope, hazard, and level of the public health emergency;  
(3) order medical and health institutions to prepare for responding to the public health emergency, and order health emergency teams to be on standby;  
(4) prepare temporary emergency response sites, and deploy equipment, materials, and means of transport needed for emergency response;  
(5) release predictive information on the public health emergency and suggestions for avoiding or mitigating harm to the public, and publish contact methods and channels such as consultation or assistance hotlines;  
(6) evacuate, relocate, and properly accommodate persons vulnerable to the public health emergency, and close public places where a public health emergency may occur or take measures to restrict the gathering of people;  
(7) other necessary preventive and protective measures as prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.  
The people's government at or above the county level shall, based on the development of the situation, adjust the warning measures in a timely manner; after the risk of a public health emergency is eliminated, it shall promptly declare the lifting of the warning and lift the warning measures already taken; if a public health emergency occurs, an emergency response shall be initiated immediately.

### 第五章　应急处置

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国家对突发公共卫生事件实行分级应急响应。

**Article 37** The State shall implement a graded emergency response for public health emergencies.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　疾病预防控制机构发现发生突发公共卫生事件的，向同级卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门报告时应当同时提出启动应急响应的建议。卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门收到建议后，应当立即组织专家进行分析研判，并报告本级人民政府。人民政府应当立即决定启动应急响应、向社会发布公告，同时报告上一级人民政府，必要时可以越级上报，并通报可能受到影响地区的同级人民政府。

**Article 38** Where a disease prevention and control institution discovers that a public health emergency has occurred, it shall, when reporting to the competent health department and disease prevention and control department at the same level, also propose to initiate an emergency response. Upon receiving the proposal, the competent health department and disease prevention and control department shall immediately organize experts to conduct analysis and assessment, and report to the people's government at the same level. The people's government shall immediately decide to initiate an emergency response, issue a public announcement, simultaneously report to the higher-level people's government, may skip levels to report if necessary, and notify the people's government at the same level of the areas that may be affected.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　县级以上人民政府启动应急响应，应当组织有关部门按照突发公共卫生事件应急预案进行处置。县级以上人民政府处置突发公共卫生事件，可以在一定范围内采取下列一项或者多项应急处置措施：  
（一）组织开展医疗救治；  
（二）控制风险源，封闭或者封存被污染的公共饮用水源、物品，封闭可能造成危害扩散的场所；  
（三）疏散、转移并妥善安置易受突发公共卫生事件危害的人员；  
（四）实施人员排查、健康监测、医学观察、预防性服药；  
（五）限制或者停止人员聚集的活动；  
（六）停工、停业、停课；  
（七）调集卫生应急队伍和其他参与突发公共卫生事件应急处置的人员；  
（八）启用临时应急处置场所，调用应急处置所需的设备、物资、交通工具；  
（九）为控制、减轻和消除突发公共卫生事件的危害需要依法采取的其他必要措施。

**Article 39** Where a people's government at or above the county level initiates an emergency response, it shall organize relevant departments to carry out response measures in accordance with the public health emergency response plan. When handling a public health emergency, the people's government at or above the county level may, within a certain scope, take one or more of the following emergency response measures:  
(1) organize medical treatment;  
(2) control risk sources, seal or seal off contaminated public drinking water sources and items, and seal off places that may cause the spread of harm;  
(3) evacuate, relocate, and properly accommodate persons vulnerable to the public health emergency;  
(4) implement personnel screening, health monitoring, medical observation, and preventive medication;  
(5) restrict or stop activities where people gather;  
(6) suspend work, business, and classes;  
(7) deploy health emergency teams and other personnel participating in public health emergency response;  
(8) activate temporary emergency response sites, and deploy equipment, materials, and means of transport needed for emergency response;  
(9) take other necessary measures according to law to control, mitigate, and eliminate the hazards of the public health emergency.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　县级以上人民政府应当及时发布突发公共卫生事件事态发展、应急处置工作进展等信息。发现影响或者可能影响社会稳定、扰乱社会和经济管理秩序的虚假或者不完整信息的，应当及时发布准确的信息予以澄清。  
县级以上人民政府采取应急处置措施，应当发布公告，明确措施的具体内容和实施范围，并进行必要的解释说明；可以确定措施实施期限的，应当同时公告实施期限；解除措施的，应当发布公告。

**Article 40** People's governments at or above the county level shall promptly release information on the development of the public health emergency and the progress of emergency response work. Where false or incomplete information that affects or may affect social stability or disrupt social and economic management order is discovered, accurate information shall be released in a timely manner to clarify.  
Where a people's government at or above the county level adopts emergency response measures, it shall issue a public announcement specifying the specific content and scope of implementation of the measures, and provide necessary explanations; where the implementation period of the measures can be determined, it shall also announce the implementation period; where measures are lifted, a public announcement shall be issued.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　突发公共卫生事件发生后，疾病预防控制机构应当开展流行病学调查，对被污染的场所进行卫生处理。  
疾病预防控制机构开展流行病学调查，可以进入突发公共卫生事件现场采集样本，询问有关人员，查阅、复制有关资料。有关单位和个人应当接受和配合调查，不得拒绝、阻挠；有关部门和单位应当予以协助。

**Article 41** After a public health emergency occurs, a disease prevention and control institution shall conduct an epidemiological investigation and carry out sanitary treatment of contaminated sites.  
When conducting an epidemiological investigation, a disease prevention and control institution may enter the public health emergency site to collect samples, question relevant persons, and consult and copy relevant materials. The relevant entities and individuals shall accept and cooperate with the investigation and shall not refuse or obstruct it; relevant departments and entities shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　医疗机构应当开展突发公共卫生事件医疗救治，优化诊疗方案，提高诊断和救治能力。在医疗救治中，应当注重发挥中西医各自优势，提高救治效果。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当建立衔接顺畅的患者快速转运机制，根据突发公共卫生事件应急处置需要，及时将患者转至具备相应救治能力的医疗机构。在患者转运过程中，应当采取必要的防护措施。

**Article 42** Medical institutions shall carry out medical treatment for public health emergencies, optimize diagnosis and treatment plans, and improve diagnosis and treatment capabilities. In medical treatment, attention shall be paid to giving full play to the respective advantages of traditional Chinese medicine and Western medicine to improve treatment outcomes.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a smooth rapid transfer mechanism for patients, and promptly transfer patients to medical institutions with corresponding treatment capabilities based on the needs of public health emergency response. During the transfer of patients, necessary protective measures shall be taken.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　因突发公共卫生事件医疗救治紧急需要，医师可以按照国家统一制定的诊疗方案，在一定范围和期限内采用药品说明书中未明确的药品用法进行救治。  
发生特别重大突发公共卫生事件时，国务院卫生健康主管部门根据突发公共卫生事件医疗救治需要提出紧急使用药物的建议，经国务院药品监督管理部门组织论证同意后可以在一定范围和期限内紧急使用。

**Article 43** Where urgently needed for medical treatment of a public health emergency, a physician may, in accordance with a unified diagnosis and treatment plan formulated by the State, use a medication method not specified in the drug instructions within a certain scope and period for treatment.  
In the event of a particularly major public health emergency, the competent health department of the State Council may, based on the needs of medical treatment for the public health emergency, propose the emergency use of drugs, and after being organized and agreed upon by the drug regulatory department of the State Council, the drugs may be used on an emergency basis within a certain scope and period.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　突发公共卫生事件发生后，国务院及其有关部门有权在全国或者跨省级行政区域范围内，县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门有权在本行政区域内，根据突发公共卫生事件应急处置需要，临时征用房屋、交通工具以及相关设施、设备、场地和其他物资，要求有关单位和个人提供技术支持，要求生产、供应基本生活必需品和突发公共卫生事件应急物资的单位组织生产、保障供应。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门临时征用房屋、交通工具以及相关设施、设备、场地和其他物资，要求有关单位和个人提供技术支持的，应当依法及时给予公平、合理的补偿；能返还的，应当及时返还。

**Article 44** After a public health emergency occurs, the State Council and its relevant departments have the authority, nationwide or across provincial-level administrative regions, and local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments have the authority, within their respective administrative regions, based on the needs of public health emergency response, to temporarily requisition houses, means of transport, and related facilities, equipment, sites, and other materials, require relevant entities and individuals to provide technical support, and require entities that produce and supply basic necessities and public health emergency supplies to organize production and ensure supply.  
Where people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments temporarily requisition houses, means of transport, and related facilities, equipment, sites, and other materials, or require relevant entities and individuals to provide technical support, they shall provide fair and reasonable compensation according to law in a timely manner; where return is possible, the items shall be returned in a timely manner.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　突发公共卫生事件发生后，县级以上人民政府有关部门应当组织协调运输经营单位，优先运送参与突发公共卫生事件应急处置的人员以及药品、医疗器械、血液、防护用品和其他突发公共卫生事件应急物资。

**Article 45** After a public health emergency occurs, the relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level shall organize and coordinate transport operators to give priority to transporting personnel participating in public health emergency response, as well as drugs, medical devices, blood, protective supplies, and other public health emergency supplies.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　突发公共卫生事件应急处置期间，县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施维持社会基本运行，保障食品、饮用水等基本生活必需品的供应，提供基本医疗服务；对未成年人、老年人、残疾人、孕产期和哺乳期的妇女以及需要及时救治的伤病人员等群体给予特殊照顾和安排，并确保相关人员获得医疗救治。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当公布求助电话等渠道，畅通求助途径，及时向有需求的人员提供帮助。县级以上地方人民政府发布信息时，条件具备的同步采取无障碍信息交流方式。  
突发公共卫生事件发生后，人民政府依法采取停工、停业措施期间，用人单位可以按照规定享受有关帮扶政策，并应当按照规定支付劳动者工资、发放生活费。

**Article 46** During the public health emergency response period, local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to maintain basic social operations, ensure the supply of basic necessities such as food and drinking water, and provide basic medical services; provide special care and arrangements for groups such as minors, the elderly, persons with disabilities, pregnant and lactating women, and sick or injured persons requiring timely treatment, and ensure that relevant persons receive medical treatment.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall publish contact channels such as assistance hotlines, ensure smooth access to assistance, and provide timely help to persons in need. When local people's governments at or above the county level release information, if conditions permit, they shall simultaneously adopt accessible information communication methods.  
After a public health emergency occurs, during the period when the people's government suspends work or business according to law, employers may enjoy relevant support policies as prescribed and shall pay workers' wages and living allowances as prescribed.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　突发公共卫生事件发生后，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织专业力量，对受到突发公共卫生事件影响的人员以及公众提供心理疏导、心理干预等心理援助服务。

**Article 47** After a public health emergency occurs, local people's governments at or above the county level shall organize professional forces to provide psychological counseling, psychological intervention, and other psychological assistance services to persons affected by the public health emergency and the public.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　突发公共卫生事件应急处置期间，县级以上人民政府应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，组织开展医疗废物收集、贮存、运输、处置等工作。

**Article 48** During the public health emergency response period, people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, organize the collection, storage, transportation, and disposal of medical waste.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　县级以上人民政府应当及时组织专家对突发公共卫生事件事态发展进行分析研判，并根据研判结果调整应急响应的级别和应急处置措施；突发公共卫生事件得到有效控制后，应当及时宣布解除应急响应，停止执行依法采取的应急处置措施。

**Article 49** People's governments at or above the county level shall promptly organize experts to conduct analysis and assessment of the development of the public health emergency, and adjust the level of emergency response and emergency response measures based on the assessment results; after the public health emergency is effectively controlled, the emergency response shall be promptly declared lifted, and the execution of the emergency response measures taken according to law shall be stopped.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　突发公共卫生事件应急处置结束后，负责应对工作的地方人民政府应当及时总结应急处置工作，并向上一级人民政府报告。

**Article 50** After the end of the public health emergency response, the local people's government responsible for the response work shall promptly summarize the emergency response work and report to the higher-level people's government.

### 第六章　保障措施

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上人民政府按照本级政府职责，妥善安排突发公共卫生事件应对工作经费。

**Article 51** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, properly arrange funds for the response to public health emergencies.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　国家依托高水平医疗机构建设国家重大传染病防治基地等突发公共卫生事件医疗救治基地，依托省级疾病预防控制机构建设区域公共卫生应急协作平台。

**Article 52** The State shall, relying on high-level medical institutions, establish medical treatment bases for public health emergencies such as national bases for prevention and treatment of major infectious diseases, and shall, relying on provincial-level disease prevention and control institutions, build regional platforms for public health emergency coordination.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家统筹医疗保障基金和公共卫生服务资金使用，促进公共卫生服务和医疗服务有效衔接，实现更好保障。

**Article 53** The State shall coordinate the use of medical insurance funds and public health service funds to promote effective linkage between public health services and medical services, thereby achieving better protection.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家建立健全公共卫生应急物资保障体系，提高突发公共卫生事件应急物资保障水平。县级以上人民政府发展改革部门统筹公共卫生应急物资保障工作。  
国家加强医药储备，实行中央和地方两级储备。国务院工业和信息化部门会同国务院有关部门，根据应对突发公共卫生事件的需要，加强国家医药储备，指导地方开展医药储备工作，完善储备调整、调用和轮换机制。  
鼓励单位和家庭根据卫生健康主管部门的提示，适量储备常用药品、防护用品、消毒用品。

**Article 54** The State shall establish and improve the public health emergency material support system to enhance the level of support for materials in public health emergencies. The development and reform departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate the work on public health emergency material support.  
The State shall strengthen the pharmaceutical reserve, implementing a two-tier reserve at the central and local levels. The industry and information technology department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments of the State Council, strengthen the national pharmaceutical reserve according to the needs of responding to public health emergencies, guide local authorities in carrying out the pharmaceutical reserve work, and improve the mechanisms for adjustment, allocation, and rotation of the reserve.  
Units and families are encouraged to appropriately reserve common medicines, protective supplies, and disinfection products according to the prompts of the health authorities.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对参与突发公共卫生事件应急处置的人员，按照国家规定采取有效的卫生防护措施和医疗保健措施，并给予适当的津贴。  
对因参与突发公共卫生事件应急处置致病、致残、死亡的人员，按照有关规定落实保障政策。  
国家鼓励医疗机构、疾病预防控制机构为本机构参与突发公共卫生事件应急处置的人员购买人身意外伤害保险。

**Article 55** Personnel participating in emergency response to public health emergencies shall be provided with effective health protection measures and medical care measures according to state provisions, and shall be given appropriate allowances.  
For personnel who become ill, disabled, or die due to participation in emergency response to public health emergencies, the guarantee policies shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
The State encourages medical institutions and disease prevention and control institutions to purchase personal accident insurance for personnel participating in emergency response to public health emergencies from their own institutions.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府卫生健康、疾病预防控制等部门在突发公共卫生事件应对工作中有下列情形之一的，由其上级行政机关责令改正，通报批评，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依法履行报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报突发公共卫生事件，或者干预突发公共卫生事件报告；  
（二）未依法发布预警、采取预警措施，导致危害扩大；  
（三）未依法启动应急响应、采取应急处置措施，导致危害扩大；  
（四）违反本法规定的其他失职、渎职行为。

**Article 56** Where, in violation of this Law, the local people's governments at various levels and the health, disease prevention and control departments of the people's governments at or above the county level commit any of the following acts in the response to public health emergencies, the administrative organ at the next higher level shall order them to make corrections, circulate a notice of criticism, and impose sanctions according to law on the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel:  
(1) failing to perform the reporting duty according to law, concealing, falsely reporting, delaying reporting, or omitting reports on public health emergencies, or interfering with the reporting of public health emergencies;  
(2) failing to issue a warning or take warning measures according to law, resulting in the expansion of harm;  
(3) failing to initiate emergency response or take emergency response measures according to law, resulting in the expansion of harm;  
(4) other dereliction of duty or malfeasance in violation of the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　违反本法规定，疾病预防控制机构有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门责令有关责任人员暂停六个月以上一年以下的执业活动直至依法吊销执业证书：  
（一）未依法履行突发公共卫生事件监测职责；  
（二）未依法履行报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报突发公共卫生事件，或者干预突发公共卫生事件报告；  
（三）未依法对突发公共卫生事件报告进行调查、核实。  
前款规定以外的医疗卫生机构、检验检测机构未依法履行报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报突发公共卫生事件，或者干预突发公共卫生事件报告的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评，可以并处十万元以下罚款；情节严重的，可以由原发证部门或者原备案部门依法吊销相关许可证或者责令停止执业活动，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分，并可以由原发证部门责令有关责任人员暂停六个月以上一年以下的执业活动直至依法吊销执业证书。

**Article 57** Where, in violation of this Law, a disease prevention and control institution commits any of the following acts, the competent health department or the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, give it a warning or circulate a notice of criticism, impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible personnel, and may also, by the original issuing authority, order the relevant responsible personnel to suspend practice from six months up to one year until his/her practice certificate is revoked according to law:  
(1) failing to perform the duty of monitoring public health emergencies according to law;  
(2) failing to perform the reporting duty according to law, concealing, falsely reporting, delaying reporting, or omitting reports on public health emergencies, or interfering with the reporting of public health emergencies;  
(3) failing to investigate and verify reports on public health emergencies according to law.  
Where a medical and health institution or testing and inspection institution other than those provided for in the preceding paragraph fails to perform the reporting duty according to law, conceals, falsely reports, delays reporting, or omits reports on public health emergencies, or interferes with the reporting of public health emergencies, the competent health department or the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, give it a warning or circulate a notice of criticism, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 100,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority or the original filing authority may revoke the relevant license according to law or order cessation of practice activities, impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible personnel, and may also, by the original issuing authority, order the relevant responsible personnel to suspend practice from six months up to one year until his/her practice certificate is revoked according to law.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　违反本法规定，发生或者可能发生突发公共卫生事件的单位未依法履行报告职责，隐瞒、谎报、缓报、漏报突发公共卫生事件，或者干预突发公共卫生事件报告的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告或者通报批评；造成严重后果的，并处一万元以上十万元以下罚款，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 58** Where, in violation of this Law, a unit in which a public health emergency has occurred or may occur fails to perform the reporting duty according to law, conceals, falsely reports, delays reporting, or omits reports on the public health emergency, or interferes with the reporting of the public health emergency, the competent health department or the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and duties, order it to make corrections, give it a warning or circulate a notice of criticism; if serious consequences are caused, it shall also impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible personnel.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门、疾病预防控制部门依据职责责令改正，给予警告，对违法的单位可以并处二万元以下罚款，对违法的个人可以并处一千元以下罚款；情节严重的，由原发证部门依法吊销相关许可证或者营业执照：  
（一）拒不执行人民政府依法采取的突发公共卫生事件应对措施；  
（二）拒不接受和配合疾病预防控制机构开展突发公共卫生事件流行病学调查；  
（三）故意编造、散布虚假突发公共卫生事件信息；  
（四）其他妨害依法采取的突发公共卫生事件应对措施的行为。

**Article 59** Where, in violation of this Law, any of the following circumstances occurs, the competent health department or the disease prevention and control department of the people's government at or above the county level shall, within its respective functions and duties, order corrections, give a warning, and may concurrently impose a fine of not more than 20,000 yuan on the illegal unit and a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan on the illegal individual; if the circumstances are serious, the original issuing authority shall revoke the relevant license or business license according to law:  
(1) refusing to implement the response measures for public health emergencies lawfully adopted by the people's government;  
(2) refusing to accept and cooperate with disease prevention and control institutions in conducting epidemiological investigations of public health emergencies;  
(3) deliberately fabricating or disseminating false information about public health emergencies;  
(4) other acts that obstruct the response measures for public health emergencies lawfully adopted.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　对突发公共卫生事件发生期间哄抬价格、牟取暴利、制假售假等扰乱市场秩序的行为，依法从重给予行政处罚。

**Article 60** For acts such as price gouging, profiteering, producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy goods, and other acts disturbing market order during the occurrence of a public health emergency, administrative penalties shall be given heavier according to law.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　违反本法规定，造成人身损害、财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 61** Where a violation of this Law causes personal injury or property damage, the violator shall bear civil liability according to law; if it constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if it constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　在中华人民共和国领域内的外国人、无国籍人应当遵守本法。对涉及外国驻中华人民共和国外交、领事机构和国际组织驻中华人民共和国代表机构以及上述机构的人员及其家属的突发公共卫生事件，有关县级以上地方人民政府在国务院外交部门的指导下开展应对工作。

**Article 62** Foreigners and stateless persons within the territory of the People's Republic of China shall abide by this Law. For public health emergencies involving foreign diplomatic and consular missions in the People's Republic of China, representative offices of international organizations in the People's Republic of China, and the personnel and their family members of the said missions, the relevant local people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out the response work under the guidance of the diplomatic department of the State Council.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　在中华人民共和国对外开放的口岸开展有关突发公共卫生事件监测、应急处置等，《中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法》等法律另有规定的，依照其规定。  
涉及食品安全、药品安全等突发公共卫生事件的应对，同时适用《中华人民共和国食品安全法》、《中华人民共和国药品管理法》等法律的规定。

**Article 63** Where laws such as the Frontier Health and Quarantine Law of the People's Republic of China provide otherwise in respect of conducting monitoring and emergency response for public health emergencies at open ports of the People's Republic of China, those provisions shall prevail.  
For responses to public health emergencies involving food safety, drug safety, etc., the provisions of laws such as the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Drug Administration Law of the People's Republic of China shall also apply.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队的突发公共卫生事件应对工作，依照本法和中央军事委员会的有关规定执行，由中央军事委员会负责卫生工作的部门实施管理。  
国务院卫生健康主管部门接到突发公共卫生事件报告后，及时通报中央军事委员会负责卫生工作的部门。县级以上地方人民政府发布预警、启动应急响应的，及时通报中国人民解放军、中国人民武装警察部队驻当地有关单位。

**Article 64** The response to public health emergencies by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be carried out in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the Central Military Commission, and shall be managed by the department responsible for health work under the Central Military Commission.  
After receiving a report of a public health emergency, the competent health department of the State Council shall promptly notify the department responsible for health work under the Central Military Commission. Where a local people's government at or above the county level issues a warning or initiates an emergency response, it shall promptly notify the relevant units of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force stationed locally.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　本法自2025年11月1日起施行。

**Article 65** This Law shall come into force as of November 1, 2025.
