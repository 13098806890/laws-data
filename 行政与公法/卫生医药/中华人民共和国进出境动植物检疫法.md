# 中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1991年10月30日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　进境检疫 第三章　出境检疫 第四章　过境检疫 第五章　携带、邮寄物检疫 第六章　运输工具检疫 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为防止动物传染病、寄生虫病和植物危险性病、虫、杂草以及其他有害生物（以下简称病虫害）传入、传出国境，保护农、林、牧、渔业生产和人体健康，促进对外经济贸易的发展，制定本法。 第二条　进出境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，装载动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的装载容器、包装物，以及来自动植物疫区的运输工具，依照本法规定实施检疫。 第三条　国务院设立动植物检疫机关（以下简称国家动植物检疫机关），统一管理全国进出境动植物检疫工作。国家动植物检疫机关在对外开放的口岸和进出境动植物检疫业务集中的地点设立的口岸动植物检疫机关，依照本法规定实施进出境动植物检疫。 贸易性动物产品出境的检疫机关，由国务院根据情况规定。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为防止动物传染病、寄生虫病和植物危险性病、虫、杂草以及其他有害生物（以下简称病虫害）传入、传出国境，保护农、林、牧、渔业生产和人体健康，促进对外经济贸易的发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted to prevent the introduction or spread of animal infectious diseases, parasitic diseases, and plant dangerous diseases, pests, weeds, and other harmful organisms (hereinafter referred to as pests and diseases) into or out of the country, to protect agricultural, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery production and human health, and to promote the development of foreign economic trade.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　进出境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，装载动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的装载容器、包装物，以及来自动植物疫区的运输工具，依照本法规定实施检疫。

**Article 2** Quarantine shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of this Law on animals and plants, animal and plant products and other quarantine objects entering or leaving the country, the containers and packaging materials carrying such animals and plants, animal and plant products and other quarantine objects, as well as means of transport coming from animal or plant epidemic areas.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院设立动植物检疫机关（以下简称国家动植物检疫机关），统一管理全国进出境动植物检疫工作。国家动植物检疫机关在对外开放的口岸和进出境动植物检疫业务集中的地点设立的口岸动植物检疫机关，依照本法规定实施进出境动植物检疫。  
贸易性动物产品出境的检疫机关，由国务院根据情况规定。  
国务院农业行政主管部门主管全国进出境动植物检疫工作。

**Article 3** The State Council shall establish an animal and plant quarantine authority (hereinafter referred to as the national animal and plant quarantine authority) to administer uniformly the national work of entry-exit animal and plant quarantine. The port animal and plant quarantine authorities set up by the national animal and plant quarantine authority at open ports and at places where entry-exit animal and plant quarantine operations are concentrated shall carry out entry-exit animal and plant quarantine in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
The quarantine authority for the export of animal products for trade shall be prescribed by the State Council in light of the circumstances.  
The agriculture administrative department under the State Council shall be responsible for the national work of entry-exit animal and plant quarantine.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　口岸动植物检疫机关在实施检疫时可以行使下列职权：  
（一）依照本法规定登船、登车、登机实施检疫；  
（二）进入港口、机场、车站、邮局以及检疫物的存放、加工、养殖、种植场所实施检疫，并依照规定采样；  
（三）根据检疫需要，进入有关生产、仓库等场所，进行疫情监测、调查和检疫监督管理；  
（四）查阅、复制、摘录与检疫物有关的运行日志、货运单、合同、发票及其他单证。

**Article 4** When carrying out quarantine, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities may exercise the following functions and powers:  
(1) Boarding vessels, vehicles, or aircraft to carry out quarantine in accordance with the provisions of this Law;  
(2) Entering ports, airports, railway stations, post offices, and places where quarantine objects are stored, processed, bred, or planted to carry out quarantine, and taking samples in accordance with regulations;  
(3) Entering relevant production sites, warehouses and other places for epidemic surveillance, investigation, and quarantine supervision and administration as required by quarantine needs; and  
(4) Consulting, reproducing, or extracting from running logs, freight bills, contracts, invoices, and other documents relating to quarantine objects.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家禁止下列各物进境：  
（一）动植物病原体（包括菌种、毒种等）、害虫及其他有害生物；  
（二）动植物疫情流行的国家和地区的有关动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物；  
（三）动物尸体；  
（四）土壤。  
口岸动植物检疫机关发现有前款规定的禁止进境物的，作退回或者销毁处理。  
因科学研究等特殊需要引进本条第一款规定的禁止进境物的，必须事先提出申请，经国家动植物检疫机关批准。  
本条第一款第二项规定的禁止进境物的名录，由国务院农业行政主管部门制定并公布。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例.md#art-9" title="被《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例》第9条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例.md#art-14" title="被《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例》第14条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 5** The State prohibits the entry of the following articles:  
(1) Animal or plant pathogens (including bacterial strains, virus strains, etc.), pests, and other harmful organisms;  
(2) Relevant animals and plants, animal and plant products, and other quarantine objects from countries or regions where animal or plant epidemics are prevalent;  
(3) Animal carcasses; and  
(4) Soil.  
Where the port animal and plant quarantine authority discovers any article prohibited from entry as mentioned in the preceding paragraph, such article shall be returned or destroyed.  
Where the introduction of articles prohibited from entry as specified in the first paragraph of this Article is necessary for special needs such as scientific research, an application must be submitted in advance and approved by the national animal and plant quarantine authority.  
The catalogue of articles prohibited from entry as specified in Item (2) of the first paragraph of this Article shall be formulated and announced by the agriculture administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国外发生重大动植物疫情并可能传入中国时，国务院应当采取紧急预防措施，必要时可以下令禁止来自动植物疫区的运输工具进境或者封锁有关口岸；受动植物疫情威胁地区的地方人民政府和有关口岸动植物检疫机关，应当立即采取紧急措施，同时向上级人民政府和国家动植物检疫机关报告。  
邮电、运输部门对重大动植物疫情报告和送检材料应当优先传送。

**Article 6** When a serious animal or plant epidemic occurs abroad and is likely to be introduced into China, the State Council shall take emergency preventive measures and may, when necessary, order the prohibition of entry of means of transport from animal or plant epidemic areas or the blockade of relevant ports; the local people's government of the area threatened by the animal or plant epidemic and the relevant port animal and plant quarantine authority shall immediately take emergency measures and, at the same time, report to the people's government at the higher level and the national animal and plant quarantine authority.  
The postal and telecommunications departments and the transport departments shall give priority to the transmission of reports on serious animal or plant epidemics and materials submitted for inspection.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家动植物检疫机关和口岸动植物检疫机关对进出境动植物、动植物产品的生产、加工、存放过程，实行检疫监督制度。

**Article 7** The national animal and plant quarantine authority and the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall implement a quarantine supervision system over the production, processing, and storage of animals and plants and animal and plant products entering or leaving the country.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　口岸动植物检疫机关在港口、机场、车站、邮局执行检疫任务时，海关、交通、民航、铁路、邮电等有关部门应当配合。

**Article 8** When port animal and plant quarantine authorities perform quarantine duties at ports, airports, railway stations, or post offices, the relevant departments such as customs, communications, civil aviation, railways, and postal services shall coordinate.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　动植物检疫机关检疫人员必须忠于职守，秉公执法。  
动植物检疫机关检疫人员依法执行公务，任何单位和个人不得阻挠。

**Article 9** The quarantine officers of the animal and plant quarantine authorities must be devoted to their duties and enforce the law impartially.  
Any unit or individual shall not obstruct the quarantine officers of the animal and plant quarantine authorities when they perform their official duties in accordance with law.

### 第二章　进境检疫

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　输入动物、动物产品、植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料的，必须事先提出申请，办理检疫审批手续。

**Article 10** Where animals, animal products, plant seeds, seedlings, or other propagating materials are imported, an application must be submitted in advance and the quarantine approval formalities shall be completed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　通过贸易、科技合作、交换、赠送、援助等方式输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的，应当在合同或者协议中订明中国法定的检疫要求，并订明必须附有输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的检疫证书。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** Where animals and plants, animal and plant products, or other quarantine objects are imported by means of trade, scientific and technological cooperation, exchange, donation, or aid, the contract or agreement shall specify the statutory quarantine requirements of China and shall also specify that a quarantine certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authority of the exporting country or region must be attached.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　货主或者其代理人应当在动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境前或者进境时持输出国家或者地区的检疫证书、贸易合同等单证，向进境口岸动植物检疫机关报检。

**Article 12** The owner or the owner's agent shall apply for quarantine inspection to the port animal and plant quarantine authority at the place of entry before or at the time of entry of the animals and plants, animal and plant products, or other quarantine objects, presenting the quarantine certificate of the exporting country or region, trade contract, and other documents.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　装载动物的运输工具抵达口岸时，口岸动植物检疫机关应当采取现场预防措施，对上下运输工具或者接近动物的人员、装载动物的运输工具和被污染的场地作防疫消毒处理。

**Article 13** When a means of transport carrying animals arrives at a port, the port animal and plant quarantine authority shall take on-site preventive measures and carry out sanitary and disinfection treatment of the personnel boarding or leaving the means of transport or approaching the animals, the means of transport carrying the animals, and the contaminated site.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，应当在进境口岸实施检疫。未经口岸动植物检疫机关同意，不得卸离运输工具。  
输入动植物，需隔离检疫的，在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的隔离场所检疫。  
因口岸条件限制等原因，可以由国家动植物检疫机关决定将动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物运往指定地点检疫。在运输、装卸过程中，货主或者其代理人应当采取防疫措施。指定的存放、加工和隔离饲养或者隔离种植的场所，应当符合动植物检疫和防疫的规定。

**Article 14** Imported animals and plants, animal and plant products, and other quarantine objects shall be quarantined at the port of entry. Without the consent of the port animal and plant quarantine authority, they shall not be unloaded from the means of transport.  
Where imported animals or plants need to be quarantined in isolation, they shall be quarantined at the isolation premises designated by the port animal and plant quarantine authority.  
Where, due to port conditions or other reasons, the national animal and plant quarantine authority may decide to transport the animals and plants, animal and plant products, or other quarantine objects to a designated place for quarantine. During the transportation and loading/unloading, the owner or the owner's agent shall take epidemic prevention measures. The designated places for storage, processing, and isolation breeding or isolation planting shall comply with the provisions on animal and plant quarantine and epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫合格的，准予进境；海关凭口岸动植物检疫机关签发的检疫单证或者在报关单上加盖的印章验放。  
输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，需调离海关监管区检疫的，海关凭口岸动植物检疫机关签发的《检疫调离通知单》验放。

**Article 15** Imported animals and plants, animal and plant products, and other quarantine objects that pass quarantine inspection shall be permitted entry; the customs shall release them on the basis of the quarantine certificate issued by the port animal and plant quarantine authority or the seal stamped on the customs declaration form.  
Where imported animals and plants, animal and plant products, or other quarantine objects need to be removed from customs supervision area for quarantine, the customs shall release them on the basis of the "Quarantine Removal Notification" issued by the port animal and plant quarantine authority.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　输入动物，经检疫不合格的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发《检疫处理通知单》，通知货主或者其代理人作如下处理：  
（一）检出一类传染病、寄生虫病的动物，连同其同群动物全群退回或者全群扑杀并销毁尸体；  
（二）检出二类传染病、寄生虫病的动物，退回或者扑杀，同群其他动物在隔离场或者其他指定地点隔离观察。  
输入动物产品和其他检疫物经检疫不合格的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发《检疫处理通知单》，通知货主或者其代理人作除害、退回或者销毁处理。经除害处理合格的，准予进境。

**Article 16** Where an imported animal fails quarantine inspection, the port animal and plant quarantine authority shall issue a "Quarantine Treatment Notification" to notify the owner or the owner's agent to handle it as follows:  
(1) For animals in which Category A infectious diseases or parasitic diseases are detected, the affected animals together with all animals of the same herd shall be returned, or all shall be slaughtered and their carcasses destroyed;  
(2) For animals in which Category B infectious diseases or parasitic diseases are detected, the affected animals shall be returned or slaughtered, and the other animals of the same herd shall be quarantined under observation at an isolation premises or other designated place.  
Where imported animal products or other quarantine objects fail quarantine inspection, the port animal and plant quarantine authority shall issue a "Quarantine Treatment Notification" to notify the owner or the owner's agent to carry out disinfestation treatment, return or destroy them. If they pass quarantine inspection after disinfestation treatment, they shall be permitted entry.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　输入植物、植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫发现有植物危险性病、虫、杂草的，由口岸动植物检疫机关签发《检疫处理通知单》，通知货主或者其代理人作除害、退回或者销毁处理。经除害处理合格的，准予进境。

**Article 17** Where an imported plant, plant product, or other quarantine object is found through quarantine inspection to have dangerous plant diseases, pests, or weeds, the port animal and plant quarantine authority shall issue a "Quarantine Treatment Notification" to notify the owner or the owner's agent to carry out disinfestation treatment, return, or destroy it. If it passes quarantine inspection after disinfestation treatment, it shall be permitted entry.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　[本法第十六条](#art-16)第一款第一项、第二项所称一类、二类动物传染病、寄生虫病的名录和[本法第十七条](#art-17)所称植物危险性病、虫、杂草的名录，由国务院农业行政主管部门制定并公布。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例.md#art-47" title="被《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例》第47条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 18** The catalogues of Category A and Category B animal infectious diseases and parasitic diseases referred to in Items (1) and (2) of the first paragraph of Article 16 of this Law, and the catalogue of dangerous plant diseases, pests, and weeds referred to in Article 17 of this Law shall be formulated and announced by the agriculture administrative department under the State Council.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　输入动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫发现有[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的名录之外，对农、林、牧、渔业有严重危害的其他病虫害的，由口岸动植物检疫机关依照国务院农业行政主管部门的规定，通知货主或者其代理人作除害、退回或者销毁处理。经除害处理合格的，准予进境。

**Article 19** Where an imported animal or plant, animal or plant product, or other quarantine object is found through quarantine inspection to have other pests or diseases not listed in the catalogues specified in Article 18 of this Law but seriously harmful to agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, or fishery, the port animal and plant quarantine authority shall, in accordance with the provisions of the agriculture administrative department under the State Council, notify the owner or the owner's agent to carry out disinfestation treatment, return, or destroy it. If it passes quarantine inspection after disinfestation treatment, it shall be permitted entry.

### 第三章　出境检疫

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　货主或者其代理人在动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物出境前，向口岸动植物检疫机关报检。  
出境前需经隔离检疫的动物，在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的隔离场所检疫。

**Article 20** The owner or the owner's agent shall apply for quarantine inspection to the port animal and plant quarantine authority before the export of animals and plants, animal and plant products, or other quarantine objects.  
Animals that need to be quarantined in isolation prior to export shall be quarantined at the isolation premises designated by the port animal and plant quarantine authority.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　输出动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，由口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫，经检疫合格或者经除害处理合格的，准予出境；海关凭口岸动植物检疫机关签发的检疫证书或者在报关单上加盖的印章验放。检疫不合格又无有效方法作除害处理的，不准出境。

**Article 21** For the export of animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects, quarantine inspection shall be carried out by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities. Those that pass quarantine inspection or pass after disinfestation treatment shall be allowed to exit the country; the Customs shall release them based on the quarantine certificate issued by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities or the seal affixed on the customs declaration form. Those that fail quarantine inspection and for which there is no effective disinfestation treatment shall not be allowed to exit the country.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　经检疫合格的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，有下列情形之一的，货主或者其代理人应当重新报检：  
（一）更改输入国家或者地区，更改后的输入国家或者地区又有不同检疫要求的；  
（二）改换包装或者原未拼装后来拼装的；  
（三）超过检疫规定有效期限的。

**Article 22** Where animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects that have passed quarantine inspection fall under any of the following circumstances, the owner or their agent shall redeclare for quarantine inspection:  
(1) the importing country or region is changed and the new importing country or region has different quarantine requirements;  
(2) the packaging is changed or originally unconsolidated goods are later consolidated;  
(3) the validity period prescribed for quarantine has expired.

### 第四章　过境检疫

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　要求运输动物过境的，必须事先商得中国国家动植物检疫机关同意，并按照指定的口岸和路线过境。  
装载过境动物的运输工具、装载容器、饲料和铺垫材料，必须符合中国动植物检疫的规定。

**Article 23** Where it is required to transport animals in transit, prior consent must be obtained from the national animal and plant quarantine authorities of China, and the transit shall be conducted through the designated ports and routes.  
The means of transport, containers, feed, and bedding materials used for loading animals in transit must comply with the provisions of China on animal and plant quarantine.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　运输动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物过境的，由承运人或者押运人持货运单和输出国家或者地区政府动植物检疫机关出具的检疫证书，在进境时向口岸动植物检疫机关报检，出境口岸不再检疫。

**Article 24** Where animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects are transported in transit, the carrier or escort shall, holding the waybill and the quarantine certificate issued by the government animal and plant quarantine authorities of the exporting country or region, declare for quarantine inspection to the port animal and plant quarantine authorities at the time of entry, and no quarantine inspection shall be conducted at the port of exit.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　过境的动物经检疫合格的，准予过境；发现有[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的名录所列的动物传染病、寄生虫病的，全群动物不准过境。  
过境动物的饲料受病虫害污染的，作除害、不准过境或者销毁处理。  
过境的动物的尸体、排泄物、铺垫材料及其他废弃物，必须按照动植物检疫机关的规定处理，不得擅自抛弃。

**Article 25** Where animals in transit pass quarantine inspection, they shall be allowed to transit; where any animal infectious disease or parasitic disease listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 18 of this Law is found, the entire herd of animals shall not be allowed to transit.  
Where the feed of animals in transit is contaminated by diseases or pests, it shall be subject to disinfestation treatment, or shall not be allowed to transit, or shall be destroyed.  
The carcasses, excreta, bedding materials, and other wastes of animals in transit must be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the animal and plant quarantine authorities and shall not be discarded without authorization.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对过境植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，口岸动植物检疫机关检查运输工具或者包装，经检疫合格的，准予过境；发现有[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的名录所列的病虫害的，作除害处理或者不准过境。

**Article 26** For plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects in transit, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall inspect the means of transport or packaging. Those that pass quarantine inspection shall be allowed to transit; where any disease or pest listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 18 of this Law is found, disinfestation treatment shall be conducted or they shall not be allowed to transit.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物过境期间，未经动植物检疫机关批准，不得开拆包装或者卸离运输工具。

**Article 27** During the transit of animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects, without the approval of the animal and plant quarantine authorities, the packaging shall not be opened or the goods shall not be unloaded from the means of transport.

### 第五章　携带、邮寄物检疫

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　携带、邮寄植物种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料进境的，必须事先提出申请，办理检疫审批手续。

**Article 28** Where plant seeds, seedlings, and other propagating materials are brought or mailed into the country, an application must be submitted in advance and quarantine examination and approval procedures shall be completed.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　禁止携带、邮寄进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的名录，由国务院农业行政主管部门制定并公布。  
携带、邮寄前款规定的名录所列的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境的，作退回或者销毁处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例.md#art-43" title="被《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法实施条例》第43条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 29** The catalog of animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects that are prohibited from being brought or mailed into the country shall be formulated and published by the competent administrative department for agriculture of the State Council.  
Where any animal, plant, animal product, or other quarantine object listed in the catalog prescribed in the preceding paragraph is brought or mailed into the country, it shall be returned or destroyed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　携带[本法第二十九条](#art-29)规定的名录以外的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境的，在进境时向海关申报并接受口岸动植物检疫机关检疫。  
携带动物进境的，必须持有输出国家或者地区的检疫证书等证件。

**Article 30** Where any animal, plant, animal product, or other quarantine object not listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 29 of this Law is brought into the country, it shall be declared to the Customs at the time of entry and shall be subject to quarantine inspection by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities.  
Where an animal is brought into the country, a quarantine certificate or other certificate from the exporting country or region must be held.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　邮寄[本法第二十九条](#art-29)规定的名录以外的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物进境的，由口岸动植物检疫机关在国际邮件互换局实施检疫，必要时可以取回口岸动植物检疫机关检疫；未经检疫不得运递。

**Article 31** Where any animal, plant, animal product, or other quarantine object not listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 29 of this Law is mailed into the country, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall carry out quarantine inspection at the international mail exchange office, and if necessary, may take the articles back to the port animal and plant quarantine authorities for quarantine inspection; no such articles shall be transported or delivered before quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　邮寄进境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，经检疫或者除害处理合格后放行；经检疫不合格又无有效方法作除害处理的，作退回或者销毁处理，并签发《检疫处理通知单》。

**Article 32** Animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects mailed into the country shall be released after passing quarantine inspection or disinfestation treatment; those that fail quarantine inspection and for which there is no effective disinfestation treatment shall be returned or destroyed, and a "Notice on Quarantine Treatment" shall be issued.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　携带、邮寄出境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物，物主有检疫要求的，由口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫。

**Article 33** Where animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects are brought or mailed out of the country, and the owner has quarantine requirements, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall carry out quarantine inspection.

### 第六章　运输工具检疫

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　来自动植物疫区的船舶、飞机、火车抵达口岸时，由口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫。发现有[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的名录所列的病虫害的，作不准带离运输工具、除害、封存或者销毁处理。

**Article 34** When vessels, aircraft, or trains from animal or plant epidemic areas arrive at the port, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall carry out quarantine inspection. Where any disease or pest listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 18 of this Law is found, they shall not be allowed to leave the means of transport, or shall be subject to disinfestation, sealing, or destruction.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　进境的车辆，由口岸动植物检疫机关作防疫消毒处理。

**Article 35** Vehicles entering the country shall be subject to epidemic prevention and disinfection treatment by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　进出境运输工具上的泔水、动植物性废弃物，依照口岸动植物检疫机关的规定处理，不得擅自抛弃。

**Article 36** Swill and animal or plant waste on means of transport entering or leaving the country shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of the port animal and plant quarantine authorities and shall not be discarded without authorization.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　装载出境的动植物、动植物产品和其他检疫物的运输工具，应当符合动植物检疫和防疫的规定。

**Article 37** Means of transport used for loading animals, plants, animal products, and other quarantine objects for export shall comply with the provisions on animal and plant quarantine and epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　进境供拆船用的废旧船舶，由口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫，发现有[本法第十八条](#art-18)规定的名录所列的病虫害的，作除害处理。

**Article 38** For obsolete vessels imported for dismantling, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall carry out quarantine inspection. Where any disease or pest listed in the catalog prescribed in Article 18 of this Law is found, disinfestation treatment shall be conducted.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由口岸动植物检疫机关处以罚款：  
（一）未报检或者未依法办理检疫审批手续的；  
（二）未经口岸动植物检疫机关许可擅自将进境动植物、动植物产品或者其他检疫物卸离运输工具或者运递的；  
（三）擅自调离或者处理在口岸动植物检疫机关指定的隔离场所中隔离检疫的动植物的。

**Article 39** Anyone who violates the provisions of this Law and commits any of the following acts shall be fined by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities:  
(1) failing to declare for quarantine inspection or failing to complete quarantine examination and approval procedures according to law;  
(2) without the permission of the port animal and plant quarantine authorities, unloading imported animals, plants, animal products, or other quarantine objects from the means of transport or transporting or delivering them;  
(3) without authorization, transferring or disposing of animals or plants under quarantine inspection in the isolation premises designated by the port animal and plant quarantine authorities.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　报检的动植物、动植物产品或者其他检疫物与实际不符的，由口岸动植物检疫机关处以罚款；已取得检疫单证的，予以吊销。

**Article 40** Where the declared animals, plants, animal products, or other quarantine objects do not conform to the actual situation, the port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall impose a fine; if quarantine certificates have been obtained, they shall be revoked.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　违反本法规定，擅自开拆过境动植物、动植物产品或者其他检疫物的包装的，擅自将过境动植物、动植物产品或者其他检疫物卸离运输工具的，擅自抛弃过境动物的尸体、排泄物、铺垫材料或者其他废弃物的，由动植物检疫机关处以罚款。

**Article 41** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, opens the packages of transit animals or plants, products thereof or other quarantine objects without authorization, unloads transit animals or plants, products thereof or other quarantine objects from means of transport without authorization, or discards carcasses, excrements, bedding materials or other wastes of transit animals without authorization, the animal and plant quarantine authorities shall impose a fine.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　违反本法规定，引起重大动植物疫情的，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes a major animal or plant epidemic, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　伪造、变造检疫单证、印章、标志、封识，依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Anyone who forges or alters quarantine documents, seals, marks, or fastenings shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人对动植物检疫机关的处罚决定不服的，可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内向作出处罚决定的机关的上一级机关申请复议；当事人也可以在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内直接向人民法院起诉。  
复议机关应当在接到复议申请之日起六十日内作出复议决定。当事人对复议决定不服的，可以在接到复议决定之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。复议机关逾期不作出复议决定的，当事人可以在复议期满之日起十五日内向人民法院起诉。  
当事人逾期不申请复议也不向人民法院起诉、又不履行处罚决定的，作出处罚决定的机关可以申请人民法院强制执行。

**Article 44** A party that disagrees with a penalty decision made by the animal and plant quarantine authorities may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, apply for reconsideration to the authority at the next higher level over the authority that made the penalty decision; a party may also, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice of penalty, directly file a prosecution with the People's Court.  
  
The reconsideration authority shall make a reconsideration decision within sixty days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration. If a party disagrees with the reconsideration decision, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reconsideration decision. If the reconsideration authority fails to make a reconsideration decision within the time limit, the party may file a prosecution with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of expiration of the reconsideration period.  
  
If a party fails to apply for reconsideration or file a prosecution within the time limit and also fails to perform the penalty decision, the authority that made the penalty decision may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　动植物检疫机关检疫人员滥用职权，徇私舞弊，伪造检疫结果，或者玩忽职守，延误检疫出证，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；不构成犯罪的，给予行政处分。

**Article 45** Where quarantine personnel of the animal and plant quarantine authorities abuse their powers, engage in fraud for personal gain, forge quarantine results, or neglect their duties, delaying the issuance of quarantine certificates, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if it does not constitute a crime, administrative sanctions shall be imposed.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　本法下列用语的含义是：  
（一）“动物”是指饲养、野生的活动物，如畜、禽、兽、蛇、龟、鱼、虾、蟹、贝、蚕、蜂等；  
（二）“动物产品”是指来源于动物未经加工或者虽经加工但仍有可能传播疫病的产品，如生皮张、毛类、肉类、脏器、油脂、动物水产品、奶制品、蛋类、血液、精液、胚胎、骨、蹄、角等；  
（三）“植物”是指栽培植物、野生植物及其种子、种苗及其他繁殖材料等；  
（四）“植物产品”是指来源于植物未经加工或者虽经加工但仍有可能传播病虫害的产品，如粮食、豆、棉花、油、麻、烟草、籽仁、干果、鲜果、蔬菜、生药材、木材、饲料等；  
（五）“其他检疫物”是指动物疫苗、血清、诊断液、动植物性废弃物等。

**Article 46** For the purposes of this Law, the meanings of the following terms are:  
  
(1) "Animals" means live animals, whether raised or wild, such as livestock, poultry, game, snakes, turtles, fish, shrimps, crabs, shellfish, silkworms, bees, etc.;  
  
(2) "Animal products" means products derived from animals, whether unprocessed or processed but still likely to spread epidemic diseases, such as raw hides, furs, meats, viscera, fats, aquatic animal products, dairy products, eggs, blood, semen, embryos, bones, hooves, horns, etc.;  
  
(3) "Plants" means cultivated plants, wild plants and their seeds, seedlings, and other reproductive materials, etc.;  
  
(4) "Plant products" means products derived from plants, whether unprocessed or processed but still likely to spread diseases or pests, such as grain, beans, cotton, oil, hemp, tobacco, seeds, dried fruits, fresh fruits, vegetables, raw medicinal materials, timber, feed, etc.;  
  
(5) "Other quarantine objects" means animal vaccines, sera, diagnostic fluids, animal and plant wastes, etc.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的有关动植物检疫的国际条约与本法有不同规定的，适用该国际条约的规定。但是，中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 47** Where an international treaty concerning animal and plant quarantine concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains provisions that differ from those of this Law, the provisions of the international treaty shall apply, except for the provisions on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　口岸动植物检疫机关实施检疫依照规定收费。收费办法由国务院农业行政主管部门会同国务院物价等有关主管部门制定。

**Article 48** The port animal and plant quarantine authorities shall charge fees for quarantine in accordance with regulations. The fee rates shall be formulated by the administrative department of agriculture under the State Council jointly with the competent departments of pricing and other relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国务院根据本法制定实施条例。

**Article 49** The State Council shall formulate implementing regulations in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　本法自1992年4月1日起施行。1982年6月4日国务院发布的《中华人民共和国进出口动植物检疫条例》同时废止。

**Article 50** This Law shall come into force as of April 1, 1992. The Regulations of the People's Republic of China on the Inspection and Quarantine of Import and Export Animals and Plants promulgated by the State Council on June 4, 1982 shall be abolished simultaneously.
