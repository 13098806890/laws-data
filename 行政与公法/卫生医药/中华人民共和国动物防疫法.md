### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对动物防疫活动的管理，预防、控制、净化、消灭动物疫病，促进养殖业发展，防控人畜共患传染病，保障公共卫生安全和人体健康，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated to strengthen the management of animal epidemic prevention activities, prevent, control, purify and eliminate animal diseases, promote the development of animal husbandry, prevent and control zoonotic infectious diseases, and ensure public health safety and human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法适用于在中华人民共和国领域内的动物防疫及其监督管理活动。  
进出境动物、动物产品的检疫，适用《中华人民共和国进出境动植物检疫法》。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to animal epidemic prevention and the supervision and administration thereof within the territory of the People's Republic of China.  
The quarantine of animals and animal products entering or leaving the country shall be governed by the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Entry and Exit Animal and Plant Quarantine.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本法所称动物，是指家畜家禽和人工饲养、捕获的其他动物。  
本法所称动物产品，是指动物的肉、生皮、原毛、绒、脏器、脂、血液、精液、卵、胚胎、骨、蹄、头、角、筋以及可能传播动物疫病的奶、蛋等。  
本法所称动物疫病，是指动物传染病，包括寄生虫病。  
本法所称动物防疫，是指动物疫病的预防、控制、诊疗、净化、消灭和动物、动物产品的检疫，以及病死动物、病害动物产品的无害化处理。

**Article 3** For the purposes of this Law, "animals" means livestock, poultry and other animals that are artificially raised or captured.  
For the purposes of this Law, "animal products" means the meat, raw hide, raw wool, down, viscera, fat, blood, semen, ova, embryos, bones, hooves, heads, horns, tendons of animals, as well as milk, eggs, etc. that may transmit animal diseases.  
For the purposes of this Law, "animal diseases" means infectious diseases of animals, including parasitic diseases.  
For the purposes of this Law, "animal epidemic prevention" means the prevention, control, diagnosis and treatment, purification and elimination of animal diseases, the quarantine of animals and animal products, and the harmless treatment of dead and diseased animals and diseased animal products.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　根据动物疫病对养殖业生产和人体健康的危害程度，本法规定的动物疫病分为下列三类：  
（一）一类疫病，是指口蹄疫、非洲猪瘟、高致病性禽流感等对人、动物构成特别严重危害，可能造成重大经济损失和社会影响，需要采取紧急、严厉的强制预防、控制等措施的；  
（二）二类疫病，是指狂犬病、布鲁氏菌病、草鱼出血病等对人、动物构成严重危害，可能造成较大经济损失和社会影响，需要采取严格预防、控制等措施的；  
（三）三类疫病，是指大肠杆菌病、禽结核病、鳖腮腺炎病等常见多发，对人、动物构成危害，可能造成一定程度的经济损失和社会影响，需要及时预防、控制的。  
前款一、二、三类动物疫病具体病种名录由国务院农业农村主管部门制定并公布。国务院农业农村主管部门应当根据动物疫病发生、流行情况和危害程度，及时增加、减少或者调整一、二、三类动物疫病具体病种并予以公布。  
人畜共患传染病名录由国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院卫生健康、野生动物保护等主管部门制定并公布。

**Article 4** According to the degree of harm that animal diseases cause to animal husbandry production and human health, the animal diseases specified in this Law are divided into the following three categories:  
(1) Category I diseases refer to foot-and-mouth disease, African swine fever, highly pathogenic avian influenza, and other diseases that cause particularly serious harm to humans and animals, may cause major economic losses and social impact, and require urgent and strict compulsory prevention, control and other measures;  
(2) Category II diseases refer to rabies, brucellosis, grass carp hemorrhagic disease, and other diseases that cause serious harm to humans and animals, may cause relatively large economic losses and social impact, and require strict prevention, control and other measures;  
(3) Category III diseases refer to colibacillosis, avian tuberculosis, soft-shelled turtle mumps disease, and other common and frequently occurring diseases that cause harm to humans and animals, may cause a certain degree of economic losses and social impact, and require timely prevention and control.  
The specific list of diseases for Category I, II and III animal diseases as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be formulated and published by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, based on the occurrence, prevalence and degree of harm of animal diseases, promptly increase, reduce or adjust the specific diseases of Category I, II and III animal diseases and publish them.  
The list of zoonotic infectious diseases shall be formulated and published by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council jointly with the competent departments of health, wildlife protection, etc. of the State Council.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　动物防疫实行预防为主，预防与控制、净化、消灭相结合的方针。

**Article 5** The guideline of prevention first, combining prevention with control, purification and elimination, shall be implemented in animal epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励社会力量参与动物防疫工作。各级人民政府采取措施，支持单位和个人参与动物防疫的宣传教育、疫情报告、志愿服务和捐赠等活动。

**Article 6** The State encourages social forces to participate in animal epidemic prevention work. People's governments at all levels shall take measures to support entities and individuals in participating in activities such as publicity and education on animal epidemic prevention, epidemic reporting, voluntary services and donations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　从事动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输以及动物产品生产、经营、加工、贮藏等活动的单位和个人，依照本法和国务院农业农村主管部门的规定，做好免疫、消毒、检测、隔离、净化、消灭、无害化处理等动物防疫工作，承担动物防疫相关责任。

**Article 7** Entities and individuals engaged in activities such as animal raising, slaughter, trading, isolation, transportation, and production, trading, processing, storage of animal products shall, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, properly carry out animal epidemic prevention work such as immunization, disinfection, testing, isolation, purification, elimination and harmless treatment, and bear the relevant responsibilities for animal epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府对动物防疫工作实行统一领导，采取有效措施稳定基层机构队伍，加强动物防疫队伍建设，建立健全动物防疫体系，制定并组织实施动物疫病防治规划。  
乡级人民政府、街道办事处组织群众做好本辖区的动物疫病预防与控制工作，村民委员会、居民委员会予以协助。

**Article 8** People's governments at or above the county level shall exercise unified leadership over animal epidemic prevention work, take effective measures to stabilize grassroots institutions and teams, strengthen the building of animal epidemic prevention teams, establish and improve the animal epidemic prevention system, and formulate and organize the implementation of animal disease prevention and control plans.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall organize the masses to properly carry out animal disease prevention and control work within their respective jurisdictions, and villagers' committees and residents' committees shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院农业农村主管部门主管全国的动物防疫工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门主管本行政区域的动物防疫工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门在各自职责范围内做好动物防疫工作。  
军队动物卫生监督职能部门负责军队现役动物和饲养自用动物的防疫工作。

**Article 9** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be in charge of animal epidemic prevention work nationwide.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of animal epidemic prevention work within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall properly carry out animal epidemic prevention work within the scope of their respective duties.  
The military animal health supervision functional department shall be responsible for the epidemic prevention work of active-duty animals of the military and animals raised for self-use.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级以上人民政府卫生健康主管部门和本级人民政府农业农村、野生动物保护等主管部门应当建立人畜共患传染病防治的协作机制。  
国务院农业农村主管部门和海关总署等部门应当建立防止境外动物疫病输入的协作机制。

**Article 10** The competent departments of health of people's governments at or above the county level and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, wildlife protection, etc. of the people's government at the same level shall establish a coordination mechanism for the prevention and control of zoonotic infectious diseases.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council and the General Administration of Customs, among other departments, shall establish a coordination mechanism for preventing the introduction of animal diseases from abroad.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　县级以上地方人民政府的动物卫生监督机构依照本法规定，负责动物、动物产品的检疫工作。

**Article 11** The animal health supervision institutions of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the quarantine of animals and animal products in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府按照国务院的规定，根据统筹规划、合理布局、综合设置的原则建立动物疫病预防控制机构。  
动物疫病预防控制机构承担动物疫病的监测、检测、诊断、流行病学调查、疫情报告以及其他预防、控制等技术工作；承担动物疫病净化、消灭的技术工作。

**Article 12** People's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of the State Council and based on the principles of overall planning, rational layout and comprehensive establishment, establish animal disease prevention and control institutions.  
Animal disease prevention and control institutions shall undertake the technical work of monitoring, testing, diagnosis, epidemiological investigation, epidemic reporting and other prevention and control of animal diseases; and shall undertake the technical work of purification and elimination of animal diseases.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励和支持开展动物疫病的科学研究以及国际合作与交流，推广先进适用的科学研究成果，提高动物疫病防治的科学技术水平。  
各级人民政府和有关部门、新闻媒体，应当加强对动物防疫法律法规和动物防疫知识的宣传。

**Article 13** The State encourages and supports scientific research on animal diseases, as well as international cooperation and exchanges, promotes advanced and applicable scientific research achievements, and improves the scientific and technological level of animal disease prevention and control.  
People's governments at all levels, relevant departments and news media shall strengthen the publicity of laws and regulations on animal epidemic prevention and knowledge of animal epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对在动物防疫工作、相关科学研究、动物疫情扑灭中做出贡献的单位和个人，各级人民政府和有关部门按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。  
有关单位应当依法为动物防疫人员缴纳工伤保险费。对因参与动物防疫工作致病、致残、死亡的人员，按照国家有关规定给予补助或者抚恤。

**Article 14** People's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, give commendations and rewards to entities and individuals that have made contributions in animal epidemic prevention work, related scientific research, and animal epidemic eradication.  
Relevant entities shall, according to law, pay work-related injury insurance premiums for animal epidemic prevention personnel. Personnel who become ill, disabled or die due to participation in animal epidemic prevention work shall be given subsidies or pensions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第二章　动物疫病的预防

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国家建立动物疫病风险评估制度。  
国务院农业农村主管部门根据国内外动物疫情以及保护养殖业生产和人体健康的需要，及时会同国务院卫生健康等有关部门对动物疫病进行风险评估，并制定、公布动物疫病预防、控制、净化、消灭措施和技术规范。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门会同本级人民政府卫生健康等有关部门开展本行政区域的动物疫病风险评估，并落实动物疫病预防、控制、净化、消灭措施。

**Article 15** The State shall establish an animal disease risk assessment system.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, based on domestic and foreign animal epidemics and the need to protect animal husbandry production and human health, promptly conduct risk assessments of animal diseases jointly with the competent department of health and other relevant departments of the State Council, and formulate and publish measures and technical specifications for the prevention, control, purification and elimination of animal diseases.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, jointly with the competent departments of health and other relevant departments of the people's government at the same level, conduct risk assessments of animal diseases within their respective administrative regions and implement measures for the prevention, control, purification and elimination of animal diseases.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家对严重危害养殖业生产和人体健康的动物疫病实施强制免疫。  
国务院农业农村主管部门确定强制免疫的动物疫病病种和区域。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门制定本行政区域的强制免疫计划；根据本行政区域动物疫病流行情况增加实施强制免疫的动物疫病病种和区域，报本级人民政府批准后执行，并报国务院农业农村主管部门备案。

**Article 16** The State shall implement compulsory immunization against animal diseases that seriously harm animal husbandry production and human health.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall determine the diseases and regions subject to compulsory immunization.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate compulsory immunization plans for their respective administrative regions; they may increase the diseases and regions subject to compulsory immunization based on the prevalence of animal diseases in their respective administrative regions, and implement them after obtaining approval from the people's government at the same level, and report them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　饲养动物的单位和个人应当履行动物疫病强制免疫义务，按照强制免疫计划和技术规范，对动物实施免疫接种，并按照国家有关规定建立免疫档案、加施畜禽标识，保证可追溯。  
实施强制免疫接种的动物未达到免疫质量要求，实施补充免疫接种后仍不符合免疫质量要求的，有关单位和个人应当按照国家有关规定处理。  
用于预防接种的疫苗应当符合国家质量标准。

**Article 17** Entities and individuals raising animals shall fulfill the obligation of compulsory immunization against animal diseases, conduct immunization of animals in accordance with the compulsory immunization plan and technical specifications, and establish immunization archives and affix livestock and poultry identification marks in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State to ensure traceability.  
If an animal that has undergone compulsory immunization fails to meet the immunization quality requirements, and still does not meet the immunization quality requirements after supplementary immunization, the relevant entities and individuals shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The vaccines used for preventive immunization shall meet the national quality standards.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责组织实施动物疫病强制免疫计划，并对饲养动物的单位和个人履行强制免疫义务的情况进行监督检查。  
乡级人民政府、街道办事处组织本辖区饲养动物的单位和个人做好强制免疫，协助做好监督检查；村民委员会、居民委员会协助做好相关工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当定期对本行政区域的强制免疫计划实施情况和效果进行评估，并向社会公布评估结果。

**Article 18** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of animal disease compulsory immunization plans, and shall supervise and inspect the fulfillment of compulsory immunization obligations by entities and individuals raising animals.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall organize entities and individuals raising animals within their respective jurisdictions to properly carry out compulsory immunization, and assist in supervision and inspection; villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist in the relevant work.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly evaluate the implementation and effectiveness of the compulsory immunization plan within their respective administrative regions, and publish the evaluation results to the public.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家实行动物疫病监测和疫情预警制度。  
县级以上人民政府建立健全动物疫病监测网络，加强动物疫病监测。  
国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定国家动物疫病监测计划。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据国家动物疫病监测计划，制定本行政区域的动物疫病监测计划。  
动物疫病预防控制机构按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定和动物疫病监测计划，对动物疫病的发生、流行等情况进行监测；从事动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输以及动物产品生产、经营、加工、贮藏、无害化处理等活动的单位和个人不得拒绝或者阻碍。  
国务院农业农村主管部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门根据对动物疫病发生、流行趋势的预测，及时发出动物疫情预警。地方各级人民政府接到动物疫情预警后，应当及时采取预防、控制措施。

**Article 19** The State shall implement an animal disease monitoring and epidemic early warning system.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve animal disease monitoring networks and strengthen animal disease monitoring.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, jointly with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate a national animal disease monitoring plan. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate animal disease monitoring plans for their respective administrative regions based on the national animal disease monitoring plan.  
Animal disease prevention and control institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council and the animal disease monitoring plan, monitor the occurrence and prevalence of animal diseases; entities and individuals engaged in animal raising, slaughter, trading, isolation, transportation, and production, trading, processing, storage, harmless treatment of animal products shall not refuse or obstruct such monitoring.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council and the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the prediction of the occurrence and epidemic trend of animal diseases, promptly issue animal epidemic early warnings. After receiving the animal epidemic early warning, local people's governments at all levels shall promptly take prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　陆路边境省、自治区人民政府根据动物疫病防控需要，合理设置动物疫病监测站点，健全监测工作机制，防范境外动物疫病传入。  
科技、海关等部门按照本法和有关法律法规的规定做好动物疫病监测预警工作，并定期与农业农村主管部门互通情况，紧急情况及时通报。  
县级以上人民政府应当完善野生动物疫源疫病监测体系和工作机制，根据需要合理布局监测站点；野生动物保护、农业农村主管部门按照职责分工做好野生动物疫源疫病监测等工作，并定期互通情况，紧急情况及时通报。

**Article 20** The people's governments of land border provinces and autonomous regions shall, based on the needs of animal disease prevention and control, rationally set up animal disease monitoring stations, improve the monitoring working mechanism, and prevent the introduction of animal diseases from abroad.  
Science and technology, customs and other departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant laws and regulations, properly carry out animal disease monitoring and early warning work, and regularly exchange information with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, and promptly notify each other in emergency situations.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall improve the monitoring system and working mechanism for wildlife epidemic sources and diseases, and rationally arrange monitoring stations as needed; the competent departments of wildlife protection and agriculture and rural affairs shall, according to their respective duties, properly carry out the monitoring of wildlife epidemic sources and diseases, and regularly exchange information, and promptly notify each other in emergency situations.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家支持地方建立无规定动物疫病区，鼓励动物饲养场建设无规定动物疫病生物安全隔离区。对符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定标准的无规定动物疫病区和无规定动物疫病生物安全隔离区，国务院农业农村主管部门验收合格予以公布，并对其维持情况进行监督检查。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定并组织实施本行政区域的无规定动物疫病区建设方案。国务院农业农村主管部门指导跨省、自治区、直辖市无规定动物疫病区建设。  
国务院农业农村主管部门根据行政区划、养殖屠宰产业布局、风险评估情况等对动物疫病实施分区防控，可以采取禁止或者限制特定动物、动物产品跨区域调运等措施。

**Article 21** The State supports the establishment of disease-free zones by local authorities and encourages the construction of disease-free biosecurity compartments in animal farms. For disease-free zones and disease-free biosecurity compartments that meet the standards prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, after acceptance inspection and passing, publish them, and shall supervise and inspect their maintenance.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate and organize the implementation of plans for establishing disease-free zones within their respective administrative regions. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall guide the establishment of cross-provincial, cross-autonomous regional and cross-municipal disease-free zones.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, based on administrative divisions, the layout of animal husbandry and slaughter industry, risk assessment, etc., implement zoning prevention and control of animal diseases, and may take measures such as prohibiting or restricting the cross-regional movement of specific animals and animal products.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国务院农业农村主管部门制定并组织实施动物疫病净化、消灭规划。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据动物疫病净化、消灭规划，制定并组织实施本行政区域的动物疫病净化、消灭计划。  
动物疫病预防控制机构按照动物疫病净化、消灭规划、计划，开展动物疫病净化技术指导、培训，对动物疫病净化效果进行监测、评估。  
国家推进动物疫病净化，鼓励和支持饲养动物的单位和个人开展动物疫病净化。饲养动物的单位和个人达到国务院农业农村主管部门规定的净化标准的，由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门予以公布。

**Article 22** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall formulate and organize the implementation of plans for the purification and elimination of animal diseases.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the plans for the purification and elimination of animal diseases, formulate and organize the implementation of purification and elimination plans for animal diseases within their respective administrative regions.  
Animal disease prevention and control institutions shall, in accordance with the plans for the purification and elimination of animal diseases, carry out technical guidance and training for animal disease purification, and monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of animal disease purification.  
The State promotes the purification of animal diseases and encourages and supports entities and individuals raising animals to carry out animal disease purification. If entities and individuals raising animals meet the purification standards prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall publish such information.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　种用、乳用动物应当符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的健康标准。  
饲养种用、乳用动物的单位和个人，应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的要求，定期开展动物疫病检测；检测不合格的，应当按照国家有关规定处理。

**Article 23** Breeding and dairy animals shall meet the health standards prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Entities and individuals raising breeding and dairy animals shall, in accordance with the requirements of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, regularly conduct animal disease testing; if the test results are unqualified, they shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　动物饲养场和隔离场所、动物屠宰加工场所以及动物和动物产品无害化处理场所，应当符合下列动物防疫条件：  
（一）场所的位置与居民生活区、生活饮用水水源地、学校、医院等公共场所的距离符合国务院农业农村主管部门的规定；  
（二）生产经营区域封闭隔离，工程设计和有关流程符合动物防疫要求；  
（三）有与其规模相适应的污水、污物处理设施，病死动物、病害动物产品无害化处理设施设备或者冷藏冷冻设施设备，以及清洗消毒设施设备；  
（四）有与其规模相适应的执业兽医或者动物防疫技术人员；  
（五）有完善的隔离消毒、购销台账、日常巡查等动物防疫制度；  
（六）具备国务院农业农村主管部门规定的其他动物防疫条件。  
动物和动物产品无害化处理场所除应当符合前款规定的条件外，还应当具有病原检测设备、检测能力和符合动物防疫要求的专用运输车辆。

**Article 24** Animal farms, isolation sites, animal slaughtering and processing sites, and sites for harmless treatment of animals and animal products shall meet the following animal epidemic prevention conditions:  
(1) The distance between the location of the site and residential areas, drinking water sources, schools, hospitals and other public places shall comply with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council;  
(2) The production and operation area shall be closed and isolated, and the engineering design and relevant processes shall meet the animal epidemic prevention requirements;  
(3) There shall be sewage and waste treatment facilities, facilities and equipment for harmless treatment of dead and diseased animals and diseased animal products or refrigeration and freezing facilities and equipment, and cleaning and disinfection facilities and equipment, which are commensurate with the scale of the site;  
(4) There shall be licensed veterinarians or animal epidemic prevention technicians commensurate with the scale of the site;  
(5) There shall be sound animal epidemic prevention systems such as isolation and disinfection, purchase and sales ledgers, and daily inspections;  
(6) Other animal epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be met.  
In addition to meeting the conditions specified in the preceding paragraph, sites for harmless treatment of animals and animal products shall also have pathogen testing equipment, testing capabilities, and specialized transport vehicles that meet animal epidemic prevention requirements.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　国家实行动物防疫条件审查制度。  
开办动物饲养场和隔离场所、动物屠宰加工场所以及动物和动物产品无害化处理场所，应当向县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门提出申请，并附具相关材料。受理申请的农业农村主管部门应当依照本法和《中华人民共和国行政许可法》的规定进行审查。经审查合格的，发给动物防疫条件合格证；不合格的，应当通知申请人并说明理由。  
动物防疫条件合格证应当载明申请人的名称（姓名）、场（厂）址、动物（动物产品）种类等事项。

**Article 25** The State shall implement a review system for animal epidemic prevention conditions.  
To establish an animal farm, isolation site, animal slaughtering and processing site, or site for harmless treatment of animals and animal products, an application shall be submitted to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level, together with relevant materials. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that accepts the application shall conduct a review in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China. If the review is passed, an animal epidemic prevention condition certificate shall be issued; if it fails, the applicant shall be notified and the reasons shall be stated.  
The animal epidemic prevention condition certificate shall specify the name (or personal name) of the applicant, the site (or plant) address, the type of animals (or animal products), and other matters.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　经营动物、动物产品的集贸市场应当具备国务院农业农村主管部门规定的动物防疫条件，并接受农业农村主管部门的监督检查。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据本地情况，决定在城市特定区域禁止家畜家禽活体交易。

**Article 26** Trading markets for animals and animal products shall meet the animal epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and shall accept supervision and inspection by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on local conditions, decide to prohibit live trading of livestock and poultry in specific urban areas.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　动物、动物产品的运载工具、垫料、包装物、容器等应当符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的动物防疫要求。  
染疫动物及其排泄物、染疫动物产品，运载工具中的动物排泄物以及垫料、包装物、容器等被污染的物品，应当按照国家有关规定处理，不得随意处置。

**Article 27** The means of transport, bedding, packaging materials, containers, etc. for animals and animal products shall meet the animal epidemic prevention requirements prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Diseased animals and their excreta, diseased animal products, animal excreta in means of transport, and contaminated items such as bedding, packaging materials, containers, etc. shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and shall not be disposed of arbitrarily.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　采集、保存、运输动物病料或者病原微生物以及从事病原微生物研究、教学、检测、诊断等活动，应当遵守国家有关病原微生物实验室管理的规定。

**Article 28** The collection, storage and transportation of animal pathological materials or pathogenic microorganisms, and activities such as research, teaching, testing and diagnosis of pathogenic microorganisms, shall comply with the relevant provisions of the State on the management of pathogenic microorganism laboratories.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　禁止屠宰、经营、运输下列动物和生产、经营、加工、贮藏、运输下列动物产品：  
（一）封锁疫区内与所发生动物疫病有关的；  
（二）疫区内易感染的；  
（三）依法应当检疫而未经检疫或者检疫不合格的；  
（四）染疫或者疑似染疫的；  
（五）病死或者死因不明的；  
（六）其他不符合国务院农业农村主管部门有关动物防疫规定的。  
因实施集中无害化处理需要暂存、运输动物和动物产品并按照规定采取防疫措施的，不适用前款规定。

**Article 29** It shall be prohibited to slaughter, trade or transport the following animals, and to produce, trade, process, store or transport the following animal products:  
(1) Animals related to the occurring animal disease in a locked-down epidemic area;  
(2) Susceptible animals in an epidemic area;  
(3) Animals or animal products that shall, according to law, be quarantined but have not been quarantined or have been quarantined and found unqualified;  
(4) Diseased or suspected diseased animals or animal products;  
(5) Dead or animals of unknown cause of death;  
(6) Others that do not comply with the relevant provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council on animal epidemic prevention.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to the temporary storage and transportation of animals and animal products necessary for the implementation of centralized harmless treatment and the adoption of epidemic prevention measures in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　单位和个人饲养犬只，应当按照规定定期免疫接种狂犬病疫苗，凭动物诊疗机构出具的免疫证明向所在地养犬登记机关申请登记。  
携带犬只出户的，应当按照规定佩戴犬牌并采取系犬绳等措施，防止犬只伤人、疫病传播。  
街道办事处、乡级人民政府组织协调居民委员会、村民委员会，做好本辖区流浪犬、猫的控制和处置，防止疫病传播。  
县级人民政府和乡级人民政府、街道办事处应当结合本地实际，做好农村地区饲养犬只的防疫管理工作。  
饲养犬只防疫管理的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 30** Entities and individuals raising dogs shall, in accordance with the provisions, regularly immunize them with rabies vaccines, and apply for registration with the local dog registration authority on the basis of the immunization certificate issued by an animal medical institution.  
When taking a dog outdoors, the dog shall be worn with a dog tag and measures such as leashing shall be taken in accordance with the provisions to prevent the dog from injuring people or spreading diseases.  
Sub-district offices and township people's governments shall organize and coordinate residents' committees and villagers' committees to properly control and dispose of stray dogs and cats within their respective jurisdictions to prevent the spread of diseases.  
County people's governments, township people's governments and sub-district offices shall, in light of local conditions, properly carry out epidemic prevention management for dogs raised in rural areas.  
Specific measures for the epidemic prevention management of dog raising shall be formulated by provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government.

### 第三章　动物疫情的报告、通报和公布

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　从事动物疫病监测、检测、检验检疫、研究、诊疗以及动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输等活动的单位和个人，发现动物染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当立即向所在地农业农村主管部门或者动物疫病预防控制机构报告，并迅速采取隔离等控制措施，防止动物疫情扩散。其他单位和个人发现动物染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当及时报告。  
接到动物疫情报告的单位，应当及时采取临时隔离控制等必要措施，防止延误防控时机，并及时按照国家规定的程序上报。

**Article 31** Entities and individuals engaged in activities such as animal disease monitoring, testing, inspection and quarantine, research, diagnosis and treatment, and animal raising, slaughter, trading, isolation, transportation, etc., upon discovering that an animal is diseased or suspected of being diseased, shall immediately report to the local competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or animal disease prevention and control institution, and promptly take control measures such as isolation to prevent the spread of the animal epidemic. Other entities and individuals, upon discovering that an animal is diseased or suspected of being diseased, shall promptly report such information.  
The entity receiving the report of an animal epidemic shall promptly take necessary measures such as temporary isolation and control to prevent delay in prevention and control opportunities, and promptly report to the higher authorities in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　动物疫情由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门认定；其中重大动物疫情由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门认定，必要时报国务院农业农村主管部门认定。  
本法所称重大动物疫情，是指一、二、三类动物疫病突然发生，迅速传播，给养殖业生产安全造成严重威胁、危害，以及可能对公众身体健康与生命安全造成危害的情形。  
在重大动物疫情报告期间，必要时，所在地县级以上地方人民政府可以作出封锁决定并采取扑杀、销毁等措施。

**Article 32** Animal epidemics shall be determined by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of people's governments at or above the county level; among them, major animal epidemics shall be determined by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and where necessary, reported to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for determination.  
For the purposes of this Law, "major animal epidemic" means the sudden occurrence and rapid spread of Category I, II or III animal diseases, causing serious threats and harm to the safety of animal husbandry production, and possibly causing harm to public health and life safety.  
During the reporting period of a major animal epidemic, if necessary, the local people's government at or above the county level may make a decision on lockdown and take measures such as culling and destruction.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家实行动物疫情通报制度。  
国务院农业农村主管部门应当及时向国务院卫生健康等有关部门和军队有关部门以及省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门通报重大动物疫情的发生和处置情况。  
海关发现进出境动物和动物产品染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当及时处置并向农业农村主管部门通报。  
县级以上地方人民政府野生动物保护主管部门发现野生动物染疫或者疑似染疫的，应当及时处置并向本级人民政府农业农村主管部门通报。  
国务院农业农村主管部门应当依照我国缔结或者参加的条约、协定，及时向有关国际组织或者贸易方通报重大动物疫情的发生和处置情况。

**Article 33** The State shall implement an animal epidemic notification system.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall promptly notify the relevant departments such as the health department of the State Council and relevant military departments, as well as the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, of the occurrence and disposal of major animal epidemics.  
If the customs discovers that animals or animal products entering or leaving the country are diseased or suspected of being diseased, it shall promptly handle the situation and notify the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs.  
If the competent department of wildlife protection of a local people's government at or above the county level discovers that wild animals are diseased or suspected of being diseased, it shall promptly handle the situation and notify the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the same level.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, in accordance with the treaties or agreements concluded or acceded to by China, promptly notify the relevant international organizations or trading partners of the occurrence and disposal of major animal epidemics.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　发生人畜共患传染病疫情时，县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门与本级人民政府卫生健康、野生动物保护等主管部门应当及时相互通报。  
发生人畜共患传染病时，卫生健康主管部门应当对疫区易感染的人群进行监测，并应当依照《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》的规定及时公布疫情，采取相应的预防、控制措施。

**Article 34** When an epidemic of a zoonotic infectious disease occurs, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of a people's government at or above the county level and the competent departments of health, wildlife protection, etc. of the people's government at the same level shall promptly notify each other.  
When a zoonotic infectious disease occurs, the competent department of health shall monitor the susceptible population in the epidemic area, and shall promptly publish the epidemic in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases, and take corresponding prevention and control measures.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　患有人畜共患传染病的人员不得直接从事动物疫病监测、检测、检验检疫、诊疗以及易感染动物的饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输等活动。

**Article 35** Persons suffering from a zoonotic infectious disease shall not directly engage in activities such as animal disease monitoring, testing, inspection and quarantine, diagnosis and treatment, and raising, slaughter, trading, isolation, transportation of susceptible animals.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　国务院农业农村主管部门向社会及时公布全国动物疫情，也可以根据需要授权省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门公布本行政区域的动物疫情。其他单位和个人不得发布动物疫情。

**Article 36** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall promptly publish national animal epidemics to the public, and may also, as needed, authorize the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government to publish animal epidemics within their respective administrative regions. No other entity or individual may release animal epidemics.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　任何单位和个人不得瞒报、谎报、迟报、漏报动物疫情，不得授意他人瞒报、谎报、迟报动物疫情，不得阻碍他人报告动物疫情。

**Article 37** No entity or individual may conceal, falsely report, delay reporting, or omit reporting animal epidemics, may not instigate others to conceal, falsely report, or delay reporting animal epidemics, and may not obstruct others from reporting animal epidemics.

### 第四章　动物疫病的控制

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　发生一类动物疫病时，应当采取下列控制措施：  
（一）所在地县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当立即派人到现场，划定疫点、疫区、受威胁区，调查疫源，及时报请本级人民政府对疫区实行封锁。疫区范围涉及两个以上行政区域的，由有关行政区域共同的上一级人民政府对疫区实行封锁，或者由各有关行政区域的上一级人民政府共同对疫区实行封锁。必要时，上级人民政府可以责成下级人民政府对疫区实行封锁；  
（二）县级以上地方人民政府应当立即组织有关部门和单位采取封锁、隔离、扑杀、销毁、消毒、无害化处理、紧急免疫接种等强制性措施；  
（三）在封锁期间，禁止染疫、疑似染疫和易感染的动物、动物产品流出疫区，禁止非疫区的易感染动物进入疫区，并根据需要对出入疫区的人员、运输工具及有关物品采取消毒和其他限制性措施。

**Article 38** When a Category I animal disease occurs, the following control measures shall be taken:  
(1) The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall immediately dispatch personnel to the scene, delimit the epidemic point, epidemic area and threatened area, investigate the source of the epidemic, and promptly report to the people's government at the same level to request a lockdown of the epidemic area. If the epidemic area involves two or more administrative regions, the common higher-level people's government of the relevant administrative regions shall impose the lockdown, or the higher-level people's governments of the relevant administrative regions shall jointly impose the lockdown. If necessary, the higher-level people's government may instruct the lower-level people's government to impose the lockdown;  
(2) The local people's government at or above the county level shall immediately organize relevant departments and entities to take mandatory measures such as lockdown, isolation, culling, destruction, disinfection, harmless treatment, and emergency immunization;  
(3) During the lockdown period, it shall be prohibited for diseased, suspected diseased and susceptible animals and animal products to leave the epidemic area, and for susceptible animals from non-epidemic areas to enter the epidemic area, and disinfection and other restrictive measures shall be taken for personnel, means of transport and related articles entering or leaving the epidemic area as necessary.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　发生二类动物疫病时，应当采取下列控制措施：  
（一）所在地县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当划定疫点、疫区、受威胁区；  
（二）县级以上地方人民政府根据需要组织有关部门和单位采取隔离、扑杀、销毁、消毒、无害化处理、紧急免疫接种、限制易感染的动物和动物产品及有关物品出入等措施。

**Article 39** When a Category II animal disease occurs, the following control measures shall be taken:  
(1) The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall delimit the epidemic point, epidemic area and threatened area;  
(2) The local people's government at or above the county level shall, as needed, organize relevant departments and entities to take measures such as isolation, culling, destruction, disinfection, harmless treatment, emergency immunization, and restricting the entry and exit of susceptible animals and animal products and related articles.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　疫点、疫区、受威胁区的撤销和疫区封锁的解除，按照国务院农业农村主管部门规定的标准和程序评估后，由原决定机关决定并宣布。

**Article 40** The revocation of epidemic points, epidemic areas and threatened areas and the lifting of the lockdown of epidemic areas shall be evaluated in accordance with the standards and procedures prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and then decided and announced by the original deciding authority.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　发生三类动物疫病时，所在地县级、乡级人民政府应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定组织防治。

**Article 41** When a Category III animal disease occurs, the local county and township people's governments shall organize prevention and control in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　二、三类动物疫病呈暴发性流行时，按照一类动物疫病处理。

**Article 42** When Category II or III animal diseases become epidemic in an explosive manner, they shall be handled as Category I animal diseases.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　疫区内有关单位和个人，应当遵守县级以上人民政府及其农业农村主管部门依法作出的有关控制动物疫病的规定。  
任何单位和个人不得藏匿、转移、盗掘已被依法隔离、封存、处理的动物和动物产品。

**Article 43** Relevant entities and individuals within an epidemic area shall comply with the provisions on the control of animal diseases made according to law by the people's government at or above the county level and its competent department of agriculture and rural affairs.  
No entity or individual may hide, transfer, or unlawfully excavate animals and animal products that have been isolated, sealed up or disposed of according to law.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　发生动物疫情时，航空、铁路、道路、水路运输企业应当优先组织运送防疫人员和物资。

**Article 44** When an animal epidemic occurs, air, railway, road and waterway transport enterprises shall give priority to arranging the transportation of epidemic prevention personnel and materials.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国务院农业农村主管部门根据动物疫病的性质、特点和可能造成的社会危害，制定国家重大动物疫情应急预案报国务院批准，并按照不同动物疫病病种、流行特点和危害程度，分别制定实施方案。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据上级重大动物疫情应急预案和本地区的实际情况，制定本行政区域的重大动物疫情应急预案，报上一级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案，并抄送上一级人民政府应急管理部门。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门按照不同动物疫病病种、流行特点和危害程度，分别制定实施方案。  
重大动物疫情应急预案和实施方案根据疫情状况及时调整。

**Article 45** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, based on the nature, characteristics and possible social harm of animal diseases, formulate a national contingency plan for major animal epidemics and submit it to the State Council for approval, and formulate implementation plans according to different types of animal diseases, epidemic characteristics and degrees of harm.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the higher-level contingency plan for major animal epidemics and the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate contingency plans for major animal epidemics within their respective administrative regions, report them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the next higher level for recordation, and send a copy to the emergency management department of the people's government at the next higher level. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate implementation plans according to different types of animal diseases, epidemic characteristics and degrees of harm.  
The contingency plans and implementation plans for major animal epidemics shall be promptly adjusted according to the epidemic situation.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　发生重大动物疫情时，国务院农业农村主管部门负责划定动物疫病风险区，禁止或者限制特定动物、动物产品由高风险区向低风险区调运。

**Article 46** When a major animal epidemic occurs, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for delimiting animal disease risk areas, and prohibit or restrict the movement of specific animals and animal products from high-risk areas to low-risk areas.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　发生重大动物疫情时，依照法律和国务院的规定以及应急预案采取应急处置措施。

**Article 47** When a major animal epidemic occurs, emergency response measures shall be taken in accordance with the law, the provisions of the State Council and the contingency plan.

### 第五章　动物和动物产品的检疫

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　动物卫生监督机构依照本法和国务院农业农村主管部门的规定对动物、动物产品实施检疫。  
动物卫生监督机构的官方兽医具体实施动物、动物产品检疫。

**Article 48** Animal health supervision institutions shall carry out quarantine of animals and animal products in accordance with this Law and the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Official veterinarians of animal health supervision institutions shall specifically carry out the quarantine of animals and animal products.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　屠宰、出售或者运输动物以及出售或者运输动物产品前，货主应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定向所在地动物卫生监督机构申报检疫。  
动物卫生监督机构接到检疫申报后，应当及时指派官方兽医对动物、动物产品实施检疫；检疫合格的，出具检疫证明、加施检疫标志。实施检疫的官方兽医应当在检疫证明、检疫标志上签字或者盖章，并对检疫结论负责。  
动物饲养场、屠宰企业的执业兽医或者动物防疫技术人员，应当协助官方兽医实施检疫。

**Article 49** Before slaughtering, selling or transporting animals, or selling or transporting animal products, the owner shall declare quarantine to the local animal health supervision institution in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Upon receiving the quarantine declaration, the animal health supervision institution shall promptly assign an official veterinarian to carry out quarantine of the animals and animal products; if the quarantine is passed, a quarantine certificate shall be issued and a quarantine mark shall be affixed. The official veterinarian carrying out the quarantine shall sign or affix a seal on the quarantine certificate and quarantine mark, and shall be responsible for the quarantine conclusion.  
Licensed veterinarians or animal epidemic prevention technicians of animal farms and slaughter enterprises shall assist the official veterinarian in carrying out quarantine.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　因科研、药用、展示等特殊情形需要非食用性利用的野生动物，应当按照国家有关规定报动物卫生监督机构检疫，检疫合格的，方可利用。  
人工捕获的野生动物，应当按照国家有关规定报捕获地动物卫生监督机构检疫，检疫合格的，方可饲养、经营和运输。  
国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定野生动物检疫办法。

**Article 50** Wild animals that need to be used for non-edible purposes due to special circumstances such as scientific research, medicinal use, or display shall be reported to the animal health supervision institution for quarantine in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and may only be used after passing quarantine.  
Wild animals artificially captured shall be reported to the animal health supervision institution at the place of capture for quarantine in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and may only be raised, traded and transported after passing quarantine.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, jointly with the competent department of wildlife protection of the State Council, formulate measures for the quarantine of wild animals.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　屠宰、经营、运输的动物，以及用于科研、展示、演出和比赛等非食用性利用的动物，应当附有检疫证明；经营和运输的动物产品，应当附有检疫证明、检疫标志。

**Article 51** Animals for slaughter, trading, and transport, as well as animals for non-edible use such as scientific research, exhibition, performance and competition shall be accompanied by quarantine certificates; animal products for trading and transport shall be accompanied by quarantine certificates and quarantine marks.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　经航空、铁路、道路、水路运输动物和动物产品的，托运人托运时应当提供检疫证明；没有检疫证明的，承运人不得承运。  
进出口动物和动物产品，承运人凭进口报关单证或者海关签发的检疫单证运递。  
从事动物运输的单位、个人以及车辆，应当向所在地县级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案，妥善保存行程路线和托运人提供的动物名称、检疫证明编号、数量等信息。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
运载工具在装载前和卸载后应当及时清洗、消毒。

**Article 52** Where animals and animal products are transported by air, railway, road or waterway, the consignor shall provide the quarantine certificate at the time of consignment; if there is no quarantine certificate, the carrier shall not carry them.  
The carrier shall transport imported and exported animals and animal products based on the import customs declaration documents or the quarantine documents issued by the customs.  
Units, individuals and vehicles engaged in animal transport shall file with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level where they are located, and properly keep the travel route and information such as the animal names, quarantine certificate numbers, and quantities provided by the consignor. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
The means of transport shall be promptly cleaned and disinfected before loading and after unloading.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定并公布道路运输的动物进入本行政区域的指定通道，设置引导标志。跨省、自治区、直辖市通过道路运输动物的，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府设立的指定通道入省境或者过省境。

**Article 53** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall determine and publish the designated passageways for animals transported by road entering their administrative regions, and set up guiding signs. Where animals are transported by road across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government, they shall enter or pass through the provincial boundaries via the designated passageways established by the people's governments of the provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　输入到无规定动物疫病区的动物、动物产品，货主应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定向无规定动物疫病区所在地动物卫生监督机构申报检疫，经检疫合格的，方可进入。

**Article 54** For animals and animal products imported into an animal disease-free zone, the owner shall apply for quarantine to the animal health supervision institution located in the animal disease-free zone in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, and may only enter after passing the quarantine inspection.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　跨省、自治区、直辖市引进的种用、乳用动物到达输入地后，货主应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定对引进的种用、乳用动物进行隔离观察。

**Article 55** After breeding and dairy animals introduced across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government arrive at the destination, the owner shall carry out isolation observation on the introduced breeding and dairy animals in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　经检疫不合格的动物、动物产品，货主应当在农业农村主管部门的监督下按照国家有关规定处理，处理费用由货主承担。

**Article 56** For animals and animal products that fail the quarantine inspection, the owner shall, under the supervision of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs, dispose of them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, and the disposal expenses shall be borne by the owner.

### 第六章　病死动物和病害动物产品的无害化处理

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　从事动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离以及动物产品生产、经营、加工、贮藏等活动的单位和个人，应当按照国家有关规定做好病死动物、病害动物产品的无害化处理，或者委托动物和动物产品无害化处理场所处理。  
从事动物、动物产品运输的单位和个人，应当配合做好病死动物和病害动物产品的无害化处理，不得在途中擅自弃置和处理有关动物和动物产品。  
任何单位和个人不得买卖、加工、随意弃置病死动物和病害动物产品。  
动物和动物产品无害化处理管理办法由国务院农业农村、野生动物保护主管部门按照职责制定。

**Article 57** Units and individuals engaged in activities such as animal breeding, slaughtering, trading, isolation, and production, trading, processing, storage of animal products shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, properly carry out the harmless treatment of dead or diseased animals and diseased animal products, or entrust an animal and animal product harmless treatment facility to carry out the treatment.  
Units and individuals engaged in the transport of animals and animal products shall cooperate in properly carrying out the harmless treatment of dead or diseased animals and diseased animal products, and shall not abandon or dispose of the relevant animals and animal products without authorization during transit.  
No unit or individual may buy, sell, process, or arbitrarily discard dead or diseased animals and diseased animal products.  
The measures for the administration of harmless treatment of animals and animal products shall be formulated by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and wildlife protection of the State Council in accordance with their respective duties.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　在江河、湖泊、水库等水域发现的死亡畜禽，由所在地县级人民政府组织收集、处理并溯源。  
在城市公共场所和乡村发现的死亡畜禽，由所在地街道办事处、乡级人民政府组织收集、处理并溯源。  
在野外环境发现的死亡野生动物，由所在地野生动物保护主管部门收集、处理。

**Article 58** Dead livestock and poultry found in waters such as rivers, lakes and reservoirs shall be collected, disposed of and traced by the people's government at the county level of the locality.  
Dead livestock and poultry found in urban public places and rural areas shall be collected, disposed of and traced by the sub-district office or the people's government at the township level of the locality.  
Dead wild animals found in wild environments shall be collected and disposed of by the competent department of wildlife protection of the locality.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定动物和动物产品集中无害化处理场所建设规划，建立政府主导、市场运作的无害化处理机制。

**Article 59** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate construction plans for centralized harmless treatment facilities for animals and animal products, and establish a government-led, market-operated harmless treatment mechanism.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　各级财政对病死动物无害化处理提供补助。具体补助标准和办法由县级以上人民政府财政部门会同本级人民政府农业农村、野生动物保护等有关部门制定。

**Article 60** Finance at all levels shall provide subsidies for the harmless treatment of dead animals. Specific subsidy standards and measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the people's government at or above the county level jointly with the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, wildlife protection and other relevant departments of the same level.

### 第七章　动物诊疗

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　从事动物诊疗活动的机构，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有与动物诊疗活动相适应并符合动物防疫条件的场所；  
（二）有与动物诊疗活动相适应的执业兽医；  
（三）有与动物诊疗活动相适应的兽医器械和设备；  
（四）有完善的管理制度。  
动物诊疗机构包括动物医院、动物诊所以及其他提供动物诊疗服务的机构。

**Article 61** An institution engaged in animal diagnosis and treatment activities shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) have premises that are suitable for animal diagnosis and treatment activities and meet animal epidemic prevention conditions;  
(2) have licensed veterinarians that are suitable for animal diagnosis and treatment activities;  
(3) have veterinary instruments and equipment that are suitable for animal diagnosis and treatment activities;  
(4) have a complete management system.  
Animal diagnosis and treatment institutions include animal hospitals, animal clinics and other institutions providing animal diagnosis and treatment services.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　从事动物诊疗活动的机构，应当向县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门申请动物诊疗许可证。受理申请的农业农村主管部门应当依照本法和《中华人民共和国行政许可法》的规定进行审查。经审查合格的，发给动物诊疗许可证；不合格的，应当通知申请人并说明理由。

**Article 62** An institution engaged in animal diagnosis and treatment activities shall apply to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level for an animal diagnosis and treatment license. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that accepts the application shall conduct examination in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Administrative License Law of the People's Republic of China. If the examination is passed, the animal diagnosis and treatment license shall be issued; if not, the applicant shall be notified and the reasons shall be explained.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　动物诊疗许可证应当载明诊疗机构名称、诊疗活动范围、从业地点和法定代表人（负责人）等事项。  
动物诊疗许可证载明事项变更的，应当申请变更或者换发动物诊疗许可证。

**Article 63** The animal diagnosis and treatment license shall state the name of the diagnosis and treatment institution, the scope of diagnosis and treatment activities, the practice location, and the legal representative (responsible person) and other matters.  
If the matters stated in the animal diagnosis and treatment license change, an application for modification or renewal of the animal diagnosis and treatment license shall be submitted.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　动物诊疗机构应当按照国务院农业农村主管部门的规定，做好诊疗活动中的卫生安全防护、消毒、隔离和诊疗废弃物处置等工作。

**Article 64** An animal diagnosis and treatment institution shall, in accordance with the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, properly carry out health and safety protection, disinfection, isolation and disposal of diagnosis and treatment waste in the diagnosis and treatment activities.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　从事动物诊疗活动，应当遵守有关动物诊疗的操作技术规范，使用符合规定的兽药和兽医器械。  
兽药和兽医器械的管理办法由国务院规定。

**Article 65** Any person engaged in animal diagnosis and treatment activities shall comply with the relevant operational technical specifications for animal diagnosis and treatment, and use veterinary drugs and veterinary instruments that conform to the provisions.  
The measures for the administration of veterinary drugs and veterinary instruments shall be prescribed by the State Council.

### 第八章　兽医管理

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家实行官方兽医任命制度。  
官方兽医应当具备国务院农业农村主管部门规定的条件，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门按照程序确认，由所在地县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门任命。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
海关的官方兽医应当具备规定的条件，由海关总署任命。具体办法由海关总署会同国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 66** The State shall implement a system of appointment of official veterinarians.  
Official veterinarians shall meet the conditions prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, be confirmed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government according to procedures, and be appointed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level of the locality. Specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Official veterinarians of the customs shall meet the prescribed conditions and be appointed by the General Administration of Customs. Specific measures shall be formulated by the General Administration of Customs jointly with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　官方兽医依法履行动物、动物产品检疫职责，任何单位和个人不得拒绝或者阻碍。

**Article 67** Official veterinarians shall perform the quarantine duties for animals and animal products according to law, and no unit or individual may refuse or obstruct them.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门制定官方兽医培训计划，提供培训条件，定期对官方兽医进行培训和考核。

**Article 68** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall formulate training plans for official veterinarians, provide training conditions, and conduct regular training and assessment of official veterinarians.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家实行执业兽医资格考试制度。具有兽医相关专业大学专科以上学历的人员或者符合条件的乡村兽医，通过执业兽医资格考试的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门颁发执业兽医资格证书；从事动物诊疗等经营活动的，还应当向所在地县级人民政府农业农村主管部门备案。  
执业兽医资格考试办法由国务院农业农村主管部门商国务院人力资源主管部门制定。

**Article 69** The State shall implement a system of licensed veterinarian qualification examination. Persons who have an associate degree or above in a veterinary-related major or rural veterinarians who meet the conditions and pass the licensed veterinarian qualification examination shall be issued a licensed veterinarian qualification certificate by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; if they engage in business activities such as animal diagnosis and treatment, they shall also file with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level of the locality.  
The measures for the licensed veterinarian qualification examination shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council in consultation with the human resources department of the State Council.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　执业兽医开具兽医处方应当亲自诊断，并对诊断结论负责。  
国家鼓励执业兽医接受继续教育。执业兽医所在机构应当支持执业兽医参加继续教育。

**Article 70** A licensed veterinarian shall personally conduct diagnosis before issuing a veterinary prescription, and shall be responsible for the diagnosis conclusion.  
The State encourages licensed veterinarians to receive continuing education. The institution where the licensed veterinarian works shall support the licensed veterinarian's participation in continuing education.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　乡村兽医可以在乡村从事动物诊疗活动。具体管理办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 71** Rural veterinarians may engage in animal diagnosis and treatment activities in rural areas. Specific administrative measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　执业兽医、乡村兽医应当按照所在地人民政府和农业农村主管部门的要求，参加动物疫病预防、控制和动物疫情扑灭等活动。

**Article 72** Licensed veterinarians and rural veterinarians shall, in accordance with the requirements of the local people's government and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs, participate in activities such as animal epidemic prevention, control and eradication of animal epidemics.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　兽医行业协会提供兽医信息、技术、培训等服务，维护成员合法权益，按照章程建立健全行业规范和奖惩机制，加强行业自律，推动行业诚信建设，宣传动物防疫和兽医知识。

**Article 73** Veterinary industry associations shall provide services such as veterinary information, technology and training, safeguard the lawful rights and interests of members, establish and improve industry norms and reward and punishment mechanisms in accordance with their articles of association, strengthen industry self-discipline, promote industry integrity construction, and publicize animal epidemic prevention and veterinary knowledge.

### 第九章　监督管理

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门依照本法规定，对动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输以及动物产品生产、经营、加工、贮藏、运输等活动中的动物防疫实施监督管理。

**Article 74** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, exercise supervision and administration over animal epidemic prevention in activities such as animal breeding, slaughtering, trading, isolation, transport, and production, trading, processing, storage and transport of animal products.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　为控制动物疫病，县级人民政府农业农村主管部门应当派人在所在地依法设立的现有检查站执行监督检查任务；必要时，经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，可以设立临时性的动物防疫检查站，执行监督检查任务。

**Article 75** For the purpose of controlling animal epidemics, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level shall send personnel to existing checkpoints lawfully established in the locality to perform supervision and inspection tasks; when necessary, with the approval of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, temporary animal epidemic prevention checkpoints may be established to perform supervision and inspection tasks.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门执行监督检查任务，可以采取下列措施，有关单位和个人不得拒绝或者阻碍：  
（一）对动物、动物产品按照规定采样、留验、抽检；  
（二）对染疫或者疑似染疫的动物、动物产品及相关物品进行隔离、查封、扣押和处理；  
（三）对依法应当检疫而未经检疫的动物和动物产品，具备补检条件的实施补检，不具备补检条件的予以收缴销毁；  
（四）查验检疫证明、检疫标志和畜禽标识；  
（五）进入有关场所调查取证，查阅、复制与动物防疫有关的资料。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门根据动物疫病预防、控制需要，经所在地县级以上地方人民政府批准，可以在车站、港口、机场等相关场所派驻官方兽医或者工作人员。

**Article 76** When performing supervision and inspection tasks, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level may take the following measures, and no unit or individual may refuse or obstruct them:  
(1) sample, retain for inspection, and conduct random inspection of animals and animal products in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) isolate, seal up, distrain and dispose of animals and animal products that are infected or suspected to be infected with epidemic diseases and related articles;  
(3) conduct supplementary quarantine for animals and animal products that should have been quarantined according to law but have not been quarantined, if conditions for supplementary quarantine exist, or confiscate and destroy them if conditions for supplementary quarantine do not exist;  
(4) inspect quarantine certificates, quarantine marks and livestock and poultry identifiers;  
(5) enter relevant places to investigate and collect evidence, and consult and copy materials related to animal epidemic prevention.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level may, based on the needs of animal epidemic prevention and control and with the approval of the local people's government at or above the county level, station official veterinarians or staff members at relevant places such as stations, ports and airports.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　执法人员执行动物防疫监督检查任务，应当出示行政执法证件，佩带统一标志。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门及其工作人员不得从事与动物防疫有关的经营性活动，进行监督检查不得收取任何费用。

**Article 77** Law enforcement personnel performing animal epidemic prevention supervision and inspection tasks shall produce their administrative law enforcement certificates and wear uniform insignia.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level and its staff members shall not engage in any business activities related to animal epidemic prevention, and shall not charge any fees for carrying out supervision and inspection.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　禁止转让、伪造或者变造检疫证明、检疫标志或者畜禽标识。  
禁止持有、使用伪造或者变造的检疫证明、检疫标志或者畜禽标识。  
检疫证明、检疫标志的管理办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 78** It is prohibited to transfer, forge or alter quarantine certificates, quarantine marks or livestock and poultry identifiers.  
It is prohibited to hold or use forged or altered quarantine certificates, quarantine marks or livestock and poultry identifiers.  
The measures for the administration of quarantine certificates and quarantine marks shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

### 第十章　保障措施

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　县级以上人民政府应当将动物防疫工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划及年度计划。

**Article 79** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate animal epidemic prevention work into their national economic and social development plans and annual plans.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国家鼓励和支持动物防疫领域新技术、新设备、新产品等科学技术研究开发。

**Article 80** The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research and development of new technologies, new equipment, new products and the like in the field of animal epidemic prevention.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　县级人民政府应当为动物卫生监督机构配备与动物、动物产品检疫工作相适应的官方兽医，保障检疫工作条件。  
县级人民政府农业农村主管部门可以根据动物防疫工作需要，向乡、镇或者特定区域派驻兽医机构或者工作人员。

**Article 81** The people's government at the county level shall equip the animal health supervision institution with official veterinarians appropriate for the quarantine work of animals and animal products, and guarantee the conditions for quarantine work.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at the county level may, based on the needs of animal epidemic prevention work, station veterinary institutions or staff members in townships, towns or specific areas.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　国家鼓励和支持执业兽医、乡村兽医和动物诊疗机构开展动物防疫和疫病诊疗活动；鼓励养殖企业、兽药及饲料生产企业组建动物防疫服务团队，提供防疫服务。地方人民政府组织村级防疫员参加动物疫病防治工作的，应当保障村级防疫员合理劳务报酬。

**Article 82** The State encourages and supports licensed veterinarians, rural veterinarians and animal diagnosis and treatment institutions to carry out animal epidemic prevention and disease diagnosis and treatment activities; encourages breeding enterprises, veterinary drug and feed production enterprises to form animal epidemic prevention service teams to provide epidemic prevention services. Where a local people's government organizes village-level epidemic prevention workers to participate in animal epidemic prevention and control work, it shall guarantee their reasonable labor remuneration.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　县级以上人民政府按照本级政府职责，将动物疫病的监测、预防、控制、净化、消灭，动物、动物产品的检疫和病死动物的无害化处理，以及监督管理所需经费纳入本级预算。

**Article 83** The people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective government responsibilities, incorporate into their budgets the funds needed for the monitoring, prevention, control, purification and eradication of animal epidemics, quarantine of animals and animal products, harmless treatment of dead animals, and supervision and administration.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　县级以上人民政府应当储备动物疫情应急处置所需的防疫物资。

**Article 84** The people's governments at or above the county level shall stockpile epidemic prevention materials needed for emergency response to animal epidemics.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　对在动物疫病预防、控制、净化、消灭过程中强制扑杀的动物、销毁的动物产品和相关物品，县级以上人民政府给予补偿。具体补偿标准和办法由国务院财政部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 85** The people's governments at or above the county level shall provide compensation for animals that are compulsorily culled, animal products and related articles that are destroyed during the process of animal epidemic prevention, control, purification and eradication. Specific compensation standards and measures shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council jointly with the relevant departments.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　对从事动物疫病预防、检疫、监督检查、现场处理疫情以及在工作中接触动物疫病病原体的人员，有关单位按照国家规定，采取有效的卫生防护、医疗保健措施，给予畜牧兽医医疗卫生津贴等相关待遇。

**Article 86** For personnel engaged in animal epidemic prevention, quarantine, supervision and inspection, on-site epidemic handling, and personnel who come into contact with animal epidemic pathogens in their work, the relevant units shall, in accordance with State provisions, take effective health protection and medical care measures, and provide such treatments as animal husbandry and veterinary medical health allowances.

### 第十一章　法律责任

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　地方各级人民政府及其工作人员未依照本法规定履行职责的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 87** If local people's governments at all levels and their staff members fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门及其工作人员违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未及时采取预防、控制、扑灭等措施的；  
（二）对不符合条件的颁发动物防疫条件合格证、动物诊疗许可证，或者对符合条件的拒不颁发动物防疫条件合格证、动物诊疗许可证的；  
（三）从事与动物防疫有关的经营性活动，或者违法收取费用的；  
（四）其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为。

**Article 88** If the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level or its staff members violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to take timely measures such as prevention, control and eradication;  
(2) issuing an animal epidemic prevention conditions certificate or animal diagnosis and treatment license to those who do not meet the conditions, or refusing to issue an animal epidemic prevention conditions certificate or animal diagnosis and treatment license to those who meet the conditions;  
(3) engaging in business activities related to animal epidemic prevention, or illegally charging fees;  
(4) other acts of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　动物卫生监督机构及其工作人员违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者农业农村主管部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）对未经检疫或者检疫不合格的动物、动物产品出具检疫证明、加施检疫标志，或者对检疫合格的动物、动物产品拒不出具检疫证明、加施检疫标志的；  
（二）对附有检疫证明、检疫标志的动物、动物产品重复检疫的；  
（三）从事与动物防疫有关的经营性活动，或者违法收取费用的；  
（四）其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为。

**Article 89** If an animal health supervision institution or its staff members violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) issuing quarantine certificates or affixing quarantine marks for animals or animal products that have not been quarantined or have failed quarantine inspection, or refusing to issue quarantine certificates or affix quarantine marks for animals or animal products that have passed quarantine inspection;  
(2) conducting repeated quarantine on animals or animal products that are accompanied by quarantine certificates and quarantine marks;  
(3) engaging in business activities related to animal epidemic prevention, or illegally charging fees;  
(4) other acts of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　动物疫病预防控制机构及其工作人员违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者农业农村主管部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未履行动物疫病监测、检测、评估职责或者伪造监测、检测、评估结果的；  
（二）发生动物疫情时未及时进行诊断、调查的；  
（三）接到染疫或者疑似染疫报告后，未及时按照国家规定采取措施、上报的；  
（四）其他未依照本法规定履行职责的行为。

**Article 90** If an animal epidemic prevention and control institution or its staff members violate the provisions of this Law and commit any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to perform the duties of monitoring, testing and assessment of animal epidemics, or forging monitoring, testing or assessment results;  
(2) failing to conduct timely diagnosis and investigation when an animal epidemic occurs;  
(3) failing to take timely measures or report in accordance with State provisions after receiving a report of infection or suspected infection;  
(4) other acts of failing to perform duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　地方各级人民政府、有关部门及其工作人员瞒报、谎报、迟报、漏报或者授意他人瞒报、谎报、迟报动物疫情，或者阻碍他人报告动物疫情的，由上级人民政府或者有关部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 91** If local people's governments at all levels, relevant departments or their staff members conceal, falsely report, delay reporting, omit reporting or instruct others to conceal, falsely report, delay reporting an animal epidemic, or obstruct others from reporting an animal epidemic, the people's government at a higher level or the relevant departments shall order correction and circulate a notice of criticism; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正，可以处一千元以下罚款；逾期不改正的，处一千元以上五千元以下罚款，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门委托动物诊疗机构、无害化处理场所等代为处理，所需费用由违法行为人承担：  
（一）对饲养的动物未按照动物疫病强制免疫计划或者免疫技术规范实施免疫接种的；  
（二）对饲养的种用、乳用动物未按照国务院农业农村主管部门的要求定期开展疫病检测，或者经检测不合格而未按照规定处理的；  
（三）对饲养的犬只未按照规定定期进行狂犬病免疫接种的；  
（四）动物、动物产品的运载工具在装载前和卸载后未按照规定及时清洗、消毒的。

**Article 92** If any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a specified time limit, and may impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan; if correction is not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed, and the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall entrust an animal diagnosis and treatment institution, a harmless treatment facility or the like to handle the matter on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator:  
(1) failing to carry out immunization vaccination for raised animals in accordance with the compulsory immunization plan for animal epidemics or immunization technical specifications;  
(2) failing to carry out regular disease testing for raised breeding or dairy animals in accordance with the requirements of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, or failing to handle them in accordance with the provisions after testing fails;  
(3) failing to carry out regular rabies vaccination for raised dogs in accordance with the provisions;  
(4) failing to promptly clean and disinfect the means of transport for animals and animal products before loading and after unloading in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　违反本法规定，对经强制免疫的动物未按照规定建立免疫档案，或者未按照规定加施畜禽标识的，依照《中华人民共和国畜牧法》的有关规定处罚。

**Article 93** If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an immunization record is not established for animals that have undergone compulsory immunization in accordance with the provisions, or livestock and poultry identifiers are not affixed in accordance with the provisions, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Animal Husbandry Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　违反本法规定，动物、动物产品的运载工具、垫料、包装物、容器等不符合国务院农业农村主管部门规定的动物防疫要求的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，可以处五千元以下罚款；情节严重的，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 94** If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, means of transport, bedding, packaging materials, containers, etc. for animals and animal products do not meet the animal epidemic prevention requirements prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, and may impose a fine of not more than 5,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反本法规定，对染疫动物及其排泄物、染疫动物产品或者被染疫动物、动物产品污染的运载工具、垫料、包装物、容器等未按照规定处置的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期处理；逾期不处理的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门委托有关单位代为处理，所需费用由违法行为人承担，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。  
造成环境污染或者生态破坏的，依照环境保护有关法律法规进行处罚。

**Article 95** If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, infected animals and their excreta, infected animal products, or means of transport, bedding, packaging materials, containers, etc. contaminated by infected animals or animal products are not disposed of in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order treatment within a specified time limit; if treatment is not made within the time limit, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall entrust a relevant unit to handle the matter on its behalf, and the expenses incurred shall be borne by the violator, and a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.  
If environmental pollution or ecological damage is caused, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations on environmental protection.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　违反本法规定，患有人畜共患传染病的人员，直接从事动物疫病监测、检测、检验检疫，动物诊疗以及易感染动物的饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输等活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村或者野生动物保护主管部门责令改正；拒不改正的，处一千元以上一万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 96** If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a person suffering from a zoonotic infectious disease directly engages in activities such as monitoring, testing, inspection and quarantine of animal epidemics, animal diagnosis and treatment, and raising, slaughtering, trading, isolating, transporting of susceptible animals, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs or wildlife protection of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if correction is refused, a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　违反[本法第二十九条](#art-29)规定，屠宰、经营、运输动物或者生产、经营、加工、贮藏、运输动物产品的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正、采取补救措施，没收违法所得、动物和动物产品，并处同类检疫合格动物、动物产品货值金额十五倍以上三十倍以下罚款；同类检疫合格动物、动物产品货值金额不足一万元的，并处五万元以上十五万元以下罚款；其中依法应当检疫而未检疫的，依照[本法第一百条](#art-100)的规定处罚。  
前款规定的违法行为人及其法定代表人（负责人）、直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，自处罚决定作出之日起五年内不得从事相关活动；构成犯罪的，终身不得从事屠宰、经营、运输动物或者生产、经营、加工、贮藏、运输动物产品等相关活动。

**Article 97** If, in violation of the provisions of Article 29 of this Law, animals are slaughtered, traded or transported, or animal products are produced, traded, processed, stored or transported, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and take remedial measures, confiscate the illegal gains, animals and animal products, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 15 times but not more than 30 times the value of the same kind of quarantined qualified animals or animal products; if the value of the same kind of quarantined qualified animals or animal products is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 150,000 yuan shall be concurrently imposed; if the animals or animal products should have been quarantined according to law but have not been quarantined, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 100 of this Law.  
The violator specified in the preceding paragraph and its legal representative (responsible person), directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall not engage in relevant activities within five years from the date of the decision on punishment; if a crime is constituted, they shall be prohibited from engaging in activities such as slaughtering, trading, transporting animals or producing, trading, processing, storing, transporting animal products for life.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处三千元以上三万元以下罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处三万元以上十万元以下罚款：  
（一）开办动物饲养场和隔离场所、动物屠宰加工场所以及动物和动物产品无害化处理场所，未取得动物防疫条件合格证的；  
（二）经营动物、动物产品的集贸市场不具备国务院农业农村主管部门规定的防疫条件的；  
（三）未经备案从事动物运输的；  
（四）未按照规定保存行程路线和托运人提供的动物名称、检疫证明编号、数量等信息的；  
（五）未经检疫合格，向无规定动物疫病区输入动物、动物产品的；  
（六）跨省、自治区、直辖市引进种用、乳用动物到达输入地后未按照规定进行隔离观察的；  
（七）未按照规定处理或者随意弃置病死动物、病害动物产品的。

**Article 98** If any of the following acts is committed in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, the operation shall be ordered to suspend for rectification, and a fine of not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed:  
(1) establishing an animal farm and isolation facility, an animal slaughtering and processing facility, or an animal and animal product harmless treatment facility without obtaining an animal epidemic prevention conditions certificate;  
(2) a market for trading animals and animal products does not have the epidemic prevention conditions prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council;  
(3) engaging in animal transport without filing;  
(4) failing to preserve the travel route and information such as animal names, quarantine certificate numbers, and quantities provided by the consignor in accordance with the provisions;  
(5) importing animals or animal products into an animal disease-free zone without passing quarantine inspection;  
(6) failing to carry out isolation observation in accordance with the provisions after introducing breeding or dairy animals across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(7) failing to dispose of or arbitrarily discarding dead or diseased animals and diseased animal products in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　动物饲养场和隔离场所、动物屠宰加工场所以及动物和动物产品无害化处理场所，生产经营条件发生变化，不再符合[本法第二十四条](#art-24)规定的动物防疫条件继续从事相关活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门给予警告，责令限期改正；逾期仍达不到规定条件的，吊销动物防疫条件合格证，并通报市场监督管理部门依法处理。

**Article 99** If an animal farm and isolation facility, animal slaughtering and processing facility, or animal and animal product harmless treatment facility undergoes changes in production and operation conditions and no longer meets the animal epidemic prevention conditions stipulated in Article 24 of this Law but continues to engage in relevant activities, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and order correction within a specified time limit; if the prescribed conditions are still not met within the time limit, the animal epidemic prevention conditions certificate shall be revoked, and the market supervision and administration department shall be notified for handling according to law.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　违反本法规定，屠宰、经营、运输的动物未附有检疫证明，经营和运输的动物产品未附有检疫证明、检疫标志的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处同类检疫合格动物、动物产品货值金额一倍以下罚款；对货主以外的承运人处运输费用三倍以上五倍以下罚款，情节严重的，处五倍以上十倍以下罚款。  
违反本法规定，用于科研、展示、演出和比赛等非食用性利用的动物未附有检疫证明的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处三千元以上一万元以下罚款。

**Article 100** If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, animals for slaughter, trading or transport are not accompanied by quarantine certificates, or animal products for trading and transport are not accompanied by quarantine certificates or quarantine marks, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not more than the value of the same kind of quarantined qualified animals or animal products; for the carrier other than the owner, a fine of not less than three times but not more than five times the transport expenses shall be imposed, and if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the transport expenses shall be imposed.  
If, in violation of the provisions of this Law, animals for non-edible use such as scientific research, exhibition, performance and competition are not accompanied by quarantine certificates, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction and impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　违反本法规定，将禁止或者限制调运的特定动物、动物产品由动物疫病高风险区调入低风险区的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门没收运输费用、违法运输的动物和动物产品，并处运输费用一倍以上五倍以下罚款。

**Article 101** : Where, in violation of this Law, specific animals or animal products that are prohibited or restricted for transport are moved from a high-risk area for animal epidemic diseases to a low-risk area, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the transportation fees and the illegally transported animals and animal products, and shall also impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the transportation fees.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　违反本法规定，通过道路跨省、自治区、直辖市运输动物，未经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府设立的指定通道入省境或者过省境的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门对运输人处五千元以上一万元以下罚款；情节严重的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。

**Article 102** : Where, in violation of this Law, animals are transported across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government by road without entering or passing through the territory of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government via the designated passage established by the people's government of that province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall impose on the transporter a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　违反本法规定，转让、伪造或者变造检疫证明、检疫标志或者畜禽标识的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门没收违法所得和检疫证明、检疫标志、畜禽标识，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款。  
持有、使用伪造或者变造的检疫证明、检疫标志或者畜禽标识的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门没收检疫证明、检疫标志、畜禽标识和对应的动物、动物产品，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款。

**Article 103** : Where, in violation of this Law, any person transfers, forges, or alters quarantine certificates, quarantine marks, or livestock and poultry identification marks, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains and the quarantine certificates, quarantine marks, or livestock and poultry identification marks, and shall also impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan.  
Where any person holds or uses forged or altered quarantine certificates, quarantine marks, or livestock and poultry identification marks, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the quarantine certificates, quarantine marks, or livestock and poultry identification marks and the corresponding animals or animal products, and shall also impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处三千元以上三万元以下罚款：  
（一）擅自发布动物疫情的；  
（二）不遵守县级以上人民政府及其农业农村主管部门依法作出的有关控制动物疫病规定的；  
（三）藏匿、转移、盗掘已被依法隔离、封存、处理的动物和动物产品的。

**Article 104** : Where, in violation of this Law, any person commits one of the following acts, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan:  
(1) Publishing information on animal epidemics without authorization;  
(2) Failing to comply with the provisions on controlling animal epidemic diseases lawfully made by the people's government at or above the county level or its competent department of agriculture and rural affairs;  
(3) Concealing, transferring, or illegally excavating animals or animal products that have been isolated, sealed, or disposed of according to law.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　违反本法规定，未取得动物诊疗许可证从事动物诊疗活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止诊疗活动，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下罚款；违法所得不足三万元的，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款。  
动物诊疗机构违反本法规定，未按照规定实施卫生安全防护、消毒、隔离和处置诊疗废弃物的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，处一千元以上一万元以下罚款；造成动物疫病扩散的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；情节严重的，吊销动物诊疗许可证。

**Article 105** : Where, in violation of this Law, any person engages in animal diagnosis and treatment activities without obtaining an animal diagnosis and treatment license, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the diagnosis and treatment activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains; if the illegal gains are less than 30,000 yuan, a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
Where an animal diagnosis and treatment institution, in violation of this Law, fails to implement health and safety protection, disinfection, isolation, and disposal of diagnosis and treatment waste as prescribed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and impose a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if the spread of animal epidemic diseases is caused, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, the animal diagnosis and treatment license shall be revoked.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　违反本法规定，未经执业兽医备案从事经营性动物诊疗活动的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止动物诊疗活动，没收违法所得，并处三千元以上三万元以下罚款；对其所在的动物诊疗机构处一万元以上五万元以下罚款。  
执业兽医有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门给予警告，责令暂停六个月以上一年以下动物诊疗活动；情节严重的，吊销执业兽医资格证书：  
（一）违反有关动物诊疗的操作技术规范，造成或者可能造成动物疫病传播、流行的；  
（二）使用不符合规定的兽药和兽医器械的；  
（三）未按照当地人民政府或者农业农村主管部门要求参加动物疫病预防、控制和动物疫情扑灭活动的。

**Article 106** : Where, in violation of this Law, any person engages in commercial animal diagnosis and treatment activities without registration as a practicing veterinarian, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the animal diagnosis and treatment activities, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than 3,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan; and shall impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan on the animal diagnosis and treatment institution where the person works.  
Where a practicing veterinarian commits one of the following acts, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall give a warning and order suspension of animal diagnosis and treatment activities for not less than six months but not more than one year; if the circumstances are serious, the practicing veterinarian qualification certificate shall be revoked:  
(1) Violating the operational technical specifications for animal diagnosis and treatment, causing or likely to cause the spread or prevalence of animal epidemic diseases;  
(2) Using veterinary drugs and veterinary instruments that do not meet the requirements;  
(3) Failing to participate in activities for the prevention, control, and elimination of animal epidemic diseases as required by the local people's government or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　违反本法规定，生产经营兽医器械，产品质量不符合要求的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期整改；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，并处二万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 107** : Where, in violation of this Law, any person produces or operates veterinary instruments, and the product quality does not meet the requirements, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, shall order suspension of business for rectification and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　违反本法规定，从事动物疫病研究、诊疗和动物饲养、屠宰、经营、隔离、运输，以及动物产品生产、经营、加工、贮藏、无害化处理等活动的单位和个人，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令改正，可以处一万元以下罚款；拒不改正的，处一万元以上五万元以下罚款，并可以责令停业整顿：  
（一）发现动物染疫、疑似染疫未报告，或者未采取隔离等控制措施的；  
（二）不如实提供与动物防疫有关的资料的；  
（三）拒绝或者阻碍农业农村主管部门进行监督检查的；  
（四）拒绝或者阻碍动物疫病预防控制机构进行动物疫病监测、检测、评估的；  
（五）拒绝或者阻碍官方兽医依法履行职责的。

**Article 108** : Where, in violation of this Law, any unit or individual engaged in animal epidemic disease research, diagnosis and treatment, animal rearing, slaughtering, dealing, isolation, transportation, or production, dealing, processing, storage, and harmless treatment of animal products commits one of the following acts, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order rectification and may impose a fine of not more than 10,000 yuan; if the person refuses to rectify, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed, and suspension of business for rectification may be ordered:  
(1) Failing to report an animal being infected or suspected of being infected with an epidemic disease, or failing to take control measures such as isolation;  
(2) Failing to truthfully provide materials related to animal epidemic prevention;  
(3) Refusing or obstructing the supervisory inspection conducted by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs;  
(4) Refusing or obstructing the monitoring, testing, or evaluation of animal epidemic diseases conducted by an animal disease prevention and control institution;  
(5) Refusing or obstructing an official veterinarian from performing duties according to law.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　违反本法规定，造成人畜共患传染病传播、流行的，依法从重给予处分、处罚。  
违反本法规定，构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
违反本法规定，给他人人身、财产造成损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 109** : Where, in violation of this Law, the spread or prevalence of a zoonotic infectious disease is caused, the person shall be given a heavier sanction or punishment according to law.  
Where, in violation of this Law, an act constitutes a violation of public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be given according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where, in violation of this Law, damage is caused to the person or property of others, civil liability shall be borne according to law.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　本法下列用语的含义：  
（一）无规定动物疫病区，是指具有天然屏障或者采取人工措施，在一定期限内没有发生规定的一种或者几种动物疫病，并经验收合格的区域；  
（二）无规定动物疫病生物安全隔离区，是指处于同一生物安全管理体系下，在一定期限内没有发生规定的一种或者几种动物疫病的若干动物饲养场及其辅助生产场所构成的，并经验收合格的特定小型区域；  
（三）病死动物，是指染疫死亡、因病死亡、死因不明或者经检验检疫可能危害人体或者动物健康的死亡动物；  
（四）病害动物产品，是指来源于病死动物的产品，或者经检验检疫可能危害人体或者动物健康的动物产品。

**Article 110** : For the purposes of this Law, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them:  
(1) “Animal epidemic disease-free zone” means an area that has natural barriers or in which artificial measures have been adopted, where no specified animal epidemic disease or diseases have occurred for a certain period, and that has passed acceptance inspection;  
(2) “Animal epidemic disease-free biosecurity compartment” means a specific small area consisting of a number of animal rearing farms and their auxiliary production facilities under the same biosecurity management system, where no specified animal epidemic disease or diseases have occurred for a certain period, and that has passed acceptance inspection;  
(3) “Dead animal(s) from disease” means dead animals that have died from an epidemic disease, died from a disease, died from unknown causes, or that may be harmful to human or animal health after inspection and quarantine;  
(4) “Diseased animal product(s)” means products derived from dead animals from disease, or animal products that may be harmful to human or animal health after inspection and quarantine.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　境外无规定动物疫病区和无规定动物疫病生物安全隔离区的无疫等效性评估，参照本法有关规定执行。

**Article 111** : The assessment of the equivalent freedom from epidemic diseases of overseas animal epidemic disease-free zones and animal epidemic disease-free biosecurity compartments shall be carried out by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　实验动物防疫有特殊要求的，按照实验动物管理的有关规定执行。

**Article 112** : Where there are special requirements for epidemic prevention of laboratory animals, the relevant provisions on the management of laboratory animals shall apply.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　本法自2021年5月1日起施行。

**Article 113** : This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2021.
