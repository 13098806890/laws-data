# 中华人民共和国农产品质量安全法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2022-09-02  
**生效日期**：2023-01-01

> （2006年4月29日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十一次会议通过　根据2018年10月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第六次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国野生动物保护法〉等十五部法律的决定》修正　2022年9月2日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十六次会议修订） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　农产品质量安全风险管理和标准制定 第三章　农产品产地 第四章　农产品生产 第五章　农产品销售 第六章　监督管理 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了保障农产品质量安全，维护公众健康，促进农业和农村经济发展，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称农产品，是指来源于种植业、林业、畜牧业和渔业等的初级产品，即在农业活动中获得的植物、动物、微生物及其产品。 本法所称农产品质量安全，是指农产品质量达到农产品质量安全标准，符合保障人的健康、安全的要求。 第三条　与农产品质量安全有关的农产品生产经营及其监督管理活动，适用本法。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保障农产品质量安全，维护公众健康，促进农业和农村经济发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated to ensure the quality and safety of agricultural products, safeguard public health, and promote the development of agriculture and the rural economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称农产品，是指来源于种植业、林业、畜牧业和渔业等的初级产品，即在农业活动中获得的植物、动物、微生物及其产品。  
本法所称农产品质量安全，是指农产品质量达到农产品质量安全标准，符合保障人的健康、安全的要求。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "agricultural products" mean the primary products derived from crop cultivation, forestry, animal husbandry, and fisheries, namely, plants, animals, microorganisms, and their products obtained in agricultural activities.  
For the purposes of this Law, "quality and safety of agricultural products" means that the quality of agricultural products meets the quality and safety standards for agricultural products and complies with the requirements for ensuring human health and safety.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　与农产品质量安全有关的农产品生产经营及其监督管理活动，适用本法。  
《中华人民共和国食品安全法》对食用农产品的市场销售、有关质量安全标准的制定、有关安全信息的公布和农业投入品已经作出规定的，应当遵守其规定。

**Article 3** This Law shall apply to the production and operation of agricultural products and the supervision and administration activities related to the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Where the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China has already made provisions on the market sale of edible agricultural products, the formulation of relevant quality and safety standards, the disclosure of relevant safety information, and agricultural inputs, such provisions shall be complied with.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家加强农产品质量安全工作，实行源头治理、风险管理、全程控制，建立科学、严格的监督管理制度，构建协同、高效的社会共治体系。

**Article 4** The State shall strengthen the work on quality and safety of agricultural products, implement source control, risk management, and whole-process control, establish a scientific and strict supervision and administration system, and build a collaborative, efficient system for social co-governance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院农业农村主管部门、市场监督管理部门依照本法和规定的职责，对农产品质量安全实施监督管理。  
国务院其他有关部门依照本法和规定的职责承担农产品质量安全的有关工作。

**Article 5** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council and the market supervision and administration department shall, in accordance with this Law and the duties prescribed, exercise supervision and administration over the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Other relevant departments of the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law and the duties prescribed, undertake the relevant work on the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上地方人民政府对本行政区域的农产品质量安全工作负责，统一领导、组织、协调本行政区域的农产品质量安全工作，建立健全农产品质量安全工作机制，提高农产品质量安全水平。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当依照本法和有关规定，确定本级农业农村主管部门、市场监督管理部门和其他有关部门的农产品质量安全监督管理工作职责。各有关部门在职责范围内负责本行政区域的农产品质量安全监督管理工作。  
乡镇人民政府应当落实农产品质量安全监督管理责任，协助上级人民政府及其有关部门做好农产品质量安全监督管理工作。

**Article 6** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the work on the quality and safety of agricultural products within their respective administrative regions, and shall exercise unified leadership, organization, and coordination over such work, establish and improve the working mechanism for the quality and safety of agricultural products, and improve the level of quality and safety of agricultural products.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions, determine the specific duties of the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration departments, and other relevant departments at the same level in the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products. Each relevant department shall, within its scope of duties, be responsible for the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products within its administrative region.  
The people's government of a township shall fulfill its responsibility for the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products, and assist the people's government at a higher level and its relevant departments in carrying out the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　农产品生产经营者应当对其生产经营的农产品质量安全负责。  
农产品生产经营者应当依照法律、法规和农产品质量安全标准从事生产经营活动，诚信自律，接受社会监督，承担社会责任。

**Article 7** Producers and operators of agricultural products shall be responsible for the quality and safety of the agricultural products they produce and operate.  
Producers and operators of agricultural products shall engage in production and operation activities in accordance with laws, regulations, and the quality and safety standards for agricultural products, act with integrity and self-discipline, accept social supervision, and bear social responsibility.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　县级以上人民政府应当将农产品质量安全管理工作纳入本级国民经济和社会发展规划，所需经费列入本级预算，加强农产品质量安全监督管理能力建设。

**Article 8** The people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the work on the administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products into the national economic and social development plan at the same level, include the necessary funds in the budget at the same level, and strengthen the capacity building for the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家引导、推广农产品标准化生产，鼓励和支持生产绿色优质农产品，禁止生产、销售不符合国家规定的农产品质量安全标准的农产品。

**Article 9** The State shall guide and promote standardized production of agricultural products, encourage and support the production of green and high-quality agricultural products, and prohibit the production and sale of agricultural products that do not meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products prescribed by the State.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家支持农产品质量安全科学技术研究，推行科学的质量安全管理方法，推广先进安全的生产技术。国家加强农产品质量安全科学技术国际交流与合作。

**Article 10** The State shall support scientific and technological research on the quality and safety of agricultural products, promote scientific quality and safety management methods, and disseminate advanced and safe production technologies. The State shall strengthen international exchanges and cooperation in science and technology concerning the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　各级人民政府及有关部门应当加强农产品质量安全知识的宣传，发挥基层群众性自治组织、农村集体经济组织的优势和作用，指导农产品生产经营者加强质量安全管理，保障农产品消费安全。  
新闻媒体应当开展农产品质量安全法律、法规和农产品质量安全知识的公益宣传，对违法行为进行舆论监督。有关农产品质量安全的宣传报道应当真实、公正。

**Article 11** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments shall strengthen the publicity of knowledge on the quality and safety of agricultural products, give full play to the advantages and roles of grassroots self-governing mass organizations and rural collective economic organizations, and guide producers and operators of agricultural products to strengthen quality and safety management to ensure the safety of consumption of agricultural products.  
News media shall conduct public interest publicity on the laws, regulations, and knowledge concerning the quality and safety of agricultural products, and exercise public opinion supervision over illegal acts. Reports and publicity concerning the quality and safety of agricultural products shall be truthful and fair.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　农民专业合作社和农产品行业协会等应当及时为其成员提供生产技术服务，建立农产品质量安全管理制度，健全农产品质量安全控制体系，加强自律管理。

**Article 12** Farmer specialized cooperatives, agricultural product industry associations, and the like shall promptly provide their members with production technical services, establish a management system for the quality and safety of agricultural products, improve the control system for the quality and safety of agricultural products, and strengthen self-discipline management.

### 第二章　农产品质量安全风险管理和标准制定

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家建立农产品质量安全风险监测制度。  
国务院农业农村主管部门应当制定国家农产品质量安全风险监测计划，并对重点区域、重点农产品品种进行质量安全风险监测。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门应当根据国家农产品质量安全风险监测计划，结合本行政区域农产品生产经营实际，制定本行政区域的农产品质量安全风险监测实施方案，并报国务院农业农村主管部门备案。县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门负责组织实施本行政区域的农产品质量安全风险监测。  
县级以上人民政府市场监督管理部门和其他有关部门获知有关农产品质量安全风险信息后，应当立即核实并向同级农业农村主管部门通报。接到通报的农业农村主管部门应当及时上报。制定农产品质量安全风险监测计划、实施方案的部门应当及时研究分析，必要时进行调整。

**Article 13** The State shall establish a risk monitoring system for the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall formulate a national risk monitoring plan for the quality and safety of agricultural products, and conduct quality and safety risk monitoring for key regions and key varieties of agricultural products. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the national risk monitoring plan for the quality and safety of agricultural products and in light of the actual production and operation of agricultural products in their respective administrative regions, formulate implementation plans for risk monitoring of the quality and safety of agricultural products in their administrative regions, and report them to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council for recordation. The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of risk monitoring for the quality and safety of agricultural products in their respective administrative regions.  
Upon obtaining information on risks to the quality and safety of agricultural products, the market supervision and administration departments and other relevant departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall immediately verify and report it to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at the same level. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that receives the report shall promptly report to a higher level. The departments that formulate the risk monitoring plan and implementation plan for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall timely study and analyze, and make adjustments when necessary.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家建立农产品质量安全风险评估制度。  
国务院农业农村主管部门应当设立农产品质量安全风险评估专家委员会，对可能影响农产品质量安全的潜在危害进行风险分析和评估。国务院卫生健康、市场监督管理等部门发现需要对农产品进行质量安全风险评估的，应当向国务院农业农村主管部门提出风险评估建议。  
农产品质量安全风险评估专家委员会由农业、食品、营养、生物、环境、医学、化工等方面的专家组成。

**Article 14** The State shall establish a risk assessment system for the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall establish an expert committee on risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products to conduct risk analysis and assessment of potential hazards that may affect the quality and safety of agricultural products. Where the competent departments of health, market supervision and administration, and other departments under the State Council find that a quality and safety risk assessment for agricultural products is necessary, they shall submit recommendations for risk assessment to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
The expert committee on risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall be composed of experts in agriculture, food, nutrition, biology, environment, medicine, chemical engineering, and other fields.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当根据农产品质量安全风险监测、风险评估结果采取相应的管理措施，并将农产品质量安全风险监测、风险评估结果及时通报国务院市场监督管理、卫生健康等部门和有关省、自治区、直辖市人民政府农业农村主管部门。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门开展农产品质量安全风险监测和风险评估工作时，可以根据需要进入农产品产地、储存场所及批发、零售市场。采集样品应当按照市场价格支付费用。

**Article 15** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, based on the results of risk monitoring and risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products, adopt corresponding management measures, and promptly notify the market supervision and administration, health, and other relevant departments under the State Council, as well as the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, of the results of risk monitoring and risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
When carrying out risk monitoring and risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products, the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level may, as needed, enter the production sites, storage places, and wholesale and retail markets for agricultural products. Fees for samples collected shall be paid according to the market price.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家建立健全农产品质量安全标准体系，确保严格实施。农产品质量安全标准是强制执行的标准，包括以下与农产品质量安全有关的要求：  
（一）农业投入品质量要求、使用范围、用法、用量、安全间隔期和休药期规定；  
（二）农产品产地环境、生产过程管控、储存、运输要求；  
（三）农产品关键成分指标等要求；  
（四）与屠宰畜禽有关的检验规程；  
（五）其他与农产品质量安全有关的强制性要求。  
《中华人民共和国食品安全法》对食用农产品的有关质量安全标准作出规定的，依照其规定执行。

**Article 16** The State shall establish and improve the quality and safety standard system for agricultural products and ensure its strict implementation. The quality and safety standards for agricultural products are mandatory standards, including the following requirements relating to the quality and safety of agricultural products:  
(1) quality requirements for agricultural inputs, scope of use, methods, dosage, safety interval, and withdrawal period provisions;  
(2) requirements for the production site environment, production process control, storage, and transportation of agricultural products;  
(3) requirements for key component indicators and other requirements for agricultural products;  
(4) inspection procedures relating to the slaughter of livestock and poultry; and  
(5) other mandatory requirements relating to the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Where the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China has made provisions on the relevant quality and safety standards for edible agricultural products, such provisions shall be followed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　农产品质量安全标准的制定和发布，依照法律、行政法规的规定执行。  
制定农产品质量安全标准应当充分考虑农产品质量安全风险评估结果，并听取农产品生产经营者、消费者、有关部门、行业协会等的意见，保障农产品消费安全。

**Article 17** The formulation and issuance of quality and safety standards for agricultural products shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations.  
In formulating quality and safety standards for agricultural products, full consideration shall be given to the results of risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products, and the opinions of producers and operators of agricultural products, consumers, relevant departments, industry associations, and others shall be solicited to ensure the safety of consumption of agricultural products.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　农产品质量安全标准应当根据科学技术发展水平以及农产品质量安全的需要，及时修订。

**Article 18** The quality and safety standards for agricultural products shall be revised in a timely manner based on the level of scientific and technological development and the need for the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　农产品质量安全标准由农业农村主管部门商有关部门推进实施。

**Article 19** The quality and safety standards for agricultural products shall be promoted and implemented by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs in consultation with the relevant departments.

### 第三章　农产品产地

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国家建立健全农产品产地监测制度。  
县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当会同同级生态环境、自然资源等部门制定农产品产地监测计划，加强农产品产地安全调查、监测和评价工作。

**Article 20** The State shall establish and improve a monitoring system for the production areas of agricultural products.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the competent departments of ecology and environment, natural resources, and other departments at the same level, formulate a monitoring plan for the production areas of agricultural products, and strengthen the investigation, monitoring, and evaluation of the safety of the production areas of agricultural products.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当会同同级生态环境、自然资源等部门按照保障农产品质量安全的要求，根据农产品品种特性和产地安全调查、监测、评价结果，依照土壤污染防治等法律、法规的规定提出划定特定农产品禁止生产区域的建议，报本级人民政府批准后实施。  
任何单位和个人不得在特定农产品禁止生产区域种植、养殖、捕捞、采集特定农产品和建立特定农产品生产基地。  
特定农产品禁止生产区域划定和管理的具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门商国务院生态环境、自然资源等部门制定。

**Article 21** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the competent departments of ecology and environment, natural resources, and other departments at the same level, in accordance with the requirements for ensuring the quality and safety of agricultural products, and based on the characteristics of the varieties of agricultural products and the results of investigation, monitoring, and evaluation of the safety of production areas, propose recommendations for the designation of prohibited production areas for specific agricultural products in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations such as the Law on the Prevention and Control of Soil Pollution, and implement them upon approval by the people's government at the same level.  
No unit or individual may plant, breed, catch, or collect specific agricultural products or establish production bases for specific agricultural products in the prohibited production areas for specific agricultural products.  
The specific measures for the designation and management of prohibited production areas for specific agricultural products shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council in consultation with the competent departments of ecology and environment, natural resources, and other departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　任何单位和个人不得违反有关环境保护法律、法规的规定向农产品产地排放或者倾倒废水、废气、固体废物或者其他有毒有害物质。  
农业生产用水和用作肥料的固体废物，应当符合法律、法规和国家有关强制性标准的要求。

**Article 22** No unit or individual may discharge or dump waste water, waste gas, solid waste, or other toxic or hazardous substances into the production areas of agricultural products in violation of the provisions of relevant environmental protection laws and regulations.  
The water used for agricultural production and the solid waste used as fertilizer shall comply with the requirements of laws, regulations, and the relevant mandatory standards of the State.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　农产品生产者应当科学合理使用农药、兽药、肥料、农用薄膜等农业投入品，防止对农产品产地造成污染。  
农药、肥料、农用薄膜等农业投入品的生产者、经营者、使用者应当按照国家有关规定回收并妥善处置包装物和废弃物。

**Article 23** Producers of agricultural products shall use agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, fertilizers, and agricultural films in a scientific and rational manner to prevent pollution of the production areas of agricultural products.  
Producers, operators, and users of agricultural inputs such as pesticides, fertilizers, and agricultural films shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, recycle and properly dispose of packaging and waste.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　县级以上人民政府应当采取措施，加强农产品基地建设，推进农业标准化示范建设，改善农产品的生产条件。

**Article 24** The people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen the construction of agricultural product bases, promote the demonstration construction of agricultural standardization, and improve the production conditions of agricultural products.

### 第四章　农产品生产

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当根据本地区的实际情况，制定保障农产品质量安全的生产技术要求和操作规程，并加强对农产品生产经营者的培训和指导。  
农业技术推广机构应当加强对农产品生产经营者质量安全知识和技能的培训。国家鼓励科研教育机构开展农产品质量安全培训。

**Article 25** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual conditions of their respective regions, formulate production technical requirements and operating procedures to ensure the quality and safety of agricultural products, and strengthen the training and guidance of producers and operators of agricultural products.  
Agricultural technology extension institutions shall strengthen the training of producers and operators of agricultural products on quality and safety knowledge and skills. The State encourages scientific research and educational institutions to conduct training on the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社、农业社会化服务组织应当加强农产品质量安全管理。  
农产品生产企业应当建立农产品质量安全管理制度，配备相应的技术人员；不具备配备条件的，应当委托具有专业技术知识的人员进行农产品质量安全指导。  
国家鼓励和支持农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社、农业社会化服务组织建立和实施危害分析和关键控制点体系，实施良好农业规范，提高农产品质量安全管理水平。

**Article 26** Agricultural product production enterprises, farmer specialized cooperatives, and agricultural socialized service organizations shall strengthen the management of the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Agricultural product production enterprises shall establish a management system for the quality and safety of agricultural products and equip themselves with corresponding technical personnel; if they do not have the conditions to do so, they shall entrust personnel with professional technical knowledge to provide guidance on the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
The State encourages and supports agricultural product production enterprises, farmer specialized cooperatives, and agricultural socialized service organizations in establishing and implementing hazard analysis and critical control point systems, implementing good agricultural practices, and improving the level of quality and safety management of agricultural products.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社、农业社会化服务组织应当建立农产品生产记录，如实记载下列事项：  
（一）使用农业投入品的名称、来源、用法、用量和使用、停用的日期；  
（二）动物疫病、农作物病虫害的发生和防治情况；  
（三）收获、屠宰或者捕捞的日期。  
农产品生产记录应当至少保存二年。禁止伪造、变造农产品生产记录。  
国家鼓励其他农产品生产者建立农产品生产记录。

**Article 27** Agricultural product production enterprises, farmer specialized cooperatives, and agricultural socialized service organizations shall establish production records for agricultural products, truthfully recording the following matters:  
(1) the names, sources, usage methods, dosage, and dates of use and discontinuation of agricultural inputs used;  
(2) the occurrence and prevention and control of animal diseases and crop pests and diseases; and  
(3) the dates of harvest, slaughter, or catch.  
The production records for agricultural products shall be kept for at least two years. The forgery or alteration of production records for agricultural products is prohibited.  
The State encourages other producers of agricultural products to establish production records for agricultural products.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　对可能影响农产品质量安全的农药、兽药、饲料和饲料添加剂、肥料、兽医器械，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定实行许可制度。  
省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当定期或者不定期组织对可能危及农产品质量安全的农药、兽药、饲料和饲料添加剂、肥料等农业投入品进行监督抽查，并公布抽查结果。  
农药、兽药经营者应当依照有关法律、行政法规的规定建立销售台账，记录购买者、销售日期和药品施用范围等内容。

**Article 28** For pesticides, veterinary drugs, feed and feed additives, fertilizers, and veterinary instruments that may affect the quality and safety of agricultural products, a licensing system shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the provincial level shall, regularly or irregularly, organize supervisory spot checks on agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, feed and feed additives, and fertilizers that may endanger the quality and safety of agricultural products, and publish the results of the spot checks.  
Operators of pesticides and veterinary drugs shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, establish sales ledgers, recording information such as the purchaser, the date of sale, and the scope of application of the drugs.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　农产品生产经营者应当依照有关法律、行政法规和国家有关强制性标准、国务院农业农村主管部门的规定，科学合理使用农药、兽药、饲料和饲料添加剂、肥料等农业投入品，严格执行农业投入品使用安全间隔期或者休药期的规定；不得超范围、超剂量使用农业投入品危及农产品质量安全。  
禁止在农产品生产经营过程中使用国家禁止使用的农业投入品以及其他有毒有害物质。

**Article 29** Producers and operators of agricultural products shall, in accordance with relevant laws and administrative regulations, the relevant mandatory standards of the State, and the provisions of the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council, use agricultural inputs such as pesticides, veterinary drugs, feed and feed additives, and fertilizers in a scientific and rational manner, and strictly implement the provisions on safety intervals or withdrawal periods for the use of agricultural inputs; they may not use agricultural inputs beyond the scope or dosage, thereby endangering the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
The use of agricultural inputs prohibited by the State and other toxic or hazardous substances in the production and operation of agricultural products is prohibited.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　农产品生产场所以及生产活动中使用的设施、设备、消毒剂、洗涤剂等应当符合国家有关质量安全规定，防止污染农产品。

**Article 30** The production sites of agricultural products and the facilities, equipment, disinfectants, detergents, and the like used in production activities shall comply with the relevant State provisions on quality and safety to prevent contamination of agricultural products.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强对农业投入品使用的监督管理和指导，建立健全农业投入品的安全使用制度，推广农业投入品科学使用技术，普及安全、环保农业投入品的使用。

**Article 31** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the supervision, administration, and guidance of the use of agricultural inputs, establish and improve a safe use system for agricultural inputs, promote scientific use techniques for agricultural inputs, and popularize the use of safe and environmentally friendly agricultural inputs.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　国家鼓励和支持农产品生产经营者选用优质特色农产品品种，采用绿色生产技术和全程质量控制技术，生产绿色优质农产品，实施分等分级，提高农产品品质，打造农产品品牌。

**Article 32** The State encourages and supports producers and operators of agricultural products in selecting high-quality and characteristic varieties of agricultural products, adopting green production technologies and whole-process quality control technologies, producing green and high-quality agricultural products, implementing grading, improving the quality of agricultural products, and building agricultural product brands.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家支持农产品产地冷链物流基础设施建设，健全有关农产品冷链物流标准、服务规范和监管保障机制，保障冷链物流农产品畅通高效、安全便捷，扩大高品质市场供给。  
从事农产品冷链物流的生产经营者应当依照法律、法规和有关农产品质量安全标准，加强冷链技术创新与应用、质量安全控制，执行对冷链物流农产品及其包装、运输工具、作业环境等的检验检测检疫要求，保证冷链农产品质量安全。

**Article 33** The State shall support the construction of cold chain logistics infrastructure in the production areas of agricultural products, improve the relevant cold chain logistics standards, service specifications, and regulatory guarantee mechanisms for agricultural products, ensure smooth, efficient, safe, and convenient cold chain logistics of agricultural products, and expand the supply of high-quality markets.  
Producers and operators engaged in cold chain logistics of agricultural products shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and the relevant quality and safety standards for agricultural products, strengthen innovation and application of cold chain technology and quality and safety control, and implement the inspection, testing, and quarantine requirements for cold chain logistics of agricultural products and their packaging, transport vehicles, working environment, etc., to ensure the quality and safety of cold chain agricultural products.

### 第五章　农产品销售

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　销售的农产品应当符合农产品质量安全标准。  
农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社应当根据质量安全控制要求自行或者委托检测机构对农产品质量安全进行检测；经检测不符合农产品质量安全标准的农产品，应当及时采取管控措施，且不得销售。  
农业技术推广等机构应当为农户等农产品生产经营者提供农产品检测技术服务。

**Article 34** Agricultural products sold shall meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products.  
Agricultural product production enterprises and farmer specialized cooperatives shall, according to the quality and safety control requirements, conduct testing of the quality and safety of agricultural products by themselves or entrust testing institutions to do so; if agricultural products are found through testing not to meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products, control measures shall be taken promptly, and such products shall not be sold.  
Agricultural technology extension and other institutions shall provide agricultural product testing technical services to agricultural product producers and operators such as farming households.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　农产品在包装、保鲜、储存、运输中所使用的保鲜剂、防腐剂、添加剂、包装材料等，应当符合国家有关强制性标准以及其他农产品质量安全规定。  
储存、运输农产品的容器、工具和设备应当安全、无害。禁止将农产品与有毒有害物质一同储存、运输，防止污染农产品。

**Article 35** The preservatives, antiseptics, additives, packaging materials, and the like used in the packaging, preservation, storage, and transportation of agricultural products shall comply with the relevant mandatory standards of the State and other provisions on the quality and safety of agricultural products.  
Containers, tools, and equipment for storing and transporting agricultural products shall be safe and harmless. The storage or transportation of agricultural products together with toxic or hazardous substances is prohibited to prevent contamination of agricultural products.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　有下列情形之一的农产品，不得销售：  
（一）含有国家禁止使用的农药、兽药或者其他化合物；  
（二）农药、兽药等化学物质残留或者含有的重金属等有毒有害物质不符合农产品质量安全标准；  
（三）含有的致病性寄生虫、微生物或者生物毒素不符合农产品质量安全标准；  
（四）未按照国家有关强制性标准以及其他农产品质量安全规定使用保鲜剂、防腐剂、添加剂、包装材料等，或者使用的保鲜剂、防腐剂、添加剂、包装材料等不符合国家有关强制性标准以及其他质量安全规定；  
（五）病死、毒死或者死因不明的动物及其产品；  
（六）其他不符合农产品质量安全标准的情形。  
对前款规定不得销售的农产品，应当依照法律、法规的规定进行处置。

**Article 36** Agricultural products in any of the following circumstances shall not be sold:  
(1) containing pesticides, veterinary drugs, or other compounds that are prohibited for use by the State;  
(2) residues of chemical substances such as pesticides and veterinary drugs, or toxic and hazardous substances such as heavy metals contained therein, do not meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products;  
(3) containing pathogenic parasites, microorganisms, or biotoxins that do not meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products;  
(4) not using preservatives, antiseptics, additives, packaging materials, etc., in accordance with the relevant mandatory standards of the State and other provisions on the quality and safety of agricultural products, or the preservatives, antiseptics, additives, packaging materials, etc., used do not meet the relevant mandatory standards of the State and other provisions on quality and safety;  
(5) animals that died of illness, poisoning, or unknown causes, and their products; or  
(6) other circumstances where the agricultural products do not meet the quality and safety standards.  
Agricultural products that shall not be sold as specified in the preceding paragraph shall be disposed of in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　农产品批发市场应当按照规定设立或者委托检测机构，对进场销售的农产品质量安全状况进行抽查检测；发现不符合农产品质量安全标准的，应当要求销售者立即停止销售，并向所在地市场监督管理、农业农村等部门报告。  
农产品销售企业对其销售的农产品，应当建立健全进货检查验收制度；经查验不符合农产品质量安全标准的，不得销售。  
食品生产者采购农产品等食品原料，应当依照《中华人民共和国食品安全法》的规定查验许可证和合格证明，对无法提供合格证明的，应当按照规定进行检验。

**Article 37** Wholesale markets for agricultural products shall, in accordance with the provisions, establish or entrust testing institutions to conduct spot-check testing on the quality and safety status of agricultural products sold in the market; if any agricultural product is found not to meet the quality and safety standards, they shall require the seller to immediately stop selling and report to the local market supervision and administration, agriculture and rural affairs, and other departments.  
Agricultural product sales enterprises shall establish and improve a system of inspection and acceptance of purchased agricultural products; if, upon inspection, agricultural products are found not to meet the quality and safety standards, they shall not sell them.  
Food producers purchasing agricultural products and other food raw materials shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, inspect the licenses and certificates of conformity; if a certificate of conformity cannot be provided, they shall conduct testing in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社以及从事农产品收购的单位或者个人销售的农产品，按照规定应当包装或者附加承诺达标合格证等标识的，须经包装或者附加标识后方可销售。包装物或者标识上应当按照规定标明产品的品名、产地、生产者、生产日期、保质期、产品质量等级等内容；使用添加剂的，还应当按照规定标明添加剂的名称。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。

**Article 38** Agricultural products sold by agricultural product production enterprises, farmer specialized cooperatives, and units or individuals engaged in the purchase of agricultural products, which, according to the provisions, shall be packaged or affixed with labels such as certificates of conformity with commitments, may only be sold after being packaged or affixed with such labels. The packaging or labels shall, in accordance with the provisions, indicate the product name, place of origin, producer, date of production, shelf life, product quality grade, and other contents; if additives are used, the names of the additives shall also be indicated in accordance with the provisions. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社应当执行法律、法规的规定和国家有关强制性标准，保证其销售的农产品符合农产品质量安全标准，并根据质量安全控制、检测结果等开具承诺达标合格证，承诺不使用禁用的农药、兽药及其他化合物且使用的常规农药、兽药残留不超标等。鼓励和支持农户销售农产品时开具承诺达标合格证。法律、行政法规对畜禽产品的质量安全合格证明有特别规定的，应当遵守其规定。  
从事农产品收购的单位或者个人应当按照规定收取、保存承诺达标合格证或者其他质量安全合格证明，对其收购的农产品进行混装或者分装后销售的，应当按照规定开具承诺达标合格证。  
农产品批发市场应当建立健全农产品承诺达标合格证查验等制度。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当做好承诺达标合格证有关工作的指导服务，加强日常监督检查。  
农产品质量安全承诺达标合格证管理办法由国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院有关部门制定。

**Article 39** Agricultural product production enterprises and farmer specialized cooperatives shall implement the provisions of laws and regulations and the relevant mandatory standards of the State, ensure that the agricultural products they sell meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products, and, based on the quality and safety control and testing results, issue certificates of conformity with commitments, committing that they do not use prohibited pesticides, veterinary drugs, and other compounds and that the residues of conventional pesticides and veterinary drugs used do not exceed the limits. Farmers and farming households are encouraged and supported to issue certificates of conformity with commitments when selling agricultural products. Where laws and administrative regulations have special provisions on the quality and safety certificates of conformity for livestock and poultry products, such provisions shall be complied with.  
Units or individuals engaged in the purchase of agricultural products shall, in accordance with the provisions, collect and keep certificates of conformity with commitments or other quality and safety certificates of conformity; if they sell the agricultural products they have purchased after mixing or repackaging, they shall issue certificates of conformity with commitments in accordance with the provisions.  
Wholesale markets for agricultural products shall establish and improve systems for inspecting certificates of conformity with commitments for agricultural products.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall provide guidance and services for the work related to certificates of conformity with commitments and strengthen routine supervision and inspection.  
The measures for the administration of certificates of conformity with commitments for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council together with the relevant departments under the State Council.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　农产品生产经营者通过网络平台销售农产品的，应当依照本法和《中华人民共和国电子商务法》、《中华人民共和国食品安全法》等法律、法规的规定，严格落实质量安全责任，保证其销售的农产品符合质量安全标准。网络平台经营者应当依法加强对农产品生产经营者的管理。

**Article 40** Where producers and operators of agricultural products sell agricultural products through online platforms, they shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the E-Commerce Law of the People's Republic of China, the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and regulations, strictly implement their quality and safety responsibilities and ensure that the agricultural products they sell meet the quality and safety standards. Operators of online platforms shall, in accordance with the law, strengthen the management of producers and operators of agricultural products.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家对列入农产品质量安全追溯目录的农产品实施追溯管理。国务院农业农村主管部门应当会同国务院市场监督管理等部门建立农产品质量安全追溯协作机制。农产品质量安全追溯管理办法和追溯目录由国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院市场监督管理等部门制定。  
国家鼓励具备信息化条件的农产品生产经营者采用现代信息技术手段采集、留存生产记录、购销记录等生产经营信息。

**Article 41** The State shall implement traceability management for agricultural products included in the traceability catalog for the quality and safety of agricultural products. The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, together with the market supervision and administration and other departments under the State Council, establish a collaborative mechanism for the traceability of the quality and safety of agricultural products. The measures for the administration of the traceability of the quality and safety of agricultural products and the traceability catalog shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council together with the market supervision and administration and other departments under the State Council.  
The State encourages producers and operators of agricultural products with information technology conditions to adopt modern information technology means to collect and retain production records, purchase and sale records, and other production and operation information.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　农产品质量符合国家规定的有关优质农产品标准的，农产品生产经营者可以申请使用农产品质量标志。禁止冒用农产品质量标志。  
国家加强地理标志农产品保护和管理。

**Article 42** Where the quality of agricultural products meets the relevant standards for high-quality agricultural products prescribed by the State, producers and operators of agricultural products may apply to use the quality mark for agricultural products. The fraudulent use of the quality mark for agricultural products is prohibited.  
The State shall strengthen the protection and management of agricultural products with geographical indications.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　属于农业转基因生物的农产品，应当按照农业转基因生物安全管理的有关规定进行标识。

**Article 43** Agricultural products that are agricultural genetically modified organisms shall be labeled in accordance with the relevant provisions on the safety management of agricultural genetically modified organisms.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　依法需要实施检疫的动植物及其产品，应当附具检疫标志、检疫证明。

**Article 44** Animals, plants, and their products that need to be quarantined in accordance with the law shall be accompanied by quarantine marks and quarantine certificates.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门和市场监督管理等部门应当建立健全农产品质量安全全程监督管理协作机制，确保农产品从生产到消费各环节的质量安全。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门和市场监督管理部门应当加强收购、储存、运输过程中农产品质量安全监督管理的协调配合和执法衔接，及时通报和共享农产品质量安全监督管理信息，并按照职责权限，发布有关农产品质量安全日常监督管理信息。

**Article 45** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, and other departments under the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a collaborative mechanism for the whole-process supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products to ensure the quality and safety of agricultural products at all stages from production to consumption.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs and market supervision and administration under the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen coordination, cooperation, and law enforcement integration in the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products during purchase, storage, and transportation, promptly notify and share information on the supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products, and, in accordance with their functions and powers, release information on routine supervision and administration of the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当根据农产品质量安全风险监测、风险评估结果和农产品质量安全状况等，制定监督抽查计划，确定农产品质量安全监督抽查的重点、方式和频次，并实施农产品质量安全风险分级管理。

**Article 46** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the results of risk monitoring and risk assessment for the quality and safety of agricultural products and the status of the quality and safety of agricultural products, formulate supervisory spot-check plans, determine the key points, methods, and frequency of supervisory spot checks for the quality and safety of agricultural products, and implement graded risk management for the quality and safety of agricultural products.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当建立健全随机抽查机制，按照监督抽查计划，组织开展农产品质量安全监督抽查。  
农产品质量安全监督抽查检测应当委托符合本法规定条件的农产品质量安全检测机构进行。监督抽查不得向被抽查人收取费用，抽取的样品应当按照市场价格支付费用，并不得超过国务院农业农村主管部门规定的数量。  
上级农业农村主管部门监督抽查的同批次农产品，下级农业农村主管部门不得另行重复抽查。

**Article 47** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall establish and improve a random spot-check mechanism, and organize and carry out supervisory spot checks on the quality and safety of agricultural products in accordance with the supervisory spot-check plans.  
The testing for supervisory spot checks on the quality and safety of agricultural products shall be entrusted to testing institutions for the quality and safety of agricultural products that meet the conditions provided for in this Law. No fees shall be charged to the parties subject to the supervisory spot checks, and fees for the samples drawn shall be paid according to the market price, and the quantity shall not exceed the quantity prescribed by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
For the same batch of agricultural products that have been subject to a supervisory spot check by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at a higher level, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at a lower level shall not conduct another duplicate spot check.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　农产品质量安全检测应当充分利用现有的符合条件的检测机构。  
从事农产品质量安全检测的机构，应当具备相应的检测条件和能力，由省级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门或者其授权的部门考核合格。具体办法由国务院农业农村主管部门制定。  
农产品质量安全检测机构应当依法经资质认定。

**Article 48** The testing for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall make full use of the existing qualified testing institutions.  
Institutions engaged in the testing of the quality and safety of agricultural products shall possess the corresponding testing conditions and capabilities, and shall be assessed as qualified by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the provincial level or the departments authorized by them. The specific measures shall be formulated by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council.  
Testing institutions for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall obtain qualification accreditation in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　从事农产品质量安全检测工作的人员，应当具备相应的专业知识和实际操作技能，遵纪守法，恪守职业道德。  
农产品质量安全检测机构对出具的检测报告负责。检测报告应当客观公正，检测数据应当真实可靠，禁止出具虚假检测报告。

**Article 49** Personnel engaged in the testing of the quality and safety of agricultural products shall possess the corresponding professional knowledge and practical operational skills, abide by laws and regulations, and adhere to professional ethics.  
Testing institutions for the quality and safety of agricultural products shall be responsible for the testing reports they issue. The testing reports shall be objective and fair, and the testing data shall be truthful and reliable. The issuance of false testing reports is prohibited.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门可以采用国务院农业农村主管部门会同国务院市场监督管理等部门认定的快速检测方法，开展农产品质量安全监督抽查检测。抽查检测结果确定有关农产品不符合农产品质量安全标准的，可以作为行政处罚的证据。

**Article 50** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's governments at or above the county level may adopt rapid testing methods recognized by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council together with the market supervision and administration and other departments under the State Council to carry out supervisory spot-check testing for the quality and safety of agricultural products. If the results of the spot-check testing determine that the relevant agricultural products do not meet the quality and safety standards for agricultural products, such results may be used as evidence for administrative penalties.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　农产品生产经营者对监督抽查检测结果有异议的，可以自收到检测结果之日起五个工作日内，向实施农产品质量安全监督抽查的农业农村主管部门或者其上一级农业农村主管部门申请复检。复检机构与初检机构不得为同一机构。  
采用快速检测方法进行农产品质量安全监督抽查检测，被抽查人对检测结果有异议的，可以自收到检测结果时起四小时内申请复检。复检不得采用快速检测方法。  
复检机构应当自收到复检样品之日起七个工作日内出具检测报告。  
因检测结果错误给当事人造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 51** Where a producer or marketer of agricultural products objects to the result of supervision and inspection testing, it may, within five working days from the date of receipt of the testing result, apply for retesting to the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs that carried out the supervision and inspection of agricultural product quality and safety or its competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at the next higher level. The retesting institution and the initial testing institution shall not be the same institution.  
Where rapid testing methods are used in supervision and inspection testing of agricultural product quality and safety, and the person being tested objects to the testing result, it may apply for retesting within four hours from the time of receipt of the testing result. Rapid testing methods shall not be used for retesting.  
The retesting institution shall issue a testing report within seven working days from the date of receipt of the retesting sample.  
Where damage is caused to a party due to an erroneous testing result, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强对农产品生产的监督管理，开展日常检查，重点检查农产品产地环境、农业投入品购买和使用、农产品生产记录、承诺达标合格证开具等情况。  
国家鼓励和支持基层群众性自治组织建立农产品质量安全信息员工作制度，协助开展有关工作。

**Article 52** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen supervision and administration of the production of agricultural products, carry out routine inspections, and focus on inspecting the production environment of agricultural products, the purchase and use of agricultural inputs, production records of agricultural products, issuance of certificates of commitment to meet standards, and other relevant matters.  
The State encourages and supports grassroots self-governing mass organizations to establish a working system for information officers on agricultural product quality and safety to assist in carrying out relevant work.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　开展农产品质量安全监督检查，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）进入生产经营场所进行现场检查，调查了解农产品质量安全的有关情况；  
（二）查阅、复制农产品生产记录、购销台账等与农产品质量安全有关的资料；  
（三）抽样检测生产经营的农产品和使用的农业投入品以及其他有关产品；  
（四）查封、扣押有证据证明存在农产品质量安全隐患或者经检测不符合农产品质量安全标准的农产品；  
（五）查封、扣押有证据证明可能危及农产品质量安全或者经检测不符合产品质量标准的农业投入品以及其他有毒有害物质；  
（六）查封、扣押用于违法生产经营农产品的设施、设备、场所以及运输工具；  
（七）收缴伪造的农产品质量标志。  
农产品生产经营者应当协助、配合农产品质量安全监督检查，不得拒绝、阻挠。

**Article 53** When carrying out supervision and inspection of agricultural product quality and safety, the following measures may be taken:  
(1) Entering the place of production and marketing to conduct on-site inspection and investigation to understand the relevant circumstances of agricultural product quality and safety;  
(2) Reviewing and copying production records of agricultural products, purchase and sale ledgers, and other materials related to agricultural product quality and safety;  
(3) Sampling and testing agricultural products being produced and marketed, agricultural inputs being used, and other related products;  
(4) Sealing up or seizing agricultural products for which there is evidence of potential quality and safety hazards or which have been tested and found not to meet the agricultural product quality and safety standards;  
(5) Sealing up or seizing agricultural inputs and other toxic and hazardous substances for which there is evidence that they may endanger agricultural product quality and safety or which have been tested and found not to meet product quality standards;  
(6) Sealing up or seizing facilities, equipment, premises, and means of transport used for illegal production and marketing of agricultural products;  
(7) Confiscating counterfeit agricultural product quality marks.  
Producers and marketers of agricultural products shall assist and cooperate with the supervision and inspection of agricultural product quality and safety and shall not refuse or obstruct such inspection.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上人民政府农业农村等部门应当加强农产品质量安全信用体系建设，建立农产品生产经营者信用记录，记载行政处罚等信息，推进农产品质量安全信用信息的应用和管理。

**Article 54** The departments of agriculture and rural affairs and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen the construction of a credit system for agricultural product quality and safety, establish credit records of producers and marketers of agricultural products, record information such as administrative penalties, and promote the application and management of credit information on agricultural product quality and safety.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　农产品生产经营过程中存在质量安全隐患，未及时采取措施消除的，县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门可以对农产品生产经营者的法定代表人或者主要负责人进行责任约谈。农产品生产经营者应当立即采取措施，进行整改，消除隐患。

**Article 55** Where there are quality and safety hazards in the process of production and marketing of agricultural products and the producer or marketer fails to take timely measures to eliminate them, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level may conduct a responsibility interview with the legal representative or the principal person in charge of the producer or marketer of agricultural products. The producer or marketer of agricultural products shall immediately take measures to make corrections and eliminate the hazards.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家鼓励消费者协会和其他单位或者个人对农产品质量安全进行社会监督，对农产品质量安全监督管理工作提出意见和建议。任何单位和个人有权对违反本法的行为进行检举控告、投诉举报。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门应当建立农产品质量安全投诉举报制度，公开投诉举报渠道，收到投诉举报后，应当及时处理。对不属于本部门职责的，应当移交有权处理的部门并书面通知投诉举报人。

**Article 56** The State encourages consumer associations and other entities or individuals to carry out social supervision over agricultural product quality and safety and to offer opinions and suggestions on the supervision and administration of agricultural product quality and safety. Any entity or individual has the right to report, accuse, complain about, or tip off against any act violating this Law.  
The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a complaint and tip-off system for agricultural product quality and safety, make public the channels for complaints and tip-offs, and upon receiving complaints or tip-offs, shall handle them in a timely manner. Where the matter is not within their own functions and duties, they shall transfer it to the department with the authority to handle it and notify the complainant or tipper in writing.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门应当加强对农产品质量安全执法人员的专业技术培训并组织考核。不具备相应知识和能力的，不得从事农产品质量安全执法工作。

**Article 57** The competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen professional and technical training for law enforcement personnel on agricultural product quality and safety and organize assessments. Those who do not have the corresponding knowledge and ability shall not engage in law enforcement work on agricultural product quality and safety.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　上级人民政府应当督促下级人民政府履行农产品质量安全职责。对农产品质量安全责任落实不力、问题突出的地方人民政府，上级人民政府可以对其主要负责人进行责任约谈。被约谈的地方人民政府应当立即采取整改措施。

**Article 58** The people's government at a higher level shall supervise the people's government at a lower level in performing its duties on agricultural product quality and safety. Where a local people's government fails to effectively implement the responsibility for agricultural product quality and safety and has prominent problems, the people's government at a higher level may conduct a responsibility interview with its principal person in charge. The local people's government that is interviewed shall immediately take corrective measures.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国务院农业农村主管部门应当会同国务院有关部门制定国家农产品质量安全突发事件应急预案，并与国家食品安全事故应急预案相衔接。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据有关法律、行政法规的规定和上级人民政府的农产品质量安全突发事件应急预案，制定本行政区域的农产品质量安全突发事件应急预案。  
发生农产品质量安全事故时，有关单位和个人应当采取控制措施，及时向所在地乡镇人民政府和县级人民政府农业农村等部门报告；收到报告的机关应当按照农产品质量安全突发事件应急预案及时处理并报本级人民政府、上级人民政府有关部门。发生重大农产品质量安全事故时，按照规定上报国务院及其有关部门。  
任何单位和个人不得隐瞒、谎报、缓报农产品质量安全事故，不得隐匿、伪造、毁灭有关证据。

**Article 59** The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments of the State Council, formulate the national emergency response plan for agricultural product quality and safety incidents, which shall be coordinated with the national emergency response plan for food safety accidents.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations and the emergency response plan of the people's government at a higher level for agricultural product quality and safety incidents, formulate emergency response plans for agricultural product quality and safety incidents within their respective administrative regions.  
When an agricultural product quality and safety accident occurs, the relevant entities and individuals shall take control measures and promptly report to the township people's government and the county people's government's departments of agriculture and rural affairs and other relevant departments at the place where the accident occurs. The authority that receives the report shall handle it promptly in accordance with the emergency response plan for agricultural product quality and safety incidents and report it to the people's government at the same level and the relevant departments of the people's government at a higher level. When a major agricultural product quality and safety accident occurs, it shall be reported to the State Council and its relevant departments in accordance with the provisions.  
No entity or individual may conceal, make false reports, or delay reports of agricultural product quality and safety accidents, nor may they hide, falsify, or destroy relevant evidence.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理部门依照本法和《中华人民共和国食品安全法》等法律、法规的规定，对农产品进入批发、零售市场或者生产加工企业后的生产经营活动进行监督检查。

**Article 60** The market supervision and administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and regulations, conduct supervision and inspection of the production and marketing activities of agricultural products after they enter the wholesale or retail market or the production and processing enterprises.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府农业农村、市场监督管理等部门发现农产品质量安全违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，应当及时将案件移送公安机关。对移送的案件，公安机关应当及时审查；认为有犯罪事实需要追究刑事责任的，应当立案侦查。  
公安机关对依法不需要追究刑事责任但应当给予行政处罚的，应当及时将案件移送农业农村、市场监督管理等部门，有关部门应当依法处理。  
公安机关商请农业农村、市场监督管理、生态环境等部门提供检验结论、认定意见以及对涉案农产品进行无害化处理等协助的，有关部门应当及时提供、予以协助。

**Article 61** Where the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level discover that illegal acts on agricultural product quality and safety are suspected of constituting a crime, they shall promptly transfer the case to the public security organ. The public security organ shall promptly review the transferred case; if it believes that there is a criminal fact and criminal liability should be investigated, it shall file the case for investigation.  
Where the public security organ determines that criminal liability is not required according to law but an administrative penalty shall be imposed, it shall promptly transfer the case to the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments, and the relevant departments shall handle it according to law.  
Where the public security organ requests assistance from the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, ecological environment, and other relevant departments in providing testing conclusions, identification opinions, and carrying out harmless treatment of the agricultural products involved, the relevant departments shall promptly provide such assistance.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　违反本法规定，地方各级人民政府有下列情形之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予警告、记过、记大过处分；造成严重后果的，给予降级或者撤职处分：  
（一）未确定有关部门的农产品质量安全监督管理工作职责，未建立健全农产品质量安全工作机制，或者未落实农产品质量安全监督管理责任；  
（二）未制定本行政区域的农产品质量安全突发事件应急预案，或者发生农产品质量安全事故后未按照规定启动应急预案。

**Article 62** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a local people's government at any level falls under any of the following circumstances, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given warnings, demerits, or major demerits; if serious consequences are caused, they shall be given demotion or dismissal:  
(1) Failing to determine the functions and duties of relevant departments in the supervision and administration of agricultural product quality and safety, failing to establish and improve the working mechanism for agricultural product quality and safety, or failing to implement the responsibility for supervision and administration of agricultural product quality and safety;  
(2) Failing to formulate an emergency response plan for agricultural product quality and safety incidents within its administrative region, or failing to activate the emergency response plan in accordance with the provisions after an agricultural product quality and safety accident occurs.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　违反本法规定，县级以上人民政府农业农村等部门有下列行为之一的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员给予记大过处分；情节较重的，给予降级或者撤职处分；情节严重的，给予开除处分；造成严重后果的，其主要负责人还应当引咎辞职：  
（一）隐瞒、谎报、缓报农产品质量安全事故或者隐匿、伪造、毁灭有关证据；  
（二）未按照规定查处农产品质量安全事故，或者接到农产品质量安全事故报告未及时处理，造成事故扩大或者蔓延；  
（三）发现农产品质量安全重大风险隐患后，未及时采取相应措施，造成农产品质量安全事故或者不良社会影响；  
（四）不履行农产品质量安全监督管理职责，导致发生农产品质量安全事故。

**Article 63** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, the departments of agriculture and rural affairs and other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level commit any of the following acts, the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given major demerits; if the circumstances are relatively serious, they shall be given demotion or dismissal; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given expulsion; if serious consequences are caused, the principal person in charge shall also resign to assume responsibility:  
(1) Concealing, making false reports, or delaying reports of agricultural product quality and safety accidents, or hiding, falsifying, or destroying relevant evidence;  
(2) Failing to investigate and handle agricultural product quality and safety accidents in accordance with the provisions, or failing to handle in a timely manner upon receiving a report of an agricultural product quality and safety accident, causing the accident to expand or spread;  
(3) Failing to take timely corresponding measures after discovering major risks and hazards in agricultural product quality and safety, causing an agricultural product quality and safety accident or adverse social impact;  
(4) Failing to perform the duty of supervision and administration of agricultural product quality and safety, resulting in the occurrence of an agricultural product quality and safety accident.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　县级以上地方人民政府农业农村、市场监督管理等部门在履行农产品质量安全监督管理职责过程中，违法实施检查、强制等执法措施，给农产品生产经营者造成损失的，应当依法予以赔偿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 64** Where the departments of agriculture and rural affairs, market supervision and administration, and other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level, in the course of performing their duties of supervision and administration of agricultural product quality and safety, illegally implement inspection, compulsory measures, or other law enforcement measures, causing losses to producers or marketers of agricultural products, they shall compensate according to law, and the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　农产品质量安全检测机构、检测人员出具虚假检测报告的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门没收所收取的检测费用，检测费用不足一万元的，并处五万元以上十万元以下罚款，检测费用一万元以上的，并处检测费用五倍以上十倍以下罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处一万元以上五万元以下罚款；使消费者的合法权益受到损害的，农产品质量安全检测机构应当与农产品生产经营者承担连带责任。  
因农产品质量安全违法行为受到刑事处罚或者因出具虚假检测报告导致发生重大农产品质量安全事故的检测人员，终身不得从事农产品质量安全检测工作。农产品质量安全检测机构不得聘用上述人员。  
农产品质量安全检测机构有前两款违法行为的，由授予其资质的主管部门或者机构吊销该农产品质量安全检测机构的资质证书。

**Article 65** Where an agricultural product quality and safety testing institution or tester issues a false testing report, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the testing fees collected; if the testing fees are less than RMB 10,000, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall also be imposed; if the testing fees are RMB 10,000 or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the testing fees shall also be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be fined not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 50,000; if the legitimate rights and interests of consumers are harmed, the agricultural product quality and safety testing institution shall bear joint and several liability with the producer or marketer of agricultural products.  
A tester who has received criminal punishment for an illegal act on agricultural product quality and safety or who has caused a major agricultural product quality and safety accident due to issuing a false testing report shall be permanently prohibited from engaging in agricultural product quality and safety testing work. An agricultural product quality and safety testing institution shall not employ the aforementioned persons.  
Where an agricultural product quality and safety testing institution commits the illegal acts specified in the preceding two paragraphs, the competent department or institution that granted its qualification shall revoke the qualification certificate of the agricultural product quality and safety testing institution.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，在特定农产品禁止生产区域种植、养殖、捕捞、采集特定农产品或者建立特定农产品生产基地的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收农产品和违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下罚款。  
违反法律、法规规定，向农产品产地排放或者倾倒废水、废气、固体废物或者其他有毒有害物质的，依照有关环境保护法律、法规的规定处理、处罚；造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 66** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a person cultivates, breeds, fishes, collects specific agricultural products, or establishes a production base for specific agricultural products in a prohibited production area for specific agricultural products, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the agricultural products and illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains.  
Where, in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations, a person discharges or dumps wastewater, waste gas, solid waste, or other toxic and hazardous substances into the production area of agricultural products, the matter shall be handled and penalties imposed in accordance with the relevant environmental protection laws and regulations; if damage is caused, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　农药、肥料、农用薄膜等农业投入品的生产者、经营者、使用者未按照规定回收并妥善处置包装物或者废弃物的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门依照有关法律、法规的规定处理、处罚。

**Article 67** Where a producer, operator, or user of agricultural inputs such as pesticides, fertilizers, and agricultural films fails to recover and properly dispose of packaging or waste in accordance with the provisions, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall handle the matter and impose penalties in accordance with the relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　违反本法规定，农产品生产企业有下列情形之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处五千元以上五万元以下罚款：  
（一）未建立农产品质量安全管理制度；  
（二）未配备相应的农产品质量安全管理技术人员，且未委托具有专业技术知识的人员进行农产品质量安全指导。

**Article 68** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, an enterprise producing agricultural products falls under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall be imposed:  
(1) Failing to establish a management system for agricultural product quality and safety;  
(2) Failing to allocate corresponding technical personnel for agricultural product quality and safety management and failing to entrust personnel with professional technical knowledge to provide guidance on agricultural product quality and safety.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社、农业社会化服务组织未依照本法规定建立、保存农产品生产记录，或者伪造、变造农产品生产记录的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处二千元以上二万元以下罚款。

**Article 69** Where an enterprise producing agricultural products, a specialized farmers cooperative, or an agricultural socialized service organization fails to establish or maintain production records of agricultural products in accordance with the provisions of this Law, or forges or alters such records, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections within a time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 but not more than RMB 20,000 shall be imposed.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　违反本法规定，农产品生产经营者有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止生产经营、追回已经销售的农产品，对违法生产经营的农产品进行无害化处理或者予以监督销毁，没收违法所得，并可以没收用于违法生产经营的工具、设备、原料等物品；违法生产经营的农产品货值金额不足一万元的，并处十万元以上十五万元以下罚款，货值金额一万元以上的，并处货值金额十五倍以上三十倍以下罚款；对农户，并处一千元以上一万元以下罚款；情节严重的，有许可证的吊销许可证，并可以由公安机关对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员处五日以上十五日以下拘留：  
（一）在农产品生产经营过程中使用国家禁止使用的农业投入品或者其他有毒有害物质；  
（二）销售含有国家禁止使用的农药、兽药或者其他化合物的农产品；  
（三）销售病死、毒死或者死因不明的动物及其产品。  
明知农产品生产经营者从事前款规定的违法行为，仍为其提供生产经营场所或者其他条件的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止违法行为，没收违法所得，并处十万元以上二十万元以下罚款；使消费者的合法权益受到损害的，应当与农产品生产经营者承担连带责任。

**Article 70** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a producer or marketer of agricultural products commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of production and marketing, recall the agricultural products already sold, carry out harmless treatment or supervise the destruction of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also confiscate the tools, equipment, raw materials, and other articles used for illegal production and marketing; if the value of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products is less than RMB 10,000, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 150,000 shall also be imposed; if the value is RMB 10,000 or more, a fine of not less than fifteen times but not more than thirty times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; for a farmer, a fine of not less than RMB 1,000 but not more than RMB 10,000 shall also be imposed; if the circumstances are serious, any license held shall be revoked, and the public security organ may also impose detention of not less than five days but not more than fifteen days on the persons directly in charge and other directly responsible persons:  
(1) Using agricultural inputs prohibited by the State or other toxic and hazardous substances in the process of production and marketing of agricultural products;  
(2) Selling agricultural products containing pesticides, veterinary drugs, or other compounds prohibited by the State;  
(3) Selling animals that have died of disease, poisoning, or unknown causes, and their products.  
Any person who, knowing that a producer or marketer of agricultural products is engaged in the illegal acts specified in the preceding paragraph, still provides production and marketing premises or other conditions, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 200,000; if the legitimate rights and interests of consumers are harmed, such person shall bear joint and several liability with the producer or marketer of agricultural products.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，农产品生产经营者有下列行为之一，尚不构成犯罪的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止生产经营、追回已经销售的农产品，对违法生产经营的农产品进行无害化处理或者予以监督销毁，没收违法所得，并可以没收用于违法生产经营的工具、设备、原料等物品；违法生产经营的农产品货值金额不足一万元的，并处五万元以上十万元以下罚款，货值金额一万元以上的，并处货值金额十倍以上二十倍以下罚款；对农户，并处五百元以上五千元以下罚款：  
（一）销售农药、兽药等化学物质残留或者含有的重金属等有毒有害物质不符合农产品质量安全标准的农产品；  
（二）销售含有的致病性寄生虫、微生物或者生物毒素不符合农产品质量安全标准的农产品；  
（三）销售其他不符合农产品质量安全标准的农产品。

**Article 71** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a producer or marketer of agricultural products commits any of the following acts, which does not yet constitute a crime, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of production and marketing, recall the agricultural products already sold, carry out harmless treatment or supervise the destruction of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also confiscate the tools, equipment, raw materials, and other articles used for illegal production and marketing; if the value of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products is less than RMB 10,000, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 shall also be imposed; if the value is RMB 10,000 or more, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; for a farmer, a fine of not less than RMB 500 but not more than RMB 5,000 shall also be imposed:  
(1) Selling agricultural products with residues of chemicals such as pesticides and veterinary drugs or containing toxic and hazardous substances such as heavy metals that do not meet the agricultural product quality and safety standards;  
(2) Selling agricultural products containing pathogenic parasites, microorganisms, or biotoxins that do not meet the agricultural product quality and safety standards;  
(3) Selling other agricultural products that do not meet the agricultural product quality and safety standards.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　违反本法规定，农产品生产经营者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门责令停止生产经营、追回已经销售的农产品，对违法生产经营的农产品进行无害化处理或者予以监督销毁，没收违法所得，并可以没收用于违法生产经营的工具、设备、原料等物品；违法生产经营的农产品货值金额不足一万元的，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款，货值金额一万元以上的，并处货值金额五倍以上十倍以下罚款；对农户，并处三百元以上三千元以下罚款：  
（一）在农产品生产场所以及生产活动中使用的设施、设备、消毒剂、洗涤剂等不符合国家有关质量安全规定；  
（二）未按照国家有关强制性标准或者其他农产品质量安全规定使用保鲜剂、防腐剂、添加剂、包装材料等，或者使用的保鲜剂、防腐剂、添加剂、包装材料等不符合国家有关强制性标准或者其他质量安全规定；  
（三）将农产品与有毒有害物质一同储存、运输。

**Article 72** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a producer or marketer of agricultural products commits any of the following acts, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of production and marketing, recall the agricultural products already sold, carry out harmless treatment or supervise the destruction of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products, confiscate the illegal gains, and may also confiscate the tools, equipment, raw materials, and other articles used for illegal production and marketing; if the value of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products is less than RMB 10,000, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall also be imposed; if the value is RMB 10,000 or more, a fine of not less than five times but not more than ten times the value of the goods shall also be imposed; for a farmer, a fine of not less than RMB 300 but not more than RMB 3,000 shall also be imposed:  
(1) Using facilities, equipment, disinfectants, detergents, etc., in the production site or production activities of agricultural products that do not meet the relevant national quality and safety requirements;  
(2) Failing to use preservatives, anticorrosives, additives, packaging materials, etc., in accordance with the relevant national mandatory standards or other agricultural product quality and safety requirements, or using preservatives, anticorrosives, additives, packaging materials, etc., that do not meet the relevant national mandatory standards or other quality and safety requirements;  
(3) Storing or transporting agricultural products together with toxic and hazardous substances.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门按照职责给予批评教育，责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，处一百元以上一千元以下罚款：  
（一）农产品生产企业、农民专业合作社、从事农产品收购的单位或者个人未按照规定开具承诺达标合格证；  
（二）从事农产品收购的单位或者个人未按照规定收取、保存承诺达标合格证或者其他合格证明。

**Article 73** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, any of the following acts is committed, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, give criticism and education and order corrections within a time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not less than RMB 100 but not more than RMB 1,000 shall be imposed:  
(1) An enterprise producing agricultural products, a specialized farmers cooperative, or an entity or individual engaged in the purchase of agricultural products fails to issue a certificate of commitment to meet standards in accordance with the provisions;  
(2) An entity or individual engaged in the purchase of agricultural products fails to collect and maintain certificates of commitment to meet standards or other qualification certificates in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　农产品生产经营者冒用农产品质量标志，或者销售冒用农产品质量标志的农产品的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门按照职责责令改正，没收违法所得；违法生产经营的农产品货值金额不足五千元的，并处五千元以上五万元以下罚款，货值金额五千元以上的，并处货值金额十倍以上二十倍以下罚款。

**Article 74** Where a producer or marketer of agricultural products uses agricultural product quality marks without authorization or sells agricultural products bearing such unauthorized marks, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order correction and confiscate the illegal gains; if the value of the illegally produced and marketed agricultural products is less than RMB 5,000, a fine of not less than RMB 5,000 but not more than RMB 50,000 shall also be imposed; if the value is RMB 5,000 or more, a fine of not less than ten times but not more than twenty times the value of the goods shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法关于农产品质量安全追溯规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府农业农村主管部门按照职责责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，可以处一万元以下罚款。

**Article 75** Where a person violates the provisions of this Law on traceability of agricultural product quality and safety, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order corrections within a time limit; if no correction is made within the time limit, a fine of not more than RMB 10,000 may be imposed.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本法规定，拒绝、阻挠依法开展的农产品质量安全监督检查、事故调查处理、抽样检测和风险评估的，由有关主管部门按照职责责令停产停业，并处二千元以上五万元以下罚款；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 76** Where, in violation of the provisions of this Law, a person refuses or obstructs supervision and inspection, accident investigation and handling, sampling testing, or risk assessment conducted according to law in relation to agricultural product quality and safety, the relevant competent department shall, in accordance with its functions and duties, order cessation of production and business, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 2,000 but not more than RMB 50,000; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　《中华人民共和国食品安全法》对食用农产品进入批发、零售市场或者生产加工企业后的违法行为和法律责任有规定的，由县级以上地方人民政府市场监督管理部门依照其规定进行处罚。

**Article 77** Where the Food Safety Law of the People's Republic of China has provisions on illegal acts and legal liability after edible agricultural products enter the wholesale or retail market or production and processing enterprises, the market supervision and administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall impose penalties in accordance with those provisions.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 78** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　违反本法规定，给消费者造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担民事赔偿责任。生产经营者财产不足以同时承担民事赔偿责任和缴纳罚款、罚金时，先承担民事赔偿责任。  
食用农产品生产经营者违反本法规定，污染环境、侵害众多消费者合法权益，损害社会公共利益的，人民检察院可以依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 79** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law causes personal injury, property damage, or other harm to consumers, civil compensation liability shall be borne according to law. Where the property of a producer or marketer is insufficient to simultaneously bear civil compensation liability and pay fines or confiscation of property, the civil compensation liability shall be satisfied first.  
Where a producer or marketer of edible agricultural products, in violation of the provisions of this Law, pollutes the environment, infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of many consumers, or damages social public interests, the People's Procuratorate may, in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws, initiate a lawsuit in the People's Court.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　粮食收购、储存、运输环节的质量安全管理，依照有关粮食管理的法律、行政法规执行。

**Article 80** The quality and safety management in the procurement, storage, and transportation of grain shall be carried out in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on grain management.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　本法自2023年1月1日起施行。

**Article 81** This Law shall come into force on January 1, 2023.
