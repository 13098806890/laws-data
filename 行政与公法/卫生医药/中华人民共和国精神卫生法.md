# 中华人民共和国精神卫生法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2018-04-27  
**生效日期**：2018-04-27

> （2012年10月26日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十九次会议通过　根据2018年4月27日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国国境卫生检疫法〉等六部法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　心理健康促进和精神障碍预防 第三章　精神障碍的诊断和治疗 第四章　精神障碍的康复 第五章　保障措施 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了发展精神卫生事业，规范精神卫生服务，维护精神障碍患者的合法权益，制定本法。 第二条　在中华人民共和国境内开展维护和增进公民心理健康、预防和治疗精神障碍、促进精神障碍患者康复的活动，适用本法。 第三条　精神卫生工作实行预防为主的方针，坚持预防、治疗和康复相结合的原则。 第四条　精神障碍患者的人格尊严、人身和财产安全不受侵犯。 精神障碍患者的教育、劳动、医疗以及从国家和社会获得物质帮助等方面的合法权益受法律保护。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了发展精神卫生事业，规范精神卫生服务，维护精神障碍患者的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of developing mental health undertakings, regulating mental health services, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of persons with mental disorders.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在中华人民共和国境内开展维护和增进公民心理健康、预防和治疗精神障碍、促进精神障碍患者康复的活动，适用本法。

**Article 2** This Law shall apply to activities conducted within the territory of the People's Republic of China to maintain and improve citizens' mental health, prevent and treat mental disorders, and promote the rehabilitation of persons with mental disorders.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　精神卫生工作实行预防为主的方针，坚持预防、治疗和康复相结合的原则。

**Article 3** Mental health work shall implement the policy of prevention first, and adhere to the principle of combining prevention, treatment and rehabilitation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　精神障碍患者的人格尊严、人身和财产安全不受侵犯。  
精神障碍患者的教育、劳动、医疗以及从国家和社会获得物质帮助等方面的合法权益受法律保护。  
有关单位和个人应当对精神障碍患者的姓名、肖像、住址、工作单位、病历资料以及其他可能推断出其身份的信息予以保密；但是，依法履行职责需要公开的除外。

**Article 4** The personal dignity, personal and property safety of persons with mental disorders shall be inviolable.  
The legitimate rights and interests of persons with mental disorders in education, employment, medical care, and obtaining material assistance from the State and society shall be protected by law.  
The relevant entities and individuals shall keep confidential the names, portraits, addresses, work units, medical records, and other information that may infer the identity of persons with mental disorders, unless disclosure is required for the performance of duties according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　全社会应当尊重、理解、关爱精神障碍患者。  
任何组织或者个人不得歧视、侮辱、虐待精神障碍患者，不得非法限制精神障碍患者的人身自由。  
新闻报道和文学艺术作品等不得含有歧视、侮辱精神障碍患者的内容。

**Article 5** The whole society shall respect, understand and care for persons with mental disorders.  
No organization or individual may discriminate against, insult, or abuse persons with mental disorders, or illegally restrict their personal freedom.  
News reports, literary and artistic works, etc. shall not contain content that discriminates against or insults persons with mental disorders.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　精神卫生工作实行政府组织领导、部门各负其责、家庭和单位尽力尽责、全社会共同参与的综合管理机制。

**Article 6** Mental health work shall implement a comprehensive management mechanism featuring government organization and leadership, departments assuming respective responsibilities, families and units doing their utmost, and the whole society participating together.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府领导精神卫生工作，将其纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，建设和完善精神障碍的预防、治疗和康复服务体系，建立健全精神卫生工作协调机制和工作责任制，对有关部门承担的精神卫生工作进行考核、监督。  
乡镇人民政府和街道办事处根据本地区的实际情况，组织开展预防精神障碍发生、促进精神障碍患者康复等工作。

**Article 7** People's governments at or above the county level shall lead mental health work, incorporate it into national economic and social development plans, build and improve the prevention, treatment and rehabilitation service system for mental disorders, establish and improve the coordination mechanism and work responsibility system for mental health work, and assess and supervise the mental health work undertaken by relevant departments.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall, based on the actual conditions of their respective regions, organize and carry out work such as preventing the occurrence of mental disorders and promoting the rehabilitation of persons with mental disorders.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国务院卫生行政部门主管全国的精神卫生工作。县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门主管本行政区域的精神卫生工作。  
县级以上人民政府司法行政、民政、公安、教育、医疗保障等部门在各自职责范围内负责有关的精神卫生工作。

**Article 8** The health administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of national mental health work. The health administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of mental health work within their respective administrative regions.  
The judicial administrative, civil affairs, public security, education, medical security and other departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for relevant mental health work within their respective duties.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　精神障碍患者的监护人应当履行监护职责，维护精神障碍患者的合法权益。  
禁止对精神障碍患者实施家庭暴力，禁止遗弃精神障碍患者。

**Article 9** The guardians of persons with mental disorders shall perform guardianship duties and protect the legitimate rights and interests of persons with mental disorders.  
Domestic violence against persons with mental disorders and abandonment of persons with mental disorders are prohibited.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国残疾人联合会及其地方组织依照法律、法规或者接受政府委托，动员社会力量，开展精神卫生工作。  
村民委员会、居民委员会依照本法的规定开展精神卫生工作，并对所在地人民政府开展的精神卫生工作予以协助。  
国家鼓励和支持工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、红十字会、科学技术协会等团体依法开展精神卫生工作。

**Article 10** The China Disabled Persons' Federation and its local organizations shall, in accordance with laws and regulations or upon acceptance of government commission, mobilize social forces to carry out mental health work.  
Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall carry out mental health work in accordance with the provisions of this Law, and assist the local people's government in carrying out mental health work.  
The State encourages and supports trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, the Red Cross Society, the Science and Technology Association and other organizations to carry out mental health work according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家鼓励和支持开展精神卫生专门人才的培养，维护精神卫生工作人员的合法权益，加强精神卫生专业队伍建设。  
国家鼓励和支持开展精神卫生科学技术研究，发展现代医学、我国传统医学、心理学，提高精神障碍预防、诊断、治疗、康复的科学技术水平。  
国家鼓励和支持开展精神卫生领域的国际交流与合作。

**Article 11** The State encourages and supports the cultivation of specialized mental health personnel, protects the legitimate rights and interests of mental health workers, and strengthens the construction of the mental health professional team.  
The State encourages and supports scientific and technological research on mental health, develops modern medicine, traditional Chinese medicine, and psychology, and improves the scientific and technological level of prevention, diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of mental disorders.  
The State encourages and supports international exchanges and cooperation in the field of mental health.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门应当采取措施，鼓励和支持组织、个人提供精神卫生志愿服务，捐助精神卫生事业，兴建精神卫生公益设施。  
对在精神卫生工作中作出突出贡献的组织、个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 12** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to encourage and support organizations and individuals to provide voluntary mental health services, donate to mental health undertakings, and build public welfare facilities for mental health.  
Organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to mental health work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant state regulations.

### 第二章　心理健康促进和精神障碍预防

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门应当采取措施，加强心理健康促进和精神障碍预防工作，提高公众心理健康水平。

**Article 13** People's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to strengthen mental health promotion and prevention of mental disorders, and improve the mental health level of the public.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　各级人民政府和县级以上人民政府有关部门制定的突发事件应急预案，应当包括心理援助的内容。发生突发事件，履行统一领导职责或者组织处置突发事件的人民政府应当根据突发事件的具体情况，按照应急预案的规定，组织开展心理援助工作。

**Article 14** Emergency response plans formulated by people's governments at all levels and the relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall include the content of psychological assistance. When an emergency occurs, the people's government that exercises unified leadership responsibility or organizes the handling of the emergency shall, based on the specific circumstances of the emergency and in accordance with the provisions of the emergency response plan, organize and carry out psychological assistance work.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　用人单位应当创造有益于职工身心健康的工作环境，关注职工的心理健康；对处于职业发展特定时期或者在特殊岗位工作的职工，应当有针对性地开展心理健康教育。

**Article 15** Employers shall create a working environment conducive to the physical and mental health of employees, and pay attention to the mental health of employees; for employees at specific stages of career development or working in special positions, they shall conduct targeted mental health education.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　各级各类学校应当对学生进行精神卫生知识教育；配备或者聘请心理健康教育教师、辅导人员，并可以设立心理健康辅导室，对学生进行心理健康教育。学前教育机构应当对幼儿开展符合其特点的心理健康教育。  
发生自然灾害、意外伤害、公共安全事件等可能影响学生心理健康的事件，学校应当及时组织专业人员对学生进行心理援助。  
教师应当学习和了解相关的精神卫生知识，关注学生心理健康状况，正确引导、激励学生。地方各级人民政府教育行政部门和学校应当重视教师心理健康。  
学校和教师应当与学生父母或者其他监护人、近亲属沟通学生心理健康情况。

**Article 16** Schools of all levels and types shall conduct mental health knowledge education for students; equip or hire mental health education teachers and counselors, and may set up mental health counseling rooms to conduct mental health education for students. Preschool education institutions shall carry out mental health education suitable for the characteristics of young children.  
When natural disasters, accidental injuries, public security incidents, or other events that may affect students' mental health occur, schools shall promptly organize professionals to provide psychological assistance to students.  
Teachers shall learn and understand relevant mental health knowledge, pay attention to students' mental health status, and correctly guide and motivate students. The education administrative departments of local people's governments at all levels and schools shall pay attention to teachers' mental health.  
Schools and teachers shall communicate with students' parents or other guardians and close relatives about students' mental health status.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　医务人员开展疾病诊疗服务，应当按照诊断标准和治疗规范的要求，对就诊者进行心理健康指导；发现就诊者可能患有精神障碍的，应当建议其到符合本法规定的医疗机构就诊。

**Article 17** When providing diagnosis and treatment services, medical personnel shall, in accordance with the requirements of diagnostic standards and treatment norms, provide mental health guidance to patients; if they find that a patient may suffer from a mental disorder, they shall recommend that the patient go to a medical institution that meets the requirements of this Law for medical treatment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　监狱、看守所、拘留所、强制隔离戒毒所等场所，应当对服刑人员，被依法拘留、逮捕、强制隔离戒毒的人员等，开展精神卫生知识宣传，关注其心理健康状况，必要时提供心理咨询和心理辅导。

**Article 18** Prisons, detention houses, detention centers, compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation centers and other places shall carry out mental health knowledge publicity for prisoners, persons detained, arrested, or subjected to compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation according to law, pay attention to their mental health status, and provide psychological counseling and psychological guidance when necessary.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上地方人民政府人力资源社会保障、教育、卫生、司法行政、公安等部门应当在各自职责范围内分别对[本法第十五条](#art-15)至第十八条规定的单位履行精神障碍预防义务的情况进行督促和指导。

**Article 19** The human resources and social security, education, health, judicial administration, public security and other departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective duties, supervise and guide the units specified in Articles 15 to 18 of this Law in fulfilling their obligations for the prevention of mental disorders.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　村民委员会、居民委员会应当协助所在地人民政府及其有关部门开展社区心理健康指导、精神卫生知识宣传教育活动，创建有益于居民身心健康的社区环境。  
乡镇卫生院或者社区卫生服务机构应当为村民委员会、居民委员会开展社区心理健康指导、精神卫生知识宣传教育活动提供技术指导。

**Article 20** Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall assist the local people's government and its relevant departments in carrying out community mental health guidance and mental health knowledge publicity and education activities, and create a community environment conducive to the physical and mental health of residents.  
Township health centers or community health service institutions shall provide technical guidance to villagers' committees and residents' committees in carrying out community mental health guidance and mental health knowledge publicity and education activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　家庭成员之间应当相互关爱，创造良好、和睦的家庭环境，提高精神障碍预防意识；发现家庭成员可能患有精神障碍的，应当帮助其及时就诊，照顾其生活，做好看护管理。

**Article 21** Family members shall care for each other, create a good and harmonious family environment, and enhance awareness of mental disorder prevention; where a family member is found to possibly suffer from a mental disorder, they shall help the member seek medical treatment in a timely manner, take care of the member's daily life, and provide proper nursing and management.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家鼓励和支持新闻媒体、社会组织开展精神卫生的公益性宣传，普及精神卫生知识，引导公众关注心理健康，预防精神障碍的发生。

**Article 22** The State encourages and supports news media and social organizations to conduct public welfare publicity on mental health, popularize mental health knowledge, guide the public to pay attention to mental health, and prevent the occurrence of mental disorders.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　心理咨询人员应当提高业务素质，遵守执业规范，为社会公众提供专业化的心理咨询服务。  
心理咨询人员不得从事心理治疗或者精神障碍的诊断、治疗。  
心理咨询人员发现接受咨询的人员可能患有精神障碍的，应当建议其到符合本法规定的医疗机构就诊。  
心理咨询人员应当尊重接受咨询人员的隐私，并为其保守秘密。

**Article 23** Psychological counseling personnel shall improve their professional competence, abide by practice norms, and provide specialized psychological counseling services to the public.  
Psychological counseling personnel shall not engage in psychotherapy or the diagnosis or treatment of mental disorders.  
Where psychological counseling personnel find that a person receiving counseling may suffer from a mental disorder, they shall recommend that the person seek medical treatment at a medical institution that complies with the provisions of this Law.  
Psychological counseling personnel shall respect the privacy of persons receiving counseling and keep their secrets.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国务院卫生行政部门建立精神卫生监测网络，实行严重精神障碍发病报告制度，组织开展精神障碍发生状况、发展趋势等的监测和专题调查工作。精神卫生监测和严重精神障碍发病报告管理办法，由国务院卫生行政部门制定。  
国务院卫生行政部门应当会同有关部门、组织，建立精神卫生工作信息共享机制，实现信息互联互通、交流共享。

**Article 24** The health administrative department of the State Council shall establish a mental health monitoring network, implement a reporting system for the incidence of severe mental disorders, and organize and carry out monitoring and special investigations on the occurrence and development trends of mental disorders. The measures for the administration of mental health monitoring and the reporting of severe mental disorder incidence shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.  
The health administrative department of the State Council shall, in conjunction with relevant departments and organizations, establish a mechanism for sharing information on mental health work, to achieve interconnection, intercommunication, and sharing of information.

### 第三章　精神障碍的诊断和治疗

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　开展精神障碍诊断、治疗活动，应当具备下列条件，并依照医疗机构的管理规定办理有关手续：  
（一）有与从事的精神障碍诊断、治疗相适应的精神科执业医师、护士；  
（二）有满足开展精神障碍诊断、治疗需要的设施和设备；  
（三）有完善的精神障碍诊断、治疗管理制度和质量监控制度。  
从事精神障碍诊断、治疗的专科医疗机构还应当配备从事心理治疗的人员。

**Article 25** To carry out activities of diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders, the following conditions shall be met, and relevant formalities shall be handled in accordance with the administrative provisions on medical institutions:  
(1) having licensed psychiatrists and nurses that correspond to the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders to be undertaken;  
(2) having facilities and equipment that meet the needs of carrying out the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders;  
(3) having comprehensive management systems for the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders and a quality monitoring system.  
Specialized medical institutions engaged in the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders shall also be equipped with personnel engaged in psychotherapy.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　精神障碍的诊断、治疗，应当遵循维护患者合法权益、尊重患者人格尊严的原则，保障患者在现有条件下获得良好的精神卫生服务。  
精神障碍分类、诊断标准和治疗规范，由国务院卫生行政部门组织制定。

**Article 26** The diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders shall follow the principles of safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of patients and respecting patients' human dignity, ensuring that patients receive good mental health services under the existing conditions.  
The classification, diagnostic criteria, and treatment norms for mental disorders shall be formulated under the organization of the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　精神障碍的诊断应当以精神健康状况为依据。  
除法律另有规定外，不得违背本人意志进行确定其是否患有精神障碍的医学检查。

**Article 27** The diagnosis of a mental disorder shall be based on the mental health status.  
Unless otherwise provided by law, no medical examination to determine whether a person suffers from a mental disorder may be conducted against the person's will.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　除个人自行到医疗机构进行精神障碍诊断外，疑似精神障碍患者的近亲属可以将其送往医疗机构进行精神障碍诊断。对查找不到近亲属的流浪乞讨疑似精神障碍患者，由当地民政等有关部门按照职责分工，帮助送往医疗机构进行精神障碍诊断。  
疑似精神障碍患者发生伤害自身、危害他人安全的行为，或者有伤害自身、危害他人安全的危险的，其近亲属、所在单位、当地公安机关应当立即采取措施予以制止，并将其送往医疗机构进行精神障碍诊断。  
医疗机构接到送诊的疑似精神障碍患者，不得拒绝为其作出诊断。

**Article 28** Except where individuals go to a medical institution on their own for diagnosis of a mental disorder, a close relative of a person suspected of having a mental disorder may send the person to a medical institution for diagnosis of a mental disorder. For a vagrant or beggar suspected of having a mental disorder for whom no close relative can be found, the local civil affairs department and other relevant departments shall, according to their respective duties, help send the person to a medical institution for diagnosis of a mental disorder.  
Where a person suspected of having a mental disorder commits acts of self-harm or endangering the safety of others, or is at risk of self-harm or endangering the safety of others, the close relatives, the person's unit, and the local public security organ shall immediately take measures to stop such acts and send the person to a medical institution for diagnosis of a mental disorder.  
A medical institution that receives a person suspected of having a mental disorder sent for diagnosis shall not refuse to make a diagnosis.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　精神障碍的诊断应当由精神科执业医师作出。  
医疗机构接到依照[本法第二十八条](#art-28)第二款规定送诊的疑似精神障碍患者，应当将其留院，立即指派精神科执业医师进行诊断，并及时出具诊断结论。

**Article 29** The diagnosis of a mental disorder shall be made by a licensed psychiatrist.  
A medical institution that receives a person suspected of having a mental disorder sent for diagnosis in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 28 of this Law shall keep the person in the hospital, immediately assign a licensed psychiatrist to make a diagnosis, and issue a diagnostic conclusion in a timely manner.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　精神障碍的住院治疗实行自愿原则。  
诊断结论、病情评估表明，就诊者为严重精神障碍患者并有下列情形之一的，应当对其实施住院治疗：  
（一）已经发生伤害自身的行为，或者有伤害自身的危险的；  
（二）已经发生危害他人安全的行为，或者有危害他人安全的危险的。

**Article 30** Hospitalization for the treatment of mental disorders shall follow the principle of voluntariness.  
Where the diagnostic conclusion and disease assessment indicate that the patient is a person with a severe mental disorder and falls under any of the following circumstances, the patient shall be placed under hospitalization:  
(1) having already committed acts of self-harm, or being at risk of self-harm;  
(2) having already committed acts endangering the safety of others, or being at risk of endangering the safety of others.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　精神障碍患者有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第一项情形的，经其监护人同意，医疗机构应当对患者实施住院治疗；监护人不同意的，医疗机构不得对患者实施住院治疗。监护人应当对在家居住的患者做好看护管理。

**Article 31** Where a person with a mental disorder falls under the circumstance specified in item (1) of paragraph 2 of Article 30 of this Law, the medical institution shall implement hospitalization for the patient with the consent of the guardian; if the guardian does not consent, the medical institution shall not implement hospitalization for the patient. The guardian shall provide proper nursing and management for the patient living at home.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　精神障碍患者有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第二项情形，患者或者其监护人对需要住院治疗的诊断结论有异议，不同意对患者实施住院治疗的，可以要求再次诊断和鉴定。  
依照前款规定要求再次诊断的，应当自收到诊断结论之日起三日内向原医疗机构或者其他具有合法资质的医疗机构提出。承担再次诊断的医疗机构应当在接到再次诊断要求后指派二名初次诊断医师以外的精神科执业医师进行再次诊断，并及时出具再次诊断结论。承担再次诊断的执业医师应当到收治患者的医疗机构面见、询问患者，该医疗机构应当予以配合。  
对再次诊断结论有异议的，可以自主委托依法取得执业资质的鉴定机构进行精神障碍医学鉴定；医疗机构应当公示经公告的鉴定机构名单和联系方式。接受委托的鉴定机构应当指定本机构具有该鉴定事项执业资格的二名以上鉴定人共同进行鉴定，并及时出具鉴定报告。

**Article 32** Where a person with a mental disorder falls under the circumstance specified in item (2) of paragraph 2 of Article 30 of this Law, and the patient or the guardian disagrees with the diagnostic conclusion that hospitalization is necessary and does not consent to the patient being hospitalized, the patient or the guardian may request a re-diagnosis and an expert evaluation.  
Where a re-diagnosis is requested in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the request shall be made to the original medical institution or another medical institution with lawful qualifications within three days from the date of receipt of the diagnostic conclusion. The medical institution undertaking the re-diagnosis shall, upon receiving the request for re-diagnosis, assign two licensed psychiatrists other than those who made the initial diagnosis to conduct the re-diagnosis and issue the re-diagnosis conclusion in a timely manner. The licensed psychiatrists undertaking the re-diagnosis shall meet and interview the patient at the medical institution where the patient is admitted, and that medical institution shall cooperate.  
Where there is disagreement with the re-diagnosis conclusion, the patient or the guardian may independently entrust an expert evaluation institution that has obtained practice qualifications according to law to conduct a forensic psychiatric evaluation of the mental disorder; the medical institution shall publish the list of publicly announced expert evaluation institutions and their contact information. The entrusted expert evaluation institution shall designate two or more evaluators who have the practice qualifications for the evaluation matter from the institution to jointly conduct the evaluation and issue an evaluation report in a timely manner.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　鉴定人应当到收治精神障碍患者的医疗机构面见、询问患者，该医疗机构应当予以配合。  
鉴定人本人或者其近亲属与鉴定事项有利害关系，可能影响其独立、客观、公正进行鉴定的，应当回避。

**Article 33** An evaluator shall go to the medical institution where a person with a mental disorder is admitted to meet and interview the patient, and that medical institution shall cooperate.  
Where the evaluator himself or herself or a close relative has an interest in the evaluation matter that may affect the independence, objectivity, and impartiality of the evaluation, the evaluator shall withdraw.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　鉴定机构、鉴定人应当遵守有关法律、法规、规章的规定，尊重科学，恪守职业道德，按照精神障碍鉴定的实施程序、技术方法和操作规范，依法独立进行鉴定，出具客观、公正的鉴定报告。  
鉴定人应当对鉴定过程进行实时记录并签名。记录的内容应当真实、客观、准确、完整，记录的文本或者声像载体应当妥善保存。

**Article 34** Expert evaluation institutions and evaluators shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws, regulations, and rules, respect science, abide by professional ethics, and conduct evaluations independently according to law in accordance with the implementation procedures, technical methods, and operational norms for mental disorder evaluation, and issue an objective and impartial evaluation report.  
Evaluators shall make real-time records of the evaluation process and sign them. The content of the records shall be truthful, objective, accurate, and complete, and the text or audio-visual media of the records shall be properly preserved.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　再次诊断结论或者鉴定报告表明，不能确定就诊者为严重精神障碍患者，或者患者不需要住院治疗的，医疗机构不得对其实施住院治疗。  
再次诊断结论或者鉴定报告表明，精神障碍患者有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第二项情形的，其监护人应当同意对患者实施住院治疗。监护人阻碍实施住院治疗或者患者擅自脱离住院治疗的，可以由公安机关协助医疗机构采取措施对患者实施住院治疗。  
在相关机构出具再次诊断结论、鉴定报告前，收治精神障碍患者的医疗机构应当按照诊疗规范的要求对患者实施住院治疗。

**Article 35** Where a re-diagnosis conclusion or evaluation report indicates that the patient cannot be confirmed as a person with a severe mental disorder, or the patient does not need hospitalization, the medical institution shall not implement hospitalization for the patient.  
Where a re-diagnosis conclusion or evaluation report indicates that a person with a mental disorder falls under the circumstance specified in item (2) of paragraph 2 of Article 30 of this Law, the guardian shall consent to hospitalization for the patient. Where the guardian obstructs the implementation of hospitalization or the patient absconds from hospitalization without authorization, the public security organ may assist the medical institution in taking measures to implement hospitalization for the patient.  
Before the relevant institution issues a re-diagnosis conclusion or evaluation report, the medical institution that admits a person with a mental disorder shall implement hospitalization for the patient in accordance with the requirements of the diagnosis and treatment norms.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　诊断结论表明需要住院治疗的精神障碍患者，本人没有能力办理住院手续的，由其监护人办理住院手续；患者属于查找不到监护人的流浪乞讨人员的，由送诊的有关部门办理住院手续。  
精神障碍患者有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第二项情形，其监护人不办理住院手续的，由患者所在单位、村民委员会或者居民委员会办理住院手续，并由医疗机构在患者病历中予以记录。

**Article 36** Where a person with a mental disorder whose diagnostic conclusion indicates the need for hospitalization is unable to complete the hospitalization procedures on his or her own, the guardian shall complete the hospitalization procedures; where the patient is a vagrant or beggar for whom no guardian can be found, the relevant department that sent the patient for diagnosis shall complete the hospitalization procedures.  
Where a person with a mental disorder falls under the circumstance specified in item (2) of paragraph 2 of Article 30 of this Law, and the guardian fails to complete the hospitalization procedures, the patient's unit, villagers' committee, or residents' committee shall complete the hospitalization procedures, and the medical institution shall record it in the patient's medical record.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　医疗机构及其医务人员应当将精神障碍患者在诊断、治疗过程中享有的权利，告知患者或者其监护人。

**Article 37** Medical institutions and their medical personnel shall inform the patient or the guardian of the rights enjoyed by a person with a mental disorder in the process of diagnosis and treatment.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　医疗机构应当配备适宜的设施、设备，保护就诊和住院治疗的精神障碍患者的人身安全，防止其受到伤害，并为住院患者创造尽可能接近正常生活的环境和条件。

**Article 38** Medical institutions shall be equipped with appropriate facilities and equipment to protect the personal safety of persons with mental disorders receiving outpatient services or hospitalization, prevent them from being harmed, and create an environment and conditions as close to normal life as possible for inpatients.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　医疗机构及其医务人员应当遵循精神障碍诊断标准和治疗规范，制定治疗方案，并向精神障碍患者或者其监护人告知治疗方案和治疗方法、目的以及可能产生的后果。

**Article 39** Medical institutions and their medical personnel shall follow the diagnostic criteria and treatment norms for mental disorders, formulate a treatment plan, and inform the person with a mental disorder or the guardian of the treatment plan, the treatment method, its purpose, and the possible consequences.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　精神障碍患者在医疗机构内发生或者将要发生伤害自身、危害他人安全、扰乱医疗秩序的行为，医疗机构及其医务人员在没有其他可替代措施的情况下，可以实施约束、隔离等保护性医疗措施。实施保护性医疗措施应当遵循诊断标准和治疗规范，并在实施后告知患者的监护人。  
禁止利用约束、隔离等保护性医疗措施惩罚精神障碍患者。

**Article 40** Where a person with a mental disorder commits or is about to commit acts of self-harm, endangering the safety of others, or disrupting medical order within a medical institution, the medical institution and its medical personnel may, where there is no other alternative measure, implement protective medical measures such as restraint and isolation. The implementation of protective medical measures shall follow the diagnostic criteria and treatment norms, and the guardian of the patient shall be informed after implementation.  
The use of protective medical measures such as restraint and isolation to punish a person with a mental disorder is prohibited.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　对精神障碍患者使用药物，应当以诊断和治疗为目的，使用安全、有效的药物，不得为诊断或者治疗以外的目的使用药物。  
医疗机构不得强迫精神障碍患者从事生产劳动。

**Article 41** The use of drugs on patients with mental disorders shall be for the purpose of diagnosis and treatment, and safe and effective drugs shall be used. Drugs shall not be used for purposes other than diagnosis or treatment.  
Medical institutions shall not compel patients with mental disorders to engage in productive labor.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　禁止对依照[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款规定实施住院治疗的精神障碍患者实施以治疗精神障碍为目的的外科手术。

**Article 42** It is prohibited to perform surgical operations aimed at treating mental disorders on patients with mental disorders who are hospitalized in accordance with the provisions of Article 30, paragraph 2 of this Law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　医疗机构对精神障碍患者实施下列治疗措施，应当向患者或者其监护人告知医疗风险、替代医疗方案等情况，并取得患者的书面同意；无法取得患者意见的，应当取得其监护人的书面同意，并经本医疗机构伦理委员会批准：  
（一）导致人体器官丧失功能的外科手术；  
（二）与精神障碍治疗有关的实验性临床医疗。  
实施前款第一项治疗措施，因情况紧急查找不到监护人的，应当取得本医疗机构负责人和伦理委员会批准。  
禁止对精神障碍患者实施与治疗其精神障碍无关的实验性临床医疗。

**Article 43** Where a medical institution implements the following treatment measures on a patient with a mental disorder, it shall inform the patient or his or her guardian of the medical risks, alternative medical plans, and other circumstances, and obtain the patient's written consent; if the patient's opinion cannot be obtained, the written consent of his or her guardian shall be obtained, and approval from the ethics committee of the medical institution shall be obtained:  
(1) surgery causing loss of function of human organs;  
(2) experimental clinical medical treatment related to the treatment of mental disorders.  
Where the treatment measure specified in subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph is implemented, and the guardian cannot be located due to an emergency, approval from the person in charge of the medical institution and the ethics committee shall be obtained.  
It is prohibited to implement experimental clinical medical treatment unrelated to the treatment of a patient's mental disorder on a patient with a mental disorder.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　自愿住院治疗的精神障碍患者可以随时要求出院，医疗机构应当同意。  
对有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第一项情形的精神障碍患者实施住院治疗的，监护人可以随时要求患者出院，医疗机构应当同意。  
医疗机构认为前两款规定的精神障碍患者不宜出院的，应当告知不宜出院的理由；患者或者其监护人仍要求出院的，执业医师应当在病历资料中详细记录告知的过程，同时提出出院后的医学建议，患者或者其监护人应当签字确认。  
对有[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款第二项情形的精神障碍患者实施住院治疗，医疗机构认为患者可以出院的，应当立即告知患者及其监护人。  
医疗机构应当根据精神障碍患者病情，及时组织精神科执业医师对依照[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款规定实施住院治疗的患者进行检查评估。评估结果表明患者不需要继续住院治疗的，医疗机构应当立即通知患者及其监护人。

**Article 44** A patient with a mental disorder voluntarily hospitalized may request discharge at any time, and the medical institution shall consent.  
Where a patient with a mental disorder falling under the circumstances specified in Article 30, paragraph 2, subparagraph (1) of this Law is hospitalized for treatment, the guardian may request the patient's discharge at any time, and the medical institution shall consent.  
If the medical institution considers that a patient with a mental disorder as specified in the preceding two paragraphs is not suitable for discharge, it shall inform the patient of the reasons for unsuitability; if the patient or his or her guardian still requests discharge, the attending physician shall record the process of informing in detail in the medical records, and at the same time put forward medical recommendations after discharge, and the patient or his or her guardian shall sign to confirm.  
Where a patient with a mental disorder falling under the circumstances specified in Article 30, paragraph 2, subparagraph (2) of this Law is hospitalized for treatment, and the medical institution considers that the patient may be discharged, it shall immediately inform the patient and his or her guardian.  
The medical institution shall, based on the condition of the patient with a mental disorder, promptly organize psychiatrists to examine and assess the patient who is hospitalized in accordance with the provisions of Article 30, paragraph 2 of this Law. If the assessment results indicate that the patient no longer needs continued hospitalization, the medical institution shall immediately notify the patient and his or her guardian.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　精神障碍患者出院，本人没有能力办理出院手续的，监护人应当为其办理出院手续。

**Article 45** Where a patient with a mental disorder is discharged from hospital and is not capable of handling the discharge procedures, the guardian shall handle the discharge procedures for him or her.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　医疗机构及其医务人员应当尊重住院精神障碍患者的通讯和会见探访者等权利。除在急性发病期或者为了避免妨碍治疗可以暂时性限制外，不得限制患者的通讯和会见探访者等权利。

**Article 46** Medical institutions and their medical personnel shall respect the rights of hospitalized patients with mental disorders to correspondence and meetings with visitors, among others. Except where temporary restrictions may be imposed during an acute episode or to avoid hindering treatment, the rights of patients to correspondence and meetings with visitors, among others, shall not be restricted.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　医疗机构及其医务人员应当在病历资料中如实记录精神障碍患者的病情、治疗措施、用药情况、实施约束、隔离措施等内容，并如实告知患者或者其监护人。患者及其监护人可以查阅、复制病历资料；但是，患者查阅、复制病历资料可能对其治疗产生不利影响的除外。病历资料保存期限不得少于三十年。

**Article 47** Medical institutions and their medical personnel shall truthfully record in the medical records the condition of a patient with a mental disorder, treatment measures, medication, implementation of restraint and isolation measures, and other content, and shall truthfully inform the patient or his or her guardian. The patient and his or her guardian may consult and copy the medical records; except where consulting or copying the medical records by the patient may have an adverse effect on his or her treatment. The retention period of medical records shall not be less than 30 years.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　医疗机构不得因就诊者是精神障碍患者，推诿或者拒绝为其治疗属于本医疗机构诊疗范围的其他疾病。

**Article 48** A medical institution shall not, on the grounds that the patient is a person with a mental disorder, shirk or refuse to treat other diseases that fall within the scope of diagnosis and treatment of the medical institution for the patient.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　精神障碍患者的监护人应当妥善看护未住院治疗的患者，按照医嘱督促其按时服药、接受随访或者治疗。村民委员会、居民委员会、患者所在单位等应当依患者或者其监护人的请求，对监护人看护患者提供必要的帮助。

**Article 49** The guardian of a patient with a mental disorder shall properly take care of the patient who is not hospitalized, and urge the patient to take medication on time, accept follow-up visits or treatment in accordance with medical advice. The villagers' committee, residents' committee, the patient's work unit, etc., shall, upon the request of the patient or his or her guardian, provide necessary assistance to the guardian in taking care of the patient.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门应当定期就下列事项对本行政区域内从事精神障碍诊断、治疗的医疗机构进行检查：  
（一）相关人员、设施、设备是否符合本法要求；  
（二）诊疗行为是否符合本法以及诊断标准、治疗规范的规定；  
（三）对精神障碍患者实施住院治疗的程序是否符合本法规定；  
（四）是否依法维护精神障碍患者的合法权益。  
县级以上地方人民政府卫生行政部门进行前款规定的检查，应当听取精神障碍患者及其监护人的意见；发现存在违反本法行为的，应当立即制止或者责令改正，并依法作出处理。

**Article 50** The health administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly inspect medical institutions engaged in the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders within their respective administrative regions on the following matters:  
(1) whether the relevant personnel, facilities, and equipment comply with the requirements of this Law;  
(2) whether the diagnosis and treatment practices comply with the provisions of this Law, diagnostic criteria, and treatment norms;  
(3) whether the procedures for hospitalization of patients with mental disorders comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) whether the legitimate rights and interests of patients with mental disorders are protected according to law.  
When conducting the inspection as specified in the preceding paragraph, the health administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall listen to the opinions of patients with mental disorders and their guardians; if any violation of this Law is found, they shall immediately stop it or order correction, and handle it according to law.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　心理治疗活动应当在医疗机构内开展。专门从事心理治疗的人员不得从事精神障碍的诊断，不得为精神障碍患者开具处方或者提供外科治疗。心理治疗的技术规范由国务院卫生行政部门制定。

**Article 51** Psychotherapy activities shall be conducted within medical institutions. Personnel specializing in psychotherapy shall not engage in the diagnosis of mental disorders, and shall not prescribe medication or provide surgical treatment for patients with mental disorders. The technical specifications for psychotherapy shall be formulated by the health administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　监狱、强制隔离戒毒所等场所应当采取措施，保证患有精神障碍的服刑人员、强制隔离戒毒人员等获得治疗。

**Article 52** Prisons, compulsory isolation drug rehabilitation centers, and other such places shall take measures to ensure that incarcerated persons, persons undergoing compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation, etc., who suffer from mental disorders receive treatment.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　精神障碍患者违反治安管理处罚法或者触犯刑法的，依照有关法律的规定处理。

**Article 53** If a patient with a mental disorder violates the Public Security Administration Punishments Law or commits a crime under the Criminal Law, he or she shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws.

### 第四章　精神障碍的康复

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　社区康复机构应当为需要康复的精神障碍患者提供场所和条件，对患者进行生活自理能力和社会适应能力等方面的康复训练。

**Article 54** Community rehabilitation institutions shall provide venues and conditions for patients with mental disorders who need rehabilitation, and conduct rehabilitation training for patients in aspects such as self-care ability and social adaptation ability.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　医疗机构应当为在家居住的严重精神障碍患者提供精神科基本药物维持治疗，并为社区康复机构提供有关精神障碍康复的技术指导和支持。  
社区卫生服务机构、乡镇卫生院、村卫生室应当建立严重精神障碍患者的健康档案，对在家居住的严重精神障碍患者进行定期随访，指导患者服药和开展康复训练，并对患者的监护人进行精神卫生知识和看护知识的培训。县级人民政府卫生行政部门应当为社区卫生服务机构、乡镇卫生院、村卫生室开展上述工作给予指导和培训。

**Article 55** Medical institutions shall provide basic psychiatric medication maintenance treatment for patients with severe mental disorders living at home, and provide technical guidance and support to community rehabilitation institutions concerning rehabilitation of mental disorders.  
Community health service institutions, township health centers, and village clinics shall establish health records for patients with severe mental disorders, conduct regular follow-up visits to patients with severe mental disorders living at home, guide patients in taking medication and carrying out rehabilitation training, and provide training on mental health knowledge and care knowledge to the guardians of patients. The health administrative departments of county people's governments shall provide guidance and training to community health service institutions, township health centers, and village clinics in carrying out the aforementioned work.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　村民委员会、居民委员会应当为生活困难的精神障碍患者家庭提供帮助，并向所在地乡镇人民政府或者街道办事处以及县级人民政府有关部门反映患者及其家庭的情况和要求，帮助其解决实际困难，为患者融入社会创造条件。

**Article 56** Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall provide assistance to families of patients with mental disorders who have financial difficulties, and report the conditions and needs of the patients and their families to the local township people's government or sub-district office and the relevant departments of the county people's government, help them solve practical difficulties, and create conditions for the patients to integrate into society.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　残疾人组织或者残疾人康复机构应当根据精神障碍患者康复的需要，组织患者参加康复活动。

**Article 57** Organizations for persons with disabilities or rehabilitation institutions for persons with disabilities shall, based on the rehabilitation needs of patients with mental disorders, organize patients to participate in rehabilitation activities.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　用人单位应当根据精神障碍患者的实际情况，安排患者从事力所能及的工作，保障患者享有同等待遇，安排患者参加必要的职业技能培训，提高患者的就业能力，为患者创造适宜的工作环境，对患者在工作中取得的成绩予以鼓励。

**Article 58** Employers shall, based on the actual conditions of patients with mental disorders, arrange work within the patients' capabilities, ensure that the patients enjoy equal treatment, arrange for the patients to participate in necessary vocational skills training to enhance their employability, create a suitable working environment for the patients, and encourage the patients for their achievements at work.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　精神障碍患者的监护人应当协助患者进行生活自理能力和社会适应能力等方面的康复训练。  
精神障碍患者的监护人在看护患者过程中需要技术指导的，社区卫生服务机构或者乡镇卫生院、村卫生室、社区康复机构应当提供。

**Article 59** The guardian of a patient with a mental disorder shall assist the patient in rehabilitation training in aspects such as self-care ability and social adaptation ability.  
If the guardian of a patient with a mental disorder needs technical guidance in the process of caring for the patient, the community health service institution, township health center, village clinic, or community rehabilitation institution shall provide it.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门会同有关部门依据国民经济和社会发展规划的要求，制定精神卫生工作规划并组织实施。  
精神卫生监测和专题调查结果应当作为制定精神卫生工作规划的依据。

**Article 60** The health administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with relevant departments, formulate mental health work plans in accordance with the requirements of the national economic and social development plan and organize their implementation.  
The results of mental health monitoring and special surveys shall serve as the basis for formulating mental health work plans.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本行政区域的实际情况，统筹规划，整合资源，建设和完善精神卫生服务体系，加强精神障碍预防、治疗和康复服务能力建设。  
县级人民政府根据本行政区域的实际情况，统筹规划，建立精神障碍患者社区康复机构。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当采取措施，鼓励和支持社会力量举办从事精神障碍诊断、治疗的医疗机构和精神障碍患者康复机构。

**Article 61** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, based on the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, make overall plans, integrate resources, establish and improve the mental health service system, and strengthen the capacity building for the prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation of mental disorders.  
The people's governments at the county level shall, based on the actual conditions of their respective administrative regions, make overall plans and establish community rehabilitation institutions for persons with mental disorders.  
The local people's governments at or above the county level shall take measures to encourage and support social forces to establish medical institutions engaged in the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders and rehabilitation institutions for persons with mental disorders.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　各级人民政府应当根据精神卫生工作需要，加大财政投入力度，保障精神卫生工作所需经费，将精神卫生工作经费列入本级财政预算。

**Article 62** The people's governments at all levels shall, based on the needs of mental health work, increase financial input, ensure the funds required for mental health work, and include the funds for mental health work in their respective fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　国家加强基层精神卫生服务体系建设，扶持贫困地区、边远地区的精神卫生工作，保障城市社区、农村基层精神卫生工作所需经费。

**Article 63** The State shall strengthen the construction of the primary-level mental health service system, support mental health work in poverty-stricken and remote areas, and ensure the funds required for mental health work in urban communities and rural primary-level areas.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　医学院校应当加强精神医学的教学和研究，按照精神卫生工作的实际需要培养精神医学专门人才，为精神卫生工作提供人才保障。

**Article 64** Medical colleges and universities shall strengthen the teaching and research of psychiatry, train specialized personnel in psychiatry according to the actual needs of mental health work, and provide talent guarantee for mental health work.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　综合性医疗机构应当按照国务院卫生行政部门的规定开设精神科门诊或者心理治疗门诊，提高精神障碍预防、诊断、治疗能力。

**Article 65** Comprehensive medical institutions shall, in accordance with the provisions of the health administrative department of the State Council, establish psychiatric outpatient clinics or psychotherapy outpatient clinics to improve the capacity for the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of mental disorders.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　医疗机构应当组织医务人员学习精神卫生知识和相关法律、法规、政策。  
从事精神障碍诊断、治疗、康复的机构应当定期组织医务人员、工作人员进行在岗培训，更新精神卫生知识。  
县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门应当组织医务人员进行精神卫生知识培训，提高其识别精神障碍的能力。

**Article 66** Medical institutions shall organize medical personnel to study mental health knowledge and relevant laws, regulations, and policies.  
Institutions engaged in the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of mental disorders shall regularly organize on-the-job training for medical personnel and staff to update their mental health knowledge.  
The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize training on mental health knowledge for medical personnel to improve their ability to identify mental disorders.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　师范院校应当为学生开设精神卫生课程；医学院校应当为非精神医学专业的学生开设精神卫生课程。  
县级以上人民政府教育行政部门对教师进行上岗前和在岗培训，应当有精神卫生的内容，并定期组织心理健康教育教师、辅导人员进行专业培训。

**Article 67** Teacher-training institutions shall offer mental health courses for students; medical colleges and universities shall offer mental health courses for students not majoring in psychiatry.  
The education administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall include mental health content in pre-service and in-service training for teachers, and shall regularly organize professional training for mental health education teachers and counselors.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门应当组织医疗机构为严重精神障碍患者免费提供基本公共卫生服务。  
精神障碍患者的医疗费用按照国家有关社会保险的规定由基本医疗保险基金支付。医疗保险经办机构应当按照国家有关规定将精神障碍患者纳入城镇职工基本医疗保险、城镇居民基本医疗保险或者新型农村合作医疗的保障范围。县级人民政府应当按照国家有关规定对家庭经济困难的严重精神障碍患者参加基本医疗保险给予资助。医疗保障、财政等部门应当加强协调，简化程序，实现属于基本医疗保险基金支付的医疗费用由医疗机构与医疗保险经办机构直接结算。  
精神障碍患者通过基本医疗保险支付医疗费用后仍有困难，或者不能通过基本医疗保险支付医疗费用的，医疗保障部门应当优先给予医疗救助。

**Article 68** The health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize medical institutions to provide basic public health services free of charge for persons with severe mental disorders.  
The medical expenses of persons with mental disorders shall be paid by the basic medical insurance fund in accordance with the provisions of the State on social insurance. The medical insurance agencies shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, include persons with mental disorders in the coverage of the basic medical insurance for urban employees, the basic medical insurance for urban residents, or the new rural cooperative medical care. The people's governments at the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State, provide subsidies to persons with severe mental disorders from families with financial difficulties to participate in the basic medical insurance. The departments of medical security and finance shall strengthen coordination and simplify procedures to realize the direct settlement between medical institutions and medical insurance agencies of the medical expenses payable by the basic medical insurance fund.  
If persons with mental disorders still have difficulties after their medical expenses are paid by the basic medical insurance, or if their medical expenses cannot be paid by the basic medical insurance, the medical security departments shall give priority to providing medical assistance.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　对符合城乡最低生活保障条件的严重精神障碍患者，民政部门应当会同有关部门及时将其纳入最低生活保障。  
对属于农村五保供养对象的严重精神障碍患者，以及城市中无劳动能力、无生活来源且无法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人，或者其法定赡养、抚养、扶养义务人无赡养、抚养、扶养能力的严重精神障碍患者，民政部门应当按照国家有关规定予以供养、救助。  
前两款规定以外的严重精神障碍患者确有困难的，民政部门可以采取临时救助等措施，帮助其解决生活困难。

**Article 69** For persons with severe mental disorders who meet the conditions for urban and rural subsistence allowances, the civil affairs departments shall, in conjunction with the relevant departments, promptly include them in the subsistence allowance system.  
For persons with severe mental disorders who are entitled to the rural five guarantees support, as well as those in urban areas who have no labor capacity, no source of income, and no legal persons to provide maintenance, upbringing, or support, or whose legal persons to provide maintenance, upbringing, or support are incapable of doing so, the civil affairs departments shall provide support and assistance in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
If persons with severe mental disorders other than those specified in the preceding two paragraphs have genuine difficulties, the civil affairs departments may adopt measures such as temporary assistance to help them solve their living difficulties.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　县级以上地方人民政府及其有关部门应当采取有效措施，保证患有精神障碍的适龄儿童、少年接受义务教育，扶持有劳动能力的精神障碍患者从事力所能及的劳动，并为已经康复的人员提供就业服务。  
国家对安排精神障碍患者就业的用人单位依法给予税收优惠，并在生产、经营、技术、资金、物资、场地等方面给予扶持。

**Article 70** The local people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall take effective measures to ensure that school-age children and adolescents with mental disorders receive compulsory education, support persons with mental disorders who have work ability to engage in work within their capacity, and provide employment services for persons who have recovered.  
The State shall, according to law, grant tax preferences to employers that employ persons with mental disorders and provide support in production, operation, technology, capital, materials, premises, and other aspects.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　精神卫生工作人员的人格尊严、人身安全不受侵犯，精神卫生工作人员依法履行职责受法律保护。全社会应当尊重精神卫生工作人员。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门、医疗机构、康复机构应当采取措施，加强对精神卫生工作人员的职业保护，提高精神卫生工作人员的待遇水平，并按照规定给予适当的津贴。精神卫生工作人员因工致伤、致残、死亡的，其工伤待遇以及抚恤按照国家有关规定执行。

**Article 71** The personal dignity and personal safety of mental health workers shall be inviolable, and the performance of duties by mental health workers according to law shall be protected by law. The whole society shall respect mental health workers.  
The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments, medical institutions, and rehabilitation institutions shall take measures to strengthen the occupational protection of mental health workers, improve the treatment level of mental health workers, and grant appropriate allowances in accordance with relevant provisions. If mental health workers are injured, disabled, or killed due to work, their work-related injury treatment and pension shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门和其他有关部门未依照本法规定履行精神卫生工作职责，或者滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，由本级人民政府或者上一级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予警告、记过或者记大过的处分；造成严重后果的，给予降级、撤职或者开除的处分。

**Article 72** If the health administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level and other relevant departments fail to perform their mental health work duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, or abuse their power, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, the people's government at the same level or the relevant departments of the people's government at the next higher level shall order them to make corrections and circulate a notice of criticism; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions of warning, recording of a demerit, or recording of a major demerit according to law; if serious consequences are caused, they shall be given sanctions of demotion, removal from office, or expulsion.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　不符合本法规定条件的医疗机构擅自从事精神障碍诊断、治疗的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令停止相关诊疗活动，给予警告，并处五千元以上一万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予或者责令给予降低岗位等级或者撤职、开除的处分；对有关医务人员，吊销其执业证书。

**Article 73** If a medical institution that does not meet the conditions prescribed by this Law engages in the diagnosis or treatment of mental disorders without authorization, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to cease the relevant diagnosis and treatment activities, give it a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given or ordered to be given sanctions of demoting their positions or removal from office or expulsion according to law; for the relevant medical personnel, their practice certificates shall be revoked.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　医疗机构及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，给予警告；情节严重的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予或者责令给予降低岗位等级或者撤职、开除的处分，并可以责令有关医务人员暂停一个月以上六个月以下执业活动：  
（一）拒绝对送诊的疑似精神障碍患者作出诊断的；  
（二）对依照[本法第三十条](#art-30)第二款规定实施住院治疗的患者未及时进行检查评估或者未根据评估结果作出处理的。

**Article 74** If a medical institution or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections and give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given or ordered to be given sanctions of demoting their positions or removal from office or expulsion according to law, and may be ordered to suspend the relevant medical personnel's practice activities for not less than one month but not more than six months:  
(1) Refusing to make a diagnosis of a suspected person with mental disorder who is sent for diagnosis;  
(2) Failing to promptly examine and assess a patient who is subject to hospitalization treatment in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 30 of this Law, or failing to make a disposition based on the assessment results.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　医疗机构及其工作人员有下列行为之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予或者责令给予降低岗位等级或者撤职的处分；对有关医务人员，暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动；情节严重的，给予或者责令给予开除的处分，并吊销有关医务人员的执业证书：  
（一）违反本法规定实施约束、隔离等保护性医疗措施的；  
（二）违反本法规定，强迫精神障碍患者劳动的；  
（三）违反本法规定对精神障碍患者实施外科手术或者实验性临床医疗的；  
（四）违反本法规定，侵害精神障碍患者的通讯和会见探访者等权利的；  
（五）违反精神障碍诊断标准，将非精神障碍患者诊断为精神障碍患者的。

**Article 75** If a medical institution or any of its staff members commits any of the following acts, the health administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall order it to make corrections, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons shall be given or ordered to be given sanctions of demoting their positions or removal from office according to law; for the relevant medical personnel, their practice activities shall be suspended for not less than six months but not more than one year; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be given or ordered to be given sanctions of expulsion, and the practice certificates of the relevant medical personnel shall be revoked:  
(1) Violating the provisions of this Law by implementing protective medical measures such as restraint or isolation;  
(2) Violating the provisions of this Law by forcing a person with mental disorder to work;  
(3) Violating the provisions of this Law by performing surgical operations or experimental clinical treatment on a person with mental disorder;  
(4) Violating the provisions of this Law by infringing upon the rights of persons with mental disorders to communication and visitation;  
(5) Violating the diagnostic criteria for mental disorders by diagnosing a person without a mental disorder as having a mental disorder.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　有下列情形之一的，由县级以上人民政府卫生行政部门、工商行政管理部门依据各自职责责令改正，给予警告，并处五千元以上一万元以下罚款，有违法所得的，没收违法所得；造成严重后果的，责令暂停六个月以上一年以下执业活动，直至吊销执业证书或者营业执照：  
（一）心理咨询人员从事心理治疗或者精神障碍的诊断、治疗的；  
（二）从事心理治疗的人员在医疗机构以外开展心理治疗活动的；  
（三）专门从事心理治疗的人员从事精神障碍的诊断的；  
（四）专门从事心理治疗的人员为精神障碍患者开具处方或者提供外科治疗的。  
心理咨询人员、专门从事心理治疗的人员在心理咨询、心理治疗活动中造成他人人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 76** Under any of the following circumstances, the health administrative department or the administrative department for industry and commerce of the people's government at or above the county level shall, according to their respective functions and duties, order rectification, give a warning, and concurrently impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if serious consequences are caused, the relevant personnel shall be ordered to suspend their practice activities for not less than six months but not more than one year, up to revocation of the practice certificate or business license:  
(1) A psychological counselor engages in psychotherapy or diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders;  
(2) A person engaged in psychotherapy carries out psychotherapy activities outside a medical institution;  
(3) A person specialized in psychotherapy engages in the diagnosis of mental disorders;  
(4) A person specialized in psychotherapy prescribes medicines or provides surgical treatment for persons with mental disorders.  
If psychological counselors or persons specialized in psychotherapy cause personal injury, property damage, or other harm to others during psychological counseling or psychotherapy activities, they shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　有关单位和个人违反[本法第四条](#art-4)第三款规定，给精神障碍患者造成损害的，依法承担赔偿责任；对单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，还应当依法给予处分。

**Article 77** If relevant entities or individuals violate the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 4 of this Law and cause harm to a person with mental disorder, they shall bear compensation liability according to law; for the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible persons of the entity, sanctions shall also be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一，给精神障碍患者或者其他公民造成人身、财产或者其他损害的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）将非精神障碍患者故意作为精神障碍患者送入医疗机构治疗的；  
（二）精神障碍患者的监护人遗弃患者，或者有不履行监护职责的其他情形的；  
（三）歧视、侮辱、虐待精神障碍患者，侵害患者的人格尊严、人身安全的；  
（四）非法限制精神障碍患者人身自由的；  
（五）其他侵害精神障碍患者合法权益的情形。

**Article 78** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and, under any of the following circumstances, causes personal injury, property damage, or other harm to a person with mental disorder or other citizens, shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) Intentionally sending a person without a mental disorder as a person with mental disorder into a medical institution for treatment;  
(2) The guardian of a person with mental disorder abandons the person or fails to perform guardianship duties in other circumstances;  
(3) Discriminating against, insulting, or abusing a person with mental disorder, infringing upon the person's personal dignity or personal safety;  
(4) Unlawfully restricting the personal freedom of a person with mental disorder;  
(5) Other circumstances that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of a person with mental disorder.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　医疗机构出具的诊断结论表明精神障碍患者应当住院治疗而其监护人拒绝，致使患者造成他人人身、财产损害的，或者患者有其他造成他人人身、财产损害情形的，其监护人依法承担民事责任。

**Article 79** If the diagnostic conclusion issued by a medical institution indicates that a person with mental disorder shall be hospitalized for treatment but the guardian refuses, causing the patient to cause personal injury or property damage to others, or if the patient causes personal injury or property damage to others under other circumstances, the guardian shall bear civil liability according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　在精神障碍的诊断、治疗、鉴定过程中，寻衅滋事，阻挠有关工作人员依照本法的规定履行职责，扰乱医疗机构、鉴定机构工作秩序的，依法给予治安管理处罚。  
违反本法规定，有其他构成违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚。

**Article 80** If, during the diagnosis, treatment, or expert evaluation of mental disorders, a person engages in provocative activities, obstructs the relevant staff from performing their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law, or disrupts the working order of medical institutions or expert evaluation institutions, he or she shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law.  
If a person violates the provisions of this Law and commits other acts that constitute a violation of public security administration, he or she shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** Whoever violates this Law and commits a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　精神障碍患者或者其监护人、近亲属认为行政机关、医疗机构或者其他有关单位和个人违反本法规定侵害患者合法权益的，可以依法提起诉讼。

**Article 82** Where a person with a mental disorder, his guardian, or his close relative believes that an administrative organ, medical institution, or another relevant entity or individual has violated this Law and infringed upon the legitimate rights and interests of the patient, he may institute legal proceedings according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本法所称精神障碍，是指由各种原因引起的感知、情感和思维等精神活动的紊乱或者异常，导致患者明显的心理痛苦或者社会适应等功能损害。  
本法所称严重精神障碍，是指疾病症状严重，导致患者社会适应等功能严重损害、对自身健康状况或者客观现实不能完整认识，或者不能处理自身事务的精神障碍。  
本法所称精神障碍患者的监护人，是指依照民法通则的有关规定可以担任监护人的人。

**Article 83** For the purpose of this Law, "mental disorder" means a disturbance or abnormality of mental activities such as perception, emotion, and thinking caused by various factors, resulting in obvious psychological distress or impairment of functions such as social adaptation in the patient.  
For the purpose of this Law, "severe mental disorder" means a mental disorder with severe disease symptoms that causes serious impairment of functions such as social adaptation, inability to fully understand one's own health status or objective reality, or inability to manage one's own affairs.  
For the purpose of this Law, "guardian of a person with a mental disorder" means a person who may serve as a guardian in accordance with the relevant provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　军队的精神卫生工作，由国务院和中央军事委员会依据本法制定管理办法。

**Article 84** The mental health work of the armed forces shall be governed by administrative measures formulated by the State Council and the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法自2013年5月1日起施行。

**Article 85** This Law shall come into force as of May 1, 2013.
