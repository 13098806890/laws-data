### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了促进体育事业，弘扬中华体育精神，培育中华体育文化，发展体育运动，增强人民体质，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to promote sports, promote the Chinese sports spirit, cultivate Chinese sports culture, develop physical culture and sport, and improve the people's physical fitness.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　体育工作坚持中国共产党的领导，坚持以人民为中心，以全民健身为基础，普及与提高相结合，推动体育事业均衡、充分发展，推进体育强国和健康中国建设。

**Article 2** Sports work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to a people-centered approach, take national fitness as the foundation, combine popularization and improvement, promote balanced and full development of sports, and advance the building of a sports powerhouse and a healthy China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　县级以上人民政府应当将体育事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划。

**Article 3** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate sports into their plans for national economic and social development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国务院体育行政部门主管全国体育工作。国务院其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内管理相关体育工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门主管本行政区域内的体育工作。县级以上地方人民政府其他有关部门在各自的职责范围内管理相关体育工作。

**Article 4** The sports administrative department of the State Council shall be in charge of national sports work. Other relevant departments of the State Council shall manage relevant sports work within their respective functions and duties.  
The sports administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be in charge of sports work within their respective administrative regions. Other relevant departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall manage relevant sports work within their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国家依法保障公民平等参与体育活动的权利，对未成年人、妇女、老年人、残疾人等参加体育活动的权利给予特别保障。

**Article 5** The State shall, according to law, guarantee citizens' right to participate in sports activities equally, and provide special protection for the right of minors, women, the elderly, and persons with disabilities to participate in sports activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家扩大公益性和基础性公共体育服务供给，推动基本公共体育服务均等化，逐步健全全民覆盖、普惠共享、城乡一体的基本公共体育服务体系。

**Article 6** The State shall expand the supply of public sports services that are public welfare and basic, promote equalization of basic public sports services, and gradually improve the basic public sports service system that covers the entire population, provides universal benefit and sharing, and integrates urban and rural areas.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家采取财政支持、帮助建设体育设施等措施，扶持革命老区、民族地区、边疆地区、经济欠发达地区体育事业的发展。

**Article 7** The State shall adopt measures such as financial support and assistance in the construction of sports facilities to support the development of sports in old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas, border areas, and economically underdeveloped areas.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励、支持优秀民族、民间、民俗传统体育项目的发掘、整理、保护、推广和创新，定期举办少数民族传统体育运动会。

**Article 8** The State shall encourage and support the discovery, collation, protection, promotion, and innovation of outstanding ethnic, folk, and traditional sports events, and regularly hold traditional sports games of ethnic minorities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　开展和参加体育活动，应当遵循依法合规、诚实守信、尊重科学、因地制宜、勤俭节约、保障安全的原则。

**Article 9** In conducting and participating in sports activities, the principles of compliance with the law and regulations, honesty and trustworthiness, respect for science, adaptation to local conditions, thrift, and safety assurance shall be followed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家优先发展青少年和学校体育，坚持体育和教育融合，文化学习和体育锻炼协调，体魄与人格并重，促进青少年全面发展。

**Article 10** The State shall give priority to the development of youth and school sports, adhere to the integration of sports and education, the coordination of cultural learning and physical exercise, and equal emphasis on physical fitness and personality, to promote the all-around development of young people.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家支持体育产业发展，完善体育产业体系，规范体育市场秩序，鼓励扩大体育市场供给，拓宽体育产业投融资渠道，促进体育消费。

**Article 11** The State shall support the development of the sports industry, improve the sports industry system, regulate the order of the sports market, encourage the expansion of sports market supply, broaden investment and financing channels for the sports industry, and promote sports consumption.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持体育科学研究和技术创新，培养体育科技人才，推广应用体育科学技术成果，提高体育科学技术水平。

**Article 12** The State shall support sports scientific research and technological innovation, cultivate sports science and technology talents, promote the application of sports science and technology achievements, and raise the level of sports science and technology.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家对在体育事业发展中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 13** The State shall commend and reward organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions to the development of sports in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　国家鼓励开展对外体育交往，弘扬奥林匹克精神，支持参与国际体育运动。  
对外体育交往坚持独立自主、平等互利、相互尊重的原则，维护国家主权、安全、发展利益和尊严，遵守中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约。

**Article 14** The State shall encourage the conduct of foreign sports exchanges, promote the Olympic spirit, and support participation in international sports.  
Foreign sports exchanges shall adhere to the principles of independence, equality, mutual benefit, and mutual respect, safeguard national sovereignty, security, development interests and dignity, and comply with international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　每年8月8日全民健身日所在周为体育宣传周。

**Article 15** The week in which August 8, the National Fitness Day, falls each year shall be the Sports Promotion Week.

### 第二章　全民健身

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家实施全民健身战略，构建全民健身公共服务体系，鼓励和支持公民参加健身活动，促进全民健身与全民健康深度融合。

**Article 16** The State shall implement the national fitness strategy, build a public service system for national fitness, encourage and support citizens to participate in fitness activities, and promote the deep integration of national fitness with national health.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家倡导公民树立和践行科学健身理念，主动学习健身知识，积极参加健身活动。

**Article 17** The State shall advocate that citizens establish and practice the concept of scientific fitness, take the initiative to learn fitness knowledge, and actively participate in fitness activities.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家推行全民健身计划，制定和实施体育锻炼标准，定期开展公民体质监测和全民健身活动状况调查，开展科学健身指导工作。  
国家建立全民健身工作协调机制。  
县级以上人民政府应当定期组织有关部门对全民健身计划实施情况进行评估，并将评估情况向社会公开。

**Article 18** The State shall implement the national fitness program, formulate and implement physical exercise standards, regularly carry out citizens' physical fitness monitoring and national fitness activity status surveys, and provide scientific fitness guidance.  
The State shall establish a coordination mechanism for national fitness work.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall regularly organize relevant departments to evaluate the implementation of the national fitness program and make the evaluation results public.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　国家实行社会体育指导员制度。社会体育指导员对全民健身活动进行指导。  
社会体育指导员管理办法由国务院体育行政部门规定。

**Article 19** The State shall implement a system for social sports instructors. Social sports instructors shall provide guidance for national fitness activities.  
The measures for the administration of social sports instructors shall be prescribed by the sports administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　地方各级人民政府和有关部门应当为全民健身活动提供必要的条件，支持、保障全民健身活动的开展。

**Article 20** Local people's governments at all levels and relevant departments shall provide necessary conditions for national fitness activities and support and guarantee the development of national fitness activities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　国家机关、企业事业单位和工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会等群团组织应当根据各自特点，组织开展日常体育锻炼和各级各类体育运动会等全民健身活动。

**Article 21** State organs, enterprises, public institutions, and mass organizations such as trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and federations of persons with disabilities shall, according to their respective characteristics, organize and carry out daily physical exercise and national fitness activities such as sports meetings at all levels and of all types.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　居民委员会、村民委员会以及其他社区组织应当结合实际，组织开展全民健身活动。

**Article 22** Residents' committees, villagers' committees, and other community organizations shall, in light of actual conditions, organize and carry out national fitness activities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　全社会应当关心和支持未成年人、妇女、老年人、残疾人参加全民健身活动。各级人民政府应当采取措施，为未成年人、妇女、老年人、残疾人安全参加全民健身活动提供便利和保障。

**Article 23** The entire society shall care for and support minors, women, the elderly, and persons with disabilities in participating in national fitness activities. People's governments at all levels shall take measures to provide convenience and guarantee for minors, women, the elderly, and persons with disabilities to safely participate in national fitness activities.

### 第三章　青少年和学校体育

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家实行青少年和学校体育活动促进计划，健全青少年和学校体育工作制度，培育、增强青少年体育健身意识，推动青少年和学校体育活动的开展和普及，促进青少年身心健康和体魄强健。

**Article 24** The State shall implement a promotion plan for youth and school sports activities, improve the systems for youth and school sports work, cultivate and enhance young people's awareness of sports and fitness, promote the development and popularization of youth and school sports activities, and promote the physical and mental health and physical fitness of young people.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　教育行政部门和学校应当将体育纳入学生综合素质评价范围，将达到国家学生体质健康标准要求作为教育教学考核的重要内容，培养学生体育锻炼习惯，提升学生体育素养。  
体育行政部门应当在传授体育知识技能、组织体育训练、举办体育赛事活动、管理体育场地设施等方面为学校提供指导和帮助，并配合教育行政部门推进学校运动队和高水平运动队建设。

**Article 25** The education administrative departments and schools shall include sports in the scope of comprehensive quality evaluation of students, and take meeting the requirements of the national student physical health standard as an important part of education and teaching assessment, cultivate students' physical exercise habits, and enhance students' sports literacy.  
The sports administrative departments shall provide guidance and assistance to schools in imparting sports knowledge and skills, organizing sports training, holding sports events, and managing sports venues and facilities, and cooperate with the education administrative departments in promoting the building of school sports teams and high-level sports teams.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　学校必须按照国家有关规定开齐开足体育课，确保体育课时不被占用。  
学校应当在体育课教学时，组织病残等特殊体质学生参加适合其特点的体育活动。

**Article 26** Schools must offer sufficient physical education classes in accordance with relevant state regulations and ensure that physical education class hours are not occupied.  
Schools shall, during physical education teaching, organize students with special physical conditions such as illness or disability to participate in sports activities suitable for their characteristics.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　学校应当将在校内开展的学生课外体育活动纳入教学计划，与体育课教学内容相衔接，保障学生在校期间每天参加不少于一小时体育锻炼。  
鼓励学校组建运动队、俱乐部等体育训练组织，开展多种形式的课余体育训练，有条件的可组建高水平运动队，培养竞技体育后备人才。

**Article 27** Schools shall incorporate extracurricular sports activities carried out on campus into the teaching plan, connect them with the content of physical education teaching, and ensure that students participate in no less than one hour of physical exercise every day during school hours.  
Schools are encouraged to set up sports teams, clubs, and other sports training organizations, conduct various forms of after-school sports training, and may set up high-level sports teams where conditions permit, to cultivate reserve talents for competitive sports.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家定期举办全国学生（青年）运动会。地方各级人民政府应当结合实际，定期组织本地区学生（青年）运动会。  
学校应当每学年至少举办一次全校性的体育运动会。  
鼓励公共体育场地设施免费向学校开放使用，为学校举办体育运动会提供服务保障。  
鼓励学校开展多种形式的学生体育交流活动。

**Article 28** The State shall regularly hold National Student (Youth) Games. Local people's governments at all levels shall, in light of actual conditions, regularly organize local student (youth) games.  
Schools shall hold at least one school-wide sports meeting each academic year.  
Public sports venues and facilities are encouraged to be opened to schools free of charge, providing service guarantee for schools to hold sports meetings.  
Schools are encouraged to carry out various forms of student sports exchange activities.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家将体育科目纳入初中、高中学业水平考试范围，建立符合学科特点的考核机制。  
病残等特殊体质学生的体育科目考核，应当充分考虑其身体状况。

**Article 29** The State shall include the subject of physical education in the scope of the academic level examination for junior and senior high schools, and establish an assessment mechanism that conforms to the characteristics of the subject.  
For students with special physical conditions such as illness or disability, their physical condition shall be fully taken into account in the assessment of the physical education subject.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　学校应当建立学生体质健康检查制度。教育、体育和卫生健康行政部门应当加强对学生体质的监测和评估。

**Article 30** Schools shall establish a system for physical health examination of students. The education, sports, and health administrative departments shall strengthen monitoring and evaluation of students' physical fitness.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　学校应当按照国家有关规定，配足合格的体育教师，保障体育教师享受与其他学科教师同等待遇。  
学校可以设立体育教练员岗位。  
学校优先聘用符合相关条件的优秀退役运动员从事学校体育教学、训练活动。

**Article 31** Schools shall, in accordance with relevant state regulations, deploy sufficient qualified physical education teachers and ensure that physical education teachers enjoy equal treatment with teachers of other subjects.  
Schools may set up positions for sports coaches.  
Schools shall give priority to employing outstanding retired athletes who meet relevant conditions to engage in school sports teaching and training activities.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　学校应当按照国家有关标准配置体育场地、设施和器材，并定期进行检查、维护，适时予以更新。  
学校体育场地必须保障体育活动需要，不得随意占用或者挪作他用。

**Article 32** Schools shall, in accordance with relevant state standards, allocate sports venues, facilities, and equipment, and regularly inspect, maintain, and update them in due course.  
School sports venues must ensure the needs of sports activities and shall not be occupied or used for other purposes without authorization.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家建立健全学生体育活动意外伤害保险机制。  
教育行政部门和学校应当做好学校体育活动安全管理和运动伤害风险防控。

**Article 33** The State shall establish and improve the insurance mechanism for accidental injuries in student sports activities.  
Education administrative departments and schools shall do a good job of safety management of school sports activities and risk prevention and control of sports injuries.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　幼儿园应当为学前儿童提供适宜的室内外活动场地和体育设施、器材，开展符合学前儿童特点的体育活动。

**Article 34** Kindergartens shall provide suitable indoor and outdoor activity venues and sports facilities and equipment for preschool children, and carry out sports activities that meet the characteristics of preschool children.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各级教育督导机构应当对学校体育实施督导，并向社会公布督导报告。

**Article 35** Education supervision institutions at all levels shall supervise school sports and make the supervision report public.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　教育行政部门、体育行政部门和学校应当组织、引导青少年参加体育活动，预防和控制青少年近视、肥胖等不良健康状况，家庭应当予以配合。

**Article 36** The education administrative departments, sports administrative departments, and schools shall organize and guide young people to participate in sports activities, prevent and control adverse health conditions such as myopia and obesity among young people, and families shall cooperate.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　体育行政部门会同有关部门引导和规范企业事业单位、社会组织和体育专业人员等为青少年提供体育培训等服务。

**Article 37** The sports administrative departments shall, together with relevant departments, guide and regulate enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and sports professionals, among others, in providing sports training and other services for young people.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　各级各类体育运动学校应当对适龄学生依法实施义务教育，并根据国务院体育行政部门制定的教学训练大纲开展业余体育训练。  
教育行政部门应当将体育运动学校的文化教育纳入管理范围。  
各级人民政府应当在场地、设施、资金、人员等方面对体育运动学校予以支持。

**Article 38** Sports schools at all levels and of all types shall implement compulsory education for school-age students according to law, and carry out amateur sports training in accordance with the teaching and training syllabus formulated by the sports administrative department of the State Council.  
The education administrative departments shall include the cultural education of sports schools in the scope of their management.  
People's governments at all levels shall provide support to sports schools in terms of venues, facilities, funds, personnel, etc.

### 第四章　竞技体育

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家促进竞技体育发展，鼓励运动员提高竞技水平，在体育赛事中创造优异成绩，为国家和人民争取荣誉。

**Article 39** The State shall promote the development of competitive sports, encourage athletes to improve their competitive level, achieve excellent results in sports events, and win honors for the country and the people.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　国家促进和规范职业体育市场化、职业化发展，提高职业体育赛事能力和竞技水平。

**Article 40** The State shall promote and regulate the marketization and professionalization of professional sports, and enhance the capacity and competitive level of professional sports events.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家加强体育运动学校和体育传统特色学校建设，鼓励、支持开展业余体育训练，培养优秀的竞技体育后备人才。

**Article 41** The State shall strengthen the building of sports schools and schools with traditional sports characteristics, encourage and support the conduct of amateur sports training, and cultivate excellent reserve talents for competitive sports.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家加强对运动员的培养和管理，对运动员进行爱国主义、集体主义和社会主义教育，以及道德、纪律和法治教育。  
运动员应当积极参加训练和竞赛，团结协作，勇于奉献，顽强拼搏，不断提高竞技水平。

**Article 42** The State shall strengthen the training and management of athletes, and conduct education on patriotism, collectivism, and socialism, as well as education on morality, discipline, and the rule of law for athletes.  
Athletes shall actively participate in training and competition, unite and cooperate, be brave in dedication, tenaciously strive, and continuously improve their competitive level.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　国家加强体育训练科学技术研究、开发和应用，对运动员实行科学、文明的训练，维护运动员身心健康。

**Article 43** The State shall strengthen the research, development, and application of science and technology for sports training, implement scientific and civilized training for athletes, and safeguard the physical and mental health of athletes.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　国家依法保障运动员接受文化教育的权利。  
体育行政部门、教育行政部门应当保障处于义务教育阶段的运动员完成义务教育。

**Article 44** The State shall protect the right of athletes to receive cultural education according to law.  
The sports administrative departments and education administrative departments shall ensure that athletes at the stage of compulsory education complete compulsory education.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家依法保障运动员选择注册与交流的权利。  
运动员可以参加单项体育协会的注册，并按照有关规定进行交流。

**Article 45** The State shall protect the right of athletes to choose registration and exchange according to law.  
Athletes may register with individual sports associations and carry out exchanges in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　国家对优秀运动员在就业和升学方面给予优待。

**Article 46** The State shall grant preferential treatment to outstanding athletes in employment and further education.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　各级人民政府加强对退役运动员的职业技能培训和社会保障，为退役运动员就业、创业提供指导和服务。

**Article 47** People's governments at all levels shall strengthen vocational skill training and social security for retired athletes, and provide guidance and services for retired athletes in employment and entrepreneurship.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　国家实行体育运动水平等级、教练员职称等级和裁判员技术等级制度。

**Article 48** The State shall implement a system of grades for sports skill levels, coach professional titles, and referee technical levels.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　代表国家和地方参加国际、国内重大体育赛事的运动员和运动队，应当按照公开、公平、择优的原则选拔和组建。  
运动员选拔和运动队组建办法由国务院体育行政部门规定。

**Article 49** Athletes and sports teams that represent the State or local authorities in major international or domestic sports events shall be selected and formed in accordance with the principles of openness, fairness, and merit selection.  
The measures for the selection of athletes and the formation of sports teams shall be prescribed by the sports administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家对体育赛事活动实行分级分类管理，具体办法由国务院体育行政部门规定。

**Article 50** The State shall implement tiered and classified management of sports events, and the specific measures shall be prescribed by the sports administrative department of the State Council.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　体育赛事实行公平竞争的原则。  
体育赛事活动组织者和运动员、教练员、裁判员应当遵守体育道德和体育赛事规则，不得弄虚作假、营私舞弊。  
严禁任何组织和个人利用体育赛事从事赌博活动。

**Article 51** Sports events shall follow the principle of fair competition.  
Organizers of sports events and athletes, coaches, and referees shall abide by sports ethics and sports event rules, and shall not engage in fraud or malpractice.  
No organization or individual may use sports events to engage in gambling activities.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　在中国境内举办的体育赛事，其名称、徽记、旗帜及吉祥物等标志按照国家有关规定予以保护。  
未经体育赛事活动组织者等相关权利人许可，不得以营利为目的采集或者传播体育赛事活动现场图片、音视频等信息。

**Article 52** For sports events held within the territory of China, the names, emblems, flags, mascots and other symbols thereof shall be protected in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
Without the permission of the sports event organizer or other relevant right holders, no person may collect or disseminate images, audio, video or other information live at the sports event for profit-making purposes.

### 第五章　反兴奋剂

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家提倡健康文明、公平竞争的体育运动，禁止在体育运动中使用兴奋剂。  
任何组织和个人不得组织、强迫、欺骗、教唆、引诱体育运动参加者在体育运动中使用兴奋剂，不得向体育运动参加者提供或者变相提供兴奋剂。

**Article 53** The State advocates healthy, civilized and fair sports, and prohibits the use of doping in sports.  
No organization or individual may organize, coerce, deceive, abet or induce sports participants to use doping in sports, nor may they provide or indirectly provide doping to sports participants.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家建立健全反兴奋剂制度。  
县级以上人民政府体育行政部门会同卫生健康、教育、公安、工信、商务、药品监管、交通运输、海关、农业、市场监管等部门，对兴奋剂问题实施综合治理。

**Article 54** The State shall establish and improve the anti-doping system.  
The sports administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, together with the departments of health, education, public security, industry and information technology, commerce, drug regulation, transport, customs, agriculture, market regulation and other departments, carry out comprehensive governance on doping issues.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国务院体育行政部门负责制定反兴奋剂规范。

**Article 55** The sports administrative department of the State Council shall be responsible for formulating anti-doping norms.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国务院体育行政部门会同国务院药品监管、卫生健康、商务、海关等部门制定、公布兴奋剂目录，并动态调整。

**Article 56** The sports administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the drug regulatory, health, commerce, customs and other departments of the State Council, formulate and publish a doping list, and make dynamic adjustments thereto.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　国家设立反兴奋剂机构。反兴奋剂机构及其检查人员依照法定程序开展检查，有关单位和人员应当予以配合，任何单位和个人不得干涉。  
反兴奋剂机构依法公开反兴奋剂信息，并接受社会监督。

**Article 57** The State shall establish an anti-doping agency. The anti-doping agency and its inspection staff shall carry out inspections in accordance with legal procedures; the relevant units and individuals shall cooperate, and no unit or individual may interfere.  
The anti-doping agency shall disclose anti-doping information according to law and accept social supervision.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上人民政府体育行政部门组织开展反兴奋剂宣传、教育工作，提高体育活动参与者和公众的反兴奋剂意识。

**Article 58** The sports administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall organize and carry out anti-doping publicity and education to raise the anti-doping awareness of sports activity participants and the public.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家鼓励开展反兴奋剂科学技术研究，推广先进的反兴奋剂技术、设备和方法。

**Article 59** The State encourages scientific and technological research on anti-doping and the promotion of advanced anti-doping technologies, equipment and methods.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　国家根据缔结或者参加的有关国际条约，开展反兴奋剂国际合作，履行反兴奋剂国际义务。

**Article 60** The State shall, in accordance with the relevant international treaties it has concluded or acceded to, carry out international cooperation on anti-doping and fulfill international anti-doping obligations.

### 第六章　体育组织

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　国家鼓励、支持体育组织依照法律法规和章程开展体育活动，推动体育事业发展。  
国家鼓励体育组织积极参加国际体育交流合作，参与国际体育运动规则的制定。

**Article 61** The State encourages and supports sports organizations to carry out sports activities in accordance with laws, regulations and their articles of association, and to promote the development of sports.  
The State encourages sports organizations to actively participate in international sports exchanges and cooperation, and to participate in the formulation of international sports rules.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　中华全国体育总会和地方各级体育总会是团结各类体育组织和体育工作者、体育爱好者的群众性体育组织，应当在发展体育事业中发挥作用。

**Article 62** The All-China Sports Federation and the local sports federations at various levels are mass sports organizations that unite various types of sports organizations, sports workers and sports enthusiasts, and shall play a role in the development of sports.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　中国奥林匹克委员会是以发展体育和推动奥林匹克运动为主要任务的体育组织，代表中国参与国际奥林匹克事务。

**Article 63** The Chinese Olympic Committee is a sports organization whose main tasks are to develop sports and promote the Olympic movement, and it represents China in participating in international Olympic affairs.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　体育科学社会团体是体育科学技术工作者的学术性体育社会组织，应当在发展体育科技事业中发挥作用。

**Article 64** Sports science societies are academic sports social organizations of sports science and technology workers, and shall play a role in the development of sports science and technology.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　全国性单项体育协会是依法登记的体育社会组织，代表中国参加相应的国际单项体育组织，根据章程加入中华全国体育总会、派代表担任中国奥林匹克委员会委员。  
全国性单项体育协会负责相应项目的普及与提高，制定相应项目技术规范、竞赛规则、团体标准，规范体育赛事活动。

**Article 65** A national individual sport association is a sports social organization registered according to law, representing China in participating in the corresponding international individual sport organization, and shall, in accordance with its articles of association, join the All-China Sports Federation and send representatives to serve as members of the Chinese Olympic Committee.  
A national individual sport association shall be responsible for the popularization and improvement of the corresponding sport, formulate technical specifications, competition rules and group standards for the corresponding sport, and regulate sports events and activities.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　单项体育协会应当依法维护会员的合法权益，积极向有关单位反映会员的意见和建议。

**Article 66** Individual sport associations shall, according to law, safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of their members, and actively reflect the opinions and suggestions of their members to the relevant units.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　单项体育协会应当接受体育行政部门的指导和监管，健全内部治理机制，制定行业规则，加强行业自律。

**Article 67** Individual sport associations shall accept the guidance and supervision of sports administrative departments, improve their internal governance mechanisms, formulate industry rules, and strengthen industry self-discipline.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　国家鼓励发展青少年体育俱乐部、社区健身组织等各类自治性体育组织。

**Article 68** The State encourages the development of various types of autonomous sports organizations, such as youth sports clubs and community fitness organizations.

### 第七章　体育产业

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　国家制定体育产业发展规划，扩大体育产业规模，增强体育产业活力，促进体育产业高质量发展，满足人民群众多样化体育需求。  
县级以上人民政府应当建立政府多部门合作的体育产业发展工作协调机制。

**Article 69** The State shall formulate plans for the development of the sports industry, expand the scale of the sports industry, enhance the vitality of the sports industry, promote high-quality development of the sports industry, and meet the diverse sports needs of the people.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall establish a coordination mechanism for sports industry development work that involves multi-departmental cooperation.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　国家支持和规范发展体育用品制造、体育服务等体育产业，促进体育与健康、文化、旅游、养老、科技等融合发展。

**Article 70** The State supports and regulates the development of sports industries such as sports goods manufacturing and sports services, and promotes the integrated development of sports with health, culture, tourism, elderly care, science and technology, among others.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　国家支持体育用品制造业创新发展，鼓励企业加大研发投入，采用新技术、新工艺、新材料，促进体育用品制造业转型升级。  
国家培育健身休闲、竞赛表演、场馆服务、体育经纪、体育培训等服务业态，提高体育服务业水平和质量。  
符合条件的体育产业，依法享受财政、税收、土地等优惠政策。

**Article 71** The State supports the innovative development of the sports goods manufacturing industry, encourages enterprises to increase investment in research and development, adopt new technologies, new processes and new materials, and promotes the transformation and upgrading of the sports goods manufacturing industry.  
The State cultivates service formats such as fitness and leisure, competition performance, venue services, sports agency, and sports training, and improves the level and quality of the sports service industry.  
Qualifying sports industries shall enjoy preferential policies on finance, taxation, land, etc. according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　国家完善职业体育发展体系，拓展职业体育发展渠道，支持运动员、教练员职业化发展，提高职业体育的成熟度和规范化水平。  
职业体育俱乐部应当健全内部治理机制，完善法人治理结构，充分发挥其市场主体作用。

**Article 72** The State shall improve the development system for professional sports, expand the channels for professional sports development, support the professional development of athletes and coaches, and enhance the maturity and standardization level of professional sports.  
Professional sports clubs shall improve their internal governance mechanisms, perfect their corporate governance structures, and give full play to their role as market entities.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　国家建立健全区域体育产业协调互动机制，推动区域间体育产业资源交流共享，促进区域体育协调发展。  
国家支持地方发挥资源优势，发展具有区域特色、民族特色的体育产业。

**Article 73** The State shall establish and improve a regional sports industry coordination and interaction mechanism, promote the exchange and sharing of sports industry resources among regions, and facilitate the coordinated development of regional sports.  
The State supports localities in leveraging their resource advantages to develop sports industries with regional and ethnic characteristics.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　国家鼓励社会资本投入体育产业，建设体育设施，开发体育产品，提供体育服务。

**Article 74** The State encourages social capital to invest in the sports industry, construct sports facilities, develop sports products, and provide sports services.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　国家鼓励有条件的高等学校设置体育产业相关专业，开展校企合作，加强职业教育和培训，培养体育产业专业人才，形成有效支撑体育产业发展的人才队伍。

**Article 75** The State encourages qualified institutions of higher learning to establish majors related to the sports industry, carry out school-enterprise cooperation, strengthen vocational education and training, cultivate professional talents in the sports industry, and build a talent team that effectively supports the development of the sports industry.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　国家完善体育产业统计体系，开展体育产业统计监测，定期发布体育产业数据。

**Article 76** The State shall improve the sports industry statistical system, carry out statistical monitoring of the sports industry, and regularly publish sports industry data.

### 第八章　保障条件

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　县级以上人民政府应当将体育事业经费列入本级预算，建立与国民经济和社会发展相适应的投入机制。

**Article 77** People's governments at or above the county level shall include sports undertakings funds in their own budgets and establish an investment mechanism that is commensurate with national economic and social development.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　国家鼓励社会力量发展体育事业，鼓励对体育事业的捐赠和赞助，保障参与主体的合法权益。  
通过捐赠财产等方式支持体育事业发展的，依法享受税收优惠等政策。

**Article 78** The State encourages social forces to develop sports undertakings, encourages donations and sponsorships to sports undertakings, and protects the legitimate rights and interests of participating entities.  
Those who support the development of sports undertakings through donation of property or other means shall enjoy tax incentives and other policies according to law.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　国家有关部门应当加强对体育资金的管理，任何单位和个人不得侵占、挪用、截留、克扣、私分体育资金。

**Article 79** The relevant departments of the State shall strengthen the management of sports funds; no unit or individual may embezzle, misappropriate, withhold, dock, or privately divide sports funds.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　国家支持通过政府购买服务的方式提供公共体育服务，提高公共体育服务水平。

**Article 80** The State supports the provision of public sports services through government procurement of services, so as to improve the level of public sports services.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　县级以上地方人民政府应当按照国家有关规定，根据本行政区域经济社会发展水平、人口结构、环境条件以及体育事业发展需要，统筹兼顾，优化配置各级各类体育场地设施，优先保障全民健身体育场地设施的建设和配置。

**Article 81** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State and based on the level of economic and social development, population structure, environmental conditions and the needs of sports development in their respective administrative regions, take overall consideration and optimize the allocation of sports venues and facilities at various levels and of various types, and give priority to guaranteeing the construction and allocation of sports venues and facilities for nationwide fitness.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　县级以上地方人民政府应当将本行政区域内公共体育场地设施的建设纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、国土空间规划，未经法定程序不得变更。  
公共体育场地设施的规划设计和竣工验收，应当征求本级人民政府体育行政部门意见。  
公共体育场地设施的设计和建设，应当符合国家无障碍环境建设要求，有效满足老年人、残疾人等特定群体的无障碍需求。

**Article 82** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of public sports venues and facilities within their administrative regions into the national economic and social development plan and the territorial space plan, and shall not alter them without legal procedures.  
The planning, design and completion acceptance of public sports venues and facilities shall seek the opinions of the sports administrative department of the people's government at the same level.  
The design and construction of public sports venues and facilities shall meet the national requirements for barrier-free environment construction and effectively satisfy the barrier-free needs of specific groups such as the elderly and persons with disabilities.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　新建、改建、扩建居住社区，应当按照国家有关规定，同步规划、设计、建设用于居民日常健身的配套体育场地设施。

**Article 83** For the construction, renovation and expansion of residential communities, the supporting sports venues and facilities for the daily fitness of residents shall be planned, designed and constructed simultaneously in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　公共体育场地设施管理单位应当公开向社会开放的办法，并对未成年人、老年人、残疾人等实行优惠。  
免费和低收费开放的体育场地设施，按照有关规定享受补助。

**Article 84** The management units of public sports venues and facilities shall make public the methods for opening to the public, and grant preferential treatment to minors, the elderly, persons with disabilities, etc.  
Sports venues and facilities that are opened free of charge or at low fees shall enjoy subsidies in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　国家推进体育公园建设，鼓励地方因地制宜发展特色体育公园，推动体育公园免费开放，满足公民体育健身需求。

**Article 85** The State promotes the construction of sports parks, encourages localities to develop distinctive sports parks according to local conditions, and promotes the free opening of sports parks to meet citizens' needs for sports and fitness.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　国家鼓励充分、合理利用旧厂房、仓库、老旧商业设施等闲置资源建设用于公民日常健身的体育场地设施，鼓励和支持机关、学校、企业事业单位的体育场地设施向公众开放。

**Article 86** The State encourages the full and reasonable use of idle resources such as old factories, warehouses and old commercial facilities to construct sports venues and facilities for the daily fitness of citizens, and encourages and supports the opening of sports venues and facilities of state organs, schools, enterprises and institutions to the public.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　任何单位和个人不得侵占公共体育场地设施及其建设用地，不得擅自拆除公共体育场地设施，不得擅自改变公共体育场地设施的功能、用途或者妨碍其正常使用。  
因特殊需要临时占用公共体育场地设施超过十日的，应当经本级人民政府体育行政部门同意；超过三个月的，应当报上一级人民政府体育行政部门批准。  
经批准拆除公共体育场地设施或者改变其功能、用途的，应当依照国家有关法律、行政法规的规定先行择地重建。

**Article 87** No unit or individual may occupy public sports venues and facilities or the land designated for their construction, may not dismantle public sports venues and facilities without authorization, may not change the functions or uses of public sports venues and facilities without authorization, or obstruct their normal use.  
If temporary occupation of public sports venues and facilities for more than ten days is necessary due to special needs, the consent of the sports administrative department of the people's government at the same level shall be obtained; if the occupation exceeds three months, approval from the sports administrative department of the people's government at the next higher level shall be obtained.  
If the dismantling of public sports venues and facilities or the change of their functions or uses is approved, reconstruction on a selected site shall be carried out in advance in accordance with the provisions of the relevant laws and administrative regulations of the State.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立全民健身公共场地设施的维护管理机制，明确管理和维护责任。

**Article 88** Local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a maintenance and management mechanism for public venues and facilities for nationwide fitness, and clearly define the responsibilities for management and maintenance.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　国家发展体育专业教育，鼓励有条件的高等学校培养教练员、裁判员、体育教师等各类体育专业人才，鼓励社会力量依法开展体育专业教育。

**Article 89** The State develops professional sports education, encourages qualified institutions of higher learning to cultivate various types of sports professionals such as coaches, referees and physical education teachers, and encourages social forces to carry out professional sports education according to law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　国家鼓励建立健全运动员伤残保险、体育意外伤害保险和场所责任保险制度。  
大型体育赛事活动组织者应当和参与者协商投保体育意外伤害保险。  
高危险性体育赛事活动组织者应当投保体育意外伤害保险。  
高危险性体育项目经营者应当投保体育意外伤害保险和场所责任保险。

**Article 90** The State encourages the establishment and improvement of an insurance system for athletes' disability insurance, sports accident insurance and venue liability insurance.  
Organizers of large-scale sports events shall negotiate with participants to take out sports accident insurance.  
Organizers of high-risk sports events shall take out sports accident insurance.  
Operators of high-risk sports events shall take out sports accident insurance and venue liability insurance.

### 第九章　体育仲裁

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　国家建立体育仲裁制度，及时、公正解决体育纠纷，保护当事人的合法权益。  
体育仲裁依法独立进行，不受行政机关、社会组织和个人的干涉。

**Article 91** The State shall establish a sports arbitration system to resolve sports disputes in a timely and fair manner and protect the legitimate rights and interests of the parties.  
Sports arbitration shall be conducted independently according to law, free from interference by administrative organs, social organizations or individuals.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　当事人可以根据仲裁协议、体育组织章程、体育赛事规则等，对下列纠纷申请体育仲裁：  
（一）对体育社会组织、运动员管理单位、体育赛事活动组织者按照兴奋剂管理或者其他管理规定作出的取消参赛资格、取消比赛成绩、禁赛等处理决定不服发生的纠纷；  
（二）因运动员注册、交流发生的纠纷；  
（三）在竞技体育活动中发生的其他纠纷。  
《中华人民共和国仲裁法》规定的可仲裁纠纷和《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》规定的劳动争议，不属于体育仲裁范围。

**Article 92** A party may, in accordance with an arbitration agreement, the articles of association of a sports organization, the rules of a sports event, etc., apply for sports arbitration concerning the following disputes:  
(1) disputes arising from refusal to accept a decision made by a sports social organization, an athlete management unit or a sports event organizer in accordance with doping management or other management provisions, such as disqualification from participation, disqualification of results, or suspension;  
(2) disputes arising from the registration or transfer of athletes;  
(3) other disputes arising in competitive sports activities.  
Disputes that may be arbitrated under the Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China and labor disputes under the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law of the People's Republic of China are not within the scope of sports arbitration.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　国务院体育行政部门依照本法组织设立体育仲裁委员会，制定体育仲裁规则。  
体育仲裁委员会由体育行政部门代表、体育社会组织代表、运动员代表、教练员代表、裁判员代表以及体育、法律专家组成，其组成人数应当是单数。  
体育仲裁委员会应当设仲裁员名册。仲裁员具体条件由体育仲裁规则规定。

**Article 93** The sports administrative department of the State Council shall, in accordance with this Law, organize the establishment of a sports arbitration commission and formulate sports arbitration rules.  
The sports arbitration commission shall be composed of representatives of sports administrative departments, representatives of sports social organizations, representatives of athletes, representatives of coaches, representatives of referees, and experts in sports and law, and the number of members thereof shall be an odd number.  
The sports arbitration commission shall maintain a roster of arbitrators. The specific qualifications for arbitrators shall be stipulated in the sports arbitration rules.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　体育仲裁委员会裁决体育纠纷实行仲裁庭制。仲裁庭组成人数应当是单数，具体组成办法由体育仲裁规则规定。

**Article 94** The sports arbitration commission shall adjudicate sports disputes through an arbitral tribunal system. The number of members of an arbitral tribunal shall be an odd number, and the specific composition method shall be stipulated in the sports arbitration rules.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　鼓励体育组织建立内部纠纷解决机制，公平、公正、高效地解决纠纷。  
体育组织没有内部纠纷解决机制或者内部纠纷解决机制未及时处理纠纷的，当事人可以申请体育仲裁。

**Article 95** Sports organizations are encouraged to establish internal dispute resolution mechanisms to resolve disputes in a fair, just and efficient manner.  
If a sports organization has no internal dispute resolution mechanism or the internal dispute resolution mechanism fails to handle the dispute in a timely manner, the parties may apply for sports arbitration.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　对体育社会组织、运动员管理单位、体育赛事活动组织者的处理决定或者内部纠纷解决机制处理结果不服的，当事人自收到处理决定或者纠纷处理结果之日起二十一日内申请体育仲裁。

**Article 96** If a party refuses to accept a decision made by a sports social organization, an athlete management unit or a sports event organizer, or the result of an internal dispute resolution mechanism, the party shall apply for sports arbitration within twenty-one days from the date of receipt of the decision or the result of dispute resolution.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条　体育仲裁裁决书自作出之日起发生法律效力。  
裁决作出后，当事人就同一纠纷再申请体育仲裁或者向人民法院起诉的，体育仲裁委员会或者人民法院不予受理。

**Article 97** An arbitral award in sports arbitration shall have legal effect from the date it is made.  
After the award is made, if a party applies for sports arbitration again for the same dispute or institutes a prosecution in a People's Court, the sports arbitration commission or the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条　有下列情形之一的，当事人可以自收到仲裁裁决书之日起三十日内向体育仲裁委员会所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销裁决：  
（一）适用法律、法规确有错误的；  
（二）裁决的事项不属于体育仲裁受理范围的；  
（三）仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁的程序违反有关规定，足以影响公正裁决的；  
（四）裁决所根据的证据是伪造的；  
（五）对方当事人隐瞒了足以影响公正裁决的证据的；  
（六）仲裁员在仲裁该案时有索贿受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁决行为的。  
人民法院经组成合议庭审查核实裁决有前款规定情形之一的，或者认定裁决违背社会公共利益的，应当裁定撤销。  
人民法院受理撤销裁决的申请后，认为可以由仲裁庭重新仲裁的，通知仲裁庭在一定期限内重新仲裁，并裁定中止撤销程序。仲裁庭拒绝重新仲裁的，人民法院应当裁定恢复撤销程序。

**Article 98** Under any of the following circumstances, a party may, within thirty days from the date of receipt of the arbitral award, apply to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the sports arbitration commission is located for setting aside the award:  
(1) There is indeed an error in the application of laws or regulations;  
(2) The matters adjudicated are not within the scope of sports arbitration;  
(3) The composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitration procedure violates the relevant provisions and is sufficient to affect the fairness of the award;  
(4) The evidence on which the award is based is forged;  
(5) The opposing party has concealed evidence sufficient to affect the fairness of the award;  
(6) The arbitrator has committed acts of soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, or perverting the law in arbitrating the case.  
If the People's Court, after forming a collegial panel to examine and verify the award, finds that it falls under any of the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph, or determines that the award violates the public interest, it shall order that the award be set aside.  
After accepting an application for setting aside an award, if the People's Court considers that the arbitral tribunal may re-arbitrate, it shall notify the arbitral tribunal to re-arbitrate within a certain time limit and order the suspension of the setting-aside proceedings. If the arbitral tribunal refuses to re-arbitrate, the People's Court shall order the resumption of the setting-aside proceedings.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条　当事人应当履行体育仲裁裁决。一方当事人不履行的，另一方当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定向人民法院申请执行。

**Article 99** The parties shall comply with the arbitral award of sports arbitration. If one party fails to comply, the other party may apply to the People's Court for enforcement in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条　需要即时处理的体育赛事活动纠纷，适用体育仲裁特别程序。  
特别程序由体育仲裁规则规定。

**Article 100** For disputes over sports events that require immediate handling, the special procedures of sports arbitration shall apply.  
The special procedures shall be stipulated in the sports arbitration rules.

### 第十章　监督管理

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条　县级以上人民政府体育行政部门和有关部门应当积极履行监督检查职责，发现违反本法规定行为的，应当及时做出处理。对不属于本部门主管事项的，应当及时书面通知并移交相关部门查处。

**Article 101** The sport administrative departments and relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall actively perform their supervision and inspection duties, and upon discovering any act in violation of the provisions of this Law, shall handle it in a timely manner. Where the matter is not within the purview of the department, it shall promptly notify the relevant department in writing and transfer the matter for investigation and handling.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条　县级以上人民政府体育行政部门对体育赛事活动依法进行监管，对赛事活动场地实施现场检查，查阅、复制有关合同、票据、账簿，检查赛事活动组织方案、安全应急预案等材料。  
县级以上人民政府公安、市场监管、应急管理等部门按照各自职责对体育赛事活动进行监督管理。  
体育赛事活动组织者应当履行安全保障义务，提供符合要求的安全条件，制定风险防范及应急处置预案等保障措施，维护体育赛事活动的安全。  
体育赛事活动因发生极端天气、自然灾害、公共卫生事件等突发事件，不具备办赛条件的，体育赛事活动组织者应当及时予以中止；未中止的，县级以上人民政府应当责令其中止。

**Article 102** The sport administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall supervise and regulate sports events and activities according to law, conduct on-site inspections of the venues for such events and activities, examine and copy relevant contracts, invoices, and account books, and inspect materials such as the event organization plan and the safety emergency response plan.  
The public security, market regulation, emergency management, and other relevant departments of a people's government at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions, supervise and regulate sports events and activities.  
The organizer of a sports event or activity shall fulfill its safety assurance obligations, provide safety conditions that meet the requirements, and formulate safeguard measures such as risk prevention and emergency response plans to maintain the safety of sports events and activities.  
Where a sports event or activity cannot be held due to the occurrence of extreme weather, natural disasters, public health incidents, or other emergencies, the organizer of the sports event or activity shall suspend it in a timely manner; if it fails to do so, the people's government at or above the county level shall order its suspension.

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条　县级以上人民政府市场监管、体育行政等部门按照各自职责对体育市场进行监督管理。

**Article 103** The market regulation, sport administrative, and other relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and regulate the sports market within their respective functions.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条　国家建立体育项目管理制度，新设体育项目由国务院体育行政部门认定。  
体育项目目录每四年公布一次。

**Article 104** The State shall establish a management system for sports events. Newly established sports events shall be recognized by the sport administrative department of the State Council.  
The catalogue of sports events shall be published once every four years.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条　经营高危险性体育项目，应当符合下列条件，并向县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门提出申请：  
（一）相关体育设施符合国家标准；  
（二）具有达到规定数量的取得相应国家职业资格证书或者职业技能等级证书的社会体育指导人员和救助人员；  
（三）具有相应的安全保障、应急救援制度和措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门应当自收到申请之日起三十日内进行实地核查，并作出批准或者不予批准的决定。予以批准的，应当发给许可证；不予批准的，应当书面通知申请人并说明理由。  
国务院体育行政部门会同有关部门制定、调整高危险性体育项目目录并予以公布。

**Article 105** The operation of high-risk sports events shall meet the following conditions, and an application shall be submitted to the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) The relevant sports facilities comply with national standards;  
(2) There is a sufficient number of social sports instructors and rescue personnel who have obtained the corresponding national occupational qualification certificates or vocational skill level certificates;  
(3) Corresponding safety assurance and emergency rescue systems and measures are in place.  
The sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within thirty days from the date of receiving the application, conduct an on-site inspection and make a decision to approve or disapprove the application. If it approves, it shall issue a permit; if it disapproves, it shall notify the applicant in writing and provide the reasons.  
The sport administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate and adjust the catalogue of high-risk sports events and publish it.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条　举办高危险性体育赛事活动，应当符合下列条件，并向县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门提出申请：  
（一）配备具有相应资格或者资质的专业技术人员；  
（二）配置符合相关标准和要求的场地、器材和设施；  
（三）制定通信、安全、交通、卫生健康、食品、应急救援等相关保障措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门应当自收到申请之日起三十日内进行实地核查，并作出批准或者不予批准的决定。  
国务院体育行政部门会同有关部门制定、调整高危险性体育赛事活动目录并予以公布。

**Article 106** The hosting of high-risk sports events and activities shall meet the following conditions, and an application shall be submitted to the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) Professional and technical personnel with corresponding qualifications or credentials are assigned;  
(2) Venues, equipment, and facilities that meet the relevant standards and requirements are configured;  
(3) Relevant safeguard measures concerning communications, safety, transportation, health, food, emergency rescue, etc., are formulated.  
The sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, within thirty days from the date of receiving the application, conduct an on-site inspection and make a decision to approve or disapprove the application.  
The sport administrative department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments, formulate and adjust the catalogue of high-risk sports events and activities and publish it.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条　县级以上地方人民政府应当建立体育执法机制，为体育执法提供必要保障。体育执法情况应当向社会公布，接受社会监督。

**Article 107** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall establish a sports law enforcement mechanism and provide necessary safeguards for sports law enforcement. The status of sports law enforcement shall be published to the public and subject to public supervision.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条　县级以上地方人民政府每届任期内至少向本级人民代表大会或者其常务委员会报告一次全民健身、青少年和学校体育工作。

**Article 108** The local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within each term of office, report to the people's congress at the same level or its standing committee on the work of national fitness, youth sports, and school sports at least once.

### 第十一章　法律责任

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条　国家机关及其工作人员违反本法规定，有下列行为之一的，由其所在单位、主管部门或者上级机关责令改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）对违法行为不依法查处的；  
（二）侵占、挪用、截留、克扣、私分体育资金的；  
（三）在组织体育赛事活动时，有违反体育道德和体育赛事规则，弄虚作假、营私舞弊等行为的；  
（四）其他不依法履行职责的行为。

**Article 109** Where a State organ or its staff member violates the provisions of this Law by committing any of the following acts, the entity to which it belongs, the competent department, or the superior organ shall order correction; and the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who are accountable shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) Failing to investigate and punish illegal acts according to law;  
(2) Embezzling, misappropriating, intercepting, withholding, or privately distributing sports funds;  
(3) Engaging in acts that violate sports ethics and sports event rules, such as falsification, malpractice, etc., when organizing sports events or activities;  
(4) Other acts of failing to perform duties according to law.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条　体育组织违反本法规定的，由相关部门责令改正，给予警告，对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分；可以限期停止活动，并可责令撤换直接负责的主管人员；情节严重的，予以撤销登记。

**Article 110** Where a sports organization violates the provisions of this Law, the relevant department shall order correction, give a warning, and impose sanctions on the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who are accountable according to law; it may also order the suspension of activities for a specified period and order the replacement of the directly responsible persons in charge; if the circumstances are serious, the registration shall be revoked.

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条　学校违反本法有关规定的，由有关主管部门责令改正；对负有责任的领导人员和直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 111** Where a school violates the relevant provisions of this Law, the relevant competent department shall order correction; and the leading personnel and directly responsible personnel who are accountable shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条　运动员、教练员、裁判员违反本法规定，有违反体育道德和体育赛事规则，弄虚作假、营私舞弊等行为的，由体育组织按照有关规定给予处理；情节严重、社会影响恶劣的，由县级以上人民政府体育行政部门纳入限制、禁止参加竞技体育活动名单；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款。  
利用体育赛事从事赌博活动的，由公安机关依法查处。

**Article 112** Where an athlete, coach, or referee violates the provisions of this Law by engaging in acts that violate sports ethics and sports event rules, such as falsification, malpractice, etc., the sports organization shall handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions; if the circumstances are serious and the social impact is egregious, the sport administrative department of the people's government at or above the county level shall include the person in the list of persons restricted or prohibited from participating in competitive sports activities; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall also be imposed.  
Where gambling activities are conducted by using sports events, the public security organ shall investigate and punish them according to law.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条　体育赛事活动组织者有下列行为之一的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门责令改正，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，给予一年以上三年以下禁止组织体育赛事活动的处罚：  
（一）未经许可举办高危险性体育赛事活动的；  
（二）体育赛事活动因突发事件不具备办赛条件时，未及时中止的；  
（三）安全条件不符合要求的；  
（四）有违反体育道德和体育赛事规则，弄虚作假、营私舞弊等行为的；  
（五）未按要求采取风险防范及应急处置预案等保障措施的。

**Article 113** Where a sports event or activity organizer commits any of the following acts, the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the organizer shall be prohibited from organizing sports events or activities for not less than one year but not more than three years:  
(1) Hosting high-risk sports events or activities without permission;  
(2) Failing to suspend a sports event or activity in a timely manner when the conditions for holding the event are not met due to an emergency;  
(3) Safety conditions do not meet the requirements;  
(4) Engaging in acts that violate sports ethics and sports event rules, such as falsification, malpractice, etc.;  
(5) Failing to adopt safeguard measures such as risk prevention and emergency response plans as required.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条　违反本法规定，侵占、破坏公共体育场地设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门会同有关部门予以制止，责令改正，并可处实际损失五倍以下的罚款。

**Article 114** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, encroaches upon or damages public sports venues or facilities, the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, stop the act, order correction, and may impose a fine of not more than five times the actual loss.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条　违反本法规定，未经批准临时占用公共体育场地设施的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门会同有关部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，对公共体育场地设施管理单位处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。

**Article 115** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, temporarily occupies public sports venues or facilities without approval, the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, order correction within a specified period; if correction is not made within the specified period, the management unit of the public sports venues or facilities shall be fined not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条　未经许可经营高危险性体育项目的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门会同有关部门责令限期关闭；逾期未关闭的，处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得。  
违法经营高危险性体育项目的，由县级以上地方人民政府体育行政部门责令改正；逾期未改正的，处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；造成严重后果的，由主管部门责令关闭，吊销许可证照，五年内不得再从事该项目经营活动。

**Article 116** Where anyone operates high-risk sports events without permission, the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall, together with the relevant departments, order closure within a specified period; if the closure is not made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated.  
Where anyone illegally operates high-risk sports events, the sport administrative department of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order correction; if correction is not made within the specified period, a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if serious consequences are caused, the competent department shall order closure, revoke the license or permit, and the operator shall not engage in the business of such event for five years.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条　运动员违规使用兴奋剂的，由有关体育社会组织、运动员管理单位、体育赛事活动组织者作出取消参赛资格、取消比赛成绩或者禁赛等处理。

**Article 117** Where an athlete uses doping substances in violation of the rules, the relevant sports social organization, athlete management unit, or sports event or activity organizer shall make a decision to disqualify the athlete from participation, nullify the competition results, or impose a ban on participation, etc.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条　组织、强迫、欺骗、教唆、引诱运动员在体育运动中使用兴奋剂的，由国务院体育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育行政部门没收非法持有的兴奋剂；直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员四年内不得从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作；情节严重的，终身不得从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作。  
向运动员提供或者变相提供兴奋剂的，由国务院体育行政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府体育行政部门没收非法持有的兴奋剂，并处五万元以上五十万元以下的罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；并给予禁止一定年限直至终身从事体育管理工作和运动员辅助工作的处罚。

**Article 118** Where anyone organizes, coerces, deceives, instigates, or induces an athlete to use doping substances in sports, the sport administrative department of the State Council or the sport administrative department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping substances; the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly responsible personnel shall be prohibited from engaging in sports management work and athlete support work for four years; if the circumstances are serious, they shall be prohibited from engaging in such work for life.  
Where anyone provides or provides in disguised form doping substances to an athlete, the sport administrative department of the State Council or the sport administrative department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall confiscate the illegally held doping substances, impose a fine of not less than 50,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; and impose a punishment of prohibition from engaging in sports management work and athlete support work for a specified period up to life.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条　违反本法规定，造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任；构成违反治安管理行为的，由公安机关依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 119** Where anyone, in violation of the provisions of this Law, causes property loss or other damage, it shall bear civil liability according to law; if the act constitutes a violation of public security administration, the public security organ shall impose a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第十二章　附则

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条　任何国家、地区或者组织在国际体育运动中损害中华人民共和国主权、安全、发展利益和尊严的，中华人民共和国可以根据实际情况采取相应措施。

**Article 120** Where any country, region, or organization damages the sovereignty, security, development interests, or dignity of the People's Republic of China in international sports, the People's Republic of China may take corresponding measures based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队开展体育活动的具体办法，由中央军事委员会依照本法制定。

**Article 121** The specific measures for carrying out sports activities by the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条　本法自2023年1月1日起施行。

**Article 122** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2023.
