# 中华人民共和国无障碍环境建设法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2023-06-28  
**生效日期**：2023-09-01

> （2023年6月28日第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　无障碍设施建设 第三章　无障碍信息交流 第四章　无障碍社会服务 第五章　保障措施 第六章　监督管理 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强无障碍环境建设，保障残疾人、老年人平等、充分、便捷地参与和融入社会生活，促进社会全体人员共享经济社会发展成果，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法和有关法律，制定本法。 第二条　国家采取措施推进无障碍环境建设，为残疾人、老年人自主安全地通行道路、出入建筑物以及使用其附属设施、搭乘公共交通运输工具，获取、使用和交流信息，获得社会服务等提供便利。 残疾人、老年人之外的其他人有无障碍需求的，可以享受无障碍环境便利。 第三条　无障碍环境建设应当坚持中国共产党的领导，发挥政府主导作用，调动市场主体积极性，引导社会组织和公众广泛参与，推动全社会共建共治共享。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强无障碍环境建设，保障残疾人、老年人平等、充分、便捷地参与和融入社会生活，促进社会全体人员共享经济社会发展成果，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法和有关法律，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution and relevant laws to strengthen the construction of barrier-free environments, ensure that persons with disabilities and elderly persons participate in and integrate into social life equally, fully, and conveniently, promote all members of society to share the fruits of economic and social development, and promote the core socialist values.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家采取措施推进无障碍环境建设，为残疾人、老年人自主安全地通行道路、出入建筑物以及使用其附属设施、搭乘公共交通运输工具，获取、使用和交流信息，获得社会服务等提供便利。  
残疾人、老年人之外的其他人有无障碍需求的，可以享受无障碍环境便利。

**Article 2** The State shall take measures to advance the construction of barrier-free environments, providing convenience for persons with disabilities and elderly persons to autonomously and safely travel on roads, enter and exit buildings and use their ancillary facilities, take public transportation, access, use and exchange information, and obtain social services.  
Persons other than persons with disabilities and elderly persons who have barrier-free needs may enjoy the convenience of barrier-free environments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　无障碍环境建设应当坚持中国共产党的领导，发挥政府主导作用，调动市场主体积极性，引导社会组织和公众广泛参与，推动全社会共建共治共享。

**Article 3** The construction of barrier-free environments shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, give play to the leading role of the government, mobilize the initiative of market entities, guide social organizations and the public to participate extensively, and promote the joint construction, governance, and sharing of the whole society.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　无障碍环境建设应当与适老化改造相结合，遵循安全便利、实用易行、广泛受益的原则。

**Article 4** The construction of barrier-free environments shall be combined with age-friendly renovation, following the principles of safety and convenience, practicality and ease of use, and broad benefit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　无障碍环境建设应当与经济社会发展水平相适应，统筹城镇和农村发展，逐步缩小城乡无障碍环境建设的差距。

**Article 5** The construction of barrier-free environments shall be commensurate with the level of economic and social development, coordinate urban and rural development, and gradually narrow the gap between urban and rural barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　县级以上人民政府应当将无障碍环境建设纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，将所需经费纳入本级预算，建立稳定的经费保障机制。

**Article 6** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate the construction of barrier-free environments into the national economic and social development plans, include the necessary funds into the budgets at the same level, and establish a stable funding guarantee mechanism.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　县级以上人民政府应当统筹协调和督促指导有关部门在各自职责范围内做好无障碍环境建设工作。  
县级以上人民政府住房和城乡建设、民政、工业和信息化、交通运输、自然资源、文化和旅游、教育、卫生健康等部门应当在各自职责范围内，开展无障碍环境建设工作。  
乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当协助有关部门做好无障碍环境建设工作。

**Article 7** People's governments at or above the county level shall coordinate and supervise relevant departments to carry out the construction of barrier-free environments within their respective responsibilities.  
Departments of housing and urban-rural development, civil affairs, industry and information technology, transportation, natural resources, culture and tourism, education, and health of people's governments at or above the county level shall carry out the construction of barrier-free environments within their respective responsibilities.  
Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall assist relevant departments in carrying out the construction of barrier-free environments.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织依照法律、法规以及各自章程，协助各级人民政府及其有关部门做好无障碍环境建设工作。

**Article 8** Federations of persons with disabilities, senior citizens associations, and other organizations shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and their respective charters, assist people's governments at all levels and their relevant departments in carrying out the construction of barrier-free environments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　制定或者修改涉及无障碍环境建设的法律、法规、规章、规划和其他规范性文件，应当征求残疾人、老年人代表以及残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织的意见。

**Article 9** When formulating or amending laws, regulations, rules, plans, and other normative documents concerning the construction of barrier-free environments, opinions of representatives of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, as well as organizations such as federations of persons with disabilities and senior citizens associations, shall be solicited.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励和支持企业事业单位、社会组织、个人等社会力量，通过捐赠、志愿服务等方式参与无障碍环境建设。  
国家支持开展无障碍环境建设工作的国际交流与合作。

**Article 10** The State encourages and supports enterprises, institutions, social organizations, individuals and other social forces to participate in the construction of barrier-free environments through donations, volunteer services, and other means.  
The State supports the conduct of international exchanges and cooperation in the work of barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对在无障碍环境建设工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰和奖励。

**Article 11** Entities and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the work of barrier-free environment construction shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with relevant State regulations.

### 第二章　无障碍设施建设

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　新建、改建、扩建的居住建筑、居住区、公共建筑、公共场所、交通运输设施、城乡道路等，应当符合无障碍设施工程建设标准。  
无障碍设施应当与主体工程同步规划、同步设计、同步施工、同步验收、同步交付使用，并与周边的无障碍设施有效衔接、实现贯通。  
无障碍设施应当设置符合标准的无障碍标识，并纳入周边环境或者建筑物内部的引导标识系统。

**Article 12** Newly built, renovated, or extended residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transportation facilities, urban and rural roads, etc., shall comply with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities.  
Barrier-free facilities shall be planned, designed, constructed, accepted, and delivered for use simultaneously with the main project, and shall be effectively connected to and integrated with the surrounding barrier-free facilities.  
Barrier-free facilities shall be provided with barrier-free signs that conform to standards, and shall be incorporated into the guidance sign system of the surrounding environment or the interior of the building.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家鼓励工程建设、设计、施工等单位采用先进的理念和技术，建设人性化、系统化、智能化并与周边环境相协调的无障碍设施。

**Article 13** The State encourages engineering construction, design, construction and other units to adopt advanced concepts and technologies to build barrier-free facilities that are humanized, systematic, intelligent, and coordinated with the surrounding environment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　工程建设单位应当将无障碍设施建设经费纳入工程建设项目概预算。  
工程建设单位不得明示或者暗示设计、施工单位违反无障碍设施工程建设标准；不得擅自将未经验收或者验收不合格的无障碍设施交付使用。

**Article 14** The construction entity of an engineering project shall include the funds for the construction of barrier-free facilities in the budgetary estimate and budget of the project.  
The construction entity of an engineering project shall not explicitly or implicitly instruct the design or construction unit to violate the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities; shall not arbitrarily deliver for use barrier-free facilities that have not been inspected or have failed inspection.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　工程设计单位应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准进行设计。  
依法需要进行施工图设计文件审查的，施工图审查机构应当按照法律、法规和无障碍设施工程建设标准，对无障碍设施设计内容进行审查；不符合有关规定的，不予审查通过。

**Article 15** The engineering design unit shall carry out the design in accordance with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities.  
Where examination of construction drawing design documents is required according to law, the construction drawing review agency shall review the design content of barrier-free facilities in accordance with laws, regulations, and the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities; if it does not comply with relevant provisions, it shall not be approved.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　工程施工、监理单位应当按照施工图设计文件以及相关标准进行无障碍设施施工和监理。  
住房和城乡建设等主管部门对未按照法律、法规和无障碍设施工程建设标准开展无障碍设施验收或者验收不合格的，不予办理竣工验收备案手续。

**Article 16** The engineering construction and supervision units shall carry out the construction and supervision of barrier-free facilities in accordance with the construction drawing design documents and relevant standards.  
The competent departments such as housing and urban-rural development shall not handle the completion acceptance filing procedures for those that have not carried out the acceptance of barrier-free facilities in accordance with laws, regulations, and the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities or have failed the acceptance.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励工程建设单位在新建、改建、扩建建设项目的规划、设计和竣工验收等环节，邀请残疾人、老年人代表以及残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织，参加意见征询和体验试用等活动。

**Article 17** The State encourages construction entities to invite representatives of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, as well as organizations such as federations of persons with disabilities and senior citizens associations, to participate in activities such as opinion solicitation and trial use during the planning, design, and completion acceptance of new, renovated, or extended construction projects.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对既有的不符合无障碍设施工程建设标准的居住建筑、居住区、公共建筑、公共场所、交通运输设施、城乡道路等，县级以上人民政府应当根据实际情况，制定有针对性的无障碍设施改造计划并组织实施。  
无障碍设施改造由所有权人或者管理人负责。所有权人、管理人和使用人之间约定改造责任的，由约定的责任人负责。  
不具备无障碍设施改造条件的，责任人应当采取必要的替代性措施。

**Article 18** For existing residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transportation facilities, urban and rural roads, etc., that do not comply with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities, people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual situation, formulate targeted renovation plans for barrier-free facilities and organize their implementation.  
The renovation of barrier-free facilities shall be the responsibility of the owner or manager. Where the owner, manager, and user have agreed on the renovation responsibility, the agreed responsible person shall be liable.  
Where conditions for renovating barrier-free facilities do not exist, the responsible person shall take necessary alternative measures.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级以上人民政府应当支持、指导家庭无障碍设施改造。对符合条件的残疾人、老年人家庭应当给予适当补贴。  
居民委员会、村民委员会、居住区管理服务单位以及业主委员会应当支持并配合家庭无障碍设施改造。

**Article 19** People's governments at or above the county level shall support and guide the renovation of barrier-free facilities in households. Appropriate subsidies shall be given to families of persons with disabilities or elderly persons that meet the conditions.  
Residents' committees, villagers' committees, residential area management service units, and owners' committees shall support and cooperate with the renovation of barrier-free facilities in households.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　残疾人集中就业单位应当按照有关标准和要求，建设和改造无障碍设施。  
国家鼓励和支持用人单位开展就业场所无障碍设施建设和改造，为残疾人职工提供必要的劳动条件和便利。

**Article 20** Units that employ concentrated numbers of persons with disabilities shall construct and renovate barrier-free facilities in accordance with relevant standards and requirements.  
The State encourages and supports employing units to carry out the construction and renovation of barrier-free facilities in workplaces, providing necessary working conditions and convenience for employees with disabilities.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　新建、改建、扩建公共建筑、公共场所、交通运输设施以及居住区的公共服务设施，应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准，配套建设无障碍设施；既有的上述建筑、场所和设施不符合无障碍设施工程建设标准的，应当进行必要的改造。

**Article 21** When newly building, renovating, or extending public buildings, public places, transportation facilities, and public service facilities in residential areas, supporting barrier-free facilities shall be constructed in accordance with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities; where existing buildings, places, and facilities do not comply with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities, necessary renovations shall be carried out.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家支持城镇老旧小区既有多层住宅加装电梯或者其他无障碍设施，为残疾人、老年人提供便利。  
县级以上人民政府及其有关部门应当采取措施、创造条件，并发挥社区基层组织作用，推动既有多层住宅加装电梯或者其他无障碍设施。  
房屋所有权人应当弘扬中华民族与邻为善、守望相助等传统美德，加强沟通协商，依法配合既有多层住宅加装电梯或者其他无障碍设施。

**Article 22** The State supports the installation of elevators or other barrier-free facilities in existing multi-story residential buildings in old urban communities to provide convenience for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.  
People's governments at or above the county level and their relevant departments shall take measures, create conditions, and give play to the role of community grassroots organizations to promote the installation of elevators or other barrier-free facilities in existing multi-story residential buildings.  
Housing owners shall promote traditional virtues of the Chinese nation such as being kind to neighbors and helping each other, strengthen communication and negotiation, and cooperate in accordance with law in the installation of elevators or other barrier-free facilities in existing multi-story residential buildings.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　新建、改建、扩建和具备改造条件的城市主干路、主要商业区和大型居住区的人行天桥和人行地下通道，应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准，建设或者改造无障碍设施。  
城市主干路、主要商业区等无障碍需求比较集中的区域的人行道，应当按照标准设置盲道；城市中心区、残疾人集中就业单位和集中就读学校周边的人行横道的交通信号设施，应当按照标准安装过街音响提示装置。

**Article 23** For newly built, renovated, extended, and conditionally renovable pedestrian overpasses and underpasses on urban arterial roads, major commercial areas, and large residential areas, barrier-free facilities shall be constructed or renovated in accordance with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities.  
Sidewalks in areas with relatively concentrated barrier-free needs such as urban arterial roads and major commercial areas shall be provided with tactile paving according to standards; traffic signal facilities at pedestrian crosswalks in city centers, around concentrated employment units of persons with disabilities, and around schools with concentrated enrollment of persons with disabilities shall be equipped with audible crosswalk signals according to standards.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　停车场应当按照无障碍设施工程建设标准，设置无障碍停车位，并设置显著标志标识。  
无障碍停车位优先供肢体残疾人驾驶或者乘坐的机动车使用。优先使用无障碍停车位的，应当在显著位置放置残疾人车辆专用标志或者提供残疾人证。  
在无障碍停车位充足的情况下，其他行动不便的残疾人、老年人、孕妇、婴幼儿等驾驶或者乘坐的机动车也可以使用。

**Article 24** Parking lots shall set up accessible parking spaces in accordance with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities, and shall set up conspicuous signs and markings.  
Accessible parking spaces shall be used preferentially by motor vehicles driven or occupied by persons with physical disabilities. Those who use accessible parking spaces preferentially shall place a special sign for vehicles of persons with disabilities in a conspicuous position or present a certificate of disability.  
Where accessible parking spaces are sufficient, motor vehicles driven or occupied by other persons with mobility difficulties, such as persons with disabilities, elderly persons, pregnant women, or infants, may also use them.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　新投入运营的民用航空器、客运列车、客运船舶、公共汽电车、城市轨道交通车辆等公共交通运输工具，应当确保一定比例符合无障碍标准。  
既有公共交通运输工具具备改造条件的，应当进行无障碍改造，逐步符合无障碍标准的要求；不具备改造条件的，公共交通运输工具的运营单位应当采取必要的替代性措施。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据当地情况，逐步建立城市无障碍公交导乘系统，规划配置适量的无障碍出租汽车。

**Article 25** Newly put into operation public transport vehicles such as civil aircraft, passenger trains, passenger ships, public buses and trams, and urban rail transit vehicles shall ensure that a certain proportion meets the accessibility standards.  
Existing public transport vehicles that have the conditions for renovation shall undergo accessibility renovation to gradually meet the requirements of the accessibility standards; those that do not have the conditions for renovation, the operating units of the public transport vehicles shall take necessary alternative measures.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to local conditions, gradually establish an urban accessible public transport guidance system, and plan and allocate an appropriate number of accessible taxis.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　无障碍设施所有权人或者管理人应当对无障碍设施履行以下维护和管理责任，保障无障碍设施功能正常和使用安全：  
（一）对损坏的无障碍设施和标识进行维修或者替换；  
（二）对需改造的无障碍设施进行改造；  
（三）纠正占用无障碍设施的行为；  
（四）进行其他必要的维护和保养。  
所有权人、管理人和使用人之间有约定的，由约定的责任人负责维护和管理。

**Article 26** The owner or manager of barrier-free facilities shall perform the following maintenance and management responsibilities for barrier-free facilities to ensure the normal function and safe use of the facilities:  
(1) Repair or replace damaged barrier-free facilities and signs;  
(2) Renovate barrier-free facilities that need renovation;  
(3) Correct acts of occupying barrier-free facilities;  
(4) Carry out other necessary maintenance and upkeep.  
Where there is an agreement between the owner, manager, and user, the agreed responsible person shall be responsible for maintenance and management.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　因特殊情况设置的临时无障碍设施，应当符合无障碍设施工程建设标准。

**Article 27** Temporary barrier-free facilities set up due to special circumstances shall comply with the engineering construction standards for barrier-free facilities.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　任何单位和个人不得擅自改变无障碍设施的用途或者非法占用、损坏无障碍设施。  
因特殊情况临时占用无障碍设施的，应当公告并设置护栏、警示标志或者信号设施，同时采取必要的替代性措施。临时占用期满，应当及时恢复原状。

**Article 28** No entity or individual may arbitrarily change the use of barrier-free facilities or illegally occupy or damage barrier-free facilities.  
Where barrier-free facilities are temporarily occupied due to special circumstances, an announcement shall be made and guardrails, warning signs, or signal facilities shall be set up, and necessary alternative measures shall be taken at the same time. Upon expiry of the temporary occupation period, the original condition shall be restored in a timely manner.

### 第三章　无障碍信息交流

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　各级人民政府及其有关部门应当为残疾人、老年人获取公共信息提供便利；发布涉及自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等突发事件信息时，条件具备的同步采取语音、大字、盲文、手语等无障碍信息交流方式。

**Article 29** People's governments at all levels and their relevant departments shall provide convenience for persons with disabilities and elderly persons to obtain public information; when releasing information on emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, and social security incidents, where conditions permit, accessible information communication methods such as voice, large print, braille, and sign language shall be adopted simultaneously.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　利用财政资金设立的电视台应当在播出电视节目时配备同步字幕，条件具备的每天至少播放一次配播手语的新闻节目，并逐步扩大配播手语的节目范围。  
国家鼓励公开出版发行的影视类录像制品、网络视频节目加配字幕、手语或者口述音轨。

**Article 30** Television stations established with fiscal funds shall provide synchronous subtitles when broadcasting television programs, and where conditions permit, shall broadcast at least one news program with sign language interpretation every day, and shall gradually expand the scope of programs with sign language interpretation.  
The State encourages publicly released film and video recordings and online video programs to add subtitles, sign language interpretation, or audio description.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　国家鼓励公开出版发行的图书、报刊配备有声、大字、盲文、电子等无障碍格式版本，方便残疾人、老年人阅读。  
国家鼓励教材编写、出版单位根据不同教育阶段实际，编写、出版盲文版、低视力版教学用书，满足盲人和其他有视力障碍的学生的学习需求。

**Article 31** The State encourages publicly published books, newspapers, and periodicals to be provided in accessible format versions such as audio, large print, braille, and electronic versions to facilitate reading by persons with disabilities and elderly persons.  
The State encourages textbook compilation and publishing units to compile and publish braille and low-vision versions of textbooks according to the actual situation at different educational stages to meet the learning needs of blind students and other students with visual impairments.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　利用财政资金建立的互联网网站、服务平台、移动互联网应用程序，应当逐步符合无障碍网站设计标准和国家信息无障碍标准。  
国家鼓励新闻资讯、社交通讯、生活购物、医疗健康、金融服务、学习教育、交通出行等领域的互联网网站、移动互联网应用程序，逐步符合无障碍网站设计标准和国家信息无障碍标准。  
国家鼓励地图导航定位产品逐步完善无障碍设施的标识和无障碍出行路线导航功能。

**Article 32** Internet websites, service platforms, and mobile Internet applications established with fiscal funds shall gradually comply with the accessible website design standards and the national information accessibility standards.  
The State encourages Internet websites and mobile Internet applications in fields such as news and information, social communication, life shopping, medical health, financial services, learning and education, and transportation to gradually comply with the accessible website design standards and the national information accessibility standards.  
The State encourages map navigation and positioning products to gradually improve the identification of barrier-free facilities and the navigation functions for accessible travel routes.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　音视频以及多媒体设备、移动智能终端设备、电信终端设备制造者提供的产品，应当逐步具备语音、大字等无障碍功能。  
银行、医院、城市轨道交通车站、民用运输机场航站区、客运站、客运码头、大型景区等的自助公共服务终端设备，应当具备语音、大字、盲文等无障碍功能。

**Article 33** Products provided by manufacturers of audio and video and multimedia equipment, mobile smart terminal equipment, and telecommunications terminal equipment shall gradually have accessible functions such as voice and large print.  
Self-service public service terminal equipment in banks, hospitals, urban rail transit stations, civil transport airport terminal areas, passenger stations, passenger terminals, large scenic spots, etc., shall have accessible functions such as voice, large print, and braille.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　电信业务经营者提供基础电信服务时，应当为残疾人、老年人提供必要的语音、大字信息服务或者人工服务。

**Article 34** When providing basic telecommunications services, telecommunications business operators shall provide necessary voice, large print information services, or manual services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　政务服务便民热线和报警求助、消防应急、交通事故、医疗急救等紧急呼叫系统，应当逐步具备语音、大字、盲文、一键呼叫等无障碍功能。

**Article 35** Government service convenience hotlines and emergency call systems such as for police assistance, fire emergency, traffic accidents, and medical first aid shall gradually have accessible functions such as voice, large print, braille, and one-touch call.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　提供公共文化服务的图书馆、博物馆、文化馆、科技馆等应当考虑残疾人、老年人的特点，积极创造条件，提供适合其需要的文献信息、无障碍设施设备和服务等。

**Article 36** Libraries, museums, cultural centers, science and technology museums, and other institutions providing public cultural services shall take into account the characteristics of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, actively create conditions, and provide literature, information, barrier-free facilities and equipment, and services suitable for their needs.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　国务院有关部门应当完善药品标签、说明书的管理规范，要求药品生产经营者提供语音、大字、盲文、电子等无障碍格式版本的标签、说明书。  
国家鼓励其他商品的生产经营者提供语音、大字、盲文、电子等无障碍格式版本的标签、说明书，方便残疾人、老年人识别和使用。

**Article 37** The relevant departments of the State Council shall improve the management regulations on drug labels and instructions, and require drug producers and operators to provide labels and instructions in accessible format versions such as voice, large print, braille, and electronic versions.  
The State encourages producers and operators of other commodities to provide labels and instructions in accessible format versions such as voice, large print, braille, and electronic versions to facilitate identification and use by persons with disabilities and elderly persons.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家推广和使用国家通用手语、国家通用盲文。  
基本公共服务使用手语、盲文以及各类学校开展手语、盲文教育教学时，应当采用国家通用手语、国家通用盲文。

**Article 38** The State promotes and uses the national common sign language and the national common braille.  
When sign language and braille are used in basic public services and when schools at all levels carry out sign language and braille education and teaching, the national common sign language and the national common braille shall be adopted.

### 第四章　无障碍社会服务

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　公共服务场所应当配备必要的无障碍设备和辅助器具，标注指引无障碍设施，为残疾人、老年人提供无障碍服务。  
公共服务场所涉及医疗健康、社会保障、金融业务、生活缴费等服务事项的，应当保留现场指导、人工办理等传统服务方式。

**Article 39** Public service venues shall be equipped with necessary accessible equipment and assistive devices, mark and guide barrier-free facilities, and provide accessible services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.  
Where public service venues involve service matters such as medical health, social security, financial services, and utility bill payments, traditional service methods such as on-site guidance and manual processing shall be retained.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　行政服务机构、社区服务机构以及供水、供电、供气、供热等公共服务机构，应当设置低位服务台或者无障碍服务窗口，配备电子信息显示屏、手写板、语音提示等设备，为残疾人、老年人提供无障碍服务。

**Article 40** Administrative service institutions, community service institutions, and public service institutions such as those providing water, electricity, gas, and heating supply shall set up low-level service counters or accessible service windows, equip with electronic information display screens, writing pads, voice prompts, and other equipment, and provide accessible services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　司法机关、仲裁机构、法律援助机构应当依法为残疾人、老年人参加诉讼、仲裁活动和获得法律援助提供无障碍服务。  
国家鼓励律师事务所、公证机构、司法鉴定机构、基层法律服务所等法律服务机构，结合所提供的服务内容提供无障碍服务。

**Article 41** Judicial organs, arbitration institutions, and legal aid institutions shall, according to law, provide accessible services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons to participate in litigation and arbitration activities and to obtain legal aid.  
The State encourages law firms, notarization institutions, judicial appraisal institutions, grassroots legal service offices and other legal service institutions to provide accessible services in light of the services they provide.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　交通运输设施和公共交通运输工具的运营单位应当根据各类运输方式的服务特点，结合设施设备条件和所提供的服务内容，为残疾人、老年人设置无障碍服务窗口、专用等候区域、绿色通道和优先坐席，提供辅助器具、咨询引导、字幕报站、语音提示、预约定制等无障碍服务。

**Article 42** Operating units of transportation facilities and public transport vehicles shall, based on the service characteristics of various modes of transport and in light of the facility and equipment conditions and the services provided, set up accessible service windows, dedicated waiting areas, green channels and priority seats, and provide accessible services such as assistive devices, consultation and guidance, subtitle station announcements, voice prompts, and reservation customization for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　教育行政部门和教育机构应当加强教育场所的无障碍环境建设，为有残疾的师生、员工提供无障碍服务。  
国家举办的教育考试、职业资格考试、技术技能考试、招录招聘考试以及各类学校组织的统一考试，应当为有残疾的考生提供便利服务。

**Article 43** Education administrative departments and educational institutions shall strengthen the construction of barrier-free environments in educational places and provide accessible services for teachers, students, and staff with disabilities.  
Education examinations, professional qualification examinations, technical skill examinations, recruitment examinations organized by the State, and unified examinations organized by various schools shall provide convenience services for examinees with disabilities.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　医疗卫生机构应当结合所提供的服务内容，为残疾人、老年人就医提供便利。  
与残疾人、老年人相关的服务机构应当配备无障碍设备，在生活照料、康复护理等方面提供无障碍服务。

**Article 44** Medical and health institutions shall, in light of the services they provide, facilitate medical treatment for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.  
Service institutions related to persons with disabilities and elderly persons shall be equipped with accessible equipment and provide accessible services in areas such as daily care and rehabilitation nursing.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　国家鼓励文化、旅游、体育、金融、邮政、电信、交通、商业、餐饮、住宿、物业管理等服务场所结合所提供的服务内容，为残疾人、老年人提供辅助器具、咨询引导等无障碍服务。  
国家鼓励邮政、快递企业为行动不便的残疾人、老年人提供上门收寄服务。

**Article 45** The State encourages service venues in cultural, tourism, sports, financial, postal, telecommunications, transportation, commercial, catering, accommodation, and property management sectors to provide accessible services such as assistive devices and consultation and guidance for persons with disabilities and elderly persons in light of the services they provide.  
The State encourages postal and express delivery enterprises to provide door-to-door collection and delivery services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons with mobility difficulties.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　公共场所经营管理单位、交通运输设施和公共交通运输工具的运营单位应当为残疾人携带导盲犬、导听犬、辅助犬等服务犬提供便利。  
残疾人携带服务犬出入公共场所、使用交通运输设施和公共交通运输工具的，应当遵守国家有关规定，为服务犬佩戴明显识别装备，并采取必要的防护措施。

**Article 46** Management units of public places and operating units of transportation facilities and public transport vehicles shall provide convenience for persons with disabilities carrying service dogs such as guide dogs, hearing dogs, and assistance dogs.  
Where persons with disabilities carry service dogs to enter or leave public places or use transportation facilities and public transport vehicles, they shall comply with relevant State regulations, wear conspicuous identification equipment on the service dog, and take necessary protective measures.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　应急避难场所的管理人在制定以及实施工作预案时，应当考虑残疾人、老年人的无障碍需求，视情况设置语音、大字、闪光等提示装置，完善无障碍服务功能。

**Article 47** When formulating and implementing work plans, the managers of emergency shelters shall take into account the barrier-free needs of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, set up voice, large print, flashing and other prompt devices as appropriate, and improve the barrier-free service functions.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　组织选举的部门和单位应当采取措施，为残疾人、老年人选民参加投票提供便利和必要协助。

**Article 48** Departments and units organizing elections shall take measures to provide convenience and necessary assistance for voters with disabilities and elderly voters to participate in voting.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　国家鼓励和支持无障碍信息服务平台建设，为残疾人、老年人提供远程实时无障碍信息服务。

**Article 49** The State encourages and supports the construction of accessible information service platforms to provide remote real-time accessible information services for persons with disabilities and elderly persons.

### 第五章　保障措施

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　国家开展无障碍环境理念的宣传教育，普及无障碍环境知识，传播无障碍环境文化，提升全社会的无障碍环境意识。  
新闻媒体应当积极开展无障碍环境建设方面的公益宣传。

**Article 50** The State shall carry out publicity and education on the concept of barrier-free environments, popularize knowledge about barrier-free environments, spread the culture of barrier-free environments, and raise the awareness of barrier-free environments in the whole society.  
News media shall actively carry out public service publicity on the construction of barrier-free environments.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家推广通用设计理念，建立健全国家标准、行业标准、地方标准，鼓励发展具有引领性的团体标准、企业标准，加强标准之间的衔接配合，构建无障碍环境建设标准体系。  
地方结合本地实际制定的地方标准不得低于国家标准的相关技术要求。

**Article 51** The state shall promote the concept of universal design, establish and improve national standards, industry standards, and local standards, encourage the development of leading group standards and enterprise standards, strengthen the connection and coordination between standards, and build a standard system for barrier-free environment construction.  
Local standards formulated by local authorities according to local conditions shall not be lower than the relevant technical requirements of national standards.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　制定或者修改涉及无障碍环境建设的标准，应当征求残疾人、老年人代表以及残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织的意见。残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织可以依法提出制定或者修改无障碍环境建设标准的建议。

**Article 52** When formulating or amending standards concerning barrier-free environment construction, opinions of representatives of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, as well as organizations such as the Disabled Persons' Federation and the Aging Committee, shall be solicited. The Disabled Persons' Federation, the Aging Committee, and other organizations may, according to law, make suggestions for formulating or amending standards on barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　国家建立健全无障碍设计、设施、产品、服务的认证和无障碍信息的评测制度，并推动结果采信应用。

**Article 53** The state shall establish and improve the certification system for barrier-free design, facilities, products, and services, and the evaluation system for barrier-free information, and promote the adoption and application of the results.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　国家通过经费支持、政府采购、税收优惠等方式，促进新科技成果在无障碍环境建设中的运用，鼓励无障碍技术、产品和服务的研发、生产、应用和推广，支持无障碍设施、信息和服务的融合发展。

**Article 54** The state shall, through financial support, government procurement, tax incentives, and other means, promote the application of new scientific and technological achievements in barrier-free environment construction, encourage the research and development, production, application, and promotion of barrier-free technologies, products, and services, and support the integrated development of barrier-free facilities, information, and services.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　国家建立无障碍环境建设相关领域人才培养机制。  
国家鼓励高等学校、中等职业学校等开设无障碍环境建设相关专业和课程，开展无障碍环境建设理论研究、国际交流和实践活动。  
建筑、交通运输、计算机科学与技术等相关学科专业应当增加无障碍环境建设的教学和实践内容，相关领域职业资格、继续教育以及其他培训的考试内容应当包括无障碍环境建设知识。

**Article 55** The state shall establish a personnel training mechanism in fields related to barrier-free environment construction.  
The state encourages higher education institutions, secondary vocational schools, and other institutions to offer majors and courses related to barrier-free environment construction, and to carry out theoretical research, international exchanges, and practical activities on barrier-free environment construction.  
Relevant academic disciplines and majors such as architecture, transportation, and computer science and technology shall increase teaching and practical content on barrier-free environment construction, and the examination content of professional qualifications, continuing education, and other training in related fields shall include knowledge of barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　国家鼓励机关、企业事业单位、社会团体以及其他社会组织，对工作人员进行无障碍服务知识与技能培训。

**Article 56** The state encourages state organs, enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, and other social entities to provide staff with training on knowledge and skills of barrier-free services.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　文明城市、文明村镇、文明单位、文明社区、文明校园等创建活动，应当将无障碍环境建设情况作为重要内容。

**Article 57** In the establishment activities of civilized cities, civilized townships and villages, civilized units, civilized communities, and civilized campuses, the status of barrier-free environment construction shall be taken as an important component.

### 第六章　监督管理

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　县级以上人民政府及其有关主管部门依法对无障碍环境建设进行监督检查，根据工作需要开展联合监督检查。

**Article 58** The people's governments at or above the county level and their relevant competent departments shall, according to law, conduct supervision and inspection of barrier-free environment construction, and carry out joint supervision and inspection as required by work.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　国家实施无障碍环境建设目标责任制和考核评价制度。县级以上地方人民政府根据本地区实际，制定具体考核办法。

**Article 59** The state shall implement a target responsibility system and an assessment and evaluation system for barrier-free environment construction. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall formulate specific assessment measures based on the actual conditions of their respective regions.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　县级以上地方人民政府有关主管部门定期委托第三方机构开展无障碍环境建设评估，并将评估结果向社会公布，接受社会监督。

**Article 60** The relevant competent departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall regularly entrust third-party institutions to conduct assessments of barrier-free environment construction, and shall publish the assessment results to the public for social supervision.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府建立无障碍环境建设信息公示制度，定期发布无障碍环境建设情况。

**Article 61** The people's governments at or above the county level shall establish an information disclosure system for barrier-free environment construction and regularly publish the status of barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　任何组织和个人有权向政府有关主管部门提出加强和改进无障碍环境建设的意见和建议，对违反本法规定的行为进行投诉、举报。县级以上人民政府有关主管部门接到涉及无障碍环境建设的投诉和举报，应当及时处理并予以答复。  
残疾人联合会、老龄协会等组织根据需要，可以聘请残疾人、老年人代表以及具有相关专业知识的人员，对无障碍环境建设情况进行监督。  
新闻媒体可以对无障碍环境建设情况开展舆论监督。

**Article 62** Any organization or individual has the right to submit opinions and suggestions to the relevant competent departments of the government on strengthening and improving barrier-free environment construction, and to lodge complaints and reports against acts violating the provisions of this Law. Upon receiving complaints and reports concerning barrier-free environment construction, the relevant competent departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall handle them promptly and provide responses.  
The Disabled Persons' Federation, the Aging Committee, and other organizations may, as needed, engage representatives of persons with disabilities and elderly persons, as well as persons with relevant expertise, to supervise the status of barrier-free environment construction.  
The news media may carry out public opinion supervision on the status of barrier-free environment construction.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　对违反本法规定损害社会公共利益的行为，人民检察院可以提出检察建议或者提起公益诉讼。

**Article 63** For acts that violate the provisions of this Law and harm the public interest, the People's Procuratorate may issue procuratorial recommendations or initiate public interest litigation.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　工程建设、设计、施工、监理单位未按照本法规定进行建设、设计、施工、监理的，由住房和城乡建设、民政、交通运输等相关主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，依照相关法律法规的规定进行处罚。

**Article 64** Where a project construction, design, construction, or supervision unit fails to carry out construction, design, construction, or supervision in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the relevant competent departments such as housing and urban-rural construction, civil affairs, and transportation shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, it shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，有下列情形之一的，由住房和城乡建设、民政、交通运输等相关主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，对单位处一万元以上三万元以下罚款，对个人处一百元以上五百元以下罚款：  
（一）无障碍设施责任人不履行维护和管理职责，无法保障无障碍设施功能正常和使用安全；  
（二）设置临时无障碍设施不符合相关规定；  
（三）擅自改变无障碍设施的用途或者非法占用、损坏无障碍设施。

**Article 65** Where any of the following circumstances in violation of the provisions of this Law occurs, the relevant competent departments such as housing and urban-rural construction, civil affairs, and transportation shall order corrections within a prescribed time limit; if corrections are not made within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 30,000 yuan shall be imposed on the unit, and a fine of not less than 100 yuan but not more than 500 yuan shall be imposed on the individual:  
(1) The person responsible for barrier-free facilities fails to perform maintenance and management duties, and cannot ensure the normal function and safe use of the barrier-free facilities;  
(2) The temporary barrier-free facilities are set up not in compliance with the relevant regulations;  
(3) The use of barrier-free facilities is changed without authorization, or barrier-free facilities are illegally occupied or damaged.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　违反本法规定，不依法履行无障碍信息交流义务的，由网信、工业和信息化、电信、广播电视、新闻出版等相关主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，予以通报批评。

**Article 66** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and fails to perform the obligation of barrier-free information exchange according to law, the relevant competent departments such as cyberspace affairs, industry and information technology, telecommunications, radio and television, and press and publication shall order corrections within a prescribed time limit; if corrections are not made within the time limit, a circulating notice of criticism shall be given.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　电信业务经营者不依法提供无障碍信息服务的，由电信主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，处一万元以上十万元以下罚款。

**Article 67** Where a telecommunications business operator fails to provide barrier-free information services according to law, the telecommunications competent authority shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　负有公共服务职责的部门和单位未依法提供无障碍社会服务的，由本级人民政府或者上级主管部门责令限期改正；逾期未改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 68** Where a department or unit with public service responsibilities fails to provide barrier-free social services according to law, the people's government at the same level or the competent department at a higher level shall order it to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if it fails to make corrections within the time limit, the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　考试举办者、组织者未依法向有残疾的考生提供便利服务的，由本级人民政府或者上级主管部门予以批评并责令改正；拒不改正的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 69** Where an examination organizer or administrator fails to provide convenience services to examinees with disabilities according to law, the people's government at the same level or the competent department at a higher level shall criticize it and order it to make corrections; if it refuses to make corrections, the directly responsible managers and other directly responsible persons shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　无障碍环境建设相关主管部门、有关组织的工作人员滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，依法给予处分。

**Article 70** Where staff members of the relevant competent departments and relevant organizations for barrier-free environment construction abuse their powers, neglect their duties, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, they shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　违反本法规定，造成人身损害、财产损失的，依法承担民事责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 71** Where anyone violates the provisions of this Law and causes personal injury or property damage, he or she shall bear civil liability according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　本法自2023年9月1日起施行。

**Article 72** This Law shall come into force as of September 1, 2023.
