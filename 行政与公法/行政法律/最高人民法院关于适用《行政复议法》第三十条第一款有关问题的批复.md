### 最高人民法院关于适用《行政复议法》第三十条第一款有关问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于适用[《行政复议法》第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-30)第一款  
  
有关问题的批复  
  
（2003年1月9日最高人民法院审判委员会第1263次会议通过，自2003年2月28日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2003〕5号  
  
山西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于适用〈行政复议法〉第三十条第一款有关问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[《行政复议法》第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-30)第一款的规定，公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关确认土地、矿藏、水流、森林、山岭、草原、荒地、滩涂、海域等自然资源的所有权或者使用权的具体行政行为，侵犯其己经依法取得的自然资源所有权或者使用权的，经行政复议后，才可以向人民法院提起行政诉讼，但法律另有规定的除外；对涉及自然资源所有权或者使用权的行政处罚、行政强制措施等其他具体行政行为提起行政诉讼的，不适用[《行政复议法》第三十条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政复议法.md#art-30)第一款的规定。  
  
此复。  
  
2003年2月25日

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2003] No. 5  
Shanxi Higher People's Court:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning the Application of the First Paragraph of Article 30 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
Pursuant to the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 30 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law, where a citizen, legal person or other organization considers that a specific administrative act taken by an administrative organ to confirm the ownership or right to use of natural resources such as land, mineral deposits, waters, forests, mountains, grasslands, wasteland, mudflats, or sea areas has infringed upon the ownership or right to use of natural resources that it has lawfully obtained, it may file an administrative action with the People's Court only after undergoing administrative reconsideration, except where otherwise provided by law; where an administrative action is filed against other specific administrative acts such as administrative penalties or administrative compulsory measures involving the ownership or right to use of natural resources, the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 30 of the Administrative Reconsideration Law shall not apply.  
This is our reply.  
February 25, 2003
