# 中华人民共和国人民警察警衔条例

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2009-08-27  
**生效日期**：2009-08-27

> （1992年7月1日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十六次会议通过　根据2009年8月27日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改部分法律的决定》修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　警衔等级的设置 第三章　警衔的首次授予 第四章　警衔的晋级 第五章　警衔的保留、降级、取消 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强人民警察队伍的革命化、现代化、正规化建设，增强人民警察的责任心、荣誉感和组织纪律性，有利于人民警察的指挥、管理和执行职务，根据宪法，制定本条例。 第二条　人民警察实行警衔制度。 第三条　警衔是区分人民警察等级、表明人民警察身份的称号、标志和国家给予人民警察的荣誉。 第四条　人民警察实行警察职务等级编制警衔。 第五条　警衔高的人民警察对警衔低的人民警察，警衔高的为上级。当警衔高的人民警察在职务上隶属于警衔低的人民警察时，职务高的为上级。 第六条　公安部主管人民警察警衔工作。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强人民警察队伍的革命化、现代化、正规化建设，增强人民警察的责任心、荣誉感和组织纪律性，有利于人民警察的指挥、管理和执行职务，根据宪法，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated pursuant to the Constitution for the purpose of strengthening the revolutionization, modernization, and regularization of the people's police force, enhancing the sense of responsibility, honor, and organizational discipline of the people's police, and facilitating the command, management, and performance of duties by the people's police.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民警察实行警衔制度。

**Article 2** The people's police shall implement a police rank system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　警衔是区分人民警察等级、表明人民警察身份的称号、标志和国家给予人民警察的荣誉。

**Article 3** Police ranks are titles and insignia that distinguish the grades of the people's police, indicate the identity of the people's police, and are honors granted by the State to the people's police.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民警察实行警察职务等级编制警衔。

**Article 4** The people's police shall implement police ranks commensurate with their post grades.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　警衔高的人民警察对警衔低的人民警察，警衔高的为上级。当警衔高的人民警察在职务上隶属于警衔低的人民警察时，职务高的为上级。

**Article 5** A people's police officer with a higher police rank shall be the superior of a people's police officer with a lower police rank. Where a people's police officer with a higher police rank is subordinate in post to a people's police officer with a lower police rank, the one with the higher post shall be the superior.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公安部主管人民警察警衔工作。

**Article 6** The Ministry of Public Security shall be in charge of the police rank work of the people's police.

### 第二章　警衔等级的设置

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民警察警衔设下列五等十三级：  
（一）总警监、副总警监；  
（二）警监：一级、二级、三级；  
（三）警督：一级、二级、三级；  
（四）警司：一级、二级、三级；  
（五）警员：一级、二级。  
担任专业技术职务的人民警察的警衔，在警衔前冠以“专业技术”。

**Article 7** The police ranks of the people's police shall consist of the following five grades and thirteen levels:  
(1) Commissioner General and Deputy Commissioner General;  
(2) Police Supervisor: first, second, and third;  
(3) Police Superintendent: first, second, and third;  
(4) Police Inspector: first, second, and third;  
(5) Police Constable: first and second.  
For the police ranks of people's police officers holding technical posts, the characters "Technical" shall be prefixed to the police rank.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　担任行政职务的人民警察实行下列职务等级编制警衔：  
（一）部级正职：总警监；  
（二）部级副职：副总警监；  
（三）厅（局）级正职：一级警监至二级警监；  
（四）厅（局）级副职：二级警监至三级警监；  
（五）处（局）级正职：三级警监至二级警督；  
（六）处（局）级副职：一级警督至三级警督；  
（七）科（局）级正职：一级警督至一级警司；  
（八）科（局）级副职：二级警督至二级警司；  
（九）科员（警长）职：三级警督至三级警司；  
（十）办事员（警员）职：一级警司至二级警员。

**Article 8** People's police officers holding administrative posts shall have the following police ranks commensurate with their post grades:  
(1) Full minister level: Commissioner General;  
(2) Deputy minister level: Deputy Commissioner General;  
(3) Full department (bureau) level: First-grade Police Supervisor to Second-grade Police Supervisor;  
(4) Deputy department (bureau) level: Second-grade Police Supervisor to Third-grade Police Supervisor;  
(5) Full division (bureau) level: Third-grade Police Supervisor to Second-grade Police Superintendent;  
(6) Deputy division (bureau) level: First-grade Police Superintendent to Third-grade Police Superintendent;  
(7) Full section (bureau) level: First-grade Police Superintendent to First-grade Police Inspector;  
(8) Deputy section (bureau) level: Second-grade Police Superintendent to Second-grade Police Inspector;  
(9) Section member (police chief) post: Third-grade Police Superintendent to Third-grade Police Inspector;  
(10) Clerk (police constable) post: First-grade Police Inspector to Second-grade Police Constable.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　担任专业技术职务的人民警察实行下列职务等级编制警衔：  
（一）高级专业技术职务：一级警监至二级警督；  
（二）中级专业技术职务：一级警督至二级警司；  
（三）初级专业技术职务：三级警督至一级警员。

**Article 9** People's police officers holding technical posts shall have the following police ranks commensurate with their post grades:  
(1) Senior technical post: First-grade Police Supervisor to Second-grade Police Superintendent;  
(2) Intermediate technical post: First-grade Police Superintendent to Second-grade Police Inspector;  
(3) Junior technical post: Third-grade Police Superintendent to First-grade Police Constable.

### 第三章　警衔的首次授予

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民警察警衔按照人民警察职务等级编制警衔授予。

**Article 10** The police ranks of the people's police shall be conferred in accordance with the police ranks commensurate with the post grades of the people's police.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　授予人民警察警衔，以人民警察现任职务、德才表现、担任现职时间和工作年限为依据。

**Article 11** The conferment of police ranks on the people's police shall be based on the current post, moral character and professional competence, duration in the current post, and length of service of the people's police officer.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　从学校毕业和从社会上招考录用担任人民警察的，或者从其他部门调任人民警察的，根据确定的职务，授予相应的警衔。

**Article 12** For those who graduate from schools and are recruited from society to serve as people's police officers, or who are transferred from other departments to serve as people's police officers, the corresponding police rank shall be conferred according to the determined post.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　首次授予人民警察警衔，按照下列规定的权限予以批准：  
（一）总警监、副总警监、一级警监、二级警监由国务院总理批准授予；  
（二）三级警监、警督由公安部部长批准授予；  
（三）警司由省、自治区、直辖市公安厅（局）厅（局）长批准授予；  
（四）警员由省、自治区、直辖市公安厅（局）政治部主任批准授予。  
公安部机关及其直属机构的警司、警员由公安部政治部主任批准授予。

**Article 13** The initial conferment of police ranks on the people's police shall be approved according to the following authorities:  
(1) Commissioner General, Deputy Commissioner General, First-grade Police Supervisor, and Second-grade Police Supervisor shall be approved and conferred by the Premier of the State Council;  
(2) Third-grade Police Supervisor and Police Superintendent shall be approved and conferred by the Minister of Public Security;  
(3) Police Inspector shall be approved and conferred by the director of the public security department (bureau) of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(4) Police Constable shall be approved and conferred by the director of the political department of the public security department (bureau) of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
Police Inspectors and Police Constables in the Ministry of Public Security and its subordinate units shall be approved and conferred by the director of the political department of the Ministry of Public Security.

### 第四章　警衔的晋级

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　二级警督以下的人民警察，在其职务等级编制警衔幅度内，根据本条规定的期限和条件晋级。  
晋级的期限：二级警员至一级警司，每晋升一级为三年；一级警司至一级警督，每晋升一级为四年。在职的人民警察在院校培训的时间，计算在警衔晋级的期限内。  
晋级的条件：（一）执行国家的法律、法规和政策，遵纪守法；（二）胜任本职工作；（三）联系群众，廉洁奉公，作风正派。  
晋级期限届满，经考核具备晋级条件的，应当逐级晋升；不具备晋级条件的，应当延期晋升。在工作中有突出功绩的，可以提前晋升。

**Article 14** People's police officers below the rank of Second-grade Police Superintendent shall be promoted within the range of police ranks commensurate with their post grades, according to the time limits and conditions provided in this Article.  
Time limits for promotion: from Second-grade Police Constable to First-grade Police Inspector, each promotion shall be three years; from First-grade Police Inspector to First-grade Police Superintendent, each promotion shall be four years. The time spent by a people's police officer in active service on training at an institution shall be counted in the time limit for police rank promotion.  
Conditions for promotion: (1) implement the laws, regulations, and policies of the State, and observe discipline and the law; (2) be competent for the duties of the post; (3) maintain ties with the masses, be honest and upright, and have a correct style of work.  
If the time limit for promotion expires and the people's police officer, after assessment, meets the conditions for promotion, the officer shall be promoted step by step; if the officer does not meet the conditions for promotion, the promotion shall be deferred. If the officer has made outstanding contributions in the work, the officer may be promoted early.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　一级警督以上的人民警察的警衔晋级，在职务等级编制警衔幅度内，根据其德才表现和工作实绩实行选升。

**Article 15** For people's police officers at or above the rank of First-grade Police Superintendent, promotion in police rank shall be selective within the range of police ranks commensurate with their post grades, based on their moral character and professional competence and work achievements.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民警察由于职务提升，其警衔低于新任职务等级编制警衔的最低警衔的，应当晋升至新任职务等级编制警衔的最低警衔。

**Article 16** If a people's police officer's police rank is lower than the minimum police rank of the police ranks commensurate with the new post grade due to post promotion, the officer's rank shall be promoted to the minimum police rank of the police ranks commensurate with the new post grade.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　警司晋升警督，警督晋升警监，经相应的人民警察院校培训合格后，方可晋升。

**Article 17** Promotion from Police Inspector to Police Superintendent and from Police Superintendent to Police Supervisor may only be made after passing training at the corresponding people's police institution.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民警察警衔晋级的批准权限适用第十三条批准权限的规定。警司、警员提前晋升的，由公安部政治部主任批准。

**Article 18** The approval authority for promotion of police ranks of the people's police shall be governed by the provisions on approval authority in Article 13. Early promotion of Police Inspectors and Police Constables shall be approved by the director of the political department of the Ministry of Public Security.

### 第五章　警衔的保留、降级、取消

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民警察离休、退休的，其警衔予以保留，但不得佩带标志。  
人民警察调离警察工作岗位或者辞职、退职的，其警衔不予保留。

**Article 19** People's police officers who retire or resign from active service shall have their police ranks retained, but the insignia shall not be worn.  
People's police officers who are transferred from the police work post or who quit or resign shall not have their police ranks retained.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民警察因不胜任现任职务被调任下级职务，其警衔高于新任职务等级编制警衔的最高警衔的，应当调整至新任职务等级编制警衔的最高警衔。调整警衔的批准权限与原警衔的批准权限相同。

**Article 20** If a people's police officer is transferred to a lower post due to incompetence for the current post, and the officer's police rank is higher than the highest police rank of the police ranks commensurate with the new post grade, the officer's police rank shall be adjusted to the highest police rank of the police ranks commensurate with the new post grade. The approval authority for adjustment of the police rank shall be the same as the approval authority for the original police rank.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民警察违犯警纪的，可以给予警衔降级的处分。警衔降级的批准权限与原警衔的批准权限相同。人民警察受警衔降级处分后，其警衔晋级的期限按照降级后的警衔等级重新计算。  
人民警察警衔降级不适用于二级警员。

**Article 21** People's police officers who violate police discipline may be given the sanction of demotion in police rank. The approval authority for demotion in police rank shall be the same as the approval authority for the original police rank. After a people's police officer is given the sanction of demotion in police rank, the time limit for promotion in police rank shall be recalculated based on the demoted police rank.  
Demotion in police rank shall not apply to Second-grade Police Constables.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民警察被开除公职的，其警衔相应取消。  
人民警察犯罪，被依法判处剥夺政治权利或者有期徒刑以上刑罚的，其警衔相应取消。  
离休、退休的人民警察犯罪的，适用前款的规定。

**Article 22** People's police officers who are discharged from public employment shall have their police ranks canceled accordingly.  
People's police officers who commit crimes and are sentenced according to law to deprivation of political rights or to fixed-term imprisonment or heavier punishment shall have their police ranks canceled accordingly.  
Where retired people's police officers commit crimes, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　国家安全部门、劳动改造劳动教养管理部门的人民警察，人民法院、人民检察院的司法警察的警衔工作适用本条例。  
国家安全部门、劳动改造劳动教养管理部门的人民警察警衔授予和晋级的批准权限，由国务院规定。  
司法警察警衔授予和晋级的批准权限，由最高人民法院、最高人民检察院参照本条例规定。  
公安部门、国家安全部门和劳动改造劳动教养管理部门中不担任人民警察职务的人员，不实行警衔制度。

**Article 23** These Regulations shall apply to the police rank work of the people's police officers of the State security departments and the departments of reform through labor and reeducation through labor, and of the judicial police officers of the People's Courts and People's Procuratorates.  
The approval authority for conferment and promotion of police ranks of the people's police officers of the State security departments and the departments of reform through labor and reeducation through labor shall be prescribed by the State Council.  
The approval authority for conferment and promotion of police ranks of judicial police officers shall be prescribed by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate with reference to these Regulations.  
The police rank system shall not be implemented for personnel in the public security departments, State security departments, and departments of reform through labor and reeducation through labor who do not hold the post of people's police officer.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民警察警衔标志的式样和佩带办法，由国务院制定。

**Article 24** The patterns and wearing methods of police rank insignia for the people's police shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本条例的实施办法由国务院制定。

**Article 25** The measures for the implementation of these Regulations shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例自公布之日起施行。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
