# 最高人民法院关于具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案件审理的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕4号  
**公布日期**：2023-04-17  
**生效日期**：2023-08-01

> 《最高人民法院关于具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案件审理的若干规定》已于2023年4月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1885次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年8月1日起施行。 2023年7月26日 最高人民法院 关于具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案 件审理的若干规定 法释〔2023〕4号 为依法妥善审理环境资源案件，规范和保障具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案件审判活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《中华人民共和国人民陪审员法》等法律的规定，结合环境资源案件特点和审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院审理的第一审环境资源刑事、民事、行政案件，符合人民陪审员法第十五条规定，且案件事实涉及复杂专门性问题的，由不少于一名具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理。 前款规定外的第一审环境资源案件，人民法院认为有必要的，可以由具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理。 第二条　符合下列条件的人民陪审员，为本规定所称具有专门知识的人民陪审员： （一）具有环境资源领域专门知识； （二）在环境资源行政主管部门、科研院所、高等院校、企业、社会组织等单位从业三年以上。 第三条　人民法院参与人民陪审员选任，可以根据环境资源审判活动需要，结合案件类型、数量等特点，协商司法行政机关确定一定数量具有专门知识的人民陪审员候选人。 第四条　具有专门知识的人民陪审员任期届满后，人民法院认为有必要的，可以商请本人同意后协商司法行政机关经法定程序再次选任。 第五条　需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加案件审理的，人民法院可以根据环境资源案件的特点和具有专门知识的人民陪审员选任情况，在符合专业需求的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理的第一审环境资源刑事、民事、行政案件，符合[人民陪审员法第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民陪审员法.md#art-15)规定，且案件事实涉及复杂专门性问题的，由不少于一名具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理。  
前款规定外的第一审环境资源案件，人民法院认为有必要的，可以由具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理。

**Article 1** : Where a People's Court adjudicates a first-instance criminal, civil, or administrative case concerning environmental resources that falls within the scope of Article 15 of the People's Assessors Law and the facts of the case involve complex specialized issues, no fewer than one people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge shall participate in the collegial panel's adjudication.  
For first-instance environmental resources cases not covered by the preceding paragraph, the People's Court may, if it deems necessary, allow people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the adjudication by the collegial panel.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　符合下列条件的人民陪审员，为本规定所称具有专门知识的人民陪审员：  
（一）具有环境资源领域专门知识；  
（二）在环境资源行政主管部门、科研院所、高等院校、企业、社会组织等单位从业三年以上。

**Article 2** : A people's assessor meeting the following conditions shall be a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge as referred to in these Provisions:  
(1) Possessing specialized knowledge in the field of environmental resources; and  
(2) Having been engaged in work for no less than three years in entities such as environmental resources administrative departments, scientific research institutes, institutions of higher education, enterprises, or social organizations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院参与人民陪审员选任，可以根据环境资源审判活动需要，结合案件类型、数量等特点，协商司法行政机关确定一定数量具有专门知识的人民陪审员候选人。

**Article 3** : When participating in the selection of people's assessors, the People's Court may, based on the needs of environmental resources adjudication activities and taking into account the types and quantity of cases, consult with the judicial administrative authority to determine a certain number of candidates for people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　具有专门知识的人民陪审员任期届满后，人民法院认为有必要的，可以商请本人同意后协商司法行政机关经法定程序再次选任。

**Article 4** : After the term of office of a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge expires, the People's Court may, if it deems it necessary, seek the assessor's consent and then consult with the judicial administrative authority to have the assessor reselected through legal procedures.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加案件审理的，人民法院可以根据环境资源案件的特点和具有专门知识的人民陪审员选任情况，在符合专业需求的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

**Article 5** : Where a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge is required to participate in the adjudication of a case, the People's Court may, based on the characteristics of the environmental resources case and the selection status of people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge, randomly select and confirm such an assessor from the list of people's assessors meeting the professional requirements.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　基层人民法院可以根据环境资源案件审理的需要，协商司法行政机关选任具有专门知识的人民陪审员。  
设立环境资源审判专门机构的基层人民法院，应当协商司法行政机关选任具有专门知识的人民陪审员。  
设立环境资源审判专门机构的中级人民法院，辖区内基层人民法院均未设立环境资源审判专门机构的，应当指定辖区内不少于一家基层人民法院协商司法行政机关选任具有专门知识的人民陪审员。

**Article 6** : A Primary People's Court may, based on the needs of adjudicating environmental resources cases, consult with the judicial administrative authority to select people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge.  
A Primary People's Court that has established a specialized adjudication body for environmental resources shall consult with the judicial administrative authority to select people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge.  
Where an Intermediate People's Court has established a specialized adjudication body for environmental resources but none of the Primary People's Courts within its jurisdiction have established such a body, the Intermediate People's Court shall designate no fewer than one Primary People's Court within its jurisdiction to consult with the judicial administrative authority to select people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　基层人民法院审理的环境资源案件，需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理的，组成不少于一名具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加的三人合议庭。  
基层人民法院审理的可能判处十年以上有期徒刑且社会影响重大的环境资源刑事案件，以及环境行政公益诉讼案件，需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理的，组成不少于一名具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加的七人合议庭。

**Article 7** : Where a Primary People's Court adjudicates an environmental resources case and requires a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the collegial panel, a three-person collegial panel shall be formed, with no fewer than one people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participating.  
Where a Primary People's Court adjudicates an environmental resources criminal case that may result in a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of no less than ten years and has significant social impact, or an environmental administrative public interest litigation case, and requires a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the collegial panel, a seven-person collegial panel shall be formed, with no fewer than one people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participating.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　中级人民法院审理的环境民事公益诉讼案件、环境行政公益诉讼案件、生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件以及其他具有重大社会影响的环境污染防治、生态保护、气候变化应对、资源开发利用、生态环境治理与服务等案件，需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理的，组成不少于一名具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加的七人合议庭。

**Article 8** : Where an Intermediate People's Court adjudicates an environmental civil public interest litigation case, an environmental administrative public interest litigation case, an ecological environmental damage compensation litigation case, or any other case concerning environmental pollution prevention, ecological protection, climate change response, resource development and utilization, or ecological environmental governance and services that has significant social impact, and requires a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the collegial panel, a seven-person collegial panel shall be formed, with no fewer than one people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participating.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实行环境资源案件跨区域集中管辖的中级人民法院审理第一审环境资源案件，需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理的，可以从环境资源案件集中管辖区域内基层人民法院具有专门知识的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

**Article 9** : Where an Intermediate People's Court exercising cross-regional centralized jurisdiction over environmental resources cases adjudicates a first-instance environmental resources case and requires a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the collegial panel, such an assessor may be randomly selected from the list of people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge of the Primary People's Courts within the area of centralized jurisdiction over environmental resources cases.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　铁路运输法院等没有对应同级人民代表大会的法院审理第一审环境资源案件，需要具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审理的，在其所在地级市辖区或案件管辖区域内基层人民法院具有专门知识的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

**Article 10** : Where a court such as a railway transport court that has no corresponding people's congress at the same level adjudicates a first-instance environmental resources case and requires a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge to participate in the collegial panel, such an assessor shall be randomly selected from the list of people's assessors possessing specialized knowledge of the Primary People's Courts within the district of the prefecture-level city where the court is located or within the area of case jurisdiction.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　符合法律规定的审判人员应当回避的情形，或所在单位与案件有利害关系的，具有专门知识的人民陪审员应当自行回避。当事人也可以申请具有专门知识的人民陪审员回避。

**Article 11** : Where a circumstance under which an adjudicator shall withdraw according to law exists, or where the unit to which a people's assessor belongs has an interest in the case, the people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge shall withdraw on his or her own initiative. A party may also apply for the withdrawal of a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　审判长应当依照[人民陪审员法第二十条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民陪审员法.md#art-20)的规定，对具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加的下列工作，重点进行指引和提示：  
（一）专门性事实的调查；  
（二）就是否进行证据保全、行为保全提出意见；  
（三）庭前会议、证据交换和勘验；  
（四）就是否委托司法鉴定，以及鉴定事项、范围、目的和期限提出意见；  
（五）生态环境修复方案的审查；  
（六）环境民事公益诉讼案件、生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件的调解、和解协议的审查。

**Article 12** : The presiding judge shall, in accordance with Article 20 of the People's Assessors Law, provide key guidance and instructions on the following work in which a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participates:  
(1) Investigation of specialized facts;  
(2) Providing opinions on whether to carry out preservation of evidence or preservation of acts;  
(3) Pretrial conferences, exchange of evidence, and inspection;  
(4) Providing opinions on whether to commission judicial expertise, and on the subject matter, scope, purpose, and time limit of the expertise;  
(5) Review of ecological environmental restoration plans; and  
(6) Review of mediation or settlement agreements in environmental civil public interest litigation cases and ecological environmental damage compensation litigation cases.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案件评议时，应当就案件事实涉及的专门性问题发表明确意见。  
具有专门知识的人民陪审员就该专门性问题发表的意见与合议庭其他成员不一致的，合议庭可以将案件提请院长决定是否提交审判委员会讨论决定。有关情况应当记入评议笔录。

**Article 13** : When a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participates in the deliberation of an environmental resources case, he or she shall express a clear opinion on the specialized issues involved in the facts of the case.  
If the opinion expressed by the people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge on such specialized issues is inconsistent with that of other members of the collegial panel, the collegial panel may submit the case to the presiding judge for a decision on whether to refer it to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision. The relevant circumstances shall be recorded in the deliberation minutes.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　具有专门知识的人民陪审员可以参与监督生态环境修复、验收和修复效果评估。

**Article 14** : A people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge may participate in overseeing the restoration of the ecological environment, the acceptance inspection, and the assessment of restoration effects.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　具有专门知识的人民陪审员参加环境资源案件的审理，本规定没有规定的，适用《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国人民陪审员法〉若干问题的解释》的规定。

**Article 15** : Where a people's assessor possessing specialized knowledge participates in the adjudication of environmental resources cases, and these Provisions do not provide otherwise, the provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of the People's Assessors Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定自2023年8月1日起施行。

**Article 16** : These Provisions shall come into force as of August 1, 2023.
