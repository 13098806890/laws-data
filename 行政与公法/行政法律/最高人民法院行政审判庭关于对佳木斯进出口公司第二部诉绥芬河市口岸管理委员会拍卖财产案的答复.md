# 最高人民法院行政审判庭关于对佳木斯进出口公司第二部诉绥芬河市口岸管理委员会拍卖财产案的答复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院行政审判庭  
关于对佳木斯进出口公司第二部诉绥芬河市  
口岸管理委员会拍卖财产案的答复  
1996年7月25日      〔1996〕行他字第14号  
黑龙江省高级人民法院：  
你院〔1996〕黑行他字第1号“关于佳木斯进出口公司第二部不服绥芬河市口岸管理委员会拍卖财产一案的请示”收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
进口货物在办妥报关手续前应根据[《海关法》第二十一条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国海关法.md#art-21)的规定，由海关监管，其他机关对进口货物无管理职权。具体案件请你院依据法律规定处理。

**Article 1** The Administrative Adjudication Tribunal of the Supreme People's Court  
Reply concerning the case of the Second Department of Jiamusi Import and Export Company v. Suifenhe  
Port Management Committee on auction of property  
July 25, 1996 [1996] Xingta Zi No. 14  
Higher People's Court of Heilongjiang Province:  
Your court's document No. [1996] Hei Xingta Zi No. 1, 'Request for instructions regarding the case of the Second Department of Jiamusi Import and Export Company refusing to accept the auction of property by Suifenhe Port Management Committee,' has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Import goods shall, before customs clearance procedures are completed, be under the supervision of the customs pursuant to the provisions of Article 21 of the Customs Law, and other organs shall have no authority to manage import goods. For this specific case, your court shall handle it in accordance with the provisions of the law.
