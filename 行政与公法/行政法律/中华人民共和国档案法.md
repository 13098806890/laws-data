# 中华人民共和国档案法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2020-06-20  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （1987年9月5日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十二次会议通过　根据1996年7月5日第八届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国档案法〉的决定》第一次修正　根据2016年11月7日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国对外贸易法〉等十二部法律的决定》第二次修正　2020年6月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十九次会议修订）

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强档案管理，规范档案收集、整理工作，有效保护和利用档案，提高档案信息化建设水平，推进国家治理体系和治理能力现代化，为中国特色社会主义事业服务，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of strengthening archives management, regulating the collection and arrangement of archives, effectively protecting and utilizing archives, improving the level of archives informatization, promoting the modernization of the national governance system and governance capacity, and serving the cause of socialism with Chinese characteristics.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　从事档案收集、整理、保护、利用及其监督管理活动，适用本法。  
本法所称档案，是指过去和现在的机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织以及个人从事经济、政治、文化、社会、生态文明、军事、外事、科技等方面活动直接形成的对国家和社会具有保存价值的各种文字、图表、声像等不同形式的历史记录。

**Article 2** The application of this Law covers activities such as the collection, arrangement, protection, utilization, and supervision and administration of archives.  
For the purposes of this Law, "archives" refers to historical records in various forms, such as texts, charts, audio and video recordings, which are directly formed by current and former state organs, armed forces, political parties, groups, enterprises, public institutions, other organizations, and individuals in their economic, political, cultural, social, ecological civilization, military, foreign affairs, science and technology, and other activities, and which have preservation value to the state and society.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　坚持中国共产党对档案工作的领导。各级人民政府应当加强档案工作，把档案事业纳入国民经济和社会发展规划，将档案事业发展经费列入政府预算，确保档案事业发展与国民经济和社会发展水平相适应。

**Article 3** Adherence to the leadership of the Communist Party of China over archives work. People's governments at all levels shall strengthen archives work, incorporate archives undertakings into the national economic and social development plans, include archives development funds in government budgets, and ensure that the development of archives undertakings is commensurate with the level of national economic and social development.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　档案工作实行统一领导、分级管理的原则，维护档案完整与安全，便于社会各方面的利用。

**Article 4** Archives work shall implement the principle of unified leadership and hierarchical management, maintain the integrity and security of archives, and facilitate their utilization by all sectors of society.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　一切国家机关、武装力量、政党、团体、企业事业单位和公民都有保护档案的义务，享有依法利用档案的权利。

**Article 5** All state organs, armed forces, political parties, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and citizens have the obligation to protect archives and the right to utilize archives according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持档案科学研究和技术创新，促进科技成果在档案收集、整理、保护、利用等方面的转化和应用，推动档案科技进步。  
国家采取措施，加强档案宣传教育，增强全社会档案意识。  
国家鼓励和支持在档案领域开展国际交流与合作。

**Article 6** The state encourages and supports scientific research and technological innovation in archives, promotes the transformation and application of scientific and technological achievements in the collection, arrangement, protection, and utilization of archives, and advances progress in archives science and technology.  
The state adopts measures to strengthen archives publicity and education, and enhance public awareness of archives.  
The state encourages and supports international exchanges and cooperation in the field of archives.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国家鼓励社会力量参与和支持档案事业的发展。  
对在档案收集、整理、保护、利用等方面做出突出贡献的单位和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 7** The state encourages social forces to participate in and support the development of archives undertakings.  
Units and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in the collection, arrangement, protection, and utilization of archives shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第二章　档案机构及其职责

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家档案主管部门主管全国的档案工作，负责全国档案事业的统筹规划和组织协调，建立统一制度，实行监督和指导。  
县级以上地方档案主管部门主管本行政区域内的档案工作，对本行政区域内机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的档案工作实行监督和指导。  
乡镇人民政府应当指定人员负责管理本机关的档案，并对所属单位、基层群众性自治组织等的档案工作实行监督和指导。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-11" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第11条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-12" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第12条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-13" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第13条引用">[3]</a></sup>

**Article 8** The national archives administrative department shall be in charge of archives work nationwide, responsible for overall planning and organization and coordination of archives undertakings, establishing a unified system, and exercising supervision and guidance.  
The local archives administrative departments at or above the county level shall be in charge of archives work within their respective administrative regions, and exercise supervision and guidance over archives work of state organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations within their administrative regions.  
Township people's governments shall designate personnel responsible for managing archives of their own organs, and exercise supervision and guidance over archives work of their subordinate units, grassroots self-governing organizations, etc.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当确定档案机构或者档案工作人员负责管理本单位的档案，并对所属单位的档案工作实行监督和指导。  
中央国家机关根据档案管理需要，在职责范围内指导本系统的档案业务工作。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-14" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** State organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall designate an archives institution or archives staff responsible for managing archives of their own units, and exercise supervision and guidance over archives work of their subordinate units.  
Central state organs, based on the needs of archives management, shall guide the archives work within their respective systems within the scope of their functions and duties.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中央和县级以上地方各级各类档案馆，是集中管理档案的文化事业机构，负责收集、整理、保管和提供利用各自分管范围内的档案。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-16" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第16条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 10** Various types of archives at the central level and at or above the county level are cultural institutions that centrally manage archives, responsible for collecting, arranging, preserving, and providing for the utilization of archives within their respective scopes of management.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家加强档案工作人才培养和队伍建设，提高档案工作人员业务素质。  
档案工作人员应当忠于职守，遵纪守法，具备相应的专业知识与技能，其中档案专业人员可以按照国家有关规定评定专业技术职称。

**Article 11** The state strengthens the training of archives personnel and the building of the archives workforce, and improves the professional quality of archives staff.  
Archives staff shall be loyal to their duties, abide by laws and disciplines, and possess the corresponding professional knowledge and skills. Archives professionals may be assessed for professional technical titles in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

### 第三章　档案的管理

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　按照国家规定应当形成档案的机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织，应当建立档案工作责任制，依法健全档案管理制度。

**Article 12** State organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations that, in accordance with state regulations, shall create archives, shall establish archives work responsibility systems and improve archives management systems according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　直接形成的对国家和社会具有保存价值的下列材料，应当纳入归档范围：  
（一）反映机关、团体组织沿革和主要职能活动的；  
（二）反映国有企业事业单位主要研发、建设、生产、经营和服务活动，以及维护国有企业事业单位权益和职工权益的；  
（三）反映基层群众性自治组织城乡社区治理、服务活动的；  
（四）反映历史上各时期国家治理活动、经济科技发展、社会历史面貌、文化习俗、生态环境的；  
（五）法律、行政法规规定应当归档的。  
非国有企业、社会服务机构等单位依照前款第二项所列范围保存本单位相关材料。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 13** The following materials directly formed that have preservation value to the state and society shall be included in the scope of archiving:  
(1) Materials reflecting the organizational evolution and main functional activities of state organs and groups;  
(2) Materials reflecting the main R&D, construction, production, operation, and service activities of state-owned enterprises and public institutions, as well as safeguarding the rights and interests of state-owned enterprises and public institutions and their employees;  
(3) Materials reflecting the urban and rural community governance and service activities of grassroots self-governing organizations;  
(4) Materials reflecting national governance activities, economic and scientific development, social and historical features, cultural customs, and ecological environment in various historical periods;  
(5) Materials that shall be archived under laws and administrative regulations.  
Non-state-owned enterprises, social service institutions, and other entities shall preserve relevant materials of their own units in accordance with the scope listed in item (2) of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　应当归档的材料，按照国家有关规定定期向本单位档案机构或者档案工作人员移交，集中管理，任何个人不得拒绝归档或者据为己有。  
国家规定不得归档的材料，禁止擅自归档。

**Article 14** Materials that shall be archived shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, be regularly transferred to the archives institution or archives staff of the unit for centralized management. No individual shall refuse to archive or keep them for personal possession.  
Materials that shall not be archived under state regulations are prohibited from being archived without authorization.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当按照国家有关规定，定期向档案馆移交档案，档案馆不得拒绝接收。  
经档案馆同意，提前将档案交档案馆保管的，在国家规定的移交期限届满前，该档案所涉及政府信息公开事项仍由原制作或者保存政府信息的单位办理。移交期限届满的，涉及政府信息公开事项的档案按照档案利用规定办理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-40" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第40条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 15** State organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, regularly transfer archives to archives, and archives shall not refuse to receive them.  
Where archives are transferred in advance to archives for preservation with the consent of the archives, before the expiration of the statutory transfer period, the matters concerning the disclosure of government information involved in such archives shall still be handled by the unit that originally produced or preserved the government information. Upon expiration of the transfer period, archives involving government information disclosure matters shall be handled in accordance with the provisions on the utilization of archives.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织发生机构变动或者撤销、合并等情形时，应当按照规定向有关单位或者档案馆移交档案。

**Article 16** Where there are circumstances such as organizational changes, dissolution, or merger of state organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, or other organizations, archives shall be transferred to the relevant units or archives in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　档案馆除按照国家有关规定接收移交的档案外，还可以通过接受捐献、购买、代存等方式收集档案。

**Article 17** In addition to receiving transferred archives in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state, archives may also collect archives through means such as accepting donations, purchases, and custodianship.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　博物馆、图书馆、纪念馆等单位保存的文物、文献信息同时是档案的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定，可以由上述单位自行管理。  
档案馆与前款所列单位应当在档案的利用方面互相协作，可以相互交换重复件、复制件或者目录，联合举办展览，共同研究、编辑出版有关史料。

**Article 18** Where cultural relics, documents, and information preserved by museums, libraries, memorial halls, and other units are also archives, these units may manage them on their own in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations.  
Archives and the units listed in the preceding paragraph shall cooperate with each other in the utilization of archives, and may exchange duplicates, copies, or catalogs with each other, jointly hold exhibitions, and jointly research, compile, and publish relevant historical materials.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　档案馆以及机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织的档案机构应当建立科学的管理制度，便于对档案的利用；按照国家有关规定配置适宜档案保存的库房和必要的设施、设备，确保档案的安全；采用先进技术，实现档案管理的现代化。  
档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织应当建立健全档案安全工作机制，加强档案安全风险管理，提高档案安全应急处置能力。

**Article 19** Archives, as well as archives institutions of state organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations, shall establish a scientific management system to facilitate the utilization of archives; configure warehouses and necessary facilities and equipment suitable for the preservation of archives in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state to ensure the security of archives; and adopt advanced technologies to realize the modernization of archives management.  
Archives and state organs, groups, enterprises, public institutions, and other organizations shall establish and improve archives security working mechanisms, strengthen archives security risk management, and enhance the emergency response capability for archives security.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　涉及国家秘密的档案的管理和利用，密级的变更和解密，应当依照有关保守国家秘密的法律、行政法规规定办理。

**Article 20** The management and utilization of archives involving state secrets, as well as the change and declassification of their classification levels, shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on the protection of state secrets.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　鉴定档案保存价值的原则、保管期限的标准以及销毁档案的程序和办法，由国家档案主管部门制定。  
禁止篡改、损毁、伪造档案。禁止擅自销毁档案。

**Article 21** The principles for determining the preservation value of archives, the standards for retention periods, and the procedures and methods for destroying archives shall be formulated by the competent national archives department.  
It shall be prohibited to alter, damage, or forge archives. It shall be prohibited to destroy archives without authorization.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　非国有企业、社会服务机构等单位和个人形成的档案，对国家和社会具有重要保存价值或者应当保密的，档案所有者应当妥善保管。对保管条件不符合要求或者存在其他原因可能导致档案严重损毁和不安全的，省级以上档案主管部门可以给予帮助，或者经协商采取指定档案馆代为保管等确保档案完整和安全的措施；必要时，可以依法收购或者征购。  
前款所列档案，档案所有者可以向国家档案馆寄存或者转让。严禁出卖、赠送给外国人或者外国组织。  
向国家捐献重要、珍贵档案的，国家档案馆应当按照国家有关规定给予奖励。

**Article 22** Where archives formed by non-state-owned enterprises, social service institutions, or individuals have significant preservation value to the state and society or shall be kept confidential, the owner of the archives shall properly preserve them. If the preservation conditions do not meet requirements or there are other reasons that may cause serious damage or insecurity to the archives, the archives administrative departments at or above the provincial level may provide assistance, or take measures through consultation, such as designating a state archives to preserve them on behalf of the owner, to ensure the integrity and security of the archives; where necessary, they may purchase or requisition the archives according to law.  
The archives listed in the preceding paragraph may be deposited or transferred by the owner to a state archives. It shall be strictly prohibited to sell or give them as gifts to foreigners or foreign organizations.  
Where a person donates important or valuable archives to the state, the state archives shall give a reward in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　禁止买卖属于国家所有的档案。  
国有企业事业单位资产转让时，转让有关档案的具体办法，由国家档案主管部门制定。  
档案复制件的交换、转让，按照国家有关规定办理。

**Article 23** It shall be prohibited to buy or sell archives owned by the state.  
Where assets of state-owned enterprises and institutions are transferred, the specific measures for transferring relevant archives shall be formulated by the competent national archives department.  
The exchange or transfer of duplicates of archives shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织委托档案整理、寄存、开发利用和数字化等服务的，应当与符合条件的档案服务企业签订委托协议，约定服务的范围、质量和技术标准等内容，并对受托方进行监督。  
受托方应当建立档案服务管理制度，遵守有关安全保密规定，确保档案的安全。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-28" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第28条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 24** Where archives, government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations entrust services such as archiving arrangement, deposit, development and utilization, and digitization, they shall conclude an entrustment agreement with a qualified archives service enterprise, stipulating the scope, quality, and technical standards of the services, and shall supervise the entrusted party.  
The entrusted party shall establish a management system for archives services, comply with relevant safety and confidentiality provisions, and ensure the security of the archives.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　属于国家所有的档案和[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定的档案及其复制件，禁止擅自运送、邮寄、携带出境或者通过互联网传输出境。确需出境的，按照国家有关规定办理审批手续。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-27" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第27条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 25** For archives owned by the state and the archives specified in Article 22 of this Law, as well as duplicates thereof, it shall be prohibited to transport, mail, carry out of the country, or transmit out of the country through the Internet without authorization. Where it is truly necessary to take them out of the country, the approval formalities shall be gone through in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家档案主管部门应当建立健全突发事件应对活动相关档案收集、整理、保护、利用工作机制。  
档案馆应当加强对突发事件应对活动相关档案的研究整理和开发利用，为突发事件应对活动提供文献参考和决策支持。

**Article 26** The competent national archives department shall establish and improve a working mechanism for the collection, arrangement, preservation, and utilization of archives related to emergency response activities.  
Archives shall strengthen the research, arrangement, development, and utilization of archives related to emergency response activities, so as to provide document reference and decision-making support for emergency response activities.

### 第四章　档案的利用和公布

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上各级档案馆的档案，应当自形成之日起满二十五年向社会开放。经济、教育、科技、文化等类档案，可以少于二十五年向社会开放；涉及国家安全或者重大利益以及其他到期不宜开放的档案，可以多于二十五年向社会开放。国家鼓励和支持其他档案馆向社会开放档案。档案开放的具体办法由国家档案主管部门制定，报国务院批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-31" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第31条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Archives kept in archives at or above the county level shall be opened to the public after twenty-five years from the date of their formation. Archives of economic, educational, scientific, technological, and cultural categories may be opened to the public in less than twenty-five years; archives involving national security or major interests, or which are otherwise not suitable for opening upon expiration, may be opened to the public after more than twenty-five years. The state encourages and supports other archives in opening archives to the public. The specific measures for opening archives shall be formulated by the competent national archives department and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　档案馆应当通过其网站或者其他方式定期公布开放档案的目录，不断完善利用规则，创新服务形式，强化服务功能，提高服务水平，积极为档案的利用创造条件，简化手续，提供便利。  
单位和个人持有合法证明，可以利用已经开放的档案。档案馆不按规定开放利用的，单位和个人可以向档案主管部门投诉，接到投诉的档案主管部门应当及时调查处理并将处理结果告知投诉人。  
利用档案涉及知识产权、个人信息的，应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定。

**Article 28** Archives shall regularly publish the catalogues of opened archives through their websites or other means, continuously improve utilization rules, innovate service forms, strengthen service functions, enhance service levels, actively create conditions for the utilization of archives, simplify procedures, and provide convenience.  
Units and individuals holding legal certificates may utilize archives that have been opened to the public. Where an archive fails to open and permit utilization in accordance with regulations, the unit or individual may file a complaint with the archives administrative department; the archives administrative department that receives the complaint shall promptly investigate and handle the matter and inform the complainant of the result.  
Where the utilization of archives involves intellectual property rights or personal information, the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations shall be complied with.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织以及公民根据经济建设、国防建设、教学科研和其他工作的需要，可以按照国家有关规定，利用档案馆未开放的档案以及有关机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织保存的档案。

**Article 29** Government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, other organizations, and citizens may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the state and based on the needs of economic construction, national defense construction, teaching and scientific research, and other work, utilize archives that have not been opened in archives as well as archives preserved by relevant government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　馆藏档案的开放审核，由档案馆会同档案形成单位或者移交单位共同负责。尚未移交进馆档案的开放审核，由档案形成单位或者保管单位负责，并在移交时附具意见。

**Article 30** The review of opening of archives in the custody of archives shall be the joint responsibility of the archive and the entity that formed or transferred the archives. For archives that have not yet been transferred to the archive, the review of opening shall be the responsibility of the entity that formed or preserves the archives, and the entity shall attach its opinions when transferring the archives.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　向档案馆移交、捐献、寄存档案的单位和个人，可以优先利用该档案，并可以对档案中不宜向社会开放的部分提出限制利用的意见，档案馆应当予以支持，提供便利。

**Article 31** Units and individuals that transfer, donate, or deposit archives with an archive may have priority in utilizing such archives, and may propose opinions on restricting the utilization of parts of the archives that are not suitable for public opening; the archive shall support such opinions and provide convenience.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　属于国家所有的档案，由国家授权的档案馆或者有关机关公布；未经档案馆或者有关机关同意，任何单位和个人无权公布。非国有企业、社会服务机构等单位和个人形成的档案，档案所有者有权公布。  
公布档案应当遵守有关法律、行政法规的规定，不得损害国家安全和利益，不得侵犯他人的合法权益。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/教育科技/中华人民共和国档案法实施条例.md#art-35" title="被《中华人民共和国档案法实施条例》第35条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** Archives owned by the state shall be published by the archive authorized by the state or the relevant organ; without the consent of the archive or the relevant organ, no unit or individual shall have the right to publish them. For archives formed by non-state-owned enterprises, social service institutions, or individuals, the owner of the archives shall have the right to publish them.  
The publication of archives shall comply with the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, shall not harm national security and interests, and shall not infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　档案馆应当根据自身条件，为国家机关制定法律、法规、政策和开展有关问题研究，提供支持和便利。  
档案馆应当配备研究人员，加强对档案的研究整理，有计划地组织编辑出版档案材料，在不同范围内发行。  
档案研究人员研究整理档案，应当遵守档案管理的规定。

**Article 33** Archives shall, based on their own conditions, provide support and convenience for state organs in formulating laws, regulations, and policies and in conducting research on relevant issues.  
Archives shall be staffed with researchers, strengthen the research and arrangement of archives, and systematically organize the compilation and publication of archival materials for distribution within different scopes.  
Archival researchers, when researching and arranging archives, shall comply with the provisions on archives management.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　国家鼓励档案馆开发利用馆藏档案，通过开展专题展览、公益讲座、媒体宣传等活动，进行爱国主义、集体主义、中国特色社会主义教育，传承发展中华优秀传统文化，继承革命文化，发展社会主义先进文化，增强文化自信，弘扬社会主义核心价值观。

**Article 34** The state encourages archives to develop and utilize their archival holdings, and to conduct education on patriotism, collectivism, and socialism with Chinese characteristics, inherit and develop the excellent traditional Chinese culture, carry forward the revolutionary culture, develop advanced socialist culture, enhance cultural confidence, and promote the core socialist values through activities such as thematic exhibitions, public lectures, and media publicity.

### 第五章　档案信息化建设

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　各级人民政府应当将档案信息化纳入信息化发展规划，保障电子档案、传统载体档案数字化成果等档案数字资源的安全保存和有效利用。  
档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织应当加强档案信息化建设，并采取措施保障档案信息安全。

**Article 35** People's governments at all levels shall incorporate archives informatization into their informatization development plans, ensuring the secure preservation and effective utilization of electronic archives, digitized results of traditional carrier archives, and other archival digital resources.  
Archives and government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations shall strengthen the construction of archives informatization and take measures to ensure the security of archival information.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　机关、团体、企业事业单位和其他组织应当积极推进电子档案管理信息系统建设，与办公自动化系统、业务系统等相互衔接。

**Article 36** Government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations shall actively promote the construction of electronic archives management information systems, and ensure their interconnection with office automation systems, business systems, and the like.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　电子档案应当来源可靠、程序规范、要素合规。  
电子档案与传统载体档案具有同等效力，可以以电子形式作为凭证使用。  
电子档案管理办法由国家档案主管部门会同有关部门制定。

**Article 37** Electronic archives shall have a reliable source, standardized procedures, and compliant elements.  
Electronic archives shall have the same effect as traditional carrier archives and may be used as evidence in electronic form.  
The measures for the management of electronic archives shall be formulated by the competent national archives department jointly with relevant departments.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　国家鼓励和支持档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织推进传统载体档案数字化。已经实现数字化的，应当对档案原件妥善保管。

**Article 38** The state encourages and supports archives, government organs, organizations, enterprises, institutions, and other organizations in promoting the digitization of traditional carrier archives. Where digitization has already been achieved, the original archives shall be properly preserved.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　电子档案应当通过符合安全管理要求的网络或者存储介质向档案馆移交。  
档案馆应当对接收的电子档案进行检测，确保电子档案的真实性、完整性、可用性和安全性。  
档案馆可以对重要电子档案进行异地备份保管。

**Article 39** Electronic archives shall be transferred to archives through networks or storage media that meet the requirements for security management.  
Archives shall inspect the electronic archives they receive to ensure the authenticity, integrity, usability, and security of the electronic archives.  
Archives may keep backup copies of important electronic archives in remote locations.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　档案馆负责档案数字资源的收集、保存和提供利用。有条件的档案馆应当建设数字档案馆。

**Article 40** Archives shall be responsible for the collection, preservation, and provision for utilization of archival digital resources. Archives that have the conditions shall build digital archives.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　国家推进档案信息资源共享服务平台建设，推动档案数字资源跨区域、跨部门共享利用。

**Article 41** The State shall promote the construction of an archives information resource sharing service platform, and advance the cross-regional and cross-departmental sharing and utilization of digital archives resources.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　档案主管部门依照法律、行政法规有关档案管理的规定，可以对档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织的下列情况进行检查：  
（一）档案工作责任制和管理制度落实情况；  
（二）档案库房、设施、设备配置使用情况；  
（三）档案工作人员管理情况；  
（四）档案收集、整理、保管、提供利用等情况；  
（五）档案信息化建设和信息安全保障情况；  
（六）对所属单位等的档案工作监督和指导情况。

**Article 42** The archives administrative department may, in accordance with the provisions of laws and administrative regulations on archives management, inspect the following circumstances of archives repositories, state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions and other organizations:  
(1) the implementation of the archives work responsibility system and management system;  
(2) the allocation and use of archives repositories, facilities and equipment;  
(3) the management of archives staff;  
(4) the collection, arrangement, preservation, provision and utilization of archives;  
(5) the construction of archives informatization and the guarantee of information security;  
(6) the supervision and guidance of the archives work of their subordinate units.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　档案主管部门根据违法线索进行检查时，在符合安全保密要求的前提下，可以检查有关库房、设施、设备，查阅有关材料，询问有关人员，记录有关情况，有关单位和个人应当配合。

**Article 43** When the archives administrative department conducts an inspection based on leads of illegal activities, it may, on the premise of meeting the requirements of security and confidentiality, inspect relevant repositories, facilities and equipment, consult relevant materials, question relevant personnel, and record relevant information; the relevant entities and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织发现本单位存在档案安全隐患的，应当及时采取补救措施，消除档案安全隐患。发生档案损毁、信息泄露等情形的，应当及时向档案主管部门报告。

**Article 44** Where an archives repository, a state organ, a social organization, an enterprise, a public institution or any other organization discovers that there is a potential risk to the security of archives in its own entity, it shall promptly take remedial measures to eliminate the potential security risk. Where any damage to archives or leakage of information occurs, it shall promptly report to the archives administrative department.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　档案主管部门发现档案馆和机关、团体、企业事业单位以及其他组织存在档案安全隐患的，应当责令限期整改，消除档案安全隐患。

**Article 45** Where the archives administrative department discovers that an archives repository, a state organ, a social organization, an enterprise, a public institution or any other organization has a potential risk to the security of archives, it shall order the entity to make corrections within a prescribed time limit to eliminate the potential security risk.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　任何单位和个人对档案违法行为，有权向档案主管部门和有关机关举报。  
接到举报的档案主管部门或者有关机关应当及时依法处理。

**Article 46** Any entity or individual shall have the right to report illegal acts concerning archives to the archives administrative department or the relevant authorities.  
The archives administrative department or the relevant authority that receives a report shall promptly handle it according to law.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　档案主管部门及其工作人员应当按照法定的职权和程序开展监督检查工作，做到科学、公正、严格、高效，不得利用职权牟取利益，不得泄露履职过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私。

**Article 47** The archives administrative department and its staff shall carry out supervision and inspection work in accordance with the statutory functions, powers and procedures, achieving scientific, fair, strict and efficient results. They shall not use their functions and powers to seek benefits, and shall not disclose state secrets, trade secrets or personal privacy learned during the performance of their duties.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　单位或者个人有下列行为之一，由县级以上档案主管部门、有关机关对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）丢失属于国家所有的档案的；  
（二）擅自提供、抄录、复制、公布属于国家所有的档案的；  
（三）买卖或者非法转让属于国家所有的档案的；  
（四）篡改、损毁、伪造档案或者擅自销毁档案的；  
（五）将档案出卖、赠送给外国人或者外国组织的；  
（六）不按规定归档或者不按期移交档案，被责令改正而拒不改正的；  
（七）不按规定向社会开放、提供利用档案的；  
（八）明知存在档案安全隐患而不采取补救措施，造成档案损毁、灭失，或者存在档案安全隐患被责令限期整改而逾期未整改的；  
（九）发生档案安全事故后，不采取抢救措施或者隐瞒不报、拒绝调查的；  
（十）档案工作人员玩忽职守，造成档案损毁、灭失的。

**Article 48** Where an entity or individual commits any of the following acts, the archives administrative department at or above the county level or the relevant authority shall, in accordance with the law, impose a sanction on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons:  
(1) losing archives under state ownership;  
(2) providing, transcribing, copying or publishing archives under state ownership without authorization;  
(3) selling or illegally transferring archives under state ownership;  
(4) tampering with, damaging, forging or destroying archives without authorization;  
(5) selling or donating archives to foreigners or foreign organizations;  
(6) failing to file archives or transfer archives on time in accordance with regulations, and refusing to make corrections after being ordered to do so;  
(7) failing to open to the public or provide and utilize archives in accordance with regulations;  
(8) knowingly having a potential risk to the security of archives but failing to take remedial measures, thereby causing damage to or loss of archives, or having a potential risk to the security of archives and being ordered to make corrections within a prescribed time limit but failing to do so within the time limit;  
(9) failing to take rescue measures, concealing and failing to report, or refusing to investigate after a security accident involving archives occurs;  
(10) archives staff neglecting their duties, thereby causing damage to or loss of archives.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　利用档案馆的档案，有[本法第四十八条](#art-48)第一项、第二项、第四项违法行为之一的，由县级以上档案主管部门给予警告，并对单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对个人处五百元以上五千元以下的罚款。  
档案服务企业在服务过程中有[本法第四十八条](#art-48)第一项、第二项、第四项违法行为之一的，由县级以上档案主管部门给予警告，并处二万元以上二十万元以下的罚款。  
单位或者个人有[本法第四十八条](#art-48)第三项、第五项违法行为之一的，由县级以上档案主管部门给予警告，没收违法所得，并对单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对个人处五百元以上五千元以下的罚款；并可以依照[本法第二十二条](#art-22)的规定征购所出卖或者赠送的档案。

**Article 49** Where a person who uses archives in an archives repository commits any of the illegal acts under items (1), (2) or (4) of Article 48 of this Law, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 500 but not more than RMB 5,000 on the individual.  
Where an archives service enterprise commits any of the illegal acts under items (1), (2) or (4) of Article 48 of this Law during the service process, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall give a warning, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 20,000 but not more than RMB 200,000.  
Where an entity or individual commits any of the illegal acts under item (3) or (5) of Article 48 of this Law, the archives administrative department at or above the county level shall give a warning, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 500 but not more than RMB 5,000 on the individual; and may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 22 of this Law, purchase by requisition the archives sold or donated.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　违反本法规定，擅自运送、邮寄、携带或者通过互联网传输禁止出境的档案或者其复制件出境的，由海关或者有关部门予以没收、阻断传输，并对单位处一万元以上十万元以下的罚款，对个人处五百元以上五千元以下的罚款；并将没收、阻断传输的档案或者其复制件移交档案主管部门。

**Article 50** Whoever, in violation of this Law, transports by any means, mails, carries or transmits through the internet the archives or their copies whose export is prohibited shall be subject to confiscation or blocking of transmission by the customs or the relevant authorities, and shall be imposed a fine of not less than RMB 10,000 but not more than RMB 100,000 on the entity, and a fine of not less than RMB 500 but not more than RMB 5,000 on the individual; and the confiscated or blocked archives or their copies shall be handed over to the archives administrative department.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；造成财产损失或者其他损害的，依法承担民事责任。

**Article 51** Whoever violates the provisions of this Law and constitutes a crime shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; whoever causes property loss or other damage shall bear civil liability according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　中国人民解放军和中国人民武装警察部队的档案工作，由中央军事委员会依照本法制定管理办法。

**Article 52** The archives work of the Chinese People's Liberation Army and the Chinese People's Armed Police Force shall be governed by the management measures formulated by the Central Military Commission in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　本法自2021年1月1日起施行。

**Article 53** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2021.
