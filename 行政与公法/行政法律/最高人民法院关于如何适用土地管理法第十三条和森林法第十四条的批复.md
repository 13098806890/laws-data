# 最高人民法院关于如何适用土地管理法第十三条和森林法第十四条的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：函〔1987〕2号  
**公布日期**：1987-07-31

---

### 正文

一、土地管理法第十三条、森林法第十四条规定，当事人之间发生的土地、林木、林地所有权和使用权争议由县级以上人民政府处理；当事人对人民政府处理不服的，可以向人民法院起诉。此类案件虽经人民政府作过处理，但其性质仍属民事权益纠纷，人民法院审理此类案件仍应以原争议双方为诉讼当事人。

**Article None** 1. Article 13 of the Land Administration Law and Article 14 of the Forest Law provide that disputes over ownership and use right of land, trees, and forestland between parties shall be handled by the people's government at or above the county level; if a party is not satisfied with the ruling of the people's government, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court. Although such cases have been handled by the people's government, their nature remains civil rights and interests disputes; when adjudicating such cases, the People's Court shall still take the original disputing parties as the parties to the litigation.

二、按照土地管理法第十三条第五款、森林法第十四条第四款的规定，在土地、林木、林地所有权和使用权争议解决以前，任何一方不得改变土地现状，不得砍伐有争议的林木，不得破坏土地上的附着物。因此在人民政府作出处理决定后，凡是当事人向人民法院起诉的，在人民法院审理期间，争议的标的物应当维持原状，所有权或者使用权的归属最终以人民法院的裁决为准。

**Article None** 2. According to the provisions of Article 13, paragraph 5 of the Land Administration Law and Article 14, paragraph 4 of the Forest Law, prior to the resolution of disputes over ownership and use right of land, trees, and forestland, neither party shall change the current state of the land, shall not fell the disputed trees, and shall not damage the attachments on the land. Therefore, after the people's government makes a ruling, if any party files a prosecution with the People's Court, during the period of adjudication by the People's Court, the subject matter in dispute shall be maintained in its original state, and the attribution of ownership or use right shall ultimately be determined by the ruling of the People's Court.

三、此类案件依法起诉到人民法院的，由民事审判庭受理。  
此复  
1987年7月31日

**Article None** 3. Where such cases are brought to the People's Court according to law, they shall be accepted by the Civil Adjudication Tribunal.
