<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院行政审判庭  
关于《呼和浩特市废旧金属管理  
暂行规定》的效力问题的答复  
1996年9月23日      〔1996〕行他字第23号  
内蒙古自治区高级人民法院：  
你院关于在审理案件中是否适用《呼和浩特市废旧金属管理暂行规定》的请示，经研究原则上同意你院意见，即：《呼和浩特市废旧金属管理暂行规定》中关于废旧金属出省区运输必须办理准运证，非法外运的由公安机关没收的规定是没有法律法规依据的。人民法院在审理此类案件中应以国务院有关规定为依据。

**Article 1** Reply of the Administrative Adjudication Tribunal of the Supreme People's Court  
on the Validity of the Interim Provisions of Hohhot Municipality on the Administration of Waste and Used Metals  
September 23, 1996 [1996] Xingta Zi No. 23  
Higher People's Court of Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region:  
Regarding your request for instructions on whether the Interim Provisions of Hohhot Municipality on the Administration of Waste and Used Metals shall be applied in the adjudication of cases, upon review, we in principle agree with your opinion, namely: the provisions in the Interim Provisions of Hohhot Municipality on the Administration of Waste and Used Metals which require that transportation of waste and used metals out of the province or region must obtain a transport permit, and that unlawful outward transportation shall be confiscated by the public security organ, have no legal or regulatory basis. In adjudicating such cases, the People's Court shall be based on the relevant provisions of the State Council.
