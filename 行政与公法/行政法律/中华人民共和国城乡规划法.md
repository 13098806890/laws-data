# 中华人民共和国城乡规划法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-23  
**生效日期**：2019-04-23

> （2007年10月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十次会议通过　根据2015年4月24日第十二届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十四次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国港口法〉等七部法律的决定》第一次修正　根据2019年4月23日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国建筑法〉等八部法律的决定》第二次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　城乡规划的制定 第三章　城乡规划的实施 第四章　城乡规划的修改 第五章　监督检查 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强城乡规划管理，协调城乡空间布局，改善人居环境，促进城乡经济社会全面协调可持续发展，制定本法。 第二条　制定和实施城乡规划，在规划区内进行建设活动，必须遵守本法。 本法所称城乡规划，包括城镇体系规划、城市规划、镇规划、乡规划和村庄规划。城市规划、镇规划分为总体规划和详细规划。详细规划分为控制性详细规划和修建性详细规划。 本法所称规划区，是指城市、镇和村庄的建成区以及因城乡建设和发展需要，必须实行规划控制的区域。规划区的具体范围由有关人民政府在组织编制的城市总体规划、镇总体规划、乡规划和村庄规划中，根据城乡经济社会发展水平和统筹城乡发展的需要划定。 第三条　城市和镇应当依照本法制定城市规划和镇规划。城市、镇规划区内的建设活动应当符合规划要求。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强城乡规划管理，协调城乡空间布局，改善人居环境，促进城乡经济社会全面协调可持续发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated for the purpose of strengthening the administration of urban and rural planning, coordinating the spatial layout of urban and rural areas, improving the human settlement environment, and promoting comprehensive, coordinated, and sustainable development of urban and rural economy and society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　制定和实施城乡规划，在规划区内进行建设活动，必须遵守本法。  
本法所称城乡规划，包括城镇体系规划、城市规划、镇规划、乡规划和村庄规划。城市规划、镇规划分为总体规划和详细规划。详细规划分为控制性详细规划和修建性详细规划。  
本法所称规划区，是指城市、镇和村庄的建成区以及因城乡建设和发展需要，必须实行规划控制的区域。规划区的具体范围由有关人民政府在组织编制的城市总体规划、镇总体规划、乡规划和村庄规划中，根据城乡经济社会发展水平和统筹城乡发展的需要划定。

**Article 2** The formulation and implementation of urban and rural plans and the construction activities within planned areas must comply with this Law.  
The term "urban and rural plans" as used in this Law includes the system of urban and rural system plans, urban plans, town plans, township plans, and village plans. Urban plans and town plans are divided into comprehensive plans and detailed plans. Detailed plans are divided into regulatory detailed plans and site-level detailed plans.  
The term "planned areas" as used in this Law refers to the built-up areas of cities, towns, and villages as well as areas that must be subject to planning control due to the needs of urban and rural construction and development. The specific scope of planned areas shall be delineated by the relevant people's government in the comprehensive urban plan, comprehensive town plan, township plan, and village plan prepared by it, based on the level of urban and rural economic and social development and the need for balanced urban and rural development.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　城市和镇应当依照本法制定城市规划和镇规划。城市、镇规划区内的建设活动应当符合规划要求。  
县级以上地方人民政府根据本地农村经济社会发展水平，按照因地制宜、切实可行的原则，确定应当制定乡规划、村庄规划的区域。在确定区域内的乡、村庄，应当依照本法制定规划，规划区内的乡、村庄建设应当符合规划要求。  
县级以上地方人民政府鼓励、指导前款规定以外的区域的乡、村庄制定和实施乡规划、村庄规划。

**Article 3** Cities and towns shall formulate urban plans and town plans in accordance with this Law. Construction activities within urban and town planned areas shall comply with the planning requirements.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the level of local rural economic and social development and in accordance with the principles of adapting measures to local conditions and being practical and feasible, determine the areas for which township plans and village plans shall be formulated. Townships and villages within the determined areas shall formulate plans in accordance with this Law, and construction in these townships and villages within the planned areas shall comply with the planning requirements.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall encourage and guide townships and villages in areas other than those specified in the preceding paragraph to formulate and implement township plans and village plans.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　制定和实施城乡规划，应当遵循城乡统筹、合理布局、节约土地、集约发展和先规划后建设的原则，改善生态环境，促进资源、能源节约和综合利用，保护耕地等自然资源和历史文化遗产，保持地方特色、民族特色和传统风貌，防止污染和其他公害，并符合区域人口发展、国防建设、防灾减灾和公共卫生、公共安全的需要。  
在规划区内进行建设活动，应当遵守土地管理、自然资源和环境保护等法律、法规的规定。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当根据当地经济社会发展的实际，在城市总体规划、镇总体规划中合理确定城市、镇的发展规模、步骤和建设标准。

**Article 4** The formulation and implementation of urban and rural plans shall follow the principles of balanced urban and rural development, rational layout, land conservation, intensive development, and planning before construction, improve the ecological environment, promote the conservation and comprehensive utilization of resources and energy, protect cultivated land and other natural resources and historical and cultural heritage, preserve local characteristics, ethnic features, and traditional styles, prevent pollution and other public hazards, and meet the needs of regional population development, national defense construction, disaster prevention and mitigation, and public health and public safety.  
Construction activities within planned areas shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations on land administration, natural resources, and environmental protection.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall, based on the actual situation of local economic and social development, reasonably determine the development scale, pace, and construction standards of cities and towns in the comprehensive urban plans and comprehensive town plans.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　城市总体规划、镇总体规划以及乡规划和村庄规划的编制，应当依据国民经济和社会发展规划，并与土地利用总体规划相衔接。

**Article 5** The preparation of comprehensive urban plans, comprehensive town plans, township plans, and village plans shall be based on the national economic and social development plan and shall be coordinated with the overall plan for land utilization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　各级人民政府应当将城乡规划的编制和管理经费纳入本级财政预算。

**Article 6** People's governments at all levels shall include the expenses for the preparation and administration of urban and rural plans in their fiscal budgets.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经依法批准的城乡规划，是城乡建设和规划管理的依据，未经法定程序不得修改。

**Article 7** Urban and rural plans approved according to law shall be the basis for urban and rural construction and planning administration, and shall not be modified without statutory procedures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　城乡规划组织编制机关应当及时公布经依法批准的城乡规划。但是，法律、行政法规规定不得公开的内容除外。

**Article 8** The authorities responsible for organizing the preparation of urban and rural plans shall promptly publish the urban and rural plans approved according to law, with the exception of contents that shall not be disclosed as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　任何单位和个人都应当遵守经依法批准并公布的城乡规划，服从规划管理，并有权就涉及其利害关系的建设活动是否符合规划的要求向城乡规划主管部门查询。  
任何单位和个人都有权向城乡规划主管部门或者其他有关部门举报或者控告违反城乡规划的行为。城乡规划主管部门或者其他有关部门对举报或者控告，应当及时受理并组织核查、处理。

**Article 9** Any unit or individual shall abide by the urban and rural plans approved and published according to law, subject themselves to planning administration, and have the right to inquire of the competent department of urban and rural planning whether construction activities concerning their interests comply with the planning requirements.  
Any unit or individual shall have the right to report or accuse any act violating urban and rural plans to the competent department of urban and rural planning or other relevant departments. The competent department of urban and rural planning or other relevant departments shall promptly accept such reports or accusations and organize verification and handling.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国家鼓励采用先进的科学技术，增强城乡规划的科学性，提高城乡规划实施及监督管理的效能。

**Article 10** The State encourages the adoption of advanced science and technology to enhance the scientific nature of urban and rural plans and to improve the efficiency of the implementation and supervision of urban and rural plans.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国务院城乡规划主管部门负责全国的城乡规划管理工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门负责本行政区域内的城乡规划管理工作。

**Article 11** The competent department of urban and rural planning under the State Council shall be responsible for the administration of urban and rural planning nationwide.  
The competent departments of urban and rural planning of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for the administration of urban and rural planning within their respective administrative regions.

### 第二章　城乡规划的制定

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国务院城乡规划主管部门会同国务院有关部门组织编制全国城镇体系规划，用于指导省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划的编制。  
全国城镇体系规划由国务院城乡规划主管部门报国务院审批。

**Article 12** The competent department of urban and rural planning under the State Council shall, together with the relevant departments under the State Council, organize the preparation of the national urban system plan for guiding the preparation of provincial urban system plans and comprehensive urban plans.  
The national urban system plan shall be submitted by the competent department of urban and rural planning under the State Council to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　省、自治区人民政府组织编制省域城镇体系规划，报国务院审批。  
省域城镇体系规划的内容应当包括：城镇空间布局和规模控制，重大基础设施的布局，为保护生态环境、资源等需要严格控制的区域。

**Article 13** The people's government of a province or autonomous region shall organize the preparation of the provincial urban system plan and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
The contents of the provincial urban system plan shall include: the spatial layout and scale control of cities and towns, the layout of major infrastructure, and areas that require strict control for the protection of the ecological environment, resources, etc.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　城市人民政府组织编制城市总体规划。  
直辖市的城市总体规划由直辖市人民政府报国务院审批。省、自治区人民政府所在地的城市以及国务院确定的城市的总体规划，由省、自治区人民政府审查同意后，报国务院审批。其他城市的总体规划，由城市人民政府报省、自治区人民政府审批。

**Article 14** The people's government of a city shall organize the preparation of the comprehensive urban plan.  
The comprehensive urban plan of a municipality directly under the Central Government shall be submitted by the people's government of that municipality to the State Council for approval. The comprehensive urban plan of a city which is the seat of the people's government of a province or autonomous region, or of a city designated by the State Council, shall be submitted by the people's government of the city to the State Council for approval after being examined and approved by the people's government of the province or autonomous region. The comprehensive urban plan of other cities shall be submitted by the people's government of the city to the people's government of the province or autonomous region for approval.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　县人民政府组织编制县人民政府所在地镇的总体规划，报上一级人民政府审批。其他镇的总体规划由镇人民政府组织编制，报上一级人民政府审批。

**Article 15** The people's government of a county shall organize the preparation of the comprehensive town plan of the town where the county people's government is located, and submit it to the people's government at the next higher level for approval. The comprehensive town plan of other towns shall be organized by the town people's government and submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　省、自治区人民政府组织编制的省域城镇体系规划，城市、县人民政府组织编制的总体规划，在报上一级人民政府审批前，应当先经本级人民代表大会常务委员会审议，常务委员会组成人员的审议意见交由本级人民政府研究处理。  
镇人民政府组织编制的镇总体规划，在报上一级人民政府审批前，应当先经镇人民代表大会审议，代表的审议意见交由本级人民政府研究处理。  
规划的组织编制机关报送审批省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划或者镇总体规划，应当将本级人民代表大会常务委员会组成人员或者镇人民代表大会代表的审议意见和根据审议意见修改规划的情况一并报送。

**Article 16** Before being submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval, the provincial urban system plan organized by the people's government of a province or autonomous region, and the comprehensive plan organized by the people's government of a city or county shall first be deliberated by the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level, and the deliberation opinions of the members of the standing committee shall be referred to the people's government at the same level for study and handling.  
Before being submitted to the people's government at the next higher level for approval, the comprehensive town plan organized by the town people's government shall first be deliberated by the town people's congress, and the deliberation opinions of the deputies shall be referred to the town people's government for study and handling.  
When submitting the provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan for approval, the authority organizing the preparation shall also submit the deliberation opinions of the members of the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level or the deputies of the town people's congress, together with the information on the revision of the plan based on such deliberation opinions.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　城市总体规划、镇总体规划的内容应当包括：城市、镇的发展布局，功能分区，用地布局，综合交通体系，禁止、限制和适宜建设的地域范围，各类专项规划等。  
规划区范围、规划区内建设用地规模、基础设施和公共服务设施用地、水源地和水系、基本农田和绿化用地、环境保护、自然与历史文化遗产保护以及防灾减灾等内容，应当作为城市总体规划、镇总体规划的强制性内容。  
城市总体规划、镇总体规划的规划期限一般为二十年。城市总体规划还应当对城市更长远的发展作出预测性安排。

**Article 17** The contents of a comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan shall include: the development layout of the city or town, functional zoning, land use layout, comprehensive transportation system, areas prohibited, restricted, or suitable for construction, various special plans, etc.  
The scope of the planned area, the scale of construction land within the planned area, land for infrastructure and public service facilities, water sources and water systems, basic farmland and green land, environmental protection, protection of natural and historical cultural heritage, and disaster prevention and mitigation shall be mandatory contents of a comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan.  
The planning period of a comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan shall generally be 20 years. A comprehensive urban plan shall also make predictive arrangements for the longer-term development of the city.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　乡规划、村庄规划应当从农村实际出发，尊重村民意愿，体现地方和农村特色。  
乡规划、村庄规划的内容应当包括：规划区范围，住宅、道路、供水、排水、供电、垃圾收集、畜禽养殖场所等农村生产、生活服务设施、公益事业等各项建设的用地布局、建设要求，以及对耕地等自然资源和历史文化遗产保护、防灾减灾等的具体安排。乡规划还应当包括本行政区域内的村庄发展布局。

**Article 18** Township plans and village plans shall be based on rural realities, respect the wishes of villagers, and reflect local and rural characteristics.  
The contents of a township plan or village plan shall include: the scope of the planned area, the land use layout and construction requirements for various constructions such as rural production and living service facilities including housing, roads, water supply, drainage, power supply, garbage collection, livestock and poultry breeding sites, and public welfare undertakings, as well as specific arrangements for the protection of cultivated land and other natural resources and historical cultural heritage, and disaster prevention and mitigation. A township plan shall also include the development layout of villages within its administrative area.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　城市人民政府城乡规划主管部门根据城市总体规划的要求，组织编制城市的控制性详细规划，经本级人民政府批准后，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会和上一级人民政府备案。

**Article 19** The competent department of urban and rural planning under the city people's government shall, in accordance with the requirements of the comprehensive urban plan, organize the preparation of regulatory detailed plans for the city, which shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level and the people's government at the next higher level for record after being approved by the people's government at the same level.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　镇人民政府根据镇总体规划的要求，组织编制镇的控制性详细规划，报上一级人民政府审批。县人民政府所在地镇的控制性详细规划，由县人民政府城乡规划主管部门根据镇总体规划的要求组织编制，经县人民政府批准后，报本级人民代表大会常务委员会和上一级人民政府备案。

**Article 20** The town people's government shall, in accordance with the requirements of the comprehensive town plan, organize the preparation of regulatory detailed plans for the town and submit them to the people's government at the next higher level for approval. The regulatory detailed plan for the town where the county people's government is located shall be organized by the competent department of urban and rural planning under the county people's government in accordance with the requirements of the comprehensive town plan, and after being approved by the county people's government, shall be submitted to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level and the people's government at the next higher level for record.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门和镇人民政府可以组织编制重要地块的修建性详细规划。修建性详细规划应当符合控制性详细规划。

**Article 21** The competent departments of urban and rural planning of city and county people's governments and town people's governments may organize the preparation of site-level detailed plans for important plots. Site-level detailed plans shall comply with regulatory detailed plans.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　乡、镇人民政府组织编制乡规划、村庄规划，报上一级人民政府审批。村庄规划在报送审批前，应当经村民会议或者村民代表会议讨论同意。

**Article 22** The people's government of a township or town shall organize the preparation of township plans and village plans and submit them to the people's government at the next higher level for approval. A village plan shall be discussed and agreed upon by the villagers' meeting or the villagers' representative meeting before being submitted for approval.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　首都的总体规划、详细规划应当统筹考虑中央国家机关用地布局和空间安排的需要。

**Article 23** The comprehensive plan and detailed plans of the capital shall take into overall consideration the needs of land use layout and spatial arrangement of central state organs.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　城乡规划组织编制机关应当委托具有相应资质等级的单位承担城乡规划的具体编制工作。  
从事城乡规划编制工作应当具备下列条件，并经国务院城乡规划主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府城乡规划主管部门依法审查合格，取得相应等级的资质证书后，方可在资质等级许可的范围内从事城乡规划编制工作：  
（一）有法人资格；  
（二）有规定数量的经相关行业协会注册的规划师；  
（三）有规定数量的相关专业技术人员；  
（四）有相应的技术装备；  
（五）有健全的技术、质量、财务管理制度。  
编制城乡规划必须遵守国家有关标准。

**Article 24** The authorities responsible for organizing the preparation of urban and rural plans shall entrust units with corresponding qualification grades to undertake the specific preparation of urban and rural plans.  
Units engaged in the preparation of urban and rural plans shall meet the following conditions and, after being examined and qualified according to law by the competent department of urban and rural planning under the State Council or the competent department of urban and rural planning of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, and obtaining a qualification certificate of the corresponding grade, may engage in the preparation of urban and rural plans within the scope permitted by the qualification grade:  
(1) having the status of a legal person;  
(2) having the prescribed number of planners registered with relevant trade associations;  
(3) having the prescribed number of relevant professional and technical personnel;  
(4) having corresponding technical equipment; and  
(5) having sound technical, quality, and financial management systems.  
The preparation of urban and rural plans must comply with the relevant national standards.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　编制城乡规划，应当具备国家规定的勘察、测绘、气象、地震、水文、环境等基础资料。  
县级以上地方人民政府有关主管部门应当根据编制城乡规划的需要，及时提供有关基础资料。

**Article 25** For the preparation of urban and rural plans, the necessary basic data such as survey, mapping, meteorology, seismology, hydrology, and environment as prescribed by the State shall be available.  
The relevant competent departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the needs of the preparation of urban and rural plans, provide relevant basic data in a timely manner.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　城乡规划报送审批前，组织编制机关应当依法将城乡规划草案予以公告，并采取论证会、听证会或者其他方式征求专家和公众的意见。公告的时间不得少于三十日。  
组织编制机关应当充分考虑专家和公众的意见，并在报送审批的材料中附具意见采纳情况及理由。

**Article 26** Before an urban or rural plan is submitted for approval, the authority organizing its preparation shall, according to law, announce the draft urban or rural plan and solicit opinions from experts and the public through feasibility study meetings, hearings, or other means. The period of such announcement shall not be less than 30 days.  
The authority organizing the preparation shall give full consideration to the opinions of experts and the public and shall attach the status of adoption of opinions and the reasons thereof to the materials submitted for approval.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划、镇总体规划批准前，审批机关应当组织专家和有关部门进行审查。

**Article 27** Before approving a provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan, the approving authority shall organize experts and relevant departments to conduct a review.

### 第三章　城乡规划的实施

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　地方各级人民政府应当根据当地经济社会发展水平，量力而行，尊重群众意愿，有计划、分步骤地组织实施城乡规划。

**Article 28** Local people's governments at all levels shall, in light of the local level of economic and social development, act within their capabilities, respect the wishes of the masses, and organize the implementation of urban and rural plans in a planned and phased manner.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　城市的建设和发展，应当优先安排基础设施以及公共服务设施的建设，妥善处理新区开发与旧区改建的关系，统筹兼顾进城务工人员生活和周边农村经济社会发展、村民生产与生活的需要。  
镇的建设和发展，应当结合农村经济社会发展和产业结构调整，优先安排供水、排水、供电、供气、道路、通信、广播电视等基础设施和学校、卫生院、文化站、幼儿园、福利院等公共服务设施的建设，为周边农村提供服务。  
乡、村庄的建设和发展，应当因地制宜、节约用地，发挥村民自治组织的作用，引导村民合理进行建设，改善农村生产、生活条件。

**Article 29** In the construction and development of cities, priority shall be given to the construction of infrastructure and public service facilities, the relationship between the development of new areas and the redevelopment of old areas shall be properly handled, and the needs of migrant workers for life and the needs of the surrounding rural economic and social development and villagers' production and life shall be taken into overall consideration.  
In the construction and development of towns, in light of rural economic and social development and industrial restructuring, priority shall be given to the construction of infrastructure such as water supply, drainage, power supply, gas supply, roads, communications, radio and television, and public service facilities such as schools, health centers, cultural stations, kindergartens, and welfare homes, so as to provide services to surrounding rural areas.  
In the construction and development of townships and villages, measures shall be adapted to local conditions, land shall be conserved, the role of villagers' self-governing organizations shall be brought into play, villagers shall be guided to carry out construction rationally, and rural production and living conditions shall be improved.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　城市新区的开发和建设，应当合理确定建设规模和时序，充分利用现有市政基础设施和公共服务设施，严格保护自然资源和生态环境，体现地方特色。  
在城市总体规划、镇总体规划确定的建设用地范围以外，不得设立各类开发区和城市新区。

**Article 30** In the development and construction of new urban areas, the construction scale and sequence shall be reasonably determined, existing municipal infrastructure and public service facilities shall be fully utilized, natural resources and the ecological environment shall be strictly protected, and local characteristics shall be reflected.  
No development zones or new urban areas of any type shall be established beyond the scope of construction land determined in the comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　旧城区的改建，应当保护历史文化遗产和传统风貌，合理确定拆迁和建设规模，有计划地对危房集中、基础设施落后等地段进行改建。  
历史文化名城、名镇、名村的保护以及受保护建筑物的维护和使用，应当遵守有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定。

**Article 31** In the redevelopment of old urban areas, historical and cultural heritage and traditional styles shall be protected, the scale of demolition and construction shall be reasonably determined, and areas with concentrated dilapidated houses and backward infrastructure shall be redeveloped in a planned manner.  
The protection of famous historical and cultural cities, towns, and villages, as well as the maintenance and use of protected buildings, shall comply with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　城乡建设和发展，应当依法保护和合理利用风景名胜资源，统筹安排风景名胜区及周边乡、镇、村庄的建设。  
风景名胜区的规划、建设和管理，应当遵守有关法律、行政法规和国务院的规定。

**Article 32** In urban and rural construction and development, scenic and historical resources shall be protected and rationally utilized according to law, and the construction of scenic and historical areas and surrounding townships, towns, and villages shall be coordinated.  
The planning, construction, and administration of scenic and historical areas shall comply with relevant laws, administrative regulations, and the provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　城市地下空间的开发和利用，应当与经济和技术发展水平相适应，遵循统筹安排、综合开发、合理利用的原则，充分考虑防灾减灾、人民防空和通信等需要，并符合城市规划，履行规划审批手续。

**Article 33** The development and utilization of urban underground space shall be commensurate with the level of economic and technological development, follow the principles of overall arrangement, comprehensive development, and rational utilization, give full consideration to the needs of disaster prevention and mitigation, civil air defense, and communications, comply with urban plans, and fulfill the procedures for planning approval.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　城市、县、镇人民政府应当根据城市总体规划、镇总体规划、土地利用总体规划和年度计划以及国民经济和社会发展规划，制定近期建设规划，报总体规划审批机关备案。  
近期建设规划应当以重要基础设施、公共服务设施和中低收入居民住房建设以及生态环境保护为重点内容，明确近期建设的时序、发展方向和空间布局。近期建设规划的规划期限为五年。

**Article 34** The people's government of a city, county, or town shall, in accordance with the comprehensive urban plan, comprehensive town plan, overall land utilization plan and annual plan, and the national economic and social development plan, formulate a short-term construction plan and report it to the authority that approved the comprehensive plan for record.  
A short-term construction plan shall take the construction of important infrastructure, public service facilities, and housing for low- and middle-income residents, as well as ecological environmental protection, as its key contents, and shall specify the sequence, development direction, and spatial layout of short-term construction. The planning period of a short-term construction plan shall be five years.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　城乡规划确定的铁路、公路、港口、机场、道路、绿地、输配电设施及输电线路走廊、通信设施、广播电视设施、管道设施、河道、水库、水源地、自然保护区、防汛通道、消防通道、核电站、垃圾填埋场及焚烧厂、污水处理厂和公共服务设施的用地以及其他需要依法保护的用地，禁止擅自改变用途。

**Article 35** The use of land determined by urban and rural plans for railways, highways, ports, airports, roads, green spaces, power transmission and distribution facilities and transmission line corridors, communication facilities, radio and television facilities, pipeline facilities, waterways, reservoirs, water sources, nature reserves, flood control passages, fire fighting passages, nuclear power stations, landfill sites and incineration plants, sewage treatment plants, and public service facilities, as well as other land that needs to be protected according to law, is prohibited from being changed without authorization.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　按照国家规定需要有关部门批准或者核准的建设项目，以划拨方式提供国有土地使用权的，建设单位在报送有关部门批准或者核准前，应当向城乡规划主管部门申请核发选址意见书。  
前款规定以外的建设项目不需要申请选址意见书。

**Article 36** For a construction project that requires approval or verification by relevant departments according to State regulations, where the right to use State-owned land is provided by allocation, the construction unit shall, before submitting the project to the relevant department for approval or verification, apply to the competent department of urban and rural planning for the issuance of a site selection opinion letter.  
Construction projects other than those specified in the preceding paragraph need not apply for a site selection opinion letter.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　在城市、镇规划区内以划拨方式提供国有土地使用权的建设项目，经有关部门批准、核准、备案后，建设单位应当向城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门提出建设用地规划许可申请，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门依据控制性详细规划核定建设用地的位置、面积、允许建设的范围，核发建设用地规划许可证。  
建设单位在取得建设用地规划许可证后，方可向县级以上地方人民政府土地主管部门申请用地，经县级以上人民政府审批后，由土地主管部门划拨土地。

**Article 37** For a construction project within an urban or town planned area where the right to use State-owned land is provided by allocation, after being approved, verified, or filed by the relevant department, the construction unit shall apply to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government for a permit for planned use of construction land. The competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall, based on the regulatory detailed plan, verify the location, area, and scope of permitted construction of the construction land, and issue a permit for planned use of construction land.  
Only after obtaining the permit for planned use of construction land may the construction unit apply to the land department of the local people's government at or above the county level for land use; after being approved by the people's government at or above the county level, the land department shall allocate the land.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　在城市、镇规划区内以出让方式提供国有土地使用权的，在国有土地使用权出让前，城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门应当依据控制性详细规划，提出出让地块的位置、使用性质、开发强度等规划条件，作为国有土地使用权出让合同的组成部分。未确定规划条件的地块，不得出让国有土地使用权。  
以出让方式取得国有土地使用权的建设项目，建设单位在取得建设项目的批准、核准、备案文件和签订国有土地使用权出让合同后，向城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门领取建设用地规划许可证。  
城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门不得在建设用地规划许可证中，擅自改变作为国有土地使用权出让合同组成部分的规划条件。

**Article 38** Where the right to use State-owned land within an urban or town planned area is provided by way of transfer, before the transfer of the right to use State-owned land, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall, based on the regulatory detailed plan, propose planning conditions such as the location, use nature, and development intensity of the parcel to be transferred, which shall be part of the contract for the transfer of the right to use State-owned land. The right to use State-owned land of a parcel for which no planning conditions have been determined shall not be transferred.  
For a construction project that has obtained the right to use State-owned land by way of transfer, after obtaining the approval, verification, or filing documents for the construction project and signing the contract for the transfer of the right to use State-owned land, the construction unit shall obtain a permit for planned use of construction land from the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government.  
The competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall not, in the permit for planned use of construction land, arbitrarily change the planning conditions that are part of the contract for the transfer of the right to use State-owned land.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　规划条件未纳入国有土地使用权出让合同的，该国有土地使用权出让合同无效；对未取得建设用地规划许可证的建设单位批准用地的，由县级以上人民政府撤销有关批准文件；占用土地的，应当及时退回；给当事人造成损失的，应当依法给予赔偿。

**Article 39** If the planning conditions are not included in the contract for the transfer of the right to use State-owned land, such contract shall be void; if land use is approved for a construction unit that has not obtained a permit for planned use of construction land, the relevant approval document shall be revoked by the people's government at or above the county level; if the land has been occupied, it shall be returned in a timely manner; if losses are caused to the party, compensation shall be paid according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　在城市、镇规划区内进行建筑物、构筑物、道路、管线和其他工程建设的，建设单位或者个人应当向城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的镇人民政府申请办理建设工程规划许可证。  
申请办理建设工程规划许可证，应当提交使用土地的有关证明文件、建设工程设计方案等材料。需要建设单位编制修建性详细规划的建设项目，还应当提交修建性详细规划。对符合控制性详细规划和规划条件的，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的镇人民政府核发建设工程规划许可证。  
城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府确定的镇人民政府应当依法将经审定的修建性详细规划、建设工程设计方案的总平面图予以公布。

**Article 40** For the construction of buildings, structures, roads, pipelines, and other engineering projects within an urban or town planned area, the construction unit or individual shall apply to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government or the town people's government designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for a permit for planned construction engineering.  
To apply for a permit for planned construction engineering, relevant documents proving land use, the design plan for the construction project, and other materials shall be submitted. For a construction project for which the construction unit is required to prepare a site-level detailed plan, such site-level detailed plan shall also be submitted. Where the regulatory detailed plan and planning conditions are met, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government or the town people's government designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue a permit for planned construction engineering.  
The competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government or the town people's government designated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, according to law, publish the approved site-level detailed plan and the general layout plan of the construction project design plan.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　在乡、村庄规划区内进行乡镇企业、乡村公共设施和公益事业建设的，建设单位或者个人应当向乡、镇人民政府提出申请，由乡、镇人民政府报城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门核发乡村建设规划许可证。  
在乡、村庄规划区内使用原有宅基地进行农村村民住宅建设的规划管理办法，由省、自治区、直辖市制定。  
在乡、村庄规划区内进行乡镇企业、乡村公共设施和公益事业建设以及农村村民住宅建设，不得占用农用地；确需占用农用地的，应当依照《中华人民共和国土地管理法》有关规定办理农用地转用审批手续后，由城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门核发乡村建设规划许可证。  
建设单位或者个人在取得乡村建设规划许可证后，方可办理用地审批手续。

**Article 41** For the construction of township enterprises, rural public facilities, and public welfare undertakings within a township or village planned area, the construction unit or individual shall submit an application to the township or town people's government, which shall report it to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government for the issuance of a permit for rural construction planning.  
The planning and administration measures for the construction of rural villagers' houses on existing homesteads within a township or village planned area shall be formulated by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
For the construction of township enterprises, rural public facilities, and public welfare undertakings, as well as the construction of rural villagers' houses within a township or village planned area, agricultural land shall not be occupied; where it is truly necessary to occupy agricultural land, after going through the formalities for the conversion of agricultural land use in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall issue a permit for rural construction planning.  
Only after obtaining the permit for rural construction planning may the construction unit or individual go through the formalities for land use approval.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　城乡规划主管部门不得在城乡规划确定的建设用地范围以外作出规划许可。

**Article 42** The competent department of urban and rural planning shall not make planning permits beyond the scope of construction land determined by urban and rural plans.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　建设单位应当按照规划条件进行建设；确需变更的，必须向城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门提出申请。变更内容不符合控制性详细规划的，城乡规划主管部门不得批准。城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门应当及时将依法变更后的规划条件通报同级土地主管部门并公示。  
建设单位应当及时将依法变更后的规划条件报有关人民政府土地主管部门备案。

**Article 43** Construction units shall carry out construction in accordance with the planning conditions; if changes are truly necessary, they must submit an application to the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government. If the content of the change does not comply with the regulatory detailed plan, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall not approve it. The competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall promptly notify the land department at the same level of the planning conditions changed according to law and publicize them.  
The construction unit shall promptly report the planning conditions changed according to law to the land department of the relevant people's government for record.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　在城市、镇规划区内进行临时建设的，应当经城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门批准。临时建设影响近期建设规划或者控制性详细规划的实施以及交通、市容、安全等的，不得批准。  
临时建设应当在批准的使用期限内自行拆除。  
临时建设和临时用地规划管理的具体办法，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府制定。

**Article 44** For temporary construction within an urban or town planned area, approval from the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government shall be obtained. If the temporary construction affects the implementation of the short-term construction plan or regulatory detailed plan, or affects traffic, city appearance, safety, etc., approval shall not be granted.  
Temporary construction shall be demolished by the builder within the approved period of use.  
Specific measures for the planning administration of temporary construction and temporary land use shall be formulated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门按照国务院规定对建设工程是否符合规划条件予以核实。未经核实或者经核实不符合规划条件的，建设单位不得组织竣工验收。  
建设单位应当在竣工验收后六个月内向城乡规划主管部门报送有关竣工验收资料。

**Article 45** The competent department of urban and rural planning of a local people's government at or above the county level shall, in accordance with the regulations of the State Council, verify whether a construction project complies with the planning conditions. If it has not been verified or the verification shows non-compliance with the planning conditions, the construction unit shall not organize the completion acceptance.  
The construction unit shall, within six months after the completion acceptance, submit relevant completion acceptance materials to the competent department of urban and rural planning.

### 第四章　城乡规划的修改

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划、镇总体规划的组织编制机关，应当组织有关部门和专家定期对规划实施情况进行评估，并采取论证会、听证会或者其他方式征求公众意见。组织编制机关应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会、镇人民代表大会和原审批机关提出评估报告并附具征求意见的情况。

**Article 46** The authorities responsible for organizing the preparation of a provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan shall organize relevant departments and experts to regularly evaluate the implementation of the plan, and solicit public opinions through feasibility study meetings, hearings, or other means. The authority organizing the preparation shall submit an evaluation report to the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level, the town people's congress, and the original approving authority, together with information on the solicitation of opinions.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　有下列情形之一的，组织编制机关方可按照规定的权限和程序修改省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划、镇总体规划：  
（一）上级人民政府制定的城乡规划发生变更，提出修改规划要求的；  
（二）行政区划调整确需修改规划的；  
（三）因国务院批准重大建设工程确需修改规划的；  
（四）经评估确需修改规划的；  
（五）城乡规划的审批机关认为应当修改规划的其他情形。  
修改省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划、镇总体规划前，组织编制机关应当对原规划的实施情况进行总结，并向原审批机关报告；修改涉及城市总体规划、镇总体规划强制性内容的，应当先向原审批机关提出专题报告，经同意后，方可编制修改方案。  
修改后的省域城镇体系规划、城市总体规划、镇总体规划，应当依照[本法第十三条](#art-13)、第十四条、第十五条和第十六条规定的审批程序报批。

**Article 47** Only under any of the following circumstances may the authority organizing the preparation modify a provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan in accordance with the prescribed authority and procedures:  
(1) a change occurs in an urban or rural plan formulated by the people's government at a higher level that requires modification of the plan;  
(2) an adjustment in administrative divisions truly necessitates modification of the plan;  
(3) a major construction project approved by the State Council truly necessitates modification of the plan;  
(4) evaluation truly necessitates modification of the plan; or  
(5) other circumstances under which the approving authority of the urban or rural plan considers modification necessary.  
Before modifying a provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan, the authority organizing the preparation shall summarize the implementation of the original plan and report it to the original approving authority; where the modification involves mandatory contents of the comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan, a special report shall first be submitted to the original approving authority, and the modification plan may be prepared only after obtaining its consent.  
A modified provincial urban system plan, comprehensive urban plan, or comprehensive town plan shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the approval procedures prescribed in Articles 13, 14, 15, and 16 of this Law.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　修改控制性详细规划的，组织编制机关应当对修改的必要性进行论证，征求规划地段内利害关系人的意见，并向原审批机关提出专题报告，经原审批机关同意后，方可编制修改方案。修改后的控制性详细规划，应当依照[本法第十九条](#art-19)、第二十条规定的审批程序报批。控制性详细规划修改涉及城市总体规划、镇总体规划的强制性内容的，应当先修改总体规划。  
修改乡规划、村庄规划的，应当依照[本法第二十二条](#art-22)规定的审批程序报批。

**Article 48** Where a regulatory detailed plan is to be modified, the authority organizing the preparation shall demonstrate the necessity of the modification, solicit opinions of interested parties within the planned area, submit a special report to the original approving authority, and may prepare a modification plan only after obtaining the consent of the original approving authority. The modified regulatory detailed plan shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the approval procedures prescribed in Articles 19 and 20 of this Law. Where the modification of the regulatory detailed plan involves mandatory contents of the comprehensive urban plan or comprehensive town plan, the comprehensive plan shall be modified first.  
Where a township plan or village plan is to be modified, it shall be submitted for approval in accordance with the approval procedures prescribed in Article 22 of this Law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　城市、县、镇人民政府修改近期建设规划的，应当将修改后的近期建设规划报总体规划审批机关备案。

**Article 49** Where a city, county, or town people's government modifies a short-term construction plan, it shall report the modified short-term construction plan to the authority that approved the comprehensive plan for record.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　在选址意见书、建设用地规划许可证、建设工程规划许可证或者乡村建设规划许可证发放后，因依法修改城乡规划给被许可人合法权益造成损失的，应当依法给予补偿。  
经依法审定的修建性详细规划、建设工程设计方案的总平面图不得随意修改；确需修改的，城乡规划主管部门应当采取听证会等形式，听取利害关系人的意见；因修改给利害关系人合法权益造成损失的，应当依法给予补偿。

**Article 50** Where, after the issuance of a site selection opinion letter, a permit for planned use of construction land, a permit for planned construction engineering, or a permit for rural construction planning, losses are caused to the legitimate rights and interests of the licensee due to the modification of an urban or rural plan according to law, compensation shall be given according to law.  
An approved site-level detailed plan or the general layout plan of a construction project design plan shall not be modified at will; if modification is truly necessary, the competent department of urban and rural planning shall hold hearings or other forms to hear the opinions of interested parties; if losses are caused to the legitimate rights and interests of interested parties due to the modification, compensation shall be given according to law.

### 第五章　监督检查

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　县级以上人民政府及其城乡规划主管部门应当加强对城乡规划编制、审批、实施、修改的监督检查。

**Article 51** The people's governments at or above the county level and their competent departments of urban and rural planning shall strengthen supervision and inspection over the formulation, approval, implementation, and modification of urban and rural planning.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　地方各级人民政府应当向本级人民代表大会常务委员会或者乡、镇人民代表大会报告城乡规划的实施情况，并接受监督。

**Article 52** Local people's governments at various levels shall report to the standing committees of the people's congresses at the corresponding level or to the township or town people's congresses on the implementation of urban and rural planning, and shall accept supervision.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　县级以上人民政府城乡规划主管部门对城乡规划的实施情况进行监督检查，有权采取以下措施：  
（一）要求有关单位和人员提供与监督事项有关的文件、资料，并进行复制；  
（二）要求有关单位和人员就监督事项涉及的问题作出解释和说明，并根据需要进入现场进行勘测；  
（三）责令有关单位和人员停止违反有关城乡规划的法律、法规的行为。  
城乡规划主管部门的工作人员履行前款规定的监督检查职责，应当出示执法证件。被监督检查的单位和人员应当予以配合，不得妨碍和阻挠依法进行的监督检查活动。

**Article 53** The competent departments of urban and rural planning of the people's governments at or above the county level shall conduct supervision and inspection over the implementation of urban and rural planning, and shall have the power to take the following measures:  
(1) to require relevant entities and individuals to provide documents and materials relating to the matters under supervision, and to make copies thereof;  
(2) to require relevant entities and individuals to make explanations and clarifications on the issues involved in the matters under supervision, and to enter the site for survey as necessary;  
(3) to order relevant entities and individuals to cease acts that violate the laws and regulations on urban and rural planning.  
The staff members of the competent department of urban and rural planning performing the duties of supervision and inspection as prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall produce their law enforcement credentials. The entities and individuals under supervision and inspection shall cooperate and shall not obstruct or impede the supervision and inspection activities conducted according to law.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　监督检查情况和处理结果应当依法公开，供公众查阅和监督。

**Article 54** The supervision and inspection information and the handling results shall be made public according to law for public inspection and supervision.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　城乡规划主管部门在查处违反本法规定的行为时，发现国家机关工作人员依法应当给予行政处分的，应当向其任免机关或者监察机关提出处分建议。

**Article 55** Where, in investigating and handling acts violating the provisions of this Law, a competent department of urban and rural planning discovers that a state organ staff member shall be given an administrative sanction according to law, it shall propose such sanction to the appointing and removing authority or the supervisory authority of the said staff member.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　依照本法规定应当给予行政处罚，而有关城乡规划主管部门不给予行政处罚的，上级人民政府城乡规划主管部门有权责令其作出行政处罚决定或者建议有关人民政府责令其给予行政处罚。

**Article 56** Where an administrative penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of this Law, but the relevant competent department of urban and rural planning fails to impose such penalty, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the people's government at the higher level shall have the power to order it to make a decision on the administrative penalty or recommend the relevant people's government to order it to impose the administrative penalty.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　城乡规划主管部门违反本法规定作出行政许可的，上级人民政府城乡规划主管部门有权责令其撤销或者直接撤销该行政许可。因撤销行政许可给当事人合法权益造成损失的，应当依法给予赔偿。

**Article 57** Where a competent department of urban and rural planning grants an administrative license in violation of the provisions of this Law, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the people's government at the higher level shall have the power to order it to revoke the administrative license or to directly revoke the administrative license. Where the revocation of the administrative license causes losses to the lawful rights and interests of the party, compensation shall be given according to law.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　对依法应当编制城乡规划而未组织编制，或者未按法定程序编制、审批、修改城乡规划的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；对有关人民政府负责人和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 58** Where urban and rural planning that shall be formulated according to law is not organized to be formulated, or where urban and rural planning is formulated, approved, or modified not in accordance with the statutory procedures, the people's government at the higher level shall order rectification and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons in charge of the relevant people's government and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　城乡规划组织编制机关委托不具有相应资质等级的单位编制城乡规划的，由上级人民政府责令改正，通报批评；对有关人民政府负责人和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 59** Where the authority organizing the formulation of urban and rural planning commissions an entity without the corresponding qualification grade to formulate urban and rural planning, the people's government at the higher level shall order rectification and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons in charge of the relevant people's government and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　镇人民政府或者县级以上人民政府城乡规划主管部门有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府、上级人民政府城乡规划主管部门或者监察机关依据职权责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）未依法组织编制城市的控制性详细规划、县人民政府所在地镇的控制性详细规划的；  
（二）超越职权或者对不符合法定条件的申请人核发选址意见书、建设用地规划许可证、建设工程规划许可证、乡村建设规划许可证的；  
（三）对符合法定条件的申请人未在法定期限内核发选址意见书、建设用地规划许可证、建设工程规划许可证、乡村建设规划许可证的；  
（四）未依法对经审定的修建性详细规划、建设工程设计方案的总平面图予以公布的；  
（五）同意修改修建性详细规划、建设工程设计方案的总平面图前未采取听证会等形式听取利害关系人的意见的；  
（六）发现未依法取得规划许可或者违反规划许可的规定在规划区内进行建设的行为，而不予查处或者接到举报后不依法处理的。

**Article 60** The town people's government or the competent department of urban and rural planning of the people's government at or above the county level committing any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the people's government at the higher level, or the supervisory authority shall, within the scope of its functions and powers, order rectification and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) failing to organize the formulation of regulatory detailed plans for a city or regulatory detailed plans for the town where the people's government of a county is located according to law;  
(2) issuing a site selection opinion letter, a permit for planning of land use for construction, a permit for planning of construction project, or a permit for planning of rural construction to an applicant beyond its functions and powers or in violation of statutory conditions;  
(3) failing to issue a site selection opinion letter, a permit for planning of land use for construction, a permit for planning of construction project, or a permit for planning of rural construction within the statutory time limit to an applicant meeting the statutory conditions;  
(4) failing to publicize the examined detailed construction plan or the general layout plan of the construction project design scheme according to law;  
(5) failing to adopt forms such as a hearing to solicit opinions of interested parties before agreeing to modify the detailed construction plan or the general layout plan of the construction project design scheme;  
(6) discovering acts of construction within a planned area without obtaining a planning permit according to law or in violation of the provisions of the planning permit, but failing to investigate and handle them or handling them not according to law after receiving a report.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　县级以上人民政府有关部门有下列行为之一的，由本级人民政府或者上级人民政府有关部门责令改正，通报批评；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）对未依法取得选址意见书的建设项目核发建设项目批准文件的；  
（二）未依法在国有土地使用权出让合同中确定规划条件或者改变国有土地使用权出让合同中依法确定的规划条件的；  
（三）对未依法取得建设用地规划许可证的建设单位划拨国有土地使用权的。

**Article 61** The relevant departments of the people's government at or above the county level committing any of the following acts, the people's government at the same level or the relevant department of the people's government at the higher level shall order rectification and circulate a notice of criticism; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) issuing a document approving a construction project to a project that has not obtained a site selection opinion letter according to law;  
(2) failing to determine planning conditions in a contract for the assignment of the right to use State-owned land according to law, or altering the planning conditions determined according to law in a contract for the assignment of the right to use State-owned land;  
(3) allocating the right to use State-owned land to a construction entity that has not obtained a permit for planning of land use for construction according to law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　城乡规划编制单位有下列行为之一的，由所在地城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令限期改正，处合同约定的规划编制费一倍以上二倍以下的罚款；情节严重的，责令停业整顿，由原发证机关降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任：  
（一）超越资质等级许可的范围承揽城乡规划编制工作的；  
（二）违反国家有关标准编制城乡规划的。  
未依法取得资质证书承揽城乡规划编制工作的，由县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令停止违法行为，依照前款规定处以罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。  
以欺骗手段取得资质证书承揽城乡规划编制工作的，由原发证机关吊销资质证书，依照本条第一款规定处以罚款；造成损失的，依法承担赔偿责任。

**Article 62** An urban and rural planning formulation entity committing any of the following acts, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government at the locality shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than two times the planning formulation fee agreed upon in the contract; if the circumstances are serious, it shall order suspension of business for rectification, and the original certificate issuing authority shall downgrade the qualification grade or revoke the qualification certificate; if losses are caused, the entity shall bear compensation liability according to law:  
(1) undertaking urban and rural planning formulation work beyond the scope permitted by its qualification grade;  
(2) formulating urban and rural planning in violation of relevant national standards.  
Where an urban and rural planning formulation entity undertakes such work without obtaining a qualification certificate according to law, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of the illegal act and impose a fine in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.  
Where an urban and rural planning formulation entity obtains a qualification certificate by fraudulent means and undertakes such work, the original certificate issuing authority shall revoke the qualification certificate and impose a fine in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article; if losses are caused, it shall bear compensation liability according to law.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　城乡规划编制单位取得资质证书后，不再符合相应的资质条件的，由原发证机关责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，降低资质等级或者吊销资质证书。

**Article 63** Where, after obtaining a qualification certificate, an urban and rural planning formulation entity no longer meets the corresponding qualification conditions, the original certificate issuing authority shall order it to make rectification within a time limit; if it fails to make rectification within the time limit, its qualification grade shall be downgraded or its qualification certificate shall be revoked.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　未取得建设工程规划许可证或者未按照建设工程规划许可证的规定进行建设的，由县级以上地方人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令停止建设；尚可采取改正措施消除对规划实施的影响的，限期改正，处建设工程造价百分之五以上百分之十以下的罚款；无法采取改正措施消除影响的，限期拆除，不能拆除的，没收实物或者违法收入，可以并处建设工程造价百分之十以下的罚款。

**Article 64** Where construction is carried out without obtaining a permit for planning of construction project or not in accordance with the provisions of such permit, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the local people's government at or above the county level shall order cessation of construction; if corrective measures can still be taken to eliminate the impact on the implementation of the plan, it shall order rectification within a time limit and impose a fine of not less than five percent but not more than ten percent of the construction project cost; if the impact cannot be eliminated by corrective measures, it shall order demolition within a time limit, and if demolition is not possible, it shall confiscate the physical objects or the illegal income, and may also impose a fine of not more than ten percent of the construction project cost.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在乡、村庄规划区内未依法取得乡村建设规划许可证或者未按照乡村建设规划许可证的规定进行建设的，由乡、镇人民政府责令停止建设、限期改正；逾期不改正的，可以拆除。

**Article 65** Where construction is carried out within a township or village planned area without obtaining a permit for planning of rural construction according to law or not in accordance with the provisions of such permit, the township or town people's government shall order cessation of construction and rectification within a time limit; if rectification is not made within the time limit, the construction may be demolished.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　建设单位或者个人有下列行为之一的，由所在地城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令限期拆除，可以并处临时建设工程造价一倍以下的罚款：  
（一）未经批准进行临时建设的；  
（二）未按照批准内容进行临时建设的；  
（三）临时建筑物、构筑物超过批准期限不拆除的。

**Article 66** Where a construction entity or individual commits any of the following acts, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government at the locality shall order demolition within a time limit and may also impose a fine of not more than one time the cost of the temporary construction project:  
(1) carrying out temporary construction without approval;  
(2) carrying out temporary construction not in accordance with the approved content;  
(3) failing to demolish temporary buildings or structures after the expiration of the approved period.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　建设单位未在建设工程竣工验收后六个月内向城乡规划主管部门报送有关竣工验收资料的，由所在地城市、县人民政府城乡规划主管部门责令限期补报；逾期不补报的，处一万元以上五万元以下的罚款。

**Article 67** Where a construction entity fails to submit the relevant completion acceptance data to the competent department of urban and rural planning within six months after the completion acceptance of the construction project, the competent department of urban and rural planning of the city or county people's government at the locality shall order it to submit the data within a time limit; if it fails to submit within the time limit, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　城乡规划主管部门作出责令停止建设或者限期拆除的决定后，当事人不停止建设或者逾期不拆除的，建设工程所在地县级以上地方人民政府可以责成有关部门采取查封施工现场、强制拆除等措施。

**Article 68** Where, after the competent department of urban and rural planning makes a decision ordering cessation of construction or demolition within a time limit, the party fails to cease construction or fails to demolish within the time limit, the local people's government at or above the county level of the place where the construction project is located may instruct the relevant department to take measures such as sealing up the construction site, forcible demolition, etc.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 69** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　本法自2008年1月1日起施行。《中华人民共和国城市规划法》同时废止。

**Article 70** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2008. The Urban Planning Law of the People's Republic of China shall be abolished simultaneously.
