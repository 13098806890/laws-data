# 最高人民法院关于审理行政许可案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕20号  
**公布日期**：2009-11-09  
**生效日期**：2010-01-04

> 最高人民法院《关于审理行政许可案件若干问题的规定》已于2009年11月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1476次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年1月4日起施行。 2009年12月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理行政许可案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2009〕20号 为规范行政许可案件的审理，根据《中华人民共和国行政许可法》（以下简称行政许可法）、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》及其他有关法律规定，结合行政审判实际，对有关问题作如下规定： 第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关作出的行政许可决定以及相应的不作为，或者行政机关就行政许可的变更、延续、撤回、注销、撤销等事项作出的有关具体行政行为及其相应的不作为侵犯其合法权益，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关未公开行政许可决定或者未提供行政许可监督检查记录侵犯其合法权益，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第三条  公民、法人或者其他组织仅就行政许可过程中的告知补正申请材料、听证等通知行为提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但导致许可程序对上述主体事实上终止的除外。 第四条  当事人不服行政许可决定提起诉讼的，以作出行政许可决定的机关为被告；行政许可依法须经上级行政机关批准，当事人对批准或者不批准行为不服一并提起诉讼的，以上级行政机关为共同被告；行政许可依法须经下级行政机关或者管理公共事务的组织初步审查并上报，当事人对不予初步审查或者不予上报不服提起诉讼的，以下级行政机关或者管理公共事务的组织为被告。 第五条  行政机关依据行政许可法第二十六条第二款规定统一办理行政许可的，当事人对行政许可行为不服提起诉讼，以对当事人作出具有实质影响的不利行为的机关为被告。 第六条  行政机关受理行政许可申请后，在法定期限内不予答复，公民、法人或者其他组织向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。 前款“法定期限”自行政许可申请受理之日起计算；以数据电文方式受理的，自数据电文进入行政机关指定的特定系统之日起计算；数据电文需要确认收讫的，自申请人收到行政机关的收讫确认之日起计算。 第七条  作为被诉行政许可行为基础的其他行政决定或者文书存在以下情形之一的，人民法院不予认可： （一）明显缺乏事实根据；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关作出的行政许可决定以及相应的不作为，或者行政机关就行政许可的变更、延续、撤回、注销、撤销等事项作出的有关具体行政行为及其相应的不作为侵犯其合法权益，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization believes that an administrative license decision made by an administrative organ and the corresponding omission, or the relevant specific administrative act made by an administrative organ on matters such as modification, extension, revocation, cancellation, or annulment of an administrative license and the corresponding omission, infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关未公开行政许可决定或者未提供行政许可监督检查记录侵犯其合法权益，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 2** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization believes that the failure of an administrative organ to disclose an administrative license decision or to provide supervision and inspection records of an administrative license infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  公民、法人或者其他组织仅就行政许可过程中的告知补正申请材料、听证等通知行为提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但导致许可程序对上述主体事实上终止的除外。

**Article 3** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an administrative action solely against notification acts such as notice to supplement application materials or a hearing during the administrative license process, the People's Court shall not accept the case, unless such acts cause the licensing procedure to be effectively terminated with respect to the aforesaid subject.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  当事人不服行政许可决定提起诉讼的，以作出行政许可决定的机关为被告；行政许可依法须经上级行政机关批准，当事人对批准或者不批准行为不服一并提起诉讼的，以上级行政机关为共同被告；行政许可依法须经下级行政机关或者管理公共事务的组织初步审查并上报，当事人对不予初步审查或者不予上报不服提起诉讼的，以下级行政机关或者管理公共事务的组织为被告。

**Article 4** Where a party initiates an action contesting an administrative license decision, the organ that made the administrative license decision shall be the defendant; where the administrative license shall, according to law, be subject to the approval of a superior administrative organ, and the party initiates an action contesting both the approval or disapproval act, the superior administrative organ shall be the co-defendant; where the administrative license shall, according to law, be subject to preliminary review and submission by a subordinate administrative organ or an organization managing public affairs, and the party initiates an action contesting the refusal to conduct preliminary review or the refusal to submit, the subordinate administrative organ or the organization managing public affairs shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  行政机关依据[行政许可法第二十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政许可法.md#art-26)第二款规定统一办理行政许可的，当事人对行政许可行为不服提起诉讼，以对当事人作出具有实质影响的不利行为的机关为被告。

**Article 5** Where an administrative organ handles an administrative license in a unified manner pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 26 of the Administrative Licensing Law of the People's Republic of China, and a party initiates an action contesting the administrative license act, the organ that has made an adverse act having a substantive impact on the party shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  行政机关受理行政许可申请后，在法定期限内不予答复，公民、法人或者其他组织向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
前款“法定期限”自行政许可申请受理之日起计算；以数据电文方式受理的，自数据电文进入行政机关指定的特定系统之日起计算；数据电文需要确认收讫的，自申请人收到行政机关的收讫确认之日起计算。

**Article 6** After an administrative organ accepts an application for an administrative license, if it fails to give a reply within the statutory time limit, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization brings a lawsuit to the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
The "statutory time limit" mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be calculated from the date of acceptance of the application for an administrative license; where the application is accepted by means of electronic data message, it shall be calculated from the date when the electronic data message enters the specific system designated by the administrative organ; where the receipt of the electronic data message needs to be confirmed, it shall be calculated from the date when the applicant receives the confirmation of receipt from the administrative organ.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  作为被诉行政许可行为基础的其他行政决定或者文书存在以下情形之一的，人民法院不予认可：  
（一）明显缺乏事实根据；  
（二）明显缺乏法律依据；  
（三）超越职权；  
（四）其他重大明显违法情形。

**Article 7** Where any other administrative decision or document that serves as the basis for the sued administrative license act falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not recognize it:  
(1) manifestly lacking factual basis;  
(2) manifestly lacking legal basis;  
(3) exceeding the scope of authority;  
(4) other circumstances of gross and obvious illegality.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  被告不提供或者无正当理由逾期提供证据的，与被诉行政许可行为有利害关系的第三人可以向人民法院提供；第三人对无法提供的证据，可以申请人民法院调取；人民法院在当事人无争议，但涉及国家利益、公共利益或者他人合法权益的情况下，也可以依职权调取证据。  
第三人提供或者人民法院调取的证据能够证明行政许可行为合法的，人民法院应当判决驳回原告的诉讼请求。

**Article 8** Where the defendant fails to provide evidence or provides evidence beyond the time limit without justifiable reasons, a third party that has an interest in the sued administrative license act may provide it to the People's Court; where the third party cannot provide evidence, it may apply to the People's Court to obtain the evidence; the People's Court may also obtain evidence ex officio when there is no dispute between the parties but the case involves national interests, public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others.  
Where the evidence provided by the third party or obtained by the People's Court is sufficient to prove that the administrative license act is lawful, the People's Court shall render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院审理行政许可案件，应当以申请人提出行政许可申请后实施的新的法律规范为依据；行政机关在旧的法律规范实施期间，无正当理由拖延审查行政许可申请至新的法律规范实施，适用新的法律规范不利于申请人的，以旧的法律规范为依据。

**Article 9** When hearing a case concerning an administrative license, the People's Court shall take as the basis the new legal norms that take effect after the applicant files the application for the administrative license; where an administrative organ, during the period when the old legal norms are in effect, delays the review of the application for an administrative license without justifiable reasons until the new legal norms take effect, and the application of the new legal norms is unfavorable to the applicant, the old legal norms shall be taken as the basis.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  被诉准予行政许可决定违反当时的法律规范但符合新的法律规范的，判决确认该决定违法；准予行政许可决定不损害公共利益和利害关系人合法权益的，判决驳回原告的诉讼请求。

**Article 10** Where the sued decision to grant an administrative license violates the legal norms in effect at the time but complies with the new legal norms, the judgment shall confirm that the decision is illegal; where the decision to grant an administrative license does not harm public interests or the lawful rights and interests of interested parties, the judgment shall dismiss the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民法院审理不予行政许可决定案件，认为原告请求准予许可的理由成立，且被告没有裁量余地的，可以在判决理由写明，并判决撤销不予许可决定，责令被告重新作出决定。

**Article 11** Where, in hearing a case concerning a decision to refuse an administrative license, the People's Court finds that the grounds on which the plaintiff requests the grant of the license are valid and the defendant has no room for discretion, it may state this in the reasons for the judgment and render a judgment to revoke the decision to refuse the license and order the defendant to make a new decision.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  被告无正当理由拒绝原告查阅行政许可决定及有关档案材料或者监督检查记录的，人民法院可以判决被告在法定或者合理期限内准予原告查阅。

**Article 12** Where the defendant refuses, without justifiable reasons, to allow the plaintiff to access the administrative license decision and relevant archival materials or supervision and inspection records, the People's Court may render a judgment ordering the defendant to allow the plaintiff to access such materials within a statutory or reasonable time limit.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  被告在实施行政许可过程中，与他人恶意串通共同违法侵犯原告合法权益的，应当承担连带赔偿责任；被告与他人违法侵犯原告合法权益的，应当根据其违法行为在损害发生过程和结果中所起作用等因素，确定被告的行政赔偿责任；被告已经依照法定程序履行审慎合理的审查职责，因他人行为导致行政许可决定违法的，不承担赔偿责任。  
在行政许可案件中，当事人请求一并解决有关民事赔偿问题的，人民法院可以合并审理。

**Article 13** Where the defendant, in the course of implementing an administrative license, maliciously colludes with others to jointly commit illegal acts and infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the plaintiff, it shall bear joint and several compensation liability; where the defendant and others illegally infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of the plaintiff, the defendant's administrative compensation liability shall be determined based on factors such as the role of its illegal act in the process and result of the damage; where the defendant has performed its duty of prudent and reasonable review in accordance with statutory procedures, but the administrative license decision becomes illegal due to the act of others, it shall not bear compensation liability.  
In an administrative license case, where a party requests a joint resolution of related civil compensation issues, the People's Court may consolidate the trials.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  行政机关依据[行政许可法第八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政许可法.md#art-8)第二款规定变更或者撤回已经生效的行政许可，公民、法人或者其他组织仅主张行政补偿的，应当先向行政机关提出申请；行政机关在法定期限或者合理期限内不予答复或者对行政机关作出的补偿决定不服的，可以依法提起行政诉讼。

**Article 14** Where an administrative organ modifies or revokes an administrative license that has already taken effect under paragraph 2 of Article 8 of the Administrative Licensing Law of the People's Republic of China, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization only claims administrative compensation, it shall first file an application with the administrative organ; if the administrative organ fails to reply within a statutory time limit or a reasonable time limit, or if the party is dissatisfied with the compensation decision made by the administrative organ, it may initiate an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  法律、法规、规章或者规范性文件对变更或者撤回行政许可的补偿标准未作规定的，一般在实际损失范围内确定补偿数额；行政许可属于[行政许可法第十二条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政许可法.md#art-12)第（二）项规定情形的，一般按照实际投入的损失确定补偿数额。

**Article 15** Where laws, regulations, rules, or normative documents do not provide for the compensation standard for the modification or revocation of an administrative license, the amount of compensation shall generally be determined within the scope of actual losses; where the administrative license falls under the circumstance specified in item (2) of Article 12 of the Administrative Licensing Law of the People's Republic of China, the amount of compensation shall generally be determined based on the actual investment losses.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  行政许可补偿案件的调解，参照最高人民法院《关于审理行政赔偿案件若干问题的规定》的有关规定办理。

**Article 16** Mediation of an administrative license compensation case shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Administrative Compensation Cases.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  最高人民法院以前所作的司法解释凡与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 17** Where any previous judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
