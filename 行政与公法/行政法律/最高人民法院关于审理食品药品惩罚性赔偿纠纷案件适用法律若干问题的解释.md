# 最高人民法院关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕9号  
**公布日期**：2024-03-18  
**生效日期**：2024-08-22

> 《最高人民法院关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2024年3月18日经最高人民法院审判委员会第1918次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年8月22日起施行。 2024年8月21日 最高人民法院 关于审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2024〕9号 为正确审理食品药品惩罚性赔偿纠纷案件，依法保护食品药品安全和消费者合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》、《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法》、《中华人民共和国食品安全法》、《中华人民共和国药品管理法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　购买者因个人或者家庭生活消费需要购买的食品不符合食品安全标准，购买后依照食品安全法第一百四十八条第二款规定请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院依法予以支持。 没有证据证明购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准仍然购买的，人民法院应当根据购买者请求以其实际支付价款为基数计算价款十倍的惩罚性赔偿金。 第二条　购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准或者所购买药品是假药、劣药，购买后请求经营者返还价款的，人民法院应予支持。 经营者请求购买者返还食品、药品，如果食品标签、标志或者说明书不符合食品安全标准，食品生产者在采取补救措施且能保证食品安全的情况下可以继续销售的，人民法院应予支持；应当对食品、药品采取无害化处理、销毁等措施的，依照食品安全法、药品管理法的相关规定处理。 第三条　受托人明知购买者委托购买的是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药仍然代购，购买者依照食品安全法第一百四十八条第二款或者药品管理法第一百四十四条第三款规定请求受托人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持，但受托人不以代购为业的除外。 以代购为业的受托人明知是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药仍然代购，向购买者承担惩罚性赔偿责任后向生产者追偿的，人民法院不予支持。受托人不知道是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药而代购，向购买者承担赔偿责任后向生产者追偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。 第四条　食品生产加工小作坊和食品摊贩等生产经营的食品不符合食品安全标准，购买者请求生产者或者经营者依照食品安全法第一百四十八条第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。 食品生产加工小作坊和食品摊贩等生产经营的食品不符合省、自治区、直辖市制定的具体管理办法等规定，但符合食品安全标准，购买者请求生产者或者经营者依照食品安全法第一百四十八条第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　购买者因个人或者家庭生活消费需要购买的食品不符合食品安全标准，购买后依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
没有证据证明购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准仍然购买的，人民法院应当根据购买者请求以其实际支付价款为基数计算价款十倍的惩罚性赔偿金。

**Article 1** Where a purchaser purchases food for personal or household consumption that does not conform to food safety standards, and after purchase requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages in accordance with the provisions of Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.  
Where there is no evidence proving that the purchaser knowingly purchased food that does not conform to food safety standards, the People's Court shall, based on the purchaser's request, calculate punitive damages at ten times the actual purchase price using the price actually paid as the base.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准或者所购买药品是假药、劣药，购买后请求经营者返还价款的，人民法院应予支持。  
经营者请求购买者返还食品、药品，如果食品标签、标志或者说明书不符合食品安全标准，食品生产者在采取补救措施且能保证食品安全的情况下可以继续销售的，人民法院应予支持；应当对食品、药品采取无害化处理、销毁等措施的，依照食品安全法、药品管理法的相关规定处理。

**Article 2** Where a purchaser knowingly purchases food that does not conform to food safety standards or drugs that are counterfeit or substandard, and after purchase requests the business operator to return the purchase price, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the business operator requests the purchaser to return the food or drugs, if the food label, marking, or instruction does not conform to food safety standards, and the food producer has taken remedial measures and can ensure food safety, the food may continue to be sold, and the People's Court shall support such request; where measures such as harmless treatment or destruction of the food or drugs shall be taken, such matters shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Food Safety Law and the Drug Administration Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　受托人明知购买者委托购买的是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药仍然代购，购买者依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款或者[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款规定请求受托人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持，但受托人不以代购为业的除外。  
以代购为业的受托人明知是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药仍然代购，向购买者承担惩罚性赔偿责任后向生产者追偿的，人民法院不予支持。受托人不知道是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者假药、劣药而代购，向购买者承担赔偿责任后向生产者追偿的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 3** Where a trustee knowingly purchases on behalf of a purchaser food that does not conform to food safety standards or counterfeit or substandard drugs, and the purchaser requests the trustee to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law or Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law, the People's Court shall support such request, except where the trustee does not engage in purchasing on commission as a business.  
Where a trustee who engages in purchasing on commission as a business knowingly purchases on behalf of others food that does not conform to food safety standards or counterfeit or substandard drugs, and after bearing punitive damages liability to the purchaser, seeks recourse from the producer, the People's Court shall not support such recourse. Where a trustee does not know that the purchased items are food that does not conform to food safety standards or counterfeit or substandard drugs, and after bearing compensatory damages liability to the purchaser, seeks recourse from the producer, the People's Court shall support such recourse according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　食品生产加工小作坊和食品摊贩等生产经营的食品不符合食品安全标准，购买者请求生产者或者经营者依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
食品生产加工小作坊和食品摊贩等生产经营的食品不符合省、自治区、直辖市制定的具体管理办法等规定，但符合食品安全标准，购买者请求生产者或者经营者依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** Where food produced and operated by small-scale food production and processing workshops, food vendors, or the like does not conform to food safety standards, and the purchaser requests the producer or business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where food produced and operated by small-scale food production and processing workshops, food vendors, or the like does not conform to specific management measures formulated by provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, but conforms to food safety standards, and the purchaser requests the producer or business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　食品不符合食品中危害人体健康物质的限量规定，食品添加剂的品种、使用范围、用量要求，特定人群的主辅食品的营养成分要求，与卫生、营养等食品安全要求有关的标签、标志、说明书要求以及与食品安全有关的质量要求等方面的食品安全标准，购买者依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定请求生产者或者经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where food does not conform to food safety standards regarding provisions on limits of substances harmful to human health, variety, scope of use, and dosage requirements of food additives, nutritional requirements of staple and supplementary foods for specific populations, requirements for labels, markings, and instructions related to hygiene, nutrition and other food safety requirements, and quality requirements related to food safety, and the purchaser requests the producer or business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　购买者以食品的标签、说明书不符合食品安全标准为由请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金，生产者或者经营者以食品的标签、说明书瑕疵不影响食品安全且不会对消费者造成误导为由进行抗辩，存在下列情形之一的，人民法院对生产者或者经营者的抗辩不予支持：  
（一）未标明食品安全标准要求必须标明的事项，但属于[本解释第八条](#art-8)规定情形的除外；  
（二）故意错标食品安全标准要求必须标明的事项；  
（三）未正确标明食品安全标准要求必须标明的事项，足以导致消费者对食品安全产生误解。

**Article 6** Where a purchaser requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages on the ground that the food label or instruction does not conform to food safety standards, and the producer or business operator defends on the ground that the defect in the food label or instruction does not affect food safety and will not mislead consumers, if any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall not support the defense of the producer or business operator:  
(1) The matters that must be indicated as required by food safety standards are not indicated, except where the circumstances provided for in Article 8 of this Interpretation apply;  
(2) The matters that must be indicated as required by food safety standards are intentionally mislabeled;  
(3) The matters that must be indicated as required by food safety standards are not correctly indicated, which is sufficient to cause consumers to misunderstand the food safety.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　购买者以食品的标签、说明书不符合食品安全标准为由请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金，生产者或者经营者以食品的标签、说明书虽不符合食品安全标准但不影响食品安全为由进行抗辩的，人民法院对其抗辩不予支持，但食品的标签、说明书瑕疵同时符合下列情形的除外：  
（一）根据[食品安全法第一百五十条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-150)关于食品安全的规定，足以认定标签、说明书瑕疵不影响食品安全；  
（二）根据购买者在购买食品时是否明知瑕疵存在、瑕疵是否会导致普通消费者对食品安全产生误解等事实，足以认定标签、说明书瑕疵不会对消费者造成误导。

**Article 7** Where a purchaser requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages on the ground that the food label or instruction does not conform to food safety standards, and the producer or business operator defends on the ground that although the food label or instruction does not conform to food safety standards, it does not affect food safety, the People's Court shall not support such defense, unless the defect in the food label or instruction concurrently meets the following conditions:  
(1) According to the provisions on food safety in Article 150 of the Food Safety Law, it is sufficient to determine that the defect in the label or instruction does not affect food safety;  
(2) Based on facts such as whether the purchaser knew of the defect at the time of purchase and whether the defect would cause ordinary consumers to misunderstand food safety, it is sufficient to determine that the defect in the label or instruction will not mislead consumers.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　购买者以食品的标签、说明书不符合食品安全标准为由请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金，食品的标签、说明书虽存在瑕疵但属于下列情形之一的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）文字、符号、数字的字号、字体、字高不规范，或者外文字号、字高大于中文；  
（二）出现错别字、多字、漏字、繁体字或者外文翻译不准确，但不会导致消费者对食品安全产生误解；  
（三）净含量、规格的标示方式和格式不规范，食品、食品添加剂以及配料使用的俗称或者简称等不规范，营养成分表、配料表顺序、数值、单位标示不规范，或者营养成分表数值修约间隔、“0”界限值、标示单位不规范，但不会导致消费者对食品安全产生误解；  
（四）对没有特殊贮存条件要求的食品，未按照规定标示贮存条件；  
（五）食品的标签、说明书存在其他瑕疵，但不影响食品安全且不会对消费者造成误导。

**Article 8** Where a purchaser requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages on the ground that the food label or instruction does not conform to food safety standards, and although there is a defect in the food label or instruction, it falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not support such request:  
(1) The font size, font style, or character height of text, symbols, or numbers is irregular, or the font size or character height of foreign characters is larger than that of Chinese characters;  
(2) There are typos, extra characters, missing characters, traditional Chinese characters, or inaccurate foreign language translations, but such defects will not cause consumers to misunderstand food safety;  
(3) The indication method and format of net content and specifications are irregular; common names or abbreviations used for food, food additives, and ingredients are irregular; the order, values, or units indicated in the nutrition facts table or ingredient list are irregular; or the rounding interval, "0" boundary value, or indicated unit in the nutrition facts table is irregular, but such defects will not cause consumers to misunderstand food safety;  
(4) For food that does not require special storage conditions, the storage conditions are not indicated as required;  
(5) There are other defects in the food label or instruction that do not affect food safety and will not mislead consumers.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　经营明知是不符合食品安全标准的食品或者明知是假药、劣药仍然销售、使用的行为构成欺诈，购买者选择依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款、[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款或者[消费者权益保护法第五十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-55)第一款规定起诉请求经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
购买者依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款或者[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款规定起诉请求经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任，人民法院经审理认为购买者请求不成立但经营者行为构成欺诈，购买者变更为依照[消费者权益保护法第五十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-55)第一款规定请求经营者承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 9** Where a business operator knowingly sells or uses food that does not conform to food safety standards or knowingly sells or uses counterfeit or substandard drugs, and such conduct constitutes fraud, and the purchaser elects to file a lawsuit requesting the business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law, or Article 55, paragraph 1 of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a purchaser files a lawsuit requesting the business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law or Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law, and the People's Court, after trial, finds that the purchaser's request is not established but the business operator's conduct constitutes fraud, and the purchaser changes the claim to request the business operator to bear punitive damages liability in accordance with Article 55, paragraph 1 of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests, the People's Court shall permit such change.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　购买者因个人或者家庭生活消费需要购买的药品是假药、劣药，依照[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款规定请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 10** Where a purchaser purchases drugs for personal or household consumption that are counterfeit or substandard, and requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages in accordance with the provisions of Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law, the People's Court shall support such request according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　购买者依照[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款规定请求生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金，生产者或者经营者抗辩不应适用[药品管理法第一百四十四条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国药品管理法.md#art-144)第三款规定，存在下列情形之一的，人民法院对其抗辩应予支持：  
（一）不以营利为目的实施带有自救、互助性质的生产、销售少量药品行为，且未造成他人伤害后果；  
（二）根据民间传统配方制售药品，数量不大，且未造成他人伤害后果；  
（三）不以营利为目的实施带有自救、互助性质的进口少量境外合法上市药品行为。  
对于是否属于民间传统配方难以确定的，可以根据地市级以上药品监督管理部门或者有关部门出具的意见，结合其他证据作出认定。  
行政机关作出的处罚决定或者行政机关、药品检验机构提供的检验结论、认定意见等证据足以证明生产、销售或者使用的药品属于假药、劣药的，不适用本条第一款规定。

**Article 11** Where a purchaser requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages in accordance with Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law, and the producer or business operator defends that Article 144, paragraph 3 of the Drug Administration Law should not apply, if any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall support such defense:  
(1) The production or sale of a small quantity of drugs is carried out for self-rescue or mutual assistance purposes without profit-making intent, and no harm has been caused to others;  
(2) The drugs are produced and sold according to traditional folk formulas, in small quantities, and no harm has been caused to others;  
(3) The importation of a small quantity of drugs legally marketed abroad is carried out for self-rescue or mutual assistance purposes without profit-making intent.  
Where it is difficult to determine whether the drugs are made according to traditional folk formulas, a determination may be made based on opinions issued by drug regulatory authorities at or above the prefecture level or relevant departments, in conjunction with other evidence.  
Where an administrative penalty decision or evidence such as inspection conclusions or identification opinions provided by administrative organs or drug inspection institutions is sufficient to prove that the drugs produced, sold, or used are counterfeit or substandard, the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article shall not apply.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准，依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定请求生产者或者经营者支付价款十倍的惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院应当在合理生活消费需要范围内依法支持购买者诉讼请求。  
人民法院可以综合保质期、普通消费者通常消费习惯等因素认定购买者合理生活消费需要的食品数量。  
生产者或者经营者主张购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准仍然购买索赔的，应当提供证据证明其主张。

**Article 12** Where a purchaser knowingly purchases food that does not conform to food safety standards, and requests the producer or business operator to pay punitive damages at ten times the purchase price in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall support the purchaser's claim according to law within the scope of reasonable daily consumption needs.  
The People's Court may determine the quantity of food needed for the purchaser's reasonable daily consumption by comprehensively considering factors such as the shelf life and ordinary consumers' usual consumption habits.  
Where the producer or business operator claims that the purchaser knowingly purchased food that does not conform to food safety standards and still purchased it for the purpose of claiming compensation, they shall provide evidence to prove such claim.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　购买者明知食品不符合食品安全标准，在短时间内多次购买，并依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定起诉请求同一生产者或者经营者按每次购买金额分别计算惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院应当根据购买者多次购买相同食品的总数，在合理生活消费需要范围内依法支持其诉讼请求。

**Article 13** Where a purchaser knowingly purchases food that does not conform to food safety standards, makes multiple purchases within a short period of time, and files a lawsuit requesting the same producer or business operator to calculate punitive damages separately based on the amount of each purchase in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law, the People's Court shall, based on the total quantity of the same food purchased by the purchaser in multiple transactions, support the claim according to law within the scope of reasonable daily consumption needs.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　购买者明知所购买食品不符合食品安全标准，在短时间内多次购买，并多次依照[食品安全法第一百四十八条](../../行政与公法/卫生医药/中华人民共和国食品安全法.md#art-148)第二款规定就同一不符合食品安全标准的食品起诉请求同一生产者或者经营者支付惩罚性赔偿金的，人民法院应当在合理生活消费需要范围内依法支持其诉讼请求。  
人民法院可以综合保质期、普通消费者通常消费习惯、购买者的购买频次等因素认定购买者每次起诉的食品数量是否超出合理生活消费需要。

**Article 14** Where a purchaser knowingly purchases food that does not conform to food safety standards, makes multiple purchases within a short period of time, and files multiple lawsuits against the same producer or business operator for punitive damages in accordance with Article 148, paragraph 2 of the Food Safety Law in respect of the same food that does not conform to food safety standards, the People's Court shall support the claim according to law within the scope of reasonable daily consumption needs.  
The People's Court may determine whether the quantity of food claimed in each lawsuit exceeds reasonable daily consumption needs by comprehensively considering factors such as the shelf life, ordinary consumers' usual consumption habits, and the purchaser's purchase frequency.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院在审理食品药品纠纷案件过程中，发现购买者恶意制造生产者或者经营者违法生产经营食品、药品的假象，以投诉、起诉等方式相要挟，向生产者或者经营者索取赔偿金，涉嫌敲诈勒索的，应当及时将有关违法犯罪线索、材料移送公安机关。

**Article 15** Where, in the course of trying a dispute case concerning food or drugs, the People's Court discovers that a purchaser has maliciously created a false impression that a producer or business operator has illegally produced or operated food or drugs, and has used methods such as complaints or lawsuits to threaten and extort compensation from the producer or business operator, which is suspected of constituting extortion or blackmail, the People's Court shall promptly transfer the relevant criminal clues and materials to the public security organ.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　购买者恶意制造生产者或者经营者违法生产经营食品、药品的假象，起诉请求生产者或者经营者承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应当驳回购买者诉讼请求；构成虚假诉讼的，人民法院应当依照民事诉讼法相关规定，根据情节轻重对其予以罚款、拘留。  
购买者行为侵害生产者或者经营者的名誉权等权利，生产者或者经营者请求购买者承担损害赔偿等民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a purchaser maliciously creates a false impression that a producer or business operator has illegally produced or operated food or drugs, and files a lawsuit requesting the producer or business operator to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall dismiss the purchaser's claim; if the conduct constitutes a false lawsuit, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, impose a fine or detention on the purchaser based on the seriousness of the circumstances.  
Where the purchaser's conduct infringes upon the rights of the producer or business operator, such as the right to reputation, and the producer or business operator requests the purchaser to bear civil liability such as damages, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院在审理食品药品纠纷案件过程中，发现当事人的行为涉嫌生产、销售有毒、有害食品及假药、劣药，虚假诉讼等违法犯罪的，应当及时将有关违法犯罪线索、材料移送有关行政机关和公安机关。

**Article 17** Where, in the course of trying a dispute case concerning food or drugs, the People's Court discovers that a party's conduct is suspected of being an illegal crime such as producing or selling toxic or harmful food, counterfeit or substandard drugs, or filing a false lawsuit, the People's Court shall promptly transfer the relevant criminal clues and materials to the relevant administrative organs and the public security organ.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院在审理食品药品纠纷案件过程中，发现违法生产、销售、使用食品、药品行为的，可以向有关行政机关、生产者或者经营者发出司法建议。

**Article 18** Where, in the course of trying a dispute case concerning food or drugs, the People's Court discovers any illegal production, sale, or use of food or drugs, it may issue a judicial recommendation to the relevant administrative organs, producers, or business operators.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本解释自2024年8月22日起施行。  
本解释施行后尚未终审的民事案件，适用本解释；本解释施行前已经终审，当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的民事案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 19** This Interpretation shall come into force as of August 22, 2024.  
For civil cases for which a final judgment has not been rendered before the coming into force of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply; for civil cases for which a final judgment has been rendered before the coming into force of this Interpretation, and the parties apply for a retrial or a retrial is decided in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, this Interpretation shall not apply.
