# 最高人民法院关于审理涉及农村集体土地行政案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕20号  
**公布日期**：2011-05-09  
**生效日期**：2011-09-05

> 最高人民法院《关于审理涉及农村集体土地行政案件若干问题的规定》已于2011年5月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1522次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年9月5日起施行。 2011年8月7日 最高人民法院 关于审理涉及农村集体土地行政案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2011〕20号 为正确审理涉及农村集体土地的行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国物权法》、《中华人民共和国土地管理法》和《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合行政审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  农村集体土地的权利人或者利害关系人（以下简称土地权利人）认为行政机关作出的涉及农村集体土地的行政行为侵犯其合法权益，提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。 第二条  土地登记机构根据人民法院生效裁判文书、协助执行通知书或者仲裁机构的法律文书办理的土地权属登记行为，土地权利人不服提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但土地权利人认为登记内容与有关文书内容不一致的除外。 第三条  村民委员会或者农村集体经济组织对涉及农村集体土地的行政行为不起诉的，过半数的村民可以以集体经济组织名义提起诉讼。 农村集体经济组织成员全部转为城镇居民后，对涉及农村集体土地的行政行为不服的，过半数的原集体经济组织成员可以提起诉讼。 第四条  土地使用权人或者实际使用人对行政机关作出涉及其使用或实际使用的集体土地的行政行为不服的，可以以自己的名义提起诉讼。 第五条  土地权利人认为土地储备机构作出的行为侵犯其依法享有的农村集体土地所有权或使用权，向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当以土地储备机构所隶属的土地管理部门为被告。 第六条  土地权利人认为乡级以上人民政府作出的土地确权决定侵犯其依法享有的农村集体土地所有权或者使用权，经复议后向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 法律、法规规定应当先申请行政复议的土地行政案件，复议机关作出不受理复议申请的决定或者以不符合受理条件为由驳回复议申请，复议申请人不服的，应当以复议机关为被告向人民法院提起诉讼。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  农村集体土地的权利人或者利害关系人（以下简称土地权利人）认为行政机关作出的涉及农村集体土地的行政行为侵犯其合法权益，提起诉讼的，属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围。

**Article 1** Where a right holder or interested party of rural collective land (hereinafter referred to as the land right holder) considers that an administrative act involving rural collective land made by an administrative organ infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, and institutes an administrative action, such case shall fall within the scope of administrative actions accepted by the People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  土地登记机构根据人民法院生效裁判文书、协助执行通知书或者仲裁机构的法律文书办理的土地权属登记行为，土地权利人不服提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但土地权利人认为登记内容与有关文书内容不一致的除外。

**Article 2** Where a land registration authority has conducted a land rights registration act based on a legally effective judgment document, a notice of assistance in enforcement of the People's Court, or a legal document of an arbitration institution, and the land right holder refuses to accept such act and institutes an action, the People's Court shall not accept the case, unless the land right holder considers that the registration content is inconsistent with the content of the relevant document.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  村民委员会或者农村集体经济组织对涉及农村集体土地的行政行为不起诉的，过半数的村民可以以集体经济组织名义提起诉讼。  
农村集体经济组织成员全部转为城镇居民后，对涉及农村集体土地的行政行为不服的，过半数的原集体经济组织成员可以提起诉讼。

**Article 3** Where the villagers committee or the rural collective economic organization does not institute an action against an administrative act involving rural collective land, more than half of the villagers may institute an action in the name of the collective economic organization.  
After all members of the rural collective economic organization have become urban residents, if more than half of the original members of the collective economic organization refuse to accept an administrative act involving rural collective land, they may institute an action.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  土地使用权人或者实际使用人对行政机关作出涉及其使用或实际使用的集体土地的行政行为不服的，可以以自己的名义提起诉讼。

**Article 4** Where a land use right holder or actual user refuses to accept an administrative act made by an administrative organ that involves the collective land it uses or actually uses, it may institute an action in its own name.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  土地权利人认为土地储备机构作出的行为侵犯其依法享有的农村集体土地所有权或使用权，向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当以土地储备机构所隶属的土地管理部门为被告。

**Article 5** Where a land right holder considers that an act of a land reserve institution infringes upon its lawfully enjoyed ownership or right to use of rural collective land, and institutes an action to the People's Court, it shall name the land administration department to which the land reserve institution is subordinate as the defendant.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  土地权利人认为乡级以上人民政府作出的土地确权决定侵犯其依法享有的农村集体土地所有权或者使用权，经复议后向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
法律、法规规定应当先申请行政复议的土地行政案件，复议机关作出不受理复议申请的决定或者以不符合受理条件为由驳回复议申请，复议申请人不服的，应当以复议机关为被告向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 6** Where a land right holder considers that a land rights confirmation decision made by a people's government at or above the township level infringes upon its lawfully enjoyed ownership or right to use of rural collective land, and after applying for administrative reconsideration, institutes an action to the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
For an administrative land case for which laws and regulations require an application for administrative reconsideration as a prerequisite, if the reconsideration authority makes a decision not to accept the reconsideration application or rejects the reconsideration application on the ground that it does not meet the acceptance conditions, and the reconsideration applicant refuses to accept such decision, it shall name the reconsideration authority as the defendant and institute an action to the People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  土地权利人认为行政机关作出的行政处罚、行政强制措施等行政行为侵犯其依法享有的农村集体土地所有权或者使用权，直接向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 7** Where a land right holder considers that administrative acts such as administrative penalty and administrative compulsory measures made by an administrative organ infringe upon its lawfully enjoyed ownership or right to use of rural collective land, and directly institutes an action to the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  土地权属登记（包括土地权属证书）在生效裁判和仲裁裁决中作为定案证据，利害关系人对该登记行为提起诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 8** Where a land rights registration (including the land rights certificate) is used as evidence in a final judgment or arbitration award, and an interested party institutes an action against such registration act, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  涉及农村集体土地的行政决定以公告方式送达的，起诉期限自公告确定的期限届满之日起计算。

**Article 9** Where an administrative decision involving rural collective land is served by public announcement, the limitation period for prosecution shall be calculated from the date on which the period determined in the public announcement expires.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  土地权利人对土地管理部门组织实施过程中确定的土地补偿有异议，直接向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但应当告知土地权利人先申请行政机关裁决。

**Article 10** Where a land right holder disputes the land compensation determined during the organization and implementation process of the land administration department, and directly institutes an action to the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case, but shall inform the land right holder to first apply to the administrative organ for adjudication.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  土地权利人以土地管理部门超过两年对非法占地行为进行处罚违法，向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当按照[行政处罚法第二十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国行政处罚法.md#art-29)第二款的规定处理。

**Article 11** Where a land right holder, on the ground that the land administration department has imposed a penalty on illegal land occupation beyond two years in violation of law, prosecutes to the People's Court, the People's Court shall handle the case in accordance with the provisions of Article 29, paragraph 2 of the Administrative Penalty Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  征收农村集体土地时涉及被征收土地上的房屋及其他不动产的，土地权利人可以请求依照物权法第四十二条第二款的规定给予补偿。  
征收农村集体土地时未就被征收土地上的房屋及其他不动产进行安置补偿，补偿安置时房屋所在地已纳入城市规划区，土地权利人请求参照执行国有土地上房屋征收补偿标准的，人民法院一般应予支持，但应当扣除已经取得的土地补偿费。

**Article 12** Where expropriation of rural collective land involves houses and other immovable property on the expropriated land, the land right holder may request compensation in accordance with the provisions of Article 42, paragraph 2 of the Real Right Law.  
Where the expropriation of rural collective land does not provide resettlement compensation for houses and other immovable property on the expropriated land, and at the time of compensation and resettlement the location of the houses has been included in the urban planning area, if the land right holder requests that the compensation standard for houses expropriation on state-owned land be applied mutatis mutandis, the People's Court shall generally support such request, but the land compensation fee already obtained shall be deducted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  在审理土地行政案件中，人民法院经当事人同意进行协调的期间，不计算在审理期限内。当事人不同意继续协商的，人民法院应当及时审理，并恢复计算审理期限。

**Article 13** In trying an administrative land case, the period during which the People's Court conducts mediation with the consent of the parties shall not be counted in the trial period. If the parties do not agree to continue consultation, the People's Court shall promptly try the case and resume counting the trial period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  县级以上人民政府土地管理部门根据[土地管理法实施条例第四十五条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-45)的规定，申请人民法院执行其作出的责令交出土地决定的，应当符合下列条件：  
（一）征收土地方案已经有权机关依法批准；  
（二）市、县人民政府和土地管理部门已经依照土地管理法和土地管理法实施条例规定的程序实施征地行为；  
（三）被征收土地所有权人、使用人已经依法得到安置补偿或者无正当理由拒绝接受安置补偿，且拒不交出土地，已经影响到征收工作的正常进行；  
（四）符合最高人民法院《关于执行〈中华人民共和国行政诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》第八十六条规定的条件。  
人民法院对符合条件的申请，应当予以受理，并通知申请人；对不符合条件的申请，应当裁定不予受理。

**Article 14** Where the land administration department of a people's government at or above the county level applies to the People's Court for enforcement of its decision ordering the return of the land in accordance with Article 45 of the Regulations on the Implementation of the Land Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) The expropriation plan has been lawfully approved by the competent authority;  
(2) The city or county people's government and the land administration department have implemented the land expropriation acts in accordance with the procedures prescribed by the Land Administration Law and the Regulations on the Implementation of the Land Administration Law;  
(3) The owners and users of the expropriated land have been lawfully provided with resettlement compensation, or have refused to accept resettlement compensation without justifiable reasons, and have refused to return the land, thereby affecting the normal progress of the expropriation work;  
(4) The conditions set forth in Article 86 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China are met.  
The People's Court shall accept the application that meets the conditions and notify the applicant; for an application that does not meet the conditions, it shall rule not to accept it.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  最高人民法院以前所作的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 15** Where any previous judicial interpretation of the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
