# 最高人民法院关于审理生态环境损害赔偿案件的若干规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕8号  
**公布日期**：2019-06-04  
**生效日期**：2019-06-05

> 《最高人民法院关于审理生态环境损害赔偿案件的若干规定（试行）》已于2019年5月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1769次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年6月5日起施行。 2019年6月4日 最高人民法院 关于审理生态环境损害赔偿案件的若干规定（试行）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  具有下列情形之一，省级、市地级人民政府及其指定的相关部门、机构，或者受国务院委托行使全民所有自然资源资产所有权的部门，因与造成生态环境损害的自然人、法人或者其他组织经磋商未达成一致或者无法进行磋商的，可以作为原告提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼：  
（一）发生较大、重大、特别重大突发环境事件的；  
（二）在国家和省级主体功能区规划中划定的重点生态功能区、禁止开发区发生环境污染、生态破坏事件的；  
（三）发生其他严重影响生态环境后果的。  
前款规定的市地级人民政府包括设区的市，自治州、盟、地区，不设区的地级市，直辖市的区、县人民政府。

**Article 1** Where a people's government at the provincial or city level, or the relevant departments and institutions designated thereby, or the department authorized by the State Council to exercise the ownership of natural resources assets owned by the whole people, fails to reach an agreement through consultation or cannot conduct consultation with a natural person, legal person or other organization that has caused ecological and environmental damage under any of the following circumstances, it may initiate an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit as a plaintiff:  
(1) A major, serious, or extraordinarily serious environmental emergency occurs;  
(2) An environmental pollution or ecological damage incident occurs in the key ecological functional zones or prohibited development zones designated in the national or provincial main functional area planning;  
(3) Other circumstances that have serious consequences for the ecological environment.  
The city-level people's government as prescribed in the preceding paragraph includes the people's government of a city with districts, an autonomous prefecture, a league, a prefecture, a prefecture-level city without districts, and the district or county people's government of a municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  下列情形不适用本规定：  
（一）因污染环境、破坏生态造成人身损害、个人和集体财产损失要求赔偿的，适用侵权责任法等法律规定；  
（二）因海洋生态环境损害要求赔偿的，适用海洋环境保护法等法律及相关规定。

**Article 2** These Provisions shall not apply to the following circumstances:  
(1) Claims for compensation for personal injury or individual or collective property losses caused by environmental pollution or ecological damage shall be governed by the Tort Liability Law and other legal provisions;  
(2) Claims for compensation for damage to the marine ecological environment shall be governed by the Marine Environment Protection Law and other laws and relevant provisions.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  第一审生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件由生态环境损害行为实施地、损害结果发生地或者被告住所地的中级以上人民法院管辖。  
经最高人民法院批准，高级人民法院可以在辖区内确定部分中级人民法院集中管辖第一审生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件。  
中级人民法院认为确有必要的，可以在报请高级人民法院批准后，裁定将本院管辖的第一审生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件交由具备审理条件的基层人民法院审理。  
生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件由人民法院环境资源审判庭或者指定的专门法庭审理。

**Article 3** First-instance ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate people's court or above at the place where the ecological and environmental damage act was committed, where the damage occurred, or where the defendant has its domicile.  
With the approval of the Supreme People's Court, a higher people's court may designate some intermediate people's courts within its jurisdiction to have centralized jurisdiction over first-instance ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit cases.  
Where an intermediate people's court deems it truly necessary, it may, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the higher people's court, issue a ruling to transfer the first-instance ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case under its jurisdiction to a primary people's court that has the conditions for trial.  
Ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit cases shall be tried by the environmental resources adjudication division of the people's court or a designated specialized tribunal.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院审理第一审生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件，应当由法官和人民陪审员组成合议庭进行。

**Article 4** In trying first-instance ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit cases, a people's court shall form a collegial panel composed of judges and people's jurors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  原告提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，符合民事诉讼法和本规定并提交下列材料的，人民法院应当登记立案：  
（一）证明具备提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼原告资格的材料；  
（二）符合[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定情形之一的证明材料；  
（三）与被告进行磋商但未达成一致或者因客观原因无法与被告进行磋商的说明；  
（四）符合法律规定的起诉状，并按照被告人数提出副本。

**Article 5** Where a plaintiff initiates an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit, meets the requirements of the Civil Procedure Law and these Provisions, and submits the following materials, the people's court shall register and accept the case:  
(1) Materials proving the plaintiff's qualification to initiate an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit;  
(2) Materials proving that the case falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 1 of these Provisions;  
(3) A statement that consultation with the defendant has been conducted but no agreement has been reached, or that consultation cannot be conducted with the defendant due to objective reasons;  
(4) A complaint that complies with the law, together with copies in the number corresponding to the number of defendants.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  原告主张被告承担生态环境损害赔偿责任的，应当就以下事实承担举证责任：  
（一）被告实施了污染环境、破坏生态的行为或者具有其他应当依法承担责任的情形；  
（二）生态环境受到损害，以及所需修复费用、损害赔偿等具体数额；  
（三）被告污染环境、破坏生态的行为与生态环境损害之间具有关联性。

**Article 6** Where a plaintiff claims that a defendant shall bear ecological and environmental damage compensation liability, the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the following facts:  
(1) The defendant has committed an act of environmental pollution or ecological damage, or there are other circumstances in which the defendant shall bear liability according to law;  
(2) The ecological environment has been damaged, and the specific amounts such as the necessary restoration costs and damage compensation;  
(3) There is a causal link between the defendant's act of environmental pollution or ecological damage and the ecological and environmental damage.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  被告反驳原告主张的，应当提供证据加以证明。被告主张具有法律规定的不承担责任或者减轻责任情形的，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 7** Where a defendant refutes the plaintiff's claims, the defendant shall provide evidence to prove it. Where the defendant claims that there are circumstances for exemption from or mitigation of liability as provided by law, the defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  已为发生法律效力的刑事裁判所确认的事实，当事人在生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件中无须举证证明，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。  
对刑事裁判未予确认的事实，当事人提供的证据达到民事诉讼证明标准的，人民法院应当予以认定。

**Article 8** Facts that have been confirmed by a criminal judgment that has taken legal effect need not be proved by a party in an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to overturn them.  
For facts not confirmed in the criminal judgment, if the evidence provided by a party meets the standard of proof in civil actions, the people's court shall find them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  负有相关环境资源保护监督管理职责的部门或者其委托的机构在行政执法过程中形成的事件调查报告、检验报告、检测报告、评估报告、监测数据等，经当事人质证并符合证据标准的，可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 9** Incident investigation reports, inspection reports, testing reports, assessment reports, monitoring data, etc., formed by departments with relevant environmental resources protection supervision and management duties or institutions entrusted thereby during administrative law enforcement, may serve as the basis for finding the facts of a case after being cross-examined by the parties and meeting the evidentiary standards.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  当事人在诉前委托具备环境司法鉴定资质的鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见，以及委托国务院环境资源保护监督管理相关主管部门推荐的机构出具的检验报告、检测报告、评估报告、监测数据等，经当事人质证并符合证据标准的，可以作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 10** Expert evaluation opinions issued by institutions with environmental forensic appraisal qualifications commissioned by a party before litigation, and inspection reports, testing reports, assessment reports, monitoring data, etc., issued by institutions recommended by the relevant competent departments of environmental resources protection supervision and management under the State Council, may serve as the basis for finding the facts of a case after being cross-examined by the parties and meeting the evidentiary standards.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  被告违反法律法规污染环境、破坏生态的，人民法院应当根据原告的诉讼请求以及具体案情，合理判决被告承担修复生态环境、赔偿损失、停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、赔礼道歉等民事责任。

**Article 11** Where a defendant violates laws and regulations to pollute the environment or damage the ecology, the people's court shall, based on the plaintiff's claims and the specific circumstances of the case, reasonably order the defendant to bear civil liabilities such as restoring the ecological environment, compensating for losses, ceasing the infringement, removing the obstruction, eliminating the danger, and making an apology.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  受损生态环境能够修复的，人民法院应当依法判决被告承担修复责任，并同时确定被告不履行修复义务时应承担的生态环境修复费用。  
生态环境修复费用包括制定、实施修复方案的费用，修复期间的监测、监管费用，以及修复完成后的验收费用、修复效果后评估费用等。  
原告请求被告赔偿生态环境受到损害至修复完成期间服务功能损失的，人民法院根据具体案情予以判决。

**Article 12** Where the damaged ecological environment can be restored, the people's court shall, according to law, order the defendant to bear the restoration liability and simultaneously determine the ecological environment restoration costs to be borne by the defendant if the defendant fails to perform the restoration obligation.  
Ecological environment restoration costs include the costs of formulating and implementing the restoration plan, monitoring and supervision costs during the restoration period, and acceptance inspection costs and post-restoration effect evaluation costs after restoration is completed.  
Where the plaintiff requests the defendant to compensate for the loss of service functions during the period from the ecological environment damage to the completion of restoration, the people's court shall render a judgment based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  受损生态环境无法修复或者无法完全修复，原告请求被告赔偿生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失的，人民法院根据具体案情予以判决。

**Article 13** Where the damaged ecological environment cannot be restored or cannot be fully restored, and the plaintiff requests the defendant to compensate for losses caused by permanent damage to ecological environment functions, the people's court shall render a judgment based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  原告请求被告承担下列费用的，人民法院根据具体案情予以判决：  
（一）实施应急方案以及为防止生态环境损害的发生和扩大采取合理预防、处置措施发生的应急处置费用；  
（二）为生态环境损害赔偿磋商和诉讼支出的调查、检验、鉴定、评估等费用；  
（三）合理的律师费以及其他为诉讼支出的合理费用。

**Article 14** Where the plaintiff requests the defendant to bear the following costs, the people's court shall render a judgment based on the specific circumstances of the case:  
(1) Emergency response costs incurred in implementing emergency plans and taking reasonable prevention and disposal measures to prevent the occurrence and expansion of ecological and environmental damage;  
(2) Costs for investigation, inspection, appraisal, assessment, etc., incurred for consultation and litigation on ecological and environmental damage compensation;  
(3) Reasonable attorney fees and other reasonable costs incurred for the litigation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  人民法院判决被告承担的生态环境服务功能损失赔偿资金、生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失赔偿资金，以及被告不履行生态环境修复义务时所应承担的修复费用，应当依照法律、法规、规章予以缴纳、管理和使用。

**Article 15** The compensation funds for loss of ecological environment service functions, the compensation funds for losses caused by permanent damage to ecological environment functions, and the restoration costs to be borne by the defendant if the defendant fails to perform the ecological environment restoration obligation, as ordered by the people's court, shall be paid, managed, and used in accordance with laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  在生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件审理过程中，同一损害生态环境行为又被提起民事公益诉讼，符合起诉条件的，应当由受理生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件的人民法院受理并由同一审判组织审理。

**Article 16** If, during the trial of an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case, a civil public interest lawsuit is also initiated for the same act of ecological environment damage, and it meets the conditions for prosecution, it shall be accepted by the people's court that accepted the ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case and shall be tried by the same adjudication organization.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  人民法院受理因同一损害生态环境行为提起的生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件和民事公益诉讼案件，应先中止民事公益诉讼案件的审理，待生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件审理完毕后，就民事公益诉讼案件未被涵盖的诉讼请求依法作出裁判。

**Article 17** Where a people's court accepts both an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case and a civil public interest lawsuit case initiated for the same act of ecological environment damage, it shall first suspend the trial of the civil public interest lawsuit case, and after the trial of the ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case is concluded, render a judgment according to law on the claims not covered by the civil public interest lawsuit case.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件的裁判生效后，有权提起民事公益诉讼的机关或者社会组织就同一损害生态环境行为有证据证明存在前案审理时未发现的损害，并提起民事公益诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。  
民事公益诉讼案件的裁判生效后，有权提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼的主体就同一损害生态环境行为有证据证明存在前案审理时未发现的损害，并提起生态环境损害赔偿诉讼的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 18** After the judgment of an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case takes effect, if an authority or social organization entitled to initiate a civil public interest lawsuit has evidence proving that there is damage not discovered during the trial of the previous case regarding the same act of ecological environment damage, and initiates a civil public interest lawsuit, the people's court shall accept it.  
After the judgment of a civil public interest lawsuit case takes effect, if a subject entitled to initiate an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit has evidence proving that there is damage not discovered during the trial of the previous case regarding the same act of ecological environment damage, and initiates an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit, the people's court shall accept it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  实际支出应急处置费用的机关提起诉讼主张该费用的，人民法院应予受理，但人民法院已经受理就同一损害生态环境行为提起的生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件且该案原告已经主张应急处置费用的除外。  
生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件原告未主张应急处置费用，因同一损害生态环境行为实际支出应急处置费用的机关提起诉讼主张该费用的，由受理生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件的人民法院受理并由同一审判组织审理。

**Article 19** Where an authority that actually incurred emergency response costs initiates a lawsuit claiming such costs, the people's court shall accept it, except where the people's court has already accepted an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case for the same act of ecological environment damage and the plaintiff in that case has already claimed emergency response costs.  
Where the plaintiff in an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case does not claim emergency response costs, and an authority that actually incurred emergency response costs for the same act of ecological environment damage initiates a lawsuit claiming such costs, the lawsuit shall be accepted by the people's court that accepted the ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case and shall be tried by the same adjudication organization.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  经磋商达成生态环境损害赔偿协议的，当事人可以向人民法院申请司法确认。  
人民法院受理申请后，应当公告协议内容，公告期间不少于三十日。公告期满后，人民法院经审查认为协议的内容不违反法律法规强制性规定且不损害国家利益、社会公共利益的，裁定确认协议有效。裁定书应当写明案件的基本事实和协议内容，并向社会公开。

**Article 20** Where an ecological and environmental damage compensation agreement is reached through consultation, the parties may apply to the people's court for judicial confirmation.  
After accepting the application, the people's court shall publish the content of the agreement for a period of not less than thirty days. After the publication period expires, if the people's court, upon examination, finds that the content of the agreement does not violate mandatory provisions of laws and regulations and does not harm national interests or social public interests, it shall rule to confirm the agreement as valid. The ruling shall state the basic facts of the case and the content of the agreement, and shall be made public.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  一方当事人拒绝履行、未全部履行发生法律效力的生态环境损害赔偿诉讼案件裁判或者经司法确认的生态环境损害赔偿协议的，对方当事人可以向人民法院申请强制执行。需要修复生态环境的，依法由省级、市地级人民政府及其指定的相关部门、机构组织实施。

**Article 21** Where a party refuses to perform or fails to fully perform a judgment of an ecological and environmental damage compensation lawsuit case that has taken legal effect or an ecologically and environmentally damaging compensation agreement that has been judicially confirmed, the other party may apply to the people's court for compulsory execution. Where ecological environment restoration is required, it shall be organized and implemented according to law by the provincial or city-level people's government and the relevant departments and institutions designated thereby.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  人民法院审理生态环境损害赔偿案件，本规定没有规定的，参照适用《最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》《最高人民法院关于审理环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》等相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 22** Where these Provisions have no provisions on the trial of ecological and environmental damage compensation cases by a people's court, the provisions of relevant judicial interpretations such as the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Civil Public Interest Litigation Cases and the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Tort Liability Dispute Cases shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  本规定自2019年6月5日起施行。

**Article 23** These Provisions shall come into force on June 5, 2019.
