# 中华人民共和国土地管理法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**公布日期**：2019-08-26  
**生效日期**：2020-01-01

> （1986年6月25日第六届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十六次会议通过　根据1988年12月29日第七届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第五次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国土地管理法〉的决定》第一次修正　1998年8月29日第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第四次会议修订　根据2004年8月28日第十届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十一次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国土地管理法〉的决定》第二次修正　根据2019年8月26日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十二次会议《关于修改〈中华人民共和国土地管理法〉、〈中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法〉的决定》第三次修正） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　土地的所有权和使用权 第三章　土地利用总体规划 第四章　耕地保护 第五章　建设用地 第六章　监督检查 第七章　法律责任 第八章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了加强土地管理，维护土地的社会主义公有制，保护、开发土地资源，合理利用土地，切实保护耕地，促进社会经济的可持续发展，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　中华人民共和国实行土地的社会主义公有制，即全民所有制和劳动群众集体所有制。 全民所有，即国家所有土地的所有权由国务院代表国家行使。 任何单位和个人不得侵占、买卖或者以其他形式非法转让土地。土地使用权可以依法转让。

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强土地管理，维护土地的社会主义公有制，保护、开发土地资源，合理利用土地，切实保护耕地，促进社会经济的可持续发展，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution to strengthen land administration, maintain the socialist public ownership of land, protect and develop land resources, rationally utilize land, effectively protect cultivated land, and promote the sustainable development of the social economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　中华人民共和国实行土地的社会主义公有制，即全民所有制和劳动群众集体所有制。  
全民所有，即国家所有土地的所有权由国务院代表国家行使。  
任何单位和个人不得侵占、买卖或者以其他形式非法转让土地。土地使用权可以依法转让。  
国家为了公共利益的需要，可以依法对土地实行征收或者征用并给予补偿。  
国家依法实行国有土地有偿使用制度。但是，国家在法律规定的范围内划拨国有土地使用权的除外。

**Article 2** The People's Republic of China shall practice a socialist public ownership of land, namely, ownership by the whole people and collective ownership by the working people.  
Ownership by the whole people, that is, the ownership of state-owned land shall be exercised by the State Council on behalf of the State.  
No unit or individual may occupy, buy, sell or illegally transfer land in any other form. The right to the use of land may be transferred according to law.  
The State may, in the public interest, expropriate or requisition land according to law and grant compensation.  
The State shall practice a system of compensatory use of state-owned land according to law, except where the State allocates the right to the use of state-owned land within the scope prescribed by law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　十分珍惜、合理利用土地和切实保护耕地是我国的基本国策。各级人民政府应当采取措施，全面规划，严格管理，保护、开发土地资源，制止非法占用土地的行为。

**Article 3** Cherishing, rationally utilizing land and effectively protecting cultivated land shall be the basic national policy of China. People's governments at all levels shall take measures to comprehensively plan, strictly manage, protect and develop land resources, and stop illegal occupation of land.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国家实行土地用途管制制度。  
国家编制土地利用总体规划，规定土地用途，将土地分为农用地、建设用地和未利用地。严格限制农用地转为建设用地，控制建设用地总量，对耕地实行特殊保护。  
前款所称农用地是指直接用于农业生产的土地，包括耕地、林地、草地、农田水利用地、养殖水面等；建设用地是指建造建筑物、构筑物的土地，包括城乡住宅和公共设施用地、工矿用地、交通水利设施用地、旅游用地、军事设施用地等；未利用地是指农用地和建设用地以外的土地。  
使用土地的单位和个人必须严格按照土地利用总体规划确定的用途使用土地。

**Article 4** The State shall practice a system of control over the purposes of use of land.  
The State shall formulate the general plan for land utilization, stipulate the purposes of land use, and classify land into agricultural land, land for construction, and unused land. The conversion of agricultural land into land for construction shall be strictly restricted, the total amount of land for construction shall be controlled, and cultivated land shall be given special protection.  
For the purpose of the preceding paragraph, agricultural land refers to land directly used for agricultural production, including cultivated land, forest land, grassland, land for farmland water conservancy facilities, and water surfaces for aquaculture; land for construction refers to land used for constructing buildings and structures, including urban and rural residential and public facilities land, land for industrial and mining enterprises, land for transportation and water conservancy facilities, land for tourism, and land for military facilities; unused land refers to land other than agricultural land and land for construction.  
Units and individuals using land must use the land strictly in accordance with the purposes determined in the general plan for land utilization.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院自然资源主管部门统一负责全国土地的管理和监督工作。  
县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门的设置及其职责，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据国务院有关规定确定。

**Article 5** The competent department of natural resources under the State Council shall be uniformly responsible for the administration and supervision of land throughout the country.  
The establishment and functions of the competent departments of natural resources of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国务院授权的机构对省、自治区、直辖市人民政府以及国务院确定的城市人民政府土地利用和土地管理情况进行督察。

**Article 6** The institutions authorized by the State Council shall supervise the land utilization and land administration of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities determined by the State Council.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　任何单位和个人都有遵守土地管理法律、法规的义务，并有权对违反土地管理法律、法规的行为提出检举和控告。

**Article 7** Any unit or individual shall have the duty to abide by the laws and regulations on land administration and shall have the right to report and accuse any act violating the laws and regulations on land administration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在保护和开发土地资源、合理利用土地以及进行有关的科学研究等方面成绩显著的单位和个人，由人民政府给予奖励。

**Article 8** The people's government shall give awards to units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in protecting and developing land resources, rationally utilizing land, and conducting relevant scientific research.

### 第二章　土地的所有权和使用权

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　城市市区的土地属于国家所有。  
农村和城市郊区的土地，除由法律规定属于国家所有的以外，属于农民集体所有；宅基地和自留地、自留山，属于农民集体所有。

**Article 9** Land in urban areas of cities shall be owned by the State.  
Land in rural areas and suburban areas of cities, except for those owned by the State as provided by law, shall be owned by peasant collectives; residential sites, privately cultivated land, and privately cultivated hills shall be owned by peasant collectives.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国有土地和农民集体所有的土地，可以依法确定给单位或者个人使用。使用土地的单位和个人，有保护、管理和合理利用土地的义务。

**Article 10** State-owned land and land owned by peasant collectives may be determined according to law for use by units or individuals. Units and individuals using land shall have the duty to protect, manage, and rationally utilize the land.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　农民集体所有的土地依法属于村农民集体所有的，由村集体经济组织或者村民委员会经营、管理；已经分别属于村内两个以上农村集体经济组织的农民集体所有的，由村内各该农村集体经济组织或者村民小组经营、管理；已经属于乡（镇）农民集体所有的，由乡（镇）农村集体经济组织经营、管理。

**Article 11** Land owned by peasant collectives which, according to law, belongs to the peasant collective of a village shall be operated and managed by the village economic collective organization or the villagers committee; land that has already been owned by peasant collectives of two or more rural economic collective organizations within the village shall be operated and managed by the respective rural economic collective organizations or villagers groups within the village; land that has already been owned by the peasant collective of a township (town) shall be operated and managed by the rural economic collective organization of the township (town).

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　土地的所有权和使用权的登记，依照有关不动产登记的法律、行政法规执行。  
依法登记的土地的所有权和使用权受法律保护，任何单位和个人不得侵犯。

**Article 12** The registration of land ownership and the right to the use of land shall be carried out in accordance with the laws and administrative regulations on immovable property registration.  
The ownership and the right to the use of land registered according to law shall be protected by law, and no unit or individual may infringe upon them.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　农民集体所有和国家所有依法由农民集体使用的耕地、林地、草地，以及其他依法用于农业的土地，采取农村集体经济组织内部的家庭承包方式承包，不宜采取家庭承包方式的荒山、荒沟、荒丘、荒滩等，可以采取招标、拍卖、公开协商等方式承包，从事种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业生产。家庭承包的耕地的承包期为三十年，草地的承包期为三十年至五十年，林地的承包期为三十年至七十年；耕地承包期届满后再延长三十年，草地、林地承包期届满后依法相应延长。  
国家所有依法用于农业的土地可以由单位或者个人承包经营，从事种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业生产。  
发包方和承包方应当依法订立承包合同，约定双方的权利和义务。承包经营土地的单位和个人，有保护和按照承包合同约定的用途合理利用土地的义务。

**Article 13** Cultivated land, forest land, grassland owned by peasant collectives and state-owned land used by peasant collectives according to law, as well as other land used for agriculture according to law, shall be contracted by means of household contract within the rural economic collective organization. For barren mountains, barren gullies, barren hills, barren beaches, etc., which are not suitable for household contract, they may be contracted by means of invitation for bid, auction, public negotiation, etc., to engage in crop farming, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery production. The contract period for cultivated land under household contract shall be 30 years, for grassland shall be 30 to 50 years, and for forest land shall be 30 to 70 years; upon the expiration of the contract period for cultivated land, it shall be extended for another 30 years, and upon the expiration of the contract period for grassland and forest land, it shall be extended correspondingly according to law.  
State-owned land used for agriculture according to law may be contracted and operated by units or individuals to engage in crop farming, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishery production.  
The party awarding the contract and the party undertaking the contract shall conclude a contract contract according to law, stipulating the rights and obligations of both parties. Units and individuals contracting and operating land shall have the duty to protect and rationally utilize the land in accordance with the purposes agreed upon in the contract.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　土地所有权和使用权争议，由当事人协商解决；协商不成的，由人民政府处理。  
单位之间的争议，由县级以上人民政府处理；个人之间、个人与单位之间的争议，由乡级人民政府或者县级以上人民政府处理。  
当事人对有关人民政府的处理决定不服的，可以自接到处理决定通知之日起三十日内，向人民法院起诉。  
在土地所有权和使用权争议解决前，任何一方不得改变土地利用现状。

**Article 14** Disputes over land ownership and the right to the use of land shall be resolved through consultation between the parties; if consultation fails, the disputes shall be handled by the people's government.  
Disputes between units shall be handled by the people's government at or above the county level; disputes between individuals, or between individuals and units, shall be handled by the people's government at the township level or the people's government at or above the county level.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the handling decision of the relevant people's government, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within 30 days from the date of receipt of the notification of the handling decision.  
Before the dispute over land ownership and the right to the use of land is resolved, neither party may change the current status of land utilization.

### 第三章　土地利用总体规划

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　各级人民政府应当依据国民经济和社会发展规划、国土整治和资源环境保护的要求、土地供给能力以及各项建设对土地的需求，组织编制土地利用总体规划。  
土地利用总体规划的规划期限由国务院规定。

**Article 15** People's governments at all levels shall organize the formulation of the general plan for land utilization based on the national economic and social development plan, the requirements of territorial consolidation and environmental protection of resources, the land supply capacity, and the demand for land for various constructions.  
The planning period of the general plan for land utilization shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　下级土地利用总体规划应当依据上一级土地利用总体规划编制。  
地方各级人民政府编制的土地利用总体规划中的建设用地总量不得超过上一级土地利用总体规划确定的控制指标，耕地保有量不得低于上一级土地利用总体规划确定的控制指标。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府编制的土地利用总体规划，应当确保本行政区域内耕地总量不减少。

**Article 16** The general plan for land utilization at a lower level shall be formulated in accordance with the general plan for land utilization at the next higher level.  
The total amount of land for construction in the general plan for land utilization formulated by local people's governments at all levels shall not exceed the control targets determined in the general plan for land utilization at the next higher level, and the amount of cultivated land to be maintained shall not be lower than the control targets determined in the general plan for land utilization at the next higher level.  
The general plan for land utilization formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall ensure that the total amount of cultivated land within their respective administrative areas shall not decrease.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　土地利用总体规划按照下列原则编制：  
（一）落实国土空间开发保护要求，严格土地用途管制；  
（二）严格保护永久基本农田，严格控制非农业建设占用农用地；  
（三）提高土地节约集约利用水平；  
（四）统筹安排城乡生产、生活、生态用地，满足乡村产业和基础设施用地合理需求，促进城乡融合发展；  
（五）保护和改善生态环境，保障土地的可持续利用；  
（六）占用耕地与开发复垦耕地数量平衡、质量相当。

**Article 17** The general plan for land utilization shall be formulated in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) Implementing the requirements for the development and protection of territorial space, and strictly controlling the purposes of land use;  
(2) Strictly protecting permanent basic farmland, and strictly restricting the occupation of agricultural land for non-agricultural construction;  
(3) Improving the level of economical and intensive use of land;  
(4) Coordinating the arrangement of land for production, living, and ecology in urban and rural areas, meeting the reasonable land needs of rural industries and infrastructure, and promoting the integrated development of urban and rural areas;  
(5) Protecting and improving the ecological environment, and ensuring the sustainable use of land;  
(6) Balancing the quantity and quality of cultivated land occupied and the cultivated land developed and reclaimed.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家建立国土空间规划体系。编制国土空间规划应当坚持生态优先，绿色、可持续发展，科学有序统筹安排生态、农业、城镇等功能空间，优化国土空间结构和布局，提升国土空间开发、保护的质量和效率。  
经依法批准的国土空间规划是各类开发、保护、建设活动的基本依据。已经编制国土空间规划的，不再编制土地利用总体规划和城乡规划。

**Article 18** The State shall establish a system for territorial space planning. The formulation of territorial space planning shall adhere to ecological priority, green and sustainable development, scientifically and orderly coordinate the arrangement of functional spaces such as ecology, agriculture, and towns, optimize the structure and layout of territorial space, and improve the quality and efficiency of territorial space development and protection.  
Territorial space planning approved according to law shall be the basic basis for various development, protection, and construction activities. Where territorial space planning has been formulated, no general plan for land utilization or urban and rural planning shall be formulated.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　县级土地利用总体规划应当划分土地利用区，明确土地用途。  
乡（镇）土地利用总体规划应当划分土地利用区，根据土地使用条件，确定每一块土地的用途，并予以公告。

**Article 19** The general plan for land utilization at the county level shall divide land utilization zones and specify the purposes of land use.  
The general plan for land utilization at the township (town) level shall divide land utilization zones, determine the purpose of each parcel of land based on the conditions for land use, and announce it to the public.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　土地利用总体规划实行分级审批。  
省、自治区、直辖市的土地利用总体规划，报国务院批准。  
省、自治区人民政府所在地的市、人口在一百万以上的城市以及国务院指定的城市的土地利用总体规划，经省、自治区人民政府审查同意后，报国务院批准。  
本条第二款、第三款规定以外的土地利用总体规划，逐级上报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准；其中，乡（镇）土地利用总体规划可以由省级人民政府授权的设区的市、自治州人民政府批准。  
土地利用总体规划一经批准，必须严格执行。

**Article 20** The general plan for land utilization shall be subject to hierarchical approval.  
The general plan for land utilization of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The general plan for land utilization of the city where the people's government of a province or autonomous region is located, cities with a population of more than one million, and cities designated by the State Council shall, after being examined and approved by the people's government of the province or autonomous region, be submitted to the State Council for approval.  
The general plan for land utilization other than those specified in the second and third paragraphs of this Article shall be submitted level by level to the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval; among them, the general plan for land utilization of a township (town) may be approved by the people's government of a city with districts or an autonomous prefecture authorized by the provincial people's government.  
Once the general plan for land utilization is approved, it must be strictly implemented.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　城市建设用地规模应当符合国家规定的标准，充分利用现有建设用地，不占或者尽量少占农用地。  
城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划，应当与土地利用总体规划相衔接，城市总体规划、村庄和集镇规划中建设用地规模不得超过土地利用总体规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地规模。  
在城市规划区内、村庄和集镇规划区内，城市和村庄、集镇建设用地应当符合城市规划、村庄和集镇规划。

**Article 21** The scale of urban construction land shall conform to the standards prescribed by the State, make full use of existing construction land, and shall not occupy or, to the extent possible, minimize the occupation of agricultural land.  
The overall urban plan, as well as the plans for villages and towns, shall be coordinated with the overall plan for land utilization. The scale of construction land in the overall urban plan and the plans for villages and towns shall not exceed the scale of construction land for cities, villages and towns as determined in the overall plan for land utilization.  
Within the planned urban areas and the planned areas of villages and towns, the construction land for cities, villages and towns shall conform to the urban plan and the plans for villages and towns.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　江河、湖泊综合治理和开发利用规划，应当与土地利用总体规划相衔接。在江河、湖泊、水库的管理和保护范围以及蓄洪滞洪区内，土地利用应当符合江河、湖泊综合治理和开发利用规划，符合河道、湖泊行洪、蓄洪和输水的要求。

**Article 22** The comprehensive regulation, development and utilization plans for rivers and lakes shall be coordinated with the overall plan for land utilization. Within the management and protection zones of rivers, lakes and reservoirs, as well as in flood storage and detention areas, land utilization shall comply with the comprehensive regulation, development and utilization plans for rivers and lakes, and meet the requirements for flood discharge, flood storage and water conveyance of river courses and lakes.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　各级人民政府应当加强土地利用计划管理，实行建设用地总量控制。  
土地利用年度计划，根据国民经济和社会发展计划、国家产业政策、土地利用总体规划以及建设用地和土地利用的实际状况编制。土地利用年度计划应当对[本法第六十三条](#art-63)规定的集体经营性建设用地作出合理安排。土地利用年度计划的编制审批程序与土地利用总体规划的编制审批程序相同，一经审批下达，必须严格执行。

**Article 23** The people's governments at all levels shall strengthen the management of land utilization plans and implement total control over the amount of construction land.  
The annual plan for land utilization shall be formulated in accordance with the national economic and social development plan, the national industrial policies, the overall plan for land utilization and the actual situation of construction land and land utilization. The annual plan for land utilization shall make reasonable arrangements for collectively-operated construction land as provided for in Article 63 of this Law. The procedures for formulating and approving the annual plan for land utilization shall be the same as those for formulating and approving the overall plan for land utilization. Once approved and issued, the annual plan for land utilization must be strictly implemented.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当将土地利用年度计划的执行情况列为国民经济和社会发展计划执行情况的内容，向同级人民代表大会报告。

**Article 24** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall include the implementation of the annual plan for land utilization as part of the implementation of the national economic and social development plan, and shall report to the people's congress at the same level.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　经批准的土地利用总体规划的修改，须经原批准机关批准；未经批准，不得改变土地利用总体规划确定的土地用途。  
经国务院批准的大型能源、交通、水利等基础设施建设用地，需要改变土地利用总体规划的，根据国务院的批准文件修改土地利用总体规划。  
经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准的能源、交通、水利等基础设施建设用地，需要改变土地利用总体规划的，属于省级人民政府土地利用总体规划批准权限内的，根据省级人民政府的批准文件修改土地利用总体规划。

**Article 25** Any modification to an approved overall plan for land utilization shall be subject to approval by the original approving authority; without approval, the land use purposes determined in the overall plan for land utilization shall not be changed.  
Where the land used for the construction of large-scale energy, transportation, water conservancy and other infrastructure projects approved by the State Council requires a modification to the overall plan for land utilization, the modification shall be made in accordance with the approval document of the State Council.  
Where the land used for the construction of energy, transportation, water conservancy and other infrastructure projects approved by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government requires a modification to the overall plan for land utilization, if such modification falls within the approval authority of the provincial-level people's government over the overall plan for land utilization, the modification shall be made in accordance with the approval document of such government.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国家建立土地调查制度。  
县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同同级有关部门进行土地调查。土地所有者或者使用者应当配合调查，并提供有关资料。

**Article 26** The State establishes a land survey system.  
The competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with other relevant departments at the same level, conduct land surveys. Land owners or users shall cooperate with the surveys and provide relevant information.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门会同同级有关部门根据土地调查成果、规划土地用途和国家制定的统一标准，评定土地等级。

**Article 27** The competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall, together with other relevant departments at the same level, evaluate the grades of land based on the results of land surveys, planned land use purposes and the uniform standards formulated by the State.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　国家建立土地统计制度。  
县级以上人民政府统计机构和自然资源主管部门依法进行土地统计调查，定期发布土地统计资料。土地所有者或者使用者应当提供有关资料，不得拒报、迟报，不得提供不真实、不完整的资料。  
统计机构和自然资源主管部门共同发布的土地面积统计资料是各级人民政府编制土地利用总体规划的依据。

**Article 28** The State establishes a land statistics system.  
The statistical institutions and the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall, according to law, conduct land statistical surveys and periodically release land statistical data. Land owners or users shall provide relevant data and shall not refuse to report, delay reporting, or provide false or incomplete data.  
The land area statistical data jointly released by the statistical institutions and the competent departments of natural resources shall serve as the basis for the people's governments at all levels to formulate the overall plan for land utilization.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家建立全国土地管理信息系统，对土地利用状况进行动态监测。

**Article 29** The State establishes a national land management information system to conduct dynamic monitoring of the state of land utilization.

### 第四章　耕地保护

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　国家保护耕地，严格控制耕地转为非耕地。  
国家实行占用耕地补偿制度。非农业建设经批准占用耕地的，按照“占多少，垦多少”的原则，由占用耕地的单位负责开垦与所占用耕地的数量和质量相当的耕地；没有条件开垦或者开垦的耕地不符合要求的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定缴纳耕地开垦费，专款用于开垦新的耕地。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当制定开垦耕地计划，监督占用耕地的单位按照计划开垦耕地或者按照计划组织开垦耕地，并进行验收。

**Article 30** The State protects cultivated land and strictly controls the conversion of cultivated land into non-cultivated land.  
The State implements a compensation system for the occupation of cultivated land. Where non-agricultural construction occupies cultivated land upon approval, the unit occupying the cultivated land shall, in accordance with the principle of "occupying how much and reclaiming how much", be responsible for reclaiming cultivated land of equivalent quantity and quality to the cultivated land occupied. If it is unable to reclaim or the reclaimed cultivated land does not meet the requirements, it shall pay a cultivated land reclamation fee in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government, and such fee shall be used exclusively for reclaiming new cultivated land.  
The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall formulate plans for reclaiming cultivated land, supervise the units occupying cultivated land to reclaim cultivated land according to the plans or organize reclamation according to the plans, and conduct acceptance inspections.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　县级以上地方人民政府可以要求占用耕地的单位将所占用耕地耕作层的土壤用于新开垦耕地、劣质地或者其他耕地的土壤改良。

**Article 31** The local people's governments at or above the county level may require the entities that occupy cultivated land to use the soil from the plough layer of the occupied cultivated land for soil improvement of newly reclaimed cultivated land, inferior land, or other cultivated land.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当严格执行土地利用总体规划和土地利用年度计划，采取措施，确保本行政区域内耕地总量不减少、质量不降低。耕地总量减少的，由国务院责令在规定期限内组织开垦与所减少耕地的数量与质量相当的耕地；耕地质量降低的，由国务院责令在规定期限内组织整治。新开垦和整治的耕地由国务院自然资源主管部门会同农业农村主管部门验收。  
个别省、直辖市确因土地后备资源匮乏，新增建设用地后，新开垦耕地的数量不足以补偿所占用耕地的数量的，必须报经国务院批准减免本行政区域内开垦耕地的数量，易地开垦数量和质量相当的耕地。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-8" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第8条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 32** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall strictly implement the overall plan for land utilization and the annual plan for land utilization, and shall take measures to ensure that the total amount of cultivated land within their respective administrative areas shall not decrease and the quality thereof shall not decline. Where the total amount of cultivated land decreases, the State Council shall order the organization of reclamation of cultivated land equivalent in quantity and quality to the decreased cultivated land within a specified period; where the quality of cultivated land declines, the State Council shall order the organization of improvement within a specified period. The newly reclaimed and improved cultivated land shall be inspected and accepted by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council jointly with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs.  
Where an individual province or municipality directly under the Central Government indeed has a shortage of reserve land resources, and after the addition of new construction land, the amount of newly reclaimed cultivated land is insufficient to compensate for the amount of cultivated land occupied, it must report to the State Council for approval to reduce the amount of reclaimed cultivated land within its administrative area, and reclaim cultivated land of equivalent quantity and quality in other areas.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　国家实行永久基本农田保护制度。下列耕地应当根据土地利用总体规划划为永久基本农田，实行严格保护：  
（一）经国务院农业农村主管部门或者县级以上地方人民政府批准确定的粮、棉、油、糖等重要农产品生产基地内的耕地；  
（二）有良好的水利与水土保持设施的耕地，正在实施改造计划以及可以改造的中、低产田和已建成的高标准农田；  
（三）蔬菜生产基地；  
（四）农业科研、教学试验田；  
（五）国务院规定应当划为永久基本农田的其他耕地。  
各省、自治区、直辖市划定的永久基本农田一般应当占本行政区域内耕地的百分之八十以上，具体比例由国务院根据各省、自治区、直辖市耕地实际情况规定。

**Article 33** The State shall implement the system of permanent basic farmland protection. The following cultivated land shall be designated as permanent basic farmland according to the overall plan for land utilization and shall be subject to strict protection:  
(1) Cultivated land within the production bases for important agricultural products such as grain, cotton, oil, and sugar as approved and determined by the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council or the local people's governments at or above the county level;  
(2) Cultivated land with good water conservancy and soil and water conservation facilities, medium- and low-yield fields that are under improvement plans or can be improved, and high-standard farmland that has been built;  
(3) Vegetable production bases;  
(4) Agricultural scientific research and teaching experiment fields;  
(5) Other cultivated land that the State Council prescribes shall be designated as permanent basic farmland.  
The permanent basic farmland designated by each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall generally account for not less than 80% of the cultivated land within its administrative area, and the specific proportion shall be prescribed by the State Council according to the actual conditions of cultivated land in each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　永久基本农田划定以乡（镇）为单位进行，由县级人民政府自然资源主管部门会同同级农业农村主管部门组织实施。永久基本农田应当落实到地块，纳入国家永久基本农田数据库严格管理。  
乡（镇）人民政府应当将永久基本农田的位置、范围向社会公告，并设立保护标志。

**Article 34** The designation of permanent basic farmland shall be carried out by taking the township (town) as a unit, and shall be organized and implemented by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at the county level jointly with the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs at the same level. The permanent basic farmland shall be assigned to specific plots and incorporated into the national permanent basic farmland database for strict management.  
The people's government of a township (town) shall announce to the public the location and scope of permanent basic farmland and shall set up protection signs.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　永久基本农田经依法划定后，任何单位和个人不得擅自占用或者改变其用途。国家能源、交通、水利、军事设施等重点建设项目选址确实难以避让永久基本农田，涉及农用地转用或者土地征收的，必须经国务院批准。  
禁止通过擅自调整县级土地利用总体规划、乡（镇）土地利用总体规划等方式规避永久基本农田农用地转用或者土地征收的审批。

**Article 35** After permanent basic farmland is designated according to law, no entity or individual may occupy or change its use without authorization. Where the site selection of key construction projects of the State, such as energy, transportation, water conservancy, and military installations, truly cannot avoid permanent basic farmland, and involves the conversion of agricultural land or land expropriation, it must be approved by the State Council.  
It is prohibited to evade the approval for conversion of agricultural land or expropriation of land involving permanent basic farmland by means such as unauthorized adjustment of the overall plan for land utilization at the county level or the overall plan for land utilization of a township (town).

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　各级人民政府应当采取措施，引导因地制宜轮作休耕，改良土壤，提高地力，维护排灌工程设施，防止土地荒漠化、盐渍化、水土流失和土壤污染。

**Article 36** People's governments at all levels shall take measures to guide crop rotation and fallowing according to local conditions, improve soil, enhance soil fertility, maintain drainage and irrigation engineering facilities, and prevent land desertification, salinization, soil erosion, and soil pollution.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　非农业建设必须节约使用土地，可以利用荒地的，不得占用耕地；可以利用劣地的，不得占用好地。  
禁止占用耕地建窑、建坟或者擅自在耕地上建房、挖砂、采石、采矿、取土等。  
禁止占用永久基本农田发展林果业和挖塘养鱼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-51" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 37** Non-agricultural construction must use land economically; where barren land can be used, cultivated land shall not be occupied; where inferior land can be used, good land shall not be occupied.  
It is prohibited to occupy cultivated land for building kilns or graves, or to build houses, dig sand, quarry, mine, or extract earth on cultivated land without authorization.  
It is prohibited to occupy permanent basic farmland for developing forestry and fruit industry or digging ponds for fish farming.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　禁止任何单位和个人闲置、荒芜耕地。已经办理审批手续的非农业建设占用耕地，一年内不用而又可以耕种并收获的，应当由原耕种该幅耕地的集体或者个人恢复耕种，也可以由用地单位组织耕种；一年以上未动工建设的，应当按照省、自治区、直辖市的规定缴纳闲置费；连续二年未使用的，经原批准机关批准，由县级以上人民政府无偿收回用地单位的土地使用权；该幅土地原为农民集体所有的，应当交由原农村集体经济组织恢复耕种。  
在城市规划区范围内，以出让方式取得土地使用权进行房地产开发的闲置土地，依照《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》的有关规定办理。

**Article 38** No entity or individual may cause cultivated land to lie idle or become barren. Where cultivated land occupied for non-agricultural construction for which the approval formalities have been completed is not used for one year and can still be cultivated and harvested, the collective or individual that originally cultivated the land shall resume cultivation, or the entity using the land may organize cultivation; where construction has not commenced for more than one year, idle fees shall be paid in accordance with the provisions of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government; where the land has not been used for two consecutive years, the people's government at or above the county level shall, with the approval of the original approving authority, take back the land use right of the entity using the land without compensation; if the land was originally owned by a peasant collective, it shall be handed over to the original rural collective economic organization for resumption of cultivation.  
For idle land obtained through transfer of land use right for real estate development within the scope of urban planning areas, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Urban Real Estate.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　国家鼓励单位和个人按照土地利用总体规划，在保护和改善生态环境、防止水土流失和土地荒漠化的前提下，开发未利用的土地；适宜开发为农用地的，应当优先开发成农用地。  
国家依法保护开发者的合法权益。

**Article 39** The State encourages entities and individuals to develop unused land in accordance with the overall plan for land utilization, under the premise of protecting and improving the ecological environment and preventing soil erosion and land desertification; land suitable for development as agricultural land shall be developed into agricultural land as a priority.  
The State shall protect the lawful rights and interests of developers according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　开垦未利用的土地，必须经过科学论证和评估，在土地利用总体规划划定的可开垦的区域内，经依法批准后进行。禁止毁坏森林、草原开垦耕地，禁止围湖造田和侵占江河滩地。  
根据土地利用总体规划，对破坏生态环境开垦、围垦的土地，有计划有步骤地退耕还林、还牧、还湖。

**Article 40** The reclamation of unused land must undergo scientific demonstration and evaluation, and shall be carried out within the reclaimable areas designated by the overall plan for land utilization and after approval according to law. It is prohibited to destroy forests or grasslands for the reclamation of cultivated land, and it is prohibited to reclaim land by draining lakes or to occupy river and lake beaches.  
In accordance with the overall plan for land utilization, land reclaimed or enclosed by reclamation that damages the ecological environment shall be returned to forest, grassland, or lakes in a planned and step-by-step manner.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　开发未确定使用权的国有荒山、荒地、荒滩从事种植业、林业、畜牧业、渔业生产的，经县级以上人民政府依法批准，可以确定给开发单位或者个人长期使用。

**Article 41** Where state-owned barren hills, barren land, and barren beaches with unconfirmed use rights are developed for crop cultivation, forestry, animal husbandry, or fishery production, such land may, upon approval according to law by the people's government at or above the county level, be allocated to the developing entity or individual for long-term use.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　国家鼓励土地整理。县、乡（镇）人民政府应当组织农村集体经济组织，按照土地利用总体规划，对田、水、路、林、村综合整治，提高耕地质量，增加有效耕地面积，改善农业生产条件和生态环境。  
地方各级人民政府应当采取措施，改造中、低产田，整治闲散地和废弃地。

**Article 42** The State encourages land consolidation. The people's governments at the county level and township (town) level shall organize rural collective economic organizations to comprehensively consolidate fields, water, roads, forests, and villages in accordance with the overall plan for land utilization, so as to improve the quality of cultivated land, increase the area of effective cultivated land, and enhance agricultural production conditions and the ecological environment.  
Local people's governments at all levels shall take measures to upgrade medium- and low-yield farmland, and consolidate idle and abandoned land.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　因挖损、塌陷、压占等造成土地破坏，用地单位和个人应当按照国家有关规定负责复垦；没有条件复垦或者复垦不符合要求的，应当缴纳土地复垦费，专项用于土地复垦。复垦的土地应当优先用于农业。

**Article 43** Where land is damaged due to excavation, subsidence, covering, or other causes, the land-using entity or individual shall be responsible for reclamation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State; if there is no condition for reclamation or the reclamation does not meet the requirements, a land reclamation fee shall be paid, which shall be used exclusively for land reclamation. The reclaimed land shall be used preferentially for agriculture.

### 第五章　建设用地

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　建设占用土地，涉及农用地转为建设用地的，应当办理农用地转用审批手续。  
永久基本农田转为建设用地的，由国务院批准。  
在土地利用总体规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地规模范围内，为实施该规划而将永久基本农田以外的农用地转为建设用地的，按土地利用年度计划分批次按照国务院规定由原批准土地利用总体规划的机关或者其授权的机关批准。在已批准的农用地转用范围内，具体建设项目用地可以由市、县人民政府批准。  
在土地利用总体规划确定的城市和村庄、集镇建设用地规模范围外，将永久基本农田以外的农用地转为建设用地的，由国务院或者国务院授权的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。

**Article 44** Where land is to be used for construction and involves the conversion of agricultural land into construction land, the procedures for examination and approval of such conversion shall be completed.  
The conversion of permanent basic farmland into construction land shall be subject to approval by the State Council.  
Within the scope of the construction land scale for cities, villages, and market towns as determined in the overall plan for land utilization, the conversion of agricultural land other than permanent basic farmland into construction land for the purpose of implementing the plan shall be approved in batches according to the annual plan for land utilization and in accordance with the provisions of the State Council by the authority that originally approved the overall plan for land utilization or by the authority authorized thereby. Within the scope of approved conversion of agricultural land, the land for specific construction projects may be approved by the people's government of a city or county.  
Outside the scope of the construction land scale for cities, villages, and market towns as determined in the overall plan for land utilization, the conversion of agricultural land other than permanent basic farmland into construction land shall be approved by the State Council or by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council as authorized by the State Council.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　为了公共利益的需要，有下列情形之一，确需征收农民集体所有的土地的，可以依法实施征收：  
（一）军事和外交需要用地的；  
（二）由政府组织实施的能源、交通、水利、通信、邮政等基础设施建设需要用地的；  
（三）由政府组织实施的科技、教育、文化、卫生、体育、生态环境和资源保护、防灾减灾、文物保护、社区综合服务、社会福利、市政公用、优抚安置、英烈保护等公共事业需要用地的；  
（四）由政府组织实施的扶贫搬迁、保障性安居工程建设需要用地的；  
（五）在土地利用总体规划确定的城镇建设用地范围内，经省级以上人民政府批准由县级以上地方人民政府组织实施的成片开发建设需要用地的；  
（六）法律规定为公共利益需要可以征收农民集体所有的土地的其他情形。  
前款规定的建设活动，应当符合国民经济和社会发展规划、土地利用总体规划、城乡规划和专项规划；第（四）项、第（五）项规定的建设活动，还应当纳入国民经济和社会发展年度计划；第（五）项规定的成片开发并应当符合国务院自然资源主管部门规定的标准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-26" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第26条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第30条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 45** For the needs of the public interest, under any of the following circumstances, if it is truly necessary to expropriate land owned by peasant collectives, expropriation may be carried out according to law:  
(1) Land is needed for military and diplomatic purposes;  
(2) Land is needed for the construction of infrastructure such as energy, transportation, water conservancy, communications, and postal services, carried out by the government;  
(3) Land is needed for public utilities such as science and technology, education, culture, health, sports, ecological environment and resource protection, disaster prevention and mitigation, cultural relic protection, community comprehensive services, social welfare, municipal public facilities, special care and placement, and protection of heroes and martyrs, carried out by the government;  
(4) Land is needed for poverty alleviation relocation and the construction of government-subsidized housing projects, carried out by the government;  
(5) Within the range of urban construction land determined in the overall plan for land utilization, land is needed for contiguous development and construction carried out by the local people's government at or above the county level with the approval of the people's government at or above the provincial level;  
(6) Other circumstances under which the expropriation of land owned by peasant collectives may be carried out for the needs of the public interest as provided by law.  
The construction activities specified in the preceding paragraph shall conform to the national economic and social development plan, the overall plan for land utilization, the urban and rural plan, and special plans; the construction activities specified in items (4) and (5) shall also be incorporated into the annual plan for national economic and social development; the contiguous development specified in item (5) shall also meet the standards prescribed by the competent department of natural resources of the State Council.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　征收下列土地的，由国务院批准：  
（一）永久基本农田；  
（二）永久基本农田以外的耕地超过三十五公顷的；  
（三）其他土地超过七十公顷的。  
征收前款规定以外的土地的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准。  
征收农用地的，应当依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)的规定先行办理农用地转用审批。其中，经国务院批准农用地转用的，同时办理征地审批手续，不再另行办理征地审批；经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府在征地批准权限内批准农用地转用的，同时办理征地审批手续，不再另行办理征地审批，超过征地批准权限的，应当依照本条第一款的规定另行办理征地审批。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-30" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第30条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 46** The expropriation of the following land shall be approved by the State Council:  
(1) Permanent basic farmland;  
(2) Cultivated land other than permanent basic farmland exceeding thirty-five hectares;  
(3) Other land exceeding seventy hectares.  
The expropriation of land other than that specified in the preceding paragraph shall be approved by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council.  
Where agricultural land is to be expropriated, the procedures for examination and approval of conversion of agricultural land shall be completed first in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of this Law. Where the conversion of agricultural land is approved by the State Council, the procedures for examination and approval of land expropriation shall be completed simultaneously, and no separate procedures for examination and approval of land expropriation shall be undertaken; where the conversion of agricultural land is approved by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council within the limits of its approval authority for land expropriation, the procedures for examination and approval of land expropriation shall be completed simultaneously, and no separate procedures for examination and approval of land expropriation shall be undertaken; if the conversion exceeds the approval authority for land expropriation, separate procedures for examination and approval of land expropriation shall be undertaken in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　国家征收土地的，依照法定程序批准后，由县级以上地方人民政府予以公告并组织实施。  
县级以上地方人民政府拟申请征收土地的，应当开展拟征收土地现状调查和社会稳定风险评估，并将征收范围、土地现状、征收目的、补偿标准、安置方式和社会保障等在拟征收土地所在的乡（镇）和村、村民小组范围内公告至少三十日，听取被征地的农村集体经济组织及其成员、村民委员会和其他利害关系人的意见。  
多数被征地的农村集体经济组织成员认为征地补偿安置方案不符合法律、法规规定的，县级以上地方人民政府应当组织召开听证会，并根据法律、法规的规定和听证会情况修改方案。  
拟征收土地的所有权人、使用权人应当在公告规定期限内，持不动产权属证明材料办理补偿登记。县级以上地方人民政府应当组织有关部门测算并落实有关费用，保证足额到位，与拟征收土地的所有权人、使用权人就补偿、安置等签订协议；个别确实难以达成协议的，应当在申请征收土地时如实说明。  
相关前期工作完成后，县级以上地方人民政府方可申请征收土地。

**Article 47** Where the State expropriates land, after approval according to legal procedures, the local people's government at or above the county level shall announce it and organize its implementation.  
Where a local people's government at or above the county level intends to apply for land expropriation, it shall conduct a current status survey of the land to be expropriated and a social stability risk assessment, and shall announce the scope of expropriation, current status of the land, purpose of expropriation, compensation standards, methods of resettlement, and social security within the scope of the township (town) and village, and villager groups where the land to be expropriated is located, for a period of at least thirty days, and shall hear the opinions of the expropriated rural collective economic organizations and their members, the villagers' committees, and other interested parties.  
If a majority of the members of the expropriated rural collective economic organization consider that the compensation and resettlement plan for land expropriation does not comply with the provisions of laws and regulations, the local people's government at or above the county level shall convene a hearing and modify the plan in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and the circumstances of the hearing.  
The owners and usufructuaries of the land to be expropriated shall, within the time limit prescribed in the announcement, present the certification materials for real estate rights and handle compensation registration. The local people's government at or above the county level shall organize the relevant departments to calculate and implement the relevant fees, ensure that they are fully in place, and sign agreements with the owners and usufructuaries of the land to be expropriated on matters such as compensation and resettlement; if it is truly difficult to reach an agreement with individual parties, this fact shall be truthfully stated when applying for land expropriation.  
After the completion of the relevant preliminary work, the local people's government at or above the county level may apply for land expropriation.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　征收土地应当给予公平、合理的补偿，保障被征地农民原有生活水平不降低、长远生计有保障。  
征收土地应当依法及时足额支付土地补偿费、安置补助费以及农村村民住宅、其他地上附着物和青苗等的补偿费用，并安排被征地农民的社会保障费用。  
征收农用地的土地补偿费、安置补助费标准由省、自治区、直辖市通过制定公布区片综合地价确定。制定区片综合地价应当综合考虑土地原用途、土地资源条件、土地产值、土地区位、土地供求关系、人口以及经济社会发展水平等因素，并至少每三年调整或者重新公布一次。  
征收农用地以外的其他土地、地上附着物和青苗等的补偿标准，由省、自治区、直辖市制定。对其中的农村村民住宅，应当按照先补偿后搬迁、居住条件有改善的原则，尊重农村村民意愿，采取重新安排宅基地建房、提供安置房或者货币补偿等方式给予公平、合理的补偿，并对因征收造成的搬迁、临时安置等费用予以补偿，保障农村村民居住的权利和合法的住房财产权益。  
县级以上地方人民政府应当将被征地农民纳入相应的养老等社会保障体系。被征地农民的社会保障费用主要用于符合条件的被征地农民的养老保险等社会保险缴费补贴。被征地农民社会保障费用的筹集、管理和使用办法，由省、自治区、直辖市制定。

**Article 48** Expropriation of land shall provide fair and reasonable compensation to ensure that the original living standards of the farmers whose land is expropriated are not lowered and that their long-term livelihoods are secured.  
Land compensation fees, resettlement subsidies, and compensation fees for rural villagers' houses, other attachments on the land, and seedlings, etc., shall be paid in full and on time according to law, and social security fees for the farmers whose land is expropriated shall be arranged.  
The standards for land compensation fees and resettlement subsidies for expropriated agricultural land shall be determined by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the State Council by formulating and publishing comprehensive zonal land prices. When formulating comprehensive zonal land prices, factors such as the original use of the land, conditions of land resources, land output value, land location, land supply and demand, population, and the level of economic and social development shall be comprehensively considered, and such prices shall be adjusted or re-published at least once every three years.  
Compensation standards for land other than agricultural land, attachments on the land, and seedlings, etc., shall be formulated by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the State Council. For rural villagers' houses among them, in accordance with the principle of compensation before relocation and improvement of living conditions, and respecting the wishes of rural villagers, fair and reasonable compensation shall be provided by means of re-arranging homesteads for building houses, providing resettlement houses, or monetary compensation, and expenses for relocation and temporary resettlement caused by expropriation shall be compensated, so as to protect the right of rural villagers to housing and their lawful property rights and interests in housing.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall include the farmers whose land is expropriated in the corresponding pension and other social security systems. The social security fees for farmers whose land is expropriated shall be mainly used for subsidies for social insurance contributions such as old-age insurance for eligible farmers whose land is expropriated. The measures for the raising, management, and use of social security fees for farmers whose land is expropriated shall be formulated by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the State Council.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　被征地的农村集体经济组织应当将征收土地的补偿费用的收支状况向本集体经济组织的成员公布，接受监督。  
禁止侵占、挪用被征收土地单位的征地补偿费用和其他有关费用。

**Article 49** The expropriated rural collective economic organization shall disclose the revenue and expenditure of the compensation fees for land expropriation to the members of that collective economic organization and accept supervision.  
It is prohibited to embezzle or misappropriate the land expropriation compensation fees and other related fees of the unit whose land is expropriated.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　地方各级人民政府应当支持被征地的农村集体经济组织和农民从事开发经营，兴办企业。

**Article 50** Local people's governments at all levels shall support the expropriated rural collective economic organizations and peasants in engaging in development and management, and in establishing enterprises.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　大中型水利、水电工程建设征收土地的补偿费标准和移民安置办法，由国务院另行规定。

**Article 51** The standards for compensation fees for land expropriated for the construction of large and medium-sized water conservancy and hydroelectric projects and the methods for resettlement of relocated persons shall be separately prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　建设项目可行性研究论证时，自然资源主管部门可以根据土地利用总体规划、土地利用年度计划和建设用地标准，对建设用地有关事项进行审查，并提出意见。

**Article 52** When the feasibility study of a construction project is being demonstrated, the competent department of natural resources may, in accordance with the overall plan for land utilization, the annual plan for land utilization, and the standards for construction land, examine the relevant matters of construction land and put forward opinions.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　经批准的建设项目需要使用国有建设用地的，建设单位应当持法律、行政法规规定的有关文件，向有批准权的县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门提出建设用地申请，经自然资源主管部门审查，报本级人民政府批准。

**Article 53** Where an approved construction project needs to use state-owned construction land, the construction entity shall, with the relevant documents prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, submit an application for construction land to the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level that has the approval authority; after examination by the competent department of natural resources, the application shall be submitted to the people's government at the same level for approval.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　建设单位使用国有土地，应当以出让等有偿使用方式取得；但是，下列建设用地，经县级以上人民政府依法批准，可以以划拨方式取得：  
（一）国家机关用地和军事用地；  
（二）城市基础设施用地和公益事业用地；  
（三）国家重点扶持的能源、交通、水利等基础设施用地；  
（四）法律、行政法规规定的其他用地。

**Article 54** Construction entities shall obtain the right to use state-owned land through such compensated means as conveyance; however, the following construction land may be obtained through allocation upon approval according to law by the people's government at or above the county level:  
(1) Land for state organs and military purposes;  
(2) Land for urban infrastructure and public welfare undertakings;  
(3) Land for key energy, transportation, water conservancy, and other infrastructure projects supported by the State;  
(4) Other land as provided by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　以出让等有偿使用方式取得国有土地使用权的建设单位，按照国务院规定的标准和办法，缴纳土地使用权出让金等土地有偿使用费和其他费用后，方可使用土地。  
自本法施行之日起，新增建设用地的土地有偿使用费，百分之三十上缴中央财政，百分之七十留给有关地方人民政府。具体使用管理办法由国务院财政部门会同有关部门制定，并报国务院批准。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-19" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 55** A construction entity that obtains the right to use state-owned land through such compensated means as conveyance shall, after paying the conveyance fee for the right to use land and other fees for compensated use of land and other expenses according to the standards and methods prescribed by the State Council, be entitled to use the land.  
As of the date of coming into force of this Law, thirty percent of the fees for compensated use of land for new construction land shall be turned over to the central finance, and seventy percent shall be retained by the relevant local people's government. The specific measures for the use and management of such fees shall be formulated by the finance department of the State Council together with the relevant departments and submitted to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　建设单位使用国有土地的，应当按照土地使用权出让等有偿使用合同的约定或者土地使用权划拨批准文件的规定使用土地；确需改变该幅土地建设用途的，应当经有关人民政府自然资源主管部门同意，报原批准用地的人民政府批准。其中，在城市规划区内改变土地用途的，在报批前，应当先经有关城市规划行政主管部门同意。

**Article 56** A construction entity using state-owned land shall use the land in accordance with the provisions of the contract for compensated use such as conveyance of the right to use land or the provisions of the document approving the allocation of the right to use land; if it is truly necessary to change the use of the land for construction, it shall obtain the consent of the competent department of natural resources of the relevant people's government and submit the matter to the people's government that originally approved the land for approval. Where the change of land use is within the urban planning area, the consent of the relevant competent department of urban planning shall be obtained before the matter is submitted for approval.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　建设项目施工和地质勘查需要临时使用国有土地或者农民集体所有的土地的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门批准。其中，在城市规划区内的临时用地，在报批前，应当先经有关城市规划行政主管部门同意。土地使用者应当根据土地权属，与有关自然资源主管部门或者农村集体经济组织、村民委员会签订临时使用土地合同，并按照合同的约定支付临时使用土地补偿费。  
临时使用土地的使用者应当按照临时使用土地合同约定的用途使用土地，并不得修建永久性建筑物。  
临时使用土地期限一般不超过二年。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-52" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第52条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 57** Where construction project construction and geological exploration require the temporary use of state-owned land or land owned by peasant collectives, approval shall be obtained from the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level. Where the temporary use of land is within the urban planning area, the consent of the relevant competent department of urban planning shall be obtained before the matter is submitted for approval. The land user shall, according to the ownership of the land, enter into a contract for temporary use of land with the relevant competent department of natural resources or the rural collective economic organization or villagers' committee, and pay compensation fees for temporary use of land in accordance with the contract.  
The user of temporarily used land shall use the land in accordance with the purposes agreed upon in the contract for temporary use of land and shall not construct permanent structures.  
The term of temporary use of land shall generally not exceed two years.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　有下列情形之一的，由有关人民政府自然资源主管部门报经原批准用地的人民政府或者有批准权的人民政府批准，可以收回国有土地使用权：  
（一）为实施城市规划进行旧城区改建以及其他公共利益需要，确需使用土地的；  
（二）土地出让等有偿使用合同约定的使用期限届满，土地使用者未申请续期或者申请续期未获批准的；  
（三）因单位撤销、迁移等原因，停止使用原划拨的国有土地的；  
（四）公路、铁路、机场、矿场等经核准报废的。  
依照前款第（一）项的规定收回国有土地使用权的，对土地使用权人应当给予适当补偿。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于破产企业国有划拨土地使用权应否列入破产财产等问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 58** Under any of the following circumstances, the competent department of natural resources of the relevant people's government may, after submitting the matter to the people's government that originally approved the land or the people's government with approval authority for approval, reclaim the right to use state-owned land:  
(1) The land is truly needed for the implementation of urban planning for reconstruction of old urban areas or for other public interest needs;  
(2) The term of use agreed upon in the contract for compensated use such as conveyance of land expires, and the land user neither applies for an extension nor obtains approval for such extension;  
(3) Due to the dissolution, relocation, or other reasons of the unit, the use of the originally allocated state-owned land is discontinued;  
(4) Highways, railways, airports, mines, etc., have been approved for decommissioning.  
Where the right to use state-owned land is reclaimed in accordance with item (1) of the preceding paragraph, appropriate compensation shall be given to the holder of the right to use land.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　乡镇企业、乡（镇）村公共设施、公益事业、农村村民住宅等乡（镇）村建设，应当按照村庄和集镇规划，合理布局，综合开发，配套建设；建设用地，应当符合乡（镇）土地利用总体规划和土地利用年度计划，并依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)、第六十条、第六十一条、第六十二条的规定办理审批手续。

**Article 59** Township enterprises, public facilities and public welfare undertakings in townships (towns) and villages, and rural villagers' houses, and other construction in townships (towns) and villages shall have a rational layout, comprehensive development, and supporting construction in accordance with the village and market town plan; the construction land shall conform to the overall plan for land utilization at the township (town) level and the annual plan for land utilization, and the procedures for examination and approval shall be completed in accordance with the provisions of Articles 44, 60, 61, and 62 of this Law.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　农村集体经济组织使用乡（镇）土地利用总体规划确定的建设用地兴办企业或者与其他单位、个人以土地使用权入股、联营等形式共同举办企业的，应当持有关批准文件，向县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请，按照省、自治区、直辖市规定的批准权限，由县级以上地方人民政府批准；其中，涉及占用农用地的，依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)的规定办理审批手续。  
按照前款规定兴办企业的建设用地，必须严格控制。省、自治区、直辖市可以按照乡镇企业的不同行业和经营规模，分别规定用地标准。

**Article 60** Where a rural collective economic organization uses construction land as determined in the overall plan for land utilization at the township (town) level to establish an enterprise or to jointly establish an enterprise with other units or individuals through the contribution of the right to use land as shares or joint operation, it shall hold the relevant approval documents and submit an application to the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level, and the application shall be approved by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the approval authority prescribed by the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the State Council; where the occupation of agricultural land is involved, the procedures for examination and approval shall be completed in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of this Law.  
The construction land for enterprises established in accordance with the preceding paragraph must be strictly controlled. Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the State Council may, according to the different industries and operation scales of township enterprises, separately prescribe land use standards.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　乡（镇）村公共设施、公益事业建设，需要使用土地的，经乡（镇）人民政府审核，向县级以上地方人民政府自然资源主管部门提出申请，按照省、自治区、直辖市规定的批准权限，由县级以上地方人民政府批准；其中，涉及占用农用地的，依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)的规定办理审批手续。

**Article 61** Where land is needed for the construction of public facilities or public welfare undertakings in townships (towns) or villages, the matter shall be examined by the people's government of the township (town), an application shall be submitted to the competent department of natural resources of the local people's government at or above the county level, and approval shall be granted by the local people's government at or above the county level in accordance with the approval authority prescribed by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government; where the occupation of agricultural land is involved, the examination and approval procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of this Law.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　农村村民一户只能拥有一处宅基地，其宅基地的面积不得超过省、自治区、直辖市规定的标准。  
人均土地少、不能保障一户拥有一处宅基地的地区，县级人民政府在充分尊重农村村民意愿的基础上，可以采取措施，按照省、自治区、直辖市规定的标准保障农村村民实现户有所居。  
农村村民建住宅，应当符合乡（镇）土地利用总体规划、村庄规划，不得占用永久基本农田，并尽量使用原有的宅基地和村内空闲地。编制乡（镇）土地利用总体规划、村庄规划应当统筹并合理安排宅基地用地，改善农村村民居住环境和条件。  
农村村民住宅用地，由乡（镇）人民政府审核批准；其中，涉及占用农用地的，依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)的规定办理审批手续。  
农村村民出卖、出租、赠与住宅后，再申请宅基地的，不予批准。  
国家允许进城落户的农村村民依法自愿有偿退出宅基地，鼓励农村集体经济组织及其成员盘活利用闲置宅基地和闲置住宅。  
国务院农业农村主管部门负责全国农村宅基地改革和管理有关工作。

**Article 62** Each rural household may own only one homestead, and the area of the homestead shall not exceed the standards prescribed by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
In regions with little per capita land where it is impossible to ensure that each household has one homestead, the people's government at the county level may, on the basis of fully respecting the wishes of rural villagers, take measures to ensure that rural villagers have a place to live in accordance with the standards prescribed by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.  
When building a house, a rural villager shall comply with the overall plan for land use of the township (town) and the village plan, shall not occupy permanent basic farmland, and shall, to the extent possible, use the existing homestead and idle land within the village. The formulation of the overall plan for land use of a township (town) and the village plan shall make overall arrangements and reasonable arrangements for homestead land to improve the living environment and conditions of rural villagers.  
The land for rural villagers' houses shall be examined and approved by the people's government of the township (town); where the occupation of agricultural land is involved, the examination and approval procedures shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of this Law.  
If a rural villager sells, leases, or donates the house, an application for a new homestead shall not be approved.  
The State allows rural villagers who have settled in cities or towns to voluntarily, with compensation, and according to law, withdraw from their homesteads, and encourages rural collective economic organizations and their members to revitalize and utilize idle homesteads and idle houses.  
The competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the State Council shall be responsible for the nationwide reform and administration of rural homesteads.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　土地利用总体规划、城乡规划确定为工业、商业等经营性用途，并经依法登记的集体经营性建设用地，土地所有权人可以通过出让、出租等方式交由单位或者个人使用，并应当签订书面合同，载明土地界址、面积、动工期限、使用期限、土地用途、规划条件和双方其他权利义务。  
前款规定的集体经营性建设用地出让、出租等，应当经本集体经济组织成员的村民会议三分之二以上成员或者三分之二以上村民代表的同意。  
通过出让等方式取得的集体经营性建设用地使用权可以转让、互换、出资、赠与或者抵押，但法律、行政法规另有规定或者土地所有权人、土地使用权人签订的书面合同另有约定的除外。  
集体经营性建设用地的出租，集体建设用地使用权的出让及其最高年限、转让、互换、出资、赠与、抵押等，参照同类用途的国有建设用地执行。具体办法由国务院制定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-40" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第40条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 63** Where collective operational construction land is designated for operational uses such as industry, commerce, etc. in the overall plan for land use and the urban and rural plan, and has been registered according to law, the owner of the land right may transfer such land to any unit or individual for use through methods such as assignment or leasing, and shall enter into a written contract specifying the boundary, area, commencement date, term of use, land use, planning conditions, and other rights and obligations of both parties.  
The assignment, leasing, etc. of the collective operational construction land as mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be subject to the consent of more than two-thirds of the members of the villagers' meeting of the collective economic organization or more than two-thirds of the villagers' representatives.  
The right to use collective operational construction land obtained through assignment or other methods may be transferred, exchanged, contributed as capital, donated, or mortgaged, except where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations or otherwise agreed in the written contract entered into by the owner of the land right and the holder of the land-use right.  
The leasing of collective operational construction land, the assignment of the right to use collective construction land and its maximum term, as well as the transfer, exchange, contribution as capital, donation, mortgage, etc., shall be implemented with reference to the state-owned construction land for similar uses. Specific measures shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　集体建设用地的使用者应当严格按照土地利用总体规划、城乡规划确定的用途使用土地。

**Article 64** The holder of the right to use collective construction land shall use the land strictly in accordance with the uses determined in the overall plan for land use and the urban and rural plan.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　在土地利用总体规划制定前已建的不符合土地利用总体规划确定的用途的建筑物、构筑物，不得重建、扩建。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-53" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第53条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 65** Buildings and structures constructed before the formulation of the overall plan for land use that do not conform to the uses determined in the overall plan for land use shall not be rebuilt or expanded.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　有下列情形之一的，农村集体经济组织报经原批准用地的人民政府批准，可以收回土地使用权：  
（一）为乡（镇）村公共设施和公益事业建设，需要使用土地的；  
（二）不按照批准的用途使用土地的；  
（三）因撤销、迁移等原因而停止使用土地的。  
依照前款第（一）项规定收回农民集体所有的土地的，对土地使用权人应当给予适当补偿。  
收回集体经营性建设用地使用权，依照双方签订的书面合同办理，法律、行政法规另有规定的除外。

**Article 66** In any of the following circumstances, a rural collective economic organization may, after obtaining approval from the people's government that originally approved the land use, take back the land-use right:  
(1) Where land is needed for the construction of public facilities or public welfare undertakings in a township (town) or village;  
(2) Where the land is not used in accordance with the approved purpose;  
(3) Where the use of the land has ceased due to reasons such as dissolution or relocation.  
Where the land owned by a farmers' collective is taken back in accordance with subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph, appropriate compensation shall be given to the holder of the land-use right.  
The taking back of the right to use collective operational construction land shall be handled in accordance with the written contract entered into by both parties, except where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门对违反土地管理法律、法规的行为进行监督检查。  
县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门对违反农村宅基地管理法律、法规的行为进行监督检查的，适用本法关于自然资源主管部门监督检查的规定。  
土地管理监督检查人员应当熟悉土地管理法律、法规，忠于职守、秉公执法。

**Article 67** The competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall supervise and inspect the violations of land administration laws and regulations.  
Where the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level supervise and inspect the violations of laws and regulations on the administration of rural homesteads, the provisions of this Law on the supervision and inspection by the competent departments of natural resources shall apply.  
The personnel responsible for the supervision and inspection of land administration shall be familiar with the laws and regulations on land administration, be faithful to their duties, and enforce the law impartially.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门履行监督检查职责时，有权采取下列措施：  
（一）要求被检查的单位或者个人提供有关土地权利的文件和资料，进行查阅或者予以复制；  
（二）要求被检查的单位或者个人就有关土地权利的问题作出说明；  
（三）进入被检查单位或者个人非法占用的土地现场进行勘测；  
（四）责令非法占用土地的单位或者个人停止违反土地管理法律、法规的行为。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-48" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第48条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 68** When performing their supervision and inspection duties, the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level shall have the authority to take the following measures:  
(1) To require the unit or individual under inspection to provide documents and materials concerning land rights for examination or copying;  
(2) To require the unit or individual under inspection to make explanations on issues concerning land rights;  
(3) To enter the site of the land illegally occupied by the unit or individual under inspection for survey;  
(4) To order the unit or individual that illegally occupies land to cease the acts that violate the laws and regulations on land administration.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　土地管理监督检查人员履行职责，需要进入现场进行勘测、要求有关单位或者个人提供文件、资料和作出说明的，应当出示土地管理监督检查证件。

**Article 69** When the personnel responsible for the supervision and inspection of land administration need to enter the site for survey or require relevant units or individuals to provide documents, materials, or make explanations in the performance of their duties, they shall produce their land administration supervision and inspection certificates.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　有关单位和个人对县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门就土地违法行为进行的监督检查应当支持与配合，并提供工作方便，不得拒绝与阻碍土地管理监督检查人员依法执行职务。

**Article 70** The relevant units and individuals shall support and cooperate with the supervision and inspection conducted by the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level in respect of illegal activities concerning land, and shall provide convenience, and shall not refuse or obstruct the land administration supervision and inspection personnel in the lawful performance of their duties.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门在监督检查工作中发现国家工作人员的违法行为，依法应当给予处分的，应当依法予以处理；自己无权处理的，应当依法移送监察机关或者有关机关处理。

**Article 71** Where, during supervision and inspection, the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level discover illegal acts committed by state functionaries for which sanctions shall be imposed according to law, they shall impose such sanctions according to law; if they have no authority to impose sanctions, they shall refer the matter to the supervisory authority or relevant authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门在监督检查工作中发现土地违法行为构成犯罪的，应当将案件移送有关机关，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，应当依法给予行政处罚。

**Article 72** Where, during supervision and inspection, the competent departments of natural resources of the people's governments at or above the county level discover that a land law violation constitutes a crime, they shall transfer the case to the relevant authority for investigating criminal liability according to law; if the violation does not constitute a crime, an administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　依照本法规定应当给予行政处罚，而有关自然资源主管部门不给予行政处罚的，上级人民政府自然资源主管部门有权责令有关自然资源主管部门作出行政处罚决定或者直接给予行政处罚，并给予有关自然资源主管部门的负责人处分。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-49" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第49条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 73** Where an administrative penalty should be imposed in accordance with the provisions of this Law, but the relevant competent department of natural resources fails to do so, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at a higher level shall have the authority to order the relevant competent department of natural resources to make a decision on the administrative penalty or directly impose the administrative penalty, and impose a sanction on the person in charge of the relevant competent department of natural resources.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　买卖或者以其他形式非法转让土地的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门没收违法所得；对违反土地利用总体规划擅自将农用地改为建设用地的，限期拆除在非法转让的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施，恢复土地原状，对符合土地利用总体规划的，没收在非法转让的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施；可以并处罚款；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-54" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第54条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-58" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第58条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 74** Where land is sold or otherwise illegally transferred, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall confiscate the illegal gains; if the agricultural land is changed to construction land without authorization in violation of the overall plan for land use, the buildings and other facilities newly built on the illegally transferred land shall be demolished within a prescribed time limit and the land shall be restored to its original state; if the change conforms to the overall plan for land use, the buildings and other facilities newly built on the illegally transferred land shall be confiscated; a fine may also be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly liable shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　违反本法规定，占用耕地建窑、建坟或者擅自在耕地上建房、挖砂、采石、采矿、取土等，破坏种植条件的，或者因开发土地造成土地荒漠化、盐渍化的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门等按照职责责令限期改正或者治理，可以并处罚款；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-51" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-55" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第55条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 75** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, occupies cultivated land to build kilns, graves, or builds houses, excavates sand, quarries stone, mines, or removes earth on cultivated land without authorization, thereby destroying the cultivation conditions, or causes land desertification or salinization due to land development, shall be ordered by the competent departments of natural resources and agriculture and rural affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level, within their respective functions and duties, to make corrections or carry out treatment within a prescribed time limit; a fine may also be imposed; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　违反本法规定，拒不履行土地复垦义务的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正；逾期不改正的，责令缴纳复垦费，专项用于土地复垦，可以处以罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-56" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第56条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 76** Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, refuses to perform the obligation of land reclamation, shall be ordered by the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level to make corrections within a prescribed time limit; if the corrections are not made within the time limit, the person shall be ordered to pay a reclamation fee, which shall be used exclusively for land reclamation, and a fine may be imposed.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　未经批准或者采取欺骗手段骗取批准，非法占用土地的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令退还非法占用的土地，对违反土地利用总体规划擅自将农用地改为建设用地的，限期拆除在非法占用的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施，恢复土地原状，对符合土地利用总体规划的，没收在非法占用的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施，可以并处罚款；对非法占用土地单位的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
超过批准的数量占用土地，多占的土地以非法占用土地论处。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-57" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第57条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 77** Where land is illegally occupied without approval or by obtaining approval through fraudulent means, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the illegally occupied land; if the agricultural land is changed to construction land without authorization in violation of the overall plan for land use, the buildings and other facilities newly built on the illegally occupied land shall be demolished within a prescribed time limit and the land shall be restored to its original state; if the change conforms to the overall plan for land use, the buildings and other facilities newly built on the illegally occupied land shall be confiscated; a fine may also be imposed; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly liable of the unit that illegally occupies land shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where land is occupied exceeding the approved area, the excess land shall be dealt with as illegally occupied land.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　农村村民未经批准或者采取欺骗手段骗取批准，非法占用土地建住宅的，由县级以上人民政府农业农村主管部门责令退还非法占用的土地，限期拆除在非法占用的土地上新建的房屋。  
超过省、自治区、直辖市规定的标准，多占的土地以非法占用土地论处。

**Article 78** Where a rural villager illegally occupies land to build a house without approval or by obtaining approval through fraudulent means, the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the illegally occupied land and the demolition of the houses newly built on the illegally occupied land within a prescribed time limit.  
Where the area exceeds the standards prescribed by the provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government, the excess land shall be dealt with as illegally occupied land.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　无权批准征收、使用土地的单位或者个人非法批准占用土地的，超越批准权限非法批准占用土地的，不按照土地利用总体规划确定的用途批准用地的，或者违反法律规定的程序批准占用、征收土地的，其批准文件无效，对非法批准征收、使用土地的直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。非法批准、使用的土地应当收回，有关当事人拒不归还的，以非法占用土地论处。  
非法批准征收、使用土地，对当事人造成损失的，依法应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 79** Where a unit or individual without the authority to approve the expropriation or use of land illegally approves the occupation of land, or illegally approves the occupation of land beyond the authorized limits, or approves the use of land not in accordance with the purposes determined in the overall plan for land use, or approves the occupation or expropriation of land in violation of the procedures prescribed by law, the approval document shall be null and void; the persons directly in charge and other persons directly liable for the illegal approval of expropriation or use of land shall be given sanctions according to law; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law. The land illegally approved or used shall be taken back; if the relevant party refuses to return it, the party shall be dealt with as illegally occupying land.  
Where the illegal approval of expropriation or use of land causes losses to a party, compensation liability shall be borne according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　侵占、挪用被征收土地单位的征地补偿费用和其他有关费用，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 80** Whoever embezzles or misappropriates the land expropriation compensation fees and other relevant fees of the unit whose land is expropriated, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　依法收回国有土地使用权当事人拒不交出土地的，临时使用土地期满拒不归还的，或者不按照批准的用途使用国有土地的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令交还土地，处以罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-59" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第59条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 81** If a party refuses to surrender the land when the right to use State-owned land is withdrawn according to law, or fails to return the land upon expiration of the temporary land use, or uses State-owned land not in accordance with the approved purposes, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order the return of the land and impose a fine.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　擅自将农民集体所有的土地通过出让、转让使用权或者出租等方式用于非农业建设，或者违反本法规定，将集体经营性建设用地通过出让、出租等方式交由单位或者个人使用的，由县级以上人民政府自然资源主管部门责令限期改正，没收违法所得，并处罚款。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例.md#art-60" title="被《中华人民共和国土地管理法实施条例》第60条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 82** Whoever, without authorization, transfers, leases, or otherwise assigns the right to use land collectively owned by farmers for non-agricultural construction, or, in violation of the provisions of this Law, transfers or leases collectively-operated construction land to a unit or individual for use, the competent department of natural resources of the people's government at or above the county level shall order correction within a time limit, confiscate illegal gains, and impose a fine.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　依照本法规定，责令限期拆除在非法占用的土地上新建的建筑物和其他设施的，建设单位或者个人必须立即停止施工，自行拆除；对继续施工的，作出处罚决定的机关有权制止。建设单位或者个人对责令限期拆除的行政处罚决定不服的，可以在接到责令限期拆除决定之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉；期满不起诉又不自行拆除的，由作出处罚决定的机关依法申请人民法院强制执行，费用由违法者承担。

**Article 83** Where, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, an order is issued to demolish, within a time limit, the buildings or other facilities newly constructed on illegally occupied land, the construction unit or individual shall immediately stop construction and demolish them on its/his own; if construction continues, the authority that made the penalty decision shall have the power to stop it. If the construction unit or individual refuses to accept the administrative penalty decision ordering demolition within a time limit, it/he may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the decision ordering demolition within a time limit, initiate a prosecution in the People's Court; if it/he neither initiates a prosecution nor demolishes on its/his own upon expiration of the time limit, the authority that made the penalty decision shall, according to law, apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement, and the expenses shall be borne by the violator.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　自然资源主管部门、农业农村主管部门的工作人员玩忽职守、滥用职权、徇私舞弊，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；尚不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 84** Any staff member of the competent department of natural resources or the competent department of agriculture and rural affairs who neglects his duty, abuses his power, or engages in malpractices for personal gain, if constituting a crime, shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law; if the act does not constitute a crime, he shall be given sanctions according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　外商投资企业使用土地的，适用本法；法律另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 85** Where a foreign-invested enterprise uses land, this Law shall apply; if other laws provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　在根据[本法第十八条](#art-18)的规定编制国土空间规划前，经依法批准的土地利用总体规划和城乡规划继续执行。

**Article 86** Before the preparation of territorial space planning in accordance with Article 18 of this Law, the approved general plans for land use and urban and rural plans shall continue to be implemented.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　本法自1999年1月1日起施行。

**Article 87** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 1999.
