# 最高人民法院关于审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件适用惩罚性赔偿的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕1号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-27  
**生效日期**：2022-01-20

> 《最高人民法院关于审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件适用惩罚性赔偿的解释》已于2021年12月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1858次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月20日起施行。 2022年1月12日 最高人民法院 关于审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件 适用惩罚性赔偿的解释 法释〔2022〕1号 为妥善审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件，全面加强生态环境保护，正确适用惩罚性赔偿，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　人民法院审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件适用惩罚性赔偿，应当严格审慎，注重公平公正，依法保护民事主体合法权益，统筹生态环境保护和经济社会发展。 第二条　因环境污染、生态破坏受到损害的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，依据民法典第一千二百三十二条的规定，请求判令侵权人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，适用本解释。 第三条　被侵权人在生态环境侵权纠纷案件中请求惩罚性赔偿的，应当在起诉时明确赔偿数额以及所依据的事实和理由。 被侵权人在生态环境侵权纠纷案件中没有提出惩罚性赔偿的诉讼请求，诉讼终结后又基于同一污染环境、破坏生态事实另行起诉请求惩罚性赔偿的，人民法院不予受理。 第四条　被侵权人主张侵权人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，应当提供证据证明以下事实： （一）侵权人污染环境、破坏生态的行为违反法律规定； （二）侵权人具有污染环境、破坏生态的故意； （三）侵权人污染环境、破坏生态的行为造成严重后果。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理生态环境侵权纠纷案件适用惩罚性赔偿，应当严格审慎，注重公平公正，依法保护民事主体合法权益，统筹生态环境保护和经济社会发展。

**Article 1** When a People's Court hears a case concerning an environmental infringement dispute and applies punitive damages, it shall be strict and prudent, focus on fairness and justice, protect the lawful rights and interests of civil subjects according to law, and coordinate ecological environmental protection with economic and social development.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因环境污染、生态破坏受到损害的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，依据[民法典第一千二百三十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1232)的规定，请求判令侵权人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，适用本解释。

**Article 2** Where a natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that has suffered damage due to environmental pollution or ecological destruction requests that the infringer be ordered to bear punitive damages liability pursuant to Article 1232 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被侵权人在生态环境侵权纠纷案件中请求惩罚性赔偿的，应当在起诉时明确赔偿数额以及所依据的事实和理由。  
被侵权人在生态环境侵权纠纷案件中没有提出惩罚性赔偿的诉讼请求，诉讼终结后又基于同一污染环境、破坏生态事实另行起诉请求惩罚性赔偿的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 3** Where an infringed person requests punitive damages in an environmental infringement dispute case, it shall specify the amount of compensation and the facts and reasons on which it is based at the time of prosecution.  
Where an infringed person does not raise a claim for punitive damages in an environmental infringement dispute case, and after the conclusion of the proceedings, separately files a prosecution for punitive damages based on the same facts of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　被侵权人主张侵权人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，应当提供证据证明以下事实：  
（一）侵权人污染环境、破坏生态的行为违反法律规定；  
（二）侵权人具有污染环境、破坏生态的故意；  
（三）侵权人污染环境、破坏生态的行为造成严重后果。

**Article 4** Where an infringed person claims that the infringer shall bear punitive damages liability, it shall provide evidence to prove the following facts:  
(1) the infringer's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction violates legal provisions;  
(2) the infringer has the intent to pollute the environment or destroy the ecology;  
(3) the infringer's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction has caused serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院认定侵权人污染环境、破坏生态的行为是否违反法律规定，应当以法律、法规为依据，可以参照规章的规定。

**Article 5** When a People's Court determines whether an infringer's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction violates legal provisions, it shall base its determination on laws and regulations, and may refer to the provisions of rules.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院认定侵权人是否具有污染环境、破坏生态的故意，应当根据侵权人的职业经历、专业背景或者经营范围，因同一或者同类行为受到行政处罚或者刑事追究的情况，以及污染物的种类，污染环境、破坏生态行为的方式等因素综合判断。

**Article 6** When a People's Court determines whether an infringer has the intent to pollute the environment or destroy the ecology, it shall make a comprehensive judgment based on factors such as the infringer's career experience, professional background or business scope; the situation of having been subject to administrative penalties or criminal prosecution for the same or similar acts; and the type of pollutants, the manner of the environmental pollution or ecological destruction, and other factors.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当认定侵权人具有污染环境、破坏生态的故意：  
（一）因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为，已被人民法院认定构成破坏环境资源保护犯罪的；  
（二）建设项目未依法进行环境影响评价，或者提供虚假材料导致环境影响评价文件严重失实，被行政主管部门责令停止建设后拒不执行的；  
（三）未取得排污许可证排放污染物，被行政主管部门责令停止排污后拒不执行，或者超过污染物排放标准或者重点污染物排放总量控制指标排放污染物，经行政主管机关责令限制生产、停产整治或者给予其他行政处罚后仍不改正的；  
（四）生产、使用国家明令禁止生产、使用的农药，被行政主管部门责令改正后拒不改正的；  
（五）无危险废物经营许可证而从事收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物经营活动，或者知道或者应当知道他人无许可证而将危险废物提供或者委托给其从事收集、贮存、利用、处置等活动的；  
（六）将未经处理的废水、废气、废渣直接排放或者倾倒的；  
（七）通过暗管、渗井、渗坑、灌注，篡改、伪造监测数据，或者以不正常运行防治污染设施等逃避监管的方式，违法排放污染物的；  
（八）在相关自然保护区域、禁猎（渔）区、禁猎（渔）期使用禁止使用的猎捕工具、方法猎捕、杀害国家重点保护野生动物、破坏野生动物栖息地的；  
（九）未取得勘查许可证、采矿许可证，或者采取破坏性方法勘查开采矿产资源的；  
（十）其他故意情形。

**Article 7** Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall determine that the infringer has the intent to pollute the environment or destroy the ecology:  
(1) where, due to the same act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, the infringer has been determined by a People's Court to have committed a crime of destroying environmental resources protection;  
(2) where the construction project fails to conduct an environmental impact assessment according to law, or provides false materials resulting in serious inaccuracy of the environmental impact assessment document, and refuses to implement the order of the competent administrative department to stop construction;  
(3) where the infringer discharges pollutants without obtaining a pollutant discharge permit, and refuses to implement the order of the competent administrative department to stop discharging, or discharges pollutants exceeding the pollutant discharge standards or the total emission control indicators for key pollutants, and fails to correct the act after the competent administrative authority orders restricted production, suspension of production for remediation, or imposes other administrative penalties;  
(4) where the infringer produces or uses pesticides that are explicitly prohibited by the state from production and use, and refuses to correct after being ordered by the competent administrative department to do so;  
(5) where the infringer engages in collection, storage, utilization, or disposal of hazardous waste without a hazardous waste operation permit, or knows or should know that another person has no permit and provides or entrusts hazardous waste to that person for collection, storage, utilization, or disposal activities;  
(6) where the infringer directly discharges or dumps untreated wastewater, waste gas, or waste residues;  
(7) where the infringer illegally discharges pollutants through underground pipelines, seepage wells, seepage pits, injection, tampering with or falsifying monitoring data, or by means of abnormal operation of pollution prevention facilities or other methods of evading supervision;  
(8) where the infringer, in relevant natural protection areas, hunting/fishing prohibited areas, or hunting/fishing prohibited periods, uses prohibited hunting tools or methods to hunt or kill national key protected wild animals, or destroys wildlife habitats;  
(9) where the infringer engages in exploration or mining of mineral resources without obtaining an exploration permit or mining permit, or by destructive methods;  
(10) other intentional circumstances.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院认定侵权人污染环境、破坏生态行为是否造成严重后果，应当根据污染环境、破坏生态行为的持续时间、地域范围，造成环境污染、生态破坏的范围和程度，以及造成的社会影响等因素综合判断。  
侵权人污染环境、破坏生态行为造成他人死亡、健康严重损害，重大财产损失，生态环境严重损害或者重大不良社会影响的，人民法院应当认定为造成严重后果。

**Article 8** When a People's Court determines whether the infringer's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction has caused serious consequences, it shall make a comprehensive judgment based on factors such as the duration and geographical scope of the act, the scope and extent of environmental pollution or ecological destruction caused, and the social impact caused.  
Where the infringer's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction causes death, serious harm to health, major property loss, serious damage to the ecological environment, or major adverse social impact, the People's Court shall determine that serious consequences have been caused.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院确定惩罚性赔偿金数额，应当以环境污染、生态破坏造成的人身损害赔偿金、财产损失数额作为计算基数。  
前款所称人身损害赔偿金、财产损失数额，依照[民法典第一千一百七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1179)、第一千一百八十四条规定予以确定。法律另有规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 9** When a People's Court determines the amount of punitive damages, it shall take the amount of personal injury compensation and property loss caused by the environmental pollution or ecological destruction as the calculation base.  
The personal injury compensation and property loss mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall be determined in accordance with Articles 1179 and 1184 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China. Where the law provides otherwise, that law shall prevail.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院确定惩罚性赔偿金数额，应当综合考虑侵权人的恶意程度、侵权后果的严重程度、侵权人因污染环境、破坏生态行为所获得的利益或者侵权人所采取的修复措施及其效果等因素，但一般不超过人身损害赔偿金、财产损失数额的二倍。  
因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为已经被行政机关给予罚款或者被人民法院判处罚金，侵权人主张免除惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持，但在确定惩罚性赔偿金数额时可以综合考虑。

**Article 10** When a People's Court determines the amount of punitive damages, it shall comprehensively consider factors such as the degree of malice of the infringer, the severity of the infringement consequences, the benefits obtained by the infringer from the environmental pollution or ecological destruction, or the remedial measures taken by the infringer and their effects, provided that it shall generally not exceed two times the amount of personal injury compensation and property loss.  
Where the infringer has been given a fine by an administrative authority or sentenced to a criminal fine by a People's Court for the same act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, and the infringer claims exemption from punitive damages liability, the People's Court shall not support the claim, but may comprehensively consider such circumstances when determining the amount of punitive damages.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　侵权人因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为，应当承担包括惩罚性赔偿在内的民事责任、行政责任和刑事责任，其财产不足以支付的，应当优先用于承担民事责任。  
侵权人因同一污染环境、破坏生态行为，应当承担包括惩罚性赔偿在内的民事责任，其财产不足以支付的，应当优先用于承担惩罚性赔偿以外的其他责任。

**Article 11** Where an infringer shall bear civil liability, administrative liability, and criminal liability including punitive damages for the same act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, and its property is insufficient to pay, it shall first be used to bear civil liability.  
Where an infringer shall bear civil liability including punitive damages for the same act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, and its property is insufficient to pay, it shall first be used to bear liabilities other than punitive damages.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织作为被侵权人代表，请求判令侵权人承担惩罚性赔偿责任的，人民法院可以参照前述规定予以处理。但惩罚性赔偿金数额的确定，应当以生态环境受到损害至修复完成期间服务功能丧失导致的损失、生态环境功能永久性损害造成的损失数额作为计算基数。

**Article 12** Where an authority prescribed by the state or an organization prescribed by law, as a representative of the infringed person, requests that the infringer be ordered to bear punitive damages liability, the People's Court may handle the matter by reference to the preceding provisions. However, the determination of the amount of punitive damages shall take the loss caused by the loss of service functions during the period from when the ecological environment is damaged until the completion of restoration, and the loss caused by permanent damage to ecological environment functions, as the calculation base.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　侵权行为实施地、损害结果发生地在中华人民共和国管辖海域内的海洋生态环境侵权纠纷案件惩罚性赔偿问题，另行规定。

**Article 13** Issues concerning punitive damages in cases of marine ecological environment infringement disputes where the place of commission of the tortious act or the place where the damage results occur is within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China shall be separately prescribed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定自2022年1月20日起施行。

**Article 14** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 20, 2022.
