# 最高人民法院关于行政机关负责人出庭应诉若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕3号  
**公布日期**：2020-07-01  
**生效日期**：2020-07-01

> 《最高人民法院关于行政机关负责人出庭应诉若干问题的规定》已于 2020 年 3 月 23 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第 1797次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年7月1日起施行。 2020年6月22日 最高人民法院 关于行政机关负责人出庭应诉若干问题的规定 法释〔2020〕3号 为进一步规范行政机关负责人出庭应诉活动，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律规定，结合人民法院行政审判工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　行政诉讼法第三条第三款规定的被诉行政机关负责人应当出庭应诉，是指被诉行政机关负责人依法应当在第一审、第二审、再审等诉讼程序中出庭参加诉讼，行使诉讼权利，履行诉讼义务。 法律、法规、规章授权独立行使行政职权的行政机关内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织的负责人出庭应诉，适用本规定。 应当追加为被告而原告不同意追加，人民法院通知以第三人身份参加诉讼的行政机关，其负责人出庭应诉活动参照前款规定。 第二条　行政诉讼法第三条第三款规定的被诉行政机关负责人，包括行政机关的正职、副职负责人、参与分管被诉行政行为实施工作的副职级别的负责人以及其他参与分管的负责人。 被诉行政机关委托的组织或者下级行政机关的负责人，不能作为被诉行政机关负责人出庭。 第三条　有共同被告的行政案件，可以由共同被告协商确定行政机关负责人出庭应诉；也可以由人民法院确定。 第四条　对于涉及食品药品安全、生态环境和资源保护、公共卫生安全等重大公共利益，社会高度关注或者可能引发群体性事件等的案件，人民法院应当通知行政机关负责人出庭应诉。 有下列情形之一，需要行政机关负责人出庭的，人民法院可以通知行政机关负责人出庭应诉： （一）被诉行政行为涉及公民、法人或者其他组织重大人身、财产权益的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的被诉行政机关负责人应当出庭应诉，是指被诉行政机关负责人依法应当在第一审、第二审、再审等诉讼程序中出庭参加诉讼，行使诉讼权利，履行诉讼义务。  
法律、法规、规章授权独立行使行政职权的行政机关内设机构、派出机构或者其他组织的负责人出庭应诉，适用本规定。  
应当追加为被告而原告不同意追加，人民法院通知以第三人身份参加诉讼的行政机关，其负责人出庭应诉活动参照前款规定。

**Article 1** The responsible person of the sued administrative agency as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law who shall appear in court to respond to the lawsuit means that the responsible person of the sued administrative agency shall, according to law, appear in court to participate in the proceedings in the procedures of first instance, second instance, retrial, etc., exercise procedural rights, and perform procedural obligations.  
Where the responsible person of an internal institution, dispatched agency, or other organization authorized by laws, regulations, or rules to independently exercise administrative functions appears in court to respond to the lawsuit, these Provisions shall apply.  
Where a person shall be added as a defendant but the plaintiff does not consent to the addition, and the People's Court notifies the administrative agency to participate in the proceedings as a third party, the activities of its responsible person appearing in court to respond to the lawsuit shall be governed by reference to the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的被诉行政机关负责人，包括行政机关的正职、副职负责人、参与分管被诉行政行为实施工作的副职级别的负责人以及其他参与分管的负责人。  
被诉行政机关委托的组织或者下级行政机关的负责人，不能作为被诉行政机关负责人出庭。

**Article 2** The responsible person of the sued administrative agency as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law includes the principal responsible person, deputy responsible persons, deputy-level responsible persons who participate in the management of the implementation work of the sued administrative act, and other responsible persons who participate in the management.  
The responsible person of an organization entrusted by the sued administrative agency or the responsible person of a subordinate administrative agency shall not appear in court as the responsible person of the sued administrative agency.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　有共同被告的行政案件，可以由共同被告协商确定行政机关负责人出庭应诉；也可以由人民法院确定。

**Article 3** In an administrative case with co-defendants, the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit may be determined through consultation among the co-defendants; or it may be determined by the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对于涉及食品药品安全、生态环境和资源保护、公共卫生安全等重大公共利益，社会高度关注或者可能引发群体性事件等的案件，人民法院应当通知行政机关负责人出庭应诉。  
有下列情形之一，需要行政机关负责人出庭的，人民法院可以通知行政机关负责人出庭应诉：  
（一）被诉行政行为涉及公民、法人或者其他组织重大人身、财产权益的；  
（二）行政公益诉讼；  
（三）被诉行政机关的上级机关规范性文件要求行政机关负责人出庭应诉的；  
（四）人民法院认为需要通知行政机关负责人出庭应诉的其他情形。

**Article 4** For cases involving major public interests such as food and drug safety, ecological environment and resource protection, public health safety, etc., which are of high social concern or may cause mass incidents, the People's Court shall notify the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit.  
In any of the following circumstances where the responsible person of the administrative agency is required to appear in court, the People's Court may notify the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit:  
(1) the sued administrative act involves major personal or property rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations;  
(2) an administrative public interest litigation;  
(3) the regulatory documents of the higher-level agency of the sued administrative agency require the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit;  
(4) other circumstances where the People's Court considers it necessary to notify the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院在向行政机关送达的权利义务告知书中，应当一并告知行政机关负责人出庭应诉的法定义务及相关法律后果等事项。  
人民法院通知行政机关负责人出庭的，应当在开庭三日前送达出庭通知书，并告知行政机关负责人不出庭可能承担的不利法律后果。  
行政机关在庭审前申请更换出庭应诉负责人且不影响正常开庭的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 5** In the notice of rights and obligations served on the administrative agency, the People's Court shall simultaneously inform the administrative agency of the legal obligation of its responsible person to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit and the relevant legal consequences, among other matters.  
Where the People's Court notifies the responsible person of the administrative agency to appear in court, it shall serve the appearance notice at least three days before the court session and inform the responsible person of the adverse legal consequences that may be borne for failure to appear.  
Where the administrative agency applies to change the responsible person appearing in court to respond to the lawsuit before the court session and it does not affect the normal holding of the court session, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，应当于开庭前向人民法院提交出庭应诉负责人的身份证明。身份证明应当载明该负责人的姓名、职务等基本信息，并加盖行政机关印章。  
人民法院应当对出庭应诉负责人的身份证明进行审查，经审查认为不符合条件，可以补正的，应当告知行政机关予以补正；不能补正或者补正可能影响正常开庭的，视为行政机关负责人未出庭应诉。

**Article 6** Where the responsible person of the administrative agency appears in court to respond to the lawsuit, he or she shall submit to the People's Court the identity certificate of the responsible person appearing in court to respond to the lawsuit before the court session. The identity certificate shall specify the basic information such as the name and position of the responsible person, and shall be affixed with the seal of the administrative agency.  
The People's Court shall examine the identity certificate of the responsible person appearing in court to respond to the lawsuit. If upon examination it is found that the conditions are not met but corrections can be made, the People's Court shall inform the administrative agency to make corrections; if corrections cannot be made or corrections may affect the normal holding of the court session, the responsible person of the administrative agency shall be deemed not to have appeared in court to respond to the lawsuit.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对于同一审级需要多次开庭的同一案件，行政机关负责人到庭参加一次庭审的，一般可以认定其已经履行出庭应诉义务，但人民法院通知行政机关负责人再次出庭的除外。  
行政机关负责人在一个审理程序中出庭应诉，不免除其在其他审理程序出庭应诉的义务。

**Article 7** For the same case that requires multiple court sessions in the same instance, if the responsible person of the administrative agency appears in court and participates in one court session, it may generally be determined that he or she has fulfilled the obligation to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit, except where the People's Court notifies the responsible person to appear again.  
The appearance of the responsible person of the administrative agency in court to respond to the lawsuit in one proceeding does not discharge his or her obligation to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit in other proceedings.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有下列情形之一的，属于[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的行政机关负责人不能出庭的情形：  
（一）不可抗力；  
（二）意外事件；  
（三）需要履行他人不能代替的公务；  
（四）无法出庭的其他正当事由。

**Article 8** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute a circumstance under paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law where the responsible person of the administrative agency cannot appear in court:  
(1) force majeure;  
(2) an accident;  
(3) the need to perform official duties that cannot be replaced by others;  
(4) other justifiable reasons for being unable to appear in court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政机关负责人有正当理由不能出庭的，应当提交相关证明材料，并加盖行政机关印章或者由该机关主要负责人签字认可。  
人民法院应当对行政机关负责人不能出庭的理由以及证明材料进行审查。  
行政机关负责人有正当理由不能出庭，行政机关申请延期开庭审理的，人民法院可以准许；人民法院也可以依职权决定延期开庭审理。

**Article 9** Where the responsible person of the administrative agency cannot appear in court for a justifiable reason, the administrative agency shall submit relevant supporting materials, and affix the seal of the administrative agency or have the principal responsible person of the agency sign for approval.  
The People's Court shall examine the reason and supporting materials for the responsible person's inability to appear in court.  
Where the responsible person of the administrative agency cannot appear in court for a justifiable reason and the administrative agency applies for an adjournment of the court session, the People's Court may permit it; the People's Court may also decide ex officio to adjourn the court session.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[行政诉讼法第三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-3)第三款规定的相应的工作人员，是指被诉行政机关中具体行使行政职权的工作人员。  
行政机关委托行使行政职权的组织或者下级行政机关的工作人员，可以视为行政机关相应的工作人员。  
人民法院应当参照[本规定第六条](#art-6)第二款的规定，对行政机关相应的工作人员的身份证明进行审查。

**Article 10** The 'relevant staff members' as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 3 of the Administrative Procedure Law shall refer to the staff members who specifically exercise administrative functions within the sued administrative agency.  
Staff members of an organization entrusted by the administrative agency to exercise administrative functions or staff members of a subordinate administrative agency may be deemed as relevant staff members of the administrative agency.  
The People's Court shall, by reference to paragraph 2 of Article 6 of these Provisions, examine the identity certificates of the relevant staff members of the administrative agency.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　诉讼参与人参加诉讼活动，应当依法行使诉讼权利，履行诉讼义务，遵守法庭规则，自觉维护诉讼秩序。  
行政机关负责人或者行政机关委托的相应工作人员在庭审过程中应当就案件情况进行陈述、答辩、提交证据、辩论、发表最后意见，对所依据的规范性文件进行解释说明。  
行政机关负责人出庭应诉的，应当就实质性解决行政争议发表意见。  
诉讼参与人和其他人以侮辱、谩骂、威胁等方式扰乱法庭秩序的，人民法院应当制止，并根据[行政诉讼法第五十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-59)规定进行处理。

**Article 11** Participants in the proceedings participating in litigation activities shall, according to law, exercise procedural rights, perform procedural obligations, abide by court rules, and consciously maintain litigation order.  
During the court session, the responsible person of the administrative agency or the relevant staff member entrusted by the administrative agency shall make statements, defend, submit evidence, debate, deliver closing arguments, and explain and interpret the normative documents relied upon.  
Where the responsible person of the administrative agency appears in court to respond to the lawsuit, he or she shall present opinions on substantively resolving the administrative dispute.  
Where participants in the proceedings or other persons disrupt court order by means of insults, slander, threats, etc., the People's Court shall stop such conduct and deal with it pursuant to Article 59 of the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当向监察机关、被诉行政机关的上一级行政机关提出司法建议：  
（一）行政机关负责人未出庭应诉，且未说明理由或者理由不成立的；  
（二）行政机关有正当理由申请延期开庭审理，人民法院准许后再次开庭审理时行政机关负责人仍未能出庭应诉，且无正当理由的；  
（三）行政机关负责人和行政机关相应的工作人员均不出庭应诉的；  
（四）行政机关负责人未经法庭许可中途退庭的；  
（五）人民法院在庭审中要求行政机关负责人就有关问题进行解释或者说明，行政机关负责人拒绝解释或者说明，导致庭审无法进行的。  
有前款情形之一的，人民法院应当记录在案并在裁判文书中载明。

**Article 12** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall issue a judicial recommendation to the supervisory agency or to the higher-level administrative agency of the sued administrative agency:  
(1) the responsible person of the administrative agency fails to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit and does not provide a reason or the reason is not valid;  
(2) the administrative agency has a justifiable reason and applies for an adjournment, but after the People's Court permits it, the responsible person still fails to appear in court to respond to the lawsuit at the rescheduled court session without a justifiable reason;  
(3) neither the responsible person of the administrative agency nor the relevant staff members of the administrative agency appear in court to respond to the lawsuit;  
(4) the responsible person of the administrative agency leaves the court without the permission of the court;  
(5) during the court session, the People's Court requests the responsible person of the administrative agency to explain or clarify relevant issues, but the responsible person refuses to explain or clarify, resulting in the inability to proceed with the court session.  
Where any of the circumstances in the preceding paragraph exists, the People's Court shall record it in the case files and state it in the judgment document.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人对行政机关具有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款情形提出异议的，人民法院可以在庭审笔录中载明，不影响案件的正常审理。  
原告以行政机关具有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款情形为由拒不到庭、未经法庭许可中途退庭的，人民法院可以按照撤诉处理。  
原告以行政机关具有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款情形为由在庭审中明确拒绝陈述或者以其他方式拒绝陈述，导致庭审无法进行，经法庭释明法律后果后仍不陈述意见的，人民法院可以视为放弃陈述权利，由其承担相应的法律后果。

**Article 13** Where a party raises an objection on the ground that the administrative agency has any of the circumstances set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, the People's Court may record it in the court transcripts, without affecting the normal trial of the case.  
Where the plaintiff, on the ground that the administrative agency has any of the circumstances set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, fails to appear in court or leaves the court without permission, the People's Court may treat it as withdrawal of the lawsuit.  
Where the plaintiff, on the ground that the administrative agency has any of the circumstances set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, explicitly refuses to make statements or refuses to make statements by other means during the court session, causing the court session to be unable to proceed, and after the court explains the legal consequences still does not make statements, the People's Court may deem that the right to make statements has been waived, and the plaintiff shall bear the corresponding legal consequences.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院可以通过适当形式将行政机关负责人出庭应诉情况向社会公开。  
人民法院可以定期将辖区内行政机关负责人出庭应诉情况进行统计、分析、评价，向同级人民代表大会常务委员会报告,向同级人民政府进行通报。

**Article 14** The People's Court may, by appropriate means, disclose to the public the situation of responsible persons of administrative agencies appearing in court to respond to lawsuits.  
The People's Court may periodically conduct statistics, analysis, and evaluation of the situation of responsible persons of administrative agencies in its jurisdiction appearing in court to respond to lawsuits, report to the standing committee of the People's Congress at the same level, and notify the People's Government at the same level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定自2020年7月1日起施行。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 2020.
