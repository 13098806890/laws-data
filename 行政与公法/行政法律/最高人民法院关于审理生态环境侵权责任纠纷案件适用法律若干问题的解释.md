# 最高人民法院关于审理生态环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕5号  
**公布日期**：2023-06-05  
**生效日期**：2023-09-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理生态环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2023年6月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1890次会议通过，现予公布，自2023年9月1日起施行。 2023年8月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理生态环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用 法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2023〕5号 为正确审理生态环境侵权责任纠纷案件，依法保护当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　侵权人因实施下列污染环境、破坏生态行为造成他人人身、财产损害，被侵权人请求侵权人承担生态环境侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持： （一）排放废气、废水、废渣、医疗废物、粉尘、恶臭气体、放射性物质等污染环境的； （二）排放噪声、振动、光辐射、电磁辐射等污染环境的； （三）不合理开发利用自然资源的； （四）违反国家规定，未经批准，擅自引进、释放、丢弃外来物种的； （五）其他污染环境、破坏生态的行为。 第二条　因下列污染环境、破坏生态引发的民事纠纷，不作为生态环境侵权案件处理： （一）未经由大气、水、土壤等生态环境介质，直接造成损害的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　侵权人因实施下列污染环境、破坏生态行为造成他人人身、财产损害，被侵权人请求侵权人承担生态环境侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）排放废气、废水、废渣、医疗废物、粉尘、恶臭气体、放射性物质等污染环境的；  
（二）排放噪声、振动、光辐射、电磁辐射等污染环境的；  
（三）不合理开发利用自然资源的；  
（四）违反国家规定，未经批准，擅自引进、释放、丢弃外来物种的；  
（五）其他污染环境、破坏生态的行为。

**Article 1** Where a tortfeasor causes personal injury or property damage to another person by committing any of the following acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the injured person requests the tortfeasor to assume ecological and environmental tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) discharging waste gas, wastewater, waste residue, medical waste, dust, malodorous gas, radioactive substances, etc., which pollute the environment;  
(2) discharging noise, vibration, light radiation, electromagnetic radiation, etc., which pollute the environment;  
(3) unreasonably exploiting and utilizing natural resources;  
(4) violating state regulations by, without approval, introducing, releasing, or discarding alien species;  
(5) other acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因下列污染环境、破坏生态引发的民事纠纷，不作为生态环境侵权案件处理：  
（一）未经由大气、水、土壤等生态环境介质，直接造成损害的；  
（二）在室内、车内等封闭空间内造成损害的；  
（三）不动产权利人在日常生活中造成相邻不动产权利人损害的；  
（四）劳动者在职业活动中受到损害的。  
前款规定的情形，依照相关法律规定确定民事责任。

**Article 2** Civil disputes arising from the following acts of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology shall not be handled as ecological and environmental tort cases:  
(1) causing damage directly without passing through ecological and environmental media such as the atmosphere, water, or soil;  
(2) causing damage within enclosed spaces such as indoors or inside a vehicle;  
(3) a real property right holder causing damage to an adjacent real property right holder in daily life;  
(4) a worker suffering damage in the course of occupational activities.  
For the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, civil liability shall be determined in accordance with relevant laws.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　不动产权利人因经营活动污染环境、破坏生态造成相邻不动产权利人损害，被侵权人请求其承担生态环境侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 3** Where a real property right holder causes damage to an adjacent real property right holder by polluting the environment or damaging the ecology through business operations, and the injured person requests the right holder to assume ecological and environmental tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，行为人不论有无过错，都应当承担侵权责任。  
行为人以外的其他责任人对损害发生有过错的，应当承担侵权责任。

**Article 4** Where an act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology causes damage to another person, the actor shall bear tort liability regardless of whether the actor is at fault.  
Where a person other than the actor is at fault for the occurrence of the damage, such person shall bear tort liability.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　两个以上侵权人分别污染环境、破坏生态造成同一损害，每一个侵权人的行为都足以造成全部损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百七十一条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1171)的规定请求侵权人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 5** Where two or more tortfeasors respectively pollute the environment or damage the ecology causing the same damage, and the act of each tortfeasor is sufficient to cause the entire damage, and the injured person requests the tortfeasors to bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 1,171 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　两个以上侵权人分别污染环境、破坏生态，每一个侵权人的行为都不足以造成全部损害，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百七十二条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1172)的规定请求侵权人承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
侵权人主张其污染环境、破坏生态行为不足以造成全部损害的，应当承担相应举证责任。

**Article 6** Where two or more tortfeasors respectively pollute the environment or damage the ecology, and the act of each tortfeasor is insufficient to cause the entire damage, and the injured person requests the tortfeasors to bear liability in accordance with Article 1,172 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a tortfeasor claims that its act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology is insufficient to cause the entire damage, it shall bear the corresponding burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　两个以上侵权人分别污染环境、破坏生态，部分侵权人的行为足以造成全部损害，部分侵权人的行为只造成部分损害，被侵权人请求足以造成全部损害的侵权人对全部损害承担责任，并与其他侵权人就共同造成的损害部分承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
被侵权人依照前款规定请求足以造成全部损害的侵权人与其他侵权人承担责任的，受偿范围应以侵权行为造成的全部损害为限。

**Article 7** Where two or more tortfeasors respectively pollute the environment or damage the ecology, the act of some tortfeasors is sufficient to cause the entire damage, and the act of some tortfeasors only causes part of the damage, and the injured person requests the tortfeasor whose act is sufficient to cause the entire damage to bear liability for the entire damage and to bear joint and several liability with other tortfeasors for the part of the damage jointly caused, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the injured person, in accordance with the preceding paragraph, requests the tortfeasor whose act is sufficient to cause the entire damage and other tortfeasors to bear liability, the scope of compensation shall be limited to the entire damage caused by the tortious acts.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　两个以上侵权人分别污染环境、破坏生态，部分侵权人能够证明其他侵权人的侵权行为已先行造成全部或者部分损害，并请求在相应范围内不承担责任或者减轻责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where two or more tortfeasors respectively pollute the environment or damage the ecology, and some tortfeasors can prove that the tortious acts of other tortfeasors have already caused all or part of the damage in advance, and request not to bear liability or to have their liability mitigated within the corresponding scope, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　两个以上侵权人分别排放的物质相互作用产生污染物造成他人损害，被侵权人请求侵权人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where substances respectively discharged by two or more tortfeasors interact to produce pollutants causing damage to another person, and the injured person requests the tortfeasors to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　为侵权人污染环境、破坏生态提供场地或者储存、运输等帮助，被侵权人根据[民法典第一千一百六十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1169)的规定请求行为人与侵权人承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 10** Where a person provides premises or assistance such as storage or transport to a tortfeasor for polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the injured person requests the actor and the tortfeasor to bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 1,169 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　过失为侵权人污染环境、破坏生态提供场地或者储存、运输等便利条件，被侵权人请求行为人承担与过错相适应责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
前款规定的行为人存在重大过失的，依照[本解释第十条](#art-10)的规定处理。

**Article 11** Where a person negligently provides premises or convenient conditions such as storage or transport to a tortfeasor for polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the injured person requests the actor to bear liability commensurate with the fault, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the actor specified in the preceding paragraph is grossly negligent, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 10 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　排污单位将所属的环保设施委托第三方治理机构运营，第三方治理机构在合同履行过程中污染环境造成他人损害，被侵权人请求排污单位承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
排污单位依照前款规定承担责任后向有过错的第三方治理机构追偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where a pollutant-discharging unit entrusts the operation of its environmental protection facilities to a third-party treatment institution, and the third-party treatment institution pollutes the environment and causes damage to another person during the performance of the contract, and the injured person requests the pollutant-discharging unit to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the pollutant-discharging unit, after bearing liability in accordance with the preceding paragraph, seeks recourse against the third-party treatment institution that is at fault, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　排污单位将污染物交由第三方治理机构集中处置，第三方治理机构在合同履行过程中污染环境造成他人损害，被侵权人请求第三方治理机构承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
排污单位在选任、指示第三方治理机构中有过错，被侵权人请求排污单位承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where a pollutant-discharging unit delivers pollutants to a third-party treatment institution for centralized disposal, and the third-party treatment institution pollutes the environment and causes damage to another person during the performance of the contract, and the injured person requests the third-party treatment institution to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the pollutant-discharging unit is at fault in selecting or instructing the third-party treatment institution, and the injured person requests the pollutant-discharging unit to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　存在下列情形之一的，排污单位与第三方治理机构应当根据[民法典第一千一百六十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1168)的规定承担连带责任：  
（一）第三方治理机构按照排污单位的指示，违反污染防治相关规定排放污染物的；  
（二）排污单位将明显存在缺陷的环保设施交由第三方治理机构运营，第三方治理机构利用该设施违反污染防治相关规定排放污染物的；  
（三）排污单位以明显不合理的价格将污染物交由第三方治理机构处置，第三方治理机构违反污染防治相关规定排放污染物的；  
（四）其他应当承担连带责任的情形。

**Article 14** Under any of the following circumstances, the pollutant-discharging unit and the third-party treatment institution shall bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 1,168 of the Civil Code:  
(1) the third-party treatment institution, following the instructions of the pollutant-discharging unit, discharges pollutants in violation of relevant pollution prevention and control regulations;  
(2) the pollutant-discharging unit entrusts the operation of environmental protection facilities that are obviously defective to the third-party treatment institution, and the third-party treatment institution uses such facilities to discharge pollutants in violation of relevant pollution prevention and control regulations;  
(3) the pollutant-discharging unit delivers pollutants to the third-party treatment institution for disposal at a manifestly unreasonable price, and the third-party treatment institution discharges pollutants in violation of relevant pollution prevention and control regulations;  
(4) other circumstances where joint and several liability shall be borne.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公司污染环境、破坏生态，被侵权人请求股东承担责任，符合[公司法第二十条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-20)规定情形的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where a company pollutes the environment or damages the ecology, and the injured person requests the shareholders to bear liability, if the circumstances specified in Article 20 of the Company Law of the People's Republic of China are met, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　侵权人污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，被侵权人请求未尽到安全保障义务的经营场所、公共场所的经营者、管理者或者群众性活动的组织者承担相应补充责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a tortfeasor pollutes the environment or damages the ecology causing damage to another person, and the injured person requests the operator, manager of a business premises or public place, or the organizer of a mass activity who has failed to fulfill the safety guarantee obligation to bear corresponding supplementary liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　依照法律规定应当履行生态环境风险管控和修复义务的民事主体，未履行法定义务造成他人损害，被侵权人请求其承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 17** Where a civil subject that is obligated by law to perform ecological and environmental risk control and remediation duties fails to perform such statutory duties and causes damage to another person, and the injured person requests such subject to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　因第三人的过错污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，被侵权人请求侵权人或者第三人承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
侵权人以损害是由第三人过错造成的为由，主张不承担责任或者减轻责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 18** Where damage is caused to another person due to the fault of a third party in polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the injured person requests the tortfeasor or the third party to bear liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the tortfeasor claims not to bear liability or to have its liability mitigated on the ground that the damage was caused by the fault of a third party, the People's Court shall not support such claim.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　因第三人的过错污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，被侵权人同时起诉侵权人和第三人承担责任，侵权人对损害的发生没有过错的，人民法院应当判令侵权人、第三人就全部损害承担责任。侵权人承担责任后有权向第三人追偿。  
侵权人对损害的发生有过错的，人民法院应当判令侵权人就全部损害承担责任，第三人承担与其过错相适应的责任。侵权人承担责任后有权就第三人应当承担的责任份额向其追偿。

**Article 19** Where damage is caused to another person due to the fault of a third party in polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the injured person simultaneously sues the tortfeasor and the third party to bear liability, if the tortfeasor is not at fault for the occurrence of the damage, the People's Court shall order the tortfeasor and the third party to bear liability for the entire damage. The tortfeasor, after bearing liability, shall have the right to seek recourse against the third party.  
If the tortfeasor is at fault for the occurrence of the damage, the People's Court shall order the tortfeasor to bear liability for the entire damage, and the third party shall bear liability commensurate with its fault. The tortfeasor, after bearing liability, shall have the right to seek recourse against the third party for the portion of liability that the third party should bear.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　被侵权人起诉第三人承担责任的，人民法院应当向被侵权人释明是否同时起诉侵权人。被侵权人不起诉侵权人的，人民法院应当根据[民事诉讼法第五十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-59)的规定通知侵权人参加诉讼。  
被侵权人仅请求第三人承担责任，侵权人对损害的发生也有过错的，人民法院应当判令第三人承担与其过错相适应的责任。

**Article 20** Where the injured person sues a third party to bear liability, the People's Court shall explain to the injured person whether the tortfeasor is also sued simultaneously. If the injured person does not sue the tortfeasor, the People's Court shall notify the tortfeasor to participate in the proceedings in accordance with Article 59 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the injured person only requests the third party to bear liability, and the tortfeasor is also at fault for the occurrence of the damage, the People's Court shall order the third party to bear liability commensurate with its fault.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　环境影响评价机构、环境监测机构以及从事环境监测设备和防治污染设施维护、运营的机构存在下列情形之一，被侵权人请求其与造成环境污染、生态破坏的其他责任人根据[环境保护法第六十五条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国环境保护法.md#art-65)的规定承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）故意出具失实评价文件的；  
（二）隐瞒委托人超过污染物排放标准或者超过重点污染物排放总量控制指标的事实的；  
（三）故意不运行或者不正常运行环境监测设备或者防治污染设施的；  
（四）其他根据法律规定应当承担连带责任的情形。

**Article 21** Where an environmental impact assessment institution, an environmental monitoring institution, or an institution engaged in the maintenance and operation of environmental monitoring equipment and pollution prevention facilities falls under any of the following circumstances, and the injured person requests it and other persons liable for causing environmental pollution and ecological damage to bear joint and several liability in accordance with Article 65 of the Environmental Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) intentionally issuing false evaluation documents;  
(2) concealing the fact that the client exceeds the pollutant discharge standards or exceeds the total discharge control indicators for key pollutants;  
(3) intentionally failing to operate or not properly operating environmental monitoring equipment or pollution prevention facilities;  
(4) other circumstances where joint and several liability shall be borne in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　被侵权人请求侵权人赔偿因污染环境、破坏生态造成的人身、财产损害，以及为防止损害发生和扩大而采取必要措施所支出的合理费用的，人民法院应予支持。  
被侵权人同时请求侵权人根据[民法典第一千二百三十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1235)的规定承担生态环境损害赔偿责任的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 22** Where the injured person requests the tortfeasor to compensate for personal injury or property damage caused by pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology, as well as reasonable expenses incurred in taking necessary measures to prevent the occurrence and expansion of the damage, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the injured person simultaneously requests the tortfeasor to assume ecological and environmental damage compensation liability in accordance with Article 1,235 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　因污染环境、破坏生态影响他人取水、捕捞、狩猎、采集等日常生活并造成经济损失，同时符合下列情形，请求人主张行为人承担责任的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）请求人的活动位于或者接近生态环境受损区域；  
（二）请求人的活动依赖受损害生态环境；  
（三）请求人的活动不具有可替代性或者替代成本过高；  
（四）请求人的活动具有稳定性和公开性。  
根据国家规定须经相关行政主管部门许可的活动，请求人在污染环境、破坏生态发生时未取得许可的，人民法院对其请求不予支持。

**Article 23** Where pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology affects another person's daily activities such as water collection, fishing, hunting, or gathering, and causes economic loss, and all of the following circumstances are met, and the claimant asserts that the actor shall bear liability, the People's Court shall support such claim:  
(1) the claimant's activities are located in or near the area where the ecology and environment are damaged;  
(2) the claimant's activities are dependent on the damaged ecology and environment;  
(3) the claimant's activities are not substitutable or the cost of substitution is too high;  
(4) the claimant's activities are stable and public.  
For activities that require permission from the relevant administrative authority in accordance with state regulations, if the claimant has not obtained such permission at the time the pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology occurs, the People's Court shall not support the claim.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　两个以上侵权人就污染环境、破坏生态造成的损害承担连带责任，实际承担责任超过自己责任份额的侵权人根据[民法典第一百七十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-178)的规定向其他侵权人追偿的，人民法院应予支持。侵权人就惩罚性赔偿责任向其他侵权人追偿的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 24** Where two or more tortfeasors bear joint and several liability for damage caused by pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology, and a tortfeasor who actually bears liability exceeding its own share seeks recourse against other tortfeasors in accordance with Article 178 of the Civil Code, the People's Court shall support such request. Where a tortfeasor seeks recourse against other tortfeasors for punitive damages liability, the People's Court shall not support such request.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　两个以上侵权人污染环境、破坏生态造成他人损害，人民法院应当根据行为有无许可，污染物的种类、浓度、排放量、危害性，破坏生态的方式、范围、程度，以及行为对损害后果所起的作用等因素确定各侵权人的责任份额。  
两个以上侵权人污染环境、破坏生态承担连带责任，实际承担责任的侵权人向其他侵权人追偿的，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 25** Where two or more tortfeasors pollute the environment or damage the ecology causing damage to another person, the People's Court shall determine the share of liability of each tortfeasor based on factors such as whether the act is licensed, the type, concentration, discharge volume, and harmfulness of the pollutants, the manner, scope, and degree of ecological damage, and the role of the act in causing the harmful consequences.  
Where two or more tortfeasors bear joint and several liability for polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the tortfeasor who actually bears liability seeks recourse against other tortfeasors, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　被侵权人对同一污染环境、破坏生态行为造成损害的发生或者扩大有重大过失，侵权人请求减轻责任的，人民法院可以予以支持。

**Article 26** Where the injured person is grossly negligent for the occurrence or expansion of damage caused by the same act of polluting the environment or damaging the ecology, and the tortfeasor requests mitigation of liability, the People's Court may support such request.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　被侵权人请求侵权人承担生态环境侵权责任的诉讼时效期间，以被侵权人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害以及侵权人、其他责任人之日起计算。  
被侵权人知道或者应当知道权利受到损害以及侵权人、其他责任人之日，侵权行为仍持续的，诉讼时效期间自行为结束之日起计算。

**Article 27** The limitation of action for the injured person to request the tortfeasor to assume ecological and environmental tort liability shall be calculated from the date when the injured person knows or should have known that its rights are damaged and the identity of the tortfeasor or other persons liable.  
Where, on the date when the injured person knows or should have known that its rights are damaged and the identity of the tortfeasor or other persons liable, the tortious act is still continuing, the limitation of action shall be calculated from the date when the act ends.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　被侵权人以向负有环境资源监管职能的行政机关请求处理因污染环境、破坏生态造成的损害为由，主张诉讼时效中断的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 28** Where the injured person claims the interruption of the limitation of action on the ground that it has requested the administrative authority with environmental and resource supervision functions to handle the damage caused by pollution of the environment or damage to the ecology, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　本解释自2023年9月1日起施行。  
本解释公布施行后，《最高人民法院关于审理环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2015〕12号）同时废止。

**Article 29** This Interpretation shall come into force on September 1, 2023.  
After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Tort Liability Dispute Cases (Fa Shi [2015] No. 12) shall be abolished at the same time.
