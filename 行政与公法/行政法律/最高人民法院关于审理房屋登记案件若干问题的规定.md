# 最高人民法院关于审理房屋登记案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕15号  
**公布日期**：2010-08-02  
**生效日期**：2010-11-18

> 最高人民法院《关于审理房屋登记案件若干问题的规定》已于2010年8月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1491次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年11月18日起施行。 2010年11月5日 最高人民法院 关于审理房屋登记案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2010〕15号 为正确审理房屋登记案件，根据《中华人民共和国物权法》、《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》、《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合行政审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织对房屋登记机构的房屋登记行为以及与查询、复制登记资料等事项相关的行政行为或者相应的不作为不服，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条  房屋登记机构根据人民法院、仲裁委员会的法律文书或者有权机关的协助执行通知书以及人民政府的征收决定办理的房屋登记行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但公民、法人或者其他组织认为登记与有关文书内容不一致的除外。 房屋登记机构作出未改变登记内容的换发、补发权属证书、登记证明或者更新登记簿的行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。 房屋登记机构在行政诉讼法施行前作出的房屋登记行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。 第三条  公民、法人或者其他组织对房屋登记行为不服提起行政诉讼的，不受下列情形的影响： （一）房屋灭失； （二）房屋登记行为已被登记机构改变； （三）生效法律文书将房屋权属证书、房屋登记簿或者房屋登记证明作为定案证据采用。 第四条  房屋登记机构为债务人办理房屋转移登记，债权人不服提起诉讼，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当依法受理：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织对房屋登记机构的房屋登记行为以及与查询、复制登记资料等事项相关的行政行为或者相应的不作为不服，提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept a housing registration act of a housing registration authority, or an administrative act or corresponding omission relating to matters such as inquiring into or duplicating registration materials, and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  房屋登记机构根据人民法院、仲裁委员会的法律文书或者有权机关的协助执行通知书以及人民政府的征收决定办理的房屋登记行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但公民、法人或者其他组织认为登记与有关文书内容不一致的除外。  
房屋登记机构作出未改变登记内容的换发、补发权属证书、登记证明或者更新登记簿的行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。  
房屋登记机构在行政诉讼法施行前作出的房屋登记行为，公民、法人或者其他组织不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 2** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept a housing registration act performed by a housing registration authority on the basis of a legal instrument of the People's Court or an arbitration commission, or a notice of assistance in enforcement from an authority with power, or an expropriation decision of the people's government, and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall not accept the case, except where the citizen, legal person, or any other organization considers that the registration is inconsistent with the content of the relevant instrument.  
Where a housing registration authority makes an act of replacing or reissuing a title certificate, registration certificate, or updating the register without changing the registration content, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept it and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
Where a housing registration authority made a housing registration act before the Administrative Procedure Law came into force, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization refuses to accept it and initiates an administrative action, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  公民、法人或者其他组织对房屋登记行为不服提起行政诉讼的，不受下列情形的影响：  
（一）房屋灭失；  
（二）房屋登记行为已被登记机构改变；  
（三）生效法律文书将房屋权属证书、房屋登记簿或者房屋登记证明作为定案证据采用。

**Article 3** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an administrative action against a housing registration act, it shall not be affected by the following circumstances:  
(1) the house is destroyed;  
(2) the housing registration act has been changed by the registration authority;  
(3) an effective legal instrument adopts the title certificate, the register, or the registration certificate of the house as evidence for conviction.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  房屋登记机构为债务人办理房屋转移登记，债权人不服提起诉讼，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当依法受理：  
（一）以房屋为标的物的债权已办理预告登记的；  
（二）债权人为抵押权人且房屋转让未经其同意的；  
（三）人民法院依债权人申请对房屋采取强制执行措施并已通知房屋登记机构的；  
（四）房屋登记机构工作人员与债务人恶意串通的。

**Article 4** Where a housing registration authority handles a transfer registration of a house for a debtor, and the creditor refuses to accept it and initiates a prosecution, if any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) the creditor's right with the house as the subject matter has been registered as a advance notice;  
(2) the creditor is a mortgagee and the transfer of the house has not been approved by him;  
(3) the People's Court, upon the creditor's application, takes compulsory enforcement measures against the house and has notified the housing registration authority;  
(4) a staff member of the housing registration authority acts in collusion with the debtor.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  同一房屋多次转移登记，原房屋权利人、原利害关系人对首次转移登记行为提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
原房屋权利人、原利害关系人对首次转移登记行为及后续转移登记行为一并提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理；人民法院判决驳回原告就在先转移登记行为提出的诉讼请求，或者因保护善意第三人确认在先房屋登记行为违法的，应当裁定驳回原告对后续转移登记行为的起诉。  
原房屋权利人、原利害关系人未就首次转移登记行为提起行政诉讼，对后续转移登记行为提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 5** Where the same house undergoes multiple transfer registrations, and the original house right holder or the original interested party initiates an administrative action against the first transfer registration act, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where the original house right holder or the original interested party initiates an administrative action against both the first transfer registration act and the subsequent transfer registration acts, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law; if the People's Court renders a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claim regarding the prior transfer registration act, or confirms that the prior housing registration act is illegal for the purpose of protecting a bona fide third party, it shall issue a ruling dismissing the plaintiff's prosecution against the subsequent transfer registration acts.  
Where the original house right holder or the original interested party does not initiate an administrative action against the first transfer registration act, but initiates an administrative action against the subsequent transfer registration acts, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院受理房屋登记行政案件后，应当通知没有起诉的下列利害关系人作为第三人参加行政诉讼：  
（一）房屋登记簿上载明的权利人；  
（二）被诉异议登记、更正登记、预告登记的权利人；  
（三）人民法院能够确认的其他利害关系人。

**Article 6** After accepting a housing registration administrative case, the People's Court shall notify the following interested parties who have not initiated a prosecution to participate in the administrative action as third persons:  
(1) the right holder recorded on the housing register;  
(2) the right holder of the challenged objection registration, correction registration, or advance notice registration;  
(3) any other interested party that the People's Court can confirm.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  房屋登记行政案件由房屋所在地人民法院管辖，但有下列情形之一的也可由被告所在地人民法院管辖：  
（一）请求房屋登记机构履行房屋转移登记、查询、复制登记资料等职责的；  
（二）对房屋登记机构收缴房产证行为提起行政诉讼的；  
（三）对行政复议改变房屋登记行为提起行政诉讼的。

**Article 7** A housing registration administrative case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the house is located, but in any of the following circumstances, it may also be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the defendant is located:  
(1) the request is for the housing registration authority to perform duties such as housing transfer registration, inquiring into or duplicating registration materials;  
(2) an administrative action is initiated against the housing registration authority's act of collecting the house property certificate;  
(3) an administrative action is initiated against the alteration of the housing registration act by administrative reconsideration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  当事人以作为房屋登记行为基础的买卖、共有、赠与、抵押、婚姻、继承等民事法律关系无效或者应当撤销为由，对房屋登记行为提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当告知当事人先行解决民事争议，民事争议处理期间不计算在行政诉讼起诉期限内；已经受理的，裁定中止诉讼。

**Article 8** Where a party initiates an administrative action against a housing registration act on the ground that the civil legal relationship underlying the housing registration act, such as sale, co-ownership, gift, mortgage, marriage, or succession, is void or shall be revoked, the People's Court shall notify the party to first resolve the civil dispute, and the period during which the civil dispute is being handled shall not be included in the time limit for initiation of an administrative action; if the case has already been accepted, the People's Court shall issue a ruling to suspend the action.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  被告对被诉房屋登记行为的合法性负举证责任。被告保管证据原件的，应当在法庭上出示。被告不保管原件的，应当提交与原件核对一致的复印件、复制件并作出说明。当事人对被告提交的上述证据提出异议的，应当提供相应的证据。

**Article 9** The defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the legality of the challenged housing registration act. If the defendant keeps the original evidence, it shall present it in court. If the defendant does not keep the originals, it shall submit copies or reproductions that have been checked against the originals and provide explanations. If a party raises an objection to the aforesaid evidence submitted by the defendant, it shall provide corresponding evidence.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  被诉房屋登记行为合法的，人民法院应当判决驳回原告的诉讼请求。

**Article 10** If the challenged housing registration act is lawful, the People's Court shall render a judgment dismissing the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  被诉房屋登记行为涉及多个权利主体或者房屋可分，其中部分主体或者房屋的登记违法应予撤销的，可以判决部分撤销。  
被诉房屋登记行为违法，但该行为已被登记机构改变的，判决确认被诉行为违法。  
被诉房屋登记行为违法，但判决撤销将给公共利益造成重大损失或者房屋已为第三人善意取得的，判决确认被诉行为违法，不撤销登记行为。

**Article 11** If a challenged housing registration act involves multiple right holders or the house is divisible, and the registration of some of the holders or part of the house is illegal and shall be revoked, the People's Court may render a judgment partially revoking it.  
If a challenged housing registration act is illegal but the act has been changed by the registration authority, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming that the challenged act is illegal.  
If a challenged housing registration act is illegal, but a judgment revoking it would cause substantial damage to the public interest or the house has been acquired by a third party in good faith, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming that the challenged act is illegal without revoking the registration act.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  申请人提供虚假材料办理房屋登记，给原告造成损害，房屋登记机构未尽合理审慎职责的，应当根据其过错程度及其在损害发生中所起作用承担相应的赔偿责任。

**Article 12** If an applicant provides false materials to handle housing registration, causing damage to the plaintiff, and the housing registration authority fails to fulfill its duty of reasonable care, it shall bear corresponding compensation liability according to the degree of its fault and its role in the occurrence of the damage.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  房屋登记机构工作人员与第三人恶意串通违法登记，侵犯原告合法权益的，房屋登记机构与第三人承担连带赔偿责任。

**Article 13** If a staff member of a housing registration authority acts in collusion with a third party to make an illegal registration, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of the plaintiff, the housing registration authority and the third party shall bear joint and several compensation liability.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  最高人民法院以前所作的相关的司法解释，凡与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
农村集体土地上的房屋登记行政案件参照本规定。

**Article 14** Any relevant judicial interpretation previously made by the Supreme People's Court that is inconsistent with these Provisions shall be governed by these Provisions.  
Housing registration administrative cases concerning houses on rural collective land shall be handled with reference to these Provisions.
