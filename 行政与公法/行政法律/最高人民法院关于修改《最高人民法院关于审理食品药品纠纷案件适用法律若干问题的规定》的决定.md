# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理食品药品纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的规定》的决定

**发布机关**：最高人民法院

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　消费者因食品、药品纠纷提起民事诉讼，符合民事诉讼法规定受理条件的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** Where a consumer files a civil action arising from a food or drug dispute, if the acceptance conditions prescribed in the Civil Procedure Law are met, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因食品、药品存在质量问题造成消费者损害，消费者可以分别起诉或者同时起诉销售者和生产者。  
消费者仅起诉销售者或者生产者的，必要时人民法院可以追加相关当事人参加诉讼。

**Article 2** Where damage is caused to a consumer due to quality issues in food or drugs, the consumer may sue the seller or the producer separately or simultaneously.  
Where the consumer only sues the seller or the producer, the People's Court may, when necessary, add relevant parties to participate in the proceedings.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因食品、药品质量问题发生纠纷，购买者向生产者、销售者主张权利，生产者、销售者以购买者明知食品、药品存在质量问题而仍然购买为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 3** Where a dispute arises due to quality issues of food or drugs, and the purchaser claims rights against the producer or seller, if the producer or seller defends on the ground that the purchaser knowingly purchased the food or drug despite its quality issues, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　食品、药品生产者、销售者提供给消费者的食品或者药品的赠品发生质量安全问题，造成消费者损害，消费者主张权利，生产者、销售者以消费者未对赠品支付对价为由进行免责抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 4** Where a quality safety issue occurs in a free gift of food or drug provided by a producer or seller to a consumer, causing damage to the consumer, and the consumer claims rights, if the producer or seller defends on the ground that the consumer did not pay consideration for the free gift, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　消费者举证证明所购买食品、药品的事实以及所购食品、药品不符合合同的约定，主张食品、药品的生产者、销售者承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
消费者举证证明因食用食品或者使用药品受到损害，初步证明损害与食用食品或者使用药品存在因果关系，并请求食品、药品的生产者、销售者承担侵权责任的，人民法院应予支持，但食品、药品的生产者、销售者能证明损害不是因产品不符合质量标准造成的除外。

**Article 5** Where a consumer adduces evidence to prove the fact of purchasing food or drugs and that the purchased food or drug does not conform to the contract, and claims that the producer or seller of the food or drug bears liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where a consumer adduces evidence to prove damage caused by consuming food or using drugs, and preliminarily proves a causal relationship between the damage and the consumption of food or use of drugs, and requests the producer or seller of the food or drug to bear tort liability, the People's Court shall support such request, unless the producer or seller of the food or drug can prove that the damage was not caused by the product's failure to meet quality standards.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　食品的生产者与销售者应当对于食品符合质量标准承担举证责任。认定食品是否安全，应当以国家标准为依据；对地方特色食品，没有国家标准的，应当以地方标准为依据。没有前述标准的，应当以食品安全法的相关规定为依据。

**Article 6** The producer and seller of food shall bear the burden of adducing evidence that the food meets quality standards. Whether food is safe shall be determined on the basis of national standards; for local specialty foods for which no national standards exist, the determination shall be based on local standards. If no such standards exist, the determination shall be based on the relevant provisions of the Food Safety Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　食品、药品虽在销售前取得检验合格证明，且食用或者使用时尚在保质期内，但经检验确认产品不合格，生产者或者销售者以该食品、药品具有检验合格证明为由进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 7** Where a food or drug has obtained a certificate of inspection and testing before sale and is still within its shelf life when consumed or used, but the product is confirmed to be non-conforming upon inspection, if the producer or seller defends on the ground that the food or drug has a certificate of inspection and testing, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　集中交易市场的开办者、柜台出租者、展销会举办者未履行食品安全法规定的审查、检查、报告等义务，使消费者的合法权益受到损害的，消费者请求集中交易市场的开办者、柜台出租者、展销会举办者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** Where the organizer of a centralized trading market, the lessor of a counter, or the sponsor of a trade fair fails to perform the obligations of review, inspection, reporting, and other obligations as prescribed in the Food Safety Law, thereby causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and the consumer requests the organizer, lessor, or sponsor to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　消费者通过网络交易第三方平台购买食品、药品遭受损害，网络交易第三方平台提供者不能提供食品、药品的生产者或者销售者的真实名称、地址与有效联系方式，消费者请求网络交易第三方平台提供者承担责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
网络交易第三方平台提供者承担赔偿责任后，向生产者或者销售者行使追偿权的，人民法院应予支持。  
网络交易第三方平台提供者知道或者应当知道食品、药品的生产者、销售者利用其平台侵害消费者合法权益，未采取必要措施，给消费者造成损害，消费者要求其与生产者、销售者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 9** Where a consumer suffers damage from purchasing food or drugs through a third-party online trading platform, if the third-party online trading platform provider is unable to provide the true name, address, and valid contact information of the producer or seller of the food or drug, and the consumer requests the third-party online trading platform provider to bear liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the third-party online trading platform provider, after bearing compensation liability, exercises its right of recourse against the producer or seller, the People's Court shall support such exercise.  
Where the third-party online trading platform provider knew or should have known that the producer or seller of food or drugs was using its platform to infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of consumers, and failed to take necessary measures, thereby causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests that the platform provider bear joint and several liability with the producer and seller, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　未取得食品生产资质与销售资质的民事主体，挂靠具有相应资质的生产者与销售者，生产、销售食品，造成消费者损害，消费者请求挂靠者与被挂靠者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
消费者仅起诉挂靠者或者被挂靠者的，必要时人民法院可以追加相关当事人参加诉讼。

**Article 10** Where a civil subject that has not obtained the qualification for food production or sales, attaches itself to a producer or seller with corresponding qualifications to produce and sell food, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests the attaching party and the attached party to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the consumer only sues the attaching party or the attached party, the People's Court may, when necessary, add relevant parties to participate in the proceedings.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　消费者因虚假广告推荐的食品、药品存在质量问题遭受损害，依据消费者权益保护法等法律相关规定请求广告经营者、广告发布者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
其他民事主体在虚假广告中向消费者推荐食品、药品，使消费者遭受损害，消费者依据消费者权益保护法等法律相关规定请求其与食品、药品的生产者、销售者承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 11** Where a consumer suffers damage due to quality issues in food or drugs recommended by false advertising, and requests the advertising operator and advertising publisher to bear joint and several liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests and other laws, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where other civil subjects recommend food or drugs to consumers in false advertising, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests them to bear joint and several liability together with the producer and seller of the food or drug in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests and other laws, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　食品检验机构故意出具虚假检验报告，造成消费者损害，消费者请求其承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
食品检验机构因过失出具不实检验报告，造成消费者损害，消费者请求其承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where a food inspection institution intentionally issues a false inspection report, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests it to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a food inspection institution negligently issues an untrue inspection report, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests it to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　食品认证机构故意出具虚假认证，造成消费者损害，消费者请求其承担连带责任的，人民法院应予支持。  
食品认证机构因过失出具不实认证，造成消费者损害，消费者请求其承担相应责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where a food certification institution intentionally issues a false certification, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests it to bear joint and several liability, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where a food certification institution negligently issues an untrue certification, causing damage to consumers, and the consumer requests it to bear corresponding liability, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　生产、销售的食品、药品存在质量问题，生产者与销售者需同时承担民事责任、行政责任和刑事责任，其财产不足以支付，当事人依照民法典等有关法律规定，请求食品、药品的生产者、销售者首先承担民事责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 14** Where food or drugs produced or sold have quality issues, and the producer and seller need to simultaneously bear civil liability, administrative liability, and criminal liability, but their property is insufficient to pay all of them, if a party requests, in accordance with the Civil Code and other relevant laws, that the producer and seller of the food or drug bear civil liability first, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生产不符合安全标准的食品或者销售明知是不符合安全标准的食品，消费者除要求赔偿损失外，依据食品安全法等法律规定向生产者、销售者主张赔偿金的，人民法院应予支持。  
生产假药、劣药或者明知是假药、劣药仍然销售、使用的，受害人或者其近亲属除请求赔偿损失外，依据药品管理法等法律规定向生产者、销售者主张赔偿金的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 15** Where a person produces food that does not meet safety standards or knowingly sells food that does not meet safety standards, and the consumer, in addition to claiming compensation for losses, claims punitive damages from the producer or seller in accordance with the Food Safety Law and other laws, the People's Court shall support such claim.  
Where a person produces counterfeit or substandard drugs, or knowingly sells or uses counterfeit or substandard drugs, and the victim or his or her close relatives, in addition to claiming compensation for losses, claim punitive damages from the producer or seller in accordance with the Drug Administration Law and other laws, the People's Court shall support such claim.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　食品、药品的生产者与销售者以格式合同、通知、声明、告示等方式作出排除或者限制消费者权利，减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理的规定，消费者依法请求认定该内容无效的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 16** Where a producer or seller of food or drugs makes provisions in a standard form contract, notice, statement, announcement, or in other ways that exclude or limit the rights of consumers, reduce or exempt the liability of the business operator, or increase the liability of consumers, and such provisions are unfair or unreasonable to consumers, and the consumer requests, according to law, that such content be deemed invalid, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　消费者与化妆品、保健食品等产品的生产者、销售者、广告经营者、广告发布者、推荐者、检验机构等主体之间的纠纷，参照适用本规定。  
法律规定的机关和有关组织依法提起公益诉讼的，参照适用本规定。

**Article 17** Disputes between consumers and producers, sellers, advertising operators, advertising publishers, recommenders, inspection institutions, or other entities of cosmetics, health foods, and other products shall be governed by these Provisions mutatis mutandis.  
Where public interest litigation is initiated by an authority or relevant organization as prescribed by law, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本规定所称的“药品的生产者”包括药品上市许可持有人和药品生产企业，“药品的销售者”包括药品经营企业和医疗机构。

**Article 18** For the purpose of these Provisions, "producer of drugs" includes the drug marketing authorization holder and the drug manufacturing enterprise; "seller of drugs" includes the drug trading enterprise and medical institution.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定施行后人民法院正在审理的一审、二审案件适用本规定。  
本规定施行前已经终审，本规定施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall apply to cases of first instance and second instance being tried by the People's Court after these Provisions come into force.  
These Provisions shall not apply to cases that have been finally adjudicated before these Provisions come into force, and for which a party applies for a retrial or which are decided to be retried in accordance with the trial supervision procedure after these Provisions come into force.
