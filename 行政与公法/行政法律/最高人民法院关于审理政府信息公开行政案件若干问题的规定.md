# 最高人民法院关于审理政府信息公开行政案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕17号  
**公布日期**：2010-12-13  
**生效日期**：2011-08-13

> 最高人民法院《关于审理政府信息公开行政案件若干问题的规定》已于2010年12月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1505次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年8月13日起施行。 2011年7月29日 最高人民法院 关于审理政府信息公开行政案件 若干问题的规定 法释〔2011〕17号 为正确审理政府信息公开行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国政府信息公开条例》等法律、行政法规的规定，结合行政审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为下列政府信息公开工作中的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益，依法提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当受理： （一）向行政机关申请获取政府信息，行政机关拒绝提供或者逾期不予答复的； （二）认为行政机关提供的政府信息不符合其在申请中要求的内容或者法律、法规规定的适当形式的； （三）认为行政机关主动公开或者依他人申请公开政府信息侵犯其商业秘密、个人隐私的； （四）认为行政机关提供的与其自身相关的政府信息记录不准确，要求该行政机关予以更正，该行政机关拒绝更正、逾期不予答复或者不予转送有权机关处理的； （五）认为行政机关在政府信息公开工作中的其他具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的。 公民、法人或者其他组织认为政府信息公开行政行为侵犯其合法权益造成损害的，可以一并或单独提起行政赔偿诉讼。 第二条  公民、法人或者其他组织对下列行为不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理：

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为下列政府信息公开工作中的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益，依法提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当受理：  
（一）向行政机关申请获取政府信息，行政机关拒绝提供或者逾期不予答复的；  
（二）认为行政机关提供的政府信息不符合其在申请中要求的内容或者法律、法规规定的适当形式的；  
（三）认为行政机关主动公开或者依他人申请公开政府信息侵犯其商业秘密、个人隐私的；  
（四）认为行政机关提供的与其自身相关的政府信息记录不准确，要求该行政机关予以更正，该行政机关拒绝更正、逾期不予答复或者不予转送有权机关处理的；  
（五）认为行政机关在政府信息公开工作中的其他具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的。  
公民、法人或者其他组织认为政府信息公开行政行为侵犯其合法权益造成损害的，可以一并或单独提起行政赔偿诉讼。

**Article 1** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization considers that a specific administrative act in the following government information disclosure work infringes upon his/her/its lawful rights and interests and initiates an administrative action according to law, the People's Court shall accept the case:  
(1) The party applies to an administrative organ for access to government information, and the administrative organ refuses to provide it or fails to reply within the time limit;  
(2) The party considers that the government information provided by the administrative organ does not conform to the content requested in the application or the appropriate form as prescribed by laws or regulations;  
(3) The party considers that the government information proactively disclosed by the administrative organ or disclosed upon another person's application infringes upon his/her/its trade secrets or personal privacy;  
(4) The party considers that the record of government information relating to himself/herself/itself provided by the administrative organ is inaccurate, requests the administrative organ to correct it, and the administrative organ refuses to correct, fails to reply within the time limit, or fails to forward the matter to the competent authority for handling;  
(5) The party considers that other specific administrative acts of the administrative organ in the government information disclosure work infringe upon his/her/its lawful rights and interests.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization considers that an administrative act of government information disclosure infringes upon his/her/its lawful rights and interests and causes damage, he/she/it may bring an administrative compensation action concurrently or separately.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  公民、法人或者其他组织对下列行为不服提起行政诉讼的，人民法院不予受理：  
（一）因申请内容不明确，行政机关要求申请人作出更改、补充且对申请人权利义务不产生实际影响的告知行为；  
（二）要求行政机关提供政府公报、报纸、杂志、书籍等公开出版物，行政机关予以拒绝的；  
（三）要求行政机关为其制作、搜集政府信息，或者对若干政府信息进行汇总、分析、加工，行政机关予以拒绝的；  
（四）行政程序中的当事人、利害关系人以政府信息公开名义申请查阅案卷材料，行政机关告知其应当按照相关法律、法规的规定办理的。

**Article 2** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an administrative action against any of the following acts, the People's Court shall not accept the case:  
(1) An act of notification by an administrative organ requiring the applicant to make changes or supplements because the application content is not clear, and such act does not have actual impact on the applicant's rights and obligations;  
(2) The party requests the administrative organ to provide public publications such as government gazettes, newspapers, magazines, books, etc., and the administrative organ refuses to do so;  
(3) The party requests the administrative organ to produce or collect government information for him/her/it, or to summarize, analyze, or process several pieces of government information, and the administrative organ refuses to do so;  
(4) A party or interested person in an administrative procedure applies to inspect case file materials in the name of government information disclosure, and the administrative organ informs him/her/it that the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  公民、法人或者其他组织认为行政机关不依法履行主动公开政府信息义务，直接向人民法院提起诉讼的，应当告知其先向行政机关申请获取相关政府信息。对行政机关的答复或者逾期不予答复不服的，可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 3** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization considers that an administrative organ fails to perform its obligation to proactively disclose government information according to law, and directly initiates an action with a People's Court, the court shall inform the party to first apply to the administrative organ for access to the relevant government information. If the party is dissatisfied with the administrative organ's reply or with the failure to reply within the time limit, the party may initiate an action with the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  公民、法人或者其他组织对国务院部门、地方各级人民政府及县级以上地方人民政府部门依申请公开政府信息行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以作出答复的机关为被告；逾期未作出答复的，以受理申请的机关为被告。  
公民、法人或者其他组织对主动公开政府信息行政行为不服提起诉讼的，以公开该政府信息的机关为被告。  
公民、法人或者其他组织对法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织公开政府信息的行为不服提起诉讼的，以该组织为被告。  
有下列情形之一的，应当以在对外发生法律效力的文书上署名的机关为被告：  
（一）政府信息公开与否的答复依法报经有权机关批准的；  
（二）政府信息是否可以公开系由国家保密行政管理部门或者省、自治区、直辖市保密行政管理部门确定的；  
（三）行政机关在公开政府信息前与有关行政机关进行沟通、确认的。

**Article 4** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an action for dissatisfaction with an administrative act of disclosing government information upon application by a department of the State Council, a local people's government at any level, or a department of a local people's government at or above the county level, the organ that made the reply shall be the defendant; if no reply is made within the time limit, the organ that accepted the application shall be the defendant.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an action for dissatisfaction with an administrative act of proactively disclosing government information, the organ that disclosed the government information shall be the defendant.  
Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization initiates an action for dissatisfaction with an act of disclosing government information by an organization authorized by laws or regulations to perform public affairs management functions, such organization shall be the defendant.  
Under any of the following circumstances, the organ that signed the document that takes legal effect externally shall be the defendant:  
(1) The reply on whether to disclose government information is submitted to the competent authority for approval according to law;  
(2) Whether the government information may be disclosed is determined by the national保密administrative department or the保密administrative department of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government;  
(3) The administrative organ communicates and confirms with the relevant administrative organ before disclosing the government information.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  被告拒绝向原告提供政府信息的，应当对拒绝的根据以及履行法定告知和说明理由义务的情况举证。  
因公共利益决定公开涉及商业秘密、个人隐私政府信息的，被告应当对认定公共利益以及不公开可能对公共利益造成重大影响的理由进行举证和说明。  
被告拒绝更正与原告相关的政府信息记录的，应当对拒绝的理由进行举证和说明。  
被告能够证明政府信息涉及国家秘密，请求在诉讼中不予提交的，人民法院应当准许。  
被告主张政府信息不存在，原告能够提供该政府信息系由被告制作或者保存的相关线索的，可以申请人民法院调取证据。  
被告以政府信息与申请人自身生产、生活、科研等特殊需要无关为由不予提供的，人民法院可以要求原告对特殊需要事由作出说明。  
原告起诉被告拒绝更正政府信息记录的，应当提供其向被告提出过更正申请以及政府信息与其自身相关且记录不准确的事实根据。

**Article 5** Where the defendant refuses to provide government information to the plaintiff, the defendant shall adduce evidence on the basis of the refusal and on the performance of the statutory obligation to inform and state reasons.  
Where a decision is made to disclose government information involving trade secrets or personal privacy for the sake of public interest, the defendant shall adduce evidence and provide explanations on the determination of public interest and on the reasons that non-disclosure may cause significant adverse effects on public interest.  
Where the defendant refuses to correct a government information record relating to the plaintiff, the defendant shall adduce evidence and provide explanations on the reasons for the refusal.  
If the defendant can prove that the government information involves State secrets and requests not to submit it in the proceedings, the People's Court shall grant the request.  
If the defendant claims that the government information does not exist, and the plaintiff can provide relevant clues that the government information was created or maintained by the defendant, the plaintiff may apply to the People's Court to obtain the evidence.  
If the defendant refuses to provide the government information on the ground that the government information is not related to the applicant's special needs such as production, livelihood, or scientific research, the People's Court may request the plaintiff to provide explanations on the special needs.  
Where the plaintiff sues the defendant for refusing to correct a government information record, the plaintiff shall provide factual grounds that he/she/it submitted a correction request to the defendant and that the government information relates to him/her/it and the record is inaccurate.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院审理政府信息公开行政案件，应当视情采取适当的审理方式，以避免泄露涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私或者法律规定的其他应当保密的政府信息。

**Article 6** When hearing an administrative case concerning government information disclosure, the People's Court shall adopt appropriate trial methods depending on the circumstances, so as to avoid leakage of government information involving State secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or other government information that shall be kept confidential as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  政府信息由被告的档案机构或者档案工作人员保管的，适用《中华人民共和国政府信息公开条例》的规定。  
政府信息已经移交各级国家档案馆的，依照有关档案管理的法律、行政法规和国家有关规定执行。

**Article 7** Where the government information is kept by the defendant's archives institution or archival staff, the provisions of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Open Government Information shall apply.  
Where the government information has been transferred to State archives at various levels, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the laws, administrative regulations, and relevant State provisions on archives management.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  政府信息涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私的，人民法院应当认定属于不予公开范围。  
政府信息涉及商业秘密、个人隐私，但权利人同意公开，或者不公开可能对公共利益造成重大影响的，不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 8** Where government information involves State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy, the People's Court shall determine that it falls within the scope of non-disclosure.  
Where government information involves trade secrets or personal privacy, but the right holder agrees to disclosure, or non-disclosure may cause significant adverse effects on public interest, the restriction in the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  被告对依法应当公开的政府信息拒绝或者部分拒绝公开的，人民法院应当撤销或者部分撤销被诉不予公开决定，并判决被告在一定期限内公开。尚需被告调查、裁量的，判决其在一定期限内重新答复。  
被告提供的政府信息不符合申请人要求的内容或者法律、法规规定的适当形式的，人民法院应当判决被告按照申请人要求的内容或者法律、法规规定的适当形式提供。  
人民法院经审理认为被告不予公开的政府信息内容可以作区分处理的，应当判决被告限期公开可以公开的内容。  
被告依法应当更正而不更正与原告相关的政府信息记录的，人民法院应当判决被告在一定期限内更正。尚需被告调查、裁量的，判决其在一定期限内重新答复。被告无权更正的，判决其转送有权更正的行政机关处理。

**Article 9** Where the defendant refuses to disclose or partially refuses to disclose government information that shall be disclosed according to law, the People's Court shall revoke or partially revoke the contested non-disclosure decision, and shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to disclose it within a specified time limit. If investigation or discretion by the defendant is still required, the court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to make a new reply within a specified time limit.  
Where the government information provided by the defendant does not conform to the content requested by the applicant or the appropriate form as prescribed by laws or regulations, the People's Court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to provide it in the content requested by the applicant or in the appropriate form as prescribed by laws or regulations.  
If, after trial, the People's Court considers that the content of government information that the defendant refuses to disclose may be processed by differentiation, the court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to disclose the portion that may be disclosed within a specified time limit.  
Where the defendant is required by law to correct a government information record relating to the plaintiff but fails to do so, the People's Court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to correct it within a specified time limit. If investigation or discretion by the defendant is still required, the court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to make a new reply within a specified time limit. If the defendant is not authorized to make the correction, the court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to forward the matter to the administrative organ authorized to correct it for handling.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  被告对原告要求公开或者更正政府信息的申请无正当理由逾期不予答复的，人民法院应当判决被告在一定期限内答复。原告一并请求判决被告公开或者更正政府信息且理由成立的，参照第九条的规定处理。

**Article 10** Where the defendant fails to reply within the time limit without justifiable reasons to the plaintiff's application for disclosure or correction of government information, the People's Court shall enter a judgment ordering the defendant to reply within a specified time limit. Where the plaintiff concurrently requests a judgment ordering the defendant to disclose or correct the government information and the reasons are established, the matter shall be handled by reference to the provisions of Article 9.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  被告公开政府信息涉及原告商业秘密、个人隐私且不存在公共利益等法定事由的，人民法院应当判决确认公开政府信息的行为违法，并可以责令被告采取相应的补救措施；造成损害的，根据原告请求依法判决被告承担赔偿责任。政府信息尚未公开的，应当判决行政机关不得公开。  
诉讼期间，原告申请停止公开涉及其商业秘密、个人隐私的政府信息，人民法院经审查认为公开该政府信息会造成难以弥补的损失，并且停止公开不损害公共利益的，可以依照[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第四十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-44)的规定，裁定暂时停止公开。

**Article 11** Where the defendant discloses government information involving the plaintiff's trade secrets or personal privacy and there are no statutory grounds such as public interest, the People's Court shall enter a judgment confirming that the act of disclosing the government information is illegal, and may order the defendant to take appropriate remedial measures; if damage has been caused, the court shall, according to law, enter a judgment ordering the defendant to bear compensation liability upon the plaintiff's request. If the government information has not yet been disclosed, the court shall enter a judgment ordering the administrative organ not to disclose it.  
During the litigation period, if the plaintiff applies for an order to stop the disclosure of government information involving his/her/its trade secrets or personal privacy, and the People's Court, after review, considers that the disclosure of such government information will cause irreparable damage and that stopping the disclosure does not harm public interest, the court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, issue a ruling to temporarily stop the disclosure.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  有下列情形之一，被告已经履行法定告知或者说明理由义务的，人民法院应当判决驳回原告的诉讼请求：  
（一）不属于政府信息、政府信息不存在、依法属于不予公开范围或者依法不属于被告公开的；  
（二）申请公开的政府信息已经向公众公开，被告已经告知申请人获取该政府信息的方式和途径的；  
（三）起诉被告逾期不予答复，理由不成立的；  
（四）以政府信息侵犯其商业秘密、个人隐私为由反对公开，理由不成立的；  
（五）要求被告更正与其自身相关的政府信息记录，理由不成立的；  
（六）不能合理说明申请获取政府信息系根据自身生产、生活、科研等特殊需要，且被告据此不予提供的；  
（七）无法按照申请人要求的形式提供政府信息，且被告已通过安排申请人查阅相关资料、提供复制件或者其他适当形式提供的；  
（八）其他应当判决驳回诉讼请求的情形。

**Article 12** Under any of the following circumstances, where the defendant has performed the statutory obligation to inform or state reasons, the People's Court shall enter a judgment rejecting the plaintiff's claims:  
(1) The information is not government information, the government information does not exist, it is within the scope of non-disclosure according to law, or it is not subject to disclosure by the defendant according to law;  
(2) The government information applied for disclosure has already been made public, and the defendant has informed the applicant of the means and channels to obtain such government information;  
(3) The action against the defendant for failure to reply within the time limit is not well-founded;  
(4) The objection to disclosure on the ground that the government information infringes upon the objector's trade secrets or personal privacy is not well-founded;  
(5) The request that the defendant correct a government information record relating to the requester is not well-founded;  
(6) The applicant cannot reasonably explain that the application for access to government information is based on his/her/its special needs such as production, livelihood, or scientific research, and the defendant therefore refuses to provide the information;  
(7) The government information cannot be provided in the form requested by the applicant, and the defendant has provided it by arranging for the applicant to review relevant materials, providing copies, or through other appropriate forms;  
(8) Other circumstances where a judgment rejecting the claims shall be entered.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  最高人民法院以前所作的司法解释及规范性文件，凡与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 13** Where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
