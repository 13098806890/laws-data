# 最高人民法院关于《人民法院审理治安行政案件具体应用法律的若干问题的暂行规定》是否适用于审理其他行政案件的批复

**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1986-10-24

### 最高人民法院关于《人民法院审理治安行政案件具体应用法律的若干问题的暂行规定》是否适用于审理其他行政案件的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于《人民法院审理治安  
  
行政案件具体应用法律的若干问题的暂行规定》  
  
是否适用于审理其他行政案件的批复  
  
法（研）复〔 1988〕9号  
  
北京市高级人民法院：  
  
你院〔 1987〕京高法字第 117号》关于人民法院审理不服海关处理的行政案件的诉讼程序法律适用问题的请示》收悉。经研究，同意你院意见，即：最高人民法院 1986年10月24日下发的《人民法院审理治安行政案件具体应用法律的若干问题的暂行规定》，只适用于人民法院审理的治安行政案件；人民法院审理其他行政案件，包括不服海关处理的行政案件，仍应依照民事诉讼法（试行）第三条第二款的规定，即“法律规定由人民法院审理的行政案件，适用本法规定”办理，不宜援引审理治安行政案件的 “暂行规定”。  
  
此复  
  
1988年 1月13日

**Article 1** Reply on Whether the "Provisional Provisions on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Public Security Administrative Cases by the People's Courts"  
Is Applicable to the Trial of Other Administrative Cases  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1988] No. 9  
Beijing Higher People's Court:  
We have received your request, [1987] Jing Gao Fa Zi No. 117, "Request for Instructions on the Applicable Law of Litigation Procedure in the Trial of Administrative Cases Involving Disputes with Customs Decisions by the People's Courts". Upon study, we agree with your opinion, namely: the "Provisional Provisions on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Public Security Administrative Cases by the People's Courts" issued by the Supreme People's Court on October 24, 1986, shall apply only to public security administrative cases tried by the People's Courts; for trial of other administrative cases by the People's Courts, including administrative cases involving disputes with customs decisions, the provisions of Article 3, Paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation) shall still apply, i.e., "administrative cases that shall be tried by the People's Courts as prescribed by law shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of this Law", and the "Provisional Provisions" for trial of public security administrative cases shall not be cited.  
Hereby this reply.  
January 13, 1988
