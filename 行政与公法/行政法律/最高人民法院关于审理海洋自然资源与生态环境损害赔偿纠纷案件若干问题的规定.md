# 最高人民法院关于审理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕23号  
**公布日期**：2017-11-20  
**生效日期**：2018-01-15

> 最高人民法院《关于审理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定》已于2017年11月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1727次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年1月15日起施行。 2017年12月29日 最高人民法院 关于审理海洋自然资源与生态环境 损害赔偿纠纷案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2017〕23号 为正确审理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿纠纷案件，根据《中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》等法律的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院审理为请求赔偿海洋环境保护法第八十九条第二款规定的海洋自然资源与生态环境损害而提起的诉讼，适用本规定。 第二条　在海上或者沿海陆域内从事活动，对中华人民共和国管辖海域内海洋自然资源与生态环境造成损害，由此提起的海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，由损害行为发生地、损害结果地或者采取预防措施地海事法院管辖。 第三条　海洋环境保护法第五条规定的行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关，根据其职能分工提起海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，人民法院应予受理。 第四条　人民法院受理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，应当在立案之日起五日内公告案件受理情况。 人民法院在审理中发现可能存在下列情形之一的，可以书面告知其他依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关： （一）同一损害涉及不同区域或者不同部门； （二）不同损害应由其他依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关索赔。 本规定所称不同损害，包括海洋自然资源与生态环境损害中不同种类和同种类但可以明确区分属不同机关索赔范围的损害。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理为请求赔偿[海洋环境保护法第八十九条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-89)第二款规定的海洋自然资源与生态环境损害而提起的诉讼，适用本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to the trial by a People's Court of actions filed for claiming compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment as specified in Article 89(2) of the Marine Environment Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在海上或者沿海陆域内从事活动，对中华人民共和国管辖海域内海洋自然资源与生态环境造成损害，由此提起的海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，由损害行为发生地、损害结果地或者采取预防措施地海事法院管辖。

**Article 2** Where an activity conducted at sea or within coastal land areas causes damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, the maritime court of the place where the damaging act occurred, where the damage results occurred, or where preventive measures were taken shall have jurisdiction over the marine natural resources and ecological environment damage compensation action thus instituted.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[海洋环境保护法第五条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国海洋环境保护法.md#art-5)规定的行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关，根据其职能分工提起海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 3** Where an authority exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration as specified in Article 5 of the Marine Environment Protection Law institutes an action for compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment in accordance with its functional division, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院受理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼，应当在立案之日起五日内公告案件受理情况。  
人民法院在审理中发现可能存在下列情形之一的，可以书面告知其他依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关：  
（一）同一损害涉及不同区域或者不同部门；  
（二）不同损害应由其他依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关索赔。  
本规定所称不同损害，包括海洋自然资源与生态环境损害中不同种类和同种类但可以明确区分属不同机关索赔范围的损害。

**Article 4** Where a People's Court accepts an action for compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment, it shall make a public announcement of the case acceptance within five days from the date of filing.  
If, during trial, the People's Court finds that any of the following circumstances may exist, it may inform in writing other authorities that exercise the power of marine environmental supervision and administration according to law:  
(1) the same damage involves different regions or different departments;  
(2) different damages shall be claimed by other authorities that exercise the power of marine environmental supervision and administration according to law.  
The term "different damages" as used in these Provisions includes damages of different types to marine natural resources and the ecological environment, and damages of the same type but clearly distinguishable as falling within the claim scope of different authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在人民法院依照[本规定第四条](#art-4)的规定发布公告之日起三十日内，或者书面告知之日起七日内，对同一损害有权提起诉讼的其他机关申请参加诉讼，经审查符合法定条件的，人民法院应当将其列为共同原告；逾期申请的，人民法院不予准许。裁判生效后另行起诉的，人民法院参照[《最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-28)的规定处理。  
对于不同损害，可以由各依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关分别提起诉讼；索赔人共同起诉或者在规定期限内申请参加诉讼的，人民法院依照[民事诉讼法第五十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-52)第一款的规定决定是否按共同诉讼进行审理。

**Article 5** Where, within thirty days from the date of the public announcement made by the People's Court in accordance with Article 4 of these Provisions, or within seven days from the date of written notice, any other authority that is entitled to institute an action for the same damage applies to participate in the proceedings, and upon examination meets the statutory conditions, the People's Court shall list it as a co-plaintiff; if the application is made after the time limit, the People's Court shall not permit it. Where a separate action is instituted after the judgment or ruling takes effect, the People's Court shall handle it mutatis mutandis in accordance with Article 28 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Civil Public Interest Litigation Cases.  
For different damages, each authority that exercises the power of marine environmental supervision and administration according to law may institute a separate action; if the claimants jointly institute an action or apply to participate in the proceedings within the prescribed time limit, the People's Court shall decide whether to try the case as a joint action in accordance with Article 52(1) of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关请求造成海洋自然资源与生态环境损害的责任者承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、恢复原状、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失等民事责任的，人民法院应当根据诉讼请求以及具体案情，合理判定责任者承担民事责任。

**Article 6** Where an authority exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration according to law requests the person liable for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement, removal of obstruction, elimination of danger, restoration to the original condition, making an apology, or compensating for losses, the People's Court shall, based on the claims and the specific circumstances of the case, reasonably determine that the liable person shall bear civil liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　海洋自然资源与生态环境损失赔偿范围包括：  
（一）预防措施费用，即为减轻或者防止海洋环境污染、生态恶化、自然资源减少所采取合理应急处置措施而发生的费用；  
（二）恢复费用，即采取或者将要采取措施恢复或者部分恢复受损害海洋自然资源与生态环境功能所需费用；  
（三）恢复期间损失，即受损害的海洋自然资源与生态环境功能部分或者完全恢复前的海洋自然资源损失、生态环境服务功能损失；  
（四）调查评估费用，即调查、勘查、监测污染区域和评估污染等损害风险与实际损害所发生的费用。

**Article 7** The scope of compensation for loss of marine natural resources and the ecological environment includes:  
(1) expenses for preventive measures, i.e., expenses incurred for reasonable emergency response measures taken to mitigate or prevent marine environmental pollution, ecological deterioration, or reduction of natural resources;  
(2) expenses for restoration, i.e., expenses necessary for taking or to take measures to restore or partially restore the functions of the damaged marine natural resources and the ecological environment;  
(3) losses during the restoration period, i.e., losses of marine natural resources and losses of ecological environment service functions before the partial or complete restoration of the functions of the damaged marine natural resources and the ecological environment;  
(4) expenses for investigation and assessment, i.e., expenses incurred for investigating, surveying, monitoring the polluted area, and assessing the risk of damage such as pollution and the actual damage.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　恢复费用，限于现实修复实际发生和未来修复必然发生的合理费用，包括制定和实施修复方案和监测、监管产生的费用。  
未来修复必然发生的合理费用和恢复期间损失，可以根据有资格的鉴定评估机构依据法律法规、国家主管部门颁布的鉴定评估技术规范作出的鉴定意见予以确定，但当事人有相反证据足以反驳的除外。  
预防措施费用和调查评估费用，以实际发生和未来必然发生的合理费用计算。  
责任者已经采取合理预防、恢复措施，其主张相应减少损失赔偿数额的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 8** The expenses for restoration shall be limited to the reasonable expenses actually incurred for current restoration and those inevitably incurred for future restoration, including expenses incurred for formulating and implementing restoration plans and for monitoring and supervision.  
The reasonable expenses inevitably incurred for future restoration and the losses during the restoration period may be determined based on the expert opinion issued by a qualified appraisal and evaluation institution in accordance with laws, regulations, and the technical specifications for appraisal and evaluation promulgated by the competent state authorities, unless the parties submit sufficient contrary evidence to rebut it.  
The expenses for preventive measures and expenses for investigation and assessment shall be calculated on the basis of reasonably incurred actual expenses and reasonably incurred inevitable future expenses.  
Where the liable person has already taken reasonable preventive or restoration measures, the People's Court shall support his claim for a corresponding reduction in the amount of compensation for losses.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　依照[本规定第八条](#art-8)的规定难以确定恢复费用和恢复期间损失的，人民法院可以根据责任者因损害行为所获得的收益或者所减少支付的污染防治费用，合理确定损失赔偿数额。  
前款规定的收益或者费用无法认定的，可以参照政府部门相关统计资料或者其他证据所证明的同区域同类生产经营者同期平均收入、同期平均污染防治费用，合理酌定。

**Article 9** Where it is difficult to determine the expenses for restoration and losses during the restoration period in accordance with Article 8 of these Provisions, the People's Court may reasonably determine the amount of compensation for losses based on the benefits obtained by the liable person from the damaging act or the pollution prevention and control expenses saved thereby.  
If the benefits or expenses specified in the preceding paragraph cannot be ascertained, the People's Court may reasonably determine the amount by reference to the average income of similar producers and operators in the same region during the same period, or the average pollution prevention and control expenses during the same period, as evidenced by relevant statistical data of government departments or other evidence.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院判决责任者赔偿海洋自然资源与生态环境损失的，可以一并写明依法行使海洋环境监督管理权的机关受领赔款后向国库账户交纳。  
发生法律效力的裁判需要采取强制执行措施的，应当移送执行。

**Article 10** Where a People's Court enters a judgment ordering the liable person to compensate for losses of marine natural resources and the ecological environment, it may also specify that the authority exercising the power of marine environmental supervision and administration according to law shall, after receiving the compensation, pay it into the state treasury account.  
Where a legally effective judgment or ruling requires compulsory enforcement measures, it shall be referred for enforcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿诉讼当事人达成调解协议或者自行达成和解协议的，人民法院依照[《最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-25)的规定处理。

**Article 11** Where the parties to an action for compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment reach a mediation agreement or conclude a settlement agreement on their own, the People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with Article 25 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Civil Public Interest Litigation Cases.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院审理海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿纠纷案件，本规定没有规定的，适用《最高人民法院关于审理环境侵权责任纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释》《最高人民法院关于审理环境民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》等相关司法解释的规定。  
在海上或者沿海陆域内从事活动，对中华人民共和国管辖海域内海洋自然资源与生态环境形成损害威胁，人民法院审理由此引起的赔偿纠纷案件，参照适用本规定。  
人民法院审理因船舶引起的海洋自然资源与生态环境损害赔偿纠纷案件，法律、行政法规、司法解释另有特别规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 12** Where a People's Court tries a dispute case concerning compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment, matters not provided for in these Provisions shall be governed by the provisions of relevant judicial interpretations, including the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Tort Liability Dispute Cases and the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Environmental Civil Public Interest Litigation Cases.  
Where an activity conducted at sea or within coastal land areas creates a threat of damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, and a People's Court tries a compensation dispute case arising therefrom, these Provisions shall be applied by reference.  
Where a People's Court tries a dispute case concerning compensation for damage to marine natural resources and the ecological environment caused by a ship, if laws, administrative regulations, or judicial interpretations provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定自2018年1月15日起施行，人民法院尚未审结的一审、二审案件适用本规定；本规定施行前已经作出生效裁判的案件，本规定施行后依法再审的，不适用本规定。  
本规定施行后，最高人民法院以前颁布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 15, 2018. These Provisions shall apply to cases of first instance and second instance that have not yet been concluded by the People's Court as of the effective date; for cases in which a legally effective judgment or ruling has been rendered before the effective date of these Provisions, but a retrial is conducted according to law after the effective date of these Provisions, these Provisions shall not apply.  
After these Provisions come into force, if any judicial interpretation previously promulgated by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
