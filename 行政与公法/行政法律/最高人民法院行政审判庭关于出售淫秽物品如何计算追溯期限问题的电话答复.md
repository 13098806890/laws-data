# 最高人民法院行政审判庭关于出售淫秽物品如何计算追溯期限问题的电话答复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发文字号**：川法研〔1991〕30号  
**公布日期**：1991-08-21  
**生效日期**：1991-08-21

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、行为人“将淫秽物品出售他人后”，应当视为其违法行为已经终了。“致使淫秽物品接连不断地在社会上转卖、复制、传播”，只能作为其违法行为的情节（即所造成的后果）来考虑，而不能视为连续或继续状态。

**Article 1** I. After a perpetrator "sells obscene articles to others", it shall be deemed that the illegal act has been completed. "Causing obscene articles to be continuously resold, reproduced, and disseminated in society" may only be considered as a circumstance (i.e., the consequences) of the illegal act, and shall not be deemed a continuous or ongoing state.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、根据《治安管理处罚条例》第十八条之规定，违反治安管理处罚条例的行为，只要超过法定追溯期限，即不能追究行为人的法律责任。  
此复。  
附：  
四川省高级人民法院  
关于出售淫秽物品如何计算追溯期限  
问题的请示  
1991年6月25日      川法研〔1991〕30号  
最高人民法院：  
行为人违反治安管理处罚条例，将淫秽物品出售他人后，致使淫秽物品接连不断地在社会上转卖、复制、传播，对行为人如何确定追溯期限的问题，我们与公安机关认识不一致，现请示如下：  
公安机关认为，行为人将淫秽物品出售后，淫秽物品继续在社会上转卖、复制、传播，应视为行为人出售淫秽物品处于继续状态。公安机关查获时，无论是否超过六个月，都可对最初出售淫秽物品的行为人进行处罚。这样有利于同这种毒害社会的违法行为作斗争。  
我们认为，行为人将淫秽物品一经出售，违法行为即告终结，根据《治安管理处罚条例》第十八条第一款的规定，其追溯期限应从出售之日起计算，在六个月内公安机关没有发现的，不再处罚。所谓继续状态是指行为人直接实施的出卖淫秽物品的行为处于继续状态，而不是行为人出售造成的后果延续。  
如何计算为当，请批示。

**Article 2** II. According to the provisions of Article 18 of the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security, if the act violating the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security exceeds the statutory limitation period, the legal liability of the perpetrator shall not be investigated.  
This is a reply.  
Attachment:  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province  
Request for Instructions on How to Calculate the Limitation Period for Selling Obscene Articles  
June 25, 1991 Chuan Fa Yan [1991] No. 30  
Supreme People's Court:  
After a perpetrator sells obscene articles to others, causing the obscene articles to be continuously resold, reproduced, and disseminated in society, we have a disagreement with the public security organ on how to determine the limitation period for the perpetrator. We now request instructions as follows:  
The public security organ believes that after the perpetrator sells the obscene articles, the obscene articles continue to be resold, reproduced, and disseminated in society, which shall be deemed that the act of selling obscene articles by the perpetrator is in a continuing state. When the public security organ seizes the case, regardless of whether more than six months have passed, it may punish the perpetrator who initially sold the obscene articles. This is conducive to combating such illegal acts that poison society.  
We believe that once the perpetrator sells the obscene articles, the illegal act is concluded. According to Article 18, paragraph 1 of the Regulations on Administrative Penalties for Public Security, the limitation period shall be calculated from the date of sale. If the public security organ does not discover it within six months, no punishment shall be imposed. The so-called continuing state refers to the continuing state of the act of selling obscene articles directly committed by the perpetrator, not the continuation of the consequences caused by the sale.  
How to calculate appropriately, please instruct.
