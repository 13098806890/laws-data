### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范行政处罚的设定和实施，保障和监督行政机关有效实施行政管理，维护公共利益和社会秩序，保护公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purpose of regulating the establishment and imposition of administrative penalties, ensuring and supervising the effective implementation of administrative management by administrative organs, safeguarding public interests and social order, and protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons or other organizations, in accordance with the Constitution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　行政处罚是指行政机关依法对违反行政管理秩序的公民、法人或者其他组织，以减损权益或者增加义务的方式予以惩戒的行为。

**Article 2** An administrative penalty refers to an act of an administrative organ imposing sanctions on citizens, legal persons or other organizations that violate the administrative management order, by means of impairing rights and interests or increasing obligations, in accordance with law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　行政处罚的设定和实施，适用本法。

**Article 3** The establishment and imposition of administrative penalties shall be governed by this Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公民、法人或者其他组织违反行政管理秩序的行为，应当给予行政处罚的，依照本法由法律、法规、规章规定，并由行政机关依照本法规定的程序实施。

**Article 4** Acts of citizens, legal persons or other organizations that violate the administrative management order and for which administrative penalties shall be imposed shall be prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, and rules in accordance with this Law, and shall be imposed by administrative organs in accordance with the procedures provided for in this Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行政处罚遵循公正、公开的原则。  
设定和实施行政处罚必须以事实为依据，与违法行为的事实、性质、情节以及社会危害程度相当。  
对违法行为给予行政处罚的规定必须公布；未经公布的，不得作为行政处罚的依据。

**Article 5** The principles of fairness and openness shall be followed in administrative penalties.  
The establishment and imposition of administrative penalties must be based on facts and shall be commensurate with the facts, nature, circumstances, and degree of social harm of the illegal act.  
The provisions for imposing administrative penalties on illegal acts must be made public; those not made public shall not be used as the basis for administrative penalties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　实施行政处罚，纠正违法行为，应当坚持处罚与教育相结合，教育公民、法人或者其他组织自觉守法。

**Article 6** In imposing administrative penalties and correcting illegal acts, the combination of penalty and education shall be adhered to, so as to educate citizens, legal persons or other organizations to abide by the law consciously.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关所给予的行政处罚，享有陈述权、申辩权；对行政处罚不服的，有权依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。  
公民、法人或者其他组织因行政机关违法给予行政处罚受到损害的，有权依法提出赔偿要求。

**Article 7** Citizens, legal persons or other organizations shall have the right to make statements and to present their own defense regarding the administrative penalties imposed by administrative organs; if dissatisfied with the administrative penalty, they shall have the right to apply for administrative reconsideration or bring an administrative action in accordance with law.  
Citizens, legal persons or other organizations that have suffered damage due to an illegal administrative penalty imposed by an administrative organ shall have the right to claim compensation in accordance with law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　公民、法人或者其他组织因违法行为受到行政处罚，其违法行为对他人造成损害的，应当依法承担民事责任。  
违法行为构成犯罪，应当依法追究刑事责任的，不得以行政处罚代替刑事处罚。

**Article 8** If a citizen, legal person or other organization has been subjected to an administrative penalty for an illegal act and such illegal act has caused damage to another person, the citizen, legal person or other organization shall bear civil liability in accordance with law.  
If an illegal act constitutes a crime and criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law, an administrative penalty shall not be imposed in lieu of criminal punishment.

### 第二章　行政处罚的种类和设定

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行政处罚的种类：  
（一）警告、通报批评；  
（二）罚款、没收违法所得、没收非法财物；  
（三）暂扣许可证件、降低资质等级、吊销许可证件；  
（四）限制开展生产经营活动、责令停产停业、责令关闭、限制从业；  
（五）行政拘留；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定的其他行政处罚。

**Article 9** Types of administrative penalties:  
(1) Warning and circulation of a notice of criticism;  
(2) Fine, confiscation of illegal gains, and confiscation of illegal property;  
(3) Temporary suspension of permit, lowering of qualification level, and revocation of permit;  
(4) Restriction on production and business operations, order to suspend production or business, order to close, and restriction on engagement in certain trades;  
(5) Administrative detention;  
(6) Other administrative penalties provided for by laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　法律可以设定各种行政处罚。  
限制人身自由的行政处罚，只能由法律设定。

**Article 10** Laws may establish all types of administrative penalties.  
Administrative penalties that restrict personal freedom may only be established by laws.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行政法规可以设定除限制人身自由以外的行政处罚。  
法律对违法行为已经作出行政处罚规定，行政法规需要作出具体规定的，必须在法律规定的给予行政处罚的行为、种类和幅度的范围内规定。  
法律对违法行为未作出行政处罚规定，行政法规为实施法律，可以补充设定行政处罚。拟补充设定行政处罚的，应当通过听证会、论证会等形式广泛听取意见，并向制定机关作出书面说明。行政法规报送备案时，应当说明补充设定行政处罚的情况。

**Article 11** Administrative regulations may establish administrative penalties other than those restricting personal freedom.  
Where laws have already provided for administrative penalties for illegal acts, and administrative regulations need to make specific provisions, such provisions must be made within the scope of the acts, types and ranges of administrative penalties prescribed by the laws.  
Where laws have not provided for administrative penalties for illegal acts, administrative regulations may, for the purpose of implementing the laws, supplement the establishment of administrative penalties. Where it is proposed to supplement the establishment of administrative penalties, opinions shall be widely solicited through hearings, argumentation sessions and other forms, and a written explanation shall be made to the enacting authority. When submitting administrative regulations for filing, the circumstances of the supplementary establishment of administrative penalties shall be explained.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　地方性法规可以设定除限制人身自由、吊销营业执照以外的行政处罚。  
法律、行政法规对违法行为已经作出行政处罚规定，地方性法规需要作出具体规定的，必须在法律、行政法规规定的给予行政处罚的行为、种类和幅度的范围内规定。  
法律、行政法规对违法行为未作出行政处罚规定，地方性法规为实施法律、行政法规，可以补充设定行政处罚。拟补充设定行政处罚的，应当通过听证会、论证会等形式广泛听取意见，并向制定机关作出书面说明。地方性法规报送备案时，应当说明补充设定行政处罚的情况。

**Article 12** Local regulations may establish administrative penalties other than those restricting personal freedom and revoking business licenses.  
Where laws or administrative regulations have already provided for administrative penalties for illegal acts, and local regulations need to make specific provisions, such provisions must be made within the scope of the acts, types and ranges of administrative penalties prescribed by the laws or administrative regulations.  
Where laws or administrative regulations have not provided for administrative penalties for illegal acts, local regulations may, for the purpose of implementing the laws or administrative regulations, supplement the establishment of administrative penalties. Where it is proposed to supplement the establishment of administrative penalties, opinions shall be widely solicited through hearings, argumentation sessions and other forms, and a written explanation shall be made to the enacting authority. When submitting local regulations for filing, the circumstances of the supplementary establishment of administrative penalties shall be explained.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国务院部门规章可以在法律、行政法规规定的给予行政处罚的行为、种类和幅度的范围内作出具体规定。  
尚未制定法律、行政法规的，国务院部门规章对违反行政管理秩序的行为，可以设定警告、通报批评或者一定数额罚款的行政处罚。罚款的限额由国务院规定。

**Article 13** Rules of the departments of the State Council may make specific provisions within the scope of the acts, types and ranges of administrative penalties prescribed by laws and administrative regulations.  
Where no laws or administrative regulations have been enacted, rules of the departments of the State Council may, for acts that violate the administrative management order, establish administrative penalties such as warnings, circulation of a notice of criticism, or a fine of a certain amount. The limit of the fine shall be prescribed by the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　地方政府规章可以在法律、法规规定的给予行政处罚的行为、种类和幅度的范围内作出具体规定。  
尚未制定法律、法规的，地方政府规章对违反行政管理秩序的行为，可以设定警告、通报批评或者一定数额罚款的行政处罚。罚款的限额由省、自治区、直辖市人民代表大会常务委员会规定。

**Article 14** Local government rules may make specific provisions within the scope of the acts, types and ranges of administrative penalties prescribed by laws and regulations.  
Where no laws or regulations have been enacted, local government rules may, for acts that violate the administrative management order, establish administrative penalties such as warnings, circulation of a notice of criticism, or a fine of a certain amount. The limit of the fine shall be prescribed by the standing committee of the people's congress of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国务院部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府及其有关部门应当定期组织评估行政处罚的实施情况和必要性，对不适当的行政处罚事项及种类、罚款数额等，应当提出修改或者废止的建议。

**Article 15** The departments of the State Council and the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government and their relevant departments shall organize regular assessments of the implementation and necessity of administrative penalties, and shall propose revisions or abolitions for inappropriate administrative penalty matters, types, fine amounts, and the like.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　除法律、法规、规章外，其他规范性文件不得设定行政处罚。

**Article 16** No other regulatory documents other than laws, regulations, and rules may establish administrative penalties.

### 第三章　行政处罚的实施机关

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　行政处罚由具有行政处罚权的行政机关在法定职权范围内实施。

**Article 17** Administrative penalties shall be imposed by administrative organs with the power to impose administrative penalties within their statutory functions and powers.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家在城市管理、市场监管、生态环境、文化市场、交通运输、应急管理、农业等领域推行建立综合行政执法制度，相对集中行政处罚权。  
国务院或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府可以决定一个行政机关行使有关行政机关的行政处罚权。  
限制人身自由的行政处罚权只能由公安机关和法律规定的其他机关行使。

**Article 18** The State promotes the establishment of a comprehensive administrative enforcement system in the fields of urban management, market regulation, ecological environment, cultural market, transportation, emergency management, agriculture, and others, to relatively centralize the power of administrative penalties.  
The State Council or the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may decide that one administrative organ shall exercise the power of administrative penalties of relevant administrative organs.  
The power to impose administrative penalties that restrict personal freedom may only be exercised by the public security organs and other organs as prescribed by law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　法律、法规授权的具有管理公共事务职能的组织可以在法定授权范围内实施行政处罚。

**Article 19** Organizations authorized by laws or regulations to have public affairs management functions may impose administrative penalties within the scope of the statutory authorization.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　行政机关依照法律、法规、规章的规定，可以在其法定权限内书面委托符合[本法第二十一条](#art-21)规定条件的组织实施行政处罚。行政机关不得委托其他组织或者个人实施行政处罚。  
委托书应当载明委托的具体事项、权限、期限等内容。委托行政机关和受委托组织应当将委托书向社会公布。  
委托行政机关对受委托组织实施行政处罚的行为应当负责监督，并对该行为的后果承担法律责任。  
受委托组织在委托范围内，以委托行政机关名义实施行政处罚；不得再委托其他组织或者个人实施行政处罚。

**Article 20** Administrative organs may, in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules, in writing, entrust an organization meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 21 of this Law to impose administrative penalties within their statutory powers. Administrative organs shall not entrust any other organization or individual to impose administrative penalties.  
The letter of entrustment shall specify the specific matters, scope, term, and other content of the entrustment. The entrusting administrative organ and the entrusted organization shall make the letter of entrustment public.  
The entrusting administrative organ shall be responsible for supervising the imposition of administrative penalties by the entrusted organization and shall bear legal liability for the consequences of such imposition.  
The entrusted organization shall impose administrative penalties in the name of the entrusting administrative organ within the scope of entrustment; it shall not re-entrust any other organization or individual to impose administrative penalties.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　受委托组织必须符合以下条件：  
（一）依法成立并具有管理公共事务职能；  
（二）有熟悉有关法律、法规、规章和业务并取得行政执法资格的工作人员；  
（三）需要进行技术检查或者技术鉴定的，应当有条件组织进行相应的技术检查或者技术鉴定。

**Article 21** An entrusted organization shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) It is established according to law and has the function of managing public affairs;  
(2) It has personnel who are familiar with relevant laws, regulations, rules and business and have obtained the qualifications for administrative law enforcement;  
(3) If technical inspection or technical appraisal is required, it shall be capable of organizing the corresponding technical inspection or technical appraisal.

### 第四章　行政处罚的管辖和适用

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　行政处罚由违法行为发生地的行政机关管辖。法律、行政法规、部门规章另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 22** An administrative penalty shall be under the jurisdiction of the administrative organ of the place where the illegal act occurs. Where otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or departmental rules, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　行政处罚由县级以上地方人民政府具有行政处罚权的行政机关管辖。法律、行政法规另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 23** An administrative penalty shall be under the jurisdiction of the administrative organ of a local people's government at or above the county level that has the power to impose administrative penalties. Where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　省、自治区、直辖市根据当地实际情况，可以决定将基层管理迫切需要的县级人民政府部门的行政处罚权交由能够有效承接的乡镇人民政府、街道办事处行使，并定期组织评估。决定应当公布。  
承接行政处罚权的乡镇人民政府、街道办事处应当加强执法能力建设，按照规定范围、依照法定程序实施行政处罚。  
有关地方人民政府及其部门应当加强组织协调、业务指导、执法监督，建立健全行政处罚协调配合机制，完善评议、考核制度。

**Article 24** A province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on local actual conditions, decide to transfer the power to impose administrative penalties of a department of a people's government at the county level, which is urgently needed for grassroots management, to a township people's government or a sub-district office that is capable of effectively undertaking such power, and shall organize regular evaluations. The decision shall be made public.  
The township people's government or sub-district office that undertakes the power to impose administrative penalties shall strengthen its law enforcement capability, and impose administrative penalties within the prescribed scope and in accordance with statutory procedures.  
The relevant local people's government and its departments shall strengthen organizational coordination, business guidance, and law enforcement supervision, establish and improve a mechanism for coordination and cooperation in administrative penalties, and improve the evaluation and assessment systems.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　两个以上行政机关都有管辖权的，由最先立案的行政机关管辖。  
对管辖发生争议的，应当协商解决，协商不成的，报请共同的上一级行政机关指定管辖；也可以直接由共同的上一级行政机关指定管辖。

**Article 25** Where two or more administrative organs all have jurisdiction, the administrative organ that first files the case shall have jurisdiction.  
In the event of a dispute over jurisdiction, the organs shall resolve it through consultation; if consultation fails, the matter shall be submitted to the common superior administrative organ for designation of jurisdiction; or jurisdiction may be directly designated by the common superior administrative organ.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　行政机关因实施行政处罚的需要，可以向有关机关提出协助请求。协助事项属于被请求机关职权范围内的，应当依法予以协助。

**Article 26** Where an administrative organ needs assistance for imposing an administrative penalty, it may submit a request for assistance to the relevant organ. If the matter for which assistance is requested falls within the functions and powers of the requested organ, the requested organ shall provide assistance according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，行政机关应当及时将案件移送司法机关，依法追究刑事责任。对依法不需要追究刑事责任或者免予刑事处罚，但应当给予行政处罚的，司法机关应当及时将案件移送有关行政机关。  
行政处罚实施机关与司法机关之间应当加强协调配合，建立健全案件移送制度，加强证据材料移交、接收衔接，完善案件处理信息通报机制。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/税务财政/价格违法行为行政处罚规定.md#art-17" title="被《价格违法行为行政处罚规定》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 27** Where an illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, the administrative organ shall promptly transfer the case to the judicial organ, and investigate criminal liability according to law. Where criminal liability is not required to be investigated according to law or exemption from criminal punishment is granted, but an administrative penalty shall be imposed, the judicial organ shall promptly transfer the case to the relevant administrative organ.  
The organ imposing the administrative penalty and the judicial organ shall strengthen coordination and cooperation, establish and improve the case transfer system, strengthen the handover and acceptance of evidence materials, and improve the mechanism for notifying case handling information.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　行政机关实施行政处罚时，应当责令当事人改正或者限期改正违法行为。  
当事人有违法所得，除依法应当退赔的外，应当予以没收。违法所得是指实施违法行为所取得的款项。法律、行政法规、部门规章对违法所得的计算另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 28** When imposing an administrative penalty, the administrative organ shall order the party to correct or correct the illegal act within a specified time limit.  
Where the party has illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, except those that shall be refunded or compensated according to law. Illegal gains refer to the sums obtained from committing the illegal act. Where otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or departmental rules on the calculation of illegal gains, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对当事人的同一个违法行为，不得给予两次以上罚款的行政处罚。同一个违法行为违反多个法律规范应当给予罚款处罚的，按照罚款数额高的规定处罚。

**Article 29** For the same illegal act of a party, an administrative penalty of a fine shall not be imposed more than once. Where the same illegal act violates multiple legal norms and a fine shall be imposed, the penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the provision with the higher amount of fine.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　不满十四周岁的未成年人有违法行为的，不予行政处罚，责令监护人加以管教；已满十四周岁不满十八周岁的未成年人有违法行为的，应当从轻或者减轻行政处罚。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../经济、税务与金融/行政法规/海关进出口/中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例.md#art-62" title="被《中华人民共和国海关行政处罚实施条例》第62条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 30** Where a minor under the age of 14 commits an illegal act, no administrative penalty shall be imposed, and the guardian shall be ordered to discipline the minor; where a minor who has reached the age of 14 but not the age of 18 commits an illegal act, the administrative penalty shall be mitigated or reduced.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　精神病人、智力残疾人在不能辨认或者不能控制自己行为时有违法行为的，不予行政处罚，但应当责令其监护人严加看管和治疗。间歇性精神病人在精神正常时有违法行为的，应当给予行政处罚。尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行为能力的精神病人、智力残疾人有违法行为的，可以从轻或者减轻行政处罚。

**Article 31** Where a mentally ill person or a person with intellectual disability commits an illegal act when he or she is unable to recognize or control his or her own conduct, no administrative penalty shall be imposed, but his or her guardian shall be ordered to keep close custody and provide treatment. Where a person with intermittent mental illness commits an illegal act when he or she is in a normal mental state, an administrative penalty shall be imposed. Where a mentally ill person or a person with intellectual disability who has not completely lost the ability to recognize or control his or her own conduct commits an illegal act, the administrative penalty may be mitigated or reduced.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人有下列情形之一，应当从轻或者减轻行政处罚：  
（一）主动消除或者减轻违法行为危害后果的；  
（二）受他人胁迫或者诱骗实施违法行为的；  
（三）主动供述行政机关尚未掌握的违法行为的；  
（四）配合行政机关查处违法行为有立功表现的；  
（五）法律、法规、规章规定其他应当从轻或者减轻行政处罚的。

**Article 32** A party under any of the following circumstances shall be given a mitigated or reduced administrative penalty:  
(1) The party proactively eliminates or mitigates the harmful consequences of the illegal act;  
(2) The party commits the illegal act under duress or deception by another person;  
(3) The party voluntarily confesses an illegal act that has not yet been discovered by the administrative organ;  
(4) The party has rendered meritorious service in cooperating with the administrative organ in investigating the illegal act;  
(5) Other circumstances where laws, regulations, or rules provide that the administrative penalty shall be mitigated or reduced.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　违法行为轻微并及时改正，没有造成危害后果的，不予行政处罚。初次违法且危害后果轻微并及时改正的，可以不予行政处罚。  
当事人有证据足以证明没有主观过错的，不予行政处罚。法律、行政法规另有规定的，从其规定。  
对当事人的违法行为依法不予行政处罚的，行政机关应当对当事人进行教育。

**Article 33** Where an illegal act is minor and is promptly corrected, and no harmful consequences have been caused, no administrative penalty shall be imposed. Where the illegal act is a first-time offense, the harmful consequences are minor, and it is promptly corrected, no administrative penalty may be imposed.  
Where the party has sufficient evidence to prove that it has no subjective fault, no administrative penalty shall be imposed. Where otherwise provided by laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.  
Where no administrative penalty is imposed on the party's illegal act according to law, the administrative organ shall educate the party.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　行政机关可以依法制定行政处罚裁量基准，规范行使行政处罚裁量权。行政处罚裁量基准应当向社会公布。

**Article 34** An administrative organ may, according to law, formulate a benchmark for the discretion of administrative penalties to regulate the exercise of the discretionary power of administrative penalties. The benchmark for the discretion of administrative penalties shall be made public to the society.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　违法行为构成犯罪，人民法院判处拘役或者有期徒刑时，行政机关已经给予当事人行政拘留的，应当依法折抵相应刑期。  
违法行为构成犯罪，人民法院判处罚金时，行政机关已经给予当事人罚款的，应当折抵相应罚金；行政机关尚未给予当事人罚款的，不再给予罚款。

**Article 35** Where an illegal act constitutes a crime, and the people's court imposes criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment, if the administrative organ has already imposed administrative detention on the party, the corresponding term of punishment shall be commuted according to law.  
Where an illegal act constitutes a crime, and the people's court imposes a fine, if the administrative organ has already imposed a fine on the party, the corresponding fine shall be offset; if the administrative organ has not yet imposed a fine on the party, no fine shall be imposed.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　违法行为在二年内未被发现的，不再给予行政处罚；涉及公民生命健康安全、金融安全且有危害后果的，上述期限延长至五年。法律另有规定的除外。  
前款规定的期限，从违法行为发生之日起计算；违法行为有连续或者继续状态的，从行为终了之日起计算。

**Article 36** Where an illegal act has not been discovered within two years, no administrative penalty shall be imposed; if the illegal act involves the safety of citizens' life and health or financial security and has harmful consequences, the said period shall be extended to five years, except as otherwise provided by law.  
The period prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be counted from the date when the illegal act is committed; if the illegal act is in a continuous or continuing state, it shall be counted from the date when the act ends.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　实施行政处罚，适用违法行为发生时的法律、法规、规章的规定。但是，作出行政处罚决定时，法律、法规、规章已被修改或者废止，且新的规定处罚较轻或者不认为是违法的，适用新的规定。

**Article 37** The imposition of an administrative penalty shall apply the provisions of the laws, regulations, and rules in force at the time when the illegal act was committed. However, where at the time the decision on the administrative penalty is made, the laws, regulations, or rules have been amended or abolished, and the new provisions impose a lighter penalty or do not consider the act illegal, the new provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　行政处罚没有依据或者实施主体不具有行政主体资格的，行政处罚无效。  
违反法定程序构成重大且明显违法的，行政处罚无效。

**Article 38** Where an administrative penalty has no legal basis or the implementing entity does not have the status of an administrative subject, the administrative penalty shall be null and void.  
Where a violation of statutory procedures constitutes a major and obvious illegality, the administrative penalty shall be null and void.

### 第五章　行政处罚的决定

#### 第一节　一般规定

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　行政处罚的实施机关、立案依据、实施程序和救济渠道等信息应当公示。

**Article 39** The information on the enforcement organ, the basis for case filing, the enforcement procedures, and the channels for relief of administrative penalties shall be made public.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　公民、法人或者其他组织违反行政管理秩序的行为，依法应当给予行政处罚的，行政机关必须查明事实；违法事实不清、证据不足的，不得给予行政处罚。

**Article 40** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization violates the administrative management order and shall be subject to an administrative penalty according to law, the administrative organ must ascertain the facts; if the illegal facts are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, no administrative penalty may be imposed.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　行政机关依照法律、行政法规规定利用电子技术监控设备收集、固定违法事实的，应当经过法制和技术审核，确保电子技术监控设备符合标准、设置合理、标志明显，设置地点应当向社会公布。  
电子技术监控设备记录违法事实应当真实、清晰、完整、准确。行政机关应当审核记录内容是否符合要求；未经审核或者经审核不符合要求的，不得作为行政处罚的证据。  
行政机关应当及时告知当事人违法事实，并采取信息化手段或者其他措施，为当事人查询、陈述和申辩提供便利。不得限制或者变相限制当事人享有的陈述权、申辩权。

**Article 41** Where an administrative organ uses electronic technology monitoring equipment to collect and fix illegal facts in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, it shall undergo legal and technical review to ensure that the electronic technology monitoring equipment meets standards, is reasonably installed, and has clear signage, and the installation location shall be made public to society.  
The illegal facts recorded by electronic technology monitoring equipment shall be authentic, clear, complete, and accurate. The administrative organ shall review whether the recorded content meets the requirements; content that has not been reviewed or fails to meet the requirements upon review shall not be used as evidence for an administrative penalty.  
The administrative organ shall promptly inform the party of the illegal facts and adopt information-based means or other measures to facilitate the party's inquiry, statement, and defense. The party's right to statement and defense may not be restricted or restricted in a disguised form.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　行政处罚应当由具有行政执法资格的执法人员实施。执法人员不得少于两人，法律另有规定的除外。  
执法人员应当文明执法，尊重和保护当事人合法权益。

**Article 42** Administrative penalties shall be enforced by law enforcement personnel who have the qualifications for administrative law enforcement. The number of law enforcement personnel shall not be less than two, unless otherwise provided by law.  
Law enforcement personnel shall enforce the law in a civilized manner and respect and protect the lawful rights and interests of the parties.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　执法人员与案件有直接利害关系或者有其他关系可能影响公正执法的，应当回避。  
当事人认为执法人员与案件有直接利害关系或者有其他关系可能影响公正执法的，有权申请回避。  
当事人提出回避申请的，行政机关应当依法审查，由行政机关负责人决定。决定作出之前，不停止调查。

**Article 43** If a law enforcement officer has a direct interest in the case or has any other relationship that may affect impartial law enforcement, the officer shall withdraw.  
If the party believes that a law enforcement officer has a direct interest in the case or has any other relationship that may affect impartial law enforcement, the party has the right to apply for the officer's withdrawal.  
Where a party applies for withdrawal, the administrative organ shall review the matter according to law, and the decision shall be made by the person in charge of the administrative organ. The investigation shall not be suspended before the decision is made.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　行政机关在作出行政处罚决定之前，应当告知当事人拟作出的行政处罚内容及事实、理由、依据，并告知当事人依法享有的陈述、申辩、要求听证等权利。

**Article 44** Before making a decision on an administrative penalty, the administrative organ shall notify the party of the content of the proposed administrative penalty and the facts, reasons, and basis therefor, and shall inform the party of the rights to make statements, present defenses, request a hearing, etc., that the party enjoys according to law.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　当事人有权进行陈述和申辩。行政机关必须充分听取当事人的意见，对当事人提出的事实、理由和证据，应当进行复核；当事人提出的事实、理由或者证据成立的，行政机关应当采纳。  
行政机关不得因当事人陈述、申辩而给予更重的处罚。

**Article 45** The party has the right to make statements and present defenses. The administrative organ must fully hear the party's opinions and shall review the facts, reasons, and evidence presented by the party; if the facts, reasons, or evidence presented by the party are established, the administrative organ shall adopt them.  
The administrative organ may not impose a heavier penalty because the party has made statements or presented defenses.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　证据包括：  
（一）书证；  
（二）物证；  
（三）视听资料；  
（四）电子数据；  
（五）证人证言；  
（六）当事人的陈述；  
（七）鉴定意见；  
（八）勘验笔录、现场笔录。  
证据必须经查证属实，方可作为认定案件事实的根据。  
以非法手段取得的证据，不得作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 46** Evidence includes:  
(1) documentary evidence;  
(2) physical evidence;  
(3) audio-visual materials;  
(4) electronic data;  
(5) testimony of witnesses;  
(6) statements of the parties;  
(7) expert evaluation opinions;  
(8) records of inspection and records of the scene.  
Evidence must be verified to be true before it may be used as the basis for determining the facts of a case.  
Evidence obtained by illegal means may not be used as the basis for determining the facts of a case.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　行政机关应当依法以文字、音像等形式，对行政处罚的启动、调查取证、审核、决定、送达、执行等进行全过程记录，归档保存。

**Article 47** The administrative organ shall, according to law, record the entire process of the initiation, investigation and evidence collection, review, decision, service, and enforcement of an administrative penalty in the form of text, audio and video, etc., and shall archive and preserve such records.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　具有一定社会影响的行政处罚决定应当依法公开。  
公开的行政处罚决定被依法变更、撤销、确认违法或者确认无效的，行政机关应当在三日内撤回行政处罚决定信息并公开说明理由。

**Article 48** Decisions on administrative penalties that have a certain social impact shall be made public according to law.  
If a publicized administrative penalty decision is changed, revoked, confirmed as illegal, or confirmed as invalid according to law, the administrative organ shall withdraw the information on the administrative penalty decision within three days and publicly explain the reasons.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　发生重大传染病疫情等突发事件，为了控制、减轻和消除突发事件引起的社会危害，行政机关对违反突发事件应对措施的行为，依法快速、从重处罚。

**Article 49** Where a major infectious disease epidemic or other emergency occurs, in order to control, mitigate, and eliminate the social harm caused by the emergency, the administrative organ shall impose a prompt and severe punishment according to law on acts that violate the emergency response measures.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　行政机关及其工作人员对实施行政处罚过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私，应当依法予以保密。

**Article 50** Administrative organs and their staff members shall keep confidential, according to law, any state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy that they become aware of during the process of imposing administrative penalties.

#### 第二节　简易程序

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　违法事实确凿并有法定依据，对公民处以二百元以下、对法人或者其他组织处以三千元以下罚款或者警告的行政处罚的，可以当场作出行政处罚决定。法律另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 51** Where the illegal facts are conclusive and there is a statutory basis, and the administrative penalty is a fine of not more than 200 yuan for a citizen or not more than 3,000 yuan for a legal person or any other organization, or a warning, the decision on the administrative penalty may be made on the spot. If other laws provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　执法人员当场作出行政处罚决定的，应当向当事人出示执法证件，填写预定格式、编有号码的行政处罚决定书，并当场交付当事人。当事人拒绝签收的，应当在行政处罚决定书上注明。  
前款规定的行政处罚决定书应当载明当事人的违法行为，行政处罚的种类和依据、罚款数额、时间、地点，申请行政复议、提起行政诉讼的途径和期限以及行政机关名称，并由执法人员签名或者盖章。  
执法人员当场作出的行政处罚决定，应当报所属行政机关备案。

**Article 52** Where a law enforcement officer makes a decision on an administrative penalty on the spot, the officer shall present the law enforcement credential to the party, fill out a pre-formatted and numbered written decision on administrative penalty, and deliver it to the party on the spot. If the party refuses to sign for receipt, this shall be noted on the written decision on administrative penalty.  
The written decision on administrative penalty specified in the preceding paragraph shall state the party's illegal act, the type and basis of the administrative penalty, the amount of the fine, the time and place, the channels and time limits for applying for administrative reconsideration or instituting an administrative action, and the name of the administrative organ, and shall be signed or sealed by the law enforcement officer.  
The decision on an administrative penalty made on the spot by a law enforcement officer shall be reported to the administrative organ to which the officer belongs for recordation.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对当场作出的行政处罚决定，当事人应当依照[本法第六十七条](#art-67)至第六十九条的规定履行。

**Article 53** For a decision on an administrative penalty made on the spot, the party shall perform it in accordance with the provisions of Articles 67 through 69 of this Law.

#### 第三节　普通程序

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　除[本法第五十一条](#art-51)规定的可以当场作出的行政处罚外，行政机关发现公民、法人或者其他组织有依法应当给予行政处罚的行为的，必须全面、客观、公正地调查，收集有关证据；必要时，依照法律、法规的规定，可以进行检查。  
符合立案标准的，行政机关应当及时立案。

**Article 54** Except for the administrative penalties that may be imposed on the spot as prescribed in Article 51 of this Law, where an administrative organ discovers that a citizen, legal person, or any other organization has committed an act for which an administrative penalty shall be imposed according to law, it must conduct a comprehensive, objective, and fair investigation and collect relevant evidence; when necessary, it may conduct an inspection in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
Where the criteria for case filing are met, the administrative organ shall promptly file the case.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　执法人员在调查或者进行检查时，应当主动向当事人或者有关人员出示执法证件。当事人或者有关人员有权要求执法人员出示执法证件。执法人员不出示执法证件的，当事人或者有关人员有权拒绝接受调查或者检查。  
当事人或者有关人员应当如实回答询问，并协助调查或者检查，不得拒绝或者阻挠。询问或者检查应当制作笔录。

**Article 55** When conducting an investigation or inspection, law enforcement officers shall proactively present their law enforcement credentials to the party or relevant persons. The party or relevant persons have the right to require the law enforcement officers to present their law enforcement credentials. If a law enforcement officer fails to present the law enforcement credential, the party or relevant persons have the right to refuse to accept the investigation or inspection.  
The party or relevant persons shall truthfully answer inquiries and assist in the investigation or inspection, and may not refuse or obstruct it. Records of inquiries or inspections shall be made.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　行政机关在收集证据时，可以采取抽样取证的方法；在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，经行政机关负责人批准，可以先行登记保存，并应当在七日内及时作出处理决定，在此期间，当事人或者有关人员不得销毁或者转移证据。

**Article 56** When collecting evidence, the administrative organ may adopt the method of sampling evidence; if evidence may be lost or become difficult to obtain later, with the approval of the person in charge of the administrative organ, it may first register and preserve the evidence, and shall promptly make a handling decision within seven days. During this period, the party or relevant persons may not destroy or transfer the evidence.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　调查终结，行政机关负责人应当对调查结果进行审查，根据不同情况，分别作出如下决定：  
（一）确有应受行政处罚的违法行为的，根据情节轻重及具体情况，作出行政处罚决定；  
（二）违法行为轻微，依法可以不予行政处罚的，不予行政处罚；  
（三）违法事实不能成立的，不予行政处罚；  
（四）违法行为涉嫌犯罪的，移送司法机关。  
对情节复杂或者重大违法行为给予行政处罚，行政机关负责人应当集体讨论决定。

**Article 57** After the investigation is concluded, the person in charge of the administrative organ shall review the investigation results and, based on the different circumstances, shall make the following decisions respectively:  
(1) If there is indeed an illegal act subject to an administrative penalty, a decision on an administrative penalty shall be made based on the seriousness of the circumstances and the specific situation;  
(2) If the illegal act is minor and no administrative penalty may be imposed according to law, no administrative penalty shall be imposed;  
(3) If the illegal facts cannot be established, no administrative penalty shall be imposed;  
(4) If the illegal act is suspected of constituting a crime, it shall be transferred to the judicial authority.  
For imposing an administrative penalty on a case with complex circumstances or a major illegal act, the person in charge of the administrative organ shall make the decision after collective discussion.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　有下列情形之一，在行政机关负责人作出行政处罚的决定之前，应当由从事行政处罚决定法制审核的人员进行法制审核；未经法制审核或者审核未通过的，不得作出决定：  
（一）涉及重大公共利益的；  
（二）直接关系当事人或者第三人重大权益，经过听证程序的；  
（三）案件情况疑难复杂、涉及多个法律关系的；  
（四）法律、法规规定应当进行法制审核的其他情形。  
行政机关中初次从事行政处罚决定法制审核的人员，应当通过国家统一法律职业资格考试取得法律职业资格。

**Article 58** Under any of the following circumstances, before the person in charge of the administrative organ makes a decision on an administrative penalty, the legal review of the administrative penalty decision shall be conducted by personnel engaged in such legal review; if no legal review has been conducted or the review has not been passed, no decision may be made:  
(1) The matter involves a major public interest;  
(2) The matter directly concerns the major rights and interests of the party or a third party and has gone through the hearing procedure;  
(3) The case is difficult and complex, involving multiple legal relationships; or  
(4) Other circumstances under which legal review shall be conducted as provided by laws or regulations.  
Personnel who initially engage in the legal review of administrative penalty decisions in an administrative organ shall pass the national unified legal professional qualification examination and obtain the legal professional qualification.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　行政机关依照[本法第五十七条](#art-57)的规定给予行政处罚，应当制作行政处罚决定书。行政处罚决定书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的姓名或者名称、地址；  
（二）违反法律、法规、规章的事实和证据；  
（三）行政处罚的种类和依据；  
（四）行政处罚的履行方式和期限；  
（五）申请行政复议、提起行政诉讼的途径和期限；  
（六）作出行政处罚决定的行政机关名称和作出决定的日期。  
行政处罚决定书必须盖有作出行政处罚决定的行政机关的印章。

**Article 59** Where an administrative organ imposes an administrative penalty in accordance with Article 57 of this Law, it shall produce a written decision on administrative penalty. The written decision on administrative penalty shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the name and address of the party;  
(2) the facts and evidence of the violation of laws, regulations, or rules;  
(3) the type and legal basis of the administrative penalty;  
(4) the method and time limit for performance of the administrative penalty;  
(5) the channels and time limits for applying for administrative reconsideration or initiating an administrative action;  
(6) the name of the administrative organ that made the decision on administrative penalty and the date of the decision.  
The written decision on administrative penalty must bear the seal of the administrative organ that made the decision on administrative penalty.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　行政机关应当自行政处罚案件立案之日起九十日内作出行政处罚决定。法律、法规、规章另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 60** An administrative organ shall make a decision on administrative penalty within ninety days from the date of filing the case of administrative penalty. Where otherwise provided by laws, regulations, or rules, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　行政处罚决定书应当在宣告后当场交付当事人；当事人不在场的，行政机关应当在七日内依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，将行政处罚决定书送达当事人。  
当事人同意并签订确认书的，行政机关可以采用传真、电子邮件等方式，将行政处罚决定书等送达当事人。

**Article 61** The written decision on administrative penalty shall be delivered to the party on the spot after being announced; if the party is not present, the administrative organ shall serve the written decision on administrative penalty on the party within seven days in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the party consents and signs a confirmation letter, the administrative organ may serve the written decision on administrative penalty and other documents on the party by means such as facsimile or electronic mail.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　行政机关及其执法人员在作出行政处罚决定之前，未依照[本法第四十四条](#art-44)、第四十五条的规定向当事人告知拟作出的行政处罚内容及事实、理由、依据，或者拒绝听取当事人的陈述、申辩，不得作出行政处罚决定；当事人明确放弃陈述或者申辩权利的除外。

**Article 62** If an administrative organ or its law enforcement officers, before making a decision on administrative penalty, fail to inform the party of the content, facts, reasons, and legal basis of the proposed administrative penalty in accordance with Articles 44 and 45 of this Law, or refuse to hear the party's statements or defenses, they shall not make a decision on administrative penalty; except where the party clearly waives the right to make statements or defenses.

#### 第四节　听证程序

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　行政机关拟作出下列行政处罚决定，应当告知当事人有要求听证的权利，当事人要求听证的，行政机关应当组织听证：  
（一）较大数额罚款；  
（二）没收较大数额违法所得、没收较大价值非法财物；  
（三）降低资质等级、吊销许可证件；  
（四）责令停产停业、责令关闭、限制从业；  
（五）其他较重的行政处罚；  
（六）法律、法规、规章规定的其他情形。  
当事人不承担行政机关组织听证的费用。

**Article 63** Where an administrative organ intends to make any of the following decisions on administrative penalty, it shall inform the party of the right to request a hearing; if the party requests a hearing, the administrative organ shall hold a hearing:  
(1) a relatively large amount of fine;  
(2) confiscation of a relatively large amount of illegal gains or confiscation of illegal property of relatively large value;  
(3) downgrading of qualification grade or revocation of a permit or license;  
(4) ordering the suspension of production or business, ordering closure, or restricting engagement in a certain profession;  
(5) other relatively severe administrative penalties;  
(6) other circumstances specified by laws, regulations, or rules.  
The party shall not bear the costs of the hearing organized by the administrative organ.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　听证应当依照以下程序组织：  
（一）当事人要求听证的，应当在行政机关告知后五日内提出；  
（二）行政机关应当在举行听证的七日前，通知当事人及有关人员听证的时间、地点；  
（三）除涉及国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私依法予以保密外，听证公开举行；  
（四）听证由行政机关指定的非本案调查人员主持；当事人认为主持人与本案有直接利害关系的，有权申请回避；  
（五）当事人可以亲自参加听证，也可以委托一至二人代理；  
（六）当事人及其代理人无正当理由拒不出席听证或者未经许可中途退出听证的，视为放弃听证权利，行政机关终止听证；  
（七）举行听证时，调查人员提出当事人违法的事实、证据和行政处罚建议，当事人进行申辩和质证；  
（八）听证应当制作笔录。笔录应当交当事人或者其代理人核对无误后签字或者盖章。当事人或者其代理人拒绝签字或者盖章的，由听证主持人在笔录中注明。

**Article 64** A hearing shall be organized in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) If the party requests a hearing, it shall make the request within five days after being informed by the administrative organ;  
(2) The administrative organ shall notify the party and relevant persons of the time and place of the hearing seven days before the hearing is held;  
(3) The hearing shall be held in public, except where State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy are involved and shall be kept confidential according to law;  
(4) The hearing shall be presided over by a person designated by the administrative organ who is not an investigator of the case; if the party considers that the presiding officer has a direct interest in the case, it has the right to apply for the presiding officer's withdrawal;  
(5) The party may attend the hearing in person or may entrust one or two agents to attend;  
(6) If the party or its agent, without justifiable reasons, refuses to attend the hearing or withdraws from the hearing without permission midway, it shall be deemed to have waived the right to a hearing, and the administrative organ shall terminate the hearing;  
(7) At the hearing, the investigator shall present the facts, evidence, and suggestions for administrative penalty regarding the party's violation of law, and the party shall present its defenses and cross-examine the evidence;  
(8) A written record of the hearing shall be made. The record shall be submitted to the party or its agent for verification and shall be signed or sealed after verification. If the party or its agent refuses to sign or seal, the presiding officer of the hearing shall note this in the record.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　听证结束后，行政机关应当根据听证笔录，依照[本法第五十七条](#art-57)的规定，作出决定。

**Article 65** After the conclusion of the hearing, the administrative organ shall make a decision in accordance with Article 57 of this Law based on the hearing record.

### 第六章　行政处罚的执行

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　行政处罚决定依法作出后，当事人应当在行政处罚决定书载明的期限内，予以履行。  
当事人确有经济困难，需要延期或者分期缴纳罚款的，经当事人申请和行政机关批准，可以暂缓或者分期缴纳。

**Article 66** After a decision on an administrative penalty is made according to law, the party shall perform it within the time limit specified in the written decision on the administrative penalty.  
Where the party genuinely has financial difficulties and needs an extension or installment payment of a fine, upon the party's application and with the approval of the administrative organ, the fine may be paid by extension or in installments.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　作出罚款决定的行政机关应当与收缴罚款的机构分离。  
除依照[本法第六十八条](#art-68)、第六十九条的规定当场收缴的罚款外，作出行政处罚决定的行政机关及其执法人员不得自行收缴罚款。  
当事人应当自收到行政处罚决定书之日起十五日内，到指定的银行或者通过电子支付系统缴纳罚款。银行应当收受罚款，并将罚款直接上缴国库。

**Article 67** The administrative organ that decides on a fine shall be separated from the institution that collects the fine.  
Except for fines collected on the spot in accordance with Articles 68 and 69 of this Law, the administrative organ that makes the decision on the administrative penalty and its law enforcement personnel shall not collect fines themselves.  
The party shall, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the written decision on the administrative penalty, pay the fine at a designated bank or through an electronic payment system. The bank shall accept the fine and turn it over directly to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　依照[本法第五十一条](#art-51)的规定当场作出行政处罚决定，有下列情形之一，执法人员可以当场收缴罚款：  
（一）依法给予一百元以下罚款的；  
（二）不当场收缴事后难以执行的。

**Article 68** Where an administrative penalty decision is made on the spot in accordance with Article 51 of this Law, and any of the following circumstances exists, the law enforcement personnel may collect the fine on the spot:  
(1) a fine of not more than 100 yuan is imposed according to law;  
(2) if the fine is not collected on the spot, it would be difficult to enforce afterwards.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　在边远、水上、交通不便地区，行政机关及其执法人员依照[本法第五十一条](#art-51)、第五十七条的规定作出罚款决定后，当事人到指定的银行或者通过电子支付系统缴纳罚款确有困难，经当事人提出，行政机关及其执法人员可以当场收缴罚款。

**Article 69** In remote, waterborne, or traffic-inconvenient areas, after an administrative organ and its law enforcement personnel make a fine decision in accordance with Articles 51 and 57 of this Law, if the party truly has difficulty in paying the fine at the designated bank or through an electronic payment system, and upon the party's request, the administrative organ and its law enforcement personnel may collect the fine on the spot.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　行政机关及其执法人员当场收缴罚款的，必须向当事人出具国务院财政部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府财政部门统一制发的专用票据；不出具财政部门统一制发的专用票据的，当事人有权拒绝缴纳罚款。

**Article 70** If an administrative organ and its law enforcement personnel collect a fine on the spot, they must issue a special receipt uniformly printed by the finance department of the State Council or the finance department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to the party; if such a uniformly printed special receipt is not issued, the party shall have the right to refuse to pay the fine.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　执法人员当场收缴的罚款，应当自收缴罚款之日起二日内，交至行政机关；在水上当场收缴的罚款，应当自抵岸之日起二日内交至行政机关；行政机关应当在二日内将罚款缴付指定的银行。

**Article 71** The fine collected on the spot by law enforcement personnel shall be turned over to the administrative organ within two days from the date of collection; the fine collected on the spot on water shall be turned over to the administrative organ within two days from the date of docking; the administrative organ shall pay the fine to the designated bank within two days.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　当事人逾期不履行行政处罚决定的，作出行政处罚决定的行政机关可以采取下列措施：  
（一）到期不缴纳罚款的，每日按罚款数额的百分之三加处罚款，加处罚款的数额不得超出罚款的数额；  
（二）根据法律规定，将查封、扣押的财物拍卖、依法处理或者将冻结的存款、汇款划拨抵缴罚款；  
（三）根据法律规定，采取其他行政强制执行方式；  
（四）依照《中华人民共和国行政强制法》的规定申请人民法院强制执行。  
行政机关批准延期、分期缴纳罚款的，申请人民法院强制执行的期限，自暂缓或者分期缴纳罚款期限结束之日起计算。

**Article 72** Where a party fails to comply with an administrative penalty decision within the time limit, the administrative organ that made the decision may take the following measures:  
(1) if the fine is not paid when due, an additional fine of three percent of the fine amount shall be imposed on a daily basis, and the amount of the additional fine shall not exceed the amount of the fine;  
(2) according to the provisions of the law, auction off the sealed-up or seized property, dispose of it according to law, or transfer the frozen deposits or remittances to offset the fine;  
(3) according to the provisions of the law, adopt other administrative compulsory enforcement methods;  
(4) apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Compulsion Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the administrative organ approves the extension or installment payment of the fine, the time limit for applying to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement shall be calculated from the date on which the period for deferred or installment payment of the fine ends.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　当事人对行政处罚决定不服，申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的，行政处罚不停止执行，法律另有规定的除外。  
当事人对限制人身自由的行政处罚决定不服，申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的，可以向作出决定的机关提出暂缓执行申请。符合法律规定情形的，应当暂缓执行。  
当事人申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼的，加处罚款的数额在行政复议或者行政诉讼期间不予计算。

**Article 73** Where a party refuses to accept an administrative penalty decision and applies for administrative reconsideration or initiates an administrative action, the administrative penalty shall not be suspended, unless otherwise provided by law.  
Where a party refuses to accept an administrative penalty decision restricting personal freedom and applies for administrative reconsideration or initiates an administrative action, it may apply to the organ that made the decision for suspension of enforcement. Where the circumstances comply with the provisions of law, enforcement shall be suspended.  
Where a party applies for administrative reconsideration or initiates an administrative action, the amount of the additional fine shall not be calculated during the period of administrative reconsideration or administrative action.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　除依法应当予以销毁的物品外，依法没收的非法财物必须按照国家规定公开拍卖或者按照国家有关规定处理。  
罚款、没收的违法所得或者没收非法财物拍卖的款项，必须全部上缴国库，任何行政机关或者个人不得以任何形式截留、私分或者变相私分。  
罚款、没收的违法所得或者没收非法财物拍卖的款项，不得同作出行政处罚决定的行政机关及其工作人员的考核、考评直接或者变相挂钩。除依法应当退还、退赔的外，财政部门不得以任何形式向作出行政处罚决定的行政机关返还罚款、没收的违法所得或者没收非法财物拍卖的款项。

**Article 74** Except for items that shall be destroyed according to law, the illegal property confiscated according to law must be publicly auctioned in accordance with state regulations or disposed of in accordance with relevant state regulations.  
The fine, the illegal gains confiscated, or the proceeds from the auction of confiscated illegal property must all be turned over to the state treasury in full, and no administrative organ or individual may in any form withhold, privately distribute, or covertly distribute them.  
The fine, the illegal gains confiscated, or the proceeds from the auction of confiscated illegal property shall not be directly or covertly linked to the assessment or evaluation of the administrative organ that made the administrative penalty decision or its staff. Except for those that shall be returned or compensated according to law, the finance department may not in any form return the fine, the confiscated illegal gains, or the proceeds from the auction of confiscated illegal property to the administrative organ that made the administrative penalty decision.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　行政机关应当建立健全对行政处罚的监督制度。县级以上人民政府应当定期组织开展行政执法评议、考核，加强对行政处罚的监督检查，规范和保障行政处罚的实施。  
行政机关实施行政处罚应当接受社会监督。公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关实施行政处罚的行为，有权申诉或者检举；行政机关应当认真审查，发现有错误的，应当主动改正。

**Article 75** An administrative organ shall establish and improve a supervision system for administrative penalties. People's governments at or above the county level shall regularly organize and carry out evaluation and assessment of administrative law enforcement, strengthen supervision and inspection of administrative penalties, and regulate and guarantee the implementation of administrative penalties.  
An administrative organ implementing an administrative penalty shall accept social supervision. Citizens, legal persons, or other organizations shall have the right to petition or report against the act of an administrative organ in implementing an administrative penalty; the administrative organ shall carefully examine the matter, and if an error is found, it shall proactively correct it.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　行政机关实施行政处罚，有下列情形之一，由上级行政机关或者有关机关责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分：  
（一）没有法定的行政处罚依据的；  
（二）擅自改变行政处罚种类、幅度的；  
（三）违反法定的行政处罚程序的；  
（四）违反[本法第二十条](#art-20)关于委托处罚的规定的；  
（五）执法人员未取得执法证件的。  
行政机关对符合立案标准的案件不及时立案的，依照前款规定予以处理。

**Article 76** Where an administrative organ implements an administrative penalty under any of the following circumstances, the superior administrative organ or the relevant organ shall order rectification, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons:  
(1) there is no statutory basis for the administrative penalty;  
(2) the type or range of the administrative penalty is changed without authorization;  
(3) the statutory procedures for an administrative penalty are violated;  
(4) the provisions of Article 20 of this Law concerning delegated penalties are violated;  
(5) the law enforcement personnel have not obtained law enforcement certificates.  
Where an administrative organ fails to promptly initiate a case that meets the case-filing standards, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　行政机关对当事人进行处罚不使用罚款、没收财物单据或者使用非法定部门制发的罚款、没收财物单据的，当事人有权拒绝，并有权予以检举，由上级行政机关或者有关机关对使用的非法单据予以收缴销毁，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 77** Where an administrative organ, when imposing a penalty on a party, fails to use the receipt for fines or confiscated property, or uses a receipt for fines or confiscated property issued by a non-statutory department, the party shall have the right to refuse and the right to report; the superior administrative organ or the relevant organ shall confiscate and destroy the illegal receipts used, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　行政机关违反[本法第六十七条](#art-67)的规定自行收缴罚款的，财政部门违反[本法第七十四条](#art-74)的规定向行政机关返还罚款、没收的违法所得或者拍卖款项的，由上级行政机关或者有关机关责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 78** Where an administrative organ collects fines on its own in violation of Article 67 of this Law, or where the finance department returns fines, confiscated illegal gains, or auction proceeds to the administrative organ in violation of Article 74 of this Law, the superior administrative organ or the relevant organ shall order rectification, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　行政机关截留、私分或者变相私分罚款、没收的违法所得或者财物的，由财政部门或者有关机关予以追缴，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
执法人员利用职务上的便利，索取或者收受他人财物、将收缴罚款据为己有，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任；情节轻微不构成犯罪的，依法给予处分。

**Article 79** Where an administrative organ withholds, privately distributes, or covertly distributes fines, confiscated illegal gains, or property, the finance department or the relevant organ shall recover the amounts, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
Where a law enforcement personnel, by taking advantage of their position, extorts or accepts property from others, or appropriates the collected fine, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; if the circumstances are minor and do not constitute a crime, sanctions shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　行政机关使用或者损毁查封、扣押的财物，对当事人造成损失的，应当依法予以赔偿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分。

**Article 80** Where an administrative organ uses or damages sealed-up or seized property, causing losses to the party, it shall pay compensation according to law, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　行政机关违法实施检查措施或者执行措施，给公民人身或者财产造成损害、给法人或者其他组织造成损失的，应当依法予以赔偿，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 81** Where an administrative organ illegally implements inspection measures or enforcement measures, causing damage to the person or property of a citizen, or causing losses to a legal person or other organization, it shall pay compensation according to law, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　行政机关对应当依法移交司法机关追究刑事责任的案件不移交，以行政处罚代替刑事处罚，由上级行政机关或者有关机关责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 82** Where an administrative organ fails to transfer a case that shall be transferred to a judicial organ according to law for investigation of criminal liability, and substitutes an administrative penalty for criminal punishment, the superior administrative organ or the relevant organ shall order rectification, and impose sanctions according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　行政机关对应当予以制止和处罚的违法行为不予制止、处罚，致使公民、法人或者其他组织的合法权益、公共利益和社会秩序遭受损害的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员依法给予处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 83** Where an administrative organ fails to stop or penalize an illegal act that shall be stopped and penalized, thereby causing damage to the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, to the public interest, or to social order, sanctions shall be imposed according to law on the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons; if the circumstances are serious and constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　外国人、无国籍人、外国组织在中华人民共和国领域内有违法行为，应当给予行政处罚的，适用本法，法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 84** Where a foreigner, stateless person, or foreign organization commits an illegal act within the territory of the People's Republic of China and an administrative penalty shall be imposed, this Law shall apply, unless otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　本法中“二日”“三日”“五日”“七日”的规定是指工作日，不含法定节假日。

**Article 85** The provisions of "two days," "three days," "five days," and "seven days" in this Law refer to working days, excluding legal holidays.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　本法自2021年7月15日起施行。

**Article 86** This Law shall come into force on July 15, 2021.
