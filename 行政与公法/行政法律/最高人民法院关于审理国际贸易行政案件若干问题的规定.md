# 最高人民法院关于审理国际贸易行政案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕27号  
**公布日期**：2002-08-27  
**生效日期**：2002-10-01

> （2002年8月27日最高人民法院审判委员会第1239次会议通过，自2002年10月1日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕27号 为依法公正及时地审理国际贸易行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法）、《中华人民共和国立法法》（以下简称立法法）以及其他有关法律的规定，制定本规定。 第一条 下列案件属于本规定所称国际贸易行政案件： （一）有关国际货物贸易的行政案件； （二）有关国际服务贸易的行政案件； （三）与国际贸易有关的知识产权行政案件； （四）其他国际贸易行政案件。 第二条 人民法院行政审判庭依法审理国际贸易行政案件。 第三条 自然人、法人或者其他组织认为中华人民共和国具有国家行政职权的机关和组织及其工作人员（以下统称行政机关）有关国际贸易的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的，可以依照行政诉讼法以及其他有关法律、法规的规定，向人民法院提起行政诉讼。 第四条 当事人的行为发生在新法生效之前，行政机关在新法生效之后对该行为作出行政处理决定的，当事人可以依照新法的规定提起行政诉讼。 第五条 第一审国际贸易行政案件由具有管辖权的中级以上人民法院管辖。 第六条 人民法院审理国际贸易行政案件，应当依照行政诉讼法，并根据案件具体情况，从以下方面对被诉具体行政行为进行合法性审查： （一）主要证据是否确实、充分； （二）适用法律、法规是否正确；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 下列案件属于本规定所称国际贸易行政案件：  
（一）有关国际货物贸易的行政案件；  
（二）有关国际服务贸易的行政案件；  
（三）与国际贸易有关的知识产权行政案件；  
（四）其他国际贸易行政案件。

**Article 1** The following cases shall fall under the category of international trade administrative cases as referred to in these Provisions:  
(1) administrative cases concerning international trade in goods;  
(2) administrative cases concerning international trade in services;  
(3) administrative cases concerning intellectual property relating to international trade;  
(4) other international trade administrative cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 人民法院行政审判庭依法审理国际贸易行政案件。

**Article 2** The administrative adjudication divisions of the People's Courts shall, according to law, adjudicate international trade administrative cases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 自然人、法人或者其他组织认为中华人民共和国具有国家行政职权的机关和组织及其工作人员（以下统称行政机关）有关国际贸易的具体行政行为侵犯其合法权益的，可以依照行政诉讼法以及其他有关法律、法规的规定，向人民法院提起行政诉讼。

**Article 3** Where a natural person, legal person or any other organization considers that a specific administrative act relating to international trade undertaken by any organ or organization with state administrative authority of the People's Republic of China and its staff (hereinafter collectively referred to as an administrative organ) infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, it may, in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law and other relevant laws and regulations, institute an administrative action with the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 当事人的行为发生在新法生效之前，行政机关在新法生效之后对该行为作出行政处理决定的，当事人可以依照新法的规定提起行政诉讼。

**Article 4** Where a party's act occurs before the new law takes effect, and the administrative organ makes an administrative handling decision regarding such act after the new law takes effect, the party may institute an administrative action in accordance with the provisions of the new law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 第一审国际贸易行政案件由具有管辖权的中级以上人民法院管辖。

**Article 5** International trade administrative cases of first instance shall be under the jurisdiction of the intermediate or higher People's Courts that have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院审理国际贸易行政案件，应当依照行政诉讼法，并根据案件具体情况，从以下方面对被诉具体行政行为进行合法性审查：  
（一）主要证据是否确实、充分；  
（二）适用法律、法规是否正确；  
（三）是否违反法定程序；  
（四）是否超越职权；  
（五）是否滥用职权；  
（六）行政处罚是否显失公正；  
（七）是否不履行或者拖延履行法定职责。

**Article 6** When adjudicating international trade administrative cases, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law and based on the specific circumstances of the case, conduct a legality review of the specific administrative act complained of from the following aspects:  
(1) whether the main evidence is true and sufficient;  
(2) whether the application of laws and regulations is correct;  
(3) whether the statutory procedures are violated;  
(4) whether the authority is exceeded;  
(5) whether the authority is abused;  
(6) whether the administrative penalty is manifestly unjust;  
(7) whether there is a failure to perform or delay in performing statutory duties.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 根据[行政诉讼法第五十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-52)第一款及[立法法第六十三条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国立法法.md#art-63)第一款和第二款规定，人民法院审理国际贸易行政案件，应当依据中华人民共和国法律、行政法规以及地方立法机关在法定立法权限范围内制定的有关或者影响国际贸易的地方性法规。地方性法规适用于本行政区域内发生的国际贸易行政案件。

**Article 7** Pursuant to Article 52, paragraph 1 of the Administrative Procedure Law and Article 63, paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Legislation Law, when adjudicating international trade administrative cases, the People's Court shall rely on the laws and administrative regulations of the People's Republic of China as well as the local regulations concerning or affecting international trade enacted by local legislatures within their statutory legislative authority. Local regulations shall apply to international trade administrative cases occurring within the corresponding administrative region.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 根据[行政诉讼法第五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-53)第一款及[立法法第七十一条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国立法法.md#art-71)、第七十二条和第七十三条规定，人民法院审理国际贸易行政案件，参照国务院部门根据法律和国务院的行政法规、决定、命令，在本部门权限范围内制定的有关或者影响国际贸易的部门规章，以及省、自治区、直辖市和省、自治区的人民政府所在地的市、经济特区所在地的市、国务院批准的较大的市的人民政府根据法律、行政法规和地方性法规制定的有关或者影响国际贸易的地方政府规章。

**Article 8** Pursuant to Article 53, paragraph 1 of the Administrative Procedure Law and Articles 71, 72 and 73 of the Legislation Law, when adjudicating international trade administrative cases, the People's Court shall take as reference the departmental rules concerning or affecting international trade formulated by the departments of the State Council within their respective authorities in accordance with the laws and the administrative regulations, decisions and orders of the State Council, as well as the local government rules concerning or affecting international trade formulated by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the people's governments of cities where the people's governments of provinces and autonomous regions are located, cities where special economic zones are located, and relatively large cities approved by the State Council, in accordance with laws, administrative regulations and local regulations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院审理国际贸易行政案件所适用的法律、行政法规的具体条文存在两种以上的合理解释，其中有一种解释与中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约的有关规定相一致的，应当选择与国际条约的有关规定相一致的解释，但中华人民共和国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 9** Where there are two or more reasonable interpretations of a specific provision of a law or administrative regulation applicable to the adjudication of an international trade administrative case by the People's Court, and one of such interpretations is consistent with the relevant provisions of an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, the interpretation consistent with the relevant provisions of the international treaty shall be adopted, except for the provisions on which the People's Republic of China has declared reservations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 外国人、无国籍人、外国组织在中华人民共和国进行国际贸易行政诉讼，同中华人民共和国公民、组织有同等的诉讼权利和义务，但有[行政诉讼法第七十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-71)第二款规定的情形的，适用对等原则。

**Article 10** Aliens, stateless persons and foreign organizations conducting international trade administrative litigation in the People's Republic of China shall have the same litigation rights and obligations as citizens and organizations of the People's Republic of China, except that where the circumstances specified in Article 71, paragraph 2 of the Administrative Procedure Law exist, the principle of reciprocity shall apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区当事人的国际贸易行政案件，参照本规定处理。

**Article 11** International trade administrative cases involving parties from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region and the Taiwan region shall be handled with reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 本规定自2002年10月1日起施行。  
2002年8月27日

**Article 12** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 2002.
