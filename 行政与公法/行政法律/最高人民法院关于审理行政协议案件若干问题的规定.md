# 最高人民法院关于审理行政协议案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕17号  
**公布日期**：2020-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理行政协议案件若干问题的规定》已于 2019 年 11 月 12 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第 1781 次会议通过，现予公布，自 2020年1月1日起施行。 2019年11月27日 最高人民法院 关于审理行政协议案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2019〕17号 为依法公正、及时审理行政协议案件， 根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合行政审判工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条 行政机关为了实现行政管理 或者公共服务目标，与公民、法人或者其他 组织协商订立的具有行政法上权利义务内 容的协议，属于行政诉讼法第十二条第一 款第十一项规定的行政协议。 第二条 公民、法人或者其他组织就 下列行政协议提起行政诉讼的，人民法院 应当依法受理： （一）政府特许经营协议； （二）土地、房屋等征收征用补偿协议； （三）矿业权等国有自然资源使用权出 让协议； （四）政府投资的保障性住房的租赁、 买卖等协议； （五）符合本规定第一条规定的政府与 社会资本合作协议； （六）其他行政协议。 第三条 因行政机关订立的下列协议提起诉讼的，不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围：

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 行政机关为了实现行政管理 或者公共服务目标，与公民、法人或者其他 组织协商订立的具有行政法上权利义务内 容的协议，属于[行政诉讼法第十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-12)第一 款第十一项规定的行政协议。

**Article 1** An agreement concluded through consultation by an administrative organ with a citizen, legal person, or any other organization for the purpose of achieving administrative management or public service objectives and containing rights and obligations under administrative law shall fall within the scope of administrative agreements as specified in Item 11 of Paragraph 1 of Article 12 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 公民、法人或者其他组织就 下列行政协议提起行政诉讼的，人民法院 应当依法受理：  
（一）政府特许经营协议；  
（二）土地、房屋等征收征用补偿协议；  
（三）矿业权等国有自然资源使用权出 让协议；  
（四）政府投资的保障性住房的租赁、 买卖等协议；  
（五）符合[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定的政府与 社会资本合作协议；  
（六）其他行政协议。

**Article 2** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization institutes an administrative action against any of the following administrative agreements, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) government concession agreements;  
(2) compensation agreements for expropriation or requisition of land, houses, etc.;  
(3) agreements on the assignment of the right to use state-owned natural resources, such as mining rights;  
(4) agreements on the lease, sale, etc. of government-invested affordable housing;  
(5) government-social capital cooperation agreements meeting the requirements of Article 1 of these Provisions;  
(6) other administrative agreements.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 因行政机关订立的下列协议提起诉讼的，不属于人民法院行政诉讼的受案范围：  
（一）行政机关之间因公务协助等事由 而订立的协议；  
（二）行政机关与其工作人员订立的劳 动人事协议。

**Article 3** An action instituted due to any of the following agreements concluded by an administrative organ shall not fall within the scope of acceptance of administrative actions by a People's Court:  
(1) agreements concluded between administrative organs for reasons such as official assistance;  
(2) labor and personnel agreements concluded between an administrative organ and its staff members.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 因行政协议的订立、履行、变 更、终止等发生纠纷，公民、法人或者其他组织作为原告，以行政机关为被告提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
因行政机关委托的组织订立的行政协 议发生纠纷的，委托的行政机关是被告。

**Article 4** Where a dispute arises in respect of the conclusion, performance, modification, or termination of an administrative agreement, and a citizen, legal person, or any other organization, as the plaintiff, institutes an administrative action against an administrative organ as the defendant, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where a dispute arises from an administrative agreement concluded by an organization authorized by an administrative organ, the authorizing administrative organ shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 下列与行政协议有利害关系 的公民、法人或者其他组织提起行政诉讼的，人民法院应当依法受理:  
（一）参与招标、拍卖、挂牌等竞争性活动，认为行政机关应当依法与其订立行政协议但行政机关拒绝订立，或者认为行政 机关与他人订立行政协议损害其合法权益 的公民、法人或者其他组织；  
（二）认为征收征用补偿协议损害其合 法权益的被征收征用土地、房屋等不动产 的用益物权人、公房承租人；  
（三）其他认为行政协议的订立、履行、 变更、终止等行为损害其合法权益的公民、 法人或者其他组织。

**Article 5** Where any of the following citizens, legal persons, or other organizations having an interest in an administrative agreement institutes an administrative action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law:  
(1) a citizen, legal person, or other organization that participated in competitive activities such as bidding, auction, or listing, and considers that the administrative organ should have concluded an administrative agreement with it according to law but the administrative organ refused to do so, or considers that the administrative organ's conclusion of an administrative agreement with another person has harmed its lawful rights and interests;  
(2) a usufructuary or a lessee of public housing of immovable property such as land or houses being expropriated or requisitioned, who considers that the compensation agreement for expropriation or requisition has harmed its lawful rights and interests;  
(3) other citizens, legal persons, or other organizations that consider that acts such as the conclusion, performance, modification, or termination of an administrative agreement have harmed their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院受理行政协议案件后，被告就该协议的订立、履行、变更、终止 等提起反诉的，人民法院不予准许。

**Article 6** After the People's Court accepts a case concerning an administrative agreement, if the defendant files a counterclaim regarding the conclusion, performance, modification, or termination of the agreement, the People's Court shall not permit it.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 当事人书面协议约定选择被 告所在地、原告所在地、协议履行地、协议 订立地、标的物所在地等与争议有实际联 系地点的人民法院管辖的，人民法院从其 约定，但违反级别管辖和专属管辖的除外。

**Article 7** Where the parties agree in writing to select the People's Court of the defendant's domicile, the plaintiff's domicile, the place of performance of the agreement, the place of execution of the agreement, the location of the subject matter, or any other place having actual connection with the dispute for jurisdiction, the People's Court shall follow such agreement, unless it violates hierarchical jurisdiction or exclusive jurisdiction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 公民、法人或者其他组织向 人民法院提起民事诉讼，生效法律文书以 涉案协议属于行政协议为由裁定不予立案 或者驳回起诉，当事人又提起行政诉讼的， 人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 8** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization files a civil action with a People's Court, and a legally effective written instrument rules not to file the case or dismiss the action on the ground that the agreement involved is an administrative agreement, if the party subsequently files an administrative action, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 在行政协议案件中，行政诉 讼法第四十九条第三项规定的“有具体的 诉讼请求”是指：  
（一）请求判决撤销行政机关变更、解 除行政协议的行政行为，或者确认该行政 行为违法；  
（二）请求判决行政机关依法履行或者 按照行政协议约定履行义务；  
（三）请求判决确认行政协议的效力；  
（四）请求判决行政机关依法或者按照 约定订立行政协议；  
（五）请求判决撤销、解除行政协议；  
（六）请求判决行政机关赔偿或者补 偿；  
（七）其他有关行政协议的订立、履行、 变更、终止等诉讼请求。

**Article 9** In cases concerning administrative agreements, "specific claims" as provided in Item 3 of Article 49 of the Administrative Procedure Law means:  
(1) claiming a judgment to revoke an administrative act of the administrative organ modifying or terminating an administrative agreement, or to confirm that such administrative act is illegal;  
(2) claiming a judgment ordering the administrative organ to perform its obligations according to law or in accordance with the provisions of the administrative agreement;  
(3) claiming a judgment to confirm the validity of an administrative agreement;  
(4) claiming a judgment ordering the administrative organ to conclude an administrative agreement according to law or as agreed;  
(5) claiming a judgment to revoke or terminate an administrative agreement;  
(6) claiming a judgment ordering the administrative organ to pay compensation or damages;  
(7) other claims relating to the conclusion, performance, modification, or termination of an administrative agreement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 被告对于自己具有法定职权、履行法定程序、履行相应法定职责以及订立、履行、变更、解除行政协议等行为的合法性承担举证责任。  
原告主张撤销、解除行政协议的，对撤销、解除行政协议的事由承担举证责任。  
对行政协议是否履行发生争议的，由负有履行义务的当事人承担举证责任。

**Article 10** The defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the legality of its acts such as having statutory authority, following statutory procedures, performing corresponding statutory duties, and concluding, performing, modifying, or terminating an administrative agreement.  
Where the plaintiff claims to revoke or terminate an administrative agreement, the plaintiff shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the grounds for revoking or terminating the administrative agreement.  
Where a dispute arises as to whether an administrative agreement has been performed, the party obligated to perform shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院审理行政协议案件，应当对被告订立、履行、变更、解除行政 协议的行为是否具有法定职权、是否滥用 职权、适用法律法规是否正确、是否遵守法定程序、是否明显不当、是否履行相应法定职责进行合法性审查。  
原告认为被告未依法或者未按照约定履行行政协议的，人民法院应当针对其诉讼请求，对被告是否具有相应义务或者履行相应义务等进行审查。

**Article 11** When trying a case concerning an administrative agreement, the People's Court shall conduct a legality review regarding whether the defendant's acts of concluding, performing, modifying, or terminating the administrative agreement have statutory authority, whether there is an abuse of authority, whether the application of laws and regulations is correct, whether statutory procedures are followed, whether the act is obviously improper, and whether corresponding statutory duties are performed.  
Where the plaintiff considers that the defendant has not performed the administrative agreement according to law or in accordance with the agreement, the People's Court shall, in light of the plaintiff's claims, review whether the defendant has corresponding obligations or has performed corresponding obligations, etc.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 行政协议存在行政诉讼法 第七十五条规定的重大且明显违法情形的，人民法院应当确认行政协议无效。  
人民法院可以适用民事法律规范确认行政协议无效。  
行政协议无效的原因在一审法庭辩论终结前消除的，人民法院可以确认行政协议有效。

**Article 12** Where an administrative agreement involves circumstances of major and obvious illegality as provided in Article 75 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court shall confirm that the administrative agreement is void.  
The People's Court may apply civil legal norms to confirm that an administrative agreement is void.  
If the cause of the invalidity of an administrative agreement is eliminated before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance, the People's Court may confirm that the administrative agreement is valid.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 法律、行政法规规定应当经过其他机关批准等程序后生效的行政协议，在一审法庭辩论终结前未获得批准的， 人民法院应当确认该协议未生效。  
行政协议约定被告负有履行批准程序 等义务而被告未履行，原告要求被告承担赔偿责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 13** Where an administrative agreement is required by laws or administrative regulations to become effective after undergoing procedures such as approval by other authorities, if such approval is not obtained before the conclusion of the court debate in the first instance, the People's Court shall confirm that the agreement has not become effective.  
Where an administrative agreement provides that the defendant has obligations to perform approval procedures, etc., but the defendant fails to perform such obligations, and the plaintiff requests the defendant to bear compensation liability, the People's Court shall uphold such request.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 原告认为行政协议存在胁迫、欺诈、重大误解、显失公平等情形而请求撤销，人民法院经审理认为符合法律规定可撤销情形的，可以依法判决撤销该协议。

**Article 14** Where the plaintiff requests revocation on the grounds that the administrative agreement involves duress, fraud, material misunderstanding, or obvious unfairness, and the People's Court, after trial, finds that the circumstances meet the revocable conditions provided by law, it may, according to law, rule to revoke the agreement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 行政协议无效、被撤销或 者确定不发生效力后，当事人因行政协议取得的财产，人民法院应当判决予以返还；不能返还的，判决折价补偿。  
因被告的原因导致行政协议被确认无 效或者被撤销，可以同时判决责令被告采 取补救措施；给原告造成损失的，人民法院应当判决被告予以赔偿。

**Article 15** After an administrative agreement is void, revoked, or determined to have no legal effect, the People's Court shall order the return of property obtained by a party as a result of the administrative agreement; if return is impossible, it shall rule on compensation at a discounted price.  
Where the administrative agreement is confirmed void or revoked due to the defendant's reasons, the People's Court may also order the defendant to take remedial measures; if losses are caused to the plaintiff, the People's Court shall order the defendant to pay compensation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 在履行行政协议过程中， 可能出现严重损害国家利益、社会公共利益的情形，被告作出变更、解除协议的行政行为后，原告请求撤销该行为，人民法院经审理认为该行为合法的，判决驳回原告诉 讼请求；给原告造成损失的，判决被告予以补偿。  
被告变更、解除行政协议的行政行为存在[行政诉讼法第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-70)规定情形的，人民法院判决撤销或者部分撤销，并可以责令被告重新作出行政行为。  
被告变更、解除行政协议的行政行为 违法，人民法院可以依据[行政诉讼法第七十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-78)的规定判决被告继续履行协议、采取补救措施；给原告造成损失的，判决被告予以赔偿。

**Article 16** Where, in the process of performing an administrative agreement, circumstances that may cause serious harm to national interests or social public interests may arise, and the defendant makes an administrative act to modify or terminate the agreement, if the plaintiff requests revocation of such act, and the People's Court, after trial, finds that the act is lawful, it shall rule to dismiss the plaintiff's claims; if losses are caused to the plaintiff, it shall rule that the defendant shall pay compensation.  
Where the administrative act of the defendant modifying or terminating an administrative agreement has circumstances provided in Article 70 of the Administrative Procedure Law, the People's Court shall rule to revoke or partially revoke the act, and may order the defendant to make a new administrative act.  
Where the administrative act of the defendant modifying or terminating an administrative agreement is illegal, the People's Court may, pursuant to Article 78 of the Administrative Procedure Law, rule that the defendant shall continue to perform the agreement or take remedial measures; if losses are caused to the plaintiff, it shall rule that the defendant shall pay compensation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 原告请求解除行政协议， 人民法院认为符合约定或者法定解除情形 且不损害国家利益、社会公共利益和他人合法权益的，可以判决解除该协议。

**Article 17** Where the plaintiff requests termination of an administrative agreement, and the People's Court finds that the conditions for termination as agreed or as provided by law are met and that termination will not harm national interests, social public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others, it may rule to terminate the agreement.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 当事人依据民事法律规范的规定行使履行抗辩权的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 18** Where a party exercises the right to defense against performance pursuant to the provisions of civil legal norms, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 被告未依法履行、未按照约定履行行政协议，人民法院可以依据行 政诉讼法第七十八条的规定，结合原告诉讼请求，判决被告继续履行，并明确继续履行的具体内容；被告无法履行或者继续履 行无实际意义的，人民法院可以判决被告采取相应的补救措施；给原告造成损失的， 判决被告予以赔偿。  
原告要求按照约定的违约金条款或者定金条款予以赔偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where the defendant fails to perform the administrative agreement according to law or in accordance with the agreement, the People's Court may, pursuant to Article 78 of the Administrative Procedure Law and in light of the plaintiff's claims, rule that the defendant shall continue performance and specify the specific content of the continued performance; if the defendant is unable to perform or continued performance has no practical significance, the People's Court may rule that the defendant shall take corresponding remedial measures; if losses are caused to the plaintiff, it shall rule that the defendant shall pay compensation.  
Where the plaintiff claims compensation in accordance with the agreed liquidated damages clause or deposit clause, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 被告明确表示或者以自己 的行为表明不履行行政协议，原告在履行期限届满之前向人民法院起诉请求其承担违约责任的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 20** Where the defendant explicitly indicates or manifests by its conduct that it will not perform the administrative agreement, and the plaintiff files an action with the People's Court before the expiration of the performance period requesting the defendant to bear liability for breach of contract, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 被告或者其他行政机关 因国家利益、社会公共利益的需要依法行 使行政职权，导致原告履行不能、履行费用明显增加或者遭受损失，原告请求判令被 告给予补偿的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 21** Where the defendant or another administrative organ exercises its administrative authority according to law for the needs of national interests or social public interests, resulting in the plaintiff being unable to perform, a significant increase in performance costs, or suffering losses, and the plaintiff requests a judgment ordering the defendant to provide compensation, the People's Court shall uphold it.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 原告以被告违约为由请求人民法院判令其承担违约责任，人民法院经审理认为行政协议无效的，应当向原告释明，并根据原告变更后的诉讼请求判决确认行政协议无效；因被告的行为造成行政协议无效的，人民法院可以依法判决被告承担赔偿责任。原告经释明后拒绝变更诉讼请求的，人民法院可以判决驳回其诉讼请求。

**Article 22** Where the plaintiff requests the People's Court to order the defendant to bear liability for breach of contract on the ground of the defendant's breach, and the People's Court, after trial, finds that the administrative agreement is void, it shall explain the situation to the plaintiff and, based on the plaintiff's amended claims, rule to confirm that the administrative agreement is void; if the administrative agreement is void due to the defendant's conduct, the People's Court may rule that the defendant shall bear compensation liability according to law. Where the plaintiff refuses to amend the claims after explanation, the People's Court may rule to dismiss the plaintiff's claims.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 人民法院审理行政协议案件，可以依法进行调解。  
人民法院进行调解时，应当遵循自愿、 合法原则，不得损害国家利益、社会公共利 益和他人合法权益。

**Article 23** When trying a case concerning an administrative agreement, the People's Court may conduct mediation according to law.  
When conducting mediation, the People's Court shall follow the principles of voluntariness and legality, and shall not harm national interests, social public interests, or the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 公民、法人或者其他组 织未按照行政协议约定履行义务，经催告后不履行，行政机关可以作出要求其履行协议的书面决定。公民、法人或者其他组织收到书面决定后在法定期限内未申请行 政复议或者提起行政诉讼，且仍不履行，协 议内容具有可执行性的，行政机关可以向人民法院申请强制执行。  
法律、行政法规规定行政机关对行政协议享有监督协议履行的职权，公民、法人或者其他组织未按照约定履行义务，经催告后不履行，行政机关可以依法作出处理决定。公民、法人或者其他组织在收到该 处理决定后在法定期限内未申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼，且仍不履行，协议内容 具有可执行性的，行政机关可以向人民法院申请强制执行。

**Article 24** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization fails to perform its obligations in accordance with the provisions of an administrative agreement and, after a reminder, still fails to perform, the administrative organ may make a written decision requiring it to perform the agreement. If the citizen, legal person, or other organization, after receiving the written decision, does not apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action within the statutory period and still fails to perform, and the content of the agreement is enforceable, the administrative organ may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.  
Where laws or administrative regulations provide that an administrative organ has the authority to supervise the performance of an administrative agreement, and a citizen, legal person, or other organization fails to perform its obligations in accordance with the agreement and, after a reminder, still fails to perform, the administrative organ may make a handling decision according to law. If the citizen, legal person, or other organization, after receiving the handling decision, does not apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action within the statutory period and still fails to perform, and the content of the agreement is enforceable, the administrative organ may apply to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 公民、法人或者其他组织对行政机关不依法履行、未按照约定履行行政协议提起诉讼的，诉讼时效参照民事法律规范确定；对行政机关变更、解除行政协议等行政行为提起诉讼的，起诉期限依照行政诉讼法及其司法解释确定。

**Article 25** Where a citizen, legal person, or any other organization institutes an action against an administrative organ for failing to perform the administrative agreement according to law or not in accordance with the agreement, the limitation of action shall be determined with reference to civil legal norms; where an action is instituted against an administrative act such as modifying or terminating an administrative agreement, the time period for prosecution shall be determined in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law and its judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 行政协议约定仲裁条款的，人民法院应当确认该条款无效，但法 律、行政法规或者我国缔结、参加的国际条约另有规定的除外。

**Article 26** Where an administrative agreement contains an arbitration clause, the People's Court shall confirm that such clause is void, unless otherwise provided by laws, administrative regulations, or international treaties concluded or acceded to by China.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 人民法院审理行政协议案件，应当适用行政诉讼法的规定；行政诉讼法没有规定的，参照适用民事诉讼法的规定。  
人民法院审理行政协议案件，可以参照适用民事法律规范关于民事合同的相关规定。

**Article 27** When trying a case concerning an administrative agreement, the People's Court shall apply the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law; where the Administrative Procedure Law has no provisions, the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law shall apply mutatis mutandis.  
When trying a case concerning an administrative agreement, the People's Court may, mutatis mutandis, apply the relevant provisions of civil legal norms on civil contracts.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 2015 年 5月1日后订立的行政协议发生纠纷的，适用行政诉讼法及本规定。  
2015 年 5 月 1 日前订立的行政协议发 生纠纷的，适用当时的法律、行政法规及司法解释。

**Article 28** Where a dispute arises from an administrative agreement concluded after May 1, 2015, the Administrative Procedure Law and these Provisions shall apply.  
Where a dispute arises from an administrative agreement concluded before May 1, 2015, the laws, administrative regulations, and judicial interpretations in force at that time shall apply.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 本规定自 2020 年 1 月 1 日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，适用本规定。

**Article 29** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2020. Where the judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall apply.
