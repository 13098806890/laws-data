# 最高人民法院关于审理反倾销行政案件应用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕35号  
**公布日期**：2002-09-11  
**生效日期**：2003-01-01

> （2002年9月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1242次会议通过，自2003年1月1日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕35号 为依法公正地审理反倾销行政案件，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》及其他有关法律的规定，制定本规定。 第一条 人民法院依法受理对下列反倾销行政行为提起的行政诉讼： （一）有关倾销及倾销幅度、损害及损害程度的终裁决定； （二）有关是否征收反倾销税的决定以及追溯征收、退税、对新出口经营者征税的决定； （三）有关保留、修改或者取消反倾销税以及价格承诺的复审决定； （四）依照法律、行政法规规定可以起诉的其他反倾销行政行为。 第二条 与反倾销行政行为具有法律上利害关系的个人或者组织为利害关系人，可以依照行政诉讼法及其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，向人民法院提起行政诉讼。 前款所称利害关系人，是指向国务院主管部门提出反倾销调查书面申请的申请人，有关出口经营者和进口经营者及其他具有法律上利害关系的自然人、法人或者其他组织。 第三条 反倾销行政案件的被告，应当是作出相应被诉反倾销行政行为的国务院主管部门。 第四条 与被诉反倾销行政行为具有法律上利害关系的其他国务院主管部门，可以作为第三人参加诉讼。 第五条 第一审反倾销行政案件由下列人民法院管辖： （一）被告所在地高级人民法院指定的中级人民法院； （二）被告所在地高级人民法院。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 人民法院依法受理对下列反倾销行政行为提起的行政诉讼：  
（一）有关倾销及倾销幅度、损害及损害程度的终裁决定；  
（二）有关是否征收反倾销税的决定以及追溯征收、退税、对新出口经营者征税的决定；  
（三）有关保留、修改或者取消反倾销税以及价格承诺的复审决定；  
（四）依照法律、行政法规规定可以起诉的其他反倾销行政行为。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall, according to law, accept administrative actions initiated against the following anti-dumping administrative actions:  
(1) Final determinations on dumping and dumping margin, injury and injury degree;  
(2) Decisions on whether to levy anti-dumping duties and decisions on retroactive levy, refund, and levy on new exporters;  
(3) Review decisions on maintaining, modifying or revoking anti-dumping duties and price undertakings;  
(4) Other anti-dumping administrative actions that may be prosecuted in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 与反倾销行政行为具有法律上利害关系的个人或者组织为利害关系人，可以依照行政诉讼法及其他有关法律、行政法规的规定，向人民法院提起行政诉讼。  
前款所称利害关系人，是指向国务院主管部门提出反倾销调查书面申请的申请人，有关出口经营者和进口经营者及其他具有法律上利害关系的自然人、法人或者其他组织。

**Article 2** Any individual or organization that has a legal interest in an anti-dumping administrative action shall be an interested party and may, in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, initiate an administrative action with the People's Court.  
The interested parties mentioned in the preceding paragraph shall refer to the applicant who files a written application for anti-dumping investigation with the competent department of the State Council, relevant export operators and import operators, and other natural persons, legal persons or other organizations that have a legal interest.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 反倾销行政案件的被告，应当是作出相应被诉反倾销行政行为的国务院主管部门。

**Article 3** The defendant in an anti-dumping administrative case shall be the competent department of the State Council that made the corresponding challenged anti-dumping administrative action.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 与被诉反倾销行政行为具有法律上利害关系的其他国务院主管部门，可以作为第三人参加诉讼。

**Article 4** Other competent departments of the State Council that have a legal interest in the challenged anti-dumping administrative action may participate in the proceedings as a third party.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 第一审反倾销行政案件由下列人民法院管辖：  
（一）被告所在地高级人民法院指定的中级人民法院；  
（二）被告所在地高级人民法院。

**Article 5** First-instance anti-dumping administrative cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the following People's Courts:  
(1) The Intermediate People's Court designated by the Higher People's Court in the place where the defendant is located;  
(2) The Higher People's Court in the place where the defendant is located.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院依照行政诉讼法及其他有关反倾销的法律、行政法规，参照国务院部门规章，对被诉反倾销行政行为的事实问题和法律问题，进行合法性审查。

**Article 6** The People's Court shall, in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Law and other relevant anti-dumping laws and administrative regulations, and by referring to the departmental rules of the State Council, conduct legality review on the factual issues and legal issues of the challenged anti-dumping administrative action.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 被告对其作出的被诉反倾销行政行为负举证责任，应当提供作出反倾销行政行为的证据和所依据的规范性文件。  
人民法院依据被告的案卷记录审查被诉反倾销行政行为的合法性。  
被告在作出被诉反倾销行政行为时没有记入案卷的事实材料，不能作为认定该行为合法的根据。

**Article 7** The defendant shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the challenged anti-dumping administrative action it made, and shall provide the evidence and the normative documents on which the anti-dumping administrative action is based.  
The People's Court shall review the legality of the challenged anti-dumping administrative action based on the case record of the defendant.  
Factual materials not recorded in the case record at the time when the defendant made the challenged anti-dumping administrative action shall not serve as the basis for determining that the action is lawful.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 原告对其主张的事实有责任提供证据。  
经人民法院依照法定程序审查，原告提供的证据具有关联性、合法性和真实性的，可以作为定案的根据。  
被告在反倾销行政调查程序中依照法定程序要求原告提供证据，原告无正当理由拒不提供、不如实提供或者以其他方式严重妨碍调查，而在诉讼程序中提供的证据，人民法院不予采纳。

**Article 8** The plaintiff shall have the responsibility to provide evidence for the facts it claims.  
Where the evidence provided by the plaintiff, after review by the People's Court in accordance with statutory procedures, is relevant, lawful and authentic, it may be used as the basis for adjudication.  
Where the defendant, in the anti-dumping administrative investigation process, requires the plaintiff to provide evidence in accordance with statutory procedures, and the plaintiff, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide, fails to provide truthfully, or otherwise seriously obstructs the investigation, the evidence provided by the plaintiff in the litigation process shall not be admitted by the People's Court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 在反倾销行政调查程序中，利害关系人无正当理由拒不提供证据、不如实提供证据或者以其他方式严重妨碍调查的，国务院主管部门根据能够获得的证据得出的事实结论，可以认定为证据充分。

**Article 9** Where, in the anti-dumping administrative investigation process, an interested party, without justifiable reasons, refuses to provide evidence, fails to provide evidence truthfully, or otherwise seriously obstructs the investigation, the factual conclusion reached by the competent department of the State Council based on the evidence available may be deemed to have sufficient evidence.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院审理反倾销行政案件，根据不同情况，分别作出以下判决：  
（一）被诉反倾销行政行为证据确凿，适用法律、行政法规正确，符合法定程序的，判决维持；  
（二）被诉反倾销行政行为有下列情形之一的，判决撤销或者部分撤销，并可以判决被告重新作出反倾销行政行为：  
1、主要证据不足的；  
2、适用法律、行政法规错误的；  
3、违反法定程序的；  
4、超越职权的；  
5、滥用职权的。  
（三）依照法律或者司法解释规定作出的其他判决。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall, when trying an anti-dumping administrative case, render the following judgments according to different circumstances:  
(1) If the challenged anti-dumping administrative action is supported by conclusive evidence, correctly applies laws and administrative regulations, and conforms to statutory procedures, the judgment shall affirm it;  
(2) If the challenged anti-dumping administrative action falls under any of the following circumstances, the judgment shall revoke it or partially revoke it, and the court may also order the defendant to make a new anti-dumping administrative action:  
1. Insufficient primary evidence;  
2. Wrong application of laws and administrative regulations;  
3. Violation of statutory procedures;  
4. Ultra vires;  
5. Abuse of power.  
(3) Other judgments rendered in accordance with laws or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院审理反倾销行政案件，可以参照有关涉外民事诉讼程序的规定。

**Article 11** The People's Court may, when trying an anti-dumping administrative case, refer to the relevant provisions on civil procedure involving foreign elements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 本规定自2003年1月1日起实施。  
2002年11月21日

**Article 12** These Provisions shall come into effect as of January 1, 2003.
