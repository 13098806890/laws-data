# 最高人民法院关于审理房地产管理法施行前房地产开发经营案件若干问题的解答

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕2号  
**公布日期**：1995-01-01

> 关于审理房地产管理法施行前 房地产开发经营案件若干问题的解答 法发〔1996〕2号 《中华人民共和国城市房地产管理法》（以下简称房地产管理法）已于1995年1月1日起施行。房地产管理法施行后发生的房地产开发经营案件，应当严格按照房地产管理法的规定处理。房地产管理法施行前发生的房地产开发经营行为，在房地产管理法施行前或施行后诉讼到人民法院的，人民法院应当依据该行为发生时的有关法律、政策规定，在查明事实、分清是非的基础上，从实际情况出发，实事求是、合情合理地处理。现就各地人民法院审理房地产开发经营案件提出的一些问题，解答如下： 一、关于房地产开发经营者的资格问题 1、从事房地产的开发经营者，应当是具备企业法人条件、经工商行政管理部门登记并发给营业执照的房地产开发企业（含中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业和外资企业）。 2、不具备房地产开发经营资格的企业与他人签订的以房地产开发经营为内容的合同，一般应当认定无效，但在一审诉讼期间依法取得房地产开发经营资格的，可认定合同有效。 二、关于国有土地使用权的出让问题 3、国有土地使用权出让合同的出让方，依法是市、县人民政府土地管理部门。出让合同应由市、县人民政府土地管理部门与土地使用者签订，其他部门、组织和个人为出让方与他人签订的出让合同，应当认定无效。 4、出让合同出让的只能是经依法批准的国有土地使用权，对于出让集体土地使用权或未经依法批准的国有土地使用权的，应当认定合同无效。 5、出让合同出让的土地使用权未依法办理审批、登记手续的，一般应当认定合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间，对于出让集体土地使用权依法补办了征用手续转为国有土地，并依法补办了出让手续的，或者出让未经依法批准的国有土地使用权依法补办了审批、登记手续的，可认定合同有效。 三、关于国有土地使用权的转让问题 6、国有土地使用权的转让合同，转让的土地使用权未依法办理出让审批手续的，一般应当认定合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间，对于转让集体土地使用权，经有关主管部门批准补办了征用手续转为国有土地，并依法办理了出让手续的，或者转让未经依法批准的国有土地使用权依法补办了审批、登记手续的，可认定合同有效。 7、转让合同的转让方，应当是依法办理了土地使用权登记或变更登记手续，取得土地使用证的土地使用者。未取得土地使用证的土地使用者为转让方与他人签订的合同，一般应当认定无效，但转让方已按出让合同约定的期限和条件投资开发利用了土地，在一审诉讼期间，经有关主管部门批准，补办了土地使用权登记或变更登记手续的，可认定合同有效。 8、以出让方式取得土地使用权的土地使用者虽已取得土地使用证，但未按土地使用权出让合同约定的期限和条件对土地进行投资开发利用，与他人签订土地使用权转让合同的，一般应当认定合同无效；如土地使用者已投入一定资金，但尚未达到出让合同约定的期限和条件，与他人签订土地使用权转让合同，没有其他违法行为的，经有关主管部门认可，同意其转让的，可认定合同有效，责令当事人向有关主管部门补办土地使用权转让登记手续。

### 正文

一、关于房地产开发经营者的资格问题  
1、从事房地产的开发经营者，应当是具备企业法人条件、经工商行政管理部门登记并发给营业执照的房地产开发企业（含中外合资经营企业、中外合作经营企业和外资企业）。  
2、不具备房地产开发经营资格的企业与他人签订的以房地产开发经营为内容的合同，一般应当认定无效，但在一审诉讼期间依法取得房地产开发经营资格的，可认定合同有效。

**Article None** I. Issues concerning the qualifications of real estate development and operation entities  
1. Entities engaged in real estate development and operation shall be real estate development enterprises (including Sino-foreign equity joint ventures, Sino-foreign cooperative joint ventures, and wholly foreign-owned enterprises) that meet the conditions for a legal person status and have been registered with and issued a business license by the administrative department for industry and commerce.  
2. A contract for real estate development and operation entered into by an enterprise that does not possess the qualifications for real estate development and operation with another party shall generally be determined as void; however, if such enterprise lawfully obtains the qualifications for real estate development and operation during the first-instance litigation period, the contract may be determined as valid.

二、关于国有土地使用权的出让问题  
3、国有土地使用权出让合同的出让方，依法是市、县人民政府土地管理部门。出让合同应由市、县人民政府土地管理部门与土地使用者签订，其他部门、组织和个人为出让方与他人签订的出让合同，应当认定无效。  
4、出让合同出让的只能是经依法批准的国有土地使用权，对于出让集体土地使用权或未经依法批准的国有土地使用权的，应当认定合同无效。  
5、出让合同出让的土地使用权未依法办理审批、登记手续的，一般应当认定合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间，对于出让集体土地使用权依法补办了征用手续转为国有土地，并依法补办了出让手续的，或者出让未经依法批准的国有土地使用权依法补办了审批、登记手续的，可认定合同有效。

**Article None** II. Issues concerning the assignment of State-owned land use rights  
3. The assignor of a State-owned land use rights assignment contract shall, according to law, be the land administration department of the city or county people's government. An assignment contract shall be concluded by the land administration department of the city or county people's government with the land user. If any other department, organization or individual, acting as the assignor, concludes an assignment contract with another party, such contract shall be determined as void.  
4. The assignment contract may only assign State-owned land use rights that have been approved according to law. If the assignment involves collective land use rights or State-owned land use rights that have not been approved according to law, the contract shall be determined as void.  
5. If the land use rights assigned under the assignment contract have not undergone the procedures of examination, approval and registration according to law, the contract shall generally be determined as void; however, during the first-instance litigation period, if for the assignment of collective land use rights, the expropriation procedures have been lawfully completed to convert them into State-owned land and the assignment procedures have also been lawfully completed, or if for the assignment of State-owned land use rights that have not been approved according to law, the examination, approval and registration procedures have been lawfully completed, the contract may be determined as valid.

三、关于国有土地使用权的转让问题  
6、国有土地使用权的转让合同，转让的土地使用权未依法办理出让审批手续的，一般应当认定合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间，对于转让集体土地使用权，经有关主管部门批准补办了征用手续转为国有土地，并依法办理了出让手续的，或者转让未经依法批准的国有土地使用权依法补办了审批、登记手续的，可认定合同有效。  
7、转让合同的转让方，应当是依法办理了土地使用权登记或变更登记手续，取得土地使用证的土地使用者。未取得土地使用证的土地使用者为转让方与他人签订的合同，一般应当认定无效，但转让方已按出让合同约定的期限和条件投资开发利用了土地，在一审诉讼期间，经有关主管部门批准，补办了土地使用权登记或变更登记手续的，可认定合同有效。  
8、以出让方式取得土地使用权的土地使用者虽已取得土地使用证，但未按土地使用权出让合同约定的期限和条件对土地进行投资开发利用，与他人签订土地使用权转让合同的，一般应当认定合同无效；如土地使用者已投入一定资金，但尚未达到出让合同约定的期限和条件，与他人签订土地使用权转让合同，没有其他违法行为的，经有关主管部门认可，同意其转让的，可认定合同有效，责令当事人向有关主管部门补办土地使用权转让登记手续。  
9、享有土地使用权的土地使用者未按照项目建设的要求对土地进行开发建设，也未办理审批手续和土地使用权转让手续，转让建设项目的，一般应当认定项目转让和土地使用权转让的合同无效；如符合土地使用权转让条件的，可认定项目转让合同有效，责令当事人补办土地使用权转让登记手续。  
10、以转让方式取得的土地使用权的使用年限，应当是土地使用权出让合同约定的使用年限减去原土地使用者已使用的年限后的剩余年限。转让合同约定的土地使用年限超过剩余年限的，其超过部分无效。土地使用年限，一般应从当事人办理土地使用权登记或变更登记手续，取得土地使用证的次日起算，或者在合同中约定土地使用年限的起算时间。  
11、土地使用权转让合同擅自改变土地使用权出让合同约定的土地用途的，一般应当认定合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间已补办批准手续的，可认定合同有效。  
12、转让合同签订后，双方当事人应按合同约定和法律规定，到有关主管部门办理土地使用权变更登记手续，一方拖延不办，并以未办理土地使用权变更登记手续为由主张合同无效的，人民法院不予支持，应责令当事人依法办理土地使用权变更登记手续。  
13、土地使用者与他人签订土地使用权转让合同后，未办理土地使用权变更登记手续之前，又另与他人就同一土地使用权签订转让合同，并依法办理了土地使用权变更登记手续的，土地使用权应由办理土地使用权变更登记手续的受让方取得。转让方给前一合同的受让方造成损失的，应当承担相应的民事责任。  
14、土地使用者就同一土地使用权分别与几方签订土地使用权转让合同，均未办理土地使用权变更登记手续的，一般应当认定各合同无效；如其中某一合同的受让方已实际占有和使用土地，并对土地投资开发利用的，经有关主管部门同意，补办了土地使用权变更登记手续的，可认定该合同有效。转让方给其他合同的受让方造成损失的，应当承担相应的民事责任。

**Article None** III. Issues concerning the transfer of State-owned land use rights  
6. In a contract for the transfer of State-owned land use rights, if the transferred land use rights have not undergone the assignment examination and approval procedures according to law, the contract shall generally be determined as void; however, during the first-instance litigation period, if for the transfer of collective land use rights, the expropriation procedures have been completed with the approval of the relevant competent authority to convert them into State-owned land and the assignment procedures have been lawfully completed, or if for the transfer of State-owned land use rights that have not been approved according to law, the examination, approval and registration procedures have been lawfully completed, the contract may be determined as valid.  
7. The transferor in a transfer contract shall be the land user who has lawfully completed the land use rights registration or alteration registration procedures and obtained the land use certificate. If a land user who has not obtained the land use certificate, acting as the transferor, concludes a contract with another party, such contract shall generally be determined as void; however, if the transferor has invested in and developed the land in accordance with the time limit and conditions stipulated in the assignment contract, and during the first-instance litigation period, with the approval of the relevant competent authority, has completed the land use rights registration or alteration registration procedures, the contract may be determined as valid.  
8. If a land user who has obtained the land use rights by means of assignment and has obtained the land use certificate, but has not invested in and developed the land in accordance with the time limit and conditions stipulated in the land use rights assignment contract, enters into a land use rights transfer contract with another party, such contract shall generally be determined as void; if the land user has invested a certain amount of funds but has not yet reached the time limit and conditions stipulated in the assignment contract, and has entered into a land use rights transfer contract with another party without any other illegal acts, and the relevant competent authority has acknowledged and consented to the transfer, the contract may be determined as valid, and the parties shall be ordered to complete the land use rights transfer registration procedures with the relevant competent authority.  
9. If a land user who enjoys the land use rights fails to develop and construct the land in accordance with the requirements of the project construction, and also fails to complete the examination and approval procedures and the land use rights transfer procedures, and transfers the construction project, the contract for the transfer of the project and the land use rights shall generally be determined as void; if the conditions for the transfer of land use rights are met, the contract for the transfer of the project may be determined as valid, and the parties shall be ordered to complete the land use rights transfer registration procedures.  
10. The term of use of land use rights obtained by means of transfer shall be the remaining term after deducting the term already used by the original land user from the term of use stipulated in the land use rights assignment contract. If the term of land use stipulated in the transfer contract exceeds the remaining term, the excess part shall be void. The term of land use shall generally be calculated from the day following the date when the party completes the land use rights registration or alteration registration procedures and obtains the land use certificate, or the starting time of the term of land use may be stipulated in the contract.  
11. If a land use rights transfer contract, without authorization, changes the purpose of land use stipulated in the land use rights assignment contract, the contract shall generally be determined as void; however, if the approval procedures have been completed during the first-instance litigation period, the contract may be determined as valid.  
12. After a transfer contract is concluded, both parties shall complete the land use rights alteration registration procedures with the relevant competent authority in accordance with the contract terms and legal provisions. If one party delays in doing so and claims that the contract is void on the ground that the land use rights alteration registration procedures have not been completed, the People's Court shall not support such claim and shall order the parties to lawfully complete the land use rights alteration registration procedures.  
13. If, after entering into a land use rights transfer contract with another party but before completing the land use rights alteration registration procedures, a land user enters into another transfer contract with a different party for the same land use rights and lawfully completes the land use rights alteration registration procedures, the land use rights shall be acquired by the transferee who has completed the land use rights alteration registration procedures. If the transferor causes losses to the transferee under the earlier contract, it shall bear the corresponding civil liability.  
14. If a land user enters into land use rights transfer contracts with several parties separately for the same land use rights and none of them have completed the land use rights alteration registration procedures, each contract shall generally be determined as void; if, under one of such contracts, the transferee has actually taken possession and used the land, and has invested in and developed the land, and with the consent of the relevant competent authority has completed the land use rights alteration registration procedures, that contract may be determined as valid. If the transferor causes losses to the transferees under the other contracts, it shall bear the corresponding civil liability.

四、关于国有土地使用权的抵押问题  
15、土地使用者未办理土地使用权抵押登记手续，将土地使用权进行抵押的，应当认定抵押合同无效。  
16、土地使用者未办理土地使用权抵押登记手续将土地使用权抵押后，又与他人就同一土地使用权签订抵押合同，并办理了抵押登记手续的，应当认定后一个抵押合同有效。  
17、以划拨方式取得的国有土地使用权为标的物签订的抵押合同，一般应当认定无效，但在一审诉讼期间，经有关主管部门批准，依法补办了出让手续的，可认定合同有效。

**Article None** IV. Issues concerning the mortgage of State-owned land use rights  
15. If a land user mortgages the land use rights without completing the land use rights mortgage registration procedures, the mortgage contract shall be determined as void.  
16. If, after mortgaging the land use rights without completing the land use rights mortgage registration procedures, a land user enters into a mortgage contract with another party for the same land use rights and completes the mortgage registration procedures, the later mortgage contract shall be determined as valid.  
17. A mortgage contract that takes the State-owned land use rights obtained by means of allocation as the subject matter shall generally be determined as void; however, during the first-instance litigation period, if the assignment procedures have been lawfully completed with the approval of the relevant competent authority, the contract may be determined as valid.

五、关于以国有土地使用权投资合作建房问题  
18、享有土地使用权的一方以土地使用权作为投资与他人合作建房，签订的合建合同是土地使用权有偿转让的一种特殊形式，除办理合建审批手续外，还应依法办理土地使用权变更登记手续。未办理土地使用权变更登记手续的，一般应当认定合建合同无效，但双方已实际履行了合同，或房屋已基本建成，又无其他违法行为的，可认定合建合同有效，并责令当事人补办土地使用权变更登记手续。  
19、当事人签订合建合同，依法办理了合建审批手续和土地使用权变更登记手续的，不因合建一方没有房地产开发经营权而认定合同无效。  
20、以划拨方式取得国有土地使用权的一方，在《中华人民共和国城镇国有土地使用权出让和转让暂行条例》（以下简称《条例》）施行前，经有关主管部门批准，以其使用的土地作为投资与他人合作建房的，可认定合建合同有效。  
21、《条例》施行后，以划拨方式取得国有土地使用权的一方未办理土地使用权出让手续，以其土地使用权作为投资与他人合建房屋的，应认定合建合同无效，但在一审诉讼期间，经有关主管部门批准，依法补办了出让手续的，可认定合同有效。  
22、名为合作建房，实为土地使用权转让的合同，可按合同实际性质处理。如土地使用权的转让符合法律规定的，可认定合同有效，不因以合作建房为名而认定合同无效。  
23、合建合同对房地产权属有约定的，按合同约定确认权属；约定不明确的，可依据双方投资以及对房屋管理使用等情况，确认土地使用权和房屋所有权的权属。

**Article None** V. Issues concerning the investment of State-owned land use rights in cooperative building construction  
18. Where a party enjoying the land use rights uses such land use rights as an investment to cooperate with another party in building construction, the joint construction contract concluded is a special form of paid transfer of land use rights. In addition to completing the joint construction examination and approval procedures, the parties shall also complete the land use rights alteration registration procedures according to law. If the land use rights alteration registration procedures have not been completed, the joint construction contract shall generally be determined as void; however, if the parties have actually performed the contract, or the house has been substantially completed, and there are no other illegal acts, the joint construction contract may be determined as valid, and the parties shall be ordered to complete the land use rights alteration registration procedures.  
19. Where a joint construction contract is concluded and the parties have lawfully completed the joint construction examination and approval procedures and the land use rights alteration registration procedures, the contract shall not be determined as void merely because one of the joint construction parties does not have the right to engage in real estate development and operation.  
20. Where a party that obtained the State-owned land use rights by means of allocation, before the promulgation of the Interim Regulations of the People's Republic of China Concerning the Assignment and Transfer of the Right to the Use of the State-owned Land in Cities and Towns (hereinafter referred to as the "Regulations"), with the approval of the relevant competent authority, uses the land it uses as an investment to cooperate with another party in building construction, the joint construction contract may be determined as valid.  
21. After the promulgation of the Regulations, where a party that obtained the State-owned land use rights by means of allocation fails to complete the land use rights assignment procedures and uses its land use rights as an investment to cooperate with another party in building construction, the joint construction contract shall be determined as void; however, during the first-instance litigation period, if the assignment procedures have been lawfully completed with the approval of the relevant competent authority, the contract may be determined as valid.  
22. A contract that is named as cooperative building construction but is in essence a transfer of land use rights may be dealt with according to its actual nature. If the transfer of land use rights complies with the legal provisions, the contract may be determined as valid and shall not be determined as void merely because it is named as cooperative building construction.  
23. If a joint construction contract stipulates the ownership of the real estate property rights, such ownership shall be confirmed according to the contract; if the stipulation is unclear, the ownership of the land use rights and the ownership of the house may be confirmed based on such circumstances as the investment of the parties and the management and use of the house.

六、关于商品房的预售问题  
24、商品房的预售方，没有取得土地使用证，也没有投入开发建设资金进行施工建设，预售商品房的，应当认定商品房预售合同无效。  
25、商品房的预售方，没有取得土地使用证，但投入一定的开发建设资金，进行了施工建设，预售商品房的，在一审诉讼期间补办了土地使用证、商品房预售许可证明的，可认定预售合同有效。  
26、商品房的预售方，持有土地使用证，也投入一定的开发建设资金，进行了施工建设，预售商品房的，在一审诉讼期间办理了预售许可证明的，可认定预售合同有效。  
27、预售商品房合同签订后，预购方尚未取得房屋所有权证之前，预售方未经预购方同意，又就同一预售商品房与他人签订预售合同的，应认定后一个预售合同无效；如后一个合同的预购方已取得房屋所有权证的，可认定后一个合同有效，但预售方给前一个合同的预购方造成损失的，应承担相应的民事责任。

**Article None** VI. Issues concerning the pre-sale of commercial housing  
24. If a pre-seller of commercial housing, without having obtained the land use certificate and without having invested in development and construction funds to carry out construction activities, pre-sells commercial housing, the commercial housing pre-sale contract shall be determined as void.  
25. If a pre-seller of commercial housing, without having obtained the land use certificate but having invested a certain amount of development and construction funds and having carried out construction activities, pre-sells commercial housing, and during the first-instance litigation period, completes the procedures for the land use certificate and the commercial housing pre-sale permit, the pre-sale contract may be determined as valid.  
26. If a pre-seller of commercial housing, holding the land use certificate and having invested a certain amount of development and construction funds and having carried out construction activities, pre-sells commercial housing, and during the first-instance litigation period, completes the procedures for the pre-sale permit, the pre-sale contract may be determined as valid.  
27. After a pre-sale contract for commercial housing is concluded, if the pre-seller, without the consent of the pre-purchaser and before the pre-purchaser has obtained the house ownership certificate, enters into a pre-sale contract with another party for the same pre-sold commercial housing, the later pre-sale contract shall be determined as void; if the pre-purchaser under the later contract has already obtained the house ownership certificate, the later contract may be determined as valid, but if the pre-seller causes losses to the pre-purchaser under the earlier contract, it shall bear the corresponding civil liability.

七、关于预售商品房的转让问题  
28、商品房的预售合同无效的，预售商品房的转让合同，一般也应当认定无效。  
29、商品房预售合同的双方当事人，经有关主管部门办理了有关手续后，在预售商品房尚未实际交付前，预购方将购买的未竣工的预售商品房转让他人，办理了转让手续的，可认定转让合同有效；没有办理转让手续的，在一审诉讼期间补办了转让手续，也可认定转让合同有效。  
30、商品房预售合同的预购方，在实际取得预购房屋产权并持有房屋所有权证后，将房屋再转让给他人的，按一般房屋买卖关系处理。

**Article None** VII. Issues concerning the transfer of pre-sold commercial housing  
28. If the pre-sale contract for commercial housing is void, the contract for the transfer of the pre-sold commercial housing shall generally also be determined as void.  
29. After the parties to a commercial housing pre-sale contract have completed the relevant procedures with the relevant competent authority, and before the actual delivery of the pre-sold commercial housing, if the pre-purchaser transfers the purchased unfinished pre-sold commercial housing to another party and has completed the transfer procedures, the transfer contract may be determined as valid; if the transfer procedures have not been completed but are completed during the first-instance litigation period, the transfer contract may also be determined as valid.  
30. After the pre-purchaser under a commercial housing pre-sale contract has actually obtained the property right of the pre-purchased house and holds the house ownership certificate, if the pre-purchaser transfers the house to another party, such transfer shall be dealt with in accordance with the ordinary house sale relationship.

八、关于预售商品房的价格问题  
31、预售商品房的价格，除国家规定“微利房”、“解困房”等必须执行国家定价的以外，合同双方根据房地产市场行情约定的价格，也应当予以保护。一方以政府调整与房地产有关的税费为由要求变更合同约定价格的，可予以支持。一方以建筑材料或商品房的市场价格变化等为由，要求变更合同约定的价格或解除合同的，一般不予支持。  
32、合同双方约定了预售商品房价格，同时又约定了预售商品房的价格以有关主管部门的核定价格为准，一方要求按核定价格变更预售商品房价格的，应予以准许。  
33、合同双方约定的预售商品房价格不明确，或者在合同履行中发生不可抗力的情况，合同双方当事人可另行协商预售商品房价格。协商不成的，可参照当地政府公布的价格、房地产部门认可的评估的价格，或者当地同期同类同质房屋的市场价格处理。  
34、在逾期交付房屋的期间，因预售商品房价格变化造成的损失，由过错方承担。

**Article None** VIII. Issues concerning the price of pre-sold commercial housing  
31. Except for cases where the state stipulates that "low-profit housing" or "housing for hardship relief" must implement the state-fixed price, the price agreed upon by the parties to the contract based on the real estate market conditions shall also be protected. If one party requests a change in the contractually agreed price on the ground that the government has adjusted taxes and fees related to real estate, such request may be supported. If a party requests a change in the contractually agreed price or rescission of the contract on the ground of changes in the market price of building materials or commercial housing, such request shall generally not be supported.  
32. If the parties to the contract have agreed upon the price of the pre-sold commercial housing and have also agreed that the price of the pre-sold commercial housing shall be subject to the verified price of the relevant competent authority, and one party requests a change in the price of the pre-sold commercial housing according to the verified price, such request shall be permitted.  
33. If the price of the pre-sold commercial housing agreed upon by the parties is unclear, or if force majeure occurs during the performance of the contract, the parties may renegotiate the price of the pre-sold commercial housing. If negotiation fails, the price may be handled by reference to the price published by the local government, the appraised price recognized by the real estate department, or the market price of similar housing of the same quality in the same area during the same period.  
34. During the period of delayed delivery of the house, losses caused by changes in the price of the pre-sold commercial housing shall be borne by the party at fault.

九、关于违反合同的责任  
35、经审查认定有效的合同，双方当事人应按照合同的约定或法律的规定履行。  
36、在合同履行过程中，由于不可抗力的原因，致使合同难以继续履行或继续履行将给一方造成重大损失，当事人提出变更或解除合同的，应予支持。因此造成的损失，由当事人双方合理负担。  
37、当事人以对合同内容有重大误解或合同内容显失公平为由，提出变更合同的，应予支持。但因下列情形之一要求变更合同的，不予支持：  
（1）合同约定的出资额、价格虽与当时的市场行情有所不同，但差别不大，一方当事人以缺乏经验不了解市场行情等为由，提出变更合同的。  
（2）合同履行的结果不是合同签订时不能预见的，而是因当事人经营不善、管理不当或判断失误等原因造成的，一方当事人提出变更合同的。  
38、合同一方有充分证据证明确系不可抗力，致使合同不能按期履行或不能完全履行的，根据实际情况，可准予延期履行、部分履行或不履行，并部分或全部免予承担违约责任。  
39、合同一方违反合同，应向对方支付违约金。合同对违约金有具体约定的，应按约定的数额支付违约金。约定的违约金数额一般以不超过合同未履行部分的价金总额为限。对违约金无约定或约定不明确的，按没有约定处理。  
40、合同一方违反合同给对方造成损失，支付违约金不足以赔偿的，还应赔偿造成的损失与违约金的差额部分。  
41、合同一方违约致使合同无法履行的，应赔偿对方的损失。实际损失无法确定的，可参照违约方所获利润确定赔偿金额。  
42、合同约定了违约金和罚款的，或只约定罚款的，只要其金额不超过未履行部分总额的，可将罚款视为违约金处理。  
43、合同一方未将对方的投资款用于履行合同而挪作他用，致使合同不能履行的，依法应承担违约责任，赔偿因不履行给对方造成的实际损失。  
44、违约方将对方的投资款挪作他用并获利的，如所获利润高于或等同于对方实际损失的，应将其所获利润作为对方的损失予以赔偿；如所获利润低于对方的实际损失的，应当赔偿对方的实际损失；如违约方所获利润无法确定的，可按银行同类贷款利率的四倍赔偿对方的损失。

**Article None** IX. Issues concerning liability for breach of contract  
35. The parties to a contract that has been determined as valid after examination shall perform the contract in accordance with the contract terms or legal provisions.  
36. During the performance of the contract, if the contract becomes difficult to continue to perform or if continued performance would cause significant losses to one party due to force majeure, and a party proposes to modify or dissolve the contract, such proposal shall be supported. The losses caused thereby shall be reasonably borne by both parties.  
37. If a party proposes to modify the contract on the ground of a material misunderstanding of the contract content or that the contract content is manifestly unfair, such proposal shall be supported. However, a request to modify the contract on any of the following grounds shall not be supported:  
(1) The amount of capital contribution or price stipulated in the contract is somewhat different from the market conditions at the time, but the difference is not significant, and one party proposes to modify the contract on the ground of lack of experience or unfamiliarity with the market conditions.  
(2) The result of contract performance is not unforeseeable at the time of contract conclusion, but is caused by reasons such as the party's poor management, improper administration, or misjudgment, and one party proposes to modify the contract.  
38. If one party has sufficient evidence to prove that it is indeed force majeure that makes it impossible to perform the contract on time or to perform it completely, according to the actual circumstances, an extension of performance, partial performance, or non-performance may be granted, and liability for breach of contract may be partially or fully exempted.  
39. If one party breaches the contract, it shall pay liquidated damages to the other party. If the contract has specific provisions on liquidated damages, the liquidated damages shall be paid in the amount stipulated. The amount of liquidated damages stipulated shall generally be limited to not exceeding the total price of the unperformed part of the contract. If there is no agreement on liquidated damages or the agreement is unclear, it shall be treated as if there is no agreement.  
40. If one party breaches the contract and causes losses to the other party, and the payment of liquidated damages is insufficient to compensate the losses, the difference between the losses caused and the liquidated damages shall also be compensated.  
41. If one party's breach of contract makes the contract impossible to perform, it shall compensate the other party for its losses. If the actual losses cannot be determined, the amount of compensation may be determined by reference to the profits obtained by the breaching party.  
42. If the contract stipulates both liquidated damages and a fine, or only a fine, as long as the amount does not exceed the total amount of the unperformed part, the fine may be treated as liquidated damages.  
43. If one party fails to use the other party's investment funds for the performance of the contract and diverts them to other purposes, causing the contract to be impossible to perform, it shall, according to law, bear liability for breach of contract and compensate the other party for the actual losses caused by the non-performance.  
44. If the breaching party diverts the other party's investment funds to other purposes and makes a profit, if the profit obtained is higher than or equal to the actual losses of the other party, the profit obtained shall be compensated to the other party as its losses; if the profit obtained is lower than the actual losses of the other party, the actual losses of the other party shall be compensated; if the profit obtained by the breaching party cannot be determined, the losses of the other party may be compensated at four times the interest rate of the same type of bank loan.

十、关于无效合同的处理问题  
45、经审查认定无效的合同，一方依据无效合同取得的财产应当返还对方。因合同无效给对方造成损失的，应按过错责任原则由过错方赔偿损失。过错方承担赔偿责任的赔偿金数额，应相当于无过错方的实际损失。双方均有过错的，按过错责任大小各自承担相应的责任。双方故意严重违反有关法律、法规而致合同无效的，应追缴双方已经取得或约定取得的财产。  
46、合作建房合同被确认无效后，在建或已建成的房屋，其所有权可确认归以土地使用权作为投资的一方所有，对方的投资可根据资金的转化形态，分别处理：  
（1）资金尚未投入实际建设的，可由以土地使用权作为投资的一方将对方投入的资金予以返还，并支付同期同类银行贷款的利息；  
（2）资金已转化为在建中的建筑物，并有一定增值的，可在返还投资款的同时，参照当地房地产业的利润情况，由以土地使用权作为投资的一方给予对方相应比例的经济赔偿；  
（3）房屋已建成的，可将约定出资方应分得的房产份额按现行市价估值或出资方实际出资占房屋造价的比例，认定出资方的经济损失，由以土地使用权作为投资的一方给予赔偿。  
47、预售商品房因预售方的过错造成合同无效的，应根据房地产市场价格变化和预购方交付房款等情况，由预售方承担返还财产、赔偿损失的责任。房屋未建成或未交付的，参照签订合同时的房价和法院裁判、调解时的房价之间的差价，确定预购方的损失数额。

**Article None** X. Issues concerning the handling of void contracts  
45. If a contract has been determined as void after examination, the property obtained by one party under the void contract shall be returned to the other party. If losses are caused to the other party due to the voidness of the contract, the party at fault shall compensate for the losses in accordance with the principle of fault liability. The amount of compensation payable by the party at fault shall be equivalent to the actual losses of the party without fault. If both parties are at fault, each party shall bear corresponding liability according to the degree of fault. If both parties intentionally and seriously violate relevant laws and regulations, causing the contract to be void, the property that has been obtained or agreed to be obtained by them shall be confiscated.  
46. After a cooperative building construction contract has been determined as void, the ownership of the house under construction or the completed house may be determined as belonging to the party that invested the land use rights. The investment of the other party may be dealt with separately according to the form of transformation of the funds:  
(1) If the funds have not yet been put into actual construction, the party that invested the land use rights may return the funds invested by the other party and pay interest at the same type of bank loan rate during the same period;  
(2) If the funds have been converted into a building under construction with a certain appreciation, in addition to returning the investment funds, the party that invested the land use rights shall provide economic compensation to the other party in the corresponding proportion by reference to the profit situation of the local real estate industry;  
(3) If the house has been completed, the economic losses of the investing party may be determined by appraising the share of the house property that the contractually investing party should have received at the current market price or by the ratio of the investing party's actual investment to the cost of the house, and the party that invested the land use rights shall provide compensation.  
47. If the pre-sale contract for commercial housing is void due to the fault of the pre-seller, the pre-seller shall bear the liability for returning the property and compensating the losses in light of changes in the real estate market price and the pre-purchaser's payment of the house price, among other circumstances. If the house has not been completed or delivered, the amount of the pre-purchaser's losses shall be determined by reference to the difference between the house price at the time of signing the contract and the house price at the time of the court's judgment or mediation.
