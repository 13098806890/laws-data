# 最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：行政法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕22号  
**公布日期**：2021-11-29  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定》已于2021年11月29日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1854次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月27日 最高人民法院 关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令 保全措施的若干规定 法释〔2021〕22号 为妥善审理生态环境侵权案件，及时有效保护生态环境，维护民事主体合法权益，落实保护优先、预防为主原则，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国环境保护法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等有关法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条　申请人以被申请人正在实施或者即将实施污染环境、破坏生态行为，不及时制止将使申请人合法权益或者生态环境受到难以弥补的损害为由，依照民事诉讼法第一百条、第一百零一条规定，向人民法院申请采取禁止令保全措施，责令被申请人立即停止一定行为的，人民法院应予受理。 第二条　因污染环境、破坏生态行为受到损害的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，以及民法典第一千二百三十四条、第一千二百三十五条规定的“国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织”，可以向人民法院申请作出禁止令。 第三条　申请人提起生态环境侵权诉讼时或者诉讼过程中，向人民法院申请作出禁止令的，人民法院应当在接受申请后五日内裁定是否准予。情况紧急的，人民法院应当在接受申请后四十八小时内作出。 因情况紧急，申请人可在提起诉讼前向污染环境、破坏生态行为实施地、损害结果发生地或者被申请人住所地等对案件有管辖权的人民法院申请作出禁止令，人民法院应当在接受申请后四十八小时内裁定是否准予。 第四条　申请人向人民法院申请作出禁止令的，应当提交申请书和相应的证明材料。 申请书应当载明下列事项： （一）申请人与被申请人的身份、送达地址、联系方式等基本情况； （二）申请禁止的内容、范围；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　申请人以被申请人正在实施或者即将实施污染环境、破坏生态行为，不及时制止将使申请人合法权益或者生态环境受到难以弥补的损害为由，依照[民事诉讼法第一百条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-100)、第一百零一条规定，向人民法院申请采取禁止令保全措施，责令被申请人立即停止一定行为的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 1** Where an applicant, on the grounds that the respondent is committing or is about to commit an act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction and that failure to stop such act in a timely manner will cause irreparable damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or the ecological environment, applies to the People's Court for adopting an injunction preservation measure in accordance with Articles 100 and 101 of the Civil Procedure Law, ordering the respondent to immediately cease a specific act, the People's Court shall accept the application.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因污染环境、破坏生态行为受到损害的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，以及[民法典第一千二百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-1234)、第一千二百三十五条规定的“国家规定的机关或者法律规定的组织”，可以向人民法院申请作出禁止令。

**Article 2** Natural persons, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations that have suffered damage due to acts of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, as well as the "organs prescribed by the State or organizations prescribed by law" as specified in Articles 1,234 and 1,235 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, may apply to the People's Court for the issuance of an injunction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请人提起生态环境侵权诉讼时或者诉讼过程中，向人民法院申请作出禁止令的，人民法院应当在接受申请后五日内裁定是否准予。情况紧急的，人民法院应当在接受申请后四十八小时内作出。  
因情况紧急，申请人可在提起诉讼前向污染环境、破坏生态行为实施地、损害结果发生地或者被申请人住所地等对案件有管辖权的人民法院申请作出禁止令，人民法院应当在接受申请后四十八小时内裁定是否准予。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法律/最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 3** Where an applicant applies to the People's Court for the issuance of an injunction when instituting an action concerning ecological environment infringement or during the course of the action, the People's Court shall rule on whether to grant the application within five days after accepting the application. In urgent circumstances, the People's Court shall make the ruling within forty-eight hours after accepting the application.  
In urgent circumstances, the applicant may, before instituting the action, apply to the People's Court that has jurisdiction over the case, such as the People's Court of the place where the environmental pollution or ecological destruction act is committed, the place where the harm results occur, or the domicile of the respondent, for the issuance of an injunction, and the People's Court shall rule on whether to grant the application within forty-eight hours after accepting the application.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　申请人向人民法院申请作出禁止令的，应当提交申请书和相应的证明材料。  
申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）申请人与被申请人的身份、送达地址、联系方式等基本情况；  
（二）申请禁止的内容、范围；  
（三）被申请人正在实施或者即将实施污染环境、破坏生态行为，以及如不及时制止将使申请人合法权益或者生态环境受到难以弥补损害的情形；  
（四）提供担保的财产信息，或者不需要提供担保的理由。

**Article 4** An applicant applying to the People's Court for the issuance of an injunction shall submit a written application and corresponding evidentiary materials.  
The written application shall set forth the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the applicant and the respondent, including their identity, service address, and contact information;  
(2) The content and scope of the injunction sought;  
(3) The fact that the respondent is committing or is about to commit an act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction, and that failure to stop such act in a timely manner will cause irreparable damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or the ecological environment;  
(4) Information on the property provided as security, or the reasons for not providing security.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　被申请人污染环境、破坏生态行为具有现实而紧迫的重大风险，如不及时制止将对申请人合法权益或者生态环境造成难以弥补损害的，人民法院应当综合考量以下因素决定是否作出禁止令：  
（一）被申请人污染环境、破坏生态行为被行政主管机关依法处理后仍继续实施；  
（二）被申请人污染环境、破坏生态行为对申请人合法权益或者生态环境造成的损害超过禁止被申请人一定行为对其合法权益造成的损害；  
（三）禁止被申请人一定行为对国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益产生的不利影响；  
（四）其他应当考量的因素。

**Article 5** Where the respondent's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction poses a real and imminent major risk, and failure to stop it in a timely manner will cause irreparable damage to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or the ecological environment, the People's Court shall comprehensively consider the following factors in deciding whether to issue an injunction:  
(1) The respondent continues to commit the act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction after the competent administrative authority has handled it according to law;  
(2) The damage caused by the respondent's act of environmental pollution or ecological destruction to the applicant's lawful rights and interests or the ecological environment exceeds the damage that prohibiting the respondent from a specific act would cause to the respondent's lawful rights and interests;  
(3) The adverse impact of prohibiting the respondent from a specific act on the national interest, public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others;  
(4) Other factors that shall be considered.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院审查申请人禁止令申请，应当听取被申请人的意见。必要时，可进行现场勘查。  
情况紧急无法询问或者现场勘查的，人民法院应当在裁定准予申请人禁止令申请后四十八小时内听取被申请人的意见。被申请人意见成立的，人民法院应当裁定解除禁止令。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法律/最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 6** When examining the applicant's application for an injunction, the People's Court shall hear the respondent's opinions. If necessary, it may conduct an on-site inspection.  
If, due to urgent circumstances, it is impossible to hear the respondent or conduct an on-site inspection, the People's Court shall, within forty-eight hours after ruling to grant the applicant's application for an injunction, hear the respondent's opinions. If the respondent's opinions are well-founded, the People's Court shall rule to lift the injunction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请人在提起诉讼时或者诉讼过程中申请禁止令的，人民法院可以责令申请人提供担保，不提供担保的，裁定驳回申请。  
申请人提起诉讼前申请禁止令的，人民法院应当责令申请人提供担保，不提供担保的，裁定驳回申请。

**Article 7** If the applicant applies for an injunction when instituting an action or during the course of the action, the People's Court may order the applicant to provide security; if the applicant fails to provide security, the court shall rule to dismiss the application.  
If the applicant applies for an injunction before instituting an action, the People's Court shall order the applicant to provide security; if the applicant fails to provide security, the court shall rule to dismiss the application.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院裁定准予申请人禁止令申请的，应当根据申请人的请求和案件具体情况确定禁止令的效力期间。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court rules to grant the applicant's application for an injunction, it shall determine the period of validity of the injunction based on the applicant's request and the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院准予或者不准予申请人禁止令申请的，应当制作民事裁定书，并送达当事人，裁定书自送达之日起生效。  
人民法院裁定准予申请人禁止令申请的，可以根据裁定内容制作禁止令张贴在被申请人住所地，污染环境、破坏生态行为实施地、损害结果发生地等相关场所，并可通过新闻媒体等方式向社会公开。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法律/最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 9** Where the People's Court grants or denies the applicant's application for an injunction, it shall issue a civil ruling in writing and serve it on the parties. The ruling shall take effect as of the date of service.  
Where the People's Court rules to grant the applicant's application for an injunction, it may prepare an injunction based on the content of the ruling and post it at the respondent's domicile, the place where the environmental pollution or ecological destruction act is committed, the place where the harm results occur, and other relevant locations, and may also make it public through news media or other means.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人、利害关系人对人民法院裁定准予或者不准予申请人禁止令申请不服的，可在收到裁定书之日起五日内向作出裁定的人民法院申请复议一次。人民法院应当在收到复议申请后十日内审查并作出裁定。复议期间不停止裁定的执行。

**Article 10** Where a party or an interested person is not satisfied with the People's Court's ruling to grant or deny the applicant's application for an injunction, it may apply to the People's Court that made the ruling for one reconsideration within five days from the date of receipt of the written ruling. The People's Court shall review and make a ruling within ten days after receiving the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, enforcement of the ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　申请人在人民法院作出诉前禁止令后三十日内不依法提起诉讼的，人民法院应当在三十日届满后五日内裁定解除禁止令。  
禁止令效力期间内，申请人、被申请人或者利害关系人以据以作出裁定的事由发生变化为由，申请解除禁止令的，人民法院应当在收到申请后五日内裁定是否解除。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../行政与公法/行政法律/最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于生态环境侵权案件适用禁止令保全措施的若干规定》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 11** If the applicant fails to institute an action according to law within thirty days after the People's Court issues a pre-action injunction, the People's Court shall, within five days after the expiration of the thirty-day period, rule to lift the injunction.  
During the period of validity of the injunction, if the applicant, the respondent, or an interested person applies to lift the injunction on the ground that the grounds on which the ruling was based have changed, the People's Court shall, within five days after receiving the application, rule on whether to lift the injunction.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被申请人不履行禁止令的，人民法院可依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定追究其相应法律责任。

**Article 12** If the respondent fails to comply with the injunction, the People's Court may pursue its corresponding legal liability in accordance with Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　侵权行为实施地、损害结果发生地在中华人民共和国管辖海域内的海洋生态环境侵权案件中，申请人向人民法院申请责令被申请人立即停止一定行为的，适用海洋环境保护法、海事诉讼特别程序法等法律和司法解释的相关规定。

**Article 13** In a case concerning infringement of the marine ecological environment where the place where the infringing act is committed or the place where the harm results occur is within the sea areas under the jurisdiction of the People's Republic of China, if the applicant applies to the People's Court for an order requiring the respondent to immediately cease a specific act, the relevant provisions of the Marine Environment Protection Law, the Special Procedure Law for Maritime Actions, and other laws and judicial interpretations shall apply.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定自2022年1月1日起施行。
