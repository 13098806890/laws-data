# 中华人民共和国人民调解法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2010-08-28  
**生效日期**：2011-01-01

> （2010年8月28日第十一届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第十六次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　人民调解委员会 第三章　人民调解员 第四章　调解程序 第五章　调解协议 第六章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了完善人民调解制度，规范人民调解活动，及时解决民间纠纷，维护社会和谐稳定，根据宪法，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称人民调解，是指人民调解委员会通过说服、疏导等方法，促使当事人在平等协商基础上自愿达成调解协议，解决民间纠纷的活动。 第三条　人民调解委员会调解民间纠纷，应当遵循下列原则： （一）在当事人自愿、平等的基础上进行调解； （二）不违背法律、法规和国家政策； （三）尊重当事人的权利，不得因调解而阻止当事人依法通过仲裁、行政、司法等途径维护自己的权利。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了完善人民调解制度，规范人民调解活动，及时解决民间纠纷，维护社会和谐稳定，根据宪法，制定本法。

**Article 1** For the purposes of improving the people's mediation system, regulating people's mediation activities, timely resolving civil disputes, and maintaining social harmony and stability, this Law is enacted in accordance with the Constitution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称人民调解，是指人民调解委员会通过说服、疏导等方法，促使当事人在平等协商基础上自愿达成调解协议，解决民间纠纷的活动。

**Article 2** For the purposes of this Law, "people's mediation" means an activity in which the People's Mediation Committee, through methods such as persuasion and guidance, urges the parties to voluntarily reach a mediation agreement based on equal consultation and to resolve civil disputes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民调解委员会调解民间纠纷，应当遵循下列原则：  
（一）在当事人自愿、平等的基础上进行调解；  
（二）不违背法律、法规和国家政策；  
（三）尊重当事人的权利，不得因调解而阻止当事人依法通过仲裁、行政、司法等途径维护自己的权利。

**Article 3** In mediating civil disputes, the People's Mediation Committee shall follow the following principles:  
(1) Conduct mediation on the basis of the parties' free will and equality;  
(2) Not contravene laws, regulations, and state policies;  
(3) Respect the rights of the parties, and shall not, because of mediation, prevent the parties from safeguarding their rights through arbitration, administrative, judicial or other channels according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民调解委员会调解民间纠纷，不收取任何费用。

**Article 4** The People's Mediation Committee shall not charge any fees for mediating civil disputes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院司法行政部门负责指导全国的人民调解工作，县级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门负责指导本行政区域的人民调解工作。  
基层人民法院对人民调解委员会调解民间纠纷进行业务指导。

**Article 5** The judicial administration department of the State Council shall be responsible for guiding the people's mediation work nationwide. The judicial administration departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for guiding the people's mediation work within their respective administrative areas.  
Primary People's Courts shall provide professional guidance to the People's Mediation Committees in mediating civil disputes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励和支持人民调解工作。县级以上地方人民政府对人民调解工作所需经费应当给予必要的支持和保障，对有突出贡献的人民调解委员会和人民调解员按照国家规定给予表彰奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages and supports people's mediation work. Local people's governments at or above the county level shall provide necessary support and guarantee for the funds needed for people's mediation work, and shall give commendation and rewards according to state provisions to People's Mediation Committees and people's mediators that have made outstanding contributions.

### 第二章　人民调解委员会

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民调解委员会是依法设立的调解民间纠纷的群众性组织。

**Article 7** A People's Mediation Committee is a mass organization established according to law for mediating civil disputes.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　村民委员会、居民委员会设立人民调解委员会。企业事业单位根据需要设立人民调解委员会。  
人民调解委员会由委员三至九人组成，设主任一人，必要时，可以设副主任若干人。  
人民调解委员会应当有妇女成员，多民族居住的地区应当有人数较少民族的成员。

**Article 8** Villagers' committees and residents' committees shall establish People's Mediation Committees. Enterprises and public institutions may establish People's Mediation Committees as needed.  
A People's Mediation Committee shall be composed of three to nine members and shall have one director; if necessary, a number of deputy directors may be established.  
A People's Mediation Committee shall have female members, and in areas where multiple ethnic groups live, there shall be members from ethnic groups with smaller populations.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　村民委员会、居民委员会的人民调解委员会委员由村民会议或者村民代表会议、居民会议推选产生；企业事业单位设立的人民调解委员会委员由职工大会、职工代表大会或者工会组织推选产生。  
人民调解委员会委员每届任期三年，可以连选连任。

**Article 9** The members of the People's Mediation Committees of villagers' committees and residents' committees shall be elected by the villagers' meeting, the villagers' representative meeting, or the residents' meeting; the members of the People's Mediation Committees established by enterprises and public institutions shall be elected by the workers' assembly, the workers' congress, or the trade union organization.  
The term of office of the members of a People's Mediation Committee shall be three years, and they may be re-elected.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　县级人民政府司法行政部门应当对本行政区域内人民调解委员会的设立情况进行统计，并且将人民调解委员会以及人员组成和调整情况及时通报所在地基层人民法院。

**Article 10** The judicial administration department of the people's government at the county level shall collect statistics on the establishment of People's Mediation Committees within its administrative area, and shall promptly notify the local Primary People's Court of the establishment, personnel composition, and adjustments of the People's Mediation Committees.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民调解委员会应当建立健全各项调解工作制度，听取群众意见，接受群众监督。

**Article 11** A People's Mediation Committee shall establish and improve various mediation work systems, listen to the opinions of the masses, and accept supervision by the masses.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　村民委员会、居民委员会和企业事业单位应当为人民调解委员会开展工作提供办公条件和必要的工作经费。

**Article 12** Villagers' committees, residents' committees, and enterprises and public institutions shall provide office conditions and necessary working funds for the People's Mediation Committee to carry out its work.

### 第三章　人民调解员

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民调解员由人民调解委员会委员和人民调解委员会聘任的人员担任。

**Article 13** People's mediators shall be composed of members of the People's Mediation Committee and persons employed by the People's Mediation Committee.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民调解员应当由公道正派、热心人民调解工作，并具有一定文化水平、政策水平和法律知识的成年公民担任。  
县级人民政府司法行政部门应当定期对人民调解员进行业务培训。

**Article 14** People's mediators shall be adult citizens who are fair and upright, enthusiastic about people's mediation work, and possess a certain level of education, policy knowledge, and legal knowledge.  
The judicial administration department of the people's government at the county level shall regularly provide professional training to people's mediators.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民调解员在调解工作中有下列行为之一的，由其所在的人民调解委员会给予批评教育、责令改正，情节严重的，由推选或者聘任单位予以罢免或者解聘：  
（一）偏袒一方当事人的；  
（二）侮辱当事人的；  
（三）索取、收受财物或者牟取其他不正当利益的；  
（四）泄露当事人的个人隐私、商业秘密的。

**Article 15** If a people's mediator commits any of the following acts in the mediation work, the People's Mediation Committee to which he or she belongs shall give criticism and education, and order him or her to make corrections; if the circumstances are serious, the unit that elected or employed him or her shall remove or dismiss him or her:  
(1) Showing partiality to one party;  
(2) Insulting a party;  
(3) Extorting or accepting property or seeking other illegitimate benefits;  
(4) Disclosing a party's personal privacy or trade secrets.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民调解员从事调解工作，应当给予适当的误工补贴；因从事调解工作致伤致残，生活发生困难的，当地人民政府应当提供必要的医疗、生活救助；在人民调解工作岗位上牺牲的人民调解员，其配偶、子女按照国家规定享受抚恤和优待。

**Article 16** People's mediators shall be given appropriate subsidies for loss of working time when engaging in mediation work; if a people's mediator suffers injury or disability due to mediation work and experiences hardship in daily life, the local people's government shall provide necessary medical and living assistance; if a people's mediator dies in the line of duty, his or her spouse and children shall enjoy pensions and preferential treatment according to state provisions.

### 第四章　调解程序

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人可以向人民调解委员会申请调解；人民调解委员会也可以主动调解。当事人一方明确拒绝调解的，不得调解。

**Article 17** A party may apply to the People's Mediation Committee for mediation; the People's Mediation Committee may also conduct mediation on its own initiative. If one party explicitly refuses mediation, mediation shall not be conducted.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　基层人民法院、公安机关对适宜通过人民调解方式解决的纠纷，可以在受理前告知当事人向人民调解委员会申请调解。

**Article 18** For disputes suitable for resolution through people's mediation, the Primary People's Court or the public security organ may, before accepting the case, inform the parties to apply to the People's Mediation Committee for mediation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民调解委员会根据调解纠纷的需要，可以指定一名或者数名人民调解员进行调解，也可以由当事人选择一名或者数名人民调解员进行调解。

**Article 19** A People's Mediation Committee may, according to the needs of mediating a dispute, designate one or several people's mediators to conduct mediation, or the parties may select one or several people's mediators to conduct mediation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民调解员根据调解纠纷的需要，在征得当事人的同意后，可以邀请当事人的亲属、邻里、同事等参与调解，也可以邀请具有专门知识、特定经验的人员或者有关社会组织的人员参与调解。  
人民调解委员会支持当地公道正派、热心调解、群众认可的社会人士参与调解。

**Article 20** A people's mediator may, according to the needs of mediating a dispute and with the consent of the parties, invite the parties' relatives, neighbors, colleagues, etc. to participate in the mediation, and may also invite persons with specialized knowledge, specific experience, or personnel from relevant social organizations to participate in the mediation.  
The People's Mediation Committee supports the participation in mediation of local social persons who are fair and upright, enthusiastic about mediation, and recognized by the masses.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民调解员调解民间纠纷，应当坚持原则，明法析理，主持公道。  
调解民间纠纷，应当及时、就地进行，防止矛盾激化。

**Article 21** In mediating civil disputes, people's mediators shall adhere to principles, clarify laws and reasoning, and uphold justice.  
Mediation of civil disputes shall be conducted promptly and on the spot to prevent the escalation of conflicts.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民调解员根据纠纷的不同情况，可以采取多种方式调解民间纠纷，充分听取当事人的陈述，讲解有关法律、法规和国家政策，耐心疏导，在当事人平等协商、互谅互让的基础上提出纠纷解决方案，帮助当事人自愿达成调解协议。

**Article 22** People's mediators may, according to the different circumstances of the dispute, adopt various methods to mediate civil disputes, fully listen to the parties' statements, explain relevant laws, regulations, and state policies, provide patient guidance, propose dispute resolution plans on the basis of equal consultation, mutual understanding and mutual concessions, and help the parties voluntarily reach a mediation agreement.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人在人民调解活动中享有下列权利：  
（一）选择或者接受人民调解员；  
（二）接受调解、拒绝调解或者要求终止调解；  
（三）要求调解公开进行或者不公开进行；  
（四）自主表达意愿、自愿达成调解协议。

**Article 23** The parties shall enjoy the following rights in people's mediation activities:  
(1) To select or accept a people's mediator;  
(2) To accept mediation, refuse mediation, or request termination of mediation;  
(3) To request that mediation be conducted openly or not openly;  
(4) To freely express their will and voluntarily reach a mediation agreement.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人在人民调解活动中履行下列义务：  
（一）如实陈述纠纷事实；  
（二）遵守调解现场秩序，尊重人民调解员；  
（三）尊重对方当事人行使权利。

**Article 24** The parties shall perform the following obligations in people's mediation activities:  
(1) To truthfully state the facts of the dispute;  
(2) To observe the order at the mediation site and respect the people's mediator;  
(3) To respect the other party's exercise of its rights.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民调解员在调解纠纷过程中，发现纠纷有可能激化的，应当采取有针对性的预防措施；对有可能引起治安案件、刑事案件的纠纷，应当及时向当地公安机关或者其他有关部门报告。

**Article 25** If, in the process of mediating a dispute, a people's mediator finds that the dispute may escalate, he or she shall take targeted preventive measures; for disputes that may lead to public security cases or criminal cases, he or she shall promptly report to the local public security organ or other relevant departments.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民调解员调解纠纷，调解不成的，应当终止调解，并依据有关法律、法规的规定，告知当事人可以依法通过仲裁、行政、司法等途径维护自己的权利。

**Article 26** If a people's mediator fails to mediate a dispute, he or she shall terminate the mediation and, in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations, inform the parties that they may safeguard their rights through arbitration, administrative, judicial or other channels according to law.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民调解员应当记录调解情况。人民调解委员会应当建立调解工作档案，将调解登记、调解工作记录、调解协议书等材料立卷归档。

**Article 27** People's mediators shall record the mediation process. The People's Mediation Committee shall establish mediation work files, and compile and archive materials such as mediation registration, mediation work records, and mediation agreements.

### 第五章　调解协议

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　经人民调解委员会调解达成调解协议的，可以制作调解协议书。当事人认为无需制作调解协议书的，可以采取口头协议方式，人民调解员应当记录协议内容。

**Article 28** If a mediation agreement is reached through mediation by the People's Mediation Committee, a mediation agreement document may be prepared. If the parties consider it unnecessary to prepare a mediation agreement document, an oral agreement may be adopted, and the people's mediator shall record the content of the agreement.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　调解协议书可以载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况；  
（二）纠纷的主要事实、争议事项以及各方当事人的责任；  
（三）当事人达成调解协议的内容，履行的方式、期限。  
调解协议书自各方当事人签名、盖章或者按指印，人民调解员签名并加盖人民调解委员会印章之日起生效。调解协议书由当事人各执一份，人民调解委员会留存一份。

**Article 29** A mediation agreement document may specify the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the parties;  
(2) The main facts of the dispute, the matters in dispute, and the responsibilities of each party;  
(3) The content of the mediation agreement reached by the parties, and the methods and time limits for performance.  
A mediation agreement document shall take effect on the date when all parties sign, affix their seals, or affix their fingerprints, and the people's mediator signs and affixes the seal of the People's Mediation Committee. Each party shall keep one copy of the mediation agreement document, and the People's Mediation Committee shall keep one copy.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　口头调解协议自各方当事人达成协议之日起生效。

**Article 30** An oral mediation agreement shall take effect on the date when all parties reach the agreement.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　经人民调解委员会调解达成的调解协议，具有法律约束力，当事人应当按照约定履行。  
人民调解委员会应当对调解协议的履行情况进行监督，督促当事人履行约定的义务。

**Article 31** A mediation agreement reached through mediation by the People's Mediation Committee shall have legal binding force, and the parties shall perform it in accordance with the agreement.  
The People's Mediation Committee shall supervise the performance of the mediation agreement and urge the parties to perform their agreed obligations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　经人民调解委员会调解达成调解协议后，当事人之间就调解协议的履行或者调解协议的内容发生争议的，一方当事人可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 32** After a mediation agreement is reached through mediation by the People's Mediation Committee, if a dispute arises between the parties concerning the performance of the mediation agreement or the content of the mediation agreement, one party may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　经人民调解委员会调解达成调解协议后，双方当事人认为有必要的，可以自调解协议生效之日起三十日内共同向人民法院申请司法确认，人民法院应当及时对调解协议进行审查，依法确认调解协议的效力。  
人民法院依法确认调解协议有效，一方当事人拒绝履行或者未全部履行的，对方当事人可以向人民法院申请强制执行。  
人民法院依法确认调解协议无效的，当事人可以通过人民调解方式变更原调解协议或者达成新的调解协议，也可以向人民法院提起诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于人民调解协议司法确认程序的若干规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于人民调解协议司法确认程序的若干规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 33** After a mediation agreement is reached through mediation by the People's Mediation Committee, if both parties consider it necessary, they may, within thirty days from the date when the mediation agreement takes effect, jointly apply to the People's Court for judicial confirmation. The People's Court shall promptly examine the mediation agreement and confirm its validity according to law.  
If the People's Court confirms the validity of the mediation agreement according to law and one party refuses to perform or fails to fully perform it, the other party may apply to the People's Court for compulsory execution.  
If the People's Court confirms the invalidity of the mediation agreement according to law, the parties may modify the original mediation agreement or reach a new mediation agreement through people's mediation, or may bring a lawsuit in the People's Court.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　乡镇、街道以及社会团体或者其他组织根据需要可以参照本法有关规定设立人民调解委员会，调解民间纠纷。

**Article 34** Townships, subdistricts, social organizations, or other organizations may, as needed, establish People's Mediation Committees by reference to the relevant provisions of this Law to mediate civil disputes.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　本法自2011年1月1日起施行。

**Article 35** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2011.
