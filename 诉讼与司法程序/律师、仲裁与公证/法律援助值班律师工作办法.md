# 法律援助值班律师工作办法

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：司规〔2020〕6号  
**公布日期**：2020-08-20

> 第二条　本办法所称值班律师，是指法律援助机构在看守所、人民检察院、人民法院等场所设立法律援助工作站，通过派驻或安排的方式，为没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助的律师。 第三条　值班律师工作应当坚持依法、公平、公正、效率的原则，值班律师应当提供符合标准的法律服务。 第四条　公安机关 （看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院、司法行政机关应当保障没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人获得值班律师法律帮助的权利。 第五条　值班律师工作由司法行政机关牵头组织实施，公安机关（看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院应当依法予以协助。 第二章　值班律师工作职责 第六条　值班律师依法提供以下法律帮助： （一）提供法律咨询； （二）提供程序选择建议； （三）帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人申请变更强制措施； （四）对案件处理提出意见； （五） 帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其近亲属申请法律援助； （六）法律法规规定的其他事项。 值班律师在认罪认罚案件中，还应当提供以下法律帮助： （一）向犯罪嫌疑人、被告人释明认罪认罚的性质和法律规定； （二）对人民检察院指控罪名、量刑建议、诉讼程序适用等事项提出意见；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　值班律师工作应当坚持依法、公平、公正、效率的原则，值班律师应当提供符合标准的法律服务。

**Article 3** The work of duty counsel shall adhere to the principles of lawfulness, fairness, impartiality, and efficiency. Duty counsel shall provide legal services that meet the standards.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　公安机关 （看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院、司法行政机关应当保障没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人获得值班律师法律帮助的权利。

**Article 4** Public security organs (detention centers), People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and judicial administrative organs shall guarantee the right of criminal suspects and defendants who have no defenders to obtain legal assistance from duty counsel.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　值班律师工作由司法行政机关牵头组织实施，公安机关（看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院应当依法予以协助。

**Article 5** The work of duty counsel shall be led and organized by judicial administrative organs. Public security organs (detention centers), People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall provide assistance according to law.

### 第二章　值班律师工作职责

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　值班律师依法提供以下法律帮助：  
（一）提供法律咨询；  
（二）提供程序选择建议；  
（三）帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人申请变更强制措施；  
（四）对案件处理提出意见；  
（五） 帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其近亲属申请法律援助；  
（六）法律法规规定的其他事项。  
值班律师在认罪认罚案件中，还应当提供以下法律帮助：  
（一）向犯罪嫌疑人、被告人释明认罪认罚的性质和法律规定；  
（二）对人民检察院指控罪名、量刑建议、诉讼程序适用等事项提出意见；  
（三）犯罪嫌疑人签署认罪认罚具结书时在场。  
值班律师办理案件时，可以应犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的约见进行会见，也可以经办案机关允许主动会见；自人民检察院对案件审查起诉之日起可以查阅案卷材料、了解案情。

**Article 6** The duty lawyer shall provide the following legal assistance according to law:  
(1) Provide legal consultation;  
(2) Provide suggestions on procedural options;  
(3) Assist criminal suspects and defendants in applying for modification of compulsory measures;  
(4) Provide opinions on case handling;  
(5) Assist criminal suspects, defendants, and their close relatives in applying for legal aid;  
(6) Other matters as provided by laws and regulations.  
In cases of pleading guilty and accepting punishment, the duty lawyer shall also provide the following legal assistance:  
(1) Explain to the criminal suspect or defendant the nature and legal provisions of pleading guilty and accepting punishment;  
(2) Provide opinions on matters such as the charges accused by the People's Procuratorate, sentencing recommendations, and application of litigation procedures;  
(3) Be present when the criminal suspect signs the plea affidavit.  
When handling a case, the duty lawyer may meet with the criminal suspect or defendant upon their request, or may proactively meet with them with the permission of the case-handling authority; from the day the People's Procuratorate begins to examine and prosecute the case, the duty lawyer may review the case file materials and learn about the case.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　值班律师提供法律咨询时，应当告知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有关法律帮助的相关规定，结合案件所在的诉讼阶段解释相关诉讼权利和程序规定，解答犯罪嫌疑人、被告人咨询的法律问题。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人认罪认罚的，值班律师应当了解犯罪嫌疑人、被告人对被指控的犯罪事实和罪名是否有异议，告知被指控罪名的法定量刑幅度，释明从宽从重处罚的情节以及认罪认罚的从宽幅度，并结合案件情况提供程序选择建议。  
值班律师提供法律咨询的，应当记录犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌的罪名、咨询的法律问题、提供的法律解答。

**Article 7** When providing legal consultation, duty counsel shall inform criminal suspects and defendants of the relevant provisions on legal assistance, explain relevant procedural rights and procedural rules in light of the stage of proceedings in the case, and answer legal questions raised by criminal suspects and defendants.  
Where criminal suspects or defendants plead guilty and accept punishment, duty counsel shall ascertain whether the criminal suspect or defendant has any objection to the alleged criminal facts and charges, inform them of the statutory sentencing range for the alleged charges, explain the circumstances for leniency or severity of punishment and the range of leniency for guilty plea and acceptance of punishment, and provide recommendations on procedural choices based on the circumstances of the case.  
When providing legal consultation, duty counsel shall record the alleged crimes of the criminal suspect or defendant, the legal questions consulted, and the legal answers provided.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在审查起诉阶段，犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚的，值班律师可以就以下事项向人民检察院提出意见：  
（一）涉嫌的犯罪事实、指控罪名及适用的法律规定；  
（二）从轻、减轻或者免除处罚等从宽处罚的建议；  
（三）认罪认罚后案件审理适用的程序；  
（四）其他需要提出意见的事项。  
值班律师对前款事项提出意见的，人民检察院应当记录在案并附卷，未采纳值班律师意见的，应当说明理由。

**Article 8** During the stage of examination and prosecution, where a criminal suspect pleads guilty and accepts punishment, duty counsel may provide opinions to the People's Procuratorate on the following matters:  
(1) The alleged criminal facts, charges, and applicable legal provisions;  
(2) Recommendations for lenient punishment, such as a lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment;  
(3) The procedures applicable to the trial of the case after the guilty plea and acceptance of punishment;  
(4) Other matters requiring opinions.  
Where duty counsel provides opinions on the matters in the preceding paragraph, the People's Procuratorate shall record them in the case file and attach them to the case file. If the People's Procuratorate does not adopt the opinions of duty counsel, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提出申请羁押必要性审查的，值班律师应当告知其取保候审、监视居住、逮捕等强制措施的适用条件和相关法律规定、人民检察院进行羁押必要性审查的程序；犯罪嫌疑人、被告人已经被逮捕的，值班律师可以帮助其向人民检察院提出羁押必要性审查申请，并协助提供相关材料。

**Article 9** Where a criminal suspect or defendant applies for review of the necessity of custody, duty counsel shall inform him/her of the applicable conditions for compulsory measures such as release on bail pending investigation or trial, residential surveillance, and arrest, relevant legal provisions, and the procedures for the People's Procuratorate to conduct a review of the necessity of custody. If the criminal suspect or defendant has already been arrested, duty counsel may assist him/her in filing an application with the People's Procuratorate for review of the necessity of custody and assist in providing relevant materials.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　犯罪嫌疑人签署认罪认罚具结书时，值班律师对犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚自愿性、人民检察院量刑建议、程序适用等均无异议的，应当在具结书上签名，同时留存一份复印件归档。  
值班律师对人民检察院量刑建议、程序适用有异议的，在确认犯罪嫌疑人系自愿认罪认罚后，应当在具结书上签字，同时可以向人民检察院提出法律意见。  
犯罪嫌疑人拒绝值班律师帮助的，值班律师无需在具结书上签字，应当将犯罪嫌疑人签字拒绝法律帮助的书面材料留存一份归档。

**Article 10** When a criminal suspect signs the affidavit of guilty plea and acceptance of punishment, if duty counsel has no objection to the voluntariness of the criminal suspect's guilty plea and acceptance of punishment, the People's Procuratorate's sentencing recommendation, or the application of procedures, duty counsel shall sign the affidavit and retain a copy for archiving.  
If duty counsel has an objection to the People's Procuratorate's sentencing recommendation or the application of procedures, after confirming that the criminal suspect voluntarily pleads guilty and accepts punishment, duty counsel shall sign the affidavit and may simultaneously submit legal opinions to the People's Procuratorate.  
If the criminal suspect refuses the assistance of duty counsel, duty counsel is not required to sign the affidavit, and shall retain a copy of the written document signed by the criminal suspect refusing legal assistance for archiving.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，在不同诉讼阶段，可以由派驻看守所的同一值班律师提供法律帮助。对于未被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人，前一诉讼阶段的值班律师可以在后续诉讼阶段继续为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助。

**Article 11** For criminal suspects or defendants who are in custody, the same duty counsel stationed at the detention center may provide legal assistance at different stages of proceedings. For criminal suspects or defendants who are not in custody, duty counsel from a previous stage of proceedings may continue to provide legal assistance to the criminal suspect or defendant in subsequent stages of proceedings.

### 第三章　法律帮助工作程序

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当在侦查、审查起诉和审判各阶段分别告知没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权约见值班律师获得法律帮助，并为其约见值班律师提供便利。

**Article 12** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall, at the stages of investigation, examination and prosecution, and adjudication respectively, inform criminal suspects and defendants who have no defenders of their right to meet with duty counsel to obtain legal assistance, and shall facilitate their meeting with duty counsel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　看守所应当告知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权约见值班律师，并为其约见值班律师提供便利。  
看守所应当将值班律师制度相关内容纳入在押人员权利义务告知书，在犯罪嫌疑人、被告人入所时告知其有权获得值班律师的法律帮助。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人要求约见值班律师的，可以书面或者口头申请。书面申请的，看守所应当将其填写的法律帮助申请表及时转交值班律师。口头申请的，看守所应当安排代为填写法律帮助申请表。

**Article 13** The detention center shall inform criminal suspects and defendants of their right to meet with duty counsel and shall facilitate their meeting with duty counsel.  
The detention center shall include the relevant content of the duty counsel system in the notice of rights and obligations of detainees, and shall inform criminal suspects and defendants upon admission that they have the right to obtain legal assistance from duty counsel.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant requests a meeting with duty counsel, he/she may apply in writing or orally. If the application is made in writing, the detention center shall promptly forward the completed legal assistance application form to duty counsel. If the application is made orally, the detention center shall arrange for someone to fill out the legal assistance application form on his/her behalf.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人没有委托辩护人并且不符合法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护的条件，要求约见值班律师的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当及时通知法律援助机构安排。

**Article 14** Where a criminal suspect or defendant has not retained a defender and does not meet the conditions for a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to provide defense, and requests a meeting with duty counsel, the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court shall promptly notify the legal aid institution to make arrangements.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依法应当通知值班律师提供法律帮助而犯罪嫌疑人、被告人明确拒绝的，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当记录在案。  
前一诉讼程序犯罪嫌疑人、被告人明确拒绝值班律师法律帮助的，后一诉讼程序的办案机关仍需告知其有权获得值班律师法律帮助的权利，有关情况应当记录在案。

**Article 15** Where duty counsel should be notified according to law to provide legal assistance but the criminal suspect or defendant expressly refuses, the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court shall record the matter in the case file.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant expressly refuses legal assistance from duty counsel in a previous proceeding, the case-handling authority in the subsequent proceeding shall still inform him/her of the right to obtain legal assistance from duty counsel, and the relevant circumstances shall be recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院需要法律援助机构通知值班律师为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助的，应当向法律援助机构出具法律帮助通知书，并附相关法律文书。  
单次批量通知的，可以在一份法律帮助通知书后附多名犯罪嫌疑人、被告人相关信息的材料。  
除通知值班律师到羁押场所提供法律帮助的情形外，人民检察院、人民法院可以商法律援助机构简化通知方式和通知手续。

**Article 16** Where a public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court requires a legal aid institution to notify duty counsel to provide legal assistance for a criminal suspect or defendant, it shall issue a notice of legal assistance to the legal aid institution, accompanied by relevant legal documents.  
Where a single batch notification is made, materials containing information on multiple criminal suspects or defendants may be attached to one notice of legal assistance.  
Except in cases where duty counsel is notified to provide legal assistance at a custodial facility, the People's Procuratorate or People's Court may consult with the legal aid institution to simplify the notification method and procedures.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　司法行政机关和法律援助机构应当根据当地律师资源状况、法律帮助需求，会同看守所、人民检察院、人民法院合理安排值班律师的值班方式、值班频次。  
值班方式可以采用现场值班、电话值班、网络值班相结合的方式。现场值班的，可以采取固定专人或轮流值班，也可以采取预约值班。

**Article 17** Judicial administrative organs and legal aid institutions shall, based on the local lawyer resources and demand for legal assistance, in conjunction with detention centers, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts, reasonably arrange the duty mode and frequency of duty counsel.  
The duty mode may combine on-site duty, telephone duty, and online duty. Where on-site duty is adopted, it may be performed by a fixed person or on a rotation basis, or by appointment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　法律援助机构应当综合律师政治素质、业务能力、执业年限等确定值班律师人选，建立值班律师名册或值班律师库。并将值班律师库或名册信息、值班律师工作安排，提前告知公安机关（看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院。

**Article 18** Legal aid institutions shall, taking into comprehensive consideration the political quality, professional competence, and years of practice of lawyers, determine the candidates for duty counsel and establish a roster or database of duty counsel. The information on the database or roster of duty counsel and the work arrangements of duty counsel shall be notified in advance to public security organs (detention centers), People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当在确定的法律帮助日期前三个工作日，将法律帮助通知书送达法律援助机构，或者直接送达现场值班律师。  
该期间没有安排现场值班律师的，法律援助机构应当自收到法律帮助通知书之日起两个工作日内确定值班律师，并通知公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院。  
公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院和法律援助机构之间的送达及通知方式，可以协商简化。  
适用速裁程序的案件、法律援助机构需要跨地区调配律师等特殊情形的通知和指派时限，不受前款限制。

**Article 19** Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall, three working days prior to the determined date of legal assistance, deliver the notice of legal assistance to the legal aid institution, or directly to the duty counsel on-site.  
If no on-site duty counsel is arranged during that period, the legal aid institution shall, within two working days from receipt of the notice of legal assistance, determine the duty counsel and notify the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court.  
The methods of delivery and notification among public security organs, People's Procuratorates, People's Courts, and legal aid institutions may be simplified through consultation.  
For cases subject to fast-track adjudication procedures or where the legal aid institution needs to deploy lawyers across regions, the time limits for notification and assignment are not subject to the restrictions in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　值班律师在人民检察院、人民法院现场值班的，应当按照法律援助机构的安排，或者人民检察院、人民法院送达的通知，及时为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提出法律帮助申请，看守所转交给现场值班律师的，值班律师应当根据看守所的安排及时提供法律帮助。  
值班律师通过电话、网络值班的，应当及时提供法律帮助，疑难案件可以另行预约咨询时间。

**Article 20** Where duty counsel is on-site at a People's Procuratorate or People's Court, he/she shall provide legal assistance to criminal suspects or defendants in a timely manner in accordance with the arrangement of the legal aid institution or the notice delivered by the People's Procuratorate or People's Court.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant submits an application for legal assistance and the detention center forwards it to the duty counsel on-site, the duty counsel shall provide legal assistance in a timely manner in accordance with the arrangement of the detention center.  
Where duty counsel is on duty by telephone or online, he/she shall provide legal assistance in a timely manner. For difficult cases, a separate consultation time may be scheduled by appointment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　侦查阶段，值班律师可以向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名及案件有关情况；案件进入审查起诉阶段后，值班律师可以查阅案卷材料，了解案情，人民检察院、人民法院应当及时安排，并提供便利。已经实现卷宗电子化的地方，人民检察院、人民法院可以安排在线阅卷。

**Article 21** During the investigation stage, duty counsel may inquire about the alleged crimes of the criminal suspect and relevant case information from the investigation authority. After the case enters the stage of examination and prosecution, duty counsel may consult case files and materials to understand the case, and the People's Procuratorate or People's Court shall arrange this in a timely manner and provide convenience. Where case files have been digitized, the People's Procuratorate or People's Court may arrange for online review of files.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　值班律师持律师执业证或者律师工作证、法律帮助申请表或者法律帮助通知书到看守所办理法律帮助会见手续，看守所应当及时安排会见。  
危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪案件，侦查期间值班律师会见在押犯罪嫌疑人的，应当经侦查机关许可。

**Article 22** Where duty counsel holds the lawyer's practice certificate or lawyer's work certificate, and the legal assistance application form or notice of legal assistance, to go through the procedures for a meeting for legal assistance at the detention center, the detention center shall arrange the meeting in a timely manner.  
For cases involving crimes endangering national security or terrorist activities, where duty counsel meets with a detained criminal suspect during the investigation period, permission from the investigation authority shall be obtained.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　值班律师提供法律帮助时，应当出示律师执业证或者律师工作证或者相关法律文书，表明值班律师身份。

**Article 23** When providing legal assistance, duty counsel shall present the lawyer's practice certificate or lawyer's work certificate or relevant legal documents to indicate his/her identity as duty counsel.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　值班律师会见犯罪嫌疑人、被告人时不被监听。

**Article 24** When duty counsel meets with a criminal suspect or defendant, the meeting shall not be monitored.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　值班律师在提供法律帮助过程中，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人向值班律师表示愿意认罪认罚的，值班律师应当及时告知相关的公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院。

**Article 25** In the course of providing legal assistance, if a criminal suspect or defendant expresses to duty counsel a willingness to plead guilty and accept punishment, duty counsel shall promptly inform the relevant public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or People's Court.

### 第四章　值班律师工作保障

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　在看守所、人民检察院、人民法院设立的法律援助工作站，由同级司法行政机关所属的法律援助机构负责派驻并管理。  
看守所、人民检察院、人民法院等机关办公地点临近的，法律援助机构可以设立联合法律援助工作站派驻值班律师。  
看守所、人民检察院、人民法院应当为法律援助工作站提供必要办公场所和设施。有条件的人民检察院、人民法院，可以设置认罪认罚等案件专门办公区域，为值班律师设立专门会见室。

**Article 26** Legal aid service stations established at detention centers, People's Procuratorates, or People's Courts shall be staffed and managed by the legal aid institution under the judicial administrative organ at the same level.  
Where the offices of detention centers, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts are located close to each other, the legal aid institution may establish a joint legal aid service station to station duty counsel.  
Detention centers, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall provide necessary office space and facilities for the legal aid service stations. Where conditions permit, People's Procuratorates and People's Courts may set up specialized office areas for cases involving guilty plea and acceptance of punishment, and establish dedicated meeting rooms for duty counsel.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　法律援助工作站应当公示法律援助条件及申请程序、值班律师工作职责、当日值班律师基本信息等，放置法律援助格式文书及宣传资料。

**Article 27** Legal aid service stations shall publicly display the conditions for legal aid and application procedures, the work responsibilities of duty counsel, the basic information of the duty counsel on duty that day, and shall place standard legal aid documents and publicity materials.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　值班律师提供法律咨询、查阅案卷材料、会见犯罪嫌疑人或者被告人、提出书面意见等法律帮助活动的相关情况应当记录在案，并随案移送。  
值班律师应当将提供法律帮助的情况记入工作台账或者形成工作卷宗，按照规定时限移交法律援助机构。  
公安机关（看守所）、人民检察院、人民法院应当与法律援助机构确定工作台账格式，将值班律师履行职责情况记录在案，并定期移送法律援助机构。

**Article 28** The relevant circumstances of legal assistance activities provided by duty counsel, such as legal consultation, consultation of case files, meetings with criminal suspects or defendants, submission of written opinions, etc., shall be recorded in the case file and transferred with the case.  
Duty counsel shall record the circumstances of providing legal assistance in a work log or form a work file, and transfer it to the legal aid institution within the prescribed time limit.  
Public security organs (detention centers), People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall determine the format of the work log with the legal aid institution, record the performance of duties by duty counsel, and regularly transfer it to the legal aid institution.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　值班律师提供法律帮助时，应当遵守相关法律法规、执业纪律和职业道德，依法保守国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私，不得向他人泄露工作中掌握的案件情况，不得向受援人收取财物或者谋取不正当利益。

**Article 29** When providing legal assistance, duty counsel shall comply with relevant laws and regulations, practice discipline, and professional ethics, keep state secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy confidential according to law, shall not disclose case information obtained in the course of work to others, and shall not accept property or seek improper benefits from the assisted person.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　司法行政机关应当会同财政部门，根据直接费用、基本劳务费等因素合理制定值班律师法律帮助补贴标准，并纳入预算予以保障。  
值班律师提供法律咨询、转交法律援助申请等法律帮助的补贴标准按工作日计算；为认罪认罚案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助的补贴标准，由各地结合本地实际情况按件或按工作日计算。  
法律援助机构应当根据值班律师履行工作职责情况，按照规定支付值班律师法律帮助补贴。

**Article 30** Judicial administrative organs shall, in conjunction with finance departments, reasonably establish subsidy standards for legal assistance provided by duty counsel based on factors such as direct expenses and basic labor fees, and incorporate them into the budget to ensure funding.  
The subsidy standard for legal assistance such as providing legal consultation and forwarding legal aid applications provided by duty counsel shall be calculated on a per-workday basis. The subsidy standard for providing legal assistance to criminal suspects or defendants in cases of guilty plea and acceptance of punishment shall be calculated on a per-case or per-workday basis by each locality based on its actual circumstances.  
Legal aid institutions shall, based on the performance of duties by duty counsel, pay subsidies for legal assistance provided by duty counsel according to regulations.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　法律援助机构应当建立值班律师准入和退出机制，建立值班律师服务质量考核评估制度，保障值班律师服务质量。  
法律援助机构应当建立值班律师培训制度，值班律师首次上岗前应当参加培训，公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院应当提供协助。

**Article 31** Legal aid institutions shall establish mechanisms for admission and withdrawal of duty counsel, and establish a system for assessing and evaluating the quality of services provided by duty counsel, to ensure the quality of services provided by duty counsel.  
Legal aid institutions shall establish a training system for duty counsel. Duty counsel shall participate in training before taking up their posts for the first time. Public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts shall provide assistance.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　司法行政机关和法律援助机构应当加强本行政区域值班律师工作的监督和指导。对律师资源短缺的地区，可采取在省、市范围内统筹调配律师资源，建立政府购买值班律师服务机制等方式，保障值班律师工作有序开展。

**Article 32** Judicial administrative organs and legal aid institutions shall strengthen supervision and guidance over the work of duty counsel within their respective administrative regions. For areas with a shortage of lawyer resources, measures such as coordinating and deploying lawyer resources within the province or city, or establishing a mechanism for government procurement of duty counsel services may be adopted to ensure the orderly development of the work of duty counsel.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　司法行政机关会同公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院建立值班律师工作会商机制，明确专门联系人，及时沟通情况，协调解决相关问题。

**Article 33** Judicial administrative organs shall, together with public security organs, People's Procuratorates, and People's Courts, establish a consultation mechanism for the work of duty counsel, designate specific contacts, communicate circumstances in a timely manner, and coordinate to resolve relevant issues.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　司法行政机关应当加强对值班律师的监督管理，对表现突出的值班律师给予表彰；对违法违纪的值班律师，依职权或移送有权处理机关依法依规处理。  
法律援助机构应当向律师协会通报值班律师履行职责情况。  
律师协会应当将值班律师履行职责、获得表彰情况纳入律师年度考核及律师诚信服务记录，对违反职业道德和执业纪律的值班律师依法依规处理。

**Article 34** Judicial administrative organs shall strengthen supervision and management of duty counsel. Duty counsel who have performed outstandingly shall be commended. For duty counsel who have violated laws or discipline, they shall, within their authority or by referral to the competent authority, be dealt with according to law and regulations.  
Legal aid institutions shall report to the bar association on the performance of duties by duty counsel.  
Bar associations shall include the performance of duties and receipt of commendations of duty counsel in the annual assessment of lawyers and in the records of lawyers' integrity services, and shall deal with duty counsel who violate professional ethics and practice discipline according to law and regulations.

### 第五章　附　则

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　国家安全机关、中国海警局、监狱履行刑事诉讼法规定职责，涉及值班律师工作的，适用本办法有关公安机关的规定。

**Article 35** Where state security organs, the China Coast Guard, and prisons perform duties stipulated by the Criminal Procedure Law involving the work of duty counsel, the provisions of these Measures concerning public security organs shall apply.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。《关于开展法律援助值班律师工作的意见》（司发通[2017]84号）同时废止。

**Article 36** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Opinions on Carrying Out the Work of Legal Aid Duty Counsel (Si Fa Tong [2017] No. 84) shall be abolished simultaneously.
