各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院，新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院：  
  
关于当事人对具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书的内容有争议提起诉讼人民法院是否受理的问题，我院陆续收到江苏、重庆等高级人民法院的请示，经研究，批复如下：  
  
根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-214)和[《中华人民共和国公证法》第三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国公证法.md#art-37)的规定，经公证的以给付为内容并载明债务人愿意接受强制执行承诺的债权文书依法具有强制执行效力。债权人或者债务人对该债权文书的内容有争议直接向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。但公证债权文书确有错误，人民法院裁定不予执行的，当事人、公证事项的利害关系人可以就争议内容向人民法院提起民事诉讼。

**Article 1** December 22, 2008  
Supreme People's Court  
Regarding a party who, with respect to a notarial debt instrument with compulsory execution effect,  
has a dispute over its content and files a prosecution,  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the People's Court Shall Accept  
Fa Shi [2008] No. 17  
Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army; and the Production and Construction Corps Branch of the Higher People's Court of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region:  
Regarding the issue of whether a party who has a dispute over the content of a notarial debt instrument with compulsory execution effect and files a prosecution shall be accepted by the People's Court, this Court has successively received requests for instructions from the Higher People's Courts of Jiangsu, Chongqing, and others. Upon deliberation, we reply as follows:  
According to Article 214 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 37 of the Notarization Law of the People's Republic of China, a notarized debt instrument that is for payment and contains the debtor's acceptance to submit to compulsory execution shall have compulsory execution effect according to law. If a creditor or debtor directly files a civil action in a People's Court due to a dispute over the content of such debt instrument, the People's Court shall not accept it. However, if the notarial debt instrument indeed contains errors and the People's Court rules not to enforce it, the party or the interested person of the notarial matter may file a civil action in the People's Court regarding the disputed content.
