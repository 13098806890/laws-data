# 最高人民法院关于公证债权文书执行若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕18号  
**公布日期**：2018-06-25  
**生效日期**：2018-10-01

> 最高人民法院《关于公证债权文书执行若干问题的规定》已于2018年6月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1743次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年10月1日起施行。 2018年9月30日 最高人民法院 关于公证债权文书执行若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕18号 为了进一步规范人民法院办理公证债权文书执行案件，确保公证债权文书依法执行，维护当事人、利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国公证法》等法律规定，结合执行实践，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称公证债权文书，是指根据公证法第三十七条第一款规定经公证赋予强制执行效力的债权文书。 第二条　公证债权文书执行案件，由被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地人民法院管辖。 前款规定案件的级别管辖，参照人民法院受理第一审民商事案件级别管辖的规定确定。 第三条　债权人申请执行公证债权文书，除应当提交作为执行依据的公证债权文书等申请执行所需的材料外，还应当提交证明履行情况等内容的执行证书。 第四条　债权人申请执行的公证债权文书应当包括公证证词、被证明的债权文书等内容。权利义务主体、给付内容应当在公证证词中列明。 第五条　债权人申请执行公证债权文书，有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回执行申请： （一）债权文书属于不得经公证赋予强制执行效力的文书； （二）公证债权文书未载明债务人接受强制执行的承诺； （三）公证证词载明的权利义务主体或者给付内容不明确；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称公证债权文书，是指根据[公证法第三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国公证法.md#art-37)第一款规定经公证赋予强制执行效力的债权文书。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, "notarized creditor's rights document" refers to a creditor's rights document that has been notarized and endowed with compulsory enforcement effect according to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 37 of the Notarization Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公证债权文书执行案件，由被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地人民法院管辖。  
前款规定案件的级别管辖，参照人民法院受理第一审民商事案件级别管辖的规定确定。

**Article 2** Cases for enforcement of notarized creditor's rights documents shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to enforcement or at the location of the property subject to enforcement.  
The level of jurisdiction for cases specified in the preceding paragraph shall be determined by reference to the provisions on the level of jurisdiction of the People's Court for accepting first-instance civil and commercial cases.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　债权人申请执行公证债权文书，除应当提交作为执行依据的公证债权文书等申请执行所需的材料外，还应当提交证明履行情况等内容的执行证书。

**Article 3** Where a creditor applies for enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document, in addition to submitting the materials required for an enforcement application, such as the notarized creditor's rights document serving as the basis for enforcement, the creditor shall also submit an enforcement certificate proving the performance status and other contents.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　债权人申请执行的公证债权文书应当包括公证证词、被证明的债权文书等内容。权利义务主体、给付内容应当在公证证词中列明。

**Article 4** The notarized creditor's rights document for which a creditor applies for enforcement shall include the notarial certificate, the certified creditor's rights document, and other contents. The subjects of rights and obligations and the content of performance shall be specified in the notarial certificate.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　债权人申请执行公证债权文书，有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不予受理；已经受理的，裁定驳回执行申请：  
（一）债权文书属于不得经公证赋予强制执行效力的文书；  
（二）公证债权文书未载明债务人接受强制执行的承诺；  
（三）公证证词载明的权利义务主体或者给付内容不明确；  
（四）债权人未提交执行证书；  
（五）其他不符合受理条件的情形。

**Article 5** If a creditor applies for enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document, and any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the case; if the case has already been accepted, it shall rule to reject the enforcement application:  
(1) the creditor's rights document is a document that is not eligible to be endowed with compulsory enforcement effect through notarization;  
(2) the notarized creditor's rights document does not state the debtor's acceptance of compulsory enforcement;  
(3) the subjects of rights and obligations or the content of performance stated in the notarial certificate are unclear;  
(4) the creditor fails to submit an enforcement certificate;  
(5) other circumstances where the conditions for acceptance are not satisfied.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公证债权文书赋予强制执行效力的范围同时包含主债务和担保债务的，人民法院应当依法予以执行；仅包含主债务的，对担保债务部分的执行申请不予受理；仅包含担保债务的，对主债务部分的执行申请不予受理。

**Article 6** If the scope of compulsory enforcement effect endowed by the notarized creditor's rights document includes both the principal debt and the secured debt, the People's Court shall enforce according to law; if it includes only the principal debt, the enforcement application for the secured debt part shall not be accepted; if it includes only the secured debt, the enforcement application for the principal debt part shall not be accepted.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　债权人对不予受理、驳回执行申请裁定不服的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。  
申请复议期满未申请复议，或者复议申请被驳回的，当事人可以就公证债权文书涉及的民事权利义务争议向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 7** If a creditor is dissatisfied with a ruling not to accept the case or a ruling to reject the enforcement application, the creditor may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling.  
If the time limit for applying for reconsideration has expired without an application being filed, or the reconsideration application is rejected, the party may file a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the dispute over civil rights and obligations involved in the notarized creditor's rights document.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　公证机构决定不予出具执行证书的，当事人可以就公证债权文书涉及的民事权利义务争议直接向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 8** If the notarial institution decides not to issue an enforcement certificate, the party may directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the dispute over civil rights and obligations involved in the notarized creditor's rights document.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请执行公证债权文书的期间自公证债权文书确定的履行期间的最后一日起计算；分期履行的，自公证债权文书确定的每次履行期间的最后一日起计算。  
债权人向公证机构申请出具执行证书的，申请执行时效自债权人提出申请之日起中断。

**Article 9** The period for applying for enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document shall be calculated from the last day of the performance period determined in the notarized creditor's rights document; if performance is to be made in installments, it shall be calculated from the last day of each performance period determined in the notarized creditor's rights document.  
If a creditor applies to a notarial institution for issuance of an enforcement certificate, the limitation period for applying for enforcement shall be interrupted from the date the creditor submits the application.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院在执行实施中，根据公证债权文书并结合申请执行人的申请依法确定给付内容。

**Article 10** In the course of enforcement implementation, the People's Court shall determine the content of performance according to law based on the notarized creditor's rights document and in light of the application of the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　因民间借贷形成的公证债权文书，文书中载明的利率超过人民法院依照法律、司法解释规定应予支持的上限的，对超过的利息部分不纳入执行范围；载明的利率未超过人民法院依照法律、司法解释规定应予支持的上限，被执行人主张实际超过的，可以依照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第一款规定提起诉讼。

**Article 11** Where a notarized creditor's rights document is formed due to private lending, if the interest rate stated in the document exceeds the upper limit that the People's Court shall support according to laws and judicial interpretations, the portion of interest exceeding such limit shall not be included in the enforcement scope; if the stated interest rate does not exceed such upper limit, but the person subject to enforcement claims that the actual interest rate exceeds the limit, the person may file a lawsuit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 22 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　有下列情形之一的，被执行人可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-238)第二款规定申请不予执行公证债权文书：  
（一）被执行人未到场且未委托代理人到场办理公证的；  
（二）无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人没有监护人代为办理公证的；  
（三）公证员为本人、近亲属办理公证，或者办理与本人、近亲属有利害关系的公证的；  
（四）公证员办理该项公证有贪污受贿、徇私舞弊行为，已经由生效刑事法律文书等确认的；  
（五）其他严重违反法定公证程序的情形。  
被执行人以公证债权文书的内容与事实不符或者违反法律强制性规定等实体事由申请不予执行的，人民法院应当告知其依照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第一款规定提起诉讼。

**Article 12** If any of the following circumstances exists, the person subject to enforcement may apply for refusal of enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 238 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the person subject to enforcement was not present and did not authorize an agent to be present for the notarization;  
(2) a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct had no guardian to handle the notarization on their behalf;  
(3) the notary handled the notarization for himself or a close relative, or handled a notarization in which he or a close relative has an interest;  
(4) the notary engaged in embezzlement, bribery, or fraudulent acts in handling that notarization, which has been confirmed by effective criminal legal documents or other means;  
(5) other circumstances that seriously violate statutory notarization procedures.  
If the person subject to enforcement applies for refusal of enforcement on substantive grounds such as that the content of the notarized creditor's rights document is inconsistent with the facts or violates mandatory legal provisions, the People's Court shall inform the person to file a lawsuit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 22 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被执行人申请不予执行公证债权文书，应当在执行通知书送达之日起十五日内向执行法院提出书面申请，并提交相关证据材料；有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款第三项、第四项规定情形且执行程序尚未终结的，应当自知道或者应当知道有关事实之日起十五日内提出。  
公证债权文书执行案件被指定执行、提级执行、委托执行后，被执行人申请不予执行的，由提出申请时负责该案件执行的人民法院审查。

**Article 13** A person subject to enforcement who applies for refusal of enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document shall submit a written application to the enforcement court within fifteen days from the date of service of the enforcement notice, and submit relevant evidentiary materials; if there are circumstances specified in items (3) or (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions and the enforcement procedure has not yet concluded, the application shall be filed within fifteen days from the date the person knew or should have known of the relevant facts.  
After a notarized creditor's rights document enforcement case has been designated for enforcement, escalated for enforcement, or entrusted for enforcement, if the person subject to enforcement applies for refusal of enforcement, the application shall be examined by the People's Court responsible for enforcing the case at the time the application is filed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被执行人认为公证债权文书存在[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款规定的多个不予执行事由的，应当在不予执行案件审查期间一并提出。  
不予执行申请被裁定驳回后，同一被执行人再次提出申请的，人民法院不予受理。但有证据证明不予执行事由在不予执行申请被裁定驳回后知道的，可以在执行程序终结前提出。

**Article 14** If the person subject to enforcement believes that the notarized creditor's rights document has multiple grounds for refusal of enforcement as specified in paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, the person shall raise all such grounds together during the examination period of the refusal of enforcement case.  
After an application for refusal of enforcement has been rejected by a ruling, if the same person subject to enforcement files another application, the People's Court shall not accept it. However, if there is evidence proving that the ground for refusal of enforcement became known after the rejection ruling, the application may be filed before the conclusion of the enforcement procedure.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院审查不予执行公证债权文书案件，案情复杂、争议较大的，应当进行听证。必要时可以向公证机构调阅公证案卷，要求公证机构作出书面说明，或者通知公证员到庭说明情况。

**Article 15** When examining a case involving refusal of enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document, if the case is complex and highly disputed, the People's Court shall hold a hearing. When necessary, it may consult the notarial files from the notarial institution, require the notarial institution to provide a written explanation, or notify the notary to appear in court to explain the circumstances.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院审查不予执行公证债权文书案件，应当在受理之日起六十日内审查完毕并作出裁定；有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三十日。

**Article 16** The People's Court shall complete the examination and issue a ruling within sixty days from the date of acceptance of a case involving refusal of enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document; if there are special circumstances requiring an extension, with the approval of the president of the court, the period may be extended by thirty days.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院审查不予执行公证债权文书案件期间，不停止执行。  
被执行人提供充分、有效的担保，请求停止相应处分措施的，人民法院可以准许；申请执行人提供充分、有效的担保，请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。

**Article 17** During the period when the People's Court examines a case involving refusal of enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document, enforcement shall not be suspended.  
If the person subject to enforcement provides sufficient and effective security and requests suspension of the corresponding disposal measures, the People's Court may permit it; if the applicant for enforcement provides sufficient and effective security and requests continuation of enforcement, enforcement shall continue.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　被执行人依照[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款规定申请不予执行，人民法院经审查认为理由成立的，裁定不予执行；理由不成立的，裁定驳回不予执行申请。  
公证债权文书部分内容具有[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第一款规定情形的，人民法院应当裁定对该部分不予执行；应当不予执行部分与其他部分不可分的，裁定对该公证债权文书不予执行。

**Article 18** If the person subject to enforcement applies for refusal of enforcement in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, and the People's Court finds after examination that the reasons are established, it shall rule to refuse enforcement; if the reasons are not established, it shall rule to reject the application for refusal of enforcement.  
If part of the content of the notarized creditor's rights document has circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of Article 12 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall rule to refuse enforcement of that part; if the part that should be refused enforcement is inseparable from other parts, it shall rule to refuse enforcement of the entire notarized creditor's rights document.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院认定执行公证债权文书违背公序良俗的，裁定不予执行。

**Article 19** If the People's Court determines that enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document violates public order and good customs, it shall rule to refuse enforcement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　公证债权文书被裁定不予执行的，当事人可以就该公证债权文书涉及的民事权利义务争议向人民法院提起诉讼；公证债权文书被裁定部分不予执行的，当事人可以就该部分争议提起诉讼。  
当事人对不予执行裁定提出执行异议或者申请复议的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 20** If enforcement of a notarized creditor's rights document is ruled to be refused, the party may file a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the dispute over civil rights and obligations involved in the notarized creditor's rights document; if enforcement of a part of the notarized creditor's rights document is ruled to be refused, the party may file a lawsuit regarding the dispute over that part.  
If a party raises an enforcement objection or applies for reconsideration against the ruling of refusal of enforcement, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　当事人不服驳回不予执行申请裁定的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议。上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起三十日内审查。经审查，理由成立的，裁定撤销原裁定，不予执行该公证债权文书；理由不成立的，裁定驳回复议申请。复议期间，不停止执行。

**Article 21** If a party is dissatisfied with a ruling rejecting an application for refusal of enforcement, the party may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling. The People's Court at the next higher level shall complete the examination within thirty days from the date of receiving the reconsideration application. Upon examination, if the reasons are established, it shall rule to cancel the original ruling and refuse enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document; if the reasons are not established, it shall rule to reject the reconsideration application. During the reconsideration period, enforcement shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　有下列情形之一的，债务人可以在执行程序终结前，以债权人为被告，向执行法院提起诉讼，请求不予执行公证债权文书：  
（一）公证债权文书载明的民事权利义务关系与事实不符；  
（二）经公证的债权文书具有法律规定的无效、可撤销等情形；  
（三）公证债权文书载明的债权因清偿、提存、抵销、免除等原因全部或者部分消灭。  
债务人提起诉讼，不影响人民法院对公证债权文书的执行。债务人提供充分、有效的担保，请求停止相应处分措施的，人民法院可以准许；债权人提供充分、有效的担保，请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。

**Article 22** If any of the following circumstances exists, the debtor may, before the conclusion of the enforcement procedure, file a lawsuit with the enforcement court against the creditor as defendant, requesting refusal of enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document:  
(1) the civil rights and obligations relationship stated in the notarized creditor's rights document is inconsistent with the facts;  
(2) the notarized creditor's rights document has circumstances such as invalidity or revocability as provided by law;  
(3) the creditor's rights stated in the notarized creditor's rights document have been extinguished in whole or in part due to performance, deposit, set-off, remission, or other reasons.  
If the debtor files a lawsuit, it shall not affect the enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document by the People's Court. If the debtor provides sufficient and effective security and requests suspension of the corresponding disposal measures, the People's Court may permit it; if the creditor provides sufficient and effective security and requests continuation of enforcement, enforcement shall continue.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对债务人依照[本规定第二十二条](#art-22)第一款规定提起的诉讼，人民法院经审理认为理由成立的，判决不予执行或者部分不予执行；理由不成立的，判决驳回诉讼请求。  
当事人同时就公证债权文书涉及的民事权利义务争议提出诉讼请求的，人民法院可以在判决中一并作出裁判。

**Article 23** With respect to a lawsuit filed by the debtor in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 22 of these Provisions, if the People's Court finds after trial that the reasons are established, it shall render a judgment of refusal of enforcement or partial refusal of enforcement; if the reasons are not established, it shall render a judgment rejecting the claims.  
If the parties also make claims regarding the dispute over civil rights and obligations involved in the notarized creditor's rights document, the People's Court may make a ruling on such claims in the same judgment.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有下列情形之一的，债权人、利害关系人可以就公证债权文书涉及的民事权利义务争议直接向有管辖权的人民法院提起诉讼：  
（一）公证债权文书载明的民事权利义务关系与事实不符；  
（二）经公证的债权文书具有法律规定的无效、可撤销等情形。  
债权人提起诉讼，诉讼案件受理后又申请执行公证债权文书的，人民法院不予受理。进入执行程序后债权人又提起诉讼的，诉讼案件受理后，人民法院可以裁定终结公证债权文书的执行；债权人请求继续执行其未提出争议部分的，人民法院可以准许。  
利害关系人提起诉讼，不影响人民法院对公证债权文书的执行。利害关系人提供充分、有效的担保，请求停止相应处分措施的，人民法院可以准许；债权人提供充分、有效的担保，请求继续执行的，应当继续执行。

**Article 24** If any of the following circumstances exists, the creditor or an interested person may directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court having jurisdiction regarding the dispute over civil rights and obligations involved in the notarized creditor's rights document:  
(1) the civil rights and obligations relationship stated in the notarized creditor's rights document is inconsistent with the facts;  
(2) the notarized creditor's rights document has circumstances such as invalidity or revocability as provided by law.  
If the creditor files a lawsuit, and after the lawsuit case is accepted, the creditor applies for enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document, the People's Court shall not accept the enforcement application. If the enforcement procedure has already commenced and the creditor files a lawsuit, after the lawsuit case is accepted, the People's Court may rule to terminate enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document; if the creditor requests to continue enforcement of the part not disputed, the People's Court may permit it.  
If an interested person files a lawsuit, it shall not affect the enforcement of the notarized creditor's rights document by the People's Court. If the interested person provides sufficient and effective security and requests suspension of the corresponding disposal measures, the People's Court may permit it; if the creditor provides sufficient and effective security and requests continuation of enforcement, enforcement shall continue.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定自2018年10月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前最高人民法院公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 25** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 2018.  
If any judicial interpretations promulgated by the Supreme People's Court before the date on which these Provisions come into force are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
