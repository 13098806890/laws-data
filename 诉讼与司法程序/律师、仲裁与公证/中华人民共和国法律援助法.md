# 中华人民共和国法律援助法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2021-08-20  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> （2021年8月20日第十三届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第三十次会议通过） 目　　录 第一章　总　　则 第二章　机构和人员 第三章　形式和范围 第四章　程序和实施 第五章　保障和监督 第六章　法律责任 第七章　附　　则 第一章　总　　则 第一条　为了规范和促进法律援助工作，保障公民和有关当事人的合法权益，保障法律正确实施，维护社会公平正义，制定本法。 第二条　本法所称法律援助，是国家建立的为经济困难公民和符合法定条件的其他当事人无偿提供法律咨询、代理、刑事辩护等法律服务的制度，是公共法律服务体系的组成部分。 第三条　法律援助工作坚持中国共产党领导，坚持以人民为中心，尊重和保障人权，遵循公开、公平、公正的原则，实行国家保障与社会参与相结合。 第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将法律援助工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、基本公共服务体系，保障法律援助事业与经济社会协调发展。 县级以上人民政府应当健全法律援助保障体系，将法律援助相关经费列入本级政府预算，建立动态调整机制，保障法律援助工作需要，促进法律援助均衡发展。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范和促进法律援助工作，保障公民和有关当事人的合法权益，保障法律正确实施，维护社会公平正义，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating and promoting legal aid work, protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens and relevant parties, ensuring the correct implementation of law, and safeguarding social fairness and justice.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本法所称法律援助，是国家建立的为经济困难公民和符合法定条件的其他当事人无偿提供法律咨询、代理、刑事辩护等法律服务的制度，是公共法律服务体系的组成部分。

**Article 2** Legal aid as mentioned in this Law refers to the system established by the State to provide free legal services including legal consultation, agency, criminal defense, etc., to citizens with financial difficulties and other parties meeting statutory conditions, and is a component of the public legal service system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　法律援助工作坚持中国共产党领导，坚持以人民为中心，尊重和保障人权，遵循公开、公平、公正的原则，实行国家保障与社会参与相结合。

**Article 3** Legal aid work shall adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China, adhere to a people-centered approach, respect and safeguard human rights, follow the principles of openness, fairness and justice, and combine state guarantees with social participation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　县级以上人民政府应当将法律援助工作纳入国民经济和社会发展规划、基本公共服务体系，保障法律援助事业与经济社会协调发展。  
县级以上人民政府应当健全法律援助保障体系，将法律援助相关经费列入本级政府预算，建立动态调整机制，保障法律援助工作需要，促进法律援助均衡发展。

**Article 4** People's governments at or above the county level shall incorporate legal aid work into national economic and social development plans and basic public service systems, and ensure the coordinated development of legal aid undertakings with the economy and society.  
People's governments at or above the county level shall improve the legal aid guarantee system, include relevant legal aid funds in their government budgets, establish a dynamic adjustment mechanism, ensure the needs of legal aid work, and promote balanced development of legal aid.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国务院司法行政部门指导、监督全国的法律援助工作。县级以上地方人民政府司法行政部门指导、监督本行政区域的法律援助工作。  
县级以上人民政府其他有关部门依照各自职责，为法律援助工作提供支持和保障。

**Article 5** The judicial administrative department of the State Council shall guide and supervise legal aid work nationwide. The judicial administrative departments of local people's governments at or above the county level shall guide and supervise legal aid work within their respective administrative regions.  
Other relevant departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall, in accordance with their respective duties, provide support and guarantee for legal aid work.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关应当在各自职责范围内保障当事人依法获得法律援助，为法律援助人员开展工作提供便利。

**Article 6** People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, and public security organs shall, within their respective duties, guarantee that parties obtain legal aid according to law, and provide convenience for legal aid personnel to carry out their work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　律师协会应当指导和支持律师事务所、律师参与法律援助工作。

**Article 7** Lawyers associations shall guide and support law firms and lawyers in participating in legal aid work.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家鼓励和支持群团组织、事业单位、社会组织在司法行政部门指导下，依法提供法律援助。

**Article 8** The State encourages and supports mass organizations, public institutions, and social organizations to provide legal aid according to law under the guidance of judicial administrative departments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国家鼓励和支持企业事业单位、社会组织和个人等社会力量，依法通过捐赠等方式为法律援助事业提供支持；对符合条件的，给予税收优惠。

**Article 9** The State encourages and supports enterprises, public institutions, social organizations, individuals and other social forces to provide support for legal aid undertakings through donations and other means according to law; those meeting the conditions shall be granted tax preferences.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　司法行政部门应当开展经常性的法律援助宣传教育，普及法律援助知识。  
新闻媒体应当积极开展法律援助公益宣传，并加强舆论监督。

**Article 10** Judicial administrative departments shall carry out regular legal aid publicity and education to popularize legal aid knowledge.  
News media shall actively carry out public welfare publicity on legal aid and strengthen public opinion supervision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家对在法律援助工作中做出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 11** The State shall commend and reward organizations and individuals that have made outstanding contributions in legal aid work in accordance with relevant regulations.

### 第二章　机构和人员

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　县级以上人民政府司法行政部门应当设立法律援助机构。法律援助机构负责组织实施法律援助工作，受理、审查法律援助申请，指派律师、基层法律服务工作者、法律援助志愿者等法律援助人员提供法律援助，支付法律援助补贴。

**Article 12** Judicial administrative departments of people's governments at or above the county level shall establish legal aid institutions. Legal aid institutions shall be responsible for organizing and implementing legal aid work, accepting and examining applications for legal aid, assigning lawyers, grassroots legal service workers, legal aid volunteers and other legal aid personnel to provide legal aid, and paying legal aid subsidies.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　法律援助机构根据工作需要，可以安排本机构具有律师资格或者法律职业资格的工作人员提供法律援助；可以设置法律援助工作站或者联络点，就近受理法律援助申请。

**Article 13** Legal aid institutions may, as needed for work, arrange staff members of their own institutions who have lawyer qualifications or legal professional qualifications to provide legal aid; they may set up legal aid work stations or contact points to accept legal aid applications nearby.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　法律援助机构可以在人民法院、人民检察院和看守所等场所派驻值班律师，依法为没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律援助。

**Article 14** Legal aid institutions may station duty lawyers at People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, detention centers and other places to provide legal aid according to law for criminal suspects or defendants without defenders.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　司法行政部门可以通过政府采购等方式，择优选择律师事务所等法律服务机构为受援人提供法律援助。

**Article 15** Judicial administrative departments may, through government procurement and other means, select law firms and other legal service institutions with excellence to provide legal aid for recipients.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　律师事务所、基层法律服务所、律师、基层法律服务工作者负有依法提供法律援助的义务。  
律师事务所、基层法律服务所应当支持和保障本所律师、基层法律服务工作者履行法律援助义务。

**Article 16** Law firms, grassroots legal service offices, lawyers, and grassroots legal service workers have the obligation to provide legal aid according to law.  
Law firms and grassroots legal service offices shall support and ensure that their lawyers and grassroots legal service workers fulfill the obligation to provide legal aid.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家鼓励和规范法律援助志愿服务；支持符合条件的个人作为法律援助志愿者，依法提供法律援助。  
高等院校、科研机构可以组织从事法学教育、研究工作的人员和法学专业学生作为法律援助志愿者，在司法行政部门指导下，为当事人提供法律咨询、代拟法律文书等法律援助。  
法律援助志愿者具体管理办法由国务院有关部门规定。

**Article 17** The State encourages and regulates volunteer legal aid services; supports eligible individuals to serve as legal aid volunteers to provide legal aid according to law.  
Institutions of higher learning and scientific research institutions may organize personnel engaged in legal education and research and law students as legal aid volunteers to provide legal consultation, drafting of legal documents and other legal aid for parties under the guidance of judicial administrative departments.  
Specific management measures for legal aid volunteers shall be prescribed by relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国家建立健全法律服务资源依法跨区域流动机制，鼓励和支持律师事务所、律师、法律援助志愿者等在法律服务资源相对短缺地区提供法律援助。

**Article 18** The State shall establish and improve a mechanism for cross-regional flow of legal service resources according to law, and encourages and supports law firms, lawyers, legal aid volunteers, etc., to provide legal aid in areas with relatively scarce legal service resources.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　法律援助人员应当依法履行职责，及时为受援人提供符合标准的法律援助服务，维护受援人的合法权益。

**Article 19** Legal aid personnel shall perform their duties according to law, provide timely legal aid services that meet standards for recipients, and safeguard the lawful rights and interests of recipients.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　法律援助人员应当恪守职业道德和执业纪律，不得向受援人收取任何财物。

**Article 20** Legal aid personnel shall strictly abide by professional ethics and practice discipline, and shall not collect any money or property from recipients.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　法律援助机构、法律援助人员对提供法律援助过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私应当予以保密。

**Article 21** Legal aid institutions and legal aid personnel shall keep confidential any state secrets, trade secrets, and personal privacy learned during the provision of legal aid.

### 第三章　形式和范围

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　法律援助机构可以组织法律援助人员依法提供下列形式的法律援助服务：  
（一）法律咨询；  
（二）代拟法律文书；  
（三）刑事辩护与代理；  
（四）民事案件、行政案件、国家赔偿案件的诉讼代理及非诉讼代理；  
（五）值班律师法律帮助；  
（六）劳动争议调解与仲裁代理；  
（七）法律、法规、规章规定的其他形式。

**Article 22** Legal aid institutions may organize legal aid personnel to provide legal aid services in the following forms according to law:  
(1) Legal consultation;  
(2) Drafting legal documents;  
(3) Criminal defense and representation;  
(4) Litigation and non-litigation representation in civil cases, administrative cases, and state compensation cases;  
(5) Legal assistance by duty lawyers;  
(6) Representation in labor dispute mediation and arbitration;  
(7) Other forms prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　法律援助机构应当通过服务窗口、电话、网络等多种方式提供法律咨询服务；提示当事人享有依法申请法律援助的权利，并告知申请法律援助的条件和程序。

**Article 23** Legal aid institutions shall provide legal consultation services through service windows, telephone, internet, and other means; remind parties of their right to apply for legal aid according to law, and inform them of the conditions and procedures for applying for legal aid.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　刑事案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人因经济困难或者其他原因没有委托辩护人的，本人及其近亲属可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助。

**Article 24** If a criminal suspect or defendant in a criminal case does not have a defender due to financial difficulties or other reasons, the person or his/her near relatives may apply to a legal aid institution for legal aid.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　刑事案件的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人属于下列人员之一，没有委托辩护人的，人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关应当通知法律援助机构指派律师担任辩护人：  
（一）未成年人；  
（二）视力、听力、言语残疾人；  
（三）不能完全辨认自己行为的成年人；  
（四）可能被判处无期徒刑、死刑的人；  
（五）申请法律援助的死刑复核案件被告人；  
（六）缺席审判案件的被告人；  
（七）法律法规规定的其他人员。  
其他适用普通程序审理的刑事案件，被告人没有委托辩护人的，人民法院可以通知法律援助机构指派律师担任辩护人。

**Article 25** If a criminal suspect or defendant in a criminal case falls under any of the following categories and does not have a defender, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall notify a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to serve as defender:  
(1) Minors;  
(2) Persons with visual, hearing, or speech disabilities;  
(3) Adults who cannot fully recognize their own conduct;  
(4) Persons who may be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty;  
(5) Defendants in death penalty review cases who apply for legal aid;  
(6) Defendants in trials by default;  
(7) Other persons prescribed by laws and regulations.  
For other criminal cases tried under ordinary procedures, if the defendant does not have a defender, the People's Court may notify a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to serve as defender.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对可能被判处无期徒刑、死刑的人，以及死刑复核案件的被告人，法律援助机构收到人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关通知后，应当指派具有三年以上相关执业经历的律师担任辩护人。

**Article 26** For persons who may be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty, and defendants in death penalty review cases, after receiving a notice from the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ, the legal aid institution shall assign a lawyer with at least three years of relevant practice experience to serve as defender.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关通知法律援助机构指派律师担任辩护人时，不得限制或者损害犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托辩护人的权利。

**Article 27** When notifying a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to serve as defender, the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ shall not restrict or impair the right of the criminal suspect or defendant to entrust a defender.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　强制医疗案件的被申请人或者被告人没有委托诉讼代理人的，人民法院应当通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供法律援助。

**Article 28** If the respondent or defendant in a compulsory medical treatment case does not have a litigation representative, the People's Court shall notify a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to provide legal aid.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　刑事公诉案件的被害人及其法定代理人或者近亲属，刑事自诉案件的自诉人及其法定代理人，刑事附带民事诉讼案件的原告人及其法定代理人，因经济困难没有委托诉讼代理人的，可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助。

**Article 29** If the victim and his/her legal representative or near relative in a criminal public prosecution case, the private prosecutor and his/her legal representative in a criminal private prosecution case, or the plaintiff and his/her legal representative in a criminal incidental civil action case, do not have a litigation representative due to financial difficulties, they may apply to a legal aid institution for legal aid.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　值班律师应当依法为没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律咨询、程序选择建议、申请变更强制措施、对案件处理提出意见等法律帮助。

**Article 30** Duty lawyers shall, according to law, provide legal assistance to criminal suspects or defendants without defenders, including legal consultation, suggestions on procedural choices, applying for modification of compulsory measures, and providing opinions on case handling.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　下列事项的当事人，因经济困难没有委托代理人的，可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助：  
（一）依法请求国家赔偿；  
（二）请求给予社会保险待遇或者社会救助；  
（三）请求发给抚恤金；  
（四）请求给付赡养费、抚养费、扶养费；  
（五）请求确认劳动关系或者支付劳动报酬；  
（六）请求认定公民无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力；  
（七）请求工伤事故、交通事故、食品药品安全事故、医疗事故人身损害赔偿；  
（八）请求环境污染、生态破坏损害赔偿；  
（九）法律、法规、规章规定的其他情形。

**Article 31** Parties to the following matters who do not have an authorized agent due to financial difficulties may apply to a legal aid institution for legal aid:  
(1) Claiming state compensation according to law;  
(2) Claiming social insurance benefits or social assistance;  
(3) Claiming pension payments;  
(4) Claiming payment of alimony, child support, or support for other dependents;  
(5) Claiming confirmation of labor relationship or payment of labor remuneration;  
(6) Claiming determination of a citizen as having no civil capacity or limited civil capacity;  
(7) Claiming personal injury damages for work-related injury, traffic accidents, food and drug safety accidents, or medical accidents;  
(8) Claiming damages for environmental pollution or ecological destruction;  
(9) Other circumstances prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　有下列情形之一，当事人申请法律援助的，不受经济困难条件的限制：  
（一）英雄烈士近亲属为维护英雄烈士的人格权益；  
（二）因见义勇为行为主张相关民事权益；  
（三）再审改判无罪请求国家赔偿；  
（四）遭受虐待、遗弃或者家庭暴力的受害人主张相关权益；  
（五）法律、法规、规章规定的其他情形。

**Article 32** Where a party applies for legal aid under any of the following circumstances, the conditions on financial difficulties shall not apply:  
(1) Near relatives of heroes and martyrs defending the personality rights and interests of heroes and martyrs;  
(2) Claiming relevant civil rights and interests due to acts of bravery;  
(3) Claiming state compensation after being acquitted on retrial;  
(4) Victims of abuse, abandonment, or domestic violence claiming relevant rights and interests;  
(5) Other circumstances prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　当事人不服司法机关生效裁判或者决定提出申诉或者申请再审，人民法院决定、裁定再审或者人民检察院提出抗诉，因经济困难没有委托辩护人或者诉讼代理人的，本人及其近亲属可以向法律援助机构申请法律援助。

**Article 33** If a party files a petition or applies for retrial against an effective judgment or decision of a judicial authority, and the People's Court decides or rules to retry or the People's Procuratorate files a protest, and the party does not have a defender or litigation representative due to financial difficulties, the party and his/her near relatives may apply to a legal aid institution for legal aid.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　经济困难的标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据本行政区域经济发展状况和法律援助工作需要确定，并实行动态调整。

**Article 34** The standard for financial difficulties shall be determined by the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government based on the economic development status of their respective administrative regions and the needs of legal aid work, and shall be subject to dynamic adjustment.

### 第四章　程序和实施

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关和有关部门在办理案件或者相关事务中，应当及时告知有关当事人有权依法申请法律援助。

**Article 35** The People's Court, People's Procuratorate, public security organ, and relevant departments shall, when handling cases or related matters, promptly inform relevant parties of their right to apply for legal aid according to law.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关办理刑事案件，发现有[本法第二十五条](#art-25)第一款、第二十八条规定情形的，应当在三日内通知法律援助机构指派律师。法律援助机构收到通知后，应当在三日内指派律师并通知人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关。

**Article 36** When handling criminal cases, if the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ discovers circumstances specified in Article 25, paragraph 1, or Article 28 of this Law, it shall notify the legal aid institution to assign a lawyer within three days. After receiving the notice, the legal aid institution shall assign a lawyer within three days and notify the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关应当保障值班律师依法提供法律帮助，告知没有辩护人的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权约见值班律师，并依法为值班律师了解案件有关情况、阅卷、会见等提供便利。

**Article 37** The People's Court, People's Procuratorate, and public security organ shall ensure that duty lawyers provide legal assistance according to law, inform criminal suspects or defendants without defenders of their right to meet with duty lawyers, and provide convenience for duty lawyers to understand case-related information, review case files, and meet with clients according to law.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　对诉讼事项的法律援助，由申请人向办案机关所在地的法律援助机构提出申请；对非诉讼事项的法律援助，由申请人向争议处理机关所在地或者事由发生地的法律援助机构提出申请。

**Article 38** For legal aid for litigation matters, the applicant shall submit the application to the legal aid institution at the location of the case-handling authority; for legal aid for non-litigation matters, the applicant shall submit the application to the legal aid institution at the location of the dispute-handling authority or where the matter occurred.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、服刑人员，以及强制隔离戒毒人员等提出法律援助申请的，办案机关、监管场所应当在二十四小时内将申请转交法律援助机构。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人通过值班律师提出代理、刑事辩护等法律援助申请的，值班律师应当在二十四小时内将申请转交法律援助机构。

**Article 39** If a criminal suspect, defendant, prisoner in custody, or a person undergoing compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation submits an application for legal aid, the case-handling authority or custodial facility shall transfer the application to the legal aid institution within 24 hours.  
If a criminal suspect or defendant submits an application for legal aid such as representation or criminal defense through a duty lawyer, the duty lawyer shall transfer the application to the legal aid institution within 24 hours.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人需要法律援助的，可以由其法定代理人代为提出申请。法定代理人侵犯无民事行为能力人、限制民事行为能力人合法权益的，其他法定代理人或者近亲属可以代为提出法律援助申请。  
被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、服刑人员，以及强制隔离戒毒人员，可以由其法定代理人或者近亲属代为提出法律援助申请。

**Article 40** If a person with no civil capacity or with limited civil capacity needs legal aid, his/her legal representative may submit the application on his/her behalf. If the legal representative infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the person with no civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, other legal representatives or near relatives may submit the legal aid application on his/her behalf.  
A criminal suspect, defendant, prisoner in custody, or a person undergoing compulsory isolation for drug rehabilitation may have his/her legal representative or near relative submit the legal aid application on his/her behalf.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　因经济困难申请法律援助的，申请人应当如实说明经济困难状况。  
法律援助机构核查申请人的经济困难状况，可以通过信息共享查询，或者由申请人进行个人诚信承诺。  
法律援助机构开展核查工作，有关部门、单位、村民委员会、居民委员会和个人应当予以配合。

**Article 41** If applying for legal aid due to financial difficulties, the applicant shall truthfully state the financial difficulty status.  
When verifying the applicant's financial difficulty status, the legal aid institution may conduct inquiries through information sharing or require the applicant to make a personal integrity commitment.  
When the legal aid institution carries out verification work, relevant departments, units, villagers' committees, residents' committees, and individuals shall cooperate.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　法律援助申请人有材料证明属于下列人员之一的，免予核查经济困难状况：  
（一）无固定生活来源的未成年人、老年人、残疾人等特定群体；  
（二）社会救助、司法救助或者优抚对象；  
（三）申请支付劳动报酬或者请求工伤事故人身损害赔偿的进城务工人员；  
（四）法律、法规、规章规定的其他人员。

**Article 42** If a legal aid applicant has materials proving to be one of the following persons, verification of financial difficulty status shall be exempted:  
(1) Specific groups such as minors, elderly persons, and disabled persons without stable sources of income;  
(2) Recipients of social assistance, judicial assistance, or special care and pension benefits;  
(3) Migrant workers applying for payment of labor remuneration or claiming personal injury damages for work-related injuries;  
(4) Other persons prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　法律援助机构应当自收到法律援助申请之日起七日内进行审查，作出是否给予法律援助的决定。决定给予法律援助的，应当自作出决定之日起三日内指派法律援助人员为受援人提供法律援助；决定不给予法律援助的，应当书面告知申请人，并说明理由。  
申请人提交的申请材料不齐全的，法律援助机构应当一次性告知申请人需要补充的材料或者要求申请人作出说明。申请人未按要求补充材料或者作出说明的，视为撤回申请。

**Article 43** The legal aid institution shall, within seven days from the date of receiving the application for legal aid, review the application and make a decision on whether to grant legal aid. If it decides to grant legal aid, it shall assign legal aid personnel to provide legal aid to the recipient within three days from the date of making the decision; if it decides not to grant legal aid, it shall notify the applicant in writing and state the reasons.  
If the application materials submitted by the applicant are incomplete, the legal aid institution shall inform the applicant at one time of the materials to be supplemented or require the applicant to provide explanations. If the applicant fails to supplement the materials or provide explanations as required, the application shall be deemed withdrawn.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　法律援助机构收到法律援助申请后，发现有下列情形之一的，可以决定先行提供法律援助：  
（一）距法定时效或者期限届满不足七日，需要及时提起诉讼或者申请仲裁、行政复议；  
（二）需要立即申请财产保全、证据保全或者先予执行；  
（三）法律、法规、规章规定的其他情形。  
法律援助机构先行提供法律援助的，受援人应当及时补办有关手续，补充有关材料。

**Article 44** After receiving an application for legal aid, if the legal aid institution finds any of the following circumstances, it may decide to provide legal aid in advance:  
(1) There are less than seven days before the statutory limitation or expiration of a time limit, and it is necessary to file a lawsuit or apply for arbitration or administrative reconsideration in a timely manner;  
(2) It is necessary to immediately apply for property preservation, evidence preservation, or advance execution;  
(3) Other circumstances prescribed by laws, regulations, and rules.  
If the legal aid institution provides legal aid in advance, the recipient shall timely complete the relevant procedures and supplement the relevant materials.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　法律援助机构为老年人、残疾人提供法律援助服务的，应当根据实际情况提供无障碍设施设备和服务。  
法律法规对向特定群体提供法律援助有其他特别规定的，依照其规定。

**Article 45** When providing legal aid services to elderly persons and disabled persons, legal aid institutions shall provide accessible facilities, equipment, and services based on actual circumstances.  
If laws and regulations have other special provisions on providing legal aid to specific groups, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　法律援助人员接受指派后，无正当理由不得拒绝、拖延或者终止提供法律援助服务。  
法律援助人员应当按照规定向受援人通报法律援助事项办理情况，不得损害受援人合法权益。

**Article 46** After accepting an assignment, legal aid personnel shall not refuse, delay, or terminate the provision of legal aid services without justifiable reasons.  
Legal aid personnel shall, in accordance with regulations, report to the recipient on the handling of legal aid matters, and shall not harm the lawful rights and interests of the recipient.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　受援人应当向法律援助人员如实陈述与法律援助事项有关的情况，及时提供证据材料，协助、配合办理法律援助事项。

**Article 47** The recipient shall truthfully state the circumstances related to the legal aid matter to the legal aid personnel, provide evidence materials in a timely manner, and assist and cooperate in handling the legal aid matter.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　有下列情形之一的，法律援助机构应当作出终止法律援助的决定：  
（一）受援人以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获得法律援助；  
（二）受援人故意隐瞒与案件有关的重要事实或者提供虚假证据；  
（三）受援人利用法律援助从事违法活动；  
（四）受援人的经济状况发生变化，不再符合法律援助条件；  
（五）案件终止审理或者已经被撤销；  
（六）受援人自行委托律师或者其他代理人；  
（七）受援人有正当理由要求终止法律援助；  
（八）法律法规规定的其他情形。  
法律援助人员发现有前款规定情形的，应当及时向法律援助机构报告。

**Article 48** Under any of the following circumstances, the legal aid institution shall make a decision to terminate legal aid:  
(1) The recipient obtained legal aid by deception or other improper means;  
(2) The recipient intentionally concealed important facts related to the case or provided false evidence;  
(3) The recipient uses legal aid to engage in illegal activities;  
(4) The recipient's financial situation changes and no longer meets the conditions for legal aid;  
(5) The case is terminated or has been withdrawn;  
(6) The recipient entrusts a lawyer or other agent by himself/herself;  
(7) The recipient requests termination of legal aid with justifiable reasons;  
(8) Other circumstances prescribed by laws and regulations.  
If legal aid personnel discover any circumstance specified in the preceding paragraph, they shall promptly report to the legal aid institution.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　申请人、受援人对法律援助机构不予法律援助、终止法律援助的决定有异议的，可以向设立该法律援助机构的司法行政部门提出。  
司法行政部门应当自收到异议之日起五日内进行审查，作出维持法律援助机构决定或者责令法律援助机构改正的决定。  
申请人、受援人对司法行政部门维持法律援助机构决定不服的，可以依法申请行政复议或者提起行政诉讼。

**Article 49** If an applicant or recipient disagrees with a decision of a legal aid institution not to grant legal aid or to terminate legal aid, they may raise the objection with the judicial administrative department that established the legal aid institution.  
The judicial administrative department shall, within five days from the date of receiving the objection, conduct a review and make a decision to uphold the legal aid institution's decision or order the legal aid institution to correct it.  
If an applicant or recipient is dissatisfied with the judicial administrative department's decision to uphold the legal aid institution's decision, they may apply for administrative reconsideration or institute an administrative action according to law.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　法律援助事项办理结束后，法律援助人员应当及时向法律援助机构报告，提交有关法律文书的副本或者复印件、办理情况报告等材料。

**Article 50** After the handling of a legal aid matter is concluded, legal aid personnel shall promptly report to the legal aid institution and submit copies or photocopies of relevant legal documents, handling reports, and other materials.

### 第五章　保障和监督

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　国家加强法律援助信息化建设，促进司法行政部门与司法机关及其他有关部门实现信息共享和工作协同。

**Article 51** The State shall strengthen the informatization construction of legal aid, and promote the judicial administrative departments, judicial organs and other relevant departments to achieve information sharing and work coordination.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　法律援助机构应当依照有关规定及时向法律援助人员支付法律援助补贴。  
法律援助补贴的标准，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门会同同级财政部门，根据当地经济发展水平和法律援助的服务类型、承办成本、基本劳务费用等确定，并实行动态调整。  
法律援助补贴免征增值税和个人所得税。

**Article 52** A legal aid institution shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, pay legal aid subsidies to legal aid personnel in a timely manner.  
The standards for legal aid subsidies shall be determined by the judicial administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the financial department at the same level, based on the local economic development level and the service types, undertaking costs, basic labor expenses, etc. of legal aid, and shall be subject to dynamic adjustment.  
Legal aid subsidies shall be exempted from value-added tax and individual income tax.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　人民法院应当根据情况对受援人缓收、减收或者免收诉讼费用；对法律援助人员复制相关材料等费用予以免收或者减收。  
公证机构、司法鉴定机构应当对受援人减收或者免收公证费、鉴定费。

**Article 53** A People's Court shall, according to the circumstances, defer, reduce, or waive the litigation costs for a recipient; and shall waive or reduce the fees for copying relevant materials, etc. for legal aid personnel.  
Notarization institutions and judicial appraisal institutions shall reduce or waive notarization fees and appraisal fees for a recipient.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　县级以上人民政府司法行政部门应当有计划地对法律援助人员进行培训，提高法律援助人员的专业素质和服务能力。

**Article 54** The judicial administrative department of a people's government at or above the county level shall conduct planned training for legal aid personnel to improve their professional quality and service capability.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　受援人有权向法律援助机构、法律援助人员了解法律援助事项办理情况；法律援助机构、法律援助人员未依法履行职责的，受援人可以向司法行政部门投诉，并可以请求法律援助机构更换法律援助人员。

**Article 55** A recipient shall have the right to learn from a legal aid institution and legal aid personnel about the handling of legal aid matters; if the legal aid institution or legal aid personnel fails to perform its/his duties according to law, the recipient may lodge a complaint with the judicial administrative department, and may request the legal aid institution to replace the legal aid personnel.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　司法行政部门应当建立法律援助工作投诉查处制度；接到投诉后，应当依照有关规定受理和调查处理，并及时向投诉人告知处理结果。

**Article 56** The judicial administrative department shall establish a system for accepting and investigating complaints about legal aid work; after receiving a complaint, it shall accept, investigate and handle the complaint in accordance with the relevant provisions, and shall inform the complainant of the handling result in a timely manner.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　司法行政部门应当加强对法律援助服务的监督，制定法律援助服务质量标准，通过第三方评估等方式定期进行质量考核。

**Article 57** The judicial administrative department shall strengthen the supervision of legal aid services, formulate quality standards for legal aid services, and conduct regular quality assessments through third-party evaluations and other means.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　司法行政部门、法律援助机构应当建立法律援助信息公开制度，定期向社会公布法律援助资金使用、案件办理、质量考核结果等情况，接受社会监督。

**Article 58** The judicial administrative department and legal aid institutions shall establish a legal aid information disclosure system, regularly disclose to the public the use of legal aid funds, case handling, quality assessment results, and other information, and shall accept social supervision.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　法律援助机构应当综合运用庭审旁听、案卷检查、征询司法机关意见和回访受援人等措施，督促法律援助人员提升服务质量。

**Article 59** A legal aid institution shall comprehensively use measures such as courtroom observation, case file inspection, soliciting opinions from judicial organs, and follow-up visits to recipients, to urge legal aid personnel to improve service quality.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　律师协会应当将律师事务所、律师履行法律援助义务的情况纳入年度考核内容，对拒不履行或者怠于履行法律援助义务的律师事务所、律师，依照有关规定进行惩戒。

**Article 60** A lawyers association shall include the performance of legal aid obligations by law firms and lawyers in the annual assessment, and shall impose sanctions on law firms and lawyers that refuse to perform or are negligent in performing their legal aid obligations in accordance with the relevant provisions.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　法律援助机构及其工作人员有下列情形之一的，由设立该法律援助机构的司法行政部门责令限期改正；有违法所得的，责令退还或者没收违法所得；对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分：  
（一）拒绝为符合法律援助条件的人员提供法律援助，或者故意为不符合法律援助条件的人员提供法律援助；  
（二）指派不符合本法规定的人员提供法律援助；  
（三）收取受援人财物；  
（四）从事有偿法律服务；  
（五）侵占、私分、挪用法律援助经费；  
（六）泄露法律援助过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私；  
（七）法律法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 61** Where a legal aid institution or any of its staff members falls under any of the following circumstances, the judicial administrative department that established the legal aid institution shall order it/him to make corrections within a time limit; if there are any illegal gains, it/he shall be ordered to return or confiscate the illegal gains; the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law:  
(1) refusing to provide legal aid to a person who meets the conditions for legal aid, or deliberately providing legal aid to a person who does not meet the conditions for legal aid;  
(2) assigning a person who does not meet the requirements of this Law to provide legal aid;  
(3) accepting property from a recipient;  
(4) engaging in paid legal services;  
(5) embezzling, privately distributing, or misappropriating legal aid funds;  
(6) divulging State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy learned during the legal aid process;  
(7) other circumstances as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　律师事务所、基层法律服务所有下列情形之一的，由司法行政部门依法给予处罚：  
（一）无正当理由拒绝接受法律援助机构指派；  
（二）接受指派后，不及时安排本所律师、基层法律服务工作者办理法律援助事项或者拒绝为本所律师、基层法律服务工作者办理法律援助事项提供支持和保障；  
（三）纵容或者放任本所律师、基层法律服务工作者怠于履行法律援助义务或者擅自终止提供法律援助；  
（四）法律法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 62** Where a law firm or a basic-level legal service office falls under any of the following circumstances, the judicial administrative department shall impose a penalty according to law:  
(1) refusing to accept an assignment from a legal aid institution without justifiable reasons;  
(2) after accepting an assignment, failing to promptly arrange for a lawyer or a basic-level legal service worker of the office to handle the legal aid matter, or refusing to provide support and guarantee for a lawyer or a basic-level legal service worker of the office to handle the legal aid matter;  
(3) conniving at or allowing a lawyer or a basic-level legal service worker of the office to be negligent in performing legal aid obligations or to terminate the provision of legal aid without authorization;  
(4) other circumstances as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　律师、基层法律服务工作者有下列情形之一的，由司法行政部门依法给予处罚：  
（一）无正当理由拒绝履行法律援助义务或者怠于履行法律援助义务；  
（二）擅自终止提供法律援助；  
（三）收取受援人财物；  
（四）泄露法律援助过程中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密和个人隐私；  
（五）法律法规规定的其他情形。

**Article 63** Where a lawyer or a basic-level legal service worker falls under any of the following circumstances, the judicial administrative department shall impose a penalty according to law:  
(1) refusing to perform or being negligent in performing legal aid obligations without justifiable reasons;  
(2) terminating the provision of legal aid without authorization;  
(3) accepting property from a recipient;  
(4) divulging State secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy learned during the legal aid process;  
(5) other circumstances as provided by laws and regulations.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　受援人以欺骗或者其他不正当手段获得法律援助的，由司法行政部门责令其支付已实施法律援助的费用，并处三千元以下罚款。

**Article 64** Where a recipient obtains legal aid by deception or other improper means, the judicial administrative department shall order him to pay the costs of the legal aid already provided and impose a fine of not more than 3,000 yuan.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　违反本法规定，冒用法律援助名义提供法律服务并谋取利益的，由司法行政部门责令改正，没收违法所得，并处违法所得一倍以上三倍以下罚款。

**Article 65** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of this Law, provides legal services under the guise of legal aid and seeks benefits, the judicial administrative department shall order him to make corrections, confiscate the illegal gains, and impose a fine of not less than one time but not more than three times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　国家机关及其工作人员在法律援助工作中滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依法给予处分。

**Article 66** Where a State organ or any of its staff members abuses its/his powers, neglects its/his duties, or engages in malpractices for personal gain in legal aid work, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be given sanctions according to law.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　违反本法规定，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 67** Where a violation of the provisions of this Law constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　工会、共产主义青年团、妇女联合会、残疾人联合会等群团组织开展法律援助工作，参照适用本法的相关规定。

**Article 68** Where trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, and other mass organizations carry out legal aid work, the relevant provisions of this Law shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　对外国人和无国籍人提供法律援助，我国法律有规定的，适用法律规定；我国法律没有规定的，可以根据我国缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照互惠原则，参照适用本法的相关规定。

**Article 69** Where legal aid is provided to foreigners and stateless persons, if Chinese laws have provisions, such provisions shall apply; if Chinese laws have no provisions, the relevant provisions of this Law may be applied mutatis mutandis in accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to by China, or on the basis of the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　对军人军属提供法律援助的具体办法，由国务院和中央军事委员会有关部门制定。

**Article 70** The specific measures for providing legal aid to military personnel and their family members shall be formulated by the State Council and the relevant departments of the Central Military Commission.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　本法自2022年1月1日起施行。

**Article 71** This Law shall come into force as of January 1, 2022.
