# 中华人民共和国仲裁法

**分类**：法律  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2025-09-12  
**生效日期**：2026-03-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了保证公正、及时仲裁经济纠纷，保护当事人的合法权益，保障社会主义市场经济健康发展，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of ensuring fair and timely arbitration of economic disputes, protecting the lawful rights and interests of the parties, and safeguarding the healthy development of the socialist market economy.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　仲裁事业的发展贯彻落实中国共产党和国家的路线方针政策、决策部署，服务国家高质量发展和高水平对外开放，营造市场化、法治化、国际化营商环境，发挥化解经济纠纷的作用。

**Article 2** The development of the arbitration cause shall implement the lines, principles, policies, decisions and deployments of the Communist Party of China and the State, serve the country's high-quality development and high-standard opening up, create a market-oriented, law-based and internationalized business environment, and play a role in resolving economic disputes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　平等主体的自然人、法人、非法人组织之间发生的合同纠纷和其他财产权益纠纷，可以仲裁。  
下列纠纷不能仲裁：  
（一）婚姻、收养、监护、扶养、继承纠纷；  
（二）依法应当由行政机关处理的行政争议。

**Article 3** Contract disputes and other disputes over property rights and interests arising between natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that are equal subjects may be submitted to arbitration.  
The following disputes shall not be submitted to arbitration:  
(1) disputes over marriage, adoption, guardianship, maintenance, and succession;  
(2) administrative disputes that shall be handled by administrative organs according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　当事人选择仲裁方式解决纠纷，应当遵循自愿原则，达成仲裁协议。没有仲裁协议，一方申请仲裁的，仲裁机构不予受理。

**Article 4** A party choosing arbitration as a means of dispute resolution shall follow the principle of voluntariness and reach an arbitration agreement. In the absence of an arbitration agreement, if one party applies for arbitration, the arbitration institution shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人达成仲裁协议，一方向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，但仲裁协议无效或者法律另有规定的除外。

**Article 5** Where the parties have reached an arbitration agreement and one party institutes a prosecution in a People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case, unless the arbitration agreement is invalid or the law provides otherwise.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　仲裁机构应当由当事人协议选定。  
仲裁不实行级别管辖和地域管辖。

**Article 6** The arbitration institution shall be selected by the parties through agreement.  
Arbitration shall not be subject to hierarchical jurisdiction or territorial jurisdiction.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　仲裁应当根据事实，符合法律规定，公平合理地解决纠纷。

**Article 7** Arbitration shall resolve disputes based on facts, in conformity with the provisions of law, and in a fair and reasonable manner.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　仲裁应当遵循诚信原则。

**Article 8** Arbitration shall adhere to the principle of good faith.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　仲裁依法独立进行，不受行政机关、社会团体和个人的干涉。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决或驳回当事人申请后当事人能否上诉问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于人民法院裁定撤销仲裁裁决或驳回当事人申请后当事人能否上诉问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于当事人对人民法院撤销仲裁裁决的裁定不服申请再审人民法院是否受理问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于当事人对人民法院撤销仲裁裁决的裁定不服申请再审人民法院是否受理问题的批复》第1条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于人民检察院对撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定提起抗诉人民法院应如何处理问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于人民检察院对撤销仲裁裁决的民事裁定提起抗诉人民法院应如何处理问题的批复》第1条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于当事人对驳回其申请撤销仲裁裁决的裁定不服而申请再审，人民法院不予受理问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于当事人对驳回其申请撤销仲裁裁决的裁定不服而申请再审，人民法院不予受理问题的批复》第1条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 9** Arbitration shall be conducted independently according to law and shall not be subject to interference by administrative organs, social organizations, or individuals.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　仲裁实行一裁终局的制度。裁决作出后，当事人就同一纠纷再申请仲裁或者向人民法院提起诉讼的，仲裁机构或者人民法院不予受理。  
裁决被人民法院依法裁定撤销或者不予执行的，当事人就该纠纷可以根据双方重新达成的仲裁协议申请仲裁，也可以向人民法院提起诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-51" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第51条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 10** Arbitration shall adopt the system of a single and final award. After an award is rendered, if a party applies for arbitration again or institutes a prosecution in a People's Court regarding the same dispute, the arbitration institution or the People's Court shall not accept the case.  
If the award is annulled or its enforcement is refused by a People's Court according to law, the parties may, regarding the dispute, apply for arbitration pursuant to a new arbitration agreement reached between them, or institute a prosecution in a People's Court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　仲裁活动可以通过信息网络在线进行，但当事人明确表示不同意的除外。  
仲裁活动通过信息网络在线进行的，与线下仲裁活动具有同等法律效力。

**Article 11** Arbitration activities may be conducted online through information networks, unless the parties expressly object.  
Arbitration activities conducted online through information networks shall have the same legal effect as offline arbitration activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家支持仲裁机构加强与境外仲裁机构和有关国际组织的交流合作，积极参与国际仲裁规则的制定。

**Article 12** The State supports arbitration institutions in strengthening exchanges and cooperation with offshore arbitration institutions and relevant international organizations, and actively participating in the formulation of international arbitration rules.

### 第二章　仲裁机构、仲裁员和仲裁协会

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　仲裁机构可以在直辖市和省、自治区人民政府所在地的市设立，也可以根据需要在其他设区的市设立，不按行政区划层层设立。  
仲裁机构由前款规定的市的人民政府组织有关部门和商会统一组建，属于公益性非营利法人。

**Article 13** Arbitration institutions may be established in municipalities directly under the Central Government and in cities that are the seats of the people's governments of provinces or autonomous regions, and may also, as needed, be established in other cities divided into districts, but shall not be established at each level of administrative divisions.  
An arbitration institution shall be uniformly organized by the people's government of a city specified in the preceding paragraph, in conjunction with relevant departments and chambers of commerce, and shall be a public welfare non-profit legal person.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　依据[本法第十三条](#art-13)设立的仲裁机构，应当经省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门登记。  
经国务院批准由中国国际商会组织设立的仲裁机构向国务院司法行政部门备案。  
仲裁机构登记管理的具体办法由国务院制定。

**Article 14** An arbitration institution established pursuant to Article 13 of this Law shall be registered with the judicial administrative department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
An arbitration institution established with the approval of the State Council by the China Chamber of International Commerce shall file with the judicial administrative department of the State Council.  
Specific measures for the registration and administration of arbitration institutions shall be formulated by the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　仲裁机构应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有自己的名称、住所和章程；  
（二）有必要的财产；  
（三）有符合本法规定的组成人员；  
（四）有聘任的仲裁员。  
仲裁机构的章程应当依照本法制定。

**Article 15** An arbitration institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) it has its own name, domicile and articles of association;  
(2) it has necessary property;  
(3) it has members of its composition that comply with the provisions of this Law;  
(4) it has appointed arbitrators.  
The articles of association of an arbitration institution shall be formulated in accordance with this Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　仲裁机构变更名称、住所、章程、法定代表人、组成人员的，应当提出申请，依法办理变更登记。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第13条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释.md#art-216" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释》第216条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第1条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-17" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》第17条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》的决定.md#art-216" title="被《最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》的决定》第216条引用">[5]</a></sup>

**Article 16** If an arbitration institution changes its name, domicile, articles of association, legal representative, or members of its composition, it shall submit an application and go through the formalities of change registration according to law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　仲裁机构终止的，依法办理注销登记。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释.md#art-216" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的解释》第216条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》的决定.md#art-216" title="被《最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》的决定》第216条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 17** If an arbitration institution terminates, it shall go through the formalities of cancellation of registration according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　仲裁机构的组成人员包括主任一人、副主任二至四人和委员七至十一人。  
仲裁机构的组成人员由法律、经济贸易、科学技术专家和有实际工作经验的人员担任。仲裁机构的组成人员中，法律、经济贸易、科学技术专家不得少于三分之二。  
仲裁机构的组成人员每届任期五年，任期届满的应当依法换届，更换不少于三分之一的组成人员。

**Article 18** The members of the composition of an arbitration institution shall include one director, two to four deputy directors, and seven to eleven members.  
The members of the composition of an arbitration institution shall be composed of experts in law, economics and trade, science and technology, and persons with practical work experience. Among the members of the composition of an arbitration institution, experts in law, economics and trade, and science and technology shall account for no less than two-thirds.  
The members of the composition of an arbitration institution shall serve a term of five years. Upon expiration of their terms, they shall be re-elected according to law, with no less than one-third of the members being replaced.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　仲裁机构应当依照法律法规和章程规定，建立健全内部治理结构，明确决策、执行、监督等方面的职责权限和程序。  
仲裁机构应当建立健全民主议事、人员管理、收费与财务管理、文件管理、投诉处理等制度。  
仲裁机构应当加强对组成人员、工作人员及仲裁员的监督，对其在仲裁活动中的违法违纪行为及时依法调查处理；需要追究法律责任的，及时移送有关机关予以处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-10" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 19** An arbitration institution shall, in accordance with laws, regulations, and its articles of association, establish and improve its internal governance structure, and specify the duties, powers, and procedures in respect of decision-making, execution, supervision, and other aspects.  
An arbitration institution shall establish and improve systems for democratic deliberation, personnel management, fee and financial management, document management, complaint handling, etc.  
An arbitration institution shall strengthen supervision over its members of composition, staff, and arbitrators, and shall promptly investigate and handle, according to law, any illegal or disciplinary acts committed by them in arbitration activities; where legal liability is to be pursued, it shall promptly transfer the matter to the relevant authorities for handling.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　仲裁机构应当建立信息公开制度，及时向社会公开章程、登记备案、仲裁规则、仲裁员名册、服务流程、收费标准、年度业务报告和财务报告等信息，主动接受社会监督。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-7" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第7条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第13条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 20** An arbitration institution shall establish an information disclosure system, and shall promptly disclose to the public its articles of association, registration and filing, arbitration rules, roster of arbitrators, service procedures, fee standards, annual business reports, financial reports, and other information, and shall proactively accept social supervision.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　仲裁机构聘任的仲裁员应当公道正派，具备良好的专业素质，勤勉尽责，清正廉明，恪守职业道德。

**Article 21** Arbitrators appointed by an arbitration institution shall be fair and upright, possess good professional quality, be diligent and responsible, honest and clean, and abide by professional ethics.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　仲裁员应当符合下列条件之一：  
（一）通过国家统一法律职业资格考试取得法律职业资格，从事仲裁工作满八年的；  
（二）律师执业满八年的；  
（三）曾任法官、检察官满八年的；  
（四）从事法律研究、教学工作并具有高级职称的；  
（五）具有法律知识，从事法律、经济贸易、海事海商、科学技术等专业工作，并具有高级职称或者具有同等专业水平的。  
《中华人民共和国监察官法》、《中华人民共和国法官法》、《中华人民共和国检察官法》等法律规定有关公职人员不得兼任仲裁员的，依照其规定；其他公职人员兼任仲裁员的，应当遵守有关规定。  
仲裁机构可以从具有法律、经济贸易、海事海商、科学技术等专门知识的境外人士中聘任仲裁员。

**Article 22** An arbitrator shall meet one of the following conditions:  
(1) Having passed the national unified legal professional qualification examination and obtained legal professional qualification, and being engaged in arbitration work for at least eight years;  
(2) Having practiced as a lawyer for at least eight years;  
(3) Having served as a judge or prosecutor for at least eight years;  
(4) Being engaged in legal research or teaching and having a senior professional title;  
(5) Having legal knowledge, being engaged in professional work in such fields as law, economic trade, maritime affairs, science and technology, and having a senior professional title or an equivalent professional level.  
Where laws such as the Supervision Officers Law of the People's Republic of China, the Judges Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Public Prosecutors Law of the People's Republic of China provide that relevant public officials shall not concurrently serve as arbitrators, such provisions shall prevail; where other public officials concurrently serve as arbitrators, they shall comply with relevant provisions.  
An arbitration institution may appoint arbitrators from among overseas persons with specialized knowledge in law, economic trade, maritime affairs, science and technology, etc.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　仲裁机构按照不同专业设仲裁员名册。  
仲裁员有被开除公职、吊销律师执业证书或者被撤销高级职称等不再具备担任仲裁员条件情形的，仲裁机构应当将其除名。

**Article 23** An arbitration institution shall maintain a roster of arbitrators according to different specialties.  
Where an arbitrator is dismissed from public office, has his or her lawyer's practicing certificate revoked, or has his or her senior professional title revoked, or otherwise no longer meets the conditions for serving as an arbitrator, the arbitration institution shall remove his or her name from the roster.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　仲裁机构独立于行政机关，与行政机关没有隶属关系。  
仲裁机构之间没有隶属关系。

**Article 24** An arbitration institution shall be independent of administrative organs and have no subordinate relationship with any administrative organ.  
There shall be no subordinate relationship between arbitration institutions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　中国仲裁协会是社会团体法人。仲裁机构是中国仲裁协会的会员。中国仲裁协会的章程由全国会员大会制定。  
中国仲裁协会是仲裁机构的自律性组织，根据章程对仲裁机构及其组成人员、工作人员，以及仲裁员在仲裁活动中的行为进行监督。  
中国仲裁协会依照本法和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定制定示范仲裁规则。

**Article 25** The China Arbitration Association is a social organization legal person. Arbitration institutions are members of the China Arbitration Association. The articles of association of the China Arbitration Association shall be formulated by the National Congress of Members.  
The China Arbitration Association is a self-regulatory organization of arbitration institutions, which supervises the conduct of arbitration institutions and their members, staff, and arbitrators in arbitration activities in accordance with its articles of association.  
The China Arbitration Association shall formulate model arbitration rules in accordance with this Law and the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　国务院司法行政部门依法指导、监督全国仲裁工作，完善相关工作制度，统筹规划仲裁事业发展。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门依法指导、监督本行政区域内仲裁工作。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-14" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第14条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 26** The judicial administration department of the State Council shall, according to law, guide and supervise the national arbitration work, improve relevant work systems, and coordinate and plan the development of arbitration.  
The judicial administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, according to law, guide and supervise arbitration work within their respective administrative regions.

### 第三章　仲裁协议

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　仲裁协议包括合同中订立的仲裁条款和以其他书面方式在纠纷发生前或者纠纷发生后达成的请求仲裁的协议。  
仲裁协议应当具有下列内容：  
（一）请求仲裁的意思表示；  
（二）仲裁事项；  
（三）选定的仲裁机构。  
一方当事人在申请仲裁时主张有仲裁协议，另一方当事人在首次开庭前不予否认的，经仲裁庭提示并记录，视为当事人之间存在仲裁协议。

**Article 27** An arbitration agreement includes an arbitration clause contained in a contract and any other agreement in writing concluded before or after a dispute arises that provides for submission of the dispute to arbitration.  
An arbitration agreement shall contain the following:  
(1) An expression of intention to request arbitration;  
(2) Matters to be submitted to arbitration;  
(3) A designated arbitration institution.  
Where one party claims at the time of applying for arbitration that there is an arbitration agreement, and the other party does not deny it before the first hearing, upon the reminder and record of the arbitral tribunal, the arbitration agreement shall be deemed to exist between the parties.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　有下列情形之一的，仲裁协议无效：  
（一）约定的仲裁事项超出法律规定的仲裁范围；  
（二）无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人订立的仲裁协议；  
（三）一方采取胁迫手段，迫使对方订立仲裁协议。

**Article 28** An arbitration agreement shall be invalid under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The matters agreed to be submitted to arbitration exceed the scope of arbitration provided by law;  
(2) The arbitration agreement is concluded by a person without capacity for civil conduct or with limited capacity for civil conduct;  
(3) One party compels the other party to conclude the arbitration agreement by means of duress.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　仲裁协议对仲裁事项或者仲裁机构没有约定或者约定不明确的，当事人可以补充协议；达不成补充协议的，仲裁协议无效。

**Article 29** Where an arbitration agreement does not specify or clearly specifies the matters to be submitted to arbitration or the arbitration institution, the parties may enter into a supplementary agreement; if they fail to reach a supplementary agreement, the arbitration agreement shall be invalid.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　仲裁协议独立存在。合同是否成立及其变更、不生效、终止、被撤销或者无效，不影响已经达成的仲裁协议的效力。  
仲裁庭有权确认合同的效力。

**Article 30** An arbitration agreement shall exist independently. The validity of an arbitration agreement that has been reached shall not be affected by whether the contract is formed or by its modification, non-effectiveness, termination, rescission, or invalidity.  
The arbitral tribunal has the authority to determine the validity of a contract.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　当事人对仲裁协议的效力有异议的，可以请求仲裁机构或者仲裁庭作出决定，也可以请求人民法院作出裁定。一方请求仲裁机构或者仲裁庭作出决定，另一方请求人民法院作出裁定的，由人民法院裁定。  
当事人对仲裁协议的效力有异议，应当在仲裁庭首次开庭前提出。

**Article 31** If a party challenges the validity of an arbitration agreement, that party may request the arbitration institution or the arbitral tribunal to make a decision, or may request the People's Court to make a ruling. If one party requests the arbitration institution or the arbitral tribunal to make a decision and the other party requests the People's Court to make a ruling, the People's Court shall make the ruling.  
A party that challenges the validity of an arbitration agreement shall raise the objection before the first hearing of the arbitral tribunal.

### 第四章　仲裁程序

#### 第一节　申请和受理

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　当事人申请仲裁应当符合下列条件：  
（一）有仲裁协议；  
（二）有具体的仲裁请求和事实、理由；  
（三）属于仲裁机构的受理范围。

**Article 32** A party applying for arbitration shall satisfy the following conditions:  
(1) there is an arbitration agreement;  
(2) there are specific arbitration claims and facts and reasons;  
(3) the matter falls within the scope of acceptance of the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　当事人申请仲裁，应当向仲裁机构递交仲裁协议、仲裁申请书及副本。

**Article 33** A party applying for arbitration shall submit the arbitration agreement, the application for arbitration and copies thereof to the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　仲裁申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的姓名、性别、年龄、职业、工作单位、住所、联系方式，法人或者非法人组织的名称、住所和法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、联系方式；  
（二）仲裁请求和所根据的事实与理由；  
（三）证据和证据来源，证人姓名和住所。

**Article 34** The application for arbitration shall specify the following matters:  
(1) the name, gender, age, occupation, work unit, domicile and contact information of the party, and the name and domicile of the legal person or unincorporated organization and the name, position and contact information of its legal representative or principal responsible person;  
(2) the arbitration claims and the facts and reasons on which they are based;  
(3) the evidence and the sources of evidence, and the names and domiciles of witnesses.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　仲裁机构收到仲裁申请书之日起五日内，认为符合受理条件的，应当受理，并通知申请人；认为不符合受理条件的，应当书面通知申请人不予受理，并说明理由。

**Article 35** Within five days from the date of receipt of the application for arbitration, the arbitration institution shall, if it considers that the conditions for acceptance are satisfied, accept the application and notify the applicant; if it considers that the conditions are not satisfied, it shall notify the applicant in writing of the rejection of the application and state the reasons.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　仲裁机构受理仲裁申请后，应当在仲裁规则规定的期限内将仲裁规则和仲裁员名册送达申请人，并将仲裁申请书副本和仲裁规则、仲裁员名册送达被申请人。  
被申请人收到仲裁申请书副本后，应当在仲裁规则规定的期限内向仲裁机构提交答辩书。仲裁机构收到答辩书后，应当在仲裁规则规定的期限内将答辩书副本送达申请人。被申请人未提交答辩书的，不影响仲裁程序的进行。

**Article 36** After accepting an arbitration application, the arbitration institution shall, within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules, serve the arbitration rules and the panel of arbitrators on the applicant, and serve a copy of the application for arbitration, the arbitration rules and the panel of arbitrators on the respondent.  
After receiving the copy of the application for arbitration, the respondent shall, within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules, submit a statement of defense to the arbitration institution. After receiving the statement of defense, the arbitration institution shall, within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules, serve a copy of the statement of defense on the applicant. The failure of the respondent to submit a statement of defense shall not affect the conduct of the arbitration proceedings.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　当事人达成仲裁协议，一方向人民法院提起诉讼未声明有仲裁协议，人民法院受理后，另一方在首次开庭前提交仲裁协议的，人民法院应当驳回起诉，但仲裁协议无效或者法律另有规定的除外；另一方在首次开庭前未对人民法院受理该案提出异议的，视为放弃仲裁协议，人民法院应当继续审理。

**Article 37** Where the parties have reached an arbitration agreement, and one party institutes a lawsuit in a People's Court without declaring the existence of the arbitration agreement, and the People's Court has accepted the case, if the other party submits the arbitration agreement before the first hearing, the People's Court shall dismiss the lawsuit, unless the arbitration agreement is invalid or otherwise provided by law; if the other party does not object to the People's Court's acceptance of the case before the first hearing, it shall be deemed to have waived the arbitration agreement, and the People's Court shall continue the trial.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　申请人可以放弃或者变更仲裁请求。被申请人可以承认或者反驳仲裁请求，有权提出反请求。

**Article 38** The applicant may waive or amend the arbitration claims. The respondent may accept or contest the arbitration claims, and has the right to make counterclaims.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　一方当事人因另一方当事人的行为或者其他原因，可能使裁决难以执行或者造成当事人其他损害的，可以申请财产保全、请求责令另一方当事人作出一定行为或者禁止其作出一定行为。当事人申请保全的，仲裁机构应当将当事人的申请依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定提交人民法院，人民法院应当依法及时处理。  
因情况紧急，仲裁协议的当事人可以在申请仲裁前依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定向人民法院申请财产保全、请求责令另一方当事人作出一定行为或者禁止其作出一定行为。当事人申请保全的，人民法院应当依法及时处理。  
申请有错误的，申请人应当赔偿被申请人因保全所遭受的损失。

**Article 39** Where, due to the conduct of the other party or for other reasons, an award may be difficult to enforce or other damage may be caused to a party, the party may apply for property preservation, request an order requiring the other party to perform a certain act or prohibiting it from performing a certain act. Where a party applies for preservation, the arbitration institution shall submit the party's application to the People's Court in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and the People's Court shall handle it in a timely manner according to law.  
In an emergency, a party to an arbitration agreement may, before applying for arbitration, apply to the People's Court for property preservation, request an order requiring the other party to perform a certain act or prohibiting it from performing a certain act in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. Where a party applies for preservation, the People's Court shall handle it in a timely manner according to law.  
If the application is erroneous, the applicant shall compensate the respondent for the losses incurred due to the preservation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人、法定代理人可以委托律师和其他代理人进行仲裁活动。委托律师和其他代理人进行仲裁活动的，应当向仲裁机构提交授权委托书。

**Article 40** A party or statutory agent may retain lawyers and other agents to conduct arbitration activities. Where a lawyer or other agent is retained to conduct arbitration activities, a power of attorney shall be submitted to the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　仲裁文件应当以当事人约定的合理方式送达；当事人没有约定或者约定不明确的，按照仲裁规则规定的方式送达。

**Article 41** Arbitration documents shall be served in a reasonable manner agreed upon by the parties; if the parties have no agreement or the agreement is not clear, they shall be served in the manner prescribed by the arbitration rules.

#### 第二节　仲裁庭的组成

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　仲裁庭可以由三名仲裁员或者一名仲裁员组成。由三名仲裁员组成的，设首席仲裁员。

**Article 42** An arbitral tribunal may be composed of three arbitrators or a sole arbitrator. Where it is composed of three arbitrators, a presiding arbitrator shall be appointed.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　当事人约定由三名仲裁员组成仲裁庭的，应当各自选定或者各自委托仲裁机构主任按照仲裁规则确定的程序指定一名仲裁员；第三名仲裁员由当事人共同选定，也可以由当事人共同委托仲裁机构主任按照仲裁规则确定的程序指定。当事人约定第三名仲裁员由其各自选定的仲裁员共同选定的，从其约定。第三名仲裁员是首席仲裁员。  
当事人约定由一名仲裁员成立仲裁庭的，仲裁员由当事人共同选定，也可以由当事人共同委托仲裁机构主任按照仲裁规则确定的程序指定。

**Article 43** Where the parties agree that the arbitral tribunal shall be composed of three arbitrators, each party shall select one arbitrator or entrust the director of the arbitration institution to appoint one arbitrator in accordance with the procedure determined by the arbitration rules; the third arbitrator shall be selected jointly by the parties or may be appointed jointly by the parties by entrusting the director of the arbitration institution in accordance with the procedure determined by the arbitration rules. If the parties agree that the third arbitrator shall be selected jointly by the arbitrators selected by each party, such agreement shall prevail. The third arbitrator shall be the presiding arbitrator.  
Where the parties agree that the arbitral tribunal shall be composed of a sole arbitrator, the arbitrator shall be selected jointly by the parties or may be appointed jointly by the parties by entrusting the director of the arbitration institution in accordance with the procedure determined by the arbitration rules.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人没有在仲裁规则规定的期限内约定仲裁庭的组成方式或者选定仲裁员的，由仲裁机构主任按照仲裁规则确定的程序确定或者指定。

**Article 44** Where the parties fail to agree on the method of composition of the arbitral tribunal or to select arbitrators within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules, the director of the arbitration institution shall determine or appoint them in accordance with the procedure determined by the arbitration rules.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　仲裁员存在可能导致当事人对其独立性、公正性产生合理怀疑情形的，该仲裁员应当及时向仲裁机构书面披露。  
仲裁机构应当将仲裁员书面披露情况、仲裁庭的组成情况书面通知当事人。

**Article 45** Where an arbitrator is in a situation that may reasonably cause a party to have doubts about his or her independence or impartiality, the arbitrator shall promptly make a written disclosure to the arbitration institution.  
The arbitration institution shall notify the parties in writing of the arbitrator's written disclosure and the composition of the arbitral tribunal.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　仲裁员有下列情形之一的，必须回避，当事人也有权提出回避申请：  
（一）是本案当事人、代理人，或者当事人、代理人的近亲属；  
（二）与本案有利害关系；  
（三）与本案当事人、代理人有其他关系，可能影响公正仲裁；  
（四）私自会见当事人、代理人，或者接受当事人、代理人的请客送礼。

**Article 46** In any of the following circumstances, an arbitrator must withdraw, and the parties also have the right to apply for withdrawal:  
(1) the arbitrator is a party or agent in the case, or a close relative of a party or agent;  
(2) the arbitrator has an interest in the case;  
(3) the arbitrator has another relationship with a party or agent in the case that may affect fair arbitration;  
(4) the arbitrator has privately met with a party or agent, or accepted entertainment or gifts from a party or agent.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　当事人提出回避申请，应当说明理由，在首次开庭前提出。回避事由在首次开庭后知道的，可以在最后一次开庭终结前提出。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-13" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》第13条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 47** A party applying for withdrawal shall state the reasons and shall make the application before the first hearing. If the ground for withdrawal becomes known after the first hearing, the application may be made before the conclusion of the last hearing.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　仲裁员是否回避，由仲裁机构主任决定；仲裁机构主任担任仲裁员时，其是否回避由仲裁机构的其他组成人员集体决定。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》第15条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 48** Whether an arbitrator shall withdraw shall be decided by the director of the arbitration institution; where the director of the arbitration institution serves as an arbitrator, whether such director shall withdraw shall be decided collectively by the other members of the arbitration institution.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　仲裁员因回避或者其他原因不能履行职责的，应当依照本法规定重新选定或者指定仲裁员。  
因回避而重新选定或者指定仲裁员后，当事人可以请求已进行的仲裁程序重新进行，是否准许，由仲裁庭决定；仲裁庭也可以自行决定已进行的仲裁程序是否重新进行。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第21条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）.md#art-22" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）》第22条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-15" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》第15条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）.md#art-16" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律若干问题的解释（三）》第16条引用">[4]</a></sup>

**Article 49** Where an arbitrator is unable to perform his or her duties due to withdrawal or for other reasons, a substitute arbitrator shall be selected or appointed in accordance with the provisions of this Law.  
After a substitute arbitrator is selected or appointed due to withdrawal, the parties may request that the arbitration proceedings already conducted be reconducted, and whether to permit such reconduction shall be decided by the arbitral tribunal; the arbitral tribunal may also decide on its own whether to reconduct the arbitration proceedings already conducted.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　仲裁员有[本法第四十六条](#art-46)第四项规定的情形，情节严重的，或者有[本法第七十一条](#art-71)第一款第六项规定的情形的，应当依法承担法律责任，仲裁机构应当将其除名。

**Article 50** Where an arbitrator falls under the circumstances provided for in paragraph (4) of Article 46 of this Law, if the circumstances are serious, or falls under the circumstances provided for in paragraph (6) of the first paragraph of Article 71 of this Law, the arbitrator shall bear legal liability according to law, and the arbitration institution shall remove the arbitrator from the panel.

#### 第三节　开庭和裁决

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　仲裁应当开庭进行。当事人协议不开庭的，仲裁庭可以根据仲裁申请书、答辩书以及其他材料作出裁决。

**Article 51** Arbitration shall be conducted by holding oral hearings. If the parties agree not to hold oral hearings, the arbitral tribunal may make an award on the basis of the application for arbitration, the statement of defense and other materials.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　仲裁不公开进行。当事人协议公开的，可以公开进行，但涉及国家秘密、他人的商业秘密或者个人隐私的除外。

**Article 52** Arbitration shall be conducted in private. Where the parties agree on public hearing, the hearing may be conducted in public, except where State secrets, trade secrets of others, or individual privacy are involved.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　仲裁机构应当在仲裁规则规定的期限内将开庭日期通知双方当事人。当事人有正当理由的，可以在仲裁规则规定的期限内请求延期开庭。是否延期，由仲裁庭决定。

**Article 53** The arbitral institution shall notify the parties of the date of the hearing within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules. Where a party has justified reasons, it may request an adjournment of the hearing within the time limit prescribed by the arbitration rules. Whether to adjourn the hearing shall be decided by the arbitral tribunal.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　申请人经书面通知，无正当理由不到庭或者未经仲裁庭许可中途退庭的，可以视为撤回仲裁申请。  
被申请人经书面通知，无正当理由不到庭或者未经仲裁庭许可中途退庭的，可以缺席裁决。

**Article 54** If the applicant, having been served with a written notice, fails to appear before the arbitral tribunal without justified reasons or withdraws from the hearing without the permission of the arbitral tribunal, the application may be deemed to have been withdrawn.  
If the respondent, having been served with a written notice, fails to appear before the arbitral tribunal without justified reasons or withdraws from the hearing without the permission of the arbitral tribunal, an award may be made by default.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　当事人应当对自己的主张提供证据。  
仲裁庭认为有必要收集的证据，可以自行收集；必要时，可以请求有关方面依法予以协助。

**Article 55** The parties shall provide evidence for their own claims.  
Evidence that the arbitral tribunal considers necessary may be collected by the arbitral tribunal on its own initiative; where necessary, it may request assistance from relevant parties in accordance with law.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　当事人可以就查明事实的专门性问题向仲裁庭申请鉴定。仲裁庭根据当事人的申请或者自行判断认为对专门性问题需要鉴定的，可以交由当事人约定的鉴定人鉴定，也可以由仲裁庭指定的鉴定人鉴定。  
根据当事人的请求或者仲裁庭的要求，经仲裁庭通知，鉴定人应当参加开庭。当事人经仲裁庭许可，可以向鉴定人提问。

**Article 56** The parties may apply to the arbitral tribunal for an expert evaluation on specialized issues concerning the ascertainment of facts. Where the arbitral tribunal, upon the application of a party or on its own judgment, considers that an expert evaluation is necessary for a specialized issue, it may refer the matter to an expert agreed upon by the parties or to an expert designated by the arbitral tribunal.  
Upon a party's request or the requirement of the arbitral tribunal, and after being notified by the arbitral tribunal, the expert shall attend the hearing. With the permission of the arbitral tribunal, the parties may question the expert.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　证据应当在开庭时出示，当事人可以质证。

**Article 57** Evidence shall be presented at the hearing, and the parties may examine the evidence.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　在证据可能灭失或者以后难以取得的情况下，当事人可以申请证据保全。当事人申请证据保全的，仲裁机构应当将当事人的申请提交证据所在地的基层人民法院，人民法院应当依法及时处理。  
因情况紧急，仲裁协议的当事人可以在申请仲裁前依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定向人民法院申请证据保全。当事人申请证据保全的，人民法院应当依法及时处理。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-17" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第17条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第18条引用">[2]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-20" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第20条引用">[3]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-23" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第23条引用">[4]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-27" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第27条引用">[5]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理仲裁司法审查案件若干问题的规定.md#art-18" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理仲裁司法审查案件若干问题的规定》第18条引用">[6]</a></sup>

**Article 58** Where there is a possibility that evidence may be lost or become difficult to obtain later, a party may apply for evidence preservation. If a party applies for evidence preservation, the arbitral institution shall submit the party's application to the Primary People's Court in the place where the evidence is located, and the people's court shall handle it in a timely manner in accordance with law.  
In case of emergency, a party to the arbitration agreement may, before applying for arbitration, apply to the people's court for evidence preservation in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. If a party applies for evidence preservation, the people's court shall handle it in a timely manner in accordance with law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　当事人在仲裁过程中有权进行辩论。辩论终结时，首席仲裁员或者独任仲裁员应当征询当事人的最后意见。

**Article 59** The parties shall have the right to debate during the arbitration proceedings. At the conclusion of the debate, the presiding arbitrator or the sole arbitrator shall solicit the final opinions of the parties.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　仲裁庭应当将开庭情况记入笔录。当事人和其他仲裁参与人认为对自己陈述的记录有遗漏或者差错的，有权申请补正。如果不予补正，应当记录该申请。  
笔录由仲裁员、记录人员、当事人和其他仲裁参与人签名或者盖章。

**Article 60** The arbitral tribunal shall record the proceedings of the hearing in writing. If a party or any other participant in the arbitration considers that there is an omission or error in the record of his or her statement, he or she shall have the right to apply for a correction. If the correction is not made, the application shall be recorded.  
The written record shall be signed or sealed by the arbitrators, the record keeper, the parties, and other participants in the arbitration.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　仲裁庭发现当事人单方捏造基本事实申请仲裁或者当事人之间恶意串通，企图通过仲裁方式侵害国家利益、社会公共利益或者他人合法权益的，应当驳回其仲裁请求。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/行政法规/公安司法/仲裁委员会仲裁收费办法.md#art-10" title="被《仲裁委员会仲裁收费办法》第10条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释.md#art-21" title="被《最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释》第21条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 61** If the arbitral tribunal finds that a party unilaterally fabricates basic facts for the purpose of applying for arbitration, or that the parties collude maliciously in an attempt to harm the national interest, the public interest, or the lawful rights and interests of others through arbitration, the arbitral tribunal shall dismiss the arbitration request.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　当事人申请仲裁后，可以自行和解。达成和解协议的，可以请求仲裁庭根据和解协议作出裁决书，也可以撤回仲裁申请。

**Article 62** After applying for arbitration, the parties may settle the dispute on their own. If a settlement agreement is reached, they may request the arbitral tribunal to render an award based on the settlement agreement, or they may withdraw the arbitration application.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　当事人达成和解协议，撤回仲裁申请后反悔的，可以根据仲裁协议申请仲裁。

**Article 63** If a party withdraws the arbitration application after reaching a settlement agreement and subsequently reneges on the agreement, it may apply for arbitration in accordance with the arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　仲裁庭在作出裁决前，可以先行调解。当事人自愿调解的，仲裁庭应当调解。调解不成的，应当及时作出裁决。  
调解达成协议的，仲裁庭应当制作调解书或者根据协议的结果制作裁决书。调解书与裁决书具有同等法律效力。

**Article 64** Before making an award, the arbitral tribunal may first attempt mediation. If the parties voluntarily agree to mediation, the arbitral tribunal shall conduct mediation. If mediation fails, an award shall be made promptly.  
If a mediation agreement is reached, the arbitral tribunal shall produce a mediation document or render an award based on the terms of the agreement. The mediation document and the award shall have equal legal effect.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　调解书应当写明仲裁请求和当事人协议的结果。调解书由仲裁员签名，加盖仲裁机构印章，送达双方当事人。  
调解书经双方当事人签收后，即发生法律效力。  
在调解书签收前当事人反悔的，仲裁庭应当及时作出裁决。

**Article 65** A mediation document shall state the arbitration claims and the terms agreed upon by the parties. The mediation document shall be signed by the arbitrators, sealed with the seal of the arbitral institution, and served on the parties.  
The mediation document shall become legally effective upon being signed for receipt by both parties.  
If a party reneges on the mediation document before signing for receipt, the arbitral tribunal shall make an award promptly.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　裁决应当按照多数仲裁员的意见作出，少数仲裁员的不同意见可以记入笔录。仲裁庭不能形成多数意见时，裁决应当按照首席仲裁员的意见作出。

**Article 66** The award shall be made according to the opinion of the majority of the arbitrators. The different opinion of a minority of the arbitrators may be recorded in the minutes. If the arbitral tribunal cannot reach a majority opinion, the award shall be made in accordance with the opinion of the presiding arbitrator.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　裁决书应当写明仲裁请求、争议事实、裁决理由、裁决结果、仲裁费用的负担和裁决日期。当事人协议不愿写明争议事实和裁决理由的，可以不写。裁决书由仲裁员签名，加盖仲裁机构印章。对裁决持不同意见的仲裁员，可以签名，也可以不签名。

**Article 67** An award shall state the arbitration claims, the facts in dispute, the reasons for the award, the result of the award, the allocation of arbitration costs, and the date of the award. If the parties agree that the facts in dispute and the reasons for the award need not be stated, they may be omitted. The award shall be signed by the arbitrators and sealed with the seal of the arbitral institution. An arbitrator who dissents from the award may sign or choose not to sign the award.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　仲裁庭仲裁纠纷时，其中一部分事实已经清楚，可以就该部分先行裁决。

**Article 68** When arbitrating a dispute, if the facts concerning part of the dispute have become clear, the arbitral tribunal may render an award on that part first.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　对裁决书中的文字、计算错误或者仲裁庭已经裁决但在裁决书中遗漏的事项，仲裁庭应当补正；当事人自收到裁决书之日起三十日内，可以请求仲裁庭补正。

**Article 69** The arbitral tribunal shall correct any typographical or computational errors in the award or any matters that have been decided by the arbitral tribunal but omitted from the award. Within thirty days from the date of receipt of the award, a party may request the arbitral tribunal to make such corrections.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　裁决书自作出之日起发生法律效力。

**Article 70** An award shall become legally effective from the date it is made.

### 第五章　申请撤销裁决

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　当事人提出证据证明裁决有下列情形之一的，可以向仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院申请撤销裁决：  
（一）没有仲裁协议；  
（二）裁决的事项不属于仲裁协议的范围或者仲裁机构无权仲裁；  
（三）仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁的程序违反法定程序；  
（四）裁决所根据的证据是伪造的；  
（五）对方当事人隐瞒了足以影响公正裁决的证据；  
（六）仲裁员在仲裁该案时有索贿受贿、徇私舞弊、枉法裁决行为。  
人民法院经组成合议庭审查核实裁决有前款规定情形之一的，应当裁定撤销。  
人民法院认定该裁决违背公共利益的，应当裁定撤销。

**Article 71** If a party provides evidence proving that the arbitral award falls under any of the following circumstances, that party may apply to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the arbitration institution is located for setting aside the award:  
(1) There is no arbitration agreement;  
(2) The matters decided in the award exceed the scope of the arbitration agreement or the arbitration institution lacks jurisdiction to arbitrate;  
(3) The composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitral procedure violated legal procedures;  
(4) The evidence on which the award was based was forged;  
(5) The other party concealed evidence that is sufficient to affect the impartiality of the award;  
(6) The arbitrator, in hearing the case, engaged in acts of soliciting or accepting bribes, engaging in malpractice for personal gain, or perverting the law in making the award.  
If the People's Court, after forming a collegial panel to examine and verify the matter, finds that the award falls under any of the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall rule to set aside the award.  
If the People's Court determines that the award is contrary to the public interest, it shall rule to set aside the award.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　当事人申请撤销裁决的，应当自收到裁决书之日起三个月内提出。

**Article 72** A party that applies to set aside an arbitral award shall file the application within three months from the date of receipt of the award.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　人民法院应当在受理撤销裁决申请之日起两个月内作出撤销裁决或者驳回申请的裁定。

**Article 73** The People's Court shall, within two months from the date of accepting an application to set aside an arbitral award, render a ruling either setting aside the award or dismissing the application.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　人民法院受理撤销裁决的申请后，认为可以由仲裁庭重新仲裁的，通知仲裁庭在一定期限内重新仲裁，并裁定中止撤销程序。仲裁庭开始重新仲裁的，人民法院应当裁定终结撤销程序。仲裁庭拒绝重新仲裁的，人民法院应当裁定恢复撤销程序。

**Article 74** After accepting an application to set aside an arbitral award, if the People's Court considers that the arbitral tribunal may re-arbitrate the case, it shall notify the arbitral tribunal to re-arbitrate within a specified period and shall rule to suspend the setting-aside procedure. If the arbitral tribunal commences re-arbitration, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the setting-aside procedure. If the arbitral tribunal refuses to re-arbitrate, the People's Court shall rule to resume the setting-aside procedure.

### 第六章　执行

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　当事人应当履行裁决。一方当事人不履行的，另一方当事人可以依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定向人民法院申请执行。受申请的人民法院应当执行。

**Article 75** The parties shall perform the arbitral award. If one party fails to perform the award, the other party may apply to the People's Court for enforcement in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. The People's Court to which the application is made shall enforce the award.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　被申请人提出证据证明裁决有[本法第七十一条](#art-71)第一款规定的情形之一的，经人民法院组成合议庭审查核实，裁定不予执行。  
人民法院认定执行该裁决违背公共利益的，应当裁定不予执行。

**Article 76** If the respondent provides evidence proving that the award falls under any of the circumstances specified in the first paragraph of Article 71 of this Law, and the People's Court, after forming a collegial panel to examine and verify the matter, shall rule to refuse enforcement.  
If the People's Court determines that enforcement of the award is contrary to the public interest, it shall rule to refuse enforcement.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　一方当事人申请执行裁决，另一方当事人申请撤销裁决的，人民法院应当裁定中止执行。  
人民法院裁定撤销裁决的，应当裁定终结执行。撤销裁决的申请被裁定驳回的，人民法院应当裁定恢复执行。

**Article 77** If one party applies for enforcement of the award and the other party applies to set aside the award, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the enforcement.  
If the People's Court rules to set aside the award, it shall rule to terminate the enforcement. If the application to set aside the award is dismissed by ruling, the People's Court shall rule to resume the enforcement.

### 第七章　涉外仲裁的特别规定

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　涉外经济贸易、运输、海事纠纷以及其他涉外纠纷的仲裁，适用本章规定；本章没有规定的，适用本法其他有关规定。

**Article 78** The provisions of this Chapter shall apply to arbitration of disputes involving foreign economic trade, transport, maritime affairs, and other foreign-related disputes; where this Chapter contains no provisions, other relevant provisions of this Law shall apply.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　涉外仲裁的当事人申请证据保全的，仲裁机构应当将当事人的申请提交证据所在地的中级人民法院，人民法院应当依法及时处理。

**Article 79** Where a party to foreign-related arbitration applies for preservation of evidence, the arbitration institution shall submit the party's application to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the evidence is located, and the People's Court shall process it promptly according to law.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　涉外仲裁的仲裁庭可以将开庭情况记入笔录，或者作出笔录要点，笔录要点可以由当事人和其他仲裁参与人签名或者盖章。

**Article 80** The arbitral tribunal in foreign-related arbitration may record the hearings in the record or make a summary of the record. The summary of the record may be signed or sealed by the parties and other arbitration participants.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　当事人可以书面约定仲裁地。除当事人对仲裁程序的适用法另有约定外，以仲裁地作为仲裁程序的适用法及司法管辖法院的确定依据。仲裁裁决视为在仲裁地作出。  
当事人对仲裁地没有约定或者约定不明确的，根据当事人约定的仲裁规则确定仲裁地；仲裁规则没有规定的，由仲裁庭根据案件情况，按照便利争议解决的原则确定仲裁地。

**Article 81** The parties may agree in writing on the place of arbitration. Unless the parties have otherwise agreed on the applicable law of the arbitration proceedings, the place of arbitration shall serve as the basis for determining the applicable law of the arbitration proceedings and the court with judicial jurisdiction. An arbitral award shall be deemed to have been made at the place of arbitration.  
Where the parties have not agreed on the place of arbitration or the agreement is unclear, the place of arbitration shall be determined in accordance with the arbitration rules agreed upon by the parties; where the arbitration rules contain no such provision, the place of arbitration shall be determined by the arbitral tribunal based on the circumstances of the case and in accordance with the principle of facilitating dispute resolution.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　涉外海事纠纷或者在经国务院批准设立的自由贸易试验区、海南自由贸易港以及国家规定的其他区域内设立登记的企业之间发生的涉外纠纷，当事人书面约定仲裁的，可以选择由仲裁机构进行；也可以选择以中华人民共和国为仲裁地，由符合本法规定条件的人员组成仲裁庭按照约定的仲裁规则进行，该仲裁庭应当在组庭后三个工作日内将当事人名称、仲裁地、仲裁庭的组成情况、仲裁规则向仲裁协会备案。  
当事人申请财产保全、证据保全、请求责令另一方当事人作出一定行为或者禁止其作出一定行为的，仲裁庭应当依法将当事人的申请提交人民法院，人民法院应当依法及时处理。

**Article 82** For foreign-related maritime disputes or foreign-related disputes arising between enterprises established and registered within a pilot free trade zone approved by the State Council, the Hainan Free Trade Port, or other areas prescribed by the State, where the parties have agreed in writing on arbitration, they may choose to have the arbitration conducted by an arbitration institution; or they may choose to have the place of arbitration as the People's Republic of China, with the arbitral tribunal composed of persons meeting the qualifications prescribed by this Law to conduct the arbitration in accordance with the agreed arbitration rules. The arbitral tribunal shall, within three working days after its formation, file with the Arbitration Association for record the names of the parties, the place of arbitration, the composition of the arbitral tribunal, and the arbitration rules.  
Where a party applies for property preservation, evidence preservation, or requests an order requiring the other party to perform a certain act or prohibiting it from performing a certain act, the arbitral tribunal shall submit the party's application to the People's Court in accordance with the law, and the People's Court shall handle it promptly in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　当事人提出证据证明涉外仲裁裁决有下列情形之一的，经人民法院组成合议庭审查核实，裁定撤销：  
（一）没有仲裁协议；  
（二）被申请人没有得到指定仲裁员或者进行仲裁程序的通知，或者由于其他不属于被申请人负责的原因未能陈述意见；  
（三）仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁的程序与仲裁规则不符；  
（四）裁决的事项不属于仲裁协议的范围或者仲裁机构无权仲裁。  
人民法院认定该裁决违背公共利益的，应当裁定撤销。

**Article 83** Where a party adduces evidence that a foreign-related arbitral award falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall, after examination and verification by a collegial panel formed by it, rule to set aside the award:  
(1) There is no arbitration agreement;  
(2) The respondent was not given notice of the appointment of an arbitrator or of the arbitration proceedings, or was unable to present its case due to reasons not attributable to the respondent;  
(3) The composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitration procedure was not in conformity with the arbitration rules; or  
(4) The matters decided in the award fall outside the scope of the arbitration agreement, or the arbitration institution lacks jurisdiction to arbitrate.  
If the People's Court finds that the award is contrary to the public interest, it shall rule to set it aside.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条　被申请人提出证据证明涉外仲裁裁决有[本法第八十三条](#art-83)第一款规定的情形之一的，经人民法院组成合议庭审查核实，裁定不予执行。  
人民法院认定执行该裁决违背公共利益的，应当裁定不予执行。

**Article 84** Where the respondent adduces evidence that a foreign-related arbitral award falls under any of the circumstances set forth in paragraph 1 of Article 83 of this Law, the People's Court shall, after examination and verification by a collegial panel formed by it, rule to refuse enforcement.  
If the People's Court finds that the enforcement of the award is contrary to the public interest, it shall rule to refuse enforcement.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条　在中华人民共和国领域内作出的发生法律效力的仲裁裁决，当事人请求执行的，如果被执行人或者其财产不在中华人民共和国领域内，当事人可以直接向有管辖权的外国法院申请承认和执行。

**Article 85** Where a party requests enforcement of an arbitral award that has taken legal effect and has been made within the territory of the People's Republic of China, if the person against whom enforcement is sought or its property is not within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the party may directly apply to a competent foreign court for recognition and enforcement.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条　支持仲裁机构到中华人民共和国境外设立业务机构，开展仲裁活动。  
根据经济社会发展和改革开放需要，可以允许境外仲裁机构在国务院批准设立的自由贸易试验区、海南自由贸易港等区域内依照国家有关规定设立业务机构，开展涉外仲裁活动。

**Article 86** It is supported that arbitration institutions establish business offices outside the territory of the People's Republic of China to conduct arbitration activities.  
In light of the needs of economic and social development and reform and opening up, foreign arbitration institutions may be permitted to establish business offices in pilot free trade zones approved by the State Council, the Hainan Free Trade Port, and other regions to conduct foreign-related arbitration activities in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条　鼓励涉外仲裁当事人选择中华人民共和国（包括特别行政区）的仲裁机构、约定中华人民共和国（包括特别行政区）作为仲裁地进行仲裁。

**Article 87** Parties to foreign-related arbitration are encouraged to choose arbitration institutions of the People's Republic of China (including special administrative regions) and to agree on the People's Republic of China (including special administrative regions) as the place of arbitration.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条　在中华人民共和国领域外作出的发生法律效力的仲裁裁决，需要人民法院承认和执行的，当事人可以直接向被执行人住所地或者其财产所在地的中级人民法院申请。被执行人住所地或者其财产不在中华人民共和国领域内的，当事人可以向申请人住所地或者与裁决的纠纷有适当联系的地点的中级人民法院申请。人民法院应当依照中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照互惠原则办理。  
外国仲裁机构对中华人民共和国的公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益加以限制、歧视的，中华人民共和国有关机构有权对该国公民、企业和其他组织实行对等原则。

**Article 88** Where an arbitral award that has taken legal effect and has been made outside the territory of the People's Republic of China requires recognition and enforcement by a People's Court, the party may directly apply to the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the person against whom enforcement is sought has its domicile or where its property is located. If the person against whom enforcement is sought or its property is not within the territory of the People's Republic of China, the party may apply to the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the applicant has its domicile or of a place that has an appropriate connection with the dispute covered by the award. The People's Court shall handle the matter in accordance with the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or in accordance with the principle of reciprocity.  
If a foreign arbitration institution restricts or discriminates against the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations of the People's Republic of China, the relevant institution of the People's Republic of China shall have the right to apply the principle of reciprocity to the citizens, enterprises, and other organizations of that country.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条　本法所称的仲裁机构包括依法设立的仲裁委员会、仲裁院等机构。

**Article 89** For the purposes of this Law, arbitration institutions include arbitration commissions, arbitration courts, and other institutions established in accordance with the law.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条　法律对仲裁时效有规定的，依照其规定；没有规定的，适用诉讼时效的规定。

**Article 90** Where the law has provisions on the limitation of arbitration, those provisions shall apply; where there are no such provisions, the provisions on the limitation of action shall apply.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条　仲裁机构依照本法和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，可以参照中国仲裁协会制定的示范仲裁规则制定仲裁规则。

**Article 91** An arbitration institution, in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Law and the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, may formulate its arbitration rules by reference to the model arbitration rules formulated by the China Arbitration Association.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条　当事人应当按照规定交纳仲裁费用。  
仲裁机构根据国家有关规定，制定收取仲裁费用的办法。

**Article 92** The parties shall pay arbitration fees in accordance with the regulations.  
An arbitration institution shall formulate the measures for collecting arbitration fees in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条　劳动争议仲裁、农村土地承包经营纠纷仲裁和体育仲裁等，适用《中华人民共和国劳动争议调解仲裁法》、《中华人民共和国农村土地承包经营纠纷调解仲裁法》、《中华人民共和国体育法》等有关法律的规定。

**Article 93** Labor dispute arbitration, rural land contract management dispute arbitration, sports arbitration, and the like shall be governed by the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Mediation and Arbitration of Labor Disputes, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Mediation and Arbitration of Rural Land Contract Management Disputes, the Law of the People's Republic of China on Sports, and other relevant laws.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条　仲裁机构、仲裁庭可以依照有关国际投资条约、协定关于将投资争端提交仲裁的规定，按照争议双方约定的仲裁规则办理国际投资仲裁案件。

**Article 94** An arbitration institution or an arbitral tribunal may, in accordance with the provisions of the relevant international investment treaties or agreements on submitting investment disputes to arbitration, handle international investment arbitration cases in accordance with the arbitration rules agreed upon by the parties to the dispute.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条　违反仲裁机构登记管理规定的，依照有关法律、行政法规的规定处理。

**Article 95** Any violation of the provisions on the registration and administration of arbitration institutions shall be dealt with in accordance with the relevant laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条　本法自2026年3月1日起施行。

**Article 96** This Law shall come into force on March 1, 2026.
