# 最高人民法院关于人民调解协议司法确认程序的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕5号  
**公布日期**：2011-03-21

> 最高人民法院《关于人民调解协议司法确认程序的若干规定》已于2011年3月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1515次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年3月30日施行。 2011年3月23日 最高人民法院 关于人民调解协议司法确认程序的若干规定 法释〔2011〕5号 为了规范经人民调解委员会调解达成的民事调解协议的司法确认程序，进一步建立健全诉讼与非诉讼相衔接的矛盾纠纷解决机制，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国人民调解法》的规定，结合审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  当事人根据《中华人民共和国人民调解法》第三十三条的规定共同向人民法院申请确认调解协议的，人民法院应当依法受理。 第二条  当事人申请确认调解协议的，由主持调解的人民调解委员会所在地基层人民法院或者它派出的法庭管辖。 人民法院在立案前委派人民调解委员会调解并达成调解协议，当事人申请司法确认的，由委派的人民法院管辖。 第三条  当事人申请确认调解协议，应当向人民法院提交司法确认申请书、调解协议和身份证明、资格证明，以及与调解协议相关的财产权利证明等证明材料，并提供双方当事人的送达地址、电话号码等联系方式。委托他人代为申请的，必须向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。 第四条  人民法院收到当事人司法确认申请，应当在三日内决定是否受理。人民法院决定受理的，应当编立“调确字”案号，并及时向当事人送达受理通知书。双方当事人同时到法院申请司法确认的，人民法院可以当即受理并作出是否确认的决定。 有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予受理： （一）不属于人民法院受理民事案件的范围或者不属于接受申请的人民法院管辖的； （二）确认身份关系的； （三）确认收养关系的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  当事人根据[《中华人民共和国人民调解法》第三十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国人民调解法.md#art-33)的规定共同向人民法院申请确认调解协议的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 1** Where the parties jointly apply to the People's Court for confirmation of a mediation agreement pursuant to Article 33 of the People's Mediation Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall accept the application according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  当事人申请确认调解协议的，由主持调解的人民调解委员会所在地基层人民法院或者它派出的法庭管辖。  
人民法院在立案前委派人民调解委员会调解并达成调解协议，当事人申请司法确认的，由委派的人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Where a party applies for confirmation of a mediation agreement, the Primary People's Court of the place where the people's mediation commission that presided over the mediation is located, or a tribunal dispatched by it, shall have jurisdiction.  
Where a People's Court, before filing a case, delegates a people's mediation commission to conduct mediation and a mediation agreement is reached, and the party applies for judicial confirmation, the delegating People's Court shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  当事人申请确认调解协议，应当向人民法院提交司法确认申请书、调解协议和身份证明、资格证明，以及与调解协议相关的财产权利证明等证明材料，并提供双方当事人的送达地址、电话号码等联系方式。委托他人代为申请的，必须向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。

**Article 3** A party applying for confirmation of a mediation agreement shall submit to the People's Court a written application for judicial confirmation, the mediation agreement, identity certificate, qualification certificate, and property rights certificates and other evidentiary materials related to the mediation agreement, and shall provide the service address, telephone number and other contact information of both parties. Where another person is entrusted to file the application on behalf of the party, a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal must be submitted to the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院收到当事人司法确认申请，应当在三日内决定是否受理。人民法院决定受理的，应当编立“调确字”案号，并及时向当事人送达受理通知书。双方当事人同时到法院申请司法确认的，人民法院可以当即受理并作出是否确认的决定。  
有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予受理：  
（一）不属于人民法院受理民事案件的范围或者不属于接受申请的人民法院管辖的；  
（二）确认身份关系的；  
（三）确认收养关系的；  
（四）确认婚姻关系的。

**Article 4** Where a People's Court receives a party's application for judicial confirmation, it shall decide whether to accept it within three days. Where the People's Court decides to accept the application, it shall assign a case number in the form of "Tiaoque Zi" and promptly serve a notice of acceptance on the party. Where both parties appear before the court simultaneously to apply for judicial confirmation, the People's Court may accept the application immediately and make a decision on whether to grant confirmation.  
The People's Court shall not accept an application in any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the matter does not fall within the scope of civil cases accepted by the People's Court or is not within the jurisdiction of the People's Court receiving the application;  
(2) the matter involves the confirmation of an identity relationship;  
(3) the matter involves the confirmation of an adoption relationship;  
(4) the matter involves the confirmation of a marital relationship.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院应当自受理司法确认申请之日起十五日内作出是否确认的决定。因特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长十日。  
在人民法院作出是否确认的决定前，一方或者双方当事人撤回司法确认申请的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall make a decision on whether to confirm the mediation agreement within fifteen days from the date of acceptance of the application for judicial confirmation. Where extension is necessary due to special circumstances, with the approval of the president of the court, the period may be extended by ten days.  
Where, before the People's Court makes a decision on whether to confirm, one party or both parties withdraw the application for judicial confirmation, the People's Court shall permit such withdrawal.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院受理司法确认申请后，应当指定一名审判人员对调解协议进行审查。人民法院在必要时可以通知双方当事人同时到场，当面询问当事人。当事人应当向人民法院如实陈述申请确认的调解协议的有关情况，保证提交的证明材料真实、合法。人民法院在审查中，认为当事人的陈述或者提供的证明材料不充分、不完备或者有疑义的，可以要求当事人补充陈述或者补充证明材料。当事人无正当理由未按时补充或者拒不接受询问的，可以按撤回司法确认申请处理。

**Article 6** After accepting an application for judicial confirmation, the People's Court shall designate an adjudicator to examine the mediation agreement. Where necessary, the People's Court may notify both parties to appear simultaneously and question them in person. The parties shall truthfully state to the People's Court the relevant circumstances of the mediation agreement for which confirmation is sought, and guarantee that the evidentiary materials submitted are authentic and lawful. During the examination, if the People's Court finds that the party's statements or the evidentiary materials provided are insufficient, incomplete, or questionable, it may require the party to supplement the statements or evidentiary materials. If a party, without justifiable reasons, fails to timely supplement or refuses to be questioned, the People's Court may treat the application as withdrawn.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  具有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予确认调解协议效力：  
（一）违反法律、行政法规强制性规定的；  
（二）侵害国家利益、社会公共利益的；  
（三）侵害案外人合法权益的；  
（四）损害社会公序良俗的；  
（五）内容不明确，无法确认的；  
（六）其他不能进行司法确认的情形。

**Article 7** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not confirm the validity of the mediation agreement:  
(1) the agreement violates a mandatory provision of a law or administrative regulation;  
(2) the agreement infringes upon national interests or public interests;  
(3) the agreement infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a person not party to the case;  
(4) the agreement harms public order and good customs;  
(5) the content of the agreement is unclear and cannot be confirmed;  
(6) other circumstances under which judicial confirmation may not be made.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院经审查认为调解协议符合确认条件的，应当作出确认决定书；决定不予确认调解协议效力的，应当作出不予确认决定书。

**Article 8** Where, upon examination, the People's Court finds that the mediation agreement meets the conditions for confirmation, it shall issue a decision of confirmation; where it decides not to confirm the validity of the mediation agreement, it shall issue a decision of non-confirmation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院依法作出确认决定后，一方当事人拒绝履行或者未全部履行的，对方当事人可以向作出确认决定的人民法院申请强制执行。

**Article 9** After the People's Court has made a decision of confirmation according to law, if one party refuses to perform or fails to fully perform the agreement, the other party may apply to the People's Court that made the decision of confirmation for compulsory enforcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  案外人认为经人民法院确认的调解协议侵害其合法权益的，可以自知道或者应当知道权益被侵害之日起一年内，向作出确认决定的人民法院申请撤销确认决定。

**Article 10** Where a person not party to the case believes that the mediation agreement confirmed by the People's Court infringes upon its lawful rights and interests, it may, within one year from the date on which it knew or should have known that its rights and interests were infringed, apply to the People's Court that made the decision of confirmation for revocation of that decision.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民法院办理人民调解协议司法确认案件，不收取费用。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall not charge any fee for handling cases of judicial confirmation of people's mediation agreements.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  人民法院可以将调解协议不予确认的情况定期或者不定期通报同级司法行政机关和相关人民调解委员会。

**Article 12** The People's Court may, on a regular or irregular basis, notify the judicial administrative organ at the same level and the relevant people's mediation commission of the cases where confirmation of mediation agreements is not granted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  经人民法院建立的调解员名册中的调解员调解达成协议后，当事人申请司法确认的，参照本规定办理。人民法院立案后委托他人调解达成的协议的司法确认，按照《最高人民法院关于人民法院民事调解工作若干问题的规定》（法释〔2004〕12号）的有关规定办理。

**Article 13** Where a mediation agreement is reached through mediation by a mediator included in the roster of mediators established by a People's Court, and a party applies for judicial confirmation, these Provisions shall be applied mutatis mutandis. The judicial confirmation of an agreement reached through mediation entrusted by the People's Court to another person after the case has been filed shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Civil Mediation Work of People's Courts (Fa Shi [2004] No. 12).
