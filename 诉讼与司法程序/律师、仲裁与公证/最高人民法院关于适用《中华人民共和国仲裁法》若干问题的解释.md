# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕7号  
**公布日期**：2005-12-26  
**生效日期**：2006-09-08

> 《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国仲裁法〉若干问题的解释》已于2005年12月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1375次会议通过，现予公布，自2006年9月8日起施行。 2006年8月23日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国仲裁法》 若干问题的解释 法释〔2006〕7号 根据《中华人民共和国仲裁法》和《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，对人民法院审理涉及仲裁案件适用法律的若干问题作如下解释： 第一条  仲裁法第十六条规定的“其他书面形式”的仲裁协议，包括以合同书、信件和数据电文（包括电报、电传、传真、电子数据交换和电子邮件）等形式达成的请求仲裁的协议。 第二条  当事人概括约定仲裁事项为合同争议的，基于合同成立、效力、变更、转让、履行、违约责任、解释、解除等产生的纠纷都可以认定为仲裁事项。 第三条  仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构名称不准确，但能够确定具体的仲裁机构的，应当认定选定了仲裁机构。 第四条  仲裁协议仅约定纠纷适用的仲裁规则的，视为未约定仲裁机构，但当事人达成补充协议或者按照约定的仲裁规则能够确定仲裁机构的除外。 第五条  仲裁协议约定两个以上仲裁机构的，当事人可以协议选择其中的一个仲裁机构申请仲裁；当事人不能就仲裁机构选择达成一致的，仲裁协议无效。 第六条  仲裁协议约定由某地的仲裁机构仲裁且该地仅有一个仲裁机构的，该仲裁机构视为约定的仲裁机构。该地有两个以上仲裁机构的，当事人可以协议选择其中的一个仲裁机构申请仲裁；当事人不能就仲裁机构选择达成一致的，仲裁协议无效。 第七条  当事人约定争议可以向仲裁机构申请仲裁也可以向人民法院起诉的，仲裁协议无效。但一方向仲裁机构申请仲裁，另一方未在仲裁法第二十条第二款规定期间内提出异议的除外。 第八条  当事人订立仲裁协议后合并、分立的，仲裁协议对其权利义务的继受人有效。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  [仲裁法第十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-16)规定的“其他书面形式”的仲裁协议，包括以合同书、信件和数据电文（包括电报、电传、传真、电子数据交换和电子邮件）等形式达成的请求仲裁的协议。

**Article 1** The arbitration agreement in “other written form” as provided in Article 16 of the Arbitration Law shall include an agreement to request arbitration concluded in the form of a contract, correspondence, or data message (including telegram, telex, facsimile, electronic data interchange and e-mail).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  当事人概括约定仲裁事项为合同争议的，基于合同成立、效力、变更、转让、履行、违约责任、解释、解除等产生的纠纷都可以认定为仲裁事项。

**Article 2** Where the parties generally agree that the subject matter of arbitration is a contract dispute, disputes arising from the formation, validity, modification, assignment, performance, liability for breach of contract, interpretation, or termination of the contract may all be determined as arbitrable matters.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构名称不准确，但能够确定具体的仲裁机构的，应当认定选定了仲裁机构。

**Article 3** If the name of the arbitration institution agreed upon in the arbitration agreement is inaccurate, but the specific arbitration institution can be determined, it shall be deemed that an arbitration institution has been selected.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  仲裁协议仅约定纠纷适用的仲裁规则的，视为未约定仲裁机构，但当事人达成补充协议或者按照约定的仲裁规则能够确定仲裁机构的除外。

**Article 4** If an arbitration agreement only stipulates the arbitration rules applicable to the dispute, it shall be deemed that no arbitration institution has been agreed upon, unless the parties reach a supplementary agreement or the arbitration institution can be determined in accordance with the agreed arbitration rules.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  仲裁协议约定两个以上仲裁机构的，当事人可以协议选择其中的一个仲裁机构申请仲裁；当事人不能就仲裁机构选择达成一致的，仲裁协议无效。

**Article 5** If an arbitration agreement stipulates two or more arbitration institutions, the parties may, by agreement, select one of them to apply for arbitration; if the parties fail to reach an agreement on the selection of the arbitration institution, the arbitration agreement shall be invalid.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  仲裁协议约定由某地的仲裁机构仲裁且该地仅有一个仲裁机构的，该仲裁机构视为约定的仲裁机构。该地有两个以上仲裁机构的，当事人可以协议选择其中的一个仲裁机构申请仲裁；当事人不能就仲裁机构选择达成一致的，仲裁协议无效。

**Article 6** If an arbitration agreement stipulates arbitration by an arbitration institution in a certain place and there is only one arbitration institution in that place, that arbitration institution shall be deemed as the agreed arbitration institution. If there are two or more arbitration institutions in that place, the parties may, by agreement, select one of them to apply for arbitration; if the parties fail to reach an agreement on the selection of the arbitration institution, the arbitration agreement shall be invalid.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  当事人约定争议可以向仲裁机构申请仲裁也可以向人民法院起诉的，仲裁协议无效。但一方向仲裁机构申请仲裁，另一方未在[仲裁法第二十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-20)第二款规定期间内提出异议的除外。

**Article 7** If the parties agree that a dispute may be submitted to an arbitration institution for arbitration or may be brought to a People's Court for prosecution, the arbitration agreement shall be invalid, unless one party applies for arbitration to the arbitration institution and the other party does not raise an objection within the period specified in the second paragraph of Article 20 of the Arbitration Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  当事人订立仲裁协议后合并、分立的，仲裁协议对其权利义务的继受人有效。  
当事人订立仲裁协议后死亡的，仲裁协议对承继其仲裁事项中的权利义务的继承人有效。  
前两款规定情形，当事人订立仲裁协议时另有约定的除外。

**Article 8** Where a party is merged or divided after concluding an arbitration agreement, the arbitration agreement shall be binding on the successor to its rights and obligations.  
Where a party dies after concluding an arbitration agreement, the arbitration agreement shall be binding on the heir who succeeds to its rights and obligations in the arbitrable matters.  
Unless otherwise agreed by the parties at the time of concluding the arbitration agreement in the circumstances specified in the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  债权债务全部或者部分转让的，仲裁协议对受让人有效，但当事人另有约定、在受让债权债务时受让人明确反对或者不知有单独仲裁协议的除外。

**Article 9** Where all or part of the creditor's rights and debts are assigned, the arbitration agreement shall be binding on the assignee, unless the parties have otherwise agreed, or the assignee expressly objects at the time of the assignment, or is unaware of the existence of a separate arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  合同成立后未生效或者被撤销的，仲裁协议效力的认定适用[仲裁法第十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-19)第一款的规定。  
当事人在订立合同时就争议达成仲裁协议的，合同未成立不影响仲裁协议的效力。

**Article 10** Where a contract is not yet effective after its formation or is revoked, the validity of the arbitration agreement shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 19 of the Arbitration Law.  
Where the parties reach an arbitration agreement on disputes at the time of concluding the contract, the fact that the contract is not formed shall not affect the validity of the arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  合同约定解决争议适用其他合同、文件中的有效仲裁条款的，发生合同争议时，当事人应当按照该仲裁条款提请仲裁。  
涉外合同应当适用的有关国际条约中有仲裁规定的，发生合同争议时，当事人应当按照国际条约中的仲裁规定提请仲裁。

**Article 11** Where a contract stipulates that the dispute resolution shall be governed by a valid arbitration clause in another contract or document, the parties shall submit the dispute to arbitration in accordance with that arbitration clause when a contract dispute arises.  
Where the relevant international treaty applicable to a foreign-related contract contains arbitration provisions, the parties shall submit the dispute to arbitration in accordance with those arbitration provisions when a contract dispute arises.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  当事人向人民法院申请确认仲裁协议效力的案件，由仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构所在地的中级人民法院管辖；仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构不明确的，由仲裁协议签订地或者被申请人住所地的中级人民法院管辖。  
申请确认涉外仲裁协议效力的案件，由仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构所在地、仲裁协议签订地、申请人或者被申请人住所地的中级人民法院管辖。  
涉及海事海商纠纷仲裁协议效力的案件，由仲裁协议约定的仲裁机构所在地、仲裁协议签订地、申请人或者被申请人住所地的海事法院管辖；上述地点没有海事法院的，由就近的海事法院管辖。

**Article 12** A case in which a party applies to a People's Court for confirmation of the validity of an arbitration agreement shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court at the place of the arbitration institution agreed upon in the arbitration agreement; if the arbitration institution agreed upon in the arbitration agreement is unclear, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court at the place where the arbitration agreement was concluded or at the domicile of the respondent.  
A case applying for confirmation of the validity of a foreign-related arbitration agreement shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court at the place of the arbitration institution agreed upon in the arbitration agreement, the place where the arbitration agreement was concluded, the domicile of the applicant, or the domicile of the respondent.  
A case involving the validity of an arbitration agreement concerning a maritime or admiralty dispute shall be under the jurisdiction of the maritime court at the place of the arbitration institution agreed upon in the arbitration agreement, the place where the arbitration agreement was concluded, the domicile of the applicant, or the domicile of the respondent; if there is no maritime court at such place, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the nearest maritime court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  依照[仲裁法第二十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-20)第二款的规定，当事人在仲裁庭首次开庭前没有对仲裁协议的效力提出异议，而后向人民法院申请确认仲裁协议无效的，人民法院不予受理。  
仲裁机构对仲裁协议的效力作出决定后，当事人向人民法院申请确认仲裁协议效力或者申请撤销仲裁机构的决定的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 13** In accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 20 of the Arbitration Law, if a party fails to raise an objection to the validity of the arbitration agreement before the first hearing of the arbitral tribunal, and subsequently applies to a People's Court for confirmation of the invalidity of the arbitration agreement, the People's Court shall not accept the application.  
After the arbitration institution has made a decision on the validity of the arbitration agreement, if a party applies to a People's Court for confirmation of the validity of the arbitration agreement or for revocation of the decision of the arbitration institution, the People's Court shall not accept the application.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  [仲裁法第二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-26)规定的“首次开庭”是指答辩期满后人民法院组织的第一次开庭审理，不包括审前程序中的各项活动。

**Article 14** The “first hearing” as provided in Article 26 of the Arbitration Law refers to the first court hearing organized by the People's Court after the expiration of the period for submitting a statement of defense, and shall not include activities in the pretrial procedures.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  人民法院审理仲裁协议效力确认案件，应当组成合议庭进行审查，并询问当事人。

**Article 15** When a People's Court hears a case concerning the confirmation of the validity of an arbitration agreement, it shall form a collegial bench to conduct examination and shall hear the parties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  对涉外仲裁协议的效力审查，适用当事人约定的法律；当事人没有约定适用的法律但约定了仲裁地的，适用仲裁地法律；没有约定适用的法律也没有约定仲裁地或者仲裁地约定不明的，适用法院地法律。

**Article 16** The validity of a foreign-related arbitration agreement shall be examined in accordance with the law chosen by the parties; if the parties have not chosen the applicable law but have agreed on the seat of arbitration, the law of the seat of arbitration shall apply; if neither the applicable law nor the seat of arbitration has been agreed upon, or if the seat of arbitration is not clearly agreed upon, the law of the forum shall apply.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  当事人以不属于[仲裁法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-58)或者[民事诉讼法第二百六十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-260)规定的事由申请撤销仲裁裁决的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 17** If a party applies for setting aside of an arbitral award on grounds not falling within the matters specified in Article 58 of the Arbitration Law or Article 260 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall not support the application.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  [仲裁法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-58)第一款第一项规定的“没有仲裁协议”是指当事人没有达成仲裁协议。仲裁协议被认定无效或者被撤销的，视为没有仲裁协议。

**Article 18** “There is no arbitration agreement” as provided in Item (1) of Paragraph 1 of Article 58 of the Arbitration Law means that the parties have not reached an arbitration agreement. If an arbitration agreement is determined to be invalid or is revoked, it shall be deemed that there is no arbitration agreement.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  当事人以仲裁裁决事项超出仲裁协议范围为由申请撤销仲裁裁决，经审查属实的，人民法院应当撤销仲裁裁决中的超裁部分。但超裁部分与其他裁决事项不可分的，人民法院应当撤销仲裁裁决。

**Article 19** If a party applies for setting aside an arbitral award on the ground that the matters decided in the award exceed the scope of the arbitration agreement, and upon examination the ground is established, the People's Court shall set aside the part of the arbitral award that exceeds the scope. However, if the part that exceeds the scope is inseparable from the other matters decided in the award, the People's Court shall set aside the entire arbitral award.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  [仲裁法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-58)规定的“违反法定程序”，是指违反仲裁法规定的仲裁程序和当事人选择的仲裁规则可能影响案件正确裁决的情形。

**Article 20** “Violation of statutory procedures” as provided in Article 58 of the Arbitration Law refers to circumstances where a violation of the arbitration procedures stipulated in the Arbitration Law and the arbitration rules chosen by the parties may affect the correct adjudication of the case.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  当事人申请撤销国内仲裁裁决的案件属于下列情形之一的，人民法院可以依照[仲裁法第六十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-61)的规定通知仲裁庭在一定期限内重新仲裁：  
（一）仲裁裁决所根据的证据是伪造的；  
（二）对方当事人隐瞒了足以影响公正裁决的证据的。  
人民法院应当在通知中说明要求重新仲裁的具体理由。

**Article 21** If a case in which a party applies for setting aside a domestic arbitral award falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 61 of the Arbitration Law, notify the arbitral tribunal to re-arbitrate within a specified period:  
(1) The evidence on which the arbitral award is based is forged;  
(2) The other party has concealed evidence sufficient to affect a fair award.  
The People's Court shall specify in the notice the specific reasons for requiring re-arbitration.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  仲裁庭在人民法院指定的期限内开始重新仲裁的,人民法院应当裁定终结撤销程序;未开始重新仲裁的，人民法院应当裁定恢复撤销程序。

**Article 22** If the arbitral tribunal begins re-arbitration within the period specified by the People's Court, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the setting-aside procedure; if the re-arbitration has not begun, the People's Court shall rule to resume the setting-aside procedure.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  当事人对重新仲裁裁决不服的，可以在重新仲裁裁决书送达之日起六个月内依据[仲裁法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-58)规定向人民法院申请撤销。

**Article 23** If a party is dissatisfied with the re-arbitration award, it may apply to a People's Court for setting aside the award in accordance with Article 58 of the Arbitration Law within six months from the date of service of the written re-arbitration award.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  当事人申请撤销仲裁裁决的案件，人民法院应当组成合议庭审理，并询问当事人。

**Article 24** For a case in which a party applies for setting aside an arbitral award, the People's Court shall form a collegial bench to hear the case and shall hear the parties.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  人民法院受理当事人撤销仲裁裁决的申请后，另一方当事人申请执行同一仲裁裁决的，受理执行申请的人民法院应当在受理后裁定中止执行。

**Article 25** After a People's Court accepts an application from a party to set aside an arbitral award, if the other party applies for enforcement of the same arbitral award, the People's Court that accepts the enforcement application shall, after acceptance, rule to suspend the enforcement.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  当事人向人民法院申请撤销仲裁裁决被驳回后，又在执行程序中以相同理由提出不予执行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 26** After an application by a party to a People's Court for setting aside an arbitral award has been rejected, if the party again raises a defense of non-enforcement on the same ground in the enforcement proceedings, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  当事人在仲裁程序中未对仲裁协议的效力提出异议，在仲裁裁决作出后以仲裁协议无效为由主张撤销仲裁裁决或者提出不予执行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。  
当事人在仲裁程序中对仲裁协议的效力提出异议，在仲裁裁决作出后又以此为由主张撤销仲裁裁决或者提出不予执行抗辩，经审查符合[仲裁法第五十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/中华人民共和国仲裁法.md#art-58)或者[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)、第二百六十条规定的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 27** If a party does not raise an objection to the validity of the arbitration agreement in the arbitration proceedings, and after the arbitral award is made, claims to set aside the arbitral award or raises a defense of non-enforcement on the ground that the arbitration agreement is invalid, the People's Court shall not support it.  
If a party raises an objection to the validity of the arbitration agreement in the arbitration proceedings, and after the arbitral award is made, claims to set aside the arbitral award or raises a defense of non-enforcement on that ground, upon examination, if the circumstances conform to the provisions of Article 58 of the Arbitration Law or Article 217 or Article 260 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the People's Court shall support it.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  当事人请求不予执行仲裁调解书或者根据当事人之间的和解协议作出的仲裁裁决书的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 28** If a party requests non-enforcement of an arbitration mediation document or an arbitral award made in accordance with a settlement agreement between the parties, the People's Court shall not support it.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  当事人申请执行仲裁裁决案件，由被执行人住所地或者被执行的财产所在地的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 29** A case in which a party applies for enforcement of an arbitral award shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the person subject to enforcement or the place where the property subject to enforcement is located.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  根据审理撤销、执行仲裁裁决案件的实际需要，人民法院可以要求仲裁机构作出说明或者向相关仲裁机构调阅仲裁案卷。  
人民法院在办理涉及仲裁的案件过程中作出的裁定，可以送相关的仲裁机构。

**Article 30** Based on the actual needs of hearing cases concerning setting aside or enforcement of arbitral awards, a People's Court may request the arbitration institution to provide an explanation or may request to review the arbitration case files from the relevant arbitration institution.  
Rulings made by a People's Court in the process of handling cases involving arbitration may be served on the relevant arbitration institution.
