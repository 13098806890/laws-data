### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为规范公证活动，保障公证机构和公证员依法履行职责，预防纠纷，保障自然人、法人或者其他组织的合法权益，制定本法。

**Article 1** This Law is enacted for the purposes of regulating notarization activities, ensuring that notarial institutions and notaries perform their duties according to law, preventing disputes, and protecting the legitimate rights and interests of natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　公证是公证机构根据自然人、法人或者其他组织的申请，依照法定程序对民事法律行为、有法律意义的事实和文书的真实性、合法性予以证明的活动。

**Article 2** Notarization refers to the activity in which a notarial institution, upon application by a natural person, legal person, or other organization, certifies the truthfulness and legality of civil juristic acts, facts of legal significance, and documents in accordance with statutory procedures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　公证机构办理公证，应当遵守法律，坚持客观、公正的原则。

**Article 3** A notarial institution handling notarization shall comply with the law and adhere to the principles of objectivity and impartiality.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　全国设立中国公证协会，省、自治区、直辖市设立地方公证协会。中国公证协会和地方公证协会是社会团体法人。中国公证协会章程由会员代表大会制定，报国务院司法行政部门备案。  
公证协会是公证业的自律性组织，依据章程开展活动，对公证机构、公证员的执业活动进行监督。

**Article 4** A China Notary Association shall be established nationwide, and local notary associations shall be established in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government. The China Notary Association and the local notary associations are social organization legal persons. The articles of association of the China Notary Association shall be formulated by the congress of members and submitted to the judicial administration department of the State Council for recordation.  
The notary associations are self-regulatory organizations of the notary profession, carrying out activities in accordance with their articles of association and supervising the professional practice of notarial institutions and notaries.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　司法行政部门依照本法规定对公证机构、公证员和公证协会进行监督、指导。

**Article 5** The judicial administration departments shall, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, supervise and guide notarial institutions, notaries, and notary associations.

### 第二章　公证机构

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　公证机构是依法设立，不以营利为目的，依法独立行使公证职能、承担民事责任的证明机构。

**Article 6** A notarial institution is a certification institution established according to law, which does not aim at profit, independently exercises the notarial function according to law, and bears civil liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　公证机构按照统筹规划、合理布局的原则，可以在县、不设区的市、设区的市、直辖市或者市辖区设立；在设区的市、直辖市可以设立一个或者若干个公证机构。公证机构不按行政区划层层设立。

**Article 7** Notarial institutions may be established in counties, cities not divided into districts, cities with districts, municipalities directly under the Central Government, or municipal districts in accordance with the principles of unified planning and rational layout; one or several notarial institutions may be established in a city with districts or a municipality directly under the Central Government. Notarial institutions shall not be established at multiple levels according to administrative divisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立公证机构，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）有自己的名称；  
（二）有固定的场所；  
（三）有二名以上公证员；  
（四）有开展公证业务所必需的资金。

**Article 8** The establishment of a notarial institution shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having its own name;  
(2) having a fixed place of business;  
(3) having no less than two notaries;  
(4) having the funds necessary for carrying out notarization business.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　设立公证机构，由所在地的司法行政部门报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门按照规定程序批准后，颁发公证机构执业证书。

**Article 9** To establish a notarial institution, the judicial administration department of the locality shall submit an application to the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for approval in accordance with the prescribed procedures, and then a notarial institution practice certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　公证机构的负责人应当在有三年以上执业经历的公证员中推选产生，由所在地的司法行政部门核准，报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门备案。

**Article 10** The person in charge of a notarial institution shall be elected from among notaries with at least three years of practice experience, approved by the judicial administration department of the locality, and submitted to the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for recordation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　根据自然人、法人或者其他组织的申请，公证机构办理下列公证事项：  
（一）合同；  
（二）继承；  
（三）委托、声明、赠与、遗嘱；  
（四）财产分割；  
（五）招标投标、拍卖；  
（六）婚姻状况、亲属关系、收养关系；  
（七）出生、生存、死亡、身份、经历、学历、学位、职务、职称、有无违法犯罪记录；  
（八）公司章程；  
（九）保全证据；  
（十）文书上的签名、印鉴、日期，文书的副本、影印本与原本相符；  
（十一）自然人、法人或者其他组织自愿申请办理的其他公证事项。  
法律、行政法规规定应当公证的事项，有关自然人、法人或者其他组织应当向公证机构申请办理公证。

**Article 11** Upon application by a natural person, legal person, or other organization, a notarial institution shall handle the following notarization matters:  
(1) contracts;  
(2) succession;  
(3) powers of attorney, declarations, gifts, and wills;  
(4) division of property;  
(5) bidding and auction;  
(6) marital status, kinship, and adoption relationships;  
(7) birth, survival, death, identity, experience, education, academic degrees, positions, professional titles, and records of criminal violations;  
(8) articles of association of a company;  
(9) preservation of evidence;  
(10) signatures, seals, and dates on documents, and conformity of copies and photocopies with the originals;  
(11) other notarization matters voluntarily applied for by natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations.  
Where notarization is required by laws or administrative regulations, the relevant natural persons, legal persons, or other organizations shall apply to a notarial institution for notarization.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　根据自然人、法人或者其他组织的申请，公证机构可以办理下列事务：  
（一）法律、行政法规规定由公证机构登记的事务；  
（二）提存；  
（三）保管遗嘱、遗产或者其他与公证事项有关的财产、物品、文书；  
（四）代写与公证事项有关的法律事务文书；  
（五）提供公证法律咨询。

**Article 12** Upon application by a natural person, legal person, or other organization, a notarial institution may handle the following affairs:  
(1) affairs that are required by laws or administrative regulations to be registered by notarial institutions;  
(2) deposit;  
(3) custody of wills, estates, or other property, articles, and documents related to notarization matters;  
(4) writing legal documents related to notarization matters on behalf of others;  
(5) providing legal advice on notarization.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　公证机构不得有下列行为：  
（一）为不真实、不合法的事项出具公证书；  
（二）毁损、篡改公证文书或者公证档案；  
（三）以诋毁其他公证机构、公证员或者支付回扣、佣金等不正当手段争揽公证业务；  
（四）泄露在执业活动中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私；  
（五）违反规定的收费标准收取公证费；  
（六）法律、法规、国务院司法行政部门规定禁止的其他行为。

**Article 13** A notarial institution shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) issuing a notarial certificate for untrue or illegal matters;  
(2) damaging or altering notarial documents or notarial archives;  
(3) soliciting notarization business by unfair means such as defaming other notarial institutions or notaries, or paying rebates or commissions;  
(4) disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy known in the course of practice;  
(5) collecting notarization fees in violation of the prescribed fee standards;  
(6) other acts prohibited by laws, regulations, or the provisions of the judicial administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　公证机构应当建立业务、财务、资产等管理制度，对公证员的执业行为进行监督，建立执业过错责任追究制度。

**Article 14** A notarial institution shall establish management systems for business, finance, and assets, supervise the professional conduct of notaries, and establish a system for investigating liability for practice errors.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　公证机构应当参加公证执业责任保险。

**Article 15** A notarial institution shall participate in notarial practice liability insurance.

### 第三章　公证员

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公证员是符合本法规定的条件，在公证机构从事公证业务的执业人员。

**Article 16** A notary is a practitioner who meets the conditions prescribed in this Law and engages in notarization business in a notarial institution.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　公证员的数量根据公证业务需要确定。省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门应当根据公证机构的设置情况和公证业务的需要核定公证员配备方案，报国务院司法行政部门备案。

**Article 17** The number of notaries shall be determined based on the needs of notarization business. The judicial administration department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall, in light of the establishment of notarial institutions and the needs of notarization business, verify and approve a notary allocation plan and submit it to the judicial administration department of the State Council for recordation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　担任公证员，应当具备下列条件：  
（一）具有中华人民共和国国籍；  
（二）年龄二十五周岁以上六十五周岁以下；  
（三）公道正派，遵纪守法，品行良好；  
（四）通过国家统一法律职业资格考试取得法律职业资格；  
（五）在公证机构实习二年以上或者具有三年以上其他法律职业经历并在公证机构实习一年以上，经考核合格。

**Article 18** A person to serve as a notary shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) having the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) being at least 25 years old and under 65 years old;  
(3) being fair and upright, abiding by the law and discipline, and having good moral character;  
(4) having obtained legal professional qualifications by passing the national unified legal professional qualification examination;  
(5) having completed an internship in a notarial institution for at least two years, or having at least three years of other legal professional experience and having completed an internship in a notarial institution for at least one year, and having passed the assessment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　从事法学教学、研究工作，具有高级职称的人员，或者具有本科以上学历，从事审判、检察、法制工作、法律服务满十年的公务员、律师，已经离开原工作岗位，经考核合格的，可以担任公证员。

**Article 19** Persons who are engaged in legal teaching or research with senior professional titles, or civil servants or lawyers who have a bachelor's degree or above and have been engaged in adjudication, procuratorial work, legal affairs, or legal services for a full ten years, and have left their original posts, may serve as notaries if they pass the assessment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　有下列情形之一的，不得担任公证员：  
（一）无民事行为能力或者限制民事行为能力的；  
（二）因故意犯罪或者职务过失犯罪受过刑事处罚的；  
（三）被开除公职的；  
（四）被吊销公证员、律师执业证书的。

**Article 20** A person shall not serve as a notary under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having no civil capacity or having limited civil capacity;  
(2) having received criminal punishment for an intentional crime or a crime of negligence in office;  
(3) having been discharged from public employment;  
(4) having had his/her notary or lawyer practice certificate revoked.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　担任公证员，应当由符合公证员条件的人员提出申请，经公证机构推荐，由所在地的司法行政部门报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门审核同意后，报请国务院司法行政部门任命，并由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门颁发公证员执业证书。

**Article 21** To serve as a notary, a person who meets the conditions for a notary shall submit an application, be recommended by a notarial institution, and then the judicial administration department of the locality shall submit the application to the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government for review and approval. After approval, the matter shall be submitted to the judicial administration department of the State Council for appointment, and the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall issue a notary practice certificate.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　公证员应当遵纪守法，恪守职业道德，依法履行公证职责，保守执业秘密。  
公证员有权获得劳动报酬，享受保险和福利待遇；有权提出辞职、申诉或者控告；非因法定事由和非经法定程序，不被免职或者处罚。

**Article 22** A notary shall abide by the law and discipline, adhere to professional ethics, perform notarial duties according to law, and preserve professional secrets.  
A notary shall have the right to receive labor remuneration, enjoy insurance and welfare benefits; have the right to resign, file a petition, or lodge a complaint; and shall not be removed from office or punished except for statutory reasons and through statutory procedures.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　公证员不得有下列行为：  
（一）同时在二个以上公证机构执业；  
（二）从事有报酬的其他职业；  
（三）为本人及近亲属办理公证或者办理与本人及近亲属有利害关系的公证；  
（四）私自出具公证书；  
（五）为不真实、不合法的事项出具公证书；  
（六）侵占、挪用公证费或者侵占、盗窃公证专用物品；  
（七）毁损、篡改公证文书或者公证档案；  
（八）泄露在执业活动中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私；  
（九）法律、法规、国务院司法行政部门规定禁止的其他行为。

**Article 23** A notary shall not engage in any of the following acts:  
(1) practicing in two or more notarial institutions simultaneously;  
(2) engaging in other remunerative occupations;  
(3) handling notarization for himself/herself or close relatives, or handling notarization in which he/she or close relatives have an interest;  
(4) issuing notarial certificates without authorization;  
(5) issuing notarial certificates for untrue or illegal matters;  
(6) embezzling or misappropriating notarization fees, or embezzling or stealing articles specially used for notarization;  
(7) damaging or altering notarial documents or notarial archives;  
(8) disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy known in the course of practice;  
(9) other acts prohibited by laws, regulations, or the provisions of the judicial administration department of the State Council.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公证员有下列情形之一的，由所在地的司法行政部门报省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门提请国务院司法行政部门予以免职：  
（一）丧失中华人民共和国国籍的；  
（二）年满六十五周岁或者因健康原因不能继续履行职务的；  
（三）自愿辞去公证员职务的；  
（四）被吊销公证员执业证书的。

**Article 24** A notary shall be removed from office by the judicial administration department of the State Council upon the request of the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, which is submitted by the judicial administration department of the locality, under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) having lost the nationality of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) having reached the age of 65 or being unable to continue to perform duties due to health reasons;  
(3) having voluntarily resigned from the position of notary;  
(4) having had the notary practice certificate revoked.

### 第四章　公证程序

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　自然人、法人或者其他组织申请办理公证，可以向住所地、经常居住地、行为地或者事实发生地的公证机构提出。  
申请办理涉及不动产的公证，应当向不动产所在地的公证机构提出；申请办理涉及不动产的委托、声明、赠与、遗嘱的公证，可以适用前款规定。

**Article 25** A natural person, legal person, or other organization applying for notarization may submit the application to a notarial institution at the place of domicile, habitual residence, act, or occurrence of the fact.  
An application for notarization involving immovable property shall be submitted to a notarial institution at the place where the immovable property is located; an application for notarization of a power of attorney, declaration, gift, or will involving immovable property may be governed by the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　自然人、法人或者其他组织可以委托他人办理公证，但遗嘱、生存、收养关系等应当由本人办理公证的除外。

**Article 26** A natural person, legal person, or other organization may entrust another person to handle notarization, except for notarization of wills, survival, adoption relationships, and other matters that shall be handled by the person himself/herself.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请办理公证的当事人应当向公证机构如实说明申请公证的事项的有关情况，提供真实、合法、充分的证明材料；提供的证明材料不充分的，公证机构可以要求补充。  
公证机构受理公证申请后，应当告知当事人申请公证事项的法律意义和可能产生的法律后果，并将告知内容记录存档。

**Article 27** A party applying for notarization shall truthfully explain to the notarial institution the relevant circumstances of the matter for which notarization is applied, and provide truthful, lawful, and sufficient evidentiary materials; if the evidentiary materials provided are insufficient, the notarial institution may request supplementation.  
After accepting an application for notarization, the notarial institution shall inform the party of the legal significance and possible legal consequences of the matter for which notarization is applied, and shall record the contents of the information and keep it on file.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　公证机构办理公证，应当根据不同公证事项的办证规则，分别审查下列事项：  
（一）当事人的身份、申请办理该项公证的资格以及相应的权利；  
（二）提供的文书内容是否完备，含义是否清晰，签名、印鉴是否齐全；  
（三）提供的证明材料是否真实、合法、充分；  
（四）申请公证的事项是否真实、合法。

**Article 28** When handling notarization, a notarial institution shall, in accordance with the rules for handling different notarization matters, examine the following items respectively:  
(1) the identity of the party, the qualifications for applying for the notarization, and the corresponding rights;  
(2) whether the contents of the documents provided are complete, whether the meanings are clear, and whether the signatures and seals are complete;  
(3) whether the evidentiary materials provided are truthful, lawful, and sufficient;  
(4) whether the matter for which notarization is applied is truthful and lawful.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　公证机构对申请公证的事项以及当事人提供的证明材料，按照有关办证规则需要核实或者对其有疑义的，应当进行核实，或者委托异地公证机构代为核实，有关单位或者个人应当依法予以协助。

**Article 29** Where a notarial institution needs to verify the matter for which notarization is applied or the evidentiary materials provided by the party, or has doubts about them, in accordance with the relevant handling rules, it shall conduct verification, or entrust a notarial institution in another place to verify on its behalf, and the relevant units or individuals shall assist in accordance with law.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　公证机构经审查，认为申请提供的证明材料真实、合法、充分，申请公证的事项真实、合法的，应当自受理公证申请之日起十五个工作日内向当事人出具公证书。但是，因不可抗力、补充证明材料或者需要核实有关情况的，所需时间不计算在期限内。

**Article 30** Where a notarial institution, after examination, considers that the evidentiary materials provided in the application are truthful, lawful, and sufficient, and the matter for which notarization is applied is truthful and lawful, it shall issue a notarial certificate to the party within fifteen working days from the date of accepting the application for notarization. However, the time required due to force majeure, supplementary provision of evidentiary materials, or the need to verify relevant circumstances shall not be counted within the time limit.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　有下列情形之一的，公证机构不予办理公证：  
（一）无民事行为能力人或者限制民事行为能力人没有监护人代理申请办理公证的；  
（二）当事人与申请公证的事项没有利害关系的；  
（三）申请公证的事项属专业技术鉴定、评估事项的；  
（四）当事人之间对申请公证的事项有争议的；  
（五）当事人虚构、隐瞒事实，或者提供虚假证明材料的；  
（六）当事人提供的证明材料不充分或者拒绝补充证明材料的；  
（七）申请公证的事项不真实、不合法的；  
（八）申请公证的事项违背社会公德的；  
（九）当事人拒绝按照规定支付公证费的。

**Article 31** A notarial institution shall not handle notarization under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity does not have a guardian to apply for notarization on his/her behalf;  
(2) the party has no interest in the matter for which notarization is applied;  
(3) the matter for which notarization is applied falls under professional technical appraisal or evaluation;  
(4) there is a dispute between the parties over the matter for which notarization is applied;  
(5) the party fabricates or conceals facts, or provides false evidentiary materials;  
(6) the evidentiary materials provided by the party are insufficient or the party refuses to supplement them;  
(7) the matter for which notarization is applied is untrue or illegal;  
(8) the matter for which notarization is applied violates social morality;  
(9) the party refuses to pay the notarization fee in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　公证书应当按照国务院司法行政部门规定的格式制作，由公证员签名或者加盖签名章并加盖公证机构印章。公证书自出具之日起生效。  
公证书应当使用全国通用的文字；在民族自治地方，根据当事人的要求，可以制作当地通用的民族文字文本。

**Article 32** A notarial certificate shall be produced in the format prescribed by the judicial administration department of the State Council, signed or affixed with a signature seal by the notary, and affixed with the seal of the notarial institution. A notarial certificate shall take effect from the date of issuance.  
A notarial certificate shall be written in the language commonly used throughout the country; in national autonomous areas, a text in the local commonly used ethnic language may be produced at the request of the party.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　公证书需要在国外使用，使用国要求先认证的，应当经中华人民共和国外交部或者外交部授权的机构和有关国家驻中华人民共和国使（领）馆认证。

**Article 33** Where a notarial certificate needs to be used abroad and the country of use requires prior authentication, it shall be authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China or an institution authorized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and by the embassy or consulate of the relevant country in the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　当事人应当按照规定支付公证费。  
对符合法律援助条件的当事人，公证机构应当按照规定减免公证费。

**Article 34** A party shall pay the notarization fee in accordance with regulations.  
For a party that meets the conditions for legal aid, the notarial institution shall reduce or waive the notarization fee in accordance with regulations.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公证机构应当将公证文书分类立卷，归档保存。法律、行政法规规定应当公证的事项等重要的公证档案在公证机构保存期满，应当按照规定移交地方档案馆保管。

**Article 35** A notarial institution shall categorize, file, and keep notarial documents in archives. Important notarial archives, such as those concerning matters that shall be notarized by law or administrative regulations, shall be transferred to the local archives for custody in accordance with regulations after the retention period in the notarial institution expires.

### 第五章　公证效力

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　经公证的民事法律行为、有法律意义的事实和文书，应当作为认定事实的根据，但有相反证据足以推翻该项公证的除外。

**Article 36** Notarized civil juristic acts, facts of legal significance, and documents shall serve as the basis for finding facts, unless there is sufficient evidence to the contrary to overturn the notarization.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　对经公证的以给付为内容并载明债务人愿意接受强制执行承诺的债权文书，债务人不履行或者履行不适当的，债权人可以依法向有管辖权的人民法院申请执行。  
前款规定的债权文书确有错误的，人民法院裁定不予执行，并将裁定书送达双方当事人和公证机构。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于当事人对具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书的内容有争议提起诉讼人民法院是否受理问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于当事人对具有强制执行效力的公证债权文书的内容有争议提起诉讼人民法院是否受理问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/律师、仲裁与公证/最高人民法院关于公证债权文书执行若干问题的规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于公证债权文书执行若干问题的规定》第1条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 37** Where a debtor fails to perform or fails to properly perform a notarized debt instrument with payment as its content and containing a statement of the debtor's willingness to accept compulsory enforcement, the creditor may apply to the competent People's Court for enforcement according to law.  
If the debt instrument provided in the preceding paragraph is indeed erroneous, the People's Court shall rule not to enforce it and shall serve the ruling on both parties and the notarial institution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　法律、行政法规规定未经公证的事项不具有法律效力的，依照其规定。

**Article 38** Where a matter that is not notarized has no legal effect according to the provisions of laws or administrative regulations, such provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人认为公证书有错误的，可以向出具该公证书的公证机构提出复查。公证书的内容违法或者与事实不符的，公证机构应当撤销该公证书并予以公告，该公证书自始无效；公证书有其他错误的，公证机构应当予以更正。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 39** Where a party or an interested person of a notarization matter considers that a notarial certificate is erroneous, he/she may request a review from the notarial institution that issued the notarial certificate. If the content of the notarial certificate is illegal or inconsistent with the facts, the notarial institution shall revoke the notarial certificate and make a public announcement, and the notarial certificate shall be void from the beginning; if the notarial certificate has other errors, the notarial institution shall correct them.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　当事人、公证事项的利害关系人对公证书的内容有争议的，可以就该争议向人民法院提起民事诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定.md#art-3" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定》第3条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 40** Where a party or an interested person of a notarization matter disputes the content of a notarial certificate, he/she may bring a civil action in the People's Court regarding the dispute.

### 第六章　法律责任

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　公证机构及其公证员有下列行为之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市人民政府司法行政部门给予警告；情节严重的，对公证机构处一万元以上五万元以下罚款，对公证员处一千元以上五千元以下罚款，并可以给予三个月以上六个月以下停止执业的处罚；有违法所得的，没收违法所得：  
（一）以诋毁其他公证机构、公证员或者支付回扣、佣金等不正当手段争揽公证业务的；  
（二）违反规定的收费标准收取公证费的；  
（三）同时在二个以上公证机构执业的；  
（四）从事有报酬的其他职业的；  
（五）为本人及近亲属办理公证或者办理与本人及近亲属有利害关系的公证的；  
（六）依照法律、行政法规的规定，应当给予处罚的其他行为。

**Article 41** Where a notarial institution or a notary commits any of the following acts, the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or a city with districts shall give a warning; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the notarial institution, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the notary, and may also impose a penalty of suspension from practice for not less than three months but not more than six months; any illegal gains shall be confiscated:  
(1) soliciting notarization business by unfair means such as defaming other notarial institutions or notaries, or paying rebates or commissions;  
(2) collecting notarization fees in violation of the prescribed fee standards;  
(3) practicing in two or more notarial institutions simultaneously;  
(4) engaging in other remunerative occupations;  
(5) handling notarization for himself/herself or close relatives, or handling notarization in which he/she or close relatives have an interest;  
(6) other acts that shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　公证机构及其公证员有下列行为之一的，由省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市人民政府司法行政部门对公证机构给予警告，并处二万元以上十万元以下罚款，并可以给予一个月以上三个月以下停业整顿的处罚；对公证员给予警告，并处二千元以上一万元以下罚款，并可以给予三个月以上十二个月以下停止执业的处罚；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；情节严重的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府司法行政部门吊销公证员执业证书；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）私自出具公证书的；  
（二）为不真实、不合法的事项出具公证书的；  
（三）侵占、挪用公证费或者侵占、盗窃公证专用物品的；  
（四）毁损、篡改公证文书或者公证档案的；  
（五）泄露在执业活动中知悉的国家秘密、商业秘密或者个人隐私的；  
（六）依照法律、行政法规的规定，应当给予处罚的其他行为。  
因故意犯罪或者职务过失犯罪受刑事处罚的，应当吊销公证员执业证书。  
被吊销公证员执业证书的，不得担任辩护人、诉讼代理人，但系刑事诉讼、民事诉讼、行政诉讼当事人的监护人、近亲属的除外。

**Article 42** Where a notarial institution or a notary commits any of the following acts, the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or a city with districts shall give a warning to the notarial institution and impose a fine of not less than 20,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan, and may also impose a penalty of suspension for rectification for not less than one month but not more than three months; shall give a warning to the notary and impose a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 10,000 yuan, and may also impose a penalty of suspension from practice for not less than three months but not more than twelve months; any illegal gains shall be confiscated; if the circumstances are serious, the judicial administration department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall revoke the notary practice certificate; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) issuing notarial certificates without authorization;  
(2) issuing notarial certificates for untrue or illegal matters;  
(3) embezzling or misappropriating notarization fees, or embezzling or stealing articles specially used for notarization;  
(4) damaging or altering notarial documents or notarial archives;  
(5) disclosing state secrets, trade secrets, or personal privacy known in the course of practice;  
(6) other acts that shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of laws or administrative regulations.  
Where a notary has received criminal punishment for an intentional crime or a crime of negligence in office, his/her notary practice certificate shall be revoked.  
A person whose notary practice certificate has been revoked shall not serve as a defender or litigation agent, except as a guardian or close relative of a party in criminal, civil, or administrative actions.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　公证机构及其公证员因过错给当事人、公证事项的利害关系人造成损失的，由公证机构承担相应的赔偿责任；公证机构赔偿后，可以向有故意或者重大过失的公证员追偿。  
当事人、公证事项的利害关系人与公证机构因赔偿发生争议的，可以向人民法院提起民事诉讼。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理涉及公证活动相关民事案件的若干规定》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 43** Where a notarial institution or a notary causes losses to a party or an interested person of a notarization matter due to fault, the notarial institution shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation; after compensation, the notarial institution may recover from the notary who acted with intent or gross negligence.  
If a dispute over compensation arises between a party or an interested person of a notarization matter and the notarial institution, a civil action may be brought in the People's Court.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　当事人以及其他个人或者组织有下列行为之一，给他人造成损失的，依法承担民事责任；违反治安管理的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）提供虚假证明材料，骗取公证书的；  
（二）利用虚假公证书从事欺诈活动的；  
（三）伪造、变造或者买卖伪造、变造的公证书、公证机构印章的。

**Article 44** Where a party or any other individual or organization commits any of the following acts, causing losses to others, it shall bear civil liability according to law; if it violates public security administration, it shall be given a public security administration penalty according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) providing false evidentiary materials to fraudulently obtain a notarial certificate;  
(2) using a false notarial certificate to engage in fraudulent activities;  
(3) forging, altering, or buying or selling forged or altered notarial certificates or seals of notarial institutions.

### 第七章　附则

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　中华人民共和国驻外使（领）馆可以依照本法的规定或者中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约的规定，办理公证。

**Article 45** The embassies and consulates of the People's Republic of China in foreign countries may handle notarization in accordance with the provisions of this Law or the provisions of international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　公证费的收费标准由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府价格主管部门会同同级司法行政部门制定。

**Article 46** The fee standards for notarization shall be formulated by the competent price department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government jointly with the judicial administration department at the same level.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　本法自2006年3月1日起施行。

**Article 47** This Law shall come into force as of March 1, 2006.
