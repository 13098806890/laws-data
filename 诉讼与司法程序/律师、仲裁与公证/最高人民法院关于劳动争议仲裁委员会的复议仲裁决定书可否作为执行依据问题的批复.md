### 最高人民法院关于劳动争议仲裁委员会的复议仲裁决定书可否作为执行依据问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于劳动争议仲裁委员会的复议仲裁决定书  
  
可否作为执行依据问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕10号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1995）豫法执请字第1号《关于郑劳仲复裁字（1991）第1号复议仲裁决定书能否作为执行依据的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
仲裁一裁终局制度，是指仲裁决定一经作出即发生法律效力，当事人没有提请再次裁决的权利，但这并不排除原仲裁机构发现自己作出的裁决有错误进行重新裁决的情况。劳动争议仲裁委员会发现自己作出的仲裁决定书有错误而进行重新仲裁，符合实事求是的原则，不违背一裁终局制度，不应视为违反法定程序。因此对当事人申请执行劳动争议仲裁委员会复议仲裁决定的，应予立案执行。如被执行人提出申辩称该复议仲裁决定书有其他应不予执行的情形，应按照[民事诉讼法第二百一十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-217)的规定，认真审查，慎重处理。  
  
1996年7月21日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Regarding the Reconsideration Arbitration Decision of a Labor Dispute Arbitration Commission  
Reply on Whether It Can Be Used as the Basis for Enforcement  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 10  
Higher People's Court of Henan Province:  
Your court's Request No. 1 (1995) Yu Fa Zhi Qing Zi [concerning whether the Reconsideration Arbitration Decision No. 1 (1991) Zheng Lao Zhong Fu Cai Zi can be used as the basis for enforcement] has been received. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
The system of final and binding arbitration means that an arbitration decision shall become legally effective once it is made, and the parties have no right to request another adjudication. However, this does not preclude the original arbitration institution from conducting a new arbitration if it discovers errors in its award. Where a labor dispute arbitration commission discovers errors in its arbitration decision and conducts a new arbitration, this conforms to the principle of seeking truth from facts, does not violate the system of final and binding arbitration, and shall not be deemed as violating statutory procedures. Therefore, if a party applies for enforcement of a reconsideration arbitration decision made by a labor dispute arbitration commission, the case shall be accepted and enforced. If the person subject to enforcement argues that there are other circumstances under which the reconsideration arbitration decision should not be enforced, the matter shall be carefully examined and prudently handled in accordance with Article 217 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
July 21, 1996
