# 最高人民法院关于执行《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕23号  
**公布日期**：1998-06-29  
**生效日期**：1998-09-08

> （1998年6月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第 989次会议通过，自1998年9月8日起施行） 为正确理解和适用修正后的刑事诉讼法，现结合人民法院审判工作实际，对执行刑事诉讼法的若干具体问题解释如下： 一、管辖 第一条 人民法院直接受理的自诉案件包括： （一）告诉才处理的案件： 1、侮辱、诽谤案（刑法第二百四十六条规定的，但是严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的除外）； 2、暴力干涉婚姻自由案（刑法第二百五十七条第一款规定的）； 3、虐待案（刑法第二百六十条第一款规定的）； 4、侵占案（刑法第二百七十条规定的）。 （二）人民检察院没有提起公诉，被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件： 1、故意伤害案（刑法第二百三十四条第一款规定的）； 2、非法侵入住宅案（刑法第二百四十五条规定的）； 3、侵犯通信自由案（刑法第二百五十二条规定的）； 4、重婚案（刑法第二百五十八条规定的）； 5、遗弃案（刑法第二百六十一条规定的）；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 人民法院直接受理的自诉案件包括：  
（一）告诉才处理的案件：  
1、侮辱、诽谤案（[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)规定的，但是严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的除外）；  
2、暴力干涉婚姻自由案（[刑法第二百五十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-257)第一款规定的）；  
3、虐待案（[刑法第二百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-260)第一款规定的）；  
4、侵占案（[刑法第二百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-270)规定的）。  
（二）人民检察院没有提起公诉，被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件：  
1、故意伤害案（[刑法第二百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-234)第一款规定的）；  
2、非法侵入住宅案（[刑法第二百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-245)规定的）；  
3、侵犯通信自由案（[刑法第二百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-252)规定的）；  
4、重婚案（[刑法第二百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-258)规定的）；  
5、遗弃案（[刑法第二百六十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-261)规定的）；  
6、生产、销售伪劣商品案（刑法分则第三章第一节规定的，但是严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的除外）；  
7、侵犯知识产权案（刑法分则第三章第七节规定的，但是严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的除外）；  
8、属于刑法分则第四章、第五章规定的，对被告人可能判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚的案件。  
对上列八项案件，被害人直接向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。对于其中证据不足、可由公安机关受理的，或者认为对被告人可能判处三年有期徒刑以上刑罚的，应当移送公安机关立案侦查。  
（三）被害人有证据证明对被告人侵犯自己人身、财产权利的行为应当依法追究刑事责任，而公安机关或者人民检察院已经作出不予追究的书面决定的案件。

**Article 1** Cases of private prosecution directly accepted by the People's Court include:  
(1) Cases to be handled only upon complaint:  
1. Cases of insult or defamation (as provided in Article 246 of the Criminal Law, except where serious harm is caused to social order and national interests);  
2. Cases of violent interference with freedom of marriage (as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 257 of the Criminal Law);  
3. Cases of maltreatment (as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 260 of the Criminal Law);  
4. Cases of embezzlement (as provided in Article 270 of the Criminal Law).  
(2) Minor criminal cases for which the People's Procuratorate has not initiated a public prosecution and for which the victim has evidence to prove:  
1. Cases of intentional injury (as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 234 of the Criminal Law);  
2. Cases of unlawful intrusion into a residence (as provided in Article 245 of the Criminal Law);  
3. Cases of infringement upon freedom of correspondence (as provided in Article 252 of the Criminal Law);  
4. Cases of bigamy (as provided in Article 258 of the Criminal Law);  
5. Cases of abandonment (as provided in Article 261 of the Criminal Law);  
6. Cases of producing and selling fake or substandard commodities (as provided in Section 1 of Chapter III of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, except where serious harm is caused to social order and national interests);  
7. Cases of infringement upon intellectual property rights (as provided in Section 7 of Chapter III of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, except where serious harm is caused to social order and national interests);  
8. Cases which fall under Chapter IV or Chapter V of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law and for which the defendant may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years.  
For the above eight categories of cases, where the victim directly initiates a prosecution with the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law. For cases where the evidence is insufficient and may be accepted by a public security organ, or where it is considered that the defendant may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years, the case shall be transferred to a public security organ for case filing and investigation.  
(3) Cases where the victim has evidence to prove that the act of the defendant infringing upon the victim's personal or property rights shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law, but the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate has already made a written decision not to pursue the matter.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 犯罪地是指犯罪行为发生地。以非法占有为目的的财产犯罪，犯罪地包括犯罪行为发生地和犯罪分子实际取得财产的犯罪结果发生地。

**Article 2** The place of a crime refers to the place where the criminal act occurs. For property crimes committed for the purpose of unlawful possession, the place of the crime includes the place where the criminal act occurs and the place where the criminal result occurs, i.e., the place where the criminal actually obtains the property.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 刑事自诉案件的自诉人、被告人一方或者双方是在港、澳、台居住的中国公民或者其住所地是在港、澳、台的单位的，由犯罪地的基层人民法院审判。  
港、澳、台同胞告诉的，应当出示港、澳、台居民身份证、回乡证或者其他能证明本人身份的证明。

**Article 3** Where one or both parties (the private prosecutor or the defendant) in a criminal case of private prosecution are Chinese citizens residing in Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, or units whose domicile is in Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, the case shall be adjudicated by the Primary People's Court of the place of the crime.  
Where a compatriot from Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan initiates a complaint, he or she shall present the resident identity card, Home Return Certificate, or other documents that can prove his or her identity.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 人民检察院认为可能判处无期徒刑、死刑而向中级人民法院提起公诉的普通刑事案件，中级人民法院受理后，认为不需要判处无期徒刑以上刑罚的，可以依法审理，不再交基层人民法院审理。

**Article 4** Where the People's Procuratorate initiates a public prosecution with an Intermediate People's Court in respect of an ordinary criminal case which it considers may be punishable by life imprisonment or the death penalty, and after accepting the case, the Intermediate People's Court considers that a punishment of not less than life imprisonment is not necessary, the Intermediate People's Court may adjudicate the case according to law and shall not transfer it to a Primary People's Court for adjudication.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 一人犯数罪、共同犯罪和其他需要并案审理的案件，只要其中一人或者一罪属于上级人民法院管辖的，全案由上级人民法院管辖。

**Article 5** Where a person commits multiple crimes, or there is a joint crime, or other cases which need to be tried jointly, if any one of the persons or any one of the crimes falls within the jurisdiction of a higher People's Court, the entire case shall be within the jurisdiction of the higher People's Court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 单位犯罪的刑事案件，由犯罪地的人民法院管辖。如果由被告单位住所地的人民法院管辖更为适宜的，可以由被告单位住所地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 6** Criminal cases of unit crime shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place of the crime. If it is more appropriate for the People's Court of the place where the defendant unit is domiciled to exercise jurisdiction, the case may be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the defendant unit is domiciled.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 对于中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约所规定的罪行，中华人民共和国在所承担条约义务的范围内，行使刑事管辖权。  
前款规定的案件由被告人被抓获地的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 7** For crimes specified in international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, the People's Republic of China shall exercise criminal jurisdiction within the scope of its treaty obligations.  
Cases provided for in the preceding paragraph shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the defendant is arrested.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 在中华人民共和国领域外的中国船舶内的犯罪，由犯罪发生后该船舶最初停泊的中国口岸所在地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 8** Crimes committed on board a Chinese ship outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the ship first docks at a Chinese port after the commission of the crime.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 在中华人民共和国领域外的中国航空器内的犯罪，由犯罪发生后该航空器在中国最初降落地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 9** Crimes committed on board a Chinese aircraft outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the aircraft first lands in China after the commission of the crime.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 在国际列车上的犯罪，按照我国与相关国家签订的有关管辖协定确定管辖。没有协定的，由犯罪发生后该列车最初停靠的中国车站所在地或者目的地的铁路运输法院管辖。

**Article 10** Crimes committed on an international train shall be subject to jurisdiction determined in accordance with the relevant jurisdictional agreements signed between China and the relevant countries. In the absence of an agreement, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the railway transport court of the place where the train first stops at a Chinese station or of the destination after the commission of the crime.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 中国公民在驻外的中国使领馆内的犯罪，由该公民主管单位所在地或者他的原户籍所在地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 11** Crimes committed by a Chinese citizen within a Chinese embassy or consulate stationed abroad shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the citizen's competent unit is located or of the place of his or her original domicile.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 中国公民在中华人民共和国领域外的犯罪，由该公民离境前的居住地或者原户籍所在地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 12** Crimes committed by a Chinese citizen outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the citizen resided before leaving China or of the place of his or her original domicile.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 外国人在中华人民共和国领域外对中华人民共和国国家或者公民犯罪，依照《中华人民共和国刑法》应受处罚的，由该外国人入境地的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 13** Where a foreigner commits a crime against the State or a citizen of the People's Republic of China outside the territory of the People's Republic of China and is subject to punishment under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the foreigner enters China.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 发现正在服刑的罪犯在判决宣告前还有其他犯罪没有受到审判的，由原审人民法院管辖；如果罪犯服刑地或者新发现罪的主要犯罪地的人民法院管辖更为适宜的，可以由服刑地或者新发现罪的主要犯罪地的人民法院管辖。  
正在服刑的罪犯在服刑期间又犯罪的，由服刑地的人民法院管辖。  
正在服刑的罪犯在脱逃期间的犯罪，如果是在犯罪地捕获并发现的，由犯罪地的人民法院管辖；如果是被缉捕押解回监狱后发现的，由罪犯服刑地的人民法院管辖。

**Article 14** Where it is discovered that a criminal serving a sentence has committed other crimes that have not been adjudicated before the judgment was pronounced, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the court that originally adjudicated the case; if it is more appropriate for the People's Court of the place where the criminal serves the sentence or the main place where the newly discovered crime was committed to exercise jurisdiction, the case may be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the criminal serves the sentence or the main place where the newly discovered crime was committed.  
Where a criminal serving a sentence commits another crime during the term of imprisonment, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the criminal serves the sentence.  
Where a criminal serving a sentence commits a crime during the period of escape, if he is captured and the crime is discovered at the place of the crime, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place of the crime; if the crime is discovered after the criminal is arrested and escorted back to prison, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the criminal serves the sentence.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 上级人民法院认为有必要审理下级人民法院管辖的第一审刑事案件，应当向下级人民法院下达改变管辖决定书，并书面通知同级人民检察院。

**Article 15** Where a higher People's Court considers it necessary to adjudicate a criminal case of first instance that is under the jurisdiction of a lower People's Court, it shall issue a written decision on change of jurisdiction to the lower People's Court and notify in writing the People's Procuratorate at the same level.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 基层人民法院对于认为案情重大、复杂或者可能判处无期徒刑、死刑的第一审刑事案件，请求移送中级人民法院审判，应当经合议庭报请院长决定后，在案件审理期限届满十五日以前书面请求移送。中级人民法院应当在接到移送申请十日内作出决定。  
中级人民法院不同意移送的，应当向该基层人民法院下达不同意移送决定书，由该基层人民法院依法审判；同意移送的，应当向该基层人民法院下达同意移送决定书，并书面通知同级人民检察院。基层人民法院接到上级人民法院同意移送决定书后，应当通知同级人民检察院和当事人，并将起诉材料退回同级人民检察院。

**Article 16** Where a Primary People's Court considers that a criminal case of first instance is of major or complex nature or may be punishable by life imprisonment or the death penalty, and requests to transfer the case to an Intermediate People's Court for adjudication, the matter shall be submitted by the collegial panel to the president of the court for a decision, and a written request for transfer shall be made 15 days before the expiry of the trial period. The Intermediate People's Court shall make a decision within 10 days after receiving the transfer application.  
If the Intermediate People's Court does not agree to the transfer, it shall issue a written decision of disapproval of the transfer to the said Primary People's Court, and the said Primary People's Court shall adjudicate the case according to law; if the Intermediate People's Court agrees to the transfer, it shall issue a written decision of approval of the transfer to the said Primary People's Court, and notify in writing the People's Procuratorate at the same level. After receiving the decision of approval of the transfer from the higher People's Court, the Primary People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate at the same level and the parties, and shall return the prosecution materials to the People's Procuratorate at the same level.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 两个以上同级人民法院都有权管辖的案件，由最初受理的人民法院管辖。尚未开庭审判的，在必要的时候，可以移送被告人主要犯罪地的人民法院审判。对管辖权发生争议的，应当在审限内协商解决；协商不成的，由争议的人民法院分别逐级报请共同的上一级人民法院指定管辖。

**Article 17** Where two or more People's Courts at the same level all have jurisdiction over a case, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that first accepted it. Where a trial has not yet commenced, the case may, when necessary, be transferred to the People's Court of the place where the main crime of the defendant was committed for adjudication. Where a dispute over jurisdiction arises, the dispute shall be resolved through negotiation within the trial period; if negotiation fails, the courts in dispute shall, level by level, report to their common higher People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 有管辖权的人民法院因案件涉及本院院长需要回避等原因，不宜行使管辖权的，可以请求上一级人民法院管辖；上一级人民法院也可以指定与提出请求的人民法院同级的其他人民法院管辖。

**Article 18** Where a People's Court with jurisdiction is not appropriate to exercise jurisdiction due to reasons such as the need for the president of that court to withdraw from the case, it may request a higher People's Court to exercise jurisdiction; the higher People's Court may also designate another People's Court at the same level as the requesting court to exercise jurisdiction.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 上级人民法院指定管辖的，应当将指定管辖决定书分别送达被指定管辖的人民法院和其他有关的人民法院。  
原受理案件的人民法院，在收到上级人民法院指定其他人民法院管辖决定书后，不再行使管辖权。对于公诉案件，应当书面通知提起公诉的人民检察院，并将全部案卷材料退回，同时书面通知当事人；对于自诉案件，应当将全部案卷材料移送被指定管辖的人民法院，并书面通知当事人。

**Article 19** Where a higher People's Court designates jurisdiction, it shall serve the written decision on designation of jurisdiction to both the designated People's Court and the other relevant People's Court(s).  
The People's Court that originally accepted the case shall, after receiving the written decision of the higher People's Court designating another People's Court to have jurisdiction, no longer exercise jurisdiction. For a case of public prosecution, it shall notify in writing the People's Procuratorate that initiated the public prosecution, return all case files and materials, and simultaneously notify the parties in writing; for a case of private prosecution, it shall transfer all case files and materials to the designated People's Court and notify the parties in writing.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 现役军人（含军内在编职工，下同）和非军人共同犯罪的，分别由军事法院和地方人民法院或者其他专门法院管辖；涉及国家军事秘密的，全案由军事法院管辖。

**Article 20** Where an active-duty serviceman (including civilian staff on active military service, hereinafter the same) and a non-military person commit a joint crime, the case shall be under the jurisdiction of the military court and the local People's Court or other special courts respectively; if the case involves State military secrets, the entire case shall be under the jurisdiction of the military court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 下列案件由地方人民法院或者军事法院以外的其他专门法院管辖：  
（一）非军人、随军家属在部队营区内犯罪的；  
（二）军人在办理退役手续后犯罪的；  
（三）现役军人入伍前犯罪的（需与服役期内犯罪一并审判的除外）；  
（四）退役军人在服役期内犯罪的（犯军人违反职责罪的除外）。

**Article 21** The following cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the local People's Court or other special courts other than the military court:  
(1) Crimes committed by non-military persons or family members accompanying military personnel within a military camp;  
(2) Crimes committed by a serviceman after going through the procedures for discharge;  
(3) Crimes committed by an active-duty serviceman before enlistment (except those which need to be tried together with crimes committed during active service);  
(4) Crimes committed by a discharged serviceman during active service (except those constituting the crime of violating military duties).

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 上级人民法院在必要的时候，可以将下级人民法院管辖的案件指定其他下级人民法院管辖。  
二、回避

**Article 22** A higher People's Court may, when necessary, designate a case under the jurisdiction of a lower People's Court to be under the jurisdiction of another lower People's Court.  
II. Withdrawal

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 审判委员会委员、合议庭组成人员及独任审判员有[刑事诉讼法第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-28)、第二十九条所列情形之一的，应当自行回避；当事人和他们的法定代理人也有权申请上列人员回避。

**Article 23** Where any member of the Judicial Committee, a member of a collegial panel, or a sole judge falls under any of the circumstances set forth in Article 28 or Article 29 of the Criminal Procedure Law, he or she shall voluntarily withdraw; the parties and their legal representatives also have the right to apply for the withdrawal of the aforesaid persons.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 审判人员自行回避的，可以口头或者书面提出，并说明理由，由院长决定。  
当事人和他们的法定代理人申请审判人员回避的，可以口头或者书面提出，由院长决定，并将决定告知申请人。

**Article 24** Where a judicial officer voluntarily withdraws, he or she may make a verbal or written request, stating the reasons, and the decision shall be made by the president of the court.  
Where a party or his or her legal representative applies for the withdrawal of a judicial officer, the application may be made verbally or in writing, and the decision shall be made by the president of the court, who shall inform the applicant of the decision.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 当事人和他们的法定代理人申请人民法院院长回避或者院长自行回避的，应当由审判委员会讨论决定，并将决定告知申请人。  
审判委员会讨论院长回避问题时，由副院长主持，院长不得参加。

**Article 25** Where a party or his or her legal representative applies for the withdrawal of the president of the People's Court or the president voluntarily withdraws, the matter shall be discussed and decided by the Judicial Committee, and the decision shall be informed to the applicant.  
When the Judicial Committee discusses the withdrawal of the president, the meeting shall be presided over by the vice-president, and the president shall not participate.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 应当回避的人员，本人没有自行回避，当事人和他们的法定代理人也没有申请其回避的，院长或者审判委员会应当决定其回避。

**Article 26** Where a person who should withdraw neither voluntarily withdraws nor is applied for withdrawal by the parties or their legal representatives, the president of the court or the Judicial Committee shall decide on his or her withdrawal.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 依照[刑事诉讼法第二十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-29)规定提出回避申请的，申请人应当提供证明材料。

**Article 27** Where an application for withdrawal is filed in accordance with Article 29 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the applicant shall provide supporting materials.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 被决定回避的人员对决定有异议的，可以在恢复庭审前申请复议一次；被驳回回避申请的当事人及其法定代理人对决定有异议的，可以当庭申请复议一次。

**Article 28** Where a person whose withdrawal has been decided disagrees with the decision, he or she may apply for reconsideration once before the resumption of the court session; where a party or his or her legal representative whose application for withdrawal has been rejected disagrees with the decision, he or she may apply for reconsideration once during the court session.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 不属于[刑事诉讼法第二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-28)、第二十九条所列情形的回避申请，由法庭当庭驳回，并不得申请复议。

**Article 29** An application for withdrawal that does not fall under the circumstances set forth in Article 28 or Article 29 of the Criminal Procedure Law shall be rejected on the spot by the court, and no application for reconsideration shall be allowed.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 当事人及其法定代理人对出庭的检察人员、书记员提出回避申请的，人民法院应当通知指派该检察人员出庭的人民检察院，由该院检察长或者检察委员会决定。

**Article 30** Where a party or his or her legal representative applies for the withdrawal of a procurator or court clerk appearing in court, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate that assigned the procurator to appear in court, and the decision shall be made by the chief procurator or the procuratorial committee of that People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 参加过本案侦查、起诉的侦查、检察人员，如果调至人民法院工作，不得担任本案的审判人员。  
凡在一个审判程序中参与过本案审判工作的合议庭组成人员，不得再参与本案其他程序的审判。

**Article 31** Investigators and procurators who have participated in the investigation or prosecution of the case, if transferred to work in a People's Court, shall not serve as judicial officers in the case.  
Any member of a collegial panel who has participated in the adjudication of the case in one trial procedure shall not participate in the adjudication of the case in any other procedure.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 上述有关回避的规定，适用于法庭书记员、翻译人员和鉴定人。其回避问题由人民法院院长决定。  
三、辩护与代理

**Article 32** The above provisions concerning withdrawal shall apply to court clerks, interpreters, and expert evaluators. The issue of their withdrawal shall be decided by the president of the People's Court.  
III. Defense and Agency

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 人民法院审判案件过程中，应当充分保证被告人行使[刑事诉讼法第三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-32)规定的辩护权利。但下列人员不得被委托担任辩护人：  
（一）被宣告缓刑和刑罚尚未执行完毕的人；  
（二）依法被剥夺、限制人身自由的人；  
（三）无行为能力或者限制行为能力的人；  
（四）人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱的现职人员；  
（五）本院的人民陪审员；  
（六）与本案审理结果有利害关系的人；  
（七）外国人或者无国籍人。  
前款第（四）、（五）、（六）、（七）项规定的人员，如果是被告人的近亲属或者监护人，由被告人委托担任辩护人的，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 33** In the process of adjudicating a case, the People's Court shall fully ensure the defendant's right to defense as provided in Article 32 of the Criminal Procedure Law. However, the following persons shall not be entrusted to serve as defenders:  
(1) Persons who have been sentenced to probation or whose sentence has not been fully executed;  
(2) Persons who have been deprived of or restricted of personal freedom according to law;  
(3) Persons without capacity or with limited capacity for civil conduct;  
(4) Active personnel of the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, a public security organ, a State security organ, or a prison;  
(5) People's assessors of the court in question;  
(6) Persons who have an interest in the outcome of the trial of the case;  
(7) Foreigners or stateless persons.  
Where the persons specified in items (4), (5), (6), and (7) of the preceding paragraph are close relatives or guardians of the defendant and are entrusted by the defendant to serve as defenders, the People's Court may permit it.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 律师、人民团体、被告人所在单位推荐的公民以及被告人的监护人、亲友，被委托为辩护人的，人民法院应当核实其身份证明和辩护委托书。

**Article 34** Where a lawyer, a citizen recommended by a people's organization or the defendant's work unit, or the defendant's guardian or relative or friend is entrusted as a defender, the People's Court shall verify his or her identity certificate and the power of attorney for defense.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 一名被告人委托辩护人不得超过两人。在共同犯罪的案件中，一名辩护人不得为两名以上的同案被告人辩护。

**Article 35** A defendant may entrust no more than two defenders. In a case of joint crime, one defender shall not act as defender for two or more co-defendants in the same case.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 被告人没有委托辩护人而具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应当为其指定辩护人：  
（一）盲、聋、哑人或者限制行为能力的人；  
（二）开庭审理时不满十八周岁的未成年人；  
（三）可能被判处死刑的人。

**Article 36** Where a defendant has not entrusted a defender and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall appoint a defender for him or her:  
(1) The defendant is blind, deaf, mute, or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct;  
(2) The defendant is a minor under the age of 18 at the time of trial;  
(3) The defendant may be sentenced to death.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 被告人没有委托辩护人而具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以为其指定辩护人：  
（一）符合当地政府规定的经济困难标准的；  
（二）本人确无经济来源，其家庭经济状况无法查明的；  
（三）本人确无经济来源，其家属经多次劝说仍不愿为其承担辩护律师费用的；  
（四）共同犯罪案件中，其他被告人已委托辩护人的；  
（五）具有外国国籍的；  
（六）案件有重大社会影响的；  
（七）人民法院认为起诉意见和移送的案件证据材料可能影响正确定罪量刑的。

**Article 37** Where a defendant has not entrusted a defender and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may appoint a defender for him or her:  
(1) The defendant meets the criteria for financial difficulty as prescribed by the local government;  
(2) The defendant indeed has no financial resources, and the financial situation of his or her family cannot be ascertained;  
(3) The defendant indeed has no financial resources, and his or her family members are unwilling to bear the costs of a defense lawyer despite repeated persuasion;  
(4) In a joint crime case, other defendants have already entrusted defenders;  
(5) The defendant has foreign nationality;  
(6) The case has significant social impact;  
(7) The People's Court considers that the prosecution opinion and the transferred case evidence materials may affect the correct conviction and sentencing.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 被告人坚持自己行使辩护权，拒绝人民法院指定的辩护人为其辩护的，人民法院应当准许，并记录在案；被告人具有[本解释第三十六条](#art-36)规定情形之一，拒绝人民法院指定的辩护人为其辩护，有正当理由的，人民法院应当准许，但被告人需另行委托辩护人，或者人民法院应当为其另行指定辩护人。

**Article 38** Where the defendant insists on exercising the right to defense on his or her own and refuses the defender appointed by the People's Court, the People's Court shall permit it and record the matter in the case file; where the defendant falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 36 of this Interpretation and refuses the defender appointed by the People's Court for a legitimate reason, the People's Court shall permit it, but the defendant shall entrust another defender, or the People's Court shall appoint another defender for him or her.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 人民法院指定的辩护人，应当是依法承担法律援助义务的律师。

**Article 39** The defender appointed by the People's Court shall be a lawyer who bears the obligation of legal aid according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 人民法院应当为辩护律师查阅、摘抄、复制本案所指控的犯罪事实的材料提供方便，并保证必要的时间。其他辩护人经人民法院准许，可以查阅、摘抄、复制本案所指控的犯罪事实的材料。但审判委员会和合议庭的讨论记录及有关其他案件的线索材料，辩护律师和其他辩护人不得查阅、摘抄、复制。

**Article 40** The People's Court shall facilitate defense lawyers to consult, extract, and copy the materials concerning the criminal facts charged in the case, and shall guarantee the necessary time. Other defenders may, with the permission of the People's Court, consult, extract, and copy the materials concerning the criminal facts charged in the case. However, defense lawyers and other defenders shall not consult, extract, or copy the discussion records of the Judicial Committee and the collegial panel, or the materials concerning leads of other cases.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 辩护律师可以同在押的被告人会见和通信。其他辩护人经人民法院准许，也可以同在押的被告人会见和通信。

**Article 41** A defense lawyer may meet and correspond with the defendant in custody. Other defenders may, with the permission of the People's Court, also meet and correspond with the defendant in custody.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 人民法院受理自诉案件后三日内，应当告知被告人有权委托辩护人；同时应当告知自诉人及其法定代理人、附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人有权委托诉讼代理人。

**Article 42** Within three days after accepting a case of private prosecution, the People's Court shall inform the defendant that he or she has the right to entrust a defender; at the same time, it shall inform the private prosecutor and his or her legal representative, as well as the parties to the incidental civil action and their legal representatives, that they have the right to entrust a litigation agent.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条 辩护律师申请向被害人及其近亲属、被害人提供的证人收集与本案有关的材料，人民法院认为确有必要的，应当准许，并签发准许调查书。

**Article 43** Where a defense lawyer applies to collect materials relevant to the case from the victim, the victim's close relatives, or witnesses provided by the victim, and the People's Court considers it truly necessary, it shall grant permission and issue a permission for investigation.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条 辩护律师向证人或者其他有关单位和个人收集、调取与本案有关的材料，因证人、有关单位和个人不同意，申请人民法院收集、调取，人民法院认为有必要的，应当同意。

**Article 44** Where a defense lawyer, having been unable to collect or obtain materials relevant to the case from a witness or other relevant unit or individual due to the witness's or the relevant unit's or individual's refusal, applies to the People's Court for collection or obtaining, and the People's Court considers it necessary, it shall consent.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条 辩护律师直接申请人民法院收集、调取证据，人民法院认为辩护律师不宜或者不能向证人或者其他有关单位和个人收集、调取，并确有必要的，应当同意。  
人民法院根据辩护律师的申请收集、调取证据时，申请人可以在场。  
人民法院根据辩护律师的申请收集、调取的证据，应当及时复制移送申请人。

**Article 45** Where a defense lawyer directly applies to the People's Court for collection or obtaining of evidence, and the People's Court considers that it is not appropriate or impossible for the defense lawyer to collect or obtain such evidence from a witness or other relevant unit or individual, and that it is truly necessary, the People's Court shall consent.  
When the People's Court collects or obtains evidence upon the application of a defense lawyer, the applicant may be present.  
The evidence collected or obtained by the People's Court upon the application of a defense lawyer shall be promptly copied and transferred to the applicant.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条 辩护律师根据[本解释第四十三条](#art-43)、第四十四条、第四十五条第一款规定提出的申请，应当以书面形式提出，并说明申请的理由，列出需要调查问题的提纲。

**Article 46** An application filed by a defense lawyer in accordance with Article 43, Article 44, or paragraph 1 of Article 45 of this Interpretation shall be made in writing, stating the reasons for the application and providing an outline of the issues to be investigated.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条 当事人委托诉讼代理人应当参照[刑事诉讼法第三十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-32)和[本解释第三十三条](#art-33)的规定执行。

**Article 47** The entrustment of a litigation agent by a party shall be executed with reference to the provisions of Article 32 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 33 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条 诉讼代理人的责任是根据事实和法律，维护被害人、自诉人或者附带民事诉讼当事人的合法权益。

**Article 48** The duty of a litigation agent is to safeguard the lawful rights and interests of the victim, the private prosecutor, or the parties to an incidental civil action based on facts and the law.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条 律师担任诉讼代理人，可以查阅、摘抄、复制与本案有关的材料，了解案情。其他诉讼代理人经人民法院准许，也可以查阅、摘抄、复制本案有关材料，了解案情。需要收集、调取与本案有关的材料的，可以参照[本解释第四十四条](#art-44)、第四十五条的规定执行。

**Article 49** Where a lawyer acts as a litigation agent, he or she may consult, extract, and copy materials relevant to the case and learn about the case. Other litigation agents may, with the permission of the People's Court, also consult, extract, and copy materials relevant to the case and learn about the case. If it is necessary to collect or obtain materials relevant to the case, the provisions of Articles 44 and 45 of this Interpretation may be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条 诉讼代理人应当向人民法院提交由被代理人签名或者盖章的委托书；如果被代理人是附带民事诉讼当事人的，诉讼代理人应当向人民法院提交由被代理人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。

**Article 50** A litigation agent shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal; if the principal is a party to an incidental civil action, the litigation agent shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal.

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条 人民法院对律师、其他辩护人和诉讼代理人查阅、摘抄、复制本案所指控的犯罪事实的材料，只收取复制材料所必需的工本费用。  
四、证据

**Article 51** With respect to the consultation, extraction, and copying of the materials concerning the criminal facts charged in the case by lawyers, other defenders, and litigation agents, the People's Court shall only charge the necessary production costs for copying the materials.  
IV. Evidence

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条 需要运用证据证明的案件事实包括：  
（一）被告人的身份；  
（二）被指控的犯罪行为是否存在；  
（三）被指控的行为是否为被告人所实施；  
（四）被告人有无罪过，行为的动机、目的；  
（五）实施行为的时间、地点、手段、后果以及其他情节；  
（六）被告人的责任以及与其他同案人的关系；  
（七）被告人的行为是否构成犯罪，有无法定或者酌定从重、从轻、减轻处罚以及免除处罚的情节；  
（八）其他与定罪量刑有关的事实。

**Article 52** Facts of a case that need to be proved by evidence include:  
(1) The identity of the defendant;  
(2) Whether the alleged criminal act exists;  
(3) Whether the alleged act was committed by the defendant;  
(4) Whether the defendant had culpability, and the motive and purpose of the act;  
(5) The time, place, means, consequences, and other circumstances of the act;  
(6) The liability of the defendant and his or her relationship with other co-defendants;  
(7) Whether the defendant's act constitutes a crime, and whether there are legally prescribed or discretionary circumstances for heavier, lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment;  
(8) Other facts relevant to conviction and sentencing.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条 收集、调取的书证应当是原件。只有在取得原件确有困难时，才可以是副本或者复制件。  
收集、调取的物证应当是原物。只有在原物不便搬运、不易保存或者依法应当返还被害人时，才可以拍摄足以反映原物外形或者内容的照片、录像。  
书证的副本、复制件，物证的照片、录像，只有经与原件、原物核实无误或者经鉴定证明真实的，才具有与原件、原物同等的证明力。  
制作书证的副本、复制件，拍摄物证的照片、录像以及对有关证据录音时，制作人不得少于二人。提供证据的副本、复制件及照片、音像制品应当附有关于制作过程的文字说明及原件、原物存放何处的说明，并由制作人签名或者盖章。

**Article 53** Documentary evidence collected or obtained shall be the original. Only when it is genuinely difficult to obtain the original may a duplicate or copy be used.  
Physical evidence collected or obtained shall be the original. Only when the original is inconvenient to transport, difficult to preserve, or shall be returned to the victim according to law may a photo or video recording that sufficiently reflects the appearance or content of the original be taken.  
Duplicates or copies of documentary evidence, and photos or video recordings of physical evidence, shall have the same probative force as the originals only after being verified as correct against the originals or proved to be authentic by expert evaluation.  
When making duplicates or copies of documentary evidence, taking photos or video recordings of physical evidence, or making audio recordings of relevant evidence, there shall be no fewer than two persons making them. Duplicates, copies, photos, and audio-visual products provided as evidence shall be accompanied by written explanations of the production process and of where the originals are kept, and shall be signed or sealed by the makers.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条 人民法院依法向有关单位和个人收集、调取、调查、核实证据，认为必要时，可以通知检察人员、辩护人到场。  
人民法院向有关单位收集、调取的书面证据材料，必须由提供人署名，并加盖单位印章；人民法院向个人收集、调取的书面证据材料，必须由本人确认无误后签名或者盖章。

**Article 54** When the People's Court, according to law, collects, obtains, investigates, or verifies evidence from relevant units or individuals, if it considers it necessary, it may notify a procurator or a defender to be present.  
Written evidentiary materials collected or obtained by the People's Court from a relevant unit shall be signed by the provider and affixed with the seal of the unit; written evidentiary materials collected or obtained by the People's Court from an individual shall be signed or sealed by the individual after confirming their correctness.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条 人民法院对公诉案件依法调查、核实证据时，发现对认定案件事实有重要作用的新的证据材料，应当告知检察人员和辩护人。必要时，也可以直接提取，复制后移送检察人员和辩护人。

**Article 55** When the People's Court investigates or verifies evidence in a case of public prosecution according to law, if it discovers new evidentiary materials that are important for determining the facts of the case, it shall inform the procurator and the defender. When necessary, it may directly obtain such materials and, after copying, transfer them to the procurator and the defender.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条 人民法院对有关单位和个人提供的证据，应当出具收据，注明证据的名称、收到的时间、件数、页数以及是否原件等，由书记员或者审判员签名。

**Article 56** When the People's Court receives evidence provided by relevant units or individuals, it shall issue a receipt, specifying the name of the evidence, the time of receipt, the number of items, the number of pages, and whether the evidence is the original, and the receipt shall be signed by the court clerk or the judge.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条 对于证人能否辨别是非，能否正确表达，必要时可以进行审查或者鉴定。

**Article 57** Where necessary, examination or expert evaluation may be conducted to determine whether a witness can distinguish right from wrong and can correctly express himself.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条 证据必须经过当庭出示、辨认、质证等法庭调查程序查证属实，否则不能作为定案的根据。  
对于出庭作证的证人，必须在法庭上经过公诉人、被害人和被告人、辩护人等双方询问、质证，其证言经过审查确实的，才能作为定案的根据；未出庭证人的证言宣读后经当庭查证属实的，可以作为定案的根据。  
法庭查明证人有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证时，应当依法处理。

**Article 58** Evidence shall be verified through court investigation procedures such as presentation in court, identification, and cross-examination before it can be used as the basis for adjudication; otherwise, it shall not be used as the basis for adjudication.  
For a witness who testifies in court, the witness's testimony may be used as the basis for adjudication only after it has been examined and verified through questioning and cross-examination by both sides, including the public prosecutor, the victim, the defendant, and the defender; if the testimony of a witness who does not appear in court is read out in court and verified in court, it may be used as the basis for adjudication.  
Where the court finds that a witness intentionally gives false testimony or conceals criminal evidence, the court shall handle the matter according to law.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条 对鉴定结论有疑问的，人民法院可以指派或者聘请有专门知识的人或者鉴定机构，对案件中的某些专门性问题进行补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。

**Article 59** Where there is doubt about an expert conclusion, the People's Court may assign or engage a person with specialized knowledge or an expert evaluation institution to conduct a supplementary expert evaluation or a new expert evaluation on certain specialized issues in the case.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条 人民法院在开庭审理时，对省级人民政府指定的医院作出的鉴定结论，经质证后，认为有疑问，不能作为定案根据的，可以另行聘请省级人民政府指定的其他医院进行补充鉴定或者重新鉴定。

**Article 60** Where, during court hearing, the People's Court has doubts about an expert conclusion made by a hospital designated by the provincial people's government, and after cross-examination it cannot be used as the basis for adjudication, the court may separately engage another hospital designated by the provincial people's government to conduct a supplementary expert evaluation or a new expert evaluation.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条 严禁以非法的方法收集证据。凡经查证确实属于采用刑讯逼供或者威胁、引诱、欺编等非法的方法取得的证人证言、被害人陈述、被告人供述，不能作为定案的根据。

**Article 61** It is strictly prohibited to collect evidence by illegal means. Any witness testimony, victim statement, or defendant confession that has been verified as obtained through illegal means such as extortion of confessions by torture, threats, enticement, or deception shall not be used as the basis for adjudication.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条 在公开审理案件时，对于公诉人、诉讼参与人提出涉及国家秘密或者个人隐私的证据时，审判长应当制止。如确与本案有关的，应当决定案件转为不公开审理。  
五、强制措施

**Article 62** When hearing a case in public, if the public prosecutor or a participant in the proceedings presents evidence involving state secrets or personal privacy, the presiding judge shall stop them. If the evidence is indeed related to the case, the court shall decide to conduct the hearing in camera.  
V. Compulsory Measures

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条 人民法院在审判过程中，根据案件情况，可以对被告人拘传、取保候审、监视居住或者决定逮捕。  
合议庭或者独任审判员认为应当对被告人撤销或者变更强制措施的，应当报请院长批准。

**Article 63** In the course of adjudication, the People's Court may, according to the circumstances of the case, summon the defendant for interrogation, release him on bail pending investigation or trial, place him under residential surveillance, or decide to arrest him.  
Where the collegial panel or the sole judge considers that the compulsory measures against the defendant shall be revoked or modified, it shall submit a report to the president of the court for approval.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条 对经过依法传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭，或者根据案件情况有必要拘传的被告人，可以拘传。  
拘传由司法警察执行，执行人员不得少于二人。  
拘传被告人时，应当出示拘传票。对抗拒拘传的，可以使用戒具。

**Article 64** A defendant who, after being summoned according to law, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or where it is necessary to summon him for interrogation based on the circumstances of the case, may be summoned for interrogation.  
The summon for interrogation shall be executed by judicial police, and the number of executing personnel shall not be less than two.  
When summoning a defendant for interrogation, a summons for interrogation shall be presented. If the defendant resists the summon, restraint devices may be used.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条 审判人员对被拘传的人，应当在拘传后的十二小时以内讯问完毕，不得以连续拘传的形式变相关押被拘传人。

**Article 65** The judicial personnel shall complete the interrogation of the person summoned for interrogation within twelve hours after the summon, and shall not indirectly detain the summoned person by means of continuous summons.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条 被告人具有下列情形之一的，人民法院可以决定取保候审或者监视居住：  
（一）可能判处管制、拘役或者独立适用附加刑的；  
（二）可能判处有期徒刑以上刑罚，采取取保候审、监视居住不致发生社会危险的；  
（三）应当逮捕但患有严重疾病的，或者是正在怀孕、哺乳自己婴儿的妇女。

**Article 66** In any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may decide to release the defendant on bail pending investigation or trial or place him under residential surveillance:  
(1) The defendant may be sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or independently applicable supplementary punishment;  
(2) The defendant may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty, but the adoption of release on bail or residential surveillance would not create any social danger;  
(3) The defendant should be arrested but suffers from a serious disease, or is a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant.

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条 人民法院对被告人决定取保候审、监视居住，应当向其本人宣布，并由被告人在取保候审决定书或者监视居住决定书上签名。

**Article 67** When the People's Court decides to release a defendant on bail pending investigation or trial or place him under residential surveillance, it shall announce the decision to the defendant himself, and the defendant shall sign the decision on release on bail or the decision on residential surveillance.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条 被羁押的被告人及其法定代理人、近亲属和律师有权申请取保候审。申请取保候审应当采用书面形式。人民法院应当在接到书面申请后七日内作出是否同意的答复。对符合取保候审条件并且提出了保证人或者能够交纳保证金的，人民法院应当同意，并依法办理取保候审手续；对不符合取保候审条件，不同意取保候审的，  
应当告知申请人，并说明不同意的理由。

**Article 68** The detained defendant, his legal representative, near relatives, and lawyer shall have the right to apply for release on bail. The application for release on bail shall be made in writing. The People's Court shall, within seven days after receiving the written application, respond whether it approves. If the defendant meets the conditions for release on bail and has provided a guarantor or is able to pay a deposit, the People's Court shall approve and handle the release on bail procedures according to law; if the defendant does not meet the conditions for release on bail and the court does not approve, it shall inform the applicant and state the reasons for disapproval.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条 对符合取保候审条件，具有下列情形之一的被告人，人民法院决定取保候审时，可以责令其提供一至二名保证人：  
（一）无力交纳保证金的；  
（二）未成年人或者具有其他不宜收取保证金情形的。

**Article 69** Where a defendant meets the conditions for release on bail and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may, when deciding to release him on bail, order him to provide one or two guarantors:  
(1) The defendant is unable to pay a deposit;  
(2) The defendant is a minor or there are other circumstances where it is inappropriate to collect a deposit.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条 人民法院应当依法严格审查保证人是否符合法定条件。符合保证人条件的，应当告知他必须履行的义务，并由他出具保证书。

**Article 70** The People's Court shall strictly examine according to law whether the guarantor meets the statutory conditions. If the guarantor meets the conditions, the court shall inform him of the obligations he must perform, and he shall issue a letter of guarantee.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条 人民法院决定对被告人取保候审，根据案件情况，可以责令其交纳保证金。保证金仅限于现金。  
人民法院应当根据起诉指控犯罪的性质、情节、被告人的经济状况等因素，决定应当收取的保证金数额。  
保证金应当依照有关规定交由公安机关收取和保管。

**Article 71** When the People's Court decides to release a defendant on bail pending investigation or trial, it may, according to the circumstances of the case, order him to pay a deposit. The deposit shall be limited to cash.  
The People's Court shall decide the amount of the deposit to be collected based on factors such as the nature and circumstances of the crime charged in the indictment, and the economic condition of the defendant.  
The deposit shall be collected and kept by the public security organ in accordance with relevant regulations.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条 对同一被告人决定取保候审的，不能同时使用保证人保证与保证金保证。

**Article 72** Where release on bail is decided for the same defendant, guarantor guarantee and deposit guarantee shall not be used simultaneously.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条 根据案件事实，认为已经构成犯罪的被告人在取保候审期间逃匿的，如果保证人与该被告人串通，协助其逃匿以及明知藏匿地点而拒绝向司法机关提供的，对保证人应当依照刑法有关规定追究刑事责任。  
具有前款规定情形的，如果取保候审的被告人同时也是附带民事诉讼的被告人，保证人还应当承担连带赔偿责任，但应当以其保证前附带民事诉讼原告人提起的诉讼请求数额为限。

**Article 73** Where a defendant who is believed to have committed a crime based on the facts of the case absconds while on bail, if the guarantor colludes with the defendant to assist him in absconding or, knowing the whereabouts of the defendant, refuses to provide such information to the judicial organ, the guarantor shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
In the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph, if the defendant released on bail is also the defendant in a supplementary civil action, the guarantor shall also bear joint and several compensatory liability, but limited to the amount of the claim brought by the plaintiff in the supplementary civil action before the guarantee.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条 被取保候审人违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)规定，被依法没收保证金后，人民法院仍决定对其取保候审的，取保候审的期限应当连续计算。

**Article 74** Where a person released on bail violates the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and his deposit is confiscated according to law, if the People's Court still decides to release him on bail, the period of release on bail shall be calculated consecutively.

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条 人民检察院、公安机关已对犯罪嫌疑人取保候审、监视居住，案件起诉到人民法院后，人民法院对于符合取保候审、监视居住条件的，应当依法对被告人重新办理取保候审、监视居住手续。取保候审、监视居住的期限重新计算。  
人民法院不得对同一被告人重复采取取保候审、监视居住措施。

**Article 75** Where the People's Procuratorate or the public security organ has released a criminal suspect on bail or placed him under residential surveillance, and after the case is prosecuted to the People's Court, the People's Court shall, if the conditions for release on bail or residential surveillance are met, handle new procedures for release on bail or residential surveillance for the defendant according to law. The period of release on bail or residential surveillance shall be recalculated.  
The People's Court shall not apply release on bail or residential surveillance measures to the same defendant repeatedly.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条 人民法院决定对被告人取保候审、监视居住，应当在宣布后立即将取保候审决定书、取保候审执行通知书或者监视居住决定书、监视居住执行通知书，送达负责执行的公安机关。

**Article 76** When the People's Court decides to release a defendant on bail or place him under residential surveillance, it shall, immediately after the announcement, serve the decision on release on bail, the notice for execution of release on bail, or the decision on residential surveillance, and the notice for execution of residential surveillance to the public security organ responsible for execution.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条 人民法院对有证据证明有犯罪事实存在，可能判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的被告人，认为采取取保候审、监视居住等措施，尚不足以防止发生社会危险而有逮捕必要的，应即决定依法逮捕。

**Article 77** Where there is evidence to prove the existence of criminal facts, and the defendant may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty, and the People's Court considers that measures such as release on bail or residential surveillance are insufficient to prevent social danger and arrest is necessary, it shall promptly decide to arrest according to law.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条 人民法院作出逮捕决定后，应当将逮捕决定书送交公安机关执行。将被告人逮捕后，人民法院应当将逮捕的原因和羁押的处所，在二十四小时内通知被逮捕人的家属或者其所在单位；确实无法通知的，应当将原因记录在卷。

**Article 78** After making a decision to arrest, the People's Court shall deliver the arrest decision to the public security organ for execution. After the defendant is arrested, the People's Court shall, within twenty-four hours, notify the family members or the unit of the arrested person of the reasons for the arrest and the place of custody; if it is truly impossible to notify, the reasons shall be recorded in the files.

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条 对人民法院决定逮捕的被告人，审判人员必须在逮捕后的二十四小时内进行讯问。如果发现不应当逮捕的，应当报经院长批准后，变更强制措施或者立即释放。立即释放的，应当发给释放证明。

**Article 79** For a defendant whose arrest is decided by the People's Court, the judicial personnel must interrogate him within twenty-four hours after the arrest. If it is found that the arrest should not have been made, the compulsory measures shall be modified or the defendant shall be released immediately after reporting to the president of the court for approval. If released immediately, a release certificate shall be issued.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条 对已经逮捕的被告人，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院可以变更强制措施：  
（一）患有严重疾病的；  
（二）案件不能在法律规定的期限内审结的；  
（三）正在怀孕、哺乳自己婴儿的妇女。

**Article 80** Where a defendant who has been arrested falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may modify the compulsory measures:  
(1) The defendant suffers from a serious disease;  
(2) The case cannot be concluded within the time limit prescribed by law;  
(3) The defendant is a pregnant woman or a woman nursing her own infant.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条 对已经逮捕的被告人，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院应当变更强制措施或者释放：  
（一）第一审人民法院判处管制或者宣告缓刑以及单独适用附加刑，判决尚未发生法律效力的；  
（二）第二审人民法院审理期间，被告人被羁押的时间已到第一审人民法院对其判处的刑期期限的；  
（三）因进行司法鉴定而尚未审结的案件，法律规定的期限届满的。

**Article 81** Where a defendant who has been arrested falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall modify the compulsory measures or release the defendant:  
(1) The court of first instance sentences the defendant to public surveillance or probation, or solely imposes a supplementary punishment, and the judgment has not yet become legally effective;  
(2) During the trial by the court of second instance, the period of custody of the defendant has reached the term of the sentence imposed by the court of first instance;  
(3) In a case that has not been concluded due to judicial expert evaluation, the time limit prescribed by law has expired.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条 对具有下列情形之一的被告人，应当变更强制措施，决定逮捕：  
（一）已取保候审或者监视居住的被告人，违反[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)、第五十七条的规定，不逮捕可能发生社会危险的；  
（二）具有[本解释第六十六条](#art-66)第（三）项规定的情形而未予逮捕的被告人，疾病痊愈或者哺乳期已满的。  
决定变更强制措施，予以逮捕的，应当通知负责执行取保候审或者监视居住的公安机关。

**Article 82** For a defendant falling under any of the following circumstances, compulsory measures shall be modified and arrest shall be decided:  
(1) A defendant who has been released on bail or placed under residential surveillance violates the provisions of Articles 56 and 57 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and failure to arrest may create social danger;  
(2) A defendant who falls under the circumstances specified in subparagraph (3) of Article 66 of this Interpretation but has not been arrested, and has recovered from the disease or the nursing period has ended.  
Where a decision is made to modify compulsory measures and arrest the defendant, the public security organ responsible for executing release on bail or residential surveillance shall be notified.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条 对被羁押的被告人需要变更强制措施或者释放的，应当将变更强制措施决定书或者释放通知书送交公安机关执行。  
六、附带民事诉讼

**Article 83** Where the compulsory measures for a detained defendant need to be modified or the defendant needs to be released, the decision on modification of compulsory measures or the notice of release shall be delivered to the public security organ for execution.

<a id="art-84"></a>第八十四条 人民法院受理刑事案件后，可以告知因犯罪行为遭受物质损失的被害人（公民、法人和其他组织）、已死亡被害人的近亲属、无行为能力或者限制行为能力被害人的法定代理人，有权提起附带民事诉讼。  
有权提起附带民事诉讼的人放弃诉讼权利的，应当准许，并记录在案。

**Article 84** After accepting a criminal case, a People's Court may inform the victim (citizen, legal person or other organization) who has suffered material losses due to the criminal act, the close relatives of a deceased victim, or the legal representative of a victim without capacity or with limited capacity, that they have the right to initiate a collateral civil action.  
Where a person entitled to initiate a collateral civil action waives the right to sue, such waiver shall be permitted and recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-85"></a>第八十五条 如果是国家财产、集体财产遭受损失，受损失的单位未提起附带民事诉讼，人民检察院在提起公诉时提起附带民事诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 85** Where state property or collective property suffers losses and the injured entity has not initiated a collateral civil action, if the People's Procuratorate initiates a collateral civil action at the time of initiating a public prosecution, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-86"></a>第八十六条 附带民事诉讼中依法负有赔偿责任的人包括：  
（一）刑事被告人（公民、法人和其他组织）及没有被追究刑事责任的其他共同致害人；  
（二）未成年刑事被告人的监护人；  
（三）已被执行死刑的罪犯的遗产继承人；  
（四）共同犯罪案件中，案件审结前已死亡的被告人的遗产继承人；  
（五）其他对刑事被告人的犯罪行为依法应当承担民事赔偿责任的单位和个人。

**Article 86** Persons who shall bear compensation liability according to law in a collateral civil action include:  
(1) the criminal defendant (citizen, legal person or other organization) and other joint tortfeasors who have not been investigated for criminal liability;  
(2) the guardian of a minor criminal defendant;  
(3) the heir of an executed criminal's estate;  
(4) in a joint crime case, the heir of the estate of a defendant who has died before the conclusion of the case;  
(5) other units and individuals who shall bear civil compensation liability according to law for the criminal acts of the criminal defendant.

<a id="art-87"></a>第八十七条 附带民事诉讼的成年被告人，应当承担赔偿责任的，如果其亲属自愿代为承担，应当准许。

**Article 87** Where an adult defendant in a collateral civil action shall bear compensation liability, if his or her relatives voluntarily assume such liability on his or her behalf, such assumption shall be permitted.

<a id="art-88"></a>第八十八条 附带民事诉讼的起诉条件是：  
（一）提起附带民事诉讼的原告人、法定代理人符合法定条件；  
（二）有明确的被告人；  
（三）有请求赔偿的具体要求和事实根据；  
（四）被害人的物质损失是由被告人的犯罪行为造成的；  
（五）属于人民法院受理附带民事诉讼的范围。

**Article 88** The conditions for initiating a collateral civil action are:  
(1) the plaintiff or legal representative who initiates the collateral civil action meets the statutory conditions;  
(2) there is a definite defendant;  
(3) there are specific claims for compensation and factual grounds;  
(4) the material losses of the victim are caused by the criminal acts of the defendant;  
(5) the case falls within the scope of collateral civil actions accepted by the People's Court.

<a id="art-89"></a>第八十九条 附带民事诉讼应当在刑事案件立案以后第一审判决宣告以前提起。有权提起附带民事诉讼的人在第一审判决宣告以前没有提起的，不得再提起附带民事诉讼。但可以在刑事判决生效后另行提起民事诉讼。

**Article 89** A collateral civil action shall be initiated after the criminal case is placed on file but before the pronouncement of the first-instance judgment. If a person entitled to initiate a collateral civil action fails to do so before the pronouncement of the first-instance judgment, such person shall not initiate another collateral civil action. However, a separate civil action may be initiated after the criminal judgment takes effect.

<a id="art-90"></a>第九十条 在侦查、预审、审查起诉阶段，有权提起附带民事诉讼的人向公安机关、人民检察院提出赔偿要求，已经公安机关、人民检察院记录在案的，刑事案件起诉后，人民法院应当按附带民事诉讼案件受理；经公安机关、人民检察院调解，当事人双方达成协议并已给付，被害人又坚持向法院提起附带民事诉讼的，人民法院也可以受理。

**Article 90** Where, during the stages of investigation, preliminary examination, or review of prosecution, a person entitled to initiate a collateral civil action makes a claim for compensation to a public security organ or the People's Procuratorate, and the same has been recorded by the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate, after the criminal case is initiated, the People's Court shall accept such claim as a collateral civil action. Where, after mediation by the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate, the parties have reached an agreement and performed it, but the victim insists on initiating a collateral civil action in court, the People's Court may also accept the case.

<a id="art-91"></a>第九十一条 提起附带民事诉讼一般应当提交附带民事诉状。书写诉状确有困难的，可以口头起诉。审判人员应当对原告人的口头诉讼请求详细询问，并制作笔录，向原告人宣读；原告人确认无误后，应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 91** To initiate a collateral civil action, a written complaint shall generally be submitted. If it is truly difficult to write the complaint, an oral complaint may be made. The judicial personnel shall conduct a detailed inquiry into the plaintiff's oral claims, make a record, and read it to the plaintiff; after the plaintiff confirms that it is correct, the plaintiff shall sign or affix his or her seal.

<a id="art-92"></a>第九十二条 人民法院收到附带民事诉状后，应当进行审查，并在七日内决定是否立案。符合[刑事诉讼法第七十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-77)第一、二款以及[本解释第八十八条](#art-88)规定的，应当受理；不符合规定的，应当裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 92** After receiving a collateral civil complaint, the People's Court shall examine it and decide within seven days whether to place the case on file. If it meets the provisions of Article 77, paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 88 of this Interpretation, it shall be accepted; if it does not meet the requirements, the court shall rule to dismiss the complaint.

<a id="art-93"></a>第九十三条 人民法院受理附带民事诉讼后，应当在五日内向附带民事诉讼的被告人送达附带民事起诉状副本，或者将口头起诉的内容及时通知附带民事诉讼的被告人，并制作笔录。被告人是未成年人的，应当将附带民事起诉状副本送达其法定代理人，或者将口头起诉的内容通知其法定代理人。  
人民法院送达附带民事起诉状副本时，应当根据刑事案件审理的期限，确定被告人或者其法定代理人提交民事答辩状的时间。

**Article 93** After accepting a collateral civil action, the People's Court shall, within five days, serve a copy of the collateral civil complaint on the defendant in the collateral civil action, or notify the defendant in the collateral civil action of the contents of the oral complaint in a timely manner, and make a record. If the defendant is a minor, a copy of the collateral civil complaint shall be served on his or her legal representative, or the contents of the oral complaint shall be notified to his or her legal representative.  
When serving a copy of the collateral civil complaint, the People's Court shall, based on the time limit for trial of the criminal case, determine the time for the defendant or his or her legal representative to submit a civil answer.

<a id="art-94"></a>第九十四条 附带民事诉讼案件的当事人对自己提出的主张，有责任提供证据。

**Article 94** The parties in a collateral civil action case shall have the burden of providing evidence for their own claims.

<a id="art-95"></a>第九十五条 人民法院审理附带民事诉讼案件，在必要时，可以决定查封或者扣押被告人财产。

**Article 95** When hearing a collateral civil action case, the People's Court may, when necessary, decide to seal up or seize the property of the defendant.

<a id="art-96"></a>第九十六条 审理附带民事诉讼案件，除人民检察院提起的以外，可以调解。调解应当在自愿合法的基础上进行。经调解达成协议的，审判人员应当及时制作调解书。调解书经双方当事人签收后即发生法律效力。  
调解达成协议并当庭执行完毕的，可以不制作调解书，但应当记入笔录，经双方当事人、审判人员、书记员签名或者盖章即发生法律效力。

**Article 96** When hearing a collateral civil action case, except for those initiated by the People's Procuratorate, mediation may be conducted. Mediation shall be conducted on a voluntary and lawful basis. Where an agreement is reached through mediation, the judicial personnel shall promptly produce a mediation document. The mediation document shall take legal effect after being signed and received by both parties.  
Where an agreement is reached through mediation and executed in court, the mediation document may not be produced, but the matter shall be recorded in the record, and it shall take legal effect after being signed or sealed by both parties, the judicial personnel, and the court clerk.

<a id="art-97"></a>第九十七条 经调解无法达成协议或者调解书签收前当事人反悔的，附带民事诉讼应当同刑事诉讼一并判决。

**Article 97** Where no agreement can be reached through mediation or a party retracts before signing and receiving the mediation document, the collateral civil action shall be adjudicated together with the criminal action.

<a id="art-98"></a>第九十八条 附带民事诉讼的原告人经人民法院传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭，或者未经法庭许可中途退庭的，应当按自行撤诉处理。

**Article 98** Where the plaintiff in a collateral civil action, after being summoned by a court summons, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or withdraws from the court during the trial without the court's permission, the case shall be treated as having been voluntarily withdrawn.

<a id="art-99"></a>第九十九条 对于被害人遭受的物质损失或者被告人的赔偿能力一时难以确定，以及附带民事诉讼当事人因故不能到庭等案件，为了防止刑事案件审判的过分迟延，附带民事诉讼可以在刑事案件审判后，由同一审判组织继续审理。如果同一审判组织的成员确实无法继续参加审判的，可以更换审判组织成员。

**Article 99** Where the material losses suffered by the victim or the defendant's ability to compensate are difficult to determine for the time being, or the parties to the collateral civil action cannot appear in court for some reason, in order to prevent excessive delay in the trial of the criminal case, the collateral civil action may be continued by the same adjudication organization after the trial of the criminal case. If a member of the same adjudication organization is truly unable to continue participating in the trial, the members of the adjudication organization may be replaced.

<a id="art-100"></a>第一百条 人民法院审判附带民事诉讼案件，除适用刑法、刑事诉讼法外，还应当适用民法通则、民事诉讼法有关规定。

**Article 100** When adjudicating a collateral civil action case, the People's Court shall, in addition to applying the Criminal Law and the Criminal Procedure Law, also apply the relevant provisions of the General Principles of the Civil Law and the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-101"></a>第一百零一条 人民法院认定公诉案件被告人的行为不构成犯罪的，对已经提起的附带民事诉讼，经调解不能达成协议的，应当一并作出刑事附带民事判决。

**Article 101** Where the People's Court finds that the act of the defendant in a public prosecution case does not constitute a crime, and with respect to a collateral civil action that has been initiated, if an agreement cannot be reached through mediation, the court shall render a single criminal and collateral civil judgment.

<a id="art-102"></a>第一百零二条 人民法院审理刑事附带民事诉讼案件，不收取诉讼费。  
七、期间、送达、审理期限

**Article 102** When hearing a criminal case with a collateral civil action, the People's Court shall not charge litigation costs.  
VII. Periods, Service, and Trial Time Limits

<a id="art-103"></a>第一百零三条 期间以时、日、月计算。期间开始的时和日不计算在期间以内；计算法定期间时，应当将路途上的时间扣除；期问的最后一日为节假日的，以节假日后的第一日为期问届满日期。但对于被告人或者罪犯的在押期间，应当至期问届满之日为止，不得因节假日而延长在押期限。  
当事人由于不能抗拒的原因或者有其他正当理由而耽误期限，依法申请继续进行应当在期限届满以前完成的诉讼活动，人民法院查证属实后，应当裁定准许。  
以月计算的期限，自本月某日至下月某日为一个月，如本月1日收案至下一个月1日、本月最后一日至下一个月最后一日为一个月的审理期限；半月一律按15日计算期限。

**Article 103** Periods shall be calculated by hours, days, and months. The hour and day from which a period begins shall not be counted within the period; when calculating a statutory period, travel time shall be deducted; if the last day of a period falls on a holiday, the day after the holiday shall be regarded as the expiration date. However, for the period of custody of a defendant or criminal, it shall last until the expiration date, and the custody period shall not be extended due to holidays.  
Where a party fails to meet a time limit due to force majeure or other justifiable reasons, and applies according to law to continue the litigation activities that should have been completed before the expiration of the time limit, the People's Court shall, after verifying the facts, rule to permit such continuation.  
For a period calculated by months, it shall be one month from a certain day of the current month to the corresponding day of the next month; for example, from the first day of the current month to the first day of the next month, or from the last day of the current month to the last day of the next month, shall constitute one month's trial period; a half month shall always be calculated as 15 days.

<a id="art-104"></a>第一百零四条 送达诉讼文书必须有送达回证。收件人本人应当在送达回证上记明收到的日期，并且签名或者盖章。如果本人不在，可以由其成年家属或者所在单位负责收件人员代收，代收人应当在送达回证上记明收到的日期，并且签名或者盖章。  
收件人本人或者代收人在送达回证上签收的日期为送达的日期。  
如果收件人本人或者代收人拒绝接收或者拒绝签名、盖章，送达人可以邀请见证人到场，说明情况，在送达回证上记明拒收的事由和日期，由送达人、见证人签名或者盖章，并将诉讼文书留在收件人或者代收人住处或者单位后，即视为送达。

**Article 104** Service of litigation documents shall be accompanied by a receipt of service. The addressee himself or herself shall note on the receipt the date of receipt and sign or affix his or her seal. If the addressee is absent, such documents may be received by an adult family member or the person in charge of receiving documents at the addressee's unit, and the recipient shall note on the receipt the date of receipt and sign or affix his or her seal.  
The date signed by the addressee or the recipient on the receipt of service shall be the date of service.  
If the addressee or the recipient refuses to receive the documents or refuses to sign or affix a seal, the server may invite witnesses to be present, explain the situation, note on the receipt the reason for refusal and the date, have the server and the witnesses sign or affix their seals, and leave the litigation documents at the residence or unit of the addressee or the recipient, after which service shall be deemed to have been effected.

<a id="art-105"></a>第一百零五条 直接送达诉讼文书有困难的，可以委托收件人所在地的人民法院代为送达，或者邮寄送达。

**Article 105** If direct service of litigation documents is difficult, service may be entrusted to the People's Court of the place where the addressee is located, or service may be made by mail.

<a id="art-106"></a>第一百零六条 委托送达的，应当将委托函、委托送达的诉讼文书及送达回证，寄送收件人所在地的人民法院受委托的人民法院收到委托送达的诉讼文书，应当登记，并由专人及时送达收件人，然后将送达回证及时退回委托送达的人民法院。受委托的人民法院无法送达时，应当将不能送达的原因及时告知委托的人民法院，并将诉讼文书及送达回证退回。

**Article 106** For entrusted service, the letter of entrustment, the litigation documents to be served, and the receipt of service shall be sent to the People's Court of the place where the addressee is located. The entrusted People's Court, upon receiving the litigation documents for entrusted service, shall register them, have them served on the addressee by a designated person in a timely manner, and then promptly return the receipt of service to the entrusting People's Court. If the entrusted People's Court is unable to effect service, it shall promptly inform the entrusting People's Court of the reason for the inability to serve and return the litigation documents and the receipt of service.

<a id="art-107"></a>第一百零七条 邮寄送达的，应当将诉讼文书、送达回证挂号邮寄给收件人。挂号回执上注明的日期为送达的日期。

**Article 107** For service by mail, the litigation documents and the receipt of service shall be sent by registered mail to the addressee. The date noted on the registration receipt shall be the date of service.

<a id="art-108"></a>第一百零八条 诉讼文书的收件人是军人的，可以通过所在部队团级以上单位的政治部门转交。  
收件人正在服刑的，可以通过所在监狱或者其他执行机关转交。  
收件人正在劳动教养的，可以通过劳动教养单位转交。  
代为转交的部门、单位收到诉讼文书后，应当立即交收件人签收，并将送达回证及时退回送达的人民法院。

**Article 108** If the addressee of litigation documents is a serviceman, the documents may be forwarded through the political department of the unit at or above the regimental level where the addressee serves.  
If the addressee is serving a sentence, the documents may be forwarded through the prison or other enforcement agency where he or she is held.  
If the addressee is under re-education through labor, the documents may be forwarded through the re-education through labor institution.  
After receiving the litigation documents, the forwarding department or unit shall promptly deliver them to the addressee for signing, and promptly return the receipt of service to the People's Court that effected the service.

<a id="art-109"></a>第一百零九条 审理公诉案件的期限，依照刑事诉讼法的规定执行。适用普通程序审理的被告人被羁押的自诉案件，应当在被告人被羁押后一个月内宣判，至迟不得超过一个半月。有[刑事诉讼法第一百二十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-126)规定情形之的，经省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院批准或者决定，可以再延长一个月。  
需要延长审理期限的，应当在期满七日以前报请高级人民法院批准或者决定。  
适用普通程序审理的被告人未被羁押的自诉案件，应当在立案后六个月内宣判。有特殊情况需要延长审理期限的，由本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。

**Article 109** The time limit for trying a public prosecution case shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law. For a private prosecution case tried under ordinary procedures where the defendant is in custody, a judgment shall be pronounced within one month after the defendant is taken into custody, and shall not exceed one and a half months at the latest. If the circumstances provided for in Article 126 of the Criminal Procedure Law exist, the time limit may be extended by one more month with the approval or decision of the Higher People's Court of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
If an extension of the trial time limit is necessary, an application for approval or decision shall be submitted to the Higher People's Court seven days before the expiration of the time limit.  
For a private prosecution case tried under ordinary procedures where the defendant is not in custody, a judgment shall be pronounced within six months after the case is placed on file. If there are special circumstances requiring an extension of the trial time limit, the time limit may be extended by three months with the approval of the president of the court.

<a id="art-110"></a>第一百一十条 审理期间，对被告人作精神病鉴定的时间不计入审理期限。  
八、审判组织

**Article 110** During trial, the time for conducting a psychiatric evaluation on the defendant shall not be counted within the trial time limit.  
VIII. Adjudication Organization

<a id="art-111"></a>第一百一十一条 合议庭的审判长由审判员担任，在审判员不能参加合议庭的情况下，助理审判员由本院院长提出，经审判委员会通过，可以临时代行审判员职务，并可以担任审判长。

**Article 111** The presiding judge of a collegial panel shall be a judge. Where no judge is available to participate in the collegial panel, an assistant judge may, upon the proposal of the president of the court and approval by the judicial committee, temporarily exercise the functions of a judge and may serve as the presiding judge.

<a id="art-112"></a>第一百一十二条 开庭审理和评议案件，必须由同一合议庭进行。合议庭成员在评议案件的时候，应当表明自己的意见。如果意见分歧，应当按多数人的意见作出决定，但是少数人的意见应当写入笔录。评议笔录由合议庭的组成人员在审阅确认无误后签名。评议情况应当保密。

**Article 112** The court session and the deliberation of a case shall be conducted by the same collegial panel. When deliberating a case, members of the collegial panel shall state their opinions. If opinions differ, the decision shall be made according to the opinion of the majority, but the opinion of the minority shall be recorded in the minutes. The deliberation minutes shall be signed by the members of the collegial panel after they have reviewed and confirmed their accuracy. The deliberation shall be kept confidential.

<a id="art-113"></a>第一百一十三条 审判员依法独任审判时，行使与本解释规定的审判长同样的职权。

**Article 113** When a judge hears a case alone according to law, such judge shall exercise the same functions and powers as the presiding judge provided for in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-114"></a>第一百一十四条 合议庭开庭审理并且评议后，应当作出判决或者裁定。对下列疑难、复杂、重大的案件，合议庭认为难以作出决定的，可以提请院长决定提交审判委员会讨论决定：  
（一）拟判处死刑的；  
（二）合议庭成员意见有重大分歧的；  
（三）人民检察院抗诉的；  
（四）在社会上有重大影响的；  
（五）其他需要由审判委员会讨论决定的。  
对于合议庭提请院长决定提交审判委员会讨论决定的案件，院长认为不必要的，可以建议合议庭复议一次。  
独任审判的案件，开庭审理后，独任审判员认为有必要的，也可以提请院长决定提交审判委员会讨论决定。

**Article 114** After a collegial panel holds a court session and deliberates, it shall render a judgment or ruling. For the following difficult, complex, or important cases, if the collegial panel considers it difficult to make a decision, it may request the president to decide to submit the matter to the judicial committee for discussion and decision:  
(1) cases where the death penalty is intended to be imposed;  
(2) cases where there are significant differences of opinion among members of the collegial panel;  
(3) cases where the People's Procuratorate files a protest;  
(4) cases that have significant social impact;  
(5) other cases requiring discussion and decision by the judicial committee.  
For a case that the collegial panel requests the president to decide to submit to the judicial committee for discussion and decision, if the president considers it unnecessary, he or she may suggest that the collegial panel reconsider the matter once.  
For a case heard by a single judge, after the court session, if the single judge deems it necessary, he or she may also request the president to decide to submit the matter to the judicial committee for discussion and decision.

<a id="art-115"></a>第一百一十五条 审判委员会的决定，合议庭应当执行。合议庭有不同意见的，可以建议院长提交审判委员会复议。  
九、公诉案件第一审程序

**Article 115** The decision of the judicial committee shall be executed by the collegial panel. If the collegial panel has different opinions, it may suggest that the president submit the matter to the judicial committee for reconsideration.  
IX. First Instance Procedures for Public Prosecution Cases

<a id="art-116"></a>第一百一十六条 人民法院对人民检察院提起的公诉案件，应当在收到起诉书（一式八份，每增加一名被告人，增加起诉书五份）后，指定审判员审查以下内容：  
（一）案件是否属于本院管辖；  
（二）起诉书指控的被告人的身份、实施犯罪的时间、地点、手段、犯罪事实、危害后果和罪名以及其他可能影响定罪量刑的情节等是否明确；  
（三）起诉书中是否载明被告人被采取强制措施的种类、羁押地点、是否在案以及有无扣押、冻结在案的被告人的财物及存放地点；是否列明被害人的姓名、住址、通讯处，为保护被害人而不宜列明的，应当单独移送被害人名单；  
（四）是否附有起诉前收集的证据的目录；  
（五）是否附有能够证明指控犯罪行为性质、情节等内容的主要证据复印件或者照片；  
（六）是否附有起诉前提供了证言的证人名单；证人名单应当分别列明出庭作证和拟不出庭作证的证人的姓名、性别、年龄、职业、住址和通讯处；  
（七）已委托辩护人、代理人的，是否附有辩护人、代理人的姓名、住址、通讯处明确的名单；  
（八）提起附带民事诉讼的，是否附有相关证据材料；  
（九）侦查、起诉程序的各种法律手续和诉讼文书复印件是否完备；  
（十）有无[刑事诉讼法第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-15)第（二）至（六）项规定的不追究刑事责任的情形。  
前款第（五）项中所说的主要证据包括：  
1、起诉书中涉及的[刑事诉讼法第四十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-42)规定的证据种类中的主要证据；  
2、同种类多个证据中被确定为主要证据的；如果某一种类证据中只有一个证据，该证据即为主要证据；  
3、作为法定量刑情节的自首、立功、累犯、中止、未遂、防卫过当等证据。

**Article 116** With respect to a public prosecution case initiated by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Court shall, after receiving the indictment (eight copies, plus five copies for each additional defendant), designate a judge to examine the following:  
(1) whether the case falls within the jurisdiction of the court;  
(2) whether the identity of the defendant as charged in the indictment, the time, place, means, criminal facts, harmful consequences, and charge of the crime, and other circumstances that may affect conviction and sentencing, are clearly stated;  
(3) whether the indictment indicates the type of compulsory measures taken against the defendant, the place of custody, whether the defendant is present in the case, and whether there are any properties of the defendant that have been seized or frozen and their location; whether the names, addresses, and contact information of the victims are listed; if it is inappropriate to list them for the protection of the victims, a separate list of victims shall be submitted;  
(4) whether a list of evidence collected before the initiation of prosecution is attached;  
(5) whether copies or photographs of main evidence capable of proving the nature and circumstances of the alleged criminal act are attached;  
(6) whether a list of witnesses who provided testimony before the initiation of prosecution is attached; the list of witnesses shall separately list the names, genders, ages, occupations, addresses, and contact information of witnesses who will testify in court and those who are not expected to testify;  
(7) where a defender or agent has been retained, whether a list clearly stating the names, addresses, and contact information of such defender or agent is attached;  
(8) if a collateral civil action is initiated, whether relevant evidentiary materials are attached;  
(9) whether the copies of various legal procedures and litigation documents from the investigation and prosecution stages are complete;  
(10) whether any circumstances provided for in Article 15, subparagraphs (2) to (6) of the Criminal Procedure Law exist where criminal liability shall not be investigated.  
The main evidence referred to in subparagraph (5) of the preceding paragraph includes:  
1. the main evidence among the types of evidence provided for in Article 42 of the Criminal Procedure Law that is involved in the indictment;  
2. where among multiple pieces of evidence of the same type, some are determined as main evidence; if there is only one piece of evidence of a certain type, that evidence shall be the main evidence;  
3. evidence of statutory sentencing circumstances such as voluntary surrender, meritorious service, recidivism, discontinuation of a crime, attempted crime, and excessive defense.

<a id="art-117"></a>第一百一十七条 案件经审查后，应当根据不同情况分别处理：  
（一）对于不属于本院管辖或者被告人不在案的，应当决定退回人民检察院；  
（二）对于不符合[本解释第一百一十六条](#art-116)第（二）至（九）项规定之一，需要补送材料的，应当通知人民检察院在三日内补送；  
（三）对于根据[刑事诉讼法第一百六十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-162)第  
（三）项规定宣告被告人无罪，人民检察院依据新的事实、证据材料重新起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理；  
（四）依照[本解释第一百七十七条](#art-177)规定，人民法院裁定准许人民检察院撤诉的案件，没有新的事实、证据，人民检察院重新起诉的，人民法院不予受理；  
（五）对于符合[刑事诉讼法第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-15)第（二）至（六）项规定的情形的，应当裁定终止审理或者决定不予受理；  
（六）对于被告人真实身份不明，但符合[刑事诉讼法第一百二十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-128)第二款规定的，人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 117** After examination of a case, the following shall be dealt with according to different circumstances:  
(1) Where the case is not within the jurisdiction of the court or the defendant is not in the case, the court shall decide to return the case to the People's Procuratorate;  
(2) Where the case fails to meet any of the requirements under subparagraphs (2) to (9) of Article 116 of this Interpretation and supplementary materials are needed, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate to supplement them within three days;  
(3) Where the defendant has been declared not guilty in accordance with Article 162, subparagraph (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law, and the People's Procuratorate re-initiates a prosecution based on new facts or evidence, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law;  
(4) Where the People's Court has ruled to permit the withdrawal of the prosecution by the People's Procuratorate in accordance with Article 177 of this Interpretation, if the People's Procuratorate re-initiates a prosecution without new facts or evidence, the People's Court shall not accept the case;  
(5) Where the circumstances provided for in Article 15, subparagraphs (2) to (6) of the Criminal Procedure Law exist, the court shall rule to terminate the trial or decide not to accept the case;  
(6) Where the true identity of the defendant is unclear, but the case meets the requirements of Article 128, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-118"></a>第一百一十八条 人民法院对于按照普通程序审理的公诉案件，决定是否受理，应当在七日内审查完毕。  
对于人民检察院建议按简易程序审理的公诉案件，决定是否受理，应当在三日内审查完毕。  
人民法院对提起公诉的案件进行审查的期限，计入人民法院的审理期限。

**Article 118** For a public prosecution case to be tried under ordinary procedures, the People's Court shall complete the examination and decide whether to accept the case within seven days.  
For a public prosecution case recommended by the People's Procuratorate to be tried under summary procedures, the examination and decision on whether to accept the case shall be completed within three days.  
The time limit for the People's Court to examine a case initiated by public prosecution shall be included in the trial time limit of the People's Court.

<a id="art-119"></a>第一百一十九条 对于决定开庭审理的案件，人民法院应当进行下列工作：  
（一）适用普通程序审理的案件，由院长或者庭长指定审判长并确定合议庭组成人员；适用简易程序审理的案件，由庭长指定审判员一人独任审理；  
（二）将人民检察院的起诉书副本至迟在开庭十日以前送达当事人；  
（三）对于未委托辩护人的被告人，告知其可以委托辩护人；对于符合[刑事诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-34)第二、三款规定的，应当指定承担法律援助义务的律师为其提供辩护；对于符合[刑事诉讼法第三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-34)第一款及[本解释第三十七条](#art-37)规定的，一般要指定承担法律援助义务的律师为其提供辩护；  
（四）通知被告人、辩护人于开庭五日前提供出庭作证的身份、住址、通讯处明确的证人、鉴定人名单及不出庭作证的证人、鉴定人名单和拟当庭宣读、出示的证据复印件、照片；  
（五）将开庭的时间、地点在开庭三日以前通知人民检察院；  
（六）将传唤当事人和通知辩护人、法定代理人、证人、鉴定人和勘验、检查笔录制作人、翻译人员的传票和通知书，至迟在开庭三日以前送达；  
（七）公开审判的案件，在开庭三日以前先期公布案由、被告人姓名、开庭时间和地点。  
人民法院通知公诉机关或者辩护人提供的证人时，如果该证人表示拒绝出庭作证或者按照所提供的证人通讯地址未能通知到该证人的，应当及时告知申请通知该证人的公诉机关或者辩护人。上述工作情况应当制作笔录，并由审判人员和书记员签名。

**Article 119** For a case decided to be tried in a court session, the People's Court shall carry out the following work:  
(1) For a case tried under ordinary procedures, the president or the division chief shall designate the presiding judge and determine the members of the collegial panel; for a case tried under summary procedures, the division chief shall designate one judge to hear the case alone;  
(2) A copy of the indictment of the People's Procuratorate shall be served to the parties no later than ten days before the court session;  
(3) If a defendant has not retained a defender, he or she shall be informed of the right to retain a defender; for a defendant who meets the provisions of Article 34, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the court shall designate a lawyer who undertakes legal aid obligations to provide defense; for a defendant who meets the provisions of Article 34, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 37 of this Interpretation, a lawyer who undertakes legal aid obligations shall generally be designated to provide defense;  
(4) The defendant and the defender shall be notified to provide, five days before the court session, the list of witnesses and expert witnesses who have clear identities, addresses, and contact information and will testify in court, the list of witnesses and expert witnesses who will not testify in court, and copies or photographs of evidence intended to be read or presented in court;  
(5) The time and place of the court session shall be notified to the People's Procuratorate three days before the court session;  
(6) The summonses to the parties and the notices to the defender, legal representative, witnesses, expert witnesses, and the persons who made the inspection and examination records, and interpreters shall be served no later than three days before the court session;  
(7) For cases tried in public, the cause of action, the name of the defendant, and the time and place of the court session shall be announced in advance three days before the court session.  
When notifying a witness provided by the prosecutorial authority or the defender, if the witness refuses to testify in court or cannot be notified at the contact information provided, the People's Court shall promptly inform the prosecutorial authority or the defender who requested the notification of such witness. A record of the above work shall be made and signed by the judicial personnel and the court clerk.

<a id="art-120"></a>第一百二十条 开庭审判前，合议庭可以拟出法庭审理提纲，提纲一般包括下列内容：  
（一）合议庭成员在庭审中的具体分工；  
（二）起诉书指控的犯罪事实部分的重点和认定案件性质方面的要点；  
（三）讯问被告人时需了解的案情要点；  
（四）控辩双方拟出庭作证的证人、鉴定人和勘验、检查笔录制作人名单；  
（五）控辩双方拟当庭宣读、出示的证人书面证言、物证和其他证据的目录；  
（六）庭审中可能出现的问题及拟采取的措施。

**Article 120** Before the court session, the collegial panel may prepare a trial outline, which generally includes the following:  
(1) the specific division of tasks among members of the collegial panel during the trial;  
(2) the key points of the criminal facts charged in the indictment and the main points regarding the determination of the nature of the case;  
(3) the key points of the case facts to be understood when interrogating the defendant;  
(4) the list of witnesses, expert witnesses, and persons who made inspection and examination records that the prosecution and defense intend to call to testify in court;  
(5) the list of written witness testimony, physical evidence, and other evidence that the prosecution and defense intend to read or present in court;  
(6) issues that may arise during the trial and the measures to be taken.

<a id="art-121"></a>第一百二十一条 审判案件应当公开进行。但是涉及国家秘密或者个人隐私的案件，不公开审理。对未成年被告人案件的审理，适用相关规定。  
对于当事人提出申请的确属涉及商业秘密的案件，法庭应当决定不公开审理。

**Article 121** A case shall be tried in public. However, cases involving state secrets or personal privacy shall not be tried in public. Trials of cases involving minor defendants shall be governed by relevant provisions.  
For a case that, upon application by a party, is indeed related to trade secrets, the court shall decide not to try the case in public.

<a id="art-122"></a>第一百二十二条 依法不公开审理的案件，任何公民包括与审理该案无关的法院工作人员和被告人的近亲属都不得旁听。审理未成年被告人的案件，适用相关规定。

**Article 122** For a case not tried in public according to law, no citizen, including court personnel not involved in the trial of the case and the close relatives of the defendant, shall be allowed to observe the proceedings. Trials of cases involving minor defendants shall be governed by relevant provisions.

<a id="art-123"></a>第一百二十三条 被害人、诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人经入民法院传唤或者通知未到庭，不影响开庭审判的，人民法院可以开庭审理。

**Article 123** Where the victim, litigation agent, witness, or expert witness fails to appear in court after being summoned or notified by the People's Court, if such absence does not affect the trial, the People's Court may proceed with the court session.

<a id="art-124"></a>第一百二十四条 开庭审理前，书记员应当依次进行下列五作：  
（一）查明公诉人、当事人、证人及其他诉讼参与人是否已经到庭；  
（二）宣读法庭规则；  
（三）请公诉人、辩护人入庭；  
（四）请审判长、审判员（人民陪审员）入庭；  
（五）审判人员就座后，当庭向审判长报告开庭前的准备工作已经就绪。

**Article 124** Before the court session, the court clerk shall sequentially perform the following tasks:  
(1) verify whether the public prosecutor, parties, witnesses, and other participants in the proceedings have appeared in court;  
(2) read the court rules;  
(3) invite the public prosecutor and the defender to enter the courtroom;  
(4) invite the presiding judge and the judges (people's assessors) to enter the courtroom;  
(5) after the judicial personnel are seated, report in court to the presiding judge that the preparatory work before the court session has been completed.

<a id="art-125"></a>第一百二十五条 审判长宣布开庭，传被告人到庭后，应当查明被告人的下列情况：  
（一）姓名、出生年月日、民族、出生地、文化程度、职业、住址，或者单位的名称、住所地、诉讼代表人的姓名、职务；  
（二）是否曾受到过法律处分及处分的种类、时间；  
（三）是否被采取强制措施及强制措施的种类、时间；  
（四）收到人民检察院起诉书副本的日期；附带民事诉讼的，附带民事诉讼被告人收到民事诉状的日期。

**Article 125** After the presiding judge announces the opening of court session and summons the defendant to appear, the following information of the defendant shall be ascertained:  
(1) Name, date of birth, ethnicity, place of birth, educational level, occupation, domicile, or if the defendant is an entity, its name, place of domicile, and the name and position of its representative in the action;  
(2) Whether the defendant has previously received any legal sanction and the type and time of such sanction;  
(3) Whether compulsory measures have been taken and the type and time of such measures;  
(4) The date of receipt of the copy of the indictment from the People's Procuratorate; if there is a collateral civil action, the date of receipt of the civil complaint by the defendant in the collateral civil action.

<a id="art-126"></a>第一百二十六条 审判长宣布案件的来源、起诉的案由、附带民事诉讼原告人和被告人的姓名（名称）及是否公开审理对于不公开审理的案件，应当当庭宣布不公开审理的理由。

**Article 126** The presiding judge shall announce the source of the case, the cause of action of the prosecution, the names (or names of entities) of the plaintiff and defendant in the collateral civil action, and whether the trial is to be held in public. For cases not to be tried in public, the reason for not holding the trial in public shall be announced in court.

<a id="art-127"></a>第一百二十七条 审判长宣布合议庭组成人员、书记员、公诉人、辩护人、鉴定人和翻译人员的名单。

**Article 127** The presiding judge shall announce the list of the members of the collegial panel, the court clerk, the public prosecutor, the defenders, the expert evaluators, and the interpreters.

<a id="art-128"></a>第一百二十八条 审判长应当告知当事人、法定代理人在法庭审理过程中依法享有下列诉讼权利：  
（一）可以申请合议庭组成人员、书记员、公诉人、鉴定人和翻译人员回避；  
（二）可以提出证据，申请通知新的证人到庭、调取新的证据、重新鉴定或者勘验、检查；  
（三）被告人可以自行辩护；  
（四）被告人可以在法庭辩论终结后作最后的陈述。

**Article 128** The presiding judge shall inform the parties and their statutory agents of the following litigation rights they enjoy according to law during the court proceedings:  
(1) They may apply for the withdrawal of any member of the collegial panel, the court clerk, the public prosecutor, the expert evaluator, or the interpreter;  
(2) They may present evidence and apply for summoning new witnesses to appear in court, obtaining new evidence, conducting a new expert evaluation or inspection, or examination;  
(3) The defendant may defend himself or herself;  
(4) The defendant may make a final statement after the conclusion of the court debate.

<a id="art-129"></a>第一百二十九条 审判长分别询问当事人、法定代理人是否申请回避，申请何人回避和申请回避的理由。  
如果当事人、法定代理人申请审判人员、出庭支持公诉的检察人员回避，合议庭认为符合法定情形的，应当依照本解释有关回避的规定处理；认为不符合法定情形的，应当当庭驳回，继续法庭审理。如果申请回避人当庭申请复议，合议庭应当宣布休庭，待作出复议决定后，决定是否继续法庭审理。  
同意或者驳回回避申请的决定及复议决定，由审判长宣布，并说明理由。必要时，也可以由院长到庭宣布。

**Article 129** The presiding judge shall separately inquire of the parties and their statutory agents whether they apply for withdrawal, whom they apply to withdraw, and the reasons for the application for withdrawal.  
If a party or statutory agent applies for the withdrawal of a judicial officer or a procuratorial officer appearing in court to support the prosecution, and the collegial panel considers that the statutory circumstances are met, it shall handle the matter in accordance with the provisions of this Interpretation concerning withdrawal; if it considers that the statutory circumstances are not met, it shall reject the application in court and continue the court proceedings. If the applicant for withdrawal applies for reconsideration in court, the collegial panel shall announce an adjournment and, after a reconsideration decision is made, decide whether to continue the court proceedings.  
The decision to grant or reject the application for withdrawal and the reconsideration decision shall be announced by the presiding judge, who shall also state the reasons. Where necessary, the president of the court may also make the announcement in court.

<a id="art-130"></a>第一百三十条 审判长言布法庭调查开始后，应当首先由公诉人宣读起诉书；有附带民事诉讼的，再由附带民事诉讼的原告人或者其诉讼代理人宣读附带民事诉状。

**Article 130** After the presiding judge announces the commencement of the court investigation, the public prosecutor shall first read the indictment; if there is a collateral civil action, the plaintiff in the collateral civil action or his or her litigation agent shall then read the collateral civil complaint.

<a id="art-131"></a>第一百三十一条 起诉书指控的被告人的犯罪事实为两起以上的，法庭调查时，一般应当就每一起犯罪事实分别进行。

**Article 131** Where the indictment charges two or more criminal facts against the defendant, the court investigation shall generally be conducted separately for each criminal fact.

<a id="art-132"></a>第一百三十二条 在审判长主持下，被告人、被害人可以就起诉书指控的犯罪事实分别进行陈述。

**Article 132** Under the direction of the presiding judge, the defendant and the victim may respectively make statements regarding the criminal facts charged in the indictment.

<a id="art-133"></a>第一百三十三条 在审判长主持下，公诉人可以就起诉书中指控的犯罪事实讯问被告人；被害人及其诉讼代理人经审判长准许，可以就公诉人讯问的情况进行补充性发问；附带民事诉讼的原告人及其法定代理人或者诉讼代理人经审判长准许，可以就附带民事诉讼部分的事实向被告人发问；经审判长准许，被告人的辩护人及法定代理人或者诉讼代理人可以在控诉一方就某一具体问题讯问完毕后向被告人发问。

**Article 133** Under the direction of the presiding judge, the public prosecutor may interrogate the defendant regarding the criminal facts charged in the indictment; the victim and his or her litigation agent may, with the permission of the presiding judge, ask supplementary questions regarding the matters interrogated by the public prosecutor; the plaintiff in the collateral civil action and his or her statutory agent or litigation agent may, with the permission of the presiding judge, ask questions of the defendant regarding the facts of the collateral civil action; with the permission of the presiding judge, the defender of the defendant and the statutory agent or litigation agent may ask questions of the defendant after the prosecuting party has finished interrogating on a specific issue.

<a id="art-134"></a>第一百三十四条 对于共同犯罪案件中的被告人，应当分别进行讯问。合议庭认为必要时，可以传唤共同被告人同时到庭对质。

**Article 134** For defendants in a joint crime case, interrogation shall be conducted separately. When the collegial panel deems it necessary, it may summon the co-defendants to appear in court simultaneously for confrontation.

<a id="art-135"></a>第一百三十五条 控辩双方经审判长准许，可以向被害人、附带民事诉讼原告人发问。

**Article 135** The prosecuting and defending parties may, with the permission of the presiding judge, ask questions of the victim and the plaintiff in the collateral civil action.

<a id="art-136"></a>第一百三十六条 审判长对于控辩双方讯问、发问被告人、被害人和附带民事诉讼原告人、被告人的内容与本案无关或者讯问、发问的方式不当的，应当制止。  
对于控辩双方认为对方讯问或者发问的内容与本案无关或者讯问、发问的方式不当并提出异议的，审判长应当判明情况予以支持或者驳回。

**Article 136** The presiding judge shall stop any interrogation or questioning by the prosecuting or defending party that is irrelevant to the case or conducted in an improper manner, whether directed at the defendant, the victim, or the plaintiff or defendant in the collateral civil action.  
If the prosecuting or defending party raises an objection on the ground that the other party's interrogation or questioning is irrelevant to the case or conducted in an improper manner, the presiding judge shall ascertain the circumstances and either sustain or overrule the objection.

<a id="art-137"></a>第一百三十七条 审判人员认为有必要时，可以向被告人、被害人及附带民事诉讼原告人、被告人讯问或者发问。

**Article 137** When judicial officers consider it necessary, they may interrogate or question the defendant, the victim, and the plaintiff or defendant in the collateral civil action.

<a id="art-138"></a>第一百三十八条 对指控的每一起案件事实，经审判长准许，公诉人可以提请审判长传唤证人、鉴定人和勘验、检查笔录制作人出庭作证，或者出示证据，宣读未到庭的被害人、证人、鉴定人和勘验、检查笔录制作人的书面陈述、证言、鉴定结论及勘验、检查笔录；被害人及其诉讼代理人和附带民事诉讼的原告人及其诉讼代理人经审判法庭宣布延期审理后，人民检察院在补充侦查的期限内没有提请人民法院恢复法庭审理的，人民法院应当决定按人民检察院撤诉处理。

**Article 138** For each alleged fact of the case, with the permission of the presiding judge, the public prosecutor may request the presiding judge to summon witnesses, expert evaluators, and persons who made the records of inspection or examination to appear in court to give testimony, or may present evidence and read the written statements, testimony, expert conclusions, and records of inspection or examination of victims, witnesses, expert evaluators, and persons who made the records of inspection or examination who are not present in court; after the court announces an adjournment, if the People's Procuratorate fails to request the People's Court to resume the court proceedings within the period for supplemental investigation, the People's Court shall decide to treat the case as withdrawn by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-139"></a>第一百三十九条 控辩双方要求证人出庭作证，向法庭出示物证、书证、视听资料等证据，应当向审判长说明拟证明的事实，审判长同意的，即传唤证人或者准许出示证据；审判长认为与案件无关或者明显重复、不必要的证据，可以不予准许。

**Article 139** If the prosecuting or defending party requests a witness to appear in court to give testimony or to present evidence such as physical evidence, documentary evidence, or audio-visual materials to the court, it shall explain to the presiding judge the facts to be proved. If the presiding judge agrees, he or she shall summon the witness or permit the presentation of the evidence; if the presiding judge considers the evidence irrelevant to the case, clearly repetitive, or unnecessary, he or she may refuse to permit it.

<a id="art-140"></a>第一百四十条 被告人、辩护人、法定代理人经审判长准许，可以在起诉一方举证提供证据后，分别提请传唤证人、鉴定人出庭作证，或者出示证据、宣读未到庭的证人的书面证言、鉴定人的鉴定结论。

**Article 140** The defendant, defender, or statutory agent may, with the permission of the presiding judge, after the prosecuting party has adduced evidence, separately request the summoning of witnesses or expert evaluators to appear in court to give testimony, or present evidence and read the written testimony of witnesses not present in court or the expert conclusions of expert evaluators.

<a id="art-141"></a>第一百四十一条 证人应当出庭作证。  
符合下列情形，经人民法院准许的，证人可以不出庭作证：  
（一）未成年人；  
（二）庭审期间身患严重疾病或者行动极为不便的；  
（三）其证言对案件的审判不起直接决定作用的；  
（四）有其他原因的。

**Article 141** A witness shall appear in court to give testimony.  
If one of the following circumstances exists and permission is granted by the People's Court, a witness may not appear in court to give testimony:  
(1) The witness is a minor;  
(2) The witness suffers from a serious illness or is extremely inconvenient in movement during the trial period;  
(3) The witness's testimony does not play a directly decisive role in the adjudication of the case;  
(4) There are other reasons.

<a id="art-142"></a>第一百四十二条 证人到庭后，审判人员应当先核实证人的身份、与当事人以及本案的关系，告知证人应当如实地提供证言和有意作伪证或者隐匿罪证要负的法律责仟。  
证人作证前，应当在如实作证的保证书上签名。

**Article 142** After a witness appears in court, the judicial officers shall first verify the witness's identity, the relationship with the parties and with the case, and inform the witness that he or she shall truthfully provide testimony and that deliberately giving false testimony or concealing criminal evidence shall incur legal liability.  
Before giving testimony, the witness shall sign an oath to testify truthfully.

<a id="art-143"></a>第一百四十三条 向证人发问，应当先由提请传唤的一方进行；发问完毕后，对方经审判长准许，也可以发问。

**Article 143** The questioning of a witness shall first be conducted by the party that requested the witness to be summoned; after the questioning is completed, the other party may also ask questions with the permission of the presiding judge.

<a id="art-144"></a>第一百四十四条 鉴定人应当出庭宣读鉴定结论，但经人民法院准许不出庭的除外。鉴定人到庭后，审判人员应当先核实鉴定人的身份、与当事人及本案的关系，告知鉴定人应当如实地提供鉴定意见和有意作虚假鉴定要负的法律责任。  
鉴定人说明鉴定结论前，应当在如实说明鉴定结论的保证书上签名。

**Article 144** An expert evaluator shall appear in court to read the expert conclusion, unless permitted by the People's Court not to appear. After the expert evaluator appears in court, the judicial officers shall first verify the expert evaluator's identity, the relationship with the parties and with the case, and inform the expert evaluator that he or she shall truthfully provide expert opinions and that deliberately making a false evaluation shall incur legal liability.  
Before stating the expert conclusion, the expert evaluator shall sign an assurance to truthfully state the expert conclusion.

<a id="art-145"></a>第一百四十五条 向鉴定人发问，应当先由要求传唤的一方进行；发问完毕后，对方经审判长准许，也可以发问。

**Article 145** The questioning of an expert evaluator shall first be conducted by the party that requested the summoning; after the questioning is completed, the other party may also ask questions with the permission of the presiding judge.

<a id="art-146"></a>第一百四十六条 询问证人应当遵循以下规则：  
（一）发问的内容应当与案件的事实相关；  
（二）不得以诱导方式提问；  
（三）不得威胁证人；  
（四）不得损害证人的人格尊严。  
前款规定也适用于对被告人、被害人、附带民事诉讼原告人和被告人、鉴定人的讯问、发问或者询问。

**Article 146** The following rules shall be observed when examining witnesses:  
(1) The content of the questions shall be relevant to the facts of the case;  
(2) No leading questions shall be asked;  
(3) No threat shall be made against the witness;  
(4) The personal dignity of the witness shall not be impaired.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall also apply to the interrogation, questioning, or examination of the defendant, the victim, the plaintiff and defendant in a collateral civil action, and the expert evaluator.

<a id="art-147"></a>第一百四十七条 审判长对于向证人、鉴定人发问的内容与本案无关或者发问的方式不当的，应当制止。  
对于控辩双方认为对方发问的内容与本案无关或者发问的方式不当并提出异议的，审判长应当判明情况予以支持或者驳回。

**Article 147** The presiding judge shall stop any questioning of a witness or expert evaluator that is irrelevant to the case or conducted in an improper manner.  
If the prosecuting or defending party raises an objection on the ground that the other party's questioning is irrelevant to the case or conducted in an improper manner, the presiding judge shall ascertain the circumstances and either sustain or overrule the objection.

<a id="art-148"></a>第一百四十八条 审判人员认为有必要时，可以询问证人、鉴定人。

**Article 148** When judicial officers consider it necessary, they may examine witnesses or expert evaluators.

<a id="art-149"></a>第一百四十九条 向证人和鉴定人发问应当分别进行。证人、鉴定人经控辩双方发问或者审判人员询问后，审判长应当告其退庭。  
证人、鉴定人不得旁听对本案的审理。

**Article 149** The questioning of witnesses and expert evaluators shall be conducted separately. After a witness or expert evaluator has been questioned by the prosecuting or defending parties or examined by judicial officers, the presiding judge shall inform him or her to withdraw from the court.  
Witnesses and expert evaluators shall not sit in on the trial of the case.

<a id="art-150"></a>第一百五十条 当庭出示的物证、书证、视听资料等证据，应当先由出示证据的一方就所出示的证据的来源、特征等作必要的说明，然后由另一方进行辨认并发表意见。控辩双方可以互相质问、辩论。

**Article 150** For physical evidence, documentary evidence, audio-visual materials, and other evidence presented in court, the party presenting the evidence shall first make necessary explanations regarding the source and characteristics of the evidence presented, and then the other party shall examine it and state its opinions. The prosecuting and defending parties may question each other and engage in debate.

<a id="art-151"></a>第一百五十一条 当庭出示的证据、宣读的证人证言、鉴定结论和勘验、检查笔录等，在出示、宣读后，应即将原件移交法庭。对于确实无法当庭移交的，应当要求出示、宣读证据的一方在休庭后三日内移交。

**Article 151** Evidence presented in court, witness testimony read out, expert conclusions, and records of inspection or examination shall be handed over to the court immediately after presentation or reading. If it is truly impossible to hand them over in court, the party that presented or read the evidence shall be required to hand them over within three days after the adjournment.

<a id="art-152"></a>第一百五十二条 对于公诉人在法庭上宣读、播放未到庭证人的证言的，如果该证人提供过不同的证言，法庭应当要求公诉人将该证人的全部证言在休庭后三日内移交。  
人民法院审查前款规定的证据材料，发现与庭审调查认定的案件事实有重大出入，可能影响正确裁判的，应当决定恢复法庭调查。

**Article 152** If the public prosecutor reads or plays the testimony of a witness not present in court, and if the witness has provided different testimony, the court shall require the public prosecutor to hand over all the testimony of that witness within three days after the adjournment.  
If, upon examining the evidentiary materials specified in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court finds a significant discrepancy with the case facts established through the court investigation that may affect a correct judgment, it shall decide to resume the court investigation.

<a id="art-153"></a>第一百五十三条 在法庭调查过程中，合议庭对于证据有疑问的，可以宣布休庭，对该证据进行调查核实。

**Article 153** During the court investigation, if the collegial panel has doubts about the evidence, it may announce an adjournment to investigate and verify the evidence.

<a id="art-154"></a>第一百五十四条 人民法院调查核实证据时，可以进行勘验、检查、扣押、鉴定和查询、冻结。必要时，可以通知检察人员、辩护人到场。

**Article 154** When the People's Court investigates and verifies evidence, it may conduct inspections, examinations, seizures, expert evaluations, inquiries, and freezes. When necessary, it may notify the procuratorial personnel and defenders to be present.

<a id="art-155"></a>第一百五十五条 公诉人要求出示开庭前送交人民法院的证据目录以外的证据，辩护方提出异议的，审判长如认为该证据确有出示的必要，可以准许出示。如果辩护方提出对新的证据要做必要准备时，可以宣布休庭，并根据具体情况确定辩护方作必要准备的时间。确定的时间期满后，应当继续开庭审理。

**Article 155** If the public prosecutor requests to present evidence not listed in the catalog of evidence submitted to the People's Court before the trial, and the defending party raises an objection, the presiding judge may permit its presentation if he or she considers it truly necessary. If the defending party states that it needs to make necessary preparations regarding the new evidence, the presiding judge may announce an adjournment and determine the time for the defending party to make such preparations based on the specific circumstances. After the determined period expires, the court trial shall continue.

<a id="art-156"></a>第一百五十六条 当事人和辩护人申请通知新的证人到庭，调取新的证据，申请重新鉴定或者勘验的，应当提供证人的姓名、证据的存放地点，说明所要证明的案件事实，要求重新鉴定或者勘验的理由。审判人员根据具体情况，认为可能影响案件事实认定的，应当同意该申请，并宣布延期审理；不同意的，应当告知理由并继续审理。  
依照前款规定延期审理的时间不得超过一个月，延期审理的时间不计入审限。

**Article 156** If a party or defender applies to summon a new witness to appear in court, to obtain new evidence, or to request a new expert evaluation or inspection, he or she shall provide the name of the witness and the location where the evidence is kept, state the case facts to be proved, and the reasons for requesting a new expert evaluation or inspection. The judicial officers shall, based on the specific circumstances, grant the application and announce an adjournment of the trial if they consider it may affect the determination of the case facts; if they do not grant the application, they shall inform the parties of the reasons and continue the trial.  
The period of adjournment under the preceding paragraph shall not exceed one month, and the period of adjournment shall not be counted in the trial period.

<a id="art-157"></a>第一百五十七条 在庭审过程中，公诉人发现案件需要补充侦查，提出延期审理建议的，合议庭应当同意。但是建议延期审理的次数不得超过两次。  
法庭宣布延期审理后，人民检察院在补充侦查的期限内没有提请人民法院恢复法庭审理的，人民法院应当决定按人民检察院撤诉处理。

**Article 157** If, during the trial, the public prosecutor finds that the case requires supplemental investigation and proposes an adjournment, the collegial panel shall grant it. However, the number of times such adjournment is proposed shall not exceed two.  
After the court announces the adjournment, if the People's Procuratorate fails to request the People's Court to resume the court trial within the period for supplemental investigation, the People's Court shall decide to treat the case as withdrawn by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-158"></a>第一百五十八条 人民法院向人民检察院调取需要调查核实的证据材料，或者根据辩护人、被告人的申请，向人民检察院调取在侦查、审查起诉中收集的有关被告人无罪和罪轻的证据材料，应当通知人民检察院在收到调取证据材料决定书后三日内移交。

**Article 158** If the People's Court requests the People's Procuratorate to provide evidentiary materials that need investigation and verification, or, upon the application of the defender or defendant, requests the People's Procuratorate to provide evidentiary materials collected during investigation and review for prosecution that are relevant to the defendant's innocence or lesser culpability, the People's Procuratorate shall be notified to hand over such materials within three days after receiving the decision on the request for evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-159"></a>第一百五十九条 合议庭在案件审理过程中，发现被告人可能有自首、立功等法定量刑情节，而起诉和移送的证据材料中没有这方面的证据材料的，应当建议人民检察院补充侦查。

**Article 159** If, during the trial, the collegial panel discovers that the defendant may have statutory sentencing circumstances such as voluntary surrender or meritorious service, but the indictment and transferred evidentiary materials contain no evidence of such circumstances, it shall recommend that the People's Procuratorate conduct a supplemental investigation.

<a id="art-160"></a>第一百六十条 合议庭认为本案事实已经调查清楚，应当由审判长宣布法庭调查结束，开始就全案事实、证据、适用法律等问题进行法庭辩论。

**Article 160** When the collegial panel considers that the facts of the case have been fully investigated, the presiding judge shall announce the conclusion of the court investigation and commence the court debate on issues such as the facts of the entire case, the evidence, and the application of law.

<a id="art-161"></a>第一百六十一条 法庭辩论应当在审判长的主持下，按照下列顺序进行：  
（一）公诉人发言；  
（二）被害人及其诉讼代理人发言；  
（三）被告人自行辩护；  
（四）辩护人辩护；  
（五）控辩双方进行辩论。

**Article 161** The court debate shall be conducted under the direction of the presiding judge, in the following order:  
(1) The public prosecutor makes a statement;  
(2) The victim and his or her litigation agent make statements;  
(3) The defendant makes a self-defense;  
(4) The defender makes a defense;  
(5) The prosecuting and defending parties engage in debate.

<a id="art-162"></a>第一百六十二条 附带民事诉讼部分的辩论应当在刑事诉讼部分的辩论结束后进行。先由附带民事诉讼原告人及其诉讼代理人发言，然后由被告人及其诉讼代理人答辩。

**Article 162** The debate on the collateral civil action shall be conducted after the debate on the criminal part has concluded. The plaintiff in the collateral civil action and his or her litigation agent shall speak first, followed by the defendant and his or her litigation agent in response.

<a id="art-163"></a>第一百六十三条 在法庭辩论过程中，审判长对于控辩双方与案件无关、重复或者互相指责的发言应当制止。

**Article 163** During the court debate, the presiding judge shall stop any statements by the prosecuting or defending parties that are irrelevant to the case, repetitive, or consist of mutual accusations.

<a id="art-164"></a>第一百六十四条 对于辩护人依照有关规定当庭拒绝继续为被告人进行辩护的，合议庭应当准许。如果被告人要求另行委托辩护人，合议庭应当宣布延期审理，由被告人另行委托辩护人或者由人民法院为其另行指定辩护律师

**Article 164** If a defender refuses in court to continue defending the defendant in accordance with relevant provisions, the collegial panel shall permit it. If the defendant requests to retain another defender, the collegial panel shall announce an adjournment, and the defendant shall retain another defender or the People's Court shall appoint another defense lawyer for the defendant.

<a id="art-165"></a>第一百六十五条 被告人当庭拒绝辩护人为其辩护，要求另行委托辩护人的，应当同意，并宣布延期审理。被告人要求人民法院另行指定辩护律师，合议庭同意的，应当宣布延期审理。重新开庭后，被告人再次当庭拒绝重新委托的辩护人或者人民法院指定的辩护律师为其辩护的，合议庭应当分别情形作出处理：  
（一）被告人是成年人的，可以准许。但被告人不得再另行委托辩护人，人民法院也不再另行指定辩护律师，被告人可以自行辩护；  
（二）被告人具有[本解释第三十六条](#art-36)规定情形之一的，不予准许。  
依照[本解释第一百六十四条](#art-164)、本条第一、二款规定另行委托、指定辩护人或者辩护律师的，自案件宣布延期审理之日起至第十日止，准备辩护时间不计入审限。

**Article 165** If the defendant refuses in court to be defended by the defender and requests to retain another defender, the request shall be granted and an adjournment shall be announced. If the defendant requests the People's Court to appoint another defense lawyer and the collegial panel agrees, an adjournment shall be announced. After the trial resumes, if the defendant again refuses in court to be defended by the newly retained defender or the defense lawyer appointed by the People's Court, the collegial panel shall handle the matter according to the circumstances:  
(1) If the defendant is an adult, it may be permitted. However, the defendant shall not retain another defender, and the People's Court shall not appoint another defense lawyer; the defendant may defend himself or herself;  
(2) If the defendant falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 36 of this Interpretation, the request shall not be permitted.  
Where another defender or defense lawyer is retained or appointed in accordance with Article 164 and paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, the period from the date of the announcement of the adjournment to the tenth day for the preparation of the defense shall not be counted in the trial period.

<a id="art-166"></a>第一百六十六条 在法庭辩论过程中，如果合议庭发现新的事实，认为有必要进行调查时，审判长可以宣布暂停辩论，恢复法庭调查，待该事实查清后继续法庭辩论。

**Article 166** During the court debate, if the collegial panel discovers new facts and considers it necessary to investigate, the presiding judge may announce a suspension of the debate, resume the court investigation, and continue the court debate after the facts are clarified.

<a id="art-167"></a>第一百六十七条 审判长宣布法庭辩论终结后，合议庭应当保证被告人充分行使最后陈述的权利。如果被告人在最后陈述中多次重复白己的意见，审判长可以制止；如果陈述内容是蔑视法庭、公诉人，损害他人及社会公共利益或者与本案无关的，应当制止；在公开审理的案件中，被告人最后陈述的内容涉及国家秘密或者个人隐私的，也应当制止。

**Article 167** After the presiding judge announces the conclusion of the court debate, the collegial panel shall ensure that the defendant fully exercises the right to make a final statement. If the defendant repeatedly reiterates his or her own opinions in the final statement, the presiding judge may stop it; if the content of the statement is contemptuous of the court or the public prosecutor, harms the interests of others or the public, or is irrelevant to the case, it shall be stopped; in cases tried in public, if the content of the defendant's final statement involves state secrets or personal privacy, it shall also be stopped.

<a id="art-168"></a>第一百六十八条 被告人在最后陈述中提出了新的事实、证据，合议庭认为可能影响正确裁判的，应当恢复法庭调查；如果被告人提出新的辩解理由，合议庭认为确有必要的，可以恢复法庭辩论。

**Article 168** If the defendant raises new facts or evidence in the final statement, and the collegial panel considers that it may affect a correct judgment, it shall resume the court investigation; if the defendant raises new grounds for defense, and the collegial panel considers it truly necessary, it may resume the court debate.

<a id="art-169"></a>第一百六十九条 附带民事诉讼部分可以在法庭辩论结束后当庭调解。不能达成协议的，可以同刑事部分一并判决。

**Article 169** The collateral civil action part may be mediated in court after the conclusion of the court debate. If an agreement cannot be reached, a judgment may be rendered together with the criminal part.

<a id="art-170"></a>第一百七十条 审判长在被告人最后陈述后，应当宣布休庭，合议庭进行评议。

**Article 170** After the defendant's final statement, the presiding judge shall announce an adjournment for the collegial panel to conduct deliberations.

<a id="art-171"></a>第一百七十一条 开庭审理的全部活动，应当由书记员制作成笔录，经审判长审阅后，分别由审判长和书记员签名。

**Article 171** All activities of the trial shall be recorded in a record by the court clerk. After being reviewed by the presiding judge, the record shall be signed by the presiding judge and the court clerk respectively.

<a id="art-172"></a>第一百七十二条 法庭笔录中的出庭证人的证言部分，应当在庭审后交由证人阅读或者向其宣读。证人确认无误后，应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 172** The part of the court record containing the testimony of witnesses who appeared in court shall be shown to the witness for reading or read to the witness after the trial. After the witness confirms that it is correct, he or she shall sign or affix a seal.

<a id="art-173"></a>第一百七十三条 法庭笔录应当在庭审后交由当事人阅读或者向其宣读。当事人认为记录有遗漏或者有差错的，可以请求补充或者改正。当事人确认无误后，应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 173** The court record shall be shown to the parties for reading or read to them after the trial. If a party considers that the record contains omissions or errors, he or she may request supplementation or correction. After the party confirms that it is correct, he or she shall sign or affix a seal.

<a id="art-174"></a>第一百七十四条 对于当庭出示、宣读的证据，审判长宣布休庭后，合议庭应当与提供证据的公诉人、辩护人等办理交接手续。

**Article 174** For evidence presented or read in court, after the presiding judge announces an adjournment, the collegial panel shall complete the handover procedures with the public prosecutor, defender, or other persons who provided the evidence.

<a id="art-175"></a>第一百七十五条 合议庭应当根据已经查明的事实、证据和有关法律规定，并在充分考虑控辩双方意见的基础上，进行评议，确定被告人是否有罪，应否追究刑事责任；构成何罪，应否处以刑罚；判处何种刑罚；有无从重、从轻、减轻或者免除处罚的情节；附带民事诉讼如何解决；赃款赃物如何处理等，并依法作出判决。

**Article 175** The collegial panel shall, based on the facts that have been ascertained, the evidence, and the relevant legal provisions, and after fully considering the opinions of the prosecuting and defending parties, conduct deliberations to determine whether the defendant is guilty and whether criminal liability shall be investigated; what crime constitutes and whether a punishment shall be imposed; what type of punishment shall be imposed; whether there are circumstances for aggravated, mitigated, reduced, or exempted punishment; how the collateral civil action shall be resolved; how the illegal gains and stolen goods shall be dealt with; and shall render a judgment according to law.

<a id="art-176"></a>第一百七十六条 人民法院应当根据案件的具体情形，分别作出裁判：  
（一）起诉指控的事实清楚，证据确实、充分，依据法律认定被告人的罪名成立的，应当作出有罪判决；  
（二）起诉指控的事实清楚，证据确实、充分，指控的罪名与人民法院审理认定的罪名不一致的，应当作出有罪判决；  
（三）案件事实清楚，证据确实、充分，依据法律认定被告人无罪的，应当判决宣告被告人无罪；  
（四）证据不足，不能认定被告人有罪的，应当以证据不足，指控的犯罪不能成立，判决宣告被告人无罪；  
（五）案件事实部分清楚，证据确实、充分的，应当依法作出有罪或者无罪的判决；事实不清，证据不足部分，依法不予认定；  
（六）被告人因不满十六周岁，不予刑事处罚的，应当判决宣告被告人不负刑事责任；  
（七）被告人是精神病人，在不能辨认或者不能控制自己行为的时候造成危害结果，不予刑事处罚的，应当判决宣告被告人不负刑事责任；  
（八）犯罪已过追诉时效期限，并且不是必须追诉或者经特赦令免除刑罚的，应当裁定终止审理；  
（九）被告人死亡的，应当裁定终止审理；对于根据已查明的案件事实和认定的证据材料，能够确认被告人无罪的，应当判决宣告被告人无罪。

**Article 176** The People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances of the case, render judgments or rulings separately:  
(1) If the facts charged in the prosecution are clear, the evidence is credible and sufficient, and the defendant's guilt is established according to law, a guilty judgment shall be rendered;  
(2) If the facts charged in the prosecution are clear, the evidence is credible and sufficient, but the charged crime is inconsistent with the crime determined by the People's Court after trial, a guilty judgment shall be rendered;  
(3) If the facts of the case are clear, the evidence is credible and sufficient, and the defendant is determined to be innocent according to law, a judgment shall be rendered declaring the defendant not guilty;  
(4) If the evidence is insufficient and the defendant cannot be found guilty, a judgment shall be rendered declaring the defendant not guilty on the ground that the evidence is insufficient and the charged crime cannot be established;  
(5) If part of the facts of the case is clear and the evidence is credible and sufficient, a guilty or not guilty judgment shall be rendered according to law; the part where the facts are unclear and the evidence is insufficient shall not be determined according to law;  
(6) If the defendant is under the age of 16 and is not subject to criminal punishment, a judgment shall be rendered declaring that the defendant bears no criminal liability;  
(7) If the defendant is a mental patient who caused a harmful result when unable to recognize or control his or her own conduct, and is not subject to criminal punishment, a judgment shall be rendered declaring that the defendant bears no criminal liability;  
(8) If the crime has exceeded the statute of limitations for prosecution and is not necessarily subject to prosecution or is exempted from punishment by a special amnesty order, a ruling shall be made to terminate the trial;  
(9) If the defendant is deceased, a ruling shall be made to terminate the trial; if, based on the ascertained facts of the case and the identified evidentiary materials, the defendant can be confirmed to be innocent, a judgment shall be rendered declaring the defendant not guilty.

<a id="art-177"></a>第一百七十七条 在宣告判决前，人民检察院要求撤回起诉的，人民法院应当审查人民检察院撤回起诉的理由，并作出是否准许的裁定。

**Article 177** If, before the judgment is pronounced, the People's Procuratorate requests to withdraw the prosecution, the People's Court shall examine the reasons for the withdrawal and render a ruling on whether to permit it.

<a id="art-178"></a>第一百七十八条 人民法院在审理中发现新的事实，可能影响定罪的，应当建议人民检察院补充或者变更起诉；人民检察院不同意的，人民法院应当就起诉指控的犯罪事实，依照[本解释第一百七十六条](#art-176)的有关规定依法作出裁判。

**Article 178** If, during the trial, the People's Court discovers new facts that may affect the conviction, it shall recommend that the People's Procuratorate supplement or change the prosecution; if the People's Procuratorate does not agree, the People's Court shall render a judgment according to law on the criminal facts charged in the prosecution, in accordance with the relevant provisions of Article 176 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-179"></a>第一百七十九条 依据[本解释第一百一十七条](#art-117)第（三）项规定受理的案件，依法作出判决时，人民法院对于前案依据[刑事诉讼法第一百六十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-162)第（三）项规定作出的判决，不予撤销。但应当在判决中写明：“被告人×××曾于×年×月×日被××人民检察院以××罪向××人民法院提起公诉。因证据不足，指控的犯罪不能成立，被×人民法院依法判决宣告无罪。”

**Article 179** For a case accepted in accordance with the provisions of Article 117(3) of this Interpretation, when a judgment is made according to law, the People's Court shall not revoke the previous judgment rendered in accordance with Article 162(3) of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China. However, the following shall be stated in the judgment: "The defendant XXX was once prosecuted by the XXX People's Procuratorate on charges of XXX before the XXX People's Court on XXX (date). Because the evidence was insufficient and the charged crime could not be established, the defendant was declared not guilty by the XXX People's Court according to law."

<a id="art-180"></a>第一百八十条 合议庭成员应当在评议笔录上签名，在法律文书上署名。

**Article 180** Members of the collegial panel shall sign the deliberation record and affix their names on the legal documents.

<a id="art-181"></a>第一百八十一条 在审判过程中，自诉人或者被告人患精神病或者其他严重疾病，以及案件起诉到人民法院后被告人脱逃，致使案件在较长时间内无法继续审理的，人民法院应当裁定中止审理。  
由于其他不能抗拒的原因，使案件无法继续审理的，可以裁定中止审理。  
中止审理的原因消失后，应当恢复审理。中止审理的期间不计入审理期限。

**Article 181** If, during the trial, the private prosecutor or the defendant suffers from mental illness or other serious disease, or the defendant escapes after the case is brought to the People's Court, rendering the case unable to continue trial for a relatively long period, the People's Court shall render a ruling to suspend the trial.  
If the case cannot continue trial due to other irresistible reasons, a ruling may be made to suspend the trial.  
After the reasons for suspension disappear, the trial shall be resumed. The period of suspension shall not be counted in the trial period.

<a id="art-182"></a>第一百八十二条 当庭宣告判决的，应当宣布判决结果，并在五日内将判决书送达当事人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人、提起公诉的人民检察院、辩护人和被告人的近亲属。定期宣告判决的，合议庭应当在宣判前，先期公告言判的时间和地点，传唤当事人并通知公诉人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人和辩护人；判决宣告后应当立即将判决书送达当事人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人、提起公诉的人民检察院、辩护人和被告人的近亲属。判决生效后还应当送达被告人的所在单位或者原户籍所在地的公安派出所。被告人是单位的，应当送达被告人注册登记的工商行政管理机关

**Article 182** If a judgment is pronounced in court, the result of the judgment shall be announced and the written judgment shall be served on the parties, their legal representatives, litigation agents, the People's Procuratorate that initiated the public prosecution, the defender, and the close relatives of the defendant within five days. If a judgment is pronounced at a scheduled session, the collegial panel shall, before pronouncing the judgment, announce in advance the time and place of the pronouncement, summon the parties, and notify the public prosecutor, legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders; after the judgment is pronounced, the written judgment shall be immediately served on the parties, their legal representatives, litigation agents, the People's Procuratorate that initiated the public prosecution, the defender, and the close relatives of the defendant. After the judgment takes effect, it shall also be served on the defendant's work unit or the public security police station at the defendant's original registered permanent residence. If the defendant is a unit, it shall be served on the administrative authority for industry and commerce where the defendant is registered.

<a id="art-183"></a>第一百八十三条 宣告判决，应当一律公开进行。  
宣告判决时，法庭内全体人员应当起立。  
宣判时，公诉人、辩护人、被害人、自诉人或者附带民事诉讼的原告人未到庭的，不影响宣判的进行。

**Article 183** Pronouncement of a judgment shall be conducted in open court in all cases.  
When a judgment is pronounced, all persons present in the courtroom shall stand.  
If the public prosecutor, defender, victim, private prosecutor, or plaintiff in an incidental civil action is not present at the pronouncement, it shall not affect the proceeding of the pronouncement.

<a id="art-184"></a>第一百八十四条 在法庭审判过程中，如果诉讼参与人或者旁听人员违反法庭秩序，合议庭应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）对于违反法庭秩序情节较轻的，应当当庭警告制止并进行训诫；  
（二）对于不听警告制止的，可以指令法警强行带出法庭；  
（三）对于违反法庭秩序情节严重的，经报请院长批准后，对行为人处1000元以下的罚款或者十五日以下的拘留；  
（四）对于严重扰乱法庭秩序，构成犯罪的，应当依法追究刑事责任。  
当事人对人民法院罚款、拘留的决定不服，可以向上一级人民法院申请复议。复议申请可以直接向土一级人民法院提出，也可以通过作出罚款、拘留决定的人民法院提出。通过作出罚款、拘留决定的人民法院向上一级人民法院申请复议的，该人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起三日内，将申请人的复议申请、罚款或者拘留决定书和有关事实、证据材料一并报上一级人民法院复议。上一级人民法院复议期间，不停止决定的执行。

**Article 184** If, during the court trial, any participant in the proceedings or any person in attendance violates the order of the courtroom, the collegial panel shall deal with the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the violation of courtroom order is minor, the violator shall be warned and stopped in court and be given a reprimand;  
(2) If the violator refuses to heed the warning, the judge may instruct the judicial police to forcibly remove him or her from the courtroom;  
(3) If the violation of courtroom order is serious, after reporting to and obtaining the approval of the president of the court, the violator shall be fined not more than 1,000 yuan or detained for not more than fifteen days;  
(4) If the courtroom order is seriously disrupted and a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
If a party disagrees with the decision of the People's Court to impose a fine or detention, the party may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level. An application for reconsideration may be submitted directly to the People's Court at the next higher level, or may be submitted through the People's Court that made the decision on the fine or detention. If the application for reconsideration is submitted through the People's Court that made the decision on the fine or detention, that court shall, within three days from the date of receiving the application, submit the applicant's reconsideration application, the written decision on the fine or detention, and relevant facts and evidence materials to the People's Court at the next higher level for reconsideration. During the period of reconsideration by the People's Court at the next higher level, the execution of the decision shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-185"></a>第一百八十五条 人民检察院认为人民法院审理案件过程中，有违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情况，在庭审后提出书面纠正意见的，人民法院认为正确的，应当采纳。  
十、自诉案件第一审程序

**Article 185** If the People's Procuratorate believes that there have been circumstances in the course of a trial by the People's Court that violate the litigation procedures prescribed by law and submits written corrective opinions after the trial, the People's Court shall, if it finds the opinions correct, adopt them.  
X. Procedure for First Instance of Private Prosecution Cases

<a id="art-186"></a>第一百八十六条 人民法院受理的自诉案件必须符合下列条件：  
（一）属于[刑事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-170)、[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的案件；  
（二）属于本院管辖的；  
（三）刑事案件的被害人告诉的；  
（四）有明确的被告人、具体的诉讼请求和能证明被告人犯罪事实的证据。  
人民法院受理[刑事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-170)第（三）项规定的自诉案件，还应当符合[刑事诉讼法第八十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-86)、第一百四十五条的规定。

**Article 186** A private prosecution case accepted by the People's Court must meet the following conditions:  
(1) It is a case provided for in Article 170 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 1 of this Interpretation;  
(2) It is within the jurisdiction of that court;  
(3) It is brought by the victim of a criminal case;  
(4) There is a clear defendant, specific claims, and evidence that can prove the criminal facts of the defendant.  
For a private prosecution case as provided for in Article 170, item (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law accepted by the People's Court, it must also meet the provisions of Articles 86 and 145 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-187"></a>第一百八十七条 [本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的案件，如果被害人死亡、丧失行为能力或者因受强制、威吓等原因无法告诉，或者是限制行为能力人以及由于年老、患病、盲、聋、哑等原因不能亲自告诉，其法定代理人、近亲属代为告诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
因前款规定的原因，被害人不能告诉，由其法定代理人、近亲属代为告诉的，代为告诉人应当提供与被害人关系的证明和被害人不能亲自告诉的原因的证明。

**Article 187** For cases provided for in Article 1 of this Interpretation, if the victim has died, has lost capacity for civil conduct, or is unable to initiate a private prosecution because of coercion, intimidation, or other reasons, or if the victim is a person with limited capacity for civil conduct or is unable to personally initiate a private prosecution because of old age, illness, blindness, deafness, muteness, or other reasons, and his or her legal representative or close relatives initiate a private prosecution on his or her behalf, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
Where the victim is unable to initiate a private prosecution due to the reasons specified in the preceding paragraph, and his or her legal representative or close relatives initiate a private prosecution on his or her behalf, the person initiating the prosecution on behalf of the victim shall provide proof of the relationship with the victim and proof of the reasons why the victim is unable to personally initiate the private prosecution.

<a id="art-188"></a>第一百八十八条 对于自诉案件，人民法院经审查有下列情形之一的，应当说服自诉人撤回起诉，或者裁定驳回起诉：  
（一）不符合[本解释第一百八十六条](#art-186)规定的条件的；  
（二）证据不充分的；  
（三）犯罪已过追诉时效期限的；  
（四）被告人死亡的；  
（五）被告人下落不明的；  
（六）除因证据不足而撤诉的以外，自诉人撤诉后，就同一事实又告诉的；  
（七）经人民法院调解结案后，自诉人反悔，就同一事实再行告诉的。

**Article 188** For a private prosecution case, if after examination the People's Court finds any of the following circumstances, it shall persuade the private prosecutor to withdraw the prosecution, or rule to dismiss the prosecution:  
(1) The conditions provided for in Article 186 of this Interpretation are not met;  
(2) The evidence is insufficient;  
(3) The statute of limitations for prosecution has expired;  
(4) The defendant is deceased;  
(5) The defendant's whereabouts are unknown;  
(6) Except where the withdrawal was due to insufficient evidence, after the private prosecutor withdraws the prosecution, he or she initiates another prosecution based on the same facts;  
(7) After the case has been concluded through mediation by the People's Court, the private prosecutor reneges and initiates another prosecution based on the same facts.

<a id="art-189"></a>第一百八十九条 自诉人应当向人民法院提交刑事自诉状；提起附带民事诉讼的，还应当提交刑事附带民事自诉状。  
自诉入书写自诉状确有困难的，可以口头告诉，由人民法院工作人员作出告诉笔录，向自诉人宣读，自诉人确认无误后，应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 189** The private prosecutor shall submit a written private prosecution complaint to the People's Court; if an incidental civil action is also initiated, he or she shall also submit a written private prosecution complaint with incidental civil action.  
If the private prosecutor genuinely has difficulty in writing the private prosecution complaint, he or she may initiate the prosecution orally, and the court staff shall make a record of the oral complaint, read it to the private prosecutor, and after the private prosecutor confirms that it is correct, he or she shall sign or affix his or her seal.

<a id="art-190"></a>第一百九十条 自诉状或者告诉笔录应当包括以下内容：  
（一）自诉人、被告人、代为告诉人的姓名、性别、年龄、民族、出生地、文化程度、职业、工作单位、住址；  
（二）被告人犯罪行为的时间、地点、手段、情节和危害后果等；  
（三）具体的诉讼请求；  
（四）致送人民法院的名称及具状时间；  
（五）证人的姓名、住址及其他证据的名称、来源等。  
如果被告人是二人以上的，自诉人在告诉时需按被告人的人数提供自诉状副本。

**Article 190** The private prosecution complaint or the record of oral complaint shall include the following:  
(1) The name, gender, age, nationality, place of birth, educational level, occupation, work unit, and address of the private prosecutor, the defendant, and any person who initiates the prosecution on behalf of the private prosecutor;  
(2) The time, place, means, circumstances, and harmful consequences of the defendant's criminal act;  
(3) The specific claims;  
(4) The name of the People's Court to which the complaint is addressed and the date of filing;  
(5) The names and addresses of witnesses, and the names and sources of other evidence.  
If there are two or more defendants, the private prosecutor shall, when initiating the prosecution, provide copies of the private prosecution complaint equal to the number of defendants.

<a id="art-191"></a>第一百九十一条 人民法院应当在收到自诉状或者口头告诉第二日起十五日内作出是否立案的决定，并书面通知自诉人或者代为告诉人。

**Article 191** The People's Court shall, within fifteen days from the day after receiving the written private prosecution complaint or oral complaint, make a decision on whether to open a case, and shall notify the private prosecutor or the person initiating the prosecution on behalf of the private prosecutor in writing.

<a id="art-192"></a>第一百九十二条 对于已经立案，经审查缺乏罪证的自诉案件，如果自诉人提不出补充证据，应当说服自诉人撤回起诉或者裁定驳回起诉；自诉人经说服撤回起诉或者被驳回起诉后，又提出了新的足以证明被告人有罪的证据，再次提起自诉的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 192** For a private prosecution case that has already been opened and, after examination, is found to lack evidence of a crime, if the private prosecutor is unable to provide supplementary evidence, the People's Court shall persuade the private prosecutor to withdraw the prosecution or rule to dismiss the prosecution; if, after being persuaded to withdraw the prosecution or after the prosecution is dismissed, the private prosecutor presents new evidence sufficient to prove the defendant's guilt and initiates a private prosecution again, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-193"></a>第一百九十三条 自诉人明知有其他共同侵害人，但只对部分侵害人提起自诉的，人民法院应当受理，并视为自诉人对其他侵害人放弃告诉权利。判决宣告后自诉人又对其他共同侵害人就同一事实提起自诉的，人民法院不再受理。共同被害人中只有部分人告诉的，人民法院应当通知其他被害人参加诉讼。被通知人接到通知后表示不参加诉讼或者不出庭的，即视为放弃告诉权利。第一审宣判后，被通知人就同一事实又提起自诉的，人民法院不予受理。但当事人另行提起民事诉讼的，不受本解释限制。

**Article 193** If the private prosecutor knows that there are other joint infringers but only initiates a private prosecution against some of them, the People's Court shall accept the case and shall deem that the private prosecutor has waived the right to prosecute the other infringers. After the judgment is pronounced, if the private prosecutor initiates a private prosecution against the other joint infringers based on the same facts, the People's Court shall no longer accept the case. If only some of the joint victims initiate a private prosecution, the People's Court shall notify the other victims to participate in the proceedings. If the notified person indicates, after receiving the notice, that he or she will not participate in the proceedings or will not appear in court, he or she shall be deemed to have waived the right to prosecute. After the first instance judgment is pronounced, if the notified person initiates a private prosecution based on the same facts, the People's Court shall not accept the case. However, if the party separately initiates a civil action, it shall not be restricted by this Interpretation.

<a id="art-194"></a>第一百九十四条 被告人实施的两个以上的犯罪行为，分别属于公诉案件和自诉案件的，人民法院可以在审理公诉案件时，对自诉案件一并审理。

**Article 194** If two or more criminal acts committed by the defendant are respectively a public prosecution case and a private prosecution case, the People's Court may, while trying the public prosecution case, try the private prosecution case together.

<a id="art-195"></a>第一百九十五条 人民法院受理自诉案件后，对于当事人因客观原因不能取得并提供有关证据而申请人民法院调取证据，人民法院认为必要的，可以依法调取。

**Article 195** After accepting a private prosecution case, if a party is unable to obtain and provide relevant evidence due to objective reasons and applies to the People's Court to collect such evidence, the People's Court may, if it deems it necessary, collect the evidence according to law.

<a id="art-196"></a>第一百九十六条 人民法院对于决定受理的自诉案件，应当开庭审判。不适用简易程序审理的，审判程序参照公诉案件第一审程序的规定进行。

**Article 196** The People's Court shall hold a court session to try a private prosecution case that it decides to accept. If the case is not tried under the summary procedure, the trial procedure shall be conducted by reference to the provisions on the procedure for first instance of public prosecution cases.

<a id="art-197"></a>第一百九十七条 人民法院对告诉才处理和被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件，可以在查明事实、分清是非的基础上进行调解。自诉人在宣告判决前可以同被告人自行和解或者撤回起诉。

**Article 197** For a case that is to be handled only upon complaint and a minor criminal case for which the victim has evidence to prove, the People's Court may conduct mediation on the basis of ascertaining the facts and distinguishing right from wrong. The private prosecutor may, before the judgment is pronounced, reach a voluntary settlement with the defendant or withdraw the prosecution.

<a id="art-198"></a>第一百九十八条 对于自诉人要求撤诉的，经人民法院审查认为确属自愿的，应当准许；经审查认为自诉人系被强迫、威吓等，不是出于自愿的，应当不予准许。

**Article 198** If the private prosecutor requests to withdraw the prosecution, and the People's Court, after examination, finds that the request is genuinely voluntary, it shall grant permission; if, after examination, the People's Court finds that the private prosecutor was coerced, intimidated, or otherwise not acting voluntarily, it shall not grant permission.

<a id="art-199"></a>第一百九十九条 对于已经审理的自诉案件，当事人自行和解的，应当记录在卷。

**Article 199** For a private prosecution case that has already been tried, if the parties reach a voluntary settlement, it shall be recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-200"></a>第二百条 调解应当在自愿、合法，不损害国家、集体和其他公民利益的前提下进行。调解达成协议的，人民法院应当制作刑事自诉案件调解书，由审判人员和书记员署名，并加盖人民法院印章。调解书经双方当事人签收后即发生法律效力。调解没有达成协议或者调解书签收前当事人反悔的，人民法院应当进行判决。

**Article 200** Mediation shall be conducted on the premise of voluntariness, legality, and not harming the interests of the State, the collective, and other citizens. If an agreement is reached through mediation, the People's Court shall prepare a written mediation agreement for the criminal private prosecution case, which shall be signed by the adjudicator and the court clerk, and sealed with the seal of the People's Court. The mediation agreement shall take legal effect after being signed and received by both parties. If no agreement is reached through mediation, or if a party reneges before signing and receiving the mediation agreement, the People's Court shall render a judgment.

<a id="art-201"></a>第二百零一条 人民法院裁定准许自诉人撤诉或者当事人自行和解的案件，被告人被采取强制措施的，应当立即予以解除。

**Article 201** For a case in which the People's Court rules to permit the private prosecutor to withdraw the prosecution or the parties reach a voluntary settlement, if compulsory measures have been taken against the defendant, they shall be immediately rescinded.

<a id="art-202"></a>第二百零二条 自诉人经两次依法传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，或者未经法庭准许中途退庭的，人民法院应当决定按自诉人撤诉处理。  
自诉人是二人以上，其中部分人撤诉的，不影响案件的继续审理。

**Article 202** If the private prosecutor, after being lawfully summoned twice, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, or leaves the courtroom midway without the permission of the court, the People's Court shall decide to treat the case as withdrawal of the prosecution by the private prosecutor.  
If there are two or more private prosecutors and some of them withdraw the prosecution, it shall not affect the continued trial of the case.

<a id="art-203"></a>第二百零三条 对于[刑事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-170)第（三）项规定的案件，不适用调解。

**Article 203** For a case provided for in Article 170, item (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law, mediation shall not apply.

<a id="art-204"></a>第二百零四条 在自诉案件审理过程中，被告人下落不明的，应当中止审理。被告人归案后，应当恢复审理，必要时，应当对被告人依法采取强制措施。

**Article 204** If, during the trial of a private prosecution case, the defendant's whereabouts are unknown, the trial shall be suspended. After the defendant is brought to justice, the trial shall be resumed; if necessary, compulsory measures shall be taken against the defendant according to law.

<a id="art-205"></a>第二百零五条 审理自诉案件，应当参照[刑事诉讼法第一百六十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-162)和[本解释第一百七十六条](#art-176)的有关规定作出判决。对于依法宣告无罪的案件，其附带民事诉讼部分应当依法进行调解或者一并作出判决。

**Article 205** When trying a private prosecution case, the judgment shall be rendered by reference to the relevant provisions of Article 162 of the Criminal Procedure Law and Article 176 of this Interpretation. For a case where the defendant is declared not guilty according to law, the incidental civil action part shall be mediated according to law or a judgment shall be rendered together with the criminal part.

<a id="art-206"></a>第二百零六条 告诉才处理和被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件的被告人或者其法定代理人在诉讼过程中，可以对自诉人提起反诉。反诉必须符合下列条件：  
（一）反诉的对象必须是本案自诉人；  
（二）反诉的内容必须是与本案有关的行为；  
（三）反诉的案件必须符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（一）、（二）项的规定。  
反诉案件适用自诉案件的规定，并应当与自诉案件一并审理。原自诉人撤诉的，不影响反诉案件的继续审理。  
十一、单位犯罪案件的审理程序

**Article 206** In a case that is to be handled only upon complaint or a minor criminal case for which the victim has evidence to prove, the defendant or his or her legal representative may, during the proceedings, file a counterclaim against the private prosecutor. A counterclaim must meet the following conditions:  
(1) The object of the counterclaim must be the private prosecutor in the current case;  
(2) The content of the counterclaim must be an act related to the current case;  
(3) The counterclaim case must meet the provisions of items (1) and (2) of Article 1 of this Interpretation.  
A counterclaim case shall be governed by the provisions on private prosecution cases and shall be tried together with the private prosecution case. If the original private prosecutor withdraws the prosecution, it shall not affect the continued trial of the counterclaim case.  
XI. Procedure for Trial of Unit Crime Cases

<a id="art-207"></a>第二百零七条 人民法院受理单位犯罪案件，除依照[本解释第一百一十六条](#art-116)的有关规定进行审查外，还应当审查起诉书中是否列明被告单位的名称、住所地，以及代表被告单位出庭的诉讼代表人的姓名、职务、通讯处。未按规定列明的，应当按[本解释第一百一十七条](#art-117)第（二）项的规定办理。

**Article 207** When accepting a unit crime case, in addition to conducting examination in accordance with the relevant provisions of Article 116 of this Interpretation, the People's Court shall also examine whether the indictment specifies the name and domicile of the defendant unit, and the name, position, and address of the litigation representative who will appear in court on behalf of the defendant unit. If such information is not specified as required, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 117, item (2) of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-208"></a>第二百零八条 代表被告单位出庭的诉讼代表人，应当是单位的法定代表人或者主要负责人；法定代表人或者主要负责人被指控为单位犯罪直接负责的主管人员的，应当由单位的其他负责人作为被告单位的诉讼代表人出庭。  
被告单位的诉讼代表人与被指控为单位犯罪直接负责的主管人员是同一人的，人民法院应当要求人民检察院另行确定被告单位的诉讼代表人出庭。

**Article 208** The litigation representative who appears in court on behalf of the defendant unit shall be the legal representative or principal responsible person of the unit; if the legal representative or principal responsible person is charged as the person directly in charge of the unit crime, another responsible person of the unit shall act as the litigation representative of the defendant unit to appear in court.  
If the litigation representative of the defendant unit is the same person as the person charged as directly in charge of the unit crime, the People's Court shall request the People's Procuratorate to separately determine another litigation representative of the defendant unit to appear in court.

<a id="art-209"></a>第二百零九条 人民法院决定开庭审理单位犯罪案件，应当通知被告单位的诉讼代表人出庭。

**Article 209** If the People's Court decides to open a court session to try a unit crime case, it shall notify the litigation representative of the defendant unit to appear in court.

<a id="art-210"></a>第二百一十条 接到出庭通知的被告单位的诉讼代表人应当出庭。拒不出庭的，人民法院在必要的时候，可以拘传到庭。

**Article 210** The litigation representative of the defendant unit who receives the notice to appear in court shall appear in court. If he or she refuses to appear in court, the People's Court may, when necessary, issue a warrant to compel his or her appearance.

<a id="art-211"></a>第二百一十一条 人民法院审理单位犯罪案件，被告单位的诉讼代表人享有刑事诉讼法规定的有关被告人的诉讼权利。开庭时，诉讼代表人席位置于审判台前左侧。

**Article 211** When the People's Court tries a unit crime case, the litigation representative of the defendant unit shall enjoy the litigation rights of the defendant as provided for in the Criminal Procedure Law. During the court session, the seat of the litigation representative shall be placed on the left side in front of the judge's bench.

<a id="art-212"></a>第二百一十二条 被告单位需要委托辩护人的，参照本解释有关辩护的规定办理。

**Article 212** If the defendant unit needs to retain a defender, the matter shall be handled by reference to the provisions of this Interpretation concerning defense.

<a id="art-213"></a>第二百一十三条 被告单位的违法所得及其产生的收益，尚未依法追缴或者扣押、冻结的，人民法院应当根据案件具体情况，决定追缴或者扣押、冻结。

**Article 213** If the illegal gains of the defendant unit and the income derived therefrom have not yet been recovered, seized, or frozen according to law, the People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances of the case, decide to recover, seize, or freeze them.

<a id="art-214"></a>第二百一十四条 人民法院为了保证判决的执行，根据案件具体情况，可以先行扣押、冻结被告单位的财产或者由被告单位提出担保。

**Article 214** To ensure the enforcement of the judgment, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, first seize or freeze the property of the defendant unit, or require the defendant unit to provide a guarantee.

<a id="art-215"></a>第二百一十五条 人民法院审理单位犯罪案件，被告单位被注销或者宣告破产，但单位犯罪直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员应当负刑事责任的，应当继续审理。

**Article 215** When the People's Court tries a unit crime case, if the defendant unit has been cancelled or declared bankrupt, but the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible for the unit crime shall bear criminal liability, the trial shall continue.

<a id="art-216"></a>第二百一十六条 审理单位犯罪案件的其他程序，参照本解释的有关规定办理。  
十二、简易程序

**Article 216** Other procedures for trying unit crime cases shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.  
XII. Summary Procedure

<a id="art-217"></a>第二百一十七条 基层人民法院受理的公诉案件，人民检察院在起诉时书面建议适用简易程序的，应当随案移送全案卷宗和证据材料。人民法院经审查认为符合[刑事诉讼法第一百七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-174)第（一）项规定的，可以适用简易程序；认为依法不应当适用简易程序的，应当书面通知人民检察院，并将全案卷宗和证据材料退回人民检察院。

**Article 217** For a public prosecution case accepted by a Primary People's Court, if the People's Procuratorate, when initiating the prosecution, makes a written recommendation to apply the summary procedure, it shall transfer the entire case file and evidence materials together with the case. If, after examination, the People's Court finds that the case meets the provisions of Article 174, item (1) of the Criminal Procedure Law, it may apply the summary procedure; if it finds that the summary procedure should not be applied according to law, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate in writing and return the entire case file and evidence materials to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-218"></a>第二百一十八条 对于公诉案件，人民检察院移送起诉时没有建议适用简易程序，人民法院经审查认为符合[刑事诉讼法第一百七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-174)第（一）项规定，拟适用简易程序审理的，应当书面征求人民检察院的意见。人民检察院同意并移送全案卷宗和证据材料后，应当适用简易程序。

**Article 218** For a public prosecution case, if the People's Procuratorate, when transferring the case for prosecution, does not recommend the application of the summary procedure, but the People's Court, after examination, finds that the case meets the provisions of Article 174, item (1) of the Criminal Procedure Law and intends to try the case under the summary procedure, it shall seek the opinion of the People's Procuratorate in writing. After the People's Procuratorate agrees and transfers the entire case file and evidence materials, the summary procedure shall be applied.

<a id="art-219"></a>第二百一十九条 告诉才处理的案件和被害人有证据证明的轻微刑事案件，人民法院可以适用简易程序。但[刑事诉讼法第一百七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-170)第（三）项规定的案件除外。

**Article 219** The People's Court may apply the summary procedure to cases that are to be handled only upon complaint and minor criminal cases for which the victim has evidence to prove. However, cases provided for in Article 170, item (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law are excepted.

<a id="art-220"></a>第二百二十条 [刑事诉讼法第一百七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-174)第（一）项规定的可能判处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制、单处罚金的公诉案件，是指被告人被指控的一罪或者数罪，可能被宣告判处的刑罚为三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制、单处罚金的案件。

**Article 220** A public prosecution case that may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or a fine only, as provided for in Article 174, item (1) of the Criminal Procedure Law, refers to a case where the defendant is charged with one or more crimes and the possible punishment to be pronounced is fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or a fine only.

<a id="art-221"></a>第二百二十一条 人民法院对公诉案件的被告人可能判处免予刑事处分的，可以适用简易程序。

**Article 221** If a defendant in a public prosecution case may be sentenced to exemption from criminal punishment, the People's Court may apply the summary procedure.

<a id="art-222"></a>第二百二十二条 人民法院审理具有以下情形之一的案件，不应当适用简易程序：  
（一）公诉案件的被告人对于起诉指控的犯罪事实予以否认的；  
（二）比较复杂的共同犯罪案件；  
（三）被告人是盲、聋、哑人的；  
（四）辩护人作无罪辩护的；  
（五）其他不宜适用简易程序的。

**Article 222** The People's Court shall not apply the summary procedure when trying a case that has any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The defendant in a public prosecution case denies the criminal facts charged in the indictment;  
(2) It is a relatively complex joint crime case;  
(3) The defendant is blind, deaf, or mute;  
(4) The defender makes a defense of not guilty;  
(5) Other circumstances where the summary procedure is not appropriate to apply.

<a id="art-223"></a>第二百二十三条 人民法院决定适用简易程序审理案件，应当在向被告人送达起诉书副本的同时，告知该案适用简易程序审理。  
适用简易程序审理的案件，送达起诉书至开庭审判的时间，不受刑事诉讼法第百五十一条第（二）项规定的限制。

**Article 223** When the People's Court decides to try a case under the summary procedure, it shall, at the same time as serving the copy of the indictment on the defendant, inform the defendant that the case is to be tried under the summary procedure.  
For a case tried under the summary procedure, the time from service of the indictment to the opening of the court session shall not be subject to the restriction of Article 151, item (2) of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-224"></a>第二百二十四条 适用简易程序审理的案件，在开庭审判前，人民法院应当将开庭的时问、地点通知人民检察院、自诉人、被告人、辩护人，也可以通知其他诉讼参与人。  
通知可以用简便方式，但应当记录在卷。

**Article 224** For a case tried under the summary procedure, before the opening of the court session, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate, the private prosecutor, the defendant, and the defender of the time and place of the court session, and may also notify other participants in the proceedings.  
The notification may be made by simple means, but shall be recorded in the case file.

<a id="art-225"></a>第二百二十五条 适用简易程序审理的案件，审判员宣布开庭，传被告人到庭后，应当查明被告人的基本情况，然后依次宣布案由、独任审判员、书记员、公诉人、辩护人、诉讼代理人、鉴定人和翻译人员的名单，并告知各项诉讼权利。  
被告人可以就起诉书指控的犯罪事实进行陈述和辩护。审判员可以出示、宣读主要证据，并听取被告人的意见。如果公诉人出庭支持公诉，在被告人陈述后，公诉人可以出示、宣读主要证据。经审判员准许，被告人及其辩护人可以同公诉人进行辩论。  
审判员在必要时，可以讯问被告人。  
被告人作最后陈述后，人民法院一般应当当庭宣判。

**Article 225** For a case tried under the summary procedure, after the judge announces the opening of the court session and the defendant is brought into court, the judge shall ascertain the basic information of the defendant, then announce in order the cause of action, the name of the sole judge, the court clerk, the public prosecutor, the defender, the litigation agent, the expert evaluator, and the interpreter, and inform the parties of their litigation rights.  
The defendant may make a statement and defense regarding the criminal facts charged in the indictment. The judge may present and read out the main evidence and hear the defendant's opinions. If the public prosecutor appears in court to support the public prosecution, after the defendant's statement, the public prosecutor may present and read out the main evidence. With the permission of the judge, the defendant and his or her defender may debate with the public prosecutor.  
When necessary, the judge may interrogate the defendant.  
After the defendant makes the final statement, the People's Court shall generally pronounce the judgment in court.

<a id="art-226"></a>第二百二十六条 适用简易程序审理的案件，被告人委托辩护人的，辩护人可以不出庭，但应当在开庭审判前将书面辩护意见送交人民法院。

**Article 226** For a case tried under the summary procedure, if the defendant has entrusted a defender, the defender may not appear in court, but shall submit the written defense opinion to the People's Court before the opening of the court session.

<a id="art-227"></a>第二百二十七条 被告人、自诉人要求证人出庭的，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 227** If the defendant or the private prosecutor requests a witness to appear in court, the People's Court may grant the request.

<a id="art-228"></a>第二百二十八条 适用简易程序审理的自诉案件，自诉人宣读起诉书后，被告人可以就起诉书指控的犯罪事实进行陈述，并自行辩护。自诉人应当出示主要证据。被告人有证据出示的，审判员应当许。经审判员准许，被告人及其辩护人可以同自诉人及其诉讼代理人进行辩论。

**Article 228** For a private prosecution case tried under the summary procedure, after the private prosecutor reads out the indictment, the defendant may make a statement regarding the criminal facts charged in the indictment and defend himself or herself. The private prosecutor shall present the main evidence. If the defendant has evidence to present, the judge shall permit it. With the permission of the judge, the defendant and his or her defender may debate with the private prosecutor and his or her litigation agent.

<a id="art-229"></a>第二百二十九条 适用简易程序审理的案件，在法庭审理过程中，发现以下不宜适用简易程序情形的，应当决定中止审理，并按照公诉案件或者自诉案件的第一审普通程序重新审理：  
（一）公诉案件被告人的行为不构成犯罪的；  
（二）公诉案件被告人应当判处三年以上有期徒刑的；  
（三）公诉案件被告人当庭翻供，对于起诉指控的犯罪事实予以否认的；  
（四）事实不清或者证据不充分的；  
（五）其他依法不应当或者不宜适用简易程序的。

**Article 229** For a case tried under the summary procedure, if any of the following circumstances where the summary procedure is not appropriate is discovered during the court trial, the trial shall be suspended and the case shall be retried under the ordinary procedure of first instance for public prosecution cases or private prosecution cases:  
(1) The act of the defendant in a public prosecution case does not constitute a crime;  
(2) The defendant in a public prosecution case shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years;  
(3) The defendant in a public prosecution case retracts his or her confession in court and denies the criminal facts charged in the indictment;  
(4) The facts are unclear or the evidence is insufficient;  
(5) Other circumstances where the summary procedure should not or is not appropriate to be applied according to law.

<a id="art-230"></a>第二百三十条 转为普通程序审理的案件，审理期限应当从决定转为普通程序之日起计算。  
十三、第二审程序

**Article 230** For a case that is converted to the ordinary procedure for trial, the trial period shall be calculated from the date on which the decision to convert to the ordinary procedure is made.  
XIII. Procedure for Second Instance

<a id="art-231"></a>第二百三十一条 第二审人民法院受理不服第一审人民法院判决或者裁定提出上诉的和人民检察院认为第一审人民法院判决或者裁定确有错误提出抗诉的案件。

**Article 231** The People's Court of second instance shall accept cases where an appeal is filed against a judgment or ruling of a People's Court of first instance, and cases where the People's Procuratorate files a protest on the ground that a judgment or ruling of a People's Court of first instance is indeed erroneous.

<a id="art-232"></a>第二百三十二条 地方各级人民法院在宣告第一审判决、裁定时，应当明确告知被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的当事人和他们的法定代理人，如果不服判决或者裁定，有权在法定期限内以书状或者口头形式向上一级人民法院提出上诉；被告人的辩护人和近亲属，在法定期限内经被告人同意，也可以提出上诉；附带民事诉讼的当事人和他们的法定代理人，可以对判决或者裁定中的附带民事部分，提出上诉。  
被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的当事人和他们的法定代理人是否提出上诉，以他们在上诉期满前最后一次的意思表示为准。

**Article 232** When announcing a judgment or ruling of first instance, local People's Courts at all levels shall clearly inform the defendant, the private prosecutor, the parties to an incidental civil action, and their legal representatives that, if they disagree with the judgment or ruling, they have the right to file an appeal with the People's Court at the next higher level in writing or orally within the statutory time limit; the defendant's defender and close relatives may also file an appeal within the statutory time limit with the consent of the defendant; the parties to an incidental civil action and their legal representatives may file an appeal against the incidental civil part of the judgment or ruling.  
Whether the defendant, the private prosecutor, the parties to an incidental civil action, and their legal representatives file an appeal shall be determined by their last expression of intention before the expiration of the appeal period.

<a id="art-233"></a>第二百三十三条 人民法院受理的上诉案件，一般应当有上诉状正本及副本。  
上诉状内容应当包括：第一市判决书、裁定书的文号和上诉人收到的时间；第一审法院的名称；上诉的请求和理由；提出上诉的时间；上诉人签名或者盖章。如果是被告人的辩护人、近亲属经被告人同意提出上诉的，还应当写明提出上诉的人与被告人的关系，并应当以被告人作为上诉人。

**Article 233** For an appeal case accepted by the People's Court, there shall generally be an original and copies of the written appeal.  
The content of the written appeal shall include: the number of the judgment or ruling of first instance and the date on which the appellant received it; the name of the court of first instance; the request and grounds for appeal; the date on which the appeal is filed; and the signature or seal of the appellant. If the appeal is filed by the defendant's defender or close relative with the defendant's consent, the relationship between the person filing the appeal and the defendant shall also be stated, and the defendant shall be named as the appellant.

<a id="art-234"></a>第二百三十四条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼原告人和被告人因书写上诉状确有困难而口头提出上诉的，第一审人民法院应当根据其所陈述的理由和请求制作笔录，由上诉人阅读或者向其宣读后，上诉人应当签名或者盖章。

**Article 234** If the defendant, the private prosecutor, the plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action, due to genuine difficulty in writing a written appeal, files an oral appeal, the People's Court of first instance shall make a record of the reasons and requests stated, and after the appellant reads or hears the record, the appellant shall sign or affix his or her seal.

<a id="art-235"></a>第二百三十五条 第二审人民法院受理的上诉和抗诉案件，必须是在法定期限内提出的。不服判决的上诉和抗诉的期限为十日；不服裁定的上诉和抗诉的期限为五日。上诉和抗诉的期限，从接到判决书、裁定书的第二日起计算。

**Article 235** Appeals and protests accepted by the second instance People's Court must be filed within the statutory time limit. The time limit for appeal or protest against a judgment shall be ten days; the time limit for appeal or protest against a ruling shall be five days. The time limit for appeal or protest shall be calculated from the day after receipt of the judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-236"></a>第二百三十六条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人通过第一审人民法院提出上诉的，第一审人民法院应当审查上诉是否符合法律规定。符合法律规定的，应当在上诉期满后三日内将上诉状连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，同时将上诉状副本送交同级人民检察院和对方当事人。

**Article 236** Where a defendant, private prosecutor, or plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action files an appeal through the first instance People's Court, the first instance People's Court shall review whether the appeal complies with legal provisions. If it complies with legal provisions, it shall, within three days after the expiration of the appeal period, transfer the petition for appeal together with the case file and evidence to the next higher People's Court, and at the same time deliver a copy of the petition for appeal to the People's Procuratorate at the same level and to the other party.

<a id="art-237"></a>第二百三十七条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人直接向第二审人民法院提出上诉的，第二审人民法院应当在收到上诉状后三日以内将上诉状交第一审人民法院。第一审人民法院应当审查上诉是否符合法律规定。符合法律规定的，应当在接到上诉状后三日以内将上诉状连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，同时将上诉状副本送交同级人民检察院和对方当事人。

**Article 237** Where a defendant, private prosecutor, or plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action directly files an appeal with the second instance People's Court, the second instance People's Court shall, within three days after receiving the petition for appeal, deliver the petition for appeal to the first instance People's Court. The first instance People's Court shall review whether the appeal complies with legal provisions. If it complies with legal provisions, it shall, within three days after receiving the petition for appeal, transfer the petition for appeal together with the case file and evidence to the next higher People's Court, and at the same time deliver a copy of the petition for appeal to the People's Procuratorate at the same level and to the other party.

<a id="art-238"></a>第二百三十八条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人及其法定代理人在上诉期限内要求撤回上诉的，应当准许。

**Article 238** Where a defendant, private prosecutor, or plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action or their legal representatives request to withdraw an appeal within the appeal period, permission shall be granted.

<a id="art-239"></a>第二百三十九条 被告人、自诉人、附带民事诉讼的原告人和被告人及其法定代理人在上诉期满后要求撤回上诉的，应当由第二审人民法院进行审查。如果认为原判决认定事实和适用法律正确，量刑适当，应当裁定准许被告人撤回上诉；如果认为原判决事实不清，证据不足或者将无罪判为有罪、轻罪重判等，应当不准许撤回上诉，并按照上诉程序进行审理。

**Article 239** Where a defendant, private prosecutor, or plaintiff or defendant in an incidental civil action or their legal representatives request to withdraw an appeal after the expiration of the appeal period, the second instance People's Court shall conduct a review. If it finds that the facts determined and the application of law in the original judgment are correct and the sentence is appropriate, it shall rule to permit the defendant to withdraw the appeal; if it finds that the facts of the original judgment are unclear, the evidence is insufficient, or an innocent person was convicted, or a lesser crime was sentenced heavily, it shall not permit the withdrawal of the appeal and shall conduct the hearing in accordance with the appeal procedure.

<a id="art-240"></a>第二百四十条 地方各级人民检察院对同级人民法院第一审判决、裁定的抗诉，通过第一审人民法院提交抗诉书。第一审人民法院应当在抗诉期满后三日内将抗诉书连同案卷、证据移送上一级人民法院，并且将抗诉书副本送交当事人。

**Article 240** A local People's Procuratorate at any level that protests a first instance judgment or ruling of a People's Court at the same level shall submit the written protest through the first instance People's Court. The first instance People's Court shall, within three days after the expiration of the protest period, transfer the written protest together with the case file and evidence to the next higher People's Court, and shall deliver a copy of the written protest to the party.

<a id="art-241"></a>第二百四十一条 人民检察院在抗诉期限内撤回抗诉的，第一审人民法院不再向上一级人民法院移送案件；如果是在抗诉期满后第二审人民法院宣告裁判前撤回抗诉的，第二审人民法院可以裁定准许，并通知第一审人民法院和当事人。

**Article 241** If the People's Procuratorate withdraws a protest within the protest period, the first instance People's Court shall not transfer the case to the next higher People's Court; if the protest is withdrawn after the expiration of the protest period but before the second instance People's Court pronounces its decision, the second instance People's Court may rule to permit the withdrawal and shall notify the first instance People's Court and the party.

<a id="art-242"></a>第二百四十二条 对附带民事判决或者裁定的上诉、抗诉期限，应当按照刑事部分的上诉、抗诉期限确定。如果原审附带民事部分是另行审判的，上诉期限应当按照民事诉讼法规定的期限执行。

**Article 242** The time limit for appeal or protest against an incidental civil judgment or ruling shall be determined in accordance with the time limit for appeal or protest against the criminal part. If the incidental civil part in the original trial was adjudicated separately, the time limit for appeal shall be implemented in accordance with the time limit prescribed by the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-243"></a>第二百四十三条 人民法院审理人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，应当通知同级人民检察院派员出庭。对接到开庭通知后人民检察院不派员出庭的抗诉案件，人民法院应当裁定按人民检察院撤回抗诉处理，并通知第一审人民法院和当事人。

**Article 243** When trying a case protested by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate at the same level to send personnel to appear in court. For a protested case where the People's Procuratorate fails to send personnel to appear in court after receiving the notice of hearing, the People's Court shall rule to treat it as a withdrawal of the protest by the People's Procuratorate, and shall notify the first instance People's Court and the party.

<a id="art-244"></a>第二百四十四条 对于在上诉、抗诉期满前撤回上诉、抗诉的案件，第一审判决、裁定在上诉、抗诉期满之日起生效；对于在上诉、抗诉期满后要求撤回上诉、抗诉，第二审人民法院裁定准许的，第一审判决、裁定应当自第二审人民法院裁定书送达原上诉人或者抗诉的检察机关之日起生效。

**Article 244** For a case where the appeal or protest is withdrawn before the expiration of the appeal or protest period, the first instance judgment or ruling shall take effect from the date of expiration of the appeal or protest period; for a case where a request to withdraw the appeal or protest is made after the expiration of the appeal or protest period and the second instance People's Court rules to permit it, the first instance judgment or ruling shall take effect from the date on which the written ruling of the second instance People's Court is served on the original appellant or the protesting procuratorial organ.

<a id="art-245"></a>第二百四十五条 第二审人民法院对第一审人民法院移送上诉、抗诉的案卷，应当审查是否包括下列内容：  
（一）移送上诉、抗诉案件函；  
（二）上诉状或者抗诉书；  
（三）第一审判决书或者裁定书八份（每增加一名被告人增加一份）；  
（四）全部案卷材料和证据，包括案件审结报告和其他应当移送的材料。  
前款所列材料齐备，第二审人民法院应当收案；材料不齐备的，应当通知第一审人民法院及时补送。

**Article 245** The second instance People's Court shall review whether the case file transferred by the first instance People's Court for appeal or protest includes the following contents:  
(1) The letter of transfer of the appeal or protest case;  
(2) The petition for appeal or written protest;  
(3) Eight copies of the first instance judgment or ruling (one additional copy for each additional defendant);  
(4) All case file materials and evidence, including the case conclusion report and other materials that shall be transferred.  
If the materials listed in the preceding paragraph are complete, the second instance People's Court shall accept the case; if the materials are not complete, it shall notify the first instance People's Court to timely supplement and transmit them.

<a id="art-246"></a>第二百四十六条 第二审人民法院应当就第一审判决、裁定认定的事实和适用法律进行全面审查不受上诉或者抗诉范围的限制。

**Article 246** The second instance People's Court shall conduct a comprehensive review of the facts determined and the application of law in the first instance judgment or ruling, without being limited by the scope of the appeal or protest.

<a id="art-247"></a>第二百四十七条 共同犯罪案件，只有部分被告人提出上诉的，或者人民检察院只就第一审人民法院对部分被告人的判决提出抗诉的，第二审人民法院应当对全案进行审查，一并处理。

**Article 247** In a joint crime case, if only part of the defendants file an appeal, or if the People's Procuratorate only protests the judgment of the first instance People's Court concerning part of the defendants, the second instance People's Court shall review the entire case and handle it together.

<a id="art-248"></a>第二百四十八条 共同犯罪案件，如果提出上诉的被告人死亡，其他被告人没有提出上诉，第二审人民法院仍应当对全案进行审查。死亡的被告人不构成犯罪的，应当宣告无罪；审查后认为构成犯罪的，应当宣布终止审理。对其他同案被告人仍应当作出判决或者裁定。

**Article 248** In a joint crime case, if the defendant who filed an appeal dies and the other defendants have not filed an appeal, the second instance People's Court shall still review the entire case. If the deceased defendant is not guilty of a crime, a verdict of not guilty shall be pronounced; if after review it is found that he is guilty of a crime, the trial shall be declared terminated. With respect to the other co-defendants, a judgment or ruling shall still be rendered.

<a id="art-249"></a>第二百四十九条 审理附带民事诉讼的上诉、抗诉案件，应当对全案进行审查。如果第一审判决的刑事部分并无不当，第二审人民法院只需就附带民事诉讼部分作出处理。如果第一审判决附带民事部分事实清楚，适用法律正确的，应当以刑事附带民事裁定维持原判，驳回上诉、抗诉。

**Article 249** When trying an appeal or protest case concerning an incidental civil action, the entire case shall be reviewed. If the criminal part of the first instance judgment is not improper, the second instance People's Court need only handle the incidental civil part. If the facts of the incidental civil part of the first instance judgment are clear and the application of law is correct, it shall affirm the original judgment by a criminal incidental civil ruling and dismiss the appeal or protest.

<a id="art-250"></a>第二百五十条 附带民事诉讼案件，只有附带民事诉讼的当事人和他们的法定代理人提出上诉的，第一审刑事部分的判决，在上诉期满后即发生法律效力。  
应当送监执行的第一审刑事被告人是第二审附带民事诉讼被告人的，在第二审附带民事诉讼案件审结前，可以暂缓送监执行。

**Article 250** In an incidental civil action case, if only the parties to the incidental civil action and their legal representatives file an appeal, the judgment on the criminal part of the first instance shall become legally effective upon the expiration of the appeal period.  
If a first instance criminal defendant who shall be sent to prison for execution is also a defendant in the second instance incidental civil action, the execution may be temporarily suspended until the conclusion of the second instance incidental civil action.

<a id="art-251"></a>第二百五十一条 对于上诉、抗诉案件应当审查下列主要内容：  
（一）第一审判决认定的事实是否清楚，证据是否确实、充分，证据之间有无矛盾；  
（二）第一审判决适用法律是否正确，量刑是否适当；  
（三）在侦查、起诉、第一审程序中，有无违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情形；  
（四）上诉、抗诉是否提出了新的事实和证据；  
（五）被告人供述、辩解的情况；  
（六）辩护人的辩护意见以及采纳的情况；  
（七）附带民事部分的判决、裁定是否适当；  
（八）第一审法院合议庭、审判委员会讨论的意见。  
审查后写出审查报告。

**Article 251** The following main contents shall be reviewed for appeal or protest cases:  
(1) Whether the facts determined in the first instance judgment are clear, whether the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and whether there are contradictions among the evidence;  
(2) Whether the application of law in the first instance judgment is correct and whether the sentence is appropriate;  
(3) Whether there are circumstances in violation of the legally prescribed procedure in the investigation, prosecution, and first instance proceedings;  
(4) Whether the appeal or protest has raised new facts and evidence;  
(5) The circumstances of the defendant's confession and defense;  
(6) The defense opinions of the defender and the circumstances of their adoption;  
(7) Whether the judgment or ruling on the incidental civil part is appropriate;  
(8) The opinions discussed by the collegial panel and the adjudication committee of the first instance court.  
After review, a review report shall be written.

<a id="art-252"></a>第二百五十二条 在第二审程序中，被告人除自行辩护外，还可以继续委托第一审辩护人或者另行委托辩护人辩护。  
共同犯罪案件，只有部分被告人提出上诉或者人民检察院只就第一审人民法院对部分被告人的判决提出抗诉的，其他同案被告人也可以委托辩护人辩护。

**Article 252** In the second instance proceedings, in addition to defending himself, the defendant may continue to entrust the defender from the first instance or separately entrust a defender to defend.  
In a joint crime case, if only part of the defendants file an appeal or if the People's Procuratorate only protests the judgment of the first instance People's Court concerning part of the defendants, the other co-defendants may also entrust a defender to defend.

<a id="art-253"></a>第二百五十三条 对上诉案件，应当组成合议庭，开庭审理。  
经过阅卷，讯问被告人，听取其他当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人的意见后，合议庭认定的事实与第一审认定的没有变化，证据充分的，可以不开庭审理。  
对人民检察院抗诉的案件，第二审人民法院应当开庭审理。

**Article 253** For appeal cases, a collegial panel shall be formed and a hearing shall be held.  
After reviewing the case files, interrogating the defendant, and hearing the opinions of other parties, defenders, and litigation agents, if the facts determined by the collegial panel are unchanged from those determined in the first instance and the evidence is sufficient, a hearing may be held without a hearing.  
For cases protested by the People's Procuratorate, the second instance People's Court shall hold a hearing.

<a id="art-254"></a>第二百五十四条 第二审人民法院开庭审理上诉、抗诉案件，可以到案件发生地或者原审人民法院所在地进行。

**Article 254** When holding a hearing for appeal or protest cases, the second instance People's Court may conduct it at the place where the case occurred or at the location of the original trial People's Court.

<a id="art-255"></a>第二百五十五条 第二审人民法院开庭审理上诉或者抗诉案件，除参照第一审程序的规定外，还应当依照下列规定进行：  
（一）法庭调查阶段，审判长或者审判员宣读第一审判决书、裁定书后，由上诉人陈述上诉理由或者由检察人员宣读抗诉书：如果是既有上诉又有抗诉的案件，先由检察人员宣读抗诉书，再由上诉人陈述上诉理由；法庭调查的重点要针对上诉或者抗诉的理由，全面查清事实，核实证据；  
（二）法庭调查阶段，如果检察人员或者辩护人申请出示、宣读、播放第一审审理期间已经移交给人民法院的证据的，法庭应当指令值庭法警出示、播放有关证据；需要宣读的证据，由法警交由申请人宣读；  
（三）法庭辩论阶段，上诉案件，应当先由上诉人、辩护人发言，再由检察人员发言；抗诉案件，应当先由检察人员发言，再由被告人、辩护人发言；既有上诉又有抗诉的案件，应当先由检察人员发言，再由上诉人、辩护人发言，并进行辩论。

**Article 255** When the second instance People's Court holds a hearing for an appeal or protest case, in addition to applying the provisions on the first instance procedure by reference, it shall also conduct the hearing in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) During the stage of court investigation, after the presiding judge or judge reads the first instance judgment or ruling, the appellant shall state the grounds of appeal or the prosecutor shall read the written protest: if the case has both an appeal and a protest, the prosecutor shall first read the written protest, and then the appellant shall state the grounds of appeal; the focus of the court investigation shall be on the grounds of the appeal or protest, and the facts shall be fully clarified and the evidence verified;  
(2) During the court investigation stage, if the prosecutor or defender applies to present, read, or play evidence that had already been transferred to the People's Court during the first instance trial, the court shall instruct the bailiff to present or play the relevant evidence; for evidence that needs to be read, the bailiff shall hand it to the applicant to read;  
(3) During the stage of court debate, in an appeal case, the appellant and defender shall speak first, followed by the prosecutor; in a protested case, the prosecutor shall speak first, followed by the defendant and defender; in a case with both an appeal and a protest, the prosecutor shall speak first, followed by the appellant and defender, and then debate shall proceed.

<a id="art-256"></a>第二百五十六条 共同犯罪案件，没有提出上诉的和没有对其判决提出抗诉的第一审被告人，应当参加法庭调查，并可以参加法庭辩论。

**Article 256** In a joint crime case, the first instance defendants who have not filed an appeal and whose judgment has not been protested by the People's Procuratorate shall participate in the court investigation and may participate in the court debate.

<a id="art-257"></a>第二百五十七条 第二审人民法院审理被告人或者其法定代理人、辩护人、近亲属提出上诉的案件，不得加重被告人的刑罚，并应当执行下列具体规定：  
（一）共同犯罪案件，只有部分被告人提出上诉的，既不能加重提出上诉的被告人的刑罚，也不能加重其他同案被告人的刑罚；  
（二）对原判认定事实清楚、证据充分，只是认定的罪名不当的，在不加重原判刑罚的情况下，可以改变罪名；  
（三）对被告人实行数罪并罚的，不得加重决定执行的刑罚，也不能在维持原判决决定执行的刑罚不变的情况下，加重数罪中某罪的刑罚；  
（四）对被告人判处拘役或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑的，不得撤销原判决宣告的缓刑或者延长缓刑考验期；  
（五）对事实清楚、证据充分，但判处的刑罚畸轻，或者应当适用附加刑而没有适用的案件，不得撤销第一审判决，直接加重被告人的刑罚或者适用附加刑，也不得以事实不清或者证据不足发回第一审人民法院重新审理。必须依法改判的，应当在第二审判决、裁定生效后，按照审判监督程序重新审判。  
人民检察院提出抗诉或者自诉人提出上诉的案件，不受前款规定的限制。但是人民检察院抗诉的案件，经第二审人民法院审理后，改判被告人死刑立即执行的，应当报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 257** When the second instance People's Court tries a case appealed by the defendant or his legal representative, defender, or close relative, the punishment of the defendant shall not be aggravated, and the following specific provisions shall be implemented:  
(1) In a joint crime case, if only part of the defendants file an appeal, the punishment of the appealing defendants shall not be aggravated, nor shall the punishment of the other co-defendants be aggravated;  
(2) If the original judgment finds the facts clear and the evidence sufficient, but the determined charge is incorrect, the charge may be changed without aggravating the punishment in the original judgment;  
(3) If the defendant is subject to concurrent punishment for several crimes, the decided punishment to be executed shall not be aggravated, nor shall the punishment for one of several crimes be aggravated while maintaining the originally decided punishment to be executed unchanged;  
(4) If the defendant is sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment with probation, the probation pronounced in the original judgment shall not be revoked, nor shall the probation period be extended;  
(5) If the facts are clear and the evidence sufficient, but the imposed punishment is obviously light, or an additional punishment should have been applied but was not, the first instance judgment shall not be revoked to directly aggravate the defendant's punishment or apply an additional punishment, nor shall the case be remanded to the first instance People's Court for retrial on the grounds of unclear facts or insufficient evidence. If a correction of the judgment is necessary according to law, a new trial shall be conducted in accordance with the trial supervision procedure after the second instance judgment or ruling becomes effective.  
Cases protested by the People's Procuratorate or appealed by a private prosecutor shall not be subject to the restrictions of the preceding paragraph. However, if after trial by the second instance People's Court in a case protested by the People's Procuratorate, the defendant is sentenced to immediate execution of the death penalty upon correction of the judgment, such sentence shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-258"></a>第二百五十八条 共同犯罪案件中，人民检察院只对部分被告人的判决提出抗诉的，第二审人民法院对其他第一审被告人不得加重刑罚。

**Article 258** In a joint crime case, if the People's Procuratorate only protests the judgment concerning part of the defendants, the second instance People's Court shall not aggravate the punishment of the other first instance defendants.

<a id="art-259"></a>第二百五十九条 提出上诉或者抗诉的理由具有[刑事诉讼法第一百九十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-191)规定的情形之一的，第二审人民法院应当裁定撤销原判，发回第一审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 259** If the grounds for appeal or protest have any of the circumstances provided in Article 191 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the second instance People's Court shall rule to quash the original judgment and remand the case to the first instance People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-260"></a>第二百六十条 第二审人民法院审理刑事附带民事上诉、抗诉案件，如果发现刑事和附带民事部分均有错误需依法改判的，应当一并改判。

**Article 260** When the second instance People's Court tries a criminal incidental civil appeal or protest case, if it finds errors in both the criminal and incidental civil parts requiring correction according to law, it shall correct the judgment together.

<a id="art-261"></a>第二百六十一条 第二审人民法院审理对刑事部分提出上诉、抗诉，附带民事诉讼部分已经发生法律效力的案件，如果发现第一审判决或者裁定中的民事部分确有错误，应当对民事部分按照审判监督程序予以纠正。

**Article 261** When the second instance People's Court tries a case where the criminal part is appealed or protested and the incidental civil part has already become legally effective, if it finds there is indeed an error in the civil part of the first instance judgment or ruling, it shall correct the civil part in accordance with the trial supervision procedure.

<a id="art-262"></a>第二百六十二条 第二审人民法院审理对附带民事诉讼部分提出上诉、抗诉，刑事部分已经发生法律效力的案件，如果发现第一审判决或者裁定中的刑事部分确有错误，应当对刑事部分按照审判监督程序进行再审，并将附带民事诉讼部分与刑事部分一并审理。

**Article 262** When the second instance People's Court tries a case where the incidental civil part is appealed or protested and the criminal part has already become legally effective, if it finds there is indeed an error in the criminal part of the first instance judgment or ruling, it shall conduct a retrial of the criminal part in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, and shall try the incidental civil part together with the criminal part.

<a id="art-263"></a>第二百六十三条 对第二审自诉案件，必要时可以进行调解，当事人也可以自行和解。调解结案的，应当制作调解书，第一审判决、裁定视为自动撤销；当事人自行和解的，由人民法院裁定准许撤回自诉，并撤销第一审判决或者裁定。

**Article 263** For a second instance private prosecution case, mediation may be conducted when necessary, and the parties may also reach a voluntary settlement. If the case is concluded by mediation, a mediation document shall be prepared, and the first instance judgment or ruling shall be deemed automatically revoked; if the parties reach a voluntary settlement, the People's Court shall rule to permit the withdrawal of the private prosecution and revoke the first instance judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-264"></a>第二百六十四条 第二审人民法院对于调解结案或者当事人自行和解的自诉案件，被告人被采取强制措施的，应当立即予以解除。

**Article 264** For a private prosecution case concluded by mediation or by voluntary settlement of the parties, if the defendant has been subjected to compulsory measures, such measures shall be immediately lifted.

<a id="art-265"></a>第二百六十五条 在第二审程序中，自诉案件的当事人提出反诉的，第二审人民法院应当告知其另行起诉。

**Article 265** In the second instance proceedings, if a party to a private prosecution case files a counterclaim, the second instance People's Court shall inform him to file a separate prosecution.

<a id="art-266"></a>第二百六十六条 在第二审案件附带民事部分审理中，第一审民事原告人增加独立的诉讼请求或者第一审民事被告人提出反诉的，第二审人民法院可以根据当事人自愿的原则就新增加的诉讼请求或者反诉进行调解，调解不成的，告知当事人另行起诉。

**Article 266** During the trial of the incidental civil part of a second instance case, if the first instance civil plaintiff adds an independent claim or the first instance civil defendant files a counterclaim, the second instance People's Court may, based on the principle of the parties' voluntariness, conduct mediation concerning the newly added claim or counterclaim. If mediation fails, the parties shall be informed to file a separate prosecution.

<a id="art-267"></a>第二百六十七条 人民法院依法开庭审理第二审公诉案件，应当在开庭十日以前通知人民检察院查阅案卷。  
自通知后的第二日起，人民检察院查阅案卷超过七日后的期限，不计入第二审审理期限。  
十四、在法定刑以下判处刑罚  
和适用特殊情况假释的核准程序

**Article 267** When the People's Court holds a hearing for a second instance public prosecution case according to law, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate to review the case file at least ten days before the hearing.  
From the second day after the notice, the period during which the People's Procuratorate reviews the case file exceeding seven days shall not be counted in the trial period of the second instance.  
14. Procedures for Approval of Sentencing Below the Statutory Punishment and Parole under Special Circumstances

<a id="art-268"></a>第二百六十八条 根据[刑法第六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-63)第二款规定报请最高人民法院核准在法定刑以下判处刑罚的案件，按下列情形分别处理：  
（一）被告人不提出上诉、人民检察院不提出抗诉的，在上诉、抗诉期满后三日内报请上一级人民法院复核。上一级人民法院同意原判的，应当逐级报请最高人民法院核准；上一级人民法院不同意原判的，应当裁定发回重新审判或者改变管辖，按照第审程序重新审理。原判是由基层人民法院作出的，高级人民法院可以指定中级人民法院按照第一审程序重新审理；  
（二）被告人提出上诉或者人民检察院提出抗诉的案件，应当按照第二审程序审理。上诉或者抗诉无理的，应当裁定驳回上诉或者抗诉，维持原判，并按照本条第（一）项规定的程序逐级报请最高人民法院核准。上诉或者抗诉有理的，应当依法改判。改判后仍判决在法定刑以下处以刑罚的，按照本条第（一）项规定的程序逐级报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 268** Cases reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval of sentencing below the statutory punishment according to Article 63, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the defendant does not file an appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not file a protest, the case shall be reported to the next higher People's Court for review within three days after the expiration of the appeal or protest period. If the next higher People's Court agrees with the original judgment, it shall level by level report to the Supreme People's Court for approval; if the next higher People's Court does not agree with the original judgment, it shall rule to remand for retrial or change the jurisdiction, and conduct a new trial according to the first instance procedure. If the original judgment was made by a Primary People's Court, the Higher People's Court may designate an Intermediate People's Court to conduct a new trial according to the first instance procedure;  
(2) Cases where the defendant files an appeal or the People's Procuratorate files a protest shall be tried according to the second instance procedure. If the appeal or protest is unfounded, it shall rule to dismiss the appeal or protest, affirm the original judgment, and level by level report to the Supreme People's Court for approval according to the procedure provided in subparagraph (1) of this Article. If the appeal or protest is well-founded, the judgment shall be corrected according to law. If after correction, the judgment still imposes a punishment below the statutory punishment, it shall level by level report to the Supreme People's Court for approval according to the procedure provided in subparagraph (1) of this Article.

<a id="art-269"></a>第二百六十九条 报请最高人民法院核准在法定刑以下判处刑罚的案件，应当报送报请核准案件的结案报告、判决书各十五份，以及全案诉讼卷宗和证据。

**Article 269** Cases reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval of sentencing below the statutory punishment shall be submitted with fifteen copies each of the case conclusion report and the judgment, as well as the entire litigation case file and evidence.

<a id="art-270"></a>第二百七十条 最高人民法院复核在法定刑以下判处刑罚的案件，予以核准的，作出核准裁定书；不予核准的，应当撤销原判决、裁定，发回原审人民法院重新审判或者指定其他下级人民法院重新审判。

**Article 270** When the Supreme People's Court reviews and approves a case with sentencing below the statutory punishment, it shall issue a written ruling of approval; if it does not approve, it shall quash the original judgment or ruling and remand the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial or designate another lower People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-271"></a>第二百七十一条 根据[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第一款规定报请最高人民法院核准因犯罪分子具有特殊情况，不受执行刑期限制的假释案件，按下列情形分别处理：  
（一）中级人民法院依法作出假释裁定后，应即报请高级人民法院复核。高级人民法院同意假释的，应当报请最高人民法院核准；高级人民法院不同意假释的，应当裁定撤销中级人民法院的假释裁定；  
（二）高级人民法院依法作出假释裁定的，应当报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 271** Cases reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval of parole without the restriction of the executed term due to the criminal element having special circumstances according to Article 81, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) After the Intermediate People's Court makes a parole ruling according to law, it shall immediately report to the Higher People's Court for review. If the Higher People's Court agrees with the parole, it shall report to the Supreme People's Court for approval; if the Higher People's Court does not agree with the parole, it shall rule to quash the parole ruling of the Intermediate People's Court;  
(2) If the Higher People's Court makes a parole ruling according to law, it shall report to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-272"></a>第二百七十二条 报请最高人民法院核准因犯罪分子具有特殊情况，不受执行刑期限制的假释案件，应当报送报请核准假释案件的报告、罪犯具有特殊情况的报告、假释裁定书各十五份，以及全案卷宗。

**Article 272** Cases reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval of parole without the restriction of the executed term due to the criminal element having special circumstances shall be submitted with fifteen copies each of the report on the case for which approval of parole is requested, the report on the offender's special circumstances, and the parole ruling, as well as the entire case file.

<a id="art-273"></a>第二百七十三条 最高人民法院核准因犯罪分子具有特殊情况，不受执行刑期限制的假释案件，予以核准的，作出核准裁定书；不予核准的，应当作出撤销原裁定，不准假释的裁定书。  
十五、死刑复核程序

**Article 273** When the Supreme People's Court reviews and approves a parole case without the restriction of the executed term due to the criminal element having special circumstances, if it approves, it shall issue a written ruling of approval; if it does not approve, it shall issue a written ruling to quash the original ruling and disallow parole.  
15. Death Penalty Review Procedure

<a id="art-274"></a>第二百七十四条 死刑由最高人民法院核准，但依法授权高级人民法院核准的除外。  
因人民检察院提出抗诉而由人民法院按照第二审程序改判死刑的案件，应当报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 274** The death penalty shall be approved by the Supreme People's Court, except where the approval is delegated to a Higher People's Court according to law.  
Where a People's Court corrects a judgment to impose the death penalty according to the second instance procedure due to a protest by the People's Procuratorate, the case shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-275"></a>第二百七十五条 报请最高人民法院核准的死刑案件，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）中级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人不上诉、人民检察院不抗诉的，在上诉、抗诉期满后三日内报请高级人民法院复核。高级人民法院同意判处死刑的，应当依法作出裁定后，报请最高人民法院核准；不同意判处死刑的，应当提审或者发回重新审判；  
（二）中级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人提出上诉或者人民检察院提出抗诉，高级人民法院终市裁定维持死刑判决的，报请最高人民法院核准；  
（三）高级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人不上诉、人民检察院不抗诉的，在上诉、抗诉期满后三日内报请最高人民法院核准；  
（四）依法应当由最高人民法院核准的死刑案件，判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期问，如果故意犯罪，查证属实，应当执行死刑的，由高级人民法院报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 275** Death penalty cases reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) For a first instance case where an Intermediate People's Court imposes the death penalty, if the defendant does not appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not protest, the case shall be reported to the Higher People's Court for review within three days after the expiration of the appeal or protest period. If the Higher People's Court agrees to impose the death penalty, it shall make a ruling according to law and then report to the Supreme People's Court for approval; if it does not agree to impose the death penalty, it shall bring the case up for trial or remand for retrial;  
(2) For a first instance case where an Intermediate People's Court imposes the death penalty, if the defendant files an appeal or the People's Procuratorate files a protest, and the Higher People's Court makes a final ruling affirming the death penalty, the case shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval;  
(3) For a first instance case where a Higher People's Court imposes the death penalty, if the defendant does not appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not protest, the case shall be reported to the Supreme People's Court for approval within three days after the expiration of the appeal or protest period;  
(4) For a death penalty case that shall be approved by the Supreme People's Court according to law, if the criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve commits an intentional crime during the period of the reprieve, and after verification, the death penalty shall be executed, the Higher People's Court shall report to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-276"></a>第二百七十六条 依授权可以由高级人民法院核准的死刑案件，中级人民法院判处死刑后，被告人不上诉、人民检察院不抗诉的，在上诉、抗诉期满后三日内报请高级人民法院核准。  
高级人民法院同意判处死刑的，应当裁定核准死刑；不同意判处死刑的，应当依法改判；认为原判事实不清，证据不足的，应当发回中级人民法院重新审判。

**Article 276** For a death penalty case that may be approved by a Higher People's Court under delegation, after an Intermediate People's Court imposes the death penalty, if the defendant does not appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not protest, the case shall be reported to the Higher People's Court for approval within three days after the expiration of the appeal or protest period.  
If the Higher People's Court agrees to impose the death penalty, it shall rule to approve the death penalty; if it does not agree to impose the death penalty, it shall correct the judgment according to law; if it finds that the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it shall remand the case to the Intermediate People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-277"></a>第二百七十七条 依授权可以由高级人民法院核准的死刑案件，判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果故意犯罪，查证属实，应当执行死刑的，报请高级人民法院核准。

**Article 277** For a death penalty case that may be approved by a Higher People's Court under delegation, if the criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve commits an intentional crime during the period of the reprieve, and after verification, the death penalty shall be executed, the case shall be reported to the Higher People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-278"></a>第二百七十八条 中级人民法院判处死刑缓期二年执行的第一审案件，被告人不上诉、人民检察院不抗诉的，应当报请高级人民法院核准。  
高级人民法院对于报请核准的死刑缓期二年执行的案件，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）同意判处死刑缓期二年执行的，应当裁定予以核准；  
（二）认为原判事实不清、证据不足的，应当裁定发回重新审判；  
（三）认为原判量刑过重的，应当依法改判。  
高级人民法院核准死刑缓期二年执行的案件，不得以提高审级等方式加重被告人的刑罚。

**Article 278** For a first instance case where an Intermediate People's Court imposes a death sentence with a two-year reprieve, if the defendant does not appeal and the People's Procuratorate does not protest, the case shall be reported to the Higher People's Court for approval.  
The Higher People's Court shall handle cases reported for approval of death sentence with a two-year reprieve separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If it agrees to impose a death sentence with a two-year reprieve, it shall rule to approve it;  
(2) If it finds that the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it shall rule to remand for retrial;  
(3) If it finds that the punishment in the original judgment is too heavy, it shall correct the judgment according to law.  
When the Higher People's Court approves a case of death sentence with a two-year reprieve, it shall not aggravate the punishment of the defendant by raising the trial level or other means.

<a id="art-279"></a>第二百七十九条 被告人被判处死刑的数罪中，如果有应当由最高人民法院核准的，或者共同犯罪案件部分被告人被判处死刑的罪中有应当由最高人民法院核准的，必须将全案报请最高人民法院核准。

**Article 279** Where, among the several crimes for which a defendant is sentenced to death, any crime shall be subject to approval by the Supreme People's Court, or where, in a joint crime case, among the crimes for which some defendants are sentenced to death, any crime shall be subject to approval by the Supreme People's Court, the entire case shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval.

<a id="art-280"></a>第二百八十条 报请复核死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件，应当一案一报。报送的材料应当包括报请复核的报告、死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件综合报告和判决书各十五份，以及全部诉讼案卷和证据；共同犯罪的案件，应当报送全案的诉讼案卷和证据。  
（一）报请复核的报告，应当载明案由、简要案情和审理过程及判决结果；  
（二）死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件综合报告应当包括以下主要内容：  
1、被告人的姓名、性别、出生年月日、民族、文化程度、职业、住址、简历以及拘留、逮捕、起诉的时间和现在被羁押的处所；  
2、被告人的犯罪事实，包括犯罪时间、地点、动机、目的、手段、危害后果以及从轻、从重处罚等情节，认定犯罪的证据，定罪量刑的法律依据；  
3、需要说明的其他问题。

**Article 280** When submitting a death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case for review and approval, each case shall be reported separately. The materials submitted shall include the report requesting review and approval, the comprehensive report on the death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case, and the judgment, each in fifteen copies, in addition to all litigation files and evidence; for a joint crime case, the litigation files and evidence of the entire case shall be submitted.  
(1) The report requesting review and approval shall state the cause of action, brief case facts, trial process, and judgment result;  
(2) The comprehensive report on the death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case shall include the following main contents:  
1. The defendant's name, gender, date of birth, ethnicity, education level, occupation, address, resume, and the time of detention, arrest, prosecution, and the current place of custody;  
2. The defendant's criminal facts, including the time, place, motive, purpose, means, harmful consequences, and circumstances of lenient or severe punishment, evidence to establish the crime, and legal basis for conviction and sentencing;  
3. Other issues that need to be explained.

<a id="art-281"></a>第二百八十一条 报送死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）复核案件的诉讼案卷和证据，根据案件具体情况应当包括以下内容：  
（一）拘留证、逮捕证、搜查证的复印件；  
（二）扣押赃款、赃物和其他在案物证的清单；  
（三）公安机关、国家安全机关的起诉意见书，或者人民检察院的侦查终结报告；  
（四）人民检察院的起诉书；  
（五）案件的审查报告、法庭审理笔录、合议庭评议笔录和审判委员会讨论决定笔录；  
（六）被告人上诉状、人民检察院抗诉书；  
（七）人民法院的判决书、裁定书和宣判笔录、送达回证；  
（八）能够证明案件具体情况并经过查证属实的各种肯定的和否定的证据，包括物证或者物证照片、书证、证人证言、被害人陈述、被告人供述和辩解。

**Article 281** The litigation files and evidence submitted for a death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) review case shall, based on the specific circumstances of the case, include the following contents:  
(1) Copies of the detention warrant, arrest warrant, and search warrant;  
(2) A list of confiscated illegal gains, stolen goods, and other physical evidence in the case;  
(3) The prosecution opinion of the public security organ or the state security organ, or the investigation termination report of the People's Procuratorate;  
(4) The indictment of the People's Procuratorate;  
(5) The case review report, court trial record, collegial panel deliberation record, and the record of the adjudicative committee's discussion and decision;  
(6) The defendant's appeal petition and the People's Procuratorate's protest;  
(7) The People's Court's judgment, ruling, pronouncement record, and receipt of service;  
(8) Various affirmative and negative evidence that can prove the specific circumstances of the case and has been verified, including physical evidence or photographs of physical evidence, documentary evidence, witness testimony, victim statements, and the defendant's confession and defense.

<a id="art-282"></a>第二百八十二条 高级人民法院复核或者核准死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件，必须提审被告人。

**Article 282** When reviewing or approving a death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case, the Higher People's Court must interrogate the defendant.

<a id="art-283"></a>第二百八十三条 复核死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件，应当全面审查以下内容：  
（一）被告人的年龄，有无责任能力，是否正在怀孕的妇女；  
（二）原审判决认定的主要事实是否清楚，证据是否确实、充分；  
（三）犯罪情节、后果及危害程度；  
（四）原审判决适用法律是否正确，是否必须判处死刑，是否必须立即执行；  
（五）有无法定、酌定从轻或者减轻处罚的情节；  
（六）其他应当审查的情况。

**Article 283** When reviewing a death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case, the following shall be comprehensively examined:  
(1) The defendant's age, whether they have criminal capacity, and whether they are a pregnant woman;  
(2) Whether the main facts determined in the original judgment are clear and whether the evidence is reliable and sufficient;  
(3) The circumstances, consequences, and degree of harm of the crime;  
(4) Whether the application of law in the original judgment is correct, whether a death sentence is necessary, and whether immediate execution is necessary;  
(5) Whether there are any statutory or discretionary circumstances for lenient or mitigated punishment;  
(6) Other circumstances that shall be examined.

<a id="art-284"></a>第二百八十四条 对报请核准的死刑（死刑缓期二年执行）案件全面审查后，合议庭应当进行评议并写出复核审理报告，审核报告应当包括以下内容：  
（一）案件的由来和审理经过；  
（二）被告人和被害人简况；  
（三）案件的侦破情况；  
（四）原审判决要点和控辩双方意见；  
（五）对事实和证据复核后的分析与认定；  
（六）合议庭评议意见、审判委员会讨论决定意见；  
（七）需要说明的问题。

**Article 284** After a comprehensive review of a death sentence (death sentence with a two-year reprieve) case submitted for approval, the collegial panel shall deliberate and write a review report. The review report shall include the following contents:  
(1) The origin of the case and the trial process;  
(2) Brief information on the defendant and the victim;  
(3) The case investigation and solution situation;  
(4) The main points of the original judgment and the opinions of the prosecution and defense;  
(5) Analysis and determination after review of facts and evidence;  
(6) The collegial panel's deliberation opinion and the adjudicative committee's discussion and decision opinion;  
(7) Issues that need to be explained.

<a id="art-285"></a>第二百八十五条 对判处死刑的案件，复核后应当根据案件情形分别作出裁判：  
（一）原审判决认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当的，裁定予以核准；  
（二）原审判决认定事实错误或者证据不足的，裁定撤销原判，发回重新审判；  
（三）原市判决认定的事实止确，但适用法律有错误，或者量刑不当，不同意判处死刑的，应当改判；  
（四）发现第一审人民法院或者第二审人民法院违反法律规定的诉讼程序，可能影响正确判决的，应当裁定撤销原判，发回第一审人民法院或者第二审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 285** For cases in which a death sentence is imposed, after review, a judgment shall be made separately according to the circumstances of the case:  
(1) If the facts determined and the law applied in the original judgment are correct and the sentence is appropriate, a ruling shall be made to approve it;  
(2) If the facts determined in the original judgment are erroneous or the evidence is insufficient, a ruling shall be made to quash the original judgment and remand the case for retrial;  
(3) If the facts determined in the original judgment are correct but the application of law is erroneous, or the sentence is inappropriate, and the death sentence is not agreed upon, the judgment shall be amended;  
(4) If it is discovered that the first-instance People's Court or the second-instance People's Court has violated the litigation procedures prescribed by law, which may affect the correct judgment, a ruling shall be made to quash the original judgment and remand the case to the first-instance People's Court or the second-instance People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-286"></a>第二百八十六条 高级人民法院复核后发回原审人民法院重新审判的案件，重新审判所作的判决、裁定，被告人可以提出上诉，人民检察院可以提出抗诉。

**Article 286** For a case remanded by the Higher People's Court to the original trial People's Court for retrial after review, the defendant may file an appeal, and the People's Procuratorate may file a protest against the judgment or ruling made upon retrial.

<a id="art-287"></a>第二百八十七条 共同犯罪案件中，部分被告人被判处死刑的，最高人民法院或者高级人民法院复核时，应当对全案进行审查，但不影响对其他被告人已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的执行；发现对其他被告人已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定确有错误时，可以指令原审人民法院再审。  
十六、扣押、冻结在案财物的处理

**Article 287** In a joint crime case where some defendants are sentenced to death, when the Supreme People's Court or the Higher People's Court conducts a review, it shall examine the entire case, but this shall not affect the execution of judgments or rulings that have already taken legal effect against other defendants; if it is discovered that there is indeed an error in the judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect against other defendants, it may instruct the original trial People's Court to conduct a retrial.  
16. Disposal of Seized or Frozen Assets in the Case

<a id="art-288"></a>第二百八十八条 人民法院对于扣押、冻结在案的被告人的财物及其孳息，应当妥善保管，以供核查。任何单位和个人不得挪用或者自行处理。  
依法扣押的货币、有价证券，应当登记写明货币、有价证券的名称、数额、面额，货币应当存入银行专户，并登记银行存款凭证的名称、内容，入卷备查。  
依法扣押的物品，应当登记写明物品名称、型号、规格、数量、重量、质量、成色、纯度、颜色、新旧程度、缺损特征和来源等，入卷备查。  
依法扣押的文物、金银、珠宝、名贵字画等以及违禁品，应当及时鉴定。  
对扣押的物品应当及时依照有关规定作价。

**Article 288** The People's Court shall properly keep the defendant's property and its fruits that have been seized or frozen in the case for inspection. No unit or individual may misappropriate or dispose of them on their own.  
Currency and negotiable securities seized according to law shall be registered with the name, amount, and denomination of the currency or negotiable securities stated clearly; the currency shall be deposited into a special bank account, and the name and content of the bank deposit certificate shall be registered and filed for future reference.  
Articles seized according to law shall be registered with the name, model, specification, quantity, weight, quality, fineness, purity, color, degree of newness, defect characteristics, and source of the article stated clearly, and filed for future reference.  
Cultural relics, gold, silver, jewelry, precious calligraphy and paintings, and other items, as well as prohibited articles, seized according to law shall be appraised promptly.  
Seized articles shall be valued promptly in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-289"></a>第二百八十九条 对于被害人的合法财产，被害人明确的，扣押、冻结机关应当依法及时返还。但须经拍照、鉴定、作价，并在案卷中注明返还的理由，将原物照片、清单和被害人的领取手续入卷备查。

**Article 289** For the lawful property of a victim, if the victim is identified, the organ that seized or froze the property shall return it promptly according to law. However, it shall be photographed, appraised, and valued, and the reasons for return shall be noted in the case file; photographs of the original items, a list, and the victim's receipt formalities shall be placed in the case file for future reference.

<a id="art-290"></a>第二百九十条 对作为证据使用的实物，包括作为物证的货币、有价证券等，应当随案移送。开庭审判时，经向法庭出示、质证后移交法庭。休庭或者闭庭时办理证据交接手续，清点、核对无误的，由经手人在清单上分别签名后予以封存。对因上诉、抗诉引起第二审程序的，第一审人民法院应当将上述证据移送第二审人民法院，并办理证据交接手续。

**Article 290** Tangible objects used as evidence, including currency and negotiable securities used as physical evidence, shall be transferred together with the case file. During court trial, after being presented and examined in court, they shall be handed over to the court. When the court adjourns or closes, the evidence handover procedures shall be completed; if the count and verification are correct, the persons handling the matter shall sign on the list respectively, and then seal the evidence. If the second-instance procedure is initiated due to an appeal or protest, the first-instance People's Court shall transfer the above evidence to the second-instance People's Court and complete the evidence handover procedures.

<a id="art-291"></a>第二百九十一条 下列不宜移送的实物，人民法院受理案件时，应当审查是否附有相关证据材料；需要鉴定（包括估价）的，应当附有鉴定结论：  
（一）大宗的、不便搬运的物品，由扣押机关开列清单，并附原物照片和封存手续，注明存放地点；  
（二）易腐烂、霉变和不易保管的物品，扣押机关变卖处理后，随案移送原物照片、清单、变价处理的凭证（复印件）；  
（三）违禁品、枪支弹药、易燃易爆物品、剧毒物品以及其他危险品，扣押机关依照国家有关规定处理后，随案移送原物照片和清单。  
对于查封、扣押的货币、有价证券等依法不移送的，人民法院受理案件时，应当审查是否附有原物照片、清单或者其他证明文件。

**Article 291** For the following tangible objects that are not suitable for transfer, when the People's Court accepts a case, it shall review whether relevant evidentiary materials are attached; if appraisal (including valuation) is necessary, the appraisal conclusion shall be attached:  
(1) For bulky items not easy to transport, the seizing organ shall make a list, attach photographs of the original items and sealing formalities, and indicate the storage location;  
(2) For items easily subject to rot, mildew, or not easy to preserve, after the seizing organ disposes of them by sale, it shall transfer with the case the photographs of the original items, the list, and the voucher of the sale proceeds (copy);  
(3) For prohibited articles, firearms and ammunition, flammable and explosive items, highly toxic items, and other dangerous items, after the seizing organ disposes of them in accordance with relevant state regulations, it shall transfer with the case the photographs of the original items and the list.  
For sealed or seized currency, negotiable securities, etc., that are not transferred according to law, when the People's Court accepts the case, it shall review whether photographs of the original items, a list, or other certifying documents are attached.

<a id="art-292"></a>第二百九十二条 人民法院受理案件时，对于侦查机关冻结在金融机构的存款，应当审查是否附有金融机构出具的证明文件原件。人民法院作出生效判决后，通知该金融机构上缴国库，同时将判决书送达有关财政机关。金融机构应当在接到执行通知书后十五日内向人民法院送交执行回单。

**Article 292** When the People's Court accepts a case, it shall review whether, for the deposits frozen by the investigation organ in a financial institution, the original certifying documents issued by the financial institution are attached. After the People's Court renders a judgment that takes legal effect, it shall notify the said financial institution to turn over the funds to the State Treasury, and at the same time serve the judgment on the relevant financial authorities. The financial institution shall, within fifteen days after receiving the execution notice, submit an execution receipt to the People's Court.

<a id="art-293"></a>第二百九十三条 对于查封、扣押的赃款、赃物依法不移送的，人民法院作出的判决生效后，由原审的人民法院通知查封、扣押机关上缴国库，同时将通知及判决书送达有关财政机关。查封、扣押机关应当在接到执行通知书后十五日内向人民法院送交执行回单。

**Article 293** For sealed or seized illegal gains or stolen goods that are not transferred according to law, after the judgment rendered by the People's Court takes legal effect, the original trial People's Court shall notify the sealing or seizing organ to turn them over to the State Treasury, and at the same time serve the notice and the judgment on the relevant financial authorities. The sealing or seizing organ shall, within fifteen days after receiving the execution notice, submit an execution receipt to the People's Court.

<a id="art-294"></a>第二百九十四条 对于人民法院扣押、冻结的赃款、赃物及其孳息，人民法院作出的判决生效后，由原审人民法院依照生效的法律文书进行处理。除依法返还被害人的以外，应当一律没收，上缴国库。法律另有规定的除外。  
对人民检察院、公安机关因犯罪嫌疑人死亡，申请人民法院裁定通知冻结犯罪嫌疑人存款、汇款等的金融机构，将该犯罪嫌疑人的存款、汇款等上缴国库或者返还被害人的案件，人民法院应当经过阅卷、审查有关证据材料后作出裁定。

**Article 294** For illegal gains, stolen goods, and their fruits that have been seized or frozen by the People's Court, after the judgment rendered by the People's Court takes legal effect, the original trial People's Court shall dispose of them in accordance with the effective legal instrument. Except for those that are returned to the victim according to law, all shall be confiscated and turned over to the State Treasury, unless otherwise provided by law.  
For a case where the People's Procuratorate or public security organ, due to the death of a criminal suspect, applies to the People's Court for a ruling to notify the financial institution that froze the suspect's deposits, remittances, etc., to turn over the said deposits, remittances, etc. to the State Treasury or return them to the victim, the People's Court shall, after reviewing the case files and examining the relevant evidentiary materials, render a ruling.

<a id="art-295"></a>第二百九十五条 对于扣押、冻结的与本案无关的财物，已列入清单的，人民法院应当通知扣押、冻结机关依法处理。被告人被判处财产刑的，人民法院应当通知扣押、冻结机关将拟返还被告人的财物移交人民法院执行刑罚。  
十七、审判监督程序

**Article 295** For property, already listed, that has been seized or frozen and is unrelated to the case, the People's Court shall notify the seizing or freezing organ to dispose of it according to law. If the defendant is sentenced to a property penalty, the People's Court shall notify the seizing or freezing organ to transfer the property to be returned to the defendant to the People's Court for execution of the punishment.  
17. Procedure for Trial Supervision

<a id="art-296"></a>第二百九十六条 各级人民法院对当事人及其法定代理人、近亲属对已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定提出的申诉，应当进行登记并认真审查处理。

**Article 296** People's Courts at all levels shall register and carefully examine and handle any petition filed by a party, their legal representative, or near relative against a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect.

<a id="art-297"></a>第二百九十七条 人民法院经审查，对不符合[刑事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-203)规定的申诉，按来信、来访处理。

**Article 297** After examination, if a petition does not meet the requirements of Article 203 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the People's Court shall handle it as correspondence or visit.

<a id="art-298"></a>第二百九十八条 受理、审查申诉一般由作出发生法律效力的判决、裁定的人民法院进行。直接向上级人民法院申诉的，如果没有经作出发生法律效力的判决、裁定的人民法院审查处理，上级人民法院可以交该人民法院审查，并告知申诉人；如果属于案情疑难、复杂、重大的，或者已经由作出发生法律效力的判决、裁定的人民法院审查处理后仍坚持申诉的，上级人民法院可以直接受理、审查，下级人民法院也可以请求移送上一级人民法院审查处理。

**Article 298** The acceptance and examination of petitions shall generally be conducted by the People's Court that rendered the judgment or ruling that has taken legal effect. If a petition is submitted directly to a higher People's Court, and if it has not been examined and handled by the People's Court that rendered the judgment or ruling that has taken legal effect, the higher People's Court may refer it to that People's Court for examination and shall inform the petitioner; if the case is difficult, complex, or major in nature, or if the petitioner persists in the petition after examination and handling by the People's Court that rendered the judgment or ruling that has taken legal effect, the higher People's Court may directly accept and examine it, and the lower People's Court may also request that the case be transferred to the higher People's Court for examination and handling.

<a id="art-299"></a>第二百九十九条 原审人民法院审查处理的申诉、上级人民法院直接处理的申诉和转交下级人民法院审查处理的申诉，应当立申诉卷。

**Article 299** For petitions examined and handled by the original trial People's Court, petitions directly handled by a higher People's Court, and petitions referred to a lower People's Court for examination and handling, a petition case file shall be established.

<a id="art-300"></a>第三百条 第二审人民法院对不服本院维持第一审人民法院裁判的申诉，可以交由第一审人民法院审查。第一审人民法院审查后，应当写出审查报告，提出处理意见，报第二审人民法院审定。

**Article 300** The second-instance People's Court may refer a petition against its own judgment or ruling upholding the judgment or ruling of the first-instance People's Court to the first-instance People's Court for examination. After examination, the first-instance People's Court shall write an examination report, propose handling opinions, and submit them to the second-instance People's Court for review and decision.

<a id="art-301"></a>第三百零一条 对最高人民法院核准死刑的案件或者授权高级人民法院核准死刑案件的申诉，可以由原核准的人民法院直接处理，也可以交由原审人民法院审查。原审人民法院应当写出审查报告，提出处理意见，逐级上报原核准的人民法院审定。

**Article 301** For petitions concerning cases in which the death sentence has been approved by the Supreme People's Court or cases in which the death sentence has been approved by a Higher People's Court under authorization, the original approving People's Court may directly handle them, or may refer them to the original trial People's Court for examination. The original trial People's Court shall write an examination report, propose handling opinions, and submit them level by level to the original approving People's Court for review and decision.

<a id="art-302"></a>第三百零二条 人民法院受理申诉后，应当在三个月内作出决定，至迟不得超过六个月。经审查，认为有[刑事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定的情形之一的，由院长提请审判委员会决定重新审判；对不符合[刑事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定的申诉，应当说服申诉人撤回申诉；对仍然坚持申诉的，应当书面通知驳回。

**Article 302** After accepting a petition, the People's Court shall make a decision within three months, and in no case shall it exceed six months. Upon examination, if it is found that there is any circumstance provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the president shall submit it to the adjudicative committee for a decision to retry the case; for a petition that does not meet the requirements of Article 204 of the Criminal Procedure Law, the petitioner shall be persuaded to withdraw the petition; if the petitioner still insists on the petition, the petition shall be rejected by written notice.

<a id="art-303"></a>第三百零三条 申诉人对驳回申诉不服的，可以向上一级人民法院申诉。上一级人民法院经审查认为申诉不符合[刑事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-204)规定的，应当予以驳回。经两级人民法院处理后又提出申诉的，如果没有新的充分理由，人民法院可以不再受理。

**Article 303** If a petitioner refuses to accept the rejection of the petition, they may file a petition with the People's Court at the next higher level. If the higher People's Court, upon examination, finds that the petition does not meet the requirements of Article 204 of the Criminal Procedure Law, it shall reject it. If a petition is filed again after being handled by two levels of People's Courts, and there is no new sufficient reason, the People's Court may refuse to accept it.

<a id="art-304"></a>第三百零四条 各级人民法院院长对本院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现在认定事实或者适用法律上确有错误，经提交审判委员会讨论决定再审的案件，应当另行组成合议庭进行再审。

**Article 304** If the president of a People's Court at any level discovers that there is indeed an error in the determination of facts or the application of law in a judgment or ruling of that court that has already taken legal effect, and after submitting it to the adjudicative committee for discussion and a decision to retry the case, a new collegial panel shall be formed to conduct the retrial.

<a id="art-305"></a>第三百零五条 最高人民法院对各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定，上级人民法院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现确有错误，可以指令下级人民法院再审；对于原判决、裁定认定事实正确，但是在适用法律上有错误，或者案情疑难、复杂、重大的，或者有其他不宜由原审人民法院审理的情况的案件，也可以提审。

**Article 305** If the Supreme People's Court discovers that there is indeed an error in a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect of any People's Court, or if a higher People's Court discovers that there is indeed an error in a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect of a lower People's Court, it may instruct the lower People's Court to conduct a retrial; for a case where the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling are correct but there is an error in the application of law, or the case is difficult, complex, or major, or there are other circumstances making it inappropriate for the original trial People's Court to adjudicate, it may also take over the case for retrial.

<a id="art-306"></a>第三百零六条 最高人民检察院对各级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，上级人民检察院对下级人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决和裁定，如果发现确有错误，按照审判监督程序向同级人民法院提出抗诉的案件，接受抗诉的人民法院应当组成合议庭重新审理；对于原判决事实不清或者证据不足的，可以指令下级人民法院再审，并将指令再审的决定书抄送抗诉的人民检察院。

**Article 306** If the Supreme People's Procuratorate discovers that there is indeed an error in a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect of any People's Court, or if a higher People's Procuratorate discovers that there is indeed an error in a judgment or ruling that has already taken legal effect of a lower People's Court, and files a protest with the People's Court at the same level in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, the People's Court that accepts the protest shall form a collegial panel to retry the case; if the facts in the original judgment are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, it may instruct a lower People's Court to conduct a retrial, and shall serve a copy of the decision to instruct the retrial on the People's Procuratorate that filed the protest.

<a id="art-307"></a>第三百零七条 人民法院决定按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，除人民检察院提起抗诉的外，应当制作再审决定书再审期间不停止原判决、裁定的执行。

**Article 307** For a case that the People's Court decides to retry in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, except for cases where the People's Procuratorate files a protest, the People's Court shall issue a retrial decision. During the retrial period, the execution of the original judgment or ruling shall not be suspended.

<a id="art-308"></a>第三百零八条 人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，应当对原判决、裁定认定的事实、证据和适用法律进行全面审查。

**Article 308** For a case that the People's Court retries in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, it shall comprehensively examine the facts, evidence, and application of law as determined in the original judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-309"></a>第三百零九条 人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审判的案件，如果原来是第一审案件，应当依照第一审程序进行审判，所作的判决、裁定，可以上诉、抗诉；如果原来是第二审案件，或者是上级人民法院提审的案件，应当依照第二审程序进行审判，所作的判决、裁定，是终审的判决、裁定。

**Article 309** For a case that the People's Court retries in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, if the case was originally a first-instance case, the trial shall be conducted in accordance with the first-instance procedure, and the judgment or ruling rendered may be appealed or protested; if the case was originally a second-instance case, or is a case taken over by a higher People's Court for trial, the trial shall be conducted in accordance with the second-instance procedure, and the judgment or ruling rendered shall be a final judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-310"></a>第三百一十条 按照审判监督程序进行再审的刑事自诉案件，应当依法作出判决、裁定；附带民事部分可以调解结案。

**Article 310** For a criminal private prosecution case that is retried in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, a judgment or ruling shall be rendered according to law; the incidental civil part may be concluded through mediation.

<a id="art-311"></a>第三百一十一条 再审案件的审理期限，依照[刑事诉讼法第二百零七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-207)的规定执行。

**Article 311** The trial period for a retrial case shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Article 207 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-312"></a>第三百一十二条 再审案件经过重新审理后，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）原判决、裁定认定事实和适用法律正确、量刑适当的，应当裁定驳回申诉或者抗诉；  
（二）原判决、裁定认定事实没有错误，但适用法律有错误，或者量刑不当的，应当改判。按照第二审程序审理的案件，认为必须判处被告人死刑立即执行的，直接改判后，应当报请最高人民法院核准；  
（三）应当对被告人实行数罪并罚的案件，原判决、裁定没有分别定罪量刑的，应当撤销原判决、裁定，重新定罪量刑，并决定执行的刑罚；  
（四）按照第二审程序审理的案件，原判决、裁定认定事实不清或者证据不足的，可以在查清事实后改判，也可以裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判。原判决、裁定认定事实不清，证据不足，经再审仍无法查清，证据不足，不能认定原审被告人有罪的，应当参照[本解释第一百七十六条](#art-176)第（四）项规定，判决宣告被告人无罪。  
十八、涉外刑事案件审理程序

**Article 312** After a retrial case has been retried, it shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the facts determined and the law applied in the original judgment or ruling are correct and the sentence is appropriate, a ruling shall be made to reject the petition or protest;  
(2) If the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling are not erroneous, but the application of law is erroneous, or the sentence is inappropriate, the judgment shall be amended. For a case tried in accordance with the second-instance procedure, if it is determined that the defendant must be sentenced to death with immediate execution, after directly amending the judgment, the case shall be submitted to the Supreme People's Court for approval;  
(3) For a case where the defendant should be subjected to combined punishment for multiple crimes, if the original judgment or ruling did not separately convict and sentence for each crime, the original judgment or ruling shall be quashed, and a new conviction and sentence shall be imposed, and the punishment to be executed shall be decided;  
(4) For a case tried in accordance with the second-instance procedure, if the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling are unclear or the evidence is insufficient, the judgment may be amended after the facts are clarified, or a ruling may be made to quash the original judgment and remand the case to the original trial People's Court for retrial. If the facts determined in the original judgment or ruling are unclear, the evidence is insufficient, and after retrial the facts still cannot be clarified and the evidence is insufficient to establish the guilt of the original trial defendant, the defendant shall be declared not guilty by judgment, with reference to the provisions of Article 176(4) of this Interpretation.  
18. Procedure for Trial of Foreign-Related Criminal Cases

<a id="art-313"></a>第三百一十三条 本解释所称的涉外刑事案件是指：  
（一）在中华人民共和国领域内，外国人犯罪的或者我国公民侵犯外国人合法权利的刑事案件；  
（二）在中华人民共和国领域外，符合[刑法第八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-8)、第十条规定情形的外国人对中华人民共和国国家和公民犯罪和中国公民犯罪的案件；  
（三）符合[刑法第九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-9)规定的情形，中华人民共和国在所承担国际条约义务范围内行使管辖权的案件。

**Article 313** For the purposes of this Interpretation, "foreign-related criminal cases" means:  
(1) Criminal cases occurring within the territory of the People's Republic of China where a foreigner commits a crime or where a Chinese citizen infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a foreigner;  
(2) Cases occurring outside the territory of the People's Republic of China where, under the circumstances provided for in Articles 8 and 10 of the Criminal Law, a foreigner commits a crime against the State or a citizen of the People's Republic of China, or a Chinese citizen commits a crime;  
(3) Cases where, under the circumstances provided for in Article 9 of the Criminal Law, the People's Republic of China exercises jurisdiction within the scope of its international treaty obligations.

<a id="art-314"></a>第三百一十四条 外国人的国籍以其入境时的有效证件予以确认；国籍不明的，以公安机关会同外事部门查明的为准。国籍确实无法查明的，以无国籍人对待，适用涉外刑事案件审理程序。

**Article 314** The nationality of a foreigner shall be confirmed by their valid entry documents; if the nationality is unclear, it shall be determined by the public security organ in conjunction with the foreign affairs department. If the nationality truly cannot be ascertained, the person shall be treated as a stateless person, and the procedures for trial of foreign-related criminal cases shall apply.

<a id="art-315"></a>第三百一十五条 享有外交特权和豁免权的外国人的刑事责任问题，通过外交途径解决。

**Article 315** The question of criminal liability of a foreigner enjoying diplomatic privileges and immunity shall be resolved through diplomatic channels.

<a id="art-316"></a>第三百一十六条 外国籍被告人在刑事诉讼中，享有我国法律规定的诉讼权利并承担义务。

**Article 316** A foreign defendant in criminal proceedings shall enjoy the litigation rights and bear the obligations provided for by the laws of China.

<a id="art-317"></a>第三百一十七条 中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约中有关于刑事诉讼程序具体规定的，适用该国际条约的规定。但是，我国声明保留的条款除外。

**Article 317** If an international treaty concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China contains specific provisions on criminal procedure, the provisions of such international treaty shall apply. However, provisions on which China has declared a reservation are excepted.

<a id="art-318"></a>第三百一十八条 人民法院审判涉外刑事案件，应当公开进行。但是涉及国家秘密或者个人隐私的案件，不公开审理。公开审理的案件，由人民法院发旁听证的，凭证入场旁听。

**Article 318** When the People's Court tries a foreign-related criminal case, it shall be conducted in public. However, cases involving State secrets or personal privacy shall not be heard in public. For cases heard in public, the People's Court shall issue admission tickets; admission to observe the trial shall be by ticket.

<a id="art-319"></a>第三百一十九条 人民法院审判涉外刑事案件，使用中华人民共和国通用的语言、文字，应当为外国籍被告人提供翻译。如果外国籍被告人通晓中国语言、文字，拒绝他人翻译的，应当由本人出具书面声明，或者将他的口头声明记录在卷。诉讼文书为中文本，应当附有被告人通晓的外文译本，译本不加盖人民法院印章，以中文本为准。翻译费用由被告人承担。  
如果外国籍被告人拒收诉讼文书的，应当依照[本解释第一百零四条](#art-104)第三款的规定处理。

**Article 319** When the People's Court tries a foreign-related criminal case, it shall use the commonly used language and script of the People's Republic of China, and shall provide interpretation for the foreign defendant. If the foreign defendant is proficient in the Chinese language and script and refuses interpretation by others, they shall issue a written statement or have their oral statement recorded in the case file. The litigation documents shall be in Chinese, with a translation in a language the defendant understands attached; the translation shall not be affixed with the seal of the People's Court, and the Chinese version shall prevail. The translation costs shall be borne by the defendant.  
If the foreign defendant refuses to receive the litigation documents, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of Article 104, paragraph 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-320"></a>第三百二十条 外国籍被告人委托律师辩护的，以及附带民事诉讼的原告人、自诉人委托律师代理诉讼的，应当委托具有中华人民共和国律师资格并依法取得执业证书的律师。  
外国籍被告人没有委托辩护人的，人民法院可以为其指定辩护人。被告人拒绝辩护人为其辩护的，应当由其提出书面声明，或者将其口头声明记录在卷后，人民法院予以准许。  
在中华人民共和国领域外居住的外国人寄给中国律师或者中国公民的授权委托书，必须经所在国公证机关证明、所在国外交部或者其授权机关认证，并经中国驻该国使、领馆认证，才具有法律效力。但中国与该国之间有互免认证协定的除外。

**Article 320** If a foreign defendant entrusts a lawyer to defend them, and if the plaintiff in an incidental civil action or the private prosecutor entrusts a lawyer to represent them in the proceedings, they shall entrust a lawyer who has the qualification of a lawyer of the People's Republic of China and has lawfully obtained a practicing certificate.  
If the foreign defendant has not entrusted a defender, the People's Court may appoint a defender for them. If the defendant refuses the defender appointed for them, they shall submit a written statement, or after their oral statement is recorded in the case file, the People's Court shall permit the refusal.  
A power of attorney sent by a foreigner residing outside the territory of the People's Republic of China to a Chinese lawyer or a Chinese citizen shall have legal effect only after it is notarized by a notary organ of the country where the foreigner resides, certified by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of that country or an organ authorized by it, and certified by the Chinese embassy or consulate in that country. However, this shall not apply if there is an agreement between China and that country on mutual exemption of certification requirements.

<a id="art-321"></a>第三百二十一条 人民法院审理涉外刑事案件及处理结果，应当及时通报当地外事部门。

**Article 321** When the People's Court tries a foreign-related criminal case and upon the outcome of the case, it shall promptly notify the local foreign affairs department.

<a id="art-322"></a>第三百二十二条 对涉外刑事案件的被告人及人民法院认定的其他相关犯罪嫌疑人，可以决定限制出境；对开庭审理案件时必须到庭的证人，可以要求暂缓出境。限制出境的决定应当通报同级公安机关或者国家安全机关。

**Article 322** For the defendant in a foreign-related criminal case and other relevant criminal suspects determined by the People's Court, a decision may be made to restrict their departure from the country; for witnesses who must appear in court when the case is tried, a request may be made to postpone their departure. The decision to restrict departure from the country shall be notified to the public security organ or the state security organ at the same level.

<a id="art-323"></a>第三百二十三条 人民法院决定限制外国人和中国公民出境的，应当口头或者书面通知被限制出境的人，也可以采取扣留其护照或者其他有效出入境证件的办法，在案件审理终结前不得离境。

**Article 323** If the People's Court decides to restrict a foreigner or a Chinese citizen from leaving the country, it shall notify the person whose departure is restricted orally or in writing, and may also adopt measures such as detaining their passport or other valid exit-entry documents; the person shall not leave the country before the conclusion of the trial of the case.

<a id="art-324"></a>第三百二十四条 对需要在边防检查站阻止外国人和中国公民出境的，人民法院应当填写口岸阻止人员出境通知书。控制口岸在本省、自治区、直辖市的，应当向本省、自治区、直辖市公安厅（局）办理交控手续。控制口岸不在本省、自治区、直辖市的，应当通过有关省、自治区、直辖市公安厅（局）办理交控手续。在紧急情况下，如确有必要，也可以先向边防检查站交控，然后补办交控手续。

**Article 324** : If it is necessary to prevent a foreigner or Chinese citizen from exiting the country at a border inspection station, the People's Court shall complete a Notice of Preventing Personnel from Exiting at the Port. If the port under control is within this province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the People's Court shall go through the procedures for delivering the control to the public security department (bureau) of this province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. If the port under control is not within this province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, the People's Court shall go through the procedures for delivering the control through the relevant public security department (bureau) of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government. In an emergency, if truly necessary, delivery of control may first be made to the border inspection station, and subsequently the procedures for supplementary delivery of control shall be completed.

<a id="art-325"></a>第三百二十五条 根据中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约，或者按照对等互惠原则，我国法院和外国法院可以互相请求，代为一定的诉讼行为。  
外国法院请求的事项同中华人民共和国的主权、安全或者社会公共利益不相容以及违反中国法律的，应当予以驳回；不属于我国法院职权范围的，应当予以退回，并说明理由。

**Article 325** : In accordance with international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, or pursuant to the principle of reciprocity, Chinese courts and foreign courts may request each other to undertake certain litigation acts on their behalf.  
If the matter requested by a foreign court is incompatible with the sovereignty, security, or social public interest of the People's Republic of China or violates Chinese law, it shall be rejected; if it is not within the functions and powers of Chinese courts, it shall be returned with an explanation of the reasons.

<a id="art-326"></a>第三百二十六条 请求与我国签订司法协助协定的国家的法院代为一定诉讼行为的，必须由所在省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院报经最高人民法院审查同意。与我国签订司法协助协定的国家的法院请求我国法院代为一定诉讼行为的，应当由最高人民法院审查后转达。

**Article 326** : To request a court of a country that has concluded a judicial assistance agreement with China to undertake certain litigation acts on its behalf, the Higher People's Court of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the requesting court is located must report to and obtain the review and consent of the Supreme People's Court. Where a court of a country that has concluded a judicial assistance agreement with China requests a Chinese court to undertake certain litigation acts on its behalf, the request shall be reviewed by the Supreme People's Court and then forwarded.

<a id="art-327"></a>第三百二十七条 向在中华人民共和国领域外居住的当事人送达诉讼文书，采用下列方式：  
（一）通过外交途径送达；  
（二）对中国籍当事人，可以委托我国使、领馆代为送达；  
（三）当事人所在国的法律允许邮寄送达的，可以邮寄送达；  
（四）当事人所在国与我国有刑事司法协助协定的，按照协定规定的方式送达；  
（五）当事人是自诉案件的自诉人或者是附带民事诉讼的原告人，有诉讼代理人的，可以由诉讼代理人送达。

**Article 327** : Service of litigation documents on a party residing outside the territory of the People's Republic of China shall be carried out by the following methods:  
(1) Service through diplomatic channels;  
(2) For a party of Chinese nationality, service may be entrusted to Chinese diplomatic or consular missions;  
(3) If the law of the country where the party is located permits service by mail, service may be effected by mail;  
(4) If the country where the party is located has a criminal judicial assistance agreement with China, service shall be effected in the manner prescribed in the agreement;  
(5) If the party is the private prosecutor in a case of private prosecution or the plaintiff in an incidental civil action and has a litigation agent, service may be effected by the litigation agent.

<a id="art-328"></a>第三百二十八条 人民法院与同我国建交国家的法院通过外交途径相互请求送达法律文书的，除该国同我国已有司法协助协定的依协定外，依据互惠原则办理。

**Article 328** : Where a People's Court and a court of a country having diplomatic relations with China request each other to serve legal documents through diplomatic channels, unless a judicial assistance agreement exists between that country and China, in which case the agreement shall be followed, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the principle of reciprocity.

<a id="art-329"></a>第三百二十九条 外国法院通过外交途径请求我国法院向我国公民以及在华的第三国当事人送达有关刑事法律文书，除有司法协助协定的外，按照下列程序处理：  
（一）由该国驻华使、领馆将法律文书交外交部领事司转递有关高级人民法院。该高级人民法院经审查后，认为可以代为送达的，应当指定有关中级人民法院送达当事人。请求方附有送达回证的，当事人应当在送达回证上签字；未附送达回证的，由负责送达的中级人民法院出具送达证明。送达回证或者送达证明由高级人民法院通过外交部领事司转递请求方；  
（二）受送达的当事人享有外交特权与豁免权的，不予送达；不属于人民法院职权范围或者因地址不明及其他原因不能送达的，有关高级人民法院应当注明不能送达的原因，由外交部领事司向请求方说明，予以退回。

**Article 329** : Where a foreign court requests, through diplomatic channels, a Chinese court to serve criminal legal documents on a Chinese citizen or a third-country party within China, unless a judicial assistance agreement exists, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The diplomatic or consular mission of that country in China shall deliver the legal documents to the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which shall forward them to the relevant Higher People's Court. After review, if the Higher People's Court considers that service may be effected on its behalf, it shall designate the relevant Intermediate People's Court to serve the documents on the party. If the requesting party attaches a receipt of service, the party shall sign the receipt; if no receipt is attached, the Intermediate People's Court responsible for service shall issue a certificate of service. The receipt or certificate of service shall be forwarded to the requesting party by the Higher People's Court through the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;  
(2) If the party to be served enjoys diplomatic privileges and immunities, service shall not be effected; if service is not within the functions and powers of a People's Court or cannot be effected due to an unclear address or other reasons, the relevant Higher People's Court shall note the reasons why service cannot be effected, and the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall explain the reasons to the requesting party and return the documents.

<a id="art-330"></a>第三百三十条 外国驻华使、领馆通过外交途径请求我国法院向在华的该国国民送达法律文书，适用[本解释第三百二十九条](#art-329)的规定。

**Article 330** : Where a foreign diplomatic or consular mission in China requests, through diplomatic channels, a Chinese court to serve legal documents on a national of that country within China, the provisions of Article 329 of this Interpretation shall apply.

<a id="art-331"></a>第三百三十一条 人民法院通过外交途径向国外当事人送达法律文书，按照下列程序处理：  
（一）请求送达的法律文书必须经高级人民法院审查，由高级人民法院交外交部领事司转递；  
（二）必须准确注明受送达当事人的外文姓名、性别、年龄、国籍及详细地址，并将该案的基本情况函告外交部领事司；  
（三）必须附有注明被请求方法院名称的送达请求书。被请求方法院名称不明的，可以请求该当事人所在地区主管法院送达。所送法律文书必须附有被请求方官方通用文字或者该国同意使用的第二国文字译本。如果被请求方对请求书及法律文书有公证、认证等特殊要求，由外交部领事司通知高级人民法院。

**Article 331** : Where a People's Court serves legal documents on a party abroad through diplomatic channels, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The legal documents requested for service must be reviewed by the Higher People's Court, and the Higher People's Court shall deliver them to the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for forwarding;  
(2) The foreign name, gender, age, nationality, and detailed address of the party to be served must be accurately indicated, and the basic information of the case shall be notified to the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs by letter;  
(3) A request for service indicating the name of the requested court must be attached. If the name of the requested court is unknown, the service may be requested from the competent court of the region where the party is located. The legal documents to be served must be accompanied by a translation into the official language of the requested party or into a second language that the country agrees to use. If the requested party has special requirements such as notarization or authentication for the request and legal documents, the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs shall notify the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-332"></a>第三百三十二条 人民法院委托我使、领馆向在外国的中国籍当事人送达法律文书，按照下列程序处理：  
（一）委托送达的法律文书必须经高级人民法院审查，由高级人民法院交外交部领事司转递；  
（二）必须准确注明受送达当事人的外文姓名、性别、年龄及详细地址，并将该案的基本情况函告外交部领事司。

**Article 332** : Where a People's Court entrusts Chinese diplomatic or consular missions to serve legal documents on a Chinese party abroad, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following procedures:  
(1) The legal documents entrusted for service must be reviewed by the Higher People's Court, and the Higher People's Court shall deliver them to the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for forwarding;  
(2) The foreign name, gender, age, and detailed address of the party to be served must be accurately indicated, and the basic information of the case shall be notified to the Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs by letter.

<a id="art-333"></a>第三百三十三条 我国法院和外国法院通过外交途径相互请求送达法律文书的收费，参照最高人民法院、外交部、司法部《关于我国法院和外国法院通过外交途径相互委托送达法律文书和调查取证费用收支办法的通知》办理。

**Article 333** : Fees for the mutual requests for service of legal documents through diplomatic channels between Chinese courts and foreign courts shall be handled by reference to the Notice of the Supreme People's Court, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the Ministry of Justice on the Measures for the Receipt and Payment of Fees for Mutual Entrusted Service of Legal Documents and Investigation and Evidence Collection through Diplomatic Channels between Chinese Courts and Foreign Courts.

<a id="art-334"></a>第三百三十四条 外国籍被告人被逮捕、审判或者在案件审理中死亡，应当通知其所属国家的驻华使、领馆，并按照有关规定处理。

**Article 334** : If a foreign defendant is arrested, tried, or dies during the trial of a case, the diplomatic or consular mission of the country to which he belongs in China shall be notified, and the matter shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-335"></a>第三百三十五条 外国法院请求我国法院提供司法协助的请求书及所附文件，应当附有中文译本或者国际条约规定的其他文字文本。

**Article 335** : A request for judicial assistance from a foreign court to a Chinese court and the attached documents shall be accompanied by a Chinese translation or a text in another language as prescribed by an international treaty.

<a id="art-336"></a>第三百三十六条 涉外刑事案件审理过程中的其他事宜，按照本解释的有关规定和其他有关规定办理。  
十九、执行程序

**Article 336** : Other matters in the course of trying a criminal case involving foreign elements shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation and other relevant provisions.  
19. Enforcement Procedures

<a id="art-337"></a>第三百三十七条 判决和裁定在发生法律效力后执行。  
下列判决和裁定是发生法律效力的判决和裁定：  
（一）已过法定期限没有上诉、抗诉的判决和裁定；  
（二）终审的判决和裁定；  
（三）高级人民法院核准的死刑缓期二年执行的判决、裁定和依据最高人民法院的授权核准死刑的判决和裁定；  
（四）最高人民法院核准死刑的判决和裁定。

**Article 337** : Judgments and rulings shall be enforced after they become legally effective.  
The following judgments and rulings are judgments and rulings that have become legally effective:  
(1) Judgments and rulings for which the statutory period has expired without an appeal or protest;  
(2) Final judgments and rulings;  
(3) Judgments and rulings approving a death sentence with a two-year reprieve rendered by a Higher People's Court, and judgments and rulings approving the death penalty pursuant to the authorization of the Supreme People's Court;  
(4) Judgments and rulings approving the death penalty rendered by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-338"></a>第三百三十八条 最高人民法院判处和核准的死刑立即执行的判决、裁定，应当由最高人民法院院长签发执行死刑命令；最高人民法院授权高级人民法院核准的死刑立即执行的判决、裁定，应当由高级人民法院院长签发执行死刑命令。

**Article 338** : For a judgment or ruling imposing the death penalty with immediate execution that is rendered or approved by the Supreme People's Court, the order for execution of the death sentence shall be signed by the President of the Supreme People's Court; for a judgment or ruling imposing the death penalty with immediate execution that is approved by a Higher People's Court pursuant to the authorization of the Supreme People's Court, the order for execution of the death sentence shall be signed by the President of the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-339"></a>第三百三十九条 被判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期间，如果故意犯罪的，应当由人民检察院提起公诉，罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院依法审判，所作的判决可以上诉、抗诉。  
认定构成故意犯罪的判决、裁定发生法律效力后，由作出生效判决、裁定的人民法院，依照[本解释第二百七十五条](#art-275)第（四）项或者第二百七十七条的规定报请上级人民法院或者由本院核准犯罪分子死刑立即执行。上级人民法院或者本院核准后，交罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院执行死刑。

**Article 339** : If a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve commits an intentional crime during the period of suspension of execution of the death sentence, the People's Procuratorate shall initiate a public prosecution, and the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall try the case according to law, and the judgment rendered may be appealed or protested.  
After the judgment or ruling finding the intentional crime has become legally effective, the People's Court that rendered the effective judgment or ruling shall, in accordance with Article 275(4) or Article 277 of this Interpretation, report to the higher people's court or request this court to approve the immediate execution of the death penalty on the criminal. After the higher people's court or this court approves, the case shall be transferred to the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence for execution of the death sentence.

<a id="art-340"></a>第三百四十条 死刑缓期二年执行期满应当减刑的，人民法院应当及时减刑。死刑缓期二年执行期满减为有期徒刑的，刑期自死刑缓期二年执行期满之日起计算。

**Article 340** : Where commutation of sentence should be granted upon expiration of the two-year reprieve for a death sentence with a two-year reprieve, the People's Court shall promptly grant the commutation. If the death sentence with a two-year reprieve is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment upon expiration of the period, the term of imprisonment shall be calculated from the date on which the two-year reprieve period expires.

<a id="art-341"></a>第三百四十一条 最高人民法院和高级人民法院的执行死刑命令，均由高级人民法院交付原审人民法院执行，原审人民法院接到执行死刑命令后，应当在七日内执行。

**Article 341** : All orders for execution of a death sentence from the Supreme People's Court and a Higher People's Court shall be delivered by the Higher People's Court to the original trial People's Court for enforcement. After receiving the order for execution of the death sentence, the original trial People's Court shall execute it within seven days.

<a id="art-342"></a>第三百四十二条 下级人民法院在接到执行死刑命令后，发现有下列情形之一的，应当停止执行，并立即报告核准死刑的人民法院，由核准死刑的人民法院作出裁定：  
（一）在执行前发现裁判可能有错误的；  
（二）在执行前罪犯揭发重大犯罪事实或者有其他重大立功表现，可能需要改判的；  
（三）罪犯正在怀孕的。  
前款第（一）项、第（三）项规定停止执行的原因消失后，必须报请核准死刑的人民法院院长再签发执行死刑命令才能执行；由于前款第（三）项规定的原因停止执行的，应当报请核准死刑的人民法院依法改判。

**Article 342** : After receiving the order for execution of a death sentence, if a lower people's court discovers that any of the following circumstances exists, it shall suspend the execution and immediately report to the People's Court that approved the death sentence, and the People's Court that approved the death sentence shall render a ruling:  
(1) It is discovered before execution that the judgment or ruling may contain error;  
(2) Before execution, the criminal exposes major criminal facts or has other major meritorious performance, and the sentence may need to be modified;  
(3) The criminal is pregnant.  
After the cause for suspension of execution under items (1) and (3) of the preceding paragraph has been eliminated, it must be reported to the President of the People's Court that approved the death sentence to re-issue the order for execution of the death sentence before execution may be carried out; if execution was suspended due to the cause under item (3) of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court that approved the death sentence shall be reported to, and a legal modification of the sentence shall be made.

<a id="art-343"></a>第三百四十三条 执行死刑前，罪犯提出会见其近亲属或者其近亲属提出会见罪犯申请的，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 343** : Before execution of a death sentence, if the criminal requests to meet with his close relatives or the close relatives request to meet with the criminal, the People's Court may permit such a meeting.

<a id="art-344"></a>第三百四十四条 人民法院将罪犯交付执行死刑，应当在交付执行三日前通知同级人民检察院派员临场监督。

**Article 344** : When a People's Court delivers a criminal for execution of a death sentence, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate at the same level three days before delivery for execution to send an officer to supervise on the scene.

<a id="art-345"></a>第三百四十五条 死刑采用枪决或者注射等方法执行。  
采用注射方法执行死刑的，应当在指定的刑场或者羁押场所内执行。具体程序，依照有关规定。  
采用枪决、注射以外的其他方法执行死刑的，应当事先报请最高人民法院批准。

**Article 345** : A death sentence shall be executed by shooting or injection or other methods.  
Where execution of a death sentence is carried out by injection, it shall be executed at a designated execution ground or detention facility. The specific procedures shall be in accordance with relevant provisions.  
Where execution of a death sentence is carried out by a method other than shooting or injection, approval from the Supreme People's Court shall be sought in advance.

<a id="art-346"></a>第三百四十六条 执行死刑前，指挥执行的审判人员对罪犯应当验明正身，询问有无遗言、信札，并制作笔录，然后交付执行人员执行死刑。  
执行死刑应当公布，禁止游街示众或者其他有辱被执行人人格的行为。

**Article 346** : Before execution of a death sentence, the judicial officer directing the execution shall verify the identity of the criminal, ask the criminal whether he has any last words or letters, and make a record, and then deliver the criminal to the execution personnel for execution of the death sentence.  
Execution of a death sentence shall be announced publicly. Parading the criminal through the streets or other acts demeaning to the personality of the person subject to execution are prohibited.

<a id="art-347"></a>第三百四十七条 执行死刑完毕，应当由法医验明罪犯确实死亡后，在场书记员制作笔录。交付执行的人民法院应当将执行死刑情况（包括执行死刑前后照片）及时逐级上报最高人民法院。

**Article 347** : After execution of a death sentence is completed, a forensic medical examiner shall confirm that the criminal is indeed dead, and then the clerk present shall make a record. The People's Court that delivered the criminal for execution shall promptly report the circumstances of the execution (including photographs taken before and after the execution) level by level to the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-348"></a>第三百四十八条 执行死刑后，负责执行的人民法院应当办理以下事项：  
（一）对于死刑罪犯的遗书、遗言笔录，应当及时进行审查，涉及财产继承、债务清偿、家事嘱托等内容的，将遗书、遗言笔录交给家属，同时复制存卷备查；涉及案件线索等问题的，应当抄送有关机关；  
（二）通知罪犯家属在限期内领取罪犯尸体；有火化条件的，通知领取骨灰，过期不领取的，由人民法院通知有关单位处理。对于死刑罪犯的尸体或者骨灰的处理情况，应当记录在卷；  
（三）对外国籍罪犯执行死刑后，通知外国驻华使、领馆的程序和时限，依照有关规定办理。

**Article 348** : After execution of a death sentence, the People's Court responsible for the execution shall handle the following matters:  
(1) The will or record of last words of the criminal sentenced to death shall be promptly reviewed. If such documents involve matters such as property inheritance, debt settlement, or family entrustments, the will or record of last words shall be handed over to the family members, with a copy kept in the file for future reference; if they involve case leads or other issues, copies shall be sent to the relevant authorities;  
(2) The family members of the criminal shall be notified to collect the corpse of the criminal within a specified time limit; if cremation facilities are available, the family members shall be notified to collect the ashes. If the family members fail to collect the corpse or ashes within the time limit, the People's Court shall notify the relevant unit for disposal. The handling of the corpse or ashes of the criminal sentenced to death shall be recorded in the file;  
(3) After execution of a death sentence on a foreign criminal, the procedures and time limits for notifying the foreign diplomatic or consular mission in China shall be handled in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-349"></a>第三百四十九条 对于判处死刑缓期二年执行、无期徒刑、有期徒刑的罪犯，交付执行的人民法院应当将判决书、裁定书、人民检察院的起诉书副本、自诉状复印件、人民法院的执行通知书、结案登记表及时送达看守所，由公安机关将罪犯交付监狱执行。

**Article 349** : For a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment, the People's Court delivering the criminal for enforcement shall promptly serve the judgment, ruling, copy of the indictment from the People's Procuratorate, copy of the private complaint, the enforcement notice of the People's Court, and the case closure registration form on the detention house, and the public security organ shall deliver the criminal to the prison for enforcement.

<a id="art-350"></a>第三百五十条 收监执行决定书应当分别送达交付执行的公安机关和监狱。罪犯需要羁押执行刑罚，而判决确定前罪犯没有被羁押的，人民法院应当根据生效的判决书或者裁定书将罪犯羁押，并送交公安机关。

**Article 350** : The decision on admission to prison for enforcement shall be served separately on the public security organ delivering the criminal for enforcement and the prison. If the criminal needs to be detained to serve the sentence but was not detained before the judgment was final, the People's Court shall, according to the effective judgment or ruling, detain the criminal and deliver the criminal to the public security organ.

<a id="art-351"></a>第三百五十一条 对于判处拘役的罪犯，在判决、裁定生效后，由交付执行的人民法院将判决书、裁定书、人民检察院的起诉书副本、自诉状复印件、执行通知书、结案登记表及时送达公安机关。

**Article 351** : For a criminal sentenced to criminal detention, after the judgment or ruling becomes effective, the People's Court delivering the criminal for enforcement shall promptly serve the judgment, ruling, copy of the indictment from the People's Procuratorate, copy of the private complaint, the enforcement notice, and the case closure registration form on the public security organ.

<a id="art-352"></a>第三百五十二条 执行通知书回执经看守所盖章后，附入人民法院的诉讼案卷。

**Article 352** : The receipt of the enforcement notice, after being stamped by the detention house, shall be attached to the case file of the People's Court.

<a id="art-353"></a>第三百五十三条 被判处有期徒刑或者拘役的罪犯，有[刑事诉讼法第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-214)第一、五款规定的情形之一，人民法院决定暂予监外执行的，应当制作《暂予监外执行决定书》，载明罪犯基本情况、判决确定的罪名和刑罚、决定暂予监外执行的原因、依据等内容，并抄送人民检察院和罪犯居住地的公安机关。

**Article 353** : If a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention has any of the circumstances provided for in Article 214, paragraphs 1 and 5, of the Criminal Procedure Law, and the People's Court decides to temporarily serve the sentence outside prison, the People's Court shall prepare a Decision on Temporary Service of Sentence Outside Prison, specifying the basic information of the criminal, the crime and penalty determined by the judgment, the reasons and basis for the decision on temporary service outside prison, and other contents, and shall send a copy to the People's Procuratorate and the public security organ in the place of residence of the criminal.

<a id="art-354"></a>第三百五十四条 判决、裁定生效后，公安机关将罪犯交付执行，监狱不予收监的，监狱应当书面说明理由，由公安机关将执行通知书退回人民法院。人民法院经审查认为监狱不予收监的罪犯不符合[刑事诉讼法第二百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-214)规定的暂予监外执行条件的，应当决定将罪犯交付监狱收监执行。收监执行决定书应当分别送达交付执行的公安机关和监狱。

**Article 354** : After a judgment or ruling becomes effective, if the public security organ delivers the criminal for enforcement but the prison refuses to admit the criminal for imprisonment, the prison shall provide a written explanation of the reasons, and the public security organ shall return the enforcement notice to the People's Court. If the People's Court, upon review, finds that the criminal whom the prison refuses to admit does not meet the conditions for temporary service outside prison as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Procedure Law, it shall decide to deliver the criminal to the prison for admission and enforcement. The decision on admission to prison shall be served separately on the public security organ delivering the criminal for enforcement and the prison.

<a id="art-355"></a>第三百五十五条 第一审人民法院判处拘役或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，判决尚未发生法律效力的，不能立即交付执行如果被宣告缓刑的罪犯在押，第一审人民法院应当先行作出变更强制措施的决定，改为监视居住或者取保候审，并立即通知有关公安机关。判决发生法律效力后，应当将法律文书送达当地公安机关。

**Article 355** : If a criminal is sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment with a suspended sentence by a first-instance People's Court, and the judgment has not yet become legally effective, the criminal shall not be immediately delivered for enforcement. If the criminal sentenced to a suspended sentence is in custody, the first-instance People's Court shall first make a decision to change the compulsory measure to residential surveillance or release on bail pending investigation or trial, and immediately notify the relevant public security organ. After the judgment becomes legally effective, the legal documents shall be served on the local public security organ.

<a id="art-356"></a>第三百五十六条 被宣告缓刑、假释的犯罪分子，在缓刑、假释考验期限内再犯新罪或者被发现判决宣告以前还有其他罪没有判决，应当撤销缓刑、假释的，由审判新罪的人民法院在审判新罪时，对原判决、裁定宣告的缓刑、假释予以撤销；如果原来是上级人民法院判决、裁定宣告缓刑、假释的，审判新罪的下级人民法院也可以撤销原判决、裁定宣告的缓刑、假释。审判新罪的人民法院对原审判决、裁定宣告的缓刑、假释撤销后，应当通知原宣告缓刑、假释的人民法院和执行机关。

**Article 356** : If a criminal sentenced to a suspended sentence or parole commits a new crime within the probation or parole period, or it is discovered that before the judgment was rendered the criminal had other crimes that were not adjudicated, and the suspended sentence or parole shall be revoked, the People's Court trying the new crime shall, at the time of trying the new crime, revoke the suspended sentence or parole declared in the original judgment or ruling; if the original suspended sentence or parole was declared by a judgment or ruling of a higher people's court, the lower people's court trying the new crime may also revoke the suspended sentence or parole declared in the original judgment or ruling. After the People's Court trying the new crime revokes the suspended sentence or parole declared in the original trial judgment or ruling, it shall notify the People's Court that originally declared the suspended sentence or parole and the enforcement organ.

<a id="art-357"></a>第三百五十七条 被宣告缓刑、假释的犯罪分子，在缓刑、假释考验期限内违反法律、行政法规或者国务院公安部门有关缓刑、假释的监督管理规定，应当依法撤销缓刑、假释的，原作出缓刑、假释裁判的人民法院应当自收到同级公安机关提出的撤销缓刑、假释建议书之日起一个月内依法作出裁定。  
人民法院撤销缓刑、假释的裁定，一经作出，立即生效。

**Article 357** : If a criminal sentenced to a suspended sentence or parole violates laws, administrative regulations, or provisions of the public security department of the State Council on supervision and management of suspended sentences or parole during the probation or parole period, and the suspended sentence or parole shall be revoked according to law, the People's Court that originally rendered the judgment or ruling on the suspended sentence or parole shall, within one month from the date of receiving the proposal for revocation of the suspended sentence or parole submitted by the public security organ at the same level, render a ruling according to law.  
The ruling of the People's Court to revoke a suspended sentence or parole shall take immediate effect upon issuance.

<a id="art-358"></a>第三百五十八条 发生法律效力的刑事判决、裁定和调解书中涉及财产内容需要执行的，由原审人民法院执行。  
附带民事判决中财产的执行，依照民事诉讼法和最高人民法院的有关规定办理。

**Article 358** : Where a legally effective criminal judgment, ruling, or mediation agreement involves property content that requires enforcement, the original trial People's Court shall enforce it.  
Enforcement of property in an incidental civil judgment shall be handled in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and the relevant provisions of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-359"></a>第三百五十九条 罚金在判决规定的期限内一次或者分期缴纳。期满无故不缴纳的，人民法院应当强制缴纳。经强制缴纳仍不能全部缴纳的，人民法院在任何时候，包括在判处的主刑执行完毕后，发现被执行人有可以执行的财产的，应当追缴。  
如果由于遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸缴纳罚金确实有困难的，犯罪分子可以向人民法院申请减少或者免除。人民法院查证属实后，可以裁定对原判决确定的罚金数额予以减少或者免除。  
行政机关对被告人就同事实已经处以罚款的，人民法院判处罚金时应当予以折抵。

**Article 359** : A fine shall be paid in a lump sum or in installments within the period specified in the judgment. If the fine is not paid without justification upon expiration of the period, the People's Court shall enforce its payment. If the fine cannot be fully paid even after compulsory enforcement, the People's Court shall, at any time, including after the execution of the principal punishment imposed, pursue recovery if it discovers that the person subject to enforcement has property available for enforcement.  
If, due to irresistible disaster, the criminal truly has difficulty paying the fine, the criminal may apply to the People's Court for reduction or exemption. After verification and confirmation of the facts, the People's Court may rule to reduce or exempt the amount of the fine determined in the original judgment.  
If an administrative authority has already imposed a fine on the defendant for the same facts, the People's Court shall offset it when imposing a fine in its judgment.

<a id="art-360"></a>第三百六十条 对判处财产刑的犯罪分子或者附带民事诉讼的判决、裁定有执行财产内容的被告人，在本地无财产可供执行的，原判人民法院可以委托其财产所在地人民法院代为执行。代为执行的人民法院执行后或者无法执行的，应当将有关情况及时通知委托的人民法院。代为执行的人民法院可以将执行财产刑的财产直接上缴国库；需要退赔的财产，应当由执行的人民法院移交委托人民法院依法退赔。

**Article 360** : If a criminal sentenced to a property penalty or a defendant against whom an incidental civil judgment or ruling contains content for enforcement of property has no property available for enforcement in the locality, the original trial People's Court may entrust the People's Court in the place where the property is located to enforce it on its behalf. After the entrusted People's Court enforces the property or is unable to enforce it, it shall promptly notify the entrusting People's Court of the relevant circumstances. The entrusted People's Court may directly turn over the property collected from enforcement of the property penalty to the State treasury; property that needs to be refunded or compensated shall be transferred by the enforcing People's Court to the entrusting People's Court for refund or compensation according to law.

<a id="art-361"></a>第三百六十一条 被判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期二年执行期问，如果没有故意犯罪，死刑缓期二年执行期满后，即应当裁定减刑。如果死刑缓期二年执行期满后尚未裁定减刑前又犯新罪的，应当依法减刑后对其所犯新罪另行审判。

**Article 361** : If a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve does not commit an intentional crime during the two-year reprieve period, after the expiration of the two-year reprieve period, a ruling of commutation of sentence shall be made. If the criminal commits a new crime after the expiration of the two-year reprieve period but before a ruling of commutation of sentence is made, the criminal shall first be granted commutation of sentence according to law, and then a separate trial shall be conducted for the new crime committed.

<a id="art-362"></a>第三百六十二条 减刑、假释案件按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）对于被判处死刑缓期二年执行的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院根据省、自治区、直辖市监狱管理机关审核同意的监狱减刑建议书裁定；  
（二）对于被判处无期徒刑的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院根据省、自治区、直辖市监狱管理机关审核同意的监狱减刑、假释建议书裁定。高级人民法院应当自收到减刑、假释建议书之日起一个月内依法裁定；案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月；  
（三）对于被判处有期徒刑（包括减为有期徒刑）的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院根据当地执行机关提出的减刑、假释建议书裁定。中级人民法院应当自收到减刑、假释建议书之日起一个月内依法裁定；案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月；  
（四）对于被判处拘役的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院根据当地同级执行机关提出的减刑建议书裁定；  
（五）对于被判处管制的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院根据当地同级执行机关提出的减刑建议书裁定；  
（六）被宣告缓刑的罪犯，在缓刑考验期限内确有重大立功表现，需要予以减刑，并相应缩短缓刑考验期限的，应当由负责考察的公安派出所会同罪犯的所在单位或者基层组织提出书面意见，由罪犯所在地的中级人民法院根据当地同级执行机关提出的减刑建议书裁定；  
（七）对于公安机关看守所监管的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯所在的看守所提出意见，由当地中级人民法院根据当地同级执行机关提出的减刑、假释建议书裁定。  
前款第（四）至（七）项规定的减刑、假释，人民法院应当自收到减刑、假释建议书之日起一个月内依法裁定。

**Article 362** : Cases of commutation of sentence and parole shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) For commutation of sentence of a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, the Higher People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall render a ruling based on the prison's proposal for commutation of sentence that has been reviewed and approved by the provincial-level prison administration authority;  
(2) For commutation of sentence or parole of a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment, the Higher People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall render a ruling based on the prison's proposal for commutation of sentence or parole that has been reviewed and approved by the provincial-level prison administration authority. The Higher People's Court shall render a ruling according to law within one month from the date of receiving the proposal for commutation or parole; if the facts of the case are complex or the circumstances are special, the time limit may be extended by one month;  
(3) For commutation of sentence or parole of a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment (including commutation to fixed-term imprisonment), the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall render a ruling based on the proposal for commutation of sentence or parole submitted by the local enforcement organ. The Intermediate People's Court shall render a ruling according to law within one month from the date of receiving the proposal for commutation or parole; if the facts of the case are complex or the circumstances are special, the time limit may be extended by one month;  
(4) For commutation of sentence of a criminal sentenced to criminal detention, the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall render a ruling based on the proposal for commutation of sentence submitted by the local enforcement organ at the same level;  
(5) For commutation of sentence of a criminal sentenced to public surveillance, the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall render a ruling based on the proposal for commutation of sentence submitted by the local enforcement organ at the same level;  
(6) If a criminal granted a suspended sentence has truly major meritorious performance during the probation period and needs to be granted commutation of sentence with a corresponding reduction of the probation period, the police station responsible for supervision shall, together with the unit or grassroots organization to which the criminal belongs, submit a written opinion, and the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal is located shall render a ruling based on the proposal for commutation of sentence submitted by the local enforcement organ at the same level;  
(7) For commutation of sentence or parole of a criminal under the supervision of a detention house of a public security organ, the detention house where the criminal is held shall submit an opinion, and the local Intermediate People's Court shall render a ruling based on the proposal for commutation of sentence or parole submitted by the local enforcement organ at the same level.  
For commutation of sentence and parole under items (4) to (7) of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall render a ruling according to law within one month from the date of receiving the proposal for commutation or parole.

<a id="art-363"></a>第三百六十三条 人民法院受理减刑、假释案件，应当审查执行机关移送的材料是否包括下列内容：  
（一）减刑、假释建议书；  
（二）终审法院的判决书、裁定书、历次减刑裁定书的复制件；  
（三）罪犯确有悔改或者立功、重大立功表现的具体事实的书面证明材料；  
（四）罪犯评审鉴定表、奖惩审批表等。  
经审查，如果前款规定的材料齐备的，应当收案；材料不齐备的，应当通知提请减刑、假释的执行机关补送。

**Article 363** : When accepting a case of commutation of sentence or parole, the People's Court shall examine whether the materials submitted by the enforcement organ include the following:  
(1) The proposal for commutation of sentence or parole;  
(2) Copies of the judgment and ruling of the final instance court and the rulings on previous commutations of sentence;  
(3) Written evidentiary materials on the specific facts showing that the criminal has truly repented or has meritorious or major meritorious performance;  
(4) The criminal's evaluation and appraisal form, approval forms for rewards and punishments, etc.  
Upon examination, if the materials provided for in the preceding paragraph are complete, the case shall be accepted; if the materials are incomplete, the enforcement organ that applied for commutation of sentence or parole shall be notified to supplement and submit them.

<a id="art-364"></a>第三百六十四条 人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当依法组成合议庭。

**Article 364** : When hearing a case of commutation of sentence or parole, the People's Court shall form a collegial panel according to law.

<a id="art-365"></a>第三百六十五条 减刑、假释的裁定，应当及时送达执行机关、同级人民检察院、负责监督假释罪犯的公安机关以及罪犯本人。人民检察院认为人民法院的减刑、假释裁定不当，应当在收到裁定书副本后二十日内，向人民法院提出书面纠正意见。人民法院收到书面纠正意见后，应当重新组成合议庭进行审理，并在一个月内作出最终裁定。  
二十、附则

**Article 365** : A ruling on commutation of punishment or parole shall be served in a timely manner on the executing organ, the People's Procuratorate at the same level, the public security organ responsible for supervising the paroled criminal, and the criminal himself. Where the People's Procuratorate considers that the ruling on commutation of punishment or parole by the People's Court is improper, it shall submit a written opinion of correction to the People's Court within twenty days after receiving a copy of the ruling. After receiving the written opinion of correction, the People's Court shall form a new collegial panel to try the case and make a final ruling within one month.  
20. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-366"></a>第三百六十六条 本解释的有关规定适用于军事法院、铁路运输法院等专门人民法院。

**Article 366** : The relevant provisions of this Interpretation shall apply to special People's Courts such as military courts and railway transport courts.

<a id="art-367"></a>第三百六十七条 本解释发布前有关刑事诉讼程序的司法解释，与本解释重复或者抵触的，以本解释为准。

**Article 367** : Where any judicial interpretation on criminal procedure issued before this Interpretation is promulgated duplicates or conflicts with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
