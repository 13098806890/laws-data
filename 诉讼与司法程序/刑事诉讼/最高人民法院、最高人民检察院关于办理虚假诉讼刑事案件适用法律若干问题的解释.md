# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理虚假诉讼刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕17号  
**公布日期**：2018-01-25  
**生效日期**：2018-10-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理虚假诉讼刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2018年1月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1732次会议、2018年6月13日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第二次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年10月1日起施行。 2018年9月26日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理虚假诉讼刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2018〕17号 为依法惩治虚假诉讼犯罪活动,维护司法秩序，保护公民、法人和其他组织合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　采取伪造证据、虚假陈述等手段，实施下列行为之一，捏造民事法律关系，虚构民事纠纷，向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，应当认定为刑法第三百零七条之一第一款规定的“以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼”： （一）与夫妻一方恶意串通，捏造夫妻共同债务的； （二）与他人恶意串通，捏造债权债务关系和以物抵债协议的； （三）与公司、企业的法定代表人、董事、监事、经理或者其他管理人员恶意串通，捏造公司、企业债务或者担保义务的； （四）捏造知识产权侵权关系或者不正当竞争关系的； （五）在破产案件审理过程中申报捏造的债权的； （六）与被执行人恶意串通，捏造债权或者对查封、扣押、冻结财产的优先权、担保物权的； （七）单方或者与他人恶意串通，捏造身份、合同、侵权、继承等民事法律关系的其他行为。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　采取伪造证据、虚假陈述等手段，实施下列行为之一，捏造民事法律关系，虚构民事纠纷，向人民法院提起民事诉讼的，应当认定为[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款规定的“以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼”：  
（一）与夫妻一方恶意串通，捏造夫妻共同债务的；  
（二）与他人恶意串通，捏造债权债务关系和以物抵债协议的；  
（三）与公司、企业的法定代表人、董事、监事、经理或者其他管理人员恶意串通，捏造公司、企业债务或者担保义务的；  
（四）捏造知识产权侵权关系或者不正当竞争关系的；  
（五）在破产案件审理过程中申报捏造的债权的；  
（六）与被执行人恶意串通，捏造债权或者对查封、扣押、冻结财产的优先权、担保物权的；  
（七）单方或者与他人恶意串通，捏造身份、合同、侵权、继承等民事法律关系的其他行为。  
隐瞒债务已经全部清偿的事实，向人民法院提起民事诉讼，要求他人履行债务的，以“以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼”论。  
向人民法院申请执行基于捏造的事实作出的仲裁裁决、公证债权文书，或者在民事执行过程中以捏造的事实对执行标的提出异议、申请参与执行财产分配的，属于[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款规定的“以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼”。

**Article 1** Where a person, by means of forging evidence, making false statements, or other means, commits any of the following acts, fabricating a civil legal relationship, fabricating a civil dispute, and instituting a civil action in a People's Court, it shall be deemed as "instituting a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) maliciously colluding with one spouse to fabricate joint debts of the spouses;  
(2) maliciously colluding with another person to fabricate a creditor-debtor relationship or an agreement to transfer property in lieu of debt repayment;  
(3) maliciously colluding with a legal representative, director, supervisor, manager, or other managerial personnel of a company or enterprise to fabricate debts or suretyship obligations of the company or enterprise;  
(4) fabricating an intellectual property infringement relationship or an unfair competition relationship;  
(5) declaring fabricated claims during the trial of a bankruptcy case;  
(6) maliciously colluding with a judgment debtor to fabricate claims or priority rights or security interests over seized, impounded, or frozen property;  
(7) other acts of unilaterally or maliciously colluding with others to fabricate civil legal relationships such as status, contract, tort, or succession.  
Where a person conceals the fact that a debt has been fully discharged and institutes a civil action in a People's Court to demand another person to perform the debt, it shall be treated as "instituting a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts."  
Applying to a People's Court for enforcement of an arbitral award or notarized debt instrument rendered on the basis of fabricated facts, or raising an objection against the subject matter of enforcement or applying to participate in the distribution of enforced property on the basis of fabricated facts during civil enforcement, shall fall under "instituting a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款规定的“妨害司法秩序或者严重侵害他人合法权益”：  
（一）致使人民法院基于捏造的事实采取财产保全或者行为保全措施的；  
（二）致使人民法院开庭审理，干扰正常司法活动的；  
（三）致使人民法院基于捏造的事实作出裁判文书、制作财产分配方案，或者立案执行基于捏造的事实作出的仲裁裁决、公证债权文书的；  
（四）多次以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼的；  
（五）曾因以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼被采取民事诉讼强制措施或者受过刑事追究的；  
（六）其他妨害司法秩序或者严重侵害他人合法权益的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person institutes a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts and any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be deemed as "disrupting judicial order or seriously infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) causing the People's Court to adopt property preservation or conduct preservation measures on the basis of fabricated facts;  
(2) causing the People's Court to open court sessions and interfere with normal judicial activities;  
(3) causing the People's Court to render adjudicative documents, formulate a property distribution plan, or accept a case for enforcement of an arbitral award or notarized debt instrument rendered on the basis of fabricated facts;  
(4) repeatedly instituting civil actions on the basis of fabricated facts;  
(5) having been subjected to compulsory measures in civil action or having been criminally investigated for previously instituting a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts;  
(6) other circumstances that disrupt judicial order or seriously infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　以捏造的事实提起民事诉讼，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一项情形，造成他人经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（二）有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第二项至第四项情形之一，严重干扰正常司法活动或者严重损害司法公信力的；  
（三）致使义务人自动履行生效裁判文书确定的财产给付义务或者人民法院强制执行财产权益，数额达到一百万元以上的；  
（四）致使他人债权无法实现，数额达到一百万元以上的；  
（五）非法占有他人财产，数额达到十万元以上的；  
（六）致使他人因为不执行人民法院基于捏造的事实作出的判决、裁定，被采取刑事拘留、逮捕措施或者受到刑事追究的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person institutes a civil action on the basis of fabricated facts and any of the following circumstances occurs, it shall be deemed as "the circumstances being serious" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the circumstance under item (1) of Article 2 of this Interpretation exists, causing economic losses of not less than one million yuan to another person;  
(2) any of the circumstances under items (2) to (4) of Article 2 of this Interpretation exists, seriously interfering with normal judicial activities or seriously undermining judicial credibility;  
(3) causing the obligor to voluntarily perform the property payment obligation determined in an effective adjudicative document or causing the People's Court to compulsorily enforce property rights, with the amount reaching not less than one million yuan;  
(4) causing another person's claim to be unenforceable, with the amount reaching not less than one million yuan;  
(5) illegally possessing another person's property, with the amount reaching not less than one hundred thousand yuan;  
(6) causing another person to be subjected to criminal detention, arrest, or criminal investigation for failing to execute a judgment or ruling of the People's Court rendered on the basis of fabricated facts;  
(7) other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款行为，非法占有他人财产或者逃避合法债务，又构成诈骗罪，职务侵占罪，拒不执行判决、裁定罪，贪污罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪从重处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person commits the act under paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, illegally possesses another person's property or evades lawful debts, and such act also constitutes the crime of fraud, the crime of embezzlement, the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling, the crime of corruption, or any other crime, the person shall be convicted and given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　司法工作人员利用职权，与他人共同实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一前三款行为的，从重处罚；同时构成滥用职权罪，民事枉法裁判罪，执行判决、裁定滥用职权罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪从重处罚。

**Article 5** Where a judicial officer, by taking advantage of his or her office, jointly commits the act under the first three paragraphs of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China with another person, the judicial officer shall be given a severe punishment; if such act also constitutes the crime of abuse of power, the crime of perverting the law in civil adjudication, the crime of abuse of power in enforcing judgments or rulings, or any other crime, the judicial officer shall be convicted and given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人等诉讼参与人与他人通谋，代理提起虚假民事诉讼、故意作虚假证言或者出具虚假鉴定意见，共同实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一前三款行为的，依照共同犯罪的规定定罪处罚；同时构成妨害作证罪，帮助毁灭、伪造证据罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪从重处罚。

**Article 6** Where a litigation agent, witness, expert evaluator, or other participant in proceedings conspires with another person to act as agent to institute a false civil action, intentionally give false testimony, or issue a false expert opinion, and jointly commits the act under the first three paragraphs of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on joint crimes; if such act also constitutes the crime of obstructing testimony, the crime of aiding in the destruction or fabrication of evidence, or any other crime, the person shall be convicted and given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　采取伪造证据等手段篡改案件事实，骗取人民法院裁判文书，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)、第三百零七条等规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** Where a person, by means of forging evidence or other means, alters the facts of a case and defrauds a People's Court into issuing an adjudicative document, if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Articles 280 and 307 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and other provisions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　单位实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款行为的，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准，对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 8** Where a unit commits the act under paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards provided in this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款行为，未达到情节严重的标准，行为人系初犯，在民事诉讼过程中自愿具结悔过，接受人民法院处理决定，积极退赃、退赔的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；确有必要判处刑罚的，可以从宽处罚。  
司法工作人员利用职权，与他人共同实施[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第一款行为的，对司法工作人员不适用本条第一款规定。

**Article 9** Where a person commits the act under paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, but the circumstances do not reach the standard of seriousness, and the perpetrator is a first-time offender who voluntarily makes a statement of repentance during the civil action, accepts the disposition decision of the People's Court, and actively returns stolen property or makes restitution, the circumstances may be deemed minor, and the person shall not be prosecuted or shall be exempted from criminal punishment; if a criminal punishment is indeed necessary, a lenient punishment may be imposed.  
Where a judicial officer, by taking advantage of his or her office, jointly commits the act under paragraph 1 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China with another person, the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article shall not apply to the judicial officer.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　虚假诉讼刑事案件由虚假民事诉讼案件的受理法院所在地或者执行法院所在地人民法院管辖。有[刑法第三百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-307)之一第四款情形的，上级人民法院可以指定下级人民法院将案件移送其他人民法院审判。

**Article 10** The criminal case of false litigation shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court at the place of the court that accepted the false civil action case or at the place of the enforcement court. If the circumstances under paragraph 4 of Article 307(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China exist, the higher People's Court may designate a lower People's Court to transfer the case to another People's Court for adjudication.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本解释所称裁判文书，是指人民法院依照民事诉讼法、企业破产法等民事法律作出的判决、裁定、调解书、支付令等文书。

**Article 11** For the purposes of this Interpretation, "adjudicative documents" refers to judgments, rulings, mediation statements, payment orders, and other documents rendered by the People's Court in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law, the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law, and other civil laws.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2018年10月1日起施行。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force as of October 1, 2018.
