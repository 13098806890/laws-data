# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和延期开庭问题的规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕4号  
**公布日期**：2019-02-25  
**生效日期**：2019-03-28

> 最高人民法院《关于修改〈最高人民法院关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和延期开庭问题的规定〉的决定》已于2019年2月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1762次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年3月28日起施行。 2019年3月27日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院 关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和 延期开庭问题的规定》的决定 法释〔2019〕4号 根据最高人民法院审判委员会第1762次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于严格规范民商事案件延长审限和延期开庭问题的规定》（以下简称《规定》）作如下修改： 一、增加规定第二条：“民事诉讼法第一百四十六条第四项规定的‘其他应当延期的情形 ’，是指因不可抗力或者意外事件导致庭审无法正常进行的情形。” 二、增加规定第三条：“人民法院应当严格限制延期开庭审理次数。适用普通程序审理民商事案件，延期开庭审理次数不超过两次；适用简易程序以及小额速裁程序审理民商事案件，延期开庭审理次数不超过一次。” 三、增加规定第四条，分四款：“基层人民法院及其派出的法庭审理事实清楚、权利义务关系明确、争议不大的简单民商事案件，适用简易程序。”“基层人民法院及其派出的法庭审理符合前款规定且标的额为各省、自治区、直辖市上年度就业人员年平均工资两倍以下的民商事案件，应当适用简易程序，法律及司法解释规定不适用简易程序的案件除外。”“适用简易程序审理的民商事案件，证据交换、庭前会议等庭前准备程序与开庭程序一并进行，不再另行组织。”“适用简易程序的案件，不适用公告送达。” 四、将《规定》第二条的“再次开庭”修改为：“延期开庭审理”。 五、将《规定》的条文顺序作相应调整：“第二条”调整为“第五条”，“第三条”调整为“第六条”，“第四条”调整为“第七条”，“第五条”调整为“第八条”，“第六条”调整为“第九条”。 本决定自2019年3月28日起施行。 根据本决定，《规定》作相应修改并调整条文顺序后重新公布。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理民商事案件时，应当严格遵守法律及司法解释有关审限的规定。适用普通程序审理的第一审案件，审限为六个月；适用简易程序审理的第一审案件，审限为三个月。审理对判决的上诉案件，审限为三个月；审理对裁定的上诉案件，审限为三十日。  
法律规定有特殊情况需要延长审限的，独任审判员或合议庭应当在期限届满十五日前向本院院长提出申请，并说明详细情况和理由。院长应当在期限届满五日前作出决定。  
经本院院长批准延长审限后尚不能结案，需要再次延长的，应当在期限届满十五日前报请上级人民法院批准。上级人民法院应当在审限届满五日前作出决定。

**Article 1** When a People's Court hears a civil or commercial case, it shall strictly observe the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations concerning adjudication periods. For a case of first instance tried under ordinary procedure, the adjudication period shall be six months; for a case of first instance tried under summary procedure, the adjudication period shall be three months. For an appeal case against a judgment, the adjudication period shall be three months; for an appeal case against a ruling, the adjudication period shall be thirty days.  
If the law provides for special circumstances requiring an extension of the adjudication period, the sole adjudicator or the collegial panel shall submit an application to the president of this court fifteen days before the expiry of the period, and shall explain the detailed circumstances and reasons. The president shall make a decision five days before the expiry of the period.  
If the case cannot be concluded after the extension approved by the president of this court and a further extension is needed, an application shall be submitted to the superior People's Court for approval fifteen days before the expiry of the period. The superior People's Court shall make a decision five days before the expiry of the adjudication period.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[民事诉讼法第一百四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-146)第四项规定的“其他应当延期的情形”，是指因不可抗力或者意外事件导致庭审无法正常进行的情形。

**Article 2** The "other circumstances under which an adjournment shall be granted" as provided in Item (4) of Article 146 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China refers to circumstances where the court session cannot proceed normally due to force majeure or unexpected incidents.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应当严格限制延期开庭审理次数。适用普通程序审理民商事案件，延期开庭审理次数不超过两次；适用简易程序以及小额速裁程序审理民商事案件，延期开庭审理次数不超过一次。

**Article 3** A People's Court shall strictly limit the number of adjournments of court sessions. For civil or commercial cases tried under ordinary procedure, the number of adjournments shall not exceed two; for civil or commercial cases tried under summary procedure or small claims summary procedure, the number of adjournments shall not exceed one.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　基层人民法院及其派出的法庭审理事实清楚、权利义务关系明确、争议不大的简单民商事案件，适用简易程序。  
基层人民法院及其派出的法庭审理符合前款规定且标的额为各省、自治区、直辖市上年度就业人员年平均工资两倍以下的民商事案件，应当适用简易程序，法律及司法解释规定不适用简易程序的案件除外。  
适用简易程序审理的民商事案件，证据交换、庭前会议等庭前准备程序与开庭程序一并进行，不再另行组织。  
适用简易程序的案件，不适用公告送达。

**Article 4** A Primary People's Court and its dispatched tribunals shall apply summary procedure when trying simple civil or commercial cases with clear facts, definite rights and obligations, and minor disputes.  
A Primary People's Court and its dispatched tribunals shall apply summary procedure when trying civil or commercial cases that meet the requirements of the preceding paragraph and have the subject matter amount not exceeding two times the average annual salary of employed persons in the preceding year of the relevant province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, except for cases that are not subject to summary procedure under laws and judicial interpretations.  
For civil or commercial cases tried under summary procedure, pre-trial procedures such as evidence exchange and pre-trial conferences shall be conducted together with the court session, and no separate organization shall be arranged.  
Service by public announcement shall not apply to cases tried under summary procedure.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院开庭审理民商事案件后，认为需要延期开庭审理的，应当依法告知当事人下次开庭的时间。两次开庭间隔时间不得超过一个月，但因不可抗力或当事人同意的除外。

**Article 5** After a People's Court commences the hearing of a civil or commercial case, if it deems an adjournment necessary, it shall inform the parties of the time for the next court session according to law. The interval between two court sessions shall not exceed one month, except for force majeure or with the consent of the parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　独任审判员或者合议庭适用[民事诉讼法第一百四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-146)第四项规定决定延期开庭的，应当报本院院长批准。

**Article 6** Where a sole adjudicator or a collegial panel decides to adjourn a court session under Item (4) of Article 146 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall report to the president of this court for approval.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院应当将案件的立案时间、审理期限，扣除、延长、重新计算审限，延期开庭审理的情况及事由，按照《最高人民法院关于人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息的规定》及时向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人公开。当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人有异议的，可以依法向受理案件的法院申请监督。

**Article 7** A People's Court shall, in accordance with the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Disclosure of Trial Process Information by People's Courts through the Internet, disclose to the parties and their statutory agents and litigation agents in a timely manner the docketing time, the adjudication period, and the deduction, extension, and recalculation of the adjudication period, as well as the circumstances and reasons for adjournments of court sessions. Where the parties or their statutory agents or litigation agents have objections, they may apply to the court accepting the case for supervision according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　故意违反法律、审判纪律、审判管理规定拖延办案，或者因过失延误办案，造成严重后果的，依照《人民法院工作人员处分条例》第四十七条的规定予以处分。

**Article 8** Whoever intentionally violates laws, trial discipline, or trial management regulations to delay case handling, or negligently delays case handling, causing serious consequences, shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with Article 47 of the Discipline Regulations for Personnel of People's Courts.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本规定自2018年4月26日起施行；最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释及规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 9** These Provisions shall come into force on April 26, 2018; where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
