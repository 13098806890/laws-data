# 最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕22号  
**公布日期**：2018-12-28  
**生效日期**：2019-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定》已于2018年12月3日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1756次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年1月1日起施行。 2018年12月27日 最高人民法院关于知识产权法庭若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕22号 （2018年12月3日最高人民法院审判委员会第1756次会议通过，自2019年1月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  最高人民法院设立知识产权法庭，主要审理专利等专业技术性较强的知识产权上诉案件。  
知识产权法庭是最高人民法院派出的常设审判机构，设在北京市。  
知识产权法庭作出的判决、裁定、调解书和决定，是最高人民法院的判决、裁定、调解书和决定。

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court shall establish an Intellectual Property Court, which shall mainly hear appeals in intellectual property cases involving strong technical expertise, such as patent cases.  
The Intellectual Property Court is a permanent adjudicative body dispatched by the Supreme People's Court and shall be located in Beijing.  
The judgments, rulings, mediation documents and decisions made by the Intellectual Property Court shall be the judgments, rulings, mediation documents and decisions of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  知识产权法庭审理下列案件：  
（一）不服高级人民法院、知识产权法院、中级人民法院作出的发明专利、实用新型专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计、技术秘密、计算机软件、垄断第一审民事案件判决、裁定而提起上诉的案件；  
（二）不服北京知识产权法院对发明专利、实用新型专利、外观设计专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计授权确权作出的第一审行政案件判决、裁定而提起上诉的案件；  
（三）不服高级人民法院、知识产权法院、中级人民法院对发明专利、实用新型专利、外观设计专利、植物新品种、集成电路布图设计、技术秘密、计算机软件、垄断行政处罚等作出的第一审行政案件判决、裁定而提起上诉的案件；  
（四）全国范围内重大、复杂的本条第一、二、三项所称第一审民事和行政案件;  
（五）对本条第一、二、三项所称第一审案件已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书依法申请再审、抗诉、再审等适用审判监督程序的案件；  
（六）本条第一、二、三项所称第一审案件管辖权争议，罚款、拘留决定申请复议，报请延长审限等案件；  
（七）最高人民法院认为应当由知识产权法庭审理的其他案件。

**Article 2** The Intellectual Property Court shall hear the following cases:  
(1) cases appealed against judgments or rulings of first instance civil cases concerning invention patents, utility model patents, new plant varieties, layout designs of integrated circuits, technical secrets, computer software, or monopoly made by a Higher People's Court, an Intellectual Property Court, or an Intermediate People's Court;  
(2) cases appealed against judgments or rulings of first instance administrative cases concerning the grant and confirmation of rights in invention patents, utility model patents, design patents, new plant varieties, or layout designs of integrated circuits made by the Beijing Intellectual Property Court;  
(3) cases appealed against judgments or rulings of first instance administrative cases concerning invention patents, utility model patents, design patents, new plant varieties, layout designs of integrated circuits, technical secrets, computer software, administrative penalties for monopoly, etc. made by a Higher People's Court, an Intellectual Property Court, or an Intermediate People's Court;  
(4) major and complex first instance civil and administrative cases nationwide as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this Article;  
(5) cases where an application for retrial, protest, or retrial is filed according to law against legally effective judgments, rulings, or mediation documents in first instance cases as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this Article, and which are subject to trial supervision procedures;  
(6) cases concerning disputes over jurisdiction, applications for reconsideration of decisions on fines or detention, applications for extension of trial limits, etc. in first instance cases as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this Article;  
(7) other cases that the Supreme People's Court deems shall be heard by the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  [本规定第二条](#art-2)第一、二、三项所称第一审案件的审理法院应当按照规定及时向知识产权法庭移送纸质和电子卷宗。

**Article 3** The trial court of the first instance cases as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 2 of these Provisions shall, in accordance with the provisions, promptly transfer the paper and electronic case files to the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  经当事人同意，知识产权法庭可以通过电子诉讼平台、中国审判流程信息公开网以及传真、电子邮件等电子方式送达诉讼文件、证据材料及裁判文书等。

**Article 4** With the consent of the parties, the Intellectual Property Court may serve litigation documents, evidentiary materials, and adjudicative documents, etc. by electronic means such as the electronic litigation platform, the China Judicial Procedures Information Online website, as well as facsimile or email.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  知识产权法庭可以通过电子诉讼平台或者采取在线视频等方式组织证据交换、召集庭前会议等。

**Article 5** The Intellectual Property Court may, by means of the electronic litigation platform or online video, etc., organize the exchange of evidence, convene pre-trial conferences, etc.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  知识产权法庭可以根据案件情况到实地或者原审人民法院所在地巡回审理案件。

**Article 6** The Intellectual Property Court may, based on the circumstances of the case, hold circuit trials at the site or at the location of the original trial People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  知识产权法庭采取保全等措施，依照执行程序相关规定办理。

**Article 7** Where the Intellectual Property Court takes measures such as preservation, it shall handle them in accordance with the relevant provisions of the enforcement procedures.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  知识产权法庭审理的案件的立案信息、合议庭组成人员、审判流程、裁判文书等向当事人和社会依法公开，同时可以通过电子诉讼平台、中国审判流程信息公开网查询。

**Article 8** The filing information, composition of the collegial panel, trial procedures, adjudicative documents, etc. of cases heard by the Intellectual Property Court shall be disclosed to the parties and the public according to law, and may be inquired through the electronic litigation platform or the China Judicial Procedures Information Online website.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  知识产权法庭法官会议由庭长、副庭长和若干资深法官组成，讨论重大、疑难、复杂案件等。

**Article 9** The Judges' Meeting of the Intellectual Property Court shall be composed of the Presiding Judge, Deputy Presiding Judges, and a number of senior judges, and shall discuss major, difficult, and complex cases, etc.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  知识产权法庭应当加强对有关案件审判工作的调研，及时总结裁判标准和审理规则，指导下级人民法院审判工作。

**Article 10** The Intellectual Property Court shall strengthen its research on the adjudication work of related cases, promptly summarize adjudication standards and trial rules, and guide the trial work of the lower people's courts.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  对知识产权法院、中级人民法院已经发生法律效力的[本规定第二条](#art-2)第一、二、三项所称第一审案件判决、裁定、调解书，省级人民检察院向高级人民法院提出抗诉的，高级人民法院应当告知其由最高人民检察院依法向最高人民法院提出，并由知识产权法庭审理。

**Article 11** Where a provincial-level People's Procuratorate files a protest with a Higher People's Court against a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document in a first instance case as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 2 of these Provisions rendered by an Intellectual Property Court or an Intermediate People's Court, the Higher People's Court shall inform it that the protest shall be filed by the Supreme People's Procuratorate with the Supreme People's Court according to law and shall be heard by the Intellectual Property Court.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  [本规定第二条](#art-2)第一、二、三项所称第一审案件的判决、裁定或者决定，于2019年1月1日前作出，当事人依法提起上诉或者申请复议的，由原审人民法院的上一级人民法院审理。

**Article 12** Where a judgment, ruling, or decision in a first instance case as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 2 of these Provisions is made before January 1, 2019, and a party files an appeal or applies for reconsideration according to law, the case shall be heard by the People's Court at the next higher level over the original trial People's Court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  [本规定第二条](#art-2)第一、二、三项所称第一审案件已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定、调解书，于2019年1月1日前作出，对其依法申请再审、抗诉、再审的，适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》有关规定。

**Article 13** Where a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document in a first instance case as referred to in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Article 2 of these Provisions is made before January 1, 2019, and an application for retrial, protest, or retrial is filed according to law, the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  本规定施行前经批准可以受理专利、技术秘密、计算机软件、垄断第一审民事和行政案件的基层人民法院，不再受理上述案件。  
对于基层人民法院2019年1月1日尚未审结的前款规定的案件，当事人不服其判决、裁定依法提起上诉的，由其上一级人民法院审理。

**Article 14** Primary People's Courts that, prior to the coming into force of these Provisions, were approved to accept first instance civil and administrative cases concerning patents, technical secrets, computer software, or monopoly shall no longer accept the above-mentioned cases.  
For cases under the preceding paragraph that are not yet concluded by the Primary People's Court as of January 1, 2019, where a party appeals against its judgment or ruling according to law, the case shall be heard by the People's Court at the next higher level.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  本规定自2019年1月1日起施行。最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force on January 1, 2019. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
