### 最高人民法院关于邮电部门造成电报稽延、错误是否承担赔偿责任问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于邮电部门造成电报稽延、错误  
  
是否承担赔偿责任问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1986〕38号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院1986年10月22日苏法经（86）14号关于邮电部门造成电报稽延、错误，影响合同的履行，给收、发报人带来经济损失，是否承担赔偿责任问题的请示收悉。经研究答复如下：  
  
邮电部门自1985年起，在电报稿纸背面的《发电须知》中说明：“电报在传递、处理过程中，由于邮电局的原因，造成电报稽延或错误，以致失效的，邮电局应按规定退还报费，但不承担其他赔偿责任。”发报人使用电报稿纸发报，应视为接受该项条件。因此，在有关电信的法律、法规另有规定之前，凡是由于电报稽延、错误受到损失的人向人民法院起诉，要求邮电部门赔偿的，人民法院不予受理，并应告知其找邮电部门解决。  
  
此复  
  
1986年12月30日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the Posts and Telecommunications Departments Shall Bear Compensation Liability for Delays or Errors in Telegrams  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1986] No. 38  
Higher People's Court of Jiangsu Province:  
We have received your request dated October 22, 1986, No. Su Fa Jing (86) 14, regarding the issue of whether the Posts and Telecommunications Departments shall bear compensation liability for delays or errors in telegrams that affect the performance of contracts and cause economic losses to senders and recipients. After study, we reply as follows:  
Since 1985, in the "Instructions for Sending Telegrams" on the back of telegram forms, the Posts and Telecommunications Departments have stated: "If, during transmission and processing, a telegram is delayed or erroneous due to the fault of the Posts and Telecommunications Bureau, rendering it ineffective, the Bureau shall refund the fee as prescribed, but shall not bear any other compensation liability." A sender who uses a telegram form to send a telegram shall be deemed to have accepted that condition. Therefore, until otherwise provided by relevant telecommunications laws and regulations, where any person who has suffered losses due to telegram delays or errors brings a prosecution to the People's Court requesting compensation from the Posts and Telecommunications Departments, the People's Court shall not accept the case and shall inform the person to seek resolution from the Posts and Telecommunications Departments.  
This is a reply.  
December 30, 1986
