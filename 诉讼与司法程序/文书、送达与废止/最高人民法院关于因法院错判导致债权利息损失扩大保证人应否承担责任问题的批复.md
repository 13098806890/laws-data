### 最高人民法院关于因法院错判导致债权利息损失扩大保证人应否承担责任问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于因法院错判导致债权利息损失扩大  
  
保证人应否承担责任问题的批复  
  
（2000年7月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1126次会议通过，自2000年8月12日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕24号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1999〕72号《关于因法院错判导致资金利息扩大的部分损失保证人应否承担责任的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
依照《中华人民共和国担保法》第二十一条的规定，除合同另有约定的外，主债权的利息是指因债务人未按照合同约定履行义务而产生的利息。因法院错判引起债权利息损失扩大的部分，不属于保证担保的范围，保证人不承担责任。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2000] No. 24  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your court's Chuan Gao Fa [1999] No. 72, the "Request for Instructions on Whether the Guarantor Shall Be Liable for the Part of Loss Caused by the Expansion of Interest on Funds Due to the Court's Erroneous Judgment," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of the Guarantee Law of the People's Republic of China, unless otherwise agreed in the contract, the interest on the principal claim refers to the interest arising from the debtor's failure to perform the obligation in accordance with the contract. The part of the loss of interest on the claim expanded due to the court's erroneous judgment shall not fall within the scope of guarantee, and the guarantor shall not bear liability.  
Reply is given hereby.
