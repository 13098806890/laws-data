### 最高人民法院关于高级人民法院将死刑案件改判为死刑缓期二年执行的判决书表述问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于高级人民法院将死刑案件改判为  
  
死刑缓期二年执行的判决书表述问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1990〕4号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（89）粤法刑一文字第12号请示报告收悉。经研究，同意你院的意见，即：根据[刑法第四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-43)第二款关于“……死刑缓期执行的，可以由高级人民法院判决或者核准”的规定,（一）对于中级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人不服，提出上诉，高级人民法院第二审时，改判为死刑缓期二年执行的，应在判决书中写明“本判决为终审判决”；（二）对于中级人民法院判处死刑的第一审案件，被告人不上诉，检察院也未抗诉，高级人民法院复核时，认为原判决的死刑不是必须立即执行，提审后直接改判为死刑缓期二年执行的，在判决书中写明“本判决为终审判决”即可。最高人民法院1988年3月24日法（研）复（1988）16号批复中规定的“……还应在判决书上另起一行写明：‘依照[刑事诉讼法第一百四十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-146)的规定，本判决为核准判处×××死刑，缓期二年执行的判决’”，今后不再适用。  
  
1990年5月30日

**Article 1** Reply on Issues Concerning the Expression in Written Judgments of Higher People's Courts When Changing a Death Penalty Case to a Death Sentence with a Two-Year Reprieve  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1990] No. 4  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your request for instructions, (89) Yue Fa Xing Yi Wen Zi No. 12, has been received. Upon study, we agree with your opinion, which is: in accordance with the provisions of Article 43, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law concerning "... a death sentence with a two-year reprieve may be adjudged or approved by a Higher People's Court," (1) for a first-instance case in which a death sentence is imposed by an Intermediate People's Court and the defendant refuses to accept the judgment and files an appeal, and upon second-instance trial, the Higher People's Court changes the sentence to a death sentence with a two-year reprieve, the written judgment shall state: "This judgment is a final judgment"; (2) for a first-instance case in which a death sentence is imposed by an Intermediate People's Court, the defendant does not appeal and the procuratorate does not protest, and upon review by the Higher People's Court, it considers that the death sentence in the original judgment is not one that must be executed immediately, and after bringing the case up for trial directly changes the sentence to a death sentence with a two-year reprieve, it is sufficient for the written judgment to state: "This judgment is a final judgment." The provisions in the Reply of the Supreme People's Court Fa (Yan) Fu (1988) No. 16 dated March 24, 1988, which stipulated that "... furthermore, a separate line shall be written in the written judgment stating: 'In accordance with the provisions of Article 146 of the Criminal Procedure Law, this judgment is a judgment approving the sentence of death for ××× with a two-year reprieve,'" shall no longer apply.  
May 30, 1990
