# 最高人民法院关于乡政府与其他单位签订的联营协议效力问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

---

### 正文

一、对于在中发〔 1986〕 6号《中共中央、国务院关于进一步制止党政机关和党政干部经商、办企业的规定》文件以后，政企尚未分开的乡政府，以其名义签订的联营协议，应视为乡政府在行使着政府和乡合作经济组织的双重职权，如无违法情况，可不按无效协议处理。需要继续履行的，应随着政企分开的进展，及时变更联营主体，完备各项法律手续。

**Article None** I. A joint venture agreement entered into in the name of a township government that has not yet separated government functions from enterprise management after the issuance of Document No. 6 (1986) of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council, i.e., the Provisions of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council on Further Prohibiting Party and Government Organs and Their Cadres from Engaging in Commerce or Running Enterprises, shall be deemed as the township government exercising the dual functions of government and the township cooperative economic organization. If there is no illegal circumstance, it may not be treated as an invalid agreement. Where the agreement needs to be further performed, the joint venture entity shall be promptly changed in accordance with the progress of separating government functions from enterprise management, and all legal formalities shall be completed.

二、对于中发〔 1986〕 6号文件以后，政企已经分开的乡政府，仍以其为联营一方签订的联营协议，根据中发〔 1986〕 6号文件的规定原则上应认定无效。但在审判实践中，要注意对具体情况进行具体分析。如联营协议虽然是以乡政府名义签订的，实际上却是由乡合作经济组织执行的，协议的内容又不违背有关法律、政策规定，作无效协议处理，不利于经济发展的，也可不作无效协议处理。需要继续履行的，应对联营主体进行变更，完备各项法律手续。  
此复  
1988年 1月9日

**Article None** II. For a township government that has already separated government functions from enterprise management after Document No. 6 (1986), a joint venture agreement entered into still with the township government as one party shall, in principle, be found invalid in accordance with the provisions of Document No. 6 (1986). However, in adjudication practice, attention shall be paid to specific analysis of specific circumstances. If the joint venture agreement, although entered into in the name of the township government, is actually executed by the township cooperative economic organization, and the content of the agreement does not violate relevant laws or policies, and treating it as an invalid agreement would be detrimental to economic development, it may not be treated as an invalid agreement. Where the agreement needs to be further performed, the joint venture entity shall be changed, and all legal formalities shall be completed.  
  
This is hereby replied.  
  
January 9, 1988
