### 最高人民法院关于对案外人的财产能否进行保全问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对案外人的财产能否进行保全问题的批复  
  
法释〔1998〕10号  
  
（1998年4月2日最高人民法院审判委员会第970次会议通过，自1998年5月26日起施行）  
  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院鄂高法〔1996〕191号《关于对案外人的财产能否进行诉讼财产保全的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
最高人民法院法发〔1994〕29号《关于在经济审判工作中严格执行〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的若干规定》第14条的规定与最高人民法院法发〔1992〕22号《关于适用（中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉若干问题的意见》第105条的规定精神是一致的，均应当严格执行。  
  
对于债务人的财产不能满足保全请求，但对案外人有到期债权的，人民法院可以依债权人的申请裁定该案外人不得对债务人清偿。该案外人对其到期债务没有异议并要求偿付的，由人民法院提存财物或价款。但是，人民法院不应对其财产采取保全措施。  
  
此复。

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
Your Court's Request No. E Gao Fa [1996] 191, "Request for Whether Property Preservation in Litigation May Be Conducted on the Property of a Third Party," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
The provisions of Article 14 of the Supreme People's Court's Fa Fa [1994] No. 29, "Several Provisions on Strictly Implementing the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China in Economic Adjudication Work," are consistent in spirit with the provisions of Article 105 of the Supreme People's Court's Fa Fa [1992] No. 22, "Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China." Both shall be strictly implemented.  
Where the debtor's property is insufficient to satisfy the preservation request, but the debtor has matured claims against a third party, the People's Court may, upon the creditor's application, order that the third party shall not make satisfaction to the debtor. If the third party has no objection to the matured debt and demands payment, the People's Court shall deposit the property or proceeds. However, the People's Court shall not adopt preservation measures against the property of the third party.  
This is our reply.
