### 最高人民法院关于日本回国孤儿与在我国的配偶离婚经我国法院调解双方达成协议可否制发调解书的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于日本回国孤儿与在我国的配偶离婚  
  
经我国法院调解双方达成协议可否制发调解书的批复  
  
一九八四年四月九日  
  
（84）法民字第4号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院一九八四年三月十六日电话请示的日本回国孤儿与在我国的配偶离婚，经我国法院调解，双方达成协议，可否制发调解书问题，经研究认为：在我国领域内进行的民事诉讼，不论当事人之国籍如何，均适用我国民事诉讼程序规范。涉外民事诉讼除适用民诉法涉外编的规定外，按该法第一百八十五条规定，涉外编没有规定的，适用该法其他有关规定。该法第九十七条规定，人民法院受理的民事案件，能够调解的，都应当进行调解，促使当事人互相谅解，达成协议。第一百零一条规定调解达成协议，应当制作调解书。这些规定对涉外民事诉讼同样适用。故国外一方来我国法院进行民事诉讼，经调解，双方达成协议，是可以制发调解书的。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a Mediation Document May Be Issued after Both Parties Reach an Agreement through Mediation by a Chinese Court in the Case of Divorce Between a Returned Japanese Orphan and His/Her Spouse in China  
April 9, 1984  
(84) Fa Min Zi No. 4  
Higher People's Court of Liaoning Province:  
In response to your telephone inquiry of March 16, 1984, concerning the divorce between a returned Japanese orphan and his/her spouse in China, and the question of whether a mediation document may be issued after both parties reach an agreement through mediation by a Chinese court, upon deliberation, it is considered that: civil actions conducted within the territory of China shall be governed by the procedural rules for civil actions of China, regardless of the nationality of the parties. In foreign-related civil actions, in addition to applying the provisions of the chapter on foreign-related civil procedures in the Civil Procedure Law, pursuant to Article 185 of that Law, matters not provided for in that chapter shall be governed by other relevant provisions of that Law. Article 97 of that Law provides that for civil cases accepted by a People's Court, where mediation is possible, mediation shall be conducted to encourage the parties to reach mutual understanding and an agreement. Article 101 provides that where an agreement is reached through mediation, a mediation document shall be prepared. These provisions shall apply equally to foreign-related civil actions. Therefore, where a foreign party comes to a Chinese court for civil proceedings and both parties reach an agreement through mediation, it is permissible to issue a mediation document.  
This is our reply.
