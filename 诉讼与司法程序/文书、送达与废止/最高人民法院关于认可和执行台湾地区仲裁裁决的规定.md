# 最高人民法院关于认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕14号  
**公布日期**：2015-06-02  
**生效日期**：2015-07-01

> 最高人民法院《关于认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决的规定》已于2015年6月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1653次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年7月1日起施行。 2015年6月29日 最高人民法院 关于认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决的规定 法释〔2015〕14号 为保障海峡两岸当事人的合法权益，更好地适应海峡两岸关系和平发展的新形势，根据民事诉讼法、仲裁法等有关法律，总结人民法院涉台审判工作经验，就认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决，制定本规定。 第一条　台湾地区仲裁裁决的当事人可以根据本规定，作为申请人向人民法院申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决。 第二条　本规定所称台湾地区仲裁裁决是指，有关常设仲裁机构及临时仲裁庭在台湾地区按照台湾地区仲裁规定就有关民商事争议作出的仲裁裁决，包括仲裁判断、仲裁和解和仲裁调解。 第三条　申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决申请的，人民法院先按照认可程序进行审查, 裁定认可后，由人民法院执行机构执行。 申请人直接申请执行的，人民法院应当告知其一并提交认可申请；坚持不申请认可的，裁定驳回其申请。 第四条   申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的案件，由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被申请人住所地、经常居住地、财产所在地中级人民法院或者专门人民法院受理。 申请人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。 申请人向被申请人财产所在地人民法院申请认可的，应当提供财产存在的相关证据。 第五条　对申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的案件，人民法院应当组成合议庭进行审查。 第六条　申请人委托他人代理申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　台湾地区仲裁裁决的当事人可以根据本规定，作为申请人向人民法院申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决。

**Article 1** A party to an arbitral award of the Taiwan region may, as the applicant, apply to the People's Court for recognition and enforcement of the arbitral award of the Taiwan region in accordance with these Provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称台湾地区仲裁裁决是指，有关常设仲裁机构及临时仲裁庭在台湾地区按照台湾地区仲裁规定就有关民商事争议作出的仲裁裁决，包括仲裁判断、仲裁和解和仲裁调解。

**Article 2** For the purpose of these Provisions, an arbitral award of the Taiwan region refers to an arbitral award rendered by a relevant permanent arbitration institution or an ad hoc arbitral tribunal in the Taiwan region in accordance with the arbitration rules of the Taiwan region regarding relevant civil and commercial disputes, including an arbitral award, an arbitral settlement, and an arbitral mediation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请人同时提出认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决申请的，人民法院先按照认可程序进行审查, 裁定认可后，由人民法院执行机构执行。  
申请人直接申请执行的，人民法院应当告知其一并提交认可申请；坚持不申请认可的，裁定驳回其申请。

**Article 3** Where an applicant simultaneously files an application for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, the People's Court shall first conduct a review in accordance with the recognition procedures; after ruling to recognize the award, the enforcement authority of the People's Court shall enforce it.  
Where an applicant directly files an application for enforcement, the People's Court shall inform the applicant to submit an application for recognition together with the application; if the applicant insists on not applying for recognition, the People's Court shall rule to dismiss the application.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的案件，由申请人住所地、经常居住地或者被申请人住所地、经常居住地、财产所在地中级人民法院或者专门人民法院受理。  
申请人向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院申请认可的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。  
申请人向被申请人财产所在地人民法院申请认可的，应当提供财产存在的相关证据。

**Article 4** A case concerning an application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region shall be accepted by the Intermediate People's Court or the special People's Court of the place where the applicant's domicile or habitual residence is located, or where the respondent's domicile, habitual residence, or property is located.  
Where an applicant applies for recognition to two or more People's Courts with jurisdiction, the People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.  
Where an applicant applies for recognition to the People's Court of the place where the respondent's property is located, the applicant shall provide relevant evidence proving the existence of such property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的案件，人民法院应当组成合议庭进行审查。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall form a collegial panel to review a case concerning an application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　申请人委托他人代理申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的，应当向人民法院提交由委托人签名或者盖章的授权委托书。  
台湾地区、香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区或者外国当事人签名或者盖章的授权委托书应当履行相关的公证、认证或者其他证明手续，但授权委托书在人民法院法官的见证下签署或者经中国大陆公证机关公证证明是在中国大陆签署的除外。

**Article 6** Where an applicant entrusts another person to act as an agent in applying for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, the applicant shall submit to the People's Court a power of attorney signed or sealed by the principal.  
A power of attorney signed or sealed by a party from the Taiwan region, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or a foreign country shall undergo the relevant notarization, certification, or other evidentiary procedures, except where the power of attorney is signed in the presence of a judge of the People's Court or is notarized by a notarial authority in Mainland China as having been signed in Mainland China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请人申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决，应当提交以下文件或者经证明无误的副本：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）仲裁协议；  
（三）仲裁判断书、仲裁和解书或者仲裁调解书。  
申请书应当记明以下事项：  
（一）申请人和被申请人姓名、性别、年龄、职业、身份证件号码、住址（申请人或者被申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应当记明法人或者其他组织的名称、地址、法定代表人或者主要负责人姓名、职务）和通讯方式；  
（二）申请认可的仲裁判断书、仲裁和解书或者仲裁调解书的案号或者识别资料和生效日期；  
（三）请求和理由；  
（四）被申请人财产所在地、财产状况及申请认可的仲裁裁决的执行情况；  
（五）其他需要说明的情况。

**Article 7** An applicant applying for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region shall submit the following documents or certified true copies thereof:  
(1) A written application;  
(2) The arbitration agreement;  
(3) The arbitral award, arbitral settlement agreement, or arbitral mediation agreement.  
The written application shall specify the following matters:  
(1) The name, gender, age, occupation, identity document number, and address of the applicant and the respondent (if the applicant or respondent is a legal person or any other organization, the name, address, name and title of the legal representative or principal responsible person of such legal person or organization shall be specified), and means of communication;  
(2) The case number or identifying information and the effective date of the arbitral award, arbitral settlement agreement, or arbitral mediation agreement for which recognition is sought;  
(3) The request and the reasons therefor;  
(4) The place where the respondent's property is located, the status of such property, and the enforcement status of the arbitral award sought to be recognized;  
(5) Any other circumstances that need to be explained.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对于符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第七条规定条件的申请，人民法院应当在收到申请后七日内立案，并通知申请人和被申请人，同时将申请书送达被申请人；不符合[本规定第四条](#art-4)和第七条规定条件的，应当在七日内裁定不予受理，同时说明不予受理的理由；申请人对裁定不服的，可以提起上诉。

**Article 8** Where an application meets the conditions set forth in Article 4 and Article 7 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall, within seven days after receiving the application, docket the case, notify the applicant and the respondent, and serve the application on the respondent. Where the application does not meet the conditions set forth in Article 4 and Article 7 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall, within seven days, rule to refuse to accept the case, and shall state the reasons for such refusal. If the applicant refuses to accept the ruling, the applicant may file an appeal.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请人申请认可台湾地区仲裁裁决，应当提供相关证明文件，以证明该仲裁裁决的真实性。  
申请人可以申请人民法院通过海峡两岸调查取证司法互助途径查明台湾地区仲裁裁决的真实性；人民法院认为必要时，也可以就有关事项依职权通过海峡两岸司法互助途径向台湾地区请求调查取证。

**Article 9** An applicant applying for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region shall provide relevant evidentiary documents to prove the authenticity of the arbitral award.  
The applicant may apply to the People's Court for ascertaining the authenticity of the arbitral award of the Taiwan region through judicial assistance between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait in the collection of evidence. Where the People's Court deems it necessary, it may also, on its own authority, request the Taiwan region to collect evidence in relation to the relevant matters through judicial assistance between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的申请之前或者之后，可以按照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定，根据申请人的申请，裁定采取保全措施。

**Article 10** Before or after accepting an application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, the People's Court may, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and relevant judicial interpretations, rule to take preservation measures upon the application of the applicant.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的申请后，当事人就同一争议起诉的，不予受理。  
当事人未申请认可，而是就同一争议向人民法院起诉的，亦不予受理，但仲裁协议无效的除外。

**Article 11** After the People's Court accepts an application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, a party who brings a lawsuit regarding the same dispute shall not be accepted.  
Where a party does not apply for recognition but brings a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the same dispute, such lawsuit shall also not be accepted, except where the arbitration agreement is null and void.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院受理认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的申请后，作出裁定前，申请人请求撤回申请的，可以裁定准许。

**Article 12** After the People's Court accepts an application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region but before rendering a ruling, the applicant may request to withdraw the application, and the People's Court may rule to permit such withdrawal.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院应当尽快审查认可台湾地区仲裁裁决的申请，决定予以认可的，应当在立案之日起两个月内作出裁定；决定不予认可或者驳回申请的，应当在作出决定前按有关规定自立案之日起两个月内上报最高人民法院。  
通过海峡两岸司法互助途径送达文书和调查取证的期间，不计入审查期限。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall promptly review the application for recognition of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region. If it decides to grant recognition, it shall render a ruling within two months from the date of docketing the case. If it decides to refuse recognition or to dismiss the application, it shall, before making such decision, report to the Supreme People's Court within two months from the date of docketing the case in accordance with the relevant provisions.  
The period for serving documents and collecting evidence through judicial assistance between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait shall not be counted within the review period.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对申请认可和执行的仲裁裁决，被申请人提出证据证明有下列情形之一的，经审查核实，人民法院裁定不予认可：  
（一）仲裁协议一方当事人依对其适用的法律在订立仲裁协议时属于无行为能力的；或者依当事人约定的准据法，或当事人没有约定适用的准据法而依台湾地区仲裁规定，该仲裁协议无效的；或者当事人之间没有达成书面仲裁协议的，但申请认可台湾地区仲裁调解的除外；  
（二）被申请人未接到选任仲裁员或进行仲裁程序的适当通知，或者由于其他不可归责于被申请人的原因而未能陈述意见的；  
（三）裁决所处理的争议不是提交仲裁的争议，或者不在仲裁协议范围之内；或者裁决载有超出当事人提交仲裁范围的事项的决定，但裁决中超出提交仲裁范围的事项的决定与提交仲裁事项的决定可以分开的，裁决中关于提交仲裁事项的决定部分可以予以认可；  
（四）仲裁庭的组成或者仲裁程序违反当事人的约定，或者在当事人没有约定时与台湾地区仲裁规定不符的；  
（五）裁决对当事人尚无约束力，或者业经台湾地区法院撤销或者驳回执行申请的。  
依据国家法律，该争议事项不能以仲裁解决的，或者认可该仲裁裁决将违反一个中国原则等国家法律的基本原则或损害社会公共利益的，人民法院应当裁定不予认可。

**Article 14** With respect to an arbitral award for which recognition and enforcement are sought, where the respondent presents evidence proving any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall, after examination and verification, rule to refuse recognition:  
(1) A party to the arbitration agreement was under incapacity under the law applicable to it at the time of concluding the arbitration agreement; or the arbitration agreement is null and void under the governing law agreed upon by the parties or, in the absence of such agreement, under the arbitration rules of the Taiwan region; or no written arbitration agreement was reached between the parties, except where the application is for recognition of an arbitral mediation award of the Taiwan region;  
(2) The respondent was not given proper notice of the appointment of an arbitrator or of the arbitration proceedings, or was unable to present arguments for reasons not attributable to the respondent;  
(3) The dispute dealt with in the award is not the dispute submitted to arbitration or falls outside the scope of the arbitration agreement; or the award contains decisions on matters beyond the scope of the submission to arbitration, provided that if the decisions on matters beyond the scope of the submission to arbitration can be separated from those on matters submitted to arbitration, the part of the award concerning matters submitted to arbitration may be recognized;  
(4) The composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitration procedure was not in accordance with the agreement of the parties or, in the absence of such agreement, was not in conformity with the arbitration rules of the Taiwan region;  
(5) The award has not yet become binding on the parties, or has been set aside by a court of the Taiwan region or its application for enforcement has been dismissed.  
If, under the national law, the subject matter of the dispute is not arbitrable, or the recognition of the arbitral award would violate the basic principles of national law such as the One-China principle or harm the public interest, the People's Court shall rule to refuse recognition.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院经审查能够确认台湾地区仲裁裁决真实，而且不具有[本规定第十四条](#art-14)所列情形的，裁定认可其效力；不能确认该仲裁裁决真实性的，裁定驳回申请。  
裁定驳回申请的案件，申请人再次申请并符合受理条件的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 15** Where, upon examination, the People's Court is able to confirm the authenticity of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region and the award does not fall under any of the circumstances listed in Article 14 of these Provisions, the People's Court shall rule to recognize its validity. Where the People's Court cannot confirm the authenticity of the arbitral award, it shall rule to dismiss the application.  
For a case in which the application has been dismissed by ruling, if the applicant reapplies and meets the conditions for acceptance, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院依据[本规定第十四条](#art-14)和第十五条作出的裁定，一经送达即发生法律效力。

**Article 16** A ruling made by the People's Court in accordance with Articles 14 and 15 of these Provisions shall become legally effective upon service.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　一方当事人向人民法院申请认可或者执行台湾地区仲裁裁决，另一方当事人向台湾地区法院起诉撤销该仲裁裁决，被申请人申请中止认可或者执行并且提供充分担保的，人民法院应当中止认可或者执行程序。  
申请中止认可或者执行的，应当向人民法院提供台湾地区法院已经受理撤销仲裁裁决案件的法律文书。  
台湾地区法院撤销该仲裁裁决的，人民法院应当裁定不予认可或者裁定终结执行；台湾地区法院驳回撤销仲裁裁决请求的，人民法院应当恢复认可或者执行程序。

**Article 17** Where one party applies to the People's Court for recognition or enforcement of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, and the other party brings a lawsuit with a court of the Taiwan region for setting aside the arbitral award, and the respondent applies for suspension of recognition or enforcement and provides sufficient security, the People's Court shall suspend the recognition or enforcement proceedings.  
A party that applies for suspension of recognition or enforcement shall provide to the People's Court the legal document proving that a court of the Taiwan region has accepted the case for setting aside the arbitral award.  
If a court of the Taiwan region sets aside the arbitral award, the People's Court shall rule to refuse recognition or to terminate enforcement. If a court of the Taiwan region dismisses the request to set aside the arbitral award, the People's Court shall resume the recognition or enforcement proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　对人民法院裁定不予认可的台湾地区仲裁裁决，申请人再次提出申请的，人民法院不予受理。但当事人可以根据双方重新达成的仲裁协议申请仲裁，也可以就同一争议向人民法院起诉。

**Article 18** With respect to an arbitral award of the Taiwan region for which the People's Court has ruled to refuse recognition, if the applicant files another application, the People's Court shall not accept it. However, the parties may apply for arbitration in accordance with a newly concluded arbitration agreement between them, or may bring a lawsuit with the People's Court regarding the same dispute.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　申请人申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决的期间，适用[民事诉讼法第二百三十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-239)的规定。  
申请人仅申请认可而未同时申请执行的，申请执行的期间自人民法院对认可申请作出的裁定生效之日起重新计算。

**Article 19** The period for an applicant to apply for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region shall be governed by Article 239 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where an applicant applies only for recognition without simultaneously applying for enforcement, the period for applying for enforcement shall be recalculated from the date on which the People's Court's ruling on the recognition application becomes effective.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民法院在办理申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决案件中所作出的法律文书，应当依法送达案件当事人。

**Article 20** The legal documents issued by the People's Court in handling cases concerning applications for recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards of the Taiwan region shall be served on the parties to the case in accordance with law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决，应当参照《诉讼费用交纳办法》的规定，交纳相关费用。

**Article 21** For an application for recognition and enforcement of an arbitral award of the Taiwan region, the relevant fees shall be paid by reference to the provisions of the Measures for the Payment of Litigation Costs.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本规定自2015年7月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前，根据最高人民法院《关于人民法院认可台湾地区有关法院民事判决的规定》（法释〔1998〕11号），人民法院已经受理但尚未审结的申请认可和执行台湾地区仲裁裁决的案件，适用本规定。

**Article 22** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 2015.  
For cases concerning applications for recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards of the Taiwan region that have been accepted by the People's Court before the coming into force of these Provisions but have not yet been concluded, and which were accepted in accordance with the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Recognition by the People's Courts of Civil Judgments Rendered by the Relevant Courts of the Taiwan Region (Fa Shi [1998] No. 11), these Provisions shall apply.
