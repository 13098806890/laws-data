### 最高人民法院关于被判处死刑的被告人在上诉期满后又提出撤回上诉的应当如何处理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院关于被判处死刑的被告人  
  
在上诉期满后又提出撤回上诉的  
  
应当如何处理问题的批复  
  
法复〔1992〕2号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于被判处死刑的被告人上诉，上诉期满后又提出撤回上诉的，应如何处理问题的请示》收悉。  
  
经研究，同意你院意见，即：被中级人民法院判处死刑的被告人在上诉期限内提出上诉，上诉期满后又提出撤回上诉的，应当由高级人民法院决定不准撤回上诉，并按照第二审程序继续审理。  
  
1992年4月8日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court on How to Handle the Case Where a Defendant Sentenced to Death  
Files an Appeal but Later Withdraws It After the Appeal Period Expires  
Fa Fu [1992] No. 2  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on How to Handle the Case Where a Defendant Sentenced to Death Files an Appeal but Later Withdraws It After the Appeal Period Expires.  
Upon study, we agree with your opinion that, where a defendant sentenced to death by an Intermediate People's Court files an appeal within the appeal period but later withdraws it after the appeal period expires, the Higher People's Court shall decide not to permit the withdrawal of the appeal and shall continue the trial in accordance with the procedure of second instance.  
April 8, 1992
