# 最高人民法院关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕6号  
**公布日期**：2014-12-08  
**生效日期**：2015-03-01

> 最高人民法院《关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定》已于2014年12月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1631次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年3月1日起施行。 2015年2月28日 最高人民法院 关于扣押与拍卖船舶适用法律若干问题的规定 法释〔2015〕6号 为规范海事诉讼中扣押与拍卖船舶，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法》等法律，结合司法实践，制定本规定。 第一条　海事请求人申请对船舶采取限制处分或者抵押等保全措施的，海事法院可以依照民事诉讼法的有关规定，裁定准许并通知船舶登记机关协助执行。 前款规定的保全措施不影响其他海事请求人申请扣押船舶。 第二条　海事法院应不同海事请求人的申请，可以对本院或其他海事法院已经扣押的船舶采取扣押措施。 先申请扣押船舶的海事请求人未申请拍卖船舶的，后申请扣押船舶的海事请求人可以依据海事诉讼特别程序法第二十九条的规定，向准许其扣押申请的海事法院申请拍卖船舶。 第三条　船舶因光船承租人对海事请求负有责任而被扣押的，海事请求人依据海事诉讼特别程序法第二十九条的规定，申请拍卖船舶用于清偿光船承租人经营该船舶产生的相关债务的，海事法院应予准许。 第四条　海事请求人申请扣押船舶的，海事法院应当责令其提供担保。但因船员劳务合同、海上及通海水域人身损害赔偿纠纷申请扣押船舶，且事实清楚、权利义务关系明确的，可以不要求提供担保。 第五条　海事诉讼特别程序法第七十六条第二款规定的海事请求人提供担保的具体数额，应当相当于船舶扣押期间可能产生的各项维持费用与支出、因扣押造成的船期损失和被请求人为使船舶解除扣押而提供担保所支出的费用。 船舶扣押后，海事请求人提供的担保不足以赔偿可能给被请求人造成损失的，海事法院应责令其追加担保。 第六条　案件终审后，海事请求人申请返还其所提供担保的，海事法院应将该申请告知被请求人，被请求人在三十日内未提起相关索赔诉讼的，海事法院可以准许海事请求人返还担保的申请。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　海事请求人申请对船舶采取限制处分或者抵押等保全措施的，海事法院可以依照民事诉讼法的有关规定，裁定准许并通知船舶登记机关协助执行。  
前款规定的保全措施不影响其他海事请求人申请扣押船舶。

**Article 1** Where a maritime claimant applies for preservation measures such as restricting the disposition of or mortgaging a vessel, the maritime court may, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, render an order to permit such measures and notify the vessel registration authority to assist in enforcement.  
The preservation measures provided for in the preceding paragraph shall not affect other maritime claimants applying for arrest of the vessel.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　海事法院应不同海事请求人的申请，可以对本院或其他海事法院已经扣押的船舶采取扣押措施。  
先申请扣押船舶的海事请求人未申请拍卖船舶的，后申请扣押船舶的海事请求人可以依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第二十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-29)的规定，向准许其扣押申请的海事法院申请拍卖船舶。

**Article 2** Upon the application of different maritime claimants, a maritime court may take arrest measures against a vessel already arrested by itself or by another maritime court.  
If the maritime claimant who first applied for the arrest of the vessel does not apply for auction of the vessel, a subsequent maritime claimant who applies for the arrest may, pursuant to Article 29 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, apply to the maritime court that permitted the arrest for auction of the vessel.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　船舶因光船承租人对海事请求负有责任而被扣押的，海事请求人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第二十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-29)的规定，申请拍卖船舶用于清偿光船承租人经营该船舶产生的相关债务的，海事法院应予准许。

**Article 3** Where a vessel is arrested because the bareboat charterer is liable for a maritime claim, and the maritime claimant applies, pursuant to Article 29 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, for auction of the vessel to satisfy the relevant debts incurred by the bareboat charterer in operating the vessel, the maritime court shall permit such application.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　海事请求人申请扣押船舶的，海事法院应当责令其提供担保。但因船员劳务合同、海上及通海水域人身损害赔偿纠纷申请扣押船舶，且事实清楚、权利义务关系明确的，可以不要求提供担保。

**Article 4** Where a maritime claimant applies for arrest of a vessel, the maritime court shall order the claimant to provide security. However, if the arrest is applied for due to a dispute over a crew labor contract or compensation for personal injury at sea or in navigable waters adjacent to the sea, and the facts are clear and the rights and obligations are definite, the court may not require the provision of security.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[海事诉讼特别程序法第七十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-76)第二款规定的海事请求人提供担保的具体数额，应当相当于船舶扣押期间可能产生的各项维持费用与支出、因扣押造成的船期损失和被请求人为使船舶解除扣押而提供担保所支出的费用。  
船舶扣押后，海事请求人提供的担保不足以赔偿可能给被请求人造成损失的，海事法院应责令其追加担保。

**Article 5** The specific amount of security to be provided by the maritime claimant as provided in Article 76, paragraph 2, of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall be equivalent to the various maintenance costs and expenses that may arise during the period of the vessel's arrest, the loss of voyage caused by the arrest, and the expenses incurred by the respondent in providing security to lift the arrest.  
Where, after the vessel is arrested, the security provided by the maritime claimant is insufficient to compensate for the loss that may be caused to the respondent, the maritime court shall order the claimant to provide additional security.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　案件终审后，海事请求人申请返还其所提供担保的，海事法院应将该申请告知被请求人，被请求人在三十日内未提起相关索赔诉讼的，海事法院可以准许海事请求人返还担保的申请。  
被请求人同意返还，或生效法律文书认定被请求人负有责任，且赔偿或给付金额与海事请求人要求被请求人提供担保的数额基本相当的，海事法院可以直接准许海事请求人返还担保的申请。

**Article 6** After the final adjudication of a case, where a maritime claimant applies for the return of the security provided, the maritime court shall notify the respondent of such application. If the respondent does not file a relevant claim action within thirty days, the maritime court may grant the maritime claimant's application for return of the security.  
If the respondent consents to the return, or if an effective legal instrument determines that the respondent bears liability and the amount of compensation or payment is substantially equivalent to the amount of security required by the maritime claimant from the respondent, the maritime court may directly grant the maritime claimant's application for return of the security.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　船舶扣押期间由船舶所有人或光船承租人负责管理。  
船舶所有人或光船承租人不履行船舶管理职责的，海事法院可委托第三人或者海事请求人代为管理，由此产生的费用由船舶所有人或光船承租人承担，或在拍卖船舶价款中优先拨付。

**Article 7** During the period of arrest, the vessel shall be managed by the shipowner or bareboat charterer.  
If the shipowner or bareboat charterer fails to perform the duty of vessel management, the maritime court may entrust a third party or the maritime claimant to manage the vessel on its behalf, and the expenses incurred thereby shall be borne by the shipowner or bareboat charterer, or be preferentially deducted from the proceeds of the auction of the vessel.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　船舶扣押后，海事请求人依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-19)的规定，向其他有管辖权的海事法院提起诉讼的，可以由扣押船舶的海事法院继续实施保全措施。

**Article 8** After the arrest of a vessel, if the maritime claimant, pursuant to Article 19 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, brings a lawsuit with another maritime court having jurisdiction, the maritime court that arrested the vessel may continue to implement the preservation measures.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　扣押船舶裁定执行前，海事请求人撤回扣押船舶申请的，海事法院应当裁定予以准许，并终结扣押船舶裁定的执行。  
扣押船舶裁定作出后因客观原因无法执行的，海事法院应当裁定终结执行。

**Article 9** Before the enforcement of the order to arrest the vessel, if the maritime claimant withdraws the application for arrest, the maritime court shall render an order to permit the withdrawal and terminate the enforcement of the arrest order.  
Where, after the arrest order is made, it cannot be enforced due to objective reasons, the maritime court shall render an order to terminate the enforcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　船舶拍卖未能成交，需要再次拍卖的，适用[拍卖法第四十五条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国拍卖法.md#art-45)关于拍卖日七日前发布拍卖公告的规定。

**Article 10** Where an auction of a vessel fails to conclude a transaction and a re-auction is necessary, the provisions of Article 45 of the Auction Law of the People's Republic of China concerning the publication of an auction notice seven days before the auction date shall apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　拍卖船舶由拍卖船舶委员会实施，海事法院不另行委托拍卖机构进行拍卖。

**Article 11** The auction of a vessel shall be carried out by the vessel auction committee, and the maritime court shall not separately entrust an auction institution to conduct the auction.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　海事法院拍卖船舶应当依据评估价确定保留价。保留价不得公开。  
第一次拍卖时，保留价不得低于评估价的百分之八十；因流拍需要再行拍卖的，可以酌情降低保留价，但降低的数额不得超过前次保留价的百分之二十。

**Article 12** When auctioning a vessel, the maritime court shall determine the reserve price based on the appraised value. The reserve price shall not be disclosed.  
At the first auction, the reserve price shall not be lower than 80% of the appraised value; if a re-auction is necessary due to a failure to sell, the reserve price may be reduced as appropriate, but the amount of reduction shall not exceed 20% of the previous reserve price.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　对经过两次拍卖仍然流拍的船舶，可以进行变卖。变卖价格不得低于评估价的百分之五十。

**Article 13** A vessel that remains unsold after two auctions may be sold by private sale. The price of the private sale shall not be lower than 50% of the appraised value.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　依照[本规定第十三条](#art-13)变卖仍未成交的，经已受理登记债权三分之二以上份额的债权人同意，可以低于评估价的百分之五十进行变卖处理。仍未成交的，海事法院可以解除船舶扣押。

**Article 14** Where a private sale conducted in accordance with Article 13 of these Provisions still does not result in a transaction, with the consent of creditors representing more than two-thirds of the registered claims accepted, the vessel may be sold by private sale at a price lower than 50% of the appraised value. If a transaction still cannot be concluded, the maritime court may discharge the arrest of the vessel.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　船舶经海事法院拍卖、变卖后，对该船舶已采取的其他保全措施效力消灭。

**Article 15** After a vessel is auctioned or sold by private sale by a maritime court, the validity of other preservation measures previously taken against the vessel shall be extinguished.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-111)规定的申请债权登记期间的届满之日，为拍卖船舶公告最后一次发布之日起第六十日。  
前款所指公告为第一次拍卖时的拍卖船舶公告。

**Article 16** The date of expiry of the period for applying for registration of claims as specified in Article 111 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall be the sixtieth day after the date of the last publication of the auction notice for the vessel.  
The notice referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be the auction notice for the vessel at the first auction.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　海事法院受理债权登记申请后，应当在船舶被拍卖、变卖成交后，依照[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-114)的规定作出是否准予的裁定。

**Article 17** After accepting an application for registration of claims, the maritime court shall, after the vessel is auctioned or sold by private sale, render an order on whether to grant or deny the application in accordance with Article 114 of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　申请拍卖船舶的海事请求人未经债权登记，直接要求参与拍卖船舶价款分配的，海事法院应予准许。

**Article 18** Where a maritime claimant who applied for the auction of a vessel directly requests to participate in the distribution of the proceeds from the auction without prior registration of claims, the maritime court shall permit such request.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　海事法院裁定终止拍卖船舶的，应当同时裁定终结债权登记受偿程序，当事人已经缴纳的债权登记申请费予以退还。

**Article 19** Where a maritime court renders an order to terminate the auction of a vessel, it shall at the same time render an order to terminate the procedures for registration of claims and satisfaction, and the application fees for registration of claims already paid by the parties shall be refunded.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　当事人在债权登记前已经就有关债权提起诉讼的，不适用[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-116)第二款的规定，当事人对海事法院作出的判决、裁定可以依法提起上诉。

**Article 20** Where a party has already instituted an action concerning a relevant claim before the registration of claims, the provisions of Article 116, paragraph 2, of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China shall not apply, and the party may appeal against the judgment or order rendered by the maritime court according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　债权人依照[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-116)第一款的规定提起确权诉讼后，需要判定碰撞船舶过失程度比例的，当事人对海事法院作出的判决、裁定可以依法提起上诉。

**Article 21** Where a creditor brings an action for confirmation of rights pursuant to Article 116, paragraph 1, of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, and it is necessary to determine the degree of fault of the colliding vessels, the parties may appeal against the judgment or order rendered by the maritime court according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　海事法院拍卖、变卖船舶所得价款及其利息，先行拨付[海事诉讼特别程序法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-119)第二款规定的费用后，依法按照下列顺序进行分配：  
（一）具有船舶优先权的海事请求；  
（二）由船舶留置权担保的海事请求；  
（三）由船舶抵押权担保的海事请求；  
（四）与被拍卖、变卖船舶有关的其他海事请求。  
依据[海事诉讼特别程序法第二十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国海事诉讼特别程序法.md#art-23)第二款的规定申请扣押船舶的海事请求人申请拍卖船舶的，在前款规定海事请求清偿后，参与船舶价款的分配。  
依照前款规定分配后的余款，按照民事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定执行。

**Article 22** The proceeds from the auction or private sale of a vessel by a maritime court, together with the interest thereon, shall, after the expenses specified in Article 119, paragraph 2, of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China are first deducted, be distributed in accordance with law in the following order:  
(1) Maritime claims secured by maritime liens;  
(2) Maritime claims secured by possessory liens on the vessel;  
(3) Maritime claims secured by mortgages on the vessel;  
(4) Other maritime claims relating to the vessel auctioned or sold by private sale.  
Where a maritime claimant who applied for arrest of a vessel pursuant to Article 23, paragraph 2, of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China applies for auction of the vessel, such claimant shall, after the maritime claims specified in the preceding paragraph are satisfied, participate in the distribution of the proceeds from the vessel.  
The balance after distribution in accordance with the preceding paragraph shall be enforced in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　当事人依照民事诉讼法第十五章第七节的规定，申请拍卖船舶实现船舶担保物权的，由船舶所在地或船籍港所在地的海事法院管辖，按照海事诉讼特别程序法以及本规定关于船舶拍卖受偿程序的规定处理。

**Article 23** Where a party applies for auction of a vessel to enforce a security interest in the vessel in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 15, Section 7, of the Civil Procedure Law, the maritime court of the place where the vessel is located or where its port of registry is located shall have jurisdiction, and the matter shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Special Maritime Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and these Provisions concerning the procedures for auction and satisfaction from the vessel.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　海事法院的上级人民法院扣押与拍卖船舶的，适用本规定。  
执行程序中拍卖被扣押船舶清偿债务的，适用本规定。

**Article 24** Where a people's court superior to a maritime court arrests and auctions a vessel, these Provisions shall apply.  
Where, in enforcement proceedings, an arrested vessel is auctioned to satisfy debts, these Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定施行前已经实施的船舶扣押与拍卖，本规定施行后当事人申请复议的，不适用本规定。  
本规定施行后，最高人民法院1994年7月6日制定的《关于海事法院拍卖被扣押船舶清偿债务的规定》（法发〔1994〕14号）同时废止。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 25** For arrests and auctions of vessels already carried out before these Provisions come into force, where a party files an application for reconsideration after these Provisions come into force, these Provisions shall not apply.  
After these Provisions come into force, the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Auction of Arrested Vessels by Maritime Courts to Satisfy Debts (Fa Fa [1994] No. 14) formulated on July 6, 1994, shall be abolished simultaneously. Where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
