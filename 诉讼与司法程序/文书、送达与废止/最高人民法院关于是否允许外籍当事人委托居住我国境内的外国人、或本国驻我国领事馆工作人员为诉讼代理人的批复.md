# 最高人民法院关于是否允许外籍当事人委托居住我国境内的外国人、或本国驻我国领事馆工作人员为诉讼代理人的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

---

### 正文

一、外籍当事人委托居住我国境内的本国人为诉讼代理人，不违背我国民事诉讼法（试行）的规定，可以准许。

**Article None** 1. Where a foreign party entrusts a national of his/her own country residing within the territory of China to act as a litigation agent, and such entrustment does not contravene the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation), it may be permitted.

二、外国驻华使、领馆官员，受本国国民的委托，以个人名义担任诉讼代理人时，亦应准许。同时根据我国参加的《维也纳领事关系公约》的规定，外国驻华领事馆官员（包括经我国外交部确认的外国驻华使馆的外交官同时兼有领事官衔者），当作为当事人的本国国民不在我国境内，或由于其他原因不能适时到我国法院出庭时，还可以在没有委托的情况下，直接以领事名义担任其代表或为其安排代表在我国法院出庭。  
你院请示中所提原居住在我国的德意志联邦共和国国民尤塔·毛雷尔，在其回国后，欲委托在上海市任教的本国人或本国驻上海领事馆工作人员，担任她离婚诉讼中代理人的问题，可参照上述意见办理。  
此复

**Article None** 2. When an official of a foreign embassy or consulate in China, entrusted by a national of his/her own country, acts as a litigation agent in a personal capacity, such action shall also be permitted. At the same time, in accordance with the provisions of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations to which China has acceded, a consular official of a foreign embassy in China (including a diplomat of a foreign embassy in China who concurrently holds a consular title as confirmed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of China) may, when the national of his/her own country who is a party is not within the territory of China or, for other reasons, cannot appear before a Chinese court in a timely manner, directly act as a representative in the name of the consular office, or arrange for a representative to appear before a Chinese court on behalf of such national, even without a formal entrustment.  
  
With respect to the issue raised in your request for instructions concerning Jutta Maurer, a national of the Federal Republic of Germany who originally resided in China and, after returning to her home country, intends to entrust a national of her own country who is teaching in Shanghai or a staff member of the Consulate of her country in Shanghai to act as her agent in her divorce action, the foregoing opinion may be referred to for handling.
