# 最高人民法院关于委托执行工作中两个问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1988〕25号  
**公布日期**：1988-06-20

---

### 正文

一、在委托执行中，受委托的人民法院认为委托法院委托执行的法律文书确有错误，应函请委托法院进行审查。在委托法院作出审查处理意见的答复后，受委托的法院如仍有异议，可向委托法院的上一级法院反映，但对委托执行的法律文书应即予以协助执行。

**Article None** 1. In entrusted enforcement, if the entrusted People's Court considers that the legal document entrusted by the entrusting court for enforcement is indeed erroneous, it shall request the entrusting court by letter to conduct a review. After the entrusting court makes a reply with its review opinion, if the entrusted court still has objections, it may report to the court at the next higher level above the entrusting court, but shall promptly provide assistance in enforcing the legal document entrusted for enforcement.

二、在委托执行中，受委托的人民法院如发现有民事诉讼法（试行）第一百八十二条规定的应当中止执行的情形时，应及时函请委托法院裁定中止执行，但不宜自行裁定中止执行。  
此复  
1988年6月20日

**Article None** 2. In entrusted enforcement, if the entrusted People's Court discovers circumstances that shall result in suspension of enforcement as provided in Article 182 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), it shall promptly request the entrusting court by letter to rule on the suspension of enforcement, but shall not rule on the suspension of enforcement on its own.  
  
This reply  
June 20, 1988
