### 最高人民法院关于产权人生前已处分的房屋死后不应认定为遗产的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于产权人生前已处分的房屋死后  
  
不应认定为遗产的批复  
  
（1987）民他字第31号  
  
贵州省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于陶冶与邓秀芳财产继承一案的请示报告》收悉。据报告称，陶庭柱、陶齐氏夫妇生有一子（陶国祥）二女（陶冶，另一女早亡）。陶庭柱于1924年死亡，遗有祖遗房屋3间。陶齐氏于1941年将3间房屋过户在儿子陶国祥名下并交了该房产权状。解放后该房产权仍由陶国祥登记，并管理使用达40余年。直至1968年陶齐氏死亡时，双方均未提出异议。1983年陶国祥死亡后，陶冶以房屋系父母遗产为由要求继承。陶冶有无权利继承此房。  
  
我们研究认为，此案讼争房屋虽系祖遗产，但陶齐氏已将产权状交与陶国祥，并在两次产权登记和私房改造中，均确定由陶国祥长期管理使用，陶冶在陶齐氏生前从未提出异议。据此应当认为该房产权早已转归陶国祥、邓秀芳夫妻共有。陶国祥死后的遗产，依法应由邓秀芳及其子女继承。陶冶无权要求继承。  
  
此复  
  
1987年6月24日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a House Already Disposed of by the Property Owner during His Lifetime Shall Not Be Recognized as Part of the Estate after His Death  
(1987) Min Ta Zi No. 31  
Gui Zhou Higher People's Court:  
We have received your court's Request for Instructions Regarding the Case of Inheritance of Property between Tao Ye and Deng Xiufang. According to the report, Tao Tingzhu and Tao Qishi, a married couple, had one son (Tao Guoxiang) and two daughters (Tao Ye; the other daughter died early). Tao Tingzhu died in 1924, leaving three ancestral rooms. In 1941, Tao Qishi transferred the three rooms into the name of her son Tao Guoxiang and delivered the title deed of the said house. After liberation, the property right of the said house was still registered under Tao Guoxiang, and he managed and used it for over 40 years. Until Tao Qishi's death in 1968, neither party raised any objection. After Tao Guoxiang's death in 1983, Tao Ye claimed inheritance on the ground that the house was the parents' estate. Does Tao Ye have the right to inherit this house?  
We have studied the case and are of the opinion that, although the house in dispute in this case was originally an ancestral property, Tao Qishi had already delivered the title deed to Tao Guoxiang, and during two registrations of property rights and the transformation of private houses, it was consistently determined that Tao Guoxiang should manage and use it for a long term. Tao Ye never raised any objection during Tao Qishi's lifetime. Accordingly, it shall be deemed that the property right of the said house had long been transferred to Tao Guoxiang and Deng Xiufang as their joint property. The estate after Tao Guoxiang's death shall, according to law, be inherited by Deng Xiufang and her children. Tao Ye has no right to claim inheritance.  
This is our reply.  
June 24, 1987
