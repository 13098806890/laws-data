# 最高人民法院第一审经济纠纷案件适用普通程序开庭审理的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕34号  
**公布日期**：1993-11-16

> （最高人民法院审判委员会第602次会议讨论通过） 法发〔1993〕34号 一、开庭前的工作 1、人民法院对决定受理的案件，应当在受理案件通知书和应诉通知书中，向当事人告知有关的诉讼权利义务，或者口头予以告知，如果已经确定开庭日期的，应当一并告知当事人及其诉讼代理人开庭的时间、地点。合议庭组成后，应当在三日内将合议庭组成人员告知当事人。告知后，因情事变化，必须调整合议庭组成人员的，应当于调整后三日内告知当事人。在开庭前三日内决定调整合议庭组成人员的，原定的开庭日期应予顺延。 2、合议庭成员应当认真审核双方提供的诉讼材料，了解案情，审查证据，掌握争议的焦点和需要庭审调查、辩论的主要问题。 3、必须共同进行诉讼的当事人没有参加诉讼的，应当通知其参加诉讼。 4、对专门性问题合议庭认为需要鉴定、审计的，应及时交由法定鉴定部门或者指定有关部门鉴定，委托审计机关审计。 5、开庭前，合议庭可以召集双方当事人及其诉讼代理人交换、核对证据，核算帐目。对双方当事人无异议的事实、证据应当记录在卷，并由双方当事人签字确认。在开庭审理时如双方当事人不再提出异议，便可予以认定。 在双方当事人自愿的条件下，合议庭可以在开庭审理前让双方当事人及其诉讼代理人自行协商解决。当事人和解，原告申请撤诉，或者双方当事人要求发给调解书的，经审查认为不违反法律规定，不损害第三人利益的，可以裁定准予撤诉，或者按照双方当事人达成的和解协议制作调解书发给当事人。 6、合议庭审查案卷材料后，认为法律关系明确、事实清楚，经证得当事人双方同意，可以在开庭审理前迳行调解。调解达成协议的，制作调解书发给当事人。双方当事人对案件事实无争议，只是在责任承担上达不成协议的，开庭审理可以在双方当事人对事实予以确认的基础上，直接进行法庭辩论。 7、开庭审理前达不成协议的，合议庭应即研究确定开庭审理的日期和庭审提纲，并明确合议庭成员在庭审中的分工。 8、开庭日期确定后，书记员应当在开庭三日前将传票送达当事人，将开庭通知书送达当事人的诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人、勘验人、翻译人员。当事人或其他诉讼参与人在外地的，应留有必要的在途时间。公开审理的，应当公告当事人姓名、案由和开庭的时间、地点。 9、开庭审理前，书记员应当查明当事人和其他诉讼参与人是否到庭。当事人或其他诉讼参与人没有到庭的，应将情况及时报告审判长，并由合议庭确定是否需要延期开庭审理或者中止诉讼。决定延期开庭审理的，应当及时通知当事人和其他诉讼参与人；决定中止诉讼的，应当制作裁定书，发给当事人。原告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，可以按撤诉处理；被告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，可以缺席判决。 二、宣布开庭 10、书记员宣布当事人及其诉讼代理人入庭。

### 正文

一、开庭前的工作  
1、人民法院对决定受理的案件，应当在受理案件通知书和应诉通知书中，向当事人告知有关的诉讼权利义务，或者口头予以告知，如果已经确定开庭日期的，应当一并告知当事人及其诉讼代理人开庭的时间、地点。合议庭组成后，应当在三日内将合议庭组成人员告知当事人。告知后，因情事变化，必须调整合议庭组成人员的，应当于调整后三日内告知当事人。在开庭前三日内决定调整合议庭组成人员的，原定的开庭日期应予顺延。  
2、合议庭成员应当认真审核双方提供的诉讼材料，了解案情，审查证据，掌握争议的焦点和需要庭审调查、辩论的主要问题。  
3、必须共同进行诉讼的当事人没有参加诉讼的，应当通知其参加诉讼。  
4、对专门性问题合议庭认为需要鉴定、审计的，应及时交由法定鉴定部门或者指定有关部门鉴定，委托审计机关审计。  
5、开庭前，合议庭可以召集双方当事人及其诉讼代理人交换、核对证据，核算帐目。对双方当事人无异议的事实、证据应当记录在卷，并由双方当事人签字确认。在开庭审理时如双方当事人不再提出异议，便可予以认定。  
在双方当事人自愿的条件下，合议庭可以在开庭审理前让双方当事人及其诉讼代理人自行协商解决。当事人和解，原告申请撤诉，或者双方当事人要求发给调解书的，经审查认为不违反法律规定，不损害第三人利益的，可以裁定准予撤诉，或者按照双方当事人达成的和解协议制作调解书发给当事人。  
6、合议庭审查案卷材料后，认为法律关系明确、事实清楚，经证得当事人双方同意，可以在开庭审理前迳行调解。调解达成协议的，制作调解书发给当事人。双方当事人对案件事实无争议，只是在责任承担上达不成协议的，开庭审理可以在双方当事人对事实予以确认的基础上，直接进行法庭辩论。  
7、开庭审理前达不成协议的，合议庭应即研究确定开庭审理的日期和庭审提纲，并明确合议庭成员在庭审中的分工。  
8、开庭日期确定后，书记员应当在开庭三日前将传票送达当事人，将开庭通知书送达当事人的诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人、勘验人、翻译人员。当事人或其他诉讼参与人在外地的，应留有必要的在途时间。公开审理的，应当公告当事人姓名、案由和开庭的时间、地点。  
9、开庭审理前，书记员应当查明当事人和其他诉讼参与人是否到庭。当事人或其他诉讼参与人没有到庭的，应将情况及时报告审判长，并由合议庭确定是否需要延期开庭审理或者中止诉讼。决定延期开庭审理的，应当及时通知当事人和其他诉讼参与人；决定中止诉讼的，应当制作裁定书，发给当事人。原告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，可以按撤诉处理；被告经传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，可以缺席判决。

**Article None** I. Pre-trial Work  
1. For a case that the People's Court decides to accept, it shall inform the parties of relevant litigation rights and obligations in the notice of acceptance and the notice of defense, or orally inform them. If the trial date has been determined, it shall simultaneously inform the parties and their litigation agents of the time and place of the trial. After the collegiate bench is formed, it shall inform the parties of the members of the collegiate bench within three days. If, after such notification, changes in circumstances necessitate an adjustment to the members of the collegiate bench, the parties shall be informed within three days after the adjustment. If the decision to adjust the members of the collegiate bench is made within three days before the trial, the originally scheduled trial date shall be postponed.  
2. The members of the collegiate bench shall carefully examine the litigation materials submitted by both parties, understand the case, review the evidence, and grasp the focus of the dispute and the main issues requiring investigation and debate during the trial.  
3. If a party that must participate in the joint action has not done so, it shall be notified to participate in the action.  
4. For specialized issues that the collegiate bench deems require expert evaluation or audit, it shall promptly refer them to the statutory expert evaluation department or designate a relevant department for evaluation, or entrust an audit authority for audit.  
5. Before the trial, the collegiate bench may convene the parties and their litigation agents to exchange and verify evidence and check accounts. Facts and evidence upon which the parties have no objection shall be recorded in the file and confirmed by the signatures of both parties. If, during the trial, the parties raise no further objections, such facts and evidence may be recognized.  
Under the condition that both parties voluntarily agree, the collegiate bench may allow the parties and their litigation agents to resolve the matter through negotiation before the trial. If the parties reach a settlement, the plaintiff applies for withdrawal of the prosecution, or both parties request a mediation letter, upon review and determination that it does not violate legal provisions and does not harm the interests of a third party, the court may rule to permit the withdrawal of the prosecution, or prepare a mediation letter based on the settlement agreement reached by both parties and serve it on the parties.  
6. After reviewing the case file materials, if the collegiate bench considers that the legal relationship is clear and the facts are evident, and with the consent of both parties, it may conduct mediation directly before the trial. If a mediation agreement is reached, a mediation letter shall be prepared and served on the parties. If both parties have no dispute over the facts of the case but fail to reach an agreement on the allocation of liability, the trial may proceed directly to court debate based on the confirmation of facts by both parties.  
7. If no agreement is reached before the trial, the collegiate bench shall promptly study and determine the trial date and the trial outline, and clarify the division of duties among the collegiate bench members during the trial.  
8. After the trial date is determined, the court clerk shall serve the summons on the parties and the notice of trial on the parties' litigation agents, witnesses, expert evaluators, inspectors, and interpreters at least three days before the trial. If the parties or other litigation participants are located elsewhere, sufficient travel time shall be allowed. For public trials, the parties' names, the cause of action, and the time and place of the trial shall be publicly announced.  
9. Before the trial, the court clerk shall ascertain whether the parties and other litigation participants are present. If a party or other litigation participant is absent, the situation shall be promptly reported to the presiding judge, and the collegiate bench shall determine whether to postpone the trial or suspend the action. If a decision is made to postpone the trial, the parties and other litigation participants shall be notified promptly; if a decision is made to suspend the action, a written order shall be prepared and served on the parties. If the plaintiff, after being summoned by summons, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, the case may be treated as withdrawal of the prosecution; if the defendant, after being summoned by summons, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, a default judgment may be entered.

二、宣布开庭  
10、书记员宣布当事人及其诉讼代理人入庭。  
11、书记员宣布法庭纪律。  
12、书记员宣布全体起立，请审判长、审判员、陪审员入庭。  
13、书记员向审判长报告当事人及其诉讼代理人的出庭情况。审判长核对当事人及其诉讼代理人的身份，并询问各方当事人对于对方出庭人员有无异议。  
14、当事人的身份经审判长核对无误，且当事人对对方出庭人员没有异议，审判长宣布各方当事人及其诉讼代理人符合法律规定，可以参加本案诉讼。  
15、审判长宣布案由及开始庭审，不公开审理的应当说明理由。  
16、被告经人民法院传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，审判长可以宣布缺席审理，并说明传票送达合法和缺席审理的依据。无独立请求权的第三人经人民法院传票传唤，无正当理由拒不到庭的，不影响案件的审理。  
17、审判长宣布合议庭组成人员、书记员名单。  
18、审判长告知当事人有关的诉讼权利义务，询问各方当事人是否申请回避。当事人提出申请回避的，合议庭应当宣布休庭。院长担任审判长时的回避，由审判委员会决定；审判人员的回避，由院长决定；其他人员的回避，由审判长决定。当事人申请回避的理由不能成立的，由审判长在重新开庭时宣布予以驳回，记入笔录；当事人申请回避的理由成立，决定回避的，由审判长宣布延期审理。  
当事人对驳回回避申请的决定不服，申请复议的，不影响案件的开庭。人民法院对复议申请，应当在三日内作出复议决定并通知复议申请人，也可以在开庭时当庭作出复议决定并告知复议申请人。

**Article None** II. Announcement of the Opening of Trial  
10. The court clerk announces that the parties and their litigation agents shall enter the courtroom.  
11. The court clerk announces the courtroom rules.  
12. The court clerk announces that all shall rise, and invites the presiding judge, judges, and jurors to enter the courtroom.  
13. The court clerk reports to the presiding judge the attendance of the parties and their litigation agents. The presiding judge verifies the identities of the parties and their litigation agents and asks each party whether they have any objection to the opposing party's attendees.  
14. After the identities of the parties are verified by the presiding judge to be correct, and no party objects to the opposing party's attendees, the presiding judge announces that the parties and their litigation agents meet the legal requirements and may participate in the litigation of this case.  
15. The presiding judge announces the cause of action and the commencement of the trial; for trials not held in public, the reasons shall be stated.  
16. If the defendant, after being summoned by the People's Court by summons, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, the presiding judge may announce a default hearing and state the legality of the service of the summons and the basis for the default hearing. If a third party with no independent claim, after being summoned by the People's Court by summons, refuses to appear in court without justifiable reasons, it shall not affect the trial of the case.  
17. The presiding judge announces the list of the members of the collegiate bench and the court clerk.  
18. The presiding judge informs the parties of the relevant litigation rights and obligations and asks each party whether they apply for withdrawal. If a party applies for withdrawal, the collegiate bench shall announce an adjournment. Withdrawal of the president when serving as the presiding judge shall be decided by the adjudication committee; withdrawal of adjudicators shall be decided by the president; withdrawal of other personnel shall be decided by the presiding judge. If the reasons for the party's application for withdrawal are not established, the presiding judge shall announce the rejection when the trial resumes, and record it in the record; if the reasons for the party's application for withdrawal are established and withdrawal is decided, the presiding judge shall announce a postponement of the trial.  
If a party is dissatisfied with the decision to reject the application for withdrawal and applies for reconsideration, it shall not affect the trial of the case. The People's Court shall make a reconsideration decision within three days and notify the applicant for reconsideration, or may make an immediate decision during the trial and inform the applicant for reconsideration.

三、法庭调查  
19、审判长宣布进行法庭调查后，应当告知当事人法庭调查的重点是双方争议的事实。当事人对自己提出的主张，有责任提供证据，反驳对方主张的，也应提供证据或说明理由。  
20、原告简要陈述起诉的请求和理由，或者宣读起诉书。  
21、被告针对原告起诉中的请求和理由作出承认或者否定的答辩，对双方确认的事实，应当记入笔录，法庭无须再作调查。  
22、第三人陈述或答辩。有独立请求权的第三人陈述诉讼请求及理由。无独立请求权的第三人针对原、被告的陈述提出承认或否认的答辩意见。  
23、案件有多个诉讼请求或多个独立存在的事实的，可按每个诉讼请求、每段事实争议的问题由当事人依次陈述、核对证据。  
24、双方当事人就争议的事实所提供的书证、物证、视听资料，应经对方辩认，互相质证。  
涉及国家机密、商业秘密的证据，当事人提交法庭的，法庭不能公开出示，但可以适当提示。  
25、凡是知道案件情况的单位和个人，都有义务出庭作证。证人出庭作证，法庭应查明证人身份，告知证人作证的义务以及作伪证应负的法律责任。证人作证后，应征询双方当事人对证人证言的意见。经法庭许可，当事人及其诉讼代理人可以向证人发问。  
证人确有困难不能出庭的，其所提交的书面证言应当当庭宣读。当事人自己调查取得的证人证言，由当事人宣读后提交法庭，对方当事人可以质询；人民法院调查取得的证人证言，由书记员宣读，双方当事人可以质询。  
26、勘验人、鉴定人宣读勘验笔录、鉴定结论后，由双方当事人发表意见。经法庭许可，当事人及其诉讼代理人可以向勘验人、鉴定人发问。  
27、双方当事人争议的事实查清后，审判长应当询问双方当事人有无新的证据提出，原告的诉讼请求或被告的反诉请求有无变更。当事人重复陈述的，审判长应当及时提醒或制止。  
28、案件的事实清楚后，审判长宣布法庭调查结束。  
29、当事人要求提供新的证据或者合议庭认为事实尚未查清，确需人民法院补充调查、收集证据或通知新的证人到庭、重新鉴定、勘验，因而需要延期审理的，可以宣布延期审理。需要当事人补充证据的，应告知其在限定期间内提供。

**Article None** III. Court Investigation  
19. After the presiding judge announces the commencement of the court investigation, he/she shall inform the parties that the focus of the court investigation is the facts disputed by the parties. The party making a claim has the responsibility to provide evidence; the party refuting the other party's claim shall also provide evidence or state reasons.  
20. The plaintiff briefly states the claims and reasons for the prosecution, or reads the complaint.  
21. The defendant presents a defense acknowledging or denying the claims and reasons in the plaintiff's prosecution. Facts confirmed by both parties shall be recorded in the record, and the court shall not investigate them further.  
22. The third party makes a statement or defense. A third party with an independent claim states the claim and reasons. A third party without an independent claim presents an opinion of acknowledgment or denial in response to the statements of the plaintiff and the defendant.  
23. If the case involves multiple claims or multiple independently existing facts, the parties may state and verify evidence sequentially for each claim or each disputed fact.  
24. Documentary evidence, physical evidence, and audio-visual materials provided by both parties on the disputed facts shall be identified by the opposing party and cross-examined.  
Evidence involving state secrets or trade secrets, when submitted to the court by the parties, shall not be publicly displayed in court, but may be appropriately indicated.  
25. Every unit or individual that knows the circumstances of the case has the obligation to appear in court to testify. When a witness appears to testify, the court shall verify the witness's identity, inform the witness of the obligation to testify and the legal liability for giving false testimony. After the witness testifies, the court shall solicit the opinions of both parties on the witness's testimony. With the permission of the court, the parties and their litigation agents may question the witness.  
If a witness is genuinely unable to appear in court, the written testimony submitted by the witness shall be read in court. Witness testimony obtained by the parties themselves shall be read by the party and then submitted to the court; the opposing party may challenge it. Witness testimony obtained by the People's Court shall be read by the court clerk, and both parties may challenge it.  
26. After the inspector or expert evaluator reads the inspection record or expert conclusion, both parties shall express their opinions. With the permission of the court, the parties and their litigation agents may question the inspector or expert evaluator.  
27. After the facts disputed by the parties are clarified, the presiding judge shall ask both parties whether they have new evidence to submit, and whether the plaintiff's claims or the defendant's counterclaims have changed. If a party makes repetitive statements, the presiding judge shall promptly remind or stop them.  
28. After the facts of the case are clear, the presiding judge announces the conclusion of the court investigation.  
29. If a party requests to submit new evidence or the collegiate bench considers that the facts have not yet been clarified and that it is necessary for the People's Court to conduct supplementary investigation or collect evidence, or to summon new witnesses to appear, or to conduct a new expert evaluation or inspection, thereby requiring a postponement of the trial, the presiding judge may announce a postponement. If the party needs to submit supplementary evidence, it shall be informed to do so within a specified period.

四、法庭辩论  
30、审判长宣布法庭辩论开始，当事人及其诉讼代理人就本案争议的问题进行辩论。辩论应当实事求是，以理服人。必要时，审判长可以根据案情限定当事人及其诉讼代理人每次发表意见的时间。  
31、原告及其诉讼代理人发言。  
32、被告及其诉讼代理人答辩。  
33、第三人及其诉讼代理人发言或答辩。  
34、第一轮辩论结束，审判长应当询问当事人是否还有补充意见。当事人要求继续发言的，应当允许，但要提醒不可重复。  
35、当事人没有补充意见的，审判长宣布法庭辩论终结。  
36、法庭辩论终结，审判长按照原告、被告、第三人的顺序征询各方最后意见。

**Article None** IV. Court Debate  
30. The presiding judge announces the commencement of the court debate, and the parties and their litigation agents shall debate the disputed issues of the case. The debate shall be based on facts and reason. When necessary, the presiding judge may, based on the circumstances of the case, limit the time for each party and their litigation agent to present their opinions each time.  
31. The plaintiff and its litigation agent make statements.  
32. The defendant and its litigation agent present a defense.  
33. The third party and its litigation agent make statements or present a defense.  
34. After the first round of debate concludes, the presiding judge shall ask the parties whether they have supplementary opinions. If a party requests to continue speaking, it shall be permitted, but the party shall be reminded not to repeat.  
35. If the parties have no supplementary opinions, the presiding judge announces the conclusion of the court debate.  
36. After the conclusion of the court debate, the presiding judge shall, in the order of plaintiff, defendant, and third party, solicit the final opinions of each party.

五、法庭辩论后的调解  
37、经过法庭调查和辩论，如果事实清楚的，审判长按照原告、被告和有独立请求权第三人的顺序询问当事人是否愿意调解。无独立请求权的第三人需要承担义务的，在询问原告、被告之后，还应询问其是否愿意调解。  
当事人愿意调解的，可以当庭进行，也可以休庭后进行。  
38、调解时，可以先由各方当事人提出调解方案。当事人意见不一致的，合议庭要讲清法律规定，分清责任，促使双方当事人达成协议。必要时，合议庭可以根据双方当事人的请求提出调解方案，供双方当事人考虑；也可以先分别征询各方当事人意见，而后进行调解。  
39、经过调解，双方当事人达成协议的，应当在调解协议上签字盖章。人民法院应当根据双方当事人达成的调解协议制作调解书送达当事人。双方当事人达成协议后当即履行完毕，不要求发给调解书的，应当记入笔录，在双方当事人、合议庭成员、书记员签名或盖章后，即具有法律效力。  
40、双方当事人当庭达成调解协议的，合议庭应当宣布调解结果，告知当事人调解书经双方当事人签收后，即具有法律效力。

**Article None** V. Mediation after Court Debate  
37. After the court investigation and debate, if the facts are clear, the presiding judge shall, in the order of plaintiff, defendant, and third party with an independent claim, ask the parties whether they are willing to mediate. If a third party without an independent claim must assume obligations, after asking the plaintiff and defendant, the court shall also ask whether that third party is willing to mediate.  
If the parties are willing to mediate, mediation may be conducted during the trial or after an adjournment.  
38. During mediation, each party may first propose a mediation plan. If the parties' opinions differ, the collegiate bench shall clarify the legal provisions, distinguish the responsibilities, and urge the parties to reach an agreement. When necessary, the collegiate bench may, based on the requests of both parties, propose a mediation plan for the parties' consideration; it may also first solicit the opinions of each party separately and then conduct mediation.  
39. If, after mediation, both parties reach an agreement, they shall sign and affix seals to the mediation agreement. The People's Court shall, based on the mediation agreement reached by both parties, prepare a mediation letter and serve it on the parties. If both parties immediately perform the agreement after reaching it and do not request a mediation letter, the matter shall be recorded in the record. After the parties, the collegiate bench members, and the court clerk have signed or affixed their seals, the record shall have legal effect.  
40. If both parties reach a mediation agreement during the trial, the collegiate bench shall announce the mediation result and inform the parties that the mediation letter shall have legal effect after being signed and received by both parties.

六、合议庭评议  
41、经过开庭审理后调解不成的，合议庭应当休庭进行评议，就案件的性质、认定的事实、适用的法律、是非责任和处理结果作出结论。  
42、评议中如发现案件事实尚未查清，需要当事人补充证据或者由人民法院自行调查收集证据的，可以决定延期审理，由审判长在继续开庭时宣布延期审理的理由和时间，以及当事人提供补充证据的期限。  
43、合议庭评议案件，实行少数服从多数的原则。评议中的不同意见，书记员必须如实记入笔录，由合议庭成员在笔录上签名。

**Article None** VI. Collegiate Bench Deliberation  
41. If mediation fails after the trial, the collegiate bench shall adjourn for deliberation and make conclusions on the nature of the case, the facts determined, the law applied, the merits and liabilities, and the result of disposition.  
42. If during deliberation it is found that the facts of the case have not yet been clarified and that the parties need to submit supplementary evidence or the People's Court needs to investigate and collect evidence on its own, it may decide to postpone the trial. The presiding judge shall, when the trial resumes, announce the reason and duration of the postponement, as well as the deadline for the parties to submit supplementary evidence.  
43. The collegiate bench shall deliberate on the case in accordance with the principle of majority rule. Any dissenting opinions during deliberation must be truthfully recorded in the record by the court clerk, and the collegiate bench members shall sign the record.

七、宣判  
44、合议庭评议后，由审判长宣布继续开庭并宣读裁判。宣判时，当事人及其他诉讼参与人、旁听人员应当起立。宣判的内容包括：认定的事实、适用的法律、判决的结果和理由、诉讼费用的负担、当事人的上诉权利、上诉期限和上诉法院。  
45、不能当庭宣判的，审判长应当宣布另定日期宣判。  
46、由书记员宣读庭审笔录，也可以告知当事人和其他诉讼参与人当庭或者在五日内阅读。  
庭审笔录经宣读或阅读，当事人和其他诉讼参与人认为记录无误的，应当在笔录上签名或盖章；拒绝签名、盖章的，记明情况附卷；认为对自己的陈述记录有遗漏或者差错，申请补正的，允许在笔录后面或另页补正。  
庭审笔录由合议庭成员和书记员签名。

**Article None** VII. Pronouncement of Judgment  
44. After the collegiate bench deliberation, the presiding judge shall announce the resumption of the trial and read the judgment. When the judgment is pronounced, the parties, other litigation participants, and observers shall stand. The contents of the pronouncement include: the facts determined, the law applied, the result and reasons of the judgment, the allocation of litigation costs, the parties' right to appeal, the appeal period, and the appellate court.  
45. If the judgment cannot be pronounced during the trial, the presiding judge shall announce that the judgment will be pronounced on another date.  
46. The court clerk shall read the trial record, or the parties and other litigation participants may be informed to read it during the trial or within five days.  
After the trial record is read or reviewed, if the parties and other litigation participants consider the record to be accurate, they shall sign or affix their seals to the record; if they refuse to sign or affix their seals, the circumstances shall be noted and attached to the file; if they consider that their statements have been omitted or contain errors in the record and apply for correction, they shall be permitted to make corrections at the end of the record or on a separate page.  
The trial record shall be signed by the collegiate bench members and the court clerk.

八、闭庭  
47、审判长宣布闭庭。  
48、书记员宣布全体起立，合议庭成员等退庭。  
49、合议庭成员退庭后，书记员宣布当事人和旁听人员退庭。

**Article None** VIII. Adjournment of Trial  
47. The presiding judge announces the adjournment of the trial.  
48. The court clerk announces that all shall rise, and the collegiate bench members and others shall withdraw from the courtroom.  
49. After the collegiate bench members withdraw, the court clerk announces that the parties and observers shall withdraw from the courtroom.
