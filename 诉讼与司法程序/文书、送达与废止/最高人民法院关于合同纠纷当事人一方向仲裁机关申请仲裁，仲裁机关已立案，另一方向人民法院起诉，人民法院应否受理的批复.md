### 最高人民法院关于合同纠纷当事人一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，仲裁机关已立案，另一方向人民法院起诉，人民法院应否受理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于合同纠纷当事人一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，  
  
仲裁机关已立案，另一方向人民法院起诉，  
  
人民法院应否受理的批复  
  
一九八五年八月三日法（经）复〔1985〕42号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院（85）沪高法办字第110号请示收悉。关于合同纠纷当事人一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，仲裁机关已立案，另一方向人民法院起诉，人民法院应否受理的问题，经研究答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国经济合同仲裁条例》第十二条第二款“一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，另一方向人民法院起诉的案件，仲裁机关不予受理”的规定。合同纠纷的当事人一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，即使仲裁机关已经立案，并发出应诉通知书，而另一方拒绝应诉，并向人民法院起诉的，只要起诉符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第八十一条规定的条件，人民法院即应予以受理。但是如果合同纠纷的当事人一方向仲裁机关申请仲裁，另一方作出答辩或者表示同意应诉后又向人民法院起诉的，人民法院不予受理。仲裁机关裁决后，当事人一方或者双方对裁决不服，可按《中华人民共和国经济合同法》第四十九条和《中华人民共和国经济合同仲裁条例》第三十三条的规定，向仲裁机关所在地的人民法院起诉。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a People's Court Shall Accept a Case Where One Party to a Contract Dispute Has Applied for Arbitration with an Arbitration Authority, the Arbitration Authority Has Already Docketed the Case, and the Other Party Has Filed a Lawsuit with the People's Court  
August 3, 1985 Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 42  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
We have received your request for instructions, Document No. (85) Hu Gao Fa Ban Zi No. 110. With regard to the issue of whether a People's Court shall accept a case where one party to a contract dispute has applied for arbitration with an arbitration authority, the arbitration authority has already docketed the case, and the other party has filed a lawsuit with the People's Court, after study, our reply is as follows:  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 12, paragraph 2, of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Arbitration of Economic Contracts, which states: "Where one party applies for arbitration with an arbitration authority and the other party files a lawsuit with a People's Court, the arbitration authority shall not accept the case." Where one party to a contract dispute applies for arbitration with an arbitration authority, even if the arbitration authority has already docketed the case and issued a notice of response to the lawsuit, and the other party refuses to respond and files a lawsuit with the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case as long as the lawsuit meets the conditions stipulated in Article 81 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation). However, if one party to a contract dispute applies for arbitration with an arbitration authority, and the other party submits a defense or expresses agreement to respond to the lawsuit and then files a lawsuit with the People's Court, the People's Court shall not accept the case. After the arbitration authority renders an award, if one party or both parties disagree with the award, they may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 49 of the Economic Contract Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 33 of the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Arbitration of Economic Contracts, file a lawsuit with the People's Court at the place where the arbitration authority is located.  
This is our reply.
