# 最高人民法院关于人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕7号  
**公布日期**：2018-02-12  
**生效日期**：2018-09-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息的规定》已于2018年2月12日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1733次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年9月1日起施行。 2018年3月4日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院通过互联网公开审判 流程信息的规定 法释〔2018〕7号 为贯彻落实审判公开原则，保障当事人对审判活动的知情权，规范人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息工作，促进司法公正，提升司法公信，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《中华人民共和国国家赔偿法》等法律规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院审判刑事、民事、行政、国家赔偿案件的流程信息，应当通过互联网向参加诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开。 人民法院审判具有重大社会影响案件的流程信息，可以通过互联网或者其他方式向公众公开。 第二条　人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息，应当依法、规范、及时、便民。 第三条　中国审判流程信息公开网是人民法院公开审判流程信息的统一平台。各级人民法院在本院门户网站以及司法公开平台设置中国审判流程信息公开网的链接。 有条件的人民法院可以通过手机、诉讼服务平台、电话语音系统、电子邮箱等辅助媒介，向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人主动推送案件的审判流程信息，或者提供查询服务。 第四条　人民法院应当在受理案件通知书、应诉通知书、参加诉讼通知书、出庭通知书中，告知当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人通过互联网获取审判流程信息的方法和注意事项。 第五条　当事人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人的身份证件号码、律师执业证号、组织机构代码、统一社会信用代码，是其获取审判流程信息的身份验证依据。 当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人应当配合受理案件的人民法院采集、核对身份信息，并预留有效的手机号码。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审判刑事、民事、行政、国家赔偿案件的流程信息，应当通过互联网向参加诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开。  
人民法院审判具有重大社会影响案件的流程信息，可以通过互联网或者其他方式向公众公开。

**Article 1** When a People's Court adjudicates criminal, civil, administrative, or state compensation cases, the case flow information shall be disclosed to the parties participating in the proceedings and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders via the internet.  
When a People's Court adjudicates cases with significant societal impact, the case flow information may be disclosed to the public via the internet or other means.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院通过互联网公开审判流程信息，应当依法、规范、及时、便民。

**Article 2** A People's Court shall disclose trial process information via the internet according to law, in a standardized, timely, and convenient manner.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　中国审判流程信息公开网是人民法院公开审判流程信息的统一平台。各级人民法院在本院门户网站以及司法公开平台设置中国审判流程信息公开网的链接。  
有条件的人民法院可以通过手机、诉讼服务平台、电话语音系统、电子邮箱等辅助媒介，向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人主动推送案件的审判流程信息，或者提供查询服务。

**Article 3** The China Trial Process Information Disclosure Website is the uniform platform for People's Courts to disclose trial process information. People's Courts at all levels shall set up links to the China Trial Process Information Disclosure Website on their own portal websites and judicial disclosure platforms.  
People's Courts with the necessary conditions may, through auxiliary media such as mobile phones, litigation service platforms, telephone voice systems, and email, actively push the trial process information of cases to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders, or provide inquiry services.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当在受理案件通知书、应诉通知书、参加诉讼通知书、出庭通知书中，告知当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人通过互联网获取审判流程信息的方法和注意事项。

**Article 4** A People's Court shall, in the Notice of Case Acceptance, Notice of Response, Notice of Participation in Proceedings, and Notice of Appearance, inform parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders of the methods and precautions for obtaining trial process information via the internet.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　当事人、法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人的身份证件号码、律师执业证号、组织机构代码、统一社会信用代码，是其获取审判流程信息的身份验证依据。  
当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人应当配合受理案件的人民法院采集、核对身份信息，并预留有效的手机号码。

**Article 5** The identity document number, lawyer's practice license number, organization code, and uniform social credit code of a party, legal representative, litigation agent, or defender shall serve as the identity verification basis for obtaining trial process information.  
Parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders shall cooperate with the People's Court accepting the case to collect and verify identity information, and shall reserve a valid mobile phone number.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院通知当事人应诉、参加诉讼，准许当事人参加诉讼，或者采用公告方式送达当事人的，自完成其身份信息采集、核对后，依照本规定公开审判流程信息。  
当事人中途退出诉讼的，经人民法院依法确认后，不再向该当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开审判流程信息。  
法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人参加诉讼或者发生变更的，参照前两款规定处理。

**Article 6** Where a People's Court summons a party to respond or participate in proceedings, permits a party to participate in proceedings, or serves a party by public announcement, it shall, after completing the collection and verification of the party's identity information, disclose the trial process information in accordance with these Provisions.  
Where a party withdraws from the proceedings midway, after being confirmed by the People's Court according to law, the trial process information shall no longer be disclosed to that party and its legal representative, litigation agent, or defender.  
Where a legal representative, litigation agent, or defender participates in the proceedings or a change occurs, the matter shall be handled by reference to the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　下列程序性信息应当通过互联网向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开：  
（一）收案、立案信息，结案信息；  
（二）检察机关、刑罚执行机关信息，当事人信息；  
（三）审判组织信息；  
（四）审判程序、审理期限、送达、上诉、抗诉、移送等信息；  
（五）庭审、质证、证据交换、庭前会议、询问、宣判等诉讼活动的时间和地点；  
（六）裁判文书在中国裁判文书网的公布情况；  
（七）法律、司法解释规定应当公开，或者人民法院认为可以公开的其他程序性信息。

**Article 7** The following procedural information shall be disclosed via the internet to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders:  
(1) Information on case receipt and filing, and case closure;  
(2) Information on procuratorial organs and penal enforcement organs, and information on parties;  
(3) Information on the adjudication organization;  
(4) Information on the trial procedure, trial period, service, appeal, protest, transfer, etc.;  
(5) The time and place of litigation activities such as trial, evidence examination, discovery, pre-trial conference, inquiry, and pronouncement of judgment;  
(6) The publication status of adjudicative documents on China Judgments Online;  
(7) Other procedural information that shall be disclosed according to laws and judicial interpretations, or that the People's Court deems may be disclosed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　回避、管辖争议、保全、先予执行、评估、鉴定等流程信息，应当通过互联网向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开。  
公开保全、先予执行等流程信息可能影响事项处理的，可以在事项处理完毕后公开。

**Article 8** Procedural information such as recusal, jurisdiction disputes, preservation, advance execution, appraisal, and expert evaluation shall be disclosed via the internet to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders.  
Where disclosure of procedural information such as preservation or advance execution may affect the handling of the matter, it may be disclosed after the matter has been handled.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列诉讼文书应当于送达后通过互联网向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开：  
（一）起诉状、上诉状、再审申请书、申诉书、国家赔偿申请书、答辩状等诉讼文书；  
（二）受理案件通知书、应诉通知书、参加诉讼通知书、出庭通知书、合议庭组成人员通知书、传票等诉讼文书；  
（三）判决书、裁定书、决定书、调解书，以及其他有中止、终结诉讼程序作用，或者对当事人实体权利有影响、对当事人程序权利有重大影响的裁判文书；  
（四）法律、司法解释规定应当公开，或者人民法院认为可以公开的其他诉讼文书。

**Article 9** The following litigation documents shall be disclosed via the internet to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders after service:  
(1) Litigation documents such as complaints, appeal petitions, applications for retrial, petitions, applications for state compensation, and answers;  
(2) Litigation documents such as Notices of Case Acceptance, Notices of Response, Notices of Participation in Proceedings, Notices of Appearance, Notices of Composition of the Collegial Panel, and summonses;  
(3) Judgments, rulings, decisions, mediation agreements, and other adjudicative documents that have the effect of suspending or terminating the proceedings, or that have an impact on the substantive rights of the parties or a significant impact on the procedural rights of the parties;  
(4) Other litigation documents that shall be disclosed according to laws and judicial interpretations, or that the People's Court deems may be disclosed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　庭审、质证、证据交换、庭前会议、调查取证、勘验、询问、宣判等诉讼活动的笔录，应当通过互联网向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开。

**Article 10** Records of litigation activities such as trial, evidence examination, discovery, pre-trial conference, investigation and evidence collection, inspection, inquiry, and pronouncement of judgment shall be disclosed via the internet to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人申请查阅庭审录音录像、电子卷宗的，人民法院可以通过中国审判流程信息公开网或者其他诉讼服务平台提供查阅，并设置必要的安全保护措施。

**Article 11** Where a party, legal representative, litigation agent, or defender applies to access audio and video recordings of trials or electronic case files, the People's Court may provide access through the China Trial Process Information Disclosure Website or other litigation service platforms, and shall set up necessary security protection measures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　涉及国家秘密，以及法律、司法解释规定应当保密或者限制获取的审判流程信息，不得通过互联网向当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人公开。

**Article 12** Trial process information involving state secrets, and that which shall be kept confidential or the access to which is restricted according to laws and judicial interpretations, shall not be disclosed via the internet to parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　已经公开的审判流程信息与实际情况不一致的，以实际情况为准，受理案件的人民法院应当及时更正。  
已经公开的审判流程信息存在[本规定第十二条](#art-12)列明情形的，受理案件的人民法院应当及时撤回。

**Article 13** Where disclosed trial process information is inconsistent with the actual situation, the actual situation shall prevail, and the People's Court accepting the case shall make timely corrections.  
Where disclosed trial process information falls under the circumstances listed in Article 12 of these Provisions, the People's Court accepting the case shall timely withdraw it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　经受送达人书面同意，人民法院可以通过中国审判流程信息公开网向民事、行政案件的当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人电子送达除判决书、裁定书、调解书以外的诉讼文书。  
采用前款方式送达的，人民法院应当按照[本规定第五条](#art-5)采集、核对受送达人的身份信息，并为其开设个人专用的即时收悉系统。诉讼文书到达该系统的日期为送达日期，由系统自动记录并生成送达回证归入电子卷宗。  
已经送达的诉讼文书需要更正的，应当重新送达。

**Article 14** With the written consent of the person to be served, a People's Court may, through the China Trial Process Information Disclosure Website, electronically serve litigation documents other than judgments, rulings, and mediation agreements to parties and their legal representatives and litigation agents in civil and administrative cases.  
Where service is effected using the method in the preceding paragraph, the People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 5 of these Provisions, collect and verify the identity information of the person to be served, and shall set up a personal dedicated immediate receipt system for that person. The date on which the litigation document arrives at the system shall be the date of service, and the system shall automatically record this and generate a proof of service to be included in the electronic case file.  
Where a served litigation document needs correction, it shall be served anew.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　最高人民法院监督指导全国法院审判流程信息公开工作。高级、中级人民法院监督指导辖区法院审判流程信息公开工作。  
各级人民法院审判管理办公室或者承担审判管理职能的其他机构负责本院审判流程信息公开工作，履行以下职责：  
（一）组织、监督审判流程信息公开工作；  
（二）处理当事人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、辩护人对审判流程信息公开工作的投诉和意见建议；  
（三）指导技术部门做好技术支持和服务保障；  
（四）其他管理工作。

**Article 15** The Supreme People's Court shall supervise and guide the trial process information disclosure work of courts nationwide. Higher People's Courts and Intermediate People's Courts shall supervise and guide the trial process information disclosure work of courts within their respective jurisdictions.  
The Trial Management Office or other bodies undertaking trial management functions of People's Courts at all levels shall be responsible for the trial process information disclosure work of that court, and shall perform the following duties:  
(1) Organizing and supervising the trial process information disclosure work;  
(2) Handling complaints and suggestions from parties and their legal representatives, litigation agents, and defenders regarding the trial process information disclosure work;  
(3) Guiding technical departments in providing technical support and service guarantees;  
(4) Other management work.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　公开审判流程信息的业务规范和技术标准，由最高人民法院另行制定。

**Article 16** The business norms and technical standards for disclosing trial process information shall be separately formulated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本规定自2018年9月1日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 17** These Provisions shall come into force as of September 1, 2018. Where judicial interpretations and normative documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
