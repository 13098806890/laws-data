### 最高人民法院关于对企业法人破产还债程序终结的裁定的抗诉应否受理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对企业法人破产还债程序  
  
终结的裁定的抗诉应否受理问题的批复  
  
法释〔1997〕2号  
  
（1997年7月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第924次会议通过，自1997年8月2日起施行）  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1996）苏申经复字第16号《关于对宣告企业法人破产还债程序终结裁定的抗诉应否受理问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
检察机关对人民法院作出的企业法人破产还债程序终结的裁定提出抗诉没有法律依据。检察机关对前述裁定提出抗诉的，人民法院应当通知其不予受理。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Jiangsu Higher People's Court:  
  
We have received and studied your Request for Instructions on Whether a Protest Against a Ruling on the Termination of the Bankruptcy and Debt Repayment Proceedings of an Enterprise Legal Person Should Be Accepted (No. [1996] Su Shen Jing Fu Zi 16). Upon deliberation, we reply as follows:  
  
There is no legal basis for a procuratorial organ to lodge a protest against a ruling made by a People's Court on the termination of the bankruptcy and debt repayment proceedings of an enterprise legal person. If a procuratorial organ lodges a protest against the aforesaid ruling, the People's Court shall notify it that the protest shall not be accepted.  
  
This reply is hereby given.
