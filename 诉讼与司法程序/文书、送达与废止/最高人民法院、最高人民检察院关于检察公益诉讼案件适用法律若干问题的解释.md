# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于检察公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕6号  
**公布日期**：2018-03-01  
**生效日期**：2018-03-02

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于检察公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2018年2月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1734次会议、2018年2月11日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第73次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年3月2日起施行。 2018年3月1日 最高人民法院    最高人民检察院 关于检察公益诉讼案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2018〕6号 （2018年2月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1734次会议、2018年2月11日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第73次会议通过，自2018年3月2日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为正确适用《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》关于人民检察院提起公益诉讼制度的规定，结合审判、检察工作实际，制定本解释。

**Article 1** This Interpretation is formulated in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China regarding the system of public interest litigation instituted by the People's Procuratorate, and in light of the actual circumstances of adjudication and procuratorial work, for the purpose of correctly applying such provisions.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院、人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件主要任务是充分发挥司法审判、法律监督职能作用，维护宪法法律权威，维护社会公平正义，维护国家利益和社会公共利益，督促适格主体依法行使公益诉权，促进依法行政、严格执法。

**Article 2** The main tasks of the People's Court and the People's Procuratorate in handling public interest litigation cases are to fully exert the functions of judicial adjudication and legal supervision, safeguard the authority of the Constitution and laws, maintain social fairness and justice, protect national interests and social public interests, urge eligible entities to exercise the right of public interest litigation according to law, and promote administration according to law and strict law enforcement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院、人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，应当遵守宪法法律规定，遵循诉讼制度的原则，遵循审判权、检察权运行规律。

**Article 3** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Court and the People's Procuratorate shall comply with the provisions of the Constitution and laws, adhere to the principles of litigation system, and follow the rules of operation of the adjudicative power and the procuratorial power.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民检察院以公益诉讼起诉人身份提起公益诉讼，依照民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法享有相应的诉讼权利，履行相应的诉讼义务，但法律、司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 4** Where the People's Procuratorate institutes public interest litigation as a public interest litigation plaintiff, it shall enjoy corresponding litigation rights and perform corresponding litigation obligations in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　市（分、州）人民检察院提起的第一审民事公益诉讼案件，由侵权行为地或者被告住所地中级人民法院管辖。  
基层人民检察院提起的第一审行政公益诉讼案件，由被诉行政机关所在地基层人民法院管辖。

**Article 5** A first-instance civil public interest litigation case instituted by a People's Procuratorate of a city (divided into districts, autonomous prefecture) shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the infringing act occurred or where the defendant has his domicile.  
A first-instance administrative public interest litigation case instituted by a Primary People's Procuratorate shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court of the place where the administrative organ sued is located.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民检察院办理公益诉讼案件，可以向有关行政机关以及其他组织、公民调查收集证据材料；有关行政机关以及其他组织、公民应当配合；需要采取证据保全措施的，依照民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法相关规定办理。

**Article 6** When handling public interest litigation cases, the People's Procuratorate may investigate and collect evidence materials from relevant administrative organs and other organizations and citizens; the relevant administrative organs and other organizations and citizens shall cooperate; if it is necessary to take evidence preservation measures, they shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law and the Administrative Procedure Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院审理人民检察院提起的第一审公益诉讼案件，可以适用人民陪审制。

**Article 7** When trying a first-instance public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Court may apply the people's assessor system.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院开庭审理人民检察院提起的公益诉讼案件，应当在开庭三日前向人民检察院送达出庭通知书。  
人民检察院应当派员出庭，并应当自收到人民法院出庭通知书之日起三日内向人民法院提交派员出庭通知书。派员出庭通知书应当写明出庭人员的姓名、法律职务以及出庭履行的具体职责。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court tries a public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate, it shall serve a notice of appearance in court on the People's Procuratorate three days before the court session.  
The People's Procuratorate shall dispatch personnel to appear in court, and shall submit a notice of dispatch of personnel to appear in court to the People's Court within three days from the date of receipt of the notice of appearance in court from the People's Court. The notice of dispatch of personnel to appear in court shall state the names, legal positions, and specific duties to be performed in court of the dispatched personnel.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　出庭检察人员履行以下职责：  
（一）宣读公益诉讼起诉书；  
（二）对人民检察院调查收集的证据予以出示和说明，对相关证据进行质证；  
（三）参加法庭调查，进行辩论并发表意见；  
（四）依法从事其他诉讼活动。

**Article 9** The procuratorial personnel appearing in court shall perform the following duties:  
(1) read out the public interest litigation complaint;  
(2) present and explain the evidence collected through investigation by the People's Procuratorate, and conduct cross-examination of relevant evidence;  
(3) participate in the court investigation, conduct debate and express opinions;  
(4) engage in other litigation activities according to law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民检察院不服人民法院第一审判决、裁定的，可以向上一级人民法院提起上诉。

**Article 10** If the People's Procuratorate does not accept the judgment or ruling of first instance rendered by the People's Court, it may lodge an appeal with the People's Court at the next higher level.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院审理第二审案件，由提起公益诉讼的人民检察院派员出庭，上一级人民检察院也可以派员参加。

**Article 11** When the People's Court tries a case of second instance, the People's Procuratorate that instituted the public interest litigation shall dispatch personnel to appear in court, and the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level may also dispatch personnel to participate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民检察院提起公益诉讼案件判决、裁定发生法律效力，被告不履行的，人民法院应当移送执行。  
二、民事公益诉讼

**Article 12** Where a judgment or ruling in a public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate has taken legal effect and the defendant fails to perform it, the People's Court shall transfer it for enforcement.  
II. Civil Public Interest Litigation

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民检察院在履行职责中发现破坏生态环境和资源保护、食品药品安全领域侵害众多消费者合法权益等损害社会公共利益的行为，拟提起公益诉讼的，应当依法公告，公告期间为三十日。  
公告期满，法律规定的机关和有关组织不提起诉讼的，人民检察院可以向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 13** Where the People's Procuratorate discovers in the performance of its duties any act that damages the ecological environment or natural resources, or infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of many consumers in the field of food and drug safety, or other acts that harm social public interests, and intends to institute public interest litigation, it shall make a public announcement according to law, and the announcement period shall be thirty days.  
Upon expiration of the announcement period, if the organs and relevant organizations prescribed by law do not institute a lawsuit, the People's Procuratorate may institute a lawsuit with the People's Court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民检察院提起民事公益诉讼应当提交下列材料：  
（一）民事公益诉讼起诉书，并按照被告人数提出副本；  
（二）被告的行为已经损害社会公共利益的初步证明材料；  
（三）检察机关已经履行公告程序的证明材料。

**Article 14** The People's Procuratorate shall submit the following materials when instituting a civil public interest litigation:  
(1) a civil public interest litigation complaint, together with copies according to the number of defendants;  
(2) preliminary evidence materials that the defendant's act has harmed social public interests;  
(3) evidence materials that the procuratorial organ has fulfilled the announcement procedure.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民检察院依据[民事诉讼法第五十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-55)第二款的规定提起民事公益诉讼，符合[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)第二项、第三项、第四项及本解释规定的起诉条件的，人民法院应当登记立案。

**Article 15** Where the People's Procuratorate institutes a civil public interest litigation pursuant to Article 55, paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law, and meets the conditions for prosecution set forth in Article 119, items (2), (3), (4) of the Civil Procedure Law and the provisions of this Interpretation, the People's Court shall register the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民检察院提起的民事公益诉讼案件中，被告以反诉方式提出诉讼请求的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 16** In a civil public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate, if the defendant files a counterclaim to make a claim, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院受理人民检察院提起的民事公益诉讼案件后，应当在立案之日起五日内将起诉书副本送达被告。  
人民检察院已履行诉前公告程序的，人民法院立案后不再进行公告。

**Article 17** After accepting a civil public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Court shall serve a copy of the complaint on the defendant within five days from the date of filing the case.  
If the People's Procuratorate has already fulfilled the pre-litigation announcement procedure, the People's Court shall not make further announcement after filing the case.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院认为人民检察院提出的诉讼请求不足以保护社会公共利益的，可以向其释明变更或者增加停止侵害、恢复原状等诉讼请求。

**Article 18** If the People's Court considers that the claims made by the People's Procuratorate are insufficient to protect social public interests, it may explain to the People's Procuratorate and suggest that it change or add claims such as cessation of infringement and restoration to original condition.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　民事公益诉讼案件审理过程中，人民检察院诉讼请求全部实现而撤回起诉的，人民法院应予准许。

**Article 19** During the trial of a civil public interest litigation case, if all the claims of the People's Procuratorate are satisfied and it withdraws the prosecution, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民检察院对破坏生态环境和资源保护、食品药品安全领域侵害众多消费者合法权益等损害社会公共利益的犯罪行为提起刑事公诉时，可以向人民法院一并提起附带民事公益诉讼，由人民法院同一审判组织审理。  
人民检察院提起的刑事附带民事公益诉讼案件由审理刑事案件的人民法院管辖。  
三、行政公益诉讼

**Article 20** When the People's Procuratorate institutes a criminal public prosecution for any criminal act that damages the ecological environment or natural resources, or infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of many consumers in the field of food and drug safety, or otherwise harms social public interests, it may simultaneously file an incidental civil public interest lawsuit with the People's Court, which shall be tried by the same adjudication body of the People's Court.  
A criminal incidental civil public interest litigation case instituted by the People's Procuratorate shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court that tries the criminal case.  
III. Administrative Public Interest Litigation

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民检察院在履行职责中发现生态环境和资源保护、食品药品安全、国有财产保护、国有土地使用权出让等领域负有监督管理职责的行政机关违法行使职权或者不作为，致使国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的，应当向行政机关提出检察建议，督促其依法履行职责。  
行政机关应当在收到检察建议书之日起两个月内依法履行职责，并书面回复人民检察院。出现国家利益或者社会公共利益损害继续扩大等紧急情形的，行政机关应当在十五日内书面回复。  
行政机关不依法履行职责的，人民检察院依法向人民法院提起诉讼。

**Article 21** Where the People's Procuratorate discovers in the performance of its duties that an administrative organ with supervisory and management responsibilities in fields such as ecological environment and resource protection, food and drug safety, state property protection, and assignment of the right to use state-owned land exercises its powers in violation of law or fails to exercise its powers, thereby causing harm to national interests or social public interests, it shall issue a procuratorial recommendation to the administrative organ, urging it to perform its duties according to law.  
The administrative organ shall perform its duties according to law within two months from the date of receipt of the procuratorial recommendation, and reply in writing to the People's Procuratorate. In emergency circumstances such as the continued expansion of harm to national interests or social public interests, the administrative organ shall reply in writing within fifteen days.  
If the administrative organ fails to perform its duties according to law, the People's Procuratorate shall file a lawsuit with the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民检察院提起行政公益诉讼应当提交下列材料：  
（一）行政公益诉讼起诉书，并按照被告人数提出副本；  
（二）被告违法行使职权或者不作为，致使国家利益或者社会公共利益受到侵害的证明材料；  
（三）检察机关已经履行诉前程序，行政机关仍不依法履行职责或者纠正违法行为的证明材料。

**Article 22** The People's Procuratorate shall submit the following materials when instituting an administrative public interest litigation:  
(1) an administrative public interest litigation complaint, together with copies according to the number of defendants;  
(2) evidence materials that the defendant's unlawful exercise of powers or failure to exercise powers has caused harm to national interests or social public interests;  
(3) evidence materials that the procuratorial organ has fulfilled the pre-litigation procedure and the administrative organ still fails to perform its duties according to law or correct the illegal act.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民检察院依据[行政诉讼法第二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-25)第四款的规定提起行政公益诉讼，符合[行政诉讼法第四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-49)第二项、第三项、第四项及本解释规定的起诉条件的，人民法院应当登记立案。

**Article 23** Where the People's Procuratorate institutes an administrative public interest litigation pursuant to Article 25, paragraph 4 of the Administrative Procedure Law, and meets the conditions for prosecution set forth in Article 49, items (2), (3), (4) of the Administrative Procedure Law and the provisions of this Interpretation, the People's Court shall register the case.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在行政公益诉讼案件审理过程中，被告纠正违法行为或者依法履行职责而使人民检察院的诉讼请求全部实现，人民检察院撤回起诉的，人民法院应当裁定准许；人民检察院变更诉讼请求，请求确认原行政行为违法的，人民法院应当判决确认违法。

**Article 24** During the trial of an administrative public interest litigation case, if the defendant corrects the illegal act or performs its duties according to law such that all the claims of the People's Procuratorate are satisfied, and the People's Procuratorate withdraws the prosecution, the People's Court shall render a ruling to permit the withdrawal; if the People's Procuratorate changes its claims to request confirmation that the original administrative act is illegal, the People's Court shall render a judgment confirming the illegality.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民法院区分下列情形作出行政公益诉讼判决：  
（一）被诉行政行为具有[行政诉讼法第七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-74)、第七十五条规定情形之一的，判决确认违法或者确认无效，并可以同时判决责令行政机关采取补救措施；  
（二）被诉行政行为具有[行政诉讼法第七十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-70)规定情形之一的，判决撤销或者部分撤销，并可以判决被诉行政机关重新作出行政行为；  
（三）被诉行政机关不履行法定职责的，判决在一定期限内履行；  
（四）被诉行政机关作出的行政处罚明显不当，或者其他行政行为涉及对款额的确定、认定确有错误的，判决予以变更；  
（五）被诉行政行为证据确凿，适用法律、法规正确，符合法定程序，未超越职权，未滥用职权，无明显不当，或者人民检察院诉请被诉行政机关履行法定职责理由不成立的，判决驳回诉讼请求。  
人民法院可以将判决结果告知被诉行政机关所属的人民政府或者其他相关的职能部门。  
四、附　则

**Article 25** The People's Court shall render a judgment in an administrative public interest litigation case distinguishing the following circumstances:  
(1) if the administrative act under suit falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 74 or Article 75 of the Administrative Procedure Law, it shall render a judgment confirming the illegality or confirming that the act is void, and may at the same time render a judgment ordering the administrative organ to take remedial measures;  
(2) if the administrative act under suit falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 70 of the Administrative Procedure Law, it shall render a judgment revoking or partially revoking the act, and may render a judgment ordering the administrative organ under suit to re-make the administrative act;  
(3) if the administrative organ under suit fails to perform its statutory duties, it shall render a judgment ordering performance within a certain period;  
(4) if the administrative penalty made by the administrative organ under suit is obviously improper, or there is indeed an error in the determination or confirmation of the amount involved in other administrative acts, it shall render a judgment making a change;  
(5) if the evidence of the administrative act under suit is conclusive, the applicable laws and regulations are correct, the statutory procedures are complied with, there is no ultra vires, no abuse of power, no obvious impropriety, or the reasons for the People's Procuratorate's claim that the administrative organ under suit should perform its statutory duties are not established, it shall render a judgment dismissing the claim.  
The People's Court may notify the people's government to which the administrative organ under suit belongs or other relevant functional departments of the judgment result.  
IV. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释未规定的其他事项，适用民事诉讼法、行政诉讼法以及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 26** Matters not otherwise provided for in this Interpretation shall be governed by the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the Administrative Procedure Law, and relevant judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本解释自2018年3月2日起施行。  
最高人民法院、最高人民检察院之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 27** This Interpretation shall come into force as of March 2, 2018.  
Where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
