### 最高人民法院关于人民法院离退休审判人员不得担任参与自己审理过的案件一方当事人的委托代理人的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院批复  
  
关于人民法院离退休审判人员不得担任  
  
参与自己审理过的案件一方当事人的委托代理人的批复  
  
法（民）复〔1990〕1号  
  
黑龙江省高级人民法院：  
  
你院黑法民字（ 1989）148号《关于人民法院离退休审判人员可否担任自己审理过的民事案件的上诉审、再审、提审的一方当事人的委托代理人》的请示报告收悉。经研究，我们同意你院的意见，即人民法院的离退休审判人员对曾经参与审理过的案件，不得担任该案一方当事人的委托代理人。  
  
1990年1月16日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether Retired Adjudicators of the People's Court May Not Act as Authorized Agents of a Party in a Case That They Themselves Have Previously Adjudicated  
Fa (Min) Fu [1990] No. 1  
Higher People's Court of Heilongjiang Province:  
Your court's report requesting instructions, Hei Fa Min Zi (1989) No. 148, titled "Report on Whether Retired Adjudicators of the People's Court May Act as Authorized Agents of a Party in the Appeal, Retrial, or New Trial of a Civil Case That They Themselves Have Previously Adjudicated", has been received. Upon review, we agree with your court's opinion, namely, that retired adjudicators of the People's Court shall not act as authorized agents of a party in a case that they themselves have previously adjudicated.  
January 16, 1990
