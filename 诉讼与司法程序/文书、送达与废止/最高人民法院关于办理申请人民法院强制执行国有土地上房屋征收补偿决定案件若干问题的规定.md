# 最高人民法院关于办理申请人民法院强制执行国有土地上房屋征收补偿决定案件若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2012-02-27  
**生效日期**：2012-04-10

> 最高人民法院《关于办理申请人民法院强制执行国有土地上房屋征收补偿决定案件若干问题的规定》已于2012年2月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1543次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年4月10日起施行。 2012年3月26日 最高人民法院 关于办理申请人民法院强制执行国有土地上 房屋征收补偿决定案件若干问题的规定 法释〔2012] 4号 为依法正确办理市、县级人民政府申请人民法院强制执行国有土地上房屋征收补偿决定（以下简称征收补偿决定）案件，维护公共利益，保障被征收房屋所有权人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国行政强制法》、《国有土地上房屋征收与补偿条例》（以下简称《条例》）等有关法律、行政法规规定，结合审判实际，制定本规定。 第一条  申请人民法院强制执行征收补偿决定案件，由房屋所在地基层人民法院管辖，高级人民法院可以根据本地实际情况决定管辖法院。 第二条  申请机关向人民法院申请强制执行，除提供《条例》第二十八条规定的强制执行申请书及附具材料外，还应当提供下列材料： （一）征收补偿决定及相关证据和所依据的规范性文件； （二）征收补偿决定送达凭证、催告情况及房屋被征收人、直接利害关系人的意见； （三）社会稳定风险评估材料； （四）申请强制执行的房屋状况； （五）被执行人的姓名或者名称、住址及与强制执行相关的财产状况等具体情况； （六）法律、行政法规规定应当提交的其他材料。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  申请人民法院强制执行征收补偿决定案件，由房屋所在地基层人民法院管辖，高级人民法院可以根据本地实际情况决定管辖法院。

**Article 1** Applications to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement of expropriation and compensation decisions shall be under the jurisdiction of the Primary People's Court in the location of the house. The Higher People's Court may, based on local actual circumstances, determine the court with jurisdiction.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  申请机关向人民法院申请强制执行，除提供[《条例》第二十八条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/土地城建/国有土地上房屋征收与补偿条例.md#art-28)规定的强制执行申请书及附具材料外，还应当提供下列材料：  
（一）征收补偿决定及相关证据和所依据的规范性文件；  
（二）征收补偿决定送达凭证、催告情况及房屋被征收人、直接利害关系人的意见；  
（三）社会稳定风险评估材料；  
（四）申请强制执行的房屋状况；  
（五）被执行人的姓名或者名称、住址及与强制执行相关的财产状况等具体情况；  
（六）法律、行政法规规定应当提交的其他材料。  
强制执行申请书应当由申请机关负责人签名，加盖申请机关印章，并注明日期。  
强制执行的申请应当自被执行人的法定起诉期限届满之日起三个月内提出；逾期申请的，除有正当理由外，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 2** When an applying organ applies to the People's Court for compulsory enforcement, in addition to providing the written application for compulsory enforcement and attached materials as prescribed in Article 28 of the Regulations, it shall also provide the following materials:  
(1) The expropriation and compensation decision and relevant evidence and the normative documents on which it is based;  
(2) The service receipt of the expropriation and compensation decision, the notice of demand, and the opinions of the person whose house is expropriated and the directly interested persons;  
(3) The materials for assessment of social stability risks;  
(4) The condition of the house subject to compulsory enforcement;  
(5) Specific information such as the name or title, address of the person subject to enforcement, and the property status related to compulsory enforcement;  
(6) Other materials that shall be submitted according to laws and administrative regulations.  
The written application for compulsory enforcement shall be signed by the responsible person of the applying organ, affixed with the seal of the applying organ, and dated.  
The application for compulsory enforcement shall be filed within three months from the date on which the statutory period for prosecution of the person subject to enforcement expires; if the application is filed beyond the time limit, except for legitimate reasons, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  人民法院认为强制执行的申请符合形式要件且材料齐全的，应当在接到申请后五日内立案受理，并通知申请机关；不符合形式要件或者材料不全的应当限期补正，并在最终补正的材料提供后五日内立案受理；不符合形式要件或者逾期无正当理由不补正材料的，裁定不予受理。  
申请机关对不予受理的裁定有异议的，可以自收到裁定之日起十五日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起十五日内作出裁定。

**Article 3** Where the People's Court considers that the application for compulsory enforcement meets the formal requirements and the materials are complete, it shall file and accept the case within five days after receiving the application, and notify the applying organ; where the formal requirements are not met or the materials are incomplete, it shall require supplementation within a time limit, and shall file and accept the case within five days after the final supplemented materials are provided; where the formal requirements are not met or the materials are not supplemented without legitimate reasons within the time limit, it shall rule that the application is not accepted.  
If the applying organ disagrees with the ruling of non-acceptance, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the ruling, apply for reconsideration to the next higher People's Court, which shall make a ruling within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  人民法院应当自立案之日起三十日内作出是否准予执行的裁定；有特殊情况需要延长审查期限的，由高级人民法院批准。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall, within thirty days from the date of filing the case, make a ruling on whether to grant enforcement; if special circumstances require an extension of the review period, it shall be approved by the Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  人民法院在审查期间，可以根据需要调取相关证据、询问当事人、组织听证或者进行现场调查。

**Article 5** During the review period, the People's Court may, as necessary, retrieve relevant evidence, question the parties, organize hearings, or conduct on-site investigations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  征收补偿决定存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应当裁定不准予执行：  
（一）明显缺乏事实根据；  
（二）明显缺乏法律、法规依据；  
（三）明显不符合公平补偿原则，严重损害被执行人合法权益，或者使被执行人基本生活、生产经营条件没有保障；  
（四）明显违反行政目的，严重损害公共利益；  
（五）严重违反法定程序或者正当程序；  
（六）超越职权；  
（七）法律、法规、规章等规定的其他不宜强制执行的情形。  
人民法院裁定不准予执行的，应当说明理由，并在五日内将裁定送达申请机关。

**Article 6** Where an expropriation and compensation decision falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall rule that enforcement shall not be granted:  
(1) Obvious lack of factual basis;  
(2) Obvious lack of legal or regulatory basis;  
(3) Obvious non-conformity with the principle of fair compensation, seriously infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of the person subject to enforcement, or failing to guarantee the basic living and production/operation conditions of the person subject to enforcement;  
(4) Obvious violation of administrative purposes, seriously harming the public interest;  
(5) Serious violation of statutory procedures or due process;  
(6) Exceeding authority;  
(7) Other circumstances where enforcement is not appropriate as prescribed by laws, regulations, rules, etc.  
Where the People's Court rules that enforcement shall not be granted, it shall state the reasons and serve the ruling on the applying organ within five days.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  申请机关对不准予执行的裁定有异议的，可以自收到裁定之日起十五日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，上一级人民法院应当自收到复议申请之日起三十日内作出裁定。

**Article 7** If the applying organ disagrees with the ruling that enforcement shall not be granted, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the ruling, apply for reconsideration to the next higher People's Court, which shall make a ruling within thirty days from the date of receipt of the application for reconsideration.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院裁定准予执行的，应当在五日内将裁定送达申请机关和被执行人，并可以根据实际情况建议申请机关依法采取必要措施，保障征收与补偿活动顺利实施。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court rules to grant enforcement, it shall serve the ruling on the applying organ and the person subject to enforcement within five days, and may, based on actual circumstances, recommend that the applying organ take necessary measures according to law to ensure the smooth implementation of the expropriation and compensation activities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院裁定准予执行的，一般由作出征收补偿决定的市、县级人民政府组织实施，也可以由人民法院执行。

**Article 9** Where the People's Court rules to grant enforcement, it shall generally be organized and implemented by the municipal or county-level people's government that made the expropriation and compensation decision, or may be enforced by the People's Court.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  《条例》施行前已依法取得房屋拆迁许可证的项目，人民法院裁定准予执行房屋拆迁裁决的，参照[本规定第九条](#art-9)精神办理。

**Article 10** For projects that have legally obtained a house demolition permit before the Regulations come into force, where the People's Court rules to grant enforcement of a house demolition decision, it shall be handled by reference to the principle of Article 9 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  最高人民法院以前所作的司法解释与本规定不一致的，按本规定执行。

**Article 11** Where previous judicial interpretations made by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
