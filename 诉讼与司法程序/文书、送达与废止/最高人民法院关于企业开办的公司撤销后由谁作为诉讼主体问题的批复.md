### 最高人民法院关于企业开办的公司撤销后由谁作为诉讼主体问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于企业开办的公司撤销后由谁作为诉讼主体问题的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1987〕42号  
  
新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院：  
  
你院1987年6月25日关于涉及新疆石河子华侨农工商联合企业（又名华侨农场，以下称华侨农场）开办的富华经销公司（简称富华公司）的经济纠纷案件应如何确定诉讼主体的请示报告收悉。现答复如下：  
  
华侨农场系全民所有制企业，富华公司为华侨农场开办的公司。经与有关部门联系，国务院国发〔1985〕102号《关于进一步清理和整顿公司的通知》也适用于企业办的公司。因此，同意你院对华侨农场适用上述通知的意见，富华公司资不抵债，华侨农场应对富华公司的债务负连带责任。鉴于华侨农场在富华公司被撤销后即成立了财产清算小组负责清理该公司的财产，根据本院1984年9月17日《关于在经济审判工作中贯彻执行〈民事诉讼法（试行）〉若干问题的意见》“企业关闭的，由其主管单位或清理人（单位）作诉讼当事人”的规定，对涉及富华公司的经济纠纷案件，人民法院应将华侨农场和富华公司财产清算小组列为共同被告。  
  
乌鲁木齐市中级法院审结的河北省青龙县皮毛厂诉富华公司购销羊皮合同货款纠纷案，在执行过程中，富华公司被撤销。根据上述原则，该院应当依照民事诉讼法（试行）第一百二十二条第一款第（六）项规定，裁定确认华侨农场和富华公司财产清算小组承担民事责任，执行原调解协议。  
  
此复  
  
1987年10月15日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning the Question of Who Shall Act as Litigation Subject After the Company Established by an Enterprise Is Dissolved  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1987] No. 42  
Higher People's Court of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region:  
  
We have received your report dated June 25, 1987, requesting instruction on how to determine the litigation subject in economic dispute cases involving Fuhua Distribution Company (hereinafter referred to as Fuhua Company) established by Xinjiang Shihezi Overseas Chinese Agricultural, Industrial and Commercial United Enterprise (also known as Overseas Chinese Farm, hereinafter referred to as Overseas Chinese Farm). Our reply is as follows:  
  
Overseas Chinese Farm is an enterprise owned by the whole people, and Fuhua Company is a company established by Overseas Chinese Farm. After contacting the relevant departments, the State Council's Notice on Further Clearing and Rectifying Companies (Guofa [1985] No. 102) is also applicable to companies established by enterprises. Therefore, we agree with your opinion that Overseas Chinese Farm shall apply the above-mentioned notice, and since Fuhua Company's assets are insufficient to cover its debts, Overseas Chinese Farm shall bear joint and several liability for Fuhua Company's debts. In view of the fact that Overseas Chinese Farm established a property liquidation team responsible for liquidating the company's property after Fuhua Company was dissolved, in accordance with the provision of this Court's Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation) in Economic Adjudication (September 17, 1984) that "if an enterprise is closed, its competent authority or liquidator (entity) shall act as litigation party," for the economic dispute cases involving Fuhua Company, the People's Court shall list Overseas Chinese Farm and the property liquidation team of Fuhua Company as co-defendants.  
  
In the case of the dispute over payment for the purchase and sale of sheepskin contracts between Hebei Qinglong County Fur Factory and Fuhua Company, which was concluded by the Intermediate People's Court of Urumqi, Fuhua Company was dissolved during the enforcement process. In accordance with the above principles, that court shall, pursuant to Article 122, Paragraph 1, Item (6) of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), render a ruling confirming that Overseas Chinese Farm and the property liquidation team of Fuhua Company shall bear civil liability and enforce the original mediation agreement.  
  
This is our reply.  
  
October 15, 1987
