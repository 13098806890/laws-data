# 最高人民法院关于证券纠纷代表人诉讼若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕5号  
**公布日期**：2020-07-31  
**生效日期**：2020-07-31

> 《最高人民法院关于证券纠纷代表人诉讼若干问题的规定》已于2020年 7月 23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第 1808次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年7月31日起施行。 2020年7月30日 最高人民法院 关于证券纠纷代表人诉讼若干问题的规定 法释〔2020〕5号 为进一步完善证券集体诉讼制度，便利投资者提起和参加诉讼，降低投资者维权成本，保护投资者合法权益，有效惩治资本市场违法违规行为，维护资本市场健康稳定发展，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国证券法》等法律的规定，结合证券市场实际和审判实践，制定本规定。 一、一般规定 第一条　本规定所指证券纠纷代表人诉讼包括因证券市场虚假陈述、内幕交易、操纵市场等行为引发的普通代表人诉讼和特别代表人诉讼。 普通代表人诉讼是依据民事诉讼法第五十三条、第五十四条、证券法第九十五条第一款、第二款规定提起的诉讼；特别代表人诉讼是依据证券法第九十五条第三款规定提起的诉讼。 第二条　证券纠纷代表人诉讼案件，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在的市、计划单列市和经济特区中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。 对多个被告提起的诉讼，由发行人住所地有管辖权的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖；对发行人以外的主体提起的诉讼，由被告住所地有管辖权的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。 特别代表人诉讼案件，由涉诉证券集中交易的证券交易所、国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所所在地的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。 第三条　人民法院应当充分发挥多元解纷机制的功能，按照自愿、合法原则，引导和鼓励当事人通过行政调解、行业调解、专业调解等非诉讼方式解决证券纠纷。 当事人选择通过诉讼方式解决纠纷的，人民法院应当及时立案。案件审理过程中应当着重调解。 第四条　人民法院审理证券纠纷代表人诉讼案件，应当依托信息化技术手段开展立案登记、诉讼文书送达、公告和通知、权利登记、执行款项发放等工作，便利当事人行使诉讼权利、履行诉讼义务，提高审判执行的公正性、高效性和透明度。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所指证券纠纷代表人诉讼包括因证券市场虚假陈述、内幕交易、操纵市场等行为引发的普通代表人诉讼和特别代表人诉讼。  
普通代表人诉讼是依据[民事诉讼法第五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-53)、第五十四条、[证券法第九十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-95)第一款、第二款规定提起的诉讼；特别代表人诉讼是依据[证券法第九十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-95)第三款规定提起的诉讼。

**Article 1** The representative actions for securities disputes referred to in these Provisions include ordinary representative actions and special representative actions arising from acts such as false statements, insider trading, and market manipulation in the securities market.  
An ordinary representative action is an action filed in accordance with Article 53 and Article 54 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 95, paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China; a special representative action is an action filed in accordance with Article 95, paragraph 3 of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　证券纠纷代表人诉讼案件，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府所在的市、计划单列市和经济特区中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。  
对多个被告提起的诉讼，由发行人住所地有管辖权的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖；对发行人以外的主体提起的诉讼，由被告住所地有管辖权的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。  
特别代表人诉讼案件，由涉诉证券集中交易的证券交易所、国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所所在地的中级人民法院或者专门人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Cases of representative actions for securities disputes shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court or the special people's court of the city where the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government is located, the city separately listed in the state plan, or the Special Economic Zone.  
Actions against multiple defendants shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court or the special people's court that has jurisdiction over the place of domicile of the issuer; actions against entities other than the issuer shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court or the special people's court that has jurisdiction over the place of domicile of the defendant.  
Special representative action cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court or the special people's court of the place where the stock exchange where the securities involved in the litigation are centrally traded, or other national securities trading venues approved by the State Council, is located.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应当充分发挥多元解纷机制的功能，按照自愿、合法原则，引导和鼓励当事人通过行政调解、行业调解、专业调解等非诉讼方式解决证券纠纷。  
当事人选择通过诉讼方式解决纠纷的，人民法院应当及时立案。案件审理过程中应当着重调解。

**Article 3** The People's Court shall give full play to the functions of the diversified dispute resolution mechanism, guide and encourage parties to resolve securities disputes through non-litigation methods such as administrative mediation, industry mediation, and professional mediation in accordance with the principles of voluntariness and legality.  
Where a party chooses to resolve the dispute through litigation, the People's Court shall promptly docket the case. During the trial of the case, mediation shall be emphasized.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院审理证券纠纷代表人诉讼案件，应当依托信息化技术手段开展立案登记、诉讼文书送达、公告和通知、权利登记、执行款项发放等工作，便利当事人行使诉讼权利、履行诉讼义务，提高审判执行的公正性、高效性和透明度。  
二、普通代表人诉讼

**Article 4** When hearing cases of representative actions for securities disputes, the People's Court shall rely on information technology means to carry out work such as case registration, service of litigation documents, announcements and notices, registration of rights, and distribution of enforcement funds, so as to facilitate parties in exercising litigation rights and performing litigation obligations, and to improve the fairness, efficiency, and transparency of adjudication and enforcement.  
II. Ordinary Representative Actions

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　符合以下条件的，人民法院应当适用普通代表人诉讼程序进行审理：  
（一）原告一方人数十人以上，起诉符合[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定和共同诉讼条件；  
（二）起诉书中确定二至五名拟任代表人且符合[本规定第十二条](#art-12)规定的代表人条件；  
（三）原告提交有关行政处罚决定、刑事裁判文书、被告自认材料、证券交易所和国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所等给予的纪律处分或者采取的自律管理措施等证明证券侵权事实的初步证据。  
不符合前款规定的，人民法院应当适用非代表人诉讼程序进行审理。

**Article 5** Where the following conditions are met, the People's Court shall apply the ordinary representative action procedure for trial:  
(1) The number of plaintiffs is ten or more, and the prosecution meets the requirements of Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the conditions for joint action;  
(2) The complaint designates two to five proposed representatives who meet the representative conditions specified in Article 12 of these Provisions;  
(3) The plaintiffs submit preliminary evidence proving the facts of securities infringement, such as relevant administrative penalty decisions, criminal judgments, materials on the defendant's admission, disciplinary sanctions imposed or self-regulatory measures taken by the stock exchange or other national securities trading venues approved by the State Council.  
Where the provisions of the preceding paragraph are not met, the People's Court shall apply the non-representative action procedure for trial.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对起诉时当事人人数尚未确定的代表人诉讼，在发出权利登记公告前，人民法院可以通过阅卷、调查、询问和听证等方式对被诉证券侵权行为的性质、侵权事实等进行审查，并在受理后三十日内以裁定的方式确定具有相同诉讼请求的权利人范围。  
当事人对权利人范围有异议的，可以自裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，上一级人民法院应当在十五日内作出复议裁定。

**Article 6** For a representative action where the number of parties is not yet determined at the time of prosecution, before issuing the announcement of rights registration, the People's Court may review the nature and facts of the alleged securities infringement through methods such as case file review, investigation, inquiry, and hearing, and shall determine the scope of rights holders with the same claims by ruling within thirty days after acceptance.  
Where a party has an objection to the scope of rights holders, it may apply for reconsideration to the People's Court at the next higher level within ten days from the date of service of the ruling, and the People's Court at the next higher level shall make a reconsideration ruling within fifteen days.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院应当在权利人范围确定后五日内发出权利登记公告，通知相关权利人在指定期间登记。权利登记公告应当包括以下内容：  
（一）案件情况和诉讼请求；  
（二）被告的基本情况；  
（三）权利人范围及登记期间；  
（四）起诉书中确定的拟任代表人人选姓名或者名称、联系方式等基本信息；  
（五）自愿担任代表人的权利人，向人民法院提交书面申请和相关材料的期限；  
（六）人民法院认为必要的其他事项。  
公告应当以醒目的方式提示，代表人的诉讼权限包括代表原告参加开庭审理，变更、放弃诉讼请求或者承认对方当事人的诉讼请求，与被告达成调解协议，提起或者放弃上诉，申请执行，委托诉讼代理人等，参加登记视为对代表人进行特别授权。  
公告期间为三十日。

**Article 7** The People's Court shall issue an announcement of rights registration within five days after the scope of rights holders is determined, notifying the relevant rights holders to register within the specified period. The announcement of rights registration shall include the following:  
(1) The circumstances of the case and the claims;  
(2) Basic information of the defendants;  
(3) The scope of rights holders and the registration period;  
(4) Basic information such as the name or title and contact information of the proposed representative candidates determined in the complaint;  
(5) The time limit for a rights holder who voluntarily serves as a representative to submit a written application and relevant materials to the People's Court;  
(6) Other matters deemed necessary by the People's Court.  
The announcement shall prominently indicate that the litigation authority of the representative includes representing the plaintiffs to participate in court hearings, changing or abandoning claims or admitting the claims of the opposing party, reaching a mediation agreement with the defendant, filing or abandoning an appeal, applying for enforcement, appointing a litigation agent, etc., and that registration shall be deemed as granting special authorization to the representative.  
The announcement period shall be thirty days.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　权利人应在公告确定的登记期间向人民法院登记。未按期登记的，可在一审开庭前向人民法院申请补充登记，补充登记前已经完成的诉讼程序对其发生效力。权利登记可以依托电子信息平台进行。  
权利人进行登记时，应当按照权利登记公告要求填写诉讼请求金额、收款方式、电子送达地址等事项，并提供身份证明文件、交易记录及投资损失等证据材料。

**Article 8** A rights holder shall register with the People's Court within the registration period determined in the announcement. If registration is not made within the time limit, the rights holder may apply to the People's Court for supplementary registration before the opening of the first-instance trial, and the litigation procedures completed before the supplementary registration shall take effect with respect to such rights holder. Rights registration may be conducted through an electronic information platform.  
When registering, a rights holder shall, in accordance with the requirements of the rights registration announcement, fill in matters such as the amount of claims, the method of receiving payments, and the address for electronic service, and provide evidence materials such as identity documents, transaction records, and investment losses.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院在登记期间届满后十日内对登记的权利人进行审核。不符合权利人范围的投资者，人民法院不确认其原告资格。

**Article 9** Within ten days after the expiration of the registration period, the People's Court shall examine the registered rights holders. For investors who do not fall within the scope of rights holders, the People's Court shall not confirm their plaintiff status.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　权利登记公告前已就同一证券违法事实提起诉讼且符合权利人范围的投资者，申请撤诉并加入代表人诉讼的，人民法院应当予以准许。  
投资者申请撤诉并加入代表人诉讼的，列为代表人诉讼的原告，已经收取的诉讼费予以退还；不申请撤诉的，人民法院不准许其加入代表人诉讼，原诉讼继续进行。

**Article 10** Where an investor who has already filed a lawsuit regarding the same illegal securities facts before the announcement of rights registration and falls within the scope of rights holders applies to withdraw the lawsuit and join the representative action, the People's Court shall grant permission.  
Where an investor applies to withdraw the lawsuit and join the representative action, such investor shall be listed as a plaintiff in the representative action, and the litigation fees already collected shall be refunded; where the investor does not apply to withdraw the lawsuit, the People's Court shall not permit the investor to join the representative action, and the original lawsuit shall proceed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院应当将审核通过的权利人列入代表人诉讼原告名单，并通知全体原告。

**Article 11** The People's Court shall list the rights holders who have passed the examination into the list of plaintiffs in the representative action and shall notify all plaintiffs.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　代表人应当符合以下条件：  
（一）自愿担任代表人；  
（二）拥有相当比例的利益诉求份额；  
（三）本人或者其委托诉讼代理人具备一定的诉讼能力和专业经验；  
（四）能忠实、勤勉地履行维护全体原告利益的职责。  
依照法律、行政法规或者国务院证券监督管理机构的规定设立的投资者保护机构作为原告参与诉讼，或者接受投资者的委托指派工作人员或委派诉讼代理人参与案件审理活动的，人民法院可以指定该机构为代表人，或者在被代理的当事人中指定代表人。  
申请担任代表人的原告存在与被告有关联关系等可能影响其履行职责情形的，人民法院对其申请不予准许。

**Article 12** A representative shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) Voluntarily serves as a representative;  
(2) Holds a proportionate share of the interests claimed;  
(3) The representative or the litigation agent appointed by the representative possesses certain litigation capability and professional experience;  
(4) Can faithfully and diligently perform the duty of safeguarding the interests of all plaintiffs.  
Where an investor protection institution established in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, or regulations of the securities regulatory authority of the State Council participates in the lawsuit as a plaintiff, or, upon acceptance of an investor's appointment, assigns staff or appoints a litigation agent to participate in the case hearing, the People's Court may designate such institution as the representative, or designate a representative from among the represented parties.  
Where a plaintiff applying to serve as a representative has a relationship with the defendant, such as being affiliated, that may affect the performance of duties, the People's Court shall not permit such application.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在起诉时当事人人数确定的代表人诉讼，应当在起诉前确定获得特别授权的代表人，并在起诉书中就代表人的推选情况作出专项说明。  
在起诉时当事人人数尚未确定的代表人诉讼，应当在起诉书中就拟任代表人人选及条件作出说明。在登记期间向人民法院登记的权利人对拟任代表人人选均没有提出异议，并且登记的权利人无人申请担任代表人的，人民法院可以认定由该二至五名人选作为代表人。

**Article 13** For a representative action where the number of parties is determined at the time of prosecution, the representative who has obtained special authorization shall be determined before prosecution, and a special explanation regarding the selection of the representative shall be made in the complaint.  
For a representative action where the number of parties is not determined at the time of prosecution, an explanation regarding the proposed representative candidates and their conditions shall be made in the complaint. Where none of the rights holders registered with the People's Court during the registration period raises an objection to the proposed representative candidates, and none of the registered rights holders applies to serve as a representative, the People's Court may deem the two to five candidates as the representatives.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在登记期间向人民法院登记的权利人对拟任代表人的人选提出异议，或者申请担任代表人的，人民法院应当自原告范围审核完毕后十日内在自愿担任代表人的原告中组织推选。  
代表人的推选实行一人一票，每位代表人的得票数应当不少于参与投票人数的50%。代表人人数为二至五名，按得票数排名确定，通过投票产生二名以上代表人的，为推选成功。首次推选不出的，人民法院应当即时组织原告在得票数前五名的候选人中进行二次推选。

**Article 14** Where a rights holder registered with the People's Court during the registration period raises an objection to the proposed representative candidates, or applies to serve as a representative, the People's Court shall, within ten days after the examination of the scope of plaintiffs is completed, organize a selection among the plaintiffs who voluntarily serve as representatives.  
The selection of representatives shall be conducted on the basis of one person, one vote, and each representative shall receive no less than 50% of the votes of the participating voters. The number of representatives shall be two to five, determined by ranking based on the number of votes received; the selection is successful if two or more representatives are produced through voting. If the first selection fails, the People's Court shall promptly organize a second selection among the top five candidates in terms of votes received.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　依据前条规定推选不出代表人的，由人民法院指定。  
人民法院指定代表人的，应当将投票情况、诉讼能力、利益诉求份额等作为考量因素，并征得被指定代表人的同意。

**Article 15** Where no representative can be selected in accordance with the preceding article, the People's Court shall designate one.  
Where the People's Court designates a representative, it shall take into consideration factors such as the voting results, litigation capability, and the share of interests claimed, and shall obtain the consent of the designated representative.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　代表人确定后，人民法院应当进行公告。  
原告可以自公告之日起十日内向人民法院申请撤回权利登记，并可以另行起诉。

**Article 16** After the representative is determined, the People's Court shall make an announcement.  
A plaintiff may, within ten days from the date of the announcement, apply to the People's Court to withdraw the rights registration and may file a separate action.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　代表人因丧失诉讼行为能力或者其他事由影响案件审理或者可能损害原告利益的，人民法院依原告申请，可以决定撤销代表人资格。代表人不足二人时，人民法院应当补充指定代表人。

**Article 17** Where a representative loses the capacity to conduct litigation or other reasons affect the trial of the case or may harm the interests of the plaintiffs, the People's Court may, upon the application of a plaintiff, decide to revoke the qualification of the representative. Where there are fewer than two representatives, the People's Court shall supplementarily designate a representative.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　代表人与被告达成调解协议草案的，应当向人民法院提交制作调解书的申请书及调解协议草案。申请书应当包括以下内容：  
（一）原告的诉讼请求、案件事实以及审理进展等基本情况；  
（二）代表人和委托诉讼代理人参加诉讼活动的情况；  
（三）调解协议草案对原告的有利因素和不利影响；  
（四）对诉讼费以及合理的公告费、通知费、律师费等费用的分摊及理由；  
（五）需要特别说明的其他事项。

**Article 18** Where the representative reaches a draft mediation agreement with the defendant, it shall submit to the People's Court an application for the preparation of a mediation agreement and the draft mediation agreement. The application shall include the following:  
(1) Basic information such as the plaintiffs' claims, case facts, and progress of the trial;  
(2) The participation of the representative and the appointed litigation agent in the litigation activities;  
(3) The favorable factors and adverse effects of the draft mediation agreement on the plaintiffs;  
(4) The allocation of litigation fees and reasonable expenses for announcements, notifications, attorneys' fees, etc., and the reasons therefor;  
(5) Other matters requiring special explanation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院经初步审查，认为调解协议草案不存在违反法律、行政法规的强制性规定、违背公序良俗以及损害他人合法权益等情形的，应当自收到申请书后十日内向全体原告发出通知。通知应当包括以下内容：  
（一）调解协议草案；  
（二）代表人请求人民法院制作调解书的申请书；  
（三）对调解协议草案发表意见的权利以及方式、程序和期限；  
（四）原告有异议时，召开听证会的时间、地点及报名方式；  
（五）人民法院认为需要通知的其他事项。

**Article 19** After preliminary examination, if the People's Court finds that the draft mediation agreement does not violate mandatory provisions of laws or administrative regulations, contravene public order and good customs, or harm the lawful rights and interests of others, it shall, within ten days from the date of receipt of the application, issue a notice to all plaintiffs. The notice shall include the following:  
(1) The draft mediation agreement;  
(2) The application of the representative requesting the People's Court to prepare a mediation agreement;  
(3) The right to express opinions on the draft mediation agreement, as well as the methods, procedures, and time limit therefor;  
(4) When a plaintiff has an objection, the time and place of the hearing and the method of registration;  
(5) Other matters that the People's Court deems necessary to notify.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对调解协议草案有异议的原告，有权出席听证会或者以书面方式向人民法院提交异议的具体内容及理由。异议人未出席听证会的，人民法院应当在听证会上公开其异议的内容及理由，代表人及其委托诉讼代理人、被告应当进行解释。  
代表人和被告可以根据听证会的情况，对调解协议草案进行修改。人民法院应当将修改后的调解协议草案通知所有原告，并对修改的内容作出重点提示。人民法院可以根据案件的具体情况，决定是否再次召开听证会。

**Article 20** A plaintiff who has an objection to the draft mediation agreement has the right to attend the hearing or submit to the People's Court the specific content and reasons for the objection in writing. Where the objector does not attend the hearing, the People's Court shall disclose the content and reasons of the objection at the hearing, and the representative and its appointed litigation agent and the defendant shall provide explanations.  
The representative and the defendant may modify the draft mediation agreement based on the circumstances of the hearing. The People's Court shall notify all plaintiffs of the modified draft mediation agreement and shall highlight the modifications. The People's Court may decide whether to convene another hearing based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院应当综合考虑当事人赞成和反对意见、本案所涉法律和事实情况、调解协议草案的合法性、适当性和可行性等因素，决定是否制作调解书。  
人民法院准备制作调解书的，应当通知提出异议的原告，告知其可以在收到通知后十日内向人民法院提交退出调解的申请。未在上述期间内提交退出申请的原告，视为接受。  
申请退出的期间届满后，人民法院应当在十日内制作调解书。调解书经代表人和被告签收后，对被代表的原告发生效力。人民法院对申请退出原告的诉讼继续审理，并依法作出相应判决。

**Article 21** The People's Court shall comprehensively consider factors such as the parties' approval and opposition opinions, the legal and factual circumstances involved in the case, and the legality, appropriateness, and feasibility of the draft mediation agreement, and decide whether to prepare a mediation agreement.  
Where the People's Court intends to prepare a mediation agreement, it shall notify the plaintiff who raised the objection, informing that such plaintiff may submit an application to withdraw from the mediation to the People's Court within ten days after receipt of the notice. A plaintiff who fails to submit an application for withdrawal within the above period shall be deemed to have accepted the mediation.  
After the expiration of the period for applying for withdrawal, the People's Court shall prepare the mediation agreement within ten days. The mediation agreement shall take effect with respect to the represented plaintiffs after being signed and received by the representative and the defendant. The People's Court shall continue to hear the actions of the plaintiffs who have applied for withdrawal and shall render corresponding judgments in accordance with law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　代表人变更或者放弃诉讼请求、承认对方当事人诉讼请求、决定撤诉的，应当向人民法院提交书面申请，并通知全体原告。人民法院收到申请后，应当根据原告所提异议情况，依法裁定是否准许。  
对于代表人依据前款规定提交的书面申请，原告自收到通知之日起十日内未提出异议的，人民法院可以裁定准许。

**Article 22** Where the representative changes or abandons claims, admits the claims of the opposing party, or decides to withdraw the lawsuit, it shall submit a written application to the People's Court and notify all plaintiffs. After receiving the application, the People's Court shall, based on any objections raised by the plaintiffs, rule on whether to grant permission in accordance with law.  
For a written application submitted by the representative in accordance with the preceding paragraph, if no plaintiff raises an objection within ten days from the date of receipt of the notice, the People's Court may rule to grant permission.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　除代表人诉讼案件外，人民法院还受理其他基于同一证券违法事实发生的非代表人诉讼案件的，原则上代表人诉讼案件先行审理，非代表人诉讼案件中止审理。但非代表人诉讼案件具有典型性且先行审理有利于及时解决纠纷的除外。

**Article 23** Where, in addition to the representative action case, the People's Court also accepts other non-representative action cases arising from the same illegal securities facts, in principle, the representative action case shall be tried first, and the non-representative action cases shall be suspended. However, this shall not apply where the non-representative action cases are typical and trying them first is conducive to the timely resolution of the disputes.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民法院可以依当事人的申请，委托双方认可或者随机抽取的专业机构对投资损失数额、证券侵权行为以外其他风险因素导致的损失扣除比例等进行核定。当事人虽未申请但案件审理确有需要的，人民法院可以通过随机抽取的方式委托专业机构对有关事项进行核定。  
对专业机构的核定意见，人民法院应当组织双方当事人质证。

**Article 24** The People's Court may, upon the application of a party, entrust a professional institution agreed upon by both parties or selected by random draw to verify the amount of investment losses, the proportion of losses deducted due to risk factors other than the securities infringement, etc. Where the parties have not applied but the trial of the case so requires, the People's Court may entrust a professional institution by random draw to verify the relevant matters.  
The People's Court shall organize both parties to cross-examine the verification opinions of the professional institution.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　代表人请求败诉的被告赔偿合理的公告费、通知费、律师费等费用的，人民法院应当予以支持。

**Article 25** Where the representative requests the defendant who loses the lawsuit to compensate for reasonable expenses such as announcement fees, notification fees, and attorneys' fees, the People's Court shall support such request.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　判决被告承担民事赔偿责任的，可以在判决主文中确定赔偿总额和损害赔偿计算方法，并将每个原告的姓名、应获赔偿金额等以列表方式作为民事判决书的附件。  
当事人对计算方法、赔偿金额等有异议的，可以向人民法院申请复核。确有错误的，人民法院裁定补正。

**Article 26** Where the judgment requires the defendant to bear civil compensation liability, the total amount of compensation and the method of calculation of damages may be determined in the main text of the judgment, and the name of each plaintiff and the amount of compensation to which each is entitled shall be listed as an appendix to the civil judgment.  
Where a party has an objection to the calculation method, compensation amount, etc., it may apply to the People's Court for review. If an error is indeed found, the People's Court shall make a ruling to correct it.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　一审判决送达后，代表人决定放弃上诉的，应当在上诉期间届满前通知全体原告。  
原告自收到通知之日起十五日内未上诉，被告在上诉期间内亦未上诉的，一审判决在全体原告与被告之间生效。  
原告自收到通知之日起十五日内上诉的，应当同时提交上诉状，人民法院收到上诉状后，对上诉的原告按上诉处理。被告在上诉期间内未上诉的，一审判决在未上诉的原告与被告之间生效，二审裁判的效力不及于未上诉的原告。

**Article 27** After the first-instance judgment is served, if the representative decides to waive the right to appeal, it shall notify all plaintiffs before the expiration of the appeal period.  
Where no plaintiff appeals within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice, and the defendant also does not appeal within the appeal period, the first-instance judgment shall take effect between all plaintiffs and the defendant.  
Where a plaintiff files an appeal within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice, such plaintiff shall also submit the petition for appeal at the same time; after receiving the petition for appeal, the People's Court shall handle the appealing plaintiff as an appellant. Where the defendant does not appeal within the appeal period, the first-instance judgment shall take effect between the plaintiffs who did not appeal and the defendant, and the effect of the second-instance judgment shall not extend to the plaintiffs who did not appeal.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　一审判决送达后，代表人决定上诉的，应当在上诉期间届满前通知全体原告。  
原告自收到通知之日起十五日内决定放弃上诉的，应当通知一审法院。被告在上诉期间内未上诉的，一审判决在放弃上诉的原告与被告之间生效，二审裁判的效力不及于放弃上诉的原告。

**Article 28** After the first-instance judgment is served, if the representative decides to appeal, it shall notify all plaintiffs before the expiration of the appeal period.  
Where a plaintiff decides to waive the right to appeal within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the notice, such plaintiff shall notify the first-instance court. Where the defendant does not appeal within the appeal period, the first-instance judgment shall take effect between the plaintiff who waived the appeal and the defendant, and the effect of the second-instance judgment shall not extend to the plaintiff who waived the appeal.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　符合权利人范围但未参加登记的投资者提起诉讼，且主张的事实和理由与代表人诉讼生效判决、裁定所认定的案件基本事实和法律适用相同的，人民法院审查具体诉讼请求后，裁定适用已经生效的判决、裁定。适用已经生效裁判的裁定中应当明确被告赔偿的金额，裁定一经作出立即生效。  
代表人诉讼调解结案的，人民法院对后续涉及同一证券违法事实的案件可以引导当事人先行调解。

**Article 29** Where an investor who falls within the scope of rights holders but has not participated in the registration files a lawsuit, and the facts and reasons asserted are the same as the basic facts of the case and the application of law as determined in the effective judgment or ruling of the representative action, the People's Court shall, after examining the specific claims, rule to apply the effective judgment or ruling. The ruling applying the effective judgment or ruling shall specify the amount of compensation payable by the defendant, and the ruling shall take effect immediately upon being made.  
Where the representative action is concluded by mediation, the People's Court may, for subsequent cases involving the same illegal securities facts, guide the parties to conduct mediation first.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　履行或者执行生效法律文书所得财产，人民法院在进行分配时，可以通知证券登记结算机构等协助执行义务人依法协助执行。  
人民法院应当编制分配方案并通知全体原告，分配方案应当包括原告范围、债权总额、扣除项目及金额、分配的基准及方法、分配金额的受领期间等内容。

**Article 30** Where the People's Court distributes the property obtained from the performance or enforcement of effective legal instruments, it may notify the securities registration and settlement institution and other entities obligated to assist in enforcement to provide assistance in accordance with law.  
The People's Court shall prepare a distribution plan and notify all plaintiffs. The distribution plan shall include the scope of plaintiffs, the total amount of claims, items and amounts to be deducted, the basis and method of distribution, and the period for receiving the distributed amounts.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　原告对分配方案有异议的，可以依据[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)的规定提出执行异议。  
三、特别代表人诉讼

**Article 31** Where a plaintiff has an objection to the distribution plan, it may raise an enforcement objection in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
III. Special Representative Actions

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院已经根据[民事诉讼法第五十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-54)第一款、[证券法第九十五条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-95)第二款的规定发布权利登记公告的，投资者保护机构在公告期间受五十名以上权利人的特别授权，可以作为代表人参加诉讼。先受理的人民法院不具有特别代表人诉讼管辖权的，应当将案件及时移送有管辖权的人民法院。  
不同意加入特别代表人诉讼的权利人可以提交退出声明，原诉讼继续进行。

**Article 32** Where the People's Court has issued an announcement of rights registration in accordance with Article 54, paragraph 1 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 95, paragraph 2 of the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China, an investor protection institution that obtains special authorization from fifty or more rights holders during the announcement period may participate in the lawsuit as a representative. Where the People's Court that first accepted the case does not have jurisdiction over the special representative action, it shall promptly transfer the case to the People's Court that has jurisdiction.  
A rights holder who disagrees with joining the special representative action may submit a declaration of withdrawal, and the original lawsuit shall proceed.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　权利人范围确定后，人民法院应当发出权利登记公告。权利登记公告除[本规定第七条](#art-7)的内容外，还应当包括投资者保护机构基本情况、对投资者保护机构的特别授权、投资者声明退出的权利及期间、未声明退出的法律后果等。

**Article 33** After the scope of rights holders is determined, the People's Court shall issue an announcement of rights registration. In addition to the content specified in Article 7 of these Provisions, the announcement of rights registration shall also include basic information of the investor protection institution, the special authorization for the investor protection institution, the right of investors to declare withdrawal and the period therefor, and the legal consequences of not declaring withdrawal.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　投资者明确表示不愿意参加诉讼的，应当在公告期间届满后十五日内向人民法院声明退出。未声明退出的，视为同意参加该代表人诉讼。  
对于声明退出的投资者，人民法院不再将其登记为特别代表人诉讼的原告，该投资者可以另行起诉。

**Article 34** An investor who explicitly indicates unwillingness to participate in the lawsuit shall declare withdrawal to the People's Court within fifteen days after the expiration of the announcement period. An investor who fails to declare withdrawal shall be deemed to have agreed to participate in the representative action.  
For an investor who declares withdrawal, the People's Court shall no longer register that investor as a plaintiff in the special representative action, and such investor may file a separate action.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　投资者保护机构依据公告确定的权利人范围向证券登记结算机构调取的权利人名单，人民法院应当予以登记，列入代表人诉讼原告名单，并通知全体原告。

**Article 35** With respect to the list of rights holders obtained by the investor protection institution from the securities registration and settlement institution in accordance with the scope of rights holders determined in the announcement, the People's Court shall register it, include it in the list of plaintiffs in the representative action, and notify all plaintiffs.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　诉讼过程中由于声明退出等原因导致明示授权投资者的数量不足五十名的，不影响投资者保护机构的代表人资格。

**Article 36** Where, during the litigation process, the number of investors who have expressly authorized falls below fifty due to reasons such as declarations of withdrawal, this shall not affect the qualification of the investor protection institution as a representative.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　针对同一代表人诉讼，原则上应当由一个投资者保护机构作为代表人参加诉讼。两个以上的投资者保护机构分别受五十名以上投资者委托，且均决定作为代表人参加诉讼的，应当协商处理；协商不成的，由人民法院指定其中一个作为代表人参加诉讼。

**Article 37** For the same representative action, in principle, one investor protection institution shall participate in the lawsuit as a representative. Where two or more investor protection institutions are separately authorized by more than fifty investors and both decide to participate as representatives, they shall resolve the matter through consultation; if consultation fails, the People's Court shall designate one of them as the representative to participate in the lawsuit.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　投资者保护机构应当采取必要措施，保障被代表的投资者持续了解案件审理的进展情况，回应投资者的诉求。对投资者提出的意见和建议不予采纳的，应当对投资者做好解释工作。

**Article 38** The investor protection institution shall take necessary measures to ensure that the represented investors are continuously informed of the progress of the case and respond to the investors' demands. Where opinions and suggestions raised by investors are not adopted, the institution shall provide adequate explanations to the investors.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　特别代表人诉讼案件不预交案件受理费。败诉或者部分败诉的原告申请减交或者免交诉讼费的，人民法院应当依照《诉讼费用交纳办法》的规定，视原告的经济状况和案件的审理情况决定是否准许。

**Article 39** For a special representative action case, the case acceptance fee shall not be prepaid. Where a plaintiff who loses the lawsuit in whole or in part applies for a reduction or exemption of litigation fees, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Payment of Litigation Fees, decide whether to grant the reduction or exemption based on the economic situation of the plaintiff and the circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　投资者保护机构作为代表人在诉讼中申请财产保全的，人民法院可以不要求提供担保。

**Article 40** Where an investor protection institution, as a representative, applies for property preservation in the lawsuit, the People's Court may not require the provision of security.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　人民法院审理特别代表人诉讼案件，本部分没有规定的，适用普通代表人诉讼中关于起诉时当事人人数尚未确定的代表人诉讼的相关规定。  
四、附则

**Article 41** Where this Part is silent on the trial of special representative action cases by the People's Court, the relevant provisions on representative actions where the number of parties is not determined at the time of prosecution in ordinary representative actions shall apply.  
IV. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　本规定自2020年7月31日起施行。

**Article 42** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 31, 2020.
