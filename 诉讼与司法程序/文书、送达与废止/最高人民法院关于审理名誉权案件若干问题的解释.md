# 最高人民法院关于审理名誉权案件若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕26号  
**公布日期**：1998-07-14  
**生效日期**：1998-09-15

> （1998年7月14日最高人民法院审判委员会第 1002次会议通过，自1998年9月15日起施行。） 1993年我院印发《关于审理名誉权案件若干问题的解答》以来，各地人民法院在审理名誉权案件中，又提出一些如何适用法律的问题，现解释如下： 一、问：名誉权案件如何确定侵权结果发生地？ 答：人民法院受理这类案件时，受侵权的公民、法人和其他组织的住所地，可以认定为侵权结果发生地。 二、问：有关机关和组织编印的仅供领导部门内部参阅的刊物、资料等刊登来信或者文章引起的名誉权纠纷，以及机关、社会团体、学术机构、企事业单位分发本单位、本系统或者其他一定范围内的一般内部刊物和内部资料所载内容引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？ 答：有关机关和组织编印的仅供领导部门内部参阅的刊物、资料等刊登的来信或者文章，当事人以其内容侵害名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。机关、社会团体、学术机构、企事业单位分发本单位、本系统或者其他一定范围内的内部刊物和内部资料，所载内容引起名誉权纠纷的，人民法院应当受理。 三、问：新闻媒介和出版机构转载作品引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？ 答：新闻媒介和出版机构转载作品，当事人以转载者侵害其名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。 四、问：国家机关、社会团体、企事业单位等部门依职权对其管理的人员作出的结论引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？ 答：国家机关、社会团体、企事业单位等部门对其管理的人员作出的结论或者处理决定，当事人以其侵害名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。 五、问：因检举、控告引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？ 答：公民依法向有关部门检举、控告他人的违法违纪行为，他人以检举、控告侵害其名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，如果借检举、控告之名侮辱、诽谤他人，造成他人名誉损害，当事人以其名誉权受到侵害向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。 六、问：新闻单位报道国家机关的公开的文书和职权行为引起的名誉权纠纷，是否认定为构成侵权？ 答：新闻单位根据国家机关依职权制作的公开的文书和实施的公开的职权行为所作的报道，其报道客观准确的，不应当认定为侵害他人名誉权；其报道失实，或者前述文书和职权行为已公开纠正而拒绝更正报道，致使他人名誉受到损害的，应当认定为侵害他人名誉权。 七、问：因提供新闻材料引起的名誉权纠纷，如何认定是否构成侵权？

---

### 正文

一、问：名誉权案件如何确定侵权结果发生地？  
答：人民法院受理这类案件时，受侵权的公民、法人和其他组织的住所地，可以认定为侵权结果发生地。

**Article None** 1. Q: How shall the place where the infringing result occurs be determined in cases concerning the right to reputation?  
A: When the People's Court accepts such cases, the domicile of the infringed citizen, legal person, or other organization may be deemed as the place where the infringing result occurs.

二、问：有关机关和组织编印的仅供领导部门内部参阅的刊物、资料等刊登来信或者文章引起的名誉权纠纷，以及机关、社会团体、学术机构、企事业单位分发本单位、本系统或者其他一定范围内的一般内部刊物和内部资料所载内容引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？  
答：有关机关和组织编印的仅供领导部门内部参阅的刊物、资料等刊登的来信或者文章，当事人以其内容侵害名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。机关、社会团体、学术机构、企事业单位分发本单位、本系统或者其他一定范围内的内部刊物和内部资料，所载内容引起名誉权纠纷的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article None** 2. Q: Shall the People's Court accept disputes over the right to reputation arising from letters or articles published in publications or materials compiled by relevant authorities or organizations for internal reference of leadership departments only, as well as disputes over the right to reputation arising from contents carried in general internal publications and materials distributed by authorities, social organizations, academic institutions, enterprises and public institutions within their own units, systems, or other certain scopes?  
A: If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that letters or articles published in publications or materials compiled by relevant authorities or organizations for internal reference of leadership departments only infringe upon the right to reputation, the People's Court shall not accept the case. If a dispute over the right to reputation arises from contents carried in internal publications and materials distributed by authorities, social organizations, academic institutions, enterprises and public institutions within their own units, systems, or other certain scopes, the People's Court shall accept the case.

三、问：新闻媒介和出版机构转载作品引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？  
答：新闻媒介和出版机构转载作品，当事人以转载者侵害其名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article None** 3. Q: Shall the People's Court accept disputes over the right to reputation arising from the republication of works by news media and publishing institutions?  
A: If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that the republisher infringes upon its right to reputation when news media and publishing institutions republish a work, the People's Court shall accept the case.

四、问：国家机关、社会团体、企事业单位等部门依职权对其管理的人员作出的结论引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？  
答：国家机关、社会团体、企事业单位等部门对其管理的人员作出的结论或者处理决定，当事人以其侵害名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article None** 4. Q: Shall the People's Court accept disputes over the right to reputation arising from conclusions made by state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other departments regarding personnel under their management in the exercise of their powers?  
A: If a party files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that the conclusions or decisions made by state organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, and other departments regarding personnel under their management infringe upon the right to reputation, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

五、问：因检举、控告引起的名誉权纠纷，人民法院是否受理？  
答：公民依法向有关部门检举、控告他人的违法违纪行为，他人以检举、控告侵害其名誉权向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院不予受理，如果借检举、控告之名侮辱、诽谤他人，造成他人名誉损害，当事人以其名誉权受到侵害向人民法院提起诉讼的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article None** 5. Q: Shall the People's Court accept disputes over the right to reputation arising from reports or accusations?  
A: If a citizen reports or accuses another person of illegal or disciplinary violations to relevant departments according to law, and the other person files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that the report or accusation infringes upon its right to reputation, the People's Court shall not accept the case. However, if a person, under the guise of reporting or accusing, insults or defames another person and causes damage to the other person's reputation, and the party files a lawsuit with the People's Court on the ground that its right to reputation has been infringed, the People's Court shall accept the case.

六、问：新闻单位报道国家机关的公开的文书和职权行为引起的名誉权纠纷，是否认定为构成侵权？  
答：新闻单位根据国家机关依职权制作的公开的文书和实施的公开的职权行为所作的报道，其报道客观准确的，不应当认定为侵害他人名誉权；其报道失实，或者前述文书和职权行为已公开纠正而拒绝更正报道，致使他人名誉受到损害的，应当认定为侵害他人名誉权。

**Article None** 6. Q: Shall disputes over the right to reputation arising from news organizations reporting on public documents and official acts of state organs be deemed as constituting infringement?  
A: If a news organization reports on public documents made by state organs in the exercise of their powers and public official acts implemented thereby, and the report is objective and accurate, it shall not be deemed as infringing upon another person's right to reputation. If the report is inaccurate, or if the aforementioned documents or official acts have been publicly corrected but the news organization refuses to issue a corrective report, thereby causing damage to another person's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon another person's right to reputation.

七、问：因提供新闻材料引起的名誉权纠纷，如何认定是否构成侵权？  
答：因提供新闻材料引起的名誉权纠纷，认定是否构成侵权，应区分以下两种情况：  
（一）主动提供新闻材料，致使他人名誉受到损害的，应当认定为侵害他人名誉权。  
（二）因被动接受采访而提供新闻材料，且未经提供者同意公开，新闻单位擅自发表，致使他人名誉受到损害的，对提供者一般不应当认定为侵害名誉权；虽系被动提供新闻材料，但发表时得到提供者同意或者默许，致使他人名誉受到损害的，应当认定为侵害名誉权。

**Article None** 7. Q: How shall it be determined whether infringement is constituted in disputes over the right to reputation arising from the provision of news materials?  
A: For disputes over the right to reputation arising from the provision of news materials, the determination of whether infringement is constituted shall distinguish between the following two circumstances:  
(1) If a person voluntarily provides news materials, thereby causing damage to another person's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon another person's right to reputation.  
(2) If a person provides news materials due to passively accepting an interview, and the news organization publishes them without the consent of the provider, thereby causing damage to another person's reputation, the provider shall generally not be deemed as infringing upon the right to reputation; however, if the news materials are passively provided but are published with the consent or tacit approval of the provider, thereby causing damage to another person's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon the right to reputation.

八、问：因医疗卫生单位公开患者患有淋病、梅毒、麻风病、爱滋病等病情引起的名誉权纠纷，如何认定是否构成侵权？  
答：医疗卫生单位的工作人员擅自公开患者患有淋病、梅毒、麻风病、爱滋病等病情，致使患者名誉受到损害的，应当认定为侵害患者名誉权。医疗卫生单位向患者或其家属通报病情，不应当认定为侵害患者名誉权。

**Article None** 8. Q: How shall it be determined whether infringement is constituted in disputes over the right to reputation arising from medical and health units disclosing that a patient suffers from gonorrhea, syphilis, leprosy, AIDS, or other diseases?  
A: If a staff member of a medical and health unit discloses without authorization that a patient suffers from gonorrhea, syphilis, leprosy, AIDS, or other diseases, thereby causing damage to the patient's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon the patient's right to reputation. If a medical and health unit informs the patient or his or her family members of the condition, it shall not be deemed as infringing upon the patient's right to reputation.

九、问：对产品质量、服务质量进行批评、评论引起的名誉权纠纷，如何认定是否构成侵权？  
答：消费者对生产者、经营者、销售者的产品质量或者服务质量进行批评、评论，不应当认定为侵害他人名誉权。但借机诽谤、诋毁，损害其名誉的，应当认定为侵害名誉权。新闻单位对生产者、经营者、销售者的产品质量或者服务质量进行批评、评论，内容基本属实，没有侮辱内容的，不应当认定为侵害其名誉权；主要内容失实，损害其名誉的，应当认定为侵害名誉权。

**Article None** 9. Q: How shall it be determined whether infringement is constituted in disputes over the right to reputation arising from criticism or comments on product quality or service quality?  
A: If a consumer criticizes or comments on the product quality or service quality of a producer, operator, or seller, it shall not be deemed as infringing upon another person's right to reputation. However, if the consumer takes the opportunity to slander or defame, thereby damaging the other person's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon the right to reputation. If a news organization criticizes or comments on the product quality or service quality of a producer, operator, or seller, and the content is basically true and contains no insulting material, it shall not be deemed as infringing upon the right to reputation; if the main content is untrue, thereby damaging the other person's reputation, it shall be deemed as infringing upon the right to reputation.

十、问：因名誉权受到侵害使生产、经营、销售遭受损失予以赔偿的范围和数额如何确定？  
答：因名誉权受到侵害使生产、经营、销售遭受损失予以赔偿的范围和数额，可以按照确因侵权而造成客户退货、解除合同等损失程度来适当确定。

**Article None** 10. Q: How shall the scope and amount of compensation be determined for losses in production, operation, and sales caused by infringement of the right to reputation?  
A: The scope and amount of compensation for losses in production, operation, and sales caused by infringement of the right to reputation may be appropriately determined based on the extent of losses such as customer returns and dissolution of contracts actually caused by the infringement.

十一、问：名誉权纠纷与其他民事纠纷交织在一起的，人民法院应如何审理？  
答：名誉权纠纷与其他民事纠纷交织在一起的，人民法院应当按当事人自己选择的请求予以审理。发生适用数种请求的，人民法院应当根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定和案件的实际情况，可以合并审理的合并审理；不能合并审理的，可以告知当事人另行起诉。

**Article None** 11. Q: How shall the People's Court hear a case where a dispute over the right to reputation is intertwined with other civil disputes?  
A: If a dispute over the right to reputation is intertwined with other civil disputes, the People's Court shall hear the case based on the claim chosen by the party. If multiple claims are applicable, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the actual circumstances of the case, consolidate the trial if consolidation is possible; if consolidation is not possible, the People's Court may inform the party to file a separate lawsuit.
