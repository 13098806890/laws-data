### 最高人民法院关于废止1993年底以前发布的部分司法解释的通知

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于废止1993年底以前发布的部分司法解释的通知  
  
法发〔1994〕16号  
  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院：  
  
根据任建新院长在第十六次全国法院工作会议报告中提出的“抓紧对过去的司法解释进行清理，对其中不适应加快改革开放和经济建设要求的，分别不同情况，进行修改、补充或者废止”的要求，我院自1993年起，本着由近及远、分期分批、抓紧进行的原则，对1979年至1993年间发布的司法解释进行了全面清理。现将经我院审判委员会讨论通过的第一批予以废止的司法解释目录印发给你们。这批予以废止的司法解释共11件，其中刑事审判方面2件，经济审判方面2件，行政审判方面4件，海事审判方面3件。予以废止的这些司法解释从即日起不再适用（有的早已自行失效）。但过去适用上述司法解释对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。清理司法解释工作尚在进行中，需要废止的司法解释，以后还将陆续分批通知你们。最高法院与有关部门联合发布的司法解释性文件需要废止的，我院将与有关部门联合发文予以废止。有些司法解释只有部分内容不适应当前审判实践需要的，我院将在全面清理的基础上，组织有关部门研究修改，各地有什么意见请及时报我院。

**Article 1** Fa Fa [1994] No. 16  
Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army:  
In accordance with the requirement set forth in President Ren Jianxin's report at the Sixteenth National Conference of Court Work, i.e., "to accelerate the work of cleaning up previous judicial interpretations, and to amend, supplement, or abolish those that are no longer suitable for the requirements of accelerating reform, opening up, and economic development, depending on the specific circumstances," our Court has since 1993, adhering to the principle of working from recent to distant, in batches, and with due diligence, conducted a comprehensive cleaning up of judicial interpretations issued between 1979 and 1993. We hereby issue to you the first batch of the catalogue of judicial interpretations to be abolished, which has been discussed and adopted by the Adjudication Committee of our Court. This batch of judicial interpretations to be abolished totals 11 pieces, including 2 in criminal adjudication, 2 in economic adjudication, 4 in administrative adjudication, and 3 in maritime adjudication. These judicial interpretations to be abolished shall no longer apply from the date of this Notice (some have already ceased to have effect by themselves). However, judgments and rulings previously rendered in relevant cases applying the aforementioned judicial interpretations shall remain effective. The work of cleaning up judicial interpretations is still ongoing. Judicial interpretations that need to be abolished shall be notified to you in subsequent batches. For judicial interpretation documents jointly issued by the Supreme People's Court and relevant departments that need to be abolished, our Court shall jointly issue a document with the relevant departments to abolish them. For some judicial interpretations where only part of their content is no longer suitable for current adjudication practice, our Court shall, on the basis of comprehensive cleaning up, organize relevant departments to study and amend them. All localities shall promptly submit their opinions to our Court.
