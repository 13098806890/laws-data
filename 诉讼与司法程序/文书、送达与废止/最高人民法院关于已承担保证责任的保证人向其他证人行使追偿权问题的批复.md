### 最高人民法院关于已承担保证责任的保证人向其他证人行使追偿权问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于已承担保证责任的保证人向其他  
  
证人行使追偿权问题的批复  
  
（2002年11月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1256次会议通过，自2002年12月5日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2002〕37号  
  
云南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院云高法〔2002〕160号《关于已经承担了保证责任的保证人向保证期间内未被主张保证责任的其他保证人行使追偿权是否成立的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国担保法》第十二条的规定，承担连带责任保证的保证人一人或者数人承担保证责任后，有权要求其他保证人清偿应当承担的份额，不受债权人是否在保证期间内向未承担保证责任的保证人主张过保证责任的影响。  
  
此复  
  
2002年11月23日

**Article 1** Interpretation No. 37 [2002] of the Supreme People's Court  
The Higher People's Court of Yunnan Province:  
Your Request for Instructions on Whether a Guarantor Who Has Assumed Guarantee Liability May Exercise the Right of Recourse against Other Guarantors to Whom No Guarantee Liability Was Claimed Within the Guarantee Period (Yun Gao Fa [2002] No. 160) has been received. Upon examination, the reply is as follows:  
Pursuant to the provisions of Article 12 of the Guarantee Law of the People's Republic of China, where one or several guarantors under joint and several liability guarantee have assumed the guarantee liability, they shall have the right to demand that other guarantors pay the shares they ought to bear, irrespective of whether the creditor has claimed the guarantee liability against the guarantors who have not assumed the guarantee liability within the guarantee period.  
This is hereby replied.  
23 November 2002
