# 最高人民法院关于审理商标案件有关管辖和法律适用范围问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕1号  
**公布日期**：2002-01-09  
**生效日期**：2002-01-21

> （2001年12月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1203次会议通过　2002年1月9日最高人民法院公告公布　自2002年1月21日起施行） 《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于修改〈中华人民共和国商标法〉的决定》（以下简称商标法修改决定）已由第九届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第二十四次会议通过，自2001年12月1日起施行。为了正确审理商标案件，根据《中华人民共和国商标法》（以下简称商标法）、《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》（以下简称行政诉讼法）的规定，现就人民法院审理商标案件有关管辖和法律适用范围等问题，作如下解释： 第一条　人民法院受理以下商标案件： 1.不服国务院工商行政管理部门商标评审委员会（以下简称商标评审委员会）作出的复审决定或者裁定的案件； 2.不服工商行政管理部门作出的有关商标的具体行政行为的案件； 3.商标专用权权属纠纷案件； 4.侵犯商标专用权纠纷案件； 5.商标专用权转让合同纠纷案件； 6.商标许可使用合同纠纷案件； 7.申请诉前停止侵犯商标专用权案件； 8.申请诉前财产保全案件； 9.申请诉前证据保全案件； 10.其他商标案件。 第二条　本解释第一条所列第1项第一审案件，由北京市高级人民法院根据最高人民法院的授权确定其辖区内有关中级人民法院管辖。 本解释第一条所列第2项第一审案件，根据行政诉讼法的有关规定确定管辖。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院受理以下商标案件：  
1.不服国务院工商行政管理部门商标评审委员会（以下简称商标评审委员会）作出的复审决定或者裁定的案件；  
2.不服工商行政管理部门作出的有关商标的具体行政行为的案件；  
3.商标专用权权属纠纷案件；  
4.侵犯商标专用权纠纷案件；  
5.商标专用权转让合同纠纷案件；  
6.商标许可使用合同纠纷案件；  
7.申请诉前停止侵犯商标专用权案件；  
8.申请诉前财产保全案件；  
9.申请诉前证据保全案件；  
10.其他商标案件。

**Article 1** The People's Court shall accept the following trademark cases:  
1. Cases challenging the review decisions or rulings made by the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board of the administrative department for industry and commerce under the State Council (hereinafter referred to as the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board);  
2. Cases challenging the specific administrative acts of the administrative department for industry and commerce relating to trademarks;  
3. Cases concerning disputes over the ownership of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
4. Cases concerning disputes over infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
5. Cases concerning disputes over a contract for transfer of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
6. Cases concerning disputes over a trademark licensing contract;  
7. Cases concerning application for pre-litigation injunction to cease infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark;  
8. Cases concerning application for pre-litigation property preservation;  
9. Cases concerning application for pre-litigation evidence preservation;  
10. Other trademark cases.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[本解释第一条](#art-1)所列第1项第一审案件，由北京市高级人民法院根据最高人民法院的授权确定其辖区内有关中级人民法院管辖。  
[本解释第一条](#art-1)所列第2项第一审案件，根据行政诉讼法的有关规定确定管辖。  
商标民事纠纷第一审案件，由中级以上人民法院管辖。  
各高级人民法院根据本辖区的实际情况，经最高人民法院批准，可以在较大城市确定1～2个基层人民法院受理第一审商标民事纠纷案件。

**Article 2** The cases of first instance as listed in item 1 of Article 1 of this Interpretation shall be under the jurisdiction of the relevant Intermediate People's Courts within the jurisdiction of the Higher People's Court of Beijing Municipality, as determined by the Higher People's Court of Beijing Municipality pursuant to the authorization of the Supreme People's Court.  
The cases of first instance as listed in item 2 of Article 1 of this Interpretation shall have their jurisdiction determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Administrative Procedure Law.  
Cases of first instance concerning civil disputes over trademarks shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Courts or above.  
The Higher People's Courts may, subject to the approval of the Supreme People's Court and in light of the actual circumstances of their respective jurisdictions, designate one or two Primary People's Courts in larger cities to accept cases of first instance concerning civil disputes over trademarks.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　商标注册人或者利害关系人向工商行政管理部门就侵犯商标专用权行为请求处理，又向人民法院提起侵犯商标专用权诉讼请求损害赔偿的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 3** Where a trademark registrant or an interested party requests the administrative department for industry and commerce to handle an act of infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark, and subsequently files an action with a People's Court for damages for infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　商标评审委员会在商标法修改决定施行前受理的案件，于该决定施行后作出复审决定或裁定，当事人对复审决定或裁定不服向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当受理。

**Article 4** Where the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board accepts a case before the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force, and makes a review decision or ruling after the Decision comes into force, if a party is not satisfied with the review decision or ruling and initiates a prosecution with a People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　除本解释另行规定外，对商标法修改决定施行前发生，属于修改后[商标法第四条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-4)、第五条、第八条、第九条第一款、第十条第一款第（二）、（三）、（四）项、第十条第二款、第十一条、第十二条、第十三条、第十五条、第十六条、第二十四条、第二十五条、第三十一条所列举的情形，商标评审委员会于商标法修改决定施行后作出复审决定或者裁定，当事人不服向人民法院起诉的行政案件，适用修改后商标法的相应规定进行审查；属于其他情形的，适用修改前商标法的相应规定进行审查。

**Article 5** Except as otherwise provided in this Interpretation, with respect to administrative cases that arise from facts occurring before the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force and that fall within the circumstances listed in Article 4, Article 5, Article 8, Article 9(1), Article 10(1)(2), (3), (4), Article 10(2), Article 11, Article 12, Article 13, Article 15, Article 16, Article 24, Article 25, and Article 31 of the amended Trademark Law, where the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board makes a review decision or ruling after the Decision on the Amendment comes into force, and a party is not satisfied and initiates a prosecution with a People's Court, the corresponding provisions of the amended Trademark Law shall apply for review; for other circumstances, the corresponding provisions of the pre-amendment Trademark Law shall apply for review.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人就商标法修改决定施行时已满1年的注册商标发生争议，不服商标评审委员会作出的裁定向人民法院起诉的，适用修改前[商标法第二十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-27)第二款规定的提出申请的期限处理；商标法修改决定施行时商标注册不满1年的，适用修改后[商标法第四十一条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-41)第二款、第三款规定的提出申请的期限处理。

**Article 6** Where a party has a dispute over a registered trademark that has been registered for one year or more at the time the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force, and is not satisfied with a ruling made by the Trademark Review and Adjudication Board and initiates a prosecution with a People's Court, the time limit for filing an application as provided in Article 27(2) of the pre-amendment Trademark Law shall apply for handling; where the trademark registration is less than one year at the time the Decision on the Amendment comes into force, the time limit for filing an application as provided in Article 41(2) and (3) of the amended Trademark Law shall apply for handling.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对商标法修改决定施行前发生的侵犯商标专用权行为，商标注册人或者利害关系人于该决定施行后在起诉前向人民法院提出申请采取责令停止侵权行为或者保全证据措施的，适用修改后[商标法第五十七条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-57)、第五十八条的规定。

**Article 7** With respect to an act of infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark occurring before the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force, where a trademark registrant or an interested party, after the Decision comes into force, submits an application to a People's Court before prosecution for adopting measures to order cessation of the infringing act or to preserve evidence, the provisions of Article 57 and Article 58 of the amended Trademark Law shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对商标法修改决定施行前发生的侵犯商标专用权行为起诉的案件，人民法院于该决定施行时尚未作出生效判决的，参照修改后[商标法第五十六条](../../民事与商事/知识产权/中华人民共和国商标法.md#art-56)的规定处理。

**Article 8** With respect to a case initiated by prosecution for an act of infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark occurring before the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force, where the People's Court has not yet rendered a legally effective judgment at the time the Decision comes into force, the provisions of Article 56 of the amended Trademark Law shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　除本解释另行规定外，商标法修改决定施行后人民法院受理的商标民事纠纷案件，涉及该决定施行前发生的民事行为的，适用修改前商标法的规定；涉及该决定施行后发生的民事行为的，适用修改后商标法的规定；涉及该决定施行前发生，持续到该决定施行后的民事行为的，分别适用修改前、后商标法的规定。

**Article 9** Except as otherwise provided in this Interpretation, for a civil dispute case concerning a trademark accepted by a People's Court after the Decision on the Amendment to the Trademark Law comes into force, if it involves a civil act occurring before the Decision comes into force, the provisions of the pre-amendment Trademark Law shall apply; if it involves a civil act occurring after the Decision comes into force, the provisions of the amended Trademark Law shall apply; if it involves a civil act that occurred before the Decision comes into force and continues after the Decision comes into force, the provisions of the pre-amendment and amended Trademark Law shall apply respectively.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院受理的侵犯商标专用权纠纷案件，已经过工商行政管理部门处理的，人民法院仍应当就当事人民事争议的事实进行审查。

**Article 10** Where a People's Court accepts a case concerning a dispute over infringement of the exclusive right to use a trademark that has already been handled by the administrative department for industry and commerce, the People's Court shall nevertheless examine the facts of the civil dispute between the parties.
