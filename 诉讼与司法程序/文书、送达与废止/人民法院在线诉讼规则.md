# 人民法院在线诉讼规则

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕12号  
**公布日期**：2021-05-18  
**生效日期**：2021-08-01

> 《人民法院在线诉讼规则》已于2021年5月18日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1838次会议通过，现予公布，自2021年8月1日起施行。 2021年6月16日 人民法院在线诉讼规则 法释〔2021〕12号 为推进和规范在线诉讼活动，完善在线诉讼规则，依法保障当事人及其他诉讼参与人等诉讼主体的合法权利，确保公正高效审理案件，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等相关法律规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规则。 第一条　人民法院、当事人及其他诉讼参与人等可以依托电子诉讼平台（以下简称“诉讼平台”），通过互联网或者专用网络在线完成立案、调解、证据交换、询问、庭审、送达等全部或者部分诉讼环节。 在线诉讼活动与线下诉讼活动具有同等法律效力。 第二条　人民法院开展在线诉讼应当遵循以下原则： （一）公正高效原则。严格依法开展在线诉讼活动，完善审判流程，健全工作机制，加强技术保障，提高司法效率，保障司法公正。 （二）合法自愿原则。尊重和保障当事人及其他诉讼参与人对诉讼方式的选择权，未经当事人及其他诉讼参与人同意，人民法院不得强制或者变相强制适用在线诉讼。 （三）权利保障原则。充分保障当事人各项诉讼权利，强化提示、说明、告知义务，不得随意减少诉讼环节和减损当事人诉讼权益。 （四）便民利民原则。优化在线诉讼服务，完善诉讼平台功能，加强信息技术应用，降低当事人诉讼成本，提升纠纷解决效率。统筹兼顾不同群体司法需求，对未成年人、老年人、残障人士等特殊群体加强诉讼引导，提供相应司法便利。 （五）安全可靠原则。依法维护国家安全，保护国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，有效保障在线诉讼数据信息安全。规范技术应用，确保技术中立和平台中立。 第三条　人民法院综合考虑案件情况、当事人意愿和技术条件等因素，可以对以下案件适用在线诉讼： （一）民事、行政诉讼案件；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院、当事人及其他诉讼参与人等可以依托电子诉讼平台（以下简称“诉讼平台”），通过互联网或者专用网络在线完成立案、调解、证据交换、询问、庭审、送达等全部或者部分诉讼环节。  
在线诉讼活动与线下诉讼活动具有同等法律效力。

**Article 1** The People's Court, parties, and other participants in proceedings may rely on the electronic litigation platform (hereinafter referred to as the "Litigation Platform") to complete all or part of the litigation procedures, including case filing, mediation, evidence exchange, inquiry, trial, and service, through the Internet or a dedicated network.  
Online litigation activities shall have the same legal effect as offline litigation activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院开展在线诉讼应当遵循以下原则：  
（一）公正高效原则。严格依法开展在线诉讼活动，完善审判流程，健全工作机制，加强技术保障，提高司法效率，保障司法公正。  
（二）合法自愿原则。尊重和保障当事人及其他诉讼参与人对诉讼方式的选择权，未经当事人及其他诉讼参与人同意，人民法院不得强制或者变相强制适用在线诉讼。  
（三）权利保障原则。充分保障当事人各项诉讼权利，强化提示、说明、告知义务，不得随意减少诉讼环节和减损当事人诉讼权益。  
（四）便民利民原则。优化在线诉讼服务，完善诉讼平台功能，加强信息技术应用，降低当事人诉讼成本，提升纠纷解决效率。统筹兼顾不同群体司法需求，对未成年人、老年人、残障人士等特殊群体加强诉讼引导，提供相应司法便利。  
（五）安全可靠原则。依法维护国家安全，保护国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和个人信息，有效保障在线诉讼数据信息安全。规范技术应用，确保技术中立和平台中立。

**Article 2** The People's Court shall follow the following principles when conducting online litigation:  
(1) The principle of fairness and efficiency. Online litigation activities shall be conducted strictly in accordance with law, trial procedures shall be improved, work mechanisms shall be perfected, technical support shall be strengthened, judicial efficiency shall be enhanced, and judicial fairness shall be guaranteed.  
(2) The principle of legality and voluntariness. The right of parties and other participants in proceedings to choose the litigation method shall be respected and protected. Without the consent of the parties and other participants in proceedings, the People's Court shall not force or covertly force the application of online litigation.  
(3) The principle of right protection. All litigation rights of parties shall be fully protected, the obligations of reminder, explanation, and notification shall be strengthened, and litigation procedures shall not be arbitrarily reduced or the litigation rights and interests of parties impaired.  
(4) The principle of convenience and benefit to the people. Online litigation services shall be optimized, the functions of the Litigation Platform shall be improved, the application of information technology shall be strengthened, the litigation costs of parties shall be reduced, and the efficiency of dispute resolution shall be enhanced. The judicial needs of different groups shall be taken into overall consideration, and litigation guidance shall be strengthened for special groups such as minors, the elderly, and persons with disabilities, providing corresponding judicial convenience.  
(5) The principle of security and reliability. National security shall be safeguarded in accordance with law, state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and personal information shall be protected, and the data and information security of online litigation shall be effectively guaranteed. Technical application shall be regulated to ensure technical neutrality and platform neutrality.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院综合考虑案件情况、当事人意愿和技术条件等因素，可以对以下案件适用在线诉讼：  
（一）民事、行政诉讼案件；  
（二）刑事速裁程序案件，减刑、假释案件，以及因其他特殊原因不宜线下审理的刑事案件；  
（三）民事特别程序、督促程序、破产程序和非诉执行审查案件；  
（四）民事、行政执行案件和刑事附带民事诉讼执行案件；  
（五）其他适宜采取在线方式审理的案件。

**Article 3** The People's Court may, after comprehensively considering factors such as the circumstances of the case, the will of the parties, and technical conditions, apply online litigation to the following cases:  
(1) Civil and administrative action cases;  
(2) Cases under the criminal fast-track procedure, cases of commutation of punishment and parole, and criminal cases that are not suitable for offline adjudication due to other special reasons;  
(3) Cases under special civil procedures, supervision procedures, bankruptcy procedures, and non-litigation enforcement review cases;  
(4) Civil and administrative enforcement cases and enforcement cases of criminal incidental civil actions;  
(5) Other cases suitable for adjudication by online means.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院开展在线诉讼，应当征得当事人同意，并告知适用在线诉讼的具体环节、主要形式、权利义务、法律后果和操作方法等。  
人民法院应当根据当事人对在线诉讼的相应意思表示，作出以下处理：  
（一）当事人主动选择适用在线诉讼的，人民法院可以不再另行征得其同意，相应诉讼环节可以直接在线进行；  
（二）各方当事人均同意适用在线诉讼的，相应诉讼环节可以在线进行；  
（三）部分当事人同意适用在线诉讼，部分当事人不同意的，相应诉讼环节可以采取同意方当事人线上、不同意方当事人线下的方式进行；  
（四）当事人仅主动选择或者同意对部分诉讼环节适用在线诉讼的，人民法院不得推定其对其他诉讼环节均同意适用在线诉讼。  
对人民检察院参与的案件适用在线诉讼的，应当征得人民检察院同意。

**Article 4** When conducting online litigation, the People's Court shall obtain the consent of the parties and inform them of the specific procedures, main forms, rights and obligations, legal consequences, and operating methods applicable to online litigation.  
The People's Court shall, based on the corresponding manifestation of intention of the parties with respect to online litigation, make the following handling:  
(1) If a party voluntarily chooses to apply online litigation, the People's Court may not separately obtain its consent again, and the corresponding litigation procedures may be conducted directly online;  
(2) If all parties agree to apply online litigation, the corresponding litigation procedures may be conducted online;  
(3) If some parties agree to apply online litigation while others disagree, the corresponding litigation procedures may be conducted in a manner where the consenting parties participate online and the disagreeing parties participate offline;  
(4) If a party only voluntarily chooses or agrees to apply online litigation to some litigation procedures, the People's Court shall not presume that it also agrees to apply online litigation to other litigation procedures.  
Where online litigation is applied to a case in which the People's Procuratorate participates, the consent of the People's Procuratorate shall be obtained.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在诉讼过程中，如存在当事人欠缺在线诉讼能力、不具备在线诉讼条件或者相应诉讼环节不宜在线办理等情形之一的，人民法院应当将相应诉讼环节转为线下进行。  
当事人已同意对相应诉讼环节适用在线诉讼，但诉讼过程中又反悔的，应当在开展相应诉讼活动前的合理期限内提出。经审查，人民法院认为不存在故意拖延诉讼等不当情形的，相应诉讼环节可以转为线下进行。  
在调解、证据交换、询问、听证、庭审等诉讼环节中，一方当事人要求其他当事人及诉讼参与人在线下参与诉讼的，应当提出具体理由。经审查，人民法院认为案件存在案情疑难复杂、需证人现场作证、有必要线下举证质证、陈述辩论等情形之一的，相应诉讼环节可以转为线下进行。

**Article 5** During the litigation process, if any of the following circumstances exist, such as a party lacking the capacity for online litigation, not having the conditions for online litigation, or the corresponding litigation procedure being unsuitable for online handling, the People's Court shall convert the corresponding litigation procedure to offline.  
If a party has agreed to apply online litigation to the corresponding litigation procedure but later changes its mind during the litigation process, it shall raise the objection within a reasonable period before carrying out the corresponding litigation activity. Upon examination, if the People's Court finds that there is no improper circumstance such as intentional delay of litigation, the corresponding litigation procedure may be converted to offline.  
In litigation procedures such as mediation, evidence exchange, inquiry, hearing, and trial, if one party requests that other parties and participants in proceedings participate in the litigation offline, it shall provide specific reasons. Upon examination, if the People's Court finds that the case involves any of the following circumstances, such as the case being complicated and difficult, requiring a witness to testify in person, or it being necessary to present evidence and cross-examine offline, or make statements and arguments offline, the corresponding litigation procedure may be converted to offline.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　当事人已同意适用在线诉讼，但无正当理由不参与在线诉讼活动或者不作出相应诉讼行为，也未在合理期限内申请提出转为线下进行的，应当依照法律和司法解释的相关规定承担相应法律后果。

**Article 6** If a party has agreed to apply online litigation but, without正当理由, fails to participate in online litigation activities or fails to make corresponding litigation acts, and also fails to apply for conversion to offline within a reasonable period, it shall bear the corresponding legal consequences in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　参与在线诉讼的诉讼主体应当先行在诉讼平台完成实名注册。人民法院应当通过证件证照在线比对、身份认证平台认证等方式，核实诉讼主体的实名手机号码、居民身份证件号码、护照号码、统一社会信用代码等信息，确认诉讼主体身份真实性。诉讼主体在线完成身份认证后，取得登录诉讼平台的专用账号。  
参与在线诉讼的诉讼主体应当妥善保管诉讼平台专用账号和密码。除有证据证明存在账号被盗用或者系统错误的情形外，使用专用账号登录诉讼平台所作出的行为，视为被认证人本人行为。  
人民法院在线开展调解、证据交换、庭审等诉讼活动，应当再次验证诉讼主体的身份；确有必要的，应当在线下进一步核实身份。

**Article 7** Litigation subjects participating in online litigation shall first complete real-name registration on the Litigation Platform. The People's Court shall verify the real-name mobile phone number, resident identity card number, passport number, unified social credit code, and other information of the litigation subject through online comparison of certificates and licenses, identity authentication platform certification, and other means, to confirm the authenticity of the litigation subject's identity. After completing identity authentication online, the litigation subject shall obtain a dedicated account for logging into the Litigation Platform.  
Litigation subjects participating in online litigation shall properly keep the dedicated account and password of the Litigation Platform. Unless there is evidence proving that the account is stolen or there is a system error, any act performed by logging into the Litigation Platform with the dedicated account shall be deemed as the act of the authenticated person.  
When conducting litigation activities such as mediation, evidence exchange, and trial online, the People's Court shall re-verify the identity of the litigation subject; if truly necessary, the identity shall be further verified offline.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院、特邀调解组织、特邀调解员可以通过诉讼平台、人民法院调解平台等开展在线调解活动。在线调解应当按照法律和司法解释相关规定进行，依法保护国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和其他不宜公开的信息。

**Article 8** The People's Court, specially invited mediation organizations, and specially invited mediators may conduct online mediation activities through the Litigation Platform, the People's Court Mediation Platform, etc. Online mediation shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, and state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, and other information not suitable for disclosure shall be protected in accordance with law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人采取在线方式提交起诉材料的，人民法院应当在收到材料后的法定期限内，在线作出以下处理：  
（一）符合起诉条件的，登记立案并送达案件受理通知书、交纳诉讼费用通知书、举证通知书等诉讼文书；  
（二）提交材料不符合要求的，及时通知其补正，并一次性告知补正内容和期限，案件受理时间自收到补正材料后次日重新起算；  
（三）不符合起诉条件或者起诉材料经补正仍不符合要求，原告坚持起诉的，依法裁定不予受理或者不予立案；  
当事人已在线提交符合要求的起诉状等材料的，人民法院不得要求当事人再提供纸质件。  
上诉、申请再审、特别程序、执行等案件的在线受理规则，参照本条第一款、第二款规定办理。

**Article 9** Where a party submits prosecution materials by online means, the People's Court shall, within the statutory period after receiving the materials, make the following handling online:  
(1) If the conditions for prosecution are met, register the case and serve litigation documents such as the Notice of Case Acceptance, Notice of Payment of Litigation Costs, and Notice of Burden of Proof;  
(2) If the submitted materials do not meet the requirements, promptly notify the party to make corrections and inform the party of the correction contents and the time limit at one time, and the time of case acceptance shall be recalculated from the next day after receiving the corrected materials;  
(3) If the conditions for prosecution are not met or the prosecution materials still do not meet the requirements after correction, and the plaintiff insists on prosecuting, render a ruling in accordance with law that the case shall not be accepted or not be filed.  
If the party has already submitted compliant materials such as a statement of complaint online, the People's Court shall not require the party to provide paper copies.  
The rules for online acceptance of appeals, applications for retrial, special procedures, enforcement, and other cases shall be handled by reference to the provisions of paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of this Article.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　案件适用在线诉讼的，人民法院应当通知被告、被上诉人或者其他诉讼参与人，询问其是否同意以在线方式参与诉讼。被通知人同意采用在线方式的，应当在收到通知的三日内通过诉讼平台验证身份、关联案件，并在后续诉讼活动中通过诉讼平台了解案件信息、接收和提交诉讼材料，以及实施其他诉讼行为。  
被通知人未明确表示同意采用在线方式，且未在人民法院指定期限内注册登录诉讼平台的，针对被通知人的相关诉讼活动在线下进行。

**Article 10** Where online litigation applies to a case, the People's Court shall notify the defendant, the respondent, or other participants in proceedings and inquire whether they agree to participate in the litigation by online means. If the notified person agrees to adopt the online method, within three days of receiving the notice, it shall verify its identity and associate the case on the Litigation Platform, and in subsequent litigation activities, understand case information, receive and submit litigation materials, and perform other litigation acts through the Litigation Platform.  
If the notified person does not clearly indicate its agreement to adopt the online method and fails to register and log in to the Litigation Platform within the period specified by the People's Court, the relevant litigation activities concerning the notified person shall be conducted offline.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人可以在诉讼平台直接填写录入起诉状、答辩状、反诉状、代理意见等诉讼文书材料。  
当事人可以通过扫描、翻拍、转录等方式，将线下的诉讼文书材料或者证据材料作电子化处理后上传至诉讼平台。诉讼材料为电子数据，且诉讼平台与存储该电子数据的平台已实现对接的，当事人可以将电子数据直接提交至诉讼平台。  
当事人提交电子化材料确有困难的，人民法院可以辅助当事人将线下材料作电子化处理后导入诉讼平台。

**Article 11** A party may directly fill in and input litigation documents such as the statement of complaint, statement of defense, counterclaim, and agent's opinion on the Litigation Platform.  
A party may, by means of scanning, re-photographing, transcription, etc., digitize offline litigation documents or evidence materials and upload them to the Litigation Platform. If the litigation materials are electronic data and the Litigation Platform has achieved interconnection with the platform storing such electronic data, the party may directly submit the electronic data to the Litigation Platform.  
If a party has genuine difficulty in submitting digitized materials, the People's Court may assist the party in digitizing offline materials and importing them into the Litigation Platform.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　当事人提交的电子化材料，经人民法院审核通过后，可以直接在诉讼中使用。诉讼中存在下列情形之一的，人民法院应当要求当事人提供原件、原物：  
（一）对方当事人认为电子化材料与原件、原物不一致，并提出合理理由和依据的；  
（二）电子化材料呈现不完整、内容不清晰、格式不规范的；  
（三）人民法院卷宗、档案管理相关规定要求提供原件、原物的；  
（四）人民法院认为有必要提交原件、原物的。

**Article 12** Digitized materials submitted by a party, after being reviewed and approved by the People's Court, may be directly used in the litigation. Where any of the following circumstances exist in the litigation, the People's Court shall require the party to provide the original or the physical object:  
(1) The opposing party believes that the digitized materials are inconsistent with the original or the physical object and provides reasonable grounds and supporting evidence;  
(2) The digitized materials are incomplete in presentation, unclear in content, or nonstandard in format;  
(3) Relevant provisions of the People's Court concerning case files and archives management require the provision of the original or the physical object;  
(4) The People's Court deems it necessary to submit the original or the physical object.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　当事人提交的电子化材料，符合下列情形之一的，人民法院可以认定符合原件、原物形式要求：  
（一）对方当事人对电子化材料与原件、原物的一致性未提出异议的；  
（二）电子化材料形成过程已经过公证机构公证的；  
（三）电子化材料已在之前诉讼中提交并经人民法院确认的；  
（四）电子化材料已通过在线或者线下方式与原件、原物比对一致的；  
（五）有其他证据证明电子化材料与原件、原物一致的。

**Article 13** Where the digitized materials submitted by a party meet any of the following circumstances, the People's Court may determine that they satisfy the formal requirements for original documents or physical objects:  
(1) The opposing party does not raise any objection to the consistency of the digitized materials with the original or the physical object;  
(2) The process of creating the digitized materials has been notarized by a notarization institution;  
(3) The digitized materials have been submitted in prior litigation and confirmed by the People's Court;  
(4) The digitized materials have been compared with the original or the physical object online or offline and found to be consistent;  
(5) There is other evidence proving that the digitized materials are consistent with the original or the physical object.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院根据当事人选择和案件情况，可以组织当事人开展在线证据交换，通过同步或者非同步方式在线举证、质证。  
各方当事人选择同步在线交换证据的，应当在人民法院指定的时间登录诉讼平台，通过在线视频或者其他方式，对已经导入诉讼平台的证据材料或者线下送达的证据材料副本，集中发表质证意见。  
各方当事人选择非同步在线交换证据的，应当在人民法院确定的合理期限内，分别登录诉讼平台，查看已经导入诉讼平台的证据材料，并发表质证意见。  
各方当事人均同意在线证据交换，但对具体方式无法达成一致意见的，适用同步在线证据交换。

**Article 14** The People's Court may, based on the parties' choices and the circumstances of the case, organize the parties to conduct online evidence exchange, and present and cross-examine evidence online by synchronous or asynchronous means.  
If all parties choose synchronous online evidence exchange, they shall log in to the Litigation Platform at the time designated by the People's Court and, through online video or other means, collectively present their opinions on cross-examination regarding the evidence materials already imported into the Litigation Platform or the copies of evidence materials served offline.  
If all parties choose asynchronous online evidence exchange, they shall, within a reasonable period determined by the People's Court, respectively log in to the Litigation Platform, view the evidence materials already imported into the Litigation Platform, and present their opinions on cross-examination.  
If all parties agree to online evidence exchange but fail to reach an agreement on the specific method, synchronous online evidence exchange shall apply.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　当事人作为证据提交的电子化材料和电子数据，人民法院应当按照法律和司法解释的相关规定，经当事人举证质证后，依法认定其真实性、合法性和关联性。未经人民法院查证属实的证据，不得作为认定案件事实的根据。

**Article 15** For digitized materials and electronic data submitted by a party as evidence, the People's Court shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, after the parties present and cross-examine the evidence, determine its authenticity, legality, and relevance in accordance with law. Evidence that has not been verified by the People's Court shall not be used as the basis for determining the facts of the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人作为证据提交的电子数据系通过区块链技术存储，并经技术核验一致的，人民法院可以认定该电子数据上链后未经篡改，但有相反证据足以推翻的除外。

**Article 16** If the electronic data submitted by a party as evidence is stored through blockchain technology and is verified to be consistent through technical verification, the People's Court may determine that the electronic data has not been tampered with after being recorded on the chain, unless there is contrary evidence sufficient to overturn this determination.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　当事人对区块链技术存储的电子数据上链后的真实性提出异议，并有合理理由的，人民法院应当结合下列因素作出判断：  
（一）存证平台是否符合国家有关部门关于提供区块链存证服务的相关规定；  
（二）当事人与存证平台是否存在利害关系，并利用技术手段不当干预取证、存证过程；  
（三）存证平台的信息系统是否符合清洁性、安全性、可靠性、可用性的国家标准或者行业标准；  
（四）存证技术和过程是否符合相关国家标准或者行业标准中关于系统环境、技术安全、加密方式、数据传输、信息验证等方面的要求。

**Article 17** If a party raises an objection to the authenticity of electronic data stored through blockchain technology after being recorded on the chain and has reasonable grounds, the People's Court shall make a judgment by considering the following factors:  
(1) Whether the evidence storage platform complies with the relevant provisions of the relevant state authorities on providing blockchain evidence storage services;  
(2) Whether there is a conflict of interest between the party and the evidence storage platform, and whether the party has improperly interfered with the process of evidence collection and storage by technical means;  
(3) Whether the information system of the evidence storage platform meets the national or industry standards for cleanliness, security, reliability, and usability;  
(4) Whether the evidence storage technology and process meet the requirements of relevant national or industry standards concerning system environment, technical security, encryption methods, data transmission, information verification, and other aspects.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　当事人提出电子数据上链存储前已不具备真实性，并提供证据证明或者说明理由的，人民法院应当予以审查。  
人民法院根据案件情况，可以要求提交区块链技术存储电子数据的一方当事人，提供证据证明上链存储前数据的真实性，并结合上链存储前数据的具体来源、生成机制、存储过程、公证机构公证、第三方见证、关联印证数据等情况作出综合判断。当事人不能提供证据证明或者作出合理说明，该电子数据也无法与其他证据相互印证的，人民法院不予确认其真实性。

**Article 18** If a party claims that the electronic data was not authentic before being recorded on the chain and provides evidence to prove it or explains the reasons, the People's Court shall examine it.  
The People's Court may, based on the circumstances of the case, require the party that submitted the electronic data stored through blockchain technology to provide evidence proving the authenticity of the data before being recorded on the chain, and make a comprehensive judgment by taking into account the specific source of the data before being recorded on the chain, its generation mechanism, storage process, notarization by a notarization institution, third-party witnessing, related corroborating data, and other circumstances. If the party cannot provide evidence to prove or make a reasonable explanation, and the electronic data cannot be mutually corroborated with other evidence, the People's Court shall not confirm its authenticity.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　当事人可以申请具有专门知识的人就区块链技术存储电子数据相关技术问题提出意见。人民法院可以根据当事人申请或者依职权，委托鉴定区块链技术存储电子数据的真实性，或者调取其他相关证据进行核对。

**Article 19** A party may apply for a person with specialized knowledge to render an opinion on technical issues related to electronic data stored through blockchain technology. The People's Court may, upon the party's application or ex officio, commission an expert evaluation of the authenticity of the electronic data stored through blockchain technology, or retrieve other relevant evidence for verification.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　经各方当事人同意，人民法院可以指定当事人在一定期限内，分别登录诉讼平台，以非同步的方式开展调解、证据交换、调查询问、庭审等诉讼活动。  
适用小额诉讼程序或者民事、行政简易程序审理的案件，同时符合下列情形的，人民法院和当事人可以在指定期限内，按照庭审程序环节分别录制参与庭审视频并上传至诉讼平台，非同步完成庭审活动：  
（一）各方当事人同时在线参与庭审确有困难；  
（二）一方当事人提出书面申请，各方当事人均表示同意；  
（三）案件经过在线证据交换或者调查询问，各方当事人对案件主要事实和证据不存在争议。

**Article 20** With the consent of all parties, the People's Court may designate a period within which the parties shall respectively log in to the Litigation Platform and conduct litigation activities such as mediation, evidence exchange, investigation and inquiry, and trial by asynchronous means.  
For cases tried under the small claims procedure or the civil or administrative summary procedure, if the following circumstances are all satisfied, the People's Court and the parties may, within a designated period, respectively record videos of their participation in the trial according to the trial procedures and upload them to the Litigation Platform to complete the trial asynchronously:  
(1) All parties have genuine difficulty in simultaneously participating in the trial online;  
(2) One party submits a written application and all parties express their consent;  
(3) The case has undergone online evidence exchange or investigation and inquiry, and the parties have no dispute over the main facts and evidence of the case.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民法院开庭审理的案件，应当根据当事人意愿、案件情况、社会影响、技术条件等因素，决定是否采取视频方式在线庭审，但具有下列情形之一的，不得适用在线庭审：  
（一）各方当事人均明确表示不同意，或者一方当事人表示不同意且有正当理由的；  
（二）各方当事人均不具备参与在线庭审的技术条件和能力的；  
（三）需要通过庭审现场查明身份、核对原件、查验实物的；  
（四）案件疑难复杂、证据繁多，适用在线庭审不利于查明事实和适用法律的；  
（五）案件涉及国家安全、国家秘密的；  
（六）案件具有重大社会影响，受到广泛关注的；  
（七）人民法院认为存在其他不宜适用在线庭审情形的。  
采取在线庭审方式审理的案件，审理过程中发现存在上述情形之一的，人民法院应当及时转为线下庭审。已完成的在线庭审活动具有法律效力。  
在线询问的适用范围和条件参照在线庭审的相关规则。

**Article 21** For cases to be heard in court, the People's Court shall decide whether to conduct the trial online by video based on factors such as the will of the parties, the circumstances of the case, social impact, and technical conditions. However, online trial shall not apply under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) All parties clearly express their disagreement, or one party expresses disagreement with正当理由;  
(2) All parties lack the technical conditions and capabilities to participate in an online trial;  
(3) It is necessary to ascertain identity, verify originals, or examine physical objects at the trial site;  
(4) The case is complicated and difficult with voluminous evidence, and applying an online trial is not conducive to ascertaining facts and applying law;  
(5) The case involves national security or state secrets;  
(6) The case has significant social impact and has attracted widespread attention;  
(7) The People's Court deems that there are other circumstances unsuitable for an online trial.  
If, during the trial of a case conducted by online trial, any of the above circumstances are discovered, the People's Court shall promptly convert to an offline trial. The online trial activities already completed shall have legal effect.  
The scope and conditions for online inquiries shall be governed by reference to the relevant rules on online trials.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　适用在线庭审的案件，应当按照法律和司法解释的相关规定开展庭前准备、法庭调查、法庭辩论等庭审活动，保障当事人申请回避、举证、质证、陈述、辩论等诉讼权利。

**Article 22** For cases where an online trial applies, trial activities such as pretrial preparation, court investigation, and court debate shall be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations, and the litigation rights of parties, including applying for withdrawal, presenting evidence, cross-examining evidence, making statements, and debating, shall be protected.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　需要公告送达的案件，人民法院可以在公告中明确线上或者线下参与庭审的具体方式，告知当事人选择在线庭审的权利。被公告方当事人未在开庭前向人民法院表示同意在线庭审的，被公告方当事人适用线下庭审。其他同意适用在线庭审的当事人，可以在线参与庭审。

**Article 23** For cases requiring service by announcement, the People's Court may specify in the announcement the specific methods of participating in the trial online or offline and inform the parties of their right to choose an online trial. If the party to whom the announcement is directed does not indicate its consent to an online trial to the People's Court before the trial, an offline trial shall apply to that party. Other parties that consent to an online trial may participate in the trial online.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　在线开展庭审活动，人民法院应当设置环境要素齐全的在线法庭。在线法庭应当保持国徽在显著位置，审判人员及席位名称等在视频画面合理区域。因存在特殊情形，确需在在线法庭之外的其他场所组织在线庭审的，应当报请本院院长同意。  
出庭人员参加在线庭审，应当选择安静、无干扰、光线适宜、网络信号良好、相对封闭的场所，不得在可能影响庭审音频视频效果或者有损庭审严肃性的场所参加庭审。必要时，人民法院可以要求出庭人员到指定场所参加在线庭审。

**Article 24** When conducting trial activities online, the People's Court shall set up an online courtroom with complete environmental elements. The online courtroom shall keep the national emblem in a prominent position, and the judges and the names of the seats shall be displayed in a reasonable area of the video screen. If, due to special circumstances, it is truly necessary to organize an online trial in a place other than the online courtroom, approval of the president of the court shall be obtained.  
Participants attending the trial online shall choose a quiet, non-interfering place with appropriate lighting, good network signal, and relative seclusion, and shall not participate in the trial in a place that may affect the audio-video effect or undermine the solemnity of the trial. Where necessary, the People's Court may require participants to attend the online trial at a designated place.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　出庭人员参加在线庭审应当尊重司法礼仪，遵守法庭纪律。人民法院根据在线庭审的特点，适用《中华人民共和国人民法院法庭规则》相关规定。  
除确属网络故障、设备损坏、电力中断或者不可抗力等原因外，当事人无正当理由不参加在线庭审，视为“拒不到庭”；在庭审中擅自退出，经提示、警告后仍不改正的，视为“中途退庭”，分别按照相关法律和司法解释的规定处理。

**Article 25** Participants attending an online trial shall respect judicial etiquette and comply with courtroom discipline. The People's Court shall apply the relevant provisions of the Rules of the People's Court of the People's Republic of China for Courtroom based on the characteristics of online trials.  
Except for reasons such as network failure, equipment damage, power outage, or force majeure, if a party fails to participate in an online trial without正当理由, it shall be deemed as "failure to appear in court"; if a party withdraws from the trial without authorization and fails to correct the behavior after being reminded and warned, it shall be deemed as "leaving the trial midway", and the matter shall be handled respectively in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　证人通过在线方式出庭的，人民法院应当通过指定在线出庭场所、设置在线作证室等方式，保证其不旁听案件审理和不受他人干扰。当事人对证人在线出庭提出异议且有合理理由的，或者人民法院认为确有必要的，应当要求证人线下出庭作证。  
鉴定人、勘验人、具有专门知识的人在线出庭的，参照前款规定执行。

**Article 26** When a witness appears in court online, the People's Court shall, by designating an online appearance venue, setting up an online testimony room, or other means, ensure that the witness does not attend the trial hearing and is not disturbed by others. If a party raises an objection to the witness appearing online with reasonable grounds, or if the People's Court deems it truly necessary, the witness shall be required to appear in court offline to testify.  
Where an expert evaluator, inspector, or person with specialized knowledge appears online, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　适用在线庭审的案件，应当按照法律和司法解释的相关规定公开庭审活动。  
对涉及国家安全、国家秘密、个人隐私的案件，庭审过程不得在互联网上公开。对涉及未成年人、商业秘密、离婚等民事案件，当事人申请不公开审理的，在线庭审过程可以不在互联网上公开。  
未经人民法院同意，任何人不得违法违规录制、截取、传播涉及在线庭审过程的音频视频、图文资料。

**Article 27** For cases where an online trial applies, the trial activities shall be made public in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations.  
For cases involving national security, state secrets, or personal privacy, the trial process shall not be made public on the Internet. For civil cases involving minors, trade secrets, divorce, etc., if the parties apply for trial not in public, the online trial process may not be made public on the Internet.  
Without the consent of the People's Court, no person shall illegally or improperly record, capture, or disseminate audio, video, graphic, or textual materials related to the online trial process.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　在线诉讼参与人故意违反[本规则第八条](#art-8)、第二十四条、第二十五条、第二十六条、第二十七条的规定，实施妨害在线诉讼秩序行为的，人民法院可以根据法律和司法解释关于妨害诉讼的相关规定作出处理。

**Article 28** If a participant in online litigation intentionally violates the provisions of Article 8, Article 24, Article 25, Article 26, or Article 27 of these Rules and commits acts that disrupt the order of online litigation, the People's Court may handle the matter in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations on disruption of litigation.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　经受送达人同意，人民法院可以通过送达平台，向受送达人的电子邮箱、即时通讯账号、诉讼平台专用账号等电子地址，按照法律和司法解释的相关规定送达诉讼文书和证据材料。  
具备下列情形之一的，人民法院可以确定受送达人同意电子送达：  
（一）受送达人明确表示同意的；  
（二）受送达人在诉讼前对适用电子送达已作出约定或者承诺的；  
（三）受送达人在提交的起诉状、上诉状、申请书、答辩状中主动提供用于接收送达的电子地址的；  
（四）受送达人通过回复收悉、参加诉讼等方式接受已经完成的电子送达，并且未明确表示不同意电子送达的。

**Article 29** With the consent of the person to be served, the People's Court may, through the service platform, serve litigation documents and evidence materials to the person's electronic address such as email address, instant messaging account, or dedicated account of the Litigation Platform, in accordance with the relevant provisions of laws and judicial interpretations.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court may determine that the person to be served consents to electronic service:  
(1) The person to be served clearly indicates its consent;  
(2) The person to be served has made an agreement or commitment to apply electronic service before the litigation;  
(3) The person to be served proactively provides an electronic address for receiving service in the statement of complaint, appeal, application, or statement of defense it submits;  
(4) The person to be served accepts the electronic service already completed by replying to confirm receipt, participating in the litigation, or other means, and does not clearly indicate its disagreement with electronic service.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院可以通过电话确认、诉讼平台在线确认、线下发送电子送达确认书等方式，确认受送达人是否同意电子送达，以及受送达人接收电子送达的具体方式和地址，并告知电子送达的适用范围、效力、送达地址变更方式以及其他需告知的送达事项。

**Article 30** The People's Court may confirm whether the person to be served consents to electronic service, as well as the specific method and address for the person to receive electronic service, by means of telephone confirmation, online confirmation on the Litigation Platform, or sending an electronic service confirmation letter offline, and shall inform the person of the scope of application, effect, method of changing the service address, and other service matters that need to be informed.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　人民法院向受送达人主动提供或者确认的电子地址送达的，送达信息到达电子地址所在系统时，即为送达。  
受送达人未提供或者未确认有效电子送达地址，人民法院向能够确认为受送达人本人的电子地址送达的，根据下列情形确定送达是否生效：  
（一）受送达人回复已收悉，或者根据送达内容已作出相应诉讼行为的，即为完成有效送达；  
（二）受送达人的电子地址所在系统反馈受送达人已阅知，或者有其他证据可以证明受送达人已经收悉的，推定完成有效送达，但受送达人能够证明存在系统错误、送达地址非本人使用或者非本人阅知等未收悉送达内容的情形除外。  
人民法院开展电子送达，应当在系统中全程留痕，并制作电子送达凭证。电子送达凭证具有送达回证效力。  
对同一内容的送达材料采取多种电子方式发送受送达人的，以最先完成的有效送达时间作为送达生效时间。

**Article 31** Where the People's Court serves documents to an electronic address voluntarily provided or confirmed by the recipient, the service shall be deemed completed when the service information reaches the system of such electronic address.  
Where the recipient fails to provide or confirm a valid electronic address for service, and the People's Court serves documents to an electronic address that can be confirmed as belonging to the recipient, the validity of the service shall be determined according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the recipient replies acknowledging receipt, or has taken corresponding procedural actions based on the content of the service, the service shall be deemed effectively completed;  
(2) If the system where the recipient's electronic address is located feedbacks that the recipient has accessed the service information, or there is other evidence proving that the recipient has received it, the service shall be presumed to be effectively completed, unless the recipient can prove that the service content was not received due to a system error, the service address not being used by the recipient, or the recipient not personally accessing the information, among other circumstances.  
When conducting electronic service, the People's Court shall keep a full record in the system and issue an electronic service certificate. The electronic service certificate shall have the same effect as a service receipt.  
Where service materials with the same content are transmitted to the recipient through multiple electronic methods, the time of the earliest effective service shall be deemed the time when the service takes effect.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　人民法院适用电子送达，可以同步通过短信、即时通讯工具、诉讼平台提示等方式，通知受送达人查阅、接收、下载相关送达材料。

**Article 32** When applying electronic service, the People's Court may simultaneously notify the recipient to view, receive, or download the relevant service materials via text messages, instant messaging tools, litigation platform notifications, or other means.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　适用在线诉讼的案件，各方诉讼主体可以通过在线确认、电子签章等方式，确认和签收调解协议、笔录、电子送达凭证及其他诉讼材料。

**Article 33** For cases subject to online litigation, each litigation participant may confirm and sign for mediation agreements, records, electronic service certificates, and other litigation materials through online confirmation, electronic signatures, or other means.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　适用在线诉讼的案件，人民法院应当在调解、证据交换、庭审、合议等诉讼环节同步形成电子笔录。电子笔录以在线方式核对确认后，与书面笔录具有同等法律效力。

**Article 34** For cases subject to online litigation, the People's Court shall simultaneously form electronic records during litigation procedures such as mediation, evidence exchange, court hearings, and deliberations. After the electronic records are verified and confirmed online, they shall have the same legal force as written records.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　适用在线诉讼的案件，人民法院应当利用技术手段随案同步生成电子卷宗，形成电子档案。电子档案的立卷、归档、存储、利用等，按照档案管理相关法律法规的规定执行。  
案件无纸质材料或者纸质材料已经全部转化为电子材料的，第一审人民法院可以采用电子卷宗代替纸质卷宗进行上诉移送。  
适用在线诉讼的案件存在纸质卷宗材料的，应当按照档案管理相关法律法规立卷、归档和保存。

**Article 35** For cases subject to online litigation, the People's Court shall use technical means to simultaneously generate electronic case files along with the case, forming an electronic archive. The creation, archiving, storage, and use of electronic archives shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of relevant laws and regulations on archive management.  
Where a case has no paper materials or all paper materials have been converted into electronic materials, the People's Court of first instance may use electronic case files instead of paper case files for the transfer of appeal.  
Where paper case file materials exist in a case subject to online litigation, the case files shall be created, archived, and preserved in accordance with relevant laws and regulations on archive management.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　执行裁决案件的在线立案、电子材料提交、执行和解、询问当事人、电子送达等环节，适用本规则的相关规定办理。  
人民法院可以通过财产查控系统、网络询价评估平台、网络拍卖平台、信用惩戒系统等，在线完成财产查明、查封、扣押、冻结、划扣、变价和惩戒等执行实施环节。

**Article 36** For enforcement adjudication cases, the relevant provisions of these Rules shall apply to the procedures of online case filing, submission of electronic materials, enforcement settlement, interrogation of parties, and electronic service.  
The People's Court may, through property investigation and control systems, online price inquiry and evaluation platforms, online auction platforms, credit punishment systems, and other means, complete online the enforcement implementation procedures such as property discovery, sealing up, distraint, freezing, deduction, conversion, and punishment.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　符合[本规定第三条](#art-3)第二项规定的刑事案件，经公诉人、当事人、辩护人同意，可以根据案件情况，采取在线方式讯问被告人、开庭审理、宣判等。  
案件采取在线方式审理的，按照以下情形分别处理：  
（一）被告人、罪犯被羁押的，可以在看守所、监狱等羁押场所在线出庭；  
（二）被告人、罪犯未被羁押的，因特殊原因确实无法到庭的，可以在人民法院指定的场所在线出庭；  
（三）证人、鉴定人一般应当在线下出庭，但法律和司法解释另有规定的除外。

**Article 37** For criminal cases that meet the conditions specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 3 of these Provisions, with the consent of the public prosecutor, parties, and defenders, the interrogation of the defendant, court hearings, pronouncement of judgment, etc., may be conducted online depending on the circumstances of the case.  
Where a case is tried online, it shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the defendant or convict is in custody, they may appear online from the detention facility such as a detention center or prison;  
(2) If the defendant or convict is not in custody but is genuinely unable to appear in court due to special reasons, they may appear online at a place designated by the People's Court;  
(3) Witnesses and expert evaluators shall generally appear in court offline, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　参与在线诉讼的相关主体应当遵守数据安全和个人信息保护的相关法律法规，履行数据安全和个人信息保护义务。除人民法院依法公开的以外，任何人不得违法违规披露、传播和使用在线诉讼数据信息。出现上述情形的，人民法院可以根据具体情况，依照法律和司法解释关于数据安全、个人信息保护以及妨害诉讼的规定追究相关单位和人员法律责任，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 38** Relevant entities participating in online litigation shall comply with relevant laws and regulations on data security and personal information protection, and fulfill their obligations for data security and personal information protection. Unless disclosed by the People's Court pursuant to law, no person may illegally disclose, disseminate, or use online litigation data or information. In the event of the above circumstances, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances, investigate the legal liability of the relevant entities and personnel in accordance with the provisions of laws and judicial interpretations on data security, personal information protection, and obstruction of litigation. If the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　本规则自2021年8月1日起施行。最高人民法院之前发布的司法解释涉及在线诉讼的规定与本规则不一致的，以本规则为准。

**Article 39** These Rules shall come into force on August 1, 2021. Where any provisions of the judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court concerning online litigation are inconsistent with these Rules, these Rules shall prevail.
