### 最高人民法院关于逾期付款违约金应当按照何种标准计算问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于逾期付款违约金应当按照何种标准计算问题的批复  
  
法释〔1999〕8号  
  
（1999年1月29日最高人民法院审判委员会第1042次会议通过，自1999年2月16日起施行。）  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院〔1998〕粤法经一行字第17号《关于逾期贷款如何计算利息问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对于合同当事人没有约定逾期付款违约金标准的，人民法院可以参照中国人民银行规定的金融机构计收逾期贷款利息的标准计算逾期付款违约金。中国人民银行调整金融机构计收逾期贷款利息的标准时，人民法院可以相应调整计算逾期付款违约金的计算标准。参照中国人民银行1996年4月30日发布的银发〔1996〕156号《关于降低金融机构存、贷款利率的通知》的规定，目前，逾期付款违约金标准可以按每日万分之四计算。  
  
本批复公布后，人民法院尚未审结的案件中有关计算逾期付款违约金的问题，按照本批复办理。本批复公布前，已经按我院1996年5月16日作出的法复〔1996〕7号《关于逾期付款违约金应当依据何种标准计算问题的批复》审结的案件不再变动。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your Court's Request for Instructions on How to Calculate Interest on Overdue Loans ([1998] Yue Fa Jing Yi Xing Zi No. 17) has been received. Upon review, the reply is as follows:  
Where the parties to a contract have not agreed upon the standard for liquidated damages for overdue payment, the People's Court may, by referring to the standard for calculating interest on overdue loans by financial institutions as prescribed by the People's Bank of China, calculate the liquidated damages for overdue payment. When the People's Bank of China adjusts the standard for calculating interest on overdue loans by financial institutions, the People's Court may correspondingly adjust the calculation standard for liquidated damages for overdue payment. Referring to the provisions of the Notice on Lowering the Deposit and Lending Interest Rates of Financial Institutions (Yin Fa [1996] No. 156) issued by the People's Bank of China on April 30, 1996, the current standard for liquidated damages for overdue payment may be calculated at 0.04% per day.  
After the promulgation of this Reply, issues concerning the calculation of liquidated damages for overdue payment in cases not yet concluded by the People's Court shall be handled in accordance with this Reply. For cases that have already been concluded pursuant to the Reply on the Issue of Which Standard Shall Be Used to Calculate Liquidated Damages for Overdue Payment (Fa Fu [1996] No. 7) issued by this Court on May 16, 1996, prior to the promulgation of this Reply, no changes shall be made.  
This is the reply.
