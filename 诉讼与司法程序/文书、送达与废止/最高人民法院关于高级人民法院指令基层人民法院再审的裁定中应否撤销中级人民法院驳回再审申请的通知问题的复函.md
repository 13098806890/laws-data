### 最高人民法院关于高级人民法院指令基层人民法院再审的裁定中应否撤销中级人民法院驳回再审申请的通知问题的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于高级人民法院指令基层人民法院再审  
  
的裁定中应否撤销中级人民法院驳回再审申请  
  
的通知问题的复函  
  
（93）民他字第12号  
  
陕西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1993）23号《关于高级人民法院指令基层人民法院再审的裁定应否撤销中级人民法院驳回再审申请的通知问题的请示》收悉。经研究认为，高级人民法院指令基层人民法院再审业经中级人民法院驳回再审申请的案件，在其再审裁定中，除写明再审期间中止原判决的执行外，还应一并撤销中级人民法院驳回再审申请的通知。  
  
1993年7月26日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the Higher People's Court's Order for Retrial by the Primary People's Court  
Shall Revoke the Notice of the Intermediate People's Court Dismissing the Retrial Application  
Notice Issue Reply  
(93) Min Ta Zi No. 12  
The Higher People's Court of Shaanxi Province:  
Your court's Request No. (1993) 23, titled 'Request for Instructions on the Issue of Whether the Higher People's Court's Order for Retrial by the Primary People's Court Shall Revoke the Notice of the Intermediate People's Court Dismissing the Retrial Application', has been received. After study, it is held that, in a case where the Higher People's Court orders the Primary People's Court to retry a case that has already been dismissed by the Intermediate People's Court for retrial application, in its retrial order, besides stating that the enforcement of the original judgment shall be suspended during the retrial, the notice of the Intermediate People's Court dismissing the retrial application shall also be revoked.  
July 26, 1993
