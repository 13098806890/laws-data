### 最高人民法院关于典当房屋在“文革”期间未能按期回赎，应作时效中止处理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于典当房屋在“文革”期间未能按期回赎，  
  
应作时效中止处理的批复  
  
（1986）民他字第3号  
  
北京市高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1985）京高法字第141号《关于宛若海诉安淑珍房屋典当一案的请示报告》收悉。据报告所述，安淑珍于一九六三年十月一日出典给宛若海三间房屋，典契载明：典期十年，典价四百五十元，如出典人到期无力回赎，由承典人按当时房价转典为卖。在履行期间，该房在“文化大革命”期间被收归公有，未能按期回赎。一九八四年十月安淑珍向宛若海提出赎房要求时，宛认为典期已过，拒绝回赎，并向通县人民法院起诉，要求确认该房产权归其所有。  
  
经我们研究认为：安淑珍出典的房屋，一九六六年九月已交由房管部门接收。由于当地政府有关部门一九六六年至一九八四年初停办回赎，致出典人在典期届满时，无法按照典契载明的期限回赎，这是在特殊的历史条件下造成的。因此，一九八四年十月安淑珍要求回赎时，不应将房屋归公和停办回赎的这段时间，计入回赎时效期内。至于你院提出增补赎金问题，如无其他特殊原因，回赎时一般应按契约规定的典价办理。  
  
一九八六年四月十一日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning the Treatment of Suspension of Time Limit for the Redemption of Pledged Houses Not Redeemed on Schedule During the "Cultural Revolution"  
(1986) Min Ta Zi No. 3  
Beijing Higher People's Court:  
Your Request for Instructions on the Case of Wan Ruohai v. An Shuzhen Concerning House Pledge, (1985) Jing Gao Fa Zi No. 141, has been received. According to the report, on October 1, 1963, An Shuzhen pledged three houses to Wan Ruohai. The pledge contract specified: the pledge period was ten years, the pledge price was 450 yuan, and if the pledgor was unable to redeem the houses upon expiration, the pledgee shall convert the pledge into a sale at the then current housing price. During the performance period, the houses were taken over as public property during the "Cultural Revolution" and could not be redeemed on schedule. In October 1984, when An Shuzhen requested redemption from Wan Ruohai, the latter considered the pledge period to have expired and refused redemption, and filed a lawsuit with Tongxian People's Court, requesting confirmation that the property rights of the houses belonged to him.  
After our study, we hold that: The houses pledged by An Shuzhen had been handed over to the housing administration department for takeover in September 1966. Since the relevant local government departments suspended redemption from 1966 to early 1984, the pledgor was unable to redeem the houses within the period specified in the pledge contract upon its expiry; this was caused under special historical circumstances. Therefore, when An Shuzhen requested redemption in October 1984, the period during which the houses were taken over as public property and redemption was suspended shall not be included within the time limit for redemption. As to the issue raised by your court regarding an increase in the redemption price, if there are no other special reasons, redemption shall generally be handled according to the pledge price stipulated in the contract.  
April 11, 1986
