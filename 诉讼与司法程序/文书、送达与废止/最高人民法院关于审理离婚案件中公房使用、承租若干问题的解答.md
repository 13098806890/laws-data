# 最高人民法院关于审理离婚案件中公房使用、承租若干问题的解答

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕4号  
**公布日期**：1996-02-05

---

### 正文

一、问：在离婚案件中，当事人对公房的使用、承租问题发生争议，人民法院可否予以处理？  
答：在离婚案件中，当事人对公房的使用、承租问题发生争议，自行协商不成，或者经当事人双方单位或有关部门调解不成的，人民法院应根据案件的具体情况，依法予以妥善处理。

**Article 1** : Question: In a divorce case, where the parties dispute the use and lease of public housing, may the People's Court handle the matter?  
Answer: In a divorce case, where the parties dispute the use and lease of public housing and fail to reach an agreement through negotiation, or where mediation by the units of the parties or relevant authorities fails, the People's Court shall, according to the specific circumstances of the case, handle the matter appropriately according to law.

二、问：夫妻共同居住的公房，在什么情况下，离婚后双方均可承租？  
答：夫妻共同居住的公房，具有下列情形之一的，离婚后，双方均可承租：  
（一）婚前由一方承租的公房，婚姻关系存续5年以上的；  
（二）婚前一方承租的本单位的房屋，离婚时，双方均为本单位职工的；  
（三）一方婚前借款投资建房取得的公房承租权，婚后夫妻共同偿还借款的；  
（四）婚后一方或双方申请取得公房承租权的；  
（五）婚前一方承租的公房，婚后因该承租房屋拆迁而取得房屋承租权的；  
（六）夫妻双方单位投资联建或联合购置的共有房屋的；  
（七）一方将其承租的本单位的房屋，交回本单位或交给另一方单位后，另一方单位另给调换房屋的；  
（八）婚前双方均租有公房，婚后合并调换房屋的；  
（九）其他应当认定为夫妻双方均可承租的情形。

**Article 2** : Question: Under what circumstances may both parties lease the public housing that the couple jointly resided in after divorce?  
Answer: Where the public housing that the couple jointly resided in falls under any of the following circumstances, both parties may lease it after divorce:  
(1) The public housing was leased by one party before marriage, and the marital relationship has lasted for five years or more;  
(2) The public housing was leased by one party from his/her own unit before marriage, and at the time of divorce, both parties are employees of the same unit;  
(3) One party obtained the lease right of public housing by borrowing funds to invest in construction before marriage, and the couple jointly repaid the debt after marriage;  
(4) One or both parties applied for and obtained the lease right of public housing after marriage;  
(5) The public housing was leased by one party before marriage, and the lease right of the housing was obtained due to the demolition of the leased house after marriage;  
(6) The housing is jointly owned by the units of both parties through joint investment in construction or joint purchase;  
(7) One party returned the public housing leased from his/her own unit to the unit or to the other party's unit, and the other party's unit allocated another house in exchange;  
(8) Both parties leased public housing before marriage and merged or exchanged housing after marriage;  
(9) Other circumstances that shall be deemed as allowing both parties to lease the housing.

三、问：对夫妻双方均可承租的公房，应依照什么原则处理？  
答：对夫妻双方均可承租的公房，应依照下列原则予以处理：  
（一）照顾抚养子女的一方；  
（二）男女双方在同等条件下，照顾女方；  
（三）照顾残疾或生活困难的一方；  
（四）照顾无过错一方。

**Article 3** : Question: What principles shall be followed in handling the public housing that both parties may lease?  
Answer: The public housing that both parties may lease shall be handled in accordance with the following principles:  
(1) Giving consideration to the party who raises the children;  
(2) Giving consideration to the female party under equal conditions between the male and female parties;  
(3) Giving consideration to the party who is disabled or has financial difficulties;  
(4) Giving consideration to the party without fault.

四、问：对夫妻双方均可承租的公房而由一方承租的，承租方对另一方是否给予经济补偿？  
答：对夫妻双方均可承租的公房而由一方承租的，承租方对另一方可给予适当的经济补偿。

**Article 4** : Question: Where public housing that both parties may lease is leased by one party, shall the lessee provide economic compensation to the other party?  
Answer: Where public housing that both parties may lease is leased by one party, the lessee may provide appropriate economic compensation to the other party.

五、问：夫妻双方均可承租的公房能够隔开分室居住使用的，可否由双方分别租住？  
答：夫妻双方均可承租的公房，如其面积较大能够隔开分室居住使用的，可由双方分别租住；对可以另调房屋分别租住或承租方给另一方解决住房的，可予准许。

**Article 5** : Question: Where the public housing that both parties may lease can be separated into rooms for separate living, may both parties lease separately?  
Answer: Where the public housing that both parties may lease has a large enough area to be separated into rooms for separate living, both parties may lease separately; where alternative housing can be allocated for separate leasing or the lessee can provide housing for the other party, such arrangement may be permitted.

六、问：离婚时，一方对另一方婚前承租的公房无权承租的，可否暂时居住？  
答：离婚时，一方对另一方婚前承租的公房无权承租而解决住房确有困难的，人民法院可调解或判决其暂时居住，暂住期限一般不超过两年。暂住期间，暂住方应交纳与房屋租金等额的使用费及其他必要的费用。

**Article 6** : Question: At the time of divorce, if one party has no right to lease the public housing that the other party leased before marriage, may such party temporarily reside in it?  
Answer: At the time of divorce, if one party has no right to lease the public housing that the other party leased before marriage and indeed has difficulty in finding housing, the People's Court may mediate or adjudge that such party temporarily reside in it, with the period of temporary residence generally not exceeding two years. During the period of temporary residence, the temporary resident shall pay a usage fee equivalent to the housing rent and other necessary expenses.

七、问：离婚时，一方对另一方婚前承租的公房无权承租而另行租房经济上确有困难的，如何处理？  
答：离婚时，一方对另一方婚前承租的公房无权承租，另行租房经济上确有困难的，如承租公房一方有负担能力，应给予一次性经济帮助。

**Article 7** : Question: At the time of divorce, if one party has no right to lease the public housing that the other party leased before marriage and indeed has financial difficulty in renting another apartment, how shall this be handled?  
Answer: At the time of divorce, if one party has no right to lease the public housing that the other party leased before marriage and indeed has financial difficulty in renting another apartment, if the party who leases the public housing has the ability to bear the expense, such party shall provide one-time financial assistance.

八、问：在调整和变更单位自管房屋租赁关系时，是否需征得自管房单位的同意？  
答：人民法院在调整和变更单位自管房屋（包括单位委托房地产管理部门代管的房屋）的租赁关系时，应征求自管房单位的意见。经调解或判决变更房屋租赁关系的，承租人应依照有关规定办理房屋变更登记手续。

**Article 8** : Question: When adjusting or changing the lease relationship of a unit's self-managed housing, is it necessary to obtain the consent of the unit managing the housing?  
Answer: When the People's Court adjusts or changes the lease relationship of a unit's self-managed housing (including housing entrusted by the unit to the real estate management department for management), it shall seek the opinions of the unit managing the housing. Where the lease relationship is changed through mediation or adjudication, the lessee shall undergo the housing change registration procedures in accordance with relevant regulations.

九、问：对夫妻双方共同出资而取得“部分产权”的房屋，应如何处理？  
答：对夫妻共同出资而取得“部分产权”的房屋，人民法院可参照上述有关解答，予以妥善处理。分得房屋“部分产权”的一方，一般应按所得房屋产权的比例，依照离婚时当地政府有关部门公布的同类住房标准价，给予对方一半价值的补偿。

**Article 9** : Question: How shall the housing obtained by both parties through joint contribution and acquiring "partial property rights" be handled?  
Answer: For the housing obtained by the couple through joint contribution and acquiring "partial property rights", the People's Court may handle it appropriately by reference to the relevant replies above. The party who obtains the "partial property rights" of the housing shall generally, in proportion to the property rights acquired, provide compensation to the other party of half the value based on the standard price of similar housing published by the relevant local government departments at the time of divorce.

十、问：对夫妻双方均争房屋“部分产权”的，可否采取竞价方式解决？  
答：对夫妻双方均争房屋“部分产权”的，如双方同意或者双方经济、住房条件基本相同，可采取竞价方式解决。

**Article 10** : Question: Where both parties compete for the "partial property rights" of the housing, may a bidding method be adopted to resolve the issue?  
Answer: Where both parties compete for the "partial property rights" of the housing, if both parties agree or their economic and housing conditions are basically the same, a bidding method may be adopted to resolve the issue.
