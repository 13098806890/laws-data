# 最高人民法院关于人民法院立案工作的暂行规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1997〕7号  
**公布日期**：1997-04-21

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院的立案工作遵循便利人民群众诉讼，便利人民法院审判的原则。

**Article 1** The case-filing work of the People's Court shall follow the principles of facilitating the people's mass litigation and facilitating the adjudication of the People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　上级人民法院对下级人民法院的立案工作进行监督和指导。基层人民法院对人民法庭的立案工作进行检查和指导。

**Article 2** The Higher People's Court shall supervise and guide the case-filing work of the lower People's Courts. The Primary People's Court shall inspect and guide the case-filing work of the People's Tribunals.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院立案工作的任务，是保障当事人依法行使诉讼权利，保证人民法院正确、及时审理案件。

**Article 3** The task of the case-filing work of the People's Court is to safeguard the parties' exercise of litigation rights according to law and to ensure that the People's Court correctly and promptly adjudicates cases.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院对当事人提起的诉讼依法进行审查，符合受理条件的应当及时立案。

**Article 4** The People's Court shall examine the litigation brought by the parties according to law, and shall promptly file a case if it meets the conditions for acceptance.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院实行立案与审判分开的原则。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall implement the principle of separation between case-filing and adjudication.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院的立案工作由专门机构负责，可以设在告诉申诉审判庭内；不设告诉申诉审判庭的，可以单独设立。

**Article 6** The case-filing work of the People's Court shall be undertaken by a specialized organ, which may be established within the Petition and Appeal Adjudication Division; if no Petition and Appeal Adjudication Division is established, it may be established separately.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　立案工作的范围  
（一）审查民事、经济纠纷、行政案件的起诉，决定立案或者裁定不予受理；审查刑事自诉案件的起诉，决定立案或者裁定驳回；对刑事公诉案件进行立案登记。  
（二）对下级人民法院移送的刑事、民事、经济纠纷、行政上诉案件和人民检察院对第一审刑事判决、裁定提出的抗诉案件进行立案登记。  
（三）对本院决定再审、上级人民法院指令再审和人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的案件进行立案登记。  
（四）负责应由人民法院依法受理的其他案件的立案工作。  
（五）计算并通知原告、上诉人预交案件受理费。

**Article 7** Scope of case-filing work  
(1) To examine the prosecution of civil, economic dispute, and administrative cases, decide to file a case or render a ruling of non-acceptance; to examine the prosecution of criminal private prosecution cases, decide to file a case or render a ruling of dismissal; to register the case-filing of criminal public prosecution cases.  
(2) To register the case-filing of criminal, civil, economic dispute, and administrative appeal cases transferred by lower People's Courts and of cases protested by the People's Procuratorate against first-instance criminal judgments or rulings.  
(3) To register the case-filing of cases where this court decides a retrial, where a higher People's Court orders a retrial, and where the People's Procuratorate files a protest under the trial supervision procedure.  
(4) To be responsible for the case-filing work of other cases that shall be accepted by the People's Court according to law.  
(5) To calculate and notify the plaintiff or appellant to prepay the case acceptance fee.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院收到当事人的起诉，应当依照法律和司法解释规定的案件受理条件进行审查：  
（一）起诉人应当具备法律规定的主体资格；  
（二）应当有明确的被告；  
（三）有具体的诉讼请求和事实根据；  
（四）属于人民法院受理案件的范围和受诉人民法院管辖。  
提起刑事自诉、刑事附带民事诉讼的，还应当符合《最高人民法院关于执行〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉若干问题的解释（试行）》中关于受理条件的规定。

**Article 8** Upon receiving a party's prosecution, the People's Court shall examine it in accordance with the case acceptance conditions provided by law and judicial interpretations:  
(1) The prosecutor shall have the subject qualification provided by law;  
(2) There shall be a definite defendant;  
(3) There shall be specific claims and factual basis;  
(4) It shall fall within the scope of cases accepted by the People's Court and the jurisdiction of the court receiving the prosecution.  
Where a criminal private prosecution or a criminal incidental civil action is initiated, it shall also comply with the provisions on acceptance conditions in the Interpretation (Trial) of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院审查立案中，发现原告或者自诉人证明其诉讼请求的主要证据不具备的，应当及时通知其补充证据。收到诉状的时间，从当事人补交有关证据材料之日起开始计算。

**Article 9** In examining case-filing, if the People's Court finds that the plaintiff or private prosecutor does not have the main evidence to prove his or her claims, it shall promptly notify him or her to supplement evidence. The time of receipt of the complaint shall be calculated from the date on which the party submits the relevant evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院收到诉状和有关证据，应当进行登记，并向原告或者自诉人出具收据。收据中应当注明证据名称、原件或复制件、收到时间、份数和页数，由负责审查起诉的审判人员和原告、自诉人签名或者盖章。对于不予立案或者原告、自诉人在立案前撤回起诉的，应当将起诉材料退还，并由当事人签收。

**Article 10** Upon receiving the complaint and relevant evidence, the People's Court shall register them and issue a receipt to the plaintiff or private prosecutor. The receipt shall indicate the name of the evidence, whether it is an original or a copy, the time of receipt, the number of copies and pages, and shall be signed or sealed by the adjudicator responsible for examining the prosecution and by the plaintiff or private prosecutor. Where a case is not filed or the plaintiff or private prosecutor withdraws the prosecution before case-filing, the prosecution materials shall be returned and shall be signed for by the party.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对经审查不符合法定受理条件，原告坚持起诉的，应当裁定不予受理；自诉人坚持起诉的，应当裁定驳回。

**Article 11** Where after examination the prosecution does not meet the statutory acceptance conditions, and the plaintiff insists on prosecuting, a ruling of non-acceptance shall be rendered; where a private prosecutor insists on prosecuting, a ruling of dismissal shall be rendered.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　不予受理和驳回起诉的裁定书由负责审查起诉的审判人员制作，报庭长或者院长审批。裁定书由负责审查起诉的审判员、书记员署名，加盖人民法院印章。

**Article 12** The written ruling of non-acceptance or dismissal of prosecution shall be prepared by the adjudicator responsible for examining the prosecution and shall be submitted to the division chief or the president for approval. The written ruling shall be signed by the adjudicator and the clerk responsible for examining the prosecution and shall be affixed with the seal of the People's Court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经审查认为起诉符合受理条件的，根据案件的不同情况，由负责审查起诉的审判人员决定立案或者报庭长审批。重大疑难案件报院长审批或者经审判委员会讨论决定。

**Article 13** Where after examination the prosecution is deemed to meet the acceptance conditions, depending on the different circumstances of the case, the adjudicator responsible for examining the prosecution shall decide to file the case or submit it to the division chief for approval. For major and difficult cases, it shall be submitted to the president for approval or decided by the adjudication committee after discussion.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　起诉经审查决定立案后，应当编立案号，填写立案登记表，计算案件受理费，向原告或者自诉人发出案件受理通知书，并书面通知原告预交案件受理费。

**Article 14** After the prosecution is examined and a decision is made to file the case, a case number shall be assigned, a case-filing registration form shall be filled out, the case acceptance fee shall be calculated, a notice of case acceptance shall be issued to the plaintiff or private prosecutor, and the plaintiff shall be notified in writing to prepay the case acceptance fee.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　决定立案后，立案机构应当在二日内将案件移送有关审判庭审理，并办理移交手续，注明移交日期。经审查决定受理或立案登记的日期为立案日期。

**Article 15** After a decision is made to file the case, the case-filing organ shall, within two days, transfer the case to the relevant adjudication division for trial and complete the transfer procedures, indicating the date of transfer. The date on which acceptance is decided or case-filing is registered after examination shall be the case-filing date.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　刑事自诉案件应当在收到自诉状、口头告诉第二日起十五日内决定立案或者裁定驳回起诉；民事、经济纠纷案件应当在收到起诉状、口头告诉之日起七日内决定立案或者裁定不予受理；行政案件应当在收到起诉状之日起七日内决定立案或者裁定不予受理。

**Article 16** For criminal private prosecution cases, a decision to file the case or a ruling to dismiss the prosecution shall be made within fifteen days from the day after receipt of the private complaint or oral complaint; for civil and economic dispute cases, a decision to file the case or a ruling of non-acceptance shall be made within seven days from the date of receipt of the complaint or oral complaint; for administrative cases, a decision to file the case or a ruling of non-acceptance shall be made within seven days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　审判庭对立案机构移送的案件认为不属本庭职责范围的，应当及时提出，报院长决定。

**Article 17** If the adjudication division considers that a case transferred by the case-filing organ does not fall within its scope of duties, it shall promptly raise the issue and report to the president for decision.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法庭经审查认为符合受理条件的起诉，报庭长批准立案；当事人直接向基层人民法院起诉的，基层人民法院应当审查受理。  
人民法庭决定立案后，应当将当事人的姓名、单位、案由、简要案情报基层人民法院统一编立案号。  
对符合受理条件的起诉人民法庭不予立案的，基层人民法院应当决定立案，交由人民法庭审理。

**Article 18** Where the People's Tribunal deems after examination that the prosecution meets the acceptance conditions, it shall report to the division chief for approval of case-filing; where a party directly prosecutes before the Primary People's Court, the Primary People's Court shall examine and accept it.  
After the People's Tribunal decides to file the case, it shall report the name, unit, cause of action, and brief case summary of the party to the Primary People's Court for unified assignment of a case number.  
If the People's Tribunal fails to file a case for a prosecution that meets the acceptance conditions, the Primary People's Court shall decide to file the case and transfer it to the People's Tribunal for trial.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对当事人不服一审判决、裁定提出上诉的案件，第一审人民法院应当及时办妥送达上诉状副本等有关手续，将案卷材料连同二审案件诉讼费缴费凭证等一并移送第二审人民法院。

**Article 19** For a case where a party appeals against a first-instance judgment or ruling, the first-instance People's Court shall promptly complete the procedures for service of the copy of the appeal petition and other relevant procedures, and transfer the case file materials together with the litigation fee payment receipt for the second-instance case to the second-instance People's Court.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　第二审人民法院立案机构收到第一审人民法院移送的上诉材料及一审案件卷宗材料，应当查对以下内容：  
（一）上诉状、一审裁判文书齐全；一审卷宗数应与案件移送函标明的数量相符。  
（二）上诉人递交上诉状的时间在法定上诉期限以内；虽然超过法定上诉期限，但提交了因不可抗拒的事由或者具有其他正当理由申请顺延上诉期限的书面材料。  
（三）附有上诉案件受理费单据或者上诉人关于缓、减、免交上诉费用的申请。对卷宗、材料不齐备的，应当及时通知第一审人民法院补充。

**Article 20** Upon receiving the appeal materials and the first-instance case file materials transferred by the first-instance People's Court, the case-filing organ of the second-instance People's Court shall check the following:  
(1) The appeal petition and the first-instance judgment document are complete; the number of first-instance case files shall match the number indicated in the case transfer letter.  
(2) The time when the appellant submitted the appeal petition is within the statutory appeal period; although it exceeds the statutory appeal period, the appellant submitted a written application for extension of the appeal period due to force majeure or other legitimate reasons.  
(3) The appeal case fee receipt or the appellant's application for deferment, reduction, or exemption of the appeal fee is attached. If the files or materials are incomplete, the first-instance People's Court shall be promptly notified to supplement them.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　第二审人民法院立案机构经查对有关材料无误的，应当填写立案登记表，编立案号，向当事人发送案件受理通知书和上诉案件应诉通知书，并将案卷材料于立案登记的第二日移交有关审判庭。

**Article 21** After checking the relevant materials and confirming they are correct, the case-filing organ of the second-instance People's Court shall fill out the case-filing registration form, assign a case number, send the party a notice of case acceptance and a notice of response to the appeal case, and transfer the case file materials to the relevant adjudication division on the day after the case-filing registration.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对当事人提出的申诉或者再审申请，认为符合受理条件的，应当登记后立卷审查。

**Article 22** Where a party's petition or application for retrial is deemed to meet the acceptance conditions, it shall be registered and then filed for examination.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对具有以下情形的再审案件，应当移送有关审判庭审理：  
（一）经审查认为申诉或者再审申请符合法律规定的条件，并报经院长批准再审的；  
（二）本院院长提交审判委员会讨论决定再审的；  
（三）上级人民法院指令再审的；  
（四）人民检察院提出抗诉的。

**Article 23** Retrial cases under the following circumstances shall be transferred to the relevant adjudication division for trial:  
(1) Those where, after examination, the petition or application for retrial is deemed to meet the conditions provided by law and has been submitted to the president for approval of retrial;  
(2) Those where the president of this court submits to the adjudication committee for discussion and decision on retrial;  
(3) Those where a higher People's Court orders a retrial;  
(4) Those where the People's Procuratorate files a protest.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　执行案件的立案工作可参照本规定执行。

**Article 24** The case-filing work for enforcement cases may be implemented by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　各高级人民法院、解放军军事法院可以根据本规定制定实施细则，并报最高人民法院备案。  
以前有关立案工作的规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
1997年4月21日

**Article 25** Each Higher People's Court and the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army may formulate implementation rules based on these Provisions and report them to the Supreme People's Court for record.  
Where previous provisions regarding case-filing work are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
April 21, 1997
