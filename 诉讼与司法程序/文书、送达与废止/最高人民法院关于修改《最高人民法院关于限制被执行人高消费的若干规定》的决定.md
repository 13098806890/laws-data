# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕17号  
**公布日期**：2015-07-06  
**生效日期**：2015-07-22

> 最高人民法院《关于修改〈最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定〉的决定》已于2015年7月6日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1657次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年7月22日起施行。 2015年7月20日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》的决定 法释〔2015〕17号 根据最高人民法院审判委员会第1657次会议决定，对《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》作如下修改： 一、将《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》修改为：“《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费及有关消费的若干规定》。” 二、将第一条修改为：“被执行人未按执行通知书指定的期间履行生效法律文书确定的给付义务的，人民法院可以采取限制消费措施，限制其高消费及非生活或者经营必需的有关消费。” 第一条　增加第二款：“纳入失信被执行人名单的被执行人，人民法院应当对其采取限制消费措施。” 三、将第二条修改为：“人民法院决定采取限制消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。” 四、将第三条第一款修改为：“被执行人为自然人的，被采取限制消费措施后，不得有以下高消费及非生活和工作必需的消费行为：” 第一款第（九）项修改为：“（九）乘坐G字头动车组列车全部座位、其他动车组列车一等以上座位等其他非生活和工作必需的消费行为。” 第二款修改为：“被执行人为单位的，被采取限制消费措施后，被执行人及其法定代表人、主要负责人、影响债务履行的直接责任人员、实际控制人不得实施前款规定的行为。因私消费以个人财产实施前款规定行为的，可以向执行法院提出申请。执行法院审查属实的，应予准许。” 五、将第四条修改为：“限制消费措施一般由申请执行人提出书面申请，经人民法院审查决定；必要时人民法院可以依职权决定。” 六、将第五条修改为：“人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，应当向被执行人发出限制消费令。限制消费令由人民法院院长签发。限制消费令应当载明限制消费的期间、项目、法律后果等内容。”

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　增加第二款：“纳入失信被执行人名单的被执行人，人民法院应当对其采取限制消费措施。”  
三、将第二条修改为：“人民法院决定采取限制消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。”  
四、将第三条第一款修改为：“被执行人为自然人的，被采取限制消费措施后，不得有以下高消费及非生活和工作必需的消费行为：”  
第一款第（九）项修改为：“（九）乘坐G字头动车组列车全部座位、其他动车组列车一等以上座位等其他非生活和工作必需的消费行为。”  
第二款修改为：“被执行人为单位的，被采取限制消费措施后，被执行人及其法定代表人、主要负责人、影响债务履行的直接责任人员、实际控制人不得实施前款规定的行为。因私消费以个人财产实施前款规定行为的，可以向执行法院提出申请。执行法院审查属实的，应予准许。”  
五、将第四条修改为：“限制消费措施一般由申请执行人提出书面申请，经人民法院审查决定；必要时人民法院可以依职权决定。”  
六、将第五条修改为：“人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，应当向被执行人发出限制消费令。限制消费令由人民法院院长签发。限制消费令应当载明限制消费的期间、项目、法律后果等内容。”  
七、将第六条修改为：“人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，可以根据案件需要和被执行人的情况向有义务协助调查、执行的单位送达协助执行通知书，也可以在相关媒体上进行公告。”  
八、将第七条修改为：“限制消费令的公告费用由被执行人负担；申请执行人申请在媒体公告的，应当垫付公告费用。”  
九、将第八条修改为：“被限制消费的被执行人因生活或者经营必需而进行本规定禁止的消费活动的，应当向人民法院提出申请，获批准后方可进行。”  
十、将第九条修改为：“在限制消费期间，被执行人提供确实有效的担保或者经申请执行人同意的，人民法院可以解除限制消费令；被执行人履行完毕生效法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院应当在[本规定第六条](#art-6)通知或者公告的范围内及时以通知或者公告解除限制消费令。”  
十一、将第十条修改为：“人民法院应当设置举报电话或者邮箱，接受申请执行人和社会公众对被限制消费的被执行人违反[本规定第三条](#art-3)的举报，并进行审查认定。”  
十二、将第十一条第一款修改为：“被执行人违反限制消费令进行消费的行为属于拒不履行人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的行为，经查证属实的，依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定，予以拘留、罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，追究其刑事责任。”  
第二款修改为：“有关单位在收到人民法院协助执行通知书后，仍允许被执行人进行高消费及非生活或者经营必需的有关消费的，人民法院可以依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定，追究其法律责任。”  
十三、将第十二条删除。  
根据本决定，将《最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定》作相应修改，重新公布。  
最高人民法院  
关于限制被执行人高消费及有关消费的若干规定  
（2010年5月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1487次会议通过，根据2015年7月6日最高人民法院审判委员会第1657次会议通过的最高人民法院《关于修改〈最高人民法院关于限制被执行人高消费的若干规定〉的决定》第一次修正，该修正自2015年7月22日起施行）  
为进一步加大执行力度，推动社会信用机制建设，最大限度保护申请执行人和被执行人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的有关规定，结合人民法院民事执行工作的实践经验，制定本规定。

**Article 1** A second paragraph is added: "For a person subject to enforcement who is included in the list of persons subject to enforcement for dishonesty, the People's Court shall take measures restricting consumption against him/her."  
3. Article 2 is amended to read: "When the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it shall consider factors such as whether the person subject to enforcement has acts of passive performance, evasion of enforcement, or resistance to enforcement, as well as the person's performance capability."  
4. Paragraph 1 of Article 3 is amended to read: "Where the person subject to enforcement is a natural person, after being subject to measures restricting consumption, he/she shall not engage in the following high-consumption acts and consumption acts not necessary for daily life or work:"  
Subparagraph (9) of paragraph 1 is amended to read: "(9) taking all seats on G-series bullet trains or first-class or above seats on other bullet trains, and other consumption acts not necessary for daily life or work."  
Paragraph 2 is amended to read: "Where the person subject to enforcement is an entity, after being subject to measures restricting consumption, the person subject to enforcement and its legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons affecting debt performance, and actual controller shall not commit the acts specified in the preceding paragraph. Where a consumption act specified in the preceding paragraph is conducted in a private capacity with personal property, an application may be filed with the enforcement court. If the enforcement court verifies the application to be true, it shall grant approval."  
5. Article 4 is amended to read: "Measures restricting consumption shall generally be applied by the person applying for enforcement by way of a written application, and the People's Court shall review and decide; if necessary, the People's Court may decide ex officio."  
6. Article 5 is amended to read: "Where the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it shall issue a restriction on consumption order to the person subject to enforcement. The restriction on consumption order shall be signed by the President of the People's Court. The restriction on consumption order shall specify the period, items, and legal consequences of the consumption restrictions."  
7. Article 6 is amended to read: "Where the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it may, based on the needs of the case and the circumstances of the person subject to enforcement, serve a notice of assistance in enforcement on entities obliged to assist in investigation and enforcement, or may publish a public announcement in relevant media."  
8. Article 7 is amended to read: "The costs of public announcement of the restriction on consumption order shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement; where the person applying for enforcement applies for public announcement in media, he/she shall advance the announcement costs."  
9. Article 8 is amended to read: "Where the person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restrictions engages in consumption activities prohibited by these Provisions due to necessities of life or business, he/she shall file an application with the People's Court, and may only proceed after obtaining approval."  
10. Article 9 is amended to read: "During the period of consumption restrictions, if the person subject to enforcement provides authentic and effective guarantee or obtains the consent of the person applying for enforcement, the People's Court may lift the restriction on consumption order; if the person subject to enforcement has fully performed the obligations specified in the effective legal instruments, the People's Court shall, within the scope of the notice or public announcement under Article 6 of these Provisions, promptly lift the restriction on consumption order by way of notice or public announcement."  
11. Article 10 is amended to read: "The People's Court shall set up a reporting telephone or email address to receive reports from the person applying for enforcement and the public concerning violations of Article 3 of these Provisions by the person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restrictions, and shall review and make a determination."  
12. Paragraph 1 of Article 11 is amended to read: "Where the person subject to enforcement violates the restriction on consumption order by engaging in consumption activities, such conduct constitutes a refusal to perform an effective judgment or ruling of the People's Court. If verified, the person shall be detained or fined in accordance with Article 111 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued."  
Paragraph 2 is amended to read: "Where a relevant entity, after receiving the notice of assistance in enforcement from the People's Court, still permits the person subject to enforcement to engage in high consumption or related consumption not necessary for life or business, the People's Court may pursue legal liability against it in accordance with Article 114 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China."  
13. Article 12 is deleted.  
In accordance with this Decision, the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Restricting High Consumption of Persons Subject to Enforcement are revised accordingly and re-promulgated.

Supreme People's Court  
Several Provisions on Restricting High Consumption and Related Consumption of Persons Subject to Enforcement  
(Adopted at the 1487th Session of the Judicial Committee of the Supreme People's Court on May 17, 2010; first amended in accordance with the Decision of the Supreme People's Court on Amending the Several Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Restricting High Consumption of Persons Subject to Enforcement, adopted at the 1657th Session of the Judicial Committee of the Supreme People's Court on July 6, 2015; the amendment came into force on July 22, 2015)  
In order to further strengthen enforcement efforts, promote the construction of a social credit mechanism, and maximize the protection of the lawful rights and interests of persons applying for enforcement and persons subject to enforcement, these Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and in light of the practical experience of the People's Courts in civil enforcement work.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施时，应当考虑被执行人是否有消极履行、规避执行或者抗拒执行的行为以及被执行人的履行能力等因素。

**Article 1** Where a person subject to enforcement fails to perform the obligation of payment specified in an effective legal instrument within the period designated by the enforcement notice, the People's Court may take measures restricting consumption, restricting his/her high consumption and related consumption not necessary for daily life or business.  
For a person subject to enforcement who is included in the list of persons subject to enforcement for dishonesty, the People's Court shall take measures restricting consumption against him/her.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被执行人为自然人的，被采取限制消费措施后，不得有以下高消费及非生活和工作必需的消费行为：  
（一）乘坐交通工具时，选择飞机、列车软卧、轮船二等以上舱位；  
（二）在星级以上宾馆、酒店、夜总会、高尔夫球场等场所进行高消费；  
（三）购买不动产或者新建、扩建、高档装修房屋；  
（四）租赁高档写字楼、宾馆、公寓等场所办公；  
（五）购买非经营必需车辆；  
（六）旅游、度假；  
（七）子女就读高收费私立学校；  
（八）支付高额保费购买保险理财产品；  
（九）乘坐G字头动车组列车全部座位、其他动车组列车一等以上座位等其他非生活和工作必需的消费行为。  
被执行人为单位的，被采取限制消费措施后，被执行人及其法定代表人、主要负责人、影响债务履行的直接责任人员、实际控制人不得实施前款规定的行为。因私消费以个人财产实施前款规定行为的，可以向执行法院提出申请。执行法院审查属实的，应予准许。

**Article 2** When the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it shall consider factors such as whether the person subject to enforcement has acts of passive performance, evasion of enforcement, or resistance to enforcement, as well as the person's performance capability.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　限制消费措施一般由申请执行人提出书面申请，经人民法院审查决定；必要时人民法院可以依职权决定。

**Article 3** Where the person subject to enforcement is a natural person, after being subject to measures restricting consumption, he/she shall not engage in the following high-consumption acts and consumption acts not necessary for daily life or work:  
(1) choosing aircraft, soft sleeper on trains, or first-class or above cabins on ships when taking transportation;  
(2) engaging in high consumption at places such as star-rated hotels, restaurants, nightclubs, and golf courses;  
(3) purchasing real estate or constructing, expanding, or luxuriously decorating houses;  
(4) leasing high-grade office buildings, hotels, apartments, or other premises for office use;  
(5) purchasing vehicles not necessary for business operation;  
(6) traveling or vacationing;  
(7) enrolling children in high-tuition private schools;  
(8) purchasing insurance financial products with high premiums;  
(9) taking all seats on G-series bullet trains or first-class or above seats on other bullet trains, and other consumption acts not necessary for daily life or work.  
Where the person subject to enforcement is an entity, after being subject to measures restricting consumption, the person subject to enforcement and its legal representative, principal responsible person, directly responsible persons affecting debt performance, and actual controller shall not commit the acts specified in the preceding paragraph. Where a consumption act specified in the preceding paragraph is conducted in a private capacity with personal property, an application may be filed with the enforcement court. If the enforcement court verifies the application to be true, it shall grant approval.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，应当向被执行人发出限制消费令。限制消费令由人民法院院长签发。限制消费令应当载明限制消费的期间、项目、法律后果等内容。

**Article 4** Measures restricting consumption shall generally be applied by the person applying for enforcement by way of a written application, and the People's Court shall review and decide; if necessary, the People's Court may decide ex officio.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院决定采取限制消费措施的，可以根据案件需要和被执行人的情况向有义务协助调查、执行的单位送达协助执行通知书，也可以在相关媒体上进行公告。

**Article 5** Where the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it shall issue a restriction on consumption order to the person subject to enforcement. The restriction on consumption order shall be signed by the President of the People's Court. The restriction on consumption order shall specify the period, items, and legal consequences of the consumption restrictions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　限制消费令的公告费用由被执行人负担；申请执行人申请在媒体公告的，应当垫付公告费用。

**Article 6** Where the People's Court decides to take measures restricting consumption, it may, based on the needs of the case and the circumstances of the person subject to enforcement, serve a notice of assistance in enforcement on entities obliged to assist in investigation and enforcement, or may publish a public announcement in relevant media.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　被限制消费的被执行人因生活或者经营必需而进行本规定禁止的消费活动的，应当向人民法院提出申请，获批准后方可进行。

**Article 7** The costs of public announcement of the restriction on consumption order shall be borne by the person subject to enforcement; where the person applying for enforcement applies for public announcement in media, he/she shall advance the announcement costs.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　在限制消费期间，被执行人提供确实有效的担保或者经申请执行人同意的，人民法院可以解除限制消费令；被执行人履行完毕生效法律文书确定的义务的，人民法院应当在[本规定第六条](#art-6)通知或者公告的范围内及时以通知或者公告解除限制消费令。

**Article 8** Where the person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restrictions engages in consumption activities prohibited by these Provisions due to necessities of life or business, he/she shall file an application with the People's Court, and may only proceed after obtaining approval.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院应当设置举报电话或者邮箱，接受申请执行人和社会公众对被限制消费的被执行人违反[本规定第三条](#art-3)的举报，并进行审查认定。

**Article 9** During the period of consumption restrictions, if the person subject to enforcement provides authentic and effective guarantee or obtains the consent of the person applying for enforcement, the People's Court may lift the restriction on consumption order; if the person subject to enforcement has fully performed the obligations specified in the effective legal instruments, the People's Court shall, within the scope of the notice or public announcement under Article 6 of these Provisions, promptly lift the restriction on consumption order by way of notice or public announcement.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被执行人违反限制消费令进行消费的行为属于拒不履行人民法院已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定的行为，经查证属实的，依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)的规定，予以拘留、罚款；情节严重，构成犯罪的，追究其刑事责任。  
有关单位在收到人民法院协助执行通知书后，仍允许被执行人进行高消费及非生活或者经营必需的有关消费的，人民法院可以依照[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定，追究其法律责任。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall set up a reporting telephone or email address to receive reports from the person applying for enforcement and the public concerning violations of Article 3 of these Provisions by the person subject to enforcement subject to consumption restrictions, and shall review and make a determination.
