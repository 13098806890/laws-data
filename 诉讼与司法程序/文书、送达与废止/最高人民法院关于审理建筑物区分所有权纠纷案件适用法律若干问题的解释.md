# 最高人民法院关于审理建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2009-03-23  
**生效日期**：2009-10-01

> 第1464次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院 审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改 〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国 工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定》修正）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　依法登记取得或者依据[民法典第二百二十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-229)至第二百三十一条规定取得建筑物专有部分所有权的人，应当认定为民法典第二编第六章所称的业主。  
基于与建设单位之间的商品房买卖民事法律行为，已经合法占有建筑物专有部分，但尚未依法办理所有权登记的人，可以认定为民法典第二编第六章所称的业主。

**Article 1** A person who has acquired the ownership of a condominium part of a building by registration according to law or pursuant to Articles 229 to 231 of the Civil Code shall be deemed as the owner referred to in Chapter 6 of Part II of the Civil Code.  
A person who has legally taken possession of a condominium part of a building based on the civil juristic act of a commodity housing sale with the construction unit, but has not yet registered the ownership according to law, may be deemed as the owner referred to in Chapter 6 of Part II of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　建筑区划内符合下列条件的房屋，以及车位、摊位等特定空间，应当认定为民法典第二编第六章所称的专有部分：  
（一）具有构造上的独立性，能够明确区分；  
（二）具有利用上的独立性，可以排他使用；  
（三）能够登记成为特定业主所有权的客体。  
规划上专属于特定房屋，且建设单位销售时已经根据规划列入该特定房屋买卖合同中的露台等，应当认定为前款所称的专有部分的组成部分。  
本条第一款所称房屋，包括整栋建筑物。

**Article 2** The houses and specific spaces such as parking spaces and stalls within the building division that meet the following conditions shall be deemed as the condominium parts referred to in Chapter 6 of Part II of the Civil Code:  
(1) Having structural independence and capable of being clearly distinguished;  
(2) Having functional independence and capable of being used on an exclusive basis;  
(3) Capable of being registered as the object of a specific owner's ownership.  
Terraces and the like that are specifically planned for a particular house and have been included in the sales contract of such particular house by the construction unit at the time of sale according to the plan shall be deemed as constituent parts of the condominium part referred to in the preceding paragraph.  
The houses referred to in the first paragraph of this Article include the entire building.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　除法律、行政法规规定的共有部分外，建筑区划内的以下部分，也应当认定为民法典第二编第六章所称的共有部分：  
（一）建筑物的基础、承重结构、外墙、屋顶等基本结构部分，通道、楼梯、大堂等公共通行部分，消防、公共照明等附属设施、设备，避难层、设备层或者设备间等结构部分；  
（二）其他不属于业主专有部分，也不属于市政公用部分或者其他权利人所有的场所及设施等。  
建筑区划内的土地，依法由业主共同享有建设用地使用权，但属于业主专有的整栋建筑物的规划占地或者城镇公共道路、绿地占地除外。

**Article 3** In addition to the common parts provided for by laws and administrative regulations, the following parts within the building division shall also be deemed as the common parts referred to in Chapter 6 of Part II of the Civil Code:  
(1) Basic structural parts such as the foundation, load-bearing structure, exterior walls, and roof of the building; public passage parts such as corridors, staircases, and lobbies; ancillary facilities and equipment such as fire protection and public lighting; structural parts such as refuge floors, equipment floors or equipment rooms;  
(2) Other premises and facilities that are neither the owner's condominium parts nor owned by municipal public utilities or other right holders.  
The land within the building division shall be jointly enjoyed by the owners for the right to use construction land according to law, except for the planned land area of the entire building that is owned by the owner as a condominium part or the land occupied by urban public roads and green spaces.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　业主基于对住宅、经营性用房等专有部分特定使用功能的合理需要，无偿利用屋顶以及与其专有部分相对应的外墙面等共有部分的，不应认定为侵权。但违反法律、法规、管理规约，损害他人合法权益的除外。

**Article 4** Where an owner, based on reasonable needs for the specific use function of condominium parts such as residential or commercial premises, uses the common parts such as the roof and the exterior wall surfaces corresponding to his or her condominium part without compensation, it shall not be deemed as infringement. However, this does not apply if such use violates laws, regulations, or the management covenant, or damages the lawful rights and interests of others.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　建设单位按照配置比例将车位、车库，以出售、附赠或者出租等方式处分给业主的，应当认定其行为符合[民法典第二百七十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-276)有关“应当首先满足业主的需要”的规定。  
前款所称配置比例是指规划确定的建筑区划内规划用于停放汽车的车位、车库与房屋套数的比例。

**Article 5** Where a construction unit disposes of parking spaces or garages to owners by means of sale, gift, or lease in accordance with the allocation ratio, it shall be deemed that its act complies with the provisions of Article 276 of the Civil Code that 'the needs of owners shall first be satisfied'.  
The allocation ratio referred to in the preceding paragraph means the ratio of the parking spaces and garages planned for parking motor vehicles within the building division to the number of housing units as determined by the plan.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　建筑区划内在规划用于停放汽车的车位之外，占用业主共有道路或者其他场地增设的车位，应当认定为[民法典第二百七十五条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-275)第二款所称的车位。

**Article 6** Parking spaces added by occupying the owners' common roads or other premises within the building division beyond the parking spaces planned for parking motor vehicles shall be deemed as the parking spaces referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 275 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　处分共有部分，以及业主大会依法决定或者管理规约依法确定应由业主共同决定的事项，应当认定为[民法典第二百七十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-278)第一款第（九）项规定的有关共有和共同管理权利的“其他重大事项”。

**Article 7** Disposal of common parts, as well as matters that shall be decided jointly by the owners as determined by the owners' assembly according to law or stipulated by the management covenant according to law, shall be deemed as 'other major matters' related to common ownership and joint management rights provided for in Item (9) of the first paragraph of Article 278 of the Civil Code.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　[民法典第二百七十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-278)第二款和第二百八十三条规定的专有部分面积可以按照不动产登记簿记载的面积计算；尚未进行物权登记的，暂按测绘机构的实测面积计算；尚未进行实测的，暂按房屋买卖合同记载的面积计算。

**Article 8** The area of the condominium part as provided for in the second paragraph of Article 278 and Article 283 of the Civil Code may be calculated based on the area recorded in the real estate register; where the real right has not yet been registered, it shall temporarily be calculated based on the actual surveyed area measured by the surveying institution; where the actual measurement has not yet been performed, it shall temporarily be calculated based on the area recorded in the house sale contract.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　[民法典第二百七十八条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-278)第二款规定的业主人数可以按照专有部分的数量计算，一个专有部分按一人计算。但建设单位尚未出售和虽已出售但尚未交付的部分，以及同一买受人拥有一个以上专有部分的，按一人计算。

**Article 9** The number of owners provided for in the second paragraph of Article 278 of the Civil Code may be calculated based on the number of condominium parts, with one condominium part counted as one person. However, the parts that have not yet been sold by the construction unit, or have been sold but not yet delivered, as well as cases where the same buyer owns more than one condominium part, shall be counted as one person.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　业主将住宅改变为经营性用房，未依据[民法典第二百七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-279)的规定经有利害关系的业主一致同意，有利害关系的业主请求排除妨害、消除危险、恢复原状或者赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
将住宅改变为经营性用房的业主以多数有利害关系的业主同意其行为进行抗辩的，人民法院不予支持。

**Article 10** Where an owner changes a residential house into a commercial premises without obtaining the unanimous consent of the interested owners pursuant to Article 279 of the Civil Code, and an interested owner requests the removal of the nuisance, elimination of the danger, restoration to the original condition, or compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support such request.  
Where the owner who changed the residential house into a commercial premises defends on the ground that a majority of the interested owners have consented to his or her act, the People's Court shall not support such defense.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　业主将住宅改变为经营性用房，本栋建筑物内的其他业主，应当认定为[民法典第二百七十九条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-279)所称“有利害关系的业主”。建筑区划内，本栋建筑物之外的业主，主张与自己有利害关系的，应证明其房屋价值、生活质量受到或者可能受到不利影响。

**Article 11** Where an owner changes a residential house into a commercial premises, the other owners within the same building shall be deemed as the 'interested owners' referred to in Article 279 of the Civil Code. An owner outside the building within the building division who claims to have an interest shall prove that the value of his or her house or the quality of life has been or may be adversely affected.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　业主以业主大会或者业主委员会作出的决定侵害其合法权益或者违反了法律规定的程序为由，依据[民法典第二百八十条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-280)第二款的规定请求人民法院撤销该决定的，应当在知道或者应当知道业主大会或者业主委员会作出决定之日起一年内行使。

**Article 12** Where an owner, on the ground that a decision made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee infringes upon his or her lawful rights and interests or violates the procedures provided by law, requests the People's Court to revoke such decision pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 280 of the Civil Code, the owner shall exercise such right within one year from the date when he or she knows or should have known of the decision made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　业主请求公布、查阅下列应当向业主公开的情况和资料的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）建筑物及其附属设施的维修资金的筹集、使用情况；  
（二）管理规约、业主大会议事规则，以及业主大会或者业主委员会的决定及会议记录；  
（三）物业服务合同、共有部分的使用和收益情况；  
（四）建筑区划内规划用于停放汽车的车位、车库的处分情况；  
（五）其他应当向业主公开的情况和资料。

**Article 13** Where an owner requests the disclosure and inspection of the following information and materials that shall be made public to the owners, the People's Court shall support such request:  
(1) The collection and use of maintenance funds for the building and its ancillary facilities;  
(2) The management covenant, the rules of procedure of the owners' assembly, and the decisions and meeting minutes of the owners' assembly or the owners' committee;  
(3) The property service contract, and the use and income from the common parts;  
(4) The disposal of parking spaces and garages planned for parking motor vehicles within the building division;  
(5) Other information and materials that shall be made public to the owners.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　建设单位、物业服务企业或者其他管理人等擅自占用、处分业主共有部分、改变其使用功能或者进行经营性活动，权利人请求排除妨害、恢复原状、确认处分行为无效或者赔偿损失的，人民法院应予支持。  
属于前款所称擅自进行经营性活动的情形，权利人请求建设单位、物业服务企业或者其他管理人等将扣除合理成本之后的收益用于补充专项维修资金或者业主共同决定的其他用途的，人民法院应予支持。行为人对成本的支出及其合理性承担举证责任。

**Article 14** Where a construction unit, a property service enterprise, or other manager, without authorization, occupies or disposes of the common parts of the owners, changes their use functions, or engages in commercial activities, and the right holder requests the removal of the nuisance, restoration to the original condition, confirmation of the invalidity of the disposal act, or compensation for losses, the People's Court shall support such request.  
In the case of unauthorized commercial activities as referred to in the preceding paragraph, where the right holder requests that the construction unit, property service enterprise, or other manager use the income after deducting reasonable costs to supplement the special maintenance fund or for other purposes decided jointly by the owners, the People's Court shall support such request. The person who engaged in the act shall bear the burden of adducing evidence for the costs incurred and their reasonableness.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　业主或者其他行为人违反法律、法规、国家相关强制性标准、管理规约，或者违反业主大会、业主委员会依法作出的决定，实施下列行为的，可以认定为[民法典第二百八十六条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-286)第二款所称的其他“损害他人合法权益的行为”：  
（一）损害房屋承重结构，损害或者违章使用电力、燃气、消防设施，在建筑物内放置危险、放射性物品等危及建筑物安全或者妨碍建筑物正常使用；  
（二）违反规定破坏、改变建筑物外墙面的形状、颜色等损害建筑物外观；  
（三）违反规定进行房屋装饰装修；  
（四）违章加建、改建，侵占、挖掘公共通道、道路、场地或者其他共有部分。

**Article 15** Where an owner or other actor, in violation of laws, regulations, relevant national mandatory standards, or the management covenant, or in violation of a decision lawfully made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee, commits any of the following acts, it may be deemed as other 'acts that damage the lawful rights and interests of others' referred to in the second paragraph of Article 286 of the Civil Code:  
(1) Damaging the load-bearing structure of the house, damaging or using electricity, gas, or fire protection facilities in violation of regulations, placing dangerous or radioactive substances in the building, thereby endangering the safety of the building or hindering the normal use of the building;  
(2) Damaging or altering the shape or color of the exterior wall surfaces of the building in violation of regulations, thereby damaging the appearance of the building;  
(3) Carrying out interior decoration in violation of regulations;  
(4) Unauthorized addition or alteration of structures, or encroaching upon or excavating public passageways, roads, premises, or other common parts.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　建筑物区分所有权纠纷涉及专有部分的承租人、借用人等物业使用人的，参照本解释处理。  
专有部分的承租人、借用人等物业使用人，根据法律、法规、管理规约、业主大会或者业主委员会依法作出的决定，以及其与业主的约定，享有相应权利，承担相应义务。

**Article 16** Where a dispute over condominium ownership in a building involves a user of the property such as a lessee or borrower of a condominium part, this Interpretation shall apply mutatis mutandis.  
Users of the property such as lessees or borrowers of a condominium part shall enjoy corresponding rights and assume corresponding obligations in accordance with laws, regulations, the management covenant, decisions lawfully made by the owners' assembly or the owners' committee, and their agreements with the owner.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　本解释所称建设单位，包括包销期满，按照包销合同约定的包销价格购买尚未销售的物业后，以自己名义对外销售的包销人。

**Article 17** The term 'construction unit' as used in this Interpretation includes a exclusive sales agent who, after the expiration of the exclusive sales period, purchases the unsold properties at the exclusive sales price stipulated in the exclusive sales contract and subsequently sells them to third parties in its own name.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院审理建筑物区分所有权案件中，涉及有关物权归属争议的，应当以法律、行政法规为依据。

**Article 18** When hearing a case concerning condominium ownership in a building, if a dispute over the attribution of real rights is involved, the People's Court shall take laws and administrative regulations as the basis.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本解释自2009年10月1日起施行。  
因物权法施行后实施的行为引起的建筑物区分所有权纠纷案件，适用本解释。  
本解释施行前已经终审，本解释施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 19** This Interpretation shall come into force as of October 1, 2009.  
Cases of disputes over condominium ownership in a building arising from acts performed after the coming into force of the Property Law shall be governed by this Interpretation.  
This Interpretation shall not apply to cases that have been finally adjudicated before the coming into force of this Interpretation and for which a party applies for a retrial or a decision is made to initiate a retrial under the trial supervision procedure after the coming into force of this Interpretation.
