# 最高人民法院关于网络查询、冻结被执行人存款的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕20号  
**公布日期**：2013-08-26  
**生效日期**：2013-09-02

> 最高人民法院 《关于网络查询、冻结被执行人存款的规定》已于2013年8月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1587次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年9月2日起施行。 2013年8月29日 最高人民法院 关于网络查询、冻结被执行人存款的规定 法释〔2013〕20号 为规范人民法院办理执行案件过程中通过网络查询、冻结被执行人存款及其他财产的行为，进一步提高执行效率，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条   人民法院与金融机构已建立网络执行查控机制的，可以通过网络实施查询、冻结被执行人存款等措施。 网络执行查控机制的建立和运行应当具备以下条件： （一）已建立网络执行查控系统，具有通过网络执行查控系统发送、传输、反馈查控信息的功能； （二）授权特定的人员办理网络执行查控业务； （三）具有符合安全规范的电子印章系统； （四）已采取足以保障查控系统和信息安全的措施。 第二条   人民法院实施网络执行查控措施，应当事前统一向相应金融机构报备有权通过网络采取执行查控措施的特定执行人员的相关公务证件。办理具体业务时，不再另行向相应金融机构提供执行人员的相关公务证件。 人民法院办理网络执行查控业务的特定执行人员发生变更的，应当及时向相应金融机构报备人员变更信息及相关公务证件。 第三条   人民法院通过网络查询被执行人存款时，应当向金融机构传输电子协助查询存款通知书。多案集中查询的，可以附汇总的案件查询清单。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   人民法院与金融机构已建立网络执行查控机制的，可以通过网络实施查询、冻结被执行人存款等措施。  
网络执行查控机制的建立和运行应当具备以下条件：  
（一）已建立网络执行查控系统，具有通过网络执行查控系统发送、传输、反馈查控信息的功能；  
（二）授权特定的人员办理网络执行查控业务；  
（三）具有符合安全规范的电子印章系统；  
（四）已采取足以保障查控系统和信息安全的措施。

**Article 1** Where a People's Court and a financial institution have established a network enforcement inquiry and control mechanism, the People's Court may, through the network, implement measures such as inquiry and freezing of deposits of the person subject to enforcement.  
The establishment and operation of the network enforcement inquiry and control mechanism shall meet the following conditions:  
(1) A network enforcement inquiry and control system has been established, which has the function of sending, transmitting, and feedbacking inquiry and control information through the network enforcement inquiry and control system;  
(2) Specific personnel are authorized to handle network enforcement inquiry and control business;  
(3) An electronic seal system that meets security specifications is in place;  
(4) Measures sufficient to ensure the security of the inquiry and control system and information have been taken.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   人民法院实施网络执行查控措施，应当事前统一向相应金融机构报备有权通过网络采取执行查控措施的特定执行人员的相关公务证件。办理具体业务时，不再另行向相应金融机构提供执行人员的相关公务证件。  
人民法院办理网络执行查控业务的特定执行人员发生变更的，应当及时向相应金融机构报备人员变更信息及相关公务证件。

**Article 2** When a People's Court implements network enforcement inquiry and control measures, it shall, in advance and uniformly, file with the relevant financial institution the relevant official documents of the specific enforcement personnel authorized to take enforcement inquiry and control measures through the network. When handling specific business, it shall no longer separately provide the relevant official documents of the enforcement personnel to the relevant financial institution.  
Where the specific enforcement personnel of the People's Court handling network enforcement inquiry and control business are changed, the People's Court shall promptly file with the relevant financial institution the personnel change information and the relevant official documents.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   人民法院通过网络查询被执行人存款时，应当向金融机构传输电子协助查询存款通知书。多案集中查询的，可以附汇总的案件查询清单。  
对查询到的被执行人存款需要冻结或者续行冻结的，人民法院应当及时向金融机构传输电子冻结裁定书和协助冻结存款通知书。  
对冻结的被执行人存款需要解除冻结的，人民法院应当及时向金融机构传输电子解除冻结裁定书和协助解除冻结存款通知书。

**Article 3** When a People's Court inquires about the deposits of a person subject to enforcement through the network, it shall transmit an electronic Letter of Assistance in Inquiry of Deposits to the financial institution. For centralized inquiry of multiple cases, a summary case inquiry list may be attached.  
Where the inquired deposits of the person subject to enforcement need to be frozen or continued to be frozen, the People's Court shall promptly transmit an electronic Freezing Ruling and an electronic Letter of Assistance in Freezing Deposits to the financial institution.  
Where the frozen deposits of the person subject to enforcement need to be unfrozen, the People's Court shall promptly transmit an electronic Ruling on Lifting Freeze and an electronic Letter of Assistance in Unfreezing Deposits to the financial institution.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   人民法院向金融机构传输的法律文书，应当加盖电子印章。  
作为协助执行人的金融机构完成查询、冻结等事项后，应当及时通过网络向人民法院回复加盖电子印章的查询、冻结等结果。  
人民法院出具的电子法律文书、金融机构出具的电子查询、冻结等结果，与纸质法律文书及反馈结果具有同等效力。

**Article 4** The legal documents transmitted by the People's Court to the financial institution shall be affixed with an electronic seal.  
After the financial institution, as the assistance enforcer, completes matters such as inquiry and freezing, it shall promptly reply to the People's Court through the network with the results of inquiry and freezing affixed with an electronic seal.  
The electronic legal documents issued by the People's Court and the electronic inquiry and freezing results issued by the financial institution shall have the same legal effect as paper legal documents and feedback results.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   人民法院通过网络查询、冻结、续冻、解冻被执行人存款，与执行人员赴金融机构营业场所查询、冻结、续冻、解冻被执行人存款具有同等效力。

**Article 5** The inquiry, freezing, continued freezing, and unfreezing of deposits of a person subject to enforcement by the People's Court through the network shall have the same legal effect as the inquiry, freezing, continued freezing, and unfreezing of deposits of a person subject to enforcement by enforcement personnel at the business premises of a financial institution.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   金融机构认为人民法院通过网络执行查控系统采取的查控措施违反相关法律、行政法规规定的，应当向人民法院书面提出异议。人民法院应当在15日内审查完毕并书面回复。

**Article 6** Where a financial institution considers that the inquiry and control measures taken by the People's Court through the network enforcement inquiry and control system violate the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, it shall raise an objection in writing to the People's Court. The People's Court shall complete the review within 15 days and reply in writing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   人民法院应当依据法律、行政法规规定及相应操作规范使用网络执行查控系统和查控信息，确保信息安全。  
人民法院办理执行案件过程中，不得泄露通过网络执行查控系统取得的查控信息，也不得用于执行案件以外的目的。  
人民法院办理执行案件过程中，不得对被执行人以外的非执行义务主体采取网络查控措施。

**Article 7** A People's Court shall use the network enforcement inquiry and control system and inquiry and control information in accordance with the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and corresponding operational norms to ensure information security.  
In the process of handling enforcement cases, a People's Court shall not disclose the inquiry and control information obtained through the network enforcement inquiry and control system, nor shall it use it for purposes other than the enforcement case.  
In the process of handling enforcement cases, a People's Court shall not take network inquiry and control measures against persons other than the person subject to enforcement who are not subjects of enforcement obligations.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   人民法院工作人员违反第七条规定的，应当按照《人民法院工作人员处分条例》给予纪律处分；情节严重构成犯罪的，应当依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** Where a staff member of a People's Court violates the provisions of Article 7, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed according to the Regulations on Disciplinary Sanctions for Staff of People's Courts; if the circumstances are serious enough to constitute a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   人民法院具备相应网络扣划技术条件，并与金融机构协商一致的，可以通过网络执行查控系统采取扣划被执行人存款措施。

**Article 9** Where a People's Court has the corresponding technical conditions for network deduction and transfer, and has reached consensus with the financial institution through consultation, it may take measures to deduct and transfer deposits of the person subject to enforcement through the network enforcement inquiry and control system.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   人民法院与工商行政管理、证券监管、土地房产管理等协助执行单位已建立网络执行查控机制，通过网络执行查控系统对被执行人股权、股票、证券账户资金、房地产等其他财产采取查控措施的，参照本规定执行。

**Article 10** Where a People's Court has established a network enforcement inquiry and control mechanism with assistance enforcement units such as administrative departments for industry and commerce, securities regulatory authorities, and land and housing management authorities, and takes inquiry and control measures through the network enforcement inquiry and control system against other properties such as equity, stocks, funds in securities accounts, and real estate of the person subject to enforcement, these Provisions shall be referred to for implementation.
