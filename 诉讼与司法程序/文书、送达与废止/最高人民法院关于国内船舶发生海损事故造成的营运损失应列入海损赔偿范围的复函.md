### 最高人民法院关于国内船舶发生海损事故造成的营运损失应列入海损赔偿范围的复函

<a id="art-1"></a>最 高 人 民 法 院  
  
关于国内船舶发生海损事故造成的营运损失  
  
应列入海损赔偿范围的复函  
  
法（交）函〔1991〕104号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院粤法经行（ 1990）第 14号《关于船舶营运损失和船员工资损失应否列入海损赔偿范围的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百一十七条第二款和第三款的规定，损害他人财产的，侵害人应当赔偿受害人因其侵权行为所遭受的实际损失，包括受害人财产的毁损、减少、灭失和为减少或消除损失所支出的费用，以及受害人在未受侵害的正常情况下可以得到的利益。因此，国内船舶在内河或沿海运输中发生海损事故造成的船舶修理期间的合理营运损失（其中包括船员工资损失），应当列入海损赔偿范围。  
  
1991年9月13日

**Article 1** SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT  
Reply on Whether Operating Losses Caused by Maritime Accidents Involving Domestic Vessels Shall Be Included in the Scope of Maritime Compensation  
Fa (Jiao) Han [1991] No. 104  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
Your court's Yue Fa Jing Xing (1990) No. 14, the "Request for Instructions on Whether Ship Operating Losses and Crew Wages Losses Shall Be Included in the Scope of Maritime Compensation," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Pursuant to the provisions of Article 117, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, where property of another person is damaged, the infringer shall compensate the victim for the actual losses suffered as a result of the infringing act, including the destruction, reduction, or loss of the victim's property, the expenses incurred to reduce or eliminate the losses, and the benefits that the victim could have obtained under normal circumstances without the infringement. Therefore, reasonable operating losses (including crew wages losses) incurred during the repair period of a domestic vessel due to a maritime accident occurring in inland river or coastal transportation shall be included in the scope of maritime compensation.  
September 13, 1991
