# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于开展法律援助值班律师工作的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**公布日期**：2017-08-29

> 三、法律援助机构可以根据人民法院、人民检察院、看守所实际工作需要，通过设立法律援助工作站派驻值班律师或及时安排值班律师等形式提供法律帮助。 工作站应当悬挂统一标牌，配备必要的办公设施，设立指引标识，并放置法律援助格式文书以及相关业务介绍资料。 工作站应当公示法律援助范围、条件、值班律师工作职责及当日值班律师基本信息等。 四、法律援助机构综合社会律师和法律援助机构律师政治素质、职业道德水准、业务能力、执业年限等确定法律援助值班律师人选，建立法律援助值班律师名册。有条件的地方可以组建法律援助值班律师库。 五、法律援助机构根据人民法院、看守所法律援助工作站法律咨询需求量和当地律师资源状况，合理安排值班律师工作时间。律师值班可以相对固定专人或者轮流值班，在律师资源短缺地区可以探索采用现场值班和电话、网络值班相结合的方式。 六、法律援助机构应当将值班律师名册或人员信息送交或告知人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关及看守所。法律援助值班律师在人民法院、看守所法律援助工作站提供值班律师服务应持律师执业证书，实行挂牌上岗，向当事人表明法律援助值班律师身份。 值班律师在接待当事人时，应当现场记录当事人咨询的法律问题和提供的法律解答，解释法律援助的条件和范围，对认为初步符合法律援助条件的当事人引导其申请法律援助。 社会律师和法律援助机构律师应当接受法律援助机构的安排提供值班律师服务。值班律师应当遵守相关法律规定、职业道德、执业纪律，不得误导当事人诉讼行为，严禁收受财物，严禁利用值班便利招揽案源、介绍律师有偿服务及其它违反值班律师工作纪律的行为。值班律师应当依法保守工作中知晓的国家秘密、商业秘密和当事人隐私，犯罪嫌疑人、刑事被告人或者其他人准备或者正在实施危害国家安全、公共安全以及严重危害他人人身安全的犯罪事实和信息除外。 七、法律援助机构要加强对法律援助值班律师工作运行的业务指导，组织开展对值班律师职责、服务内容、执业纪律、刑事诉讼法律知识方面的业务培训，及时统计汇总犯罪嫌疑人、刑事被告人涉嫌罪名、简要案情、咨询意见等信息，定期运用征询所驻单位意见、当事人回访等措施了解值班律师履责情况，对值班律师实行动态化管理。 法律援助机构要向律师协会通报法律援助值班律师履责情况。律师协会要将法律援助值班律师履责情况纳入律师年度考核及律师诚信服务记录。 司法行政机关要加强对律师提供值班律师服务的日常监督管理，总结并不断提升值班律师服务质量水平。对律师在值班律师工作中违反职业道德和执业纪律的行为依法依规处理。 八、人民法院、人民检察院、看守所为法律援助工作站提供必要办公场所和设施。看守所为法律援助值班律师会见提供便利。 人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、司法行政机关建立刑事法律援助工作联席会议制度，定期沟通法律援助值班律师工作情况。 九、对于律师资源短缺的地区和单位，法律援助机构要根据律师资源和刑事法律援助需求等，统筹调配律师资源，探索建立政府购买值班律师服务机制，保障法律援助值班律师工作正常有序开展。 十、国家安全机关适用本意见中有关公安机关的规定。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>四、法律援助机构综合社会律师和法律援助机构律师政治素质、职业道德水准、业务能力、执业年限等确定法律援助值班律师人选，建立法律援助值班律师名册。有条件的地方可以组建法律援助值班律师库。

**Article 4** . A legal aid institution shall, by comprehensively considering the political quality, professional ethics, professional competence, years of practice, etc. of social lawyers and lawyers of the legal aid institution, determine candidates for legal aid duty lawyers and establish a roster of legal aid duty lawyers. Where conditions permit, a pool of legal aid duty lawyers may be established.

<a id="art-2"></a>五、法律援助机构根据人民法院、看守所法律援助工作站法律咨询需求量和当地律师资源状况，合理安排值班律师工作时间。律师值班可以相对固定专人或者轮流值班，在律师资源短缺地区可以探索采用现场值班和电话、网络值班相结合的方式。

**Article 5** . A legal aid institution shall, based on the demand for legal consultation at the legal aid workstation of the People's Court and the detention house and the local lawyer resources, reasonably arrange the working hours of duty lawyers. Lawyers may be assigned for duty on a relatively fixed individual basis or on a rotating basis. In areas with scarce lawyer resources, a combination of on-site duty and duty by telephone or online may be explored.

<a id="art-3"></a>六、法律援助机构应当将值班律师名册或人员信息送交或告知人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关及看守所。法律援助值班律师在人民法院、看守所法律援助工作站提供值班律师服务应持律师执业证书，实行挂牌上岗，向当事人表明法律援助值班律师身份。  
值班律师在接待当事人时，应当现场记录当事人咨询的法律问题和提供的法律解答，解释法律援助的条件和范围，对认为初步符合法律援助条件的当事人引导其申请法律援助。  
社会律师和法律援助机构律师应当接受法律援助机构的安排提供值班律师服务。值班律师应当遵守相关法律规定、职业道德、执业纪律，不得误导当事人诉讼行为，严禁收受财物，严禁利用值班便利招揽案源、介绍律师有偿服务及其它违反值班律师工作纪律的行为。值班律师应当依法保守工作中知晓的国家秘密、商业秘密和当事人隐私，犯罪嫌疑人、刑事被告人或者其他人准备或者正在实施危害国家安全、公共安全以及严重危害他人人身安全的犯罪事实和信息除外。

**Article 6** . A legal aid institution shall submit or notify the roster of duty lawyers or personnel information to the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ and the detention house. A legal aid duty lawyer, when providing duty lawyer services at the legal aid workstation of the People's Court or the detention house, shall hold a lawyer's practice certificate, work with a nameplate displayed, and indicate to the party the identity of a legal aid duty lawyer.  
When receiving a party, a duty lawyer shall, on the spot, record the legal issues consulted by the party and the legal answers provided, explain the conditions and scope of legal aid, and guide the party who is preliminarily deemed to meet the conditions for legal aid to apply for legal aid.  
A social lawyer or a lawyer of a legal aid institution shall accept the arrangement of the legal aid institution to provide duty lawyer services. A duty lawyer shall comply with relevant legal provisions, professional ethics, and practice discipline; shall not mislead a party in litigation; shall be strictly prohibited from accepting property or money, and from using the convenience of duty to solicit cases, introduce paid lawyer services, or engage in any other acts violating the work discipline of duty lawyers. A duty lawyer shall, according to law, keep confidential the State secrets, trade secrets, and privacy of parties learned in the course of work, except for the facts and information of crimes endangering national security, public security, or seriously endangering the personal safety of others that a criminal suspect, defendant, or other person is preparing or committing.

<a id="art-4"></a>七、法律援助机构要加强对法律援助值班律师工作运行的业务指导，组织开展对值班律师职责、服务内容、执业纪律、刑事诉讼法律知识方面的业务培训，及时统计汇总犯罪嫌疑人、刑事被告人涉嫌罪名、简要案情、咨询意见等信息，定期运用征询所驻单位意见、当事人回访等措施了解值班律师履责情况，对值班律师实行动态化管理。  
法律援助机构要向律师协会通报法律援助值班律师履责情况。律师协会要将法律援助值班律师履责情况纳入律师年度考核及律师诚信服务记录。  
司法行政机关要加强对律师提供值班律师服务的日常监督管理，总结并不断提升值班律师服务质量水平。对律师在值班律师工作中违反职业道德和执业纪律的行为依法依规处理。

**Article 7** . A legal aid institution shall strengthen professional guidance on the operation of the work of legal aid duty lawyers, organize professional training on the duties, service content, practice discipline, and knowledge of criminal litigation law of duty lawyers, timely collect and summarize information such as the alleged crimes, brief case facts, and consultation opinions of criminal suspects and defendants, regularly take measures such as soliciting opinions from the units where the workstation is located and returning visits to parties to understand the performance of duties by duty lawyers, and implement dynamic management of duty lawyers.  
A legal aid institution shall notify the lawyers association of the performance of duties by legal aid duty lawyers. The lawyers association shall include the performance of duties by legal aid duty lawyers in the annual assessment of lawyers and the records of lawyer integrity services.  
A judicial administrative organ shall strengthen daily supervision and management of lawyers providing duty lawyer services, summarize and continuously improve the quality and level of duty lawyer services. Acts of lawyers violating professional ethics and practice discipline in the course of duty lawyer work shall be handled according to law and regulations.

<a id="art-5"></a>八、人民法院、人民检察院、看守所为法律援助工作站提供必要办公场所和设施。看守所为法律援助值班律师会见提供便利。  
人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、司法行政机关建立刑事法律援助工作联席会议制度，定期沟通法律援助值班律师工作情况。

**Article 8** . The People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, and the detention house shall provide necessary office space and facilities for the legal aid workstation. The detention house shall provide convenience for the meeting between legal aid duty lawyers and their clients.  
The People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ, the state security organ, and the judicial administrative organ shall establish a joint meeting system for criminal legal aid work and regularly communicate on the work situation of legal aid duty lawyers.

<a id="art-6"></a>九、对于律师资源短缺的地区和单位，法律援助机构要根据律师资源和刑事法律援助需求等，统筹调配律师资源，探索建立政府购买值班律师服务机制，保障法律援助值班律师工作正常有序开展。

**Article 9** . For areas and units with scarce lawyer resources, a legal aid institution shall, based on lawyer resources and the demand for criminal legal aid, coordinate and allocate lawyer resources, explore the establishment of a government-purchased duty lawyer service mechanism, and ensure the normal and orderly development of legal aid duty lawyer work.

<a id="art-7"></a>十、国家安全机关适用本意见中有关公安机关的规定。

**Article 10** . State security organs shall apply the provisions of this Opinion concerning public security organs.
