### 最高人民法院关于拐卖人口案件中婴儿、幼儿、儿童年龄界限如何划分问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于拐卖人口案件中婴儿、幼儿、儿童  
  
年龄界限如何划分问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1989〕5号  
  
福建省高级人民法院：  
  
你院闽法研字〔 1988〕第 45号《关于拐卖人口中婴儿、幼儿、儿童年龄界限如何划分的请示》收悉。经研究，现答复如下：  
  
最高人民法院、最高人民检察院，公安部 1984年3月31日《关于当前办理拐卖人口案件中具体应用法律的若干问题的解答》中，关于婴儿、幼儿，儿童的年龄划分，应以不满一岁的为婴儿，一岁以上不满六岁的为幼儿，六岁以上不满十四岁的为儿童。  
  
在办理奸淫幼女案件中，幼女的年龄按刑法第139条的规定执行，不满十四岁的均为幼女。  
  
1989年7月7日

**Article 1** Reply on the Issues of How to Define the Age Limits for Infants, Young Children, and Children in Cases of Abduction and Trafficking of Persons  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1989] No. 5  
  
Higher People's Court of Fujian Province:  
  
Your court's document Min Fa Yan Zi [1988] No. 45, titled "Request for Instructions on How to Define the Age Limits for Infants, Young Children, and Children in Cases of Abduction and Trafficking of Persons," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
In the "Answers to Certain Questions Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Currently Handling Cases of Abduction and Trafficking of Persons" jointly issued by the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security on March 31, 1984, the age division for infants, young children, and children shall be as follows: those under one year of age shall be considered infants; those one year of age or older but under six years of age shall be considered young children; and those six years of age or older but under fourteen years of age shall be considered children.  
  
In handling cases of raping a young girl, the age of a young girl shall be implemented according to the provisions of Article 139 of the Criminal Law; all females under fourteen years of age shall be considered young girls.  
  
July 7, 1989
