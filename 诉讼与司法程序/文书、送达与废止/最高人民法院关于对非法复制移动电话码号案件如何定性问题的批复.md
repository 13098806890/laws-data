# 最高人民法院关于对非法复制移动电话码号案件如何定性问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法复〔1995〕6号  
**公布日期**：1995-09-13

---

### 正文

一、对非法复制窃取的移动电话码号的行为，应当以盗窃罪从重处罚．盗窃数额以当地邮电部门规定的移动电话入网费计算，销赃数额（减去空机成本价后的数额）高于当地邮电部门规定的移动电话入网费的，其盗窃数额以销赃数额计算

**Article None** 1. The act of illegally copying and stealing mobile phone numbers shall be subject to severe punishment as the crime of theft. The amount of theft shall be calculated based on the mobile phone activation fee prescribed by the local postal and telecommunications department. If the amount from the sale of stolen goods (the amount after deducting the cost of the empty handset) exceeds the mobile phone activation fee prescribed by the local postal and telecommunications department, the amount of theft shall be calculated based on the amount from the sale of stolen goods.

二、对明知是他人非法复制的移动电话而倒卖的，应当以销脏罪追究刑事责任。

**Article None** 2. Anyone who knowingly resells illegally copied mobile phones shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of selling stolen goods.

三、对明知是非法复制的移动电话而使用，给他人造成损失的，应当以盗窃罪追究刑事责任，其盗窃数额以移动电话合法用户的实际损失计算。实际损失无法直接确认的，应当以合法用户的移动电话被复制后的月缴费额减去被复制前6个月的平均电话费推算；合法用户使用移动电话不足6个月的，应以实际使用的月平均电话费推算。  
1995年9月13日

**Article None** 3. Anyone who knowingly uses an illegally copied mobile phone and causes losses to others shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of theft. The amount of theft shall be calculated based on the actual losses of the legitimate mobile phone user. If the actual losses cannot be directly confirmed, they shall be calculated by deducting the average monthly phone bill of the six months before the copying from the monthly bill after the legitimate user's mobile phone was copied. If the legitimate user has used the mobile phone for less than six months, the calculation shall be based on the average monthly phone bill of the actual usage period.  
September 13, 1995
