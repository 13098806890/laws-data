# 最高人民法院关于涉外民商事案件诉讼管辖若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕5号  
**公布日期**：2002-02-25  
**生效日期**：2002-03-01

> （2001年12月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1203次会议通过　2002年2月25日最高人民法院公告公布　自2002年3月1日起施行） 为正确审理涉外民商事案件，依法保护中外当事人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第十九条的规定，现将有关涉外民商事案件诉讼管辖的问题规定如下： 第一条　第一审涉外民商事案件由下列人民法院管辖： （一）国务院批准设立的经济技术开发区人民法院； （二）省会、自治区首府、直辖市所在地的中级人民法院； （三）经济特区、计划单列市中级人民法院； （四）最高人民法院指定的其他中级人民法院； （五）高级人民法院。 上述中级人民法院的区域管辖范围由所在地的高级人民法院确定。 第二条　对国务院批准设立的经济技术开发区人民法院所作的第一审判决、裁定不服的，其第二审由所在地中级人民法院管辖。 第三条　本规定适用于下列案件： （一）涉外合同和侵权纠纷案件； （二）信用证纠纷案件； （三）申请撤销、承认与强制执行国际仲裁裁决的案件； （四）审查有关涉外民商事仲裁条款效力的案件；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　第一审涉外民商事案件由下列人民法院管辖：  
（一）国务院批准设立的经济技术开发区人民法院；  
（二）省会、自治区首府、直辖市所在地的中级人民法院；  
（三）经济特区、计划单列市中级人民法院；  
（四）最高人民法院指定的其他中级人民法院；  
（五）高级人民法院。  
上述中级人民法院的区域管辖范围由所在地的高级人民法院确定。

**Article 1** First-instance foreign-related civil and commercial cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the following People's Courts:  
(1) The People's Courts of economic and technological development zones approved by the State Council;  
(2) The Intermediate People's Courts located in the seats of provincial capitals, autonomous regions' capitals, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) The Intermediate People's Courts of special economic zones and cities with independent planning status;  
(4) Other Intermediate People's Courts designated by the Supreme People's Court;  
(5) Higher People's Courts.  
The regional jurisdiction scope of the above-mentioned Intermediate People's Courts shall be determined by the Higher People's Courts in their respective localities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对国务院批准设立的经济技术开发区人民法院所作的第一审判决、裁定不服的，其第二审由所在地中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Where a party disagrees with a first-instance judgment or ruling rendered by a People's Court of an economic and technological development zone approved by the State Council, the second instance shall be under the jurisdiction of the Intermediate People's Court in the locality.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本规定适用于下列案件：  
（一）涉外合同和侵权纠纷案件；  
（二）信用证纠纷案件；  
（三）申请撤销、承认与强制执行国际仲裁裁决的案件；  
（四）审查有关涉外民商事仲裁条款效力的案件；  
（五）申请承认和强制执行外国法院民商事判决、裁定的案件。

**Article 3** These Provisions shall apply to the following cases:  
(1) Cases concerning foreign-related contracts and tort disputes;  
(2) Cases concerning letter of credit disputes;  
(3) Cases concerning applications for setting aside, recognition, and compulsory enforcement of international arbitration awards;  
(4) Cases concerning the examination of the validity of foreign-related civil and commercial arbitration clauses;  
(5) Cases concerning applications for recognition and compulsory enforcement of civil and commercial judgments and rulings of foreign courts.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　发生在与外国接壤的边境省份的边境贸易纠纷案件，涉外房地产案件和涉外知识产权案件，不适用本规定。

**Article 4** These Provisions shall not apply to cases concerning border trade disputes occurring in border provinces adjacent to foreign countries, foreign-related real estate cases, and foreign-related intellectual property cases.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　涉及香港、澳门特别行政区和台湾地区当事人的民商事纠纷案件的管辖，参照本规定处理。

**Article 5** The jurisdiction over civil and commercial dispute cases involving parties from the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Macao Special Administrative Region, or the Taiwan region shall be handled with reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　高级人民法院应当对涉外民商事案件的管辖实施监督，凡越权受理涉外民商事案件的，应当通知或者裁定将案件移送有管辖权的人民法院审理。

**Article 6** The Higher People's Courts shall supervise the jurisdiction over foreign-related civil and commercial cases. Where a People's Court accepts a foreign-related civil and commercial case beyond its authority, the Higher People's Court shall notify or rule that the case be transferred to a People's Court with jurisdiction for trial.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本规定于2002年3月1日起施行。本规定施行前已经受理的案件由原受理人民法院继续审理。  
本规定发布前的有关司法解释、规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 7** These Provisions shall come into force on March 1, 2002. Cases that have already been accepted before the coming into force of these Provisions shall continue to be tried by the People's Court that originally accepted them.  
Where any relevant judicial interpretations or provisions issued before the promulgation of these Provisions are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
