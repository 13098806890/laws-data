# 最高人民法院关于土改后不久被收养的子女能否参加分割土改前的祖遗房产的批复

**分类**：司法解释

### 最高人民法院关于土改后不久被收养的子女能否参加分割土改前的祖遗房产的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于土改后不久被收养的子女  
  
能否参加分割土改前的祖遗房产的批复  
  
（1986）民他字第6号  
  
河南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1985）豫法民字第 5号《关于经土改确权的祖业房产能否按参加土改的人口分析以确定遗产范围的请示报告》收悉。  
  
据你院报告称，被继承人马希良家有十六间祖遗房屋，土改时由其母马韩氏、马希良夫妇及其次女、三女（长女土改前已出嫁）五口人填登了土地房产证。一九五三年马希良收马海庆为养子（当时一岁）。三个女儿离开家庭后，在经济和生活方面对父母各自尽了赡养扶助义务。一九八三年前，马韩氏及马希良夫妇先后去世，为分割处理十六间祖遗房屋，三个女儿与养子发生纠纷。  
  
经我们研究认为：对土改已确权的房屋，一般应以确定的产权为准，由参加土改的家庭成员进行析产，其中被继承人应得的份额属于遗产。本案讼争的房屋系祖遗房产，土改时没有变动；马海庆土改后不久被收养，以后对该房屋长期进行了使用和管理。根据此案具体情况，可按马家参加土改的人口加上养子共七人先行析产，然后确定马希良夫妇的遗产数额。马希良夫妇从析产中所得的份额及马希良继承其母马韩氏的份额属于他们的遗产，其三个女儿及养子都有继承权。至于每人继承份额多少，应视具体情况合理确定。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年二月十三日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether Children Adopted Shortly after Land Reform May Participate in the Division of Ancestral Property Inherited before Land Reform  
(1986) Min Ta Zi No. 6  
Henan Higher People's Court:  
We have received your Report on Request for Instructions, (1985) Yu Fa Min Zi No. 5, titled "Report on Request for Instructions on Whether Ancestral Property Confirmed by Land Reform May Be Analyzed Based on the Population Participating in Land Reform to Determine the Scope of the Estate."  
According to your report, the decedent Ma Xiliang's family had sixteen ancestral rooms of houses. During the land reform, the land and property certificate was registered in the names of five persons: Ma Xiliang's mother Ma Hanshi, Ma Xiliang and his wife, and their second and third daughters (the eldest daughter had married before the land reform). In 1953, Ma Xiliang adopted Ma Haiqing as his adopted son (who was then one year old). After the three daughters left the family, they each fulfilled their duty of support and assistance to their parents in economic and daily life. Before 1983, Ma Hanshi and Ma Xiliang and his wife successively passed away. A dispute arose among the three daughters and the adopted son over the division of the sixteen ancestral rooms.  
Upon our study, we hold that: For houses that have been confirmed by land reform, the confirmed property rights shall generally be taken as the basis, and the family members who participated in the land reform shall partition the property, among which the share that the decedent should have received shall constitute the estate. The houses in dispute in this case are ancestral property, which remained unchanged during the land reform; Ma Haiqing was adopted shortly after the land reform and has long used and managed the houses thereafter. Under the specific circumstances of this case, the partition may first be carried out based on the population of the Ma family participating in the land reform plus the adopted son, totaling seven persons, and then the amount of Ma Xiliang and his wife's estate shall be determined. The shares obtained by Ma Xiliang and his wife from the partition and the share inherited by Ma Xiliang from his mother Ma Hanshi shall belong to their estate, and all three daughters and the adopted son shall have the right of succession. As to the share each person shall inherit, it shall be reasonably determined in light of the specific circumstances.  
This reply is hereby given.  
February 13, 1986
