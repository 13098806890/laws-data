各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，人民检察院，解放军军事法院、军事检察院：  
自1993年起，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院共同对1979年至1993年两院联合发布的司法解释进行了全面清理。现将经最高人民法院审判委员会、最高人民检察院检察委员会讨论通过的第一批予以废止的司法解释目录印发给你们。这批予以废止的4件司法解释从本通知发布之日起不再适用（有的早已自行失效）。在此之前适用上述司法解释对有关案件作出的判决、裁定不再变动。清理司法解释工作尚在进行中，应当废止的联合发布的司法解释，今后还将陆续分批通知你们。有些司法解释只有部分内容不适应当前司法实践需要的，我们将在全面清理的基础上研究修改，各地有什么意见请及时报告我们。

**Article 1** Notice on Partial Judicial Interpretations  
Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; Military Courts and Military Procuratorates of the Chinese People's Liberation Army:  
Since 1993, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate have jointly conducted a comprehensive review of the judicial interpretations issued jointly by the two authorities from 1979 to 1993. The first batch of the catalogue of judicial interpretations to be abolished, which has been discussed and approved by the Adjudication Committee of the Supreme People's Court and the Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate, is hereby distributed to you. These four judicial interpretations to be abolished shall no longer be applicable as of the date of issuance of this Notice (some have already ceased to have effect by themselves). Judgments and rulings rendered before this Notice in relevant cases that applied the aforesaid judicial interpretations shall not be altered. The work of clearing up judicial interpretations is still ongoing, and judicial interpretations jointly issued that shall be abolished shall be notified to you in subsequent batches. For those judicial interpretations where only part of their content is not suitable for current judicial practice, we shall study and revise them on the basis of comprehensive review; if you have any opinions, please promptly report them to us.
