# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2023〕14号  
**公布日期**：2023-12-05  
**生效日期**：2024-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定〉的决定》已于2023年12月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1908次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年1月1日起施行。 2023年12月18日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭 若干问题的规定》的决定 法释〔2023〕14号 根据第十四届全国人民代表大会常务委员会第五次会议审议通过的《全国人民代表大会常务委员会〈关于修改中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的决定》的相关规定，对《最高人民法院关于设立国际商事法庭若干问题的规定》作如下修改： 一、将第二条第一项修改为：“（一）当事人依照民事诉讼法第二百七十七条的规定协议选择最高人民法院管辖且标的额为人民币3亿元以上的第一审国际商事案件；” 二、将第八条第一款修改为：“国际商事法庭审理案件应当适用域外法律时，可以通过下列途径查明： （一）由当事人提供； （二）通过司法协助渠道由对方的中央机关或者主管机关提供； （三）通过最高人民法院请求我国驻该国使领馆或者该国驻我国使领馆提供； （四）由最高人民法院建立或者参与的法律查明合作机制参与方提供； （五）由最高人民法院国际商事专家委员会专家提供； （六）由法律查明服务机构或者中外法律专家提供；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　最高人民法院设立国际商事法庭。国际商事法庭是最高人民法院的常设审判机构。

**Article 1** The Supreme People's Court shall establish the International Commercial Court. The International Commercial Court shall be the permanent adjudication body of the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国际商事法庭受理下列案件：  
（一）当事人依照[民事诉讼法第二百七十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-277)的规定协议选择最高人民法院管辖且标的额为人民币3亿元以上的第一审国际商事案件；  
（二）高级人民法院对其所管辖的第一审国际商事案件，认为需要由最高人民法院审理并获准许的；  
（三）在全国有重大影响的第一审国际商事案件；  
（四）依照[本规定第十四条](#art-14)申请仲裁保全、申请撤销或者执行国际商事仲裁裁决的；  
（五）最高人民法院认为应当由国际商事法庭审理的其他国际商事案件。

**Article 2** The International Commercial Court shall accept the following cases:  
(1) First-instance international commercial cases in which the parties have agreed by agreement to the jurisdiction of the Supreme People's Court in accordance with Article 277 of the Civil Procedure Law and the subject matter amount is RMB 300 million or more;  
(2) First-instance international commercial cases under the jurisdiction of a Higher People's Court that are considered to need trial by the Supreme People's Court and are so permitted;  
(3) First-instance international commercial cases that have a significant national impact;  
(4) Cases applying for arbitration preservation, applying for setting aside or enforcement of international commercial arbitration awards in accordance with Article 14 of these Provisions;  
(5) Other international commercial cases that the Supreme People's Court considers shall be tried by the International Commercial Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　具有下列情形之一的商事案件，可以认定为本规定所称的国际商事案件：  
（一）当事人一方或者双方是外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织的；  
（二）当事人一方或者双方的经常居所地在中华人民共和国领域外的；  
（三）标的物在中华人民共和国领域外的；  
（四）产生、变更或者消灭商事关系的法律事实发生在中华人民共和国领域外的。

**Article 3** Commercial cases that have any of the following circumstances may be deemed as international commercial cases as referred to in these Provisions:  
(1) One or both parties are foreigners, stateless persons, foreign enterprises or organizations;  
(2) The habitual residence of one or both parties is outside the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) The subject matter is located outside the territory of the People's Republic of China;  
(4) The legal facts giving rise to, modifying or extinguishing a commercial relationship occur outside the territory of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　国际商事法庭法官由最高人民法院在具有丰富审判工作经验，熟悉国际条约、国际惯例以及国际贸易投资实务，能够同时熟练运用中文和英文作为工作语言的资深法官中选任。

**Article 4** The judges of the International Commercial Court shall be selected by the Supreme People's Court from among senior judges who have rich experience in adjudication, are familiar with international treaties, international practices, and international trade and investment practices, and are able to proficiently use both Chinese and English as working languages.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　国际商事法庭审理案件，由三名或者三名以上法官组成合议庭。  
合议庭评议案件，实行少数服从多数的原则。少数意见可以在裁判文书中载明。

**Article 5** When the International Commercial Court hears a case, a collegial panel composed of three or more judges shall be formed.  
The collegial panel shall deliberate on the case in accordance with the principle of majority rule. The minority opinion may be recorded in the adjudicative document.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国际商事法庭作出的保全裁定，可以指定下级人民法院执行。

**Article 6** A preservation ruling made by the International Commercial Court may designate a lower people's court for enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　国际商事法庭审理案件，依照《中华人民共和国涉外民事关系法律适用法》的规定确定争议适用的实体法律。  
当事人依照法律规定选择适用法律的，应当适用当事人选择的法律。

**Article 7** When the International Commercial Court hears a case, it shall determine the substantive law applicable to the dispute in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Application of Laws to Foreign-Related Civil Relations.  
Where the parties choose the applicable law in accordance with legal provisions, the law chosen by the parties shall apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国际商事法庭审理案件应当适用域外法律时，可以通过下列途径查明：  
（一）由当事人提供；  
（二）通过司法协助渠道由对方的中央机关或者主管机关提供；  
（三）通过最高人民法院请求我国驻该国使领馆或者该国驻我国使领馆提供；  
（四）由最高人民法院建立或者参与的法律查明合作机制参与方提供；  
（五）由最高人民法院国际商事专家委员会专家提供；  
（六）由法律查明服务机构或者中外法律专家提供；  
（七）其他适当途径。  
通过上述途径提供的域外法律资料以及专家意见，应当依照法律规定在法庭上出示，并充分听取各方当事人的意见。

**Article 8** Where the International Commercial Court should apply foreign law when hearing a case, it may ascertain the law through the following channels:  
(1) Provision by the parties;  
(2) Provision through judicial assistance channels by the central authority or competent authority of the other party;  
(3) Provision upon request by the Supreme People's Court to the embassy or consulate of China in that country or the embassy or consulate of that country in China;  
(4) Provision by participants in the legal ascertainment cooperation mechanism established or participated in by the Supreme People's Court;  
(5) Provision by experts of the International Commercial Expert Committee of the Supreme People's Court;  
(6) Provision by legal ascertainment service institutions or Chinese and foreign legal experts;  
(7) Other appropriate channels.  
The foreign legal materials and expert opinions provided through the above channels shall be presented in court in accordance with legal provisions, and the opinions of all parties shall be fully heard.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人向国际商事法庭提交的证据材料系在中华人民共和国领域外形成的，不论是否已办理公证、认证或者其他证明手续，均应当在法庭上质证。  
当事人提交的证据材料系英文且经对方当事人同意的，可以不提交中文翻译件。

**Article 9** Where the evidentiary materials submitted by a party to the International Commercial Court are formed outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, regardless of whether notarization, certification, or other authentication procedures have been completed, such materials shall be subject to cross-examination in court.  
Where the evidentiary materials submitted by a party are in English and with the consent of the opposing party, the party may not submit a Chinese translation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　国际商事法庭调查收集证据以及组织质证，可以采用视听传输技术及其他信息网络方式。

**Article 10** The International Commercial Court may use audio-visual transmission technology and other information network methods for investigating and collecting evidence and organizing cross-examination.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　最高人民法院组建国际商事专家委员会，并选定符合条件的国际商事调解机构、国际商事仲裁机构与国际商事法庭共同构建调解、仲裁、诉讼有机衔接的纠纷解决平台，形成“一站式”国际商事纠纷解决机制。  
国际商事法庭支持当事人通过调解、仲裁、诉讼有机衔接的纠纷解决平台，选择其认为适宜的方式解决国际商事纠纷。

**Article 11** The Supreme People's Court shall establish an International Commercial Expert Committee and select qualified international commercial mediation institutions and international commercial arbitration institutions to jointly construct with the International Commercial Court a dispute resolution platform that organically links mediation, arbitration, and litigation, forming a 'one-stop' international commercial dispute resolution mechanism.  
The International Commercial Court shall support parties in choosing through the dispute resolution platform that organically links mediation, arbitration, and litigation the methods they consider appropriate for resolving international commercial disputes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国际商事法庭在受理案件后七日内，经当事人同意，可以委托国际商事专家委员会成员或者国际商事调解机构调解。

**Article 12** Within seven days after accepting a case, with the consent of the parties, the International Commercial Court may entrust a member of the International Commercial Expert Committee or an international commercial mediation institution to conduct mediation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　经国际商事专家委员会成员或者国际商事调解机构主持调解，当事人达成调解协议的，国际商事法庭可以依照法律规定制发调解书；当事人要求发给判决书的，可以依协议的内容制作判决书送达当事人。

**Article 13** Where, after mediation presided over by a member of the International Commercial Expert Committee or an international commercial mediation institution, the parties reach a mediation agreement, the International Commercial Court may, in accordance with legal provisions, prepare and issue a mediation document; if a party requests a judgment, the court may prepare a judgment based on the content of the agreement and serve it to the parties.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　当事人协议选择[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一款规定的国际商事仲裁机构仲裁的，可以在申请仲裁前或者仲裁程序开始后，向国际商事法庭申请证据、财产或者行为保全。  
当事人向国际商事法庭申请撤销或者执行[本规定第十一条](#art-11)第一款规定的国际商事仲裁机构作出的仲裁裁决的，国际商事法庭依照民事诉讼法等相关法律规定进行审查。

**Article 14** Where the parties have agreed by agreement to submit to arbitration by an international commercial arbitration institution specified in paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Provisions, they may, before applying for arbitration or after the commencement of arbitral proceedings, apply to the International Commercial Court for preservation of evidence, property, or conduct.  
Where a party applies to the International Commercial Court for setting aside or enforcement of an arbitral award made by an international commercial arbitration institution specified in paragraph 1 of Article 11 of these Provisions, the International Commercial Court shall review the application in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law and other relevant legal provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　国际商事法庭作出的判决、裁定，是发生法律效力的判决、裁定。  
国际商事法庭作出的调解书，经双方当事人签收后，即具有与判决同等的法律效力。

**Article 15** Judgments and rulings made by the International Commercial Court shall be legally effective judgments and rulings.  
A mediation document made by the International Commercial Court shall, upon receipt and signature by both parties, have the same legal effect as a judgment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　当事人对国际商事法庭作出的已经发生法律效力的判决、裁定和调解书，可以依照民事诉讼法的规定向最高人民法院本部申请再审。  
最高人民法院本部受理前款规定的申请再审案件以及再审案件，均应当另行组成合议庭。

**Article 16** A party may apply for retrial to the headquarters of the Supreme People's Court in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law in respect of a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document made by the International Commercial Court.  
Where the headquarters of the Supreme People's Court accepts an application for retrial or a retrial case as specified in the preceding paragraph, a separate collegial panel shall be formed.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国际商事法庭作出的发生法律效力的判决、裁定和调解书，当事人可以向国际商事法庭申请执行。

**Article 17** With respect to a legally effective judgment, ruling, or mediation document made by the International Commercial Court, the party may apply to the International Commercial Court for enforcement.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　国际商事法庭通过电子诉讼服务平台、审判流程信息公开平台以及其他诉讼服务平台为诉讼参与人提供诉讼便利，并支持通过网络方式立案、缴费、阅卷、证据交换、送达、开庭等。

**Article 18** The International Commercial Court shall provide litigation convenience to litigation participants through the electronic litigation service platform, the adjudication process information disclosure platform, and other litigation service platforms, and shall support the use of online methods for case filing, fee payment, case file review, evidence exchange, service, and court hearings, among others.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定自2018年7月1日起施行。

**Article 19** These Provisions shall come into force as of July 1, 2018.
