# 最高人民法院关于经济合同的名称与内容不一致时如何确定管辖权问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法复〔1996〕16号  
**公布日期**：1996-11-13

---

### 正文

一、当事人签订的经济合同虽具有明确、规范的名称，但合同约定的权利义务内容与名称不一致的，应当以该合同约定的权利义务内容确定合同的性质，从而确定合同的履行地和法院的管辖权。

**Article None** 1. Where an economic contract signed by the parties has a definite and standardized name, but the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract are inconsistent with the name, the nature of the contract shall be determined based on the content of the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract, thereby determining the place of performance of the contract and the jurisdiction of the court.

二、合同的名称与合同约定的权利义务内容不一致，而且根据该合同约定的权利义务内容难以区分合同性质的，以及合同的名称与该合同约定的部分权利义务内容相符的，则以合同的名称确定合同的履行地和法院的管辖权。  
1996年11月13日

**Article None** 2. Where the name of the contract is inconsistent with the content of the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract, and it is difficult to distinguish the nature of the contract based on the content of the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract, and the name of the contract is consistent with part of the rights and obligations stipulated in the contract, the place of performance of the contract and the jurisdiction of the court shall be determined based on the name of the contract.  
November 13, 1996
