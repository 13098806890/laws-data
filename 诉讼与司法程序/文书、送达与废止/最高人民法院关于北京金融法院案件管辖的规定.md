# 最高人民法院关于北京金融法院案件管辖的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕7号  
**公布日期**：2021-03-16  
**生效日期**：2021-03-16

> （2021年3月1日最高人民法院审判委员会第1833次会议通过，自2021年3月16日起施行） 为服务和保障国家金融管理中心建设，进一步明确北京金融法院案件管辖的具体范围，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于设立北京金融法院的决定》等规定，制定本规定。 第一条　北京金融法院管辖北京市辖区内应由中级人民法院受理的下列第一审金融民商事案件： （一）证券、期货交易、营业信托、保险、票据、信用证、独立保函、保理、金融借款合同、银行卡、融资租赁合同、委托理财合同、储蓄存款合同、典当、银行结算合同等金融民商事纠纷； （二）资产管理业务、资产支持证券业务、私募基金业务、外汇业务、金融产品销售和适当性管理、征信业务、支付业务及经有权机关批准的其他金融业务引发的金融民商事纠纷； （三）涉金融机构的与公司有关的纠纷; （四）以金融机构为债务人的破产纠纷； （五）金融民商事纠纷的仲裁司法审查案件； （六）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件，以及申请承认和执行外国法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件。 第二条　下列金融纠纷案件，由北京金融法院管辖： （一）境内投资者以发生在中华人民共和国境外的证券发行、交易活动或者期货交易活动损害其合法权益为由向北京金融法院提起的诉讼； （二）境内个人或者机构以中华人民共和国境外金融机构销售的金融产品或者提供的金融服务损害其合法权益为由向北京金融法院提起的诉讼。 第三条　在全国中小企业股份转让系统向不特定合格投资者公开发行股票并在精选层挂牌的公司的证券发行纠纷、证券承销合同纠纷、证券交易合同纠纷、证券欺诈责任纠纷以及证券推荐保荐和持续督导合同、证券挂牌合同引起的纠纷等第一审民商事案件，由北京金融法院管辖。 第四条　以全国中小企业股份转让系统有限责任公司为被告或者第三人的与证券交易场所监管职能相关的第一审金融民商事和涉金融行政案件，由北京金融法院管辖。 第五条　以住所地在北京市并依法设立的金融基础设施机构为被告或者第三人的与其履行职责相关的第一审金融民商事案件，由北京金融法院管辖。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　北京金融法院管辖北京市辖区内应由中级人民法院受理的下列第一审金融民商事案件：  
（一）证券、期货交易、营业信托、保险、票据、信用证、独立保函、保理、金融借款合同、银行卡、融资租赁合同、委托理财合同、储蓄存款合同、典当、银行结算合同等金融民商事纠纷；  
（二）资产管理业务、资产支持证券业务、私募基金业务、外汇业务、金融产品销售和适当性管理、征信业务、支付业务及经有权机关批准的其他金融业务引发的金融民商事纠纷；  
（三）涉金融机构的与公司有关的纠纷;  
（四）以金融机构为债务人的破产纠纷；  
（五）金融民商事纠纷的仲裁司法审查案件；  
（六）申请认可和执行香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区、台湾地区法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件，以及申请承认和执行外国法院金融民商事纠纷的判决、裁定案件。

**Article 1** The Beijing Financial Court shall have jurisdiction over the following first-instance financial civil and commercial cases that shall be accepted by an Intermediate People's Court within the jurisdiction of Beijing Municipality:  
(1) financial civil and commercial disputes arising from securities and futures trading, business trusts, insurance, negotiable instruments, letters of credit, independent guarantees, factoring, financial loan contracts, bank cards, financial leasing contracts, entrusted wealth management contracts, savings deposit contracts, pawn, bank settlement contracts, and the like;  
(2) financial civil and commercial disputes arising from asset management businesses, asset-backed securities businesses, private fund businesses, foreign exchange businesses, financial product sales and suitability management, credit reporting businesses, payment businesses, and other financial businesses approved by competent authorities;  
(3) disputes relating to companies involving financial institutions;  
(4) bankruptcy disputes with a financial institution as the debtor;  
(5) cases for judicial review of arbitration in financial civil and commercial disputes;  
(6) cases for application for recognition and enforcement of judgments and rulings of courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Macao Special Administrative Region, and Taiwan region on financial civil and commercial disputes, as well as cases for application for recognition and enforcement of judgments and rulings of foreign courts on financial civil and commercial disputes.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　下列金融纠纷案件，由北京金融法院管辖：  
（一）境内投资者以发生在中华人民共和国境外的证券发行、交易活动或者期货交易活动损害其合法权益为由向北京金融法院提起的诉讼；  
（二）境内个人或者机构以中华人民共和国境外金融机构销售的金融产品或者提供的金融服务损害其合法权益为由向北京金融法院提起的诉讼。

**Article 2** The following financial dispute cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Financial Court:  
(1) lawsuits filed with the Beijing Financial Court by domestic investors on the grounds that securities issuance or trading activities or futures trading activities occurring outside the People's Republic of China have infringed upon their lawful rights and interests;  
(2) lawsuits filed with the Beijing Financial Court by domestic individuals or institutions on the grounds that financial products sold by or financial services provided by financial institutions outside the People's Republic of China have infringed upon their lawful rights and interests.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在全国中小企业股份转让系统向不特定合格投资者公开发行股票并在精选层挂牌的公司的证券发行纠纷、证券承销合同纠纷、证券交易合同纠纷、证券欺诈责任纠纷以及证券推荐保荐和持续督导合同、证券挂牌合同引起的纠纷等第一审民商事案件，由北京金融法院管辖。

**Article 3** The first-instance civil and commercial cases such as disputes over securities issuance, securities underwriting contracts, securities trading contracts, securities fraud liability, and disputes arising from securities recommendation and sponsorship and continuous supervision contracts, and securities listing contracts, of companies that have publicly issued shares to unspecified qualified investors and are listed on the Selected Layer of the National Equities Exchange and Quotations, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　以全国中小企业股份转让系统有限责任公司为被告或者第三人的与证券交易场所监管职能相关的第一审金融民商事和涉金融行政案件，由北京金融法院管辖。

**Article 4** First-instance financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that are related to the regulatory functions of a securities trading venue and in which the National Equities Exchange and Quotations Co., Ltd. is the defendant or a third party, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　以住所地在北京市并依法设立的金融基础设施机构为被告或者第三人的与其履行职责相关的第一审金融民商事案件，由北京金融法院管辖。

**Article 5** First-instance financial civil and commercial cases that are related to the performance of duties of a financial infrastructure institution established according to law with its domicile in Beijing Municipality, and in which such institution is the defendant or a third party, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　北京市辖区内应由中级人民法院受理的对中国人民银行、中国银行保险监督管理委员会、中国证券监督管理委员会、国家外汇管理局等国家金融管理部门以及其他国务院组成部门和法律、法规、规章授权的组织因履行金融监管职责作出的行政行为不服提起诉讼的第一审涉金融行政案件，由北京金融法院管辖。

**Article 6** The first-instance financial-related administrative cases that shall be accepted by an Intermediate People's Court within the jurisdiction of Beijing Municipality, where a lawsuit is filed against an administrative action taken by the People's Bank of China, the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, and other national financial regulatory authorities, as well as other constituent departments of the State Council and organizations authorized by laws, regulations, and rules, in the performance of their financial regulatory duties, shall be under the jurisdiction of the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人对北京市基层人民法院作出的涉及[本规定第一条](#art-1)第一至三项的第一审金融民商事案件和涉金融行政案件判决、裁定提起的上诉案件和申请再审案件，由北京金融法院审理。

**Article 7** Cases of appeal and applications for retrial filed by a party against judgments and rulings made by a Primary People's Court of Beijing Municipality on first-instance financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases involving Items (1) to (3) of Article 1 of these Provisions, shall be tried by the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　北京市辖区内应由中级人民法院受理的金融民商事案件、涉金融行政案件的再审案件，由北京金融法院审理。

**Article 8** The retrial cases of financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that shall be accepted by an Intermediate People's Court within the jurisdiction of Beijing Municipality, shall be tried by the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　北京金融法院作出的第一审民商事案件和涉金融行政案件生效裁判，以及北京市辖区内应由中级人民法院执行的涉金融民商事纠纷的仲裁裁决，由北京金融法院执行。  
北京金融法院执行过程中发生的执行异议案件、执行异议之诉案件，以及北京市基层人民法院涉金融案件执行过程中发生的执行复议案件、执行异议之诉上诉案件，由北京金融法院审理。

**Article 9** The effective judgments and rulings made by the Beijing Financial Court in first-instance civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases, as well as the arbitration awards on financial civil and commercial disputes that shall be enforced by an Intermediate People's Court within the jurisdiction of Beijing Municipality, shall be enforced by the Beijing Financial Court.  
Cases of enforcement objection, lawsuits of enforcement objection arising during the enforcement process of the Beijing Financial Court, as well as cases of enforcement review, and appeal cases of lawsuits of enforcement objection arising during the enforcement process of financial-related cases by the Primary People's Courts of Beijing Municipality, shall be tried by the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　中国人民银行、中国银行保险监督管理委员会、中国证券监督管理委员会、国家外汇管理局等国家金融管理部门，以及其他国务院组成部门因履行金融监管职责作为申请人的非诉行政执行案件，由北京金融法院审查和执行。

**Article 10** Non-litigation administrative enforcement cases in which the People's Bank of China, the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission, the China Securities Regulatory Commission, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, and other national financial regulatory authorities, as well as other constituent departments of the State Council, are the applicants as a result of performing financial regulatory duties, shall be reviewed and enforced by the Beijing Financial Court.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　当事人对北京金融法院作出的第一审判决、裁定提起的上诉案件，由北京市高级人民法院审理。

**Article 11** Appeal cases filed by a party against first-instance judgments and rulings made by the Beijing Financial Court shall be tried by the Beijing Higher People's Court.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　北京市各中级人民法院在北京金融法院成立前已经受理但尚未审结的金融民商事案件和涉金融行政案件，由该中级人民法院继续审理。

**Article 12** Financial civil and commercial cases and financial-related administrative cases that have been accepted but not yet concluded by the various Intermediate People's Courts of Beijing Municipality prior to the establishment of the Beijing Financial Court, shall continue to be tried by those Intermediate People's Courts.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本规定自2021年3月16日起施行。

**Article 13** These Provisions shall come into force as of March 16, 2021.
