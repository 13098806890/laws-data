# 人民法院在线调解规则

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕23号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-27  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 《人民法院在线调解规则》已于2021年12月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1859次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月30日 人民法院在线调解规则 法释〔2021〕23号 为方便当事人及时解决纠纷，规范依托人民法院调解平台开展的在线调解活动，提高多元化解纠纷效能，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等法律的规定，结合人民法院工作实际，制定本规则。 第一条　在立案前或者诉讼过程中依托人民法院调解平台开展在线调解的，适用本规则。 第二条　在线调解包括人民法院、当事人、调解组织或者调解员通过人民法院调解平台开展的在线申请、委派委托、音视频调解、制作调解协议、申请司法确认调解协议、制作调解书等全部或者部分调解活动。 第三条　民事、行政、执行、刑事自诉以及被告人、罪犯未被羁押的刑事附带民事诉讼等法律规定可以调解或者和解的纠纷，可以开展在线调解。 行政、刑事自诉和刑事附带民事诉讼案件的在线调解，法律和司法解释另有规定的，从其规定。 第四条　人民法院采用在线调解方式应当征得当事人同意，并综合考虑案件具体情况、技术条件等因素。 第五条　人民法院审判人员、专职或者兼职调解员、特邀调解组织和特邀调解员以及人民法院邀请的其他单位或者个人，可以开展在线调解。 在线调解组织和调解员的基本情况、纠纷受理范围、擅长领域、是否收费、作出邀请的人民法院等信息应当在人民法院调解平台进行公布，方便当事人选择。 第六条　人民法院可以邀请符合条件的外国人入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织的民商事纠纷。 符合条件的港澳地区居民可以入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区居民、法人或者非法人组织以及大陆港资澳资企业的民商事纠纷。 符合条件的台湾地区居民可以入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为台湾地区居民、法人或者非法人组织以及大陆台资企业的民商事纠纷。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　在立案前或者诉讼过程中依托人民法院调解平台开展在线调解的，适用本规则。

**Article 1** : These Rules shall apply to online mediation conducted by means of the People's Court Mediation Platform before case filing or during litigation proceedings.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在线调解包括人民法院、当事人、调解组织或者调解员通过人民法院调解平台开展的在线申请、委派委托、音视频调解、制作调解协议、申请司法确认调解协议、制作调解书等全部或者部分调解活动。

**Article 2** : Online mediation includes all or part of the mediation activities conducted by the People's Court, the parties, mediation organizations or mediators through the People's Court Mediation Platform, such as online applications, referral and entrustment, audio-video mediation, preparation of mediation agreements, application for judicial confirmation of mediation agreements, and preparation of mediation documents.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　民事、行政、执行、刑事自诉以及被告人、罪犯未被羁押的刑事附带民事诉讼等法律规定可以调解或者和解的纠纷，可以开展在线调解。  
行政、刑事自诉和刑事附带民事诉讼案件的在线调解，法律和司法解释另有规定的，从其规定。

**Article 3** : Online mediation may be conducted for disputes that may be mediated or settled according to law, such as civil, administrative, enforcement, private prosecution, and criminal incidental civil actions where the defendant or criminal is not in custody.  
For online mediation of administrative, private prosecution and criminal incidental civil action cases, if laws and judicial interpretations provide otherwise, those provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院采用在线调解方式应当征得当事人同意，并综合考虑案件具体情况、技术条件等因素。

**Article 4** : A People's Court that adopts online mediation shall obtain the consent of the parties and shall consider comprehensively the specific circumstances of the case, technical conditions, and other factors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院审判人员、专职或者兼职调解员、特邀调解组织和特邀调解员以及人民法院邀请的其他单位或者个人，可以开展在线调解。  
在线调解组织和调解员的基本情况、纠纷受理范围、擅长领域、是否收费、作出邀请的人民法院等信息应当在人民法院调解平台进行公布，方便当事人选择。

**Article 5** : Judicial officers, full-time or part-time mediators, specially invited mediation organizations and specially invited mediators, and other entities or individuals invited by the People's Court may conduct online mediation.  
Basic information of online mediation organizations and mediators, the scope of disputes accepted, areas of expertise, whether fees are charged, and the inviting People's Court shall be published on the People's Court Mediation Platform to facilitate the choice of the parties.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院可以邀请符合条件的外国人入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为外国人、无国籍人、外国企业或者组织的民商事纠纷。  
符合条件的港澳地区居民可以入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区居民、法人或者非法人组织以及大陆港资澳资企业的民商事纠纷。  
符合条件的台湾地区居民可以入驻人民法院调解平台，参与调解当事人一方或者双方为台湾地区居民、法人或者非法人组织以及大陆台资企业的民商事纠纷。

**Article 6** : The People's Court may invite foreigners who meet the conditions to join the People's Court Mediation Platform and participate in the mediation of civil and commercial disputes where one or both parties are foreigners, stateless persons, foreign enterprises or organizations.  
Residents of Hong Kong and Macao who meet the conditions may join the People's Court Mediation Platform and participate in the mediation of civil and commercial disputes where one or both parties are residents, legal persons or unincorporated organizations of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region, or Hong Kong-invested or Macao-invested enterprises in the mainland.  
Residents of the Taiwan region who meet the conditions may join the People's Court Mediation Platform and participate in the mediation of civil and commercial disputes where one or both parties are residents, legal persons or unincorporated organizations of the Taiwan region, or Taiwan-invested enterprises in the mainland.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院立案人员、审判人员在立案前或者诉讼过程中，认为纠纷适宜在线调解的，可以通过口头、书面、在线等方式充分释明在线调解的优势，告知在线调解的主要形式、权利义务、法律后果和操作方法等，引导当事人优先选择在线调解方式解决纠纷。

**Article 7** : Where case-filing personnel or judicial officers of a People's Court consider a dispute suitable for online mediation before case filing or during litigation, they may fully explain the advantages of online mediation by oral, written, online or other means, inform the parties of the main forms, rights and obligations, legal consequences and operational methods of online mediation, and guide the parties to choose online mediation as the preferred method for dispute resolution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　当事人同意在线调解的，应当在人民法院调解平台填写身份信息、纠纷简要情况、有效联系电话以及接收诉讼文书电子送达地址等，并上传电子化起诉申请材料。当事人在电子诉讼平台已经提交过电子化起诉申请材料的，不再重复提交。  
当事人填写或者提交电子化起诉申请材料确有困难的，人民法院可以辅助当事人将纸质材料作电子化处理后导入人民法院调解平台。

**Article 8** : A party that consents to online mediation shall fill in identity information, a brief description of the dispute, a valid contact telephone number, and an address for electronic service of litigation documents, etc. on the People's Court Mediation Platform, and upload electronic prosecution application materials. If the party has already submitted electronic prosecution application materials on the electronic litigation platform, it shall not submit them again.  
If a party has genuine difficulty in filling in or submitting electronic prosecution application materials, the People's Court may assist the party in converting the paper materials into electronic form and importing them into the People's Court Mediation Platform.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　当事人在立案前申请在线调解，属于下列情形之一的，人民法院退回申请并分别予以处理：  
（一）当事人申请调解的纠纷不属于人民法院受案范围，告知可以采用的其他纠纷解决方式；  
（二）与当事人选择的在线调解组织或者调解员建立邀请关系的人民法院对该纠纷不具有管辖权，告知选择对纠纷有管辖权的人民法院邀请的调解组织或者调解员进行调解；  
（三）当事人申请调解的纠纷不适宜在线调解，告知到人民法院诉讼服务大厅现场办理调解或者立案手续。

**Article 9** : Where a party applies for online mediation before case filing and falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall return the application and handle it respectively:  
(1) The dispute for which the party applies for mediation is not within the scope of cases accepted by the People's Court, it shall inform the party of other dispute resolution methods that may be used;  
(2) The People's Court that has established an invitation relationship with the online mediation organization or mediator chosen by the party does not have jurisdiction over the dispute, it shall inform the party to choose a mediation organization or mediator invited by the People's Court with jurisdiction over the dispute for mediation;  
(3) The dispute for which the party applies for mediation is not suitable for online mediation, it shall inform the party to go to the litigation service hall of the People's Court to handle mediation or case filing procedures on site.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　当事人一方在立案前同意在线调解的，由人民法院征求其意见后指定调解组织或者调解员。  
当事人双方同意在线调解的，可以在案件管辖法院确认的在线调解组织和调解员中共同选择调解组织或者调解员。当事人同意由人民法院指定调解组织或者调解员，或者无法在同意在线调解后两个工作日内共同选择调解组织或者调解员的，由人民法院指定调解组织或者调解员。  
人民法院应当在收到当事人在线调解申请后三个工作日内指定调解组织或者调解员。

**Article 10** : Where one party consents to online mediation before case filing, the People's Court shall, after consulting its opinion, designate a mediation organization or mediator.  
Where both parties consent to online mediation, they may jointly select a mediation organization or mediator from among the online mediation organizations and mediators confirmed by the court with jurisdiction over the case. If the parties agree that the People's Court shall designate a mediation organization or mediator, or fail to jointly select a mediation organization or mediator within two working days after consenting to online mediation, the People's Court shall designate a mediation organization or mediator.  
The People's Court shall designate a mediation organization or mediator within three working days after receiving the party's online mediation application.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在线调解一般由一名调解员进行，案件重大、疑难复杂或者具有较强专业性的，可以由两名以上调解员调解，并由当事人共同选定其中一人主持调解。无法共同选定的，由人民法院指定一名调解员主持。

**Article 11** : Online mediation shall generally be conducted by one mediator. If the case is major, difficult, complex or highly specialized, it may be mediated by two or more mediators, and the parties shall jointly select one of them to preside over the mediation. If they fail to jointly select, the People's Court shall designate a mediator to preside.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　调解组织或者调解员应当在收到人民法院委派委托调解信息或者当事人在线调解申请后三个工作日内，确认接受人民法院委派委托或者当事人调解申请。纠纷不符合调解组织章程规定的调解范围或者行业领域，明显超出调解员擅长领域或者具有其他不适宜接受情形的，调解组织或者调解员可以写明理由后不予接受。  
调解组织或者调解员不予接受或者超过规定期限未予确认的，人民法院、当事人可以重新指定或者选定。

**Article 12** : A mediation organization or mediator shall, within three working days after receiving the information on the People's Court's referral or entrustment for mediation or the party's online mediation application, confirm acceptance of the People's Court's referral or entrustment or the party's mediation application. If the dispute does not fall within the mediation scope or industry area provided for in the mediation organization's charter, clearly exceeds the mediator's area of expertise, or has other circumstances unsuitable for acceptance, the mediation organization or mediator may state the reasons and not accept it.  
If the mediation organization or mediator does not accept or fails to confirm within the specified time limit, the People's Court or the parties may designate or select anew.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　主持或者参与在线调解的人员有下列情形之一，应当在接受调解前或者调解过程中进行披露：  
（一）是纠纷当事人或者当事人、诉讼代理人近亲属的；  
（二）与纠纷有利害关系的；  
（三）与当事人、诉讼代理人有其他可能影响公正调解关系的。  
当事人在调解组织或者调解员披露上述情形后或者明知其具有上述情形，仍同意调解的，由该调解组织或者调解员继续调解。

**Article 13** : If a person presiding over or participating in online mediation falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall disclose such circumstances before accepting the mediation or during the mediation process:  
(1) Being a party to the dispute or a close relative of a party or litigation agent;  
(2) Having an interest in the dispute;  
(3) Having other relationships with the party or litigation agent that may affect impartial mediation.  
If a party still consents to mediation after the mediation organization or mediator discloses the above circumstances or knowing that such circumstances exist, the mediation organization or mediator shall continue the mediation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　在线调解过程中，当事人可以申请更换调解组织或者调解员；更换后，当事人仍不同意且拒绝自行选择的，视为当事人拒绝调解。

**Article 14** : During online mediation, a party may apply for replacement of the mediation organization or mediator. If after replacement the party still does not agree and refuses to make its own choice, it shall be deemed that the party refuses mediation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院对当事人一方立案前申请在线调解的，应当征询对方当事人的调解意愿。调解员可以在接受人民法院委派调解之日起三个工作日内协助人民法院通知对方当事人，询问是否同意调解。  
对方当事人拒绝调解或者无法联系对方当事人的，调解员应当写明原因，终结在线调解程序，即时将相关材料退回人民法院，并告知当事人。

**Article 15** : Where a party applies for online mediation before case filing, the People's Court shall consult the other party's willingness to mediate. The mediator may, within three working days from the date of accepting the People's Court's entrustment for mediation, assist the People's Court in notifying the other party and inquiring whether it agrees to mediate.  
If the other party refuses mediation or cannot be contacted, the mediator shall state the reasons, terminate the online mediation procedure, immediately return the relevant materials to the People's Court, and inform the party.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　主持在线调解的人员应当在组织调解前确认当事人参与调解的方式，并按照下列情形作出处理：  
（一）各方当事人均具备使用音视频技术条件的，指定在同一时间登录人民法院调解平台；无法在同一时间登录的，征得各方当事人同意后，分别指定时间开展音视频调解；  
（二）部分当事人不具备使用音视频技术条件的，在人民法院诉讼服务中心、调解组织所在地或者其他便利地点，为其参与在线调解提供场所和音视频设备。  
各方当事人均不具备使用音视频技术条件或者拒绝通过音视频方式调解的，确定现场调解的时间、地点。  
在线调解过程中，部分当事人提出不宜通过音视频方式调解的，调解员在征得其他当事人同意后，可以组织现场调解。

**Article 16** : The person presiding over online mediation shall, before organizing the mediation, confirm the method by which the parties will participate in the mediation and handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If all parties have the technical conditions for using audio and video, designate them to log into the People's Court Mediation Platform at the same time; if they cannot log in at the same time, after obtaining the consent of all parties, designate separate times for conducting audio-video mediation;  
(2) If some parties do not have the technical conditions for using audio and video, provide venues and audio-video equipment for their participation in online mediation at the litigation service center of the People's Court, the location of the mediation organization, or other convenient places.  
If none of the parties have the technical conditions for using audio and video or refuse to mediate through audio-video means, determine the time and place for on-site mediation.  
During the online mediation process, if some parties propose that mediation through audio-video means is inappropriate, the mediator may, after obtaining the consent of the other parties, organize on-site mediation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在线调解开始前，主持调解的人员应当通过证件证照在线比对等方式核实当事人和其他参与调解人员的身份，告知虚假调解法律后果。立案前调解的，调解员还应当指导当事人填写《送达地址确认书》等相关材料。

**Article 17** : Before the start of online mediation, the person presiding over the mediation shall verify the identities of the parties and other participants in the mediation by means such as online comparison of certificates and licenses, and inform them of the legal consequences of false mediation. If mediation is conducted before case filing, the mediator shall also guide the parties in filling in the "Confirmation of Service Address" and other relevant materials.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　在线调解过程中，当事人可以通过语音、文字、视频等形式自主表达意愿，提出纠纷解决方案。除共同确认的无争议事实外，当事人为达成调解协议作出妥协而认可的事实、证据等，不得在诉讼程序中作为对其不利的依据或者证据，但法律另有规定或者当事人均同意的除外。

**Article 18** : During online mediation, a party may freely express its wishes and propose dispute resolution plans through voice, text, video, or other forms. Except for undisputed facts confirmed jointly, facts or evidence admitted by a party as a compromise for reaching a mediation agreement shall not be used as a basis or evidence against it in litigation proceedings, unless otherwise provided by law or all parties consent.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　调解员组织当事人就所有或者部分调解请求达成一致意见的，应当在线制作或者上传调解协议，当事人和调解员应当在调解协议上进行电子签章；由调解组织主持达成调解协议的，还应当加盖调解组织电子印章，调解组织没有电子印章的，可以将加盖印章的调解协议上传至人民法院调解平台。  
调解协议自各方当事人均完成电子签章之时起发生法律效力，并通过人民法院调解平台向当事人送达。调解协议有给付内容的，当事人应当按照调解协议约定内容主动履行。

**Article 19** : If a mediator organizes the parties to reach a consensus on all or part of the mediation requests, it shall prepare or upload the mediation agreement online, and the parties and the mediator shall affix their electronic signatures on the mediation agreement. If a mediation agreement is reached under the auspices of a mediation organization, the electronic seal of the mediation organization shall also be affixed; if the mediation organization does not have an electronic seal, the sealed mediation agreement may be uploaded to the People's Court Mediation Platform.  
The mediation agreement shall take legal effect from the time when all parties have completed their electronic signatures, and shall be served on the parties through the People's Court Mediation Platform. If the mediation agreement contains a performance obligation, the parties shall voluntarily perform in accordance with the content of the mediation agreement.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各方当事人在立案前达成调解协议的，调解员应当记入调解笔录并按诉讼外调解结案，引导当事人自动履行。依照法律和司法解释规定可以申请司法确认调解协议的，当事人可以在线提出申请，人民法院经审查符合法律规定的，裁定调解协议有效。  
各方当事人在立案后达成调解协议的，可以请求人民法院制作调解书或者申请撤诉。人民法院经审查符合法律规定的，可以制作调解书或者裁定书结案。

**Article 20** : If all parties reach a mediation agreement before case filing, the mediator shall record it in the mediation transcript and close the case as an extra-litigation mediation, and guide the parties to voluntarily perform. If judicial confirmation of the mediation agreement may be applied for according to laws and judicial interpretations, the parties may apply online. After examination by the People's Court and if it complies with the law, it shall rule that the mediation agreement is effective.  
If all parties reach a mediation agreement after case filing, they may request the People's Court to prepare a mediation document or apply for withdrawal of the prosecution. After examination by the People's Court and if it complies with the law, it may close the case by preparing a mediation document or a ruling.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　经在线调解达不成调解协议，调解组织或者调解员应当记录调解基本情况、调解不成的原因、导致其他当事人诉讼成本增加的行为以及需要向人民法院提示的其他情况。人民法院按照下列情形作出处理：  
（一）当事人在立案前申请在线调解的，调解组织或者调解员可以建议通过在线立案或者其他途径解决纠纷，当事人选择在线立案的，调解组织或者调解员应当将电子化调解材料在线推送给人民法院，由人民法院在法定期限内依法登记立案；  
（二）立案前委派调解的，调解不成后，人民法院应当依法登记立案；  
（三）立案后委托调解的，调解不成后，人民法院应当恢复审理。  
审判人员在诉讼过程中组织在线调解的，调解不成后，应当及时审判。

**Article 21** : If no mediation agreement is reached through online mediation, the mediation organization or mediator shall record the basic circumstances of the mediation, the reasons for the failure of mediation, any conduct that causes an increase in the litigation costs of other parties, and other circumstances that need to be brought to the attention of the People's Court. The People's Court shall handle the matter according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If a party applied for online mediation before case filing, the mediation organization or mediator may recommend resolving the dispute through online case filing or other channels. If the party chooses online case filing, the mediation organization or mediator shall push the electronic mediation materials online to the People's Court, and the People's Court shall register the case according to law within the statutory time limit;  
(2) If mediation is referred before case filing and mediation fails, the People's Court shall register the case according to law;  
(3) If mediation is entrusted after case filing and mediation fails, the People's Court shall resume the trial.  
If a judicial officer organizes online mediation during litigation and mediation fails, it shall adjudicate promptly.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　调解员在线调解过程中，同步形成电子笔录，并确认无争议事实。经当事人双方明确表示同意的，可以以调解录音录像代替电子笔录，但无争议事实应当以书面形式确认。  
电子笔录以在线方式核对确认后，与书面笔录具有同等法律效力。

**Article 22** : During online mediation, the mediator shall simultaneously create electronic transcripts and confirm undisputed facts. If both parties expressly consent, the mediation audio recording and video may replace the electronic transcript, but the undisputed facts shall be confirmed in writing.  
After the electronic transcript is verified and confirmed online, it shall have the same legal effect as a written transcript.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民法院在审查司法确认申请或者出具调解书过程中，发现当事人可能采取恶意串通、伪造证据、捏造事实、虚构法律关系等手段实施虚假调解行为，侵害他人合法权益的，可以要求当事人提供相关证据。当事人不提供相关证据的，人民法院不予确认调解协议效力或者出具调解书。  
经审查认为构成虚假调解的，依照《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等相关法律规定处理。发现涉嫌刑事犯罪的，及时将线索和材料移送有管辖权的机关。

**Article 23** : If during the review of an application for judicial confirmation or the issuance of a mediation document, the People's Court discovers that a party may have engaged in false mediation by means such as malicious collusion, forgery of evidence, fabrication of facts, or fictitious legal relationships, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of others, it may require the party to provide relevant evidence. If the party fails to provide relevant evidence, the People's Court shall not confirm the validity of the mediation agreement or issue a mediation document.  
If upon review it is determined that false mediation has been constituted, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant legal provisions. If suspected criminal offenses are discovered, the clues and materials shall be promptly transferred to the competent authorities.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　立案前在线调解期限为三十日。各方当事人同意延长的，不受此限。立案后在线调解，适用普通程序的调解期限为十五日，适用简易程序的调解期限为七日，各方当事人同意延长的，不受此限。立案后延长的调解期限不计入审理期限。  
委派委托调解或者当事人申请调解的调解期限，自调解组织或者调解员在人民法院调解平台确认接受委派委托或者确认接受当事人申请之日起算。审判人员主持调解的，自各方当事人同意之日起算。

**Article 24** : The period for online mediation before case filing is thirty days. If all parties consent to an extension, this limit shall not apply. For online mediation after case filing, the mediation period for ordinary procedure is fifteen days, and for summary procedure is seven days; if all parties consent to an extension, this limit shall not apply. The extended mediation period after case filing shall not be included in the trial period.  
The mediation period for referred or entrusted mediation or for mediation applied by a party shall be calculated from the date on which the mediation organization or mediator confirms acceptance of the referral or entrustment or confirmation of acceptance of the party's application on the People's Court Mediation Platform. If a judicial officer presides over the mediation, it shall be calculated from the date on which all parties consent.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　有下列情形之一的，在线调解程序终结：  
（一）当事人达成调解协议；  
（二）当事人自行和解，撤回调解申请；  
（三）在调解期限内无法联系到当事人；  
（四）当事人一方明确表示不愿意继续调解；  
（五）当事人分歧较大且难以达成调解协议；  
（六）调解期限届满，未达成调解协议，且各方当事人未达成延长调解期限的合意；  
（七）当事人一方拒绝在调解协议上签章；  
（八）其他导致调解无法进行的情形。

**Article 25** : The online mediation procedure shall be terminated under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The parties have reached a mediation agreement;  
(2) The parties have reached a settlement on their own and withdrawn the mediation application;  
(3) The party cannot be contacted within the mediation period;  
(4) One party expressly indicates that it is unwilling to continue mediation;  
(5) The parties have significant differences and it is difficult to reach a mediation agreement;  
(6) The mediation period has expired and no mediation agreement has been reached, and the parties have not reached a consensus on extending the mediation period;  
(7) One party refuses to sign or affix a seal on the mediation agreement;  
(8) Other circumstances that render mediation impossible.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　立案前调解需要鉴定评估的，人民法院工作人员、调解组织或者调解员可以告知当事人诉前委托鉴定程序，指导通过电子诉讼平台或者现场办理等方式提交诉前委托鉴定评估申请，鉴定评估期限不计入调解期限。  
诉前委托鉴定评估经人民法院审查符合法律规定的，可以作为证据使用。

**Article 26** : If mediation before case filing requires expert evaluation, the personnel of the People's Court, the mediation organization or mediator may inform the parties of the pre-litigation entrusted expert evaluation procedure, guide them to submit an application for pre-litigation entrusted expert evaluation through the electronic litigation platform or on-site procedures, and the period for expert evaluation shall not be included in the mediation period.  
If the pre-litigation entrusted expert evaluation is examined by the People's Court and conforms to the law, it may be used as evidence.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各级人民法院负责本级在线调解组织和调解员选任确认、业务培训、资质认证、指导入驻、权限设置、业绩评价等管理工作。上级人民法院选任的在线调解组织和调解员，下级人民法院在征得其同意后可以确认为本院在线调解组织和调解员。

**Article 27** : People's Courts at all levels shall be responsible for the management of the selection and confirmation of online mediation organizations and mediators at their level, professional training, qualification certification, guidance for joining, permission settings, performance evaluation, etc. Online mediation organizations and mediators selected by a higher People's Court may, after obtaining their consent, be confirmed by a lower People's Court as its own online mediation organizations and mediators.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　人民法院可以建立婚姻家庭、劳动争议、道路交通、金融消费、证券期货、知识产权、海事海商、国际商事和涉港澳台侨纠纷等专业行业特邀调解名册，按照不同专业邀请具备相关专业能力的组织和人员加入。  
最高人民法院建立全国性特邀调解名册，邀请全国人大代表、全国政协委员、知名专家学者、具有较高知名度的调解组织以及较强调解能力的人员加入，参与调解全国法院有重大影响、疑难复杂、适宜调解的案件。  
高级人民法院、中级人民法院可以建立区域性特邀调解名册，参与本辖区法院案件的调解。

**Article 28** : People's Courts may establish special invitation mediation rosters for professional industries such as marriage and family, labor disputes, road traffic, financial consumption, securities and futures, intellectual property, maritime affairs, international commercial matters, and disputes involving Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and overseas Chinese, and invite organizations and individuals with relevant professional capabilities to join according to different specialties.  
The Supreme People's Court shall establish a national special invitation mediation roster, inviting deputies to the National People's Congress, members of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, well-known experts and scholars, mediation organizations with high reputation, and individuals with strong mediation capabilities to join, to participate in mediating cases that have significant impact, are difficult and complex, and are suitable for mediation in courts nationwide.  
Higher People's Courts and Intermediate People's Courts may establish regional special invitation mediation rosters to participate in the mediation of cases in courts within their jurisdiction.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　在线调解组织和调解员在调解过程中，存在下列行为之一的，当事人可以向作出邀请的人民法院投诉：  
（一）强迫调解；  
（二）无正当理由多次拒绝接受人民法院委派委托或者当事人调解申请；  
（三）接受当事人请托或者收受财物；  
（四）泄露调解过程、调解协议内容以及调解过程中获悉的国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私和其他不宜公开的信息，但法律和行政法规另有规定的除外；  
（五）其他违反调解职业道德应当作出处理的行为。  
人民法院经核查属实的，应当视情形作出解聘等相应处理，并告知有关主管部门。

**Article 29** : If an online mediation organization or mediator commits any of the following acts during the mediation process, a party may file a complaint with the People's Court that made the invitation:  
(1) Compelling mediation;  
(2) Refusing to accept the People's Court's referral or entrustment or a party's mediation application multiple times without justifiable reasons;  
(3) Accepting a party's entrustment or accepting property;  
(4) Disclosing the mediation process, the content of the mediation agreement, and state secrets, trade secrets, personal privacy, or other information not suitable for disclosure learned during the mediation process, unless otherwise provided by laws and administrative regulations;  
(5) Other acts that violate mediation professional ethics and should be dealt with.  
If after verification the People's Court finds the complaint to be true, it shall, depending on the circumstances, impose dismissal or other corresponding measures, and shall inform the relevant competent authorities.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本规则自2022年1月1日起施行。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本规则不一致的，以本规则为准。

**Article 30** : These Rules shall come into force as of January 1, 2022. If any judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Rules, these Rules shall prevail.
