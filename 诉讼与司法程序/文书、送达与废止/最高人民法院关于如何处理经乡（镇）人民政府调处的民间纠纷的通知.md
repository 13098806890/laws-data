# 最高人民法院关于如何处理经乡（镇）人民政府调处的民间纠纷的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕21号  
**公布日期**：1993-09-03

---

### 正文

一、民间纠纷未经司法助理员调解或者乡（镇）人民政府调处，当事人直接向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理，不得以未经调处为由拒绝受理。

**Article None** 1. Where a civil dispute has not been mediated by a judicial assistant or handled by a township (town) people's government, and a party directly prosecutes in a People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law and shall not refuse acceptance on the grounds that it has not been handled.

二、民间纠纷经司法助理员调解，当事人未达成协议或者达成协议后又反悔，如果一方直接向人民法院起诉，或者先请求乡（镇）人民政府处理但不服调处决定而就原争议标的向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理，以原纠纷的双方为案件当事人。

**Article None** 2. Where a civil dispute has been mediated by a judicial assistant, and the parties have not reached an agreement or have repudiated the agreement after reaching it, if one party directly prosecutes in a People's Court, or first requests the township (town) people's government to handle the matter but is dissatisfied with the handling decision and prosecutes in a People's Court regarding the original subject matter of the dispute, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law, and the parties to the original dispute shall be the parties to the case.

三、人民法院对经司法助理员和乡（镇）人民政府调处的民间纠纷，经审理后应当依法作出判决、裁定或者制作调解书。法律文书的内容不应涉及是否维持、变更或者撤销原调处意见，但如果原来所作处理有违背法律、法规的情况，应当依法予以纠正。

**Article None** 3. For a civil dispute that has been handled by a judicial assistant and a township (town) people's government, after trial, the People's Court shall according to law render a judgment, ruling, or prepare a mediation document. The content of the legal instrument shall not involve whether to uphold, modify, or revoke the original handling opinion, but if the original handling violates laws or regulations, it shall be corrected according to law.

四、民间纠纷经司法助理员调解达成的协议或者经乡（镇）人民政府所作的调处决定，当事人向人民法院申请强制执行的，人民法院不予执行。  
1993年9月3日

**Article None** 4. Where a party applies to a People's Court for compulsory execution of an agreement reached through mediation by a judicial assistant or a handling decision made by a township (town) people's government regarding a civil dispute, the People's Court shall not enforce it.  
September 3, 1993
