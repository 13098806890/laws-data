有重大失实行为如何适用法律问题的批复  
（2009年1月6日最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会  
第七次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2009〕1号  
甘肃省人民检察院：  
你院《关于公证员出具证明文件重大失实是否构成犯罪的请示》（甘检发研〔2008〕17号）收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
《中华人民共和国公证法》施行以后，公证员在履行公证职责过程中，严重不负责任，出具的公证书有重大失实，造成严重后果的，依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第三款的规定，以出具证明文件重大失实罪追究刑事责任。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2009年1月7日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
January 7, 2009  
  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on How to Apply the Law to the Act of a Notary Issuing a Notarial Certificate with Major Inaccuracies  
(Adopted at the Seventh Session of the Eleventh Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on January 6, 2009)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2009] No. 1  
  
People's Procuratorate of Gansu Province:  
  
Your Request for Instructions on Whether a Notary's Issuance of a Certificate with Major Inaccuracies Constitutes a Crime (Gan Jian Fa Yan [2008] No. 17) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
After the Notarization Law of the People's Republic of China came into force, where a notary, in the course of performing notarial duties, seriously neglects responsibilities and issues a notarial certificate containing major inaccuracies, causing serious consequences, the notary shall, in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 3 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of issuing a certificate with major inaccuracies.  
  
Hereby this reply.  
  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
January 7, 2009
