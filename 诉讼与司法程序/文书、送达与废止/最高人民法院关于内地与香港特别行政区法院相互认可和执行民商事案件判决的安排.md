# 最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行民商事案件判决的安排

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕2号  
**公布日期**：2019-01-14  
**生效日期**：2024-01-29

> 《最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行民商事案件判决的安排》已于2019年1月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1759次会议通过，现予公布，自2024年1月29日起施行。 2024年1月25日 最高人民法院 关于内地与香港特别行政区法院 相互认可和执行民商事案件判决的安排 法释〔2024〕2号 根据《中华人民共和国香港特别行政区基本法》第九十五条的规定，最高人民法院与香港特别行政区政府经协商，现就民商事案件判决的相互认可和执行问题作出如下安排。 第一条　内地与香港特别行政区法院民商事案件生效判决的相互认可和执行，适用本安排。 刑事案件中有关民事赔偿的生效判决的相互认可和执行，亦适用本安排。 第二条　本安排所称“民商事案件”是指依据内地和香港特别行政区法律均属于民商事性质的案件，不包括香港特别行政区法院审理的司法复核案件以及其他因行使行政权力直接引发的案件。 第三条　本安排暂不适用于就下列民商事案件作出的判决： （一）内地人民法院审理的赡养、兄弟姐妹之间扶养、解除收养关系、成年人监护权、离婚后损害责任、同居关系析产案件，香港特别行政区法院审理的应否裁判分居的案件； （二）继承案件、遗产管理或者分配的案件； （三）内地人民法院审理的有关发明专利、实用新型专利侵权的案件，香港特别行政区法院审理的有关标准专利（包括原授专利）、短期专利侵权的案件，内地与香港特别行政区法院审理的有关确认标准必要专利许可费率的案件，以及有关本安排第五条未规定的知识产权案件； （四）海洋环境污染、海事索赔责任限制、共同海损、紧急拖航和救助、船舶优先权、海上旅客运输案件；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　内地与香港特别行政区法院民商事案件生效判决的相互认可和执行，适用本安排。  
刑事案件中有关民事赔偿的生效判决的相互认可和执行，亦适用本安排。

**Article 1** The mutual recognition and enforcement of effective judgments in civil and commercial cases between the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall be governed by this Arrangement.  
The mutual recognition and enforcement of effective judgments concerning civil compensation in criminal cases shall also be governed by this Arrangement.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本安排所称“民商事案件”是指依据内地和香港特别行政区法律均属于民商事性质的案件，不包括香港特别行政区法院审理的司法复核案件以及其他因行使行政权力直接引发的案件。

**Article 2** The term "civil and commercial cases" as used in this Arrangement refers to cases that are of a civil and commercial nature under the laws of both the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, excluding judicial review cases tried by the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and other cases directly arising from the exercise of administrative powers.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　本安排暂不适用于就下列民商事案件作出的判决：  
（一）内地人民法院审理的赡养、兄弟姐妹之间扶养、解除收养关系、成年人监护权、离婚后损害责任、同居关系析产案件，香港特别行政区法院审理的应否裁判分居的案件；  
（二）继承案件、遗产管理或者分配的案件；  
（三）内地人民法院审理的有关发明专利、实用新型专利侵权的案件，香港特别行政区法院审理的有关标准专利（包括原授专利）、短期专利侵权的案件，内地与香港特别行政区法院审理的有关确认标准必要专利许可费率的案件，以及有关本安排第五条未规定的知识产权案件；  
（四）海洋环境污染、海事索赔责任限制、共同海损、紧急拖航和救助、船舶优先权、海上旅客运输案件；  
（五）破产（清盘）案件；  
（六）确定选民资格、宣告自然人失踪或者死亡、认定自然人限制或者无民事行为能力的案件；  
（七）确认仲裁协议效力、撤销仲裁裁决案件；  
（八）认可和执行其他国家和地区判决、仲裁裁决的案件。

**Article 3** This Arrangement shall not apply for the time being to judgments rendered in the following civil and commercial cases:  
(1) Cases concerning maintenance, support between siblings, dissolution of adoption, guardianship of adults, liability for damages after divorce, and division of property upon dissolution of cohabitation tried by the Mainland People's Courts, and cases concerning whether a decree of judicial separation should be granted tried by the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;  
(2) Cases of succession, estate administration or distribution;  
(3) Cases concerning infringement of invention patents and utility model patents tried by the Mainland People's Courts, cases concerning infringement of standard patents (including original grant patents) and short-term patents tried by the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, cases concerning the determination of licensing rates for standard essential patents tried by the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, and intellectual property cases not provided for in Article 5 of this Arrangement;  
(4) Cases of marine environmental pollution, limitation of liability for maritime claims, general average, emergency towage and salvage, maritime liens, and carriage of passengers by sea;  
(5) Bankruptcy (winding-up) cases;  
(6) Cases concerning the determination of voter qualifications, declaration of a natural person as missing or dead, and determination of a natural person as having limited or no capacity for civil conduct;  
(7) Cases concerning the confirmation of the validity of arbitration agreements and the setting aside of arbitral awards;  
(8) Cases concerning the recognition and enforcement of judgments and arbitral awards of other countries and regions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　本安排所称“判决”，在内地包括判决、裁定、调解书、支付令，不包括保全裁定；在香港特别行政区包括判决、命令、判令、讼费评定证明书，不包括禁诉令、临时济助命令。  
本安排所称“生效判决”：  
（一）在内地，是指第二审判决，依法不准上诉或者超过法定期限没有上诉的第一审判决，以及依照审判监督程序作出的上述判决；  
（二）在香港特别行政区，是指终审法院、高等法院上诉法庭及原讼法庭、区域法院以及劳资审裁处、土地审裁处、小额钱债审裁处、竞争事务审裁处作出的已经发生法律效力的判决。

**Article 4** The term "judgment" as used in this Arrangement includes, in the Mainland, judgments, rulings, mediation statements, and orders of payment, but does not include preservation rulings; in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, it includes judgments, orders, decrees, and costs certificates, but does not include anti-suit injunctions or interim relief orders.  
The term "effective judgment" as used in this Arrangement:  
(1) In the Mainland, refers to judgments of second instance, judgments of first instance that are not subject to appeal according to law or that have not been appealed within the statutory time limit, and the aforesaid judgments rendered through retrial procedures;  
(2) In the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, refers to judgments that have become legally effective and are rendered by the Court of Final Appeal, the Court of Appeal and the Court of First Instance of the High Court, the District Court, the Labour Tribunal, the Lands Tribunal, the Small Claims Tribunal, and the Competition Tribunal.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本安排所称“知识产权”是指《与贸易有关的知识产权协定》第一条第二款规定的知识产权，以及[《中华人民共和国民法典》第一百二十三条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-123)第二款第七项、香港《植物品种保护条例》规定的权利人就植物新品种享有的知识产权。

**Article 5** The term "intellectual property" as used in this Arrangement refers to the intellectual property rights provided for in Article 1, paragraph 2 of the Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights, and the intellectual property rights enjoyed by right holders in respect of new plant varieties under Article 123, paragraph 2, item (7) of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and the Plant Variety Protection Ordinance of Hong Kong.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　本安排所称“住所地”，当事人为自然人的，是指户籍所在地或者永久性居民身份所在地、经常居住地；当事人为法人或者其他组织的，是指注册地或者登记地、主要办事机构所在地、主要营业地、主要管理地。

**Article 6** The term "domicile" as used in this Arrangement means, where the party is a natural person, the place of household registration or the place of permanent residence status, or habitual residence; where the party is a legal person or any other organization, the place of registration or incorporation, the place of its principal office, principal place of business, or principal place of management.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请认可和执行本安排规定的判决：  
（一）在内地，向申请人住所地或者被申请人住所地、财产所在地的中级人民法院提出；  
（二）在香港特别行政区，向高等法院提出。  
申请人应当向符合前款第一项规定的其中一个人民法院提出申请。向两个以上有管辖权的人民法院提出申请的，由最先立案的人民法院管辖。

**Article 7** An application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment under this Arrangement shall be made:  
(1) In the Mainland, to the Intermediate People's Court at the domicile of the applicant or the domicile or place of property of the respondent;  
(2) In the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, to the High Court.  
The applicant shall submit the application to one of the People's Courts meeting the requirements in subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph. Where an application is made to two or more People's Courts with jurisdiction, the People's Court that first dockets the case shall have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请认可和执行本安排规定的判决，应当提交下列材料：  
（一）申请书；  
（二）经作出生效判决的法院盖章的判决副本；  
（三）作出生效判决的法院出具的证明书，证明该判决属于生效判决，判决有执行内容的，还应当证明在原审法院地可以执行；  
（四）判决为缺席判决的，应当提交已经合法传唤当事人的证明文件，但判决已经对此予以明确说明或者缺席方提出认可和执行申请的除外；  
（五）身份证明材料：  
1.申请人为自然人的，应当提交身份证件复印件；  
2.申请人为法人或者其他组织的，应当提交注册登记证书的复印件以及法定代表人或者主要负责人的身份证件复印件。  
上述身份证明材料，在被请求方境外形成的，应当依据被请求方法律规定办理证明手续。  
向内地人民法院提交的文件没有中文文本的，应当提交准确的中文译本。

**Article 8** An application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment under this Arrangement shall be accompanied by the following documents:  
(1) A written application;  
(2) A copy of the judgment sealed by the court that rendered the effective judgment;  
(3) A certificate issued by the court that rendered the effective judgment, certifying that the judgment is an effective judgment, and if the judgment contains enforceable content, also certifying that it is enforceable in the place of the original court;  
(4) Where the judgment was rendered by default, supporting documents proving that the parties were duly summoned, unless the judgment has expressly stated this fact or the defaulting party applies for recognition and enforcement;  
(5) Identity documents:  
1. Where the applicant is a natural person, a copy of the identity document shall be submitted;  
2. Where the applicant is a legal person or any other organization, a copy of the registration certificate and a copy of the identity document of the legal representative or principal responsible person shall be submitted.  
Where the above identity documents are made outside the requested party, the certification formalities shall be completed in accordance with the laws of the requested party.  
If the documents submitted to the Mainland People's Court are not in the Chinese language, an accurate Chinese translation shall be provided.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请书应当载明下列事项：  
（一）当事人的基本情况：当事人为自然人的，包括姓名、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；当事人为法人或者其他组织的，包括名称、住所及其法定代表人或者主要负责人的姓名、职务、住所、身份证件信息、通讯方式等；  
（二）请求事项和理由；申请执行的，还需提供被申请人的财产状况和财产所在地；  
（三）判决是否已在其他法院申请执行以及执行情况。

**Article 9** The written application shall state the following:  
(1) Basic information of the parties: where the party is a natural person, including the name, domicile, identity document information, means of communication, etc.; where the party is a legal person or any other organization, including the name, domicile, and the name, position, domicile, identity document information, means of communication of its legal representative or principal responsible person, etc.;  
(2) The request and the reasons therefor; if enforcement is sought, the property status and location of property of the respondent shall also be provided;  
(3) Whether the judgment has been applied for enforcement in other courts and the status of such enforcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请认可和执行判决的期间、程序和方式，应当依据被请求方法律的规定。

**Article 10** The time limit, procedure, and manner for applying for recognition and enforcement of a judgment shall be governed by the laws of the requested party.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　符合下列情形之一，且依据被请求方法律有关诉讼不属于被请求方法院专属管辖的，被请求方法院应当认定原审法院具有管辖权：  
（一）原审法院受理案件时，被告住所地在该方境内；  
（二）原审法院受理案件时，被告在该方境内设有代表机构、分支机构、办事处、营业所等不属于独立法人的机构，且诉讼请求是基于该机构的活动；  
（三）因合同纠纷提起的诉讼，合同履行地在该方境内；  
（四）因侵权行为提起的诉讼，侵权行为实施地在该方境内；  
（五）合同纠纷或者其他财产权益纠纷的当事人以书面形式约定由原审法院地管辖，但各方当事人住所地均在被请求方境内的，原审法院地应系合同履行地、合同签订地、标的物所在地等与争议有实际联系地；  
（六）当事人未对原审法院提出管辖权异议并应诉答辩，但各方当事人住所地均在被请求方境内的，原审法院地应系合同履行地、合同签订地、标的物所在地等与争议有实际联系地。  
前款所称“书面形式”是指合同书、信件和数据电文（包括电报、电传、传真、电子数据交换和电子邮件）等可以有形地表现所载内容的形式。  
知识产权侵权纠纷案件以及内地人民法院审理的[《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的不正当竞争纠纷民事案件、香港特别行政区法院审理的假冒纠纷案件，侵权、不正当竞争、假冒行为实施地在原审法院地境内，且涉案知识产权权利、权益在该方境内依法应予保护的，才应当认定原审法院具有管辖权。  
除第一款、第三款规定外，被请求方法院认为原审法院对于有关诉讼的管辖符合被请求方法律规定的，可以认定原审法院具有管辖权。

**Article 11** Where any of the following circumstances is met and the relevant action is not subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the requested party under the laws of the requested party, the court of the requested party shall deem the original court to have jurisdiction:  
(1) At the time the original court accepted the case, the domicile of the defendant was within the territory of that party;  
(2) At the time the original court accepted the case, the defendant had an office, branch, representative office, place of business, or other institution that is not an independent legal person within the territory of that party, and the claim was based on the activities of such institution;  
(3) In an action arising from a contract dispute, the place of performance of the contract was within the territory of that party;  
(4) In an action arising from a tort, the place where the tortious act was committed was within the territory of that party;  
(5) The parties to a contract dispute or other dispute over property rights and interests have agreed in writing to be subject to the jurisdiction of the place of the original court; provided that where the domiciles of all parties are within the territory of the requested party, the place of the original court shall be the place of performance of the contract, the place where the contract was signed, the place where the subject matter is located, or another place having a substantive connection with the dispute;  
(6) The parties have not raised any objection to the jurisdiction of the original court and have appeared and defended; provided that where the domiciles of all parties are within the territory of the requested party, the place of the original court shall be the place of performance of the contract, the place where the contract was signed, the place where the subject matter is located, or another place having a substantive connection with the dispute.  
The term "written form" in the preceding paragraph means a contract, letter, or data message (including telegram, telex, facsimile, electronic data interchange, and email) that can tangibly express the content contained therein.  
In cases of intellectual property infringement disputes, civil cases of unfair competition as specified in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China tried by the Mainland People's Courts, and cases of passing off tried by the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the original court shall be deemed to have jurisdiction only if the place where the infringement, unfair competition, or passing off act was committed is within the territory of the original court, and the intellectual property rights or interests involved in the case are entitled to protection under the laws of that party.  
Apart from the provisions in the first and third paragraphs, where the court of the requested party is of the opinion that the jurisdiction of the original court over the relevant action conforms to the laws of the requested party, it may deem the original court to have jurisdiction.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　申请认可和执行的判决，被申请人提供证据证明有下列情形之一的，被请求方法院审查核实后，应当不予认可和执行：  
（一）原审法院对有关诉讼的管辖不符合本安排第十一条规定的；  
（二）依据原审法院地法律，被申请人未经合法传唤，或者虽经合法传唤但未获得合理的陈述、辩论机会的；  
（三）判决是以欺诈方法取得的；  
（四）被请求方法院受理相关诉讼后，原审法院又受理就同一争议提起的诉讼并作出判决的；  
（五）被请求方法院已经就同一争议作出判决，或者已经认可其他国家和地区就同一争议作出的判决的；  
（六）被请求方已经就同一争议作出仲裁裁决，或者已经认可其他国家和地区就同一争议作出的仲裁裁决的。  
内地人民法院认为认可和执行香港特别行政区法院判决明显违反内地法律的基本原则或者社会公共利益，香港特别行政区法院认为认可和执行内地人民法院判决明显违反香港特别行政区法律的基本原则或者公共政策的，应当不予认可和执行。

**Article 12** Where the respondent provides evidence proving any of the following circumstances, the court of the requested party shall, after examination and verification, refuse to recognize and enforce the judgment for which recognition and enforcement is sought:  
(1) The jurisdiction of the original court over the relevant action does not comply with the provisions of Article 11 of this Arrangement;  
(2) Under the law of the place of the original court, the respondent was not duly summoned, or although duly summoned, did not have a reasonable opportunity to make statements or present arguments;  
(3) The judgment was obtained by fraud;  
(4) After the court of the requested party had accepted the relevant action, the original court accepted an action on the same dispute and rendered a judgment;  
(5) The court of the requested party has already rendered a judgment on the same dispute, or has recognized a judgment on the same dispute rendered by another country or region;  
(6) The requested party has already rendered an arbitral award on the same dispute, or has recognized an arbitral award on the same dispute rendered by another country or region.  
Where the Mainland People's Court is of the opinion that recognition and enforcement of a judgment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region would manifestly violate the fundamental principles of the laws of the Mainland or the social public interest, or where the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is of the opinion that recognition and enforcement of a judgment of the Mainland People's Court would manifestly violate the fundamental principles of the laws of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or public policy, recognition and enforcement shall be refused.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　申请认可和执行的判决，被申请人提供证据证明在原审法院进行的诉讼违反了当事人就同一争议订立的有效仲裁协议或者管辖协议的，被请求方法院审查核实后，可以不予认可和执行。

**Article 13** Where the respondent provides evidence proving that the proceedings before the original court violated a valid arbitration agreement or jurisdiction agreement concluded by the parties in respect of the same dispute, the court of the requested party may, after examination and verification, refuse to recognize and enforce the judgment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被请求方法院不能仅因判决的先决问题不属于本安排适用范围，而拒绝认可和执行该判决。

**Article 14** The court of the requested party shall not refuse to recognize and enforce a judgment solely on the ground that a preliminary issue in the judgment falls outside the scope of application of this Arrangement.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对于原审法院就知识产权有效性、是否成立或者存在作出的判项，不予认可和执行，但基于该判项作出的有关责任承担的判项符合本安排规定的，应当认可和执行。

**Article 15** Adjudications of the original court on the validity, establishment, or existence of intellectual property rights shall not be recognized or enforced; however, where the adjudication on liability made on the basis of such adjudication complies with the provisions of this Arrangement, it shall be recognized and enforced.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　相互认可和执行的判决内容包括金钱判项、非金钱判项。  
判决包括惩罚性赔偿的，不予认可和执行惩罚性赔偿部分，但本安排第十七条规定的除外。

**Article 16** The content of judgments to be mutually recognized and enforced shall include monetary adjudications and non-monetary adjudications.  
Where a judgment includes punitive damages, the part of punitive damages shall not be recognized or enforced, except as provided in Article 17 of this Arrangement.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　知识产权侵权纠纷案件以及内地人民法院审理的[《中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法》第六条](../../经济、税务与金融/贸易、竞争与市场/中华人民共和国反不正当竞争法.md#art-6)规定的不正当竞争纠纷民事案件、香港特别行政区法院审理的假冒纠纷案件，内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行判决的，限于根据原审法院地发生的侵权行为所确定的金钱判项，包括惩罚性赔偿部分。  
有关商业秘密侵权纠纷案件判决的相互认可和执行，包括金钱判项（含惩罚性赔偿）、非金钱判项。

**Article 17** In cases of intellectual property infringement disputes, civil cases of unfair competition as specified in Article 6 of the Anti-Unfair Competition Law of the People's Republic of China tried by the Mainland People's Courts, and cases of passing off tried by the courts of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the mutual recognition and enforcement of judgments between the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall be limited to monetary adjudications determined on the basis of the infringing acts occurring in the place of the original court, including the part of punitive damages.  
The mutual recognition and enforcement of judgments in cases of trade secret infringement disputes shall include monetary adjudications (including punitive damages) and non-monetary adjudications.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行的财产给付范围，包括判决确定的给付财产和相应的利息、诉讼费、迟延履行金、迟延履行利息，不包括税收、罚款。  
前款所称“诉讼费”，在香港特别行政区是指讼费评定证明书核定或者命令支付的费用。

**Article 18** The scope of property payment to be mutually recognized and enforced by the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall include the property ordered to be paid in the judgment and the corresponding interest, litigation costs, additional sum for delayed performance, and interest on delayed performance, but shall not include taxes or fines.  
The term "litigation costs" in the preceding paragraph means, in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the costs as assessed in a costs certificate or ordered to be paid.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　被请求方法院不能认可和执行判决全部判项的，可以认可和执行其中的部分判项。

**Article 19** Where the court of the requested party is unable to recognize and enforce all adjudications of a judgment, it may recognize and enforce part of the adjudications.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　对于香港特别行政区法院作出的判决，一方当事人已经提出上诉，内地人民法院审查核实后，中止认可和执行程序。经上诉，维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。  
内地人民法院就已经作出的判决裁定再审的，香港特别行政区法院审查核实后，中止认可和执行程序。经再审，维持全部或者部分原判决的，恢复认可和执行程序；完全改变原判决的，终止认可和执行程序。

**Article 20** Where an appeal has been filed by a party against a judgment rendered by a court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the Mainland People's Court shall, after examination and verification, suspend the recognition and enforcement proceedings. If the original judgment is upheld in whole or in part after appeal, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be resumed; if the original judgment is completely altered, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be terminated.  
Where the Mainland People's Court orders a retrial of a judgment it has rendered, the court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall, after examination and verification, suspend the recognition and enforcement proceedings. If the original judgment is upheld in whole or in part after retrial, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be resumed; if the original judgment is completely altered, the recognition and enforcement proceedings shall be terminated.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　被申请人在内地和香港特别行政区均有可供执行财产的，申请人可以分别向两地法院申请执行。  
应对方法院要求，两地法院应当相互提供本方执行判决的情况。  
两地法院执行财产的总额不得超过判决确定的数额。

**Article 21** Where the respondent has property available for enforcement in both the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the applicant may apply to the courts of both places for enforcement.  
At the request of the court of the other party, the courts of both places shall provide each other with information on the enforcement of the judgment in their respective places.  
The total amount of property enforced by the courts of both places shall not exceed the amount determined in the judgment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在审理民商事案件期间，当事人申请认可和执行另一地法院就同一争议作出的判决的，应当受理。受理后，有关诉讼应当中止，待就认可和执行的申请作出裁定或者命令后，再视情终止或者恢复诉讼。

**Article 22** Where, during the trial of a civil or commercial case, a party applies for recognition and enforcement of a judgment rendered by a court of the other place on the same dispute, the court shall accept the application. After acceptance, the relevant action shall be suspended, and upon the rendering of a ruling or order on the application for recognition and enforcement, the action shall be terminated or resumed as the case may be.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　审查认可和执行判决申请期间，当事人就同一争议提起诉讼的，不予受理；已经受理的，驳回起诉。  
判决全部获得认可和执行后，当事人又就同一争议提起诉讼的，不予受理。  
判决未获得或者未全部获得认可和执行的，申请人不得再次申请认可和执行，但可以就同一争议向被请求方法院提起诉讼。

**Article 23** During the period of examination of an application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, where a party brings an action on the same dispute, the court shall not accept it; if it has already been accepted, the action shall be dismissed.  
After a judgment has been fully recognized and enforced, where a party brings an action again on the same dispute, the court shall not accept it.  
Where a judgment has not been recognized and enforced, or has not been fully recognized and enforced, the applicant shall not apply again for recognition and enforcement, but may bring an action on the same dispute before the court of the requested party.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　申请认可和执行判决的，被请求方法院在受理申请之前或者之后，可以依据被请求方法律规定采取保全或者强制措施。

**Article 24** In applying for recognition and enforcement of a judgment, the court of the requested party may, before or after accepting the application, take preservation or compulsory measures in accordance with the laws of the requested party.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　法院应当尽快审查认可和执行的申请，并作出裁定或者命令。

**Article 25** The court shall expeditiously examine the application for recognition and enforcement and render a ruling or an order.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　被请求方法院就认可和执行的申请作出裁定或者命令后，当事人不服的，在内地可以于裁定送达之日起十日内向上一级人民法院申请复议，在香港特别行政区可以依据其法律规定提出上诉。

**Article 26** Where a party is dissatisfied with a ruling or order made by the court of the requested party on an application for recognition and enforcement, in the Mainland, the party may apply to the next higher People's Court for reconsideration within ten days from the date of service of the ruling; in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the party may appeal in accordance with its laws.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　申请认可和执行判决的，应当依据被请求方有关诉讼收费的法律和规定交纳费用。

**Article 27** An application for recognition and enforcement of a judgment shall be subject to the payment of fees in accordance with the laws and regulations of the requested party concerning litigation costs.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本安排签署后，最高人民法院和香港特别行政区政府经协商，可以就第三条所列案件判决的认可和执行以及第四条所涉保全、临时济助的协助问题签署补充文件。  
本安排在执行过程中遇有问题或者需要修改的，由最高人民法院和香港特别行政区政府协商解决。

**Article 28** After the signing of this Arrangement, the Supreme People's Court and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region may, through consultation, sign supplementary documents on the recognition and enforcement of judgments in the cases listed in Article 3 and on the assistance in preservation and interim relief as referred to in Article 4.  
Any problems encountered in the implementation of this Arrangement or the need for amendments shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　内地与香港特别行政区法院自本安排生效之日起作出的判决，适用本安排。

**Article 29** This Arrangement shall apply to judgments rendered by the courts of the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region from the date on which this Arrangement takes effect.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　本安排生效之日，《最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行当事人协议管辖的民商事案件判决的安排》同时废止。  
本安排生效前，当事人已签署《最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行当事人协议管辖的民商事案件判决的安排》所称“书面管辖协议”的，仍适用该安排。

**Article 30** On the date on which this Arrangement takes effect, the Arrangement of the Supreme People's Court on Reciprocal Recognition and Enforcement of Judgments in Civil and Commercial Cases between the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Pursuant to Agreement on Jurisdiction by the Parties concerned shall be abolished simultaneously.  
Where, before this Arrangement takes effect, the parties have signed a "written jurisdiction agreement" as referred to in the Arrangement of the Supreme People's Court on Reciprocal Recognition and Enforcement of Judgments in Civil and Commercial Cases between the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Pursuant to Agreement on Jurisdiction by the Parties concerned, that Arrangement shall continue to apply.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本安排生效后，《最高人民法院关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互认可和执行婚姻家庭民事案件判决的安排》继续施行。

**Article 31** After this Arrangement takes effect, the Arrangement of the Supreme People's Court on Reciprocal Recognition and Enforcement of Judgments in Marriage and Family Civil Cases between the Mainland and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall continue to be implemented.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　本安排自2024年1月29日起施行。

**Article 32** This Arrangement shall come into force on January 29, 2024.
