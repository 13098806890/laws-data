# 最高人民法院关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕10号  
**公布日期**：2016-04-24  
**生效日期**：2016-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2016年2月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1677次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年5月1日起施行。 2016年4月24日 最高人民法院 关于审理消费民事公益诉讼案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2016〕10号 为正确审理消费民事公益诉讼案件，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国侵权责任法》《中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　中国消费者协会以及在省、自治区、直辖市设立的消费者协会，对经营者侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益或者具有危及消费者人身、财产安全危险等损害社会公共利益的行为提起消费民事公益诉讼的，适用本解释。 法律规定或者全国人大及其常委会授权的机关和社会组织提起的消费民事公益诉讼，适用本解释。 第二条　经营者提供的商品或者服务具有下列情形之一的，适用消费者权益保护法第四十七条规定： （一）提供的商品或者服务存在缺陷，侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益的； （二）提供的商品或者服务可能危及消费者人身、财产安全，未作出真实的说明和明确的警示，未标明正确使用商品或者接受服务的方法以及防止危害发生方法的；对提供的商品或者服务质量、性能、用途、有效期限等信息作虚假或引人误解宣传的； （三）宾馆、商场、餐馆、银行、机场、车站、港口、影剧院、景区、娱乐场所等经营场所存在危及消费者人身、财产安全危险的； （四）以格式条款、通知、声明、店堂告示等方式，作出排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理规定的； （五）其他侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益或者具有危及消费者人身、财产安全危险等损害社会公共利益的行为。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　中国消费者协会以及在省、自治区、直辖市设立的消费者协会，对经营者侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益或者具有危及消费者人身、财产安全危险等损害社会公共利益的行为提起消费民事公益诉讼的，适用本解释。  
法律规定或者全国人大及其常委会授权的机关和社会组织提起的消费民事公益诉讼，适用本解释。

**Article 1** This Interpretation shall apply to consumer civil public interest lawsuits initiated by the China Consumers' Association and the consumers' associations established in provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government against operators for acts that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous unspecified consumers, or that endanger the personal or property safety of consumers, or otherwise harm the public interest.  
This Interpretation shall apply to consumer civil public interest lawsuits initiated by organs or social organizations as provided by law or authorized by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　经营者提供的商品或者服务具有下列情形之一的，适用[消费者权益保护法第四十七条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-47)规定：  
（一）提供的商品或者服务存在缺陷，侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益的；  
（二）提供的商品或者服务可能危及消费者人身、财产安全，未作出真实的说明和明确的警示，未标明正确使用商品或者接受服务的方法以及防止危害发生方法的；对提供的商品或者服务质量、性能、用途、有效期限等信息作虚假或引人误解宣传的；  
（三）宾馆、商场、餐馆、银行、机场、车站、港口、影剧院、景区、娱乐场所等经营场所存在危及消费者人身、财产安全危险的；  
（四）以格式条款、通知、声明、店堂告示等方式，作出排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任等对消费者不公平、不合理规定的；  
（五）其他侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益或者具有危及消费者人身、财产安全危险等损害社会公共利益的行为。

**Article 2** Where an operator provides any commodity or service falling under any of the following circumstances, Article 47 of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests shall apply:  
(1) The commodity or service provided has defects, infringing upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous unspecified consumers;  
(2) The commodity or service provided may endanger the personal or property safety of consumers, without truthful explanations and clear warnings, or without indicating the proper methods of using the commodity or receiving the service and the methods of preventing harm; or false or misleading publicity is made regarding the quality, performance, use, validity period, or other information of the commodity or service provided;  
(3) The business premises such as hotels, shopping malls, restaurants, banks, airports, stations, ports, cinemas, scenic spots, entertainment venues, etc., pose a danger to the personal or property safety of consumers;  
(4) Through standard terms, notices, statements, in-store announcements, or other means, provisions are made to exclude or limit consumers' rights, reduce or exempt the operator's liabilities, or increase consumers' obligations, which are unfair or unreasonable to consumers;  
(5) Other acts that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous unspecified consumers, or that endanger the personal or property safety of consumers, or otherwise harm the public interest.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　消费民事公益诉讼案件管辖适用最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国民事诉讼法〉的解释》第二百八十五条的有关规定。  
经最高人民法院批准，高级人民法院可以根据本辖区实际情况，在辖区内确定部分中级人民法院受理第一审消费民事公益诉讼案件。

**Article 3** The jurisdiction over consumer civil public interest lawsuit cases shall be governed by the relevant provisions of Article 285 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
With the approval of the Supreme People's Court, a Higher People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances of its jurisdiction, designate certain Intermediate People's Courts within its jurisdiction to accept first-instance consumer civil public interest lawsuit cases.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　提起消费民事公益诉讼应当提交下列材料：  
（一）符合[民事诉讼法第一百二十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-121)规定的起诉状，并按照被告人数提交副本；  
（二）被告的行为侵害众多不特定消费者合法权益或者具有危及消费者人身、财产安全危险等损害社会公共利益的初步证据；  
（三）消费者组织就涉诉事项已按照[消费者权益保护法第三十七条](../../民事与商事/综合与程序批复/中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法.md#art-37)第四项或者第五项的规定履行公益性职责的证明材料。

**Article 4** The following materials shall be submitted for initiating a consumer civil public interest lawsuit:  
(1) A complaint that complies with Article 121 of the Civil Procedure Law, together with copies equal to the number of defendants;  
(2) Preliminary evidence that the defendant's acts have infringed upon the lawful rights and interests of numerous unspecified consumers, or endangered the personal or property safety of consumers, or otherwise harmed the public interest;  
(3) Evidentiary materials showing that the consumer organization has performed its public interest duties with respect to the matters involved in the lawsuit in accordance with Item (4) or (5) of Article 37 of the Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院认为原告提出的诉讼请求不足以保护社会公共利益的，可以向其释明变更或者增加停止侵害等诉讼请求。

**Article 5** Where the People's Court considers that the claims made by the plaintiff are insufficient to protect the public interest, it may explain to the plaintiff and suggest that the plaintiff modify or add claims such as cessation of infringement.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院受理消费民事公益诉讼案件后，应当公告案件受理情况，并在立案之日起十日内书面告知相关行政主管部门。

**Article 6** After accepting a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case, the People's Court shall publish a notice of the acceptance and, within ten days from the date of docketing, notify the relevant administrative department in writing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民法院受理消费民事公益诉讼案件后，依法可以提起诉讼的其他机关或者社会组织，可以在一审开庭前向人民法院申请参加诉讼。  
人民法院准许参加诉讼的，列为共同原告；逾期申请的，不予准许。

**Article 7** After accepting a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case, if other organs or social organizations that may initiate a lawsuit according to law apply to participate in the lawsuit before the first-instance trial, the People's Court may permit their participation.  
If the People's Court permits participation, they shall be listed as co-plaintiffs; if the application is made after the time limit, it shall not be permitted.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　有权提起消费民事公益诉讼的机关或者社会组织，可以依据[民事诉讼法第八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-81)规定申请保全证据。

**Article 8** An organ or social organization entitled to initiate a consumer civil public interest lawsuit may apply for preservation of evidence in accordance with Article 81 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院受理消费民事公益诉讼案件后，因同一侵权行为受到损害的消费者申请参加诉讼的，人民法院应当告知其根据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定主张权利。

**Article 9** After accepting a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case, if consumers who have suffered damage from the same tortious act apply to participate in the lawsuit, the People's Court shall inform them to assert their rights in accordance with Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　消费民事公益诉讼案件受理后，因同一侵权行为受到损害的消费者请求对其根据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定提起的诉讼予以中止，人民法院可以准许。

**Article 10** After a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case is accepted, if consumers who have suffered damage from the same tortious act request to suspend the lawsuit initiated by them under Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court may permit such suspension.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　消费民事公益诉讼案件审理过程中，被告提出反诉的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 11** During the trial of a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case, if the defendant files a counterclaim, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　原告在诉讼中承认对己方不利的事实，人民法院认为损害社会公共利益的，不予确认。

**Article 12** If the plaintiff admits facts unfavorable to itself during the proceedings, and the People's Court considers that doing so would harm the public interest, such admission shall not be confirmed.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　原告在消费民事公益诉讼案件中，请求被告承担停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险、赔礼道歉等民事责任的，人民法院可予支持。  
经营者利用格式条款或者通知、声明、店堂告示等，排除或者限制消费者权利、减轻或者免除经营者责任、加重消费者责任，原告认为对消费者不公平、不合理主张无效的，人民法院可予支持。

**Article 13** In a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case, if the plaintiff requests the defendant to bear civil liability such as cessation of infringement, removal of obstruction, elimination of danger, and making an apology, the People's Court may support such request.  
Where an operator uses standard terms, notices, statements, in-store announcements, or other means to exclude or limit consumers' rights, reduce or exempt the operator's liabilities, or increase consumers' obligations, and the plaintiff claims that such provisions are unfair or unreasonable to consumers and requests a declaration of invalidity, the People's Court may support such claim.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　消费民事公益诉讼案件裁判生效后，人民法院应当在十日内书面告知相关行政主管部门，并可发出司法建议。

**Article 14** After the judgment in a consumer civil public interest lawsuit case takes effect, the People's Court shall, within ten days, notify the relevant administrative department in writing and may issue judicial suggestions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　消费民事公益诉讼案件的裁判发生法律效力后，其他依法具有原告资格的机关或者社会组织就同一侵权行为另行提起消费民事公益诉讼的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 15** After the judgment in a consumer civil public interest lawsuit becomes legally effective, if other organs or social organizations that otherwise have standing to sue file another consumer civil public interest lawsuit with respect to the same tortious act, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　已为消费民事公益诉讼生效裁判认定的事实，因同一侵权行为受到损害的消费者根据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定提起的诉讼，原告、被告均无需举证证明，但当事人对该事实有异议并有相反证据足以推翻的除外。  
消费民事公益诉讼生效裁判认定经营者存在不法行为，因同一侵权行为受到损害的消费者根据[民事诉讼法第一百一十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-119)规定提起的诉讼，原告主张适用的，人民法院可予支持，但被告有相反证据足以推翻的除外。被告主张直接适用对其有利认定的，人民法院不予支持，被告仍应承担相应举证证明责任。

**Article 16** Where facts have been confirmed by an effective judgment in a consumer civil public interest lawsuit, in a lawsuit initiated by a consumer who has suffered damage from the same tortious act under Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law, neither the plaintiff nor the defendant needs to provide evidence to prove such facts, unless the party disputes such facts and has sufficient contrary evidence to overturn them.  
Where an effective judgment in a consumer civil public interest lawsuit finds that the operator has engaged in illegal conduct, in a lawsuit initiated by a consumer who has suffered damage from the same tortious act under Article 119 of the Civil Procedure Law, if the plaintiff claims to apply such finding, the People's Court may support it, unless the defendant has sufficient contrary evidence to overturn it. If the defendant claims to directly apply a finding favorable to it, the People's Court shall not support it, and the defendant shall still bear the corresponding burden of proof.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　原告为停止侵害、排除妨碍、消除危险采取合理预防、处置措施而发生的费用，请求被告承担的，人民法院可予支持。

**Article 17** If the plaintiff incurs reasonable expenses for taking preventive and remedial measures to cease infringement, remove obstruction, or eliminate danger, and requests the defendant to bear such expenses, the People's Court may support such request.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　原告及其诉讼代理人对侵权行为进行调查、取证的合理费用、鉴定费用、合理的律师代理费用，人民法院可根据实际情况予以相应支持。

**Article 18** The People's Court may, based on the actual circumstances, support the plaintiff's claim for the reasonable expenses incurred by the plaintiff and its litigation agent for investigation and evidence collection of the tortious act, the costs of expert evaluation, and reasonable attorney's fees to an appropriate extent.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本解释自2016年5月1日起施行。  
本解释施行后人民法院新受理的一审案件，适用本解释。  
本解释施行前人民法院已经受理、施行后尚未审结的一审、二审案件，以及本解释施行前已经终审、施行后当事人申请再审或者按照审判监督程序决定再审的案件，不适用本解释。

**Article 19** This Interpretation shall come into force on May 1, 2016.  
For first-instance cases newly accepted by the People's Court after the effective date of this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall apply.  
For first-instance or second-instance cases that have been accepted before the effective date of this Interpretation but have not yet been concluded after its effective date, as well as cases that have been finally adjudicated before the effective date of this Interpretation but for which the parties apply for a retrial or which are decided to be retried under the trial supervision procedure after the effective date, this Interpretation shall not apply.
