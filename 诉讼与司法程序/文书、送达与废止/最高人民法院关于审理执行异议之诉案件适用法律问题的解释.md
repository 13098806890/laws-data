# 最高人民法院关于审理执行异议之诉案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕10号  
**公布日期**：2025-07-23  
**生效日期**：2025-07-24

> （2024年12月14日最高人民法院审判委员会第1938次会议通过，自2025年7月24日起施行） 为正确审理执行异议之诉案件，依法保护当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国企业破产法》等法律规定，结合审判实践，制定本解释。 第一条　案外人依照民事诉讼法第二百三十八条规定，在执行过程中就执行标的提出执行异议，由提出异议时负责执行该执行标的人民法院审查处理；案外人、当事人对执行异议裁定不服，自裁定送达之日起十五日内向作出执行异议裁定的执行法院提起执行异议之诉的，人民法院应予受理。 案外人未在《最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》第六条规定的期限内提出异议，人民法院裁定不予受理的，可以依法另行向申请执行人、被执行人等主张权利。 第二条　金钱债权纠纷的财产保全、执行中，执行标的存在轮候查封、扣押、冻结的，案外人提起执行异议之诉，以首先查封、享有担保物权等优先受偿权的申请保全人、申请执行人为被告，以其他已知的轮候查封的申请保全人、申请执行人为第三人。 第三条　案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，人民法院判决不得执行该执行标的的，应同时判决解除执行措施并写明相关查封、扣押、冻结裁定书案号。案外人可以持生效判决请求相关执行法院解除执行措施。 第四条　案外人提起执行异议之诉并依照民法典第二百三十四条等规定就执行标的的归属提出确权请求的，以被执行人为被告。 第五条　案外人提起执行异议之诉并以被执行人等为被告提出返还原物、返还价款或者交付标的物、办理转移登记手续等给付请求的，人民法院可以合并审理，法律另有规定不宜合并审理的，应当分别立案。 第六条　案外人执行异议之诉案件审理期间，申请执行人请求人民法院继续执行并提供相应担保的，由负责审理执行异议之诉的人民法院裁定是否准许。执行法院依法继续执行的，案外人执行异议之诉案件按照下列情形分别处理： （一）案外人就执行标的不享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益的，判决驳回诉讼请求； （二）案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，执行标的由申请执行人通过拍卖、抵债等执行程序受让的，判决不得执行该执行标的，并撤销相关拍卖或者抵债裁定；已向申请执行人交付的，同时判决申请执行人返还，拒绝返还的，强制执行； （三）案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，执行标的已由他人通过拍卖、变卖等执行程序合法取得的，判决不得执行该执行标的变价款，执行法院向案外人发放变价款；已向申请执行人发放变价款或者已向被执行人退还剩余变价款的，同时判决申请执行人、被执行人返还，拒绝返还的，强制执行；执行法院向案外人释明执行标的已由他人合法取得而案外人拒绝受领变价款的，应当将变价款予以提存，并告知案外人自提存之日起五年内可以随时领取。 在前款第二项、第三项规定情形下，案外人认为申请执行人请求继续执行错误，给其造成损失的，可以依法另行向申请执行人、执行担保人等主张权利。 第七条　案外人执行异议之诉案件审理或者再审申请审查期间，执行案件已经结案，执行法院未对执行标的进行处分且执行措施已经解除的，人民法院应当裁定终结诉讼或者终结审查。原由执行法院作出的执行异议裁定失效。案外人根据本解释第四条、第五条提出的确权、给付请求，人民法院可以继续审理或者审查。 第八条　执行异议之诉案件审理或者再审申请审查期间，人民法院对作为执行依据的原判决、裁定等依法决定再审，执行标的系原判决、裁定等所涉争议标的以外的财产，或者案外人可能享有足以排除担保物权等优先受偿权的强制执行的民事权益的，执行异议之诉案件可以继续审理或者审查，不能认定案外人享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益的，执行异议之诉案件应当中止审理或者审查。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　案外人依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-238)规定，在执行过程中就执行标的提出执行异议，由提出异议时负责执行该执行标的人民法院审查处理；案外人、当事人对执行异议裁定不服，自裁定送达之日起十五日内向作出执行异议裁定的执行法院提起执行异议之诉的，人民法院应予受理。  
案外人未在[《最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定》第六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于人民法院办理执行异议和复议案件若干问题的规定.md#art-6)规定的期限内提出异议，人民法院裁定不予受理的，可以依法另行向申请执行人、被执行人等主张权利。

**Article 1** Where a person not involved in the case raises an objection to the enforcement of the subject matter of enforcement during the enforcement process in accordance with Article 238 of the Civil Procedure Law, the People's Court responsible for enforcing the subject matter of enforcement at the time of raising the objection shall review and handle the case; if the person not involved in the case or any party is dissatisfied with the ruling on the enforcement objection and, within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling, files an action objecting to the enforcement with the enforcement court that made the ruling on the enforcement objection, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
Where a person not involved in the case fails to raise an objection within the time limit prescribed in Article 6 of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Handling of Cases of Objection and Reconsideration in Enforcement by People's Courts, and the People's Court rules that the case shall not be accepted, such person may separately claim rights against the applicant for enforcement, the person subject to enforcement, or others according to law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　金钱债权纠纷的财产保全、执行中，执行标的存在轮候查封、扣押、冻结的，案外人提起执行异议之诉，以首先查封、享有担保物权等优先受偿权的申请保全人、申请执行人为被告，以其他已知的轮候查封的申请保全人、申请执行人为第三人。

**Article 2** In the property preservation or enforcement of a dispute over a monetary claim, where the subject matter of enforcement is subject to successive seizures, impoundments, or freezes and a person not involved in the case files an action objecting to the enforcement, the applicant for preservation or the applicant for enforcement that first made the seizure or enjoys a right to priority of payment such as a security interest shall be the defendant, and other known applicants for preservation or applicants for enforcement with successive seizures shall be the third parties.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，人民法院判决不得执行该执行标的的，应同时判决解除执行措施并写明相关查封、扣押、冻结裁定书案号。案外人可以持生效判决请求相关执行法院解除执行措施。

**Article 3** If a person not involved in the case enjoys a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement over the subject matter of enforcement and the People's Court decides that the subject matter of enforcement shall not be enforced, the court shall at the same time decide to release the enforcement measures and specify the case numbers of the relevant rulings on seizure, impoundment, or freeze. The person not involved in the case may, by holding the effective judgment, request the relevant enforcement court to release the enforcement measures.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　案外人提起执行异议之诉并依照[民法典第二百三十四条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-234)等规定就执行标的的归属提出确权请求的，以被执行人为被告。

**Article 4** Where a person not involved in the case files an action objecting to the enforcement and, in accordance with Article 234 of the Civil Code and other provisions, makes a request for confirmation of the ownership of the subject matter of enforcement, the person subject to enforcement shall be the defendant.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　案外人提起执行异议之诉并以被执行人等为被告提出返还原物、返还价款或者交付标的物、办理转移登记手续等给付请求的，人民法院可以合并审理，法律另有规定不宜合并审理的，应当分别立案。

**Article 5** Where a person not involved in the case files an action objecting to the enforcement and, with the person subject to enforcement or others as the defendant, makes a claim for performance such as the return of the original property, return of the price, or delivery of the subject matter, or handling of the registration of transfer, the People's Court may try the cases together; if otherwise provided by law and it is inappropriate to try them together, the court shall file separate cases.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　案外人执行异议之诉案件审理期间，申请执行人请求人民法院继续执行并提供相应担保的，由负责审理执行异议之诉的人民法院裁定是否准许。执行法院依法继续执行的，案外人执行异议之诉案件按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）案外人就执行标的不享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益的，判决驳回诉讼请求；  
（二）案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，执行标的由申请执行人通过拍卖、抵债等执行程序受让的，判决不得执行该执行标的，并撤销相关拍卖或者抵债裁定；已向申请执行人交付的，同时判决申请执行人返还，拒绝返还的，强制执行；  
（三）案外人就执行标的享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，执行标的已由他人通过拍卖、变卖等执行程序合法取得的，判决不得执行该执行标的变价款，执行法院向案外人发放变价款；已向申请执行人发放变价款或者已向被执行人退还剩余变价款的，同时判决申请执行人、被执行人返还，拒绝返还的，强制执行；执行法院向案外人释明执行标的已由他人合法取得而案外人拒绝受领变价款的，应当将变价款予以提存，并告知案外人自提存之日起五年内可以随时领取。  
在前款第二项、第三项规定情形下，案外人认为申请执行人请求继续执行错误，给其造成损失的，可以依法另行向申请执行人、执行担保人等主张权利。

**Article 6** During the trial of a case involving an action objecting to enforcement filed by a person not involved in the case, if the applicant for enforcement requests the People's Court to continue enforcement and provides corresponding security, the People's Court responsible for trying the action objecting to enforcement shall rule on whether to permit the request. If the enforcement court continues enforcement according to law, the case involving an action objecting to enforcement filed by the person not involved in the case shall be handled separately in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) If the person not involved in the case does not enjoy a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement over the subject matter of enforcement, the court shall decide to dismiss the claim;  
(2) If the person not involved in the case enjoys a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement over the subject matter of enforcement, and the subject matter of enforcement has been transferred to the applicant for enforcement through enforcement procedures such as auction or debt conversion, the court shall decide that the subject matter of enforcement shall not be enforced and shall revoke the relevant auction or debt conversion ruling; if the subject matter has already been delivered to the applicant for enforcement, the court shall at the same time decide that the applicant for enforcement shall return it, and if the applicant refuses to return it, compulsory enforcement shall be carried out;  
(3) If the person not involved in the case enjoys a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement over the subject matter of enforcement, and the subject matter of enforcement has been lawfully acquired by another person through enforcement procedures such as auction or sale, the court shall decide that the proceeds from the sale of the subject matter of enforcement shall not be enforced, and the enforcement court shall distribute the proceeds to the person not involved in the case; if the proceeds have already been distributed to the applicant for enforcement or the remaining proceeds have been returned to the person subject to enforcement, the court shall at the same time decide that the applicant for enforcement or the person subject to enforcement shall return them, and if they refuse to return them, compulsory enforcement shall be carried out; if the enforcement court explains to the person not involved in the case that the subject matter of enforcement has been lawfully acquired by another person and the person not involved in the case refuses to accept the proceeds, the court shall deposit the proceeds, and shall inform the person not involved in the case that such person may claim them at any time within five years from the date of deposit.  
Under the circumstances specified in items (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph, if the person not involved in the case considers that the applicant for enforcement's request to continue enforcement is erroneous and causes losses to such person, such person may separately claim rights against the applicant for enforcement, the enforcement guarantor, or others according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　案外人执行异议之诉案件审理或者再审申请审查期间，执行案件已经结案，执行法院未对执行标的进行处分且执行措施已经解除的，人民法院应当裁定终结诉讼或者终结审查。原由执行法院作出的执行异议裁定失效。案外人根据[本解释第四条](#art-4)、第五条提出的确权、给付请求，人民法院可以继续审理或者审查。

**Article 7** During the trial of a case involving an action objecting to enforcement filed by a person not involved in the case or the review of an application for retrial, if the enforcement case has been concluded, the enforcement court has not disposed of the subject matter of enforcement, and the enforcement measures have been released, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the action or terminate the review. The ruling on the enforcement objection previously made by the enforcement court shall cease to have effect. If the person not involved in the case makes a request for confirmation of rights or a claim for performance in accordance with Articles 4 and 5 of this Interpretation, the People's Court may continue the trial or review.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　执行异议之诉案件审理或者再审申请审查期间，人民法院对作为执行依据的原判决、裁定等依法决定再审，执行标的系原判决、裁定等所涉争议标的以外的财产，或者案外人可能享有足以排除担保物权等优先受偿权的强制执行的民事权益的，执行异议之诉案件可以继续审理或者审查，不能认定案外人享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益的，执行异议之诉案件应当中止审理或者审查。

**Article 8** During the trial of a case involving an action objecting to enforcement or the review of an application for retrial, if the People's Court decides according to law to retry the original judgment, ruling, or other document that serves as the basis for enforcement, and if the subject matter of enforcement is property other than the disputed subject matter involved in the original judgment, ruling, or other document, or if the person not involved in the case may enjoy a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement on a priority right of payment such as a security interest, the case involving an action objecting to enforcement may continue to be tried or reviewed; if it cannot be determined that the person not involved in the case enjoys a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement, the case involving an action objecting to enforcement shall be suspended in trial or review.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　执行异议之诉案件审理或者再审申请审查期间，人民法院裁定受理被执行人破产案件的，执行异议之诉案件应当中止审理或者审查，管理人接管债务人财产后，执行异议之诉案件可以继续审理或者审查。

**Article 9** During the trial of a case involving an action objecting to enforcement or the review of an application for retrial, if the People's Court rules to accept the bankruptcy case of the person subject to enforcement, the case involving an action objecting to enforcement shall be suspended in trial or review. After the administrator takes over the debtor's property, the case involving an action objecting to enforcement may continue to be tried or reviewed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　案外人执行异议之诉案件经审判监督程序发现支持案外人排除强制执行确有错误，人民法院认定案外人不享有足以排除强制执行的民事权益，判决驳回案外人诉讼请求的，原相关执行法院按照原顺位恢复执行；执行标的已合法转让给他人，人民法院裁定撤销原判决、终结诉讼的，申请执行人可以依法另行向被执行人、案外人等主张权利。

**Article 10** If, through the adjudication supervision procedure, it is discovered that the support for the person not involved in the case to exclude compulsory enforcement is indeed erroneous in a case involving an action objecting to enforcement, and the People's Court determines that the person not involved in the case does not enjoy a civil right or interest sufficient to exclude compulsory enforcement and decides to dismiss the claim of the person not involved in the case, the original relevant enforcement court shall resume enforcement in the original order; if the subject matter of enforcement has been lawfully transferred to another person and the People's Court rules to revoke the original judgment and terminate the action, the applicant for enforcement may separately claim rights against the person subject to enforcement, the person not involved in the case, or others according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院对登记在被执行的房地产开发企业名下的新建商品房实施强制执行，案外人以其系商品房消费者为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及一般金钱债权的强制执行，并能够证明其主张同时符合下列条件的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）查封前，案外人已与房地产开发企业等签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；  
（二）查封前，案外人已支付全部价款，或者已按照合同约定支付部分价款且查封后至一审法庭辩论终结前已将剩余价款交付人民法院执行；  
（三）所购商品房系用于满足家庭居住生活需要。  
案外人起诉请求被执行人办理商品房所有权转移登记手续，符合前款规定的，人民法院依法予以支持。  
人民法院判决驳回案外人诉讼请求的，案外人交付执行的剩余价款应予及时退还。

**Article 11** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on a newly built commercial housing registered under the name of a real estate development enterprise that is subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that such person is a consumer of the commercial housing, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of the right to priority of payment for construction project costs, the mortgage right, and ordinary monetary claims, and is able to prove that its claims simultaneously meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had already signed a lawful and valid written sales contract with the real estate development enterprise or others;  
(2) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had already paid the full price, or had paid part of the price in accordance with the contract and, after the seizure but before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, had delivered the remaining price to the People's Court for enforcement;  
(3) The purchased commercial housing is used to satisfy the needs of family residential life.  
If the person not involved in the case files a lawsuit requesting the person subject to enforcement to handle the procedures for the registration of transfer of ownership of the commercial housing, and the conditions in the preceding paragraph are met, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.  
If the People's Court decides to dismiss the claim of the person not involved in the case, the remaining price delivered by the person not involved in the case for enforcement shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　执行法院冻结被执行的房地产开发企业的预售资金监管账户，案外人以其已向该账户交付购房款，且房屋买卖合同已经解除为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除相应购房款的强制执行并申请向其发放，事由成立的，人民法院应予支持。  
执行法院对被执行的房地产开发企业的建筑物及其占用范围内建设用地使用权实施强制执行，符合前条第一款第一项、第三项规定的商品房消费者因房屋不能交付且无实际交付可能导致房屋买卖合同已经解除，提起执行异议之诉，请求在建筑物及其建设用地使用权的变价款中排除相对应的强制执行并申请向其发放的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 12** Where the enforcement court freezes the pre-sale fund supervision account of a real estate development enterprise that is subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that such person has paid the purchase price into that account and the housing sales contract has been rescinded, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement on the corresponding purchase price and applies for distribution to such person, if the reason is established, the People's Court shall support the request.  
Where the enforcement court enforces compulsory enforcement on the buildings of a real estate development enterprise that is subject to enforcement and the right to use construction land within the occupied area, and a commercial housing consumer who meets the conditions specified in items (1) and (3) of the first paragraph of the preceding article, because the housing cannot be delivered and the lack of actual delivery may result in the rescission of the housing sales contract, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude the corresponding compulsory enforcement from the proceeds from the sale of the buildings and the right to use construction land and applies for distribution to such person, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院对登记在被执行人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以其系不动产的买受人为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及一般金钱债权的强制执行，案外人与被执行人签订合法有效的书面买卖合同且在一审法庭辩论终结前交付执行法院的价款足以代为清偿相应主债权的，人民法院应予支持。  
符合前款规定的案外人起诉请求抵押权人按套办理抵押权注销登记手续的，人民法院应予支持。  
人民法院按照本条第一款规定判决不得执行，申请执行人可以申请将案外人依本条第一款交付的价款替代被执行人清偿相应债务。  
人民法院判决驳回案外人诉讼请求的，案外人交付执行的价款应予及时退还。

**Article 13** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that such person is the buyer of the immovable property, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of the right to priority of payment for construction project costs, the mortgage right, and ordinary monetary claims, if the person not involved in the case has signed a lawful and valid written sales contract with the person subject to enforcement and, before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, has delivered to the enforcement court a sum of money sufficient to repay the corresponding principal claim on behalf of the other party, the People's Court shall support the request.  
If a person not involved in the case who meets the conditions in the preceding paragraph files a lawsuit requesting the mortgagee to handle the procedures for the deregistration of the mortgage on a per-unit basis, the People's Court shall support the request.  
If the People's Court decides that enforcement shall not be carried out in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article, the applicant for enforcement may apply to use the price delivered by the person not involved in the case under paragraph 1 of this Article to repay the corresponding debts on behalf of the person subject to enforcement.  
If the People's Court decides to dismiss the claim of the person not involved in the case, the price delivered by the person not involved in the case for enforcement shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院对登记在被执行人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以其系该不动产买受人为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除一般金钱债权的强制执行，并能够证明其主张同时符合下列条件的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）查封前，案外人已与被执行人签订合法有效的书面买卖合同；  
（二）查封前，案外人已支付全部价款，或者已按照合同约定支付部分价款且查封后至一审法庭辩论终结前已将剩余价款交付人民法院执行；  
（三）查封前，案外人已合法占有该不动产；  
（四）非因案外人自身原因未办理不动产所有权转移登记。  
人民法院判决驳回案外人诉讼请求的，案外人交付执行的剩余价款应予及时退还。

**Article 14** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that such person is the buyer of that immovable property, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of ordinary monetary claims, and is able to prove that its claims simultaneously meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had already signed a lawful and valid written sales contract with the person subject to enforcement;  
(2) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had already paid the full price, or had paid part of the price in accordance with the contract and, after the seizure but before the conclusion of the debate at the first instance trial, had delivered the remaining price to the People's Court for enforcement;  
(3) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had lawfully taken possession of that immovable property;  
(4) The failure to register the transfer of ownership of the immovable property is not due to reasons attributable to the person not involved in the case.  
If the People's Court decides to dismiss the claim of the person not involved in the case, the remaining price delivered by the person not involved in the case for enforcement shall be promptly returned.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民法院对登记在被执行人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以被执行人已将该不动产向其抵偿债务为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除一般金钱债权的强制执行，并能够证明其主张同时符合下列条件的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）案外人与被执行人存在真实的债权债务关系且债务履行期限已届满，案外人与被执行人在查封前已签订合法有效的以不动产抵债协议；  
（二）有证据证明抵债金额与抵债时执行标的的实际价值基本相当；  
（三）案外人在查封前已合法占有该不动产；  
（四）非因案外人自身原因未办理不动产所有权转移登记。

**Article 15** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that the person subject to enforcement has transferred that immovable property to such person as satisfaction of a debt, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of ordinary monetary claims, and is able to prove that its claims simultaneously meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) There exists a genuine creditor-debtor relationship between the person not involved in the case and the person subject to enforcement, and the debt performance period has expired, and the person not involved in the case and the person subject to enforcement had signed a lawful and valid agreement to transfer the immovable property as satisfaction of the debt before the seizure;  
(2) There is evidence to prove that the amount of the debt satisfaction is substantially equivalent to the actual value of the subject matter of enforcement at the time of the debt satisfaction;  
(3) The person not involved in the case had lawfully taken possession of that immovable property before the seizure;  
(4) The failure to register the transfer of ownership of the immovable property is not due to reasons attributable to the person not involved in the case.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院查封前，符合下列情形之一，可以认定为[本解释第十四条](#art-14)、第十五条中的“非因案外人自身原因”：  
（一）案外人与被执行人已共同向不动产登记机构提交办理所有权转移登记申请；  
（二）案外人已请求被执行人履行办理所有权转移登记手续等合同义务，或者因办理所有权转移登记与被执行人发生纠纷并已起诉或者申请仲裁等；  
（三）新建商品房尚不符合首次登记条件；  
（四）已办理买卖合同网签备案；  
（五）被执行人等通知案外人办理不动产所有权转移登记而其未怠于办理；  
（六）其他非因案外人自身原因的情形。

**Article 16** Before the seizure by the People's Court, any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as 'not due to reasons attributable to the person not involved in the case' as referred to in Articles 14 and 15 of this Interpretation:  
(1) The person not involved in the case and the person subject to enforcement have jointly submitted an application to the immovable property registration authority for the registration of transfer of ownership;  
(2) The person not involved in the case has requested the person subject to enforcement to perform contractual obligations such as handling the procedures for the registration of transfer of ownership, or has filed a lawsuit or applied for arbitration, etc., due to a dispute with the person subject to enforcement over the registration of transfer of ownership;  
(3) The newly built commercial housing does not yet meet the conditions for first registration;  
(4) The online filing and recording of the sales contract has been completed;  
(5) The person subject to enforcement or others have notified the person not involved in the case to handle the registration of transfer of ownership of the immovable property, and the person not involved in the case has not been negligent in handling it;  
(6) Other circumstances not due to reasons attributable to the person not involved in the case.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民法院对登记在被执行的发包人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以其与被执行人约定以不动产折抵工程债务为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除抵押权和一般金钱债权的强制执行，并能够证明其主张同时符合下列条件的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）案外人依据[民法典第八百零七条](../../民事与商事/民法典/中华人民共和国民法典.md#art-807)规定，在查封前行使建设工程价款优先受偿权，与被执行的发包人签订合法有效的以不动产折价协议；  
（二）有证据证明抵债金额与抵债时执行标的的实际价值基本相当。  
案外人起诉请求被执行人办理不动产所有权转移登记手续，符合前款规定的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 17** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the developer that is subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that the person not involved in the case has agreed with the person subject to enforcement to convert the immovable property into satisfaction of construction debts, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of the mortgage right and ordinary monetary claims, and is able to prove that its claims simultaneously meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) The person not involved in the case, in accordance with Article 807 of the Civil Code, exercised the right to priority of payment for construction project costs before the seizure and signed a lawful and valid agreement with the developer that is subject to enforcement to convert the immovable property into payment at a discount;  
(2) There is evidence to prove that the amount of the debt satisfaction is substantially equivalent to the actual value of the subject matter of enforcement at the time of the debt satisfaction.  
If the person not involved in the case files a lawsuit requesting the person subject to enforcement to handle the procedures for the registration of transfer of ownership of the immovable property, and the conditions in the preceding paragraph are met, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院对登记在被执行人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以该不动产系用于产权调换的征收补偿为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求排除建设工程价款优先受偿权、抵押权以及其他债权的强制执行，并能够证明其主张同时符合下列条件的，人民法院应予支持：  
（一）查封前，案外人已与房屋征收部门、房屋征收实施单位等依法签订征收补偿性质的协议；  
（二）用于征收补偿的不动产的位置明确特定。  
案外人起诉请求被执行人办理不动产所有权转移登记手续，符合前款规定的，人民法院依法予以支持。

**Article 18** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that the immovable property is used as expropriation compensation in the form of property rights exchange, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement of the right to priority of payment for construction project costs, the mortgage right, and other claims, and is able to prove that its claims simultaneously meet the following conditions, the People's Court shall support the request:  
(1) Before the seizure, the person not involved in the case had already signed an agreement of an expropriation compensation nature with the housing expropriation department, the housing expropriation implementation unit, or others according to law;  
(2) The location of the immovable property used for expropriation compensation is clearly specified.  
If the person not involved in the case files a lawsuit requesting the person subject to enforcement to handle the procedures for the registration of transfer of ownership of the immovable property, and the conditions in the preceding paragraph are met, the People's Court shall support the request according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院对登记在被执行人名下的不动产实施强制执行，案外人以在查封前已与被执行人签订合法有效的书面买卖合同且已按照合同约定支付价款，并已办理了合法有效的不动产预告登记为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求停止处分，事由成立的，人民法院应予支持；符合物权登记条件，案外人请求排除强制执行的，人民法院应予支持。

**Article 19** Where a People's Court enforces compulsory enforcement on immovable property registered under the name of the person subject to enforcement, and a person not involved in the case, on the ground that before the seizure, it had signed a lawful and valid written sales contract with the person subject to enforcement, paid the price in accordance with the contract, and completed a lawful and valid advance notice registration of the immovable property, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to cease disposition, if the reason is established, the People's Court shall support the request; if the conditions for registration of real rights are satisfied, and the person not involved in the case requests to exclude compulsory enforcement, the People's Court shall support the request.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　不带租拍卖、变卖等情况下的强制执行中，案外人以在查封前已与被执行人签订合法有效的书面租赁合同并合法占有使用执行标的，且已按照合同约定支付租金为由，提起执行异议之诉，请求在租赁期内排除一般债权的不带租强制执行，事由成立的，人民法院应予支持；符合上述规定条件的案外人签订租赁合同及合法占有使用执行标的均在抵押权设立之前，请求在租赁期内排除抵押权的不带租强制执行的，人民法院应予支持。  
申请执行人可以对带租拍卖、变卖等情况下的强制执行提出书面异议。执行法院作出执行裁定后，案外人不服，自裁定送达之日起十五日内向执行法院提起诉讼的，按前款规定处理。  
申请执行人对执行裁定不服，可以自裁定送达之日起十五日内以承租人、被执行人为被告向执行法院提起诉讼，请求不带租强制执行执行标的的，人民法院经审理，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）承租人符合本条第一款规定的，判决驳回诉讼请求；  
（二）承租人不符合本条第一款规定的，判决准许不带租强制执行该执行标的。

**Article 20** In the case of compulsory enforcement without lease in situations such as auction or sale, if a person not involved in the case, on the ground that before the seizure it had signed a lawful and valid written lease contract with the person subject to enforcement, lawfully possessed and used the subject matter of enforcement, and paid the rent in accordance with the contract, files an action objecting to enforcement requesting to exclude compulsory enforcement without lease of ordinary claims during the lease period, if the reason is established, the People's Court shall support the request; if the person not involved in the case who meets the above conditions signed the lease contract and lawfully possessed and used the subject matter of enforcement before the establishment of the mortgage right, and requests to exclude compulsory enforcement without lease of the mortgage right during the lease period, the People's Court shall support the request.  
The applicant for enforcement may raise a written objection to compulsory enforcement under a lease in situations such as auction or sale. After the enforcement court makes a ruling on enforcement, if the person not involved in the case is dissatisfied and files a lawsuit with the enforcement court within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling, the case shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
If the applicant for enforcement is dissatisfied with the ruling on enforcement, the applicant may, within fifteen days from the date of service of the ruling, file a lawsuit with the enforcement court against the lessee and the person subject to enforcement as defendants, requesting compulsory enforcement of the subject matter of enforcement without lease. The People's Court, after trial, shall handle it separately in accordance with the following circumstances:  
(1) If the lessee meets the conditions in paragraph 1 of this Article, the court shall decide to dismiss the claim;  
(2) If the lessee does not meet the conditions in paragraph 1 of this Article, the court shall decide to permit compulsory enforcement of the subject matter of enforcement without lease.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　案外人与被执行人、申请执行人之间恶意串通，通过伪造证据，或者单方捏造案件基本事实，以执行异议之诉妨碍依法执行的，人民法院应当驳回其诉讼请求，并根据情节轻重予以罚款、拘留；涉嫌刑事犯罪的，人民法院应当将犯罪线索移送公安机关。  
诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人等诉讼参与人适用前款规定。  
案外人等通过虚假诉讼等方式致使执行标的无法执行或者价值减损等，给申请执行人造成损失的，应当依法予以赔偿。

**Article 21** If a person not involved in the case, the person subject to enforcement, and the applicant for enforcement maliciously collude with each other, by forging evidence or fabricating basic facts of the case on one side, to obstruct enforcement according to law by means of an action objecting to enforcement, the People's Court shall dismiss the claim, and impose a fine or detention depending on the seriousness of the circumstances; if the act is suspected of constituting a crime, the People's Court shall transfer the criminal clues to the public security organ.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to litigation agents, witnesses, expert evaluators, and other litigation participants.  
If a person not involved in the case or others cause the subject matter of enforcement to be unenforceable or its value to be diminished, etc., through means such as false litigation, thereby causing losses to the applicant for enforcement, compensation shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　申请执行人依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十八条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-238)规定提起执行异议之诉的，参照本解释的相关规定处理。

**Article 22** Where the applicant for enforcement files an action objecting to enforcement in accordance with Article 238 of the Civil Procedure Law, the case shall be handled by reference to the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　本解释自2025年7月24日起施行。

**Article 23** This Interpretation shall come into force as of July 24, 2025.
