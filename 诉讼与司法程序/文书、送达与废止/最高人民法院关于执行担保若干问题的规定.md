# 最高人民法院关于执行担保若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕4号  
**公布日期**：2018-02-22  
**生效日期**：2018-03-01

> 《最高人民法院关于执行担保若干问题的规定》已于2017年12月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1729次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年3月1日起施行。 2018年2月22日 最高人民法院 关于执行担保若干问题的规定 法释〔2018〕4号 （2017年12月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1729次会议通过，自2018年3月1日起施行）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称执行担保，是指担保人依照[民事诉讼法第二百三十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-231)规定，为担保被执行人履行生效法律文书确定的全部或者部分义务，向人民法院提供的担保。

**Article 1** For the purpose of these Provisions, "enforcement guarantee" means a guarantee provided to a People's Court by a guarantor in accordance with Article 231 of the Civil Procedure Law to guarantee that the person subject to enforcement performs all or part of the obligations determined by an effective legal document.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　执行担保可以由被执行人提供财产担保，也可以由他人提供财产担保或者保证。

**Article 2** An enforcement guarantee may be provided by the person subject to enforcement by means of property guarantee, or by another person by means of property guarantee or suretyship.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　被执行人或者他人提供执行担保的，应当向人民法院提交担保书，并将担保书副本送交申请执行人。

**Article 3** Where a person subject to enforcement or another person provides an enforcement guarantee, they shall submit a guarantee letter to the People's Court and deliver a copy of the guarantee letter to the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　担保书中应当载明担保人的基本信息、暂缓执行期限、担保期间、被担保的债权种类及数额、担保范围、担保方式、被执行人于暂缓执行期限届满后仍不履行时担保人自愿接受直接强制执行的承诺等内容。  
提供财产担保的，担保书中还应当载明担保财产的名称、数量、质量、状况、所在地、所有权或者使用权归属等内容。

**Article 4** A guarantee letter shall specify the basic information of the guarantor, the period of suspension of enforcement, the guarantee period, the types and amounts of guaranteed claims, the guarantee scope, the guarantee method, the guarantor's commitment to voluntarily accept direct compulsory enforcement if the person subject to enforcement still fails to perform after the expiration of the period of suspension of enforcement, and other contents.  
Where property guarantee is provided, the guarantee letter shall also specify the name, quantity, quality, condition, location, and attribution of ownership or use right of the guaranteed property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公司为被执行人提供执行担保的，应当提交符合[公司法第十六条](../../民事与商事/公司与破产/中华人民共和国公司法.md#art-16)规定的公司章程、董事会或者股东会、股东大会决议。

**Article 5** Where a company provides an enforcement guarantee for a person subject to enforcement, it shall submit the company's articles of association and a resolution of the board of directors or the shareholders' meeting or the general meeting of shareholders that comply with the provisions of Article 16 of the Company Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被执行人或者他人提供执行担保，申请执行人同意的，应当向人民法院出具书面同意意见，也可以由执行人员将其同意的内容记入笔录，并由申请执行人签名或者盖章。

**Article 6** Where a person subject to enforcement or another person provides an enforcement guarantee and the applicant for enforcement agrees, the applicant shall issue a written consent to the People's Court, or the enforcement personnel may record the content of such consent in the record and have it signed or sealed by the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被执行人或者他人提供财产担保，可以依照物权法、担保法规定办理登记等担保物权公示手续；已经办理公示手续的，申请执行人可以依法主张优先受偿权。  
申请执行人申请人民法院查封、扣押、冻结担保财产的，人民法院应当准许，但担保书另有约定的除外。

**Article 7** Where a person subject to enforcement or another person provides property guarantee, they may go through the formalities for the publicity of security interests such as registration in accordance with the Property Law and the Guarantee Law; if the publicity formalities have been completed, the applicant for enforcement may claim priority of compensation according to law.  
Where the applicant for enforcement applies to the People's Court to seal up, distrain, or freeze the guaranteed property, the People's Court shall grant the application, unless otherwise agreed in the guarantee letter.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院决定暂缓执行的，可以暂缓全部执行措施的实施，但担保书另有约定的除外。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court decides to suspend enforcement, it may suspend the implementation of all enforcement measures, unless otherwise agreed in the guarantee letter.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　担保书内容与事实不符，且对申请执行人合法权益产生实质影响的，人民法院可以依申请执行人的申请恢复执行。

**Article 9** Where the content of the guarantee letter is inconsistent with the facts and materially affects the lawful rights and interests of the applicant for enforcement, the People's Court may resume enforcement upon the application of the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　暂缓执行的期限应当与担保书约定一致，但最长不得超过一年。

**Article 10** The period of suspension of enforcement shall be consistent with the agreement in the guarantee letter, but shall not exceed one year at most.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　暂缓执行期限届满后被执行人仍不履行义务，或者暂缓执行期间担保人有转移、隐藏、变卖、毁损担保财产等行为的，人民法院可以依申请执行人的申请恢复执行，并直接裁定执行担保财产或者保证人的财产，不得将担保人变更、追加为被执行人。  
执行担保财产或者保证人的财产，以担保人应当履行义务部分的财产为限。被执行人有便于执行的现金、银行存款的，应当优先执行该现金、银行存款。

**Article 11** Where, after the expiration of the period of suspension of enforcement, the person subject to enforcement still fails to perform the obligations, or during the period of suspension of enforcement, the guarantor commits acts such as transferring, concealing, selling, or damaging the guaranteed property, the People's Court may resume enforcement upon the application of the applicant for enforcement and directly rule to enforce the guaranteed property or the property of the surety, but shall not change or add the guarantor as a person subject to enforcement.  
The enforcement of the guaranteed property or the property of the surety shall be limited to the portion of the property for which the guarantor shall perform the obligations. If the person subject to enforcement has cash or bank deposits that are convenient for enforcement, such cash or bank deposits shall be enforced first.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　担保期间自暂缓执行期限届满之日起计算。  
担保书中没有记载担保期间或者记载不明的，担保期间为一年。

**Article 12** The guarantee period shall be calculated from the date on which the period of suspension of enforcement expires.  
If the guarantee letter does not record the guarantee period or the record is unclear, the guarantee period shall be one year.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　担保期间届满后，申请执行人申请执行担保财产或者保证人财产的，人民法院不予支持。他人提供财产担保的，人民法院可以依其申请解除对担保财产的查封、扣押、冻结。

**Article 13** After the expiration of the guarantee period, if the applicant for enforcement applies for enforcement of the guaranteed property or the property of the surety, the People's Court shall not support it. Where another person provides property guarantee, the People's Court may, upon its application, lift the sealing up, distraining, or freezing of the guaranteed property.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　担保人承担担保责任后，提起诉讼向被执行人追偿的，人民法院应予受理。

**Article 14** After the guarantor assumes the guarantee liability, if it initiates a lawsuit to seek recourse against the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall accept the case.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　被执行人申请变更、解除全部或者部分执行措施，并担保履行生效法律文书确定义务的，参照适用本规定。

**Article 15** Where a person subject to enforcement applies to change or lift all or part of the enforcement measures and guarantees to perform the obligations determined by the effective legal document, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本规定自2018年3月1日起施行。  
本规定施行前成立的执行担保，不适用本规定。  
本规定施行前最高人民法院公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 16** These Provisions shall come into force on March 1, 2018.  
Enforcement guarantees established prior to the coming into force of these Provisions shall not be governed by these Provisions.  
If any judicial interpretation published by the Supreme People's Court prior to the coming into force of these Provisions is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
