司法解释和司法解释性质文件的决定  
  
法释〔2012〕12号  
  
为适应形势发展变化，保证国家法律统一正确适用，根据有关法律规定和审判、检察工作实际需要，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院会同有关部门，对1979年底以前联合制发的司法解释和司法解释性质文件进行了集中清理。现决定废止1979年底以前制发的13件司法解释和司法解释性质文件。废止的司法解释和司法解释性质文件从本决定施行之日起不再适用，但过去依据下列司法解释和司法解释性质文件对有关案件作出的判决、裁定仍然有效。  
  
决定废止的1979年底以前制发的部分司法解释和司法解释性质文件目录（13件）

**Article 1** August 21, 2012  
Supreme People's Court Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Decision on Annulling Part of the Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature Promulgated before the End of 1979  
Fa Shi [2012] No. 12  
To adapt to the changes in the situation and ensure the uniform and correct application of state laws, and pursuant to the relevant legal provisions and the actual needs of adjudication and procuratorial work, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate, together with the relevant departments, have conducted a collective review of the judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature that were jointly promulgated before the end of 1979. It is hereby decided to annul 13 judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature promulgated before the end of 1979. The annulled judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature shall no longer apply from the date this Decision comes into force, but the judgments and rulings rendered in the relevant cases on the basis of the following judicial interpretations and documents of a judicial interpretation nature in the past shall remain valid.  
Catalogue of Part of the Judicial Interpretations and Documents of a Judicial Interpretation Nature Promulgated before the End of 1979 Decided to Be Annulled (13 Items)
