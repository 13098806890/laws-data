# 最高人民法院关于人民法院赔偿委员会审理赔偿案件程序的暂行规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1996-05-06

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　赔偿请求人依法向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定的，应当递交赔偿申请书一式四份。赔偿请求人书写申请书确有困难的，可以口头申请。口头提出申请的，应当记入笔录，并填写《口头申请赔偿登记表》一式四份，由赔偿请求人签名、盖章。

**Article 1** A claimant for compensation who, according to law, applies to the Compensation Committee for a decision on compensation shall submit a written application for compensation in quadruplicate. If the claimant for compensation genuinely has difficulty in writing the application, he or she may make an oral application. Where an oral application is made, it shall be recorded in the笔录 and the "Registration Form for Oral Application for Compensation" shall be completed in quadruplicate, and the claimant for compensation shall sign and affix his or her seal thereto.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　赔偿请求人依法向赔偿委员会申请作出赔偿决定的被侵权事项，应当先经过依法确认。根据赔偿法第二十条第二款的规定，被要求确认的机关不予确认的，赔偿请求人有权申诉。赔偿委员会不受理要求确认的申诉案件。

**Article 2** The infringed matter for which a claimant for compensation applies to the Compensation Committee for a decision on compensation according to law shall first be confirmed according to law. Pursuant to the provisions of Article 20, paragraph 2 of the State Compensation Law, if the organ requested to make confirmation refuses to do so, the claimant for compensation shall have the right to lodge a petition. The Compensation Committee shall not accept a petition case requesting confirmation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　赔偿请求人提出赔偿申请，除符合赔偿法第六条规定的条件以外，还应当提供以下相关的法律文书和证明材料：  
（一）经依法确认有赔偿法第十五条、第十六条规定情形的法律文书，包括：人民法院一审宣告无罪并已发生法律效力的刑事判决书、人民法院二审宣告无罪的刑事判决书、人民法院依照审判监督程序再审宣告无罪的刑事判决书、人民检察院不起诉决定书或者公安机关释放证明书；  
（二）经依法确认有赔偿法第三十一条规定情形的法律文书；  
（三）赔偿义务机关作出的赔偿或者不予赔偿决定书。赔偿义务机关逾期未作出决定的，应当提供相关的证明材料；  
赔偿义务机关是侦查、检察或者监狱管理机关的，应当提供上一级机关作出的复议决定书。复议机关逾期未作复议决定的，应当提供相关的证明材料；  
（四）其他相关的法律文书、证明材料。

**Article 3** In addition to meeting the conditions prescribed in Article 6 of the State Compensation Law, a claimant for compensation who submits an application for compensation shall also provide the following relevant legal documents and evidentiary materials:  
(1) Legal documents confirming according to law the existence of circumstances specified in Article 15 or Article 16 of the State Compensation Law, including: a criminal judgment of first instance rendered by a People's Court acquitting the defendant that has taken legal effect, a criminal judgment of second instance rendered by a People's Court acquitting the defendant, a criminal judgment rendered by a People's Court upon retrial in accordance with the adjudication supervision procedure acquitting the defendant, a decision of a People's Procuratorate not to prosecute, or a certificate of release issued by a public security organ;  
(2) Legal documents confirming according to law the existence of circumstances specified in Article 31 of the State Compensation Law;  
(3) A decision made by the organ liable for compensation on compensation or on denial of compensation. If the organ liable for compensation fails to make a decision within the time limit, relevant evidentiary materials shall be provided;  
Where the organ liable for compensation is an investigation, procuratorial, or prison administration organ, the reconsideration decision made by the organ at the next higher level shall be provided. If the reconsideration organ fails to make a reconsideration decision within the time limit, relevant evidentiary materials shall be provided;  
(4) Other relevant legal documents and evidentiary materials.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　赔偿委员会收到赔偿申请后，应当在七日内决定是否立案，并及时通知赔偿请求人。  
缺少有关证明材料的，应当通知赔偿请求人予以补充。收到赔偿申请的时间应当自材料补充齐全后起算。

**Article 4** After receiving an application for compensation, the Compensation Committee shall, within seven days, decide whether to place the case on file and shall promptly notify the claimant for compensation.  
If relevant evidentiary materials are lacking, the claimant for compensation shall be notified to supplement them. The time of receipt of the application for compensation shall be calculated from the date on which the materials are completed.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　经审查，认为赔偿请求人的赔偿申请依法不属于赔偿委员会受理的，应当告知赔偿请求人向有关机关提出赔偿申请，或者转请有关部门处理，并通知赔偿请求人。

**Article 5** Upon examination, if it is considered that the compensation application of the claimant for compensation is not within the jurisdiction of the Compensation Committee according to law, the claimant for compensation shall be informed to submit a compensation application to the relevant organ, or the case shall be referred to the relevant department for handling, and the claimant for compensation shall be notified.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　赔偿委员会立案后，在依法作出决定之前，赔偿请求人申请撤回赔偿申请的，应当准许。  
二、案件审理

**Article 6** After the Compensation Committee places a case on file and before a decision is made according to law, if the claimant for compensation applies to withdraw the compensation application, the application shall be permitted.  
II. Adjudication of Cases

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　赔偿委员会决定立案审理的赔偿案件，应当指定专人负责办理。

**Article 7** For a compensation case that the Compensation Committee decides to place on file for adjudication, a designated person shall be assigned to handle it.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　赔偿委员会立案后，应当于十五日内将赔偿请求人的赔偿申请书副本送达复议机关和赔偿义务机关。

**Article 8** After placing a case on file, the Compensation Committee shall, within fifteen days, serve a copy of the compensation application of the claimant for compensation on the review organ and the organ liable for compensation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　赔偿委员会根据审理案件的需要，可以通知赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和复议机关的有关人员或者相关证人提供有关情况、案件材料、证明材料，或者到人民法院接受调查。

**Article 9** The Compensation Committee may, as needed for the adjudication of a case, notify the relevant personnel of the claimant for compensation, the organ liable for compensation, and the review organ, or relevant witnesses, to provide relevant information, case materials, or evidentiary materials, or to appear before the People's Court to accept investigation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　赔偿委员会对赔偿请求人和被请求的赔偿义务机关、复议机关调查取证，应当分别进行。

**Article 10** The Compensation Committee shall conduct investigations and gather evidence separately from the claimant for compensation and the requested organ liable for compensation and the review organ.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　经审查，赔偿案件事实清楚、证据确实、充分的，应当写出赔偿案件审查报告，并附有关案卷和证明材料，报请赔偿委员会主任提交赔偿委员会审理。

**Article 11** Upon examination, if the facts of a compensation case are clear and the evidence is truthful and sufficient, a review report on the compensation case shall be prepared, together with the relevant case files and evidentiary materials, and shall be submitted to the director of the Compensation Committee for submission to the Compensation Committee for adjudication.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　赔偿案件审查报告应当包括以下内容：  
（一）案件的由来；  
（二）赔偿请求人的基本情况，赔偿义务机关、复议机关的名称及其法定代表人；  
（三）赔偿请求人申请事项及理由；  
（四）申请的赔偿案件确认情况、赔偿义务机关的决定情况以及复议机关的复议情况；  
（五）承办人审查认定的事实及依据；  
（六）处理意见和理由。

**Article 12** A review report on a compensation case shall include the following contents:  
(1) The origin of the case;  
(2) The basic information of the claimant for compensation, the names of the organ liable for compensation and the review organ, and their legal representatives;  
(3) The matters and grounds of the application of the claimant for compensation;  
(4) The confirmation status of the compensation case applied for, the decision status of the organ liable for compensation, and the review status of the review organ;  
(5) The facts and basis established and examined by the person handling the case;  
(6) The handling opinion and reasons.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　赔偿委员会审理案件依法不公开进行。

**Article 13** The Compensation Committee shall adjudicate cases not in public according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　赔偿委员会讨论案件，实行少数服从多数的原则。赔偿委员会半数以上委员的意见为赔偿委员会的决定意见。

**Article 14** The Compensation Committee shall, when discussing a case, apply the principle of the minority being subordinate to the majority. The opinions of more than half of the members of the Compensation Committee shall be the decision opinion of the Compensation Committee.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　赔偿委员会认为重大、疑难的案件，必要时由赔偿委员会主任报请院长提交审判  
委员会讨论决定。审判委员会的决定，赔偿委员会应当执行。

**Article 15** For a case that the Compensation Committee considers major or difficult, when necessary, the director of the Compensation Committee shall report to the president to submit it to the Adjudication Committee for discussion and decision. The Compensation Committee shall implement the decision of the Adjudication Committee.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　赔偿委员会审理的案件，应当分别下列情形依法作出决定：  
（一）赔偿义务机关决定或者复议机关复议决定适用法律正确，赔偿方式、赔偿数额适当的，应当决定予以维持；  
（二）赔偿义务机关决定、复议机关复议决定适用法律不当的，应当撤销原决定，依法作出决定；赔偿方式、赔偿数额不当的，应当作出变更决定；  
（三）经依法确认有赔偿法第十五条、第十六条、第三十一条规定情形之一，赔偿义务机关或者复议机关逾期未作决定的，应当作出赔偿或者不予赔偿的决定；  
（四）赔偿请求人申请赔偿事项属于赔偿法第十七条规定的国家不承担赔偿责任的情形，或者已超过法定时效的，应当作出不予赔偿的决定。

**Article 16** For a case adjudicated by the Compensation Committee, a decision shall be made according to law in light of the following circumstances respectively:  
(1) If the decision of the organ liable for compensation or the reconsideration decision of the review organ correctly applies the law and the method and amount of compensation are appropriate, the decision shall be upheld;  
(2) If the decision of the organ liable for compensation or the reconsideration decision of the review organ incorrectly applies the law, the original decision shall be revoked and a decision shall be made according to law; if the method or amount of compensation is inappropriate, a decision to modify shall be made;  
(3) If one of the circumstances specified in Article 15, Article 16, or Article 31 of the State Compensation Law has been confirmed according to law, and the organ liable for compensation or the review organ fails to make a decision within the time limit, a decision on compensation or on denial of compensation shall be made;  
(4) If the matter for which the claimant for compensation applies for compensation falls under the circumstance where the State does not bear compensation liability as specified in Article 17 of the State Compensation Law, or if the statutory limitation has expired, a decision to deny compensation shall be made.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　赔偿委员会审理案件作出的决定，应当制作人民法院赔偿委员会决定书。

**Article 17** For a decision made by the Compensation Committee upon adjudicating a case, a written decision of the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall be prepared.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院赔偿委员会决定书，应当载明以下事项：  
（一）赔偿请求人的基本情况，赔偿义务机关、复议机关的名称及其法定代表人；  
（二）赔偿请求人申请事项，赔偿义务机关的决定、复议机关的复议决定情况；  
（三）赔偿委员会认定的事实及依据；  
（四）决定的理由与法律依据；  
（五）决定内容。

**Article 18** A written decision of the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall state the following matters:  
(1) The basic information of the claimant for compensation, the names of the organ liable for compensation and the review organ, and their legal representatives;  
(2) The matters applied for by the claimant for compensation, and the decision of the organ liable for compensation and the reconsideration decision of the review organ;  
(3) The facts and basis established by the Compensation Committee;  
(4) The reasons and legal basis for the decision;  
(5) The content of the decision.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民法院赔偿委员会决定书由赔偿委员会主任审核签发，加盖人民法院院印。

**Article 19** A written decision of the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall be reviewed and signed by the director of the Compensation Committee, and shall be affixed with the seal of the People's Court.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　赔偿案件应当在三个月内作出是否赔偿的决定。因案件情况复杂，三个月内不能作出决定的，经本院院长批准，可以延长一个月；仍不能作出决定需要再延长审理期限的，应当报请上级人民法院批准，再延长的时间最多不得超过三个月。  
三、执行

**Article 20** A decision on whether to grant compensation in a compensation case shall be made within three months. If a decision cannot be made within three months due to the complexity of the case, the period may be extended by one month with the approval of the president of the People's Court; if a decision still cannot be made and a further extension of the adjudication period is necessary, approval shall be sought from the People's Court at the next higher level, and the further extension shall not exceed three months at most.  
III. Enforcement

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　赔偿委员会决定一经作出，即发生法律效力，必须执行。

**Article 21** Once a decision of the Compensation Committee is made, it shall take legal effect and must be enforced.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民法院赔偿委员会决定书应当根据决定事项，分别送达赔偿请求人、赔偿义务机关和复议机关。  
四、其他规定

**Article 22** A written decision of the Compensation Committee of the People's Court shall, in accordance with the matters decided, be served separately on the claimant for compensation, the organ liable for compensation, and the review organ.  
IV. Miscellaneous Provisions

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　赔偿委员会决定生效后，赔偿委员会如发现原认定的事实或者适用法律错误，必须改变原决定的，经本院院长决定或者上级人民法院指令，赔偿委员会应当重新审理，依法作出决定。

**Article 23** After a decision of the Compensation Committee takes effect, if the Compensation Committee discovers that the original findings of fact or application of law are erroneous and the original decision must be changed, the Compensation Committee shall, upon a decision by the president of the People's Court or an instruction from the People's Court at the next higher level, retry the case and make a decision according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　公民、法人和其他组织依法提起赔偿申请，可以委托律师、提出申请的公民的近亲属或者所在单位推荐的人，以及经人民法院许可的其他公民为代理人。

**Article 24** A citizen, legal person, or other organization that initiates a compensation application according to law may entrust a lawyer, a close relative of the citizen filing the application, a person recommended by the entity to which the citizen belongs, or other citizens permitted by the People's Court as agent ad litem.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　赔偿委员会是人民法院审理赔偿案件的审判组织。赔偿委员会下设办公室，负责办理具体事宜。

**Article 25** The Compensation Committee is the adjudication organization of the People's Court for adjudicating compensation cases. The Compensation Committee shall have an office under it, which shall be responsible for handling specific matters.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　人民法院是赔偿义务机关的，审理赔偿案件可参照本规定。  
1996年5月6日

**Article 26** Where a People's Court is the organ liable for compensation, the adjudication of compensation cases may be conducted by reference to these Provisions.
