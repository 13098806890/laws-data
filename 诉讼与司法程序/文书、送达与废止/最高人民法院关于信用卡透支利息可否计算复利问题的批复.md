### 最高人民法院关于信用卡透支利息可否计算复利问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于信用卡透支利息可否计算复利问题的批复  
  
法复〔1996〕18号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（1996）粤高法民第5号请示收悉。关于长城万事达信用卡透支利息是否可以计算复利的问题，经研究，答复如下：  
  
关于信用卡透支利息的计算方法，中国人民银行银发（1992）298号《信用卡业务管理暂行办法》作了规定，应当按该办法规定的方法计算。该办法对透支利率的规定已含有惩罚性质。所以，信用卡透支利息不应当再计算复利。  
  
1996年11月29日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether Compound Interest May Be Calculated on Credit Card Overdraft Interest  
Fa Fu [1996] No. 18  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions No. 5 (1996) Yue Gao Fa Min [1996]. Regarding the issue of whether compound interest may be calculated on the overdraft interest of Great Wall MasterCard credit cards, upon study, we reply as follows:  
The method for calculating credit card overdraft interest has been stipulated in the Interim Measures for the Administration of Credit Card Business (Yin Fa [1992] No. 298) of the People's Bank of China, and shall be calculated in accordance with the method prescribed therein. The provisions of the said Measures on overdraft interest rates already have a punitive nature. Therefore, no compound interest shall be further calculated on credit card overdraft interest.  
November 29, 1996
