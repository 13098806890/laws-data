# 最高人民法院关于修改《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排》的决定法释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕1号  
**公布日期**：2019-12-30  
**生效日期**：2020-03-01

> 根据《中华人民共和国澳门特别行政区基本法》第九十三条的规定，最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区经协商，对2001年签署的《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排》（以下简称《安排》）进行了修改。《安排》的修改文本已于2019年12月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1790次会议通过，于2020年1月14日签署，现予公布。《安排》的修改文本自2020年3月1日起生效。 2020年1月14日 最高人民法院 关于修改《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院 就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取 证据的安排》的决定 法释〔2020〕1号 经最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区协商，决定对《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排》（法释〔2001〕26号，以下简称《安排》）作如下修改： 一、将第二条修改为：“双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，通过各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院进行。最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院可以直接相互委托送达和调取证据。 “经与澳门特别行政区终审法院协商，最高人民法院可以授权部分中级人民法院、基层人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院相互委托送达和调取证据。” 二、增加一条，作为第三条：“双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，通过内地与澳门司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递；不能通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的，采用邮寄方式。 “通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的司法文书、证据材料等文件，应当确保其完整性、真实性和不可修改性。 “通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的司法文书、证据材料等文件与原件具有同等效力。” 三、将第三条改为第四条，修改为：“各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院收到对方法院的委托书后，应当立即将委托书及所附司法文书和相关文件转送根据其本辖区法律规定有权完成该受托事项的法院。 “受委托方法院发现委托事项存在材料不齐全、信息不完整等问题，影响其完成受托事项的，应当及时通知委托方法院补充材料或者作出说明。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  内地人民法院与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件（在内地包括劳动争议案件，在澳门特别行政区包括民事劳工案件）相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，均适用本安排。

**Article 1** This Arrangement shall apply to the mutual entrustment for service of judicial documents and collection of evidence between the People's Courts of the Mainland and the courts of the Macao Special Administrative Region in civil and commercial cases (including labor dispute cases in the Mainland and civil labor cases in the Macao Special Administrative Region).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，通过各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院进行。最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院可以直接相互委托送达和调取证据。  
经与澳门特别行政区终审法院协商，最高人民法院可以授权部分中级人民法院、基层人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院相互委托送达和调取证据。

**Article 2** The mutual entrustment for service of judicial documents and collection of evidence between the two sides shall be conducted through the Higher People's Courts and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region. The Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region may directly entrust each other with service of documents and collection of evidence.  
Upon consultation with the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region, the Supreme People's Court may authorize some Intermediate People's Courts and Primary People's Courts to mutually entrust service of judicial documents and collection of evidence with the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  双方相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据，通过内地与澳门司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递；不能通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的，采用邮寄方式。  
通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的司法文书、证据材料等文件，应当确保其完整性、真实性和不可修改性。  
通过司法协助网络平台以电子方式转递的司法文书、证据材料等文件与原件具有同等效力。

**Article 3** The mutual entrustment for service of judicial documents and collection of evidence between the two sides shall be transmitted by electronic means through the Mainland-Macao Judicial Assistance Network Platform; if transmission by electronic means through the Judicial Assistance Network Platform is not possible, it shall be transmitted by postal service.  
Judicial documents, evidence materials and other documents transmitted by electronic means through the Judicial Assistance Network Platform shall guarantee their integrity, authenticity and non-modifiability.  
Judicial documents, evidence materials and other documents transmitted by electronic means through the Judicial Assistance Network Platform shall have the same effect as the originals.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  各高级人民法院和澳门特别行政区终审法院收到对方法院的委托书后，应当立即将委托书及所附司法文书和相关文件转送根据其本辖区法律规定有权完成该受托事项的法院。  
受委托方法院发现委托事项存在材料不齐全、信息不完整等问题，影响其完成受托事项的，应当及时通知委托方法院补充材料或者作出说明。  
经授权的中级人民法院、基层人民法院收到澳门特别行政区终审法院委托书后，认为不属于本院管辖的，应当报请高级人民法院处理。

**Article 4** Upon receipt of a letter of entrustment from the court of the other side, each Higher People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region shall immediately transmit the letter of entrustment and the attached judicial documents and related documents to a court that has the authority to complete the entrusted matter in accordance with the laws of its jurisdiction.  
If the entrusted court discovers that there are problems such as incomplete materials or incomplete information in the entrusted matter, which affect its completion of the entrusted matter, it shall promptly notify the entrusting court to supplement the materials or make explanations.  
If an authorized Intermediate People's Court or Primary People's Court, upon receipt of a letter of entrustment from the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region, considers that the matter is not within its own jurisdiction, it shall report to the Higher People's Court for handling.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  委托书应当以中文文本提出。所附司法文书及其他相关文件没有中文文本的，应当提供中文译本。

**Article 5** The letter of entrustment shall be submitted in Chinese. If the attached judicial documents and other related documents are not in Chinese, a Chinese translation shall be provided.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  委托方法院应当在合理的期限内提出委托请求，以保证受委托方法院收到委托书后，及时完成受托事项。  
受委托方法院应当优先处理受托事项。完成受托事项的期限，送达文书最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起两个月，调取证据最迟不得超过自收到委托书之日起三个月。

**Article 6** The entrusting court shall submit the entrustment request within a reasonable period to ensure that the entrusted court, upon receipt of the letter of entrustment, completes the entrusted matter in a timely manner.  
The entrusted court shall give priority to handling the entrusted matter. The time limit for completing the entrusted matter shall not exceed two months from the date of receipt of the letter of entrustment for service of documents, and shall not exceed three months from the date of receipt of the letter of entrustment for collection of evidence.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  受委托方法院应当根据本辖区法律规定执行受托事项。委托方法院请求按照特殊方式执行委托事项的，受委托方法院认为不违反本辖区的法律规定的，可以按照特殊方式执行。

**Article 7** The entrusted court shall execute the entrusted matter in accordance with the laws of its jurisdiction. If the entrusting court requests that the entrusted matter be executed in a special manner, the entrusted court may execute it in such special manner if it considers that doing so does not violate the laws of its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  委托方法院无须支付受委托方法院在送达司法文书、调取证据时发生的费用、税项。但受委托方法院根据其本辖区法律规定，有权在调取证据时，要求委托方法院预付鉴定人、证人、翻译人员的费用，以及因采用委托方法院在委托书中请求以特殊方式送达司法文书、调取证据所产生的费用。

**Article 8** The entrusting court shall not be required to pay the costs, taxes or charges incurred by the entrusted court in serving judicial documents or collecting evidence. However, in accordance with the laws of its jurisdiction, the entrusted court shall have the right, when collecting evidence, to require the entrusting court to advance the fees of expert witnesses, witnesses, and interpreters, as well as the costs arising from the adoption of the special manner requested by the entrusting court in the letter of entrustment for service of judicial documents or collection of evidence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  受委托方法院收到委托书后，不得以其本辖区法律规定对委托方法院审理的该民商事案件享有专属管辖权或者不承认对该请求事项提起诉讼的权利为由，不予执行受托事项。  
受委托方法院在执行受托事项时，发现该事项不属于法院职权范围，或者内地人民法院认为在内地执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或社会公共利益，或者澳门特别行政区法院认为在澳门特别行政区执行该受托事项将违反其基本法律原则或公共秩序的，可以不予执行，但应当及时向委托方法院书面说明不予执行的原因。  
二、司法文书的送达

**Article 9** Upon receipt of the letter of entrustment, the entrusted court shall not refuse to execute the entrusted matter on the ground that the laws of its jurisdiction confer exclusive jurisdiction over the civil or commercial case being tried by the entrusting court or that it does not recognize the right to institute proceedings in respect of the requested matter.  
If, during the execution of the entrusted matter, the entrusted court discovers that the matter is not within the functions and powers of a court, or the Mainland People's Court considers that executing the entrusted matter in the Mainland would violate its fundamental legal principles or social public interest, or the Macao Special Administrative Region court considers that executing the entrusted matter in the Macao Special Administrative Region would violate its fundamental legal principles or public order, the entrusted court may refuse to execute the matter, but shall promptly provide a written explanation to the entrusting court of the reasons for such refusal.  
II. Service of Judicial Documents

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  委托方法院请求送达司法文书，须出具盖有其印章或者法官签名的委托书，并在委托书中说明委托机关的名称、受送达人的姓名或者名称、详细地址以及案件性质。委托方法院请求按特殊方式送达或者有特别注意的事项的，应当在委托书中注明。

**Article 10** When requesting service of judicial documents, the entrusting court shall issue a letter of entrustment bearing its seal or the signature of a judge, and shall state in the letter of entrustment the name of the entrusting authority, the name of the person on whom service is to be made, the detailed address, and the nature of the case. If the entrusting court requests that service be made by a special method or there are matters requiring special attention, it shall indicate this in the letter of entrustment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  采取邮寄方式委托的，委托书及所附司法文书和其他相关文件一式两份，受送达人为两人以上的，每人一式两份。

**Article 11** Where the entrustment is made by postal service, the letter of entrustment and the attached judicial documents and other related documents shall be in duplicate; if there are two or more persons to be served, each person shall receive a duplicate.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  完成司法文书送达事项后，内地人民法院应当出具送达回证；澳门特别行政区法院应当出具送达证明书。出具的送达回证和送达证明书，应当注明送达的方法、地点和日期以及司法文书接收人的身份，并加盖法院印章。  
受委托方法院无法送达的，应当在送达回证或者送达证明书上注明妨碍送达的原因、拒收事由和日期，并及时书面回复委托方法院。

**Article 12** After completing the service of judicial documents, the Mainland People's Court shall issue a certificate of service; the Macao Special Administrative Region court shall issue a proof of service. The certificate of service or proof of service issued shall specify the method, place and date of service and the identity of the recipient of the judicial documents, and shall be affixed with the seal of the court.  
If the entrusted court is unable to effect service, it shall indicate on the certificate of service or proof of service the reasons preventing service, the grounds for refusal and the date, and shall promptly reply in writing to the entrusting court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  不论委托方法院司法文书中确定的出庭日期或者期限是否已过，受委托方法院均应当送达。

**Article 13** The entrusted court shall effect service regardless of whether the date or time limit for appearance specified in the judicial documents of the entrusting court has passed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  受委托方法院对委托方法院委托送达的司法文书和所附相关文件的内容和后果不负法律责任。

**Article 14** The entrusted court shall not bear legal liability for the content and consequences of the judicial documents and attached related documents entrusted for service by the entrusting court.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  本安排中的司法文书在内地包括：起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、授权委托书、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证以及其他司法文书和所附相关文件；在澳门特别行政区包括：起诉状复本、答辩状复本、反诉状复本、上诉状复本、陈述书、申辩书、声明异议书、反驳书、申请书、撤诉书、认诺书、和解书、财产目录、财产分割表、和解建议书、债权人协议书、传唤书、通知书、法官批示、命令状、法庭许可令状、判决书、合议庭裁判书、送达证明书以及其他司法文书和所附相关文件。  
三、调取证据

**Article 15** Judicial documents in this Arrangement in the Mainland include: copies of complaints, copies of appeals, copies of counterclaims, copies of defenses, powers of attorney, summonses, judgments, mediation statements, rulings, payment orders, decisions, notices, certificates, certificates of service, and other judicial documents and attached related documents; in the Macao Special Administrative Region they include: duplicates of complaints, duplicates of defenses, duplicates of counterclaims, duplicates of appeals, written statements, defenses, objections, rebuttals, applications, withdrawal statements, acknowledgments, settlement agreements, property inventories, property division schedules, settlement proposals, creditors' agreements, summonses, notices, judges' orders, commands, court permits, judgments, collegiate bench rulings, proofs of service, and other judicial documents and attached related documents.  
III. Collection of Evidence

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  委托方法院请求调取的证据只能是用于与诉讼有关的证据。

**Article 16** The evidence requested to be collected by the entrusting court may only be evidence related to the litigation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  双方相互委托代为调取证据的委托书应当写明：  
（一）委托法院的名称；  
（二）当事人及其诉讼代理人的姓名、地址和其他一切有助于辨别其身份的情况；  
（三）委托调取证据的原因，以及委托调取证据的具体事项；  
（四）被调查人的姓名、地址和其他一切有助于辨别其身份的情况，以及需要向其提出的问题；  
（五）调取证据需采用的特殊方式；  
（六）有助于执行该委托的其他一切情况。

**Article 17** The letter of entrustment for mutual entrustment of collection of evidence shall state:  
(1) the name of the entrusting court;  
(2) the names, addresses and all other circumstances helpful for identifying the parties and their litigation agents;  
(3) the reasons for entrusting the collection of evidence and the specific matters for which evidence is to be collected;  
(4) the names, addresses and all other circumstances helpful for identifying the persons to be investigated, and the questions to be put to them;  
(5) the special method to be used for collecting evidence;  
(6) all other circumstances helpful for executing the entrustment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  代为调取证据的范围包括：代为询问当事人、证人和鉴定人，代为进行鉴定和司法勘验，调取其他与诉讼有关的证据。

**Article 18** The scope of collection of evidence includes: questioning parties, witnesses and expert witnesses; conducting expert evaluations and judicial inspections; and collecting other evidence related to the litigation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  委托方法院提出要求的，受委托方法院应当将取证的时间、地点通知委托方法院，以便有关当事人及其诉讼代理人能够出席。

**Article 19** If the entrusting court so requests, the entrusted court shall notify the entrusting court of the time and place of evidence collection, so that the relevant parties and their litigation agents may be present.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  受委托方法院在执行委托调取证据时，根据委托方法院的请求，可以允许委托方法院派司法人员出席。必要时，经受委托方允许，委托方法院的司法人员可以向证人、鉴定人等发问。

**Article 20** When executing the entrusted collection of evidence, the entrusted court may, at the request of the entrusting court, permit judicial personnel of the entrusting court to be present. When necessary, with the permission of the entrusted party, the judicial personnel of the entrusting court may put questions to witnesses, expert witnesses, etc.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  受委托方法院完成委托调取证据的事项后，应当向委托方法院书面说明。  
未能按委托方法院的请求全部或者部分完成调取证据事项的，受委托方法院应当向委托方法院书面说明妨碍调取证据的原因，采取邮寄方式委托的，应及时退回委托书及所附文件。  
当事人、证人根据受委托方的法律规定，拒绝作证或者推辞提供证言的，受委托方法院应当书面通知委托方法院，采取邮寄方式委托的，应及时退回委托书及所附文件。

**Article 21** After completing the entrusted matter of collection of evidence, the entrusted court shall provide a written explanation to the entrusting court.  
If the entrusted court fails to complete all or part of the collection of evidence as requested by the entrusting court, it shall provide a written explanation to the entrusting court of the reasons preventing the collection, and in the case of entrustment by postal service, it shall promptly return the letter of entrustment and attached documents.  
If a party or witness refuses to testify or declines to give evidence in accordance with the laws of the entrusted party, the entrusted court shall notify the entrusting court in writing, and in the case of entrustment by postal service, it shall promptly return the letter of entrustment and attached documents.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  受委托方法院可以根据委托方法院的请求，并经证人、鉴定人同意，协助安排其辖区的证人、鉴定人到对方辖区出庭作证。  
证人、鉴定人在委托方地域内逗留期间，不得因在其离开受委托方地域之前，在委托方境内所实施的行为或者针对他所作的裁决而被刑事起诉、羁押，不得为履行刑罚或者其他处罚而被剥夺财产或者扣留身份证件，不得以任何方式对其人身自由加以限制。  
证人、鉴定人完成所需诉讼行为，且可自由离开委托方地域后，在委托方境内逗留超过七天，或者已离开委托方地域又自行返回时，前款规定的豁免即行终止。  
证人、鉴定人到委托方法院出庭而导致的费用及补偿，由委托方法院预付。  
本条规定的出庭作证人员，在澳门特别行政区还包括当事人。

**Article 22** The entrusted court may, at the request of the entrusting court and with the consent of the witnesses and expert witnesses, assist in arranging for witnesses and expert witnesses from its jurisdiction to appear in court in the other party's jurisdiction to give testimony.  
During the period of stay of witnesses and expert witnesses in the territory of the entrusting party, they shall not be subject to criminal prosecution or custody for acts committed or rulings made against them in the territory of the entrusting party prior to their departure from the territory of the entrusted party; they shall not be deprived of property or have their identity documents withheld for the execution of a penalty or other punishment; and their personal freedom shall not be restricted in any manner.  
After the witnesses or expert witnesses have completed the required procedural acts and are free to leave the territory of the entrusting party, if they remain in the territory of the entrusting party for more than seven days, or if they leave the territory of the entrusting party and return on their own, the immunity provided in the preceding paragraph shall cease.  
The costs and allowances arising from the appearance of witnesses and expert witnesses in court before the entrusting court shall be advanced by the entrusting court.  
For the purposes of this Article, the persons appearing in court to give testimony in the Macao Special Administrative Region shall also include parties.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  受委托方法院可以根据委托方法院的请求，并经证人、鉴定人同意，协助安排其辖区的证人、鉴定人通过视频、音频作证。

**Article 23** The entrusted court may, at the request of the entrusting court and with the consent of the witnesses and expert witnesses, assist in arranging for witnesses and expert witnesses from its jurisdiction to give testimony by video or audio.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  受委托方法院取证时，被调查的当事人、证人、鉴定人等的代理人可以出席。  
四、附则

**Article 24** When the entrusted court collects evidence, the agents of the parties, witnesses, expert witnesses, etc. being investigated may be present.  
IV. Supplementary Provisions

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  受委托方法院可以根据委托方法院的请求代为查询并提供本辖区的有关法律。

**Article 25** The entrusted court may, at the request of the entrusting court, make inquiries on behalf of the entrusting court and provide the relevant laws of its jurisdiction.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  本安排在执行过程中遇有问题的，由最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区终审法院协商解决。  
本安排需要修改的，由最高人民法院与澳门特别行政区协商解决。

**Article 26** If problems arise in the course of implementation of this Arrangement, they shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region.  
Any amendments to this Arrangement shall be resolved through consultation between the Supreme People's Court and the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  本安排自2001年9月15日起生效。本安排的修改文本自2020年3月1日起生效。

**Article 27** This Arrangement shall take effect as of September 15, 2001. The amended text of this Arrangement shall take effect as of March 1, 2020.
