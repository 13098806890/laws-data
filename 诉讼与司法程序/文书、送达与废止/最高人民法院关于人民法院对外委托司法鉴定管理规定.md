# 最高人民法院关于人民法院对外委托司法鉴定管理规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕8号  
**公布日期**：2002-02-22  
**生效日期**：2002-04-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 为规范人民法院对外委托和组织司法鉴定工作，根据《人民法院司法鉴定工作暂行规定》，制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated to regulate the People's Court's external commissioning and organization of judicial expert evaluation, pursuant to the Interim Provisions on Judicial Expert Evaluation of the People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 人民法院司法鉴定机构负责统一对外委托和组织司法鉴定。未设司法鉴定机构的人民法院，可在司法行政管理部门配备专职司法鉴定人员，并由司法行政管理部门代行对外委托司法鉴定的职责。

**Article 2** The judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall be responsible for the unified external commissioning and organization of judicial expert evaluation. In a People's Court without a judicial expert evaluation institution, full-time judicial expert evaluation personnel may be assigned in the judicial administrative department, and the judicial administrative department shall exercise on its behalf the duty of external commissioning of judicial expert evaluation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 人民法院司法鉴定机构建立社会鉴定机构和鉴定人（以下简称鉴定人）名册，根据鉴定对象对专业技术的要求，随机选择和委托鉴定人进行司法鉴定。

**Article 3** The judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall establish a roster of social expert evaluation institutions and evaluators (hereinafter referred to as evaluators), and shall randomly select and commission evaluators to conduct judicial expert evaluation according to the professional and technical requirements of the evaluation subject.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 自愿接受人民法院委托从事司法鉴定，申请进入人民法院司法鉴定人名册的社会鉴定、检测、评估机构，应当向人民法院司法鉴定机构提交申请书和以下材料：  
（一）企业或社团法人营业执照副本；  
（二）专业资质证书；  
（三）专业技术人员名单、执业资格和主要业绩；  
（四）年检文书；  
（五）其他必要的文件、资料。

**Article 4** A social expert evaluation, testing, or assessment institution that voluntarily accepts the commission of the People's Court to engage in judicial expert evaluation and applies to enter the roster of judicial expert evaluators of the People's Court shall submit an application and the following materials to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court:  
(1) a copy of the business license of an enterprise or social organization legal person;  
(2) professional qualification certificates;  
(3) a list of professional and technical personnel, their practicing qualifications and main achievements;  
(4) annual inspection documents;  
(5) other necessary documents and materials.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 以个人名义自愿接受人民法院委托从事司法鉴定，申请进入人民法院司法鉴定人名册的专业技术人员，应当向人民法院司法鉴定机构提交申请书和以下材料：  
（一）单位介绍信；  
（二）专业资格证书；  
（三）主要业绩证明；  
（四）其他必要的文件、资料等。

**Article 5** A professional and technical person who voluntarily accepts the commission of the People's Court in his/her individual capacity to engage in judicial expert evaluation and applies to enter the roster of judicial expert evaluators of the People's Court shall submit an application and the following materials to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court:  
(1) a letter of introduction from the work unit;  
(2) professional qualification certificates;  
(3) proof of main achievements;  
(4) other necessary documents and materials.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 人民法院司法鉴定机构应当对提出申请的鉴定人进行全面审查，择优确定对外委托和组织司法鉴定的鉴定人候选名单。

**Article 6** The judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall conduct a comprehensive review of the evaluators who have submitted applications, and shall select the best to determine the candidate list of evaluators for external commissioning and organization of judicial expert evaluation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 申请进入地方人民法院鉴定人名册的单位和个人，其入册资格由有关人民法院司法鉴定机构审核，报上一级人民法院司法鉴定机构批准，并报最高人民法院司法鉴定机构备案。

**Article 7** For entities and individuals applying to enter the roster of evaluators of a local People's Court, their qualification for inclusion in the roster shall be examined by the judicial expert evaluation institution of the relevant People's Court, submitted to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court at the next higher level for approval, and reported to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the Supreme People's Court for record.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 经批准列入人民法院司法鉴定人名册的鉴定人，在《人民法院报》予以公告。

**Article 8** Evaluators who have been approved for inclusion in the roster of judicial expert evaluators of the People's Court shall be publicly announced in the People's Court Daily.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 已列入名册的鉴定人应当接受有关人民法院司法鉴定机构的年度审核，并提交以下材料：  
（一）年度业务工作报告书；  
（二）专业技术人员变更情况；  
（三）仪器设备更新情况；  
（四）其他变更情况和要求提交的材料。  
年度审核有变更事项的，有关司法鉴定机构应当逐级报最高人民法院司法鉴定机构备案。

**Article 9** Evaluators that have been included in the roster shall undergo the annual review of the relevant judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court and submit the following materials:  
(1) an annual business work report;  
(2) changes in professional and technical personnel;  
(3) updates of instruments and equipment;  
(4) other changes and required materials.  
If there are changes in the annual review, the relevant judicial expert evaluation institution shall report them level by level to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the Supreme People's Court for record.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院司法鉴定机构依据尊重当事人选择和人民法院指定相结合的原则，组织诉讼双方当事人进行司法鉴定的对外委托。  
诉讼双方当事人协商不一致的，由人民法院司法鉴定机构在列入名册的、符合鉴定要求的鉴定人中，选择受委托人鉴定。

**Article 10** The judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall organize the external commissioning of judicial expert evaluation for both parties to the litigation based on the principle of combining respect for the parties' choice with designation by the People's Court.  
If the parties to the litigation fail to reach an agreement through consultation, the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall select a commissioned evaluator from among the evaluators included in the roster who meet the evaluation requirements.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 司法鉴定所涉及的专业未纳入名册时，人民法院司法鉴定机构可以从社会相关专业中，择优选定受委托单位或专业人员进行鉴定。如果被选定的单位或专业人员需要进入鉴定人名册的，仍应当呈报上一级人民法院司法鉴定机构批准。

**Article 11** If the specialty involved in judicial expert evaluation is not included in the roster, the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court may select the best commissioned entity or professional from relevant social specialties to conduct the evaluation. If the selected entity or professional needs to be included in the roster of evaluators, the matter shall still be submitted to the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court at the next higher level for approval.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 遇有鉴定人应当回避等情形时，有关人民法院司法鉴定机构应当重新选择鉴定人。

**Article 12** In circumstances such as where an evaluator shall recuse himself/herself, the relevant judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall select a new evaluator.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 人民法院司法鉴定机构对外委托鉴定的，应当指派专人负责协调，主动了解鉴定的有关情况，及时处理可能影响鉴定的问题。

**Article 13** When the judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court commissions an evaluation externally, it shall assign a dedicated person to coordinate, proactively understand the relevant circumstances of the evaluation, and promptly deal with issues that may affect the evaluation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 接受委托的鉴定人认为需要补充鉴定材料时，如果由申请鉴定的当事人提供确有困难的，可以向有关人民法院司法鉴定机构提出请求，由人民法院决定依据职权采集鉴定材料。

**Article 14** If a commissioned evaluator deems it necessary to supplement evaluation materials, and if it is genuinely difficult for the party applying for the evaluation to provide them, the evaluator may submit a request to the relevant judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court, and the People's Court shall decide to collect the evaluation materials ex officio.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 鉴定人应当依法履行出庭接受质询的义务。人民法院司法鉴定机构应当协调鉴定人做好出庭工作。

**Article 15** An evaluator shall perform the obligation to appear in court and accept questioning according to law. The judicial expert evaluation institution of the People's Court shall coordinate the evaluator's preparations for appearing in court.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 列入名册的鉴定人有不履行义务，违反司法鉴定有关规定的，由有关人民法院视情节取消入册资格，并在《人民法院报》公告。

**Article 16** If an evaluator included in the roster fails to perform obligations or violates the relevant provisions on judicial expert evaluation, the relevant People's Court shall, depending on the circumstances, revoke the qualification for inclusion in the roster and make a public announcement in the People's Court Daily.
