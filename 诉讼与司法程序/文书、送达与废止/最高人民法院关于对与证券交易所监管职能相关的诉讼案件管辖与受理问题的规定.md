# 最高人民法院关于对与证券交易所监管职能相关的诉讼案件管辖与受理问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕1号  
**公布日期**：2005-01-25  
**生效日期**：2005-01-31

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、根据[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第三十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-37)和[《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》第二十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/行政诉讼/中华人民共和国行政诉讼法.md#art-22)的有关规定，指定上海证券交易所和深圳证券交易所所在地的中级人民法院分别管辖以上海证券交易所和深圳证券交易所为被告或第三人的与证券交易所监管职能相关的第一审民事和行政案件。

**Article 1** I. In accordance with the relevant provisions of Article 37 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 22 of the Administrative Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Intermediate People's Courts in the localities where the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange are located shall respectively have jurisdiction over first-instance civil and administrative cases related to the regulatory functions of the stock exchanges where the Shanghai Stock Exchange or the Shenzhen Stock Exchange is the defendant or a third party.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、与证券交易所监管职能相关的诉讼案件包括：  
（一）证券交易所根据《中华人民共和国公司法》、《中华人民共和国证券法》、《中华人民共和国证券投资基金法》、《证券交易所管理办法》等法律、法规、规章的规定，对证券发行人及其相关人员、证券交易所会员及其相关人员、证券上市和交易活动作出处理决定引发的诉讼；  
（二）证券交易所根据国务院证券监督管理机构的依法授权，对证券发行人及其相关人员、证券交易所会员及其相关人员、证券上市和交易活动作出处理决定引发的诉讼；  
（三）证券交易所根据其章程、业务规则、业务合同的规定，对证券发行人及其相关人员、证券交易所会员及其相关人员、证券上市和交易活动作出处理决定引发的诉讼；  
（四）证券交易所在履行监管职能过程中引发的其他诉讼。

**Article 2** II. Litigation cases related to the regulatory functions of a stock exchange include:  
(1) Litigation arising from handling decisions made by the stock exchange against securities issuers and their related persons, members of the stock exchange and their related persons, and securities listing and trading activities in accordance with the provisions of laws, regulations, and rules such as the Company Law of the People's Republic of China, the Securities Law of the People's Republic of China, the Securities Investment Fund Law of the People's Republic of China, and the Measures for the Administration of Stock Exchanges;  
(2) Litigation arising from handling decisions made by the stock exchange against securities issuers and their related persons, members of the stock exchange and their related persons, and securities listing and trading activities pursuant to the lawful authorization of the securities regulatory authority under the State Council;  
(3) Litigation arising from handling decisions made by the stock exchange against securities issuers and their related persons, members of the stock exchange and their related persons, and securities listing and trading activities in accordance with its articles of association, business rules, and business contracts;  
(4) Other litigation arising from the stock exchange's performance of its regulatory functions.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、投资者对证券交易所履行监管职责过程中对证券发行人及其相关人员、证券交易所会员及其相关人员、证券上市和交易活动做出的不直接涉及投资者利益的行为提起的诉讼，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 3** III. Where an investor files a lawsuit against an act taken by a stock exchange in the course of performing its regulatory duties concerning securities issuers and their related persons, members of the stock exchange and their related persons, or securities listing and trading activities that does not directly involve the interests of the investor, the People's Court shall not accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 4** IV. These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
