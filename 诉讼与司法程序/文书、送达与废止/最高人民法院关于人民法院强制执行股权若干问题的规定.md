# 最高人民法院关于人民法院强制执行股权若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕20号  
**公布日期**：2021-11-15  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于人民法院强制执行股权若干问题的规定》已于2021年11月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1850次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月20日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院强制执行股权若干问题的规定 法释〔2021〕20号 为了正确处理人民法院强制执行股权中的有关问题，维护当事人、利害关系人的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国公司法》等法律规定，结合执行工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称股权，包括有限责任公司股权、股份有限公司股份，但是在依法设立的证券交易所上市交易以及在国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所交易的股份有限公司股份除外。 第二条　被执行人是公司股东的，人民法院可以强制执行其在公司持有的股权，不得直接执行公司的财产。 第三条　依照民事诉讼法第二百二十四条的规定以被执行股权所在地确定管辖法院的，股权所在地是指股权所在公司的住所地。 第四条　人民法院可以冻结下列资料或者信息之一载明的属于被执行人的股权： （一）股权所在公司的章程、股东名册等资料； （二）公司登记机关的登记、备案信息； （三）国家企业信用信息公示系统的公示信息。 案外人基于实体权利对被冻结股权提出排除执行异议的，人民法院应当依照民事诉讼法第二百二十七条的规定进行审查。 第五条　人民法院冻结被执行人的股权，以其价额足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债权额及执行费用为限，不得明显超标的额冻结。股权价额无法确定的，可以根据申请执行人申请冻结的比例或者数量进行冻结。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称股权，包括有限责任公司股权、股份有限公司股份，但是在依法设立的证券交易所上市交易以及在国务院批准的其他全国性证券交易场所交易的股份有限公司股份除外。

**Article 1** For the purposes of these Provisions, equity includes the equity of a limited liability company and the shares of a joint stock limited company, excluding the shares of a joint stock limited company listed and traded on a lawfully established stock exchange or traded on other national securities trading venues approved by the State Council.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　被执行人是公司股东的，人民法院可以强制执行其在公司持有的股权，不得直接执行公司的财产。

**Article 2** Where the person subject to enforcement is a shareholder of a company, the People's Court may compulsorily execute the equity held by such person in the company, but shall not directly execute the property of the company.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-224)的规定以被执行股权所在地确定管辖法院的，股权所在地是指股权所在公司的住所地。

**Article 3** Where the competent court is determined based on the location of the equity subject to enforcement in accordance with Article 224 of the Civil Procedure Law, the location of the equity refers to the domicile of the company in which the equity is held.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院可以冻结下列资料或者信息之一载明的属于被执行人的股权：  
（一）股权所在公司的章程、股东名册等资料；  
（二）公司登记机关的登记、备案信息；  
（三）国家企业信用信息公示系统的公示信息。  
案外人基于实体权利对被冻结股权提出排除执行异议的，人民法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十七条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-227)的规定进行审查。

**Article 4** The People's Court may freeze the equity belonging to the person subject to enforcement as recorded in any of the following materials or information:  
(1) The articles of association, register of shareholders, and other materials of the company in which the equity is held;  
(2) The registration and filing information of the company registration authority;  
(3) The public notice information of the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.  
Where a person other than a party raises an objection to the freezing of the equity on the basis of substantive rights and seeks to exclude enforcement, the People's Court shall review the same in accordance with Article 227 of the Civil Procedure Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院冻结被执行人的股权，以其价额足以清偿生效法律文书确定的债权额及执行费用为限，不得明显超标的额冻结。股权价额无法确定的，可以根据申请执行人申请冻结的比例或者数量进行冻结。  
被执行人认为冻结明显超标的额的，可以依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)的规定提出书面异议，并附证明股权等查封、扣押、冻结财产价额的证据材料。人民法院审查后裁定异议成立的，应当自裁定生效之日起七日内解除对明显超标的额部分的冻结。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall freeze the equity of the person subject to enforcement only to the extent that the value thereof is sufficient to satisfy the amount of claims and enforcement expenses determined by the effective legal instrument, and shall not freeze an amount clearly exceeding the subject matter. Where the value of the equity cannot be determined, the freezing may be carried out based on the proportion or quantity of the equity for which the applicant for enforcement requests freezing.  
Where the person subject to enforcement considers that the freezing clearly exceeds the subject matter, such person may raise a written objection in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law, attaching evidentiary materials proving the value of the equity or other property that has been sealed, impounded, or frozen. After review, if the People's Court rules that the objection is established, it shall lift the freezing of the portion that clearly exceeds the subject matter within seven days from the date when the ruling takes effect.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民法院冻结被执行人的股权，应当向公司登记机关送达裁定书和协助执行通知书，要求其在国家企业信用信息公示系统进行公示。股权冻结自在公示系统公示时发生法律效力。多个人民法院冻结同一股权的，以在公示系统先办理公示的为在先冻结。  
依照前款规定冻结被执行人股权的，应当及时向被执行人、申请执行人送达裁定书，并将股权冻结情况书面通知股权所在公司。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于人民法院强制执行股权若干问题的规定.md#art-19" title="被《最高人民法院关于人民法院强制执行股权若干问题的规定》第19条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 6** When freezing the equity of the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall serve a ruling and a notice of assistance in enforcement on the company registration authority, requesting it to make a public notice in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. The freezing of equity shall take legal effect from the time when it is made public in the publicity system. Where multiple People's Courts freeze the same equity, the court that first completes the public notice in the publicity system shall have priority in freezing.  
Where the equity of the person subject to enforcement is frozen in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the ruling shall be served in a timely manner on the person subject to enforcement and the applicant for enforcement, and the company in which the equity is held shall be notified in writing of the freezing.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被执行人就被冻结股权所作的转让、出质或者其他有碍执行的行为，不得对抗申请执行人。

**Article 7** Any transfer, pledge, or other act of the person subject to enforcement that hinders enforcement with respect to the frozen equity shall not be asserted against the applicant for enforcement.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院冻结被执行人股权的，可以向股权所在公司送达协助执行通知书，要求其在实施增资、减资、合并、分立等对被冻结股权所占比例、股权价值产生重大影响的行为前向人民法院书面报告有关情况。人民法院收到报告后，应当及时通知申请执行人，但是涉及国家秘密、商业秘密的除外。  
股权所在公司未向人民法院报告即实施前款规定行为的，依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-114)的规定处理。  
股权所在公司或者公司董事、高级管理人员故意通过增资、减资、合并、分立、转让重大资产、对外提供担保等行为导致被冻结股权价值严重贬损，影响申请执行人债权实现的，申请执行人可以依法提起诉讼。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court freezes the equity of the person subject to enforcement, it may serve a notice of assistance in enforcement on the company in which the equity is held, requesting it to submit a written report to the People's Court on the relevant circumstances before carrying out acts that have a significant impact on the proportion or value of the frozen equity, such as capital increase, capital reduction, merger, or division. After receiving the report, the People's Court shall promptly notify the applicant for enforcement, except where state secrets or trade secrets are involved.  
Where the company in which the equity is held carries out acts specified in the preceding paragraph without reporting to the People's Court, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 114 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
Where the company in which the equity is held or its directors or senior officers intentionally cause the value of the frozen equity to be seriously impaired through acts such as capital increase, capital reduction, merger, division, transfer of major assets, or provision of external guarantees, thereby affecting the realization of the claims of the applicant for enforcement, the applicant for enforcement may initiate a lawsuit according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院冻结被执行人基于股权享有的股息、红利等收益，应当向股权所在公司送达裁定书，并要求其在该收益到期时通知人民法院。人民法院对到期的股息、红利等收益，可以书面通知股权所在公司向申请执行人或者人民法院履行。  
股息、红利等收益被冻结后，股权所在公司擅自向被执行人支付或者变相支付的，不影响人民法院要求股权所在公司支付该收益。

**Article 9** Where the People's Court freezes the dividends, bonuses, and other income derived by the person subject to enforcement from the equity, it shall serve a ruling on the company in which the equity is held and request it to notify the People's Court when such income becomes due. The People's Court may, by written notice, require the company in which the equity is held to pay the matured dividends, bonuses, and other income to the applicant for enforcement or the People's Court.  
After the dividends, bonuses, and other income are frozen, if the company in which the equity is held pays or indirectly pays such income to the person subject to enforcement without authorization, such payment shall not affect the People's Court's requirement for the company to pay such income.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被执行人申请自行变价被冻结股权，经申请执行人及其他已知执行债权人同意或者变价款足以清偿执行债务的，人民法院可以准许，但是应当在能够控制变价款的情况下监督其在指定期限内完成，最长不超过三个月。

**Article 10** Where the person subject to enforcement applies to dispose of the frozen equity by itself, and such disposal is consented to by the applicant for enforcement and other known enforcement creditors, or the proceeds from such disposal are sufficient to satisfy the enforcement debts, the People's Court may permit it, provided that the court shall supervise the completion within a specified period under the condition that the court can control the proceeds, and such period shall not exceed three months.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　拍卖被执行人的股权，人民法院应当依照《最高人民法院关于人民法院确定财产处置参考价若干问题的规定》规定的程序确定股权处置参考价，并参照参考价确定起拍价。  
确定参考价需要相关材料的，人民法院可以向公司登记机关、税务机关等部门调取，也可以责令被执行人、股权所在公司以及控制相关材料的其他主体提供；拒不提供的，可以强制提取，并可以依照[民事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-111)、第一百一十四条的规定处理。  
为确定股权处置参考价，经当事人书面申请，人民法院可以委托审计机构对股权所在公司进行审计。

**Article 11** When auctioning the equity of the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall determine the reference price for the disposal of the equity in accordance with the procedures specified in the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Determination of Reference Prices for Property Disposal by People's Courts, and shall determine the starting price with reference to the reference price.  
Where relevant materials are needed for determining the reference price, the People's Court may obtain them from the company registration authority, tax authority, or other departments, or may order the person subject to enforcement, the company in which the equity is held, and other entities in control of the relevant materials to provide them; if they refuse to provide, the court may compulsorily obtain them and may handle the matter in accordance with Articles 111 and 114 of the Civil Procedure Law.  
To determine the reference price for the disposal of the equity, the People's Court may, upon a written application of a party, entrust an audit institution to audit the company in which the equity is held.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　委托评估被执行人的股权，评估机构因缺少评估所需完整材料无法进行评估或者认为影响评估结果，被执行人未能提供且人民法院无法调取补充材料的，人民法院应当通知评估机构根据现有材料进行评估，并告知当事人因缺乏材料可能产生的不利后果。  
评估机构根据现有材料无法出具评估报告的，经申请执行人书面申请，人民法院可以根据具体情况以适当高于执行费用的金额确定起拍价，但是股权所在公司经营严重异常，股权明显没有价值的除外。  
依照前款规定确定的起拍价拍卖的，竞买人应当预交的保证金数额由人民法院根据实际情况酌定。

**Article 12** Where the People's Court entrusts an appraisal of the equity of the person subject to enforcement, and the appraisal institution cannot conduct the appraisal due to a lack of complete materials necessary for the appraisal or considers that the lack affects the appraisal results, and the person subject to enforcement fails to provide the materials and the People's Court cannot obtain supplementary materials, the People's Court shall notify the appraisal institution to conduct the appraisal based on the available materials and shall inform the parties of the adverse consequences that may arise from the lack of materials.  
Where the appraisal institution cannot issue an appraisal report based on the available materials, the People's Court may, upon a written application of the applicant for enforcement, determine the starting price at an amount appropriately higher than the enforcement expenses based on the specific circumstances, except where the company in which the equity is held has seriously abnormal operations and the equity obviously has no value.  
Where the auction is conducted based on the starting price determined in accordance with the preceding paragraph, the amount of the deposit to be paid in advance by the bidder shall be determined by the People's Court at its discretion based on the actual circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院拍卖被执行人的股权，应当采取网络司法拍卖方式。  
依据处置参考价并结合具体情况计算，拍卖被冻结股权所得价款可能明显高于债权额及执行费用的，人民法院应当对相应部分的股权进行拍卖。对相应部分的股权拍卖严重减损被冻结股权价值的，经被执行人书面申请，也可以对超出部分的被冻结股权一并拍卖。

**Article 13** When auctioning the equity of the person subject to enforcement, the People's Court shall adopt the method of online judicial auction.  
Where, based on the reference price for disposal and in light of the specific circumstances, the proceeds from auctioning the frozen equity may be significantly higher than the amount of claims and enforcement expenses, the People's Court shall auction the corresponding part of the equity. If auctioning the corresponding part of the equity would seriously impair the value of the frozen equity, the court may, upon the written application of the person subject to enforcement, also auction the frozen equity in its entirety for the portion exceeding the required amount.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　被执行人、利害关系人以具有下列情形之一为由请求不得强制拍卖股权的，人民法院不予支持：  
（一）被执行人未依法履行或者未依法全面履行出资义务；  
（二）被执行人认缴的出资未届履行期限；  
（三）法律、行政法规、部门规章等对该股权自行转让有限制；  
（四）公司章程、股东协议等对该股权自行转让有限制。  
人民法院对具有前款第一、二项情形的股权进行拍卖时，应当在拍卖公告中载明被执行人认缴出资额、实缴出资额、出资期限等信息。股权处置后，相关主体依照有关规定履行出资义务。

**Article 14** Where the person subject to enforcement or an interested party requests that the equity shall not be compulsorily auctioned on the ground that any of the following circumstances exists, the People's Court shall not support such request:  
(1) The person subject to enforcement has not fulfilled or has not fully fulfilled the obligation to contribute capital according to law;  
(2) The subscribed capital contribution of the person subject to enforcement has not yet reached the time limit for performance;  
(3) Laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, or other provisions impose restrictions on the voluntary transfer of such equity;  
(4) The articles of association of the company, the shareholders' agreement, or other documents impose restrictions on the voluntary transfer of such equity.  
Where the People's Court auctions equity that is subject to the circumstances specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, it shall specify in the auction announcement the amount of subscribed capital contribution, the amount of actually paid-in capital contribution, the term for capital contribution, and other information of the person subject to enforcement. After the equity is disposed of, the relevant entity shall perform the obligation to contribute capital in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　股权变更应当由相关部门批准的，人民法院应当在拍卖公告中载明法律、行政法规或者国务院决定规定的竞买人应当具备的资格或者条件。必要时，人民法院可以就竞买资格或者条件征询相关部门意见。  
拍卖成交后，人民法院应当通知买受人持成交确认书向相关部门申请办理股权变更批准手续。买受人取得批准手续的，人民法院作出拍卖成交裁定书；买受人未在合理期限内取得批准手续的，应当重新对股权进行拍卖。重新拍卖的，原买受人不得参加竞买。  
买受人明知不符合竞买资格或者条件依然参加竞买，且在成交后未能在合理期限内取得相关部门股权变更批准手续的，交纳的保证金不予退还。保证金不足以支付拍卖产生的费用损失、弥补重新拍卖价款低于原拍卖价款差价的，人民法院可以裁定原买受人补交；拒不补交的，强制执行。

**Article 15** Where the change of equity is subject to approval by the relevant department, the People's Court shall specify in the auction announcement the qualifications or conditions that bidders shall meet as prescribed by laws, administrative regulations, or decisions of the State Council. Where necessary, the People's Court may seek the opinions of the relevant department on the bidding qualifications or conditions.  
After the auction is concluded, the People's Court shall notify the buyer to apply to the relevant department for approval of the change of equity by presenting the transaction confirmation. If the buyer obtains the approval, the People's Court shall issue a ruling on the auction transaction; if the buyer fails to obtain the approval within a reasonable time, the equity shall be re-auctioned. In a re-auction, the original buyer shall not participate in the bidding.  
If the buyer, knowing that it does not meet the bidding qualifications or conditions, still participates in the bidding and fails to obtain the departmental approval for the change of equity within a reasonable time after the transaction, the deposit paid shall not be refunded. If the deposit is insufficient to cover the costs and losses incurred by the auction and to make up for the difference between the price of the re-auction and that of the original auction, the People's Court may rule that the original buyer shall make up the shortfall; if the original buyer refuses to make up the shortfall, compulsory execution shall be carried out.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　生效法律文书确定被执行人交付股权，因股权所在公司在生效法律文书作出后增资或者减资导致被执行人实际持股比例降低或者升高的，人民法院应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）生效法律文书已经明确交付股权的出资额的，按照该出资额交付股权；  
（二）生效法律文书仅明确交付一定比例的股权的，按照生效法律文书作出时该比例所对应出资额占当前公司注册资本总额的比例交付股权。

**Article 16** Where the effective legal instrument determines that the person subject to enforcement shall deliver equity, and the actual shareholding ratio of the person subject to enforcement is reduced or increased due to a capital increase or reduction by the company in which the equity is held after the effective legal instrument is rendered, the People's Court shall handle the matter separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the effective legal instrument has already specified the amount of capital contribution corresponding to the equity to be delivered, the equity shall be delivered based on that amount of capital contribution;  
(2) If the effective legal instrument only specifies a certain percentage of equity to be delivered, the equity shall be delivered based on the proportion that the amount of capital contribution corresponding to such percentage at the time when the effective legal instrument was rendered bears to the total registered capital of the company at the current time.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在审理股东资格确认纠纷案件中，当事人提出要求公司签发出资证明书、记载于股东名册并办理公司登记机关登记的诉讼请求且其主张成立的，人民法院应当予以支持；当事人未提出前述诉讼请求的，可以根据案件具体情况向其释明。  
生效法律文书仅确认股权属于当事人所有，当事人可以持该生效法律文书自行向股权所在公司、公司登记机关申请办理股权变更手续；向人民法院申请强制执行的，不予受理。

**Article 17** In the trial of a dispute over confirmation of shareholder status, if a party raises a claim requesting the company to issue a capital contribution certificate, to record the party in the register of shareholders, and to file with the company registration authority, and such claim is sustained, the People's Court shall support it; if the party has not raised the aforesaid claim, the court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, explain the matter to such party.  
If the effective legal instrument only confirms that the equity belongs to the party, such party may, by presenting the effective legal instrument, apply to the company in which the equity is held and the company registration authority for the change of equity procedures; if the party applies to the People's Court for compulsory execution, the application shall not be accepted.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院对被执行人在其他营利法人享有的投资权益强制执行的，参照适用本规定。

**Article 18** Where the People's Court compulsorily executes the investment rights and interests of the person subject to enforcement in other for-profit legal persons, these Provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本规定自2022年1月1日起施行。  
施行前本院公布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。
