# 最高人民法院关于在确定经济纠纷案件管辖中如何确定购销合同履行地问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1996〕28号  
**公布日期**：1996-09-12

---

### 正文

一、当事人在合同中明确约定履行地点的，以约定的履行地点为合同履行地。  
当事人在合同中未明确约定履行地点的，以约定的交货地点为合同履行地。  
合同中约定的货物到达地、到站地、验收地、安装调试地等，均不应视为合同履行地。

**Article None** 1. Where the parties expressly agree on a place of performance in the contract, the agreed place of performance shall be the place of performance of the contract.  
Where the parties do not expressly agree on a place of performance in the contract, the agreed place of delivery shall be the place of performance of the contract.  
The place of arrival of goods, the place of arrival at the station, the place of inspection, the place of installation and commissioning, etc., as agreed in the contract, shall not be deemed as the place of performance of the contract.

二、当事人在合同中明确约定了履行地点或交货地点，但在实际履行中以书面方式或双方当事人一致认可的其他方式变更约定的，以变更后的约定确定合同履行地。当事人未以上述方式变更原约定，或者变更原合同而未涉及履行地问题的，仍以原合同的约定确定履行地。

**Article None** 2. Where the parties expressly agree on a place of performance or place of delivery in the contract, but change the agreement in writing or in any other manner unanimously recognized by both parties during actual performance, the place of performance of the contract shall be determined in accordance with the changed agreement. Where the parties do not change the original agreement in the aforesaid manner, or change the original contract without addressing the issue of the place of performance, the place of performance shall still be determined in accordance with the original contract.

三、当事人在合同中对履行地点、交货地点未作约定或约定不明确的，或者虽有约定但未实际交付货物，且当事人双方住所地均不在合同约定的履行地，以及口头购销合同纠纷案件，均不依履行地确定案件管辖。  
本院以前有关购销合同履行地的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。  
1996年9月12日

**Article None** 3. Where the parties do not agree on or have an unclear agreement on the place of performance or place of delivery in the contract, or where there is an agreement but the goods are not actually delivered, and the domiciles of both parties are not in the agreed place of performance, as well as disputes over oral purchase and sale contracts, the jurisdiction of the case shall not be determined based on the place of performance.  
Where the previous judicial interpretations of this Court concerning the place of performance of purchase and sale contracts are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.  
September 12, 1996
