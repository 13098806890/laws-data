### 最高人民法院关于需要再审而又发现遗漏了诉讼第三人的案件应指令哪一审法院按什么程序再审问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于需要再审而又发现遗漏了诉讼第三人的案件  
  
应指令哪一审法院按什么程序再审问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1986〕18号  
  
青海省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（86）青法经发字第3号请示收悉。关于上级人民法院对业经二审终审的经济纠纷案件，发现确有错误需要再审，但又发现原来的一审、二审遗漏了诉讼第三人，应指令哪一审法院按什么程序再审的问题，经研究，答复如下：  
  
《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第一百六十条规定：“人民法院按照审判监督程序再审的案件，原来是第一审的，按照第一审程序审判，所作的判决、裁定，当事人可以上诉；原来是第二审的，或者是上级人民法院提审的，按照第二审程序审判，所作的判决、裁定，是发生法律效力的判决、裁定。”根据这一规定，对业经二审终审的案件需要再审的，无论是上级人民法院提审或指令原二审法院再审，都须按第二审程序审判。原二审法院再审，应按照最高人民法院《关于贯彻执行〈民事诉讼法（试行）〉若干问题的意见》（12）办理，即：第二审人民法院在诉讼进行中发现需要更换、追加当事人的，在更换、追加后，经调解，当事人之间不能达成协议时，应当将案件发回原审人民法院重新审判。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年五月二十一日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Question of Which Trial Court and Under What Procedures a Case Requiring Retrial and Involving an Omitted Third Party Litigant Shall Be Referred for Retrial  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1986] No. 18  
Higher People's Court of Qinghai Province:  
Your Request No. (86) Qing Fa Jing Fa Zi No. 3 has been received. With respect to the question of which trial court and under what procedures the higher people's court shall refer a retrial for an economic dispute case that has been finally adjudicated upon second instance, when errors are found and retrial is necessary, but the first instance and second instance courts previously omitted a third party litigant, upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Article 160 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) provides: "For a case retried by a people's court under the adjudication supervision procedure, if the case was originally tried at first instance, it shall be tried under the first instance procedure, and the parties may appeal against the judgment or ruling made thereby; if the case was originally tried at second instance, or if the case is brought up for trial directly by a higher people's court, it shall be tried under the second instance procedure, and the judgment or ruling made thereby shall be a legally effective judgment or ruling." According to this provision, when a case that has been finally adjudicated upon second instance requires retrial, whether the higher people's court brings the case up for trial directly or instructs the original second instance court to conduct a retrial, the trial shall be conducted under the second instance procedure. The original second instance court conducting the retrial shall proceed in accordance with Paragraph (12) of the Supreme People's Court's Opinions on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), namely: if the second instance court, during the proceedings, finds that a party needs to be replaced or added, and after such replacement or addition, mediation fails to reach an agreement between the parties, the case shall be remanded to the original trial court for a new trial.  
This reply is given hereby.  
May 21, 1986
