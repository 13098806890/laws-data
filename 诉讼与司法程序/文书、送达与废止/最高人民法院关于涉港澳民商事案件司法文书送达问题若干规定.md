# 最高人民法院关于涉港澳民商事案件司法文书送达问题若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕2号  
**公布日期**：2009-02-16  
**生效日期**：2009-03-16

> 最高人民法院《关于涉港澳民商事案件司法文书送达问题若干规定》已于2009年2月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1463次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年3月16日起施行。 2009年3月9日 最高人民法院 关于涉港澳民商事案件司法文书 送达问题若干规定 法释〔2009〕2号 为规范涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区民商事案件司法文书送达，根据《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》的规定，结合审判实践，制定本规定。 第一条  人民法院审理涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的民商事案件时，向住所地在香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的受送达人送达司法文书，适用本规定。 第二条  本规定所称司法文书，是指起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证等与诉讼相关的文书。 第三条  作为受送达人的自然人或者企业、其他组织的法定代表人、主要负责人在内地的，人民法院可以直接向该自然人或者法定代表人、主要负责人送达。 第四条  除受送达人在授权委托书中明确表明其诉讼代理人无权代为接收有关司法文书外，其委托的诉讼代理人为有权代其接受送达的诉讼代理人，人民法院可以向该诉讼代理人送达。 第五条  受送达人在内地设立有代表机构的，人民法院可以直接向该代表机构送达。 受送达人在内地设立有分支机构或者业务代办人并授权其接受送达的，人民法院可以直接向该分支机构或者业务代办人送达。 第六条  人民法院向在内地没有住所的受送达人送达司法文书，可以按照最高人民法院《关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互委托送达民商事司法文书的安排》或者最高人民法院《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排》送达。 按照前款规定方式送达的，自内地的高级人民法院或者最高人民法院将有关司法文书递送香港特别行政区高等法院或者澳门特别行政区终审法院之日起满三个月，如果未能收到送达与否的证明文件且不存在本规定第十二条规定情形的，视为不能适用上述安排中规定的方式送达。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  人民法院审理涉及香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的民商事案件时，向住所地在香港特别行政区、澳门特别行政区的受送达人送达司法文书，适用本规定。

**Article 1** When a People's Court hears a civil or commercial case involving the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region, these Provisions shall apply to the service of judicial documents on a person to be served whose domicile is in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Macao Special Administrative Region.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  本规定所称司法文书，是指起诉状副本、上诉状副本、反诉状副本、答辩状副本、传票、判决书、调解书、裁定书、支付令、决定书、通知书、证明书、送达回证等与诉讼相关的文书。

**Article 2** The term "judicial documents" as used in these Provisions refers to copies of the statement of prosecution, copies of the statement of appeal, copies of the statement of counterclaim, copies of the statement of defense, summons, written judgments, written mediation agreements, written rulings, payment orders, written decisions, written notices, written certificates, acknowledgment of service receipts, and other documents related to litigation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  作为受送达人的自然人或者企业、其他组织的法定代表人、主要负责人在内地的，人民法院可以直接向该自然人或者法定代表人、主要负责人送达。

**Article 3** If the natural person to be served, or the legal representative or principal person in charge of an enterprise or other organization to be served, is within the mainland, the People's Court may directly serve the judicial documents on such natural person, legal representative, or principal person in charge.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  除受送达人在授权委托书中明确表明其诉讼代理人无权代为接收有关司法文书外，其委托的诉讼代理人为有权代其接受送达的诉讼代理人，人民法院可以向该诉讼代理人送达。

**Article 4** Unless the person to be served expressly indicates in the power of attorney that his or her litigation agent has no authority to accept the relevant judicial documents on his or her behalf, the litigation agent appointed by such person shall be the litigation agent authorized to accept service on his or her behalf, and the People's Court may serve the documents on such litigation agent.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  受送达人在内地设立有代表机构的，人民法院可以直接向该代表机构送达。  
受送达人在内地设立有分支机构或者业务代办人并授权其接受送达的，人民法院可以直接向该分支机构或者业务代办人送达。

**Article 5** If the person to be served has established a representative office within the mainland, the People's Court may directly serve the judicial documents on such representative office.  
If the person to be served has established a branch or a business agent within the mainland and has authorized it to accept service, the People's Court may directly serve the judicial documents on such branch or business agent.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  人民法院向在内地没有住所的受送达人送达司法文书，可以按照最高人民法院《关于内地与香港特别行政区法院相互委托送达民商事司法文书的安排》或者最高人民法院《关于内地与澳门特别行政区法院就民商事案件相互委托送达司法文书和调取证据的安排》送达。  
按照前款规定方式送达的，自内地的高级人民法院或者最高人民法院将有关司法文书递送香港特别行政区高等法院或者澳门特别行政区终审法院之日起满三个月，如果未能收到送达与否的证明文件且不存在[本规定第十二条](#art-12)规定情形的，视为不能适用上述安排中规定的方式送达。

**Article 6** When a People's Court serves judicial documents on a person to be served who has no domicile within the mainland, it may serve in accordance with the "Arrangement between the Supreme People's Court and the High Court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region on Mutual Entrustment of Service of Judicial Documents in Civil and Commercial Matters" or the "Arrangement between the Supreme People's Court and the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region on Mutual Entrustment of Service of Judicial Documents and Taking of Evidence in Civil and Commercial Matters."  
If service is effected in accordance with the preceding paragraph, upon the expiry of three months from the date on which the Higher People's Court or the Supreme People's Court within the mainland delivers the relevant judicial documents to the High Court of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region or the Court of Final Appeal of the Macao Special Administrative Region, if no proof of service or non-service is received and none of the circumstances specified in Article 12 of these Provisions exist, it shall be deemed that the method of service provided for in the above arrangements cannot be applied.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  人民法院向受送达人送达司法文书，可以邮寄送达。  
邮寄送达时应附有送达回证。受送达人未在送达回证上签收但在邮件回执上签收的，视为送达，签收日期为送达日期。  
自邮寄之日起满三个月，虽未收到送达与否的证明文件，但存在[本规定第十二条](#art-12)规定情形的，期间届满之日视为送达。  
自邮寄之日起满三个月，如果未能收到送达与否的证明文件，且不存在[本规定第十二条](#art-12)规定情形的，视为未送达。

**Article 7** A People's Court may serve judicial documents on the person to be served by mail.  
When serving by mail, an acknowledgment of service receipt shall be enclosed. If the person to be served does not sign the acknowledgment of service receipt but signs the mail return receipt, service shall be deemed to have been effected, and the date of signing shall be the date of service.  
If, upon the expiry of three months from the date of mailing, although no proof of service or non-service is received, but the circumstances specified in Article 12 of these Provisions exist, service shall be deemed to have been effected on the date of expiry of the period.  
If, upon the expiry of three months from the date of mailing, no proof of service or non-service is received and none of the circumstances specified in Article 12 of these Provisions exist, service shall be deemed not to have been effected.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  人民法院可以通过传真、电子邮件等能够确认收悉的其他适当方式向受送达人送达。

**Article 8** A People's Court may serve judicial documents on the person to be served by facsimile, email, or other appropriate means capable of confirming receipt.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  人民法院不能依照本规定上述方式送达的，可以公告送达。公告内容应当在内地和受送达人住所地公开发行的报刊上刊登，自公告之日起满三个月即视为送达。

**Article 9** If a People's Court cannot serve in accordance with the above methods provided for in these Provisions, it may serve by announcement. The content of the announcement shall be published in newspapers and periodicals distributed publicly in the mainland and in the place of domicile of the person to be served, and service shall be deemed to have been effected upon the expiry of three months from the date of announcement.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  除公告送达方式外，人民法院可以同时采取多种法定方式向受送达人送达。  
采取多种方式送达的，应当根据最先实现送达的方式确定送达日期。

**Article 10** Except for service by announcement, a People's Court may concurrently adopt multiple statutory methods to serve the judicial documents on the person to be served.  
If multiple methods are adopted, the date of service shall be determined according to the method by which service is first effected.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  人民法院向在内地的受送达人或者受送达人的法定代表人、主要负责人、诉讼代理人、代表机构以及有权接受送达的分支机构、业务代办人送达司法文书，可以适用留置送达的方式。

**Article 11** A People's Court may apply the method of service by leaving the documents at the premises when serving judicial documents on the person to be served who is within the mainland, or on the legal representative, principal person in charge, litigation agent, representative office, or any branch or business agent with authority to accept service of the person to be served.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  受送达人未对人民法院送达的司法文书履行签收手续，但存在以下情形之一的，视为送达：  
（一）受送达人向人民法院提及了所送达司法文书的内容；  
（二）受送达人已经按照所送达司法文书的内容履行；  
（三）其他可以确认已经送达的情形。

**Article 12** If the person to be served has not gone through the procedures of signing for the judicial documents served by the People's Court, but any of the following circumstances exists, service shall be deemed to have been effected:  
(1) The person to be served has mentioned the content of the served judicial documents to the People's Court;  
(2) The person to be served has already performed in accordance with the content of the served judicial documents;  
(3) Other circumstances under which service may be confirmed as having been effected.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  下级人民法院送达司法文书，根据有关规定需要通过上级人民法院转递的，应当附申请转递函。  
上级人民法院收到下级人民法院申请转递的司法文书，应当在七个工作日内予以转递。  
上级人民法院认为下级人民法院申请转递的司法文书不符合有关规定需要补正的，应当在七个工作日内退回申请转递的人民法院。

**Article 13** If a lower People's Court, in serving judicial documents, needs to forward them through a higher People's Court in accordance with relevant provisions, it shall attach a letter applying for forwarding.  
Upon receipt of the judicial documents forwarded by a lower People's Court, the higher People's Court shall forward them within seven working days.  
If the higher People's Court considers that the judicial documents forwarded by the lower People's Court do not comply with relevant provisions and need to be supplemented or corrected, it shall return them to the lower People's Court that applied for the forwarding within seven working days.
