# 最高人民法院关于债务人在约定的期限届满后未履行债务而出具没有还款日期的欠款条诉讼时效期间应从何时开始计算问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于债务人在约定的期限届满后未履行  
债务而出具没有还款日期的欠款条诉讼  
时效期间应从何时开始计算问题的批复  
1994年3月26日      法复〔1994〕3号  
山东省高级人民法院：  
你院鲁高法〔1992〕70号请示收悉。关于债务人在约定的期限届满后未履行债务，而出具没有还款日期的欠款条，诉讼时效期间应从何时开始计算的问题，经研究，答复如下：  
据你院报告称，双方当事人原约定，供方交货后，需方立即付款。需方收货后因无款可付，经供方同意写了没有还款日期的欠款条。根据《中华人民共和国民法通则》第一百四十条的规定，对此应认定诉讼时效中断。如果供方在诉讼时效中断后一直未主张权利，诉讼时效期间则应从供方收到需方所写欠款条之日的第2天开始重新计算。  
此复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of When the Limitation of Action  
Shall Start to Count Where a Debtor Issues an IOU  
with No Repayment Date after Failing to Perform  
the Debt upon Expiry of the Agreed Term  
March 26, 1994 Fa Fu [1994] No. 3  
Higher People's Court of Shandong Province:  
Your Request for Instructions Lu Gao Fa [1992] No. 70 has been received. With respect to the issue of when the limitation of action shall start to count where a debtor, after failing to perform the debt upon expiry of the agreed term, issues an IOU with no repayment date, upon study, the reply is as follows:  
According to the report from your court, the parties originally agreed that upon delivery of goods by the supplier, the purchaser shall make immediate payment. After receiving the goods, the purchaser, having no funds available for payment, and with the supplier's consent, wrote an IOU with no repayment date. Pursuant to Article 140 of the General Principles of Civil Law of the People's Republic of China, this shall be deemed to interrupt the limitation of action. If the supplier has never asserted its rights after the interruption of the limitation of action, the limitation period shall start anew from the second day after the supplier receives the IOU written by the purchaser.  
This reply.
