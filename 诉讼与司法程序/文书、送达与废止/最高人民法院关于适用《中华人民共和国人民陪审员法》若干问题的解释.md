# 最高人民法院关于适用《中华人民共和国人民陪审员法》若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：诉讼与非诉讼程序法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕5号  
**公布日期**：2019-02-18  
**生效日期**：2019-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于适用〈中华人民共和国人民陪审员法〉若干问题的解释》已于2019年2月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1761次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年5月1日起施行。 2019年4月24日 最高人民法院 关于适用《中华人民共和国人民陪审员法》 若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕5号 为依法保障和规范人民陪审员参加审判活动，根据《中华人民共和国人民陪审员法》等法律的规定，结合审判实际，制定本解释。 第一条　根据人民陪审员法第十五条、第十六条的规定，人民法院决定由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判的，合议庭成员确定后，应当及时告知当事人。 第二条　对于人民陪审员法第十五条、第十六条规定之外的第一审普通程序案件，人民法院应当告知刑事案件被告人、民事案件原告和被告、行政案件原告，在收到通知五日内有权申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判案件。 人民法院接到当事人在规定期限内提交的申请后，经审查决定由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判的，合议庭成员确定后，应当及时告知当事人。 第三条　人民法院应当在开庭七日前从人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定人民陪审员。 人民法院可以根据案件审判需要，从人民陪审员名单中随机抽取一定数量的候补人民陪审员，并确定递补顺序，一并告知当事人。 因案件类型需要具有相应专业知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，可以根据具体案情，在符合专业需求的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。 第四条　人民陪审员确定后，人民法院应当将参审案件案由、当事人姓名或名称、开庭地点、开庭时间等事项告知参审人民陪审员及候补人民陪审员。 必要时，人民法院可以将参加审判活动的时间、地点等事项书面通知人民陪审员所在单位。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　根据[人民陪审员法第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民陪审员法.md#art-15)、第十六条的规定，人民法院决定由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判的，合议庭成员确定后，应当及时告知当事人。

**Article 1** According to Articles 15 and 16 of the People's Assessors Law, where a People's Court decides to form a collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges for adjudication, it shall promptly inform the parties after the members of the collegial panel are determined.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对于[人民陪审员法第十五条](../../宪法与国家机构/法律及决定/中华人民共和国人民陪审员法.md#art-15)、第十六条规定之外的第一审普通程序案件，人民法院应当告知刑事案件被告人、民事案件原告和被告、行政案件原告，在收到通知五日内有权申请由人民陪审员参加合议庭审判案件。  
人民法院接到当事人在规定期限内提交的申请后，经审查决定由人民陪审员和法官组成合议庭审判的，合议庭成员确定后，应当及时告知当事人。

**Article 2** For cases under ordinary first-instance procedures other than those provided for in Articles 15 and 16 of the People's Assessors Law, the People's Court shall inform the criminal defendant, the civil plaintiff and defendant, and the administrative plaintiff that they have the right, within five days of receiving the notice, to apply for trial by a collegial panel with people's assessors.  
After receiving an application submitted by a party within the prescribed time limit, where the People's Court, upon examination, decides to form a collegial panel composed of people's assessors and judges for adjudication, it shall promptly inform the parties after the members of the collegial panel are determined.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院应当在开庭七日前从人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定人民陪审员。  
人民法院可以根据案件审判需要，从人民陪审员名单中随机抽取一定数量的候补人民陪审员，并确定递补顺序，一并告知当事人。  
因案件类型需要具有相应专业知识的人民陪审员参加合议庭审判的，可以根据具体案情，在符合专业需求的人民陪审员名单中随机抽取确定。

**Article 3** The People's Court shall randomly select and determine people's assessors from the list of people's assessors seven days before the court session.  
The People's Court may, based on the needs of the case adjudication, randomly select a certain number of alternate people's assessors from the list of people's assessors, determine the order of substitution, and inform the parties thereof at the same time.  
Where the type of case requires people's assessors with corresponding professional knowledge to participate in the collegial panel adjudication, the People's Court may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, randomly select and determine them from the list of people's assessors that meet the professional requirements.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民陪审员确定后，人民法院应当将参审案件案由、当事人姓名或名称、开庭地点、开庭时间等事项告知参审人民陪审员及候补人民陪审员。  
必要时，人民法院可以将参加审判活动的时间、地点等事项书面通知人民陪审员所在单位。

**Article 4** After the people's assessors are determined, the People's Court shall inform the participating people's assessors and alternate people's assessors of the cause of action, names of the parties, place of court session, time of court session, and other matters related to the case.  
Where necessary, the People's Court may notify in writing the unit in which the people's assessor works of the time, place, and other matters concerning participation in the trial activity.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民陪审员不参加下列案件的审理：  
（一）依照民事诉讼法适用特别程序、督促程序、公示催告程序审理的案件；  
（二）申请承认外国法院离婚判决的案件；  
（三）裁定不予受理或者不需要开庭审理的案件。

**Article 5** People's assessors shall not participate in the adjudication of the following cases:  
(1) Cases that are to be tried in accordance with the Civil Procedure Law under special procedures, summary procedures, or procedures for public notice and exhortation;  
(2) Cases involving an application for recognition of a divorce judgment of a foreign court;  
(3) Cases that are ruled not to accept or do not require a court session.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民陪审员不得参与审理由其以人民调解员身份先行调解的案件。

**Article 6** People's assessors shall not participate in the adjudication of cases in which they previously conducted mediation in the capacity of a people's mediator.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　当事人依法有权申请人民陪审员回避。人民陪审员的回避，适用审判人员回避的法律规定。  
人民陪审员回避事由经审查成立的，人民法院应当及时确定递补人选。

**Article 7** A party has the right, according to law, to apply for the withdrawal of a people's assessor. The withdrawal of a people's assessor shall be governed by the legal provisions on the withdrawal of adjudicators.  
Where the grounds for withdrawal of a people's assessor are established upon examination, the People's Court shall promptly determine a substitute.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院应当在开庭前，将相关权利和义务告知人民陪审员，并为其阅卷提供便利条件。

**Article 8** The People's Court shall, before the court session, inform the people's assessor of the relevant rights and obligations, and provide them with convenient conditions for reviewing the case files.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　七人合议庭开庭前，应当制作事实认定问题清单，根据案件具体情况，区分事实认定问题与法律适用问题，对争议事实问题逐项列举，供人民陪审员在庭审时参考。事实认定问题和法律适用问题难以区分的，视为事实认定问题。

**Article 9** Before the court session of a seven-member collegial panel, a list of fact-finding issues shall be prepared. Based on the specific circumstances of the case, it shall distinguish fact-finding issues from issues of application of law, and enumerate each disputed fact issue for the reference of the people's assessors during the trial. Where it is difficult to distinguish between a fact-finding issue and an issue of application of law, it shall be deemed a fact-finding issue.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　案件审判过程中，人民陪审员依法有权参加案件调查和调解工作。

**Article 10** During the adjudication of a case, a people's assessor has the right, according to law, to participate in the investigation and mediation work of the case.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　庭审过程中，人民陪审员依法有权向诉讼参加人发问，审判长应当提示人民陪审员围绕案件争议焦点进行发问。

**Article 11** During the court session, a people's assessor has the right, according to law, to question the participants in the proceedings. The presiding judge shall remind the people's assessor to focus the questions on the disputed issues of the case.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　合议庭评议案件时，先由承办法官介绍案件涉及的相关法律、证据规则，然后由人民陪审员和法官依次发表意见，审判长最后发表意见并总结合议庭意见。

**Article 12** When the collegial panel deliberates on a case, the judge handling the case shall first introduce the relevant laws and evidence rules involved in the case. Then, the people's assessors and judges shall express their opinions in turn. The presiding judge shall express his or her opinion last and summarize the opinion of the collegial panel.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　七人合议庭评议时，审判长应当归纳和介绍需要通过评议讨论决定的案件事实认定问题，并列出案件事实问题清单。  
人民陪审员全程参加合议庭评议，对于事实认定问题，由人民陪审员和法官在共同评议的基础上进行表决。对于法律适用问题，人民陪审员不参加表决，但可以发表意见，并记录在卷。

**Article 13** When a seven-member collegial panel deliberates, the presiding judge shall summarize and introduce the fact-finding issues of the case that need to be determined through deliberation and discussion, and list the case fact issues.  
People's assessors shall participate in the collegial panel deliberation throughout the entire process. For fact-finding issues, the people's assessors and judges shall vote on the basis of joint deliberation. For issues of application of law, the people's assessors shall not participate in the voting, but may express opinions, which shall be recorded in the file.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民陪审员应当认真阅读评议笔录，确认无误后签名。

**Article 14** A people's assessor shall carefully read the deliberation record, and sign it after confirming that it is correct.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　人民陪审员列席审判委员会讨论其参加审理的案件时，可以发表意见。

**Article 15** When a people's assessor sits in on a meeting of the adjudication committee discussing a case in which he or she participated as a trial participant, he or she may express opinions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　案件审结后，人民法院应将裁判文书副本及时送交参加该案审判的人民陪审员。

**Article 16** After the case is concluded, the People's Court shall promptly send a copy of the judgment document to the people's assessor who participated in the adjudication of the case.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　中级、基层人民法院应当保障人民陪审员均衡参审，结合本院实际情况，一般在不超过30件的范围内合理确定每名人民陪审员年度参加审判案件的数量上限，报高级人民法院备案，并向社会公告。

**Article 17** Intermediate and primary people's courts shall ensure balanced participation of people's assessors. Taking into account the actual circumstances of the court, they shall reasonably determine the maximum number of cases in which each people's assessor may participate annually, generally within a range not exceeding 30 cases, report it to the higher people's court for record, and make a public announcement.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院应当依法规范和保障人民陪审员参加审判活动，不得安排人民陪审员从事与履行法定审判职责无关的工作。

**Article 18** The People's Court shall, according to law, regulate and ensure the participation of people's assessors in trial activities, and shall not assign people's assessors to engage in work unrelated to the performance of their statutory adjudicative duties.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本解释自2019年5月1日起施行。  
本解释公布施行后，最高人民法院于2010年1月12日发布的《最高人民法院关于人民陪审员参加审判活动若干问题的规定》同时废止。最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，不再适用。

**Article 19** This Interpretation shall come into force as of May 1, 2019.  
After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning People's Assessors Participating in Trial Activities issued by the Supreme People's Court on January 12, 2010 shall be simultaneously abolished. Any judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court that are inconsistent with this Interpretation shall no longer apply.
