### 最高人民法院关于对破产案件的债务人未被执行的财产均应中止执行问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对破产案件的债务人未被执行的财产  
  
均应中止执行问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕9号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法执（1993）字第 4号《关于执行案件已冻结的款能否再作为破产财产清偿的请示报告》收悉，经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国企业破产法（试行）》关于“人民法院受理破产案件后，对债务人财产的其他民事执行程序均应中止”的规定，以破产案件的债务人为被执行人的执行案件，执行法院虽对该债务人的财产已决定采取或者已经采取了冻结、扣留、查封或扣押等措施的，仍属于未执行财产，均应当依法中止执行。  
  
执行程序中止后，该执行案件的债权人，可凭生效的法律文书向受理破产案件的人民法院申报债权。如果受理破产案件的人民法院裁定宣告债务人（被执行人）破产，被中止执行的财产应当作为破产财产；如果破产案件审理终结，债务人不被宣告破产，被中止的执行程序可恢复进行。  
  
1993年9月17日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue That All Properties of the Debtor in Bankruptcy Cases That Have Not Been Enforced Shall Be Suspended from Enforcement  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 9  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your Court's Chuan Gao Fa Zhi (1993) No. 4 "Request for Instructions on Whether Funds Already Frozen in Enforcement Cases Can Be Used as Bankruptcy Property for Distribution" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
In accordance with the provision of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) that "after a People's Court accepts a bankruptcy case, all other civil enforcement procedures against the debtor's property shall be suspended," for enforcement cases where the debtor in the bankruptcy case is the person subject to enforcement, even if the enforcement court has decided to adopt or has adopted measures such as freezing, withholding, sealing up, or seizing the debtor's property, such property shall still be considered property not yet enforced, and enforcement shall all be suspended according to law.  
After the enforcement procedure is suspended, the creditor in the enforcement case may file a claim with the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy case based on the effective legal instrument. If the People's Court that accepted the bankruptcy case rules to declare the debtor (person subject to enforcement) bankrupt, the property subject to the suspended enforcement shall be treated as bankruptcy property; if the bankruptcy case concludes and the debtor is not declared bankrupt, the suspended enforcement procedure may be resumed.  
September 17, 1993
